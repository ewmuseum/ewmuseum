Reliving a nightmare, part 6: Only one kind of madness remained… March Madness…

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-02 I’ve had my mind play tricks on me. I’ve seen things I wasn’t so sure I should be seeing. They say that the eyes are portals to the soul. But at this moment in time my eyes must be portals to another or some sort of another realm. For what I see standing before me isn’t something I’ve ever seen before. Something I had never thought I’d be seeing. Somebody I thought I’d never see again… Life and death are both mysteries that have plagued man since the dawning of time. The meaning of life is a question many men have asked. A question many men die never knowing. Death a mystery in it’s self. Was there a life after death? Could somebody come back to life? I had thought I knew the answer to at least this question, no. But the person standing in front of me makes me wonder if I’ve ever known anything. I motioned for the person to sit in the booth across from me. Shedding layers of ragged and torn coats the old man did so. The same old man I had thought I left dead in an alley not just thirty minutes before. At that time the old man had a dull half dead gleam in his eyes. As if he was on his last legs of life. He was beaten down and shattered by life it’s self. But now his eyes burned brightly by what I thought was a new longing for life. His skin was now no longer pale and brittle looking. He looked at least ten years younger. What had changed this man so? “Rip.” He said in a voice that sounded entirely different than from before… I thought he had died. I was confused by him knowing my name. I hadn’t spoken a word when I had seen him last. I just sat and listened. Listened to who I thought was a dying man. But he wasn’t dead he was here sitting across from me and talking and looking as though he was in perfect health. “I know you got a million questions running together inside your head.” No joke about that. But wouldn’t he if a dead man was sitting in front of him. “But I don’t have the time to answer all of your questions.” Answer just one then, how are you alive? “Back in that alley you do something for me. Something most other people wouldn’t have done. That’s why I am here, to repay you.” Repay me? For killing you? I know now that what everybody has been saying is true, I’m insane, I’m a psychotic freak, and I need to be locked up; For my own good as well as everybody else’s. “Inside that raging storm inside your head is a voice. A voice that you have been fighting for a very long time now.”How in the h*ll did he know about that? I’ve never told anyone that before, NOBODY. “Listen to that voice Rip.” Would you stop calling me that. My name isn’t Rip it’s his name and I’m not listening to him. “That voice in your head is the answer. You have to learn to listen to it as well as control it.” Listening to it is bad enough. Controlling it is damn well impossible. “You probably think I’m just an old man that knows nothing. But if life and… death have taught me anything it’s to listen to yourself.” Death… taught you something… are you dead? I’m not imagining this you really are dead… and I’m talking to you… a dead man… “You freed me, released me, and set me free. I feel I owe you and I was granted one last thing to do. And that was to talk with you. So to answer your questions I know your asking inside that head of yours, yes I am dead.” But how can this be? It’s not realistic? It’s impossible? And why me? “You need to stop thinking of all these questions. I told you I can’t answer them. Just listen to me carefully. I may be your only hope of setting yourself free.” Set myself free, how am I suppose to do that? And what do you mean? “That voice inside you head. You need to let it out of it’s confines. Let it grow. Let it spread though out yourself. He is what set me free. And he will set you free.” But he’s a monster… “You think that you fear him. But you fear nothing but yourself. Release that fear and allow him to free you. It’s the only way the voices will stop. You and him are one.” As if repeating the old man the voice replys. We are one… What is this old man doing? He’s going to release the freak. He’s going to release the Ripper. We are one… But what if this old man is right? What if this freak inside of my head is part of me and not something plaguing me? What if the freak and I are one. We are one… The old man stared at me. I had a hard time keeping him focused. He seemed to be moving, swirling. Something was happening to me. Something inside of me was changing. My head it felt as though a nuclear weapon went off inside of it. My vision was getting blurry. My thoughts jumbled. With each passing second the voice in my head was getting stronger and stronger. Yes, let me out. Let me bring my pain and suffering upon this world. Let me show them what it is like to be psychotic let me show them what it is like to be a freak. Let me show them what the man known as the Ripper means. I tried to fight it. I tried to keep him locked away. I couldn’t… I can’t… The voice seemed stronger than ever before. I seemed more alive than ever before. It was as if the old man fed it, nurtured it, and gave it life of its own. I was afraid now of the voice, more so than ever before. Before I couldn’t ignore it, but at times I could control it. But now it seemed as if it were alive. I was afraid that the voice would take over. I was afraid that this night would be my last. That this night would be the beginning for it, would it live on as I faded away… Your weak, your nothing, only together can we be strong, only together can we become somebody. Only as one united can we stand tall. Yes you need to release us… set us free. I had lost sight of the old man. I tried to focus in on him but he was gone. And I felt as though so was my mind. I was talking to a dead man. The voice inside my head grows stronger with each passing second. I knew that after tonight that I would never be the same again. I had lost all sense of who I was long ago. Since then the voice has lead me from time to time. Between those visits from the voice; I did just as though I thought any normal person would. Me stalking Cal Seaver, it was the voice. Me killing the old man, it was the voice. What had happened to Emily so long ago, it was the voice. I had all of this figured out. It was all his fault, not mine. We couldn’t be the same. Him and me. The voice and myself. We were different, not the same. I had it all figured out. That was until the old man. He came and he talked. What he said didn’t really make sense. It didn’t even seem to register anything. But now things seem different. Things I blamed on the voice, they seem different. Me, I seem different. The voice in my head, it seems different. It all began to come together. The voice wasn’t in my head. The voice wasn’t some other being. I wasn’t haunted or plagued by the voice. I recall seeing the voice alive standing before me. His hands soaked in blood. I recall looking down and seeing my own hands soaked in blood. That’s when I realized something I should have so long ago, I AM THE VOICE. I AM THE RIPPER…AND I AM FREE… I stood, no longer wanting to eat, walked towards the entrance of the diner. Pushing back the glass door I walked out into the world. The brisk night air caressed me. It filled my lungs and gave me life. The sky was clear as if signifying my mind and its new state of clearness. The voice in my head was no longer there. I had fought and defeated the madness within… Only one kind of madness remained… March Madness…
