This Boat Is Totally Sub-Par

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #2 PPV Our scene opens up on the large boat that is known as the Wrestle Vessel. It’s the ship that will take PCW superstars to Montego Bay for the July 28th Pay Per View that may decide the future of PCW. Of course that’s not what we’re here about today. Today, we’re just here to relax and… “What in the name of Bon Dieu are all of these disgusting people doing here?!” That, of course, would be the lovely voice of Rush Mason, as it comes loudly and obnoxiously while the scene finally opens up to show Rush and Jordan X Shabazz standing on the deck. Rush, of course, is wearing a festive Hawaiian shirt and baggy khaki shorts while Jordan is wearing his regular suit, looking as though he’s boiling inside of it. “No, but seriously, all of you have to leave right now.” We can see that Rush is standing on one of the main decks, where hundreds of people are either tanning, refreshing themselves with a drink, or just enjoying the beautiful ocean scenery. Rush is trying to address all the people, but obviously, his plan isn’t working too well. Rush: Jordan, what in the hell are these people doing on my boat?! A beautiful black woman who is lying, sunbathing on her back, is mesmerizing Jordan. His eyes are locked on her body as everyone can hear Rush’s frantic ranting, but everyone also ignores them. Jordan turns to Rush, barely paying attention, in response to him. Jordan: Wha…What’d you say Rush? I was laying my eyes upon a pretty piece of flesh, indeed. Rush rolls his eyes and grabs Jordan’s hand, and pulls him up from the chair, startling Jordan and bringing a groan from Jordan, along with some resistance towards Rush’s efforts to pull him away. Rush: Let’s go, Jordan. We need to find some place that isn’t full of these commonfolk hicks. Jordan throws his hands up, not understanding at all what Rush’s problem is, but reluctantly following behind and watching on as Rush walks up the stairs to the next deck. Rush sees a whole cluster of empty lounging sun-chairs and decides to lie down in one, as Jordan sits in the one next to him. Rush: Now this, this is what my boat is all about. Jordan rolls his eyes, this time, at Rush’s very boastful claims about the boat. Lying back for one moment, Rush seems almost entirely relaxed until…. AIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!! This of course, brings Rush to his feet, while Jordan just looks on, not even pretending to care about the noise, just wanting to lie down. Rush: Oh my god…they’re…they’re…. Rush’s face contorts in horror as he sees why all of the chairs are empty over here: there is a baby pool less than fifty yards away. Children frolic everywhere in the shallow pool, splashing and causing as much noise as possible without a care in the world. Rush: Midget Commonfolk Hicks!!! Oh Bon Dieu, this isn’t fair!! Why do you punish me with the presence of these sub-par beings?! Jordan, almost fed up, and not for the first time, with Rush’s antics, sits up and sees the attraction, which is the source of Rush’s lunatic raving. He looks over to Rush, who is making frantic motions up towards, the sky and addresses his friend in a calm voice. Jordan: Rush, they’re called children. You should try to- Actually on second thought, you shouldn’t try to look into having one. Rush turns to Jordan, eyes wide and a frustrated look on his face. The Voodoo Villain puts his hand on his chin, looks back towards the children, and then again at Jordan as he speaks to the man. Rush: Jordan, don’t you see?! These are sub-par children!! Jordan lowers his head, sighing and groaning, which as you will see, is not an infrequent act in this promo, or whenever Jordan is around Rush. Jordan pulls off is suit jacket and looks at Rush, who somehow still has a perfectly straight face. Jordan: Yeah, they’re annoying, that’s how kids are. Now can you please get over it so that I can get some rest? As Jordan goes back to take off his jacket and lie down, Rush gives him a look as if Jordan were some sort of deranged psychopath. Rush: Jordan, maybe you don’t have the Bon Dieu given gift to see this, but I can see their disgusting second rate skin and their ugly little bathing suits. They’re all second-rate! They’ll be second rate for all of their life! We can not spend time in the presence of these buffoons! Jordan looks at Rush with a look of utter disbelief. He puts his jacket underneath his head as he closes his eyes, and mutters to Rush. Jordan: Rush, you do whatever you want. I’m taking a nap. If you want to find T roC, he’s up on the top deck tanning. Rush shakes his head at Jordan as the Voodoo Villain walks off, looking for a place that is not absolutely reeking of inferior beings… “Fine, but when I find the hot white girls, I am not coming to get you...'
