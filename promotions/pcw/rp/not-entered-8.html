Chapter 1: The Return BeginsDay 1: Back on track (It has been only overnight since BladeD finally got a release to begin wrestling again after a major accident he had in a “mini” war… These wars are called “BattlePowers” and they are used to simulate a war in real time… These “BattlePowers” aren’t as near as dangerous as real wars, but they can put people in severe pain and damage.. I am a prime example… except I was setup. I was put in the situation and they knew I was going to get hurt.. Yet I don’t have the proof to prove it.. But I have my theories that lead to the true… Only I will have to speak of them other day… Anyway, back on topic.. These “BattlePowers” show you everything that can and most likely will happen in any type of war.. The only problem is that it wasn’t fully tested at the time.. I knew that of course, so I knew what I was getting myself into…for the most part… Like I said before I will get into this on a different day..) Saber: Well, I never thought I would see this day… You are back… It is amazing… BladeD: One moment… ::BladeD picks the telephone:: Yes General.. I understand fully. You are just going to have to understand…. :: The General interrupts BladeD:: General: Look! I know what happened in the past, but the fact of the matter is this, you are back and you need to start taking on duties again like usual. BladeD: I refuse to start talking orders until I get this solved and proven… You may not have been apart of this tragedy, but the fact of the matter GENERAL, is that I was hurt on purpose... That was no accident six months ago I will promise you that… You should know better than me general. General: BladeD… To be honest with you, I did except better of you that day six months ago. I just figured that you finally made a mistake in your life. You have been flawless for so long I figured it was finally your mistake in your career. BladeD: Yea. I was big enough to be my first mistake… That is why it is hard to prove that my injury wasn’t an accident, but it is just that I don’t make mistakes like that. I have learned too much and done too much to make a mistake on my own that I made out there in BattlePowers. General: Well BladeD, I don’t know what much to tell you. I can’t really help you. It would make me look bad if I tried helping you and the last thing I would want to do is make myself look bad… You know what I mean? BladeD: I understand fully. Well, I am out for now. General: BladeD… Out! ::Click:: (BladeD hangs up the phone and sits back in the couch and looks over to Saber, then grabs the remote control and turns on the big screen television… OSW programming is on and BladeD is watching Corg being attacked by Ivan.) BladeD: If I had my chance that wouldn’t be Ivan saving Tempest in the ring, even though those two go back a long time… Like the start of the year… ::laughs:: Saber: Maybe. BladeD: Shut up dude! ::lol:: Before I have to hurt you or something. Saber: …… BladeD: Anyway. Corg is lucky that I am not in OSW. Saber: ::cough:: ::cough:: …. Ok. Anyway, didn’t Corg call you on the phone last night. BladeD: Yea he did actually. He was talking to me about this Cruiserweight title match he is having right now… Saber: Ahhh, so I bet you know who wins then… Dang you. :-\n BladeD: ::takes a sip of his drink:: Well, ::laughs:: Actually I don’t.. ::laughs again:: .. ::lays down on the couch:: To be honest with you, Corg wouldn’t tell me if he lost or not… I tried talking him into it, but oh well. Saber: Good… Because I hate it when you know something and I don’t.. BladeD: ::Lol:: And why is that? Saber: Because you won’t ever tell me and it makes me really mad. BladeD:: ::BladeD rolls over and laughs really loud, but tries to be quiet about it:: Anyway, the match is over, so I am going to go ahead and turn off the T.V. There is no reason to watch the rest of the event. Saber: Well, BattlePowers is going to be tough for me you know… Just to change the subject. BladeD: You are not going through BattlePowers… Don’t worry about it.Saber: WTF? Why not? BladeD: Are you crazy? If I let you go into BattlePowers those people would try to destroy you just like they tried to destroy me. Saber: You don’t know that for sure… BladeD: Don’t be an idiot Saber… You are my brother for crying out loud… If they wanted to take me out there for sure want to take you out… And if they tried to do anything near what they did to me they would kill you Saber…. I am very serious… They would end up killing you because you don’t have near the experience in your life right now to be able to dodge anything what I was put through… I didn’t even fully dodge it and I was the most valuable man of the year in American for four straight years. You aren’t even close to an award like that yet in your life, so there is no chance I am letting you go through BattlePowers… Besides you have enough experience in your life right now to the point where you don’t even need to go through BattlePowers and when the time comes I will make sure you are trained properly for war… The good ol’ fashion way that I was taught… This BattlePowers is just an advance for all the pus** that can’t learn how to win a war the real way… I am sorry, but to be honest with you I think because of this BattlePowers crap I am going to lose a lot of respect for upcoming war mates. Saber: Well, if that is way you feel at it then all right, but I really want to go through it… It seems fun. BladeD: Yea, and I bet it will seem fun once you get hurt too. Saber: Forget you! BladeD: Wow! ::laughs:: Saber getting mad… How rare… Saber: ::laughing and speaking at the same time:: Shut…up!! BladeD: ::hahaha:: That sounded so stupid… You trying to talk and laugh at the same time… ::hahaha:: Saber: I hate you!! BladeD: Kk.. Anyway, like I said… When the time comes you will be ready and I will make sure you are ready. (The scene cuts there out of nowhere…. BladeD seems to be back on track, but let’s hope he gets “on track” about “BattlePowers,” so we can find out more about it and what exactly happen to BladeD just six months ago)
