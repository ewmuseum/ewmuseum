The plan of attack

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-11-02 Bob has some catching up to do with everyone in his life. The way Tony rushed the conversation on the phone and called a meeting immediately makes it obvious it's been a big week. Bob opens up the navy blue wooden door by the door handle and walks into a large room with various pieces of furniture. Tony sits at the table with a concerned expression on his face as Bob enters. The room has a large table with many chairs neatly tucked in under the tabletop. There is a bar fridge in the corner with a television resting on top of it. The rest of the room is filled with various storage furnishings such as a bookcase and a wardrobe. 'Come in.... We have a lot to talk about.' Tony doesn't even greet Bob which is quite unusual something serious must have happened. Bob pulls a chair out from under the table and sits down without saying a word. 'The blacks are still coming in although they aint coming in as fast.' Bob still doesn't say anything he knows there's more to come. 'We have lost a lot of men.... It seems they can't be matched strength Vs strength.... We're ganna have to go from brute to brawn.' Tony's eyes don't leave Bobs as he prepares to continue. 'We're in a bad state Bob.... I don't know if we can get out of it alive.... We might have to run and find somewhere else to start up.' Bob looks shocked by the comments Tony has just made. Tony doesn't say something like this easily things would have to be very bad for him to say something so important. 'If one of our men walks down a street and there's a nigger fuck there they'll kill him.... They don't even care about the cops.... Seems they all look the same so the cops can't tell who did what.... I dunno how they get away with it.' Tony looks down to the ground before looking back up to Bob. 'We will stay for now but we have to find a way to take them out.' 'It doesn't matter to them.... They have five times the number of men we have.' Bob swallows deeply as he can't believe what he is hearing. 'There must be a way we can get rid of them.... How did they get so many so fast anyway?' 'Seems every black man is in a gang and that's the only gang in Amber Hill so they all join.' Bob can't believe what he is hearing. How much things have changed since the good old days where only the elite where recruited. 'We will have to take them all out then.... Get some men together and tell them to scout around and when their men leave their houses I want our guys to raid them and kill every woman and child inside.' Tony's eyes widen as he looks up at Bob he can't believe what Bob is saying. What Bob is telling him to do is inhumane and heartless. 'Don't you think that's a little overboard?' 'They are only black anyway.... What use do they have?' We can now see Bob's evil side. Enough emotions, enough love, enough crying it's time for Bob Vurdo the man to step up to the plate. Bob Vurdo the man however is a cruel heartless man with a sadistic view on life. He views everything as an object to him and only people who get close to him are seen in a different way. 'All right.' Tony isn't going to go against the words of Bob intact he is quite pleased to see Bob Vurdo back as the sick sadistic fuck that he is. 'Now we have to find a way to get rid of the real problems permanently.... I'm talking about the actual gang members.' Tony smiles as a thought comes to his head. 'We will infiltrate their ranks.... We have to use the black men's stupidity to our advantage.... If we can get a few men into the gang then we will be set as we will know their plans.... We can them hit them with force from the inside and the out.' Bob knows this is a do or die situation and he likes the idea Tony has. It just might work, but how will we get some men into their gang when they're black. Tony thinks for a second about the execution of the plan. 'We need to find the right men.... They can't look Italian at all and they must be trustworthy.... Unfortunately I can't think of anyone so we're back to sq...' Bob knows just the man for the job. 'I know someone perfect for the job.' ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob walks through the corridor toward Grimgor's room at the back end of his mansion. He opens the door up without knocking and barges into the room where Grimgor is sleeping. Bob walks up beside the bed of his new wrestling manager and grabs his shoulder. Bob shakes Grimgor violently as the little dwarf wakes up and wipes his eyes. He turns over in his bed onto his back and looks up at Bob squinting due to just being asleep. 'What do you want eh?' Grimgor doesn't seem to happy with Bob waking him up. 'I have a job for you.' 'Go on.' 'I want you to join the black gang in Amber Hill for me.' Grimgor looks confused by what Bob is saying but Bob continues. 'You will still be working for me only you will be infiltrating their gang.' Grimgor begins to understand now. 'Ah... I know that trick. Yeah when I was doing battle against the greenskins in LSD land we used a similar tactic.... Although it didn't work to well as we didn't have the same colored skin.' Grimgor then realizes what he has just said in comparison to what Bob has asked him to do. 'What's a greenskin?' 'It's an orc or a goblin.' Grimgor then begins calculating the characteristics of an orc compared to a black man. He realizes how many similarities they have. They hunt in packs, they are stupid, they have colored skin and they smell really bad. 'I'll do it.... I have good experience with these folk.' Bob nods his head and smiles down at his wrestling manager. 'Now you have to get rid of the beard.' 'No.... Never..... Me beards me pride and joy.' Grimgor shakes his head and closes his eyes in a stubborn manner. 'Fine.... Then you can pack your bags as you are moving into your own home.' Bob gets up from the bed and begins walking off knowing perfectly what will happen next. 'All right.... You're an evil man Bob Vurdo.... An evil man.'
