Date: Sunday Night...after the PPV. “Cher Dieu ont svp la pitié sur leurs âmes… Dear God…please have mercy on their souls…. They know nothing of what they are about to do, because they are innocents when measured against the vast experience that he possesses. Like babes fresh from the womb…they eagerly reach for the milk so that they can be nurtured. But instead, they shall not break free from the maternal innocence that they have compared to him. No…their lives shall be aborted now, so that they can be spared of the immeasurable pain that he shall deliver upon them. I’m not a praying woman, Seigneur … But desperate times call for desperate measures…and it’s desperation that shall fuel the feeble efforts against monsters. My friends have always said that I was too nice for my own good…that my heart is set too gingerly upon my sleeve. Well, there’s nothing I can do about it, and all I can do now is hope and pray for the well being of him, as well as those that he harms. He does so much harm…so much harm… Sometimes I find myself weeping at night within my lonely bed, frightened by dreams of his carnage, other times sobbing because he is not there alongside me. The nights are the hardest time to deal through, as it’s when one can sit in the depths of darkness and ponder the complexities of life. But I cannot muster a reason for why he continues to effortlessly slaughter the innocent. There’s nothing I can do for the innocent that fall victim to his hands…nothing except pray that they find grace, mercy, and a speedy recovery from their many injuries. So many tears I have shed for those many slain in battle… So many nights have I wished for him to be by my side, instead of out God knows where harming people that have no right to be harmed. There’s so many of them…so many…many… My head throbbed sometimes, maybe my own punishment for what my dear has done to the weak and pathetic. A shuffling lapse of pain, as if each and every life was shooting through my head as they were made to suffer. It is my own punishment, for I deserve more than a small amount of pain for pledging my devotion and love for him. If it is sin, then I am hell bound. But I will be with my love while burning…we shall both bathe in eternal torment embracing each other in torrid and antagonizing bodies, hearts ripped from our chests so that worms may have a home. But it all can happen…so long as I have him. Must I be melodramatic so much? My friends, my close good friends, they would have me institutionalized for such behavior. They care so much for me, and they look out for me in my weakened condition. And I look out for them in return, by praying for their well being. I care so much for them, as for the well being of everyone else. I don’t want to see anyone be hurt…I feel responsible for the souls that he ravages. Why, o why…do they persist in going to their limits against him? It’s proven time after time that doing so only leads to destruction by his hands… Sometimes I ask myself why I love him…why I dedicate my life to him. Then I remember what he used to be…before he became the monster that he is now. I want to bring that man I fell in love with back…and by doing so; maybe I can make the world a better place for everyone. By ridding them of a beast…a beast that no one can stop. He won’t stop, not until he finishes what he set out to accomplish. All of these men coming against him, they’re nice men. They have no qualms with him, all they want is to make a chance for them, make a chance for their own lives to be something. Is that so much a sin that God finds to smite them for their sacrilege? Man can be good… they can… But that is why it happens, isn’t it? Because they CAN be good, and they CAN do good, but all in all, what happens is that they end up doing what’s wrong…their efforts are wrong, their motives are wrong, and their ideals of being on top are wrong. How it makes me weep each time I see a poor soul out there, only wishing to achieve their dreams, and in the end, become a victim of a nightmare. But…there is nothing I can do actively…I am merely one woman…one god-fearing woman who takes pity upon they who foolishly throw their lives away. I’ve seen them come and go since he left me…and each of them I have said a prayer for, so that they are not cursed by his presence for the rest of their lives. I send my prayers with my love…” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- A dark room…bedroom…showered lightly with moonlight from the open balcony…everything set in a grayish hue from the light of dusk… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “I remember how he used to be…he was never this behemoth of a man that strikes fear into the hearts of men. He never used to be such an ogre. I remember the days when he used to be normal…as normal as he could be, back in those carefree days of our youth. We used to do all sorts of things together, hand in hand. Those were lovely days…which turned soon to horrible nights. He disappeared…and I never saw him again. I prayed for his return, but never did he come back. That brought nothing but sorrow to my soul…and now I stand before you, God…a vessel of despair and sadness. But I have compassion, where he does not. I have grace and mercy where he offers up cruelty and insane brutality. I offer love where he harbors hatred. I ask you dear Lord to make me the vessel for his redemption. If that is the only way that he can be saved from his own crimes, then I am willing to be a sacrifice for the one I love.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Sheer curtains flutter in the night breeze…a hazy form outlined behind them on the balcony. Clear drops of liquid splatter a trail towards the adjacent door. Drop…by drop…by drop… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Searching for him was hard…a never ending task at some points. But determination is the muse of love. When I found him in this place called PCW, I knew it was him, despite his outwardly and inwardly changes. The next step would be seeing to getting him back to me. I don’t think you can ever feel the desperation of losing a love, and still fighting the inner depths and demons that strive to break one from the inside. It takes a person of pure strength and power to do such a thing…instead of becoming a shell of what they formerly was. It’s always easy for a person to drop all their ideals and means to become jaded and bitter, hating the world because of their loss. But I…I keep going, because I know that there are more men that are being hurt everyday because I lost him. Keeping them in my prayers is my form of repentance. I’m so sorry for them all, Lord. I hope they can accept my apologies…I truly mean them. I know he’s not sorry for what he’s done…that would require a conscience. I am sad to see and say that he no longer has that. It died long ago…along with his promising love to me. Souls are needed so that one may have remorse for their actions… I had believed that his soul was given to me, along with his heart…but I see it’s now swallowed by darkness.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Winds push back the white fabric…and standing there is a woman. Her white lace nightgown was tugged and toyed by the wisps of wind, like a child pulling at the skirt of a mother he adored. She stood on bare feet and stared up at the moon, her hands clasped together at her bosom as if she were praying. And shimmering locks of chocolate cascaded around her shoulders, crimped and waved like the surf of the Caribbean. Her face was shielded by the hair, but her sniffling could be heard softly over the blowing winds. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “All in all…my journey to seek penitence for him will not end until I obtain it. …There are three that I have to pray for now…they’ve jumped into his way. All of them with the same ambitions, but they have no real idea that they step in the path of a madman. Its children putting their hands to the stove…burning is imminent. I don’t want any more people to be hurt by him…the more he harms the more his soul becomes lost in that abyss. I pray he doesn’t hurt them too much…I pray that he only stuns them, instead of crippling them like so many else. Please God… …I don’t know what else to do… Please…help me help him… Dieu soit avec le mon fiancé. Amen.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The woman’s face becomes visible, her glazed eyes staring upwards, as her beautiful, yet timid looking face stained with streaks of tears. The mere image of her face strikes chords of disturbance, a sudden view of her face bringing about an outburst from the plains of the psyche: “SAULE!” Akiyama snapped his eyes open in the dark room. Silence filled the air, but he still heard that word echo through his ears and into his mind. He stood up from his kneeling position against the corner of the hotel room and walked towards the bedroom. Opening the door, he peered into the darkness and found no one within the room. The balcony curtains fluttered…a breeze blowing through the open doors. He stepped forward, and the god’s eyes trailed down, zeroing in on the trailing drops of fluid upon the carpet. He moved on, stepping onto the balcony. It was there that he found her…crouching on the railing of the balcony with her back facing the 3 story drop onto the sandy dunes of the beach. Her hair was wild, limp over her face and shadowing everything but her lips and chin. The nVg glared into her closer…and found tears streaked on her face… …Tears of blood… “Saints are recognized as those eminent for piety or virtue.” She muttered her voice bleak and drained. “They hold their beliefs to their hearts and will go on believing in them no matter what. Even if their beliefs are sheer stupidity…” She turned towards the god, who had folded his arms to lean his back against the railing next to her. He lowered his head, closing his eyes in a calm fashion, though his brow still wrinkled in a stern look. Her eyes looked dead themselves, normally her pure black eyes hold more life then they did now. “You heard the name that rang out in my mind, Akiyama. It was French…for Willow.” A growl rumbled from his chest, as his brow grew more wrinkled. She slowly looked down to his arms, at this point merely staring off into space rather than focusing. “I saw that woman. She still believes those lies she told herself all those years ago. She still has hopes that you will belong to her again. Beliefs…become weakness… because they are nothing but dreams and wishes when they are impossible to achieve. There’s a huge difference between knowing and believing. We know that you will murder three men in the ring on the next show. They believe they have a chance of winning. We know that they have no chance of winning. They believe that you can be stopped. We KNOW you cannot be stopped. The beliefs are silly notions…while the known facts are as real as the threat of anthrax.” A fresh streak of tears came down the sides of her face. Only two, one from each eye…and her face still remained frozen in its unemotional dead status. “She prays for the men you will maim…for you as well. As if it will make a difference in the long run. This prophecy has touched a nerve with me…there’s something about this woman that bothers me. I’ve thought for a moment…and decided that maybe it’s time that you’ve severed your ties to humanity for good. There is no way you can rise above the ashes of humanity if you still exist within them. Yes…purge your past, so that you may preserve your future.” The red drops patter upon the ground, the only sound amidst the silence between the god and the Divine Prophetess. “The Willow Marquette shall find us soon…and when that happens, you can rid yourself of one more link to your past…to your humanity. Then after your humanity if destroyed, you move onto the world…Dit ainsi la Prophétesse Divine.” Akiyama flipped his eyes open at the display of French by Cyclonica, giving her an angered look. She shook her head, a sigh rising up from her. “I mean…so says the Divine Prophetess…par le Vide…” -=-=-=- FIN -=-=-=-
