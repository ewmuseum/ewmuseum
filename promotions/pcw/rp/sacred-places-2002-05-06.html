Sacred Places

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-6-02 OOC Note: My apologies for not having a better RP output this past week. [detail withheld] and I’ve just had my mind on many different things lately, so again I apologize. Thanks, Dustin. Far away from all the insecurities Locked away from ever passing eyes Somewhere between the void of the mind and our home, the earth There is a place I go there whenever I need to Just a stone’s throw away My peace is my home Please come inside… *He was already beginning to crack from the pressures of PCW re-stardom. The life of such an entertainer was quite demanding, and Jeremy needed to regain at some of his peace of mind, even if it only meant gaining a very small portion. There was indeed a place, a sacred one at that he found long ago. No one ventures inside. No one ever interrupts his social slumber. It was as if nature had carved the perfect little slice of heaven to sift up to, to clear his mind. It was a beautiful place indeed. Obscured by trees in a nearby park, the grass grew beautifully to majestic heights, the frogs jumped busily around without a care in the world, at least their own, and a small pond sat in the middle of it all where Stryfe would sit at the edge of and let his head drift into the ripples below.* *It’s been a half of a year since he visited his hiding spot. Never had he a chance to before. He would fly all the way home, off the road just to see this tiny patch of land.* *Looking in the water, he didn’t see himself looking back. He never did. Always a new person would look back at him from below, much reminiscent of a newborn child looking up at his father, his creator. But what did he see below, a beaten old man? Yes. Perhaps this was a sign of the near future considering the fact he did not have his mind set on the match ahead of him. No. This was time for himself, his “me” time.* *The reflection suddenly changed. No longer was Jeremy staring up but the PCW newcomer, Streak. It was quite a sight to say the least. It soon became that and more. The water began to rise up before, and soon the it took shape as the man himself.* SPLASH! *This was like no ordinary splash. It felt like the weight of the world covering him; like knives sticking into every single inch of his body. The pain was too much. It showed as he passed out.* 1………2………3! Streak is victorious! *He walked out of the arena with his head held low in shame. Why did he succumb to someone like Streak. He knew the answer, but he didn’t want to accept it. He tried to run out before anyone else would see him, but there was no chance. Laughter and jeers flooded his ears as he tried to cover up his face. There was no escaping the humanity that rejected him.* Wake up! *And that he did… He was still soaking wet, and probably bruised and bloodied all over. He crawled over back to the pond to see the damage done to him… … Again he saw a different man looking back.* “Brother?” *His own flesh and blood from years past was now staring back at him. Even to this day he can barely cop with his loss from so many years past. A vision spread out from the water in front of him.* February 27, 1990: 12:18 P.M. *So far it was simply another average day at the Connors residence. Jeremy’s parents were preparing lunch for the boys at the time. Jeremy was downstairs watching his television at the time when his brother Adam walked into the room. As usual, Adam had no desire to watch what his little brother was and an argument broke out.* “I don’t wanna watch this crap!” Adam exclaimed to him. Jeremy answered back, “Well too bad! I got here first!” He yelled back even louder, “Give me the remote you little fuck!” *Adam began grabbing for the remote, which his brother tightly clasped. The young Stryfe smacked his hand away and all hell broke loose. Kicking, punching and choking their way through the living room they fought over an insignificant little device. But at their young ages, it meant everything. Soon Jeremy began ramming his brother’s head into the floor below grimaced in pain. This was nothing new. They were always fighting. He got his remote back at the price of Adam’s blood. He didn’t care though. He had to do whatever it took to defend himself.* “That’s enough of this!” Shouted their father as he pulled his son away from Adam lying on the floor. “That’s what you get you son of a bitch.” He whispered to him. ……… “That was the last thing I ever said to him. How would I have known I would lose him soon after? If only I could change that horrible mistake.” *The vision before his eyes disappeared as all he saw was the blackened water rippling once again. Only closing his eyes for a second, he blinked again to see someone else looking back in the water again. Not exactly the man he had expected, but more of… a goblin. Once again, the water rose up and took form of his adversary, Syko. Stryfe still outsized him of course, yet it grew bigger… … and bigger… … and bigger… …And soon it towered all the way up to the sky above him. The strange being let out a huge laugh as his giant fist slammed into the ground below. Stryfe moved out of the way just in the nick of time. He rolled back out of the area, back into the civilization of the park. To find himself making quite a scene running out of nowhere in front of all the people passing by.* *But there was rumbling, shaking or laughing to be heard. He slowly walked back into the long grass, which grew to heights taller than himself and he saw nothing. The reflection of the gobbling had disappeared from sight. From his mind.* He suddenly remembered as his conscious mind slammed back into his head, “I have a match to get to!” *It was time to face another challenge lying ahead of him, and now was his chance to prove his future wrong. He could still overcome. Time was not up yet.* I must leave this place and venture back into reality. I have the power to control my destiny. No one will stand in my way. But I shall return….
