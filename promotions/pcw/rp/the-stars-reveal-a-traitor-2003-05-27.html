The Stars Reveal A Traitor...

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 27 - 03 Twelve haunting insignias burn of solar fire, standing upon poles of steel and resting upon cradles of iron. Like great sentinels, twelve men stand before their respective emblem, the fires of the great pit before them dancing across their faces like shadows in the night. Each man stares into the ever-raging madness of the chaotic flames with cold emotion, never flinching from the stone-melting heat of the fire. The surrounding evergreens are basked in a bright orange aura that extends to the skies above, swirling about like a maelstrom of fire orange and coal black. Stones of ghostly white make up the border of the pit, extending a foot or so vertically to protect bystanders from the monstrous inferno’s consuming tongues of raging hell. The twelve men never budge, however. Their faces are free of fear, panic, or surprise. They look upon the fire pit as if it was their home, or their family. They look up the tongues of fire as if those very tongues dwell within their mouths. Their disturbing calm is broken not by fire nor by the hovering maelstrom… but by a voice. “Fellow Children of the Stars…” A man draped in strange obsidian-colored armor from head to toe steps forth onto the edge of the cliff towering a good ten feet above the fire pit and folds his arms with ease across his black torso. The fires dance across his armor and light him like a giant candle, as if he was born of the flames himself. “I have called you here together to make you aware of the dark days ahead for our denied faith,” His voice booms of power and devotion, but his crystal blue eyes read turmoil and sadness. “THEY are in uprising against us…” Heads turn to each other in confused murmur, and then move in unison to the visage of the armored figure. His jet-black hair swings despite the lack of wind and his fire-enlightened lips curl stiffly into a frown. He looks at each of the emblem bearers with a stern eye and nods. “The Star Axis…” The man of the first symbol, a blood red studded star of 9 points, steps forward and thrusts his fist out towards the armored man in a salute of sorts. “Born of malevolence and bred of lunar nobility, the Star Axis greets you, Lunis Superia.” The bearer of the Star Axis then withdraws to his original spot and clasps his hands behind his back, never taking his eyes from the man standing atop the cliff, who turns to the next symbol. “Brotherhood Of The Machine… step forward…” A ghastly figure wearing an out-dated, bloodstained gas mask steps forth away from his respective symbol, a vertical half circle with a smaller, inverted version of the same thing resting within, and pounds the left side of his torso once in a show of salute. “We know no rival nor ally, and we ride upon automated clouds of destruction. We are the Brotherhood of the Machine, greeting you, Lunis Superia.” He then steps back, pulls what appears to be a mechanical sword hilt from his side, and raises it to the air. All look on as the man’s finger falls upon a red BotM symbol on the hilt and causes a sparkling blade of titanium to burst forth from the slit on top of the hilt. Like a bolt of lightning, the blade shoots forth and stops at a two-foot extension, glittering in the light of the fire. The man replaces the sword in his belt and folds his arms across his chest, “The Elder wishes to know what the trouble is, Lunis Superia, as do the other cult Elders, I’m sure.” Another murmur begins to emit from the other eleven cult representative, but the outstretched arm of the Lunis Superia silences them. “Sistine, my brothers. I know you are all concerned, and I am aware of the Elders’ feelings. The stars are misaligned and read of trouble, as I’m sure most of you have realized by now…” “But its meaning puzzles us,” cries the representative of the seventh symbol, a cross that has been divided vertically. “The Numentian Elder has fallen into a deep state of subliminial concentration, perplexed by this strange occurrence. The rest of the council is concerned and my clan brothers have begun morbid speculation. Relief my worry, Lunis Superia!” The other representatives call for the same in mob unison, also wishing for an answer to their worry. The Lunis Superia raises his hands to silence them once again and nods, “Worry not, for we have nothing to truly fear. The Tiamats watch us with a parental eye, and wish us no harm. If the star alignment worried them, I’m sure the Elders would have been contacted. I believe the alignment to be-“ “Have you all considered that it could be a natural occurrence that we have not observed before?” The BotM representative chimes in with a snide tone to his muffled voice. “I have,” states the Lunis Superia with a smug grin across his clean-shaven face. “But that seems unlikely, as recent times hint at a dark omen approaching. Troubles have arisen from within the factions… dare I say… there’s a traitor among us.” The twelve men fall into a quiet uproar of accusations and speculation, nearly interrogating each other as to who the traitor might be. The Lunis Superia looks on through his black shades, staring unto the world with a shadowy gaze. He turns his gaze to the swirling maelstrom above, taking in its silent madness, before cracking an angry grin and looking back to the quarreling faction representatives, who cry out for the head of the traitorous scum. The leader of the Burning Stone faction cries the loudest, with the fanatical madness of a bloodthirsty zealot, “FIND HIM! KILL HIM! DESTROY HIM!” “Sic, that is the plan! I believe this star alignment to be a WARNING that will point the traitor out!” The Lunis Superia looks up and points to the sky, revealing the series of five bright stars that seem to be in aligned in a strange, arrow-type pattern. Panicked murmurs arise from the twelve men, but the Lunis Superia silences them once more. “But fear not, for the Tiamats have not left us without means to decipher this strange astrological code. OBSERVE!” Quick as a flash, the Lunis Superia reaches into the inner folder of his jacket and pulls out what appears to be a large magnifying glass. A chuckle arises from somewhere within the coalition of clan reps and causes the Lunis Superia to scour the half-circle with a puzzled eye. “Is someone amused?” The figurehead for the Brotherhood of the Machine steps forward defiantly and folds his arms across his chest, “The gods would be ashamed if they saw one of their top acolytes using such a simple tool. The Brotherhood can provide much better, if you wish.” However, the Lunis Superia shoots a VERY nasty sneer at the representative, causing him to step back cautiously. “We shall use what the TIAMATS have supplied us with. Now everyone, step to the pit of solar fire and look to the night sky.” In almost pseudo-machine unison, the twelve men take a step forward and stand before the blazing hole of fire, resisting its stone-melting heat. They all turn their heads to the sky and gaze at the maelstrom and stars above with solemn looks on their faces. The Lunis Superia clutches the large circular glass with both hands and twists it around, staring out at the anxious figureheads. “May En’Flarim have mercy on who the stars reveal to the be the traitor…” With that, he holds the glass to the sky like the Statue of Liberty holds up her torch and aligns it with the strange stars. Almost instantly, 5 sharp beams of light breaks through the glass and reflect at an angle. The fiery surface of the stars above cause the beams of light to dance about on the representatives, never settling on one of them but still scaring the shit out of them. The Lunis Superia watches on carefully as the beams of light contort and dance about… before finally settling down on one man… “Huh… WHAT THE?!?! PROPOSTOROUS!!” They have settled on the figurehead of the Brotherhood of the Machine… “I should have known… Washington’s forte is MACHINES, after all!” The Lunis Superia sneers angrily, burning a hole through the panicked clan rep with his eyes. “You know what the punishment for being a traitor is!” “WAIT! YOU HAVE A-“ Almost instantly, the other eleven members pounce upon the ‘traitor’. He struggles and fights like an animal possessed by a demon on speed, but they’re still able to hoist him over their heads with ease . The Lunis Superia walks to the area of the cliff perpendicular with the BotM rep’s body and crouches down to a single knee. “The Tiamats will be disappointed to know a member of the Brotherhood is a traitor… but they’ll be pleased to know he’s dying a traitor’s death, as well. Goodbye…” The rep lets out a blood-curdling screech of horror before feeling his body whipped down into the blazing pit of fire. The other eleven men step back quickly as the pit bursts in an angry spew of fire and madness, consuming the rep’s still screaming body. The other eleven men suddenly droop their heads in a silent prayer, wishing for his body to be consumed slowly. The Lunis Superia rises to his feet and stares down into the flaming chaos with an emotionless look of cold upon his face: “Fucking traitor…” [End.]
