Official Press Conference for Flaymin's House of Torture!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in the media room at PCW Towers. 10 reporters…. Held hostage at gunpoint of course…. Sit in the chairs provided, ready to interview Flaymin. Flaymin steps out from behind the makeshift curtains with Walt Disney IV by his side. They sit down beside each other at the table and look out at the sea of uninterested reporters] Flaymin: It’s nice you could all make it here today! [A collective grumble is heard from the reporters. Walt Disney IV looks at the reporters and waves cheerfully] Walt: I only knew it was a matter of time before Disney World joined forces with Professional Wrestling! Reporter: But won’t this tarnish the reputation of your World Famous Theme Park? Walt: Nah…. I’m insured. Flaymin: Walt believes in me.We both think that ‘Flaymin’s House of Torture’ will be the greatest ever attraction in the Disney Stratosphere! Reporter: What about that giant golf ball? Walt: We’re tearing that down to build a Starbucks. [The reporters all shake their heads in disbelief. Meanwhile, Flaymin reaches into his pocket and pulls out a shrivelled piece of paper] Flaymin: As a special treat for all you people, I’m going to unveil to you the design of my new ride. This is a world first, so you’d better thank your lucky stars that you decided to attend this press conference. [The reporters all edge closer towards Flaymin in anticipation. Flaymin holds up the crumpled piece of paper for all to see. On the paper is a bad, hand drawn picture of the ride. Flaymin smiles proudly as the reporters gasp in awe] Reporter: This is a prank, isn’t it? Walt: No, actually this is the official and only blueprint for the ride. Reporter: But if the blueprint is here with you as we speak, then what are builders back at the site referring to as they construct the ride? Flaymin: They’ve been trained with telepathic abilities. We sat them down and forced them to watch a ‘7th Heaven 12 Hour Marathon’. After only five minutes they realised the next eleven hours and fifty-five minutes were going to be hell.At that point we realised they could see into the future. Reporter: But EVERYONE knows that 7th Heaven sucks. Walt: I thought the show was quite charming. [Flaymin looks at Walt and raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: You have to excuse Walt, he’s been suffering from Alzheimer’s Disease ever since Euro-Disney crashed and burned. [One of the reporter’s points towards the top of the blueprint in Flaymin’s hand] Reporter: What’s that spiky thing in the top corner? Flaymin: That’s the…. Sun. Reporter: What about those flat things at the bottom? Flaymin: They’re the dead people. Walt: We here at Disney World proudly support Euthanasia!! If you’re old, smelly, look like a monkey’s ass and can’t tell the difference between Rosie O’Donnell and Rose McGowan then you deserve to die. Flaymin: And why not die in style at Flaymin’s House of Torture!! Walt: We really expect a whole lot of geriatrics to show up for the ride.It’ll be the biggest event for old folks at Disney World since the Murder She Wrote-Matlock porno a few years back. Flaymin: Speaking of pron~!…. I mean Porn, Walt and I are on the verge of collaborating on another successful venture- Disney Pornoville!!! Reporter: But isn’t Disney World aimed at young children? Flaymin: That’s alright, you’re never to young to get some action. Reporter: Even the 7 and 8 yearolds!?!? Walt: That won’t be a problem.We’ve had specially moulded miniature condoms created just for the kiddies…. And PCW wrestlers with small dongers. Flaymin: Like El Diablo.He’s our best customer. Reporter: With all this controversial content about to commence at Disney World, aren’t you afraid you’ll damage the reputation of the entity? Walt: Are you kidding?People Love Porn.People Love Deadly Rides. At Disney World we have both. Reporter: Any possibility of uniting ‘Flaymin’s House of Torture’ with a porno element? Walt: We haven’t thought that far ahead. Flaymin: Yeah, we spent all week drawing up this blue print. [Once again, Flaymin holds up his dodgy map of the ride for everyone to see] Reporter: Flaymin, moving towards the PCW side of things, are you looking forward to your cage match with El Diablo this Friday? Flaymin: Forget the wrestling cage, I could just shove that moron into a birdcage. Reporter: What have you done with his mask, considering it was such an important part of his life? Flaymin: I forget.Last I saw it, it was in the bathroom and I was using to wipe my ass whenever nature called. Reporter: On Sabotage you meet your old nemesis Tim Walden for fourth time, are you ready to continue your winning streak against him? [Flaymin’s eyes pop wide open and jaw drops instantly] Flaymin: Walden….. again? AAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGHHHHHHHH!!!!!! [Flaymin gets up out of his chair, yelling and screaming.He pushes over the table and runs out of the room shrieking and howling in despair] END~!
