Remembering the old days...

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/1/02 The scene starts off with a birds eye view of Impact’s head, slowly panning to the top of his shoulder… his fellow workers are in his path… Heavenly Voice - The Pay Per View is a most glorious event… it will be the second one for Impact… but yet, no one seems to know who this man is. Impact waves to fellow workers, but all ignore him… Impact – No one knows who I am… Impact slouches his back, depressed that no one really cares for the annoyance… Heavenly Voice - The Pay Per View comes to us this Sunday with him taking on five other men… who are these five other men? Impact brightens up, smiling gleefully; he points a finger up. Impact – I know, I know! Heavenly Voice – Shut up all ready, can’t you see I am trying to tell the freaking story? Impact crosses his arms and turns his head around. He snaps his head upwards and downwards. Impact – Humph! Jeez, sorry… (whispering) schmuck.. Heavenly Voice – His first opponent is… Impact lightens up again pointing his right index finger up. Impact – Tim Walden! Tim Walden! Tim Walden! Heavenly Voice – (Clears throat) Excuse me! Impact shrugs and puts on a innocent face. Impact – Whaaaat…? Heavenly Voice – Let me get on with this so we can tell the audience the freaking story! Impact backs off with his arms raised in the air and a sharp ninety-degree angle. Heavenly Voice – That’s better… well… his first opponent is Tim Walden… Impact steps up again… Impact – And if he wins this Friday, it’ll be a TV Title match! I would looooooveeee that! Heavenly Voice – OH MY GOD! JUST FORGET IT! I AM TRYING TO TELL THE DAMN STORY, AND YOU KEEP ON BUGGING ME! AHHHH! WHY DON’T YOU TELL IT INSTEAD?! A sudden slam and thud is heard… Impact – Oops… Impact shrugs and pulls up a chair and a desk a sits… Impact – Well… since the rude guy isn’t saying anything now, I must now have to tell you all who I am up against... THE Multiple Man, D-Kay Calyx, Big Daveylicious and Afwa Kilmanjaro… Impact flinches and shakes… Impact – I’m scared… Heavenly Voice – HAHAHA! YOU ARE SCARED! HAHAHA! Impact looks a bit angry and raises his eyebrows. Impact – Hey, I’m taking on five other men! Impact puts up his right hand and shows all five fingers. He shakes it around, Booker T style! (He doesn’t stare at it like an idiot however…) Heavenly Voice – Ok, ok shut up all ready… Impact puts his arms on his hips and looks upward. Impact – No you! Heavenly Voice - … Impact rolls his eyes and starts to motion with his mouth, umm… Impact - … Heavenly Voice – If you shut up, I’ll tell the freaking story, ok? Impact’s face brightens up and shakes his head agreeingly. Impact – K! Heavenly Voice - We’ll take this story back a few years back… back… back… WAY back, when Impact, a man who is 27 currently, relives the story of when he was only a meager 15 years old… Impact – HEY THAT AIN’T MEAGER! Heavenly Voice – Ugh… The scene transforms to a classroom with black desks lined up with 8 high school kids sitting at each one. There are 4 sections with two desks per section as 8 people sit there… except for this one kid… Heavenly Voice - There he is, that one kid, standing up while all others are sitting in their seats listening to the teacher… Teacher – Sit down Drew… Heavenly Voice - If you didn’t know, this kid is obviously Impact, but when he was a little tiny 15 year old… Impact – HEY! I WASN’T TINY! It’s true, Impact wasn’t that small… Heavenly Voice – Anyway, when he was not tiny, but 15… Impact – That’s better! Heavenly Voice – … his name was Drew… It still is… but I suggest for you not to call him that… Teacher – Sit down Drew! The teacher folds her arms and points to Drew and puts the finger towards the chair. Drew immediately follows up by sitting in the seat… Teacher – Ok… start the experiment! The desks are decorated with microscopes and in the middle lies a tray with many instruments and items. Some items include a beaker and some bottles with liquid inside… a test tube rack, fit with 8 test tubes, lie inside. On the desk where Impact is sitting are 3 girls… the first girls name is Shazia, the second Grace, and the third Lu Lu. Impact (Drew) – So… what do we have to do? Shazia, a dark faced girl slaps Impact on the back. Shazia – You stupid idiot! Don’t you freaking ever listen to the teacher Drew? Impact shakes his head. Impact – Nope… Shazia shakes her head in disgust, crossing her arms in the process… Shazia – Oh my goodness, you are so annoying… you ALWAYS ask that question when we have lab class! Impact puts his arms up and shrugs. Impact – O well! Shazia points to the tray and shakes her finger around it. Shazia – Make yourself useful… read the sheet that Ms. Soldano gave you and do something… Impact reaches for the tray and pulls it over to himself when Shazia grabs his wrist… Shazia – Don’t do anything stupid Drew… Impact shakes his head agreeingly… Impact – Okay… While Impact takes out the beaker and other bottles, Shazia punches Impact in the arm… Shazia – You know for a person with a last name of Driven, you aren’t a very driven person academically… Impact turns around and nearly drops a bottle of some kind of acid but scoops it up just in time… Impact – O yea… that’s why I have a 97 average and you have a 65… Shazia looks at Impact with a scornful look dropping her eyes and glaring at him… Shazia – HEY, SHUT UP! Shazia whacks him once more in the arm. Impact – OUCH! Shazia goes back to her work while Impact continues in whatever she was doing. … Heavenly Voice – Yo Impact, how the heck is it possible for you to have a 97 average when you didn’t pay any attention in class? Impact – O, I did pay attention… but like now, I was still annoying… Heavenly Voice – O ok… … Impact fiddles around with the bottles spinning them on his finger, ever so perfectly, but only to be spoiled when… Ms. Soldano – DREW! PUT THAT DOWN! Impact quickly puts it down… Ms. Soldano isn’t a small woman, in fact, she’s huge… waist line… shoe size… the whole nine and even a half yards! Impact – Hmm… I wonder what this is… Impact uncorks a bottle with a clear liquid and puts his nose into the nozzle sniffing it… Impact – AHH! AHH! IT BURNS! IT BURNS! Grace looks up from her writing she was doing. She looks at Impact grabbing his nose and his eyes watering… Grace – What’s wrong?! Impact relinquishes from holding his nose and smiles… Impact – Nothing… Grace looks at Impact and goes back to writing on her paper… Grace – Oh my god, that kid is so annoying… Impact goes back to the bottle… Impact – Oh my god, that girl is so stupid… Impact fiddles around with more bottles and looks down at the sheet of paper that Ms. Soldano has handed out. Impact – Step one… take bottle labeled A and pour it into test tube 1. Impact looks around in the tray for a bottle labeled A… when he finds it, he quickly pours it into the test tube, but with this quickness comes a price… Impact – Oops, I spilled some… The teacher looks up from her sleeping… … Heavenly Voice – What an incompetent teacher! Impact – No… her sleeping is my fault… Heavenly Voice – My god! How annoying were you back then… Impact – You thought I am annoying now? You are lucky you weren’t in my school! Heavenly Voice – What was your school anyway? Impact – Umm… the Bronx High School of Science I think… I forget, the god damn school doesn’t even have a wrestling team! Heavenly Voice – OH MY GOD! THE BARBARIANS! … Ms. Soldano sees the spilled liquid and gets up from her seat. She points to a rack of paper towels behind Impact. Ms. Soldano – Well, go… Impact – SOMEONE HELP! I SPILLED LIQUID ON THE TABLE! Ms. Soldano entusiastically points towards the paper towel rack in back of him. Ms. Soldano – Drew, just look… Impact – SOMEONE HELP! O DEAR GOD! SOMEONE HELP! Ms. Soldano walks towards Impact and goes for the paper towels… Impact – I’M SORRY GOD! FORGIVE ME! Ms. Soldano looks down at Impact and shakes her head. She follows by rolling a couple of sheets of paper towels and hands it to Impact. Impact – O god! Thank you! These towels you have made have saved my life! Ms. Soldano walks back to her desk nodding her head disagreeingingly as people from other rows start to laugh uncontrollably. Kid – Oh my god… Drew, you going to give Soldano a heart attack! Impact gets up and goes down to his knees and under the desk… Impact – Oh my god! We’re going to be attacked by hearts! AHHH! The kid laughs even harder as the class joins him. Ms. Soldano gets out of her seat and saps her hands on the table. The class quiets down as Impact gets up from under the desk and sits on his seat. Ms. Soldano – That’s better… now WORK! Impact fiddles around with more bottles… he grabs one in his hand and starts to throw it in the air… he catches it… Impact – Hey look! I’m juggling. His surrounding table mates look and shake their heads, to say, what an idiot… Impact – C’mon! See me juggle! Impact turns his head… but it is just enough for the bottle to slip from one of his hands… Impact – NOOOOOOOOOO… Grace - AHH! Shazia - IDIOT! Lu Lu - CATCH IT! CATCH IT! CATCH IT! [TO BE CONTINUED]
