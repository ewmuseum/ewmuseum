No Saga: The Realm of Dreams (Prelude to Nightmares)

30 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-30-01 Come. Step inside. As you can see this isnt even a world, nor is this a place where any emotion can be felt or scene. This is a realm. A realm with spirals and squares, shapes and colors. No substance. No laws. There is no gravity but people can't fly. Unless they can. Then they do. Sometimes they don't want to fly. Then they don't. But the ones that do, will. Its that kind of place. Theres a fence in the beginning, near the marble road. At least I think their is. Some people see it but others claim that its a red brick road with a small black gate in front of it. There really is no way to possibly say what it is. The road, which is either made out of the finest white and black marble road or perfectly lined up brick. It depends on who you ask, and if you can ask them... anyway, this road winds for miles, or a short distance. On the sides of the road all you can see for miles is beautiful forests, the trees have been there for centuries and outgrow even the mighty redwoods... theres some swamps scattered in the father reaches, and possibly a could meadows and farmland with rolling green hills. I think. Thats what I heard at least. Sometimes it can be quite dark, nothing but black, dead trees, looking like everything was scorched over once or twice by some sort of horrible forest fire. I guess its a matter of where you enter maybe. After you walk through the forest, on that winding path thats brick or marble or something, you end up reaching a lake... this lake to its right has a waterfall that cascades about 40 feet in the air, or is it 10... maybe its not a waterfall at all, it could have been the sewage run off... almost like a abandoned radioactive plant. No, right now I think its the lake. Its a oasis after the long walk through the wooden swamp land. The water is perfectly clear, when you look behind you for some reason the forests dissapear, I think you can see them again if you walk over the sand dune, I don't think anyones ever tried, but behind you is nothing but sandy beaches... the kind you remember from when you were a kid, the waves crash into the sand and make the ten or thirty feet in front of you wet with mud. The good mud. Most people spend a while here, and every step they take unleases a new memory, its like a virtual mind landmine. They walk down the beach and let the sand squish between their toes, just like when they were kids. Like you remember right? Eventually they get past the abandoned boardwalk from the beach in their hometown and notice the bridge, suspended by nothing but hovering there over the small or large ocean. The bridge is nothing special, its the view to the left and right... looking behind you you see the beach, but on the sides of you all you see is the crashing water, leaving rainbows with its spray, and the birds... the hundreds of birds flying through the cavern carved out of the mountains. The view is breathtaking... oh, were you afraid of heights? Well you aren't now. Is that a bald eagle? The trek continues, you walk forever, until the sun sets and it becomes night out... did it feel like forever? No... well thats good, it shouldn't have. Forever is just a form of time. It can't be felt around these parts. Its night out though, and you got to witness the greatest sunset of your life, the colors were moving and swirling, you could swear by it but know better. The bridge finally ends and you step off into a grassy area, its almost perfectly flat, but all you can see is those rolling hills in the distance... a patch of this grass leads to them, and on your sides are tree's, every fruit you loved... even that one fruit you had that you could never pronounce the name of, the one you ate when you first proposed to your wife on the trip. You start to cry a little at the memory, her eyes were beautiful that day werent they. Remember when you got down on your knee and almost dropped the ring? You remember as if it happened, you relive it right now... because of the fruit on that tree in this orchard. Its a great feeling isn't it. The tears are for your happiness, and you lay down on the soft grass... and you look up and thought you saw fireworks. A blast and then the fluttering, the Fourth of July... when your first kid was born. You looked out the window while you were holding him and saw the blasts over the river, the reflection of them humbled you. You started to tear up again, but slowly drifted to sleep under the moonlight. In the morning (was it morning?) you awoke and walked some more, getting past the clearing and over the hills, it wasn't a long walk like the other ones. You seemed to have landed next to a gate, this time its always the same... black, raising high into the sky, a gargoyle perched on each of its metallic poles (did the one on the right just move?). Maybe you were in the wrong place, or went around in circles, you go to turn around... but the minute you do the gates swing open, almost as if this was predetermined. You look behind you and decide to walk forward, something is pulling you toward the area, two large fountains spurt clear water from the middle, sustaining perfect timing and balance with eachother. The marble walkway slowly fades into a pitch-black, its almost unnoticible that it does. At the end of it you reach another hurdle, but your stamina is better then when you were all state in Football. Man, that was a great year for you wasn't it. Remember when you would run the stairs behind the stadium so you could beat your friends 40 Yard Dash time. Yeah. So you start jogging up the stairs, no pain, not even from the knee injury that stopped your chance in the pros, your feeling better then ever so you sprint up them, and in no time you reach the top. The pinnacle of your journey, you reached where you were supposed to be, this is where you were drawn to ever since you passed those gates, all that 'time' ago. So what do you see? No. Don't feel bad. That is your casket... the one sitting before the mansion. Yup. That is you in it. No, don't ask questions. Remember the car? Yeah, you and your wife went to the opera didnt you? Oh, sorry, dinner.. I forgot it was your anniversary. Do you remember the stop light? No... Oh now you do. Yeah, the car swerved in from the right and hit you on your side, you were thrown from the car and your wife was trapped in the seat, her seatbelt did save her from death though. Oh, don't cry, it wasn't your fault... you lived a fine life, thats why your here... Yeah, I know you miss her. But you won't miss her much longer... Just walk through the doors. All your memories will be there. What is this place you ask? Why doesn't it seem right? Wh-... Slow down now, some questions arent supposed to be answered. This? This is the realm of dreams... and it exists because one man exists, it filters through him... and its place of residence is in your head, this is your memories, your love, everything. You can spend eternity here if you like, all your friends, family, anyone that touched you. Its a Utopia. Just through the doors. Thats right. Your welcome. Goodbye. End.
