Tasteful Delicacies

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-5-02 [Fade In] [Hot weather. One of those days when it’s almost unbearable. It rained earlier, and the humidity is awful. The sun beats down, baking the sidewalk, and causing steam to rise up from the asphalt road. A couple walks hand in hand, though their hands must be sweating something vicious in such uncomfortable heat. The street are far from busy, this is Oxford, Mississippi, not New York. African Americans are common in this area. The couple is PCW star, and Stylin With Streak web site owner, ‘Scandalous’ Ryan ‘Streak’ Stephens, and his wife Dana.] [Stephens’ eyes browse the area they patrol in search of a soda shop, where they can get our of this god awful heat, and get a cold soda, a chicken salad sandwich, and maybe a scoop of ice cream. It’s an old part of town and there has to be one around here somewhere. There is antique stores galore, a guitar shop, a shoe store, and a pharmacy but still no sign of a soda shop. Back to the lots of African Americans. As Stephens watches the blacks near the area, he can’t help but think of Jordan X Shabazz and Rush Mason, his ex-stablemates. He had just begun to get to know the two guys, and it seemed as though he and Jordan might form some sort of bond, but not now.] [The Malice Crew had lost two very solid members; however, it would not hold them back. Stephens knew that the hoss himself, Duke would never let that happen. This was a good opportunity for Stephens, his stock had risen after a big pay per view win over Makonie, and now being one of only five Malice members he would have a good chance at making an impact within the stable. His wife Dana now removes her hand from his, the sweat is just gross, he holds on to his forearm and more or less lets him lead her along though she’s feisty, if she didn’t want to be led, she wouldn’t. She wears the pants in the relationship.] Dana: Baby, this weather is killing me! Surely there’s a soda shop around here somewhere! Ryan: I’m sure there has to be one on this next block. I guess we should have drove around instead of getting out and walking, this was a bad idea. Dana: One you made of course. [Ryan sighs, he knows she’s just messing with me. They walk on, both wearing shorts and sleeveless t-shirts. Her hair is pulled back in a bun so that you can see the tiny spots of perspiration on the back of her neck, not sexy, not at all. They make their way onto the next block, and finally Ryan spots a soda shop called, “Tasteful Delicacies” and points at his with his right index finger.] Dana: It’s not nice to point. [she teases] Ryan: There’s just no winning with you is there? Dana: This is one of those times where after I answering you’re going to go on a five minute tirade about The Multiple Man isn’t it? Ryan: Well baby, it is the day before the match, and I haven’t talked about him the last two days. He even cut an awesome promo where all he did the whole time was look for a towel so he could go to the beach. [The couple walks briskly towards the soda shop, anxious to get out of the heat as Dana responds.] Dana: Let me guess, there’s no winning, with me just as there’s virtually no winning against Multiple Man when he brings his A game huh? Ryan: I wasn’t going to say that. [She smirks hard at him.] Ryan: Fine, so I was! Dana: You’re getting too predictable. Your fans are going to star thinking you’re stale. Maybe you should cut a promo where all you do is go around looking for a towel or something. Don’t focus on your match at hand, or any other thing regarding wrestling, merely a towel. I got it! Ryan: Got what? Dana: An idea for a promo for you. Talk to the camera with a sock puppet, that would get you over big time. Ryan: I’ll pass! Seriously though, you’d think I was being sarcastic if I said TMM cut a sweet promo about a towel, but I’m not that’s what he does. He’s off the wall, unpredictable, and entertaining. The fans love him, perhaps more so then they love my juicy rumors. Dana: That’d be an affirmative Ryan! [She winks at him, and laughs, though he doesn’t laugh back. He’s not mad, but instead gives her a look of, ‘oh you’re so funny.’ They have reached the soda shop entrance and he opens the door for her. She walks in as little bell dings signifying to the staff there that someone has entered. She steps onto the hardwood floor of the shop, and he gently nudge her forward this his hand in the small of the back as he steps in and the door closes behind them. The cool air instantly hits them.] Ryan: Wow, it feels good in here! Dana: Sure does! [Their Multiple Man conversation is put on brief hold as Stephens and his wife make their way over to a small little table, made of white metal. They sit on little chair made of the same white medal, featuring flowered cushions, that tie to the back of the chair, and slide around all over the place if you’re not still. They’re rather irritating, but it’s nice to be inside in the cool air.] [A waitress comes up and hands them two menus and takes their drink orders then goes to retrieve the gifts as their TMM conversation ensues.] Dana: How do you plan to approach him? Ryan: I’m not sure how I can approach him. He’s unpredictable. He has no set style, he’ll use a different style in every match for a whole month sometimes. He’s not stupid like Makonie either; I’ll just have to withstand whatever attack TMM has, and try to outlast him in the end. It’s no secret that I’ll be patient, take a beating from hell, waiting for that one time when my opponent messes up and I can capitalize victoriously. Dana: If you don’t get beat too bad in the beginning, if you can draw the match out, they usually do make a mistake and then it’s all yours. Ryan: Indeed, I try to make it last so long, so that is a pure heart battle, and then if it is, I’m the only one who can prevail. Eventually, there mind falters, they’re not mentally strong enough, it’s just a matter of taking the match deep enough for that to occur. [The waitress returns with their drinks and sets them on the table in front of them. Ryan got a lemonade, and Dana got Diet Dr. Pepper. She asks if they’re ready to order, and they decline, asking her to come back in a few minutes. Ryan hates eating until he’s good and cooled off.] Dana: It’s hard to believe in only seven matches you’ve earned yourself an opportunity to vie for the television title #1 contendership. I’m inclined to think that’s moderately impressive. Besides you have a lot of momentum going for you right now. It would be really nice to see you beat TMM. Ryan: It can be done, and I firmly believe that I can do it. If Kramer Wild Wing can defeat him, then why can’t I? No disrespect to Kramer though, he’s been working hard! You know what though baby? Here I have this match with TMM, and yet I can’t get Makonie out of my mind. He fucking gave me these stitches with all those cheating ass chair shots, and these fractured ribs as well. Dana: You beat him Ryan. You out lasted all those chair shots, you endured everything he through at you, and you won. Everyone knows who the better wrestler is now, forget him, focus on a run at the television title. It’s time we got some gold around your waist! Ryan: Perhaps, but I want Makonie again. I want revenge for these stitches, and these ribs. [He rubs his hand first across the stitches in his head, and then gently taps his fracture ribs. He looks at his wife, she’s adorable. He smiles, he’s happy to be with her.] Dana:
