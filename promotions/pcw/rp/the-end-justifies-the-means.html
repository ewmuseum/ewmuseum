The End Justifies The Means.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/07/2002 (RP#4 for PPV) As much as people hate him. As much as the PCW fans detest him for the things he has done. As much as they watch the acts he commits with disgust. As much as he quest for success and power appals them... They would swap places in a second. He has a beautiful young wife, enough money to retire now and never run out if he lived until he got bored of it. He has the loyalist following of Hubbard’s zealots that anyone could ask for... And he has the chance to walk into Turmoil next week with the PCW World Title around his waist or over Ellie~!’s shoulder. These have come at a cost though.. The things he has done and seen would haunt a lesser man until he could take it no more.. But Kannon has no regrets, in his mind he is entirely justified. But if he has done all the terrible acts to get what he has, what will he do to win what he hasn’t? “All I want is that one thing that has eluded me....” With those words the senses are flooded by the sound of traffic and our shot fades in on downtown Hubbardville... To be more exact the Mayor’s Office just off Wehatenicolekidman Boulevard and before you get to Tomcruiseisnotgay Drive. On the pale stone steps, surveying all that is around his stands the familiar figure of Xavier Kannon, now 4 days removed from his chance to win the PCW Title. Xavier “This...” The Gold Patron Meritorious turns around and looks at the building itself, reminiscent of a Greek building with tall stone pillars holding up the roof and intricate patterned engravings on the wall depicting moment’s from Scientology’s admittedly short past. Kannon is dressed unusually smart, probably as part of his new duties as mayor of he city... He’s decked out in a well tailored Black suit, with Purple shirt and Black tie, and on his feet are a pair of shiny Black shoes, the merest hint of Purple stitching able to be seen around the sole. His long, Red hair is tied back neatly in a braided ponytail, an act that brings out his relatively pale skin and bright Green eyes. Xavier : “... I have.” As he stands, looking up fondly at the building from which he controls the operation of Hubbardville, the city he helped found as a refuge for oppressed Scientologists, his fellow Hubbard followers walk past, in front of the building, all turning their heads left or right as if in military parade fashion to get a good look at their hero, a man who has not only fought and campaigned for them.. But now watches over them. Kannon looks almost sombre, in a seemingly reflective mood as he looks down at the floor by his feet, then follows the building’s frontage all the way to the roof that from his angle stretches far into the late afternoon Wisconsin sun. Xavier : “I wanted to be Mayor.... And so did Ribisi....” With his piercing Green eyes still pointed upwards to the sky, Kannon turns his head left, to 10 o’clock where he sees the L Ron. Space Needle, his old campaign headquarters and the building from which his closest rival to be Major tumbled to one of his many deaths, punctures the sky. Xavier : “I wanted it more.....” His look of deep thought then breaks into something all to familiar, his trademark smirk.. A look that has seen his all the way through PCW. When he won the Cruiserweight Title, he smirked hat smirk. When he imploded Asylum out of his own jealousy, he smirked that smirk. When he took the Continental Title off Jeremy, he smirked that smirk. When he announced the Ultimate Authority’s taking over of PCW, he smirked that smirk. When he managed to beat Duke Williams at Supremacy, he smirked that smirk and when he left the ring on Turmoil with his 3 rivals down and out, he smirked that smirk. Xavier : “..... So I took it.” He then turns on the heel of his designer left shoe and walks confidently down the steps, Hubbardville bold and bright, looming in front of him. Workers nod to him as they walk past in their suits, young children.. The future of the religion play in the many parks where there is no pollution to taint the air. Taking a deep breath Kannon takes it all in... The financial district, the perfect public transport system, the top ranking school in the country... The Scientologist’s Utopia. Xavier : “I wanted a City...” Continuing to walk along the street in front of his Mayoral office, children with their mothers point to the Red headed Scientologist who strides so confidently before them.. Asking their parents if he is indeed the great Xavier Kannon they have head so much. He ignores them all, not as an insult, but as a strange mix... He doesn’t need anything from them so he doesn’t go out of his way to talk to them.. And to keep up the certain element of mystery that surrounds him as he walks openly though his populous. Xavier : “.... So it was built.” His path can be traced by those standing still... Gazing in awe at the casual man saunters by them.. Kannon in Hubbardville being like a Hollywood Star in Los Angeles... You’d expect to see them in places but never really believe it’s them. For once he seems humble... No security, no big fuss, no boasting... Almost as if he doesn’t want to be noticed.. Just wanting to reassure himself by seeing all he has accomplished.. Ultimately to convince himself that the end justified the means. Xavier : “And Hubbard would be proud of me.” He walks around the corner not even looking ahead of himself, knowing that in a city full of polite Scientologists that someone would move out of his way. In any other place he would have been knocked to the floor by some stressed out man coming off a 12 hour shift... Not in Hubbardville. It’s atmosphere is almost sedated, everyone happy with where they are and what they have.. But never losing the ambition of a person who has less. On his left is the Bank of Hubbardville building as he casually strolls over to the ATM machine and leans in close for the retina scan and in a second his retails are up on the screen. Xavier : “Balance.. Print...” At his command a perfectly sealed envelope with “Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon-Hall” is ejected.. Slowly, on a sliding tray from the machine and Xavier takes it before walking away, looking at the name in Gold Italics on the front. Xavier : “I’ll have to get “Mayor” added to that.” Walking along he undoes the envelope and pulls his statement out, casually tossing the rubbing into one of the litter bins that are placed every 5 meters along the street. He unfolds it and scans his calculating Green eyes down the piece of paper that millions would kill for. Xavier : “I always wanted it...” On it, the statement shows Kannon’s wealth in raw numbers... His income from his Gold Patron Meritorious position, his PCW contract and his shares in the company enough give him more money than all but a select group could ever dream of, but add to it he proceeds from Hubbardville’s numerous businesses he oversees.... He has, the proverbial, “It All”. Xavier : “...And I got it.” He folds the statement up and tucks it into his inside pocket, unbuttoning his jacket and loosening his tie as he heads out of the built up area of the city and towards his home. His Green eyes are constantly moving, not wanting to miss a thing that is happening around him as he walks through the long shadows being cast as the Wisconsin sun starts to dip below the Hubbardville skyline. Within 5 minutes he is at his house.... A large, grandly designed house with a painstakingly designed landscaped garden.. Still, looking quite humble for a man of his stature. Kannon walks up the path, almost breaking into a jog, the simple pleasure of arriving home to his wife up there with all his conquests of power and payments of money. Never has someone seemed so content with what he has.. As should he be given all he has done, and done to others to get it.. Still repeating to himself over and over and over, as if it is the thought that never leaves his head, a constant motto to both remind and inspire him. The end justifies the means. Xavier : “I wouldn’t give this up for the world....” Walking around the side of his home all that borders it to the north east are lush Green hills, and in the trees that stand to the north west the birds sing their own tunes, living a much simpler life than the Scientologist. Xavier : “...I prefer my own.” Kannon opens the door to his Hubbardville residence and hangs his jacket up, then slides his shoes off and pushes them with his feet across the wooden floor before walking further into the house, glimpsing at the mail to see no more has arrived since he last checked. From the next room walks his wife, Eleanor. Hew mouth is moving but her words do not penetrate the content silence Xavier has around him. His usually cold an calculating Green eyes soften with one look at her. She continues to talk, dressed in a White peasant top and headscarf with a pair of dirty denim flared jeans... Her bare feet squeeking on the varnished floor as she fidgets them, still giggly around her husband. Holding her close to him, he kisses the top of her head fondly before she scurries off into the next room again, the Scientologist still not hearing her words.. Then again, he doesn’t need to. He got more out of the mere sight of her, after only a few hours apart saying more than every word ever spoken. Xavier : “There is one thing I still lack...” He moves off in the opposite direction to Eleanor and walks into his living room, a testament to minimalism and collapses onto his Black leather sofa, reaching to his right and pressing 2 buttons on the remote without even having to look at them. Directly the wall mounted, flat screen TV in front of him comes to life, showing the last minute of Turmoil.... He watches it, not blinking the whole time so as not to miss a millisecond of it. Xavier : “...But I will have it soon...” As he pictures cuts out he rewinds again, blinking to bath his eyes before another viewing.. Watching himself stand tall above his rivals, the only men who stand between him and the PCW World Title.. The Title that will give him the recognition he turned his back on the fans for... The Title he ended Asylum for, believing he would always be held back from it. The picture fades out again and against the White wall the screen shows Black, Kannon’s face breaks out past a smirk into a full blown smile, the lack showing in his eyes which exude confidence, not believing for one second he won’t succeed.. Only wondering how. Xavier : “And the end...... Shall justify the means.”
