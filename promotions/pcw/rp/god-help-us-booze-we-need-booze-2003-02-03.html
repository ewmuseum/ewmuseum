GOD HELP US! BOOZE! WE NEED BOOZE!

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-3-03 The scene opens to Adam, AJ and Jaslene sitting in an airplane. AJ is sitting by the window, Jaslene is in the middle and Adam is stuck in the aisle. Adam looks pissed. The flight attendants are going through the routine as Adam looks to AJ. AJ starts to laugh silently at him, mocking him. Adam scowls. Stryker: It’s not fair! I called the window seat in the car and you know it! Adam thrusts his finger into AJ’s face. Trinidad: But who is in the seat? Stryker: Only becau- Trinidad: That’s right me! AJ slaps Adam’s hand away as the captain comes over the loudspeaker. Captain: Good morning and welcome aboard US Airways flight zero-nine-six-two with non stop service to Sydney, Australia. We’ll be taking off in about fifteen minutes or so. We’ll reach an altitude of thirty thousand feet and cruise at speeds of three to four hundred miles per hour. Please remain seated with your seatbelts securely buckled until we have reaches cruising altitude. Thank you and enjoy your flight. Trinidad: Well at least he’s not drunk. Jaslene: AJ! She smacks him in the arm. Trinidad: Ow! What was that for? Jaslene: Of course he’s not drunk! He’s got the lives of at least two hundred and thirty people in his hands. Trinidad: So? With that kind of pressure, now I’m sure he’s put a couple back already. AJ throws his hand up in defense as Jaslene motions like she is going to hit him again. Adam laughs. Jaslene smacks Adam. Stryker: Ow! What did I do? He’s the one accusing the pilot of being drunk! It wasn’t me! Jaslene: Don’t egg him on! I swear it’s like you two are f**king idiots together! I wish you would go back to being enemies, at least then I’d the pleasure of watching one of you get your asses kicked. They both give Jaslene a look before looking at each other. Stryker: Well that’s not very nice. Trinidad: I think she’s really mad at us Adam… She sinks into the seat, crossing her arms in front of her chest as a look of anger comes to her face. Stryker: I think you are right AJ… Trinidad: Yeah, come on Jaslene, we were only playing around. I didn’t mean anything by it I’m sure that the pilot is a very professional man and hasn’t had a drop today. She glares at AJ. Stryker: I don’t mean to egg him on…it just kind of happens. Come on, we’re really sooooooooory. Jaslene starts to crack a smile. Jaslene: Not only are you two idiots, you’re also the biggest suckers I know. Stryker: That hurts Jas…deep down inside that hurts… The plane starts to move as Jaslene looks at Adam and laughs at him. All three sit back in their respective seat as the plane begins to pick up speed. Adam reaches up, takes a hold of his nose and blows to pop his ears. Jaslene upon seeing this burst out into an uncontrollable fit of laughter. Adam looks to AJ and shrugs. A wicked smile comes to AJ’s face as he nods to Adam. Adam nods back. AJ grabs his nose and makes the same face. They both trap Jaslene up against her seat as tears begin to roll down her cheek. Jaslene: Hahahaha…hehe…hoho…ohhhh stop…I can’t take anymore! She holds her ribs as AJ and Adam keep going. Jaslene: Hahahahahahaha….STOP! She slaps both Adam and AJ across the face. Both men let go of their noses and begin to rub their cheeks. Jaslene finally manages to pull herself under control as the plane levels out and the seatbelt sign goes off. Trinidad: Damn Jaslene! That really hurt! Jaslene: I’m sorry…hehe…but you guys weren’t going to…haha…stop…hehe…were you? They look to each other and take serious thought poses. After a couple of moments of chin rubbing and head scratching they both look to her. Stryker & Trinidad: Nope. Jaslene: See? I had to! My ribs were killing me, I really couldn’t take anymore. Stryker: It couldn’t have been any worse than any match you had with Amira or Chryste, could it? Jaslene shakes her head. Stryker: Speaking of which…where is Amira? Wasn’t she with you while you were in the states? Trinidad: Yeah but she took a flight out a couple of days ago. She’ll be there when we get in to pick us up. Personally I think she was getting a bit worried about Hank. Jaslene: Hank? Who is that? Stryker: AJ’s pet kangaroo. Jaslene’s jaw drops. She turns to AJ. You have a pet kangaroo? That’s sooooo cool! Can I ride him when we get there? Trinidad: Uh…I don’t know if Hank will be up for that but we’ll see. Stryker: Say how long is the flight anyway AJ? Trinidad: I don’t really know, I spent most of the flight over here sleeping but if I had to guess I would say thirteen hours maybe? Jaslene: THIRTEEN HOURS?!?! I have to spend the next thirteen hours sitting between the two of you? Oh god…Miss! Can I get some booze or something over here?! Stryker: Would you calm down Jas? You didn’t complain when we flew to Sicily. Jaslene: Well that wasn’t a commercial flight and I didn’t have to deal with the two of you. Stryker: Well it wouldn’t be so bad if SOMEBODY had given me the damn window seat like I had called. AJ doesn’t respond, he merely looks out the window knowing that Adam is watching him. Adam growls finally getting AJ to turn from the window. Trinidad: Hm? Did you say something? I wasn’t paying attention. The view out my window is just mesmerizing. Stryker: You son of a bitch… Jaslene: Ok that’s enough from the both of you already. I swear you two are children. I don’t want to have to deal with this for another thirteen hours. Jaslene sits up in her seat and scans the aisle looking for a Flight Attendant. She spots one and calls out. Jaslene: Miss? Excuse me Miss? A Flight Attendant comes walking over. Flight Attendant: Yes? Is there something I can get for you Miss? Jaslene: Booze and lots of it! These two are going to drive me insane before we get to Sydney. The Flight Attendant smirks as she reviews AJ and Adam. Flight Attendant: Yeah they look like a couple of hell raisers to me. I’ll go see what I can get you. Jaslene: Thanks. The Flight Attendant walks off down the aisle. As Jaslene returns to her seat Adam leans over. Stryker: Booze Jas? Come on, we aren’t that bad are we? Jaslene looks at Adam and then at AJ. She mocks the two of them by rubbing her chin and scratching her head. Jaslene: Uh…yeah you are that bad now that I think about it. Trinidad: Oh come on, we’ll be good. There is no need to drink yourself into a stupor. The Flight Attendant comes back with a drink for Jaslene. She hands it to Jaslene before walking off. Jaslene takes a sip. Stryker: So what did she get you? Jaslene: Water. Suddenly a screen drops down in the middle of the aisle as one of the Flight Attendants steps out into the middle of the aisle. Flight Attendant: Excuse me, can I have your attention please? We’ll be starting one of our in flight movies in just a moment. It’s starring Mandy Moore and Shane West…the story of two North Car- Adam and AJ look to each other as their eyes fill with horror. Flight Attendant: It’s A Walk to Remember please enjoy. Stryker & Trinidad: GOD HELP US! BOOZE! WE NEED BOOZE! The scene fades as Jaslene just starts to laugh as AJ and Adam keep calling for booze.
