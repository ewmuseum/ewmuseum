House Party!! Part One

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.17.02 OOC NOTE: BOOYAH! During these times in which you think many things might be right or wrong, one things for sure: losing isn't that hard to compensate for. Usually, you'd think losing is a bad thing right, when you're only a hand away from gaining back your Tag Team titles only to have it slip under his grasp. Yet, there's an opportunity in all of this: a singles title shot. But before that title shot, you must celebrate in style. Yes, the biggest thing when you get a home is throwing the bash for it, and our protagonist is no stranger to parties. Party over Dante's house! Drunk white bitches for everybody! In the American pastime, nothing is more sacred than lavishly throwing a party for yourself and friends, and when it comes to a House Party, you tend to bring your very best, or very worst to the party. Smell that cracker air, will you? As that phrase is said, the scene opens up towards the outskirts of Houston, Texas, a spacious manor stretching out 3,000 square acres and costing a bundle - 4.7 million dollars. Standing in front of it a few yards away happens to be one half of the former Tag Team champions Playaz Inc., Dante Jackson. He smirks to himself, wearing a pair of baggy black shorts along with a Houston Astros rainbow colored baseball jersey. By his side happens to be his wife, Tracy wearing her J-Lo impersonation dress from the video 'Jenny From The Block'. She's clutching her 8-month-old daughter Simmone holding her up as the family looks in front of their new home; the marvelous structure stands on its own as a work of art. Dante: 'Now this, this is something you can smile about.' Tracy: 'I SO totally know!' Dante eyes her, before turning to face his house, loving the view and everything. Dante: 'You know, it’s a shame that I'll have to leave for Turmoil tonight, then to Belgium, and then back to the States for Alliances!' Tracy: 'So...err.... um.... aren’t you supposed to be happy that you get to beat whitey or something? I mean ALL us black folks do!' Dante: 'That's not the point my booty shaker, the point is that I get to win titles in the process! I mean, I could win the Cruiserweight, then the Tag, and then split between the Television, Continental and the WORLD TITLE?! Its heavenly!' Tracy shrugs in her usual naive manner. She isn't a fanatic of wrestling, although she happens to cover for the Wrestling Tattler website. Still it doesn't mind her and her very trivial essence that Dante loves her for that, even when she can be a odd person sometimes. Tracy: 'Yeah...like um, aren't you going to talk about your opponents and stuff?' Dante: 'What for, I mean they aren't going to understand the first time, and that portion is rather passé. Besides, the label's sent me this 9x platinum plaque since I've grown tired of having people listen to my stuff, so its all good. Erm, what in the hell are you doing?' He looks down at her, clucking and bowlegged walking towards the door, squawking and giving that booty wiggle that has been more customary in most hip hop videos today. Tracy: 'Cluck! Cluck! I’m a chicken head! Bock! Bock! Its...SO...WONDERFUL!' Dante: 'Don't you think your Ellie~! shenanigans have come a bit...um...what the hell is the word I'm looking for?' Tracy places her index finger on her chin, looking over everything around her as Dante continues to look down, pacing and wondering for the word. Tracy: 'Isn't it...like SO past pranking?' Dante: 'Don't even want to tell you the truth on that one. So, when are we going to get into this house?' Tracy: 'I think, right now guy?' The couple nods towards one another as they walk up a step at a time to the 'mountain top' of a home as a note hangs from the handle, swinging gently with a slight breeze to it. Dante walks over towards it, picking it up with a slight tug, before reading the note out loud. Dante: 'Hello new neighbors, welcome to the neighborhood. Now, that you’re hear, you are entitled to 1) Keep the noise down, 2) Stay out of trouble, and 3) Do not let any black people inside of your home. If you are a black home owner, then you will receive your DAILY CROSS BURNING ON YOUR YARD AND A SLEW OF RACIST COMMENTS COMING YOUR WAY!' Tracy: 'Um, is that bad?' Dante: 'Trace, its more than bad, IT’S A WHITE 'I HATE BLACK FOLKS COMMENT!' Tracy: 'Wow, I thought we were evil…' Dante: 'Let’s just get inside the house, before any crazy saltine crackas come and try to yell his white eccentric comments and wants to throw his diamonds at us.' Tracy: 'OOH! DIAMONDS!' Dante groans as the couple makes their way into the home via, a slight tug of the knob. Pulling open, the view is just absolutely BOOYAH! A star lights up in Dante’s eye as he begins to tenderly cry before Tracy springs up right behind him, laughing. Inside the house, '03 Bonnie and Clyde' by Jay-Z and Beyonce Knowles plays in the background as the scene swiftly changes to night as the two party throwers smile. Dante: 'I think its time to meet our guests.' 'FUCK ALL DAT BULLSHIT!' A loud roar of busting music and people stomping along the ground hits as the ground starts to shake violently. Tracy screams out in a ghoulish manner. Tracy: 'EARTHQUAKE!' Soon, a bumble load of people rush through the door almost breaking the foundation off from the hinges. As the smoke dies down, its none other than the ghetto ambassadors of the hood, Playaz Inc. Each member is decked out in lavish attire except for Ali Khadafi whom has stuck to basic blue jeans and a black T-shirt. Duzza, his braids tied back in a rubber band as he slinks towards the drink area, smiling as he holds a bottle of Cristal` in his hand. Wearing a red denim jacket with matching red pants to match. Under the jacket happens to be a red and black Playaz Inc. T-shirt, under the Philadelphia 76ers logo. He chuckles, before beginning the normal black routine of raiding the fridge. Dante: 'Duzza! Get out of there!' Duzza closes the fridge, gripping onto the KFC Bucket of Chicken and various drinks. Statuz Quo comes up and sweeps Tracy up, catching her off guard. Tracy: 'Woah, this only happens at my other night job!' The members of Playaz Inc. stare at her oddly. Playaz Inc collectively: 'Never mind...' Dante: 'Yeah, now look guys we’ve gotta think alright? I’ve got a chance to get closer to that Cruiserweight title, and then Kannon gets the idea of booking all of us in a...' Duzza: 'Black on Black Violence match?' Dante: 'Yeah! The nerve of him, and then I gotta face this chick name Brandi, who can tax my ass if I beat her, the boss or my former boss in the HIT, and also...Gaz Green.' Ali: 'Ain’t you forgeting the other dude?' Dante: 'Naw, but then I can make Cristal' the drink of PCW and we all can get title belts if we win Alliances!' Statuz Quo: 'And yo point is?' Dante: 'My point is that we can win a lot of gold, especially me and you Quo. We can get them bitches P.W.A and get our titles back.' Statuz smirks. Statuz Quo: 'So, what you sayin' is that if any of us do everything you just said, then white people will pay attention to us?' Dante: 'EXACTLY!' Duzza: 'I’m in.' Ali: 'Me too.' Dante: 'Statuz...' All eyes focus upon the vocal member of Playaz Inc as he looks at the three of them, first Duzza and Ali, before moving to Dante a eager look in his eye. Statuz Quo: 'Fo sho.' Moving closer towards the door happens to be Dark Gimmick and The End respectively, dragging along the floor with eagerness to cause some disharmony in the party atmosphere. Dark Gimmick: 'Just why the hell are we here and not off slaughtering people?' The End: 'Because, Goths are a minority, and need to be represented equally. Plus, I need to do some 'Autumn Burning'. What you get for him?' Dark Gimmick: 'A handy cut off stake with Thug ROC at the top, you?' The End: 'My special Pineapple Upside Down cake.' The sheer mention of The End's cake brings the small party to a halt, causing everyone to look at him with accusing stares. The End: 'What?' Everybody turns back to face whatever they were originally doing as Dante stands around, in the middle, lighting a blunt with rookie Jermaine James. The two laugh, before Statuz Quo and the rest of Playaz Inc break it all up. Statuz Quo: 'Duh fuck is dis shit?' Dante: 'Just a new black man trying to get his feet wet in the wrestling world. Jermaine, meet Statuz Quo.' Statuz Quo: 'The fuck why would I want to talk to dis bitch ass cracka wannabe, licking white man nuts?!' Jermaine James: 'Bra, its all fucking cool, chill out.' Statuz Quo: 'NAW!' Jermaine James: 'N.A.W?' Gaz Green: 'Ha, those black guys are trying to make a new N.W.A!' All: 'N.W.A?! FUCK DA POLICE!' Soon as that is heard, 'Straight Outta Compton' by N.W.A blazes through the stereo system as many hop into the living room, shaking and grooving on the dance floor. One mentioned person is Nerezza, clutching onto one woman by his arm. She sways left and right, before laying her head against the broad man’s chest. Nerezza: 'Yo G, I’m gonna hit this biznitch up real quick and then, CHOCOLATE CITY BABY!' Gaz 'The Jester' Green: 'I holla at ya, let me go find some white booty, FO SHO!' Nerezza: 'Did you get the ear muffs?' Gaz 'The Jester' Green: 'Yeah, why?' Nerezza: 'Statuz Quo be coming our way homes.' The duo runs around the house, passing right through the pioneers of black Tag Teams in Primetime Championship Wrestling; Da Westsidaz. The group comprised of Riptah and Short Circuit happen to latch a hold upon the DJ for the night, relatively out-spoken RUSH commentator - Vanilla Skillz. Vanilla Skillz: 'Sup my chocolate brothas?' Riptah: 'The only damn thing chocolate about you is when you end up near a pile of shit. Now why in the hell do you have THIS at a BLACK party?!' Riptah holds up a copy of 'WHITE BOY GREATEST HITS!' - a mix CD comprised of many white acts whom never made it big, such as Snow, Vanilla Ice, MC Hammer (okay, he was black, but he had the audacity to wear parachute pants!) and other failed black acts. Skillz smirks at the CD. Vanilla Skillz: 'Boye, dat CD be the jam of the year foo!' Short Circuit: 'Man, let me find some weed before I SERIOUSLY FUCK THIS CRACKA UP!' Cutting to the outside, we see a light blue Cooper pull up in the driveway as the lights shut off thanks to a person cutting off the ignition. Stepping out of the car is the lovely OSW starlet, Tempest with the current PCW Television Champion the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. The Trapezoid carries his trusted Math Book o Doom, while Tempest carries a Lava Lamp, neatly decorated in sparkles. HIT knocks on the door politely. Tempest: 'Err, HELLO?' The HIT looks around suspiciously, before turning to face Tempest. HIT: 'Ivan better not show up, don’t want to ruin my TV title by his old grudges. You know I beat him at Intervention right, and then I became the Cruiserweight champion before I lost it on a Pay Per View card, then I beat Kid Wonder for the Television before I lost that and now I have it again, have you got all that?' Tempest: 'Yeah, but next time, control your spit.' HIT: 'ITS SPIT!' Tempest: 'Like I said, spit!' HIT: 'SPIT!' Tempest: 'Spit!' Tracy opens the door while the two stars are arguing. HIT: 'SPI - oh look the doors open.' Tracy: 'Erm, yeah aren't you one of those teacher dudes that Dante's got to fight, or fuck I can’t remember things you know. Of course he could face that ole’ jolly Scientologist Xavier Kannon, but he’s a meanie so I hope you beat him!' HIT: 'Err, is the answer to that the postulate square of the rectangle basis x+2-2x=c2?' Tracy: 'Like that's SO hard to figure out!' The HIT turns to Tempest, almost saddened by the answer. The HIT: 'She doesn't know? SHE DOESN'T KNOW?!' Tempest: 'She’s in her Eleanor Hall-Kannon mode, don't pay it no attention.' Tracy: “Like, WHATEVER!' Tracy storms off as the two shrug their shoulders and step into the party. Outside, Magnum Wolf, former or current FSW star, I keep on forgetting stands near the tool shed, pulling out a bucket of lighter fluid and a lighter. Suddenly, The End poofs right in front of Magnum. Magnum Wolf: 'Ahh!' The End: 'What the hell are you scared for?' Magnum Wolf: 'I thought somebody’d get suspicious.' The End: 'Suspicious? I’m here to help!' With that in mind the dastardly evil doers set out in forth of crashing the party, and thereby giving the DUCK~! new people to feast on. WILL THEY BE STOPPED?! WILL DANTE WIN ALL OF THOSE TITLES?! WILL PLAYAZ INC. BE SUCCESSFUL?! WHAT MORE CAN HAPPEN AT DANTE’S PARTY?! WHY AM I TALKING LIKE RENT A HERO'S NARRATOR!?!?! FIND OUT, SAME PARTY-TIME, SAME PARTY CHANNEL! Dante: 'HOLY BOOTY SHORTS!' I SAID THE PHRASE ALREADY! Dante: 'Oh, well fade to black then.'
