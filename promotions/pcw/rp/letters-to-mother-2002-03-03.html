Letters to Mother

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd March 2002 (spoken) “Folks, I'd like to sing a song about the American dream. About you, about me. About the way our American hearts beat Way down in the bottom of our chests. About that special feeling we get in the cockles of our hearts, Maybe below the cockles, maybe in the sub-cockle area, Maybe in the liver. Maybe in the kidneys. Maybe even in the colon - I don't know. (singing) I'm just a regular Joe, with a regular job. I'm your average white, suburbanite slob. I like football, and porno, and books about war. I've got an average house, with a nice hardwood floor. My wife, and my job, my kids and my car, My feet on my table - and a cuban cigar. But sometimes that's just not enough, To keep a man like me interested! (Oh no! No way! Uh uh!) No, I gotta go out and have fun, At someone else's expense! (Oh yeah! Yeah yeah! Yeah yeah yeah yeah yeah!) I drive really slow, in the ultra-fast lane, While people, behind me, are goin' insane! I'm an asshole! (He's an asshole!) I'm an asshole! I'm an asshole! (He's a asshole! Such an asshole!) I use public toilets, and I piss on the seats. I walk around in the summertime sayin' 'How about this heat?' I'm an asshole! (He's an asshole! What an asshole!) I'm an asshole! (He's the world's biggest asshole!) And sometimes I park, in handicapped spaces, While handicapped people, make handicapped faces! I'm an asshole! (He's an asshole!) I'm an asshole! I'm an asshole! (He's a real fucking asshole!) Maybe I shouldn't be singin' this song - Ranting and raving and carrying on. Maybe they're right when they tell me I'm wrong. . . . . NAHHHHH! I'm an asshole! (He's an asshole! What an asshole!) I'm an asshole! (He's the world's biggest asshole!) (spoken) You know what I'm gonna do? I'm gonna get myself a 1967 El Dorado convertible - Hot pink! With whale-skin hubcaps, an all-leather cow interior, and big brown baby seal eyes for headlights! Yeah! And I'm gonna drive down the highway at 100 miles an hour, Gettin' one mile per gallon, wolfin' down quarter pounder cheeseburgers from McDonalds That come in those old-fashioned, non-biodegradeable styrofoam containers. And when I'm done suckin' down those greaseball burgers, I'm gonna throw the containers right out the side, And wipe my mouth on the American flag, and there's not a God-damn thing anybody can do about it! You know why? Two words - nuclear fuckin' weapons! Russia, Germany, Romania, they can have all the democracy they want, They can have a big democracy cakewalk right through the middle of Tiennamen Square And it won't make a lick of difference, because we got the bombs. Okay? John Wayne's not dead, he's frozen! And when we find a cure for cancer, We're gonna thaw'r out the Duke and he's gonna be pretty pissed off! You know how pissed the Duke's gonna be? Have you ever taken a cold shower? Well multiply that by fifteen million and that's how pissed the Duke's gonna be. I'm gonna get the Duke, and Lee Marvin, and John Casavetti and a case of Whiskey, And I'm gonna drive down to Texas - (Hey! Hey! Hey! HEY! You know you really are an asshole?) Why don't ya just shut up and sing the song pal? (sung) I'm an asshole! (I'm an asshole!) I'm an ashole! I'm an asshole! (He's the world's biggest asshole!) A, S, S, H, O, L, E! Everybody! A, S, S, H, O, L, E! (trail into non-vocal chanting and barking) (spoken) I'm an asshole, and I'm proud of it. “ – Dennis Leary Dear Mum, I hope you find the March Madness tickets enclosed in this little note. I’m already at the stadium getting some last minute training so I had these sent around to you via UPS... of course the plane tickets are also on the way but are apparently running an hour behind so you should get them in a little bit. Anyway, thanks for tea and all, it was great. I’ll have to get you to send that recepe for those cookies to me, because they were great... what was in them, I thought it was like raisins and cinnamon or something. Anyway, they really were good. Even Natalia liked them, and usually if it’s not got Russian on the jar or it’s in pickle then she doesn’t like them. You asked before if I really liked my job, and I said I didn’t know because I didn’t have much of a reference point for other careers. Well I’ve decided. I do like my job, and I plan to do it for the rest of my life. If Ivan Stanislav can still wrestle at forty odd, then so can I; that’s for certain. Besides the retirement packages Duke Williams has worked out for the workers is rather good, so I have every reason to stay on here. I noticed the other day that my good friend Steve started to speak with the royal “we”. I say Royal because well that is generally the only people who use such a term, apart from that dufus Akiyama, but then again he is a qualified Dufus from Dufus University in Dufus State. In fact he was so dumb at college that he turned up for Football practice wearing a pair of ice skates. Well ok, maybe he’s not that stupid, but he’s been hit in the head a lot and I think sometimes it gets to him with all this “we” crap. It’s a good job he’s not in Kindergarden because all it would take is a teacher to hear him the wrong way and he would end up with a smacked bottom. I’m worried about Legend Inc. Not that he’s going to win the March Madness thing, but because well... he’s rather like me. Ok so he doesn’t wear goggles and stuff, but it’s the first person I can directly relate to over issues of race. For instance, a while back I called myself a Japo-American and joked about a state called Japo-America. People called me racist and claimed I was trying to invent a new country. Now seriously, don’t you think I would have named it better? Utopia... something like that, but not frickin’ Japo-America. That’s just plain retarded, yet people believed what people believe, and they end up with rubbish printed like that. Oh yeah, Natalia wanted me to invite you along to your celebratory trip to Disneyworld if I win the World Title on Sunday. You can meet Steve, he’s about your age so you’ll have something in common. Just I ask one thing... he’s a bit... well... if you show any sort of response he’ll probably think he’s died and gone to Vegas or something. So please, it’s a bit weird to ask but... act civil and polite and all that but don’t indicate anything else. Having Steve date my mother would just be plain bizarre, even for me. Anyway, I’m gonna get. I’ll see you in a few hours when you get here and we’ll go out for dinner or something, ok? It’ll be fun... oh and I’ve included tickets so you can bring along that little moron Robert. I can remember when we were kids, we tricked Kate into thinking we had ridden this box down the stairs when in fact we wanted her to go first... three broken ribs and a concussion... I did tell Robert to get out the way the little moron... Kate was annoyed too cos she split a nail. Heck, I’m just glad this is all coming back to me now. I’ll stop talking now because I’m sure the other tickets have now arrived. Because... I’m... Excellent; Alexander I. Miyagawa
