I Watched

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/16/02 What makes a killer? Honestly, what creates him? Is one born with the instinct inside, to take the life of another.....is it an inherited trait? A product of your upbringing? Or is it mere destiny that casts you into this role? I've killed many. Countless. Boundless are the piles of bodies of the dead. Without number. But what made me what I am? I have traveled through the ages. I have watched them all. So many killers. So many. 1892 A young woman was interrupted by a shrieking voice. 'Come quickly!', she implored. So down she went, and gazed upon a sight that would never leave her. A body in a chair. Her employer, his skull shattered. The maid turned to the girl at her side....looked into her eyes. What did she see there? What in that moment did she see in the eyes of the girl? I was there. I saw the glint of horrible mirth beneath the thin facade of innocence. 1947 A young lady is murdered. Sawed literally in half. Your legs splayed to the east, and to the west. Prior to this you'd never even struck such a pose. Immortalized. Immortalized in shame. I was there as well. You were always such a good girl. Taunting and teasing....I knew what was inside your mind. Did you? Now look at you. Will people remember your life? Will people remember your name? No. They'll remember the name Hollywood bestowed upon you. I was there as well, Dahlia. I watched your killer. Worthless scum. A drunk. He'd done it before. He didn't even know what he was doing, truthfully. He died a pathetic death before the police even realized he was in fact the killer. An accident. Nothing but an accident. 1969 An actress eight months pregnant. Found laying, smeared with her own blood. A rope around her neck. On the wall, one simple word. 'PIG'. I was there. I looked into her face as she was strangled, as both her and the child inside her screeched to a halt. I was somewhere else too. I was a bit away, watching a shabby looking gentleman. He had orchestrated it. He was as much of a sinner. More so. He was guilty of wrath AND sloth. He still is to this day. Helter Skelter indeed. 1973 I watch as a good looking fellow, a 32 year old former law student bashed the brains in of women and children. I watched his face. I watched her face. I watched his face as he was electrocuted as well. Yes I did. 1979 I was there when police found piles and piles of bodies under the clown's house. I was there for his death as well. I've watched so many deaths. So many. 1991 I watched a skinny, well spoken fellow assault young boys. I watched as he took their skulls. I watched as one boy actually escaped him. I watched as he actually talked the police into a tale of how his victim, drugged and incoherent was simply his drunken lover and apologized for his actions. I watched the police escort the victim back to the killer's home. I watched him strangle the boy. 2002 I watched a puerto rican man walk through an arena. I watched him enter combat. I watched another place a gun to his head. I watched him fire. I watched the life drain from him. I watched you, Charlie. So many I have watched. Mark my words today. As the life drains from you. I will watch again.
