Opposing Societies?

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th April ‘Well damn me,’ muttered Sivasian Krikorian as he pulled his rented saloon car upto the car park of a large mall near his home. Today the sun shone, and, although he knew it was hot, Sivasian refused to change his dress. ‘Honey,’ his wife started as usual, ‘you look ridiculous, take some of those layers of clothes of.’. ‘And start to try and conform to the American way darling?’ he replied, smartly. ‘YEAH!’ he gleamed back. ‘No way!’ he snapped back, adjusting his shoulders and pulling his sniping cut off finger clothes further up his hands, ‘ and remind me,’ he started, ‘why we’re here?’. ‘To look for the society which is America, and which is so bad,’ she replied, walking, and warbling her ass towards the hall entrance, with several men staring at nothing but her two cheeks, and not her facial ones. ‘Right….’ He said, ‘and let me guess, you’ll fit some shoe shopping into that category as well?’ ‘Well duh…’ she said, ‘I’ve totally got to get into the niche of checking out the American society with the right clothing, and accessories!’ He briefly smiled before rolling his eyes. Sivasian didn’t usually muck about much and venture away from what was in the end business, unless he was around his wife. He walked towards the mall, the crowd also staring at him, but more because of his long Ukrainian black fur trench coat, complete with strapping boots and his black gloves. He looked around nervously, feeling uncomfortable being around the wrong sort of people, in a different culture, in a different country, in a different society. ‘Come on!’ squealed his wife, and pointed towards the clothing store, ‘We’ll see if we can find anything for me inside here.’ They walked towards the clothing store, and wandered in. Sivasian, as was usual when shopping with his wife, stood and held her diamond crusted handbag, whilst she threw various garments onto the rails, checked the material, saw if it suited her and THEN the price tag. ‘Ugh…’ she muttered, in the same light as Rush did when he saw white people, ‘they don’t have any size eights! How is a girl suppose to reveal her sexy body when you’re wearing baggy, silly flowery dresses!’ she sulked, and slumped against the WIDE mirror in the corner. ‘Well there ya go,’ starts Sivasian, lacing his hands through some cotton tee shirts, also which appear huge and wide, ‘first point to our society check, American people only want clothes that are huge, mainly because those little eating machines cannot physically stop themselves from buying and eating that disgusting fast food stuck…it’s truly horrible.’ ‘Yah…obviously,’ says his wife, staring out the shop window towards a portly man and woman, eating two portions of McDonalds each, and getting it all around their mouths. ‘And the pessimism of the place terrifies me too,’ says Sivasian, before pointing to two women, and homing in on their conversation. ‘Well, does my ass look HUGE or WHAT? Tell me Mom, I wanna know, you’re still hip, you’re fashionable, how does this look?’ says the first woman, who’s getting on the larger side of life. Her mother, a woman who looks no older than 35, so obviously had her young, has several studs in her ears, and is blatantly a goth. ‘UGH!’ screams Sivasian, before returning to his usual ice cold state, ‘it’s your body, do what you want.’ He mutters under his breath. ‘Well, what about education, that’s a big part of society,’ says Mrs.Krikorian, before twiddling her hair in her finger. Sivasian sighs, leans across and kisses her, before clenching his fists and returning them down to the side of his body. ‘Right, let’s go and find out.’ He commands, before marching out of the shop. Staying focused on a bench ahead, with three young black American teenagers on, he marches over, stops next to the bench, and stares at them for 3-4 seconds, as they eat on some cornbread. ‘Hey, shouldn’t you be in the fields, picking sugar cane?’ he asks one of the large black boys. ‘Heh yo, yo he’s dizzin uz bro,’ the kid says to his friend. Sivasian’s wife taps him lightly on the arm, and he turns round, ‘Yes, my darling,’ he responds. ‘Sivasian, the trade for black slaves ended some 200 years ago…’ she said, ‘everyone’s equal now in modern day society.’ ‘It’s unusual to see a negro in the street in Ukraine, they play soccer, sure, but they don’t openly take a seat!’ he replies. ‘Heh man, you’re racist’, one of the black guys asks. ‘Not racist, more ignorant,’ his wife replies, and tugs Sivasian away before trouble brews. ‘Not a good way to entertain the new American neighbours, not a very good start at all,’ says his wife. ‘Well, what about the Egyptian society?’ asks Sivasian, eyeing everyone around him. He clenches his fists and reframes himself from lashing out against Americans, against people he would like to kill. ‘Meh, they’re all in the past,’ his wife replies, ‘They’re nothing now.’ ‘Stupid society,’ Sivasian says, ‘why can’t anyone else be like the Ukraine, that’s a perfect mix of pleasure and work, hot with cold, cows and goats…’ ‘Cows and goats?’ his wife says questioning. ‘Meh, I said the first thing that came into my head,’ he replies, before turning back around and walking towards the entrance again.
