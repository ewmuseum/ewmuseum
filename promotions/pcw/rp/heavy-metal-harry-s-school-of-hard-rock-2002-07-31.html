'Heavy Metal' Harry's School of Hard Rock

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 31 [The scene opens on a suburban high school in Los Angeles, California. The structure looks more like a large and sophisticated office building instead of a school. With all of the perks like vending machines, fully equipped gyms, spas, saunas, and a coed locker room...wait...mark that last one off, that is more of a dream than anything. But with all those perks many of the children take advantage of their school, they take advantage of their privledges, and they dont' appreciate what people have given to them. Snot nosed, unappreciative punks don't deserve a beautiful structure like this high school. They deserve to be crammed into small spaces with no air conditioning in the spring and no heat in the winter. If they are going to act live punks, then they should be treated like punks. The unappreciative kids end up in summer school, making up their credits so they don't have to repeat the same grade over again. It's ashame to keep giving kids a chance. If you fail, then you fail. If that's what the person wants then that's what the person should receive. Plain and simple] [With a shortage of teachers this year the town is forced to grab substitute teachers from across the tri-state area. They have been successful for the first part of the summer, but now the school stands scratching their heads as they need a teacher to be able to babysit a small group of misfits who didn't do well in Algebra. They searched far and wide but no one would step forward for this assignment. Was it that the teachers were lazy and wanted to enjoy their summer? No. Was it that Algebra is a dying subject? No. Is it that these group of high school students are the worst in the area? Yes. It's nothing out of Lean On Me or anything. This is the suburbs and any kid who lives in Da 'Burbs can't be capable of that much mischief. Trust it] [The search was on for someone to babysit. The school went into past alumni and they came across one name that they should be passing up. They know him as Harry Gordo. But PCW fans know this man as 'Heavy Metal' Harry. With desperation they called him up and in his busy schedule Harry agreed to watch these fine students, but for a hefty dollar amount. The school agreed and Harry is set to be an Algebra teacher just for a few short hours] [There are 5 students scattered all about the classroom, waiting for their teacher to arrive. They can't wait to see who they get so they can annoy the piss out of him or her and get out early. Lets face it...it's a pain in the ass to wake up early on your summer vacation to come to school. (Editors note: If these assholes would have done fine during the actual school year they wouldn't be in this position.)] [A couple sits in the corner, slightly making out. The guy, with long blonde hair and wearing a T-shirt that reads 'F*CK Sellouts'. His girl friend is gothed out in black arm bands, a black belly shirt, black jeans, and black lipstick. The only thing that isn't black on her is her pale white skin. A black young man sits the front of the classroom, away from all of the other kids. He knows he's one out of five black kids that goes to the school and he knows the muscle he carries around this place. Everyone fears him. He wears a Wu-Tang T-shirt with extremely baggy pants, and large boots. There is a very shy looking girl in the middle of the room that wears a Korn T-shirt, blue jean shorts, and sneakers. That leaves the very fat kid in the opposite corner who has a puddle of drool forming beside his desk as his marijuana leaf hat is turned backwards on his head] [The door opens and the man known as 'Heavy Metal' Harry walks through the doorway. Harry, decked out in his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots, walks to his desk and looks to the kids. The four awake members look at him in disbelief, not really expecting this kind of person to walk into their classroom. The fat kid in the corner wakes up and sees Harry. His eyes nearly pop out of his head] Fat Kid In The Corner: YOUR HARRY!! [Harry seems shocked] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: How'd you know my name, man? Fat Kid In The Corner: Because your 'Heavy Metal' Harry from PCW. This is freakin' awesome! [Harry shrugs his shoulders, not really sure what the fat kid is talking about. His eyes are bloodshot beyond all belief and his mind is hazy. He really doesn't know what he's supposed to do. It's school and he has to teach these kids. He needs to give them something to enlighten their minds. Something that they can use in their lives once they leave high school. Harry then remembers back to his high school days. Actually he can only remember one day when a teacher was...teaching...it was Biology so the teacher was teaching...well...Biology. Harry remembered being upset that he had to learn about the human body and how it works. He would have rather been rocking out to some Quiet Riot or Bon Jovi. He was going to give the kids a lesson IN HARD ROCK!!] Harry: Ok, man! Listen up! [For some reason these misfit kids seem to be paying attention. They want to see what these long haired freak has to say] Harry: I don't know what I'm supposed to do, so I'm going to teach you about something that is far out, man. Fat Kid In The Corner: Can you teach us how to wrestle? Harry: No, man. That is far out too, but it's a different kind of far out. Besides your too fat to wrestle. [The other kids laugh] Harry: Your now enrolled in...HARRY'S SCHOOL OF HARD ROCK!! Black Kid In The Front: DAT'S WACK!! Harry: Wack, man? Black Kid In The Front: WACK!! DIS IS STRAIGHT WACK!! I don't wanna be learnin' about no hard rock! That sh*t is straight up stupid. Just a bunch of noise...dah dah dah!! [Harry has a look of pure seriousness across his face. He approaches the black kid and looks down at him] Harry: You dare criticize rock like that, man? Black Kid In The Front: YEAH, BITCH!! Harry: BLASPHEMY, man.... [Harry grabs a ruler from the desk and slams it down on the kids hand. The black kid falls to the floor holding his hand in agonizing pain. He looks back up at Harry who has this very crazy look in his eyes] Black Kid: NAW!! You just f*cked up! I'm goin' to da principals office and suing your bitch ass!! [The black kid storms out of the room holding his hand] Harry: That wasn't far out, not far out at all, man. [Harry notices the couple in the corner. He approaches them with ruler in hand] Harry: What's so funny, man? Blonde Haired Guy: It's about time someone whipped that nig-- Harry: I am an equal oppurtunity hand basher, man. I will not hesitate to use this. Blonde Haired Guy: Listen, you come in here wearing your Twisted Sister T-shirt like your some kind of cool guy. Your thinking that your going to relate to some of us because you listen to heavy music. YOU DON'T KNOW JACK ABOUT HEAVY MUSIC!! You don't know what's goin' down in the underground. [Harry giggles to himself] Harry: That rhymed, man. Guy Girlfriend: It did. Blonde Haired Guy: Shut up. You can't relate to us. I bet you don't even know any new metal bands. Your just some stupid wrestler who is stuck on the 80's because you refuse to grow up. [Harry plays with his ruler, looking at the guys hand] Harry: What are your favorite bands? Blonde Haired Guy: Animalistic Terror Bunnies, Sh*tting Bricks, Grabbing Penis's with Robotic Hands, Lifting Excriment with Rubber Gloves, and Holy Sh*t We're Underground! Harry: Huh? Blonde Haired Guy: EXACTLY!! That's what I'm talking about. Those are the greatest damn bands with the greatest music of all time. They are great underground bands that nobody even knows of. I party with most of them too. They are cool. Harry: What happens when they get big, man? Blonde Haired Guy: It's not about getting big. Those guys won't sell out. Selling out is for pussies!! Look at all the popular bands. Linkin Park SUCKS!! They totally sold out. Metallica went and got haircuts and all they care about is money!! They SUCK! Harry: So a band has to be underground to like them, man? Guy's Girlfriend: Yeah! Harry: What do you think about Twisted Sister? Blonde Haired Guy: They suck. They look like a bunch of clowns on crack! Their music was so manufactured it wasn't even funny. They had no vision. Harry: Quiet Riot, man? Blonde Haired Guy: They thought they were leading a heavy metal revolution or something. They just really sucked and didn't even know how to play metal. Just another manufactured band by a major label. Harry: What about Bon Jovi, man? Blonde Haired Guy: HAHAHA!! THEY ARE HORRIBLE! Bon Jovi is the poster boy for selling his soul! [Harry slams the ruler down on his hands three straight times, leaving the punk kid is tears. He begins crawling toward the door but then Harry begins smacking him in the ass with the ruler and in the back] Harry: RAH, man!!!! [Harry kicks the punk kid out of the room and slams the door behind him] Harry: Nobody puts down my guys, man!! NOBODY!! [Harry looks down at the girl in the Korn shirt] Harry: Hi, man. Girl in Korn shirt: Bon Jovi doesn't suck. Harry: I know, man. Girl in Korn shirt: Would do you think about Korn? [Harry thinks about it for a second and licks his lips] Harry: Corn is really good, man. Especially on the cob with lots of butter spread on it. Fat Kid: She means the band Korn, not the food!! Harry: Corn? Fat Kid: Korn. Harry: Corn, man? Fat Kid: Korn...with a K Harry: Corn with a K, man? Fat Kid: ARGH! [The fat kid walks out of the room with his tiny desk still attached to him because he is so obese] Harry: Far out, man. [The door swings open quickly and the other half of The Cowabunga Connection, and Harry's brother, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy appears in the doorway. He wears his button down Hawaiian flower dress shirt, blue and yellow swim trunks, and black sandals. Tommy looks extremely excited about something] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE!! Even though we were bogusly missing in action at Wrestle Vessel, we have number one contender shots on Fusion, dude! Harry: Far out, man. TTT: Lets go, dude! Harry: Ok, man. [Tommy runs out as Harry begins his way out. He looks at the goth girl who's boyfriend he smacked out of the room. He walks over to her, slips her his number and leaves the room] Goth Girl: What a freak. Girl in Korn Shirt: He was kinda cute. Goth Girl: He doesn't get the underground. Girl in Korn Shirt: Shut up, your annoying. Goth Girl: Korn sold out too. Girl in Korn Shirt: Why is everyone a sell out? Goth Girl: They are!! [The girl in the Korn shirt smacks the goth girl in the hands with the ruler and begin having a cat fight. Clothes rip off, cleavage is exposed, and nudity is soon shown. Too bad there was nobody there to witness this greatness] Blackness.
