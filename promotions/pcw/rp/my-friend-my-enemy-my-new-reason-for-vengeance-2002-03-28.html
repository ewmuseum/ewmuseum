My Friend, My Enemy...My New Reason For Vengeance.

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/03/02 The shot opens up in a quiet cemetery, the light drizzle that is falling adds an extra shimmer to the lush Green lawns that surround the monuments to past lives. “My friend......My enemy.......My reason for even further vengeance....” We pan around, past lavishly engraved Gravestones to where a poorly assembled Wooden Cross, 2 sticks tied together with string is stuck into a mound of damp soil. “...I know who’s fault this REALLY is and do not worry, I will give you the vengeance in Death that you never had in Life....” At the foot of the cross, leant against the mound of earth is a bunch of Purple flowers bound in Black string and Green plastic. “....I apologise for the acts I perpetrated upon you, for I was blinded by ambitions...Deceived by the promise of something more....” The camera zooms in on the flowers before rising up, following the vertical stick as raindrops fall the other way until a name is seen carved shallowly in the horizontal stick.... “D r a k e M a x w e l l R I P” “....Now the blindness has been replaced by vision so clear feel I can see for miles....” The camera breaks away from the crude cross and shows he 1st couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and his valet Eleanor Hall looking down at Drake’s symbol to life, left after his death. Xavier : “Maybe if you had converted to Scientology rather than sticking with your out-dated Christian values you may be able to hear me now, rather than living out the rest of your life at Hubbard’s beck and call....” The Scientologist is dressed all in Black. A long Black coat that collects the rain and the droplets stick to the fabric, under it he has a Black silk shirt and a pair of his Black, worker-cut jeans with Purple embroidery snaking down the left leg to a pair of smart black shoes that have collected mud and grass on the toes from the loosened earth. His long Auburn hair is neatly tied back and his bright Green eyes stand-out in the murky conditions of the afternoon rain. Xavier : “....Then again, if it were not for one man, and one man alone you would not be in this situation, you’d be running around in the sun as the hills become alive with the sound of music, a smile beaming from your face as you run with the anim-....Well, maybe not, but at least you wouldn’t be dead.” Beside him his valet nods, dabbing under her eyes with a tissue as her mascara runs. Eleanor : “Wouldn’t........Be.........Dead!” The preppy valet is wearing a long, Black leather coat with a tight, fitted Black shirt under it, just open enough to show a glimpse of her cleavage. With this she has a knee length Black shirt and tall Black boots. he long, wavy Brown hair is loose and cascading down to the small of her back, picking up the light shower in beads of water. Xavier : “The world in reality knows who’s fault your death was Drake, I know too...As I’m sure you do. But I bet you the idiotic PCW viewers will be blaming this n the Celestials, for they may have been the ones that banished you Drake, but in reality we know you were killed long before that, and I have to live with myself for being part of it....” The Scientologist carries a solemn look on his face, and in a very rare event, he appears to be sincere as his regular smug grin is nowhere to be seen, the same with Ellie~! Xavier : “I believed what I was doing was the right thing, I was convinced that it was you, Drake, who was at fault, I did not know then that I was being exploited, that I was being used, that I was a mere puppet in someone else’s plan.....I was being WHORED OUT...” The Gold Patron Meritorious pulls a bottle of Scotch out of his pocket and takes a few steps towards the grave and screws the top off. Xavier : “So here is to you Drake Maxwell, the man who beat me to win the NTICW World Title, an honour that meant something until a certain person used you then buried you, under the illusion he was going to make a name for you before dropping you in an instant when something better came along...” He takes a long swig of the Brown liquor before pouring some over Drake’s mound of earth. Xavier : “To the man who I teamed with in NTICW last days, the man who helped fight for NTICW against Eternal Wrestling while a certain person sat in PCW, not even knowing what people like me went through before we even walked into the PCW offices...” He takes another sip then does the same for Drake, sending a trickle of Whiskey into the earth around his cross. Xavier : “To the man that I wrongly attacked, wrongly hated....You may have kidnapped Justin Howard but I should have applauded you for it...Drake, I should have down-right HELPED you! But I apologise, I was blinded by the lights of Asylum, I turned on you...And I will have to live with hat until the day I stop caring or forget it!” Another glug of the Scotch goes down Xavier’s throat and the warmth of the liquid shows with Xavier’s cheeks becoming more rosy. He empties the rest of the bottle onto the earth, drenching the Purple flowers with the Brown liquid before holding the bottle up next to the cross. Xavier : “To Drake Maxwell, an Ally, an Enemy...And the man who made me see I was nothing but a Pawn....Goodbye....” He toasts the cross with the bottle but a little too hard and the cross, not properly supported by the damp earth around the cross drops backwards onto the sodden grass behind it. Ellie~! looks at it with a furrowed brow. Eleanor : “Er...Was it, like, MEANT to do that?” The Scientologist ignores the whiny voice of his preppy valet and casually tosses the bottle down onto the cross and rolling a few inches pat it before it rests in the wet grass. Xavier : “Come on Ellie~! it’s time to go......Drake deserves the revenge in death he never had in life...And we both know who the retribution is aimed at...” He says these words without looking away from the cross then he turns around and walks past Eleanor who is trying to work out who he meant. Eleanor : “Er.....We, like...DO?“ She trundles after her man and they begin to navigate the paths that criss-cross the cemetery’s lawns and graves. Xavier : “He may have only perished on Tuesday, but Drake’s spirit was killed on march 3rd....He was killed at March Madness, his work in PCW was un-done, the Blood he spilt for the company was rendered worthless.....He had no reason to live...And it is one mans fault.” As the couple walk, the rain appears to stop and the Dark Grey clouds become highlighted by the afternoon sun lighting them from above, trying to burn through. Ellie~! has been in deep thought through Kannon’s words, trying to remember back a few weeks to the last PPV. Eleanor : “Is it.....Er....Howard?“ She looks to her man with hopeful eyes and when he nods a broad, proud grin crosses her face as she glows with pride. Eleanor : “YAY ME!!!” The Scientologist ignores his valet’s celebrations and keeps his solemn expression as he sun finally breaks through and beams of light perforate through the clouds and bury themselves into the earth, reflecting strongly off the wet grass. Xavier : “It was not the Void that killed Drake, it was nor Jay Sylo...Howard killed him, and the sad thing is that son of a bitch doesn’t even know it...” Despite getting the last bit right, Ellie~! now looks lost as to the reasoning and her curious look returns, just nodding along with his words. Xavier : “Look what he did.....Not only did he get Bobby Stormbringer to beat the poor young Maxwell senseless, he actually got me, a man with a history with Drake, a man who has fought alongside him...He got me to oppose him too....Something I now regret for I know I was not a friend of Howard, I was a lackey...And we’ve all seen what happens when a lackey has enough...” More sun breaks through and the scene becomes increasingly bathed in light as Xavier’s words become more passionate. Xavier : “Then came March Madness, not only did Jeremy SCREW Drake by having Stormbringer come and help him....He go-....He COERSED Drake’s only friend, the End, to turn on him....Howard left Madness with no Justin Howard, no Continental Title, no friends and no spirit left in his beaten body.....Jeremy Howard killed Drake and if he wasn’t so damn SELF CENTRED it should be eating him up inside....But no, he is still angry at me, just because I am not longer under his command...” Eleanor sneers as she hears the verbal assault on Jeremy, buying her man’s words. Eleanor : “Under.......His........Command!” Xavier : “That is what ALL of this is about, this is why my poor, innocent valet is being made to wrestle, because just for once I did something that I didn’t get approved by him, that I did something I didn’t run by him first.....I actually spoke my heart and look where it got me, Howard couldn’t have that, so he ended Asylum and blamed it on me...” Ellie~! nods along to his words as they walk through the afternoon sun towards the elegantly crafted Black iron gates they lead out of the cemetery. Xavier : “Spring Fling will be a turning point. I will NOT back down from Jeremy as many have in the past, I shall not doubt myself as I KNOW I am right...I will do to him what he has done to so many others, I will destroy his legacy, I will leave him with nothing, I will put him back when he started.....Friday is where it will begin, I will follow from Corey’s lead and I will pin your shoulders down.....The name Xavier Kannon will become synonymous with TWO things.....KILLING ASYLUM and ENDING JEREMY HOWARD....In the name of Drake Maxwell and all the others that Krayzie and his little gang killed...” With these parting words the 1st couple of Scientology walk out of the gates towards Ellie~!’s Black Jeep Cherokee which reflects the sun right into the camera lens as the shot fades to Black.
