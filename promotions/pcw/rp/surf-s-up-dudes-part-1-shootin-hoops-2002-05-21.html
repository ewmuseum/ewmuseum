Surf's Up, Dudes - Part 1 - Shootin' Hoops

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 21 [The scene opens on the bouncing of an orange globe. It's remarkable how this globe bounces up and down, up and down with such a consistent rate. The black lines that appear throughout the ball seem almost hypnotizing with each bounce to the ground. A logo of a man dribbling a ball and the letters 'NBA' appear every now and then when the ball bounces] [Up and down is what can describe the PCW career of the Cowabunga Connection. They were brought in to make the tag team division a most bodacious place to be, and they did. After destroying every tag team in their wake and even making most of them quit or retire the tag division became scarce. Politically Incorrect made a dictatorship of the tag team titles and used the Ultimate Authority to hold them down. The Heartbreakers were all about PINK and at Supremacy they dethroned the evil dictators to win the tag titles, for only a short hour] [The entire roster, besides Big Alan Steele and that bogus dude Tyler Nelson, were fired. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry felt crushed inside, not really knowing if they'd ever make it back into the halls of PCW. The men felt they had let down their fans, let down their superiors, and most of all...let down themselves] [Then came the rebirth. The Cowabunga Connection, along with the other tag teams of the former PCW, were rehired! The brass of the PCW have given the most bodacious dudes a second chance to show how gnarly they truly are. With a Round Robin tag team title tournament around the corner the tubular dudes who kick major butt in a far out way are not going to drop the ball again] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: FOR THREE!!!! [The bouncing orange globe smacks against the rim of a basketball hoop] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Brick, man. [Lagusta Park. It's a totally radical park in the Los Angeles residential area. You've got tennis courts, basketball courts, gnarly playgrounds, and a most gigantic baseball field that can serve as a bodacious football playing area. The sun high in the sky signals that it is around noon time, with most people at work or in school. Tommy and Harry and really the only ones in the park and they are enjoying their solitude to play a little round ball] TTT: The sun was in my eyes, dude. ['Totally Tubular' Tommy wears a blue and yellow basketball jersey with the number 69 and the word 'Tubular' on the back. A pair of blue and yellow shorts accompany the jersey and a pair of black sandals] Harry: Michael Jordan has nothing on me, man. ['Heavy Metal' Harry wears a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots, which is his attire for everything that he does...no matter what. Harry shoots the ball in the air and that is all that he comes up with, AIR] TTT: HAHA! AIR BALL! Harry: Shut up, man. [The ball bounces to the fence and then its picked up by two little black mysterious hands. Tommy and Harry look on in dismay as the little black hands dribble the ball like lightning. The scene then zooms out to show a little black boy, about the age of 7] TTT: It's Chauncey, dude! [The little black boy wears a red bandana, a black Tu-Pac T-shirt, and a very baggy pair of jeans which almost fully cover his expensive looking basketball shoes] Chauncey: Heads up, YO! [He fires the basketball in a passing motion toward Harry. Due to Harry's love of weed his motor skills have suffered drastically and the basketball cracks Harry directly in the crotch] Harry: OOF, man! [Harry falls to the ground and writhes in agony. Chauncey trots over to the basketball and dribbles toward the hoop for a picture perfect lay up] TTT: Aren't you supposed to be in school, little compadre? Chauncey: Naw, man. I don't gots ta go to school, yo. I got my sh** together. TTT: THAT'S BOGUS!! You need to go to school to stop that HEINOUS swearing, dude. [Chauncey rolls his eyes and then holds his hand up in Tommy's face] Chauncey: Nigga, PLEASE!! I'll go to school and end up sounding like yo punk ass, FOO! [Tommy seems to be really upset now as he places his hands on his hips] TTT: We went over this before, Chauncey. My name isn't Fu, it's Tommy. [Chauncey dribbles the ball through his legs a couple of times] Chauncey: NAW, man! I'm not sayin' that's your name, FOO!! TTT: You just did it again, dude! You called me Fu. I'm not Asian. Chauncey: I know, foo. TTT: Stop, it's bogus. Chauncey: Whateva, foo. [Harry finally climbs to his feet with a large amount of pain still filling his face. He taps Chauncey on the shoulder to get his attention] Chauncey: Whatchoo want, foo. Harry: I...don't...like you, man. Chauncey: Tough sh**, foo! [Chauncey stuffs the basketball right back in Harry's crotch making him collapse again. Chauncey quickly rebounds the ball off of Harry's nuts and nails another perfect layup] Chauncey: Dat's how it's done, foo! TTT: That was totally heinous, Chauncey. Me and Harry have a round robin tournament to get ready for and if you keep hitting him in the nutsack, he'll never be ready, compadre. [Chauncey stops his dribbling and looks down at the motionless Harry, moaning like a little girl] Chauncey: It's his own damn fault that he got hit up in his balls. God damn, foo. [Chauncey goes back to dribbling and now seems to be interested in Tommy's upcoming matchup] Chauncey: Yo, man. What be up wit' you in PCW? Some tag team tournament and sh**? TTT: A most brutal tournament, dude. Our most lethal foes, Politically Incorrect, are in the mix and so are the last PCW tag champs, The Heartbreakers. Chauncey: Those dudes are gay, yo. TTT: TOTALLY! Chauncey: Any nigga runnin' up in pink should be burned in hell, foo. TTT: I'm not Fu. I'm Tommy. Chauncey: I KNOW, FOO!! TTT: Tommy. Chauncey: FOO!! TTT: Anyway...there are a bunch of these bogus new teams running around. We have to face one of them. The New Iconz. They spell their name with a Z, talk about most bogus grammar. See what happens when you don't go to school, compadre? [Chauncey whispers to himself] Chauncey: I see what happens when my momma be f**kin' chimps and sh**... TTT: What was that? Chauncey: Nothin', foo. TTT: Tommy....please... Chauncey: AIGHT, FOO!! [Harry begins rising to his knees now, his feet beat red with agonizing pain] TTT: Harry, this is a most gnarly beginning for us. Ultimate Authority used their BOGUS Authority to take away our Number one contenders shot away from us. The Heartbreakers get the shot and then they WIN! Talk about having the most radical luck in the world. Harry: Yeah....man.... TTT: The Heartbreakers owe us big time, compadre. Look what happened. We do nothing but totally dominate the tag team division and then those pink bogus dudes walk in and take the titles from us. I still stand by that we are the uncrowned Tag Team Champions of PCW! Harry: Pink..is not....far out, man.... [Chauncey is in the background now shooting jumpers and hitting nothing but net with each one] TTT: Dude, I'm just a little freaked out why that little compadre keeps thinking I'm an Asian guy named Fu. Harry: I think he's saying... FOOL, man. TTT: I'm an Asian man named Fool? That's even MORE BOGUS! [Harry shows a look of frustration which mixes with his pain] Harry: No, man. He's calling you a fool, not calling you Fu. TTT: I get it, dude. That little black dude thinks that I'm an Asian man named Fool. [Chauncey seems bored with nailing perfect jumpers and looks over to Harry] Chauncey: YO!! Set me up, FOO! TTT: NOW HE'S CALLING YOU FU, DUDE!!! Harry: Huh, man? [Chauncey passes the ball over to Harry, but Harry is looking over at Tommy and gets cracked in the crotch for a third time] Harry: ARGH, man!!!! TTT: BOGUS!!! [Chauncey begins laughing wildly as the scene then fades to black]
