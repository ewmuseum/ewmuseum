Title: Explaining Their Craft Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: TodayAgain The Halls of Justice. The finisher of Politically Incorrect. And the first three words of their theme, Metallica's '...And Justice For All'. But, beyond this, what does the term mean? When Powell sinks in his full nelson camel clutch, followed by Lovecraft's reverse crab, it, barring interference, signals the end of a contest. Still, the name strikes one as odd, as, despite being politicians, Politically Incorrect does not seem to stand for justice very often. Politically Incorrect, for a while, did not seem to be living up to their job. However, as of late, George W. Bush and Dick Cheney, the corporate president and vice president, fairly or not fairly, seem to be very much like Politically Incorrect indeed. Businessmen all over with political ties seem quite corrupt in today's society, a society of greed and corruption. Enron, Arthur Andersen, WorldCom. These are the names of but a few companies and firms caught up in the scandals that have rocked America. People have lost faith in our economy, and even now it struggles, doing its best to regain confidence and power, to return to the days of 10,000 Dow closings. Politically Incorrect is considered evil because they cheat to win wrestling matches. Politicians and businessmen are considered corrupt because they cheat people out of their life savings? Who, then, is the greater evil? Politically Incorrect say the politicians. And, as such, for the first time, are truly embarrassed of their profession. ::The WrestleVessel, while a nice ship for the wrestlers, also houses a large contigent of fans, looking forward to the Pay-Per-View on Sunday. The fans range from families, excited to be able to be on the ship with the wrestlers and meet and greet their heroes. One would expect men like Big Alan Steele, Miyagawa, and even DarkStar to be approached by fans. However, a young, seventeen year old boy is about to approach the tandem of Politically Incorrect.:: Boy: Umm, Mr. Lovecraft? :A surprised Lovecraft looks up from the copy of Forbes he is reading in his chaise lounge on the deck, not used to being approached by fans..:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Can I help you? Boy: I was wondering...could I have your autographs? ::He holds up a press photograph of the old Ultimate Authority. By the picture of Tim Walden is the former PCW jobber's signature. Despite himself, Reginald smiles, and pulls out a pen.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Where did you find Walden to get that signed? Boy: FSW show.. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ah...so, you're a fan? I didn't think we had many of those... ::Reginald finishes his signature with a flourish, as he passes the pen and picture to Powell, he signs, silently.:: Boy: I know, I don't understand why though.. William S. Powell: I do. :The boy turns to face The Master Debater, expecting an explanation, and one is forthcoming.: William S. Powell: It's the whole politician stigma. NO ONE likes politicians. Stereotypically, we lie, cheat, steal, and screw female interns. Well, maybe the last part's true...but still. For the most part, politicians go into the business because they care about the world, and want to change it for the better. And, there are those that suck at it majorly, and those that do well with it... ::Reginald smiles, as he's heard this speech before, and knows exactly where it is going to go.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: All politicians are 'corrupt' in some way. But, as long as their corruption doesn't hurt anyone else, nothing is wrong. However, when a politician or businessman is selfish enough to be corrupt in ways that harm others, that is as low as low can be.. :The boy smiles, a somewhat cynical look on his face.:: Boy: You know, organized crime used the same statement. A Chicago mobster differentiated graft as dirty graft, and 'honest' or clean graft. William S. Powell: I'll be damned...someone actually knows that! Boy: Well, I do read up on my criminal history every now and then..especially that time period... William S. Powell: I'm a city councilman from Des Moines, Iowa. I've done some things that would be corrupt, that people know about..yet, no one wants to impeach me, or kick me out, because I've done my honest to God best to help my small part of the world. It is something I am proud of, and something I greatly cherish. Reginald V. Lovecraft: If not for those scum, we could be proud to be politicians. But there are always those who cast a black eye on our profession. That is why we are booed, yet it is why we must also perservere against the likes of the Neo Void God, Pretty Cool Guys, and Heartbreakers. And it is why we will once again wear those titles we carry in your photo. ::Powell hands the picture back to the boy, as he thanks Politically Incorrect, and runs off. The two men sit in their chaise lounges, comparing notes briefly.: William S. Powell: Nice kid...nice to know we have some support.. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Does it really matter? We ARE the greatest tag team in this industry. Now, let's relax...we must be in top form.. ::The politicians kick back, and go into full 'Chillin'' mode, as we fade away...:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
