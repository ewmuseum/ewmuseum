Moving In

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/06/02 2.................... 3..................... 4 The Supreme Machine stands stone faced starring straight ahead watching the floor indicator tick over as the elevator makes it's way quickly and silently up towards the thirty second floor.  He is on his way to check out his new residence, two floors of prime New York City real estate located high above the bustling streets.  He is dressed in a light, loose fitting short sleeved black shirt that hangs down over the top of a pair of light blue denim jeans.  His long black hair is tied tightly back in a ponytail that hangs halfway down his back. 5................ 6................. 7 Beside him in the otherwise empty elevator stands Ramone Devereaux, an interior designer aged about fifty that was hired by The Supreme Machine's management company to decorate his  new abode.  Ramon is dressed in a tailored dark blue Versaci suit with a white shirt and matching Versaci neck tie.  His graying hair is coiffured immaculately and the sickly sweet smell of expensive aftershave wafts from him, filling the elevator to the obvious displeasure of The Supreme Machine. 8.................. 9.................10 As the two men stand in silence, Ramone takes the occasional glance at the huge man standing beside him.  He is obviously impressed with The Supreme Machine's chiseled  torso. 11............... 12............... So, where do you work out? 13............... The Supreme Machine looks down at the obviously effeminate interior designer. Sorry? 14.................. 15.............. Ramone becomes a little flustered but maintains his composure magnificently. I was just wondering where you work out.  You do work out right? 16............... 17............. No, I don't actually.  I'm just naturally built like this. Ramone raises his eye brows and his mouth opens in an over exaggerated show of surprise as he playfully slaps The Supreme Machine's left arm.  He gives the huge bicep the slightest of squeezes before pulling away. 18.................. Oh, you kidder!  I know you work out, I've seen the gym you've had set up, silly. 19.................. The Supreme Machine sighs softly to himself and goes back to watching the floor indicator ticking over.    19......... 20.............. 21.............. I've seen your ring too WHAT? 22.......... 23............... The ring you've had set up in your gym.  Are you a boxer? The CCW Hall of Famer takes a deep breath before turning back to face Ramone. I'm a wrestler. 24.............. 25................ Ramone's eye's widen in delight as he raises his right hand to cover his open mouth. Oh really?  How interesting.  What kind of wrestling do you do? 26.............. 27............... I wrestle for a company called PCW. 28..............29............... Ramone eyes The Supreme Machine up and down as he starts to breathe heavily. I'll have to come and check you out. 30............ 31.............. I don't think it would be your kind of thing. Oh, I'll try anything once.  How about you? 32 The elevator stops and the doors slide silently open.  The Supreme Machine lets out a sigh of relief as he quickly exits into a large white room.  Ramone Deveraux comes scampering along behind him. So this is your private foyer.  Now, you mustn't forget to use your key in the elevator.  That's the only way you can get it to stop on this floor. He hands The Supreme Machine a large silver key as he heads towards a solid looking wooden door set opposite the elevator.  Ramone takes another silver key out and unlocks the wooden door.  He hands the key to The Supreme Machine as he turns the knob and pushes the door open. And this is your key to the front door.  TA - DA! The interior designer steps through the door and waits for his client to take in the surroundings.  The Supreme Machine walks slowly through the door and looks around the room.  Huge windows showing the New York City skyline dominate one side of the massive space, while strange works of modern art adorn the other three sides of the white walled room.  The floors are of polished timber and The Supreme Machine's leather boots squeak on it as he crosses the room to inspect a Magritte painting. Is this an original? Ramone Deveraux smiles as he rushes across the room to stand by his client's side. But of course.  Nothing but the best for you is what I was told.  Spare no expense they said. The Supreme Machine turns away from the painting and looks around the rest of the room.  He notices two white doors that almost blend in to the walls.  Ramone sees where he is looking and points in that direction. They lead to the rest of this floor.  Your sleeping quarter's and gymnasium are upstairs.  Would you like me to show you? The five time CCW Heavyweight Champion notices a package sitting on a small table next to the door he had just walked through.  His boots squeak on the polished wooden floor as he walks towards it. No, I think I'll be able to find everything on my own thanks.. He reaches the small table an picks up the package.  Oh, you're getting mail already I see. Is it from...... your...... girlfriend? The Supreme Machine tears open the large yellow envelope and looks inside.  There is a video tape and some written notes.  He takes the tape out and places it down on the table. I don't have a girlfriend actually. Ramone Deveraux comes scampering across the room and stands next to The Supreme Machine, trying to read the notes that he is starting to read. Oh, really?  A big strong man like yourself?  I thought you would have had women throwing themselves at you. The Supreme Machine ignores him as he begins to read through the pages he had taken from the envelope.  It is the material he had requested from his agent regarding his first PCW opponent, The Multiple Man.  He quickly leafs through the pages and finds a photo of The Multiple Man that he looks at quickly before putting it down on the table.  Oh my God!  Who's that? Ramone Deveraux stands staring at the photo that The Supreme Machine had just put down. That's my opponent in my next match, The Multiple Man. Ramone picks up the photo and looks closely at The Multiple Man's pixilated head. Why his head looks like some sort of cubist nightmare! Oh my goodness!  He does have a nice body though. The Supreme Machine snatches the photograph from Ramone and puts it back on the table.  He turns to face the interior designer. Look, I have a lot of work to do before my next match, so I think I'd really like to be left alone now if that's OK. Ramone looks slightly hurt. But you haven't seen the rest of your new home yet! The Supreme Machine puts down the notes he was reading and begins escorting Ramone towards the wooden door that leads to the foyer. I think I'll be able to find my way around. He puts a hand on the interior designer's shoulder and gives him a gentle push in the desired direction. But I want to get your opinion on the wall hangings in the master bedroom! The Supreme Machine pushes Ramone's shoulder a little harder, forcing him to move. I'm sure they're great.  If there's a problem I'll let you know. But...... But.....  Ramone Deveraux slowly begins moving towards the foyer. But..... I haven't showed you the Portuguese tiles in the bathrooms! I'm sure I'll love them.. Now if you don't mind. He pushes Ramone gently through the wooden doors and towards the elevator. But.... But.... The Supreme Machine pushes the button to bring the elevator up to the thirty second floor. Look, I'm sure everything is just fine.  Thanks for all your effort but if you don't mind I really have a lot of work to do. But what about curtains?  You haven't told me what kind of curtains you want. The elevator arrives and the doors slide silently open behind Ramone.  The Supreme Machine pushes him gently into the elevator. I'll call you and let you know.  Now thanks again. He steps back as the doors begin to slide closed. You'll call me?  Really?  When? The Supreme Machine turns and begins to walk away from the elevator. OK.  Bye. When are you going to call?  Where are you wrestling next?  I'm free on the weekend! Finally the elevator doors close and The Supreme Machine is left in silence.  He shakes his head as he walks back into his new dwelling. Jesus Christ! He continues muttering as he picks up the contents of the package he received with the information on The Multiple Man.  He looks quickly at the photo and laughs. Cubist nightmare..... I can see that. The Supreme Machine takes a quick look around the huge room and sees an entertainment centre built into the white wall just near the Magritte.  He walks over and puts the video in the machine.  He hits play and goes and sits on the dark leather sofa as the television comes to life.  Now lets see just who I'm up against on Tuesday. The Multiple Man appears on the television screen as the scene fades to black.
