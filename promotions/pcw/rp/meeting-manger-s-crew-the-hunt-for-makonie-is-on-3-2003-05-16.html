Meeting Manger's Crew...The Hunt For Makonie Is On-3.

16 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/16/03 Fate is a funny thing sometimes…leaving you in places you never thought you would be, giving you no choice, but to follow the path that’s in front of you and remembering the path that is behind you. Remembering all the things that have led you to the point you are at now…your destiny. Some believe my destiny is to become the next PCW Raising Star Champion…they could be right, I am not going to disagree with them, it sure looks like that is what’s going to happen. As for it being my destiny I am not sure I can agree with that, because I don’t believe it to be so…it’s nothing more then fate that I am in right now. It’s only allowing me to take a step forward in my path. My destiny has long been since decided, from my birth…ever since I can remember, I was being trained to work for the government. Defending this country to the best of my abilities, getting nothing in return for it. I mean how do you give a medal to a man who doesn’t exist? Who does things that never happened? If I die, the Central Intelligence Agency is going to pass it off as if I was never a part of them. Secretly letting me be a hero, but outside a rebel. Not exactly the life people in vision for someone, but it’s the life I live and the life that I continue to live. Sure there are ways out…ways to make sure that my enemies and friends believe I am dead. I took that way out and now I am back…doing what I do best and I would pick nothing else. No one understands why I do the things that I do. Maybe they just don’t want to look at the horrible truth, or maybe they just fear what I am becoming…a mindless assassin. That’s not what I am because of…Haley. She took out the Chameleon inside of me, the assassin, letting me bury him in the back of my mind because I knew I didn’t need him anymore, because I had something else to live for…her. Now she is gone, taken from me and I have nothing to live for…except the Chameleon. *** Norticfest is already starting to take it’s toll on the young operative…after just a few hours, Matt is beginning to question if he made the right choice when he decided to join the skinheads and hunt down Mark Makonie…his enemy. The past few hours, have reaffirmed his hatred for the group who preaches white power, but he is going to continue on…looking for his princess. Walking slowly over the dead grass, Jackson lets his long brown trench coat hang on the ground, being careful not to step on it with his dress shoes. Next to him, is his nazi recruiter Tom Manger, who is dressed ever so sloppy in his sweaty white tee shirt and black jeans…the uniform for choice at Norticfest, being that every other skin head is dressed the same except for the Chameleon, who is wearing a white dress shirt and black dress pants…the other is the nazi that he believes to be Makonie. After Turmoil, Matt is glad that he spotted the man that is holding Haley hostage…knowing that he would soon be able to strike out against him and take back what is rightfully his…not a title or a win/loss record, but Haley Hilton, his princess…the one thing that he lives for. Every event in the past month has been triggered by his desire to make sure she stays safe…the undefeated record and dominate in the Raising Star division has been because of his love for her. As the duo continues to walk across the field, Jackson wipes the sweat off his angular face with his coat sleeve, feeling the hot LA sun beat down on the flesh coloured swimming cap that sits upon his head, to hide the fact, that he isn’t actually bald and saving the Chameleon’s under cover work. It may seem a little extreme, but he knows that it’s all going to pay off in the end…when he gets his target. Even with his mind swooshing back and forth…wondering if his decision is correct, he rests assured in the fact, that once he squeezes the trigger and Makonie dies, everything is going to be okay…everything is going to be back to normal. Everything is going to be acceptable once Makonie breathes his final breath…it’s the will of the Chameleon…his destiny. Standing side by side with this nazi, he knows it’s true…he isn’t putting himself through hell just for nothing, to come out empty handed. He would never allow it to happen like that…just like he won’t allow himself to come out of Supremacy without the Raising Star Title. He doesn’t want to think about PCW, about the gauntlet match, but in truth it’s been the only thing on his mind all day. He can’t seem to shake it even with his current surroundings and disgust for the people around him…every time it gets close to exiting out of his thoughts, something else forces it back into the fore front of his mind. Controlling his every thought, making the Chameleon’s masterful game of chess stagger a bit. He is too far a head in it; with a small bit of staggering…he is only going to slow down before he finally places Makonie in checkmate…into death. That’s just the way he plays and it’s just the way he wins. He doesn’t dare to question it or the Chameleon’s motives, because Matt Jackson isn’t a killer…he is a wrestler and that’s all he wants to be, but new times demand something else. I wish we could stop moving around…my body can’t take all this walking. Under normal circumstances, everything would be fine, but these aren’t normal. My body is beaten and torn from the captors and the punishment inside the ring…there is no sign of it getting better though, being triple booked is sure to add pain. At least isn’t an excuse to get out of here and get to Fusion though…I am not sure how much more of this nazi bullshit I can take. Letting a sigh out of his barrel of a chest Matt continues to follow closely behind the foul smelling nazi, trying to hold himself back from vomiting. He lets his hawk like brown eyes scan the area, trying to get his mind off of everything. Nothing comes of it though, as everything around him seems to remind him of PCW. Off to his left, the massive stage, reminds him of Society and how he believes he is going to make Jackson conform…which of course Matt knows is highly unlikely. Over by one of the tents are the two men the Chameleon just met a few hours ago. The hero for the cause, his face one of stone and his uniform the same as Manger’s, except for the blade attached to his belt…the blade that has slayed countless women. The man just continues to eye Matt, hoping that he is going to be lucky enough to use the blade on him, just like Stone Burn’s hopes to do. No such luck though, the Chameleon won’t die to such a meaningless person…weapon. Next to him is the American sniper that was kicked out of the Army for killing a Ukrainian. Believing that he can change the world and his past with a simple riffle…getting the skinheads message across with the pull of a trigger. Making Matt wonder if Krikorian thinks he can change the PCW with Society…knowing that in the end, they are just going to come up short because the only way to chance society is to do it for the right reasons. Growing impatient with the journey Jackson pipes up to Manger. “Where exactly are we going? I am starting to get a little tired, my body is beaten and I have to get too Fusion soon…to spread the message.” Nodding Manger looks down at Matt’s wrist and notices his silver Fossil watch. “Well it will just be a few more minutes, he is right over in that tent and I think you really will enjoy meeting him,” Manger says, picking up his pace a bit more for the impatient Jackson. Both men almost jogging across the field now, finally reach a medium sized tent, much smaller then the circus tents where the venders are, but still large enough to let Jackson stand to his full 5’8” height. Walking in, there is only one man in the tent; he is wearing a silver jacket and silver pants. Who is this guy? James Edwards of the skinheads? He probably has dreams just like James…hoping that he can further the movement, where James hopes that he can do the unthinkable to knock me off my throne…take the record away from me? Highly unlikely, I am not about to let it slip away now. The space looking man stands up and walks towards the men, his fat face lighting up like a lunar eclipse. He reaches out his hand towards the Chameleon who shakes it, believing the old man to be harmless. Letting go, the man quickly turns to Manger and salutes him, almost causing Matt to giggle as their flabby stomachs jiggle. “I am glad you are still here John…this is our newest recruit Simon. I just knew he was going to have to meet the only raciest we had in the space program,” Manger bellows out, laughing the whole time…letting Jackson get a view of his chipped yellow teeth, which forces the operative to cringe and makes him Run his petite hands over his white teeth, being glad that they are still in tact. “Yes, it’s a pleasure sir…I didn’t know there were any skinheads in NASA, but I am glad there are,” Matt lies, letting his eyes lock onto the pale green eyes of the retired astronaut. “Well someone had to keep those niggers out of space,” John tells him, letting his slender lips curl up. “Your damn right you did Mr. Edwards,” Manger hisses. “Stupid niggers shouldn’t have been let out of Africa. It’s all right though, because soon they are all going to be curb kicked and we will rejoice.” John Edwards? What the hell is this guy going to do, talk to my dead grandpa on the other side? God these are some weird people…I need to get out of here and get to Fusion, this is starting to become too much for me. The two men laugh, letting Matt have a minute to glance at his watch. Quickly looking back up at the men, he takes a step back and turns toward the door, causing both men to stop and stare at him. “I am sorry I have to cut this so short, but I have to head off to Fusion, but don’t worry…I will be back as soon as the shows over and we will get to meet further Mr. Edwards…maybe you can even tell me about NASA and how you guys pinned the Russians.” John nods, as Matt pushes the flap of the tent back and walks out onto the grassy field. The cool breeze instantly hitting his sweaty tan skin, looking around he does on final scan for Makonie, before beginning the sprint to his car. Damnit! He must have all ready left for Fusion…I have to find him and get Haley back…I have too.
