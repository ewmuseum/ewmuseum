Hubbardville the town that rocks, Malice Style!!!

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/7/02 -Duke is hanging out telling jokes behind the counter of his brand new hotdog stand in Hubbardville. It’s the grand opening of Hubbard Red Hot’s and the joint is jammed with Scientologists. The store walls are covered in Scientology memorabilia like Battlefield earth posters and L. Ron Hubbard photos. Pictures of Celebrities also fill the wall including a picture of Duke sending Xavior Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound.- Mayor: We’re honored to have you open up shop here in Hubbardville. Williams: Well after reading through some Hubbard’s transcripts I was ecstatic as hell when I saw the man himself fancied a good hot dog!! So I knew my mission was to make the only official hot dog of Scientology and give it the name of our great founder the Hubbard hotdog. Made with only Scientologist blessed lips and assholes of divine Scientologist herded bovine. Hey isn’t that what the Ultimate Authority is made of lips and assholes?? -The men share a hardy laugh.- Mayor: I can’t believe Kannon would stoop so low as to try to cheat one of his own kind. -Duke grins.- Williams: Yea and he’s hanging out with Nazi’s!! -The mayor damn near chokes on his Travolta Burger.- Mayor: A nazi?? They hate us and have been trying to oppress us for years. Williams: Yea Kannon gave this guy his job!! -The mayor makes a nasty face.- Mayor: That’s just not very Scientologist of him. That’s something a……a…….a……A CATHOLIC WOULD DO!!!!! -Duke pats the mayor on the back after delivering such a nasty insult towards Kannon.- Williams: It’s ok hoss, have a free bottle of Hot Hoss Hubbard BBQ Sauce. On the house. Mayor: Um Duke you truly are a saint this Hot Hoss Hubbard BBQ Sauce is the oral version of Battlefield earth. -The mayor grabs his bottle and moves on so Duke chats with the camera- Williams: So Kannon, it’s set in stone. You and I one on one at Supremacy for my Continental title. No more games, speeches, infiltration, just plain Malice. I see at Fusion you and I will be in the same ring in a massive tag match-up. Big Al and Big Duke versus you and Nelson and some other puds. Riddle me this Kannon. What does a meteorologist in a blizzard and Nelson in the bedroom with you have in common? -Duke sips down his cherry coke through a straw.- Williams: Give up?? Both are concerned about inches and how long it will last. -Duke laughs at his riddle for a few seconds.- Williams: Kannon I’ve been in this game for a long time and I know this match will be a wash. How long will it last and will I or anybody else be able to escape it with out injury right before the Pay-Per-View. Everybody in this match is looking to put somebody else in the hospital the night before the big show. You’re a sissy and a bitch and its time to take you punishment like a man. -A man walks up to the counter and interrupts Duke to place an order.- Resident: Let me get an order of your E-Meter enchiladas, some extra Hoss Hubbard Hot Sauce, a Duke Williams Malice Style 14 inch dog, and an order of your Courtney Love’s Cajun Fries. Williams: Ok is that all? Resident: And what’s the Kannon platter? Williams: A fruit dish. -Both men laugh.- Resident: Duke we’re happy to have you in town. We all know Scientologists are leagues above the rest of the population but Kannon flaunts it a little too much. Plus I think, he thinks, he’s not just an equal but better then us. Truth is we’re all equals in the eyes of Hubbard. Williams: That’s right my brother. Kannon is a heathen and a cancer to Scientology. I think I even saw him stealing funds out of the church collection plate. Resident: Really? I knew not to trust him. Williams: Yup, did you know Kannon is in a gang of corporate criminals that make the Enron executives look like Jim Henson’s Muppets. They employ a criminal, a child molesting CEO, a fowl mouthed personal director. These are Kannon’s peers. Complete dirt balls. Resident: My son will throw out his XK shirt when we get home in favor of a Duke Williams one. A man of principle and vision you are a true savior. -Duke hands the man his food as he grins on. He pulls off to the side and starts to whisper to the camera.- Williams: You see that Kannon, a Savior. I truly am a great man and nothing can stop me. GOD DAMN IT!! -Duke slices his finger on a paper sitting on the table. The little paper cut hurts like hell as he wipes the little dribble of blood off his finger.- Williams: Damn little things hurt like a bitch with nipple rings. XK ask your self this. Was it all worth it?? Sure you’ll get these people back and I’ll be on my way. But they will never look at you the same again. There will always be a nugget of distrust you will always have to deal with. I’ll be on my way with a new feud, a new pleasure hole, and a new title. You’ll be left picking up the pieces to your pathetic little life wishing you were half the man of the Malice Man. So again let me ask was it worth it?? Huh? Did you think you were going to out smart this cagey veteran?? DO you really believe Ellis when she says she’s never had it Malice Style in the back of the church last week under the Hubbard photo?? HUH?? Ellie do you really believe Kannon when he says he’s never had it Malice Style with Nelson under the desk in his office? Damn you kids are all sick in the head!! Big Alan Steele and myself will prove to the world Friday that the only difference between the Ultimate Authority and a box of sh*t is the box. Oh watch out!! Don’t step in it. The UA is going to be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound with a lot of help from on Big Alan Steele. -A man dollies in a crate with a big machine in it. Following him in is the mayor of Hubbardville. – Williams: What’s this? Mayor: It’s a gift from the city to Hubbardville Dogs!! Williams: I love gifts what do we have? -The Mayor has a smirk on his face.- Mayor: You know we Scientologists are superior in intellect and on the forefront of technology. Williams: Well of coarse! Mayor: One of our newest inventions is a virtual reality simulator. So real it’s amazing. Williams: Sounds cool as hell is it safe? -The mayor laughs- Mayor: Of coarse it is! Just the other day we let one of our test subjects use it for a test run in a Chicago simulation. He lived so it must be ok, now we’re putting it on the market. -Duke helps the mover set it up in a side corner.- Williams: I know Chicago like the back of my hand what’s this simulation about? -The Mayor’s smile shines bright.- Williams: What is it? Mayor: It’s a simulation of the Playboy Mansion!! Williams: Hot Damn!! This is wonderful! The hell with site seeing in the windy city I’m gonna beaver hunt in babeland!! -Duke has a very happy look on his face.- Williams: This town rocks, Malice Style!!! Mayor: Maybe we could adopt that as our slogan!! Hubbardville the town that rocks, Malice Style!!! Williams: It does have a certain ring to it! Mayor: I’ll get the sign maker on that right away…..hoss!! -Duke laughs as the mayor exits.- Williams: Who would have thought that a town in Wisconsin could ever rock malice style!! What a wonderful day in the world of Williams. Kannon how’s your day going? You and the mystery van still crossing this great land of ours stealing celebrities trying to impress us? Kannon you and I are brothers now. There is no need for all this fighting and jealousy. The Jan Brady bit is getting old, I know you can’t help it that I’m better then you. -Duke stops himself when he realizes he’s starting to sound like the rest of the Science boobs.- Williams: How may times do you need to be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound before you learn not to mess with Malice. Kannon I have the world on my side including your people. I have the Malice Crew backing me. I have Ellie staring at my package the whole match. What more do I need?? -Duke pauses and thinks over his rhetorical question.- Williams: The death of the UA and my Continental title would be a good start. Friday at Fusion is the last time you can hide behind your back up. This Sunday at Supremacy its Malice Time!!!! -Duke goes back to serving the lips and assholes to the Hubbardville community.-
