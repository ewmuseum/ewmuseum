Metal Gear Spoofage (part 1)

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/02/02 A door slams, echoing through a cavernous darkness. A sole figuresits.......he watches the new entrant to this room with a look of wearymirth. 'I should've known you were behind this, Colonel.' The shadow of the military man falls over him.......paces back and forth.His voice rings out......aged and gruff. Colonel: That's no way to treat an old war buddy Snake. I just invited youhere so we could talk....... The man leans his head back....remembers. We haze out to the same man, laying flat on his back in a run-downcabin. An empty bottle of Mad Dog rests in his hand.....around him apack of huskies flock, licking his bare beergut and generally acting likeanimals. Suddenly a knocking breaks the drunken stupor......angry voices shout. Soldier: Open up Snake, we know you're in there! Snake raises head briefly......then lets it fall again. Snake: Slag off! Soldier: Come on Snake, we're here to kidnap you and drag you aboard asubmarine headed for the Bering Sea so you can be dragged into a one mansuicide mission against nuclear armed terrorists! A brief mumbling ensues on the far side of the wooden door......finallyafter a conference the voice speaks again. Soldier: Ahem.....we mean we're here to sell you delicious Girl Scoutcookies Snake! Again Snake looks up, his eyes full of drunken suspicion. Snake: .........are they thin mints? Soldier: Yes Snake.....thin mints! The man stares blankly.....then stumbles to his feet. He makes his wayover to the door, swings it open. Snake: They better not be staleAGH!!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! The awaiting soldiers tazer Snake into submission.........he lurches,then falls flat on his face into the cold Alaskan snow. Snake: (Glaring) There were no COOKIES. Colonel: Sorry for the ruse but we've got a serious situation here. Snake watches his old friend carefully, then turns his head asanother enters the room. Snake: Who's this? Colonel: This is Dr. Bethany. She was sent here as our advisor onbiogenetics. Bethany: It's a pleasure to meet you Snake. Snake: Why do you have a syringe in your hand? And why is it filled with abubbling red liquid? Bethany: (Smiling) Don't worry. This won't hurt a bit. Snake: Is that smoke coming out of that thingAGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! The doctor injects Snake with the strange looking smokingchemical....the Colonel shakes his head. Colonel: Honestly Snake I'll never understand why you scream three timeswhenever the slightest injury befalls you. Snake: (Rubbing his arm) Shut up! That hurt. Colonel: Anyway.....we've called you here because the world is indanger. The high tech special forces unit GREYHOUND has commandeered anuclear weapon. Snake: GREYHOUND? I thought they were disbanded. As a matter of fact you'renot even a Colonel anymore from what I recall.....weren't you drummedout of the service for that crossdressing scandal? Colonel: Never mind that. GREYHOUND is your former unit.....and onethat I was a commander of. You know them better thananyone.......that's why we want you to sneak into the base, rescue thehostages they've taken and take them down. Snake: Why not call in the Army? The Navy? The Marines? The National Guard?The League of Women Voters? Colonel: Because we're not cruel, Snake. Snake: ....... Colonel: Anyway stop asking dumb questions. You're accepting this mission, like it or lump it. Snake: Why would I be stupid enough to do that? Colonel: Because you're the only one who can. Snake stares blankly at his former commanding officer.......finallyafter an eternity....... Snake: No deal. Colonel: Oh yes, that liquid Bethany injected you with is actually a retrovirus which is programmed to cause the cells of your testicles to explode upon our signal. Snake : ...................tell me what you know. And so a short time later a lone soldier finds himself propelled throughsub-zero Alaskan waters.......bubbles gurgle from his mouth as he yellsand attempts to guide the torpedo he was launched from. Finally he crashesscreaming into the wall of the base.......his dud missile shatters intoa million pieces. High up above a terrorist in a white jumpsuit paces......hearing theloud thud he looks about. Guard: Huh? What was that noise? The guard makes his way over, peers into the water.......watches asSnake's unconscious body floats up, landing face down on the shore. Guard: Hmph. With a shrug the terrorist makes his way away.......a loud ringing inSnake's ear stirs him. He groggily sits up, clutches his head. Colonel: Snake can you hear me? Snake: Uuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuugh. Colonel: This is no time to be screwing around. We just got word the boss ofthe terrorists shot down 18 F-16s with a Hind chopper! Snake: You're kidding. Colonel: Not only that but he delivered their bullet ridden severed heads toour sub! Our men found them in the mess hall in place of their usual slop. Snake: And how the hell did he manage that? Colonel: He's very tricky. Snake: Hm. Colonel: Snake, you've gotta rescue the Darpa Chief and President Baker! Snake: Why should I? Colonel: Um.......Bethany? Suddenly a terrible pain erupts from Snake's groin area......he doublesover in agony. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!! Guard: Huh? What was that noise? Once more the highly trained terrorist makes his way over.......kneels,staring at Snake who looks up at him teary eyed. Guard: (Shrugging) Hmph. Wacky marching music plays as our hero makes his way through the base. Pastterribly inept guards he strolls, finally making his way into the airducts.As he inches his way through once more his ear rings, causing him to slamhis head off the metal ceiling. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!! Snake's voice echoes through the airduct.........eventually emerging atan opening far away, where two guards on either side turn simultaneously. Guard: Huh? What was that noise? Guard: Hmph. Clutching his ear angrily, Snake snarls at his caller. Master: Snake, how are ya? Snake: Master? How did you get the frequency for my ear thingy? Master: I'm very tricky. Anyway I just thought I'd phone in to give youuseless advice about the environment. Did you know the average North Alaskanbark slug will secret at least 3 ounces of fluid in it's lifetime? Snake: Master, what does that have to do with anything? Master: The Alaskan husky emits a foul odor that while undetectable to bothhumans and 99% of the species on earth, drives away the fearsome Manuvianmonkey wasp without fail. Snake: Riiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiight. And so onward crawls our hero..........with Master spouting off uselessknowledge in his ear, still he pushes on. Eventually he reaches a smallcell.........drops in and stands face to face with a startled black man. Snake:.........you're the Darpa Chief........? Up to Snake runs the Chief, a fellow bearing more than a startlingresemblance to Asa Fountain. Darpa Chief: You gotta get me outta here man! Those fools are LOCO, bro! Snake: Whatchu talkin bout about Asa? Darpa Chief: They got a nuclear weapon man! And it ain't just no normalnuclear weapon.......it's a nuclear equipped walking battle tank! Snake: (Looking on) Metal Gear............ The warrior turns, holds his chin deep in thought. Meanwhile behind him theChief pulls out a large knife and advances slowly. Just as he preparesto stab though Snake turns around again......he hides it behind hisback. Snake: ..............what are you doing? Chief: Uh, nothing.....listen Snake, to launch the nuke they need twocodes. They've already got the first.......uh, mine. Snake: You talked? Chief: No....it's Kreller Mantis. He can look into the future andknow what you're going to do before you do it! Snake: More likely he just looks at the damned booking board. Chief: What? Snake: Nothing. Once more Snake turns around......behind him the Chief quickly sets apacket of C4 at Snake's feet. Snake: (Turning around) So how can I stop it....... Asa panics and quickly steps on the C4 attempting to hideit........unfortunately this sets the bomb off, disintegrating theChief and sending Snake flying from the cell. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!! Snake hits the far wall and comes to a painful stop on the floor. As he liesthere a soldier hesitantly walks up and points a rifle at his out cold head. Soldier: (Nervously) Don't move! Being knocked completely out, Snake obeys the order.....the soldiercontinues. Soldier: I said don't move......! Still no movement from Snake. Soldier: I'm no rookie! Snake continues his lesson in unconsciousness. Soldier: You talk too much. Suddenly an alarm sounds........the door to the hallway bursts open. Inrushes 6 well armed terrorists......the first in the line though tripsover Snake's carcass. The second trips over him.......thethird.....like dominoes they fall, and not five seconds into theintrusion all lie on the floor. Soldier: Hmph. The odd soldier walks away shaking her hips, to an elevator whereshe turns around. Snake finally raises his head and sees a bizarrefigure wrapped in leather floating above his erstwhile comrade. Suddenlyreality shifts........ Snake bears witness to an odd scene. Asa Fountain stands strapped to alarge torture device......around him a threesome watches. The man inleather.....an older man with a set of revolvers, and an incrediblyangry looking gentleman with a trenchcoat and a burned face. Liquid Clyde: You fool.......you've killed him! Hosselot: I'm sorry sir, I could have sworn he'd survive 6 bullets to thelung. I didn't count on his being such a wimp. Kreller Mantis: His shielding was very strong.......I could notdetermine his future booking. Liquid Clyde: Damn it all! Now how the hell will we launch the nuke? Kreller Mantis: Boss......I have a good idea......... Snake flashes back again just in time to see the soldier aiming her gun athim and fires. He dives out of the way.......the soldier runs for theelevator. She grins, and once more the figure in leather appears.He hisses at Snake from behind his gas mask........... Kreller Mantis: Good girl......just like that......yes, daddylike........ Suddenly both of them are gone.......Snake grips his ear. Snake: Bethany I just had some sort of hallucination. Is it that brown acid Itook back at Woodstock? Bethany: No Snake it was more than likely a mental feedback loop. Think of it aspsychometric interference. Snake: Psychawhasat? Bethany: I don't know, it's just a big word I made up to sound impressive. Leaveme alone. Bethany hangs up the connection........with a grunt Snake continuesonward. To the basement of the structure, where he demolishes a way toreveal an odd sight. A rather fat fellow stands strapped to apillar.....several brightly colored wires lead from him to a series oflumps clearly marked 'C4'. Snake slowly walks to the man and reaches out. Baker: Noooooooo.........don't touch it! Snake pulls his hand back at the last second, shocked. Snake: C4! Suddenly a bullet rings out.....flying straight over Snake's head,right through his hair and leaving a bald streak running through his mullet. Snake: (Slowly feeling his hair) You bastard! Out from behind the hostage steps the old man Snake had seen previously inhis hallucination. Hosselot: So you're the one the boss keeps talking about. I am....... Snake: I know.......you're Toby Keith right? Hosselot: Er.........no. I am Special Operations GREYHOUND, RevolverHosselot! Hosselot spins his gun around on his finger.....Snake looks on inadmiration. Snake: Toby Keith! Wow I've always wanted to meet you........can I getyour autograph? Hosselot: DIE! The gunman draws and fires.....Snake once more dives dramaticallyout of the way. He runs for the corner and clutches his ear. Snake: Colonel come in! I'm being shot at by Toby Keith! Colonel: .......if you say so Snake. Snake: Dammit! Why won't anyone believe me! Another bullet whizzes by Snake's head, interrupting his whining. He runsfor the far side of the room........Hosselot mocks him. Hosselot: Don't you want to settle this? Snake: I just want your autograph Mr. Keith! Finally Hosselot's bullets pin Snake to the wall......he presses flatagainst it as a series of gunshots draw an outline around him. Hosselot sneersand readies a final shot......then screams as a blur drops from abovesevering his hand at the wrist. Hosselot: MY HAND! The blur speeds around the room, severing the cables holdingPresident Baker and sends him tumbling to the ground. He meekly looks up asthe blur takes shape....... Baker: That exoskeleton! .........I've never seen it before in mylife! The exoskeleton materializes into the shape of a ninja. With a wicked lookingsword in it's hand it stalks towards Snake. Snake: Who are you? Akininja: We are like you...........we have no names. Snake: We? Akininja: AGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHH!!!!!!!!! The cyborg ninja begins shaking violently and screaming........it dropsto the floor and begins doing the Curly Shuffle. Finally itstands......makes three laps around the room, kicks Hosselot in the shinand vanishes. Hosselot: (Hopping away) We'll meet again! The gunman vanishes from sight.......Snake helps Baker to his feet. Snake: Can you talk? Did the terrorists get your code? Baker: Yes.......they did. Snake: Was it Kreller? Did he read into your booking? Baker: No......they........tortured it out of me. Snake: (Disgusted) You talked? Baker: I can't help it I'm ticklish......that sick bastard Hosselot brokeout the feather duster........ooooooooooooh......... Baker slinks to the ground in pain........Snake kicks the ground. Snake: Now they have both codes........they can launch anytime! Baker: There's a way to stop them........there's an override card.Insert it and the launch is kaput. Snake: Where is it? Baker: A soldier has it.......the one that wiggles her ass as she walks. Snake: Hm.......wiggle ass........ As Snake stands pondering ass, President Baker limps to his feet. Baker: Snake........there's something else I have to tell you. Snake: What? The President merely stands there.......stares blankly at Snake. Snake: Um........what is it? Still the President says nothing.......finally he begins hitting hischest with a closed fist. Baker: I should be dropping dead of a heart attack any second. For a full minute Snake merely watches Baker.......finally the old manscreams in pain. Baker: There it goes......ARGH!!!!!!!!! President Baker falls on his face......Snake kneels by him, takes hispulse. Snake: What the hell. To Be Continued
