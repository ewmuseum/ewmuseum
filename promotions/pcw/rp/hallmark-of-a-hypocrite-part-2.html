Title: Hallmark of a Hypocrite- Part 2 Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered The hush hangs over the library, hanging heavily in the air. The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid has just made a very serious charge. By this statement, he theorizes that Xavier Kannon is, in fact, not a Scientoloigist. The HIT has been well known for mind games in his career, kidnapping and drinking the blood of the wife of his now deceased foe, Samuel Potright. He kept all of PCW guessing in his role as the 10/18 Assailant. Are Trapezoid's charges merely mental warfare against the founder of Hubbardville? Yet......maybe they are not. Trapezoid referred to his charge as a theorem. A serious thing indeed, as the Sultan of Sides holds mathematics at a near sacred level. Can Trapezoid prove his theorem? Or will the Gold Patron Meritorious find a loophole in the postulate? We shall see.......for here comes the proof of Trapezoid's Fifth Theorem, otherwise known as? The Kannon Killer. Things are about to get downright PERSONAL. ::Fade back in to The HIT, perusing the printouts that he had made earlier. He looks up from his work, shuffling through the printouts...:: The HIT: 'Know thyself...and the truth shall set you free.' A quotation straight from the pen of L. Ron Hubbard in his essay 'My Philosophy'. Now, what does this have to do with my indictment of one Christian Xavier Kannon? It begins a series of statements from Mr. Hubbard that dictate the purposes of his philosophy of Scientology. :Moving to the next page, he continues reading.: The HIT: 'Therefore, in Scientology, we are not concerned with individual actions and differences. We are only concerned with how to show man how he can set himself free. This, of course, is not very popular with those who depend upon the slavery of others for their living or power. But it happens to be the only way I have found that really improves an individual’s life. Suppression and oppression are the basic causes of depression.' In essence, if Xavier WAS a Scientologist, he would be rolling over in his grave. :Placing the paper on the ground, Trapezoid shakes his head in a reproaching manner.:: The HIT: Hubbard essentially states that Scientology is unpopular with those who depend on others for their power.....namely, you. If Scientology is meant to combat depression, it therefore also combats supression and oppression. Both of these are tools of yours to degrade the PCW roster, to humble men such as myself, Rent-A-Hero, DarkStar and Teresa Tomas....but by furthering oppression, you also further depression, and thus stand against the religion you claim to support! :Trapezoid continues leafing through the information, coming to another interesting page.:: The HIT: Xavier, if L. Ron Hubbard were alive today, he'd probably be someone you'd oppress. He fought as a young man in World War II. Combat had left him both blinded, with optical nerve damage, and lame, with injuries to back and hip. He'd be someone you'd leave to die on a street, since we all can tell your contempt for the mentally and physically handicapped! But no, Hubbard refused to throw in the sponge. Despite his family abandoning him, further oppressing his existance, he worked back from his injuries, in a true story of mind over matter. And he lived to impart his theories to the world. If it had been up to you, however, you would have just let the man die.....let him live out his days in squalor, oh yes, and probably hit him with a disabled tax to boot. :Trapezoid sighs audibly, almost as if sick of sullying himself with all of the Scientology information.:: The HIT: This isn't to say I support the theories of Mr. Hubbard in the least. In fact, there's considerable evidence to show that he's a con artist, who copied his ideas from various other sources and used Dianetics and Scientology to bring in shit loads of money. Well, I've finally figured out why you have to tax so damn much. Being a Gold Patron Meritorious in the Church of Scientology would cost you a million annually. But, I got in contact with the financial department....and, it doesn't seem like you've dropped a cool mil on Scientology dues. So, where does all of this tax money go? THAT is the real question. I think I've done a sufficient job of demonstrating that you're not a follower of the Church of Hubbard. But, the above question is NOT answered. Lord knows how much money Walden taxes the wrestlers for, but HIT knows where the money goes! For, I have YET ANOTHER THEOREM for you Xavier. :Trapezoid begins rapidly scrawling on the back of one of the pieces of paper with a red pencil. He writes frantically, smiling as he finishes..: The HIT: Mr. Kannon....your ruin is emminent. For, with this theorem, the destruction of your credibility is completed. On Tuesday....in the second round of this tournament, you shall be defeated, and left laying in the center of the ring. And then, whether it be Mr. Jackson or Miss Wilson in the semifinals, it shall merely be a matter of destroying them, and advancing onto my FOURTH matchup at Alliances! And Alliances will be my day to shine! But for now....Kannon, it's time to go to war! I'M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I'M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I'M GONNA KICK YO' ASS! :Fade out, as the Trapezoid leaves, to go prepare a lesson on his new theorem...: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
