Every Story Has A Beginning

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 May 2002 Destiny....... Destiny is a word that gets misused allot, people believe destiny is there ultimate goal, what they strive to do, and once they have achieved their 'destiny' they believe their lives are fulfilled. However this is not the true meaning of destiny. You see many people have many different views of what the true definition of destiny is, but none of them come as close as The Dark Messiahs......... Now even though The Dark Messiahs may not be able to control their own destiny, they certainly know what awaits them in the coming future. The reason for this is simple. You see even though The Dark Messiahs can not change their destiny, they can change the way they approach it. Now I know what you are thinking, how is this possible??? Well quite simply it is because The Dark Messiahs can......... The scene opens up in a forest, where it is we do not know. There is a cold chill in the air, and the sky above is turning gray, it certainly isn't the best day for a stroll in the forest. Tall oak trees surround us. Now in any other light these trees would look like ordinary trees, but with the gray sky and the sun hiding behind a dark cloud the trees seemed to have been warped into fearsome monsters, which tower over us like they are ready to devour us. Suddenly a flash of lightening strikes the ground just about 5 feet away from us, this is quickly followed by the crack of thunder. As we look to the sky the heavens begin to open and drops of rain fall to the ground. The rain becomes heavier, large drops of rain splash onto the ground. Along with the sound of the rain, we hear a faint howling sound and screams. Suddenly a flash fills the screen....... The scene has now changed, we are still in the same forest and the rain is still pouring down but we now stand behind 2 boys. We are unable to see the boys' faces but judging from their height they are about the age of 13 or 14. Their clothes seemed ripped the shreds, the reason why their clothes are ripped to shreds stands in front of them on top of a rock.......it's a wolf!!!! The wolf is snarling bearing his blood soaked teeth to the boys. The wolf's eyes are cold and blank of emotion as it stares upon its next 2 victims. Both the wolf and the boys are soaked through, the wolf's fur dripping wet, as well the boy's hair and skin. Their surroundings are completely different from that of which we saw before. Even though there are still tall Oak trees present, the scene mostly consists of jagged rocks. The surface beneath their feet is mud, which now covers both the boys' uncovered feet. The boys breathe heavily, they have obviously been trying to run from this creature for quite a while, and now they were cornered, nowhere to run. The boys look over at each other showing us half of their faces. Something seems familiar about the faces, but what??? One of the boys has crystal blue eyes, and shaven black hair, he has a slim face. He has a cut down his cheek, that looks pretty deep, but it doesn't seem to bother the boy. This boy's eyes are full of fear, he knows that in a few seconds he could be dead. The other boy has deep green eyes, and cheek long black hair. His face like the other boy's is slim. This boy has a cut close to his eye, obviously a wound from the wolf. Both boys look at each other with dread in their eyes, they look towards the wolf and then at each other again. The boy with the blue eyes speaks first.......... Boy 1:(Scared) What do we do??? We can't go anywhere!!! Boy 2:(Scared) I don't know. Maybe we should accept it and just let the wolf kill us!!! Boy 1: But I don't want to die!!! Boy 2: Neither do I, but we don't have any other cho.............. Before the boy can finish the wolf has pounced and leaps towards the boys, but the boys are able to move out of the way just in time. Now the boys stand either side of the wolf circling it carefully. The wolf snarled and growled causing both to jump back slightly, they knew this could be it for them, but the adrenaline was bumping through their veins, even at this young age they seemed to know what to do. Suddenly both boys pounced onto the wolf. The wolf was not expecting this and howled in disbelief. Both boys with all their might began to pound away at the wolf, this did not effect the wolf one bit and it jerked it's back to throw both boys off. Both boys landed on their backs, but quickly tried to scramble up, the green eyed boy got to his feet, but before the blue eyed boy could the wolf had pounced and was on his chest pushing him down. The wolf let out a howl and a growl as it stares down. Both the boy and the wolf's eyes are locked and for a split second there seems to be a silent peace between the boy and wolf as they stare into each other's eye. The wolf’s head diving at the boy’s neck soon breaks this silence. The boy closed his eyes in hope that it would be quick and painless. However the strike did not come as the boy opened his eyes to seen the other boy trying to wrestle the wolf to the ground. These boys weren't giving up without a fight. The blue eyed boy, suddenly got a second rush, and charged in to help. As he came running in he flew at the wolf's head with a dropkick. The impact sent the wolf to the ground yelping in pain, but the boys were finished there, they had got a taste for it. In a flash both boys grabbed a loose rock and held them high and brought them crashing down on the wolf's head and torso. The jagged edges of the rocks pierce the wolf's skin, and blood burst from its body splattering both boys. The blood continues to flow as the boys continue to beat away at the now dead corpse of the wolf. Something has snapped in these boys’ heads, no longer are they beating on the wolf for survival but for the pleasure of it. Their eyes are now cold and blank like the wolf's. Suddenly there is a rustle in the trees, probably a squirrel of some sort, but it is enough to bring the boys out of themselves. Both drop their stones and sit back looking at their now blood soaked hands. They look from their hands and then to the wolf, which lies there with blood still pouring from it's cuts. They look up at each other, and a sick smile seems to cross their faces. The blue eyed boy stretches out his hand towards the other........... Boy 1: My name is Daniel Steel by the way. Boy 2: (Shakes Steel's hand) Mike Stone, pleased to meet you. Daniel Steel: (Releases from handshake and smiles) So what you doing out in the forest all on your own??? Mike Stone:(Smiles back) Looking for answers. Daniel Steel: (Confused) What do you mean 'answers'??? Mike Stone:(Smirks) I think you already know what I mean. Stone looks up at Steel, and Steel looks back. They seem to stare at each other for a few moments, until Steel's eyes widen in realisation. Daniel Steel:(Excited) You mean, your looking for....... Mike Stone:(Interrupting) Yep, I’m looking for them as well. Daniel Steel: Do you know where we can find them??? Mike Stone: I have an idea, but The Dark Lords are hard to find. I got a tip off that they live somewhere in this forest....... Daniel Steel:(Serious) The Dark Lords have been around for centuries, they move from place to place, how can you be so sure they are here??? Mike Stone: (Smirks again) Because........ Before Stone can finish there is another rustle in the trees. We look over to see what had caused the commotion, but as we do there is another sound in the direction of Stone and Steel, it sounds like some sort of gun silencer. We quickly snap round to where Stone and Steel were, but they are gone!!! The scene fades to black leaving the smell of wet leaves and blood in our noses........ The scene comes back now in a warehouse. The sunlight streams through the windows placed at the top of the steel warehouse walls. Even though we are inside there is still a cold chill in the air. This chill isn't because of the weather outside but because of the atmosphere. The smell of the warehouse is similar to that of the forest, even though there is no wet leaves there is certainly the smell of blood. This warehouse has obviously been witnessed to some violent acts. The floor beneath our feet is hard concrete and as we step forward our footsteps echo around the warehouse. Now everything in this warehouse seems to be normal, apart from the 2 tables placed in the centre. What are they doing there??? We are about to find out as two men walk into view. Both men are cloaked hiding all their face and features. The cloak they wear is similar to that of which we have seen the Dark Messiahs wear, but these are not just black robes, they also have a golden stripe going down the middle. One of the cloaked men holds some sort of large book, which we are unable to see clear enough to actually make out what it is. The posture of the men seems very strong and tells us that these men carry a lot of power. As both men approach the tables, the cloaked man holding the book throws it on the table. We are now able to see the title of the book, which is, 'The Dark Lord's Ritual.' This tells us these men are in fact The Dark Lords that we heard Stone and Steel speak of earlier. The cloaked figure checks the book is ok, before turning to the other cloaked figure........ Cloaked Figure:(Confused) Master Wolf, I am confused, why have we chosen these 2 boys??? Surely they are too young. Master Wolf:(Sick Smile) You see my apprentice that is where you are wrong. You see even though these boys may look young, they have the courage and strength that surpasses any of ours. I mean you saw what happened in the forest, these boys stared fear directly in the face, and beat the crap out of it. Now those boys could have died back there if they had chosen too but they didn't and that's why they will make the perfect vessel for Gorak. Apprentice:(Giving In) You are right my master I apologise for questioning your judgement. Master Wolf: Think nothing of it my apprentice. Now let us continue. (Turning from apprentice) Bring them forwards!!!! Suddenly a huge crowd of cloaked figures comes marching through the warehouse doors, chanting something in a strange language. Like Master Wolf and the apprentice the faces of the cloaked men can not be seen and the baggy ropes hide their figures. On top of the crowd of cloaked figures they carry the unconscious Steel and Stone. Master Wolf smiles evilly at the sight of the 2 boys. He turns back to the apprentice....... Master Wolf:(Excited) This is it my apprentice, we have finally found the chosen. The apprentice smiles back evilly as the men place both Steel and Stone on the wooden tables. The atmosphere that now fills the warehouse is now one of darkness. This is the sort of atmosphere where you know some evil magic is going to take place. A few of the cloaked men secure Steel and Stone to the tables, and join the rest of the men who have made a circle around the tables. Master Wolf stands in between the 2 boys and looks around the circle of his fellow Dark Lords. Another sick smile crosses his face as he addresses them...... Master Wolf: My fellow lords, today before you, you see our chosen. Many years we have searched for 2 men who could carry the voices of our Lord Demon Gorak, and I do believe our search is finally over......... There is a cheer from the circle of Dark Lords as Master Wolf continues...... Master Wolf: Now let us waist no more time, and let us begin the ritual. There is a mumble of agreement from the Dark Lords as they begin the chant and sway back and forth. Master Wolf looks over to the apprentice who is in awe of what is going on. Master Wolf:(Angry whisper) Apprentice.......The Book!!!! The apprentice quickly turns around to see Master Wolf's angry glare and quickly grabs the book and moves over to the side of Master Wolf. Master Wolf now stands at the heads of both Steel and Stone. He then nods to the apprentice who opens the book and starts to speak in that strange language we heard before...... Apprentice: Gorak, calla sic boram tha...... As the Apprentice continues to read Master Wolf translates into English raising his arms into the air..... Master Wolf: Gorak, lord of lords, hear my words......Accept these 2 as your vessels!!! Their minds are your minds, their souls are your souls, their life is your life!!! Gorak here my words, let the darkness come and let the evil reign, accept these 2 as your warriors. Let them hear your words, and let them learn the ways of evil!!! Gorak hear my words........ From the inside of his cloak Master Wolf pulls out 2 daggers and places them on Steel's and Stone's chest. He continues the incantation....... Master Wolf:......Their blood is your blood, and as their blood spills let it run with yours. Come to us, our master, and our lord!!! You are free at last!!!! With that Master Wolf pushes hard down on the daggers and cuts across the boy's chest!!!! As he does this Steel's and Stone's eyes shoot wide open and they scream in pain, as the scene fades to black!!! Well another twist in the tale of The Dark Messiahs, what to the torment souls have in store for us next time??? Tune in to find out!!!
