Legends of the NES: Duck Hunt! (Part 1)

13 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Legends of the NES: Duck Hunt! (Part 1) Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: 11/13 After a near-death experience, it is unlikely that the Trapezoid will be driving his bus again. But, he needs SOMETHING to pass the time on the long road to Alliances. He needs something, and he has found something. In the form of three little letters. NES. Yes, the Nintendo Entertainment System, now an 8-bit antique for some, but for others, the height of gaming. Trapezoid picked it up cheap at a yard sale in England, during PCW's time there. Now, he puts it to use, along with the games he had bought. Although, I think even Trapezoid will be surprised with the parallels he sees... Let's join him, shall we? :The NES sits plugged into a jack in the HIT's hotel room. Next to it, flanking it on both sides, are nice refreshing cans of Mountain Dew. Hooking the controller into the system is none other than the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid himself. However, into the other slot, he is plugging in another device. An odd device, yet one familiar to long time NES fans....:: The HIT: Well, I've almost managed to kill myself AND one of my students in a yellow school bus in Belgium. And, quite honestly, I'm feeling pretty pissed off right now! I need to release some anger...and, I'm going to do it with the help of my trusty NES system! There is the possibility of a fourth match at Alliances for yours truly....for the PCW Cruiserweight Title. This, of course, assumes I can get through the tournament.....and the first round of that is against Syko... :Trapezoid smiles, as he selects a cartridge, sliding it into the system.:: The HIT: But, it is not Syko that I am most afraid of. It is his accomplish! The entity that ATE THE VOID, and the next PCW World Heavyweight Champion. Yes.....THE DUCK! Now, I realize that, in a fair fight, I stand no chance at all against the duck's awesomeness. But...I looked through this collection of games, and I realized, that there IS one that can help me! It is one that will teach me how to DESTROY the most powerful entity in PCW! ::As the Archbishop of Angles slides the power button to “on”, the television screen depicts the title screen of one of the all-time Nintendo classics….:: The HIT: Destroying a duck is not an easy thing to do, you know! I’ll have an easier time defeating the Pranksters, Front Office, and Playaz Inc at Alliances than killing that damned pesky duck! I’d rather be a one man team in the Alliances match itself than have to face the duck untrained! But, this game will teach me the ways of duck killing! Who says video games aren’t educational? ::The words “Duck Hunt” are written across the screen.:: The HIT: YES! DUCK HUNT! One of my favorite games of all time! It shall help me in the one way that exists for destroying an evil, psychotic, Void-eating duck! Forget hiring The Chef to make me some duck confit! Well, I guess I always COULD do that, BUT NOT UNTIL I HAVE BLASTED THE EVIL CRITTER SEVERAL TIMES WITH A LIGHT-GUN WITH A TRIGGER THAT CAN BE HEARD FOUR STATES AWAY! ::HIT cackles, a slow, long cackle as he sizes up his menu options. He is presented with three options: “1 Duck, 2 Ducks, and Clay Shooting.” The HIT: Hmmmm..Clay Shooting? Why would they have Clay Shooting in a game called DUCK HUNT? No, it’s definitely the ducks I want! But two ducks at once? That’s too severe of a task! Best I start with one duck… ::He selects one duck, as the familiar duckhound runs across the bottom of the screen. Trapezoid reaches for his weapon, holding it out in front of him, execution style. The HIT: YOU’RE ALL GONNA DIE, DUCKS! The first duck goes flying onto the screen. Trapezoid fires the Zapper rapidly, or, at least as rapidly as the piece of shit firearm will allow him to fire it. He misses narrowly several times, causing the duck to fly away, and the duckhound to pop up, laughing. The HIT: WHAT?! HOW DARE YOU MOCK ME, YOU STUPID DOG! That damn dog must be the Duck Hunt equivalent of Syko! A lazy, good for nothing jackass that only pops up when his duck does something good! GRRR! I HATE THAT SYKO! AND I REALLY HATE THAT DAMNED DOG! ::As most players of Duck Hunt probably have at some point, The HIT takes the gun, and wildly fires, missing another duck. As the dog pops up, Trapezoid tries in desperation to shoot the duck, but unfortunately, it doesn't go away. As the next duck is about to swoop in, HIT presses the barrel of the Zapper against the tv screen, and fires. Due to pixel issues, the gun drops the duck.:: The HIT: HAH! TAKE THAT, MOTHER DUCKER! ::He continues firing, taking down the remaining ducks, as he busts out the infamous Trapezoid Dance: The HIT: YEAH! I KILLED THE DUCK! I'M A BAD MAN, DAMNIT! :Trapezoid continues dancing around in a ridiculous circle, and then turns off the game system. He unplugs the Zapper, wrapping its cord around the barrel of the gun, and then holsters it in his pocket.:: The HIT: MY DUCK GUN WORKS! I wonder if I can modify it for use on chickens like Rent-A-Hero and Tim Walden...but for now, I must focus on Syko and his duck. For us....this is about pride. The PCW Continental Champion versus the PCW Television Champion. Two men who, to my knowledge, have held the Cruiserweight Title before. But now....even Syko'd agree with me that this tournament makes it mean just that little bit more! The best athletes in this company....hell, the winner of our match takes on either PURE's best luchador or the owner of this company! Pride is on the line....and the winner of this tournament can make a very legitimate claim to being the greatest athlete in PCW. And Syko......good though you may be, I'm better. :Trapezoid turns, to head out the door of the room....: The HIT: Were this a Continental Title matchup, I might give you the edge..but we're fighting in a match down my alley. It's a one on one match....and, while I'm known best for being a Hardcore son of a bitch, I'm a damn good technical star as well. And, the Goblin's going to find that out....first hand. Syko.....time draws near. I'm not going to beat you down into any grass, however. I'm going to do it one, two, three in the center of the ring! ::HIT smiles, pulling the gun out of his pocket, as he walks out the door...fade to pencils.:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
