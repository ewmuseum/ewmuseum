Harry's Decision - Part 1 - Ragamuffin Revisited

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13, 2003 [At Los Angeles Mental Ward there are people there that would strike absolute fear into the mind of the sane. In fact, most of the inmates of the Mental Ward truly believe that they are sane and that the sane are the ones who are truly insane. Do they really see those imaginary people they talk to? Is it just a show so that they don't have to live out on the streets anymore? Or are these men just so truly dangerous that it's a blessing they are kept in their straight jackets and rubber rooms? There is one man in these hallowed halls that has a connection to PCW's Continental Champion, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, and that man is Randy Raggagu who is also known as...Ragamuffin. The professional skateboarder with the ugly Italian face and thick black Italian hair who has had a vendetta for Tommy for over a year now. Ever since Tommy used Ragamuffin as the brunt of all his jokes at parties it's a fire that has been burning inside Randy for so long. It was only recently that he's found some closure on that part of his life and has moved on to other things. Even though Tommy did trick him into thinking that Shailyn was in love with him and sent him back to the mental ward, Randy found a way in his heart to TRULY forgive the Tubular One for the follie of his ways] ['Heavy Metal' Harry stands in the visitors waiting room looking a bit anxious. His brown long hair is pulled back into a perfect pony tail with no hair sticking up. He's in a sky blue dress shirt with the top button open to his masculine chest. He wears a pair of navy blue dress pants and black dress shoes. In fact, at first glance it hardly even resembles The Heavy Metal One anymore, it's like someone else took over. Actually if one thinks about it someone else DID take over Harry's body...or maybe he just got a piece of himself back. Being in a stoned haze for 13 years can eject a certain personality trait and install another. Harry is proving that theory quite well as he taps his foot and just does a lot of sighing] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE!! This is totally bodacious!! [Walking from the outside lobby is the man of the hour, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. He wears a dark blue silk dress shirt that is unbuttoned to show off his bodacious body. He's looking down at his glistening abs and chest as he walks into the waiting room. The very attractive secretary behind the desk seems to be interested in the stud that has just walked into the room. She keeps her beautiful green eyes pasted on him to see what the hunk of beef is up to] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Sitting in this waiting room, wanting to talk to an insane person? No, this is NOT bodacious. In fact, this is the opposite of bodacious... Tommy: Bogus. Harry: ...yes, it's bogus. Your the one who dug the grave with Ragamuffin, not me. All this crap about me getting kidnapped and put into the crazy schemes of getting back at him...it just seems so ABSURD! No wonder the guy is stuck in a place like this, you probably drove him absolutely crazy. [Tommy has a smile across his face that could easily reach a mile wide. He's proud of the insults he hurled at Ragamuffin, after all he is a super villain] Tommy: It was most rad the way I started calling him Ragamuffin. You know...I wasn't the one to start doing it in the first place. It was YOU if I remember that totally gnarly day correctly. Harry: Well, if I did then I did. I know how to handle things the adult way and I'm going to do it right here and right now. Hopefully I'll be able to rekindle everything that you did so, in case he does get out, he won't come back after either of us. Especially me, considering I don't remember doing half the crap to him. [Tommy just shrugs and looks back down to his abs. His nostrils are flaring like crazy as if he's trying to smell something like a dog. Harry sort of hunches downward to look at Tommy's abs to see what the big deal is. He doesn't really notice one, except the nice odor that is coming off of his body. It's like a fresh spring morning mixed with the scent of Christmas trees. It actually is a very nice smell] Tommy: I smell bodacious, don't I? Harry: I have to agree with you on that one. Tommy: It's axe. Harry: Axe? Tommy: The name of the body spray is called Axe and it's a guarentee that you'll get some bodacious babes. Not that I NEED a guarentee because I can already do that, but it's nice to sometimes not have to work so hard. Just let the scent do all the talking. [Harry just has a blank look on his face] Harry: Right. Tommy: I'm gonna go hang out by the elevator and test this stuff out. [Harry just looks confused now] Harry: Why do you need to stand beside an elevator? Tommy: Dude, that's what they did in the commercial. Dude is all on an elevator and the babe stops it and they GET IT ON! I've done that once and I'd like to do it again, it's most radical. Harry: Well, good luck... [As Tommy walks away the secretary behind the desk smiles up at Harry] Secretary: Mr. Raggagu is out of his cell and he's ready to see you now. Harry: Thank you. [Bells ring and buzzers buzz loudly as the large metal door slides open to let Harry through. He travels down a long hallway and as he gets further down he can hear all the screaming and talking going on. 'HELP ME!', 'They are trying to eat my heart!', 'I am God', and 'Please kill me!' fill Harry's ears as he tries to find the right room. All he notices are that there are offices with guards in them and they are wearing headphones...obviously to drown out the horrible screaming filling the hallway. Harry stumbles to the end of the hallway, just in front of a line of bars and it seems he's found the correct room] Ragamuffin: Is that you, Harry? [Randy used to look like one of the skinny rails he used to grind on, but he's really filled out since then. His face looks fuller and he's not as rat faced as he used to be. He cut all of his hair off and it's pretty much like a crew cut, which REALLY does a lot more for the guy. It seems he's completely changed every aspect about his physical appearance except for the orange jumpsuit that he has to wear since he's an inmate and all. Harry slowly walks into the room sort of regretting his coming, but it's all part of his decision] Harry: It's me, Randy. If you ARE Randy. [The thin man lets out a small chuckle and he smiles probably for the first time Harry has seen since that day at the beach house when he was with Shailyn] Randy: I'm Randy, all right. Different in body and mind. Now it looks to me that you have also done the same for yourself. I'd have thought you were some male model in those department store ads. Haha! [Harry nods and smiles as he looks over his own wardrobe. Harry then slides one of the plastic chairs over to him and takes a seat across from Ragamuffin who isn't restrained at all. A lone guard stands in the corner of the room just looking straight ahead trying to make it seem like he's not even there. Harry notices the shock stick on his belt and the HUGE biceps the man has. If anything goes down Harry will be thankful of this man] Harry: I'm just going to cut right to the chase. I've come to a decision to leave California and pursue things on my own. About three weeks ago I began to discover things about myself that I never knew existed. The world started making a little more sense and a future started to unfold before my eyes. I felt that before I venture on my journey that I'd stop to the people I've affected over the years and help put some past behind us because I may never get a second chance to do so. [Randy just has a smirk on his face the whole time that Harry was talking. It's kind of weird because Harry doesn't know if it's good or bad really. Should he be frightened that Randy is going to leap over the table and smash his face into the floor? Or she he just keep his cool and just think that maybe Randy is happy for Harry moving on] Randy: You stopped smoking weed, didn't you? [Harry just audibly laughs] Harry: Yes, Yes I did. Randy: That's the best move I've ever seen you make since I've known you. Harry: I have to agree. Randy: What does Tommy have to say about all of this? I bet he's beside himself that your leaving. Harry: He laid out one of those really crappy guilt trips on me. It would make a child feel guilty but no one with an intelligence over a seven year old. But I had to give him effort for at least wanting me around so I gave him the benefit of the doubt and told him I'd stay until March Madness was over. Randy: He'll try to cook something up to keep you longer. Harry: I know, I read the plans already. [Randy laughs] Randy: You mean that he wrote things down?! Harry: Drew them actually. For some reason he drew a cow, a catapult, and a bunch of naked women. Does that make any sense to you? Randy: Sort of. Harry: How so? Randy: That he got into your stash and was toking up. HAHA! [The two men just share a good laugh together. Harry looks up and into the dark eyes of Ragamuffin wondering exactly what was so HORRIBLE about the guy. What was it that Tommy didn't like? Maybe it could be that inner kindness that Randy possesses and his will to attract the ladies with it. Maybe Tommy was just jealous that Randy was going to steal his thunder so he did what any male would do, he insulted and belittled the guy until everyone viewed him as some little nothing. That's just ashame...] Harry: I am truly sorry for what's happened to you though... Randy: Sorry? Your sorry for me being in a mental institution? Harry: I feel I'm sort of responsible for pushing you over the edge, even though I don't really recall most of it. Randy: Well, neither do I to be honest. I just know that this place is the best thing to ever happen to me. Harry: What?! Randy: I'm serious, Har. Ever since they put me back in here I've really found myself more than I'll ever know. I know what I am and why I've been put on this earth. I'm here to help people, the ones who are in dire need of some help. The lost and confused of the world. The people behind these walls really need some extra help and I'm the one to do it. Harry: Well, by the screaming it seems like your fighting a lost cause. Randy: You have a point but sometimes you have to take a few steps back to really leap forward, Har. It's only a few of them that are so deep into their delusions, but with those guys means two more people I've helped through their dark times. Harry: And you don't ever dream of getting out of here? Randy: Everyday. Don't get me wrong...I want to be on the outside world again, but for now there is so much more I can do here right now. My time will come. [Harry just stares at Randy and smiles. It's amazing how strong the human spirit can truly be after rough times. It seemed that Randy would have gone completely mad and just never have snapped out of it, but he kept strong. Tommy could take a few lessons from Randy, but he'll save that for another day] Harry: Well, I've really gotta get going, Randy. Tommy has the pay per view coming up and he needs to prepare as much as possible. Randy: I know. A huge title defense and a battle royal for the heavyweight title is something VERY big. I never thought I'd see him get so far in so little time. I am proud of him for getting this far and hopefully he'll get further. If he could just open his eyes to the world around him and stop trying to convince himself of different things then he'll TRULY succeed in life. Harry: I couldn't have said it better. Thanks, Randy. [The two men share a strong handshake and Harry slowly backs out of the room. The hallway isn't filled with as many screams as before, or maybe it's just that Harry doesn't notice them as much. Who knows. As Harry makes it back to the waiting room he notices that the secretary isn't there any longer. Maybe she went on a lunch break or something. As Harry turns the corner of the lobby and to the main elevator that's when he puts two and two together....] Harry: Tommy, you MORON! [The elevator door opens as if on command and Tommy is smiling like a boy on Christmas as the beautiful blonde secretary is buttoning up her blouse] Tommy: That Aerosmith song is TOTALLY RIGHTEOUS, Har! LOVE IN AN ELEVATOR!! Harry: Of course. We've got work to do, Mr. Axe. Secretary: Call me! [The door closes and the scene fades to black]
