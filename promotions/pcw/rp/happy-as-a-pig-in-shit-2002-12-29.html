Happy As a Pig in Shit

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29.12.02 Happy as a pig in shit?Happy as a baby suckin’ tit?Or grumpy as a Redneck’s DWI?Head hung low, but pride still high. Abner’s been through all that crap,From beer, to bars, to baseball bats.He’s used ‘em all, shot ‘em up,From Kannon’s car, to the warden’s truck. Wielding shotgun in hand, empty space in brain,The Rednecks here to play a game.Kannon, Gaz and all the Front,RUSH, and Chet and even the Monk. Decimation came and Decimation passed,And what we saw was kickin’ ass!Duke, and Tomas, ‘n Abner too,Winnin’ matches, n' drinkin’ da booze! But then come Friday, pale and gray,Bastard, Deamont, ruined the day.But rest assured, hippie will pay,The Rednecks come through, and the shotgun leads the way. Adrenaline’s next, then comes Fusion,Will we be there, or will we be out boozin’?Who’s to say, and who’s to care,One things for sure: we won’t be loosin’! Abner clears his throat and steps down from the podium. Looking out into the crowd, he notices his fellow Rednecks rejoicing in the corner. Tomas bends over and pulls Duke into her chest as the tears flow from his eyes. Red-eyed from either drunkenness, happiness, or maybe even both, it makes no difference to Abner. Pride fills his heart, as his Boss, his Hoss, Duke Williams is pleased by his poetry. Abner’s done his job. “That was…..” sniff, sniff, “…..so beautiful!” Duke sobs as Tomas turns back to Abner, nodding her head in approval. “Ya done well Abner, I reckon you done well…..” wiping a tear from her own eye. The rest of the audience seems less impressed though. Looking out into the masses of long-haired hippies, gray-bearded English professors, and a clique of “casually” dressed neo-yuppies, Abner notes that their facial expressions are different than that of his friend’s. Some are shocked, some in disbelief, but most ignoring the reciting, taking Abner as a joke. But it’s not funny……it’s heart-felt. The mic squeals as feedback loops back through the PA system. Tapping the mic with his left hand, Abner ducks down and hops off the stage. “Pssst, Abner!” Abner’s eyes light up as he looks back toward Tomas. Facing the crowd again, he bends over and majestically bows. The crowd doesn’t know what to make of it. Eyeing one another quizzically, the audience shrugs. But after the first clap breaks through the silence, others follow the trend. And soon, they’re all clapping for Abner, who stands back grinning in delight. “Thanky, y’all…..thanky!” Abner walks back up to the podium and verbalizes his appreciation through the mic. Looking to his boss, a bouncer in the stage corner slowly moves forward, nudging Abner aside. He cautiously motions for the bigger man to leave, as the next speaker is prepared to speak. “Oh, sorry”, Abner whispers at a short-haired geek, awating to read his piece. The geek breaks lets out a nervous smile before waving shyly at the Redneck, carelessly bumping into the bouncer. “Hey…..” “Oh…..sorry sir.” A few moments later, Abner’s back over in the company of his friends. Content with his success, he feels that he’s left a positive impression on Dartmouth’s scholarly poets. Smart people, Abner reckons that they “done liked it”. “Yep….” Abner nods his head. “That was one fine, mighty perdy poem there Ab…..” Duke sniffles. And then, looking back up to his rookie DRA, “But lets blow this joint like a negro and git are-selves some nice, cheap alc-o-hol!” “Yeah Abner, great p………YEE-HAW!” Tomas lets out a whoop. Abner shrugs. “Okay.” And so heading out the door, the three Rednecks are off into the streets of Hanover, NH, looking for the closest, cheapest state liquor store!
