Overhearing...

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13 August 2002 The god and the devil go against the sinners… Usually it’s the devil who provokes sinners to sin, and then the god punishes the sinners…or forgives them. Not this time. Akiyama has never really made solid contact with Tyler Nelson or Xavier Kannon. The three of them just didn’t have much to say to each other. What, is the god going to talk about the value of the yen to the CEO…or ask Kannon if Ellie’s going to the Hollywood premiere? No…the two of them have their own little bubbles they live in…and Akiyama’s just the needle to pop them out of their grandeur. But Nelson…he’s getting quite close to being enlightened. He and his Dark Knights seem to be getting more and more enlightened each day…and it hasn’t gone unnoticed. But Kannon…yeah, he constantly puts himself into the sinner’s throne. He does whatever he can to make sure that he holds all the cards, that he is on top of the game, that he becomes the king of the world. Turmoil was no exception…he nearly started a riot there with his words about the history of PCW being obsolete now in this day and age. He said he would perform a killing blow for the sake of the roster and the sake of PCW… …end Tyler Nelson… Last time the Divine Prophetess remembered…Xavier Kannon wasn’t the savior of PCW. And if he was so set on saving this place for the sake of the future…then the savior’s going to have a hard time getting past the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. What a nice game that’s unfolding here before us all…the dark ones are the heroes where the normal, popular ones are the greater evil…yet all are sinners in the eyes of the god. But of course the devil is the greatest sinner of them all…for he wished to defy the god…and for his trouble was banished from the heavens into the fiery wreckage that is his hell. And in this day and age…the sinners are growing on the rise…and now the god and the devil must join forces for this one time…to fight two of the biggest sinners ever to set foot into PCW. Words alone almost brought about entire chaos that night….the quaint night coincidentally called Turmoil… Almost…is an understatement. “WE NEED MORE OFFICIALS OUT HERE NOW!!” The backstage was pandemonium at that point, everyone running in all directions for some strange reason. Wrestlers had their bags packed and were flooding for the parking lots. Referees were running outside while road agents were on their phones calling in police. The voices of the fans outside became one collective…a humming roar of anger, hatred, and bile for one man…it sounded like the moans of hell itself… But to the Divine Prophetess…it was like music to her ears… “You hear that lovely sound, Akiyama?” she asked the god, who stood beside her with his arms folded at his chest. The two of them stood in the hallways amidst the chaos of panicking staff, more in the way than out of the way. Cyclonica tilted her head back and listened more closely to the wave of animosity being harvested by the fans. “That sound…is the sound of the End Times approaching.” She raised her arms slowly, her ice blue fingers lightly dancing across the air. “Such hate…such odium…the melody just makes me want to dance.” She swings her arm back in a surge, being taken over by the hatred of the people outside. Her razor nails slash across the throat of a nearby road agent, and with a gag and a crimson spray, he falls to the ground. Akiyama looks down at the bleeding mortal and shrugs. Cyclonica holds her blood tipped finger to her lip, an apologetic look on her face. “Whoopsie.” Her attention is taken away from the soon dead man to the voice echoing from the outside. “THEY are the men you WORSHIP?!?!? It’s the fact that they are PCW legends that I want to win this fucking company and mold it in my vision... It isn’t an accident that they all fucked off to OSW you know, after spending over a year surrounded by such peons as you lot I’d want to work somewhere else!” Cyclonica’s face drops into a cold stare. “Oh…great…Kannon’s bleating again about PCW.” she looked up at the god, who listened intently. “I find it sad that even as the champion he still gets no respect. That’s just proof that he’s not worthy of that title.” “What do you want? You want MEANSTREAK to come out and At Death’s Door me?” The eyebrow of the nVg rises at the mention of Streak. The Divine Prophetess lets a small smirk out from the corner of her lips. “Meanstreak isn’t here…why don’t you go out there and give him the next best thing? He’ll find the ADA to be just as potent as the ADD. Who should fear a monster anyway when there’s a god striking terror into the minds, bodies, and souls of humanity?” A platoon of officers come rushing past the two Celestials on their way to the outside. They continue on listening to the PCW champ address the people. “I amSICK AND FUCKING TIRED of having the history of this place shoved down my throat EVERY DAMN DAY..... I AM THE CHAMPION... ME.... XAVIER KANNON!!!” “Not for d@mn long…” the cold Celestial muttered, her icy bitterness coming out at full force suddenly. Just then, Tiffany Jennings comes rushing by with her purse in hand. She’s dressed in a white halter top, and black skintight blue jeans and black boots. She looks around in a panic as the fans outside grow more and more volatile. “It’s anarchy out there!!” she shrieked, her breath heavy. “They want Kannon’s blood!” “People in hell want ice water too…” Cyclonica responded curtly. “Fact is, they’re not getting it.” “What are you talking about?? Kannon’s stirring them into a frenzy!! If the rest of the security doesn’t get here soon, we’re talking full blown RIOT!” Cyclonica’s face didn’t go from emotionless. “And…where’s the point in your talking? Riot means one less PCW Champ to worry about…then Akiyama can shove his fist down some flat-scan’s throat and take what belongs to him. If we really wanted this to end, we’d go out there and end it ourselves. You think those fans will be to eager to kick Kannon’s ass if Akiyama goes out there and does it first?” Tiffany looked on with a pleading face. “They WHY don’t you go out there and stop this madness??” She holds her hand out before her, looking at her nails nonchalantly. “Meh…we’re not very font of you mortals…what do we care if you kill each other? We’re going to kill you all eventually…saves us the trouble.” “Ugh! You’re hopeless!!” Tiffany storms off down the hall, turning back at the end of the hallway to give them a final word. “I’ll just go find someone who CAN be a MAN!!” She turned around…and fell down the flight of stairs with a yelp. “Watch that first step, human…” Cyclonica blows on her nails and sighed. “Why be a man anyway when you can be a GOD?” Turning back to the Kannon Rant outside… “I AM WHO YOU SHOULD BE TALKING ABOUT!!! I AM A PCW ICON.. I HAVE NOT LEFT YOU!!!! YET I STILL HAVE TO HEAR EVERY FUCKING DAY ABOUT “THE GOOD OLD DAYS“... GOOD?!?! GOOD?!?!?!” “Good lord…he’s still at it. If he keeps up, he may just get mauled by those morons out there.” “THEY ARE HISTORY.. JUST THAT... AND HISTORY IS THERE TO BE FORGOTTEN AND COME THIRD GLORY... WHEN PCW BECOMES MINE.... PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING WILL START A NEW CHAPTER IN IT’S HISTORY.... NO KREMMENS, NO DOUG TROYS, NO REVEREND JOHN HULLS, NO TRASHCAN MANS... NO TYLER NELSON... THE ONLY THING IN IT WILL BEME!” A low growl comes from within Akiyama, his eyes going jagged at Kannon’s words. Cyclonica only sighs, brushing her hands off. “Yeah…I know what you mean…I want to shut him up too. But we’ll have to wait until Friday…then we can lay waste to him and El Diablo. Tyler Nelson just better stay out of our way.” “Tyler.... I ACCEPT your challenge on behalf of every PCW wrestler who has had to sit through people comparing us to the old guard... Who’ve had to listen as people reminisced about early days, about how it was...” “Enough… let’s go Akiyama.” Cyclonica turns on her heels and heads for the back door. Akiyama keeps his eyes locked towards the outside for a few stalling seconds before following Cyclonica. “His ideals are sound, but his motives are twisted. Yeah, it’s time for the next generation of PCW…and the history must be destroyed to move in the future….but in HIS eyes, only he will benefit from this new rise. We’re not going to stand by while he tries to shape PCW into his image…no frickin way. If that happens, we’ll have John Travolta and Tom Cruise giving color commentary and Jenna Elfman announcing. Greed is his ultimate goal…and in the end he will end up a shell of what he used to be…traveling on the same path as his enemy now…then these events will unfold yet again…with another rising star taking his place. Well no more!” She stopped and looked back at the Neo Void God, glaring up into his eyes. “We end the cycle here and now…with Kannon. We’re going to keep that future from happening…then later we can destroy the universe….give or take a few years. First thing we do…instill the FEAR OF GOD into him on Fusion…Next, we break him and take his gold.” Akiyama nods himself, his eyes growing fierce with anger. Cyclonica stabs her icy glare into his own eyes harder, never wavering, and never faltering. “So says the Divine Prophetess…” And so it starts…the ones who are Celestial going on to stop the sinner Kannon and the Devil El Diablo…two men in this place who have no respect for the god…and denies his own fear of the omnipotent. But Akiyama’s not doing this for the people…screw the people. He’s doing this to remind Xavier Kannon that he’s nothing more than a flat-scan human with a crappy human belief as his meal ticket. To keep this worm from blaspheming about being a god…he would have to learn now that there’s only one god to exist within PCW…and he should bow down to him and beg for mercy… There is no m e r c y however… …Everyone receives their Absolute Destiny Apocalypse…sooner or later…
