XD -is still giggling from those B.S. manipulation segments- ~*~ Fade in... The sound of a contented sigh. “Mmm...Dig those palms deeper…No, no, no! Palms, I said palms! Not your nails!! Didn’t anyone ever teach you how to massage? ...Mm, much, much better...” The camera pans out to reveal a small mattress, sitting in an area right by a Jacuzzi. Anarchy lies, her face to the side, her head resting on her folded arms, on a towel. Her eyes are closed, and a small, contented half-smile forms on her indigo lips. Her long, curly blue hair is at its full, glossy, prismatic glory. It’s practically glowing in the light, contrary to its blood-soaked consistency as previously seen. Anarchy smirks, opening an opalescent violet eye. “When are those massage therapists going to be over? I need a real professional...no offense to you...” The camera pans back to reveal Jenna straddled on Anarchy’s back, massaging it carefully. Anarchy closes her eyes once more, awaiting a response her from her blonde pamper. Jenna shrugs and looks down at the blue-haired Asylum member. ”Um...I’m not sure. They said they’d send ‘em over soon...and since when are you so into massages?!” To that response, Anarchy opens one pale blue-purple eye and glares up at Jenna. She emits a low, feral growl, and with a single motion, gets up and sweeps Jenna off her back. The honey-haired slave of Anarchy is sent sprawling to the floor with a cry. Anarchy slowly gets off the elevated massage table, cracking her neck. She turns to look down at Jenna, who lies on her back, slowly attempting to get back up. Anarchy mumbles something about the producers wanting to see more ‘Lesbo action’, and curses them under her breath. “Well, I don’t know, you mindless little bimbo...could it be just last week on Fusion I was hung like a Bloody Popsicle from a meat hook by Cyclonica? And I need a bit of healing for these stiff, thawing muscles?!” Jenna slowly gets up, backing away from Anarchy. She looks hesitant, knowing saying anything to Anarchy in this state is done while walking on thin ice. “Well, you did a pretty good job defeating Brandi on Turmoil for her title, you know?” “Do I look like I’m doing so fucking dandy now? Of course I’m healing—but even with your ignorant, limited knowledge of wrestling—you should be able to tell I wasn’t looking so hot in my match! I’m doing fat better now, but fuck it—what the Hell is wrong with wanting a goddamned massage?!” “Nothing...Jesus, Anarchy, I didn’t say there was—” “Good. Then shut your trap.” “O-kay—” Anarchy levels Jenna with a glare that finally shuts her up. “Anyways...wasn’t what Clyde and Bethany did to Bobby Stonecutter hilarious?!” Jenna winces. “Eh...actually, I think it was kind of—no, I know it was REALLY sickening!” Anarchy arcs a blue eyebrow. “Girl, you have no sense of humor—” “Humor?!?!? “—Or vengeance. I think he deserved it, that annoying little prick. Freakin’ dickslap...” “Are you even allowed to say ‘dickslap’ on TV?” Jenna laughs. “You could be fined...” Anarchy rolls her eyes. “Do I look like I care?” “...No...” “Alright then.” ...After an uncomfortable silence... “Soo...I hear your booked on Turmoil...against Teresa Tomas and Cecelia Verucana... Not to mention, with you teaming with that sicko Cyclonica!!” Anarchy glowers at Jenna, and cocks an eyebrow. “’Sicko’?!” “Yes, ‘sicko’!! I’m sure your memory goes as far back as last Friday, when Cyclonica bled you like a stuck pig—” The sickening crack of the heel of Anarchy’s palm connecting with Jenna’s jaw echoes throughout the room. Jenna cries out, and falls to the floor once more. “So now I’m a ‘stuck pig’, eh, you little cunt?!”, spits the blue haired goddess. “If you ever dare disrespect me like that again, Jenna, I will personally disembowel you like a ’stuck pig’! Fucking whore...and you dare think your opinion of Cyclonica holds any significance to me?!” Jenna doesn’t reply, she only turns away, holding the part of her face that Anarchy hit. “Fuck it... Whatever, I’m goddamned thirsty. Go get me a drink.” “What do you want me to get you?” Anarchy smirks. “A Blue Frost Gatorade.” “Okay...” Jenna gets up with an angry sigh, and wipes blood from her mouth. She walks out, cursing and muttering “Temper, temper...” Luckily for her, Anarchy is too busy staring off into the distance with a smirk to bother hearing her complaints. The blue-attired female lights up a Newport Medium lazily, and takes a long drag of it. She pops the lighter back into her pocket. Anarchy shakes her head with a smile, as she climbs back onto the massage table. She rolls carefully on her back, staring up at the ceiling. Her eyes sparkle with amusement as she speaks. “Tim Walden...what the Hell have you gotten yourself into...” Before she is to finish...if she even had any further warning to convey, Jenna walks back in with a 20 oz. bottle of Blue Frost Gatorade...for the Ice Angel herself. Ignoring Jenna for a long moment, Anarchy takes another drag of her cigarette. She pulls the smoke into her lungs intricately, and then exhales the smoke in a donut-shapes puff. Jenna eyes the cigarette with a disgusted look, and notes the ring print of dark lipstick on the cigarette. Anarchy seems to finally notice Jenna. She takes the Gatorade from her calmly, popping open the bottle, and pours the ice blue liquid through her lips. Jenna sits back down uncertainly, and finally begins to talk once more. “The Gatorade’s good enough for Anarchy?” Anarchy only smirks, her temper not flaring for once. “Yes, Jenna...it’s just cold enough...” “Uh, that’s good... So...um, what’s your opinion of Teresa Tomas and Cecilia Verucana?” “Eh...Teresa’s definitely one tough cookie. Besides streaking that hair black, all Shakira-eque, she’s definitely talent to watch out for...” “Mm...Shakira’s not all that bad...” Anarchy takes another small puff of her cigarette. After exhaling, she finishes off the Gatorade, and tosses the bottle over her shoulder casually. “Yea, the girl’s mad hot and all... But anyways, I haven’t seen as much of Verucana. But from what I’ve seen of her, she’s definitely no Romana. Thank God for her... I’m just basically looking for some good competition with them two... I know they won’t disappoint me. But all the same, there is no doubt in my mind that the...’Divine Prophetess’ and I will pull out a win...” “Dude, how do you put up with Cyclonica? Didn’t she just drain you of pints of blood and all? I mean, no offense, but...still!! It’s kinda sick!” “That is none of your concern. Sure, we’re not like your average petty little playground rivals or something...we don’t mind getting a bit bloody... But she’s...tolerable. I hope for our sake that Cyclonica will be willing to work with me for the match... ‘Cause for some reason, I think she’d rather work against me. But if we lose, I’d rather it be because Cecilia or Teresa pins one of us, not because Cyclonica’s not willing to completely cooperate with me.” Anarchy puts out her cigarette on a small ashtray on the little coffee table next to the massage temples. “Oh...right. oh, I think I heard somewhere that you’re booked on Fusion.” “Hmm? I am? Against who?” “Um...I’m not sure. These four dudes—” Anarchy glares at Jenna with a look that says “Gee, that’s so fucking helpful”. Jenna shrugs back helplessly. “Anarchy!!”, she whines. “You know I hate wrestling!! I mean, all these guys look the same to me...the only people I really know are the Asylum, the Front Office, that burned sicko dude with the little girl, Cyclonica, and Aki...Akira...Akima...Ayika—?” Anarchy rolls her eyes. “Akiyama. Get it right.” “Right. But otherwise, I’m like, ‘what’?, you know??” Anarchy snickers softly. “Girl, sometimes you sound so much like Ellie~!, its scary...Must be a blonde thing.” “Hey!! And what?! I wasn’t even talking like a valley girl...” “Never mind...” Jenna makes a face, and turns to Anarchy again. “Oh! Oh yea...I forgot to mention...” “Hmm?” The blue haired female arcs a matching blue eyebrow. Jenna bites her lip and nods. “Well, its kind of a match where you have to like, defend your Cruiserweight Belt?” Anarchy’s expression goes slack with the idea, and then blank, and then she smirks. “Eh, they’re just testing me. As in...’Look, Anarchy, you’ve had this title for a week. Now that you’ve gotten a little taste of the gold, it’s time to lose it again. Mm, now let’s see who you should job to’—” Anarchy is interrupted by her phone ringing. She rolls her violet orbs in annoyance, and pulls out her small silver and indigo cell phone from her pocket, and answers. She walks out of the room for privacy. The camera cuts back to Jenna for a bit, who sits there on the floor, twirling her honey-colored locks around her fingers. A minute later, Anarchy walks back in with a smirk. “...That was just Daemon. We just...had a talk, and he told me who my opponents for Friday are. Let’s see...I’ve got Celt, Tomas, and Loc. Woo. If they think I’m going to break under the pressure of my first title defense and job...meh. But the matter that concerns me at this current moment is my match for Turmoil...and win we will...” Anarchy tucks her cell phone back into the pocket of her jeans. She leans against the massage table, brushing a lock of azure curls from her face. She looks somehow exotic; even in her plain choice of clothes—a baggy dark blue hooded shirt that practically covers the faded blue denim shorts that adorn her long toned, tanned legs. Anarchy leans into the camera with a smirk on her midnight blue lips. “Teresa...Cecelia... Pin this, bitch.” Fade out...~*~
