Solitary Confinement: The Woes of a Skitzo

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-10-02 Skitzo walked along the quiet streets of Brunei, Brunei Darussalam admiring the culture and the ways of this people. Tuesday night he would tag with Syko against the Culinary Cunt himself, Chef and Kramer Wild Wing. Thinking to himself he wondered if anyone really knew this was going to be a handicap 3 vs. 2 match. Skitzo works in mysterious ways, as was showcased at the Mall Brawl…THE SAME MALL BRAWL HE HELPED SYKO TO WIN HIS CONTINENTAL TITLE IN!!! Tuesday night wasn’t a problem; Skitzo could rely on his partner for help, albeit Syko was most of the time getting a boner over the Asian/European/Eastern chicks or wherever the fuck they’re from, in the crowd. Friday night on Fusion – gawd how Skitzo loves to analyse his matches and opponents – he would face the might of Semore Cocks. A schizophrenic versus a pothead has many implications, such as the pothead getting the shit kicked out of him through his nose by said Schizoid. If a man that has split personalities can help a Goblin to beat a Redneck Goth wannabe, by god he can whoop the tar out of a guy that snorts drugs imported through some guy’s ass. Skitzo’s head flooded with more and more thoughts, though they never seemed to show through as he admired the stalls selling little trinkets and shit, the kind of stuff this place is famous for. Skitzo tittered at the people he knew nothing of, glad to finally be out of Japan and on the way to another battle. Wrestling was of course the thing that keeps Skitzo in perfect balance with sanity and maturity………or maybe just sanity. Skitzo: I’m in corner shop heaven… Skitzo: Half of these bastards aren’t even clean; look at ‘em pawning away those goods for a few measly-…erm…whatever the fuck the currency is here… Skitzo: For talking sake let’s say it’s manure… Skitzo: Manure works for me… Skitzo turned down into a small alley, to find two people trading what appeared to be spices. Taking a deep breath, Skitzo coughed and spluttered at the sickening smells that entered through his nose. Skitzo: Goddamnit! Everything smells like shit! Skitzo: Easy tiger…I happened to notice one thing after the Mall Brawl… Skitzo: That we pissed off Chef? Skitzo: Who’s the #1 Contender for the Television Title? Skitzo: WH-WH-WH-WHAT?!?!?!? Skitzo: If you won that, we’d be Champions of all Television land! Skitzo: Keep your mind on the fights that matter boys… Skitzo: Who said that? Skitzo: Me… Skitzo: Oh…shut up then… Skitzo gave himself a soft slap across the face before taking up a small flight of stairs and stopping before the street ahead. Everything was cramped and claustrophobic. Skitzo backed off suddenly back down the stairs, white flashes suddenly blasting across his field of vision as he delved back into his past. March 23rd, 2002--------------------- …I don’t know if anyone can here me…I pray some day I will see the light will shine in through my window…I have been in confinement for what feels like years…though word from my fellow asylumni claim it has been thirty nights and fourteen hours since they put me in here…I just want to get out of here…I have barely enough room to stretch my legs in here…yet it feels like I’m being watched…like there’s someone else in here…though I am the only one… The asylum was quiet most all the time. William Williams had been admitted originally for having pulled all his teeth out apparently. That was a lie. Anyone can say they ripped out their hair or spiked their fingernails, but William was admitted for more sinister reasons. The claims of a madman running wild in the woods at night were haunting people all over the bayou; children and animals alike were kept back from this beast within the comfort of their own homes. It only took one child for everything to go wrong, for while the authorities spent weeks and a half looking for the “man who talks to himself”, the man himself was hiding out in a quiet cove, comforting the body of a lifeless 8-year old who had followed him unbeknownst of his mental state, and ended up falling foul of the nature plunder that is a swamp. Drowned and in the arms of a madman…how would that go down with the police? Surely he would be committed for a dastardly act such as murder…it must have been so, for seven months later William Williams found himself in solitary confinement…his sixth week on the program to restore his sanity…or perhaps even kill the beast within forever. Solitary confinement can do a lot to someone. For some men, they are slowly but surely brought back to reality via an unstable mental process, while other simply go mad with boredom, and either masturbated until they bled or tried to escape. Williams could feel the many, many imprints on the walls around him from where the nails had dug in, and even been ripped right out of the finger. He did not want to die for what he was, so he did what no one else had done before…and made up a friend. We all make up our own little imaginary friends to talk to in times of need, but for a grown man in his sixth week of solitary confinement to do so, and for the following year, the extreme circumstances surrounding him would mean things could only get worse for the poor guy. Which they did. Although he seemed OK when he was released from the asylum, little did he know what the consequences would be, not only from the constant conversations he had with himself when no one else was around, but being trapped inside a space 6ft x 1ft for weeks on end with only a tube to drink water through and 12hrs exercise time from midnight in a private yard can do some weird things to a man already far from fucked up. “The Birdman of Alcatraz” had his feathered friends to keep him busy…Skitzo had fuck all except his own thoughts, a few songs he could remember, and an urge to rub one off…yet even though Skitzo was a Cajun raised poorly, he had manners and was a gentlemen when the time called for it, so he would wait until he got out to relieve the urge in whatever way he could…that was legal of course. So basically, a year’s worth of solitary confinement ended in Skitzo becoming just that, and gaining a fear or phobia or reluctance to small spaces. What relevance that has to the fact Skitzo and Syko must vanquish the might of Chicken Killaz Inc. or the fact Semore Smoke is a dead Virginian walking…and Skitzo’s the one holding the high powered rifle one will never know, but in any case everyone learned a little bit about Skitzo’s past today…and if that won’t do for a time when Skitzo is walking around another foreign place with fuck all to do…well…I GOT TWO WORDS FOR YA………………… “The End”
