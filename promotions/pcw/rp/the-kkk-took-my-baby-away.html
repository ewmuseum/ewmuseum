The KKK Took My Baby Away

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered She has such little eyes, but they are much larger than the last time that we saw them. Blue as ocean diamonds - just like her old man. Snow stares down at his growing child and silently asks himself where the time went... When she was born - August of 2001 - the phenom that we now know as the Killin’ Villain, Jason Snow, was still on the rise. August, however, was long before he would become the three time PCW Champion of the World, as he is now - controversial or not. The champ reaches into the tiny carriage where his nine-month old, giggling, daughter. Her hair has grown thick and blond - much like her fathers’. It’s also fairly curly, which is a characteristic not found in the Killin’ Villain, but adorable in the little girl. Despite the curls, she does resemble her father very much. And when Snow looks down at her, we see something beyond his massive ego... if only for a moment... Snow: “Maybe it wasn’t such a bad thing that Turmoil and Fusion were cancelled...” Strange words from the Killin’ Villain, and not laced with the usual sarcasm or self-promotion. When in the presence of this young female, the man we know and hate as the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World turns to butter and melts all over our screen. It’s almost hard to hate him at times like these... Snow: “Even though I would LOVE to have a chance to beat the living...” Snow hesitates as his eyes are drawn to the side of the carriage where a note has been left by his loving, yet controlling, wife: “NO SWEARING NEAR THE BABY.” Snow takes a deep breath and grits his teeth... Snow: “...heck... out of Big Alan Steele and Tyler Nelson prior to our meeting at Supremacy - an event tailor made for myself.” Snow sighs again. Our shot widens, revealing the outdoor scenery of a park somewhere in Toronto. The weather is warm, mixed with a cool breeze that ever-so-gently tosses Snow’s long blond hair around on his shoulder. Quietly, we can hear the baby giggle... Snow: “But I suppose...” His face warms... Snow: “Time off does have it’s advantages...” At times like these, we are forced to give serious though to the question: Does Snow suffer from multiple personality disorder? Probably not, but it’s hard to believe that at one moment he can be so harsh, and the next, so warm. He sits on a park bench, leaning over and staring at his young daughter. In the background, we see someone - we can’t make out who - peaking out at the Killin’ Villain from behind a tree... Snow: “I suppose we should get on our way to Montreal soon. Supremacy is only a few days away...” Just then, the mysterious figure hiding behind the tree steps out into the open. He’s accompanied by another figure who’s much larger and dressed in a sailor suit. We hear the first man’s thick southern accent... Unknown Man: “C’mon Billy Bob! We ain’t got much time! We gotta pay this bastard back for what he did to us!” EARL! The mysterious man is none other than Earl, who Snow met nearly a week ago in a Trailor Park in West Virginia. Earl, along with his lackey, Billy Bob, are loyal and high-ranking members of the KKK. They also happen to be thick-blooded rednecks. Nearly a week ago, Snow nearly joined their taboo organization due to a misunderstanding. If you would like to read Snow’s entire adventure with the KKK, please refer to the promo entitled: “Snow Joins the KKK!?!?” Earl: “Hurry the hell up!” This last statement catches the attention of the Killin’ Villain. He turns around to look at the men behind him. Earl looks almost the same, except he’s now wearing a “PCW” t-shirt and a pair of large sunglasses from the 70's. He has thick grey hair that’s parted to the side and is a slightly chubby man. His companion, Billy Bob, is simply fat. He also has a thin, wiry, beard that makes his disguise all the more ridiculous - he’s dressed in a sailor suit, disguised as a schoolboy. Earl, seeing Snow’s attention has turned to them, quickly falls into character... Earl: “Er... Come along, son.” Obviously, and over-dramatically acting, Earl motions Billy Bob forward. Billy Bob spits... Earl: “Oh my!” Earl, who’s now fairly close to the Killin’ Villain, looks into the carriage where baby Haley Snow is resting... Earl: “What an adorable little girl!” Snow’s eyes narrow - Earl and Billy Bob look familiar, but Snow can’t put his finger on their identities. That’s not too unusual for Snow. He doesn’t remember much that doesn’t stroke his own ego... Snow: “Who in the hinges of...” Snow hesitates as he remembers the sign taped to the side of the carriage... Snow: “...heck... are you?” Snow gets a glimpse of Billy Bob, who’s now licking a lolly pop and adds... Snow: “And what is that supposed to be?” There’s a moment of silence as Billy Bob and Earl exchange nervous glances while Earl quickly comes up with an answer... He tries to hide his southern accent... Earl: “Um, uh... The name’s... Lrae... yeah! Mr. Lrae.” Snow: “By Greatness! That’s the stupidest name I’ve ever heard!” Earl: “And uh... this here’s m’boy - ... uh... Bob Billy. Yeah.” Snow looks at Billy Bob/Bob Billy for a long moment, his eyes full of suspicion. The thirty-some-odd year old man surely doesn’t LOOK like a child. But Snow has been known to be naive at times... Snow: “And exactly how old is this ... boy ... of yours?” Earl/Lrae looks at Billy Bob/Bob Billy - he obviously wasn’t expecting a question like this. Soon, a smooth grin crosses his lips as a plan comes to mind. He points to little Haley... Earl/Lrae: “How old’s that there youngin’ of yers?” Snow’s eyes narrow... Snow: “Nine months...” Earl/Lrae’s eyes light up... Earl/Lrae: “Well! What a coincidence! It just so happens that little Bob Billy is nine months old too!” Snow, once again, takes a good long look at Billy Bob/Bob Billy... Snow: “That’s the ugliest baby I’ve ever seen! You should really get it checked out. I’m SURE it has some sort of disease! And it may well be contagious, so keep it away from my Superior Spawn!” Neither KKK member had anticipated this. Earl/Lrae takes a hard look at the full-grown bearded man who’s posing as his nine-month old son. Out of desperation, he points off to the side... Earl/Lrae: “Quick! Look over there!” Snow, absent-mindedly turns to the direction he was pointed in: Grass, trees, and the occasional animal pass his view. His eyes narrow... Snow: “Is this supposed to be important? I see nothing worthy of my Superior attention! Nothing! I only see some inferior trees! Maybe a peasant bird or two! And some ugly child playing in the mud - worsening his own predicament. Peasant? Are you listening to me!?!? PEASANT!?!?” Snow turns around to face the disguised rednecks only to find that they have disappeared. AND DISAPPEARED WITH HALEY!!! Snow, however, doesn’t realize this right away. He stares at the scene for a long moment... Snow: “Now... I could have sworn by all my Greatness that there was something there a minute ago... hmm...” Snow is silent for a few moments as he scratches his stubbly chin, his cold blue eyes narrow in thought. Suddenly, it occurs to him - Where did Haley go? Next, he thinks - Haley is gone... finally, he realizes the seriousness of the situation and exclaims... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS!!” Snow’s jaw drops and his eyes grow wide. In this moment, with Snow frozen in a mixture of fear and fury, the scene fades to black. Want to find out what happened to little Haley Snow? Tune in next time, for the second part of “The KKK Took my Baby Away.”
