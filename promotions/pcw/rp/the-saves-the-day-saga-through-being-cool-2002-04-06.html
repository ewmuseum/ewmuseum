The Saves the Day Saga: Through Being Cool

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-06-02 Everyone relys on Fate but in realisticness, he is an idiot that decides everything through a game of chess. Obviously, Fate never loses, its the most annoying thing I have had to accept in this world since I realized I had to live. Fate knows every move, everything thats going to happen. I guess you could call what he does cheating, but he's Fate, its what he does... he knows everyone and everythings destiny... he once told me exactly when some milk in my refridgerator went bad, yeah, impressive, he forgot to tell me that on the way home I was going to be nearly killed by some kid tweaking with a switchblade.... Thats the kind of guy Fate is. I hate Fate.---------------------------------Somewhere, in a place where no human and few specters could reach, Fate played Chess, for the uncountable time, in a uncountable amount of years he has been Fate. This time, however, something went horribly wrong. CHECKMATE Fate looked at the board again, and flinched.-------------------------------- Sometimes, sad things happen to people that seem to be the happiest around. Take this story for example. I can remember when I was in High School, before I dropped out due to the amount of hell I was put through by the thugs, preps, and jocks, there was this one boy. He was basicly guaranteed sucess, star quarterback, second seat Sax on in band, Principals list... you name it and not only did this kid do it, but he probably did it twice. He put me through complete shit. Everytime we would even be remotely close to eachother in the hallway I could hear him yell, 'Hey, Fagzail, come carry my books!', and, normally I would let most people just continue on their day thinking 'Hey, i bet that kid thinks Im cool now', but, he called me Fagzail? It wasnt even creative, it didn't rhyme, it wasnt a paly on words. It was nothing, just like him... see, around Sophomore year I realized that I had something deep inside me, not just the petty mind pyro I could do to people when I was bored. I had some sort of apathetic gift, I could look at him, and I saw his entire life laid in front of him... like it was just a TV show or a movie or something. In a way, I enjoyed it, because now I realized that this bastard was gonna get what he deserved for messing with me and people like me. But... actually... it was kind of depressing. I still had a heart back then, I still felt things like pity and remorse, this was before life turned me around and fucked me over so bad I regressed into the insanity that we will refer to as my life now. Those emotions return every now and then, but not often, and usually they return when I'm forced to filter the few people on this earth that are good and true to the next world... that always makes me a bit teary. This guy though, he never deserved to live, but he did... and he was given about ninethousand more opportunities in life then me, despite the fact he was a dick with ears. Fates a bitch. When I dropped out I never saw the guy again... but he still existed... for the last few years... and sitting in my realm his presence was alerted to me... And did he get what he deserved... Adam Quinn sat on his bed, long night partying last night obviously. He had to deal with the backlash of his bitch of a mother saying he was wasting his life. Where did she get off, how could she possibly believe that he, of all people, was wasting his life... He reached over to the table and grabbed the rubber tourniquet It was none of her business what he did in his free time. This was his life, and he would live it how he wanted. He grabbed his needle from the same table, tightining the tourniquet with his teeth This is his life. He shot it into his vein, a seasoned pro, and stood up And he could live it the way he wanted Untying it, he smiled as the buzz reached his brain, a car light illuminated his room for a second, and a shadow could be seen in the corner At this point, he decided that it was time for a trip to the store, nothing makes a day of illegal drug use complete without getting tanked up and possibly beating on your girlfriend when she wont give you car head. Oh, sorry, I'm getting ahead of myself arent I. He moved in a way that suggested he was attempting the world speed record for the nonchalant walk. Usually when you do something wrong and try to make it so people dont notice, your acting to innocent, and in this world, noone is innocent. In a twist of fate (ugh, that word again) it was obvious that this was one of those spontaneus things, for Adam slammed straight into a man wearing a dark leather trench coat. The man growls and mutters something under his breath. Adam grows angry with him. Adam: Well Pardon me for Living... The taller man growled, backhanding him and sending him sprawling to the ground. Man: NOONE GETS PARDONED FOR LIVING! Adam, already tweaking, stood, ready to fight, but was kicked in the gut hard enough to expel blood from his mouth, the man (who Adam now realized had blue hair...) casually wiped it off of his jacket and kneeled down next to him. Man: ...You look familiar. Adam: Take my money... please... jus-... Man: SHUT UP! Where did you go to high school? Why does he want to know this, why can't he just leave me alone. Adam: ...Palmyra.. please... don-... DarkStar smiled. DarkStar: Remember me? Gavyn Drazail? You used to talk to me every day in the hallway Adam barely remmebered the boy, but now that he was much bigger then himself he was glad he didn't remember that he made fun of him viciously, not tal-... why does my stomach hurt... DarkStar: Or should I say... disrespected. Adam looked down, noticing a needle in his stomach... one of his own. Adam: ..wh... DarkStar: Have you ever seen the Crow? Adam? He couldnt speak. DarkStar: You see, there was this scene, where Eric Draven forced one of the people who killed him to O.D. on heroin... it was incredibly poetic, and he told a decent joke about nailing Christ to a wall during it... anyway, I guess your wondering where I'm going... DarkStar grabbed another needle. DarkStar: I'll take your near comotose silence as a yes... you see, Draven didn't care about being caught, so he did it in a form of a Crow, and let someone see him... I on the other hand... well... I don't care if I get caught either, but I've always said, the best way to get revenge is to make sure you can exact it even after the person is dead... He jabbed the needle into Adam's arm. DarkStar: And here you are, Captain of the Straight A Can get any Woman he wants but chooses to fuck with the little goth kid that noone else really cared about in high school team, found dead, of a heroin overdose, outside of a shitty liquor store. He squirted a bit of the next needles liquid out, allowing air to get into it. DarkStar: Poetic, No? --------------------------------- See, thats Fate, we met on that street because of Fate, well.. not directly because of him, I think he had already lost his little chess game by this point... so we met because of me being Fate, although you really can't be Fa... oh you get the point, i'll explain later. Let's just say that if complete and utter chaos was lightning, I would be the sort to stand on a hilltop in a thunderstorm wearing wet copper armor and shouting 'All gods are bastards'. I breed Chaos, and situations like this, especially considering Fates horrible stupidity, are where I thrive... This, is my flavor country. To Be Continued
