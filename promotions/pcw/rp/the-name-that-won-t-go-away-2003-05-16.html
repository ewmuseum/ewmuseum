The Name That Won't Go Away

16 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/16/03 As Thursday turned to Friday the rain only continued to pour down. Even when the sun had begun to rise the rain only seemed to get heavier. It was now ten in the morning and the sewers were beginning to overflow with the large amount of rain that had been pouring from the gray skies over Massachusetts for the last two days. “God there’s nothing to do, maybe I should go shopping.” I thought to myself as I looked out the window and noticed that the my mother car was still there which meant that she would probably be downstairs walking around aimlessly while waiting for me so that she could chew me out once more for the prank that I had pulled on her in the form of a mother’s day gift from hell. “I don’t think mom would mind that much if I took her car out to the mall.” I continued to think to myself though there was no denying that if I took my moms car to the mall it would be just another opportunity for her to have a drama fest about how much of a problem child I am, even if I almost never did anything wrong. “Ah well, she’ll have to deal if she doesn’t like it. Not like I’m going to crash the car or anything.” I finished off my thoughts before creeping down the carpeted stairs as quietly as possible. I scanned the area carefully to make sure my mother was nowhere in sight. Quickly I dashed off towards the key holder on the wall which itself was shaped like a key and carved out of wood. I had reached my first destination as I grabbed the keys to her brand new Honda Civic and dashed off towards the car, mastering my first step. “Now to get out of the driveway.” I said aloud to myself as I started to back out of the driveway and head off to my destination, the mall. Surely enough with my luck the sound of the engine coming alive was enough to trigger my mom running to the front door to see where the noise was coming from. It was too late for her though because no sooner had she gotten to the door had I taken off with her car, leaving her standing behind with an angered face that told me that the moment I got home, I was going to pay a great price. “Good job sweet cheeks!” I heard the voice of the devlish mini Gaz whisper to me, proud of my rebellion but I was too busy focusing of my destination to pay much attention to him. -Several minutes later- I made it to the mall without a single call from my mom. She must have forgotten that I had brought my cell phone with me. That’s was fine though in my eyes, the last thing I wanted from her was a call where all she would do is scream and yell while demanding that I bring her car back. “Good now that she’s taken care off it’s time to head off and get myself some clothes.” I said with a smile, satisfied that I had once again taught my mother that if she didn’t start respecting me I would only make her life worse. I walked off in the direction of The Limited a place where I got most of my clothes. Though the price tag was rather high I had no problem spending my money considering I’d earn it all back in no time. I grabbed myself a pair of slightly faded blue jeans that flared out slightly at the bottom along with a pink tank top to go along with it before making my way up to the register where the woman at the cash register greeted me with a smile. “Will you be paying with cash or credit card today?” She asked me as she took the clothes off the hangers and rang them up on the cash register before neatly folding them up and placing them in the bag. “Credit card.” I answered equally as pleasant as her as I dug through my purse in search of my credit card. After grasping a hold on it I placed it on the counter and waited as she slid the card through the machine which processes the information. “Excuse me Miss but do you have any form of identification.” She questioned just as pleasantly as before. I nodded my head in response before reaching back into my purse and placing my identification on the counter as well. The woman swiped the card from the counter and looked back at me from a puzzled glance. “Is something wrong?” I questioned as she took another look at the card and then the computer monitor which she stood behind. “Yes.” She answered with a brief pause before explaining the issue to me. “The name on the credit card is Tessa Windsor-Green while the name on your license is Tessa Windsor.” “WHAT!” I shouted aloud before looking at the place on the monitor where the cashier was pointing out where my name surely enough was Tessa Windsor-Green. “I’m sorry Miss but we can’t accept your credit card without proper identification.” The cashier informed me before handing back both my credit card and license. Instead of putting up with an argument to point out the obviously fact that both last names had Windsor in it I walked off and tried to put it behind me. “Ugh how on earth did the credit card company find out that I am a Green?” And then the obvious answer dawned upon me. “GAZ! Gah, does he ever stop?” I questioned myself knowing that the jokes would probably continue on into Fusion meaning that my probability of winning was a slim chance with he, Nerezza, and Jon all in the same ring even if Gaz was supposed to be on my team. The only thing I could do now is just hope that the pranks wouldn’t continue to go any further from here and that people would remember that I was truly Tessa Windsor and not Tessa Windsor-Green. So much for a solution to a rainy day, and to top it all off I had to go home to face my angry mother. Hopefully things would change once I got to Afghanistan, but that seemed unlikely with a prankster who I was legally married to and his vampire buddy around.
