Hopefully I'll never have to come to Roswell again (Part 1)

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th of July, 2002 Coming off of a big win over the PCW Heavyweight Champion Big Alan Steele and Big Al’s greatest foe, Frisco on Tuesday’s edition of PCW Turmoil, ‘The Pharaoh’ Titu Xansiv now rushes across the United States of America in a vain attempt to get to Roswell, New Mexico where - in the superstitious town of supposed alien sightings - he’s scheduled to make some form of fan appearances for the townsfolk. Whatever lies in store for our young and valiant hero is, as one would presume, currently unknown, BUT… there’s no doubt in my mind that we’ll find out soon enough… This is a town of mystery. This is a town of suspicions. And, my friends, this is a town of people who don’t even trust their own cat not to be an Alien. Folks, this is Roswell, New Mexico!! Numerous dusty streets, dusty houses and sometimes even dusty people reside here, but though these houses, buildings and people may one day cease their existence in this infamous little place on a map it’s clear that one thing will always reign supreme…until the VERY end of TIME!! ‘Aliens…Crashed…In…Roswell…’ …. Oh… And ‘The… government… are… keeping… it… hush, hush…’ These two sentences seem to literally run the lives of the people who live in the town. These rumours - and of course they are rumours - dominate the very balance of Roswell, tipping its already fragile poise into the ‘abnormally strange’ zone. When a young professional wrestler came to Roswell and stated that he came from Cairo, the people of the town rejoiced in the streets exclaiming such babbles as “He has returned! RETURNED I TELL YOU!” and “Is it the Alien Messiah?” for another to reply, “No, that’s Chris Chase, you idiot.” And then for that original speaker to exclaim, “NO! He’s The SANIT, fool!” Indeed Titu Xansiv’s coming to Roswell was indeed strange, abrupt and – in need of a better word – Alienisered. Then again that isn’t even a word, so lets just get off of this damned introduction and into the goddamn RP! Pharaoh sat, a smile spread over his face, and a jug of beer in hand. It’d been a hard day. His promotional stints were going well - that was true to say - but these Roswell natives were mad, MAD I tell ya’! Next to him sat old farmer Giles, a man he’d met in town a day ago, after he’d arrived. Giles seemed to be the only person in Roswell who was sane, then again the hospital in town WAS selling tablets that - when taken - made the man hallucinate to think he or she was sane – and Pharaoh seemed to remember seeing a small jar of them on the old farmer’s table in his kitchen hours ago – but, the guy seemed ok, and Pharaoh was fine with that… The Pharaoh: “It sure gets hot in these parts, Mr Giles.” Farmer Giles gave Xansiv a lopsided glare and then took a swig of what he called ‘HIS moonshine, sunny’. Giles: “It always is, boy. I remember back in the day when I was only a young whippersnapper, ya’ never used to get whether like this. IT WAS HOTTER back THEN!! This whether - the whether you damn well call ‘HOT’ - boy, O boy, it ain’t nuthin’ like what it used to be! This is second-rate heat; I tells ya’!” Pharaoh, not really daring to go into a violent discussion involving the whether, decided that it was best to change the subject. The Pharaoh: “So then, err…do you have any daughters?” Farmer Giles frowned, took another slip out of his huge bottle of…well the gods knows what, and winced, all the time glaring at the young Egyptian warrior. Giles: “Ai, I have two, you won’t have met ‘em yet, though. AND DON’T EVEN GET ANY IDEAS ABOUT RUNNING OFF WID ‘EM!” Pharaoh was beginning to sweat a little now. This discussion was starting to become…hostile - you could feel it in the air in was so thick. The Pharaoh: “Oh don’t you worry, Mr Giles, I have a wife! And we’re…err, planning on having a baby, too…” Giles’ lip stuck out a little - in a suspicious sort of way, of course. Giles: “Well just as long as you ain’t after none of my daughters--...” The Pharaoh: (Quickly) “I’m not, Mr Giles. I swear.” Giles - supposedly, in Pharaoh’s opinion, the sanest of the Roswell natives - nodded, looking out over the endless cornfields of his farm. Giles: “What did you say your name was again, sunny?” The Pharaoh: “Wrestling, or real?” Giles: (Sighing) “Real, boy! REAL!!!” Pharaoh - looking slightly confused by the reason Giles wanted his real name – gazed over the sea of dim yellow corn in the darkness. The Pharaoh: “Titu Xansiv, sir.” Farmer Giles – which was possibly the most unoriginal name for a farmer this writer could come up with - takes a swig of his alcoholic beverage and grumbles something under his breath. Giles: “Hmm, I could ‘ave sworn there used to be a ‘Xansiv’ who lived around these parts, not so long ago, mind you. Don’t hear much about the old bugger now, but back in the day…well about two years ago ACTUALLY, he was all in the news. His son went missing, ya’ see. Used to be an archaeologist or summat … Yep….” Pharaoh frowned realizing that he was once an archaeologist and indeed he’d never really found out about his past involving his parents. Had Giles, Roswell’s so-called ‘sanest man’, actually given him the key to the metaphorical chest he needed? Giles: (Continuing) “See, I thought I recognised your name, son. So, I thought to myself, ‘Giles,’ I thought to myself, ‘This could be the lost son, so why not tell him about Ol’ Mr Xansiv!’. But then again, you’re one of them there wrestling types, not no archaeologist.” The Pharaoh: “Mr Giles, I believe you’ve just given me a new quest. I thank you, sir.” Pharaoh rose from his rocking chair on the front porch of the ‘Giles Family Farm’ and made his way down the cobblestone path to the estate’s exit. As he walked, though, something dropped from his loincloth, hitting the floor with a dull flop. Pharaoh looked down and to his horror there sat a bright pink thong. His terrified eyes rose to those of Farmer Giles who - because of the sudden thong drop - had ceased his drinking of the moonshine in mid-guzzle… The Pharaoh: (Puzzled and frightened) Shit! How the BLOODY HECK did THAT get there?!” Moments later Pharaoh was being chased down the dusty path back towards town followed hastily by the silvery bullets of Farmer Giles’ shotgun! Giles: (As Pharaoh faded from view) “Hot dang! That’s the forth bastard this month who’s said he hasn’t slept with my dang daughters! Lyin’ city folk! I’ve gotta’ quit trustin’ ‘em so damn easily!” Giles took another swig of his moonshine and then began to steadily walk back to the house. It then occurred to him that he didn’t even have any daughters… “Catch him, Catch HIM!” The voices echoed around the town square like a bee behind a microphone. The Pharaoh watched in confusion as a group of disgruntled Roswell natives rushed - in a pack vaguely resembling something from a Frankenstein movie – after a very small man, wearing a grey cloak… The Pharaoh: (Confused and out of breath) “What …in the …name of…Thoth?” Titu SLOWLY walked towards the crowd, mostly because Farmer Giles – a man who he’d considered to be a friend and NOT insane – had chased him from his farm with a shotgun. Still Titu couldn’t quite remember EVER seeing any of the supposed daughters that Farmer Giles said he had. Very strange, really… And where the in the unholy name of Osiris did that pink thong come from?! What’s up wid dat?! The running crowd passed Pharaoh and so – knowing that his only hope of ever finding out what was going on lied within the hustle of people - our brave hero, and the gods know he must have been brave to join with THESE people, merged with them. The Pharaoh: “What’s going on?” Man: “It’s YODA! Master YODA! Goddamit, he’s here in OUR town! THEY’VE LANDED, I TELL YOU! LANDED!” The ‘Star Wars’ character? Thought Xansiv as he walked alongside the man. These people are madder than I first gave them credit for! Titu looked onwards. Up ahead a small character did INDEED run from the flock of fans, poachers, bounty hunters and quite possibly escaped madmen from the town’s asylum - but it couldn’t possibly be ‘Yoda’ from Star Wars, right? He was all in the imagination of George Lucas and only a publicity stunt from Lucas Film limited. The Pharaoh: “You are kidding, right?” The man next to him frowned, seemingly disgruntled. Man: “What the HELLL do you mean am I kidding? THAT’S YODA DAMMIT! YODA I SAY” Pharaoh sighed and decided to continue the chase – mainly because he couldn’t get back out of the crowd he was running alongside. Minutes past before the thoughts of Farmer Giles’ haunting words - involving a man who’s last name matched his own – came to mind. Out of curiosity Xansiv looked towards the man standing next to him and asked the inevitable. The Pharaoh: “You don’t happen to know a man who goes by the name of ‘Xansiv’ around these parts do you?” Man: “Oh! You’ll be meaning ‘Gregory Xansiv’, the old man who lives by himself in the suburbs! Right?” The Pharaoh: “Hmmm…Well that’s the thing, I aren’t entirely sure--…” Man: “WAIT ONE MINUTE! You’re not…Titu Xansiv are you? The Pharaoh?” Without consideration of the possible outcomes Pharaoh nodded. Man: “Oh…my…GOD!” The man’s face lit up, as though a deep fire burned within. Man: “I hadn’t the slightest idea. I’m a big wrestling fan!” Pharaoh smiled nervously. He’d dealt with ecstatic fans before, but never had he really encountered ecstatic fans from ROSWELL. The Pharaoh: “Well I thought you’d have known, I’ve been in town for a day or so now.” Man: “I hadn’t the slightest--…” Pharaoh interrupted. The Pharaoh: “I’m real sorry to cut you off, friend, but do you know exactly where this man - this Xansiv guy - actually lives?” A roar went up in the crowd simultaneously distracting the man. Man: “Looks like something happened! Yoda must have escaped!” He punched the air. Man: “This is the forth time in a week!” Reconsidering his anger, the man jumped up and down trying to get a better view over the taller men and women in the army of Roswell locals. At length he ceased his movement and turned to Pharaoh. Man: (Sighing) “Looks like he’s climbed up that drainpipe and got onto the roof of the building!” Titu sighed and went to try again. After all, he needed to find out the whereabouts of this ‘Xansiv’ character… The Pharaoh: “Look--…” Man: “It’ll be the Scientologist again, I’ll bet.” The man, clearly unmoved by Titu’s question, was beginning to annoy our young and valiant hero, but then he realized what the man had said. His words began to sink in on him and at last his mind uttered the words “What did he mean by ‘It’ll be the Scientologists again!”? The Pharaoh: “What do you mean by that?” Man: (Sighing) “The Scientologist, man. They’re always messing stuff up for us! Showing the Aliens ways out of the city, helping them to escape, we hate them here in Roswell… Goody-goody bastards! They’re stopping our research on the Aliens that crashed here years ago!!!” The Pharaoh, one of the four men who would possibly battle it out this Sunday for the PCW heavyweight title, opened his mouth to speak and then decided not to. Man: “We call it our ‘SCIENTOLOGY THEORY!” The Pharaoh: “Oh? I have to fight a Scientologist this Sunda--…” Man: “WELL KICK HIS FUCKIN’ ASS! AND DO IT FOR ROSWELL!!!” Pharaoh took a step back… The Pharaoh (Nervously) “I …will do, my friend…” Man: “Hell YES! You do that and you’ll forever be a friend of our friendly community!” Pharaoh winced. Friendly?! He thought. These people, they’re insane! Titu nodded reluctantly before pushing past the people speedily. The man waved goodbye but of course Pharaoh didn’t look back to see him saluting. Once he’d got to the front of the crowd he clambered up onto a bench and went to jump off of it, but it wasn’t to be. The crowd quickly recognized him, and as more pointed him out, more chanted to him. Crowd: “GET YODA! GET YODA! GET YODA!” Pharaoh turned. Man: “Pharaoh! Get up on to the roof and find Yoda for us!” Woman: “Yeah, common! Do it for your fans!” Boy: “Pwease, Mr Phawaoh, it’s the weast you can do…” A bead of sweat fell from Titu’s brow, hitting his nose with a thud – which was strange since the crowd made so much noise that one would have thought the sound of a 50 kg weight being dropped in the town square right now wouldn’t have made MUCH if ANY noise at all. The Pharaoh: Me? Up there?” Titu wiped the sweat from his forehead. The people of Roswell were beginning to freak him out. YEAH, it was funny when he first arrived; I mean the Alien theories, the abductions stories, and even the sightings were amusing. But NOW…now things were different… He looked up at the building. Crowd: “Yeah!” Man: “Yes! You’re big…” Woman: “And strong enough…” Boy: “And cweawly capable!” Pharaoh sighed. If he did scale the shop and get onto the roof then perhaps he could find a ladder at the other side of the fairly SMALL building and escape the mob of Yoda hunters. He agreed that doing so might actually turn out to be more beneficial than useless… Bounty hunter: (Trying to look inconspicuous) “Yes…do it for…the fans…not for me so I can claim a rich reward from NASA…” The Pharaoh: “Alright! Alright, I’ll do it, for you guys…my…fans!” Pharaoh hopped down off the bench and walked towards the wall’s surface. It’d be a tough climb, but if he did manage it then he’d be able to get away from these maniacs. He looked around for something to hang onto and found a stone that stood out against the rest of the brickwork. Taking it in his hand he pulled himself up and found a windowsill just an arms length away from it. He grasped that and pulled himself up again. Time passed and eventually he got to the roof. He pushed himself onto it and breathed heavily, not even taking the time to look around. The Pharaoh: “Ok, this is it!” Pharaoh set off running towards the other side of the roof. As he’d suspected, and more or less HOPED, there was a ladder at his disposal, OBVIOUSLY it was there through a true miracle of the Gods. He grinned, and jogged in the direction of it only to stop abruptly in his tracks. He rubbed his eyes theatrically and glared at what stood in front of him. “YODA!” he said in shock. TO BE CONTINUED…
