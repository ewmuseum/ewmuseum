PCW Wars: New Hope Part 3

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15 - 10 - 02 Through the darkness we make out to silhouettes navigating through the desert in a landspeeder, passing rocks and mountain ranges that appear as nothing more than blurs. Zooming up close we make out the distinguished Rush-3PO piloting gracefully whilst his owner Alexander Skywalker leans over the back of the craft adjusting something in the motor compartment. A. Skywalker: 'How's that tin ribs?' said the farmers-boy chuckling to himself. Rush-3PO signals that everything is fine and Alex turns back into the wind-filled cockpit shutting the canopy as he lowers into his seat. Swaying through a curved canyon, the vehicle heads straight into the rising red sun of Tatooine and this particular side of the planet brightens once more. A. Skywalker: 'That weird Kenobi guy lives out here somewhere but I can't see how that R2 unit could've made it his far. We must've missed him. Uncle Owen is going to be so mad, now I'm gonna have to go in my own pocket and keep the drink flowing until he suffers memory loss!!!' Rush-3PO: 'Don't count your penny's just yet dude, what’s that shit on the scanner?' A. Skywalker: 'It looks like our droid, dead ahead.... Hit the accelerator!' Watching the landspeeder with interest is two Sand People, or Tuskan Raiders as they're sometimes called. One bares a large rifle and aims, as he is about to fire his colleague grips the shaft of the weapon and draws it away causing them to bicker in barbaric language. Deciding to bide their time the two Tuskan Raiders flee the scene via two large Banthas, which resemble wholly-mammoths with long flowing brown hair and looping horns. Returning to the Skywalker search party, we find the landspeeder parked, hovering unaided as Alexander walks over to the runaway droid, armed only with a laser rifle similar to that of the Sand People. A. Skywalker: 'Hey, whoa, where do you think your taking your metal ass?' The little droid whistled a feeble response as Rush-3PO stands menacingly behind. Rush-3PO: 'Masturbator here....' Alex coughing as an indication towards his name, which is Master Alex and not Masturbator or Master Baker for that matter. Rush-3PO: 'Hmm-... Sorry, Master Alex is your owner now. So stop talking shit about this imaginary friend Charlie Loc Kenobi and lose the mission briefing 'cause your days as a robotic rebel are now over!' A. Skywalker: 'We had better go home, stop tap doesn't last forever and Uncle Owen is a nasty piece of work sober!' Suddenly the fugitive droid bounced about, whistling gibberish and acting rather frantic as though he feared for his life, a normal human expression, except for the fact that a machine was displaying these symptoms. A. Skywalker: 'What's wrong now dude?' Rush-3PO: 'Oh... He says that we got company! We got some ugly critters approaching from the southeast' A. Skywalker: 'Sand People! Or worse! Could even be.... THE BYRON CALVER WELCOMING COMMITEE!!!' Using the height of a nearby ridge Alex inspected the southeast with his electro binoculars identifying two Banthas unattended to, when without warning his view is obstructed by a ruthless Tuskan Raider. The droids back off from the howling beast that crashes his staff at the young Skywalker, defended only by the lad’s rifle. Again the unintelligent creatures tries his luck when again his efforts are thwarted by the echoing moans that flood the canyon causing the assailant to flee in terror. R2 D2 cowers in the shadows of a looming alcove as the sounds grow in volume. The sound grows until a shabby old-desert-rat-of-a-man appears with a slight touch of Asian to his face, he kneels down besides Alex before addressing the R2 unit. C. Loc Kenobi: 'Hey there! How you doing? Come here often... Err, don't worry about your friend he'll be fine' The R2 unit waddled over to his fallen master beeping his concern. Charlie Loc however simply lowered his hand on the boy’s forehead and Alexander Skywalker began to instantly come around. A. Skywalker: 'What the fuc-... I mean what happened?' C. Loc Kenobi: 'You had your ass handed to you, that's what happened - But rest easy son, it's been a long day!' A. Skywalker: 'Kenobi? Charlie Loc Kenobi? The stranger nods, lifts the beaten Alexander and indicates for him to follow. Alexander ponders and then pursues the man known as Charlie Loc Kenobi who has already made much progress, accompanied as always by the droid collective of Rush-3PO and R2 D2. The scene as always changes. This time we find the four-man group sitting in the spartan dwelling of Charlie Loc Kenobi, which is cluttered with desert junk giving an eccentric awe. Catching the conversation mid-way we witness the first steps for Alexander Skywalker as he embarks into the world of the Jedi. C. Loc Kenobi: 'Your Uncle Owen is a loser' said the wise old man making the 'L' shape with his finger and thumb, holding the pose. C. Loc Kenobi: '... Your father was in the Clone Wars and not a galaxy tour guide. He was the best pilot period and a cunning warrior, a Jedi Knight and a good friend. Which reminds me....' The old man rummaged in a musty trunk and turned brandishing a short chrome handle with various electronic gadgets attached to it. A. Skywalker: 'Wow - It's a.... What is it?' C. Loc Kenobi: 'This is your old man's lightsaber. This is the weapon of the Jedi Knight. Not as trendy as firing bolts of electricity with a blaster but an elegant weapon for a civilised time. For over a thousand years....' Alex looked down at his wrist, notably at his watch and glances back at Charlie Loc Kenobi. A. Skywalker: 'Is this going to take long?' C. Loc Skywalker: 'Don't interrupt! For over a thousand years generations the Jedi Knights governed the galaxy by injecting peace and justice in the Old Republic. Before the dark times, before the Empire....' A. Skywalker: 'Now this is the part where you tell me that a pupil of yours called Darth Pharaoh abused the force and hunted down the Jedi almost into extinction, one of those unlucky soles was my father!?!' Kenobi squinted and re-composed himself before saying, C. Loc Kenobi: 'Booyakah - You saved me the trouble of saying that boring speech for the billionth time in my life. By the way, what's the force?' A. Skywalker: 'Who knows, some funny shit that makes things happen, gives you the edge in battle yadda yadda yadda....' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Not quite smart-ass. It's an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds us and penetrates us. It binds the galaxy together, well that’s what some Lucas guy told me, and anyway don't you think we should take a look at that message!' R2 follows the command and begins to play the full footage of Princess Anarchy's plead. Anarchy: 'Is this thing working... Oh, General Kenobi, years ago you served my father in the Clone Wars. Now he begs you to help him in his struggle against those b*stards, The Empire. I regret that I can't present my fathers gift in person but as you know shit happens, shit like your ship being under attack preventing my mission of bring you to Alderaan. Anyways I have stored vital information in the memory of this droid, information which may determine the survival of the Rebellion, but... Only my father can access it' The footage fizzled out as the transmission is cut short by static. Charlie Loc scratched his head and then turned his attention back to that of Alexander Skywalker. C. Loc Kenobi: 'You must learn the force and come with me to Alderaan, hell are you in?' A. Skywalker: 'Your damn right, let's blow this joint and get going - First though I had better tell the farmers...' C. Loc Kenobi: 'You mean Aunt and Uncle!?!' A. Skywalker: 'WHATEVER!!!' Again the scene changes, this time however, it is within the confines of an Imperial Stardestroyer, to be more exact a conference room on board the ship. Generals and Imperial Senators sit around the oval table fixed in conversation, first to speak is a slimy individual in uniform known as Mr. Tatooine, formerly Mr. U.K. He continues to rant as the Dark Syth Lord Pharaoh walks in, accompanied by the Grand Moff Tarkannon. Mr. Tatooine: 'Any attack made by the Rebels would be like soooo useless, no matter what plans they got hold of over ebay. This station is now the ultimate power in the universe. Can we blow up some insignificant planet, like, well Earth, please, pretty please....' Tarkannon: 'SILENCE PEON!!! By the way who is this guy? Is he even a scientologist?' Mr. Tatooine: 'Err-... Well-... It never mentioned choice of Religion on the application?!?' Tarkannon: 'You lie! But we shall over look your foolishness because you had a valid point, I would praise the name of Hubbard if I were you, Lord Pharaoh please continue...' Darth Pharaoh: 'Hmm, as I was saying don't be too proud of this technological terror you have constructed. I know destroying planets is a big stress reliever but it is insignificant in comparison to the force!' Mr. Tatooine: 'Don't try to frighten us with your sorcerer's ways, Lord Pharaoh. You would only need to remove your mask but if I may be permitted to comment, your devotion to this religion hasn't conjured up those pesky plans has it, huh?!?' Darth Pharaoh: 'Well....' Mr. Tatooine: 'Well what, arr-... Cat got your tongue. See I prove to you all that Lord Pharaoh is nothing more than a fraud!' Suddenly Mr. Tatooine begins to choke and turn blue under Pharaoh's spell. Tarkannon: 'That's it KILL HIM~!' Darth Pharaoh: 'I find your lack of faith disturbing!' The scene reverts back to Tatooine where the landspeeder passes the giant Sand Crawler, which appeared as a burnt out shell, Jawas slaughtered everywhere, but the collective simply pass by uninterested. C. Loc Kenobi: 'Nice weather we're having don't you think?' The craft finally stumbles upon the burning homestead of the Skywalker residence. Debris everywhere and smoking holes give the impression of battle, similar scenes to those witnessed earlier with the destruction of the Jawas. A. Skywalker: 'Aunt Beru, Uncle Owen...' The young apprentice runs over to two smoldering figures, their features long gone but odds suggest that neither family member made it. His face expressing anger and hate, the surviving Skywalker heads back to his vehicle accompanied by the trio of Rush-3PO, R2 D2 and Charlie Loc Kenobi. C. Loc Kenobi: 'Well look at it this way, you don't need your Uncles permission now, huh?!?' Alex's eyes widening with fury he turns to address his newfound mentor. A. Skywalker: 'Well I was always told to look on the bright side' Alex peers down at the raging flames of his family's corpses. 'At-least now I don't haven't got to shell out for a cremation, best we get this journey started....' All four enter the miniscule craft and jet into the distance. Only thing visible was cobias amounts of sand as the Rebel Alliances only hopes head to Mos Eisley spaceport, a place renowned for crime and decay. As the landspeeder continues at pace the scene slowly closes and the screen eventually blackens. - To be continued -
