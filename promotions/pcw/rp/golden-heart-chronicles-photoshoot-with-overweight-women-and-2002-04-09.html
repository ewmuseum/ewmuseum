Golden Heart Chronicles: Photoshoot with Overweight Women and Nature Incorporated (Chap 3 Part 2)

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/02 Golden Heart Chronicles Chapter 3 Part 2: The first two chapters of the Golden Heart chronicles were the insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we were able to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The first two chapters in the Golden Heart Chronicles are where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. This chapter and the following will focus on Juan and his relationship with being a fighter. He puts other things behind him and works on becoming a determined fighter. Read and Enjoy, hopefully this will fulfill you’re thoughts on how Juan Borro really is. Time: 4:12 P.M Location: HollandScene: Photo Shoot. The scene opens with Juan Borro wearing a bear costume posing with his leg in the air giving the Golden Goal to an animal cartoon figure with various different oversized women and squirrels. If you’re wondering why Juan is doing this, there is a long story behind it. First, Juan is taking the last few pictures, totally not excited and pretty fed up that he had to do this to begin with. The smell of the fat ladies around him starts to get to the head of Juan, but he shakes it off. Not to mention Juan’s fright of squirrels. As the photographer finally takes the last picture Juan runs off in disgust back to his dressing room. “Those tree rats you call squirrels kept putting their disease infected tails near me. And the fat ladies must have been sitting on skunks they smelt so bad.” Juan says with full disgust to the magazine editor. “That’s just too bad, maybe you should have read through the agreement before you signed it in the first place.” The editor replies. “Believe me, I’ll be making sure that article doesn’t hit the racks anytime soon. Juan Borro is a well respected man and his demands will be properly met, you’ll see soon enough.” Borro says fully confident in himself. “Don’t worry Juan you’ll be even more respected after the public sees that you’re a supporter of Overweight Women with Nature cause and magazine.” The editor says with a straight face. Juan has had it up to his head in all of this garbage he’s been put through and storms out of the building as fast as he can. When Juan goes running out, he accidentally bangs into a man, knocking him to the ground. “ Excuse me, I was just on my way out of this place.” Juan says as he picks the man up and dusts him off. When the man turns around Juan notices it is none other than the man who he had a training session for just a few days, Hans Daigen. “Ahh Van, how iz ze doing. I zought I vas zeposed to zee you just a few dayz ego.” Hans says in his strong accent. “ Oh, yea, I’m kind of sorry I’ve been running low on time lately and it must of completely skipped my mind.” Juan replies with trying to convince Hans that he’s speaking the truth even though he isn’t. Juan really knows that the reason he didn’t go back was because he felt he got nothing done the first time him and Hans met, so he didn’t want to do it all again. Of course Juan isn’t stupid enough to tell the truth, so he’s just making the entire thing up hoping Hans will believe it. “Ahh I zee, so vat are you doing in front of zis vat lady magazine company.” Hans says looking at Juan and the building he just came out of. “It’s a long story, but I guess I’ll tell you it anyway, since I have nothing better to do.” Juan replies getting ready to give the long story. (Scene goes to yesterday morning.) Scene opens with Juan sitting down in his hotel room, with his manager sitting there with him, while the two discuss public appearances for Juan during his stay in Norway. The scene starts about half way into the conversation. “I know exactly what to do?” Juan says jumping up from his seat, proud that he found way get publicity. “What’s that?” Juan’s manager replies. “Well what better way to get out there then be in a magazine. Not only do I get pictures of my gorgeous face, but I get to be in something that will be published everywhere for the hold world to see.” Juan says proud of his idea. “Good thinking, I’ll look for some good magazine companies and we’ll meet early tomorrow morning to go over it all.” Juan’s manager says while being in a rush to get to his next appointment. “Great, I’ll see you then.” Juan replies. Juan’s manager then goes off to his next appointment. For most of the day Juan pretty much did his usual routine and work out. Nothing special out of Juan, just the usual schedule for him. As for Juan’s manager on the other hand, well that was a little bit different. After all of his appointments with different magazine companies endorsement adds, he saw a big building that thousands of people were around. Juan’s manager decided to go check it out, but only for a little while, before he’d half to go back to his apartment and get some sleep. He arrived at the gathering and notices it was a huge bash going on. He became interested and went inside. When he went inside of the party that spelt the beginning of all of these problems. Next thing that seemed to happen was Juan’s manager passes out on the floor completely hung over after the previous night. (Next Morning) The next morning comes and it’s eleven am when Juan’s manager finally awakens, in a ripped up suit and stained shoes. He quickly glances at his watch still a bit hung over, but notices it’s ten minutes before he’s supposed to meet Juan. Just incase he’s not fully capable to tell time he asks someone cleaning up the house the party took place in. “Excuse me, what time is it?” “Ten minutes until 11.” The man replies. Juan’s manager gets all-nervous and quickly rushes off to his apartment where he gets his suit off, takes a quick shower, and find some new clothes. He then sets off for Juan’s apartment for the meeting. While he’s driving he realizes he forgot to do a huge thing, and that was find a magazine for Juan to appear in. He suddenly goes into a huge shock, completely nervous. He even get hick ups until he passes a building where he notices the last word means magazine in English. The manager pulled over and quickly made some plans with the secretary inside for times for Juan to appear and what magazine for him to be specialized in. “This is great, we’ll just need you to sign the contract and we’ll be on our way. It’s great to know such a huge soccer star would offer himself to do such a thing.” The secretary at the front desk says. The manager doesn’t pay much attention with what she says and continues. He notices he’s already late to meet with Juan so he signs the contract without reading through it at all. The manager just smiles, hands in the contract and goes off to go get Juan back at his hotel. When he arrives Juan isn’t happy with him being so late. “Where were you I’ve been waiting here almost a complete twenty minutes? Do you know what happens when people keep me waiting?” Juan says completely angered and fed up. “Sorry Juan.” the manager replies. “So did you find me an excellent magazine for my gorgeous face to be appearing in?” Juan says striking a vain pose. “Yes, I did, but it’s a secret. The secretary was telling me how a large group of women read it, so I was sure you’d like it.” the manager replies. “Excellent.” Juan says as he twiddles his fingers the way Monte Burns on the Simpson’s does. “Let’s go to the photo shoot now, they have everything already set up.” The manager says as he leads the way to his car. Juan follows very pleased with the work his manager has done. The two get in the car and drive over to the magazine company where Juan signs in. After Juan signs in he enters the shoot and notices a few odd stuff. He sees animals and these fat women in front of him. “You can go pose over there for the photo shoot.” The director of the magazine says directing Juan. “I’m not going near there.” Jun replies. (Scene Ends and goes back to present.) “Well you see after that they pretty much saw the contract my manager signed, and made me appear in the magazine or else I’d be sued.” says Juan . “Vat did you do to ze manager?” Hans replies. “Let’s just say he’s fired.” Juan says with a smirk on his face. Hans laughs after Juan’s comment, and Juan walks off. “Well I better be going, I have a few things to go through. I hope you enjoyed the little story.” Juan says as he begins to walk off before Hans stops hi in his steps. “Zus before ze go I mus tell you, I zon’t believe you in vy you zidn’t return to ze gym. Vut, you better be zere tommorow. Are we clear.” Hans says after giving a huge smile. Meanwhile Juan just frowns and walks off, not being pleased that he has to be going back to that gym.
