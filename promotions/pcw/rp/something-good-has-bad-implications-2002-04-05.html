Something good, has bad implications

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-02 Enough about Xavier Kannon. This man has ruled the thoughts of Jeremy Howard ever since he sucker punched the Krazed Lunatic three days after March Madness. But, such isn't the case today. Today, the Krayzie One is going to flush any thoughts of Xavier Kannon out of his mind, so that he can make it to Elkhart Central High School and secure his deal. The deal being Jeremy's sponsorship of a high school boys basketball tournament for all of Elkhart County. Xavier Kannon boasts of being rich all the time. Thing is, what has he ever done for anyone with all of that money? Other than put a host of Scientology Churches here and there to force people into his religion, he's done absolutely nothing. Now, the same can't be said of Jeremy Howard. This man came into quite a deal of money during his stint as leader of Greed Inc. And now, since his brother is free, the Krazed Lunatic has charitable ideas for that money. He actually cares about his people. Xavier Kannon doesn't. So, if the Gold Patron Meritorious would like any ideas on how to treat his people, that is if they even mean anything to him, then he'll watch the Krayzie One in action. Because, as the scene fades in from black, Jeremy and Jessica can be seen standing right in front of the high school in which Jessica graduated back in 1999. It shows too, being Jessica is wearing a Central colors, blue and gold, t-shirt that fits snug to her chest. Jessica: I'm so excited. I get to see my old school again. By the way Jessica's acting, you'd think you gave candy to baby. The bleach blonde beauty claps her hands together in excited fashion, arousing a smile out of the Krayzie One. Jeremy: Damn, it looks alot bigger than when you went here. Jessica: It's because they've been adding on. Jeremy, wearing dark blue worker pants with a black belt and a plain white t-shirt, puts his arm around his lady, ushering her in the front door. To Jeremy's surprise, teenagers are running all over the halls, trying to get from one class to the next within the time span allotted to them. Jeremy grins, reliving some of those days when he was almost late to class because he was messing around with Jessica during those intervals. Jeremy: Hopefully, within this class of kids, there are no Scientologists. Jessica frowns. Jessica: We all would be better with one less Scientologist. Jeremy: We'd all be better if I could just slam a chair right upside Xavier Kannon's egotistical head. It's hard to believe that five weeks ago, these two men were friends as stablemates in Asylum. But, what started off as a great friendship ended in a bitter rivalry three days after March Madness. For, its that one night that will go down in the history books as the night that Asylum exploded from within, as did the friendship between Xavier Kannon and Jeremy Howard. Jessica: I still say you should let me go to Fusion with you. Jeremy: No. It took Jeremy all of two seconds to answer. Jeremy: I told you already, I have a plan. As Jessica starts to argue with Jeremy, they both notice that the halls of the school have become increasingly bare, with most of the students having made it to class. A bell rings loudly in the halls, signalling the start of class. Jeremy, whose standing side by side with Jessica, turns his head toward her. Jeremy: Well, I think its time to get this taken care of. Jessica: This basketball tournament is going to SO rule. Jeremy starts walking, followed by Jessica close to his side. Along their way toward the principal's office, to make the donation for the basketball tournament, something catches Jeremy's eye. Jeremy stops dead in his tracks, peering toward a ladies bathroom. In the section that blocks hall way walkers from watching the females handle their business inside, a group of three well built men have a petite Puerto Rican girl blocked against a wall. One of the Men: I told you bitch, you're going to do as I say. The man, wearing a gold chain around his neck, holds her by the throat. The Puerto Rican girl sobs, holding her hands up, not knowing what to do. A different man than the one shouting, whose got on a FUBU jacket, chooses to grope the poor petite female's chest. She's so scared, she barely resists. She's crying uncontrollably, fearing for what's going to happen next now that everyone else is in class. Puerto Rican Girl: Please no..... No.... Jessica, having been through an ordeal similar to this, reacts. Jessica: I think she said no! Standing in the doorway leading into the female's bathroom is only Jessica, flaunting her black leather pants. Obviously, the third man in the group took this as an invitation..... Bad move. The Third Man: I suppose you want some too? Man in FUBU jacket: Just grab her and bring her in here. She can have some too. The Third Man: You want me, don't you? He grabs Jessica by her wrist, getting ready to pull her into the bathroom along with the scared Puerto Rican girl. That is, until all three of these men are graced with the presence of the single most maniacal motherf^cker to ever grace the face of this Earth, Jeremy Howard. Jeremy: I think the lady said no. Jeremy, nonchalantly, causes the third man to break his grasp of Jessica's wrist. The third man, differentiated from the others because of a tattoo on his right forearm, tries to play things off. Man with Tattoo: Man, we were just trying to have some fun. Jeremy: Fun, huh? All three men knod their heads, 'yes'. Man wearing Chain: Aren't you that wrestling guy, Krayzie? A smile comes to Jessica's face. Jessica: Hell yes it is! Jeremy: That would be me. So, can you tell me why your friend had his hand on my girlfriend's wrist? Man in FUBU jacket: Man, I'm like your biggest fan man. I can't wait until you beat Kannon within an inch of his life. Jeremy: A fan? You're my fan? The man in the FUBU jacket knods his head, agreeing with Jeremy. Man with Tattoo: So, you down with us man? That maniacal look crosses Jeremy's face. Right now, as these men watch his every movement, Jeremy Howard morphs into Krayzie within his mind. And, when Jeremy becomes Krayzie, nothing is out of the question. Rather, it's like meeting the edge of insanity. Jeremy: Hell f^cking no! You're not my fans. This takes the three men by surprise. Their eyes widen. Jeremy: My fans don't disrespect a lady like this. And, furthermore, if you don't remove your hands from her chest, I'm going to break it. Man in FUBU jacket: Ain't no reason to get all hyped up. Jeremy: No reason? Jeremy grabs the man, blocks a punch, then slams the teenage boy into the wal,l placing his forearm into the man's throat. His air passage is cut off, making it hard for him to breathe. Jeremy: You think this is all funny, don't you? How about this lady? She's scared out of her mind because of your sick joke! Losing a fan like you would be my pleasure! Jeremy moves his forearm away from the man's throat, shoving him to the ground. The man with a tattoo starts to go for a punch to Jeremy's face, but thinks better of it because he's probably seen what this man can do as witnessed many times in PCW. The petite Puerto Rican girl rushes over to Jessica's arms, trying to find some sort of comfort in Jeremy's lady/valet. Man wearing Chain: Spider. The man with the tattoo turns toward his friend. Man with Tattoo: Huh? Man wearing Chain: What do you say we make a statement do our wrestling friend here. He seems to think we're some kind of pushovers. The man known as Spider, probably because of the web tattoo on his right forearm, withdraws a switchblade from his pocket. He knods his head, revealing a sly grin. The man wearing the six point star chain around his neck look at Jeremy, smirking. Man wearing Chain: Not the big shot anymore, are we? Jeremy: You think that little pick stick is going to drive any fear into me? Crack! Before the man with the tattoo can make any use of his pathetic weapon, Jeremy drops him to the ground with his patented superkick. The man's head slams into the concrete tile wall, then he falls forward onto the hard ground. His friend, whom wears the chain, looks down at his friend, 'Spider'. Now, its time for Jeremy to get hostile and make his point. Jeremy: Obviously, you don't have a f^cking clue who I am. Jeremy pauses, allowing the man to bring his head back to facing the Krayzie One. Jeremy can sense the fear beginning to rise within this man's soul. One of his friends is out cold on the ground and the other is just beginning to stir. Is he next to feel the same fate? Jeremy: I don't care if your a Scientologist or a two bit thug. I'm the motherf^cking Krazed Lunatic! Jeremy points toward his chest three times, exclamating the point. Puerto Rican Girl: Yeah! Jeremy: And, if I ever hear of any shit like this again, I'll show you why I earned the nickname..... This is my city and I'll be damned if trash like you ruins it. By now, the man in the Nike jacket has gotten back to his feet. But, neither man standing is willing to come within an inch of Jeremy Howard. Apparently, they caught the match in which the Krayzie One defeated all of Management en route to a victory over Tyler Nelson. So, these three would be a piece of cake. Jessica: You're lucky. This time, he's got better things to do. Jessica, ushering the Puerto Rican girl to follow with her, begins making her way toward the principal's office to seal the deal on the charity basketball tournament. Jeremy, on the other hand, continues to staredown these vile men who sought harm on a female twice as small as them. Jeremy: Ever meet a psychotic ending? The scene begins fading away, as the camera begins to close in on Jeremy's face. Just this maniacal stare alone is probably doing more damage to these men than violence. Because, the carnage that rests within the eyes of Jeremy Howard has only yet to be revealed. Suddenly, Jeremy begins walking into the bathroom, all alone with these three men..... to black!
