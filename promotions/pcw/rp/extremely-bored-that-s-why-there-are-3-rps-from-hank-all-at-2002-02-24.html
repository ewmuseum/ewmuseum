Extremely bored that's why there are 3 rps from Hank all at once... Just getting on the net again tonight as well with three all typed up.

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 ::Segment Three:: --- Scene sets with Hank, Ganker, and Janelle in Duzza's Black Bentley pulls into the PCW parking lot followed by a blue ford pick-up with Frank and his wife. They all get out of their vehicles and meet at a wooden table, Hank places his Extreme Title belt on that wooden table. Hank: Dude, I might have to dump Janelle for yo wife. --- Janelle smacks her man Hank for being stupid. Janelle: I wouldn't let you, it took me too long to realize that you felt the way you did and my feelings for you. I wouldn't just let you toss me to the side. Hank: Wasn't serious babe. Janelle: I know, but just thought I'd get in a good word and let you know that anyway. (smiles) Hank: That's my Janelle, the woman I love. Ganker: Go get geared up for yo match, yo only have fourty-five minutes. Hank: Plenty of time to talk and get ready. Follow me if you will please. --- They actually listen to Hank and follow him to the locker room of Team Nirvana. They enter and take their seats, Frank and his wife on the black leather couch, Janelle on Hank's black leather recliner chair, and Ganker left with the brown fold up chair, metal even. Hank sits grabs his gym bag and walks into his bathroom to get ready. Ganker: So, what yo think of the locker room digs? Frank: Nice, I can honestly say I can understand why somebody would want to be one of the PCW Elite like Hank. Ganker: And me... Frank: You're one of those conceited people aren't you? Ganker: Can yo blame me? Janelle: Oh great, here it comes again... (sighs) Frank: What have I done? Ganker: (thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! Frank: (slapping forehead) Damnit~! --- Just then Hank comes out with black wrestling tights, no shirt, long black leather trench coat, black elbow braces, black knee braces, black wrestling boots, white wrist bands, and a black headband. Hank: Ah, all ready to beat the hell outta LeBlanc and Core, so refreshin' of a feelin'. Ganker: Wouldn't know and wouldn't care if I did. Hank: Freak... Ganker: Damn proud of it. (laughs) Hank: (laughing) Me too~! Frank: Nice television. --- They all stare at the flat screen television personally ordered by Hank and Ganker themselves. Frank: That what we watching the show on? Hank: Yup... Ganker: I might get a ringside view, if yo know what I mean. Hank: Interfere and I hack yo sack. Ganker: Damnit, I offer my services to help and he threatens me for it. Hank: (grins deviously) Yup~! Ain't it jus' so damn grand? Ganker: No... Hank: Come one Janelle, we need to headout to the ring for my match. Janelle: Cool. Ganker: Ho... Hank: (interrupting) Ganker, it's different when she interferes to help me and when yo do, she's my woman who I love and yo jus' one of my bestfriends, tag team partner as it turns out. Ganker: Cool, I get to be lazy and hangout back here. Frank: Can we hang him for you? Ganker: (quivers) HELP~! Don't leave me alone with him~! (scared sh*tless) Frank: (laughing) Just kidding little buddy. Ganker: (relieved) Whew... Hank: (laughing) Come Janelle, I have a match. Janelle: I know, I look forward to it. --- Scene fades out to absolute/complete/utter nothingness as Janelle and Hank exit the locker room, leaving Ganker, Frank and his wife, Ana enters and sits in the black leather recliner chair. Segment Ends
