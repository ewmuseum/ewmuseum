Date: Season II, Episode 4 ﻿*********************************************The Snow ShowSeason II, Episode 4The Way the Water Forms************************************ Location: Hattoni MansionDate: February 20, 2003Time: 2:30 pm “Don’t you guys ever turn a fucking light on?” Snow joked as he entered the dim office of JustinHattoni, the young Don of the Hattoni family. Justin was sitting behind his desk, as always. Hisnumber one henchman, Petey the Vise was leaning against a bookshelf at the wall opposite thedoor. Johnny Silver was there too - sitting in an antique green chair to the right of the Justin’strademark heavy oak desk. Everything was imposing. “Just in case the Feds try to get some snap shots when Petey has a smoke by the window,”Johnny Silver said with a smile, glancing over his shoulder at the Petey. “How’ve you been,kid?” “Been better,” Snow answered truthfully. Life was crashing down around him at the moment, asit always seemed to be. Krista’s pregnancy was a dangerous chemical when it came in contactwith his explosive temper, and of course, his explosive temper was the reason he was invited tothe Hattoni mansion. “How about you, Johnny? How’s your mother?” Johnny nodded without giving an answer. “You know, Jason, you did quite a number on the Verrelli kid,” Petey spoke up as he pulled acigarette from the inside of his black blazer and headed toward the window, his designatedsmoking area. “I fucking knew it,” Snow said with an agitated grin. “I knew this was about that little fuckingprick.” “Glad you’re so concerned,” Justin Hattoni said. The room always seemed a little extra silentafter Hattoni spoke. “The kid’s been in and out of the fucking hospital for two weeks.” “He’s lucky he hasn’t been in the morgue the whole time.” Snow was absolutely remorseless,no matter how much it conflicted with Hattoni. “He was a member of the family,” Johnny Silver said. Justin, behind his desk, remained silentthough his eyes became instantly hardened on the only blond in a room of dark, and silver,haired Italians. “He wasn’t made,” reasoned Petey in a small attempt at a real defence for Snow, other than theremorseless quips that the young man was satisfying himself with. “His father was made,” Johnny said. “His father was a great man.” “I don’t care who his fucking father was,” Snow said, his voice becoming a shade more angry. “Jason,” Justin Hattoni piped in, “I should let you know that it’s in your best interest to loweryour voice.” Snow kept himself silent but his eyes spoke every curse he had inside of him for Marty Verrelli,the father of Krista’s unborn child. Snow wanted to murder Verrelli in cold blood a few weeksback when the incident in question occurred. All in all, he came pretty close. “I get the point,” Snow finally spoke up, and without waiting for a word, he left the office. *** Location: Snow’s ResidenceDate: February 22, 2003Time: 10:05 am She woke up with her arms cradling her stomach. Sometimes Krista thought she could feel thechild inside her, but she knew that it was just her imagination. She wasn’t that far along yet. She had seen Marty once, in the hospital, since the savage beating that Snow gave the youngman. “Marty,” she said to him with a choke when she saw him lying in the bed on that day withbloodstains all over his face. He had been handsome before. “Hey,” he said as he managed to smile. One of his front teeth was chipped. Another was sostained with blood that it had turned pink. “I was wondering if you were going to come.” “I’m so sorry,” she said, unable to hold the tears back. “I’ve had a lot of time to think in here, Krista,” he said to her as he took her hand. “I know wehaven’t known each other for long, but I think maybe this baby isn’t such a bad thing. Badlytimed, maybe.” “We don’t have to keep the baby,” Krista said, squeezing Marty’s hand. She had never believedin abortion before, but now, in these circumstances, it seemed so much more humane than any ofthe alternatives. She wasn’t ready for a child. “Krista,” Marty said quietly as tears filled his swollen eyes. “I don’t think I want to do that.” She hadn’t expected him to say that, so she quickly turned the conversation to shallower matters.
