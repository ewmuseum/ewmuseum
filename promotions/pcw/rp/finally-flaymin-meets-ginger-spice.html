Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin had infiltrated the pesky intercom of Ginger Spice’s mansion…. [Scene picks up where we last left off, with Flaymin dodging stray bullets as he busts into the mansion of Ginger Spice. He runs across the freshly mowed lawn and jumps over the veranda and onto the hard marbled floors.He writhes in pain for a moment, before he gets up and dusts all the grass off his shoes. He looks around to make sure he isn’t attracting any attention before he decides to walk around the house in the hope of finding some sort of back entrance. In the corner of his eye, Flaymin notices a gigantic pool with a flowing fountain in the centre of it. Flaymin tilts his head in interest and gazes at the aqua wonderland before his eyes] Flaymin: Should I take a quick dip in the pool? Damn it, why is life full of so many f*cking crossroads! [Flaymin decides against taking a dip in the pool and continues to walk….. however once again his eyes revert back to the sparkling pool and the gushing fountain….] Flaymin: Quick dip.One minute. [Unable to control his urges, Flaymin walks towards the pool to have a quick swim. He opens the gate to the pool and closes it behind him. He puts down his bag and begins to take off his shirt only to hear a thick British accent berating him] Ginger: ‘ey, what the fuck do you think your doing, eh? [Flaymin turns around only to see Ginger Spice furiously marching towards him] Flaymin: I was uh…. Cleaning the pool. [Flaymin picks up his bag once again] Flaymin: See, I’ve got my tools in here. Ginger: You fit a pool cleaner in that small bag? Flaymin: Sure.Its one of those connecting pool cleaners…. You know, like lego. [Geri turns around and signals to her bodyguard to come over and escort Flaymin out of her property] [Flaymin drops the bag in his hands and begins to plea to Ginger] Flaymin: No! No!Please, just let me explain! Ginger: *sigh* You have three seconds. Flaymin: I’m here on a mission to make the world a better place. Ginger: Wow… you really do take your pool cleaning seriously… Flaymin: No, not that! Tell me, what are the similarities between Captain Planet and The Mighty Morphin Power Rangers? Ginger: Well…. They definitely don’t clean pools. Flaymin: FORGET ABOUT THE DAMN POOL!! [Geri takes a step back in shock at Flaymin’s sudden outburst] Flaymin: Individually, the Power Rangers and the Planeteers are nothing.But when they combine forces out pops that giant robot and Captain Planet! [Geri rolls her eyes] Geri: And the moral of this story is…. Flaymin: I can’t believe both those shows got axed, buuuut that’s besides the point.I just wanted to show you that individually The Spice Girls blow..Lets face it;You’re Yoga videos suck…Posh Spice on the other hand… well, I’m sure she’s quite good at sucking.Scary Spice has bad teeth…Sporty is an ugly lesbianAnd as for Baby Spice- the poor girl is no good if she’s not on her back with her legs in the air! Do you see what I’m getting at here? [Geri stares at Flaymin in bewilderment] Geri: You might have to draw me a diagram… Flaymin: Ok, that explanation might be a little confusing. I know! [Flaymin sticks his pinky finger up his nose and emerges a second later with some snot on his hand while Geri watches on in disgust] Flaymin: See this booger? Geri: Eeewww!! Flaymins: Its pretty small huh and not all that impressive. But if I pick my nose five times, look how much booger I can whip up. [Flaymin reaches back into the eternal snot supply in his nostrils and pulls out some snot on four more successive occasions. He then puts all the five little boogers together to form one impressive looking giant snot ball] Flaymin: Pretty snazzy huh? Geri: As much as I’d hate to admit it… that does look pretty good. Flaymin: So you see, as individual Spice Girls you have nothing to offer.But with your forces combined you all make a pretty impressive musical group. [Geri strokes her chin in deep thought] Geri: I see….Well, what can I do about it? [Flaymin grabs his nose and squeezes his nostrils together as blood dribbles out] Flaymin: Well the first thing you can do is get me some tissues, I think I re-opened that wound in my nostrils] Geri: Come inside and I’ll fix you up. Now, what plan did you have in mind to re-unite The Spice Girls? [Flaymin puts his arm over Geri’s shoulder as they walk into the house] Flaymin: You just wait and see…… [Scene fades to black]
