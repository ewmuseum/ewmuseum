'90 Back To Haunt Us

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-11-02 Life drifts by within the blinks of an eye. In your last breath of life you realize that there is so much you missed out on. There is so much to comprehend, yet no time left in which to do it. Some feel life is nothing more then a stepping-stone towards something greater in life. Though, when life is premature, when a man dies before his time it is truly a horrific fixation. Murder is the ultimate sin, the peak of abhorrence! And vengeance must be obtained. Flashes of light hit the bloodied walls. Police investigators held their cameras loosely. A sombre feeling overcame each individual in the room. The stench of rotting flesh and unwashed basketball attire infected the clothes and burned the senses of those people. The faux wood walls cried out in pain while the drenched rug remained silent. Italian leather-clad feet cautiously floated over the crime scene in an attempt not to disrupt. A man with a notepad jotted down useless information. Potential motive, details about the crime scene should anything be doctored, and the replies of neighbours and the disoriented parents. He surveyed the room, gazing at every corner. His face was expressionless, like he did not care what he was seeing, some may call it professionalism others would call it the every day life of a police investigator. A single strand of bleached blonde hair hung down, covering Jamie’s right eye. He was wet all over. His hair did not stand up in its usual way, instead it soaked down and lay, drenched on top of his head. His blue denim jacket and ‘Celtics’ t-shirt beneath it stuck to his skin as drips fell from the bottom of them. reality. He stood motionless, with his mouth open slightly. People pushed passed him in the small room; they looked at him in an aggravated way, as he did not have the ‘courteousness’ to move out of the way for them. Though Jamie did not care about such things, he seemed to care about nothing. His mind distant, away from his body. He thought back to the time he had spent with the man that lay on the blue-carpeted floor before him, with cold blood slowly dripping from his wide-open mouth. The expression on Jamie’s face was one of utter remorse. Like he felt guilty for not even saying good-bye to this man. He knew it was not his felt, yet still, culpability still filled his veins. “Time of death, 17:56.” One of the men in white jackets around the corpse said into a small tape recorder. Though Jamie already knew the truth, these words sent a chill down his spine. He hung his head lower than it already was. He looked at the blue carpet as a drop of water splashed from his face onto the floor, was it a raindrop? No. Was it perspiration? No. It was a single tear that summed up the emotion Jamie felt at the moment, but he knew deep down is his heart that the melancholy he felt then would soon turn into fury. A hand rises up and rests on Jamie’s shoulder. “Are you okay Jay?” A low yet tender voice asks. “No, no I’m not Joey.” Jamie replied truthfully. Everyone in the room was either a Celtic, police or a medical worker. It was a room in one of the Celtics very own warehouses, where Chris Keily was working that very night. He wore blue jeans and a Boston Celtic’s basketball shirt, which was completely tarnished with blood. Jamie did not feel like concentrating on his match with Ali Khadifi on Fusion, for he knew his heart was just not in it. After the emotion that inflicted Jamie’s soul had been released, a thought came to his head… “Who would dare to implement a deed such as this?” He thought carefully pondering every enemy the Celtics had ever faced… “The Triads, The Diablos, the Westies, the Hoods? No… they would realize the brutal repercussions that would come with a deed such as this, they knew not to go this far.” Jamie continued to contemplate; more gangs and thugs went through his head. They zoom through his mind all seeming less likely then the last. Jamie’s head span like never before, he had to depict the people behind this barbaric act! “Hey! There’s a note here!” Jamie is awoken from his trance by the words from one of the officers. “It says ‘Remember ‘90’. What the hell does that mean?” He asked bewilderedly. Though Jamie knew exactly what it meant. It made every bone in his body ache, it made his mouth go dry and it sent a sensation through his body. He then realized who exactly it was that had killed Chris for ’90 was coming back to haunt the Celtics and vengeance was on the cards.
