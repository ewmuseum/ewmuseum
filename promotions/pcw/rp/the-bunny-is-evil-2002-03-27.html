The Bunny is Evil!

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/02 For most people in America, and all over the world, with the exception of Jews, Muslims, Hindus, Buddhists, Shintos, Taoists, Confucianists, and anyone who still believes in mythology, Easter is an important time of the year. It celebrates when Jesus gave up his own life to pay for all of humankind's sins. It celebrates the life of Jesus and the great things in life he did. Also, on television networks, Easter is a time for showing the movie 'The Ten Commandments' starring Charlton Heston for the 48th time. Good Friday is the day that Fear Factory will win the tag team titles... Easter also has a special meaning to Rudolph von Hackenmann... It is the perfect time to decieve and to do evil. No, Rudolph isn't going to poison people with candy, like some sick bastards do on Halloween... Rudolph has another plan in mind... (The scene slowly fades in to show Rudolph von Hackenmann, standing in a purple bunny costume, in the middle of the main room of his shoe factory. Standing next to him is Mason. Rudolph and Mason are talking about their plans for the day...) Rudolph: This has to be my most devious plan yet... I got hired as the Easter bunny this year at K-Mart. I'm gonna give out candy, sure! But, it's gonna be loaded with brainwashing potion! (Mason turns his head toward Rudolph, wanting to hear more.) Rudolph: Yeah. Then, I'll make the kids give some candy to their parents and brainwash them too. Finally, I'll tell them some stuff, and they will believe it! (Mason sighs.) Mason: And? Rudolph: That's it... (Mason sighs.) Mason: Well, can I come with you? (A frown of 'no' comes across the face of Rudolph.) Rudolph: No. Mason: C'mon! I'll help you distribute candy! Rudolph: No. I can do it myself. I'm supposed to be the Easter bunny! The Easter Bunny doesn't have sidekicks with deadly fevers! (Mason, with a look of understanding, but sadness, on his face, walks to the upstairs. Rudolph grabs a large basket full of candy off of the conveyor belt nearest the door and walks out it, with the scene slowly fading away... The scene fades back in to show the front of a K-Mart store, with all of its small gum vending machines, and the plush toy machine to the side. On one of the benches right in front of the door sits Rudolph with a basket of candy. A couple with a small child walks by.) Rudolph: Happy Easter! Would you like some candy? (The store is dead today, and nobody seems to wants Rudolph's candy. Through the sliding glass door comes another couple with two children. They stop and look at Rudolph. He sees the perfect opportunity.) Rudolph: Happy Easter! Would you like some candy? (The two adults walk over and grab some candy out of the basket. Rudolph's face grins in an evil smile.) Rudolph: I finally got my first victim... I mean, happy people! Yes, my first happy people! (Suddenly, the door opens and Mason walks in. Not, paying attention, Rudolph starts to talk.) Rudolph: Happy Easter! Would you... (Rudolph looks at Mason.) Rudolph: What the hell are you doing here? (A child leaving the store looks over at Rudolph.) Child: Mommy! The Easter bunny just swore at that weird guy! (The mother of the child smacks him in the head and pulls him out of the store by his arm. Rudolph gets severely angry.) Rudolph: See! You're making me lose victims... I mean happy people! (A young male walks into the store and looks at Mason.) Man: Damn dude! Take a shower! Mason starts to growl lowly under his breath. Another man walks into the store and shudders at the sight of Mason. Mason goes all out on this one.) Mason: SHUT THE HELL UP YOU! (The man runs into the store, scared. Rudolph sighs.) Rudolph: Sit down... (Through the door comes another family with a child. The child walks toward Rudolph's candy basket, but his mother grabs his arm.) Mother: You stay away from that disgusting man... (Rudolph starts to get slightly more angry at Mason.) Rudolph: I told you... Now parents won't let their children have candy because you're here! Mason: Yeah, I guess you were right... (Mason slips his hand into the basket and grabs a piece of candy. He puts the candy in his mouth.) Rudolph: Don't eat! That... (Mason starts to slouch on the bench.) Rudolph: I think it's time for you to go home! (Mason gets up.) Mason: I guess so. (Mason walks out the door and Rudolph laughs. Through the door leading into the store walks an old, bald, fat man in a K-Mart vest, white shirt, and black pants. He walks up to Rudolph with an angry frown on his face.) Fat Guy: We've got a lot of complaints that you were talking with a filthy guy. Rudolph: Yes sir. He's my annoying stalker of a tag team partner. (The fat guy, apparently the manager, laughs at Rudolph.) Rudolph: I'm fired, aren't I? Manager: Yup! Rudolph: Damn... (Rudolph walks out of the store with his Easter Bunny suit on, and the scene slowly fades to darkness...)
