Date: Season I, Episode 17 (SEASON FINALE) ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason I, Episode 17 (Season Finale)“Life’s Going to Change, Young Man”************************************ Location: Snow Residence, New York City SuburbsDate: January 23, 2003Time: 8:40 pm “Pay them,” Snow said to his lawyer over the phone. “Pay all three of them.” His hands were trembling. The lawyer was silent for a long moment. “You want to pay them?” he asked. “But Jason! We’re closer now than ever.” “I don’t care,” Snow told his lawyer. “This has gone on long enough. I want all three of thosebastards paid, in full, by tonight.” “Tonight!?” the lawyer exclaimed. “Jason, you don’t understand! There’s paperwork! There’sa process.” “I’ll be home if you need me,” Snow said. “Make it happen.” With that, he hung up the phoneand rolled up sleeve to take a look at the “X” that Justin Hattoni carved onto the inside of hisright forearm. It was the sign of a Made man in the Hattoni family. He sighed and put his sleeve back in place. *** Location: Snow Residence, New York City SuburbsDate: January 24, 2003Time: 9:30 pm When he walked through the front door of his house, Snow hoped his family wouldn’t notice theblood on his right pant leg. Airport security didn’t notice - he was confident that his familywouldn’t either. As he tossed his winter jacket over a chair and brushed the snow flakes from his neatly slickedblond hair, he couldn’t wait to get upstairs and go to bed. Murdering three people, each athousand miles apart, is a hard day’s work. Now, the families of the three men who were suingSnow would get to enjoy the multiple millions that he had just paid them. Of course, it was only a matter of time before the Feds made the connection between the threemurders. That’s when Justin Hattoni’s men would swear that Snow was at the presentation of“Starlit Morning” with them. Still, it would be all over the news soon enough. Snow hoped tobe in Shanghai when the story hit. They’ll fear you, Justin Hattoni’s words rang through his head. Everyone will fearyou. He closed his eyes for a moment, standing still in the kitchen, and lost track of time. When he found himself, he was consumed by the warm embrace of his tiny wife. She wassmiling so sweetly. He faked what he could. Without a word, she took him by the hand and intothe living room. Snow was glad that the television was off. Krista was waiting there, smiling also, even though her eyes seemed hollow. “I’ve got news,” Raven told him as she sat him down on the sofa between her and her youngsister. “Big news. I’ve been waiting to tell you because I wasn’t sure how to break it to you -but with your trip to Asia coming up, I guess now’s as good as ever.” “News?” Snow said blankly, though he was pretending to smile. “What news.” “Good news,” Raven said sweetly. Snow looked over his shoulder to the younger sibling, whowas still smiling. He should have realized what the news was, but men are naive sometimes -especially men with a million things on their mind. Snow’s mind was ready to implode under the pressure. The fact that he was a murderer, Krista’snew boyfriend and taste in men in general, and of course, Sunday’s date in Shanghai. He stillhad no idea what hell Clyde was bringing to Asia with him. And then, she gave him the news. “I’m pregnant,” she said, beaming. Snow was expressionless. Raven didn’t seem to notice. She threw herself into his arms and clutched him tightly by theback of his white t-shirt. He wondered if there were blood stains back there. Raven wouldn’tnotice anyway - she was too happy. And deep down, Snow was wonderfully happy. The newswas just too much for him to process on top of everything else going on. And then, over his wife’s shoulder, he spotted Krista. Tears were streaming down her face andshe was beginning to shake. For the first time, he began to think of how oddly she had beenacting lately. “What’s wrong?” he asked her with a straight face. Raven finally released her joyous deathgripand turned to her sister, unable to understand why she had received the news with tears ofsadness on two separate ocassions. “I’m pregnant too,” answered Krista softly. It was the calm before the storm. With her sisterand brother-in-law gaping through wide eyes, Krista bawled hard in her own little hands. Herhair was soon soaked in tears, and when she looked up at the people she loved most in the world,she realized just how big of a mistake she made with Marty Verrelli, a mere month ago.
