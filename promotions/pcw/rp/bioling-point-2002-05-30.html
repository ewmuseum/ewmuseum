Bioling Point

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-30-02 'I'm Ready,' I said as I sat down upon the hard bed of one of the WITs. It was early on Friday, and I was refreshed after a few days in the hospital. I'm no doctor, but one would surely acquiesce to the fact that it was stress keeping me down for the most part, not injuries from the car accident. My stay had been pleasant, as I divided my time between stirring through the deepest thoughts in my head, and scribbling down essays to later compile into an anthology I titled The Warrior Chronicles. While for those days I had been ambitiously leaping into an egression, it was time to get back to work. I knew that within a few hours I would be facing off against Kid Krazie, Heaven Rayne and Mark Makonie for the Rising Star Title, and I needed to train. I was looking to start off my stay in PCW with a good record, and did not want my reputation disparaged by lack of preparedness. It was time that I ignore the troubles of my life--which just happened to be at a culmination--and focus on the one thing I really needed to: wrestling. 'Okay, WITs, listen up,' explained Minion to the three Warriors In Training. We all sat together in the WITs' quarters, a small room at the back of an abandoned shop. 'The training exercise we are about to attempt is one that requires much thinking, and that requires much constraint on the part of the Infinite Warrior. Your task is to say whatever you can that will make Trigger angry. His part is to take to heart your comments, but not let his anger get control of him.' I stood up, taking a deep breath. I was a warrior--I am a warrior, but the one thing I have never been able to fully control is my rage. It storms up inside of my like a fire when I least expect it, taking me and all witnesses off guard. The idea of this training project came to me as I watched a promo by Mark Makonie. I am a mysterious man, but from what he could muster up about me, Makonie immediately realized that I have an anger-management problem. I do not know what was more ignorant of him… to think my anger is a weakness, or that he let me know he thinks so. Either way, I would use his observance against him… that is if I could accomplish the difficult feat that lay before me. WIT 1 was first… his name unluckily came chronologically first, and therefore he was always at the beginning of the line. As always, the trainee started off a bit slow, unsure of how deep an insult to attempt. 'Umm… you're so weak that PCW booked you against a girl!' I wanted to laugh. Did he truly think such a petty indignity would take away my will? 'Oh well,' I thought. 'He will learn.' 'It is true, young WIT,' came my reply, 'that one of my opponents for this evening is female… but not just any girl is Heaven Rayne. In fact, of all the new wrestlers that have come to Primetime Championship Wrestling over the past few weeks, she has been called the best. I do not look at her as being weaker than me in any way… perhaps even stronger. She will be a challenge. But I will not back away.' Blushing, WIT 1 turned away. Next up was WIT 2… he was quite different from the previous, and when it came to answering questions on the spot, WIT 2 was definitely the best. Indeed, his comments sparked a bit of emotion in my warrior soul, but I didn't give any signs of letting my guard down. 'In your debut match against Duzza,' started my pupil, 'Goeren interfered and kicked you're ass so bad, you didn't know what hit you.' It took me several moments to find the correct response, but once I did so, my harsh words chilled the room of any comfort. 'Perhaps that is so… Perhaps that is so… But you must understand one thing: he interfered. You cannot judge my strength by the way I perform taken off guard after already competing in a grueling match. Aaron Goeren may have used cheap tricks and succeeded in attacking me… but do not fret because there will be hell to pay. Mark my word, when I get my chance, I will be ready to strike. And when I strike, not thinking of the past, present, or future… not thinking of the benefits of my actions, or possible consequences… when I strike, I will strike harder, faster, and more frightening than you have ever dreamed of me doing. Aaron Goeren has a reason to fear. When I get my chance… everyone has a reason to fear…' Perhaps it was the fact that my voice was not angry as I said the words, or perhaps it was simply because the meaning behind them was true. I was ready to destroy Goeren. After the WITs had fully recovered, which seemed like forever, it was the third WIT's turn to tempt the hatred inside of me. He expanded on the last question in such a disrespectful manner that I have come to know him by. 'Trigger… you're all talk. You make threats; just like you did concerning Goeren, but do you ever act on them? No! All you ever do is talk.' It was interesting seeing the WITs prepare insults to hurt my character. After all, they had spent the past two months learning sternly to not mess with me or Minion… What made them think I wouldn't snap now? What made them think I wouldn't grab one of them by their scrawny little necks and throw them out the goddamn window?! 'Cool down,' I told myself. If my wrath got in the way at Friday Fusion, then I would have no chance at gaining the Rising Star Championship. I refocused on the situation, and gave WIT 3 a reply that stunned everybody. 'Is that so, WIT? Am I all talk, as you say? No… I believe you are wrong. Yes, I make threats. Yes, I speak with vigor, forcing the victims of my fury to take notice. But am I all talk? No. I just poured out my emotions to you--constrained as they may have been--which I feel towards Aaron Goeren. He brutally harmed me… he almost cost me a match… I want revenge. Revenge has an odd way of approaching me, WIT. It seems as if whenever I really, really want it… I find a way of achieving it.' The WITs and Minion were still confused. What was I talking about? What was my point? Oh, they would find out soon enough. 'It is a lucky thing that what I just described to you occurs often enough. If I had no way of receiving justice for others' actions against me, I feel as I would not be able to contain my vicious ire. Keep in mind, WITs, that if I happen to blow, everyone is coming down with me… But it seems the good people at Primetime Championship Wrestling have fulfilled my needs, as they have booked me and Duzza in a handicapt match this Tuesday against Goeren.' I hesitated, then added with a smirk, 'Revenge will be mine!' It was now time for The Insane One himself, Lance Minion, to attempt to irritate my mental equilibrium. In the back of my mind, I couldn't help but feel a little worry. This was Minion's specialty… he was master of mind games. His tactics were at all times relentless, and even a strong warrior like I could not fend off his evil tricks. 'You are going to be no match for Mark Makonie or Kid Krazie tonight. They are seasoned veterans, and you are nothing but a jobber. They have both had experience wrestling in PCW… Krazie was the previous holder of the Rising Star Championship.' The Insane One continued. 'And Mark Makonie? Do you think he's going to take it easy on you? Do you think he'll give you the respect that you so eagerly demand. Makonie hates you. He hates everyone. And between he, Makonie, and Rayne… you don't stand a chance!' I was surprised. In fact, I was more than surprised… I was disappointed. Lance Minion, the Master of Mind Games, had truly let me down in this half-hearted attempt to weaken me. 'Makonie can hate me if he likes. Rayne can get all the rave reviews she wants. Krazie can win as many titles as he can hold. That still doesn't change one thing: I am a warrior. If they have it in them, all three of my opponents will give me their all. If I have it in me… which I most certainly do… than I will counter their attacks, and I shall deliver my own.' This game was getting interesting. After having gone through all four men, we began over again, starting with WIT 1. This time around, I could tell that he would have a better comment. I saw the gleam in his eyes that could only be pride. He spoke without being asked, anxious at first, but then quite hesitant, as if he was saying something he shouldn't be. 'Trigger… on the day that you got hit by the car, when we visited you in the hospital, you claimed that you had been out looking for us on that morning. We all said that we had been in the abandoned shop, just like always. Do you remember?' I nodded. Of course I did! They could tell me all they wanted that it was only a bump to my head to cause such a ludicrous thought, though I knew in my heart that there was foulness in play. I cannot deny what I saw with my own eyes… they were not at the abandoned shop that day. Cringing, I remembered feeling so surely that Minion had something to do with it. I hated myself for finally giving in to the earnest statements of the WITs. I guess deep down I had just wanted things to get back to how they were… I wanted things to get back to good. I swallowed hard as WIT 1 continued on. 'Well… Well when we told you that we had not been out that day… when we told you that we were at the abandoned shop… we lied.' In a split second my fiery eyes darted like bullets towards WIT 1, at the same time along with Minion, who stared just as furiously. I felt my anger boiling up down inside of me, yearning to come out. I physically had to fight down the rage, as it tried to unleash itself against the others in the room. There have been few times in my life that I had felt such anger inside of me… I almost did not know what to do. I stood up, trying to choke it down. The fearsome adrenaline rapidly soared to my muscles, and I had an insane sensation to inflict terrible pain. WIT 1 crawled back on his bed as if the shadow of doom was upon him. Everyone eyed me with fear, with the exception of The Insane One, who seemed to have just as much anger as I. Realizing that I had to end this situation fast, I forced my rage to momentarily subside, and strained a smile across my face. 'Very good. All of you have done well. It is time, however, that we stop this form of training, as your gaining skill is weakening my endurance. After all, I must save some energy for my match tonight.' Everyone was happy with my comments. This was not fun anymore, not for anybody. But I was not done yet… 'I cannot stop training now, though. I have missed crucial days as my opponents have been heavily preparing themselves, no doubt, and unless I catch up, there is no telling how poorly I perform. So I say we move on to the next exercise…' Like a separate personality, my anger rose with glee. I addressed all three WITs with a mean scowl. 'This time, I'm going to play a game on you…'
