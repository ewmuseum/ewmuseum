Getting Where? There! Pt.1

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/03/02 'YAHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO' screamed Christopher V Knight as he sat on the floor with his eyes glued to the tv screen. Teddy Knight finally walked into thr room, wih food on a plate he was carrying, he let his free hand go convincingly and letting it smack right across the back of Chris' head. Christopher didn't notice anything as Teddy just woundered what the hell he was at. 'Chris' he laughed a little. 'Are you looking at gay porn again?' he laughed, nearly dropping his food from his own joke. Chris again egnored what Teddy had to say. Suddenly Chris screamed out again. 'TAKE THAT DICKSLAP! AFTER WE'RE DONE WITH YOU, YOUR ASS WILL BE HOTTER THAN KEN HOM'S WOK!' he shouted as Teddy dropped his food back on his plate. Chris still sitting on the floor with his knee's now was pulled around by Teddy. 'What THE HELL ARE you on about?' he asked Teddy. He grabbed the remote and Chris slapped out of his hands, Chris stood up and looked straight at Teddy. 'WE'RE GOING TO, WE'RE GOING TO....' Chris laughed and stopped, pulling himself up and screamed as he jumped in the air 'FUSION AND TURMOIL!' he screamed, as these words were finally pushed into Teddy's head he got up and started jumping around like Christopher, they kept on for about a minute screaming out anything they could to describe their feelings, suddenly Teddy dropped into the sofa. 'DUDE' he sighed in dissapointment for some reason. Chris dropped himself to the floor and landed on his ass he squealed out in pain, but didn't let it get to him. With his legs crossed and arms folded and a goofy dissapointed look on his face he, looked at Teddy. 'WHAT I DONE, WHAT'S HAPPEND?!?!' he quickly asked, as Teddy just sighed again. 'How are we going to get there?' he said. Chris put his little finger in his mouth, the immitation of Dr.Evil was one of the best probably anyone had seen, but that wasn't the point getting to the arena was. 'We can use our skateboards!' said Chris, as Teddy dropped his head in his hands. 'Perhaps th-that w-was a bad idea eh, Teddy?' Chris trembled. Teddy just stood up and grabbed his plate, he put it in the kitchen and then started to walk around the living room, clenching his fists he didn't know what to do. Chris then stood up and looked at his younger, and more intelligent brother. 'Lets dress up as Nuns they get free bus passes, and bus fairs, all the time!' smiled Chris as Teddy woundered why the hell is was with his brother and it had only been three days since they had been in PCW. 'Perhaps' woundered Teddy. 'But i think we need to take a few hours out, get the video camera?' asked Teddy as he pointed to where it was, Chris just looked on at him. 'Where is it?'. Teddy just sighed and grabbed the camera and shoved it in Chris' chest with tremendous force. 'THERE IT IS!' he shouted as Chris smiled. 'SPANK YOU!' he chuckled to himself as both men walked out to the garage! We came back about five or ten minutes, the garage door was open but it was dark as anything. Chris' and Teddy's own home video camera was set up directly outside so it could show all the garage, suddenly a voice came from the darkend car park. 'ONE, TWO, ONE, TWO, THREE, FOUR, GO!' shouted someone as we heard the smashing of drum sticks together. Just then Christopher jumped out into the light, shouting something as he jumped in the air with his famous party bass giutar trick. 'DUDE! EXCELLENTAI!' he shouted, jumping up with his giutar and making the sign of the devil. Only to now able to land right, and fall on his ass. Still he kept playing taking a second each to get up where he was needed, as he kept playing the light in the garage came on, as Teddy Knight was standing there strumming his electric giutar and their friend Jack smashed the drums will full force. 'FREAKIN' YEAH!' laughed Teddy, as he laughed with Jack. Suddenly Christopher jumped up and taking a strum of his bass giutar his strings broke, slashing and poking them in his eye, he dropped to the floor. Teddy and Jack dropped their instruments themselves, and walked over to Chris. They looked down on him and just sighed. 'YOU HAD TO DO THAT AGAIN DIDN'T YOU, JUST TRYING TO IMPRESS THE BIG RECORD LABLES, EH?' moaned Teddy, as he continued as Jack laughed. 'Well let me tell you this, you're not getting a record deal....' Teddy tried to continue but Jack was tapping him on the sholder, still wanting to speak to Chris he turned around at Jack. ' WHAT, DUDE?' Teddy screamed in Jack's face. 'Firstly Teddy, get a breath mint' giggled Jack he then continued. 'Secondly, look over there!' said Jack. As Teddy looked over, and Jack helped Christopher up a limo pulled up to the Suburban Uprising a huge man, around three-hundred pounds of fat stepped out, with gold chains and black glasses on also a cigar in his left hand. Christopher suddenly walked up to him, even if it wasn't in a straight line he got there and he took the cigar out of the big man's hand. 'Can i have smoke?' asked Chris. The man just laughed 'Sure you can, kid. Now who's the brain's behind all of this?' he asked looking at Teddy and Jack. Teddy stepped forward. 'Me! Now what you want?' he asked not really wanting to know what the man had to say, he just wanted to find a way how they could get to the arena for Friday. Suddenly the big man spoke up. 'HEH, HEH, HEH, HEH' he laughed in a deep tone. 'HEH, I'm Dave GottaLotta Money and i want to sign your little boy band to a record deal' he said. 'COOL' Jack shouted as Teddy wasn't convinced. 'Is that all?' Teddy asked. 'HEH, HEH, NO!' continued the Dave GLM. 'I want you guys to start touring right away, and also SELL-OUT with me!' he laughed, grabbing another cigar from his pocket. Suddenly Christopher slipped and fell into Dave GLM's back, the big man couldn't tell really. As now Chris look right at him. 'MAN THAT TOOK ME ABOUT THREE MINUTES TO SMOKE, THAT'S SOME NICE STUFF THERE! GOT ANYMORE?' Chris asked stupidly. Dave just laughed again, as he looked back at Teddy. 'SO YOUZ GONNA SELLOUT WID ME OR VWAT, DUDE?' he asked. Teddy just cut back with something he may regret talking about his favourite band. 'DUDE WE'RE NOT BLINK 182!' he shouted, as Jack looked on astonished. Suddenly Christopher spoke up taking another inhalation of his cigar. 'CAN I BE THE NEW BRITNEY SPEARS! HIT ME BABY ONE MORE TIME!' he shouted, as Jack and Dave laughed. Chris finally took one more inhale of his cigar and dropped it to the floor, he quickly grabbed a garden chair and sat down it, quickly falling asleep. Teddy now looked over at him over his face came an evil grin, he smiled and then walked over to Dave. 'Ok! We'll think about it but you have to do US' as he said 'us' he pointed to Jack, himself and Christopher. Dave smiled and looked at Teddy. 'Hmm i seez, watcha want?' asked Dave, the probably billionaire. 'You can give us something we really need, give us a car!' laughed Teddy. Dave looked at him and shook his head, and then took his cigar in his mouth and lit it up. 'Sure ican do dat! It'll be here tomorrow righton seven o'clock in da morning, and you know something we'll continue you this talk later, you hears me?' said Dave, as he looked at all three dudes. 'SURE!' smiled Teddy, as him and Jack turned away. Dave GLM grabbed the door of his limo, and quickly sped off just leaving nothing but dust behind him, with Christopher asleep after to much....well you know what! Teddy and Jack walked into the Knight Borther's house. They looked at each other and smiled, Teddy knew one thing. 'SURFER'S SUCK!' no that's not it, he knew one thing. He and Christopher were going to Fusion.
