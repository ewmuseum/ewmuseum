Making of a commercial (Part 2) and a vadalization to an important statue...

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-9-02 With Friday Fusion getting cancelled and without a match until Supremency, Mark has more time to prepare for the battle in hell on Sunday night. Jordan X Shabazz in the first match and then he has a contract match at the middle of the pay per view for PCW's new drink. Mark has paid most of his focus on getting Kahlua Iced Coffee to get to be PCW's next drink instead of Jordan at this point. We left Mark right before he was getting ready to shoot the commercial for Kahlua. The scene opens up to where it last was with Mark standing up on the set with the backdrop in a coffee shop. It is computer simulated with people ordering different kinds of coffee and people sitting down, drinking their coffee and talking amongest each other about the latest gossip. 'Action' says one of the workers as he slaps the top half of the slapper down on the rest and the cameras start rolling. 'Don't you just hate coming into the coffee shops to get that great taste of coffee in the morning? says Mark into the camera the is rolling to his face. 'Well now you don't have to have that problem anymore. With the Kahlua Iced Coffee you can get that great flavor of Kahlua and coffee at the same time.' Mark says as he holds up a bottle of Kahlua Iced Coffee and he points to it. Mark looks at Mike, the director, who is right behind the camera that is rolling, giving Mark a thumbs up. After having sweaty palms at first and now being as comfortable as he is at home, Mark starts to loosen up in front of the camera as they still have the cameras rolling. 'With Kahlua being their spokesperson to sell this great non-alcoholic beverage I expect to see this product in everyones hand after Supremency.' says Mark as he starts to untwist the cap off of the Kahlua Iced Coffee. 'This is the next drink of PCW, drink Kahlua Iced Coffee.' Mark says once again as he holds up the bottle in the air and then he takes a drink. One of the workers says cut, the same guy that was in control of the action slapper. Mark gets off of the set and he goes over and he sits in the chair that has the script on it. Mike and the camera men start to talk amongst themselves about Mark's commercial. 'Mark get over here.' says Mike as he points at Mark telling him to come to him also. 'We need to speak with you about your commercial.' Mark just looks at Mike and then he sees Mike finger pointing to Mike signaling for him to get over there so that them two can speak. Mark gets up off of the brown chair and he starts to head over across the dark cement that was laid a couple of years back. It has some cracks in it, but that doesn't matter to anyone who works in the building or who comes by for a visit like what Mark is doing right now. Mark gets over to Mike and the five camera men that were shooting from their cameras. 'Yeah Mike, you wanted to see me over here.' says Mark as he looks at Mike and then at the camera men people. 'About your commercial..' says Mike and then pausing as he looks at the camera men. 'We need to shoot another part real quick because you never take your eyes off of the camera.' 'What are you talking about?' asks Mark having a puzzled look in his face. 'When you looked at me and then paused for a second, we need to redo that part again.' Mike says as he keeps contact with Mike's eyes. Mark shakes his head and he goes over to the set again with the coffee shop still in the background. The worker that had the action slapper heads in front of the camera once again as he says this. 'Kahlua Iced Coffee commercial featuring Mark Makonie take two.' says the worker as he slaps the action slapper down with it making a loud noise, sending Mike a little starttled. 'With Kahlua having me being their spokesperson for their new product, I feel real privleged to try and get this product out to the people.' Mark says as he keeps his eyes on the cameras this time. 'With it being non-alcoholic even the little people can enjoy this significant coffee taste, even better than the coffee shops. Also, with me representing this drink at the PCW pay per view, Supremency, I expect to see this new drink all over the television after I get this to be PCW's official drink. Drink Kahlua Iced Coffee, PCW's next official drink!' Mark says as he lifts up the Iced Coffee and then he takes a drink as the camera fade to black. Mark again goes over to the brown chair that he sat in before. He picks up a magazine that features the model Pam Anderson on the front. Mark likes what he sees and he flips the magazine open to the article on the sizzling model. The magazine shows Pam in her bikini and the crotch of his pants starts to rise to the picture and Mark knows what is going on. He looks down at his crotch and he starts to hit himself there. 'Go down!' says Mark as he hits his crotch and the camera crew and Mike look over at Mark. 'You don't have a right to be up right now because I have a very important event going on at the time.' Mark shakes his head as he waves over at Mike to signal that he is alright. Mark then looks at the Pam article reading about her success on VIP and in the movie Barbwired. Mark's crotch area goes down to normal size after the talk Mark had with it. 'Mark get over here once again.' says Mike as Mark looks up from the magazine. Mark sets down the magazine, still opened to the page with Pam posing in her bikini. Mark starts to make his way across the concrete floor once again as he looks at the camera crew surrounding Mike. Mark's palms start to sweat once again as he goes over to Mike and his chair to have once again another talk. 'Well it looks like your day with us is finished.' says Mike as he looks at Mark right in the eyes. 'All we need to do is go into the editing room and edit this. Then we will get it on the television or we will have you come back if we need to shoot this again.' Mark does not say anything he only nods at Mike and then he picks up his shirt from the ground by the set. Mark then goes across the concrete towards the back door and he puts his hand on the silver door handle that turns in a clockwords motion. He turns the knob and pushes on the metal door at the same time so that the door and open. Mark makes his way outside, letting the metal door slam behind him as he goes out into the heat of the day. The sun is shining and beating down on top of Mark's bald head. Mark takes a pair of blue Oakley Minute sunglasses from one of his front pockets to put them on. 'Lets see what we can do for the remainder of the day.' says Mark as he starts to take off the Kahlua shirt and to put his red shirt back on about the no African Americans. 'We could goto the museum about the Holocaust, about Martin Luther King, or just sit at home.' Mark stops talking to himself and he starts to look around at his surroundings and to find out where he is in town. There is a junk yard across the way from the studio and then there is the Martin Luther King museum with his monument right in front of the entrance to the museum. Mark gets a thought in his mind about the monument and he starts to head down the sidewalk in the middle of the town. 'Where can I find a hardware shop around here?' asks Mark to himself as he heads down the sidewalk even more, people moving with him and people coming at him from the other direction. Mark starts to look around at the buildings and he crosses a street in attmept to find a hardware shop. He sees one down the street and he starts to run towards it, passing people on both sides of him. He gets to the hardware store, dodging a bike coming his way and almost crashing into the shin of his leg. He goes into the hardware store, with the bells chiming from above the door. 'May I help you sir?' asks the old fat guy with the white beard working at the front of the desk. 'Yeah I need a can of your black spray paint.' says Mark looking around at the shop and then looking at the man who asked him the question. 'Be right back sir, just wait here for a minute.' says the old man getting out from behind the desk and going to the aisle with the spray paint in it. Mark heads over to the front counter and he sees what is on the counter. There is the quater game where if you catch a quater you win a free box of nails. Then there are some cards with the business address on it with the name. The old man comes back with his spray paint and he goes behind the desk once again. 'That will be $3.43.' says the man as he scans the paint and setting it up on the counter. Mark pulls out a five from his front pocket which is a little wet from the sweat of Mark's leg. He sets it up on the counter and with his old wrinkly hands, the old man reaches up on the counter and he gets the five dollar bill that Mark put up. The man puts in five dollars at the cash machine and the drawer pops out and hits the old man in the stomach. 'No bag sir!' says Mark as he grabs his paint and his change while running out of the store. A few coins drop on the floor of the store as Mark opens up the door with the bells chiming once again. Mark stuffs the one dollar that he got back in his front pocket that he got his five dollar bill from. Mark comes to the stop light and crosses over to the other side of the street so that he can go to the museum with a stature of Marting Luter King in front of it. 'Lets see how people will react to this act by yet me again.' says Mark as he makes his way up the stairs to the museum. 'Ever since I have been in this town, I have tried to demolish or vadalize every African American statue or figure that I can to prove a point that we white people are better at everything that they think they are better at.' Mark takes the black cap off of the black spray paint and he puts it in his other front pocket of his blue jeans. He looks up at the gray stone statue of the African American man who gave faith to his own race to think that they were better than the white race. Mark gets up on the base of the statue and he starts to do his graffti on the back of Martin Luther King. He spray paints a Confederate Flag on the back and then he gets down from the statue. Mark heads to the front and on the base of the statue he starts to write: Die African Americans or become slaves. 'Get out of here you white f*****!' says a voice from the entrance to the museum. Mark stands up and he takes off running, grabbing the cap from inside in pocket and putting it back on the can. Mark starts to run down the sidewalk, knocking into every African American person that he sees. The scene fades as Mark has his head back, laughing an evil laugh that can be heard from a mile away.
