The past, Robert and a broken heart!

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th June It's almost like a dream for El Diablo! Not only is he able to make and direct his own film, he has a large cast and his rehearsals for the film are already being aired on PCW television, giving his film more exposure to the public! Nothing could be better for El Diablo! Or could all that change? Just what happens next in this fairytale of El Diablo? 'Shhhhhhh!!!!' says the librarian trying to keep the noise to a low level in her establishment. The librarian is an old lady, in her late fifties it would seem, and always has a stern look on her face. She has long, thinning gray hair that is tied into a ponytail with a thin black band. She is sitting behind a replica of an old Victorian table, with a computer behind it on a small pull out table and on the desk are several displays of books as well as a name tab on the desk. 'Mrs J. Heaney' It says in thick gold lettering onto the black background. Mrs Heaney wears a red cotton cardigan and a white shirt underneath it with a curved collar. The library is quite large and has many rows of, again replica Victorian bookcases full of many tales, from L-Ron Hubbard to Roald Dahl and Mikhail Bulgakov to Bill Hicks. As can be seen a wide range of books and tales. Many wooden tables are at the front of the library with only two seats to each, indicating that the library likes quietness and personal reading only. The carpet in the library is a dark gray color, that matches the bleakness of the setting, but nevertheless the library is quite a fine one. There are only a handful of people seated at the front of the library reading, some children, but the majority adults, and of course there will be several people wandering the library looking for the books of their choice. But where is El Diablo in all of this? Surely he's not going to.... In a swift movement the library doors swing open and who walks in but El Diablo. Diablo wears his typical Mexican flag style face paint, and has his long black hair loose as ever. He has on a white t-shirt and black denim jeans with his black sneakers, and around his neck is a video camera. Lord only knows why El Diablo would have that video camera with him in the library. 'Ah Robert, where to film next?' asks the Mexican to his video camera that he has so aptly named Robert. He finishes stepping through the doors and slams them shut right behind him. 'Shhhhh,' hisses the librarian with no courtesy to look up at who was making the noise. El Diablo doesn't look at her either and carries on with his business. 'See that table there, Robert,' whispers Diablo to his camera, 'That could be the star of my next film project,' Just what on earth could El Diablo be thinking? A table being the star of a movie? Uh oh, has Diablo taken a turn for the worse? 'What was that Robert?' asks Diablo to his camera while holding it to his ear, 'You want to what?' continues Diablo. Some of the seated people begin to stare at El Diablo and his strange behaviour. 'No, don't be silly, I can't burn these people,' states Diablo, to a few disturbed glances from those reading their books, 'Can I?' he asks again, while shifting his eyes across the whole library. El Diablo then seems to go motionless and looks around him, all he sees is black and goes into a moment of confusion. POP POP Diablo wonders what the sounds are and looks to his left, and to his surprise he sees a miniature him with a white gown and halo. 'Whoa, whoa, whoa!' states El Diablo in disbelief, 'Not you guys again?' he says. He quickly looks to his right shoulder and sees a mini him in red and horns. 'Oh man, what now?' he asks them as blackness completely fills around him instead of the familiar library. The devil starts to prod him in the cheek with a mini pitchfork. 'OW,' shouts El Diablo in some pain, 'What the hell are you? Some sort of farmer?' Diablo rubs his cheek. 'Is there even any reason why you two are here again?' asks Diablo to his mini selfs. He looks over onto each shoulder and neither one of them speaks. Instead the devil starts prodding him some more with his pitchfork, and the angel has taken off his halo and is using it as some sort of painful frisbee and is throwing it into the side of El Diablo's head. 'Ahhhh, stop it, damn you,' shouts Diablo to the two mini horrors, 'STOP TORTURING ME!!!!!!' screams the Mexican Devil. He tries to fend them off and slowly drops to the floor in pain. He almost seems to black out, but a noise keeps him around. 'Shhhhhh,' it says annoyingly. El Diablo slowly gets to his feet, and realises that there was no devil or angel on his shoulder, but then what happened? He looks across at the librarian who has a book covering her face. 'Could you please stop saying that?' states Diablo annoyed. A short silence occurs. 'It's a library, it's supposed to be quiet,' states Mrs Heaney. She slowly lowers her book, and as she does El Diablo gasps in horror. 'Miss Heaney?' asks a shocked Diablo. She looks at him and squints, then suddenly realises who it is. 'Raul!' she states sternly with the very same look on her face. El Diablo starts to remember back when Mrs Heaney was his English teacher in school and how she used to treat him so badly..... 'Shhhh, there's no time for a flashback, Raul,' she interrupts. Diablo just stands still for once wondering how he has managed to run into an old teacher of his that was so harsh she would make Tyler Nelson look like a kitten. What mess has El Diablo found himself in now? Could this ruin him once and for all? Mrs Heaney slowly gets to her feet and approaches El Diablo, only he doesn't move, he can't move, he can't move due to his fear of the woman. 'Raul, you look stupid with that camera,' she comments harshly. As she does, she reaches round Diablo's neck and unhooks it causing it to drop to the ground and smash into several pieces. 'There, that's better,' she comments, and turns away walking back to her desk. Diablo drops to his knees and holds the bits of the broken camera in his hands and his eyes start to fill up. 'Robert!!!!' he yells, 'What has she done to you?' Tears start to roll down his cheeks slowly. Diablo gets up slowly and stares at the now seated Mrs Heaney. 'You'll pay for this, you evil, evil woman,' he says to her with malice. She looks up again, at him. 'Shhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh,' is all she comments in her annoying tone. Diablo shakes his head, looks down at Robert, and starts to head out of the library with a disheartened look on his face. What now for El Diablo? How will this affect him? And just how will he fare against Heaven Rayne on Friday Fusion after this? It's a truly heartbreaking situation for El Diablo.
