Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 11)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/9/02 In a lonely chamber, the commando known as Raiden holds the lifeless corpse of George W. Bush. Having stood by helplessly as he nuked half the free world, then proceeded to choke to death on a pretzel, he now harbors a state of weary shock. Raiden: What do I.... Jack phones in to the Colonel, breathing heavily. Raiden: Colonel, the president....he's.... Colonel: I know. He died just in the nick of time too.....if he had dropped any more nuclear bombs we'd all probably have been dead in about three days. Raiden: How long do we have now? Colonel: Roughly four days. Raiden: ...... Colonel: But that isn't important. You should follow his last request and go find the scientist stuck down below. Raiden: But the place is probably flooded from the bomb Vampir set off! Colonel: Bring your rubber ducky. Raiden: Huh? Colonel: It always made you feel secure when you were a small child, right? Raiden: Colonel I never had a rubber ducky. On the monitor the Colonel looks on blankly....then reaches into his pocket and produces the mysterious booklet again. Colonel: (Thumbing through) You did so, it says right here in the script. Raiden: The script is wrongAGH!!!! Yet again a wave of pain rocks the balls of Jack. He falls to his knees in agony. Colonel: The script is never wrong. Raiden: (Tearing up) The script is never wrong..... Colonel: Remember that. The Colonel signs off, then just as he does Snake signs on. Snake: How you doing kid. Raiden: (Holding his crotch) Never better. Snake: You look it. Raiden: The president just choked to death on a pretzel, half the known world has been blown up by nuclear missiles and my balls really hurt. Snake: Well at least they haven't started up Ron yet. Raiden looks up somewhat dumbfounded. Raiden: You KNEW about Ron? Snake: Of course. Raiden: Why didn't you tell me? Snake: You never asked. Raiden: That's not a goodOW! Jack falls backwards as somehow or another a rock manages to sail through his monitor and bean him in the forehead. Snake: How soon we forget. Raiden: How....did....you....do....that.... Snake: I'm very tricky. Raiden: You probably knew the Big Shell was just a camu....camanini.... Snake: Huh? Raiden: Shut up, I've been hanging around the president too long! You knew it was a coverup for the development of Ron too didn't you? Snake: Maybe. Raiden: Maybe!? Snake: Well, it all happened around two years ago. I went aboard a tanker right around where you're standing now. I had heard there was a new Metal Gear there....and I was right. Bobby: Yeah. Snake: Shut the hell up. Bobby: (Closing his eyes) Sorry. Snake: Anyway, when I got down there Hosselot showed up. He shot his supposed best friend Ivan Gurlukovitch and the Violator, Fortune Cookie's father. Raiden: But I heard him say he kicked Ivan down a ladder. Snake: Wanna go ask him yourself what really happened or wanna shut up already and let me talk? Raiden: ...... Snake: I don't know what his deal was, but he said something like 'I'm taking it back'. Then....uh....well, I accidentally sunk the tanker. Bobby: Snake's a real screwup. Snake: Bobby, SHUT UP. Bobby: Sorry! Snake: It shouldn't have been a problem, I had a lifeboat prepared for just such an emergency. Snake turns, glaring at Stormbringer. Snake: Or at least I was SUPPOSED to. But someone forgot to bring it along. Bobby: I was busy! Stalking elementary school playgrounds for hot dates isn't easy you know! Snake: Ugh! Regardless, I got out. No thanks to him. Raiden: (Snores) Snake: ..... Again a rock manages to fly seeming out of the screen, smashing into the dozing Raiden's face. Raiden: OW! Snake: Get your bishonen ass down there and find the scientist! Groaning, Jack signs off and makes his way back the way he came, eying the electric floor. Raiden: (Sighs) Knowing no other way across, our hero steps onto the panel with both feet. Raiden: EEAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!!!!! Shuddering and smoking and twitching, Jack again finds himself launched through the air and into a wall on the other side of the death trap. Slumping painfully to the ground, he shakes his head. Colonel: Jack you were aware the master control for the electric floor was in the president's room. Raiden: Shut UP. In between mutterings of death threats and grumblings of pain, Jack stumbles off to a nearby elevator and presses the down button. It begins to lower....he then looks down curiously to find his feet wet. Raiden: Huh? Raiden raises his feet a little, seeing they are now emerged up to his ankles. Then his knees. Then his torso. Raiden: Hey! Racing to the control panel on the elevator, Jack slams the up button as fast as he can. Unfortunately it does nothing, and the water quickly rises to his mouth. Raiden: Aaggggghgurglegurgle! With the water completely filling the elevator, Raiden waves his arms frantically....then the door opens, the box reaching the ground floor. Jack swims as fast as he possibly can, to an opening where he surfaces and gasps for air. Jack: (Breathing) Heeeuuuuuuuuh! Shaking off the effects of panic, Raiden looks about.....then his ear rings. Leah: Jack.... Raiden: (Shutting his eyes) Gotta go. Again our hero dives, drowning out the voice of his girlfriend. Onward, onward he swims through seemingly endless chambers.....eventually getting to a door, which he struggles to open underwater. Raiden: (Pulling) Haaaaaamph. Finally with a creak it does open....and what he sees causes him to silently scream, bubbles rising. Raiden: Mmmmmmph! The body of the bomb disposal expert Vampir drifts towards him. However half his face is missing.....his black skin ripped away to reveal a white female face beneath. Behind him a volume of various books drift, all of which oddly enough have the author's names crossed out in crayon and the word 'Vampir' written above. Raiden: (Stares in horror) Quickly Jack swims past the corpse, into the next chamber where he surfaces with another gasp and holds his earpiece. Raiden: Snake, I found Vampir! Snake: ...... Raiden: (Babbling) He wasn't really black! He wasn't even really a man! Snake: ....... Raiden: Why won't anyone believe me!? Snake: Right. Raiden: Argh! Hanging up again, Jack pulls himself out of the water and wrings himself off. He then enters the next chamber, finding yet another sight which causes him to stop. Raiden: You. In the center of this chamber a figure sits on top of a pool of water. With legs crossed he seems to meditate.....then he opens his eyes, smiling at Raiden. Raiden: Still tickin huh? Argy: Unfortunately Hell had no chipmunks. Raiden stares blankly at Argy, who pauses for a moment then shakes his head. Argy: Er, I mean....hell had no koala bears. Another blank stare. Argy: I'll get it one of these times, just hang on. Raiden: ....... Argy: Hell had no outhouses? From somewhere in the distance a Japanese voice faintly screams. Kojima: (Muffled) Read script! So hard, read script? Stupid head! Raiden: What the hell was that!? Argy: Er.... The oddly flamboyant vampire scratches his chin. Argy: Hell hath no fury like a bisexual's scorn? Kojima: (Muffled) DAMN YOU! Raiden: I'm scared. Argy blinks, then slowly stands upon the water. As he does though he seems to lose his balance, flailing his arms. Argy: No! With that the bizarre one sinks like a stone beneath the tide. Raiden: .....uh..... For a long moment all is silence. Then at last Argy pulls himself up to the surface again, standing atop it. Argy: Stop looking at me. I'd like to see you do that trick. Raiden: ..... Argy spreads his arms, breathing deeply and trying to put Raiden on edge. It works as the rookie soldier yells, pumping bullets rapidly at the specter. Raiden: (Firing) Aaaaaaagh! The vampire however simply sways to the left, to the right, does a split, rises, spins, moonwalks around the bullets. He then grabs his crotch, letting out a Micheal Jackson-esqe squeal. Argy: Ahee-hee-hee! Raiden: Damn! Argy turns his head, grinning slightly. Argy: Human muscles are quite eloquent. They speak out quite clearly what a person's next move will be. Raiden: What's mine going to be then? Argy: To try in vain to shoot me several more times. Raiden: AAAAAGH! Surely enough Jack snaps off a flurry of pistol shots. Argy again dances out of the way, finishing with a pelvic thrust. Raiden: Urgh! Argy: (Smiling slightly) This should be fun....well worth the wait. Argy sticks out his snakelike tongue, slides it around. However it gets it wrapped around his fangs....he grunts in a panic. Argy: Mmmhph, little help, hmamamhph! Raiden: ..... Yanking with both hands, the oddball pulls his tongue free. Clearing his throat, he continues. Argy: You've become quite a nuisance. But you won't save the scientist. Raiden: You knew I was coming? Argy: (Ignoring him) And did you really think killing the president would stop us? Think again! Raiden: I didn't do it! Argy: (Disgusted) You expect me to believe he died accidentally? What, that he choked to death on some pretzel, or something? Raiden: Actually yes. Argy: Feh! Suddenly a series of alarms begin blaring throughout the chamber. Over the PA system a familiar, tired sounding voice speaks. Kriegidus: Final check for Metal Gear Ron underway. All personnel report to your stations if you want to live....or don't, and die. It doesn't matter to me much. Argy turns his head, smirking at Raiden. Argy: Well, looks like they're ready to go. It's a shame you aren't going to be around long enough to stop them. The vampire glances down at the water he is standing on. Argy: This isn't just seawater you know. It's a special mix....the byproduct of the many orgies I sustained over the past few weeks. My various fluids filled this pool with a bacteria, giving it a high oxygen content. Buoyancy is non-existent. Once you fall in, you don't get up! Raiden: (Gulps) Argy reaches into the front of his jeans, producing what appears to be a razor sharp dildo. Argy: Don't worry, once you drown I will fetch your corpse. You'll make a nice plaything. Raiden: No!!!!! Panicking, Raiden looks around trying to find some measure of escape. However there is none to be had. Argy: (Snarling) Show me what you've got! Jack's eyes widen in horror as Argy closes in on him, thwacking his horrible weapon in the palm of his hand..... To Be Continued
