Looking for a place to brood (1/8th Boda spoof).

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ........ We start with an excerpt from last Sunday's March Madness: Crowd: 3… 2… 1!!!! The buzzer sounds and “Bulls on Parade” by Rage Against the Machine hits the speakers as Corey Williams charges down to the ring not wanting to waste any time. Clay: Now coming to the ring representing the New Breed, “Controversial” Corey Williams!!!! CCW slides into the ring and cockily walks over to the two struggling superstars hanging on the ropes and gingerly pokes at them with his feet. Wonder: What is your Legend going to do now Duke? CCW starts to kick a little harder at the Neo Void God but Aki’s leg is still to tangled in the ropes for him to be freed. Dave: Well Aki’s not going anywhere as long as his leg stays tangled. Duke: And it would take a Neo Void God to pry that leg vice grip off oh Aki. Corey realizes this too as he reaches over and starts to untangle the ropes. Aki lets go of the bottom rope and this swing of momentum lets Legend Inc swing upward and he grabs Corey by the back of his hair and hauls him over slamming him onto the ring floor. Corey Williams has been eliminated!!! 'Ah god! Do we have to keep on watching this!?' 'OK, OK! I'll fast forward to the GOOD bit.' ::As our camera fades in we hear CCW sigh, his head buried deep in his palms. Times are not good for CCW, just a month after jobbing to Hank, just a few weeks after he finally got over it, and now this happens. Going out in the March Madness Battle Royal, not just going out, but going out early, and not just going out early, but going out first!:: Corey: Look, I can't hang around here anymore, let's go brood somewhere. Chris: Brood? Speak for yourself, I'm on top of the world! Watch this.... ::Chris has fast-forwarded to the 'GOOD bit' and pushes the play button. Again we return to March Madness.:: Dave: Boda has done it! What a way to finish off an excellent PPV!!! Mexi and Boda have their hands raised when Chris Chase slides back into the ring unbeknownst to them with a steel chair and nails Mexi in the back of the head first dropping him like a sack of potatoes and then catching Boda next. Duke: That’s a sore loser, Hoss. On a side note EMT’s have finally arrived out and are at the aid of the injured Snow. In the ring Chase continues to pound away at Boda’s skull with the steel chair. Dave: WHAT A SORE LOSER!! CHRIS CHASE IS TRYING TO INJURE THE NEW CHAMP!! Jeff: A vacant title would be better than Boda holding it! The crowd lets out a huge pop as Red Rock, D-Kay Calyx, and Tim Walden rush out of the back. Chris Chase immediately slides out of the ring as the Wild Deuces slide in. Dave: THE WILD DEUCES JUST SAVED BODA FROM INJURY!! Jeff: And that's why they're jobbers. Dave: CHRIS CHASE IS SUCH AN ASSHOLE!! WHY COULDN'T HE LET BODA ENJOY THIS MOMENT HE'S EARNED!! Jeff: Because we don't want PCW to die. And a Boda title reign kills PCW. The Wild Deuces help Boda to his feet. Chris Chase stands on the stage and stares down at Boda. Boda stands in the ring and stares down Chase. ::Chris Chase watches on in delight, his eyes gleaming as he stares at his own act of psychotic violence. Even watching his good friend Chris in his finest hour doesn't make Corey feel any better. Call him selfish but.... ah to hell with it, he's selfish, he's not gonna try and pretend that he's not. In fact, there will be no more pretending anymore. And the worst part about March Madness? After all the controversial things Corey had done, trashed Agent Orange's grave, joined Wild Deuces, left Wild Deuces for New Breed just a day later, beat the crap out of badge, PURPOSELY job to Hank, freak wit 'cho Momma, after all those controversial things he had done FOR the fans, what did they do when he was eliminated? They laughed! They laughed at CCW like he was some sort of jobber! CCW is NOT a jobber! Corey gets up off the couch he is sitting in, in the apartment of Chris Chase.:: Corey: Look, I'm going by myself then. I'm too depressed to just party. ::Corey gets up and walks for the door, the scene fades out.:: ::Twenty men went in last Sunday, and eventually they all left it. But one man left first with nothing but his hurt pride, whilst another man left last, holding the PCW Heavyweight Championship belt in his hands. Life's a bitch, eh? Our next scene opens in a bar, how goddamn predictable. Those of us who are frequent watchers of Corey promos will instantly notice the lack of controversy in the room. In fact, the atmosphere is downright depressing. We see Corey slumped over the bar, a drink of Jack Daniels by his head.:: 'Rough Day?' ::The bartender enters the screen, looking very clichéd in an olden days style way. Wearing an apron and a sporting a long moustache. In fact if it wasn't for the towel and glass in his hand we'd think he was a barber. Corey manages to drag his head up from the bar and gives him a look that could kill.:: Corey: You have no idea...... for you are nothing but an ordinary bar tender. You have no idea what it is like to be bestowed with the responsibility of being the single source of controversy for junkies worldwide. Do you know what it's like to go in there and bust your ass for the people only for them to laugh in your face?! Bartender: Well, uh..... I guess not. Corey: Of course not! And when was the last time the fans turned their back on you and began chanting 'Krayzie!' and 'Boda!', despite the fact that both of those individuals were lacking in controversy of any shape OR form!? Bartender: Well, I uh.... can't say that's ever happened to me. Corey: Yes well you can have it I say. No longer will I let the fact that I am better than everyone else force responsibility on me. No longer will I let my former love for the fans get in the way of doing what I want! You people booed me, but you will be sorry without me. Coz from know on I'm saying to hell with the fans! There's not gonna be anymore pausing mid way through a controversial stunt to check if there's a camera on me. No longer will I be a tap that the fans can just turn on or off when the feel like it. From now on there will be 100% controversy 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 356 days a year, whether the fans like it or not! And you know why.... because controversy.... is what I wanna see! Bartender: Err.... that's great. Corey: And another thing.... ::The bartender has to listen to drunk's stories day in and day out, and there's always a time to draw the line.:: Bartender: Look buddy, I don't care what you wanna see, just drink up and then get out. ::Little does he realise that Corey is not drunk, just depressed, and that his sad sob story is actually true, well at least from his point of view:: Corey: That's exactly your problem, you don't care. None of you fans care about me. But guess what.... ::Corey pick up his glass of Jack Daniels and smashes it over the head of the bartender, he instantly collapses to the mat. Corey leans over, staring at the motionless body furiously.:: Corey: Now you made ME not care! You wanna laugh at me? Sure, that's fine, as long as you find career-ending injuries funny! ::The fact that this man probably doesn't even know what PCW is is irrelevant, Corey is taking out his pent up anger upon this person.:: Corey: You people wanna boo me? I'll give you something to boo. I'll be so goddamn controversial I'll make Clyde look like a nun, I'll make Kid Wonder look like nothing more than a spoiled brat, I'll make Tyler Nelson look like the kid from work experience, and I'll make Boda look like the p*ssy he is! And then you'll finally realise...... and it will be too goddamn late! ::Corey spits on the body of this bartender, at the same he is spitting on the fans. Not that they liked him in the first place, but in Corey's mind every time they've booed him over the last month it was the equivalent of a cheer, it made him special, made him feel needed. Boos he could handle, but laughter he could not, CCW ain't no goddamn clown, he's an entertainer but he sure as hell ain't a comedian. But now there is no ifs or buts about it, no more 'you love to hate me's, Corey doesn't care. He's gonna do what he wants to do, not what the fans wanna do. It just so happens that Corey is one sick son of a bitch, and he WILL have his revenge on the fans. Corey walks out of screen and off the camera, and the scene fades out. But this time, that doesn't mean that Corey's life fades out. Corey's life doesn't end when the cameras stop rolling, he's not gonna be here at your whim. And although he entered this bar depressed, and devoid of controversy, he is now perhaps leaving it with his controversy realer than ever.:: !The End!
