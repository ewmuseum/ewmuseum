Origin - Dark Wrestling Alliance

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th April 2003 So finally after getting his big break in arguably one of life’s most demanding professions, Gareth Green or New Blood as he was now known was unemployed. Another roadblock and another opportunity to question himself but the waiting was soon to be over as the Dark Wrestling Alliance’s promoter Dave Time had already paid the young rookie some attention and the ink was still wet when he made his debut. Again there weren’t any colossal crowds, no media coverage and an egotistical roster of veterans bullying backstage. The bright lights of Primetime Championship Wrestling continued to be a myth, the light at the end of the tunnel but the Jester to be was to preoccupied with his latest business venture. For some reason Dave Time overlooked every Cruiserweight feature and was hell bent on molding New Blood into a Hardcore performer hence the debut being a Tables Match against the much loved and soon to be retired Big Mack. Admittedly some of the names adorning the roster were in dire need of makeovers but the new kid on the block was exactly the fresh piece of meat they craved to abuse. The curtain came up and New Blood came crashing down through a wooden table but fortunately for the rookie was that the tablecloth was none other than the mountain of flesh known as Big Mack. Victory number one was in the bag and the tally soon mounted, a name was emerging and one after another the veterans fell to a new blood of talent or simply the man named New Blood. The company that as mentioned homed Double MJ the man who opened this world to a young bartender working in the City Of Sin was about to unleash another signing, one they had built up on a pedestal. The eerie silhouette manifested on the video wall was this new name and every week a countdown would appear and after weeks of Sesame Street his debut loomed. His ridiculing promos grew tiresome and as the video wall blazed into life with the number six the PA system roared through the arena with Linkin Park’s ‘One Step Closer’ and so emerged the man with the reputation, the man nicknamed The Instigator Of Injury. In truth the two never once faced each other. The man was later unveiled as Jimmy King or to those watching Primetime Wrestling Academy with a keen eye Scott King, another labeled with no stability for he, like his career, fizzled out. The twist in this tale came when Jimmy wormed his friend Johnny Hitchin into the Hardcore Championship Match at the companies forth-coming Pay-Per-View to face New Blood, the match to be conducted under a Ladder Match stipulation. Maybe it would be a good time to inform the masses that both Jimmy and Johnny were swaying to the Billy and Chucky, pretty self explanatory and thus Steel Thunder was born. The match? New Blood was crowned the first ever DWA Hardcore Champion and smothered the title like a newborn baby. Challengers stepped up to the batting plate each and every week yet the outcome always remained the same, a successful title defence. Ironic really, the Hardcore Champion couldn’t be dethroned but the World Heavyweight Championship was being passed around like a Chinese whisper and so the strap went from the artist formerly known as Double MJ, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza to The Eagle and at the Pay-Per-View it would nestle in the bosom of its third holder. For a small time promotion things in the ring were running smoothly but backstage the nightmare had resurfaced, manifested into a being hungered by power. New Blood being naïve only needed to bask in the glisten of his title but if the turmoil of the backstage had been brought to light then maybe the journey to Primetime Championship Wrestling would’ve taken a different path. That being hungered by power had rallied a union and quite possibly it may because of this that Dave Time injected his name into the Pay-Per-View’s Main Event where both competitors would duel for the companies most covenant prize. The scene was set, World Heavyweight Championship bout, The Eagle versus Mad Dawg Munn or for a more recognizable alias, Munny Mcmahon. The Angel Of Death soon seeped in; killing the company from its very foundations but not before New Blood could unify his existing Hardcore Championship with that of the Light Heavyweight. Crowned DWA Double Gold Champion New Blood not only took Mr. Smooth’s title but his career for that was the movie stars last match and was actually the turning point of his own career. Dave Time vanished and the reigns of the Dark Wrestling Alliance were entrusted to the promotions first Champion, Nerezza. The roster become younger and younger while the veterans become bitter and bitter and the black sheep that was New Blood was oozing with confidence, a confidence that needed shattering. His friend and former partner stepped up to the batting plate like so many before him but instead of wrestling the titles from him he decided to play politics and told the world, “As from this evening, New Blood will not only defend at-least one Championship, be it the Hardcore or Light Heavyweight every week but he must also honour his commitments of defending either at Pay-Per-Views!” That speech imbedded into the mind of the rookie, not in the mind of the competitor New Blood but also in that of the fading Gareth Green. Amusingly the Double Gold Champion continued to retain thus fuelling his ego and his head swelled to breaking point where his love for both titles overwhelmed him. New Blood began suffering paranoia. It was during this era that a man stepped from the shadows and slowly became the thorn in New Blood’s side, that man, Inferno. A trend was occurring within the Dark Wrestling Alliance, people couldn’t title themselves with decent aliases but at-least looking down from the pedestal that is Primetime Championship Wrestling Gareth Green, better known as The Jester can ask, “What happened to them all?” Most vanished into obscurity and some were never heard of again. Politics took a strangle hold on the company and following Dave Time, Nerezza to handed over the reigns. Mad Dawg Munn had succeeded in his quest for not only the World Heavyweight Championship, not only the creation of an illegal stable but to be installed as the company’s sole proprietor. People wrestled on borrowed time, their careers elapsing with every passing show and New Blood’s final curtain call came when he was ordered to wrestle in a Triple Threat, stipulated was that both Championship’s would be put on the line against the divisions number one contenders. His greed, his King Midas touch had been his own undoing and on the brink of career suicide he wrestled then walked… New Blood walked out on both the contest and the company. All that was the Dark Wrestling Alliance evaporated and the memory of New Blood lost as a telephone call changed his life. Weeks of unemployment had finally filled the voids of his mind and the death of New Blood meant that Gareth Green could resurface from under his shadow. That call was the opportunity to study his craft at Primetime Wrestling Academy where he would be tutored by the communities star studded names, his acceptance however came at a cost, for New Blood was to remain dead and buried. In need of an alias Gareth Green decided to concentrate all his efforts on creating a polar opposite to his Dark Wrestling Alliance counterpart and so the era of Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green was born. - To be continued -
