Semore Smoke=Comedian?

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 21, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into the small locker room area of what seems to be a small auditorium. The backstage area isn’t as big as those of the wrestling arenas he’s been to, but it’s adequate for what he’s there to do. Semore is wearing his best informal wear of a white shirt with a black leather jacket and a pair of white baggy jeans a pair of Timberlands to top it all off. In front of him is a TV that is playing the amateur act that he’s there working with. The man is virtually unknown to the world of comedy, but his name is Ryan Breen. Ryan, like Semore, is an up and coming stand-up comedian and hopes to grace the big time some day. They’re both booked on the same night to do a double header that will last roughly an hour. Ryan was finishing his act when the scene opened up to show Semore watching it… Ryan Breen: Yeah, I swear, that’s the truth. You got these white boys that act black, then the black boys that act white, then the ones that you don’t know what they’re acting like. The Asians are acting black, it’s a holy mess. Just stay what you are! Black guys are always going to have bigger dicks, white guys are always going to be somewhat goofy, and the Asians… well… they’ll… always be short! Thank you again, you’ve been great! {{{{Smoke had to crack at the last joke. The whole show from Breen was of how the world has changed and how everyone is acting like someone that they’re actually not. How could Semore top this? Maybe picking on celebrities such as wrestlers, himself in particular, and he possibly could pick on his little drug use problem as well. Breen steps off the stage carrying his bottle of water with him. About ten seconds later he burst into the locker room where Smoke is sitting and Semore stands up from his chair and shakes hands with Ryan and gives him a side smile…}}}} Semore Smoke: You’re damn good, I see you going far. {{{{Breen looks happy for the compliment as he starts to jump around the room…}}}} Ryan Breen: You really think so? I’m a huge fan of yours, you know your wrestling and all. I believe you’re a top-notch entertainer. I think you can pull of a stand-up act with no problem. {{{{Smoke shakes his head as he loses the smile and actually sounds serious for a few seconds…}}}} Semore Smoke: No. I should have gone up there first, I shouldn’t be the main event tonight I don’t think I’ll be that funny. All I’ll be doing is making some drug jokes, pick on myself, and some of the wrestlers I work with, that’s about it. {{{{Ryan calms down enough to sit down on a chair in the far corner to take a swig of his water. He looks at Smoke with a serious look and says…}}}} Ryan Breen: That’s what I thought about my show. All I was going to talk about was the racial profiling and difference in the world. When you hear something like that, it just sounds like something from school or something boring. But I made it funny and entertaining. I think you can pull this off with no problem. {{{{Smoke nods and then smiles as he grabs a Coke can that was sitting on the TV. Smoke drinks the last bit of the Coke in it and then holds it in his hand as he gets ready to go on stage…}}}} Semore Smoke: I hope you’re right. The last things I want to do are going out there and make a fool of myself. {{{{A stagehand carrying a clipboard walks into the locker room and looks at Smoke and says while checking on his clipboard…}}}} Stagehand: You’re on in one Mr. Smoke. {{{{Smoke nods and the stagehand leaves. Smoke turns to face Ryan again and says with a smile…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, it’s my turn to be thrown to the wolves. I hope I’m funny enough, if I can hear you laughing from the backstage area then I know I’m doing all right. {{{{Ryan smiles a cocky smile…}}}} Ryan Breen: Ok. Semore Smoke: Now I don’t mean any fake laughing either, I want it to be what we call in the wrestling business, to be legit. You got that? {{{{Ryan swigs on his water and nods as he understands. Semore then turns and heads through the door towards the stage area. He makes his way to the stage in about ten or so seconds and stands there as the audience starts to settle down. The PA announcer nods at Smoke who nods back, then the announcer does his announcement for Semore…}}}} Announcer: And now, our next performer, from Wilson, North Carolina, you know him as a wrestler for the PCW, but today his aim isn’t to body slam you but to make you laugh, lets all give it up for, Semore Smoke! {{{{Smoke takes a deep breath and then pulls the pack of cigarettes from his pocket and puts them in one hand and walks out on stage with the Coke can in one hand and the pack of cigarettes in the other. The fans are surprisingly giving a better reaction then he expected as the entire audience is standing and applauding Smoke’s every move on stage. Smoke places the Coke can on the stool beside the microphone and then pulls a cigarette out from the pack and places it in his mouth. He then pulls out a lighter and lights the cigarette and takes a long drag on it, letting the flavor fill his lungs for a second. He looks out at the crowd and then up at the lights. Then his show begins, throughout the show Smoke paces back and forth in front of the microphone, sometimes taking it out of its holder and walking around the stage…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well…. You know I’ve been having nightmares about this night. Yeah, I woke up once last week covered in sweat because I dreamed that I wasn’t funny enough and that you guys literally booed me off the stage. But that ain’t happening tonight, nope. You know the only bad thing about being a wrestler, is that when you talk about your fellow workers on TV, it’s mostly faked well I’m going to take this opportunity to talk about the wrestlers as I see them. First off we’ve got that Jerome “Blayze” Jordan, whom I’m facing in that four corners match for the Rising Star title this Sunday. Blayze is a straight up idiot, if you had talked to the guy, you’d realize that he’s either been smoking a little too much, or he’s always had the IQ of a ten year old. Blayze, pick your spots son, I swear, I asked the guy what two plus two was, you want to know what I got? Five! Come on Blayze, you can do better then that. Actually I was lucky I got a number out of him, considering he sat there thinking about it for about three hours. I’d believe that he and that Jeremy Johnson referee are related, yeah I know, so what, Jeremy’s white, does that matter? I think Ryan pointed that out to us earlier tonight. But you know how Jeremy will count, “1…..2….. what comes after 2?”. The guy can’t count! Yet he’s a referee! Last I knew he had lost more times then anyone in PCW history, I think I know why. He couldn’t count the referee’s count so he couldn’t kick out. The fans should just referee his matches for crying out loud. What about Eddie Mandrake, this Bounty Hunter type guy, he scares the day lights out of everyone, except me. Mandrake is a wimp, straight up he is. I saw the guy backstage before a show and he was worried that he would hurt Scorpion, in fact it was before his debut where he choked Scorpion out. He was afraid that he would hurt Scorpion badly, Mandrake is a pussy, let’s just put it that way. Eddie, dude, you’ve got the strength and the talent, then why not use it? Because you’re afraid to! And that’s why you’ll lose Sunday for sure. Speaking of Scorpion, I think I might know why little Eddie was afraid he’d hurt him. The guy is more fragile then anyone I’ve ever worked with, I mean even more then Lance Perfection and Scott Taylor put together! And that’s pretty soft! The last show we did for PCW I ran in and attacked Mandrake and Scorpion, well Mandrake was crying after we went backstage, but I think I saw Scorpion crying in the ring. The guy said I was being too rough. Come on! You’re a wrestler for crying out loud, if you can’t take the heat, then get out of the kitchen. Hmm… who else should I show as a somewhat fraud… what about Xavier Kannon? All right, what’s wrong with Kannon, ok he’s a great wrestler, but the guy isn’t really what he says he is. Kannon is supposed to endorse Snapple right? What did I see him drinking backstage every show so far since my debut in PCW? Sobe! The guy was drinking Sobe! All right, now that’s a hypocrite for you. Not only that, but I don’t think he’s a real Scientologist. I asked him what Scientology was and he never got back to me on the answer! He sat there and just looked at me with a blank stare and then changed the conversation in mid-stare, probably hoping I wouldn’t catch onto what he was doing. Hmm… I think that’s enough about PCW wrestlers, what about myself? Hmm… I’m a proven druggy, I love cigarettes to death, and I can’t stop sniffing that damn Coke! {{{{Smoke grabs the can of Coke and puts it up to his nostrils and starts to sniff loudly in front of the microphone, so far the whole crowd has been giving an up roar for the inside information on their favorite superstars, as well as some of Semore Smoke’s self jokes…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah man, you can’t help but sniff that damn Coke. Actually I’ve never touched that stuff, I don’t do hard drugs, I might occasionally smoke a joint… all right, not occasionally, maybe every hour, but that’s because it relaxes me. In fact I’m high as hell right now, talking to you all. But hey, that’s not a bad thing as a friend of mine would say, it’s a good thing. Thank you, you’ve been great! {{{{The audience all stand and applaud Smoke louder then that of what he got as he came on stage. He was actually funny! He pulled it off! Semore walks off the stage, dragging on the cigarette and then throwing it on the stage after finishing it. He leaves the Coke can behind and is just happy to be off the stage. As he comes off the stage and runs right into Ryan Breen who is still chuckling from the jokes…}}}} Ryan Breen: Dude, you’re hilarious! I’ve never seen a pot head go up there and tell such funny jokes! Are you really high right now? {{{{Smoke smiles and nods…}}}} Semore Smoke: There isn’t a time that I’m not, I really do smoke about every hour. In fact I’m due for one in about ten minutes. Ryan Breen: Oh dude! You have to score me a hit! {{{{Smoke just nods as he pulls out cigarette case and opens it, inside are pre rolled joints that he rolled at home. He pulls two out and hands one to Breen and puts one in his mouth, he lights his first and then hands Breen the lighter so he may light his. They both take a long drag on the joint and then lighten their spirits. The scene fades on the two new stand-up comedians as they rejoice for a job well done…}}}}
