A Shameless Homage To Black Books (Part Two).

10 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/06/01 Xavier Kannon’s arrogance has never been in question... After all this is the man who took an embarrassing Battle Royal elimination so badly that it resulted in a 6 week stretch of violence, back-stabbing and eventually led to him buying 20% of PCW and the Ultimate Authority’s rise at Spring Fling... ... But it now seems that this arrogance isn’t confined to PCW or wrestling... It’s filtering into his Scientological activities as he has managed to track down, at the cost of at least 2 known deaths the only copy in known existence of “The Legacy of Xenu”, a book that predicts the coming of a “Son of Xenu”, a Scientologist of unbelievable power who will lead the faith into the future.. ....And being Kannon, he naturally thinks this person will be him. So little over 36 hours from Turmoil where he will be locked in a Cage with Alexander Miyagawa, who just last Friday flew off the top rope and hit a 5 Elements Frog Splash on Kannon, he finds himself in London around the corner from this said book... But will it be as simple as just buying it? “Why are there books here? Every day I wake up, come down here, open the shop and there are books.... Books everywhere.... Water, water everywhere but not a drop to dr-... No wait, it’s BOOKS!!!” Our shot opens up in a dusty, haphazard book shop, wall to wall books, seemingly stacked in no order, just thrown on the shelves. A desk overlooks this shop, and through the haze of smoke surrounding it we see a slender man dressed all in Black... Black scruffy shirt, Black jeans and a dusty Black jacket. His dark Brown hair is scruffy, sticking out everywhere as he surveys the shop, inhabited by a couple of bemused customers who look at the man, puzzled, before he again speaks in his strong Irish accent. Irish Owner : “You..... *Clicks fingers* MAN...” He speaks with disdain as he puts out his cigarette and takes a swig from an open bottle of Red wine, pointing at an elderly man who is flicking through a leather bound copy of the Complete Works of Charles Dickens... Irish Owner : “Why do you buy books? They have them on TV you know.. Like books with pictures, but the pictures move and there are no words!” The man looks back at him, not thinking of anything to say. Irish Owner : “And what’s better is you don’t have to come in here to buy them, which means I don’t have to get up early which works out better for both of us so WHY... WHY GOD DO PEOPLE WANT BOOKS!!!” He drops his head into his arms, and a sobbing sound is heard as the 2 customers look at each other and slowly put down their books then creeping out of the door, trying no to stir the Irishman. The 2 men walk off in different directions and as we follow the Dickens fan, he passes a couple of familiar faces. “So... Like, where do you buy books in England? Do you get them from a Cricket?” The camera switches to show none other than Xavier Kannon and his fiancé Eleanor Hall walking towards the book shop. The Preppy valet is wearing a tight White “I *Heart* London” t-shirt with a pair of sandblasted flared Jeans and carries a large Brown teddy bear decked out in a Union Jack jacket... Her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is tied back in a pony tail and a large pair of sunglasses sit atop her head. The Scientologist looks to Eleanor, wondering if she is joking... Then remembering it’s Ellie~! and she is indeed serious. Xavier : “Nooooo.... Cricket is a SPORT... Like Baseball but lasts 5 days or something... Books are still bought in book shops, just like America.” This appears to be somewhat of a revelation to the ex-Extreme Champion as they continue their walks down the London back street. Eleanor : “They have book shops in America too? Wow.... Maybe there is one hidden away at the Mall or something?” Wisely, Kannon decides to hold his tongue, not wanting to make a comment that will only leave him with an insulted and stroppy fiancé all the way back to the US... Something he DEFINITELY doesn’t want. Xavier : “Er.. Yeah, they’re new......Ah, here we go... This appears to be our place...” He looks up to see the name above the door, written in Gold on the building’s Black frontage BLACK BOOKS... Outside on the slabbed pavement are boxes full of battered books and cardboard price cards and the windows are covered in cobwebs and dust, making it impossible to see through. Xavier : “Jeremiah said “Black Books” before he was killed or dragged away for torture or whatever they did that means I don’t have to pay him...” Ellie~! casts a distasteful eye over the dirty outside to the shop as Kannon walks around to the door, opening it to sound a bell that seemingly awakens the temperamental Irishman from his despair. “WE DON’T SELL BOOKS... I KNOW IT SAYS WE ARE A BOOK SHOP... AND YOU MAY SEE A LOT.. A LOT OF BOOKS HERE BUT WE DO... NOT... SELL... BOOKS!!!” The aggressive Irish voice shouts out through the hazy smoke that hangs in the shops atmosphere. Xavier : “So the books with prices on must be your cover for World domination then?” The Irishman looks back at him, a completely blank expression from Xavier’s comeback and neither man moves or speaks until the Irish voice bellows out again. Irish Owner : “MANNY, MANNY... THERE ARE MORE OF THEM... CUS... COS... COMMUNISTS.. NO.. CUSTOMERS! THEY KEEP COMING IN THE SHOP AND SPEAKING... AND BREATHING.. THEY’RE TAKING MY OXYGEN, I CAN HARDLY BREATH.. AGHHHH!” The eccentric owner grabs his throat and pretends to choke as a bigger man with long, stringy Brown hair and a beard walks out from the back and looks at Kannon, then the owner. Manny : “Bernard, this is how “it” works... They buy books for more than you do, then you use that money for yourself and to buy new books.... Then the cycle goes on and on...” Bernard : “I know... It’s terrible isn’t it.. IT NEVER ENDS!!!” He again collapses his head into his arms on the desk as the bearded assistant turn to look at Kannon while in the background we see Ellie~! looking through book after book. “Hello? Ever heard of PICTURES in England...” Kannon tries to ignore her whining and turns to Manny. Xavier : “I want a book.” “THEY ALWAYS WANT A BOOK!!!!! ALWAYS!!! EVEN ON WEDNESDAYS... AND MONDAYS... AND SATURDAYS!!!” The harsh, sobbing Irish voice bellows out again as Manny proceeds. Manny : “And what is the name of this book.. We have books... Lots of them... All with covers... and pages... No half books here.” Xavier : “It’s called “The Legacy of Xenu”.” Before Manny has a chance to reply, Bernard raises his tired head off the desk and looks to Xavier with bloodshot eyes. Bernard : “Sorry, we don’t sell comics.. Goodbye, lock up on your way out...” His head drops again. Manny : “Ah... That book... The book, with that on the front... Okay, using my new Alphabetical System it should be under L... Right here...” He walks over and plucks a book off the shelf before studying the cover and dropping his head down disappointed. Manny : “Bernard, why is War and Peace in the “L” section?” “Becausse..... I... I... Took an average of W and P and came up with L.... There! There’s your answer! Manny : “The average of W and P is G!!! Not L!! You just put the books anywhere didn’t you. “N-... Yes... Yes I did.... And it’s my shop and I can do what I want!” Manny then looks at the shelf but doesn’t spy the book anywhere, then Ellie~!’s voice is heard. “Judging by his random positioning of War and Peace in that section of the shelf you just have to extrapolate his reasoning coefficient to the power of randomness and divide that answer by the cube route of the possibility that he’s just thrown the book in the bin... Which then, by transferring the answer to a simple 8 dimensional grid reference it should make the book... Here...” Bernard’s head raises up off the desk while Manny and Kannon slowly turn their heads to the valet who just happens to be holding a copy of “The Legacy of Xenu” up to them as the jaws of the 2 book shop workers drop, Kannon seeing it in a different perspective. Xavier : “So you found it looking for one with picture?” Eleanor : “Yup... There is a man standing on a rock.... Or is it a planet...” She hands the book to her man who turns and drops it on he desk, sending a small cloud of dust up that wafts right in front of Bernard’s face. Xavier : “So... How much?” Bernard sneers as he looks from the book to the cash register, not looking to enthusiastic about the sale. Bernard : “I dunno..... 18 thousand pounds!” Xavier : “SOLD!” The Scientologist drags his wallet out and pulls out the currency to the shock, and the hint of a smile from the Irish owner and the paper touches his hand... Xavier : “A pleasure doing business with you my friend...” The Scientologist takes Ellie~!’s arm and they walk out of the dingy shop and back into the light, seeming a lot brighter than usual due to the darkness of Black Books. Kannon holds the book up to himself, studying the cover as the shot starts to fade out to Black... Xavier : “And the chosen one is...” He begins to flick through the lavishly decorated and inscribed pages as our shot finally hits Black. “GIOVANNI FUCKING RIBISI!!!! HEY! I WANT MY EIGHTEEN THOUSAND POUNDS BACK!!!”
