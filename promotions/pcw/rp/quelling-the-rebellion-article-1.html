Quelling the Rebellion.....Article 1

2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/01 (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) April 2, 2002Entry 22 I felt the humiliation was more than I could bear. My defeat at the hands of an ingrate the likes of White Mexican was a truly colossal blow. Granted my mind was not as focused as is customary, certainly not in light of my recent tribulations. I had vowed to ravage the opposition in the Global Title Tournament, to crush and annihilate all of these overrated comedians. Yet unfortunately divine right would grant none of it, and I met my demise at a significantly earlier stage than I anticipated. I clashed with a damned late-night Comedy Central reject, a heralded icon of Primetime Central who garners more accolade than his juvenile antics deserve ……and met failure. I reckon it was by some act of divine reprimand, though the reason for my condemnation I have yet to determine. Hokey reasoning you say? Bah! Any moron who sees that phony border-jumper’s work in the ring inevitably realizes his success has been due to the ever-present plague we refer to as backstage politics. Such was the case that night, and though I choose not to plead my case the fact of the matter is I met with a company backstabbing. And regardless of the circumstances, politics or not, I was shamed. I realize I ran tirelessly through this subject in my account following that match, yet these facts are instrumental in light of my forthcoming statements. So bear with me.I was on the verge of leaving the Primetime Central organization. ‘Contractual obligations’ be damned, my contract was breached the moment the WWA inexplicably shut its doors and I have a plethora of lawyers at my beck and call prepared to prove so…..thus the opportunity was there. It was a matter of merely taking it. Add to the situation the truth that my revelations refused to cease, despite my elimination from the tournament. I deliberated my options over a series of days, and seeing as my employer promotion had obviously collapsed there was little other alternative….no shows or appearances I was obligated to. It was a no-brainer, I wasn’t wanted and someone in the business the length of time as I have been realizes such things.During this lengthy several-day thought process, I received a package in the mail. Much to my surprise, the parcel contained the offer for a contract in Primetime Championship Wrestling; I was being offered a position with arguably the most elite wrestling promotion in the Western Hemisphere, and the chance to vie with the premier competition in our industry. I shall spare the trifle details, but I will leave it at this: following some hefty negotiation, I now sit writing at this desk as an employee of PCW. The benefits were hard to decline, to say nothing of the opposition I would certainly have the chance to contend with. Only a few days prior to the present, I was informed my first match would be tag team competition with Chris Havenstorm (whom, I am told, also gave his signature the day following that of my own) against the likewise newly-signed tandem of Suburban Uprising. I make it no secret that at this revelation, I had to stifle a chuckle. Why? Well, for a few reasons actually. First of all, let’s look at the irony of the name. Suburban Uprising? Am I the only one to sense an obvious contradiction? Face it, chumps like this are the epitome of the privileged yuppie foundation I have been striving to annihilate throughout my tenure touring the States. What the hell are they ‘uprising’ against? Aren’t the unfortunate youth of the social aristocracy being pampered enough these days? Maybe the rich uptown majority just isn’t what it used to be, I don’t know and I can only venture guesses. Maybe these deprived children were given a 2001 Mercedes Benz for their birthday instead of the 2002 Italian sports coup they demanded…..or worse yet, their allowances were lowered because they broke curfew. So, in turn, this is their rebellion; they resolved to form a second-rate tag team of socially affluent, yet horridly oppressed ‘angst-driven’ youth. Oh the horror! My thoughts on this are simple: God forbid Chris and I meet another setback this coming Friday, and at the hands of another of the infinite clones infecting the Primetime Central tag team ranks no less. Gee……yet more wise-cracking adolescents from the mean streets of suburban Peoria or Greenwich or wherever this latest batch of mongrels comes from. My apologies if I don’t follow the origin of every two-bit punk claiming to ascend out of the ‘repressed hellhole’ that is the San Fernando Valley…..obviously I need to lay down the 9mm I use to defend my shoddy slum apartment late at night from PCP-crazed maniacs, switch on the boob tube and take more notice of beloved MTV culture. Possibly they’re going to beat myself and Havenstorm into submission with skateboards and surfboards, and then ‘pistol-whip’ us with their cell phones. And for the final touch, summon a mob of Abercrombie & Fitch-laden teenies to show us how it’s really done on the mean streets! Cowabunga dude!...... (Oh wait, sorry, wrong team there….) Alright, I can’t keep up this charade any longer. This is a travesty, it’s a debacle. Hell it’s a veritable slap-in-the-face to myself and Chris Havenstorm! I came into PCW to tango with the best adversaries our profession can boast. I signed up to lock horns with Akiyama, to fight Xavier Kannon and Miyagawa. I arrived on the scene to clash with icons the likes of Frisco and Legend Inc. And what do I get for a welcome? I get tossed a flavor-of-the-week duo no one will remember in 6 months!! Well I guess you have to start somewhere, huh? I didn’t expect to waltz into a federation of this caliber and instantly be proclaimed as some messiah sent to dig PCW out of their ever-deepening hole. The real superstars will come in time. To reach the pinnacle of this business you have to start from the bottom……and that’s nothing new for me. Respect is earned in professional wrestling, as it is every facet of life. Difficult maybe but it will be earned, not even PCW is exempt from that reality……. * * * His period of reminiscence was disrupted by the abrupt blaring of the telephone, which he had set across the room on a short plastic stand riddled with notches from the teeth of its past owner’s dog. Jack lay down his pen and thrust back his chair with a sharp screech, uttering a grunt as he stretched his legs in response to the prolonged period at his desk. A muddled curse escaped under his breath as he strode irritably across the room and snatched the receiver from its rest. “What ya want?” For a moment there was nothing on the opposing end but static and a low hum. After three or four seconds an elderly voice replaced the noise. “Hello, Mr. Shanahan?” “Yeah, that’s me. Whatcha want?” came his gruff reply. “Ah, Bruce, I’m glad I caught you. This is Bishop McCarver, calling from Philadelphia.” Jack’s face flushed momentarily, feeling rather awkward after his curt remark. “Oh, deeply sorry Bishop…..” “Nonsense, Bruce, it’s alright. I accidentally dropped the receiver when you picked up, a typical person would have probably imagined it to be telemarketers and hung up…..they usually have a short pause between the time you pick up the phone and their spiel. Heheh.” Jack, expression unchanged, plopped down on his worn leather couch and set his left leg onto his right. “Is something wrong, Bishop? This is a fairly long distance call….” “In honesty, Bruce my boy, I was curious as to your mental state in recent days. Father Craven extended me word of your unfortunate setback last week and I opted to contact you personally and inquire as to how you are getting along.” The memory of his defeat drew Jack’s free hand into a balled fist. It was a concept he felt had deeper repercussions than a simple shot to his ego (and record)….if the Bishop’s theory was true, he realized his failure had further-reaching implications than simply himself. “In all honesty,” replied Jack semi-despondently, “it hits worse than any loss I’ve endured for a long time. Because this one may prove to affect more people than just the two involved. It’s an unusual feeling…..” “What’s that, Bruce?” “The feeling that this time I’ve let down more people than just myself. In all honesty it’s been a long time since I’ve fought on behalf of anyone other myself or for anything besides my own gain…..” “There are four capable individuals left in that competition, my friend. Besides, my theory may have been mistaken. Maybe this ‘Vampir’ was not the cause of your visions, he may be a pawn just as the other one-twenty-eight involved.” This aroused a grin and a husky snicker from the Boston native, leading to a lengthy pause on the other end. “But that’s not to say what you’ve been experiencing is a hoax, Bruce. Just because one element of a theory is refuted doesn’t invalidate the entire notion. Everything that has happened recently has a valid explanation, and unfortunately I fear it’s not one the vast percentage of the scientific community can readily accept.” “You’ve affirmed that point nearly a dozen times in the two times I’ve talked to you, Reggie,” came Jack’s reaction. “Alright, for the sake of argument we’ll say I’ve been appointed as some sort of ‘supernatural medium’ like you claim. We’ll say there really are ancient ‘Elder Gods’ projecting their thoughts through my dreams from their slumber in deep-sea citadels, or from their imprisonment beneath desert sands in prehistoric cities…..contained merely by enchantments and unworldly relics constructed by even older deities. Have I got this right so far, Reggie?!” The sarcasm was evident in VJ’s voice, as was his growing impatience and blossoming fury. McCarver remained silent for several seconds, and Jack could detect the sound of rustling pages from his caller’s end. He pulled himself from the comfort of the aging couch and dragged himself into the kitchen, whipping open the refrigerator door with a flourish. His eyes flashed over the meager contents as his ‘advisor’ finally spoke again. “I realize how difficult it is for you to accept this, Bruce, I really do. I felt likewise many years ago after being initially accepted into the ministry. But we, the Church, have access to extensive archives of records and testimonies encompassing just this subject alone. Oil tanker sinkings, aircraft disappearances, earthquakes…..they all have a purpose and you don’t always hear the origin of these disasters on the news, and for good reason. The same holds true for many poor souls encased in padded rooms and straitjackets. They babbled about formless entities defying natural order, hibernating in oceanic depths awaiting resurrection, and for their trouble these so-called ‘lunatics’ were locked away….but it doesn’t mean they were crazy….” “This is insane,” mumbled VJ as he popped the tab from a Rolling Rock beer and tossed it into the grimy sink. “It is difficult to disclaim popular dogma, Bruce, I told you I realize that. Bear in mind I was once in the same boat as you find yourself now…..” “But you didn’t have a high-ranking member of the Catholic Church telling you that cosmic demigods are making you their personal plaything either, Reginald! On the freakin’ contrary, you were never in the same boat I am now! It’s like a psychologist saying ‘I know how you feel’ to a person afflicted with dementia! Or the CEO of a Fortune 500 company saying ‘I feel your pain’ to a drunken wino!” “Very well, let me say this. You’re not alone, Bruce. You are not the only recorded case of this happening to. As a matter of fact, we have at least four documented accounts of persons currently living in the Boston area suffering the same affliction as you right now.” VJ’s can of beer slowly clanked down on the soiled counter, his eyes unblinking and focused straight ahead with a incredulous gleam. “You’re jokin’……right?” Hence came a mild snicker across the line. The bishop cleared his throat and the rustling of flipping pages again became audible. Jack leaned forward, supporting himself on the counter separating the living room and kitchen. “I am like George Washington, Bruce my friend, I cannot tell a lie. We have four individuals taking up residence in the immediate Boston area who have accounted to us revelations not tremendously dissimilar from your own. And like you, we know of them only because they opted to seek religious counsel for explanation. As far as we know, there could be hundreds suffering the same burden but whom have simply chosen to remain silent about their mental hardship. I have profiles on them here, and the same for records of their dreams.” “How similar are we talkin’ about here, Reggie?” inquired Jack, hesitantly taking another sip of his beer. “One dreams consistently about swimming through a sprawling underwater metropolis with amorphous beasts populating the ‘city’. Often it concludes with a glimpse of a massive formless beast with the hybrid features of octopi and man, formed seemingly of a protoplasmic base. Typically this unfortunate person awakens after being enveloped by an omni-directional slew of alien chants…..” “Not as bad as mine, I wager…..” murmurs VJ as he crumples his empty can and whips it back over his shoulder, bouncing with a clatter into the sink alongside the tab. “At least that poor guy didn’t see mutilated people crucified on the walls……” “Actually, we do have one female whose dreams share that trait with yours,” countered the bishop. “Often times she finds herself wandering down long hallways, with tormented souls hanging eviscerated on the walls like some blasphemous mural. The screams they utter in her dreams have nearly driven the poor woman mad…..Father Coleman of St. Paul’s Cathedral in upper Boston has seen her 5 times in the past month to offer guidance, hoping those wretched deities will grow bored and leave her in peace. If they don’t subside soon, I fear her destination soon will be the Ozark Asylum outside town…..” “Ya got any advice for me, Reggie?” queries Jack, retaking his spot on the couch. “Normally I would condemn the mindset of desensitization,” the bishop replies in earnest. “But in your case I firmly believe that standing is what has caused you retain your sanity throughout this ordeal…..quite the irony in my humble opinion. Normally I consider those desensitized to be lost, spiritually dead…..but in this predicament, it may be your lone saving grace.” “I’ll get along…..no wannabe gods are gonna get me committed….” “Glad to see you’re so steadfast,” snickers McCarver. “Well I’m in the middle of negotiating some things involving you personally, my friend, I’ll be sure to alert you when they are finalized……but for now I must depart, but it was a pleasu….” “What ya talkin’ about?” asks VJ. “What you negotiatin’?” “I’ll get into the specifics at a later date, Bruce,” assures the bishop. “Hope things work out…..good luck with your match Friday, farewell for now……” Before Jack can dig deeper into McCarver’s intent, he’s met with a steady buzz across the line. With a grumble he lays down the phone and returns to his desk, mind racing as he clutches at his near-depleted Bic pen. “It gets more and more bizarre by the day……” * * * (memoirs continued) I have just received a telephone call from Bishop McCarver in Philadelphia. Primarily it regarded my recent defeat, and what is yet to come in my future. The most astounding theme of the conversation was a rather interesting little tidbit no one bothered to inform me of earlier: the fact there are more like me, suffering the same type of wicked hallucinations as myself. And to boot, some live not 10 miles from me! For the time-being, I think it’s time for a walk; I need fresh air to clear my head. Albeit the 9mm concealed in my pocket, seeing as how a embarking on a casual evening walk in inner Boston is a tad different than strolling in the hills of the Appalachians. Maybe not as tough as the urban jungle of Poway, San Diego of course…….heheh. But alas, I figure another visit to Randall may be in order within the following week. I’ve dredged part of the story out of the old boy and his superior, but there are still things I’m being left in the dark about. Craven is going to enlighten me a bit further, though it may be against his wishes. I watched his back for nearly an entire grueling decade, and extended me the same courtesy; I expect his full cooperation now as I did then. Let’s end with this. I’ve endured my own little unrelenting hell for nearly two months now, and who knows what it has done to my reasoning. I trust my old friend will oblige my request. The exact toll this curse has wrought upon me I cannot yet predict. Yes, I am a time-bomb. Just when will I go off? Hell, it may require but a meager catalyst. And betrayal may be just that medium.....
