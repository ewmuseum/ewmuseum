A Promo About Nothing

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/08/02 (Seinfeld music) BelpbubbaBelptbubbaBelpBelpBelp…Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun Dun..BelpbubbaBelpBelp… The camera fades in… We see the Chef leaning over a hot pot, stirring some and liquid, and casually flinging in herbs and spices as if he was eyeballing the whole dish. A puff of steam rises up from the pot, and the sweet smell of a well-done stew tickles the sense and lifts the nose of the Culinary Conquistador. He smiles, knowing very that his talents in the kitchen are unrivaled, and his herb sprinkling abilities are unquestioned. The Chef: Ah, I have done it again! Another brilliant soup is created! The Chef grabs a ladle from the far side of the counter and dips it into the soup. He grabs a ladle full of the bubbling hot liquid and blows on it, cooling the soup to its core for an imminent taste test. The Chef: You are bold…but are you also…tasty??? The Chef quickly gulps the spoonful of soup and licks his lips. He stands back and wafts the surrounding air towards his face, feeling the vibe that the soup has created, recognizing it’s right to smell good, and his right to smell it smelling good. The Chef: Hmmm… The Chef pauses, and searches; not physically but mentally... He looks through page after page of knowledge in his mind. His brain scatters the millions of books and pamphlets read, and scans the many conversations with chefs of the past. He pulls up talk shows and cooking shows, filling his mind to the brim with gastronomy, sweet beautiful gastronomy… The Chef: BUT OF COURSE!!! IT NEEDS SOME TARRAGON!!! The Chef sprints over to the corner cupboard and searches frantically for the tarragon. He tosses bottled of fresh herbs aside, mindlessly scavenging for that one perfect additive, the maker or breaker of his ultimate culinary masterpiece!!! The Chef: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The Chef slams his hand into his chest and grips his shirt tenaciously. He pulls at his shirt as his he were yanking his heart right out of his body… Alas, the tarragon is nowhere to be found! The Chef: Bertha!?!? Honey!?!? Sweetie-Pie!?!? Did you finish the tarragon and forget to write me a Post It Note saying to get MORE tarragon at the supermarket!?!? The Chef listens, but hears nothing but the sound of ‘Roxanne’ by the Police emanating from his Sony AM/FM kitchen radio. The Chef: I wonder if she’s asleep, or gorging… The Chef steps out of the kitchen, making sure to turn down the heat on the soup to simmer in accordance with the rules and regulations of upper west side Manhattan apartments. The Chef: Angel Pie!?!? Soufflé of Pleasure!?!? AAAHHHH!!! The Chef giggles like a little school girl that just been told she’s, “not ugly” by the guy she’s really wanted to kiss for a long time. The Chef: You tease you… The Chef then suddenly remembers his reason for addressing his portly princess. The Chef: Ahem… Bertha retracts her tentacles from the Chef’s posterior. The Chef: Bertha…I wanted to find out what happened to the tarra… Suddenly the Chef’s apartment door swings open and in walks errr…slides a very shook up Yakov Jakov… The Chef: Yakov!?!? What the heck are you doing here…? Yakov: Just picking up some grub Chef… The Chef: Wait a second, you’re Jewish right!?!? Yakov: Whoa, who died and made you the Gestapo!?!? SHEEE-BANGGG!!! YOWZA!!! The Chef: Um, you’re holding a plastic packet of thinly sliced Virginia ham!!! Isn’t that against the whole eating “kosher” thing??? Yakov: Ok Cheffy, I’ll tell ya the truth, I’m not eating this ham, it’s for the small Japanese men living in some cabinet drawers in my apartment! The Chef: Wha? Yakov: They promised to help me close down the Kenny Rogers Roaster restaurant whose bright lights have keep me up for the last five nights… The Chef: For Virginia ham? Yakov: Actually it’s for a Chef Salad…I installed a disposable drain in my shower… The Chef: Huh? Yakov: I’m also going down to the Bank to pick up some Blood to give to an ailing lawyer that has promised to help me sue a tobacco company that is responsible for causing lung cancer in my proctologist, who also happens to be an Anti-Dentite, just like you!!! The Chef: Hmmm, sounds very familiar… Whoa! There is this inconceivable force that’s urging me to call you by your last name and your last name only!!! Suddenly Manuel Hung, clad in a New York Yankees t-shirt and blue jean shorts, stomps his way into the kitchen. Manuel Hung: Everyone is against me! The Chef: (Slowly going insane) Maybe if I click my heels together three times I’ll go back to France…to suckle on the webbed feet of another beautiful Cancan dancer… Manuel Hung: Don’t you agree with me Daddy!?!? The Chef closes his eyes and leans over, grabbing a bottle of tarragon lying on his dresser. He holds it close. The Chef: I know you’ll never leave me… The Chef opens his eyes, and THE BOTTLE IS EMPTY!!! The Chef: AAAAAHHHHHHH!!! The Chef wakes up in his bed, once again in a peaceful state. He leans over and sees Julia Louis-Dreyfus on the other side… The Chef: Close enough… Hey Elaine! “Watching Ellie” sucked prairie oysters! Dreyfus: Fuck you!
