Past Regression III of III

12 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th February 2004 Back to your Roots Part III of III Note: This is a series of three RPs detailing the background of Afwa, and will be a basis of the Afwa/Jay Pheonix feud It stars: Chief Priest Tainafi Malietoa Tautolo 'Towla' Mintola Keeper of the Rock Sailele Tyhdres The Gore The Tongan Terror Afwa Fades in.. Tautolo in a frantic state appears to rush into the room where Afwa is sleeping, awaking the sleeping giant, saying.. 'Afwa, quick, we have to get out of here..' says Tautolo in a fearful frame of mind. Afwa looks at Tautolo and pushes him down, saying 'Me no go, me Afwa, me stay..' 'Afwa, don't you see what he is doing to you? I never thought he would have this much control over you. Afwa, you need to think about this, is The Keeper of the Rock a good friend to have or is he using you for his own means?' 'I see you both are up, I take it you enjoyed your sleep and are ready what for today is about to bring.' says the evil Keeper of the Rock, with a smirk upon his brow. 'AFWA GO FIGHT TONGAN! AWFA CRUSH TONGAN!' 'Keeper of the Rock, I am glad you are going to be helping us fight the barbarian hoard, but what I don't like is how you have some mind manipulation over Afwa. Keeper of the Rock, I don't trust you..' Before Tautolo can utter the hurtful words, Keeper of the Rock points at Afwa and Afwa hammers a fist into the stomach of his best friend Tautolo 'Towla' Mintola, causing Tautolo to whine. 'See what happens to those who defy the Keeper of the Rock.. Afwa like my trained assassin that is what you shall become.. Now Afwa, finish the job..' Afwa Kilmanjaro stands over the fallen Tautolo grabs him by the throat, lifts him way up into the air, and just as Tautolo is about to be taken for a ride, Keeper of the Rock shakes his head and Afwa just puts Tautolo down.. 'Tautolo, I could easily have had Afwa destroy you there and don't you forget that. I am Keeper of the Rock, Keeper of the Seven Scrolls and Tautolo if you do not wish for me to force you to suffer the fate of the Barbarian hoard, then you shall show me your respect or you shall surely PERISH..' 'When are we to leave..' 'Soon, very soon, but for now I have to prepare Afwa in the special art of combat, as it clear he is a stupid dumb animal who would at this precise moment get slaughtered by the Barbarian hoards.' With a sneer to his tone Afwa speaks saying, 'Afwa no slaughter! Afwa know how to kill. Afwa no need teaching, he strong, he have the POWER..' The Keeper of the Rock at this smirks at the pure desire of Afwa, saying.. 'True words my stupid one. Though I think now is the time for the talking to end, for the battle is out there and we shall be victorious, the hoards will fall at the sound of my name and I Keeper of the Rock shall send fear down the spines of each and everyone of them, making them know just why I am their Alpha, Omega and why in time Afwa shall be their END..' 'Keeper of the Rock, you speak of an epoch, what is that?' 'In the history of time, a war shall escalate where the two sides prepare to destroy each other, this was is known as the Epoch.. And now this Epoch is upon us, this war is upon us and only one can survive..' says Keeper of the Rock with a snarl to his tone.. 'AFWA READY FOR PEE-PUHL! AFWA READY FOR TONGAN DOLL!' says Afwa in a angered manner. 'I think we should set off back to Somata then, as Tainafi needs us..' 'Tainafi, who be that?' 'Tainafi BE CHEIF! Tainafi CHEIF!' Keeper of the Rock smiles saying.. 'Chief. .. Chief of what?' 'Tainafi is the High Priest of the Tribal legion of Somata, he is second in command to Great High Chief Kilmanjaro, father of Afwa Kilmajaro, murdered at the brute hands of The Tongan Terror..' 'This war is not for them.. The war is for Afwa and I.. We shall bring down the Terror and we shall rule Somata..' 'AFWA BE KING!' smirks Afwa.. 'Afwa is the rightful ruler of Somata, but his mental state is not good enough at the present for in his heart he has the mind to rule, but in his head he has the mind to kill and seek revenge for those who cursed his name and butchered his family..' Tautolo says in a questionable manner. 'How will we get to The Tongan Terror? His mighty palace is guarded by some hundred Barbarian Hoards..' 'The walls of the city at the name of Afwa shall fall. As the beast is unleashed from within, the Tongan Armies shall now who their rightful leader is and they shall come crawling to Afwa, they shall beg for mercy and in the name of the Keeper of the Rock, THEY SHALL BE SAVED..' says Keeper of the Rock with a deep apocalyptic approach. 'AFWA CONVERT THEM ALL! AFWA FORCE THEM EAT PINEAPPLE!' 'See how dumb he really is..' laughs Keeper of the Rock. 'Keeper of the Rock, you are one rather sick individual, you joke at the expense of others and you try to make out you are for the people of Somata. Keeper of the Rock, you may have Afwa and Tainafi under your spell, but me, you'll never take me..' says Tautolo with a frown forming. “I see you are not for us.. Oh, what a shame..' At this Afwa Kilmanjaro once again grabs a hold of Tautolo, only this time he grabs Tautolo in the deadliest move to date, the Lava Crush. Afwa squeezes the life out of Tautolo, as if he was a grape being pressed for wine making. With the fading of Tautolo, Keeper of the Rock seems to be enjoying this rather ritualistic sadistic yet somewhat spiritual attack. Afwa slowly releases the hold, yet Tautolo does not seem to be aware, as he is out cold with the huge frame of chiselled rock known as Afwa standing over him sharking his thumb in the air.. “Tautolo, you try and cause Keeper of the Rock harm, you now suffer.. Afwa teach you that no match for him. You always try and be like me, yet me have the power, you just a fool standing in my way, now Afwa hungry, Afwa want meat..' 'Afwa by showing what you just did there, and by the time you destroy The Tongan Terror I shall give you some raw meat you'll never forget. And also you shall get a statue, which you'll keep as your mascot to the brutal slaughter of The Terror, but Afwa, this shall not be ingested, for it shall be of solid gold..' 'Afwa hungry though!' says Afwa, rubbing his tummy. 'Eat the golden Pineapple, the statue of my ancestors and you will die.. The prophets fortell of a beast coming to save the people of Somata and Afwa, once you slaughter The Terror, the prophets shall have been right, for Afwa, me must leave now, we must forget all of this and go in search of the Terror, for he is what we want, he is who we must kill..' 'Afwa avenge family!' says Afwa with a sneer.. Slowly the cameras fade away from Phoenix Rock, as Afwa Kilmanjaro and Keeper of the Rock are seen trailing through the scorching heat, though Tautolo is not with them, for he like a dead carcass has been left on the Rock, waiting for the vultures to debauch him. Keeper of the Rock now has a large jail like chain around the neck of Afwa, as Afwa goes to look back to see his former best friend, who he believes he has killed, Keeper of the Rock grabs at his chain, forcing Afwa to turn back and continue walking past Phoenix Rock and on the road to face The Barbarian Hoards. 'Afwa, you must forget all that happened back there..' says Keeper of the Rock with a cold firm stare.. 'Tautolo Afwa friend! No more Tautolo magic!' says Afwa in a state of regret. 'Never mind him, think of the feast we shall have once The Tongan Terror is no more. Think of what Tautolo would have done to prevent you from being at my right hand, he was a waste and he got to meet his maker..' says Keeper of the Rock with a firm manner.. 'Me scared..' says Afwa.. 'Scared, how can a 486lbs monster be scared. The Barbarian Hoards are not scared they are willing to fight for their lives and Afwa if you wish to turn back now and forget you ever knew me and let the Tongan Terror take delight in knowing he wiped out Somata, then go now, but don't ever return, for the day you do, I shall make you suffer..' 'Me Afwa go get nasty Barbarian meanie heads! Me eat raw meat first..' 'Afwa, I admire your desire to be like raw meat, for they are strong aniamals, full of the nutrients needed and it is with the meat that you learn how to be Afwa and it is why I shall give you the Golden Pineapple of my ancestors when The Tongan Terror is no more..' 'Me want food now..' says Afwa stamping his feet, making the ground shake.. 'Afwa, don't act like a child, for you may be stupid, but you are not dumb..' says Keeper of the Rock with a smile, as he realises the two words mean the same, shame about Afwa, as he, well he's just a dumb animal who knows nothing better then to kill a man and crush his skull between their bare hands.. The two Samoans continue their path to reach the majestic palace of the Tongan Terror, soon after walking about 50 miles due East, seen in front of them are some large iron girded gates, with reinforced wire around the edges. Seen standing at the gates are two guards dressed in turbans and carrying cutlasses around their waist. Since today is rather hot, the guards appear to be sweating like pigs. Suddenly Keeper of the Rock has an idea and soon after a rock is seen thrown at one of the guards, the guard turns around, but there isn't anybody there, so h goes back to his duties. Again another rock is thrown and this time it hits one of the guards on the head, he turns around and thinks the other guard is throwing things at him and thus the two guards raise their swords at each other. After a slight mini swordfight, Keeper of the Rock and Afwa quickly race up behind the guards, Afwa grabs one and Keeper of the Rock grabs the other, Afwa then takes the cutlass and with one blow severs the head of the guards using the cutlass, causing the heads to roll to the floor and for blood to flow, which of course makes Afwa go frantic as he hates the sight of blood, but Keeper of the Rock calms him down as the two men slowly take the clothes of the Barbarian Hoard guards, manage to put them on thus disguise themselves, the two men then enter the gates of the palace and Keeper of the Rock just smiles.. 'Afwa we did it, we entered the Palace, now oh stupid one, you find The Terror and avenge your family..' says Keeper of the Rock with a joyful look. 'Afwa happy, Afwa get to kill..' smiles Afwa. Slowly Afwa is seen wandering off, meanwhile in the far court of the Palace 'The Tongan Terror' himself is seen talking to his most feared commander, other then him called Tyhdres The Gore. Tyhdres is of course a Germanic leader who defected from the legions of Germaine when The Tongan Army attacked their cities and murdered their families. Tyhdres in a rather non recognisable voice is heard speaking to The Tongan Terror. 'Ve must send zee armies into zee camps and desrtroy all zee cities. There is no time to leave anyzing unturned, everyzing shall have to be transferred, for ze have word that ze tribes are preparing zee war and that zee have a man who has the devil working for him..' 'Too much blood has been spilt, too many lives have been lost, all for what..' 'Zee must not give up hope..' 'Tyhdres, I have decided that this war is not getting us anywhere and that peace with the people should be made..' 'Peace.' mocks Tydhres.. 'There shall be NO PEACE! ALL SHALL BE KILLED! Just like I destroyed the cities of Germania, I shall destroy Somata.. The people want to revolt against me, then I shall burn down what is left of their village and just like I slaughtered High Chief Kilmanjaro, I shall..' 'NO YOU KILL DADDY! YOU SUFFER! YOU DIE..' Slowly the beast of Afwa is seen charging with a cutlass raised high, he runs straight at Tyhdres the Gore, pushing him out of the way and then as he sees The Tongan Terror, The Terror goes to withdraw his sword, yet it appears as if he is having problems doing do, thus Afwa does not think twice and spins his sword into the air, slicing off the head of The Tongan Terror, at which Keeper of the Rock is seen in the distance smiling.. Afwa then looks over at the head of The Tongan Terror and says.. 'You Terror no more.. Afwa he go kill you.. He avange family.. You kill daddy and Afwa no like.. Now Afwa see who you are..' Afwa slowly picks up the head of The Tongan Terror, which is still showing a few signs of blood, Afwa tears at the mask of The Tongan Terror and just lets out a loud.. 'AAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!' 'What is it..' Afwa shouts saying.. 'AFWA KILL DADDY! AFWA KILL DADDY!' The cameras show the face of The Tongan Terror, which is none other then Afwa Kilmanjaro's father, the Great High Chief Kilmanjaro. Suddenly a switch is flicked and the lights come back on and Afwa is seen seated on a chair inside a very darkened room, Keeper of the Rock is seen and he just smiles as the final part of Afwa's regression treatment is over.. Keeper of the Rock looks up and says.. 'Afwa, now you have managed to surpass the fears that are inside you, you have managed to go back through time and see just what went wrong.. Afwa, now you must believe me when I say that soon Afwa the time shall come when you are at top of the mountain and are the one people look towards, but now Afwa, you must seek out the revenge upon that one who has caused you harm..' 'Afwa no understand..' says Afwa Keeper of the Rock in a philosophical manner says.. 'Afwa since I began this treatment, you made the point that you were the one who slaughtered your own father, for it was he who took on the persona of The Tongan Terror, and it was he who caused the death of your mother.. The reason for this, nobody will ever know, but Afwa somebody is out there who is like The Tongan Terror and Afwa in the name of your father, I command you destroy him, kill him for he is the Indian, Jay Phoenix and Afwa, very soon, just like you slaughtered your own FATHER, so shall you slaughter Phoenix...' Afwa looks at Keeper of the Rock and says.. 'AFWA KILL PHEONIX! AFWA HURT PHOENIX! AFWA SLAUGHTER PHOENIX! AND AFWA CRUSH PHOENIX!!' 'I see the killer is still within you.. And as promised Afwa, here is your pineapple..' says Keeper of the Rock Keeper of the Rock then presents to Afwa the most spectacular solid gold object, the object being a pineapple on a wooden plinth, engraved with the words 'Slaughterer of men..' Afwa Kilamajaro then takes it and raises it into the air, the cameras then fade up, as Keeper of the Rock is seen smiling, and Afwa is seen playing with his new toy..
