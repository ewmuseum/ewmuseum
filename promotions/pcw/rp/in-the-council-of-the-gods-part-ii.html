In The Council Of The Gods...[Part II]

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: some day in april, i forget which... [The scene reopens where it left off with the Pharaoh and Horus just about to enter the Godly temple in the heavenly realm. As you will already know Pharaoh is here to be ‘dethroned’ from Godhood and so all of his powers, excluding his immortality according to Horus, will be drained from every pore in his body. Pharaoh stands staring sternly into the brightly lit area, the amazingly crafted pillars that line the temples floor, simultaneously holding up the roof, cast down long grey shadows upon the marble floor. Towards the far distance of the temple a vast selection of twenty marble podiums stand ominously, an opening in the temples roof aids light to flow down upon the figures that stand proudly behind the platforms, basking them in warmth. Each of these distant figures stand facing Pharaoh and the God of the Sky with deadly seriously looks painted on their handsome faces. This is the council.] ‘Hello and welcome, at last, to the temple of the Godly Council.’ Exclaims one of the Gods. [Pharaoh and Horus stand stationary, both looking out into the eyes of the Gods in the council; a bead of sweat falls from Pharaoh’s forehead and hits the floor with a tiny patter.] ‘Won’t you please join us?’ says the Godly being. [His long white beard, braided and well kept, shimmers in the sun from the opening above his head with an unearthly brightness. The God, as well as his fellow council members, looks down upon Pharaoh and his mentor as the two begin to slowly track towards the feet of the marble podiums. Pharaoh, still carrying the drone-like Isiy, gulps as he, and his master, finally reach a large circular engraving in the marble floor, an elegant patterned spreads like rivers from the sea out of the circular design and here Titu stands waiting, as though he were a prisoner convicted of a disturbing crime…] ‘Who is this that you have brought with you, Pharaoh?’ questions the mighty God. The Pharaoh: It is my wife, Zeus… Zeus: I see, and why have you brought her here with you? The Pharaoh: Because… [Before Pharaoh can finish his words Horus cuts the young God off.] Horus: Because she is in some sort of trance, obviously something that Akira Akiyama has evilly forced upon her! [The Gods look at one another and low murmurs echo about the place. Zeus frowns and then cracks his fist down upon the podium in front of him. The other Gods speedily fall silent.] Zeus: Horus, the Egyptian God of the Sky, why do you automatically blame this on the Neo Void God, Akiyama? Horus: Because Isiy was kidnapped from her husband and tortured for many countless hours under the watchful eye of the voltage fighter, he has something to do with the state of this young Goddess! I tell you; Akiyama has played a foul game with young Titu here for weeks now! He has done this as a metaphorical ‘last scar’ in his feud with my pupil, The Pharaoh! [Once again the Gods chatter amongst themselves, speaking of Horus’ wise words.] Zeus: This is a truly evil act that has obviously taken place, but to blame it all on one being, one of Akiyama’s godly status is something unspeakable, Horus… [Zeus pauses for a moment.] Zeus: Bring Isiy to me, Titu… [Without question Pharaoh walks towards the mighty Zeus, king of the god, still bearing Isiy over his muscular shoulder. He steps up towards the podium and places Isiy down on her feet. The enchanting Goddess stands motionless, a blank expression plaguing her beautiful visage. Zeus leans forward and studies the young beauty with a thoughtful look.] Zeus: Yes…foul play has obviously been at work here…but I’m more than sure she’ll be fine within the hour…Akiyama’s magic is much weaker than you all think, and that is to say Akiyama did this to her, it could have been one of his minions, or possibly even a Mortal… [Pharaoh breaths a deep sigh of relief.] Zeus: Now…on with the procedure… [Zeus coughs a little to clear his throat.] Zeus: Titu Xansiv, at PCW Spring Fling you lost a pin fall to Akira Akiyama, which was to decide which of the two of you were to be the Gods of PCW…as I have already mentioned, you lost…and so you must now lose your godhood right here in the council of the Gods… [Pharaoh takes a slow step back and then frowns at Zeus.] The Pharaoh: I have a single question before we go through with this… [Zeus nods thoughtfully.] Zeus: Very well…what is it, Pharaoh? The Pharaoh: As you already know, for the past few weeks my friend and mentor, Horus has been working with me to help me train in the ways of the Gods for my matches against the evil Akiyama. After I have been dethroned from Godhood will Master Horus here be aloud to remain with me on earth? [Zeus contemplates the question with a thoughtful look about his wise mug. At length the mighty God speaks in a thunderous tone.] Zeus: I’m afraid I can’t allow that, Titu… The Pharaoh: But why? Zeus: Do not question me, Pharaoh! Horus will stay here from now on in the valley of the Godly beings; his home in the clouds is where he belongs, we are not ones to allow Mortals to interact with immortals! [Pharaoh is about to argue back when master Horus places his wrinkled hand upon Xansiv’s shoulder.] Horus: He has spoken, Titu, do not argue, you will only make it worse for yourself… [Saddened by the news of his mentors inevitable leave from his side Pharaoh sighs and awaits Zeus’ next word on his situation.] Zeus: Titu, I must now ask you to stand back in the centre of the ‘disk’… [Pharaoh slowly walks away from the podium, his head hung in shame. He moves towards the circular engraving in the marble floor and stands in the very centre of its patterned design with a look of anger plaguing his handsome face. Horus watches with a painful expression as Zeus raises his arms in the air…] Zeus: Are you ready? [Slowly, and with fierce eyes Pharaoh looks back up at Zeus…] The Pharaoh: Are you? [The other Gods stir uncomfortably. The evil intent in Titu’s eyes is menacing. Zeus shakes off the thoughts of dismay he had developed after witnessing the darkened eyes of Pharaoh.] Zeus: What do you mean by that? [Pharaoh laughs grimly, but he does not answer. The other Gods chatter amongst themselves again before Zeus slams his fist down upon the marble surface in front of him] Zeus: ENOUGH! [The King of the Gods turns back to Xansiv, staring at the Egyptian in disgust.] Zeus: Very well…have it your way… [Zeus chants something out aloud in ancient Greek, and then suddenly a lightning bolt, the infamous lightning bold of which the Greek god has used to smite many an adversary, grows bright in his hand. Pharaoh watches, unmoving now and with hatred present in his dark eyes as Zeus raises it high in the air before the bolt is hurled across the room and hitting Pharaoh with a deafening clash. The other Gods watch as Pharaoh flies backwards, slides across the floor and then twitches slightly, smoke, dark and hot rises from Titu’s chest, the Egyptian is rendered unconscious.] Horus: What have you done?! Zeus: He is no longer a God…I have dethroned him! Horus: That is not how we went about dethroning Gods when I was a member of the council! Zeus: Many things have changed since you were with us last, Horus… [Zeus smiles maniacally… Horus scowls intensely.] Zeus: Now take him and his wife back to the mortal realm…we have more pressing matters to take care off… [Horus walks towards Isiy and pulls her arm so that she follows in the direction Horus tugs her in. The two then walk towards Pharaoh. The seemingly weak and frail God then kneels down and picks Pharaoh up over his shoulder. He then turns back to the Gods…] Horus: Things have changed here…things have changed for the worst! Zeus: We are all entitled to our own opinions, Horus… [The Gods laugh but Zeus does not share in their chuckle.] Zeus: Now take him back… Horus: His immortality will still be within him you know… Zeus: But what can he do with immortality? He will live forever, that’s all… Horus: We’ll see… Zeus: I’m sure we will… [Horus turns back towards the entrance of the temple and leaves. Outside the sun still shines down brightly, and the plants and foliage are as green as ever. The birds sing in the trees and the thin streams glitter in the light. Suddenly Isiy, still being led down towards the Godly doorway by Horus begins to cry out. Horus turns quickly to see what is the matter but alas Isiy quickly falls to her knees.] Horus: Isiy? [The Goddess shakes her head slowly to and fro and then she looks up. Her blue eyes shine vigorously in the sun of the Godly realm.] Isiy: H-H-Horus? Horus: Yes…it is I, child… [Horus laughs happily, obviously pleased that some good amount of fortune has finally fallen upon the three.] Isiy: W-what’s going on? Why is Titu hung over your shoulder, and where are we? [Horus’ beaming smile falls short, and a saddened look becomes present on his face.] Isiy: What is the matter? Horus: Many things have happened since you were last awake, my dear… Isiy: What do you mean? Horus: Spring Fling has passed, and Titu rescued you from the clutches of that parasite, Akiyama. As you will know the match was a two out of three falls style battle and for each fall something was on the line. Isiy: I didn’t know about any of this…I can only remember being tortured and kidnapped by Akira…then I passed out…the wicked God put a curse on me…I remember that much… [Horus sighs.] Horus: I knew it! [The Egyptian God of the sky scowls and then cracks the cane in his hand down on a nearby stone, denting the stick and seemingly annoying the aged God even more.] Isiy: Calm down, Horus, and tell me what happened afterwards… [Horus reframes himself and then continues with the story.] Horus: Well after you had been taken by the Neo Void God Titu challenged Akiyama to a match at Spring Fling, it was to be named a trinity match. Three pin falls would be involved and the person to score two of the pins would win the match. Each of the three falls had something on the line, the first fall was for the TV title, the loser of the second fall would have to have his godhood taken from him, and the final fall was for your freedom. Isiy: What happened? Horus: Well before the PPV Titu managed to defeat Akiyama for the TV title. A most impressing win I must say… [Isiy’s face beams with a huge smile.] Horus: So Titu went into Spring Fling bearing the TV title around his waist. He retained the title and keeps it to this very day…things didn’t go all to plan though. Titu lost his Godhood in the second fall, hence the reason why we are here today… Isiy: T-Titu isn’t a G-god anymore? [Horus shakes his head with a saddened expression.] Horus: I’m afraid not… [Isiy looks her husband up and down and then sighs deeply.] Isiy: So what’s going on now? Horus: We are in Heavenly realm at the moment, young Isiy. Titu just had his godhood taken from him…he’s a bit banged up but he’ll be fine when he reawakens. I’m afraid this is the first and last time you’ll ever see this wondrous place. [Isiy gazes around herself in awe…] Isiy: This is a beautiful place, beautiful but frightening… [She shivers as though an ice cube had been run up her back.] Horus: Yes, yes it is…but we must leave this place now… [Horus helps Isiy up off the ground and the two, along with Pharaoh, still slung over the apparently scrawny and fragile Gods shoulder. Eventually they come to the heavenly doorway again, the very same that he and Titu entered earlier on. Horus drops Pharaoh back down on the ground. He dusts himself off and then looks down at Isiy.] Horus: Take care of him, Isiy… [Isiy eyes up Horus in confusion.] Isiy: You’re not coming back? [Horus shakes his head, and then hugs Isiy…he sighs and then begins to walk away from the Egyptian splendour. At length the old man turns back and faces Isiy. By now he is a fair distance away yet his dark silhouette can be made out against the setting sun. ] Horus: No…………I’m afraid not……… [The scene fades to black leaving Isiy looking on at the old man as his form disappears over the horizon…]
