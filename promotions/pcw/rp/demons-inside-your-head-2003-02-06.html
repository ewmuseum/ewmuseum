Demons Inside Your Head

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-6-03 Good and evil. They lay inside everyone, from P.W.A. to Tessa to Chris Storm. What side the people take is up to them. Would they fall to the evil or would good rise over all? In recent weeks of emotional turmoil, many close to Chris Storm have been wondering which side he would lean to. Just recently they have discovered the answer. Chris has walked toward the light and embraced his good. Yet evil is not so easily defeated. Inside of shying away from the battlefield it lays dormant, waiting for the moment when it may strike again. So it watches from its hiding spot, it’s chance. It’s dark and cold. Bitter winds rip through the air showing no mercy to any that dare stand against it. Screams fill the air yet they cannot be heard. It’s like a whisper against the wind. There is a light shining through it all though. The light is dim but still visible. Following it there is a twisted metal throne. It lies in the middle of the area, standing all alone. Sharp spikes expel from the large throne, covering it. Placed in front of the throne are two large oval holes that provide the light. Looking closely through the wholes you can see a first person view of young Alex. He’s playing catch with someone, but you cannot tell whom. He throws the ball and the holes follow the course of the ball until a glove catches it. A growl echoes out through the empty area. It sounds as if it is coming from the deranged throne. Looking closer at it, it appears someone it sitting in the throne. Their elbows rest on the arms of the throne. Their hands locked together making a fist which the person’s chin rest on. The growl gets louder as the vision through the ovals is the ball being thrown back to Alex. He catches it with a smile and laughs. A familiar voice rings out, silencing the screams of pain. “I can’t believe this. Look at what has happened to him. I had such hopes for him, but he proved that he really is nothing more then a loser.” The voice commands. The voice booms out, rumbling the area. The voice sounded exactly like Chris Storm’s. There was even the same tone of anger he has when he becomes angry. The voice speaks again. “I should have known this was going to happen. He was far to weak to be one of us. He was an accident. We should have erased him a long time ago. That was our failure.” The person standing in the throne now leans back and stands up from the hair. A mare of blond hair flows down to the back of his neck. His face is slightly turned showing a scar running down the side of his face. He stands in front of the ovals watching what appears to be the world through someone else’s eyes. He walks forward toward the ovals, his hand extended out to touch them. His camouflage pants covering his black army boots, move step and step closer. He stops right before it, only inches from touching it. The light shines though the ovals and hits the person’s extended hand. Smoke begins to rise from it as the skin closely burns away. The person doesn’t pull his hand back. Instead he continues to stare on at the world from his closed prison. The light soon passes and the skin stop burning. The burnt skin starts to repair itself as darkness covers it once more. “So this is how it’s going to be? I will remain in this dark and desalinate place while he is allowed to roam the earth free? Ha, that is his decision I guess, but he threw away his career. I could have made him larger then life. Under my control he would have been an unstoppable force. He would have been a challenge for any in the PCW. Yet he allowed her to turn him into a pathetic excuse for a wrestler. Well fine, he will come crawling back to me when P.W.A. destroys him and his little girlfriend.” The figure states with a sure tone in its voice. His chin rises as he talks. The figure turns around to face the throne again. When it does it reveals that the figure looks just like Chris Storm. Yet it has scars over it’s face and he looks older. To go along with the camouflage pants and army boots, the look a like is dressed in a green vest with a yellow tank top under it. He returns to his seat on the throne and looks up at the eyes again to watch the world. From all these it’s assumed that the eyes belong to none other then Chris Storm. At the moment Chris is carrying Christa in his arms while helping get Evan and Alex inside the house. The sun is slowly sinking into the horizon casting bright colors or orange and red on the sky. A hand is seen wrapping around Chris’ neck from behind. Leaving only one person out of the view it must be Tessa. Chris turns his head allowing himself to look at Tessa. He moves in closer and closes his eyes, obviously to kiss her. They break the kiss and Tessa leans her head on his shoulder as they stand outside. Chris turns his attention back to the beautiful sky. Going back to the Chris Storm look-a-like, he has an annoyed and angry look on his face. His hands are locked onto the ends of the arms of the throne. He has a tight grip on them, almost crushing the ends with his bare hands. “He will come to understand that he is one with me. He and I share the same bond and past. It is only a matter of time until he realizes that. As long as he continues to sail through those easy matches though, he will pretend that everything is all right. He must suffer the pain of defeat and sorrow so that he will understand why he needs me. I need P.W.A. to strip those titles from him and his little blushing bride to be. I need someone to show him that he is weak in his current state!” his voice gets louder and louder the more he thinks about it. He releases his iron grip on the arm of the throne and slams his fist down on it. An idea seeps into his head. That devious smile that Chris gets when he concocts a plan crosses his face. He raises his hand and extends it toward the eyes. With a wave of his hand the vision changes from the romantic sunset to the horrific sight of Tessa’s locker room trashed. Obscenities spray-painted all over the walls. “I need him! The person that was responsible for this. They showed to him that he didn’t sit on top of the world. It’s perfect; this person can strike deep down inside of him. When his eyes fell on this vision of hate I could feel the pain and anger. I was feeding off the feast of emotions running through him.” He shouts, a bit of laughter hidden in his voice. He rubs his hands together as he once again waves his hand in front of the eyes, changing it back to what Chris was seeing at the moment. Chris was now getting ready to go to sleep. The tiredness was weighing heavy on his shoulders as he lay down to go to sleep. Although he was ready for his peaceful slumber, the question remained on how much longer would it last? He still had to defend his titles against P.W.A. and then shortly then after defeat Dorn Tracer in a match. Plus he still needed to figure out who destroyed Tessa’s locker room and why. But for now all that was put aside as he laid his head down onto the pillow and let himself slip into a world where he knows only what he can image.
