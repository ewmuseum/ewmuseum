Training Day

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Training Day Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 09/03/03 “Er.... How long has THIS been here>“ We open up on the furrowed and confused brow of PCW’s Xavier Kannon as he stands at the door of what used to be his larder, but has since defied the laws of physi-.. Well, just saying “The laws” will be shorter... To become a gym, complete with wrestling ring. Xavier : “So... Which turnbuckle is my Muesli in?” Still in his robe, hair matted and scruffy from sleep, Kannon scans the new addition to his home. “Oh, yeah, I kinda... Like... Changed some stuff...” Behind him, dressed in her pink silk robe walks his wife, Ellie~!, an innocent look on her face. She scurries to him, happy at the sight of the wrestling ring, and not really wondering where all their food went. Xavier : “When you say ‘Changed’... Do you mean totally removed something and for some unknown reason, built a Gym where the cereals used to be?” Flashing a cheeky grin, Ellie~! looks out the top of her vacuous eyes at him. Eleanor : “Yup! Don’t worry though, I moved all the important stuff...” The Scientologist lets out a sigh of relief, happy that his ditzy and sometimes overly eager wife hasn’t just removed an ENTIRE room’s content from their house. Xavier : “Good, where’s th-” Before he can ask, Eleanor confesses... Well, not really confesses as she doesn’t really care, we’ll go with ‘reveals’... Eleanor : “Well, by ‘important’ I mean I moved the Pop Tarts!” Rolling his eyes, Kannon accepts that he’ll have to go hungry... However, talk of breakfast foods hasn’t made him forget that suddenly the dimensions of his house are very much different and that he’s looking at a wrestling ring... But something small troubles him. Xavier : “That ring doesn’t look right... It’s just, well, off....” Shrugging, Ellie~! dutifully chirps out the reason. Eleanor : “Well DUH! It is an OSW one...” Xavier “Ahhhhhhhh”s... Then starts to wonder why it’s an OSW ring. Eleanor : “It’s probably albino or something...” Eyes narrowing curiously, Kannon slowly steps into the new room his house acquired over night, making his way towards the ring. Grinning, Ellie~! follows him, happy with her new playground. Xavier : “So why is this here again? Wait... I know...” Rolling his head as if it were obvious, Kannon finally twigs as to why. Xavier : “It’s because you applied to Queen of Extreme isn’t itARGHHHHHH!!!” Suddenly he surges the upper half of his body back as a knife flies from the ring, shaving past his nose and narrowly missing, before embedding itself in the far wall. Xavier : “What in Xenu’s name was that?!?” As if nothing just happened, Ellie~!’s pale blue eyes look to her man blankly. Eleanor : “Like, what was what?” Startled that even SHE didn’t notice a 6” blade whistling through the air between them, Kannon points to the wall where the knife is lodged. “Ummmm”ing, Ellie twiddles her hair, reluctant to answer. Xavier : “THAT is what!” Pursing her lips to resist answering, she finally caves. Eleanor : “Okay, but don’t say anything as you might hurt his feelings...” Kannon double-takes, wondering who the Utah “He” is. Eleanor : “The ring is Bi-Polar... So you know? He may try to stab you... Or “Violate” you, or, like SO try to shove you in a trash compactor and stuff.” Stunned, taken-aback, and in a state of disbelief, Kannon can only whisper. Xavier : “Bi-Polar?” Then, looking to the knife and then feeling his nose where it just avoided contact, he questions it... As if that would do any good, we’re talking about a ring with a mental condition here. Xavier : “HOW CAN A RING BE BI-POLAR?!?!?” Looking as if she wants to keep their voices down so the ring doesn’t hear, Ellie~!’s “Shhhhhhhhhh”s her man and whispers. Eleanor : “I think it’s parents died when it was young or something.” REALLY not knowing what to say, Kannon just cocks his head to the side... Waiting for Ellie~! to say it’s a joke... Which she doesn’t. Eleanor : “So, just be careful...” Thinking things over and weighing things up in his head, Kannon finally thinks he has a summary of the situation... Now, time to test it. Xavier : “Okay, so let me catch up here...” Keeping a nervous eye on the Bi-Polar ring, Xavier runs through. Xavier : “As you’re in Queen of Extreme, you suddenly decided to make our pantry into a Gym, with a wrestling ring that just HAPPENS to be Bi-Polar and want to stab a lot of things?” Grinning and nodding, Ellie~! agrees. Eleanor : “Yup, that’s about it.... I’m, like, SO going to win the SO Queenzzies of being WAY Extreme and stuff! YAY ME!” She does her little bounce on the spot and applauds herself as Kannon wonders what he’s gotten himself into having agreed to help her train for it. Xavier : “Er, my little Poon Puppy, I think they just call it QoE.” Not liking that name much, she sneers. Eleanor : “Well that’s GaY!” Tiring of this already, Kannon makes a suggestion, hoping a bit of extra time will... Well, change everything. Xavier : “How about we grab breakfast, then we can.... Well, come back here and.... You know...” Eleanor : “Stuff?” He nods. Xavier : “Yeah.” Seeming keen on the idea... Well, the part that involves Pop Tarts, she scurries off back to the hallway leaving Kannon to sigh and shake his head. Xavier : “If she didn’t drop at the click of my fingers or take it from all directions...” Walking back to the door, he suddenly screws his face up and ducks as another knife spins through the air and embeds itself into the door. Looking up to the skies and hoping for an intervention from Xenu himself, Kannon strolls out the door leaving just the Bi-Polar ring.... “Fuck turnbuckles. Fuck ropes. Fuck ringposts. Fuck stairs. Fuck canvas. Fuck aprons...” ... As we fade to black. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
