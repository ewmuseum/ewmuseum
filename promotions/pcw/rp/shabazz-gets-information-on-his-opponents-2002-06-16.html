Shabazz Gets Information On His Opponents...

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/16/02 OOC: Don't get mad if I'm too racist for you...[/OOC] Jordan X Shabazz went to Delaware when we last saw him to try and get himself into a small community center to speak to a group of crack heads about how to shake their habits and move on to the future. He was going to tell them that University was the way to happiness. He was going to tell them that the only way that a black man would succeed in the world was if they stopped wearing baggy pants and claiming to represent certain gangs. But that was all for naught, as Shabazz and his friend Dante Jackson spent their time inside the community center chillin’ with the owners of the place at a party where drugs were being abused. Jordan messed up a chance to preach and get some publicity. But in Jordan’s mind, he also lost the opportunity to change the lives of a certain few people who had been drug addicts and now could have changed the lives of those people, had he not have gone to the party. One of Jordan’s opponents for Friday Fusion, The End, is legally blind, and he also is half black. So Jordan, being the type of guy that he is, Jordan has decided to spend a third of his afternoon helping a blind man around the city of Davidsonville, Delaware, sponsored by the National Blind Men Association, which is located in St. Williamson’s, Delaware. The other third of Jordan’s day will be spent at a Military compound where he will try and search the files to get information on another one of his opponents for Fusion, John Crichton. And the final third of Jordan’s day, he is going to go to a Mexican restaurant to eat like he assumed that El Diablo would. The scene opens up inside of a small place that appears to be in a classroom like setting. There aren’t desks, rather tables with some chairs beneath them. Some of the chairs are occupied, some are not. All of the men are wearing dark sunglasses. They all seem to be distant. A woman standing at the front of the room is speaking to them. Woman: “Okay, so you all are going to be picked up today by Celebrities, and they will take you out to eat, and in general have fun!! This is something that we never would have expected to happen, you guys should be grateful.” Some of the men aren’t too happy about having to leave their homes. Most of the people in the classroom are currently residing in the sort of Group Home for blind men. Before one of the blind men can scream an angry reply at their caretaker, Jordan X Shabazz walks through the door, smiling. Jordan X Shabazz: “Hello, hello! What’s going on ladies and gentlemen? It’s me, Jordan X Shabazz from the PCW, I know you guys probably don’t watch because you can’t see anything, but it’s all gravy. I’m going to pick somebody that I’ll spend a few hours with, we’ll go out and get some food, we’ll watch…Erm… listen to some of the baseball game, and maybe I could buy you a porn magazine that you could feel your way around.” The caretaker doesn’t do anything but smile as Jordan X Shabazz’s motor mouth continues going speaking nonsensical things, mumbling to himself about having to wrestle the End. Jordan X Shabazz: “Say, miss, think you could get me a black blind guy?” The woman ignores him and grabs one of the older, white blind guys. She walks him over to a now angry Jordan X Shabazz. Woman: “This here is Jack, Jack Jameson, he is the one that you will take out for the day. Be very careful with him, he’s old, and very delicate. Please don’t do anything that would induce a heart attack.” Jordan just nods his head furiously and he grabs Jack by his arm and slowly walks towards the door. Jack waves his walking cane around wildly, but it doesn’t bother Shabazz. *********************** It’s later on in the day now. Shabazz has lost the old man. That’s right. Jordan X Shabazz took the old man to a coffee shop where they ate and spent a few minutes talking. Jordan was trying to find out if their was any way possible that you could go undetected and unnoticed by a blind man. Jack, the blind man that Jordan was with, didn’t answer. So Jordan became frustrated, Jordan, being the mean bastard that he is, took Jack to the subway station… and he left him there. That’s right… Left him there. The man could accidentally fall in front of a subway, he could get lost and never find his way home, he’s old and senile, and is now in trouble. Jordan thought of all these things, and he still let it happen. *Sigh* Mean ol’ Civil Rights Activist. Now Jordan is at a library. There were no military compounds in the area where Jordan could possibly find information on John Crichton. So he has decided to go to a library and use the Internet. He was going to try and get on the PCW’s website to find some info that he could use against John Crichton. Jordan sits at the computer, as it logs on to the PCW’s website. He maneuvers his way around the line ups for house shows and cards, and he gets to the roster page, and finds John Crichton’s name. Jordan begins reading aloud. Jordan X Shabazz: “Real name… Dave J. Constantinople!” Jordan X Shabazz breaks out into laughter. Jordan X Shabazz: “That is the sorriest white boy name I ever heard in my life!” Jordan breaks out into more childish Civil Rights laughter as he scrolls down and checks out the stats on John Crichton. Jordan X Shabazz: “Television champion for one hour…” More laughter from Shabazz. Jordan X Shabazz: “I’d rather not hold a damned title if they were going to take it away from me an hour later! That’s sad, real sad, and it’d only happen to a white boy, I wish a Muthafucka would try and take a title from me…” Shabazz goes back to reading about John Crichton’s past, not really finding anything too interesting. And then Jordan comes across the death of John Crichton’s former manager, and lover, Aeryn Sun. Apparently she was murdered at the hands of a certain nemesis that John Crichton had, Mr. Fleming and a Ms. Zemoff. Shabazz appreciates life, well, life of his friends and black people. Crichton and his ex-lover, Aeryn Sun, were neither, so typically… Jordan X Shabazz: “HAHAHAHHAHAH! HOLY SHIT! HAHAHAHAHHAHA! This is so funny, I can’t wait ‘til I see this white boy again. I’ll make fun of him so…” “Could you please keep it down.” a loud voice came. Jordan X Shabazz: “Sorry, I was just laughing at the death of somebody. It won’t happen again, it was wrong, I’m sorry.” Jordan X Shabazz closes out the window on the computer and he shuts it down, now that he knows one of John Crichton’s weaknesses, expect him to exploit it greatly. ******************************* Jordan was supposed to head to a Mexican restaurant where he would learn to relate with El Diablo by having some Mexican food, but Jordan, being the intelligent, ideal African American that he is, has decided to head the immigration offices of Delaware. Our scene opens up as the day gets later and later towards night. The sun is beginning to set. Yet the immigration offices are still packed with Africans, Asians, Europeans, and the people that Jordan came to harass, Mexicans/Hispanics. Shabazz enters the immigration office and he seats himself by the Africans, trying to blend in. Shabazz in reality was preparing to run to the front of the room and preach his racist beliefs. He was looking for the moment when the security wouldn’t be paying attention, and their was nobody at the front of the room. After no more than 12 minutes, his opportunity presented itself. Shabazz ran to the front of the crowd of immigrants, and began screaming. Jordan X Shabazz: “ALL OF YOU EXCEPT THE AFRICANS SHOULD LEAVE DAMN IT! GO BACK TO MEXICO! STOP STEALING OUR CARS AND COPYING OUR MUSIC!! YOU MEXICAN'S ARE BORDER HOPPING BASTARDS, LIKE THAT EL DIABLO! AFRICANS CREATED THIS WORLD! I LOVE AFRICA! I AM AFRICAN AND NOT AFRAID TO SAY IT!!” Shabazz is grabbed by two security guards are dragged to the door where he was ejected and told never to come back. All in all, he did a lot of bad things in the day, yet he didn’t get in trouble. El Diablo, John Crichton, and The End, his opponents for Fusion, Shabazz learned some traits that they all had. Now he must use them to his advantage.
