Revenge Saga Part 12: KIMLAR! The Master of Time! And Crichton Vs an old adversary…

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/6/02 Hunted Saga Part 12: KIMLAR! The Master of Time! And Crichton Vs an old adversary… Last Time… “ Do not doubt my powers evil one! Or I will go back in time and prevent you from ever being born!” Kimlar states, getting very frustrated indeed. “Yeah Right! Sure you will” Crichton muttered aloud, almost taunting Kimlar. Kimlar closes his eyes and starts to shake. “There! Mission done!” Kimlar declares valiantly. Crichton just stands there staring at Kimlar, with a blank expression upon his face. “Erm…Kimlar…I’m still alive!” Crichton states, sarcastically. Kimlar opens his eyes and realises that Crichton is in fact still standing right in front of him. “It should take a few minutes, hold on!” Kimlar states. Crichton sighs. “Listen buddy, I gotta be somewhere. I’m not going to die today…” Crichton trails off. “I’d like to debate that actually!” The voice declares. Both Crichton and Kimlar stared at the man, as he stepped off the fire stairs, guns pointing at both men. Crichton visibly sighs. “Why does this shit always happen to me!” He curses. Crichton stared blankly as the man stalks ever closer to him and Kimlar. Crichton had recognised the man instantly, and couldn’t believe it. It was Dan Lyons. Crichton remembered the last encounter he had with Lyons, he and Aeryn barely got out of it alive. He looked over at Kimlar, who was now stood beside him, and noticed the fear in his eyes. Not much of a Super Hero he silently concluded. “Well if it isn’t my old friend John Crichton!…” Dan Lyons begins, shifting his view to Kimlar. “…And someone trying to impersonate Elvis…badly!” Kimlar strides forward confidently. “I am KIMLAR! Master of Time!…” Kimlar declares. Dan Lyons silently strides forward and smashes the butt of his gun, on Kimlar’s jaw. Kimlar goes down like a sack of spuds. “I don’t think he gives a shit!” Crichton whispers to himself. Crichton bends down, grabs Kimlar under the armpits and drags him to his feet. “So…uh…wh…who is this guy anyhow?” Kimlar questioned, gingerly rubbing his jaw. Crichton sighs. “Another enemy of mine. His name is Dan Lyons.” Crichton explains, not wanting to tell Kimlar the full extent of his history with Lyons. Kimlar nods in understanding. “He’s after you as well then!” He declares. Lyons cocks his shotgun and edges closer to Crichton. “So Elvis, what the hell are you doing here, with this creep?” Lyons questions. Kimlar looks blankly at Lyons and does not respond. Crichton nudges him slightly. “He’s talking you!” He whispers. “Oh right. I’m a superhero and I came to bring him down.” . Lyons nods with a smile on his face. “I see!…” Lyons begins, turning to Crichton. “…Crichton you hang around with all of the scum don’t you!” Kimlar shakes with fury. “What the hell is that supposed to mean?” Kimlar questions, visibly angry. “Well I mean you won’t even admit to being friends with Crichton. Your trying to squirm your way out of it, by declaring that you were some kind of superhero.” “I am a superhero dammit!” Kimlar declares trying to stride forward, but being held back by Crichton. “Let him go Crichton! I’ll teach him not to lie!” Lyons commands. A smile develops upon Crichton’s face. “Look at you! You’re pathetic! Salivating at the mouth at the chance to maim and murder! Now who’s the villain Lyons.” Crichton shouts, his anger getting the best of him. “I’m only a killer now Crichton, because you made me this way! You’re the one that slaughtered my family, you’re the one that ruined my life! You and everyone you know, deserve to die.” Lyons shouts back. “No Lyons. Fleming was the one who destroyed your family! He was the one who chased me and Aeryn into your diner that day! He was the one who was shooting at us. I had to shoot back, and unfortunately my bullet hit the gas tanks stored at the back of your diner.” Crichton explains, managing to maintain his temper. “Bollocks! He was the one that provided me with the necessary training and weaponry to track you down!” Lyons replied, not doing so well to keep his temper in check. “And why do you think he did that?…because he wanted another ally, he needed help in bringing me in alive!, but you were not interested in keeping me alive were you Lyons, you wanted me dead. One of Fleming’s greatest mistakes I imagine. I am not the only one to blame for this!” Crichton explains. A smile develops upon Lyons’ face. “You know, what you’re right! You’re not the only one to blame. That bitch you call ‘Aeryn’ is also responsible. Where is she anyhow?” Lyon’s questions. Crichton lowers his head a little. “No…Lyon’s…she’s dead. She was also killed by Fleming. Looks like we’ve got something in common.” Crichton replied, not wanting to elaborate on the details. “Well, it looks like justice has already been served! Fleming did to you, the exact same thing you did to me! For the first time in many months I believe that there is a god! And now, I get to finish you off!” Lyons states. “I think not villain, for KIMLAR! The Master of Time!…and various furry animals, will stop you!” Kimlar states, stepping forward valiantly. “No! Kimlar, you really shouldn’t do that!” Crichton whispers attempting to hold back Kimlar. Lyons cocks his shotgun once more and takes aim at Kimlar. “Looks like Elvis wants to be first! Okay I can deal with it!” Lyons states. Kimlar does not flinch. “Now my beauties, attack!” Kimlar declares. Crichton sighs, but then out of the corner of his eye, he spots something moving. He moves his head to look and spots at least a dozen squirrels clambering onto the roof and making their way over to Lyons. Crichton looks the other and spots more of the furry creatures heading towards Lyons. Crichton breaks a smile, which then develops into a laugh. Lyons looks at him oddly, just as series of twenty squirrels begin to attack him. Lyons manages to throw a few off, but is eventually over come by the creatures. The shotgun falls from his hands onto the floor. Realising the battle is lost, Lyons turns and runs away towards the fire stairs. “I’ll be back John Crichton, mark my words.” Lyons shouts, squirrels stick ravaging. “Well Kimlar, I’m sorry I doubted your powers!” Crichton declares, patting Kimlar on the shoulder. “Well Crichton, they really are pretty laughable…but very effective.” Kimlar states making his way over to the fire escape stairs. “I thought you came here to ‘take me down’?” Crichton questions. “I will take you down on another day Crichton, today we were allies, but next time, I will show no mercy!” Kimlar states, walking out of Crichton’s view. Crichton shakes his head. He really couldn’t believe the series of events lately, but he was kind of glad of Kimlar’s assistance. He wouldn’t be alive if Kimlar hadn’t been here. Although he would have liked the work out of hand to hand combat. His match tonight on Friday Fusion would be tough, and he wanted some ‘realistic’ training under his belt. A four way dance, against some of the most feared and respected men, upon the PCW roster. El Diablo, The End and Jordan Shabazz. He was particularly looking forward to getting a little revenge upon Shabazz from Tuesday Night, when he was ‘comforting’ Jaden. I’ll show him comforting Jaden! Crichton thought visciously. On one hand he wanted to win the match and on the other he wanted revenge upon Shabazz, during the match these two will be in conflict with one and other, he only hoped the right choice would prevail…but he doubted it.
