Colonel Sanders

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-20-02 Brandi pulls up through the narrow space they call a drive-in. She sits impatiently in her black 2002 Camaro. She’s running late. She was supposed to pick up dinner and meet Rent-A-Villain back at her place. Now, thanks to traffic and a long line at the store, she’s going to be late. There’s a line of four cars in front of her. She taps her hand against the steering wheel. She hates being late. Especially to see RAV. The line of cars creeps forward until she’s finally at the speaker. She rolls down her window and waits for the crackly voice to come out of the box. Voice- This…s…KFC. Wha…can I get…you? Brandi leans out of her window and talks in the direction of the box. Brandi- I need two of your leg wing meals. Both with Cokes. Voice- Tha…seven fifty at the nex…indow. Brandi has to wait once again for the cars in front of her to hurry up so that she can pull up to the window. While she waits, she rummages around in her purse and pulls out the right amount of money. When she finally makes it to the window, she quickly hands the money to the drive-through woman and takes her bag of food. Next, she is handed the two drinks which she carefully places in the drink holders between the drivers and passenger seat. She quickly pulls out of the drive through lane, and quickly looks through the bag to make sure that her order is right. When she pulls out the box and finds that there are no legs, only wings, she gets frustrated and pulls her car into a parking space. Brandi- Always when I don’t have time for this! Brandi grabs the food, gets out of the car, and slams the door behind her. She walks inside Kentucky Fried Chicken only to find a line longer than the drive-through. She looks at the line, then hurries to one of the workers. Brandi- Look, I just went through the drive-through, and you guys got my order wrong. KFC Guy- You’ll have to go wait in line. Brandi- But I just waited in line! You’re the ones that messed up. KFC Guy- All these other people are in front of you. I can’t help you. Brandi storms to the back of the line and crosses her arms. She shoots a glare over at the worker, and grips the bag of food in an iron-tight fist. This line moves much slower than the one outside. It’s obvious that the cashier hasn’t been here for long, and is still trying to get the hang of working the register. Brandi sighs and waits. Brandi- I can’t believe this. I should’ve brought my phone in with me. Then I could at least call Rent-A-Villain and tell him I’ll be late. I mean, it’s probably going to look really bad showing up to my own house late. Brandi inches forward in line. Finally, to her relief, another cash register is opened and for a second the line seems to move faster. When she finally gets to the counter, she throws the bag of food down on it. Brandi- Ok, I just went through the drive-through line, and you guys messed up my order. I’m supposed to have two leg and wing meals, but both meals only have wings. I’m kind of in a hurry, so could I just get my legs and be on my way? The KFC guy punches a few buttons on his register and looks up the order in the computer. He shakes his head slowly. KFC Guy- No, you order both meals with wings only. That’s what it says right here. He points it out to Brandi on his computer screen. She gets more frustrated. Brandi- No, I didn’t! I’m telling you right now that I ordered them with both wings and legs. Are you trying to call me a liar? KFC Guy- No, I’m just showing you what the computer says. It says that you ordered them both with wings. Brandi- I heard you the first time, but that’s not what I ordered! KFC Guy- Look, calm down, would you? You’re making a scene and I’m afraid I’m going to have to ask you to leave. Brandi- Ask me all you want. I’m not leaving here until you fix this. KFC Guy- Well, there’s nothing I can do. Brandi- Alright then. I want to talk to your manager. Brandi leans over the counter and looks in the back. Brandi- Hello! Are any of you the manager? Everyone stares at Brandi, but no one responds. The cashier looks embarrassed and he tries to calm Brandi down. KFC Guy- Alright. I’ll go get the boss, but if you don’t calm down I’m going to have to force you to leave. Brandi laughs and makes her way to a booth beside the wall. Brandi- You go get your boss and unless you think that you can physically move me out of here, I suggest you keep your mouth shut. She has a seat just as the cashier goes off to find his boss. It doesn’t take long before the kitchen door swings open and Colonel Sanders himself walks out. He looks around the room, and walks over to Brandi. Sanders adjusts his glasses, and has a seat right next to Brandi. Brandi looks shocked. Sanders- So, I hear that you’re having a bit of a problem. Tell me about it. Brandi- Well, it’s just that I didn’t get what I ordered so I came inside and that guy is trying to tell me I got exactly what I ordered. I was supposed to meet this guy for dinner, and now I’m late and I don’t even have what I was supposed to bring. Colonel Sanders throws his hands against the table and laughs. Sanders- Is that all? You’re late to meet a guy? Brandi nods and Sanders looks her over. He reaches over and runs his hand up and down her leg. Then he winks at her. Sanders- Well, why don’t we make sure that you’re a little bit later? Brandi backs against the edge of the booth and stares at the old man. She shakes her head. Brandi- Nah, why don’t you just fix up my order so I can be on my way? Sanders- Well, I’m afraid I can’t do that. You see, I like you. Colonel Sanders always gets what he likes, do you understand? Brandi- All I understand is that you’re like 70 and you’re really grossing me out. Sanders- I may be old, but I’ve still got what it takes. He runs his fingers up and down Brandi’s leg again and leans in towards her face. He reaches up with the other hand and grabs one of her breasts. Brandi looks shocked and she head butts the old man as hard as she could. His glasses break and he falls backwards. Brandi jumps up from the booth and kicks him a few times. Brandi- Now, trying to explain to Rent why I’m late and why I have no food with me. Brandi steps over the fallen body of the Colonel and rushes out of the building.
