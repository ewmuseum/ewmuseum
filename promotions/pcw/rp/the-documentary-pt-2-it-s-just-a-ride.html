The Documentary pt.2: It's Just A Ride

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The world is like a ride in an amusement park. And when you choose to go on it, you think it's real because that's how powerful our minds are. And the ride goes up and down and round and round. It has thrills and chills and it's very brightly coloured and it's very loud and it's fun, for a while. Some people have been on the ride for a long time and they begin to question, is this real, or is this just a ride? And other people have remembered, and they come back to us, they say, 'hey - don't worry, don't be afraid, ever, because, this is just a ride...' And we... kill those people. 'Shut him up.' 'We have a lot invested in this ride. Shut him up. Look at my furrows of worry. Look at my big bank account and my family. This just has to be real.' Just a ride. But we always kill those good guys who try and tell us that, you ever notice that? And let the demons run amok. But it doesn't matter because: It's just a ride. And we can change it anytime we want. It's only a choice. No effort, no work, no job, no savings and money. A choice, right now, between fear and love. The eyes of fear want you to put bigger locks on your doors, buy guns, close yourself off. The eyes of love, instead, see all of us as one. Here's what we can do to change the world, right now, to a better ride. Take all that money that we spend on weapons and defences each year and instead spend it feeding and clothing and educating the poor of the world, which it would many times over, not one human being excluded, and we could explore space, together, both inner and outer, forever, in peace. - Bill Hicks A huge line of people are waiting in queue for some rollercoaster at what must be an amusement park, within the queue stand El Diablo, Angela, Kurt and Leroy. El Diablo is of course frustrated at being stuck with 'vermin' all around him, Angela tired because of the high heels she is wearing, Leroy keeping an eye on things and Kurt filming these escapades for Diablo's television documentary- although it seems no one will be airing it. Angela has her husband's arms around her, probably to cover up, but nonetheless she feels safe in his hands. She has her long dyed black hair loose and is wearing a rather revealing top and short skirt- she is a real looker. The Mexican Devil also has his hair loose and is wearing a loose black shirt and baggy denim jeans, almost as if he is attempting to look cool. Leroy looks likes a typical bodyguard, donned in an all black suit with matching sunglasses and even a 'walkie talkie' on him. Kurt is dressed the same as Leroy, but has the digital video camera with him to film El Diablo and his life. 'That's perfect, just keep your arms around her- the audience will love this sensitive side,' says Kurt wandering around the happy couple. El Diablo shakes his head annoyed. 'Aren't you taking this cameraman thing a bit far?' asks El Diablo, to one of the three people he trusts with his life. Kurt stops walking around and places the camera to his side, but still switched on. 'I am not just a cameraman, I am an artist! And this is my project, so just follow what I say,' Kurt says seemingly a tad annoyed. The Lord of Mexico smiles and nods. 'Remember who pays you though,' he whispers arrogantly. Kurt then backs off and remains filming, and as he does he quickly turns around to film Leroy. 'WHOA! No pushing in and no getting near your vast superior, El Diablo,' Leroy yells at some people trying to walk by them. One of them, a middle aged balding man stands up to the much bigger Leroy. 'This is great action,' mouths Kurt while filming this. 'What gives you the right to say who can do what? And who is superior, you, you APE!' yells the balding man at Leroy. Leroy smirks and nods and then shoves the man down to the ground. 'Being hired security gives me that right,' Leroy comments calmly in the direction of the man as he turns back around to people booing in the queue. El Diablo releases an arm from around Angela to pat Leroy on the arm to show his gratitude for such a great job done. Kurt zooms in on El Diablo's face as he does. 'Now would be a good time, to tell the people your thoughts on things,' he says calmly. Diablo nods and releases his last arm from around Angela. 'Not a bad idea, Kurt,' The Lord of Mexico states. He then coughs, almost like he is on stage and warming up to public speaking. 'Well, Alliances would be as good a topic as any,' Diablo mutters, 'It's El Diablo, Clyde and the PCW World Title and what goes hand in hand with Clyde? No, not the PCW Title, which is soon to be mine, but corruption. As has been seen over the last four weeks, Clyde has shown what a cheat he is, and how corrupt he can be, and I can guarantee he will attempt to do all that and more at Alliances to beat the unbeatable man- EL DIABLO!' Diablo yells this passionately and breathes heavily to get his breath back. 'So, who's going to win?' Kurt asks, almost like an interviewer. The Mexican Devil stares into the camera, really hyped up, while in the background Leroy can be seen shoving some more people away from El Diablo. 'Who's going to win? Do you really need to ask that? It's obvious on who the victor will be, it will be I, and for what reasons? 1. It is a 1on1 match up, with rules. 2. There will not be a corrupt referee in the ring. And 3. I AM THAT TALENTED TO BEAT THE BURNED MAN I AM EL DIABLO DAMMIT, and to me that says it all,' Diablo hisses at the camera. Kurt turns the camera to face him, and smiles, 'El Diablo to be new PCW Champion at Alliances!' He then turns it back around to face El Diablo and zooms it in on him again. 'Anything you'd like to add?' he questions his friend and boss. Diablo wipes the sweat from his forehead, and is really worked up just talking about Alliances. 'Life is just a ride, but life is MY ride, PCW is MY ride, the PCW Title is MY life and to complete my ride I must win that Title, that Title is my only desire and on Turmoil following Alliances I will address the world as new PCW Champion, so join me then as I complete my ride,' Diablo surprisingly says calmly into the camera. Diablo turns back around and places his arms around his wife, little does he do know at this point that it is imperative he does that, but momentarily people start fighting each other, all due to Leroy shoving people into other people. 'FUCK,' Leroy shouts as hoards of people come his way. Diablo at this point is just trying to maintain the safety of his wife Angela. 'What a show,' Kurt gleefully says, standing back from the fighting and filming it all, until a man gets thrown into him and causes the camera to cut out.
