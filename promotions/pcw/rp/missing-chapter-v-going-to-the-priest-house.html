Missing: Chapter V: Going to the Priest' House

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Chapter 5 I stared up the stone walk-way to a small, one story house. The house was a plain one, with white X-90 siding and a garage to the right side. There was a door rigth beside the garage and to the left of that, a bay view window from living room inside the house. The house was silent, nothing could be heard. With caution, I headed up the walk path. I was at Fredrick Thomas' house, or Father Thomas to most of his parishoners, if he was real. Fredrick was supposed to be Ivan Chistov, the one man I was looking for and if this was my only chance to catch him, I would go for it. I still hadn't decided what to do if Fredrick wasn't in this house. One thing that was bugging me and was certainly on my mind was the fact that Ivan Chistov had six known aliases. I had only found four. What if the other two aliases were what led me to find him? I wouldn't doubt it because with the luck I had, it was bound to be true. What about Henry Claval? The master mind could be anywhere in the world, and for all I knew, Ivan was with him as well, in his house, laughing at me for trying such an impossible mission. Henry was described as being 'armed and dangerous' and I didn't want to take my chance with him. I reached the door way and took a deep breathe. I tried to peek in the curtain in the bay window but could see no movement from inside the house. Knowing I had no other option and wouldn't leave Germany without knowing, I knocked on the door of the priest's house and waited. ..... ..... ..... ..... ..... There was no answer. I started to wonder if maybe my knocking wasn't loud enough. My hands were all sweaty and I was shivering all over my arm. This time, my eye caught the door bell on the left side of the door. Abruptly after thinking it, I ringed the door bell. The ding from inside the house told me that the door bell worked fine and loud. ..... ..... ..... ..... ..... Still no answer. I took it that nobody was home. I peered over my shoulder to the left to the church where I suspected the priest presided mass. There were no cars in sight anywhere and the parking lot was empty. No church. Disappointed that I didn't get any leads at all, I turned around to walk to my rented car. I was about four steps closer to my car when an old women's voice from behind me spoke. Women: Hello? I turned around slowly. This was it! I forgot all about the nervousness and put on my detective look. I looked at the women, only about thirty five or so. SyN: Hello? Who is the priest here? The women opened the door to the house all the way and stood in the threshold. She looked as if she were thinking of the right words to say. Women: Well, nobody yet. I just fill in for the masses until a new priest is assigned. No priest! I came all the way here to Germany to find Ivan Chistov or Fredrick Thomas and I found neither of them? Instead, I found this nice women who didn't have a clue at my intentions. I calmy kept my cool about the whole situation. SyN: Who used to be the priest here? Women: Father Thomas. He left on personal matters about a month ago. The replacement priest will be here later on this week if that is who you are looking for. None of this made sense. How could Father Thomas leave Germany a month ago and go to America, stealing money? How long was Chistov a part of the Krishna? The missionary back in America said he was a loyal member so he must have been in touch for a fairly long time. SyN: Okay, thank-you. Can I ask what your name is? The women smiled at me. Women: Clair Rivence I nodded and waved to the lady, walking over to my car. It was doomed. I would never find Ivan Chistov...
