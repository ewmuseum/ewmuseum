Snow on the Tonight Show

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered One year... One year ago, an egotistical twenty-two year old Jason Snow stepped into PWA after having successful, yet short, runs in two Indie Feds: SIW and UWLM. What a ride it’s been since then... The blue eyed kid signed a PWA contract on May 3, 2001. He went on to become one of PWA’s first two graduates, being signed to PCW2 on the same day as his well-known rival, Vampir Nosferatu. Indeed, Snow’s career has been one of firsts. In PCW2, the entire wrestling world took notice of the young phenom. He soon captured the PCW2 Rising Star strap - the first ever PCW2 Rising Star Championship awarded. He then became the first man to win the PCW Rising Star title in his first PCW match. Snow’s career in PCW has also been monumental. He was the first graduate of PWA to become the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World. He was the first Canadian to accomplish the same feat. He was, however, the second man to win (if you can call it that) the PCW Championship on three separate occasions. Indeed, there may be some weight to Snow’s claim that he is the most successful man in the history of PCW... Jay Leno: “Alright folks, my first guest tonight is one that I’m very excited about...” The scene opens on the set of the Tonight show where Jay Leno sits behind his desk looking much the same as always. The cocky host continues his introduction... Leno: “You see, I have a confession to make. I - Jay Leno - am a wrestling fan. And no, I don’t mean your average wrestling fan, I mean a HUGE wrestling fan. I still remember when Kid Wonder was cool! That’s how much of a wrestling fan I am!” Reacting to a bright red sign, the audience laughs at Jay’s joke, which came at the expense of what must no doubt be an angry Kid Wonder... if he’s watching, that is... then again, Kid Wonder watches everything... doesn’t he? Leno: “So that’s why I’m so excited about my first guest tonight. He is PCW’s current Heavyweight Champion of the World and the self proclaimed most successful man in the history of PCW - give it up for Jason Snow everybody!” Leno’s band plays while the reluctant Killin’ Villain steps out to a welcome of cheers that he’s not used to - when Snow’s in front of an audience, he expects to be boo’ed. It’s like an instinct now. He’s spent the majority of his career, except for a few stints here and there, as one of the most hated men in all of wrestling... Leno: “Welcome to the show, Jason!” Snow, sporting the PCW title on his shoulder, stares out into the audience in awe. He doesn’t mind the cheers, though he may be confusing them for envy. He’s a bit more dressed up than usual - his long blond hair is tied back tonight, and he’s wearing a button-down black silk shirt. Other than that, it’s the usual - jet black leather pants, Snake Skin boots, and a look on his face that would screw terror into the heart of a lion... Snow sits down and glares at Leno, who doesn’t seem to talk about... Leno: “Jason Snow, it is a pleasure to have you on.” Snow adjusts his championship... Snow: “Of COURSE it is!” Leno laughs, as does his audience... Leno: “You know, I caught a PCW show the other night - I think it was Sabotage - and I happened to hear you say that you are the most successful man in PCW.” Snow nods... Leno: “I just want to take everyone back to the beginning. You started out in SIW, right? And what was that? A small Indie Federation based in... Montreal, was it? Montreal?” Snow scowls... Snow: “And what a lowly federation full of inferior maggots that was... by Greatness, if it weren’t for a chosen few, along with myself, it surely would have KILLED fans of boredom.” Leno chuckles once more at Snow’s comment, like he seems to chuckle at everything that the Killin’ Villain says... Leno: “So what are your memories of SIW?” Leno has a serious look on his face, prompting the Killin’ Villain to lean back in his seat and ponder for a moment... Snow: “General domination and the envy of all - pretty much the same as PCW.” Leno laughs hard this time, even going so far as to slap his knee. Snow stares at him, curiously, through narrow eyelids. Snow is clean shaven, something that we rarely see with him, though we do notice that he’s sporting new, fairly long, sideburns... Leno: “Ok then, lets talk about PCW.” Snow: “Lets... peasant.” Snow smirks... Leno: “Ok, well, you came into PCW through PWA, correct? Snow doesn’t answer. He simply holds a steady glare on Leno. The audience is not so quick to laugh at Snow’s comments anymore. Those cold blue eyes have an element of seriousness in them... Leno: “Ah, yeah. And then... uh... you know, I’ve watched you for years, but after looking through the file that my people dug up on you, I had no idea just how much you’ve accomplished. You were the first man to graduate PWA ever to become the PCW World Champion. You were the first Canadian PCW World Champion. AND, you are now a three time PCW Champion.” The audience claps and cheers until Snow’s voice kills their applause... Snow: “You’re forgetting, plebe, that I’m also the first, and only, SUPERIOR PCW Champion of the World. AND I have been the Superior Champion for more days than any of the previous paper champions that PCW and it’s peasant fans have tolerated!” Leno laughs, slaps his palm down on his desk and sticks out a thumb in the direction of the young PCW Champion... Leno: “This guy is great, isn’t he? He cracks me up!” The audience applauds for Snow once again, which seems to confuse the blue-eyed champ. He glares out over them through narrow eyes while Leno continues to clap with a smile above his over-sized chin... Leno: “Ok... now Snow, you’ve got a pretty big match coming up, correct?” Snow smirks... Snow: “...If one could call it that.” Leno: “Well, still, you’re fighting Tyler Nelson and Big Alan Steele at Supremecy...” Snow: “Must we get into this? If you want to know who WILL win the match, you need look no further than the title... Supremecy. Do you see any OTHER Superior Individuals in the match? I don’t! Now damn it, peasant, I DEMAND that you stop wasting my time - I’ve already wasted enough of it by gracing you with my damn presence!” Jay clears his voice and dramatically loosens his tie, prompting the audience giggle at his mock discomfort. Once more, Snow’s eyes narrow. Leno decides that maybe things really are getting a little too uncomfortable... Leno: “Well folks, we’ll be right back after these messages with our second guest of the night!” Suddenly, Snow’s eyes grow wide and he leans in, his voice raised... Snow: “Another guest!?” Leno, surprised at Snow’s response hates to turn toward him... when he finally does, he finds himself locked in the angry gaze of those cold blue eyes... Starting to sweat, he speaks... Leno: “Well, yes, Jason...” Snow interrupts... Snow: “YOU, peasant, will refer to me as your Imperial Lord and Master.” Leno sits quietly for a moment and stares at the steady-faced Killin’ Villain. Leno’s own oversized jaw is dropped a bit - he’s hoping that Snow will reveal this is all a joke. Too bad for Leno... again, he tries to explain... Leno: “You see, on this show, we have a series of guests and we all talk with each other.” Snow doesn’t take the news too well... Snow: “You don’t need any other guests, peasant! Don’t you understand the GREATNESS that oozes from my every pore!? Do you not know that each and every one of your viewers craves every moment that they see me. And THAT brings me to another point!” Snow stands up and stares directly into the camera. In the background, we can see Leno mouthing the word “Commercial!”... Snow: “All of you ladies in the audience, be your of pork or disease, I ask you to please - PLEASE - avert your eyes from the feast of genetic perfection that is my body every now and again. You see, it is well-documented that the mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!” Finally, the scene cuts as the station goes to commercial... Commercial When the scene reopens on the set of the Tonight show, Snow is sitting at the far end of the sofa with his arms folded across his chest in anger and stubborness. Leno, who seems pleased to have finally gotten the young wrestling star to calm himself, smiles as he introduces his second guest... Leno: “And now, folks, I’m pleased to introduce my second guest. He is a country western legend and one of the best songwriters of all time. Ladies and gentlemen, please give a warm welcome to WILLIE NELSON!” Snow immediately leaps to his feet... Snow: “WILLIE NELSON!!!” Jay’s eyes grow wide as saucers as Snow rushes toward the side of the stage as soon as Willie comes into sight. Luckily, a group of stage hands are able to restrain the young PCW wrestler. Nelson, with Ole Trigger (his trusty, battle-scarred, guitar) in hand, fails to notice the commotion for a moment... Willie’s notorious for his marijuana use... Snow: “Damn you Nelson! DAMN YOU! ALL YOU TEXAS NELSON’S ARE THE SAME!! DO YOU THINK I’VE FORGOTTEN YOU GRAY HAIRED OLD BASTARD!? DO YOU!? DOOOO YOOOOOU!??!” Nelson squints... Willie: “Do... Do I know you, son?” Snow is still being restrained - with great effort - by the stagehands... Snow: “Ooooohhhh,don’t play smug with me Nelson! I know how you operate! You and that bastard son of yours!” Willie looks confused... Willie: “Er... which son would that be, now? I can’t keep track of ‘em all...” Willie’s ignorance to Snow’s conspiracy theory only enrages him more. For those who don’t know the history between Jason Snow and Willie Nelson, please refer to the promo “And Willie Begat Tyler”. It seems that Snow truly believes that there is some relation between Willie and Tyler, both being Nelsons from Texas. Their last confrontation ended with Snow lying unconscious after being decked by Ole Trigger, which has not-to noticeably been given a layer of duct tape (not noticeable considering the previous condition of the guitar)... Snow: “Oh! Damn you Willie Nelson!” Snow’s face becomes misshapen with his words... Snow: “DAMN YOU STRAIGHT TO HEEELLLLLLL!!!!” Willie: “I can’t say I remember you, son.” Again, note Willie’s marijuana use... Snow: “You soon will when you have a lifetime souvenir! My Snake Skin boot lodged a foot and a half in your ASS!!” Snow momentarily breaks free of the stagehands but trips over his own Snake Skin boots and lands flat on his face when Willie wields his guitar like a baseball bat... Willie: “Bring it on, son. I’ll show ya how we tangled in the hills. Ol’ Hank showed me a thing or two about yer... wrasslin’.” Willie’s entire statement is made in a monotone type voice, void of emotion. Snow’s eyes, fearing Ole Trigger, widen with rage. A moment later, the fear and memory of what happened when Willie last struck him with the guitar fade away and the young PCW Champion charges the country music legend. Luckily for everyone, the stagehands catch Snow at the last second. Luckily for Willie because Snow would likely have torn him limb from limb. Luckily for Snow because Willie has just taken a wild swing with Ole Trigger. Luckily for Jay Leno, because he doesn’t want to deal with a lawsuit. ... Snow, restrained by the stagehands and being pulled off the stage, screams one last threat to his nemesis - his words strained and his eyes wild... Snow: “OH! Don’t think you’ll get away from me THAT easy, Willie! You won’t always have your damned guitar with you! You have to put it down sometime Nelson! And believe me, I’ll be there! Oh yes, Nelson! I’ll have my day, Willie Nelson! I’LL HAVE MY DAAAAAAAAAYYYYYYY!!!!!” With mayhem consuming the stage of the Tonight Show, and the Killin’ Villain out of sight, the scene graciously fades to black...
