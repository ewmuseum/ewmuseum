Invasion of Work... and the Arrival of a TRUE Hero!!

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-05-02 Friday Fusion...It'll be the second ever match that any member or contingent of PCG have been able to show their faces to the world.... via the screens of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling' of course... it'll be the second match that the duo have had to face in their short existance here with the company... and better still, it'll probably turn out to be their most brutal for a very long time... however, with that match resting at the back of their minds... what else could be said about the teams seemingly 'Blind Mans Walk' into a contest full of nothing but gross, horrific honey monster like animals? What else could be said about their clear stupidity to walk into the contest and attempt to win the most violent Championship in the world?! Kids are going to be watching this match... and 'Pretty Cool Guys' are supposed to be setting the example to the troubled youth of America! 'Pretty Cool Guys' are supposed to be telling my kids... telling your kids... and everyone elses kids about the dangers of playing with metal objects... and hitting people with them. Tsk, tsk... the days when you had to be squeaky clean to be able to do anything worthwhile in the music business... now you can turn out to be one of the most horrific and dangerous men on the planet... and nothing would happen to you! NOTHING AT ALL FOR GODS SAKE!!! What is this world coming to?! Why are they allowed to be doing stupid things like the above?! Why are they... ** SMACK!! ** Phew... its a good job that strange magical and invisable hand came down and hit me like it did... otherwise I, the narrator of this very talented team would have been damned to an eternal period of ranting on about how 'squeaky clean' boy bands are supposed to be... and why nailing people with steel objects is rea... ** SMACK!!! ** OK, OK... Jesus H Christ... I see the bloody point about how I'm not even allowed to say anything on the matter in all of the above information. Fine, then I'll just have to go about this in the more different way... and get along with the story that all of America (and England... and Japan... and Korea... and Wales) have bothered to tune in... to watch exclusivly. Now, for todays episode of 'Pretty Cool G', I've decided that I'm going to ditch their usual track of the 'Dragonball Z' recap music and go with something a little more interesting... you know, I'm always the one to believe that the better the intro music, the better the show is going to be in all... and are any of you morons at home going to tell me otherwise? ** SMACK!!! ** ... Now that was just plain mean... my face is all red and you keep distracting me from the work at hand... damn you and your magical invistable powers! Damn you to the eternal pit of hell... infact, I wish Rent-A-Hero was here... just so he could use his powers.. kick your ass and then leave me to deal with the show in nothing but complete and... utter peace! ... Ok... no more sense of sound from anyone... and now I'm able to get back on track with the segment that should have really kicked off a little more than a few moments ago. Anyway, we left our heroic duo of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore as they entertained a guest in the form of... Nick Carter... who was crying like a little girl over the rest of his band-mates. Rich on the otherhand, didn't really like anything that the rival Boy-Band member had to say and left the table without giving much of his condolance to the fallen Star... and THEN, when it seemed like nothing else could happen... When it seemed like the world was going to be quiet until the end of the episode, we were welcomed by a stranger standing at the house door. Who is this new character? Why the hell did Nick even bother staying until the end of the last episode... and why is Rich Sherwin being so cold to some people that he doesn't seem to like? All this will be answered along with more when you check out the following episode of... 'Pretty Cool G'... | - ^ - ^ - | Chapter 2 : The time has come... we think...Installment II : Special Guest #2?!... Hello...Bonjour...and Welcome to the forth ever episode of 'Pretty Cool G'... to let the record hang on down and hang pretty straight, I would just like to inform everybody that Rich Sherwin will not be the man doing the narrating for this episode... instead, it is I... the 'Nameless Wonder' of PCW that will be taking control of this episode... and depending on how well I do... will hopefully see me onto being the esteemed narrator for the bigger episodes of the show as well. Regardless of that little bit of information... I have found it upon myself to inform you that Rich Sherwin is nothing more than a Poser than a real player of the game... his so called 'Intellectual Advantage' doesn't exist over Moore... no way, not a chance is Rich Sherwin even better than me... hell, if I wasn't so wrapped up in everything at the moment... then maybe, just maybe I would have done something to Sherwin that'll say... 'Hey... you... NO... I'm the one with the Intellectual Advantage around here... I'm not the one who has to download Wetslife songs and think that they're the real band... oh no.' Damned idiot... 'Thank God' Sherwin decided to go out for the day... otherwise I would never have been able to even get this close to the secret call booth... because, after all... if you saw the last episode of the show, you'll understand that this confined space... filled with the 'Mystic Dark Void' of PoP... is nothing more than the shamble of shambles of Verona... Verona? Well... in either case, before I start on the show myself... watch this nice little tape of me... singing along to a song... oh, for you people that haven't put two and two together by now... I'm Ryan Moore... the better looking, better dancing... better all around talented than my friend, Rich Sherwin... its a shame to have to see... but its true, he's the talentless one of the duo... much like other famous Pop Stars... PJ & Duncan...Claire & H...Jordan X Shabazz and... whoever he was teaming with at any time... Much like PJ, Claire... and the dude who's name I can't remember... we carry our teams through the world... taking them to the top and littering the cause with nothing but Gold... ** There is a short, yet very sharp knock heard... ** Eh, what the in the world would have caused something like that to happen? People, I don't know why that knock came about... I think it has something to do with the technical aspect of things... Something to do with the 'technical' things eh? What are you doing back here Sherwin... you said you were going to be out all day long... All Day long... bah, you must have been reading the calender for last week you bloody fool... I haven't even gone out today... not get the 'F' out of my video box and let me do my frickity job! Your frickity job? Wasn't that to save a whale or something? ** We hear somebody grab into the area and then a body hit the walls a few times before seemingly being ejected from the room. Another body is heard entering the immediate area and sitting down on what sounds to be... a comfortable chair? There is a long silence from the room before we hear a human voice... ** Testing... Testing... 1,2,3... Thank God... Ryan didn't mess up too much of the data around here.... hmm, how much video tape has he used... oh my God, that bloody idiot has just screwed up on about three minutes of perfectly good video footage.... and we're on the air too... oh f*cking great... hmm, excuse me for a few moments fans, just will I tackle through all of the other minor problems... What did I do Richie? What did you do? Do NOT get me started on what you just did... I was going to broadcast the second installment of the story... now I'm going to have to start within the next few moments... just get lost... grr, sing a song or something... ** Some footsteps are heard pacing away from the immediate area before Sherwin finally starts to do the show... ** Good... a little bit of peace and quiet, just what somebody as hot as me needs when they're trying to broadcast some of the best entertainment known to man. Well, folks, the third installment slowly begins where we left off... at the front door of the very home that belongs to both Rich and Ryan.. ** A little scene changer sound players... its followed by the brief period of silence in the history of... the... WOOOORLD!! ** The first picture slowly begins to open back inside the confinements of the PCG Household. I was standing at the front door, reading to walk into the open when a rather tallish man stood infront of me. My mouth dropped open as the sheer size and build of the character captivated me... it was purely amazing... stunning amazing even.Then, just a few seconds afterwards, he stepped into the light of the room.. looking around at all the people that were currently in the home... all three of them, including me... 'Hi..', he began, continuing to alternate his look between the three of us. There was a brief period of silence before he finally continued, '... my names Goku and I'm here... looking for two boys going by the names of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore.' Ryan remained at the table, his jaw dropped to the ground in delight... for he could not believe that his 'Cartoon Hero' had made his way down from the planet of the 'unreal' and into theb world that is well... real? Infact, to be more precise about everything that was happenning, we were all pretty much taken back by the appearence of Goku. He remained standing for a few more moments, awaiting a reply.. and soon grew tired of having to get an answer. The legendary 'Saiyan Hero' looked from side to side before shaking his head a little. 'Oh well... I guess I've been se...', he tried to say before I managed to cut in. 'No.... No... Mr Goku, you're at the right house alright... we just didn't expect you... I mean, we didn't know that somebody of your status was coming around any time too soon... I mean, its almost insane to think that a Hero like yourself would come along and... help..' Goku smiled a little bit, obviously flattered by not only the nervousness that floated around me like a bad habit, but at the fact that I was taken back with his sudden appearance. Ryan Moore slowly began to move away from his chair at the table... Nick Carter also began to shift his weight from the seat a few seconds afterwards. What was going to happen here one would only be able to wonder... what was going to happen? 'You... you're.... Kakarot... or... like, Goku...', Moore began to stutter out, shaking at excitement after meeting his hero of all time. Goku was taken back a little by the apparent over-excitement of Ryan... but he didn't really let it show that badly. Nick soon walked up to Goku and extended his right hand... looking up to the Saiyan Hero, dead in the eye. Goku looked back down and nodded somewhat before moving over to the breakfast table where three bowls still remained. 'Well fellas... whats for breakfast?', Goku called... it was slowly beginning obvious that we had the right man... just not at the pivital moment in our careers. I mean, its not as if we had alot of money floating around this area... and with somebody who could eat as much as the Saiyans... well, lets just say that I'm not going to be the richest kid around the town for all that long... 'Whats for breakfast?', I asked back in a questionable tone, obvious trying to lay forth the fact that 'Breakfast' had already been served here... and that he wouldn't be able to have anything to eat unless he were willing to just go out and get it himself. Ryan Moore looked at me with a confused look on his face... it seemed as though he hadn't quite taken in what I'd said to Goku. 'Well... Rich, for breakfast we had... some cereal..' I looked over to Ryan and raised my hand.. as if I was going to swing it down and hit him with a swift bitchslap. Coming off at first glance, I didn't quite know or understand Ryan had ducked down at the very feel of an attempted blow... but still, I guess there are just some things that man was never supposed to touch. Goku turned around from his seat at the table and gazed at all three of us from his seated place. With his left hand, he pounded down on the table... breaking the weak structure into two seperate pieces.All three of us stood shell-shocked at the damage that this man had just been able to cause... he simply returned the look with a little glare that read like, 'What?'. Goku tried to place his hand down upon the table again... but fell forward, directly into the debris. 'Uh Oh... I'm really sorry guys!', he said, jumping back to his feet almost immediatly. The Saiyan Warrior begins to fiddle around with loose pieces of wood, trying desperatly to fix the table that he'd broken in such a simple fashion. We all stared at him in amazement as he scurried through the broken remains of table... trying his damnest to piece together what was left of the structure. I decided to take a step forth and inform him that there was nothing to worry about... but the Saiyan could sense my presence and quickly shot out with, 'No really... just give me five minutes and the thing will look as good as new...' ** Five blessful minutes passed us by... weeeeeeeee ** 'Ah... thats that finished guys... what do you think of it now?', Goku asked, looking at all three of us 'guys' for responces to what had just been laid forth. 'Erm... it looks... modern...', Ryan Moore said before he voice began to fade out into the darkness of never-never land. You could tell by the expression on his face that he didn't appreciate what had just been done here. 'Wow... it looks different!', Nick Carter exclaimed at the top of his voice... obviously 'down' with all that stuff about modernising your home and 'what-not'. 'Yes... it looks like the old table... just a little older than it should be looking at this moment in time...', I answered, feeling rather shocked and worried about what the rest of the house could look like if Goku is left alone for even one minute. My heart began to pound for a moment... I was a little lost in my own home... especially with the destruction of the table and all. 'So... Mr Goku... erm, what brings you here?', I asked, trying to take as much of the attention off the.. now multilated table as possible. Goku looked around the immediate area and nodded somewhat, obviously showing his intellectual admiration for the decor I had chosen in the home. There was a few moments of silence before he laid his eyes over to us 'guys' at the back and proceeded with his answer. 'Well... King Kai asked me to come on down to Earth and teach you two boys a little move called Fusion.... but you see, I'm only supposed to be here for 24hrs... and now I have 30mins left...' All three of us simply gasped at Goku when he informed us that he only had thirty minutes left on earth. How on earth could somebody like him have wasted so much time... without even coming to visit us... we were shocked... but not quite as shocked as anything we'd ever seen before. 'Ok boys.... its time for the work to begin... you have two more days to learn this whole move... I have faith in you... you're going to need it...' And with that, Goku began to power up... not realizing that the show wasn't even about him in any context. As the scene faded off into the darkness of evil... we were leave with a few questions that begged a couple of required answers.Will Goku be able to teach our heros the Fusion Dance?What the fuck did he do in those other 23hrs 30 mins?!Will Nick Carter be able to replace Piccolo as the man who teaches the Fusion Dance when Goku passes on?!All this and more... next time... on Pretty Cool G!!!
