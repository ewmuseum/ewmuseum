Let the Fun and Games Begin (part1)

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/10/03 In just a few days PCW would have it’s first ever show in the airship, which is cruising the sky as we speak. Everyone had already boarded whether they be fans, entertainment, crew, or the wrestlers themselves. Along with this were many heartfelt goodbyes as the big contraption would be in the air for many weeks, making it impossible to visit friends and family. Onto the ship everyone brought luggage and essentials, and some even reluctantly brought along their fears. How could PCW be so sure that there wouldn’t be some eerie accident that blew the airship up, or send on a non-stop flight to the ground? Those were some of the questions swimming through their minds as they chewed their nails away in fear and anticipation. Luckily one person wasn’t flawed by this fear. That person was Brianna Washington who had said her goodbyes and happily boarded, expecting only good to come of this outlandish experience. Earlier in the day before the airship had taken flight….. The hustle and bustle of the major crowd lined up (or lining up) for the PCW airship was at an all time high. If you were late you would have to face the living nightmare of fighting through the crowds, trying to grab your spot on the ship before someone else claimed it. This account held true for Brianna Washington who had been stuck in traffic on the way over. Her red Saturn pulled up in the parking lot and she got out, instantly attracted to the beautiful monstrosity. “Look at it Milo. It’s magnificent!” she buzzed merrily. “Yeah it is a sight.” Milo replied groggily stepping out of the passenger side ready to take on the daunting task of helping Brianna with her luggage. “I can’t wait!” she says giddily running to her trunk, popping it open to reveal several large brown suitcases. Milo walks over to her side placing his right hand on his khaki pants, and his left behind the collar of his light blue button up shirt. “Are you sure we can carry all that through that huge crowd? Personally I just got my nails done and if I mess them off I am going to be upset.” “I just got my nails done too and you don’t see me complaining.” Brianna replies flashing her red nails with black acrylic designs on them. “Besides I don’t expect you to carry anything. Just put it on the trolley.” With that Brianna reaches in the far corner of the trunk and pulls out a small trolley with wheels. She places it on the ground and then pulls up the handle creating more space. She then starts placing the luggage on it getting absolutely no help from Milo. “Uh excuse me?” she says irritated, staring back up at Milo. “Oh alright!” Within seconds the entire luggage is placed securely on the trolley, and they start the treacherous walk towards the crowd, which is seemingly disorderly as people are trying to get on the airship, growing highly impatient. The two stop at the back of the line due to the thickness. Brianna looks around over people’s heads, trying to find a way to get through. “Well I guess we’ll have to resort to Plan B.” Brianna whispers to Milo. “Plan B??” Brianna reveals the many buttons on the panel across the top of the trolley that makes Milo shake his head. “Seeing as how it would be pointless for me to go up there with you, I guess it’s it for a while huh?” Milo says in a shaky voice. “Yeah I guess it is….” Brianna says looking back to Milo who has a look of utter grief on his face. Brianna walks away from her luggage and towards Milo giving him a big hug. The two have never been apart more than three days since Milo had become her roommate several years ago. And the thought of not having Brianna around for guidance, and to drive him insane, disheartens Milo a great deal. “Promise me that you’ll kick major ass while you’re up there Brianna.” Milo says breaking away from the hug realizing she has to get going. “I’ll try my best.” She replies giving Milo a huge ear to ear smile. The two hug one last time and Milo goes back to the red Saturn, taking off, waving to Brianna as he disappears. Brianna presses an oval shaped black button on the trolley and a tray looking thing shoots out from the bottom with boosters in the back. She presses another button and the boosters come to life. One more button is pressed and the trolley starts to float in the air. Brianna then directs it over the top of the crowd, which freaks a lot of people out, not sure if it’s going to crash on top of them. Brianna manages to land precisely on the pad leading into the airship where she is greeted by a man in a black tuxedo, with a clipboard in hand. “Ms. Brianna Washington, nice to see you make it.” The man says checking her name off the list. “Here’s the key to your room. You’ll find the room number on the key.” “Thank you.” “I hope you enjoy your stay on the PCW Airship.” He says quickly ready to sign in the next person. As Brianna enters the airship, the scene fades to black. To be Continued……
