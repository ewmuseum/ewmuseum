Let the Game Begin.

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-6-03 Religions. There are literally thousands of them in the world, each with thousands upon thousands of followers that give them strength. Millions of people base their entire lives around their religion, even going as far as killing in the name of such a thing. One must wonder what has made religion so powerful. Often times it is the basis of wars, murders, profiling and the list just goes on and on. While the whole thing does have its downsides, it does have some pros. Religion stands as a beacon of light for those who are troubled. It gives people a sense of belonging and something to believe in. For one Dominion, he found the whole thing to be blasphemy. That was his own personal opinion and while he did hold that view, he had nothing against anyone else’s views. What other people believed in he respected and all he asked for in return was the same respect given to his views. While Dominion never held anything against anyone else who had a certain belief, those same people who cry about being accepted are often the ones who condemn Dominion for his opinions. The truth is, Dominion was a man who needed to see things with his own eyes in order to believe them. He was also a man who believed in science and through detailed research, the explanation of life given by science greatly outweighed the explanation given by most of the dominant religions. Thus, given the wars and the deaths related to religion, Dominion opted to believe in none of it. The whole idea was nothing to him. He saw no need for such a thing in his life. He believed in himself and that was the end of it. However, there were cases when one’s beliefs proved beneficial to Dominion. He was a man who would go to any lengths to complete his tasks and if that meant using one’s religion against him, what had to be done indeed had to be done. ********** “Just watch for her.” Wolfson commanded, speaking into the cell phone he held to his ear. “I don’t care, Eve. You said you didn’t have a problem with this. When she steps out of the building, do your thing.” [ Wolfson paused, leaning back on the wall of the building he stood just outside of. ] “Hell if I know.” Wolfson continued. “He said he’s flying her in today. When she steps out, take care of her. We’ve got to send a couple messages to this guy.” [ Pausing again, Wolfson brushes off the exterior of his black leather coat. ] “I’m in place.” Wolfson answers. “I’m about to do my part now. Just remember. This female is trippin’ right now. She’s all wigged out so just scare her. That’s all ya gotta do. And besides, this wanker is so wrapped up in his stupid delusions he’s leaving himself wide open.” [ Wolfson pauses yet again, allowing the party on the other end, no doubt his sister Eve, to reply. ] “Ok, good.” He replies. “Handle your business and I’ll see you later.” [ Wolfson ends the connection and slides the phone into his jacket pocket. Turning around, he takes a step back in order to take a better look at the cathedral he’s in front of. ] “Your weaknesses are my strengths.” Wolfson whispered to himself. “And I’m only getting stronger.” [ Wolfson pulled the large wooden door open and stepped inside. The light emitting from the various flames throughout bounced off his skin, giving his body a tone to suit the colors of his surroundings. Wolfson headed straight for the altar at the front of the large room. A beautiful statue stood at the helm and the detail was almost overwhelming. Raising his shades up to his forehead, Wolfson gazed upon the statue intently. ] “En’Flarim.” Wolfson whispered, lowering his head. “I come to you today humbly begging for your guidance. An enormous task lies in the immediate future. I am but a shell of the man I must be to accomplish said task.” [ A silence ensued, as Wolfson nodded his head slowly. ] “I understand, my goddess.” Wolfson continued. “Your wisdom is priceless. I sacrifice my life to it. Why being is in your hands, grand deity. Deliver me from my world of despair and delusion to the grand utopia that is your path. Replenish my exhausted soul.” [ Wolfson pauses, slowly looking up at the statue in front of him. ] “As I gaze upon your immense beauty…” Wolfson continued. “I see myself as I wish to be. I see the powerful force that resides inside me and I beg of you, grand deity, allow your great wisdom to consume me. EMPOWER ME!” [ Suddenly, Wolfson’s arms are thrown out to his sides and his chest shoots forward. He begins shaking violently, rising higher and higher until he’s standing on his toes. Finally, he drops to the floor, kneeling before the statue. ] “Oh powerful and grand En’Flarim.” Wolfson said, looking upward. “I feel…” [ He closed his eyes for a moment. ] “STUPID!” Wolfson shouted, hopping to his feet. “And by the way, get a new dress. You look like a fuckin’ slut in that thing.” [ Wolfson laughed to himself for a moment, staring up at the statue. ] “What?” Wolfson asked, cocking his head. “Kill everyone, you say?” [ Wolfson chuckles to himself as he starts to pace back and forth. ] “Y’know, Flarmie…” Wolfson starts, waving his index finger. “You’ve been a very bad girl. There was absolutely no reason to attack Jessica the way you did. Maybe there is more to that look that you got goin’ on there. Ya know, the whole slut thing. It’s almost as if you’re jealous of Jessica. Do you want Dorn all to yourself? It’s that little fact that he’s the only one who sees you, isn’t it?” [ Wolfson slowly shakes his head, stepping up onto the altar where the statue stands. Placing his arm around the statue’s neck, he leans on it. ] “Dorn’s a weird cat.” Wolfson says, nodding. “Our illustrious Shooting Star Champion, eh? I’ve got to admit, though, you do have quite a hold on him. His whole world is revolving around you. That kind of devotion doesn’t happen over night. You must have had your dirty little mitts in his life for quite some time. For that, Flarmie, I gotta take my hat off. You’re almost as good at the whole manipulation thing as I am.” [ Removing his arm from the statue’s shoulders, Wolfson takes a step back and claps a few times. ] “What’s that you say?” Wolfson asks, shooting his head backward. “You’re only making him better and stronger?? That’s insane. Look at the guy. I mean, just the sight of you makes him drop to his knees. He’s not even brave enough to look at you. That’s not right, Flarmie. Not right at all. You’re making the man weak. Very weak. If he cowers in front of you, what’s to say he won’t start cowering in front of me? What can you really take from him? You’ve got nothing to threaten him with. Whereas I could take many things from that young man. His ability to walk, for one.” [ Shrugging his shoulders, Wolfson begins to slowly circle the statue. ] “You’re not so tough.” Wolfson says, looking the statue up and down. “I’m not even godly and I could whip your ass. But that’s not what I’m about. I’m just here to talk. Real pity you’re not talking back, by the way. I mean, you talk to Dorn, don’t you? With all your commands, your challenges, your divine wisdom. He confides in you, you know.” [ Stopping just in front of the statue again, Wolfson runs his hand down the bottom half of his face, smoothing out his goatee. ] “You’re his strength.” Wolfson admits. “Does it make you feel good to know that you’re all the man has in his life? So much so that you’ve got to violently attack any female that comes close to him? If you ask me, I think you’re the one who needs the guidance, Flarmie. Let me guess. Now stop me if I’m wrong, ok?” [ Wolfson takes a step back, resting his chin in his hand. ] “Your freshman, no, sophomore year of college.” Wolfson continues. “The biggest sorority party of the year. You get real drunk and just because the guy says he loves you, you put out. You never hear from him again. Ever since, you’ve had this need to be controlling. Not only that, but since our good buddy Dorn is the first guy since to take some interest in you, you will defend that interest at any cost. Am I right?” [ Wolfson looks up at the statue’s face, nodding. ] “I thought so.” He says. “Well ya know what? I think you’re a sick, twisted freak, dig? That’s right, I think you’re psycho. And you know what I think of you? Do you really want to know?” [ Shrugging, Wolfson spits in the statue’s face. ] “That’s what I think of you and everything you stand for.” Wolfson demands. “So let that be a lesson to you.” [ Wolfson begins laughing to himself, shaking his head. ] “Ok.” He starts. “And that’s that. Do you see how stupid you look, Dorn? I mean, I’m sorry player, but the truth is…” [ Wolfson taps his head with his index finger. ] “It’s all in here.” Wolfson continues. “Look at this thing, man. It’s a friggin’ statue. The bitch ain’t real. But y’know, now that I think about it, maybe she is, eh? Maybe I’m just not in touch with the forces enough to see her in her true form. Maybe she’s kicking me in the ass right now and I just don’t feel it.” [ Wolfson shrugs, making his way around to the back of the statue. Once there, he leans up against the statue, resting on his elbow. ] “But seriously, Dorn.” Wolfson continues. “There’s nothing wrong with having a belief like this. It’s just that you’re taking it a bit far. I’m trying to help you; I hope you can see that. The truth is, you’re my game, Dorn. You’re my entertainment. I don’t want you to freak out and lose your mind on me. After all, if I lose you, what do I have left in Primetime? Who’s strings am I going to pull? Honestly, I kinda like you, son. You’re out there. I like that. Hell, my sister thinks you’re hot. You got a lot goin’ for you. But don’t worry, Dorn. I’m going to help you. I’m going to make everything better.” [ Wolfson removes his weight from the statue. He leans forward, then leans backward with a little bit of force, forcing the statue to tip forward a little. However it manages to stand straight again. ] “Look, Dorn.” Wolfson says, looking up at the statue. “One push and it can all go away. All those demons in your head. I can make all of them go away. No more will this thing be a burden on your shoulders. No longer will your head pound with worries of not doing right by En’Flarim. You can once again join us, Dorn. Once again live in a world of sanity.” [ Leaning against the wall just behind the statue, Wolfson begins to push it forward, closer to the edge of the altar. ] “By the way, Dorn.” Wolfson continues. “I know what happened with Jessica. I don’t know if it was actually you in her building, or if you have someone working with you that was there, but that was not En’Flarim. Someone was in her boiler room and they cranked her heat up. Doesn’t she matter to you at all? You’d cause so much mental anguish and pain to that girl over this? Well I wouldn’t worry about her anymore. Eve is at the airport awaiting her arrival. She’ll take care of Jessica for you.” [ Again, Wolfson pushes the statue a little further. ] “Consider all of this a wake-up call of sorts, Dorn.” Wolfson commands. “We’re going to make you focus on what’s important in life. I’m eager to see how you respond to what’s about to happen to Jessica. Your good friend. Although I suppose I shouldn’t wonder. I mean, you were able to sit by and watch as Jessica passed out to that heat. So maybe you won’t care. Either way, we’re going to find out. You’re my project, Dorn.” [ Finally, Wolfson slams his body up against the statue, forcing it to slip off the short ledge and hit the floor. Upon doing so, it shatters into pieces. ] “Oops.” [ Hopping down to the floor, Wolfson reaches down and picks up a piece of what was once the statue’s face. He seats himself on the ledge, looking it over. ] “Not so much in the strength department, eh?” Wolfson asks in a rhetorical fashion. “So what now, Dorn? Are you going to become a man and fight? Or are you going to break down without your source of strength here? It’s gone now. Not coming back, you hear me?” [ Tossing the piece of statue over his shoulder, Wolfson stands back up and dusts himself off. ] “Friday Fusion.” Wolfson begins. “You’ve got me, Cozette du Pres and Jade breathing down your neck. You better get your shit straight, son, or that shiny Shooting Star title will be no more. It’ll revert back to it’s Rising Star form and then you’ll have to live with the fact that you lost it because of your own inability to rationalize. And what if I’m the man to take it to you? I sure as hell plan on doing my best to do that. I mean, sure, you are a game to me, but I play to win, dig? And what then? Can you really stomach seeing that title around my waist? The very title you have taken so much pride in. The very title that you revolutionized. All of that can go down the drain tomorrow night. Can you handle that?” [ Wolfson takes a moment to survey the damage on the floor before beginning his walk toward the exit. ] “We’re going to find everything out tomorrow night.” He continues. “And believe me, I’m going to do what I can to open your eyes. This game isn’t challenging me like it should. If knocking you upside your dome piece, and trust me, I’m gonna hit you pretty hard, will make you wake up, I’m going to do what I gotta do. It really makes no difference to me. So go ahead, Dorn. Get man. Get real mad that I just took the single more important thing in your life. Get real pissed off. That way it’ll be even easier to take the second most important thing from you. I smell that Rising Star Championship. Four corners? May god help you.” [ With that, Wolfson shoves the door open and steps outside again. Our cameras fade to black. ]
