The Hell That is Boda-Land - Part Two - Robots and Everything Automatic equals Lethal Toasters?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5 April OOC Note – Meh, you’ve probably already guessed by now, that this roleplay is referenced, although only in the slightest amount, to MGB’s “Everything is Automatic”. Download and listen to at will…it’s a fairly good song. The next promo after this will see the return of the Flashdance series…and then the next two or three will conclude this series The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals…… RP#4 for Spring Fling… Location: The Outer Circle of Boda-Land, in the depths of Chris Chase’s mind, currently in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Time: Unknown, probably an early morning on April 4th. Chapter Seven: The Hell that is Boda-Land – Part Two – Robotics and Everything Automatic equals Lethal Toasters? (Mental note – Never Drink Carling whilst Roleplaying, especially after the fifth can… A mildly wacky alternative…) “~(Mechanical Whirring)~ Once again, for the eighteenth time, welcome to Boda-Land, the Celebratory Theme Park for the whole family. You are now in the outer circle of the park, home to the “Laker’s Paddle Boats”, the Miyaga-Coaster and the Boda-go-Round. Pay no attention to the androids that supervise your movements around the park – this is simply to ensure that no rogue thugs attempt to dethrone the men responsible for this park. After all, your enjoyment is our secondary objective; after satisfying our own selfish needs. That is all. Enjoy your stay here in Boda-Land…” “Dustin! Did you really have to follow me out here? I mean, it’s all well and good that you’ve already lost our means of contact with the operative…but this is just taking the proverbial piss! With your chicken suit and all…we’re bound to get captured just for looking stupid, which got us both into this whole mess in the first place. Where’d Serenity go, perchance? I thought she’d at least stay in contact with us if her goal is the same as ours…” “Don’t worry Chris, I mean, what chance do these stupid androids have of capturing her…and us, if they’re all offline. See, that one’s slumped over a garbage can, and that one’s got its head stuck in a ticket office. Heh, it’s funny that they all look like either Miyagawa or Boda in some way, don’t you think? Do you think that Chase meant this to be like this?” “Of course Chase did! It’s his dream isn’t it? I mean, it’s easy to tell just how much pent up frustration that the dude has about these two men, these two buffoons that are hanging on for dear life, trying to keep his actions to a very strict minimum. Do you have any idea of what that feels like? Well, of course you do…you work for the nimrods, and so does yours truly. At least he’s getting the job done for us…project C-C-S is going better than anyone could ever have hoped. Our colleagues will soon be rising against our oppressors en masse, and there’s nothing that Boda-Boy can do about it!” “So they’re all ready then?” “Of course not! There won’t be any need for them to be ready if this ‘Chris Chase’ gets the job done on his own…” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ Nothing in this life can be taken for granted. One miscalculation and it can all blow up in your face…especially in this modern world of bombs, explosives, automatic weaponry, dipshits and Nimrods. Boda and Miyaga-jobber have misread the fine print…they’ve underestimated the most underrated person in the history of the PCW promotion. They seem more focused in chasing fictional stories, chasing that golden wild goose…that wild goose that’ll eventually turn around, peck their faces out…before vanishing into a cloud of smoke. Their mistakes are nothing but trivial…the typical trite you’d expect from idiots of their calibur. Heck, as wrestlers…they’re just one sandwich short of a picnic…but in social terms, the picnic’s been washed out by a storm…and Boda somehow managed to drop the basket into a river. Talking about dropping the ball… And slapping some sense into them is going to take a little more effort than I’d first thought. You’d think that would have occurred as if it were some automated machine, you throw an insult…they change to whatever effect. But no, these people have the reactions of a snail stuck in a pot of glue whilst smoking some MJ…that’s right, slower than a slow thing…the slothenly creatures that these guys are. And let’s face it, they obviously aren’t going to change their ways…unless I’m somehow mistaken. Last year’s Boda was pretty much the same Boda that we have now, a useless, slow, wholesomely irritating moron…only younger – that’s the extent of it all…and some people never change… Last time in Boda-Land, Chris Andrews and Dustin Downey had discussed the plot that would eventually overthrow Miyagi-ha and Boda from their pedestal as rulers of the World, whilst I make my way past a horde of robotic soldiers, and through into the next stage of my battle… Tomorrow has arrived early. The Outer Circle is being lit up by the aura of the rising sun. It’s 6 o’ clock in the morning, and there’s no sign of my presence in this area from last night…other than the corpses of robotic soldiers,strewn all over the area. Visitors to the park trickle through gradually, as the PA system greets them for the eighteenth time this morning. Chris Andrews strolls along the concrete, littered path…looking around in some caution…as his pal ‘Dipshit’ Dustin Downey follows, ala the real Dustin Downey to yours truly in the real world. There is a half-hearted confident grin on Andrew’s face…an effect of “The Lack of Chris Chase” syndrome. Without Chris Andrews becoming Chris Chase, he never became that awesomely talented and cocky wrestler that he was destined to become...and wound up under Boda’s rule after a chance meeting with Dustin Downey. The two got thrown into hard labour for violating one inane law, and have been plotting their escape ever since….:: Dustin: So, are you still mad at me for losing the radio unit? I mean, I honestly thought it belonged in the corndog shack, and when I realised that it didn’t...it had vanished off the face of this earth. Sheesh, this chicken suit is getting a little unwieldy… ::. Dustin is still dressed in that chicken suit from the previous day, still walking around without a head to disguise himself. Not like the disguise would work…after all, wearing a badge saying “El Pollo Dustino” isn’t exactly going to make him less obvious. He halts for a second, feeling around his back…trying to free himself from the chicken suit. But obviously to no avail, as he shouts for Andrews, who had continued walking for the next 200 yards….:: Dustin: Alright…let’s see if I can get out of this damned thing. ~(Struggles)~ Oh bugger…I seem to have gotten myself trapped in this darn thing. Damn Chris Chase for dreaming up this little scenario…I suppose that it’s revenge for the whole incident with the tar and feathers…and explaining to all those angry Mexicans that I wasn’t really El Pollo Diablo. CHRIS! CHRIS! A little help here! ::. Andrews throws his arms up in frustration, and turns around with an exasperated frown on his face…back tracking the 250 yards or so that he’d blatantly walked, ignoring the mumblings of his co-worker. He flicks his medium length brown hair in exasperation, as he steps up to Dustin…and strikes him with a sharp backhand, which sends Dustin to the floor, and the zip into oblivion. Pulling himself up from underneath the rumble of the suit, Dustin dusts himself down…admiring the robotic carnage lying around him, whilst also rubbing his forehead from the fierce backhand from his would-be former Tag Partner. Andrews sighs, also surveying the carnage with a little admiration, smiling at the sight of yet more android replicas of Boda and Miyawaga lying in state over the desolate park terrain….:: Chris (Andrews): I’ve gotta hand it to this Chris Chase, he certainly knows how to take on the scourge of employees everywhere…these androids belonging to the Boda-Miyagawa coalition. And this wanton destruction is fresh…so Chris must be nearby…don’t you think Dustin? Dustin: Come again? Think? Of course I don’t think…what are you, a moron or something? Well…it’s too bad for you mister, for you see…I’m THE ONLY MORON HERE, Damnit! What good is it that there’s more than one moronic person in any one of these skits? As the folks down in the kitchen say Chris, “Too many cooks spoil the broth” and too many morons spoil the wacky skit… Chris (Andrews): Wacky? How on earth is this ‘Wacky’? Are you completely wack, Dustin? Besides you and your silly gestures, there’s nothing making this anything more than humorous…for it to be wacky, you’d need to have at least one person shot out of a cannon, three clowns…two midgets and a Dustin Downey in a pear tree. Get out of that tree Dustin, that’s a freakin’ Banana Tree. Yes, the Banana…the typical diet of Chris Sloboda…hence the widespread existence of Banana trees in the Boda-Land theme park…and the main reason that middle aged women are commonly assaulted by Spider Monkeys on every given day… ::.~(Slaps forehead)~ Now that one tenth of my audience has been thoroughly ushered away by the eternal wackyness of the High Impact duo that never was High Impact, due to the fact that they’re fans of the Vampir Nosferatu style symbolism occasionally shown in the likes of these promotions…we swiftly move on to the Inner circle…the next stage in the hunt for Boda and Miya-jobber. Unwittingly being followed by the apparitions of what might have been, I lean against a park bench…watching punters queue to their doom in the “Chamber of Utter Boredom O’ DOOM~!”. The sheer blindness of these cretins astounds me, why, they’re no worse that the men that are torturing them…exposing them to horrors beyond comprehension of the average man – their Promos… The things I’ve seen, the things that I’ve experienced in the past year in this industry will astound and amaze you…the great freak show of a history, stretching right back to the beginning of my turbulent love affair with the federation…those flirtatious times as the young hero, put against significant odds…created from the hopes and fears of two nations that needed someone to look up to. ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase was and is my name…although the meaning may have got lost around the time that I got crucified. I’m not the living recreation of Jesus Christ, I’m not even a distant descendant of the original Messiah. I’m Jewish whilst being Christian…I’m basically a rather confused and mixed up man…although not to the extent of my overly idiotic former Tag Team partner… The Inner circle is more of a ghost town rather than a densely populated Inner City area. Not many visitors ever make it into the Inner Circle, those that do have either got a high tolerance of things related to Boda and his ass-bandit of a lackey, or they’ve just been lucky. Thankfully for me, I have both traits…in spades I might add. After taking care of the first wave of sentries that blocked my passage into the Inner Circle…mainly due to the fact that I don’t actually have any proof of how I got in here, I’m faced with my second enemy…a more formidable opponent, and in droves. The central core of Boda-Land is protected by an electric sea…a stream of high voltage electricity that fizzes and crackles loudly behind me. Before I can even get near it…there’s a little matter of the Rabid Toaster Piranhas. The time’s 9 AM…and as I’m pondering to myself…there’s a loud yell from behind me….:: Voice: Chase! Thank my lord Chris Chase in heaven that I managed to catch up with you. First of all, I’ve lost all contact that I had with you…and due to Dustin…we accidentally awakened half of the robotic sentries…the damn idiot. Other Voice: Well…it’s just our luck that two ‘Cluster’ Midget bombs were fired in our direction from a cannon on the veranda…taking out whatever remnants there were left of those freakin’ robots…~(grunts angrily)~ Despite that…I HATE MIDGETS! ::.Somehow in the infernal laws of the Universe and Chris Chase promos, I had been caught up by the slaves of Boda-Land, Chris Andrews and Dustin Downey…of course, I don’t really need to explain. Jeez…you’d think that I’d at least have some liberty to destroy Boda and Miyagaaaaaaaawa on my own, wouldn’t you? And no cocky little asshole and his moronic sidekick is going to stop me, mwahahahahahaha! ~(Pause)~ Ok…shoot me if I begin to sound like Boda again, will you? .:: Chris: What in the blue flaming pits of Wisconsin are you doing here? Didn’t you ask for me to be the lone operative, the guy that would singlehandedly bring down to size the reign of Boda and Miya-jobber…after all, it’s my frickin’ dream for MY sake! Chris (Andrews): Funny, I was thinking the same thing…why did we have to come all the way here, when it’s your dream after all. I mean, who could have caused this? ::. The two of us glare at Dustin, who at first seems oblivious to us, but upon meeting our icy glares…he recoils, trips over a rope and sends an approaching horde of Rabid Toaster Piranhas circling in a parabola into a trash compactor. He gets up, and looks at us with a befuddled stare….:: Dustin: What? Why are you looking at me in that way? After all…I did just save us all…yeah, the funny comic addition saves the day once again. Don’t tell me that there’s another swarm of Rabid Toaster Piranhas…sheesh…why’d Boda have to go to the liberty of making so many automated defenses? What? ::.To Be Continued…Fade to White!.::
