A Forgotten Gift

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-28-03 Chris grabbed the boxes from the hallway and threw them on his shoulder. The last boxes to be unpacked was his. Chris started to walk down the hall when suddenly he stopped. He turned back and looked at Tessa. 'Uh Tessa, where do I unpack this stuff?' he asked her. A little confused. They completely forgot to resolve the issue on where Chris will sleep. He held the boxes in one hand and scratches his head. Tessa bites her bottom lip. She looks upstairs to where the kids are. Shouts of their fight still. She looked back at Chris. 'Well, I am not sure. What do you want to do?' she asked him back. Hoping to relieve herself of the pressure of answering it. Chris smiled, knowing what she was doing. Tessa hated to hurt anyone feelings. It was in her nature. It was also one of the reasons Chris loved her so much. Chris put the box down and walked up to her. He wrapped his arms around her. Tessa put her head on his chest. 'I tell you what Tessa. We will just share a room and Evan and Alex can have their own room. How does that sound?' he told her. 'That sounds perfect.' she purred. Chris released his grip and walks back to the boxes. He scooped them up and carried them to Tessa's room, which was now his room also. Tessa followed in behind him. Chris looks around for a place to drop them and Tessa simply points to the closet area. Chris drops them off and kneels down in front of them. Different boxes are shattered about in the room. All of them have Tessa's name wrote in black marker. Tessa just turned and flopped down on the bed backwards. She let out a sigh as she hit. Glad the hard stuff was out of the way. Chris got up and looked over at her. She was laying on the bed with her arms out. Chris smiled to her and crawled into the bed, laying down next to her. Chris rested his head onto her arm. He pulled his head up next to her's and started to rub his cheek against her's. 'Tessa, you don't have a problem sharing a room with me do you?' he asked her. Tessa rolls over and looked at him. Chris sat up on his side, they stared into each other's eyes. 'Why would I have a problem with it?' came her reply. She had a confused look to her as she said it. Chris wasn't really expecting that answer. He simply let it off with a laugh. Chris rolled over and kissed her, then climbed out of the bed. He kneeled back in front of the boxes. Chris pulls out his pocket knife and slices open the box. As soon as he pulls back the flaps on the box he started to pull out items in the box. He came across another box inside the brown box. Chris looked at it with a curious look on his face. 'Tessa Bear, come here for a second.' he called out. Tessa walked over to see what the matter was. Chris was now sitting down by the boxes, holding the new one he found. 'Uh I found this. Here you might want to have a look at it.' Chris hands her the box. Tessa studies the box for a second, making sure it might not be a trap of some kind. Soon, however, her curiosity overcomes her and she pops open the top of the box and looks in. Her eyes light up with joy as she pulls out a large, brown, teddy bear. In it's arms was a box. Tessa didn't even seem to notice the box as she gave the teddy bear a huge hug. She turned her head and looked at Chris. 'Thank you so much! This is wonderful.' Chris merely smiled. 'It seems your new friend has something for you.' he said while slightly laughing. Tessa released the hug and looks at the bear again. She finally noticed the box in it's arms. She put her hand on it to open it, her hand shaking. The box slowly opens as Tessa pulls out to small pieces of paper. “What’s this?” she asked him. Chris only smiled and looked on. Tessa’s eyes turn back to the paper. Her jaw almost hits the ground as she realizes what it is. She simply mouths, “Oh my god...” Chris’ smile gets bigger and brighter as he watches her reaction. Tessa jumps up, dropping her teddy bear. She spins and twirls around the room knocking over boxes and different items. Tessa turns her back to Chris and falls back. He lungs forward and catches her, letting her lay on his lap and chest. She wraps her arms around his neck and looks up at him. The paper still in her hand behind Chris’ head. A large grin on Tessa’s face as she stares up at him. The sounds of small footsteps stampede down to see what was causing all the noise. The room is soon filled with everyone in the house, the children standing in the doorway. “What is going on?” asked the littlest Evan. Tessa turned her head and looked at all of them. She pulled the hand holding the paper away from Chris and held it up for the children to see. “Chris and I are going to Paris!” she yelled with glee. As soon as the words escaped her lips and was already hugging him again. Tessa’s joy was short lived as Christ chimed in, “But what about us?”. The room went silent.
