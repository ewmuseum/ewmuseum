Starbucks and Wristlocks: The Return of the Goggles

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd August 2002 Ominous, the disastrous. The enigmatic. None of these things have been called of, to Miyagawa. You stupid prat has been used on several occasions, and “champion” on even more, but nothing distinctive… nothing unique. And this is where we join the man… the legend… and Alexander Miyagawa too, sitting next to Jeremy Johnson outside a Starbucks somewhere in Anaheim. Or at least, it looks like Anaheim… must be the one in three tourists wearing a Disney t-shirt or something. Oh, and the one in ten people still deluded to the fact that they think that the Mighty Ducks are actually a good team and will defeat the St. Louis Blues next season… to which we all know they won’t. Johnson sits there drinking some sort of chilled coffee… you know the ones with like the bubble caps and the straws. For some reason he is wearing his PCW Ref’s outfit whilst sitting there. Miyagawa on the other hand, is wearing a black shirt with an A in a circle in white on it with the sleeves cut off, showing off his new muscles that he’s been developing over the last four weeks since his time off from PCW. A young lady walks up to the table with a pen and a notepad. Young Lady: Can I get your autograph? Looks at the paper and reaches out to take the pen. Miyagawa: Sure thing. Who do you want me to sign it to? The young lady pulls the pen and paper back out of Miyagawa’s reach to the surprise of JJ and Miyagawa. Young Lady: And who are you? I wanted JJ’s autograph… he’s a ref for PCW, don’cha know? Miyagawa raises and eyebrow in surprise at this as Jeremy happily signs the note to “Sandy” who is apparently this lady’s kid sister, but I think we can all confirm that it’s probably her name anyway, but she’s one of the 85% of JJ fans who simply refuse to admit it. Miyagawa: But… but… don’t you know who I am? The person who we expect is this “Sandy” character looks at Miyagawa and shakes her head quickly. Sandy: Nope. Are you one of the stage crew… OH WAIT! I know! A look of relief appears on Miyagawa’s face as she says that at last. Miyagawa: Good. Now who do you want it signed to? Sandy looks surprised at him still suggesting to sign the paper. Sandy: Why would I want JJ’s boyfriend to sign this piece of paper, I don’t even know your name and I doubt my kid sister would know who you are either. Miyagawa and JJ go completely silent at the table looking straight at “Sandy”. Miyagawa: Heh! I’m not JJ’s Boyfriend! I’m former PCW World Champion Alexander Miyagawa! Sandy looks at him some more. Sandy: You can’t be. He wears goggles. You aren’t wearing them. Miyagawa: I haven’t worn the goggles since November for god’s sake! I don’t wear them anymore because people cheered the goggles more than they cheered me! Sandy: Sheesh, no need to shout. Sandy turns around and walks away from the table, at which point JJ finally realises something. JJ: Hey! I’m not gay! They sit there in silence for a few minutes. Miyagawa carefully opens another packet of sugar from the table and pours it into his previously untouched coffee before stirring it with one of those long wooden stick things you get in Starbucks for some reason. Miyagawa: Goggles. Will I never escape them… damn the goggles. JJ: Being gay… will I never escape it… I like women… I even picked up chicks at Kannon’s bachelor party, but still… I get accused of being gay. Life isn’t fair. Miyagawa looks up from his coffee and looks over at JJ. Miyagawa: There’s only one thing for it. Give the people exactly what they want. JJ’s face turns completely white. And you know exactly how red his face is normally, so as you guess; this is a pretty drastic change underway here. Then suddenly it turns all the way back, past it’s previous red and into a deep crimson. He stands up from the table on the sidewalk and slams his fists against the metal table. JJ: I AM NOT BECOMING GAY FOR ANYONE!!!!!!!!!!!!! Miyagawa looks up at him calmly. Miyagawa: Who said anything about you becoming gay? Come on, we have to go shopping. JJ glares at him some more. JJ: I AM NOT WEARING BONDAGE GEAR FOR ANYONE!!!!!!! Miyagawa continues to look calmly up at him, takes a last sip of his coffee and leaves it wear it sits before standing and pushing his chair under the table. Miyagawa: Goggles, moron, goggles. We have to go shopping for goggles. If that’s what the people want, then that is what the people shall receive.
