Wait, Blind People Were Castrated?

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.12.02 […The scene pops up in the middle of the mind of Dante Jackson. Of course from the last time we brought something to you, our local dimwit happened to be stuck in somewhat of a mind-lapse. He wasn’t totally himself as a black sky hangs over Jackson’s mind at the moment. Light sky clouds happen to form as not only Abraham Lincoln but River Phoenix as well. Odd as it seems, these are his guides in the mind. As you may remember, River Phoenix is well - River Phoenix when he overdosed and Abraham Lincoln is in his normal attire of bow tie and black suit with stove top hat…] […Screams are heard overhead as Dante ducks and weaves out of harms way - knowing that he can’t be touched as long as he believes as the vision isn’t real. Of course he should believe that it is real and that he is just out of his mind right now. For now they are on the yellow brick road showing nothing but moments of the Rush jobbing and being nailed by other people’s finishers…] Dante: So this is the Rush Road of Failure? Thought it’d be… Abe: Longer? River: *hic* He probably frickin’ thought the frickin’ crap would be a mile and two vaginas long! *hic*. [...Jackson sighs at the contemporary party continues to walk down Rush Mason's memory lane. Looking downward, they can see new photos forming of how Rush would be beaten by Dante Jackson for the pinfall...] Dante: Erm, is this me or a bad hallucination? Abe: Call it what you want, but all you know right now is that there is no such thing as ghost. Dante: That's from Scooby Doo and you know it! River: *hic* Mother freaker probably jerked off to it again... Abe: SHUT UP! Dante: That's swell fellas, but I still don't know just why you have me down this here road. [...Abe sighs towards the relevant idoicy of Jackson as they continue downward, catching even the missed episodes of Friends where Rush was a 'so called' special guest. The Greatest Man Alive is now shown jobbing to the guy who jobbed to H.G. Wells, and then Chris Knight, and then getting his ass kicked by the H.G. Wells guy...] [...Abe fluffs his long beard as River takes it with him its time for a joint. He takes a FAT one out of his pocket and blazes it up, causing on the outside for Dante to start coughing. Abe whips around, looking directly into River who then stops, a cynical look on his face...] Abe: And just what the hell are you doing there? River: Blazing up, why does that ALWAYS upset you? [...Abe sighs...] Abe: I just don't like the fact that you're smoking in front of me, I have feelings too you know! Dante: For the love of crap on a bamboo stick you're a fucking spiritual guide, you don't have ANY FEELINGS FOR ANYTHING! [..Begin odd stare period here...] Abe: Oh. [...Continuing down the road, the group happens to take in some of the sights around them. Such as the DD Cup Breast for a Sun, and the large Foam Hand for a moon and dick slap. Okay, it couldn't be called a dick smack because Trevor Hawke owns that term. Sucks to be him, but its all good in the end. Soon, 'Why Do Birds Suddenly Appear' by The Carpenters plays overhead as a loud KAW! sound can be heard from down the road...] Dante: This apart of the trip? Abe: Yeah, before we get to Spongebob Land, we have to go through him... Dante: Who's him? Janet Reno? Sissy Spacek, OPRAH?! River: No, Janet is on ass fuck lane, and Sissy happens to be on I Don't Give A Fuck About Your Dumb Movies Boulevard. Oprah is sitting in a bucket of KFC right now, so I don't think she's coming out. Abe: Last time she did, when you put her on a feeding frenzy and ate nearly everybody of Jobberville in the process! Large Freaking Bird With Huge Googly Eyes: KAW! I'MA FREAKING BIRD FROM THE MOVIE 'THE BIRDS!' RICHARD NIXON WAS MY BABY MOMMAS SECOND COUSINS FIRST UNCLES SISTER! NONE OF YOU FREAKS ARE GOING TO GET PAST ME L.F.B.W.H.G.E! OR YOU'LL HAVE YOUR PENIS PECKED OFF! [...Dante shrugs, before the bird hops in front of the traveling three ring circus. A smile creeps on Dante's face, as he launches himself into a karate pose. The bird does the same as the scenery behind them changes into a modernized Kung Fu dojo! A odd expression comes on Abe's face as it looks as if he's sick to his stomach...] Abe: Why does he always cause trouble? River: *hic* He's a damn canary who hit his growth spurt and dyed himself black. You're point *hic* being? Abe: No, you don't get it - doesn't The End happen to do things like this? River: I dunno *hic*, answer me why the hell is a camera right there on the other side of the room, will you? [...As Abe's view pans around, we can see a television crew already set up in the far reaches of the corner. In a short distance, the camera crew happens to lighten up, raising up their equipment to be shown by yet another camera. As the camera pans back to Abe and River Phoenix, both men raise eyebrows and shrug at the group of people, before turning their attention back to Dante and the Large Freakish Bird With Huge Googily Eyes...] Dante: You ready? Large Freaking Bird With Huge Googly Eyes: KAW! I'M MORE READY THAN A VAGINA FOR A PENIS! River: Is it me or is vagina getting used alot around here? I mean The End might say it and its all fine and dandy, but what then? When will it end and this fabricated world ends up on the frtiz like Battlefield Earth 2: Scientology Saves The World? Abe: Hush, Kannon doesn't want anybody to mention that. He'd tax spirits if you did something like that anyway. [...The two items (Dante and Large Freaking Bird With Huge Googly Eyes) square up, looking one another in the eye before the two of them leap into the air, ala Matrix style and begin kicking each other at a furious pace. The two men leap back a bit, before Jackson catches the bird in the face with a spinning cresent kick. The bird falls back, howling with his beak in pain, before throwing a underhanded fish blow, catching Dante in the stomach. Dante clutches his chest in obvious pain as the bird rises up to a crane like pose...] Large Freaking Bird With Huge Googly Eyes: GET READY FOR THE FREAKING KAW KICK KAW! [...BAM! A sickening shot to the former Tag Team champion's face sends him hurling backwards into a wooden pilar. Snapping back, he spirals downward to the wooden plank, clutching his head. He rises back upward to a standing position, running full steam ahead at the bird, throwing furious blows that catch the being off guard. The being ducks back, letting Dante lead over him before the bird lifts his feet and catches Dante with a couple of scissor kicks into the stomach. Dante lands on his feet, kneeing the bird in the head before following that up with a back elbow to the beak...] Abe: Who would have thought that they knew Kung Fu from just watching a movie called - River: SHUSH! THIS IS A SPOOF, NO REAL NAMES OR THINGS TO BE KNOWN DAMMIT! [...The Bird falls backwards before Dante kicks him in the stomach. Growing like a unsmacked dog after meeting Rush Mason, he lifts the bird up, clutching his leg in a Fisherman suplex like hold, before lifting the entire body upon his shoulders. Jumping upward in a spiral he crashes through the plank, screaming his head off...] Dante: BUTT - River: SLAM TO KILL WHITEY! [...Abe gives River a confused look...] Abe: What was THAT? River: What? Dante: My Butt- River: Slam To Kill Whitey? Abe: You and I both know that its the Butt- River: *hic* I suggest you stick to the guidelines and don't say anything nearly and remotely to a television show that comes on Fox Saturday. [...River sips on more of his hard drink...] Abe: Ultimate - [...BOOM! A gunshot rings through the room as Abe stands still, feeling the back of his head ala some repeat of him dying thanks in large part to John Wilkes Booth. He touches, feeling a hole before sticking a finger there. He plays around, chuckling retardedly as he falls to the floor. Standing over him happens to be the Mayor of Dante Jackson's Mind - The Mayor...] The Mayor: He won't listen will he? River: *hic* I guess frickin' not. The Mayor: Well that's what happens when you try to call people not what they are... [...Abe pops back up, sighing...] Abe: I wish you never do that. The Mayor: I shot you in the head, call everyone fake one more time and I shoot you where you won't grow the fuck back. Dante: His balls, but I thought spirits and blind people don't have any. [...The Mayor nods...] The Mayor: They don't! [...Cue DUN DUN DUNNNNNN...] Dante: The End has no penis? No wonder he wanted me to sing songs with him and Michelle Branch. I thought he was a coolio, but he's a foolio. [...The three spirits nod...] The Mayor: That teaches you, never be friends with blind people unless they haven't had their penises chopped off by us. All 4: AGREED.
