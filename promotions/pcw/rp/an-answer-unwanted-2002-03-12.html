An Answer Unwanted

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/12/02 *Monologue* March Madness. The madness did start on that pay-per-view. For one man in particular his madness has just begun since that pay-per-view. No not Jason Core beating LeBlanc for the Extreme title. That was happening anyway. I’m talking about the madness that has gone through Big Daveylicious’s head since March Madness. I mean Tim’s supposed to be this great technical wrestler, right? Well, if technical wrestling involves Walden using his odor against Big D than we might as well call Uncle Bob Jimmy Jack Joe Cletus in Buttussle, Tennessee that he is a great technical wrestler and that he should be pro with his famous bean stew! Instead of calling Walden the “Heart and Soul of the PCW” we might as well call him the “Fart and Gas of the PCW”. What does Walden get with his efforts? A mad Big D. You all shall thank Tim Walden for that. Especially, “Amazing” Mike Storm. Whose gonna get his a$$ handed to him by an AMAZING Daveylicious Bomb by an AMAZING wrestler known as Big Daveylicious. (The scene fades to March Madness. The Six Pack Elimination match has just started. Afwa begins to make his way out of the ring. Meanwhile, D-Kay has Big D back on his feet. D-Kay sends Big D into the ropes. Big D bounces off the ropes. D-Kay is waiting for him with a kick to the stomach. Big D doubles forward. D-Kay locks him in for the suplex. D-Kay goes to lift the bigger Big D into the air, but Big D blocks it. Before D-Kay can counter it, Big D drops him with a DDT.) Jeff: Why is he getting to hit any moves? He didn't Rp! Dave: KAYFABE! Jeff: What? The truth going to hurt his feelings? Dave sighs. (Tim Walden locks a sleeperhold on TMM in the center of the ring. TMM starts to fade. The referee drops to the mat to check on a tap out by TMM. Nothing. Walden tightens the hold on TMM. Afwa reaches Impact on the outside of the ring. Afwa brings Impact to his feet and lifts him into the air for a suplex. In mid air, Impact turns the move into a variation of a reverse DDT. Afwa hits the floor hard! We zoom back to see we are watching a replay of March Madness from a new SONY big screen TV inside Big Daveylicious‘s living room. He sits staring off at the pay-per-view with confusion wearying black boots, black pants, and an unbuttoned poly shirt. Sitting on the leather couch is his personal lackey, Michael Graves.) Big Daveylicious: Didn’t rp? What the hell is an rp? (As Big D scratches his head he looks back at the TV with Walden droping the hold on TMM, choosing to search another path of enlightenment. That path leads him into the big boot of Big D. Walden slams into the mat. Big decides his done playing with Walden early and sets him up for the powerbomb. Big D lifts Walden into the air. But, before Big D can slam Walden out of his misery, Walden releases gas. The staunch smell is enough of an advantage for Walden to nail a tornado DDT on a dizzy Big D. Walden somehow gets it in him to hook the leg. 1...... 2...... 3!!!!!!!!! ) Dave: {From TV} BIG DAVEYLICIOUS HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Jeff: {From TV} Thank God that non-Rping jackass is out of here. (Suddenly, TV fades to black as Big D turns off the TV with his remote. He scratches his chin pondering on what Jeff Marx is talking about.) Big Daveylicious: Damn, I think we need a new announce team. Could somebody please tell me what the hell is rping? *I can. * (Big D looks around his living room sitting in his chair. He only sees Graves sitting there quietly.) Big Daveylicious: Who was that? Michael Graves: Well, I don’t know what role-playing is, Graves. *For I am whom created you. * Big Daveylicious: Father? Michael Graves: Your father is role-playing? *No. Nor, am I God...* Big Daveylicious: Well, Satan you’re looking for the wrong guy than. You must be looking for El Diablo or DarkStar. (Michael stamps his foot on the floor.) Michael Graves: Just because I come from the third sector of Hell doesn’t mean I’m Satan! *No, I’m not the devil. I am your creator or as most people call it your handler* Big Daveylicious: You can’t handle me?! Michael Graves: I wish. *Silence you incompetent fool! If I want I can change this scene to Al’s House of Anal sex for you only to be raped by gay men! I own you! * Big Daveylicious: I’ll be quiet. *Smart choice. You want to know what rping is right. * Big Daveylicious: …Yeah. *It’s short for role-playing. * Big Daveylicious: Role-playing? Isn’t that were the woman dresses like a cop and the guy dresses like a girl? Michael Graves: I’ll be the cop. *That’s a kinky way of having sex with different roles. I’m talking about role-playing. Basically it’s writing a stories and skits. But the type of role-playing Marx was talking about is role-plays or basically which are supposed to be wrestlers behind the ring doing promos. * Big Daveylicious: You mean role-plays are promo’s I see on TV and fall asleep to. Michael Graves: I guess you don’t want me to be the cop. I bet you weren’t listening to me damn it! (Graves stomps his feet in front of Big D.) Michael Graves: Aren’t you even listening to a word I am saying?! *In your world the role-plays your opponent’s handler writes is a promo to you. Marvelous isn’t it? * Big Daveylicious: No, I’m marvelous. (Michael Graves’s eyes grow wide as he jump up and down angrily.) Michael Graves: You know after all the work I do I should leave you in your pity! I get you all the tapes from your opponent’s interviews. I carry your luggage to the arena. I do every damn thing you ask at will! But yet you ignore me! *No, I’m marvelous. You’re just a character I created! * Big Daveylicious: How come I haven't won any titles yet, then? *Because of interference.* Big Daveylicious: But you said that your role plays... *Uh...there's no time for foolishness. There's a bomb in your house and you got 10 seconds!* Big Daveylicious: Sh*t! *10* Michael Graves: Sh*t? *9* (Big D gets up off his chair quickly, pushes a pondering Graves out of his destination as he runs towards his window of his living room.) *8* Michael Graves: What’s a matter got the runs? *7,6,5,4,3,2…* (Big D leaps into the air through the window forcing his body to break through the glass window. The window shatters as he lands onto the pavement of his driveway on his side. Big D closes his eyes as he expects his house to explode. He stands up, clutch’s his side, and dusts himself off. ) Big Daveylicious: I guess the bomb is a dud. *Mwahahaha! You should have seen your face light up after I said I had a bomb in your house. * Big Daveylicious: You b@stard! Now, I need a damn new window! *B@stard?! I’m not the one who left Graves in the house after being told.* Big Daveylicious: But you said there was a bomb! Michael Graves: Big D? Why are ya talking to yourself? (Big D turns his head up to see Graves standing outside his front door. Big D face fills with frustration as he looks at Graves. ) Michael Graves: And what is it about a bomb? Big Daveylicious: You mean you didn’t hear the voice? He said that there was a bomb in the house. Michael Graves: Theirs a bomb in the house?! Oh, my God! (Michael jumps right onto the concrete pavement of Big D's driveway landing onto his chest. Michael looks up at a standing Big D just shaking his head.) Big Daveylicious: I thought you worshipped the devil. Michael Graves: Just because I come from the third sector of hell were all men are hot sweating grueling hours near fire doesn't mean I don't believe in God. (Michael stands up doesn't dust himself off. His hands and face have met the pavement giving him scratches.) Michael Graves: How come the bomb didn't explode yet? Big Daveylicious: I said that he said there WAS a bomb in the house. WAS being the keyword. Michael Graves: You mean to tell me there was a bomb in the house?! Big Daveylicious: He said so and he tricked me. (Michael stands up shakes his head with a tear rolling down his cheek to the scratch on his face.) Michael Graves: You mean to tell me you’d leave me if without warning about my life being in danger?! Big Daveylicious: Well...uh Michael Graves: You selfish meany! I’m sorry if my complicated life is inconvenient for your perfect existence! (Michael runs off out of Big D’s driveway running stupidly out of our view. All Big D can do is shrug. But he than pauses.) Big Daveylicious: Oh, crap. (He looks back to see if he had missed Graves. Indeed, he did.) Big Daveylicious: Damn, now who is gonna go to the store and buy me a new window? Who is gonna take me to the strip bar? (Big D falls to his knees, looks up in the sky with his fist up screaming.) Big Daveylicious: Whyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy?! *’Cause I Run This Game* (With that the scene fades to black as Big D pauses.)
