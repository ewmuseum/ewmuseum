Saving Green's Privates!

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03 - 11 - 02 A bitterly cold summers day had been subjected to over-cast sky that bare only grey dense clouds, sheltering the hoard below. The sound of battles rages on crashing like thunder, safely at distance thought Gaz Green who cowers unnoticed in the back of an open-top patrol boat. His comrades showing signs of wear and experience with trenches of dirt adorning their respected brows and cheekbones nestled in thick, bristled facial hair. He sits motionless, hand firmly grasping his standard weapon (M1 Garand) and clad in his military uniform he trembles to each thought of horror he may endure, through his dream-like state he listens to the carnage surrounding him. 'GREEN - FOCUS~!' bellowed the commanding officer, before feeling the wrath of the ocean as a towering wave hits the side of the boat with a firm impact. Shaking his head to acknowledge, he retorts 'Huh... Yes, sir!' His murmurs of a reply are almost drowned out by the heavy wheezing of the small unit; obviously the conditions and circumstances of such events have taken their toll. The platoon collected their thoughts, embraced in the surrounding activity as the angle hatch lowered. The troubled waters, laid under-siege by the concrete gun turrets that occupied either side of the foothill. 'MOVE OUT!!!' roared the commanding officer in an authoritive tone. The twelve man party descended into the ice cold sea, many weighed down by their equipment, causing death by drowning whilst others feel the metallic fragments hurtling from above as the machine guns continue to fire. 'What am I doing here?' thought Gaz Green, wading through the water, weapon held high above his head. The monstrous waves make navigation difficult at best and the constant shower of bullets simply decrease the chances of survival as Gaz continues to strive further on. Under heavy fire he scrambles to his commanding officer. 'Green I want you to cover the first wave of platoons....' said the commander in bated breath. 'Cover? With all due respect, we should concentrate our efforts on taking out the barbed wire fencing' said the lowly private with an untimely interruption. His eyes wandered over the fence, seeking a weakness they may take advantage of. 'THAT'S AN ORDER PRIVATE!!!' roared the authority figure. Gaz simply gestured with his hand and ran out into open fire, momentarily being a sitting duck he aimlessly returned fire with a few wayward shots and finally found shelter within a damp trench. Lying on his chest he shuffles forward trying to gain a few inches of visibility but his plotting being thwarted by a mortar landing only feet away. 'WHAT THE F....' Besides the crashing of explosives and metal ricochets, the clouds became swamped with activity as various aeroplanes scout overhead. Pass after pass they make, giving Gaz an uneasy feeling but he ventures on leaving the confines of the trench and charging towards the barbed wire fencing. Making haste, Gaz comes under fire forcing him into a detour and behind a large manually constructed barricade, which appears to be occupied by another member of his platoon. 'I've been pinned behind this for some time now... I must make it to the boundaries, so that requires some cover fire from you private!' asked the officer with a look of desperation. 'On my mark, then you charge to that fence like a bat out of hell' replied Gaz Green grasping his M1 Garand and replenishing his limited ammo. Counting down with his fingers, he cries 'NOW!!!' The officer lethargically runs forward and almost succumbs to the torrent of bullets, his run covered by the marksmanship of Gaz Green who pulls back on the trigger and squeezes off two rounds, emptying the barrels in the process. Both individuals make it safely across the beach and prepare to confront the barricade fencing and as it stands impenetrable be the best choice of word to describe it. 'Now what? We can't go over and we can't go under, leaving one other option...' said the unknown officer with a puzzling glare. 'We'll have to go through it then!' blurted Gaz, determined to further their current stalemate. 'That'll require explosives!' replied the officer. 'There's an engineer around here somewhere?' Brandishing a worn pair of binoculars he raises them to his eyes and curiously peers around the existing beach trying to locate the elusive engineer who had been assigned to such a task during their mission briefing. Lowering his binoculars and squinting, he re-aligns them for a second opinion. 'Err.... Found Him~!' The officer directing Gaz towards the right hand side of the beach with his out-stretched finger he assures him that the engineer is safely tucked away from sight. Gaz reluctant to place his life on the line realises that this operation is their only course of action and sets hot footed across the battle field, focusing straight ahead he doesn't take any notice of the surrounding gun and mortar fire. Barely avoiding a thunderous explosion he ducks clenching his head as the shower of sub-soil falls from the heavens and then continues on his objective, until he stumbles before the engineer. 'Engineer?' questioned the inquisitive Gaz Green. 'You could say that' retorted the engineer, sitting crouched he tightly clenches the sticks of dynamite and cradles them close to his chest. 'I'm not asking, rather telling you that we need those explosives to by-pass the fencing!' forcefully said the low ranked officer. Nodding to acknowledge his request they retrace Gaz Greens previous steps, the latter offering cover fire to ease the torrid passage back to the barbed-wire fencing. Wary of their footing they finally emerge unscathed and the engineer began to work his magic, setting the explosives with his nimble fingers he turns and buries his head in the sand with a five-star leap to safety. The flash followed by the bang and the proposed passageway emerged from the smoke, allowing the mobile infantry the opportunity to attack, which they grasped with both hands. Gaz takes lead and hurtles through the open ground, heading towards the entrance way of said towers he is covered by his supporting task force of platoons who off-load cobias amounts of ammo. 'Why do I get the sinking feeling that I should've been an entertainer, comedian even?' thought the silent assassin Gaz Green. Descending upon a bunker Gaz finds himself confronted with a German soldier, who has an uncanny resemblance to Tim Walden. 'Walden?' asked the puzzled Gaz as he cleared his throat. 'NICHT SHOOT - NICHT SHOOT - NICHT SHOOT~!' Both men obviously feeling the pressure of war and it is the German to react, going for his fire-arm and having trouble to unbuckle it from its holster he is shot dead by an advancing allied soldier who turns out to be the commanding officer from the opening scene. 'WHAT THE....' The troops of the allied forced gather in numbers and secure the outer perimeter, followed by the tactics of the commanding officer who has yet another daunting mission he bestows upon Gaz Green. 'Green - Job well done!' he began before cutting straight to the objective. 'Green, I want you to secure the lower levels of the compound and we'll take the mission off your hands once completed....' Gaz simply nods with a subtle smile, reloads the chamber of his weapon and creeps around the blind corner and through a large metallic doorframe, straight into the bowels of the building. His eyes flicker back and forth but everything appears desolate, that is until a concealed German takes a warning shot, with the projectile imbedding itself into the wall. 'Aufenthalt Zurück... Verbündetes Schwein~!' roared the soldier as he cowers behind a barrel in a kneeling position. 'Lassen Sie Ihr weopon fallen?' he continued in his strong German voice. Gaz throws a small stone to draw his attention and the soldiers curiosity became his down fall as he fired a bullet into his opponents chest with blood washing the behind wall. Checking the resulting carcase he turns the body over to reveal a Duke Williams look-a-like as Gaz can only look with disbelief. 'Duke Williams?' he murmured, wiping away the excess sweat from his brow. Not to dwell on the situation, Gaz continues into the belly of the beast, still keeping a lookout he reminisces over the deaths of both the German soldiers, both of which resembling two of his scheduled opponents in the Main Event. Walking into a connecting room he treads lightly still haunted by those two memories and doesn't notice the blind-sided assault as another German lays the butt of his rifle into the temple of Gaz Green almost rendering him unconscious. 'Verbündetes Schwein!!!' Gaz beaten down by the blow of the head shot looks up at his assailant and stares in horror, Xavier Kannon. The PCW CEO stood menacingly down at the lowly private as the room became bathed in German soldiers, all of which having uncanny resemblances to those awaiting at Friday Night Fusion, Darkstar, Totally Tubular Tommy, Skitzo, The End to name but a few. As Gaz looked upon his captors with a glassy glare, the hoard of superstars clad in German military uniforms beat the single officer mercilessly to the point of losing consciousness. Suddenly the scene blackens and reopens to Gaz Green screaming within the confines of his bed, obviously the entire episode had been a figment of his imagination whilst he had been asleep. He sits up in a puddle of his own sweat and soaks in a deep breath, panting heavily he whispers to himself. 'Talk about being a marked man - I can't sleep without being in fear of losing my Tag Team Championship to the dirty, underhanded tactics of the other competitors!' 'Vampire with a hand grenade would've made for a giggle, instead I get a Front Office, Dark Age, Playaz Inc, Celestial and Team Loser beating... Did I miss anybody?' continued the Jester slowly coming around, eyes flickering violently. The scene slowly draws to a close, fixated on the flickering image of the television set that repeatedly plays the introductory footage of the Playstations smash hit 'Medal Of Honour'.
