The Ectasy of Gold...

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Ectasy of Gold... Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 20, 2002 The ecstasy of gold... In the history of America, and indeed the world, gold, and the pursuit of it, have changed the lives of innumerable people. In 1849, gold was discovered at Sutter's Mill, and the floodgates opened. The Chinese, the Irish, and myriad Americans poured westward, searching for fortunes of their own. The same occurred later...with the Klondike gold rush in Alaska. More Americans poured in looking for money. For money leads to wealth. Wealth leads to power. Power leads to happiness. Who says money can't buy happiness? A really poor man. But, there is one final case of gold in American history. The Nez Perce Indians lived a happy life, until gold was discovered on their Idaho reservation. Enter the United States military. The Nez Perce were hunted mercilessly, and indeed nearly wiped out. All for the sparkle of gold. Anyone need two guesses as to who the U.S. military is? Thought not. Because, then, as now....the government was quite...Politically Incorrect. ::In the Southwestern corner of the United States, Spanish and Mexican influence is everywhere. And, as with all other culture, the best part is the fine cuisine. A fine Spanish restaurant is the location for a meeting. William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft sit at a table, with Powell's campaign manager, Roger Schultz. Lovecraft's intern, Katherine Willis, is nowhere to be found. Yet, in the middle of the table sits a giant notepad. On it is written the members of International Incident, with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore circled...:: Roger Schultz: William....Reginald...this is it. The culimination of three weeks of work in Primetime Championship Wrestling, and an undefeated record. On Sunday Sabotage, you two face Golden Game for the PCW Tag Team Titles. William S. Powell: Wow... I've only been in this business six months....and here I am, almost at the pinnacle of tag team competition. Reginald V. Lovecraft: I've been around...well, three weeks. Thanks man! You're the best partner I ever had!! ::The two men shake hands. Roger rolls his eyes, as he garners their attention.:: Roger Schultz: It's not that easy you two. International Incident will be quite keen to keep the tag titles in their alliance. I've looked up the group's membership.Golden Game is decidedly the weak link of the group. I mean, they have Flaymin Aymin! ::Reginald and Powell turn to each other. They stare, nod, and....:: Powell and Lovecraft: BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! William S. Powell: That's rich! Flaymin Aymin sucks! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I don't know Will....after all.....he HAS beaten Tim Walden innumerable times. William S. Powell: That would mean something, except Walden sucks. If he didn't, it would, but he doesn't, so IT doesn't. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Wow. That was very confusing. William S. Powell: Thanks. Roger Schultz: Damnit man, these guys also have the Cruiserweight Champion Anton the Kid, PCW Grand Slam Champion Frisco, and The Chef. William S. Powell: The Chef?! WHO THE HELL CALLS THEMSELF THE CHEF?!Can I hire him to work in the mansion to make me my Dover sole? Roger Schultz: Probably not. He's an extremist militant Islamic man, who is against the US, and sautees for the forces of evil. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ouch...damn, I needed a good chef. My current one can't make souffle anymore. Roger Schultz: He also has a manager, Bertha Stewart, a truly large fat woman. Large fat people disgust me... William S. Powell: Tell me about it. I tried to get a bill passed in the city council banning fat people from wearing shorts. Sadly, half the council ARE fat people who wear shorts, so it failed. Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's worse in the south! Fat people can wear shorts year round! It's horrible! FAT PEOPLE SUCK!!!! Roger Schultz: Stop talking about fat people! Damnit, Golden Game's gonna be PISSED going into this match. You guys jumped them shortly after you came here, and its unlikely they've forgotten that? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Who cares?! They can't do anything. Here, let my partner display his knowledge of Spanish, and Golden Game. William S. Powell: Partido de Oro, ustedes son idiotas! Nosotros somos el par mas bueno en PCW. GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOLLLLLLLLLLLLL!!!! Reginald V. Lovecraft: What he just said was: 'Golden Game, you guys are idiots! We are the best tag team in PCW. GOOOOOOOOOAALLLLLLL!!!' Why did you throw that 'GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOALLLL!' in there? William S. Powell: Besides, if I screwed up my Spanish, Borro and Klaymore would at least understand the GOOOOOOOOOOOOAAALLLL!!! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ah, I see..... ::Desperately, Schultz begins to try to get his charges back under control:: Roger Schultz: Guys..this is all well and good, but Golden Game poses a threat. They are the tag team champions. You may not like or respect them, but they know what they are doing in the ring. William S. Powell: Roger, chill. We are Politically Incorrect. Sunday, we destroyed Misplaced! They didn't get one move in! Whereas Lovecraft and I got in every single one of our trademarks! They experienced The Executioner's Chair! And more importantly, Carey took a trip to The Halls of Justice..and there is no one here who can withstand the awesome power of the Halls. No one. Golden Game shall soon find out...we seek no truth.....winning is all....... Reginald V. Lovecraft: For Sunday.....will be Election Day.... Roger Schultz: And on Election Day....Politically Incorrect ALWAYS triumph!! ::Fade to...:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically Incorrect:The Next PCW Tag Team Champions ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
