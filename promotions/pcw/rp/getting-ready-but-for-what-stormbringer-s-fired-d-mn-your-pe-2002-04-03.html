Getting Ready... (But for what? Stormbringer's fired...) D*mn your pessimism!

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/03/02 'Indeed, the battle to end all battles shall soon commence. And preparations must be made. You know your enemy's strengths and you know your enemy's weaknesses. You know your own strengths, and you know your own weaknesses. All that must happen before you fight... is to rid yourself of weakness. The purge begins, as will soon the fighting....' -- The End Video Promo, Audio Voice Over: The End The camera fades in showing The End and Chalymar eating breakfast together. The End eats some toast and an egg sunny-side up. Chalymar eats more than he does, bacon, sausage, pancakes, and two scrambled eggs. Her attire is very casual, just a UM tee and jeans; her light tanned skin and brown curly hair stand out from the white walls and blue counter and cabinets of the kitchen in which she and The End eat. There are keys on the table by Chalymar's left hand. It would appear there would a road trip in the very near future. The End:'Chalymar… hasn’t really been much of an issue as far as PCW is concerned. We’ve lived together for a while now, yet few have heard of her. It’s for a reason, the secrecy. Chalymar is too good for all the barbarism that is inherently a main part of the operations in PCW.' Though only a very good lip reader could make out what Chalymar and The End are saying (thanks to their apparently fast speech), the smiles on their faces would make the scene light-hearted. Whatever The End says, it may be considered in good humor as evidenced by the laugh she shows on-camera. The End's voice over continues. The End:“Quite simply, I wanted to keep her safe, and away from the corrupting agent that is the PCW. I think she’s helped me once in my first round GTT match with Nick Extreme, cutting a promo against him for me as I was busy dealing with other matches for PCW.” The camera shows some magnets on the refrigerator of the focus group Chalymar attends. A hand moves across the camera, and Chalymar pulls it away from its current view, shaking her head, 'no.' She then goes back to what she was doing: cleaning the dishes. Chalymar washes the dishes in the sink, and The End dries them. The End:'Even though I travel all around – I’m only getting booked to one show a week since March Madness, so I have had more time to spend at home – she stays by my side, lobbying for me with the bookers to get me signed to good matches, and she's also my cheerleader, though I don't really ask that of her. She stays at home during the shows, so she won't be put in harm's way at the arenas. And yet, this still may not be enough.' The camera cuts to footage of Chalymar's bedroom, where she packs some clothes, makeup, and toiletries in a bag that looks to have already been packed the night before. The End is not in the scene. The End:'Since he’s fired, Bobby Stormbringer doesn’t have anything but time. He may try something… he may even kidnap Chalymar. Why, but to throw me off my game at the pay-per-view, of course. He is without a job at the moment, but I’ll get him to the pay-per-view for our match myself to finish what is started.” Chalymar zips up her bag, fixes her hair in the mirror, and leaves the room. The End:“About the only thing I've disclosed to the PCW is my current address, to which my paychecks are mailed. That information would be easy enough to obtain. Stormbringer, Kira… maybe even Carl Everstrong if Stormbringer could convince him, could come to the house, kidnap Chalymar, use her to get me to throw my match... similar things have happened.' In the kitchen, The End packs a small red cooler full of food and drinks for the road. The End:'I won't allow Chalymar to be placed in danger. And I will not allow for that to get in the way of my match at Spring Fling.” Cut to a scene of the camera facing away from the front of The End's house, a little in front of the doorway. The End is packing some bags into the Chalymar's silver Ford Navigator. Chalymar is sitting in the driver's seat, starting up the SUV. A bit of exhaust billows from the pipe. The End:'So the best thing to do would be to keep Chalymar mobile. Out of harm's way.' The End finishes packing the random assortment of bags and pulls down the trunk door to close it. The End:'Away from the house...' He makes sure that the door is secure, and then moves around to the front passenger's side of the car. After a brief word with Chalymar that cannot be discerned, The End backs away from the SUV and Chalymar pulls away. The End:'... Away... from me.' The End moves across the lush green lawn in front of his house, which contrasts from the blacktop concrete road leading away from it (though matching perfectly with the open green field by which his house stands), to his front door. Turning back (perhaps to the sound of Chalymar's SUV) one more time, The End walks into his house and closes the front door. The camera fades out. Only The End's voice remains. The End:'She will not tell me where she is. She will not contact me in any way. I will call her. I don't know when, it may be after the pay-per-view, and it may not. But I cannot afford any distractions...' There is a bit of a pause. The End:'I have to make preparations... and the first step is complete....' A large two story house, painted white but the layers of paint fading... The shingled roof is falling apart in front of the camera. A large oak tree in the front of the house looks to be struck by lightning, not fallen, but split halfway down the trunk, with the dirty green leafy canopy pointed away from the house toward the small wooded area it faces. The bushes in front of the house are dead, and the screen door in front of the blue main door is fallen off the hinges, lying across the dilapidated porch with the roof collapsed in. The End:'This house... my childhood...' The door opens and the camera steps in. The End's voice plays over the silent video. The End:'The first four years of my life were spent in this house... and I remember like it was yesterday....' The camera moves through the door and turns to the immediate left, where a four-foot-long hall leads into a living room. Glass is broken in, shards lying on the soiled carpet. A few candles are strewn on the ground, the expensive candleholders they rested in long stolen. A thick layer of dust coats the blue kiddy furniture. The End:'I remember playing in this room as a toddler, a few of my toys all throughout the room. This room was one of the 'safe rooms.' I could go all around this place and never hurt myself. My mother designed the room that way. The whole top floor was used for my parents' bedroom and guest rooms since relatives were always stopping through....' The camera moves through to the dining room at the far end of the room, which has a large wooden table with a glass chandelier hanging by a thread over the table. A few chairs are pushed on the ground, and the window in this room is broken as well, but the yellow (originally white) curtains kept the glass from getting far into the room. The End:'The dining room... never was in here much. This is where houseguests were treated to dinner....' From the dining room, a sliding wooden door leads to the kitchen. The stove and sink are intact, just covered in dust, and there's a few grease burns on the stove. The cupboards are open, long-expired food scattered around. Insects... some living, some dead, are among the bits of cereal, bread, and chips. The kitchen is separated from the family room by a four & one-half-foot-long counter. The counter is dusty, and a rat moves across when the camera comes through. There are no chairs; they are all thrown through the windows to the back of the house. The family room has an area of the carpeting, a rectangle that's five shades of black lighter than the surrounding carpeting... a TV used to be there. The End:'In this room, I watched Sesame Street or whatever was on that my mother put me in front of.' The camera moves out of the family room back to the hallway that bypasses the living room and dining room going straight to the door. The camera then climbs the stairs in front of the door and goes through the door to the right once it reaches the top. There is a messed up bed, how long since anyone has slept in it that cannot be determined. Little else remains, the room looted by vandals. The End:'This is the master bedroom. As far as I can guess, this is where I was conceived. Not much else I wish to say. Move along.' The camera enters some of the guest rooms. They are all the same: a pull out bed frame with no mattress (whoever looted the place really needed a mattress... The camera, finding nothing of interest, returns downstairs. It begins to move out the door, but turns around and moves back to the family room through the hallway by the stairs. There, sitting on the ground is The End. He speaks to the camera now. The End:'This house... this represents my past....' A pause, with a sigh, comes from The End. The End:'Often have I thought about my past... the details are not too clear, even to me....' The End stands up and moves over to the wall. He reaches out and touches it with his left hand. The End:'If anything, all I have is the memories kept in this house....' A 'flicking' sound... The End:'But those memories... are just distractions....' The End brings the lit lighter into view, running his fingers through the flame. The End:'And I need no distractions...” The End tosses the lighter away. The camera follows its path through the air away from The End to the back of the room, where it lands safely... in a pile of gasoline soaked curtains. The camera quickly exits the house and fades out. An indiscernible time later, the camera fades back in from a position away from The End's old house on a hill. The house, already run down, is burning to the ground. The End sits in front of the house, facing it in a sort of meditative trance, drawing in the energy from the fire. The End's voice over picks up once more. The End:'Chalymar is sent away. She is no longer a distraction.... 'My past, what little of it I have shared... that is no longer a distraction....' The scene of the burning house fades away with The End's last words. The End:'There is but one more thing I must do... and then the preparations will be complete....' A graveyard, so dark and windy, a thunderstorm rolling in from the east is the cause. The trees sway in the sharp gusts, mists from the oncoming storm already falling upon area. Rows and rows of headstones, dedicated to late fathers, mothers, siblings, friends, and distant relatives... and two in particular of special interest. In the rear southwest corner of the graveyard, are two headstones of particular interest. The first says; 'Akiko Yamato, 1957-1981. Came to us to start a new life. A wonderful friend. She will be missed.' The second says; 'Robert Green, 1954-1981. War hero, entrepreneur, and pillar of the community. A great loss to us all.' The hard rain begins to fall. The camera, covered in a rain guard, backs away, showing both headstones at once. The sound is activated. The thunder is close, striking within a few hundred feet of the graveyard. The cameraman, ever vigilant, maintains his position. A flash of lightning illuminates the area enough to show the headstones, watched over by the statue of a man (only the silhouette is made out) standing on a four-foot-tall limestone platform, standing with arms stretched to heaven. Another flash of lightning, and the camera shakes. That bolt hit closer. The light shows the statue moving... coming to life. It's not a statue, it's The End. With a simple black shirt and pants, his braided hair blowing in the wind, and face covered in rain, The End looks blankly at the camera. Turning around, The End stands between the headstones, placing his hands on each. He speaks in an aside to the camera. The End:'Robert, Akiko. My parents. So long ago when they died in that car accident. I was but four years old...' Another flash of lightning. The End moves off camera and returns with a sledgehammer. Rearing back, The End begins destroying his parents' headstones.*Crik* *Crik* *Crik* *Crack* The End:'I sometimes think if my parents would be proud of me were they still alive. My choice to wrestle, my choice to continue doing so even after the business cost me my sight... I could go into other careers... perhaps.' The camera records every second of The End's act. Crack after crack, the headstones fall to pieces. The End:'I can't afford that now. Too many distractions. They're dead... no sense in speculation.' The End moves off camera. The camera zooms out and shows the open graves of The End's parents. The End returns with a canister of some liquid. In the darkness between lightning flashes, The End's silhouette can be made out to be unscrewing a lid. The End:'This is the last step in my preparations... the distractions are dealt with...' The End pours the liquid into the graves and walks away. The camera stays trained on the open plots, showing smoke rising from the graves; the acid that The End poured into the graves, disintegrating everything within down to the molecular level. Water doesn't help anything, except that it makes the acid work better. The End:'The purge is complete....'
