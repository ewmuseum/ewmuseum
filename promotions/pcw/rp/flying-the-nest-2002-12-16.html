Flying The Nest

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Flying The Nest Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 16/12/02 “Ahhhhh.... About time I finally moved out of that New Jersey Cesspool...” Our shot opens up under the cold Blue winter sky of Hubbardville, Wisconsin, the home of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon and the religion of Scientology. After the stressful week, Kannon finally seems relaxed back in his beloved city, his cuts from Friday night have healed and the wrinkles brought about by stress have started to fade. Xavier : “And now I’m going to make sure I never have to go back there, well, except to throw Duke out of his office when I finally find a loophole to ditch him through.” When in Hubbardville, Xavier seems at one with himself. The city he founded, a shining beacon in the dark night of oppression for the nation’s Scientologists, a city they can call home without having to worry about what others think... And more importantly both a Tax-Haven and somewhere out of the duristiction of most U.S law and Government agencies. Xavier : “Finally, PCW will have a home city to be proud off.” The peaceful looking Scientologist is standing up on the hillside of Jenna Elfman Gardens in the centre of Hubbardville, standing under the L. Ron Hubbard memorial and looking out at the new East Riverside development of the City, where a large “Hubbardville Construction Company” sign is seen next to earth moving machinery that work to dig foundations. Xavier : “PCW Head Offices, Floors 14-25 Travolta Plaza, The Riverside, Hubbardville, Wisconsin... I’ll have to get around to updating the business cards.” After days of snarling, sneering and yelling, the Xavier Kannon we see today is a different man. The dressing for his forehead has been removed and only a faint trace of a cut can be seen where his long mane of hair is tied neatly back, and his toned body is once again hidden by a smart Grey suit perfectly tailored for him by Wilson’s of Hubbardville. The face gives off the tell tale signs of a man totally at peace with himself, the Green eyes are bright and vibrant with no bags or creases, and the mouth forms a natural, subtle smile without even noticing it, even his face is as cleanly shaven as it’s been in weeks. Xavier : “Slowly but surely, everything I detest about PCW has been corrected. Tyler Nelson and the Phantom Booker were despatched off, the Tag Team belts have a far more respectable holder, albeit nobody, Duke is a week away from having his old body give out on him and we’re finally getting free of New Jersey, PCW’s OLD home.” Looking all around him at the beautiful surroundings of the gardens, Xavier begins to stroll, no destination, no route, just letting the nature and the beauty guide him. The air is still and bitter, his breath being exhaled in mist into the crisp cold around him, ears devoid of the sound of birds after their migration. “XAVI!” Alas, the silence is broken and Kannon snaps out of it, not that he minds though as his hailing is followed by the sight of his wife, Eleanor. Eleanor : “That man, you know? The one in the hat? At the, like, building thing? He won’t let me blow stuff up and stuff!!!” The little prep is dressed for the weather in a brightly coloured woollen hat, stripy scarf, long sheepskin coat, Purple woolly jumper and flared jeans. She crosses her arms across her as she walks, cuddling herself for more warmth. Xavier : “I know my little Fuck Ferret, I told him to. Once they’re finished I’ll.. Um... Buy you something to blow up.” Ellie~! stamps her feet in a huff as Kannon walks to her and puts an arm around her shoulders, his wife reciprocating and holding him around the chest, resting her head on his shoulder as they walk down the hill. Eleanor : “Okay, but I want something, like, REALLY big to blow up then... They’re all bashing stuff and won’t let Ellie~! join in.” They look just like any other couple as they walk towards the gates that lead them out of Jenna Elfman Gardens and to the edge of the Waterfront, where towering spires and buildings a plenty are rising up to flank the River Hubbard. Xavier : “After we finally move everything here, my little Poon Puppy, you can have everything you want.” The change in scenery hits immediately. Gone is the Green grass highlighted at the tips with frost, gone are the hardy flowers braving the cold and the trees still hanging on to some of their leaves. Instead there are construction workers milling around, rubble making up most of the floor and giant metal scaffolds embracing bland Grey concrete structures. Eleanor : “Like, which one is going to be our Offices and stuff?” Pointing with the finger of the arm he has around Ellie~!’s petite shoulders, he guides her eyes towards the tallest of the current erections, situated right by the river and the focal point of all the other work around it. Looking like a simple Grey skeleton, it still exhudes that feeling that it will be something truly special. Eleanor : “That it?” The tone of her voice shows she doesn’t quite see the potential in it, the building that will be the greatest of all the structures within Hubbardville not having mush effect on the little prep. Xavier, on the other hand is more optimistic. Eleanor : “It looks kinda, like, well... Sucky!” As they walk through the outskirts of the construction site, every workman who walks past acknowledges the couple. Fame in Hubbardville is not a burden, they have the utmost respect of all those in the city who are truly appreciative for what Kannon, along with his fellow famous Scientologist have given them. Xavier : “You won’t be saying that when you see the first ever PCW Commissioner’s Office with it’s own Sauna, Mudbath, Jacuzzi and personal Masseuse, my little Shag Squirrel.” Finally, something to perk Ellie up and out of her apathy. The fact she is Commissioner of the world’s leading wrestling federation eclipsed by the fact she’ll have her own Sauna in her office she only ever used to watch cable in while Kannon worked anyway. Eleanor : “Sauna? Like... YAY ME!!!” She hops on the spot and applauds herself before leaping at her husband and hugs him, Xavier almost taken aback. Eleanor : “With all that stuff, I’ll never be a stressed little Ellie~! so can book, like, WAY loads of good matches!” While she seems happy with that fact, Kannon gulps and starts making a mental list of other luxuries to add to her office to stop her doing any work as the pair continue their journey through the Riverside, seemingly heading towards a large group of porta-cabins bearing the name and logo of the Hubbardville Construction Company. Xavier : “How about your own en-suite health spa?” Ellie~!’s eyes widen. Eleanor : “Like, SO yeah!” Thinking of more things to keep her from actually doing her job, Kannon reels them off, each one meeting a welcome response from his wife. Xavier : “The cast of Melrose Place on call to act out any episode you want? You’re own Clothing Store?” Breaking out of her eagerness and excitement for a second, Ellie~! looks worried. Eleanor : “Um, but won’t that... Like... leave me SO no time to do my job and stuff?” With her head resting on his shoulder, Ellie~! doesn’t see the smug smile that has made it’s way onto Xavier’s face a he holds her tight to him, the little prep only feeling the shrug his shoulders go through. Xavier : “You’re screwing the boss, you don’t have to work!” The news, despite it’s rather crude delivery, brings a content smile to the little prep’s face as she yawns and snuggles up to his a bit more as they walk, letting out a few tired words. Eleanor : “Like... Yay me!” As the sound of their feet in the sandy gravel marks their approach, a man walks down the steps of the Cabin and holds up a hand to wave to Kannon, the Scientologist acknowledging with a friendly nod of his head. “Welcome, Mr Kannon.” The man is Mitchell Blake, head Architect for this project and more importantly a Scientologist from the Des Moines, Iowa church... The first ever founded by Xavier himself. Xavier : “Mitchell, hello. How are the plans for the 18th floor looking?” Mitchell and Xavier, Ellie still held to him, walk towards one of the tables set up around the cabins where the various Blueprints are, they each reach their side of the table at the same time and exchange a handshake, Ellie~! lifting up a weary and cold arm to give a little wave. Eleanor : “Like, hi, and stuff.” Finding a tag saying “18”, Blake rolls the large plan out, appearing to show a large floor divided up into 3 main offices. Mitchell : “Everything you wanted is here now Mr Kannon, we’ve changed this space here to allow the plumbing for the spa in here.” Various spots on the Blueprint are pointed too, Kannon looking on pleased with what he sees... Ellie~! just seeing a load of lines she tries to use to spell her name. Xavier : “Ah, I see Tim’s giant abacus could be accommodated, that should inspire him to tax a few more of the Peons I have working for me.” Mitchell does a double-take, realising he’s working for Kannon and wondering if he is classed as a Peon, while Ellie~! follows the lines with her fingers. Eleanor : “E.....L....L again.... I...... E....Um, can I get a desk in the shape of a tilde?” Looking puzzled by the request, Blake looks to Kannon who just shrugs. Mitchell : “Of course.” Tracing her finger over an imaginary tilde shaped desk, she makes an exclamation point out of the North wall and bounces of the spot, clapping. Eleanor : “I spelled my name! YAY ME!” The Architect arches his eyebrows as Xavier gives him a ‘Happens all the time’ look. Nodding in acknowledgement, Blake then rolls the plan back up and gestures his departure to the couple. Mitchell : “Well, I have some builders to liase with, you know where I’ll be.” After saying their goodbyes, the trio become just a pair and the now calm Ellie~! is embraced by her man, the pair looking at the Tower’s skeleton being constructed before them. Xavier : “Goodbye New Jersey, hello Hubbardville.” The little prep rests her head on Xavier’s shoulder as the shot fades to Black on the couple staring up at the soon to be new PCW offices contrasting against the cold Blue Hubbardville sky. “Hub....... Bard...... Ville! YAY HUBBARD!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
