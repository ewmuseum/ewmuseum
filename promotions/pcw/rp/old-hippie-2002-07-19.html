Old Hippie

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-19-02 He turned thirty-five last SundayIn his hair he found some grayBut he still ain't changed his lifestyleHe likes it better the old waySo he grows a little garden in the back yard by the fenceHe's consuming what he's growing nowadays in self defenseHe get's out there in the twilight zoneSometimes when it just don't make no sense -The scene opens up on the home of the Malice Man in Missoula, Montana. Duke is wearing a buckwheat t-shirt and a pair of sleeping pants. He stands in front of the mirror where he’s trimming up his beard.- Williams: What the f*ck is this? -Duke cuts a some gray out of his whiskers.- Williams: Damn it you bastard!! You can take away my speed, skills, and sexual stanima but nobody f*cks with my hair. You hear me you stupid f*ck! -Duke shakes a fist at God.- Williams: What can you do? I need to accept the things I can not change. Wait a minute I can change this. Nothing a little hair dye can’t fix. Hosssssssssssssssssss. -Duke walks over to the kitchen and grabs a bowl and fills it with fruit loops. He then grabs a home grown joint from his stash. He sits down at the table and gobbles down some fruit loops as he puffs the stuff. He is looking over the booking schedule for PCW.- Williams: That dipshit!! I see Nelson wants to play with the Malice Man. Test his will. A match with Clyde and Darkstar. Two on one. I can handle it hoss. Just make sure that ref keeps his nose out of my business. This will not be a wrestling match it will be a fight. I will do all I have to do to rip the skin off of these pricks and throw them into the salt pool. -Duke takes a big hit and stares off into the light.- Williams: Darkstar congrats on winning the gold. I bet you think your hot shit now don’t you? How’s it going to feel when you become the shortest lived Continental champion in the history of PCW? Do you understand the ramifications your about to face? Do you understand how bad I want that gold back around my waist. What I will put myself through to get it. More importantly what I will put you through to get it. Dark Ages ooohh damn I’m scared. You guys are freaks. We aren’t afraid of freaks we laugh at them. We put them on display in the circus and the Jerry Springer show. So come at me with your eyeball rings cus I’ll rip them out. I don’t understand you but I don’t need to when your laid out on the mat. He gets off on country musicCause disco left him coldHe's got young friends into new waveButs he's just too friggin oldAnd he dreams at night of WoodstockAnd the day John Lennon diedHow the music made him happyAnd the silence made him cryYea he thinks of John sometimesAnd he has to wonder why -Duke puts the dirty bowl in the sink as he looks over at a photo of him and the late Waylon Jennings. A sad look crosses his face. As he remembers the good times.- Williams: Yea know what the problem is hoss? The music today is all about hate and violence. It’s all about disassociating yourself from society. Become a freak and your cool. Well daddy let me tell you that’s not the way its works. Cool is not a ring in your nipple it’s a state of mind. A sense of control. Slick like a New York City Pimp. You jive kid? Look at all the fans I have. You know why I have them Darkstar? Cus they know I’m cool. I don’t strive for it, it just comes to me. -Duke snaps his fingers and mellows out.- Williams: Darkstar let me lay down the cold hard truth for you. When I fired you which was an enjoyable experience. I made a mistake. You stood for something. You were an unsung hero to the fans who didn’t have one. The freaks who left the realm of reality into the world of Manson and metal. They need a strong confident leader. They don’t trust authority and there under the wacko understanding that our great government is corrupt. At least with you they had a role model. -Duke rubs his nose with his thumb.- Williams: You see Darkstar I’m much better then you. I’m smarter, better looking, and wiser. Those kids should listen to me and they would be all right. But we both know they wouldn’t. They are programmed by the scum ass music infiltrating out youth’s minds. Programmed with the code of Satan himself The code of rebellion and anger. -Duke sits back down.- Williams: You could have influenced those kids in a positive manor and you did. However, when the deck was turned against you. You did like every other kid in the new millennium. You blamed somebody else. You can’t take your own blame. Pass the buck it’s the MTV generation trend. You my friend are a sad site. Not only did you take the low childish path. You did what everybody else has done to those kids. -Duke claps his hands then throws them in the air. Williams: You sold out kid. -Duke smiles.- Williams: Your principle and what you stand for are now officially fucked! Right in the ass kid. Malice Style hoss! Bang-bing-boom its over. I’ve talked to these kids and all they talk about is selling out. When a band changes their art to better their career. You know what I’m talking hoss I’ve heard you say it. Everybody preaches about how bad selling out is. I’ve heard the sermons from your mouth Darkstar. Then you sell out yourself. HA what a hypocrite. -Duke laughs hard.- Williams: What’s it like to be a puppet for the system? What’s it like to be Nelsons tool. Tell me? Tell your fans why you work with in the system. Why the independent leagues couldn’t satisfy you? Tell us hoss why you sold out? If you take a shit in my glass of water that’s pretty bad. But hoss you’re like a shit in the ocean it don’t matter.
