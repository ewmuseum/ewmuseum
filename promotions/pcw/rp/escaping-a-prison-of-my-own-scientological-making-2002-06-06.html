Escaping A Prison Of My Own Scientological Making.

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/06/02 He won the chance before March Madness but for a second he took his eye off the ball... His shot at the PCW World Title ended in 3 minutes and 8 seconds.... Those 3 minutes and 8 seconds would end up destroying PCW’s oldest stable, Asylum from the inside as Kannon took out his frustrations on Jeremy Howard, the man who had done most to help him. But this Tuesday, within the confines of a Steel Cage he again fights for a shot at the PCW World Title.... And could the opponent be any more fitting than Miyagawa.... The oldest serving member of Asylum and best friend of Jeremy Howard.... However, Kannon’s desire to hurt Miyagawa at Turmoil has nothing to do with Asylum or the Krayzie one.... This match is about Ellie~! On Tuesday, Miya handed over his Extreme Title to Eleanor Hall, valet of Xavier for seemingly no reason... Until the Japo-American pointed out as Champion Ellie~! would then have to fight one half of Politically Incorrect in a no interference match... Kannon, not one to like being duped, is out for revenge and at Turmoil he will have that chance.... But will he drop the ball again? “We are the Cha-...... I am the Champion my fri-..... Ellie~!..... And I’ll keep on figh-.... That song sucks....” Our shot opens up on... Well, not much actually as we are shown a dark room. The small amount of light filtering through the blinds shows a King-size bed where a couple are shown slumbering... And from the tired, breathless mutterings from one of them it can be assumed that they are Eleanor Hall, PCW’s Extreme Champion and Xavier Kannon. Eleanor : “I’m Extreme... I’m Extreme... I’m like So Extreme....” As the Preppy valet tosses and turns, talking softly in her sleep we start to zoom in on the bed just as the Moon moves from behind cloud cover who throw pale Blue light upon the top half of the bed, showing the couple’s heads clearly. Eleanor : “I....... SO.... Rule....” With these last words she stops fidgeting and settles down, finally comfortable and at ease so she can drift off into deeper sleep... ...With the new-found light we get closer and close to Xavier’s face, which even in his sleep bares a cocky grin. He reaches up and pulls the pillow further under his head then rolls over to drape an arm over his valet as the camera shot cuts accordingly to keep his face in focus. ....We zoom in further, now only showing the bridge of his nose, his closed eyelids that shield his bright Green eyes and the strands of loose Auburn hair that fall over them. The face of the Scientologist then screws up and his lips part for a brief second to softly speak in a bemused voice... Xavier : “Huh?” With these words the camera shows only bright White for a seconds or two before we see Kannon again, standing against an all White background dressed in his wrestling attire of Black leather trousers, Black elbow pads and White & Black striped wrist tape... ...Unlike before where his hair was falling over his face it is now tied neatly back and his bright Green eyes are no longer closed in slumber but are open and looking all around him. Xavier : “Where in Hubbard’s name.....” He starts to wander, and before his eyes the White floor starts to change colour, gradually getting darker until it becomes the Blue colour of the PCW ring.... When he looks up the entire ground, as far as his bemused eyes can see is that colour.... Xavier : “I really shouldn’t eat cheese before I go to bed.... It must be being digested by my Scientological digestive system and playing around with my Scientological synapses.....” He turns on the spot, looking around 360 all around him but seeing nothing on the horizon... Xavier : “Wow.... This is as bleak and desolate as The Phantom Phaggot’s personalities... No, wait... There is technically something of substance here...” Sighing heavily the Gold Patron Meritorious just picks a direction and starts to wander, deciding he’s at least better go somewhere... Xavier : “My Scientological brain says this most Un-Scientological direction.. Towards that... Horizony thing....” After 10 or so strides, the sound of metal grinding on metal is head and a huge THUD resonates.... Xavier : “In Xenu’s name...” Around him a Steel Cage drops down, imprisoning him in 4 walls of Steel Mesh that cut him off from the bleak and featureless landscape.... The ground shakes, nearly knocking Kannon off his feet and causing him to quickly shift his weight to stay on his feet. Xavier : “Oh Hubbard... Please don’t let this be symbolism?” The Gold Patron Meritorious walks over to the cage and shakes it with both hands, feeling that it is indeed real and not merely a figment of his imagination..... Well, technically it IS in his imagination because it’s a dream... Or a vision... Or a...Meh, just get a thesaurus and make your own up... Xavier : “I’m in a Cage match.... A Cage drops around me.... I thought my Scientological brain could think of something a little better than that.... Maybe the Cage falling on Miyagawa, less relevant but far more entertaining....” He takes a defeatist kick at the cage, watching the mesh shake with the impact before turning and walking to the other side and shaking it again... Xavier : “Hmmm.... If this is really a projection of my Scientological thoughts and anxieties then shouldn’t Miyagawa and Ellie~! be here to resolve the whole “He gives her the Title so that she has to fight Reginald Lovecraft in a no interference match....” “You’d think....” These words cause Kannon to dart around, scared, to see Miyagawa standing behind him in a rather relaxed dress of Hawaiian shirt and sunglasses... Miyagawa : “But with me turning up in your RP would make it a “Multi-Person” RP and we all know they suck.... Plus if we were really in a cage then you’d probably want to hit me in advance for your valet being killed at Fusion!” The Scientologist growls at Miyagawa’s prophesy. Miyagawa : “Any way, I’m off to get back to my dream which was a whole lot more entertaining than this... Say “Hi” to Hubbard for me....” As Miya disappears in a puff of smoke his form is replaced by the more slender one of Eleanor Hall, proudly displaying the Extreme Title on her shoulder as she yawns... Eleanor : “Like... In my OWN dream...” She waves and also disappears, leaving a small waft of smoke to fade out in front of the cage and for Kannon to rolls his eyes back and wander around in the cage some more.... Xavier : “Okay.... I’m in a Cage match with Miyagawa... I get that, and to win in a Cage match you have to... His eyes light up as he catches on. Xavier : “....Climb the Cage!” He takes one step towards the mesh wall then leaps up onto the side and starts to scale it but as he looks up his Green eyes widen and his jaws drop when he sees the Cage stretches up as far as he can see.... Xavier : “Hey.... This Cage isn’t regulation size!!!” He drops back down to the floor and hammers his fists into the Cage, looking all around him and walking back and forth, the confinement and boredom starting to get to him... “I AM THE VOICE OF L RON HUBBARD....” The face of the Scientologist contorts, puzzled as a huge voice echoes all around him, even making the mesh rattle all around him. “ON TUESDAY YOU CAN MAKE A GIANT STRIDE FOR SCIENTOLOGY WHEN YOU WIN A SHOT AT THE PCW WORLD TITLE.... AND I KNOW THIS BECAUSE I AM L RON HUBBARD!!!” The Scientologist covers his ears as the voice booms again, blowing his Auburn hair back. As hands are removed from ears, Kannon’s puzzled face looks right up, between the sky touching walls of the Cage and into the distance... Xavier : “No you aren’t.” “ER....I AM TOO!!!!!” Xavier : “John..... I recognise your voice... I have seen all of your films from Grease to Domestic Disturbance...” “SOMEBODY ACTUALLY WATCHED THAT?” Xavier : “Unfortunately... So give it up... I know you aren’t Hubbard so what are you doing pretending to be him in my dream sequence?” “ASK YOURSELF, IT’S YOUR DREAM... I WAS BUSY SOCKING IT TO KEL-” The Scientologist screws his face up and covers his ears, the resistance to what John was about to announce enough to eliminate the actor from his subconscious thoughts. Xavier : “Damn the “Reactive” Mind to Utah.... Urgh, I can’t believe I am locked in a prison of my own Scientological making.... And I’d only just fallen asleep too, I’m going to be stuck here for hours.... Unless...” He drops down and sits cross legged in the centre of the Cage, steadying his breathing and claming himself down... .... For the next 2 minute he does nothing other than slow his breathing then speaks... Xavier : “Right arm.... Up........... And Down.....” A few seconds after he says “Down” we hear a dull thud from nowhere followed y a soft moan from Eleanor... “.....Get...... Off.....” His face breaks into a smile as he realises it is working, using his own “Reactive” Mind to control his won physical body.... Which is some Scientologist’s thing... Honest... Xavier : “Right arm Up........ Left........ And Down!” Briefly cutting back to the bed we see Kannon’s right arm lifting up and moving left to hover over Eleanor’s head before dropping down onto it... Knocking Ellie~! out of her sleep, something she doesn’t appreciate as we return to Xavier’s dreamscape. “Eugh... Fine, take THAT!!!” The Dreamscape begins to crumble as we cut to the real world where Ellie~! lines her right leg up and kicks back towards the Scientologist’s groin..... and although the covers mask whether it hit the target or not, Kannon’s face says it all. Xavier : “ARGHHHHH!!!!” He reaches down and grabs his package, his face screwed up in pain then he rolls right and off the bed, landing with a dull thud on the floor where he rolls up into the foetal position, wincing in pain. On the bed Ellie~! grins to herself, rubbing her hands together. Eleanor : “Like....SO... Extreme!!!” With Kannon on the floor she pulls all the covers over to her side and gets comfortable before drifting back to sleep while Xavier’s pained voice is head from the other side, whispering as the shot fades to Black... “Thank........ You...... Urgh, now I know how Aymin felt...”
