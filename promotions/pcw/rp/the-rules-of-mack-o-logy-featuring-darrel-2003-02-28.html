The Rules of Mack-O-Logy featuring Darrel

Title: The Rules of Mack-O-Logy featuring Darrel Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 2-28-2003 ..::/::..Our story begins not quite how we left off, as we now find the handsome young black man from our last part, standing behind a milk white screen. He stands tall, adorned in his golden silk robe while rubbing the neatly shaved goatee on his chin. He cracks a tempting grin, looking to the camera while rubbing his hands and saying..::/::.. Darrel:”Wassup…you may know me as Darrel….you know, Rent-A-Hero’s “player” friend if you will. Now last time you ummm…what’s he call his audience again?” Narrator: -Speaking from above- ”Rent-Heads young man….” Darrel:”Yea…yea, right. Anyways “Rent-Heads”, my boy wanted to learn the ways of the “mack”. He wanted to know what it took to have women fallin’ to their knees begging to LICK HIS BOOTS. Now…I’m not saying I don’t have what it takes to teach him and everything….BUT LOOK AT HIM? I mean, look what I got to work with?!”..::/::..A huge picture of Rent-A-Hero, striking a Hulk Hogan-esque muscle pose while wrapped in a Flash replica costume, appears on the white screen behind Darrel as he steps aside for the world to get a good look. Darrel simply shakes his head in shame, looking back to the audience..::/::..Darrel:”He’s whiter then Michael Jackson, cornier than a Kansas corn field, and the guy thinks you need to APPLY for a blow job!! Needless to say…I have agreed to take on the heavy task of making him a player. I won’t give up….well…until “Hits From The Streets” comes on, can’t miss mah BET. Anyways, on with the MACK-O-LOGY….pay attention, cause I won’t be making bootleg copies of this lata. Without further ado….ROLL THAT BEAUTIFUL MACK FOOTAGE!” Mack-O-Logy 69-The Preface ..::/::..The footage rolls on the white screen as we find Darrel and Rent-A-Hero inside what looks to be the average 5th grade classroom, complete with blackboard and the wide, wooden desk with an apple sitting on the very edge. There is only one desk, and sitting in it is our hero. He watches on attentively as Darrel is standing at the front of the classroom in one of his trademark silk robes, a piece of red chalk in hand as he looks to our hero..::/::..Darrel:”Welcome to Mack-O-Logy 69, I am your instructor, Darrel. Now you might ask….”..::/::..Rent-A-Hero raises his hand with excitement, looking to get a question of his chest obviously as Darrel calls on him. Our hero stands up like a good student and asks..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Umm, not to be rude Darrel, but do you happen to have a last name? I mean I know mine is “A-Hero”, but geez….I’ve NEVER known your last name.”Darrel: -sighs and replies in sarcasm- “My last name is “Nuts” Rent, and my middle name starts with a “D”…don’t make me say it.”Rent-A-Hero: -sits down while mumbling- “Geez…what kind of last name is that?”Darrel: -turning back to the chalkboard- “Now as I was saying. Most people ask me, ”Darrel can I suck your dick?” Now when they’re not asking me THAT they’re asking, “Darrel, what is Mack-O-Logy?” Well…it’s quite simple. Mack-O-Logy is the study of getting fine ass broads to be jocking your stuff, nah wa’ I mean?(“Know what I mean?” for the ebonically challenged) These ho’s have got to WANT you, NEED you, CHASE you like a drug or somethin’…nah wa’ I mean?”..::/::..Rent-A-Hero raises his hand, Darrel pointing to him before the polite hero stands attentively. He then asks while scratching his honey blonde hair..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Will you take points off if we “don’t know” what you mean? Cause you say it quite often my Mack-O-Logy master, but I NEVER know what you mean!”Darrel:”Rent….sit your spandex covered ass down fo’ I lose it yo.” Rent-A-Hero: -begrudgingly- “Alright….but I’m only doing it cause my legs are tired.” ..::/::..Darrel simply shakes his head as he uses the red chalk to write on the board. Shortly, a set of rules is written in crimson red upon the chalkboard, Darrel calling each one out as he gets to them..::/::..RULE #1-Never tell a female you love her.Darrel:”Your setting yourself up for failure here playa if ya do that. The girl gets all attached…you can’t play the field no mo’, and your on lockdown for God know’s how long. Try to find ANY OTHER way to express feeling for her other then that.”..::/::..Rent-A-Hero reluctantly raises his hand, Darrel clenching his teeth as he acknowledges our hero to ask his question…::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Umm….about this “playing the field”…what system do I play it on, and is this “field” an import game, because I’ve never heard of it.” ..::/::..Darrel is lost for words at the mere stupidity of Rent-A-Hero at the moment. He simply opens up the drawer on his desk, pulling out a roll of moving tape before walking off screen. ..::/::..!!~ZZZZZZZZZZZZZCTH~!! !!~BITCH-SLAP~!! !!~ZCCCCCCCTCH~!! ..::/::..The camera pans over to where the sounds of carnage took place, finding our hero’s arms taped to the desk with the silver moving tape and a red hand print across his cheek as Darrel whistles, spinning the roll of tape about his index finger. He puts it away under the desk then continues with his lesson..::/::..RULE#2-NEVER…EVER EVER EVER EVER EVER….CALL A GIRL BY THE WRONG NAME! Darrel:”That’s just asking for trouble. Being a player, you have to have all your girls in check and MAKE SURE you know all their names. Calling “Sara” by “Tonya” will almost certainly end up with you getting your ramrod chopped son.”RULE#3-Never spend the nightDarrel:”You hit, then you quit. Just like a 9 to 5, you punch in and you punch out my boy. Stay over and it’s gonna get all serious….and spending the night may lead to….-shudders-…..CUDDLING!!” –holds back the urge to throw up-..::/::..Our hero waves his left pinky in the air, the only part of either of his hands not taped down to an extreme by Darrel. Darrel pulls the tape from under his desk, preparing to taper him up yet again if the question is not to his liking..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Umm…..what’s the harm in cuddling Darrel…I mean, it feels good. That and it makes you and her all nice and warm and stuff. What’s so evil about it?”..::/::..Darrel looks spooked in the face, slowly inching towards Rent-A-Hero until he is right in his face. The usually cool and collected mack looks fearful as he delves into the evil that is…cuddling..::/::..Darrel:”Oh…so you think cuddling is COOL huh? What if I told you that the moment you let that girl get that soft embrace…and she wraps her leg over yours and squeezes tight…THAT YOUR TRAPPED IN A WEB OF STALKING AND CLINGING!!! THE MOMENT YOU CUDDLE, TTHAT BI**H’LL BE CALLING YOU AND PAGING YOU 9-1-1 ALL THE TIME, SHOWIN’ UP TO YOUR DOOR WITH BLOCKBUSTER MOVIES AND WANTING YOU TO HOLD HANDS IN THE MALL…WEARING’ SWEATERS FROM JCPENNY AND SH**!!! DON’T DO IT RENT…DO IT AND YOU’VE SOLD YOUR SOUL TO THE DEVIL ITSELF!!!”-finally calming down, snapping from his trance- “But other then that, I wouldn’t be too worried.”..::/::..Rent-A-Hero is clinging to his seat, scared out of his mind at Darrel’s description of a common and romantic pastime by couples. Darrel starts to peel back the tape from Rent-A-Hero’s hands…looking to his Kryptonian mack in training as he says..::/::.. Darrel:”Now you know the rules of engagement….it’s now time we got you prepared.” Rent-A-Hero:”WIZZER! Am I gonna have to challenge FIVE flaming mack’s in three piece suits? Or maybe I can slay a set of warrior women to prove my mastery of my newfound mack knowledge?” ..::/::..Darrel crushes Rent-A-Hero’s wide-eyed hopes with a simple open-palmed slap to the face that echoes through the classroom, peeling back more tape as our hero winces in pain..::/::.. Darrel:”You REALLY gotta give me the name of your supplier…whatever your smokin’ MUST be good. Anyways, we gotta get you the right gear man. Your gonna go from wearing spandex and masks….to rockin’ Tim’s and wearing Sean John. To be a mack, you gotta wear the uniform.” Rent-A-Hero:”I mean, that’s all good and well and everything my good player…but WHY WOULD I WANT TO WEAR ANOTHER MAN ON ME?! I’m sure this “Sean John” wouldn’t appreciate you wanting me to wear him.” Darrel: -looking to the camera- “Yo….you may wanna’ fade out right about now. I’m about to smack this foo’ like Tina Turner at a “Wifebeaters Anonymous” meeting.”..::/::..As requested, the scene fades, sounds of hard smacking filling the air as our hero can be heard screaming..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”HOLY BIZNITCH SLAPS!! MAKE IT STOP….MAKE IT STOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOP!!!”.::/::..With that said, the quest continues to turn Rent-A-Hero from full-time superhero…to a living, breathing mack master. WILL RENT-A-HERO DISCOVER THE WAYS OF THE MACK?! WILL HE STOPPED GETTING SMACKED LIKE A BIOTCH?! IS CUDDLING REALLY A SIN?!
