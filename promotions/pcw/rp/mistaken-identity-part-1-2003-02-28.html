Mistaken Identity (part 1)

28 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/03 It had been several days since Kramer had last contacted Brianna. Instantly she became worried that something happened to him, because he had been so adamant about contacting her somehow each day, ever since he left Grimer behind. Though she could never figure out how he got her number or address (since she wasn’t listed), she was always sort of happy to receive calls or packages from him because it brought her to a whole new world of mystery and danger. She could never figure out if that was more of a good thing or bad thing however. The sun was looking just about ready to set as a figure came barreling down the sidewalk in a jogging suit with a pair of headsets on. It was Brianna, who must have been out and running for a while due to the huge amount of sweat pouring down her smooth face. As she ran her body shifted in an even motion as her hair swung back and forth like a pendulum in the tight ponytail. She rounded the corner sluggishly as the workout was starting to take its toll. Instead of stopping she kept on the path of the sidewalk letting the soothing music be her push to keep going. As she goes further down the road she has to dodge a game of cops and robbers being played by a group of younger children. A police car coming from the opposite direction stops as Brianna keeps on jogging. “Ma’am.” a chubbier balding male cop said in Brianna’s direction. Of course Brianna doesn’t hear as music is blasting in her ears. The cops take this as a sign of disrespect so they walk closer. The fat cop’s partner, a fitter, younger black man, grabs Brianna by the shoulder softly, which causes her to jolt around a bit frightened. Brianna removes her headphones resting them across her neck and sizing up the two officers trying to figure out what they want. “Ma’am we are going to have to ask you to come with us to the police station.” the older man says in a wheezy yet bold tone. “What for? Is something wrong?” Brianna replies getting really worked up here. “It will all be explained at the station ma’am.” The younger man throws out gripping her lightly by the arm and directing her across the street to the parked cruiser. Having seen enough cop shows, she knows not to resist so she begrudgingly follows and takes a seat in the back. The cruiser takes off down the street with the black guy at the wheel. Several minutes of pure silence pass by before the car parks besides others exactly like it. They get out and open the door for Brianna, and as they are walking in Brianna notices the younger officer following behind her. Maybe he is thinking she might try to take off, but why? They travel down a long white corridor with cold steel benches places along the walls. The hall comes to an end and they all veer to the right to a room which is opened. The two male officers escort Brianna to a chair that is set behind a hard wood table then leave. “What in the world is going on?” Brianna quietly asks. Before her mind can wander anymore, a tall, slender, middle aged man with a dark black mustache comes in with rolled up shirt sleeves and a manila folder clutched in his grip. The look he shoots at Brianna before taking a seat in a chair on the other side of the table sends a chill down her spine. “Officer Riley and Marx said you were confused as to why you were here.” he says with no emotion at all. “Yes sir. And I think it’s time I got that answer.” “Look you can play games all you want but you know why you’re here and I wanna know why you did it. They don’t pay me enough to stay here with you all day so fess up now so we can both go on our merry little ways….or well I will at least.” he snickers tossing the manila folder to the table, sitting back in the chair with arms crossed over his chest. “That’s the problem! I don’t know why I’m here.” It’s obviously not the reply he’s looking for which is evident as he uncrosses his arms and sits at the edge of his seat staring a hole right through Brianna. “You want to play the “I don’t know” game do you? Well Ms. Emerson let me tell you something right now….” As soon as she hears the last name she knows it’s just a case of mistaken identity so she interrupts the man instantly. “My name is not Ms. Emerson, it’s Brianna Washington. This is just all a misunderstanding.” she says vying for his trust in her words. “Like I was saying before you rudely interrupted. I know how you criminals think and operate. I have put up with responses such as yours for years and it just never changes. I would have figured you of all people would have just fessed up considering the huge amount of evidence stacked against you.” saying this he points to the folder which Brianna eyeballs a minute. “I am telling you that is not me.” Brianna protests growing irritated with his inability to believe what she says. “Ok look in the folder.” he picks it up and slides it across the table to Brianna he stops it with her left hand and opens it up. Brianna stares down at the mass of paper looking at various police reports. Armed robbery, murder, what? They think I did all of this? This is just…just absurd. She flips through a couple more pages and comes across some pictures of the murder and robbery scenes. Then she moves those papers out of the way and comes across a composite sketch that a witness had submitted. Sure enough it looked almost identical to Brianna! Still not believing her eyes she turned the pages yet again coming across a photo from what looks like a security camera. There a person is with a black hat, and black suit on with Brianna’s exact facial features. Brianna starts to tremble as she shuts the folder and stares down blankly at the table. No wonder this guy things I did it. All the signs point to me. I mean a picture does say a thousand words after all. But what happens if I am convicted of all of these horrendous crimes and have to face jail time? My life will be ruined! My career at PCW will be ruined, and I would have never experienced the self pleasure in winning a title. I suppose I shouldn’t really be worrying about my match on Fusion right now, more on how I can convince everyone that I am just not this person! Tears threaten to surface in her eyes, but she fights them back looking up at the officer who is giving her an icy stare. There is something about his presence and way he presented himself to Brianna that just doesn’t make sense in her mind. Something about him just seemed phony. As Brianna and the man continue with a stare down the scene slowly fades to black. To Be Continued…..
