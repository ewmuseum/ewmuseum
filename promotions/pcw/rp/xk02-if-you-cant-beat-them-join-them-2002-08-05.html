XK02 : “If You Can’t Beat Them. Join Them.”

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/02 XK02 : “If You Can’t Beat Them. Join Them.” Location : Kannon-Hall Residence, Hubbardville, Wisconsin.Date : Monday 5th August, 2:35 am. “Urgh... Goths.. Goths... Hate... Goths.... Hate ‘em... Gonna hate me a Goth....” Our shot opens up on the place, lightly freckled face of the PCW Commissioner Eleanor Hall deep in slumber. Her light complexion is highlighted by a haunting Blue coming off the Hubbardville moon as she mumbles from barely parted, full lips. Eleanor : “Goth... Make Ellie~! Red... Red SO not in this season... Or last.... Or next...” Despite her mumbling about Fusion’s incident during which she was covered from head to toe in fake blood by the Dark Age, she looks at peace. No tossing. No turning. On Friday she was instantly able to put the thoughts of the attack out of her mind. The adrenaline that was pumped through her combated any after effects and following a quick shower and a change into one of the five or so other outfits she brings to shows, she was ready to walk out with her man into the cauldron of hate that was the Reunion Arena. Eleanor : “Ellie~!.... Hate.... Goths!” She lets out a light. breathless sigh and smiles in her sleep at her last statement. The camera zooms out from her eerily lit face to show that she is alone in her marital bed, only a navy Blue silk sheet covering from her lower back to her upper thigh, the pale young skin of her figure revealed and highlighted in bright White and Blue tints just like her face. The shot once again zooms in closer to her face, just a few strands of her Brown hair, highlighted with Blond falling out of place down her cheeks. She mumbles more. More Goth hating. But the more she hates the more she thinks of the Goths. The more she starts to relive the trauma. The adrenaline long past and the slow, sedated shock rearing it’s ugly head to the little prep. Eleanor : “Goths.... Goths.... Blood...” For once her sleep looks less peaceful and she stars to squirm around, the sheet migrating south before she reaches in her sleep, unconsciously and pulls the soft fabric up over her once more, wrapping herself in the silk as she starts to toss more, her eyes screwing up and her head pushing back into the mattress, trying to get it away from something that only exists within it. Eleanor : “Ellie~!.... Ha-....Goth.... Ellie~! Goth....” With these words her eyes slowly open, their wide, Bambi-like pale Blue eyes replaced with darkened Orbs... Deep Brown irises and dilate, wide Black pupils like black holes. She slowly sits up, pulling the sheet up around her to cover her 32B breasts and she pivots on her pert bottom, turning so she sits on the edge of the bed, her slender frame not even causing one squeak or creek that would cut through the Hubbardville night air. Eleanor : “Feeling.... Weir-... Sinister.” Clamping the top of the sheet under her chin, the prep rubs her darker than natural eyes with both hands to help her wake up and she once more grabs the sheet and ties it around herself before getting up and walking over to the mirror which houses below it her large, extensive make-up collection. The Cosmetics value more than most people’s cars... Colours for every occasion... But this time she will only be looking for one look. With the adrenaline leading up to that night’s main event, and the jubilation afterwards when she was installed as Commissioner, all thoughts of the Dark Age’s attack are free to penetrate her thoughts.. The vacant space that best describes her memory now houses the very feelings she had when the band of Goths doused her in the sticky, dark Red mixture. Eleanor : “Hmmm, Black methinks.” She goes straight for the Black eye shadow and flicks on the small lamp that is fixed above the mirror, lighting the path for her to apply the cosmetic, which she does liberally, making her eyes Blackened all around them, cast in deep shadow. When she has finished with the she scans along one of the many rows before her, looking for one thing amongst the hundreds before locking her now dark eyes upon it.. Long lash effect Black mascara. She unscrews the lit and barely bothers to remove the excess before resisting blinking as she runs the brush along her upper and lower eyelashes, giving them a deep colour, greater length and seemingly more volume. Eleanor : “Looking.... Like a Go-... Like how I want to.” The mascara is returned and a deep red lipstick now finds it’s way into her petite right hand and leaning in closer to the mirror, the Crimson shade is applied to her full bodied lips. Eleanor : “Mmmmmmmmmmm-Wha!” She puckers up to the mirror, admiring her handiwork and returns the lipstick to it’s place before pulling the pin from her hair, sending the wavy locks down. Reaching for the closest bristle brush she can, the prep then begins to brush her mane with purpose, her hair losing it’s natural waviness until it is perfectly straight, falling down around the sides of her pale face, casting more shadow around it to give her a dark, mysterious.. Sinister look in the early morning moonlight. As she looks out from her shadowed visage with her deep, blackened eyes she turns to make her way to her huge walk-in wardrobe, each step not making the slightest of a creak on the soft carpeted floor. A quiet noise from one of the adjoining rooms fails to get her attention, but it attracts that of the camera. *CLINK* The sound of glass on glass ushers us into the living room of the Kannon-Hall residence where the PCW World Heavyweight Champion is seen at the drinks cabinet, pouring a measure of Cognac into a large, bulbous glass. There are no lights on in this room either, just the bay windows to let in the moonlight, brighter than ever in the clear, pollution free Hubbardville sky. The pale light helps to brings out the colours of Kannon’s form... The blanket to White and Blue lightening his skin and bringing out his piercing Green eyes and flowing Red locks. Xavier : “The Title.... 50% of PCW... The Commissioner... Why would I need to go to Slam!” On the drinks cabinet beside the Cognac bottle is his unstamped ticket to OSW Slam! the TV show of PCW’s closest rival, it has been crumpled in the Gold Patron Meritorious’ hand and just seemingly discarded... A memento of one of his more dastardly acts, threatening to drop to World Title on the competitor’s TV show unless he got what he wanted. As always. He got what he wanted. Not only did he pin Tyler nelson 1.. 2.. 3 in the middle of the ring, his wife is now the Commissioner. In his Scientological mind the dousing his wife took has also been forced to the back, along with his emotions such as compassion, deep out of thought... More for her than him. Not wanting to say anything that would remind her of the incident. Too late. Xavier : “It’s just all too easy... Where is the fun in gambling if you know you are going to win. What I need is for Hubbard to throw me an obstacle to overcome.. A true test for my Scientological body and mind.... Something the Dark Age are not..” He swirls the liquor around the glass and breathes in it’s bouquet before taking a sip, muttering to himself in pure arrogance. The arrogance of a man who has had it all his own way. The arrogance of a man who is free to do what he pleases. The arrogance of a man who will never have to face any repercussions. The arrogance of a man who is creating new sins with every hour that passes. Xavier : “It feel like Snow isn’t the only member of our team incarcerated... I too am locked inside a prison of my own success.. Hoping the key comes to me in a great Scientological challenge.. or ANYTHING to break me free from the sedation... MY sedation of having everything my way.” With Kannon it seems there is always something to complain about it. A mind that is incapable of knowing true inner peace even through the science of Dianetics. No amount of auditing and no E-Meter developed will ever gave Xavier Kannon a clear mind. It isn’t in his genetic make up. And speaking of make-up. “Eh-Hum...” From the doorway from the hall the sound of a female softly clearing her throat is heard and Kannon’s head slowly turns to see what he knows is his wife.. But now as he knows her. *SMASH* The glass of Cognac shatters on the varnished floor in front of the drinks cabinet, taking the same route south that the Scientologist’s jaw has as he stares in disbelief at the form of Ellie~!... The Goth Generation. Xavier : “Holy Hubbard in the sky with Xenu...” The prep had not stopped at the make-up... She is now donned out in a Black velvet corset, encasing her torso tightly and pushing her bust up.. Couples with this she wears a Black leather skirt, only showing a few inches of her pale flash before a pair of Black leather boots cut them off above the knee. She looks right at her husband from the shadows cast over her face from her straightened heir, a Black choker now present around her throat. The prep then strides towards her husband who is standing in absolute shock. Xavier : “I ask for a challenge.. And you never disappoint me Hubbard.” His Green eyes to not leave her body as she walks... All be it rather shakily in the dangerously high heels of the boots.. Towards him. The brain of Eleanor Kannon-Hall... Ellie~!.. Doesn’t quite work the same as most women’s. After all, who else could love both Xavier and the things he does... So within it there are certain safeguards.. Quirky attributes that most of the time make her even more adorable in the eyes of the Scientologist.. And then there are times like this. We’ve seen it before when she caught Preppylitus after not insulting enough people.. Now, with the thoughts of Friday, and more importantly, the idea, design and realisation of Goths in her mind they have invaded her subconscious in her sleep and taken form. If you can’t beat them. Join them. And now Kannon must beat them for her. Xavier : “My little Fuck Ferret... What have they done to you?” She reaches him and immediately wraps her arms around him, pressing her full Red lips against his pale, thin pair and with her hands, finger hails painted jet Black, she slides the Scientologist’s silk shirt from his shoulder and pulls his reluctant arms around her as she massages his tongue with her own. The Scientologist pulls away. Fearing what his wife has become. Repulsed by her image. Xavier : “What have they DONE to you?” Her right hand touches his left cheek, her Black nailed fingers stroking down to his stubbled chin, her big, dark eyes looking into his. Eleanor : “Well DUH! I’m, like a GOTH!” Behind his back, the Scientologist’s left hand fumbles around the speed dials on his cellphone.. Out of sight and out of mind from his newly Goth wife. He hits 7, the hits call... They know hear what is said and will know to come. Xavier : “They made you a Goth?” She smiles broadly as she leans in close to his face and licks the tip of his nose then quickly turns and walks away from him, urging him to follow as she backs towards the bedroom with inviting eyes. Eleanor : “Noooooooo... Ellie~! made Ellie~! a Goth...” Her voice then softens and she looks at him through the shadows of her lowered head. Eleanor : “Don’t you like it?” With help on the way, the Scientologist has to play along... He follows the trail of seduction that Ellie~! is leaving him as she backs towards the bedroom, now managing to cope with the high heels of her Black leather boots. She disappears around the corner, the Black velvet corset then being thrown onto the hall floor as Xavier looks up to the ceiling. Xavier : “Xenu, who crossed the cosmos for us... Give me strength as I keep her... Occupied.. Until help arrived.” With a deep breath and a swig from the Cognac bottle, the Gold Patron Meritorious follows his wife out of the living room to wherever and whatever she has planned for him until Xavier’s contacts show up to “Remove” the Goth from her. *Beep Beep* *Beep Beep* *Beep Beep* “Like, SO URGH! Moonlight is replaced by the morning sun rising from the East over Hubbardville and we once again see the PCW Commissioner, Ellie~! This time she is reaching for the snooze button on the alarm clock beside the bed. Beside her, Xavier is undisturbed by the noise and continues his slumber after the exhausting night before. Eleanor : “My ass feels funny...” The prep fidgets, obviously uncomfortable as she tries to get back to sleep she catches a glimpse of her face in the mirror, slightly blurred by the tiredness of her eyes. She then wastes no time in rolling over and smacking Kannon around the head. Xavier: “OWWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!!!!!! WHAT IN HUBBARD’S NAME IS THAT FOR?!?!?” The smack bursts him from his slumber and his eyes dart all around him before settling on Ellie~! who is still wearing half the make-up from the night before.. Seemingly minus the Goth side. Eleanor : “Um.. You, like, SO giving me a Black eye!!!!” Kannon’s head collapses back onto his pillow as he tries to go back to sleep. Xavier : “Long story... Didn’t hit you... Explain later...” With each word he sounds more sleepy until just after he says “Later” his eyes close. next to him Eleanor looks down at him suspiciously before trying to get comfortable herself. Eleanor : “Fine.. Then you can explain why my ass feels so sore too...” As our shot fades out to Black we see Kannon’s face, one eye flicking open, wide and Green than ever as the full events of the night before return to him.
