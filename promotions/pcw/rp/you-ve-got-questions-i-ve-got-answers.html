You've Got Questions? I've Got Answers!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/02 - 4/27/02 (The scene opens in one of many Gold’s Gym out in Houston, Texas with Kid Krazie doing his vigorous routines on the weights. He is working on his legs pressing twice his own weight as some other weight lifters gaze in amazement as they walk past. Krazie begins to grunt and groan in exertion as his leg muscles bulge due to the strain. He screams out as he reaches the final five reps.) Krazie: “COME ON!” (Krazie racks the weight and pins them so they don’t come crashing down as he stands. He grabs his towel and water bottle before he makes his way to the next piece of exercise equipment. Toweling off his sweet and gulping a few swallows of H2O he takes a seat on the plump leather bench for the bench press putting his water bottle and towel on the ground under the bench.) Voice: “Hey…” (Krazie looks behind him and sees a huge muscular guy standing behind the equipment with his hands resting on the bar and a smug look on his face.) Krazie: “…” The guy looks around and then back at Krazie with a confused look on his face.) Voice: “Name’s John, but my friends call me Big J.” (Krazie nods his head still with a stern and determined look on his face.) Big J: “So… how much weight you want?” Krazie: “Three-hundred and thirty five pounds…” (Big J places the appropriate weight on each side evening the weight out as Krazie lays back and gets ready to lift.) Big J: “I never caught your name…” Krazie: “That’s because I never threw it out there!” (Krazie hoists the bar up with a grunt of effort as Big J stands back watching with that same smug look.) Big J: “Four… Five… Six… Seven… Eight… Nine… Ten… Eleven… Twelve… Thirteen… Four… Fourteen… Come on, last one… There ya go!” (Krazie racks the weight with the help of Big J and sits up grabbing his water bottle. He swallows some water and then looks back at Big J who is just standing back with his arms crossed over his chest.) Krazie: “Name is Kid Krazie, friends call me Krazie.” (Big J extends his hand and Krazie takes it and they shake. After the hand shake Big J walks over and sits on the bench next to the one Krazie is sitting on.) Big J: “I’ve never seen you here before…” Krazie: “First time here.” Big J: “Oh really? You trying to get in shape or what?” Krazie: “Nah… I am a wrestler for Primetime Championship Wrestling. Lu-Lu thought it’d be a good idea to train so here I am!” (Big J nods his head as Krazie takes another drink of water.) Big J: “Lu-Lu?” Krazie: “It’s my lover slash manager slash valet slash friend…” Big J: “Oh really?” (He begins to look around as Krazie nods his head.) Big J: “Where is she?” Krazie: “They wouldn’t let me bring her in so I have her waiting outside for me.” (Krazie stands and begins to make his to the next exercise equipment with Big J following like a lost puppy.) Big J: “Wouldn’t let her in??? Why wouldn’t they let her in?” Krazie: “No idea… They said it’d be a safety hazard if I brought her in, but I don’t know why!” Big J: “Damn! She must be hot!” Krazie: “As hot as cold steel can be…” (Big J stops and looks on with confusion as Krazie begins setting up for the next exercise. He shakes his head telling himself he’ll worry about it later as he sprints over to get in position to spot Krazie as he is ready to begin lifting.) Big J: “Here we go… One… Two… (blah blah blah blah) Good job!” (Krazie racks the weight and gets to his feet stretching as Big J comes round and stands next to Krazie looking at him as he stretches.) Krazie: “Well, I’m done!” Big J: “Really? That’s it?” Krazie: “I’ve been here for two hours, I think I’ve lifted enough!” (Krazie walks to the men’s locker room not even noticing that Big J is right behind him. As Krazie gets ready to shower he finally notices a naked Big J standing behind him also getting ready to shower.) Big J: “Oh! No… don’t think I am following you… I… uhm… I have to hit the showers too, you know… You know what I mean!?!?” (Krazie turns around and walks into the shower as Big J follows him in. Krazie proceeds to take a shower two showerheads away from Big J who for some reason keeps looking over Krazie’s way. The scene fades as Krazie continues to shower trying to forget the presence of the wandering eyes Big J.) ****DON’T DROP THE SOAP KRAZIE!!!**** (As the scene re-opens with Krazie and Lu-Lu, the inanimate shopping cart, racing to their apartment in their supped up ’32 Ford Coupe. Krazie can’t seem to sit still as he keeps fidgeting and squirming behind the wheel as they weave in and out of traffic.) Lu-Lu: “Are we in a hurry?” Krazie: “And the answer to that is… ‘When you’re sitting on the potAnd you feel something hotDiarrhea! (Insert Fart Noise) Diarrhea! (Insert Fart Noise) When you’re standing against the wallAnd you feel something fallDiarrhea! (Insert Fart Noise) Diarrhea! (Insert Fart Noise)’” (After a life threatening twenty-minute drive they finally pull up in the parking lot to their one bed, one bath small apartment. Krazie jumps out after putting the Coupe in park running towards the front door while digging in his pocket trying to get a hold of his keys.) Lu-Lu: “Hey aren’t you forgetting the fact that I don’t have LEGS! I can’t get out!!!” (Krazie swings the front door open and rushes into the bathroom as Lu-Lu remains in the Coupe waiting for Krazie to rescue her.) Lu-Lu: “Dang it, I’m going to be in here for hours! Krazie takes the longest craps in the world!” (Like Lu-Lu said, an hour later Krazie comes strolling out with some tissue paper stuck to the bottom of his foot, whistling a little tune with a satisfied look on his face.) Lu-Lu: “Krazie!? You better get your ass over here if you ever plan on having sex again!!!” (Krazie sprints over to the car. After opening the door and placing the cart on the ground Krazie and Lu-Lu make their way into the apartment as the scene fades yet again.) *** “Have you ever wondered just where exactly Lu-Lu came from? Ever wonder what her background really is? Just what is Kroger Island? Well, stay tuned for all the answers!*** (The scene re-opens with Krazie sitting on floor leaning against their piss stained, cushion less couch with his arm wrapped around Lu-Lu.) Krazie: “Tell me what it was like growing up…” Lu-Lu: “What?” Krazie: “I’m curious as to what Kroger Island is like…” Lu-Lu: “It’s great! My parents were George and Andi, they were the two that made me, I had hundreds upon hundreds of brothers and sisters. I think I was the eightieth born… But I can’t really remember… I learned how to roll with my older sister Tabitha, and I learned how to be strong and carry a lot of heavy things by my older brother Frank… Then one day a large truck came to Kroger Island with the Kroger Name on it, and it took so many of us away!!! I was so sad because I saw so many of my other brothers and sisters just sitting there as the rest of us got placed in the back of the truck!” (Lu-Lu pauses as Krazie just sits there listening.) Krazie: “So what happened next?” Lu-Lu: “Well, I was taken to a Kroger’s and from there I busted my ass for twelve years carrying anything and everything! Twelve years of carrying crap sure takes a toll on you! I became dented from cars and had a wobbly wheel and so the powers that be decided I wasn’t good enough to carry on! So they stuck me near that dumpster with some of my brothers and sisters who were also not fit to go on… After I saw you I just knew there was something special and you know the rest!” (The two sit back, Krazie drinking some Jack Daniels and Lu-Lu just sitting back next to her man.) Lu-Lu: “Hey babe? I found two tickets to Indiana for this Friday…” (Krazie grins as he nods his head to Lu-Lu.) Krazie: “Yeah, I got some unfinished business to deal with…” Lu-Lu: “What is it?” (Krazie points to a small scar on his forehead as his grin is replaced by a look of sheer anger.) Krazie: “Revenge will be mine! I will not have some fool come and interfere in my matches or after my matches or even before my matches just because he’s got some beef with me!” Lu-Lu: “Why did he jump you anyway?” Krazie: “He’s jealous I got a beautiful woman and he has no one! Maybe its because we were in RSE together and he knew I was better than him! Or how maybe it’s because I am white and he hates me for that!” (Krazie nods his head as Lu-Lu just sighs.) Krazie: “I don’t know why he has a problem with me, but one thing I know is that he won’t get away with that cheap shot! I got to face Andy Murray on Sunday and I’m not going to lose my shot at the Rising Star Title because of this guy! It just ain’t going to happen!” (Krazie stands up and storms into the kitchen grabbing another bottle of Jack D. to replace the empty one in his hands.) Lu-Lu: “Did you hear the lineup for Tuesday?” Krazie: “Yeah I did! You see who was in that lineup besides the Springboard Kid?” Lu-Lu: “Yeah I did…” Krazie: “Duzza! Me, him, and so many others all vying for that Extreme Championship!” Lu-Lu: “You could very well be the Rising Star champion along with the Extreme Champion!” (Krazie returns with his bottle of J.D. and plops down on the cushion less couch as Lu-Lu rolls around to face Krazie.) Krazie: “I may be a lunatic and my name may say Krazie (crazy) but I am not stupid! There is no way for me to be a double champion! So you know what? I am more than happy to hold that Rising Star title and bring it some prestige!” Lu-Lu: “So what are you going to do on Tuesday on Turmoil?” (Krazie begins to chuckle as he thinks of what he is going to do.) Krazie: “I am going to make sure that Duzza does not get the title!” (The scene ends with Krazie laughing hysterically followed by a big swallow of the potent Jack Daniels.)
