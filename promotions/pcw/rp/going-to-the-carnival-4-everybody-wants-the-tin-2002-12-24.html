Going To The Carnival [4] Everybody Wants The Tin

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24.12.02 The last time we left our men of ghetto, ebonics and foul language, they were stuck amongst the Candian police and highway patrol as Dante picked up a piece of what seems to be a reminent of the PCW Tag Team titles, currently vacated and placed into safe keeping at Santa's workshop in New Hampshire. The group, whether they be male or female has joined Playaz Inc. in this adventure, no matter how ludicrious it may be. Wavy eyes and hard liqour accompany the young group as the scene opens towards towards the Maine border. It seems that after the Toronto incident in which Dante ended up becoming a Matrix wannabe and atleast in some odd way, placed two highway patrol men and police officers in the hospital, treating for gunshot wounds as Dante heftly placed a few bullets inside of their chest cavaties. But, the question is, why are they on this voyage to gain the PCW Tag Team titles for a second time? If you look back since the dawn of September, there have only been two PCW Tag Team champions, and three title changes in the span, quite a record. The feud between Playaz Inc. and Gaz Green along with Nerezza happened to reach fever pitch when it came towards the PCW Tag Team titles. In all, there had not been a truthful great pair of champions since Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart won them back at PCW's Supremecy event. Their quest for the gold ended at first on October the sixth, where in Japan they had won the titles from Pranks With Attitude, whom had taken the titles away from Mark Makonie and Duzza, the champions before them who were anonmously paired together and thus formed a tag team. Because of the racial boundaries between the two men, they could not act as a cohesive unit, thus making P.W.A vulnerable to extracting the titles from them. On this night in October, the champions stepped in the ring with Pranks With Attitude, but had a ploy up their sleeves. Eleanor Hall-Kannon, the wife of the Chief Executive Officer of PCW, Xavier Kannon happened to play a role for which Playaz Inc were set to win, in a one sided no disqualification match up. A smile brought upon the faces of Quo and Jackson as they road the wave, until another female stopped them deep in their tracks for title supremecy. The date of October the twenty-ninth will be forever etched in stone for the African American tandem as they faced Pranks With Attitude in a routine title defense. For the weeks leading up towards it, Dante, Quo, Gaz, and Nerezza had tossed back and forth various comments and words about their opposition, thus making this title defense all that more important. Playaz Inc. thought they had leverage over the tandem, having beaten them on three seperate occasions while P.W.A had not a single pinfall, or blemish towards the team's record. The estranged wife of Alexander Miyagawa made a difference however, as she helped Gaz shock his way into winning the PCW Tag Team titles for P.W.A a second time. Since then, the division has become run-down, off worse than a project home controlled by H.U.D. The division which used to be a thing of beauty, a thing of wonder and excitement for PCW fans has dropped signifcantly, and with the departure of P.W.A thanks to a disagreement between the partners, the titles has been thrown up for every single man and woman inside of a PCW or RUSH lockeroom to try and win them. Only problem is, Playaz Inc. is the only team that has truthfully come back from the grave and be a 'team'. 'Um, tell me why the heck is there a piece of the Tag title stuck on the road,' Tracy whispers to Dante. Its towards two o'clock, a time where most of the men and women in New Hampshire are getting their very last minute shopping done and corrected if they had goofed up any items. 'Really, tell me.' Dante looks over into a small compartment bag that he has just about every single item he'll need on this trip, including an advanced copy of his second CD, 'Tha 2nd Comin.' A smile crosses his face as he picks up the piece of the belt before pocketing it in his hand. 'I just found it, it was calling out to me as if it were a ring of maginifcent power.' Tracy reaches over Dante's back. 'May I see the piece of gold?' 'NEVER!' the flamboyant record artist screams out, sending Tracy back abit. His eyes gape open as the screams from the outside of their makeshuft camp site awaken Statuz Quo, a few feet over in a tent of his own. 'Motherfucka.' 'Statuz, stop sayin' that! That's probably yo favorite word in the world. Gawd.' Sheeca comments as Quo picks himself up from the tent. Spreading open the cover, he looks around him as cars zoom by, closely guarded by Maine border patrol, watching and waiting. Soon, a large roar of pedestrians come crashing through the border as Quo grows wide eyed towards the entire scene. 'The hell?' The group makes their way towards the campsite as a light fire is drawn out as one member of the group lights a match. 'Mr. Quo...' 'Son of a bitch, I knew we wuz gonna run into sum whitez!' he curses to himself as Dante comes out of hiding. 'The hell?' He turns his head towards the men and women standing in front of him. 'Ohhh -' 'Shite?' one man calls out, before a woman comes behind him and smacks him in the back of his head. 'Stupeed, you're not supposed to say anytheeng.' Dante eyes Quo, who eyes Dante back in a reflex type of deal as Tracy comes from behind Dante, wrapping herself in a blanket. 'Great, more whit -' 'Shut it woman.' a man with a fairly long brunette hair and a knap sack around his back barks towards Tracy. 'We came here for the tin.' 'What tin?' Dante raises his eyes, trying to play as if he had nothing in his pockets. Quo nudges him on the shoulder. 'Dawg, give them the tin.' 'Tin? This comin' from the dude that got my ass kidnapped?! And then let those two idiots fuck up my damn play?' Tapping on his shoulder, Tracy spins Dante around. 'You were freakin' on somebody?' 'Naw!' Dante plays the stupid card, knowing that the naiive mind of Tracy wasn't going to comprehend. Dante begins to sort it out with Tracy before he is interrupted by one of the group, pulling him away from his wife. The man snarls, almost in an ogre like manner at Dante who wifts his hand around his nose. 'Damn, you've got some serious tongue issues man, care to offer some Binaca, some Tic Tacs, some Skitzo tongue bather?' 'SHUT IT!' the ogre screams at Dante who stands, a bit perturbed by the man's spoken tongue as he twirls around, before looking the man straight in the face. 'Look, I don't have any of your precious tin, and I wouldn't have any if I went to the 80's! Go cry me a river, if you can't figure it out.' Soon, the rest of the group howls in almost drone like fashion. 'OOOOOH! CRY ME A RIVER! OH! CRY ME A RIVER!' Tracy turns to Sheeca, 'Timberlakes.' Sheeca nods at Tracy as they both turn their heads towards the group as Statuz throws his arms up in the air. 'Look, I've had bout enough of you peeps tryna pull this Lord of The Rings bull on me and Dante, so will you please STEP THE FUCK OFF?!' The group huddles in a football like manner, before turning and trying to square off against the men and women in the group as Dante, Quo, Tracy and Sheeca lean back in fright, before Quo and Jackson pull out pistols, aiming them at the group. The group backs off, frightened. 'They've got TIN KILLERS!' one man screams out as the group backs off. 'They'll kill us all!' Dante and Quo shake their heads, 'Damn, they've got to be the absolute STUPIDEST MOTHERFUCKAS I'VE EVER SEEN! WORSE THEN THOSE CRYPS WHO TRIED TO KIDNAP ME!' Aiming the guns squarely at the men and women, Quo and Jackson run forward, scarring the people away as Dante laughs. 'They didn't know the pistols were toys...' BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! Quo blasts a few shots in the air, horrifying Jackson and Tracy who's eyes are bugging out at the moment. 'Nucca, I alwayz packs my heat.' Dante shakes his head, before going back inside the tent, Tracy following behind him as Quo and Sheeca stand outside, waiting and hoping more white people will run up on the group, before the scene finally fades to black.
