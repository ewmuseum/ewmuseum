Rent-a-Hubbard To The Rescue

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Rent-a-Hubbard To The Rescue Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 28/10/02 “Oh my God, he’s going to jump.....” “Someone talk him down....” “Er.... Like, SO no way.... He might be poor and I am TOTALLY not risking him touching me if he is...” Our shot opens up on a bridge crossing a still-looking river... A small crowd gather around to cast their eyes upon a shaking man who is clinging to the outside of the bridge’s guard rail, nervously looking down. “Like.. HELLO?!?! What are you all standing here for, the view sucks!” From in amongst the crowd emerges PCW’s preppy little Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall who doesn’t exactly look concerned for the man’s life, more where she will view his plunge from. Eleanor : “How often do you get to see someone, like, SO jump off a bridge to a WAY watery grave!” To the disgust of all the concerned citizens, Ellie~! scurries over in her high heels, the tap attracting the attention of the man who is hugging the Iron structure and staring down between his legs at the unforgiving river way below. “DON’T TRY TO STOP ME!” Ellie~! is now standing about 6 feet away from him, leaning on the barrier and looking at him with a broad grin on her face. Eleanor : “Don’t worry.... I’m, like, SO not going to stop you!” It appears that the man was expecting a rather different response and looks up from the fall below him to see the little prep waving at him. Eleanor : “Hi! I’m Ellie~!” The jumper is dressed in a ragged looking White short sleeved pinstripe shirt, loosely done up Red tie and Black dress pants... By his shiny Black shoes sits a Brown briefcase. A moment is exchanged between the unlikely pair as Ellie~!’s innocent yet anticipation laden face meets the man’s anguish stricken one... His brow showing signs of severe stress and his whole body shaking. “I can’t take it anymore.... I hate my job.... My wife left me..... My kids don't even recognise my voice...” Ellie~! looks disinterested and pulls the multiple bands and bracelets out of the way of her watch, before looking back at the man inquisitively. Eleanor : “So..... Is there a program? Or a timetable? A scheduled interval?” She raises her eyebrows and tilts her head, waiting with her Bambi-esque pale Blue eyes for an answer. As she keeps looking doe-eyed at him, the beads of anxious sweat keep rolling down his face. “I’LL JUMP RIGHT NOW! I WILL!“ Rather uncharacteristically for the little prep, she actually seems concerned and frantically gestures with her hands. Eleanor : “Er....HELLO?!?!? Like, SO don’t jump and stuff!” The jumper looks touched by Ellie~!’s concern and adopts a more relaxed position, draped over the guard rail.... At the same time the Commissioner rummages around in her handbag and pulls out her cell phone. Eleanor : “Xavi would kill me if he missed something like this.... No pun intended.” The little prep hits a speed dial and waits for an answer as the man looks at her in bemusement, then grabs the Iron bars in panic when he accidentally knocks his briefcase off the thin overhang.... His terror-filled eyes watching it spin and twist before splashing into the water. Ellie~!’s head shoots around, her hair flying after it before she breathes a sigh of relief after thinking she missed the leap. Eleanor : “Xavi, like.... SO hurry up and come to the bridge thing!.... Oh, Ellie~! thinks you’ll like!” She hangs up then looks back to the nervous man as if she didn’t just make a phone call telling someone to come and watch his suicide. Over the other side, on the pedestrian path the crowd has swelled and are abuzz with speculation and rumour. “I think she’s trying to talk him down...” “I heard his wife left him...” “From this height it’d be like hitting concrete! What? It would!” Back by the ledge, Ellie~! struggles to find something to say as the man slowly cranes his neck to look down at the cold, Grey water below. Eleanor : “Soooooo, you’re, like, jumping then?” The man’s pale face nods, his hair matted to his forehead with sweat... Grabbing the Iron lattice that forms the barrier with one hand, and loosening his poorly done up tie with the other. “There’s no turning back now....” Leaning over the rail, the little prep stands on tip toes and looks at the size of the ledge curiously. Eleanor : “I could, like, SO turn on that.... I had Ballet lessons when I was young.... YAY ME!“ She does her usual little jump on the spot and applauds herself, the man’s sweaty left hand sliding off the rail and he desperately grabs it again, hugging tightly and breathing a sigh of relief. Eleanor : “You’re not, like, very good at this jumping thing are you?” Just as the man goes to reply, a familiar voice is heard. “What did you want to show me my little Poon Puppy...” Towards them walks Ellie~!’s husband, and CEO of PCW, Xavier Kannon. His bright Green eyes look inquisitively at the scene before greeting his wife with a peck on the cheek. Kannon standing behind the little prep and hugging her around the waist, the pair wait in anticipation. Xavier : “So when’s he going to jump?” Despite the fact he plans on taking his own life, the man looks a little uncomfortable with the onlookers. “I hope my wife is happy now.... She’ll have no need for the divorce soon.... She’ll have the house, kids and all my savings...” The mention of money instantly changes he expression on Xavier’s face from anticipation to curiosity. Xavier : “When you say SAVINGS... How much are we talking about?” Eleanor : “Talk..... Ing.... About?” His death approaching, the man sees no reason to be secretive about his finances. “I dunno.... With tax rebates, stock options and shares.... Probably close to 100k.. So-” In a complete U-turn of personality, the Scientologist rushes over and grabs the man. Xavier : “Don’t jump... There are so many things in life to live for.... Ruining people’s dreams, Piledriving Orphans through tables, kicking people when their down.... Use that money to buy yourself something worth living for!” The man’s blood shot eyes stare at him confused as the shot fades to Black, only the eerily reassuring voice of Xavier being heard.... “.... Scientology my friend, Scientology!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
