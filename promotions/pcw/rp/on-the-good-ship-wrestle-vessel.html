On the good ship Wrestle Vessel.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: now On Friday Fusion we saw Semore Smoke come out of his shell a little for the first time since his arrival into PCW. The young man acted in a disrespectful way towards both Kano and CCW. This is not exactly something Corey should be happy about upon first inspection, but when we take a deeper look at it perhaps this is a good sign. For is it not CCW who is well known for his disrespectful actions towards his elders, the tendency to rebel and break all the rules. Perhaps, just perhaps, Corey's mind games have worked even better than he suspected. Semore Smoke's actions on Friday Fusion were more reminiscent of the actions of a younger Corey Williams than anything else. Has CCW's words rung true in the mind of Semore Smoke as was hinted on Friday, or will Semore Smoke and Jon Kano fight side by side like we saw on Tuesday Turmoil? If we analyze both Fusion and Turmoil simultaneously instead of each show in its singular form, the answer just maybe neither. We may just see Semore Smoke choose neither man, neither old school nor new school, instead opting to abuse the trust given to him by each man in order to take them both on. The triple strap match on Sunday at PCW's Wrestle Vessel is most certainly one of the most unpredictable matches in the history of PCW. For it is not only the winner that needs to be determined, but also in which the way it will happen. Either way, we know that something WILL happen this Sunday and it may just shock PCW. ::Our scene opens up in mid-air, for originality's sake, 300 or so feet above sea-level. To be more exact, this promo begins inside a helicopter as it moves across the Caribbean Sea. The sun gleams down upon the spherical glass of the helicopter as it leans left and heads for a small white blob on the water's surface. As the helicopter comes closer and closer towards the white blob we see that it is not just a white blob but rather a cruise ship. A ship more commonly known as the 'Wrestle Vessel' to the PCW faithful. The helicopter continues on its path straight at the vessel. As it speeds up and goes faster and faster, we see more of the boat, even able to notice the people aboard on the top deck. And as it gets closer and closer, those people scramble in all directions trying to get away from the helicopter which is now storming towards the boat at an alarming rate. Just as it seems destined to puncture a hole right into the body of the boat it pulls up and just misses the railing on the deck. Once above the boat, the helicopter stops dead, hovering for a second before lowering down towards the gap that has appeared on the deck due to the people scrambling for their lives. The helicopter lands on the metal deck with a small bump. By this time people have realized that their lives are in no danger (for now) and have begun to crowd around the helicopter. However six bodyguards come from nowhere and begin to clear up some space to the door of the helicopter. With the space cleared out a red carpet is rolled put towards the entrance of the helicopter. The six bodyguards suddenly pull out trumpets and play a small tune before a short, slimy-looking man in a suit produces a microphone.:: Short Slimy-looking man: And now, ladies and gentlemen, one of the brightest stars in the PCW cavalcade. Coooooooooooooooooooooooooooorrrey Willllllllliaaaaaams! ::The helicopter door swings open and out steps Corey Williams, sporting a black silk shirt and black leather pants. Immediately cameras go off everywhere, their flashes reflecting against the dark lenses of CCW's shades. CCW walks down the carpet, a duffel bag slung over his right shoulder. CCW continues to walk down the carpet until he reaches the short slimy-looking man. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a wad of one hundred dollar bills, which he places into the hands of short slimy-looking man. This exchange of money quickly transforms into a handshake.:: Corey: Mick my man, how's it going. Mick (Short Slimy-looking man): Perfect Mr. Williams. Corey: Now, have you done as I asked on the cell phone and upgraded me to the deluxe suite? Mick: Yes, with your own personal entourage as a complimentary service of our humble ship. Corey: Good... ::Corey reaches into his pocket and pulls out a larger wad of bills which he hands to Mick once again.:: Corey: And Mr. Smoke? Mick: The same treatment, don't worry, we'll be taking care of him. Corey: Great... ::CCW reaches into his pocket and pulls out a folded up piece of paper.:: Corey: See that he gets this note will you? ::Mick takes the note and places it in his pocket.:: Mick: Sure thing Mr. Williams. Corey: Excellent... ::Corey reaches into his pocket again and this time pulls out a humorous wad of bills, which Mick accepts gratefully.:: Mick: Will there be anything else Mr. Williams? Corey: No... ::CCW turns to leave for his deluxe suite when all of a sudden he notices the swarms of people surrounding him. He turns back to Mick.:: Corey: Actually, there is one more thing. Hand me that microphone... ::Before the spineless Mick can hand it to him CCW has already snatched it from his hands. He turns to the crowd of fans all desperate to get a glimpse of the PCW star before he wrestles tomorrow night. As he approaches them they get louder and louder.:: Corey: Wow... A perfect reception for a perfect entrance. There's only one thing I'd like to say to all those people who came so far and spent so much in order to see the boys and I put on a little show called Wrestle Vessel..... ::The fans pop like hell at the mention of the PCW PPV.:: Corey: You're all a bunch of pathetic losers! ::The insult is obviously met with a heap of boos.:: Corey: You know, if it wasn't for the fact that you guys have no class and no dignity for yourselves, resulting to pettily living your life through successful people like me, then maybe, just maybe, people like Semore Smoke wouldn't have to be ashamed to call themselves a 'fan favorite.' Don't be surprised when Semore Smoke fulfills his destiny and turns on each and every one of you tomorrow night, because you needy pieces of shit drove him away! ::The fans are now irate at CCW's blatant and arrogant insults of them, so much so that they begin to throw things at him. CCW ignores this and simply grins before taking a short bow.:: Corey: Thank you, thank you. ::The young billionaire turns on his heel and walks off for his deluxe cabin, exuding a certain pompous arrogance in his stride which makes him better than everyone else. Scene fades out.:: Is this the type of behavior we can expect from Semore Smoke in the coming months? Will Corey mould Smoke into his shape? Will Smoke let him? What about Jon Kano, will he and Smoke form some sort of bond? Will Smoke learn the type of respect for Kano that CCW never could? Will he see through CCW's lies and false promises? Or will Smoke decide that he is better than both men? The man has plenty of confidence placed in him by the legions of PCW fans, will he believe them in thinking that he doesn't need a mentor? Something will happen, the only thing that will not happen is that we will NOT see CCW and Kano team up. At least that's a given. !The End!
