What to get Ryckman

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/02 Scene opens to just outside of a very large hotel building. The camera pans the front of the building, and the parking lot to the right of the building. Standing out in the lot next to a 2002 Chevy Tahoe that is black. The man is PCW reporter Jason Maddux. He is in casual attire and standing there leaning on the side of the truck. A moment later Steve Johnson walks around the corner and starts toward his vehicle. He looks up and notices that Jason is leaning against his vehicle. He looks toward him rather annoyed and continues to walk toward him. When he is just a few feet away Jason straightens up and extends his hand toward Steve. Steve just walks past him to the driver side door. As he unlocks the door, he looks over to Jason who is a little concerned. Steve: How many times am I going to have to tell you Jason not to lean on my vehicles? You have no respect for other people’s property. Jason: Sorry Steve. I just always forget about that little rule of yours. I don’t know why though. You have had that same rule every since you got your first car. You remember that car Steve? Steve: Yeah I remember that piece of junk. In fact the mention of it reminds me of some horrible times. Jason: Yeah, but there were some good times too. Like the time me, you, and Little Jay went out and got so drunk, that we couldn’t even see straight enough to start the car. Steve and Jason begin to laugh. Steve: Yeah I remember that. I also remember the time you and Little Jay took my car without asking and wrecked it into a light pole, because you were both trying to get some car full of girl’s attention instead of paying attention to the road. What ever happened to Little Jay I wonder? Jason looks at him like he is out of his mind. Jason: You remember you got so mad about the car that you put Jay in a wheel chair for the rest of his life. You two really haven’t spoken since. Are you feeling all right? Steve (grin on his face): Yeah I am fine. Guess that kind of reaction does tend to make people a little wary of you. Now lets go get something to eat. I am tired of standing here talking about the old days with you. I have more important things on my mind. Jason: Like Tuesday night? Steve: No like dinner tonight! Now get in if you are going. The two get into the Tahoe and Steve start it up. He sits there letting it warm up a moment. Then puts it into drive and pulls out of the lot. He turns right and out onto the street. They sit in silence a moment. Then Steve begins the conversation again. Steve: Cade came by the gym today. Jason: Is he still trying to talk you into retiring like him? Steve: He was going to. He wanted to know if I wanted any of his advice. I told him he couldn’t tell me anything I didn’t already know better than him. I sent him on his way. Jason: You told Cade off? WOW, you two have been like best friends since you got into the big times. I would never have guessed that was coming. Steve: Yeah well we just don’t see eye to eye as far as a wrestling career goes. He thinks having a couple of World Titles in some smaller companies is a good enough career. I have always been focused on one day winning the PCW World Title. He is content with what he has accomplished. But I am far from satisfied. I need to prove myself even farther. Jason: Will winning the PCW World Title be enough for you? Steve looks at Jason like he just said the dumbest thing he has ever heard. Steve: That is the dumbest thing I have ever heard! (SEE I TOLD YOU) It is not just about winning the title once. I want to not only win it, but to have the longest and greatest reign of any PCW Champion ever. I want to become one of the few greats to make the PCW Hall of Fame. That is why I have worked so da#n hard to get here. I will not blow this opportunity like other have. Jason: You haven’t changed one bit since you started going after this dream Steve. Every since I can remember you have wanted to join the biggest and best company in the world and be their champion. The only thing that has changed is the company you wanted to dominate. There I was struggling through high school and college so I could become a writer and journalist. Everything just seemed to come so da#n easy for you in school, both athletically and scholastically. I always hated how easy the work came for you and you just didn’t seem to care. All you have ever wanted is this. Steve: Yes but it wasn’t easy to get here either I can assure you of that. Besides if you hadn’t struggled to get here, then you wouldn’t appreciate it nearly as much. You are one of the best reporters PCW has ever had. At least you seem to get out better question than any of the others do. Now where are we going to eat? Jason: Don’t know. How about Italian? Steve: That is fine by me. We can go to the Olive Garden. They shouldn’t be to busy this time of the evening. The scene switches from the two sitting in the vehicle talking to them standing in the parking lot of the Olive Garden. As Steve thought earlier the place is not very busy.The two stand there for a moment before they start to walk toward the front door. When they reach the front door they walk in and the hostess greets them. She is a fine blond woman, with large breasts. Hostess: Hello gentlemen. My name is Dawn, and I will show you to your table. Will it be smoking or none smoking? Steve: None smoking thank you. Dawn shows them to their table. After they are seated Jason looks up at her and smiles. He then gives her a wink. She just smiles sweetly, more because it is her job then anything else. Steve just rolls his eyes at Jason and his weak attempt. Jason: So what would a guy have to do, to get your phone number? Dawn: Well the most important thing would be that he would need to be at least halfway attractive and not look like a boar. She smiles and walks away. Jason has a sad look come over his face and just shakes his head. Steve is laughing at him and almost falls out of his seat. Jason looks over at him with an angry look. Jason: It is not that funny. I can’t believe you would laugh at me like that. You are drawing the attention of every one that is in this place. Now control your self and stop causing a scene. Steve: Sorry its just, that may have been the worst job I have ever seen you do with getting a woman’s attention. You really have gotten much worse than you were as a teenager. You know your attempt to pick up that woman reminds me of Ryckmans attempt to intimidate me. No wait you actually had a chance to pick her up. Jason: Speaking of Ryckman, I heard you two had a little face to face recently. How did that go? Steve (with a grin on his face): The way it would be expected to go, I suppose. He came up to me at the vending machine in the arena and tried to play one of his pathetic mind games with me. Called me a little man; that sort of crap. He wants so badly for people to believe he is this big, bad, crazy man with the power to destroy any and all in his path. It is comical really. He is a big man and could very well be a great superstar some day, if he wouldn’t try so hard to make people believe he is some kind of mental monster. Any way that is enough about him and his pathetic attempts. Where is the waitress I am ready for a beer and some food. Finally a waitress arrives to take their orders. When she walks up Steve orders a beer, and Jason orders some white wine. They then both order what they want to eat. When the waitress walks away. Jason begins to laugh. Steve (confused look on his face): What is so damn funny Jason? Did you take something before you came to the hotel? Jason: No I was just thinking you really should get Ripper something for all his trouble of attempting to make people believe your match will be a good one. You know what I mean? Steve: Yeah I do. But what in the world would you get an overgrown sack of buffalo dung like Ripper as a consolation prize? I will have to think about this one. The two sit there chanting some more when the waitress bring them their food and drinks. When she sets them down Steve drinks about half of the beer before Jason can even get a sip of white whine. Steve then gets a fork full of the food he had ordered and eats it. Just as he does a man in some hospital clothing walks in through the doors and waits to be seated. The man is accompanied by a few friends that all seem to be doctors as well. Steve puts the fork down and a grin comes over his face. Steve: I have just thought of what I am going to get. It will be perfect and he should feel real good about it. Jason: Really, what have you thought of? Steve starts getting up. Jason looks at him in shock. Steve: Sorry I haven’t got the time to explain right now. I will tell you about it later. Right now I have to go. Jason(as Steve walks toward the front door): Hey where are you going? What about the food and beer you ordered? (Steve walks out of the front doors) I guess I will take care of the check then? Jason sits there in an bad mood for a moment. Then he decides he better at least eat what he ordered since Steve had just stuck him with the tab. The scene fades away to black.
