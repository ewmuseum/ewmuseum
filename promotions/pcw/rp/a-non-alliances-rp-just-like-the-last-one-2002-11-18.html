Date: 11.18.02 Last time when we left our hero in the midst of ghetto-ism at his very own party, a bit of dumbfound ness came upon him. When he sent the first installment of the dimwitted party, he forgets to mention that it wasn't for Alliances and that the true viewing wasn't even meant to air for Alliances itself! 'Darn it! Didn't I tell you not to let that tape be sent?!' The scene opens up during the middle hours of the party as Dante lounges back in an executive chair, surrounded by photos and paintings of Africa, Black America, and himself, just to be a bit egoist about his works. 'Tracy! Just where in the world are you? That tape needs to be gotten back from the people! NOW! I'll go on a superpower trip and tax forgetting things! As you can currently see, the former Tag Team champion is in no mood to be a jokester. His head his now tied down by a red doo-rag as he still wears his Houston Astros Throwback jersey and pants. Soon, crashing into the door happens to be Tracy, decked out in a too die for short red skirt; similar to the one that woman wore in that Harrison Ford flick that starred Michelle Pheffier. Dante: 'Well, what happened?' Tracy: 'Well you big silly goose, the darn tape got mixed up with your Alliances set, and your regular show thing! The same thing happened last month!' Dante: 'Yeah, but it didn't matter last month because I could do that!' Tracy grumbles before storming off out and back towards the party as Dante clicks on his computer, as normal when he doesn't have time to make sexual tapes with Tracy, or even deny reports of him and Tyra Banks being an item. He shrugs as he pops up his e-mail, raising an eyebrow upon it. Dante: '52 files? I thought I cleaned this stuff out last week.' He clicks on the file linking him to the Hotmail Website, pulling up his current inbox. He looks over the sender, before a name catches his eye, repeatedly over and over again. Dante: 'Xavier Kannon, Xavier Kannon, Xavier Kannon, Ron Jeremy? Xavier Kannon…XAVIER KANNON!!! UGH!' Dante, being his very impatient self beings to click at the very top one highlighting over the title 'Read Notice'. He clicks and clicks more, almost as if he were pulling it off the top of a stack. Looking over it, he scrolls down past the sender and contacts items, before heading down right into the subject. 'Dear Playaz Inc.' 'YOU SUCK AND THE FRONT OFFICE WILL WIN ALLIANCES! HAHAHAHAHAHA!' Signed, PCW Owner and CEO – Xavier Kannon. 'P.S. WE'LL WIN!' Dante quickly deletes the message, before clicking on the next one entitled, 'P.S.' He stops for a second, thinking it's a ploy before clicking on it. It opens up to nothing but gay porn spreading all over his desktop! Dante: 'MY EYES! THEY'VE BEEN REDUCED TO CRACKER LIKE SPECTICALS! BAD EYES! BAD MAN PRON! BAD! I MEAN PORN! PORN!!' He shuts off the monitor, before Tracy walks back in, her hands firmly placed on her hips as she taps her foot impatiently. Dante turns to face her, starry-eyed. Dante: 'Hum…you need something dear?' Tracy: 'The computer pleezies!' Dante: 'Yeah, computer. Sure you need it hun?' A smile grows on her face. Tracy: 'Oh, you know I do sweetie!' Dante: 'Really bad?' Tracy begins hopping up and down like a bunny rabbit, almost excited by each attempt Dante has for her. He waves his fingers around, drawing her closer to the computer, before letting her sit in the executive chair. Tracy: 'OOH! Feels like your…' Dante: 'Save the dirty talk for later.' Tracy: 'I meant your dining chair you big silly goose!' Dante smirks a bit, before shaking his head solemnly wishing his ditz of a wife would get the hint and make her way away from the computer filled with naughty photos of guys and guys and more dudes and even more dudes. Hey, you get the picture. She clicks on the monitor, and instantly her eyes light up briefly, almost like a child on Christmas day. Tracy: 'Oooh! Manly tools!' On the outside, Dante struggles with the fact that his equally horny wife likes the thought of pictures of men out of their clothes. White men to be exact, when she has an Adonis husband starring her in the face every single day of the week! Dante: 'This so sourly sucks. Oh well, if one woman scorns me, another shall get her payback!' Tracy steps out of the computer room, flabbergasted by the sight that she looks around, eying anyone if they're near before shutting the door and locking the latch. She squeals ever so more like a child, as Dante sits on the stairs, almost with a sulk on his face. Soon, one of those silky fine booty girls you've seen in such idealisms such as 'Gimmie The Light' by Sean Paul, or 'Hot In Herre' by Nelly come walking by Dante, alerting a smile on his face. Dante: 'Well, hello there.' He begins to talk to her in a almost Don Juan like voice, the same type of voice that he happened to swoon Tracy with as the woman sits down with him, just about step away from him as the two begin to converse. Woman: 'Aren't you one of those pro wrestlers that work for that place in New Jersey?' Dante: 'Oh, PCW? Yeah, I'm one of the stars.' The woman chuckles, just about smiling at everything Dante happens to say as the scene revolves upward and into the face of Tracy, back in the computer room and just about dog tired from view all of the…manly pornographic pop up ads displayed on her husband's computer. She wipes a brow of sweat off of her face, almost confounded by the entire scenario as she closes the computer once more. Tracy: 'Manly tool overload…' She restarts the computer, picking from scratch on the internet and picking up on Math Teachers for Dummies. Pulling the search engine up, she looks over and over, pulling up more that 717, 000 web pages containing the stylings of the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid's book and teachings. Tracy grows wide eyed. Tracy: 'Jesus, how many things can a nerd have on this thing? Its like...a darn whitey thingy doodie!' She clicks on the site, just about covering the entire Hardcore Isoscles Trapezoid's career from Point A to B as she seems happy with her search. Tracy: 'Oooh! I'm smart!' She giddily jumps up from the seat as she opens the door open once more, a content smile on her face as she pumps up and down the staircase, her feet tapping along the stair with each step.She walks over to the dancefloor, searching for Dante to report her findings. Tracy: 'Dante! I found it! Dante?' She turns left, right, but the man is nowhere to be found, about as vanished as a Brandi workrate these days. She hurries over towards the kitchen, still looking for her hubby as he happens to be outside, laughing it up with that female from earlier. Dante steps outward as Tracy opens the door, close to almost seeing Dante as the woman hugs upon him, kissing him on the cheek and neck as if he were a neckbone. Dante pushes her off, just about as Tracy sees this, her eyes blunt red and almost scary looking. Dante: 'Erm, I think you pissed her off.' Tracy growls, before a huge ball of smoke appears around her. The smoke begins to cover just about the entire front patio area as the smoke continues to unfurl. As the smoke dies down, the complete appearence of Tracy has changed. No longer is she the beautiful African Amercian woman that she usually is. Now she looks completely like Buffy the Vampire Slayer - complete with Goth Boots, book of corny jokes and whitey stake killer! Tracy the Hoe Slayer: 'Hoe..watch out, because the SLAYER IS ON THE LOOSE!' Dante: 'Oh dear god. First I have fake Goths, trash talking extreme teacher/pedophiles, and a Greedy CEO sending me Gay Porn?! What has the world come to?!' Tracy yells in a Zena like fashion yells out before tossing a stake at the woman. The point falls bluntly, as the two women look at it, surprised. Tracy: 'Crap, guess I'll slay you the old fashion way. BIOTCH!' Dante: 'Note to self: Stop ordering Statuz Quo's Ebonics for Crackas.' Tracy yells once more, jumping onto the woman with a fierce sense to her, throwing away most of the woman's clothing before punches begin flying. The woman on the bottom happens to be hysterical, nearly scared for her life as Tracy takes in no prisoners as to punch the living hell out of the woman. Dante pulls Tracy off of the woman, restraining her as the woman appears to be... WINNONA RYDER?! Dante: 'Jesus, now the theif visits my house!' Winnona Ryder: 'I swear I didn't do it!' Tracy: 'Caught this hoe trying to take our good Gucci and Burberry!' Dante eyes the faint Ryder, before turning his back towards her. Tracy spits on the woman, before taking her items back. Tracy: 'This is for Dracula!' She kicks Ryder in the jaw. Tracy: 'This is for Zoolander!' She punches the star of Mr. Deeds in the face, causing the woman to fall on her knees, blood pouring from her nostrils. Tracy: 'And this! This is for uhh, for um...FOR BEING A B MOVIE ACTOR!' Tracy unleashes a fury of blows, covering the former non felon from head to toe, almost catching her off guard by the shots. As Tracy continues to deliver her ass whupping of Winonna, Dante turns his back beginning to walk back into the house. Dante: 'I'll never be able to show myself in a black neighborhood again...'
