At The Arena...

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 ::Segment One:: --- Scene sets with Hank backstage the PCW arena, set for his triple threat Extreme Championship Title defense. Has his title set by him on a wooden table with Janelle, and Ganker sitting in a couple of metal fold-up chair, watching him jump up and down and do some push-ups, sit-ups, whatever seems to be the right stretch he thinks to get him more pumped and ready to face two rising starts PCW has to offer and are currently throwing at him. Hank then grips his PCW Extreme Championship in hand and places it over his shoulder. Janelle and Ganker get up and begin to walk as Willy Verdain, a newcomer to the PCW interviewers, walks over to Hanka with a big smile on his face. He has his cameraman and a microphone. Willy: Hello, Mr. Gunyon, my name is Willy Verdain and I am a newcomer to the PCW and I thought it'd be a good start to have my first interview with such a well-respected athlete such as yourself. Hank: Crawling in the dark, looking for the answer... That's my motto. Willy: Right, but anyway, you're moments away from defending that PCW Extreme Championship you've worked two years to acheive. You're defending that very Extreme Championship against Karl LeBlanc and Jason Core, any final comments you have for this particular match? Hank: Well Will, (laughs as he strokes his chin) I am Hank, I am the most extreme. I am the extreme of extreme and if Jason, Karl, whoever wants to try to take my title, they can wait in line, for when I damn well feel like placing my championship on the line. Now unfortunately for Jason and Karl, tonight it is on the line, not my choice, but none the less, it is on the line, which means I get to get extreme on both Karl and Jason. I get to show them what makes me the most extreme of extreme of the PCW Elite. Team Nirvana circles around my greatness. Ganker is a great tag team partner, but I was given the shot to shine. They saw a passion in me. They knew I could get the damn job done when it came right down to it. Ask Doug Troy if you do not believe me. I was the underdog in that match big time, but thanks to Sobe assistance and now my official softdrink, I managed to hit the Off-Beat and pin that punks shoulders to the mat for the three count, hints placin' myself among the elite PCW superstars. Now Jason Core, the man is a newcomer who will find out jus' how hard it is to compete on the PCW level, even the lowest PCW, where I currently lay my mark. I personally find it favorable down at the bottom, not the best competition, but enough to call yo self a god. I have aquired such a talent and gimmick, it's not even funny. (laughs) Willy: What about LeBlanc, you both have one win over one another? Hank: That win was a fluke and LeBlanc will never manage to pin me again. I am the single best Extreme Champion and as long as I hold this title, it'll never fall into the hands of that big tall piece of sh*t who calls himself Karl LeBlanc. I am God, now watch me prove it later on tonight as I defend this Championship in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. --- Cheers from somewhere unknown can be heard. Ganker: Creepy... Janelle: Cheap pop~! Hank: Me mimicing my idol Mick Foley. Only one day may I have the honor to fight like he does in such an extreme passion. FOLEY RULES~! Ganker: (thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! Hank: Had to go there didn't yo? Ganker: (shrugs and nods) What can I say, I'm an oppertunist. Hank: Then how bout yo prove it this Tuesday when we fight the team of AFL-CIO, yo don't know them like I do, I've seen them wrestle and how they are in the back. Ganker: I've watched their matches on television. I know their style as well, they are good, but we're better. Hank: Yo study? (shocked) It's a god given miracle, but for some reason I don't remember deliverin' that one. --- Silence comes across the backstage. Hank: What? Ganker; Yo ain't God, I am... (thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! Don't yo forget it~! Hank: I'll try not to. (pauses) What am I not suppose to forget? --- Hank laughs as Ganker sighs. Willy: (annoyed) Well anyway, Hank, good-luck and you already answered my other questions without knowning it. Hank: I did? What were they? (confused) Willy: About AFL-CIO and your match against them this Tuesday Turmoil. Hank: Oh, yeah, good team they be, but too bad they suck too much to gain a win over the most prestigious team ever in the history of PCW and any damn federation we decide to grace. Willy: Oi... (slaps forehead) Hank: Dude, don't hit yo forehead, it's bad to hit yo-self. (laughs) --- With that said Hank whacks himself with the PCW Extreme Championship one good one. Ganker: Wait, we still have an hour right? Hank: Erm, yo have all-night boyo, yo ain't booked, only I am. Ganker: Oh that's right~! (pauses) I meant how long yo have until the match? Hank: Couple hours. Ganker: Sweet deal, wanna hit the pool hall for a quick Sobe and game of pool? Hank: Money? Ganker: F**k, yo crazy? Hank: Yes, but that's beside the point. Ganker: Yeah it is, (laughs) but I jus' thought I'd help clear yo head and help yo focus, yo concentrated a week or so for that match and now it's time to relax and clear that head. Hank: Some old guys theory I think. Ganker: Erm, yeah. Hank: LeBlanc and Core don't stand a motha' f**kin' chance~! --- Hank shoves Willy back as he puts his arm around Janelle who smiles at him, Ganker shakes his head as they walk off to Hank's Bentley, black Bentley he stole off Duzza in NZW. Scene fades out to absolute/complete/utter nothingness.
