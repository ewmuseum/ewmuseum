The Show That Never Made It To Air

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 18th, 2002 (The scene opens as the 'Whose Line Is It Anyways?' logo flashes. The camera then zooms on Drew Carey who is in the audience with his microphone.) Drew Carey: On tonight’s show, Wayne Brady, Colin Mockery, Ryan Styles and professional wrestler Mike Frampton. Come on down and let's have some fun. (Drew walks down the steps and takes his seat at his chair.) Drew Carey: Welcome to Whose Line Is It Anyways? the show where the points don't matter. That's right the points don't matter just like police officer in Harlem. (The crowd gives a laugh as do all the contestants minus Mike Frampton.) Drew Carey: We are going to start off with a game called Props, this is for all four contestants. These guys will be paired up and given a prop each. Then they will try and do as many funny things as the can with it. Mike and Wayne you two get this. (Drew hands Mike a large, floppy stick like object.) Drew Carey: Ryan and Colin here's yours. (Drew hands Ryan a gigantic hat.) Drew Carey: And begin. (Mike holds the object between his legs.) Mike Frampton: Welcome to the Playboy mansion. (The crowd laughs. The camera then switches over to Ryan and Colin. Ryan steps into the hat.) Colin: Well I couldn't afford a rabbit but... (Ryan is pulled out. Drew calls for the buzzer.) *BUZZ* Drew Carey: Alright I am giving twenty billion points to Wayne for stating out of that. Mike Frampton: Damn you suck. Drew Carey: Alright it's time for one of our favourite games. Scenes from a hat. (The 4 men line up 2 on each side. Drew pulls out a piece of paper.) Drew Carey: Unlikely items to be sold on ebay. (Colin walks out.) Colin: Hmmm, Drew Carey's pubic hairs. *BUZZ* (Mike walks forward.) Mike Frampton: Wow, an Andy Murray autograph. My bid, two cents. *BUZZ* (Ryan walks forward.) Ryan: Click, click, oh my goodness, a lock of Colin Mockery's hair. *BUZZ* (The camera shows Colin's face and Drew laughing. Mike walks forward.) Mike Frampton: Amazing, the world's fattest and slobbiest featuring Andy Murray on DVD. *BUZZ* Drew Carey: Alright folks that's it for now. It's time for a commercial break. (As the show fades to a commercial Drew walks towards Mike.) Drew Carey: Mike! Mike Frampton: Yes Drew? Drew Carey: I am afraid that this isn't working out. Mike Frampton: Well what do you mean? Drew Carey: You're just not funny. Not to mention you won't stop using this show as a platform for your own career. Mike Frampton: Look, give me another chance. Drew Carey: Sorry but we already have your replacement. Bring out the new kid! (From the back steps out Michael Graves.) Mike Frampton: You have to be kidding me. Michael Graves: MIKEY! Mike Frampton: Graves, we aren't friends anymore. Michael Graves: WHAT? Mike Frampton: Big Daveylicious has somehow gotten the idea that he made me. The bastard thinks that he is the only reason I am here. Damn it Michael, I am the maker of my own destiny. Davey is a snot nosed son of a gun and darn it you are his friend. Thus we are not. Michael Graves: But Mikey! Mike Frampton: I know this is hard. But I do have a message for you to give to Davey. (Mike pulls his fist back.) Michael Graves: Mikey nooo! *CRACK* (Drew Carey falls down.) Mike Frampton: Give that to Davey and tell him soon he'll be getting it from me. Oh by the way, could you also deliver that to Murray. I know you two are intimate so it should be no problem. Michael Graves: I'm not gay! Mike Frampton: Sure you aren't. (Mike walks off as security chases him and Drew receives medical attention. The scene fades out.)
