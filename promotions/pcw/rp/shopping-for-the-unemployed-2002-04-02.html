Shopping for the Unemployed

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/02 OOC: A small break from the Crunkit because I wanted to show Rush's reaction to being fired. The scene opens up on a large grocery store, an outside shot of the building. The name is blurred because of copyrights and a lazy RP writer who doesn’t want to think of a name. Outside, cars of all sizes and shapes can be seen and then the camera switches to the inside of the building. Inside, there are people walking all around the store, shopping for every kind of imaginable food. However, the camera zooms in on two figures. They are of course, two members of the Malice Crew, Rush Mason and Jason Core, recently fired as part of Rupert Healey’s PCW house cleaning. Rush: Look! There! Jason: Rush, what’re you talking about? Rush: A quarter! Quick, grab it! Jason: How much difference does one little- Rush: A LOT! Jason reluctantly kneels down and picks up the quarter, handing it to Rush, who looks down at it as though it were the most beautiful thing he had ever seen. Rush: Do you realize what this means? Jason: No what? Rush: We can buy an extra pack of Ramen Noodles! WHOO! He slaps hands with Jason, who is looking confused at his partner. Rush’s celebratory yelling and such has attracted the attention of the people around him. As he finishes off his celebration, Jason begins to push the cart away towards the first aisle of the store. Rush follows, still clutching the quarter in his palm. Jason begins to pile loads of things into the cart. Rush: WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!?! Jason: Rush, calm down, I know you want Dunkaroos, but that’s not until the next aisle. Rush: THAT’S WAY TOO MANY GROCERIES! Jason: It’s okay, Rush. Just settle down. We can get the Dunkaroos next, I promise. Rush: It’s not about the goddamn dunkaroos! We don’t have enough money to buy all that food; we have to ration ourselves, J-Core. Now, by my calculations, we can each drink two bottles of water a day supplemented by a roll of Ritz crackers. Jason: Don’t be retarded, we’ve both got quite enough money to buy these groceries. Rush: That’s what those banks want you to think, but I know that Healey is siphoning money out of accounts as we speak. I can feel my monetary value, even as a god, going down by the minute. Jason sighs and continues to push the cart, while Rush has ceased his yelling, which once again has caused all of the surrounding people to stare queerly at the Crunk as Hell God. Rush: Please, J-Core, you’ve got to believe me here. I know that you THINK we have enough money, but this is way too much food! What’s this? Milk? We can’t afford luxuries like this! Jason: Rush, calm down. You’ve never been fired before, have you? Rush: Come to think of it no. I’ve been rejected from a football draft, but never fired. Jason: It’s going to be ok. We have enough money between us to last us for a very long time. Rush: I really wish I could believe you. That’s what Stewart Hix thought and look where he is now. Jason: Smoking weed with teenagers on a moldy couch? Rush: Exactly. Jason: But wasn’t that where Hix hung out WHILE he was in PCW? Rush: Beside the point, do YOU want to sit out on the curb with me, dancing for quarters? Maybe you could sing a little ditty, I’m sure that passersby would love to give requests. Jason: Rush, we’re not going to be poor. Rush: How can you be so sure? Jason: I have a bank account of money and you do too. Healey has no access to them. The only person in PCW who does is Duke, and he’s not an idiot, he won’t hand that over to Healey. Rush: Says who? He already has to sit back and watch the most powerful superstars get fired, what makes you think he can stop Rumpert McHealey from taking all of our money anyway? What if Duke loses his President’s job, huh? Do you think that The Phantom Booker will hire us back? Jason: I have no idea… But who says he will be the new owner of PCW? Rush: It doesn’t matter who it is. We better start looking at OSW or RUSH wrestling. Jason: Maybe, but I’m not willing to accept defeat just yet. Not after re-starting my career just to get back to the elite spot in PCW. Continuing up and down the aisles, they arrive at the meat section. Jason begins to order over the counter. Jason: Let’s see, I’ll get one pound of the honey-roasted ham and the same for the roast beef, and add some- However, Rush, interrupts. Rush: Actually, let’s just make that five pounds of tuna fish. The guy at the counter gives Rush a weird look, and then hands him the wrapped package, ready to be taken to the cash register. Jason: What in the hell was that about? Rush: Necessary precautions. A little tuna fish goes a long way, especially when you’re hungry. Jason: Oh god Rush, why don’t you realize that we’re not poor? Rush: Why don’t you realize that you bought the expensive brand of Campbell’s Chicken Soup!?! We can’t be worrying about our health when we’re skin and bones! Jason: Rush, standing in the unemployment line with you is gonna be a bitch. Rush: We should be lucky we still have clothes to stand in. Jason: Boy, what a fun week this is gonna be. The two continue to shop as the scene abruptly cuts out, because fired employees don’t deserve anymore promo time.
