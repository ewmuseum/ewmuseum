The Flight To Fusion

17 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/17/03 You know, the skinheads have really opened my eyes and shown me how the world outside my world works. I have seen things that most people don’t dare to dream about and I have done things most people don’t dare to speak of. None of which make me ashamed of myself…make me want to go back in time and change them, some how making my life different. Making me a different person. I did the things I did because it was what I was trained to do…to be a solider. I followed the commands I was given and pulled the trigger on those who I had to pull it on. To protect this country to no benefit for myself…doing it because that’s what I was trained to do. Most people are given medals for their work in the field and they go down as heroes…I can’t go down as a hero because I don’t exist, no one utters the name Christian MacManus unless it’s some rumour they heard. I have no shame in that, not fearing if I will be forgotten and denied, as soon as someone better comes along and takes my place, killing me just like I was an old legend. It wouldn’t bother me in the least, because of the simple fact…it’s my time and my fate. You can’t fête about something, that’s going to happen wither you choose it to or not, you just have to take the hits as they come. Now, as I am on my way to Fusion, I know that is why I am putting it all on the line to get Haley back, she is the reason that I know that if I die, someone will remember me…she will remember me. Knowing that I was doing the only thing that I knew how, breaking my promise to her…becoming the Chameleon once again. Right now none of it matters though, because in a few short hours, I am going to be stepping into the ring and continuing my winning streak. It’s not what I care about though, I care about Turmoil and the gauntlet match…knowing that it’s my one chance to prove to Makonie and Jason Hilton that I am the one who is on the hunt now and they are the prey. I am not just going to sit back anymore and let them control my princess…I am going to save her and make sure everything is all right again, making sure that Makonie pays in full for his crimes…death. ** Sifting in his plane seat rather uneasy Matt tries to get comfortable, but nothing comes of it, as the plastic arm jabs into his right side and the middle-aged man’s left elbow that continues to bump him, hitting the countless amounts of bruises and cuts that cover his toned 5’8” frame…forcing him to grind his teeth so he doesn’t yell out in pain. It wouldn’t be an unlikely response though, this feels worse then the beating he took at the hands of Violence Jack and Society. Even the slightest movement, has been causing him pain over the past few weeks…he knew it was going to be like this, being booked on every Turmoil since Spring Fling and the massive damage he sustained at the hands of the captors and Makonie. None of it matters, just as long as he can have Haley squeezing him tightly soon; bring pain back to his wounds…pain that he wants instead of being forced to take. Sitting upward in his seat, Jackson stretches making his grey suit wrinkle into thousands of tiny creases…letting him get momentary relief from the cheap coach seat he is in. The CIA would never let him travel in anything better and he wouldn’t allow it…it’s the job of the Chameleon to blend in, which is much easier done in coach, when he looks like nothing more then a casual business man. Letting his dark brown eyes scan the passengers around him…he is glad to see the many heads of full hair and the variety of dress, something that he missed more then he knew while he was at the white power concert…Norticfest. Their cheap excuse to gather a bunch of sweaty neo-nazi’s in a field and let them all believe that they are worth something and that they are doing the right thing by hating minorities…people like Mark Makonie. They are nothing, but scared kids looking for people who will take them in…not caring what they have to say or do, just as long as it looks like they are loved. That’s the reason Makonie is doing the things he is, because he has nothing and he is hoping that in some weird way Jason Hilton will be the father that he never had…I never knew my father, but you don’t see me spouting racial slurs off to feel welcome somewhere. Feeling the pain of the man’s elbow jabbing into his side, the operative turns towards the older man, his angular face full of resolve. “Excuse me sir, would it be at all possible for you to try not to jab your elbow into me?” Matt asks, his voice full of wrought anger and his eyes backing it up, looking like flames dancing over a fire. The old man jerks his neck to face Jackson and lets a smile over come his wrinkled face. He pushes what’s left of his white hair over into a semi-comb over and continues to show Matt his white dentures…the agent responses with a smile of his own, revealing his perfect teeth, feeling bad for snapping at the man. “I am sorry…didn’t even notice that I was doing it. I guess with some of these aching joints, I just happen to miss things that simple,” the man replies, running his bear like hands over the front of his white dress shirt, smoothing out a few of the wrinkles the seven hour plane ride has caused. “It’s all right, I guess I am just a little on edge and my body is so sore…I shouldn’t have snapped on you like that sir. I should be the one saying I am sorry, not you. I was totally out of line,” Matt offers, trying to set the man at ease and not the look of regret that is currently on his face. “So why are you going to Kabul? Business?” The man questions, hoping that Matt won’t let him go back to his lonely nothingness…giving him just that extra bit of niceness that he hasn’t received in a number of years. Praying that Jackson won’t just consider him another old man with nothing to offer…like so many others. “Not exactly, I am going there for PCW’s Friday Fusion, I guess you could call it a business trip,” Matt whispers, trying not to let anyone else on the plane hear him…then again it probably doesn’t matter because no one knows who Matt Jackson is, he is just another face on the roster. Happy like a child who just got his first new bike, the old man sits up giving his full attention to the agent, glad that someone finally took time out of their day to notice him…to do more then just look at him and turn the other way. “I grandson watches wrestling all the time…big fan of Ivan in the OSW. I can’t say I watch it much though,” The man says, with a bright smile, like one that would light up the blind side of the moon. Ivan, who the hell can respect that man? Communism is a failed practice, but yet the blind oaf is too dumb to notice it. Worst off he has Tempest under his thumb and the rest of his rag-tag crew would follow him into anything…how can a kid look up to someone who has done nothing, but preach a dead belief? Trying not to make the man feeling bad Matt nods. “Ah yes Ivan, what a fantastic wrestler…one day I hope that people will look up to me the way that they look up to him…it would be an honour.” “So, do you have a big match on this Fusion or whatever you called it?” “Not really, the match I am in is pretty basic. My real worry is on Turmoil…where I have to partake in a gauntlet match, it almost scares me knowing that every single man in that match is gunning for no one else, but me. The rest of them are meaningless to them…just as long as they can get to me,” Matt explains…not hiding the aggression in his voice. The man pats him on the back, which makes the ex-Ranger wince in pain, but he masks it with a smile…not wanting the man to think that he did something wrong, because in reality it’s Matt that is doing everything wrong. Leaving Norticfest before he could get Haley back…letting her stay in the hands for Makonie for a few more days. The pressure of the situation is finally starting to set in on Jackson, letting the swear form over his brow, only to quickly subside as the cool air of the plane’s air conditioner hits him in the face, like a semi. Flicking a piece of his gelled brown hair out of the way, Matt turns his full attention back to the old man. It’s nice to get a break from being the Chameleon…going back to being Matt Jackson. Even if it’s for a few hours, it’s a lot better then continuing to break the promise I made to Haley…the promise to be human and not just some heartless killing machine created by the government. “Would I know any of these people in this gauntlet and is it going to be tough?” The man asks, pleading for Matt to continue talking to him and not just to turn forward and jam the knees of his black dress pants into the chair in front of him. “Probably not, we are all in something called the Raising Star Division…it’s just basically a chance for most guys to get their feet wet. As for the gauntlet being tough? I am not sure if it will be or not…the advantage is in my hands right now because I only have to face on man, because I come out last, but who really knows…it could happen countless different ways.” Countless different ways in, which I can lose Haley, and thus be forced into conforming to Jason Hilton. Everyone thinks I care about the streak and winning this match, winning the match is something I care about, but not because of the streak, but because of Haley. I want her back and I am going to get her back! “I am sure you will do fine in it, you look like a pretty strong young man. I may actually just have to tune in to see how you do.” “That would be great…it’s always nice to have someone out there watching you,” Matt says, with a hint of disappointment in his voice. I wish Haley was out there watching me and cheering for me, but instead she is in the hands of a raciest bastard…not for long though, not if I have anything to do about it. I will fight to get her back and fighting is exactly what I am doing to do.
