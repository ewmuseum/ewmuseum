Soothing The Savage Beas-...Um...Valet.

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/03/02 The shot opens up back at the Hotel Room of the 1st Couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall where they have returned to after the events at Friday Fusion. As usual they have spared no expense and having no money restrictions placed on them due to Ellie~!’s family’s money, Xavier money from Scientology and their Snapple~! endorsement. “GRRRRRRRRRR!!! That was, like, SO NOT HOW IT WAS MEANT TO HAPPEN!” Xavier’s Preppy valet comes storming into the room, a look of pure spite on her face, her mascara running sending trickles of Black liquid down her chin. Eleanor : “I HATE HER I HATE HER I HATE HER I HATE HER!” Ellie~! is wearing a skinny fitting Pink t-shirt with Heaven Sent written across her 32B chest in sparkly silver. With this she has a pair of flared and faded jeans with Sliver sequinned stars down the legs and a rainbow fabric belt. On her feet she has a pair of Red suede Adidas sneakers and on her wrists she has numerous bracelets and bands, along with a chunky watch. She stamps her feet in frustration as she yells through to the bathroom from which she has just emerged. She dries under her eyes with a tissue, still looking visibly upset. Eleanor : “Was going to take her face and....And.....AND RUB IT INTO THE MAT!” She grabs a pillow and starts to run it onto the duvet, pushing down so herd that her fingernails, with the varnish recently removed, go White at the tips and her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights falls over her hate-filled face. Eleanor : “Then I was going to BASH her head into it!” Ellie~! then lifts up the large pillow and starts to hit it against the bed, she lets go and grabs another pillow, holding it above her head. Eleanor : “Than I wanted to grab a chair and...And....CAVE HER SLUTTY LITTLE SKULL IN!!!” She swings the other pillow down onto the one on the bed, over and over again, tears still running down her cheek. She then bounces the pillow off the other one and it lands the other side of the bed before she collapses onto the bed herself and mutters to herself in a sulky voice. Eleanor : “It’s not fair...It’s not fair...It’s not fair...” As she sits on the bed, upset at not being able to inflict any real harm on Jessica, the voice of Xavier is heard shouting from the bathroom. “Don’t be too upset my little Fuck Ferret....I mean, you don’t want to beat her up too badly, after all, you might catch something!” His words appear to offer his valet some comfort as she pulls her hair out of her face and down her back, mumbling to herself. Eleanor : “Yeah...Like Whorolitus!“ The Scientologist doesn’t hear her soft, agitated mumble and continues to try and calm his hot headed valet down. “The best way to hurt her isn’t by hitting her my little Poon Puppy, it isn’t by insulting her and it certainly isn’t by pinning her...” On the bed Ellie~! looks up and through the door, now taking notice of Xavi’s words, her pale Blue eyes Red and puffy. Eleanor : “It isn’t....” She speaks in a snuffly voice before blowing her nose. Just wait and see her face in 8 days time....The look of pure anguish and fear as I stand above her fallen, bleeding, broken boyfriend, the father of her child, holding his Continental Title, his very existence at my mercy...Then you will see true hurt...” The thought of this brings the familiar evil smile back to Ellie~!’s face as she imagines the anguish of Jessica. “....Not that her husband is unable to move his legs...Or his arms...Or anything below his neck...” Her puffy eyes seemingly come to life as Xavier continues, a sparkle of malice returning to them. “.....But that her meal ticket, the man she whores herself out to for money and fame...The reason she isn’t in some single mother’s hostel....Has been put out of commission!” From the bathroom walks Xavier with a wicked grin on his face as he towel dries his long, Auburn hair. He is wearing nothing but a White towel wrapped and tucked around his waist. Xavier : “So what if you didn’t get a clean slap across her ugly face...Imagine that same face staring into the ring at Spring Fling, her hands held up to it, eyes wide in shock and jaw dropping in disbelief as she sees her boyfriends head bounce off the mat and his lifeless body drop limp onto the canvas....Now THAT is entertainment.” The Gold Patron Meritorious grabs a Purple hair tie from the dresser and ties his hair loosely back before leaping onto the bed beside Ellie~! where she drapes herself across his chest, a look of hatred for Jessica present in her eyes. Xavier : “See my little Poon Puppy, you need not lower yourself to even touching the likes of her, she is below you....Leave the so-called family to me...” Ellie~! is smirking at the Scientologist’s words as Xavier strokes her long wavy hair, relishing the task he has ahead of him, brought about by the falll-out of March Madness and those 3 Minutes & 8 Seconds that went on to destroy Asylum. Xavier : “Just picture the scene....April 7th, around 11pm, the Emergency Room, Exam room 2, Howard lying on the bed, praying to that God most of PCW’s braindead worship for the feeling to return to his limbs....His girlfriend crying her tears of pity all over him, holding their son’s head the other way so he can’t see what his father is reduced to...The brother, Justin, the man I HELPED FREE...Reduced to nothing but a Blood or Kidney donor for the supposed PCW legend!” Both of them are grinning broadly at his sick prediction, picturing the scene of misery he could cause. Xavier : “....And Jeremy, as you would have seen at Fusion, you unlawful oppression of me has made me seek allies....People who think on the same level as me...As opposed to the likes of you and Miyagawa in Asylum these 2 are my kind of people...Politically Incorrect.” Ellie~! rolls off him and rubs her eyes which seem to be less puffy now, she grabs a pillow and slips under the covers, muttering softly. Eleanor : “Wrestling....Bored...Sleep...Do it quietly and don’t wake me up...” The Scientologist sighs at his valet’s reverting t character as she tires of wrestling talk and gets comfortable for her nap as Xavier seemingly looks into nothingness as the events at Fusion play out in his head. Xavier : “Unlike in Asylum where I was Black Sheep, where I was the Outsider, where I was made to feel unwelcome because of my faith despite what I did for you all at Shang-Haied....Unlike all that I have things in common with Powell and Lovecraft...” A sneer starts to form in the left corner of his mouth as his Green eyes look right through the camera lens which zooms right in on his face to exclude the sleeping Eleanor. Xavier : “....We all HATE people like you! You see Jeremy, you may have somehow managed to pull a successful wrestling career out despite an obvious lack of talent, you may be able to afford a nice little house, a trophy girlfriend and a little brat to take all your money when you are dead.....You even mange to afford a huge fleet of cars and do up old ones in your spare time between oppressing people’s beliefs and insulting my lovely, pure, innocent and loving girlfriend....” He looks down to the peacefully sleeping Ellie~! and the camera gets a shot of her at ease face in the midst of her nap before returning to Xavier’s face as he shakes his head. Xavier : “...But you were never meant to have that money Jeremy, you lack the class to handle it....Although we all know your girlfriend’s lack of class means she’ll handle anything for a twenty! You are what is wrong with society Jeremy, just because you work hard, just because you spill your blood doesn’t mean you should be rewarded for it!” The Gold Patron Meritorious lowers his voice as she notices Ellie~! fidgeting as she sighs softly in her sleep. Xavier : “One percent of the USA owns ninety seven percent of the wealth for a reason Jeremy, people like me and William S. Powell are meant to have money, we do good things with it and together are doing Des Moines, Iowa a great favour by building a Church of Scientology to it, helping it flourish. So what if an old folks home is gone? What did they contribute? Who cares about a school playing field? Kids have no money to put into the economy.....We do good things like that, while you, you spend it all on cars...While I benefit the community you pollute the atmosphere and choke their children with your exhaust fumes....” The Gold Patron Meritorious shakes his head at the thought, having no shame in resorting to using the environment in his constant battle to deface Jeremy Howard. Xavier : “....And to think that the fans boo me and cheer you...” He shakes his head before looking down at his peacefully sleeping valet. Xavier : “....I’ll never understand...And I don’t think I even want to understand anything that involves a bunch of prejudice, un-washed miscreants...Now if you don’t mind...My valet, one that doesn’t give birth to children out of wedlock and the call others classless needs some sleep and so do I....” He stares into the camera for a few seconds as it starts to fade to Black. Xavier : “What are you waiting for...Go!” With his agitated face snarling at he camera we hit Black.
