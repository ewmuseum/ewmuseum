To Hell and back...Even God makes mistakes....Part 1

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-18-2002 Sometimes, even God makes a mistake……… Scene opens into a luxurious office of pure blinding white. The desk is white, the walls are white, the carpeting is white as are the couch and chairs. A small man in a white suit sits behind the white desk, his white hair brushed back to reveal an extremely handsome and pleasant face. His eyes are not white though, they are a very pale blue, and it would seem that he possesses all the knowledge of the universe within the depths of those strange orbs. He smiles and shakes his head as he begins to speak…. Man: You all know me in some way or another, though I will admit that I have gone by many different names. Some know me as Allah…Others, Jehovah. To some I am merely God. To be honest, none truly suit me. For our purposes here you can know me for who I really am…Bob. That’s right. Your Lord God is just an all knowing fellow named Bob. Simple, yet to the point. Bob reaches into his desk and pulls out a stack of white papers. He leafs through them until he finds what he is looking for, smiles, and turns his gaze back upon the camera. Bob: Many have questioned the nature of God, or me, but few truly understand how difficult this job of mine is. I mean, I have to dictate where and when someone dies, who gets a miracle and who doesn’t, when babies are born and the list goes on and on. For me, that translates into mountains of paperwork, and I despise paperwork. But, I AM God, so I do my best to make sure that mistakes are kept to a minimum. However, sometimes even the most perfect of beings screws up, and that is what I am here to talk about. I want to tell you all a story. A story of a man who died at the wrong time. The story of a man who shook things up here so much that we were forced to send him back to his earthly domain, although I did have some fun with him along the way. It all started…. Scene cuts to show a busy street in downtown Tokyo, Japan. Cars and small delivery trucks zip this way and that, and the pedestrians go about their business. The shot comes to rest on a fellow who doesn’t seem to fit in. For one, he stands nearly two heads taller than the Japanese citizens, but that is not really why he stands out. He stands out because even in the hustle and bustle of busy Tokyo, Venneman Pendragon wears a golden shirt of chain mail armor, dark leather pants, a golden band that holds his hear back and he has a sword strapped to his side. Now if that is not odd in a place where people are suited and the only weapon you might catch on one is a cell phone, I don’t know what is. Pendragon is shuffled along with the crowd, looking here and there at the huge buildings interspersed with the more ancient landmarks, when someone screams. The shot cuts to show a young woman lying on the ground, reaching for what appears to be a baby carriage. The carriage, however, speeds away down a slight incline towards a very busy street… Venneman: Good CHRIST! I must save yonder maiden’s little one! Instantly, Pendragon begins to sprint toward the carriage. It rolls into the street directly in front of a speeding box truck. Pendragon reaches it just in time, and pushes it out of the way. He sighs knowing the baby is safe and turns, only to be hit head on by the truck. He is flung through the air, a distance of some twenty feet, and lands with a sickening thud. The screen goes black……… “What in the name of all ‘tis holy happened? Where am I? Why am I unable to move? Who plays this vicious prank upon the Lord King?” Suddenly, the screen begins to turn from black to a harsh red color. We can see Pendragon, still clothed in what he had on before, only this time, his armor is black, his sword is black, and he is shackled to a wall. Flames lick up all around him but he pays no mind to the heat. He flexes his arms and tests the chain’s strength and then smiles…. “If one shackles a king, one must make sure to use a strong chain.” With a mighty pulls, Pendragon breaks the chains that hold his wrists and rubs each of his sore joints in turn. He then heads off, in search of some answers. He walks along a raised path that is bordered by rising flames on either side. At last, he comes to a large outcropping upon the stone path. In this outcropping lies a man. He is chained to the ground and the flesh upon both of his legs appears to have been flayed away. Blood drips off of the clean bone, and Pendragon gapes in horror. Venneman: What treachery is this? Who would do such a thing to a man? Who art thou? The man opens his eyes and stares directly at Pendragon. He shakes his head in pain… Man: Then you know not where you are? You may call me Jeffrey. Who are you? Venneman: I am the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. Where am I? Jeffrey: Why, HELL of course, King! A shudder courses through Pendragon and suddenly he is not so steady on his feet. He sits down quickly with a thud as his ass hits the ground. He gasps in disbelief…. Venneman: This must be a mistake! Lord Pendragon is a man of ultimate nobility! I am unmatched in virtue be it amongst commoners or in battle… Jeffrey: Make no mistake. This is hell, and each man has his own. Just wait till they find you yours. Venneman: And what be your transgression? To deserve this? Jeffrey: My hell is to be eaten alive. Slowly. The demons come and hack off pieces to eat, and then they leave. Once there is nothing left, my body returns to me and the cycle starts once more. Ludicrous, is it not? Venneman: You look quite familiar. Jeffrey is it? Might I have the courtesy of thy last name? Jeffrey stares at Pendragon, amazed… Jeffrey: Why, DAHMER OF COURSE! Pendragon leaps up and draws his sword…. Venneman: A vile eater of man flesh. Thy has planted the seeds thy reap now, murdering cannibal! I shall listen to no more of this. Jeffrey pleads with him to stay, but Pendragon has already started down the path once more, Excalibur held in an offensive position… “Hell. I cannot be dead. I have so much more left to do! And if I am dead, then surely I deserve better than Dahmer. This is ludicrous! Someone around here must know the truth of what goes on here. And mark me, I shall find him!” The scene fades from Pendragon in Hell and switches back to the white office. Bob, er, God, sits at his desk, a small smile on his face… Bob: Yeah, even I make mistakes. Pendragon was not supposed to die by the truck. And he most certainly was not meant for Hell. I probably could have pulled him from that dark domain as soon as I was alerted of his arrival there, but GOLLY! How often does one of his caliber end up in the land of the Dark Lord? This would be interesting. Pendragon and Lucifer. That would be a conversation I wanted to see, so I left him there for the time being. Heck, even god has to have a little entertainment now and again. You regular mortals are so boring, always doing the same thing! And up here, the internet is shoddy….We are kind of out of the loop, so dial up is the best we have. I know, it sucks. That is why I leaped at the chance to watch this warrior wreak havoc on the confines of hell! God shuffles some papers and makes a neat stack on his desk… Bob: Yeah, sometimes if gets pretty boring up here. Everyone kneeling and praying to me. Sometimes, I wish I had someone to talk to on my level. I do have a lot to share. And, just maybe, someone could shake things up here a bit….Angels become so damned complacent! Keep watching, though, my children. The story only gets better from here….Good night for now! God chuckles as the scene slowly fades, though not to black…The screen merely goes white…. >>>TO BE CONTINUED
