Title: Et tu, Brute? Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Today Caesar's Palace. Named after perhaps the greatest battlefield general in the world's history, Julius Caesar. His intution, bravery, and never-say-die attitude brought him victories on battlefields from Persia to Gaul, to even the British Isles. So, maybe it isn't all that surprising that Politically Incorrect sit in the lobby of Caesar's Palace, fresh off of a round of gambling at Bally's. Sure, they go for the atmosphere, but there is something else there. The atmosphere is breathtaking. Breathtakingly symbolic. Politically Incorrect feels a bit like a resurrected Caesar. They mightily conquered the tag team division for a month and a half, only to have it stolen from them by Tyler Nelson and The Phantom Booker. Yet, unlike Gaius Julius Caesar, Politically Incorrect have a second chance. As the phoenix that is PCW rises from the ashes, it does so with a tournament, unlike any it has ever held. Eight tag teams in a round robin! Has PCW ever even HAD eight tag teams before? Probably not. But, despite the inconvience, Politically Incorrect looks at this as another way to cement their reputation as the greatest tag team in history. Da Westsidaz never had to go through this. Nor the Blood Brothers. Nor Golden Game. Which is why the politicians have redoubled their efforts. After all, a place in history awaits! ::It is two tired politicians that sit in Caesar's lobby, looking around them at fine Roman arquitecture. Roger Schultz sits with them, clad in his powder blue suit., carrying a larger briefcase to carry Politically Incorrect's winnings. Powell and Lovecraft are all smiles, apparently having done quite well in the casino. Powell grins, counting out a stack of $100 bills: William S. Powell: 1......2..............3.............4.............5... ::Let's skip a bit, shall we...:: William S. Powell: 14....15! 15 $100 dollar bills! I won $1500 today at the blackjack tables! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And I $500 at craps, but, we must not be content to rest on the laurels of our windfall. After all, tonight is the return to action of Politically Incorrect! William S. Powell: Yes! It has been far too long since PCW Supremacy...our path to vengeance against the Heartbreakers and Tyler Nelson begins this eve! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Indeed.. It is difficult to believe that the Price Brothers persist against us. They are stubborn newcomers, but they are just that. Newcomers. From the Primetime Wrestling Academy, looking to make a name off of Politically Incorrect. It's quite irritating at this point. The Prices do not comprehend what they are doing. They are actually looking forward to stepping into the ring with Politically Incorrect. William S. Powell: They believe us to be some pushover tandem, they completely ignore our past history! They underestimate us. US! POLITICALLY INCORRECT! THEY IGNORE THE GREATEST TEAM IN HISTORY! Roger Schultz: Gentlemen, Prices, if I can call you gentlemen, I advise you not to ignore Politically Incorrect. These men do not enjoy being trifled with, and/or ignored. They are Politically Incorrect, and will have no problem with destroying you. To them, you are nothing more than an annoyance, a mosquito in dire need of squashing. And squash you they shall....for they are Politically Incorrect. Election Day is this evening. Get used to it. ::Fade to black.:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
