Fuck Rehab, I Want Revenge

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07 - 08 - 02 Scene opens up in a semi-small fitness room where a man can be seen sitting in the corner with his back to the camera. He bends at the knees, and back up again, performing reps of squats over and over. The camera zooms in on the weights and notices there happen to be 500 pounds attached to each side. The man is lifting up and down, his breathing is a bit rapid but nobody seems to notice that. Across the room there are a couple guys wearing red collared, white polo shirts, with a badge that has each of their names. James, and Tony. The two men stare bewildered at the man doing the reps, neither breaking the silence. Suddenly the man lifts the bar a final time before collapsing to one knee. The bar crashes down across his shoulders, as he supports the weight, the two guys rush over to help him remove it, but he raises a hand to stop them. He forces the weight up as he shakily rises to his feet again, finally replacing the bar on it's holder. As he spins, he glares at the two men who are just standing there shaking their heads, as the mans face is now known. Tony: That was insane man. You realize that was 2 hours? James: WE could have been fired for allowing you to put yourself through that. That was our ass, not yours. So next time why don't you just take it a bit easier. Tony: Yeah Ricky, I mean... Ricky raises his hand again, causing the men to stop and silence ensues. Ricky: There won't be a next time. I am ready to return. James: Uh, Ricky, I'm sorry bud, but you are going to need a medical clearance to return to action. The doctor's evaluation, said 6 months of extensive rehabilitation. Not 1. Ricky: I said...I am ready. You know as well as I do, NOBODY could subject themselves to the punishment I do everyday. Everyday I am in here pumping weights, doing squats, crunches, bench pressing, curls, sit ups, pull ups, I DO IT ALL! There is NOBODY in here that is going to tell me I'm not ready for a return. Ricky storms from the fitness room as he towels the sweat from his face. The two trainers stare at each other for a brief second before Tony begins to speak. Tony: We could make a recommendation. James: He's not ready. There is no way he could be ready. Tony: Not ready? How can you say that. 500 pounds on that bar, 2 hours, solid, non-stop and he was barely even breathing hard. We have seen this man kill himself over the past month, and all you can say is he's not ready? I mean hell, he does pull ups, in reps of 150. He does squats in reps of HOURS, not by count. He can do 85 sit ups a minute. This man is in the best shape of his life. So what? He hurt his knee. It was a small time injury. A minor set back. They THOUGHT it was an ACL, WE know better now. There is NO WAY a man with a ruptured ACL could work out that way. It is physically impossible. James: Before I make any recommendations, I want him to see the facility physician. I want a through examination. I would like to know what the physician thinks as well. Tony: Deal. The two men exit out of the training room and shut off the light, closing the door behind them. Once in the corridor, they spin to see Ricky standing right beside the door. They both jump being a bit startled. James: Ricky..Uh... Ricky: Save it. Tony, thank you for your support. James, I always knew you were a snake in the grass. James: Ricky. I just don't want to see you back in here, which is exactly what is going to happen if you are released to soon. Think about it Ricky. You are barely 19 years old, you have suffered a severe back injury, one that would end most peoples careers. One that got you before your career ever really began. Ricky: And I walked away from it. After 2 years of Rehab, here I am. Standing, walking and WRESTLING. It is what I do! James: Now you got this knee injury. I just want to make sure things are going to go ok from here. Ricky: Things will be fine. I will be fine. Everything will be fine. Just make the recommendation. James: I can't do that Ricky. Not until you are checked out by the physician. Ricky: Why are you doing this to me? Listen to Tony. I mean damn, I am in the best shape of my entire career right now. So what, I have a bruised knee. Big deal, bumps and bruises happen. All I need.... Tony: Ricky. Ricky stops talking as Tony cut him off. He is a bit shocked by the audacity he has. He listens as Tony starts to speak out again. Tony: Ricky. Maybe, just maybe James is right on this one. Regardless of the doctors diagnosis, I have my recommendation set in stone. But you have to understand, maybe you aren't ready. Maybe you need a bit more rehab. I don't see it, nor do I think that you do. However, just to satisfy all minds, let's head upstairs and go get you checked out, so you can get back into what you love. Ricky: Fine. If this is what it is going to take to get me out of here, and back into action then so be it. Let's go. Tony leads Ricky down the hallway who shoots James a pissed off glance before the two of them enter the marble floored elevator. Ricky reaches out and stops the doors from closing. He steps out of the elevator. Ricky: I am going to take the stairs. Tony: You don't have anything to prove to me Ricky. Ricky: It's for me. I figure a bit more exercise can't hurt. The doors slide shut as Ricky heads into the stairwell. He starts rounding the corners on the staircase as he watches the floors go by. He glances at the numbers over the door each time he passes. He gets to the 5th floor when he suddenly hits a puddle, where someone had spilled some water, or something, and he hits the floor. He lands directly on his knee as he grimmaces a bit. He gets to his feet and dusts his pants off then continues up to the 8th floor. He exits into the hallway and sees Tony standing by the elevator. Tony: Feel better now? Ricky: Much. Let's do this. The two of them head down the long empty hallway, and enter the first door on the left. Ricky takes a seat as Tony goes over to talk to the registration clerk. They speak for a few moments before Tony calls Ricky over and they disappear into the back office. The head down another shorter hallway and enter exam room 4. Ricky hops up on the table, sitting at the end with his legs dangling off, as Tony sits in the chair in the corner. The doctor comes in reading the chart from Ricky's previous medical history. Dr: Well, this is quite an accomplishment. A torn ACL and you are already back up on your feet. Impressive to say the least. Ricky: It wasn't torn. Just bruised and battered is all. Dr: It says right here, that it was torn. Oh wait, here is a small note on the reverse side, yes, I see. Anyhow, impressive still none the less. It is my understanding that you feel you are ready to return to action. Ricky: Correct. Dr: It is also my understanding that you were sent for 6 months of correction rehab and you have done merely a month. Ricky: Correct. Dr: Well, only time will tell. Let's have a look at you. The doctor pushes the leg of Ricky's workout pants up and he begins to feel about the knee. He notices a small knot that sends pain shooting throughout the leg, every time he touches it. Dr: Does that hurt? Ricky: Didn't even know it was there. Ricky clenches his teeth as the doctor, squeezes about it a few more times. Tony suddenly distracts the doctor. Tony: Hey doc, I was wondering... Dr: Give me just a minute, I will answer all your questions. The doctor feels around a bit more not finding anything else, then lowers the pants leg. Dr: Well, it would seem that there is some swelling and internal bruising. Which is a VERY bad thing. However, you said it caused you no pain. So, my guess is it is nothing major, we can run some x-rays if you'd like, just to be sure. Ricky: Nah, I'm not worried about it. It's nothing I am sure. Dr: Well, other then that, everything looks good. Amazing recovery Ricky. Amazing. I would be more than happy to sign your release. Ricky: Thanks doc. Dr: Now, did you have a question Tony? Tony: Nope, you covered it. I was just going to point out the knot is all. Dr: Good enough. The doctor turns and leaves the room, as Tony shuts the door. Tony: What the fuck Ricky!? I thought you said... Ricky: I'm fine. Tony: I saw the pain Ricky. That knee is not ready to go is it? Don't you dare lie to me either Ricky. Ricky: Look, I fell in some water coming up the stairs. That is all it's from, I guarantee it. Don't sweat it. Tony: Ricky..why are you doing this? You know... Ricky: I know that I am ready to return as well as you do. My knee is fine. If the knot was pre-existing, you think I would have been able to do squats for 2 hours at a time? You think I would have walked up the stairs. Damn you Tony, don't back out on me now. Tony: You know what, I will still sign the recommendation, BUT, if you happen to come back here, I want no part of it. If that knee gets destroyed I want nothing to do with it. Ricky: All I am asking is for you to sign the paper. I don't plan on coming back. Tony: Fine. Consider yourself released for action. Ricky: Thank you.
