Prelude to something retro.

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 15, 2002 [We begin a week ago, just after Eddie Mandrake’s match at Turmoil, and Eddie’s first win in over a month. Eddie, still wearing the clothes that he wrestled in, and Spider, who is holding onto a bottle of champagne. The halls are well lighted, and it looks rather fresh in the corridors heading to Eddie’s locker room. There is a lot of hooting and hollering coming from the two men.] Eddie: YEAH BABY! I FINALLY SNAPPED THAT FUCKING CURSE I HAD ON ME! WHOO! Spider: YEAH! I TOLD YOU I’D LEAD YOU TO THE PROMISED LAND! Eddie: Now…I have a title shot at the cruiserweight title! BOO YEAH! Spider: Here’s some hooch for ya boy! [Spider takes the champagne bottle and shakes it. It sprays everywhere, but Spider tries to aim it to Eddie’s open mouth. Eddie manages to get a lot of it into his mouth, but most of it ends up on his chest. Eddie closes his mouth and swishes the booze around, then swallows. Eddie holds his hand to his face, and moves it up and down, testing to see if he can still see. He then gains a puzzled look on his face.] Eddie: Hey…what gives man? I thought this was going to be some good hooch. Spider: I’m not going to lie to you. It is rather cheap. I picked it up from the Niagara vineyard before we left, and I tried to ferment it behind the radiator in my Charger. Eddie: Uh…Why? Spider: Well…we are not done by a long shot. Next week you have Cyclonica… Eddie: Please, she’s nothing. Spider: Well…we will watch the match later, but in the mean time, I want to introduce you to someone. Come. [Spider and Eddie only walk for another thirty seconds back to Eddie’s locker-room. When they enter there is a rather young man in the locker room, on a cell phone. He’s wearing a rather nice suit. He has blonde hair and brown eyes. He was just finishing a conversation on his cell phone…] Man: Ok, I have to go, the clients are in the room…bye. [He hits the end button on his cell phone.] Man: Hey guys... Eddie: Who the hell are you? Spider: Eddie…I would like to introduce to you, John Goldwyn, the son of the man who created an entertainment empire, but retreated into nothingness. His family sold the company to MGM, and he is now one of the proprietary share-holders. John: How are you? [John extends a hand out to Eddie. Eddie simply stands there with a rather sour look on your face. John’s welcoming smile turns into a face of indifference.] Spider: Don’t mind Eddie, he always has the DTA attitude. John: I see. Well, I have the entire set of paperwork ready… Eddie: Whoa…hang on…what are you planning Spider? Spider: I was going to ask you this eventually. You see, Mr. Goldwyn, represents MGM, and with that entertainment group…They want to revive a certain televised athletic competition… Eddie: You mean you want me to defect to CCW? Spider: What the hell are you thinking? Of course not…I am still getting money from them, and I don’t want you to go there and annihilate everyone…ever. I want them to survive, so I can get more cash from them. Anyway, he wants to go and deliver one new episode of American Gladiators to the brass at MGM…if it’s good enough, then they will deliver it to TNN to show as a Primetime Special. This means…more money in your pocket, more money in my pocket…and some more recognition in your hat. John: Indeed…you get to prove to the world, that you are one of the top athletes in North America. [Eddie looks to the left with the gears in his brain turning, thinking of the Machiavellian move to his life.] Hmm…American Gladiators…I remember that show…most of the guys on that show were less than 200 pounds and were just handled like rag dolls…I am more than that…I hate to ask my masters what the fate of those Gladiators are going to be. Even more than that…my opponent…what fate will he suffer. [Eddie turns to John.] Eddie: Who will be my opponent? John: We are still determining that…However, we have it narrowed down to Coz Worthington, Wesley Berry, and Rico Costantino… Eddie: Give me all of them, and you have a deal. Spider: Eddie, are you nuts?! All those guys have either won the tournament, or were runner-ups to it. Eddie: And what the hell am I? I am a former bounty hunter, beating insurmountable odds is my fucking profession! Do you doubt my ability within an athletic competition?! I mean, come on! I’m a pro wrestler now, what are you…New?! Spider: Okay…Okay…You want three other contenders, plus the gladiators, that’s fine…but I am warning you…do not over extend yourself. Eddie: Fine…now get out of here, I need to watch the Cruiserweight Title match…ALONE! Spider: John…we’ll discuss the main factors of the appearance outside. [Spider and John leave the locker-room. Eddie has a seat on the bench, as the television monitor begins to flicker to the match in progress…] I can’t believe that someone can fall so far from sanity to think that they are actually immortal. Although immortals cannot be killed, they can be slowed down…stopped…but never killed. Hell, my constant battles with others of my kind…will be nothing in comparison. I was simply policing the streets, eliminating them of the filth that were my victims…heh…It seems rather entertaining at the time…placing the eyeballs in random places around the death trap…it gives new meaning to the words “Being Watched.” Making paintings out of the blood and entrails of whatever was left…Some people thought it was art…when it was really…my job… Jeff: The goddess is going to destroy Tomas! Dave: She’s targeted the throat of Tomas, which is a intelligent battle plan from the champion. Duke: If Tomas can’t deep throat it after this match, I’m gonna be pissed. Dave: Must you make sexual innuendos like that on the females? Cyclonica gets back up, pulling Tomas with her into the corner. She props her up before backing up and raising her right leg. Then she snaps in two snapping roundhouse kicks into Tomas’s throat before spinning around and cracking a hooking side kick into the back of her head. Tomas falls down on all fours on the mat as Cyclonica spins around and crouches down next to her, glaring into her face. Cyclonica: Cry for me, child… Dave: Cyclonica is sadistic… Jeff: Sadistic chicks are hot…because that means they’re kinky, and that means they take it up the— Dave: Asshole… Jeff: Oh, so you know? The Neo Void…what is it? Is it a place? Is it a state of mind? Is it a group of people? Or is it a piece of mould on cheese? This place where Cyclonica claims to be a goddess from…Hell, it could be could be on my old patrol route. I may have encountered this person repeatedly, and yet I may have spared her…from a fate worse than death…capture and interrogation by me. If anyone saw what I did to that idiot Trigger…They should know what that was…The sickening thud of that chair onto the head…and the repeated shots before it, drawing the fluid of life…I will draw her life fluid…even if it takes me a lifetime to do it. Duke: Almost lost…like father, like daughter. Cyclonica begins to sit up, shaking her head slowly as Tomas gets back to her feet and runs for the ropes. She bounces off and flips over Cyclonica, nailing her with a flipping snapmare onto the sitting Divine Prophetess! Cyclonica rolls out of the ring as Tomas watches her closely. The fans begin to cheer louder, knowing what’s about to happen now. Dave: We’re about to fly outside here! Cyclonica walks around, shaking her head of the pain on the outside as Tomas bounces against the rope in wait. Cyclonica suddenly turns back around just as Tomas comes flying over the top with a cross body!!!! Dave: CROSS BODY!! CROSS BODY!! Duke: WEAK MOVE! WEAK MOVE!! She hits Cyclonica…but Cyclonica drops down only to one knee!! Also, the Divine Prophetess catches Tomas in her arms, driving her ribs into her knee! Dave: Cyclonica countered with a rib breaker on Tomas, but that most definitely wasn’t intentional! Jeff: She’s Celestial…it’s in their blood to counter automatically, even if their bodies are under assault! Cyclonica stands back up with Tomas in her hands, and surprisingly throws the girl back in a fallaway slam on the ramp! Jeff: ONSLAUGHT!! MAKE YOUR FATHER PROUD!! … My bones crack, my body yearns for the pain…the fear…the screams, that she can provide. I will take her to the central hive…where she will be feast upon, by not only my superiors, but my colleagues, my inferiors, and myself…taking the biggest piece. It will be a waste of a life…But the beginning of a new one. Everyone has a plan for a dark age…Whereas myself…I only seek advancement. The furthering of the person called…”me”. Hell, Inphino Blitz proved that simple guns cannot stop her...on the other hand…an Agoniser…WILL stop her. [Eddie begins to crack his hands, with intensity beginning to increase. The pulse begins to rise, respirations increase, and Eddie’s eyes grow as the match between Cyclonica and Teresa Tomas ends.] You thirst for blood as I do…Perhaps we are not so different after all. A mistress of Pain…and I…a master of death. It’s a damn shame that we are not on the same side. Nonetheless, when we meet, It may be our battle of Armageddon. Only one side will be victorious…and that side…will…be….MINE! [Eddie arises from his seated position, staring at the screen where Cyclonica has a referee as her play thing.] Eddie: It’s been done. Goddess my ass, she is nothing more than a common slave…In seven days…who will survive…the quasi-goddess…or the Mandrake…Of course…it will be the Mandrake. Underestimate me…and you’ll pray that I won’t take you alive. [He walks out of the room into the shower portion of the locker-room as we fade to black…for now.]
