Is This The End?

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/04/03 You did what I didn’t think was possible; you made it rain…rain blood. With a heavy hand, Matt pulls open the door to his apartment, gritting his teeth in pain, as he applies pressure to his left leg, but he takes it in stride, stepping out of the way in the narrow hall, so that the door can open. The blood is once again flowing from his leg, putting pressure on it, helped for a while, but it wasn’t a permanent solution. Standing in the apartment doorway, he notices most of the lights are off and only hears the sound of voices, in the dimly lit room, just behind the wall, that his green eyes are currently locked onto. Without a second though about it, Jackson reaches inside his long red trench coat, and pulls out the silenced pistol. Taking a few strides across the tile floor in the kitchen of his apartment, Matt quickly reaches the door…he presses himself against the powder white plaster wall, and peers around the corner, making certain that everything is all right…in his right hand, the pistol is gripped tightly in his black gloved hand. Seeing it’s only Haley and her father, he breaths a sigh of relief, placing the gun back into its shoulder holster, which is enclosed in the coat. He walked into the living room, with a limp unfamiliar to Haley, sending her instantly into a frenzy of worry, and she should be worried, Matt is forming his own river of blood on the blue carpet of the living room. His leg is getting worse by the second and something needs to be done about it now. Walking past the two leather recliners, Jackson stops in the middle of the room, next to the large oak coffee table, which is in front of the leather couch, that Haley and Jason are currently occupying. Off to his right, is the big screen television he recently bought, but other then that, the room is bare for the most part. Stopping the Stampede, tries to regain some of his breath, as his angular face goes pale, the drips of sweat pouring down his brow, burning his eyes a bit, as a small bit escapes his attempt not to let it fall in. Everything is getting blurry, as he stands, bracing himself on the table, trying not to fall flat on his face, but it’s almost inevitable, that he’s going to pass out soon, his body is losing and has lost a lot of blood, it’s only a matter of time, until he is flat on the ground, vulnerable, just like he is in PCW…fearful of another loss, his stronghold, is broken through, and it’s only a matter of time, until everyone around him knows. Haley leaps up off of the couch and reaches Matt, in a quick few strides, her denim jeans swaying back and forth, but even with her quick speed…she is just half a second to late, as his legs give out from under him, sending him toppling onto the floor. His head lying in a pool of his own blood, his full five foot eight inch body shaking sweat pouring down it, covering the tight black body suit underneath his trench coat in a layer of water, forcing his body to go cold. He’s slowly going into shock, despite his best efforts, there is no way around his fate. This normally unthreatening wound, is threatening wound to the young agent, because he can’t just go to a hospital like normal people and get it stitched up…they would place him in jail for murder, something he doesn’t want to have happen at this moment. Haley picks up his head, which is painted red, from the blood it encountered when he hit the floor. Putting it into her curvy chest, letting the blood wipe on her light blue shirt, the tears slowly, but methodically down her slender cheeks and on to the face of Matt, who is too busy slipping in at out of consciousness to know what is happening. Just trying to keep himself aware enough, so he can tell them how to help him…how to help him not die. Not yet, I won’t let some hick beat me…not today, his mind sneers, forcing the images of Abnear into his head…from the moment the van pulled up and the brother of Stephan stepped out, to challenge him, until the moment in which, he pulled the trigger, ending the life of the man who wanted to take his…who might still take his life. It’s far too soon to tell exactly, but Jackson can feel the cold dead hand of the Grim Reaper on his shoulder. Even as much as he wants to deny it, it’s not possible, he’s never felt this feeling before, but it’s just as he thought it would be. “Matt…” Her voice cuts the silence in the room, even though it’s more of a whisper. Almost choking on the tears, continuing to hold his head tightly to her body, fearing that she is going to soon lose him, but she knows if she doesn’t act quickly, it’s only a matter of time…until she is crying this same way on top of his coffin. She doesn’t know what to do though. Her mind is so blurry, watching the blood spill out onto the floor, seeing his pale white face covered in his life force. His barrel of a chest, moving up and down, slower and slower as the seconds pass, in which seems like hours, but is nothing more then just Haley freaking out at the situation. “We need to get him to a hospital…” Jason looks into her pale blue eyes, knowing she wants to try to do what’s best for Matt, but there’s no way they are taking him to the hospital. After his thirty odd years in the law profession, Hilton knows that this situation isn’t going to be one best handled by the police, but Haley isn’t clearly thinking…her minds racing, worrying about the man, who protects her no matter what goes wrong, but in the time he needs her to protect him, she just isn’t prepared to follow through. She wants to reach out and help him, but she can’t, something’s holding her back…fear. “Didn’t you hear me? We need to get him to a hospital…” Her voice booms, louder then ever before, but it’s still muffled by the tears that keep rolling down her face. Matt hears her words and tries to reply, but is unable too, as he is choking on his own blood. Even in his own pain, he wants to help comfort her, but it doesn’t seem possible, as it’s getting harder and harder for him to breath…his air supply is becoming less and less. The time is rapidly approaching, unless Jackson acts quickly, he’s going to die. “Matt please…I don’t want you to die. I still need you…” Her voice penetrates his ears, forcing his mind back into full alert, but everything still seems dim and blurry…it doesn’t seem like there is much he can do, as the blood continues to pour out onto the carpet below. He’s dying in the arms of the one he holds dear though, that’s something he can be happy with, though it pains him to know Haley is going to have to go on with the burden of living without him…protecting herself and getting herself out of trouble, but this is payment for his sins…his life. Coughing some of the blood out of his lungs, Matt raises a bit…letting his wooden rosary swing against his chest. He falls back into position, proving it was nothing more then a momentary jolt. Haley pulls him in closer and holds on, knowing it could very well be the last time she ever does it. Jackson struggles to get the blood out of his airway, but there’s a good chance the bullet hit an artery. This isn’t something they ever trained him for…to survive if you were just a second too slow, or ended up having some reserve about killing, something they never expected to happen. “Haley…” His voice is soft, almost an unnoticeable whisper, but she hears it…like it was broadcast over a live speaker. She holds the tears back for a second, leaning down closer to his face, so her ear is right by his mouth, all the while her blonde hair is tickling his face…giving the agent a chance to smile, even in death she makes him happy. Something to Hitokiri never thought he would feel, but just as he achieves it, it’s all going to be taken from him, because he didn’t want to have to kill again. “Y-you have to stop the bleeding…heat up an iron, you’re going to have to stick it on the wound…” His voice is starting to fade quickly, trying to get his last instructions for life out…hoping that she will be strong enough to follow them, but even if she isn’t, he doesn’t mind much, because these last few months have been some of his happiest…with her by his side, making him laugh and sharing in his pain and suffering with him. Being strong enough to carry the burden of his past; forgetting it with a grain of salt if needed. “Jason, you need to get a dish towel and put it in my mouth…please do it now…” The tears rolls down her face once more, as she sets his head back down onto the floor, watching his entire body rest in a pool of his own blood. His tall spiky blonde hair, has fallen from his usual height and his now resting down on his fore head mainly, drenched in blood and sweat…his emotions runny all over the board, his fear of leaving Haley, his sadness in knowing he might never step into a PCW ring again…letting the government have their way and close it, causing his friends too be out of work, but worst off all the pain in his heart, knowing that Haley is going to have to bury him. What a way for the Humanoid Typhoon to die, he thinks. Thinking and feeling the same things, as probably almost everyone else I have killed…wondering the same things that they wondered and wishing that they could go on living, Each had their own reasons I am sure, just as I do, but I didn’t care…they had to die, but now that I am in the same position, I can see it a little more clearly. Maybe I shouldn’t have been so quick to pull the trigger, but it doesn’t matter now, I am going to pay for my sins. Standing behind the now dying agent, Jason Hilton kneels down, getting his grey suit covered in blood, but ignoring it, as he props Matt up against his knee, forcing him to sit up and placing his hand in front of his mouth to make sure, Jackson is still breathing…he is, but it’s very slow and shallow. Matt’s not going to be able to hold on much longer, if he keeps losing blood the way he is, but it’s slowed down now that Hilton propped him up, but not enough to stop it completely. “Damnit Matt, why can’t you be like everyone else…you would be fine if you were at the hospital right now, but you just have to be difficult, but you aren’t dying on me yet…you will go on living for Haley…” His voice is loud and rasp with anger, but it’s still comforting to The Humanoid Typhoon to know that at least someone doesn’t want him to die just yet, but maybe it’s the best thing. Knowing he will never have to kill another person again, wither it being right or wrong…no more innocent people will have to pay the dearest price, which is their life and all he has to do is stop fighting. It’s only a small price to pay, to make up for all his mistakes, but Haley. Standing in front of Matt is his beautiful girlfriend, burning hot iron in her hand. She quickly takes a knee, not paying any mention to the massive amount of blood, which she is now kneeling in. She doesn’t have time to pay attention to it…it’s time for her to save him for all the times he came to her rescue. Too pay him back in the best way she can, by saving his life. “Haley…I love you…” He gasps out, letting his heavy eyelids fall shut. Grabbing Jackson’s mouth with his one free hand, Jason pries it open, speedily sticking the blue dishtowel into his mouth and he pulls back on it, making sure it won’t fall out. In a moment of shock, Haley just stares in disbelief at the whole situation, taking it in fully for the first time probably. Jason looks at her for a moment, watching the tears roll down her face. He shakes his head, knowing what is daughter is going through right now must be hell, but she needs to snap out of it, she needs to save Matt. “Haley!” He screams, almost instantly taking the blonde princess out of her thoughts and bringing her back to the harsh reality, that she needs to place the iron on Matt’s leg and stop him from bleeding…stop him from dying, so that he might go on and protect others…the innocent people who deserve to live. She pushes one of the three flaps at the bottom of his trench coat up, revealing the hole that the blood is coming from. With purpose she places the iron over the wound and closes her eyes…setting it on top of the area. A mix of pain and heat flood his body, sending his near lifeless corpse surging forward…involuntarily biting down on the towel, but he doesn’t scream out in pain. He remains unmoving, except for the few twitches of his body. His eyes remain shut, but his breathing still continues, even though it’s shallow. Pulling the iron off his leg, Haley falls back on the floor and begins to cry harder then ever…setting the iron face up on the floor, she puts her head in her petite hands and just cries away. Jason removes the towel from Matt’s mouth and lays his back down on the floor, checking to make sure he’s still breathing. Standing up, he makes his way over to Haley and crouches down next to her, putting his large arms around his daughter, who looks at Matt and then buries her head in his massive chest and continues to sob. Matt lays motionless on the floor…everything fading to black, as his senses start to fade…he can barely make out the sounds anymore. Is this really the end? He wonders, picturing Haley one last time, before everything fades to blackness…
