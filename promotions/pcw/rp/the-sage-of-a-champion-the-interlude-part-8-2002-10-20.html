The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 8

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-20-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 8” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 3: The Mission Begins :: BladeD’s parents really don’t want him doing this, but it is going to have to be done. BladeD’s Mother is allowed to be with BladeD via radio every step of the way, since she seems to be very nervous about all of this. The radio is also for the Commander to say in contact with BladeD the whole time as well. That way if BladeD runs into a problem or something unplanned occurs, then the Commander is right there to help him out if at all possible, but instead of the scene being on BladeD we are going back to the office of NMA of NYC where Commander Fuller and General Powers are going to finish the letter they were reading. :: Commander Fuller: We should have finished this letter earlier because there might be more valuable information that Mr. President and BladeD may need to know. General Powers: Yea, but we didn’t have the time… at least we have the time now to finish the letter. Be thankful for that. Commander Fuller: Yea, I guess you are right, so let’s finish reading. Metal Armor like I said is the most diabolical machine ever created and it is two stories tall, but you are probably thinking. What does it look like? What color is it? What kind of weapons does it have? ECT. Well, let me begin to explain everything in a deeper matter. Overview: Metal Armor is… well of course made out of metal. It is the most top of the line metal you could ever have. Think of the video game Mech-Warrior and you will have a better idea of Metal Armor looks like. Face: There is a control panel, like if you were driving the machine or any type of navigation, This control panel rests in where the eyes of the machine form, so basically if you can think of how big the eyes of the machine our you will know how big the control panel is. I don’t have the details of the exact size, but there is enough room to move around a bit and that is about it. There isn’t a mouth or eyebrows or anything. I heard that somewhere on the top of Metal Armor’s head two opens are made where gun like objects come up and shot out heat seeking missiles, but that is just a rumor I have heard and I don’t know that for a fact or not. It is just a good, educated guess, that you need to watch out for while battling this machine because they are heat seeking missiles, so they can be extremely hard to dodge and they are very powerful. I do not know if it is possible to destroy these gun like objects with bullets because I really don’t know how stable they are, but you can always try and see what happens because it would be a very good idea to at least try. Also I am not sure if actual people are going to be in the control panel because I heard that from some other people outside of Metal Armor there is a control center that can control everything without actually being inside of Metal Armor, but I don’t know too much about the inside of Metal Armor, so they made have people inside the control panel. I was told that the glass is also bullet proof, so there is no need to trying to kill the guys inside of the control panel if there is anyone in the control panel. If one of your men does find a way to get into the control panel that would always be good and have them kill the guys inside of Metal Armor as well, because then the machine might not be able to do it’s job at 100% which obviously will help you out a whole lot. Upper Body: The chest of the machine almost looks ripped, but that was just a fancy part of the design. The left arm carries the primary weapon and the right arm carries the secondary weapon. The upper body is probably the stronger area of metal armor, but I could be wrong because I heard his legs may be the strongest area, but basically his upper body is around that area of strongest. There is no use in trying to penetrate Metal Armor from the upper body because it is just a waste of ammo and bullets. Don’t try to penetrate the arms either. I know you would try this to take about Metal Armor’s weapons, but it isn’t going to do any use. There is too much metal to get through to destroy an arm or any part of the upper body for that matter and I have heard the most bullets will just be rejected and bounce right off the upper body not really harming Metal Armor at all. Lower Body: His waist looks almost like a belt and I have heard that there are two guns that shot out bullets, but I have no clue what guns are used or the bullets, so I can’t really tell you much there. The legs are supposed to be the strongest part of Metal Armor’s body, but I have heard that his legs are pretty weak. I am not sure, but they may be the best place to try and take down Metal Armor, so if you can find anything to destroy the legs, that would be my first plan of action on Metal Armor, but I am not sure. I am just going by what I have heard and the reports I have read. I haven’t gotten the chance to check out Metal Armor for myself yet. I heard something was wrong with the right leg of Metal Armor, but I don’t know what to tell you other than have your guy(s) to look for something unusual about the right leg. Primary Weapon: On the left arm… Some kind of rocket launcher… I couldn’t find out what type it was. It is almost like they made up one of their own or want to be very secretive about it because there are no records of what kind of rocket launcher it is or the kind of rockets inside of it, so all I can tell you is watch out. Secondary Weapon: On the right arm. An AK-47. Now I find this very strange for Metal Armor. Metal Armor isn’t supposed to be designed, to be used more than once, so I would have expected a grenade launcher or something of that sort for the secondary weapon because once Metal Armor is launched it will most likely never be used again, but I guess for safety purposes Russia decided they needed an AK-47. I am still puzzled over that though because it makes no sense. Maybe something will spark in your heads or something, but from what I have learned in this type of warfare. A weapon of that kind is useless. Locations of Weapons: Most Powerful Weapon(s): The heat-seeking missiles are actually the most powerful weapons. That is because they follow their enemies until they hit them and many can be fired at once. About 12 heat seeking missiles will be out at a time, so really it is more powerful than the rocket launcher in the long run and that is why the weapon is actually being used. In fact, it will even be used a lot more then you probably think it would be. The only time the rocket launcher will be used is to destroy building and things of that sort. When it comes to men the AK-47 and heat-seeking missiles will be used, so I would strongly advise you to watch out for those heat-seeking missiles. Weakest Weapon(s): That AK-47. I can’t explain to you how much that puzzles me because I wouldn’t expect the book to tell you to have an AK-47 as the secondary weapon because it is just to weak for a machine of this kind. You guys know what an AK-47 is in the states, so I would suppose that I don’t need to say anything else about an AK-47. Hot Spot(s): The upper body and it can move pretty quick, so watch out for some nice foot work and an all powerful upper body. It is the almost indestructible part of Metal Armor. Like I said before. I don’t even try wasting your bullets on this part of Metal Armor because there isn’t everything you are going to have that is going to be strong enough to take down any of the upper body of Metal Armor. As Metal Armor does have fancy feet, so watch for that. You wouldn’t want to get stomped on by this machine…. It would probably kill you. (heh heh) Weak Spot(s): The legs are supposed to be the strongest part because he has fancy foot work, but I heard that won’t built on that good, so that is the part of Metal Armor you are probably going to want to attack the most if you are going to attack Metal Armor at all. Don’t worry about attacking the feet because that isn’t going to work and don’t attack the kneecaps either. Those are the two places you don’t want to attack because I know for a fact those two parts of the legs are very strong. You either want to go for the shins or the thighs. I would think of Metal Armor like a normal person and go for the shins, but that decision is up to you and I can’t really help you in that area. Just have your men go for whatever they feel most comfortable I guess. That is really the best advice I can give you. Amount of ammo:Rocket Launcher: 230,120,000 rockets. You are thinking… OMG!!! How can it hold that many? I am thinking the same thing, but Metal Armor is supposed to be able to destroy all of the U.S. if it has to. AK-47: 100,700,000 bullets. Heat-Seeking Missiles: 600,500,000 bullets.I have no clue where it has the room to store all of this ammo, but it is also two stories high, so I guess there is room for millions of ammo. That is the correct amount, so keep that in mind if you do send in men, because it could take out all the men you dish out and some to say the least. :: The scene is going to cut here. We haven’t gotten the chance to hear from the Commander or General on what they think of all of this, but they are probably going to get worried because this Metal Armor machine is one bad apple. It isn’t going to be an easy task at all to take down this machine. Especially, for someone that is only 14-years-old. ::
