It's All About Alliances, Section C

Date: (Alliances #7 Celestials “You ready for this… boy?” The god stood tall before his parents…the elderly couple of about mid/near six-foot status. On the balcony above overlooking the courtyard, Cyclonica, Glacia, Anarchy, and Jenna stood watching what could be a once in a lifetime spectacle. People said that about Alliances…once in a lifetime chance to win this thing… Shame most of their lifetimes are about to end with this one. Yoshihiro was on Akiyama’s left hand side…his arms folded about his chest. Yukina was on the right…her hands folded behind her back. But both parents…they held the same firm focused gaze. “Are they really going to fight?” Jenna whispered to Anarchy. Anarchy nudged her, a silencing hiss seeping from her lips. “Of course they are.” Jenna looked across Anarchy to Cyclonica, whose eyes stared down in the courtyard with interest. “You think they would go this far just to stand here and do nothing?” “But Akiyama would kill them! They’re so old!” Jenna whimpered, a concerned look turning towards the older couple down below. Cyclonica shook her head at the wench. “These two took out El Diablo and El Romantico alone.” “El Romantico wasn’t in PCW at the time.” Anarchy reminded her. “You mean Juan Borro, don’t you?” Cyclonica shrugged, leaning her forearms against the railing. “Same thing…you’re just blind to Kayfabe.” Glacia sighed, a vapor escaping from her blue lips. “You sure that wasn’t Kevin Klaymore?” “Either or. They both sucked on equal terms. It was hard to tell the two apart.” Akiyama’s face was blank as his fists balled up at his side. Yukina’s skirt blew in the chilled breeze that began to blow…a strand of her hair fluttering as well. “Time to teach you some manners, son. Are you ready?” Akiyama gave them a sharp nod, his body crouching down into a Karate stance. Yoshihiro gave a small chuckle as he looked at his son. “You’re still using the same stance? You haven’t been training like you should have, boy!” he scolded. “If you have, you would have learned by now!” The nVg didn’t ask for corrections as his roar echoed into the chilly November sky… Parental Guidance Inphino’s head darted into the direction of the yell. All eyes turned…as a matter of fact. Alexander J Maxis and Ryoko Godai stood up from their seats under the canopied table, the two of them removing their sunglasses simultaneously with awed expressions on their faces. Mina de Margo remained leaned against the door leading inside of the Blitz dojo…her uninterested face finally showing a hint of curiosity. Hana Maxis slowly slid out of her Karate stance and stared wide eyed in the direction of the wooded path in the back of the yard. She looked to Inphino questioningly… what unholy monster could that voice belong to? Then another one boomed through the air…a growl of fierceness…one that belonged amidst battle. It would be heard soon…though the people on the receiving end will not be able to appreciate its sound. That second sound began to jar Hana’s memory…and she was the first to speak, a soft whisper audible to them all as they stood in silence. “………Akiyama………?” ---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===--- The End and Sandy Wong were walking down the path leading from the side of the mountain…where Jahana Castle stood alone, like a fortress of darkness watching over the small town below. It was a dirt road, with trees on the left side and rocky grounds on the right. It was only a few twists and turns before leading down to the main road into the town. End folded his arms across his chest. “This is a test, you know.” “What?” Sandy looked up at him, her attention suddenly jolted back towards him from something else. “Alliances,” he explained. “This is a test for me. I’m going against a huge sum of people in one night. This is nothing to fool around with.” “You got that right. The top of PCW is in this match, including the top of OSW, RUSH, and FSW. Lot of things to prove in this competition.” End nodded at her words. “There’s a big prize to gain from all this.” Sandy nodded as well, slipping her hands into the pockets of the mid-thigh-length trench coat she wore. “Four title shots…that’s nothing to scoff at.” “No…not that…” End stopped in his tracks, halting Sandy in hers as well. She looked into his pallid eyes, wisps of hair scattered before them…but he would never see them. But he would see victory…that he was determined to see this Sunday… “The reward for this battle…is the fight itself.” Then End shrugged nonchalantly. “But four title shots are nothing to scoff at, mind you.” Sandy Wong smiled warmly at him…and though Skylar Green couldn’t see such a pleasant thing… He could feel it… They both looked back at the castle…taking notice just as so many others of the roar that avalanched from above. End sighed, shaking his head as he began walking down the path once more. “He is relentless…gotta feel sorry for all the guys that want to hold him back.” Wong stared at End walk on ahead before turning back towards the castle. The growl came next…bouncing off the atmosphere in a guttural grating. Then she looked ahead…jogging to catch up with End. In comparison…everyone was trying to catch up with the Celestials. They were the most intense group in PCW…no matter low the odds looked for them…no matter how high the other stables seemed to have luck. That luck would run out…eventually… And its expiration date was this coming Sunday, November the twenty-fourth. ---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===--- They were quick…anyone with eyes could admit that much. Akiyama threw a punch, and found air. They were already behind him by that time. A sweeping roundhouse kick found the same result, mom and dad ducking swiftly under his massive leg. Akiyama’s leg pivoted as he brought his leg back down, his back facing towards his standing parents. That was bad…you never face your back towards anyone in a fight. Four rapid punches in the kidneys from two sets of hands taught him that lesson…and two simultaneous snap front kicks reinforced the point. Akiyama growled, his hand clutching his lower back as he jumped away from them, landing on his feet facing them. Through his winced eyes he saw them as he had first saw them… his father with his arms folded over his chest, and his mother with her hands folded behind her back. Their superior stances were frustrating…like this wasn’t any effort for them at all. Yukina pulled one hand out from behind her, looking down at her clear nails. “Are you finished yet?” she asked, blowing on her nails before rubbing them against her chest. No one taunted the Neo Void God…not even his own parents… He dashed at her, shooting his leg up for the Divine Sword. A surprised look jumped on her face before she rolled under his leg. When she turned back into a vertical position, she barely had a second before crossing her arms across her chest…a reverse hook kick already in her direction. Her block took the impact, and sent her falling back. She shifted her feet back and landed in a guard position, her arms still crossed before her. She looked up past her arms to see Akiyama…his back towards her, and a cocky smile on his face. Cyclonica rubbed her chin. “Interesting…” But Akiyama didn’t stand there long gloating on the first landing attack…right after that he snapped instantly towards his father, who had not moved from his position since before. Yoshihiro saw the striking arm coming, ducking and sidestepping out of his reach. He came back up, and saw Akiyama coming around 360 degrees with the same swinging strong lariat! He avoided the clothesline, leaning back to allow it to pass over…but it was close. Close enough that he could feel the wind off the velocity of the swing…close enough to feel the warmth of his son’s arm… Akiyama backed off as Yoshihiro leaned back up. He looked back at his son, taken back by the attack, but not surprised to say the least. Don’t worry…if dad wasn’t surprised, the rest of you will be in that cage. The god cracked his knuckles as he slid back into his Karate stance, his fists out before him, one leg pivoted in front of the grounding leg. Yukina walked over next to her husband, placing a hand on his shoulder. “If you’re quite done…” she began, her eyes turning towards Akiyama with a cold anger. “I think we’ll begin.” “WHAT??” Jenna exclaimed, leaning against the balcony railing shocked. “They haven’t started yet?” Anarchy watched in awe at the three of them down in the courtyard. “I never knew that father actually knew any legitimate martial arts.” “You think all three hundred pound monsters know is pounding on your opponent with their fists without any technique?” Cyclonica asked insulted by that statement. “Boy, you have another thing coming. Ever hear of Kimbusa?” “FSW Fatty?” Anarchy asked. Cyclonica nodded, her eyes looking up to the sky to clarify that answer. “Yeah…you can call him that. Anyway, he’s Sumo.” “Yeah?” Anarchy nodded at that fact. “Maybe FSW has some talent in there after all.” The Divine Prophetess rolled her eyes at that comment. “The only one out of the lot of them, if you ask me.” “Not to rain on anyone’s parade here…” Cyclonica’s mother interrupted. The two sisters and Jenna turned towards Glacia, who stood with her arms folded and her eyes locked down below on the Akiyama family. “But has anyone cared to wonder where the house homosexual is?” Cyclonica slapped her forehead. “ARRRGH!! BOCA!!” ---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===--- The Door to the pantry swung open, and Cyclonica and Jenna stood peering in. Inside the kitchen pantry, Boca sat there crossed legged on the floor, his hands folded across his knee. He was sitting next to the vegetables with a smile on his face. “What are you smiling about?” Cyclonica demanded. Del Inferno shook his head at the two women politely. “Oh…NOTHING. I just kept myself occupied…that’s all…” Cyclonica muttered something under her breath before waving him out. “Come on! You have to train for our match! You’re going to be prepared or I’ll kill you in the ring.” That wasn’t a threat…it was a promise. Boca stood up obediently and walked out of the pantry. Cyclonica sniffed the air inside and glared at Jenna with a look of disgust. “Throw away all the carrots in here.” She ordered. “And if you see anything else phallic in here…burn it.” With that said, Cyclonica walked away. Jenna sighed as she pulled a pair of latex gloves from her pocket. “Why me?” she asked herself pityingly. The scene faded out on Jenna rifling through the vegetable bin. “Ugh…dear Lord. Either this squash is rotten…or….EWWWW!” -=-=-=-=-To Be Continued-=-=-=-=-
