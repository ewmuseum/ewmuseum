Chases and Explosions of Krayzie Proportions

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 20th, 2003 The scene fades into to a shot of a crystal blue lake, shimmering in the warm Indiana sun. The camera continues its watchful eye on the modest body of water, catching nothing more than a few pontoon boats filled with families and a handful of water skiers out and about. That is, until a speedboat comes racing, recklessly, across the view. Just seconds behind it, showing the same amount of carelessness, a second follows. Suddenly, a small graphic appears in the middle of the screen: 'TWENTY MINUTES EARLIER' The camera opens up at a lakeside grill and tavern, showing PCW owner and Hall of Famer, Jeremy Howard sitting across from his beautiful wife, Jessica. Atop them, a little more toward the background, a sign reads: Simonton Lake Grill and Tavern. Jessica, sporting a sexy pair of blue jean shorts and a provocative white top, is seen eating a cheeseburger while the love of her life appears deep in thought, watching something that has apparently caught his eye. Jessica: Honey, something wrong? Jeremy's facial expression barely changes as he continues watch. Jeremy: Nah, I'm cool. Jessica: Then why the serious look? The Krazed Lunatic puts his right index finger to his mouth, signalling Jessica to be quiet. Then, cautiously, he motions for her to turn around. Jeremy: You see that man over there? Jessica: Which one? There's like ten or twenty. Jeremy: The one wearing the black shirt with the markings. Jessica's eyes kind of widen. Jessica: Yeah.... He seems fairly familiar. Jeremy: He should. Jeremy pauses, a maniacal look begin to form on the face of the one man who refuses to quit when everyone else would have already quit. Just ask Ivan Stanislav. Jeremy's voice lowers. Jeremy: He's the man who held our son captive for Stan Hinkle. Jessica: The man from the occult?!?!? Jeremy's eyes widen, getting that 'shut the hell up honey before you blow my cover' look. But, of course, Jeremy being the chivalrous warrior that he is, won't tell his wife that. Instead, the Krayzie One chooses to reinforce the right index finger to the mouth motion. Jessica: Wha? Jeremy: Honey, I'm trying to figure out why the hell this man is in Elkhart! Jessica's beautiful facial features shift to a look of horror. Jessica: You don't think!?! Jeremy doesn't answer, choosing to not worry his wonderful wife anymore than he has to. Seconds pass, Jeremy not letting up on his little investigation. The man, looking to be in his late thirties, sits but a mere three tables over from one of wrestling's more famous couples, eating a plate from the grill. He doesn't appear to know right away that the Krayzie One has his sights set on him.... Yet. Jeremy: Honey, if anything happens, I want you to grab the car and get home as soon as possible. Jessica: What about you? Jeremy: Don't worry about me. You just make sure our son is safe. Jessica knods, understanding the importance of her mission. It's been almost a year and a half since Jeremy rescued his son from the hands of an occult, a group who held their son against his will for Stan Hinkle. Jessica: Jeremy. Jeremy turns his head from his investigative watch to his wife for a second. Jeremy: Yes? Jessica: Thanks. A look of puzzlement strikes Jeremy. Jeremy: For? Jessica: For always trying to protect us. Even with your career to handle, you always went out of your way to try and keep Troy and I as safe as possible. Jessica pauses, becoming a little choked up. Jessica: Even now, with this whole Kannon thing going on, you put your family first before yourself. I can't help but feel a little guilty for asking you to leave wrestling... to leave your career. Jeremy smiles, knowing in his heart he's doing the right thing. Jeremy: Honey, you only asked for what you wanted in your heart. If you can't share your glory with the one you love, then there is no glory... There's just an empty win with no one to share it with. Jessica: I just don't want you to resent me later for this. Jeremy: Well, let me put this into perspective for you. I could have kept my career in PCW going and continued to ignore you and Troy, all the while causing you two to resent me for not being there. Or, I could show my son that even at the end of the day, nothing in this world is more important than love, especially the love of his mother. A tear drops from Jessica's eye, her heart seemingly touched. Jeremy: My real friends understand why I did what I had to do and that's all that matters to me. Jessica: I love you. Jeremy: I love you t.... All of the sudden, the man that Jeremy was watching makes a break for the lake, racing toward it like a robber leaving a bank. Jeremy, never a man to turn down a chance at excitement, gives his wife the look to get back to the house and Troy before bursting away from his table to chase after this mysterious man that has caught the eye of the Krazed Lunatic. Jessica, a bit worried because of the fagueness of the situation to her, tears off for her car to get back to Troy, the cute three year old son of Jeremy and Jessica. Jeremy: Get back here, you son of a b*tch. Jeremy continues his pursuit, blazing across a stretch of sand. The mysterious man can be seen leaping into a white speedboat, reading 'Devil's Advocate' on the back. He guns it, spitting water everywhere as he takes off away from the pursuing Krayzie. Jeremy, only seconds behind the man, leaps into the first speedboat he comes across, a baby blue waverunner with a black stripe down the side. Jeremy: Coming after my son, b*tch? I'll murder ya. Jeremy guns his speedboat, the owners chasing after him on foot. Too late, Jeremy tears off across the lake, in hot pursuit. It's only a matter of minutes before Jeremy catches up with his target, completely showing no regard whatsoever for the water pedestrians. Jeremy: Get back here. Daddy has a murderous spanking for ya. Jeremy comes up beside the boat, known as 'Devil's Advocate'. In sticking closely to his namesake, the lunatic minded owner of PCW slams his speedboat into the mysterious mans. The impact splits the two apart, knocking off a piece of the boat from each. The mystery man begins to head for a section of the lake where boats can also be launched. Jeremy spots him, then tears off after him. Jeremy: Chicken sh*t. This ain't over. Come to my stomping grounds and think you're getting away so easily. Not! Jeremy kicks his boat into high gear, to an almost dangerous level. The mysterious occultist who held Jeremy's son away from him for Stan Hinkle gets closer and closer to the boat dock, apparently not in the mood to have a speedboat fight with his maniacal aggressor. Not so fast. The Krayzie One slams his boat into the front of the other man's boat, violently, tearing off a section of it. The mysterious man jumps from his boat into the lake and swims as fast as he can toward the shore, a mere ten feet away, while his boat begins to sink. Jeremy: I told you, best believe someone's payin' the pied piper. B*tch! Jeremy, quite possibly the wildest man to ever be known as the owner of PCW, cruises along in his boat, it still intact barely after the collision. He watches as his target begins to climb out of the water... and into a nearby S-10 pickup truck that sits nearby! Jeremy: Oh hell no! Again, showing no care for personal safety, Jeremy guns the speedboat as hard as he can, picking up more and more speed as he goes along. The mysterious man opens the driver's side door, then gets in, trying in vain to get his engine started. The engine hits, just as Jeremy's boat hits the boat launch AND GOES FLYING INTO THE AIR, NAILING THE BED OF THE PICK UP TRUCK! Jeremy, visibly hobbled a little bit, leaps out of the boat, which no longer has a front end as it has mixed with the bed of the truck to become one. Sputtering, the engine dies. Jeremy: It's time to answer for your sins now. Jeremy stalks over to the driver's side door and rips the mysterious man out of the truck, throwing him to the ground. The man pulls out a knife and lunges right for the Krayzie One. Jeremy sidesteps the attempt, then grabs the man by the wrist before twisting his arm into a vicious lock. Jeremy: First, you take my son for Stan Hinkle and now you're trying to stab me? Punch! Sporadic drips of blood begin at the corner of the man's jaw. Jeremy: So, Keith, is there a good reason as to why you chose to visit Elkhart? The man, apparently known as Keith, smiles, wickedly. Keith: I think you know why. Jeremy: I figured Hinkle would be done with me after I f*cked him up back in the day. Keith: Who said anything about Hinkle. That broke down joke lied to me. Wonderment covers Jeremy's face, who still continues to play things cautious. Before, a year and a half to two years ago, this man did the bidding of Stan Hinkle, former Governor of the state of Michigan. So, if not Hinkle, then who? Jeremy: Then why?!?! Jeremy's face becomes more stern, his lack of wanting to play games showing. Keith: To finish the job. Jeremy: Job? Keith: Yeah, the job. Jeremy: What f*cking job!?!?! Keith, showing his abundance of disdain, merely spits to the ground beside him. Jeremy: What f*cking job!?!?! Again, Keith shows no signs of wanting to tell Jeremy. Jeremy: Fine, b*tch. I'll make your ass talk. Jeremy, in that fit of rage that he gets into when pushed to the brink of hatred, grabs Keith by the back of the neck and drags him over to the lake before stuffing his face into the lake, a few seconds, before pulling it back up, Keith choking. Jeremy: Ready to tell me now? Keith: No! In the far distance, sirens can be heard. To add to that, flames begin to show in the front of the boat.... the part that is embedded in the truck. Jeremy: Alright. You're either going to tell me why the hell you've come to Elkhart or I'ma have your punk ass locked away with Elkhart's finest criminals... Some of which are good friends of mine. Keith: Whatever. You'll be arrested too. Jeremy: Maybe so, but it'll be worth it. Keith: What about your match with Kannon that I see you're so interested in? Jeremy: First things first and family's first. For the first time since Jeremy caught him, a sense of fear begins to invade the soul of Keith. Obviously, this man has found out the one secret that any opponent of Jeremy learns: The Krazed Lunatic will do WHATEVER it takes for his family. Jeremy: One last time. What f*cking job!?!?! Keith: To kill you! By now, the fire between the speedboat and the truck rages, serving as a hellish back drop to this meeting of hated enemies. Thankfully, the two men are standing in about a foot of water, safe for now from the blaze. Keith: Hinkle only took your son to lure you away from your safety to a place where he could have you taken out. He figured at some point you'd find you son, that he'd end you there. Jeremy: So Hinkle's grand plan was to kill me? Keith: Pfft. After you beat his son so badly in that stupid amateur match, he wanted to kill you then for embarassing his family. A look of extreme anger crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: I'ma take that fool out once and for all! Keith: If he doesn't get to you first. Jeremy: He won't. I mean, look at the trash he hires to do his bidding. Hell, he lies to you and you still try to finish the job? Trash. Keith: What are you going to do with me? Jeremy puts his hand to his chin, a thinking pose he strikes. Jeremy: I beat that fools son badly, he gets pissed and begins plotting to have me killed for embarrassing him. He fails, so he has my son taken away so that he can lure me to a place to get the job done... Only one problem.... He never counted on me being crazy enough to get out of the place alive? Keith: Basically. Like I care. Punch! Jeremy: You're going to care. I have a friend at the Elkhart Police Department whose a big Krayzie fan. I wonder how many charges we can get for this one. Oh, and you'd better believe that you be getting out of Elkhart for a long time. Jeremy pulls Keith out of the water, to escort him toward the oncoming horde of police and fire trucks that begin to pull up on the scene. Keith, for whatever reason, decides to grab his knife that lay on the ground from the previous attack. Once again, he lunges for Jeremy, trying to save himself from a life of prison. Before he can land the knife into the Krayzie One though, three of the Elkhart Police Department's finest officers fill Keith with a series of shots. The scene fades out as Keith drops to his knees, then onto his stomach..... Dead
