Date: 5-30 Prolouge O poder é entendido mal. A maioria acreditam mentiras verdadeiras do poder na força física. Aqueles que sabem a verdade, sabem mentiras verdadeiras do poder em sua alma. Or so Menolly’s father used to say so. She really didn’t believe that saying. She tended to more believe the exact opposite of that. But Menolly never really listened to her father’s advice.. or much of anything else he had to say to her. The only time she ever paid attention to him was when he taught her families tradition of Ju-Jitsu. She was the fourth generation of the Xenos to learn the traditional Maeda-style Japanese Ju-Jitsu. Menolly’s great-grandfather Jorge Xenos learned the style right at the side of Ju-Jitsu legend Carlos Gracie, the founder of Gracie-style Ju-Jitsu. But unlike the Gracie family, the Xenos stuck to the true from of the Maeda-style not modifying it to their own needs. Jorge Xenos felt there was no reason to change an already good thing. Menolly felt the same way as her her great-grandfather. She enjoyed and believed in the Maeda-style. She had mastered the style at the ripe young age of twelve. A feat not even her father acomplished. But soon Menolly had grown bored with fighting all the same opponents, and beating them time and time again. She even had pulled off a couple wins over the third generation Gracie’s, which made her ego soar. Menolly had been a fan of Lucha Libre style wrestling when she younger.She liked the flying and acrobatics of it. Something that her Ju-Jitsu teachings didn’t offer her. So it was no to surprise to anyone except her father when she at age fourteen she wanted to goto Mexico City to study the art of Lucha Libre. Her father nearly had a heart attack at this revulation and strictly forbade her to go. Menolly was a stubborn girl, and eventually through her Mother’s interference was allowed to go live with her uncle in Mexico City. The next year and a half with Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre, the most respected federation in Mexico, was the best time of her life. She worked hard learning all the acrobats and training her body to make it flexible enough to compete with the great lady luchadores. With an undaunting attitude she trained her body and mind to become the best wrestler she thought she could be. After the year and a half of training, she was ready for her first official match. Though she didn’t win, she was ecstatic at the rush of adrenaline she felt while wrestling. At just under sixteen years old she was competing with the Lucha greats, something she greatly enjoyed. So now at seventeen she was ready to take on the task of making it in America. She had just signed with the world renowned Primetime Championship Wrestling. She couldn’t be determined to make it then she was now. Now was her time… Her time to show the world what a seventeen-year-old girl from Brazil could do.. She was the Brazilian Fury.. Menolly Xenos. § Her lime green eyes shimmered with a fiendishly childlike delight. Which wasn’t uncommon for the young Brazilian girl name Menolly Xenos. Most say she liked a lot like her British mother, the color of her hair was that of the sun reflecting off the sand on a hot day, her eyes a deep green of a dense forest or the other skin of a lime, which the latter was where she picked up her childhood nickname “Lime”. Of course the now seventeen-year-old Menolly would glare at the person calling her that. Mostly that being her father, who now stood in front of the golden haired young woman, an aggravated look in his brown eyes. If there was anyone who could push Ferdinand Xenos’s buttons, it was his unruly, disobedient daughter. She had gone against just about everything he had ever told her to do. But the thing that broke his heart the most was the fact she refused to take of the Ju-Jitsu school that had been in their family for four generations. His look of aggravation faded as quickly has it appeared, he lowered his head as it shook in yet another bout of disappointment. “Por que deve você perseguir esta carreira em wrestling? Eu era dependendo de você para fazer exame sobre desta escola. Mas naturalmente eu não sou decepcionado, mim conheço-o não tenho nenhum interesse em fazer assim.” “How many times do I have to explain to you? I love wrestling, it’s my passion now.. Just as Ju-Jitsu was when I was little. Don’t get me wrong father. I still love Ju-Jitsu, and eventually I will take over the school from you. But right now, I want to go after my dream. Pursue my asperations, I want to be a wrestling star in America. I want to see the lights of New York City, hear the sounds of Los Angeles. Why can’t you just be supportive of me for once in your life. The only thing that matters to you is your happiness and this school. What I want has never had any bearing on your plans for me. I’m not like you. I don’t want to be stuck here in Brazilia for my whole life. I want to travel and see and experience everything I can before I become old.” Menolly stressed to her father, her face turning into a look of longing hoping her father would understand. Ferdinand’s face turned red with anger in a split second. ” Contudo outra vez você desonra-me falando que língua bruta do inglês. Fale no português como eu perguntei de você em assim muitas ocasiões.” She drew back from her father’s harsh words. She knew he hated her speaking in English. Not that he couldn’t speak it. It was more the fact he thought the English language was barbaric and very bad sounding to his ears. He forbid anyone speaking it in his presence. Even his own wife who was from England. Menolly took in a ragged breath that was more formed as a sigh. “Gene, mim deve falar o inglês a fim preparar-se para meu primeiro desengate a América. Se eu o repugnar... Eu desculpo-me. Sincerly. Mas eu continuarei falando o inglês. Se você não gostar d. Então eu suponho-me não tenho nada mais mais dizer-lhe.” Menolly father scratched his rough five-o-clock shadow and shrugged. “Menolly. Tanto quanto eu te amo... Eu suponho este um permanente adeus. Eu não tenho nada à esquerda dizer-lhe. Se você vier sempre a seus sentidos. Então você será bem-vindo para trás em minha casa. Até que então você estiver inoperante a mim.” With that Ferdinand turned it back on his only child and strode quickly out of the room without another word. In his mind he pictured Menolly as a small girl learning the basics of Maeda-style, the frustration on her face as she tried to learn the basic grappling and striking moves of the style. A tear formed in the corner of eye. As he whispered, “I’ll love you forever my little Lime.” Menolly her head lowered and determined look on her face turned the opposite direction and walked out the door towards the main house. “I love you Dad.. But like always.. I’m going to do what’s best for me.” She strode out the door towards her future. § Menolly sat in her lockerroom her forest eyes fixed towards the wall. She thought maybe she wasn’t ready for the big leagues. After her first lost she started doubt herself. Questioning her own unique abilities. She hadn’t put up too much of a fight against Sergio the Bullfighter. Even though he was male.. She was roughly her size. Nearly the same height and weight. She couldn’t place the exact past where she had messed up to cost her match. With a sigh she stood and walked towards the door. She was just being critial of herself. It was only her first match in a world-wide federation. The pressure on her shoulders to perform to the best of her ablility must have made her extremely neverous.. But of course.. There is always next week.. She wasn’t about to lose again. Not anytime soon anyway. To be continued......
