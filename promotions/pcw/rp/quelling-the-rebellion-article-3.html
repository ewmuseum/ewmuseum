Quelling the Rebellion.....Article 3

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/02 (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) Yet another overblown history lesson. Is that all these jokers are good for?! It’s bad enough that Jeremy Howard, Xavier Kannon and the rest of those headlining ingrates waste my time with long-winded recaps any moron can unearth in the PCW archives…..at least they have the background and seniority to get away with it. But then you get greenhorns with one appearance under their belt babbling tediously about their juvenile ‘backstage antics’ of Tuesday night. Then I cringe and it dawns on me why the business as a whole is on the decline. Why? Simple…..because the f*cking promoters are more worried about ratings and ‘new generation’ appeal than competition. Comedy and fraternity-style toilet humor rules the day…..it’s a crying shame. Someone needs to rejuvenate this wretched company, instill some f*cking values into its lifestream. I didn’t become a piece of walking scar tissue in Japan, recover from a broken neck and lay down for potential-lacking nobodies in the minors to come to a ‘top-notch’ federation that serves better as a Comedy Central-programming prelude to ‘The Daily Show’ than legitimate sports entertainment / professional wrestling. Dime-a-dozen acts like Suburban Uprising are through desecrating my profession! Fusion, tonight…..the first step on the long hard road to retribution for this spiraling business. No more mockery. No more empty showboating. Just pure, relentless brutality. Me….Bruce Shanahan, ‘Violence Jack’…..I’m here to wrestle. I don’t sing or dance, I don’t flash overabundant wealth, and I sure as hell ain’t pretty. I was not christened the God of Extreme for perpetrating propagating the rinse-and-repeat horseplay of these f*cking MTV-culture drones I have the damn honor of manhandling tonight. In the past, prior to my annihilation of that chump Anton the Kid in the Global tourney, I have opted to go on little ‘field trips’ for research. Gather intel about the enemy I guess you could say. As much as I hate the idea of setting foot on that playground, I’ve decided to depart on another field trip…..into the grounds of the local yuppie playpen itself, Harvard University. I want to get a general consensus of the attitude punks like Theodore and Christopher have, because it’s sure as hell a mystery to me. Abercrombie & Fitch laden rebels……go figure. I always thought it was an oxymoron of sorts, but what do I know, eh? Well glancing at the old watch I imagine it’s high-time I get on with it. May as well get this over with. Sometimes though, you have to make sacrifices for the benefit of the greater good. I’ll be sure to wear my Blood For Blood windbreaker, that way I won’t find myself unwittingly attracting any more pesky college gluttons than I need to for my ‘research’. I’ll finish this particular recording when I get back from the playpen. * * * Jack walked briskly over the shortly-cropped lawn of one of the Harvard gardens, picturesque during the spring and summer months yet currently comprised of nothing but wilted shrubs and the lifeless remnants of assorted flowers. Mercifully the air was unseasonably chilly and a brief snow flurry had just ceased; thus the campus was relatively bare with a diminutive frequency of passerby. “The kiddies are locked up in their pens,” breathed VJ, a light mist billowing forth from his mouth and nostrils. “Keepin’ warm…thank God….” He thrust his hands into the side pockets of his windbreaker, inhaling and exhaling deeply before maneuvering off the lawn and onto the sidewalk. Up ahead, in a tight circle, stood a crew of four males. Two were bundled in what seemed to be two to three layers of clothing, with earmuffs and cotton gloves, with another in a sky-blue Nautica hooded sweatshirt and the other in a yellow FUBU pullover. The two in less clothing were shivering. VJ couldn’t help but smirk as he crossed the clay cobblestones of the sidewalk, finally coming to a halt beside one of the shivering Harvard students. He let out a gruff chuckle, catching the attention of the foursome. “Beautiful day out here, ain’t it?” he laughed with an obscured sense of disdain. “Sun is shining, birds are chirping……what more do ya want?” The youth in the Nautica shirt gazed with bewilderment at VJ. “Are you nuts, bro?!” he said in disbelief. “What more do I want? How about for the temperature go above freezing!” “It’s twenty-five freakin’ degrees!” exclaimed Jack, legitimately astonished. “Bullsh*t,” mumbled another of the two sufficiently bundled-up students, wearing a winter vest over a white Abercrombie sweater. “Can’t be above fifteen…..” “You’re nuts, old man,” laughed the FUBU-sporting teen. “There ain’t no damn way….it’s cold as hell out here.” “I’ve lived around here my whole life,” VJ replied in retort. “This ain’t sh*t…” “Don’t look like you’ve lived around here your entire life,” laughed the first of the group with crossed arms. “At least not in this area…..nice f*cking jacket…..” (Calm down, Bruce…..don’t do anything irrational. Junior’s father here is probably Robert Shapiro or something…..) Doing his best to keep his temper in check, VJ cast a goodhearted smile. “I’ve had it a long time. Can’t bear to get rid of it.Actually guys, I’m wandering around campus for a reason. You see, I’m with the publicity department of a company…..” “Can it, Jack,” spat one of the youths standing at VJ’s left. “We know who you are, the four of us watch pro wrestling. Seen you in the WWIWF and ICW on a few regional networks….we follow PCW and heard you signed with them after White Mexican blew your ass out of the water two weeks back.” (Damn, this may prove to be easier than I figured.) “Fine,” replied VJ, dropping the semi-dopey grin adorning his face. “I ain’t in the publicity department. But I was asked to get some opinions….” “If you’re referring to that little match of yours at Fusion tonight,” cut off the FUBU-wearing student, “fact is Suburban Uprising is gonna whip your ass!” A look of perpetual shock flashed across the face of the Boston resident. He scowled and removed his glasses, folding and placing them into his back jeans pocket. “What ya base that on, son?” he snarled. “Uprising is da bomb, dawg!” said the up-to-now silent member of the group. “How can ya compete wit dat sh*t?!” “Kindly reiterate,” growled VJ. “And do so in English this time….” “You’re just an asshole!” declared another of the group. “You think any of us want to see a egocentric piece of trailer park trash beat guys SU?! I don’t know what the hell you’re smokin’ in whatever rathole you live, but lighten up! Drop the attitude! Personally, we all wanna see you get f*cking fired! It was bad enough seeing you run people like us down just because we have more than you do on those local sports networks, but on national TV?! Good lord…” He continued to ramble on, but in general his words had begun to trickle in one ear and out the other. VJ’s temper was diminishing little by little, and one of the youths noticed his right fist clench into a fist. Jack’s upper lip curled into an irate sneer. (Attitude my f*cking ass, you scrawny little prick…..keep going and see what happens……) As the tension mounts between the volatile thug and crew of young scholars, the sound of a cell phone ringing (to the sound of a Tantric song no less) breaks the ominous atmosphere and the Nautica-clad youth steps off the walk to take the call.Moments later he slides the phone back into his pocket and gestures to the other three. “Yo guys, we gotta jet. Drop it and let’s head out.” With a scoff the quartet brush by VJ in unison, who stands motionless with a paralyzing rage. His mind races, he deliberates the possibility of chasing them down like mangy dogs and giving them a lesson they’ll never get in any of their 101 courses. His brief soliloquy is cut short by muffled, rowdy chants across campus. VJ calms slightly as his ears perk up to the repeating mantra emanating from several hundred yards off, and he forgets the insolent students long enough to follow the chants toward the source.He trots up an embankment, where the nature of the various chants become evident through the open windows of the gymnasium of their origination. “Meat sucks! Save a cow! Save a life!.....” Who knows what did it.....maybe the comments of his teenage detractors, maybe his view of the Harvard populace's incessantly hypocritical ways, or maybe otherwise. But Violence Jack, with the fire of hatred burning in the recesses of his beady eyes, stalked down that embankment and into the gymnasium….. * * * (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) Alright, the ‘poll’ didn’t work out too well. I almost got myself arrested for assault and did get charged with interfering in a peaceful rally, or whatever the hell the pigs told me that last arraignment was officially referred to…..but as much as I’ve been thrown in jail for the first one, I know damn well what they call it at least. Yet another rare indictment to add to the list against yours truly. Sort of surprised though, I always thought morons like that always got all jaded and tongue-tied around celebrities (yeah, I consider myself that by now) and kissed their ass. Either these Cali sh*theads have some really tight line of influence on those snot-nosed yuppies or those punks were just asinine. Still, I don’t doubt they represent the percentage of most fans. Everyone loves a clown and nobody likes a critic. Then again, ask me if I give a damn. That’s what will make this victory all the more gratifying. Thrashing the glorious social avatars of America’s precious Gen-X babies; what a grand little stress-buster. Powtay’s Prodigal Offspring versus Boston’s Bastard Son…..when worlds collide. Heheheh…… Welcome to your exile, Chris and Teddy.
