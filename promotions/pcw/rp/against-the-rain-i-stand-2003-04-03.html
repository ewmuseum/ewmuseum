Against the Rain I Stand

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-03-03 The scene opens at a lone bridge. Fog rolls over the ground as the moon cast a shadow over the earth, bathing it in it’s light. “It’s a damn cold night.” A voice calls out in the night air. The crackle of thunder echoes in the background signaling the coming of a storm. Small droplets of rain have already started to fall. There is a street lamp proving the only source of light. A lone figure stands under the light leaning against the edge of the bridge. “Where is she?” the person asks. He folds his arms over the stone ledge of the bridge and looks down at the water below. The falling rain distorts his reflection in the water. “I guess she’s not coming.” He says with a soft sigh. The man pushes away from the ledge and looks into the sky as he leans back against the ledge now facing in the opposite direction. “What am I doing out here?” he asks to no one but himself. He looks around to see no one around. Shifting something under his overcoat, a glimmer of gold shines through. He pulls it out to reveal one half of the PCW Tag Team championship titles. The light rain pitter-patters on the metal. “Here I am standing in the rain in the middle of the night clinging to the little bit of hope I have left.” His words are cold like the night air. Holding the title out in front of him he looks at himself in it. His blond hair is wet from the raindrops. His ocean blue eyes seemed stranded since the events of the past few days. His face is long with wear showing how tired he is. “They took from me everything I held dear. My career was ripped from my fingers and dashed to bits. I sit at home now and watch others doing what I live to do, wondering why I wasn’t asked to wrestle this show. Then they crossed the line and took the one thing I hold dearer then anything else. They took the woman I love from me.” His hands start to shake; his grip gets tighter on the title. Calming himself down, he tries to continue. “I loved her with all my heart. Every ounce of my soul belonged to her. Yet on that ill-fated day my life came to an end. I can still remember it now. Every night I have to lie awake and wonder why this happened to me. Why my one chance at true love was taken from me.” He lowers his head and presses the gold against his face. The rain starts to fall even harder now as thunder and lightning ripples through the air. He lifts his face up now with an anger look upon him. He looks directly forward. “P.W.A. and the PCW have insulting me. They have dragged me through the mud. But soon they will see that a man can only take so much abuse before he’ll rise back to his feet and strike back. My time is coming soon. I sworn before I would get them, thinking they did all they could to me. But I was wrong.” He just shakes his head in shame. Walking forward he brings the title into the light letting his reflection shine through in the gold. The face of Chris Storm stares at him. “Look at how far I fell in the world. I am a broken man. I can’t even bring myself to enjoy the time with the ones I love anymore. Every moment I see them I can only think of what happened. It breaks my heart so that it’s just easier for me to avoid them all and try not to think about it. Maybe…” his voice starts shaking. Chris turns toward the bridge again and looks out to the water. He quickly walks toward the ledge. “Maybe I should just end it all here. Get rid of my pain and suffering. It would be the best for everyone. Tessa could go on and finally find someone that can take care of her. Christa, Evan, and Alex would have an actual father figure in their life. Not some clown in short pants running around in a ring trying to perform for the masses like some little show monkey.” His words are rapid, slurring over each other as he gets to the ledge. He hits the ledge with his chest and nearly flips over the ledge into the water. He manages to stop himself as he looks down at the now rushing water. The rain is falling harder and faster now. The storm is picking up covering the sky with black clouds. Behind Chris, lightning streaks across the blackness serving light against the darkness. His breath picks up as the thoughts race through his head. Should he? Is it worth it? Despite the rain he still is sweating about it. “Give it all up? Go ahead and end my life in the rushing waters below? How my life has come to this? What did I do to deserve this? These thoughts run through my head are maddening! P.W.A., PCW, the Tournament, the Tag Team titles…” He stops. Everything seems to go silent for him. The sound of the rain and lightning are blocked from his ears. The only sound he can hear is his last thought. “The Tag Team titles…” Chris pulls the PCW Tag Team title up to look at it, his image reflecting in the bright gold of the title. “You…you did this to me. It’s your entire fault! If you weren’t around my waist then the P.W.A. wouldn’t have targeted us. Tessa wouldn’t have because so engulfed with the idea of keeping you! I don’t understand what the big deal is. You are nothing more then a piece of tin on a leather strap. I know how I can change my life around. If I get rid of you then there will be no more Nerezza or Gaz Green trying to ruin my life. No more Tessa Green being wrapped up in the idea of being the Tag Team champions. Everything can go back to the way it uses to be!” Slinging the title out, Chris holds it over the river below. His grip is locked on tight around the strap of the belt. The memories of all the shit that has been happening to him starts running through his head like a broken record. Chris’ grasp on the title starts to loosen as he starts to lose more and more hope. Tears start to roll down his eyes. “Chris…” Against the night a voice calls out. “Chris…” Again it speaks to him. The voice is so soft and gentle. More tears start to roll down his face as Chris pulls the tag strap in close and hugs it. He sits down against the ledge and curls up. He starts to shiver from the cold air and fear wrapped around his body. “I just couldn’t do it. I’m so weak!” he shouts as his fist slams against the bridge in anger. “It’s all I have left in this world, my status as being the reigning PCW Tag Team champion. It’s my only link to Tessa still. We both hold one of these titles. It is our bond to one another. It may not be wedding rings like I had wanted but it’s still something I can say that is OURS!” Chris clings to the title close to his chest, wrapping both of his arms around it. The rain continues to fall without compassion on the head of the Perfect Storm. Chris shivers at the sound of the lightning. Through the cold and the darkness comes the voice of an angel to him. “Chris…” His head looks up from the title. His chest goes in and out as his breathing is deep and long. Chris looks around from where the voice came from. It’s like a warm blanket that wraps around him keeping him safe from the hate in the world. Each time it says his name he calms down more and more. Chris slowly makes it back to his feet. He looks out for the voice, still seeing no one around for as far as the eye can see. He yells out, “TESSA! Are you there?” There is no reply. Chris franticly looks around for someone, anyone that is calling out to him. As he wanders around he keeps the Tag title close to his beating heart. Like a lost child Chris searches for the source of the majestic voice. “Tessa can you hear me? Answer me! Call out to me again please!” he pleads. The rain and thunder beat down on Chris still. Chris pays it no attention as he continues on with his search for the calming voice of the night. Finally the voice calls out to him once more. “…you are a failure.” Shocked and stunned Chris just stands in the rain. His security blanket violently ripped from him. The PCW Tag Team title falls out of Chris’ hands and hits the ground with a thud. His breath is caught in his throat. Chris falls to his knees, broken and crushed from the words of the night. “…no…” It’s all Chris can manage to say as he falls to his knees, tears rolling down his eyes. Chris falls down and grabs his title from the ground and clutches it close to his chest. His forehead presses against the ground as he pulls the tag strap as close to him as he can manage. He cocks back his head and lets out a roar. “NO! I won’t believe it!” “…you are a failure.” Again the voice speaks through the darkness. Chris just shakes his head, refusing to believe it. “I’m not a failure! I’M NOT!” he shouts as he comes back to his feet. Like a wild man Chris comes back to his feet and looks around for the source of the voice. He shouts at the top of his lungs as he swings the title around without reason. Chris starts to throw wild punches at the rain drops. His blond tips are plastered to his forehead, weighed down from the water. “Just watch…the Continental Contendership I will show everyone just how much of a failure Chris Storm is. I will prove my worth to the PCW and to the world! I am Chris Storm and you will hear me RAR!” Against the night air he stands, breathing in and out deeply. He stands in the middle of the bridge looking out at the blackness of the sky. Lightning and thunder rains down from the heavens, unnoticed by the Perfect Storm. The PCW Tag Team title hands from his grasp, the gold shimmering in the light of the nearby light pole. The angelic voice fades from his ears as the sounds of the rain and thunder slowly begin to come back to him. “I will be the Continental champion…I will be the Tag Team champion…I will be your king.”
