The Ides of March.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Assume this was on 3/15/02 Darkness falls on the majestic front gate of Stately Steele Manor: wrought copper bars twisted into the symbol of Zeus, a thunderbolt plowing through a circular enclosure, turned a mint green from age and elements. The Grecian pillars rising up on both sides contain the gate, vines of ivy curling up the ornate carvings and spreading outwards unto the red brick wall that encloses the expansive confines of the estate. The estate is unusually active tonight. All the lights in the Manor remain shining despite the growing Californian power deficit. Guards patrol the front walkway, their Pantene Pro-V blonde hair shining from light of the moon. The astute observer would be able to hear Big Alan Steele’s trusted manager Sue Saksnussum barking out orders over the lull of the crickets. Stately Steele Manor is on lockdown. Now, if you were that astute observer, you might have noticed something… odd out of the corner of your eye. A trick of the night? A streak of darkness? You might have seen something vault the wall with amazing deftness and agility, but the logical mind would probably have dismissed it as maybe a passing car’s headlights or maybe a squirrel looking for a snack, for no human could move like that manner. And thusly the ninja strikes. Creeping around a tree, the hooded form waits patiently as the two Graces patrol the front walk, rifles strapped to their backs. The moment both their backs are turned, there is again a streak of black, the masked man silently moving across the East lawn and diving behind a set of bushes just as the blondes turn back to that section, their brows furrowed, wondering to themselves if they had seen something. Shrugging, they go back to the routine, the man in black belly-crawling around the corner of the East wing. Slapping himself against cool texture of the house, the eyes look up, scanning the façade of the building. The neo-classical deco of the home has many windows for viewing the sunrise and subsequently many of the bedrooms are on this side of the home. But the architecture lends itself to a flaw in that one portion is indented for a balcony, creating a jutting section from an already indented part. The ninja sees this opportunity to keep his presence a secret and reaches into a small satchel riding in his back, pulling out a gun-like mechanism with a three pronged grapple. A quick hiss sends the grapple flying towards the roof, clamoring slightly above. Gripping the rope, the man quickly shimmies the rope towards the roof, standing on the enclosure for the balcony. A quick twist of the hand and the grapple falls back down, the gun reeling in the line silently. Leaping up and grabbing the roof tiles, the ninja hoists himself onto the roof and crouches low, silhouetted against the shining of the near-full moon. In the moonlight, the terrain of the roof looks like a foreign world, with the slanted tiles creating an odd assortment of shadows accompanied by the heaving, hilly design of the architect. Sprinting silently, the man ducks behind the main fireplace leading into Big Alan Steele’s study. The reason for the stealth is pretty obvious – sitting at the highest point of the roof is a rather bored looking blonde clutching at her rifle, tossing small rock-like bits of tile across the roof with small clatters. The form runs like a cat, on all fours, to another section of the roof that rises up, sliding against the side. Picking up a small piece of tile, he waits is turn. The blonde chucks another piece forward, and it rattles home into the gutters. A few moments pass and that is when the man in black tosses his tile across to the West side of the roof, the tile clacking across the roof. The new sound spooks the blonde, who rises and fires indiscriminately towards the unaccounted for noise. Seizing the opportunity, the ninja races from behind and pulls out a small aerosol can, spraying a cloud of mist that envelops the poor girl’s head. The weapon clatters from her hands and her eyes roll back into her head. Her legs turn to rubber and she falls backwards and the man catches her, gently lowering her to the cool tiles. A quick check of her vitals reveals that she is still alive, as evidenced by her noxious snoring, to which the ninja allows himself a small, grim smile. Creeping across the roof, he looks at the area that the Grace has fired towards in her panic-stricken attack. Brightly colored feathered darts stick into the tiles, on of which the black man reaches down and grabs. He examines it closely, smelling it, tasting it with a gentle lick. Again, the wry smile. Tranquilizing darts. He stuffs a few into his satchel and moves towards the front of the roof, slithering on his stomach until slightly hanging over the roof. There is the expanse of the Manor front lawn, the two Graces plodding through their steps. A few minutes pass as the ninja tries to gain insight into the manner of their movements, but eventually gives up due to the unpredictability of blondes. Pulling a small tube from his sack, he places the darts into the end of the tube and waits his chance. The blondes turn away from each other and the ninja blows heavily into the tube, the dart whistling through the air and striking the one in the neck. She immediately falls as if hit by a truck. The other blonde can sort of hear her crumple, and turns to see her friend sleeping peacefully. The girl opens her mouth to sound the alarm, but instead only manages a yawn as she falls on top of the other Grace, a red-feathered dart protruding from her thigh. The man wastes no time and heads over to approximately the center of the home, he fixes a cord to a statue overhang and moves across the roof back to the West portion. With total confidence, he leaps off the roof, the cord pulling taut, and he swings gracefully across the front of the home, reaching the apex of his arc at the East side. With amazing dexterity, he reaches into his tunic and tosses a baseball-sized object crashing through the window. He then swings to the other side as alarms resound throughout the Manor, the sounds of Sue screaming out directives. The device tossed through the window explodes, the concussive force blowing out the lights, and the smoke grenade fogs up the room. The door opens, a phalanx of blondes enters, now coughing at the noxious fumes. Soon, they fall unconscious to the sleeping gas, falling across the bedroom in peaceful slumber. Sue can be heard shouting at them to get out in the hallway, voice muffled by her shirt. The ninja re-climbs to the roof and races to the chimney, climbing on top of it. Checking to make sure its not lit, he slides down in a hail soot and grit into the fireplace. He waits for a few moments behind the fireplace protective glass, so as to not give away his position with the black cloud of chimney build-up over the years. Listening carefully, he can tell that Sue and the Graces are busily running around upstairs trying to figure out just where the intruder is. A grin on the face of the ninja as he finally exits the fireplace and stalks his way through the study and into the foyer. Sliding silently through the foyer and into the main hall, the man shakes with almost uncontrolled glee as he makes his way down to the… and he stops. His eyes grow wide as he catches a glimpse of a sight in the kitchen that makes him stop and turn. Sitting in the kitchen table in all its magnificence is a turkey sandwich: lettuce, tomato, onion, Swiss, dripping with dijionaise mustard and thick sourdough bread. A pickle spear sits enticing him to the right and a handful of rippled potato chips beckon from the left. The ninja’s eyes are wide, and he puts a hand to his growling stomach. His head jerks upwards, following the sounds of the confusion upstairs before again focusing on the sandwich. He creeps into the kitchen, wary of a trick. He lowers his head to that of the table, scrutinizing the situation, hand running along his face in a blend of excitement and confusion. With great trepidation, he slowly reaches forward, gently probing at the sourdough. The pressure spot gives to his touch with a small indentation, and dijionaise oozes out the side. A trickle of drool glistens from the ninja. Hands poised over the tasty morsel, he slowly lowers his hands around the meal and grips, pulling it from the plate. He raises quickly, holding the sandwich out from his, body tensed and eyes closed, waiting for the trick. A moment passes and nothing happens. Grinning lasciviously, he turns and stuffs a huge bite into his mouth. Snick! The man pauses in mid-munch, a look of sheer terror in his eyes. Looking over his shoulder, the man in black can see the plate slowly descend through the table. Turning and running, losing all sense of secrecy, he sprints from the kitchen. Loge tube slide out of the hallway walls and begin firing hundreds upon hundreds of brightly colored feathered darts into the air. The man gripping the sandwich leaps and flips and tucks and rolls, but despite his defensive movements, sharp stabbing pains hit him in his back, his thigh, his buttocks… all of which quickly turn numb and spread through his body. A testament to his stamina, his legs wobble but he does not succumb to the intense barbiturates coursing through his veins. Stumbling out through the hall and into the foyer, he staggers his way towards the front door… “Hey guy…” The female voice causes him to look up. Standing on the railing of the uppermost part of the stairway, Sue Saksnussum perches like a gargoyle, a sneer on her lips. The man in black can do nothing but watch as she launches herself into the air, swinging across the room on a cord tied to the chandelier, and planting both feet into the chest of the ninja, sending him flying back, crashing through the front door and into unconsciousness… ……………… SPLASH!! The bucket of ice cold water jolts the man back into reality. Dripping wet and head fogged from a hangover, he slowly opens his eyes to the pain of the lights. Sue, smug grin firmly ensconced on her mug, drops the bucket to the floor with a clunk and quickly steps forward to rip off the mask of the ninja. We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he sits heavily chained to a chair in the kitchen of Stately Steele Manor. Sue steps back, arms folded against her chest. Behind her, many of the Graces sitting at the table in various states of duress, trying to shake off the aftereffects of the drug-induced naps they’ve recently awoken from. At the center of the table, burning brightly, sits a huge birthday cake, dark smoke belching from the almost obscene amount of candles. Big Alan Steele: (squirming) “Augh! No! NO! Beware! BEWARE the ides of March!!! Curse my love of juicy, processed poultry!!” Sue: (harshly) “That’s enough, Al! Every year it’s the same thing – you knock us out with tranquilizers and ignore your birthday! Hell, we haven’t celebrated your birthday in 4 years! Who do you think you are? A Puerto Rican baseball player?!” BAS: (eyes closed shut) “The ides of March… BEWARE!!” Sue: “This isn’t like some cartoon where you don’t get older Al. We all get older. It’s natural and right. Just because you don’t acknowledge it doesn’t mean you aren’t aging…” BAS: (singing) “I, Big Alan Steele, am not listening!! La la la la la la…” Sue: “Stop it!! I’m tired of waking up the next day with a splitting headache, with the presents returned and the cake buried in the backyard! Just deal with it!!” BAS: (eyes clamped shut, still singing) “La la la la la…” Sue: (motioning) “Candi, Steffi – pull his eyelids back.” Steffi: (cracking knuckles) “With pleasure.” The girls walk over to the juvenile antics of Big Al, and grip his head with their sharp, talon-like nails. Steele squirms and screams, but the manage to each get a hold of an eyelid and force it open. BAS: “NO! NO! What manner of sin could a man commit in a single lifetime to befall such a horrific fate?!” Sue: “It’s a birthday, Al! Not a crucifixion! Get ready to sing, girls!” BAS: “I am nigh immortal! I am a man-god!! Hi-Keeba!!” Sue: (blowing a tuning whistle, singing w/ the Graces) “HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YOU! HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YOU!!” BAS: “NO! STOP! YOU ARE KILLING ME!!” Sue and Graces: “HAPPY BIRTHDAY BIG AAAALLLLLLL!!!” BAS: “NO! STOP!!” Sue and Graces: “HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YOOOOUUUUUU!!!” BAS: “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!” A final yell, the song finished, Big Al slumps in his chair, defeated. BAS: (quietly sobbing) “You have done it. You have weakened the protector of PCW righteousness and San Jose by a year, further speeding me to the great beyond that is Death’s icy cold grip.” Sue: (sighing) “Get a grip, Big guy. Now, let’s get to those presents. Most are from various super-villains, you know. A nice gesture, doncha think?” Sue points over to Terri, dressed in full bomb-squad gear poking at a pile of ticking, smoking and otherwise dangerous looking packages, some of the lesser and more sensitive presents blowing up from the 20 foot pole she’s using. BAS: (dejected) “Hi-Keeba.”
