Date: not entered Canada… hmm… supposed to be one of the nicest places around the World… supposed to be one of the most enjoyable holiday locations to visit. We’re not talking in the sense where there’s about three hundred million people rushing from door to door, drinking a few beers before chucking up everywhere. No, we’re talking about the kind of enjoyment that comes with the tranquillity… that comes with the smooth aroma… the sweet smell of calmness… in a sense, it could be compared to the Calm that is expressed greatly within the walls of ‘Final Fantasy X’… but to really make such a judgement would be stupid, especially since the World isn’t being threatened by something with the power of ‘Sin’ and ‘Yu Yevon’. The third instalment of this mini-series sees the first meeting between PoP-Star Icon… and PoP Star. Rich Sherwin… the talented wonder-icon-star… will be meeting one of the people whom he would like to join his legion… his army… his regiment of talent-dripping Stars, all of who… will be ready to play their parts… in the Ultimate Uprising of PoPular music. Welcome back? I guess that’s the kind of message that I should be using… especially since I hope a lot of you people deciding to look at this, have been able to enjoy looking through the other two pieces of the puzzle. So, here we are… in the Country of Canada… the very location that so-many people like to insult, that so many people… will make fun of and what-not. If I were… lets see, about… ehm, something like… two years younger, then maybe the whole reason for my decision to reach this Country would be to unfold the amazingly surprising myth… of ‘The Canadian Floppy Head’. Judging by my usual antics around the United States, I wouldn’t scold you for assuming that was the real idea behind my visit… but that’s not the case. As stated before, I’m here to look at somebody who could prove to be a valuable member of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ band within the months to come. Therefore, without any further wasted time… lets get this entire shindig… on the ROAD!! The picture slowly begins to open up outside a relatively large building. As the scene shifts around all four sides of the location, we eventually come to a sign that reads, “Audition Hall – Where you attempt to Audition and we take your money”. Upon covering this sign, the camera slides down the wall… getting a half-descent view of the gravel coated front before reaching a pair of ‘Double Doors’. The picture was relatively empty… with the few odd faces here and there. We could see Rich Sherwin seated on a bench outside the location, a relatively young female adult taking up the position next to him. Sherwin looks to be happy… seated next to this blonde bombshell… the woman on the other-hand, choosing to hide her ‘Delight’ and express a sour look of both boredom and frustration. Upon closer inspection, we’re able to see that the mouth of Sherwin is jabbing away… filling the air with various comments that suggest egotism, machismo… among other fabricated crap. We move toward Sherwin and this women, waiting patiently as the conversation continues to progress in its one-person manner. “Woah… did I happen to tell you all about the time I wrestled for a company called ‘Extremely Suicidal Wrestling’? You see… there was this one time… I was in the locker room area, waiting patiently for my match to begin… and well, that PCW Superstar, Scorpion… well, back in the day when we both wrestled for the indies, he walked into my locker room… and well, he…”, Sherwin tried to continue with the story, but the woman seated next to him raised her hand quickly. Rich looked at her with a glare that read, ‘Why in the hell did you go and do that?!’ and made it obvious that he was mentally saying those words with intent. She looked at him with a look of ‘Bitchiness’… something that would usually be frowned upon by anyone… but hey, she’d just had to listen to Sherwin rabble on and the like about various different points in both his Music and Professional-Wrestling Career. As she lowered her hand from out of the face of Sherwin, he spoke up toward her with, “… HEY! Why did you have to got all out and cut me off like that? I was telling you about the time that Scorpion came into my locker-room and gave me an ‘Atomic Noggie’ for saying that N’Sync would take out Ja Rule if they worked as a complete unit.” “You know…”, she started, allowing Sherwin to settle down before saying anything in response to his claims of being subjected to an ‘Atomic Noggie’ in ESW for his comment, “… that story you just told me, has to be… something of the biggest pile of crap that I’ve ever been able to hear in the history of my life. Scorpion… wasting his time with a nobody like yourself? Geez boy, don’t make me get up and walk away in a wheel of laughs.” Sherwin looked shocked… the women had just dismissed his account of such an event and even accused the Mulit-Millionaire Wrestling/PoP Star of being nothing but a ‘Nobody’. They looked into one another’s eyes and the camera moved in a little more… capturing just the burning infernos that were being ignited in the eyes of both characters. The background when black… everything else seemed to vanish… and then, the powers of the Mystical Psyche began to run amok, bursting out of their eyes… hitting each other in a battle that would equal the great power of the Force between both ‘Darth Vader’ and ‘Obi-Wan Kenobi’. The picture soon faded back into its generic background… and the bolts of Psyche Lightning vanished. “How… dare you sit next to me… and tell me that the story I just told you is nothing but a bunch of… poop! How dare you, have the audacity to sit in the space next to me… on this Wooden Chair… and inform me, that the story of Tapenga and the White Horse in Maine was nothing but a creation from the depths of my mind!… and yeah, I didn’t forget about you spitting the arrogant claim that my story about being Noggie’d was nothing but a fake ass too. Its people like you… who come and sit down, next to awesome athletes like myself… and claim that everything we say is fake… so, go on… GET OUTTA HERE!”, he says, screaming out the last three words like a crack-addict demanding his fix… although in this case, drugs weren’t the problem with the outburst… it was all down to the fact that somebody couldn’t find it in themselves to just… believe. The women remained seated in the confinements of her chair for a little bit longer, looking at Sherwin with a glare as if to say… “He, he, he… you can’t tell me what to do… what to do… what to do… you can’t tell me what to do… ha, ha, he, he, hoo!’. The ‘Champion of All Things created for PoP’ stood up, towering above the seated woman and hissed the kind of ‘Hiss’ that’d be heard by a crazy-assed child, looking to scare away the ‘Girl Next Door’ who had juvenile crush on him. “Oh My God… you are SO getting slobber and spit… and ewww… all kinds of nasty stuff over my nice clothes. You… may well be a member of the Elite Wrestling Company… Primetime Championship Wrestling… but by God, you are one {bleep} up son of a {bleep}!”, this Woman screamed out before getting up from her seat and storming off in the opposite direction to Sherwin. Our hero stood tall and waved her off into the sunset, turning around to face an imaginary selection of fans, waving as if he’d just saved everyone from disaster. In the sensitive scenario, Sherwin had just saved the world from a stuck-up bitch who just couldn’t take anything… but in the eyes of business, he’d just killed a potential buyer of the next PCW ‘Pay-Per-View’ show. Regardless of all of this… Sherwin had won a battle on the ‘Battlefield’… not one that he’d be able to rush home and tell the kids about… and not one that he’ll be boasting to his fans about, but one… that will forever leave his name engraved in the book of “Pathetic Losers winning against Bitchy Women”. “Yes! Another strike in the column of wins… for Rich Sherwin against the World… oh, how today… is the day that nothing… and by God, I mean… nothing, will be able to go wrong! For Christ’s Sake… I am the KING… I’m on-top of the WOOOOOOOOOORRRRRLLLLLLDDDDDD”, Sherwin screamed out at the top of his voice, punching his arms into the air, celebrating everything that was to celebrate a win as pathetic as his own. Upon reaching the end of his continuous scream of ‘World’, Sherwin dropped to both knees on the ground, folding over and pounding both hands on the ground. He remained in this same position for about five seconds before rising back onto his knees, and getting back up to his vertical basis. “Oh Yes Rich…”, he started before continuing, “… You are the only member… of Primetime Championship Wrestling… that has the ‘Eye of the Tiger’… you are the only member… of Primetime Championship Wrestling… that has everything good about them… and by God, when the time comes… you’re going to be the only person in the history of the company… to have become a ‘PCW Grand Slam Champion’… and a holder of several Grammy Awards!” Sherwin continued to walk around on the spot in his cheery mood, fuelled by the small dreams of life that would usually seem way ‘Out of Reach’ to realistic person. He waits a few moments before cheerily walking over to the pavement, turning left and facing the ‘Double Door’ entrance to the complex. Still smiling, he pulls open the door upon the left side and steps into the building, his face still beaming of Victory and Musical Achievement Awards. Richie walked into the hall of the building and looked around at the half-filled seating arrangement about twenty paces infront of him. He smiled at all the people who had now turned their attentions to the ‘New Entrant’. Amazingly enough, all the people looking at him in silence wasn’t the thing to catch Sherwin off… it was the sound of a gruff woman sitting behind the reception desk that took that task… and succeeded with an amazing degree of success. “HEY!”, her voice bellowed, piercing the ears of everyone that was already seated within the establishment. Sherwin was rocked out of his “Glory Mode” and immediately looked over toward her with the lingering sensation of ‘Fear’ falling upon him. The ‘thing’ continued to glare over from the reception desk and a few moments later, the entire Establishment was forced to hear voice again as she said, “… What line of business are you coming here for? A Standard Talent Audition… a Musical one… acting, dancing… or maybe you’re looking to break into the game known as Pornography? I’m sure we can use a stud like you in the next flick.” Sherwin was taken back by the last comment and sniggered to himself a little. For somebody like himself, being in a Porno video was a Big no-no… a very big one at that. He cautiously began to walk toward the desk, stopping about three inches short of being within reaching distance. “Erm… I’m not here for one of these… auditions… especially since I’m earning more than everyone in this room at the moment… but hey, gloating about the fact I have two successful contracts running next isn’t the real agenda. Infact, I’m here to talk to somebody who’d been here for some… talent audition with a new band… I believe he travels these parts under the name of ‘Stimpo’… although, I’m yet to be able to confirm anything.”, Sherwin explained, making sure that was always out of reaching distance… after all, there’s no sure-fire way to be safe against somebody that was as big… disgusting… and ugly as the ‘Thing’ glancing Sherwin’s way. He looked over to the half-filled room again, primarily to get away from having to make eye-contact with the thing that’ll probably be floating in his dreams for days… weeks… months… hell, even years to come. The ‘thing’ behind the desk pounded a few keys on a keyboard… hidden by the top coating of her ‘Workplace’ and pressed ‘Enter’ before using the mouse to scan up and down something on the monitor infront of her. Sherwin waited patiently for something in the region of five minutes, his eyes moving around the room at a random speed… eye contact with the behemoth of a thing… never being made unless it was absolutely necessary. She continued to scan around the screen until she seemed to have found something. With a shrill yell of delight, she read through some more details before turning back up to our icon. “Hmm… it seems as though your little friend Stimpo was here for an audition or something a few days ago. I’m not sure if this is still correct or anything… but apparently, according to the database we have here… it says that your little buddy is going to be coming around in something like… five minutes.”, she stated, sounding a little less gruff than before… and for some reason… joyful. Maybe she’d just got bored of being nasty to every single living creature… or maybe, there was something about Stimpo that she found to be a little more enjoyable than the average person. Sherwin didn’t know what the reason was for her sudden change in attitude… and to be perfectly honest, it wasn’t something that he was willing to kill himself about, but… at the end of the day, if you’re able to change the mood of people that strange… you’re bound to be able to perform wonders. Rich looked at his watch, getting a good indication of the time… and then the time in five minutes. He sighed heavily, not really wanting to have to wait for somebody… but in this situation, he knew that if anything was going to happen… it would happen through the element of patience. “Five Minutes or so?… oh well, its a lot better than having to wait another two hours or something. Thanks for… your help I guess.”, is everything that Sherwin managed to blurb out before walking over to the half-filled seated area and taking a seat on his own along the sides. With the wheels in motion for the meeting between PoP Icon and PoP Hopeful, many people can only start to think about the kinds of words that will be said between both parties. Will this meeting between both Rich Sherwin and Stimpo really prove to be valuable?… will Stimpo be able to provide something more than just tame laughter to the “Faction” that will be ‘The Pretty Cool Guys’? To find out the answers to all of these questions… and a lot more, including if that ‘thing’ was just happy to see Stimpo’s name… tune in next time!
