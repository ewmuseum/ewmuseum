The 'Madness' Infects New Breed; Taking care of the so-called 'legends' third.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today ::As the scene opens up we see a shot of the Savvis Center. Before our narrator (that's me) can get a word in the camera zooms out to reveal PCW reporter Cal Seaver standing in front of the centre.:: Cal: I'm here at the Savvis Center in St. Louis, Missouri, the very place where history will be made in some 24 hours. The place where 20 men will fight for the right to hold one title, the most prestigious title in all of professional wrestling, at the Pay Per View called March Madness. And let me tell you something, it may seem peaceful here right now, but in just a day or so, this place is gonna go nuts, and amidst all the madness, a new champion, more than likely, will be crowned. In fact, the odds of Snow retaining are seemingly 19 to 1. I'm standing out here with a bunch of guys who can possibly make Snow's odds a little better, PCW superstars, New Breed members.... ::We see a head enter our screen and whisper into Cal's ear. Cal turns his head and looks at the person who whispered to him, confused. He shakes his head. Suddenly a pair of hands enter the screen and grab Cal by the scruff of his neck, lifting him off the ground.:: Cal: ....A-a-and TRUE legends, Corey Williams and Chris Chase! ::The hands let go of Cal, he drops back down to the ground, the hands then push him off camera.:: (Singing) 'Meet me in St. Louie, Louie......' ::The camera zooms out to reveal the honour of the hands, one 'Controversial' Corey Williams, with an eerie smile about him.:: Corey (singing): St. Louie... by the fair! ::Corey Williams laughs to himself in an evil and superior way.:: Corey: Hello marks, smarks and controversy junkies alike, welcome to the Savvis centre in good 'ole St Louie. And what a time it is, Pay-Per-View time! Professionals wrestling's monthly Superbowl, this is the place to be, and the time is now. They call it March Madness but it seems simple enough. Twenty men will go in, twenty men will leave, nineteen over the top rope, one in a blaze of glory. And without making and namby-pamby speeches I can tell you right now that the man leaving with the belt is gonna be........ ::Corey opens his mouth and we see his mouth form the sound C, the beginning of his own name, but he is interrupted.:: 'Jason Snow' ::The camera zooms out again, this time to accommodate Chris Chase into it. Corey's head snaps in the direction of Chris Chase, the two look into each other’s eyes, trying to figure something out. Corey nods and turns back to Cal, giving a weak, embarrassed smile.:: Corey: Of course, Jason Snow, precisely what I was about to say. We're New Breed and we'll stick together, Snow is our headman and we gotta look after him. It's time to set personal glory aside for the good of New Breed. ::Just by looking at Corey we can tell he doesn't really mean it, what snakes New Breed are, lying amongst themselves. I guess that's why they're best friends, coz they got a lot in common.:: Chris: Yeh, Snow's been working hard and he deserves the win, we're just here to take some pressure off of him. Corey: Yes.... yes of course we are. Chris: But enough about that... ::Chris quickly changes the subject, he instead opts for a subject that CCW and himself are more comfortable with, how much better than everyone else they are.:: Chris: I mean it's not as though the other 17 guys are gonna give us much of a challenge anyway. I mean, we're New Breed! Nobody gives as challenge. The last person to challenge me was Xavier Kannon, and where is he now Cal? Cal: Uhh.... He's a successful PCW wrestler and a cherished member of Asylum. ::Chris scowls.:: Chris: So? He's not Cruiserweight Champion, is he? ::Chris points to the Cruiserweight Belt currently around his waist.:: Chris: He's not a member of the most envied stable in PCW history. And for the love of Blasphemy he sure as hell ain't 'The Messiah' Chris Chase, as much as he WISHES he were. Corey: Yeh, I'm getting a little sick and tired of everyone wanting to be us, I mean it was fun at first, but now it's just a little lame. And what's worse are the so-called 'legends', which in translation means 'old farts' who are desperate to hold onto their spot, the spot that they no longer deserve the right to hold. We're the NEW legends of PCW, not these washed up has-beens like Jon Kano, Legend Inc, Boda and Tyler Nelson. Jon Kano is an idiot, I sarcastically called him a legend once and he's been trying to jump my bones ever since. Chris: ....Homo. I couldn't help but notice him stink up our ratings last Friday, I mean, no wonder everyone is seeing OSW as such a threat. Corey: Yeh I wish Tyler Nelson would send HIM to OSW, let him stink up THEIR ring for a change. Cal: Tyler Nelson? You can't say stuff about Tyler Nelson like that! Corey: Why not? Are we meant to be afraid of him? Cal: Well I guess not, but you still can't say stuff about him like that... Corey: For Chase's sake man, tell us why! ::Chase smiles slyly.:: Chase: I think I know what's going on here. Corey: You do? What? Chase (melodically): Someone's got a boyfriend, someone's got a boyfriend. ::Cal tries to ignore Chase, not even looking at him. Corey joins in on the fun.:: Corey: So Cal, is it serious? How many dates have you guys had? Cal: I-I don't know what you're talking about. Chris: Did he buy you chocolate? Corey: Did he bring you flowers? Chris: Did he overpower you? Corey: Did he do POLITICALLY INCORRECT things to you? Cal: ENOUGH! WILL YOU TWO SHUT THE HELL UP! I'M A HAPPILY MARRIED MAN! ::Cal's outburst silences Corey and Chris..........for a moment.:: Chris: .......Do I smell lover's tiff? ::Cal runs away, breaking into tears. Leaving Chase looking on confused.:: Chris: Geez, what's his problem? ::Corey hits Chris in the arm, but in the way a friend would casually hit his friend if he was acting like a dick.:: Corey: NOW look what you've done. It's no wonder nobody but Dustin will interview you. Chris: Me? I'm not the one who always has to be 'controversial.' Look, I'm going to get some coffee from the shop across the road, you want one? Corey: Yeh, latte would be nice. ::Chris walks off screen to get the coffee, leaving Corey the sole man on camera. Corey's eyes follow Chris as he slowly walks off, when he thinks that Chris is far enough away, he turns to the camera.:: Corey: Chris can think whatever he wants, the fact is that despite being a superior individual like as myself, Jason Snow isn't Corey Williams. And if there's one person I'd like to see as champ more than Jason Snow, it's Corey Williams. Hell, Snow's had the belt, now it's my turn. My turn to take PCW into a new CONTROVERSIAL era, my turn to make history, my turn to be in the spotlight. I'm no Chris SLOBoda, my theory is that first impressions last. Big Boda Daddy? The guy has been in contention for the belt so long that he should be known as the Big Boda GRANDaddy! Not Corey Williams, I don't wait around, banking on second and third chances. One chance, one success. 'Controversial' Corey Williams, the NEW World Champ! ::Corey is staring into the camera, his eyes burning right through it and to the people watching those TV sets at home. Chris comes back and Corey turns back into innocent 'let's make Snow win' mode just in time. Chris enters the camera with one coffee in hand.:: Corey: Hey! Where's my coffee? Chris: Ah Sh*t! I forgot it. Corey: You forgot it, dumb f*ck, I guess I'll just have to go get it myself. How's that saying, if you want it done right, do it yourself. Chris: Ok, I'm real sorry Corey. ::Corey walks off, this time Chris' eyes follow HIM.:: Chris: .....No Corey, I'm Chris Chase, I NEVER forget! ::Chris turns his head towards the camera, a cocky smile upon his face.:: Chris: Just like I have yet to forget what it felt like, being in the spotlight, at the top of the federation, much like I was in PCW2. I never forgot what it felt like to be the man that everyone knew was the best. Jason Snow, great man and great friend, unbelievably talented wrestler. But when he was in diapers in the Rising Star title division, I was Chris Chase, the pin-up boy of PCW2. Tonight I might have a chance to regain my former glory, and if I get that chance, I'm gonna take it. Y'know, what Corey said was right, if you want something done right, do it yourself. And Jason Snow is a big man, he can handle himself, I know I too will be handling myself. And if he and I get in the same place and the same time, I won't be one to stand aside, and we will see who the better man is. ::Chris Chase pauses, thinking about the very trouble his own words are causing, whilst Corey's words were slightly more out of passion for the belt, Chris Chase has always been a more calculated man, yet just as confident in his own abilities.:: Chris: Legend? If Legend Inc is considered a legend then you can call me the exact opposite of that. I don't need to suck Duke William's c*ck to get my shot at the title, I earned my shot with my athletic ability. I don't want to be a legend, I want to be a messiah. And by the time Monday swings around, you will all find yourself bowing down to your Messiah AND New Heavyweight Champion, Chris Chase! ::Chris goes to do some sort of facial pose for the camera, but sees Corey returning from the corner of his eye. Chris straightens himself up as Corey enters the screen. The two's eyes meet by chance, and they lock. Chris and Corey enter a stare-down, each wondering whether or not they are truly better than the other. Corey backs the silence, as he remembers himself and his problem.:: Corey: You wouldn't happen to have any money, would you? ::Chris sighs.:: Chris: C'mon. ::Chris walks off the screen closely followed by Corey. Will the madness infect New Breed on Sunday? I guess you'll just have to wait and see. The scene faces out.:: !The End!
