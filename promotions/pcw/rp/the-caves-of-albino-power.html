The caves of Albino Power

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 March &#039;03 The scene opens up inside some hidden and dark caves. In the caves we see a group of individuals running around with weapons and yelling stuff at each other. Obviously this group in the caves is some sort of terrorist organization. One thing that stands out about this group is the color of their skin, it’s pure white. Also standing out is their white hair and their red eyes. As the group of about twenty to thirty run around yelling orders at each other, we see in the center of the cave an outsider to the group. In fact this outsider is none other then Chet King. And standing next to Chet King is Red Whit (A man, the leader of the Albino Power organization in fact, whom Chet King previously had a meeting with about an appearance on Chet’s talk show, however negotiations failed) Chet’s casual pair of denim jeans and a ‘The Chat King Wrestling Show’ t-shirt contrast him to the outfits of Red Whit and the other Albino Power members who are all wearing white shirts and red and white army style pants. Red Whit: Well, well, well. Chet King. Thanks for stopping by. Chet King: What did you want me for? I was busy preparing for the next episode of my show. I was tempted not to come here for this meeting. Red Whit: Well it’s good you didn’t say no to an Albino with a baseball bat, it could have been a very dangerous move on your behalf. Chet King: Yes, well I have to say I wasn’t too pleased about your crony bursting into the studio shouting things like ‘Albino Pride’. Red Whit: That’s too bad for you then. Now the reason I’ve bought you hear is that I want a favor from you. Chet King: And what would that be? Red Whit: I want you to advertise some Albino Power propaganda during the commercials of your show. Chet King: How much are you willing to pay for the commercials? Red and the others start to laugh. Red Whit: I don’t think you understand Chet, we’re not going to be paying you anything. Although if you put on these commercials for us, then we won’t hurt you. Chet King: I thought we made an agreement at our last meeting, where we decided you wouldn’t appear as a guest on my show if I gave you the name of an Albino hater. Red Whit: Oh speaking of that, thanks for giving me the name of that Gaz fellow. Me and the boys took care of him. Chet looks a bit worried considering all he told Red at the last meeting that a Gaz (in reference to Gaz Green who was Chet’s upcoming opponent) was insulting Albino’s, and this Albino Power group definitely beaten up Gaz Green lately. Red Whit: You looked a bit shocked, what’s the matter? Chet King: Oh, umm, don’t worry. You still haven’t answered my question. Red Whit: What was your question? Chet King: We made an agreement that you wouldn’t appear on my show if I gave you the name of an Albino hater. We made our transaction, so why are you going back on your word and wanting to appear on my show? Red Whit: Haven’t you listened to a word I’ve said? I don’t want to appear as a guest on your show, I just want you to advertise Albino Power propaganda on your show. Chet King: That’s basically the same thing, it all promotes something that I don’t want to have any part of. Red Whit: It’s not the same thing, they’re two completely different scenarios. You all agree with me, don’t you? The group surrounding Red and Chet all start nodding their heads like a bunch of yes-men. Red Whit: See? Two completely different scenarios. Chet King: Even if they are, I don’t want to have anything to do with either of them. Red and the group start laughing again. Red Whit: We don’t care if you don’t want anything to do with it, you have no choice in the matter Chet. As the fifteen or so men surrounding Chet give him some menacing looks, Chet sighs knowing that he’s beaten. Red Whit: What do you say Chet? Chet King: I guess I’ll air the commercials. Red Whit: For free? Chet sighs again. Chet King: Yes, for free. Red Whit: Fantastic, we’re going to be producing them this week. We’ve hired some fancy director and producer in LA to help us. Chet King: Sounds good. Red Whit: It shall be, I’m rather excited about what the final product will look like. As Red talks and gets as excited as a giddy school girl over the thoughts of the upcoming Albino Power commercials, an idea then flashes into Chet’s head. Chet King: Hey Red, how about since I’m doing a favor for you in airing these commercials for nothing, can you do me a favor? Red Whit: I am doing you a favor, I’m going to make sure my group doesn’t lay a finger on you. Chet King: Come on Red, I just want a small favor. It’d make your group really intimidating if you could pull it off. This causes a bit of excitement from Red and the group. Red Whit: What do you want us to do? Chet King: This week I’m fighting a guy named Two Ton, and I think it’d be great if you could take him out for me before Tuesday. Just beat him up, don’t hurt him too badly or anything. Just enough so I can get an easy win over him. Red rolls his eyes. Red Whit: You think that if our group takes out one guy we’ll get recognition. Chet King: Oh it’s not just an ordinary guy. Taking out Two Ton will be tougher then taking an army of a thousand normal humans. Red Whit: Are you serious? Is this guy a monster or something? Chet King: Well when he steps on scales at a truck weighing station, the scales break. Red and the group are now very interested. Red Whit: And you need us to take him out to beat him? Chet King: I don’t need you to, as I’ve beaten him before albeit DQ, but it would be nice to secure an easy win. Red Whit: And if our organization can take out this mammoth man we’ll gain recognition? Chet King: I guarantee it. You’d gain more then you will from the commercials I’m going to air on my show. Red and the nearest people around him have a quick discussions amongst themselves. Red Whit: Chet, we’ve decided to accept this mission. While we’re at it, is there anyone else you want us to meet that will gain us some recognition and push forward our cause of Albino dominance? Chet King: I would suggest meeting up with Jon Kano, but I don’t know if Kano will even turn up for our match on Friday, he’s apparently not happy with PCW at the moment, and with Syko in control I don’t blame him one single bit. Any action Kano decides to take, he has my support. Red doesn’t seem to really care. Red Whit: Yeah whatever Chet. Thanks for stopping by and thanks for doing business. We’ll get those commercials sent to you before the end of the week and we’ll go on our search for this mammoth figure known as Two Ton. Chet King: Thanks Red. I guess I’m going to have to be blindfolded and taken in some truck to get away from here, just like I was when I was told to come here. Red Whit: It’s nothing personal Chet, we just don’t like people knowing where the Albino Power caves are. Chet King: I understand. We leave the scene as Chet is blindfolded by one of the Albino’s and lead out of the cave to a waiting truck.
