I have to defend my gold?

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: I have to defend my gold? Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 7/16/02 -Duke is at a San Francisco clothing store buying some new cloths for the Wrestle Vessel prepared to set sail in a few weeks. He is trying on Hawaiian shirts. His cowboy hat is replaced by a captains hat.- Williams: Clyde I have your hero right here hoss. -Duke grabs his crotch for the camera. Some men in the back ground pay attention as it is San Francisco.- Williams: Darkstar you’re a fucking ball sack man. -Duke checks out the unbuttoned shirt in the mirror. He sucks in his guy a bit.- Williams: Who’s this Goblin kid? Where did they drag this drag queen from? Looks like a starving Ethiopian with mascara on. That don’t impress me much. -Rantul, Duke fashion consultant brings in a captains jacket.- Rantul: Mr. Duke here is the perfect jacket for that cute hat. -Duke looks dead pan in the camera as he tries on the captains jacket.- Williams: This is the hell that is my life. -Duke looks like a fish boat captain.- Rantul: Adorable! Williams: I look like a goddamn fairy. I’m going for the Jimmy Buffet look not the little Richard -Duke takes the jacket off as Rantul exits to the back looking for something else.- Williams: What a predicament I’m in. -Duke rubs his thumb against his nose.- Williams: I have to step in the ring with five freaks I don’t like. Two of which I have to work with. -Duke takes off the goofy hat.- Williams: Only this time I can’t walk away and get the bats. I can’t set up the dark dorks to smash their heads in. Why? Because my Continental title is on the line. -Duke itches his crotch- Williams: I have to baby sit Tommy because he is caring for my gold. Now you know, I know, they know, this guy is a little off his rocker. -Duke makes circles with his pointer finger by his ear.- Williams: The guy is f*cking fruit loops. Can you blame him living in this hellhole. That is not my problem. My problem is this whack job can lose my title. He can hand over the only piece of gold worth a damn in PCW to some nose ring punk. Some kid who hasn’t had a shower since Lewinsky was blowing the president. -A black t-shirt catches the Malice Man’s eyes.- Williams: So I’m stuck, once again, bailing Tommy out of the deep end. And can somebody tell me who the f*ck Chris Chase is? Who is Chris Chase? -Williams turns to a male couple shopping. The turn away and quickly walk away.- Williams: See they don’t even know words let alone this hump Chris Chase. Who booked this piece of sh*t? Nelsons fingerprints are all over this one. -Duke sighs in disgust.- Williams: Dark Dumps listen up. None of you high school drop outs will EVER capture gold in the PCW. Your losers. Come on look at you!! You kids couldn’t keep your jobs the first time what’s different this time. -Duke shakes his head as Rantul brings out a pink shirt. He immediately turns around and heads back.- Williams: Darkstar you said I was washed up? You said I was the reason the company failed. You my friend have got a lot of guts to say that. You still ain’t got brass ball but you got guts. Listen up hoss while I lay down the brass tacks. -Duke grabs a flask out of his jean pockets and sips down some love.- Williams: Dark jobbers, Nelson not giving a damn, dark jobbers, Ty Nelson, washed up dark gimmicks, and Ty, are the reasons this company sunk. Was I the captain on deck? You’re damn right. I saved the ship but lost a lot in doing so. I could have ran to OSW but I stuck it out. Too bad you got fired. Too bad you sucked worse then the worse. Anna Keyston was on the roster before you. -Duke laughs at that tidbit.- Williams: I built Jax and the ICWA. I was the star that brought his company to the top. I left it sink. I came here to PCW and helped run him into the ground. You my friend don’t have that power. You my friend don’t have the fans. You my friend don’t have much except that little lady. I will snatch her and play with her snatch…..Malice Style. -Duke gyrates his hips then gets uncomfortable with the homo’s looking at him.- Williams: Cylde, you burnt piece of dog vomit. I’m going to kick you so hard in your shriveled up, over cooked, balls, that they will fly through your body and replace your eyes in that socket. Oh the pain. Your about as useful here as you were fired. -Duke winks at a young blonde chick walking by.- Williams: I like my whiskey on ice and my women on fire. You on the other hand like everything on fire. To bad the same can’t be said of your barley luke warm career. I think the Goblin kid has more talent then you do. -Duke puts on the black shirt, and Stetson hat he came in with.- Williams: You just don’t have it anymore. Your shock appeal…excuse the pun…is gone. Your ability to go that much lower and sicker then everyone else has been matched. Your heat is in the kitchen and out of the ring. When you first came to PCW a man sleeping with a kid meant something. My how times have changed. Now half of the US priests sleep with kids. The shock is gone killer. Is Johnny Cash the best you can do? The man is already dead. Not very shocking. -Duke gives Rantul the finger as he exits the shop.- Williams: Was it me or was Rantul a little light in the loafers? -Duke tries to hail a cab.- Williams: Go home Clyde. Hang it up. It’s where you truly belong. You kicked the hell out of me. Took over my show. Insulted me and tore me apart. In other words you had your fun. Unfortunately you didn’t kill me. I’m still here and the show is still going on. Your fading my man, like a fart in the wind. Hoss you didn’t stink that bad to begin with. -Duke can’t get a taxi so he takes off his shirt in the hot sun. All of a sudden three cabs line up to pick him up.- Williams: What a strange city. So anywho this Tuesday I will defend my Continental title by sending the Dark Dorks Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. -Duke gets in the cab and it drives away.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
