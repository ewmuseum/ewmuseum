Girl Talk [C] - The Sister

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March whatever Sayaka and Mina watched as Sonata walked back into the room and sat down, but her clothes were different than before. Instead of the black and yellow outfit she wore, she now wore black leather pants, with a yellow mesh halter-top with a black tank top underneath. Without saying a word, she reached over and poured herself a cup of tea. Mina gave Sayaka a look before looking to her friend. “Um, who was at the door, Sonata?” “Yeah, and what’s with the fashion change?” Sayaka chimed in. Sonata looked up at them, her dark eyes narrowing, and her yellow painted lips pursed together. Then completely ignoring the two of them, she raised the cup to her lips and sipped the tea. The two women just stared as Sonata lowered the cup and glared back up at them. “She has poor choice in associates.” “What?” Sayaka lowered her cigarette, the fifth thus far, and wrinkled her brow across the table at Sonata. “What are you saying there, kiddo?” “Spare me your euphemisms.” Sonata tossed her black and yellow hair from out of her face and stretched out, leaning back on her elbows in a relaxed fashion. Mina inched closer to Sayaka, trying to keep her voice low. “Something’s going on here…” she murmured, barely audible outside of their proximity. “I’ve never seen Sonata like this before.” “And you never will see her like this.” Sonata interjected, yawning while looking up at the ceiling. Mina and Sayaka watched her closely, worried for their normally calm and collected friend. Just then…Sonata walked into the room. “What are you doing in my seat?” she said, hands on hips. Mina and Sayaka looked on with mouths agape as the other Sonata shrugged her shoulders at the standing Sonata. “Didn’t see your name on this here seat,” she stood up, however, and brushed her hands off on the back of her leather pants. “But I know if you don’t get it you’ll start whining about it…like you used to do when you were small.” “I did not WHINE!” her voice got pitched as she glared at her pants wearing self. “Whine, cry, complain, bitch…take your pick of the adjectives.” Skirt wearing Sonata stuck her tongue out at pants wearing Sonata, her finger pressed against her lower eyelid and pulling down. Mina and Sayaka just sat there and watched the double vision unfold. Pants wearing Sonata noticed their bewilderment though. “Aren’t you going to introduce me? How rude are you?” Skirt wearing Sonata realized they were in the room, and turned towards them with a sheepish grin. “Right! So sorry, I forgot all about you two. This is Sayaka Mitsui, my best friend, and Mina de Margo, my neighbor. Guys, this is my sister…Symphony.” “Symphony?” Mina and Sayaka blurted that one out simultaneously. Symphony bowed arrogantly, one arm in front and one behind her back. “Yours truly.” She looked up with a smug smile at the two of them. “I know you’re charmed.” “You’re twins?” Mina asked surprised. Symphony nodded, giving a taunting round of applause for her. “Kid wins a prize!” Sonata sighed, sitting back in her seat as her twin sister moved across to the other side of the table. “Yeah, we’re twins. Not a lot of people notice it for some reason…or know about us.” “More like know about me,” Symphony stated, reaching over and grabbing a cookie off the tray. Sayaka watched as she broke it in half and stuck a piece in her mouth, enjoying the texture. “Sonata, in the years that I’ve known you you’ve never told me you had a twin.” Symphony swallowed her cookie. “You didn’t tell her about being married to Force for a while either…but hey, all secrets come out from the dark eventually.” Sayaka whirled her eyes towards her friend’s twin in caution. “What? How’d you know about that?” then she snapped towards Sonata. “Did you tell her about that?” Sonata sighed, lowering her head and slumping her shoulders. “No…no I didn’t.” “It’s all up here, “kiddo”.” Symphony tapped her temple, a yellow-lipped smile beaming on her face. Mina nodded slowly, beginning to understand what was going on. “Psychic,” she muttered in awe. “That’s right, kid. Psychic. And you’ll never find another as great as me!” Sonata shook her head, a knowing smile on her face. “You have yet to meet Cyclonica.” “PFFT!” her twin waved her hand at the mention of that flaw. “She ain’t got nothing on me. Plus she ain’t certified by the Muse Council! She’s just some slut running around playing mind games with people.” “And you are?” Sayaka asked. Symphony sat up, leaning her elbows against the edge of the table. “I…am a master of the mind. A neural artist that weaves telepathic notes and creates a beautiful psychic…symphony…” “Hence the name, huh?” Sonata shook her head at Mina. “Actually, we’re named for our family’s tradition of classical music. We both play a number of instruments quite well.” “Though I’m quite out of practice lately, from my work,” Symphony added drolly. “Though I can’t speak for my dear sister though.” Mina nodded, paying attention to the conversation. “What is your work?” Symphony shrugged with a perceptive beam. “Oh…look into my eyes and tell me what I do.” Mina and Symphony leaned across the table and locked eyes. Mina watched for a second, seeing first yellowy amber eyes…but then seconds later the color began to fade, a darkness swirling behind irises like brewing storm clouds. Symphony’s smile didn’t seem as bright as her sister’s now… “I’m sure you can see now that the context of my work is quite null … and ---.” “Void.” All heads turned towards the doorway, where the Neo Void God stood glaring at them all. Dressed in a black turtleneck and blue jeans, he leaned against the open door frame with his arms folded across his wide chest. Though there were four women in there, only one of them had his attention. “Akiyama!” Symphony said with a smile, standing slowly to her feet. It was wise to move slowly around him, for one wrong move would result in the monster ravaging wildly. She raised her arms to her side in a friendly gesture. “It’s been a while since we’ve had the pleasure of conversing!” “You have some explaining to do, Sonata…” he didn’t even hear her words, eyes locked on one twin, but words locked on another. “Letting a Void Element into our home…unforgivable terms, and we feel that given your previous actions we’ve been FAR too forgivable lately.” Sonata stood up and bowed deeply, her hands crossed in front of her lap. “I’m sorry, but I didn’t think you would mind so much! She is my sister after all!” “She’s a VOID ELEMENT.” Akiyama began to walk towards her, arms still folded, but that didn’t mean he couldn’t and wouldn’t strike without warning. “And her being a psychic isn’t making us like her any f-ckin much either.” Mina and Sayaka stood up from the table, watching slowly as Symphony backed against the wall. Sayaka reached slowly behind her back, but the sudden halt of the god did the same for her. “We wouldn’t pull that sh-t if we were you.” Still, eyes piercing into the psychic Symphony. Sayaka withdrew her hand quickly, astonished he knew her intentions. “Come on now, Aki. Don’t tell me you’re going to hold the entire Psychic race accountable for your own f-ck-up rogue daughter, now are you?” He lowered his arms. That was answer enough. Sonata dashed across the room, putting herself between her sister and the incoming god. “Akiyama! Please! She has no intention of harming you! She is only my guest!” “She’s set to be a guest in f-ckin the emergency room in a sec.” No one liked the way he cracked his knuckles simply by clenching his fists. That was like a death chime, the last sound you hear before destruction. “Listen to them!” Mina pleaded with him. “It’s true!” Aki had nothing to say to a Blitz supporter. As a matter of fact, he scorned her words completely by scooping the twins up in the air by their throats. Akiyama stared into matching faces twisted in discomfort as he clutched tighter around their windpipes. Sayaka ignored the god’s early warning and drew her magnum, aiming it at his skull. “Drop ‘em NOW!!” “Or what, you’ll shoot us?” he turned towards her with dark eyes, fearing not the pellet of her pistol. “We’d be out of the way before you’d pull the f-cking trigger, ya dumbass. And then you’d hit your best friend…wouldn’t want to do that if you love her so damn much!” “You don’t know what you’re doing! Let them go now!” Mina asked him again, with more force this time. A chuckle came out from the gravelly god’s voice. “We can’t believe this sh-t. First you conspire against us with the Japanese government, then you bring in an assassin, an ally of Inphino Sh-tz, and a Void Element into our home?? What’s next, Sonata?? Blitz himself?? Xavier Kannon?? WELL??” “AKI!!” Symphony gargled through clutched neck. “You’ll regret putting your hands on me!!” “Please…the remorse is too much to bear.” And so he gripped… “You’ll regret killing the one person that can create unity out of that chaos you call a mind!!” Well, well…the god could be bargained with after all. The twins dropped to the ground, both of them reaching for their throats gasping for invisible life like for the first time ever. With a yank on their collars, Aki raised them to their feet, looming over Symphony with attentiveness on his annoyed face. “We’re listening…” -=-=-ToBeContinued -=-=-
