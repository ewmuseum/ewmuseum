The Hero Saga: Skyline...

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 24, 2002 The skyline of Philadelphia... It is night time in the fair metropolis. The children are placed in their beds, sleeping until the morning greets them.. the parents are watching their late night TV, waiting until something tickles them the right way. Everyone is calm and tired. Everywhere is quiet and lonely. Except here... For the nights of Philadelphia are not as calm as they would lead on. The minute the sun dissapears below the river and carries its life giving light down to the other side of the world with it, the minute darkness gives its side of the story to the few people up to see it, thats when it begins... Thats when the world needs saving... Somewhere there is a child who needs saving... Somewhere there is evil that needs thwarting... Somewhere there is a bunch of stuff that needs something... And who will do these things? The camera pans up the side of one the tall buildings, dramticly speeding up all of the floors till it reaches the crest. All you can see is the gravelly top of the roof, the spire raising high into the air above it, a ruffling can be heard as the music reaches a crechendo. Something runs in front of the camera, spinning around and clinging to the tip of the roof. AND THEY SAY THAT A HEEEEEEROOOOO WILL SAVE US!!!!! GOTH-MAN! The shadows cape flies behind him and, for no explainable reason, a lightning bolt illuminates the dark night sky, the only thing visable of the caped crusader is his black mask... And his blue hair. TO BE CONTINUED!
