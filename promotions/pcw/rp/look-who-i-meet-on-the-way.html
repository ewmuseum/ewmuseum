Look who I meet on the way......

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: You&#039;re living in the past man, quit living in the past! ::As the scene opens up we see a limo driving along an empty road. Farms to the left and right of it, and road all the way in front of it. The limo passes a big green sign, the camera stops following the limo and instead zooms in on the sign, there we see the words 'Melbourne 5.' Our cameras fade into the interior of the limo, where we see Corey Williams much in the same way we saw him last time. Wearing black leather pants and a plain black T-shirt reading 'Yo Momma' on it.:: Chauffeur (heavy Aussie accent): .....Bloody sh*thouse weather we're having this summer, eh? ::Corey's attention is reluctantly drawn to the chauffeur, who he has barely noticed until the man decided to stink up this promo with his voice.:: Corey: You should see my country, we're just finishing up with Winter. Chauffer: What are you, a Yank or something? ::Corey laughs at the thought of the question.:: Corey: A country? Why would someone of my stature need a country? There are no limits to my abilities, so why would I limit myself to just ONE country? I speak the universal tongue, the language understood by everyone, the language of controversy. After all, it IS what the people wanna see. ::The chauffeur turns around to face Corey, whilst still driving, and squints at him for about 5 seconds.:: Chauffer: Yep, just what I thought, a bloody Yank. (under breathe) And from the sound of things you've probably been YANKing your chain a little too much. Corey: What!? Did you not hear me!? I said that I am universal, I don't make one country my entire life, only uncreative losers would do something like that. Guys like Miyagawa, The Chef or El Diablo. These guys have got nothing else going for them so they spend their lives getting cheap pops from their countrymen.... pathetic. You won't find New Breed doing that. And which most envied stable contains the World Champ? Why I believe its New Breed! Chauffeur: Uh hunh.... that's great. So what brings you to the land of the lucky people? Corey: Why, luck itself. But not the luck of your country, the luck given to me by myself. Fortune favours the best, remember that my friend and maybe you'll be able to stay out of prison for a day or two.... convict. Anyway, I just happened to come across a large sum of money during my last stay in your country. ::Corey pats his pocket, contently feeling his full wallet, but suddenly he notices it's not there.:: Corey: Hey! Did you steal my wallet convict!? Chauffeur: What!? Of course not! ::Corey feels his OTHER pocket, a noticeable bulge there, and it sure as HEEL ain't because he's happy to be here.:: Corey: Oh never mind, wrong pocket. Although you can never be too careful around you guys.... Chauffeur: And what do you mean by 'us guys'!? Corey: Don't lie to me! I know your history....... convict. ::The chauffeur rolls his eyes.:: Chauffeur: Mate, you're an idiot. Corey: So what are you gonna do? MURDER me? Chauffeur: Maybe, if you don't shut up. Corey: For the love of Chase, there's no reason to take that tone with me, maybe they should send you back to the prison cell you came from! ::The Chauffer turns around, looking mighty peeved, not even paying attention to the road.:: Chauffeur: Oh yeh!? Well maybe you should go F..... ::And we never get to hear the end of that sentence, because suddenly there is a screeching of brakes and then and SLAM! We hear the strange sound of metal against metal, of glass breaking, of your momma moaning. We feel the aftermath, coming like a whiplash as the bodies in the cars struggle catch up to it. And then we see the carnage..... ok maybe that's a little over dramatic. In fact it's just a small bump at the front of the other cars limo. The men from both parties get out, not the chauffeurs but the actual important men, getting out from both the limos involved in this crash. The camera follows Corey as he walks to meet the other man, as they meet halfway between both vehicles, the camera scrolls up to find out who the other limo's owner is. It's one of those moments that you just know will affect the outcome of the promo. Will Corey be bumping into Boda? Or possibly Afwa Kilmanjaro? Could it be the President of the United States? Or the Prime Minister of Australia? .......Or perhaps it's just an ordinary everyday Australian, an old man in fact, and he doesn't look too happy.:: Old Man: Excuse me? What the hell did you just do to my car? Do you know who I am? Corey: No, but I'm sure you know who I am, and I'm also sure this is the greatest moment of your life. ::The old man squints at Corey, trying to figure out who he is, suddenly his eyes grow wide.:: Old Man: Hey wait a minute! Well, I never..... Y-you're PCW superstar Corey Williams! ::Corey puts his hands on his hips and smiles contently.:: Corey: Why of course I am. Old Man: I'm a huge fan of your work, the names Peter Hollingworth.... ::The Old Man looks at Corey, expecting a reaction, he doesn't get one.:: Corey: Pleased to meet you Pete. ::The Old Man is confused, he tries again.:: Peter: Y'know, former Archbishop and current Governor-General of Australia, Peter Hollingworth. Corey: Pray tell, who or what is a Governor-General? Peter: Well it's the best job in the world.... Corey: You mean SECOND best, after all, I AM a New Breed member. Peter: Of course, but Governor-General DOES come close, I get payed to do nothing but sign forms and listen to the Queen crap on and on all day. Corey: The Queen, now that does sound interesting. There's something I always wanted to know about the Queen.... Peter: Well then ask away.... Corey: Does she put out? ::Corey grins mischievously, knowing that he has just been controversial. Unfortunately he does not get the expected reaction from Peter Hollingworth, who is grinning back slyly.:: Corey: You're joking! She doesn't, does she? Peter: Hey, this is the Royal Family we're talking about, they're so dysfunctional they make the Macmahons look like that family from the TV show Seventh Heaven! ::Corey nods, I guess you learn something new everyday.:: Corey: Well that's great, but I still don't know who you are. ::The Governor-General sighs, giving it one last try.:: Peter: Look, I'm that guy who sleeps with little children. Corey: Ohhhh, now I remember! It was in all the newspapers, in fact, I'd have to say it was rather.... CONTROVERSIAL. Peter: Look, I'M NOT a child molester, it's just that the press has let this story get out of hand. ::Corey looks confused.:: Corey: So? What's you're problem? Peter: Well.... now everyone hates me! Corey: You're making that sound like a bad thing! Peter: Well it is, the public don't support me anymore. Corey: THE PUBLIC? THE PUBLIC! You're the frikken Governor-General, you don't need 'the public.' You're superior to the public in everyway, they have no control over you. Peter: Hey.... you're right! Corey: Of course I am! Besides, deep down, 'the public' may hate you, but they really just admire you, they are just envious of you, because they know, deep down, that they NEED you! Peter: Yeh! Corey: They need someone to hate, and it takes the VERY BEST to be hated. You think it's easy to be hated? No, of course not! You, my friend, are gifted! You have a talent, and you're laying it all on the line FOR the people! And what gratitude do they show you? NONE! They should be thanking you! But instead they are cheering guys like Afwa Kilmanjaro, what did he ever do? Nothing! Peter: Afwa Kilmanjaro? ::Peter is puzzled but shakes his head and ignores the comment.:: Peter: Look, thanks a lot Corey. You've really helped me! ::Talking more about himself than the Governor-General, Peter's voice suddenly reminds Corey that there is a world outside of him.:: Corey: Hunh? Oh you.... yeh, great, you can go now, I'm done with you. ::Peter turns to walk off, but his wallet 'accidentally' falls out of his pocket and onto the ground. the Governor-General tries to look surprised.:: Peter (in a really bad acting voice): Oh, my wallet. Do you mind picking that up for me Corey? I have a really bad back. ::Corey looks Peter up and down, a disgustED look on his face, he stares at the Governor-General's fat ugly head, THIS man has a disgustING look on his face. Corey shakes his head.:: Corey: You make me sick.... pervert! ::And with that Corey kicks up his heels and turns to walk back to his limo. He gets in and the limo speeds off into the sunset. The scene fades out.:: !The End!
