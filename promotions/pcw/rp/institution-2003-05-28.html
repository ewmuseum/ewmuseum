Date: 05 - 28 - 03 We rejoin our bad ass and protector of all that happens to be good in PCW, Dante Jackson, as he raises his head upward towards the heavens. Thunder cackles behind him however, as rain begins to set upon the shallow outskirts of Kuwait City, drenching our hero in soaked rain water and the stench of sewage dew. He takes a few steps forward, looking around him at the surroundings currently holding him back into confinement as it seems that a gate circling him in a square shaped box has entrapped our hero with the essential barbed wire and electric spikes protruding out of the small circular holes. As the rain continues to tread downward upon Dante, the scene quickly shifts inward within the military position holding Dante as men in white sparkly lab coats pace around the perimeter, carefully analyzing each and every mechanism inside of the plant. Lab Technician: “Dave!” One lab coat cries out, with a scruffy beard and an oily face. Surrounding him in glass participants happen to be rooms filled with posters, wrestling paraphernalia, and other technical men & women just like him whom happen to work and slave here in this down trodden facility. Lab Technician: “You’ve forgotten to cut off the generator!” Dave: “Shawn, you keep telling them that and we’ll get found out!” With a breath, Shawn, the original lab technician claiming foul walks off, shrugs his shoulders as he folds his fingers inward his coat pocket area. The same however cannot be said for Dave as he steps through a rotating door as another lab coat goes over paperwork stacked upwards of three to four inches in length. Another lab coat, this time with a yellow hard hat and safety goggles strapped to his face continues to check things off as Dave takes his steps into the room. Dave: “Peter!” Peter: (groaning & whiny voice) “Aw Dave! I was in the middle of something!” Dave: “Grading papers?” Peter: (sigh) “It’s my kid, she keeps screwing up in that Trapezoid’s class back in the States. I wish I were there helping her out though. It sucks around here without any chaos and evil dictators around.” Dave: “Hey, somebody had to clean up around here. And why be back in the States anyway? We get enough payment just controlling the minds of Iraqi and Kuwaiti wrestling fans here at the Kuwait Institute of Wrestling Sciences.” Peter nods, chuckling at the prospect of someday moving the Institute outside of Kuwait and inside the United States of America. Until then however, his dreams are shot kaput as Dave shakes him out of his lucrative dream of ownership. Dave: “Will you come on? The presentation starts in four minutes!” Peter: “Oh by the gods, do we have too?” Dave: “If you want to keep your job and still make millions of Iraqi money and not have those U.S pencil pushers on your back then you’d better go.” Peter gets up, twitching his bushy mustache as he follows behind Dave towards the main hall where a few hundreds of screaming school children anxiously awaits the men to begin their presentation. As most of the boys and girls of Kuwait’s Third School of The Narcoleptic wait as their teacher or instructor gives them directions, hanging overhead the crowd happens to be a large white background banner stretching out towards both ends of the walls reading: “WELCOME TO THE PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING EXPO!” Moving towards the outside, the silhouette of Dante stands, pounding at the walls of the facility, as he screams and cries out at the top of his lungs – patient and yet all the more desperate in finding something to get inside. VROOOOM! Turning his back towards the gates however, Dante watches over his shoulder as a Jeep SUV roars through the wind and crashes through the gates, sending sparks of electric currents everywhere! Dante: “Jesus!” He dodges out of the way of the oncoming vehicle as it finally makes a stop plowing through the wall of the facility. As the engine dies down, a body slumps outward, pushing aside the car’s driver side door as the body slouches over towards the muddy banks, collapsing inward. Dante: “By the GODS!” Our hero looks inside at the small factory for wrestling paraphernalia before his eyes begin to bug out in an odd, yet incessant tone as Dave catches view of the Messiah, dragging him back towards the tour. Dave: “Dude, did you get lost or something?” Dante: “Unhand me!” Dave: “But you’ll miss the tour!” Dante: (louder) “I said unhand me!” Dave: (shouting) “But you’ll MISS THE FRICKIN’ TOUR!” Dante: (shallow voiced) “What tour?” Dave: “The Kuwaiti Institute of Wrestling Sciences Tour. Its in the main lounge.” Dante looks at the nerdy Dave before boasting to himself. Dante: “If I go, do you promise me to get me food and shelter?” Dave: “Uh, like sure!” A smile crosses the face of our big and bold hero as he takes a few steps forward to shake Dave's hand. Gripping tightly, Dave can only grimace in pain as the Messiah releases his grip. Dante: “HI-KEEBA!” Dave: “Oh, you want to go to our Big Alan Steele Center?” Dante ponders for a moment. Dante: “I’d rather not…”
