The Grappling Gourmet: Part #1

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/5/02 The camera fades in… We see a medium-sized studio audience clapping like a bunch of mindless zombies as some hackneyed talk show easy-listening music plays in the background. The camera pans around and catches the various stereotypical audience members. There’s grandma, the cowboy, the Islamic terrorist, the rabbi, the priest, the Satanist, the other Satanist, yet another Satanist, the Wall St. tycoon, grandpa, the WWII veteran, the Vietnam veteran, the crazed pregnant woman, a young reckless boy, a pot-belly pig, the computer nerd, and the all important loony derelict homeless man holding up a sign that states, “For Hot Sex call Scott Naket at [detail withheld], beware males, he’s a talker.” The announcer’s voice brims with excitement as he gets ready to start the show. The camera then pans over to the set, which is designed in the style of 18th century French architecture. Off course kitchens didn’t look so good back then with the rat infestation, numerous fires, and lack of available places to take a piss; so the designers threw a bunch of expensive crud (multi-colored tile floors, pearl white counter tops, Delta faucets, a toaster) into the set to bring it into the 21st century. Announcer: Hello folks and welcome to the first episode of the brand new Food Network cooking series…”The Grappling Gourmet”!!!…and here’s your host….THHHHHEEEE CHEEEEEEEEEFFFFFFFFFF!!! Instead of an applause button flashing, little men dressed in devil suits run out behind the stand and poke the audience members in the bum with plastic pitchforks, screaming at them to applaud…they do. The Chef strolls out onto the set in his normal white suit, white hat, “Kiss me I’m not Irish” apron, and his trusty wooden rolling pin, Ol’ Woody. The Chef: Thank you for the warm applause! And thank you little devil men for extracting such a wonderful ovation! The devil men scurry out to the backstage area, leaving many red swollen Asses behind them. The Chef: Welcome to “The Grappling Gourmet”, my new variety cooking show! YAAAAYYYYY!!! No reaction from the crowd. The Chef: Listen folks, am I going to have to bring the little devil guys out again? An immediate applause is heard. The Chef: Excellent! Now that we’ve all settled down, let’s begin this cooking show with an irrelevant music interlude; take it away Xena Scorpio!!! A pasty-looking female with body rings attached to every orifice imaginable starts to strum a giant golden harp, playing some of the most craptastic melodies imaginable. The Chef grits his teeth with disgust, but let’s the dame play because she’s unfortunately got a record deal with the parent company. The Chef: Thank you Xena! Remember to pick up her new album, “When the Fig leaves the Newton” at store everywhere! Um… The Chef walks over and talks with the cue card guy, probably wondering if “store” was a misprint. The Chef: Wow, I see, it’s actually sold at…um…one store…way to go! The Chef smiles at Xena, who spits at his general direction. The Chef shrugs off the harsh female contact and turns to address his crowd of Culinary School Dropouts! The Chef: For our inaugural show, I am going to prepare for you a very tasty treat! Do you folks like tasty treats!?!? The camera quickly pans to the pedophile in the audience that nods his head approvingly. The Chef grabs a microphone and heads up into the audience and start for an imminent disaster. He walks over to the pedophile. The Chef: What’s you favorite treat, sir? Pedophile: Really any type of candy, Chef… The Chef: And why’s that? Pedophile: It’s good bait for kid lovers like me… The Chef: Oh really!?!? How many have you had? Pedophile: About 68… The Chef: Wow!!! Your wife needs to get a medal or something! Pedophile: Wife? The Chef: Oh, you adopt…how thoughtful! Pedophile: Adopt? The Chef: Um…I’ll talk to someone else now… The Chef walks away from the pedophile and onto the priest. The Chef: Hmmm, I won’t go there… The priest looks on indignantly as the Chef moves onto the old women in the audience. The Chef: Hello ma’am, what’s your name? Woman: Name’s Dixie Stevens, and I’m 92 years young! The Chef: Goodness! What keeps you going? Woman: The three “aps”! The Chef: What? Three “aps”? Woman: Yeah! NAPS, CRAPS, AND CHAPS! The Chef shakes his head The Chef: Well that explains the foul stench, and why you are feeling my testicles…um…PLEASE STOP!!! The woman lets go. The Chef: Well I guess no one else wants to share their favorite treat with us!?!? A black man dressed in gangsta attire raises his hand. The Chef: Wait, this show isn’t dead yet! The Chef scurries over and sticks the microphone near the black man. Black Man: Yeah, gave I have grandma’s phone number? She’s off da hook, know what I’m sayin’!?!? The audience applauds and laughs without the help of small men carrying pitchforks. The Chef slaps himself in the forehead, overcome with displeasure. The Chef: I think I need a cold shower… Heck, I’ll go “polar bearing”… The Chef walks back down to the kitchen studio and turns towards his politically incorrect audience. The Chef: Well, today’s tasty treat is going to be a delicacy, a scrumptious piece of culinary heaven. So divine, you’d swear you were eating out an angel…or a demon for you Satanists in the crowd. A crowd member with a pentacle star tattooed on his forehead nods at the Chef's remark. The Chef: Well, without any further adieu…let’s make some a Triple Layer Mocha-Almond Torte with a sweet raspberry sauce and ground cinnamon, served with a side of hot chocolate syrup!!! Everyone in the crowd begins licking their lips, anticipating the imminent quest of the cake from the oven to their bellies. The Chef: But unfortunately…some culinary school drop out has forgotten to pick up some espresso at the supermarket!!! The crowd gasps as the Chef holds up an empty contained of espresso-coffee power… The Chef: Rather than belittle the staff, or propose conspiracy theories, let’s try making another dessert… Crowd: MO-CHA! MO-CHA! MO-CHA! The Chef: I’m sorry! I don’t have the ingredients!!! Satanist: Hey guys, I think that snooty French chef just wants the coffee all for himself!!! The Chef: Oh really!?!? Well why don’t you channel one of your demonic buddies to put a curse on me!?!? Satanist: Maybe I will!!! The Chef: A beginner at Dungeons and Dragons would have a better chance at creating a monster that you, you pansy ass devil worshipper!!! Satanist: We don’t actually worship the devil, we view that humanity makes up the divine presence that is God… The Chef: Really? Quite interesting… Satanist: Yeah, you should really read one of our pamphlets… The Chef nods, and then remembers why he was yelling in the first place. The Chef: Hey wait a second, didn’t you just threaten me!?!? Satanist: Yeah, but I thought we made up… The Chef: I don’t think we did, and if there’ anyone else in the audience that wants this ¼ cup of espresso, be my guest!!! The Chef whips out his wooden rolling pin and sets the container behind him. The Chef: Who wants a go!?!? Announcer: Just a reminder to our audience: each and every one of you has been given a replica of the Chef’s rolling pin as a free gift for joining us today! The Chef suddenly freaks out. The Chef: Leaping linguine!!! Every single member in the crowd looks under their seats for the rolling pin. Announcer: It’s in your gift bag on the side of your seat! The Chef: Shut up Charles!!! Announcer: Sorry Chef, just doing my job… The Chef turns and sees rows upon rows up angry food show patrons, ready to become vigilantes fighting for truth, justice, and mocha cake… “F*CKING WATCH OUT MOTHA F*CKAAAASSS!!!” Suddenly the backstage door bursts open, and Bertha Stewart, clad in a skin tight black leather dominatrix outfit stands in the doorway, holding a cattle prod, a pack of pork rings, another pack of pork rinds, and a Nerf Bow and Arrow set. Bertha: I’m here to f*cking save you f*ckface!!! As the crowd advances on the Chef, Bertha begins her mission. She fights through the endless rows of people, tossing them aside, stampeding over them, zapping them with the cattle prod Mountie-style, and shooting Poisoned-tipped Nerf Arrows at them. Actually they aren’t poison-tipped, she just like to stick them up her ass… The Chef: What is that stench… Isn’t dramatic irony great!?!? The Chef: Bertha, I can’t hold out much longer, the bone fragments are starting to accumulate on my rolling pin; it’s too heavy to swing!!! Bertha heeds the Chef’s urgent call for help and fights through the crowd with an even greater ferocity. Announcer: Will the Chef and Bertha survive this attack by crazed and hungry crowd members!?!? Will the Chef convert to Satanism a live the hedonistic lifestyle!?!? Is Burger King a good place to go on a first date like the commercial says!?!? We’ll find out…SAME CHEF TIME…SAME CHEF CHANNEL!!! NaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNaNa…THE CHEF!!! The camera fades to black as a starving audience swarms around the Chef…
