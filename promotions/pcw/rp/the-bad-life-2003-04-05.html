The Bad Life

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/03 The scene opens up to a giant snow globe. Inside surrounded by glittery white flakes, is a house similar to that of Brianna Washington's. Actually it's more than similar, it's exactly alike! It has the same flat roof, patched up window on the second floor, and front porch with two chairs on the right. A hand reaches out and lifts the globe up and shakes it violently, setting it back down on its stand carefully. Now those glittery white flakes are floating around the house at a fast pace, creating a serene feeling. The good times…. The fake snow continues to float around for another minute keeping the peaceful feel. Finally all the snow has fallen and it just sits at the bottom. Very slowly the area inside the snow globe starts to turn black, until that is all we see. The bad times…. The peaceful feel has left being replaced by anger, and worry. The snow globe starts to shake violently on it’s own before blowing up, shattering into tiny pieces. In the end it’s just the bad times that seem to stick with you…. This image slowly fades out, and opens to a different scene. It opens up to Brianna sitting on a park bench in a dark red shirt and black baggy pants, surrounded by a muddy ground and a dark sky warning that more rain is near. It’s apparent that she doesn’t care about the warning signs emitted from the sky, as she just sits there staring straight ahead with a frown etched across her face. The camera stays on her eyes, which don’t flicker even for a second as she just continues to stare. “Brianna!!” an elated voice yells as he rushes over to her. Running towards Brianna is Milo who has on a purple silk shirt and black jeans that are only slightly loose fitting. The smile on his face as he runs to Brianna slowly fades away as he gets right next to her noticing her glassy stare. “Brianna, I have been looking all over the place for you. What are you doing out here?” he asks very concerned as he takes a seat on the damp bench beside her not caring that the seat of his pants now have a huge wet spot on them. There is silence for several seconds as Milo looks at Brianna trying to figure out why she isn’t responding to him. “Brianna I’m talking to you! You had me very worried girl. I missed my yoga class just to come out and find you, and you know how much I love my yoga.” he laughs a bit, but stops realizing that she isn’t reacting to what he said. A big flash of lightening shoots through the sky as light rain starts to fall from the sky. A big boom of thunder follows shortly after which causes Milo to jump of the bench with a big jolt. “Brianna come on! This is a big storm, and I am sure you don’t want to get yourself electrocuted.” “Please just leave me be.” she says, which is barely noticeable as her lips only move slightly. “I’m not leaving you out here, now get your ass up and come home!” he goes to pull her up but she just pushes him away, not budging. “Milo, I am serious, leave me alone! I’m not going home until I feel good and ready.” “What is your problem?” Milo asks demandingly. “Just explain to me why you have done nothing but sit here on your ass for five hours straight looking like a sick dog. Explain to me why you have to be so damn stubborn.” Brianna turns her head to face Milo and she stands up with the rain now falling harder soaking the both of them equally. “You want to know what my problem is Milo? You really want to know?” Brianna says throwing her arms around in a fit of anger. “Yes.” “My problem is that I haven’t been scheduled to wrestle for nearly two weeks. My problem is that Skitzo doesn’t seem to take me seriously, and I fear that when this heat between us is over, he will still have the belt instead of me. My problem is that I haven’t heard from Kramer for over a month and I don’t know if something bad happened to him. My problem is that Thomas called my up the other day to inform me that he couldn’t get me the grant needed to continue my work on the gyrosbot. My problem is that my life is on a downward spiral and I can’t stop it!!!!” A tear forms in the corner of Brianna’s eye and flows down her face accompanying the rain. Milo stands there with his mouth opened and an awestruck look upon his face. Brianna turns and starts to walk away. Milo finally comes to life and jogs behind her trying to catch up. “Brianna you can’t let that all bring you down. You’re better than that. The Brianna I know wouldn’t just give up.” “Who says I’ve given up?” Brianna starts walking faster and Milo stays at her heels thinking desperately of a way to get her to stop. That is until he realizes that anything he says won’t make a difference. He stops and Brianna starts getting farther and farther away. She notices that he isn’t behind her anymore so she turns back to see his soaked figure just standing there. “I’m not going to fight with you anymore Bri. You do what you want to do, just don’t say I didn’t try to help. Just know that when you feel like talking I’m always here for you, and I always will be. You can’t shut me out forever.” Milo turns and starts walking the opposite way with his hands dug into his pockets, which causes Brianna to go through a moment of contemplation. “I’m sorry.” She says to herself as she walks back over to the bench and resumes her spot. The scene slowly fades to black as Brianna goes back into her emotionless stare.
