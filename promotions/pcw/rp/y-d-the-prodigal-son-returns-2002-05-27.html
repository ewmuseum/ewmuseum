Y.D: The Prodigal Son Returns

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th May 2002 As PCW re-opened it’s doors just days ago many old faces appeared on the roster list yet again mixed in with the likes of some new competition. Names such as Corey Williams, Alexander Miyagawa and Xavier Kannon spring into mind at the thought of PCW’s loyal employee’s, but along with their return some questions come up un-answered….What happened to these superstars when PCW closed it’s doors on them? What turn did their lives take? How did they survive the days without knowing if PCW would ever embrace them again?While a few of these questions may forever remain un-answered, one superstar has stepped forth out of the shadows to tell his tale…one man has risen from his failure of being fired!That man was Charlie Loc….and this is his story! Young and Dangerous Pt I: The Prodigal Son Returns…. Drums could be heard in the background of the streets as the day broke over the city of Saigon, Vietnam. The day had just begun and along with it’s citizens, the city had just opened it’s eyes to yet another long, hard working day and presumably an even longer night. As most people take the day as it comes it seems that the city itself would too…. As the day begins in a seemingly run-down market place, the streets of a sleeping Vietnam now rise to the occasion and store keepers begin to open the shutters of their stores and set-up shop for the long day of sales and tourism that lie ahead. No sooner has it begun to open than people begin to flock in! Hundreds upon hundreds of people come out of nowhere to crowd the market place and give it life as the sound of morning and the drums that echo around the market place are soon pushed down in volume as the hustling and bustling of people begins to take over. Everything seems to fall into place, the women all flock to the stores that they love to do their shopping, the men going to the hairdresser or browsing around looking at what they need, the children showing around White tourists who seem to take a snapshot of anything and everything around them and finally the brooding teenagers move to their corners, protecting their territory and making their presence felt. Occasionally a squad of uniformed police officers can be seen amidst the see of people as they tap their batons and look around from beneath their dark sunglasses making sure that everything is in check, whilst the teenagers sit in their groups passing a cigarette or two around staring at them intensely possibly feeling somewhat threatened…. Apart from the obvious tension between the two factions of law and teenagers, the atmosphere of the market place is amazing! Everything seems to be alive and well and it’s almost as though Saigon is in for an almost perfect day! They say all good things must come to an end…some sooner than others! As the uniformed police officers slowly make their way out of the busy market place the teenagers in their own separate corners all begin to look at each other as trouble brews between them. Everyone but the teenagers themselves seem oblivious to this as no-one seems to notice nor care that a fight may perhaps ensue at some point between a group of these youths. Occasionally a store owner will pop their head out of their stall to see what’s brewing but doesn’t really seem to take note of what’s going on. As the frenzy of shopping continues to cause the market place to buzz the tension begins to slowly rise and the smell of a fight mixes in with that of some sweet smelling tourists and some rather foul smelling local folk. The staring continues to go on between the youth’s on their corners as neither of the boys sitting there attempt to make a move towards their opposition, it’s like they’re playing a waiting game! A well known rule about fighting is that you get into less trouble if you don’t swing the first punch, thus you provoke your opponent to hit you first and claim it as an act of self defense….looking like the 'hardened' sort of street kids they would probably know this, still the only movement is done in their own groups! As the morning wares on and slowly transforms into day, the market place begins to pick up the pace and more and more people begin to flood in as though the main gates weren’t opened before. The store owners however don’t seem to mind too much, after all it just means more money for them, but the youth’s on the corner do on the other hand….. When there’s more people, it’d be more obvious that criminal activity would go on, the smartest youths would operate in between the thick crowd of people and use it to their advantage…but this thought would quickly be cut out because in between the sea of people, barely visible guards roam, camouflaging themselves between the frenzied Vietnamese shoppers! 'Freshest Fish In Saigon! No better place to buy fish than here~!' Over the hustle and bustle of the market place a loud Oriental voice can be heard as a sweaty, chunky looking man wearing a white apron and a chef hat stands atop his stall holding a huge fish up by the tail, yelling out to the sea of people as they all turn to stare and see what the commotion is. Advertisement must be banned in a huge place like that…because no sooner has the man advertised his fish monger store than a group of police officers band together and march on towards his stall, looking with hardened expressions on their faces up towards the man. It is like a sign from the gods! As the police officers band together to stop the man from attracting too much attention a smallish looking, seemingly well dressed, dark boy can be seen darting through the crowd making his way over to the group of youths sitting down on the left side of the square. The rather tough looking Viet sitting in the middle of the group nods his head towards the child and then leans down to hear what he has to say, the boys around him, supposedly his crew all turn their attention to the child who is delivering the message, awaiting their leaders command. The child finishes whispering in the teenagers ear as he nod’s to him and then turns to his own group of youths and begins to wave his hands around, his long hair and baggy clothing fluttering about as the child runs over to the other group of teenagers on the other side of the square who have their necks craned wondering what’s going on…. A battlefield can be anything from the greenest meadow or the darkest plain, it seems that the battlefield could very well be the market square because as the child begins to deliver the 'message' to the opposing group of teenagers, the leader and another larger looking Viet from the other group begin to make their way across the square towards their foes… BLEEP~! BLEEP~! The shrill sound of a whistle cries over the loud noise of the buzzing market place as majority of eyes direct themselves over towards the fish monger who is now getting a severe beating from the police officers as bystanders around the stall begin to shake their heads and walk off. More uniformed officers arrive from the other end of the market place as the whistle continues to blast off into the air and the fish monger himself seems to be putting up a decent struggle for his freedom! All the attention that had just been directed at the fish mongers soon fades out as the child who appears to be a 'Messenger Boy' finishes telling the other group of youth’s what’s happening and then scurries off, leaving the leader staring up towards the two teenagers who are moving over towards him. It’s like a Clint Eastwood movie, the two men move out to accept a challenge of some sort as the one man stays back with his men being ice-cool and planning everything in his head…the only difference being that this wasn’t going to be a gun fight, nor a fair one at that! At the sign of a hand gesture or a word both 'crews' would lash out and defend their turf! Whatever the message that was given to both men, it must have been big news because the leaders themselves were making their presence felt! Once again no-one in the market place seemed to notice the confrontation between the two rival groups of teenagers, nor did they seem to care! A woman or two would occasionally glance in the direction with a worried look on her face before sighing and returning back to what she was doing, accepting it as a part of every day life in Vietnam…. They say that the hardest 'Gangsters' or 'Criminals' come from places like New York City or Italy but to tell the truth, New York is nothing compared to what goes on in Khmer, Cambodia or Saigon, Vietnam! Death and violence is a part of everyday life in Vietnam, thus a reason why no one seems to care about the fight that seems to be brewing between the two crews! All of a sudden everything seems to go slow, the leader that is sitting down throws his cigarette out of his mouth and steps up to face the other leader who is approaching him as the two men seem to size each other down! The big chunky looking teenager smiles before throwing his huge fist through the air sending it crashing into the leaders jaw, laying him out on the dusty ground of the market place! 'DU MA MAI~!' The teenager who had just got knocked down screams at the top of his voice in his native tongue as his crew jumps on top of the two men who just approached him and then the other crew runs from their side of the market square and collide! Everyone in the market seems to stop what there doing and look suddenly, as the violence erupts it becomes a spectacle! Everyone drops what they are doing and clears a solid patch of ground for the two rivals to fight on as the sound of slapping and crunching can be heard as fists and feet fly through the air, sending clouds of dust flying up! BLEEP~! BLEEP~! BLEEP~! The shrill blast of the whistle sounds again as Police officers run in from every direction to stop the ruckus in the middle of the market place and show the strong hand of the law in Saigon. As all this is happening, a man from either side of the crews slink back off into the darkness and walk around the outside of the stalls, avoiding the commotion and moving off towards a gate hidden behind the cloth of a trinket shop….a built looking Asian teenager with short hair nods towards his counterpart before walking up towards the gate. The sounds of the war can still be heard as he approaches the two men guarding it, tapping them on the shoulder and pointing towards the fight. 'They in trouble, they tell me they need help!' The teenager exclaims as the two guards nod at him and run from their post as he motions for the other youth to come join him. Together they open the gate and in steps a familiar looking face in the sea of people. With his head hung low and two blonde fringes and a ponytail showing on a pair of black pants, a white top and a backpack slung over his shoulder, the man take a draw from his cigarette. The fighting continues to go on in the background of the market place as the two teenagers usher him in shaking his free hand as he exhales the smoke and throws the cigarette away, both teenagers look around quickly, checking to see if they had been followed as the one who spoke before speaks again… 'Good to see you home…Charlie' The familiar man looks up revealing himself to be back in Saigon and back in business! He smiles at the two teenagers as they nod their heads and quickly take him off into the maze of stalls and shops avoiding the fight and sneaking him within the walls he was exiled from…. The blistering Sun rises over the market place as the brawl finally comes to a halt, the blood staining the brown dust and the shopping all continuing. It would seem that the day has reached it’s peak in Saigon, Vietnam and it would seem that the presumably long night, had just been affirmed! The police officers in Vietnam had just apprehended and cleaned the gritty streets of two small crews and made the city and streets a bit safer, but it seems that it was a small price to pay as an even bigger problem just snuck in the backdoor……the Vietnamese Viper had just returned home!
