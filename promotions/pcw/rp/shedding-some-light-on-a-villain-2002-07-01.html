Shedding some light on a villain

1 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 1st, 2002 We want Inc.! We want Inc.! We want Inc.! The name rings out in a thousand tones and resounds with the echoes of success. Wait - that's pretty lame isn't it? Bah! Here's an idea: What are you doing right now? Reading a book? An interesting book? Is it a long novel or a short story? Hey, I have an idea! Are you close to the TV? Grab the remote and start flipping channels...[waits]...yeah, that's it...now what's that channel? I think it starts with an 'E'....Da Na Na! Da Na Na! That's it! SportsCenter! My faithful viewers, your 'World Renowned' hero has made it to the big show. He is headed to primetime and is going to be on a box near you. There were those of you who doubted his ability...there were those of you who doubted his sincerity...hell, there were those of you who doubted his sanity! But let's be serious here for a moment because we all know that Legend Inc. isn't as crazy as one Jeremy Howard - although, who is? Recap! Recap! Recap! Well, for all of those viewers who didn't try to report Legend Inc. to the police for gambling and to the FCC for hijacking the ESPN SportsCenter Showcase, Legend Inc. indeed got the bid to be on the SportsCenter showcase. In fact, he outbid everyone including one godfather of wrestling, Vinny Mac. It was intense, but in the end the viewers will get what they want...what they need...they will get their fix: A Legend Fix. The scene opens up to Tom Lexian sitting up in bed at a hotel in New York. The hotel is called 'The Redemption.' Go figure. It's about 9:38 am in the morning - have you had breakfast yet? Legend Inc.: [scratching his head] Aw geez....that was a rough night. Robert Yarney: [sits up in bed next to Tom] Sure was. Legend Inc.: [looks over at Robert] Oh my god...oh my...no, it couldn't be... Robert Yarney: [shakes his head] Keep your pants on, Tom...we had to sleep together, remember? You didn't want to pay for the double beds. Legend Inc.: I didn't? Robert Yarney: [stands up and wipes his eyes] No...you didn't. You're the biggest cheapskate I know...well, you know that already. It shouldn't come as a complete shock to you. Legend Inc.: I don't remember anything...last thing I recall was getting on a plane bound for New York.... Robert Yarney: ...and you did. We arrived here late last night and you were a bit - drunk. Legend Inc.: Drunk? I never get drunk. Robert Yarney: Well you were drunk last night, buddy. One too many shots of whiskey I think. Legend Inc.: They don't serve whiskey on planes. Robert Yarney: That guy next to you did. He had an entire coat side full of assorted alcoholic beverages. You two got in some really macho argument about who can really hold their liquor. Needless to say, you lost. Legend Inc.: [rubs the temples on his forehead until they turn a bright green] Oh god,...I hate drinking. I don't know why so many people do it. Robert Yarney: I don't know. They're depressed, they've got problems... Legend Inc.: I've got problems... Robert Yarney: No, you have an extremely important interview to do, that's your problem. I'm talking about different kinds of tribulations that people go through. But hell, don’t worry about it. After all, you’re the Continental champion now, remember? Legend Inc.: I know. I still can’t believe it. But I have more things to worry about. Like my situation isn't bad enough anyways...besides, I still don't know how many reasons someone could possibly have to drink. Robert Yarney: Lots of reasons, actually. Who cares? Now you know better the next time you think about taking another shot. Legend Inc.: That's for sure. What time is it... Robert Yarney: [checks the clock] It's about 9:30 am. We have to be down at the studios tonight by 8:30 pm if we're going to make the live interview. Legend Inc.: Live interview? Robert Yarney: Yeah. You're doing an interview with the one and only Stuart Scott after the video montage of you and your upcoming match at Tuesday Turmoil, only it’s being held on Wednesday. Whose that guy? Rent-A-Villain? He’s nuts. Legend Inc.: Yeah, he is. I don’t know what his deal is, but apparently he’s forgotten what it means to be a good guy. What kind of questions are they going to ask me? Robert Yarney: [putting some pants on] I don't know. They could ask you anything. I just hope that these guys wouldn't try to harass you or get you down. They are notorious for trying to embarrassing people on there. Legend Inc.: Well, I'll try to stay cool on there. Robert Yarney: Just don't let them get to you. The last thing we need is some incident. Legend Inc.: An incident? Robert Yarney: Yeah, remember that entire Vader thing at that international news studio? Legend Inc.: Vader? As in Big Van Vader? Robert Yarney: Yeah, the big dude with the mask. Well, the entire story was that he was on the show and the host had begun to make fun of him and even implied to him that what he did for a living was 'fake.' Legend Inc.: Oh, I'm sure that didn't go over so well with Mr. Vader. Robert Yarney: It didn't. He got pretty pissed off and decided to get a bit physical with the host. I'm sure the host figured out that wrestling wasn't quite so 'fake.' Legend Inc.: I don't doubt it. But then again, I don’t know if I’d be able to not make fun of Rent-a-Villain. It just sounds ridiculous to be him. I’ll have to make this match a reminder that good guys can’t turn evil. Robert Yarney: So what exactly are you going to wear to this formal event? Legend Inc.: Formal event, huh? I thought it was an interview. Robert Yarney: Well you don't want to look like a bum on there. I'm sure these guys have some preconceived view on what wrestlers look like. Legend Inc.: Preconceived view? Like leather pants, really dark shades, and a muscle shirt? Robert Yarney: Probably something like that. Legend Inc.: I guess I could go in a suit or something. Robert Yarney: [surprised] A suit? Tom, you never wear suits. In fact, the last time you wore a suit I was just a glimmer in my wrestling trainers' eyes. Legend Inc.: Hey, I can make it work. I am 'Legend Inc.' after all. There's no doubt that I can take him to the next level, and if that means that I have to put some monkey suit on with a tie - that's what I'll do. Robert Yarney: [nods his head] Alright. Well, I'm going to start some breakfast going around here. What do you want to eat? Legend Inc.: I don't really care. I'm about to take a shower anyways. Robert Yarney: [heads out] Cool beans. I'll be in the kitchen. * * * Tom walked out of the room and into the main hallway. He looked around confused since he didn't remember which rooms lead where. He randomly chose the closest door and opened it...oops, empty. A closet. Tom slammed the door shut and walked further down and opened another door. This time he had hit the jackpot....a bathroom. It was actually rather plush with teal walls and a tiled floor. Tom walked in with a towel and set it down on the toilet. He pulled the curtain back and jumped back in surprise as he saw an individual standing there before him. Legend Inc.: [shocked] Dude...what the fuck are you doing here? The Joker: [holding a microphone above the shower] Well I'm not rinsing off, that's for damn sure. Legend Inc.: I can't believe this shit....of all the bone headed stunts you have pulled since I've known you...this might just take the cake. The Joker: [steps away from the shower] Look, I wasn't here to spy on you taking a shower or anything perverted like that.... Legend Inc.: Really? You weren't trying to spy on me? That's funny, because that looks like a microphone you're trying to install above my shower head. The Joker: [looks back] Ah yes, standard procedure actually. Legend Inc.: [pushes the Joker back into the shower] I want answers, and I want them now. The Joker: [looks angry] Oh...that was a big mistake, Lexian. You never, ever put your hands on me. Legend Inc.: We had a deal, asshole. You were supposed to help me get onto the Showcase special and I would get the job done on my end of the bargain inside of the ring where I will beat the living hell out of those three other losers. The Joker: I fulfilled my end of the bargain. You are on the Sportscenter Showcase tonight. And thanks to who? Not you, my friend. Me. That's right. The ol' Joker did exactly what he said he was going to do. Now, what bullshit response are you going to give me now? Legend Inc.: I didn't give you permission to be here. The Joker: I don't need permission. I go where I want, when I want. I'm in charge, Lexian, not you. You'd better figure that out sooner than later, or this relationship will be terminated. Legend Inc.: [picks up a towel that had fallen on the floor] What else do you want from me now? It's not like I need help giving an interview. The Joker: I don't want to talk about the interview. Legend Inc.: [sighs] Oh great, you want to criticize my wrestling right? You want to tell me which moves I need to perform and what holds to do and... The Joker: Lexian, shut up. I need to talk to you about Vince McMahon. Legend Inc.: McMahon? What about him? The Joker: I saw him during the bidding. He was the one that continued the bidding after everyone else was done and almost ruined the entire plan. Legend Inc.: I noticed. The Joker: He wants you. Legend Inc.: He wants me? [looks at McMahon incredulously] Like, he wants me...as in, physically? The Joker: You have been watching too many movies. Do you think he's attracted to you or something? He wants your talent in his promotion. Legend Inc.: He already had his shot. I'm not going to give his crap promotion any more attention than I do the Santa Claus begging for change during Christmas at the mall. The Joker: You might not want to join, but I promise you he will not stop trying to recruit you. He's probably one of the most determined men I've ever seen. Legend Inc.: Determined or anal retentive? The Joker: [shrugs] One or the other. I highly doubt he cares which one he is. But I'm telling you right now to watch your back. Legend Inc.: What is he going to do? Send those lackeys for me and hit me over the head with a club? Then put me in the ring and force me to wrestle? The Joker: You can joke around all you want, but sooner or later he's going to figure out a way to put you in a very compromising situation. Legend Inc.: What exactly do you mean by a 'compromising' position? The Joker: I mean, he's going to put you between a rock and a hard place. You're going to have to choose between him and something very important to you, and that's where he's going to reel you in. Legend Inc.: He's not going to get me. The Joker: How can you be so sure? You got a promising career ahead of you in the PCW, but it means nothing if you have to hook up with his promotion. Do you know what they'd do to you? Legend Inc.: I'd be jobbed for months probably. They'd give me the lowest cards available and pay me nothing. I'd be a nobody again. The Joker: I've seen in happen to a lot of people. Really talented guys that deserved to be at the top of our sport and instead are relegated to being at the bottom of the barrel. That's exactly why I'm here - to make sure that it doesn't happen to you. Legend Inc.: Well it's not. The Joker: Really? I'm glad that you are so sure of yourself because I'm not. McMahon always gets his way, and you're on his list. Legend Inc.: I'll be fine. I can take care of myself. Now, would you mind leaving so I can take my shower and get ready for the show? The Joker: [looks at Tom] You know, there comes a time when you need to appreciate the help you get. Because one day that help will be gone and you'll look back on all of your success and want to thank the people that were there for you. Legend Inc.: That's you, right? The Joker: I don't need your thanks. I need to make sure that people that deserve to be at the top make it there, without any backstage hagglings or third party consulting. [begins to walk away] Tom stood there with a towel in his hand. He just stood in the middle of the bathroom with an expression-less look on his face. What the Insider had said actually made some sense. Vince McMahon got whatever he wanted because he had a)the power to do so and b)a shitload of money. Tom didn't doubt that he could put him in a compromising position - he was just trying to stand up to the Insider. But Tom continued to wonder how he would be able to repel McMahon's continued persistence... Tom shook his head and put the towel back onto the toilet. He turned on the shower and jumped in. Yelp! Good move, Tom...the water is still cold. But being the tough guy Tom was he didn't jump out of the shower...nope! He stayed in and waited for the water to warm up. As Tom began soaking his body with the supreme cleansing power of 'Dove' soap, Tom began thinking about the Insider's comments about thanking people that help you. Bullshit. It was true that the Joker was helping him with his promos right now, but by no means was he off the hook for all the BS he has given to him in the mean time. Besides, the Joker still hasn't given him any inside dirt about his opponents. Sure, he set up the loan and everything but... Tom shook his head in confusion as he began realizing what the Joker really has done for him. He washed the soap off and watched it drain out....life sure was getting harder... But he was still the Continental champion. No one could take that from him.
