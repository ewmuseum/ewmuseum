Locked up.... for what?

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/23/2002 [Wow.... what a night. First the pinfall over Snow, leaving not only Snow, but also the rest of PCW wondering if indeed I should be the champion. Then, that interview with Sports Illustrated went well. Ah, the bar was a small, little quaint place. Nice atmosphere. But, I feel something isn't right. I don't remember much after arriving at that bar, and talking to one guy] ‘Wake up.... you got a visitor.’ *The C.O walks off, tapping his baton on the bars of Boda’s cell. Boda slowly opens his eyes. The gray ,cold ceiling is the first thing he sees. Sitting up, he stares at the steel bars, the doorway to the cell he has spent the night in.* [YAWN] *He stands up, and lets out a yawn, and a stretch. His back sore from the inadequate accommodations last night. Walking over to the toilet, the uneasy feeling of the cold floor under his bare feet, he takes a morning relief piss.* [AHHHH] *Stepping over a half a foot, he turns on the warm tap water. Brownish colored liquid flows, in spurts. Running his hands under the water, he splashes some on his unshaven face, before looking in the mirror* [What the he1l happened last night?] *Boda stands there, looking in the mirror. His reflection shows a worn out, tired soul. He turns his attention to the running water below. Boda puts his right hand in the water, then runs it through his blonde hair.* [All I remember is that obnoxious guy] *Boda walks to the cot near the wall. He grabs his shirt from the pillow that he rested his head on last night. Pulling on the shirt, he hears the C.O coming back.* C.O: ‘Hey, Mr. Pro-Wrestler......any day now. Your lawyer is here. Get a move on’ *Boda clears his throat, its as dry as the Sahara Desert. The stale taste of Bacardi Silver on his tongue. The smell of cigars and cigarettes emanate from his clothes. A warm shower is needed, but alas, being in this place does not have the luxuries Chris Sloboda is used to* [I only had a few drinks. How did I get here?] *Boda pulls on his sneakers, then buttons his blue jeans up.* C.O: ‘Are you ready pretty boy? Let’s go.’ [I remember winning the match last night, then going out for a few drinks, that’s all I remember now. Why is my head so cloudy?] *Chris looks at the impatient C.O. The Officer gives Boda a dirty glancing over. Chris walks to the bars, and nods at the C.O, who opens the bars. Chris steps out into the hallway, in front of the C.O. He stands there as the correction officer (C.O) puts leg shackles and handcuffs on Chris.* [D@mn, now I know what it feels like to be Karl LeBlanc.] *Boda is led down the hall, past about five other cells. He looks at one inmate, who has his back turned to us, feverously bopping the bishop. Chris reads the name off the back of the mans shirt* [Boise Correctional Institute? D@mn.... Chris.... think... what happened last night?] *Just past the cell where the man is warming up the heat seeking moisture missile, the C.O opens the door leading from the cells to the rest of the facility. Boda steps through, and into the lobby. Flashes erupt from the cameras of new reporters. Chris tries to hide his face, and the C.O’s quickly escort him past the media.* [Wow.... this is getting freaky. You’d think I killed some one last night.....OH SHIT.... I didn’t kill some one, did I?] *Once the C.O and Boda exit the lobby, they head through another set of doors, and down another long, lonely hallway. On the wall, pictures of ‘Most Wanted’ by the Boise Idaho police.* [well, at least I’m not on those pictures.] C.O: ‘You have 10 minutes alone with your lawyer.’ [Lawyer? I called my lawyer last night? Nah.....I can’t remember last night. I wish I knew what the hell was going on around here.] *As Boda and the C.O get to the end of the hallway, a larger man, in a C.O’s uniform, but with more insignia than the one leading Chris to the meeting with his lawyer, steps out of a door, and taps the C.O on the shoulder. Boda and C.O stop, just in front of the door at the end of the hallway.* C.O: ‘What’s up sarg?’ *The bigger C.O, the Sarg apparently, steps in front of Boda and his escort C.O* Sarg: ‘Just got word that Mr. Smith is still in County General. Apparently he’s slipped into a coma.’ *Both C.O’s look at Boda* [Mr. Smith? Hospital? Coma? What the he1l happened last night?] C.O: ‘Any word on if he’ll be okay?’ Sarg: ‘It’s still in the air right now. He could survive, he could crash and burn. He did suffer alot of head trauma last night.’ *Boda finally speaks up, to the disliking of the C.O’s* Boda: “So....what happened?” *The C.O shoves Boda into a wall, holding him there. Boda struggles to get free, but can not because of being restrained.* [D@mn you.... you little jackass.... you’re lucky I’m in shackles, or you’d get your ass handed to you.] *The Sarg pulls the C.O off, telling him to calm down. Boda stands up straight, rubbing the back of his head with both handcuffed hands. He stares at the C.O, showing a look of anger in his eyes. Sarg tells the C.O to leave* Sarg: ‘Tell you what.....I’ll take over from here. Go back to your post.’ C.O: ‘Okay Sarg. And you... you better hope John Smith pulls out of this alive, or else.’ Sarg: ‘That’s enough Officer Smith’ *The C.O walks off down the hall, leaving Boda in the control of Sarg.* [C.O Smith? He can’t be related to this Mr. Smith in the coma in the hospital] Sarg: ‘Wow, Officer Smith is sure pissed off at you. Well, if it was my brother you hospitalized last night, I’d be the same too. Well, let’s go. Your attorney is here.’ Boda: “Sure. Hey, can you do me a favor?” Sarg: ‘What’s that?’ Boda: “What the he1l happened last night? Why am I in here?” Sarg: ‘Can’t remember last night huh?’ Boda: “No. Weird thing too, my head doesn’t hurt at all, it’s just, I can’t seem to remember anything after arriving at that bar last night, and ordering a drink.” Sarg: ‘Well, you’ve been arrested, and blamed for allegedly beating John Smith last night. The beating was severe enough to hospitalize him, and now, he is in a coma.’ Boda: “I don’t know any John Smith. So, what’s going to happen? I’ve got matches to wrestle in this week. Plus, the PPV in St. Louis. I need to make it there, and win the World Title.” Sarg: ‘To be honest, unless some how, your name gets cleared in connection to this attack.... you’re going to be here, in Boise for a long time. You are facing 10-15 years for the brutal attack.’ *Boda turns aside, not facing the Sarg anymore. He thinks hard about last night, but nothing comes to mind.* Sarg: ‘Let’s go inside. Your attorney is waiting for you.’ *The Sarg leads Boda through the double doors, and into a bright white room, with a large mirror on one wall. Boda sits at the table, and the Sarg leaves.* [If I could only remember last night. This shit shouldn’t be happening. I wouldn’t purposely injure some one, and put them in the hospital. THINK BODA, THINK.] *Suddenly, the white door opens, and in steps a well dressed man. He plops his briefcase down on the table, and looks at Boda. Chris looks back at the man, and shoots a half smile* Boda: “Thanks for coming Brad. How’d you get the call?” Brad the Lawyer: ‘Actually, it was on the late news last night, and I got the call from Rich DeVoss. What happened?’ Boda: “That’s the funny thing Brad.... I don’t recall anything, other than this. I beat Snow in a six-man tag match last night, scoring ANOTHER victory over the pathetic excuse for a World Champion. I did a brief interview with Sports Illustrated, mostly about my purchase of the Magic and some about the upcoming PPV, and how the world should watch as I will walk out the World Champion in St. Louis. Then, after saying bye to D-Kay, Red Rock, Boolie and Tim Walden, I walked to my car with Mexi, and we talked about some shit. I said bye to him, and headed to a bar about 5 miles up the road from the Arena. Once there, I was recognized, and treated well, even had my first drink on the house. Bacardi and Coke, that’s what it was. That’s all I remember. Oh.... and there was this one guy, really rude bastard too. He had on a ‘SNOW’ t-shirt, and I joked around with him about things, and Jason Snow. That’s the last I remember anything, other than waking up here this morning.” Brad the Lawyer: ‘Did you say, a man wearing a ‘SNOW’ t-shirt?’ *Boda nods his head. Brad pulls out a few pictures, and shows Boda* Brad the Lawyer: ‘Well, this man, wearing a ‘SNOW’ shirt was taken from the Dead Dick Bar last night, and is in a coma now. Witnesses there say you got enraged by him being a Jason Snow fan, and literally tried to kill him. They even reported that you piledrived the man onto the hood of a car in the lot. Chris, I must say, unless concrete evidence surfaces.... you’re in trouble. Badly. Meaning, no World Title, no ownership of the Magic, no more family to go home to visit.’ *Boda lowers his head, trying to not look at the pictures, or at Brad. The scene fades as Brad takes a cell phone call, and Boda just sits there, in his chair shaking his head back and forth.* (to be continued)
