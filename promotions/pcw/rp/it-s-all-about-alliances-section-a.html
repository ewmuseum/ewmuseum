Date: Today “What brings you to grace us with your presence?” It was strange enough as it is for anyone to venture all the way out to Sado Island to visit anyone…but it was a shock for these guests to show up at Inphino’s home. Sitting side by side on the black leather couch in the living room was Alexander J Maxis, his wife, Ryoko Godai, and their daughter, Hana Maxis. Yeah…a corporate mogul/yakuza crime lord, a mad genetics scientist, and their daughter…paying a social visit to the Black Dragon Master. AJ Maxis was wearing a black pin stripe three-piece suit, with a white shirt and black tie. His dark gray hair was pulled back in a small ponytail at the base of his skull. Ryoko Godai, as always, was in her black suit dress, the split skirt going down to her ankles and peaks of the white blouse underneath peeking through from the sleeves and collar of the black suit jacket. Her straight/curled black locks were cascading around her shoulders, and her glasses were perched atop of her nose. Hana Maxis wore a long sleeved black blouse with folded cuffs, a open collar, and tight silver jeans with zippers on the side. Her honey brown hair was down to her shoulders much like her mothers, but was straighter than hers. Inphino sat to the right of them on a black stool…still wearing his black bondage pants and long sleeved mock tee. “Well, Inphino,” Hana said, taking charge of the conversation. “I persuaded mother and father to take the day off from work, and then I arranged for a nice outing in the country. And since we were in the neighborhood…” “What neighborhood?” Maxis muttered, looking down at his tie as his daughter spoke. “There’s only two blasted houses out here.” Hana gave her father a sharp look before going on with the conversation. “The day will been a good family outing for us all. How about your day?” Inphino shrugged his shoulders, his head giving a short shake. “Morning training, meditation, Mina was preparing breakfast before you arrived. It’s been fine so far.” Mina walked back into the room with a tray of tea and pastries. Setting them down on the coffee table, she took a stand next to Inphino and looked at the Maxis family sit there…quite silent actually. “What a lively bunch,” Mina whispered to Inphino sarcastically. Ryoko Godai sat perfectly still, her eyes staring off into space as her hands were folded politely in her lap. Mina took notice of her and called out to her. “Hey…ma’am. Is there something wrong?” She got no response from “Mrs. Maxis”. Hana nudged her mother, stirring her from her thoughts. Ryoko looked over to Mina and gave her a weak smile. “You have a lovely home!” she blurted out, not paying a lick of attention to what had been said before. Most people did that when there was nothing worth putting interest in. “Gee…thanks.” Mina returned the smile, weak for weak, and backed out of the room. “I’m checking on breakfast. Excuse me.” As soon as Mina left the living room, Maxis leaned over towards Inphino with a friendly, though faux smile. “So…let me get this straight…Akiyama lives right next door to you?” Inphino’s thumb pointed behind him, towards the back yard. “Right behind me.” “And you two haven’t confronted each other yet?” A shake of the head from the Black Dragon Master. “As long as he stays on his side of the ravine, I honestly don’t have a problem with him…. though there is a lot of noise coming from over there. His people stay over there, and Mina and I don’t venture over.” AJ Maxis nodded slowly, processing the info. “That’s interesting. I may press my luck and venture over there for a visit. I haven’t seen him since he left my establishment.” He turned to his wife and tapped her in her arm. “You hear that, dear? Akiyama’s right next door.” “No tea for me, thanks!” another automatic response from Ryoko Godai. Maxis and Hana stared at the woman, who blinked and bowed her head apologetically. “Sorry…I was going over some data from last night’s experiments.” “Ugh…I swear…” Hana shook her head, leaning over against the arm of the sofa and sighing. Some people just thought themselves more important that the situation they’re involved in… Right? Title: Meet the Parents Akiyama stood there standing before his parents, shirtless in his vinyl zippered pants and silver buckled boots. The expression on his face was startling, a rare thing against his normally stern, angry, or apathetic gaze. Jenna backed slowly out of the kitchen, her place being to inform Anarchy of the guests, leaving the five of them alone. Yukina and Yoshihiro Akiyama… the parents of the Neo Void God… “Is that how you greet your parents now?” Yuki asked in a hurt tone, her eyes never leaving the god’s. “You think you’re too “godly” to give your own parents a hug?” Akiyama obliged rather quickly, leaning down and embracing his mother in his arms. He turned to his father next and shook hands before giving him a hug. As he stepped back, he was embraced by the final person…Ayumi Hamasaki. “Nice to see ya again, Aki-Aki!” she beamed, a bright smile on her pretty face. Akiyama patted her on her forehead before she stepped back at his parents’ side. Cyclonica, who had been sitting at the kitchen table the entire time, stood up and approached them all with caution. Yuki and Yoshihiro looked over at the girl, their eyes scrutinizing her. “So…this is the daughter we’ve heard so much about?” Akiyama’s father asked him. The god nodded, and the older man looked over to Cyclonica. “Well then, young lady, it’s a pleasure to meet you. We are your grandparents, Yuki and Yoshihiro Akiyama.” The two of them bowed at Cyclonica, the Divine Prophetess staring at them strangely. Yuki came back up from her bow, giving Cyclonica a softer, though strict look. “This is your second cousin, Ayumi Hamasaki.” Cyclonica didn’t see the overly enthusiastic hug come from the J-pop diva. The Divine Prophetess’s eyes bulged out of her head as Ayu hugged her tightly, despite the fact that she was still in her underpants. Ayu stepped back and bowed finally at her new cousin. “Nice to meet you finally!” she said. Then she let out a sound of surprise as she remained in a bowed position. “Oh my, you’re not dressed are you?” Yuki looked over to Akiyama sharply. “You let your children go around like this in this weather, Akira? What if she catches cold? Then what?” Akiyama shrugged his shoulders at his mother, not noticing the quick slap across his head he received from his father. A low groan came from the nVg as he looked back over to him. “Don’t give your mother that look. You know very well that its just about winter, and you know just as well that it gets exceptionally cold in mountainous regions. Didn’t you learn anything from those wilderness training trips I took you on?” “While you’re getting dressed, young lady, we can go meet our other granddaughter.” Yuki turned to Akiyama, not paying any attention to the annoyed look on Cyclonica’s face. She was prepared to talk back, but Yuki turned back to her with a harsh look, her eyes flaring up at the 19 year old. “Is there something I can help you with before you go get dressed?” Cyclonica would have said something…but at this point even she knew that it was no use. She sighed as she walked off into the castle, muttering under her breath the entire way. Ayu smiled as she took Akiyama’s arm. “Now let’s go meet my other cousin!” Akiyama ushered them out of the kitchen just as Boca was coming to on the other side of the table. The god glared back as Boca stood up, his loud moans more than audible. “Why’d you have to hit me like that, papi? I haven’t been hit like that since Stephano found out I had ran the batteries out on the Double Decker Dildo!” Akiyama had no choice…a hard Hand of Fate sent Boca flying into the walk in pantry, crashing into the shelves in the back of the room. Akiyama closed and locked the door behind him just as his parents stepped back into the kitchen. The god pressed himself against the wall of the pantry and gave them an innocent look. Yoshihiro waved him on. “Enough lollygagging.” Akiyama walked his parents and his cousin across the courtyard and up the flight of stairs leading to the bedrooms. He stopped in front of the first bedroom and knocked…well, it was more of a pounding of his fist against the oak door. Jenna poked her head out from the room and smiled at them all. “Yes?” she said with a big friendly grin. Yuki’s brow wrinkled. “This is your other daughter? I thought this was the maid.” “I’m not the maid!” Jenna said, sticking her tongue out at her. Akiyama raised an eyebrow at her questioningly. Jenna looked back into the room quickly. “Well, Ana’s not really ready for guests yet. But come back in a few minutes, and---“ Yoshihiro wasn’t one for waiting. He stepped forward and gave it a hard shove with his palm. The door flung open, and everyone looked in with surprise. Anarchy was on her back in the bed, with her legs stretched apart and held up in the air by nooses attached to the ceiling. Not nude, but she was wearing black panties and a black tank with the anarchy symbol printed on it in red. Ana sat up and gave a surprised smile at them all. “Um…morning.” Akiyama sighed as Ayu’s hand went to her gaping mouth. “Is THIS your daughter, Akira?” Yoshihiro asked his son. Akiyama nodded, prompting Yuki turned her gaze from Anarchy to Akiyama. “Why is the maid in the room with your daughter…in this position?” “I’m not the maid!” Jenna exclaimed once more. Ana hissed at Jenna, motioning for her to cut it out. Then Ana looked at Mr. and Mrs. Akiyama with the same smile. “Well…the maid is helping me dress because I sprained my ankle not too long ago, and it’s difficult for me to move about.” Ayu’s face was blank, signaling that she was generally confused about something. Yoshihiro thought it over for a second before nodding. “Fine. We’ll wait for you in the courtyard. Please do not keep us waiting.” The parents and Ayu walked out of the bedroom, followed by Akiyama, who gave his daughter a look before closing the door on them Anarchy looked over at Jenna with an amused reaction. “Never seen that look on his face.” she muttered. “What look?” Ana leaned back and chuckled. “Looked like he wanted to be put out of his misery.” Then she waved Jenna in her direction. “As much as I hate quickies…” Akiyama came trotting behind his family down the stairs. Yuki and Yoshihiro stopped dead in their tracks in the center of the courtyard, spinning around together and looking at Akiyama. Ayumi bumped right into them and staggered back into the arms of her cousin. Akiyama sighed as he pulled her aside and folded his arms in front of his parents. “So you’re a wrestler huh?” Yoshihiro asked him. Akiyama nodded slowly at him, the question so obvious since they knew it since he was banished from Japan. Yoshihiro nodded back, looking up at the clear sky. “I can only assume you’re basing your technique on the martial arts I taught you.” The god groaned, his hand going to his forehead. Meanwhile… “Those are his parental units?” Cyclonica stepped out from the darker portion of the upstairs balcony alcove. She was now dressed in her basic attire, tight black vinyl pants, black high-heeled silver stiletto boots, tight black mesh transparent long sleeved mock tee, and an ice blue halter underneath. She nodded at the voice that questioned her. “That’s who they identified themselves as. But I recognize their faces. They’re legit.” Cyclonica leaned against the balcony railing on her forearms, peering down at the backs of the Akiyama family, Akiyama facing towards them. “The other woman is Ayumi Hamasaki, pop idol, cousin of the god.” A white wisp came floating away from the person…cigarette smoke most likely. “What is the reaction that the god had towards them?” “Same as any son seeing his parents after a long period of time. Shock, then submissive to parental will.” She shook her head at that statement. “It is not good.” “Then I see that the next phase will have to come into action.” The cold voice responded. “Summon the others and proceed according to orders.” Back down in the courtyard… Anarchy was being helped down the stairs by Jenna, dressed in a pair of black cargo pants and her tight anarchy tank underneath a blue jacket…with crutch accessories. Yoshihiro and Yuki turned towards her as soon as she reached the last step. “What is wrong with you, child?” Yuki asked sharply. “My ankle... sprained?” she did just tell them earlier. Yuki gave a disapproving look at her granddaughter. “What are the crutches for?” Anarchy gave them a troubled look. “Um…ankle…sprained?” maybe they weren’t getting it. There were plenty of people that just didn’t get facts when they were presented before them. Defeat… frailty…weakness… Maybe the Neo Void God would have to help them face facts…face facts with his fists. “You don’t need those.” Suddenly Yuki snapped her leg out with lightning quickness! Both crutches snapped underneath the weight of Anarchy, and the girl almost collapsed right there if she didn’t jump back on one foot. “What the hell are you DOING??” Ana yelled in a panic as Yuki stepped towards her, her hands lowered at her sides. “My ANKLE….is SPRAINED!” “When I was your age I could walk on a sprained ankle for weeks, and still defeat opponents without breaking a sweat!” Yuki stomped into Anarchy’s good foot, holding her down before delivering a double palm strike to her midsection. Ana fell back hard on the cold stone ground, glaring up through narrow eyes at her grandmother. Yuki stood over her, her eyes stern as she brushed off a speck of lint. “Hmph. I’m surprised. You’re a wrestler as well, and you can’t protect yourself with a broken ankle?” she looked over at her son with disapproval. “Are you teaching your children anything?” Yoshihiro took notice of Akiyama’s silver hair and shook his head. “Anything besides dying their hair different colors?” Ayumi started laughing…which was slowly killed when everyone glared in her direction. “Oh…sorry.” Anarchy glared at Akiyama as Jenna helped her back to her feet. “Who the HELL are these old coots?” Yoshihiro folded his arms, turning his attention to her. “These two old coots just so happen to be your grandparents.” Boy, that was a shocker for her. Anarchy’s face flipped upside down as she gave a huge smile at Yuki. “Er….HIYA GRANNY!” “Granny” turned away from Anarchy for Akiyama. “Where is the other daughter? I want to see if she is as inept in fighting as this one.” “Maybe we can find out…as soon as she stops watching us.” Yuki was the only one not stunned by his statement. Yoshihiro closed his eyes with a nod, agreeing with himself. “COME ON DOWN, YOUNG LADY. AND BRING YOUR FRIEND WITH YOU.” All eyes turned upwards to the alcove, where Cyclonica stood with a staggered look. But her friend didn’t seem so shocked…none at all to say the least. In fact, she was as cold and cool as her name insinuated… Glacia…
