Revisitation...

27 August 2002

Date: 08/27/02 The crowd roars. The commentators all stand in awe of the spectacle they saw in the ring. It took 7 minutes, but they fell. Oh, how they fell. Dave: What a show! Jeff: That wasn’t a show, that was the New Human Highlight Reel of PCW in action! “Bombshell” by Powerman 5000 rocks the speakers as the camera flashbulbs illuminate the ringside crowd area a bright blue. There were many high spots tonight, the new Human Highlight Reel of PCW gave the crowd any number of great moments to take home with a smile on their face. And there he stands in the middle of the ring, his championship belt around his waist, his opponents unconscious at his feet. Dave: The End successfully defends his Cruiserweight Title, and I’ve got to say that I don’t know if anyone would want to challenge it after what we just saw! Jeff: Are you kidding? He might as well retire it now, no one wants a piece of that! The End stands victorious, having successfully defended the Cruiserweight Championship against Brandi and Mark Makonie. It seemed they were uncharacteristically weak tonight... Or perhaps that The End was uncharacteristically violent... EMT’s are carting his opponents away. They have many broken bones and won’t be wrestling for quite some time to come. He didn’t mean to injure them so... it wasn’t his intend coming in. Something just... took over. Strange roars and growls fill his ears... A womans screams in horror and pain. There is strange chanting in some unknown tongue that sounds Latin, but too fast and broken; almost demonically visceral. “What’s... What’s going on?! Sandy?!” The End moves all around, but he can’t make out anything. The heart-wrenching sounds seem to come from everywhere and nowhere. He knows he is running, but he can’t feel the ground, can’t tell where he is going if indeed he is. “Where am I?” A voice comes to him in the emptiness. It’s whispered to the point that gender is indeterminant. Give into what you are. “Where is that voice coming from? Who’s there?” An object flies at The End. He catches it. Liquid flies into his face and drips into his open mouth. Blood... It is a body. He feels the face. Sandy... Anger wells up inside The End, and he pulls his sword from it’s sheath. “Who are you? Where are you? Face me!” The voice laughs. Never taking responsibility... Never accepting the truth... Sandy’s head falls back. The End holds her head up. It completely separates from the shoulders. The End backs away and touches his blade. There is already blood upon it. How does it feel to destroy that which means so much to you? “I didn’t do this!” The End drops his sword and starts to run in the opposite direction. The blade doesn’t even make a sound when it hits the ground, though there’s no way of knowing it did in this vacuum. The voice doesn’t get farther away, and the smell of blood is still as pungent as ever. In fact, The End is covered in blood from head-to-toe, completely drenched it. “What’s happening?!” Sandy Wong’s head falls back into The End’s hands. He drops it, but it seems to fall right back into his hands. As though some force were holding it up to his face level, the head is now staring straight into The End’s eyes. He can see it! The head is half decomposed, soaked in its own blood. The right front lobe is hacked off, flies and maggots overflowing from within and eating the brain and the right eye. The smell is putrid, The End cannot stand it, but try as he might he cannot release the head. The eyes look right at The End and the severed head of Sandy Wong speaks to him. “I never should have gotten involved with you,” it says. “You are a destructive force to everything around you.” The End shakes his head. He’s crying tears he hasn’t shed in years. “No... that’s not true...” Sandy Wong’s head vanishes into thin air (come to think of it, The End hasn’t even breathed since he can remember this ordeal started, nor has he felt the need to). He takes his arms down to his side. Two figures appear in front of him. They’re extremely decomposed and the acrid smell of rotting flesh is so concentrated that The End actually falls to his knees, choking. The bodies in front of him are standing in place, the limbs hanging at the center of gravity below them wherever it may be. They are beyond recognition, but one is shorter with wider hips than the other one. The taller body releases a ghastly moan, one of torture and pain, sadness and misery. It is scratchy, but male. “You... did this to us...” The End looks up, wide-eyed and sick to his stomach. He recognizes the voice. “Dad?” He crawls forward to his deceased mother and father... but gains no ground. They remain the same distance apart. His mother chimes in. “We died... because you wanted... to go to the park... instead of staying home like... we had planned...” The End stands to his feet and walks, his arms outstretched trying to reach them. He speeds to a jog, and then a full run. But they always keep a distance of 20 feet from him. “No! I couldn’t have known! None of us could! Where are you going?” Suddenly, The End reaches the edge of a cliff. Below, there is fire and brimstone. The End has to stop himself and regain his balance to prevent falling in. He can see down the edge. The pit stretches as far forward and down as to be infinite. There are loops of flames that lick the pitch black sky above it. The pit is filled with bodies, all burning alive for eternity. Every one of them... They are all dead because of you... The End looks up. He is in the pit. The fire, the faceless agonizing souls... they are all upon him. Arms and hands, disfigured and dismembered bodies all bear down upon him. He can’t move. His limbs are pulled apart away from his body. It’s like he’s on a torture rack. “I didn’t do anything!” And that’s the most evil you could ever do... Give into what you are... Pain shoots through The End’s nerve endings as there are sharp pinching sensations that start at his skin and penetrate deep. He’s being eaten alive! And get with the fucking program! I awake on the couch, and I’m back to being blind. A dream... very realistic, but it was a dream. Why do I feel so cold? I never leave the air on... “Sky?” Sandy... it’s good to hear her voice. It’s a shame that she has to stay here, at least I have a big enough place. To be my manager and I can’t even pay her... a true friend. But the company is nice enough. “What the?” I hear Sandy take a step back. She gasps. Moving closer, her steps are cautious, her breathing shallow and broken. Something’s got her spooked. I’m still really cold. “What happened?!” she says in urgent fright as she puts a warm towel around me. It feels good, but seems to sink onto me, and doesn’t flow and move like it should. I put my hands to my head. Blood... all over me. I run my hands down my arms and chest and feel what seems like hundreds of bite marks. I think I’m awake... but am I? I’ve been like this for weeks now... seeming to exist in some waking dream, each one more horrific than the last, each one seeming to leave me with some form of... stigmata by the time I awake. What’s happening to me? For the first time in my adult life... I am afraid. Afraid of what might be happening... Afraid of what I might be turning into... I reach a hand out to Sandy, not saying a word but hoping that she is still there, still safe. As supportive as she always is, she takes it and falls to her knees, hugging me around the neck and most likely getting herself all bloodied up. That... That’s what I’m most afraid of... Hurting those I care most about... I’ve been having some strange feelings. Like there’s something inside of me that wants to hurt people. Craves blood... I don’t know what it is... but it could very well come out on Turmoil... And Fate keep Sandy from being in the way if that happens...
