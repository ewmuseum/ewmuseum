Killin Time on The Road... NBA Ballers style

8 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/08/04 Being on the road is part of this business. Hell, I’d like to take some time off, go back to Orlando, hit the rides at Universal Studios and Islands of Adventure. But right now, that’d be suicidal. Well, not literally, but it’d kill the momentum I have going into the PPV on May 2nd. I’m 2-0 in this Face to Face challenge, with another challenger coming head on at me this Saturday. I’m a favorite to win the World Title, and in doing so, it’d be my third reign in PCW. But sometimes, between the Saturday shows, after traveling from one city to another, there isn’t much to do. Sure, most of the time I’ve got personal appearances for autograph sessions, and well, I’m working on a book too. But there are other times, when even a ‘broken down, should be retired’ 30-ish man needs to cut back, and relax. And tonight’s the night for that. Tomorrow, I’ve got one of them autograph sessions lines up, at a Sports Authority in Memphis, from noon till about 4 P.M. Good shill huh? Oh, by the way, it’s sold out, and I hear there are already fans lined up, waiting to see me. So much for being too old and broken for this business, as my once good friend tells everyone week, after week. So, to relax, I’m doing my favorite pastime. BASKETBALL. ONE on ONE. Playstation 2 style baby. Yep, just picked up the newest PS2 game…. NBA BALLERS. Hell, I hear a rumor that I’m a hidden character in the game. Nah, just kidding. But as soon as Knox gets his ass over here, I’m throwing the disc in, and going to beat his punk ass. “KNOCK, KNOCK” A knock comes to a door behind Boda. The PCW Hall of Famer stands up, lifting himself off the bed he was sitting on. He walks over to the door, popping it open to show Ryan Knox, a pizza, and a case of Jim Beam and Coke. Ryan walks into the room, and plops the pizza on a table near the bed. ‘Took ya long enough Ryan. Where the hell ya been?’ Boda kids around with his pal. Knox puts the case of alcohol into the refrigerator, tossing one to Boda and taking one for himself. ‘Hey man, ya know, I go to town, I order this pizza, and dammit, if everyone stops in to talk to me, to hand out with the greatness that is me.’ A cocky smile comes to Ryan’s face. ‘Bullshit. What’s his name?’ Boda tosses a manly gay joke at Knox. The Future scorns Chris, but the two laugh it out. Boda pops across the room, popping the disc into the PS2. Chris plops himself back on the bed, grabbing the controller off the floor. Knox sits down on the floor, grabbing a controller and a pillow for his head. ‘Dude, how is it that we can play a game, a mere two nights from defending our seeds in the Face to Face tourney?’ Ryan asks the obvious. After all, both men have matches this weekend. Ryan’s being the main event. Boda pushes a few buttons before responding to his stablemate. ‘Actually, I can sit here and play with ease. I’m not worried about my opponent. But dude, come on… you’re facing Propaganda, in the main event… how is it YOU can sit here, eat pizza, drink alcohol and not go prepare for that match. I’m telling ya, Propa is a big tough son of a bitch. If I was you, I’d make sure to work out tomorrow and get ready for the big altar boy.’ Boda let’s Ryan in on the stats and story behind Propaganda. The screen plops on, and Boda puts on Customize Ballers. Ryan looks at Boda, wondering what he’s doing. ‘Come on, I wanted to be Paul Pierce. What are ya doing? And don’t you think you’re taking your opponent this week a little to easy? He’s a big man too.’ Ryan tries to pick a real Baller, only to have Boda override it. ‘Ryan, I’m facing an ex-steroid popping, overweight, balding, shriveled dick, pile of human waist. And besides, if that so called wrestler got beaten by Cal ‘The Busted’ Roberts….. do you REALLY think I need to be worried? But to give ya a preview, I’m creating two players for this game…. One will be moi, whom I’ll play as. And the second guy will be 6’5, 325 lbs, bald and ugly…. The Abyssmal Warrior. And guess who gets to play him?’ Boda sneers at Ryan. The Future shakes his head. ‘Me. Well, bring it on punk. I’m sure The Power of The Warrior will push me on to victory as I dunk all over your ass.’ Ryan sits up, getting ready to tango. BODA THE ABYSSMAL WARRIOR ONE ON ONE FOR IT ALL ‘I’ve heard this game is really realistic. So let’s see. Ya ready? Game on boy.’ Boda and Ryan start to play. The game is shown, with a 6 foot 11 video game Boda dunking over The Warrior for the first point of the game. Warrior takes the ball to the top of the key, bounces it off Boda’s head. But Boda puts his hand out to grab the ball, turns and slams it home for the second basket of the game. ‘YEAH BOY. Four nothing. Bring it punk.’ Boda twirls the left analog stick, mashes a few buttons and laughs again. ‘Come, shoot the ball Ryan.’ Knox growls, and tosses a now empty Jim Beam and Coke bottle across the room. ‘WHAT THE FUCK? He’s slow, he can’t jump. What the hell did ya do? Ryan screams, almost crying with anger. After all, Chris did tell him the game is lifelike. ‘Dude, I tossed in our attributes and stats from real life. I can’t help it if he can’t get it up. Hell, should have called him Kano.’ A loud laugh comes from both men. ‘And guess what… That’s game boy.’ Knox tosses the controller to the floor, and stands, walking over to the table he plopped the pizza on. ‘Fuck that…. Okay, point taken, you’re going to destroy this guy on Saturday. Tell ya what, next game…. I’m going to be Yao, you be Shaq… let’s see who really is better.’ Knox takes a slice and bites into it. ‘Yum… Anchovy and bacon, my favorite.’ ‘That’s fine, besides…. I can’t let you play as The Warrior to much, you might actually make him better. Yeah right. Give me a slice of that pie and a new drink man. We’ll play for real.’ And there ya have it. I’m not to worried about the power-less Warrior. I’m kinda happy I get a warm up match, for much more tougher opponents like Sktizo or The Demon. Hell, when Ryan beats Props, I might have to face him. That’d be fun. More fun than beating his ass on NBA Ballers. Time for another game.
