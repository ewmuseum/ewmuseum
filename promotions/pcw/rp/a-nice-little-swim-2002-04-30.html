A nice little swim

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/2002 NOTE: THIS TAKES PLACE ON MONDAY EVENING. ~Why does Kano fight for the common man, when he is a man who is worth nearly 40 million dollars? Why does he stand up against Tyler Nelson and the rest of the Ultimate Authority when on the outside, it looks as if Kano should be fighting along side them? Simple. Jon Kano has never forgotten where he came from. Jon Kano grew up in a decent neighboorhood, but there was crime in his neighboorhood. After all, he was born and raised in 'Sin City' Las Vegas, Nevada. Just two blocks over from Kano's childhood home was a notorious drug house. Jon's parents did all they could to make Jon and his brother's life more enjoyable and safer than theirs were. Jon never forgot what he experienced in his childhood in Las Vegas. Jon Kano has often said that he isn't a hero, but his dad is. Kano's father spent twenty years in the U.S. Navy and then worked another 22 years as a procurement specialist for the U.S. Government. Kano has always worked for the working man, despite the fact that he is a man of tremendous means.~ *The scene opens up outside of Jon Kano's hotel at the huge swimming pool. Kano is crouched down inside of the pool at the shallow end so only the upper part of his torso and head is above water. Kano's hair is soaking wet from the water in the pool and just seems to be relaxing in the pool as he begins speak.* Kano: What a beautiful day. I actually get to relax for once and enjoy some water therapy to nurse my many wounds from the b*tch-like attacks on the part of the so-called Ultimate Authority. *Kano is suddenly interrupted by a a beautful young blonde woman who slowly and seductively has swam to 'The Man who Rocketh the Mosteth'.* Woman: Hello, Mr. Kano. I was wondering if you have seen my towel. Kano: I can't say that I have. Woman: Oh, well, do you think I could go to your room and dry off? *Kano bites his lip and looks off in the distance, contemplating his answer. He then looks back to the lovely young woman.* Kano: Well, as much as I would love to have you do that, well, I am a happily married man now. Up until about a month ago, I thought that was an oxymoron. Woman: Well, if you change your mind, my room number is 156. *After saying that, the woman slowly swims back to where she came.* Kano: Son of a bitch. Where was I? Oh yeah, now I remember. I have quite a bit on my plate here now. I mean I may as well paint a bullseye on my chest and tell everyone to bring it. In the past two weeks, I have been busted wide open and have had my blood spilled countless times. I have tasted my blood and I liked it. Because I am like a boomerang, I'll go away for a second, but I'll come right back. So this week, I got an extreme title match with forty five other competitors, I guess its that many, and I really don't give two sh*ts, a f*ck, and a god damn about the Extreme Title, so Nelson would be lucky if I even show up for that match. Christ, I don't want a title that has already been soiled by having Karl LeBlanc hold it. *Kano chuckles to himself.* Kano: And on Friday, big match. This match pits three of the best from the old school against each other. There is one thing bothering me, though. I saw Boda's promo where he called me and I quote, 'his so-called best friend.' That troubles me. Does Boda have a problem with me? Does he feel that I have abandoned him? Does he feel that I have stabbed him in the back? Well if he does, the least he could do is tell me this is how he feels instead of saying it on TELEVISION!!! But, I am a forgiving person. I forgive Chris for doing that. That is just the kind of stand up guy I am. But he just wants to put himself into that little zone of his, and he talks about him kciking my ass AGAIN. Thats amazing, considering we are tied one to one in matches against each other. And at least, I have my win over him on tape for the whole world to see, whereas he doesn't have his supposed ass kicking of me on tape. I guess that whole 'Cold War' match was lost in the archives. What a shame, it was a hell of a match. *Kano smiles in that half-sadistic smile of his.* Kano: But enough about my good, longtime, personal friend Chris 'Boda' Sloboda, lets talk about Miyagawa, the other third in this match. Miyagawa, my favorite cruiserweight of all time, what an honor to step in the ring with this man. I can't wait to wrestle him once again. There is just one thing I wish was in this match. That one thing is Ming. Yes, Ming. Why? It is time for a travel through time. Think back to November or December of 2000. It was a ladder match involving several superstars in PCW at the time. One was myself, one was Ming, and one was this newcomer in his first match in PCW, his name was Miyagawa. It was a great match. It had everything. It had suspense, drama, action, injuries, blood, and a variety of great moves from great competitors. But with all the great things in that match, there is one thing in that match that stands out, a thing that has become part of the PCW folklore. That one thing was when I picked up Ming and I actually used him as a weapon on Miyagawa. You see, my two opponents have one thing in common, they have each been World Champion. All three of us would have that in common, if I ever focused on winning the World Title. While these two spent all of their time aspiring to grab that brass ring, to stand at the top of the PCW mountain, I was doing something slightly more important than my own personal glory. I was fighting Tyler Nelson and his corrupt regimes that made up the hierarchy of PCW for the better part of the last two years. I was fighting for the tradition of PCW. I was fighting for the future of PCW. I was fighting for ever man, woman, or child who ever watched PCW and wanted more entertainment for their hard earned money. I fought for MY PCW, because Kano is PCW. You cannot have PCW without Kano and Kano would be nothing without PCW. I fought for others who could not fight for themselves, while my two opponents fought for themselves and nothing else. Some things never change. And if I have to Kanoplex my very own best friend, Boda, to get my point and message across to the greater masses, so be it. And that is why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano hops out of the pool and goes to dry off and retire to his hotel room.* ~Jon Kano, defender of the working man. Oh my God, after he retires from wrestling, he might have a career in politics.~
