Celebration.

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Celebration. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: April 3, 2002 Celebration. When one has a significant event in their life, the first thing they think of is PARTAY! Yes indeed, partying is the greatest way to celebrate anything, and Politically Incorrect know that damn well. However, finding a place to party at times can be a pain in the politician. After driving around for a while, Politically Incorrect have found a place, a small Italian restaurant. William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft are happily partaking of plates of spaghetti and meatballs, the PCW Tag Team Titles resting gently over their shoulders. A quiet Wednesday night in Des Moines is about as quiet as anything. And so, the immaculately dressed politicians are enjoying a quiet evening. The calm before the storm is here. At Spring Fling, Fear Factory, Golden Game, and the Cowabunga Connection will come storming in. Can our favorite politicians hold the dyke? Don't bet against it. ::William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft are both rather proud of their achievement on Fusion. As such, they are sitting in the restaurant enjoying a meal. Mealtime is almost over, as Powell adjusts his cufflinks, and then his newest piece of gold, the one over his shoulder.:: William S. Powell: We did it, Reginald. Just as we stated we would. Reginald V. Lovecraft: We were first in the match. William S. Powell: Election Day for Fear Factory. Reginald V. Lovecraft: The Corruption System for the conspiracy nerds, Misplaced. William S. Powell: A little honest graft for Metallyx.. Reginald V. Lovecraft: The Cowabunga Connection lost the Primary Election. William S. Powell: And, Election Day on the tag team champions, Golden Game. As we said, it was Election Day. And, on Election Day, Politically Incorrect were triumphant. And that is indicated..by these. ::Both men raise their titles high, drawing odd stares from some patrons of the restaurant.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yet, beating every tag team that meant anything in Primetime Championship Wrestling in one evening was not enough. Three of them return at Spring Fling. Having failed to defeat the force that is Politically Incorrect individually, they shall throw their combined might at us in a four way dance. William S. Powell: This is nothing new for me. I'm used to third parties forming a coalition to try and take me down. Yet they will fail. In the 1896 election, the Populist party allied with the Democrats to sponsor William Jennings Bryan, the deliverer of the Cross of Gold Speech, and champion of free silver. And yet, he was still defeated by William McKinley and the Republicans. Moral of the story? More groups does not necesarrily guarentee victory. ::Powell pauses to take a sip of his Cabernet Sauvignon. Sipping the red wine lightly, he places the cup down, and gestures over to his partner.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: We are the incumbents now, PCW. And, as incumbents, we are rather hard to dislodge. Powell here has an iron grip on his seat on the city council. Here's one advantage PCW has on politics. When someone comes to try and unseat you, you LEGALLY have the right to kick the living crap out of him or her, and throw him back into the pile of hopefuls. We established ourselves as the ONLY team meaning anything at Fusion. Since then, Golden Game, and the entire International Incident have been in disarray...it is evident that our victory has altered the current state of PCW. William S. Powell: Indeed, Mr. Lovecraft. It becomes further obvious that we atop the tag team ranks. If Golden Game implodes, we will be the veteran tag team of PCW. The days of the Blood Brothers and Da Westsidaz roaming Primetime Championship Wrestling are over. The fans and superstars have voted Politically Incorrect into office as YOUR tag team champions of the world. And, it is an office that we intend to hold for a VERY VERY long time. ::Both men clink wine glasses.:: William S. Powell: A toast then... a toast to the ongoing reign of Politically Incorrect! Toast to our dominance! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes, Mr. Powell! Our time has begun. Primetime CHampionship Wrestling is in for good times ahead, despite its financial woes. Although, we will warn you now..this reign will be.......POLITICALLY INCORRECT. William S. Powell: Spring Fling shall simply reaffirm what we have known and proved.On Election Day, Politically Incorrect is ALWAYS triumphant! ::Fade to....:: Paid for by the friends of Politically Incorrect!PI: The Reigning Tag Team Champions! ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
