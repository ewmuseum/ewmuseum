Miyagawa in: A Very One Sided Conversation

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th June 2002 Tis days like these that make you sweat. Or so they say. With the abduction of Big Alan Steele by the dastardly Frisco on Turmoil, our intrepid hero (and I don’t mean Rent-a-Hero, Rent-a-Folks) Alexander Miyagawa is facing the prospect of facing down not only his current adversary Xavier “I’m involved with a cult” Kannon but also his long time adversary Frisco “Oh look I’ve pinned Miyagawa with an F-Bomb once again” del biscuit del binohoho. So what would you do in a situation like that? Well, of course, you would receive plenty of phone calls from your worried wife, wouldn’t you? Because after all... David Arquette: WRESTLING’S NOT FAKE!!!!!!!!!!!!! Thank you David. My point precisely. Since it’s not fake and all, it makes perfect sense that the lovely Natalia Miyagawa (of which the film “Birthday Girl” was not based upon) would be worried about her husband Alexander Miyagawa taking on both Xavier Kannon and Frisco on his own without the help of the PCW World Champion Big Alan Steele. So there is none other than Alexander Miyagawa sitting in his hotel room whilst on the phone running up what is probably an extraordinarily large hotel phone bill after he phoned Natalia just to tell her not to worry. Miyagawa: Natalia don’t worry. But on the other end of the phone is a completely different story as Natalia is a woman. Therefore she worries. And could probably worry for the motherland in the worrying world championships and bring home the gold for mother Russia. The camera cuts into a phone conversation shot. You know the half and half shot, with one side of the screen devoted to Miyagawa and the other devoted to Natalia. Natalia: Of course I worry. I’m your wife. Listen, I have an idea, why don’t you get a bodyguard. Miyagawa: Huh? A bodyguard? Natalia: Da, a bodyguard. Miyagawa: I’m a professional wrestler, what do I need a bodyguard for? Natalia: Michael Jackson has bodyguards. Miyagawa: Michael Jackson’s bodyguards were professional wrestlers. So doesn’t that make me just as good as my own bodyguard? Natalia: There’s an agency in Anaheim, I could phone them and get them to send someone to you. Yes, I think I’ll do that. Miyagawa: No, don’t do that, trust me, I don’t need a bodyguard. If all else fails, I expect someone just Duke or something will simply step into the role and take on Kannon and Frisco with me. You never know I might actually pin Frisco for like the first time in my career. Natalia continues with what she previously said without listening to a single thing that Alexander just said. And such is the way of the female of the species. Natalia: I have the number some around here. Do you carry mace? Miyagawa groans. Miyagawa: I’m a professional wrestler. The only type of mace I’m used to is a heavy duty medieval maces if for some reason they get involved in a match. Natalia: You should carry mace. It’s good for self defence. Miyagawa: I once made up my own form of martial arts for fun because Kriegman had too. He has Kriegman-do, I have Miya-jutsu. I even taught people classes in it, although only Steve got the knack of it. People just didn’t like wearing the martial arts goggles. Natalia: So I’ll phone the bodyguard agency this afternoon and get them to send someone out to you. I’ll charge it to your gold card, ok? Miyagawa: You haven’t listened to a single thing I’ve said have you? Natalia: Oh, and also I want to go see the new Nicole Kidman film when you get back, so I already went ahead and booked us some tickets. I guess I’d better book a third for your new bodyguard. Miyagawa: I said “you haven’t listened to a single thing I’ve said, have you?” Then; and only then does Natalia come out with a typically female response. Natalia: Of course I did dear, just I didn’t respond to any of it. Ok, I’ve got to go; I’ve got the book group coming around. There’s a weird little Scottish man in it called Rab Murray. Miyagawa: Rab Murray? Like Andy Murray? But she is already gone. Miyagawa shakes his head in disbelief as he places the handset back onto its stand as he realises that he just had a conversation with himself, and gave probably twenty bucks to the hotel for the pleasure. Plus he’s going to have a beefcaked version of JJ following him around and jumping through doors for him. What a friggin day, eh?
