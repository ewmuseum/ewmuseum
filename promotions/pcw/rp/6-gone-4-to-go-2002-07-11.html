6 Gone, 4 To Go...

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/07/02 Ever since he first attacked the Pharaoh at Wednesday Turmoil last week, something has changed about Xavier Kannon. Feuding with an old enemy seems to have brought out a primal instinct in the Scientologist, and with this he has been sickeningly brutal in his actions against both Titu Xansiv and his wife, but also with members of the general public. Under his commend a man who had only VISITED Egypt was beaten and thrown in the River Hubbard only to be washed up dead 2 days later... After the Dark Age attacked him, he got his revenge by ordering the death of Gangrel... Then on Monday, with their only crime being recording “Walk Like an Egyptian” he had the Bangles gassed. Although in Hubbardville he is not Governed by US law, when these acts happen outside of Scientology’s home city, there is only so mush bribery and blackmail can do, hence Scientology’s legal representatives must now act to quench Kannon’s killer instinct before he breaks the law brought in to stop members taking advantage of Scientology’s clandestine powers... The 10 STRIKES RULE. “Look..... You have killed SIX people in your last THREE PROMOS!!!! This HAS to stop!!!!” Our stop opens up in the offices of Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones... The biggest legal firm in Hubbardville where PCW’s Xavier Kannon, wrestling’s only Scientologist, is being lectured to by his crack legal team... Something he doesn’t seem too bothered about as he tries to repel their fears. Xavier : “Well TECHNICALLY I haven’t killed a single person.... Ellie~! killed Bobby Stormbringer though.. You know that crack head PCW had?” The team of 4 lawyers in front of them look in shock at Eleanor who is sitting on a sofa at the back of the office filing her nails and looking generally disinterested with the proceedings. Eleanor : “Yup... I was all, like, YOU and then I SLAPPED him and his head just like rolled off and I was all YAY ME!!! I just killed a man and he was all.. Well, dead!” The Lawyers just look to the petite and totally harmless looking prep with disbelief that she could do anything like that. “Um... Back to me, I’m the one paying your retainers...” There perplexed glares are broken you a wide eyed and impatient Xavier, causing their eyes to cross the room back to him as he drops back into one of the extravagant dark Red leather chairs that are in the room. Lawyer #1 : “As we were saying... You’ve had 6 people ki-” The hungry, moral less Lawyer is cut off as the Gold Patron Meritorious simply raises a palm to him, not looking at the man but something insignificant that has grabbed hit attention. The Lawyer drops his hands to his side in a submissive gesture as Kannon’s bright Green eyes turn to him, pleased with his obedience. Xavier : “First off.... That guy they found on the banks of the River Hubbard WASN’T killed by me... He was killed by some other Scientologists because he went to Egypt and I hate Egyptians for you information. From the back of the room Ellie~! is heard talking to herself as she continues working on her nails. “For...... Your...... Information!” Again the team of 4 smartly dressed “Legal Eagles” are distracted but a finger click from Kannon brings them back to him. Lawyer #2 : “So you actually ordered them to kill him?” Deep in thought, the Scientologist ponders it before nodding and looking back at them. Xavier : “Yeah.. I suppose you can say that.” The Lawyers confer with one another as Kannon runs a hand through his long Red hair, not used to, or liking being the one questioned. Lawyers as One : “THAT’S ONE KILL!” Kannon goes limp in disbelief and narrows his eyes. Xavier: “Oh COME ON?!?!?!?” His protest is met with death ears by the stern faced employees of Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones who continue browsing their profitable clients file as Ellie is heard chuckling to herself, taking a brief second away from reading Cosmo to make sure her nails are right. Eleanor : “That’s...... So....... One!” Her husband’s head snaps around o look at her but her giggling face is already hidden behind the magazine. The Gold Patron Meritorious sighs and shakes his head before turning back to face his inquisition. Lawyer #3 : “Now... Tell us about this Gangrel.... His death has also been traced back to us.” Kannon rolls is eyes, already tiring of not being in control of proceedings. Xavier : “That was all above boar, you can check with Scientological Killings ‘R’ Us, they have all the paper work.” The 4 smartly dressed men before him confer again, their words just a dull murmur to the pathetic Scientologist. Lawyer #4 : “That’s also a kill I’m afraid.” Kannon throws his arms up into the air to exclaim his disbelief. Xavier : “NO WAY!!!! He was a VAMPIRE for Hubbard’s sake, the LIGHT killed him!!!! All I ordered was for them to kill him but they never REALLY got the chance because as soon as they opened the curtains he fri-.... Okay, I’ll give you that one.” He then gestures for them to get on with the next case and one of them pulls out a sheet marked “THE BANGLES“ from the rather large folder that have for Kannon. Lawyer #1 : “Now.... We had to do a BIG pay off to stop the Bangle’s record company kicking up a fuss.... Now, I believe you gave the order for them to be killed on Monday?” Kannon nods rather too casually for someone just asked that question. Xavier : “Yup, sounds right.” Again there is more discussions within their group as Kannon just lifts up a leg to admire his shoes before they again attract his attention. Lawyer #3 : “Tell us Mr Kannon, what were the circumstances leading up to their untimely deaths?” Even though it was only a few days ago he has so little remorse.. Or even a sense of caring about it that he has to think hard to remember it. “Um.. They were alive and then they weren’t! How much detail do you want Mr Suity guy!” The bored, whiny voice of his wife is again heard as Kannon starts to run through the events. Xavier : “Okay.. They sung “Walk Like An Egyptian” and seeing as I hate Egyptians I was going to get them to change it to “Walk Like A Scientologist” but little did I know they turned into fat drug addicts and couldn’t sing so I just had them gassed instead.” Again the 4 Lawyers weigh up the evidence that Kannon has given them before looking to him and shaking their heads at him, much to his annoyance. Xavier : “WHAT?” Lawyer #3 : “That’s 4 strikes Mr Kannon.” Kannon... Even though not quite knowing what in Hubbard’s name is going on puts his head in his hands, peeking a Green eye through his fingers. Xavier : “Is that WITH the other two?” “The..... Other...... Two?” They all shake their heads in sync. Xavier : “IN XENU’S NAME!!!! So just because I kill SIX people I get SIX strikes?” He pauses for a brief moment before something dawns on him. Xavier : “WHAT IS A STRIKE?!?!?!?” One of the lawyers passes Kannon a sheet of paper which he barely reads as it’s explained to him, Ellie~! whiny voice heard again in the background. “Boooooooooooorrrrrrrrrrrring!” The Scientologist looks back up from the document and the employees of Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones begin to explain. Lawyer #2 : “A Strike is the equivalent of a Major Crime that would constitute a Grand Jury.... You’re allowed 10 of them, but when you commit your 11th the Police are allowed to talk to you...” Kannon looks at them in disbelief. Xavier : “What? So now Scientologists can’t just commit crimes and have you buys bribe the relevant authority’s for us?” All 4 shake their heads. Lawyer #4 : “You are currently on 6... So you only have 4 more during your entire lifetime before we have to urn you over to the Police...” The Gold Patron Meritorious mutters under his breath. Xavier : “So now Scientology has a Judas too...” He gets up, a very reluctant manner to him as he gives them a nod of slight agreement, Ellie heard folding her magazine behind him as she gets up. “Can I get 10 strikes before I have to come to another SUCK fest like this?” She walks up behind Xavier and drapes herself over him, indicating she is keen to leave and Xavi kisses her on the cheek in acknowledgement. Lawyer #1 : “So are you clear about the new situation?” Kannon hakes his head in defeat. Xavier : “YES..... I have 6 strikes... Can I go now?” They all nod and with his wife on his arm leaves the room, closing the door behind them as we fade to Black. “Urgh... You know what I feel like?” “I.. Like... Dunno...” “KILLING SOMEONE!!!” As we hear the sound of 4 worries Lawyers sprinting after him we finally hit Black.
