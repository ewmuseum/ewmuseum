Reconstruction: Part III

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 24, 2002 Previously... Two very big black men came driving down in their cart and climbed out. They stood nose to nose with Jack and Johnny, and Johnny standing at a whopping 6’5”, only reached the shorter one’s chin. Just as they started screaming, a TO BE CONTINUED... sign flashed on the screen as it faded to PINK! ...end highlights. Jack and Johnny both looked at the men and looked back at each other. They quickly hopped in the cart, Johnny shoving Jack over to the passenger’s side, and sped away...running over each of the black men’s feet in the process. The scene fades out and what fades in is an outside shot of The Heartbreakers home in Chicago, Illinois, the night before Fusion. A deep narrator’s voice engulfs the scene: Sometimes men are brought together like parcels in a basket. Sometimes they grow together like blossoms on a bush. Then, of course, they really belong to one another, because they have the same life in them. The men in this house have been growing together for a long time. It is at least a year since the first time of their meeting, the story of a team coming together, to unite as one. It has been a slow task, because the right chemistry between the two has not always been easy to find, and both Jack and Johnny wanted to keep free from conventionality in the thought and close to greatness in the picture. It is enough to cause a man no little shame to see how small is the fruit of so long labor...the labor of capturing the tag team titles for a second time. It would have to begin with the dethroning of a new team, a supposed new era. Shots of The Dark Messiahs fade in. The team that everyone has been saying will be the “next best thing.” What about Jack and Johnny? What about the Heartbreakers? Where was their moment in shining glory? Was it as short lived as the hour that they got to hold on to those prestigious PCW tag team titles? Would they be booted right out of the spotlight the second they stepped into it? Not if the PINK! had anything to say about it. Fade in to inside the house. The boys were abed and sleeping...in separate beds you perverts! The clock on the wall ticked loudly and lazily, as if it had time to spare. Outside the rattling windows there was a restless, whispering wind. The room grew light, and dark, and wondrous light again, as the moon played hide-and-seek through the clouds. The boys, wide-awake and quiet in their beds, were thinking of the match tomorrow night, and its significance. 'It was not what I expected,' Jack said to himself, 'that was not the thing which filled me with so strange a longing. I am not greedy like the pitiful Tyler Nelson, but it just didn’t feel right,” Jack continued to think. As if on command the camera shifted to Johnny’s room, and he was thinking about the same thing, almost as if their thoughts transitioned into one another. “But the title belt is what I long for. I can think of nothing else. Never have I felt so before. It seems as if I had been dreaming until now -- or as if I had just slept over into a new world. It seems as if I missed out on my dream, even though I was there,” Johnny added. The camera shifted back to Jack’s room. “We only had them for an hour,” Jack said. Shift back to Johnny’s room. “An hour...” Johnny mysteriously added, as if it went with Jack’s thought. As the camera continued shifting back and forth between the two rooms, their thoughts began to coincide so well that the cameraman could make a sentence out of both of their thoughts. “There,” Jack said. “is,” Johnny continued. “only...” “...one...” “...thing...” “...left...” “...to...” “...do,” Johnny finished. “Destroy all who decide to mess with the PINK!” They both said together. Of course the Heartbreakers were speaking of every other tag team that they would have to defeat in this “Round Robin” tournament set up by the greedy Tyler Nelson himself. The Heartbreakers were number one contenders since they were the champions last. But then again, who cared for champions in the old world where they used to live? No one wanted those belts more than The Heartbreakers, but what did they matter. That was the old PCW, and this is a completely new look for the organization. “Out with the old and in with the new” as the old saying goes. The Heartbreakers would be lucky if they made it past the first round. Gradually the boys lost themselves in sweet fancies of becoming champions again, and suddenly he found themselves again, in the charmed land of sleep. They wandered in far countries, rich and strange; they traversed wild waters with incredible swiftness; marvelous creatures appeared and vanished; they lived with all sorts of men...not in that way...in battles, in whirling crowds, in lonely huts. They were cast into prison. They fell into dire distress and want. All experiences seemed to be sharpened to an edge. This prison was the very place they were almost two months ago when they formed a tag team and came back to PCW. They very bottom...the pit without glory or praise, and most importantly...the pit without PCW gold. This prison didn’t even have the PINK! As the nightmare plagued each of them throughout the early morning, to make matters worse, flashes of the Dark Messiahs defeating them in New Jersey in an almost eerie, prophetic way, haunted their dreams. As pictures of the title belts floated off into the air popped into their heads, they went running after them...trying to fly, but failing miserably. Seconds later both of them popped up out of their PINK! satin sheets, dripping with sweat. Simultaneously, they ran into the hallway to tell the other about the dream, however they just stared at each other in silence...as if they both knew. They walked downstairs in PINK! robes to get the morning started with a nice fresh pot of coffee. “Hey Jack, you’re never going to believe the dream I had,” Johnny said. “...I think mine is a little scarier,” Jack obliviously said. As the two sat down to discuss their same dreams of failure, the scene faded out and the “TO BE CONTINUED” text graced our presence yet again as the screen went to PINK!
