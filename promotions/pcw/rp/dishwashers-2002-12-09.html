Dishwashers

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 9, 2002 [It's a proven fact by the bodacious televison gods, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy equals ratings. Well, so Tommy likes to think. Ever since becoming a totally radical singles star Tommy has been under the impression that his heightened image is due to the high ratings PCW television receives when he's on there. Even if it's just his tiny little video clip in the opening credits of Friday Fusion, the people are tuning in JUST TO SEE THAT! It's not like they even care about Clyde, Rent-A-Hero, Duke Williams, Xavier Kannon, The End, Rush Mason, or Tim Walden...they are only tuning in to see Tommy...the sole reason why PCW is even floating on it's own. Tommy does know he lost on Fusion, lost the title that should be around his waist at this very moment. It's all right thought because it's not going to be a very hard task to regain the title that is rightfully his. He already beat Rush Mason in singles competition a week earlier, and by default that makes The Tubular One the Coninental Champion. Well, uncrowned for right now. Tommy was betrayed by The Chef and Tim Walden on Fusion, which hurt his righteous chances of holding the title. It doesn't matter does it, dudes and dudettes? Lets face facts, Tommy has been getting screwed out of titles ever since he stepped foot in PCW. It's like some kind of curse that hung over like a totally bogus THING. It never left ever since that tag team title match against Politically Incorrect at March Madness. Now Tommy just shrugs it off like it's nothing, like it's happened to him thousands of times, as if it's like breathing...it's expected] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: TOTALLY BOGUS CHEF DUDE!!! BOGUS!! BOGUS BOGUS BOGUS!!!! [Tommy slams a dish on the floor and it shatters into tiny pieces, spreading on the slippery tiled kitchen floor. Cooks and waiters look back in shock at the surfer trying to cope with his heinous rage. Tommy is wearing a dirty, wet apron over his blue and yellow silk shirt and blue dress pants. He looks most stylish for a compadre who is doing dishes at Applebees. Another man in an apron walks into the scene shaking his head. He wears a hairnet over his black hair and an apron that is identical to Tommy's. The mysterious hairnet man has a broom and dustpan to pick up the pieces of plate, again] TTT: It's all bogus, Har! Every little thing that I try to make work in my wrestling career ends up being TOTALLY BOGUS! Tag team....BOGUS!! Now...my singles career is getting that distinct heinous odor of...... [Tommy pauses for his former tag team partner, and full-time bro, for him to answer] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Onion rings, man. [Tommy looks at Harry as if Dee Snider of Twisted Sister were coming out of Harry's nose] TTT: WHAT?! Onion rings?! Dude, I was trying to say that it was going to be BOGUS. Which it is! Bogus. Pure bogus. Bogus Bogus Bogus, that's what it is. Harry: Are you sure it's bogus, man? TTT: Totally. [Harry nods, wearing an apron as well. His is clean though as he holds a red and white dishtowel in his hands. The hairnet holds his long brown hair back from getting onto the clean plates, that way Applebees doesn't get into some heinous trouble, via Harry hair. The Heavy Metal One can only chuckle to himself thinking about the moments that transpired to washing dishes at Applebees] TTT: Dude, it's not funny. Harry: Yeah it is, man. TTT: Har, I'm 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, the most bodacious wrestler in PCW! I'm one of the most decorated and gnarly surfers in the world, I'M GREAT!! I have a righteous reputation in this city, in this COUNTRY, and I'm washing dishes!! This is bogus, but not as bogus as losing out in the Continental Title match on Friday. Harry: Your the one who forgot your wallet, man... TTT: Harry... Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Don't remind me, compadre. [Harry shrugs drying off a few dishes, with Twisted Sister songs running in his head. He doesn't mind drying a few dishes for that far out meal he had. Jalpeno poppers, onion rings, two burgers, and a bowl of french fries, man! Now that's a meal of champions. Ever since he retired he doesn't have to watch his weight, not like he did it before, but now he can really let loose. Eat whatever he can come across...well, anything within reason, unless Harry is stoned out of his mind] Harry: I can't believe you tried to feel up that waitress, man. [Tommy rolls his eyes and lets out a sigh, sticking his gloved hands in the water to make sure they get all the excess food off of them] Harry: You tell this babe that you don't have your wallet to pay, and then you tell her she has a nice rack, and then you try to feel it, man...now not even I WOULD HAVE DONE THAT, MAN!! [Tommy looks at the off-white wall in front of him with rage building up in his baby blue eyes. It's bogus to think that the waitress was not digging him, which is something Tommy has never experienced. He figured it would be a no brainer...just a little Tommy charm and they'd be out of the door by nine. The babe wasn't taking any of it. 'Babe, did it hurt?' 'Did what hurt?' she replied. 'When you fell from heaven, bodacious babe' Tommy said with the cheesiest smile he could muster. All the babilicious waitress could do was put on a fake laugh, humor this good looking guy who is about to give her a monster tip] Harry: It was cool the way those guys charged at us, man. I thought they were going to put us in one of those choke holds, man. You know...like the one we saw on COPS. ['Babe, are those ju ju bees under your shirt or are you just happy to see me?' Tommy said, now changing his cheesy smile to one of a devious child. The girl wasn't humored as he looked down at her fairly large chest to notice she WASN'T aroused in any way. 'Ummm, nope' she said with a look of distaste] Harry: I really think your losing your pimp-like touch, man. It could be because your getting older, you know. Most things get better with age, but I think you've boinked all your greatness out, man. That's not far out...but you had some wild times..... [Tommy looks over at Harry confused about that last statement. Tommy then looks back to the wall, looking at it as if it were his next opponent, Chet King. Some bogus dude riding the fame of the White Mexican...trying to live through that gnarly dude. Chet King is nothing but a totally bogus nut swinger who could never step into the bodacious boots of The Tubular One. He'll make short work of Chet...and then take out the heinous aggression he has out on him as well] Harry: ...you should go to one of those sperm banks, man. Drop some Tubular juice in a cup and save that for a rainy day. ['Come on, babe...I'm sure there are plenty of bodacious things I can do to you' Tommy said with his hand reaching up onto her breast. The waitress didn't know what to do, she was sort of shocked that Tommy would be feeling up her breast. 'Ummmmm....' she could only say as Tommy looked up at her like a scolded puppy dog. Women cannot resist the gnarly puppy dog look, Tommy thought. He's in like sin. 'GET OFF OF ME!' She yelled with contempt in her big brown eyes. Tommy took his hand back as if a totally bogus crocodile were ready to chomp down with bogus force. That's when the bus boy's and waiters ran over to put a stop to the awkward situation. The muscle bound bus boys were ready to put a hurting on the professional wrestler, but, luckily, Harry let out one of those insane sneezes that sent snot flying into their faces. ACHOOOO!!! It was like a hurricane in Applebees, dudes and dudettes. Hurricane Harry struck with enough force to lay out two bus boys. 'OH MY GOD!! I've got snot in my eye!' one of them screamed. Other customers laugh, some look in disgust, but not ONE OF THEM said God Bless You. Bastards] Harry: Thank God that fat guy had a pack of tissues on him, man. There was no way I could use my napkin with all that grease on it...I'd break out like a teenager, man. [Then...after the smoke was cleared. The bus boys cleaned their faces off from the large pools of snot and spit...Tommy learned one thing that he didn't want to hear. 'I HAVE A BOYFRIEND, YOU SONOFA---' She screamed like a banshee, going on and on. Tommy didn't hear anything after the boyfriend part. Having another dude never stopped babes from falling to their knees. Then he figured it all out. Two words were uttered that made sense.... KELLY SLATER!!!] Harry: Isn't that weird that your most hated surfing enemy was the boyfriend of that chick, man? WEIRD! [Kelly Slater, the man who almost dethroned Tommy of his bragging rights of being the best surfer in the world. The man is like Kryponite. Everytime he comes around Tommy it seems that The Tubular One is powerless in every aspect of life. The dude brings down our hero like a depressed alcoholic with nothing left to lose. The babe couldn't see Tommy's greatness, or his pimp-like ability because of the Slater Kryponite that plagued her soul. If Tommy would have known he would have not bothered, and just taken the dishwashing job willingly] TTT: BOGUS!!!!!! [Tommy slams down another plate, smashing it to pieces again. The black haired man with the hairnet walks by again with his broom and dustpan. Harry notices him and chuckles a little bit] Harry: Aren't you getting tired of that, man? Black haired man: No, I freakin' love it. [The sarcastic tone is not picked up by Harry] Harry: That's good to hear, man. You don't find many janitors who enjoy their jobs. THAT'S GOOD FOR YOU!!! [The black haired man nods and carries the dustpan full of plate out of the back door. It leads to the parking lot in the back which is for employees only. Luckily, Tommy parked back there earlier in the night to get a closer spot. The black haired janitor walks up the blue 2002 Mustang convertible and pours the pieces of plate in the driver seat. He walks away laughing as he pulls a cigarette from his T-shirt pocket. The scene zooms on the front seat to show a large pile of plate pieces] Black haired man: He's gonna be pickin' dat outta his ass for weeks!!! [Fade to black]
