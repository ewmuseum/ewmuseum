Reunited Part 2

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/30/03 As afternoon turned into the evening and the sun slowly began to fall below the clouds of nights I looked over at the man I at one time wanted to marry and rested my head on his shoulder as I enjoyed the romantic setting. How I wished things could go back to the way they used to be. I’d do anything to bring the old times back even if it meant getting my assed kicked by Krikorian or losing my chance to take the television title from Dorn. But the old days couldn’t come back and I knew it. It seemed Chris had moved on and so had I, but this opportunity seemed like the right time to try to patch things up and bring them back as good as new but before I could do that I had to get a question out of my mind that had been plaguing me since he had disappeared. “Chris this has been bothering me ever since Spring Fling. Why did you leave and not tell me where you were going?” I questioned as I pulled my head off his shoulder and looked him in the eyes. It wasn’t an easy question to ask but in my mind it needed to be asked to clarify why he felt leaving and never coming back was the best choice. “It was just time to move on.” He answered with a tinge of uncertainty in his voice. Perhaps now that he was realizing that there were better options for him to choose from and that’s why he was uncertain, but I didn’t let it bother me, perhaps for him that was the right choice and that was all that mattered to me, I just wanted to see him happy. But happy with Allison, why was she so much better? Maybe he just wanted to be with Christa and Alex again. “But with Allison, why her?” I questioned, feeling as though I had been stabbed in the back by the person I loved the most. Chris knew I disliked Allison and the many reasons why so why did he feel so much happier with her and why did he feel this was the right choice? “Because Allison can crave my hunger which you could never do. All you ever did was deny me of what I wanted from you the most.” He answered as I shuttered in disgust at the thought of him and Allison sleeping in the same bed. “Oh.” I replied in some disappointment as the thought of him actually never caring for me crossed my mind. If all he wanted in Allison was that then there is no doubt that was all he wanted from me as well. I sighed as I tried the best I could to block the thoughts of him and Allison being together from my mind but it was not working as well as I wished it would. “But you know Tessa Bear; I love you so much that I’d be willing to come back if you wanted me back.” He said as he patted me on the back as my ears perked up at this offer. Perhaps he really did care. “Are you serious?” I sputtered out, unable to believe the offer that just fell onto my ears. Would he really come back? “Well …” He said as he leaned in closer to me placing his hand on my stomach and bringing his head closer to my ears as his voice dropped to a whisper. “I’m sure you’d be willing to show me the other side of you if I came back now.” He said as his hand crept further up my stomach as he now hovered over me and leaned in so that I was pinned against the hood of the car. “After all you have really no other option now.” He continued as he reached down to the pocket of his blue denim jeans and yanked at a bulge in his pants which was exposed to be a small but threatening knife which he moved closer to my throat so that the cold steel teased me and told me that I had no other option if I wanted to live to make it to Fusion, or even get a chance to take Dorn Tracer’s title from him. “Chris?” I questioned as I stared into the eyes of the man that hovered above me. Inside his blue orbs I could see hatred glowing inside the reflection of the knife that shown in his eyes. I couldn’t believe that I was about to believe to meet my end courtesy of the man I was supposed to marry at one time. This couldn’t be happening, it seemed far too surreal but it was happening and there was no way of waking myself up from this nightmare that was taking place with me as its star and unfortunate victim. “Chris, oh dear don’t you wish? You know you’re quite the foolish one Tessa. Did you forget all about me? Did you forget that I was put on this earth to outdo Chris? Did you forget all about Ein?” His name hissed inside my ears as the memories of him hit me all at once. He was the one I saw at the one the library that day that made Chris’ blood boil and gave me goose bumps with the way he could manipulate Chris into his weakest stage with just his presence. He was the one who always tried to get to Chris through using me as a possible victim or just rubbing it in Chris’ face that he was the so called better twin just because he did not have the same soft heart that Chris had. How could I have been so stupid to forget that he had hooked up with Allison just to get on the nerves of me and Chris? Now I had gotten myself into a predicament which there was no way out of and I was reminded of that as the pressure of the knife pressing against my neck was increased as Ein moved in closer. “So darling what’s your choice?” To be continued…
