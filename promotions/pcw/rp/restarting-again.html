Restarting... again.

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/02 Daily Missive to the Masses™: If there is grass on the field, play ball! [With that simple phrase we cut rapidly from the darkness that surrounds those glowing words that have appeared on our screens. Welcome to Tolby Illinois. Population, eleven thousand, two hundred, and thirty seven. It is also the home to a few minor celebrities. People such as the Shakespearian Actor, Pete Sears, Jim Miller, and Edward Lambert.] [This small town is not known for much in the way of excitement. It is peaceful, tranquil, and almost entirely calm. If you ever watched the movie Pleasantville, then you are close to what Tolby Illinois is like. Well… almost. Tolby is in color.] [It is this small town that a black Jeep Grand Cherokee is currently pulling into. The driver of that Jeep Cherokee?] [Edward Lambert.] [Through the town he drives, the tires of the jeep rolling over small bumps and holes in the road. Past a store called “Kens”. Past the local Sears. Past the government building which holds the courthouse, the police station, and in the back, the fire station. Past everything in the town he drives, until he comes to a turn down a small street which he takes. Down that street, which by the way is called “Horseshoe Drive” he cruises, until at the end of the street, he parks the jeep outside of a house that by now is very well know, his home.] [BloodAngel climbs from the Jeep and after grabbing two black bags from the rear of the Jeep, he slowly makes his way up the pathway toward the house. He puts the bags down on the step and opens the glass door. After a moment of fumbling with his keys he extracts the correct one and slides it into the lock with a click.] [The key does not turn.] [Puzzled he stands there for a moment before trying once more. Again the key does not turn. Now very puzzled he reaches down to the mat before the door (It reads “Go Away” by the way) and pulls out a spare key. Into the lock that key slides but does not turn. BloodAngel puts the key back under the mat and returns his keys to his pocket. Then, he closes the glass door and walks back down to the Jeep. When he opens the door to the Jeep he reaches to the dash and presses a button on the remote for the garage door opener. [The garage door does not budge. Now more irritated than perplexed BloodAngel returns to the door to his home. He opens the glass door and knocks loudly.] [There is no answer, and thusly he knocks again.] BloodAngel [BA]: KIA! OPEN THE DOOR! [There is no response to his knocking, or to his shout. After a few moments he knocks on the door again, harder this time. Still his banging on the door elicits no response. After a few more times of this, Edward Lambert, the man known as BloodAngel shakes his head and closes the glass door as he trudges back to the Jeep with his bags. The bags get thrown into the back of the Jeep again, and he climbs back into the Jeep, and after a few more moments the engine roars back to life, and he tears off down the street.] [And inside the house sits Kia Kupalo. Tears streak her face, and her light brown hair hangs limply about her head. She rests her hand on her pregnant stomach and tears flow all the more.] [The Jeep tears down the road, it blasts through a red light makes a sharp turn beside the courthouse and comes to a rolling stop outside of a small building with the name “Trader Vic’s” emblazoned on the wall. BloodAngel slams the Jeep into Park and hops out of the vehicle. He reaches into the Jeep and pulls out a pack of Lucky Strike Filters and puts them in the pocket of his crimson button up shirt before closing the door. Then, he walks to the door to the bar, and steps inside.] [If anyone saw the inside of the bar for the first time, they would instantly classify it as seedy. People play pool off to one side of the bar, pictured, most of them very old line the walls, and an old style long wooden bar dominates one entire wall. This is the type of bar where one would expect to see pool sharks and other undesirables hanging out at.] [However, each and every single man and woman inside this bar is wearing a police uniform. Everyone except BloodAngel and older man standing behind the bar. Heads look up as BloodAngel enters, some hands raise in a wave, others simply turn back to what they were doing previously. The man behind the counter stares at BloodAngel and what was apparently a smile, fades from his face as BloodAngel walks up and seats himself at the bar.] BA: How goes it Vic? [Meet Vic Dawson. Retired police officer, now living his dream of owning a bar. The gray haired sixty six year old man stares at the man who has seated himself in his bar.] Vic Dawson[Vic]: Edward… Have you been home yet? [BloodAngel nods his head.] BA: Yeah, Kia changed the locks on me. Scotch, on the rocks. [Vic shakes his head.] Vic: Not today. Kia was in here a week ago and I promised her that I wouldn’t serve you another drink for quite some time. [BloodAngel stares across the counter at the man who watched him fall face first through a table the week of his winning the NWC World Title from the useless slug Lance Marshall. He pulls a cigarette from the pack in his pocket and lights it with his monogrammed Zippo.] BA: Did she say why? Vic: Said she wanted you sober when she decided to let you back into the house. [BloodAngel stares at Vic with the cigarette dangling from his lips, the smoke rising from it wreaths his head like a halo.] BA: She wanted me sober when she decided to allow me back into my own home? Vic, do you have any clue what I have been through these past four weeks? [Vic shakes his head negativly.] Vic: Not really, I don’t follow wrestling, you know that. BA: The NWC closed, defunct. Dead, I lost my job. So, I took a new one, I'm working for the PCW, a far cry different from the NWC that I know, new faces, new competition, new people who have no clue who the hell I am, the worst part of it all is trying to once again recreate myself into who I am supposed to be. I loath having to restart my career. [Notice please, BloodAngel is angry. It is not a good idea to get him angry. Chef take note.] [Vic stares at him, the rest of the patrons stare at him, and if you could believe it, the mexican guy who is there to drop off a shipment of beer is staring at him.] BA: Ok, so maybe I spoke kind of loudly there. But would you please, pour me the fucking Scotch, and tell me what the fuck is going on! [Vic reaches under the counter and pulls out a glass. Into that glass goes ice, and a slug of Dewars Single Malt Scotch. He slides it across to BloodAngel who immediately knocks back the drink.] BA: Another. [Vic pours another drink, BloodAngel knocks that one back. Vic pours another and once again BloodAngel knocks it back. Then, he takes a drag from his cigarette and stares hard at Vic.] BA: Alright, I feel a bit better, now explain. [This is the point where we are going to fade away from Edward for a moment and focus on Kia. She sits inside their house, on the black leather couch. Her face is streaked with tears and shuddering sobs still rack her body from time to time. Held in her left hand is a white cordless phone, currently it is pressed against her ear. This took place three days ago.] Kia Kupalo[KK]: Mom? [We do not magically get to hear what the person on the other end of the line says, but we can imagine.] KK: Mom, [her back heaves again] I want you to come stay here for a few weeks. [A pause. In light of the situation, you could almost call it a pregnant pause.] KK: I don’t want to see that bastard for a while. Not after what he did. Not right now. [We fade back to the bar where BloodAngel is currently seated. Vic has poured him another drink and BloodAngel stares into the glass as Vic speaks.] Vic: So a week back Kia got this letter in the mail. It wasn’t really a letter, it was more of a package. BA: Dear lord, I was joking about mailing Ibrahim Seck to her! [Vic stares at him oddly and shakes his head.] Vic: No. I don’t know what an “ Ibrahim Seck” is, but it wasn’t one. The package held photographs. Ten pictures she told me. [BloodAngel nods and knocks back the Scotch. Vic refills it as BloodAngel speaks.] BA: Ok, so someone sent her some pictures. So what? [As Vic stares at BloodAngel his features harden, and the small smile it held fades.] Vic: Pictures of you with another woman. Some Asian Woman. You were holding her in a most… familiar manner. [Right about now is when BloodAngel’s jaw hits the floor and we fade back to Kia.] KK: I can’t believe that he cheated on me. [Back to BloodAngel.] BA: Asian Woman? The only Asian woman I even know is Akane, the girlfriend of Hiro, and I would rather have my balls cut off and shoved into my mouth than sleep with her! [Vic shrugs his shoulders and stares at BloodAngel.] Vic: I told her she was being silly. But she showed me more pictures. Pictures that put you in… shall we say a compromising position. BA: So, what you are saying, is that someone sent Kia pictures of me with an oriental woman? [Vic nods, then he reaches under the counter and pulls out a thickly stuffed manilla envelope. This he slides across to the soon to be father who sits before him.] Vic: Take a look. Not very pretty. [BloodAngel lifts up the package and pulls out a stack of pictures. He leafs through them slowly staring intently at them, one after another.] BA:[muttering under his breath] fuck… fuck… what the fuck… fuck… [The first picture that comes up is BloodAngel, holding Akane (Pronounced A-KANE) with one arm around her throat, while his hand ruffles her hair. The second, BloodAngel with one arm draped across her shoulders with the both of them grinning from ear to ear.] BA: Those two are easily explainable. [Picture three however is not. BA and Akane are leaned close over what appears to be an intimate dinner, a half eaten steak rests on his plate, a fish of some sort sits on hers. Their faces are a few scant inches apart and the two of them appear to be having a conversation. BloodAngel is grinning his nastiest grin, and Akane is grinning her most wickedly.] BA: Crap. That is when Hiro had run to the john! [Vic holds his hands up and shakes his head.] Vic: It’s your story, tell it how you want to. [Flip, BloodAngel turns to the next picture. Akane sits beside him on a park bench, BloodAngel is obviously drunk and is laughing up a storm while Akane stares at him with a smile.] BA: That’s it, I’ve seen enough. [He shoves the papers back into the envelope and pushes it back to Vic.] BA: Did it say who sent the pictures? [Vic nods and produces a small slip of paper with a name and an address printed atop them.] BA:… [BA stares at the name and knocks back the latest Scotch.] BA: Akane Kawahara. [He looks up at Vic.] BA: I just got set up by the bitch from hell. [Vic stares at him and nods.] Vic: I tried to tell Kia that. [BloodAngel stares at him.] BA: And? KK[From behind BA]: I told him I was going to make a call. [BloodAngel spins around and stares at his pregnant wife. Then he stares at the woman who stands behind her. The glass of Scotch in his hand falls from deadened fingers and shatters on the floor at his feet.] KK: I called my mother. [BloodAngel stares at Mary Kupalo. If anyone in the world could be considered his truest nemesis, this woman would be that person. She smiles sweetly at him.] Mary Kupalo[Mary]: Hello Eddie. Can’t you say hello to your mother-in-law? BA: Jesus H. Christ. And I thought one bitch trying to ruin my life was bad. [Fade.]
