Pumping up for Mall Brawl!

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 17, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within yet another sun filled day with little wrong with the weather. Apparently we're still in the land of the rising sun as the scene is of a small shopping center that must be down the road from some huge structure as vehicles are speeding up and down the road at a high rate and there are so many of them. The market place is extremely small, but seems to resemble some of the ones that you might see in New Orleans where they have market set-ups almost everywhere downtown. These markets are selling grocery items, such as fruit, vegetables, pre-made sandwiches, fish, and other food items. Semore Smoke is now plainly seen as he towers over all of the Japanese people around him walking down the street. He looks a little glum as he had lost the Extreme title just a couple of days earlier, but he has a chance at a higher prize and that is keeping his hopes up for the moment. After the untimely promo from his Sapporo hotel room, he had seem to go a little crazy, during his match with Skylar Green also known as The End, he had done all that he could to put him away, but The End just kept coming. Well, now it's his turn to keep coming, with a crazed feeling in his bones, he continues to search for yet another Primetime Championship Wrestling championship, this one being the number 2 prize in the game, the PCW Continental title to be exact, currently held by the now gothic Front Office President, Duke Williams. Smoke continues to walk by the small fruit stand until he gets to the last wooden set-up. He stands at the counter looking around at the various food products, but you can tell that he doesn't have food on his mind. Smoke gives three raps on the counter with his fist and a man appears from the back, which was blocked by a curtain, and comes up to the front. The man says something in Japanese, but Semore can't comprehend it… Semore Smoke: Sorry, do you speak English? {{{{The man smiles and nods his apologies to Semore Smoke…}}}} Man: Yes, I sure do Mister. What could I… err… be of service of? {{{{Smoke looks around at the food items and smirks…}}}} Semore Smoke: Do you have any food for the soul, my good man? Man: Ah! So that is why you have come. I should have recognized you to begin with, you are that Semore Smoke fellow from PCW are you not? Semore Smoke: Yep, that's me. So do you have the stuff old man? Man: Just a minute. {{{{The man looks around and when he sees that no one is watching, he heads through the curtain and back to the back again. Smoke waits several seconds as the man looks for the item that Smoke has requested. Smoke starts to tap his feet on the sidewalk as he waits and even begins to look impatient. The man returns through the curtain after about a minute or so and in his hand appears to be a small bag of some sort. As he comes closer to the counter, you can see that the contents of the bag are green, greener then that of what Smoke would normally see in a small baggy such as this…}}}} Semore Smoke: That's the stuff. I heard you get your shit from Vietnam the same shit the United States soldiers were on during the Vietnam War. Man: That's right my man. This is the purest marijuana you'll find in the whole world its more potent then most hydro plants. Semore Smoke: Great, I'll take it then! I'll need every bit of it for my match tomorrow. Man: Ah, so you are participating in the huge PCW Mall Brawl event that is taking place right down the road at Grandberry Mall? I wish you the best of luck, Mister. I believe you lost your other title to someone earlier this week though, no? Semore Smoke: Yeah… {{{{The clerk rummages to hide the baggy inside a brown paper bag so Smoke can carry it out without anyone really noticing. The man even throws a few Apples into the bag for god measure…}}}} Semore Smoke: I'm still a little depressed about that, I should have won that match… I mean come on! The guy is blind! I beat Bernard Colt for the title, I then beat the former World Champion of the Hardcore NTICW, and then I beat the voodoo master of PCW himself, Rush Mason. But when it came to Skylar Green, Ah! I don't know how he did it! But no worries, I'll win the PCW Continental title for sure, and put the record straight. {{{{The man finishes with the packaging and concealing of the illegal drug and then looks up at Smoke…}}}} Man: That'll be fifty American dollars. Semore Smoke: That cheap eh? All right, here it is. {{{{Smoke rummages in his pockets and pulls out a roll of cash, he pulls a crisp fifty-dollar bill from the roll and throws it on the counter. He then grabs the brown paper bag with the stuff in it, and walks away with a smile on his face as the man behind the counter says…}}}} Man: Thank you, come again. {{{{Smoke reaches into the inside pocket of his brown leather jacket and rummages around his white t-shirt for something. He finds it and smiles and even wider grin, he brings out his cigarette case, or for him, his weed case, and opens it up. Inside is an assortment of joints that are already rolled. He takes one and sticks it in his mouth, he takes a light from the case and lights the joint up. He then puts the lighter back and then snaps shut the case and puts it back in his pocket. He takes a long puff on the joint, and then lets the green smoke pour out of his mouth. He walks down the street a little ways, passing several Japanese people, some dressed in business suits, other in street clothing. There are no more small shops to be seen, and now just huge buildings have began to pop up, most of which are for technological discoveries and such, in which Japan ultimately leads the world in. Smoke continues to smoke away at his joint, not getting any looks from anyone as he walks down the street doing so. It seems that the Japanese people really don't care if you're smoking the green weed, but if you're selling it, then they pounce all over you faster then Tempest in a room full of men. (OOC: eW24-7 Chat joke, :p). Suddenly, the sidewalk broadens up. The tall buildings stop for a second, and a long driveway leads to a double parking lot into what must have been a field at one time. There standing is the mall that Semore and the rest of PCW will fight in for the coveted PCW prize. He looks at the oversized mall with anticipation in his eyes, and then says to himself…}}}} Semore Smoke: 'Tomorrow, tomorrow, I'll love ya tomorrow, you're only a day away!' Tomorrow, I rewrite history, I will win the PCW Continental title, PCW, watch out, because the long time veteran who has always awaited this chance since joining the PCW family, is on the loose. {{{{The scene fades on Smoke as he once again breaks into hysterical laughter like in his last promo. This time he suddenly stops the laughter and gets a serious look in his eyes as he stares at the mall, he takes a long puff off the joint, and sits there, contemplating his next move…}}}}
