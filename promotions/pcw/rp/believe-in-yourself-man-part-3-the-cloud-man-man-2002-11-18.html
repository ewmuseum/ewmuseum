Believe In Yourself, Man - Part 3 - The Cloud Man, Man

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 18, 2002 [He doesn't have rock hard abs. His muscles aren't bulging out every which way. He doesn't spend time underneath the tanning bed making his skin look like a tender cooked turkey on Thanksgiving. He doesn't see the point in doing all of that, actually he is just too damn lazy to even do those things. What he is, physically, is a chubby, pale, mess of a man. And he's completely fine with that. Cruiserweight?! That's how everyone responds when he tells them about the tournament that is happening. They reply as if Harry were entering the Miss America pageant, which actually isn't a bad idea because think of all the chicks you'd meet. Anyway, it's like these people don't understand that Harry does make the weight requirement to enter into the world of the Cruiserweights. The gut decieves them and they let their closed minds take over. They try to ridicule him, make him feel like less of a man. It never works, of course, because Harry is always high. He's too damned stoned to notice what's going on] ['Heavy Metal' Harry still lies on the greenish generator atop of Hotel California. He doesn't know what time it is, just that it's extremely dark now. He can't see the stars any longer from the incoming clouds, coming due west. Looks like it may rain a little. They need some rain, but not now. Harry is enjoying himself out on the generator, away from that madness in 578. 'TOtally Tubular' Tommy, the surfing genius and Harry's brother, tried to trick him into going to his room. There would be one of the fattest and ugliest women of all time. Harry saw it in a dream sequence, almost like he could feel what was going to happen. He's turning psychic. That's freakin' far out, man! The wind blows a tiny bit and his Twisted Sister T-shirt flutters upward to reveal his pale, white gut] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I wonder if I can predict what's going to happen to me on Tuesday, man? That would be far out. [Harry lies back for a few more moments, wishing that the clouds would part so that he could see the stars. Unfortunately, the clouds aren't going to listen considering that Harry has no power over that. He notices, with assitance from the moonlight, that some of the clouds look sort of like symbols. This is pretty bitchin', man. Harry notices that a small portion looks sort of like a wrestling ring. The ropes accented as another cloud connects with it revealing to be a very muscular man with arms raised in the air. This cloud figure looks read to nail a high flying move. Another cloud comes on to connect and it's a very weird figure...like he's almost fat. More clouds reveal a crowd standing by, looks like these cloud fans are laughing at the fat man lying on the mat. That fat cloud man sort of looks like Harry, but no...that can't be him...he's not THAT fat, man. A Twisted Sister T-shirt appears on the fat cloud man and Harry's eyes fill with horror. That can't be. That muscular man on the top rope cannot be defeating him! Impossible, man. That means that other cloud dude is Gaz Green, a dude who is certainly not bitchin', unlike Harry who is the most far out dude in PCW. It could be a sign though, because he's only on the top rope. Harry now knows a fate that could await him. He will move out of the way, then defeat the totally not far out Gaz Green. Move on to prove to all those non-believers that 'Heavy Metal' Harry will be the Cruiserweight Champion] [Suddenly a few clouds begin floating down from the sky, toward the rooftop. Harry braces himself against the generator, knowing full well what's about to happen: Attack Of The Clouds! They knew he wanted them to go away, to begone so that he could see the far out stars. They have come to extract revenge against PCW's resident headbanger. Damn. So, this is going to be the end of Harry, killed by a couple of clouds. How lame is that, man. The two vapors from the sky merge together and begin forming a somewhat human form. The cloud figure sort of reminds Harry of that far out Michelin Man from those bitchin' tire commericials. Maybe this death won't be so bad after all] Harry: Hey, man. Cloud Figure: Hello, Harry. Harry: How'd you know my name, man? [Harry stares at the cloud with fear, confusion, and curiousity. Needless to say Harry's stubble filled face is contorted in a very ugly position] Cloud Figure: I know many things, Harry. I know that your a recovering sexual addict, you like moonlit walks on the beach with that special someone, and you still eat your boogers. Harry: I DO NOT, MAN!! Cloud Figure: Yes, you do. Harry: Ok...I do...but don't tell anyone, man. Cloud Figure: Your secret is safe with me, Harry. [Harry comes down from the generator that he has spent so much time on. His back feels very relaxed from the constant vibrations, a far out free massage. Harry looks over the cloud man, trying to make out what it looks like. It's just in the shape of a man, but it doesn't have any physical features. It's pretty bitchin' though. It's like that guy from the Dick Tracy movie, No Face] Harry: How come your here, man? Cloud Figure: I saw you down here just hanging out on the generator, I figured that I'd come down and rap with you about some things. Harry: Like what kind of things, man? Cloud Figure: Important things. Harry: What kind of important things, man? Cloud Figure: The important things in life. Harry: What kind of important things if life, man? Cloud Figure: Ugh, I could do this all night, but I'm not going to. [Harry is confused. Important things, about life. What kind? This cloud man makes less sense than Tommy does, but Tommy didn't float out of the sky, so this guy has that going for him, man] Cloud Figure: You have a very big match coming up on Tuesday and I was just making sure that you've got your game face on. [Harry makes a very mean looking face] Harry: See that, that's my game face, man. Cloud Figure: You just look really ugly there. Harry: But it's my game face, man. Cloud Figure: Game face is a metaphor. Harry: So game face is some kind of pill? Cloud Figure: Oh boy... [The cloud figure is quite upset at Harry's stupidity. He knew that Harry wasn't exactly the brightest Christmas light in the long string, but he never realized he'd be this bad. Maybe the year of wrestling has damaged his head more than the cloud man had thought. He needs Harry to be as ready as possible with every aspect of his mind focused on this match, because it's big. This could be the moment the cloud man has been waiting for since he formed from a passing warm front in the Caribbean Sea!] Cloud Figure: Listen, I need you to win this match for me. Us cloud people are huge wrestling fans and we enjoy making very large bets on these matches. It helps to magnify the fun factor of the whole atompsphere, plus there are some very kick ass fights that break out. Anyway, I've placed a very large sum of money down on you in the Second Round of the Cruiserweight Tournament. I need you to win, because if you do then I'm set. I can finally move out of the Stratosphere and move closer toward getting off of this planet. Combine with the moisture and fly out into space. Maybe visit all kinds of planets and see what's going on...traveling rocks. [Harry looks at the cloud man with a very blank stare. Did this cloud just say that he's betting on him? Clouds can bet? Well, ten minutes ago Harry didn't know that clouds could float out of the sky and form themselves into a man. Now they are betting on him. What in the name of meteorology is going on here?] Harry: How much did you bet on me, man? Cloud Figure: I bet two million cloud bucks on you. [Harry's eyes grow extremely wide] Harry: Two million cloud bucks, man?! Cloud Figure: That's right. [Harry seems extremely flattered that someone would go and bet a huge amount of...ummm...well, cloud bucks on him, but there is still something quite weird about this. No, it's not that it's a cloud that he's talking to, he's gotten over that for right now. It is something else. Why would this cloud go and put some much faith into him? He's just some fat guy who everyone makes fun of] Harry: But why me, man? Cloud Figure: Because you've got something special that nobody else can see. It's called 'heart'. For some reason I have this cosmic connection going on and it's telling me that your the man to bet on. That no matter how many times Cliclius, The Cloud God, makes fun of me I'll have the satisfaction in knowing that your the one who is going to come out on top. You may seem like you don't care about what goes on in that ring, but when the time comes you prove yourself each and everytime. Your going to become Cruiserweight Champion, Harry, and it'll be because you believed in yourself. Count on it. [Harry nods with a half smile on his face. Now how come a cloud knows this? Why can't all the chicks here on earth know these things? That he's got heart, chicks would eat something like that up, man. Meh. It doesn't matter. The cloud dude said that Harry was going to get the big W and that's what is going to happen] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE!!!! SMILE!!!! [Harry's brother, wearing his Hawaiian button down shirt and khaki cargo shorts, stands over Harry with a Polaroid camera in his hand. It flashes and Harry is blinded. All that he can see is a giant ball of white light. He can see out of his peripheral vision all right, not well, but all right. He rubs his head and realizes that he's no longer on the generator atop the roof. He's in his bed, with the black satin sheets. His Twisted Sister posters on the wall, naked women, and gnarly cubby holes to store his sweet weed. He can't see these things right now, but he knows that they are here. Well, they better damn well be because if someone stole his stuff there would be hell to pay] Harry: What's going on, man? TTT: Just snapping a gnarly shot of the newest couple, compadre. [Harry looks to his left to see a figure of a woman lying on his bed. IT'S AVRIL!! IT's GOTTA BE!] Harry: AVril!! FAR OUT, MAN! TTT: Totally, dude. Now you two should kiss for a totally excellent snapshot for the photo albums. [Harry doesn't need to think anymore. He dives onto Avril and begins kissing her all over the place. Tommy snaps pictures left and right with the Polaroid pictures popping out like crazy. The group of men and women in the background laugh loudly as the couple makes out. Harry stops for a second to admire his true love, to look into those beautiful eyes and see his destiny. He looks...and it's not Avril....] Harry: NOT FAR OUT, MAN!!! [Harry runs out of his room with speed that he's never shown before. Tommy laughs loudly as the fat woman with the very skimpy black lingerie lies on the bed with a satisfied smile on her face. She had a nice piece of Harry, a long piece of Harry. Tommy laughs and shakes his head, loving to mess with his brother...loosen him up before a big match. That's when Jabba The Slut smiles at Tommy] Fat Woman: Come over here, baby. TTT: That's all right, babe, I'm recovering from sexual addiction. No, meaningless sex. Fat woman: Who said it was meaningless? [That's when Harry's black door closes behind Tommy and the sound of a dresser being pushed against the door is heard. No, they didn't do this. All his gnarly friends have just locked him in Harry's room with one of the most revolting women ever made from God's hands. Totally bogus, dudes] TTT: HELP ME, DUDES!!!!! [Fade to black]
