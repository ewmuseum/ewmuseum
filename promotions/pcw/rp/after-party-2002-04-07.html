After Party

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/02 *Monologue*Spring Fling. A make or break in one man’s career. The one man is desperate. Desperate to make a good name for himself in the PCW. Who’s this desperate man? Big Daveylicious. He sees in the Six-Pack Challenge match for the Extreme title an opportunity. Not only an opportunity to win PCW gold. An opportunity to make a name for himself. An opportunity to become an Extreme champion that doesn’t fight LeBlanc on a daily basis. Karl LeBlanc. Current PCW Extreme champion due to Big D performing a brain buster on Core into a chair giving LeBlanc the gold. Convicted for killing four people including his father. A man who got into the PCW most likely bent over for Nelson and OG. But let’s be honest does anybody actually think LeBlanc is good? What’s that? You don’t even know who he is? Like I said he’s a ratings killer or should I say company killer. Orpheus Grant. Nelsons 10,912 bodyguard and Karl LeBlancs new prison lover. You want to know why LeBlancs quiet? It’s because of guys like OG sticking it to him every time. “The Rush” Rush Mason. Lost to our man, Big Daveylicious before and for the second time he will at Spring Fling. The man calls himself “the Greatest Man Alive” while he hangs out with a redneck and a wash up who’s lost to LeBlanc not once...but TWICE! Sorry, but the Rush is gonna get crushed. Badge. Former stable member of Big D’s in the past. While in the Thugs for Hire Big D had the biggest winning record of the group. Will these two remain friends after Spring Fling? It all depends. Jason Core. Coward? Yes, I mean defending your championship against the likes of Karl LeBlanc on a daily basis doesn’t make you a great champion. It makes you a coward. No wonder Duke signed LeBlanc. So Core can beat him over and over again. It’s sad enough they’ve had 10 match’s against each other. That’s 10 perfectly good match’s wasted. Will Core get his revenge? No, of course not he lost to LeBlanc TWICE! (The scene fades outside of a white stucco, three-story beach house. The house seems trashed as we see trash on the lawn. A various rolls of toilet paper on the house and on the lawn. A taxi pulls up on the driveway. We can hear the engine running as the passengers door opens and out steps a muscular formed man with bleached blonde hair and a black goatee known simply as Big Daveylicious. He’s still in the same clothes as he wore in the mental institution. Khaki pants, a plaid shirt, and a ratty robe. The taxi pulls out of his driveway. Big D looks at his house for a moment and pauses.) Big Daveylicious: What the hell? (He storms off to his open front door. We can still see the lights are on as he walks into the house. We can hear his stereo system booming off dance music. Various beer bottles everywhere in living room. He sees a few drunks passed out laying on his leather couch. He tries to wake them up by shaking them.) Big Daveylicious: Wake up! (No response. Big D than slaps one of the drunks across the face. The drunk falls off the couch. Looks up to see the angry eyes of a mad Big Daveylicious. The drunk puts his hands to his face.) Drunk: What’s a matter wit you dude?! Big Daveylicious: What the hell happened?! (The drunk stands himself up and puts his hand on Big D’s shoulder. Big D seems annoyed by this.) Drunk: Man, you missed a hell of a party. We had beers, naked women... (Big D’s eyes bulge.) Big Daveylicious: NAKED WOMEN?! Did you say naked women?! Drunk: Yeah, dude we had naked women. A whole load of them. Dancing everywhere! (A tense Big D yells at the drunk.) Big Daveylicious: GET OUT! Drunk: I’m not lying they were buck naked! Big Daveylicious: I’m sorry but I’m afraid you didn’t understand what I meant. I meant...GET THE HELL OUT! (The drunk moves a few steps back and goes through Big D’s front door exiting our view. Big D scratches his chin.) Big Daveylicious: A party with naked women in my own home and I wasn’t invited! (Big D slaps the other drunk asleep on the couch. This time Drunk#2 rolls off the couch landing on his back. Looking up at the angry eyes of Big D as Big D peers over him.) Drunk#2: I’m not sucking your dick man. Maybe for a nickel. Big Daveylicious: Ugh. Get the hell out! Drunk#2: Okay! A penny. (Big D hoists the drunk by his collar looking at him tensely.) Drunk#2: Don’t I need to be on my knees for this? Oh, man I don’t (Big D tosses the drunk over his couch.) Big Daveylicious: GET THE HELL OUT OF MY HOUSE! Drunk#2: Party pooper! (The drunk leaves exiting the front door out of our view. Big D shakes his head.) Big Daveylicious: Party pooper? I didn’t even poop in the party. Hell, I wasn’t even here! (Suddenly, we hear foot steps. Big D looks to his left to find who is coming down his stair case and down come the feet of a muscular man with a top hat on known as Mike Frampton. Big D’s starts to close his eyes as he notices Mike has no pants on! Mikes wearing a black thong with a bowtie in the middle of the crotch. Mikes carrying a garbage bag with him and a beer in one hand.) Big Daveylicious: Ughhhhhhh! Mike Frampton: Uh.....Big D your early. Why do you got your eyes closed? Big Daveylicious: Damn it, Mike put some pants on! That’s gay. Mike Frampton: What’s wrong with this world? Can’t a man wear a tuxedo thong in front of his friend without being called gay? I’m just a man comfortable with my heterosexuality. Are you comfortable with yours? Big Daveylicious: Yes. Mike Frampton: Prove it. (Big D opens his eyes to be totally disgusted at the view of Mike. So disgusted he pukes on the floor. Mike shows a concerned look to his tag team partner. ) Mike Frampton: Are you ok? (Big D looks at Mike awkwardly.) Big Daveylicious: Wait a minute... that’s my thong! (Mike shows a look of awkwardness for a moment.) Mike Frampton: Well, uh...I needed something to wear for the party. (Big D takes off the ratty robe he is wearing and throws it to Mike. His tag partner catches it.) Big Daveylicious: Cover yourself for my eyes! Mike Framtpon: Sorry, if you want it back I’ll give it to you. (Mike goes to take off the thong but it interrupted.) Big Daveylicious: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO! KEEP IT! (Mike puts on the ratty robe as Big D looks the other way.) Mike Frampton: Dave, I’m sorry.. Big Daveylicious: Mike...get out of my house and put some clothes on. Mike Frampton: But...Big D if I go I can’t clean your house! Big Daveylicious: GET THE HELL OUT! Mike Frampton: Alright...I’m going! But see who’s cleaning that mess in your room! See who’s gonna sort your socks and clean your dishes! (Mikes leaves storming off out of our view thankfully. Big D shrugs as he turns to his staircase. He goes up his stair case looking at his achievements of his career on his wall all framed. He climbs the staircase until he reaches the 3rd floor of this rather large beach house. He walks through the hallway towards the door of his room. We notice beer bottles hanging around the hallway. Big D opens his to his room to find nothing different about it. His king size bed is there. Nothing touched. He walks towards his bed.) Big Daveylicious: What a shity day... (He yawns sits back on his bed but jumps right off it as soon as he sits down. He has the biggest look of being grossed out. ) Big Daveylicious: UGGGGH! That’s not a pillow! (Suddenly, the bashful face of Big D’s lackey, Michael Graves pulls his head up from the covers.) Michael Graves: I know. Big Daveylicious: UGHHHHHHHHHH! Is there any naked women?! (The bathroom of Big D’s room opens and a naked obsess woman comes out. Big D ) Naked Fat Chick: Did somebody say pizza? Big Daveylicious: I’m blind! (Big D closes his eyes from the hideous sight as we fade into fat.)
