Cigarettes and Alcohol.

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 30, 2002. The scene opens up into another Jackson Rust promo…oh boy. The scene seems to be outside of Maine’s capital, Augusta, in which case we find Jackson, Al, Steve, and Kimberly sitting in a local park, as a band is playing several hundred yards away. Al is sipping on a beer, as Steve gulps down his…on the other hand, Jackson is sitting down with a Pepsi in his hand, as does Kimberly. Jackson Rust: Y’know…I really wish they didn’t have opening acts. Kimberly: Why? Jackson Rust: Do you honestly like watching a band struggle through its career? Narrator: I find cruel humor out of it. Al: Not you again! Narrator: What’s wrong with me? Bathroom-trinket-boy… Al: *sighs* Narrator: Hey, Steve…Jackson, Kimberly. Jackson Rust: Heya. Kimberly: Nice to see that you’ve…decided to join us. Steve: H-h-hey. Narrator: Who’s playing? Jackson Rust: I don’t know…really, but it’s one of those bands that tried out with an intimidating name, but got nowhere fast. Narrator: What’s the band name? Kimberly: Injured. Jackson shrugs his shoulders, as he drinks from his can o’ soda. Narrator: Can’t handle the alcohol, tonight, Jacko? Jackson looks at the Pepsi in his hands, and then proceeds to look at Al and Steve, who are nearly wasted. I mean, being here for three hours, listening to the same opening act, can only lead you here. Jackson Rust: Meh. I was already trashed before the band started. I got bored, grabbed a few Pepsis… Jackson points behind him, where behind the bench they sit at, a rather big pile of crunched soda cans lie. Jackson Rust: And now I’m sobering up. I figure if the band goes on any longer, I’ll get wasted again. Narrator: Oh. Hey, Steve, want to do us all a favor? Steve: W-w-wh-what? Narrator: Take the conveniently placed steel chair, and take out the band up on the stage. Steve, who is definitely closed to be wasted, nods, shrugs, and picks the chair up. Steve: O-oh-okay. Steve, with chair in hand, stands up, and walks away, going to the stage. Narrator: Ten bucks says he vomits before reaching the stage. Jackson Rust: You’re on. Narrator: A’right. As they trio of Al, Kimberly, and Jackson sit back on the bench, they wait…and wait…and wait…and— Kimberly: I think they understand the concept of waiting. Narrator: I don’t know…some of them may be like Chris Storm… Jackson Rust: What about him? Narrator: Oh…oh, nothing. Jackson Rust: C’mon! Tell me! Narrator: It’s nothing, really. Jackson shrugs. Jackson Rust: Fine…hey, Al…how about covering…your…ears? Al nods, and begins to cover his ears. Narrator: No! Al sighs, as Kimberly shrugs, looking out to the stage as Steve starts stumbling in the distance. Jackson Rust: Then are you going to tell me? Narrator: Yes~! Just don’t tell Al to cover his ears, or else no one will be able to hear me! Al: Oh my…we wouldn’t want THAT to happen. Al rolls his eyes. Jackson Rust: Then tell me! Narrator: Fine… Jackson waits…that’s all…he just…waits. Narrator: Ahem. Well, Chris has been having visions of killing you in his dreams. Jackson shrugs. Jackson Rust: Is that all? I’ll just have to explain to him that sniffing aerosol cans is bad for you, and how it can be bad. Narrator: He says you are trying to take Tessa away from him. Jackson is taken back, in obvious shock, and in an uncaring manner. Jackson Rust: Wow…I guess it’s a little more than aerosol cans…huh? Kimberly: God damn it, Jackson…Chris isn’t sniffing anything! He just thinks you’re taking Tessa away from him, because he’s not used to her having a male friend, but he finds it okay if he, himself, as several female accomplices around the house…so in that case, it makes him an overprotective, jealous boyfriend who is hell-bent on destroying you, so that you are out of her life, so that she is isolated with just himself around the house, with his other female friends. Kimberly takes a deep breath, after explaining that extremely long run on sentence. Narrator: I couldn’t’ve said it better myself. Jackson Rust: Can you repeat that? I lost you after, “God damn it”, so…could you? Kimberly glares at Jackson, in a angered manner. Kimberly: No. Jackson sighs, and shrugs. Jackson Rust: So…Chris is trying to kill me because I’m a friend of Tessa? Kimberly: You did hear me! Jackson Rust: I did? Meh. Narrator: Yeah…that’s the basic, overall, brief rundown of his psychotic, idiotic, dubious behavior. He even went to the length of infringing one of you promos, attacking a bum! Jackson gasps. Jackson Rust: NO! Al: Yes…he did. Narrator: But he did it in a more malicious manner. Jackson sits still, not sure of what to do. Jackson Rust: My ideas…my promos…my beautiful promos…ripped off… Al: *sighs* All of a sudden, a loud metallic clanging noise is hear from afar, as the trio turn their attention toward the stage, where they see a figure, wielding a steel chair, taking out the lead singer with a shot to the head! Jackson Rust: Oh…it’s Steve. The figure of Steve then turns around, and throws the chair to the lead guitarist, who promptly catches it, allowing Steve to hit a Van Daminator that would make Rob Van Dam cringe with envy! The guitarist falls back, as Steve then picks the chair up, and wraps it around the cranium of the drummer, who has been staring in shock! Narrator: Nice Van Daminator. Jackson Rust: Agreed. Al: Very nice. Kimberly: I better call up Rob Van Dam’s lawyers, so they don’t sue us for the use. Jackson Rust: No need…just call it the Von Stevenator. Kimberly shrugs. Kimberly: Good idea. As Steve rampages across the stage, knocking the drum set over onto the fallen drummer, the fans go wild. Steve grabs a guitar that was dropped on stage, and swings it maniacally into the stage, unintentionally taking out several of the now-cheering fans! Steve then…well, he then vomits…followed by another cheer from the crowd. Jackson Rust: You owe me ten bucks! Narrator: Dammit. Al: Steve can be rather dangerous when asked to be. Jackson Rust: Good thing he doesn’t do it on his own. Steve then picks the microphone from the stage, and raises it to his mouth, screaming. Steve: I-I-I… The fans are in anticipation to see what the super-duper-unknown-destroyer has to say. Steve: I….g-g-got-gotta t-t-take a-a s-sh-shit! The fired up crowd cheers as Steve drops the microphone, and runs into the crowd, who promptly pick him up, and pass him overhead until the crowd ends, and Steve hits the ground. Getting up quickly, he dusts himself off, and goes into a dead sprint for the nearest toilet. Kimberly: That was a little much, eh? Jackson Rust: Yeah… Al: We are so going to be sued for this. Narrator: And a hefty lawsuit it will be. Jackson sighs, as the scene fades out into black.
