Why Does Trouble Always Follow Me?

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.04.2002 Harlem, New York City. One of the poorest places in the Western world, but it is the place Duzza grew up. And as a song once said, you can take the man out of the ghetto, but you can’t take the ghetto out of the man. Now recently Harlem has become a pretty fashionable place, thanks to the worldwide success of hip-hop. And now they are more white people than ever before. But they’re not living in the ghetto, they’re just pretending. But Duzza has no need to pretend. He grew up in Harlem, and until a few years ago, lived there. He still has many friends back in Harlem, and whenever he can he goes back into Harlem, to keep it real The scene opens on a sunny, spring day. The sun is high in the sky and there is not a cloud in the sky. There is a long, wide road with cars parked along each side and a long row of terraced houses; probably all converted into one-bedroom apartments. The camera zooms to the front of one of the houses. On the stoop sit three, young African American males. They turn out to be Duzza and two friends Pookie and Brandon. All are sitting around, laughing and sipping on something. Duzza is dressed in a pair of very baggy yellow jeans, a tight black wife-beater that exposes his muscular arms and chest, and he has a black doo-rag tied around his head. On his left shoulder he has a white cross tattooed on him. He has his hair set back in long cornrows, which run down his neck. He is also wearing some jewelry. He has two big silver hoops in each ear lobe, a big platinum bracelet, a silver Rolex and he has 3 big platinum chains around his neck. One has a small tag at the bottom with “M.W” – his initials, on the tag, engraved with small diamonds. One chain has a big silver cross at the end of it and the 3rd chain has a silver dollar sign on it. Duzza is lying across the stoop, while Pookie and Brandon are both sitting down opposite him. Pookie, a small guy, with a big afro, is drinking something out of a bottle, in a brown paper bag, while Brandon, who is of average size is just sitting chilling. Pookie: “ Duzz, homie, tell me you did not lose your first match” Duzza: “ F*ck off” Pookie: “ You lost your first match! Damn dawg, you lost to that Asian dude?” Duzza: “ Hey it wasn’t my fault, I had that guy beat, then some ignorant ass cracker jumped him, costing me my match! You believe that” Brandon: “ Damn dawg, you trying to be the black Hank~! or something dawg?” Duzza: “ Oh hell no. Man that fool stole my Bentley! He still ain’t given it back, but nah man, ain’t no way I’ma pull a Hank, I got a match wit the cracker who cost me my match soon, I’ll get his white ass back” Pookie: “ Well you’d better dawg, ‘cause you be representing Harlem dawg, and we don’t want no loser representing us!” Duzza: “ Hey Pookie, I ever told you how much I hate chu?” Pookie: “ Every time you see my dawg” Brandon: “ Forget ‘bout the cracker anyway, ain’t you got a match at the ppv?” Duzza: “ Man, I’m in some big ass battle royal man, for some Rising Star title” Brandon: “ Your gonna win right dawg?” Duzza: “ No doubt! Well, after I kick Ben J. what ever the f*ck his name is, then I’ll go onto win against all them crackers I in the match with!” Pookie: “Y’know Duzz, some people would say your full of shit” Duzza: “ Rather full of shit, than full of that damn alcohol dawg. You ever stop drinking it?” Pookie: “ I’ll stop drinking the day you get me a record deal” Duzza: “ A’ight dawg. Well man, I didn’t come into Harlem just to talk yo your black ass” Pookie: “ Then why did you come into Harlem?” Duzza: “ Man, I have to pick up my gun, you believe my wife tried to clean it! F*cked the whole thing up. So I took it to Gun Shop Jimmy the other week, then he phoned me earlier to come pick it up” Brandon: “ Aww man, and here I was thinking you just missed your homies” Duzza: “ Yeah…anyway” Pookie: “ Well? What we waiting for, let’s go pick up your gun, then we’ll go next door into the liquor store so I can get some more” Duzza: “ Man, who would put a liquor store next to a gun shop? I mean, how many times a day do you think they’re held up at gun point a day in that shop?” Brandon: “ Well, if we don’t include the times when me and Pookie do It, I’d say only once or twice a day” All three of them laugh, as they get up of the stoop and start walking up the street. After walking for only 5 minutes they come to the basketball courts. Duzza stops, and leans up against the chain fence, looking into the courts. Pookie: “ Tell me your not worn out already! Man your supposed to be a athlete, geez” Duzza: “ Man, I ain’t worn out. I’m just looking at the courts. Man, we used to chill on these courts every day. The whole gang man. You me, Jay, Kell, Jamal everyone, we all used to spend are days up here, just balling in the sunshine” Pookie: “ Yeah…that’s nice and all Duzz. But I just drank the last of my juice, so let’s get to getting’ yeah?” Duzza: “ Man, I miss these days. Sh*t done changed man, life ain’t as good no more. Man, all we ever wanted to do was get the fuck out of the hood, and now I’m out. I wanna come back” Brandon: “ Duzz, if you wanna swap the Hampton’ mansion for my one bedroom apartment, then dawg, jus’ holler at me aight?” Once again all, three laugh and continue to walk on. They eventually come to the gun shop. There is a big “Jimmy’s Gun Shop” sign above the door. Inside the shop, guns line the walls. Some hunting rifles, some obviously illegal machine guns. At the front of the shop is a large glass counter, where there are all different types of bullets. The shop is empty, so Duzza rings the little bell on the counter a couple of times and eventually the owner comes out from behind the shop. The man is a small, 5’4ish white man, dressed from head to toe in camouflage gear. He has balding ginger hair and looks and smells like a redneck. Owner: “ DUZZA!” Duzza: “ JIMMY! Hows life man?” Jimmy: “ Ahh, business is a bit slow but that’s life. It seems nobody wants guns anymore?” Duzza: “ For real? Man, I never thought I would see the day. Well bro, I got a bigfoot hunt coming up at the end of the month I’ll need a lot of hunting stuff then man” Jimmy: “It’s nice to know I can always count on you and your crazy Bigfoot hunts. Hey Duzz, did I ever tell you about the time I came to face with a Bigfoot back in ‘69?” Duzza: “ Yes Jimmy. Five or six times actually man” Jimmy: “ Oh… well do you want to hear it again?” Duzza: “ Err, well I’m kinda on a tight schedule here man” Pookie: “ What? Duzz you said you had the entire day free” Duzza: “ Pookie!? You’re a fool” Jimmy: “ Ahh great. Well sit down and I’ll tell you my Bigfoot adventure” About three hours later, Jimmy finally finishes his story. All three men look like they’re asleep, just with their eyes open. Jimmy: “ Yep and that is how I got the name of one eyed Jimmy… damn that Bigfoot… anyway boys, what can I do for y’all today?” Duzza: “ Huh? Oh oooh, dawg, I came to pick up my gun that you fixed for me” Jimmy: “Ahh, I will be right back” Duzza: “Pookie, when he gets back with my gun, I’m take it and shove it up your ass. Can’t believe I had to sit through that damn Bigfoot story again!” Brandon: “ Yeah Pookie, you should really be shot for doing that to us” After a few more minutes of arguing, Jimmy finally comes back and is carrying the gun. The gun is a large silver handgun. Duzza can’t help but smile when he looks at it. He hands Jimmy some money before asking… Duzza: “ Yo Jimmy my man, any chance you got a box or something? Because I don’t really want to walk around with a big ass hand gun and I don’t think it’ll fit into my pockets” Jimmy: “ I’m afraid not Mr. Duzza, some white guy came in earlier and bought up all my boxes! Very strange man” Duzza: “ A’ight man, well, thanks anyway” Duzza, Pookie and Brandon all leave the shop, but stop just outside. Duzza: “ What am I gonna do? I can’t walk around with a hand gun out in the open man” Pookie: “ Ahh, don’t worry about a thing. Just hold it behind your back, no one will notice it, trust me” Duzza: “Trust you! I ain’t no fool” Brandon: “ Aww c’mon Duzz, what have you got to lose anyway?” Duzza: “ What have I got to lose!? Everything?! My house, my cars, my fame, my jewelry, I could lose everything! Geez” Pookie: “ Duzz, listen nothing will happen ok?” All three walk into the liquor store. Behind the counter sits a middle aged Asian man, who quickly looks up at them as they enter, before looking back down at the magazine he is reading. There is another customer in the store, a young woman, who is just minding her own business in the corner. Pookie quickly goes to find some liquor, while Duzza tried to conceal the unloaded weapon and Brandon just doodles about. Brandon: “ Duzza! Yo look at this man” Duzza, with out thinking quickly spins around to look at what Brandon is talking about, exposing the weapon. The female customer notices this and quickly screeches Customer: “ He’s got a weapon! He’s got a weapon!” Duzza: “ What? Who? Aww sh*t” The man behind the till quickly panics Worker: “ Please please, take what ever you want, please, do not kill me, please take what ever you want” Duzza: “ Dude, it ain’t even loaded, man listen I was just bout to…” Worked: “ Take whatever you want please, please no shot me, just take what ever you want” Pookie: “ For real?! Ha, sounds cool to me” Duzza: “ Nah man, listen you don’t understand I ain’t gonna shoot nobody I just got this from…” Worker: “ Please, take anything but don’t kill me” Duzza: “ Man, you tripping” Out of the corner of his eyes, Duzza notices Pookie picking up all the liquor he can Duzza: “ Pookie, put that sh*t down man” Unluckily for Duzza, the staffer always has a gun underneath the till and quickly pulls it out, when Duzza turns around there is a gun pointed at him Worker: “ Put the gun down and leave” Duzza: “ What? Man listen, this gun ain’t even loaded, it’s just a big misunde-“ Worker: “ Put the gun down before I shoot” Duzza: “ Err guys…RUN” As fast as he can Duzza and Brandon sprint out of the store, leaving Pookie with the bottles of liquor back in the shop. Duzza and Brandon run like they’ve never run before and within in 5 minutes the are back at Brandon’s house, both sweating and panting Duzza: “ Man, F*ck this, I got a battle royal and a match with some ignorant cracker and you got me out here getting threatened for my life?! F*ck this, man I’m out of here and I’ma go find some cracker to take my frustration out on man, peace easy” As the scene starts to fade, Duzza walks away from Brandon and quickly gets into his jeep, before pulling away at a fast speed and onto Friday Fusion and then Spring Fling…
