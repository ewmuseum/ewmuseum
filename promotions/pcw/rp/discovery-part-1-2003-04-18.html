Discovery, Part 1

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 18, 2003 [It feels like yesterday since he won the PCW World Championship. It feels like yesterday that the PCW roster looked at him like he was just a surfer joke. It feels like yesterday that he was teamed up with his brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, as the Cowabunga Connection. Fear Factory, Politically Incorrect, Suburban Uprising, The Heartbreakers...all those teams came and went. They always reached that point of bodaciousness and got shots at the tag team titles, but they never truly came through. They were really the only mainstay tag team in PCW for a few months battling so many random teams that they didn't even really remember why they were fighting anymore. Tommy remembers the Golden Turnbuckle and feeling that uncanny feeling of being free once again with the singles wrestling. All of those different opponents he was facing and a change in how he viewed his style. He took it to the semi-finals against a legend named Inphino Blitz. It would only be a short time later that fate sticks it's ugly head up and grants Tommy a silent wish that he's still sort of sad to see to this day. Akiyama and, the now, Skylar Green nailed Harry with a double team move that nearly broke his back in half. The doctors fixed him up quickly but Harry was to stay away from the ring as much as possible. Tommy couldn't believe that the Cowabunga Connection was finished already, before they could even etch their legacy into the books. Tommy tried to convince Harry that his back was fine and they could continue on, but Harry refused and pushed Tommy toward his singles career. Tommy was frightened, beyond belief. He wanted the shot at singles glory once again, but he wasn't ready for all of it. His mind was going in fifty different directions all at once and so he went out to the ring and just winged it. It turns out he started winning match after match after match against high profiled opponents. A locker room that once scoffed at the Cowabunga Connection was beginning to take notice to a force in the making. It was a blur rising up through the ranks and Tommy doesn't exactly know how he got there. He needed to get inside the heads of his opponents and make them extremely angry. He used to be a fun loving guy, a compadre who everyone in the community looked up to, but that changed once the winning started happening. He won the Continental Title with a most memorable battle at Decimation and reigned with the title a record breaking two months. He took all comers and some of the hottest talent in wrestling. It has all gotten into his head that he is truly one of the greatest wrestlers of all time. Is it true? That's only up to opinions and speculation, but in Tommy's mind he is one of the greatest of all time. He then proved that by finally winning the PCW World Title. Now every time he walks into the locker room the looks are totally different then back in the tag team days. He isn't a joke anymore, he's a full blown threat now. Their eyes of jealousy and they are eyes of men who are hunting for him now. Tommy loves every single moment as he slowly walks through the hallways. Former PCW Champions looking in disgust, thinking that they are better champions, and rookies who believe they could take Tommy if they were given a chance. When Tommy enters his own locker room, separate from all those other guys, he begins to laugh. They can be jealous, they can think that he doesn't deserve it, they can complain about luck, they can complain about being the greatest, but the fact still remains that 'Totally Tubular' Tommy is PCW World Champion. And he plans to stay there for a very long time] ['Heavy Metal' Harry, the gnarly brother and manager of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, stands at the stove looking at the knobs. His long brown hair falls into his face a little bit but his concentration is so strong on the dials and buttons on the stove. He doesn't really remember stoves being this complicated with burners and fire that can come from every which way. All he wants to do is make some scrambled eggs with a side of home fries. It's a breakfast of champions and he's not asking for much. Harry just sighs as the still picture of Twisted Sister on Harry's shirt moves upward with the sigh] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: This stove is stupid, man. [Harry smacks it on the side and stares at it for a few moments. He was actually hoping that by smacking it that it would somehow begin to work...he was wrong] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, what's going on in here? [Tommy comes gliding into the room with a one million dollar smile on his face. He wears a blue robe with yellow stitched writing on his right breast that has the initials 'TTT' on it. His fuzzy blue slippers are blue as well with the same initialing on the top. His blonde locks of hair stick upward as if he had a rough night of sleeping, but with his unexpected lady guest he didn't really think about that sleep thing too much] Harry: I'm trying to figure out this stove, man. Tommy: I have to totally agree with you on this one, Har. The stove is totally bogus. Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy picks up some newspaper flyers and tosses them aside. Harry looks on with confusion as to why Tommy is just chucking those coupons aside like nothing, but then suddenly realizes their cleaning lady Maria takes care of that stuff now. She's a nice chick, but needs to learn English] Tommy: Put on your Wednesday bests because we're going to Denny's, dude! Harry: Lenny's? Tommy: Denny's. Harry: Lenny's? Tommy: DUDE! It's Denny's!! They have those totally bodacious grand slam meals. Harry: I don't like baseball, man. Tommy: DUDE!!! Just give me a few minutes to change into something less morning like. [Tommy disappears back into the back bedrooms as Harry kicks the stove once more. Harry takes a seat onto one of the leather couches and moans in relief at how comfortable these are. It was a pretty far out idea to go get leather, all Harry's idea too. A couple of slams sound off and then the new cleaning lady Maria appears from the back bedrooms. She is a far out chick with fully tanned skin, a nice shapely figure, and large beautiful breasts. She's muttering Mexican stuff under her breath and rolling her eyes alot. Harry turns around on the couch and the two lock eyes] Harry: MA GUSTA FRIJOLES!! (Translation: I like beans) [Maria just rolls her eyes and walks out of the living room. Harry follows her with his eyes and shrugs. Maria looks a bit disgruntled this morning. Usually she doesn't have a wrinkle on her uniform or a hair out of place and it already looks like she's been through a war. It could be that she had a rough night as there were some scary thunderstorms last night, but she could have snuck in a few extra hours of sleep. It's not like they would have been angry about it or anything because Tommy is too wrapped up in his World Title defense at Spring Fling] Harry: Are you all right, man? [Maria doesn't answer but just begins washing the dishes in the kitchen] Harry: If it's about a guy then you can just tell me about it, man. I'm here to listen. [Maria still has her back turned as she begins to scrub a couple of pans that she used to make pasta in the night before. Harry remembers that far out dinner because it reminded him of eating at The Olive Garden. Tommy was in a rare performance last night laying down that far out charm that he has with the chicks. He let Maria eat with them, which most employers don't do, but Tommy is cool like that. All Maria did last night was laugh with tears streaming down her eyes. Harry thought that he slipped some far out reefer into the pasta but it wasn't really a high laugh...just that she found something funny. He doesn't recall anything after the dinner, but he does recall a moment where Maria and Tommy slipped into the back. Maybe he was going to show her his far out surfing trophies. The one that is like five feet tall is the best because it sort of reminds Harry of a golden midget] Harry: Did Tommy show you his far out Trophies last night, man? [Maria just continues to scrub the dishes, acting like Harry isn't even there. If Harry had an ounce of reality in him he'd notice that he was being completely ignored. Only thing is that Harry is completely stoned and just thinks that Maria can't speak any English, but insists on asking her questions in English anyway. It's the freakin' universal language of the world, everyone should be required to speak it, man] Harry: I won a trophy once. It was back in the second grade in the spelling bee contest. I still have the trophy in my room. Wanna check it out, man? [Maria continues cleaning as Tommy comes from the back now wearing a tank top and blue jean shorts. His hair looks a little better but not much. It's not like it matters anyway because the ladies still flock to him] Tommy: I'm starving, dude! Lets go. Harry: How come Maria is being weird, man? [Tommy pauses for a few short moments] Tommy: We had sex all night last night. She's probably exhausted, dude. Harry: Oh. So, your still on track to sleep with all kinds of babes to let out the bad luck? Tommy: Totally. I can't have any bad luck in me for Spring Fling, dude. I have to be released of my bogusness and discover that gnarly point of my soul that will leave me bad luck free. Harry: Yeah, man. Tommy: It's not like I really need it or anything. Vendakamps Pentagram is a total loser just like Rent-A-Jerkface and 'Dude, I'm Blinde' Skylar Green. [The two men walk out of the sliding glass door and toward the 2002 Blue Mustang. Tommy hops in the driver seat as Harry plops down into the passenger seat] Harry: Do you ever miss the Cowabunga Connection, man? [Tommy starts up the car and seems to think about the question with as much thought as possible] Tommy: Yes. Harry: We could have been the best ever, man. Tommy: Totally. Harry: Fate sucks, man. Tommy: Totally, but sometimes its always for the best. [Tommy puts the car into gear and the Mustang backs out of the driveway] Fade to black
