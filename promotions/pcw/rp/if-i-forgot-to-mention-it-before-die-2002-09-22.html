If I Forgot To Mention It Before.... Die

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/09/02 “Burn, Demon.... Burn...” Our shot opens up on the grotesque, sneering face of PCW’s sick killer... Clyde. “Ready for the INFERNO?” The face is motionless, no change in expression or even a blinking of an eye. “Well, as the saying goes....BURN BABY BURN!“ A sudden burst of flames engulfs the Demon’s face... It’s expression lost within the ball of flickering fire. The head is nothing more than a dark shadow as the fire licks an orbit around it. “As you so eloquently put it... DIE!“ The flames retreat and are sucked back, leaving a mass of dripping, melted plastic that is charred and Black where the flames licked it. “So many ways to burn you Clyde, so much fun in testing them out.” The shot pulls away from the molten and smouldering mannequin to show that it is just one of an Army of Clyde dummies... Lined and rowed up in what looks to be a warehouse. Each one has the Demon’s dirty, shaggy Brown hair, the burned faces all uniform and each mannequin dressed in rags. “Testing........ Them.......... Out!” The camera pans away from the Clyde mannequin Army to show the smirking faces of PCW’s first couple... Xavier and Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Putting down a Flame-thrower, the PCW Champion eyes them up and down before settling on one that has a Brown “Welcome” mat by it’s feet.. Xavier : “1E, right leg forward one step.” On his head, almost being swallowed by his long mane of Strawberry Blonde is a headset that he speaks into, watching the mannequin for signs that his order is being carried out. Eleanor : “Forward........ One........ Step!” With a click and a whirr, the synthetic Demon’s right foot moves off the concrete floor and creeps towards the Brown, roughly textured mat.... It’s foot slowly descends onto it and presses into the object. Xavier : “WELCOME!” The Gold Patron Meritorious grins smugly as the mat combusts, the flames starting to creep up the mannequins leg, the fire slowly clawing it’s way up the rags until it shields the whole figure from view... Running it’s course until there is nothing left to burn and the melting plastic dripping down starts to block the flame’s source. Xavier : “Oh... That one is GOOD! Hubbard himself could have devised a contraption such as that.” Ellie~! keeps her big, pale Blue eyes transfixed on the smouldering Clyde dummy... The dying flames reflecting both in her vacuous eyes and on the World Title she has draped over the shoulder of her Black spaghetti strap top. Xavier : “Does it do anything else?” With the immediate effect of it wearing out it’s stay in Kannon’s fond memories, he eagerly awaits something more which sounds like it may be coming when a beeping sequence is faintly heard. Beep... Beep... Beep... Beep... Beeeeeeeeeeeeeeeep!!! *BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM* In the blink of an eyes, the “Welcome” mat disintegrates in an explosion that sends the Clyde dummy launching into the air and nearly scraping the top of the warehouse before dropping back down and clearing out a good handful of other mannequins as it lands. The Champion nods in approval, giving a reserved applause. Xavier : “Okay... Order me 5 of those... What do you have next?” A faint, crackly voice is then heard coming from his ear-piece. Xavier : “Isn’t that a little sick and inhumane?..... I LOVE IT!” The bright Green eyes of the PCW Champion sparkle in anticipation for the next trick as his eyes are diverted to the dummy on the end of the first row which has a Pay Packet in it’s right hand. Eleanor : “Yeah, he loves it and I, like, SO do too...... What is it again?” As the Gold Patron Meritorious points his intrigued wife’s attention to the dummy, it’s hand starts to rise and open the envelope. The tearing of the paper is then followed by the propulsion of a liquid from the envelope that fizzes on contact with the mannequin’s face. Eleanor : “Like... WAY cool.... It’s fizzing like Sherbet!” The liquid causes the mannequin Clyde’s face to smoke, plumes of vapour being expelled from the boiling face as the liquid eats right through it. Entranced by the vicious reaction between acid and face, Ellie~! rushes over to the mannequin and closes one eye, keeping her distance as she looks right through the hole that has been bored through the faux Clyde’s face. Eleanor : “Wow, I can, like, SO see through his face!” The PCW Champion also seems to approve of the rather disgusting weapon, watching the remains with glee as Ellie~! scurries back over to him. Xavier : “It would almost make paying some people worth it if their faces got burned through.” Beside him, the excited little Commissioner points over... Over enthused by being able to see right through the acid tunnelled head. Eleanor : “Got..... Burned..... Through!” Switching his eyes from the molten mass and the Acid holed face, the PCW CEO still seems like he’s hoping for something else. Xavier : “There anything that works... I dunno.... Internally?” Again the crackly voice from his headset guides him to a mannequin in the 2nd row, close to the middle that is holding a rather unappetising burger in it’s hand. With a clunk and a whirr, the arm robotically raises and deposits the greasy piece of junk food into the mannequin Clyde’s mouth. Eleanor : “Ewwwwwzzies... If I had to eat that I’d, like, SO kill myself too!” The robotic arm lowers and nothing seems to happen. Eleanor : “I, like, wouldn’t have taken this long either...” The curious couple wander over to the mannequin and Ellie~! looks at it curiously, tilting her head and observing it’s burned face... She then grins, and pulls her right hand back. Eleanor: “Like....SO TAKE THAT!“ With a rather weak swing of her open palm, the little Commissioner strikes the Demon’s cheek with the PREP SLAP O’DOOM~!“ Xavier : “ELLIEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE~!“ Just as the weak slap connects with the plastic cheek of the Demon, as if in slow motion the face begins to bulge. Eleanor : “Uh-Oh...” The Scientologist leaps across towards his preppy wife, tackling her to the floor as the head finally looses it’s integrity and explodes in a huge fireball that sends flames and hot plastic flying all around. *BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM* With a thud, the couple land on the floor, Kannon covering Ellie~! as best as he can until the splats that sound out when hot plastic hits cold concrete can be heard no longer. Slowly, Kannon sits back up and looks around to see that where he landed was the only place spared a liberal dotting of hot plastic. Xavier : “That’s L. Ron!” Kannon looks to the skies and salutes before pulling Ellie~! up as the shot begins to fade out, the little prep staring at the now headless Clyde mannequin as we hit Black. “Like... WOW!... I didn’t know I could hit that hard!”
