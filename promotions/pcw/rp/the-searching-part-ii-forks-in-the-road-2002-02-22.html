Song listening guide: 'A Place For My Head' by who else? Linkin Park! Just something to kill some time.. I might charge everyone ten bucks and send it off to charity, 'cause I'm cool like dat. It looks like my team'll face Juan Borro.. if he can get outta prison.. some NTICW guy, Pila, or something. I can't remember, I'd have to look. Plus that Badge. What a 'pig' he is...[/b] [b]KIRA: So who's on your team, do you know yet?[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: All Orlando. Boda..[/b] [b]KIRA: Oh, well that's a smart recruitment. Say, doesn't he own part of the Magi-[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: ..and Kid Wonder.[/b] [b]KIRA: Huh!?[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: I need to be healthy just so I can have the stamina to put up with Amanda and K-Dub's persistent nonsense. And baby girl... that's no b.s.![/b] [i]The scene slowly fades to black as Kira stands there with her hands on her hips, looking down in shock at The Showstopper. Meanwhile, B.S. cackles his head off, knowing all too well how utterly ludicrous the notion of a Boda, B.S., K-Dub team really is. The last thing we can make out is Richie Syxx's first GTT promo debut on the PTC Channel... And now....[/i] [b][i]* * *[/i][/b] [i]Sarah Hughes!! Yeah! Woooo-hoo! Take that you Russian bit- Er. Sorry. We're on..? Okay.... 'm sorry. Many challenges and interesting things happen each and every day when you are a PCW superstar. To anyone else in Primetime Central, for that matter... but [b]especially[/b] a wrestler from PCW. The first promo you have seen from Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer took place back on Friday. Now this one takes place early on Wednesday. That is four days in between. Friday night at PCW Fusion, B.S. couldn't overcome all the personal challenges. Not this time. As a result, that buffoon Red Rock has managed to retain his title. As PCW Rising Star Champion. Not that big a deal, in the grand scheme of things, except for the fact that B.S. wants to keep his small chances at an eventual PCW Title Sweep alive. Well, at least one minus the Cruiserweight title, most likely. But The Showstopper is definitely the type to bounce back. What an stunning, incredible three day span for your favorite neighboorhood anti-hero. After further recouperating, scouting of Hank~!, and watching some NTICW Showtime on Saturday, quickly there was Sabotage. Believe me, for the exception of the heartless, savage beatdown on Xavier Kannon early on, [b]Asylum[/b] did the sabotaging. Miyagawa is still [b]YOUR[/b] World Television Champion. No contest, but oh well. Stupid International Incident. Bobby Stormbringer, without even telling his fellow stablemates, showed up to the arena and the hunt was on. With no match scheduled for him, B.S. had free reign. A free reign of [b]February[/b] Madness. Just ask your [b]precious[/b] Drake Maxwell. After kidnapping the brother of two-time former PCW World Champion Jeremy Howard just to 'get over'. After seducing an old acquaintance of B.S. into his inconcievable plans, that being The End. After perpetrating a vicious assault on the body of Xavier Kannon? Well. Pain and dehabilitation can be a most effective teacher. The Showstopper is most willing to be the professor for this course. All those familiar are instantly aware of what the course is called. Old School One-Oh-One. Sledgehammer to the knee. Sledgehammer to the guts. Sledgehammer to the forehead, and nearly right through the other side. Storm On The Horizon. Boy. That sure is one [b]Fallen[/b] Angel. Did we mention the fact that Jeremy 'Krayzie' Howard is now [b]YOUR[/b] number one contender for the PCW Continental title?? No? There you go, then. After another session of chillin' and traveling the day after, here comes Tuesday. Turmoil. After a sudden variation of 'The Perfect Storm'.. *ahem* ..hang on here. *ahem* [b]Hank jobbed to B.S.~! B.S. I say~!![/b] Ahh. There we go. No, B.S. is not your new Extreme Champ. Awwww. However! B.S. gets something a little better than that. A bit more exposure. A bit more exposure as in his first PCW main event of any kind. That would be at the PCW March Pay-Per-View that counts for February, or whichever way they are doing it. The World Heavyweight title, and only twenty people will be eligible for it. That would be at only his [b]second[/b] PCW PPV. Hmm. That's not all that bad. So now here we are. Wednesday. Thursday is of course.. another monotaneous travel day to Boise, Idaho for Fusion on Friday. Jamaica to [b]Idaho[/b]. Talk about your f*ckin' culture shocks, man. The only member of Asylum wrestling that night.. technically a member.. is Darkstar, who's been doing things solo as of late. So B.S. may not even need to be there, but The End is also wrestling. So you never know. But not being booked for this particular show is a glorious, glorious thing. Because then on Saturday, the real nightmare begins.. or possibly a dream through all the havoc. We shall see. Just after noon, B.S. will face his most well-known opponent in his long career for the GTT's fiirst round. By far, the most recognizable. Richie Syxx. On top of it all B.S. represents PCW, while Syxx represents OSW. So the pressure from the rest of the locker room is on. Win or lose, injured or healthy, that day ain't over yet. B.S. must take the fastest aircraft available to Los Angeles for the NTICW Playboy Mansion Match. The show begins that night on the east coast at eight sharp. This means five o'clock in L.A. and not much time to recooperate. This isn't a one-on-one match, either. It's a grand clusterf*ck with his personal assistant Carl Everstrong competing too! Well, wrestling. It's not like Carl will be much 'competition' for anyone. Maybe 'wrestling' is even too strong of a word. Ah well. He'll be in the match. [b]Then[/b], it's back to PCW for his tag match of epic proportions the very next day. Sunday night. Back to Sabotage. At least the venue is close and travel will be simple. [b]Calgary.... Alberta, Canada~![/b] One of the five cities in North America that B.S. has once called home, this one briefly in his late teens. Monday, you ask..? Ohhh... flop over and pretty much die. Hey, it works for The End and Darkstar, so why not??[/i] [b][i]* * *[/i][/b] [i]--2-20-02, 9:43 am local time, Wednesday morning--[/i] [i]The scene fades in just several moments after B.S.'s second NTICW promo, on Carl Everstrong in a waiting area outside a tiny office. A pivotal office in B.S.'s quest, here in Kingston, Jamaica. The balding and overweight Carl is wearing the typical tourist garb. Red and orange spotted bermuda shorts, flip flops of a dull gray, and a white button down shirt with blue pinstripes. Carl is also wearing a cheap watch and some golden-rimmed spectacles. Carl is leaning back in a black, uncomfortable steel folding chair. Carl also leans his head against the white wall behind him, looking straight up, slapping his thighs for something to cut through the mind numbing silence. The sounds of far off ringing phones, the ruffling of many papers, and gruff voices fill the air, mostly off to Carl's right and out of sight. After a short, ho-hum moment of Carl patiently waiting, we can just barely hear a door creak open, and we see Carl perk up and look back over to his left. The Floridian transplant from Connecticut stands up, and the camera shot finally pans all the way over to show a very distressed Bobby Stormbringer in his sleeveless, ancient Kid Wonder t-shirt and denim jeans. The black and blue custom sunglasses (I need a little redo down the road, Miya! ;p), the T-macs, the blazing red hair, the timex watch that may never break down, all in their proper places. Mr. Prime Time stands there a couple feet in front of the Chief Of Police's glass office door with a few official-type papers and manilla folders in his left hand and his other hand is half-jammed into a front jeans pocket. B.S.'s face has become very stricken, very sullen all of a sudden. He looks at the floor with wild.. yet.. cool and calculating eyes as if he is building towards something, something big. You know. Like just before Booker does a Spinerooni?? Yeah, that's the one.[/i] [b]CARL: Sir? What's the verdict?[/b] [i]B.S.'s head snaps up towards his personal assistant and he frowns deeply.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: Things took so long because this wasn't just the work of one person. 'ill-equipped'.. to handle an investigation of this stature, and they are way too freggin' stubborn to ask for actual help with it, that's what's been taking so d*mn long. The skid marks from the tires, the trajectory of the path of the car before it hit the pole.. all these meaningless things to recreate the accident, that's what else they've been doing. At least part of it, I'm sure other 'self-stimulating' things is what these people have [i]really[/i] been doing, I swear..[/b] [b]CARL: Is there anything else you can use, sir? Anything you can go on..?[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: Oh, there is something to go on, alright. They've narrowed down one of the prints. It's a one in a googleplex chance of it [i]not[/i] being this guy. Sorta like that O.J. Simpson DNA thing, ya know..?[/b] [i]B.S.'s anger seems to have faded from his face for now, and he is left with just simple sadness.
