Bad Date (Part 1)

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 24, 2003 Tyler and Kalli sit across from each other at a place of fine dining. Tyler attempts to look his best for his lady, as he is dress up in a gray suit with a solid black tie. He sips his clear glass of water as he stares in to the eyes of the woman that he had been absent from for quite sometime. She looks better then ever tonight. Her brown hair is put up with bobby pins and her low cut, black dress drives Tyler wild. He looks at her, up and down, happy that he can finally have some quality time with his best friend and girlfriend. She stares back at him with a sparkle in her mysterious brown eyes. “Its nice to finally get to sit down and talk with you. You’ve been so busy lately. It almost seems like you go out with your buddy John more than me,” says Kalli as she lays a pink colored cloth napkin on her lap to catch any residue that may fall. She smiles a toothy smile that exemplifies her pearly white grill. “Hey, no one will ever replace you. Remember when I came to New York as the new guy. You were so helpful. You are always going to be number one to me,” says Tyler thoughtfully, wanting to assure his girlfriend that his feelings haven’t changed for her in the least bit. “Well that means a lot to me. I’m just lucky to have a guy like you. I know that you are busy with wrestling and comedy all at the same time. I guess I should be thankful that you squeeze me in,” Kalli says as she grabs a menu from in front of her and opens it up. Tyler shakes his head in disagreement over this comment. “Hey, I don’t squeeze you in. I value our time together. Out of everything that I have going, you are top priority,” Tyler says in a strong voice to make his point clear. Kalli nods and raises her eyebrows to signify that she was wrong in saying that. Yet, she continues on with the same point. “Top priority? Let’s not go overboard. It’s all right that you are busy. No one is pointing fingers at this table,” Kalli assures. She doesn’t want Tyler to think that if he pursues an active career in entertainment that he will lose her. She attempts to assume the loving and caring girlfriend role. “Ok, busy or not, you better be ready for some changes to be made around here. I know that I have been neglecting you somewhat, and I plan to make it up to you,” Tyler assures. He knows full well that he had been putting a lot of extra time in to his wrestling and comedian career. “Oh really? How are you going to do that?” Kalli inquires, wondering what he may have meant by that comment. “I have my ways. I’ll cut back on my time with the club and PCW is just going to have to deal with the absence of the great Tyler Preston for a while,” Tyler informs her. She shakes her head in disapproval, not wanting him to soil his careers over her. She doesn’t want to have that burden on her shoulders. “I look forward to that. But if things start to get busy, don’t worry about it. Just talk to me first and I’ll understand,” Kalli replies. While she wants to see more of her man, she still wants him to know that being successful in his careers is just as important. “I will do that. Oh yea, I got you a little something that you can wear to remember me when I’m not around,” Tyler says with a smile on his face as he reaches in to his suit coat pocket. Kalli’s eye light up, happy to be receiving a gift. Tyler poles out a long, red box and slides it over the white table clothe to Kalli on the other side. Kalli accepts the box with a smile and take it in her hand. She lifts off the top to reveal a diamond bracelet setting on a huge chunk of cotton to keep it from breaking. Kalli lifts the sparkling treasure from the box and looks at it with wonder. She is amazed at its elegance and can hardly find the words to speak. “Oh Tyler, you didn’t have to do that. It’s so beautiful and expensive. I really can’t accept this from you. I know that you have been low on money ever since you came to town,” Kalli says with care. Many woman would take the great gift and run, but Kalli is more concerned with what it cost Tyler to purchase. “Please woman, I get my fair share of money. Working two different entertainment jobs has its benefits. Just enjoy it, because it comes from the heart,” Tyler replies. This puts Kalli at ease somewhat, knowing that Tyler has sufficient funds. She lays the giant gem back into the box and puts the lid on. She slides it to the side, but still keeping an eye on her new prized possession. “As good as that is, a piece of jewelry will never replace the actual thing,” Kalli says as he leans over the table for a kiss. Tyler begins to lean forward, bracing himself on his chair when a young female decked out in a bright pink dress suddenly interrupts them. She has medium length blonde hair, and proves to be very attractive. “Tyler Preston right?” The woman asks with a smile. Tyler leans back and settles once again in his chair. He leaves Kalli kissing the air, and then flashing an angry glare at the source of the interruption. “Yes, that’s me. Is there something that I can help you with?” Asks Tyler, thinking that this may concern an issue of business. Kalli glances over the menu in hostility, angered over the fact that they can again, not have a decent time alone. “I knew it was you. My friends over there at that table dared me to come and talk to you. We love your comedy act and we especially love watching you wrestle,” the woman says in a flirtatious voice. Kalli sighs a deep breath but Tyler doesn’t pick up on it. The woman points at her table where four other gorgeous women sit giggling over the accepted dare of the woman at the table. “That’s really great, I’m glad that you are such a fan,” Tyler says, trying to end the conversation so that no more damage could be done to the date that is already going down hill. His attempts are unsuccessful as the woman stays at Tyler’s side like an anchor. She touches his shoulder. “Could I trouble you for a picture?” Asks the woman as she sorts through her black leather purse to reveal a silver camera. Tyler looks at Kalli to try to make a decision on what to say, but the woman acts to fast for him to even think about it. “I…guess…you can,” stammers Tyler, barely getting the words out as the woman plops down on his lap. She adjusts the skirt of her dress to make sure her undergarments aren’t exposed. “Hey, who is that?” the woman asks rudely as she points a crooked index finger in Kalli’s direction. Kalli returns the rude point with a scrunched up face in spite of her. “That’s Kalli Stearns, she’s my girlfriend, and you are kind of interrupting our date here. Can we just make this quick?” Tyler suggests, knowing that Kalli is getting more and more upset at this turn of events. “Your girlfriend? Well it’s so good to meet you. You want in on this picture?” The woman asks almost as if she is befriending Tyler’s love. Kalli gets up, agreeing with the woman’s every command so that they can get this ordeal over with. “Sure, why not? Where should I stand?” Asks Kalli as she approaches Tyler side where his lap is still harboring the young woman. Kalli doesn’t exactly like the idea of her sitting on Tyler’s lap. “No, I meant TAKE the picture. It’s the big silver button on the top, but wait until the flash charges up,” the woman instructs as she tosses the camera in Kalli’s direction. Kalli catches it and snaps the picture as fast as she can. The woman smiles a huge fake smile. The flash reflects off both of their shiny faces. The woman bounces off of Tyler’s lap and takes her camera. “Thanks so much Tyler. Thank you also Kelly,” the woman says as she leaves the table to return to her giggling friends. Kalli frowns, and Tyler immediately tries to dig himself out of the hole that he buried himself in. “I’m sorry Kalli, I hate fans like that,” Tyler says, trying to convince her that he doesn’t even like the attention that women like that give him. “Yea right, it seems pretty rough. She was sitting on your lap Tyler, right in front of me,” Kalli says, almost in tears as she hides behind the giant menu. Tyler reaches over and caresses her arm. She peaks out from the top of the menu and his touch. “Kalli I’m sorry. I had no control. Don’t worry, now it’s just me and you, no more interruptions,” he assures. She musters out a fake smile, but it is better than nothing. Just as the words of assurance leave Tyler’s mouth, John Gargano comes busting through the entrance to the restaurant decked out in a suit of his own. He ignores the man that takes reservations at the front. Tyler glues his eyes to his friend and slumps down not wanting John to see him. John’s presence would just make Kalli feel even more useless. John searches around, glancing at the entire room of tables until he reaches Tyler’s. He spots Tyler, and says a few inaudible words to the man in the lobby. He begins to make his way over to Tyler, ready to ruin the date even more.
