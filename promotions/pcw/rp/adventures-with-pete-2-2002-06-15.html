Adventures with Pete [2]

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 15, 2002 So much for deeper storylines and character dev. You guys get the OBSCENITY now. [The scene opens to a train traveling up to Reno. It is a regular Amtrack train, which usually cuts up and down the California coast, and occasionally up to Nevada. It is nightfall, and almost every single light on the train is off, except for the dining hall. There, two mysterious figures are still up and arguing into the wee hours of the morning.] Pete Rose: [takes another gulp of his beer] I’m telling you kid, the Marlins could not have been beaten by anyone. That’s why they won the World series. Tom Lexian: Pssh! Rose, you have no clue what you’re talking about. They bought that championship…hell, that entire payroll was over 150 million dollars. If you don’t win a world series with that payroll, you need to get out of baseball. Pete Rose: Money had nothing to do with it! It was the players that won it! Tom Lexian: Of course the players won the championship, but how could any team compete when all of the players were on one team. It was a monopoly of money, my friend. Nothing more. And that sir, is unfair. It’s just like this guy, Tyler Nelson. The guy has loads of money and tries to take advantage because of that. But there are good people, like myself, willing to fight the tyranny. And that’s balance between good and evil. Pete Rose: I cannot agree with you, Tom. I just can’t. Money makes the world go ‘round, and either you have it or you don’t. [Tom dropped his glass on the ground, spilling beer all over the floor. He was fuming mad now, and clenched his fist, as if he was about to destroy Pete Rose.] Pete Rose: [staring down at the broken glass] Now why in the hell did you go and do that for? Tom Lexian: Pete, I hate money. I always have, and I always will. It’s unfair because this world is built upon it. Hell, sometimes I think that the Earth itself has a golden core…kind of like Jason Core, except he’s not here anymore. I remember when he and I would go drinking, except now I drink with Duke Williams. But that mother fucker can drink. Pete Rose: [mouth begins to drool] Wow, that be fucking awesome… Tom Lexian: [throws a napkin at Pete] Wipe your mouth, you slob. It’s not really made of gold. Anyways, we’re too wrapped up in ourselves to realize that money means nothing. After our money is gone, where are we? Nowhere, that’s where we are. Right out of our throne and down into the shitter. Look at MC Hammer, or Vanilla Ice… Pete Rose: [shrugs, taking another sip of his drink] So what? You just named too very badly dressed rappers with incredible large hair and rhyming lyrics. Tom Lexian: But they precisely prove my point, Pete. They had it all, albeit, for a very short period of time. While they were on top, they enjoyed the finest meals, the finest women, and the finest cars that the world had to offer. Once their success left, everything else left along with it. Hammer and Ice found themselves duped with a big ass check in front of them. Pete Rose: What exactly are you trying to imply, Tom? That I’m somehow obsessed with money? That I, Pete Rose, cannot stray away from the almighty dollar? Because if so, we’re going to have at it right here and now. [Tom laughs at Pete’s last comment, just cracking his knuckles.] Tom Lexian: Pete, I’m the wrestler and you’re the baseball player. Do I really have to remind you again? I fight as a profession…you hit a ball with a stick… [Pete stands up, wielding his bat. Tom stands up as well, not knowing what Pete is going to do next.] Pete Rose: [angrily] You might take me as just another dumb jock, but I’m not! I am a person, god dammit! Plus, I have a really big, large bat. I’m not afraid to use it. Tom Lexian: You truly are a simple creature. [Pete begins to swing the bat, destroying the table they were just sitting at.] Tom Lexian: What?! What was that for? Pete Rose: [shrugs] I was trying to scare you… Tom Lexian: [frustrated] Well, you failed. Not only did you fail not to scare me, but you just raised our tab for the train ride by ten fold. We have to pay for that table, you know? Pete Rose: I’m sorry, Tom. I’m a terrible person. I’ll pay for it all… Tom Lexian: Yeah, I’ll have to admit – you do suck as a person. And what money would you use to pay for this anyways? Aren’t you thousands of dollars in debt? Pete Rose: Only a couple thousand. You see, that’s why I requested to be on this mission. If we are able to find the amulet, I would be able to go back in time, fix all of these damn mistakes that I somehow ran into during my lifetime, and make the wrongs into rights. Hell, who knows, maybe if I didn’t screw up so much, I might be a millionaire right. Tom Lexian: Yeah, a millionaire instead of the bat wielding psycho you are today, right? Pete Rose: [shrugs] We all have to be something in this world, right? I guess that’s me. [Tom was about to make a comment about how Pete needs to get a grip on his shortcomings, when a waitress came by with some more drinks. She notices the smashed table and nearly faints.] Waitress: What in god’s name happened here? Pete Rose: [scratches his head, playing stupid] Gee, I don’t know. Must have been a freak hurricane or something. Waitress: [skeptical] A hurricane? Inside of the train? Tom Lexian: Ignore him miss, he’s just a bit insane. The clues speak for themselves. I mean, he’s holding a bat, he’s obviously delusional…he was the one who destroyed this table. I’m not usually a tattle tale, but this had to be said. The guy’s a raving lunatic, and I can’t stick up for him anymore. I’m truly afraid of this man. Waitress: [places her hand over her mouth in shock] Oh my god…do you think I should call the captain? We could have him thrown off the train… Tom Lexian: [trying to hold back his laughter] No, I would hate to have the train stop just to have my friend here thrown off… Waitress: Oh, that’s okay. We usually throw off passengers while the train is moving – saves time that way. Pete Rose: [getting scared] Oh my god…oh my god…Tom, tell her I’m not crazy! Tell her I’m normal! Oh my god! [Pete is jumping up and down like a gorilla while pleading Tom to clear his name. The waitress is slowly backing away from Pete, looking very scared.] Waitress: [whispering to Tom] Oh my…I think he’s having another episode. Maybe we should call the captain… Tom Lexian: No, no…I believe I can handle this. Uhm, could you leave us for a moment? I would like to have a word with him. [reaches into his wallet, pulling out a blank check and signing it] This is for the damage caused. I’ll pick up the tab for it. Waitress:[leaving] Okay…if you need any help, just shout. I’ll be back with plenty of security officers. [The waitress leaves, and as soon as she does, Tom falls to the ground laughing while Pete is red in the face from embarrassment.] Pete Rose: I can’t believe you let her believe that I was some psychotic baseball players! This is outrageous! Tom Lexian: C’mon, she didn’t even recognize who you were. She probably wasn’t even a baseball fan. Pete Rose: Knowing my luck, she’ll probably realize who I was and send in one of those damn stories to the National Enquirer about me. The next headline will read how I fathered her alien baby or something. Tom Lexian: If it makes you feel any better, I did pay for the table. No charge for you. Pete Rose: I would have paid for it… Tom Lexian: Hah! I doubt that, Pete. I see what you do with your debts. Apparently you think they decrease the longer that you don’t pay them. Pete Rose: Oh well…[notices Tom’s shoes]…hey, nice shoes! Tom Lexian: [looking down] Oh, thanks. I got them for real cheap…only four hundred and forty-two dollars! Pete Rose: [floored] Whoa! You spent that much money on shoes? Are you crazy? What the hell do they do for you? Tom Lexian: [sarcastically] Well, they are called Nike Awesomes! You figure it out, genius. They are just so comfortable, and they make me feel twenty pounds lighter. I feel more agile in them. Pete Rose: Twenty pounds lighter? From some shoes? C’mon! Give me a break. You spent four hundred dollars to feel like you lost twenty pounds? Tom Lexian: Pete, I wouldn’t talk. I think you would pay plenty of money to lose twenty pounds. After all, you are looking a bit hefty around the waist now, aren’t you? Pete Rose: [defensive] Don’t talk about my weight. It’s not my fault Slim Fast doesn’t work and Jenny Craig just isn’t hardcore enough to handle my problem. But I’m tired of talking about myself. I just realized that I don’t know anything about you. Now…you’re a wrestler, aren’t you? Tom Lexian: Yeah, that’s right. Pete Rose: Now, how much money do you make betting on matches? Tom Lexian: [shocked] What the fuck? I don’t bet on matches. This isn’t baseball, Pete. We aren’t barbarians. Besides, I’m not a jack ass that I would bet on my own sport, unlike some people here. Pete Rose: [looking around] Are you talking about me? Tom Lexian: [sarcastically] Nooooo! Pete Rose: Fine, fine…I get the picture. Why is this wrestling so important to you anyways? I always thought it was just a couple of guys beating their brains in. Tom Lexian: Of course, people say that when they’re not a wrestler. I don’t blame them, after all, no one can understand wrestling until they’ve stepped into the squared circle. I’m in the PCW, one of the most famous, the most heralded, and the most prominent wrestling organizations in the world. Pete Rose: What’s that stand for? Purely Comical Wrestling? Tom Lexian: [shrugs] I wouldn’t doubt it. There’s this guy named Akiyama who sends people into this Neo Void. I don’t know about you, but I don’t believe in that kind of stuff, but I can’t doubt that these guys just aren’t coming back. Where do they go? Hell? I’m not messing with that guy. Pete Rose: He sounds like a monster. Tom Lexian: And he looks weird, sort of like Canseco before the steroids. Pete Rose: I don’t remember Canseco before the steroids. Tom Lexian: That’s fine, I don’t either. Anyways, this Tuesday I’m fighting Xavier Kannon and Snow for a shot at the Continentel title. Now this Snow fellow is a former world champion. That means he was the best. But c’mon, SNOW? I remember snow when I was a little kid. It was so delicate and fragile. Hell, I’d even roll it into a ball and toss it around. Now you’re telling me I can’t beat a guy named Snow? Pete Rose: What about Kannon? A cannon is dangerous. Tom Lexian: Sure, sure. I mean, he was the former Continentel champion, but he has this thing that really ticks me off. It’s this tilde thing. I mean, everywhere I go, I see the tilde. What is it? What does it do? Well, I figured out a way to stop the insanity, and it’s to fight fire with fire. I’ve invented the 8==0! Pete Rose: What the hell is that? Tom Lexian: Isn’t it obvious? It’s the 8==0! What it means is that I’m more of a man than you are, and thus I will defeat you. I am more of a man than most of the PCW roster, but that isn’t the point. To beat the tilde, you got to be the tilde. And I intend on doing that right down to the bitter end. Pete Rose: But people aren’t going to understand what it means. Tom Lexian: People will be people. Those who don’t get it can continue with their tildeness, but the true believers will follow…the OBSCENITY! 8==0! Pete Rose: Now, that’s not very nice to say, Tom. Tom Lexian: [shrugs] Sorry Pete, I’m feeling a little cynical today. I’ll be good. And come this Tuesday, I will earn a shot to win the Continentel title, and win some gold. The last title I held was some tag team title with Frisco. It’s like an after thought. [The train stops, and the captain’s voice comes over the intercom] Captain: We have arrived in Reno. Please exit your cabins as soon as possible, as we’re sweeping through the train for a known psychopath. He is a Caucasian man, balding, and with a very large beer gut. He is very hostile and dangerous. We believe he has a bat. Pete Rose: [scared] Tom, Tom…I think they’re looking for me. Tom Lexian: Probably. Let’s get out of here before they find you and throw you into a straight jacket. Pete Rose: Hurry! Hurry! Hurry! Tom Lexian: Pete, shut up! If you’re that scared why don’t you…why don’t you…uhm, why don’t you just jump out of the window or something? Pete Rose: You think it’ll help me escape? Tom Lexian: Absolutely. [Pete suddenly jumps through a nearby train window, and disappears running into the train station. Tom watches him shaking his head. Some people just can’t take sarcasm.]
