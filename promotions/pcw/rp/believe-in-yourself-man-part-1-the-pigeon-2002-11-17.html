Believe In Yourself, Man - Part 1 - The Pigeon

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 17, 2002 [Did you ever stay awake for twenty four hours to just watch the sunrise and then watch it set? Maybe you should. The feeling is absolutely mind blowing and the rush through your body is indescribable. You watch night go to day and then day go to night. When the sun hides behind the hills, the colors are so amazing that you begin wondering where colors came from. Then it goes to where exactly did words come from? Who invented words? Who said the first word and declared that these things that would roll from our mouths would be called 'words'? Where did we come from? Who created us? Who created the person that created us? It's mind pounding, giving an immediate headache. Shake it off for a moment and go back to looking at the sunset. Beautiful, man. The way the orange blaze blends with blue, purple, and pink. The Lord above has a most far out paint brush. Gotta give him credit for that...and the whole creation of man, but that's another story, a long and involved one. Wonder where wrestling originated from? He doesn't know. That's why he's wondering. He never was smart, and not paying attention in school didn't help that much either. Nor did smoking bowls in between classes. A lot of things don't help the human body but we do them anyway. Wrestling for instance, it does not help out the human body at all. The brutal slams, brutal submission holds, and insane high flying manuevers. Most wrestlers become physically handicapped, but why? Why do this to your body for the price of glory, man? Well, it's a simple answer. Unfortunately, he can't come up with it right now, but he will be sure to figure it out later. At this moment, atop of Hotel California's rooftop, 'Heavy Metal' Harry is stoned out of his freakin' mind, man] [The sun still has a few more inches to go and it's gone until morning. What's left of the sun shines onto Harry's dark brown eyes which are surrounded by tears and enlarged veins, or bloodshot. He sits on top of a generator which controls the air conditioning system that runs through California's premiere hotel. The humming of the large generator runs through Harry's body giving him a generic massage on the ass. The twenty story structure doesn't come close to the far out skyscrapers in downtown Los Angeles, but it's high enough to get away from everything else, man. Right now Harry needs to get away, it's all becoming too much for him. Money, Power, and Women have entered his life. All he did was win his first ever singles match. One little win and his life did an entire 180 turn. He was just Tommy's tag team partner. One half of the Cowabunga Connection. He smoked a lot of pot. He listened to a lot of Twisted Sister. He masterbated to a lot of Hustler magazines. The masterbation is over. Thank God. Or don't thank God. Maybe it's his calling to spank the monkey ever now and then. But not ten to fifteen times a day. That's just wrong, sick, and perverted. It was sexual addiction, man. He's cured. Vilas Lavagna wasn't the brother of Avril Lavigne. Not even close, but Harry still overcame the odds to advance in the Cruiserweight Title tournament. Maybe this is his time to shine, man. Tommy took it to the limit in the Golden Tournbuckle Tournament, why can't he do the same in this one? Yeah, man!! It's going to happen. The Cruiserweight Title will never be the same once it's around the rock hard gut of 'Heavy Metal' Harry] [A pigeon flies onto the generator, pecking it's way toward the future Cruiserweight Champion. Harry looks over at the rat, posing as a bird. It stops only inches from Harry's right leg. Harry begins worrying what this pigeon has in mind. If it even thinks about dropping bird turds on his leather pants, it's gotta another thing coming. Hey, and in speaking of another thing coming, Judas Priest is going to be rolling through town, man! FAR OUT!] Pigeon: Coo... [Harry stares at the pigeon for a few moments. The 'coo' is how pigeons communicate. Harry was thinking about where words came from earlier, and these are pigeon words. Coo. Guess the far out Lord didn't have enough time to give the pigeons of the world more of a vocabulary. Really, why would you want to give pigeons more vocabulary. They are flying rats that carry totally not far out diseases. If they talked it would be fifty times worse than it already is, man] Pigeon: Coo...COO! [The pigeon pecks forward, coming centimeters from poking Harry in his leg with it's diseased beak] Harry: Hey, man! Watch it! You stay on your side of the generator, and I'll stay on my side, man. [The pigeon stands still for a few moments and then begins backing off just a tad. Harry raises his thick brown eyebrows upward, surprised that this pigeon listened to him. Can it really understand what he's saying? This could be a most far out discovery. It's time for Harry, Animal Scientists, to break it down...'Heavy Metal' style] Harry: Hop on one foot, man. [The pigeon hops up and down on one foot] Harry: Sit, man! [The pigeon sits] Harry: Roll over, man! [The pigeon rolls over] Harry: Play dead, man! [The pigeon falls onto it's back and pretends that it's met a cruel fate. Harry is trying his best to contain himself, he doesn't need this far out pigeon taking off on him. This is a bitchin' breakthrough] Harry: DO THE MACARENA, MAN!! [The pigeon does the macarena] Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!!! Pigeon: Coo. [Harry looks closely at this talented pigeon. For some reason on that last coo he could understand what it was trying to say to him. Can he really talk pigeon? Is he turning into some kind of far out Dr. Doolittle? This could be it. He could be able to talk to animals, man. This is far out. What a discovery] Harry: What's up, man. Pigeon: Coo. Harry: Yeah, man. I know what you mean. This place is pretty polluted. Pigeon: Coo...Coo...Coo Coo. Harry: Come on, man, don't say that about yourself. You look like a most far out pigeon. There is a reason that Lila, the girl pigeon, doesn't want you, man. She's probably just afraid to rush into a long term relationship. Because once you get to that point, suddenly she's knocked up with fifteen kids cooing all over the place. Then you have to go out and get a job to support all of them because she's a lazy, not far out babe, man. Pigeon: COOOOOO... Harry: Damn skippy, man. Pigeon: Coo. Coo Coo Coo. Harry: Yeah, man. You just have to give her some far out space. Let her think things through, stay away. She'll come flying back to your nest to do the wild thing because she can't take another moment without you, man. Trust me, the sex will be bitchin', man! [Actually Harry has no idea what this kind of sex feels like. He's only had sex twice, but the pigeon doesn't know that] Pigeon: Coo!! Harry: Your welcome, man. Pigeon: Coo...Coo Coo Coo...Coo Coo. Cooooo! Coo Coo...PCW...Coo. Harry: Did you just say PCW, man? Pigeon: No. Harry: Ok...just checking, man. Pigeon: Coo. Coo Coo. Harry: Yeah, man. It's been a long road to get where we are now. I mean, we weren't even put in the tag team title match at Alliances. Now how badly does that suck, man? You bet your pigeon ass that it sucks! We're the greatest tag team, man. We know what we can do to those other punks, and the Front Office knows that too. If they really wanted to get those...things...that you get during Pay Per Views so companies get money, man...can't think of the word, man. Pigeon: Buy rates...Coo. Harry: Yeah, man!! Thanks! Buy rates!! If PCW really wanted buy rates then they would have put us in the tag team match, man, but they didn't. That's why Tommy is pushing for me to concentrate on this totally far out Cruiserweight tournament, man. Pigeon: Cruiserweight?!...Coo. Harry: Why does everyone say that, man? I am a Cruiserweight. But I'm going to do this thing, become the Cruiserweight Champion, man. For some reason when I beat that vilas guy I just had this sudden feeling that I was destined for this, man. That I was meant to have that belt around my love gut! It wasn't the Mary Jane talking either, it was fate. It's already etched in far out stone that I'm going to walk out of Alliances the Cruiserweight Champion. Pigeon: Coo Coo. Harry: I don't know where I got this positive attitude, man. I think Tommy has finally rubbed off on me, man. I'm just believing in myself, man. That's what you have to do if you want to get Lila to be with you forever. You just need to believe in yourself and things will fall into place. It's just weird sometimes because just when you feel like you should give up on it all, it happens. That single solitary moment you've been waiting for all that time, then you keep truckin', man. That's what I'm doing. I've been looking for signs to wake me up out of this daze, this lazy rhythm I've fallen into and suddenly I'm out of it. I'm training, eating right, and actually listening to what Tommy has to say, man. Pigeon: Coo. Harry: I know he talks a bunch of nonsense sometimes, but that's just his nature, man. [Harry doesn't even wonder how the hell this pigeon knows Tommy. Come on, it's a talking pigeon, Harry is still trying to deal with that fact] Pigeon: Coo. Harry: Thanks, man. I'm going to need all the luck that I can get. I'm going to wish you luck with Lila...remember just believe in yourself. [The pigeon nods and then flies away, leaving Harry alone on the generator once again. He smiles up at the sky which has become a shade of blue, purple, and black now. A few stars appear in the distance leaving Harry with an even larger smile on his face. You don't really see the stars in LA...well...ACTUAL stars, not the celebrity kind. You see those people all the time in LA, and you get pretty damn tired of it really. Most of them suck ass anyway, man] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE!!! [Harry flings upward from the generator. The surfer's voice runs through his ear canal like a not so bitchin' alarm clock. Was he asleep on that generator? Holy sh*tballs, he was. He should not have smoked two bowls. That always happens. He falls asleep and thinks that he is really awake. Or maybe he was never asleep in the first place and just blanks out to what he's doing. What the hell is he thinking, man? He's too stoned to figure it out. He'll do it later with all the other stuff he needs to work out] Tommy: Dude, Avril Lavigne just came over to the party, man! [Harry turns away from Tommy for a few moments, not really believing what he says. Why would Avril even come to Hotel California to see him? Doesn't make any sense. Avril was just a silly little crush that will pass with time. Harry just needs to cope with the healing process right now] Tommy: DUDE!! She's in your room...and ready to go! She want's your bodacious buddy!! [Harry raises his eyebrows, looks back at Tommy, and then a smile grows across his stubble filled face] Harry: She wan'ts my buddy, man? Tommy: Dude, she wants it all, compadre! Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!!! [Scene fades to black]
