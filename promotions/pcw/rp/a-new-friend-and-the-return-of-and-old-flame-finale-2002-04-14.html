A New Friend, and the Return of and Old Flame - Finale

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14, 2002 The scene fades in from pink and a large, Previously… text flashes at the top of the screen as the following plays for the fans to remember what happened last time: As Jack was trying to utter out a rebuttal to Johnny’s harsh comments, the sound of a door creaking is heard in the background from the front door. Jack jerks his head towards the door and a woman’s voice is heard as a look of gleam comes across Jack’s face… “Honey…I’m home!” The scene fades to pink and Heartbroken Production appears on the screen followed by a large… TO BE CONTINUED… We pick right back up where we left off. Jack pops off of his seat and sprints to the front door only to lift Christina up off of her feet to give her a big hug and a kiss as Johnny stares in “love disgust” at the table. “Well, I think that would be my calling to leave, so you two have a nice time and I’ll see you later,” Johnny Heart states as he gets up from the table and begins jogging to the front door. “Bye guys,” he says just before leaving, but gets absolutely no response as Jack and Christina stare into each other’s eyes, love struck. The guise on Jack’s face is priceless. Christina doesn’t seem to be as happy as he is though. She seems pleased to see him again, but is not nearly as excited as he is. Jack of course has reason to be excited as he has wondered about what has happened on Christina’s trip ever since she left months ago. Jack has spent more time with his tag team partner in the past 3 months than he has talked to Christina…in fact, he hasn’t even talked to her for about a month and a half. “How was your trip,” Jack seemed to mutter out, hoping that she would say it was boring and how much she missed her boyfriend. “It was the most fun I have ever had in my entire life on a photo shoot,” she said excitingly. “I met so many great people!” Jack’s happiness turned into a look of sorrow, somberness if you will. Exactly what he dreaded she would say came out of her mouth in a matter of seconds. “Who all did you meet?” Jack questioned as he takes a big gulp in fear of what might come out of her mouth next. “I met some great models, they were really fun to be around and I think I made a lot of lifetime friends, in fact one of them is coming over here today,” she said. A huge sigh of relief and then a comical grin graces the face of Jack after Christina says this. The cynical thoughts that enter Jack’s mind cause him to go back to his old fun loving self. “Threesome!” Jack exclaimed. After a small pause and a smile from Christina… “Sure, I’m definitely in babe,” she said in a serious manner. Jack pauses and thinks about her being serious about this. He wasn’t expecting this at all, but now that she did, he runs upstairs without continuing his conversation and comes back down in a pink robe with a pipe in his mouth and his hair in the “perfect” position. He looks up at the “ceiling mirror” that covers the entire area of his house and checks himself out. He points up as if he’s pointing to God, but in reality he is pointing at himself in the mirror as he winks. Within seconds of him coming downstairs, a knock is heard at the door and Jack’s eyes light up. Christina turns around to answer the door and a big-chested, gorgeous model is standing on the other side…the only problem for Jack is that this model…is a man. The boyish excitement that Jack had vanished just as quick as it came. The strikingly good looking man enters the door and kisses Christina on the lips and gives her a rose. An infuriated Jack storms to the front door and slams it shut on his face. “What the hell are you doing Christina?!?” He yelled. “What do you mean? I didn’t know he was going to kiss me and bring a rose, but it was awful sweet of him…why don’t you ever do anything like that for me?” She replied back. “That’s not the point right now, we’re supposed to be together, unconditional love…remember?” Jack said. “I do love you sweetie, and Jacques is just a friend of mine…” she said, before being interrupted by Jack. “Jacques?? Jacques??” Jack begins laughing hysterically and then swings the door back open. “Come on in Jacques…you’re more than welcome in my home…I know a guy with the name Jacques couldn’t steal my girlfriend away…” Jack sarcastically said. The Frenchman enters the house and extends his hand to shake Jack’s but pulls it away as Jack extends his. “Hoi, Hoi, Hoi…you think I will let this beautiful hand of mine touch such a scoundrel as yourself…think again you pitiful American,” Jacques stated to Jack. Jack looks at him puzzled, confused, in utter shock. Christina just ignores the fact that the man just made fun of her boyfriend. It seems that Jacques is almost the same personality that Jack used to have before Jack became humorous. Jacques is a cocky, arrogant, egotistical man that no one can stand to be around…no one except for Christina that is. Jack seems to realize what’s going on here…Christina is going to leave Jack for this new guy, an old version of himself…well, not if he has anything to say about it. As Jacques and Christina sit down at the table, Jack goes over and sits down on top of the countertop. He begins looking in the mirror at himself, just to check and see if this guy looks any better than him and realizes that no one can come close to that feat. Upon glancing over at Jacques, Jack notices that he is looking in the mirror at himself as well, and Christina is just gazing into his eyes. Irritated, Jack stands up and begins to mutter out some words… “Hey Jacques! Why do-” Jack said before being interrupted by Jacques. “Jacky, Jacky, Jacky…don’t you understand that I am better than you in every aspect of life…you lead a lousy career, can’t win a fight and you’ve got no girl,” Jacques said in a cocky tone. “Bu-” Jack tried to say before being interrupted again. “And you stand no chance with your new little butt buddy in the tag team division of PCW. Sensations of Annihilation, Misplaced…they’re both going to kill you and Johnny in your meaningless matches on these upcoming cards. I alone could kick the crap out of both of you…why? Because I’m prettier, that’s why!” Jacques said, standing to roll up his sleeves as if he wants to fight. “Boys…can’t we all just get along?” Christina innocently tried to say before getting a collective… “Shut Up!” …from the both of them. Jack charged and Jacques and tried to spear him into the kitchen table, but he moved and Jack slammed his head against the chair. Jacques began stomping all over a helpless Valentine. Not before long a creak at the front door is heard and… “I forgot my-” …is said by Johnny Heart before realizing what is happening to his tag team partner. Johnny rushes over to the aid of Jack, pulling Jacques off of him and punching him in the face. After a kick to the gut, Johnny lifts Jacques high in the air and drops him with a big spinning power bomb. Jack gets up and brushes himself off before stomping on Jacques a few times. “Nobody does that to me in my house…lift him up Johnny, this boy needs a Heart Attack,” Jack stated. “Leave him alo-” Christina uttered before Johnny raked the chest of the helpless Frenchie. Following the chest rake, Johnny nailed the chin of the foreigner with a huge super kick…Jack jumped on the countertop and then flew off hitting a flying bulldog onto the broken table pieces. “Oh my God!” Christina screamed. “Why’d you do that?!?” “So now you’re taking his side,” Jack exclaimed. “He was the one who started this in the first place.” “Jack, he’s gay…he wasn’t hitting on me,” she said. “This is so typical of you.” Christina storms upstairs as Jack and Johnny laugh at the little queer Frenchman lying in the broken table pieces. They high five and the scene fades to pink as the ever so famous Heartbroken Production text appears on the screen.
