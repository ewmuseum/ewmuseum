Bring Me Back To Life - Part 2: The Road Ahead

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 22 - 03 When you lose someone you love, it leaves a tremendous gap. A gap that can hardly be filled enough to sustain happiness for more than a meagar moment. So what do you do? You have to find something to fill it, or you'll slip into a brooding madness. But what could ever possibly replace something so precious? ... Another love. -------------------------------- The road before us seemed to stretch on for days. It was a never-changing, subtle scene of gray, white, and yellow. A flowing sea of wofting green grass passed us by like a dream, a peaceful one, crying out for those suffering agony to sit in its tiny waves and forget life's troubles. Boy, what I would have done for something like that... Overcoming the suffering that life brings worth was never an easy thing for me. Even the slightest thing, such as a dropped grade in school, would cause me some grief. I have never really figured out why I let things get to me so much, and it's beyond me how I've managed to avoid ulcers or any kind of brain tumor from all the mental anguish. And with the episode I was going through, I'm surprised I didn't have a HEART attack... Breezing along in the backseat of our dad's Pontiac Grand Am, I just stared out to the road, watching it go by like Life around me. It all seemed to move so fast. One minute you're being born, the next you're dying. But yet, in such a short time span, so many things can happen... so many good things... and so many bad things. And let me tell you, I wasn't in the greatest mood. It felt like there was a huge lead weight tied around my heart, and the actual weight was sitting on my stomach. I felt sick but couldn't force to make myself throw it up at the hospital. I was feeling dizzy every now and then, for virtually no reason. I knew the events that had transpired were causing it, but these things came without warning... it was just a tangled mess. A very tangled web had been woven, so to speak. The only thing that came from me during the ride was the occassional sigh as I stared out the window, oblivious to my surroundings. Dad was obviously driving and my brother Marcus would look back at me every now and then and give me a puzzled look. I of course saw him, but made no move to acknowledge that I had seen him. He'd then shoot me a nasty look and turn his creepy green eyes back to the road. What a wonderful family... 'I miss her already,' I blurted out with a muffled voice, since my lower jaw was pressed against the palm of my hand. My voice was clogged with sorrow and I knew that, but that didn't stop me any. My brother slowly turned towards me with an annoyed look on his face, the reasons unknown to me. 'Let's not talk about it, alright Dorn??' He snapped at me, like I had spoken of something very taboo. It was our fucking deceased MOTHER I was missing, for En'Flarim's sake! 'We know you miss her, but if you sit there and dwell on it, you'll get more upset!' Marcus then turned back around with a shake of his head, like he was disciplining a little kid. Dwell on it?? How could I not?? I had lost someone who would have given up all 24 hours of the day for me, and yet, I was getting RIDICULED for 'dwelling' on it? That was enough to snap me out of my pseudo-coma and put me right in the nasty mood I had recovered from not too long ago... which was caused by Marcus. What a surprise. I uncradled my head from my palm and gave him a shocked look, 'DWELL on it, Marcus? I'm sorry if I cared for Mom and don't consider it DWELLING! I consider it CARING!' And in all honesty, that's what I truly thought. Marcus obviously didn't though. My brother whipped his head back and scowled at me, a look so nasty it could curdle milk, 'JUST SHUP UP ALREADY, ALRIGHT! We'll just talk about it later! Just shut up about it!' Without even waiting for my reply, he turned right back around. What a guy. The next thought that crossed my mind was how Dad was taking this. He hadn't said a word since ordering us back to the car from the hospital, and even then, he looked and acted extremely bothered. Understandably so, but the doctor claimed Dad had him pull Mom's plug. Was Dad feeling guilty? I'd probably commit suicide if I EVER thought of killing Mom, but Dad had done it. The man that loved her, married her, and lived with her for years called for such a thing. Was it because he loved her and wanted the best for her? I wanted to think so, but a sixth sense I have told me that wasn't the case. I didn't want to believe it at first, but slowly... it begin to dawn up me that my hunch might be true. But why? Why would Dad have... KILLED Mom? The questioned plagued me as we continued down the rural road, passing the occasional house. Marcus said something to me, but I didn't even hear him. I'm sure he said it again, but I still didn't catch it. Finally, he slugged me forcefully on the shoulder, causing me to whip my angry gaze in his direction. 'WHAT?' I spat in such a rage that it SURPRISED me, but it didn't concern me. 'What do you want?!' 'Get your shit together, Dorn! We're almost there!' He snapped, and I looked out the front window. Sure enough, we had reached the graveyard. My father still hadn't said a word, and it was beginning to unnerve me. He was usually pretty talkative, and I'm sure he would have stepped in during the fight between me and Marcus. What was up with him? I would soon find out. [End.]
