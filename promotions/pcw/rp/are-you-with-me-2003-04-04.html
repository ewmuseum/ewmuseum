Are you with me?

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/03 Snow falls out of the sky for yet another miserable day. To me it seems like things will never get better, despite the fact that this weekend we would have the chance to cease the Continental Championship within our grasp. But how could I expect such a thing to occur when Chris refused to even speak to me. Why would he bother to cooperate with me if he suddenly cared for me no more because of a simple mistake? Why would he bother to even show up? He wanted nothing to do with me anymore so I might as well flush my hope of winning this tournament along side of him down the drain. Perhaps there was hope though. We had been through hell and back before, so why couldn’t we do it again. Why couldn’t he realize that though I held a new last name now, I was still the Tessa he knew long and before this accidental bonding? Pondering these questions to myself I took a deep breath and crossed my fingers that once I reached the top of the stairs and approached him that he would finally understand and agree to work with me during this tournament to perhaps gain us yet another championship. The only thing left for me to do now was to talk to him, so with that I exhaled and began to ascend the stairs. 'Leave me blind so I may not see your sins' I heard a wise man say this before. Lying on my back I closed my eyes and thought to myself. I still had little hope left inside my body and soul. I knew that if I allowed myself that small bit of hope that something or someone would rip it from me violently. My Tag Team title was the only piece of evidence that I had any form of a successful career and yet now it was going to be taken from me within a short amount of time. Rolling over to my side I tried not to think about it. It was easier that way. Yet it was still taking its toll on me. Trying to rid myself of the pain has caused me to rid myself of my joys also. I couldn't hold my little Evan because every time I saw him I knew that soon he would be taken away by some woman I did not even know. No more could I kiss Tessa on the lips for she belonged to another man. Although she wanted no part of the relationship much like he did, the two was legally bonded by marriage and therefore made it a sin to enjoy the love Tessa could give him. After didn't another wise man once say, 'Honor thy neighbor?' Knock, Knock, Knock The sound of my fist knocking up against the bedroom door echoed throughout the narrow hallway. Outside I stood with hopeful thoughts thinking that perhaps this could be the moment where my bad luck turned around and Chris popped out of the door with cheery eyes and apologized for acting the way he had. But then I realized that was highly unlikely, I mean I’d probably act the same way if Chris was legally bonded to a girl I couldn’t stand. “Chris please open the door.” I shouted from outside the door, hoping that he would at least let me in. Even the slightest sign that he still cared for me would have cheered me up at this point in time. 'Maybe I should just tell Tessa that…' The sound of someone knocking at the door interrupted me from my train of thought. I opened my eyes and crawled out of the bed. I already knew who it was. I knew Tessa's knock by now. Her hands are small and she doesn't have much force but it's still louder then when the kids knock. I opened the door to see her frowning face. '….' I couldn't say anything. I was too speechless. Before me she stood weak and vulnerable. I had seen Tessa down and out before but never like this. Her skin was pale; her eyes looked tired, a frown hung from her lips. I'll admit she didn't look her best but for some strange reason I actually had forgotten that her last name was Green. I forgot the pain I was feeling. I looked at her, she looked at me. It was just like the moment I first felt that ping of love I had for her, when she was laying in the cake with me holding the chair ready to deliver it upon her head. Then she had looked at me with the same eyes that she did now. Those eyes asked me, 'why? Why are you doing this to me?' I couldn't do anything but take in her beauty, not able to say a thing to her. “Chris.” I began to start up as I stared at him through the crack of the door that was open. “Can we talk?” Saying the first thing that came to my head I moved out of the way to let her into the room. 'Sure.' I walked into the room and took a seat on the bed. Chris followed behind me but instead of sitting down on the bed beside me he took a seat on the carpeted floor, unable to keep his focus on me. “Chris please listen to me. I know you are upset about the wedding thing but it wasn’t my fault I swear.” I started up, my voice quivering nervous as to what his reaction to all this may be. Perhaps he didn’t want to bring this back up. Perhaps he was sick of having to have the name Tessa Green land on his ears day in and day out and rub salt into his wounds that seemed like they would never heal. I cringed at the mere mention of her wedding. 'Why does she insist on bringing it up so much' I had to ask myself. Tried as best I could to keep my eyes off her, I couldn't help but look at her sitting on my bed. I had to turn my attention away from her so I looked over to the Tag Team title sitting on the dresser. I still couldn't bring myself to words. “I know you hate hearing all this Tessa Green stuff as do I, but there’s nothing I can do about it. But I want to know if you are with me on Saturday when the tournament takes place.” I said, feeling his actions tug at my heart like he wanted to rip it out of me and show me just all the pain he had been feeling as a result of the marriage of Gaz and I. 'The tournament, is that what she came in here for?' I asked to myself silently. Here I had thought she just wanted to make sure things was alright between us but it turns out all she cares about is if I will be on her side for the tournament. Have I not been on her side all along? Why would that change now? I grinded my teeth as I started to think about it. She cares more about winning then she does about our relationship. Maybe that is why we have been together so long. She just wanted to a free ride to the top. She saw a man who was talented and loved the sport so she knew she could hook up with him and be carried to the top. Anger boiled inside of me. All these thoughts were just running through my head now. I rose up from the carpet and walked over to the doorway. I placed my hand on the frame and looks back at her sitting on the bed. I could only utter out 'We'll see' to her. I knew if I said more to her I might say something I would regret. So with those words I left her to herself in my room, walking down the stairs and toward the door for a walk that would hopefully calm me down. Being left behind on the bed I called out to him but he seemed to have no interest in listening to me so I simply sighed before flopping back and allowing my back to impact with the mattress. It seemed as though there was nothing that was going to fix the relationship between Chris and I, and with that I began to realize that without Chris by my side supporting me, our days as tag champs were numbered.
