Voyage to the Gold: Conclusion

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 12, 2002 Our brains were in a whirl. It was the night of Supremacy, and we had been on this voyage for a long time, even longer than the story may portray. We have been on this voyage our entire lives. Life comes down to a few moments, and this is one of them. This match would be our judge, jury and executioner. This one single moment in our lives would decide the present, past and future. This one match was absolutely the bottom line, it was it...if we did not win tonight, our efforts will have been wasted. For all of the PINK! children, for all of the PINK! fans, for everything that represents the PINK! We had to do it. Directly, Jack had shut the door of his closet and was ready to go. He started shouting at Johnny. 'Come down at once you wretched flamer! We have to go!' Jack screamed at the top of his lungs with sweat dripping down the side of his face in nervousness. He yelled at the foot of the stairs in sort of a frenzy. Not even an echo answered him however. Then all of the sudden, a sparkling glimmer appeared at the top of the stairs. Johnny was standing there in somewhat of a 'Greek God' pose, wearing a large, heavy black robe with pink sparkles glistening the entire room. He held another robe draped across his extended hands...a pink one with black sparkles. The sparkles lit up his ever so livid, hard face...closed against any fear or nervousness whatsoever. Jack watched as he came down step by step as if he was a ring bearer in a wedding. Jack stepped back with what looked to be a tear in his eye; to go along with the sweat dripping down his face, it made for an ever humorous expression. Johnny extended the robe out to Jack and Jack just stared at it with a priceless picture on his face. 'For me?' Jack questioned with a tear. 'This is so special, I think I'm going to cry.' Jack gently took the robe and Johnny helped him put it on. 'I know how much this match means to you Jack, so I took the liberty of trying to make this a special occasion. I had these made the other day and I wanted to surprise you,” Johnny said. “Thank you...thank you so much!” Jack exclaimed. Jack reached out to Johnny and laid a big hug on his tag team partner, tears rolling down both of their cheeks. It was almost as if we were in a parallel dimension. Jack and Johnny showing this kind of emotion? Who would of thought they would care about anything or anyone than their own individual selves? Although they were only caring about each other, it still made for a picturesque, yet awkward moment to see. In fact, it was two men crying and hugging each other...but it was ok, because this was special...wasn’t it? The glow of embers was all but out. As the sunset fell a PINK! aura came over the sky. It was as if the sky was sending them a sign, a sign that they would defeat the evil and corrupt Politically Incorrect. They hopped in their PINK! Cadillac and sped off towards the sunset and to the airport. They both pulled out pitchers of PINK! lemonade and began drinking them. “PINK! lemonade...the drink that gets us Heartbreakers pumped for every match,” Johnny said, in almost an advertisement type voice. After the discomfited place for an advertisement, being that it was after the very emotional moment for the pair, the two got right back in their sensitive state and began thinking about the fact that tomorrow, they could be PCW World Tag Team Champions. The thought was almost orgasmic... Not even in the days when the belt meant nothing more than a jobber belt would the Heartbreakers have felt any less excited about this match. The belts‘ very own aura, or presence, seemed to give off some sort of power to the Heartbreakers...the same kind of power that they receive when someone mentions or wears the color PINK!. However, on the radio, The Heartbreakers heard a public service announcement that Des Moines, Iowa would be trying to rid of the color PINK!. This put a big damper on the feelings that the Heartbreakers had possessed. How could any place in the world not want PINK!? It was the color of champions, the color of style, the color of their lives. “How could they do that, how could they want to get rid of the color PINK!?” Jack screeched at the top of his lungs. “This is ridiculous! I am outraged. I am infuriated. I am offended. I am flat out upset,” Johnny responded. Suddenly they became intensely alive to the whole situation. They bounded back from their emotional state and became very energized about the match tonight, almost crazed to defeat the men that want to outlaw PINK! in Des Moines, Iowa. Their hands trembled but and Jack jerked the wheel of the Cadillac out of pure rage. He was burly, venerable, a little indignant, and full of obsession. Johnny began punching the dashboard of the car. He then pulled out what looked like a makeup case and began rapidly applying makeup. “I’m going to look the best I EVER have when I kick these Politically Incorrect uglies!” Johnny screamed. In an almost girly fashion, Jack snapped out of his craze and asked Johnny; “Oh...good idea stud...give me some when you’re done,” Jack said. And so the scene faded as they reached O‘Hare airport. With the sudden, free, spontaneous agility of a crazed tiger, the Heartbreakers had to have the advantage going into this match. Politically Incorrect was trying to take away the one thing that meant the most to both Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart...PINK! The vigor, the instinctive precision of their minds, were something amazing. The PINK! would be in full effect tonight at the pay-per-view. Politically Incorrect has been the champions longer than anyone in PCW history and it is up to the boys in PINK! to end that reign. Change is freedom, change is life. The politicians especially should know that. It's always easier not to think for oneself, we let the politicians think for us. Find a nice safe hierarchy and settle in. Don't make changes, don't risk disapproval, don't upset your syndics. It's always easiest to let yourself be governed. There's a point, in everyone‘s life, when you have to choose whether to be like everybody else the rest of your life, or to make a virtue of your peculiarities. Those who build walls are their own prisoners. The Heartbreakers are going to go fulfill their proper function in the social organism and the political hierarchy here in PCW. The voyage to the gold has ended, they have reached their destiny...it’s time to unbuild walls.
