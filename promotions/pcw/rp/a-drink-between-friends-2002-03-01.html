A drink between friends

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/1/2002 ~Determination. The greatest single reason behind the greatest things to ever happen in history. The Sons of Liberty were a group of revolutionaries before the American Revolution who were determined to realize their dream of the American colonies being free of British rule. The Sons of Liberty were so determined that they committed acts of treason (i.e. Boston Tea Party, Boston Massacre, hell, the whole damn revolution was an act of treason). Yet these brave men were so determined that they didn't care about the consequences of their actions. These men had balls to spare. Jon Kano is much like these men. He has been the only man to have the, er, testicular fortitude to stand up to the establishment of PCW. That is why he is so admired by the fans and the wrestlers in the locker room. Jon Kano never backs down from a challenge, nor from the unjust owner of PCW, Tyler Nelson. Jon Kano has determination running through his veins right now. His determination is not centered on one person, nor a small group of people, but rather nineteen other people. Twenty men, in total, will enter the PCW ring at March Madness. One man will remain, and that man will be crowned the PCW World Champion. He will have truly earned the right to be called, 'Champ.' This man will not be some no-name, talentless jobber whose market price is a dime a dozen. This man will have to be a man who is a big name, a man who is worthy of the world title, and a man with the necessary in-ring skills to win the title and to keep it pure. With that given criteria, half of the field has been eliminated. It leaves about six men: Miyagawa, Boda, Snow, Tyler Nelson, Jon Kano, and The Chef. Now that is a nice little field of possible winners, isn't it? These are men who all can claim to be legitimate contenders, but none of them are like Jon Kano. No one on this earth is like Jon Kano. But who should really win the title? It will come down to who wants it the most and who is the most deserving. All of these men, except for Kano and Miyagawa, have either held the World Title or had numerous opportunities to capture the title. Miyagawa might be able to pull it off, but due to his size, the odds are against him. Jon Kano is big, not only physically, but in his heart. He fears no one. He didn't fear Ivan Stanislav or the Red Army. Kano, each and everytime, charged at Stanislav or the Red Army with no hint of fear. He did his best, but the numbers were against him. The numbers are against everyone in this match. But determination will separate the men from the boys.~ *The scene opens up inside of a huge penthouse suite in Kansas City, Missouri, the location of Friday Fusion, the last PCW event before March Madness. Jon Kano is at the bar included within his suite. Kano, dressed in a March Madness t-shirt, jeans, and black boots, is making himself a martini. Suddenly a knock comes to Kano's suite's door.* Kano: Yeah. Who is it? Voice: It's Chris, Jon. Kano: Come in, the door is unlocked. *The door opens and in walks Chris Sloboda, better known to PCW fans, as Boda. Boda walks over to Kano, shakes his hand, and grabs another martini glass for himself. Boda sits on the bar, in all of his PCW-paraphenailia dressed glory.* Boda: Can you spare another martini for an old friend? Kano: Yeah. I was expecting you. I got enough booze for us to get royally sh*tfaced before Fusion. Boda: You can get sh*tfaced, I'll stay half-way sober. Kano: It's about that time, Chris. Boda: What time? Kano: I get the same feeling every time I have a big match. This is the earliest I have gotten this feeling. Do you realize forty-eight hours from now, one of us could be the PCW World Heavyweight Champion? Boda: Yeah. But there are eighteen other guys that could win, too. Kano: Yeah, but you and I have a really good shot at winning. Boda: And why is that? Kano: We have the size. Boda: I have always heard that size doesn't matter. Kano: Is that what your wife tells you? *Both men share a laugh.* Boda: That is just one thing. Kano: I wasn't finished. Boda: Oh, please elaborate. Kano: We have the size, experience, heart, and ability to win this whole damn thing. Boda: Do you actually think that we have that great of a chance? Kano: I know that you think you have a great chance of winning, and I'll tell you one thing. YOU DO. I look at you, Chris, and I see a man who was a joke. You left, improved, and have surpassed me. When you were first in PCW, I was much better, I was the dominant big man in PCW. When you returned, you were a new man. You have become bigger in status than I, but I am back and better than ever. I was in a funk for along time, I was so fixated on taking out Tyler Nelson, that I couldn't concentrate on winning my matches. Then a few days before Decimation, I realized that I am just one man and I cannot take down Nelson's regime by myself, and I know that corrupt people corrupt their own regimes into extinction. Ever since Decimation, I have dominated, I have won nearly twice as many matches as I have lost, and I am better now than I have ever been. You and I are starting our primes of our careers. Our future is not known, and we cannot control it, but we can sure as hell do our best to make an impact that will never be forgotten. I look at myself in this match and I see one thing and one thing only. Boda: What is that? Kano: Gold. I see the aftermath of the March Madness battle royal. I see myself standing in the middle of the ring with the PCW World Title around my waist. And, Chris, do you know what is one of the best things in our favor? Boda: What? Kano: You and I have drawn two very good spots. Boda: We aren't at the end. Kano: But we aren't at the beginning. If we were to have drawn say, numbers 18 and 19, and one of us would win, then people would claim it was just the luck of the draw. We are right in the middle. Some men will be gone, some yet to come, we get to hit who ever comes down and into the ring, before they have a chance to defend themselves, and something like that could be a deciding factor. Boda: I suppose you have a point. Are you about done with that f*cking martini? Kano: Oh yeah, I forgot I was even mixing. *Kano pours Boda a martini out of the martini mixer and then pours himself one. Both men toast each other and take a sip of their martini.* Boda: Damn this is martini is so dry, it hurts. Kano: Are you bitching? Boda: No, I just forgot that you make the martinis this dry. Kano: There is just one thing I am really concerned about in this match. Boda: What is it? Kano: A person. Boda: Nelson? Kano: No, I have kicked his ass before and I'll kick his ass again at Madness, hell I might do it tonight just for sh*ts and giggles. Boda: Snow? Kano: No. Boda: Kid Wonder? Kano: No, he is too early on in the match, and he is just too egotistical to survive. His cockiness will f*ck him in this match. Boda: Legend, INC? Kano: No, he is too early, also. Plus, I think there is too many people out to get him from bad feelings from his first stint in PCW. Boda: Miyagawa? Kano: No. Too small. Boda: The Chef? Kano: No, knowing him, he will probably stop and pray to the East half way through the match, get kicked in the back of the head and out and over the top rope to the floor. Boda: Well, then, who? Kano: YOU. Boda: ME? Kano: YEAH, YOU. Chris, I love you like you were my brother. I know how much you want the World Title. I know how much you want to be the World Champion. I know that you will have your wife and children at ringside and I know you want to win it in front of them. I know you probably want it more than just about anyone in this match, except me. I say this, with all of the love and respect in my heart towards you, if it comes down to you and me at the end of this match, I will TAKE YOU OUT. Boda: I'll do the same with you. Kano: I would expect nothing less from you, Chris. You know why? Boda: Why? Kano: Just because you and I are cut from the same stock. You and I are the men who want this the most. You and I are determined to win this, and not a single person or group can deny one of us from winning this match. Why? Because you and I are WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as the two friends continue to drink their martinis and think of their upcoming match, which could decide their future within PCW.* ~Determination. These two men look determined to realize their dreams of PCW World Championship gold, but the question is, who's destiny is it to become the PCW World Heavyweight Champion?~
