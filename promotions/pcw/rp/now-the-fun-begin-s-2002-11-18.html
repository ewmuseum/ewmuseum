Now the Fun Begin's

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/18/02 In a tandem motion a pair of large weighted dumbbells pumps rhythmically up and down, with a pair of brown hands clasped to them. A drop of sweat falls to the ground slowly, suspended in the thick air, almost as it if were being held up by a spiritual force. “This is the part of the game when we must look past ourselves and see that there are so many variables Mike. I’ve been there, I know what its like to win this thing.” The clanging of the metal bars as they hit the ground rings forth in the weight room of Timo Bolamba. He stands across the room from his partner in crime, Mike Marvelous. These two hand picked FSW athletes will be competing in the biggest extravaganza of their lives, PCW Alliances. “Mike, it isn’t that you can get beat up, it isn’t that you will be hurt, its that burn man. Once the cages drop, your adrenaline will soar, your fingers will tingle with anticipation, your heart will pound like it wants to break out of your chest. Its something I just cant describe, though I’m sure you know.” Mike nods, giving a slight grin to Timo as he picks up one of Timo’s dumbbells and almost falls over, grunting. “Yeah, I used to be in PCW too bud. I know all about it. So umm, lemme ask you something.” “Shoot” Mike weighs his words heavily, and shrugs a bit. He knows deep down the competitor Timo is, but something was out of whack this week. He seemed as if he was seriously trying to pull a rabbit out of a hat. The muscles on Timo’s arms ball up as he grabs a towel and wipes his face dry. “Well, you have been training like almost non stop lately. In fact, I haven’t seen you do anything outside of this house except for shows. Don’t you want to go do something?” The Samoan grabs a water bottle and sprays his face lightly. The water beads up and rolls down, dripping onto his chest and down past the tribal tattoo on his lower stomach known as a Pe’a. Timo’s body had become increasingly ripped lately, his muscle tone taking on that of a Bruce Lee type. Every time he flexed his body, he wasted no motion. Mike pulled his wallet out and looked inside. “Shit, why cant we go get some food? I’m starving.” “Shh. Come on man, we are in training. Do you expect to win if you are always out eating greasy food and chasing tail?” Mike shrugs and smiles. “It’s a lot more fun than this. Sure there’s nothing wrong with a good old fashioned workout, but what you are doing is insane. It’s like ever since Tyler Nels…” “Shaddup!” Mikey holds his hands up as Timo shoots him a death glance. The Samoan Ass Kicking Machine spat on the ground and turned to a heavy bag, rocking it with a right hand. The bag swings around, and there is a picture of Nelson taped to it, mostly torn apart. Timo waits as the bag spins, and drills it again, dead on right in the nose…rather where the nose would be. “Hey man, it’s me…you know, Mikey? Don’t play the fool. You beat me for that belt you carry, you know damned well I respect that Timo. But this is just wrong, you are killing yourself in here, hell you haven’t even worked on that new car you bought, and you LOVE cars!” Timo grits his teeth again, unusually silent. Normally he was quite jubilant, but lately, ever since mob Rule showed up he had taken a seriousness that was unreal. Timo wasn’t the type of guy to shirk off his workout rituals, but as Mike had said, lately, there was no leaving. No antics, just straight up tenacity. “Mike, there are some things you just don’t understand. When I am down, I have to get back up…I don’t know why. Right now, we are down. We just cant put these guys away, and I cant let it get to the point that Azala and Afwa are in the same boat as Cassius. Look, you and I both know he can’t beat Angelo, not with these odds.” “Timo, you are talking about a guy we are supposed to support, and you aren’t doing much of it. You should be training with him right now, not me. I can handle myself you know. Tyler and Angelo don’t scare me, and if we get a chance to put on the show of a lifetime in PCW, then I say lets do it.” Timo turned and cracked his knuckles. He even had a bit of a smile on his face. “That’s right…” “What’s right?” Timo’s million-dollar smile broke for the first time in days as he seemed to have come upon an idea, lord knows what he had cooking in his crazy mind. One thing was for sure though, when he had the look…the same one he was giving Mike, something fun was indeed cooking in his head. “Oh…I always did enjoy putting on a show for PCW. I think you are right Mike…we need to go have some fun. This has been long overdue, and I think that the Alliances match just might be the opportunity I have been looking for.” “Umm…for what?” Timo watched as the bag slowly spun around and he cocked his fist back, drilling the picture of Tyler Nelson, and ripping a hole in the bag. He reaches down and grabs a barb wire wrapped Tiki Torch and spins in his fingers. “Dude, it’s time to cut loose. There has been this chain around my neck since we got to FSW, and it seems like I have just been fighting so much I forgot how much fun it was to bring my opponent to the edge. All the pain, all the blood, all the tears, washed away by a bitter river of rage. I can’t live like this Mike.” Mike nods and turns on his chair, looking Timo in his green eyes. The Five Star Wrestling Champion was grinning like Snow in a strip club as he balled his fists and flipped the torch from hand to hand. The complex simplicity that was Timo shined as he turned to grab Tyler Nelson tee shirt. “Timo, you umm…yeah. Just do what you plan to do.” “You don’t know do you?” “Know? I’m afraid I don’t follow.” Timo tosses Mike a shirt, and The Canadian opens it, revealing an Angelo Deville slogan along with the mobsters face. He looks at the crazy Samoan and shrugs putting it on. “What is the point behind this?” “Mike my good man, you said we need to enjoy ourselves right?” “Right” Timo pulls out a shirt for each man in “Mob Rule” and then reveals a bunch of Halloween masks. He smiles as he tosses one to Mike, and then slips on a Tyler Nelson mask. He clears his throat and does his best Nelson impression. “Hello America, my name is Tyler Nelson, the Greedy Grabass, if you will. Now, I own many things, but one thing I don’t own is a pair of testicles. That’s why I shop at Earls discount prosthetics. I can find anything I want there, be it big, or as I prefer, raisin sacked.” Mike laughs his ass off as he reveals the methods behind Timo’s madness. He puts the Angelo mask on and holds his hands in the air. “I am not a crook.” Timo turns his head to the side in thought. “Dude, that was Nixon, wasn’t it?” Mike shrugs. “They are both going to have the same eventual ending.” Timo looks confused, even under the idiotic mask he wears. “Spirew T. Agnew is going to pardon him?” Mike simply laughs a bit and shakes his head. “No way dude.” “I’m afraid I don’t really get it.” Mike is quick to oblige though, and soon it is completely clear. “Hi, my name is Angelo Deville, a half time crook who just can’t seem to hit it big. When I get depressed, I turn to my hero, and reach for his own brand of produce, Richard Nixon’s ImPeaches.” Mike takes the mask off. “What do you think?” Timo shrugs a bit and laughs. “It needs work, but then again, nobody ever said Angelo was all that bright, so I guess you got the role down pact. Come on man, I got an idea for some fun.” Mike chucks the mask down and walks out of the room, and as he does Timo tosses his mask on a bench. He turns toward the bag again as it swings and sand pours out of the hole that used to be Tyler Nelson’s face. The Champion grins a bit and flicks the light off, darkness shrouding the room. He turns and closes the door offering one last bit of dialogue before the camera fades to black. “See you at Alliances boys, it’s time for me to show you all why I am The Champion. Good luck.”
