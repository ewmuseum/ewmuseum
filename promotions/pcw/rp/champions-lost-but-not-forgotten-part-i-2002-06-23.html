Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part I]

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 23, 2002 Among so many effective and talented teams, it is difficult to give a preference to one over all the rest. Yet, certainly, even amid PCW’s remarkable teams, the last champions must be given high rank. Perhaps their story will be remembered when even teams like Da Westidaz and Goovil have been obliterated by centuries of time. At least, of the many teams that have since arrived since the time of Jack and Johnny, not one has yet succeeded in equaling or even approaching them. In one way 'Gold and Glory' shows a marked contrast to The Heartbreakers earlier matches. Not only does it invade a region more remote than even the battle against Poltically Incorrect when they captured their first titles, but this match here abandons all that Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart have learned in their tenure as a tag team in PCW. In order that they must really work together in this match, and not let the other fall behind. They have to show us what true champions can do in certain conditions, and if they can pull through when the odds are stacked against them like this. Undefeated in PCW Tag Team championship matches, The Heartbreakers are. People have talked about the Heartbreakers like their time is gone...their reign was a one shot deal. Yet further, they have more experience than any other team in the ring Sunday. Those that say their time has not passed are saying that they will not win because they are two focused on another two men...Pretty Cool Guys. As the Heartbreakers embark on their quest to become champions, they are also on a journey to be a part of a band. This past week has been a rocky road for The Heartbreakers, losing to Cowabunga Connection on Monday and costing Pretty Cool Guys their match against them on Friday. How will the two teams be able to work together come Turmoil, and will this lack of focus hurt their chances at regaining what they believe is rightfully theirs? Only time will tell. The work in this business is painstaking, and it is not an easy task. So many teams fighting for the same prize, the odds are stacked against everyone in the ring this upcoming Sunday. No one can be a favorite, yet everyone seems to be underdogs. Our story is about the last people ever to where the belts around their waists, The Heartbreakers, who are in the same boat as every other team in PCW, and no one wants to fall overboard. Jack Valentine: “You know, if we were to become champions again, that would make us the only active ‘2-Time’ champions in the federation today?” Jack holds up his index and middle finger close together in a very provacative fashion. Johnny Heart: “You know, you’re right Jack, there has only been a handful of teams to hold those belts more than once, and we will be the next. After all, how could you have a list of 2-Time champions without having the PINK! listed right next to it?” The cameras zoom out to reveal Jack and Johnny sitting on a large steamboat in the middle of a large body of water, nothing to be seen on either side of them. It was sort of a desolate area, not a place one would normally see the boys in PINK! as they are usually somewhere trendy, or flashy...after all, it fits their image. Oddly enough, the cameras didn’t show any captain, or crew, or any other person of the sort...just Jack and Johnny. The destination of the ship was unknown, the purpose of the ship was unknown, the purpose of Jack and Johnny on that ship was also unknown. Pretty much everything about this was unknown, sort of like the tag team battle royal at Gold and Glory, or the mis-construed friendship of The Heartbreakers and Pretty Cool Guys. We’ve never really known much about Jack or Johnny...about their pasts, their personal lives...all we get to see is what they bring to the ring. However, any of us could see from watching a Heartbreakers promo that they are a couple of rich, powerful yet feminine men who are always worried about their sense of style, more than their sense or perception of the world around them. Somewhat humorous, yes...talented, of course...but The Heartbreakers were never thought of as one thing which is so true to their hearts. They might as well come out of the closet and say it... ...they love boats. (What did you expect?) Jack and Johnny could never get enough boating, and today was no exception. They were out on the crisp, cool waters of Lake Michigan, enjoying the sun and the beautiful tan that they were receiving. Jack Valentine: “This boat needs a job.” Johnny Heart: (With a perverted smirk) “What kind of job would that be Jack?” Jack Valentine: (Grossly playing along) “I don’t know, why don’t you guess...” Johnny Heart: “I don’t want to say it.” Jack Valentine: “Then why don’t you just DO it.” Johnny Heart: “Ok...” Johnny Heart licks his lips and bends over Jack’s lap. He reaches his hand down below and pulls up a paintbrush. Johnny Heart: “This boat needs a paint job!” Jack Valentine: “You got it...don’t forget to BLOW dry it when you’re done. Johnny begins painting “The Heartbreakers” in PINK! on the outside of the moving boat. Upon finishing, Johnny begins “blowing” on the paint to let it dry and amidst all of that, Jack smacks Johnny on the ass. Jack Valentine: “A job well done partner, a job well done.” Johnny Heart: “You know another job that I’m looking forward to doing Jack?” Jack Valentine: “What’s that partner?” Johnny Heart: “Teaming with Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore on Turmoil this week.” Jack Valentine: “Yeah, you’re right...it’s our big chance to prove to those singing studs that we are worthy of joining the band. I would just love to be with them in a band, all of us holding each others long, black...” Johnny Heart: “...microphones?” Jack Valentine: “Yeah, microphones...that’s it.” Johnny Heart: “I don’t know if they are going to be too happy with us though after what you did on Fusion though.” Jack Valentine: “Forgiveness is bliss. You can see on this tape right here that I didn’t mean to hit Ryan Moore...it was an accident.” Jack Valentine slides a tape in the slot on the small TV in front of him. The only thing that appears on the screen however, is static. Johnny Heart: “Damn things...they’re never big enough.” Johnny and Jack both look at each other with an awkward pause before Jack smacks the...TV...a few times and the footage is shown. Johnny Heart jumps up onto the side ring apron where the referee stops him from getting into the ring, but Jack Valentine slides in from the opposite side and pulls Tommy up off of Ryan Moore, he whips Tommy to the ropes, but as he does Moore starts to get to his feet and when Valentine goes to dropkick TTT, he inadvertently dropkicks Moore whom stumbles forward and knocks his partner Sherwin off of the ring apron. Moore is then rolled up by Tommy, and by now the referee has pushed Heart off the apron and Valentine has escaped the ring. The ref makes the count. 1...2...3 Dave: The Cowabunga Connection have taken it by a mistake from the Heartbreakers. Jeff: The idiots shouldn't have been out here anyway. Duke: And the Pretty Cool Guys will agree with you on that, hoss! Jack Valentine: “See.” Johnny Heart: “Yeah, I guess you’re right...if you look at it again, you’ll see that if it was anyone’s fault, it was Ryan Moore’s!” Jack Valentine: “You know, you’re right, we should definitely be forgiven and let into the band now. Their band sucks without PINK! anyways.” Johnny Heart: “You know, you’re absolutely right Jack...absolutely...it’s all about the PINK!” Jack Valentine: “Cheers Johnny.” Johnny Heart: “Cheers.” The pair clings their glasses filled with PINK! lemonade together as they continue to bask their self-proclaimed perfect bodies in the sun. ...TO BE CONTINUED...
