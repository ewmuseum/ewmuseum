Exploring the Soul Part 1: The First Love

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/03 Exploring the Soul Part 1: The First Love I was quite young… I remember seeing her for the first time and my heart practically fell to the floor. It wasn’t about sex or money; it was about pure and utter passion. A passion I can no longer make grip with. Tell me about her I you don’t mind. Well it was in about November, the leaves were becoming brittle and the winter was just around the way… I was walking through the streets like I would just about every day. Two Miles north to Cesar’s Tavern, then I’d cut through a large alley behind the Cinema and go back. I never had a clear agenda from day to day, but I always made it a priority to take this walk. The weather was cold and I shoveled through the brittle leaves kicking them aside. I never had to worry about anybody getting in my way when I was walking, because I wasn’t a very picturesque person to be around. My clothing was torn and the colors would become dull and distorted. My hair was long and botched on the sides. Every now and then I would try to cut my hair myself with an Armadillo carved knife I carried. It was most likely used more for protection purposes then to cut hair. So this girl… Yes, the girl. Well it was getting really cold as I was making my way back. I thought about finding a large store to warm up in because the smaller ones would just kick me out and call the police. I huddled in a store by the name of Victoria’s Plaza. It was a smaller version of an American mall. The place was immaculate and the people who owned it liked me because I would occasionally clean their chimneys. That’s when I saw her. Her eyes were bright blue and her hair was a golden blonde. Her skin looked like porcelain that had been so thoroughly cleaned that you can see your reflection in it. And those lips wow those lips. Lips that could make a boy fall to the floor and never want to get up again. It almost made me feel unworthy to look at her as my skin was discolored with the dirt it had acquired over the days. I couldn’t help myself from not staring at her though. It was definitely those blue eyes that got me. I knew she must have been from northern Spain, because in Barcelona not many people have blue eyes. I take it this girl came from wealth… Yes, extreme wealth, though I didn’t discover it until I saw her father come over. He was a large man with broad shoulder and a thick mustache. He was wearing a wool hat that I wished I could get my hands on. It had those funny looking earlaps, but when you were cold those earlaps sure paid off. I thought about going over, but when I saw the attire and gestures of the father I knew I would just be shooed away and I would cause more of a ruckus then anything. I was about to leave, when an unfortunate miracle happened. I fell to the floor after tripping on a hanger and right as my body pounded against the floor I saw her eyes lean in my direction. She looked straight at me and I didn’t know what to do. So like all young and uneducated boys I ran away. I figured the best way to escape a difficult situation was to run. And I’m guessing you left the Plaza at that point… I sure did. I ran all the way back to the small vacant room I slept in behind the church. I don’t know why I ran the whole way, but I guess it helped me think as the cold air pressed against my cheeks. As I slept that night I thought about every minor detail and tiny moment that took place in that plaza. I thought about it so much that I could practically paint a picture of every scene that took place. I also couldn’t get her face out of my mind. The way she turned around and just stared at me. I couldn’t figure out whether she was looking at me thinking what a fool, or if she actually thought something positive about me. I figured I’d put it to rest like everything else that happened to go right in my life. I didn’t think I’d ever see her again so I just let thinks be. So how did you ever get to meet her again? That’s where the luck of love came in. It was a few weeks later and the weather was once again terrible. I was on my same morning walk when the rain began coming down extremely heavy. The dirt on my body just washed away and my hair became extremely flat and drenched. I ran as fast as I could to Victoria’s Manner again. I just made it before the really heavy rain began to fall. I was grateful that I didn’t get caught in the storm, but I was even more grateful when I turned around and saw the blue eyes and golden blonde hair of the girl who momentarily stopped my heart. She had come back and I couldn’t believe it. I took it as a sign, much like I did many things in my life. I knew that if God allowed her to come back for me to see again, then something was meant to be. So I sucked up all the courage in my small and skinny body and walked toward her like a soldier making his way to the front line. I cleared my mind of every possible bad thought and went for it. I stood right behind her, until I finally got her attention. *Pause* So what happened? Well at first she just walked away. I couldn’t find any words to say so I just stood there silently. I thought it was all over and then she turned around and said I’ve seen you here before. I couldn’t believe she remembered who I was so I just smiled. From there we began to talk. She never acted as if she was higher or any better than me. We were just two people who enjoyed one another. The storm prevented everyone from leaving the plaza so we had a lot of time to just talk. I had nowhere to go so I didn’t mind one bit. I didn’t easily open up to people, but with her it just seemed so simple. I don’t even remember all the things I told her, but I know I told her a lot. And what happened after that day, did you ever see her again… Yes, she’d go to the plaza every week at the same time with her father, who didn’t at all approve of me. I would meet her there every week and she would distance herself from her father to be with me. We did that for quite some time. It was some of the best days of my life. Then what happened? Then everything just ended. One day she didn’t show up, then the next week, and then the week after that. At first I thought she might have been on vacation or had moved, but I knew she would have told me. Till this day it’s just big mystery. I suppose my first love ended in a quite negative manner. Do you think she may have influenced your life now, and the role women play in it? She may have, but I know I’ve never felt anything as strong as I did with her. She had something I couldn’t find in anyone else. She loved me for the poor, skinny, weak boy I was. She didn’t care about the looks, the money, and the fame. To her, she would have loved me either way. What was her name? Marie, sweet sweet Marie.
