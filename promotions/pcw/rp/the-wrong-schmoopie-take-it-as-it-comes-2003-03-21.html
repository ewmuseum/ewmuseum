The Wrong Schmoopie - Take it as it Comes

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/03/03 All was quiet in the house, silence reigned supreme over the stately manor as sleep regained its unrelenting grasp over the occupants of the household. Outside, the nigh watchmen paced the boundaries, the animals slept in their pens, the water trickled in the gardens and the plants closed their flowers to the darkness. All was well. Lacuna was asleep; she had been for some time now and wished to remain so for another eight or so hours. It was just gone three AM, and Lacuna had been in bed for at least five hours now. She held sleep very highly on her list of priorities and had done from an early age. It just made sense to her that if you were not tired then you made a much greater success in the following day. It had always been like this. Unconsciously, she stirred slightly and threw a tanned left arm wildly across the bed, turning to lie face down into the multitude of pillows adorning the end of her bed. She hadn’t bothered to draw the curtain surrounding her four-poster bed, opting instead for the closing of the black velvet curtains across the huge bay windows with the customary window-seat. Her raven black hair had begun to matt slightly in the heat of the radiated room. She had opted for turning the air conditioning off while she slept, so as not to have to listen to the constant groaning from the infernal machine as it sucked the precious warmth from the room. The pitch-black room was full of shapes, sinister shadows in the mysterious half-light that occupied the large room. Across from her bed was the TV area, with the large wall mounted machine sitting silently surrounded by two luxurious leather sofas that glinted slightly in the tiny stream of light from the crack in the door. There was the dining table and its singular padded wooden chair, the bathroom area with its bamboo framed curtain obstructing the view, the desk that should act as a study (but is generally used for piling with useless junk) and the now-empty music room, which would soon be filled with all wonder of musical equipment for Lacuna to get angry with and smash up when she misses a chord. Then, there was the shadow that moved. With a slight grunting noise and the rubbing of material on the leather sofa, a thin shadow rose to its feet and staggered across the room. Lacuna didn’t move, her eyes were still closed and her brain off in never-never land somewhere. The shadow stood still and raised its long arms into the air, stretching with another grunt. The shadow turned its head slightly, towards the unconscious vessel of the sleeping Lacuna, and with a scratch of its arse, it began to tiptoe silently across the room towards the bed. With cat-like agility, the feet of this trespassing shadow crept swiftly around the strewn soft toys and empty pizza boxes as it approached the large bed with and suddenly, when it became within reach, it pounced. Lacuna’s eyes shot open, and her hand swung out from underneath a pillow, smashing a bat around the side of the shadows head with a crack and a feminine grunt as the shadow falls off the end of the bed and onto a man-sized soft toy, which squeaks loudly like some sort of dogs toy. “Ow Bitch!” shouted the figure that now lied on the floor, clutching it’s head with a thin right arm. Lacuna moved slowly to her feet and, while making sure to be keeping a strong hold onto the bat, clapped loudly, sending the strip lighting flickering to full brightness. Momentarily blinded, Lacuna rubbed her hazel eyes with her elbow and, when she recovered, she glanced down to the figure eon the floor. It was Jade. “What the hell do you think you are doing, creeping around like that?” screamed Lacuna, who was still disorientated from her rude awakening. She placed the bat down and glanced at the strewn figure of Jade, who rubbed her head gingerly. “I kinda fell asleep on your sofa last night” began Jade, tentatively “Then I started wandering around, thinking this was my room, and I saw you so I thought I would pay you a surprise” Jade sat up slightly and crossed her legs like an obedient child at a primary school, trying desperately to gauge the reaction of Lacuna, who was still confused by all the action so early in the morning. Lacuna sat back down and grunted loudly, throwing the bat back towards her pillow mound. She glanced at Jade and smiled as the lump on the side of her face began to develop slightly. “Well, sorry about that I suppose, you bloody well shouldn’t have even come in here, should you?” added Lacuna, as if to back up her own thoughts “Well why do you keep that thing under your pillow?” “Well, if morons like you try to come in, then I can shut them up, can’t I?” Lacuna smiled, and Jade reciprocated the look “Were going out today! Somewhere exciting!” she announced, jumping to her feet. Lacuna rolled her eyes back into her head and fell backwards with a soft sounding noise as she hits down onto the multitude of pillows “Yeah, why not? In about another, well, eight hours, yeah?” Lacuna did not want to move, and was not used to being awake at such an hour. “Oh!” moaned Jade angrily “I’m awake now, I cant go back to sleep again, especially not with a lump this size on my head” “Well tough then, because I bloody well am” “Are not” responded Jade childishly, a grin spreading across her face like a pig in mud “Am too, now go away!” shouted Lacuna, who pulled her duvet around her head and pretended to sleep, which was to the clear annoyance of Jade who jumped to her feet in protest. “You are so not going back to sleep, missy. I am not gonna even think about letting you” responded Jade playfully, as she perched on the end of lacuna’s bed. “Zzzzz” responded Lacuna, who was clearly not asleep “You cant fool me, lady, your not going back to sleep, ever!” Lacuna sat up angrily and slapped Jade around the face, giving her one hell of an evil glare to back it up “Listen, I am never going to beat Tessa if I don’t get any sleep am I?” Lacuna snapped at the form of Jade, who giggle slightly “I thought you didn’t want to face Tessa anyway, wanted Storm instead, ya?” “Things have changed” her face became sharp and steely “Either or is fine for me, I am just going to win so we can get those tag title shots” “Did you not heat the Turmoil card? We have already got them!” bounced Jade happily Lacuna eyed her wearily, as if it was just a trick to get her out of bed. “Your lying, I can tell” “Nu-uh” “Yu-uh” “Nu-uh” “Oh this is stupid, have we really got them?” “Yeah, we have. The Schmoopies vs. Significant Others, Tuesday Turmoil” “Oh” Lacuna went silent. Things inside of her head had begun to whirl uncontrollably as she woke up and smelt the proverbial coffee. She didn’t even have to beat Tessa for the shot at the tag titles, it was just walking straight into her path. She didn’t have to wait to get at Storm any more, because they could get him anyway! Oh joy of joys! Then again, she could cause Storm even more angst if she destroyed Tessa as well, and then destroyed them both. She had to prove that things had changed, and had changed in the favour of Lacuna and Jade. “So you’re coming then?” Jade was still bouncing on the end of the ultra-springy mattress “No” replied Lacuna simply as she kicked Jade in the arse, sending her flying off the bed and onto the floor “Now go away before I call in security to get rid of you” And, rather upset at being over ruled with her head dragging low, Jade left the room and slammed the door heavily, making sure she stamped her feet all the way down the corridor. Lacuna smiled. ”Child.”
