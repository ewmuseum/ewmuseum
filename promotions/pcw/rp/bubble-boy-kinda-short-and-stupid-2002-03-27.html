Bubble Boy (kinda short and stupid)

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/02 (The scene fades in from darkness to show Rudolph's laboratory in the shoe factory. Inside, on the floor in the center of the room, are many pieces of thick inflatable plastic. Rudolph is currently busy using his glue gun, sticking the pieces together perfectly. He talks to himself while he works.) Rudolph: This should keep him from dirtying my house before that Easter party... (Rudolph continues to glue the many pieces together, before he gets a large wall of plastic. He tips it over onto the floor and grabs another large piece of plastic, this one being some sort of plastic door. Rudolph goes to glue the piece to the rest of the plastic, but gets glue on himself.) Rudolph: Dammit! That's hot! (Rudolph wipes his hand on his shirt and continues to glue. He finally glues the door in place. He stops working for a second.) Rudolph: Pretty soon this thing won't be able to fit through the door. Better go out into the main room... (Rudolph grabs the plastic pieces and drags them through the door of his laboratory and into the middle of the main room. He walks back into the room and brings the glue gun out, its long electrical cord leading back through the laboratory door. The door to the right side of the room quickly opens and Mason Creeper comes strolling out from the stairway. He walks over next to Rudolph and sits down. Rudolph quickly slithers away.) Mason: What are you making now? Rudolph: I'm making you a bubble, so you won't get my house dirty at my upcoming Easter party. (Mason looks confused.) Mason: Easter party? Cool! (Rudolph sighs, and continues work on his bubble. After working in silence for another two minutes or so, the bubble is complete. A huge, round, clear plastic object sits in the middle of Rudolph's floor. Rudolph opens the door.) Rudolph: Well, there ya go! (Mason frowns angrily.) Mason: I'm not getting in that thing! Hell no! (Rudolph frowns as well.) Rudolph: I guess you'll have to leave for the Easter party then. No alcohol for you... No nice women for you... No... Mason: I get the point! Okay, I'll get in the bubble, but only at the party! Wait, this looks like fun! (Mason climbs into the bubble and Rudolph closes the door. Mason sticks his hands on the top of the sphere and starts to roll around the room, laughing the whole time.) Rudolph: Easier to entertain that an illiterate three year old... (Rudolph walks to the right side of the room and goes up the stairs, slamming the door behind him before he walks up. Mason rolls into the doorway of Rudolph's laboratory.) Mason: It won't fit... (Mason tries harder and harder to squeeze through, and finally he goes, barely keeping the bubble in one piece. He tries to stop, but keeps on rolling. He rolls into the table and dumps the sleeping potion on the floor. He bounces against the right wall. He knocks a bunch of animals and potions on the floor. He finally stops the bubble in the middle of the room.) Mason: Oh crap... Maybe I should wear this in our match on Friday! Nobody will be able to hurt me, and I can roll them over! Cool! (The scene quickly jumps out to the main room, where Rudolph is seen entering from the right side of the room. He closes the door behind him and walks into the middle of the room.) Rudolph: Where is that moron? (Rudolph glances over toward his laboratory to see three gerbils running out.) Rudolph: NO!!!! (Rudolph runs into the laboratory and sees Mason in his ball, rolling all over the room. He reaches into the side pocket on his laboratory coat and pulls out a safety pin. He sticks the pin into the bubble. The bubble slowly deflates, and Rudolph opens the door.) Rudolph: How the hell did you get into my laboratory? Mason: It was a tight squeeze, but I just brought the bubble through! (Rudolph sighs.) Rudolph: I guess i'll just have to wrap you in plastic wrap for the party... (The scene slowly fades away, and Rudolph mouths inaudible words at Mason, with a frown on his face the whole time. Complete darkness takes over the screen.)
