The Anarchy Saga: Endnote.

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-03 Hooligans. Anarchy doesn't just begin in the poorly lit basements of 'revolutionaries' with nothing but their dim computer screen whirring and downloading and uploading and posting. See, you would think that this is how it starts. One person becomes a virus, he convinces two people, who convince four people, until a small hoarde of angry townspeople are advancing on their local Nike Distribution center. Now that is beauty. Flame, shoes, capitalism, greed, all being destroyed under one flag, a dark flag. Anarchy is what it is, a beautiful expression of human nature. It isn't just the weird kids, or the guys who hated authority, it isn't the sociopaths (although in my DarkStar's case, it helps.), hell... it isn't even the ambitious politicians. It is an ingrained thought process that buries itself in even the most tame of people. It is built inside your blood to rebel and to cause destruction, to cause pain and bloodshed, to try and destroy as much as you possibly can. Human beings are a race of creatures that enjoy devestation. It is what they do. Name anyone else that slowly kills themselves by overpopulating and harming their very own ecosystem. Name another kind of animal that punishes its own kind for simply doing what nature intended them, thinning out the population. You can't because as sickening as it is, humans are original. Humans have their own bullshit iconoclast system of beliefs that fluxtuate depending on what color the next persons skin is, or what the situation was. Instead of blanket right, they do sporadic wrong. Sadly, the only thing DarkStar could do was to fling down his citizenship, although he would have destroyed his human ties in a heartbeat if it were possible (Although it was, he was a god, albeit a non-practicing one.) It is obvious everywhere you look. The news. Television shows. The paper. Movies. Video games. Humans have more of a bloodlust then Vampir Nosferatu in a unknown writers convention. Take Soccer for example. Soccer is a sport where two teams of people take to a grassy field on a sunny day and they kick a sphere around until one team puts it in the net, whoever does it the most after a certain amount of time wins the game and gets the pride. It is simple, it is clean, it is a game. Do humans take it that way? No. Humans take it as war. Humans take it as passion and truth. Leeds and Galatasaray, two teams, on a field, kicking the ball. The crowd screams. The players clash. The referee makes a call. The fans get angry and curse the other fans. A punch is thrown. Chairs are thrown with it. Rocks, bottles, wood. The players, who although do not like eachother have no real bad blood, are escorted off the field. The fans have the field now and they attack, they bleed, the die. Under what banner? Sport? Pride? What? Stupidity. Humans are idiots. DarkStar can be seen in the shadows of the arena, watching, waiting, enjoying the carnage that can so easily be caused by something as simple as, oh, say, making sure a referee makes the wrong call. Something that easy can incite thousands of human beings to hurt eachother. Something as simple as Greed. Does it sound familiar? It should. If you are American. Turn on the TV and take a good look at your presidents war in Iraq. The parellels indeed should be correct. Thank you. This is my introductions endnote.
