The Searching - Part I - Rude Awakenings

16 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-16-02 Song playing guide: 'With You' by Linkin Park. (If you have it, listen to the lyrics or read them. It fits this promo perfectly..) [b]BOBBY STORMBRINGER[/b] [b][u]The Searching - Part I - Rude Awakenings[/u][/b] [i]Then.... (1-18-02, during PCW Friday Fusion from Kingston, Jamaica. From 'Driven, Part IV, Defused Storm')[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: See ya out there, Air.[/b] [i]B.S. looks at Tyler confidently while he points up at the monitor, indicating the ring. Aireborn nods, equally smug about his own natural abilities.[/i] [b]AIREBORN: Sooner than you think, bud. Just don't get upset when you come up short.[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: Heh! Yeah, whatevah.[/b] [i]B.S. gives a slight wave to everyone and jogs towards the parking lot and his rented vehicle so he could get out of there before the traffic reaches a new stage of insanity. He bypasses the guards from the beginning of the evening and the still rather large amount of fans hanging about outside the arena. He slows down about fifteen yards away from the Camero and fiddles around in one of his inside trench pockets, searching for the keys. He pulls them out as he reaches the trunk of the car and promptly drops them on the ground.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: Sh!t.[/b] [i]B.S. drops to a knee and picks up the keys, his hands beginning to shake again as a result of the knowledge that he's about to drive again, and even worse, this time it will be at night. B.S. shuts his eyes and tries to slow his mind's thoughts a little more. After a moment of deep breathing so he can continue on his way, he opens his eyes to see underneath the car, a pair of black boots on the other side of the vehicle. The Showstopper rises to his feet, his face contorted in befuddlement at who this can be. Instead of the identity of the person and whose feet that belongs to, he recieves a black rod of some sort right on top of his head. Hard. The last thing B.S. sees is a trickle of blood running down past his eyes. Then another. And another. Whether Bobby Maxwell Stormbringer likes it or not, this scene cuts to black. Black as the moonless night above. And now....[/i] [b][i]* * *[/i][/b] [i]In the last PCW promo seen for Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer, he decided to take a very short vacation for Valentine's Day. Thursday, he brought his soon-to-be girlie friend Kira York to Salt Lake City, Utah for a bit of the Olympics while on his way to Los Angeles for Fusion on Friday night. The Showstopper is in for several rude awakenings. The first rude awakening wasn't all that bad, just a minor... no, well, [b]major[/b] annoyance and reunion at the end of the Olympic night, Thursday night. We'll get back to what exactly that was very soon, but for now it is the next day, and some of those events are shown to you. Late Friday afternoon, Los Angeles. Time for Fusion. Time for Red Rock. Time for the PCW Rising Star title belt battle. Time for Hank~! and B.S.'s possible entry into the March Madness main event. Time for the GTT and a virtual living legend in Richie Syxx, which is only the biggest match B.S. has ever had. Yes, that even includes Trent Storm in Thrillfest.. ..and time for one more of those phone calls.[/i] [b][i]* * *[/i][/b] [i]--2-15-02, 5:29 pm PST, [b]before PCW Friday Fusion[/b]--[/i] [i]PCW's Bobby Stormbringer sits in yet another hotel room, this one in L.A., naturally. They all look the very same. Two beds covered in sheets and quilts. A very long dresser with a mirror above it. A bathroom. A sink and even larger mirror. White, folded towels everywhere. A small television with its puny remote. The brown trash bin with the plastic bag inside. The gigantic air conditioning unit. The curtains and blinds that literally block out the sun. B.S. sits on one of the beds, facing the other. Mr. Prime Time (as in the two-time MWF Prime Time Champ) has the reciever of the only phone in the room pressed against his ear. This is because B.S. is speaking to someone on the other line. Duh.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: Yes, I realize I called a few days ago. I didn't get the info I was looking for.... mm-hmm. Well, that's because your 'Chief' wasn't in..... well, is he there now? Well gee, [i]good[/i], let me speak to him. Now. Please.[/b] [i]B.S. fishes a couple blue and yellow asprins off the endtable in between the beds and sticks them in his mouth while getting his response from the Jamican police, before downing it with a Dr. Pepper. This isn't a promo for PCW, so he can lay off the [b]Snapple~![/b] for a few moments, you see. But are these pills a sign that B.S. is suffering a relapse with his 'international flu virus'..?[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: You think I give a flyin' f*ck if he's 'busy' or not? Here's your answer. Uh, [i]no[/i]. I've been waiting one month, almost to the day for some four-one-one on who set me up and attacked me in your lil' country. My personal assistant told me you have new information! So I want to be told that new information. I'm from freggin' PCW, and we generated a ton of new money into that p!ss poor country of yours, so I want my just due! I have been far too patient, and I will be patient no longer. I'm a very, very busy man, and I will not have this.. this 'mystery' hanging over my head. I want to find out who ambushed me like a coward so I may give [i]him[/i] his just due, already. Or her, but it's probably a 'him'.[/b] [i]For some reason, the police officer or secretary on the other end of the line doesn't hang up on him after his rant. After a lengthy pause of listening, B.S. wrenches his neck for a quick pop, and responds yet again:[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: Good. Thank you. Nice to see that you might actually do your [i]job[/i] every once in a while.[/b] [i]B.S. pauses and turns his head in the other direction from the phone to let loose with a hacking cough. Uh-oh... B.S. turns back and puts the reciever back to his ear.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: So go get him, then...... no, don't put me on hold, ya jackass, just put the phone down. Onto the desk. Rise up from your chair. Put the donuts down. Walk to yer chief's office. Tell him to take line six or whatevah it is and start listening to The Showstopper! There. Is that simple enough for you?[/b] [i]B.S. was restraining himself all he could not to call him a peasant, he really was. Stormbringer sighs as he has apparently been put on hold anyway. B.S. sniffles slightly as he turns the TV on with the remote, switching it to the PTC Promo Channel. Being shown is an hour-long block of PCW promos, but the ones for the GTT First Round could pop up at any time now. All that was on at the moment was The Chef. The Chef... Here was a guy who had garnered a limitless amount of respect from Bobby Stormbringer for how he 'left' the business. In an incredible upset, he became PCW World Champion over Trashcan Man and Ivan Stanislav at a Pay-Per-View. The very next show? He 'retires' while on top of the wrestling world and drops the belt. How awesome is that?? If only more people treated the 'Game' with more reverence like that. Let's face it. Most don't. Apparently, The Chef doesn't either. Now he's come back wielding the Koran, which is fine. Now he's come back to PCW praising the ways of Islam and Allah. Which is fine. Did I mention that he's supporting the 'Nine-Eleven' Al-Kaeda terrorists? [b]Nothing[/b] about that is fine. So B.S. has gone from looking up to Chef before he joined Primetime Central, to absolutely being disgusted by him these days. His cooking really is quite spectacular... but that doesn't even come [b]close[/b] to excusing his pathetic speeches and mantras. Finally, B.S. is taken off hold.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: ...Hmm? Yes, hello..... yeah, I was told that you had some new details about the incident that happened after PCW arrived in Kingston...... mm-hmm. Yep..... Er, yes, the Canadians were definitely cheated. Yes..... of course it's the right thing to do for both pairs to be given the Gold...... No, no, no. It wouldn't be right to take them away from the Russians...... Well, if they are ever found to be guilty of knowing about the judge nonsense beforehand they should be stripped of them and banned..... Yeah- .....What I'm saying is that no evidence supporting that has come out. Yet. Look can we stop talking about-[/b] [i]B.S. sighs and groans deeply as he's cut off yet again.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: Yes...... mm-hmm. I'm sure that the Jamaican bobsled team will do very well for themselves again in these Olympics. (...whatevah, nothin' but jackasses...) What? No, I didn't say anything, you must be hearing things. Feedback in the hearing aid, since you must have it turned up too loud. No really. I mean it.[/b] [i]B.S. rolls his eyes, sneezes, and pauses for more meaningless dribble.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: Look, about the 'incident'...... what? What do you mean you want me to come back there? ......Well, can't you just tell me over the phone? ....Why not, d*mnit? ......I'll keep my temper as long as I want to. My [i]temper[/i] right now is directly affected to how much you're jerkin' me around! Fine.. okay.. that's alright. I'm not going to yell at you forever, it's pointless. Not like I can change your intelligence level all by myself. That and I have to go to 'work' in an hour...... H*ll, I dunno when. I guess.. I suppose maybe Monday, next week. If not then, then Wednesday. I've got this big match with Hank Gunyon to think about, not to mention Richie Syxx...... [i]wrestling[/i] matches, like the ones you just had a month ago? Don't you...... ah, so you [i]do[/i] remember. Small miracles, I swear.............. look, shaddup for a second. I'll get there when I get there, sometime next week.[/b] [i]The Showstopper rolls his eyes another time, clicks his tongue, and hangs up the phone with a ringing clank, not bothering to continue the aggravating conversation any longer. Just then, the fair, lovely, and adorable Kira walks in with her arms loaded with strawberry and lemon cough drops, Robutussin, Vix Vapo-Rub, Thera-Flu, and various other accessories from the Gift Shop downstairs. She runs in and drops the 'goodies' all over the bed next to B.S., and her terrific red hair goes flying every which way from the effort. When Bobby can see her face, he smirks at the cute, not-so-innocent face before him. Kira lets loose a couple giggles before speaking up:[/i] [b]KIRA: Is that enough to do the job, hon?[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: Heh, let's hope so. I'm just too busy to get sick again.. or for the original sickness to rise up again.. or whatevah the heck is going on with me. Red Rock, Hank, Syxx. I just really need to be in peak condition right now...[/b] [b]KIRA: I know, babe. But you're really good, I think you can handle these guys even while you're not all the way healthy.[/b] [i]B.S. coughs again and flashes his 'good friend' a quick, weak smile.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: Thanks for the vote of confidence, darlin'. I just gotta work through all this strangeness.. besides all of that, I have to be fit again before early March.[/b] [b]KIRA: Uh, why? Is there an important match or somethin'?[/b] [i]B.S. manages a smug smirk somehow and shakes his head vigorously.[/i] [b]STORMBRINGER: I'm setting up a pickup game. B-ball. Three on three, back in Orlando. Just something to kill some time.. I might charge everyone ten bucks and send it off to charity, 'cause I'm cool like dat. It looks like my team'll face Juan Borro.. if he can get outta prison.. some NTICW guy, Pila, or something. I can't remember, I'd have to look. Plus that Badge. What a 'pig' he is...[/b] [b]KIRA: So who's on your team, do you know yet?[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: All Orlando. Boda..[/b] [b]KIRA: Oh, well that's a smart recruitment. Say, doesn't he own part of the Magi-[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: ..and Kid Wonder.[/b] [b]KIRA: Huh!?[/b] [b]STORMBRINGER: I need to be healthy just so I can have the stamina to put up with Amanda and K-Dub's persistent nonsense. And baby girl... that's no b.s.![/b] [i]The scene slowly fades to black as Kira stands there next to the bed with her hands on her hips, looking down in shock at The Showstopper. Meanwhile, B.S. cackles his head off, knowing all too well how utterly ludicrous the notion of a Boda, B.S., K-Dub team really is. The last thing we can make out is Richie Syxx's first GTT promo debuting over on the PTC Channel.... Why in the world would B.S. subject himself to the horrors of that egoholic Kid Wonder on his team? Is it just B.S. trying to take every opportunity to get under K-Dub's skin, or is is it some sort of strange 'O-Town unity and solidarity'? Is B.S. too under the weather, too distracted for someone with the talents of the Rising Star Champion, Red Rock? How will B.S. approach this upcoming, all-important qualifying contest with someone as (in)famous as HANK~!..? Has B.S. decided to follow through and be committed and deadly serious about his most important match [b]ever[/b] with the OSW's Syxx, and the Global Title Tournament as a whole? Just who ambushed B.S. one month ago, seemingly without any provocation?? And finally, when will Bobby and Kira just smooch and get it over with!?! We'll see. Soon enough. Now go home and live an actual life, ya losers![/i] [b]TO BE CONTINUED. THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION. © 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.[/b]
