Skylar, no one cares about the history of the joke you call your career

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 March &#039;03 The scene opens with Chet King sitting on the same chair he was last time, staring down the barrel of the camera, but this time the backdrop behind him is thousands of bright fluorescent colors flashing. Staring at these colors is enough to make anyone trip out. Chet King: You know it was suggested that I do a groovy promo involving an adventure with flashing colors and hippies. I decided against it however, and I’ve decided to continue to do a bit of trash talking. The flashing colors disappear and are replaced by a grey wall. Chet King: The question was who should I talk about, but that question was easily answered when I turned on my TV today, and saw for the billionth time this past week the history of the biggest joke of a career there ever has been. Ofcourse I’m talking about Skylar Green. Skylar, why have you forced me to invest hours upon hours of my time into listening to the history of your career? For the last week I’ve been sitting down, turning the TV on and expecting to see something interesting, and all I get is a history of your career. I don’t care about how you got where you are today, no one does. Maybe if you were someone worth while we’d care, but your not. Heck, maybe if you’d done just one interesting thing in your whole career then we’d care, but you haven’t. Sure you can tell Sandy Wong, the FBI, or whoever it is you want to, if for some absolute unknown reason someone wants to hear about the joke that is your career, but do us all a favor and don’t put it on TV. No one cares. To tell you the truth, watching the history of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor’s relationship would be more interesting…actually, that was a bit harsh, sorry Skylar, your career’s not that annoying. But in saying that, it’s still a joke and something that shouldn’t be being told on TV. Who cares about your career in PURE, EW, UWWC, HWF or any other fed that you were in? Infact, who cares about your career in PCW? The answer is no one. Not me, not Violence Jack, not The Pimply Faced Intern Kid, no one. I do seem to remember giving you a verbal lashing only about three months ago, so I don’t want to repeat myself. Although since you’ve been able to put a history of your career onto TV, I guess it’ll be fine if I just give a quick history of my previous verbal abuse of you. Basically I told you what I’m telling you now. Your career’s a joke. Your so called wrestling highlights and achievements are petty. You came 3rd in Alliances and the Mall Brawl, you’re the tallest cruiserweight in PCW history, you’re a two time cruiserweight champion and a one time extreme champion. *Sarcastically* Wow! How exciting! You’re the greatest wrestler of all time Skylar! I love you! But I do have to give you your props where they’re deserved, being the first and only blind wrestler in PCW is impressive. And I’ll be the first to admit that if I was told a couple years ago that a blind person could wrestle for a company like PCW, I’d have laughed. Though you’ve proven it can be done, and for that I give you props. However, I think being blind is what has given you the misconception that people actually care about you or your career. You must have little dream sequences running through your head when you go to places, imagining what’s happening around you. You must imagine that when you’re at an autograph signing that there’s a line of thousands waiting to get your autograph. You must imagine that people are lining up from as far as 50 miles away. You imagine that people have been camping out for weeks, possibly even months, just to get your autograph. You’re wrong though Skylar, there’s usually only three people lining up for your autograph. The first is some clueless individual who’s never watched wrestling in their life, and doesn’t know who you are. They hope that maybe you’re someone and they go get your autograph in the hopes that they can sell it later, only to be disappointed when no one wants to buy it off them because they don’t care about you. The second is someone who knows who you are, and feels sorry for the joke known as your career. They go get your autograph out of pity for you. The third is a con artist, who realizes that you’re not actually the smartest person in the world and gets you to sign an autograph. What you don’t realize is that you’re not signing just any old sheet of paper, you’re signing a cheque, or another form involving the movement of funds. Then the next thing you know you’ve just lost thousands of dollars and the FBI is on your ass for tax evasion. Come next week though, there’ll probably be another two more people wanting your autograph. They’ll fall under the feeling sorry for you category after watching your poor performance at March Madness and having listened to the history of your joke of a career. Getting back to what you imagine is going on when you’re walking around places. You imagine that people are staring awestruck at you, admiring your every move. The truth is though that people just give you a weak smile out of pity, and just shake their heads thinking about your sad career. I think March Madness is going to perfectly sum up and conclude the history of your career that you’ve forced me to listen to. You’re going to get punked out by a bitch named Violence Jack. Then you’ll crawl down to the ring, enter the battle royal, and be kicked out straight away in a very uneventful manner with no one really caring. Now if having your ass kicked with no one caring doesn’t sum your career up, then I don’t know what does. Should you chose to issue a response to this Skylar I’ll be pleased. Do me a favor though, don’t make your reply private, air on TV instead of another history of your joke of a career. This will save people having to sit through, and feeling sorry, for the history of your career for at least one more night. Or have you finished with your history now? That’s good if you have, you can now go and retire safely knowing that you let people know about the joke that you call a career.
