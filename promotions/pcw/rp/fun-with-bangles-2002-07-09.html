Fun With Bangles.

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/07/02 When Xavier Kannon hates someone.. He REALLY hates them... Not just “Sick at the sight of them“ hate.. This is “Hate their families and anything to do with them“ hate.... Kannon not liking you is NOT a good thing as many will testify.. But having the temperamental Scientologist loathing you with every ounce of his being is quite a dangerous thing as the Pharaoh is finding out. Already he and his Wife have been attacked backstage and then Kannon went even further and broke a Chair over his head.... This was all before Xavier was then viciously attacked by the Dark Age after he defected from Tyler Nelson... Which seems to have pushed Xavier even further over the edge. With a small measure of revenge gained over the Goths will the passing of Gangrel, Xavier is free to focus on the Pharaoh... His oldest enemy.... And a man he almost believes benefits from the abuse Kannon puts him through... .. But where will he stop? “...They go Way-O Way-O.... Way-O Way-O....” Our shot opens up on the Sideshack recording studios in Los Angeles, California.... Although not Hubbardville, a place where Scientology has a strong foodhold from it’s roots in the 50’s.... And all around us the warble typical of a 80’s girl band is heard.... Not the 4 Non-Blondes... The other one... “...Walk Like An Egyptian....” ...That’s right, we are looking in on the Bangles as they prepare for their comeback tour by re-recording one of their most famous songs... Honestly.... Anyway, the 3 or 4 of them.. Let’s go with 4... Are all in their booth with their mics as they squeal out the song with their long past their prime vocal chords. “Way-O Way-O..... Way-O Way-O!” ..Due to the long amount of time passed since they first released the song they have forgotten most of the words and thus have to rely on the “WAY-O’s” to carry the song for them... Yup, they do. “That was great girls...” In front of the mixing desk, the sound technician gives them the standard post-recording encouragement as he wonders what kind of mastery he will have to pull to make their husky 40 Marlboro a day voices sound vaguely female.... Or more importantly, like female singers. He then coughs to himself as he takes his finger off the intercom button. Technician : “... For a bunch of penniless crack whores who blew their cash on Rocks and need to make a comeback just to score....” The 4 gi-.. Wait, women.. Stand the other side of the sound proof glass and smile to one another, nodding along in their agreement that their voices DON’T sounds like nails down a blackboard accompanying “Don’t Let Me Get Me” by Pink playing in hell.... In other words something that sounds about as bad as you can get. Random Bangle : “Wow.... We should do some more songs.... How about “Fucking in Heaven” by Fatboy Slim.. That’s what the “Trendy” kids like.... They’re stupid and will spend lots of money on CD’s.. After all they buy Pink and Missy!” The technician, if he had either a soul of a conscience would step in and say it is a bad idea.... But as you will find out soon, he hasn’t so instead he just sits back in his chair and lights a Camel Light... Hoping he doesn’t laugh so hard a lung falls out of his mouth. Random Bangle : “Yeah, we should really do this girls... We’ll just have to make sure all of us get to sing....” Behind the glass the conceited and rather apathetic sound technician chuckles to himself further as he blows smoke rings. Technician : “Good idea you skanky bitches.. We have 4 girls lined up to sing over you so we might as well have to use them all....” As he swings his feet up onto the mixing desk and turns the volume up with his toe, the Bangles begin to belt out there cover of Norman Cook’s “You’ve Come A Long Way.. Baby” track... “Bangle One is Fucking in Heaven...” “Bangle Two is Fucking in Heaven... ” “Bangle Three is Fucking in Heaven... ” “Bangle Four is Fucking in Fucking in Fucking in Heaven.... ” By this time the technician can no longer be seen as he has fallen backwards off his chair and is holding his ribs.. Not because of the fall but because his spleen can almost be seen bursting out of his body as his sides split. Random Bangle but NOT Bangle 4 : “Hey... She got to Fuck more!” This complaint ends their song... To the benefit of all those who’s ears work properly, and those who are death who could POSIBLY regain their hearing at the wrong time and jab a knife into their ear to end the millisecond of pain. Bangle 4 : “Well I went through my stash quicker than you and my Dealer said that’s how to pay him off....” Her 3 Bandmates then take, as Ally McBeal calls it “A Moment”... That’s Ally the show, as it’s actually John Cage that says it.. But anyway, a moment can be summed up easily as looking at her maybe with a raised eyebrow. Bangle 4 : “Oh.. You meant the SONG, that’s just how it goes.” The other 3 give her a suspicious look that says “Guess Who’ll Be Fired First...”. A voice is then heard in their booth as the technician has pulled himself up and decides to suggests a song for them to murder... “How about “Look At Me” by Gerri Halliwell?” Bangles : “SURE!!! ” He dusts himself off and leans back as the screeching capable of cutting through the air and splitting the atom starts.. Or “Singing” as they call it. Bangle One : “LOOOOOOOOOOOK AT ME!!!! I’M A DR-” “DRAG QUEEN!” Bangle One : “..-F THAT’S YOUR FANTASY...” Yup, that’s right... He suggested the song not because he likes Gerri, or hates her, or just thinks she is a pretentious anorexic bitch.. It’s because he wants to sing over their words to insult them without them hearing.. Clever guy, maybe clever enough to be a SCIENTOLOGIST *Wink* *Wink* *Nudge* *Nudge* Bangle 2 : “LOOOOOOK AT ME!!!! I’LL DO ANYTHING IF YOU LE-” “GIVE ME A FEW ROCKS BECAUSE I’M A WASTED BLOATED DRUGGED UP WHORE!!!” Bangle 2 : “...-T ME BE!!!!” Oh the wonder of ignorance... Best shown in middle aged groups making a revival. Bangle 3 : “LOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOK AT ME!!!!!!!! I’LL BE A-” “A WASHED UP COLLAPSED VEINED POT BELLIED SKANK....” Bangle 3 : “....-Something rhyming with ME...” Bangle 4 : “LOOOOOOOOOOK AT ME!!!!!! I’LL D-” This time however it isn’t the cocky, self assured voice of the technician that speaks, but rather the whiny, Preppy voice of none other than Eleanor Kannon-Hall, the Wife of wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon... Eleanor : “Like... WHY would people want to look at HER?!?!? I mean her body? Ewwww... Her hair? Ewwww... Her clothes? EWWWWWWZIES and that VOICE? If there is one, like, SO annoying thing about someone it’s their voice...” Behind her, not wanting to make a comment and get a stroppy Prep of a Wife on his case all afternoon walks Xavier Kannon... Arriving to meet the sound technician WHO WAS A SCIENTOLOGIST AFTER ALL!!! *Evil Laughter* Xavier : “Please save my little Shag Squirrel’s ears and fade those 4 wailing Mooses down Rick...” The techie who seems to be friendly with the Gold Patron Meritorious cuts the sound off and we are left looking through the sound proof glass at 4 over weight, badly dressed and groomed near middle aged women... Kind of resembling a Seal enclosure at a zoo... Only Seals probably grunt in key. Eleanor : “Can I throw them a Fish?” Kannon drops back into one of the seats and looks at the 4... Things... Still belting out the Hubbard awful song from Starvation Spice herself. Xavier : “So those are the ones who sung “Walk Like An Egyptian”... Painting an image of Egyptians as fun loving people did a funny dance that involved pushing one hand out here, and then doing it lower behind you...” Technician : “That’s the one...” The Scientologist... As in the wrestler one sighs to himself. Xavier : “They must pay for painting a false impression of the embodiment of Un-Scientological EVIL that are Egyptians! They don’t just walk like Egyptians all day.. THEY OPPRESS SCIENTOLOGISTS... They LOCK PEOPLE IN TOMBS... THEY’RE EVIL!!!” Behind him his Preppy bride sneers as she thinks of the Pharaoh and his Wife. Eleanor : “They...... Are....... Evil...” Although it’s not quite sure whether she WAS actually thinking about Titu and Isiy, or the 4 fat women with wide open mouths looking like the finalists of a Pavaraotti in Drag competition behind the glass. Xavier : “Think we can get them to do a new version called “Walk Like A Scientologist”... You know, lock them in there with no food, alcohol or drugs until they give in?” The technician doesn’t reply in words but rather fades up the sound again for a brief second.... “.....-OK AT M-....” ... Before fading it down again and turning to see the 1st couple of PCW hunched over with their ears covered so tightly their finger nails have turned White. Xavier : “Okay.... Just gas them then...” Technician : “You sure... A lot of people seem to be dying in your promos nowadays...” The Scientologist just walks up to the glass and puts his hands on it, smirking to himself then waving to the “Singers”... Xavier : “I have a lot of repressed anger.” The sound techie just shrugs then presses the big Red button on his mixing board... All studios have them, honestly... And the 8 jets in every corner of the recording booth starts to spray a fine mist into the room. Eleanor : “Um.... They seem to be... Like... Enjoying it!” The scene before them is now something of grotesque beauty... The 4 large women are now staggering out like Goldfish, opening their mouths wide with every step to gulp in some of the lovely toxic gas... Eleanor : “I’m....... Speech...... Less...” It could almost be set to some beautiful string laden music as the bloated women glide along to incorporate more of the gas into their system before the walking mounts of food and drug excess drop down dead on the floor. Xavier : “It always takes a little bit away from a truly evil act when they actually take some enjoyment out of it...” The Gold Patron Meritorious actually seems quite upset about this fact as the shot starts to fade to Black on him and his Wife getting up ready to leave. Eleanor : “Yeah... Aren’t they, like, meant to bang on the glass and beg or something? I didn’t see a single tear!!!” As the dejected couple walk out of the door the techie dons his gas mask and goes to heave the bodies out.... And we hit Black. “Well THAT’S ruined my Scientological day!”
