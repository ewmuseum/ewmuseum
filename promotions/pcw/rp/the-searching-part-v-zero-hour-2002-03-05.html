The Searching - Part V - Zero Hour

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-05-02 NOTE: Please read the *legal disclaimer* at the bottom of the promo when you finish. It'll explain the strange point of view. =) .Song listening guide: 'The Way I Am' by Eminem, from the Marshall Mathers LP BOBBY STORMBRINGER 'The Searching' - Part V - Zero Hour Then.... (2-27-02) RANDY'S VOICE: Oh yes, there were two others involved. Perhaps I shall tell you the names? Perhaps not. So I suggest you follow my instructions. Since you are family, brother, I will make sure I will not indispose you. No excuses for you getting maimed by Maxwell next week, or at your precious little Pay-Per-View before that. A few snickers from Randy this time, as B.S. just shakes his head in barely controlled fury. RANDY'S VOICE: Ah yes. So you will first do this. March ninth. Be here at our home. Orlando. Take yourself a quick vacation, perhaps to Disney World. It is funny, we have been here for years, you and I. Have we ever been there without being out-of-state tourists?? B.S.'s eyes grow wild again, now out of confusion. The Showstopper knows what is coming next. RANDY'S VOICE: Are you still following me, understanding me, brother!? You will show up at the new NTICW's biggest show of all. Magic Kingdom Massacre. That was your favorite theme park anyway, when we were kids. You hated all that walking in Epcot. Damn World Showcase. STORMBRINGER: ...Why? RANDY'S VOICE: You just be there. Not just there, but in the middle of the ring before the telecast ends. I will give you further instructions. If you are a very good boy I'll even slip you a name. Or other important information. It's not during a PCW show. It's right at home. Very convienient, and I expect you then. If you don't find this tape in time for the Pay-Per-View. You have failed. If you show? You succeed. You know all that is needed for now. Goodbye... brother. There is a soft click, and the message is over just like that. A message of a meeting of some sort. B.S. makes sure to take the tape out and then he stands. He stares bitterly at the tape recorder and then the camera shot takes a ground level view of it. The recorder is crushed under The Showstopper's heel. And now....
