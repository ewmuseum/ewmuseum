Enter: The Crazy Suburban Ninja Clan

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tribute to BAS and Tmm Long ago, there was a time where darkness was a way of existence for some. Ancient shadows mark the beginning of a silent breed, conceived and birthed in those dark ages. They were loyal not to themselves, but to their numbers. Swiftly, they would go off into the night, cascading as if the breeze though the darkness. Haste was made. Each to his own would serve well, tapping into dishonor to maintain his own honor. They had no choice, yet willed it so, that they might become the perfect apparition of a shadow. Successfully, they made due… Never detected… not until it was too late. To see one of these shifting apparitions was to see your maker. Failure was never an option, unacceptable to the dark race… They would not live with themselves, having been shamed by failure. However, they solved the problem in their traditional manner. They were true to their clan, deceitful to their enemies. They were strong, graceful, and deadly. They were the enigmatic race of the Medieval Japanese era. They were Ninja. Ninja… The original Pokemon of Japan. In their day, they were huge. Every household had a horde of Ninja’s… They went like hotcakes 24/7. Hell, in that day… 99% of the population was Ninja. (Note: The suicide rate was through the roof.) But, today with all the modern technology, and what not, in this ever evolving world, there is no need, seemingly for Ninjas… (Except in movies.) But, how could they be replaced?! WHERE HAVE ALL THESE STEALTH ASSASSINS VANISHED TO?!?! Thursday, Sept 19th, 2002Glenview, IL135 Washington St.8:00 PM Nightfall has arrived for the small, well-established “Tree City,” USA. It’s quite calm out, notably warm for a fall evening. Inside this cozy little home, there’s an electric hum. An old woman residing here sits in the glow of her microwave, watching a bag of popcorn pop. There is a small creak above… The old gal looks up for a moment, at the slightly swayed hanging light fixture above. Unnerved, perhaps, she decides to ignore it. As the kernels cease to pop, she rises to stop the microwave, popping the door open… A rustle of leaves in her front yard catches her attention now… She scurries to her living room window, pulling a curtain aside… Nothing. Wait… A pair of blurs amongst the branches of the pine tree in her front yard scale into the depth of the greenery… Turning slowly, the old woman heads back for her kitchen. Little does she seem to know, is that the small circular, slotted vent for her attic has been disengaged from its place, allowing access for two NINJAS leaping from the tree, and diving head first through the small threshold, landing with in the attic as lightly as a feather. A pack of Ninja gathered at the rear end of the house quietly tiptoe in a straight line towards the suburban home. The first in the line whips out a grappling hook, which is quickly launched, to the roof. A quick tug locks it into place and in a heartbeat they all scale the home, climbing the slanted roof, and heading for the chimney… Flood windows become doorways for a flood of Ninja… Meanwhile, the old lady dumps the fluffy popcorn into a big bowl, but suddenly she hesitates again. This time, there was a definitely a sound INSIDE her home… in fact, right inside her living room… She lets the popcorn, bag and all, fall into the bowl as she creeps into he living room, the area seemingly empty minus herself. Old Lady: “You’re all late. Such a noisy pack of fools…” “FOOLS?!” “SHH!!! We are supposed to be stealth…” Old Lady: “Morons… I can hear you.” “No, you idiot, wait! Ahhh!” “GAH!” “I’m slipping!” There is a loud succession of thuds as the Ninja in the chimney tumble out the mantle, to the living room floor, black ash fogging up the room. As they roll out, they bring a big mess with them. The is a groan of disappointment all over the house… Old Lady: “DAMMIT!! I JUST STEAM VACCED THE ROOM TODAY!” The old woman’s eyes glow red. A pair of Ninja’s drop down from the ceiling, some worm in through a ventilation shaft, a few more from a closet… The Ninja from the chimney rise, brushing themselves off. Ninja #21: “Oh yeah, so BECAUSE IT’S SO hard sitting around playing old house wife all day…” Ninja #16: “Do I smell popcorn?!” Old Lady: “HOUSE WIFE?!” Suddenly, the old lady rips off her shawl, sweater, and blouse to reveal he muscular, bald headed, male body, shrouded decoratively in black. Big Ninja guy: “Do you idiots have any idea how hard it is to keep this place tidy, so no one suspects us?! I slave night and day keeping this home in tact for my Clan to come home to… All I ask is…” “AHHHHHH!!!!” Suddenly, the large front window is shattered to shards as five shadow knights come crashing through, having swung from the roof with grappling hooks. The Ninja already inside shield their eyes from the rain of glass, as the ambushing attackers release the grappling hooks, hitting the carpet floor rolling… More hurl themselves in follow, and others smash their way in from the back. With a mighty roar, the attackers unsheathe their confidence of weaponry… only to be silenced by the sight of already arrived Ninja. Ninja #3: “Uhhh… My clock said we were 5 minutes early… Sorry, Sensei…” The large Sensei’s eyes beam red, his nostrils venting steam… Sensei: “Shuttup, and make yourselves ‘At home’. I made popcorn…” --- Basement8:30 PM “Alright, alright, mouths shut, ears open! The weekly SNC meeting has begun!” The murmuring of the Ninja slows, almost 40 or so squeezing into the basement. They kneel in rows, before a stage of sorts, the backdrop being a giant bronze gong. The big ninja formerly referred to as “Old Lady” tries to shut the clan up… Soon enough, they all are seated, passing the popcorn around. The Clan leader sets up a tripod canvas with a display. The lights dim and a beam of light shines upon the lone ninja. Sensei: “Tell your boss what is wrong with this?” The Clan all squint at the grid on the canvas, on which a line graph is illustrated. A slight murmur circulates. Sensei: “You don’t know?” Silence. Sensei: “WELL THE PROBLEM SEEMS TO BE RIGHT HERE!!” The Ninja master attempts to pinpoint a specific location on the diagram, only he accidentally unsheathes his katana… The canvas hits the floor in two, merely causing the clan to be more confused, as the awkward murmur would signify. Sensei: “DAMMIT!!” Ninja #23: “What’s this all about?” Sensei: “WHAT?! WHAT?! IT’S ABOUT OUR FALURE AS A NINJA CLAN!!! THAT’S WHAT!” Hushed, the room watches the Ninja master carefully. Sensei: “We haven’t pulled have the weight we’ve gained on revenues! As far as I’m concerned, this bumbling group of fools is non-existent!” Suddenly, an outraged Clansmen Ninja flips to his feet with a clenched fist, shaken violently at the Sensei! Ninja #40: “BAH! It is this pitiful ‘Land of Opportunity’s’ fault!” Another follows. Ninja #31: “I second that… The homeland was our turf…” Sensei: “Indeed, it had been…” Ninja #31: “NOBODY HERE NEEDS NINJA!!!” Sensei: “Ignorance!!! There is so much untapped business here, hell, we could have saved ENRON, and got rich… here we can thrive with the proper advertisement!” The entire room breaks into a murmur as yet another outraged Clansmen rises… Ninja #7: “YOU MEAN THE SAME ADVERTISEMENT THAT HAS PREVENTED US FROM HAVING THE SAME NEIGBORS FOR MORE THAN A FEW WEEKS?!?!” Cut to a shot of a front door. There is a sudden whizzing sound, followed by the impact of 3 arrows impeding themselves into the door. The door opens, and at that point they see the flaming, bleached skull in a basket on their front step, the reaction anticipated… Cut back to the angry Clan leader, scowling. Sensei: “IMPOSSIBLE!! THE GREETING BASKET/CALLING CARD IS A BRILLIANT IDEA!!” The Clan goes off on a verbal tangent, the uproar lasting a good ten minutes before… Knock-Knock! They all heard it. All the way upstairs, from the front door, the sound had echoed deep into the basement when the Clan all pulled their blades halfway from its sheath. Sensei: “Oh shit! I forgot to pay the mortgage!!!” “MORTGAGE?!” Suddenly, the entire room goes white, a hissing smoke filling the air. The smoke dissipates rather quickly, leaving the Sensei to find out soon enough he’s all alone again.--- We find that dastardly, devious little Goblin on the front step of the 135 Washington St. residence, scratching his chin thoughtfully at the broken front window. Syko the Goblin: “…I hope this is the right address.”
