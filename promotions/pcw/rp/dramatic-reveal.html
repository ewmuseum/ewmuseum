Date: not entered (It’s another wholesome day here in THE Multiple Man’s secret lair. It’s now early afternoon and Tmm is only now finishing his wake up ritual with a nutritious breakfast of umm… looks like spaghetti, Kool Aide and a Pop-tart, untoasted. Calmly, he sifts through the morning paper; instinctively skipping past large boldfaced headlines like ‘BEACH ROBOT DOUBLE GOES HAYWIRE!’ for the eminently more satisfying material in the comic section. The doorbell rings.) Tmm: “Bah, never fails.” (The door shudders from impatient kicks planted on its surface.) Tmm: “Hang on, hang on!” (He tosses the paper to the side and rises to get the door while the kicks grow in both intensity and frequency.) Tmm: “Jeez, what a grouch.” (He flings open the door and comes face to face with a ninja of blood-red coloring. The ninja curses heartily and flings a small wall safe at Tmm! The chunk of tempered steel barely misses as Tmm dodges to the side and launches a mighty kick… which the Red Ninja casually catches and uses to dump Tmm on his backside. Tmm rolls to his feet, but the Red Ninja does not follow up.) Red: “What do you think you’re doing?” (Tmm blinks.) Tmm: “Defending myself from imminent assassination. Is this some sort of a trick question?” (The Red Ninja snorts laugher.) Red: “Yeah, like anyone would bother with that for you.” Tmm: “You did.” Red: “You’re confusing a slippery grip for interest.” (Oozing distain, the ninja tugs off the crimson cowl revealing the scornful features of official Entourage thug, Keira Marshall.) Keira: “Grow up already. Nobody cares in the slightest what you do.” Tmm: “Do not! Wait… do so? What was the question again?” Keira: “Yeah. Just get the safe. I don’t wanna carry it anymore.” (Briskly, she strides past Tmm, leaving him to lug the hefty weight all by his lonesome.) Tmm: (grunting) “Oooof! How about a little help here?” Keira: “Lift with the legs.” Tmm: “Gee thanks. I was hoping for something a little more tangible.” Keira: “It’s not THAT big. I managed to get it up all the steps just fine. Now get your mangy butt over here.” Tmm: “Ahem. Me boss. You underling. We ain’t married, so mind your tongue.” Keira: “Okay, fine. Get your mangy butt over here… please.” Tmm: “You’re pushing it. What is IN this thing? Gold bars?” Keira: “One would hope so. Where’s the blowtorch?” Tmm: “Kitchen, next to the forks.” Keira: “Gotcha.” (She vanishes through the doorway, and sounds of rummaging can be heard. THUMP. Tmm drops the safe as gently as possible on the coffee table. The cheap particleboard of the table bends slightly, but doesn’t break.) Tmm: ”Where did you get this thing?” (Distance and an intervening wall vaguely muffle Keira’s voice, but it is still fairly audible.) Keira: “CCW.” Tmm: “And do they know of their generous donation?” Keira: “Eh, they’ll figure it out eventually, I’d imagine.” (She steps back into the room. Blowtorch in one hand, a sparker and dark welding glasses dangling from the other.) Keira: “Still, it was the strangest thing…” Tmm: “Aside from you waltzing off with one of their safes?” (She shrugs off the sarcasm.) Keira: “Partly. I flew down there to do the obligatory camera stuff for their latest PPV, right?” (Tmm’s head snaps up.) Tmm: “That reminds me…” (He points straight at the camera.) Tmm: “Syko, you’re goin’ DOWN!” (He drops the pose.) Tmm: “Just paying the bills there. Okay… you were saying?” Keira: “Yeah… anyway, I flew down there and the whole place is absolutely buzzing with activity, but I’m not needed at the moment. So, as usual, I zonked out on the couch in the old secret lair, the one in the locker room, y’know? I’m out for five, six hours. I wake up, and it’s like the pod people landed. Not a person in the joint but me, no cars in the lot except for my rental. Nothing and nobody with zero indication why. Creepy.” Tmm: “Very. So what did you do?” Keira: “The only thing I could do… looted the place. Had to hire a tractor trailer to cart all the stuff away.” Tmm: “Of course. Get a good price?” Keira: “Still in the air. I haven’t finished inventory yet.” (She tugs on the goggles and lights up the blowtorch.) Keira: (narrowing the flame) “I’ll know in a few minutes.” (She touches the searing beam to the hinges as the camera fades out.)
