Date: not entered Ever take a freefall from an airplane over the city? It’s exhilarating…especially when you know there’s nothing to save you… No parachute…no nets…nothing… Just me and the god…falling… That’s what everyone wanted us to do…fall… Nobody likes us…they quite despise us. Everyone… Was it because we proclaimed ourselves better…because we proclaimed ourselves above them? People didn’t like when people put themselves above others. It lowered their own personal worth… But…why do they hate us for proclaiming the truth? Religions will fail…Goggles will break…Cowboys will be shot down…Snow will melt…Pharaohs will be buried…Devils will burn in hell…and Demons…they will be rebuked. But gods…gods will survive through the elimination…the god will stand alone. The wind whipped against us as we saw the earth rushing closer towards us. The rooftops of the buildings were drawing larger. The god was in a horizontal splash, his arms stretched out in a swan dive. I matched his fall, but I kept my eyes down on the ground. We were still dressed in our costumes…the disguises we used to get on the plane and get all the way to Tokyo. It was about time we shed our skin. The plane hit the runway frantically, but it managed to land without trouble. The stairway came rolled out by the crew, followed by a platoon of armed soldiers. They flanked out below the entrance of the plane and aimed their rifles in its directions. First person to come out…was Sonata, followed by Matrix. Ushio Kuonji led them by gunpoint. “What’s going on?” Sonata asked, baffled about what was going on. Matrix shook his head, still wearing his camouflage, looking like a preppy Japanese teen. “Calm down mom.” He said, playing the family act down to the wire. “We’re going to be okay.” “Yeah, keep telling yourselves that.” Ushio snapped. “You two helped in bringing a known threat to the country INTO THE COUNTRY! You’re lucky if you aren’t executed by gunpoint.” “What are you talking about?” Sonata asked him as they walked down the stairs. “Where are my husband and my daughter?” Ushio looked to the sky as the guards intercepted Sonata and Matrix, handcuffing them and leading them off to be questioned. “Tokyo is about to explode…” he muttered in awe. An office building…professional looking men and women move back and forth from desk to desk in silence…papers and manila folders in hand… Their silence was broken…as well as the roof. Something large crashed through it…and continued to crash for about three floors. With electrical wires snapping, debris caving in, and people screaming…the smoke cleared… And a monster of a man encased in unidentifiable metal…crouching in a bestial stance. By his inhuman side…a woman wearing black and ice blue vinyl and mesh knelt on one knee. Her ice blue hair draped her face…protecting the soul-stealing gaze from the mortals around them. She stood up…and threw her hands into the air. “Humans…bow down on your faces and welcome home…the NEO VOID GOD!” The mixture of screams and roars close the scene to darkness…
