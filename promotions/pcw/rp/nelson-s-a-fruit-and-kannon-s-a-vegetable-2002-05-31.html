Nelson's a fruit and Kannon's a vegetable.

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-02 -Duke Williams is pushing a shopping cart with his girl friend Kesha at a Dominick Food Store in Chicago, IL. Duke is wearing an old Willie Nelson T-shirt from like the 70’s, blue jeans, white gym shoes, chrome shades, and a Chicago White Sox cap. Kesha is looking hot in her milk chocolate skin, braided hair, and a flower dress. The couple is doing their weekly grocery shopping and it’s the first time Duke’s been in a grocery store in a long time.- Williams: Damn what’s with all these florescent lights?? You can get cancer in this mutherf*cker. Kesha: Well damn baby you can’t have a spot light on you at all times. Williams: Couldn’t they put up a bunch of 40watt wall lamps? Kesha: People don’t come here to f*ck they come here to buy food. Williams: Well it’s all the same to me. -The two walk down the aisle as Kesha tosses items into the cart. The come across the meat aisle, Kesha tosses two steaks into the cart.- Williams: Wait a minute. Kesha: What? Williams: Them damn things cost seven bucks a piece. Kesha: So? Williams: That’s a lot of money for a hunk of meat. Kesha: You’re a pain in the ass for a hunk of meat. Williams: Damn I could go to Denneys and get a steak, eggs, salad, soup, and half ass service for nine bucks. Kesha: But this is a Black Angus. Williams: I don’t care what color its anus is, seven dollars is too much. Kesha: You make a lot of damn money to be bitching over the price of a steak. Williams: It’s the principle behind it. I’m not going to let some corporate steak rancher get rich while the little people go hungry. Who owns this Black Anus? I bet it’s Tyler Nelson. Kesha: So do you want the damn steak or not? Williams: Yea get it this time but I’m going to Montana this weekend and I’m gonna get us a few foes. Kesha: Deer? Williams: Damn right I can get deer steak for free. Kesha: Deer steak? Williams: Some good old fashion venison. Kesha: I ain’t eating not god damn Bambi. Williams: Its not Bambi it was the bad deer in that movie. Kesha: There was no bad deer in that movie. Williams: Yes there was. He looked like Ty Nelson before the aids slimmed him down. Kesha: Your nuts. -The couple continues down the aisle.- Williams: Did you know today May 31st the Catacombs of Rome were discovered by accident? Kesha: What the f*ck are you babbling about? Williams: The Catacombs of Rome. Kesha: I’m calling the damn cable company and we’re getting rid of that history channel. Williams: No, I’ve been studying up on Greek stuff so I can have something to talk too with Big Alan Steele. You know friendship is a hard thing to create. Kesha: So your going to impress him with your vast, scripted knowledge of greek stuff. Williams: Yea Kesha: What happens when he steps out of the your scripted info. What if he asks you want the Catacombs are? Williams: They’re brushes for your pets, I think? Cat’s especially. -Kesha laughs at Duke’s lack of foreign culture.- Williams: What that’s not right? Kesha: I think you may come out of this thing with a foe instead of a friend. Williams: I can always fall back on the fact that bedroom Malice Style originates from Greece. Kesha(sarcastic): Maybe we should send them a thank you card. Williams: That a wonderful idea!! Where’s the thank you card section? Kesha: You’re amazing. -Duke smiles- Williams: Thank you. -The couple continue down the aisles to the fruit section.- Kesha: Aren’t you concerned with what Nelson and Kannon have up their sleeves. Williams: Ty Nelson’s stock has plummeted since the arrival of the booker. What ever he can think up the Malice Crew will end it. I have no fear but fear itself. Kesha: Yea you’re a regular Teddy Roosevelt. Williams: I was president you know! -Kesha rolls her eyes as Duke picks up a carrot and a star fruit.- Williams: You see this star fruit? Kesha: Yea. Williams: This represents Nelson since he thinks he’s a star and he’s a fruit. Kesha: Makes sense. Williams; The Carrot is Kannon because he’s nothing but a vegetable who can’t think for himself and the orange matches his hair. Kesha: I can see the resemblance. -Duke reaches into the cart and pulls out a bottle of whisky and a shish kabob.- Williams: Now you see I’m this here bottle of Whiskey cus I’m American proud, strong, make you crazy, and hard as a rock. The shish kabob is Steele since its Greek, sharp, meaty, and grade A beef. Now watch when the two go up against each other. -Duke takes the bottle of whiskey and pulverizes the carrot by pounding on it. Duke then takes the kabob and stabs the stick into the star fruit stabbing it over and over till its dead. People begin to stare as Kesha calms the fruit stabber down.- Williams: You see that Kesha? Come Turmoil what’s left of the Ultimate Authority will be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound…..ancient Greek Malice Style!! -The camera fades to yellow and black as the Malice Man goes back to shopping. -
