A visit to the Doctor

2 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/2/01 Well doc the problem is that I really do not feel to good about myself. That is what you can expect when you go around and beat up women you say you’re in love with, you won’t feel to good about yourself. We open in a large room that is very clean and tidy. The walls are all white and it appears to be an office. As the camera zooms out a bit we see book shelves full of books on various human subjects. Then we see the back end of a couch with some feet sitting on top. At this angle we can not see who is lying on the couch. There is a large desk with a huge chair behind it off to the right. There are arms resting on the arm rests of the chair but we can not tell either from this angle that is sitting there. The men continue to talk. Man: But doc I told you already that she wouldn’t stop disrespecting me. She keeps flirting and dating other men! Doc (Laughing): That is because she was never your you little twit! Haven’t we already gone over this time and time again? Man: Yes doc. But it is tough for me to understand. I really and truly loved her. Why couldn’t she love me back? Doc: Of course you are having a hard time understanding this. You are a mental midget. You don’t comprehend anything more difficult than a box of rocks. And did you ever think that maybe she couldn’t love you because you are quite honestly the ugliest thing I have ever seen on this earth. She would have to be a vegetable to love you. You need to stop worrying about her and move no with your pathetic life. Let’s talk about your career. How is it going so far? Man: Horrible as well. I won my first match but only because I got a very lucky break from a ref I had to pay off before the match. I would have easily lost if not for that ref. Doc: So your career is in no better shape than your romantic life. Basically you have no life at all. Why would you pay off the ref before your big debut match? Man (With shock in his voice): Because I suck ass! The man I had to face is a highly skilled athlete in the art of technical skill. He would have beaten me so bad that even the voice in my head would have disowned me. Doc I had to, so that I wouldn’t be totally humiliated in my debut. Doc: I see. You have no wrestling skill either so you turn to the age-old art of using money to get you out of a jam. Did you even think about attempting to face the man first, before resorting to your coward ass money scheme? Man: Yes I did Doc. Doc: Well what happened? Man: Well see he found me at my hotel the night before the match and we got into a fistfight. He blooded my face and damn near killed me right there. I knew right then for sure I could never beat him on my own. Doc: Why exact ally did he come to the hotel looking for you? Man: “Sighs” First we had a little face to face that afternoon at an arena. Nothing happened, I just tried to mess with his head. I failed, so I thought I would scare him another way. So I went out into the lot and ripped the brake cable from a vehicle I thought was his, but it wasn’t... Doc (cutting the man off): Wait just a minute. After one face to face you went out and tried to take him out of the match all together? I thought you said the buying off the ref was when you knew you couldn’t beat the man. Why did you feel the need to try and take him out before the match? Man: No. The ref was just my final defense. You see I knew I couldn’t beat him.... Well pretty much as soon as I found out about the match. I just failed to do anything about it before the match could take place. See I told you I sucked ass. Doc: Clearly you have some major problems there. My advice would be to go and kill yourself. Take some pills, jump off a bridge, whatever you think you could accomplish with out screwing up. Man(Clearly in shock): Doctor! You are supposed to help keep me from doing things like that. Not try and encourage it. Isn’t there some other way to help me? Doc: You could always castrate yourself. Then you would no longer desire women, or the need to feel like a man by wrestling. Man (Still in shock): I can’t do that either doc. My penis is small enough as it is. I already pretty much feel like I have nothing down there any way. Some other way please! Doc (serious tone): Well then you leave me only one other alternative. Since you are a flop with women, you have no brains, or penis then there is only one thing in the world you could do and not screw up. Man (Excited): Yes what is it? Doc: You must move to Tibet and become a monk. They do not have sex, so there would be no need for women. That clearly suits your talents in that department. Plus they have no brains since they do not have anything to do with women or beer. Again this is a big plus for you, since you also are brain dead. And then of course since they do not like women, clearly they have no penis. Which you just said you pretty much do not have one either. So there you are. A Tibetan Monk. Have a good day and send in my next patient. As the man gets up from the couch the camera switches its angle allowing us to see who it is. It is a midget size man wearing a black T-Shirt that has, “PCW Resident Freak” on the front in red letters. He has on torn jeans and the name “Ripper” in black paint on his forehead. As he walks out and closes the door behind him the camera shifts back to its original position. After a few moments we hear the door open then close. A few moments after that two more feet hang over the end of the couch. Doc: Welcome again. Where would you like to start today? Man: Well doc, you see I have this big match on Sunday. It is for a title and I have been all week long fighting this beast inside of me. It has finally gotten out and I am worried I will not be able to control it. What should I do? Doc: That is a problem. Well clearly you suffer from mental delusions. You are no more a beast or have the powers of one than you have the ability to win this match on Sunday. For a bit slower guy like you, that means you have none of those. No beast, no power, and clearly no chance. Man: But I have been hearing this voice in my dreams. They scare me to death. I have lost the last hold to my old self. Help me doc, I even see warriors killing evil monsters as a link to my match this Sunday. Doc (Laughing): You have been dreaming about a warrior taking out three evil monsters, and you some how think it is a prelude for your match on Sunday. That has to be the dumbest damn thing I have ever heard in my life! Have you been doing drugs before you go to bed at night? Man: No sir. I have just been going to bed. Why? Doc: Well there is your problem. You are clearly a man who needs to have drugs to keep from saying things that make you sound like a total jackass. My advice is that you go out and buy you some either heroin, or cocaine and use some every night before you go to bed. That way when you have these crazy visions and dreams you can blame it on substance abuse and not the fact that you are a total loser. Man (Bewildered): That can’t be a very good idea. Drugs are not a good thing for me. Is there any other way? Doc: Well you could just try running away and never looking back. Just leave here and go. Maybe become a mountain man and live off that land you love so much. Then you won’t have to worry about reaching for an unattainable goal on Sunday night, because that is a mountain top that no beast, dream, or any other ridicules crap like that will help you climb it. For you it is an unattainable height that you will never be able to climb. Now your time is up as just send in the last guy on your way out. Again as the man gets up the camera angle shifts to see a huge fat man with ice cold spiked blue hair. The man gets off the couch and pulls his pants up to cover his crack as he walks out the door and closes it. The camera once again shifts back to its original position. Suddenly we hear the door open and close and another pair of feet over the end of the couch. Man: Doc I am scared to death. I have some how one a title in PCW and now I have to defend it against three men who are clearly more dedicated and better athletes than me. I am just a sorry no count ass who stumbled into a title victory. What should I do? Doc: Well first you should go out there before the match up on Sunday, tell the world how big a fluke you winning the title was, and that you only wish to relinquish the belt before you get seriously injured. How does that sound? Man: Like I am a bit of a wuss. Doc: Well aren’t you? Man: Of course! But I can’t make that public. My parents would disown me forever if I did anything like that. I am in so much trouble. Doc: Ok here is an idea. You could show up in the match. Get the shit kicked out of you for about two minutes, which is all you would last if you tried any way, then get pinned and get the heck out of dodge. Then you wouldn’t lose face because you at least showed up, and that is the extent of your talent any way. That is what you should do. Man: That sounds a bit better but I still don’t think they would like it. Is there anything else? Doc: Sure there is the last resort. You could go out right after you leave here and find a bar. Then you drink beers, and mixed drinks until you finally pass out. But make sure you drink enough so that before you pass out you get so much in you that you have to be rushed straight to the emergency room and have your stomach pumped. Then you would be in the hospital and would have a legit excuse for being a coward. Man (Confused): But couldn’t that kill me? Doc: Theoretically it could. But you would only be doing you and the rest of the world a favor by taking your pathetic, no talent carcass out. Then no one would have to worry about feeling the need to vomit win you attempt to cut a promo or try to wrestle. That is the best thing you can do. Drink till you have to be pumped. Now leave my office. Your time is up and I have a lot more important things to do today. Man: But I have only been in here twenty minutes! Doc: Yeah and in that time we covered the only thing you have going for you. Drinking till you are pumped. No go! The camera again shifts to the couch where the man is getting up to leave. As you might have already guessed it is some one that is dressed to look like Lance Bell. He walks out as the camera shifts back to the desk. The man in the chair finally turns toward the camera revealing what you have probably already guessed unless you are one of those three jobbers we have been talking about here, that the man is Steve Johnson. He is wearing some a suit and tie. He smiles a bit as he looks into the camera. Steve: You see Ripper, Sylo, and Bell your problems are simple. You all simply suck, and none of you have a prayer of even making it a close contest on Sunday. Just take my free advise and follow your individual instructions I went over here today and you may be able to save yourself the embarrassment and pain of getting your ass’ handed to you. Now you had all better go and get your bags packed or your life savings of one hundred dollars withdrawn. If not, then it will be your funeral and I will see you on Sunday. Steve begins to laugh loudly. He turns the chair back around away from the camera and the scene fades away to black.
