Paradox Backlash... OR 'There are no plotholes!'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (Terror rules supreme at THE Multiple Man’s secret hideout!) Val: “The shield won’t hold out much longer!” Tmm: “MAKE it hold out! And keep shooting!” (The hastily cobbled together contraption in the center of the living room spits sparks and acrid smoke, despite Valerie’s best ministrations!) Val: “Dammit Tmm, I’m a scientist, not an Engineer!” (The entire lair shudders at a massive impact from outside!) Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]!” Val: “Oh… I guess I am an Engineer. Forgot about that.” (WHANG! She kicks the housing of the device with a foot. Immediately, the smoke lessens, but does not stop.) Steve: (howling from the roof) “Get more ammo up here, NOW!!!” (The tremendous ripping noise of HV’s man-portable Gatling gun thunders on and on. Another blast rocks the watertower!) Tmm: “Where the hell is Keira?! Or that god dammed Palm Pilot of Death when we need them?!” (The shield generator burst into flames, and random electrical arcs cascade throughout the area!) Val: “That’s it! It canna do no more! It’s gonna blow!” (She vaults over the wet bar and dives out of sight! The machine whines a high-pitched cry that steadily grows higher with each passing breath!) Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]!” (Roughly, he bashes Tmm with the butt of his rifle and tosses him behind the bulk of the sofa. WAAA-BOOOOOOM!!! With an ear-shattering roar, the generator detonates in a massive explosion that blows Raul across the room and sends him crashing into the far wall with a sickening crack. Raul’s blank eyes stare sightlessly from a head twisted at an unnatural angle.) Tmm: (groggily) “N… nooo!” (He crawls painfully from behind the sofa, choking as the nauseating stench of burnt flesh perfumes the air. Forcing his concussion-blurred vision into some semblance of clarity, he dearly wishes he hadn’t. The wet bar blazes like a bonfire in which nothing could survive.) Steve: (shrieking) “Where in the fuck is that ammo?! It’s closing in!” (The Gatling’s fury abates abruptly.) Steve: “It’s coming!!! It’s coming!!! It’s coming!!! It’s coming!!! IT’S COM…” (Like the cannon before him, Steve too is silenced. The walls shimmer and ripple as waves of distortion, no longer fended off by the shield, assault their very existence. Tmm stares dumbly as the ripples become more and more pronounced… almost as if the very walls were simultaneously heaving for breath and shivering from a sharp chill.) Tmm: (dully) “Why aren’t they breaking from strain?” (He slumps as reality finally gives up and shatters into millions of fragments both too small and too infinitely huge to comprehend. Colors gain taste, touch becomes visible, sound smells oppressive as the skein of what is unravels faster than faster than wool sweater at a kitten convention. All dissolves into nothingness.) Voice: “WE ARE NOT HAPPY WITH YOU. YOU REALIZE THAT, DON’T YOU?” (Tmm, or rather, the essence of Tmm in the Platonic Ideal expression of things, blinks its non-eyes and tries to take in the unfathomable.) Idealized Tmm: “…” (The impression of two smallish figures dressed in screaming yellow tights and flaring red capes both does and does not appear.) Idealized Tmm: “Consistency Man? Continuity Man? What are you two doing here?” (The two, moving in perfect mirror imagery of each other, turn away from Tmm while looking straight at him in his abstractness.) Con-men: ”YOU THOUGHT WE WERE JUST DERANGED MIDGETS WITH OBSESSIVE COMPULSIVE DISORDERS? YOU OF ALL PEOPLE SHOULD REALIZE THAT EVERYTHING IS TRUE, EVEN FALSE AND IMPOSSIBLE THINGS. WE ARE THE GUARDIANS OF REALITY, EXACTLY AS WE HAVE ALWAYS STATED.”Idealized Tmm: “But what happened?” Con-men: “THE STRAIN ON REALITY WAS TOO GREAT. MORE IMPOSSIBILITIES OCCURRED IN TOO SHORT A SPAN THAN COULD BE COMPENSATED FOR. VIOLATED TOO OFTEN, THAT WHICH BINDS SNAPS, AND EVERYTHING BECOMES MORE UNSTABLE THAN CARROTOP’S MOMENT OF FAME.” (Tmm thinks of whistling which, in this state, is exactly the same as doing it.) Idealized Tmm: “That’s pretty tenuous. It’s a wonder it held up for as long as it did.” Con-men: “QUITE.” Idealized Tmm: “So what happens now?” Con-men: “WE SHUNT YOU OFF TO A DIFFERENT CONTINUUM, GIVE YOU NEW FORM, AND ACT LIKE THE RECENT PAST NEVER, EVER HAPPENED.” Idealized Tmm: “But why me? Are you doing this for the others, too?” Con-men: “NO.” Idealized Tmm: “Why do I deserve this, then?” Con-Men: “YOU ALONE HAVE GRANTED US AIR TIME, AND WE FIND THAT WE LIKE IT. NO OTHER OF THE REALITY CHECKS IN PLACE HAVE SUCH PRESS, AND THE FAME GAINS US FREE DRINKS AND HOT CHICKS. WE WOULD LIKE IT TO CONTINUE.” Idealized Tmm: “Hmm… can I pick the continuum?” Con-men: “YOU MAY. CHOSE ANY ONE YOU WANT, THERE ARE AN INFINITE NUMBER OF SELECTIONS AVAILABLE.” Idealized Tmm: “Very well, I would like be sent to a universe that is exactly the same as the one before in every way… EXCEPT that a certain moron named Flaymin Aymin never existed.” Con-men: “HMMM…” Idealized Tmm: “What? There a problem?” Con-men: “YES… AND NO.” Idealized Tmm: “Could you be more vague?” Con-men: “YES, WE CAN SEND YOU TO SUCH A PLACE. AFTER ALL, THE CHOICES ARE INFINITE… BUT THERE IS SOMETHING OF A SNAG.” Idealized Tmm: “Which is?” Con-men: “THE CONCEPT WHICH YOU CALL FLAYMIN AYMIN IS SO DOWNRIGHT… DENSE, THAT IT’S A WONDER THAT HE HASN’T COLLAPSED IN ON HIMSELF AND FORMED A BLACK HOLE.” Idealized Tmm: “That’s impossible!” Con-men: “AGAIN, THE ANSWER IS BOTH YES AND NO. SINCE IT ISN’T A PHYSICAL DENSITY, YOU ARE CORRECT. THERE IS NO WAY THAT HE WILL IMPLODE, EVEN IF HE WANTED TO.” Idealized Tmm: “I smell a ‘but’ in there somewhere…” Con-men: “… BUT SINCE THE SHEER MASS OF STUPIDITY RESIDES IN THE METAPHYSICAL REALM, IT CREATES THE METAPHYSICAL EQUIVALENT OF THE MATERIAL BLACK HOLE. WARPED TIME AND SPACE BECOME ANALOGOUS TO WARPED LOGIC AND CREATIVITY. HIS VERY PRESENCE WARPS THEM INTO BIZARRE SIMULACRUMS OF THEMSELVES, COMPLETELY UNRECOGNIZABLE TO THE HUMAN MIND. RATHER LIKE WATCHING THE TWILIGHT ZONE WHILE HAVING A BAD ACID TRIP.” Idealized Tmm: “And that means…” Con-men: “YOU STILL HAVE A MATCH WITH HIM, AND HE’LL STILL BE LESS PLEASANT TO DEAL WITH THAN BEING WHAPPED WITH A DEAD FISH. SORRY.” Idealized Tmm: “Can’t be helped, I suppose. And I guess one can even learn to like being whapped with dead fish if it happens often enough… Still, it was a wonderful dream.” Con-men: “IT IS NON-TIME THAT WE GO.” Idealized Tmm: “Lead on, bwana…” (Transition like a bad jump cut. We see the inside of THE Multiple Man’s secret lair again, whole and undamaged. The entire Entourage, also whole and undamaged, is piled on the battered old couch, watching ‘Beastmaster’ for the upteenth million time. Tmm blinks and looks around wildly as two sets of memories slam themselves together.) Tmm: “What? Who? How? Where? Ouch!” (This last comes from a rather nasty poke in the ribs from Keira.) Keira: “Shh! This is the part where they make that guy a monster! My favorite!” Steve: (mumbling) “It would be, you soul sucker.” (The bickering, as usual, escalates rapidly. Tmm breathes a deep sigh of contentment as the camera fades out.)
