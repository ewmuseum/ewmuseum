Life is Good

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.24.03 Yes, life is good. People are finally climbing out of their dark crevices that were created by the rocks they hid under, to see the light. And that light, of course, is me. The momentum had begun to roll and the boulder, the juggernaut empire that I was beginning to bring forth, was springing forward at a breakneck pace. Relief is here and its name is Silvio Rochelli! My name’s been tossed around more than once. Little whispers of ‘Hey, that new Rochelli guy is pretty good’ have now become loud shouts and been expressed in more than just voice but in t-shirts, in print and on brightly colored signs held high above the heads of all the PCW fans with common sense! …Indeed, PCW’s fans with common sense, all three of them. I am not deaf! I hear the choruses of boos and the insults being verbally thrown at me as I walk down the aisle and bring my saving graces to the ring. I see the middle fingers and crotch chops pointed in my direction as I attempt to finally bring entertainment worth the price of admission. …And I smell the body odor of each fan doing such things. Out of the sheer niceness of my heart, I weed out such pieces of trash as Matt Jackson. Not only did I neutralize him and his fake campaign of propaganda where he attempted to express himself in such a way that he was not, a good guy and a welcome face to his industry, but I got rid of him completely! And thus freed your minds! Freed your minds to the truth: That the only fresh, new, welcome face in this industry shall be me. …But do the PCW fans realize that? No! Through their own blatant stupidity and ignorance, their minds clouded in such an intense jealousy that they fail to see my good intentions, the PCW fans to see. But all that will change on Fusion. If life is considered good now, life will be great after the event this Friday. It’ll be a great night for myself and the entire PCW as a whole. Not such a great night for James Edwards or SyN of course but who honestly gives a damn about a space man and an illiterate wrestler who can’t even spell his name right? No one? Yes, that’s what I thought, too. With the Rising Star Championship around my waist, it regains credibility as a TRUE wrestler and deserving champion will have finally come along. Dorn Tracer may have ‘made a name’ for the belt but I shall make a new name… And that name will be the sweet name of yours truly, Silvio Rochelli. After Tracer, there was Jackson who held the belt for a mere three days, which just proves how much of a fluke Jackson’s winning of the belt was in the first place. And then there was Edwards who only beat Matt Jackson once out of a five times. And I’ve eliminated Matt Jackson, so Edwards shall be a day at the beach, only with a more fitting reward than sunburn. After Friday Fusion, it will be confirmed that Matt Jackson’s supposed spot as ‘the best up-and-coming wrestler in the PCW will have been vacated and filled again by a certain nameless millionaire Italian. And all those who have refused to open their eyes and see the future big star of this federation will be forced to open their eyes. And those who have shown nothing but blatant disrespect towards me shall be FORCED to respect me because there is no position higher demanding of respect than a champion. Let it be known that June 27th shall mark the beginning of what will go down as the longest Rising Star Championship title run to ever hit this federation, even surpassing the record of Dorn Tracer. Who is going to derail me? Stone Burns, the misogynist who hates women supposedly so much but has trouble defeating one? SyN, the illiterate wrestler? Or maybe it will be Abner, the drunk who can barely find his way to the ring without falling off of the ramp. The answer is D, none of the above. James Edwards, we may be on an airship amongst the clouds but you must really have you head in the clouds to believe you are going to regain that title you have ultimately stripped the credibility from. You may be a want to-be astronaut but your mindset must really be in outside space if you even think of thinking that you stand a chance. It’s been carried on to me that you’ve been having nightmares. Poor baby has a bad case of ‘bad dream syndrome.’ I say this: Welcome to a bad dream scenario that you can’t wake up from. You’re heading towards your last days as the Rising Star Champion. Tick tock, tick tock, three days left. And don’t bother pinching your arm because I am a nightmare you will have to face and you definitely won’t get around. It doesn’t get any simpler than that. Yes, indeed, life is good. And the best part of it is: It’s only going to be getting better for me in the very, very near future.
