Jeremy 'The Jobber' Johnson picks up Natalia's sister from the airport. Aka Triple J finds a girl who smiles at him before she shouts at him

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd April We rejoin JJ as the walks out of Anaheim International Airport carrying a bag. He’s dressed in Miyagawa’s wardrobe... yep, just Angel from the TV show, long black jacket, black shirt, black jeans and black trainers. He’s pushing one of those baggage trolleys, and it has three matching suitcases stacked on it neatly. He steps out onto the pavement and sticks his thumb out for a cab. A black guy stands near him also doing the same. Man: Damn, my brothers will be pissed, twenty goddamn minutes and not a single cab... this world is racist! RACIST I SAY! RACIST! Walden glances over but doesn’t really take anything in as he continues to hail his own without much success. Suddenly then a quite young, but still pretty damned attractive lady walks up beside Walden and sticks her thumb out... and two cabs pull up immediately... the black man leaping in one and screaming... Man: HA! TRYING TO DECIEVE THE NIGGER!!! FOOLED YOU SUCKAS!!! NOW DRIVE ME TO KFC ALREADY BOY!!! DRIVE!!! JJ and the young girl look over to the black man driving off in his yellow over priced airport taxi. The driver of the other one gets out of the other one (the one that stopped nearest to JJ and the girl anyway) and starts unloading JJ’s luggage in the back of the cab. JJ opens the door, and silently the girl sits down in the cab. JJ runs around the other side of the cab and gets in to the other side of it. He sits down nervously next to the young lady and buckles up his seat belt. JJ: So... um... how are you enjoying the US so far? She turns to him and smiles for a second. He probably has a heart attack... mainly because he’s not used to women in general smiling at him... men... particularly those of the sort that Chris Chase is a member of, for sure, but not women... that’s just... unnatural. Lady (in a husky Russian accent): From, the passport checks and the metal detectors that I have only seen of your country so far, da I like it. Maybe you should ask me after I’ve spent more than ten minutes in the land of the free, da, perhaps? JJ smirks back at her trying to be reassuring. JJ: I’m sorry, but I’ve forgotten your name already, I’m really bad with names... I thought that Natalia was Natasha until Miyagawa nearly hit me when I kept trying to get her attention by yelling Natasha at her repeatedly. Lady: Yelling? JJ thinks for a split second about this young woman’s sister lying in a hospital bed and the moron sitting next to her who just gave the impression that he screams at her on a regular basis. JJ: Um... I was trying to get her attention... I couldn’t find the ironing board. It turned out that Miyagawa had broken it and hadn’t told her yet. Lady: What is she treated as then, some kinda of maid? Some kinda of cleaner? Some kind of Russian slave? JJ shakes his head furiously as the driver gets back in the card and starts to drive. Driver: So where will it be to? JJ: You know the old gym on Dunn Road? Now opened as the Miyagawa Dungeon? Driver: Ah yes, I know the place... ten minutes. JJ: Alright, that’s cool. The lady still looks at JJ, which makes JJ more than his normal level of nervousness. And considering how high that normal level is (it covers most situations... spiders... flies in the room.... standing at bus stops with Amish....) it’s quite an achievement in itself. Lady: So you were saying... she is a cleaner in a dungeon? JJ shakes his head furiously once again. JJ: Nononononono... it’s called a Dungeon as a kind of reference to Stu Hart’s wrestling dungeon... it’s a kind of school. Miyagawa has been renting it out for Puroresu training. Lady: Bah, Russian wrestling is far superior to your Nancy Japanese dancing. Comrade Stanislav rules the wrestling world from upon his column of communism. JJ: (Muttering) And I thought Natalia was the radical one as well... Lady: What? JJ: Huh? I just said you never told me your name again. Lady: My name is Lara, and yours is Jeremy, I contacted Alexander via email and he gave me the low down on you. JJ: Alexander? Everyone here just calls him Miyagawa, lady. Lara: My name is Lara, and you may address me as such. JJ: Are you sure you wouldn’t prefer Princess Lara of the Russian Empire! Lara: WHAT DID YOU SAY?! JJ goes completely pale. JJ: Um... did I just say that out loud? Lara nods at him. JJ: Oh... whoopsie? I guess we’ll be there in eight minutes... and I guess its gonna be a long eight minutes.
