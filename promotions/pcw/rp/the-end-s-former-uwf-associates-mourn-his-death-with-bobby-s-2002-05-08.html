The End's Former UWF Associates Mourn His Death, with Bobby Stormbringer Hosting in Orlando, FL (Might not get this, but it's really a spoof of Darkstar's Funeral RP a while back, since we're in the Endorsement Match together)

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/02 The sky was dark, the heavens crying. The masses gathered together in the beautifully furnished and flowered funeral home in Orlando, FL to pay their respects to the dearly departed. The building is plain white on the outside, though built like a Georgian mansion with gadrooned pillars flanking the doors and supporting the roof overhand. Windows line the funeral home, in which flowers are placed on the interior. Flowers also make up 50% of the area around the funeral home. Lilacs, lavender, and violets would be giving off a pleasant smell, but the rain masks it. The grassy area is large, the grass soft and thick, with only a black pavement driveway with clearance for one car in a semi-circle that leads in and around, and back out. A stream of covered black bodies files in two at a time, and all take their seats inside. At the door, UWF referee Billy Blindman (played by Judge Mills Lane in a black suit) checks people at the door. The first UWF person to arrive is Triple X (Triple H, we were able to get Paul Levesque for this shoot) with his bodyguard Josh (cast by Andrew Martin, known in the WWE as Test). Josh has a standard gray business suit on, while Triple X is wearing a black business suit. Blindman eyes Triple X and Josh in their business attire. It’s not exactly appropriate for the funeral. Blindman: “Um… I think you’re slightly out-of-dress for this event.” XXX:“It doesn’t matter. This event should be dedicated to me. I can’t believe that some wrestler named Bobby Stormbringer is throwing a party dedicated to The End, when I’m so much more deserving than that puss, End.” Blindman shakes his head in confusion and disdain, though he looks down and away from the much more muscular Triple X. Blindman:“Sir, you do realize this is a funeral, right?” Triple X recoils. Turning his head to Josh, his face seems to say, “How could we forget?” XXX:“Really? And here I am without a gift for the family! Hold on, I’ll be right back.” Blindman:“Wait!” Triple X and Josh leave the way they came, hurrying out to the Dollar Store and Publix to get a cheap gift for The End’ family. They ignore Billy Blindman as he tries to call after them. Giving up on Triple X and Josh, Blindman goes back to attending the door. A few minutes later, Ronin (Keanu Reeves) walks in dressed like Neo from The Matrix. Ronin:“So, a funeral, huh? Funny, Bobby Stormbringer’s throwing this little get-together when I don’t think anyone’s actually sad about The End’s death. But I guess someone had to do this.” Blindman finds Ronin’s name on the list and looks up at him. Blindman:“Here to pay your respects?” Ronin looks at Blindman, half-disgust, half-surprise. Ronin:“What? After everything The End is responsible for me going through, you expect me to ‘pay my respects?’ Hell no. I’m here to piss on the corpse and drag my ass over the grave. THAT’S comeuppance.” Ronin brushes past Blindman and goes inside to take his seat. Blindman rolls his eyes and shakes his head, marking Ronin’s name off the list of guests yet to arrive. The raining is not very hard, more of a strong drizzle. The sky is dark, but that’s more from the time of day than the clouds. It’s summer, and the sun rises later, so the overcast skies of the early morning funeral makes it look darker than it really should be. Shortly, Triple X and Josh return with a gift. XXX:“Here you go, a bottle of Fanta and a basket of condolence cheeses for the family. Josh.” Josh hands the plastic-wrapped basket over to Billy Blindman. Blindman accepts the gift, but just sets it at the ground. Blindman:“I tried to tell you before you left that there are no family members are here. He doesn’t seem to have any.” Triple X looks at Josh and shrugs. He walks inside, saying one last thing to Blindman in an aside as he passes. XXX:“Oh well, it’s only $3.79. Keep it.” The next wrestler to show up is (Michael Clarke Duncan of The Green Mile and most recently The Scorpion King) DOA, wearing a nice black suit. Blindman:“Ah, thank you for coming to pay your respects.” DOA, who was silent and blank-faced before, springs up in Blindman’s face. DOA:“WHAT THE F*CKING HELL ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT, YOU PUNK B*TCH PIECE OF SH*T! THE LAST THING I’M HERE TO DO IS RESPECT ANYONE, LET ALONE THAT DEAD C*NT, THE END!” Blindman cowers down, hunched away smaller than his upright height, which still is only 5’8”. Blindman:“I’m sorry! I’m sorry, please, just… enter.” DOA gives Blindman a hard shove, pushing him back onto the wet ground. Blindman retrieves the guest list, which landed a few feet away from him, and crosses off DOA. DOA goes inside. Blindman gets up and returns to the door. From the inside, Bobby Stormbringer’s voice calls out to him. ”I think it would be in your best interests to alter what you say,” Stormbringer calls from inside. “That’s the tenth time this morning you’ve been abused for your choice of words.” Blindman:“Yeah, yeah…” A little later, Ashton White (Shawn Michaels speaking in a bad English accent), Working Class Hero (Sean O’Haire with a buzz cut), and Flea (yes, the actual Flea) show up. Ashton and WCH are dressed for the occasion in black suits. Flea, on the other hand, is wearing a green fishnet cut-off sleeve shirt, tie-dyed orange cargo pants, and Army boots. Blindman eyes Flea. Blindman:“We have a dress-code for these proceedings.” Flea holds his arms out to his sides level with his waist and looks at himself. Then he looks over to WCH and Ashton for support. Flea:“What?” Ashton shakes his head at the ground and pats Flea on the shoulder. Ashton:“I told you that your outfit clashed like the British and French at Waterloo.” WCH:“Would it have been so hard to put on the suit I got for you?” Flea puts his hands to his hips, looking with disbelief at his two associates. Flea:“I can’t believe you can’t see how trendy my new style is. It’s a hit in that one town….” Blindman sighs with frustration and marks off all their names. Blindman waves them in, but says one last thing while pointing to Flea. Blindman:“Just… don’t sit up front. And try not to move around too much. We don’t want you to draw a lot of attention.” Flea, Ashton, and WCH move along. WCH whispers to Blindman as he passes. WCH:“I’ll keep an eye on him.” Ashton, WCH, and Flea disappear from sight. Blindman stands his post at the door. A few moments later, Flea runs back out, snatches the basket of cheeses and Fanta, and runs back inside. Blindman shakes his head (people are doing that a lot in this RP) and brings his attention back to his assignment. Later on, Bobby Stormbringer’s girlfriend Kira comes out from the building and approaches Blindman. She has a black dress on, more like a black blouse and mini-skirt, but still the all-black motif is kept intact (too bad the same can’t be said for Flea). Her hair is straightened for the first time, and a dark brown. Kira:“Hey, Bill. How’s the guest list coming along?” Blindman skims his list. Blindman:“So far, we’ve got Ronin, DOA, Triple X w/Josh, the LCrue w/Flea, and Mike and Jake Snappy here. There are some others that came that put in to come earlier, but they’re not really important.” Kira:“Extras?” Blindman:“Yeah.” Kira:“Ah. Well, we’re still about two hours away from beginning the service. Sorry to keep you out here so long.” Blindman:“It’s alright, ma’am.” Kira:“Good. Just keep waving people in when they get here.” Kira turns away and walks back into the building. Blindman resumes his post. The rain continues to fall and the people continue to come. And to think, all this activity… all this fuss over The End… and it’s a funeral. {Time Compression: Two Hours) Most of the guests have arrived, but some are still on their way. We've dealt enough with getting past Billy Blindman, so let's go inside and see how things are going. The camera fades in with Ashton sitting at the bar, next to an unfamiliar man. Ashton:'It's a shame, ya know, everything that's happened. First the Threat comes in and then their leader gets killed. We've already had Criss Croft die, and now The End, who just left the UWF a few months ago, gets killed so soon after?' The man replies, though with blank expression and in monotone. In fact, he just looks straight ahead at the back wall of the bar. Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Ashton:'I mean, it gets to where you think that wrestling's getting too dangerous. Maybe that retirement would be a wise idea before I get killed myself.' Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Ashton stops himself, confused. Ashton:'Um, right. You alright man?' Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Ashton shakes his head in confusion and anger. Ashton:'I'm serious, cut it out!' Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Ashton:'Yeargh!' As Ashton is about to bear down on the man with punches and kicks, Kira stops him. Kira:'Ashton, stop! Don't bother him; he's just an extra. He only has one line.' Ashton steps back, brow furrowed in even more confusion. He turns and walks off. Outside, Terror Australis, a stable of Australians consisting of Mike Blast, Jr. (Kid Kash), NYM (he’s never revealed what his initials stand for; in this RP, he will be played by ECW standout, Nova), and Southern Assassin (Crowbar) arrives, looking spiffy in their stylish black funeral suits that look more like black vest/shirt/pant combos that should be on a runway in Milan. MBJ:'Okay, we're all here.' Blindman:'... You are?' Blindman knows who they are; he’s just messing with them. NYM:'Uh, Terror Australis?' SA:'You referee all our matches in UWF.' Blindman ruffles the list, searching through its names. Blindman:'What was the name again?' SA and NYM are frustrated. MBJ still tries to be patient with Blindman. MBJ:'Terror Australis.' Blindman goes back through the list. Blindman:'Could you... spell that please?' NYM slaps Blindman across his left ear. Blindman:'Ow. Fine, go in.' Terror Australis passes Blindman, muttering curses at him. Back inside the funeral parlor, Triple X and Josh are speaking with DOA near the front right wall facing the stage from which the eulogy will be given. Triple X and Josh are indifferent to the proceedings, while DOA is angry. And it very clearly shows on his face. XXX:'Wow, the first guy to leave UWF and die. We’ve hated The End ever since we were in UWF together, and we were just about to go find him and beat him to death. And look what happens. Let this be a lesson to us, we need to be more proactive. Either that, or we can plot to kill people, and it’ll just happen soon enough anyway.' DOA:'I don't care, he was going to die around this time whether it was us or someone else who did it. And getting back to our own in-fed business, The Threat is crushed after our last PPV, and now it'll be that much easier to see to it that The End gets company in Hell.' Josh:'I want to get my on the JCrue. Those reject Secret Service agents... I've got everything they've got and more. I TOOK A BULLET FOR STAR JONES ONCE! And you know that's an extremely fat ass target to cover.' DOA and Triple X eye Josh as if he had just committed cardinal sin. Josh doesn’t understand and shrugs in a body language that suggests a question. Josh:'What?' XXX:'You're not supposed to talk in this RP. You're a non-speaking extra.' Josh gets visibly upset. Josh:'What? I'm your personal bodyguard and I don't get any lines?' Josh grabs a roving extra, in fact, the same one Ashton talked to earlier, by the neck and pulls him into the conversation. Josh:'Even this guy gets a line in the RP!' The extra responds since he was addressed, albeit indirectly. However, given that he only has one line, he can’t say more than this. Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' DOA lashes out, punching the man square in the nose. His nose breaks, blood spurts out; the man’s eyes roll back into his skull and he collapses unconscious on the floor. Triple X and Josh look at DOA in confusion. They know he’s a violent person, but even so, they weren’t expecting this. XXX:'What did you do that for?' DOA:'I just hadn't attacked anything in a while. I needed to 'release'.' Josh:'Forget you, I don't even have any lines in this RP! Who do I take it up with?' Triple X points off-camera to a black man, about 6'3' 230 pounds, wearing glasses (that would be me); he sits at a Toshiba laptop computer, tacking at the keys. DOA:'He's scripting this thing take it up with him.' Josh storms off-camera to argue with the writer of the RP. Triple X and DOA return to their conversation. XXX:'So anyway, now that The End bought the farm, the only other people on our ‘To Kill’ List is the Threat, so I think it's time we stopped playing games and finished them.' DOA:'Yeah. They aren't sh*t; never have been. I could take them all on and win.' XXX:'Not before I get my turn.' Josh returns, not in nearly the same state as before. DOA:'You beat the hell out of the writer?' Josh:'I was, but something stopped me.' In a second, we'll move over to Ronin. Josh:'You can't! I'm not done complaining about my lack of lines here!' Listen, when I was in UWF last year, you weren’t anything more than a Non-Player-Character, and I doubt that’s changed. XXX:'He's got ya there. Triple X and DOA walk off-camera. DOA seems to gain about four inches in height, this being when he steps onto the chest of the extra with all of his weight. A moment later, Flea takes the extra's watch. Josh just stands there. Ronin is already in his seat. El Tamale, (played by the Jerry McGuire Kid) a 3’6” tall Mexican luchador wearing all black mask, cape, and boots, is sitting next to him trying to converse. Of course, he doesn’t speak one word of English. El Tamale:'(Something in Spanish.)' Ronin:'I can't understand you.' El Tamale:'(Something in Spanish).' Ronin rolls his eyes while looking badass in his Matrix-style outfit. Ronin:'Really, I don't have a clue what you're saying, stop talking to me.' El Tamale:'(Something in Spanish.)' Ronin jumps up and grabs the passing WCH. He pulls him down and sits him in the seat by El Tamale; he does this all while looking badass in his Matrix-style outfit. Ronin:'This is WCH. Talk to him.' WCH, not a full-time wrestler but familiar enough with El Tamale, begins to talk with him. WCH:'(Something in Spanish?)' El Tamale:'(Something in Spanish.)' WCH:'(Something in Spanish!)' WCH and El Tamale look at Ronin and laugh. Ronin grimaces at them. Ronin:'What, you think you're metter than be?' Ronin stops himself, realizing he’s made himself look stupid by messing up his line. He looks around at the other people in the room. WCH and El Tamale, along with Ashton, Triple X, DOA, Josh, and Terror Australis laugh at Ronin. He is about to respond, but his cell phone rings. Ronin:'Hello?' Lawrence Fishburn:'Follow the white rabbit, Neo.' Ronin:'F*CKING STOP IT!' Ronin spikes his phone at the floor and stamps in with his foot. Ronin:'How many of you saw that coming?' All hands in the room, even those of the extras, go up into the air. Ronin turns and walks out. Ronin:'F*cking writer. Never should've signed up for this sh*tty RP.' Josh jumps in front of the camera. Josh:'However much Ronin was made fun of, it's better because he at least got some lines. I haven't had one line scripted in this entire RP!' The extra with the broken nose passes by, back to consciousness and wearing a bandage over his nose. Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Josh turns down beats down on the extra. In the background, Flea is trying to sell the stolen Casio watch to UWF Vice President Brandon Walker (Mike Awesome). Flea shoves the watch into Walker’s face, even though it’s obvious that he is wearing a better, more expensive Rolex on his left wrist. Flea:'It's a good watch! Look at it!' Brandon squints his eyes and notices some dark red-violet stains on the digital Casio watch. Brandon:'Is that blood?' Flea turns away from Brandon and hurriedly tries to wipe off the blood with his fishnet shirt. Brandon:'And what's with your clothes? You look like a hippie-goth.' From across the room, WCH and El Tamale laugh and point at Flea. Flea:'No one can appreciate my style.' Southern Assassin steps in from wandering around the room and mingling. SA:'That really doesn't count as a style. That's fashion potluck.' Though Josh is still beating down on him, the extra speaks about Flea’s attire. Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Flea:'Hey, shut up!” Kira goes up onto the small raised platform that serves as the stage. Kira:'Everyone, please. Have a seat. We're ready to begin the service.' Ashton:'Finally.' Most everyone takes a seat. Some snoring comes from a sleeping man in the back. Standing at the left wall from the stage are DOA, Triple X, and Josh. Josh:'I can't believe I still don't have any lines scripted!' The extra lying on the ground by Josh speaks. Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Josh stomps the extra. Josh:'Shut up!' Terror Australis turns around and looks at Josh. SA:'Keep it down!' NYM:'El Tamale's up!' El Tamale comienza su habla. El Tamale:'¿Era el arrendajo End un amigo muy estimado a todos nosotros... que es yo bromear? El arrendajo End era una persona terrible, y aunque yo nunca hubiera deseado la muerte sobre nadie él definitivamente no dirigió lo que se podría llamar una vida buena.' Con la excepción de WCH, nadie en los asientos puede entender que qué El Tamale dicen. Ellos dicen entre dientes entre sí mismos. El Tamale dirige WCH. El Tamale:'¿Qué parece ser el problema? ¿Digo yo la cosa injusta? ¿Manning dónde cuando yo lo necesito?' WCH:'Yo no pienso es lo que usted dice, pero cómo usted lo dice. No muchas personas en la audiencia pueden entender el español.' El Tamale:'¿Usted piensa?' La voz de Bobby Stormbringer viene de la espalda de la habitación. Bobby Stormbringer:'Tener todo el ambiente y la narración en el español probablemente no ayudan nada.' Kira sube en la etapa y chuchicheos, 'Es muy agradable de usted salir. Gracias.' El Tamale cabecea y sale la etapa para encender, el aplauso forzado de la audiencia. Kira:'Um... thank you, El Tamale. The next to speak is Brandon Walker.' Brandon Walker walks upon stage. There is more snoring. His suit makes him look like James Bond. He takes a piece of paper out from his jacket and unfolds it. He clears his throat and begins. Brandon:'I wrote a little poem about this. 'Ding dong, the b*tch is dead!Which dumb b*tch, that d*mn punk b*tch.Ding dong, the stupid b*tch is dead! 'Waltzing into UWFActing like he ran the place;Now we all are truly blessed,Someone in PCW crushed in his face 'Thought he was the true world champ;He got cut down like a tree.He had more tricks than a $2 tramp,But we actually enjoyed watching him bleed 'Now the putz is gone,our mission's done.We've all won,Now let's have some fun And send the man that weighs one metric tonTo dance on The End's withered testicles.' Mike Meyers in the Fat Bastard suit jumps on stage and starts dancing a jig. Brandon:'Ding dong, the b*tch is dead!Which dumb b*tch, that d*mn punk b*tch.Ding dong, the stupid b*tch is dead!' Brandon finishes his poem and bows. The audience gives him a standing ovation. Brandon is cheered off the stage. As the audience winds down, Kira comes back on stage. The man continues snoring loudly. Kira:'Okay, next to speak is Jeremy Johnson.' Jeremy Johnson (played by Hardcore Holly and wearing a plain black suit that doesn’t appear to be for anything) comes in and takes the stage to speak. Johnson:'You know, I never liked The End.' Jeremy Johnson stops, leaving it at that. He never even took his hands out of his pockets. Johnson was never very articulate about his feelings, he was one of those guys to talk in the ring and the ring alone. Kira comes back up with the snoring still going on. Kira:'Thank you, Jeremy Johnson. That was... succinct. Anyway, next up is... oh Lord... Flea.' Kira steps down off the stage as Flea comes up. Now that everyone gets a real good look at Flea, the audience laughs at him. Flea shouts at them. They calm down, and Flea starts to speak. Flea:'Well, I'd just like to say that... uh... line?' Bobby Stormbringer's voice comes from the back of the room. Bobby Stormbringer:'Dammit, Flea! We’re doing this RP live! Just make something up!' DOA and Triple X laugh at Flea. XXX:'Dumb*ss.' Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Josh looks with evil eyes at Flea, who is still on stage fumbling for words. He never told anyone he’s been dealing with stage fright for the longest time. Flea:'Uh...' From the side, DOA charges in and flips Flea for a loop with a HUGE clothesline. DOA picks Flea back up and hits the Dead End, his finisher spinning butterfly DDT. DOA stands up and kicks Flea a few times before taking the stage. DOA:'I don't think anyone minded that. Now let's just finish this up so we can go desecrate the body.' DOA leaves the stage. Ashton and WCH come and scoop Flea off the stage and toss him out in front with Ronin. Ronin looks at the body. Ronin:'Might be something in there worth seeing after all.' Ronin leaves Flea with a kick to the ribs and goes back inside, where Josh has commandeered the stage. Josh:'That thing gets lines in this RP and I don't? What the hell is going on here?' There are various comments from the crowd in response to Josh’s whining. Ronin:'No one gives a d*mn about your problems.' SA:'Yeah, we're insulting The End right now.' Ashton:'And besides, you may not have any lines scripted, but do you realize you've still spoken more than I have in this RP?' A shocked look of 'Duh' comes over Josh's face. Josh:'Um... wow. I never thought about it like that. Boy, do I feel dumb.' A familiar voice speaks up. Man: *sighs*'Yeah, it's really sad.' Josh jumps off the stage and punches and kicks the extra even more. Kira comes back onstage. Kira:'Are there any other speakers?' Voice:'ME.' The camera and the audience looks back to the door to see Nathan “Bagger” Bagley, a stoner wrestler from Bakersfield, California (played by Chris Benoit) standing in the center of the aisle. His voice boomed up to the stage. He looks determined. He knows what he's going to say. Bagger moves onto the stage as Kira steps off. Bagger:'Hmph. Look where we are. Do you remember all those times I beat you within an inch of your life, End? I remember when I first saw you here in the UWF. The little man that I saw as being nothing more than a mischievous kid with a slingshot of a mouth; Dennis the Menace, that's all you were. 'To think you called yourself a superstar in MY FED? While you're spending eternity having pineapples shoved up your *ss alongside Hitler, wondering where you went wrong, just remember that the only reason you even lasted as long as you did in the UWF is BECAUSE I WILLED IT! 'You were nothing! A little snot-nosed punk that thought he ran the show. You couldn't run a sandbox! I--' Bagger addresses the sleeping man. Bagger:'Am I putting you to sleep? You will listen with respect when I talk! Who is this?' Bobby Stormbringer:'We're not sure. He was in here when we showed up, and he won't wake up.' The writer apologizes for a terrible use of a reference to Half Baked Bagger shakes his head and exhales in disgust, returning to his speech. Bagger:'Ugh. Anyway, frankly, I'm disappointed that you are dead. Were it me in the ring with The End, I'd kidnap him and make every waking moment of his life a Bakersfield Death Match. 'I am truthfully tempted to kill myself just to go after End and kick his ass in Hell. But I'll just have to settle for others until out little... reunion. 'Remember... no one is safe.' Bagger leaves the stage and stands on the wall opposite DOA, Triple X, and Josh. Bobby Stormbringer comes up on stage. He speaks in his usual ego-filled bravado, carrying an aura of perceived grandeur that only he could. He hasn’t even taken off his trademark sunglasses that he says Poser 4 artists have such a hard time doing right. Stormbringer:'Thank you all for participating, and hopefully we've all rehearsed this enough so that we'll be ready for after the Supremacy Molsson Endorsement Match when we can do this for real.' There is applause from the audience. Bobby Stormbringer:'As for the mock funeral, we’re all done here. In the back, I’ve got tons of life-sized voodoo dolls of The End. You can do with them as you wish. Oh, and Kira brought carrot cake and brownies at the bar.' Everyone goes to eat and hang, stab, and burn The End in effigy. Quite a weird turn of events, huh? Special thanks to Bobby Stormbringer for allowing me to use him in my RPs until he returns, and thanks to Darkstar for participating in the Molsson Match so that I may construct an RP targeted to him.
