The Stupidest Wrestler Ever

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-27-02 [Fade In] [We’re in a room featuring light green and light blue curtains, and various other surroundings that inform us we’re in the bedroom of a female. The lights are out, but the morning sun is seeping through the closed blinds of the room. In one corner of the room is a desk, stationed at the desk is PCW star ‘Scandalous’ Ryan Stephens. His laptop is setup on the desk that features a heart-shaped pencil holder and a pencil sharpener covered in Lisa Frank stickers. He’s viciously typing away at the computer, and it wakes up his wife who is laying in the girly bed. She lifts her head from the pillow and pleads…] Dana: Ryan, it’s early, come back to bed! [Ryan adverts his attention from the computer to his wife. He laughs, as she is still virtually asleep, her eyes half closed, hair tossed about all over the place. His laugh doesn’t warrant a response from her, but Ryan speaks to her.] Ryan: Baby, I just watched via Webcast a Mark Makonie feature. Apparently he’s running for the National Socialist Party of America. [Ryan laughs quietly mostly to himself, but Dana notices now wiping the sleep from her eyes.] Dana: Well what’s so funny about that? Ryan: [In utter sarcasm] It’s just that he’s so articulate baby. I mean Mark wants to lead people and yet his IQ can’t be much over a hundred. I bet he wrestles in a blind rage, because he’s not smart enough to try to think of how to pick his opponent apart, and counter big moves like most wrestlers. He wants to be a leader of this party, and yet he’s a moron, not at all smart like Hitler was. You have to be smart to lead people, you have to be, regardless of how stupid and ignorant a cause and the people following that cause. Dana: Well look a there baby, you determined one of his tendencies without even having to go watch him fight Akiyama. Now why don’t you crawl back in bed with me for a bit? Your sister’s old bed is really comfortable. Ryan: Well I was going to go for a morning run, and then my mom said something about cooking us breakfast, but I am still kind of full from all that fish I ate at your parents last night. Give me just a few more minutes and I’ll come back to bed baby. [She sighs and nods, and then rolls over and lets her head collapse onto the pillow. The couple visited her parents last night, and then spent the night and Ryan’s parent’s house, in his little sister’s old room since his old room featured twin beds. Today Ryan, Dana, and both of their Mom’s would go house shopping in the Woodlands before Ryan had to catch his plane back to Tennessee for Fusion.] [Ryan begins looking at a transcript for the Makonie segment featured on television last night, and laughing about all the mistakes involved. Ryan speaks to himself as he reads through the transcript.] Ryan: [laughing] Makonie is going to get upsetted. [still laughing] Upsetted? What kind of word is that? Not only that, he claims the sun is beating down on the groud. The groud for crying out loud. [Ryan scrolls down on the transcript sent to him, by someone who helps out the Malice Crew behind the scenes. Ryan can’t help laughing as he continues catching various mistakes in Makonie’s segment.] Ryan: Definatly !? What the hell kind of word is that? Is he trying to say definitely or defiantly or what? I mean come on. Mark could do us all a favor and at least take his time writing up a good transcript before coming on television and trying to corrupt youngster minds. [pauses] What am I thinking? Mark doesn’t have a transcript; he reads cue cards! Yeah, that’s what he does! He just can’t read the cards very well so he mis-pronounces a few words here and there I suppose. [Ryan decides to pass by numerous other mistakes and scrolls down the transcript to Mark’s lengthy and inspiring speech. Once again you sense the sarcasm. As he reads over Mark’s speech he speaks to himself, still caught up in the earlier mistakes.] Ryan: Look at me, I’m Mark Makonie! Hey I just made a rhyme. Come youngsters, and old people alike, follow me and become a Nazi so that you can spend all your time filling the world with hateful thoughts instead of learning how to read. [Stephens laughs aloud in his own depiction of what he thinks Makonie’s speech will read like. He laughs so loud that his wife rolls over, and in a slurred, sleepy speech states…] Dana: Stop making so much noise and come on to bed! [Ryan makes a face that reads ‘oops’ all over his face, but he continues reading the text on the computer.] 'Now I think that the African American race is not as good as us white race… Ryan: The African American race is not as good as us white race? He’s speaking to a white audience against African American’s and yet he speaks as though he is an African American! Once again I’m beginning to see that perhaps I’m fighting the dumbest man who has ever wrestled, much less lived. Hell, he turned on a light, which came from, well we’re not sure where it came from, but he turned it on in broad daylight. He claimed he was nervous. For once Makonie spoke the truth. He was very nervous, because he was hoping that the words on the paper were spelled how they sounded or he would get them wrong, and look stupid in front of all the voters. [Stephens wipes a tear off his cheek caused by the side splitting laughter he was holding in as he read the remainder of Makonie’s speech. There was no reason to call attention to other grammatical mistakes, he’d made his point. Instead he switched gears from the speech itself to Makonie’s upcoming Fusion match with Akiyama.] Ryan: I could not be happier with the current situation. Mark is all worried about me, and how he won’t be able to live with himself if he doesn’t beat me at Gold and Glory. In fact, he’s so focussed on me right now, that he is looking past his match with Akiyama thinking to himself that it’s an unimportant tune up match. In reality, that’s probably what it is, a tune up match; however, it’s a tune up match that will give Makonie another loss in the loss column bringing his impressive PCW record to 3-7-1, and yet he claims I’m the low-carder. [Stephens sighs, and he shuts the computer down. He’s due for a rumor, but the bed looks so inviting, with his gorgeous wife laying their anxiously awaiting his return. He stands up from the chair at the desk, still talking to himself as he makes his way over to the bed.] Ryan: Makonie is making a severe mistake if he looks past the Japanese Monster known as Akiyama. Akiyama is currently single handedly dismantling the tag division, and has a list of moves that could end the career of any man if they don’t adequately prepare to defend against them. Akiyama is the Neo Void God for crying out loud. If Makonie isn’t smart enough to prepare for a match like that, then he might as well prepare to be banished forever! [snickers to himself] Though I hope that doesn’t happen because then I wouldn’t get to prove everyone wrong and massacre the stupidest wrestler in PCW history at the Pay Per View! [Stephens lifts up the sheets and slides into bed with his wife. He moves over close to her and drapes his arms over her. She changes her position slightly to accommodate him, and then he pulls the covers over their heads so that we can’t see them. In frustration, the camera fades out.]
