History #3 Training

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/4/03 (January 2003) “Come on ten more” screamed a voice that echoed around the sports hall before it penetrated James’s eardrums. His arms were shaking violently as he stared up at the pair of knees in front of him “I can’t” cried out James as a small bead of sweat fell from his nose and landed on the heavily varnished floor. James’s arms finally buckled as he collapsed on the floor the side of his head connecting solidly with the floor. His chest began to rise and fall heavily, his lungs demanding oxygen as he was on the verge of passing out. A pair of shoes appeared in front of him and began to pace up and down. “You’re pathetic James, you know that. You were recommended to me by someone i had faith in, he said you had the fire in your belly. The drive and desire to be something in this business but you don’t. You are just another wannabe who will never amount to anything.” Said the owner of the feet as they began to walk away from the fallen body of James. “Why don’t you just piss off back to NASA, spaceman.” James looked up once more his eyes staring at the man in front of him. With every last ounce of strength in his body he pulled himself into a press up position once more, curling his fingers into fists supporting his entire body weight on them. Slowly he lowered himself touching the tip of his nose on the floor before straining to push himself up locking his arms straight before repeating the process another nine times before collapsing once more. “There’s your ten ok, you can insult me all you want but never insult my dream” gasped James as he struggled for air pulling himself up to his knees. James’s trainer took a few steps forward in front of the kneeling figure of James and began to walk around the former NASA man his eyes glancing up and down him, judging him in everyway possible. “You know the rumour is spaceman that the only reason you are hear right now is because some woman was told that she had the job and you didn’t. You someone who all his life thought of himself as the dominant alpha male beaten by someone who doesn’t even posses the Y chromosome, how small does that make you feel James? Does it make you feel insignificant? Is that why you want to wrestle James to make you feel more like a man?” snarled the trainer as he began to walk away from James once more in disgust. James’s temples began to throb through anger and immense frustration. Pulling himself to his feet he began to stumble forward, his legs still weak from the training but his anger pushing him forwards. He reached his trainer and in a burst of pure emotion span him around and clasped a hand tightly around his neck squeezing tightly. “That person without the Y chromosome was my sister you bitch. You can leave her the hell alone ok. Never ever say anything bad about her again or I will pop your little head right off your neck, do you understand me?” screamed James which resulted in small amounts of spittle landing on the trainers face. The trainer stared directly into James’s eyes a fire raging between both of them. A sly smile formed on his mouth as he raised his knee viciously into the midsection of James immediately causing him to release his grip around the trainer’s neck. James’s face turned red as he became flustered and moved his hands to cradle his ribs, which felt as though they were piercing through his skin. As the pain proceeded to move through his body James dropped to his knees gasping for breath, his lungs felt as though they would explode at any minute. “You’ve got the fire, I’ll admit that but judging by the state that you are in now I am guessing that you have never been hit before, that’s fine I can teach that, teach you to ride punches maybe even block a few of them. Your hunger though that can’t be taught and I like It.” Spoke the trainer his voice had now changed to a much friendlier tone and to some extent light hearted showing just how unstable the trainer was. “I don’t know a thing about wrestling, are you sure you can get me wrestling in time for the show?” asked James, the air slowly beginning to pass through his body once more, rejuvenating his tired and worn muscles” The trainer leaned down and helped James to his feet who staggered forward slightly clasping tightly onto James’s arm. “ I dunno son when is the first show” asked the trainer looking inquisitively at James who looked at the trainer before dropping his head despondently “May” said James staring at the floor. Without another word the trainer let go of James who fell forward flat on his face. The trainer left the room shaking his head and mumbling expletives leaving James simply with one word on his lips. “What?”
