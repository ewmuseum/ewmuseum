The Hell that Is Boda-Land - Part One - The Workers Sing a Song of Mass Mutiny

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd April 2002 Quick OOC Note: For this PPV series…I’ve decided that from now on, each will have at least one reference to a Matt Good song…:-D. The song for this roleplay is ‘The Workers Sing a Song of Mass Production’, from MGB’s Audio of Being Album. I tell you, I really fucking recommend this Indie Band. Get hooked, blame Dustin Downey…not me! :-D. Now, for an idea encompassing the song ‘Everything is Automatic’…GOD THAT FUCKING RULES! The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals…… RP#3 for Spring Fling… Location: The rather cramped staff area of Boda-Land, in the depths of Chris Chase’s mind, currently in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Time: Unknown, seemingly a late afternoon on April 3rd 2002. Chapter Six: The Hell that is Boda-Land – Part One – The Workers Sing (Well, Discuss) a Song of Mass Mutiny… “~(Radio Fizz)~ Chase…Chris Chase, are you there? Hello? Is this thing on? TALK TO ME DAMNIT! Ok, I’ll just take a few deep breaths, and try to be caught on the surveillance cameras…jeez, this thing is as obvious as a frickin’ brick! Oh…it is a brick, who in god’s name could have placed that there? Grr…if I catch the moron who replaced my brick for my radio, I’ll smack the retard around the back of the head so hard, his ears will be ringing for a week!” “Did someone mention the word…idiot? Why, I’m an idiot, a stupid moronic creature with no real sense of reality. Heck, I’d be a former PCW2 Cruiserweight Champion had it be the fact that Chris Chase never truly existed. Now, I’m just a lowly janitor…” “What are you mumbling about Dustin? Can’t you see that I’m trying to find my one method of contact with someone with the skills to take down our poor paying, slave driving boss? Why, he’s handed him his ass on numerous occasions in the real world, so why can’t he do the same in his own dreams? Chris Chase is a bonefide talented individual, a knowledgable sod for someone of twenty-three years of age…and besides, what in the Flaming pits of Wisconsin are you doing here? Didn’t you get canned a week ago?” “Meh…I didn’t get ‘Canned’…I got “Canned”, there’s a freaking difference, you know. Instead of being fired, that ass spelunker in the boss’ chair and his lackey began throwing cans of Tuna at me. What are their names again?” “~(Grunts Frustratedly)~ You know that you were told these on your very first day, yeah? Sheesh Downey, can you write things down for once? These guys running the park and the World Title Corporation…are Chris Sloboda, and his lackey…Miyagawawawawawa-whatever his name is…” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ I’ve been here for days now, trapped in the eternal cyclic motion of a recurring dream…one of my absence…throwing me into a world ruled by nimrods and self-righteous idiots who can’t make a good proper insult to save their worthless selves. A world ruled by Boda and his eternal lackey of a rival…Miyagawa. God, god if you created this world…then you must have really screwed up, or spilled something to cause this abomination. The only salvation that this world has is its employees, those slaves to the system that work long hours at a low pay rate…the likes of Hot Dog Stall vendor Chris Andrews and Janitorial assistant ‘Dipshit’ Dustin Downey…hard at work, plotting against the fall of the tyrannical rule that Boda has over the world. With the help…of yours truly. Boda and Miyagawa control the World Title division…and it’s Chris Chase’s job to break that cycle before it pulls us down the drain… You know, you cretinous fans of Professional wrestling…Spring is finally upon us. It’s a time for new beginnings, it’s a time for fat children and Dustin Downey to stuff themselves full with chocolate that the ‘Easter Bunny’ gave them. Ficticious notions, like that of Boda actually being a half-decent professional wrestler…and Miyagawa actually being half-entertaining. Why do you think that Boda placed a bid in for the PCW company? Those who can’t wrestle take charge of the company…why on earth did you think that Tyler Nelson bought out the PCW in the first place? After all, he couldn’t beat Jeremy Howard for crying out loud. And these half-assed wrestlers are sending the federation into a spiraling state of affairs; Boda, Nelson…heck, even Duke Williams. Only the likes of Chris Chase can save the federation from taking lower ratings…Chris Chase equals buy rates, which equals a bold and bright future for the PCW. The future is bright…the future is in the able hands of ‘The Messiah’… The Staff area of Boda-Land…a miniscule area for which the staff of the park, the slaves of the park, can relax for little more than ten minutes at a time…per day. Coats are strewn all over the benches, benches are missing legs…there is a general stench of neglect coming from the room. Security cameras bear down upon the two men chatting to each other in the room, one holding a brick in his hand for some unknown reason, and the other wearing a Chicken suit and a large purple hat. Both of which look not unlike the likes of a dynamic duo formerly known as High Impact back when the equivalent of the latter was employed by the PCW. Yeah…that’s right…Chris Andrews (Chase) and Dustin Downey…aforementioned workers in this desolate, hellish place…two of the many thousands of workers, heading the plot against the tyrannical leaders that drive them to the brink of their sanity by making them labour for long days and nights…constantly working, constantly having to put on a good face for the public (Of which there is very little). Andrews frowns as he glares at a propaganda poster put up by Boda’s minions… ‘The Family’….:: Dustin: Ah…so that’s what their names were! Thanks for telling me…err…who are you again? ::. Andrews slaps Dustin sharply around the back of the head, sending the head part of the Chicken suit flying across the room and down the laundry shoot. Dropping to his knees in anguish, Dustin lets out a slow, painful scream at the loss of part of his suit…whilst Chris simply slaps himself in the forehead in pretty much the same way that I do….:: Chris (Andrews): Andrews…Chris Andrews, you frickin’ MORON! For my sake, I could use your moronic notions as much as I need a damn hernia. Speaking about stuff that I need, have you by chance seen a small radio unit? About so big…answers to the name of “The Radio Unit”…come on, you must have seen it somewhere…or else we can’t get into contact with our operative…Chris Chase, you know, the guy that could have prevented this all if only he had truly existed… Dustin: But you’re… ::. Andrews nods, with a know-it-all smirk on his face, surveying the room for any prying workers that could otherwise alert the man in charge…Boda-Boy, into their shenanigans, thoroughly preventing the possibility of a probable mass mutiny. Gun shots can be heard in the distance, shouts and screams of anguish as yet another lone man falls to the bullet of the Boda-Miyagawa Armed Coalition after an unsuccessful protest for fair wages. Andrews continues….:: Chris (Andrews): Meh…I’m only a character that’s familiar to the viewing audience. Heck, why go to all the trouble of creating a whole new character for the audience to eventually accept, when you’ve got a character that’s already a national phenomenon. The same goes for you, my moronic co-worker. Besides, for this mutiny to function…we need a born leader…we need the equivalent of Chris Chase! Dustin: But isn’t he supposed to be some bossy, arrogant and over-confident twerp who just happens to be overwhelmingly talented and an excellent candidate for PCW World Champion. Oh…I see your point…if we can get someone capable of outwitting Boda…then we can win! ::. Andrews nods….:: Chris (Andrews): Yes Dustin…and being the eternal genius that I am, I concocted the brilliant idea of having the real Chris Chase here, at least in his own dream form…we can produce a literal army of Chris Chase’s…each being to the exact specifications of the perfect wrestler! The project of the Millennium! C-C-S!! ::. Dustin cocks his head, scratching it with a rather befuddled and questioning look on his face. After all…I’m hardly the perfect wrestler, no matter how big my ego may be. Heck, I’m close to absolute perfection, but I lack some of the strength of the bigger guys, I lack some of the speed of the pure Cruiserweights…and I lack some of the lunatic tendencies of hardcore pros and raving lunatic wrestlers. A well-balanced masterpiece, poetically moving piece of wrestling machinery. Cocky and confident, willing to take on the biggest of challenges…notably the capture of the PCW World Title, the key to Boda’s power…and the key to halting Miya’s advances. I mean, let’s face it…the PCW World Heavyweight Title, no matter what Sebastian Toomes has to say about it, is the most prestigious title in the industry… It’s been held by notable legends such as Doug Troy, the Reverend, Frisco, Meanstreak, Alan Kriegman and future legends of the calibur of Jeremy ‘Krayzie’ Howard and Jason Snow. It’s had its fair share of failures…Steward Hix, Miyagawa and Boda come to mind…but we all know that story. Fact is, the PCW World Title combined with the the youthful exuberance of ‘The Heterosexual Messiah’ Chris Chase…is the key to unlocking the greatest mystery of all: What does the future hold? As usual…Dustin Downey is on hand to accompany Andrew’s earlier notion with the usual moronic comments and witticisms that he is famed for. A shadow appears at the doorway, and eventually gets larger as the two argue….:: Dustin: C-C-S? Crippled Crab Soufflé? Chris (Andrews): No… Dustin: Canadian Camel Syndrome? ::. Having the patience of a stubborn mule…Andrews immediately puts a halt to the proceedings, somehow sensing the moronic tendencies of Dustin Downey…knowing that it could take all day for him to finally work out the full meaning of the abbreviation. He sighs, going to smack Dustin with a fierce backhand, but halting for some unknown reason. Yet another shot, and more screams ring out in the distance…followed by the fizz of electricity being pumped into rowdy crowds of people…complaining due to queue times. The screams go on for long this time, until the electricity stops flowing. The light bulbs flicker…and Andrews sighs once again….:: Chris (Andrews): Are you a complete idiot? Sheesh, can’t you figure out anything for yourself? Meh…forgot I ever mentioned those, you’d only ramble about how much you are an idiot. C-C-S stands for Chris Chase Simulation…the project designed by yours truly to regenerate co-workers into people worthy of the stature of Chris Chase…great fighter and born leader. I am the first of the Chris Chase simulatees…genetically identical to Chase in every conceivable way. After all…this is his dream, don’t you know? Dustin: But if this is Chase’s dream, why doesn’t he just send a nuke gift wrapped to Boda and his lackey, whatever his name is? ::. Andrews shrugs as the shadow draws nearer…outlining a female shape as it makes its way down the stairs from the surface….:: Chris (Andrews): I don’t know…why don’t you go ask him that, huh? Perhaps he isn’t as ambitious as I first suspected…after all, I’m working as a Hot Dog Vendor for crying out loud! Unless, this is all to do with the whole issue of Boda holding him back…the whole idea that Boda’s been there to keep Chris from making massive leaps in performance…and that Boda could never quite get the job done properly…right? ::. Yeah…that’s right. The self-sufficient bastard has held me back ever since he stepped into the PCW2. Moving down from the pedigree of the PCW to the lower PCW2, he was placed in my position…the position that I practically deserved. Thankfully, after seeing off Shawn Johnson…I was thrown into the spotlight deservedly by the Reverend and the story continues and so on. Fact is, I’ve always had to pause to shove the nimrod out of the way for a year now, popping up at regular intervals…keeping me from reaching my ultimate potential. Why, it was probably he who gave the notion to Ivan Stanislav to interfere in my match against Hans Kruger…leaving me for dead and for the loss. And it was probably Boda who conspired to introduce Dustin as my new enemy…avoiding facing me, getting a cheap win. Fact is, apart from that tainted win…he’s never beaten me…and I’ve pretty much ruled over him in terms of performances ever since we first met. I mean, I no-sold his move for crying out loud! So…after six hours of passing…the Workers are singing a song of Mass Mutiny…Chris Andrews, the Hot Dog Vendor…and Dustin…the chicken suit wearing Janitorial Staffer…preaching to their co-workers about their plans for a better and brighter future…free from the clutches of Boda and Miyagawa. But, as they stand on a soap box pedestal…in an area that’s had the CCTV cameras disabled…just who is that woman accompanying the pair? Does Boda-Land have a strip joint? Well…yes, it does. Why? Don’t ask me…I just imagined the whole scenario…you freaking dolts! A stripper with a PhD stands beside the duo…by the name of Serenity. A loud, obnoxious, highly intelligent dead ringer for Salma Hayek in every sense…she likes to say the word ‘Schmuck’. Of course…where am I in all of this madness? MEANWHILE Still in the outer circle of the park, crawling through a patch of long grass…I survey the next target and objective to carry out…the demolition of the secondary arsenal of Boda…that largely guards the outer circle. The robots…not big hulking beasts of the calibur of Metal Gear…but more so along the lines of the lumbering likes of the Daleks. Everything is Automatic? Well…that certainly doesn’t make everybody alright now…does it? TO BE CONTINUED…FADE TO WHITE!!.::
