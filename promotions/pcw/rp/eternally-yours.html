Eternally Yours.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered “Hi-Keeba!” Crash! The tinkle of glass as a black-robed ninja spirals out of a third floor building like a bullet shot from a gun. The limp body falls and lands with a sickening thud directly in front of the toe of trusted manager Sue Saksnussum’s feet. Her dark brown hair tied back in a tight bun off matching her dazzlingly-yellow business suit, she takes a leisurely drag at her cigarette before aiming a vicious kick into the sack of broken bones before her. Reveling in the crunch her sensible footwear has caused, she looks around to see if anyone in the on looking crowd noticed her cruelty. They are too busy looking at the flames. And the crashed helicopter on the roof. And the ninja crawling on the ledges. And the brief and occasional glimpses of our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, in the windows, who we now rejoin. “I doubt that was really necessary.” Sue gags on her smoke, swinging around wildly to find the deep, rich voice taunting her. Completing a full turn, her eyes dart this way and that, a malevolent look in her eye. A seemingly permanent grin etched into his lantern jaw, a bulging man in a bright silver outfit floats down from the sky, his royal blue cape fluttering in the breeze like some majestic bird. His red boots and under-over-wear creating a great look for the hero with the blonde, flowing locks. A giant ‘E’ emblazoned on his chest in a lightning script indicates that The Eternal has arrived on the scene! The crowd ‘oohs’ and ‘ahhs’ his magnificent entrance. Sue flicks her almost gone cig at him, snapping her fingers at disgust for missing his eye. “So, the great and all-powerful Eternal has finally shown up,” Sue says ‘The Eternal’ like it was another ‘Police Academy’ movie, “What, another book signing keep you from doing REAL crime fighting?” “I WAS going to take care of this, but your boy was already on the helicopter,” The Eternal mumbles, Sue shoots him a scornful sideways look. “Were you waiting for an Act of Congress? You coulda had this one a while ago…” The Eternal bristles at the implication. “You see, us REAL superheroes have to follow certain guidelines…” “The Masochist IS registered as a superhero, in a very real and legally binding sense.” Sue points over to a heap of a body, which, if pressed, could be identified as the Masochist. “Uh,” The Eternal searches his brain for a witty retort, but fails, “Yeah, well, the others wanted me to talk to you anyways about your glory-hogging, especially since Supes is pissed that you always carry Kryptonite in your pocket, and Green Lantern hates the fact that you always wear yellow out to ward him off.” Sue grins evilly, “They shouldn’t be so sensitive. Wusses.” “Besides,” continues The Eternal, “I just figured Steele had too much going on what with his wrestling career and all. He’s poised for the championship, isn’t he?” Another ‘Hi-Keeba!’ and a shower of glass as yet another Oriental assassin tastes concrete at about 80 MPH. Sue and The Eternal sidestep the falling carcass with deftness before Sue replies. “Yup. The big match is Friday against… ugh… Snow. He screwed us out of our rightful shot Sunday, but we’ll make things right.” The Eternal folds his arms across his chest and rubs his ample jaw line with a gloved hand, “Hmmm. Good for you guys. You know, a lot of us at the Hall of Justice are pulling for him to win.” Sue gets a startled look, turning to The Eternal fully. “WHAT?!?! Are you high or something? I thought you guys HATE Big Al cuz he’s not union.” The Eternal sniffs in derision, “Of course we hate him! But… should he get the PCW championship, he’ll probably have less time for his famous ‘training sessions’ out here…” “Ah! I see!” Sue muses, “Jackals to the end. The more time involved in PCW, the less time cleaning up the streets and making you CHUMPS look bad in the process. Always working the angles, eh Eternal?” The Eternal grins and makes a plaintive gesture, “So sue me for looking out after my job. Things are tough since Big Al has hit the town. But I do have to say, a lot of us at the Hall are pretty impressed with the job you’ve done with him. Managing that oaf must be a mental minefield.” Sue rolls her eyes, indicating the Eternal just has no idea. She reaches into her purse and flicks a cigarette out of her pack, flipping it into the air and catching it in her mouth like some sort of nicotine daredevil. She whips out a Zippo and starts flicking. “Thanks, and you aren’t kidding. That brain of his has been scrambled by more falls and chairshots than any Grand Slam Breakfast at Denny’s. But I love him like a brother. He’s basically a good guy and he trusts me implicitly. You know, you just can’t BUY his kind of loyalty…” Sue growls as her normally reliable Zippo fails to flame. The Eternal raises an eyebrow in surprise. “Brother? You mean…” “Mean what? Wait… you thought…” Sue giggles at the thought, “You thought me and Big Al were involved? Heaven forbid! First of all , he’s not my type. I prefer some storage in the attic. Secondly, he married to his oaths upon high, so even if I had wanted to, and I don’t, he wouldn’t.” A wry smile begins to touch The Eternal’s mouth. “So then why are you here? Dedication?” “Well, sort of,” Sue replies, still flicking her Zippo, “Just being supportive. Also, the Big Guy has a hard time with the press, so I’m here to divert them, PR-wise. Damn this lighter!!” “Allow me.” Before Sue can say anything, two thin red beams shoot from The Eternal’s eyes, expertly lighting the end of the cigarette. Sue smiles and takes a puff, allowing the carcinogenic plumes to ease her nicotine habit. “Nice trick,” Sue smarms, “Heat vision, huh? Not bad…” The Eternal moves in a bit closer, his muscles rippling through the shining material. “Yeah, but all of us at the Hall are really impressed with your work with Al.” “All of them?” “Well,” The Eternal silently chuckles while looking Sue up and down appraisingly, “At least I am.” Up above, more glass rains down and another screaming ninja plummets to the ground, crumpling into a formless heap a mere inches from Sue and The Eternal. But the two scarcely notice. Sue rubs her polished nails against her blazer, before holding them out in front of her. “Well, I have a knack of turning weaklings into men of Steele,” accompanied by a smoldering look at The Eternal. He runs his tongue along the inside of his cheek. “I’ll bet you make them solid in all the right places, now dontcha?” Sue smiles. “You bet I do. So… are you really eternal?” “I’ll last until the very end, MISS Saksnussum,” The Eternal boasts, “that I guarantee. I am a superhero, after all.” “I think I’d like to find out just how ‘super’ you are.” Sue runs an appreciative eye over the spandexed hero. “I’m super in ALL the right places, baby.” Sue raises a finger to her lips. “Perhaps you’d… like to show me?” WHOOSH!! A silver and yellow streak flashes across the San Jose skyline, just as Big Al emerges, grinning and sooty, from the burning building, carrying out two limp ninja by the throats. Seeing the bohunkular Greek emerge, the Police and Fire Departments fly by and rush into the building to save any unfortunates inside. Steele raises the ninja triumphantly and let’s loose with a bellowing “Hi-Keeba!” The press rush him, and begin yelling at him. “Mr. Steele! Mr. Steele!!” Big Al smiles and holds up his hands, dropping the unconscious forms to the ground. “My friends, you know the drill as well as do I… all questions shall be answered by my trusted manager…” Big Al makes a sweeping gesture towards where Sue USED to be. His jaw drops at the empty space, and sweat begins to bead on his forehead as the press moves in for the kill… “Mr. Steele! Why do you constantly throw yourself into such dangerous situations?” “Uh…” BAS sweats, “I do it for my fellow man and, of course, the children - the little ones.” “Your fellow man? Does that mean you are gay?” “Little ones? Does that mean midgets? Are you homosexually attracted to midgets?” “What?” Steele’s head starts to spin. “No! I just mean that… er, the gods demand that I…” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele as he storms into Stately Steele Manor, an EXTRA edition of the San Jose Chronicle clutched tightly in his fist. Fierce eyes dart across the foyer as he slams the door in disgust, the force tearing it off the hinges. Graces squeal in fear and make beelines for safe havens. Big Alan Steele rears back in anger and roars… “SUE!!!” Seething, Big Al stomps up the stairs and turns down the hallway… and nearly runs over his trusted manager, as she sits on the floor, leaning against her door, smoking a cigarette, and wrapped only in a bed sheet. Her face is one of… satisfaction. She slowly turns to her charge, a lazy smile on her face. “By the gods, read what you have wrought!” Steele growls as he tosses the handful of newspapers at Sue, “Without your guidance, the local scribes have once again perpetrated against me crimes of indecent passions!” Sue slowly rustles through the papers, finding the front page: EXTRA!! EXTRA!! Big Alan Steele Confirms it: “I’m GAY!”“I love midgets best,” gushes superhero.Gay community rallies around the ‘Rainbow Warrior.’ Steele shakes his fist angrily at the lounging Sue. “You failed miserably in your attempt to ward of that pack of bloodthirsty…” he pauses, noticing his words not having any effect. “What is this? Have you nothing to say? Are you even listening? What manner of sin must a man commit in a single lifetime to render him susceptible to the ills of a impotent manager…” Sue giggles at impotent. “Well, our little friend wasn’t so impotent.” Big Al raises an eyebrow, not accustomed to this unnatural side of Sue. Normally, she’s cruel and despotic. Ruthlessly efficient. Directing Big Al around with lead-pipe cruelty. But seeing her so relaxed, her face flush with pleasure… Steele’s mind can’t even BEGIN to understand it! Sue sucks down a cloud of smoke, “Relax, Al. I’ll spin it in the morning, no worries.” “By Zeus, wait but a minute!” Steele’s eyes go wide. “What ‘friend?’ By what do you mean?” “Why, The Eternal! He’s in there.” “Really!” Big Al beams, “Excellent! Tis been far too long since me and The Eternal have exchanged pleasantries! And imagine the convenience of his usual appearance in yon bedroom! What fortune!” Sue smile goes ear-to-ear. “Yeah, imagine the fortune! Wait, Al! Don’t go in there!” But Steele has already burst through the door and has entered, hand outstretched to shake hands. There on a sheet-less bed lies The Eternal, wearing only what God afforded him, eyes wide, a smile etched into his face… Permanently. Big Al, unfazed, goes to the edge of the bed and puts his outstretched hand over The Eternal. “Greetings, fellow demi-god!” says Big Al, “Tis a pleasure to again make your acquaintance! I have followed your deeds since a mere child, and I find you to be one of my… favorite… and most noble… of…” Big Al trails off as the expression on The Eternal fails to change. Steele follows his unseeing gaze, hoping to find what the man is staring at. Two large burn holes are in the ceiling, so Big Al also begins to stare at them. Sue saunters into the room, the sheet flowing behind her like Cinderella’s dress. She looks at Al staring, and finally a small scornful frown manages to pierce her pleasant features. “Al,” says Sue, “What are you looking at?” “Shhh! Lord of All Eternity and I are keeping an eye on this strange growth on the roof…” “Al, those are burn marks.” Sue sighs, “He made those when he got excited. TOO excited.” BAS blinks, not understanding. Sue again sighs a deep, heavy sigh, coming from the depths of her being. “Big Al, he’s dead.” “Nonsense!” scoffs Big Al. “Look how he looks!” The corpse just stares at the ceiling. Big Al again slowly follows his unseeing gaze… “Snap out of it Al! He died. Trust me!” Steele is flabbergasted. “BY THE GODS!! Tis but a serious quandary!! How can he die?! He’s The ETERNAL!!” “I guess he couldn’t hang with me,” Sue says with no small amount of pride. Big Al’s head darts around, as he searches the room. He finds a blanket and tosses it over the body. Steele scowls at Sue, shaking his head in disbelief. “What shall we do?” A nervous Steele stutters, “You have murdered a great warrior, speeding him along to the Elysian Fields before his time! You shall go down in history as the woman who killed the Eternal! The gods have been angered! We must atone and repent! REPENT!!” Steele pokes at the bundle under the blanket. He lifts it for but a second, as a rank odor assaults him. “Ewwww! All stiff and stale!! By the gods, woman, you’ve sucked the life right out of him!!” “Honey,” purrs Sue, “You have NO idea…” “SILENCE you lascivious witch! What are we going to do?!” Sue thinks for a minute, before answering. “Let me get dressed. I know what to do.” Scene change. Big Alan Steele face against a dark backdrop, his somber tones cutting through the silence. “My friends, we come here tonight to recognize a great and powerful hero…” “No time,” pants Sue, “We gotta get this done before the cops come.” Sue grunts as she pushes an long object, about 6 feet 4 inches, wrapped in a blanket off the Golden Gate Bridge. It plummets to the water with a splash, sinking into the murky depths. Steele frowns. “Hardly the way for a true hero to end his days.” “Good thing he’s superhuman, or that fall would’ve folded him up like a card table. But now listen,” Sue snarls, “We’ve already gone through this… is it really anyone’s fault what happened?” Steele pauses before slowly answering, “No.” “And,” Sue continues, “is it wrong for us to merely preserve the integrity of a great hero?” Another long pause from Steele. “No, but I suppose I just don’t see how forcing The Eternal to the briny deep preserves his memory…” “Now at least people won’t know how he died as he did, in the saddle. He had a wife and kids, I hear. Besides, now some unscrupulous upper-villain can take the credit and he goes out like a hero, like he should.” “I guess so, but… that’s the third this year!! You are like a praying mantis!! Succubus!! BLACK WIDOW!” Sue stops him. “No buts, and keep your voice down! Now, put on you ski masks and let’s get outta here. I think I hear sirens…” Big Al sighs dejectedly before pulling his ski mask into place. “Hi-Keeba.” Both get into a black car, Sue tapping the hood as she enters. “Wheelman! Move it!”
