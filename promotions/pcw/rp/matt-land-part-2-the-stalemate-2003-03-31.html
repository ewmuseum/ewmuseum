Matt Land, Part 2- The Stalemate

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/31/03 On the third floor of Boston’s Ritz-Carlton, there was a tapping on the hotel door. Inside, an immensely tall man, whose well-tailored pin-strip suit made him appear even larger than his nearly six foot five inches of height, came rushing out of the bedroom. His bald head, fringed by perfectly groomed gray hair above his temples, was like the skull of some anointed lord of some royal court, where kings, princesses and princes, sought out his wisdom, no doubt delivered with the eyes of eagles and the voice of a prophet. Although his rushing figure revealed a vulnerable anxiety, even this did not diminish his image of dominance. He was important and powerful and he knew it. All this was in contrast to the younger woman he admitted through the door. There was little that distinguished the young guest she is short and rather small compared to the enormous monstrosity of the man that stands in the doorway. She has a look like she has been defeated on a great battlefield and is just a fallen warrior looking for some place to go. “Come in quickly,” the man barks, staring at his visitor who’s casual wear of jeans and a ripped tee shirt is probably ill fitted for the situation and it has seen better days. The man’s face is one of stone and hides his worry and thoughts well. He has never encountered a situation like this and he isn’t sure how to proceed with it. “Did you come with everything I asked for?” “Oh yes, indeed,” his gray-faced visitor answers, adjusting her sandy reddish brown hair. “You look well Mr. Andrew Clarkson,” she continues in a thin voice while studying her host and glancing around the opulent suite. It was something out of the ordinary for her to be in a place as marvelous as the suite is and she is in total awe. “They took her,” screams Andrew Clarkson of British Intelligence, a master of finding his way around anything and a very high-ranking official. He has done some of the most high profile mission in Britain and other countries in England, and this has earned him the respect of many people in Parliament and his fellow colleges. It has also earned him a lot of money, which he isn’t afraid to flaunt around. “They took who? The young lady responds, hinting that she already knows the answer to her own question. She figures the person that he is talking about is none other then Sarah Clarkson, his daughter and her best friend, even though they hadn’t always gotten along they were still best friends and the words made the fallen warrior sick at her stomach. “Sarah, some brass from the American Government grabbed her I know it,” he starts, his voice wrought with anger. “Those sons of bitches have been trying to get through red tape and get information on me for months and they failed. So instead of fighting the legal system for it, they took her and I have no clue where she is now.” “How can you be sure it was Americans?” She asks, her jade eyes scanning the room, taking notice of the open bottle of Vodka. In all her time of knowing Andrew, he had never been one to drink. The situation was getting to him and it had only been going on for a few hours. He was losing is edge, but the young lady had seen it coming. Everyone loses something when they start to get older and Clarkson was losing his ability as an intelligence officer, but he was still a huge psychical presence. “I am sure and that is why I called you. I need your help because there is no one else I can trust. You know Sarah better than anyone else and I know you know how important this is,” he tells her in a fury, the balding agent stared at the gray-faced young lady in front of him. The years of stress and beatings took their affect on her and made her look much older then she really is. “I would love to help you Andrew because Sarah is my best friend, even though we have had some ruff patches, we are still a great team, but you along with your power hungry friends were the ones that tossed me out of British Intelligence. That still leaves a sour taste in my mouth and I need those resources to find her any ways and you took them away from me. You might as well of killed your own daughter when you did that because we all knew this day would come!” “You little bitch, you are just going to let her be torched and killed? You are really going to allow that to happen, after I took you into my home and practically raised you as my own?” Andrew yells at her, raising a hand like he is going to hit her and then quickly drawing it back, noticing what he was about to do to the young lady he considered a daughter. “I am sorry, this whole situation just has me going out of my mind. I want her back so badly and I know you can do it. Anything you need, I can get you permission from British Intelligence to use the facilities, files and anything you else need. I will pay you anything you want, I just want her back by any means necessary.” The young lady stands there, shaking the jade bracelets down her arm and then puts them back into place, only allowing them to fall once more. She wants to help him get Sarah back, but it isn’t going to be easy, she has been out of the game for a long time and even with the few free lance missions she did as of late. She was letting her own age catch up with her and as much as she wanted to say that it wasn’t. None of that matters to her now though, because she is the only one who can get Sarah back unharmed. Just by agreeing to come here she had made it her place to agree to save the young girl, who had done so much for her. They had always come from different worlds, Sarah a wealthy princess, who had everything by all accounts. Claire a poor “trailer trash” whore, who had nothing and had to get by on stealing and even, served jail time for it. “I will help you get her back…” *** “Wake up bitch,” Matt yells, as he struts into the interrogation room located in the CIA’s warehouse somewhere on the docks of Washington, DC. His slender face is masked by a cocky smile and his brown eyes are cold and dark, as he affixes them on is objective- Sarah Clarkson. The young daughter of the British Intelligence officer that was being held against her will. She has been locked up in this small room for a few hours now and it didn’t look like she was going to be released anytime soon. Matt quietly stands staring at the wreck that is Sarah Clarkson, her long black hair has been pushed back so many times that it won’t stay back any longer and her hazel eyes are blood-shot from the sleep deprivation that she has been facing. Every time it seems like she might be able to get an ounce of sleep her opponent Matt Jackson appears to knock her out of it in some way or another. The human chess game has come to a stalemate and both players are staring at each other. Waiting for the other to make their move, but the deadlock is in place and time might, as well be standing still. The ex-Ranger has been here before; this is nothing new to him. He knows that it is just a matter of days until Clarkson, crumbles at his hands. He can tell that the events of the past few hours are starting to wear on her and she is breaking. He will either get the information he so desires, or she will just go out of her mind. He doesn’t care which one comes first because she is nothing, but a commodity to him. He will find out the information some other way if she can’t provide it. He runs his hands through his brown hair and slams his hands down on the table, making Sarah almost jump out of her seat and she probably would have except for the fact that she is tied to it. “How are we doing this morning?” Matt calmly asks her, snickering at the humor he applied to his question. He is doing great this morning, a lot better then he was, when he made the original grab on Sarah. The only difference is that his white dress shirt has lost the fresh pressed look it had and his black dress pants are a tad wrinkled, but not a major change to his look and it still strikes fear into the young lady in front of him. Sarah remains silent, trying to stare down the former leader of the Venom project. The place where he got his start and got his title of assassin. She didn’t know these facts; in fact she still didn’t even know whom the man was that was holding her captive. All she knew that he is a skilled professional and there is no way out of the cross hairs that he has placed her in. His eyes grow smaller and he continues to focus them on her, like a hawk that has just found a mouse and is ready to swoop down and take it’s meal. He stays calm, even though she is trying to defy him and get under his skin. It is the only way she might be able to stay alive. She needs to convince him that she has information that he needs and maybe then he would allow her to stay alive until someone comes and saves her. “You know you are starting to get on my nerves Lac…” He quickly notices his mistake and stops himself from calling her Lacuna Debris, one of his opponents on Turmoil tomorrow. He knew that it was going to present a problem, but he figures that he was just good enough to avoid it. He was wrong; wrestling is slowly starting to invade his thoughts and actions. He is bringing his hatred of PCW into his other work and it pisses him off. “I am going to make this real cut and dry. You can tell me what I want to know and be freed with no mention of what happened here, or you can be the strong silent bitch that holds out the information. It is no skin off my back either way because I am leaving in a few short hours and I am going to get some sleep. You on the other hand are going to be forced to wake up every hour on the hour and questioned until you finally do break.” She knows that he is telling the truth and she envies him for teasing her with sleep. She will force herself to hold out for a few more days. She is going to do everything in her power to make sure that he has a hard time breaking her and at least with him gone for a few hours, she might be able to relax somewhat and regain her thoughts and get everything in order. The next few days are going to be important and she knows it, but she isn’t going to fall into checkmate so easily. “Well, then I will see you soon. Just remember bitch, there isn’t anyway you are going to beat me. I am the best in this business,” Matt hisses at her, turning on his heels and swaying out of the room, only to slam the metal door shut. Leaving Sarah Clarkson alone with herself, scared and alone in the dark room. You think you can beat me? Highly unlikely, I am the best, I am Matt Jackson
