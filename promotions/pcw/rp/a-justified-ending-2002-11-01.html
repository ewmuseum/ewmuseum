A justified ending

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-11-02 Evil Vs Evil Gangster Vs Mobster it's a war that can only end in bloodshed. This has become Bob's life. He has never felt so empty or alone without Rosanna. He can't even begin to believe what she must be feeling right now. Rosanna and Bob have been put through hell during the last week all because of one mans greed. That man is none other than Ron Jackson the leader of the new wave of black men moving to Amber Hill. 'I have never felt like this about anyone before.' Bob can remember saying this to his mother as she smiled at him in pure happiness. Bob has never been truly happy and she thought this would be his big break. No, everything good in Bob's life doesn't last for long before it is shattered. Karma? Maybe if it is true or maybe it's just pure bad luck. 'When I first saw her everything else stopped all I could see was her.' Bob remembers telling Tony this when he asked about her. Tony has always been pushing for Bob to meet a girl and have children so there would be a younger Vurdo to hand the family over to. These memories only make it more painful for Bob as he lays on his bed staring at the ceiling of his bedroom. Tears are gently rolling down his cheek as he thinks about the girl he loves Rosanna. He is interrupted by his cell phones ringing. Bob reaches into the pocket of his pants and answers 'Hello.' 'Hello Bob its Tony.' Bob has wanted to apologize for what happened a few days ago but hasn't had the chance to yet. 'Look I'm sorry about the other day its just...' 'I understand but I have good news for you. We have found the place where Ron Jackson lives.' 'Where?' Bob can't wait to find out so he can go over there and get his woman back and get his revenge. 'Twenty Five Falcon Grove. You must wait for us to back you...' Bob hangs up the phone and gets out of his bed. He walks over to the rack where his coat hangs and he takes it off before putting it on. He opens up the wardrobe and splits his shirts in the middle of the rack. He then opens up a little compartment revealing two handguns and a knife which Bob places in his coat in various places. ------------------------------***------------------------------- Bob Vurdo's black Mercedes pulls up in front of a suburban house on a quiet street during the still night. There is not a cloud in the sky which is well lit up by the full moon. Bob pulls two guns out of his jacket and walks to the door of the house of Ron Jackson. Bob closes his eyes and takes a few deep breaths in before bracing himself for the life or death situation. With one powerful kick Bob sends the door off its hinges and takes a few steps inside the small vacant kitchen which leads off to the dining room. Bob knows he must be quick as he wasn't quiet with the knocking down of the door and Ron could have the time to arm himself with a weapon. Bob moves through the kitchen and opens up another door which leads into a small corridor. Bob opens up the first door to his left which is a closet filled with towels and bed sheets. Bob moves to the next door on the right and opens it up before pointing the gun inside the empty bathroom. Bob walks up to the last door on the corridor and without thinking twice opens it up revealing Ron Jackson who is standing about the strapped down Rosanna. Ron looks up at Bob in surprise before running at him. Ron jumps at Bob and tackles him to the ground where Bob loses control of the guns as they scatter off down the corridor. Ron hits Bob twice in the jaw opening up his lip but Bob manages to overpower him and roll him over to get onto. Bob then delivers a hard forearm to the jaw of Ron Jackson then another and another. Ron is now just semi conscious and his face is a bloody mess. Bob pulls a knife out from his jacket and looks down at the man who put him through so much pain. The man who put the woman he loves through so much torture. Bob isn't going to just kill him instantly Ron Jackson's death will be a slow and painful one. Bob knows the human body very well and were to strike a certain type of blow. Bob then thrusts the knife right into Ron Jackson's lungs and almost instantly Ron starts coughing up blood. Bob then gets up and turns to Rosanna. He can't believe what he is seeing she has bruises all over her face and her two front teeth sit right next to her head. Bob moves over to Rosanna who is crying in joy to see Bob. She can now see the end of the torture that has ruined her life. Bob unties her from the bed and she sits up almost immediately making the sheets fall from her half naked body which is badly bruised in all of the areas. Rosanna can no longer speak she has been through too much to talk about it all right now. Bob unties her fully before breaking down in tears at the sight of his girlfriend. You can tell Ron Jackson has put her through a lot just by looking at her upper body. Rosanna gets up from the bed wearing just a pair of underpants which are wet and smell like urine. Rosanna stumbles a bit as she hasn't walked for a few days and Bob helps her by putting her arm over his shoulder. Rosanna takes her arm off Bob's shoulder and without saying a word walks into the hallway where she sees Ron Jackson gasping for air as he looks up at Rosanna for the last time. Bob is to overwhelmed to do anything he just walks a few paces behind the love of his life who has just been put through so much. Rosanna bends down and takes the knife our of Ron Jackson's lungs before stabbing him viscously four times in the same area. She bursts out in tears and Bob goes to comfort her with a hug. Rosanna would have faced a death like Ron Jackson's rather than going through what she has gone through. Rosanna can't face the world any longer and for one last time she removes the knife from Ron Jackson's heart. Rosanna takes one last look at Bob before lowering her head and sending the knife into her throat killing herself instantly. Bob is mortified he falls back from the crouching position and begins crying against the wall. Bob curls himself up in a little ball where he will cry until Tony arrives to take him home. The damage has been done to the mental health of Bob Vurdo. He is now extremely dangerous and full of rage. Anyone who stands in his way will become another person who he can take his anger out on. You can only feel sorry for men like Flaymin Aymin and The End as they will have to face the emotionally controlled Bob Vurdo in the coming week.
