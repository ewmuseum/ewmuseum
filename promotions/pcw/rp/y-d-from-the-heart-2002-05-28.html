Y.D: From The Heart

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th May 2002 The story of Charlie Loc began it’s path in a Market Place somewhere in the city of Saigon, Vietnam. Blood was spilt and laws were broken as he defied his exile and returned to his home town in search of something to do after his recent firing in PCW...Not only did Charlie bring himself back to his home with his belongings but he also bought with him a strange feeling that something wasn’t right. Perhaps it was just coming from America and the fact that he was just fired from his job that hung over his head like a dark cloud, or maybe it was something bigger than that. Could Charlie survive without Wrestling? Was he going to return to his 'old ways'? Why did his return to Saigon feel wrong?Only time could tell it’s tale as the day turned into night and Charlie became 'acquainted' once more with his old friends, and even enemies….. Young and Dangerous Pt II: From The Heart Bass could be felt in the ground of the cold, city streets of Saigon as night spread it’s blanket across the sky and signaled for the fun to begin. The black sheet that was darkness was like a wake up call…calling all freaks, Gangsters, prostitutes and rich people to come out and enjoy the many thrills that the night life itself held. If the city was thought to be alive during the day, it was nothing compared to what it was at night… It was early into the night, but late enough for the city to unleash it’s weapons as Sleazy Strip, Dance and Karaoke clubs opened their doors all down the streets, attracting everyone from the young teenagers trying to be 'cool' to the top business men being escorted by 'lovely' Vietnamese Hookers or seen escorting their colleagues to a good time. The night was itself was like a creature…a creature that somehow managed to lure everything that couldn’t been seen during the day and then make it come it during it’s prime and hunt it down and take them over! Although it was young the night began to quickly shift itself into attack mode as people started to crowd onto the streets, lining up to get into the Underground clubs or having a cigarette or meal at a shady restaurant overlooking the streets. It was like a scene out of a movie or a novel. Everything beautiful, young, grotesque or strange filled the street with life and made the atmosphere buzz as though something really special was going to happen, as though the night wasn’t a normal night! Friday nights are usually the nights that everyone goes out and parties on…everyone throws off the ties that they wore to work or take out the hair ties that were holding their hair in place so that they may look presentable and just let go of themselves! Even the biggest 'Geek' so to speak has fun on a Friday night somehow or some way. But for some reason this Friday night just seemed different from the rest…it was as though it had that special 'Saturday Night' type feel only it was indeed a Friday night…nothing else could explain why the night felt so special…or so wrong! Brightly colored neon lights shone out like beacons in the street, flashing their logo’s or their pictures to advertise their club and attract people. All along the sidewalk of the street were queues upon queues of people lining up to get into their favorite hot spots. Maybe it was the bright, hot pink pair of breasts that lit up that attracted so many people to the underground club which was simply known as 'La Singh' or maybe it was just what people felt like at the time, but whatever it was 'La Singh' had a very strange feeling about it…. The line up for La Singh was gigantic, bigger than any other club on the street! People from all walks of life were standing in line talking or smoking, just waiting for the two huge, mean looking bouncers near the double doors of the club to open the little velvet rope and let them through! Some of those people must have been waiting there for hours, they didn’t seem to really care though! From the looks on some of the faces, the business men who were standing around laughing with huge grins on their round Asian faces had been drunk for a fair while now, just enjoying the time they had away from their work…. Others like the group of four small looking girls wearing skimpy school girl outfits looked like they had been standing in line since they had finished school no less than a few hours ago! They had tired expressions on their faces but from the looks of the people who exited the club, it would be all worth their while! The Bass continued to pulsate into the concrete as a high pitched Asian girl’s voice could be heard singing, muffled from the sidewalk. One of the big bouncers nodded his head to a group of about 20 people standing near the front of the line and clicked off the velvet rope as it was like a toilet had just been flushed. The line moved forward like a surge of electricity as everyone pushed and shoved their way through the two doors which had been opened to make room for them as strobe lights flooded onto the street and the singing became clear as inside the club silhouettes of people dancing could be seen. BOOM~! The door slammed shut as one of the bouncers clicked the velvet rope back onto the pole and the line stopped moving forward again prompting a few cries from some annoyed clubber’s like 'Du Ma!' and 'Dil Lay Lo Mo'. The bouncers just grinned toothy Asian smiles and stood with their hands folded not paying attention to them…. Suddenly out of nowhere three familiar looking figures appeared walking on the sidewalk with chains dangling around their neck. They could be recognized as none other than Charlie Loc and his two 'Leing Sei' that had helped him through the market place before, all three of which were dressed to impress! It seemed that the three men were out to do business as they would usually do on a Friday night! Charlie with his hands dug into the pockets of a flowing black trench coat with baggy black micro fibre pants and his long fringes and pony tail flowing in the cool breeze. The first teenager with the same baggy pants and a white top and the second teenager with a huge puffy red jacket and baggy gray jeans all walking along with their heads hung low. As they walked along side the line the Asian girls all stopped to stare, some pointing and giggling at the trio others licking their lips and hoping that they may meet up with them later on into the night. Charlie seemed to keep his ice cold expression on as the other two teenagers beside him smiled and nodded their heads in acknowledgment, causing them to giggle and blush even more. The music continued to pump and drone onto the sidewalk as the trio finally made their way to the front of the club. The teenager wearing the jacket reached inside and produced a thin piece of material with a card attached to the end of it, faintly reading 'La Singh VIP' in Asian writing. One of bouncers looked the three over before nodding his head and motioning behind him as the other bouncer opened the huge doors letting the lights flood onto the street and the music become loud and clear once more. People at the front of the line began to push and get rowdy as the bouncer folded his arms across his chest and grinned malevolently as the other closed the doors behind the three men as they entered the seedy underground club….La Singh! 'OVER HERE DAI LO!' One of the teenagers yelled at the top of his voice as his friend and Charlie walked off into the crowd of people dancing and watching the DJ and Strippers do their thing on the stage and podiums further off to the back of the club. The other teenager slightly pulls Charlie’s coat as the two walk off to the stairs over the other side of the club, Charlie not taking his eyes off of the strippers as he moves off, bobbing his head to the sound of the smooth Asian beats that the DJ is pumping. Together all three of them clank up the metal stairs as the group of four school girls before come down from the top, squeezing past them on the way down. The two teenagers move up to the top as Charlie is left slightly behind them as one of the school girls pinches his Asian ass causing him to turn around with a cheeky grin on his face as the girls all lick their lips and look him up and down as he shakes his head and follows his friends. The beats continue to vibrate the floor as everything becomes a little softer as they enter the 'lounge and bar' area of the club. Lining the sides of the walls are comfortable looking red sofa’s which change color when the strobe lights flash over them with tables and cigarette trays in the middle, giving off a full 'Gangster' sort of feeling to the club…. Ahead of the three teenagers is a bar that is shining like a beacon! Each and every alcoholic drink available looks as thought it’s stocked behind the bench of the bar as young girls and business men sit on the stools ordering the bar-tender around while having a cigarette and a good time! One of the teenagers leans over and whispers something into the others ear as he goes to the bar to order some drinks as the other takes Charlie off to the side…the sinking feeling that something bad is going to happen suddenly re-emerges! The teenager pushes a barely visible door open as himself and Charlie walk into a room where it looks as though just about every Gangster in Saigon is sitting! Drinking, smoking, playing pool or just chilling out! The teenager and Charlie make their way up a small set of stairs their feet clunking on the wood as the sounds of the club fade out into the distance! It’s like their own executive box! A large window overlooking the rest of the club below, a set of strippers doing their own thing and everything else in the one room! The teenager with Charlie whispers something into his ear as Charlie nods his head, causing everyone in the room to look up at him…. Everything in the room stops! The hard looking guys playing pool hold up, the guy sitting on the couches drinking put their cups down and other just sit with cigarettes hanging out their mouth staring intensely at Charlie! Each and every set of eyes in the room is focused on him….. Charlie looks around, his usual ice cold expression covering his face as he stares back at everyone whos looking at him, the only sound in the room is the music pumping from the club below! Suddenly one of the hardest, darkest guys sitting down gets up with a cigarette in his mouth. His fringes coming down to his chin and his goatee dangling in the air, his baggy FUBU clothing dangling off of him along with the jewelry. He looks no older than 18 but everyone looks as he confronts Charlie….. 'You’ve decided to come back Loc…what you want here? You think we want you back?' The man takes the cigarette out of his mouth and hold it in the same hand as he’s holding a glass of liquor. His rough voice sounding out in the room. Charlie just stares back as the man takes a sip of his drink, smiles at him and moves forward as he talks. 'Let me get this straight…you leave to live with White Boys…you desert us when we need you in a fight and you expect everything to be ok?' Once again Charlie just stares at him. The man shakes his head as a pissed off look crosses over his face and the feeling that something bad is about to happen rises! The Asian is about to speak again as Charlie cuts him off speaking his own words of wisdom… 'I not do this deliberately…I come to make apology Sapiet' The words leave Charlie’s mouth as the tension for a fight about to break out rises in the room as everyone looks towards the pissed off guy known as Sapiet who is standing up to Charlie. 'I not come here to cause trouble, I come back because I know this where I belong! I know I messed up….but that in the past! You need me….' Charlie speaks again as Sapiet shakes his head. 'We need you? What you think this is Charlie? You the one who got us in trouble! You the reason why the Khmer came after us!' Sapiet almost shouts at the top of his lungs at Charlie who takes his hands out of his pocket and scratches the side of his face. Sapiet looks behind him and motions for Charlie to come join him on the sofa. The game of pool continues and everyone goes back to what they were doing as Sapiet and Charlie sit next to each other. Sapiet pulls a cigarette out of his pocket and places it into Charlie’s mouth before placing his drink down and lighting it. The music continues to blare from the club below as Charlie takes a drag from the cigarette Sapiet just lit him as the two begin to have a heart to heart conversation. 'I see what you do in the states Charlie, I hear what you did with Young and Dangerous over there and I know that you have what it takes! But what you not getting is this….you betrayed us and right now there are people on the street who would kill you if they knew you were here!' Sapiet shakes his head and takes a draw from his own cigarette. 'But Charlie, I never be one to fight you! We grow up together, we made NG! Not your brother, not your cousin, you and me! But you turn your back on us Charlie, you cause us trouble with the Khmer and you run….what that tell us?' Sapiet exhales the smoke and shakes his head. 'That why I come back….I learnt a thing or two in the states Piet! I no fool to fighting anymore, I can sort out everything with the Khmer once and for all!' Charlie exhales the smoke and then sits silently peering out the window of the club as the sound of the pool ball cracking another can be heard in the room. 'It good to hear Charlie, but how you plan on doing that? You nothing Charlie, you even admit it yourself!' Sapiet chuckles sadistically to himself as he drops some ash in the cigarette tray on the table before taking a sip of his drink. Charlie still looking expressionless shakes his head and stands up before reaching into his trench coat and producing a rather large, shining machete and placing it on the table. He looks down at Sapiet as everyone in the room turns to focus their attention on Charlie and the now wide eyed Sapiet. 'I sort it out! They nothing….' Charlie speaks almost silently. Sapiet shakes his head in disbelief before taking another sip of his drink and putting his arm around Charlie and making an announcement. 'Everyone, this Dai Lo here saying he going to get rid of the Khmer problem….I want to welcome him back to NG!' Everyone in the room’s faces change into a sly grin as Sapiet pats Charlie on the back and everything continues. The music of the club below continues to pump out the beats as the party goe’rs below have no idea what’s going on. The bad feeling turns somewhat worse as Charlie makes a deal to re-unite himself with his old crew of NG, 'New Generation' as the night wares on, on the cold windy streets of Saigon, Vietnam outside. The night has begun yet another tale as Charlie Loc promises to eradicate a problem he left everyone with before. For some reason the night is going to seem to go for one hell of a long time, the Vietnamese Viper has indeed officially been let lose on Saigon! To Be Continued....
