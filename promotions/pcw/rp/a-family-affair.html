A Family Affair

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Reedited 4-5-02 Song listening guide: 'Hands Clean' by Alanis Morrisette BOBBY STORMBRINGER A Family Affair (NTICW MKM PPV Aimz/B.S. SWERVE~!) The scene switches to the constantly busy main entrance of the Magic Kingdom. Hordes of people still stream through the turnstiles, both coming and going. The shot focuses in on the only person that isn't moving. A man that is standing some ten or fifteen yards in front of the ticket counters and staring straight ahead towards the entrance, as if he is expecting someone. The camera view zooms in to the face of the unidentified figure to show that the person is PCW's Bobby Stormbringer. The commentators break into the scene to try to clear things up. Diamond: Wait a minute! What is this guy doing around NTICW again? Last time we saw him he was jobbing to Politically Incorrect and the key to the city of Des Moines, Iowa! Ha! Milloy: I know the whole story. Before his first match in PCW, he was attacked and set up for a drunk driving charge and they gave him a concussion. Diamond: Poor baby jobber. Milloy: Shut up, I'm not finished. After a while he found out that his brother was one of the three guys that ambushed him and that there were two others. His bro told him that he would give him a name if he showed up at the Pay-Per-View tonight. I guess Big Daddy Rick gave him permission to stick around. Diamond: Damnit that's Mona's job, not his. Actually this guy isn't so bad come to think of it. He made Walden job out to a photo of Aimz! Who'll ever forget that!? Fountain: Hey, you're right. This jive turkey is cool. He'll be hostin' this basketball game fo' PTC in a couple weeks. Diamond: What's so cool about that? Fountain: Because I will be there lending my expertise to the situation. Diamond: Exper-what?? Fountain: Frolicious. Diamond: Ahh... I get it. The Showstopper wears a pair of long denim shorts, T-Mac sneakers with tube socks, a black Asylum t-shirt, an Orlando Magic cap that's on backwards, and his trademark. Lightning-streaked Oakley shades. After a few moments, Mr. Prime Time moves off to the side and out of the way of most people. B.S. raises his arm to look at his watch. Just as he does, someone bumps into his back hard with their shoulder. Stormbringer winces and spins around to lash out at this tourist. Stormbringer: Ow, hey! Why don't you watch where you're.. going.. Bobby's voice fades off as he finds himself standing there in front of someone he's wantd to meet for a good long while now. Aimz. Milloy: What in the world is she doing out there!? She has a match to get ready for! Diamond: Don't worry Milloy, that trash just wants a timeout for a piece of B.S. just like your mom always wants a timeout for a piece of me- There are the faint sounds of headphones being dropped and punches being thrown. Stormbringer: W-what are you doing out here..? The moment becomes very awkward, mostly because B.S. seems increasingly more and more nervous and uncomfortable. The harmless crush is very evident. Amy Campbell seems confused for a couple of seconds herself but after a few seconds Aimz smiles up at him and seems to even recognize him. She doesn't answer his question, however, at least not yet. Aimz: Say... you're that Bobby Stormbringer, am I right? That 'B.S.' guy? B.S.'s eyes grow wide for just a few seconds. He seems surprised that she even knows who he is. Aimz' Canadian accent is as noticeable as ever. Stormbringer: Yeah. Uh, we only talked a couple times before, but through e-mails. Aimz smirks a nasty smirk, but at the same time a harmless one. Stormbringer assumes from a look like that that she is about to test him. Aimz: I heard around that you're a big fan..? Stormbringer: Heh. Oh.. of yours? Umm... yeah, something like that. But we can chit chat later. Don't you have a match with Mona coming up soon that you should be getting ready for? Why are you over here? Aimz: Someone told me they needed to see me. It's nice of you to worry but I'll be fine. Doesn't take much 'preparation' to rip a bitch skank's head off, you know? Stormbringer: Yow. I hear ya. But... who needed- Aimz: Big Daddy Rick. Don't ask me why. Stormbringer: That's strange. He told you this himself? The smallish redhead shakes her head gently. Aimz: An intern.. one from his office? Stormbringer: Did he at least tell you this in person?? Aimz: Nnnooo.. sort of on the phone? You know how it is, we don't exactly have the time to fly to the NTICW offices. Aimz gives Stormbringer a pleasant smile, one that seems to make him at least partially relax about the two of them meeting for the first time. But B.S. is also getting more worried by the second. Stormbringer: I hate to tell you this. But to use a term you're probably familiar with? I think you've been hosed. Randy: Sure. But she's not the only one. The sound of what might as well be B.S.'s very own voice coming from right behind him understandably makes B.S. jump. Bobby takes a couple steps back towards the entrance and he glares at the person he's been waiting on. Which simply means he was lurking in the Magic Kingdom all along. Aimz looks at Bobby, then towards Randy Stormbringer. Then she peers back at Bobby again. Aimz: I'm guessing you guys are twins? Stormbringer: Hmph. Technically speaking, yeah. This is a reasonable conclusion because the only difference between the two is that Randy's blazing red hair is cropped short while B.S. has let his grow long and wild. Both are clean-shaven, both are fairly attractive. Randy is dressed in a business suit and carries a silver briefcase in his hand while two huge bodyguards or cronies of some sort also stand close by in business suits. Apparently this is 'insurance' against a B.S. attack. Stormbringer: You stupid punk. They aren't using rings here! How was I supposed to stand in one and wait for you? Randy: How was I to know? Like I care. Do not worry, you have not failed my test, brother. Stormbringer: Enough. Tell me who else attacked me, now! Randy: Life is not that simple and you know that. Now pipe down. Stormbringer: Did you set Aimz up to be here..? Why did you? Randy: She came here of her own accord. You want to cut to the chase? Fine. Let us cut to the chase then. Randy flips open the briefcase in one fluid motion and quickly produces two manilla folders. Then he hands one to Bobby and one to Aimz. He shuts the briefcase with a loud click. Randy: In there you will see all of the information you want inside. Actually it is not what you want but it is what you need. Both folders are the same, but I figured you would want your own copies. Aimz opens her folder and looks through it blankly while B.S. skims through it quickly, looking for a name of one of his attackers. In doing so he happens upon one important page and reads that one more throughly. After a few seconds he gasps. Aimz: What? What is it? Stormbringer: I.. what the.. this has to be a forgery! A fake! Randy: Ha ha. I assure you not. You have a keen eye for these sort of things, brother, you will confirm soon that this is most definitely not a joke. Aimz: If it's a joke can I be let in on it? Randy: Like I said it is by no means a joke. But certainly my dear. You deserve to know. B.S. looks on in a state of shock, flipping through a few papers that only serve to erase his doubts even further. Forgeries of this exact an age and nature are virtually impossible. Randy steps up to Aimz and takes her hand before kissing the back of it lightly. Randy: Welcome to the family, young one. Aimz: What!? Randy: It appears our dear old drunk of a mother took the time to sleep around with your father on one of her many.. 'excursions' into Canada. Business trips. Please! I found these documents hidden in the attic of our family home. I have had them checked and rechecked. Thoroughly. Our father isn't dead after all. Our father is in fact your father, Miss Campbell. This would explain why our names were changed. Probably wasn't even going to be Sheridan, either. You are my one half-sister. You are Bobby's one half-sister. So can we expect you for Thanksgiving, then? B.S. slips himself in between Aimz and Randy, facing Randy with a snarl. Stormbringer: That's why you brought me here, and her too?? What's your game you freak!? Randy: My game is what you claim yours is. The truth. Heh, I must admit that you have done well. So I will tell you this. One of the two men? Aimz tries to sort all of this through her mind while B.S. literally swallows his anger. Stormbringer: ...Yeah? Randy: One of the two men is NOT Drake Maxwell. Good evening brother. Yes and good evening to you as well... sister. Before B.S. can even react the two bodyguards are suddenly right beside Randy. Bobby and Amy have to step aside as the other three men almost walk right through them and head for the exits. Randy raises a hand and he wiggles his fingers in a half-hearted wave goodbye. B.S. calls out to his demented sibling out of desperation. Stormbringer: You said you'd give me a name! I need to know who tried to take me out, damn you!! Randy does not respond as he and his cronies disappear under a wave of moving people. Bobby and Amy both look at the ground for a moment as their minds race. B.S. must be thinking about his deceased parents and his estranged brother and how he has just been led to someone that is real, tangible family. So Bobby's REAL father was alive, and in reality, the father that Amy had grown up with... that is, if these papers were true. The proverbial prick Bobby knew as 'Dad' was dead, but it's just another prick... that's not his dad? Damn... Slowly the two blazing redheads look up at each other and stare at one another, closed folders in their hands. The moment has become more awkward than any other they could of shared before. The shot changes back to that of a shocked and stunned NTICW commentator's table. Milloy: So what do you have to say about that, Diamond? Diamond: I say if ya can't keep it in your pants... keep it in the family! THIS HAS BEEN A B.O.T.S. RE-PRODUCTION. (c) 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.
