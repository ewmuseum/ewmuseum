P.W.A - The Guest For The Holy Grail? (Part 1)

7 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07 - 11 - 02 A dark, cold night serves as our backdrop. Set in the English equivalent of Las Vegas Nevada, namely London we bare witness to what looks like a caper involving three masked individuals. Above the smog-infested streets below, we capture the criminals plotting the heist; away from prying eyes they discuss their roles. 'This had better be worth it!' groaned a mountain of a person towards his two associates. The three unknowns were clad in black spandex cat suits, faces hidden beneath ninja-esque masks, concealing their identities yet each one distinctive from the next. There was a colossal figure with broad shoulders and a petite individual with a feminine physique standing barely over five foot, taking commands however from another person of average build and a distinctive voice. 'Believe me, this will change society as we know it!' retorted the individual in his authoritive role as he looked across from his rooftop, survielling the opposite building, which was a large sports complex. The two other assailants made last minute equipment checks, rummaging through fabricated bags they join their colleague’s side and look directly upon the buildings skyline. The small lady-like figure stepped forward brandishing a cross-bow and fired the sharp instrument into the arena's concrete structure, creating a tight-rope effect, securing the line by imbedding a grappling hook their side of the passageway. 'So far, so good-....' murmured the on looking leader-like individual. The two henchmen strapped the deadweight of the bags around their shoulders and descended down the zip-line on castors with specialised equipment that fastened around their waists, similar to that of a rock climber. Without so much of a hiccup the caper was running smoothly as the two safely made it across, followed closely by the boss character, straddling the three individuals to an overlapping ledge. Obstructed by that of a large glass-panel they appear to have run into problems. It looks as though the window will slide to one-side and open, yet it homes a security feature that requires two metallic contacts that must remain in contact of one-another at all times, obviously unless the alarm was inactive. 'WHAT THE F-....' hissed the behemoth with a snarl. Looking defeat in the face, the pert figured character handed over what appeared to be the foil wrapping of a stick of gum. Sliding the window and gum wrapping into place as a contact the window screeched open, sizeable enough for a full-grown man to fit through. Dropping into the corridor below, the three individuals entered the belly of the beast and began removing their masks with a dose of suspense. 'Arrrrrr-......' huffed one removing the restraint, unveiling the Conniving Comic of Primetime Championship Wrestling Gaz Green. Watching on the trio of Pranks With Attitude were showcased to the world. Gaz Green taking centre stage alongside the brooding vampire known as Nascosta Ireul Nerezza and their recent acquisition of Russian beauty Natalie Miyagawa. As we know it isn't common for such high-profiled celebrities to enter into organised crime, unless referring to that of Jason Snow or possibly the mounting list of rule breaking offenders like John Leslie, Gary Glitter and Winona Ryder. With breaking and entry added to the devious duo's list of accolades, this begs the question, 'What has P.W.A got installed for those involved with Friday Night Fusion's Main Event?' 'So what the hell are we doing here?' asked the Living Nightmare, holding his pose of intimidation. 'Patience is a virtue squire!!!' replied the jovial Jester, continuing this episodes on-going suspense. Gaz Green pressed his body mass against the wall, ushering himself along in the direction of the locker-room area out back. Masquerading as your typical jewel thief, Gaz's associates join his side as the three members of Pranks With Attitude strife sideways into the bowels of the building. With stealth like tactics they venture under the surveilling camera's that stare down at the corridor from their fixed positions, held feet above the flooring, yet mastermind Gaz 'The Jester' Green has foreseen these minor restrictions and knows how to maneuver past. Pressing through a set of double doors they stumble into the backstage. Browsing over the nametags it appears that the building under corruption is the arena staging Friday Night Fusion, obviously enough, those who will be set upon by the founder of Prankology may very well be apart of the title bout scheduled for the Main Event. Passing the doors marked with Eddie Mandrake, Clyde and Mark Amulet the trio stare down the door, which will home the dizzy diva Ellie and more importantly company CEO and opponent Xavier Kannon. 'You’re putting your jobs on the line just to get one up on Xavier Kannon?' muttered team valet Natalie Miyagawa, swaying her head in disapproval. 'Kannon? Oh-... We have bigger fish to fry!' interrupted the Lord Of Laughter with a sinister smile that doesn't de-throne the reluctance of his manager/ agent. 'Why on earth would you waste your time with Ellie?!?' continued the former wife and valet of Alexander Miyagawa. Simply shrugging off the question of his team mate, Gaz Green began tampering with the locking mechanism of the door, struggling to open the entrance with two metallic rods, when suddenly the frame caved in under the huge boot of Nerezza. Looking up with a star struck expression the trio set hotfooted into the locker-room of Hubbard’s first couple Xavier Kannon and wife Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Snooping about, rummaging through company and personal belongings alike Gaz held up what appeared to be colour-coded lingerie. Nerezza simply fingered through various documents and Natalie played the watchman, keeping lookout when in a triumphant pose the Jester held up a strange looking device that could've made a cameo appearance in 'Men In Black'. 'Hmm-... What's that Gaz?' questioned the inquisitive vampire, his canine teeth in full view of the cameras. 'This be an Ebonics-Eradicator~!' answered the PCW comedian, trying to rally impressive facial expressions, failing however as his colleague's offered nothing short of confused glares. 'Sorry for being a little slow on the uptake but - WHAT IN THE NAME OF CHRISTOPHER LEE IS AN EBONIC ERADICATOR?!?' bellowed the satanic goliath, drool washing the underside of his gaping mouth, eyes flamed over in rage and looking every part the thirst driven vampire. The Jester's mouth dropped ajar as his face could manage to muster that of a terrified return, Natalie remains incoherent as she remained loyal to her task by keeping a watchful eye on any neighbouring activity. 'Err-... Arr-... Well-... Hmm-... It's a-...' stuttered the quivering P.W.A member, his words trembling simultaneously with his lip, holding a striking resemblance to Thunderbirds Parker. 'It's a what?' asked the Angel Of Fear once more. 'It's a Ebonics-Eradicator!!!' proclaimed the Velvet Trickster, his answer holding no stroke with fellow Pranks With Attitude member Nascosta Ireul Nerezza who wrapped his talons around the throat of his associate, slowly tightening his grasp with each passing second. Is this the end of Pranks With Attitude? Is this the end of Gaz Green's life? Is there such thing as an Ebonics-Eradicator and if so can we all donate a little something to buy Statuz Quo one? - To be continued -
