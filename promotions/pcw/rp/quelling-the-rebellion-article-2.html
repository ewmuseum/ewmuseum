Quelling the Rebellion.....Article 2

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/02 A knock swiftly echoed from the door of Father Randall Craven, who sat perched over a stack of official documents with ink pen in hand. His head rose and eyes elevated to meet the stern figure of Bruce Shanahan, his guest’s eyes shadowed by sunglasses and his stocky form clad in typical street attire. What his eyes concealed, however, was evident in his forbidding facial expression. Nonetheless, Father Craven conveyed a welcoming smile and extended hand in the direction of his unexpected visitor. “Ah, Bruce!” exclaimed the merry pastor. “This is a pleasant surprise! I always welcome a distraction from this accursed paperwork….” “Can it, Randall,” interrupted VJ, declining the handshake and lowering himself into the plush velvet cushion facing the minister’s desk. Craven looked taken aback by the harsh attitude Jack put forth upon his entrance. His face flushed in heartfelt concern, and with a slanted nod of the head and furrow of the brow he sunk back into his seat. “What is the trouble, old friend?” asked the reverend in sincere inquiry. “If you need guidance, all you must do is request it. There’s no need to walk in and treat me like I’m your enemy…..” “I don’t need guidance,” interjected Jack in aggravation. “I need answers. I need to know what you’re still suppressin’ from me.” The tension was thick, and Craven had begun to fiddle nervously with his pen under the inert gaze of his long-time companion. “I…..I’m sorry, Bruce,” stammered Craven in discomfort, tapping the utensil on his desk in discomfort. “I can’t say I know what you’re talkin’ about....” “Don’t feed me that bullsh*t!” VJ replied as he cut off Craven. “You’ve been treatin’ this like a damned government project. Ya tryin’ to withhold something from me, like it’s top secret policy? What is it that’s so freakin’ vital to the Church you can’t tell me?! “I assure you, Bruce….I have no knowledge of what you’re saying. And in the same manner, I don’t know where you’re coming from with these allegations….” “You wanna know where the f*ck I’m coming from, Randall?!” erupts VJ, springing from his chair. “Try this on for size! I’ve scrounged around the minors for nearly a decade. I’ve my flesh shredded into tatters and been placed in IC units for burns and blood loss in the Far East more times than I care to recall. Then I paid my dues yet again when I returned to the States, campaigning in promotions I can’t call anything more than refuse bins! I finally get my shot in the big leagues, and coincidentally it happens to coincide with the time I begin to get bombarded with revelations that would make Jeffery f*ckin’ Dahmer look rational!” Jack’s mind is racing, his words equally as rapid. “Meanwhile, bear in mind, I got a pair of upstart, trendy rejects frolickin’ around the PC Dubbia’ locker room like they’re the next big thing and threatenin’ to spoil my debut! Do ya f*ckin’ hear me, Randall?! I’m in danger of having my debut ruined by two pieces of yuppie trash with all the sense of a rock and the wrestling ability of two junior high wrestlin’ team alternates! That may not mean the slightest to you but this is my life!! You have your God, your Christ, your Holy Spirit, your tabernacle. I have beatin’ people up! The wrestling ring is my sanctuary, that’s where I preach and I do it with my fists! And in case you haven’t given it much thought, it’s a bit difficult to focus in my line of ‘work’ when your thoughts are compacted with visions of demons and grisly sacrifice! I don’t intend on havin’ a pair of overprivileged chumps steal my thunder, but if I’m gonna get through this thing I need a bit of cooperation! You and your Philly cohort are still holdin’ something back, and I’ve been around the block enough times to recognize it! You of all people should realize that!” Bruce’s livid diatribe was neither unexpected nor unprovoked, but it unsettled Craven nonetheless. He shook his head fervently, casting his hands loosely into the air in a despondent show of bewilderment. “I don’t know what you want me to say, Bruce!” shouted Craven. “I’m trying to conceive exactly what you want me to say, but I’m drawing a blank! I already divulged you the theory on what is causing your anguish, beyond that I’m oblivious to what you’re aiming to get out of me!” Father Craven shuddered under the impact of Jack’s fist slamming down on the desk. He reclined back in his seat, eyes meeting the cold glare of his unstable comrade. “Your ‘superior’ dropped hints during a short phone conversation two days ago,” responded Jack, his tone having regained its normal and unwavering rigidity. “He said he was having some ‘negotiations’ involving me. Seein’ as how you are the one who has been givin’ the old man reports on me, I figured ya were in the loop.” Jack noticed Craven purse his lips, his eyes dart to the right as his grip tightened on the pen. With a forlorn sigh the minister heaved himself from the seat and walked in the direction his eyes had maneuvered, VJ still leaning over the desk with thick, brawny arms supporting his weight. His eyes remained unmoved, his senses audibly picking up the rustle of paper as his overburdened mind continued to race. His breathing was erratic, a common effect of his unbridled temper, and light perspiration had appeared on his brow.A packet of papers were shoved into his field of vision, warranting a raised eyebrow and inquisitive grunt from the Boston hoodlum as he slowly drew the papers from the grasp of his colleague. Craven’s eyes were turned away, facing the portrait of Christ adjacent to the doorway. “What the hell?.....” murmured Jack, flipping through the sheets. “Profiles?.....who are these guys?!” “I know very little of them,” replied Craven without hesitation. “Again, this is an issue Bishop McCarver would be better suited to brief you on. They will help you, I can guarantee that. And you can help them as well….” VJ cocked an eyebrow in the direction of Craven, withdrawing his shades to peruse the profiles. “Page one, Marcus Stryker,” he muttered, examining the first page before skipping to the next. “Hometown: Dayton, Ohio. Weight of 239 pounds, height of 6 foot 1, graduate of John Morris University. Page two, Jago Sensaki. Out of Osaka, Japan, blah blah….” Jack threw down the files on the desk following his last agitated remark, the impending rush of wind sending a pair of Craven’s own documents cascading onto the floor. “What am I lookin’ at?” demanded VJ. “Is there any f*ckin’ reason you got the personal profiles of four yuppies layin’ around your office? These guys priests?” “No, Bruce,” responded Craven, gesturing back toward the files. “Skim through their vital facts and tell me what they have in common.” VJ thumbed through the dossiers again, eyes searching for constants between the quartet profiled before him. After a handful of minutes, he plopped down the files once more and crossed his arms. “College grads, that’s about all I could distinguish.” “What else, Bruce?” pressed Craven, placing a thumb to his chin. “Different ethnic backgrounds, lived in different areas, different hobbies….for Pete’s sake I don’t know, Randall! What, they all got the same favorite color?! They genetic freakin’ experiments, what am I lookin’ for here?!” Craven’s left index finger came into view, pinpointing a detail classified directly beside their education levels. VJ squinted to identify it. “Um……it says this ‘Stryker’ is well-studied in the occult. That what ya were lookin’ for?” His guess was confirmed with an affirmative nod. VJ swiveled his head back to glance at the descending profiles, and the other trio each held ‘certification’ as occultists. He still failed to get the jist of Craven’s intention. “That’s awfully cute there, Randall. But what exactly does a band of trivial occultists have to do with your crew of renegade gods using me for their amusement?” Craven stifled a snicker as he trudged behind his desk and proceeded to yank a bulky wooden crate from beneath the footrest. He removed the lid and dug through its contents, emerging from the ensuing blizzard of showering white foam pellets with a leather-bound tome and a manuscript, with an array of newspaper clippings included in the hind cover of the latter. He casually lay them on the desk and glided them gently into his friend’s hand. Following an instant of momentary eye contact, Father Craven pivoted and turned his back to Jack. “I had these shipped to me from Bishop McCarver. The book you don’t need to study or anything; just skim it and you’ll get a notion of the true power of the forces at work here. As for the manuscript and clippings…..heheheh, well read through the clippings and compare them to the correlated accounts in the manuscript.” VJ ran his calloused hand over the jet cover of the book, passing over the title printed in raised golden lettering, Unaussprechlichen Kulten, the title of which turned his eyes the size of half-dollars. “It’s translated, don’t worry,” laughed Craven as he noticed the baffled expression and bulging eyes of his visitor. “Roughly translated it means ‘Nameless Cults’. Written by the explorer Friedrich Wilheim von Junzt decades ago….” “Don’t think I’m gonna become some sort of occult aficionado,” growled VJ, skimming through several opening passages. “But if ya think this’ll reclaim my sanity, I’ll give it a look….” As his eyes darted around the focused page, he registered the creaky rumble of Craven sliding his desk drawer open and the fumbling of yet more papers. His view was diverted to a new manuscript slid onto the German title. “Feery’s Notes on the Necronomicon,” informed Craven, striding across the room to gaze out the window into the bustling street. “My only copy….and one I need returned, as it is rather rare to find a competent interpretation of that particular work. Similar goes for Kulten.” “I still find it sorta hard to accept that all this trash is approved by the Church as a whole,” stated Jack. “You’re tellin’ me they have proof half of this crap is true? That’s absurd….” Randall rotated his head to look upon his cynical acquaintance. “What would be even more absurd is the chaos that would ensue if the Vatican released information about a veritable army of semi-omnipotent deities hibernating around the Earth just waiting to be released from aeon-old prisons. You ever consider that possibility, Bruce?” The thug’s eyes rolled to face Craven, who approached from the window with clasped hands at his waist. He inhaled deeply, casting a fleeting glance out the window at the wary passerby. “It’s better they remain ignorant in this situation…..sometimes in life, ignorance truly is the best option. Taking into account the impact the concept of war has on the majority of the populace, contrast that to the terror that would be invoked on mankind if they realized the horrors obscured beneath their very feet. Some slumbering inside crypts in the depths of the Pacific, other under the dunes of the Africa deserts or entombed in the peaks of the Scottish Highlands. The belief even exists that entire cities rest on the spines of gargantuan, mile-long beasts of cosmic ancestry….” “You’re not helping my case any here, Randall,” said VJ with a mocking undertone. “Here I thought ya were supposed to relieve my stress and now you’re addin’ to it. Miserable bastard….” With a hearty laugh Craven tossed his near arm over the shoulder of VJ. “Take those things hope tonight, keep ‘em a few days and scan through them. I’ll get back to you Tuesday or Wednesday, or stop by sooner if you need anything.” VJ scooped up the two manuscripts and placed them in the front cover of the leather-bound German volume, tucking them under his arm and replacing his shades. Following a simple handshake and exchange of farewells, Jack was out the door to leave Craven to ponder the dilemma at hand. * * * Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan April 5, 2002Entry 26 I feel like I’ve been reading the gibberish of a madman. I can find no other way to explain it. How the church condones the very idea of the existence of blasphemies of this nature is bizarre. Not so much that wretched German book, whatever the hell it’s called, but this manuscript on the Notes of the Necronomicon is simple lunacy. My good friend Randall was even so kind as to translate some major parts of this Sumerian babble into some simple English……even these damned ‘Notes of the Necronomicon’ are confusing. Here’s a couple examples of this madness I’m referring to, simplified:“Cthulhu is a large green being with the overall physical resemblance of a human but with the head of a squid, huge bat-wings, and long talons. Cthulhu rests in a tomb in the city of R'lyeh which sank beneath the Pacific Ocean aeons ago. In part the Great Cthulhu is dead, yet not truly dead, as he and his fellow inhabitants of R'lyeh sleep the millenia away in torpor. R'lyeh comes to the surface as random interval periods of time, and his dreams influence the minds of sensitive individuals across the globe to depict his image, slay, and found cults dedicated to him. R'lyeh sinks after a short time (or has done so in the past), but the day will soon come when it rises to the surface permanently and great Cthulhu strides across a world thrown into chaos and anarchy from his telepathic sendings.”How f*cked up is that?!I have yet to delve into this other manuscript with the newspaper clippings, and I have no clue what my old friend is getting at with this one. For now my thoughts are on this maniacal collection of notes. This sh*t is giving me the willies, I don’t mind admitting it. I may as well transcribe some of this here; someday maybe I’ll skim through here and it’ll back some memories. Well you got Ithaqua, which I gather to be some massive beats confined in the far north somewhere. Not much else on this one. Nyogtha is a weird one too, and though I don’t know much about it according to this book or these notes it’s called the Thing That Should Not Be that lives beneath the ground somewhere. Only recorded it because this f*cker used to be worshipped in Salem Massachusetts as some bringer of magic by witches. Azathoth.....this one is funny and a bit chilling at the same time if you actually believe most of this mumbo-jumbo could be real. Blind idiot god, but is credited by these cult freaks as having created All Matter. A bit looney for me, so we’ll move on.Ah, f*ck it. I’m not even going to bother. Aight, I’ll agree with good old Randall and that senile bishop and admit maybe I am having my mind prodded by some dark, telepathic beings. I’m open-minded enough to accept something even as outrageous as that. But a formless beast sleeping beneath the sea, waiting to essentially swallow up humanity? A blind insane god who created the entire f*cking universe?! I’ll go along with some mad cult who’s not a big fan of my wrestling ability and have some paranormal powers…..that’s even a bit hokey but I suppose I can even accept that much. But I draw the line at an entire f*cking army of demigods from outside our known universe waiting to claim Earth as their own little stomping ground. Good lord, in honesty I’d think a denomination with the faith in One Almighty God and a One True Messiah as these people wouldn’t be so worried about these ‘wannabe-gods’. Unless I misunderstood something of course, I don’t know……Well, just under 24 hours away….PCW here I come! Hopefully my dreams will leave me in tranquility tonight and I can get some decent rest. Then again, if I was a pair of overhyped c*cksuckers named Suburban Uprising, I’d pray I didn’t get a wink of rest tonight. Tomorrow night, I’m giving this fed a kick in the ass and lifting it out of this downward spiral it’s reportedly been in. I have yet to speak to Chris, but I trust I can count on him to watch my back. I have a feeling I may need my back watched; I imagine honor isn’t high on the priorities list of two sorry party-animals the likes of these clowns.Time to silence this uptown revolution.
