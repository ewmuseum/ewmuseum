An Insight to My Past

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-21-02 (OOC…This is a short biography roleplay that I made, trying to sum up all of my last roleplays. I wrote this to allow you to understand the events that my character has been through, and how she is coping with what she has become. My biography on my life story page will be longer, and more detailed, as I will have time in the future to expand it. Thanks!) *A small insight to my past…* Las Vegas, Nevada, June 17, 2000. Heaven rides shotgun of a red Mercedes Benz Convertible with white leather interior and no top. She sports a green halter top and jean shorts, as her sandy blonde hair sways in the breeze. Three other people are in the car. They are all laughing and joking loudly and there are a few empty beer bottles on the floorboard to indicate that they may have been drinking. They act as though they may be under the influence of alcohol, but their actions may very well be only a result of the excitement of being in Vegas. They ride along one of the busiest streets in town, 70 miles per hour, or so it seems. Heaven is having the time of her life, her hair blowing wildly out the window, her voice seeming to overpower the loud, chaos and liveliness of Las Vegas at 10:00 at night, as she sings out of tune, along with the radio. The yellow, orange, blue, purple, and red blinking, neon lights of the city, flash crazily, only they appear exaggerated to Heaven...maybe because of alcohol. The wheels of the Mercedes Benz squeal immensely on the black paved street. Glass breaks and shatters....Heaven hears screams...she feels her already nauseated self throw up uncontrollably...and then her head bounces off of the glove box instantly as the scene fades to black. Three hours later, Heaven wakes up in St. Johnston's Hospital. She has had a concussion and both of her legs have been seriously injured, maybe even broken. Other than that, Heaven is ok, and in much better condition than her three companions. Jason, her boyfriend of five years, and driver of the Mercedes, was instantly killed in the crash. Her two other friends are in bad shape as well. Sarah, a beautiful, young ambitious valedictorian, is lying unconscious in a coma. Mick, fun loving and handsome, strong and built was a quarterback and Heaven's life long next door neighbor is now in critical condition and will most likely be paralyzed from the waist down for the rest of his life. So much for their innocent dreams--so much for their futures. Of course Heaven has no knowledge of her friends' fate at this point. Heaven is only aware of being all alone in a dark, unfamiliar room. She doesn't remember the crash and is confused about her surroundings. She tries to sit up but instead she vomits all over herself as a result of a concussion and probably drinking as well. Then, she tries to get up to clean herself off, but as she does she makes the startling discovery that she cannot move or feel her legs. She becomes frightened and frantically tries with all her might to move her legs and get out of bed. Eventually, with lack of success, she becomes exhausted and passes out from the pain. Months down the road, while Heaven is in the middle of her physical training, she begins to seriously think. Once she's stronger, she decides she wants to do something for herself. Now that Jason is gone, she has no intentions of settling down and having a family. She was never really that enthused about college, either. Sarah was the intellectual...Sarah was the class valedictorian. Heaven has always been a fan of professional wrestling. She has watched wrestling on television. Before she met Jason, she dreamt of being a wrestler. But she fell in love and wanted to someday make a life with Jason. Now, there is nobody or nothing to stop her. She decides that is what she wants to devote her life to. She's going to work her ass off and live out her dream. The Angel’s words… “Her name is Heaven and she is three months old. She is a blessing from the lord. He has heard the prayers sent up from you and your husband. God has answered, my child, cry no more. I am watching over you. I saved your life today, and I gave you new life as well, this darling infant. Here... take her and raise her as your own, for she is but she was not conceived in a womb. Love this child, and do not tell her what she is until she is an adult and has already established a lifestyle. I do not want this knowledge of her origin to have an effect of the person she becomes. By the time she is grown and settled, having already developed a personality, she will be coming upon the years where her aging will be hindered, and she will need to be aware of why. She will not be shocked to find out the truth, for once it is mentioned to her, she will have reconciliation of the time before she was sent to earth even though she is a baby. It will all come back to her, believe me, for she is truly and angel and God works in mysterious ways. Go now, my deed is done, remember only this...When the day comes, tell her she is God's gift and it is his will that she help others. Her powers will come in time, tell her to fear not, just have faith. She will understand it by and by. Congratulations and good luck, though you won't need luck because I will be watching always, and you have this bundle of joy here that I place in your arms. She will bring your life content. No matter what hardships anybody else brings into your life, you will over look them and be bothered not because of this overwhelming joy.
