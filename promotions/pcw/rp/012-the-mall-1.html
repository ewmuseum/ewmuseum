012 - The Mall [1]

2002

Date: 10.12.02 'They didn't hear us the first time did they? We'd told them we'd do it and now they listen!' Coming into Red October, the thought of having new Tag Team champions suddenly was a known thing, not an 'if' as it were the week before. Gaz Green and Nerezaa have been through it all just to get those Tag Team titles, but then came Sunday. With Sunday came the super-event Red October. With that brought Playaz Inc. to the table for the first time in a tag team title match. The stage was set for the very moment the bell rang, and that for those fifteen minutes or more, the Playaz of PCW were free in their own zone. From bell to bell, it might have been seen that Playaz Inc. were on the ropes, to the untrained eye. You didn't focus on the parts where the allowance of maneuvers made the match seem more worth it than usual. That Playaz Inc. were only making sure that the fans got their money worth. Of course they did, as Duzza came on to 'root' for the home team as the team brought it home, back to the ghettos and slums of America and rubbed in the face of middle America. To us, Middle America consists of those who earn more than $75,000 and have a yearly income more than the black man. That doesn't go for black people only, but Native Americans, and even the mixed folk roaming the lands. They did it for every team that dared to show their asses and still continue to dominate the competition. In the heart of everyone, there lies a bit of Playaz Inc. Yeah right. Its quite all right though, as the scene slowly fades into that of a secluded mall in Tokyo, Japan, otherwise known as PURE country to many. Those busy roaming around the mall in search for items for a personal taste are sent off guard from the sirens and howling screams of people clamoring through the mall's main corridor, or walkway. 'MOVE BITCH, GET OUT THE WAY, GET OUT THE WAY BITCH, GET OUT THE WAY!' The theme song for moving out of a person's way blasts throughout the mall as little Chinese girls accompany a large float, shrieking in their voices the song, lisping the words. The large float happens to carry the words, PLAYAZ INC. streaming downward in bold lettering. The float happens to consist of a foam replica of Gaz Green being Genocidal Overdosed, by none other than Dante Jackson. The top header of the float reads out in lavish red color with black base, 'CHAMPIONS! BETTER THAN THE WHITE MAN, SUPERIOR THAN THA OTHER MAN!' Standing back, on one side of the float, with a microphone in hand happens to be the one and only Lyrical Messiah - Dante Jackson sporting a thunder light Japanese head wrap, along with a white wifebeater and black jeans tied down to his midsection with a belt. The loud and proud member of Playaz Inc. struts around, carrying the famed PCW Tag Team title sportingly on his chest, shrouding the belt holding up his pants. He behemoth's through the area like a man on the stage, intensity raw. 'OH NO! THE FIGHTS OUT, I'M BOUT TO PUT YO LIGHTS OUT, GET THE F--- BACK, GUARD YO GRILL, THERE SOMETHING WRONG, WE CAN'T STAY STILL!' His raw outlook on how things are going are overshadowed as a few men over towards the corner of Nagati and Sappo inside of the mall happen to be in quite the struggle. They seem to be tussling over a console of some instance, while a large crowd happens to swallows them whole. Dante begins to lead the carnival into the mass of people standing there, and yells into his microphone, 'WHAT'S GOING ON OVER HERE?' 'Blockie!' one man pointed out, parting way between the men as Jackson looked over between the two men and saw them tussling over the symbol of how screwed up a rematch clause happens to be. Pac-Man. 'Pac-Man?' Dante questions. 'This is all about a high score?' 'Konichiwa!' Dante turns over, looking for a translator, but ends up having to call upon the services of one large man, standing a full six feet and five inches in length and bulky size hovers around the two hundred eighty pound mark. Wearing a flipped red bandanna Tupac style, with a red wifebeater and matching pants. As he continues to make his way towards Dante, he repeatedly begins to stick his red size 14 Timberland Boot in the hinds of people who happen to step in his way. Jackson glares at the scene for a second. 'I asked for a translator, not a black pasty white dude beater!' The man, who brown pupils happen to show that fiery temper of his are shaded by thick black sunglasses. Hanging from his shoulder happens to be the shiny PCW Tag Team Championship belt, imprinted with his name at the very inserting of title holder. The only difference about the title is the red insignia that happens to cross out the PCW on the belt. Instead, there happens to be a PLAYAZ notation on the faceplate. 'Cuse' me shawty, but I gotsa make sum shit happen n dis bitch.' he parts through the crowd huddled up against the arcade console. Dante greets him with a urban handshake (your secret clubshake like handshakes) before telling him the situation. 'Erm, these idiots playin' Pac Man?' 'Yeah, about as worse than a Gaz Green joke, but equally as redundant as a white man working on UPN.' Dante looked over at Quo who eyed the two men jerking away at the joystick, positioning for high score. 'And these two want the high score.' 'The High Score? You say dat like its biblical.' Quo jokes. 'To many Pac players, it is.'Quo stares at Jackson with an odd look, before venturing off, trying to find something other than the wily rap genius to amuse him. Jackson, smitten with the game, pushes another man out of the way, fighting his way into the main pocket in front of the console. 'Back, back! Back you savages!' Jackson swatted away at the unlucky flock of passerby who duck and try their best to scatter away. 'Godzilla!' one screams out as the entire loot go sprinting off in the distance. 'Come back! Watch your Lyrical Messiah win!' he pleads with them, but its no use. They're long gone and Jackson is now stuck, secluded away from friends and those wanting to see his successes. He takes a slight step backwards, before positioning his hand on the joystick. 'Okay, Mrs. Pac-Man and the Mall for which I am to dominate, meet Mr. King of Rap.' Moving back a bit, he moves the small joystick over, starting upa new game against the three lovable characters he is set to defeat once again. He cackles loudly, using only his index finger and his thumb to position the character on screen against the forces of Pac-Evil.
