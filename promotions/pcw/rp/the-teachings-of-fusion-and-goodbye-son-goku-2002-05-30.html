The teachings of Fusion... and Goodbye Son Goku :(

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30-05-02 The 'Price is Right'....Cha-Cha-Cha-Cha-CHING!!...Wow, I've always wanted to be able to use something like that in the concise form that is more commonly known to you people as a 'Promo'.. or to us boppin' PoP artists as a 'Promotional Video'. It would appear as though the careers of our two heroic warriors are slowly, but very surely beginning to flourish within the grounds of this very land. A great career start point with a well fought victory against 'Misplaced'... an upcoming battle of all the extreme wits... and all the extreme attitude in that good ol' fashioned 'Schools Out' contest... and then, followed by that is the very next round of the 'Tag Team Round Robin Tournament'... this week, the boys having to face the guys whos names were just mentioned in the above opening. So, what does make the Price right?Would the price happen to be determined in the form of Michael times Chet equals $1.95?Would the price happen to be determined in the form of Chet times Michael equals $1.55?Hell... would the price just happen to be any numbers placed together, Michael and Chet being the two boys that are able to place the green label on whether they think the specified amount is... too much... too little... or just about right! However, regardless of how you may wish to look at the facts that lay themselves before you about this suprising Tag Team.. let it be known that isn't so much the very focal point of this new video-tape... oh no, the time of the amazingly intense battle of 'Extreme Carnage' for the 'Extreme Championship' has yet to be decided... and there is still a whole day until this very special contest is anywhere close to getting underway! Now, with regards to the way that the last two shows were opened... with all the fighting and everything, I would like to take the time to introduce myself to everyone around the world... to everyone that has become sad, depressed or lonely... and would like to chat to the member of a Boy Band who is also feeling the same vibes at this very moment in time. For now, gone are the figments of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore from this here room... instead, they continue to train under the guidance of Kakarot... while I, Nick Carter... sit happily in the video booth to give narration on the peril-filled task that awaits them. Therefore, in the words of our untimely hero... que... the DRAGONBALL Z... recap music! ** The immediate area begins to fill with the cool blue vibes of the Dragonball Z Recap music... a tune that has been playing since the beginning of time... but has still been unable to captivate the more advanced of minds into believing that its truely a work of art. ** Last time... on Pretty Cool G...The character at the front door of the PCG Household... was infact, none other than one of the greatest heros in the recent history of time... his name... Goku... or to some of you more involved fans, Kakarot. The Saiayan Warrior then engaged in a small time conversation with each of our wonderful heros... informing them that he had been sent by King Kai to teach them the 'Fusion' Technique ahead of Friday Fusion... where Ryan Moore will be taking part in the most extreme battle for one of the most 'Extreme Championships' around the world.In an attempt to get everyone into the mood of things... Goku informed his new friends that he only had 30minutes left on this world... just half an hour to teach the kids how to do the very dance... 'Sherwin and Moore... theres only 30minutes left until I have to go back...' Rivetting outcomes within the 'Pretty Cool' household.. truely shocking events that would only normally be measured in your nearest edition of 'The National Enquirer!' With Goku in the middle of his transformation.. that being his decision to power up at the end of the last episode... despite only being a 'Bit Player' in this series... its enough to make a grown man weep. Will our 'Pretty Cool' heros be able to learn the starting steps for the Fusion Dance?... and will Goku ever be able to reveal why he has wasted twenty three and a half hours of his time on Earth? To find out the questions to all this and more... KEEP WATCHING! | - ^ - ^ - | Chapter 2 : The time has come... we think...Installment III : Fusion Dance? The picture slowly begins to open on the grounds of a large area... there isn't a thing in sight... so thankfully, the location can't be described all too well. The view begins to turn to the right slowly, moving in something of a circle if you will. A few moments pass... Goku comes into view... at least, the back of the Saiyan Hero comes into view. At the moment, he's standing still with his arms crossed... looking off into the distance at whats probably the duo of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore. The view continues to shift to the right... Nick Carter slowly comes into view, standing next to Kakarot himself. The silence around the immediate area is eerie... almost too weird for it to be something of a worthwhile status. Goku slowly tilts his head to the right and says a few words to the 'Substitute' Trainer... 'You're going to have to learn everything I teach these guys alot faster than you'd be expected to Nick...', Goku informed him before his voice trailed off into the depths of nothingness. Nick Carter looked a little worried about the task at hand... sure, he was one of the greatest dancers in the world and all, but this 'Fusion Dance' was like nothing else that had ever been brought to light on a Television Screen... unless you were a frequent viewer of Dragonball Z. If you're not... then you have no idea what I'm talking about. In which case, just continue to view for the novalty value.. and not the understanding of things. 'Erm... are you sure that I'll be able to pass on a technique like this to both Ryan and Rich... I mean, I'm the same as they are... if only a little better...', Nick protested, sounding very sure that he'd be unable to teach anything to the Super-Powers known to the world as Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore. Goku turned to face his worried substitute, that look of complete calmness spreading across his face like nothing else. Nick Carter returned Goku's calm, collected stare with one that was very... sheepish to say the very least. 'Its alright Nick... I'm sure you would have been able to pick up everything that I teach Rich and Ryan... after all, I have placed my complete faith in you... its not down to the fact that I really want to do such a thing, but without Piccolo around.. you're the best thing to him.', Goku told the scrawny dancer of the 'Backstreet Boys'. Nick was beginning to look a little more worried with every passing second. Goku however, decided that enough was enough with regards to this and he duely ignored the looks of his assistant, returning his glare to the duo of both Rich and Ryan... who were still trying to get through the movements that followed with Fusion. The view moved to the left this time... Goku and Nick Carter falling out of the picture and the duo of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore coming in. The two of them were dressed in Gi's... just like little Goten and Trunks from Dragonball Z itself. The only major difference was the size of the uniforms... this being down to the fact that Sherwin and Moore were both adults... and also significantly older than their counterparts. The two came into full view and then the view moved across so that both men were standing and looking into the view of the picture. Sherwin and Moore turned to look at each other once before nodding somewhat. 'Ok Ryan... lets take this from the top, exactly the way that Son Goku showed us...', Rich called out before getting into position along with Ryan. Both men stood with their arms outstreched to the side. Ryan was on the left side of the screen so his right arms had been extended. Sherwin was on the right side of the picture, so his arms both pointed to the left. 'Fu.... sion!', was called by both Rich and Ryan as they brought their arms other their head to point toward each other, keeping the same pose where their arms were both extended. While doing this movement, the two men had side-stepped three paces into the middle of the gap. Goku nodded somewhat to show his acceptance of the move so far. After which, when the '... sion' came into play, both Sherwin and Moore brought their arms across to the other side, their inner leg coming across their body. Both their fingers and knees pointed in the same direction... but opposite from one another. With a loud scream of 'HHHHAAAAA!', Sherwin and Moore brought their arms to the inside of the pose, forming something of a C with their head in the middle. The two men allow their index fingers to touch each other, making sure that the pose is completly parallel. Sherwin and Moore continue to look dead ahead, the expressions on their faces reflecting something of a look thats deadly serious about everything. Goku looks impressed with the attempt by the PoP icons and applauds them. 'Good work boys!', he called out at the top of his voice, making sure that both Ryan and Rich were able to hear his words of praise. Rich and Ryan break away from the pose and look at one another... watching the drips of sweat fall down from their faces and hit the floor. Nick Carter was shocked... looking on amazement at the way that these two were able to handle the dance with such expertise. They slowly began to trudge over to Goku himself... before stepping infront of him and awaiting further instructions on what to do. 'Well boys...', he started before looking at both Ryan and Rich individually, '.. I don't know what to say about the speed that you have been able to learn the Fusion Dance... I'm truely impressed at the way that you've taken the stride into your move... training up to an excellent level and showing me that humans aren't as weak as my buddy Krillin...' Rich and Ryan look at one another, their faces drop as Goku comments on the human race being weak. We all know that humans are not the best of battle dolls for the world of war when compared to the mighty Power of the Saiyans... but oh well, maybe something interesting will happen within the next few moments or so. We wait for something to happen between all three of the heros... but nothing does. Rich is the first person to speak up and break the terrible silence. 'Son Goku... do you really think that Ryan and myself will be able to fuse into a new Warrior before the show tomorrow night?', he asked, looking a little worried about being able to even fuse in the way that Goku had previously informed the world on episodes of Dragonball Z. Since we'd only see the Saiyans do this jig... how did we know that PCG would be able to follow through and do the same thing? Goku struggled for a few moments to think of an answer that wouldn't be as crushing as the full blown 'Nah'... and within what appears to be seconds, the legend answers ths call... 'Well Rich, I'm not sure if you're really going to be able to fuse into the new warrior like my son and Trunks did.. I mean, they were both Super-Saiyans... and could easily beat both of you with both arms tied behind their backs!', Goku informed them both, laughing a little and scratches the back of his head in a sheepish mood. Rich and Ryan Moore continued to look in their flabbergasted way... unable to believe the way that Son Goku is doubting them after such an intense period of training. Nick Carter takes a few steps back from the select crowd of three... trying desperatly to distance himself from the characters in question. 'H-H-H-Hey... Guys... I-I-I-I'm... just gonna go to the house and make... s-s-s-s-s-s-something...', he managed to stutter before turning around and beginning to run away like one of the craziest animals in the world. It was obvious that the pressure had got to his head... such a shame we guess... guess not, they say. Goku wipes his hand over his face.. worried about the Piccolo-standin running off as such. Rich and Ryan look a little shocked... but certainly not rocked by the decision of Nick Carter. 'Oh no... my only Piccolo stand-in has run off like a little baby... and she-at, I'm going to have to go back to the check-in station.. looks like my time is up here...', Goku told the guys before turning away and beginning to slowly walk off. Rich and Ryan looked at him as he walked off into the sunset, worried about what action they should take next... especially when they don't have the guidance of somebody as good as Goku... 'What are we going to do now Richie?', Ryan asked, his voice beginning to tremble as the realisation that Goku wouldn't be coming back settled in... 'We train... train until we get this right...', Rich answered, sounding more confident than his fellow band member... but still a little worried about how effective their training is going to be... as the scene slowly faded out... deep, into black... Next time... on Pretty Cool G...Did 'Pretty Cool Guys' succeed in fusing and winning the Extreme Championship?Will Rich and Ryan Moore be able to give us some insight into their Round Robin match with The Price Brothers in two days time...... and, will these boys be able to perform their chart smashing debut-single in the next video? All this and more... NEXT TIME!!!
