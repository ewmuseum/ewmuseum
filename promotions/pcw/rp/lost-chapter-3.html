Lost Chapter #3

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-5-03 Mark is standing in the street of the little town down south. The same one that we last left him at. He is looking at every black person possible with the grin on his face like something is up his sleeve. He is making sure that everyone knows his presence is felt or at least they know he is around. “You damn Nazi!” An mid old black lady yells as she holds the hand of her young son. “Get the hell out of our town cracker!” A thug yells as his chains sway back and forth with his movements as he walks past. Mark starts to get a smile on his face as more of the black community walks by him. He is getting a feeling of happiness since he knows that they don’t like his presence here and they will soon get a shock of the century from him. Mark continues to walk more deep into the town and to get farther into more of the black community. “Why the hell are you smiling you damn skinhead?” A black guy asks in a shouting voice as Mark walks by the family of blacks. Mark squints his eyes as he is looking at the dad of the family. He passes on but is remembering the face of that guy for future reference. Then Mark notices these two familiar faces that are right next to the same pole where he got rung up at. He starts to go up towards them as they notice him out of the corner of their eyes. The two men start to back away from the pole as Mark is pushing people out of his way to get to the tow men who rung him up. “Get back here you coons!” Mark shouts as the two black men start to run and Mark starts to chase. Mark starts to run after the two men as they take off like rockets as soon as they see Mark power walking over to them. Little children, boys, teenagers, and adults are starting to shout down the streets and going into the streets because of the mayhem. Mark is a gaining on the two men as they start to run down the sidewalk side by side. “What the hell were you thinking last night?” The guy who was smoking the joint says to the other guy as they are running next to each other. “I thought it was a free shot dawg so I took it, since look what shit this cracker has done to this town bro.” The other guy says as they are looking straight ahead and not at each other. “Now look what shit you got us into dawg!” Says the guy who distracted Mark the night before to have the guy take the free shot. The two men can feel the hot air coming from Mark’s mouth as he is reaching closer and closer to reaching distance. As Mark reaches for the two, they split into two opposite ways. One going into a alley way that is filled with garbage and the other in the middle of the street. Mark is shocked about the move and he looks down the alley way and he starts to chase the man down there. “I got you where I want you now!” Mark screams with emotion as he starts to run after the middle aged 20 year old kid. Mark starts to sprint and not too far from starting Mark is already gaining ground on this kid and he can feel he is going to catch him. An evil smile forms across his face as he is running and thinking about his move when he is going to catch him. How sweet is this going to be to Mark? Mark jumps over the metal trash can as the garbage is already out laying on the ground and the papers are blowing all over the alley from the wind picking them up. The black guy is slowing down tremendously as all the marijuana he has been smoking has been catching up with him. Mark finally is now inches from grabbing him and taking him down as he reaches out for his shirt and pulls him in close to his body. Mark takes him down to the warm heated cement ground. “I swear I didn’t mean to do it dawg.” The black guy pleads as Mark is on top of his back with the evil smirk still on his face. “Dawg?” Mark asks in a question as he starts to laugh. “You never ever should call me your fuckin dawg again or else I would have to guy your ass like a pig on a farm.” The guy gulps in his throat as he is starting to shake about some ideas that might be going through Mark’s head. What is Mark thinking about doing to the guy and how is he feeling about Mark? The guy is thinking to himself why he hit Mark the night before. He didn’t really mean to do it, but it was out of anger from what he has started in this town. “I have a great idea.” Mark says as he is starting to laugh a bit and chuckle. Mark gets up from the ground and he starts to drag the guy out of the alley and to the street. Mark is kicking at the guy’s ribs when he drops him and tells him to get up from the ground and to walk himself. But the black guy just refuses and this makes Mark even more mad as he is dragging him more and more, scrapping up his arms and legs as he is being dragged to the street. “Are you going to follow my orders now?” Mark asked as he drops the guy down on his stomach. “Fuck naw!” The man yells as he is starting to spit up some blood. “Have it your way then.” Mark says as he starts to drag the guy’s body now and enters the street. Mark drops his body on the side of the street next to the cement curb. Mark directs all the attention towards him as he is setting up the man’s face on the curb. People know what is coming right now and they all are running from scared of watching. “Bite that curb you fucking worthless coon!” Mark says in anger as the black man does what he is told. Mark sets up his shot that he is going to take on the man. He steps back and frames up the man’s head as he then goes up to it real close. The guy’s face is in pain already and he knows this is the ending to his life and is wondering why he did do what he did the night before to Mark. But before he can think about it his whole world blacks out as that is the end.
