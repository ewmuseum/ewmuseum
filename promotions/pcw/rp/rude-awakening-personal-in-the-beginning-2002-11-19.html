Rude Awakening – Personal – In the Beginning

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 19th, 2002 I no longer stood alone… I had allies, friends to count on. People who looked at the word for what it truly was, and not for what it could be. It was peaceful, graceful, for once we did not fear for are lives. We stood strong… We remained a force of power… A group of enlighten giants… A team like no other ever created… But then something stopped the happiness. Imagination started to run wild, and panic brought chaos to what could have been order. Are own started to turn on us, friends becomes foes. But I still kept strong for as long as I could. I still had friends. Blassa… Safina… They were counting on me to survive and live on. It came to my decisions and whether or not I was to go back for them. Should they be left to die, or should others die for them. It was clear now, we had to amount something to help. We couldn’t just stand back and watch as are own ideas and thoughts were crushed by the weight of a greater force. It was time. VIOLENCE… The guns cracked, the fire burned in are eyes, as houses were torched, cattle slaughtered, and food trampled. Death kissed are necks, evil burned are souls, and for one waking moment even I wanted to die with them. But all I did was coward away, hide from what was my idea, and run from my own fate. I should have died. Not my friends, even my enemies. This world changed us into monsters. We only cared for are own beliefs, are own identity. Soon allies turned, and before long it was all out war, Everyone for them self…Everyone for their own life…Everyone for their survival… But me, I just ran. I couldn’t face it till today. It was horrible. I ran and took what I could and what I needed to survive. I gathered my small group of you and Blassa and just ran. Last I knew, everyone was dead. No one won, no one lost. Just millions of ties which left millions dead and thousands starving or homeless. They shouldn’t have fought…They should have just lived together…. I was the one who started the fight, and my own body ran. My mind was purely in the battle, by my soul would never win the world. I caused all the death. I caused all the pain. IT WAS MY FAULT… «««§»»» She just sat there starring at me, locking her blue eyes onto mind, searching my insides for the rest of what possibly could be the change of my life. My eyes started to wonder the room, making sure not to come into contact, so not to draw any questions. But even she knew what was going on and sooner or later would ask something. “You never told me this before.” If times were different, my life would still be trapped in the jar that is my inner obsessions, but it was time for this world to find the true man behind the mask. I started to grow nervous hoping to invade the question with no responds. He finger tapped her chair, waiting for me to do something, give her a sign or in the very lest understand her comment. The calmness just made the situation more depressing, while my thoughts started to race without control. The silence needed to be lifted, so I had to pour it all out. “You see why I kept hiding all these years.” “Is this why your own parents abandoned you?” My heart didn’t want to answer, but I nodded in acceptance. I tried to calm down, but every moment a new story came back. My heart would start to race, patting against my chest, beating harder and harder every minute. Moist sweat poured from my forehead, my hands started to tremble as we re-called the memories, brining up wounds that would never heal. Safina’s eyes narrowed as she worried over what was wrong. She knew what was happening, she was there right in the middle of the battle like I was. But she was taken prisoner, capture by are enemies and forced into things she didn’t believe were right. “You know how hard time were back then Safina.” “Yes I do. And I know we came out alive. But what else happened? What are you not telling me?” My thoughts didn’t want to be released from within, but some how I felt liable to tell her what truly happened. My eyes closed and tried to remember everything that had happened those many years back. She was waiting on my every move, hopping to get a piece of history she had missed. “It all started when you were gone.” «««§»»» We had just taken back are small town on the outskirts of Thailand, near the boarder. We didn’t use force; it was just left for us. It was early in the war and weapons were not being used. Instead people used their words, which made for more of a inner style battle. I had the honor of commanding a small group that was still very young. I was only seventeen. “Are you sure this is what the temple wants?” The same question was asked every day. Are job was to keep the temple safe, but we managed to lose it in the first few weeks. Students became fighters in only a few short months. Are training was nearly complete and for some of us, like me, we were to represent Thailand. Now we represented everything we stood for. There was five groups in all of this. The government group that wanted everything done their way. The church wanted peace and for people to obey the lord, which didn’t fall well. There was the freedom rebels that really didn’t have a goal besides causing terrorism. The people of Thailand formed their own group to survive. Then there was us all on are own. We had the purpose of defending are personal ideas. We were known only as one name… THE REALISTS… Yes the name wasn’t glorious, but it served its purpose. We owned the temple and used for a training facility. But the word temple brought hatred toward us, as people believed acts of prayer, not combat should have been studied. “Who are we missing,” I asked to everyone, trying to make sure I didn’t loose anyone. But things changed quickly. I learned I had lost two people, two friends, two of my only friends. This was a striking blow to my self-esteem, something that would take me weeks to come over. If only this day would end I could get on with more pressing matters. Darkness fell and I stayed awake all night wondering what torture was going on. Wondering who would save them. Trying to devise a play of rescue. But all I knew was that I had lost my only friends and I had to get them back. «««§»»» Safina glazed over me, not knowing what to make of this situation. “I don’t know why you got all upset. I wasn’t in any danger and neither was Blassa.” I couldn’t help but feel guilty. “You don’t understand what I went through. Not knowing if you were safe or even alive. Blassa and you know me inside and out. I couldn’t just abandon my allies like that. Not in those times.” I could feel my body temperature rise and my heart begin to pound as I become more defensive of the situation at hand. I paced back and forth, trying to find the right words to say. The tension was getting to me, crawling up on my back and digging into my skull. “It was bad Safina. The thoughts of what they might do to you and Blassa just ran through my mind all night. I could sleep, I couldn’t eat and I couldn’t think straight.” “You never acted like this before. What has happened since then? Ever since you were told to leave, these memories have came up.” My head throbbed and the tension wasn’t making this experience any easier on me or her. But some how I knew she was going to push me into the rest of the tale and make me announce everything. What did I have to lose anyway? I was already banished; I had nothing to go for, so why not tell something that has been inside me for almost ten years. They still didn’t know were I had gone. I had rescued them, but from then on out I was a changed man. Something inside me told me to leave and face something else until the war was over. Someone told me to run… And run I did until everything was peaceful again. The bloodshed was over and now it was time for me to come clean about my mistakes. It wouldn’t be easy but it had to be done. No more running from what is my own fate. «««§»»» The guns cracked with fury, the ground trembled under the battle that raged on the plains above. We commanded are assault on the bunker that housed Blassa and Safina. Thousands of are men trying to save two who were lost. Friends…Allies… We raced toward the bunker; shoots just missing are heads by inches. Some not so lucky, they are hit in a fatal area and are lost without trace. I couldn’t take it anymore. I stopped dead in the shooting and looked around, watching as are group stormed the bunker, fighting back and building an upper hand. This war was raging, but no one knew it was going on outside the country. It was clear to us now that this thick marsh would become are final resting-place if we were not careful. Except for me… I was long gone. I was too young to listen to the screaming of dying men. The images filled my head and soon controlled every movement. I had to leave the fight. Leave this city and more importantly, leave Thailand. I packed up and moved across fields and plains, mountains and hillsides until I reached the boarder. I was free from the violence. I had gotten word Safina and Blassa were safe but now that meant nothing to me. I had my whole life to plain and worry about. So I did the only thing I could. I left Thailand in searched of a path… I had found my calling… «««§»»» “You could have told me this before,” Safina shouted down at me as I sat starring at the same place in the floor. I could bare to let her see me a broken down man. I was a coward and I ran from what should have been my own fate. I took lives and sacrificed them for my own. “I’m sorry.” It was hard for me to admit this. My spirit never wanted this to be told. But in a way the atmosphere in this room and the sunlight the seemed to creep its way across the floor and shine into my eyes, opened the door to a whole new beginning for me. I had lied about my age. I still held something from her. I wasn’t seventeen but more like eight. “When times were tough Safina, I had to act quickly.” You can’t imagine what I went through as a child. At least you had parents to go back to when this was over.” She picked my head up and looked me in the eye. The anger had long left her face and now she was curious again. She smiled and shook her head, sitting back down in her chair. “I never did know you Vilas. Were did you go from there.” I looked at her again and smiled back. The mood had lighten up and it was easy to show my emotions. But still something wanted me to keep my life a secret. Something wanted me to live behind a mask. I still wanted to represent and there was only one way I could. I had to learn to compete. “I think it’s time to come clean. I will tell you everything.” «««§To be continued§»»»
