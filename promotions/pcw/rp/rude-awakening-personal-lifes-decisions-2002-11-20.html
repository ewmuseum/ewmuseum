Rude Awakening – Personal – Life’s Decisions

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 20th, 2002 The breeze brushed the back of my skin, a sensation that only came from a fall morning where the leaves were just about to fall off the tree’s and the days become short and cold, but still it was the time of most beauty and grace. Safina and myself, decided it would be best t continue are discussion out in the worlds bliss and soak in the foliage that made the thoughts easier to come through and out the mouth. Safina seemed more calm then usual. The day must have filled her thought will so many ideas and this weather is helping to cope with the past memories. It’s hard to tell what Safina is thinking because she hides it more then anyone I know. She still is the same girl from so many years ago, but not me, I have changed dramatically. “Something is troubling you Safina.” I knew something was wrong, she usually is asking questions or trying to find the answers, but now she was different then normal. When I decided to tell her about many years ago, I didn’t know how she would take it or even how should we react. She was a new person in my eyes, and really she hasn’t changed a bit. “I’m just thinking of what you said, or rather what you were going to say.” It always amazed me how people could tell what I was going to say, or rather what I was thinking. And really no one had any clue what I would say or what would even become of me in the first place. But Safina was different. She could tell what was going on inside my mind, but my picture was still a blurry and clearly out of focus. “I’m just wondering why you never came to me before? I could have understood what you were saying, but you hide it from me for so many years.” I had to say something, and leaving an empty whole would only make things worse for me, and for her, as she would continue asking questions till both of us couldn’t listen anymore. Now was the time for me to come clean. Keeping it tucked away will only make the whole bigger. “Do remember Safina, that I was still very small and very inexperienced in any combat. I will not lie.” There was more to it then that. Something told me to continue on, but the wind seemed to push the words right from the mouth and brushed them down the street and away into the hills. I was scared… I was afraid of my own death… I still needed to give her an answer or even a reason. But still my stomach was in such a mess that coming up with anything was out of the question. I had to force it out and even then it wasn’t clear. “An enemy helped me with my destiny.” She only looked at me starring me down in a fit of anger. Talking with the enemy was bad enough, but listening to them was not acceptable. She stormed down the street and stopped, just to put some distances between is. She needed some time to her self, to reflect on the matter. It would do well for everyone. I sat on the curb and watch the dead leaves fall from the trees and the clouds dance across the sky. It was a once in a life time choice that had to be made on the spot… It was my life and more importantly my destiny… Safina didn’t see through my eyes but could I really blame her. She had gone through so much in so little time. It was going to take more to convince her I made the right choice. I decided now would be the best time, as I sat up and walked over to her grasping her shoulder with the palm of my hand. “It wasn’t easy, believe me that. But in some way I was glad I did what I had to do. It’s not something I look back on, but it’s something I am proud to do.” ««««§»»»» The dirt seemed to cling to my shoes, slowing down my pace and weaking my spirit as I set out to free my self of the gore that was going on. My worst fear was coming true, my friends were living what should have been my fate, my destiny, but instead my choice was to run away. **crack** **crack** The ring of the guns was still echoing in my ears and the sheer horror of the destruction played over my eyes and paused when I blinked. I had seen what was my wish in life, I had seen a side of man that I should have never saw. This is why I fend for my self and not for some spirit, lurking over every shoulder and every rock that lies in the sand. Time seemed to fly by in seconds. Hours lasted minutes and soon I was left alone in the cold dark night, clinging to the only possessions I still had in my hand. The roar of the guns, the sound of men and women moaning, while their bodies lay in the dirt, dying of wounds not treatable sent a shock through my body. They were the hero’s and the leaders. Then there was me… A waste of time and space on this planet… I took food, medicine and what ever else I could carry and just ran.. Hide.. Cowered away… I need an escape, and more importantly a way out. I needed to survive. ««««§»»»» “Are you alright son,” said a voice, as I felt the warm and soft sheets rap against my skin and fill me with a joy of safety. I opened my eyes to find man, much older then I was starring down at me with a cup or steaming tea. He smiled down and handed me the cup and felt my head for a fever. “Your lucky to be alive after a night like that.” The steam blocked his words out and I quickly began to wonder were I was. It didn’t matter to me, I was safe and there was nothing to worry about any longer. I grinned as I took a small sip of the tea. I looked at the man who had saved me from death last night and tried to figure him out. “You’re not like the others.” His face had a look of confusion as I stared to question who he was. The collar gave him away, and looking around the room it was easily noticeable to see the pictures of mystical beings. “I am not like whom,” he added, trying to figure me out, just as much as I was trying to figure him out. “You’re not like the other religiests we have meet before.” He still wasn’t following my words. Maybe I wasn’t clear or maybe he had no idea. He shook his head slowly, not knowing what I was talking about. “So your not apart of this on going battle,” I asked, taking another few sips form the tea cup and swallowing it down my throat. “No, I am part of a group trying to stop the violence. We found you last night almost dead at the side of the road covered in your own blood. What happen to you?” I swallowed, “I was in a battle to rescue my friends.” He stood up and walked back to his kitchen that was right beside the bed my body was resting in. He poured himself a cup of tea and looked at me from a distances, wondering were my story will lead. “So you are one of the rebels then,” he asked making his way back around the counter and in to the living room area. I shoot back at him with a scorn of furry, “Good heavens no. I am a realist. We do what is best for are selves.” The priest nodded, well at least I thought that’s what he was. He smiled and sat back in a large leather chair, sipping from his tea and trying to make of my story. I don’t think he believed me, because he would have said something by now. “We were rescuing my friends when I just couldn’t last another minute.” “What happen,” he asked, now looking more concern and interested in my story. I hesitated to tell him, but something told me just to say it, “I got scared. I ran from my destiny and ran from what should have been my turn to die.' “Some would call that a risk worth taking. Saving your own life when you can’t take anymore.” I didn’t reply, but just sat still, looking into my half drank tea and looking for Safinas and Blassa”s face. I got out of the bed and started to walk around, getting what little feeling I had in my legs stronger then before. I walked to his large window and looked out at the day that was before. A beautiful day, filled with blue skies, but something made me fell this was just an illusion. “So tell me something, were are you going anyway,” he asked, causing me to turn around and stair at him without an answer for even the most simple question today. All I could do was answer no, but even that wasn't'’ good enough. “I have to help my friends.” “Do you,” he replied quickly. “Well of course I do. That would make me just as bad as the enemy if I didn’t return. He stood up and walked over and looked out the window. I could see the black hair on his head was perfect and his clothing was the most unique I had ever seen. He looked down at me and smiled. “You know, the first thing a realist as you call your self should understand is that you must fend for yourself and your own ideas.” I nodded slowly, not really sure what to make of the statement. “I’m not going to lecture you of the holly spirit, but you seemed to be a very troubled young boy.” He was correct, and I just walked away. My last thoughts were on my self at the moment, and even he could see that through me. My path was lost under my friend’s thoughts. “I am lost. My path is not clear. My life is on hold.” He seemed concerned and wanted to help, but I kept pushing him farther back, praying he would keep to himself, but a part of me wanted his help and so far that part was winning. “You must look at your own choices. Do what you want to do son.” I had to shoot back with an idiotic answer, “I want to help my friends. But I don’t want to die.” He nodded, “No one wants to die it just happens.” “I must leave then.” My only choice was to leave… Run.. Cower away again… “You need time to think. You should not return to battle or what ever you decide until you are sure you want to live that fate.” I will start now… Fate is only a word…It has no meaning…I am in control and no one will stop that… “I know what must be done,” I said, walking towards his door and turn the nod to enter the wilderness. He came behind me and stopped me with his arm. I looked back and he nodded to me. “You must do what is right for your own self. Your path is your choice. What ever your hearts desire is, you should grasp it and continue that journey. Friendship is worth dying for, but you have to evaluate your own life first.” I smiled and nodded back, continuing to walk down his steps and back on the road again. Were I was going was still in the wind, but I at least had an idea of what my life would be for. My heart had it’s choice and that was my life long dream. I would represent my country… I would fight for honor and pride… I would compete for respect… ««««To be continued»»»»
