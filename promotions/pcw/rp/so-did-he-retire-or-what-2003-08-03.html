So did he retire or what?

3 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/3/03 (From The Malice Man’s Website to his fans) They say a mind is terrible thing to waste. Some might say that being a pro-wrestler is a waste of your mental capacity. I disagree. I have always tried to beat my opponents before a fist is ever thrown. In my younger days it was through fear and intimation; now its whit, spin, lies, or simple mind games. It’s amazing what you can learn by simply listening to what one says. Most people rant off rubbish and filler that is as ridiculous as trash talk and in your face antics. When I was younger I could count on my strength, body, and ring speed. That is behind me. All my wars are now won outside of the ring and around it. I realize this, and accept it. Have my in ring skills diminished so much that I can’t win in the ring? My pay-per-view win/loss record indicates just that. Should I accept retirement? Should I move on? Is the chapter of Malice really over? I don’t think so but I’m still reflecting on that. I’m ready to retire but something isn’t right. I know I can still compete with the kids out there today. Look at our current world champion. I beat him out of the ring. I outsmarted him in every aspect of the feud. Then when it mattered inside the ring I also won. I out fought the young stallion and had the match in my grasps. Then age kicked in. How could I over look the threat on the outside of the ring? That’s a rookie mistake. Kannon won. Good for him. But I hung with him and could have beaten him. Just when I’m ready to call it quits something drags me back. It is an addiction stronger then that of any drug. A mental sickness that feeds my sanity and gives my pride and ego a massive feeling of ecstasy. How do you walk away from Nirvana? A victory equals Camelot; a defeat is eternal Hades. Depression sets in with every loss. But even with that risk I need more. You cannot comprehend the craving of the sport if you’ve never done it. The cheer of the crowds, the flash of the cameras, the heat of the ring lights. That’s what its all about hoss. It is what drives each and every athlete that has ever stepped in any ring or arena. I cannot change my past. I cannot live on what I once did. If I’m to stick around its to win the ultimate orgasm. The PCW world title. I have lived the kind of life that most men only dream of. I have f*cked four Playboy playmates at the same time and blew my load all over them. That feeling of bliss and ultimate gratification is not even close to the feeling of holding up the world title. I did it once in ICWA and I will do it in PCW. No matter what the critics think. I’m 42 to years old now and my dream is fading fast. I have nowhere to go when its all over. But I keep hiding my aches and make the stage and try to kick the footlights out again. On my stereo right now is Jimmy Buffet’s “Changes In Latitudes, Changes In Attitudes.” As I write this you don’t know how true that is. For too long I have been the nice guy. I let it slide by and move on. They say the squeaky wheel gets the grease. Well hosses I’ve been a well-oiled machine for too long. Time to do a little squeaking. Time to get the attention of those that want to bury me. Those who want me to retire and forever be done with me. An insult to say the least they put me in the ring with Chris Chase. Another young punk with no respect for what I have done. Or any clue of where I’m going to go. This kid see this as a great thing. He gets to fight the Malice Man. The guy willing to put anybody over for the betterment of the company. Right? Wrong. Not anymore. Poor Chris Chase. The kid has no idea how bad I need to hurt him. Life is a journey with twists and turns at every corner. My life is more then half over if we look at national statistics. I look at that as a minor set back. If you think Malice is dead or defunct then you don’t know me. If you think my goals are set to high then your looking from down too low. If the sky is going to fall in then dig your ass a trench and deal with it. Malice is pulsating and stronger then ever. The goal has been set and will be reached. Hoss that is the Honky Tonk Truth. Duke WilliamsPCW President Resident Malice Man. =w=
