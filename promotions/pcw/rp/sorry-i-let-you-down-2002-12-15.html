Sorry I Let You Down

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15, 2002 A muffled scream cries out in the darkness. Help! Help! The voice faded further away into the darkness as I darted up from the seat, and ran in the direction of the voice. The scream was Christa’s. I followed it further as the light grew smaller and smaller until I could see nothing, only follow the voice and stop it from crying. I shouted and screamed as loud as my lungs would permit calling out for Christa hoping that she would respond and say ‘Tessa, I am fine.’ Instead the voice let out one last cry for help followed by a murderous scream and then silence. The type of silence you never want to hear. Nothing to be heard for miles, just silence in its simplest form, and then I woke up. Sitting up from my bed on Chris Storm’s couch my blue eyes shot open and I looked around as the glowed scanning the room, looking for anything that might be creeping around at this time of night. It was simply a nightmare brought on by fear, or was it? I slipped my feet into a pair into teddy bear shaped slippers that I had kept for several years now. They were beaten and torn yet I still clung onto them and refused to left go of them simply because my brother Brandon had given them to me, and they were one of the few lingering memories I had left of Brandon after his death several months ago. Walking out to the kitchen I felt a cool breeze hit me. I looked down the hallway and noticed that the front door was open. Maybe that damn cat had found another way to sneak in once more. Walking to the door I looked outside and noticed nothing but the wind. It crept through the bare trees silently, as the braches shook slightly. Walking back inside I pushed the door closed with my foot and then walked over to the guest bed room to see if everything was okay. There sat Alex, fast asleep in his bed. Beside him a slender figure draped up under the blankets to keep warm. I walked over to the bed placing my hand on sheets to comfort Christa. Running my hands across the sheets I noticed that the feeling running across my hand was much too soft to be Christa. I figured that maybe Alex was making too much commotion so she covered her head with a pillow to drown out all the noise. I pulled up the sheets and there was the pillow, but where was Christa? I pulled the pillow up and spotted nothing. Maybe she had a nightmare similar to mine and went to Chris to comfort her. I closed the door to the guest bed room and walked over to Chris’ room, pushing the door open as silently as possible to keep from awakening him. I looked on the bed and there was Chris, and beside him a figure draped under the blankets that he was clutching onto him. I let out a sign automatically assuming that the figure beside him was Christa. I walked over to the bed and sat down beside the figure scooping it from under the blanket into my arms, planning to comfort the figure and put it back in its bed. Looking down I noticed that the figure was not Christa; it was the teddy bear that I had bought for Chris. I threw the teddy bear down beside Chris jumping up from the bed as fear began to trace through my mind. I walked back to the front door as something caught my eye that I had not seen before. It was a little piece of paper that had a note written in crimson red ink. I looked down and my world fell apart. On this letter the worst possible words in the world had seized my by the throat and chocked me with tears. It read… We have defeated you once more. See you on Sunday. My worst fears were confirmed. Christa was gone, and it was my fault. I should have known better to leave the door unlocked. I should have known better than to let the kids sleep alone in a room with nothing or no one to save them should something go wrong, and something did go wrong, very wrong and there was nothing I could do about it. It was too late. I could call the cops, but what good would that do? It would simply mean I would not get the sweet revenge that I had waited so long for. I could go after them on my own right now, but what good would that do? I had no clue where they had gone and what they had done with Christa. I ripped the note to shreds and lashed out at the front door in anger, slamming it shut and then wailing out as the tears poured down my face. I was defeated before Sunday even came. What was I to do? I could not bare to awaken Chris and tell him what had happened. That would simply make matters worse to see him suffer the same emotional pain that I was going through at the moment. I leaned up against the front door as my back slid down its side as I fell into a ball and pulled my legs closer to my head as the tears fell down my cheeks. I was a let down. I let down Brandon. I promised him before he died that his children would be safe with me. I promised him that no harm would come their way. But where was Christa now? For all I know she could be laying in a puddle of the blood we shared for so long, preparing to visit her father and live with him once more. I wanted to punish myself for breaking this promise, but I couldn’t. I had to hold off my emotions till Sunday. Till the time for sweet revenge came, and I would get it. Not just on Shin, but I would seek revenge on Mani as well. It was a promise.
