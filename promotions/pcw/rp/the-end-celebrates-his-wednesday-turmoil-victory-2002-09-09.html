The End celebrates his Wednesday Turmoil victory...

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/09/02 Trigger sits in the back of the club in jeans and brown cowboy boots, with the lyrics 'Dilemma' by Nelly and Kelly Rowlands (you know, one of those OTHER girls from Destiny's Child) playing to the beat of Scarface's 'My Block,' expertly mixed by MTV-famous DJ Skribble. He drinks a bottle of Hennessey like he doesn't even want to. Isolated in the corner, he doesn't even watch the others celebrate The End's #1 Contendership to the PCW Extreme Title. It was an easy match, one where The End dominated his other three opponents and beat them in record time for a four-way match. Nerezza sits down next to him, his red leather coat buttoned up covering every inch of him save for his pale white hands. He drinks a can of Rezz Bull, his own special drink to keep him from getting blood cravings. Looking through his purple chic shades at Trigger, Nerezza extends a bit of conversation. Nerezza:'He IMed you, too?' Trigger doesn't do anything. Nerezza:'I thought you were injured?' Trigger just drinks the Hennessey, not even paying attention. Nerezza leans over and waves his left hand in front of Trigger's face. Nerezza:'Hello? Anyone at home?' With a puff of smoke, some heat, and an orange mushroom cloud, Trigger spontaneously explodes. In his place, all that is left is the bottle of Hennessey, his wallet, and a black ash outline of his body in the chair. Nerezza looks around to make sure no one is looking and pockets the wallet, walking away. A distance aways, at the bar, Jesse Ramey shakes his head, scratching his chin through his thick beard and and takes William Powell's bet. Ramey:'You're on!' Powell takes off his gray trenchcoat, revealing a crisp navy blue suit. He leaves his seat, strokes his short blond hair back, and walks over two seats to a Latina woman, brown-haired, tanned, measuring 38-28-38. She's wearing a glittered red dress and ordering a martini. He clears his throat and takes out his wallet as he speaks to her. Powell:'Excuse me, ma'am, but would you mind if I bought you anything you could possibly want for a few nights of sex?' Woman:'Of course!' Back over on the opposite end of the blue flannel-clad Ramey, Reginald Lovecraft finishes his bourbon and wipes it on his the sleeve of his light-brown double-breasted business suit coat. Lovecraft:'Never bet a rich man if he can pick up chicks.' The End walks up to the bar and orders a Sprite. He doesn't drink alcohol, the wuss. Anyway, there are congratulations from general well-wishers who have showed up to The End's celebratory party this Tuesday night 24 hours before Wednesday Turmoil even begins... The End stands up on the bar and addresses the patrons within Club Pearl, his black shirt and pants standing out from the red-lighted walls. The End:'Thank you all for coming and joining me as I celebrate my victory over Jon Kano, Carl Cash, and Scorpion tomorrow to gain the #1 contendership to the Extreme Title currently held by Semore Smoke. Even if he manages to keep it this week, I will most definitely win it from him next week.' Semore Smoke is in his jeans in the back, smoking weed in the kitchen. He's too baked to care about what The End says. Up front, The End continues his speech. The End:'My win was to be expected... After all, who did I have to face? Jon Kano, a man who had extreme difficulty in taking out COREY WILLIAMS -- of all people -- out of our misery; Carl Cash, a man stuck in the 80's who's got nothing much to his name in WT worth mentioning save for that he tried to join EW and he was in NTICW when it.... you know, sucked; and last, and in some ways the least, Scorpion. A man who's abysmal track record with PCW means that he failed to capture the Rising Star title when there was no one with any particular talent challenging it... in fact, after being in PCW for a few months, he just now put out an introductory promo!' The guests all laugh. But there is one in the back, wearing green Army fatigues, who stands up onto a table and replies to The End. BladeD:'What are you talking about? The match hasn't even taken place! And Carl Cash is going to win...' Everyone stares back at BladeD, while Jesse Ramey approaches him... *Twenty minutes later...* BladeD climbs out of the green dumpster and brushes the discarded fruit shavings off of his body. BladeD:'Dammit, that's the third club tonight...' The End points to the middle of the crowd, more of a wave about 180 degrees in front of him. The End:'But enough about me, there's someone else of importance in attendance tonight. I have no idea where he is... Mani Shigeru! He was GW's first Oriental World Champion, though he now he's signed with DWO. Give him a hand!' The circle surrounding Mani Shigeru spreads out a little, everyone clapping for him. Tim Walden, sitting at the bar about five feet to The End's left, holds up his right arm as far as his tight white coat will allow and shouts. Walden:'Speech!' Cianan, lounged at the bar next to Walden, is plainly dressed in denim shorts and an LA Lakers jersey. Cianan:'Dumbass...' Mani Shigeru approaches the bar and hops up on it. He stands there for a few moments. It's not that he doesn't know what to say, but being mute, he can't really say anything. The people, not really familiar with him, wait for something. With nothing to do, he flexes his bicep muscles through his cybernetic biosuit, green due to the time of night, and begins nodding and pointing at random intervals while pacing back and forth along the bar. Meanwhile, in the back... Chef:'THESE ORDERS ARE BACKING UP!' PCW Hall of Famer, The Chef, is stacking a trout flambe in a lemon butter sauce on top of a rare T-Bone steak with a side of couscous and asparagus. The plates are stacked two feet above the level of the 4 foot tall tabletop. Semore Smoke, his glazen eyes staring right through Chef, just stands there nodding. Chef:'I can't keep making food if you don't get it out there! I bet there's tables waiting two hours for their food, why aren't you taking it to them?' Semore just shrugs his shoulders. Smoke:'I dunno, I wrote it down on my list of things to do. The first thing was smoke weed, and...' There is a pause for ten seconds with Smoke gigglinig and scratching his chin in thought, before he brings his head back up from the floor to Chef's eyes and says: Smoke:'Actually, I can't remember anything after that.' The kitchen door flies open, and Smoke runs out. There are a number of French curses coming from the kitchen, and a rolling pin, cheese grater, and 12-inch pan-handled skillet fly out the door after Smokey. Done with his silent 'speech,' Mani Shigeru is about to leave the bar, but someone brings up a point to his attention. Violator:'I am the Violator!''Prepare for Total Violation.' Shigeru stays his position on the bar, turning tothe 7-foot-tall Violator, who has climbed up onto the bar as well. The DWO star responds the only way he can, initiating a debate. Shigeru:'....' Violator: *With conviction*'Prepare for Total Violation.' Shigeru:'....' Violator: *Slight confusion*'I am... the Violator?' Shigeru:'....' Violator: *Epiphanical*'Prepare for Total Violation!' Asylum member and former PCW Tag-Team Champion Nerezza just stares at the two from the back of the room, Rachel Leigh Cook and Selma Blair hanging on either arm wearing the same black blouse/mini-skirt combination. Nerezza:'I am so freaking confused right now.' Cianan:'Join the club.' Lovecraft:'Just drink the confusion away. Enough alcohol and anyone makes sense.' The End is getting congratulations on his win from Rent-A-Hero (who wears his Spiderman outfit and had to beg to be let into the club), when William Powell accosts the PCW Continental Champion. Powell:'I've seen you on television.' RAH:'Yes, good citizen, I am quite the popular wrestler for PCW...' Powell:'No... I mean Japanese television. You're Mang-A-Hero.' Rent-A-Hero adopts a panicky tone of voice and stutters his words. RAH:'What? What are you-- no, I'm not... where did you hear? I'm not Mang-A-Hero!' Powell smiles and nods. Powell:'I knew it was you. No one else would take on such a ridiculous gimmick...' Powell has more to say, but he is stopped in speech... MAH:'I would appreciate, Sir Politician, if you would kindly refrain from confusing the Crusader of American Comic Book Magic -- Rent-A-Hero -- with the Defender of the Manga and Anime tradition... namely, myself, Mang-A-Hero! For we are as separate as night and day, Superman and Jubei from Ninja Scroll, as different as Transformers is from Robotech or Macross...' Powell scratches his head and becomes apologetic. Powell:'I'm sorry, man, didn't mean to get things mixed up. See ya.' As Powell walks off, MAH shrinks about two inches in height and loses two inches in width, losing the mask and the white spandex outfit to go to blue-and-yellow X-Men jumpsuit, with a plain face and bushy black hair upon his head. He shakes RAH's hand. RAH:'Thank you, X-Man Morph, I owe you a gargantuan Super-Saiyan favor, more than Galactus could bestow upon a being....' Morph shrugs. Morph:'No problem.' With that, the X-Man walks out of the club. Of course, as he exits, bright lights shine upon him... Sentinel:'X-Man Morph, you are under arrest... Surrender yourself and you shall not be harmed.' Morph:'Aw, crap! Not this again...' Back with The End, Tim Walden presents him with a piece of paper. The End crumples it up and throws it away. The End:'Let's try this again. Why don't you TELL the blind man what you want?' Walden:'I tried to be discreet, but if you want it that way. This party is a taxable item under the 'Promo Party Tax' statute in Article 5, Section 17 of the PCW tax bylaws that I just created right now.' The End:'Promo?' Walden points to a camera, even though The End can't see it. Walden:'There's cameras filming this, so it's a promo, and we can tax it.' The End shrugs. The End:'You can't bring your own cameras and tax me for a promo.' Walden:'And what are you going to do about it?' The End:'Nothing, really. But I would like to discuss this further, come into the office with me.' The End walks Tim Walden over to a single door and opens it up. He allows Walden to go through first. Walden steps through the door and he stands in the alley, BladeD sitting on the ground. The backdoor slams behind him and is locked. Walden quickly regains his composure and spins around to face BladeD, pointing at him while holding the ever-growing book of PCW tax bylaws. Walden:'You! You work for PCW... I assume... You're sitting on the ground, so I'm going to have to fine you on our 'Sitting in filth' tax.' Around the other side, the Suburban Ninja Clan is guarding the front door. All forty are running bouncer duty tonight, 10 outside wielding anything from claws to hook-swords to shuriken and three-pronged sai. There really aren't anyone else showing up, they just keep the Uprising out, all of them are dressed in red from head-to-toe, as this part of town is frequented by the Bloods. Statuz Quo:'Whatchu mean we can't get in?' Ninja #13:'We've strick orders not to allow any members of Black Attack into the club tonight.' Dante Jackson:'We ain't no Black Attack, crackas!' Rush Mason shakes his head, his arms crossed as he stands away from the others by the curb. Rush:'Do you two ever try NOT to keep up the stereotypical thug black image?' Also turned away, Vanilla Skillz and the Masked Mullet go across the street to the McDonald's, where the rejects are congregating and having their own 'Big Mac Party.' Of course, not one of them has any money and there will be blood spilt when it comes time to pay... Back inside, Syko is sulking, taking shots from a tall glass. He looks depressed, disheveled. The perfect image of the rummy down-on-his luck. Only, he's sober and imbibing water like it was whiskey. Syko: *With slurred speech*'Gimme a-muh-ver!' Woody Harelson, wearing hemp pants (Semore Smoke stole his shirt to light it up), shakes his head. Harelson:'I think you've had enough to drink, Mr. Goblin, sir.' Syko throws the glass at the bottles behind the bar, causing the shelf to fall and the glass to crash to the floor, breaking into hundreds of pieces. Syko:'I'll tell you when I've had enough!' In the back, Chef slips some GHB into a New England Clam Chowder and calls Boca Del Inferno over to pick it up. Chef:'You see that midget out there?' Chef points to Syko. Chef:'Take him this food, and when he's finished, he's all yours.' Boca eyes Syko hungrily and licks his lips. He snatches the soup away and hurries over to Syko... Amber Token and Jackson Rust are talking with The End about his win. Rust is wearing a white rugby uniform, while Amber has on a purple V-Cut shirt and blue hot-pants. Token:'So, you're successful in PCW and PURE. Must be nice.' Rust:'You are so lucky you won in the GTT. I'll kick your ass next time.' The End pats Jackson Rust on his head. The End:'I'm sure you will. And hey, tell me what else happens in the dream, okay?' Amber Token breaks in. Token:'Got any advice for an aspiring superstar?' The End shrugs. The End:'Find guys like Rust, and then build your career on shoving their feet into their mouths.' Token:'Ah.' The End:'Guys like Jon Kano. No one cares about him, yet he tries to remain in the public eye, and how does he do it? By wrestling people who haven't been good at wrestling since Clinton was still in office. Guys like Carl Cash. He's living in the past, which means that he can't ever concentrate on the beatings he receives today. Guys like Scorpion. I have no idea who he is, but then again, neither does anyone else.' The End finishes his conversation with Amber Token and goes off to have a good time and party with the others in attendance. Perhaps it's cocky to celebrate winning a match that hasn't happened yet, but what ever happened to self-confidence? The End versus Carl Cash versus Scorpion versus Jon Kano. The #1 contendership to the Extreme Title is on the line. Catch Wednesday Turmoil to see it The End was wrongly premature in his partying, or if he was just getting things started early...
