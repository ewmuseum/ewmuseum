XK05 : “The Ground Below.”

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/08/02 XK05 : “The Ground Below.” Location : Compaq Center in Houston, Texas.Date : 9th August, 10:05pm. “GET ME THE FUCK DOWN FROM HERE YOU PEONS!“ Crucifixion... The method of death dealt to Jesus Christ himself. But when the son of God walked through Gethsemane before being arrested and forced to carry his cross to his death, he knew he had been betrayed.. He knew one of his disciples would give him up.... Xavier Kannon has no Judas, only himself. For he is not being Crucified for the sins of others... This is all of his own making, as Tyler Nelson has said. Kannon’s greed, arrogance and callousness has led to him being hoisted up high on a cross above the ring. Xavier : “IF I AM NOT DOWN FROM HERE IN TWO MINUTES YOU ARE ALL FIRED AND YOU AND YOUR FAMILIES CAN FUCKING STARVE!!!“ He isn’t a happy Scientologist. 25 feet above the ring he hangs... Rope burning his wrists and angle where he has tried to free himself even through there is nowhere to go but down... High above the ground below. In the upper eshalons of this arena the sound is different.. it’s muffled. The fans who are staying behind to see the Champion on the Crucifix create a dull murmur following the huge pop they gave the sight when the lights first came on. “We’re trying Mr Kannon.... We’re trying...” It was a mixed night for the Scientologist... His momentum was snowballing on after he destroyed Trigger.. Sending a message to his rookie challenger that he was only chosen because he wanted a break.. But the acts that occurred just 2 minutes ago could not have been predicted by even the mind of this most cynical Scientologist. While others Crucified by the Dark Age were nailed to the cross out of bloodlust... Kannon was only tied. This Crucifixion was symbolic as Tyler Nelson wants to take something far more precious to Kannon than his blood. His power. Xavier : ““WELL FUCKING TRY HARDER YOU PEON!!!“ But Tyler Nelson and Xavier Kannon think very differently. When the Dark CEO made his challenge up to the cross that housed Kannon, it was about saving Kannon. Tyler put all he had on the line to entice Xavier into “Salvation in the Cell” because he wants the Scientologist to see the error of his ways. But in saving Kannon he may end up destroying himself.... For if Kannon accepts the match and wins at Third Glory.... He’s unstoppable. He would be the CEO. He and the Phantom Booker would own all of PCW. His wife would still be Commissioner and he would still hold the World Title. Talk about “An offer too good to refuse”. “Are you, like, STILL up there?!?!?” He now looks down past his prone body to the ring below where his wife Eleanor now stands, looking up concerned at her husband. Xavier : “YES MY LITTLE POON PUPPY.. I AM STILL UP HERE...” Hanging high up in the arena he at least has time to think, when his brain isn’t preoccupied by the burning of the rope marks. In his mind he is not being punished by Tyler Nelson. He is not being saved by Tyler Nelson... He is being rewarded. There is no salvation for those not interested in it. You cannot help someone who doesn’t think he needs it and you cannot teach someone the error of their ways if they believe they are right. He steamrollers over the company. Does whatever he wants. And he is presented with a chance to own it all. Whoever said doing the right thing paid off was an idiot. Xavier : “BECAUSE THESE IDIOTS STILL HAVEN’T MANAGED TO LOWER ME DOWN.... IN HUBBARD’S NAME HOW HARD CAN IT BE YOU MORONS!?!?!?“ The workers try to ignore the berating they are getting from the Champion and try and figure out how to lower the crucifix.. All around them camera flashes going off, fans snapping a picture as if the Scientologist was an animal in a zoo. Xavier : “STOP TAKING PHOTOS YOU PEONS!!!” Even in his predicament Kannon is his same old self. Even when helpless he insults others... Workers. Fans. The only people left around him now the Dark Age and commentators have gone. As always his brain then goes into overdrive... While many wrestlers do not think enough, Kannon thinks too much. What if they never get him down? What if he is still dangling on the cross.. The symbol of Christianity.... The Crucifix who’s very proximity to the Champion makes him want to burn like a creature of the night would touched by a Christian symbol that repulses him so much.... What if he doesn’t make it to Turmoil to fight Trigger... Would the Infinite Warrior become PCW Champion? He doesn’t like this thought. Xavier : “GET ME THE FUCK DOWN NOW!!!!!!!!“ Has Kannon spread himself too thin? He has the Dark Age.... Trigger.... And Akiyama to deal with. The band of blood thirsty Goths wanting to show him salvation. A hungry young rookie who lives pure and fights clean wanting to realise his dream. The Neo Void God purely wanting Gold and to destroy the Champion. All wanting to beat him for their own reason. Salvation. Ambition. Destruction. “Hey.... I’ve got your belt...” In the ring below Eleanor stands with the PCW Title over her shoulder... An act probably meant to reassure Xavier only making him more frustrated... Taunted by the belt he owns but cannot reach. But after Tyler Nelson’s words just a few minutes ago.. The belt has taken on a different meaning... The belt is now a Pawn in his game with the CEO... It is something else he will throw into the middle... To up the ante with... In the hand where everything is on the line. The belt will either win him PCW or lose him everything. He mutters to himself. Xavier : “Correct....MY Belt...” He wasn’t going to let Trigger win it even if they had to drive the rookie to this arena and lift the ring up 25 feet. He wasn’t going to let Akiyama get near it.... He NEEDS this belt not for it’s meaning. Not for it’s stature.. But as a gambling chip. Something to throw on the table at Third Glory. He knows he can beat Trigger... Trigger is a sparring partner. Young, hungry and someone who will learn from Xavier... But as seen in the last two hours he lacks the meanstreak, the viciousness to beat Kannon... He has heart.. But so did Jeremy Howard, so did Duke Williams, so did Miyagawa. Heart doesn’t win matches. If it did, Xavier may try to get some. Still he stares down at his wife, but it is not her he is focusing on but the belt. His belt. Xavier : “As it will be after Third Glory... When I own this place... When I am the CEO...” The Scientologist cares not for PCW history. The names of it’s legends do not interest him. He has no respect for it’s veterans and no belief in it’s rookies. He was not around when Dawning kicked off PCW, or for New Dawning... But he will make his mark at Third Dawning. Can he possibly refuse Tyler Nelson’s challenge? Or will the thoughts of power lead to the realisation of defeat? Will being so close to ultimate power cause him to take his eye of the ball against Trigger? Will facing the Infinite Warrior be his Infinite Mistake? Xavier : “When I have it all and Nelson.. Trigger... Akiyama....” He doesn’t believe it for a second. Xavier : “Have nothing.” He jolts... The rope is cut and the cross descends. Plummeting to the ground below.
