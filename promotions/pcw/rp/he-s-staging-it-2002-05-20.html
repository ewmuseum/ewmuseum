He's Staging It

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-20-02 [Fade In] [It’s Thursday, in the middle of the afternoon. It’s been raining for a while, and it’s just now begun to subside. It’s still rather dark outside, and so virtually little light seeps in from the cracked curtains in room 112 of the Holiday Inn where “Scandalous” Ryan “Streak” Stephens and his wife Dana have been staying for quite some time now.] [Dana is sitting in a chair at the round table near the window reading a People magazine by lamplight. Her bare feet are in the chair, and her knees are pulled up to her chest so that you can not see whatever emblem may be on her T-shirt. Ryan is on the floor with his back propped against the foot of the bed. He has the channel changer in his right hand and is flicking through the channels trying to find something mildly entertaining when a though crosses his mind. He refrains from looking up, remaining focussed on the channels he flips through, but he inquires to his wife.] Ryan: Dana! Dana! [she looks up from her magazine] Are there any of those People’s Court shows or anything like that on television this time of day? Dana: I don’t really think so, but I don’t watch TV that much so I’m not sure. Ryan: Well I saw where Makonie was having a trial, and I didn’t figure that it would be on television except for the clips they show on the Prime Time Wrestling channel, but I thought maybe I could learn more about the court system. Dana: [looking back down at her magazine] I’m pretty sure they only come on in the mornings and late at night, but you might find one. [looks back up] Why do you care anyhow? If he stays in jail, he’ll forfeit and you’ll get a free win. Ryan: Bah, I don’t want him to forfeit. I want to wrestle him so I will know what he has before my big match with him on Gold and Glory. Besides some of the clips I’ve been seeing of the Makonie trial are pretty bizarre to me. I think that perhaps he’s staging the trial’s events to gain publicity or something. I should probably write a rumor about it. [He reaches up from his spot on the floor and slides his ‘idea’ notepad off of the top of the television, and then begins scanning the tabletop in which the TV sits for a writing utensil, but doesn’t see one. He places the notepad on the floor next to him.] Dana: [she sighs] You and those rumors! What makes you think he’s staging it? Seems like a lot of trouble to go through. Ryan: Well I mean there was already the case in which he hadn’t so much as mentioned being in a bar fight and having to go to court to anyone, especially back when it happened during is NTICW days. [Ah yes, now he’s spotted a pen, over on the table by his wife’s magazine.] Dana: Yeah, that seemed funny to me as well. Ryan: Further more don’t you think that if a Nazi clad freak like Makonie fought someone as prolific as Denzel Washington in bar we’d all have heard about it, and yet we haven’t heard anything until now, almost a year later. Hey toss me that pen will you! [pointing at it] Dana: Well, yes, that also seems odd, but perhaps it’s not the same Denzel Washington. [She grabs the pen and tosses it to him, it’s a good throw. He catches the pencil with his left hand, never taking the channel changer out of his right hand. They’re very nonchalant as if they throw and catch stuff all of the time. Perhaps they do, both former baseball/softball players respectively.] Ryan: Maybe not, but how many Denzel Washington’s do you know? Besides that I have further proof that Makonie may just be pulling everyone’s chain. Dana: And what’s that? Ryan: He claims the judge got up from his stand and handed the pictures to the jury members to look at. I haven’t watched much court TV, but I’ve watched Judge Mathis and Judge Judy both before, and NEVER EVER do they stand up from their bench during the case. I mean isn’t that what the bailiff is for anyhow? [She’s trying to seem interested, but really she could careless. She just wants to read her magazine. She gets tired of hearing about wrestling every single day.] Dana: [sighs] I think so. Ryan: [he’s to into it to realize she’s doesn’t care] Not only that, but the pictures apparently are the greatest, most coolest pictures ever because they show fingerprints on Denzel’s coat in the picture. Besides that the pictures show proff. Now I’m not quite sure what proff is, but it must be some really technical law term because I’m unfamiliar with it. Dana: Baby, I think that the pictures are separate from the finger prints on the coat, and your sarcasm is tiring me. [She smiles at him, in a polite you need to shut up kind of way. It’s finally beginning to dawn on him that she doesn’t want to listen to his verbal tirade, and so he stops talking for a minute and continues to flip through some channels.] Ryan: Baby! Dana: Yes Ryan? Ryan: After he went to jail he set on a stool! They don’t even have stools in jail cells. Dana: [takes deep breath so not to lose her patients.] Enough already Ryan, you’re reading way to deep into all of this. Why do you have to prove him wrong anyway? Why don’t you just beat him in the ring, and prove all the people who didn’t predict you to win wrong. That would seem like a far better accomplishment to me. [Stephens has been put in his place and he knows it. He’s still not ready to let it die, but he doesn’t want to agitate his wife any more. In a muffled tone he speaks…] Ryan: I just hope he gets out of jail for Fusion so that I can pick up some of his tendencies, and better learn his style before the pay per view. Dana: I’m sure he’ll find a way to get out. A lot of wrestlers seem to just wave their magic wand and get out of jail. For that matter do whatever they want. I mean you seem to be one of the few realistic ones. Some of these wrestlers seem to travel great distances in very short times. Other wrestlers arrive in limousines when they are mere mid-carders and don’t make that kind of money. And then you have people like Akiyama and his Neo-Void crap. He’s so full of shit. [Stephens’ eyes light up as he hears his wife curse. Her curse words are always few and far between so when she’s cussing she’s either tired of listening to him or trying to get a point across. In this instance it’s both. He knows better than to mess with her when she starts cussing.] Ryan: You’re right baby! You’re so very right! Dana: Are you mocking me? Ryan: No! Dana: [she smiles at him] I didn’t think so. Oh and honey! Ryan: Yes Dana? Dana: He still thinks you’re a low carder even though you’re the number four contender to the television title, which I assume would make you a mid-carder. So I’m changing my mind, why don’t you just go ahead and prove him wrong in the ring of course, as well as all those doubters. [He goes to open his mouth, but she chimes in first.] Dana: Shhh! I’m trying to read baby! [She smiles to herself, but he does not see. He slumps his shoulders a little, unaware that she’s really not all that annoyed, but merely messing with him. He adverts his attention back to the television. He became so flustered he forgot that he was going to jot down a rumor in his notepad. We fade out.]
