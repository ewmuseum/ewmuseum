...To Repent, Divine...

Date: not entered Man: Forgive me, father…for I have sinned. =©=Say hello to Jake. Here he stands in this booth within the St. Mary’s holiness Episcopal Catholic Zion AME Church of Christ over on 86th street doing what he feels is the right thing…much like Duke has been doing. He stands in a white polo shirt and tan khaki pants inside the dark booth with his head lowered. His black hair dangles down his face past the tip of his sharp nose. Something must have had him feeling guilty. At least he felt guilty for his sins…instead of embracing them like one Malice Man. Now…back to the action.=©= Voice: The father’s away, lad. He’s taking himself a break after his last confession. But I’m here to listen to your sins, my son. =©=Jake looked off to the side, a bit uncomfortable, but that didn’t stop him from continuing. =©= Jake: Okay…let’s try this again. Forgive me, MOTHER…for I have sinned. Mother: Go on, my child. Jake: Well…it’s been about three months since my last confession. I’m not a very religious man…some would call me atheist considering what I believe. Mother: You’re not alone, my son. There are many heathen souls out there like yours that have no faith in what they cannot encounter for themselves…they’re called fools. Jake: O…kay…well anyway, I was married three months ago to this wonderful Catholic girl named Katie. She’s so great. Mother: This is your first marriage, is it not? Jake: Yes…how did you know? Mother: You’re still capable of falling in love. That’ll change about your sixth marriage. =©=Jake took a slight look up at the divider separating his face from the mother’s face. =©= Jake: Excuse me, mother? Mother: Pay an old Irish woman her ramblings, lad. Continue….you were saying about your wife Katie? Jake: Oh…yes. Well things have started out great. But unfortunately, they had to get complicated. Mother: That’s natural, dear. Life always gets complicated. Mankind is too feeble minded to fully comprehend the simplicities of their existence. Jake: I feel that too…sometimes I wish I could just know the meaning of life, and everything will be all right. Mother: The answer lies within God, my son. Go on… Jake: Yes…well there’s this woman at work…nice woman…very nice woman…really very nice woman…REALLY very nice woman. Mother: I GET THE POINT. =©=Jake cleared his throat as he caught himself. =©= Jake: Sorry, mother. Anyway, she had came on to me one day…and instead of doing the right thing…I gave into temptation and cheated on my wife! =©= Jake sighed as he lowered his head back down, his shame exhumed. After a few seconds of silence, he looked back up towards the window. =©= Jake: Mother….? Mother: Sooooo……what’s the problem here now? =©= Jake stared flabbergasted at the window at the mother’s words. =©= Jake: Did you hear me, mother? I committed ADULTERY!! Mother: You’re kidding me, right? Please tell me you did more than that… Jake: Mother!!! What are you saying? Mother: Look, lad, do you know how many infidelity confessions we get going through here on a daily basis? At first, it’s all “OOOOOH, you filthy skank!!” but now after hearing like a zillion of them it’s no big deal. =©=This is where Jake goes dumbfounded here, folks. =©= Jake: What? Mother: Yeah…according to someone I know, if it feels good to you, it’s not wrong! Jake:…I’m not getting what you’re saying. Mother: It’s like this here, junior…in today’s day and age, society had abandoned morals and standards. It’s all about doing what you feel is right…not about what has been mandated to be the “right thing to do”…you get it? It’s all about having the right to do whatever you want because it’s your life. You want to cheat on your wife and ruin your marriage, be my guest. You want to smoke weed, drink liquor, and screw yourself up, go right ahead. You want to go around shooting off your mouth like you have verbal diarrhea…then let the sh!t fly. Jake: MOTHER!!! Mother: Sorry, son…pardon my language, but I sometimes tend to forget that I’m a nun. But I hope you see my point. If you feel like you want to do it, then do it. Understand? Jake: I think so…this is all so confusing. Mother: You want a little advice? Jake: That’s what I’m here for, you know. Mother: If you stand back and think…you notice that everything that you want to do because you feel is right…is always…ALWAYS…the wrong thing to do. Just because you have a right to does not mean you should do it. You could smoke all the weed, drink all the booze, screw all the skirts, and eat all the pop tarts and eggos you want…but in the long run, it wont prepare you for the long fight ahead. Jake: You’re saying if I think it’s right…it’s wrong? Mother: Right. Jake: Then it’s right? Mother: Wrong. Jake: Okay…you lost me again. =©= The Mother Superior sighed from within her booth. Jake held his head in his hands as she took another stab at explaining it to him. =©= Mother: If you think it’s right…it’s wrong, and you shouldn’t do it or it’ll mess you up later on…got it? Jake: Ohhh…I see… Mother: Good. Jake: So what should I do about my wife? Mother: Does she know? Jake: No. Mother: What do you feel you should do? Jake: Part of me wants to tell her…but the other half of me wants to not tell her. I’m not sure what do to. Mother: That’s natural. You’re human…and error is just inside of you from birth. It’s technically not your fault you screwed up…but it’s your genetic makeup to blame. Best thing you can do now is just do what you feel is right… Jake: But then that’ll be the wrong thing to do. Mother: You’re screwed either way, kid…don’t knock a easy solution. =©=Jake heard the mother preparing to step out, but his voice stopped her. =©= Jake: Am I forgiven? Mother: Pardon? Jake: Are my sins forgiven? Mother: What does an atheist care if God forgives his sins or not? Jake: In this one case…it would make me feel better if I was forgiven. =©= Another low sigh from Mother dearest. =©= Mother: You are forgiven… Jake: Thank you, mother. I feel a lot better now. Mother: You should. It’s been said… “…To repent, divine…”…repenting is god-like. Jake: Heh…sometimes I wish I were godlike. Mother: (muttering) Fat chance, flat-scan. Jake: What? Mother: Nothing…nothing at all. Jake: Thank you, mother. Have a nice day. =©= Jake stepped out of the confessional a new man…with a burden off his shoulders…or at least felt if a burden was off his shoulders. A few silent minutes passed by before the mother stepped out of her confessional. =©= Cyclonica: Thought that mortal moron would never leave. He sounds just as foolish as Duke did earlier. Stupid human ramblings…all he did was reiterate what we said and commentated with mundane rants. Really did most of our verbal job for us…didn’t he? =©=Standing against the wall next to the confessional booth and just coming into focus was the Neo Void God. He was vertical this time…standing at his full height with his arms folded across his chest and his eyes closed. His head nodded slowly at the words of the Divine Prophetess. =©= Cyclonica: Duke’s been asking a lot about who’s the bad guy here. There’s no confusion about it between us. Humanity’s flawed thinking labels him as the good guy…and us the bad guy because we remind them that morals still exist in this world of immoral ways. As if we ever cared what a mortal thought about us. He’s not even putting any physical effort into training for this bout, Akiyama. He’s just loafing about mortally running is gums like usual. That’s just Divine, though. If he thinks that he doesn’t need to stretch his limbs or train before our match, then you’re not held responsible if he breaks a hip or if his back goes out. Hell…if he has a heart attack in the center of the ring, place a foot on him for the count and we’ll say the Void took him from within. No arguments there. =©= She shook her head, trailing a long stream of black and ice blue hair behind her like a silk veil. =©= Cyclonica: He really made himself look bad with that assumption thing…All we can do now is hope that he repents for his crimes verbally against you, Akiyama. Then maybe he’d be one step away from being human and closer to being Divine…then at least he’d be a bit of a challenge for you. You’re going to be the bigger man anyway after you defeat him…after this is over, don’t forget to repent for wasting time on veteran jobbers past retirement. =©= Cyclonica started walking towards the entrance of the church. Akiyama followed her down the line of pews towards the doors silently. =©= Cyclonica: …So says the Divine Prophetess. =©=
