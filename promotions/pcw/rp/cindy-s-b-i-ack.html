Cindy's B(i)ack

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 Jan &#039;03 The scene opens up with Chet King sitting backstage in his dressing room following a loss to Dante Jackson via a camera to the head. He’s sitting on the couch we saw him sitting on earlier in the show when we saw the return to PCW of Chet’s manager (Manager, not girlfriend) Cindy Bi. Chet King: I can’t believe I lost to Dante. Chet punches the couch in frustration. Chet King: Shanghaied is meant to be my day, but Dante beat me. Chet once again punches the couch in frustration. Chet King: If I see Dante again tonight I’m going to knock him out with a camera shot to his head soo he can get a taste of his own medicine. For a third time Chet punches the couch in frustration. As Chet does this the shot pans out and we see sitting next to Chet on the couch is none other then Cindy Bi, who is wearing a white t-shirt, a black mini skirt, and has her hair streaked with many different colors. Cindy Bi: Don’t worry about it Chet. You don’t need to look for Dante, you’ll get your hands on him again soon enough. Chet King: I haven’t heard anything about receiving a rematch. Are you trying to get me one? Cindy Bi: Well I’ve been talking with the front office and negotiating matches for you. Chet King: And you got me a rematch with Dante Jackson? Cindy Bi: Well, not quite. But I have got you a match with Tim Walden on Turmoil. Chet doesn’t look too happy about this. Chet King: Tim Walden?!? Your first night back as my manager and you get me a match with Tim Walden? I don’t want to face Tim Walden! I want to face Dante Jackson! Cindy Bi: Look Chet, you may get to get your hands on Dante once more. Chet King: When’s that going to happen? Cindy Bi: When I was talking to the front office they informed me that this year, once again, there will be a twenty man battle royal for the PCW World Championship. Which means that if both you and Dante make it in and you’re in the ring together at the same time then you can get your hands on him then. A smile appears on Chet’s face. Cindy Bi: You’re happy with that? Happy to wait until March Madness to get your hands on Dante? Chet King: Heck no, but for some reason I know longer care about fighting Dante again. I guess that’s because my sense of care seems to be on finally winning the PCW World Championship. Cindy Bi: And on defeating Walden on Tuesday? Chet King: Heh, well that shouldn’t be too hard, I’ve never had trouble defeating Walden before. Cindy Bi: You’ve never fought him one on one before. Chet King: But I’ve defeated him in at least one triangle match. Cindy Bi: You shouldn’t get too cocky Chet, one on one is a different thing. Chet looks a bit shocked about what Cindy has just said. Chet King: Come on Cindy! Have you already forgotten about what goes on PCW? Cindy Bi: What do you mean? Chet King: You should remember that Walden doesn’t win, Walden loses. He loses! Therefore he will lose on Tuesday! Cindy starts to laugh. Cindy Bi: Oops! I guess I really have been away from PCW too long since I forgot a simple fact like that. Chet King: Don’t worry about it. Cindy Bi: Chet, how come you only did one part of ‘The History of Shanghaied’ show. I thought it was meant to be a two part show. Chet King: What happened was the network executives thought, considering the recent trend, that Shanghaied would be late, and we’d have to air the second part when Shanghaied was meant to start as a delaying tactic so that viewers wouldn’t get angry about the pay per view being late. But unfortunately the show was on time so part two never got to air. Cindy Bi: Yeah, I hate when shows come on time. Chet King: I know, and I’m a bit worried that this might be a trend. I don’t want to have shows coming on the air when they’re supposed to. Cindy Bi: It’s one of the big fears that I have. Chet King: Oh well, I guess having shows coming on the air when they’re supposed to is something we’re just going to have to get used. Cindy Bi: Hmm, so Chet, you want to hear what’s been going on in my life while I’ve been off PCW TV? Chet King: I’d love to, but this promos almost out of time, and I wouldn’t want viewers to miss the excitement of you telling me what’s been going on, so how about you save it for some other time when I do a promo? Cindy Bi: But then I’ll never get to talk about what’s been happening, after all you seem to be doing a promo once a blue moon these days. Chet King: Are you saying I have a low work rate? Cindy Bi: Yes, a very poor one. One that could get you fired if your not careful. Chet King: What are you talking about? Cindy Bi: I’m just saying that your lucky that I’m back Chet. I’m going to get rid of your low promo rate and make you pump on many promos, and then you’re going to go climbing back up the ladder of success starting first with Walden, and then the world! Chet King: Then the world? What are you talking about? Cindy Bi: World title I meant. Chet King: Oh yeah, that’s right. First Walden then the world! The camera fades out while focusing on Cindy Bi for a good twenty seconds, allowing all those horny men (and women) out there to relieve some tension.
