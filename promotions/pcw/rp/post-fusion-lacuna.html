Post Fusion Lacuna

Date: After Fusion So was that it? One minute, that was all it took for her to complete a full three hundred and sixty degree turn over the whole matter of Jade. She was no less than a hypocrite, the lowest form of life. It wasn’t the principle of the thing; the fact she was again going to team with Jade made next to no difference to her, it was that she was the one who suggested it. How could she be capable of such a thing? She was never the one who would go back on her word, especially over something she felt so strongly about. She had hated Jade, hated her with every single ounce of muscle sinew in her whole bloody body! Heh, maybe she still did, you didn’t have to like someone to realise that you made a good team. She ran a hand tentatively through her raven black hair and began to pace around in a neat circle around the hapless Brook, who had been dragged in as a listening device. Brook’s head had been crudely bandaged; he had refused medical attention after Fusion and had attempted to do it himself, thus hiding his balding scalp from view. He was perched in an overly large leather armchair in the middle of an overly large room in Lacuna’s overly large house. “Please stop with the pacing, your making me dizzy” The well cultivated tones of Brook rang out with an authority, his BBC English putting forwards the stereotype that all Englishmen indeed sounded like Geoffrey from ‘Fresh Prince’ “No! I’m trying to think here!” she shouted back angrily, biting into her lip “Its hard for the bread winners you know?” “Technically…” He smiles”… I do work for my money, hence making me a breadwinner. One nil” His smile broadens to a greater extent, causing the aging skin on his cheeks to crack slightly. Lacuna stops pacing and glares at Brook. She doesn’t like to be stopped, and so she made the feeling felt by planting her booted right foot hard into the shin of Brook with a nasty thudding sound. “Don’t be smart with me, I’m really not in the mood for such pleasantries. I am sure you will not do it again, am I right?” Lacuna pats him on the head and continues to pace slowly around him. Brook new better than to reply to that; in her twisted meanings, that question was somehow rhetorical. He didn’t want his other shin coming into contact with her wicked boot. “Why did I do it?” She says, breaking the silence with her neat and tidy accent “Kick me? Well I was acting a little big headed…”Lacuna interrupts with a slap around the back of his bandaged head, making him jump to his feet in pain. She laughs like some strange sort of hyena and sits him back down gently, making sure to comfort him sarcastically. “I meant say I would ally with Jade, it’s just not like me” “I wouldn’t hardly know, I was locked in the boot of the stupid car for about two hours” The sarcasm in his voice was evident to all with ears, he made as little effort to hide it as he did to hide his baldness; none what so ever. He flinched heavily as Lacuna raised a hand as if to slap him again, instead she patted him on the back in an odd friendly moment from the Englishwoman. She smiled broadly as she began to speak again. “You see, that’s why I don’t fire you, you stupid lump. Your actually quite funny in a sarcastic sort of way” She stopped pacing and sat down in the identical chair seated opposite to him “But I still don’t understand the Jade Issue” “Maybe there is nothing to understand here” Her face adopted a frown “I don’t follow you” “What I am saying is, maybe you have convinced yourself that you don’t like her. You know how much you got along when you helped her that time. You enjoyed fighting her, fighting with her and terrorising her. Maybe you do like her except you don’t want to” This was a tad too brave of Brook and he knew it as soon as the final words had left his lips. Lacuna did not like to be questioned, and it usually resulted in an angry mood swing “She STOLE from me on this first bloody time we met! It’s impossible to like someone like her! I cant believe you! You just don’t support me! Hmph!” But as she slammed the door closed and leant on its wooden strength, she couldn’t help but think he might actually be right.
