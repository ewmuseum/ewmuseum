Haley...Is That You?

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/21/03 Everyone says that it’s just a given…that it’s almost impossible for Matt Jackson not to win the Raising Star Title at Supremacy and he once again showed why on Fusion, picking up another win…setting himself at 5-0. Even with everyone’s best efforts, they seem to come up just a bit short in the end…but everyone is still trying not giving up hope for themselves yet. The string of wins has done nothing more, but have people gunning for Matt now, wanting to knock him off his throne and show that Matt isn’t anything more then a product of hype. Three men get their chance at Supremacy, get their chance to knock the only undefeated man out of the competition and claim the title that he’s worked so hard for. Taking what doesn’t belong to them, but getting the chance nonetheless…it’s fate that they are in the position they are in and it’s fate Matt’s where he’s at right now. Still Matt might not be at his usual form, after Fusion and that horrible beating he took from Ivan, causing his face to be burned and blistered and the back of his head to have countless numbers of stitches and even though his match at Turmoil wasn’t as grueling, Edwards still managed to knock a few of the stitches and blisters open…giving the whole field a major break…a chance. You know I thought I would hate Ivan for what he did to me…causing me the pain that I am currently in, but I respect him and respect what he did. Coming all the way to Kabul to defend Tempest…proving that he deserves her and showing that he is still a master of PCW, without being here for a year. I respect you Ivan, not the things you stand for or believe in, but I respect you. Matt Jackson is sprawled out on the large king sized bed in his hotel room, the sheets still perfectly made and unwrinkled, as he lays atop it, eyes closed and a white bandage wrapped around his head. He is wearing his black wrestling tights and no shirt, which reveals a lot of blisters on his barrel of a chest and also shows the cuts and bruises from the constant beating over the past few weeks. Rolling on his side, the Chameleon grabs the back of his head, feeling the soft cotton bandage, he sits up and continues to hold the back of his head. Letting his brown eyes look across the room, he notices the small wooden night stand next to the bed, and directly in front of it, the small black television that is perched atop. Lying back down, his angular face is one of pain, covered in blisters and burn marks. “Why the hell did I try to stand up to Ivan,” he asks aloud. Letting his head gently sit on the pillows that are hidden under the sheets below him. They bring him a bit more comfort then his hands, but not much as the pain still surges through his body, causing him to wince every now and then. “That would be a good question honey…why did you stand up to Ivan?” A female voice asks, cutting through the room and forcing Matt too sit up again and begin to scan his eyes around the room…looking for the source of where the voice came from, hoping that it’s the voice he believes it to be. His eyes dart to the right corner of the room, where the blonde princess is leaning against the power white wall, her arms crossed over her curvy chest, wrinkling her pink Abercrombie tee shirt and her dark denim hip huggers swishing with her constant crossing and uncrossing of her feet, letting her white sneakers rub against each other. Locking into her pale blue eyes, Matt let’s a smile overcome his pained face, showing his perfect white teeth. She just stands leaning against the wall, smiling, like an apparition…making the agent believe that she isn’t really there and this is just his concussion playing a trick on him, forcing him to tell himself, that she really isn’t there in front of him. He can’t tell if it’s true or not though, he wants to just jump up and run over to her, but he also doesn’t want to get over there and have it be just the wall…having her not be there and be out of his life again. At least if it’s just an apparition, he can just lay here and look at her, letting a part of her be by him in a way…letting her be his once more, even if it’s just for a few minutes. She just continues to stand there, a smile splitting her slender face and showing her white teeth. Going against all his beliefs Jackson jumps up, ignoring the pain coming from his 5’8” frame and slowly limps over to her, wishing that this whole thing could be real, but knowing that it’s probably not. Gliding over the soft carpet, Matt slowly makes his way to his princess…wishing that this were real. Each step he makes, he believes that Haley is just going to poof into smoke and make his dream become his reality…his nightmare. The nightmare that is his life without Haley. Standing in front of her, he just stares in disbelief that she’s still standing there, arms crossed over her chest, she cocks her head to the side like a confused puppy, but doesn’t let the smile fall from her pink lips, which shine with the lip gloss she placed on them, just before Matt came too. Brushing the bit of brown hair that hangs down from the bandage, she leans forward and hugs him, squeezing him tight, like a clamp holding onto a piece of wood. Still standing in disbelief, Jackson just hugs her back, feeling her flesh, making sure that this isn’t a dream…it’s reality. “I-I-I can’t believe that you are here,” he whispers into her ear, his voice almost fading out towards the end, as the tears start to stream down his tanned face. Gripping her tighter with each breath, not wanting to let go for anything…hoping that they could just stay like this forever and forget everything that’s happened. It won’t stay like this though, because he knows that he is expected to show up to Supremacy and compete for the Raising Star title. Even with Haley nestled in his arms, the Raising Star title, is fresh on his mind. It’s been imprinted there over the past month, making him want it more and more. The yearning fire burning deep inside of him to claim his prize…his payment for the hard work of the past month, taking all the beatings and gathering all the wins. The two finally stop the embrace and Matt takes a step back to look at his beautiful girl friend and just stares into her eyes, wiping the tears away with his hands, but all the while continuing to smile, as Haley does the exact same, smearing some of the power on her face, but still looking perfect to the operative. Grabbing her hand, the couple walk over to the bed and each take a seat on the end of it, Jackson being very careful, as not to let go of Haley’s hand…worried that he might lose her once again and be forced into the downward spiral of his life…alone. He doesn’t want that, he doesn’t want to win the match at Supremacy, with out her in his corner and he doesn’t want to hold that belt above his head, without her there to help him hold it up. “So why are you here?” He asks softly, letting his voice cut through the tiny bedroom, like a knife through butter. Breaking the silence that stood for so long, taking Haley aback almost like she doesn’t understand his question, staring at him with a distraught look on her face. Hugging him again, she offers: “It’s all over…my dad is giving up. He saw everything you went through to keep me and he is just going to let us be together. Isn’t it great…no more of anything, just you and me.” Nodding his head, the agent continues to hold her hand and stare into her eyes, almost like amazement at the fact that it’s all over, that they can once again be together like they were before…not having some Mark Makonie step in the way of it, or maybe it’s because he doesn’t have to be a part of the skinheads anymore, seeing the filth and wishing that he could have found another way. None of it matters now though, his baby is back and nothing is going to take her away from him a second time…nothing. Just like nothing is going to stop him from claiming the Raising Star title as his own, nothings going to stop his win streak…nothing. No person is going to stand in his way and that’s how it’s been planned, ever since day one, everyone knew this was a possibility. Xavier Kannon wouldn’t have brought him in if it was any different, but this time instead of hurting the PCW, he is keeping it open, tossing a blind eye one way and forcing the CIA to think that nothings happening. “I am so glad you are here and so glad you are with me,” Matt begins. “Winning the Raising Star title without you at Supremacy would have been horrible, but now everything is all right again and in perfect sink.” Brushing her petite hand across his face, Matt winces a bit in pain, as she crosses over the blisters and burns that were placed there by Ivan. The burns that he received from a man who believes in false hopes and beliefs, just like those who are daring to enter into Supremacy with him…believing that they can overcome something that no one has been able to yet. Doing something that no one in PCW has been able to do this month and in fact ever since Matt got to the PCW and that’s beat him in a singles match. Out of the countless number who tried, he stopped them dead in their tracks and now it’s time for him to reign on top. “I watched what I could of you when those bastards had me, they took me to every PCW event and I saw most of your matches,” Haley tells him, twisting her blonde hair on her finger and then letting it fall back into place, before doing it all over again…showing that she really doesn’t care, but loves Matt enough to tell him these things. “It was hell, but the pay off is going to be worth it…the pay off is already worth it, being you are here by my side. That’s what I have wanted a long, not some piece of metal or some record, I have only wanted you and now I have that I don’t care about anything else,” Matt lies a bit. He really does care about that piece of metal and record. He wouldn’t have worked so hard if he didn’t, but he also cares about Haley and if he had to give it all up for her…he would in a second. That isn’t a choice he has to make and so, now he can have his cake and eat it too. Grabbing the back of his head, Jackson almost jumps forward, as the pain surges through his body. Not letting go of his hand, Haley holds on tighter, making him look at her and making some of the pain go away. “You need to lay down Matt, you took quite a beating from Ivan and facing Edwards on Turmoil didn’t help. You should have taken the night off, but you couldn’t you had to be in that ring and I don’t hold it against you,” she says, helping him back onto the bed, placing his legs at the bottom of it so he can lay down. His eyelids begin to get heavy, as she continues holding his hand…making sure that he is all right, as he starts to fall asleep. Finally he is asleep, his barrel of a chest moving up and down with each breath. She lets go of his hand and puts it next to him and stands up, pacing the room, never taking her eyes of Matt. “You are going to be the next Raising Star Champion I know it!”
