The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Four: Can I take your Order Mister Satan?

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th July 2002 The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#3 for PPV (28th July 2002)… Chapter One: The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Four: Can I take your Order Mister Satan? “DUSTIN DOWNEY! HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE’S A MINION! A LOUSY, STEALING, ROTTEN LITTLE CUR! NOT SO STRANGER…BUT HEEEE’S A MAIMER!” “Hey! Get outta here you bunch of freeloading demon minions! You frickin’ well heard me you loons, get outta here before I kick you out! ARRRRRRRR! BE GONE YA DEMON LANDLUBBERS BEFORE OL’ CAP’N DUSTIN GETS HIS HANDS ON YE! ARRRRRRRRR!” ::.~(Musings of Chris Chase)~ Dustin’s been talking to thin air. Not that I’m surprised by that, I’m sure that thin air’s a great conversationalist, but there comes a point when it becomes highly annoying, and quite agitating to find a slightly moronic and foolish pal of mine yelling profanities at thin air in a public place. It’s a day on from the incident at court, I’ve had the opportunity to recover from twenty hours of grueling tolerance of a clichéd argument from a certain Quinton Flynn, and from the sight of Dustin using the courtroom bailiff as a battering ram against a crowd of Satan worshipping whores. Yeah…those two incidents and the several times that Dustin attempted to smother me with a pillow last night. Oh don’t worry about me, I’ve no-sold death too many times now just to lose sight of it… It’s becoming quite a useful habit… It’s Friday, and finally I’ve managed to tempt my benefactors into donating a little extra promotion time in preparation for my valiant conflict against Clyde on Sunday, I’d say there’s enough there for a couple more promotions at least, not that I’m going to reveal exactly what. The Pay-Per-View’s becoming subject to many an opinion these days, thanks to exposure to new sources. The main focal point for the evening will be the mini-tournament for the PCW World Heavyweight title, with the supposed #1 Contender Xavier Kannon facing off against the Pharaoh and the big and bold hero, Big Alan Steele combating none other than El Presidente, Frisco. There’d be no better pleasure than seeing Xavier Kannon blow the big one again, heck, it’s almost a hobby of mine – with such fond memories from Shanghai’ed and March Madness. It’s a shame that I’m not going in the match to see to his failure personally, but I’m sure either The Pharaoh or Big Al has it covered. Besides, I’ve got more Satanists to fry than you can shake a Big Al sized stick at… If Tyler Nelson and his band of Merry Satanic Jobbers want a ruckus, then they’ve certainly got one. There’s been too much speculation recently about the future of the company if Nelson continues this descent down this badly lit path. Soon, Satanism will be the in thing again, children will be running around killing kittens and the number of pedophiles is bound to rise for sure. And the worst thing is, it’ll all be marketable. Great, for the protection of sanity, it’s time to wield the big weapons. Unfortunately not in a public place. You catch us in an oceanic park, the kind of place where children run around merrily trying to get their hands bitten off by seals and such. This is Florida for you…a brief excursion before heading off with the PCW Wrestle Vessel to the venue for the PCW Wrestle Vessel Pay-Per-View. It’s a sweltering 30 degrees Celsius, and I’m wearing nothing but a pair of shorts. No, not leather shorts…essentially, they’re cargo shorts, worn quite loosely around my waist. Sitting on a grassy knoll, it’s high noon, and Dustin’s finding the presence of his own personal demons a little too exasperating….:: Dustin: ~(With a sigh)~ Well…that’s them sorted out for the time being, I managed to threaten them off with an angry stance followed by continued yelling and the promise that I’d feed them to a Sea Lion if they got anywhere within a 500 yard radius of me. Say, would you smothering you with a pillow be doing you a favor? Chris: ~(Sarcastically)~ Certainly Dustin, but I’m sure that you wouldn’t be doing the rest of the world any favors now then, would you? Dustin: Drat! Curse you…the whole fucking lot of you! Chris: …Now if you don’t mind my overly creepy and imbecilic cohort, I’d like a little time to myself to work on my preparation for that match against Clyde on Sunday… by SLEEPING my damn ass off! Oh…the trials of being in the public eye…I’m too tired to mock Satanists today… ::. Dustin’s an easily led character, which is all too obvious from his willingness to follow me around without question, without wanting to go off on his own. I guess that’s the rich kid in him. I need sleep, heck with Dustin attempting to smother me for the most part of the previous night, I could use this time to dream about buxom women, beating the fuck out of my foes and going off with their preppy wives. Not anyone in particular of course. Dustin, instead, feeling that going to sleep on the top of grassy knoll is too usual for him, begins to trek through the Oceanic Park, admiring the sights and sounds of the maritime world. Well, that’s the idea at least. There’s a faint whistle of wind, a small breeze picking up on a sweltering day as Dustin begins the slow walk down the grassy knoll towards the Sea Lion pool. Kids pass him, pointing at him as the guy from the television, or the guy that ate forty funnel cakes in a row on that Caribbean Cruise and ended up being chased around a particularly large ship by twenty black men. I don’t want to go into any other details after that… It’s too damn confusing to explain, but the gist of it details a horde of lobsters, beer and a game of Poker… Perplexing stories aside, Dustin continues past those pointy children, the kind you like to take a boot to, and runs his hand down the Sea Lion pool, trying to get a rise out of the Sea Lions that bask on the surface of the pool. Not satisfied with getting no response from the marine mammals, he reaches inside the pocket of his faux fur coat, pulling out what looks like a can of Sardines. Peeling it open, he throws the whole lot into the pool, setting off a battle of wits, cunning and pure Sea Lion instinct in an instant….:: ARF! ARF! ARF! GYAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHH! SOMEONE’S PUT A FUCKING CAN OF SARDINES IN THE SEA LION POOL! SECURITY GET THE BASTARD THAT DID THIS!!! ARFFFFFF! ARF! ARF! ARF! Chris: ~(Half asleep)~ Would you fucking well keep it down over there, can’t you see that I’m trying to sleep here? Tch...Sea Lions. You’d think that someone had thrown a can of sardines in there…the cretins… ::. I hate being right almost as much as I hate being wrong, it’s a trade-off that I don’t particularly ever want to come to. The uproar coming from the Sea Lion pool is immense as half a dozen Sea Lions battle it out for a single can of Sardines as children and grown adults are reduced to tears at the sight of nature in action. It’s almost like coming home from a day of school to find your parents at it on top of the washing machine…except messier, and there’s even more of a fishy stench to the whole thing. A swirling mess of fish guts and Sea Lion blood, the power of nature is awesome indeed. So, as I peacefully nap on the pinnacle of a grassy knoll underneath a palm tree, Dustin blunders past the whole thing in blissful ignorance of the utter chaos that he’d created, managing to escape the attentions of the security personnel by managing to look normal enough to be passed off as an innocent by-stander. The sun’s reflected in the electric eel tank ahead of him. Electric Eels are famed for their ability to generate electricity through their own biological energy, not that Dustin’s all that interested in that. No, it’s something far more outlandish than the process of energy transfer in Electric Eels… Dustin’s drawn to the reflection in the sides of the tank, as if were some pile of suitably naughty magazines. He peers into it, and is somewhat taken aback by the sight he sees, not naughty magazines, but nothing more than the sight of a semi-phenomenal demon head, seemingly stuck between the panes of glass. Dustin and I have had no end of run-ins with floating heads…most of them being the bloated egos of self-righteous buffoons that we fight or whatever, but the very few are nothing except celestial beings, the very rulers of the Universe. And Dustin just happens to come across the one famed for death, pestilence and for the creation of Satanic Jobbers. Yep…you know as the Devil. Dustin knows him by his middle name. Satan, it seems, isn’t some ideology thought up by overly worried peasants in the dark ages – not the PCW Stable, dimwits. So, Dustin’s a minion of Satan…this brings up all manner of delightful promotion making possibilities – and explains why the idiot’s been trying to kill me with a pillow for the last twelve hours or so….:: Voice: HEY YOU! Yes…you…the dopey looking one in the false fur coat. Are you Dustin Downey? The Dustin Downey that accompanies that meddling Chris Chase to such outlandish places like an Oceanic Park? ::.Dustin, although somewhat perplexed by all this, instinctively falls onto the tactic passed on from Downey to Downey over the centuries, and begins to fall into the habit of the moronic dialogue that he’s renowned for, nodding his head with much enthusiasm in response to his latest friend….:: Dustin: Oh sure…in fact I pretty much dragged him here instead, the lazy bugger. You know, a mountain is easier to move than Chris is when he’s in the kind of mood that he’s in pretty much all of the time. Say, you don’t happen to know any decent chili recipes do you? Voice: Meh…I’m sure one of the other minions can take care of that for you. The name’s Satan, you might know me by other names such as ‘The Devil’, ‘Lucifer’ or ‘Billy Ray Cyrus’…I’m quite impressed with your work regarding the Sea Lions of late. ::. Moderately baffled by exactly what this embodiment of Satan means by the work regarding the Sea Lions that the head seems to be particularly pleased about, Dustin takes the whole sentence like a compliment, feeling particularly pleased with himself as he stands before this pane of glass, talking to an invisible talking head inside this pane of glass and completely baffling the passing tourists who take pictures of the moron, thinking that he’s one of those goofy mascots that wander aimlessly around the park, harassing young children and causing no end of general chaos. If I were a mindless tourist who’d never actually seen anything like it, I’d be in exactly the same sinking boat. But having known the idiot for well over a year now, it’s no more unusual than curling with penguins… You just know that Ivan would hate that….:: “Satan”: And so, pre-empting that incident, I called upon my liaison in the Wal-Mart Corporation to ensure that you sold your soul to me when you bought that forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts and packet of cereal. And now I have the most powerful weapon in my arsenal, ever! Bwahahahahahahaha! ::. You just know how Dustin would react to that. He’d question the ethic, he’d be perplexed by the very thought of himself actually being useful. Sure, he’s no liability, that’s for sure. But I think that he’d think that Satan must had gone through some personality and mind-warping situation in the past year or so to think that Dustin could ever be a mighty weapon in the hands of evil. Come to think of it, he’d probably annoy people to death with his lengthy list of stupid questions and idiotic anecdotes. Not an effective weapon against someone who’s managed to put up with it for well over a year now, someone like myself for example. I’m the Anti-Dustin in effect, the whole idea of there being some Universe warping effect of the two of us being in the same vicinity is negated by the fact that the world’s remained relatively normal – despite the recent vogue for Satanic Jobbers – of late….:: Dustin: I hate to interrupt your maniacally evil laughter, but I’m nothing more than a moron who passes himself off as a professional wrestling referee. Exactly how can I be of any use to you…apart from the obvious humor related stuff. Did I ever tell you about the one with the weasel, park ranger and pack of rabid llamas? ::.”Satan” shakes his head, which is about the only thing that he can move as of now, as the chaotic wake left by the whole incident with the Sea Lions and the can of sardines begins to rear its ugly head again, with the security staff still searching the premises for the culprit, accusing some of the other members of staff for being irresponsible….:: ALRIGHT YOU! TALK! WHO PUT THE FUCKING CAN OF SARDINES IN THE SEA LION POOL? YOU LOT ARE NOW BEING HELD RESPONSIBLE FOR ANY LOSS OF PROFIT THIS PARK MIGHT SUFFER AS A RESULT OF THE FILMING OF “WHEN SEA LIONS FIGHT: PART THREE”…DO YOU HAVE ANY IDEA OF WHAT YOU’VE DONE TO THE REPUTATION OF THIS FAMILY THEMED PARK? “Satan”: Like I said Dustin, Excellent Work. I’ve been meaning to put this place out of business for a long while now. These damn Family-Orientated theme parks get my goat, if you have the slightest idea of what I’m going on about… Dustin: Sure…they’ve captured your goat, you haven’t been able to feed off it’s blood, so you got so ticked off that you had to bring in lil’ old Dustin Downey in to kick the asses of Family-Orientated Theme Parks. Go ME! ::. The sound of palm hitting forehead can be heard way off in the distance, coming from a certain grassy knoll….:: “Satan”: Gah, I don’t think you quite got the point there Downey… Dustin: Tch…it’s nothing unusual. In fact, my buddy Chris tells me that I never quite get the point of things, which I can live with – I’m not particularly fond of pointy objects you see… “Satan”: Yes, Yes…whatever. Now, what I want you to do is to kill Chris Chase. Surely that shouldn’t be too difficult for you. Now beat it…I’m late for an appointment with Hitler, something about smothering him with a wad of bees. Now…run that errand Dustin…you know you can do it… Singing Minions: DUSTIN DOWNEY! HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE’S A MINION! A LOUSY, STEALING, ROTTEN LITTLE CUR! NOT SO STRANGER…BUT HEEEE’S A MAIMER!” …. To be continued…
