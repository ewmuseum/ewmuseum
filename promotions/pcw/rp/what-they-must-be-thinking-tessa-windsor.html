Date: not entered Ack. Morning. I don't wanna wake up. Waking up sucks. But there's an autograph session. Dang. Okay, okay, I'm up. Stupid alarm clock. Can't ignore it, it never plays the same alarm twice. Then again, that's why I bought it. I'm up. Ah! Stupid sun. I need to get those blinds fixed. Meh, do it later, I always say. What's it been, a couple of months now? This wrestling thing doesn't exactly leave much free time. I'm lucky to make it back here very often. Okay, I'm up. Where's that snooze button? Well, it would help if I opened my eyes. Ah! The sun. Right. Up, up, upupupupupupupupup.... I think I turned on the light. I know I hit a switch. Something's on. I hear an electric humming noise. Wait, that's the fan. Okay, the light's on now. But my eyes are still closed. No point in that, right? I suppose I could just turn it off. Habit, I'll get used to the light. I'll adapt. I always do. Ah, the water feels good. Starting to wake up, and not just say so to fool my body. Where's that loufa sponge? Oh, yeah, threw it out yesterday. Was supposed to get a new one. Meh, just use soap and washcloth. Dang, I forgot how uncomfortable this is against the skin. I need to get another loufa sponge. How does Chris stand these things? Coffee... I need coffee. That shower'll last another 10 minutes before I run back to bed. Is there any coffee here? Ah, there it is. Empty can? Darn you, Chris! Chris... he's supposed to meet me there anyway, I'll just tell him to bring some coffee. Yeah... Hey, Chris? On your way to... you're there. Is there coffee? Crap. Well, tell them I'll be late. You know I can't function in the morning without coffee. Yeah, usual place. It's not too crowded. See you later, hun. Bye. Hey, there's still some Coco Puffs left. I thought I ate them all. Man, I rediscover my house everytime I come home. I need to spend more time here. Why do I ever leave? Oh, right, El Romantico's stalking me. The harassing phone calls all hours of the day, the sexually explicit pictures he sent me. Chris took them. I don't think he destroyed them, just walked out with them... Meh. I look out my window, and I just see the bushes. And I think to myself that he's out there, somewhere. That sounds like something I heard of before. I can't remember. Somewhere... Oh well. I'll get to finish this up soon. I'll beat him and win the Cruiserweight Title. Rent-A-Hero is no less a tool than the self-proclaimed Loverboy, but he's at least a nice tool. I'm growing everyday. Getting better everyday. I'm still little more than one of the Schmoopies in everyone's mind. And though I'm the face of the team, everyone calls Chris as getting me to where I am. I want to prove them wrong. I want to show them that I wasn't just a fluke in WIW. That I'm better than to just be confined to a Women's division like in XCWF. I am Tessa Windsor. I'm just as good as anyone else. In the ring, I'm not a female first, I'm a wrestler first. Just need to get everyone else to see things that way, and not just stare at my breasts. I REALLY wish Sally Saddle would stop doing that. She creeps me out. But I can see it. 5 years down the road, when I'm married to Chris Storm, or Jackson Rust, or anyone else from WIW who's not doing anything and comes to PCW... and I've held all the singles titles to be offered to me in PCW, and I'm the first PCW World Champion to bear a child... then they'll respect me for being a great wrestler. I don't want special treatment, I could've stayed in a sissy job for that. I wanna wrestle. El Romantico has to learn that. PCW has to learn that. The world has to learn that. School's in session...
