Celebrity Allotments #1: The Beginning

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th April 2002 WE move towards somewhere unusual for Miyagawa. We’re in a garden. Well I say that lightly since it’s not exactly a garden. It’s a big field. With sky scrapers all around it and traffic and stuff, but it’s a field. It’s a grassy field, although the area that Miyagawa and Syko are standing in has in fact been dug over so it’s all brown and soiley. Syko has a small trowel in his hand and is dressed in a workman’s shirt that looks like it was stolen from Mark Estep’s ring wear collection. Miyagawa is dressed also suitably like a farmer, including the straw hat and toothpick... obviously hay is hard to come by. Miyagawa: Ah young Syko, smell that fresh country air. This is what life is all about, isn’t it... out here in the country digging at our land. Soon we will grow vegetables, and then we will donate them to poor people as we don’t want to eat vegetables... and thus we give back to the community. Syko turns to Miyagawa who gets a shovel and continues to turn the soil over. Miyagawa is smiling as he does this. Syko: Ok, three questions. Miyagawa nods at Syko and indicates for him to continue. Syko: One. Why is there a field in the centre of Los Angeles, and why are we planning to plant vegetables in it? Oh and WHY IS THERE A FIELD IN THE CENTER OF LOS ANGELES? Miyagawa: It’s disused green belt land. You can’t build skyscrapers on it, you can’t turn it into a park... however there is nothing against farming on this... so one day... I had a dream... that little black girls and little white... no wait, wrong dream. Celebrity Allotments, that was the dream. Syko just shakes his head in disbelief as the strange thoughts that seem to be all too frickin common from Miyagawa reappear once again out in the open. Yes it’s that time of the month again... Syko: Look. I’m Syko. I’m a freaking Goblin. Even I don’t try to run an allotment in the CENTER OF LOS ANGELES. WHAT IS WRONG WITH YOU? Miyagawa steps back and puts his hands up in a defensive position. Miyagawa: Hey Syko, chill man. This is our chance to give something back to the community. We grow them veggies. Surely that’s a worthy cause. Syko just looks completely and utterly frustrated at this senseless rubbish that Miyagawa has dragged him down here to do, and convinced him to dig over a ten meter square area, admittedly with a digging machine, but still... they did have to pick the weeds out. Syko: And you haven’t heard of donations? Miyagawa: Bah, if we gave them donations, they’d only spend it on things like... books... and stuff. Veggies are much more important. Good for your bones are veggies. Syko: So why don’t you eat them instead? Miyagawa laughs for a few seconds pretty damned heartily as well at that. Miyagawa: Are you kidding? There’s no way I’m going to eat anything grown in a city with this much pollution, it’ll probably kill me! Syko just shakes his head in disappointment, and throws his trowel down. Before grabbing a rake and starting to rake the earth. He stops for a second and looks at Miyagawa. Syko: You think your going to loose don’t you? Miyagawa raises an eyebrow at Syko for saying that completely out of the blue. Miyagawa: Eh? What are you going on about now? Syko: At Supremacy. Old people get allotments because they want something to do when they retire. If you loose on Supremacy, you will be effectively retired. This is your damned retirement package isn’t it. Miyagawa shakes his head but not exactly in a completely convincing manner. Miyagawa: Nah, you’ve got to be kidding, I’m not doing something like that... what do you think I’d do next, buy a pipe? Maybe get a shed and a greenhouse down here with a few bottles of sherry and sit down here reading the paper, drinking a glass of sherry and smoking my pipe? Syko nods in a violent manner. Syko: Yes I think you are planning to do that exactly. In fact I’d bet on it. You WANT to retire so you don’t have to do any more work, you spineless coward. Miyagawa: What? You can’t talk to me like that; I’m your tag partner. Syko: Why not, coward? What you going to do? Retire on my ass? Miyagawa: I’m going to kick your ass, that’s what. Syko: That’s too much exercise for you, you spineless coward. You are going to complain for a bit, then go to Sabotage, loose to Stryfe, complain some more... then go to Supremacy and complain even more and then you’ll be out of a job. Miyagawa looks a little panicked at the sudden violent manner of Syko the freakin’ Goblin all of a sudden. Miyagawa: I’ll have Natalia and the baby... family is more important. Syko: But she’ll bring up how you lost of “Uncle” Boda. Maybe “Uncle” Boda will take the kid backstage to PCW because you won’t be able to because you’ll be a washed up old has been with no job, no skills and a freakin allotment. Miyagawa: Wo wo wo whoa whoa WHOA. **sticks up his hand to Syko** I’m selling ten meter square plots of land here for five hundred dollars each. I’m going to make about five grand from this. And it only cost me three hundred bucks for the farming rights. This has nothing to do with Stryfe who I will defeat. Nothing to do with Boda who I will send packing, and besides... it’s none of your damned business because “Daddy” Miyagawa is going to get some god damn revenge on “Uncle” Boda right after “Daddy” Miyagawa kicks Syko’s ass around this large patch of grass! Syko: There, that’s much more like the Miyagawa I know. Miyagawa: Besides which the sherry, shed and greenhouse are arriving later and I’m going to need a hand to put them up. Miyagawa grins at Syko as this first instalment of the Saga of Celebrity Allotments finishes for the moment...
