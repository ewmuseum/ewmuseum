The Chef Meets a New Enemy

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/20/02 ***This promo is dedicated to a man that I feel is dividing America, and should be denounced publicly for his statements regarding 9/11 among other statements. The camera fades in… The Chef stands in front of a desk holding a few sheets of paper in his hands. He is dressed in his normal Islamic Militant Chef attire, and looks to be sweating profusely. Behind the Chef and his desk is a flag with the International Incident logo on it (Which is the American flag being separated by the flames of a growing fire with the fire text “I2” in the middle). The Chef wipes the sweat from his forehead. The Chef: Whew, it’s hotter than a Dutch oven in here! The camera moves up and down as if the cameraman was nodding in agreement. The Chef: Well, be that as it may, I have come before you infidels to educate you on the horrible hypocrisy that is Christianity! Yes, that’s right, Christianity! The Chef holds up a piece of paper with a graphical image of a cross. The Chef: About one third of the world’s population considers themselves Christians. Gag me with a spatula! This religion is flooded with so many contradictions that Noah wouldn’t be able to survive it! The Chef rips up the paper as a sign of disrespect for the symbol of Christianity. The Chef: Yes, yes, Jesus was a prophet, and a very good one at that, but he was no son of Allah! How could he be!?!? The Virgin Birth, the Resurrection, the thingy with the little flames going around the room; those were all stories that were written by senile old pieces of rancid meat at least twenty years after Jesus’ death! Talk about a bunch of contradictions, these guys can’t even agree on when the fellow was baptized! And what’s this fascination with his blood and flesh? Isn’t that cannibalism folks!?!? Suddenly there comes a knock at the door. The Chef: There’s a door here!?!? The cameraman nods, shaking the camera. The Chef: Hmmm, come in! The door bursts open, and who else but the champion of Christianity steps into the stuffy dark room. The Chef: No…No…NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Into the sight of the camera comes…JERRY FALWELL!!! Falwell: Sit down devil worshipper, for the true voice of the lord has come!!! The Chef: Back off you fat piece of trash, I run the show here! I swear, I'll sauté your ass with raspberries, radicchio, and rutabagas! Falwell: Oh yeah! Well what do you believe in good sir!?!? The Chef: I believe that if you grow two more double chins you’ll be as fat as my cloned girlfriend! Falwell reacts hostilely to the Chef’s choice words. Falwell: You are what’s wrong with American today! You are one of those new age satanic worshippers poisoning our relationship with God! It’s because of your sins that America is suffering incredible hardships! The Chef: Ok, let me remind you that you declared a war on Tinky Winky! Let’s see here…sin…hmmm? Well if you would actually read your freaking scripture and stop trying to pretend you know what Allah wants you’d realize in John 9:2 that you’re a freaking retarded infidel culinary school dropout! Falwell: Bah, I’ve read the Bible more times than I’ve had sex; twice… The Chef: OOOOHHHH!!! I guess I have to be afraid of you! It’s “Mr. All Mighty” wielding a book of faith and values and pestering the entire world with his contradictory gibberish! You call yourself a zealot!?!? I call you a buffoon!!! Falwell: Buffoon!?!? Who’s worshipping a prophet that promotes violence towards other men!?!? The Chef: I can’t believe you just say that Falwell… I’m promoting violence!?!? What have you Christians done over the past two millennia? In the name of the “Prince of Peace” you have unleashed many inquisitions, holy wars, witch-hunts, executions, assassinations, massacres, poisonous monkeys, Tom Arnold, and let’s not forget you’ve got lots of blubber too! Falwell: Oh, I know what you are, talking about my physique and all…you’re a homosexual!!! The Chef: Oh come on, that just doesn’t even make sense. I’m banging practically the most hideous looking chick in the whole wide world! Wait, that does really make sense… Falwell: That’s right, you are an abomination! You are the reason we were bombed! The Chef: Listen; I didn’t even join the Taliban until after Sept. 11th! Plus…I’m not gay! Falwell: But in your heart you know you are…come to the chosen flock my friend. Admit your sins, and proclaim to the world that Jesus Christ died on the cross for your sins!!! The Chef: I…I…I……. Falwell grins as if he was saying in his head, “Now I’ve got the bastard”! The Chef: I…. CRACK!!! Jerry Falwell all of a sudden drops to the ground face first. He lies there motionlessly, with a huge lump on the backside of his head. The Chef: I…LOVE YOU BERTHA!!! The Chef runs over and hugs the portly princess, who happens to be holding a rolling pin, which seems to be broken in half. Bertha: F*cking let go, remember what f*cking happened the last time you f*cked with me, mother f*cker!?!? The Chef thinks back… The Chef: Oh yes, that tremendous gaseous explosion close my restaurant for two weeks! Bertha: Hey b*tch @$$, t’was your mother f*cking sh*tty as f*ck fault, ho skank! The Chef: Yes, I know. Hey, how about I treat you to a meal at Ponderosa? Bertha: F*cking sirloin tips!?!? I’m already f*cking there, sh*t @$$! Bertha scampers off, causing minor earthquakes to occur along the San Andreas Fault. The Chef: I’ll be right behind you! Just give me a few seconds alone with Mr. Falwell… The Chef drops a copy of the Koran on the ground, and tries as hard as he can to lift up the fat foul-mouthed worship leader. The Chef: Can’t…prepare…fat…guy…for…Conversion…Ah to heck with this! The Chef pulls the pocket version of the Koran out of his shirt pocket, and SMACKS Falwell across the face with it! He then lets go of the blasphemer and walks out of the room, slamming the door behind him. As Falwell lays on the cold floor alone, the camera zooms in to his Bible, which he dropped after the rolling pin shot. Falwell’s outstretched arm motionlessly points to a part of the scripture which says… Romans 18: “If is it possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone.” The camera fades to black.
