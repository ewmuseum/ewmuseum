The Dating Game...revisited???

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/06/03 Technically stars don’t rise, what with them being a combination of gases being a huge mass floating in space for eternity. In fact stars don’t even move, shooting stars are nothing more than fragments of meteorites burning up as they enter the earths atmosphere, comets are slightly different following a set course hooked on the gravity being forced to follow the same orbit over and over. In fact if you were to look out into the night sky and stare at the stars twinkling in their stellar dance how would it make you feel knowing that those very stars you were looking at, no longer existed and were dead, the light just taking so long to travel to reach earth. Even after their own death the stars shine down on us. But today is not a time for being sombre thinking of how things will all eventually fade and die, today was a time for celebration and new beginnings for a new star had been born, a man had been able to beat his jinx and somehow get the win over a man who had been the plague to his career since the very first day. For at turmoil James Edwards had turned the odds around and defied the critics he had beaten Matt Jackson and in doing so had got himself a rather nifty little belt to keep up his silver stellar parachute pants. Even before the scene fades in the sound of a heavy thumping can be heard, not the sound associated with beating someone’s face with a solid mallet but more of a hardcore dance beat playing over and over again, the sound emanating from behind a pair of heavy wooden doors which have been tastelessly decorated in multi coloured streamers and balloons the doors suddenly fly open with a man stumbling out his cheeks puffed out and his face a funny shade of green, his eyes glaze over before suddenly releasing the contents of his cheeks onto the heavily varnished wood floor, the vomit landing with a heavy splat before dispersing itself evenly among the surface. The man glances up briefly staring straight ahead of him. Drunken Party Goer: There that’s better. And with those words barely out of his mouth the mans knees buckle causing him to fall face first into the pile of his own vomit, moving past the vomit stained man and the heavy wooden doors is a large sports hall that has clearly been rented out. It is packed with people all dressed in a variety of colours and outfits dancing around the hall how a chicken dances around without its head. A series of long tables occupy one side of the room containing typical party food ranging from twiglets, chicken drumsticks to pineapple and cheeses on a stick. At the far end of the table is the reason for the unconscious man as copious amounts of alcohol stacked high on the table with plenty more in reserve should the party goers somehow get through the vast amount in front of them. From every available spare portion of wall a balloon or streamer or paper chain was hanging in a vein attempt to bring some life or verve to the party, but what seemed to work most was the disco ball that had been positioned above the very centre of the room, hypnotising a few of the more drunken party goers, proving inspiration to dance for the rest. At one end of the room is stood the man of the hour James Edwards his newly acquired rising star title flung over his left shoulder. Standing tall, proud and very unsteady in his tuxedo with various stains on it James stood out like a sore thumb with all the other partiers choosing to come in much more casual clothes along the line of shorts and t-shirts, it was entering summer after all and the temperature was raising slowly day by day. A small bead of perspiration dripped from the end of James’ nose and landed on the floor in another small amount of liquid, which at the current moment in time could have been anything of an alcoholic nature. A small number of revellers have gathered round James each with a small glass of clear liquid in their hands, the smallest member of the group hands James one of the glasses causing James to sway more before looking down at the glass that had been placed in his hands his eyes trying to focus on it. Small man: Dude it’s Sambuca we’ve been drinking it all night I can’t believe you’re still standing. James: You take that back I beat him fair and square. Small man: Err ok but anyway everybody ready on the count of three one two… hey has anyone seen James. James: I’m James. Small Man: No the other James. James: I’m James! James’ words are becoming more and more slurred as he steps forward causing the circle of revellers to stand back slightly for a number of reasons ranging from he could blow at any second, he could fall at any second or they didn’t think that vomit and shorts was the designer look for this time of year. Steadying himself his arms in front of him, nodding slowly James pulls himself up to a more upright position breathing in heavily. James: I’m ok, seriously I’m ok that dude you were after John ran out a few minutes ago after we did some tequila slammers poor lad couldn’t quite keep up with the lunar one I’m afraid oh no indeed I took him to the limits then bam his engine died oh yes when he went toe to toe with me he had no idea what he was in for just like Matt Jackson. Did I tell you how I beat him, how I got this? James taps his shoulder completely missing the belt which had now fallen to the floor after sliding off of the material of his tuxedo although at this stage of inebriation James is blissfully unaware of this, fortunately one of his friends a tall gentleman with a face full of stubble had picked it up off the floor dripping in random fluid both body and alcohol and placed it down on a table. Small man: Yes James you’ve told us a few times about how you beat him, about how you defied the odds, about how you proved everyone wrong and about how you reversed his finisher twice, it’s a great story and we are really happy for you but we want to drink so shut up about it and get ready. One two three. A fraction of a second after the word three the entire circle of friends and colleagues raised their glasses and poured the clear liquid down their throats, a few coughing immediately afterwards in their own way of attempting to deal with the fire that was engulfing their bellies though all except for James whose glass was at his lips but something had caught his eye. Standing at the entrance of the sports hall was a small brunette woman whose small red dress showed off her curves without even trying to a woman who James had known all but briefly a woman who James thought he had loved, a woman who had chosen the worst possible time to walk back into his life because at the current moment in time, James didn’t feel like he could walk. Taking a few steps forward tentatively seeing exactly how much weight and pressure his legs could endure at present James proceeded to move towards Michelle swaying left and right like a penguin on ice eventually he reached Michelle who moved backwards trying to avoid the pungent odour of alcohol that was oozing from every pore in his body. Michelle: I heard that you won your match, I just had to come and say congratulations and see you one last time. James: What do you mean one last time? James attempted to move closer to Michelle who proceeded to back away once more causing memories of the dating game to surge through James’ mind stopping him from pursuing her. Michelle: I can’t be faced with seeing you week in week out on television, knowing that you care for me yet at the same time failing to acknowledge me. I can’t go out during the day without fear of bumping into you and it being awkward between us because we both deny our feelings, I just came to tell you congratulations before I leave. James: Where are you going? Michelle: To stay with my parents in Australia, they retired there a few years ago and I’ve always been meaning to go and see them. James: Well when are you coming back…I can’t just not see you again. Michelle: It’s a one way ticket James, I’m not coming back you said it yourself last week I’m a distraction to you, look what happened when you were without me, you went and achieved something that you could not do when I was around, you beat Jackson when I wasn’t there you wouldn’t have done if I was there. Goodbye James. With that said Michelle turned around and gently avoiding the fallen vomit covered body of the other James walked down the corridor away from James her stiletto heels causing the halls to echo as she marched down the them before turning a corner away from James possibly forever. In the main room the DJ changed records as ‘It’s my party and I’ll cry if I want to’ by Lesley Gore began to echo around the sports hall until the sound waves made their way out and connected with James’ eardrums as tears began to well up in his eyes as he fell to his knees watching where Michelle had just left his life.
