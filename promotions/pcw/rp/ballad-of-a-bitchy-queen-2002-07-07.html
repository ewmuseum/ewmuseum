Ballad of a Bitchy Queen

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/07/02 A horrible noise fills the air of a smoke filled room. Breaking the eardrums, causing the mind to lose its grip on sanity...........yes, we truly have entered Hell. We pan in on a glowing and quite gaudy neon sign, “Karaoke” blinking over and over through the hazy air. On the stage of the establishment a Japanese man with coke bottle glasses rocks back and forth, clutching his microphone tightly as he warbles. Japanese Man: I been rooking fol rove In arr the long praces'An now I finay roundSomeron who care for meNo ma’er stormIt you who help me face itAnd now it safe to sayThat you rewwy care for meeeeeeeeeeee As the gentleman hits his final note he hands of all in attendance raise, not for applause but to cover their ears tightly. In the distance glass shatters as he continues his last word. Japanese Man: ......eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee......... A chubby fellow in the first row’s head pops like a grape, his body falling limply to the floor. Japanese Man: (Bowing) Rank you! Rank you reery ruch! The crooner makes his way off the stage, as a chubby man with curly hair bounds up to the mic. William Shatner: Alright........let’s give it up for.........that guy! Yeah! The former TV icon pumps his fist, to the complete apathy of the crowd. William Shatner: Now.......can we get......another volunteer? Nothing but silence greets Shatner’s words........faintly the sounds of crickets chirping raise from the dark. William Shatner: ...........good! Love......the enthusiasm! That’s........what I like to hear! A tumbleweed somehow or another manages to drift through the top of the captive audience. William Shatner: I’ve........never seen that happen before. Shaking his head, the MC continues. William Shatner: Well..........if no one wants to come up........guess I’ll sing! Bill Shatner begins caressing the microphone in a very sickening manner......leans over and croons. William Shatner: Oh.....we got a great big convoy.....oh......yes we do....... “STOP!” A shrill voice pierces the air, thankfully ending Convoy before it begins. Shatner looks down, scanning the audience. William Shatner: What’s......the meaning of this? Bethany: It means you suck! From out of the crowd pops the evil little girl known only as Bethany........she clambers up to the stage, as behind her slinks Clyde shaking his head. William Shatner: I.....do not suck. Bethany: Stop talking like the goddamn Preacher! From the front row a tall pale man waves his hand. Preacher: I’ve......been everywhere man. Clyde: We know, shut up before I kick your ass at chess again. Preacher: Sorry.......my bad. The demon shakes his head again tiredly. Clyde: Why the hell am I here again? Bethany: Because we never do anything fun anymore. Clyde: What, killing that drunken fratboy after we screwed up the brakes on his car wasn’t fun? Bethany: Yeah it was, but non-murdering fun. Clyde: There is no such thing and you know it. Bethany: True, but stop correcting me dammit! Bethany wheels back and kicks Clyde in the shin..........Shatner begins to say something, only for the hellspawn to turn and kick him too. William Shatner: Ow..........ow.........ow! Clyde: (Holding his leg) What the hell do you want me to do then? Bethany: Get up here and sing for me! The midget murderer hops off the stage, leaving the burned man to hobble to the mic and shrug. Clyde: What the hell am I supposed to sing!? Bethany: Anything! Sing about the big fat redneck you’re currently torturing! Sing about you and me! I don’t give a flying goddamn! Clyde: ...........fine. Clyde straightens the mic to a wave of feedback.........clears his throat. Clyde: This is dedicated I guess to Duke Williams, and to Bethany. Bethany takes a seat next to the Preacher, looking up at the platform. Clyde begins singing, though instead of his own voice the voice of Johnny Cash somehow or another rings forth from his mouth. Clyde: There's a story in our town of the bitchiest girl around.......blood stained dress and eyes of blue, how she’d take a stab at you Behind Clyde out of nowhere pops Scourge and Ricardo Montalban, singing the chorus. Scourge and Ricardo: How she’d take a stab at you. Clyde: She crushed my balls in a door, but I still loved that rotten whore, who killed the boy at the candy store. Scourge and Ricardo: Dream on, dream on, bitchy queen, vilest girl we've ever seen. From the bottom of the stage sails a brick........Clyde ducks, causing it to strike Scourge in the forehead. He continues undaunted as Bethany curses. Clyde: She was tops in all, they said, driving nails through my head. She had everything it seemed, not a care, this bitchy queen. Scourge and Ricardo: Not a care, this bitchy queen. Clyde: Other boys couldn’t offer more, so she loved the killer next door, who eviscerated a random whore. Scourge and Ricardo: Dream on, dream on, bitchy queen, should have heard that dumb whore scream. Clyde: He would dump her ass next spring, save his money by not buying a ring. But one day a drunken lout came to town to take her out. Scourge and Ricardo: Came to town to take her out. Clyde: Drunken Duke could offer more, so she left the killer next door, drinking the blood of the screaming whore. Scourge and Ricardo: Dream on, dream on, bitchy queen, we can’t wait to hear you scream. Clyde: Very soon she was a star, on Duke’s broke down chicken farm. With a wooden fence around, and he made her milk the cow. Scourge and Ricardo: And he made her milk the cow. Clyde: All of this was at her door, so she missed the killer next door, who stabbed the dude at the video store. Scourge and Ricardo: Dream on, dream on, bitchy queen, dumbest girl we've ever seen. Clyde: Then one day she fell in crap, so she stabbed Duke in the lap, gave up all her farm hand chores and castrated him while he snored. Scourge and Ricardo: Chopped his dick off while he snored. Clyde: Do I have to tell you more, she came back to the killer next door, who gutted the guy at the store. Now, this story has some more, but you won’t hear it from that whore, or from Duke cause he’s no more. The audience sits in complete silence...........all is still, save for the Preacher who smiles and does a cabbage patch. William Shatner: Get.....off my stage. The former Star Trek icon pulls out a machine gun out of nowhere.....the burned man hits the deck, producing a grenade. Clyde: (Pulling the pin with his teeth) Eat this! Bethany: DUKE THIS IS ALL YOUR GODDAMN FAULT!!!!! YOU WILL DIE!!!!!!!!! The demon throws the explosive........William ducks, causing it to land in the open mouth of the dancing Preacher. His eyes widen.........he gulps. The former PCW champ then pats his stomach with a closed fist. Preacher: ........uh oh. A massive explosion erupts, taking our feed away and causing us to mercifully fade to static.........
