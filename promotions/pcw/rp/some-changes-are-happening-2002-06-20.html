Some changes are happening!!

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.20.02 :.fade in.: Just days away till me and Ron finally get back into the ring. This should be a time in which we live our lives for. I said before that we have been out of action for about three weeks till we finally got booked for tuesday turmoil on the 25th. Why am I not excited? Why do I not feel that I am 100 percent? This isn't like me. I always am up for a challenge. My blood starts flowing and my heart starts pumping faster everytime I hear that Suicidal Tendencies will be in an arena competing. There just is something that is inside of me that feels something is missing. I feel that I can't go on. What can it be? I find myself inside of a room that is completely dark. I have not talk with my brother in a day, he must be worrying what it is that is going on with me. I can tell him I have no fucking clue, because I don't and it kills me! I sit in a corner trying to focus in on anything that I can see in this small room but I can't. My eyes have failed me!!! I try to move my arms and can't. I feel like I can't move them, like they are stuck together. What has come of Mike Andrews. Where am I? I need some answers! Just as I say that I hear a generator turn on as the light in the room come on. My eyes shut automatically as they are sensative to the light, as I have been sitting here for quite some time. Again, I ask myself where am I. I look around the room and see nothing but padding. What the fuck, this can't be not again! I think to myself not to look down because I have a feeling I know where I am at.....I don't listen. I look down and see that my arms are wrapped up and around my body. My nightmare has come back. I am in a damn psych ward again. What did I do to deserve this. I did nothing wrong. Just yesturday I was with Ron I was chatting with him, what happened. I then hear foot steps. I use the wall to pull myself up. I walk over to the glass window and see if someone was coming. I then see a man with a blue pull over walking towards my way. I quickly move back into my corner. The door opens. 'Hey buddy, someone is here to check you out, he says he knows you!' I was thinking to myself could it be James...he bailed me and Ron out last time we were in one of these joints but why would he do it again. I looked at the man and nodded my head at him. I stood up leaning against the wall still in the room. I focus down the hall and see that Ron was walking this way. First thing on my mind was how am I supposed to explain this to him. Then I thought to myself, its my brother he will listen. Ron reached the door. He gave me a stern look. I looked at him and quickly looked right back down. I was embarrassed. But still I didn't know what happened. We made our way to the desk so Ron can sign the release papers. He hadn't said anything to me since he got there. After he signed the papers he looked at me and starting to walk off. The guard let unstrapped me and I followed. The walk to the car was probably the longest walk in the fucking world. I wanted to ask Ron if he knew what happened, but then again, I didn't. In the car we start to go down the street, complete silence. No radio or anything. All you can hear is the breathing between the two of us. I couldn't stand it anymore, I turned to Ron. He must of sensed that I was going to say something because he quickly came out.....'It's going to be okay'. What a relief. But I still wanted to know what happened. I just went for it. 'Ron!?' He looked at me. 'Yeah!' 'What the hell happened, why was I put back into that place.' 'Look man, I can only tell you that you wouldn't want me to describe to you what you did lastnight. I guess you seemed to get enraged when you were talking about our upcoming match and the pay per view. You just got out of hand and started to do things that weren't quite right. So I called them. While you were knocked out, they took you.' I couldn't believe that he would do that, but then again he was only looking out to what was best for me...I hope. 'What was I supposed to do Ron. We are going to be up against a man who has not even wrestled in a tag match and already all the talk is starting...who is Akiyamas partner going to be....dah dah dah...bunch of shit and I am getting tired of it!' 'Look Akiyama is a good wrestler, the man knows his way around the ring. You know I'm not even looking at this match for nothing Mike.' 'What!' 'Our match at Turmoil can only mean one thing, that is we can walk out with a win and go one up in our win column....that's it. Now at the pay per view, that's a different story.' 'So you don't care if we loose at Turmoil?' 'No!' 'Why!?' 'Mike, the pay per view we have the chance to walk out with the titles. Isn't that what we came here to do!' 'Yeah!' 'And we have our shot. We have alot of things to do till the pay per view, there are alot of good teams involved and we are not going in there being the softest. So get your attitude on what's at hand and let's take out the tag teams in PCW and walk out with those belts.' 'Yeah!!!!' 'Mike, everything is going to be alright!' We stop talking as we continued down the road. No telling where we were heading, all I know is that it's not home........ :.fade out.:
