Title: John Wayne and Jesus Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 1/10/03 The Multi-million dollar colonial mansion sits tucked under the backdrop of Ajax Mountain in Missoula, Montana. The property belongs to a one Duke Williams. The wrestler who lives his life somewhere between John Wayne and Jesus. Part hippie and part rough neck the wrestling legend emerges from his house dressed like Johnny Cash in 1963 where nothing but black. In his mouth between his teeth he grips a 30 dollar cigar. “Fuck” yells the Malice Man in frustration. It dawns on Duke as he watches the door slam shut that he left his keys in his other coat. It’s been a hellish few weeks. The divorce with his sixth wife was finalized on Wednesday and he is again left Lonesome, On’ry, and Mean. Wrestling and his career has not even been processed in his mind in weeks. But tonight his sole focus is again the sport that he loves to hate. “Mother fucking, cock sucking, ass chewing, jizzem jumping, son of a bitch” yells the angry Malice Man into the cold Montana air. Duke sighs with frustration as he kicks his two hundred dollar ostrich skin boots against the cold steel door of his cell. Duke then remembers his hidden key laid in the fake rock on his lawn. His cold breath tails him like a jet liner flying across the skies as he walks over to the phony rock. He opens it up and pulls out the emergency key for the emergency situation. “Always have a back up plan” Duke says. A lesson he was taught as a young man before wrestling. Back when he ran coke and contraband for the Cubans when he wasn’t in college. Duke reminisces for a second about the time he was chased by the DEA with helicopters in the deserts of Arizona. Thank God he had an out switching cars in Yuma or his ass would still be rotting in cellblock ten. But that life is a long time gone. Duke uses the key to enter his spacious property filled with gold records and pictures of his wrestling career. Before getting his keys from his PCW staff coat he walks over to the bar which sits in the back of the front room and pours him self a glass of whiskey. Laying on the counter going stale is a bag of Indo. “Why let that go to waste?” philosophies Duke as he rolls a fat J. Duke rolls the joint with as much speed and accuracy as chef would chop a piece of celery. He puts down his cigar and lights up his mind altering smoke. The phone rings but Duke just sits down and tokes his life away. The answering machine picks up the call and starts to play the message out loud. “Duke?? This is Ann Venomen staff organizer for PCW. We haven’t seen a promo from you in like a week. You can get fired for that so we hope you cut one soon. Thanks.” The machine goes click as the call hangs up. “Why don’t you cut this honey” yells Duke as he grabs his crotch and gives the machine a dirty look. “Two fucking years and she wants to threaten me? Fuck her. Duke takes a few tokes then puts out the remaining hash and heads to the closet to grab his keys. His blood boils with anger from the threat laid on him by that fat, fucking, office clerk. Duke grabs his keys from the pocket of one of the numerous hanging coats. He slams his keys down on the table as he storms to his production room. The room has a camera pointing to a leather chair with a small coffee table next to it. The background is a photo gallery of Duke’s greatest wrestling moments. Duke walks over to the camera and turns it on to record a promo then sits down in his black leather chair. His chrome shades reflects the professional studio light shinning from the setup behind the camera. “Ahhhhh Buckaroo’s its scamp Malice time again. I bet you wonder what happened to me?” Duke asks as he scratches his mountain beard. “I really don’t want to talk about it but for you kiddies listening at home, all women are money hungry fucking whores. Now lets get down to brass tacks shall we hoss?” Duke adjusts his ass and crosses his leg across his knee as continues into the camera. “Kannon’s still an asshole, Ellie still blows, Gaz is still a cock sucker. Am I all caught up now? Listen Gaz when I watch you trying to be me I just shake my head in disgust. First off I hate to shatter your tiny fucking world but I can give a shit about you and I don’t think your funny. As I matter of fact I’d rather watch the unfunny comics on Comedy Central then have to sit through one of your promos.” Duke uncrosses his leg and shakes his head in disgust. “Your like a fucking, ahhh, ahhhh, you know, one of them fucking practical jokers their aren’t really fun. I guess what I’m trying to say is your Carrot Top.” Duke makes a face like that’s what he was getting at. ”Bingo Hoss. Seriously kid your nothing more then a butt toy for Kannon. I know, I saw what he used to do with little Walden. You know why rabbits don’t make noise when they have sex??” Duke raises his eyebrow as he waits for an answer. “Its cus they got cotton balls hoss. You see that simple joke ain’t really that simple. The moral of the story is what’s in front of us is the obvious. Its just sometime its so fucking blunt we miss it. Now you may be wrapped up in the little push your getting from joining Kannon but the fact is your nothing but a second string player in the Bengal’s hoss. And if you ain’t a fan of the N-F-L let me tell you that isn’t a complement.” Duke goes silent for a second as he has an angry look on his face. “Lets cut the funny business and cut to the chase. This weekend I return to the ring to send you ass WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND and I hope Kannon’s watching him so I can show him what Malice Style is all about. Duke walks out of the room as the camera stays in place. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
