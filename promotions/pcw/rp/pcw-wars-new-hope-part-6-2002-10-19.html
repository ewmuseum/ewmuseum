PCW Wars: New Hope Part 6

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 - 10 - 02 A. Skywalker: 'Are you alright? What's wrong?' The saga reopened as the mentor Charlie Loc Kenobi falters, almost fainting in the comfort of the Millennium Eagle, watched on by the concerned Alexander Skywalker and the droid collective of Rush-3PO and R2 D2 who voices his own anguish with subtle whistles. C. Loc Kenobi: 'I feel a great disturbance in the force.... It was as though a million voices cried out at once in terror before being silenced almost immediately. I fear something dreadful has happened....' Charlie Loc rubbed his forehead, which has been bathed in sweat and turns his attentions at the training student of Alexander Skywalker. C. Loc Kenobi: 'You had better get on with your exercises!' Gaz enters the back. Gaz Solo: 'Well I told you I could shake them Imperial slugs!' A. Skywalker: 'I knew you could shake them, after all shaking is what you do best....' Gaz Solo: 'Redneck I told you about your masturbating jokes - Don't let me send the Wookie back here because it be you having a taste of your own bestiality medicine....' A. Skywalker: 'Err-... Why thank-you!' In the back Cherezza plays holographic chess with the R2 unit, watched on by the smart mouthed Rush-3PO. R2 D2 takes makes his move via a computer terminal and the piece moves causing the Wookie to roar in anger, obviously losing. Rush-3PO: 'That'd be a fair move bitch - Screaming like a little white baby won't change a darn thing!' Gaz Solo: 'Let him have it squire. It isn't wise to upset a Wookie!' Rush-3PO: 'But dude, nobody worries about upsetting a bodacious droid like me and my main man R2 here....' Gaz Solo: 'That's cause droids don't pull out peoples arms from their sockets when upset. Wookie's are sore losers and have been known to do that sort of shit when provoked' Rush-3PO: 'Good point... Right, R2 let the fur ball win!' R2 D2 voices his opinion with a hoard of random beeps. Rush-3PO: 'What do you mean you refuse to lose to a eight-foot carpet with teeth, did you hear what the captain said about upset Wookies... You will lose or it will be my bronze coloured boot making contact with your AMD processor!' Alex continues to be beat down by his sparring partner with niggling laser fire, eventually conceding to the droid whilst being studied under the watchful eye of Charlie Loc Kenobi. A. Skywalker: 'What’s the point, I may as well have my head beaten against a brick wall!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Remember, a Jedi can feel the force flowing through him. This time actually try to hit the droid instead of re-enacting Zorro, he wore a mask and you wear rags, there's a substantial difference!' Alex took up his position and swayed frantically at the droid who weaves past the wayward blade of the lightsabre. Charlie Loc noticing that the young apprentice is finding this task a little tedious waved his finger and the droid hurtled across the room, smashing against the Eagles dense casing, Alex believes he has accomplished the task by himself. A. Skywalker: 'WHO'S THE MAN~!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'I shall proof the force doubter' looking upon the skeptical captain who's been amusing himself with the antics of the young Skywalker. 'Gaz you are about to tell me that you have traveled the far reaches of the galaxy and have seen many strange things but nothing to suggest that everything is bonded together by a greater power, impressive huh?!?' Gaz Solo: 'Not really - I was going to ask, where in the blue hell as Alderaan vanished to?' as the smuggler gazes through the corridor and onto the cockpits broad window. Suddenly as the engines ease up from coming out of hyperspace, the craft was bombarded by asteroids, shaking the vessel violently and shuddering the interior like marbles in their storage box. Gaz Solo: 'WHAT THE FUCK! Sorry old-timer but this just isn't adding up. This meteor shower isn't charted and can someone explain how a planet just up and leaves?' The Wookie remains calm as he flips several switches. Alex joins his mentor and captains side out of curiosity as the emergency continues, the aftermath of Alderaan bathing the darkness with rubble. A. Skywalker: 'What's going on? You dumb shit... This isn't no Alderaan, it's a bleeding meteor shower!' Gaz Solo: 'Our position is correct but as you can see... No Alderaan!' A. Skywalker: 'What do you mean? You let a Wookie drive and he's gone the wrong way, admit at-least that much....' Cherezza lets out a thunderous grunt, startling the Jedi wannabe. Gaz eases the tension of the claustrophobic cockpit and assures his passengers that these are the co-ordinates to Alderaan and the planets disappearance was unforeseen. Gaz Solo: 'Listen kid, it ain't here... Its as though Alderaan has been blown away!' A. Skywalker: 'What? How? C. Loc Kenobi: 'Destroyed by the Empire... Evil bastards, can't capture two droids but can wipe out an entire planet, it doesn't make sense!' Gaz Solo: 'Are you all there old man - Not even the entire Starfleet could destroy a planet, it would take fire-power of great magnitude....' As the group contemplates these events a humming alarm sounds, accompanied by a red light flickering on the control panel. Gaz Solo: 'It's another ship... And before you say something dumb, it’s an Imperial fighter!' A. Skywalker: 'Maybe it followed us....' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Are you really that dumb? Fighters are short-range crafts and somehow I doubt that one would even dare go after us... It'll be like a suicide mission, but in saying that young grass-hopper, there aren't any space stations out this way!!!' A. Skywalker: 'Uh... Alrite, but what if it were part of a convoy and had got lost?' Gaz Solo: 'Listen dummy, this isn't some citizen bander looking for his rig buddies here. We're talking an Imperial TIE fighter aimlessly flying in the deepest depths of space, now that doesn't restore confidence! Right for having to put up with this idiot here I have some major issues, which need to be addressed so I either hit him and hopefully shut his mouth for good, throw his droids out through the escape hatch or blast that-...' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Blast away - Remember the other two may jeopardise your payment!' The TIE fighter continues to head towards a large moon that illuminates and radiates a comforting light. A. Skywalker: 'He's heading for that small moon?!?' Gaz Solo: 'JESUS CHRIST, NO ALLAH, I MEAN THE FORCE....' C. Loc Kenobi: 'I'll second that... That be nooooooo moon, try a bleeding space station people!' Gaz Solo: 'Tie me in bondage and photograph to your hearts content, whilst calling me Ellie... You’re absolutely right. Full reverse! Chewy, lock in the auxiliary power' The mechanical monstrosity continues to loom over the Millennium Eagle as the engines fire back up, only this time in reverse. The forgotten TIE fighter makes its final approach as the gargantuan battle station forcefully draws the Rebel ship closer, its engines short circuiting in the tug-of-war. A. Skywalker: 'Why are we still moving towards it?' Gaz Solo: 'We're caught in some kind of tractor beam, do'h, speak the obvious!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'No this is the work of the force! I mean tractor beams, please... You've been watching to many Star Trek repeats' Shutting down the engines, the vessel merely floats into the metal moons docking bay. Passing the huge, gaping doors, the pirate ship is pulled through with minimum effort. It's cargo blinded by the lighting from within, the equator of this structure accommodates simply the docking area leaving a vast interior to be explored. Voice over the intercom: 'Those returning from your hourly lunches, please could you clear twenty-three-seven. We are about to open the magnetic field' As the ship rests easy on the interior of the Death Star, they find a thirty-man strong team of Stormbringers waiting eagerly for inspection. The hangar doors closing to an echoing thud, the crew realise that they are now at the hands of the Empire. - To be continued -
