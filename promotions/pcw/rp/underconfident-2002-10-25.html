Title: Underconfident Featuring: Tim Walden Date: October 25, 2002 The road to success was supposed to be easy with friends in high places..Where the hell did I go wrong? I looked up at the ceiling, as seemed to be a trend recently...The last couple of weeks had been an exercise in pure torture, as while the Front Office was still chugging along for the most part, there was always something in my way of making the leap from just being 'that prick who taxes people' to 'such a cocky little prick now'..I don't know what to do, it all seems like life is making its plans to create a conflict of interest with my own..First that Southern tax evader and her little bitch antics, thinking she could get the better of me with that stunt she pulled back on that Tuesday, then that damn Trapezoid showing up and running his dirty tricks..Every time we meet lately, it's always the same way: He attacks, I strike back, he pulls a cheap move to frustrate me and Kannon shows up like a real business partner should to stop the lunacy...But therein lies the true agony of the situation. I can't get the fair advantage over him, he's always one freakin' step ahead and I end up ineffective again..Couldn't beat him for a pin, couldn't tax him out, couldn't stop him when he attacked my co-workers..Can I beat him at all? ...I used to be able to... I turned, my head too clogged by the thinking all at once, this early in the day...7:34 already?..Aching from a mixture of post-fight soreness and hangover, really should've drank less that night but I couldn't help it..The hopelessness rushed in on me when I got back to the hotel room, and the rum in the fridge almost seemed to sense it and call back to me for the moment..A call I should never have answered, but sometimes circumstances just render one weak. I grudgingly pushed up the covers and rolled myself out of bed, dragging my feet as I wandered to the bathroom in my money-patterned pajamas...My first thought being to rinse what remaining scent rested in my mouth away, I quickly took in the mouthwash, letting it wander before the necessary expectoration. I brushed my hair up and back, dragging it away from my face before going through with the remainder of my morning routine..When it was all said and done, I was down in the lobby, trying to enjoy my free Continental breakfast..No replacement for a belt that was all but mine before fate intervened in the form of a fat-mouthed mathematician, but at least it kept my mind off anything that was bothering me for the moment to eat a morning danish, with a glass of apple juice on the side... 'Hey, you're that wrestler guy, aren't you?'...And on the table, on the floor, stupid barely adolescent kids, I could tell from the voice...'Like, Tim What's-your-name...' I swiftly sapped up the spilled juice with my napkin, turning to the fourteen-or-so-year-old boy, the poor-looking sort, with a glare...'Walden, and yeah, that's me...Who are you?' The kid looked down at me somewhat, a bit of a disadvantage of my sitting but not worth getting bothered over..The early-morning raspiness that I spoke with on this occasion obviously put a little fear into his words. 'Jeremiah Thompson, Mr. Walden..I'm just wondering why you're here today, aren't you supposed to be with PCW?' His knowledge was a little savvy, but still rather inadequate.. 'Yes, and actually I'm still with them, Turmoil was here last night..You know, you really should've purchased tickets if you wanted to see the show, since my co-workers and I had an okay appearance and you really ought to have come to see it.' I figured I may as well give the kid a hard sell to make sure he didn't miss any more shows needlessly... ...He responded just a little surprisingly. 'Eh, didn't honestly know, not to mention that you didn't look all that impressive on Fusion anyway..'This set me off a little, as one would expect... 'Hold on just a minute! I put my heart into that brawl on Friday, and I can't help it if Mr. Hardcore Isoceles Travesty decided to seek employment in dishonourable fashion...Attacking staff members to attain a contract really had to be the lowest form of effort, so I thought that in spite of his past titles, I would teach him a lesson in fairness..That being that if it was fair for him to brutally pummel my co-workers, it was fair for him to get the living crap beaten out of him as soon as he met up with us...However, I screwed up. I let my guard down when I had the advantage. Now do you see what went wrong?' He looked to be thinking for a moment..'Yeah, I see..Can I have your aut--' 'Autograph? Sorry, I charge for those..' I might've been on a downer, but I sure as hell wasn't letting any grubby kids cheat me out of my fair profit. Besides, I had misery, so I didn't mind making company at this point. The boy's facial expression simmered down to disappointment before he walked off..I felt no guilt, it was nothing I wasn't used to doing already..I wanted my revenge, and I wasn't above taking a cold stance toward other people to get it...But I still had lingering doubts. Mall Brawl...My opportunity to earn my revenge the easy way...Gone. I had no excuse to blow that opportunity, it was supposed to be easy to win a 2-on-1 situation, and I lost on a roll-up. A freakin' roll-up..Just 10 seconds later and Kannon would've been on scene to finish the job...Just one second later and Brandi would've made the save..How the hell did I let myself stay down for 3? He kicked out of everything we threw at him, and that little egotist lucks out on a bloody schoolboy...It shows weakness on my part that I couldn't manage to kick out of a pin whose purpose is to take advantage of the element of surprise, but was utilised at a point where it shouldn't have been enough of a shock to distract me into defeat...I must beat him myself to be truly satisfied, but I still don't know if I have it in me after taking such an utter collapse there..I want to believe it was a one-time fall, a momentary slip of my concentration, but something eats at me from inside, telling me I just don't have it in me at this point, that if the fight is at all difficult, I'll just end up doing it again, miss my opportunity once more and fall back into failure's grasp...Why? What could have happened that would break my fighting spirit that severely? I myself am unsure at this point, but I'm just overcome by the negative energy clouding my mind...Every time I feel confident, the moments replay.. ...Chef setting up for the Salad Shooter...Taking the Violating Death Drop nine consecutive times...And now losing to that infernal Trapezoid, just when I felt victory was in my grasp...The question is no longer that of whether I will win... ...But moreso whether I can feel like I still have the ability to do so when it counts. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
