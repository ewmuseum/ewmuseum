Tubular Tykes Foundation

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 25 [The scene opens on a bright blue and yellow sign. As the scene zooms out it shows that the sign reads 'TUBULAR TYKES'. The letters are a very bold, dark yellow with a blue outline on the letters. The sign covers most of the two story building that its attached to. There is a large group of people crowded around the building with smiles and handshakes going all around. Many people are dressed very nicely and seem very professional about themselves. A few news trucks gather together and seem to be taking a break before all the festivities happen. A large red ribbon is at the front entrance of the building, which would signify the opening of this building. A very stressed out looking man in a dark suit, holds a microphone that says 'Channel 9'. He's the only newscaster that is recording right now, apparently wanting to get the scoop, but nothing is really happening] Newscaster: This is Skip Timmons for Channel Nine News! We are here on the west side of Los Angeles for a very special opening, and thats the second TUBULAR TYKES Facility! Its a very special time for the community of Los Angeles and the MENTALLY HANDICAPPED! [The scene then changes from the stressed out newscaster, Skip Timmons, to a much better looking man. That man is 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. His bright blonde hair glows in the sunlight, his teeth are white and straight, and his face is tan and blemish free. The newscaster speaks over the gnarly picture] Skip Timmons: 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, as he is called these days, opened the first facility over two years ago. [The scene then loses the bodacious Tommy picture and shows a picture of a very high tech looking structure. It's filled with all kinds of windows and golden frames. It looks like its something from the future] Skip: It was state of the art, and Tommy was not held back at all when it came to the money. He gave mentally handicapped children a chance to be heard, he gave them a chance to fit in, and most of all...he gave them a second chance. [The scene then goes back to the stressed out reporter. By the dark bags underneath his eyes you can tell this man hasn't had much sleep in the past few days. Even with some makeup and good lighting you can still see the awful baggies. Honestly, how can a man really get sleep when his wife is having sex with animals? Thats right...animals! Surfing the internet and accidentally coming onto a special barnyard site happens these days, doesn't it? There she was, in all her glory, being photographed with this horse. It was sick, disgusting, and against all morales. Through all of this Skip Timmons must go on, and he must stay strong] Skip: My wife screws horsies. [The newsman stops, realizing what he said. He then shrugs and decides to go on with his story] Skip: Many children have been helped by The Tubular One over the past few years. One amazing story is of little Gregory Janson, the blind child who is mentally handicapped. [The scene then shows a little boy, around age 9 or so, with his hand curled up into his chest and wearing dark sunglasses. His tongue is wailed out of his mouth with large droplets of drool sagging down to his sloppy joe stained T-shirt] Skip: Little Gregory was just absolutely hopeless before he began attending the Tubular Tykes institute, but once he was admitted things began to turn around. The boy could only eat, sleep, and poop. [Skip pauses and has an inquiry for his fellow camera man] Skip: Can I say poop on the air? [The camera man's voice bursts in from off scene] Camera man: Yeah, I guess so. Your just not allowed to say sh*t, f*ck, and c*cksucker. [Everything stops for a few moments] Skip: You just said them. Camera man: Yeah, but we can just edit that out. [There is another slight pause] Skip: We are live. Camera man: Oh... [Skip shrugs with a smile and decides to go on] Skip: Just knowing those three things, Gregory was quick to excel. He began writing his own name and speaking his own name. Many other words followed with his speech and writing. Only two years later, little Gregory is in the LA public school system. Just one of the fine stories of Tubular Tykes. [Skip laughs to himself before going on with the story] Skip: I wonder if little Gregory knows how to spell horse f*cker. [Skip laughs to himself for a few moments and then begins to part through the crowds. He needs to find some people to interview, because if he doesn't then his boss will kill him. Skip spies a few mentally challenged children with a very attractive looking young woman. She wears the Tubular Tykes blue and yellow employee T-shirt and tight daisy duke jean shorts. That is the woman's uniform for working at Tubular Tykes. God bless Tommy. Skip approaches the woman and decides that she'd be perfect to interview, because she works here. And she probably doesn't like to bob on horse...] Skip: Hello, Miss. My name is Skip Timmons from Channel Nine News. We're here doing coverage of this fine opening and we were wondering if we could ask you a few questions. [The woman begins fixing her hair, pulling it out of the pony tail that it is in. Apparently she feels that she looks better with her hair down. It doesn't matter either way really, she's still one bodacious babe] Bodacious Worker Babe: Sure! Skip: How long have you worked for the Tubular Tykes Foundation? Bodacious Worker Babe: Since it opened two years ago. Skip: Have you ever met Tommy? [The womans face lights up] Bodacious Worker Babe: Oh yes, we've met on many occasions. Skip: Really? Did you date him? [She gives a sly smile to the camera] Bodacious Worker Babe: You could say that. Skip: What did you do? Bodacious Worker Babe: We played Yahtzee. Skip: Yahtzee? Bodacious Worker Babe: Yeah, it was the best times of my life. [Skip seems confused by this information. How can you have the time of your life by playing a game? A very crappy game at that! He notices the sly smile and the way her body language is gearing. Yahtzee is just a code word! Tommy had long nights of sex with this young hotty. Well, go Tommy. Skip then looks to the camera with a large smile] Skip: I bet my wife loves long games of Yahtzee with horses! [There is then a very large area of silence. Then a very drone, retarded sounding voice chimes in] Retarded sounding voice: I like Horssssey's!! [A little boy with a blue and yellow baseball cap, that says 'I'm Tubular', steps beside the very disturbed newscaster. The boy has his hand folded into his chest and his right leg is bowed inward, so its hard for him to walk. His voice is very loud and drags on much too long] A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! [Skip smiles and decides that a cute little retarded kid will do great for ratings] Skip: So, your a big fan of the man that started this I see. A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! Skip: How does it feel to have the man who helped you get ahead in life is a professional wrestler? A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! Skip: That's great. Do you think that the team of Cowabunga Connection will become PCW Tag Team champions this Friday? A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! Skip: What about his partner, 'Heavy Metal' Harry? A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! Skip: Who's the best wrestler in PCW? A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! Skip: Who is the best Yahtzee player? A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! Skip: Who is the greatest surfer of all time? A Tubular Tyke: TOMMMEEE!! [Skip laughs, feeling that this kid is somehow entertaining in a way. Now he'll stump the kid, show the world that his intelligence is low] Skip: What does my wife like to suck? A Tubular Tyke: HORRSSSEEEYYY!! Skip: HAHAHA! This Tubular Tyke program is the best!! [The camera man then turns the camera to the front entrance where there seems to be a slight commotion going on. Two unknown men have taken the stage and security guards all file along, trying their best to fend off the intruders. One of the men has long brown hair, wears a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. In fact, with that stocky build and the bloodshot eyes that guy looks like 'Heavy Metal' Harry. The other man has bright blonde hair and looks just like every woman's dream. Perfect tan, bright smile, baby blue eyes, and toned muscles that just need rubbing. It can only be...'Totally Tubular' Tommy! Everyone gasps in unison, as the Cowabunga Connection begins posing for the photographers] Skip: Holy poopstain, its the Cowabunga Connection! COWABUNGA!! COWABUNGA!! COWABUNGA!! Camera man: Thank you, JR. Skip: Who's JR? Camera man: Nevermind. [Harry and Tommy then walk to the podium, ready to give a most bodacious presentation] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDES AND DUDETTES! [The crowd cheers, happy that Tommy is hear for this special occasion] TTT: This is a most bodacious moment for me. I took time out of my most BRUTAL training to be hear today, for the TUBULAR TYKES!! [The crowd blares in another cheer as faint sounds of 'TOMMMEEE!!' are heard throughout the crowd] TTT: The Tubular Tykes have always had a most righteous place in my heart. Everytime a gnarly little dude graduates from Tyke University and goes into the public school system a big part of The Tubular One grows! 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: The second facility will also have gnarly work out equipment to help focus on developing TUBULAR mentally challenged athletes! Not to mention the most bodacious surfing simulator where all the little dudes can pretend to be just like me, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy! Harry: YEAH, MAN! [The crowd cheers again as Tommy now pulls out a pair of scissors, ready to begin the cut on the red ribbon at the entrance. Tommy then turns back to the microphone for one last thought] TTT: On Friday, the Cowabunga Connection has the most challenging match of it's career, dudes. About all of the tag teams will come down to a most VICIOUS gauntlent match for the PCW Tag Team Titles. All the dudes in the match are TOTALLY bogus. Look at Golden Game...they are totally into that Fidel dude. Harry: Fieldy, man? KoRn is far out, man. [Tommy looks at Harry as if he had just committed a sin] TTT: Your supposed to like only 80's metal, dude. Harry: Oh yeah!! KoRn sucks, man. [Tommy smiles as goes on ranting] TTT: Politically Incorrect are just even more bogus. They are into these politicians are all democratic. Its like...dudes...just be gnarly to one another! There is no need to fight over bogus political stuff. Harry: Politics suck, man. TTT: As for Fear Factory, we toasted those dudes a week ago. Rudy Poo and Mason Crapper. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: TO TUBULAR TYKES!! [Tommy then quickly turns around and cuts the ribbon. The crowd cheers and the photographers begin going crazy with the pictures. People begin filing inside. Then a police officer rides by on a horse. Skip Timmons sees the horse and begins laughing wildly] Skip: Hey!! Is my wife under there?!?! [The cop looks at Skip as if he's insane. Suddenly people begin looking underneath the horse] Cop: Well, I'll be a bare assed babboon!! [There is a woman hugging the bottom of the horse very tightly. She looks at everyone around her in horror. Skip Timmons, her husband, falls to the concrete laughing like a madman, finally losing his mind completely] Skip: Look at her!! She's getting busy on horse Co-- TTT: THAT'S TOTALLY BOGUS, DUDE!!!!! Harry: NOT Far out, man. Not far out at all, MAN!! [The scene then fades to black]
