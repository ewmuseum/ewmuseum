Date: October 6 of 2002 A.D. (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan’) They have come to call my crusade foolish, christening my actions as the limited works of a vengeful madman. And for that I can find no suitable response other than glorious laughter. I mock their ignorance as it shrouds their own fear, another of the ridiculous follies of human nature. What man fears, he either ignores or derides. Soon the Earth shall come to the realization that my actions are not to be parodied or dismissed. What started as a mission of petty vengeance has blossomed into something of infinitely greater bearing. Earth’s very destiny clings to my own fate. In my failure, the eternal balance of yin and yang remains in check and the great pendulum continues to sway without change. But in my success….or should I say, the success of the Old Ones who act through me as their Earthly vassal…..the winds of change shall shift and the race known as man shall be demolished under the emergent power of the Great Old Ones. And there will be nothing to save the meager forces of Heaven from falling under the masters as they again run amok across the cosmos. Everyone must start small, and my initial orders are no great exception. To some, the skirmish involving the factions of the PCW and PURE promotions has potentially cataclysmic repercussions in the world of professional wrestling. But to me….’tis but another day. Another night, another monthly Pay-Per-View event, and another haughty band of industry rebels to vanquish. The Old Ones, the glorious masters from beyond the void, scoff at such an insignificant Earthly conflict. The Black Pharaoh will not even involve himself, and as such has commanded I quickly nullify this measly campaign waged by the forces of PURE and their brash ‘general’, Jamal Wilson. I shall have no qualms about doing putting the pups back in their place. In fact I’ll revel in it. In my transition, nothing was lost of the wanton hatred or the thirst for spilling blood that consumed my being. The vengeance never died. Woe by the children of Japan. * * * Shanahan lay sprawled across the bed of his hotel room, his piercing gaze cast across the dim room at the carven effigy he carried as a symbol of luck. Produced in the shape of Great Cthulhu was the curious statuette, fashioned from onyx and hunched in a crouching position on its brawny haunches. The thing was adorned with folded back bat-wings, a mass of writhing tentacles at the mouth and eyes of shimmering scarlet crystal. After the original was mistakenly left in the Anaheim warehouse a week ago, he had abruptly demanded that a new one be sculpted immediately and blessed under edict of the Old Ones names’. The winged figurine bestowed on Shanahan a sense of divine duty and exponentially increased his lust for the blood of his enemies. The battle with the forces of PURE was only hours away, and even now he could feel the anger welling inside. Revulsion churned and gripped his black heart in a serene frenzy that would lay dormant until the time came to unleash it. From those crystal-carven eyes spilled projected visions of his enemies: Enmity, Cianan, S2, Ali Khadafi, Jamal Wilson……..their silhouettes pressed against the star-lit heavens, clawing and shrieking with chilling pleas for compassion as a throng of shadowy masses descended from high above to take their sacrifices…… A sudden spasm wracked the lightly dreaming cultist, shaking him from his visions and drawing him bolt upright on the rumpled bed to glance out the window. From below he could discern a mob of armed soldiers as they filed from an unmarked blue van resting on the corner of the parking lot. They collected just outside the rear doors below, garbed in fatigues and helmet-crowned gasmasks. In their hands they clutched semi-automatic weapons, and in the center the leader clutched a floor-map of the hotel. Bruce praised his gods for the psychic warning, as it was rhetorical their reason for being there. Discernible through the grime of the window was the melded form of the BC emblem he knew decorated the battle uniforms of the Blackhelm Coalition. The stolen Necronomicon, Bruce. They’ve come for it. Don’t let those mongrels of God take it. Shanahan leapt from his bed, snatching the burlap bag enclosing the book and then taking the carven effigy of Great Cthulhu. Before darting out of the room, his mind briefly registered the pulsating aura of the other unholy item in his possession which he had nearly neglected. He dashed across the room and reached beneath his bed, fishing out the invaluable Shining Trapezohedron artifact. The back door was hurled open below, and a hushing barrage of cries filled the rear lobby to silence the bystanders. The armed soldiers of the Blackhelm unit advanced into the lobby in combat formation just as above, on the fourth floor, Shanahan slipped from his room into the vacant corridor and toward the door leading to the old-fashioned fire escape lining the west side of the building. * * * “Shit!!” roared the operation commander, slightly muffled by the mask covering his face. He hammered his fist onto the bed of the vacated room in rage just as his radio crackled to life. “Sir! Culprit has been spotted making his way down the fire escape! West side of the structure! He’s holding a burlap bag, contents unknown.” Captain Grange slung his carbine rifle back over his shoulder, motioning to those checking the room. A swaying rifle barrel struck the teetering table lamp, sending it crashing to the floor. “Move out!” * * * Bruce glanced momentarily back up the escape, sliding his hands from the rail and dropping onto the concrete below. “Now what?” he growled through bared teeth. Have faith, mortal. It’s all being taken care of. Never doubt the influence of those you revere. This trifle annoyance been foreseen……and it is being handled. Ah yes….and turn around…. “Hands on your head! Hit the ground!” Petty vermin…..ugh. Do as they ask, at least for the moment. Bruce clasped his hands behind his head, not an unfamiliar sensation as he briefly recalled this identical experiences occurring so many times in earlier life. A horde of armed sentries stalked into the sunlight from through the rear door, weapons leveled at his prone body on the rain-soaked concrete. Enjoy the fireworks, mortal….. “Shanahan!” boomed the commander as his men took up his side in a watch pattern. “Surrender the book and yourself to us! There is no need for anyone to be harmed here today!” “On the contrary,” Bruce thought silently and morbidly to himself, the macabre chuckling of the disembodied Black Pharaoh reverberating through his mind. “’Anyone’ shall die this afternoon…..” Rifle fire abruptly tore through the steady ranks of the Blackhelm unit. Three went down with cries of anguish, weapons crashing onto the street. The leader, a captain apparent by the linked silver bars on his collar, frantically looked in all directions before another burst of phantom fire ripped into his subordinate beside him. “Dammit! Open fire! OPEN FIRE!!” His unit crouched in a circle, another torrent of heavy-arms fire exploding through the empty back lot and into the windows of the surrounding district from the Coalition regiment. With the agents of the Coalition distracted, launching volley after volley of rifle fire into the abandoned upper floors of the old city district resting on the outskirts of his hotel, Shanahan crept stealthfully away with the blasphemous book tucked safely beneath his jacket. He could hear more bodies hit the ground as the perimeter-occupying attackers, those worshippers of the land of the Rising Sun summoned in past nights through dreams by the Black Pharaoh, resumed their onslaught from the safety of distance and fortified walls. Miserable fools, replied a solemn and echoing tone within his mind. Their follies simply know no bounds….. Bruce’s pace slowed to a leisurely stroll, his hand caressing the Shining Trapezohedron resting snugly in the bag. “What of tonight, m’lord?” he solicited from the sinister entity of the abyss. If it were possible to hear a smirk, Bruce did so at that moment. Meanwhile, in the background, the sound of rifle fire quelled. Blood streamed through the cracks in the dilapidated parking lot as Shanahan made for the solitary taxi sitting so conveniently in wait on the east side of the hotel.
