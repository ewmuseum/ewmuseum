Life Without The Blade (Part II)

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/12/03 I sat home listening to the quite arousing broken record of anchor reports coming from the gibberish speaking South Korean news anchors. They'd been reporting the abrupt and haste sniping of two pedestrians in the urban city of Ch'ongju, near to where I would be battling at the time and event of Turmoil (Cheongju, South Korea).. but him being the headstrong motley I knew he would have a few mistakes and I'd been trying to point out those mistakes in my guidelines but he just probably trashed my ideas. He randomly went up to a defenseless looking woman of Asian descent (well at least from the appearance) and shot her in front of a crowd of pedestrians.. and then scrambling thinking that the police and investigative forces in South Korea would make this sudden case of murders haphazard. I knew he was a moronic thinker and should've left him with nothing so he wouldn't put me in any kind of trouble.. But if he was in trouble.. I was in trouble. Seeing the odds would be stacked against him, he would be the snitch that he'd always been and point me out.. To bring myself and himself down, together. My Life Without The Blade continued as my body was doused in sweat, and my misogyny was going in to a drought. I thought back to when I was more elaborate and organized where I had my sepulcher in the designated Bainbridge Necropolis and place the bodies there without notice. My ploys became more and more senseless and I began to lose thought from it but this negativity wouldn't bring me down because I never thought of my plan as dubious. The campaign I was running would work and be displayed from here on and would start in my wrestling career to where it would be open to millions and millions of harkeners and viewers. But continuing my bleak living I dismally sat on the pad's sofa, which was far from comfortable because it's age began to show. The drapes in my apartment were jerked shut and the light was blocked from bathing the place.. and seeing that I was too lethargic to stand up and switch on the lights I left myself in the darkness my life had continued to go through. But pondering in the dullness of the apartment, thinking about the impossible scheme of me now murdering the outside pedestrians and fellow commuters I had an idea that should've appeared much longer ago. From the past occurrences that had happened, me murdering the dainty yet baleful school teacher, Anna Blasch, having my daughter's murder arrange, and then to the random killing of a women in the urban streets of Bainbridge. It needed to yield.. feeling as if my ideas were being purloined by a thief I thought somewhere else.. Some place where there were women and a chance for me to REALLY make a point and impact.. I had verbally quarreled with them several times but I was thinking more fatal. As Frank and his idiotic ways of murdering were beginning to 'wreak havoc' over South Korea like a sudden epidemic I wanted to break in to an easy, far from difficult and cunning method of murder... There were plenty of women I could get my hands on and not be accused of criminal acts. My misogyny would be spread as television viewers would see such things and be motivated by such people like myself... and in the lightless room I thought, the company that had put me in this motel-like building, was just inspiring an aspiring genius and intelligent Misogynist. The once jovial yet secretive murderer, myself would be advertised and broadcasted worldwide.. And yes it was a sadistic and baleful idea but brilliant in my case. It was the ceasing of random and unpremeditated victims on the street walkways. The PCW would be the place where my misogyny would expand like a helium-filled balloon and would be an inspiring concept to many who would soon follow my feelings. I would once again begin to attenuate the female gender, and continue my once well thought out plan to diminish the ogre-filled gender. PCW would be the benefactor of my killings. My plot would convene at a certain time and date but assurance would be added to my strategy. The deflation of women in the world would embark and more specifically in the PCW and I wouldn't be the culprit of the homicides. No one would daunt the idea of me starting the once and once again ingenious gambit built by the misogynist, Stone Burns. And I kept contemplating as what I had been thinking was certain to succeed.
