Blair Witch: Shining on an attack of the Bitch?

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd May It is now obvious that El Diablo has numerous mental problems, but what can be done to help him? Will winning the PCW Television Title from The Pharaoh cease his madness? Or will it just be the start of a man on an insane power trip as the champion? Whatever happens, the fans now seem to be behind El Diablo after his recent changes. El Diablo is standing outside on a large acre of grass, out in the middle of nowhere. In front of Diablo on the grass is a tent that has been pitched up near perfectly. The tent is blue and small; it can only fit two people in. Diablo however is not alone, with him, and still tied up by strong rope is his wife, Angel, and on the ground on a nice piece of paper is his video camera. He likes to call his camera Bob, the reason why is unknown and El Diablo has not told anyone why, not even his estranged wife. But then again, why should he? Diablo is wearing some camouflage green pants and a tight white vest, showing off his muscular 216lbs physique. Also he wears black combat boots and of course his Mexican flag face paint under his eyes, as his long black hair blows in the gentle breeze. Angel is tied up and seated in the makeshift doorway of the tent, she is wearing the very same clothes she was kidnapped in- well after having the mud washed off of her from the Flaymin Aymin mud wrestling fight. The clothes are a tight black mini skirt and a tight white shirt, with her red bra in view. Her hair is starting to grow back and is already part way down the back of her head. El Diablo has literally taken him and his wife to the middle of nowhere, a perfect secluded spot. The grass is green, no mud traps around and several blossoming trees nearby. And Diablo's red car is parked only a few metres away in case he wants to up and leave at any point. The weather is perfect, especially for the hour it is, the wind blows gently as the sky begins to blacken as the day gets later. The Mexican Devil stands proudly outside of the tent he pitched and nods in approval of his work. 'This should last us the night,' speaks Diablo calmly to his wife. Angel just looks at him, and is still worried about how long he is going to hold her captive. El Diablo shudders. He is obviously cold now, but before heading into the tent he picks up his video camera from the piece of paper and places the strap around his neck. The wind picks up a little and the piece of paper slowly blows away into the distance. He walks over to the tent and ducks down, as he looks ready to get in. First he rolls Angel in fully and gets inside himself. He leaves the zipper door open for a while so he can catch some filming later on. Inside of the tent are two separate sleeping bags, a flashlight and a bag of chips, in case they get hungry during the night. Diablo shuffles over to his side of the tent and stares outside in awe of the beautiful night. Everything is silent, as the wind gently blows and no insects that could be around don't make a sound. 'Why did you take me camping?' asks Angel, breaking the impeccable silence. Diablo looks disgruntled and looks over at his wife. 'Look at it, look at the beauty and serenity, this is paradise,' states Diablo to Angel's shock. Angel begins to look at her husband in a whole new light. 'What is it?' whispers El Diablo to his wife, noticing that she was staring at him. She shakes her head. 'No, what is it?' he badgers her with eagerness to know. She looks at him and smiles. 'You've really changed, haven't you?' Angel asks him with the first smile she has had in a long time. El Diablo simply shrugs, and puts his index finger over his lips to indicate he wants her to be silent. He then lifts the video camera up to his face and switches it on and starts recording the silence of the night by etching closer and closer to the tents opening. Angel leans back the best she can whilst being tied up and just watches him film while staying silent herself. 'It's beautiful,' says the new Diablo with a gleam in his eye, filming the night scene. After a few minutes The Metaphor of Magic stops filming and switches the camera off. 'No matter how beautiful it is,' he says, 'We'll need all the warmth we can get,' finishes Diablo. He starts to zip up the tents door with one hand whilst grabbing ahold of the flashlight with the other. He flicks the button that switches the flashlight on and finishes zipping the door shut. 'There, now it should start to feel warmer,' El Diablo, says to his wife, who is now shivering on her side of the tent. 'T-t-thanks,' she struggles to speak as her teeth rattle together. Diablo places the flashlight in the center of the tent and leans over to his wife, he opens up her sleeping bag and gently places her in it, and zips it shut again. 'Better?' he asks with some concern for her well being. She still shivers but seems a bit warmer. 'Y-yes thanks,' Angel says while keeping her head down, but her eyes open. Diablo smiles and proceeds to get inside of his sleeping bag while leaving the flashlight on in the center of the tent. He keeps his camera wrapped around his neck and remains awake. A few moments of silence pass. 'Why are you doing this to me?' asks Angel, breaking the silence. She is obviously referring to her kidnapping by her estranged husband. He looks across at her with a stern look on his face. 'Why am I doing this to YOU?' says El D. with heavy emphasis on the 'I' and 'You' Angel nods. 'Yes, why?' she persists in having a reason. El Diablo just stares at her a moment, in utter disgust. 'Well, why did YOU kiss Jenna? Why did YOU stick with Aymin? Why did YOU leave me?' responds Diablo in question, whilst still emphasising the 'You' Angel rolls over in her sleeping bag so that she doesn't have to see her husband. 'Because you were an idiot,' she mutters. El Diablo simply sighs and switches the flashlight off and starts to head to sleep like Angel. Several hours pass. ~ ~ ~ Until rustling at the tent wakes up El Diablo. He immediately sits up and looks over at his wife whom is still sleeping. The rustling continues and Diablo knows it's not the wind as the top of the tent is motionless. El Diablo looks extremely worried and quite frightened by the freak occurrence. 'Oh my, they're after me, and they've found me,' he whispers to himself in worry for his well being. Or maybe El Diablo is just being paranoid. At this point he slowly leans over to his wife as the rustling outside of the tent continues. He shakes her in an attempt to awake her. But it only half works. 'Oooh Aymin, you're so big,' she moans in her dreaming state. Hearing that only infuriates Diablo, who then roughly shakes her, making Angel suddenly spring up from her sleep, but still drowsy. 'What?' she asks at her abrupt awakening. Diablo moves closer to her in panic. 'There is something out there,' he whispers to her. She looks around and sees the outside of the tent rustling. She gasps with fear, and then whispers to her husband. 'Well, go and see what it is,' Diablo shakes his head reluctantly. 'Oh Aymin,' she moans softly. This causes El Diablo to pick up the flashlight and switch his camera on. He leaves the camera hanging around his neck as he shifts his way out of the sleeping bag. He then slowly unzips the tent door, as the rustling gets louder. 'I can't believe I'm doing this,' he mutters under his breath. He steps out of the tent and walks around the tent in fear. He holds the flashlight and can't see a thing, yet the rustling continues. El Diablo at that point, then decides that it must be the wind and breathes a sigh of relief. He turns around and out of nowhere something jumps on him, knocking him down to the ground. El Diablo struggles for a few seconds. 'Oh no, it's got me,' he shrieks loudly in fear, 'They've finally got me,' he finishes. He suddenly gains control and flashes the flashlight over the thing on top of him. 'Heeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeres Johnny!!!' exclaims the thing. Diablo then looks at it a moment, and it is none other than the Taco Bell Chihuahua. Diablo shrugs the dog off of him and stands up, wiping the dirt off of him and looking down at the dog. 'You???' shouts Diablo the Chihuahua. The dog looks up at Diablo. 'I scared you huh?' it states in its typical Mexican accent. El Diablo shakes his head. 'No, not at all,' Diablo finishes. The Chihuahua says it best when he laughs at Diablo. 'I told you not to steal MY thunder in that commercial, but you had top, didn't you?' the dog yells at El Diablo. Diablo starts to walk back towards the opening of the tent and the dog follows him by biting onto the end of Diablo's pants. El Diablo looks down at the dog. 'I regret ever rescuing you from Aymin,' he says to the Chihuahua. 'Who are you talking to?' shouts Angel from inside the tent. El Diablo gets to the tent opening and steps in with the dog still attached to his pants. 'The dog, I'm talking to the dog,' he answers her, 'The dog talks,' he finishes. Angel smirks. 'Like she's going to believe that,' the Chihuahua says once releasing the grip on El D's pants. El Diablo looks at his wife. 'Hear what?' she responds. 'The dog talking then,' Diablo states with a passion, 'Go on, talk again,' he asks the dog. Angel listens carefully. 'Bark, bark,' says the dog. Angel shakes her head. 'You're insane and I'm going back to sleep,' Angel says as she puts her head back down and closes her eyes. The dog barks again. 'I'm going to get you,' it states to El Diablo. El Diablo looks downwards at the dog and then to his wife. 'Holy crap, I am Dr. Doolittle,' he exclaims, 'And the dog is trying to kill me' he shouts in some sort of fear. He then kicks the dog out of the tent forcefully and quickly zips up the tent door. He crawls into a corner and curls into a ball while shifting his suspecting eyes around. Angel notices his fear. 'Wuss,' she says.
