The lone member of SOCIETY?

2 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 2, 2003 Begin Transmission. “SOCIETY, the time is now to make the statement that must be made. You MUST obliterate your opposition!” (Michael Chang has returned to SOCIETY’s side, after the very brief absence. The President of the O.S.L. stands in his ubiquitous tuxedo, beside SOCIETY, who, as always, goes bare-chested, so that his ultimate message can be seen.) “So, SOCIETY, how has your training gone for this contest?” “It goes well, President Chang.” (Chang smiles, but SOCIETY does not. Something is amiss.) “What is wrong, SOCIETY?” “Nothing, Mr. Chang.” “You cannot fool me, boy, now tell me what is the matter!” “I feel alone…” (Chang sighs. This was a moment he knew was coming. SOCIETY was still a teenager. In his mind, he has had to prepare for what would happen when SOCIETY realized that he was virtually alone in the world.) “SOCIETY, you have been trained to be better than everyone else. One unfortunate, err, side effect of this, is that there are none your equal.” “Not even you?” (As much as Chang hates to admit it, he must..) “Not even I.” “Then, why do I follow you? Why do I listen to an…inferior?” (The words sting Chang, but he has the Order’s answer ready.) “While you are superior to me, you are not perfect. You will only be perfect once you achieve your goal. And, your protocol cannot be completed without help. I provide you with that help. That is my job, my destiny in life.” (SOCIETY looks upset.)“So, you are the only companion I am doomed to have?” “Not exactly, SOCIETY. There are women as well.” “Women?” “Well, err…I’ll explain to you later.” (SOCIETY nods, appeased, as Chang is thankful to have squirmed his way out of THAT particular line of questioning.) “What you should be worrying about is the matchup you have against three other men on Tuesday Turmoil. It is a challenge, but not far beyond your first matchup. What you did to The Ahtumoth shall stand as an example to all who oppose our order of society!” “Indeed it shall, President Chang. He is but the first neck in my path to snap.” “Be wary of your opponents, however. Krikorian is back once again to face you. He is a wily man, not to be trusted, and possessing of a certain innate skill.” (SOCIETY nods, having recognized something in the ex-sniper himself.) “It is interesting to me, Chang. Krekorian seems like a man who, given his profession, would be an ally of ours if not for the circumstances..” (Chang grins, ready to teach SOCIETY another important life lesson.) “SOCIETY, in war, it is imperative to note several things. The first is an old Arab proverb..” “You mean, the enemy of my enemy is my friend?” “Precisely, but there is more to it. In war, alliances constantly shift, to the point where your enemy is your friend. It is vital that you not shun an alliance that is advantageous due to past feelings. Your job is not emotional. Your job is just that, your job.” “So, you mean that even Shane Wethers could be an ally someday?” (Chang wrinkles his nose at the thought.) “Well, perhaps not EVERYONE..let us not speak of that slime. You shall destroy him, and in doing so, do a great favor for mankind worldwide.” “Sir! Yes, sir!” “Excellent! Now, tell them what they are going to do come Tuesday!” “Oh, must I? I’ve been saying it a lot lately…” “You need to! We’re putting it on the Order t-shirts!” (SOCIETY sighs.) “Fine then….prepare to CONFORM….TO SOCIETY!!” (SOCIETY and Chang turn to leave…as we fade away to static.) End Transmission.
