Don't Mess With The Monkey (part 1)

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/03/02 The scene opens up outside of Kramer's two story home in Tampa Bay Florida. Its another simple weathered day in Florida. The sun is out and shining and the wind is barely noticeable. We see the front porch which has vacated lawn chairs to the right and a small table holding a cactus on the right. In the middle of the porch is the front door which suddenly bursts open. Holding several black garbage bags is Kramer wearing a baseball cap which he has on backwards, a black shirt with a gray wolf on the front, and lion on the back, khakis, and finally sandy colored shoes with bright orange shoe laces. He caries them to the edge of the street and places them next to other bags already there. They he rubs his hands together as he walks back inside. The scene switches from the outside of his house to the inside which is bountiful with furnisher and other accessories everywhere. Kramer walks into his living room and flips on his big screen tv. Instead of sitting right down he decides he needs something to eat so he goes into the kitchen. A special news report pops onto the tv screen. Its a news reporter named Nancy Deern. Nancy Deern: The Rumpledink Circus has had an escapee. Binky the monkey got out of his cage just a few hours ago and is currently running loose in our area. Lets go to Miles Churn for more information. Miles: I am here with the ring master and owner of The Rumpledink Circus, Alfred Winnipeg. Is there any information you can give the folks at home about Binky? Alfred: Yes, he is a brown and white squirrel monkey with a long tail. He is also wearing a red collar. If you spot him please call 1-822-DinkCircus. Please do not try to catch him on your own. Binky can get pretty hostile around strangers. Miles: So parents please lock your kids inside and keep on the look out. Back to you Nancy. Kramer comes back into the living room with a sandwich and small bag of cheetos. He flips the channels around not really caring for the news. He finally rests on a Looney Toons episode. There he sits for a short while watching television while munching down on his sandwich and chomping down on Cheetos. As he is finishing up a crash can be heard from his attic. Kramer leaps up to his feet. Kramer: What the heck!? He bolts up the stairs and is ready to open the door to the attic when he pauses for a mere second. He walks into his room and when he comes back out he has a wooden bat in hand. Kramer: Now I'm ready. He slowly pulls the door open and walks up the steps trying with all of his might not to make a sound. He finally reaches the attic and steps in noticing the window which is open, and an old coat rack that was knocked over. He marches towards the window and peers out but he doesn't see anything. Kramer closes the window and starts to walk when he sees something moving from beneath the white sheets draped over a couch he used to have in his living room. With bat raised, he walks towards the couch and slowly lifts the sheet. He bends down to look under it but nothing is there. As he gets back up standing tall he sees a monkey sitting on top of the couch eye balling him! Kramer: Oh....its a cute little monkey! Hey fellow what are you doing up here? Kramer slowly approaches it putting his hand out trying not to scare it. His fingers finally touch the top of its head. He pets the monkey, and soon his other hand comes over as he tries to pick the monkey up. Big mistake! The monkey jumps at him and clings onto his head! Kramer is flailing around while screaming at the top of his lungs. He tries hard to peel the monkey off but it just won't budge. After a few minutes of struggle the monkey leaps away and flies down out of the basement like a bat out of hell. Kramer reaches up to his face and feels a bit of blood from the scratches the monkey caused. Kramer: That was way too freaky! Where the heck could a monkey had come from anyway? The sound of objects thundering to the ground and breaking can be heard from downstairs. Kramer: It looks like I have other things to worry about first. Kramer races down the stairs in hopes of stopping this mass destruction. When he reaches the living room again he almost cries as he sees pictures, vases, and even his TV are knocked over and broken! To fight fire with fire he picks up the broom from his kitchen and starts looking for the little rascal. Kramer: Here monkey monkey monkey! Come out where Uncle Kramer can see ya. Come on you little devil get out here! The monkey appears. It looks ready to attack Kramer again as he backs it up in the corner. Kramer raises the broom and is about ready to beat the crap out of it. But he slowly puts it down. Kramer: Oh I can't do that to you little guy. As Kramer puts the broom down the monkey latches on to his leg. Instead of fighting it Kramer picks up the phone and dials the SPCA. Kramer: Yeah I need someone to come to my house to get a stray. Man: What kind of animal is it sir? Kramer: You see this may sound weird but its a monkey. Man: A monkey? Sir does this monkey have a red collar? Kramer: Hold on let me check. Kramer bends over and looks at his leg. He looks around the monkeys neck and sure enough its a red collar. Kramer: How did you know that? Man: Sir you just found Binky the monkey. Kramer: Hmmm? Man: He escaped from the Rumpledink Circus earlier today. Do you think you can keep him there until we arrive? Kramer: Sure. Kramer hangs up the phone and walks over to his couch. He sits down and Binky releases its hold on his leg as he climbs on to the couch next to Kramer. The scene slowly fades to black. Just under a day away from a huge PPV March Madness and Kramer has to deal with a monkey? With all of this will he be able to manage against three men he has had no real incounters with? This isn't just any old match. Its for the number on contendership of the Cruiserweight Title, something Kramer has held before. And to top it off its a tables match, something Kramer hasn't had much experience in. Can Kramer, an old timer compared to his opponents, manage to defeat these fresh faces and start off to a road of success? Start off to a road he has tried to travel on many times before but has failed?
