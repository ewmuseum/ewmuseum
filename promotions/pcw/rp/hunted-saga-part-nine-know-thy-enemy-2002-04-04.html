Hunted Saga Part Nine: Know Thy Enemy...

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/02 Crichton, the deliverer of trouble, has decided that the best way to keep himself out of the custody of the army and out of the interest of the new found enemy, Ms. Zemoff – an independent weapons contractor, intent on capturing Crichton for the US Military to curry their favour in order to get a new weapons building contract from them, was to make it as difficult as possible for his enemies to find him. The only way to do that, as far as Crichton knew was a cross-country trip, with the PCW. For kind of trip, there had to be cash to keep the boat moving as the saying goes, and so Aeryn and Crichton go to the bank to withdraw their savings from the bank. As Crichton as become accustom to however, trouble is not far around the corner, as Fleming is already waiting for them in the bank and a couple of minutes later a bank heist is progress. Evidence enough to suggest that where Crichton goes trouble soon follows… The camera opens up back inside the bank Manager - Mr. Lebowitz’ office – where Crichton and Aeryn are still sat in they’re allotted Mahogany chairs, as Mr. Lebowitz goes through some documents, which will ultimately allow Aeryn to withdraw her savings from the bank. Sat before them in a black suitcase, containing her money and as Mr. Lebowitz finishes signing the documents before, he pushes the case in Aeryn’s direction. “This is now y….” Mr. Lebowitz began being cut short by a loud booming noise. Crichton had been in the army long enough to realise that the noise came from some kind of gun and he felt the same sickly feeling of danger grip him once again. Crichton grabs the suitcase and hits it in the cloak cabinet, Aeryn and Mr. Lebowitz look at him in bewilderment. “Don’t get me wrong I’m not a pessimist, but a gunshot in a bank ain’t a good sign.” Crichton explains. Mr. Lebowitz stands up with a shocked look upon his face and Crichton noticed how pale the man looked. “Are you saying that there is a bank robbery in progress?” Mr. Lebowitz questions hoping that the inevitable isn’t the answer. “I can’t say that sir, but I am taking precautions just in case I …” Crichton trails off as the office door bursts open – almost off its hinges – and a man wearing a long black leather jacket and a stocking over his head enters the room, waving his sleek looking shotgun around. “Like we said out there, this is a bank robbery! So get your asses out there with the others!” The robber began, then looks over to Aeryn. “Hey baby hows about me and you goin’ one on one.” Crichton feels his blood boil at these last statements and lets his anger take over and attempts to rush the robber. The robber however, simply puts the butt of his shotgun into Crichton’s gut and then again across his face. Crichton simply slumps to the floor on his hands and knees, spitting out blood onto Mr. Lebowitz’ once perfect carpet. The robber kneels down beside Crichton. “Ah I see we got a feisty one in our midst! Well let me reassure you guy, next time I won’t hesitate to blow your brains out, if your that stupid to challenge me again that is!” The robber states menacingly, grabbing Crichton under the arm and dragging him to his feet. The robber motions to Mr. Lebowitz and Aeryn, with his gun in the direction of the door. “Go on get your asses out there!” The robber demands. Reluctantly they both head towards the door, Crichton on the other hand has no choice in the matter and is dragged into the main, marble tiled banking area. Crichton is simply thrown to the floor and Aeryn kneels down beside the prone body. Aeryn takes her jumper off and places it behind Crichton’s head, to prop it up. She looks on intently as Fleming stealthily crawls over to Crichton and Aeryn’s position. Aeryn looks on his too begins to check Crichton’s vital signs and generally to see if his target is okay. For if Crichton died he would not be paid and there would also be a blemish upon his great career record and he definitely could not allow that to happen. “He’s gonna be fine. He’s just got a little concussion, it’s really nothing to worry about.” Fleming states confidently, with a little relief in hi voice. Aeryn breathes a sigh of relief. “Thank you. You risked a lot just coming over here to check that and I appreciate it.” Aeryn thanks. “It’s no problem really.” Fleming replies. Fleming decided that in the kind of situation he found himself in, there was very little to do than know thy enemy. Then if Fleming’s target did somehow manage to escape, at least Fleming would have some insight into his targets mind frame making it a little easy to track him down again. These methods he was about to incorporate, were not strictly by the book for his company, but Fleming was also a loose cannon. “We need to do something, we cannot allow the bastard thieves to escape with other people’s money” Aeryn states defiantly. Fleming develops a distasteful look upon his face and begins to shake his head. “Why do we need to do anything? It isn’t our problem, we might as well just ride it out and most people gave insurance for these such cases.” Fleming declared. Truth be told he didn’t like the idea of petty criminals getting away with this crime, but he couldn’t risk the targets life in any sense of word. Fleming knew that if Aeryn got involved in this thing, Crichton would too and in all likely hood they would be killed. “We at least have to try!” Aeryn almost pleads. “This kind of thing will get you killed and it will get your man here killed. The risk is too great we might as well just let it happen. This way there will be no casualties.” Aeryn nods in agreement, realising the wisdom in the man’s voice. Aeryn suspected that he knew about such matters and decided not to question the comments further. Then one of the bank robbers walks over to them and reloads his shotgun in front of them for fear effect. “Now, which one of you all is the bank manager. I’ll give ya three seconds to own up, then I start shootin’ people.” The robber declares, attempting to intimidate the audience before him. Aeryn was intimidated, but also knew the frailty of the bank manager, after the meeting she and Crichton just had with him. Aeryn turns to the bank manager and nods a ‘no’ to tell him that he shouldn’t own up. The robber steps forward menacingly. “One…Two…” The robber instantly stops counting as the old Mr. Lebowitz slowly gets to his feet. “I’m the bank manager.” He declares, stating the obvious. The bank robber smiles. “Good for you, now give me the key!” The robber demands. “What key> I don’t know…” Mr. Lebowitz trails off as the robber grabs him and throws him against the wall. The robber then raises the shotgun up to eye level of Mr. Lebowitz. “Don’t give me that ya old fool, I know there’s a key, so just hand it over and nobody will be hurt!” The robber suggests with an incredibly violent tone embedded within his voice. “Okay, Okay, calm down the key is in my pocket I’ll…” Mr. Lebowitz trails off before the robber delves into the mans pockets, one after the other until he finally finds the key. The robber takes the key out of his pockets and holds it up to the light, entering the room via the dark blue blinds. He nods with a smile upon his face then throws the key over to the other robber. The robber then grabs Mr. Lebowitz by the collar and throws him in the direction of the crowd with great force. He then points a single finger in Mr. Lebowitz’ direction. “If you ever defy me again you old fool, you will get the full force of this shotgun.” The robber orders before joining his fellow robber in the banks vault. Aeryn notices that Mr. Lebowitz is shaking profusely and so stealthily crawls over to him. She puts her arm round his shoulders in an attempt to calm the old man down. “It’s okay Mr Lebowitz, everything will be okay.” Aeryn soothes, but then Mr. Lebowitz grabs his left arm and begins to shake even more. Aeryn was no doctor, but she instantly recognised the first stages of a heart attack and by the look of Mr. Lebowitz, she reckoned that it was a very bad heart attack. Fleming crawls over soon after the attack. “Heart attack?” Fleming ventures. Aeryn simply nods and then looks back into the face of the old man. Then a stirring noise can be heard back in the direction in which Fleming came. He and Aeryn both look around to she Crichton sitting up, feeling the back of his head carefully and then feeling his jaw. “What in the hell hit me?” Crichton question with pain etched into his face. Aeryn smiles at the comments. ‘Just like Crichon’ she thought. She then turns to Fleming. “Hey man, you look after Mr. Lebowitz, while I check on Crichton.” Aeryn commanded. Fleming simply nodded in response as she lets her grasp go of Mr. Lebowitz and silently crawls over to Crichton. “Hey what’s been going on since I was out?” Crichton questions. “Nothing much, except Mr. Lebowitz has just had a heart attack.” Aeryn explains, trying not to panic Crichton. “Oh hell, we’d better try and get him some help.” Crichton comments before getting to his feet. Crichton looks over towards the robbers. “Hey guys, this guy needs medical attention!” Crichton shouts in their direction. With that one of the guys exits the bank vault and walks over to Crichton. “What the hell are you hollerin’ ‘bout fool?” The robber questions. “I said this bank manager needs medical attention, he’s had a heart attack.” Crichton repeats. “Why should I give a fuck?” The robber questions. “Because when you get caught for this thing and if this man’s dies, you’ll also be charged with murder!” Crichton states almost patronisingly. The expression on the robbers face does not change and he simply shoves Crichton back down to the floor. “We ain’t gonna get caught!” The robber declares, before walking away back into the vault. The robber gets to the door of the vault, just as the other robber flies out of the door with astonishing speed and hits the wall with such velocity, a dint appears in the plaster work as he slumps to the ground. The other robber simply looks at his friends. “What the fuck is goin’ on around here?” The robber questions in surprise. Then as the robber turns to enter the vault and red and blue blur flies out from the place and lands upon the posing wall with grace. Crichton looks at the strange creature and recognises it to be none other than Spider Man. Spider Man then fires webbing at the other robber and as a resultant the robber is fixed to the wall. Crichton stands up. “Hey Spidey, this guy is having a heart attack over here, you gotta get him to a doctor!” Crichton shouts. Spider Man simply web swings over, grabs Mr. Lebowitz and disappears from everyone’s sight. Crichton laughs to himself as the camera cuts to outside the bank, the timeframe being approx. one hour later. Crichton and Aeryn are seen standing by their white car, with their black briefcase full of money in hand, as Fleming walks past. Hey, thanks for the help back there!” Aeryn shouts over to Fleming. Fleming walks over to Aeryn and Crichton and stands before them. “Hey that’s okay, let me guess your John Crichton.” Fleming declares. Crichton instantly felt his blood rush to his head, he didn’t like this, he didn’t like this at all. But then he figured that nobody from the army had found his identity and if they had he would probably in there custody. “Yeah, why?” Crichton asks hesitantly. “Well, because your coming with me!” Fleming declares. Pointing a pistol at Crichton. Crichton takes a step back in shock. “What the hell are ya doin’?” Crichton questions. “I’m from a special government agency, hired to track you down John. Now come quietly and no blood will be spilt.” Fleming replies. Crichton nods, “Ah right!” Crichton simply says as he knocks the direction of the barrel of the gun away from him, then whacks Fleming across the face. Fleming stumbles backwards momentarily in shock at the speed of Crichton’s attack. Crichton follows it up by booting Fleming’s wrist, forcing Fleming’s gun out of his hands. Crichton then plants a knee into Fleming’s gut before, ramming his face into the metal boot of his white car. Fleming doesn’t get up from this, but Crichton doesn’t wait to find out and gets into the driving seat of the car and starts the engine. Fleming pushes himself up off the ground and slumps over the boot of the car. The car then begins to move and in a second the car moves away at speed. Fleming simply slumps to the floor on his back. A smile grows upon his face and it slowly develops to a maniacal laugh, as the camera fades to black.
