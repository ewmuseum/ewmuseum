Scattered Life (part 3 final)

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-4-03 A replay of previous events plays on the screen. It goes from Brianna, to her confrontation to with the woman, to her talking to Raphael, then her leaving, then finding out what Thomas had done, to her getting onto the bus, and then it stops. The man has just gotten on the bus, and takes a seat, like he had done before, and it rolls from that point. 'I don't mean to be rude, but do I know you? You look so familiar.' she says in his direction. 'You might. I know you though.' 'How?' 'You're a PCW wrestler. I have been a big fan of PCW for quite some time and follow it constantly.' he replies not looking up, but inside of his bookbag. 'So have I. That's why I decided to join in the first place.' Brianna continues to stare the man down, and is shocked to see something jump out of his bookbag. At first it scares her a bit, but seeing the cute little creature climb out on top of the bookbag and just sit there relaxes her fear. 'Is that your pet?' 'Yeah, this little dude has been with me for a while now.' The man reaches down and scratches the top of the animal's head. It's at that point that Brianna's face lights up as an imaginary lightbulb starts to flash brilliantly in her head. She takes one more good look at the man as she scrunches her face trying to compare him to the image in her head. Of course the mouse should have given it away, but Brianna didn't really think much of it until now. 'You're Kramer Wild Wing aren't you?' Brianna asks with a little squeak in her voice. 'No I'm just Kramer Rinc now.' he replies not really suprised that she recognized him. 'Whatever happened to you? You were having a great run in PCW and then you were just gone.' Brianna finds herself standing up and taking a seat closer to Kramer waiting to hear his story, as she has always admired him for the things he had done and accomplished. The bus slows down as it comes to another stop. Kramer stands up with Grimer up on his shoulder. 'This is my stop.' He starts to walk and he gets off. The scene cuts to us seeing his rough face and the bus behind him. The door closes and as Kramer starts walking towards the right, we see Brianna standing there, trying to catch up to him. 'Wait!' She gets him to stop as he turns around and manages a smirk. 'Persistent aren't we?' He asks with a little chuckle in his voice. 'To be honest I haven't been having a great day, and would just like someone to talk to. Never would I have guessed I would meet you. I'm not going to claim you were my favorite wrestler or anything, but I always loved watching you and your finisher was one of my favorites! Very simple to do but interesting.' 'Your making me blush over her.' he flashes a big smile as he reaches out his hand and catches a small raindrop that falls from above. 'Since it seems as though a storms coming, would you like to go get some coffee so we could chat more?' 'Yeah, I would really like that.' Kramer being the gentleman that he is, extends his arm and Brianna puts hers through his as he leads her down the street and into a cafe. The cafe is nice and quaint, with very few people inside. Kramer and Brianna take a seat in the corner which is dimly lit giving them a bit more privacy in their conversation. A waitress with spiked black hair and a pale complexion walks over to take their orders. Brianna orders a cappichino, and Kramer orders a simple cup of black coffee. As they wait for her to come back with their drinks, Kramer taps on the table and Grimer runs down from his shoulder and sits where Kramer's finger is at. Kramer then reaches into his backpack and pulls out a little chair and Grimer jumps into it looking as relaxed as a little mouse can. Brianna looks pleased with the little chair as she cracks a grin. 'You never did tell me what happened to you and your PCW career.' Brianna throws out trying to start up the conversation while checking out her well manicured fingernails. Kramer sits there seeming as though he is trying to think of where to start. 'Well my father had fallen ill, and I devoted my time to being at his bedside which left little room for wrestling. Besides there was no point in carrying on if he wasn't there to support me. He was the foundation to my career, and since he wasn't there it just toppled. Then one day.....one day he passed on.' saying this causes Kramer's voice to crack as he tries fighting back the heartache that the memory brings on. 'Since then it's just been me and little Grimer.' 'I'm so sorry to hear about your father.' Feeling that she has upset Kramer in opening up a bad memory, she doesn't say anything after that and things grow very quiet until the waitress returns with their drinks. They both take sips and then place thier cups down onto the table. Kramer starts to tap his fingers on the table looking around as the silence has taken up everything once again. Kramer looks out the cafe's glass windows and sees two big men pull up wearing suits and looking very unapproachable. Kramer stands up in a jolt as a sense of fear crosses over him. 'Something wrong?' Brianna asks seeing his facial expression. 'I have to go.' His voice is shaky and strained as he reaches into his bag and tosses an envelope to Brianna. He takes off into the men's bathroom just as the guys come strolling into the cafe. They look around and see Brianna looking distraught but pay her no mind as they walk back out as quickly as they came in. Brianna looks very confused as she holds the envelope in her hand. She then looks down to see Grimer sitting there fast asleep on the chair. What is she supposed to do with him? The scene slowly fades out. If life wasn't messed up enough, now Brianna finds herself stuck with a prodigy mouse. Questions begin to scroll through her mind as she stares out towards Grimer's direction. It sucks to have a SCATTERED LIFE....
