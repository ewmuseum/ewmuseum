The Training - Chapter 2 - Gaining favour

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th August 2002 ***OOC Note: The following RPs are from a series I started while I was back in PWA. I shall be carrying on the series here in PCW so I figured it might help to provide you with the beginning segments of the series. Thanks :-)*** Chapter 2 – Gaining favour (Women in the world of wrestling are very scarce, and those who do find employment are normally hired for their physical ‘attributes’ rather than their actual wrestling skills. Charlotte is looking to be one of the latter. Sure, her body is capable of beating anyone in a beauty contest, but she feels she can be more than that. At the end of the day, it is the people who win titles that are remembered, not those propping up the bar dancing around a pole.) (Charlotte stretches out on the couch in a room affectionately known as the living room, snuggling up to the soft brown fur like material the seating device is wrapped in. Balanced carefully in her hands is a bowl of Kellogg’s Corn Flakes, but as she scoops the food into her mouth her attention isn’t drawn to the semi-skimmed milk dripping down her chin. Her eyes are focused on the television, the colours flickering and lighting up the otherwise dark room. It is six o’clock in the morning, the curtains are drawn, and the only source of light shines out the television. The frenzied sound of a wrestling announcer emanates from the small speakers, just loud enough for the sound to vibrate the molecules required for Charlotte to hear the articulate words. These words let us know that at this tranquil, perfect hour of the morning, Charlie is watching Japanese women’s wrestling.) Commentator: SHOOTING STAR FROGSPLASH! She hit every inch of that! (The commentary wasn’t in Japanese, as one might expect, but the world’s most popular language of English. After her visit to the gym, she had told the owner Bill her plans to become a professional wrestler. Although he was profoundly against the principle, he caved in to Charlotte’s charm, and the following day he brought in some of his Japanese videos for her to study. They were cheap, home made, with very poor quality that contained lots of static and interference. Even the commentary was bad, executed by some young American who appeared to enjoy screaming ‘HOLY SHIT!’ at the top of his voice. But the wrestling was amazing, and that’s all that mattered to Charlotte. Some of the moves were breathtaking, and whenever she closed her eyes visions of flying through the air flowed freely in her dreams. She couldn’t wait to paint a spectacle with her movements. But as she placed her cereal bowl on the clean red floor, the creaking of the floorboards upstairs could be heard. Silence closed in once more, until the pained cry from the bedroom.) Joe Nero: CHARLOTTE! WHY ARE YOU WATCHING WRESTLING VIDEOS AT THIS HOUR? (Joe didn’t even have to see the proof, he knew what she was doing. Ever since that fateful moment in the kitchen when she revealed her deepest desire, she hadn’t been the same. Every spare moment was no longer spent with each other, but the television and those damn wrestling videos. Grumbling to himself, Joe reached up the back of the wooden bedroom door and put on his black cotton dressing gown. Once out onto the landing, he slid slickly into his slippers, before descending carefully down the stairs, each individual step creaking so loud he was positive every cat in the neighbourhood would wake up. Holding carefully onto the rail, he finally reached the bottom, and made the left turn into an isolated living room, as since moving in three weeks ago they had only unpacked the bare essentials. In all her beauty, Charlotte was opposite him, lying on the sofa, transfixed by the television. She gave a casual wave, followed by an incoherent rumble of greetings, but her stare never relocated to another target. Whilst rubbing his eyes sleepily, Joe slumped down in his cosy arm chair. Slightly worried about his girlfriend, he enquired as to what wass going on.) Joe: What’s going on Charlotte? (There is no reply, but a significant snort which indicates she does not appreciate being interrupted during her valuable educational viewing.) Joe: Ever since you discovered wrestling it’s almost been like you’re not interested in me anymore. Anything I suggest is resigned to the trash container as a stupid suggestion. Yesterday I had to do everything, and simultaneous to my generosity, you watched those darn videos as if your life depended on them. I could even say you were obsessed. We haven’t even had sex for three nights, because when I come to bed you claim you have to ‘learn some new spots’. Well I think you owe me an explanation don’t you? Of what status is our relationship in your life Charlie? (Joe is all worked up by now, and Charlotte grudgingly accepts she cannot learn anything from watching her unique videos at this time. Reaching for the remote, she presses the sponge like red button, resulting in the TV screen sweeping to a dull black, and the whirring of the video player slowing down to a halt. Sitting up abruptly, Charlotte reaches for the lamp. The light dazzles the young couple for a few seconds, and they take time out for their eyes to adjust. With a big sigh, Charlotte sets about the ominous task of explaining the magic surrounding her new occupation.) Charlotte: Joe, I understand this isn’t easy for you. Up until now you have had my undivided attention, because there hasn’t been anything else in my life. Our relationship is still the most important thing in the world to me, but I need a distraction, something to fall back on. It’s not healthy to concentrate all my energy on one aspect of life. I want to feel the whole spectrum, experience what is there to be experienced. Joe: Does that mean you have to ignore me? Up until now our relationship has been amazing. Lots of interaction, fun moments, great sex, but all of a sudden you seem to have lost interest. You think everyone in wrestling has dire relationships? Charlotte: Now you come to mention it the success rate isn’t all that high. All the time on the roa... (Her voice trails off as she sees Joe cock back his head and look at the textured white ceiling. He eventually brings his head back down and stares longingly into Charlotte’s eyes.) Joe: At first I was concerned because wrestling isn’t the most welcoming place for a beautiful young woman such as yourself. They see your good looks, visions of dollar signs glaze their eyes, then they chew you up for as long as they can then spit you out, leaving you with zilch. But in addition to that, you’ve become possessed. Nothing else interests you. Not even me. Charlotte: It’s just at the beginning stage Joe. When a young boy receives a new toy at Christmas, he will enthusiastically play with it to his hearts content, and everything else is ignored for the time being. But eventually the other fun making objects will interest him once more. Joe: So now I’m just a toy, something you can play with as and when pleases you? (Charlotte visibly lets out a heap of air, exasperated that Joe cannot see her point.) Charlotte: Of course not Joe. I love you and I always will do, but wrestling is like nothing else I have ever experienced, it’s pure escapism. Ever since I hit my fifth birthday, I have had to live with the burden that I am illiterate, and with that burden came the realisation that I was different to everyone else, because everyone else could do things I can’t and probably never will be able to do. Any job which involves reading or writing, gone. Any part of every day life which involves reading or writing, gone. Any man who doesn’t like me not being able to read or write, gone. And I owe a lot to you, because you don’t care about my impediment. (Joe’s face lights up briefly, glad he’s at least done something right. He leans forward in his chair, wanting to hear what his girlfriend is driving at.) Charlotte: Because of my illiteracy though I’ve never been able to have a job, and you know it. Virtually every job out there requires at least minimal reading and writing. Not much, but enough to rule me out as a potential employer. When wrestling came along, it was like my own personal messiah. Wrestling doesn’t require book reading, and performing a body slam doesn’t demand the writing of an essay. All I need is my own body and brain. For the first time I have something I can do, and because the emotion is so new to me, I treasure it almost as much as I treasure you. Joe: Sure. (Charlotte reaches down next to her empty cereal bowl, and grabs the china mug by the handle. The warm liquid runs over her lips, into her mouth and down her throat, in a moment of reflection.) Charlotte: I know it must be hard for you Joe. Previously you’ve had me all to yourself, and I’m a pretty sweet thing to have! (Joe comes and sits next to Charlotte and holds her hand. They laugh together in one of those happy moments that a couple cherishes in their memories.) Charlotte: But please be patient with me. It’s a difficult time, finding my own feet out in the real world. Paying more attention to my videos than to you is not intentional, it’s just an automatic response at the moment. Are you with me? (Joe smiles at Charlie, their eyes locking together. They’re glad they’re on the same page and that a crease in their relationship has been well navigated.) Joe: I’m with you.
