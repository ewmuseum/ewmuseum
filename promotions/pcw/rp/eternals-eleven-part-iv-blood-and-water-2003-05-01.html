::~Eternals Eleven~:: (Part IV- Blood and Water)

1 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/01/03 Prelude: Music, one of the most important sources of everyday life. What we hear is usually what we believe and what we believe is what spurs us on. Bands such as The Beatles, The Rolling Stones and then Nirvana have given us the influence to do certain things in our life, just like an innovator. Three excellent musicians were formed by fate to bring the passion back into music and give the people something to believe in. With a clash of guitars, highly-strung lyrics and catchy punk anthems, Ice Age are quickly becoming the highest selling band since the Beatles.Now it’s time to put all rumours aside and turn negatives in to positive, hence the importance of the E-Fest. For one of the very first times, PCW has a hand in promoting a Festival that fans will remember for a lifetime, but until then Shane has to continue the hunt for a security team so the Festival doesn’t turn into a disaster. Shane has already assigned one but has a long way to go. Now its time for the next step in what we like to call ‘Eternals Eleven’. ____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________ The scene begins in the late evening. Shane Wethers is sat in an outside bar in central Chicago, wearing his usual attire consisting of black baggy jeans with a chain hanging from the pocket, a black t-shirt that says 'The Eternal Punk' in bold blood red letters and aluminous yellow Nike air trainers. Shane's bleach blonde spiky is in the usual style and Shane wears his usual Gucci sunglasses to protect his over-sensitive eyes. He is sat in the bar next to a close friend and associate, Mikey Fishmore. Mikey is a smartly dressed businessman, currently working as a full-time con man and one of London’s most successful leading men. Shane sits back on a blue chair with a pint of lager in his hand, whilst Mikey sits back with a drink of martini and lemonade, admiring the quiet atmosphere “Well here we are Guv” Mikey states in a sly tone of voice “Chicago, time to bring Eternals Eleven up to two” Mikey takes a drink from the glass and places it back down on the oak table in front of them. “Alright, three if you include myself” he adds. Mikey and Shane laugh lightly ”Yeah, I quite like getting away” Shane says whilst scratching his head, “I mean we spent so long at home in England whilst arranging a rock festival of the century and as if it couldn't get any better, we are taking small vacations. I know it ain't exactly Hawaii but hell, I could definitely get use to the different lifestyles out here.” Shane takes a deep breath, breathing in the musty air of Chicago “...Besides,” Shane adds “I get pick the chance to make use of my dumb-founded cousin. The London Boys and the mafia, that’s one hell of a team.” Mikey shakes his head and laughs, amused at Shane's modesty “At least it’ll take things off your mind, ahead of your matches this week and next” Mikey points out, whilst puffing away on his cigar. Shane sits back in his chair as a cocky grin appears on his face, almost as if he was plotting something within the dark reaches of his mind ”Don't you worry about that...” Shane reassures him “I have it all taken care of.” Shane looks across at Mikey “You see, this Friday I’m up against some washed up never-been who really needs to retire, Adam Stryker” Shane says with a glint in his eye “He says I better get ready? Yet the guy hasn’t even heard of me. His lack of knowledge is going to hit him in the face, literally”Shane reaches forward picking up his glass and takes a long drink from it. He places it back down onto the table and glances at his watch.“Stryker has gotta be the most Borrrrrrrrrinng guy in PCW history” Shane adds with a cocky expression upon his face, “damn.....if the guy keeps this up Eldarado will be makin' a comeback!” Mikey coughs and takes another sip from his glass. He acts as if he's about to ask a billion-dollar question “Then you got your four corners match at Turmoil” Mikey reminds him, “win both and you’ll have quite a following, but you’re a main target for every one of those nobodies”. Shane frowns and stares into space, suddenly a sadistic grin appears on his face as Mikey watches on rather concerned “The world is crying out for a role model like myself,” he replies “as soon as I accomplish my goal then Christmas will have come early. They are all talented athletes, but they aren’t exactly the sharpest knife in the drawer” Mikey shrugs and scratches his head “I wouldn’t know,” Mikey adds with a small grin and croaky voice “one of the guys thought up a name like ‘society’ all by himself.” Shane grins and nods his head “You never know” Shane replies, “it could have taken him hours, probably due to too many chair shots to the head. I wonder how the real society is going to feel when I make him bleed infront of all of his peeps” Mikey agrees and takes a puff on his cigar again, looking into thin air “I bet he’s really a smart guy,” Mikey adds, “Maybe he’s got one of the non-active brains or something” Shane and Mikey fall into laughter as they lean forward and finish their drinks. Shane stands up, pushin the chair under the table “Well that’s Turmoil, but this Friday Stryker will realise that if a teeny-bopper is some one who beats the hell out of lame-ass pickledicks with an attitude problem then I am the Elvis of the teeny bopper world.” Mikey grins and nods his head “Anyway, go speak to Huey and stop putting it off” Mikey declares. 'I'm not putting it off...' Shane says before hesitating a little '...ok, maybe i am' 'Well don't, you can't afford to ponder over the situation' Mikey points out, 'Its a hit or miss situation,' Mikey says with a sigh 'but if i can remember Huey correctly, he'll be happy to help out' Shane turns to walk away “you got my number. Just gimme a call when you've done and I'll meet you. We can pick up some beers and maybe a pizza.” Shane nods his head and feels his stomach rumble Shane: Yeah...yeah that’s a good idea. I aren't gonna be too long anyway, I'm pretty hungry. Mikey: Ok, later man. Shane turns and walks across the street towards another joint on road. (...to be continued)
