Getting Extreme - Part 1 - Sky Diving

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 28 [The scene opens on an airplane flying high in the sunny sky. The rays of sunlight beam off the stainless steel airplane, making the light reflect into the camera. The plane seems to be about twenty thousand feet in the air, a nice height for very extreme sky divers who are looking for a super rush] [Inside the plane isn't very large as it's hard to fit just the three men who plan on sky diving today, and they are: 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, and their newly acquired friend and healer, Manfred. Harry and Tommy are dressed in their wind suits, helmets, and parachutes strapped to their backs. Manfred sits in the back corner with black, bushy mustache twitching like crazy, showing that he is truly nervous about this. He wears his perfectly tailored tuxedo, with the parachute stapped to his back] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Sky diving is totally bodacious, dudes. This is truly the extreme of the extreme. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry shows off a cheesy grin as he peels off some sort of sticker and then slams it on his helmet. The sticker reads 'Twisted Sister'. Manfred still sits in the corner just shaking his head, not really thinking that sky diving is the right thing to do right now. Manfred, even though he can be slow sometimes, knows that this isn't a way to prove that your extreme enough to be PCW Extreme Champion. Maybe Harry and Tommy are taking things a bit too far] [Tommy sees Manfred's nervousness] TTT: Manfred, there is nothing to be afraid of, dude. You just dive out of the plane, then once you get down to five thousand feet you pull the cord! It's simple, even HARRY can do it. [Harry turns around to Manfred and gives him a thumbs up] TTT: See. [Manfred just looks up at Tommy and shakes his head wildly] TTT: Come on, dude! This is to prove to all of our bodacious fans that we are extreme enough to hold the PCW Extreme Title. What better way then to show them our sky diving expedition! [Manfred then holds up one finger, signifying that only one man can win the Extreme Title] TTT: Your right about that, dude, but me and Harry are best friends. You have to understand that we sometimes share the same brain, and with one dude with the title, the other dude has it too! So, we both will win no matter what, compadre. [Manfred shrugs and shows a complete look of confusion as he doesn't understand that last comment. Sharing a brain or not, one man will win the title and get to hold it while the other gets nothing] TTT: There are going to be a lot of dudes in that rumble in Cinncinatti. Now we have to show them intimidation, and we have to show these dudes exactly what we're made of. There are other tag teams in the mix too, but none of them are as close as me and Harry are to each other. None of them! We are the most bodacious tag team in PCW, and now we are about to prove that we are the most extreme in PCW. Harry: Yeah, man. [Manfred nods wildly, agreeing with Tommy. He then points to the door and then points to Harry and Tommy. He's signifying that they are PCW stars and should jump without Manfred, who has no part in being in PCW. Even though he did help deliver a surfboard to Politically Incorrect on this past Friday Fusion] TTT: Your part of the team now, Manfred. You took away that most bogus illness that was in me for over a week. I'm much better now and I owe all of that bodaciousness to you, MANFRED!! [Manfred nods a bit with a smile on his face, proud to have helped out The Tubular One. Then Manfred thinks about smashing into the ground from twenty thousand feet, and that would be quite a horrible thing. He then shakes his head, now holding onto a pipe above him] Harry: He's a fraidy cat, man! [Tommy then walks over to Harry, who is standing near the opened door of the plane, anxious to dive out into the sky] TTT: Dude, this match is going to be totally hard. It's going to be an extreme match in the Cincinatti Airport. Imagine the totally gnarly extreme things you can do in an airport, dude. [Harry smiles devilishly] Harry: Like run over people in one of those airport carts, man. [Tommy laughs loudly thinking about the stoned Harry driving in an airport cart, running over fellow PCW wrestlers] TTT: That would be a most bodaciously hilarious sight, dude. Or we could hit someone with the Bodacious Bombs off those jet way things down onto the runway! That could be PCW highlights for years to come, dude! [Harry smiles, then thinks about that for a moment. Now this is an every man for himself type of deal. Even though he and Tommy are the most bodacious tag team in PCW they have to forget that, considering singles gold is on the line. Why would they use the Bodacious Bombs, Cowabunga Connection's finisher, in an every man for himself match?] Harry: That would be far out, man. I was thinking this is every man for himself and that we wouldn't need to do that, man. TTT: Yeah, but we can team up and kick bogus butt until we are the only two compadres left in the match. Then we'll battle it out to see who is the most extreme of the Cowabunga Connection! Harry: But what if your knocked out and I decide to pin you during the match, man? TTT: Why would you do that, dude? If we are going to team up then you should help me up and make sure no other bogus dudes pin me. It's only logical, compadre. [Harry seems to be getting frustrated about this. It's EVERY MAN FOR HIMSELF and Tommy is making it seem like this affair is going to be Cowabunga Connection versus everybody] Harry: There is a singles title on the line, man. If I'm going to win that thing then I will pin you to get it, no matter when I do it, man. TTT: Dude! Why are you being totally bogus about this?! We're tag team partners and your trying to bring heinous tension between us only a couple days before the match! Harry: Far out, man. Then we can just go in there as enemies, man. [Harry smiles, feeling that he is going to get his way] TTT: DUDE! [Tommy pushes Harry, then Harry pushes Tommy into the other side of the airplane. Tommy looks at Harry in surprise that he had enough strength to push him that hard. What the hell has gotten into Harry?! Is this Extreme Title shot truly getting to his head, hence making him quite bogus?] [Tommy then grabs Harry in a headlock, wrenching it in as tight as possible, to maybe pop that bogus idea out of his head. Harry just uses the eye poke technique to get Tommy to let go. Now Tommy is blind and Harry is extremely light headed from that super tight headlock. Manfred finds courage and gets up from his corner to help break up this meaningless fight. He stands between the men and gives them each a stern look. He towers over both of them, and is using his size to intimidate his newly found friends] TTT: He started it, dude. Harry: No way, man. [Manfred once again gives them a stern look with an arm on each man's chest. Harry and Tommy both look at each other viciously, which is something quite rare] TTT: You need to stop being so bogus over this Extreme Title! We're tag partners, we shouldn't be doing this. I don't see the Heartbreakers fighting each other, compadre! Harry: Who cares, man! TTT: I care, dude. This team can be the next great tag team champions. We need to stop this fight over the Extreme Title. We can just go out there and have a most bodacious good time like we always do, and kick some bogus butt! [Manfred smiles at Tommy and gives him a thumbs up, happy that Tommy is looking to heal the wounds] Harry: But I WANNA BE EXTREME CHAMPION, MAN! TTT: Dude, you never know, you just might be that. I was just saying we can be Cowabunga Connection until it's just us left! Then you can face me. Harry: NO, MAN!! I'LL KICK YOUR ASS! TTT: SHUT UP, DUDE! YOUR BEING BOGUS! Harry: Am not! TTT: Are too! Harry: Am not! TTT: Twisted Sister SUCKS, COMPADRE!!! Harry: GRAH, MAN! [Harry pushes Manfred's large arm out of the way and goes after his life long best friend and tag team partner. Manfred separates them again] TTT: Get off, Manfred! We'll settle this the most bodacious old fashion way! [Manfred shakes his head viciously] Harry: Yeah, man. Let go! [Manfred shakes his head once again, standing his ground] Harry and Tommy: LET GO! [They push Manfred backward with full force, sending him out of the airplane. They would hear him scream in anger, but he's mute and can't say a word. Maybe that's a good thing. Both men walk to the edge and see the small dot of Manfred falling quickly to the ground. They then look at each other, then back toward the dot that represents Manfred, then back at each other] Harry: Sorry, man. TTT: Me too, dude. Harry: Do you think he'll pull the string, man? TTT: I hope so. Harry: Far out, man. [The scene then fades to black as Harry and Tommy leap out of the airplane to enjoy their day of sky diving]
