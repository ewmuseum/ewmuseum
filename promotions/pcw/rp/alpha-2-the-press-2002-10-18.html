Alpha [2]: ...The Press...

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.18.02 Everything from here on out; Dante Jackson would sit in reflection of what just happened before. Sure, it gained him two matches and still brought him close to finding what victory actually tasted like even more, but still it wasn't a single juvenile point in his mind that loss was around the corner once again. Inside of a rain entrenched window on the outside; he sat in silence starring at the pitter and patter of raindrops tapping along the window sill. That is; until he bowed down and looked into a blank piece of notebook paper with an ink pen sitting right next to the rings. He couldn't find a single word in his head to put down on the paper; a case of writers block gone bad to a talented writer, he thought to himself. None of it mattered to him though, just writing down little memoirs of how life goes. A knock on the door puts all of his thoughts in disarray. 'Who in the fuck...' he grumbled to himself, before opening the door, facing a short man with plastic rimmed glasses. He only stood there, before Dante sighed to him, 'Well? The doors open, and waiting for closure.' The smaller man looked back at Dante with a glee in his eyes, and a buck-toothed grin on his face. Dante only smirked at the man, before returning to his pen and pad, jotting things down that came from his brain. The attire for the occasion wasn't much than a gray designer suit with a nice dress shirt and a colorful tie to go underneath. 'Stevens...' Dante chuckled to himself, before rubbing his head with his free left hand. 'Well, Dante; its about the New-' 'Oh, not you too!' Dante interjected before Stevens could finish the sentence. Stevens sat down on the bed, fidgeting with his fingers, trying to come up with a bullshit story to please his client. 'You know we had to discuss it sometime or another! Either you give them a guilty plea and its fifteen days in jail. Otherwise...its up to two years.' Dante's eyes widened at the mention of the last words, 'Two years? For a disturbing the peace charge? What kind of shit is that?' 'Its New Orleans shit, that's what it is.' Stevens quipped, pulling out a pack of Marlboro's and a lighter. 'You want one?' he instigated to Dante for taking one, but the playa rejected the offer. 'Suit yourself.' 'Don't fucking smoke in my room Stevens,' Dante warned him, albeit a little glib about it. 'its bad for my health and yours.' Stevens grunted, before disposing of the light fixture to blacken his lungs. He quietly coughed, before turning towards Dante. 'I see you're tuning up your rhymes.' 'It helps me think; besides why the fuck do I need counsel from a guy who can barely hang onto his wife?' Dante asked slyly of Stevens, who jerked off of the bed and onto his feet, hardly laughing. 'Fuck you.' It was all Stevens had for a rebuttal towards Dante's comment as he only could tell it like it was. Hell; not getting any from the wife does that to you sometimes and I guess this is the point of the day where Stevens jokingly didn't take them all to accord. He twisted around with his spectacles, before Dante could oblige him with a retort, 'Aren't we pissed off? I thought you came here to discuss legal battles, not playing 'Foul Mouth.' 'Doesn't it surprise you?' Dante cracked his neck, 'Does what?' 'The whole part of you having to end up back in Philadelphia, its maddening.' Stevens stated. Dante smiled to him, 'Now you're becoming that English guy off of Test Drive for PS2. I mean, what the fuck is wrong with happening a little hustle go on in your lives?' 'Where are you getting at?' Stevens asked. He only meant the obvious by it as he shifted back to the pen and paper on the coffee table. Stevens stopped this, by slamming his hand on the table; interrupting whatever thought process Dante happened to be going through. 'Answer my question.' 'What the fuck?' Dante vehemently asks of Stevens, boiling with rage for trying to stop his creative notion. 'Explain it to me how you can take all of this so damn easy?' Stevens examined the whole Philadelphia case, penning back towards the initial report of the incident in the Mayor's office. Dante responded, 'Because simply, I am the fucking Lyrical Messiah, a person who takes all the HUGE bullshit and makes it small.' Stevens sighed, before knocking his head along the wall, guttural moans echoing from his lips. Whispering; 'why' in repeated voices, three. Dante only chuckled at the look on Stevens face; before getting up from his chair and looking his lawyer dead in the eye. Stevens wiped the small substantial tear from his eye and nodded to Dante. 'Its my case, not yours.' He walked out of the room; taking a brown briefcase with him in hand and Dante stood there; leaned up against the small pillar of doorway to support him. His eyes were weak, and the neurons sent him to closed the door. Tiring, muscles acing and mind racing towards a migraine; all he could do was think of how to end it all with a single breath. He thought deeply about this; this mad raving poet of the ghetto who could have done difference about his life, yet kept towards a lifestyle lived by so many souls whom have passed so many tears. He thought about this; before realizing that Fusion would come back from pause on Friday. The question fired back at him like a revolver bullet aimed directly at Kenny Rock's head.'How does it feel to gear up for another shot at the top spot in the country?' Six months ago, nobody had heard of Dante Jackson on the rap scale, only few glimpses of the man at work. 8.5 million copies later, he still can't believe that his star has gotten so big. Seemingly trivial moments past, the reporters questions dart inside and out of his mind. He looks at them, before flashing his ever used to smile and answering their question, 'Its like a roller coaster, and its at the very peak of high.' One man, about thirty two years old with a cornflower blue Polo shirt and some khaki slacks stands up in front of the podium, a few feet away. 'Mr. Jackson sir -' 'Please call me Dante, Mr. Jackson sounds like I'm the King of Pop!' laughter followed after the comment, before Dante nodded to the reporter once more. The reporter continued on, 'Well, Dante; its been notified by the Associated Press that you have been indicted on counts of disturbing the peace, and rioting for the city of Philadelphia on account of that you caused such an occurrence. Any comment on that?' 'What's your name?' Dante asked the reporter; sternly fixated on the badge clipped to the breast pocket of the Polo shirt he was wearing. 'Jo-Joesph Jones.' the reporter stammered. Dante pondered, clutching his chin. 'Well Mr. Joesph Jones, if I were telling the explicit truth, then you'd think forty lackluster talents from all over the world could beat Playaz Inc. If I were telling a lie, then you'd think that Playaz Inc. would run amuck over two dumbasses from all over the world. Need I say more?' Jones shakes his head, before turning over to another colleague of his; a nineteen year old with a gold goatee and tarnished yellow hair; dimmed down a few notches. He stands up in a tailor made black suit with a blue dress shirt underneath with the collar folded along the back of the suit jacket. 'Trey Eversole, 411 Wrestling. So are you predicting that Playaz Inc. will get a victory Friday night?' 'Its not a prediction. Its pure logic and if you don't understand that? For those whom have lost to me, its their fucking problem, not mine.' A sight of laughter follows as Dante exits the podium proceeding. He walks out of the back door, exiting towards a bright sunny day in Long Island, New York. He wears a Islanders Hockey Jersey with black pants and a pair of Nike Men's Battlegrounds Air Max Elite. He climbs inside of the driver seat of his 2003 Mercedes Benz and closes the door right behind him. He cranks up the car, only to a notch behind the drive notion. The radio dial appears on the screen as he looks at the station: Hot 97.1 and leans back into the chair, starring deep into the sun. It was a plan, taken back from the land of rhyme.Taken back from the year 1999A space age odyssey known to woman and manI come out of the game with a master planThe Jack to your AceThe Mace to your FaceIt rains down jagged drops and shines centerpiece notchThe names the DJ from Brook to the bayLooked out my window and saw a dead bird soarBlinked out my eye while there's a omnious knock at the doorWho am I?The man who criesA solider who diesA man you never can defineWho am I... He dreamed inside of his mind; thoughts escalating from victory to defeat. And all of it happened so quickly, that within a blink of an eye they passed on and away from him. He switched the car into drive and backed out of the complex's parking lot before heading down towards the Brooklyn Bridge. An abrupt destination saw him land in the front of a local eatery, small yet known for the small jokes and nice cheese pizza. All he could whisper to himself, while enticing company of his own was how he was going to do things his own way. 'Hello sir, how may I take your order?' The question loomed out in front of him, before he nodded his head and shook away, his stomach wanting nothing but victory to quench its thirst. Looking up at a big screen television; he saw the likes of Doug Troy, Frisco, Rent-A Hero, Jason Snow, Clyde, Xavier Kannon running rampant in their various foray of spotlight in their heyday. He muttered words towards himself, before looking up at the person in front of him - his tag team partner. You might know him as the one half of the current PCW Tag Team champions, the only undefeated man in PCW as of the moment, a man of the Playaz Inc brotherhood. Or maybe you can just call him Statuz Quo. 'Sup baby?' Quo asked Dante, who looked up at the Syndicate champion with a glee in his eyes. 'Sup playa?' 'Nuthin much, jus' tryin' to get ready for dem' hoes on Thursday.' Statuz told him. Dante nodded. 'Fo' sho', dem fools always talking shit, tryin' to figure something out to beat a playa, NOBODY can beat a playa. I thought chu wuz still in ATL, holdin' down sum of dem Crip bitches?' Dante asked. 'Couldn't stay home dat damn long anyway; these peeps get away with too much shit and we can't let that happen. Especially not three times in a fuckin row.' Statuz boasted. Dante rolled up a joint, placing it into his mouth before getting a lighter and preparing to light it. Whispers from other people in the restaurant cause Dante to look around at them as if they were the idiots; in his mind yeah they were. 'Them, I mean we do enough day in and day out and what the fuck do we get in return? Shown the hell up?' Dante pondered for a second, before ending his statement. 'not fuckin' happenin' again.' A smile grew on both of their faces, before they enjoyed the cuisine of the restaurant and laughing it up about how typical of it was for them to be facing the entire PCW roster for a title belt, people coming from the PWA, from platforms that Dante had known of since he got into the sport. 'So, what's up for these cats?' Statuz asked, sipping on a bottle of Cristal'. His body accustomed to the usual day in and day out wear of stardom. Wearing a Jason Kidd New Jersey Net Away Jersey with a pair of Iceberg shorts and Gucci gray and black Air Force Ones on his feet, nothing didn't speak of the word glamour on him. 'You sho' about wreckin' these fools?' 'Oh fo' fuckin' sho'!' Dante exclaimed. Statuz nodded to him, before exiting the hotel suite and moving down towards his own. Dante looked down at the figure of how many copies were shipped and how many were bought of his first album. The label sent out a million for Street Manifesto, and nine-tenths of that happened to be caught up. He had the highest first week output by anybody on the year. He went to sleep that night dreaming. Dreaming of adding another million and W in the column.
