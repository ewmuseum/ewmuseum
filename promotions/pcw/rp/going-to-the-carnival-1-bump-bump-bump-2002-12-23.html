Going To The Carnival [1] Bump Bump Bump

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.12.02 'I don't think you're quite the man for the job, but its Carnival of Carnage my friend, not some fantastic soap oprea played out by television stars!' The scene happens to be darkened, by shallow glass, white drapery, and a vast amount of machines hooked up to bodies and bodies of men and women inside of the hospital. Various peopel lay in beds, suffering from head wounds, stabbings, shootings, piercings, flame retardant chemicals, or Vice City Syndrome, take your pick. The bland white walls are either marked up by crayons, posters entralling signs of 'Get Well Soon' and 'Hope You Feel Better'. As the camera pans away from those, a scruffy looking Caucasian man walks into view, sporting a light green jacket and blue jean pants. 'Who's the man here lov? Paul Kent buddy! Not a single one of you wankers can fuck with me!' 'Sheeca, cut that bullshit out outside, the fucka's giving me a damn headache.' 'Nigga, yo head already messed up from that chairshot, shut up and let me handle it!' He walks around, his hands pointed upon his hips as if he were a spitting image of Mick Jagger as he struts down the corridor. 'Hey luv, you know me - Paul Kent, no that's right I'm the world famous Paul Kent, call me the guv'nor round here.' he tells nobody in particular, but his voice loud enough to excite a few members inside of the hospital. The man struts around, until a young woman approaches him, slamming him up against the wall. 'Look,' the woman stated briefly, before hoisting the man up even more. Even though he looked to have a tough guy persona, Paul Kent was nothing more than a loud mouth, broken down from a little boy who happened to watch too much eighties television.'can you please just shut it? You're worse than that Gaz Green character.' He nods, before the woman drops him back on the ground. She turns around, her slender curves leading her back in the room. Paul pops right back up, 'Yeah, that's right - walk away like a pussy, I'll knock you spark out!' The woman smirks, before peeling back a shower curtain revealing to be the former PCW Tag Team Champion and former RUSH Syndicate Champion Statuz Quo, laying back with his head rested back on a pillow thanks to a well placed Chet King chair shot during the ending of the Treble Jeporday match at PCW's Decimation Pay Per View. 'Muthafucka,' Quo lifts his head up from the pillow, but a seering pain causes him to drop back down, clutching his head as his hands reach over for the nurse pad. The woman comes over to console him. 'Aww, poor baby.' 'Bitch, shut the fuck up and help me!' the former Syndicate champion and current champion of ebonics screams out as the woman backs away, before releasing a deadly right hand which Quo blocks. 'Are you out yo mind?!' 'Who you callin' bitch?' the woman screams out as she shuts the door. Soon, a parked car can be heard from downstairs as the camera cuts away from there and down into the streets of Toronto, various passerbys and bike riders weaving their way in and out of traffic. Slamming the door of a black Cadillac Escalade 2003 edition, is Dante Jackson still fuming from last night's loss in the sleeper of the night Treble Jeporday match. He cracks his neck as the passenger door slams shut. On the opposite side of him, the wife of Jackson, Tracy still looks as fine as she usually does in her supermodel look for the day. 'Somebody told me Quo was here.' the African American states as his wife hangs onto his arm. As they take their steps into the hospital, the screams from the upper medical room can be heard as Quo tosses a lamp at the female. He grunts widly, before leaping back onto his feet. 'Sheeca, get yo ass here!' 'Dammit Quo, didn't I tell yo ass never to call me a bitch? I'll fuck you up for it!' the woman sharply bites into the leg of Quo who howls out in pain as he tries to shake her off. Sheeca pushes herself off of Quo, before charging ahead with all her might. Quo ducks an incoming punch and lifts her by the legs. Holding her up for what looks to be a sidewalk slam attempt, the woman screams playfully as Quo tosses back ontot he bed. Quo snickers, 'Why you keep wantin' to fight me?' Sheeca snickers before the two kiss. Scanning back down to the lobby, Jackson and Tracy enter the hospital as Dante still shows the few bruises he received from the match last night. Smiling back at Tracy he leans over the counter of the hospital, intent on looking for where his tag team partner happens to be. Looking at the fair woman in nurse uniform in front of him, Dante raises an eyebrow. 'Um, hello. I'm looking for a Statuz Quo.' The woman paises him no attention whatsoever, Dante turns around and whispers towards Tracy who shrugs her shoulders. 'Tell her a little louder...' Dante nods, 'Alright, alright.' turning back tot he woman in front of him, the former Tag champion smiles brightly before tapping the desk once more, the woman fainting an eye towards Jackson. 'Like I said before, I am looking for a friend name Statuz Quo, I heard he was in this hospital.' The woman sighs, going back to what she was doing before Dante slams his hand on the counter. 'Look bitch, I don't give a fuck if you hear my ass or not, but you need to fucking tell me where the fuck is Statuz Quo or I'll be Tim Walden and go Spaztastic Combustic on your ass!' Jerking her head towards Dante in a start, the woman props herself upward and stands in a military position, a non robotic look on her face. 'Erm, are you alive?' 'Yeah, but you didn't have to threaten me with Tim Walden. You could have said Kai and just slapped me with a fifteen part story that not a single person will go on watching.' The woman tells the rap superstar, who 'oooohs' to the comment, before turning his back towards the receptionist. He whistles, 'Wacko.' before spinning his finger in his ear to show that the woman probably was clearly clincally depressed and up to the skull in tranquilzers. 'That's nice,' he repeats to the woman with fair auburn hair and a nice complexion towards her. 'but where is Statuz Quo?' Turning her head towards the security cameras stationed near the front desk, the woman looks into the left most television screen, on the center row as she spots the bald head of Statuz Quo looking upward in the camera as Sheeca sits on him, grinding. Her eyes bulge open as the loud scream of 'FUCK!' echoes throughout the hall as the receptionist turns her head back towards the Lyrical Messiah. 'Well, he happens to be sexing up a woman right now.' 'Aight,' Dante nodded. 'Just tell him that Dante Jackson is on his way to get up there because we have Tag titles to win...'
