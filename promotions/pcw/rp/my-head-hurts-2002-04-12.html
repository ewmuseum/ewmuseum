My....Head.....Hurts....

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/04/02 Ellie~!’s Prologue : We, like, finally got rid of him! Rather than accepting his defeat like a man, Jeremy Howard had to use his brutish nature to exact some revenge on Xavi. So after he caved his skull in with a big stick, Tyler SO TOTALLY fired him! That, like, SO rules! However, I don’t think Xavi is too happy, after all, it was his head that got the pounding. But he did get to be the Commissioner though, and I’m not quite sure YET what that means, I’m sure I’ll work it out on...What I do know is that it makes me COMMISSIONERESS ELLIE~!...And I know what that means, it means YAY ME!.... “MY FUCKING HEAD IS KILLING ME! I WANT MRI’S! I WANT A CAT SCAN! A HEAD CT! I WANT A CHEM SEVEN! I WANT A CBC, LIGHTS, TOX SCREEN AND ANYTHING ELSE THEY HAVE ON ER!!!” Our stop opens up on the PCW Continental Champion Xavier Kannon sitting on a hospital bed with his head heavily bandaged, in fact, there are so many bandages around it it just isn’t funny. The Scientologists has a pained look on his face as the splitting pain from Jeremy Howard’s Blackjack blows still shoot around his skull. He is still wearing his wrestling gear of a pair of Black worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green embroidery snaking down the left leg to where a pair of Black, Purple and Grey Skechers adorn his feet. Xavi has also managed to pull on a Black COMMISIONER t-shirt with Purple lettering that looks like it must have been out on before the bandages, seeing as the collar wouldn’t fit around them. Xavier : “I could have brain damage! I can’t have brain damage! I’M THE COMMISSIONER OF PCW! It’s my job to...Er...Commission! How can I do that if I am sitting in a wheel char, dribbling and shouting XAVI! over and over again!” The young Doctor who stands before him looks at the man strangely, having seen hypochondriacs day in and day out he seems to have little patience for a man who exaggerates his injuries. Doctor : “Sir, we told you, there is no damage. We will prescribe you some pain killers and you may experience some mild headaches for the next few days....Other than that there is no lasting damage.” ......I think Xavi was a bit annoyed about having his head turned into a punch bag...You know, like, the kind of punch bag you hit with a big Black thing really hard. Anyway, I suppose you are wondering where the brand new Commissioneress is? Well, you like SO should be...... As soon as the Physician finishes speaking the piercing Green eyes of the Gold Patron Meritorious look up to him with spite emanating from them. Xavier : “No LASTING damage? THEY USED TO SAY THAT SMOKING WASN’T BAD FOR YOU! THEY STILL SAY THERE IS NO SUCH THING AS GULF WAR SYNDROME! I could leave this place and a blood vessel could pop, my head could explode and I’ll drop down dead in the gutter...I CAN’T HAVE THAT HAPPEN TO ME! I have matches to Commission!” The Doctor furrows his brow at Xavier’s angry outburst and again checks the chart, seeing that his patients pupils were fin indicating no damage to his brain. Doctor : “Frankly Sir, that isn’t going to happen...” “I found him...He said that if you didn’t get everything you asked of as a fellow Scientologist then he would fire anyone who denied it to you...After he made them was the homeless people for 3 weeks...” Both men turn to look as Xavier’s valet Eleanor walks into the examination room, Kannon’s Continental Title slung over her shoulder proudly. .....It’s amazing how many people are Scientologists! I mean there is Xavier... Tom... John... Kirstey... That one we say on Tuesday, you know, Shaft...Um.... Jenna.....Thingy....That other one....And the head of that hospital too! Although he isn’t a Gold Star one like Xavi so he has to do what I say, and not just because I’m the Commissioneress..... She is wearing a White, skinny fitting t-shirt with COMMISSIONERESS written in Purple across her 32B chest. With it she has a pair of faded Blue hipster jeans with a rainbow coloured fabric belt, and around her wrists she has hr usual plethora of bands and bracelets, topped off with a chunky watch. Her long Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights if un-tied and cascades down the small of her back, just reaching the top of her jeans. The Doctor turns to face her, worried by the news that one of his superiors is dictating practices to the staff and he is curious to find out who it is as behind him Xavier still holds his heavily bandaged head, grimacing in pain. Doctor : “Doctor Kline, the Chief Resident said that?” He asks it in a shocked voice, hoping that someone else who knows the Emergency Room like he would issue a mandate like that. Ellie~! seems confused at his question, wondering who that man is. Eleanor : “Who is Docto Kline? And why would I want to talk to him if he is only a Chief Resident? Why would a fine Scientologists work in a place like this? And why haven’t you given him a skull transplant or something...His head hurts you know!” The Doctor’s facial expression can best be described as ‘Dejected’. Not only is he 7 hours into a second shift, not only is he still $49,000 in debt from Medical School, but now he has to deal with an awkward patient, his preppy girlfriend, and their friend in high places. He sighs and tries to work out who gave the order. Doctor : “Did it come right from the top? Is Mr George the head of the hospital a Scientologist?” Ellie~! sneers and rolls her eyes. Eleanor : “Pur-LEESE!!!!K Like we would talk to a mere hospital manager, this order comes from like, WAY higher up!” This news seems to shock Xavier as much as it does the Doctor, who is now getting quite worried given his demeanour towards Xavier before. Doctor : “Is it the trustees? Or the Senate? IS GEORGE BUSH A SCIENTOLOGIST?” With these panicked words Ellie~! takes a rare moment to think, looking up to the ceiling as she ponders it over. Eleanor : “No...Wait...I think it WASThat George guy....” The Doctor now appears to be relenting, realising that he is being controlled from up above and that he will have to carry out the un-necessary teats and waste valuable time treating the Scientologist and his every whim...That and deal with Ellie~! too. Doctor : “Okay Mr Kannon, I will carry out the tests you requested...Now if I can draw some blood....” As the Doctor summons a Nurse into take a sample of the Scientologist’s blood his eyes widen and he pulls his arm away, looking at the pair with wild eyes. Xavier : “YOU CAN’T TAKE MY BLOOD! I MAY NOT HAVE ANY LEFT! I WAS CUT OPEN ON SUNDAY AND ON WEDNESDAY! My veins could be dry! Anyways, how do I know that you would not take my fine Scientological blood and try to clone me? You’d b surprised how much a clone of an advanced physical specimen like me could fetch on the Black market!” ....For some reason Xavier doesn’t like his blood being taken...Or people in White coats...but then again you can’t blame him. He is, like, the best wrestler ever in the history of the world so if them made another one he’d...Um...Not be?...... The Nurse and the Doctor look to each other, showing the bemused look that only people who meet Xavier and Ellie~! face to face can manage. The Doc gestures for the Nurse to leave it and she heads out of the room to tend to something actually important. Doctor : “So what do you want us to do for you Mr Kannon, without blood we can’t do some of the tests you asked for.” As Xavier thinks about what he wants done, Ellie~! takes the chance to interject herself into the discussion, pointing out excitedly what tests she wants to see. Eleanor : “Like...PUT HIM IN THAT TUBE THING! Those things, like, SO rule!” Xavier : “Yeah...Put me in one of them, that should be able to see if that sick, unprovoked, unwarranted attack by Jeremy Howard has had any lasting effect on my perfect Scientological brain...And if it has, then I can sue that sack of shit for every penny he owns until his Wife is out working the streets and his kid is taken away from him!” The Doctor nods tentatively and writes the tests on the cart, Xavier relishing the prospect of finding out if he can sue Jeremy Howard, even though the flip side to that would be him having brain damage. Ellie~! walks over to the bed and drapes the Continental Title over his shoulder as the shot fades out on Xavi’s face. .....I like this Commissioners thing...People do what I say which is hella cool because I, like, say some really neat stuff!..... “Sir, you can’t take that in there with you, it will damage the machine....” We fade back in on a prepped and ready Xavier about to be placed in, as Ellie~! described it, That Tube Thing ready to have his brain scanned for any damage from the sick Blackjack blows he received as payback for his acts perpetrated against Jeremy Howard. Xavier : “What do you mean I can’t take my Continental Title in with me? THIS IS A SYMBOL OF MY RELIGION! OF SCIENTOLOGY! This symbolises our fight with and victory over oppression! Would you take a cross from around an...Eugh...Christian’s neck?” The technician looks back to him blankly. Technician : “Um...Yes we do!” Xavier : “WELL THAT WEAKNESS WHEN IT COMES TO THEIR FAITH PROBABLY EXPLAINS WHY THEY ARE NOT THE FASTEST GROWING RELIGION IN THE WORLD!!!” The technician thinks about what he is saying with curiosity in her eyes and on her voice. Technician : “Islam?” The wild Green eyes of Kannon lock onto her and he bellows up from the trolley he is lying on. Xavier : “NO NOT ISLAM! SCIENTOLOGY! DIANETICS! THE FOLLOWINGS OF HUBBA-....HUH?” He is cut off as his body is fed into the machine, sliding in head first and on his back into the claustrophobic confines of the scanner. It then flickers into life, lighting the interior as blinding flashes run along the tube, reflecting off the Continental Tile. “Wow.....We, like, SO have to buy one of things...Does it cook Popcorn too?” Ellie~! stands in the room by the machine, looking on at the sight of it entertained. Next to her the technician stands, wondering whether her question was a joke or not...But given the preppy tone in the valet’s voice she assumes it was real. Technician : “No...It doesn’t cook Popcorn...In fact, it isn’t even working. If I put it on now the Belt in there would fry the entire machine, like putting a fork in a microwave...” The valet looks at her confused, wondering what a fork in a microwave would look like before realising that her man is being scammed. Eleanor : “You mean he isn’t rally being scanned? That’s it...I’M, LIKE SO TELLING ON YOU! We Kn-...Well, he knows the head of this place and he can have you beheaded...Or fired or something!” Sensing that Ellie~! is, to be generous, scatty, the Technician quickly out thinks her and pulls a pen from her lab coat’s breast pocket and passes it to the valet. Technician : “If you don’t say anything you can have this pen...Look, it has a light on the end and if you press this button here.....It lights up so you can write in the dark...” Eleanor takes the pen nervously and plays around with it for a few seconds, inquisitively, like a kitten with it’s first ball of yarn before she tucks it into her pocket and looks back at her. Eleanor : “Okay, you have bought my silence again...But don’ do it again...Or next time I tell....” Ellie~! folds her arms, looking pleased with her self thinking she has come out of the situation the victor, watching Xavier laying in the flashing tube of light as the shot fades to Black. .....That was a pretty cool night, despite the fact that Xavi’s head has a lump the size of Everla....Emare....That really big mountain on it...He got to go in the light tube and I got a pen! I’d call that a pretty successful trip...Anyways, now I am a busy Commissioneress I have to go and...Um...Do what I am meant to do...When I found out what that is...Anyways...Byeez!
