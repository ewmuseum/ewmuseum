Damn Multiple Personalities!

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/02 *Monolouge* How far is too far? Do we actually know how far it is? I mean is going too far ending a former PCW Cruiserweight champion with two men brutally attacking him with a chair and a baseball bat? Is going “too far” Power Bombing a man onto a guardrail? Hell no! Not for two men who have been pent up with anger the past months. Especially, Big Daveylicious. Despite the amazing brutal annihilation to “Amazing” Mike Storm should thank them for sending his PCW career or even wrestling career out of the world. Now, don’t get me wrong I’m sure the amazing career end was painful for Storm. That’s if he’s alive. But Big Daveylicious ended his career along with Mike Frampton! Introducing the world to the Sensation of Annihilation. That means something. Now, his name will be added to the others who’s PCW careers have been ended by Big D. Maybe Metallyx will be next on that list or maybe they’ll be spared with an inch of their life. I take it that Metallyx are big fans of Metallica. Metallyx should listen very carefully to one particular Metallica song. That song being “No Remorse.” Because that’s all the S.O.A are gonna be giving them while their in the hospital. After missing all those title shot opportunities Big D has pentad up all his anger. Finally, this Sunday he gets to make up for it as he goes against Jason Core for the extreme title. Unleashing all his hate and anger taking it out on the poor subject Jason Core. (The scene fades to white stucco, three-story beach house’s driveway. A black hummer pulls up on the driveway. We hear the engine be turned off as the driver’s door of the hummer opens and out comes the occupant of the hummer. He’s a muscular formed man with bleached blonde hair and a black goatee. The driver is as we figured out Big Daveylicious dressed in the appropriate attire for St. Patrick's day. Armani suit, a green top hat, and green Oakley sunglasses. He is carrying two cases of beer. He puts one of the cases of beer on the driveway, and he shuts the car door. As he picks up the other beer case he makes his way to his destination of his steel front door. He knocks on it, waits, and nothing.) Big Daveylicious: That’s odd. Graves better open the door. (He knocks on the door, waits, and once again nothing.) Big Daveylicious: This is disgraceful a man of my existence shouldn’t open a door. Very well. (He puts one beer case on the pavement, opens his front door, grabs the one beer case again and walks into his home. Inside the home we see his lackey, Michael Graves in his usual gothic wear sitting in Big D’s recliner with a bag of pop corn and a box of tissues. He is watching a soap opera from the big screen TV.) Michael Graves: {Sniff. Sniif} No! Why don’t you love him?! He saved Timmy! (Big D passes by as he puts the beer cases on his wooden floor. Graves grabs a tissue from the tissue box and wipes his eyes. Big D pulls out the remote of the TV out of his pocket.) Michael Graves: What is she gonna say? Is she gonna marry him? (The TV turns off as Big D pushed the Power button to his remote. Graves stares back only to find Big D behind a leather couch. Michael stomps his feet like a little girl.) Big Daveylicious: Graves, you shouldn’t watch that crap. Michael Graves: That crap was good! Now, I’ll never know what she said! Big Daveylicious: How come you didn’t answer the door? Michael Graves: Sorry, it was an emotional roller coaster. I peed in my pants when I had to pee. Big Daveylicious: Next time open the door. Michael Graves: What do I look like a damn maid?! Big Daveylicious: No, a lackey. (Michael’s eyes bulge out and he stomps up and down angrily.) Michael Graves: You’re never damn thankful for anything I do for you! Remember those chocolate chip cookies I made you?! Big Daveylicious: No, you never made me cookies. Michael Graves: Well…uh…I would and you wouldn’t care. So there! (Big D shakes his head.) Big Daveylicious: Where’s Mike? Michael Graves: I’m here, damn it! Are you hearing those voices again? Big Daveylicious: I’m talking about Frampton. Michael Graves: What about me?! It’s Mike this. Mike that. You never ask for me. Big Daveylicious: Because you’re my lackey and he’s my tag team partner. Michael Graves: I could have been your partner. Big Daveylicious: Errr…Were not homosexuals. Michael Graves: He’s in your room. Big Daveylicious: My room? Why the hell is he in my room?! Michael Graves: What do I look like Framptons b*tch? (Big D shrugs as Graves folds his arms. ) Big Daveylicious: Your starting to act like a b*tch. Why don’t you just relax and have a beer? (Michael shakes his head Big D grabs a beer bottle out of a case, takes off the cap, and takes a sip. He turns left to his staircase. He goes up his stair case looking at his achievements of his career on his wall all framed. He climbs the staircase until he reaches the 3rd floor of this rather large beach house. He walks through the hallway towards the door of his room. He opens the door fast like in a panic. Only to find Mike Frampton wearing Gucci shoes, Aramani shirts, and Big D’s signature Oakleys staring off in Big D’s mirror. Big D looks rather pissed about it.) Big Daveylicious: What the hell are you doing in my clothes?! Mike Frampton: That’s no way to talk to Mr. Extravaganza. Why a man of my existence shouldn’t be yelled at. Big Daveylicious: That’s my nickname, Mike. I can get you for copyright infringement. Mike Frampton: I’m sorry, wannabe. Your not Big Daveylicious. I am! (Big D rolls his eyes. Mike turns to Big D.) Mike Frampton: Did you just roll your eyes at the almighty, Big D? How dare you?! Big Daveylicious: Damn, you Mike and your multiple personalities! (Big D falls to the ground on both knees with his beer bottle dropping shattering on his floor.) Big Daveylicious: Whyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy?!*’Cause I Run This Game!* Big Daveylicious: No, not you again! I’m not listen to you! Get out of my head! Mike Frampton: What a spas. Big Daveylicious: That’s it! (He jumps at Mike and slaps the back of Mikes head multiple times. Mikes mood changes like he’s another person. Big D stops hitting him) Mike Frampton: Would you like some tea and crumpets? Big Daveylicious: Holy Gray Poupon! Your personality changes when I hit you in the back of the head. Mike Frampton: My word, sir. (Suddenly, Big D turns to see Graves standing outside his doorway with a women in a suit. He wonders if they‘d been watching him the whole time.) Michael Graves: Hey, Big D. Big Daveylicious: Graves, I didn’t ask for the hooker today. Michael Graves:...this is Dr. Nina Hanhorn. Big Daveylicious: Hello, Nina. Dr. Nina Hanhorn: That’s Doctor. I'm with the County Family Services Department. Big Daveylicious: I get it. You just want me to be your crazy boy and you want to be my doctor! As kinky as it maybe. I’ll be your crazy little patient! Dr. Nina Hanhorn: Graves, tells me you’ve been hearing voices and he also says Frampton has multiple personalities. Mike Frampton: I’m sorry but whom the bloody hell is Mike Frampton?! Big Daveylicious: Ugh. I don’t hear voices. *Yes, you do. Your nothing but a low level loser!* Big Daveylicious: No! I’m not a loser! Your the loser! Get out of my head! (Big D calms himself down looks at the Doctor and Graves all looking at him crazy.) Dr. Nina Hanhorn: I want you two, to come with us so we can talk some more about these voices. Big Daveylicious: Ah, kinky I like! Mike Frampton: Will we still be able to make our match‘s, Miss? Dr. Nina Hanhorn: Yes, President Duke Williams has worked it all out where you’ll be able too. Mike Frampton: Jolly good! (With that the scene fades to black.)
