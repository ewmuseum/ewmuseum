Date: Season I, Episode 15 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason I, Episode 15“Killing Mossilano”************************************ Location: Ghost Blue Lumina, New York City Back Road Date: January 20, 2003Time: 4:30 pm Snow’s eyes were steady on the black Chevy Blazer as they followed it down the long, narrow,and lonely road just outside New York City. Snow wasn’t very familiar with the area, but TheDragon, who was driving their blue Lumina, seemed to know it well. They stayed far enough behind to be only a speck in the rearview mirror of the Blazer, but theystalked it skilfully just the same. Snow trusted The Dragon’s judgement when it came to thedistance they kept from John Mossilano, the man they were hunting, and his guards who weredriving him down this out-of-the-way road. It must have been a shortcut, The Dragon had earliersaid, or maybe they were just trying to avoid company. Snow and The Dragon hadn’t spoken in quite some time now. They just hunted on, keepingtheir eyes at the black Blazer, even though it disappeared around small corners from time totime. Finally, Snow spoke. “You know,” he said to his companion, “we’re never going to find a place more deserted thanthis to get the job done. And it could be years before anyone realized what happened to him.” The Dragon’s brow wrinkled. “How can we do him out here? Do you want to get into a gunfight with four guys, five counting the old man, driving in a truck that could run us off the road?” “Then I guess we’ll just have to get them out of the truck before we do it,” Snow said with asigh. The Dragon gave Snow a puzzled look, but the younger occupant of the car didn’t notice. Hefell deep into thought for a long moment before finally turning to the Dragon with inspired eyes. “Speed up,” Snow told The Dragon. “Get this thing going as fast as it can fucking go!” “We’ll catch up to them,” The Dragon protested. He was beginning to think that the Don, JustinHattoni, had placed his faith in the wrong man when choosing Snow for this job. “We can’t getthem out of the car if they know who we are! If anything, they’ll run.” “We’re not going to catch up to them,” Snow said, and for a moment, The Dragon was relieved. The sense drained when Snow continued. “We’re going to pass them.” *** Location: Snow Residence, New York City SuburbsDate: January 20, 2003Time: 4:35 pm Lying on her back in her bed, Krista stared up at the ceiling in the bed room that she was finallygetting used to. It was a little smaller than the one that she had in Canada, after she had movedin with her sister, and a little larger than the room she had lived in with her father and stepmother while growing up. She was able to get away from her sister without giving her an explanation for the suddenoutburst downstairs. As soon as she learned that her sister was pregnant, she couldn’t hold backthe tears. In reality, she was happy that her sister was having another baby. Something else was ripping through her. Wrapped in silence, she closed her eyes and tried to escape life in the oblivion of sleep. *** Location: John Mossilano’s Blazer, New York City Back RoadDate: January 20, 2003Time: 4:35 pm John Mossilano sat comfortably in the back of the black Chevy Blazer as it cruised down thelong, narrow road. He liked taking roads that were rarely used. City traffic was something thathe liked to avoid as much as possible. “Look at this dumb fuck,” the body guard driving the car said as he looked in his rearviewmirror. From behind, a blue Lumina was catching up to them quickly as it flew through theroad. “Fucking kids,” scowled the guard in the passenger seat. “They’ll learn when they fly off theroad and kill themselves in the ditch.” Mossilano pays little attention as the blue Lumina flies past their Blazer, and speeds up the roaduntil it is out of sight. *** Location: New York City Back Road, 2003Date: January 20, 2003Time: 4:40 pm The blue Lumina was overturned in the center of the road when the Blazer came upon it. Smokepoured upward from the upside down engine, and the front and back windshields were bothsmashed. With the road blocked, the driver of the Blazer is forced to stop, and he and the guardin the passenger seat exit the car. “Fucking kids,” the driving guard cusses as he tosses his cigarette aside. “What’d I fucking say? Huh? What’d I fucking say!? Driving like fucking maniacs and thinking they’re fuckingimmortal. Well, look at the little bastards now!” “Maybe we should call an ambulance,” the second man said. He was a lot younger than theother, and didn’t look one hundred percent Italian like everyone else in the Blazer. The first guard, who was driving the Blazer knelt down to take a look in inside the wreckedvehicle. “What the fuck!?” he said as he turned to his companion. “There’s nobody fucking inthere!” “How can no body be in there?” the second guard asked. “No one could just walk away from awreck like this.” At that moment, the two guards exchanged desperately terrified gazes. Each would be the lastthing that the other man saw in his lifetime. The Dragon leapt up from behind the overturned Lumina, toting an Uzi. He sprayed the scenewith bullets, immediately dropping the two men who had approached the car. Blood flowedfrom their carcasses in spurts and thrusts as their muscles died, moments after their brainsalready had. Quickly and fiercely, bullets came from inside the Blazer, busting the front windshield. Theycame from pistols, but were shot rapidly just the same. The two guards remaining fired a heavywave on the Dragon, who fell back as one of the flaming hot metal bullets made a gash in hisright arm. It was only a grazing hit, but painful all the same. He sunk back down behind the shelter of the Lumina and took the time to reload his heavy dutyfire power. He waited in silence, without moving. The flaw in Snow’s plan was that for thenext few moments, The Dragon would be left blind to what was going on beyond his vehicularshelter. Then he heard it - one door slammed shut, and then another. The guards had left the Blazer, andwouldn’t be so gullible as to let The Dragon take another shot. If he showed himself now, heknew, one of them would no doubt have ample time to put a bullet in his head. And then, the tense moment was made much more comfortable. Two shots rang through the air,followed by the sound of two carcasses falling dead to the pavement. The Dragon took a deepbreath, closed his eyes, and thanked his God that he was still alive. Slowly, and with his Uzi cradled like a child, yet ready for action, The Dragon rose until he wasstanding, staring out at the scene on the deserted road. He wiped the sweat from his brow andsquinted against the sun’s light that the snow was throwing in his face. Jason Snow stood over his two dead guards and the four total carcasses that were sprawled onthe pavement with their blood rivers flowing into a mutual lake. His yellow hair blew around inthe breeze and his frozen blue eyes stared down at his victims without remorse. And then, justas mechanically, he turned to the Blazer, raised his pistol, and fired two shots through the backwindow and into the cranium of John Mossilano. The old Don slumped in his seat, and Snow approached the vehicle to remove the body.
