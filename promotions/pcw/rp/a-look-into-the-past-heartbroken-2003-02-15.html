A Look Into The Past: 'Heartbroken'

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2 - 15 - 03 Ahh… young love. It’s a beautiful thing, and can produce some of the best months that someone will ever see in their life. The feeling of having someone so special by your side at all time, loving you through thick and thin, can be the best feeling in the world. Your mind is at rest and nothing ever goes wrong. It’s all just a big free ride… … or so it appears. Now don’t get me wrong, as young love is still wonderful. But, there can be some VERY ugly moments, due to strong feelings coming out as anger instead of love. I should know… “DORN! WHY ARE YOU BEING LIKE THIS??” “Like what?!” The shout match has been going on for quite some time between the lovely couple of Dorn Tracer and Jessica Carter. Explosions of rage and confusion burst like geysers from the two, their faces red from the blood boiling within their veins. It’s quite sad… they’re known to be the best couple around, too. The 16 year olds, however, don’t seem to be getting along too well at the moment. “Why don’t you care??” Shrieks Jessica, her tear-laden face continuing to turn red. Her voice trembles with exhaustion and sorrow, causing her words to come out in spurts. Her lovely brownish-green eyes now water instead of shine. Dorn opens and closes his fists angrily, trying to control himself. His relationship has been on the rocks for quite some time now, due to various reasons. “I DO care, Jessica! Just fucking understand, all right? It’s all been out of my control!” His voice booms across his basement, where the two are having their shouting match. “YOU DON’T CARE!! GOD! You just haven’t called me for four days! That’s stupid you’re too fucking busy for your girlfriend, Dorn!” The poor girl seems to be losing herself in her anger. The rage emitting from her beautiful face contorts and twists it into something horrible, a visage that should never been seen on such a beautiful creature. “I JUST HATE YOU SOMETIMES!” The Astronomer is having a hard time coping with the situation. He HAS been awfully busy, working and having to go places with his family. But it’s not his fault. She’s blaming him for shit that’s not his fault. She wants to kill him over stuff that’s not his fault! And he knows that. “IT’S NOT MY FUCKING FAULT! I TOLD YOU THAT! GET OFF MY BACK ABOUT IT!” “NO! NO, I WON’T!” Jessica shrieks back, her voice reverberating across the stonewalls of the basement like a rubber ball. “I SHOULDN’T FUCKING HAVE TO! I’M YOUR GIRLFRIEND, YOU’RE SUPPOSED TO SPEND TIME WITH ME!” The tension is becoming all too thick. Dorn can feel a wave of fury building within his body like a great black hole, sucking his sanity into an eternal prison. He throws his hands up angrily and turns away from her, pacing along a straight line that’s not there. Jessica sighs loudly with hints of malice emerging from deep within her throat. She crosses her arms and shakes her head. “GOD!” She yells, beneath her breath. “This is fucking stupid! It’s not even my fault and you’re going crazy on me!” The Astronomer cries from across the room, his voice beginning to tremble with fury and sorrow. “You’re so fucking thick-headed!” “I DON’T CARE, DORN! I-DON’T-CARE!” Jessica shrieks, throwing her arms down angrily and stomping her little foot into the hard, unforgiving concrete of the cellar. “IT’S FUCKING STUPID YOU DON’T EVEN GIVE A SHIT ABOUT OUR RELATIONSHIP ANYMORE!” Dorn had heard this rubbish before. She pulls this shit on him every time they’re in an argument and he ignores her so he can calm down and collect his thoughts, as to not cause more trouble. She dubs it as not caring about the relationship, and Dorn’s getting quite tired of the bullshit. He loves Jessica with all of his heart, and she understands how much she cares about them, but damn… “I’m so sick of you saying that,” The Astronomer spits, irritated beyond his own wits. “I do too care! And I’ll fucking leave if you say it again!” The lovely young lady stomps her foot again, over and over on the hard floor. She yells in disgust of her boyfriend and begins to cry hard, the tears streaming down her cheek like a mini-Niagara Falls. Her heart continues to shatter to many pieces as her mind desperately searches for a way to keep their relationship together. She can’t take this… it’s becoming too much… why doesn’t he want to listen? Why doesn’t he care? Does he even love her anymore? He just acts like it’s no big deal and calls her names. It’s hurting her so much… “GOD! Why do you always have to leave?? You can’t ever have a civil conversation with me!” Dorn licks his teeth in irritation and fires back, his voice filled with malice and confusion” I GUESS OUR YEAR OR SO OF CONVERSATIONS WEREN’T CIVIL, WERE THEY?!” “STOP YELLING AT ME!” Jessica cries, starting to get a slight migraine from the barrage of loud sounds slamming her brain from her own love. “WHY ARE YOU SO FUCKING MEAN TO ME?! WHAT DID I DO THAT WAS SO WRONG?!” Dorn struggles to find an answer that will ‘please’ her. In all truthfulness, and in Dorn’s humble opinion, she hasn’t listened to him a bit for the past 4 or 5 days. He’s been trying to explain to her the importance of the things he’s been doing and that they’re out of his hands, but she continues to complain and rave about them day after day. He’s tried to make it up to her, but she blew it off like he was doing absolutely nothing worthwhile. And now, he’s trying to pound it into her head verbally. And like always, she’s hardheaded about it… almost over the edge. “ YOU’RE NOT BEING ANY FUCKING NICER, SO DON’T PUSH IT ON ME!!!!!” It was a weak answer, but it was all he had. She apparently wasn’t taken back by it, either. “SEE?! WHY ARE YOU BEING LIKE THIS?! GOD, I DON’T EVEN WANT TO BE WITH YOU ANYMORE!!!!!!!!” Her last words were like a toothed razor blade heating to 360 degrees stabbing him in the heart. Dorn gives her a look of disbelief and puzzlement, his eyes widen and his jaw dropped. He can feel his heart begin to crack and tear, splintering within the essence of his skeleton. Tracer puts a hand to his heart and gives Jessica a horrified look, who frowns with tears pouring from her eyes. “What…?” His response is quiet, almost panicked. “Why…?” “I just don’t want to do this anymore, Dorn! You don’t even care that we NEVER get to talk and you don’t care how hurt I am! GOD!” Jessica wipes her eyes across her forearm and sniffles pathetically, still beat with sorrow. “You’re not who I fell in love with… I can’t do it anymore…” Her voice trails off into crying, swelling from deep within her throat. She turns away from him and buries her face within her hands, unable to hold back the emotional demons tearing away at her heart. Jessica knows she loves Dorn more than life itself and doesn’t WANT to leave him… but she feels as if she has no choice. Dorn doesn’t know what to say, but he definitely doesn’t want this. “Jessica… why? Please…” He moves closer to her, compelled to comfort her in her sorrowed state of mind. “Look, we can fix this… haven’t we always?” Jessica, however, gives Dorn an unwanted answer. “I don’t want to fix it this time, Dorn. You just hurt me too much…” Her sweet, saddened voice is muffled within the folds of her hands, tears streaming down her arms. Dorn can feel his knees getting weak as the realization of it all begins to slam his body, physically and mentally. He stumbles over to the old couch and falls down into it, unable to bring himself to look at her. “I don’t believe it…” He whispers to himself, still unable to bring himself to the fact. Jessica overhears his thoughts and turns to him, her tear-stained face twisted with hateful sorrow. “Dorn, I know you don’t care or anything, but I’m going to go… I’m tired of crying day after day and I just want to go home.” The Astronomer refuses to answer her, absolutely awestruck that his beautiful, eventful relationship of a year just came crashing down in a matter of seconds. He just can’t bring himself to say anything to her. What did he do wrong? He treated her well throughout the months, so why is she dumping him? He treated her like a queen… what the fuck. Jessica obviously doesn’t realize that he’s utterly dumbfounded by the whole thing, and in a snide manner, comes to the conclusion that he’s just ignoring her again. Overcome with fury and gnashing sorrow, she whips around and stomps out of the basement, leaving the young Astronomer to his thoughts. They’re broken when the wooden door slams upstairs, signaling the departure of his ex… Jessica Carter. His life has just walked out the door. Dorn loves her so much… he cannot bear to be without her. His spirit will crumble without her. What does he do now? “I can’t fucking believe…” Dorn hisses to himself, overcome with puzzlement. “What went so wrong?? I can’t FUCKING BELIEVE IT!” His last words burst with spontaneous anger and he kicks the wooden coffee table over, effectively shattering the glass cups on it as they smash into the concrete. “What do I do now… who do I turn to… I’ve got nothing…” He buries his face in his rough hands and begins to rock back and forth in the couch, becoming increasingly panicked and awestruck. The brewing chaos in his mind begins to eat away at his sanity, giving him thoughts of violent outbursts. “NOTHING!!!!!” Dorn bursts from his cover and shoots up out of the couch, starting to furiously pace back and forth in front of the couch. His eyes dart back and forth, as if looking for an answer to his problems. His heart definitely is no help, as it burns with sorrow within his chest. His mind is in panic, working 100 miles an hour and going absolutely nowhere. What should he do now? His spirit is crushed and his life is over… Now what? The mind of the 16 year old tries to find an answer… tries to find a way… but his eyes end up finding it. Is it a gun with which to shoot himself? Is it poison to drink? A phone to call Jessica and tell her he wants her back, groveling before her feet? No… His eyes come upon the savior and they light up, signaling that Dorn has found his answer. What does he need Jessica for? He’s got his notes. He’s got his books. He has Astrology and the stars. [End.]
