Oh my greatness! (part one)

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/02 (Sorry that this isn't very good. or if it appears to have no purpose. I guess I made it like that. After a week of working on it, and getting nowhere thanks to having to run this place, I got tired of looking at it. It'll be 'to be continued' from where I leave off. Also, I need Snow to contact me because I've got an idea :P)__________________________ 'I'm a bad man. I must be the greatest.' -Muhammad Ali 'The World's Greatest' -Song by R. Kelly 'The Great One' -Nickname of Wayne Gretzky and Dwayne 'The Rock' Johnson 'The Greatest Show on Earth' -Circus put on by Ringling Brother and Barnum & Bailey 'Oh my greatness!' -Jason Snow What are/were these people saying? Do/Did they honestly believe they were the greatest? Are/Were these comments made in self-promotion? Or are/were these comments said on behalf of someone else. Someone who actually is great. Someone who, in all actuality, is the greatest person, place, or thing to ever grace in pile of suck that is planet Earth. Someone who just happens to work under the microscope that is Primetime Championship Wrestling. Someone named Kid Wonder. It is true. Kid Wonder, before he was born into his great body, was really Muhammad Ali. Then when the body of the current great known as Kid Wonder was born, the soul of K-Dub went into it and he left Ali a shell of his former self. Feeble and weak compared to what the soul of Kid Wonder made him. It is true that R. Kelly, the Clyde of R&B music, wrote a song titled 'The World's Greatest' after the World's greatest human being, Kid Wonder. He even went as far as having a huge celebration in a wrestling ring to honor the greatness that is Kid Wonder. The Ringling Brothers and Barnum & Bailey, the people behind that corny circus, weren't talking about their circus when talking about the 'Greatest Show on Earth'. Instead, they were looking into the future. They took a time machine built by some poor shmuck that had the brief honor of having Kid Wonder's soul occupy him and went ahead in time to see tape of Kid Wonder in action. And after watching the tape, they had no choice to deem the great Kid Wonder as the 'Greatest Show on Earth'. Nothing else could match what they were watching. And they're right. Nothing, not a man, woman, animal, or any other thing, can match Kid Wonder's greatness. Many admirers and worshippers such as current PCW paper champion Jason Snow might think differently, but it's just a trick they're playing on themself. The greeatest was, is, and forever will be Kid Wonder. That brings us to today. We are outside of a library in Portland. The Multnomah County Public Library to be exact. A library generally for the public has been booked for today's 'Greatness Convention' to be held. A sign outside the library reads 'Greatness Convention' in big letters. Then, right under it, the sign reads 'Monday at 12pm'. There are people walking outside in the cold Portland weather, but nobody is approaching the library. Probably because they weren't invited to this invitation only convention. Nobody walking by even seems to care about what could possibly be going on inside the library. But as a curious person myself, I'm going in. Move to the inside of the Multnomah County Public Library in Portland. A large, rectangular table sits in the center of the room. But not even half of the seats have been occupied. In fact, there are only five people sitting at the table. The table itself is a faded brown color. It can seat ten people along each side and one person at each end. A lot of empty chairs sit along the table. But, still, there are people there, too. And they all sit on the same side of the table. First off, at the very end of the table sits Amanda. She sits away from everyone else because she doesn't want to be near such disgraces. Amanda is wearing her normal pink halter top with 'STAR' written in silver glitter across the chest. She's also sporting her trademark black leather pants that young girls all across the World wear in an attempt to match her greatness. Amanda sits at the far right edge of the table. Over on the left side is where the other people sit. On the left edge is the man who basically faked death so he could skip the Smackdown tapings and Raw to be here. Fresh off his near death experience at the hands of Hogan, Hall, and Nash, The Rock is in the building. He's wearing his normal silk shirt, unbuttoned of course. He's also sporting the black workout pants. Rock doesn't have a scratch on him despite getting hit by an 18-wheeler while in an ambulance. Must be taking the same juice HHH was taking when he was unharmed after plummeting on his head in a car dropped by Austin from a high height. Sitting next to The Rock, shaking slightly due to his depressing health, is the greatest boxer to ever grace a boxing ring, Muhammad Ali. Ali is wearing a plain grey t-shirt and and some black jeans. Nothing special. He's just having a hard time sitting still. Next to Ali is R&B singer, and child lover, R. Kelly. He's wearing the crappiest in expensive wear. Clothes from Jay-Z's clothing line presumably. He isn't, however, carrying any tapes around of his encounters with young girls. Maybe the police confiscated them all. Who knows? Finally, sitting next to Kelly is the greatest hockey player to ever put on skates. He was also a part of the Canadian hockey team in Salt Lake City in a business manner. He holds more records than anyone in hockey can imagine, including that backstabbing bastard who deserves another concussion, Eric Lindros. The guy sitting there is, of course, Wayne Gretzky. Wayne is wearing a black business suit with a black coat and black pants. He looks very professional. All the people sitting at the table are facing the direction of one man in particular. One man greater than all those people, and all the other people in the World combined. One man who made the great force known as PCW. One man known to the World as Kid Wonder. Wonder clears his throat. 'Goddess and gentlemen,' Wonder addresses to the people sitting at the table, 'welcome to my greatness convention. For those of you that have been living under a rock all your lives, I am Kid Wonder. I am the greatest single being on this miserable planet. I am the man that makes the world go 'round. I am the man who made each and every one of you what you think you are. I am the man you are not only to respect, but to worship. I am, well,' Wonder pauses and smirks. 'I am Kid Wonder.' The guys are just sitting there. Ali shakes slightly because he can't control it. Amanda, being the only girl in the room, raises her hand. Wonder glances over and spots what she's doing. He turns in her direction and just smiles. 'Yes Amanda?' 'You're also my boyfriend,' the lovely lady says with a smile. Kid Wonder grins. 'Which proves without a shadow of a doubt that I'm the greatest man alive,' he says with a smirk. 'Only the greatest man deserves the greatest woman. And you are definitely the greatest of them all.' The King & Queen of PCW share lovey-dovey smiles. 'Whoa! Whoa! Whoa! Whoa! Whoa!' The Rock interrupts. 'You talk about being the greatest man alive. And that's all fine and dandy. If you, in your own feeble mind, believe you are greatest than The Rock, then good for you. But the least you could do is give The Rock your name.' 'I already told you,' 'It doesn't matter what your name is,' shouts out The Rock. Kid Wonder, who is looking at The Rock, does not look to pleased. The Rock continues: 'You, Kid Wonder, talk about being greater than The Rock. You, Kid Wonder, talk about being the greatest human being alive. You, Kid Wonder, talk about Amanda, a little two dollar whore, as being the greatest woman alive. You, Kid Wonder, try making yourself to be greater than The Rock and the millions,' Rock pauses for that fan reaction he usually gets, but can't get here because people in the room really don't care, 'and millions of The Rock's fans. But all you really are, Kid Wonder, is a bona fide, roody poo candy ass!' Kid Wonder looks down at the ground while still facing The Rock's direction. He sighs and shakes his head. The 'Quintessential Teen Idol' can't believe what he is hearing from someone as annoying and insignificant as The Rock. 'Tell me, Rock,' Kid Wonder raises his head up so he's looking at Rocky, 'why exactly are you here?' 'Well, Kid Wonder, The Rock was invited here by someone smarter than you,' he answers. 'Someone who recognizes The Rock's greatness.' 'Well,' Kid Wonder slowly starts to approach The Rock, 'get the hell out of here. It is obvious you have an inferiority complex and you don't deserve to be here. You are worthless! You are far from great! I don't want you here so you will leave!' 'And why should The Rock listen to you,' Rocky asks/tells wrestling's premier cruiserweight. 'Because,' Wonder is in Rock's face, 'if you don't leave, I will tell every single worthless piece of trash you call a fan that you weren't really hit by an 18-wheeler and that you are nothing but a lying, no-good, flaming sellout.' The Rock, easily standing above Kid Wonder, looks down at the former 4-time PCW Cruiserweight champion with disgust. After a few tense moments, Rocky starts to back away. 'The Rock's not leaving because you told him to, oh no,' The Rock claims. 'The Rock is gonna leave because The Rock is The Great One. The Rock is the people's champion. The Rock is...' Kid Wonder interrupts: 'Yeah, yeah, that's real nice,' Wonder says without any interest in the matter. 'Now get out!' Rocky slowly backs away from Kid Wonder and makes his exit from the library. Wonder, not trusting a piece of crap like that, keeps an eye on him as The Rock does leave. Once The Rock has made his exit, Wonder walks over towards a wall. At the wall Wonder walks to, there is the great thing called the gry erase board. Those things just rock. Wonder stands in front of the board. 'Now that we've weeded out the crap,' Wonder says as he looks at the rest of the people in the room, 'let's get down to business.' Kid Wonder reaches up. he grabs some sort of string. He pulls it down and a poster of former two-time PCW Heavyweight champion, Jeremy Howard is pulled down for all to see. 'This,' Wonder says with a stern look on his face. he points at the poster, 'is the enemy.' And with that, we don't go on any further right now. Instead, we see Wonder's lips move some and the picture mercifully fade to black.
