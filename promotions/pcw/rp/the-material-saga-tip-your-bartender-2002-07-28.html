The Material Saga: Tip Your Bartender.

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-28-02 The shot faded in to see four pairs of feet walking forward, the two on the end were smaller, a pair of little girls black dress shoes and a pair of black platformed boots. The two in the center wore a weathered pair of combat boots and a brand new set of high eyelet Docs, they were half obscured by large, baggy pants. The voices of the four could be heard, it was obvious who they were by the looks on passerbys faces. Various PCW officials and lucky fans who were on the boat walked by and said hello. Sucking up. This was obviously the future of the federation. 'I hope the liquors good here,' said DarkStar, the camera still focusing on the fours feet, 'we tend to have a history of finding the worst possible dives wherever we are.' Clyde scoffed, 'The beer was always cheap wasn't it.' Well, that much was true. In more ways then one. Most of it was like having sex in a boat..... fucking close to water. This was different though. They get whatever alcohol they want for free. Thanks to the brilliant 'negotiation' skills of Tyler Nelson (which nowadays revolve around just plain striking fear into the hearts of people). The mere thought of real, hard liquor just had something happy click inside DarkStars mind. It was great to be successful. The shoes continued moving forward as they took the left to enter the Wrestle Vessel bar, the camera took a dramatic pan out but stopped behind DarkStar, he glanced around the corner and saw Lexian walking away and down the deck in the opposite direction. He muttered under his breath, contemplating taking him out now, 'You are too good for that Gavyn, in due time... in due time.' Clyde called out for him to come to a table in the corner of the room, DarkStar walked over and basked in the glory of the looks he was recieving. For once it wasnt because of his black lipstick or his dozen piercings, it was because he was DarkStar. PCW's Continental Champion. He flared his nostrils, taking in the overwhelming smell of salt water, alcohol, and vinyl seating. He slid in next to Talena, she was giggling and trying to keep her eyes away from the other side of the table, DarkStar glanced up and saw Clyde near tears as Bethany was sitting on a brown booster seat, she was furious. 'I swear I am going to choke you all to death with rotting fish tonight.' DarkStar smiled and grabbed a menu, something to eat with his alcohol sounded like a great idea. Clyde sat on the other side casually reading his, glancing up only to chuckle at Bethany. She growled at the crayons they placed in front of her. Talena contained her giggling only long enough to trade her menu for Bethanys childrens menu. Somewhere in DarkStar's mind he knew this wasnt going to end well for the waiter. 'Did you notice that the cartoon fish looks a little bit like Red Lobsters lobster? I wonder if they are related.' Everyone at the table slowly looked at Talena, it seemed like each of them had something really intelligent to say, but before they could speak they realized that the cartoon fish really did look alot like that damned lobster. 'Would you all like something to drink?' The waiter asked in the trained way that all waiters ask questions in. It makes you wonder if outside of work they ever ask people questions just like that, like if they put on their little blue and red uniforms and wander around going, 'Would you like me to rub your balls for you?' or 'Would you like me to walk up and down around your table without really doing anything for 40 minutes, then expect 20 percent?' DarkStar folded his menu and nodded, 'Can I have a Screwdriver? Heavy on the vodka of course.' The waiter nodded and wrote it down on his little notepad, the pen had a little yellow fuzzy thing on the top of it. This guy seemed way to cheerful, 'Talena what do you want?' She smiled and spoke, she had a captivating voice, it was one of the things that came with her second sight. You took notice of her mouth everytime she had something to say, her perfect painted lips part, those shiny white teeth move, and her tongue seems to linger extra long on certain letters, 'I'll have a Bacardi Silver... thank you.' She smiled politely, Clyde was next, his order was a forgone conclusion. 'Jack Daniels... on the rocks....' he thought for a second, 'except no rocks.' The burned man leaned back further into his chair, nearly bumping heads with the man behind him. The waiter looked down at Bethany and smiled wide, kneeling down a little, 'And for you little lady?' She growled and looked up at him, her tiny hands pulling his shirt and yanking him close to her, 'I'll take a bottle of Rum, just the bottle. Nothing fancy, got it pretty boy???' The waiter pulled away and looked nervous, 'Uh... we cant serve minors, its illegal young lady, why would you want alcohol anyway.' She was seething in her seat. 'I'll bring you a nice Chocolate Milk, sound good?' He pasted his false smile back on and walked away. DarkStar squinted, his aura was horribly confused. 'That fucker is going to get his eyeballs ripped off if he gives me anymore trouble.' Bethany crossed her arms, her sundress looked quite becoming, 'Aw, calm down pudding, he doesn't know better.' Talena looked up and across the table, fiddling with the salt shakers, 'You sure have that right, while hes here making money, his girlfriend is out sleeping around.' She snorted and started to play with some spilt salt, DarkStar nodded slowly, 'Poor guy, has no clue whats going to happen when he gets home.' Clyde and Bethany just looked at them funny. Clyde halfway chuckled, 'I'm glad I just kill people, it must suck to be all mystical and crap.' The waiter wandered over with the drinks, the orangish vodka mixture in front of DarkStar, a bottle of Bacardi with a glass, a Chocolate Milk for Bethany, and he placed a empty glass in front of Clyde, he poured it three quarters full, 'Leave the bottle.' He really had no choice. 'Heh... are you uh........... ready to order yet?' DarkStar gave him his menu, 'We wont be eating much, just bring some Buffalo Wings and a plate of Potato Skins' He looked around the table, 'What? I heard they were good?' They all shrugged, almost simultaniously sipping on their alcoholic beverages. Time began to pass, and lord did they take advantage of the free beer. Some more then others. The scenes slowly began to fade into eachother, the four teammates eating and drinking, Talena stood up on the plasticty chair and declared herself the sexiest woman alive. None of the men could really complain, she giggled and fell into DarkStars lap. Clyde nearly polished off the bottle, Bethany nearly castrated the waiter, the scenes quickly blurred over and over again, signifying the partying the night before the PPV. The last scene faded into the next, Clyde and Talena helping (a better word would be dragging) DarkStar down the deck of the ship, he babbled about how sorry he felt for the waiter. 'Man...' Clydes speech was very slurred but he was coping fine 'Dark had wayyyyyyyyyyyyyyy to much to drink.' Talena hiccuped and giggled, nearly tripping and dropping DarkStar, 'I love him soooooooo much.' Clyde threw DarkStar into his bed, Talena plopped down next to him. 'Bye everyone... see you tommorow. Good luck and all that crap, just beat Lexian, Star.' DarkStar nodded, almost half assleep as he hit the bed. Talena crawled in under his arm and closed her eyes, her make up running. It would be a long day tommorow. 'Things have changed, for the better or for the worse, depending on your side,' DarkStar thought to himself as he lie there in his drunken stupor, 'but no matter what, one thing would always be true...' He looked up slightly, the Continental Title sitting next to what he was going to be wearing to the ring tommorow night at the PPV. 'I am the best.' The camera switched to a shot from the ceiling, Talena in her dress laying next to the blue haired freak, he held her a little tighter. 'and.... I am the luckiest.' fin
