Frisco SHOOTS! (At birds)

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-19-02 (The crackling of hundreds of assault rifles rings out across the blue Summer sky, as a flock of birds passes frantically overhead. With another blast of gunfire--this one louder and from a shorter distance, 2 birds are hit. One immediately falls to the ground and the second tries to fly further, but meets its flailing downfall seconds later.) 'Aha! Got the bastards!' (The sky is still focused on as the rifles continue to shoot.) 'Goddamnit, you attention span-lacking anal warts, did you not observe my marksmanship!?' (A chain of murmuring and gunfire is replaced by applause and praise.) 'Orale! Excelente!' 'Good shot, Presidente!' 'Excellent shot!' 'Magnífico!' 'You despicable fiend!' (We come down from the sky to observe a desert camp for weapons training. Hundreds of Francisco's militiamen are lineed up in their fatigues, with AK-47s and M16s in hand. The Imperial Militia of El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina immediately returns to target practice after the commanding officer motions to them to do so. The men fire on pop-up human-form targets which rise from the sandy hills before them. To the side of the men, Frisco--looking like a personification of fascism in his dressy military uniform--all decorated with medals, ribbons, and emblems, holds his wicked custom-made assault rifle in the air and turns his pleased smile into a frown as he looks at his critic--the mental patient who claims to be Asklepios--the demigod of healing and medicine and son of Apollo.) Frisco: 'What did you just say!?' (Asklepios gives Frisco a dirty look and immediately runs out towards the downed birds. Once he arrives at the corpses, he stands above them as he closes his eyes and chants.) Asklepios: 'Oh, great father, render unto me thy godly power! Let thy mercy and healing flow through your offspring, so he may replenish the lives that have been prematurely denied of them!' (Asklepios waves his mighty staff--a cane entwined with dirty gummy worms over the victims and chants in a gibberish that resembles ancient Greek.) Asklepios: 'Papimenos kalakeropalas optameno kyrenealiomanus!' (Frisco and his secretaries stand back shaking their heads at the self-proclaimed demigod who tries to revive the dead birds.) Frisco: 'Fucking nutcase.' (Meanwhile, Asklepios has picked the bloody, bullet-hole filled birds up in his hands and begins to spin around in circles.) Asklepios: 'Ortaneos aviatenialokon pereniomaklaenus!' (Asklepios trips over a rock and falls forward onto the ground--crushing the birds between the ground and his chest. Frisco and his men laugh loudly.) Frisco: 'That's not how you heal them, fucktard!' (Asklepios frowns and picks the flattened fowl off his chest. He then holds them up high and chants another phrase.) Asklepios: 'Aviatokonoman!' (Asklepios blows as he releases the birds into the air.) Asklepios: 'Fly!' (The bloody clumps of feathers merely drop back to the ground, just as dead as they were before, but this doesn't phase Asklepios, who continues to look up in the air.) Frisco: 'Ahem!' (Asklepios smiles as he looks toward the sky and waves.) Asklepios: 'Thank you, oh great father! I shall have my friends sacrifice a thousand grapes in your honor!' (One of Frisco's stooges looks over to him and nudges his arm.) Stooge #1: 'Do we really have to sacrifice grapes?' (Frisco smirks.) Frisco: 'No, idiot. We're not sacrificing anything for this loon.' (Asklepios begins his walk back towards Frisco and his secretaries with a scowl.) Asklepios: 'Treacherous villain. You did not have to attempt such vicious acts against those creatures. I was forced to call upon the great Apollo to give me the power to make them fly again.' Frisco: 'Well, um, I think the great Apollo screwed you. I would get a refund.' Asklepios: 'What do you mean by such disrespect remarks!?' (Frisco points to the dead birds.) Frisco: 'The birds are still dead.' (Asklepios looks back at the corpses.) Asklepios: 'Foolish child, you do not understand. Those are the shells of your malicious deeds. The birds are safe and well, but those two shells will haunt your life until the day you arrive in Hades!' Frisco: 'Yo--....' (One of Frisco's stooges leans in to interrupt.) Stooge #2: 'Sir, I saw something like this on a movie called The Green Mile. He can heal people and cough it out.' Stooge #1: 'Yeah, yeah! He brought that rat back to life and took away that lady's brain tumor!' Stooge #2: 'And then he put the tumor in that prick and made him crazy and then the prick shot the dude who killed those kids! That was awesome!' (Frisco frowns and Asklepios looks on in confusion.) Stooge #1: 'Do your cough thing now!' (Asklepios frowns.) Stooge #2: 'He's gonna cough it up. Just wait a minute.' Asklepios: 'I do not cough, you infantile baboons!' (The stooges look down in disappointment. Frisco shakes his head and speaks up.) Frisco: 'Listen, you delusional shit-brained goat felcher, you will apologize for speaking to me in such an insubordinate tone and go inside the base until you are summoned. You are beginning to make me regret we busted you out of the nuthouse with all your recent defiance and crazy business.' (The bearded, toga-wearing mental patient picks up his mighty staff--a cane entwined with gummy worms, and bravely approaches Frisco.) Asklepios: 'I will do nothing of the sort! Thy cruel disregard for inferior creatures angers the Gods!' (Frisco rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'As far as you're concerned, I'm the only God here. So straighten up and fly right!' Asklepios: 'Blasphemy! You are not a God!' (Frisco frowns and barks out a command as he points at the offender.) Frisco: 'Soldados! Preparen a emplear al objectivo!' (Within seconds, the South American militiamen are standing and pointing their weapons at the angry son of Apollo.) Frisco: 'Oh? I'm not?' (Asklepios begins to stammer.) Frisco: 'I do hope you will reconsider.' Asklepios: 'I-I-I...apologize. Yes, yes I must have mistaken you for someone else...less powerful. This happens quite frequently. I was cursed by Zeus long ago and the spell has not fully worn off yet. Please accept my forgiveness, kind sir.' (Frisco puts his arm around the fearful man and walks with him.) Frisco: 'Of course...' (Asklepios breathes a sigh of relief.) Asklepios: 'Thank you! You shan't regret this!' (Frisco looks at his loyal soldiers and nods his head. The commanding officer instructs the soldiers to resume their practice. Frisco and his toga-wearing friend continue to walk.) Frisco: 'You see here, Mr. Asklepios, we are in a different kind of world--a far cry from your own perception. Every living being on this planet is out for one thing and one thing only...' Asklepios: 'To please the Gods. Yes, yes I know this.' (Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'No, the complete opposite. Everyone lives their lives for their own advancement and happiness. Those birds that were flying above us were no exception. They could care less about my achievements. Those ungrateful flying fucks don't care if I win my bid for the presidency of Argentina, nor do they care if I regain my World Title.' Asklepios: 'Bu--...' Frisco: 'Yes I know! It's a crime! An absolute crime. After all I've done for this world--the hundreds of dollars I've given to charitable organizations. The hope I've given to millions of Argentineans who are eager to have me as president so we can finally defeat and enslave the British. After all the hours of promos I've cut to entertain the masses with my crazy adventures! And not to mention the excellent 5-star matches I put on every time I step in the ring!' (Frisco whips out a cigar from his jacket pocket and lights it up.) Frisco: 'Yes, after all I've done for this world, I still get birds flying across my line of sight without stopping to greet me with respect. They just fly by with not so much as a salute or chirp of praise. Ingrates. All of them.' Asklepios: 'Sir, they're birds.' (Frisco frowns and blows out a puff of smoke.) Frisco: 'Obviously, you're too insane to understand such a concept, but I assure you it's all true. Humans and birds alike. They don't respect me like I deserve to be respected! This is why I do what I do. This is why I shoot birds from the sky and hurt men in the ring. And this is exactly why I'm running for president of Argentina. I will get my respect, one way or another. And I will teach everyone to respect eachother. I'm the good guy here. I'm the superhero.' Asklepios: 'I'm confused.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Yes, my good man, it is confusing to many. The people see me trying to hurt Big Alan Steele and they boo me! The ungrateful scabs do not realize the magnitude of what I'm trying to accomplish here. I'm trying to rid the world of a menace--a man who goes around forcing his will upon people who he does not agree with. Steele is the villain here. He's the jerkoff who wants everyone to be like him, so he uses his muscles to do so. I'm trying to defeat a bully who abuses the concept of 'good' to get his barbaric way.' Asklepios: 'And he's trying to kill me too!' Frisco: 'Exactly. Like you said, Zeus sent him down to kill you. And you've experienced first hand what his father is like.' (Asklepios shudders.) Asklepios: 'Zeus is a sadist!' (Asklepios quickly looks up and around and hides behind Frisco.) Frisco: 'Indeed.' (Frisco blows smoke into the air as he tilts his head to watch his soldiers diligently firing at their targets.) Frisco: 'But we'll stop them. I assure you. We'll stop them all.' (Frisco guides the insane man to his side and continues to walk with him as his soldiers unload thousands of rounds of ammunition. The scene fades out.)
