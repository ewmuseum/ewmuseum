All The Planes In The World And He's On Mine...

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30.07.02 The scene opens up at JFK Airport, New York City. The sun is shining brightly over the city that never sleeps, planes are coming in and taking off regularly as you would expect at an airport the size of JFK. The scene moves into a jumbo jet parked on the runway, the plane is packed full. In the first class section of the plane is Duzza. Sitting next to the window, with a empty seat next to him. This is the only seat free in the section, as every other seat is taken up by important looking business men. Duzza is dressed in a very baggy white T-shirt, a pair of baggy black jeans and he has his hair set back in long cornrows. He has placed his bag on the seat next to him, which is just full of things that you cannot live without. A wallet, RnB and hip hop cds, a walkman and of course a basketball. Duzza cannot sleep at night without having played basketball, so he has to carry a ball with him. It is then, the pilot speaks over the P.A system Pilot: “Hello, this is your pilot speaking, we are just waiting for one customer to take his seat on the plane and then we will take flight for our destination of Haiti” Duzza sits back in his seat and waits for the plane to take off, when the customer who was late walks up the aisle. He speaks to Duzza and Duzza instantly recognizes his voice Customer: “This is my seat, so move your bag” Duzza looks up and doesn’t want to belive who is standing next to him Duzza: “MAKONIE!” Mark Makonie: “DUZZ?” Duzza: “Oh hell no, you ain’t sittin’ next to me” Makonie: “That’s for sure, I ain’t sitting next to you” A air hostess then comes over to see what’s going on Hostess: “Excuse me gentlemen, what’s the problem here?” Duzza: “I ain’t sittin’ next to this nazi fool, find him a place somewhere else” Makonie: “What? Why do I have to move? You move you cotton pickin…” Hostess: “Gentlemen please, now I’m sorry but this flight is fully booked, either you sit next to each other or you got off the plane, it’s your choice” Makonie: “Meh… fine I’ll sit down” Makonie picks up Duzza’s bag that was on his seat and throws it onto Duzza Duzza: “Cracka, you best watch yo’self” Makonie: “Whatever Duzz, I still haven’t forgotten you for making us lose that match” Duzza: “Me?! Fool, you was supposed to have had my back in that match, you should have been there to break the pin count ya nazi scum” Makonie: “Duzza, fuck you” Duzza: “Fuck me? Nigga, fuck you, you ain’t nothin’ but a hater” Makonie: “Yeah and you’re the reason I’m a hater, you and your kind, why don’t you just all go back to Africa?” Duzza: “I’ll go back to Africa when your white ass goes back to Europe, fool” Makonie: “Well, you better not mess up in this match Duzza, you mess up again and you’ll be in trouble” Duzza: “Fuck you gon’ do? I see you in the street and I’d kick yo ass, if it wasn’t for the fact they’re women and children on this plane I’d kick yo ass now” Makonie: “Your all talk Duzz, all talk” It is now that the plane finally takes off and heads for Tahiti Duzza: “Finally, we’re off the ground” Makonie: “Heh, that’s that exact opposite to your career” Duzza: “Cracka, you ain’t exactly no main eventer yourself now are ya” Makonie: “I’m higher than you Duzz, that’s right” Duzza: “Yeah whatever, if you didn’t have a fucking nazi sign tattoo’ed on your arm you would be just another cracker with a attitude” Makonie: “Duzz, you keep talking and I’m going to have to do you like them coons the Uprising did to you” Duzza: “The Uprising? Bitch please, they ain’t shit. Look at them now, Rush is the only nigga left now. Thug roC left just like I said he would and now Jordan quit too, I’m the survivor here, see they ain’t nothin’ but a flash in the pan, jus’ like you dawg, jus’ like you” Makonie: “I don’t care about the Uprising, I’m thinking about the Refugees now” Duzza: “Why you sweating them turban wearing cracka’s for?” Makonie: If you had a tag team partner who was as useless as I do, you’d be worried. But no, you got lucky, you got a great tag team wrestler in me” Duzza: “Man, you ain’t all that. You need this win as bad as I do. Thinking you all above me jus’ ‘cos you be white, this is 2002 dawg, whitey don’t mean shit no more, it’s a black mans world” Makonie: “Whatever Duzza, you must be the most annoying person I have ever met, of all the coon and the gooks and the spiks and all of them, you’re the most annoying” Duzza: “Fuck you, you only hatin’ cos you know I’m above you” Makonie: “Above me!?” Duzza: “That’s right, I was here before you and I’ll be here after you, mark my words” Makonie: “Mark you words? I didn’t know you could even spell” Duzza: “You need a slap” At this time, the air hostess comes along, with an angry look on her face Air hostess: “Listen guys, either you to shut up or I’ll kick you off the plane without a parachute, comprende?” Duzza: “He started” Makonie: “Did not” Duzza: “Did so” Makonie: “Did not” Air Hostess: “SHUT UP. Please, your both acting like children, so please just shut up and be quiet” Both Duzza and Makonie quiten up. But only for a short time, 2 minutes later there back at it again Duzza: “Makonie, why you think the office is teamin’ us up?” Makonie: “They’re trying to ruin me” Duzza: “Ruin you? I’m the black man who has to work with a nazi damnit” Makonie: “And I’m a nazi surrounded by coons for crying out loud” Duzza: “You’re a ignorant mother fucker, you cross me and I will kick yo ass” Makonie: “You won’t do shit” Duzza: “Yeah, that’s what I want you to think. See, I want you to think I’m harmless then the moment you let your guard down, BOOM, I beat your ass down” Makonie: “Man I’m sick of your shit” Duzza: “Nigga, fuck you. I’ma listen to some R. Kelly and I don’t wanna hear you or even see your ugly bald head until our match” Makonie: “Yeah, listen to that pedophile crap” Duzza: “Fuck you cracker, he’s innocent, I know R. and that’s my dawg, fuck you, innocent until proven guilty, mother fucker” The scene fades to black as Duzza cranks up the volume on the walkman and Makonie turns away from Duzza with disgust, no doubt pondering how on earth the two will work together in their match against the refugees in Tahiti…
