Date: 3/25/02 The camera fades in… The Chef stares out his apartment window watching the millions of flakes of snow fall to the ground as if God was combing his hair and wasn’t using Head and Shoulders. Cars speed on by down the street as if there wasn’t any change to the surface, and ultimately slide around until they land behind another car causing major pile-ups downtown. The people walk across the bustling street, hurrying towards their destination. They failed to realize the relatively horrible conditions of the sidewalk, and slip and fall either face first or backwards onto the hard, unforgiving surface. Schoolchildren, now with a free day to do whatever they want, sling snowballs across the street at each other and manage to nail a few bigwig Wall Street capitalist men holding fresh copies of the Journal in their hand, this provokes a smile and a chuckle from the Chef. The Chef: You know what Bertha? Bertha: What’s the mother f*cking dilley-yo f*ck @$$ sh*t!?!?! The Chef: Right now as I look out of our small window and into the humongous world out there, I truly believe that there’s an Allah… Bertha: What the f*ck!?!? Isn’t that the whole b*tch @$$ f*cking basis to you sh*t for brains angle!?!? The Chef: Yes… But those little tykes throwing grenades of frozen water at American capitalists makes me feel even more special, and even more of a fan of Allah’s work. Bertha: Don’t tell me you had some f*cking poppy seeds in your mother f*cking skank @$$ humping toast again? That f*cking opium gets you sh*tty as f*ck high as a mother f*cking kite, f*cking sh*t. The Chef: You won’t be calling me a pansy any longer after you tried my olive quiche! Bertha: Is it f*cking in the oven!?!? The Chef: Ye… Before the Chef can even mutter a “Yes”, Bertha has pulled the semi-cooked quiche out of the oven, and swallowed it whole, included the metal pan container. Bertha: MMMM, f*cking good, but It needs more sh*t @$$ f*cking time to heat the f*ck up! The Chef, seeing the poor rhinoceros saddened by his angry tone, embraces her and reassures her of his undying clone love. The Chef: There there my little raspberry tart. How about a nice bubble bath to ease your downtrodden self? I’ve got Mr. Bubble! Bertha: Come on d*ck @$$, I’m too big to f*cking fit in the freaking f*cking sh*tty tub for f*cking sakes! The Chef does a 360 degree flip and lands face first on the floor. Bertha: F*cking c*cksucking @$$ humping mother sh*tter @$$ b*tch! The Chef rolls around on the ground clutching his right cheek. Bertha: My f*cking FAT FACTOR is my stink @$$ f*cking weapon, b*tch! Bertha: I f*cking got all the b*tch @$$ power I f*cking need to f*cking take out that f*ck skank @$$ b*tch Sue F*cking Saskatchewan! The Chef: I think her last name is Saksnussum… Bertha: (Interrupting) I don’t f*cking care if her name is b*tch sh*t f*ck @$$ Saskatoon! The Chef pulls himself back up for the ground, nursing his rib area, which was damaged by Bertha’s carelessness. The Chef: So you think you’ve got Sue’s number as far as Turmoil goes, huh? Bertha: It’s f*cking in the full of sh*t bag, hussy @$$… The Chef: So what’s your game plan? Bertha: You f*cking think I’m going to tell you and all those other mother f*cker who are going to f*cking read the promo!?!? F*CKING N-O!!! The Chef: Come on, just a tad of your strategy? Bertha: She can f*cking offer me anything she want, I ain’t gonna take the mother f*cker! The Chef: Somehow I highly doubt that…but listen Bertha, this match is very important; the stipulation for my match at Spring Fling with Big Alan Steele will be decided through your encounter with Sue! Bertha: I f*cking now f*ckdaddy! Don’t you f*cking think I read the sh*tty @$$ b*tch f*cking papers? And how about your f*cking match, b*tch @$$!?!? The odds are overwhelmingly in our favor, as you can plainly see… Bertha: Well isn’t that just f*cking peachy then… The Chef: Yes, yes it is… Praise be to Allah for peachiness! KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK! The Chef and Bertha turn towards the door, wondering who could be wanting in at this time of the day. The Chef: Coming! The Chef picks up his trusty rolling pin, Ol’ Woody, and heads towards the door. Bertha bites her dirty fingernails in a display of sheer anxiety. The Chef opens the door, and a postal deliveryman stands in the hallway. “TELEGRAM FOR THE CHEF!!!” The Chef: Telegram? People are still sending those? “You better believe it sir! My job even comes with a dental plan!” The Chef: Dental Plan!?!? The PCW union has been working for more than a year for one, and still has been unsuccessful, I envy you Mr. Telegram guy… “That’s what they all say!” Bertha: Get to the f*cking Telegram already b*tch! “Yes ma’am! The telegram reads…Villainous Chef; you are a terrible scourge that I shall love to rid from the PCW! Tonight our valets will fight for the right for one of use to declare a stipulation, and when my Sue comes out on top, my choice of a match will make you dread you ever injured me! The Fates are smiling upon me, and after I go Olympic style on you behind, I will enjoy the fruits of Bacchus with my lovely Siren Sue. Oh and one more thing…HI-KEEBA!!!” The Chef: I have no idea who that could be… Bertha walks up to the Chef shakes him violently. Bertha: That’s a f*cking telegram from Big Alan Steele you f*cking sh*t for brains moron! The Chef: (Suddenly realizing what Bertha said was true) Oh…OOOHHHH! That fiend! That infidel! That…remarkably good speller. The Chef admires the telegram. “Actually sir, we spell check every telegram!” The Chef: AH HA! So he isn’t quite the great speller as I once expected… Well, I can see that the tides are turning! Telegram man, take this down… “Yes sir!” The Chef: Praise be to Allah for idiots like you who think you can sauté the @$$ of the Culinary Conquistador! I don’t care what match will be chosen for our date at Spring Fling, because I know I’ll sauté your culinary school dropout @$$ with the finest meat, add some green peppers, onions, and roll you up in freshly baked pita bread for the perfect gyro! Bertha: Mmmm, I could f*cking go for gyro… The Chef: I praise you for your heroic bravery Steele, but bravery will get you nowhere in life, in fact I believe bravery is your Achilles’ heel! You always have to do the “right thing” and someday you will be faced with a choice, and you heroic nature will prove to be your downfall! Adios Enemigo! “Ok, thank you sir, that will be twenty dollars for the telegram delivery”. The Chef: What!?!? That’s outrageous! Oh…all right…I’ll do it! The Chef hands the man a twenty. The deliveryman salutes the Chef and scampers off. The Chef: Bertha, we are heading into Chefcon 5! Bertha: What the f*ck? The Chef: It’s just my parody of US emergency military procedures… Bertha: Oh, I see f*cko… The Chef: Prepare for the worst, because the worst is yet to come my dear… Bertha: (Saluting) F*cking preparing right now! Bertha opens the refrigerator door and crams down a few Long Johns as the Chef sighs. The camera mercifully fades to black.
