What's Worse Than Clyde?

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/09/02 “Um... Why don’t you, like, just build a fake Clyde and kill that instead?” Our shot opens up on the naive face of PCW’s preppy Commissioner Eleanor Hall, her expression looking as if her previous statement was the most obvious thing in the universe. Eleanor : “That would, you know, save him tearing you limb from limb and setting you on fire... And stuff!” Her Pink lips give a happy little grin as the camera pans to show the face of her husband, the PCW Champion Xavier Kannon... Looking deep in thought as Ellie~! waits eagerly. Eleanor : “Okay, so here comes the bit where you make up some excuse not to use my idea... Blaming it on REALITY or whatever...” Kannon’s look of thought breaks into a smile, his pointed finger shaking to emphasise that he wants to run with the idea, and Ellie~! looking as surprised as surprised comes without the special “Extra Surprised” expansion pack. Xavier : “My little Poon Puppy, you may just have cracked it...” He jets up from his seat and kisses his wife on the lips, his excitement at her idea still startling the little prep. Eleanor : “Is this, like, sarcasm.... Because if it is...” With all the power she can muster.... So decidedly less that that exhuded from a Hamster running in it’s wheel... She tries to give Kannon a dead arm, the impact not even registering with the Champion. Eleanor : “And if not... Then, like.... YAY ME!!!!“ As she celebrates her ludicrous idea, Kannon starts to pace back and forth, gesturing wildly as he brings all his and Ellie~!’s idea together. Xavier : “All I need to do is bring all of Clyde’s personality traits together to form a CLYDE MARK TWO!“ Already the little prep is lost. Eleanor : “Who’s Mark?” Kannon keeps throwing out his plan, Ellie~! doing her best to keep up. Xavier : “If I find people who resemble parts of Clyde... I can form not only another Clyde... But a SUPER Clyde!” The wildly excited face of Kannon isn’t reflected in the confused face of his wife. Eleanor : “Is that, like, a Bird? Or a Plane?” His pacing then comes to a stop as the inevitable consequence of the creation hits the front of his mind. Xavier : “A Clyde that can destroy Clyde....IT’S PERFECT!!!“ Showing that she vaguely understands what her husband is going on about, even though she had the initial idea, the prep gets a little more inquisitive. Eleanor : “So, um.... Like, where are you going to get the bits from?” The smile on the CEO’s face can best be described as “Cheshire Cat” as the plan runs over and over in his Scientological brain. Xavier : “Oh, fear not my little Fuck Ferret... I think I know where to go...” “ROBERT! THE INTERNET PAEDOPHILE POLICE ARE HERE TO SPEAK TO YOU AGAIN!!!!” Our shot cuts to a dark and dingy basement somewhere in the slum area of Cape Canaveral, Florida... Where a grotesquely fat man in his mid-20’s and wearing a tight, ill-fitting t-shirt is sat at his computer talking on AIM to 12YrOldGrl. “I KEEP TELLING YOU CRACK WHORE MOTHER... THE AGE OF CONSENT ON THE INTERNET IS 11!!!“ As he cybers the young girl, he switches between that and a Hotmail window where he types away with vigour.. Dribbling from the mouth as he reads out what he types. “I love you... I want to be with you... Your boyfriend doesn’t love you as much as I do... Come join the new BOTS... Oh, and tell Kreller I hate him, and WHY ISN’T BODA E-MAILING ME!?!?! Anyway, watch Turmoil this week where I reveal that I am really Kannon and the whole thing was a SWERVE~! Oh, and eVolution is so much better than anything... Blah blah blah blah...” Suddenly a knocking is heard at the basement’s door and the man manages to turn his huge frame around in his tight fitting chair to face it, the very effort of which causes him to break into a sweat. “Must be the 4 Pizzas I ordered to get me through this mammoth typing session...” Much resembling a beached whale, “Robert” heaves himself over to the door.. Each step making a sound as if the earth below it is straining under the weight of his bloated, unwashed body. “What?” His porky hands grip the door handle, drenching it in his sweat before pulling it open to show nobody there. “HAHAHAH!!! I KNOW YOU’RE THERE AIMZ!!! I KNOW YOU ARE HERE WANTING M-” *ZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ* Suddenly something attached to 2 cords hits him in his bitch-tit sporting chest and dig into the sweaty, spotty flab before channelling enough Volts to knock the huge man out... His huge bulk spreading out like a pancake as he lands on the dirty yard. A familiar voice is then heard walking out from the dark bushes beside the dirty, derelict Wilkerson residence. “Well.... We now have the PYSCHO part ready, and a bit of the Paedo to boot!” “Psycho.... Part..... Ready! The camera zooms in on the convulsing form of Robert Wilkerson, shaking like a jelly in a rather hypnotic.. Yet disgusting way as our shot fades out. “OH YOU GOT IT GOIN’ ON LIL’ HO!!!!” The shot leaves the darkness and opens up on the face of famous Paedophillic R&B star R Kelly, seemingly enjoying the company of some females. “Shake that ghetto booty on over here Girly....” Kelly’s eyes widen as they transfix themselves on the shaking ass of one of his “Ho’s”.... “Ah yeah, I’m the World’s Greatest, bitch!” The shot then pulls out to show that his is surrounded by the Los Angeles Under-13’s Choir.... The idol of Duzza’s bumps and grinds against the 12 year old, singling lines from his hits as he eyes the not yet ripe Peach up. *KNOCK* *KNOCK* “Oh shit... They ain’t gonna believe the look-a-like story twice.... Bitches, be 18 or summin’!” Before R Kelly can even open the door, it is kicked in and a net flung over the child-loving R&B star, followed by several tranquilliser darts before the figure of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon walks in and looks down at the man, then his surroundings. Xavier : “Hmmmmm, they’re a little old for Clyde, but Bobby should balance it out... LOAD HIM UP!!!” As Kannon directs people to remove the wank-singing paedo, the shot rapidly cuts away to somewhere even worse than R Kelly’s Paedo den or the Wilkerson household... HELL... Well, come to think of it, it doesn’t seem quite so bad compared to the last 2. “Haha.. I am the Devil.. I’m EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEVIL!!!!! EVIL I SA-UGH!!!” Just as we see Satan himself, his Red body drops down onto the hot stone floor of hell, showing Ellie~! standing behind him with a big stick in her hand. Eleanor : “Um... HELLO?!?!?!?... Like get some AIR CONDITIONING!“ The shot then cuts from the annoyed face of the little Prep to a telephone booth like contraption that houses Robert Wilkerson, R Kelly and Satan. “Okay.... Time to make me a Clyde!” Standing by a large lever is the PCW Champion Xavier Kannon, at his side his enthusiastic wife Ellie~!... Awaiting the end product of the off experiment... A new and improved Clyde that he can use to destroy the old one. *CLUNK* The lever is pulled and the 3 are lost in ripples of electricity and smoke.. Screams and groans being heard as the new personification of evil is made.... ... Eventually the smoke clears and the first couple of PCW stare into the reaction machine... A look of pure fear in their eyes. Eleanor : “Um... That SO isn’t Clyde...” The little prep starts to back away, hiding behind her husband who’s agasp and whispering to himself in abject terror. Xavier : “What have we done?” A voice is heard from the reactor, sounding somewhat familiar. Thanks for bringing me back, Xavi... My old Asylum team mate!” Kannon backs away, Ellie~! holding on to him in order to protect herself from the figure composed of pure evil that stands before them.. Flashes of bright Red hair being seen through the clearing smoke along with a pair of sunglasses with lightning flashes across them... Like Macho Man used to wear. “NO REALLY... I MEAN IT.” Both gulping, Kannon and Ellie~! turn to each other... Their fearful eyes locking onto each others as the shot fades out to Black. “ARGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!“
