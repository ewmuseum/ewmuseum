Syrup

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 11, 2002 [A very long sigh is let out from one of the most bodacious dudes in Primetime Championship Wrestling, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. He lies in his waterbed, the blue and satin sheets covering his lower body. The two half naked women lying on each side of him says it all, Tommy has still got it. Even after being bogusly rejected by Kelly Slater's babe at Applebees Tommy somehow dug down deep into his gnarly soul and showed that he could still work it with the babes. Lets face it, Tommy hasn't played Yahtzee since those heinous counceling sessions for sexual addiction. It was a VERY righteous release with these babes. It was all night, into the morning, and almost through the afternoon. Yeah, it's something to be most proud of...] SANDY WONG IS A HUMAN BEING, I CANNOT GIVE AWAY A HUMAN BEING. [For some reason that sentence rings through Tommy's ears. Yes, he has just had sex, but for some reason he can hear that totally bogus dude The End telling him that giving away a human is not right, that it's bogus. Why would The End pop into Tommy's head? Why would that totally heinous blind dude just pop up like that? The End has been nothing but a thorn in Tommy's side lately. He asked nicely for his Christmas wishes and The End denies him, and Tommy knows exactly why The End did that...HE'S JEALOUS! Tommy has one thing that The End doesn't have, and that's SIGHT. Yeah, compadres, Tommy can see the bodacious babes lying next to him. The End wouldn't be able to enjoy the splender of looking at these babes, he'd have to feel around...which isn't a bogus thing. It's all about jealousy. It's always been about jealousy, ever since day one. It's like it follows Tommy around and everybody despises him for some reason. Tommy can't help it that he's a totally gnarly sex symbol and was born with natrual good looks! He can't help it that he has the natrual talent to be the greatest professional wrestler EVER] SANDY WONG IS A HUMAN BEING, I CANNOT GIVE AWAY A HUMAN BEING. [All Tommy wanted was for Sandy to come over, entertain his bodacious bro, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. The dude has been through some heinous things in the past weeks, and he just needs some much needed release. Harry has always had a thing for Sandy, ever since those early interviews she conducted with the Cowabunga Connection. Harry just felt he had this far out connection with her that was COSMIC, man. He couldn't explain it, just that it gave him a special feeling inside. But now she's hanging around the blind, bogus Celestial dude who could never be half the man that Harry could be. Harry could really provide some gnarly things for Sandy...like...ummm...you know, he could provide...well, he could provide some stuff and it is TOTALLY BODACIOUS stuff!] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Give me the syrup, man. [Tommy and Harry now sit in a local Denny's, taking down some bodacious breakfast's that they serve there. The place is pretty crowded for mid-afternoon as it's around lunch time, but not for the former Cowabunga Connection. They need their breakfast like Michael Jackson needs another nose job. Tommy, in his Hawaiian dress shirt, khaki cargo shorts, and black sandals, stares outside of the window to the grassy grades of an office complex. His mind wandering about his future, his great future in PCW. The fact that he should be Continental Champion right now, and that The End is going to pay for being so bogus to them, especially not hooking Harry up with some lovin'] Harry: Hey, earth to Tommy, pass the syrup, man. [The Tubular One still does not hear his bodacious bro asking for the syrup. Tommy knows that coming up on Fusion he's going to be teaming with some other bogus dudes, but it'll be a most excellent chance to get his hands on The End. Walden, Rush, and Jackson will be in Tommy's corner just as a precaution in case The End tries to do some cheating stuff. If that happens then Tommy will most likely tag out, probably to Rush since he's the Champ, and he needs to get roughed up a little bit] Harry: Tommy is gay if he doesn't pass me the syrup, man! [Tommy quickly passes the syrup over to his brother, who wheres a large smile across his face as he pours the syrup all over his pancakes. Tommy shakes his head, shaking out the cobwebs. He's really letting this End Christmas wish thing get to him, it's totally bogus] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, do you really believe that I can take The End? [Harry is still drowning his pancakes with the syrup] Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Well, of course you'd agree with me, compadre. But, I'm talking like as if you were just a common PCW fan, no favoritism, nothing to do that bogus stuff. Just you watching the match, who would you be pulling for? [Harry continues his syrup onslaught] Harry: Well, if I were the casual fan I'd have to go with skill. The End, I believe, has much more skill that Tommy...because let's face it wrestling fans...THE END IS BLIND. That's right, he's blind and still wows crowds with his great offense, probably because he's Celestial and God-like. Tommy, I'll have to say is very agile. He can nail you with powerful moves, yet get on that top rope and fly with the best of them. So, in conclusion to your question...I'd have to say that The End would.... [Tommy gives Harry a very evil stare] Harry: ...get his keister beaten by Tommy, in TUBULAR fashion..........man. TTT: Your high, aren't you? [Harry continues pouring the pool of syrup on his pancakes] Harry: Of course, man. [Tommy looks at the syrup overflowing on Harry's plate, the pancakes aren't even visible any longer] TTT: Dude, could you put any more syrup on there? [Harry empties the syrup container, shrugs at Tommy, and then looks up at the waitress. She looks down at the plate with a large smile. Harry then hands the empty syrup container to her] Harry: Could you get me some more syrup, man? [The waitress looks at Harry like he's gone insane. She shrugs and takes the empty syrup container toward the back. Harry smiles like a little child at his pool of syrup and the pancakes that are buried underneath somewhere] TTT: Would you like PANCAKES with your syrup, compadre? Harry: No, man. TTT: Har, this whole Christmas wish has been bugging me. I feel so bogus about not getting you Sandy Wong. You should be sitting with her right now, dude. I should be Sandy and she should be the babe pouring your syrup on your pancakes, she should be doing that every morning. Just for you, the compadre that will treat her SO MUCH better than The End. [The waitress returns with a full container of syrup] Waitress: There ya go, syrup boy. [Harry looks up with a large smile on his face] Harry: Thank you, man. TTT: Dude, I can't WAIT until Friday though. I'm finally going to be able to get my hands on The End. I'm going to beat the living crap out of that bogus blind dude. Show him exactly what happens when you don't grant Tommy his Christmas wish, especially when it's for his totally bodacious bro. Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry begins dumping more syrup on the already overflowing pool on his plate. His face is bright, knowing that this is going to be the most far out thing he's ever eaten. Tommy scoops up a few straggling scrambled eggs and gobbles them up, gently pressing his white napkin against his face. He just watches as his brother further drowns the pancakes, which they have probably already been soaked through with syrup ten times over] TTT: I just hope that my partners don't go and try to blow it for me, dude. You know how other compadres can be when they have something they want. SO SELFISH! Always thinking about themselves, when The Tubular One could be giving The End the beating of his life. [Harry continues to pour on the syrup] TTT: Tim Walden did try to trick me last week on Fusion, which I feel cost me the title in the end...hehe..THE END...ARGH! [Tommy pounds the table top and some of the syrup splashes up and onto the floor, Harry watches it closely and makes sure he pours the syrup over where the lost syrup was at] TTT: His name is like EVERYWHERE...it's heinous. I'm probably going to go most insane before Friday....whew...maybe I need some therapy. [Harry pours on] TTT: Wait...what am I saying? I don't need therapy, dude. I just need a most bodacious night, like the one that I had last night with those two bodacious babes. I need to be the Tubular One, I need to just cut loose, have a blast, and when The End gets in my face I'll TOTALLY kick his ass. [Harry finally stops pouring] Harry: Far out, man. [Harry inspects the syrup again, then the container, and then he looks at the plate full of syrup as some of it leaks down the sides of the plate] Harry: That is JUST not enough syrup, man. [Fade to black]
