At the bus stop.....(Part Dos)

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-13-02 ::Introduction:: Mark does not see why people smoke weed? People can die from it when they take it or they have an over dose. That is the worst thing for Mark, if he sees a white raced man sitting on the side of the street smoking a joint. That is like going against what people say. Only the African Americans are the ones who smoke the joints and who waste their life on just a high. How many other Americans do that? Well that is hard to tell since there are a lot of people who depend on the drug, just like Semore Smoke. That choice is theirs, and not mine, all I am saying is that you are a disgrace to the white race every time you sit down and light up a joint. After that first puff, it seems that you can’t stop and that is where your life ends right there. ::End Introduction:: Mark is sitting down on the bus stop bench, just like we left him last. He pulls his black bag over to his feet, making a scratching sound across the cement. Mark then unzips the bag and he pulls out a pamphlet to a museum. He has it in his hand and he starts to look through it. “Learn all about the Holocaust and what affects it had on America.” Mark reads as he is flipping through the pamphlet, looking at the pictures and reading the headlines. Mark then hears people talking behind him and they are just a group of African American teenagers trying to figure out what they are going to do next on their day of shopping. Mark starts to get up from his seat as they start to see Mark rise up. The boys then start to scatter and Mark just sits back in his seat. “African Americans are the people who fear me the most.” Mark says as he moves his black bag right next to the bench as he puts his hands together. “I don’t know what it is with me. I don’t see if intimidate them or if it is just my looks that shy them away. I know that I am not a very good looking guy, but I guess that I have that evil look in my eye and face that makes them all scared of me.” Mark looks up as a bus starts to pull up on the side of the street. Mark gets his bag and he slings it over his shoulder. He starts to go over to where the bus has just stopped, in between the two white lines. The clear bus doors open as it reveals the face of the driver. The driver is an older white man who has a grayish black beard. He has grayish black hair, but not much of it. He is dressed in just casual clothes as Mark heads up to the opened doors. “Is this the number nine bus?” Mark asks as he puts one of his feet on the steps of the bus. “Yes sir it would be that bus.” The driver says as he gives Mark a wink after he says that. Mark goes up the steps, one after the other as he puts some change in the toll place. He starts to walk back to the back of the bus as he sees that there aren’t anymore seats available. Mark then looks up at the roof as he notices some straps that he can hang onto to keep him stable. But that is the last resort for Mark. He then looks at who is in the back of the bus and there is a family of African Americans sitting in a whole row of seats. Mark looks at the family and he then gets a grin on his face. “You all get up so that I can have my seat.” Mark says as he puts his hand on the mother and then grabs some of her shirt on the shoulder. “We were here first and we are not going to move.” The dad says as he starts to hold his wife’s hand. “Well let me put it this way.” Mark starts to say as he looks down at the wife’s face and she has a look of fear on her face. “If you don’t move then I will just have to move your wife for you, and then just throw her around this bus. Then I will go to your kids and make sure that they would never see you again.” Mark says as he starts to pick up the wife. “Now you don’t want that to happen, do you?” “No, but we are free just like your race so we have some rights in this world.” The dad says as Mark still has a hold of his wife. “So what you are telling me is that you want to see your wife sitting down here in the aisle, with a bloody face?” Mark asks as he has the wife all the way up off of her seat. “NO!” The dad screams as he starts to get up out of his seat. “You would never do that to her.” “Do you want to find out?” Mark asks as he starts to move to wife out in the aisle. The dad takes his kids out of the row and Mark lets go of the wife. Mark then smiles at the family and Mark the goes and takes a seat on the row that they just got out of. Mark then spreads his legs out across the rest of the seats that are empty. “I love the power of the white race.” Mark says as he starts to close his eyes to go to sleep. *** An Hour Later The breaks on the bus start to screech across the pavement as it makes all of the passengers on the bus go forward. It makes Mark go out of his seat, and onto the ground. Mark then picks himself up off of the floor as the family of African Americans are still standing and hanging onto the straps that are attached to the roof. “We have arrived at the destination of the Holocaust Museum of Houston.” The bus driver says as he comes on to the PA system. “I hope that you enjoyed the ride over here and that we made it to please your pleasure.” Everyone on the bus gets up from their seat as does Mark. Mark starts to scoot across the row towards the aisle. The African American family let go of the straps and the kids are crying. “Dad my legs hurt.” The little boy says as he starts to have tears run down his eyes. “Don’t worry son it will be alright, we are here now.” The dad says, patting the kid on the head and making him stop crying. The dad then gives a grin towards Mark as he tries to make his eyes look evil, but it is not working. Mark starts to laugh a bit as the family then gets bombarded by people getting out of their seats. It makes the family squeeze together and to have the scrunched by the white race. Mark then follows what everyone is doing and he goes into the aisle of the bus. He goes in front of the African American family as he wants to get away from them as soon as he can. “I HAVE HAD ENOUGH!” The dad of the family screams as he is getting squashed by everyone on the bus. “Shut your face you coon.” Mark shouts as he looks behind him to take a look at the reaction of the dad. He just looks like he is pissed off and he starts to grind his teeth. People start to head off of the bus looking at the bus driver and telling him thanks for the ride there. Mark starts to move up on the bus as people are getting off. Mark starts to look in the seats of the bus to see if anyone has left anything. Then while passing a row, Mark sees a do rag just laying there. He picks up the gangster piece and he stuffs it in his pocket. “Thanks.” Mark says as he smiles at the bus driver when he goes by. Mark heads down the bus steps, one after another until he gets to the cement of the sidewalk. Mark then goes up a couple of steps as he looks up and he sees the museum. It has a big sign on it as it reads: Holocaust Museum of Houston. Mark smiles as he starts to walk up the steps and he sees a statue at the top of the steps. He starts to run to it as he notices who it is. It is a statue of Hitler and Mark starts to get near the cement piece. Mark gets up to the piece close and he looks up at it. He smiles and he starts to read what it says on the slate. “The most powerful man during the Holocaust.” Mark starts to read the slate and he looks up from the slate, and then looks back down. “This statue is not in remembrance of Hitler; it is to show the scum’s face to the world that flipped this world upside down.” Mark stops smiling as this is a disgrace to Hitler. Mark steps away from the statue and then he does the hail Hitler sign to the statue with his hand in the air. The African American family sees Mark do the sign. “Daddy, what is that man doing to that statue?” The little boy asks as the dad looks at Mark. “Son, I don’t want you to ever do that in your life as that is a disgrace to our race.” The dad says as he looks down at the little kid. The little kid then does the sign, right as Mark turns around to see the kid. Mark smiles and the realizes it is a little African American boy who just did that and then gets a grin on his face as he sees who is right next to the kid. The dad and Mark then sits there, looking at the family and the at the Hitler statue. “That dad made that kid do that, to show a disgrace for Hitler.” Mark says right as he grinds his teeth together. “He made his kid do, in a mockery of Hitler. That is just disgusting to see.” Mark says as he then turns his back and he starts to walk into the museum with some events on his mind. Those events will not be revealed right now as only one of them can. That is the match with Smoke on Turmoil in which that is the first step Mark has to take to become a champion in PCW.
