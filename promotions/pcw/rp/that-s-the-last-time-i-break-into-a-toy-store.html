That's The Last Time I Break Into A Toy Store...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.04.2992 The scene opens in some seedy part of New York City. Probably somewhere in Queens. It is the early morning, like 1am in the morning. There is not a soul around. There is a long road, very wide with parked each side of the road, opposite long terraced houses. Smoke, caught in the street lamps can be seen rising from manholes in the middle of the street. Of all the cars in the street only one appears to have anybody in it. That is a large black jeep. A BMW X5, about $48,000 brand new. A fine piece of equipment. In the car, a muscular African American man is sitting at the wheel. On the street, it is pretty quiet and there is just one guy walking along the street. That man also appears to be a pretty muscular African American. He walks up to the black jeep and after looking around to see if the coast is clear he jumps into the car. The two men shake hands in a ghetto-fabulous way. Once in the car it is much easier to see who the two men are. One is current PCW superstar Duzza and the man behind the wheel of the car is current NTICW star and Civil Rights leader Jordan X Shabazz. Duzza is dressed in a pair of baggy black jeans, a black “Harlem” FUBU shirt, about size XXXL, he has a black beanie over his head covering his long cornrows, which just stick out a bit at the end and has two diamond earrings in his ear lobes. Jordan X meanwhile is dressed in a pair of black pants, a black tight jumper, a black beanie and he has black gloves and shows on. Duzza: “A’ight dawg, what was so important that you had to ring me at this time of night man?” Jordan X: “Well, you owe me a favor right man?” Duzza: “Yeah man, I probably do” Jordan X: “Well, do you want to help a brother out now?” Duzza: “Man, you know I’m always good to help a brother out, what’s the deal?” Jordan X: “Well, do you want to make some extra cash on the side man?” Duzza: “Man, I’ma true hustler you know I can turn down no opportunity to make the green dawg!” Jordan X: “I was hoping you was gonna say that, ok you wanna hear the plan?” Duzza: “ Yes, obviously” Jordan X: “ Ok, there’s this toy store of 57th avenue, ok, now it is dead easy to break into the store, and from there we can round up some toys and sell them on the black market for a profit” Duzza: “We’re going to rob a toy store?” Jordan X: “Yeah, easy cash” Duzza: “Ok, now did you just say we’re going to rob a toy store? Or am I tripping again?” Jordan X: “Yes Duzz, gees man, what’s wrong with you? It’s easy money” Duzza: “Man, I have carried a gun to a funeral, but damn don’t you think stealing from a toy store is just…wrong?” Jordan X: “Nah man, it’ll be fine, now are you in or out?” Duzza: “Man, I already dragged myself out here anyway, I may as well come with chu” Jordan X: “I knew I could count on you Duzz” Jordan starts up the powerful jeep engine and they pull away. The roads are pretty empty as anyone awake at this time is either in a club or homeless. Duzza: “Jordan, man I was wondering who would buy stolen toys anyway? I mean, guns and drugs, hell even porn, there is a market for, but stolen toys?” Jordan: “Oh yeah, it’s a real big industry man, go up to like Connecticut and there’s a real big money for it” Duzza: “ Man, white people will waste money on anything I tell ya” Jordan X: “That is so true Duzz, so true” Duzza: “Y’know JC..” Jordan: “Why you disrespect me like that Duzz?” Duzza: “Huh? What you mean man?” Jordan X: “My name is Jordan X Shabazz, not Jordan Crazy or JC ok?” Duzza: “Umm..ok…anyway man, can I continue you with what I saying now?” Jordan X: “Please do” Duzza: “Well man, for a Civil Rights leader your one corrupt mother f*cker, I mean stealing kids toys! That’s cold man” Jordan X: “It’s a cold world my friend, a cold world. Anyway, were here?” They pull up outside a small toy store. There are small wooden toys in the front window. A small fence runs around the shop. The shop looks like somewhere a elf would live. Jordan goes to the back of the car and pulls out a small tall box and some long black rope. While Duzza just looks at the small, innocent toy shop. He has done some bad things in his lifetime but even he knows that grown men stealing from a toy shop is just wrong. Duzza walks up to the front door, he is about to put his fist through the window, when Jordan X stops him Jordan: “Duzz, what the hell are you doing?” Duzza: “What did it look like? I’ma break into this store” Jordan X: “Duzz, listen this isn’t your liquor store back in the hood, this place has an alarm” Duzza: “For real!? Damn” Jordan X: “Ok Duzz, we can get on the roof easily by using this fence, so just follow what I do” Jordan jumps up onto the fence that runs around the back of the store and from there easily climbs on the roof, Duzza eventually follows him up there Duzza: “Umm J, why are we on the roof? There must be a easier way to get in that this” Jordan: “Ok Duzz, here’s the plan, I tie this rope around you, then I lower you in and you fill up this bag with toys then I raise you back up and we’re out” Duzza: “ Sorry what? Ain’t nobody tying no rope around me man” Jordan: “Listen, I’m not going to try and lynch you, not let’s just do this ok?” Duzza: “Man, when I said I’d help you out, you never mentioned tying rope around me and lowering me into a damn toy shop” Jordan: “Well sorry, for not going into a detailed plan of what we would be doing” Duzza: “So you should be” Jordan: “Ok, Duzz I’m sorry, now please let me just tie this around you” Jordan moves closer to Duzza Duzza: “Step back Negro, step the fuck back. We’re on a roof, don’t mess with a man when your on a roof” Jordan: “Listen Duzz, please, I’ll lower you down, while I watch to make sure no one is coming” Duzza: “Or… I lower you down and I’ll be lookout” Jordan: “Duzz, you are one stubborn mother f*cker” Duzza: “Man, you should meet my wife” Duzza ties the rope around Jordan’s waist and slowly lowers Jordan X into the store. Jordan lands on a small counter. Jordan: “Duzza, don’t let go of the rope” Duzza: “Man, what you think I am? Actually don’t answer that” After about 5 minutes of Jordan X filling the bag he has of toys he can reach from the counter he is on, he jumps of the counter and on to the floor. Suddenly, with out warning alarms go off all around him. All out on the street, house lights start turning on. Duzza panics and drops the rope Duzza: “Aww sh*t…f*ck this I’m out of here” Duzza runs to get of the roof, when Jordan X hollers at him Jordan X: “Don’t leave me Duzz, you can’t leave me” Duzza: “ Man, use the freaking door” Jordan X: “It’s locked damn it. And you can’t get away, I got the car keys” Duzza: “ Aww shit” Duzza quickly runs back to the window on the roof where Jordan got in. He tells him to throw him the rope and after about a minute of Duzza pulling away Jordan finally emerges out from the store. Both men quickly run and jump down from the roof when sirens can be heard in the distance. Both men jump into the BMW and speed away into the darkness as the scene starts to fade, both no doubt praying that they get away with this…
