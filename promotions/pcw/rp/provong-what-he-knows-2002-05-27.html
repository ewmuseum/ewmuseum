Provong What He Knows

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/05/02 Kannon....... Extreme Title? Something about it just doesn’t sound right. Xavier has been Cruiserweight Champion, Continental Champion, even Commissioner.... But none really scream HARDCORE!. Maybe Violence Jack, maybe the Rush, Big Davylicious.... All would not raise an eyebrow if they were to hold the Extreme Title aloft... But Kannon.... Extreme Champion? Maybe it SOUNDS a little strange.... But Xavier is more than a sound ring technician. At Shang-Haied 2 he flew off a balcony, against Jeremy Howard both men took injuries that could have ended careers and at Supremacy Kannon had to wipe his own blood from his eyes to even see Duke to pin him. Kannon..... Hardcore? In his own style, his own unique way, the ex-Commissioner is Hardcore. This Friday he has a chance to show he can not only wrestle... But fight. Blood will be spilt, weapons will be brandished and injuries will be inflicted.... Can Kannon win it? Can he escape the School grounds and take the Extreme Title... Maybe? Kannon? Extreme Title? “NO YOU FOOL!!!! WHERE IS THAT EXPONENTIAL GOING..... NO, THE NAPERIAN LOGARITHM IS TAKEN OF THE “R” AND MOVED OVER TO THE OTHER SIDE!!!” “Okay, calm down... My mistake.... So what do we have on the left hand side?” “Once the brackets have been multiplied out we are left with Xac + RY + 4ac + FRT + 9.81....” Our shot opens up in the Department of Mathematics at the Courtney Love Institute in Hubbardville, where a collection of Scientology’s finest minds come together to discuss new theories and methods. At the front of a near-deserted lecture theatre is a team of 5 Mathematicians, we know this because they carry “The Sign of the Mathematician”.... The Brown leather patches on the elbows of a tweed jacket... ....Oh, and I promise the janitor won’t come along and solve the equation.... Even if he is a Scientologist. Mathematician #1... Or #2... Maybe #3 : “Hmmmm.... This is a tricky one.... Proving the conundrum using this formula has been our greatest challenge since yesterday.... When we were told about it!” The 5 scuttling little Maths boffins each hold their own piece of White chalk and each scribble in a separate section of the Blackboard.... Pulling numbers out of their heads and substituting formulas in with ease. Mathematician *Number Indetermined* : “Wait..... If we square root this, then we can substitute in the Grendal’s equation which means we can eliminate that....” All 5 of them stop, look, put 3 fingers up for their chin to rest on. 5 Mathematicians : “Hmmmmmmmmmm....” As 4 of them run it over in their head, hoping it is wrong so they can ridicule the other one and vindicate themselves for not knowing it, whistling is heard from outside the door. *CLANG* Footsteps are then heard as a young man carrying a mop and bucket walks in, whistling a ditty tune as he places the bucket on a desk and casts an eye over the equation. Janitor : “Hey, what if the-” Sensing the threat of another mathematical brain in the room, the pack hunter mentality effects the group and they turn in unison just as the janitor, who in no way resembles Matt Damon in any way as he is not a Scientologist... Yet.... Goes to grab a piece of chalk himself and do something, well, Math-y to solve the equation, or prove it... Or something... 5 Mathematicians : “DON’T TOUCH THAT!” The janitor, looking as if this isn’t the first time it has happened holds up his hands and gets back to his duties, walking back out the door and muttering under his breath. Janitor : “I’d divide the right hand side by 7XR...” As all 5 watch him leave, deadly silent and not taking their bespectacled eyes off of him they suddenly shake off their frosty glares and talk amongst each other. One Of Them : “How about we divide the right side by 7XR?” Other One, Looks The Same : “Just what I was thinking....” As they get back to their arduous and let’s face it, rather boring task we move backwards through the rows of seats to show none other than wrestling’s only Scientologist... Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon who is impatiently drumming his fingers on the desk just loud enough to distract the mathematicians. They all turn to look up at Xavier who is staring past his leather trouser-clad legs that are resting up in the desk..... Kannon simply makes a casual gesture towards the board with his right hand. Xavier : “.....Carry on.....” As they get back on with their job, creating their usual hum of enthusiasm and chalk scribbling noises, Xavier runs his hands through his long Auburn hair and ties it back, looking up to the ceiling rather dreamily. Xavier : “5 days to go.... I can work that one out without chalk and a Black board... Yes, Friday minus Monday = 5..... 5 days until I once again feel leather around my torso, and that feeling on the small of my back as I press the poppers on the belt to buckle it around me.....” He again resumes his drumming but is not even looking in the direction of he Maths work to see the agitated twitching of the 5 Mathematicians trying to work through the annoyance of the rhythmic tapping. Xavier : “...It will nicely with the collection..... Cruiserweight..... Continental..... Then Extreme.... A Triad.... A Trio.... A Triv-.... Urgh.... The nerdiness in this room is infectious.... THREE.... That will make three Titles.... And all I have to do is take out a less than select bunch of jobbers, newbies, Borro and Miyajobber.... And then... LOVECRAFT!” The relaxed and apathetic face of Kannon contorts into a snarl at the mention of his supposed Ultimate Authority stablemate. Xavier : “When LOVECRAFT is getting a 7 on 1, 6 on 1, 5 on 1, whatever on 1 beatdown in the school gym or science lab.... He shouldn’t rely on me helping him.... Where was he when the Jobber Crew... No, wait, that is the Malice Crew.... The Newbie Jobber Crew, of Phantom Phive or whatever they want to call themselves were ASSAULTING... Not attacking... ASSAULTING US?” The aggravation on his face is clear for all to see, after thinking he had pulled the SWERVE~! by resolving his differences with Tyler Nelson to fight alongside him.... DA BOSS~! and the ex-Commish were upstaged and outsmarted as the Phantom Booked pulled another Ace out of the Hole. Xavier : “Well..... I will be in the same place as he was..... Looking after number one.... I have no desire to get into a toe to toe brawl.... I’m not a fucking Gypsy travelling town to town bare knuckle fighting and I am not lowering myself to using trashcans or whatever junk comes to hand.... I will win it like I win all my other matches.... By being that little bit better and that little bit smarter.” As he goes to run his palm along the top of his tied back Auburn hair, he stops just past his hairline and pokes around at the deep cut to the head he received at Supremacy when blood was pouring down his face, into his mouth and up his nose as he struggled for breath. Xavier : “I can bleed, I can scrap.... But I am above them all.... The Extreme Title is the Lowest Common Denominator...” The Scientologist chuckles to himself and grins smugly. Xavier : “No wonder the PCW fans like it so much...... The Extreme Title needs rescuing.... It needs wrestling brought back to it. Extreme isn’t about hitting each other with sticks.... Extreme is doing what you HAVE to do to win, doing ANYTHING to gain that extra advantage..... And when it comes to that I am as Extreme as they come...” His Green eyes now look bored, just staring up at a fixed position on the ceiling that has nothing to justify his attention. As the camera zooms in his eyes can be seen creeping down to look over to the Board where the 5 men are still scribbling rapidly. Xavier : “Hell, or whatever the Scientological equivalent is... Probably Utah, even those people down there are Extreme..... Extreme is NOT Violence Jack and his anti-Authority stance.... Extreme is NOT the Rush with his Street Attitude and Extreme CERTAINLY isn’t.... Well, anyone in the damn match other than me.... Extreme needs a makeover and I’m it’s artist...It ti-” “GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS! GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS!” He slowly rolls his eyes at the breaking of his focus by an excited voice from the front of the lecture theatre and as he rotates his waist to take his legs off the desk he looks down at the board. One Of Those Maths Guys : “WE’VE DONE IT! WE’VE PROVEN IT! IT’S NOW OFFICIAL!!! THIS IS THE GREATEST DISCOVERY TO COME OUT OF HUBBARDVILLE SINCE WE DISPROVED THE WHOLE ISLAMIC FAITH ON SATURDAY!!!” Despite the grandiose claims from the Mathematicians, Xavier is slow in acknowledging them and in a lethargic fashion he leans forward onto the desk and rather tiredly gestures with his hand again. Xavier : “......And?” Despite his apparent apathy they all seem excited about their find, pointing up to the scribblings that cover a whole 5 boards before reaching a final formula.... K + E = R .....They all look to him, proudness beaming from their smiling faces and bright eyes. Kinda The Maths Leader Guy : “It’s proven Mr Kannon..... After a solid day of squaring, rooting, multiplying out and substituting in.... It’s fact! KANNON + EXTREME = RATINGS!!!“ The 5 of them high five each other in a rather nerdy way, laughing nasally to salute their triumph and looking with hopeful eyes at the Gold Patron Meritorious.... He who they wish to please.... .....But, typical of Kannon today he just shrugs and puts his feet back up in the desk, reclining back and yawning as the shot fades to Black. Xavier : “Meh....” He lets out a yawn and scratches his arms and legs out to their extremities as we hit Black. “.....Tell me something I don’t know...”
