You people are all nuts!!

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/19/02 -The sight is Wrigley Field in Chicago, IL.home of the lovable losers the Chicago Cubs. In the stands is another lovable loser dressed in all black he is “The Malice Man” Duke Williams. He is signing autographs and taking picture’s in the back of the stands.- Williams: right on hoss!! -A young college looking girl undoes he top and exposes her perfect tits.- Girl: Can you sign this? Williams: Sure thing let me clear a spot off. -Duke gives her boozem a little lick before putting his Hancock on her hot top. She shows it off to the crowd as they cheer her on. Security comes to escort Duke out of the general population and into the back.- Security: Come on Duke lets take you back stage its almost time for the seven inning stretch. Williams: Damn hoss I was just getting into it. Security: Sorry you were causing a disturbance. Williams: Disturbance from what another Cubs loss? -Security escorts Duke into the broadcast booth. Just as he steps in the booth the sky opens up and a massive down pour starts falling on the crowd. The announcers calling the action are Chip Carey and Stone.- Chip: Here comes the rain!! Stone: Look who just walked in! Chip: The man who was going to sing our 7th inning stretch pending this rain delay he is PCW star Duke Williams. Stone: welcome to our rain delay party here in Chicago, Illinois! Duke: Yea thanks killer its good to be here. Stone: A saw you shmoozing with the crowd out there they love you!! Duke: Boys let me ask you what’s not to love about the Malice Man? Stone: HA HA HA good point. Chip: Most people don’t know this but I’m an avid wrestling fan. I heard Pharaoh earlier today talking about your upcoming match on Turmoil saying your time has past and your not in the same league as the new stars of PCW. Williams: HE said all that? Stone: He sure did do you have a retort? Williams: Well I’m not one who’s know to fire back at anybody. Trash talk isn’t my style. -Duke pauses and all three men laugh.-Williams: This kid is nothing more then a tag team competitor trying to make a name for himself. Former TV champion? Big deal. He say’s I can’t compete with the kids of today. The kid smells like urine and he’s worried about my skills. Come on. Chip: Urine? Williams: Yea the guy smells like somebody pissed all over him. He reeks. I gag at the thought of stepping in the ring with pee boy. That and he’s a terrorist! Chip: A terrorist?? Williams: Oh yea come on the writings on the wall. Young, middle eastern. Male. I bet he’s a sleeper cell waiting to strike America!!! He was spotted in Montana. Now ask yourself this, what is a Middle Eastern kid doing in Montana? I bet there a terrorist training camp there. Damn moo-slems and a-rabs. Look I’m not a prejudice guy but really this kid is loonytoons. Keep him away from airplays and explosives. Better yet put him in the ring with an all American like Duke Williams. I’ll take his terrorist ass and beat it in not just to prove I still have it at 42 but also for all of America and the apple pie loving people of the amber waves of grain. Chip: Come on that’s a stereotype isn’t it? Williams: What do you mean? Oh I see we’re on WGN TV and you have to be politically correct. Don’t want to offend anybody or piss off some listeners or more importantly advertisers. Screw that I’m Duke Williams and I can say what I want. Stone: Moving right along what about your other two opponents. Williams: You remember the heavens Gate cult? Chip: Yea those weirdo’s in California who killed themselves thinking they would go on a spaceship. Williams: Yup right on the money. Why do I bring that up? Well its simple these two whack jobs are the two remaining members of that cult. Akiyama thinks he’s a God. Good for him. Him and about half of the forth floor of the Bellevue mental institution Stone: Your saying he’s unbalanced? Williams: Unbalanced? This guy is a see-saw!! The closest he comes to God is the ass of his lady friend. I’m talking a perfect ten and then some. But him no he’s nuts. He is a strong in ring talent though. But you know what so am I hoss. Chip: Chris Chase?? Williams: The messiah? See another f*cking nutball! You do realizes I’m the only sane member of the PCW roster. Chase beat me a little over a week ago. Big deal. The match was meaningless. But you see now I’m on a mission to prove a lot of people wrong. Stone: Do you feel like your fans are turning on your?? Williams: I think my hairy assed hillbillies are a little confused…yes. Stone: So are we going to see the Malice Man of old? Williams: If I have to bite a holy air off and make it truly wholly I will do so. If I have to pop out a terrorist eyes with my bare fingers I will do so. If I have to see if a messiah can really rise up from the dead I will conduct that experiment….Malice Style hoss. Chip: Would you say things are going to get Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound? Williams: Hoss I think that’s the honky-tonk truth. =w=
