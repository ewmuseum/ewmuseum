10 Months On

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: 10 Months On Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 18/11/02 *WHACK!* The sound of a chair shot echoes throughout the arena as it vibrates the eardrums of the Shanghai crowd. *WHACK!* Steel on skull. Oh, how they cheered. The valiant upstart, Xavier Kannon stands face to face with an un-phased Russian Bear while the Chinese crowd call for another shot. “January.... Things were very, very different.” Since the paths of Xavier Kannon and Ivan Stanislav last met a lot has changed. A lot is similar. Much is different. “January was the beginning of the end for Asylum.” Standing between the enraged Communist and the battered, broken Miyagawa is the brave Scientologist with Asylum colours displayed for all to see. For the night Ivan and Xavier last met it was the Hubbard follower that people cheered for. Cheered until their throats felt raw. Cheered each time the chair hit the Russian. Cheered as Stanislav roared and decided Kannon wasn’t worth it. For back then, Kannon wasn’t worth it. “As I said, much has changed.” Ivan left PCW. For a brief moment the fans were grateful for his loss. Little did they know he would front the company that that nearly drove PCW out of business. Little does he know it, but Ivan is one of the men to blame for Kannon. Ivan helped make OSW. OSW helped weaken PCW. When things are weak, men like Xavier Kannon pounce. “Thank you, Ivan. Thank you.” The cheers for Kannon didn’t last long. Jeremy Howard took him under his wing, many would have killed for a chance like that. He was the ‘Golden Child’ of Asylum. His blood was spilt to help his mentor. But he saw his mentor as a master. “Xavier Kannon will never live in anyone’s shadow. Ask anyone... If they’re still around.” Xavier found a new light to follow. A new tune was being played by a new Pied Piper and the Scientologist was quick to follow. That tune was composed in part, by Ivan Stanislav. “I’ll take power over cheers any day of the week.” Nobody thought he would, but using every trick in the book he beat Jeremy Howard. In January Xavier was a new hope for PCW fans. By April he was the embodiment of all they detested. Something else changed along this part of the timeline. PCW. In January it was thriving, prosperous. Then Old School Wrestling happened. “A Vulture never kills it’s own prey.” PCW gates were down. PCW merchandise sales were down. PCW PPV buy-rates were down. PCW was down. A CEO on Trial. A Company in the hands of it’s creditors. An unexpected Alliance. “Cheap at half the price.” And so it begun, Xavier got his ‘Foot in the door’. April’s begining saw him plant the seed that finally flowered at 3rd Dawning. Ivan and OSW had succeeded. Under the bitter reign of Jane Reagan they drove PCW to the edge. To the brink of extinction. But a cornered animal fights fiercest... And with Tyler Nelson and Xavier Kannon ruling the roost, the fierce was equally partnered by greed and corruption. “We both were after something during the summer, Ivan. You wanted your little glitter fairy... All wanted was my own way.” The parallels continued. PCW fans jeered Xavier Kannon just as they had Ivan. Kannon turned on Tyler Nelson just like the Russian Bear had done and was still hated. At Wrestle Vessel, the Scientologist captured the World Title just as the Communist had done. He tucked a PPV Title defence under his belt.... Many ways similar yet poles apart. “And so the paths turn towards one another, and the point where they cross was seen.” Ivan and OSW weakened PCW and Kannon was born. From there he grew and grew, each obstacle was moved or eliminated but the shadow of OSW was still cast over the offices of Primetime. “I don’t need to fear anything, I employ people to do that for me.” A memo was written up. A Fax was sent. Kannon wanted to see who would answer his call. The call reached OSW and just as the chairshots had resonated through the Russian’s skull 10 months ago, the words of the Scientologist vibrated in his ear. “I knew you couldn’t resist.” His route back to PCW was not easy. Before he could get his taste of PCW’s owner, he had to get by OSW’s owner... Jane Reagan. But get by her he did, and on November 24th, Ivan Stanislav will set foot in a PCW arena once again. No Longer the Communist Menace. No longer detested. No longer with his Red Army. “So who do you come looking for first, Ivan?” The PCW he returns to will not be filled with reminders of the past. There won’t be talk of old times. For Ivan isn’t returning to the PCW he knew, he’s entering the 3rd Age. There is a new rule, the Front Office not Management. There are no longer most of the men he fought for his World Title. Can it even be called ‘Returning’? Well, I suppose the letters are still the same. “I’ll take it as a compliment.” Xavier once called himself and Ivan the ‘Alpha Males’. Many young fighters want to target the top guys to get noticed, to get a reputation as a Lionheart... Xavier just wants to get them out of the way, and so far, somehow, he’s done it. The slight frame of the Scientologist has wriggled his way up to the top. His body has been bent, broken and bruised but somehow, he always finds a way... But is there a way past, or through, Ivan Stanislav? “An extra foot. An extra 150 pounds. Another remnant of PCW’s past to be archived and tucked away.” Paths that took the pair far from one another in January converged yesterday. In his PCW office Xavier was confronted by Ivan. Across a wall of security they stared. Ivan’s stern face surely noticing the uncertainty on Xavier’s. Has he finally bitten off more than he can chew? “At Shanghaied I went 1-0 up Ivan, and I’m not in the mood to let you equalise.” Where once the Russian Bear walked away from the newbie, he is now striding towards the embodiment of PCW. It’s no longer rookie v established star. It’s Ex-Champion v Ex-Champion, PCW CEO v OSW Champ, Ego v Bravery. “Still think I’m not worth it, Stanislav?” In the Alliances cages, Ivan will not be walking away this time. Equally, there’ll be no escape for Xavier when he comes face to face with the Russian Bear. “I got where I am by picking my fights carefully.” But the moment Ivan’s foot stepped back into the PCW offices, the fight picked Kannon. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
