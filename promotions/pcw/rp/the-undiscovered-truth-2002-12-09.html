The Undiscovered Truth

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/09/02 The Undiscovered Truth The day was Sunday December 8th, the day I made a pivotal mistake. It seemed like something so simple, but it ended up developing into something gigantic. If I could only go back in time and change my choices I would. It’s too late now, and I have to live in fear. That’s right FEAR! Not a scared fear, but one much greater, a fear that the real me might be discovered. {Misty Shadow} I was sitting on a park bench. I was being quiet and to myself while writing some simple poetry, when a woman in a pair of scrubs sits beside me. The woman was quite attractive and I quickly took notice. Though I had some sinful thoughts, I restraint myself, and kept going on with my business. Then everything went downhill…. AHH! I grabbed my left shoulder in pain, as the feeling generated from it was unbearable. Are you all right? I turned to the woman next me and noticed the concern on her face. As we locked eyes she stood there motionless. I feared an attraction and quickly turned away. It’s just an injury I have from work, it should go away, and all my other injuries eventually go away. You should really get that check out it could be quite serious. Injuries like one to your shoulder are not to be taken lightly. I showed some concern as I wondered if this shoulder could cause a problem come Friday in my big match. I don’t know, usually all these injuries disappear the next morning. Just to make sure, why don’t you follow me back into the hospital, I’ll have a quick x-ray taken without anyone knowing and then you’ll be on your way. We’d be in and out real quick. Come on you can’t pass up that deal. She then slowly graced her hand on my leg and shot up doing all I could do, and that was follow her and give her what she wanted. On the way there she tried to make some small talk, but I had some other stuff blocking up my mind. I was a little worried about this x-ray seeing as I had never gone to a doctor in my life. I had no idea what to expect and wasn’t sure If I needed whatever treatment they’d supply. I finally arrived in the office. It was kind of cold and all around me were people nursing other injuries and with severe problems. The place in a whole made me feel very unwelcoming. The woman had gone to the back to free up the X-ray machine and then called me in. We’re ready. Follow me. I got up and followed her as I looked around and saw people in pain at every room I laid eyes on. It was something I had never really been exposed too. Is there a problem? No, none at all, just was looking around. Yea, well this is a real astonishing place, we’ve won countless award from the board on health for Spain, they say were one of the best hospitals in this entire country. Romantico then looks over at a man bleeding from the head with death stamped on his face. Yea, sure is a great place. She opens a door and I quietly enter to see nothing but darkness and one long chair with a laser light above it. What’s that? That’s what you’ll be taking your x-ray on. It seems kind of…. Are you scared? Trying to seem courageous and as tough as I really am I swallow the truth and put on a more manly showcase. No I just thought It would be more intimidating than a chair. Well you must be a strong man then? She then smiles toward me, and dusts the seat off, telling me it’s ready to be sat on. Here you go, it’s all ready. I walk over and sit down as she adjusts everything that needs to be adjusted. She puts a large robe on me and then tells me to lay back and close my eyes. She then leaves the room. Don’t move this will take just a second. I secretly sit their worried and troubled as to what this will lead to. I then here a click and feel a slight force headed toward me, then just as soon as it began it ends. The woman comes back in the room and takes the robe off of me. That’s it, now I just have to go over the x-rays and I’ll get back to you. Where can I reach you? I then grabs a piece of paper from my wallet and copy down my number. I hand it to her and she sweetly graces my hand and walks back to her office. Have a good day. As do you. I then leave the hospital feeling stupid about how I worried over nothing; meanwhile the woman was in her office reviewing the x-rays. She takes out the sheet and can’t believes what she sees. What she’s looking at it not the normal body structure of a human, but rather something abnormal? Oh my God, who is this man? TO BE CONTINUED……….
