Clay: YOUR WINNERS… SKYLAR GREEN AND JAMES EDWARDS! For the past three hours I had not moved from in front of the screen, just staring at it watching my moments glory over and over again, at the time it had not sunk in properly, it all seemed to be unreal a dream. I remember seeing Titu standing screaming at the referee to make the count after hitting Sky with the jewel of the nile, I knew that I only had one chance as I grabbed his legs and bundled him over for the pin.Hearing the crowd cheer afterwards was phenomenal, the cheering, the reaction as I pinned one of the top heels in the company and someone who is a member of the power stable. All of my previous doubts had been scrapped, no longer was I worried about the ins and outs of wrestling, no longer was I preoccupied with other things on my mind, as I had tasted victory and it was sweet as candy James rose to his feet from the reclining chair, cracking his neck as he stood, although he had come away with the victory he had definitely taken a few good smacks, once again his inexperience showing, too eager and as a result he got smacked about a bit. That was all in the past though and was retrospective, if ifs and ands were pots and pans there would be no need for tinkers vans. Leaning forward he turned off the television set, the images remaining for a second before disappearing to the accompanying sound of crackling static, the last image seen being that of the referee Buzz slamming his hand down on the mat for the third and final time needed to grant James the victory. I didn’t want time to continue, if I could just stop it here I would do, I had won my very first bout and in doing so it had put me on top of the world I cared not what happened now, I would never be remembered as the guy that didn’t win, never won…couldn’t win. I had got rid of one of my demons that had haunted me. Taking a deep breath James looked at the plain white ceiling, a few cracks appearing where damp had began to seep through the plaster, he knew that he had to move sooner or later, or at least pay for repairs, the way things were going at any moment the entire ceiling could fall down on him and that would not help his career one bit. Walking slowly across the room, his bare feet sinking into the lush green carpet as he did so James approached a small calendar hanging on the wall, tracing his finger along he stopped on the 9th of May 2003 and tapped his finger lightly on the date for a few moments as though thinking deeply about something. James reached deep inside the pocket of his black denim jeans and pulled out a small bic biro and started scribbling frantically in the space adjacent to the 9th of May. Smiling to himself James nodded briefly before turning one hundred and eighty degrees and leaving the room through an old green door. The room fell silent, except for a quiet buzzing of a fruit fly, which had been there all along, it swept gracefully across the room from the very top of the wooden bookshelf in the far corner of the room, which contained numerous historical texts and landed neatly next to the character and the date the 9th of May. Next to the date were three small bullet points obviously previously written by James before he had left the room the notes were short and sweet. Go food shopping Visit laundrette Kick Mark Makonie, SyN’ and Stone Burns’ asses
