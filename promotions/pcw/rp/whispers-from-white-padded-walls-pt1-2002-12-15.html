Whispers from white padded walls: Pt1

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/12/02 'I received word from a little bird today' Jeremiah croaked out in his usual murky screeching voice. 'Oh really?' Gideon answered without looking up from the book which lay on his lap as he sat there on a small wooden chair, his feet resting comfortable on the small table opposite him. The candlelight in the small dingy room in which he and Jeremiah were currently subsiding in didn't provide the most suitable light for any condition, but it Gideon made due with it. 'You remember out friend Jacob?' 'Refresh my memory if you will' 'Come Gideon, you know full well who I mean, just as much as you know this new dictatorship of RUSH at the hands of...' 'DON'T!......mention that worthless Scientologist's name to me' 'Very well, but I will mention Jacob's name again.' 'There are many Jacob's in this world Jeremiah, many who I have et and many more who I am likely to meet providing I choose to live that long.' 'But none like Jacob Riley' A broad silence fell between the two, Gideon knew all along of the Jeremiah he spoke of, yet tried to put off the topic of him being confronted. He was clearly unsuccessful in doing so. 'What of him' Gideon snapped the book shut and set his feet down off the table and placed the book there, and turned the chair to face Jeremiah. 'I know you remembered him' croaked The Elderly Evil through a sly grin. 'This little bird of mine informed me that the workers at the asylum have taken a new keener interest to his condition' 'Is that so?' 'Yes, yes it is. Apparently his condition has grown extremely worse.' 'Shame' 'In many ways yes. Nevertheless, the fact is his condition has changed.... but his story of you hasn't.' Gideon looked down into the eyes of Jeremiah sharply. 'Not a single solitary word has morphed or changed. He still cries your name from the dark corners of the room he resides in night after night. Yelling your name, warning others of your name. Praying to the good Lord in heaven that he never has to set eyes upon you again.' 'What has this got to do with me?!' Gideon was obviously growing hostile. He wanted an immediate answer, no riddles or stalling, just a straight up ultimatum. Jeremiah, however, loved watching Gideon grow tense. It was a reassuring reminder that he was in control of Gideon. 'Well....... the resident workers at the asylum have grown more and more worried at this story. They have informed the proper authorities of it. They agreed to look further into it. A new detective has taken it upon himself to reopen the case, and look into it. Detective Martin Bauerman I believe, young and fresh, looking to impress the higher officials of his profession. He wishes to speak with Jacob in the next few days, hear his story. If his story remains the same, in which it does, he will no doubt come looking for the myth of Gideon Poe.' 'You seem to forget your name was mentioned quite independently in that myth as you call it of Jacobs' 'Oh I didn't forget my dear Gideon, and believe me this is not a schoolyard taddle tale I'm uttering. Think of it as more of an objective.' 'You want me to silence Riley?' 'Oh Gideon, I wouldn't put it like that. I don't want the man to inhale an other inkling of oxygen into his lungs from this earth.' Gideon chuckled sarcastically, seemingly un amused. 'That's your answer to everything isn't it? Death! Murder them!' 'To put it simply, yes!' 'Well answer me this Jeremiah............. when are you to free me from this burdened life I live by granting me that blessing in disguise?' 'Oh dear boy...... I'm never that generous?' Jeremiah then smiled wickedly as Gideon ran his hand through is long black hair. He was aware of his objective. Both he and Jeremiah were facing being found out for all their evil deeds. Yet to Gideon, that itself as well, was a blessing in disguise.
