My night in jail....the true story.

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/9/02 Duke Williams sits in a leather chair smoking a big punch cigar in his Montana mansion deep in Gods country. He stares into the camera with an angry look on his face. In his hand is a glass of water and a cherry fruit pie. He begins to speak.- Williams: You think with all the good I’ve done for PCW, that karma would be on my side. I have, with the help of the other members of the Front Office saved PCW. I’m what this world needs a true good guy. I’m sorry the fans and my co-works can’t identify that with power comes responsibility and tough decisions. But people that’s not what I’m here to talk about. -Duke takes a drag on his cigar as he shakes his head.- Williams: You see Sunday night I was visiting some friends in Starkville, California. It was late and my posse had flown the coup. I was mad chilling in my super eight crib when I started feeling exceptional happy. NOW MY FRIENDS you KNOW I WOULD NOT LIE TO YOU!!! I could not lie to you. So you have to believe me that my feeling of happiness was NOT brought on by any illicit activities, herbs or pills. -Duke shakes his head no.- Williams: I was HIGH on Hubbard!!! Feeling good about life so I did what any man of joy would do and that was exit my hotel room and skip through the streets. -Duke smiles.- Williams: I was in such a good mood I was skipping along and picking flowers!! I had the world on a string!! Nothing could stop me. Except them nasty boys in blue who like picking on longhaired hillbillies. (The camera cuts to a police officer in uniform sitting at his desk with a map of Starkville, CA behind him on the wall.) Cop: My name is Deputy Jim Cox and I was on call Sunday for the Williams arrest. First off let me say that the perp was staying in a hotel know for heavy drug activity and prostitution. Now please understand that Starkville is a very peaceful community of mainly retired folks. We run a top-notch organization that is very fair. Now here in Starkville we do have a curfew of 1am unless you have a porpoise to be on the streets. You can’t just roam, its against the law at that time of night. We received a call at about 2am stating a large man wearing jeans, no shirt, and suspenders was skipping through the streets picking flowers off of private property. We received the call and my self and officer Petty responded. -The camera cuts back to Duke Williams who is siting in his chair smoking the cigar.- Williams: Now my friends I had a big smile on my face and a daisy in my ear as I was spreading peace through the lowly town of Starkville. They were being blessed by the Malice Man. (The Camera cuts to an old lady who is sitting in here lime green kitchen drinking a cup of coffee.) Lady: My name is Betty Wayne and I live on Pine street here in Starkville. It was Sunday night and my husband Harold and I were sound asleep. We like to watch the Murder She Wrote then go to bed at about eight. I was awaken at about 1:30am by a devilish sound coming from my front lawn. I rushed to my curtains and I saw a massive sized man urinating in my garden. He already picked about half of my prize-winning wild flowers. He finished up his business and skipped away holding a hand full of my flowers. I immediately called the police. -The shoot comes back to the Malice Man in Missoula.- Williams: So here I am having done nothing wrong skipping down the road when who shows up??? John Q. Law and a posse of about thirty-five cops with Billy clubs and mace in hand. They wanted to beat me down for something I didn’t know what for. Every time I tried to ask what I did wrong I was told to shut up and threatened with the club. One of the cops took the flower out of my ear and squashed it with his evil boot. I tired to tell them who I was but they just treated me like some crazed hillbilly hippie. Like I was some kind of wrestling fan and NOT the PRESIDENT of PCW. This rubbish was unwarranted. (The camera cuts back to the cop at his desk.) Cop: One other Deputy and Myself responded to the call and found are selves looking at a very large man.. He was belligerent and irate and looped up on something. We calmly talked Mr. Williams down and had him sit on the curb. He throw a flower from his ear at my partner so I cuffed him. We then calmed him down and talked. He agreed that if we let him go he would return straight to him hotel room and there would be no more problems. We shook on it I released Mr. Williams for the restraints. He began to walk away. My partner and myself sat in our squad car and started to write up the paper work on the incident. Some afterwards we heard laughing coming from the back of our car. -We rejoin Duke for his side.- Williams: So one pig hit me upside the head with the baton and I got angry. I got on my cell phone and called my lawyer. The police got scared so they let me go. I’ll get a Scientologist lawyer on them and there will be nothing they can do. They would be marked men with no hope like Miyagawa!! So they backed off and let me go. They all pulled away. There were so many of them it looked like a parade. I can’t blame them for being afraid of the Malice Man. -Duke flexes his aging muscles.- Williams: At this point I went from happy to pissed. So I took my silly string out of my back pocket that I had left over from the party earlier that night and wrote a message on the back of the police car. They heard me and arrested me. (We rejoin the police officer.) Cop: Mr. Williams had written a message on the back of our car. We had enough and arrested him. We emptied his pockets which had sleeping pills among other things in it and throw him in a cell for the night. -Back to Duke.- Williams: I see they can’t take a joke as they put me in a cell for the night. With out my pills I couldn’t sleep so I yelled all night for them. They were my heart pills I could have died. I threw my boots, screamed, and raged and nobody came. They are not very nice people that’s for sure. I’m glad I treat those under me with respect in PCW. You think the world would return that favor. They won’t even let me tell them that I’m Duke Williams. IT was bullshit. (The cop again) Cop: The next morning we let him go on bail and gave him back a guitar pick and some flowers that were in his pocket. He gave us a dirty look as he threw the flower in the trash can. -Duke rejoins us.- Williams: The next morning they let me out because I was innocent and they knew it. Then they insulted me by handing me MY flowers back with a smirk. I’m never going back to Starkville again. I’m calling Kannon to get me some lawyers so we can buy this town and turn it into a shopping mall. -Duke gives the camera the finger as it fades.- =w= Based on the song Starkville City Jail by Johnny Cash Well, I left my motel room, down at the Starkville Motel,The town had gone to sleep and I was feelin' fairly well.I strolled along the sidewalk 'neath the sweet magnolia trees; I was whistlin', pickin' flowers, swayin' in the southern breeze.I found myself surrounded; one policeman said: 'That's him.Come along, wild flower child. Don't you know that it's two a.m.' They're bound to get you.'Cause they got a curfew.And you go to the Starkville City jail. Well, they threw me in the car and started driving into town;I said: 'What the hell did I do?' He said: 'Shut up and sit down.'Well, they emptied out my pockets, took my pills and guitar picks.I said: 'Wait, my name is...' 'Awe shut up.' Well, I sure was in a fix.The sergeant put me in a cell, then he went home for the night;I said: 'Come back here, you so and so; I ain't bein' treated right.'Well, they're bound to get you, cause they got a curfew,And you go to the Starkville City Jail. I started pacin' back and forth, and now and then I'd yell,And kick my forty dollar shoes against the steel floor of my cell.I'd walk awhile and kick awhile, and all night nobody came.Then I sadly remembered that they didn't even take my name.At 8 a.m. they let me out. I said: 'Gimme them things of mine!'They gave me a sneer and a guitar pick, and a yellow dandelion.They're bound to get you, 'cause they got a curfew,And you go to the Starkville City Jail.
