Last minute pep talk

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/31/02 Scene shows LeBlanc backstage. It's moments before he jerks the curtain to kick off Fusion... it's time for LeBlanc to regain his Extreme Title from Jason Core. 'OG' Orpheus Grant stands alongside the Swamp Dawg. They are surrounded by an entourage of armed prison officials. Warden Riley stands near the curtain and looks at his watch. Warden: Come on, let's go... what's the damn hold up? Stage hand: You guys are on in two minutes Mr. Riley... stand by. Warden: Yeah, yeah, yeah... I've been standing by for over 24 hours... when's Fusion gonna start? Stage hand: Two minutes, sir. Please, be patient with us here. Warden: Why don't you go back there and tell that big, black SOB with the handcuffs on to be patient. With any luck, he'll rip your head off and THEN we can get out to the ring. Stage hand: Well, Mr. LeBlanc's gonna have to be patient as well. Warden (sarcastically): Mr. LeBlanc is not capable of being patient. He has only one thing on his mind right now, and that's to tear Jason Core in half. Maim the sonofabitch and end his f*cking career. That broke dick bastard doesn't deserve to wear Swamp's Extreme title belt. Core is too soft... too much of a f*cking pussy for the hardcore division. Stage hand: Well, I sure as hell wouldn't ever step in the ring with LeBlanc. Meanwhile, towards the back of the room, OG continues to pump up LeBlanc. OG: Come on nigga! Git hyped biatch! This is yo time man... Yo time ta git dat mutha f*ckin belt back. Knowin' you da biggest, baddest and blackest SOB in da whole f*ckin PCW. Sheeeought, nigga... you can't be lettin' some playa hatin' hairy ass pussy like Jason Core carry dat belt round. LeBlanc hops up and down, swiveling his head, loosening his neck and shoulders. He nods after every word OG says. OG: Put diss mutha f*cka on ice tonite Swamp... send Core back in a goddam pine box. You extreme brutha... you da f*ckin head nigga in charge in diss hood, yo. Now git yo black ass out ta dat ring and f*ck diss mutha f*cka up!!! The Swamp Dawg pounds his fist together, grits his together and lets out a ferocious growl. Scene fades to black as the curtain opens and the heads towards the ring.
