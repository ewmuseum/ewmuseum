Gone Surfing

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th of Dec, 02 Chet King is a man who has always had the philosophy that to outwit and defeat your opponent, you must be able to think like your opponent. That’s why we open up in a scene most people probably never would of though they’d see Chet; surfing at a beach. The reason he’s surfing, simple, to hang out with people with the same mind set as Chet’s Turmoil opponent, Totally Tubular Tommy. Chet’s currently out the back of the surf, on a 6”1 board, the same height as Chet, trying to catch waves, but failing miserably. Chet turns to one of the surfers out the back with him, and tries to start a conversation with him to try to get into the same state of mind as TTT. Chet King: You surf often? Surfer: Yo dude, I’m out here, catching some gnarly waves twelve hours a day. Chet King: I take it you like the beach then? Surfer: It’s bodacious dude! Chet King: You good at surfing? Surfer: I’m like totally awesome. Chet King: How long did it take you to become so good? Surfer: I don’t know dude, I think I’ve just always been radical. Chet King: Ok, fair enough. Do you reckon that you could do be a favor? Surfer: What’s that dude? Chet King: I was wondering if I could hang out with you for the day. You see, I’m a talk show host and wrestler, maybe you’ve seen me, and I’ve got a match this Tuesday night with some guy who’s attitude towards life is a lot like yours, and I’ve always felt that to beat some one you need to be able to think like them? The surfer seems to have just tuned out as he stares out into the ocean. Chet King: Is that cool? Surfer: Sorry, is what cool, dude? Chet King: What I just asked you. Surfer: You asked me something? Whoa dude! I never even, like, heard you. Chet King: I was just wondering if I could hang out with you for the day. Surfer: Sure dude, you lookin’ to get some surfin’ tips of me? Chet King: Err, yeah, that’s it. Surfer: Gnarly. What you no about surfin’? Chet King: Not much, this is actually the first time I’ve been surfing in about fifteen years. The surfer looks shocked about Chet’s last statement. Surfer: Whoa dude! Where you been living? I think you’ve got some serious issues bro, I mean dudes who don’t surf are just weird. Chet King: I’d like to have had the opportunity to do some surfing, but I’ve just been too busy with work. Now the surfer looks really shocked. Surfer: Whoa! You work? Your crazy man! Why would you conform to society and work when you can be a radical awesome dude who surfs all day, and chills all night? Chet is about to say something, but stops himself. Surfer: Dude, look a good set is coming in, it’s time to catch some gnarly waves and do some bodacious moves. Party time! Chet looks bemused at the onset of enthusiasm in the surfer as the set of waves comes as all the surfers catch them. The surfer whom Chet is hanging out with, paddles furiously, allowing him to catch a wave which he goes up onto the lip of and gets air off it before coming back down it. Chet tries to follow suit, and paddles hard and fast in the attempt to catch a wave, however when Chet gets on it he tries to stand up, but fails miserably as he slips, and Chet along with his board get sucked under the wave and sent to shore via the white wash. Once on shore Chet starts to mumble to himself. Chet King: This is just great. First I get Dante Jackson on my show and he insults me for no reason at all, and now I’ve come surfing to try and get into the mind set of Totally Tubular Tommy, and I end up getting beaten up by the ocean. This has been a great last couple of days, just great. Chet picks himself, and his board up, and continues to mumble to himself. Chet King: I’ve got two freaking matches this week, one against TTT and a four man tag match which will include Dante Jackson in it, I can’t allow myself to get beaten up by the ocean before I even step foot inside the ring. Chet then sees the surfer he was talking to out the back coming into shore. Surfer: Dude, you got like totally wiped out! (Chet doesn’t seem at all pleased.) Chet King: Don’t remind me. Surfer: Don’t worry about dude, these things happen to newbies like you. Chet King: Yeah, well I don’t think I’m going to go back out there, I’ve got to keep myself injury free for work this week. Surfer: You think about work to much, dude. You need to lay back and chill. Chet King: Well I’d be up for doing that, if that’s what your going to be doing, as I need to get in TTT’s mind set. The surfer seems to have been distracted, and not heard Chet King, as he appears to be checking out the bottom of a good looking twenty year old woman, in a yellow bikini. Surfer: That’s one awesome ass! Chet notices the woman as well, and as most men, and some women, would do in Chet’s position, takes a look. Chet King: You’re right, that is one awesome ass! Surfer: So what you up to for the rest of the day? Chet King: I was planning on hanging out with you, remember? Surfer: Oh yeah, that’s right dude, awesome. What’d you say your name was? Chet King: It’s Chet King. Surfer: Cool, my name’s Derek. Chet shakes the hand of Derek. Chet King: Good to meet you Derek. Derek: Thanks dude, good to meet you as well. Chet King: So are you going back out for a surf? Derek: Nah, I think I might head in for a while. I’m going to go back to the surf club and chill with a few friends of mine. We’ll probably smoke a bit of green stuff. Chet King: You’re going to smoke weed? Derek: Of course dude. It helps you chill. You want to join us? Chet King thinks to himself for a moment, as he isn’t a drug user, yet he does want to get in the mind of TTT. Derek: So you want to come dude? Chet King: If anything is going to help me get into TTT’s mind, then I guess smoking weed should do the trick. I’ll join you! Derek smiles at the news. Derek: Awesome! This should be so bodacious and radical and gnarly and cool! This will be far out! Let’s go dude! Derek leads the way to surf club, as Chet King follows with a confused look on his face as he didn’t understand what Derek just said.
