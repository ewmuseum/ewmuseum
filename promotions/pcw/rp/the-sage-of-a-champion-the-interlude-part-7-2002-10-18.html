The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 7

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-18-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 7” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 2: Metal Armor? :: The scene we last left off was a very emotional one. BladeD’s mother was shedding tears because she doesn’t want her boy going on this mission knowing the fact that BladeD may not come back. BladeD on the other hand was very clam and collected. He is ready for the task on hand. :: BladeD: Look mom. I have to do this. Trust me. I have all the experience in the world. If I wasn’t as good as I am, then they would have someone much older than I am with more experience than me doing this job. BladeD’s Mother: That is what I don’t understand. I would like that there are plenty of people with more experience that would be better at going into this mission then my 14-year-old son. Commander Fuller: Well what you have to understand is that sometimes isn’t how much experience that you have had that counts. It is the quality of the experience you have that counts. The quality of your son’s experience exceeds people with five or ten years more of experience that other people have. You can have ten years of experience, but not be as good as someone that has only had five years of experience. Do you understand where I am coming from on that statement? BladeD’s Mother: Yes, I do sir. Commander Fuller: I am glad you do and I hope you realize that… not in a mean way or anything, but you have to let your son do this or force will have to be applied. Basically, you can’t say no. BladeD’s Mother: That isn’t right that I can’t say no, but if BladeD wants to do it, then he can do it. If he doesn’t want to do it, then you can’t make him do it. BladeD: I want to do it mom. Look, I know what I am doing out in field. Commander Fuller: Here BladeD. Take this. :: Commander Fuller hands BladeD a map of the area he is going to be in. :::: BladeD begins to look over the map very carefully. :: BladeD: Someone please give me a pen or pencil. :: BladeD is handed a pen and begins to circle places on the map. :: Mr. President: Wow BladeD. Are you drawing something? BladeD: Hah aha. No, actually I am marking places on this map. Places that I am going to be covering and whatnot. I don’t know this area of the world at all, but I do know places that I am going to be going on, so I want to have them circled. Most of my mission is going to be nighttime, so I am going to need some kind of key chain flashlight or something, so I can see the map at night. Mr. President: Why would you say most of your mission is going to be at night? No one has said most of your mission is going to be at night. BladeD: Because, you would have to be a crazy fool to do something of this sort during the daytime. I wouldn’t last two seconds in the daytime. In the daytime is when everyone is up and about. At night is when only the security guards and people of that sort are around. Commander Fuller: That right there is a prime example of BladeD intelligence. Most people would complain about doing things at night because they can’t see or some reason like that, when BladeD already knows that it is best to do things at night when less people are around. BladeD: I hate doing things during the daytime anyway. It just feels weird trying to crawl around places and different things doing that spy type of thing in the daytime. I am just… uncomfortable for some reason. I don’t know. Like I said about the map, I will need a flashlight because I am going to be looking at that map constantly. Commander Fuller: Once you get into the MAC building I am going to need you to try and find a map of it. I am not sure if there are going to be any because I doubt there are, but if you find any place of engineering, like a place in the back in authorized areas, maybe you can find a blue-print of the building somewhere. That map right there is the only map I have available to you. BladeD: It is ok, even if I don’t find a map in the building I am will be able to find my way around. It just might take me a little bit longer. Commander Fuller: But remember that time is of the assents, so if you can find a blue-print or any map of any kind, it would save you time… time that we may need for something else before Metal Armor goes off. BladeD: I got one question for you though.Commander Fuller: What is that? BladeD: Am I going to have to fight Metal Armor in anyway? Commander Fuller: Well…. :: pause :: Yes in a way you might have to, but I hope it doesn’t come that far. You need to some how deactivate the machine. Once Metal Armor is turned on the person in the letter said there is no possible way to turn it off other than destroying it, but I doubt that seriously, so you may have to fight it while finding a way to deactivate it, but let’s hope that you don’t have to come that far and you can deactivate before it even launches. BladeD: I will try my best. Is there going to be anyone else working for me? Like a sidekick or something? Commander Fuller: No, BladeD. You are going to be strictly on your own in this mission. Well, you aren’t going to be completely alone because you are going to have a radio that you are going to need to keep attached to you at all times. With this radio I can talk to at anytime and you can talk to me at anytime. That way if you get into a problem or something I can help you get out of it, or give advice. I am going to be with you every step of the way. BladeD: Ok, that will be good. BladeD’s Mother: Will I be able to stay here at times and listen to the radio so I can talk to my son? Commander Fuller: If it makes you feel any better, then during anytime of the day you can come to office whether I am busy or not and you may sit down for however long you want. It will not bother me at all. BladeD’s Mother: Thank you, Commander. Mr. President: I got a question for you BladeD and/or Commander. If BladeD is only going to work during the nighttime and this mission is going to take more than any day, then what is he going to do during the daytime hours? BladeD: I am going to try and find somewhere to sleep. Commander Fuller: That is why he has the ability to talk to me because when he needs to sleep he can keep the radio on just in case someone tries to sneak up on him or something. I can hear stuff in the background in case he needs to wake up and get out of there, so he doesn’t get caught. Mr. President: Ok, that makes sense. :: Well, that is going to end it for now… Looks like BladeD is almost ready to head out and face Metal Armor, but the only problem is that the letter is still unfinished. I wonder if they are going to even finish reading the letter, because I would at least like them too. Don’t know about you guys though. Just stay tuned and maybe you will find out. ::
