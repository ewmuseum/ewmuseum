It's Time For A Change

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/03 There comes a time in everyone’s life when a change is needed. It could be a change in physical appearance, a change in personality, or just a change in performance, but no matter what everyone goes through changes in their life. In my life the past month I had been dealing with the change of not having Chris around as well as the change of having my mother living the same house as me once more, and the change that being a Green brought. The only problem was that all of these changes were changes I’d rather live without. I wanted Chris back, my mother and I did not get along at all, and most of all I hated being married to Gaz Green. So today would be a chance to make a change that I would finally enjoy. I was going to put away my sorrow over losing Chris and for once actually help another person fight their sorrow. That person would be the child who I had adopted four months back. For the past month I had been so selfish, facing only my own misery with the disappearance of Chris, along with the annoyance of my mother and Gaz, and forgetting that this kid was only four years old and must be suffering from much more misery than me considering that one of his parents was dead and the other was locked in jail because of the murder of wife. Evan probably feared the same would happen to me each and every time I mentioned how Gaz and I did not get along. Inside his mind he probably feared the moment he would walk to the living room and see me lying on the floor dead as a result of the rivalry between Gaz and me. I wished that I could show Evan that Gaz quite frankly wasn’t the type capable of murder, but how was I supposed to do that if I never wanted to introduce Evan to the Prince of Pranks? A little conversation was the easiest and only solution I could think of, so if that is what it would take to make me a better adoptive parent as well as solve the solution to Evan’s fears, then so be it. “Evan are you awake?” I questioned as I banged my fist softly against his bedroom door which was shut to obviously give him some privacy. “Hold on I’m getting dressed!” The four year old shouted I response as I heard the scampering of his feet across the carpet perhaps to take cover just incase I walked in. “Okay but hurry up, I’ve got a surprise planned for you today.” I said, hoping that this would stir up enough excitement in him so that he would hurry up so that we could get the show on the road. “A surprise?” He question with a large amount of excitement in his voice. “I’m all ready let’s go!” He continued on as he emerged from behind the bedroom door and ran to my side as if he were going to guide me down the stairs to the car. “Slow down kiddo!” I shouted as he cut in front of me and ran down the steps and through the front door which sat just a few feet away from the stairs but before he could hear me the front door was shut and he was out the door. “Where do you think you are going?” My mother cut in before I could catch up to Evan and exit out the front door. “I’m going out to treat Evan to some ice cream.” I responded hoping that she would not start to stir up chaos over such a little thing. “Ice cream this early in the morning? Tessa you are spoiling that child.” My mother retorted in disgust obviously just wanting to tear my parenting skills apart in any way she could. “Mom give me a break, I’m almost never around as it is. It’s the least I can do the poor kid.” I replied as I grabbed my light pink spring coat from the coat closet and headed towards the door before my mother managed to squeeze out another one of her thoughts before I left. “Then maybe you should leave that pathetic career of yours and become a better parent considering I’d never want to have you as a parent if I were that poor kid.” Her voice hissed as her blue eyes glowed with hatred directed towards me. I almost chocked in amusements as these words stumbled from her mouth. If there was anyone who should be considered a bad parent it was her. All she ever did was take cheap shots at me, her only daughter, simply because she didn’t like the path in life I chose. “Excuse me but when did you become mother of the year? Quite frankly if there was anybody who knew how to be a bad mother it’s you!” I shouted back sick of her smart ass replies as I slammed the door shut behind me and made my way to the car where Evan was waiting in the backseat of the car with his seatbelt already fastened. “What took you so long?” He questioned as his four year old curiosity overcame him. “Nothing, just told my mother where we were going.” I said as I climbed into the driver’s seat of the car and shut the door shut as the engine roared to life. “Tessa why do you and your mom hate each other?” He popped yet another question that had really hit me like a dagger to the heart. I hated for Evan to see my mother and I fighting since I did not want him to think that all families had such hatred abiding inside them although that was all he ever seemed to see when I was around. My hatred of my mother, my hatred of Gaz, he probably thought I hated him as well although that was far from the truth. “Because she doesn’t think I make a good parent for you.” I said to put it as simple as I could for the child whom I adopted four months back. “Is it because you don’t get spend a lot of time with me?” He questioned in a softer voice to show that he was reluctant to ask such a question but still wanted to know the answer. “Yes Evan.” I replied “But don’t you worry that is all going to change trust me. I want to defeat Sivasian Krikorian this week on Turmoil and I want to successfully team with Gaz and Skylar so that we can get the win, but you know want I want to do even more? I want to spend time with you and I promise I will.” I replied hoping that it would get the child’s mind off the arguments between my mother and I, and surely enough it did but my only hopes were that I could live up to my promise and not let Evan down, the way I felt I let Chris down back at Spring Fling.
