The Flaymin Aymin Show: Part 2

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, The Flaymin Aymin Show was in full swing.Flaymin, playing the role of a talk show host, was in the midst of sorting out the difficulties that had arisen between his valet Heather and the new addition to Flaymin’s Crew- El Diablo’s wife, Angela. [The scene picks up where we last left off, with Flaymin standing in the centre of his lounge room watching Heather and Angela argue. Suddenly, Flaymin puts his hand to his ear and nods his head accordingly] Flaymin: Ladies, ladies, I’ve just been told by the director that we’re going on the air again- so get ready. [The two women stare at Flaymin and momentarily wonder what could possess them to squabble over such an idiot….. Slowly, their eyes wander down to his crotch and they begin to grin] Flaymin: Ok ladies, we’re back on the air!!Ahem, now Angela what is it you wanted to get off your chest…. besides those heaving…. voluptuous…. juicy…. boo- whoops! Got a little of track there, didn’t I!?! Angela: Are you going to let me speak now? Flaymin: Speak! Speak Speak!That’s all you’ve been yacking about since you got here.What does this place look like…. A Speaking Farm? [The room grows eerily silent] Flaymin: Ok bad example…. You…. can speak….. now. Angela: Good. [Angela turns to face Heather] Angela: Now, I don’t know what your problem is with me but I just want to work this out. Heather: So do I. Angela: Good, so where do we start? Heather: I don’t know.All I know is that all this bickering and arguing has to end or this whole alliance is doomed. Angela: You’re right.Lets shake on it and put everything between us totally into the past. Heather: Agreed. [Angela extends her hand and shakes Heather’s hand.They smile momentarily as they have reached an acceptable truce] Flaymin: Gee ladies, this all looks fine and dandy, but you’re forgetting about one very important issue. Heather: And that would be? Flaymin: See that?You’ve totally locked it out of your conscience, pretending as if it doesn’t exist. [Heather turns to Angela in confusion.Meanwhile, Angela just shrugs her shoulders] Flaymin: Well ladies, allow me to jolt your memories as this mystery thing is revealed NOW!!! [Flaymin runs over and turns on the radio again so that the corny jazz music can start playing.As the music begins to blare out of the speakers, Flaymin walks over to the door and opens it. A second later, Fernando the Zebra and his care-taker Pablo, walk into the room.Pablo sits on the chair besides Angela while Fernando the Zebra remains standing up. Flaymin walks back over to the radio and turns off the crappy jazz music] Flaymin: Now ladies, is there something you would like to say to the Zebra?Because I’ve been hearing lately that his feelings have been hurt and it’s as if he’s not a part of the team.Any thoughts on that? Angela: You’re an idiot. Heather: I second that. Flaymin: Look, you can be as childish as you want, but… you now…have to be… mature and…. OH JUST QUIT BEING BITCHES AND ANSWER MY QUESTIONS!! Angela: How do you expect us to talk to a zebra? Flaymin: Hello!Earth to Angela!The boy sitting next to you is a Zebra Talker Guy! Heather: You mean like a horse whisperer. Flaymin: Same shit, different smell. [The two women turn to look at Pablo and then at Fernando] Heather: Ok, I got a question. Flaymin: Go right ahead. Heather: Zebra’s are from Africa. Flaymin: According to National Geographic, yes.But that is yet to be confirmed. Heather: Well what I want to know is why you have a little latino kid taking care of a Zebra who’s been named after an ABBA song? Flaymin: ABBA’s music transcended music forever.Their terrific music will be remembered for a lifetime…. Like MC Hammer…. Or even Milli Vanilli.So don’t you go blaming it on the rain! Angela: WHAT THE F*CK!?!?! Flaymin: Hush, you.Pablo, is there anything that Fernando would like to say? [Pablo leans over to Fernando and begins to whisper into his ear.In response, Fernando the Zebra nods his head and lets out a little neigh] Pablo: He said that he doesn’t like these two women. Flaymin: See that ladies, you’ve scarred poor Fernando for life. Pablo: He also said that he loves ABBA’s music. Flaymin: That’s right- I’m sure Fernando can definitely hear the drums now. Angela: Alright, alright!This is becoming ridiculous. How are we supposed to believe that this little kid can speak to zebra’s? [Flaymin sighs and then shakes his head] Flaymin: Haven’t you seen the Jungle Book?Little Mowgli used to speak to Baloo the Bear all the time.Children and animals share a bond. Heather: You mean besides the fact that they shit all over the place? Flaymin: Well now your just being insensitive to poor Fernando. [Fernando gets up and heads for the door.Pablo also gets up and follows the zebra out of the room, scowling at Heather and Angela on his way out] Flaymin: Have you no compassion in your hearts?For all I know, Fernando and Pablo might not want to speak to either one of you ever again. Heather: Eh, fine by me. Angela: Yep, me too.Lets get some lunch. Heather: Sure. [Heather and Angela leave the room together, leaving Flaymin alone in his lounge room] Flaymin: Well I guess there’s no point in explaining ‘Flaymin’s final thought’ now…. [Scene fades to black]
