Date: Are you on the list? Spotlights dance rhythmically about upon the dusky strata, weaving between luminescent stars trapped within its endlessness. Follow the lights to find the locale they mark, down below, where the colossal theatre of sanctioned brutality glows. The arena boasts a full house, and the frantic hordes of automobiles and civilians jamming into the parking lot would vouch for that. The evening promises to be spectacular. Box office suffered the wrath of fanfare, the tickets ripped from the reels. PCW v. OSW? If there ever was a night to blow a weeks pay on admission for four, it would be tonight… “WHAT IN THA’ BLUE HELL DO YA’LL MEAN MAH TICKET IS INVALUD?!” Unwavering an inch, the scrawny ticket taker at Gate 1 looks the tobacco chewing roughneck in the eyes. Ticket Taker: “I mean, ‘Sir, please step aside so that the line behind you may get through.’” Disgruntled, the ruffian takes one look at the long line of pale Euro-Trash behind him, rolling their eyes, and puffing on cigarettes. The gloves come off, the chewing goo gets spat, and the Austin 3:16 hat is promptly thrust to the ground. Average Wrestling Fan 3:16: “DA TARNATION?! I PAYED A MONTH’S SALARY TA’SEE P-C-DUYEAH ACTION! YOU BEST BE LEMME’N IN!” Ticket Taker: “I’d love to, buuut…” Smugly, the gatekeeper strokes the velvet rope the sets him apart from the mullet crowned hick. THAT IS, UNTIL HE IS CAUGHT BY THE COLAR IN THE JAWS OF CONFEDERATE JUSTICE!!! Average Wrestling Fan 3:16: “AH SAID LEMME IN!!” Average Wrestling Fan 3:16: “AH SAID PUT ME DOWN, DAGGONE IT!!!” …He squeals, as two brawny bruisers, who are unshaken by his kicking and flailing haul him off. Back at the gate, the ticket taker has unlatched the velvet rope, to allow the Euro-Trash to shuffle in. PCW v. OSW? No. OSW. OSW. OSW. If you’ve come for PCW… You’ve come to the wrong show.
