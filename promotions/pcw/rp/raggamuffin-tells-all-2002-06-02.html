Raggamuffin tells all

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 2 [The scene opens on a pair of dark blue Vans skateboarding shoes which are standing on a moving skateboard. The feet that control the board are doing switches every few seconds at a high rate of speed, which shows this man has an idea of what he's doing. The camera zooms out to show a verk dark skinned young man wearing a red 'Spank My Monkey' T-shirt and tan cargo pants. His slicked back black hair shines in the bright afternoon sunlight, showing off his true Italian roots. The man named Randy Raggagu, professional skateboarder, skates through a local office complex called Foster Plaza. The place is filled with large stair sets, picnic tables, and bitchin' docking platforms for crazy skaters to nail their new 360 flip tricks. Randy nails an ollie on a sidewalk just outside a building that says 'Alcomp' and skates with a good bit of speed to a stairset of about six steps and nails a 180 ollie to the street below. He steps off the board and pops it up to his hand and looks up to the sky with a satisified smile. The man named Randy Raggagu looks to the camera and smiles] Randy Raggagu: There is nothing like a great skate session to start off your day. Lets face it, I am one of the best in the world. Tony Hawk? Pft, I taught that punk everything that he knows. Rodney Mullen? I'm the one who came up with all those tricks that the new school skaters use, its just that I didn't want to make it seem like I was some sort of God. Even though I am... [Randy holds his 'Rhino' skateboard by the Destructo trucks and begins walking through one of the many parking lots in the office complex. The very arrogant skater knows that he has some very assholeish qualities but he can't help it that he was blessed with the gift of being the greatest skateboarder in the history of skateboarding] RR: The real reason I asked to be followed by the likes of you is because I've got some things to clear up when it comes to the Cowabunga Connection. I have known both of them since early high school days and I can reveal very interesting details about their lives that nobody, and I mean NOBODY would ever know. [The parking lot tilts upward into a small hill, and leads to a large grassy area with about six picnic tables lined up together. Obviously this is some sort of picnic area for the office personel that wish to eat outside. Randy takes a seat at one of the picnic tables and lays his skateboard on the top of the table. Double R then goes into one of his large pockets on his cargo pants and pulls out a pair of very expensive Oakley sunglasses. He places them on his face with another arrogant smile] RR: Harry, has and always will be a pothead. He was born with pothead genes and can't fight the urge to smoke pot. He will forever be cursed to listen to 80's heavy metal, which is absolutely GOD AWFUL! Of course, he will always be cursed with never getting a girl in his life. The only reason he's ever had one is because he takes Tommy's sloppy seconds, only when they are super drunk. Harry wouldn't have them any other way. [Randy then turns to his right, and there is another camera in his face as if he's some sort of newscaster or something] RR: Tommy is very insecure about so many things in his life. His hair has to be perfect, his clothes have to be perfect, and his looks have to be perfect. He is constantly asking fellow friends if everything is in place because he doesn't want to lose the 'bodacious babes'. It's such a shame to see that our Creator wasted all those good looks on such a freakin' moron like Tommy. Yes, he is a moron if you haven't noticed. I've often wondered if he got dropped on his head when he was a baby, but if you hear stories about his father then you can understand. I don't know what Tommy's mom saw in that stupid man, because that woman is a FOX!! And still is....mmmmm mmmm good, just like Campbells soup. [Raggagu then stands from the picnic table and walks out of the picnic area over to another parking lot which is designed for a local restaurant called 'Piccolo Mondo'. Randy goes to the window and looks inside to see many high class people enjoying their very expensive food. Randy turns to the camera and shrugs as he keeps walking through the parking lot] RR: Now, the Round Robin tag team tournament....isn't it ashame that those morons are still in it? I mean they should be out and long gone after getting the crap beaten out of them by those newbies, Suicidal Tendencies. I nearly pissed myself in laughter. Now they go against a team they've defeated on many occasions, and thats Misplaced. A nerd and a cricket player...I'd say they certainly are Misplaced. Do I think that my 'friends' the Cowabunga Connection, will have a chance against this team? Not a freakin' chance!! After seeing that horrible performance against a couple of newbies like Suicidal Tendencies, Misplaced will kill them, and I hope literally. [Randy chuckles to himself as thoughts of a dead Cowabunga Connection goes through his head. Yeah, it's very sick sounding but if you were made fun of constantly by a moron and a stoner you'd want to see them dead too, trust him. He still walks through the Picolo Mondo parking lot] RR: After seeing Tommy snapping into some kind of psycho the past week I'm becoming more and more happier. I love to see those men in misery. Is Tommy a dangerous, evil incarnate now? NOT EVEN CLOSE!! [Randy begins laughing, holding his sides because they hurt so much from the laughter] RR: He's not doing it for show, he really is one pissed off mofo. Though he couldn't even hurt a fly the way he's pissed. I mean he broken a window and threw around some shoes, and then he breaks all his surfing trophies...HOW STUPID WAS THAT?! [Randy begins laughing again, stopping in the parking lot as tears roll down his face] RR: Then came a tragedy to every stoner who likes Cowabunga Connection, and that was Tommy breaking Harry's bong. It is a stupid freakin' bong that makes absolutely no sense to me, but then the idiots had a funeral for it and everything. I really believe that they can't take the pressure of being in a big time federation like PCW. I think maybe they need to be demoted to PWA again. Now that would be perfect for both of them!! HAHA!! [Randy still laughs, feeling so much pleasure seeing the Cowabunga Connection in such misery and pain. Randy feels like he's turning into a sick man but it can't be sick when it's two idiots like the Cowabunga Connection] RR: Tyler Nelson, if you can hear me you have to fire the Cowabunga Connection. You have to get them the hell out of PCW and bring in another tag team that is ACTUALLY worthy of their spot in PCW. Because I know that these guys don't care about their spots, they are too stupid to even know what the hell their spot is. They could probably be the highest grossing tag team in the history of wrestling but they'd rather smoke hash and try and play a stupid board game with 'bodacious babes'. So, Tyler Nelson, you have to do what is right and you have to end the reign of the Cowabunga Connection and give all of us a breath of fresh air. [Randy has cleared the laughter for a serious plee to PCW headmaster Tyler Nelson. Double R feels that his job is done and begins walking back over to the picnic area where he left his skateboard. That's when he hears a vague sound of The Beach Boys playing, but he feels that maybe it's his imagination because he's been talking about those morons. He makes his way across the Picolo Mondo parking lot and down into the picnic area. As he goes to grab his skateboard he begins sniffing the air, he swears that he can smell weed. The sound of The Beach Boys and weed? It can only mean that the Cowabunga Connection is in the area but they are too stupid to come sneak up on Randy] RR: It can't be...they would never know that I was here. [Randy seems a little uneasy now as he jumps on his skateboard and begins rolling to the exit of the office complex. As he rolls he swears that he has seen the reflection of a blue 2002 mustang in one of the buildings windows. Randy's eyes begin to bulge out in fear as he feels that the Cowabunga Connection have found him, and they've come to collect their prize...Randy's head] RR: I'm not scared, not scared at all!! I'm not afraid of those two morons. They can be in the fastest car in the world and they would never catch me on this deck. NEVER!! I'm the best skateboarder, the best ladies man, and I'm probably a better wrestles then they are!!! WOO! Now come and get me you stoned, surfing morons! [Suddenly a car barrels down the road to the exit and Randy dives to the side of the road. He then looks up to see a RED 2002 Mustang pull up to the stop sign. Four very beautiful blonde women in two piece bikini's fill the Mustang. All of them stare at Randy as if he's insane. The Beach Boys play loudly and one of the girls holds a joint in her hand. They then make a quick left turn and go down the large hill to the main road. Randy sighs loudly feeling the relief wave over his body] RR: That was freakin' close. [Randy goes back into the middle of the street with his deck. He shakes his head and once again wears his arrogant smile] RR: What are the odds that four hot babes like that would be listening to The Beach Boys in a red 2002 Mustang, and even smoking some weed. Jeez, what a fool I was to think that Harry and Tommy would come up here to hunt me down. I mean they wouldn't even know that I was sending a message to PCW about how much they suck and that they should be fired from PCW. There is no way they could have gotten wind of that. I am RANDY RAGGAGU, the greatest man alive!! [Then the 2002 blue Mustang barrels down and nails Randy from behind. Randy's prone body rolls over the windshield and falls off the roof of the car to the concrete. His face is frozen in pain as he groans] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: That's what you get for being bogus toward us, Raggamuffin!! [Tommy looks like a man with a vengence in his black, silk button down dress shirt and black dress pants] TTT: How dare you be that heinous and tell PCW that we should be fired. We're going to become the next PCW tag team champions, compadre. You can count on that bodaciousness!! [The camera pans over to Harry, wearing the cracked Steelers football helmet on his head] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man!! [The scene then fades to black]
