The Task at Hand Part 3

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/21/2002 'You see this is what I am talking about, Robert Vurdo,' arrogantly states Jon Kano. The self-proclaimed 'REAL Legend' is sitting in the back of a a strech limo, smoking a fine cigar, and sipping a glass of fine Merlot. He is dreesed in blue jeans, a pair of Doc Martin boots, and a black t-shirt with the logo for Sunday's Pay Per View extravaganza, Alliances, across the chest. 'Bobby-boy, a champion should hold himself in this regard. You shouldn't be concerned with what some disgusting fat-body in the crowd thinks of you, but of how to present yourself. PCW is not some little indy fed that holds its events at the local YMCA or in a high school gymnasium on Sunday afternoon, we are the leaders and innovators of the industry that I have dedicated my life for the last six years. Now Bob, you are probably thinking why I carry myself as I do when I am not a champion. Well, that just shows your gross incompetence and ignorance. I am a three time, three time, three time PCW champion. I have held championship titles in every single wrestling organization that I have ever been a part of in my illustrious, legendary career. I am not just talking about tag titles or a title that everyone in the federation had held, but several World Heavyweight titles. This Extreme Title, would normally not mean jack shit to most people, myself included, but it's different right now. I would hold a different title here in PCW for the FOURTH MOTHERF*CKING TIME, BABY!!!!!' yells Kano. After saying this, 'The Man who Rocketh the Mosteth' takes a puff off of his cigar and a sip out of his Merlot wine. 'Ahhhhhh, Bob, you must learn to enjoy the finer things in life. If you do, you will be half way to living the PCW lifestyle that I set the standard for. Bob, I am the best big man to ever step foot in PCW ring. I am not some bar-brawling drunk straight from the gutter, I am a six foot ten, three hundred pound, technically sound, muscular drunk. There is a difference, and don't you EVER forget it,' warns Jon Kano, taking another puff off of his cigar. 'In closing, Bob, it doesn't matter what I say, or what you say, this is all just hype. Come Friday, it will be time for both of us to shut the hell up and to get down to business. If there is one thing I know, it is business, I mean I am the founder and CEO of a Fortune 500 company. Bob, when our match is all over, and you are laying on the mat, looking blankly into the lights, with a pain radiating throughout your body like cancer courtesy of the deadly duo of the KANOSPEAR AND KANOPLEX, you will then truly understand what I am talking about now, and why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF,' foretells the prophetic Kano as he puts out his cigar, and slams what is left of his Merlot.
