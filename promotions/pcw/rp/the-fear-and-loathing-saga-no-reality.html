The Fear and Loathing Saga: (no)Reality...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/??/02 It was truely mind boggling, that this little drug addicted freak could negotiate a clause in my contract that could help me get the best revenge I can. I could ruin the reputation of PCW and Xavier Kannon in one swoop. Just imagine the conversations... 'Hi, can I get seventeen pounds of marijuana, a kilo of cocaine, 200 dollars worth of acid, a few E-tabs, three cases of Budweiser, your mescaline special for the day, and some vodka... No, I won't be paying with cash, charge it to Primetime Championship Wrestling, thanks.' It was brilliant. 'Its all on the company, well... former company, dollar. Its a great plan, is it not?' It really was. I couldn't help but be amazed. My brain was already comprehending whether I should get the drugs first, or my plane ticket first. I figured smuggling drugs onto a plane in this day and age wouldnt be a good idea. While they were arresting me twenty guys with turbans would smuggle in a nuclear warhead the size of Alaska and go completely unnoticed. Thats the problem with America today, like I said before, missing the forest for the paper factory. People look too closely at things and always miss everything that completely dwarfs their scrutinization. You watch Bush on TV and people discuss how much of a moron he is and how he doesn't know Putin from Peanut Butter... what they don't mention is how he is going to send us to war randomly, or how he is destroying the budget, or how he hasn't done anything of importance at all. No, they worry more about his mispronunciation of the word 'Reality'. Alot of people mess up on that word now a days. Especially my kitten, Talena. She hasn't ever been grounded in this reality, she would rather go for a walk in the clouds ('They aren't fluffy' she would always say, 'more like walking through onion soup then marshmellows.') then worry about todays issues. But she never missed the forest, she could still see the trees fine, she loved the trees and, despite her insanity, could name every last one of them. What made her special is her ability to see the forest, the trees, the paper factory, the desolate wasteland, the sapling, the forest, the trees, and the rest of the cycle to its completion. She was the daughter of Destiny. It was insanity. Thats another thing that has always confused me about me and her, we are the link between the supernatural world and the living world, reality and fantasy, gods and zealots, unicorns and horses, everything and nothing. Yet we sit here doing drugs in my car and driving towards the airport to get tickets on some trip that Talena planned in advance before even being aware it would happen, all at the expense of Primetime Wrestling Incorporated. 'What a strange and wonderful world we live in.' I said, she didn't speak. There was nothing for us to say that would make this moment any more odd.
