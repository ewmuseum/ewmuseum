Title: Beginning of the End... Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered On the plane again..... The life of a professional wrestler is spent more in airplanes and in cars than in arenas at times. And so it is that Trapezoid finds himself in the Utah airport...his body literally mush, trying to figure out a way to get to the arena in time to warm up and stretch out. Four matches in one night is no picnic. Never has been. Never will be. But now, it is the difficulty of the situation that makes its impression on the Trapezoid. The doubts begin to flood his mind. Sure, he all but knows he can defeat Tim Walden. But what if he cannot? What THEN? He'd still be PCW Television Champion, but he'll have lost a good chunk of what makes him who he is. His pride. Besides, his partner Teresa's nowhere to be found, so the Cruiserweight Title match is his best shot at a win. And Trapezoid DOES NOT want to go 0-4. The yawning Trapezoid sits in a chair, waiting for the flight to board. With him, he carries a Gameboy Advance. And in it, the sign he's sold out to the Devil.. Pokemon Silver..... The HIT: GOTTA CATCH 'EM ALL!! ::Yes, our intrepid Trapezoid has fallen victim to that most odious of Japanese fads.....POKEMON! He pushes the buttons, in the middle of a fierce battle. On the field he has a Raticate, battling it out against the might of a Spearow! Spearow gets ready, and attacks Raticate with the devastating PECK! But does this faze the Trapezoid? NO! Like a five year old kid, he screams out his next move to the entire airport.:: The HIT: RATICATE, SUPER FANG ATTACK! :On screen, Raticate complies, sinking his teeth deeply into the evil bird, causing blood to spurt out everywhere! Actually, it just triggers a on screen flash, causing the Spearow to lose health, but damnit, it SHOULD bleed!: The HIT: POKE BALL, GO! ::The Poke Ball is released, cradling the injured Spearow in its confines, before displaying the sweetest message of all to a Pokemon trainer....:: 'Spearow was captured.' The HIT: ALRIGHT! I CAPTURED A SPEAROW! The stares surrounding him turn to chuckles AND stares......as Trapezoid realizes just how sick he truly must be....:: The HIT: Bizzaro-World has officially been fostered upon us. Amber returns, Walden has a girlfriend, and I'm sitting here playing Pokemon. I'm thinking WAY too much about Alliances. But, then again, how can I NOT think so much about Alliances? I have to admit, it, with the possible exception of Trick or Treat IV, is the biggest night of my life. I have a shot at two titles. A shot at a soda contract. And an opportunity to tear apart The Front Office inside a steel cage in Alliances. Four contests, all so different in scope... ::Trapezoid sighs deeply, shutting off the Pokemon game.:: The HIT: The tag titles.....the Pranksters with Attitude...by the way, I'd like to thank Gaz for his help against that cheating bastard Dante Jackson. That said though, in the ring, the extent of my thanks will be your elimination from the match! And then, Playaz Inc.....the oft-missing Quo and aforementioned cheating bastard Dante! No threat there.....and then the newest couple of PCW. Damnit all to hell, I'll be damned if I let the Front Office win the tag titles! DAMNED I TELL YOU! DAMNED! :The Gameboy is slid back in Trapezoid's pocket...:: The HIT: Then the ladder match with Timothy....I've talked quite enough about that match already, haven't I? Walden will fall down, down down from the heights of the ladder. Plain and simple, that's what is going to occur. Apologies to Timothy, but, in a hardcore environment, he is NOWHERE near my equal! NOT EVEN CLOSE! Walden's a good technical competitor, but he doesn't know brass knuckles from a lead pipe. When the weaponry gets involved, Timmy's going to take one look at the ladder, wet himself, and try to run away. But, once the ladder rises, THERE IS NO ESCAPE TIMMY! LITERALLY, FOR YOU, THERE IS NO EVASION! ::A can of Mountain Dew makes its appearance, pouring down the gullet of the Trapezoid. The refreshment lends him a dose of energy, as he sips its lemony goodness down.:: The HIT: Then, the Soda Contract match in the Alamo...A&W root beer versus Mountain Dew. How can you support ROOT BEER?! It's not even really beer! IT JUST CAPITALIZES ON THE BEER NAME TO LURE IN UNSUSPECTING YOUTHS WHO WANT BEER! CHEAT! WEASEL! And so Tim......I'M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GR--oops, I forgot.....Alliances.... ::The woman at the front desk of the airline announces boarding. HIT stands, walking for the gate..:: The HIT: That'll have to wait..... ::Fade to black.............BOARDS! YES! BLACKBOARDS!: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
