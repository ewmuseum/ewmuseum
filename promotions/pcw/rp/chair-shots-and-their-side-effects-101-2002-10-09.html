Chair Shots And Their Side-Effects 101

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09 - 10 - 02 In one week Pranks With Attitude suffered a roller-coaster of emotions, one week they had accomplished life long dreams and nightmares, one week that will be forever documented both in the archives and minds of all those involved. This week in question was the build up and aftermath of Red October.... First the Asylum collective defied the odds by defeating, then Champions Duzza and Makonie in a title bout to become the new PCW Tag Team Champions. Defying odds became normality as Pranks With Attitude turned the tide on the ratings war between two of television's goliath networks UPN and CNN with their debut show 'P.W.A Tonight'. THEN.... As an act of malice, CEO Xavier Kannon sanctioned a series of matches; the new Tag Team Champions would run the gauntlet. Pitting Pranks With Attitude firstly against the number one contenders Playaz Inc, they would then go-on to face the winners of the contest featuring Redemption Song and Cowabunga Connection, but politics had already dictated an outcome. The scene opened in the confines of Gaz Green's Las Vegas apartment, the Jester pacing back and forth impatiently near the telephone, his eyes glancing down at his glistening Rolex that displays 11:32 am. 'Nerezza I know I'm seeing some shrink today but this poor time keeping could drive a man mad!' murmured the fidgeting comic. The PCW comedian had lived up to his word and removed the brown bristled that adorned his face only 24 hours earlier and smartened his appearance back to the green business suit we have been accustomed to. 'NNNNNEEEEERRRRREEEEEZZZZZZZZZZZAAAAA!!!' cringed the Emerald Menace once more with a snarl. Suddenly the telephone sprang to life and blared its monotonous tone throughout the luxurious apartment, stopped abruptly by the alert Gaz Green who lifts the receiver to his ear. 'Hello....' The line deadened as Gaz listened with keen interest before concluding the conversation. 'Right.... I'll be out in a minute or so, just keep the engine ticking over!' The Velvet Prankster stepped out of his dwelling with haste in his stride, closing the door with a thud, followed by the rattling of keys the scene momentarily closed. The image re-opened outside in the heart of a densely populated city, Gaz Green waltzing over to the recognisable vehicle owned by the devious demon Noscosta Ireul Nerezza. 'Hey, wasup brother, lay some skin on meeeeeee!' joked the Lord Of Laughter peering into the drivers’ side, window lowered enough to make out the distinctive features of Nerezza. 'Save the black bantering for the Psychiatrist....' hissed the hidden goliath as he sat purring the engine of his purple hearse. A heavy stench of worn tires filtered into the air as does the smog from neighbouring cars that lay parked along the sidewalk. 'NEREZZA TO THE BAT CAVE!!!' roared the Jester as he pulled his 226 lbs body into the passenger side, the car jerking forward swaying between the on-coming traffic. Again the scene drew to a close as the hearse vanished from sight under a cloud of dust. The likely hood is that the Conniving Collective are heading for the mental institute mentioned by Nerezza who feels his comrade maybe a little hazy after sustaining a brutal chair shot from the newest acquisition of 'The Uprising' Duzza. 'No messing around Gaz, I need to know whether you can go the distance this Friday night in the return match with Playaz Inc, baring in mind that it's a steel cage stipulation...' informed the Living Nightmare before an untimely Gaz Green interruption. 'No rules, no repercussions and the winner will be the team who make it out... Alive!' The scene finally re-opening to the stare down of P.W.A who psyche one another as they sit in a long stretching waiting room. Flickering lighting, set off by bad electrics and white washed walls were the only thing worth noting as a cold aura filtered into the chamber of wait. 'Mr. Green I presume?!?' questioned a gowned individual who startled his recipient with his stealth like approach. 'Y-Y-YEAH!!!' retorted the Prince Of Pranks making the doctors acquaintance with a handshake. 'Hmm-... Follow me Mr. Green' continued the doctor, yet to be unveiled. The duo walk over to a door marked 'Doc. George Andrews' leaving the lonesome behemoth in the company of him self. The camera reverted back to the mental evaluation as PCW's Gaz Green squared off against the judge, jury and executioner Doctor George Andrew who stood between him and his chance to compete at Friday Night Fusion for the Tag Team Championship. The room was rather basic as the camera swayed, a table and set of chairs currently occupied by both parties and various other medical instruments in glistening chrome. 'This evaluation is basically a series of tests. First I'm going to show you some ink patterns and I would like you to tell me what you see...' instructed the Psychiatrist brandishing a piece of card with a blob of ink smearing. 'Err-... Arr-... I know, the PCW Tag Team Titles!' cried the Jester in glee. 'Good - And this one?' said the doctor holding another. Only noticeable difference being the colour, the Jester squinted his eyes, paused and finally answered. 'AGAIN THE PCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP....' 'And this one, please Mr. Green look harder and tell me the first image that springs to mind?' continued Mr. Andrews with a huff, a touch of frustration. 'A BLOB OF INK!' bellowed the King Of Komedy drawing a smile and comment from his evaluator. 'Oh, progress at last....' 'Alongside the Tag Team Titles' interrupted the former NTICW superstar. The room drowned in silence, a slight twitch from the doctor who smiles subtly grinding his teeth. Gaz looks on exchanging a smile of his own, causing the Medical Practitioner to try his hand at another test. 'Err-... This next exam centres on spontaneous answers. For example I shall say a word and you have to say the first thing that comes into your head, so if I should say cat I would expect you to respond with dog!' 'Why can't I say squirrel or even ferret, I mean dogs are like so yesterdays pets!' said Gaz Green, a slight mockery of PCW's dizzy diva Ellie who had played a substantial part in P.W.A dropping the ball to Playaz Inc. 'Ok - So are you ready....' replied the institute worker. 'Steady...' grinned the Instigator Of Injury to the glares of confusion. 'Steady? What-...' injected George Andrews, a dumbfounded expression swamping his stern face. 'What? Sorry can't think of anything for that' proclaimed the Asylum member with a false showcase of teeth, his gaping smile disheartening the doctor further as reached the pinnacle of his tether. The doctor rubbed his face before staring cold-heartedly at the sarcastic comic, slowly boiling to his brim when without warning he erupted like a raging volcano. 'ARGH - I SAY, YOU LISTEN AND RESPOND! Isn't difficult so make the effort and we'll get through this a lot quicker, now P.W.A?!?' asked the Psychiatrist, calming his explosive attitude to that of a pussycat. 'P.W.A... The Academy!' returned Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'Good - Now Academy...' said Doctor George Andrews. 'WRESTLE TATTLER!' 'Yes, very good. Now Wrestle Tattler?' questioned the paid help once more. 'The Phantom Booker!' whispered the PCW comedian. 'And again....' 'Phantom Booker... Hmm-... GOD, followed by Evan Levine...' flowed the Jester as the evaluator intruded upon his momentum. 'Excellent~! Now Evan Levine....' 'Well Levine to me is Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW) which in-turn is owned by Xavier Kannon, who's married to Commissioner Ellie, who screws everyone in one form or another....' hissed the 'P.W.A Tonight' host, the camera picking up on an evil glint in his eye. 'Oh, let's try Commissioner Ellie again?!?' 'Well Ellie always intrudes on the affairs of Pranks With Attitude, who were robbed of their Tag Team Title reign by Playaz Inc and... THAT'S IT SQUIRE! I HAVE TOO RECTIFY THIS MISS-HAP OF MY CAREER BEFORE I CAN MOVE ON. THIS FRIDAY WHEN WE WIN BACK OUR RIGHTFUL PROPERTY I SHALL BE RESTORED TO MY FORMER SELF!!!' gloated the unpredictable Jester, shaking the doctors hand in an act of goodwill before fleeing the enclosure via the door with a skip in his step. The door had closed leaving George Andrews standing motionless in a statue like state, trying to fathom the events that had transpired minutes earlier. 'I knew I should've been a 'Gynaecologist, the mind is far too complicating!' concluded the exhausted practitioner strolling over to his desk and removing a partially filled bottle of whiskey. The scene drew closed, leaving a black screen in place of the image. - The End -
