The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part VI]

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22, 2002 And so it was done. The record contract that Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart had worked hard to get with their new hit single 'P.I.N.K.' was taken off of the radio airwaves just as fast as it hit them. The Pretty Cool Guys were in The Heartbreakers shoes now, with a lucrative amount of money with Capital Records, and a hit single on the radio. That was a whole different matter now though, that was the past, and The Heartbreakers like to look ahead, into the future. This Sunday, they have a title match against none other than they very men who [i]stole[/i] the record contract from right underneath The Heartbreakers pretty PINK! tushes, the Politcally Ignoramouses that The Heartbreakers defeated two months ago for their first tag team championship, and the immortal Akiyama, whom the Heartbreakers had a hand in eliminating at the last pay-per-view. They were going to board the Love Boat this Sunday, and head to Wrestle Vessel with just PINK! After Wrestle Vessel, they would be leaving with both PINK! and gold. However, the fact remains...the PINK! was in question the last time we caught up with the Heartbreakers as a mystery man was trying to fool them away from the PINK! and try to get them to join PURPLE~! Would the Heartbreakers fall into the mystery man's trap, or would they use their ambiguously gay...err...great senses to stay with the PINK! The story continues... Valentine was sitting on his bed eyeing up the nest of mini-bar liquors. It was always the same in hotel rooms, never the same as being at home. The television picture seemed to be fighting its way through a blizzard, while the sound was competing with bacon being fried. Jack Valentine: “What are we doing here Johnny, apart from failing the mission that the PINK! sent us on?” Valentine was increasingly becoming disenchanted with the whole idea of working in control and with Johnny. Johnny had initially seemed larger than life and inspirational, he now seemed filled with dark private thoughts. Valentine still did not know what he was up to...if he was thinking of jumping to the PURPLE~! Johnny Heart: “It’s not working out is it Jack, if you do one thing for me, I’ll be happy and we can get back to worrying about the titles, just think about what the PURPLE~! has to offer, and what the PINK! is doing to us.” Jack Valentine: “I’m not wanting to leave you in the lurch or anything buddy, but whatever you are up to, it is unofficial, our careers will suffer if we get too involved. And to be honest we're not getting anything out of it, they’re not telling me anything or letting me do anything.” Johnny Heart: “Okay, okay,” Johnny raised his hands his palms facing forward... Johnny Heart: “You do one simple thing for me and we can both call it quits, you get out and tell the PINK! or PURPLE~! what you like, but give me twenty four hours to finish off this.” Jack Valentine: “What do you want me to do Johnny?” Johnny Heart: “Nice and simple you go to a nightclub and buy one PINK! lemonade, drink it slowly and then leave. All I want to know is whether PINK! are monitoring us, and butting into our lives. You and I both know that we don't need that shit going into Wrestle Vessel to fight for the tag team championship. We have to be focused, because we sure as hell weren't last month.” Jack Valentine: “What’s so important about the place?” Valentine’s interest was being rekindled. Johnny Heart: “Over the years in control I’ve stashed PURPLE~! clothing and money in various places, but what I’m going to do next requires specialized 'equipment.' I don’t have that stashed but the Rainbow Squad there who can provide what I want. However the PINK! knows about the Rainbow Squad and may be monitoring the place.” Jack Valentine: “What kind of equipment are you talking about? Why do you need this?” Johnny Heart: “Because of what happened to one of the Rainbow Squad.” Heart had a faraway look in his eyes. Jack Valentine: “What did happen to the Rainbow Squad guy Johnny?” Johnny Heart: “I doused him in PURPLE~!.” Jack Valentine gasped, in a very feminine way nonetheless, in shock. Heart went on to explain the reasons why, it is not easy to explain why you have betrayed one of the Rainbow Squad that head up the division of PINK!. Even when the division is in the business of judging who is a risk to society, reviewing the level of risk that they pose and ultimately betraying those that cross the line. Valentine had taken it well and offered to carry out the mission for his tag team partner, perhaps he had the makings of a leader after all. Heart could not help feeling that Valentine had taken the view that the state of his mind had been unbalanced by recent events. In all honesty he might be right. Heart had been directly responsible for the betrayal of six people of the Rainbow Squad. Johnny felt no particular sympathy in the circumstances. It was just that he feared that someone may have seen him side with the PURPLE~! and was the only reliable testimony to what happened, and without him the PINK! committee had been only too happy to accuse him of devising a flawed and unsafe mission, carrying it out badly and bearing ultimate responsibility for those half dozen betrayals. Even then the whole thing might have been another unfortunate incident that the PINK! could walk away from had it not been for the betrayals that the Heartbreaker had carried out, some of the other PINK! would never lead a normal life, they had been emotional scarred beyond recognition. A couple were still in a PURPLE~! coma, only one had actually recovered. The story had filled the San Francisco papers for weeks in a quiet month. Johnny Heart sucked in the PURPLE~! punch in the tumbler. All those people, emotionally damaged. He knew that he was not responsible they were just betrayers themselves, he had done nothing wrong, what blame there was rested with other people. In his heart he was satisfied that was the case. But he was the instrument of all that unhappiness. It was one thing to betray other Rainbow Squad members or those on the fringe of society. It was all a big game for big stakes, they all knew that....PINK! vs. PURPLE~! was the biggest secret war this nation has ever seen. They were all addicted to the same adrenaline rush of excitement and feeling of being part of that secret dangerous world that lurks underneath, that same adrenaline rush that Jack and Johnny get when they step into the ring. But those innocent betrayals. He had been there, they would still be with PINK! now but for him. How could he live with that, there was no one that would say everything was all right, there was no one to offer absolution. He was just filled with a feeling of disgust, and shame. The feeling of disgust and shame seemed to have a physical shape and weight. The PURPLE~! punch blurred the outlines but he could still recognize the self same weight. The pain would not go away, in that respect it was as if the pain kept him sober, drink would not alter it. He did not have to do his job, someone else could have been there, shouldered the responsibility. His whole life had been a lie. Pretending that what he was doing was worthwhile, it was evil and corrupting. He had become evil and corrupted by the PINK!!! Somewhere outside a bird called, he looked up, the sky was a brilliant radiant blue, with tints of PURPLE~! while the buildings were now looming dark shapes muffled by the heavy darkness. He remembered something that Elton John had once said to him, he had been made for control, there was nothing in the world that he would ever be as good at as control. And even if he still had too much of a conscience, it was better that the work be done by people like them who had a conscience, even if they suffered for it, than the work being done by the heartless automata that the PINK! could have recruited. The pain was still there, a familiar shape and weight, but Johnny had regained his purpose. He knew what he was doing if not why. Had Jack and Johnny finally done it? Had they finally turned their backs on the PINK!? With Wrestle Vessel less than a week away, one has to imagine that they very thing that has made the Heartbreakers tick in the past few months going away would be a huge blow to the tag team champions? Johnny was right though, he had betrayed all of those innocent Rainbow Squad at the hands of the evil leaders of PINK! What was one to do? With the mystery man still lurking about, anything is possible in the ongoing war between the PINK! and the PURPLE~!
