The Keyholder

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-13-02 Fear is just a locked gate, and you have the key. “But how do I use it? And more so, how will this help me defeat my opponents on Turmoil?” You’re not really worried about that, are you? “Well no… I just…” I had nothing to say. In truth, I was worried about the upcoming match. Desperately. I would be against five other competitors, competitors that were worthy of my gold. Just get inside their heads. What do you know about your opponents? I thought long and hard. “Nothing.” Don’t be foolish. Kris Burden? I did feel foolish. Of course I knew about Kris Burden… of course I knew of his unwillingness to give up, how time and time again he would attempt the impossible. Goooood! “He took a stupid risk when he faced me the first time. He almost came out on top. The second time, at Gold & Glory, he attempted the impossible. The impossible was impossible.” Then why do you fret your encounter the third time? You are still the impossible, are you not? The voice in my head—the one I called the Voice of Evil—was right. Why should I have doubt in doing something I had done already multiple times? Kris Burden was not a worry. “What about the others?” Venneman Pendragon? What do you know about him? My answer came even slower than the first. I realized that lately I had been slipping. Not keeping up on my research. Unable to determine the next moves of my foes. Who was Venneman Pendragon? I should be able to answer off the top of my head… even though I had never before heard this name. Something had been stopping my mind from exceeding the normal boundaries. “The WITs…” Yes! Yes! Now you see! It was those infernal creatures who you so courageously demanded to train. You took your mind off the task at hand, your own warrior soul, and concentrated too hard on those students. And look how they treated you in return! They abandoned you! They were nothing. Forget the Warriors In Training, for they are of the past. Think deep. Tell me what you know of your opponents… I tried to think. It didn’t work. But then I remembered; knowledge did not always come from thought. I looked deep, deep down inside of myself… and suddenly it all started flooding back to me. The knowledge—the real knowledge. The knowledge of a warrior. “Venneman Pendragon. Fancy name, fancy techniques. Will certainly attempt to put on a show, to make the match look good. His soul is not rooted in evil. He’s just doing his job, doing it the way only he can. What I must throw at him is pure spirit. The thing that only I possess!” Do you know whom it is talking? Do you know whom it is jumping to conclusions that should never exist? It is the voice of an Infinite Warrior. It is the voice of Trigger. Continue! What do you know of Jaden? “She shields herself behind a veil of attitude. She acts tough, but inside is truly and deeply insecure. She will talk harsh, and she will fight harsh. But when she looks into my eyes and sees the fury of my soul, her weakness shall be exposed. She will be nothing then.” Ah, exactly. Heaven Rayne, what do you know of her? “I have fought her before, have I not? Yes, now I recall. It is she whom I defeated to win the Rising Star gold in the first place. She has come back for more it seems. Oh, what a fool. She truly believes that her spirit alone will carry her far. She calls herself an angel. So did my Voice of Reason. In the end, Heaven Rayne will end up destroyed and distraught, only another victim to the duty of a warrior.” And it is so. But the last opponent… you have not taken much notice to. You think he is another puzzle to solve. But indeed, you two have met before, met in a bloody battle far, far back… “Scorpion! Now I see, now I see! We have met before, yes. I remember it like it was yesterday, with his cockiness, creating nicknames for himself on the fly. Ha! The only nickname he should have stuck to was coward. He thought he could beat me. Without any special strength, without anything to back himself up, he truly believed that he could defeat the Infinite Warrior. We never got to fight. But when we meet this Tuesday, a grudge from the past shall be renewed. And this time, the blood really will spill…” You have done it! You have gone back to your warrior ways, viewing your opponents deep below the surface. A normal man could never see so clearly. You know more about them than they do of themselves. You are not worried now, correct? “Correct!” You have no doubt of your foes’ untimely demise, correct? “Correct!” You stand before a locked gate, Trigger. But have no worries. You are the keyholder.
