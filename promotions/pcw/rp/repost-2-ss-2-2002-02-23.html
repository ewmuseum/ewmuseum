Repost #2 SS#2

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-02 Segment One: Hank and Ganker are sitting down in a coffee shop sipping on a couple bottles of Sobe. Hank is looking pretty depressed after hearing what Janelle had to tell him and Ganker looks concerned that it will affect his performance inside the ring as he takes another swing of his Sobe. Hank is wearing black baggy JNCO jeans, white wife beater, and a long black leather trench coat. Ganker is wearing baggy blue jeans, long sleeve Ring Side Entertainment black t-shirt, and a black leather jacket. Hank's PCW Extreme Championship is laying down beside him as he picks it up and throws it across the room and spits in its direction. Ganker- What's up wit' yo man? What Janelle have to say to yo? Yo been acting freakin' crazy ever since yo got here. Hank- I prefer not to talk bout it boyo. (sips Sobe) Ganker- She leave yo? Hank- I said drop it boyo~! Ganker- Fine, then will this affect yo match this Sunday? Hank- F**k if it does, like I give a sh*t bout that. Ganker- What's wrong wit' yo man~!? What did she say~! Hank- F**k off~! Ganker- Tell me or I'll yack yo sack off~! Hank- Not in the mood for yo f**kin' bullsh*t~! Ganker- (raising voice) I'm not in teh mood for yo sh*t, but I tolerate it anyway~! So straighten up or I'll bomb yo a**~! Hank- (snarls and growls) Leave me alone... Ganker- Fine, but when yo do wanna talk, remember, I'm yo friend no matter what. Team Nirvana until the end. Hank- The end come sooner than I thought... Ganker- I'll jus' ignore that. Segment Two: Ganker left the coffee shop and headed to the hotel room where Janelle sits on the bed awaiting Hank to return. She looks really worried about Hank and her relationship as she holds her stomach. (guess what) Meanwhile Ganker knocks and Janelle gets up and answers the door. Ganker is invited in as he pulls over a chair and Janelle sits back on the bed. Ganker- What's up wit' Hank? Janelle- It's nothing for you to be concerned about... Not even sure it's true. Ganker- What did yo tell him to make him all upset? He's my friend and I wanna know god damnit~! Janelle- Then ask him... Ganker- He won't tell me, he's all depressin' to talk to. So why don't yo jus' tell me? Janelle- I... I can't. Ganker- I see, then I guess I am jus' outta luck. (Ganker gets up to leave then turns back at Janelle) I am Hank's bestfriend, I am beside the man one hundred percent and what's his business and generally my business. I'd give my life for that bastard, jus' remember that... (now continues to leave) Janelle- Wait~! Ganker- (turning back) Yes? (grins) Janelle- I think... I'm pregnant... Ganker- If this ain't stealin' storylines from the WWF. (laughs) Janelle- One minor correction, I don't lie like that bytch. (grins) Ganker- Actual... Janelle- Watch it~! Ganker- Yes ma'am. Janelle- (smiling) I'm going to take another test to be sure, but if I am, I'm going to have to take a little time off the road and Hank doesn't want to be without me. Ganker- I can see why, he's really psyched to have such an incredible woman such as yo-self by his side now. He's finally found success now that he's found yo. I mean his past managers and valets have brought him misery, but yo, yo brought him success. I can understand why he wouldn't want yo to be away form his side. Yo mean so much to him. Janelle- I'm really thinking it's because he doesn't want the responsibility of having a child. That he only wants the pleasure of child making, not the actual child. Ganker- What man doesn't? (laughs) Janelle- Think this will distract him from Sabotage? Ganker- He'd have to be unhuman t... Let me rephrase that, cause I'm not sure Hank is human. Janelle- (laughing0 He is... Ganker- Yeah, well he'd have... No, he ain't normal either. Well let's jus' leave it at he'll be distracted durin' Sabotage. Janelle- Okay... Ganker- Well I gotta go, get back and find out where Hank ran off to. My luck he's tryin' to kill himself right now. Damn suicidal freak. Janelle- Yeah, he's so sweet. Ganker- Yo both sick... (smiles) Yo still my friends though. Janelle- Thanks Ganker. Ganker- Hell, I'm jus' bein' a friend. Janelle- Well, yo doin' a good job. (smiles) Segment Three: X-Pac and Tazz are shown sitting across from Hank in the coffee shop now, trying to bring cheer to his face once more. X-Pac- Come on bro, cheer up~! Tazz- Yeah dawg, what's wrong? Hank- Shut the f**k up and leave me the f**k alone~! Tazz- I'll choke you out, now spill~! Hank- Shut up boy, I know yo better than yo know yo-self. Tazz- Er... Uh... X-Pac- He got yo bad man... (laughs) Tazz- Well if you decide you wanna talk bout it, then you know where to find us... X-Pac- (interrupting) More than likely. Tazz- (interrupting) Cause he was us for a longwhile. ---X-Pac and Tazz exit as Guy and 0-Factor walk over. Hank- Is this the attack of Hank's Character's day? Guy- Hey~! Don't make me hit you with the most Dominat... Hank- Dominating Factor of them all... Blah, blah, blah, shut the f**k up and leave me alone. 0-Factor- Reality Factor man, realize that you ain't the only man on this Earth. Show some consideration. You have a heart, why don't you try using it? Hank- I ripped out my heart and stomped on it. Save women the trouble of doing it later. Guy- Oi... I think he needs a trip in downtown. 0-Factor- Pull the van around? Hank- Try to put me in yo van, see if I don't end up kickin' yo a** more than I want to right now. 0-Factor- Yipe~! Guy- Let's go, I think there's a bank we can rob down the street. Louie Rico needs that mill from us remember? 0-Factor- How can I forget, he swore if we didn't pay that cash back he'd have our testicles for desk decorations. Hank- (laughing) Good ol' Louie, can always bring a smile to my face wit' his so called tortureous schemes. Guy- At least we managed to make him smile. (shrugs) 0-Factor- (nods) Uh huh... ---Guy and 0-Factor get up and leave as Ganker Ransolver reenters the coffee shop scene and walks over and smacks Hank until he bleeds. Ganker then sits across from him with anger all over his face. Ganker- Yo woman could be pregnant and yo only think' bout yo-self~!? Hank- Nope... I'm thinkin' bout us, the factor that I don't want that kid growin' up wit' out a dad and I don't wanna give up my job in PCW. Ganker- Erm... Hank- Not to mention the time Janelle would have to take off the road and away from me would be jus' death to me. I'd end up killin' myself. Ganker- She might not be though, never know. Hank- Nah, she is, I'm positive. It's the way my life works. The minutes something good happens to me in my life, something drastic has to happen to ruin it all, and mess it all up for me. Ganker- Sorry man, but (laughs) I'll take good care of your Extreme Championship... Hank- Touch the title and yo might as well say good-bye to yo sack. Ganker- Sick freak, but if yo truly love Janelle, do what's right. Hank- I won't quit PCW, I won't quit wrestlin'. I still have too much in me to quit. Ganker- I'm pretty sure things will work out. Yo have well earned yo break in life, I'm sure now ain't yo time. Hank- Probably right, so I'll jus' try to put this by me and focus all my attention this Sunday Sabotage to beating and defeating Jason Core and Karl LeBlanc... (cough) Two times, two times, two times~! (cough) Ganker- (laughing) DDP wannabe... Hank- He's my idol, that's not a bad thing... That's a Good Thing~! (grins) Ganker- Damn yo have the single most yellow teeth I've ever seen. Hank- Proby do.
