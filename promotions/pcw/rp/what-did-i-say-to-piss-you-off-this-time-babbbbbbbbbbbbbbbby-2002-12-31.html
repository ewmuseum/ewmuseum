Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: What did I say to piss you off this time babbbbbbbbbbbbbbbBY Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/31/02 “Big Balls in Cowtown” by Asleep At The Wheel blares out of the old Wurlitzer Juke Box in the basement bar of Duke Williams home. The Malice Man sits behind the counter wearing all black from his house slippers to his Stetson hat. It’s a Bloody Mary morning in the Williams house as Duke shakes up the tomato delight. He flips through his the channels on his high definition plasma TV only to find Springer on so he flips it over to Fox News and leaves it. Turmoil in the Middle East is the least of his problems as a war rages on his home front in PCW. Williams: What a way to start a new year. A pending yet definite war, a weak economy with a bleak outlook, cloned babies, terrorism, and the Osborns. The hoofs of the four horsemen of the apocalypse are beating the pavement ready to cleanse the world and open the gates of hell. But of the forthcoming third year of the new millennium these problems are not my immediate concern. “The Malice Man” takes a sip of his potion and smirks. Williams: 2003 years after my hippie savior left this earth in dramatic exit I’m left fighting a new war. Duke shakes his head in disgust. Williams: You see dipship a.k.a. Xavier Kannon brought Rush to PCW. Then they found out what a wart on a pricks tip he is and now they don’t like him. Who can blame them I sure can’t. So now he’s being in ass to everybody and tensions back stage our like TNT fucking dynamite hoss. Duke smacks a glass down hard against the counter to make his point. Williams: Everybody…EVERYBODY is at each other’s throat and I have to fix the problem. Just as I stripped Nelson of his presidency and weakened him enough to be taken out. I must do the same to PCW and Kannon. I have an opportunity to send him Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound yet again this Friday and then AGAIN on the Sunday show. Duke smiles at the opportunistic chance at his disposal coming this week. Williams: Fusion I get to step in the ring with triple T, Rent-a-Hero, and my pal dipshit. First off who the hell is booking this jive ass shit. TTT?? What the hell is that a phone company. Let me spell it out for you in your language you hippie dippie dunce. Duke gets a clueless look on his face. Williams: Like Tubular Tommy man your like gonna get your ass handed to you in like a basket dude. It’s going to be like a major wipe out man. You know? Your trying to hang ten with a Big Mamou of a wave dude. You like so not ready for the main event you bogus fucking hack. Duke gets serious. Williams: WHOA!! I see Rent-A-Hero is back yet again. Yeah. Let me get excited. Duke whips his finger in the air like he’s excited. Williams: Ok I’m done. Duke places his celebratory finger on his glass and takes a drink. Williams: Rent I’m sick of seeing you. You couldn’t save a spray painted popcorn fart from the wind killer. I though you were some kind of hero. Kid I’m the hero. I’m the one raising my glass up against evil forces and succeeding. I fight the oppression of the system and give the people something to cheer for. You fall in love, turn evil, disappear, and return and expect anybody to give a damn?? Please you a comic hero nobody reads. Nightwing, the Green Lantern, Aquaman? Who gives a shit your no super or bat man bitch. You should just f*cking retire hang up your pansy tights and give it a rest. Let the real cowboy’s roam on the range hoss. If you need a gig I’m sure you can make some cash doing birthday parties hoss. Duke laughs at the pathetic nature of Rent-A-Hero. Williams: Kannon oh Kannon can you hear me now? Nobody likes you anymore. You’ve warn out the realm of bullshit one can take. Get over yourself or let me do it for you. Fusion your ass is mine hoss. No trash talk just Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound.
