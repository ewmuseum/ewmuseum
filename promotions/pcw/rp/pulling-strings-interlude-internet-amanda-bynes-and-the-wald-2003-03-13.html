Pulling Strings - Interlude - Internet, Amanda Bynes, and The Waldof Astoria

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13th, 2003 [If you're wondering just why we're not in our normal, 'novel' format - well go ahead and see for yourselves. Gearing up for March Madness, the odd ball thought that happens to be what you would always consider a clusterf*** of major proportions to be. A cluster! Right now, Dante Jackson is stuck in Hollywood, instead of New York tending to 'other' matters.] Dante Jackson: THE HELL DO YOU MEAN AMANDA BYNES HAS A SERIES ON NICK AND NITE?! [Dante is in serious search of thinking just how to get his movie across to the people, which starts shooting Spring Break next week, instead of March Madness this week because of it being March Madness and all. I mean, the proportions of Dante even becoming Continental or even World Champion is absurd to any smark to say that Dante has had even remotely the month of Veneman Pendragon or the champion, or the Cruiserweight champion is ridiculously placed out of proportion.] Dante: I wish he didn't use so many big words... [Sitting in front of a computer right now, Dante happens to be on the internet, a brand of thinking I thought was never possible...] Dante: I heard that... [Anyway, he pulls up the internet movie database, or imdb.com for those who feel like going there and begins to type in the name 'Amanda Bynes' at a slow pace, before hitting the enter button repeatedly. The page pulls up, including every single movie and television show that she's ever been on. Dante shakes his head, groaning and everything included.] [The phone rings inside of the Waldorf as Dante jumps up, in a start looking around the for the phone before picking it up.] Dante: ....heloo? Jason Cunningham (over the phone): Oh please, don't act like that. [Dante continues to surf the internet.] Jason (over the phone): Still surfin' the little hot chick right? Dante: THE GIRL GETS A MOVIE! AND EVERYTHING! AND I...I GET REGARDED AS THE 2003 RUSH MASON?! WASUPWITDAT?! Jason (over the phone): Stop using WWE catchphrases, you know how PCW doesn't tend to like that. Dante: Oh hush! They don't care about it! For whatever purposes, a big nose could come out of the sky, call me an 'entertainer' and tell him to 'dance for him!' [Suddenly, Jason taps Dante upon the shoulder as he continues surfing the internet for purely his own enjoyment before Jason spits into the air, the wad landing completely on the head of Jackson.] Dante: Odd, that felt gooey, yet splendid. Jason: You are even more the idiot I thought you weren't. Dante: Hey, I am not an idiot! I just play one on TV! And how long were you standing there? [Dante whiffs the chair around as Cunningham stands in a complete superhero stance, his arms bent at the side as he calls out his name in the same fashion.] Jason: CUNNING HAM! Dante (in Jim Ross voice): PUT THE WOMEN AND CHILDREN TO BED! Jason : Really now? And you say that using women as objects of gratuituous nudity as in wet hot pant contest aren't really used to be 'sending the children to bed after?' Dante : I didn't understand what you just said, but riiiight. [Jackson shakes his head as Cunningham steps into the picture, closing the phone door as he snickers.] Jason : Um, you didn't do anything to upset the authorities did you? Dante : NO! Jason : Then ask me why we're in Totally Tubular Tommy mode? [Dante thinks for a moment, before a little lightbulb pops above his head and a light smile quirks on his face.] Dante : Look, I had an ephiphany! Jason : A what? Dante : Epiphany, I saw it on one of those zip up jackets at a flee market, but it works though. Last night, in a dream, I was visited by none other than.........FRISCO! [Jason gives a dumb look as a cheesy smile evokes Dante to stand proud like a superhero.] Jason : That has to be, the worst idea you've ever told me! And I've heard bad, including Skylar Green wanting a feud with that dog from Eddie, but that....you getting a vision from a PCW Hall of Famer is completely, ludicrious! Dante : I thought it was Ludacris? Jason : Same name, different spelling. Gah, if I were racist and had a scar over my eye, you'd get this now wouldn't you? [Dante shakes his head.] Dante : I...I don't follow. [The scene around him is fairly the same from a few weeks ago inside of the Waldorf-Astoria hotel. Knocked over books from the shelf as a few are drawn upon the very table in the kitchen. Lights inside of the hotel are farily illuminious as Jackson wanders around in complete silence from his partner in crime, Jason Cunningham. Wearing a white PCW polo shirt with khaki slacks and brown rimmed glasses, the needy public relations man is all business, while his conformist friend in walking around in slippers, rubber ducky boxers and a shower cap on his head.] Jason : Its called symbolism, I think guys like Hessian and Vampir Nosferatu use or used it at one point in time. Dante : He took things he didn't even say, or create and called them his, its not symbolism if you can't pull it off with a simple tug of how things go, now can ya? [Cunningham looks around, walking over towards the short book stack and opening up a few novels by Sir Authur Conan Doyle and H.G. Wells. Dante picks up the book by Wells before thinking to himself.] Dante (thinking): Oh, and this was the guy Drake Maxwell was impersonatin'! Jason : That 'guy' was one of the best author's PERIOD. [Dante shrugs his shoulders, laughing to himself though as he walks towards the front door of the apartment room, still dressed in the aformentioned boxers and shower cap, not waiting on Jason or anything to help him. Jason continues to flip through H.G.Wells as Dante takes a whiff of the hallway air.] Dante : Ah, there's nothing better than...HEY YOU! [He points his finger towards an old lady who only stares at Dante in complete disgust.] Old Woman: Me? Dante : Um, yeah, was I pointing to you? Because if I was, then you are in violation of trying to catch a peek at me. Old Woman: I most certainly was not! [If you can't tell by now, the old woman is completely white, there's nothing stopping Dante from even saying that she could be remotely black, which in that part she would come across his face and slap the taste out of his mouth.] Dante : YOU WERE! AND DON'T LIE! THERE ARE CAMERAS! [The old woman shakes her head and walks away, very slowly from Dante as he stands, starring at her before the scene fades to a White Trash Wrestling Alliance promo. WTWA, THE BEST BACKYARD INTERNET FED OUT THERE!]
