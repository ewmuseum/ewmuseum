(Friday) B.S., 'The End' Skylar Green, Young Tiger, and Duzza all end up gathering in the middle of the court a few minutes later for a quick Eternal Wrestling reunion that mostly entails of making fun of those sorry sumb!tches Jax and Shane Wethers. Things begin to settle down as more PTCers and more members of the general public file in to this event for charity and take their seats over the next half hour. Ten bucks for the general public, five to those that don't participate in the entertainment. So if a wrestler buys a ticket and then participates? They get a refund. What great motivation! We can fade to black safely for now. Because The Phantom Booker himself repells from the ceiling and his connected cables into his pre-designated spot on top of the rafters. TPB continues to do his best Sting impression, panty hose-on-da-head and all. Now all is right with the world. Until Syko the Goblin decides to show up. Chris Matthews suddenly cups his hands around his mouth and screams for no apparent reason: MATTHEWS: And baby.. that's no b.s.!! B.S. responds by cupping his hands together as well while he chats with Duzza about who the finest half-white / half-black people in the world really are. STORMBRINGER: Heh. Thanks, man! No really- BAH~! You know the rest! Later still.... (Saturday) B.S. grins for a short moment at the thought of his dream match going down someday, but in a professional and sportsmanlike way.. Amy Campbell versus Bobby Stormbringer. STORMBRINGER: Oh well. I'll have to wait for an interfed PPV when neither of us are very busy. Either that or maybe tag together. It would be up to her since I'm willing to kick almost anyone's arse at any given moment whether the fans like him or not. Or 'her' for that matter. No really. I mean it. B.S. shrugs and shows his impressive Owen Hart-ish fangs in a sneer for a split second. (R.I.P. bro) STORMBRINGER: Ah yep. You're wondering about Randy I bet. The fecker has disappeared. I mean off the Earth. He's trying to sell the family home to the highest bidder. His wife? Nowhere to be seen. Am I surprised? No. But for some reason the bastid loves turning my life upside down at least once every couple of years. I've looked through it backwards and forwards with my lawyers endlessly and our other birth certificates are legit. The odds are too low for anything else. Randy and I are the biological twin sons of Amy Campbell's father. Go freggin' figure. Way too coincidental, man... B.S. sighs to himself at the torment of The Wheel Of Fate before he takes another sip of his drink. STORMBRINGER: Then there's Drake Maxwell and The End. Skylar's been as silent as he is blind and Maxwell..... Max is Max. I don't even wanna hang around with these f*ckers for too long since I got more important things to do. Like Extreme and/or TV belts to win. A sister to protect. A tournament to win. A 'Perfect Storm' to reveal. Not my finishing move... *EDITED* Until Turmoil then, Badger. Time to dance one more time. You're a big warmup for the Fusion four way I got for the honor of facing Aki. A good warmup. Since you're better than any of those guys in the match. Except me. I'll show you that Tuesday. Enough ranting I have scouting to do. You'll see the b-ball game during GTT hours. Here's your obligitory tease. The special rules, the introduction, and things starting off with.... a bang. Oh yeah. As far as the Tempest tape? Well. Just reminding myself for what Amy's in for. Tempest tore up Thrillfest like no other, and not even Meanie should take her lightly now that she has two years more experience. After all... Knowledge is power. Absolute knowledge is absolute power. And baby? That's no b.s.! And now.. back to Friday.... * * * --3-15-02, Friday morning, 10:58 am EST, day of the big GAME!-- Welcome back. B.S.'s narrator has been sacked. Since The Showstopper can already do just about everything else, then why not? B.S. will be doing this on his own. I would be that rising legend in 'THE GAME of Human Chess.' The game must get underway soon because many of the PTC wrestlers have shows to go work in tonight. Friday night. The game was supposed to be on Thursday night which is a definite day off for all PCWers, but scheduling issues prevented that. The overhead strobe lights play along the tan carpeted arena court... You heard me. Carpet. With black carpet accurately representing all the lines necessary for a proper basketball game. Something to switch things up a bit, Mr. PT supposes. The arena is completely enclosed like a small arena as it is more state-of-the-art than any dingy, run-of-the-mill gym. Everything besides the carpet is normal for the smallish arena. Windows are on one side high upon the wall, crowd and seats on the other. People continue to stream in from the side exits and take the best seats they can, as close to their best friends as they can. Evne though this is still a semi-late arriving crowd, the main lights over the court go dark while the ones over the bleachers and rafters go dim. The crowd disingenuinely ooos and ahhhs at the basic lack-of-light display. For some reason Paint It Black by the Rolling Stones starts to play on the PA system. We all know that yours truly, Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer, loves a good entrance! The jackasses I like to call friends play along in the PTC superstars section around the middle of one half the court from the first row back to the fifth. ZERO: B! S! B! S! You're gonna lose to me! Cause you're a looooooser!! DAVIDSON: B! S! MATTHEWS: B! S! MO~!: B! S! RENT-A-HERO: B! S! COREY WILLIAMS: BOB-BY SUCKS! BOB-BY SUCKS! This silliness goes on for a while until finally a spotlight shines over a man with his head lowered. He raises it and his shades are actually off! Yeah it's me there is no need for suspense, eh? This must be a special event after all. I'm wearing my brand new PCW t-shirt, only distributed by the new B.O.T.S. (Bringers OF The Storms) company. The front reads in royal blue futuristic lettering against black: 'Was I ever REALLY in Asylum..?' I turn around deliberately and everyone including you the viewer can see the back in the same simple style. 'NO!! Really. I mean it.' Some people laugh at me and others boo just for the sake of booing me and my brilliance. I'm pretty used to the petty jealousy by now. *sighs* So since I don't want the show to run late (or for my promo to not make the deadline) I cut right to the chase. No not your lord and savior, I mean the actual point! STORMBRINGER: Hello and welcome to the PTC 'Street' Ball basketball game! A mild but pretty decent pop rises from the combination of PTCers and Orlando area residents. STORMBRINGER: Thank you all for donating just a little bit of your.. ahem!... 'hard'-earned cash for the well-founded CJS Fund! Raised a uh.. er. A lot of money for someone or something over the years. Yep. Sure has. You can almost.. almost hear crickets in the background due to the pindrop silence of Kid Wonder Center at the moment. STORMBRINGER: Um. C'mon! It was only five or ten bucks anyway. No really. I mean it. The crowd finally decided to help me out with the applauding. Probably because of the shameless catch-phrasing, though. 'Bout freggin' time either way! STORMBRINGER: Allow me to introduce to you your house entertainment for the evening. My fello mulatto dawg that I will someday rule the world with... Duzza! And he is not.. repeat NOT Crunk As Heaven. He is Crunk As Heelllllll, THE! RUSH! More spotlights turn upon the two supreme masters in the ways of good music immeditately start up with scratching and just basically tearing up 'It's Goin' Down' by the X-Ecutioners and that.. that guy from Linkin Park. Whatever his name is, I dunno. One's a god, the other is just laidback and just generally cool. It is played at a medium level of volume since such an important person is talking. Good decision on their part. STORMBRINGER: Our cameramen filming all the action that is sure to go down tonight. First for my personal collection of memorable.. erm.. moments! My personal assistant Carl Everstrong- A spotlight falls over and illuminates the always stoic Marcy-from-Peanuts-like Carl. Sir this, sir that. STORMBRINGER: Then from Canadian Heat Zone. The undefeated World Title Contender! At least to the best of my knowledge! Chrrrrris Matthews! A spotlight splashes over young Chris who looks utterly nervous about operating a machine that I can actually afford these days. If Chris breaks it... I look at my watch and g*ddamnit, we gotta move on from this. I make a cut motion to my neck. Duzza and I agreed that if I did that then the volume would go so low listening to it would be difficult for anyone. Errr... maybe The End could manage, but I don't care about that too much. STORMBRINGER: I gotta hurry here guys because we're on a tight schedule. It took so long to start because of Badge's fooling around with his sl*t in the bathroom most likely. Considering the time needed, i'd say the technique is poor at best! No really. B.S. & Crowd: I... mean... it! The crowd responds in general laughter and 'whooooaaa'ing that I'm gonna piss off the jackass pretty badly for telling the truth about his skank but.. oh freggin' well. But to majorly surprise all of you. These promos, unlike most others, are very expensive to produce. Especially to keep that arena price gouger Kid Wonder happy! The rent is ridciulous to the max. So we'll be back as soon as the rest of the intros and rules can be released without me going bankrupt or some crap and nosense. Until then, look for a dinner date with my sis-tah Aimz. Timo? we'll have to see about that. B.S. at OSW Slam in Orlando!? We'll have to just wait and see about that too. And baby? That is no... b... s. TO BE CONTINUED. THIS HAS BEEN A B.O.T.S. PRODUCTION.
