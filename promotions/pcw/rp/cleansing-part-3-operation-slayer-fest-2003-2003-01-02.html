Cleansing (part 3) - Operation Slayer Fest 2003

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02 January 2003 Prologue: As seen in the 'Cleansing (part 1)' Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa were involved in a bar room brawl with a vampire in a hornets nest known as the '6th Gate' where it concluded with the demise of the creature. However the troubles of the Front Office look to have continued as the dynamic duo faced yet another threat as a group of street thugs cornered them outside brandishing an array of weaponry. Almost a swerve, the plot thickened as the group were the collective known as 'Blood Bath' a select few vigilantes who cleanse the streets of the vampire threat, a pack of slayers so to speak. Again it came to light that this unforeseen meeting was actually an initiation test where Gaz Green would have to prove his vampire ties had been severed, passing the charade the Jester offered a bounty for the assassination of former partner and friend Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. So with the resources of The Front Office at his disposal Gaz 'The Jester' Green has given the contract of Nerezza to this fellowship most likely under the watchful eye and approval of CEO Xavier Kannon. The next instalment of this ongoing saga opens in at The Front Office Head Quarters, a tall glass building where the workings of Primetime Championship Wrestling are discussed. Opening on a ground floor suite, a large spacious room furnished with desks and office utensils, a small door in the corner reads 'Walden/ Wilson' and through the tinted glass we can make out two silhouettes going about their business. - THUD - Suddenly the room’s door swings ajar and through the entrance emerges the Corporate Jester accompanied by the Soviet Scarlet Natalia Miyagawa. Storming in several seconds later like a SWAT team was the collective known as 'Blood Bath' headed by their leader Gunn who crashed down his body weight on a chair, feet up he waits for the meeting to commence. The remaining four-man party follow suit and sit as a unit-glaring forward at their new employee of Gaz Green who stands before a white board with various words cluttering over its surface. 'Settle down people!' roared the Emerald Menace indicating for silence so that he may inform his new colleagues of his master plan. The murmurs soon vanquish and Gaz coughs subtly to clear his throat before concluding his initial sentence. 'To work gentlemen - Feast your eyes on 'Operation Slay Fest 2003' where we will take down our target Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. May I remind you that Nerezza isn't just any vampire, besides be your brooding silent type he tends to have a nasty temper, which I have first hand experience-....' continued the Front Office Entertainment Manager before Gunn injected himself with an untimely interruption. 'As much as I admire your pretty pictures there, this Nerezza is just another bloodsucker, so the same equation applies to him as would it with every other vampire-....' The mute team of Blood Bath roared their approval, rallying behind their illustrious leader Gunn who smugly glares over to his new employer Gaz Green who voices his retort. 'Equation? What equation-....' 'POINTY WOODEN THING + HEART = VAPORISED VAMPIRE!!!' replied Gunn who's statement draws a smile from both members of the Conniving Collective. Gunn kicked back his chair and strolled over to Gaz Green who stands looking over his personal crop of bounty hunters who differ one to the other, a bunch of oddities thought the Jester. However the hired help wouldn't need insurance policies neither thought Gaz Green as he reflects over his plan, which may be both his and Blood Bath's last but a small price to pay for the removal of the thorn in his side. 'DESPERATE TIMES CALL FOR DESPERATE MEASURES!' 'So squire since we shall be all working together don't you think you should introduce me to your band of merry men?' asked the Lord Of Laughter nodding his head over to the flock who sit in silence. Pivoting his head up and down in a 'Yes' motion, Gunn pointed to the first individual in this line and informed the Front Office Entertainment Manager and his valet both the person's name or call sign so to speak and their role within the Blood Bath collective. Bane - Basically this fine specimen, mass of muscle is the heavy artillery. Vampires are physically strong, highly evasive and have an arsenal of tricks so to combat this disadvantage we have the behemoth Bane, our own personal tank so to speak. The being Bane was simply a spectacle. A hulk of muscle and a towering figure, somewhat a mute he glares with intimidating glances, more so with the use of black contact lenses. Clad in a tight fitting tank top, his arms littered with tattoo's the individual looked more a wrestler than a vampire slayer. New Blood - The brains of this outfit. New-Blood is the weapons expert, a crack shot and the Blood Baths personal blacksmith, this individual is a little smug and cocky but makes up for his arrogance with the perfect mind for vampire slaying. Similar statue to that of the Jester he could indeed be a long lost relative. Even to the point of having a similar mind, standing rather smug he looks rather cheeky showcasing his own personal arrogance. Casually dressed he has no stand out features except striking side burns and fragments of red highlights littering his dark hair. Judgement - This individual right here is your contingency plan, if all else fails then this guy has more guts than brains. Your ideal slayer, puts those first and himself last, he's basically the clean up operation, nothing special to comment on, he's no better or worse than anyone before him. Dark and mysterious the man shelters himself behind dark sunglasses and his attire is clad all in black coverings, ranging from his long flowing leather trench coat to his steel toe-capped builders boots. Somewhat secretive he sits toying with two wooden stakes, juggling them between his fingers he may indeed be Asian but its difficult to tell from first inspection. C.B. - A fitting alias on both accounts, firstly his name is Carl Black and he's head of communication. You have your intelligence, electrical wizard, and surveillance, basically he's a one-man operation who gets the word off the street and incorporates the information around this array of tricks. A scrawny youth who may just come out of his teen years, the youngster sits with a lap top under his arm and appears as an African-American, probably a high school drop-out. Nothing special to comment on, he sits with baggy clothing, baseball cap, usual black youth so to speak, possibly a sports fan since his top has 'NY' insignia's splashed over the fabric. Gaz merely nodded his approval of this band before returning to the white board and more importantly the task in hand. Littered over the board in felt tip markers was the lay-out of a plan, masterminded by the Corporate Jester and expected to be fulfilled by Blood Bath who's sole purpose is to take out Nerezza, thus eradicating at-least one threat of the Front Office. 'It's a simple operation - Silent but violent, Nerezza uses a dank dwelling as his hangout when he's scheduled for competition and believe me he's a traditionalist so we can expect him to at-least attend. It's a simple question of treading lightly and dealing with the problem in a violent manner, I shall be at-hand to over-see the operation but once this deal is concluded then your contract vanishes, this must not be traced back to the Front Office!' issued the Prince Of Pranks. Gunn extended his hand and both parties exchanged a pleasant handshake concluding the deal on a good note. As they gestured towards one-another Natalia pulled a large fabric sports bag from behind a metallic cabinet, Gaz gazed with a dumbfounded expression and his valet unveiled the latest bag of tricks. Opening the bag both men peered in to see an assortment of gadgets, the latest in vampire slaying technology, ranging from silver tipped harpoon guns to implements such as night vision and mace. 'Now this beats using wooden stakes, huh?!?' snarled Gunn holding a jovial facial expression, holding the harpoon launcher aloft in a triumphant pose, rallying a positive response from the on looking Blood Bath collective of Bane, New Blood, Judgement and C.B. As the bag emptied under the guidance of Natalia and Gaz Green the scene closed, fading to black and ending yet another segment in this saga entitled 'Cleansing'. Again we're left on a cliffhanger as the end looks nigh for Nerezza, unless the vampire has a retort of his own, all to be unveiled in the fourth and final part-.... - To be continued -
