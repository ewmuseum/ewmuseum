Gonna Find a Way

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th April 2002 Some days just feel “that” way. You know, like your life is a joke and god is pissing on you. The days when your bank decides to cancel your overdraft and wants it all repaid in a lump sum... those kinds of days. But that is not to worry about, since we are sitting at a crossroads... literally. No, this isn’t a theoretical crossroads, this is an actual crossroads. And Miyagawa and JJ are sitting in Miyagawa’s British Racing Green coloured BMW Z3 (all cheer cos the Z3 hasn’t been seen for a month... and it is soooo pretty.) The bright Californian sun beats down on the ground, and the duo have the hood down while the car is stopped at the side of the road. They only each where t-shirts and jeans, with their jackets thrown in the back of the car. However they are struggling over a map which each party is holding one end of. Miyagawa points at a bit on the map, and follows it down. Miyagawa: That is the way we need to go. JJ points at another, seemingly random part of the map and follows it upwards. JJ: That is the way we need to go. They look at each other, determined to get their own way across. Miyagawa: You’re being stupid, you can’t even read maps. JJ: I can! I was in the Boy Scouts! Miyagawa laughs at JJ. Miyagawa: You are IN the Boy Scouts. JJ’s expression changes to one of annoyance now... although it was verging on that before the comment, so it’s not that big and great of a massive change, is it? JJ: I am NOT in the Boy Scouts still. Miyagawa laughs at JJ out loud. Miyagawa: Did you get your CAMP badge? JJ’s eyes go all small and beady instantly because this joke was used before much to JJ’s humiliation. JJ: Yes I did get my Camp badge, but not for the reasons that you think... I didn’t get it because I was suddenly acting all camp. Miyagawa: True, you were acting all Camp already, hence why you got your CAMP badge in the cub scouts too. JJ squints in the midday sun at Miyagawa. Miyagawa: TARD RAGE! TARD RAGE!!! JJ: Look, this is where we have to go **jabs at finger with map** Miyagawa: No, this is where we are meant to be going **also jabs at map with finger** And this is my car, and I’m driving, so this where we’re going. JJ: Well I bought the map at the last service station and we are going here... **jabs at map with finger** or else we have no map. Miyagawa: Have no map eh? Denial of service eh? Miyagawa grabs at his half of the map and pulls it, and JJ grabs at the other half and pulls it... and you guessed it... rip down the middle, each person has one half of the map. JJ looks furious. JJ: Dammit, you owe me ten bucks for this map! Miyagawa looks at the half in his hands and the half in JJ’s hands. Miyagawa: Actually I owe you five bucks because I only have half the map here, and you have the other half. Shall we get going? JJ: This isn’t over here Miyagawa... I shall get my... REVENGE. Miyagawa: Shush, we’ve got to get to the arena so I can speak to Boda... why did I let you talk me into not flying for once... we would have been there already. JJ: Didn’t you ever seen Road Trip? Miyagawa: Yes I did, and how did you get the part in that as the bloke who screws the big fat mamma? JJ: I’m not the little guy! I’m the stud muffin with the girlfriend who shags Amy Smart! Miyagawa clicks his fingers around JJ’s face, to much of a lesser effect. Miyagawa: Wake up JJ, your dreaming. JJ: Just drive dickwad. Miyagawa turns the radio on which ironically has “Are you Gonna Find My Way” by Lenny Kravitz playing right at this moment before lowering his foot on the gas. Miyagawa: Gonna find a way, gonna get a way... gonna... JJ: What’s this? Pop Lovesongs 101? Miyagawa: Gah.
