Title: Exasperation Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Oct.17, 2002 The office looks like an unnatural disaster swept through it..The paperwork is all over the room, but not in a usual form of true chaos, papers stacked up in clusters throughout like a sort of orderly anarchy, sprouting from the central stem of the desk of Tim Walden...Tim himself is slumped over the desk, fully clothed unlike last we saw of him on Tuesday Turmoil, almost as if he were critically wounded as the camera spirals upward over him in a shot used in the occasional dramatic film, though entirely out of place in this case...The camera reaches a peak of the ceiling before coming back down in an arc to face the front of the desk and the picture window behind, as the Financial Reclamation Executive finally pulls himself up... Tim: Yeesh..Who'd've thought taxing people could cause all of this paperwork? Tim immediately slaps himself in the forehead. Tim: If there's one thing worse than asking questions of yourself, it's asking STUPID questions of yourself...Ah, there's an idea. Tim sighs deeply and hits the intercom button, staring down at the masses of paper with a look of near-perplexity... Tim: Greg? Greg: Yes, sir? Tim: Get me the legal department on line 1. Greg: All right, sir...Will there be anything else? Tim ponders over the question, head perched on his open semi-fisted hand as he looks up with a mixed expression, lying somewhere between exhaustion and boredom originally, but squirming for an answer that it seems is just beyond the cusp of his line of thought.. Tim: ...And a danish..And not one of those rubber ones either, I know Crowley thought that was funny, but if he keeps sending me phony food, I might have to ask Brandi to send him a real pink slip...No, I do NOT care to hear your speculations on what a fake pink slip is at the moment, Greg, just get me the legal department and the danish. Greg: Okay, sir, they'll be there in a minute. With that, the intercom goes silent..And Tim immediately pulls open a drawer of his desk, scrounging about for something... Tim: Hmm..Maybe I should work on my memoirs...Wait, that's not really useful for a minute's time...What to do, what to do, don't feel like working but don't have any time to spend on anything major...Of course! Tim immediately takes out one of those executive toys with the five metal balls that clack back and forth, starts it up...And proceeds to stare at it. Well, I'd say the idea well has run dry...Back, forth, back, forth, back forth as the phone rings on his desk, and he hastily picks it up, shoving the toy back into the drawer. Tim: Yes?...What do you mean, I can't sue her to get my clothes back?! She stole them clean off my body!...Well, yeah, I let her take them off and I didn't make an effort to stop her from tying me to the chair...Oh, stop laughing already!! If you can't find a loophole, maybe we should've hired somebody who can! Bah, goodbye to you too...Well, that's someone who isn't getting a raise this year. Tim hangs up, more aggravated than before, then looks out the window, seeing a clear, cheery day, and a smile arises to his face...But it fades, as he turns back to his paperwork strewn about the office, not least of which is a requisition for a better lock for the office door... Tim: Damn Tomas..Thinks she can get the last word on me..I'll show her, stupid bitch goes and leaves me just about naked for over an hour on end, then refuses to give back my suit...It's not enough that I miss the rest of the show, it's not enough that I was tied to my own office chair, it's not enough that Brandi showed up and saw me like that, though at least she was nice enough to untie me so I could get to my closet before I left the arena, now that piece of trailer trash had to go and keep the ones she took from me..She learned from our previous encounter, but obviously she's missed the most valuable lesson of all...Do not mess with the Front Office. Well, I guess now she's just going to have to suffer the consequences.... Tim looks toward the door for a second, but sighs as soon as he does so...Frustration is evident as he hits the intercom button again. Tim: Greg? Greg: Yes, sir? Tim: Where the hell is my danish? Greg: It's coming, sir, we just had a minor shortage in catering, but I'm sure it will be here in another couple of minutes.. Tim stops the conversation abruptly with a push of a button, then sits back down.. Tim: Well, at least one of us has the title..Of course, that only means more people will be gunning for us as a group now, they know they can't take us on in a fair fight and come out on top...They're the underdogs, jealous of our power now, don't they see that we're keeping things in a better order than those messes of power groups we've had in the past year? I mean, the ring crews are getting paid, the matches are being made fairly, and all they can think about is their own wallets! What selfish little whiners, we're doing all that we can to make PCW the best they can be and all they say is 'What the hell is with these taxes?!' Ugh..At least they seem to have the sense to stay out of our way for the most part, though.. Tim pauses, thinking and reaching into his pocket, his hand moving slightly as it wanders around for whatever it's looking for..He looks serious, as he pulls out a screw..The same one he had been given a couple of weeks back, on Fusion, by that little boy, the one that was obviously connected to the mysterious tron video at Red October... Tim: This is what I don't get...All I know is that the guy attacked Bob Charlie, and that he's not done there..He's gotta have a plan, since he obviously hasn't done anything yet to follow up, he's working off of pure paranoia for the moment...Well, it won't work..No matter how important this guy is, he's obviously got nothing to worry about with our 4 people in the Mall Brawl to his 1 self..He must be insane to even think of attacking us, when it's obvious that we have the numbers advantage...Of course, almost any advantage can be overcome, but only if he manages to catch us off guard, from behind, while split up for any number of reasons, or with an attack with a piece of equipment capable of defeating four people...Okay, maybe there is a reason to be paranoid. But still, I know that if whoever it is doesn't play their cards exactly right, there probably won't be much left of whomever it is to threaten us in future... Tim exasperatedly looks at the door and sighs, then pushes the call button again.. Tim: All right, I didn't want to have to do this Greg, but if you're not going to get my danish to me on time, I have no choice.... Greg: Please no, sir, I value my job and it brings me pleasure to work for you, I can't help it that the catering staff was out of them at the time, give me one more chan-- Tim: Sheesh..I meant no choice but to go down there and confront that worthless catering staff myself, can't you at least wait for me to finish my sentences before grovelling?! Greg: Er..sorry. Tim: Damn assistants, first Nathan goes and runs off leaving me to look like a fool, now this sort of ridiculousness.. Tim lets go of the intercom, then gets up, irritatedly walking out of the spacious office, the crinkles of the paperwork trod under his feet providing a curious soundtrack to his exit, as he finally opens the door, leaves the light on, then shuts it, as the scene fades to black. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
