Mexican Mind Games

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/28/02 Desperate Times Call for Desperate Measures.......at least that’s what they say. Desperation causes people to do strange things indeed, but just how desperate do people have to be before they crack under the pressure? And when they plummet off the cliff of reality, how far do they fall before they catch themselves? Some people sink into the abyss and never return. Some barely make it to the edge before realizing the road they are on just doesn’t seem quite right. Then there are those that are caught between the edge and the unknown bottom. Those people travel along in limbo, searching for any way out that they can find. The line between right and wrong begins to fade away, and reason starts to take a back seat to anger, hatred, and even fear. Soon their emotions are dictating their actions and leading them on the path to utter chaos. Last time we saw the CEO of PCW and Greed, INC Tyler Nelson, he was admiring a shiny, chrome .38 caliber snub-nose revolver. Where did this place him on the path to insanity? Was he on the edge? Did he just fall off the cliff? Or perhaps he had already fallen and disappeared into the abyss? Many questions need to be answered, but there is only one person who can answer them.....and he’s sorta busy at the moment. We fade in to the streets of Kansas City, Missouri.....sight of this coming Friday’s edition of PCW Fusion. As a matter of fact, we are somewhere outside of the Kemper Arena near downtown. For a place that attracts many visitors from out of state the area looks like shit. If he didn’t know better, the CEO would have thought that this was Kansas City’s skid row. Homeless people litter the area around the arena, covering up with any scrap of cloth or paper that happens to come blowing their way. Trash bags are lined up in back of the arena awaiting pick-up from the sanitation crews. The vagrants find these bags a rather cozy resting spot on cold days like today. The temperature barely creeps above 20 degrees with a wind that whips the skin like a leather strap. The blowing debris in the air looks like a whirlwind as it moves down the sidewalk. As we follow one of the floating papers that travels on the air down the street, it suddenly wraps itself around the Armani suit covered leg of Tyler Nelson. The CEO trudges along on this brisk day in a pair of Bruno Magli shoes, one of his trademark black Armani suits, and a long, black, wool trench coat. Tyler seems oblivious to the trash wrapped around his leg as he pulls his collar up tight around his neck, then stuffs his hands deep within the trench coats pockets. Steam rises from his mouth as Tyler breathes the cold winter air. He walks toward the Kemper Arena, and begins to mumble to himself. “I’m the CEO, who the hell are you,” questions Tyler nearly incoherently. As we look around we see that there is no one in the vicinity of him. “I’m the greediest player in the game. No one can stop me.” Tyler continues on his trek and reaches the arena. Directly in front of him lay several of the homeless and destitute people. He walks up to an old man curled up against the wall, wrapped in yesterdays newspaper and cuddling a bottle of NightTrain. The CEO shuffles to a stop and looks down at the bum, then gives him a tap with his foot. The old man barely registers a response, so Tyler gives him a swifter kick. “Hey!” says Tyler. The old man’s eyes slowly open and try to focus on the person that has awoken him from his alcohol induced slumber. He moves to wipe the sleep from his eyes and scowls up at the CEO. “What the hell do you want? We’re not bothering anyone out here,” grumbles the old man in a raspy voice. “I’m the CEO, who the hell are you,” asks Tyler of the old man. The hobo looks up at the CEO quizzically, not knowing exactly how to answer. “Ummm....you okay sonny?” “I’m the CEO, who the hell are you,” demands the CEO with a little more conviction in his voice. The old man looks around and sits up on his elbow. “The name’s Pete.” Without warning Tyler fires a kick to the face of the old man, knocking him back to the ground and causing blood to spurt from his mouth. “You’re not him,” mumbles Tyler in a monotone, eerie voice. The old man gargles blood and covers his mouth as Tyler shuffles away from him. The bum crawls the opposite way, not taking his eyes off the CEO. Tyler moves down the side of the arena, staring blankly into space. “They won’t take my greed.” Another homeless person lays up ahead, a Hispanic looking young man. He had been watching the events that took place between Tyler and the old man. The young man, clad in several layers of drab looking, tattered clothes and a stocking cap, keeps a wary eye on the CEO. As Tyler walks up to the young man, he asks him the same question he demanded of the old man, “I’m the CEO, who the hell are you?” The young Latino’s eyes squint and he speaks in a thick accent. “Whas that ese? Jou talkeen to mee?” A fire lights in the eyes of Tyler. “It’s you!” The CEO’s eyes grow wide and a stream of drool pours out of the corner of his mouth. “I’ve found you....now I’m going to KILL YOU!!” “W-What are jou talkeen about, loco?” stammers the young man as he starts to scramble to his feet. He doesn’t get very far before he is sent reeling back down by a right hand from the CEO. The Latino’s blood begins to boil and he whips around to attack Tyler, but he is met by the snub-nose barrel of the chrome .38 caliber handgun. The young man turns a ghostly white as he sees his life flash before his eyes. “DIOS MIO!! NOOOOOO!!!” cries the young man. “It’s all your fault, Mexican,” says Tyler accusingly. “You’re the reason that they are trying to take my greed. If I get rid of you, they will stop trying to take my greed away from me.” Tyler looks deranged as he aims the handgun right between the eyes of the young man, a sick grin forcing it’s way across his lips. The homeless Latino begins to sob as he begs for his life. “Meester pleease.....I don wanna die!!” pleads the young man. The CEO grabs the Latino by his clothes and pulls him closer, still holding the cold barrel of the gun against the man’s forehead. The sinister grin Tyler wore on his face fades and is replaced by a vile sneer. He snarls as he slowly cocks the hammer of the revolver, “I’m the fuckin CEO, you Mexican son of a bitch!” Tyler squeezes the trigger and the young man closes his eyes, letting out a blood curdling scream. CLICK.....nothing. The CEO frowns and looks down at the gun. He squeezes the trigger again. Nothing happens. He stares at the gun as he shoves the whimpering Latino to the ground. The young man curls up and covers his head with his arms for protection as Tyler raises the gun to eye level. He cocks his head from side to side, rotating the shiny gun in all directions. He pops the chambers out to the side and holds the gun up to the light. It’s not loaded. Tyler just shrugs his shoulders, “Guess I better get some bullets.” He looks around innocently and shoves the gun back in his coat pocket. Some of the other homeless people from around the arena have started to gather around, but they scatter like roaches as Tyler spins around on his heel. He tightens the collar up around his neck once again and shoves his hands deep down in the pockets of the trench coat, then trudges away from the bawling homeless Latino. “Don’t worry, Mexican, you’ll pay on Friday......you’ll pay dearly,” Tyler warns to no one in particular as he heads off down the street. The scene fades to black as he makes his way down the street.
