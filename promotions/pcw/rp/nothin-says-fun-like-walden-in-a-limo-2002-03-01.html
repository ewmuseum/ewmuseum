Nothin' says fun like Walden in a limo!

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Nothin' says fun like Walden in a limo! Featuring: Tim Walden Date: March 1, 2002 (The scene opens upon a busy city road, cars rushing by on both sides of the street, though a great deal of snow has built up on the roadsides, and drifting across the sidewalks, it returns to where the street cleaners swept up..A limousine comes into view, ivory in hue, pulling out of a parking lot before proceeding along the road...The scene switches to the inside of the limousine, where Tim Walden is sitting rather cheerfully in the back seat, his confidence obviously buoyed in the past couple of days, having gone from losing to Flaymin Aymin and Kevin Sleeper, directly into another Invincible Tim run in the Playboy Mansion and a solid win over Big Daveylicious on his home turf, or a reasonable facsimile thereof in Calgary, Alberta, Canada...Tim is dressed tonight in his typical wear, however the CJS shirt is conspicuously absent, gone in favor of what appears to be...A nice, black, non-descript shirt...This is a fashion statement, the statement being 'No comment.' Tim quickly takes a swig from an unmarked bottle, for the sake of Snapple, then turns his attention to the camera in front of him..) Tim: Wow, what a weekend..Just when I figured I was washed-up, the ol' invincibility comes back in force, just in time to establish my presence again..Juan Borro and his admittedly attractive valet, Epic, Argyle, DAMN near Violator, and finally making Drake Maxwell tap out on the very floor of the Playboy Mansion...Sure, I got a little overenthusiastic at one point, but dang it, who wouldn't have been pumped after all that, I mean, really, I actually beat up the Violator, I was on a great roll...If only I'd just kept to myself instead of getting into that argument with Stormbringer, and yes, Bobby, I heard what you said about me the night after and I was rather offended, I could very well be NTICW Extreme Champion right now! But instead, I have bigger fish to fry in Primetime, for the very next day, I looked the Syndrome straight in the eye, in front of my home crowd, or a near facsimile thereof, being that it's only a day's drive away..Big Daveylicious put up a nice fight, and honestly, sometimes early on I felt like CJS was right around the corner, egging him on to give it all he had..But when I had the advantage, he tried to shift it with a low blow, and that just wasn't right....I knew it was his style however, so I came prepared, and the idiot's self-absorption proved his own undoing, as the Syndrome appears to have submitted to the very man who proclaimed its appearance. However, of all that happened that weekend, what got me was the reaction that I heard from my very own Prairie crowd...When I took that kick to the groin, what did they do? (Tim looks uncertainly downward, then turns his head to the side to stare out the semi-tinted window in a saddened manner...) Tim (sipping his drink nervously): They......cheered......They actually were happy for the prospect of my being rendered sterile...I was, to put it mildly, shocked..And it was then, then that I saw, on the cusp of one of my most important matches in my career, against Miyagawa for the Television Title, the error of my ways. For while I had proven to most of the people that I had truly turned over a new leaf from being some idiot who would help out any cause, no matter how evil it was, just for the sake of the damn money, I had forgotten about my ignorant behavior in my first few months towards my own home country..I got myself caught up in the whole concept of Canadian Jobber Syndrome, and in doing so, I'd offended many of my compatriots with the thought of moving away from their homes for the reasoning that just because losers like Rich Cannon and Jason Folts were total failures, and most of the other Canadians of PCW left for various reasons, from mystery-clouded disappearances to getting destroyed in the ring, that it was somehow related to the mere fact of being Canadian..I mean, that must be one of the most ludicrous ideas I've ever come up with, right up with Carbonated Milk being enough to win me a soda deal, the mere thought that one's nationality could cause failure in the ring is just laughably incompetent...There's lots of things that can make people fail miserably, just look at my Pay-Per-View opponents! (The camera stops its mild movement, perhaps fixed on a tripod now because the cameraman knows he'll be in for a long, steady shot..) Tim: First of all, we have 'THE' Multiple Man, 2 times now having lost to me, firstly tapping out in the Mall Brawl, then losing a tough battle mere days later to allow me a shot at Jeremy Howard, and the rest of that is history..The guy gets so many opportunities to get into the Continental Title hunt, and where's he ended up for his failures? Competing with the likes of some of my other opponents, possibly with a hope to gain the TV Title if I were to be unfairly quadruple-teamed...Then we have Big Daveylicious, whom in all the time I've known him hasn't managed to earn a victory over anyone more substantial than Bob, or occasionally Brandi if he was lucky. The most memorable thing he's ever done was stripping Kya Brady, and we all know how hard THAT is.. (Tim grins broadly and winks at the camera, before taking a longer drink of the beverage, then quickly turning the air vent in front of him to blast cold air in his face briefly, his eyes shutting briefly in the cool refreshment..) Tim: About as hard as beating Impact, just take him around for a while, show him a fun time by going easy on him, and as soon as you want it, he'll be all too eager to please..After watching him fight D-Kay, that's the basic gist of brawling with him it seems, just ease up on him enough so that the pummeling Impact takes will be just enough for him to want to take the easy way out, and that's all she wrote...But then again, I really oughta leave a loser like him to get beaten by one of the other guys in the match, make Afwa Kilimanjaro's day with a nice quick jobber to slaughter, he needs it after Timo Bolamba abandoned him, getting the last laugh on him for all the stuff he pulled while Tyler Nelson's lackey...Not an enviable position unless you get the sort of benefits Afwa's coasted through his career on...Management status, help constantly, crooked booking of matches...Afwa may be a giant, but he hasn't learned to win on his own merit yet, that is if New Breed doesn't lay a cheap beatdown on him before he even gets to St. Louis...And this leaves one man, one oasis of talent in a sea of jobbers...D-Kay Calyx, my fellow Deuce and friend...I'd like to say that we won't work together in the match, but honestly, there's not much chance of the match going by without the other guys getting caught with some double-Deuce action, so in all likelihood, it'll come down to one of us. Now, what will you do then, the critics will scoff, idiots like Chris Chase and Corey Williams who doubt the solidarity of the Wild Deuces..Well, I'm naturally assuming that D-Kay will do as he did at Shanghaied 2 with Red Rock, and lie down to bring the gold, presuming there IS gold this time, since Miyagawa will rank among my top challenges yet in PCW history, and rarely have I been met with such a challenge and backed down, or failed..Sure, Flaymin Aymin beat me thrice, so what? I underestimated him once, got cheated out of the win once, and didn't come in the right mental state for the game the last time..No more of that, Miyagawa is NOT to be underestimated, Asylum won't cheat me...Except maybe that snake in the grass Stormbringer..And I am NOT going to bring anything less than my A game to face the goggle-wearing freak one-on-one on Fusion, and just as long as I do that, I'm going to be satisfied with myself...To score another major victory over a major star of PCW, and regain the glory of the TV Title...But it isn't the same..I should've been able to leap into the upper echelons of stardom after facing Jeremy and taking him...But my friends and Jeremy's enemies worked together to alter the course of the match, and I was given the gold, abruptly downgrading my stock through the dirty manner of the win...I can't let my stock be dropped by another cheap run-in by my fellow Deuces..I must earn this gold myself, that's what the important thing is on Friday..I can live through just coming up short, as long as it's on its own merits, but if Clyde or somebody was to cost Miya the title, I would be stuck doubting myself again, wondering if I just had some odd luck with a major star once again..Or, I could pretend it never happened, make myself believe I'd earned it and erase that self-doubt....But what does it matter at the moment? I haven't even left Calgary yet, and I don't plan to until the fans like me again! (Tim leans forward in his seat, with an odd determination for the sudden shift of the focal point of his thoughts...But hey, it could be worse, easily..However, his determination is tarnished with the nervous uncertainty as to where he is going. He presses the button on the divider panel, using the intercom to speak to the driver..) Tim: Er, driver...Do you think you could cancel the plans to go to the, um, golf course for now? Driver: All right, where do you want to go NOW? Tim: How about the zoo? Everybody loves to go to the zoo, there's probably lots of potential fans there... Driver: All right, we'll be there in about 15 minutes.. Tim: All right, may as well just lay back and relax then... (Tim lays across the seat, his legs held in a semi-squat due to the small amount of space available on the seat, even lengthwise...His eyes begin to close slowly, as he inhales gradually...Then slowly exhales...A squeal of brakes is heard, and Tim rolls forward off the seat, fortunately not getting hit too severely, though it was sharp enough for the cameraman's feet to slip into the frame..He gets up quickly, then sits back down..) Tim: Actually, I think I'll just remain seated..Let's just quickly close this one out, I want some private time before we get rolling at the zoo.. (Tim quickly utilizes his seatbelt and shoulder harness, pulling them across him before fastening them, as the scene fades to black rather abruptly, before suddenly fading back in on a 16-year-old boy in front of a computer, slapping himself in the forehead while typing furiously, as the scene fades back to black...Come on, no more false finishes...There, it's stuck faded now!) ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
