The newspaper headline

29 April 2003

Date: 29th April So now everyone is suddenly having dreams eh? Apparently no one can defeat any of the four men going toe to toe on Tuesday night, and apparently this is a new era for PCW. Stone Burns, Matt Jackson and James Edwards are once again new opponents for Sivasian, they’ve been in PCW for as long as him or shorter, and so , as with his two previous opponents, they’d be working super hard to get the win. No one would slack, no one was confident enough not to cover ALL the bases. No one will leave their back turned, no one’s cocky enough, has enough in ring experience to do that, and so this match will be tougher than anything Sivasian has ever had to do before. He has one extra man to take down to win from his last match, and what is surely going to prove to be one of his most physical experiences of his life are surely going to go down at Turmoil. Tougher than Siberia, tougher than Iceland, tougher than anyplace he’s sniped before in his life. *** Sivasian was spent, it had taken much out of the man. His physical health was good, as good as it could be for a 43 year old, but this wrestling business was tough. Matches, although 20 minutes long, were tiring for the 43 year old, and he was generally pleased he’d have at least a week inbetween his matches. An ice pack adorned his head, touching against his pale, line ridden forehead. Each line and each scare on his face was said to tell a story, to tell the facts of his life in nothing more than physical body markings. He lay back in the thick brown armchair that was stationed in his houses living area, and kicked the footstool from under him. A few more plasters aided in his recovery, from what had been a frantic debut in the PCW ring. Sivasian sighed to himself, his arm was weak, both in fact. He reached over for a sip of his water, only to find it empty. ‘Oh honey!’ he shouted through the house, so loud it nearly tore it in two. ‘Yes my lovely?’ came a sweet reply from out the room. ‘Could you bring you’re favorite PCW superstar a glass of water?’ he replies back. Seconds later his wife comes into the room, with a small glass of water in her hand. She smiles sweetly towards her husband, before winking. ‘I didn’t think Matt Jackson would like some water…’ she says sarcastically. Sivasian sighs as he drinks his water, the adoring couple look into each other’s eyes for a second before a thud appears around the corner. ‘The Kiev Weekly!’ the injured PCW wrestler shouts in a beaming voice, ‘go get! Go get!’ he orders his wife. Mrs.Krikorian strolls out the room, as Sivasian admires her backside in her tight fitting faded blue jeans. He smiles to himself, as he imagines how many other men would like to be in his situation with THAT woman. She comes back in a while later, and hands the paper to her beloved husband, before being sent on her way, back to cooking the families dinner. Sivasian opens the paper and flicks through the paper for a while, until he suddenly freezes on one stop, staring straight ahead. A high, long pitched scream comes from his mouth, and his wife comes hurrying back in. ‘Honey! What’s the matter? Did you re-break your wrist, need a change of plaster? Twisted your ankle?’ she asked, as she stares over her husband, still staring blankly ahead. ‘No! Look…loo….’ He stutters his last few words and remains silent, having not moved the paper at all for the last 15 seconds, staring at one page, about the 10th in. ‘What darling?’ she says again, trying to keep the situation cool. ‘The….’ He blurted out, before throwing the paper down. Mystified, his wife picks the paper up, before giggling a little bit. ‘Sivasian, it only REALLY tells it like it is, it says you lost…’ ‘BUT! READ IT! It said I lost!’ he replies. ‘You did lose in your debut and first PCW match, it’s rustiness, you shouldn’t win your first 13 in a row or whatever, the ups come with the downs, and so long as you’re not poor anymore, and can provide for me and the boys anymore, nothings wrong, is it?’ she replies. ‘NO! But I didn’t lose…..’ he says. His wife glares back at him and shakes her head, ‘Really….’ He finishes his sentence. ‘YOU LOST!’ she shouts back, annoyed at her husbands ignorance. ‘But I, me, Sivasian Krikorian, the soviet sniper did not LOSE damn it. I did not tap out, I did not get pinned 1,2,3 , the rules of the match said that I lost , yes. But I did not give up. I was on my way to winning, before that Egyptian just gave it RIGHT up.’ He says, losing his cool for perhaps the first time ever. ‘Now, calm down Sivasian.’ ‘Calm? CALM! I AM CALM!’ he says. ‘That’s it Siv, I’ll make dinner while you calm down, once you’re calm you may come and have dinner.’ She says, commandingly. Not wanting to argue Sivasian nods, ‘yes ma’am.’ ‘So,’ he says to himself, ‘we’ve gotta go Sivasian. We’ve gotta reach out, you’re facing three freaks this time. Nothing as highly prized as this winner. The Russian army wouldn’t accept them dogs, except maybe the agent, in fact I think Stone Burns might come in handy…no wait, their nothing on you, nothing.’ He splashes his face with water suddenly and stares ahead, ‘I’m talking to myself now?’ he questions, before slumping in his chair. ‘Sure I lost, but not badly , right?’ he says. ‘RIGHT!’ No reply. He nods to himself, ‘Of course you’re right Sivasian.’ ‘Why thank you Sivasian, you’re looking nice today. Now, a win on Tuesday would do nicely, wouldn’t it?’ ‘You know Sivasian, ‘ says Sivasian, ‘I believe you’re right.’
