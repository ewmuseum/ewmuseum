Flying To New Zealand

Date: Aaaaah I suck Eddy too careless sometimes, he forgot to pack a book for his long flight to New Zealand. He had two brand new books, but had been forgetful enough to disregard them upon his drive to the airport. Of course, he’d sleep all the way to New Zealand anyway, so it wouldn’t matter. When he hauled his bag up to security and got through without having to remove any article of clothing, Eddy made his way to the plane. It had been delayed for 30 minutes because the plane wasn’t ready for takeoff. Now it was, and now it was time for his match against Mark Makonie. He leaned his head back into the fabric of the seat he sat in. The man next to him was a skinny man with short brown and a business suit. Up from however, a very LARGE man was pushing himself into a seat. 'I’m sorry sir, you’ll have to buy both seats…', said the pilot. 'No! I can fit', said the fat man, pushing himself in. 'Are you comfortable like THAT?' The man has his sides squeezed in like pliers. The pilot shrugged and walked off. 'I’m fine! Oh no, I have to go to the restroom. Hey…I’m stuck!' The fat man tugged and tugged and couldn’t get out. 'Help!' 'Mommy, look at the fat man.' '…Bobby! That’s your father and my husband!' 'My dad’s a cow?' The boy looked up with blonde hair and green eyes. 'BOBBY! That’s not funny', the mom said. 'Hehe…' The fat man got out, but he had the aisle to work with. Shielding his eyes as the fat man’s ass squeezed by, Eddy glanced downward and hoped the flight would get off and end. He wanted in New Zealand. 'Ladies and Gentlemen, your feature movie presentation is….' Eddy listened, and hoped it wouldn’t suck. 'Kangaroo Jack.' 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!', Eddy said in his mind. Eddy then leaned back and forced himself to sleep. 'Sleep!!!! 'Our feature presentation.' 'Fall asleep before movie starts…Fall asleep before movie starts…' 'We will now start Kangaroo Jack. Enjoy.' 'HOW CAN I?' Eddy fell asleep imagining washing his dryer spin, simply for the boredom that came from it. He woke up a little later, Kangaroo Jack ending gladly, as he got up. 'Hey, you have a book or something?', Eddy asked the man next to him. 'Yeah.' 'Do you have any extra one I can read?' 'Erm…I have some weight loss book.' Eddy looked at the man. He WAS skinny. 'Doesn’t look like you need it.' 'Yeah, I know I’m not to fat…' 'Have anything else? Horror? Mystery? Romance? Action?' '…Lord Of The Rings?' 'That’ll work. Which book?' 'The first, and second.' 'Well, I’ll just reread them.', Eddy said as the man handed him a black book with a picture of Gandalf on it, walking through a field. When Mr. Bilbo Baggins of Bag End announced that he would shortly be celebrating his eleventy-first birthday with a part of special magnificence, there was much talk and excitement in Hobbiton. Eddy read, and read, and often imagined himself as a Lord Of The Rings character. … 'Legolas-Skylar, hurry, we must protect Helm’s Deep!' 'Aragorn-Tiger, Helm’s Deep is in trouble! We must hurry! Gimli-Pete, hurry!' Gimli/Pete was fighting off a bunch of orks. 'PLEASE DON’T HURT ME!!!!!!!!!!' Gimli-Pete killed an ork and started running up to Legolas-Skylar in fear. 'You must kill them Legolas-Skylar!' Legolas-Skylar shot a bow that hit the ork and killed it, and followed with another arrow which killed another. 'Hurry!', Aragon-Tiger shouted. Eddy read BOTH books on his ride It was that long. He got in a LOTR’s mood so he asked the man if he had the 3rd book in the series. 'Nope…I have it, but I left it home.' 'Argh…' 'Sorry.' 'It’s OK.' They were nearing New Zealand though.
