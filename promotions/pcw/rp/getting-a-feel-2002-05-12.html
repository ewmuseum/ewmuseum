Getting a feel

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12/2002 ~Here we are. We are just a few hours away from Supremacy. A few hours away from the beginning of the end of the Ultimate Authority. A few hours away from Tim Walden's final lesson from his former teacher and mentor, Jon Kano. Who will prevail? Will Walden be taken to school one more time by Jon Kano? Will Tim Walden overcome his former teacher and step out of the shadow of his mentor? Ego has destroyed many great men. If you take a man with good skills, push him to the end of the universe, and give him everything he wants, including what he wants to hear, you create this thing called an egomaniac. This is what has happened to Tim Walden. When Walden came to Kano, he was a young up and comer in PCW who wanted to get ahead. Kano saw in Tim Walden this one thing that can make or break a wrestler, the one thing being POTENTIAL. Kano took Walden underneath his wing and taught him nearly everything Kano knows about the wrestling industry. Walden took these lessons to heart and blossomed into a solid wrestler, but one thing came with him. A thing called an ego the size of Texas. Kano taught him, and hooked him up with Boda to apply the lessons in the ring. Walden then slapped Kano in the face when he joined forces with the one man walking the face of the earth that Kano cannot stand, Tyler Nelson. Nelson supplemented Walden's ego with a position within the company formerly held by Jane Reagan, the office of the Director of Personel. Walden went apeshit, so to speak, with the power of the office. He fired and hired at will, and then he made one of the single greatest mistakes in his life. He attempted to and succeeded in firing Jon Kano. Granted, Kano was rehired with one hell of a pay raise a couple days later by Duke Williams, but the principle of the thing pissed Kano off. And if there is one thing Walden does not want at Supremacy, it is for Kano to come to the ring as a pissed off 'Man who Rocketh the Mosteth.'~ *The scene opens up at the Pepsi Forum in Montreal, Quebec, Canada, the location of tonight's Supremacy Pay Per View presented by the greatest wrestling organization in the world today, Primetime Championship Wrestling. The arena is empty, save for a few ring hands and handymen walking around, making final preparations for tonight's pay per view. In the ring, Jon Kano, dressed in a black 'Supremacy' t-shirt, baggy blue jeans, and brown Doc Martin boots. He is 'getting a feel for the ring' (ala bouncing around the ring, testing the ropes, etc.). He finally stops, and sits up on the top turnbuckle as he begins to speak.* Kano: Can you smell it? It's in the air. The air is thick with anticipation and excitement for tonight's PPV. I am jacked up for the first time in a long time. I am gitty as a little school girl. I get to take extreme pleasure tonight in teaching Timothy Walden why I am a REAL LEGEND. I am the man who did, indeed, taught Mr. Walden the most important aspects of his wrestling knowledge that he uses today. He learned his physical skills from a wrestling school. When I took him out to the middle of nowhere, I taught him to look within himself to truly become a great wrestler. I taught him about the fire that burns within all of us legends. I taught him in an extreme form and he learned more in the three days of physical and mental hell he spent with me than he learned in the twenty some odd years previous. I saw Timmy boy's weak ass promo. I saw and heard the panic in his voice and actions. I heard him speak of his stress. WHAT ABOUT ME? I run a fortune 500 company, I have a wife, and I am one of professional wrestling's true REAL LEGENDS. And this little asshole wants to talk about HIS STRESS? Why the hell do you think I smoke? Why the hell do you think I drink? It relieves stress. F*CK the stress, Tim needs to worry about me. *Kano hops off the turnbuckle and walks over to the ropes, nearest the aisle way.* Kano: He needs to worry about when he walks down this aisle tonight. He needs to worry about when he steps through these ropes. He needs to worry about when he looks across the ring tonight and sees me. He will look into my eyes. He will feel that lump in his throat. He will then know then that school is in SESSION. He will know that he will forever curse the day he denounced me. He will realize that he is truly f*cked. I taught Timmy everything important HE KNOWS, but not everything I KNOW. What does that mean, mental majors? It means that I am more knowledgable than Walden about wrestling. And if knowledge is power, doesn't that mean Timmy is basically damned? Jesus Christ, Tim can't win for losing. He is already feeling the pain. He is shaking right now, a little trickle of urine is beginning to run down his leg. And just to think, the match hasn't even begun. His utter fear of me will make me victorious alone, I don't even need my undeniable wrestling skill advantage over Walden. I almost feel sorry for Tim Walden, and I would feel sorry for him if he wouldn't have fired my ass a couple weeks back. That is how that little son of a bitch wants to repay the lessons I taught him? What an ungrateful pissant. And tonight, in front of thousands of fans in the stands and millions watching on Pay Per View why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano leaves the ring and makes his way to the backstage area.* ~Tick, tick, tick. The time is winding down until the school bell rings for Walden's first period class. It is going to be a long ass school day for our current Director of Personel.~
