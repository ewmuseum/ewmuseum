More Than Just A Destination

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-06-02 The top is sometimes just another barrier, an artificial ’top’ if you will. Like climbing to the top of a mountain, and realizing you are only half way up. For no one can really determine what the top is. Is it always just one step further to absolute stardom? Who knows, but Jamie Celt does indeed want to find out, for he has taken the next stage towards the top. He has signed a contract with the elite of all… Primetime Championship Wrestling. The black corvette drove at an average of 50 miles per hour through the streets of New Jersey. The streets were packed with people. Some going to work, so to school and some just… just searching. Jamie Celt felt like that was exactly what he was doing, and every step he took seemed to be in the wrong direction. But not this one, for once he had gone the right way…he anticipated anyway. The said corvette had a Boston City number plate. All the windows were shut tight and due to the slightly darkened window, no one could really see through it. Inside the car sat five men. In the middle of the back seat set Jamie Celt. He wore a PCW encrypted t-shirt. It was black with blue letters saying simple ’PCW’ on it, and the sleeves of it reached down to Jamie’s elbows. On his legs he wore baggy blue jeans with a black belt strapping them up on Jamie’s waist. His shoes were branded ’Nike’ and were black with dark blue parts here and there. Jamie’s hair stood spiked up. It was shaved around the sides and the tipped of his rather short spikes were tinted blonde. His hair had a ‘wet effect’. His face stood glaring out the front window. Look on at the road in front. Coming ever closer to his destination, though, where he was going was much more than just a destination, to him anyway. Four friends, and four Celtic members at that accompanied Jamie. The driver was a strong well built man with a thick neck, tanned skin and black hair. His name was Peter, he was quite high up in the Celtic ranks for a 23 year old. In the front passenger’s seat set Gerald, Anton’s best friend and cousin. He was small, yet well built like Jamie. Beside Jamie sat Fred and Jake. They were two older Celtic members, and one was Jamie’s brother, that being Jake. He had come to support Jamie as he headed to the PCW Headquarters for the first time since signing of Tuesday Turmoil, which was announced by the Front Office. Jamie’s eyes were glistening. He though deeply about what his future would bring. After months of chaos and confusion in his life it was finally looking like things were getting back on track. The war with the Triads was over with a verbal treaty being agreed, and the Celtics came out victorious and regained the fear and respect the once had from other gangs throughout Boston and beyond. Jamie, being the son of Anthony Celt, the leader of the Celtic splayed a big part in this victory. He participated in the epic battle, which was the decider in the brutal war between the Irish natives and the Chinese gang. Jamie nearly gave his life in that battle, and risked his brothers, but victory was what made it all worthwhile. On the wrestling front NTICW had collapsed, out of nowhere the company could not cope. WT officials froze the company. Whether it would ever come back or not, Jamie did not know. All he knew is he was the last ever NTICW World Champion. And that has always done a lot for people when they go to PCW. Maybe that NTICW wasn’t the same quality as the one of Xavier Kannon, Rush Mason and the Pharaoh’s day. But it was a big achievement in the rookie like Jamie Celt’s eyes. “So Jamie, you sure you have everything, because I didn’t drive 12 hours to New Jersey just to have you forget something.” Pete said with a smile. Jamie puts a light smile on his face and nonchalantly replied… “Yeah Pete, I am pretty sure.” “You’re not going to be in Boston much anymore, are you Jamie.” Gerald says with emotion in his words. This question hit Anton hard. It came from nowhere. He kept the stance he had, and replied to Gerald once again in a casual, laid back manner. “I don’t know Gerald. You guys know I will come back as much as I can. But this is my life now, my career.” Jamie said gravely. Gerald seemed disappointed with Jamie’s answer. Anton new he would not have the time he had when at NTICW. They had one card a week and the competition was no where near as tough as it would be in PCW. He would have to spend a lot of time in the gym, and in other places that would boost, his fitness, strength and skill. It would all require so much dedication. His other life, that being his one in Boston, may have to go hold, for who knows how long. The others seemed to feel awkward. This discomfort was broken when the sound of a fax was heard. For yes indeed, a fax was coming through the car fax machine that Jamie had re-directed from his own. “What’s this?” Fred asked curiously. Fred grabs it and begins to read, several seconds later he puts a rather confused and annoyed look on his face. “Ah, Jamie, you might want to read this.” Fred said. Jamie takes the fax from Fred’s hands and reads it allowed casually. “Due to reasons that will go un-noted all PCW Wrestling will pay a40% tax on their wages by order of the front Office.” Jamie reads. He puts the letters down and smiles in disbelief. “Are they serious. I haven’t even got my first paycheck and already I lose nearly half of my salary. Jeez, Xavier Kannon really is an asshole. Jamie knows Kannon’s reputation, and new he wouldn’t be pushed around, for he could not show any weaknesses, or he would be done before he had started. As the card drove on they reached a crowded streets filled with huge buildings. Finally the card stopped. Jamie looked out of his window to the left, and there he saw it, a huge complex, it was the PCW headquarters. Fred got out of the car and was followed by Jamie then they rest got out. Jamie opened the trunk and took out a suitcase and a Nike Sports bag. He flung the bag over his shoulder and carried the case in his left hand. He looked around at his friends and brother. “Well this is it guys. I’ll be home soon. Make sure you watch Turmoil. I’ll want to make an impression on my debut against Trigger.” Jamie said with sentiment. “Yeah, knock ‘em dead Anton.” Jake, Jamie’s older brother tells him. “Heh, I’ll try.” Jamie replied with a smile. His friends got in the car and drove off without another word. They were like that; they did not like to express their emotions. Jamie once again looked in front of him; he was looking at his future. As he walked u towards the complex he though deeply about his match against Trigger. A veteran compared to Jamie, who was only 19, on the verge of 20. Trigger was the longest reigning Rising Star title. He was a great competitor and had taken the likes of PCW World Champions to the edge. What a match for Jamie to debut on. He knew he had to win. He had no other choice. He could not let himself be beaten on his debut. He would give anything and everything to his match. He walked up the steps of the huge complex, knowing this was indeed much more than just his destination.
