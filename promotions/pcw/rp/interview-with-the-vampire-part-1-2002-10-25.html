Interview with the vampire, part 1

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/10/02 'How many horror movies have you seen about vampires? You must have seen at least one. Interview With The Vampire. The Lost Boys. Blade. Any one of those Hammer Horrors with Peter Cushing and Christopher Lee. So why hasn't anyone bothered to drive a stake through my heart yet, hm? I'll tell you why. Because they're scared that I'll begin to bleed all over the ring and they'll be responsible for my death. Not that death seems to bother people around here, nor the police. In any case, they just don't want to try it in case... you know... something else happens. So let me give a short demonstration...' The lights of the room suddenly sprang on. Immediately, the face of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza was bathed in luminescence, highlighting his pale skin. The dark rings underneath his eyes were apparent, even through the circles of red that sit in metal frames tried to mask them. The room was next to force it's way into your eyes. It was some sort of a garage, messy and unclean. Shelves of wrenches, spray-paint and bolts surrounded it, some of them smashed. Evidence that a struggle had taken place lurks in the scattered tool kits and a dented oil drum. The windows were mostly blacked out, except for one which was smashed open anyway. A little rain from outside wandered in, collecting inside a foam coffee cup. 'Cosy, isn't it?' Nerezza looked to his right. His face had an eerie grin on it. The kind you got as a child when you knew JUST what to do with that bug you managed to catch. Or when you had a BB gun and you remembered where you saw those annoying birds that wake you in the morning. You had that smile at least once in your lives. Now he had it. He reached up and gently hit the lightbulb to set it swinging to and fro. As the rays of light circulated the room in a vague oval, he looked back towards the tripod and camera he had set up, then lowered his red sunglasses to the tip of his nose. 'If there's one thing that I've learned from watching PCW events from the past, it's that most people in the federation are into... how do I say it... bondage. Ellie handcuffing herself, Akiyama's numerous abductions and so on. None of that particularly matters to me, but they say you have to try something at least once... so I did.' As the vampire stepped from in front of the camera, he swooped to one side and a muffled cry from many sources began to fill the air. The frame of the host appeared to move behind the camera, then a slight jerking of the picture lead to it looking down, slowly falling until the picture settled on three young women dressed in clothes that suit prostitutes. Of course, judging by the way their faces are made up, they WERE prostitutes. Nerezza stepped out from behind his camera and leaned down next to the one on the left. All three of these women were tied up, laying on their stomachs with their hands tied behind their backs, then their ankles tied together and tied once more to their hands. Their mouths were covered in grey duct tape. The muffles of the girl Nerezza seemed to have chosen grew louder as he crouched down, slowly scratching a long fingernail along her face. He was in a position that looks as though he were about to lock on a Camel Clutch, but instead of delivering the classic hold, he looked up at the camera with THAT look once more. A slight giggle escaped his mouth and stayed in his voice. 'Now before this looks like a pornographic video, I can assure you that these restraints are simply to prevent escape. Now, allow me to show you the true methods of vampirism. I regret to inform Mr. Green that I lied once before about my bite simply turning a human to a vampire. If we did that, then every vampire on this planet would first be a meal, then another mouth to feed. Nature requires a balance, so we can bite as many times as we like and the victim shall remain quite human... if that can be considered an advantage.' He looked at the girl and with a lightning-fast strike, tore open her cheek. The girl bucked around, trying to shake the weight off her and moaned as loud as she could, but the adhesive held her lips fast. As the blood began to slowly gather into a spot in her cheek, Nerezza stepped off her back. The two next to her were rocking side to side and to and fro in some hopes of escape. Their caterpillar-like movements brought no success and merely caught the eyes of their captor. The inhuman's heavy boots thumped on the floor as though he wanted to be heard approaching them. The one in the middle was flipped over by one nudging with the boot, landing on the third. Nerezza raked his nails down her abdomen, producing four red lines that spilled a tiny amount of blood each. Her squeals form a duet with the first, making a wail of pain that sounded like Bach's Toccata and Fugue in D Minor to Nerezza. As the organ reached it's peak, he formed an orchestra with the final girl by thrusting one nail as far as he can down into her throat. It was stopped by somethign and he withdrew it slowly, savouring the panic-filled groaning. 'So, onto how to make a vampire. You see, when I was turned, I was bitten on the lip. What I didn't know at the time was that the vampire that kissed me had also drawn her own blood. As soon as she bit my lip during the kiss, she drank mine for only a millisecond before the blood mixed with her own and flowed back into mine. So a vampire may only be made by the exchange of blood. Well, I think I have enough on this hand from all three test subjects, so...' Nerezza licked each finger in turn, staining his previously pristine white teeth a filthy, bloody red. His tongue turned a horrid scarlet as it snaked out to devour the last drops. The girl on the right had stopped moving, obviously from her lungs being flooded with internal fluids. The other two were still in so much pain and shock, that the trauma had prevented them from even noticing. Nerezza's crimson grin grew even bigger when he perceives it. In hopes of not disturbing the others too much more, he whispered next to the camera... 'Oops... I can't hear a heartbeat any longer... ah well. Two will do for this one. Now then, to infuse them with the human and vampire hybrid blood that will flow through me for a while. Once again, I lied slightly. The species of vampire that I was turned by, the Ireulim, need human blood to survive. The Ireulim are the most physically gifted of the vampire species that I know of. However, just after I was turned, I was approached by another vampire, who told me that I was to abide by the rules of the supposed council. I wasn't aware whether he meant a human council that governs the vampires or a council of vampires for vampires, but the result was the same. He was different, however. He was of a much more spiritual breed, the Gaeadim. They have the ability to hear thoughts, turn into mist and are generally more like ghosts. They also have no need at all for human blood, they feed on the negativity that humans produce. Every time you cry, or get angry, or even hear another pathetic song on the radio, they feed on that. Well, when one vampire drinks the blood of another...' The tale was cut short as one of the girls rolled onto her back, nudging herself against Nerezza's jeans. He cast a frustrated glance down at his prey, whose skimpy top was tainted from hot pink to cold red. The vampire then flipped her back onto her stomach, putting her back to square one. A series of helpless moans once more started to fight against the gag, but to no avail. 'Maybe I should go online to get some quality equipment... anyway, when one vampire consumes another, he gains their attributes. The one that tried to suppress me was... unfortunate. So I now only need human blood to empower my other form. Now then, let's get back to business. Feeding tiiiiiiiiime!' He sang as his fingernails, still a little stained with blood, pierced tiny holes into the tape covering the first girl's mouth. Immediately, she attempted to scream, but nothing above regular noise level came out. The vampire then took off his sleeveless red duster coat and dropped it carelessly to the floor. Next, he pulled up the left sleeve of his black T-Shirt, which in all irony was one of the Asylum T-shirts that stopped producing only last week. Another fast slash sprinted down his arm, with apparently no effect. 'At this stage, the human blood will be mixed with my own. For the next few days, my physical abilities will be a little more enhanced but the main effect is that I can change my body. But we'll leave that for when I need it. Ah, here we are...' Specks of red begin to form on the upper arm of the twisted lecturer, merging together rapidly and growing into a dome that finally gets pulled down by its own weight. The red line travels down Nerezza's arm and trickles onto his hand. Quickly, he holds his victim's head in place and keeps a fingernail poised above the tiny puncture in the tape. The dark red gradually slips inside, provoking a retching noise as the girl tastes the blood. Nerezza leaves her alone for moment as he slips over to the middle girl, then stabs a hole into her gag too. As he rotates his arm towards the camera again, it looks as though the wound he inflicted on himself never existed. 'Oh, that's another point. Vampires have an extremely fast metabolic rate, which is why most others need to feed many times a day on blood. Their bodies need a constant supply of fuel to keep up with the output they produce. So, if you'll excuse me again...' The vampire's face shows a small instant of pain this time as his claw plunges deeper. Blood spurts out and a few drips land on the camera lens, obscuring the second girl's face as Nerezza force-feeds the viscous liquid to his captive. She too finds the taste revolting, but her action earns her a hard slap across the face. 'Hey, your friend had an excuse, you told me YOU were the one that swallowed. Now drink it!' A loud sobbing leaked through the miniscule hole, which Nerezza covered with a huge paw. The girl had no choice but to ingest the foul fluid. She shook her head from side to side, trying to indicate she's done as she'd been commanded and to release the grip. A look of satisfaction swept across Nerezza's red gums as both the girls had drunk his poison. 'Well, now that I had two prostitutes to swallow my juice, I think I'll go to bed for the night. Wait...' The sadist went over to the dead one and looked along her still body. Lifting up her black PVC shiny jacket, he looked at the label that stuck out. With a cruel look of humour on his face, he turned directly towards the camera. 'Store overnight in a cool dry place, away from bright lights.... Well... it's chilly, they're not wearing that much anyway and the rain's kept out, mostly... yes, this'll do.' As he said this, the cut on his arm began to solidify, clotting, turning to a brown colour and then to pink before finally blending in with his pale skin. Nerezza shivered as though he had just drunk a strong alcoholic drink, then looked at the spot where he'd inserted his claw. 'There we go... see that? Right then... we'll continue this in the morning.' The monster picked up his coat with one hand and shook his other arm until the sleeve rolled back down, then draped the duster over his shoulders before moving behind the camera once again, where the scene fades to black. FZZT. * * * FZZZZZT. 'Well, this appears to be rolling nicely.' The room is as it was the night before. Three female corpses, two of which aren't true corpses. Their skin was the same shade as the makeup they wore, near white. The third was blue and purple all over. She slept on a blanket of red that had dried into the floor, never to open here eyes again. Next to her were the new children of Nerezza. Their father took centre stage between them, gazing over at the failure. He marched over to the lifeless vessel and placed a hand on her forehead. 'Cold... well no wonder, look at these clothes! Just a thin jacket, left open for goodness sakes! And these boots might look good but that skirt isn't enough to keep you warm and look at these, hardly covering anything!' The vampire picked at her fishnets and shook his head in mock sorrow. After shuffling the body to one side amongst a pile of oily rags and tarps, he concenterated his attention on the table next to the overflowing coffee cup. He'd placed a walkman with a single large speaker connected to it in the middle of the table, with the cassette door open. From his jeans pocket, he produced a tape and slid it into the walkman, closing the door and pressing the rewind button. As it cycled backwards, he interlocked his fingers and held them to his stomach, glancing at the camera with his head turned sideways. 'I'm now going to show you probably what you've been waiting to see. Last night was the only night you'll see me without my armour. All other times, I'll be wearing it to protect myself from the uninformed. Watch and listen closely, I'll only do this once.' As he finished the sentence, the walkman clicked stop. Nerezza extended an arm that seemed to dislocate and grow, stretching over to the table and reaching a length that would be impossible for any human but the most extreme of contortionists to achieve. He tapped the play button and the solitary speaker breathed a crackling hiss as the tape began to deliver it's message, as did the Victor Frankenstein of the video. Or maybe Dracula. The music began - Moonlight Sonata. The slow, haunting tune began to creep around the garage and Nerezza closed his eyes, as though absorbing the tune like the negative energy he spoke of before. 'Ahhh, Beethoven... ahem. Before we go on, I did that by the use of the regeneration technique. Like a controlled form of cancer, the cells in the area we require begin to grow rapidly and on a design dictated by our bodies. To return them to normal, the simply accelarate the necrotisation of those particular cells. Now, let's show you the truth behind Cushing and Lee's movies. Vampires are not killed outright by sunlight. As primarily nocturnal creatures, they have an aversion to it, but most vampires who walk in the sunlight wear sunglasses. A trait that I believe most of you do, although some of you even wear them indoors.... for some unknown reason. A vampire that exposes their eyes will probably be blinded for a few minutes after just one glance. For this, I'll need a volunteer. I think this one will do...' Stooping down, the huge fist of Nerezza closed around the fake blonde hair of the girl that he'd fed his blood to first. Yanking her up sharply, he contracted his arm that had stretched out, bringing it back to normal length. With it, he ripped off the tape that had bound the girl's lips most of the night and held her screams at bay. As she struggled to life, two voices awoke inside her... one craved for blood, the other for freedom. 'At this stage, the last remnants of her humanity will still be with her. She'll refuse to kill, become all mopey and go hungry for so long, then gradually accept it as the nuclei of her cells, particularly her brain, are taken over by the vampirism and her instinct to live, known to you budding psychologists as the libido, will be indistinguishable from her thanatos, or death instinct. Killing will be a means of survival for her unless she finds another source of sustenaince. Smile for the camera, now...' Nerezza pulled tightly on the newborn vampire's hair, forcing her to open her mouth wide. At that moment, her grabbed her mouth and pressed her cheeks inward, forcing it open. Her canine teeth had grown visibly, now standing out from her other teeth. The cruel captor looked at her face, then yanked her around, forcing her to face the shattered window. Her unprotected eyes yielded to the merciless spears of sunlight and she closed them, grinding her teeth together as she yelped in pain. 'Haha, there we go! Now it'll be a few minutes before she'll be able to see again. As I shall now prove...' Taking a step backwards, Nerezza walked in half a circle around her and waved a hand in front of her eyes. The female vampire merely stood still, groaning from the invasion of her eyes. Nerezza shoved a swift palm to her nose, taking her off her high heels and sending her onto her rear, hard. She hit the stone floor with an audible thump which caused her to gasp in shock. Her hands were now secured in wrappings of tape, making them into useless flippers like a turtle. The professor of demonology continued his insane lesson with the same sadistic smirk... 'Now then, the old legend of driving a wooden stake through the heart is true... although wood doesn't make a difference. The key to eliminating these vampires is to make them bleed to death. Of course, since they bear some of my genetic pattern, they'll also be able to feed off any human negativity. Luckily, we're in a place set for demolition by 1 PM. The charges are set and in around... ooh... 6 hours and 58 minutes, there won't be enough left of them for an ant to pimp. So, without further ado, let's demonstrate.' Nerezza placed a boot firmly on the subject's shoulder, pinning her to the ground. Once again extending his arm, it slithered to his left, underneath the table and retrieved his scythe... as the arm shrunk back to normal, Nerezza took another beaming chuckle at the camera, before holding the weapon in both hands and swiping it through the air. The body remained still for a second before the fabric of the excuse for a dress began to unravel, alongside the skin and innards of the victim. The sound of trickling was barely heard above the classical music, but still detectable as a river of red ran along the ground, just like the blue corpse that had attracted a gathering of vermin underneath the tarp. 'Now note the fact that due to the lack of blood, she can't regenerate herself. Otherwise, we'd be able to donate those legs to someone trying to break into the adult movie business. So... two down, one to go...' Kicking the two sections of his child away, Nerezza moved his attention to the final one, who had awoken and was already squirming and moaning helplessly. He looked around the garage and inhaled through his nose. The coppery, metallic smell was thick in the air and the Moonlight Sonata seemed to have mixed with it, creating a torturous, slow atmosphere of approaching discomfort. Wiping the bloodstained scythe on possibly the only clean rag in the room, Nerezza smiled almost sweetly at his final victim. His purple hair hung down in two curtains past his chin. The writhing vampire looked up, ready to receive his message. 'You went out onto the streets last night to sell your body, which I have bought. So there's no use complaining. Prostitution isn't exactly legal in this part of the world and although murder is just as forbidden, this would be appropriate justice. Besides...' The heartless beast then walked forward, making his captive inch backwards, one shuffle at a time. The cat-and-mouse game continued until an oil drum jammed against the wall marked the finish line. Nerezza raised his scythe one more time above his head... 'The end justifies the means...' And the tape stopped playing with one final click. Blackout.
