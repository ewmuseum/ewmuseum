Lacking Something....Evil

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.23.02 (#7 PPV) “Ya know, Ray Ray, we haven’t done anything evil in quite a while…” This promo, like so many others, commences with the voice of Rush Mason, sounding thoughtful as he apparently addresses his large manager. “I mean, we haven’t even pulled any acts of deviance or plans to cause minor disturbances!” With that, the scene fades in from black, and we can see Rush Mason and Ray Ray, driving along in Rush’s signature vehicle, a baby blue 4.6 Range Rover. At the helm is Rush, probably because his manager can’t be trusted with any machinery larger than a Micro Machine. Rush: Ya know? The huge low-intelligence life form doesn’t respond, leaving a strange silence in the car. Rush turns for a moment, looking at Ray Ray, and sights that he is sleeping, and a loud snore soon makes that fact public. “Zzzzzz….” Rush keeps his eyes on the freeway for another moment or so, but soon he becomes bored and shakes Ray Ray awake. Ray Ray: Huh? Halle Berry ain’t…. Looking around, the man of large girth comes to the unfortunate realization that he isn’t pleasantly dreaming any longer. He is in the car with an obnoxious and rather moody man. Ray Ray: Dawg, you woke me from a tight dream. However, his employer feels very little sympathy and once again addresses him, asking for more evil ingenuity, although it’s very not likely to have anything intelligent emit from Ray Ray. Rush: While you were wasting time, I was making an important point that I’ve been noticeably un-conniving ever since you came on board. I need to do something repulsive. Seemingly in thought about this, Ray Ray scratches his shaven chin, his eyes up at the sky through the sun roof. Rush waits and looks for an answer, but is met with only more silence, and then yells at the manager again. Rush: Well…? You’re the stereotype here. Tell me how to ‘Fuck, Fuck, Fuck Tha Police!’ Ray Ray finally turns to Rush, finally paying attention. He then shoots a question out of the blue, as he definitely wasn’t paying attention thus far. Ray Ray: You say somethin’? Smacking his forehead, Rush makes his point very clear as he shouts into Ray Ray’s left ear. His tone is rather annoyed, as he is repeating himself for the third time in the few minutes that we have witnessed in this promo. Rush: I need something malicious to do. All this not doing any evil….I could be falling into Tweenerdom. *gasp* I could be turning into…KANO!! Of course, there is no such devastating action occurring, but this certainly grabs Ray Ray’s attention. He scrunches up his face, thinking hard, at least for his miniscule brain, but comes up blank, shrugging as he replies to Rush. Ray Ray: Uhh…I dunno. Maybe….beat up some white people? Sighing again loudly, Rush gives up asking Ray Ray about evil actions for the moment, and continues to pry as to why an NWA-supporting stereotype would not know how to cause even a small ruckus. Rush: You mean that you call yourself an indignantly stereotypical black man and don’t even know how to start a simple riot or mug innocent bystanders at all? In response, Ray Ray shrugs. He then slowly replies, making sure to choose his words carefully as to not seem like too much of a coward in front of Rush. Most likely, the Voodoo Villain doesn’t find Ray Ray the exact picture of courage, but meh. Ray Ray: I….uhh…not really the criminal type, ya dig? Rush raises an eyebrow, as if intrigued by what “not the criminal type” might mean. However, before Ray Ray can continue with his explanation, Mason cuts in with another question. Rush: So, you’ve never committed a crime in your life? Gulping, Ray Ray responds in what he hopes will only be a small loss of face. His voice seems timid, very much unlike his usual pronouncements with such intellectually stimulating vigor. Ray Ray: No. I committed a crime once. I had a book from the library and I never ‘turned it. Uhh….fuck those mofos….yo? The sentence trails off, sounding more like a question as it ends, and Ray Ray’s face looks quite strange. He tries to make a smile, but also has a look of uncertainty upon him. Rush: You didn’t return a book? A misdemeanor? With that, our hero goes off into a rampant cackling storm, and just barely keeps control of the car, as Ray Ray sighs regrettably. Rush finally, after a few hilarious moments, brings his head up, and looks at his model citizen manager. Ray Ray: You know what they say, crime doesn’t pay. Rush grins widely, and gives a small roll of the eyes, while the perhaps not so uncomfortably stereotypically man tries to avert the conversation away from his lack of felonious behavior. Ray Ray: So….what about Alliances? You’ve got it in the bag, right? Immediately changing moods to re-assure Ray Ray of his impending victory at the Alamodome, Rush sounds boastful and arrogant as he relays his prediction of how events will unfold on Sunday night. Rush: Sunday, November 24th. It shall become a historic moment in PCW history. Rush Mason will become the number one contender in the first step towards my hall of fame World Title reign. Ray Ray nods, apparently liking the way that Rush’s plan seems to work out. His eyes grow large as he thinks of the food that the manager of a World Champion would get to have backstage. He then continues to massage his employer’s ego, his mouth watering at the thought of delicious steaks before every Turmoil and Fusion. Ray Ray: So, you gonna steal it from Kannon and Akiyama? As the words roll of Ray Ray’s mouth, Rush’s eyes grow bright with what seems to be an idea. His face lights up, and he lifts up a finger, as he swerves to turn off the freeway. Rush: Steal? Bag? Ray Ray, don’t you get it? I know the perfect evil act! His manager’s face looks uncertain yet again, as he fears the answer of his question, but doesn’t even have to ask, as Rush reveals the answer to him before he can ask. Rush: We can rob a bank! Ray Ray gulps in fear, and waits for Rush to give him a friendly pat on the back and say ‘Just kidding, buddy.’ Ray Ray: Uhh…Rush, that’s kinda against the law. Scratching his chin, Rush thinks it over and does have to agree that robbing a bank is in fact, quite against federal law. He is seemingly stuck, but then Ray Ray throws another better suggestion. Ray Ray: Ya know, I got a hitman friend. I ain’t totally white boy. We could go see him and see if he’s got any- Rush interjects, cutting off his manager. Rush: Relatively legal, yet nasty and distasteful enough to make me feel positively evil again?! Fantastic idea! Ray Ray was going to suggest get a sandwich, but he just nods, and Rush drives off in the direction of Ray Ray’s dictation of directions while the scene fades into black, while we are left with the last of their conversation as it hangs in the air… “This friend is pretty evil, eh?” “Think Alan Kriegman…but he can make a mean sandwich…”
