The Chases (double meaning!)

Date: March Song listening guide: 'Scar Tissue' by the Red Hot Chili Peppers. Because I just got that Californication on CD. =) BOBBY STORMBRINGER The Chases Without a doubt, the chase is on. All of Asylum are in hot pursuit. Goals that must be met, they will be met. Failure would end in travesty. Despite the cliche' saying, failure is almost always an option. The point is that none of them want to think about the alternatives. None of them want to even contemplate or consider what would go down if they did not fulfill their personal objectives. No person on the planet wants to walk further into their lives with regrets. Actions and inaction that will never be changed. Some things are too life-altering in the most negative of ways to let happen. So let's get a little more specific. Nah... a lot more specific. * * * --2-26-02, 4:51 pm Central time, two hours until PCW Turmoil-- The scene fades up into the sight of an unusually reserved and relaxed Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer, sitting at a local Milwaukee tavern with a pen in his hand, scribbling some kind of doodle onto a large napkin. B.S. is wearing an old pair of blue jeans, his white and blue T-mac sneakers, an old gray (or is that 'grey'?) timex, a pair of trusty, custom sunglasses with three straight streaks of lightning running across the lenses, and of all things that you'd never expect.... A black and red New Breed t-shirt!? So B.S. sometimes reverts back to 'fan mode' and 'marks out' for Jason Snow and Corey Williams, in particular. Big deal! What's going to happen, are Asylum going to throw him out of the faction? Um, no. So shaddup. Besides, B.S. still has no qualms about destroying either one of the cocky bastids at any given time. Any given time may just be March Madness. The camera pans away and shows that the pub's patrons are scarce at the moment in this non-prime time, non-happy hour time of day. We also notice that Carl Everstrong, The Showstopper's personal assistant, is nowhere to be seen. If B.S. could hear us he would no doubt explain that Carl is a valuable, hard-working hand that is presently needed elsewhere. Suddenly, B.S. thows his free arm off and lazily flicks off the.. camera.. behind him- now wait a minute. That wasn't in the script we agreed upon, you ungrateful- Yours truly, the narrator/handler is apparently... *continues to read off a sheet with the pre-written promo on it, and he sighs, defeated* Uhh. The narrator is cut off by B.S., as the flipping of the bird was meant for him. So you can hear us. Okay. I'm guessing that's a pretty damn strange way of tell me that Carl's gone for another reason..? NOT TO MENTION BREAKING YOUR KAYFABE!! STORMBRINGER: Psst.. hey, jackass.. Carl isn't here because I can't spend hour after hour talking to that freggin' goofball. I needed time away. Now here I have you to tolerate. Perfect. Juuuusst perfect. *sighs* Fine, Marty McFly, whatevah. Be like that. A few ruffians are playing darts on the far end, while a few more hard-workin' folks are just playing a little pool. Playing pool rather badly, but at least they are having some good, clean fun. Just about everyone else, however, are here doing what they are actually supposed to be doing. Gettin' down and gettin' drunk! YEEEE-HAW! STORMBRINGER: Dude, shaddup! Are you high on something?? Pheh. So much for the good clean fun. Or bad, dirty fun- doesn't matter so long as it's fun! The bartender wanders over to B.S. who is still scribbling away. Bartender on his normal side, while B.S. sits on the high barstool indian-style somehow, naturally on the other side reserved for the patrons. STORMBRINGER: Didn't Walden do a freggin' promo like this, what... yesterday? Damnit, jerky, if you don't stop I won't type out your second one for the GTT! STORMBRINGER: Alright, alright! Geez. Tightwad. Now stop making my promos look just like Green, already. (Narrator's note: That ungrateful buttmunch means The End..) *grumbles and seems to quickly count to ten* The bartender.... you see!? You made me lose my train of thought! Oh yeah, the bartender is wearing a white apron, a clean one for once. He wears a tan shirt with the bar's brown company logo high on the chest. STORMBRINGER: Damnit man. Do you really think anyone cares about what a peasant is wearing?? Okay, that's it! You want a time lapse, ya big baby? Then you got a time lapse! We'll come back when you stop breaking the rules like a moron. You moron. STORMBRINGER: Pfft. Some 'benevolent creator' you a- * * * --2-26-02, 5:01 pm Central time, two hours until PCW Turmoil-- Screw the description, this chump doesn't deserve it. Go ahead, you think you're so perfect, let's see you do this without me. STORMBRINGER: Who's breaking the all-important kayfabe now?? If you interrupt me again I'll smash your little face in, bele' dat. So forget about your fat, helpless bartender that looks too much like Carl! The bartender that has been standing there in limbo for nearly six or seven minutes through the time lapse has just been just wiping out an already washed mug with a rather unsanitary-looking gray towel all this time, staring at B.S. with crazy, stuck-in-time eyes. Finally, he wanders off to cackle and guffaw with the joker at the rusty pinball machine. Bobby, how did you- STORMBRINGER: I am The Showstopper. I stop the show. Promos and in the ring. It's in my contract. Bah~! Bobby freggin' Stormbringer gives yours truly a smile and a wink.. the kind of smile and wink that comes from sardonic, mentally unbalanced mother f- *MARCH SMACKNESS!!* -kers, OW!! STORMBRINGER: This is my promo now, alone, so I suggest you just... get the hell outta here. No really. I mean it. *Robert Wilkerson then gets literally kicked in the *ss, and the more sensitive, slightly more insecure handler/narrator leaves just as the massive sobbing begins* STORMBRINGER: Good! I'm more sick of that guy hanging around than Everstrong, I swear to you all. So my objective tonight... before my idiot 'director' wasted all sorts of time... was to talk about the current state and direction of Asylum. No.. none of us are standing still at all. I mean look at these promos on the PTC Channel! Jeremy Howard is trying like hell to get his son back, and soon his brother.. why after all this time, I dunno.. but, I mean, at least he's showing good inititive. Better late than never. Poor, poor Maxwell. I heard that the odds of him surviving his match at the Pay-Per-View are only up at Vegas to fifteen-to-one. What a sucker this guy is! Perhaps he should of been more careful about what he wanted for Christmas, and not a fued with Krayzie. Not to mention the rest of Asylum battering him about like a cheap version of Pamela Anderson. That was kinda redundant, but you get what I mean. Then there is Miya. At least I always just call 'im 'Miya', so I'm in the clear. I agree with Sam, Steve, whatevah his buddy's name is. Isawa is pretty good, but I've also always liked that name, Alexander. If I ever had a daughter..? Alexandria. Aw yeah, heh, that rules. C'mon, damnit, you know it does. Then there is Xavier. Uh.. sh!t, what's he chasin' after.. oh I know! The right to shill Snapple~! instead of that evil witch of a valet, ELLIE~! Hey, this is fun, no wonder HANK~! does this so much. Natural redheads~!! Yeeah. Right on. Ooo, look at all the pretty tildes. *screams at B.S. from outside the closed door to the tavern about the kayfabe again, not to mention actually wrapping the promo up sometime today, but is seemingly ignored* STORMBRINGER: So, er, what was I talking about? Oh, Xavier. Plus he is fighting for the right to shill Snapple~! in Canada! Damn you, Chase. Just... damn you. I mean Vampir and Clyde have done mighty low things in the past, but.... Then of course, there's me. Your Mr. Prime Time. I don't care if I have to travel from OSW to CCW. From GWF, to NTICW, and back. I will find out every name that was involved in setting me up, for giving me a concussion. For trying to get me locked away, at least for a few nights. It's about the principle at this point. Just gotta get home.. gotta get home. Besides, I haven't seen Kira for two weeks.... *muffled, from outside* B.S. lowers his head and trails off, not wanting to share his truest feelings for- STORMBRINGER: Stop it!! ........... STORMBRINGER: Asylum.. we're chasing something else. Chasing a TV Title record. Chasing a Title Sweep or two. Chasing a Continental Championship. Chasing a couple freak-punks who need to learn a thing or two about not disregarding the wrong people. Chasing the World Heavyweight title. A whole bunch of us get to do that last one. It's not just the result. It's the chase. I really can't tell you if I can become World Champion so soon. After so long... But I can tell you that the ride itself should be fun. I won't be worried about that Drake Maxwell, not on that night. Jeremy's got that covered, for the most part. Barring the screwjobs and SWERVE~! and all, of course. K-Dub, Nelson, New Breed, Boda, Skylar... this should be real good for The Showstopper, virtual clusterf*ck or not! For those who still haven't learned yet. Still haven't looked above their empty heads... they will feel The Perfect Storm, after a perfect forecast for St. Louis. And baby... that sure as hell is no... b... s! Hmph. Alright, get back in here and do your job, R-Dub. I'm gonna go shoot some pool before I get to the show. ....Ahh, finally. What a jerk, eh..? I think he poked me in the flippin' eye on that last shot. Anyway, yes, B.S. goes to take some teeny-boppers to school in the ways of billiards. We zoom in on that napkin that B.S. was writing on, as the nosy bastids we are. As we get close enough we can see that it's.. It is a wonderfully accurate profile of NTICW's Aimz. Oh great, this again. It is colorless, except the hair has been meticlously colored in with a red pen, the outline of all of it done with a red pen. The detail is quite astounding. Yet curious, obviously. We switch over to B.S. on the other side of the bar, smirking in all his glory next to a guy and a lady in their late teens, seemingly disappointed. BOY TEEN: Alrighty, dude, you got that close ball thingy or whatever it is. You break. LADY TEEN: Don't choke now, guy. A whole thirty bucks on the line! The blonde girl giggles annoyingly as B.S. leans over the pool table slightly, with pool cue in hand. Only a half-smirk develops this time in the corner of his mouth for how little thirty dollars means to him at this point, like a drop in the bucket. Competition, however, is still competition. Will Asylum be able to fulfill their personal goals while not being distracted long enough to get the job done in the ring? Why can't B.S. force Aimz out of his head, even though he is completely and utterly head over heels for Kira York? Why does B.S. have to come home to Orlando to tell us just who was the first of three men to ambushed him like an animal?? Just how will this revolutionary but new PCW superstar fare on the wrestling world's biggest stage!? I would actually answer the questions this time, honest. But first, B.S. hits a vicious break on the white cue ball and the instant it strikes the other multi-colored balls in the middle of the table, we cut straight to black. THIS HAS BEEN A B.S.
