Scientologists think better then the rest. Duke joins the club.

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/3/02 -Duke Williams sits in a large auditorium in the heart of Hubbardville, Wisconsin. Duke’s home away from home. Filling up the room are about two-hundred members of the scientology community. At the podium is one of the masters of Scientology religion. He begins to speak in a gracious and gentle manor as only a Scientologist can.- Speaker: Tonight we welcome a new member into our family. After completing TR9: Tone 40 with bullbait. Duke Williams is now officially a Scientologist after he offers up his Big Win speech in front of all of you. I am proud to have the honor to introduce to you the newest…… -Before the con-artist at the podium can bring Duke up to speak a fat fellow with some artificial hair runs in flapping his arms like a duck headed south for the winter. The humorous bovine interrupts the whole ceremony with a new allegation.- FatFellow: Mr. Speaker I need to interrupt, some new information from with in the community has challenged this induction. -Some grumbles are heard through out the audience. FatFellow: Duke motives and character is under suspicion from a dominant member of this community. Speaker: I see. That leaves me only one option to discover Dukes motives in this whole process…..lets bring out the E-Meter!!!! -The crowd starts to cheer wildy.- Crowd: E-Meter…E-Meter….E-Meter…E-meter!!! Speaker: With that lets bring out the machine itself along with our special guest meter reader the great John Travolta!! -Two lovely ladies dressed in glittery clothing push out the E-Meter like it’s a prize on the Price is Right. The crowd is going ape shit and their hoots and hollers increase as Travolta enters doing a little bit of a dance and waving to his peers.- Speaker: Duke Williams its time for an E-Meter test come on down!! -Some music is cued up as Duke walks up to the stage, shakes John’s hand, and sits down in the chair.- Speaker: We need complete silence from the crowd. -The crowd on command goes quite.- Travolta: Duke I need you to hold on two these two sticks. Williams: John I don’t know you that well. -A spotlight shines on the two men. Duke is handed two metal cylinder that connect to a machine that looks like a speedometer mixed with a cheap radio on a bad episode of the original Star Trek.- Travolta : Answer the questions with yes and no answers only. Williams: Yes Travolta: Are you Duke Williams? Williams: Yes Travolta: True. Are you know as the “Malice Man”? Williams: Yes Travolta: True. Was Pulp Fiction one of your favorite movies? Williams: Yes. Travolta: True!! Good. Was battle field earth? Williams: Yes. -Duke starts to sweat as he answers that question. – Travolta: True. I mean who doesn’t love that movie!! WEEEEEEEEE!! -Duke looks at the machine with a smirk.- Travolta: Are your motives for joining the Scientology religion sincere and pure? Duke: Yes. Travolta: He’s lying. -The crowd starts to boo.- Travolta: How could you man. -The speaker is about to rule when a man walk in with a check in his hand.- Speaker: Mr. Williams’s check for 30,000 dollars has just cleared are you sure that you read the machine right John?? Travolta: Hold on let me double check. -JT flips a switch and comes up with a big smile on his face.- Travolta: Whoops looks like I didn’t turn on the Elmo switch. Its is true he is sincere!! -The audience cheers wildly as the lights come back on.- Speaker: Thank you John, and thank you Duke!! -John exits to the theme to “Welcome Back Kottor” he dances his way out to the cheering crowd.- Speaker: Mr. Williams you may now present us with your big win speech. -Duke walks up to the stage in a suit and tie and no sun glasses or hat. – Williams: Thank you it’s an honor to speak in front of my new family. I admit when I first heard of Scientology I thought it was a scam or just pure bunk. After reading the readings of L. Ron Hubbard I have changed my opinion one hundred percent. The only way this world will ever survive and take mankind to that next level is if we all study the words of our wise founder. No race, no creed, no background, just Scientology. Man working hand and hand with man to create a better more advance world. No war, no sickness, maybe even no death. WE can create this and achive this by getting the words of Hubbard out to the world. The older folk out there are lost in their Christianity and Crunk. Our army of goodness and intelligence comes from the youth of this world. We need to recruit them to our ways at a young age before this world corrupts them. -The audience listens attentively.- Williams: What interests the kids better then pro-wrestling??? Now I know my good friend Xavior Kannon. My hero!! The man who brought Scientology to me is carrying that torch. The only problem with that is the mass population does not like him. I however am the most popular wrestler in the PCW and the world. I’ve been on a cereal box for Hubbards sake. Not only am I a wrestling star but also the host of a popular radio show that all the kids listen too. My goal is not to take Kannon’s role but to aid him in his mission of educating the world to the ways of Scientology. This Friday on Fusion I must battle a non- Scientology by the name of Frisco. This man is a bean picking anti-Hubbard with pure evil in his heart. He laughs at Scientology and all its principles. HE SPITS IN THE FACE OF HUBBARD!!! -The crowd boo’s Frisco.- Williams: But fear not. My good friend Kannon has done what any good Scientologist would do and that’s get involved. Oh yes what a guy!! Just for the sake of Scientology this man puts himself in the match as a special referee!! Now I know its true when they say us Scientologist think at a higher level then those who are not pure. With Kannon giving me the upper hand WE can have a guaranteed Scientologist versus Scientologist Pay-Per-View match at Supremacy. Is that a bad thing?? NO. Think of it this way. Every fan in that stadium will be cheering for a Scientologist! It’s a win/win situation. Then when we give the crowd the best damn match of the night they too will know and understand the strengths and benefits of becoming a Scientologist. After the Pay-Per-View I will shake Mr. Kannon’s hand win or lose and then we both will refocus to see how to spread the word even farther. But step one Kannon, MUST do the RIGHT thing and count the one-two-three on that venomous Frisco. Then Scientology will rule all!!! -The crowd goes wild and rushes the podium in the practice know as the love bomb after a member completes training and his big win speech.- Man: We love you man!! Man2: You can Kannon will rule the PCW!! Man3: Kannon is my good friend and he will do the right thing. Williams: I’m sure he will why would he risk his religious acceptance? Man3: He’s one of us to the core I know he will help you brother Duke. -Duke shakes hands on his way out of the love bomb till he reaches the door where oddly enough a PCW camera crew is standing by waiting for Duke.- PCWGuy: What are your feelings about Xavior Kannon placing himself in your match for the number one contender spot for his title?? Williams: My simpleton friend I have no fear that my brother in Hubbard will do the right thing for Scientology. I love Xavior Kannon he is my hero. He has turned me on to the ways of Scientology and for that I will always be thankful. Together as Scientologists we will put forth the greatest PPV match PCW has ever seen and everybody will see that PCW can get it done Hubbard Style!!!! -Duke gives the camera a smirk and a wink to his fans.- Williams: Ain’t that right Kannon. -Duke laughs like a mad man as he heads for his pick truck which sticks out like a sore thumb in the cluster of BMW’s and Cadillac’s. The camera fades on Duke Williams speeding away.-
