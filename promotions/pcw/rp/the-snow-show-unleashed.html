Date: Season II, Episode 2 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason II, Episode 2“Unleashed”************************************ Location: Snow Residence, New York City SuburbsDate: February 3, 2003Time: 1:50 pm Into small jagged shards, the tall empty drinking glass exploded against the far wall of thekitchen, opposite the table. Most of the glass fell into the kitchen sink, which was directly belowthe point of impact, thought some shards flew in every direction and broke into even smallerpieces on the floor. Snow’s eyes snapped across at his sister-in-law, Krista, who looked fearfully back at him. Sheknew that he would be angry, but she was starting to question her decision to finally confronthim about it. The silence had gone on long enough, she thought. “I’ve got enough fucking problems,” he told her sharply. “Believe me, I’ll deal with this andthat little cocksucker ‘father’ in good time. For now, I don’t even want you leaving the fuckinghouse!” “You can’t just ignore it!” she retorted as she burst into tears. “I’m not ignoring it!” he screamed. “I’m just trying to give myself time to cool off - and that’sfor your benefit! Believe me, if it weren’t for your sister, Verrelli would already be afucking corpse!” “I love him!” Krista shrieked, laying her head down in her arms. “Well, that’s nice to know,” Snow said a little quieter, but the edge was still sharpening. Hisbreath was getting hotter with his face. It was a shot aimed with bad intentions, and he knew ithit the mark. Krista sulked in her arms. Compassion stood no chance against the stubborn wall in Snow’s mind. “You’re nineteen years old,” he told her through clenched teeth as if informing her of somethingshe hadn’t realized. “What in the hell are you going to do with a baby?” “No one said I was going to have the baby!” Krista snapped quite suddenly, lifting herhead from her tear-soaked forearms. She knew immediately, even before she said it, that theargument was over. There would be no further discussing anything with her brother-in-law. Hisface reddened and his jaw tightened to the point of pain. Krista had momentarily forgotten about her sister’s miscarriage last summer. She only forgotfor a second. Snow’s eyes attacked her as violent as they were able while sitting in their sockets. Suddenly, he rose from the table and hurled it across the room. It struck the far wall’s lowcupboards, and three of its legs broke loose from the impact. Snow felt no remorse for his sudden outburst. “Did he put that shit in your head!? Did he!?” Snow screamed, eight inches away fromhis sister-in-law’s terrified face. He didn’t even care now that Raven was out of the house forthe afternoon - he would have done the same thing even if she had been sitting right next to him.“You know I’m going to fucking kill that little cocksucker, don’t you!? Is that what youfucking wanted!? Is it!?” Krista couldn’t say anything. She began to tremble violently under the pressure of her brother-in-law’s rage, and the tears began to gush as if they were collectively busting through a dam. Her face went very pale instead of turning red, like most faces do while crying. She wasabsolutely terrified. As much as she loved the man looking in her face, she knew that beneath,and inside, all of his protection and love for her, there lurked a monster consumed by a morepowerful hatred than she had ever heard of. She also knew that his threat on Marty Verrelli’s life - her boyfriend’s life - was far from empty. The beast inside Jason Snow was in full control now, and the caring and gentle man that heusually was was utterly and completely at the beast’s mercy. She could only guess what he wascapable of in a moment like this. When he stomped out of the kitchen, she was relieved that at least he didn’t go to the door andexit the house. She was horrified, however, when she saw him put the living room telephone onthe floor and stomp it until it was a mess of broken plastic and wires. Despite her franticprotests, he did the same to both phones upstairs. He even spent a couple of minutes searching for the cell phone before he realized that his wife,Raven had it on her shopping trip. When he turned toward the door, he found that Krista hadalready run through it, toward his Blazer on the outside. She planned to get there before he did,so she could drive to Verrelli’s house, leaving Snow stranded at home until he cooled down. She didn’t realize that Raven had one set of keys with her, and the other set was in the backpocket of Snow’s jeans. With burning eyes, he stalked outside. Krista was smarter than he gave her credit for - she locked herself inside the truck. Snow, however, was angrier than Krista had given him credit for - he smashed thedriver’s side window with one quick shot of his elbow. Glass fell over Krista’s lap as shefrantically screamed for her brother-in-law to cool down. There was no hope of that. In a moment of clarity, she regretted what she had earlier said to Snow. “Jason!” she screamed. “Please! Don’t do this!” She was wailing now and couldn’t seeanything through the burning tears. “Don’t do this! I love him! I love him!” She didn’tknow what else to say. Snow was unmoved. He mercilessly threw the door open and pulled her from the inside. He was rough with her, andhad her pregnancy not been stuck in the back of his mind, he probably would have been rougher. She landed on her knees in the driveway and she stayed there, crying helplessly and frantically,“Please!” she wailed over and over. He couldn’t even hear her anymore - it had already become a background noise. A moment later, he was gone, and Krista was left alone in the driveway, still crying loudly. “Please!” she cried once more, before finally just collapsing into a heap. She stayed outside forquite some time, weeping helplessly to herself. Her tears dropped from her cheeks and chin tothe driveway, where the puddles froze to the pavement. *** Location: Marty Verrelli’s Apartment, New York CityDate: February 3, 2003Time: 2:30 pm He only knocked on the door once. It was more of a punch than a knock - but the boom that itmade was sure to get Verrelli’s attention. He’ll probably bring a gun to the door, Snow thoughtwith a smirk, and he was right. Marty Verrelli, tall and handsome as he was, stared terrified into the horrible blue eyes of JasonSnow. There was a gun in his right hand. Unfortunately for the young man, he was too stunnedby Snow’s look of sheer hatred to aim it. His hesitation cost him dearly. Snow quickly grabbed Marty’s wrist and with a hard twist, disarmed the young man. “Don’t you remember what I told you was going to happen the next fucking time you pulled agun on me, cocksucker!?” Snow asked through clenched teeth as he grabbed Verrelli’s collarand pulled him close. “You’re getting really fucking good at pissing me off!” Snow wasscreaming now, but even in the middle of his fury, he remembered to close the apartment door -just in case things got messy. With terrifying force, Snow pushed Marty Verrelli away and the young man stumbled onto hisback. “Listen, Jason,” Marty began to beg, “I can fucking explain, man! This is about Krista, Iknow! And we fucked up, that’s true, but...” He was silenced by a stiff kick in the mouth. “You’re right,” Snow said as he leaned in close. His eyes were burning now, and his unshavenface seemed to lose all of its handsome features. “You fucked up!” Snow kicked Marty hard inthe ribs and the young man toppled over on his side, coughing and spitting blood from the gashon the inside of his lower lip. “Calm down!” yelled Marty from the floor as he tried to regain his footing. “Too fucking late for that!” Snow snapped as he picked a lamp up from beside the sofa andhurled it against the floor next to Marty. “My fucking sister-in-law is pregnant and youwant me to calm down!” Snow reached down and roughly dragged Marty to his feet. Marty was horrified by the strengthof the angered man but was helpless to fight against it. A second time, Snow pulled Marty closeenough to feel the young man’s breath in his face. “Let me fucking tell you something, Verrelli!” he hissed. “I’ve been looking out for that kid forthe better part of a fucking decade!” “She’s not a kid!” argued Marty with his bloody mouth. “What the fuck do you know about her!?” Snow bursted as he shook the Marty violently. “Whatthe fuck do you know about anything!? I tried for years to keep her away from cocksuckers likeyou! And now I find out that you got her pregnant!” Overwhelmed by rage, Snow backhanded Marty. The impact of the blow sent him back to thefloor. “And then,” Snow continued, “now she’s starting this shit about not having the baby! I’llfucking kill you for that!” Marty looked surprised, but only for a moment - it only took that long for Snow to crack himwith a hard right hand. A second followed, and then a third. Soon, Snow found himself pinningMarty to the floor and pounding punch after punch into the young man’s open face. Bloodsplashed onto the floor with every blow. “Please!” Marty begged. His face was a liquid mask of crimson and his short black hair wasnow completely soaked. Cuts and gashes ran all over his face and he was sure that his nose wasbroken, in at least one place. Every shot knocked him a bit further from reality, which explainedwhy each shot hurt a little less. “Time to fucking die!” Snow hissed at him as he sent a final right hand crashing into Marty’sbusted face. After that, he turned his attention to the young man’s throat. Despite Marty’sshrieking protest, Snow wrapped his strong hands around his victim’s neck and began squeezingout his life. For Marty, every second was a minute of excruciating pain. For Snow, every minute was a second of rage and hate. *** Location: Snow Residence, New York CityDate: February 3, 2003Time: 2:50 pm She felt dead. Her eyes were wept dry and left burning. Her stomach was numb from the constant turning. Krista was like another blanket on her bed, staring lifelessly up at the ceiling of her room. Howcould things have gotten so horrible, she wondered a final time. It felt like an hour before shefinally blinked.
