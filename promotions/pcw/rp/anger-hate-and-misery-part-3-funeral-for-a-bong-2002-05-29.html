Anger, Hate, and Misery - Part 3 - Funeral For A Bong

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 29 [The scene opens on a broken in half Pittsburgh Steelers helmet. The painted on yellow signature that reads 'Bongus' is broken in two, separated from the tries and tribulations of it's use. The face mask and mouthpiece are cracked and missing pieces. The gloomy afternoon doesn't help this sad, sad moment. The sky turning gray and the wind picking up only adds to the mourning that one 'Heavy Metal' Harry feels at this moment. Atop a secluded grass hill, Harry looks amazingly handsome for the first time in his life. His long brown hair is pulled back in a pony tail, his goatee has been trimmed and doesn't contain crumbs of the food he had the night before, and his Twisted Sister T-shirt has been replaced by an Armani suit and expensive Gucci dress shoes, with were borrowed from his best friend and tag team partner, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. Harry's eyes are puffy and red, but not from smokin' the refer, but from the tears that run down his cheek. He looks down to the hole he dug and can't help but let the tears flow from him] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Jerome Bongus was always there for me, no matter what, man. He brought me things in my life that nobody else could bring, not even Jessica Alba, man. It was an invention that surpassed all the others. Bongified, Bongladesh, Bong the Schlong...none of them could ever live up to one of the greatest bongs of all time. You inspired other great bongs like Jerry Bongice, Drew Bongsoe, and Tony Bongagusa. You can be proud, Bongus, because you were the first and the innovater of a new bong nation, man. You took the bong world by the balls and said 'I'm far out, man!' You were truly great in every aspect of getting high. You've aided in clouding many minds of women who soon had hot sex with me, man! I WILL TRULY CHERISH THAT!! TRULY, MAN!! [Harry begins letting out a few sobs as he pulls out a piece of notepad paper which looks like it's been through a war. Snot stains and nacho cheese are spread here and there throughout the note, but Harry doesn't seem to really care at all] Harry: I wrote you a poem, man. I call it...'The Bongification of Proclamation' The Bongification of Proclamationby 'Heavy Metal' Harry Joy is but a three letter wordSo, this I've heardIt's joy that you give my heartEnough to always make a new start I've never met someone like youI hope you feel the same tooNobody understands the bond we shareBut we will smoke and just not care Remember that time we made loveYou were like a sweet little doveNow that I think, it was not youIt was that chick from Scorpion King, named Kelly Hu My brain is fried beyond all beliefI need you now so I can have true reliefThe man one TV says Dial 1-800-Call ATTBut I'd much rather take a giant pee Star Wars kicks ass In so many waysI can't wait until Natalie Portman gets nakedI'm counting the days Snot fills this poem nowI wish I could tell you howBut that could take a yearTo make it seem so clear I'll wipe my noseOn the rubber hoseAnd eat nachos and cheeseWith the greatest of ease Joy is but a three letter wordBut Bong is a four letter wordDon't take a wiz on the electric fenceAs my poem makes absolutely no f**king sense Harry: ... The End. [Harry then begins to breakdown like a little child. He leans over the broken helmet that was one a beautiful bong, and hugs it one last time] Harry: NOOOO!! God NO, MAN!!! [As Harry pours his heart out to his greatest creation ever, a man watches from the distance. The scene pans over to another small grade of hill, just a little bit above where Harry is burying his bong. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, decked out in all black, stands beside his blue 2002 Mustang convertible. His bright blonde hair doesn't really look so bright anymore and neither do his blue eyes. With the collar of the black trenchcoat protecting his neck from the wind, Tommy seems to be in another place] *** Harry is one sad compadre, but he needs to learn that it wasn't me that did this bogusness, but the heinous dudes who are holding us down. Harry, your going to have to understand that the only way to shine, even in bogusness like this, you must take your destiny in your own hands. I have an Extreme Title match on Friday, and it's something that I feel is totally heinous in a bogus kind of way, and I will win it. Is it my bodacious destiny to become PCW Extreme Champion? No, it's not but I can always do that on the way to my true destiny and that's the PCW Tag Team Titles. *** [Tommy stops his thoughts as he watches Harry begin to lower the football helmet in the hole] *** The bogus forces of PCW have always found ways to hold us down. While in NTICW I was climbing the ladder, on my way to becoming not only one of the greatest NTICW wrestlers, but a LEGEND of NTICW. What did PCW do? They offered me a most radical contract, shining the bright light directly into my face. What did I do? I just signed without even a heinous thought in my head. Instead of becoming a singles wrestler and dominating every singles division, they put me with my best friend to help make the tag team division a much more bodacious place. We succeeded in doing that, but what did we get for it? We got the BOGUS SHAFT!! Every corner we turned we were screwed out of our rightful shots at the tag team gold. Xavier Kannon saves PI, and Tyler Nelson, that BOGUS, HEINOUS, BOGUS STUPID MORON TOOK OUR NUMBER ONE CONTENDERSHIP AWAY!! *** [Tommy's rage begins to heighten to an all time high. He begins slamming his fists into the hood of his very expensive and very new Mustang. Dents begin forming immediately, benind inward toward the engine. Tommy then moves from the hood to the wheels, kicking the chrome rims and dark black tires] *** The only thing that those bogus dudes care about is keeping their spot. They'll do anything in their power so that they can keep their HEINOUS status as great wrestlers!! Well, isn't that just so awesome or radical of you to do?!?! To just push The Cowabunga Connection under a f**king rug so that you all can keep your f**king little bogus f**king high status?!?! Well, F**K THAT!!! *** [Tommy then grabs a large rock, about the size of a basketball, and rams it into the windshield of his very own Mustang. The windshield is completely shattered, looking like millions of spiderwebs intangled together. Tommy breathes heavily, satisfying his anger and now realizing what he has done. When he should feel upset and sorry that he did what he did, he smiles and begins to laugh] *** What am I worried about? I'm rich, I can just buy a new Mustang if I want. I truly am TOTALLY TUBULAR!! *** [Tommy then looks back down to see that Harry didn't have the heart to bury the broken helmet. Harry wears what is left of Jerome Bongus, but unfortunately its only half of a helmet with a hanging chinstrap] Harry: It's staying with me forever, man. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: It's most cool, dude. Harry: I'm going to miss him, man!!! [Harry collapses into Tommy's arms, crying little a small child. Tommy, acting like a strong willed mother, pats Harry on the head as he stares into the gloomy California sky with hate in his eyes] *** Don't worry, Harry, we will surely gain revenge against these, dudes. Anyone who has wronged us in our careers, and even our lives are going to pay a most bogus price for their sins. We will reign Tubular and it's only a matter of time before we truly breakout from the invisible arms holding us down, compadre *** [The scene then fades to black]
