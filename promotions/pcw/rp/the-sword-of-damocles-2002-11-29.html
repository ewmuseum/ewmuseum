The Sword Of Damocles

29 November 2002

Title: The Sword Of Damocles Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 29/11/02 “Then he chanced to raise his eyes toward the ceiling. What was it that was dangling above him, with it's point almost touching his head? It was a sharp sword, and it was hung by only a single horsehair. What if the hair should break? There was danger every moment that it would do so.” Dionysius, eyes perpetually creeping up to cast a gaze at the ceiling. Knowing that far above his head there was something hanging that he could not do anything about... But could equally not lose face my moving. Now, detached from myth and in the real world and present time, there is a man who feels the same ‘burden’. “Say what you will,” mutters Xavier in monotone, seemingly bitter at the implied accusation, “Something cannot hang above you once you’ve surpassed it.” When Xavier looks up, he sees no sword. The reflection he sees is not that of a Blacksmith’s forging, but the light reflected off the lenses of Black sunglasses... Sunglasses that angry eyes once stared at him through. But Kannon will never admit it, he’d just dismiss it with lines like, “I rid myself, and PCW of him.”, trying to imagine the sword away. “He’s gone, forgotten.” would follow but not ring any truer. “There once was a king whose name was Dionysius. He was so unjust and cruel that he won himself the name of tyrant. He knew that almost everybody hated him, and so he was always in dread lest some one should take his life.” Sound familiar? Even the most cold hearted and incompassionate human beings must have something to keep them in check, something that even once disposed off hangs above them to cast a shadow on their everyday business. “What was his is now mine,” the Scientologist can now boast, “For I shall show I can do a better job.” In trying to out do someone every day of your life, you are their prisoner. In replicating someone’s achievements you’re never leaving their shadow. “I see no sword, I see no horse’s hair.” he will deny, for Xavier’s Sword of Damocles takes on a very different form, “There is nothing that troubles my open and liberated mind.” All the Dianetics courses his vast fortune could buy can’t remove the dark cloud hanging above him every day when he wakes up, and every night when he passes into sleep. There is no horse hair, just a long Black mane to border the dark glasses. 'Yes,' said Dionysius, 'I know there is a sword above your head, and that it may fall at any moment. But why should that trouble you? I have a sword over my head all the time. I am every moment in dread lest something may cause me to lose my life.' No literal sword shall fall and no blade shall pierce the skull of Xavier Kannon, and he will never fear for his life with the number of security he employs... But there will forever be that spectre dangling over him, that itch he cannot reach and that craving he will never quench. “Go on, say his name,” sighs Xavier, giving in. “Say the name of Jeremy Howard, as you seemingly must.” Hit the nail on the head. The paths of Xavier Kannon and Jeremy Howard overlap in so many ways it can’t be coincidence, but it always seems like Xavier is playing catch-up. Whatever he achieves, Jeremy did it first. “I don’t want to be Jeremy Howard,” the RUSH owner spits out with disgust in his mouth, “Never has a comparison so insulted me.” But plentiful they are, the very CEO’s chair that marks Xavier’s power was once host to the man he despises most. It’s always hanging above him. The Continental Title he has proudly strapped around his waist came directly from that of The Krayzie One. “Came?” is the retort in an insulted tone, “I believe ‘Won’ is the word you’re looking for.” How must it feel to walk in someone else’s footsteps? Not see the glorious light of day from in the shadow of he who’s footprints your soles are drawn to by outside forces. No matter how hard you try, imitation will never lead to superiority. 'Let me go,' said Damocles. 'I now see that I was mistaken, and that the rich and powerful are not so happy as they seem. Let me go back to my old home in the poor little cottage among the mountains.' On Tuesday, Xavier has a chance to fit the mould of Jeremy Howard a little more snugly than before, in facing Clyde and Duke Williams, the Scientologist is one win away from becoming a two time PCW Champion. Sound familiar? Oh, how Xavier sneers at the accusation he is in anyone’s shadow. “You can’t be in a shadow when the limelight is on you,” boasts Kannon, “After all, if I were mimicking ‘Him’... I’d be just another Peon rather than PCW’s pinnacle.” Maybe that’s next, because before long the horse hair could snap. And so could Kannon. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
