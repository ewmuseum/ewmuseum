Family Renunion

Date: NOW Two men would walk into the ring, one would stand the other would fall, that was the idea. But things don't always work out exactly as planned. After a long and strenuous battle of power durability and strength both men were drawn at a stalemate. And although we saw them both on the floor, the reality of the situation was that neither man was budging an inch. They battle raged on, and slowly as the match continued the match changed, it was no longer a contest to see who would gain respect by winning, but with this match threatening to injure both men permanently it became a contest to see who could gain respect by surviving. After all the headlocks and armbars, after all the suplexes and backbreakers, after all the Kanoplexes, Kanospears, True Contoversies and Politically Incorrect Frog Splashes you can handle, after all the blood, sweat and even occasionally tears (most of which came from hissy fits thrown by Corey) put into this match, it all came down to a contest of who could stand up. Corey stood up. Neither man had won, both had gained tremendous respect from the audience. 'Did you see the way I beat the crap out of Kano in front of all of his loser fans last Sunday?' But that's not the way Corey sees things. 'That's right, I destroyed that son of a bitch. He never really stood a chance.' ::Our scene opens up at an old folk's home. On camera we see Corey Williams talking to an anonymous old woman in a wheelchair, who doesn't really seem very interested in what Corey is saying anyway and is staring out the window with a dazed look instead.:: Corey: Hey! Pay attention! ::Corey snaps his fingers in front of the old woman's face, his head slowly turns towards Corey but his eyes look right through him. This is good enough for Corey, as long as he has enough to go on and fool himself into thinking the old woman is listening to him.:: Corey: That's better. Anyway, back to last Sunday. I dominated the entire match and showed Kano why they call me the most controversial man on the planet. ::The old woman blinks, looking at Corey blankly.:: Corey: Say, do you know what I heard Kano say when I beat him down so bad that his Momma couldn't even recognise him? ::The old woman does not answer Corey's question, which was rhetorical anyway so Corey moves on.:: Corey: As I was about to finish Kano off when that bitch got on his knees and I heard him say 'please don't hurt me Corey; I'm old.' The nerve of the guy! After all he'd done to disrespect me despite the courteous professionalism I'd shown him from day one. After all the times he'd claimed that he wasn't an old guy, here he was going back on his word and USING his age as a bargaining tool in his request for mercy! Naturally I took that Kano by his big fat head and through him into the turn post and knocked his ass flat for 10 straight seconds; piece of cake. I didn't even have to break a sweat. ::The camera cuts to the old woman again, there is dead air for about five seconds before returning to a shot of Corey.:: Corey: And don't even get me started on that Semore Smoke guy! Just when I established myself as the TRUE legend of PCW he comes along and, like the TRUE doped-up idiot that he is, jumps on the bandwagon of my name, using cheap tactics and attacking me FROM BEHIND! Isn't that just bloody how it happens in this despicable World today, as soon as I'm rid of all the hangers on the next CCW wannabe comes along thinking that they can walk my walk and talk my talk and steal my success, my spotlight. But Semore Smoke will find out, just like all the other CCW wannabes that there's nothing like an original. ::From an incredibly fired up Corey Williams we cut to another blank silent stare from the old woman, only this time with a twist; she's drooling!:: Corey: So..... nice place you have here. ::Corey looks around at the retirement home, the old folk seem pretty happy and are all chatting comfortably throughout the room, all except the old woman that is. Corey turns back to the old woman, sitting in the chair in the corner staring blankly. She is the oldest looking woman in the room, her hair is pure white and in patches where baldness has yet to win the fight, her skin is old a frail, her arms are constantly trembling as she stares blankly.:: Corey: Geez, you're not much of a talker are you? Look, I gotta go soon, I've got a match with the Pharoah to be at. Do you know the Pharoah? He's that semi-nigger who might as well be a mummy because he'd have more charisma that way. Man, I can't wait to get my hands around that little bitch and make him job harder than he did to Moses. Do you think I'll win? ::Corey looks up at the old woman, the camera cuts to her and again we have dead air for five seconds. The camera then cuts back to Corey, who seems particularly pissed at something the old woman 'said'.:: Corey: Oh, I see. A bit of a Pharoah fan are we? Well I'm sure you two will be very happy together. ::The camera cuts to the ever-mute old woman, again, nothing is said. But this only further infuriates CCW.:: Corey: Oh, giving me the silent treatment, eh? Well after I'm through with your precious Pharoah he'll be receiving INTENSIVE treatment! ::Again the camera cuts to the old woman, she still stares dumbly at Corey. Our camera cuts back to Corey Williams who suddenly throws a punch right at the old woman, knocking her to the ground.:: Corey: Don't use that tone with me bitch! I'm sick of your ungratefulness! ::Corey begins to stomp into the old woman as an alarm goes off and we hear screams from the receptionist. Finally two security guards come and grab CCW by the arms and drag him away. As CCW is being taking away his has one last sentence for the old woman, who is now on the floor unconscious.:: Corey: Nice to see you again, Mom. ::As CCW disappears behind the exit door our camera again returns to the old woman, Corey's Mom. Sent mentally ill by Corey's actions the last time they met at CCW's Father's funeral and sent to this old folk's home by CCW after he stripped her of all his Father's assets. Her hair is grey and patched due to stress, her body is old, fragile and disturbed. The scene fades out.:: !The End!
