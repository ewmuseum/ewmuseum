Yin and Yang

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/13/02 And so there were Yin and Yang, the two fundamental forces in the universe according to Chinese cosmotology. Everything that is exists because of them. Yin, the positive and masculine principle of the universe, Yang, the feminine and negative principle of the universe; the yin and yang are as different as night and day, but as such are complimentary. There cannot be one without the other. Separately, Yin and Yang are useless. The two need each other to realize the full potential each has. And without the other, each would lose all meaning and importance, and thus effectively everything created by Yin and Yang would vanish from existence. You may ask why I choose to begin in such a manner. I would respond by asking why not? One with any measure of intelligence can draw the parallels between the Yin and Yang of nature, and the Yin and Yang of PCW, more specifically the Yin and Yang of the Cruiserweight Division. Surely you can see how the concepts of Yin and Yang mirror the personas of Alexander Isawa Miyagawa and Charlie Loc. No? Then let me explain in more detail. First, Charlie Loc. Loc represents the Yin in the Cruiserweight Title relationship. Now, Yin encompasses the qualities of passivity, depth, darkness, cold, and wetness. And I can easily point out how Charlie Loc has all of these less-than-desirable attributes. Femininity: Charlie Loc is a little p*ssy, we all know that. Negativity: He's so negative about everything that's not about him. Not only that, but a man like this holding the Cruiserweight Title can only hurt the division. Passivity: Charlie Loc has yet to defend the PCW Cruiserweight Title. I know I'm the #1 contender, but I took advantage of a rarely-utilized contract clause that allows me to defer my #1 contendership to the pay-per-view. So, I will challenge the title (not a particular person whom may wear the belt) at the next PPV, and until then Charlie Loc is free to defend the title against whomever he chooses. Of course, he is passive from fear of losing the title, and so unless he defends the belt on the July 19th Firday Fusion, he'll have gone a full three weeks without defending the title. How's that for inactivity? Depth: Not so much depth of character, rather this is a category that I would stretch to mean the size of Charlie Loc's mouth. He speaks about himself as if his abysmal Cruiserweight Title reign means something. Loc has yet to prove that his win wasn't a fluke, and until he does, he really doesn't have any right to boast about himself. And yet he comes out almost every night to remind the PCW fans that he's the best thing since sliced bread, never bothering to back up what he says. Charlie Loc talks a lot of trash. He's got to have depth for all of it to come from somewhere. Darkness: Charlie Loc lives without the light of enlightenment. He's not very smart, and doesn't think things through. Oftentimes he is outwitted by an opponent, and must resort to using the words 'You nothing...' in a rather sad attempt to break them down. One thing Charlie Loc definitely possesses is darkness of memory. He blacks out what he doesn't like. To refresh his memory, here is a short list of but some of the wrestlers who have pummeled Charlie Loc to within an inch of his life; Jonathan Drachen, The HIT, myself, Jon Kano, Sunshine, Heaven Rayne, Reeco Suave, International Incident, everyone in the March Madness Rising Star Battle Royal, and so on and so forth. Cold: This one is quite simple, Charlie Loc has no heat behind him. He had to shoot Sunshine to get the fans to care about him, but again it comes back to the lack of intelligence. If Loc was smarter, he would have realized that the PCW fans really didn't care about Sunshine. The act itself was a terrible thing, but they soon remembered that it was Juan Borro that died and came back to their senses. Wetness: Charlie Loc still has much to learn about the way this business works. Even the newest PWA cadet would agree that Loc is a wet-behind-the-ears rookie. Loc's just not made for this business. He's a scared little boy pretending to be a gangster, only he's in a company full of men who don't tolerate that crap. Duke Williams, Xavier Kannon, Tyler Nelson; those men aren't trying to be anyone. They aren't even trying to be themselves, they are their own men. And then you have some dwarf from Vietnam who's doing his best to be the perfect cross between Chris Tucker and Jackie Chan. And he wonders why everyone hates him. Second, Miyagawa. Miya represents the Yang in the Cruiserweight Title relationship. Now, Yang encompasses the qualities of activity, height, light, heat, and dryness. And I can easily point out how Miyagawa has all of these desirable attributes. Activity: You'll never see a moment in PCW where Miyagawa is not doing something to further his career, or just to keep himself in the public eye as a major player in this federation (or in this business for that matter). He's held multiple championships in PCW. And even when he's not holding a championship, everyone is asking, 'What's Miya doing?' Not because they haven't seen him, but because they know that he's got something big in store. Miya never sits still, never stagnates. He's always doing something, always active. Height: Not height in the most basic term, the guy's only 5'10'. No, by height, I refer to the heights that Miyagawa has risen to in PCW. He's considered (rightfully so) in the upper echelon of PCW wrestlers, and I won't at all be surprised to hear him voted into the PCW Hall of Fame. Miyagawa came in as nothing, wrestling Ming and doing whatever he could to draw attention to himself. Now, he's held the PCW World Title and he needs to but lift a microphone to his mouth for the world to take notice of him. Light: Miyagawa is smart. He's outsmarted other wrestlers time and again. And to take it a different way, he's light in that he's within the cruiserweight limit. So, the man can effectively challenge any title he wants, and he does. And he has, I believe. The only titles he hasn't held are the Rising Star and Continental Titles. And no one expects him to go back to the Rising Star division, so all Miyagawa would really need to do is win the Continental Title before his own little title sweep would be complete. Miyagawa also thinks beyond himself. He doesn't need to come down to the Extreme or Cruiserweight Divisions. He does it to bring more press and exposure to the titles. That is very honorable. Heat: Simple enough. Miya's got some much heat behind him it doesn't even need to be explained. Charlie Loc knows this, so he's trying to leech heat off of Miya. Dryness: Miya stays cool and collected under extreme situations. This means that Charlie Loc can't force Miya into fighting a battle with emotions, perhaps the only fight Charlie Loc would stand a chance of winning. And so there were Yin and Yang, the two fundamental forces in the universe according to Chinese cosmotology. Everything that is exists because of them. Yin, the positive and masculine principle of the universe, Yang, the feminine and negative principle of the universe; the yin and yang are as different as night and day, but as such are complimentary. There cannot be one without the other. Separately, Yin and Yang are useless. The two need each other to realize the full potential each has. And without the other, each would lose all meaning and importance, and thus effectively everything created by Yin and Yang would vanish from existence. And then, there was another force. In the middle of Yin and Yang, there was one universal influence. It is not invisible, but not much attention is really paid to it. Yin and Yang depended on each other, but they never knew that it was the invisible force that held everything together. It is this force, one that lives for order as well as chaos, which exists in something of a natural limbo, that brings everything together and makes it significant. Without this force, Yin and Yang would cease to function, and everything would stop. This force, unknown even in Chinese cosmotology, is simply referred to as The End.
