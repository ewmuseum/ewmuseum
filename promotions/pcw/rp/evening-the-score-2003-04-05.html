Evening the score

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-03 “Alright, of course, I understand,” A.J. Trinidad says with dampened spirits as he hangs up the telephone in his Chicago apartment. A.J.’s apartment as never looked more bleak, unwelcoming, or even unlivable. Dishes left to fester in their own filth in the sink, pillows and blankets thrown about around the living room where A.J. had obviously been sleeping as of late. The television remains on if for no other reason than to just supply background noise. All of the “life” that normally accompanied his being was all but dead. “Who was that?” Asked Amira stepping from the bathroom wrapped in a towel that was tucked just over her breasts. “Immigration services” A.J. replies. “They said that regardless of how human he seems, they just cannot allow a kangaroo citizenship of the United States.” “You tried to get citizenship for Crespo?” She questioned again while scurrying across the cold kitchen floor towards the bedroom. “What for?” “I thought that if Crespo was a legal citizen of the United States, I’d be able to file a missing persons report, get Smoke arrested, and move on with life.” A.J. sluggishly said watching Amira disappear into the bedroom. “I guess it was a stupid idea.” Poking her head out from behind the bedroom door Amira simply smiles at A.J., “you’re so adorable when you’re trying to save a friend.” Thinking of that, A.J. looks over to the balcony storage room where he had stashed Adam Stryker and remembered that he had yet to tell Amira about what had happened on Friday night. Walking out the sliding glass door, A.J. breathes in deeply a breath of fresh city air, well, maybe not so fresh, then turns and unlocks the door where Stryker had been imprisoned the last fourteen hours. As the door creaked open, the ray of sunlight caught Adam Stryker lying on the floor struggling still to get out of his bindings. His eyes squint at the site of the sun and the silhouetted form of A.J. Trinidad bends down and sharply slaps Adam across the head. “What the hell do you think you are doing?” A.J. asked in a menacing tone, almost taunting Adam. “Now you know what if feels like to be imprisoned, have your freedom stolen from you rendering you helpless. You know what if feels like to not be in control of your destiny. GET UP!” Trinidad hoists Stryker to his feet, which had been tied together along with his hands. The gag in his mouth disenables him from speaking his case, but the fire in his eyes told A.J. enough of the fight that still existed with in Adam. “Why do you still have this vendetta against me!” A.J. screams at Adam while pushing him towards the guardrail. “Are you on a fucking suicide mission?” Trinidad looks over as Stryker does the same. Thirty-four floors up above hard concrete street can be a very convincing ally in times like these. Stryker swallows hard before his eyes focus back to Trinidad. “Mpphmhmhmhmhmhmpphmhmmhmmmhmmhmphph” Stryker struggles through the gag in his mouth as A.J. begins to lean him over the edge. “What is that Adam?” A.J. asks cockily. “I can’t understand what you are saying, or pleading?” The wind whips sharply against the building as car horns drown out any screams to be had from potential falling victims on a beautiful day like today. A.J. reaches in and tears away the gag from Adam’s mouth violently. “Care to start begging for you pathetic fucking life now?” “Listen A.J.” Adam begins to plead, “I know this is all fucked up, but I can lead you to Crespo! Think about it man, I’ve spoken with Smoke, I know what his plan is, I know how to get to him.” A.J., holding Stryker over the edge of the balcony stops to think for a minute about Adam’s offer when Amira comes barging out the door drying here hair, “A.J.! What the hell are you doing!” “Oh shit!” A.J. yells as he looses his grip on Stryker who subsequently begins to slip through his hands as the scene fades to black.
