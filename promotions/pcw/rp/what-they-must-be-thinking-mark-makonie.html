What They Must Be Thinking: Mark Makonie

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered What the hell is Chet King talking about? Promos? Damn, I can't remember the last time I did one of those. Maybe PWA? I don't know. I think the last time I had a camera in front of me outside of an arena was when I was beating Jordan X Shabazz back into the cotton fields in NTICW. So, without bringing any cameras with me to have my every word and action recorded, how can Chet King have anything to say on that subject? How could he know I was in Mexico, or at a coffee shop or that I spilled coffee? Chet King lacks normal mental abilities, so extramental abilities such as psychic powers are obviously beyond him. And I'm going to rule out anyone telling him that this happened, Chet King himself said that no one would tell anyone. And I don't believe he was there, how could he work on 'promos' against that worthless half-breed Skylar if he was sitting in the cafe? So Chet King has an addiction to cameras. Big deal. He's not getting booked anyway, so he needs to be surrounded by them. Myself, I'm seen in matches, and that's enough for me. I may be a white supremacist, but it is worse to be a narcissist. I mean, really, he's is so high on himself that it's pathetic. Compared to other Aryans, I'm not special, I'll admit that. But on the racial scale, there are many that are beneath me. Chet King, on the other hand, feels he's better than everyone. Yet he spends so much time thinking about others, and worrying about what they are doing. It's rather sad to see, and hear. Chet King is no doubt sitting in his home right now, in nothing but a bathrobe and dirty boxers shorts, watching some more 'promos' and scratching his ass thinking of a possible rebuttal. But here's what I want to know... Where is Chet King getting the video for these 'promos?' Hell, does PCW even do promos anymore? Chet King doesn't realize that in all those months he was gone because his massochistic tendencies toward his fellow wrestlers put him out for nearly a year that PCW has changed. People aren't camera-hogs. We get airtime on Turmoil, Fusion, and the pay-per-views. We don't cut promos anymore because we've got lives. He doesn't, he's stuck in the past. And even if he were to stop, he's been going by the old ways for so long that he'd go bored-crazy sitting around the house. Chet King, a poor excuse for a member of the white race... must have some Slavic blood in him. Chet King has never had a particularly quick wit, just a sharp one. So I sit here and wonder just how long Chet King spent trying to think of what to say to me? How many crumpled up sheets of scrap paper are in and around his wastebasket, scribbled insults about me filling each page? How much has he dreamt about me? But what I really want to know is a) how is it happening and b) when will it stop? Now, as for how Chet King knows about all this stuff about me... it's not like it means anything much. He can't really do anything with such knowledge. Hold it against me? How, he himself undercut what I did to such an extent that he couldn't use this 'phantom tape' to blackmail me. Or, what about the other round of insults he tossed around? Yes, he also had some words for Skylar Green. Again, it focused on some non-existent promo that Skylar supposedly did. Now, I don't know what channel Chet King is watching. Maybe that new 'Pull something out of Chet King's ass and sling it at the camera' channel. Chet King does seem as though he lacks that .02% that separates humans from chimpanzees. I'd bet dollars to doughnuts that he shaves his whole body everyday just to pass for human. Apparently Skylar 'forced' Chet King to listen to the inferior recount his history. I would agree that whatever Skylar has to say is not worth anyone's time, but Skylar has not put out any promos. He knows to keep his face off of the camera. It's merely Chet King who has a problem with that. I have my associates pay attention to that the PCW channel, rarely are new promos played anymore. People don't do them. Recaps of the shows, file promos and interviews are shown from the months and years past. So these promos that Skylar or I did... I have no idea what Chet King is talking about, or where any footage of this would come from. Skylar? Talking to Sandy? Or the FBI? Heh... Don't take this as defending the mongoloid, but Chet King missed some key points. Skylar can't talk to the FBI, he somehow got out of his issues with the,. I'm assuming because he's walking the streets instead of sitting in a cell where he really belongs. And Skylar can't talk to Sandy because the bitch is dead. Violence Jack killed her, just like she deserved. And he'll do the same to Skylar at March Madness, but I'm getting ahead of myself. I know that Skylar can't be involved with either of them now because the FBI isn't investigating him anymore and Sandy's rotting in hell. I know this because unlike Chet King, I pay attention. But if that did happen at some point, even though there's not promo ANYWHERE that would prove Chet King right that Skylar did put anything up (and on that subject, how could Skylar FORCE Chet King to listen to anything? Was he sitting there holding Chet King's head to his speakers? The idiot...), there's still the more important matter that Chet King somehow knows what I'm doing. And I'm not on-camera. Never have been. But that doesn't matter, Chet King will probably hear about this and quote me word for word, despite the fact that this is not a promo, I'm merely sitting on my porch talking to you, Jax. Indeed, it is puzzling... Unless.... Chet King is sending cameras to follow people around, just so he'll have something to talk about. It makes sense, why else is he completely silent until he produces some 'phantom promo' that nobody did, just to talk about them? He's probably having me taped right now, so that he can sit at home and masturbate to any mention of him I give. Wait a minute... DAMMIT, GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY BUSHES, KING!
