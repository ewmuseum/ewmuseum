Heavy-Duty Bonding Session

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/09/02 Contrast. Organization and Disagreement.. Existence and Chaos. Yes and No. Contrast. Light and Dark. Smile and Frown. Big and Small. Contrast. Duzza and Makonie. Gaz Green and Nascosto Nerezza. Champions and Challengers. Contrast. The ebonics and culture of Malek 'Duzza' Williams. The Ghetto man, raised on hip-hop, rap and Harlem tough life. The image of The White Man, the enemy and the endless struggle for equality or dominance, his mission. Contrast. Mark Makonie. The man who tried to live a normal life but was drawn into the Third Reich's influence. The Perfect World, his mission. Practically anything inferior, his enemy. And yet somehow, the uneasy tension of their creeds and beliefs has allowed them to gel together for a time. The Negro and the Cracker, the superior and the imperfect. Bonded by the gold worn separately and yet as one around their waists. Contrast. Gaz Green. Las Vegas bred, growing up in the bright lights and filled with the promises of opportunity. Thrown away in favour of an addiction, the confusion and distress of the unsuspecting. The ultimate joke leading to the ultimate smile, his mission. The entire world, his victims. Contrast. Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. 4 months old and living on blood and anguish. Denying both humans and vampires, finding solace only in one who shares his contempt for his own race. A world free of human problems, his mission. Humans and Humanity, his eternal reminder of what he left behind. The laughter and the anger, the eternal smile and the forever tormented. Bonded by rejection and a hunger for the downfall of others. Contrast. Duzza's fierce slugging and brutal brawling style. Makonie's precise and calculated aerial assaults. Contrast. Gaz's unusual, rule-breaking and all-round techniques. Nerezza's submission based and technically sound moves.' Contrast. A frayed and decaying friendship. Contrast. A chaotic organization spreading organized chaos. 'Well, if I can translate, you're trying to say they're on the end of their respective tethers while we're functioning as a unit?' 'About that.' Nerezza wiped his sunglasses on his T-Shirt and replaced them, perching them on the edge of his nose. 'They're in disarray and can't keep themselves on track. They're not on the same page, the same book - those fools aren't even in the same library.' Gaz nodded slowly and stayed in his crouching position. 'By the way, Rezz... care telling me WHY we're in a glue factory!? I think Red Rum just went past us... in a tube!' he boomed, trying to make himself heard over the machinery they were approaching. 'Quite simple!' he bellowed back. 'We're going to steal some of this super-strong stuff and teach Duzza and Makonie a lesson about bonding! Duke Williams already had a dose of the glue and I'm sure he had a good time freeing himself.' Gaz kept himself ducked down, looking as bottle after bottle of the adhesive passed him on a conveyor belt. 'So Skin-head is going to probably tear some skin off trying to move and R. Kelly will never touch the sky when his feet are glued to the floor... gotcha!' Nerezza's arm stretched out and held itself in place as each new tube was blocked by his hand. Satisfied only when around four bottles had fallen into his clutches, he retracted the inhuman limb, depositing the bottles in the various pockets of his coat. 'Now then Gaz... this main machine here is what you need to do your stuff with! Around 240 volts of pure P.W.A should do it!' the vampire shouted. Gaz pulled the pristine white glove from his hand to reveal his classic hand-buzzer, severely cranked up to levels that wouldn't merit the Lion or indeed the CE symbol. Tensing a fist, a few crackles of electricity sparked from the tip before he delivered a quick punch to the control panel of the machine. Immediately, red warning lights began to flash as the part that filled the bottles began to overload, spraying one long line of hot glue onto the conveyor belt. Nerezza gave a cruel smirk and Gaz produced a banana from his pocket. 'Mmm... delicious!' the Jester slurped and began to devour it. 'Chew carefully, you don't want indigestion...' his partner cackled. A team of security men rushed into the disturbed area, spotting the duo. One quickly ran over to stop the conveyor while the rest of the force stared down the pair. Gaz calmly chewed away on his banana and Nerezza placed a hand on his hip and leaned back with a smug grin, placing a hand to his chin. 'YOU, GREENIE! WHAT THE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU'RE DOING!?' screamed one of the factory men, despite the lack of noisy machinery any more. Gaz stood still and looked back innocently, taking another bite. 'You know, it's rude to talk with one's mouth full, but equally rude to force one to do so?' Nerezza said, pushing his red lenses up the bridge of his nose. 'What?' piped up another of the burly men. Gaz finished his banana and was left with the skin in his hand. 'Exit stage right?' The Emerald menace chirped. Nerezza took a look at the mob, then nodded. The Pranks leapt over the conveyor belt with a spectacular somersault while the Attitude vaulted over, heaving his heavier frame across the sea of glue. Behind, the gathering of thuggish security began their chase, hoping to catch up with the scheming duo. Gaz turned and threw the banana peel into their tracks. In a situation only seen in cartoons, one piled into another as the mass of muscle tripped over each other and landed in a puddle of sticky white mess. Not taking their chances, P.W.A quickly bolted off down a corridor with the stolen glue, cackling away among a pile of cursing as the hired help squirmed in the inescapable mess.
