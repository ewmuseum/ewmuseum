Pulling Strings, The Eight Part of

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.03.03 Location: North Shore Arms – Seattle, WashingtonDate: 02.03.03Time: 0135 Hours Tossing and turning inside of the small queen sized bed; his eyes wafted inward and outward trying to gain access to neurotic dreams of sleep. But the actions of Friday would continue to harden him for whatever until the tide was loss altogether. Wrapped in polka dotted white sheets with black trim at the edges, his feet curled up into the air as a brisk wind slapped his ashy ankles. Shortened cover, and an even cooler Seattle breeze cooped him into thinking his sleep was going to be serene, peaceful, a warm splash of blue with a tint of yellow shining in. There was no way he’d get this sleep for a regular basis, not after what occurred on Fusion, for the Viking warrior had come back, and set his sights particularly astray – at him. His mind parlayed over his head as the scene played in front of him, a short reminder of him standing on top of a plank, looking over as his child swims and splashes playfully as the other children around her do the same thing. Next to him, his common wife stands with a bathing suit rivaling that of some Victoria Secret catalog. They both smile, a bare hardened chest with the looks of an Adonis as he walks back over towards the sandy beaches, sitting down on a folding chair, enjoying his view of the beaches when a beach attendant comes over and flashes a phone in front of his eyes, the short mention of words between them is enough for Dante to question, “Yes?” The voice rings out in front of him, “You have a call sir.” “Fine, I’ll take it.” He responds quickly, snatching the Motorola away from his hands and accessing the control panel, looking and checking to see whom the call is from and the reason of the call. Bolded words flash in front of his eyes. Pamela Jackson; Location – George Washington Hospital A picture of the woman’s face plasters on the phone’s screen interface as he holds the phone closer towards his ear. He answers, “Mom?” “Dante,” the woman shrieks out, but holds a calm tone towards her, not trying to alert anyone inside of the room beside her. The operating facilities happen to hold about six thousand five hundred people inside of it. Two hundred of those are on staff, medical, and the other six thousand and one hundred are incoming or outgoing patients. “Its your father…” His eyes begin to water into the crescent moon like shaped bend down into ovals, “What happened…Mom…” “Something happened while he was working out this morning, you know him trying to keep in physical and tip top condition right?” Dante nods, “Yeah, he’s like the old man version of Ben Affleck. But what’s the problem?” “Well, it’s hard to say really – oh? Yes? Well, is it that bad?” she questions to another person away from the phone, probably a lab coat that would do his job and get paid for it, not really paying much mind or attention towards the victims or the families, just to get his paycheck.“He’s had a heart attack, but he’s…” she couldn’t utter the last words before she quietly began to sob, breaking down into uncontrolled sadness as he held the phone tighter and tighter towards his ear, trying to pin point exactly what his mother was saying. Was this a dream, a bad case of unknown paradox to him that he could only escape by a pinch of the flesh and an instant jolt back to reality? “He’s gone!” she screams as tears pour down her face, as the same with her son. “He’s gone,” he sharply mutters to himself, before hearing the phone crash back down to the ground, smacking the sand with a thud, leaving a known imprint only for beach goers to find for him. An then he woke up in a start, sweat bedding down his forehead as he pulled back the covers and moved himself right over toward the stationed mirror facing upright against a while palm backdrop. “Okay, get a grip, get a grip.” He repeats to no one in particular as he wipes his face down with a towel. He sighs to himself, his heart beating faster and faster and faster before subsiding slow. “Right now, your hair is a mess and I suggest you get some help.” “No, no,” he mutters to only himself as he stares into the mirror. “I’m not the one that needs help, I’m perfectly fine!” He chuckles loudly, before shutting off the mirror light and letting his headrest softly back down to sleep. He dreams and dreams oddly about the same one dream, the alternate conclusions and possibilities of it before the memory completely fades and the rising gleam of the sun is already shining upon his face. “What, is it morning already?” Location:Angelo’s Bistro – Seattle, WashingtonDate: 02.03.03Time: 0950 Hours “What’ll be same old same old?” the bartender asks an old man sitting upon the bar in business suit and tie. He grips with age like everyone else, hairs forming over his near baldhead with wrinkles aligning his forehead and cheeks. He nods, “Sure, I’ll go for whatever you’ve got.” “NICK!” “Oh geez,” the old man sighs to himself as Dante Jackson comes sprawling into the picture, barely recognizable by the general public inside of the diner. His hair has been brushed half assed, causing him to wear a black Mitchell and Ness Seattle Sonics jersey with an old Lenny Wilkins jersey and black pants sagging past his Gluteus Maximus. “Dante!” he tried to play it off, as he was welcoming in the Lyrical Messiah in with welcome arms, but Dante stops in mid point, looking around oddly. “What’s wrong? Just a few days ago you were chilling in the Playboy Mansion, something up?” Dante shakes his head, “nothing I can’t sweat.” Nick shakes his head repeatedly; “Okay then, do you mind me eating my damn sandwich in peace?” Dante chuckles before exiting the diner, shaking his head in silence, letting his eyes wander upon billboards citing pictures of various models, sports figures, and then there’s him, plastered on a skyscraper with Tha 2nd Comin’ insignia plastered right next to his face. “I’ve sold four million copies in less than a full mouth on the market. There’s nothing that can stop me right now, I’m on a high!” he assures himself before feeling a jolt through his knee, his phone “blowing up” on side of him as he picks it up from the pocket. “Yo?” “To defeat the swordsman, you must control the mind.”
