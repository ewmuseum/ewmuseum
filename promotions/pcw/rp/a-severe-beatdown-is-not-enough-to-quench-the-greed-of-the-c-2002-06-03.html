A severe beatdown is not enough to quench the greed of the CEO!

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/03/02 “You boys done fucked with the wrong CEO, and now you are going to PAY” Immortal words from the immortal one himself, Tyler Nelson, that should serve as a warning to not only the group of punks that were beat down on Fusion but to the entire wrestling world as a whole. No one messes with Tyler Nelson and comes away unscathed, as The Phantom Booker’s gang of homo’s found out rather painfully. It’s the same message that has been delivered time and time again from the CEO, yet people STILL feel the need to test him. Each and every time Tyler delivers on the goods and puts the antagonists back in their place…..or worse. One Tyler Nelson does not take kindly to people who screw with his agenda. What exactly is his agenda, you might ask? First on the list is regaining his PCW World Championship, which temporarily sits around the waist of a most unworthy and brutally dumb man that goes by the name of Big Alan Steele. Dozens of people have conspired to keep that title away from Tyler, ranging from Jane Reagan to Howard Porter to Duke Williams to The Phantom Booker. With a long list of saboteurs like that it’s easy to understand the difficulty in recapturing the top prize in the sport. It will happen, it’s only a matter of time. Second on the agenda is eradicating any and all opposition to The Ultimate Authority’s rule. Many groups have voiced their opposition in recent weeks, jealous of the money, power, and respect that The Ultimate Authority has earned and demands. People like Duke Williams, who if it wasn’t for Tyler Nelson would still be toiling away in relative obscurity wrestling in backyard feds for bags of peanuts, and The Phantom Booker, who would have found himself living out of a gutter for the rest of his life if the CEO hadn’t bailed him out of his financial predicament and bought PCW, continually insist on being a gargantuan pain in the ass of DA BOSS~! These pests will be exterminated like a boot stamping on a cockroach, and that is a promise from Tyler Nelson. Ungrateful fucks. Seems quite simple, doesn’t it? Two minor tasks that seem to clutter the appointment book of the most powerful man in wrestling, Tyler Nelson. Well, things get rolling on Turmoil as Tyler Nelson and Xavier Kannon, the four star general of The Ultimate Authority, will seek to dismantle, decapitate, demolish, discombobulate, disorient, and in other words destroy Duke Williams and Big Alan Steele. It certainly looks like a black day in the lives of the redneck and the big goof as they are totally, completely, and utterly overmatched and in over their heads. Then we move on to Fusion, where we will hear the swan songs of William S. Powell, The Phantom Booker, and Duke Williams (should he even be able to function properly after the beating he is going to receive on Turmoil). The CEO will walk all over those three ungrateful pieces of trash and squash their respective stables all in one fell swoop. The master plan is in effect, and it is foolproof. So how does the CEO feel after the unveiling of the all new and vastly improved Ultimate Authority on Fusion? Let’s see….. We catch up with the CEO as he sits at the breakfast table in his new stately mansion somewhere in the California hillside. The sun is just rising over the ridge behind the house as Tyler rests on the back porch, taking in the serenity of the early morning. Dressed only in his silk pajama bottoms, the CEO takes a sip of orange juice as the butler brings out his plate of eggs, French toast, and an English Muffin. “Will that be all, sir?” asks the butler in a faint voice. Tyler simply brushes the servant away with his hands, giving him a foul look. The butler quickly disappears back into the spacious mansion leaving the CEO to his breakfast. The swimming pool in back is immaculately landscaped with various stone sculptures positioned around it and beautiful shrubbery surrounding it to give a sends of privacy. There is a man-made waterfall on the far side which trickles down into the pool soothingly. Rolling hills serves as the backdrop of the breathtaking view. A sigh comes from the CEO as he surveys the land. “I’m going to have to change all of this if I’m to stay here,” grumbles Tyler. “Totally unacceptable.” Just as the CEO is about to dive into his breakfast we hear the click-clack of footsteps on the white marble patio. Panning over we see a beautiful woman, looking like a model straight from the pages of a fashion magazine. Her long brown hair flows effortlessly down from her head to the middle of her back. The woman’s long legs lead up to a short red silk robe that is tied loosely in the front. Her full lips form a smile as she takes a seat next to Tyler. “Good morning, Tyler,” the gorgeous woman coos seductively. Tyler looks over at her and gazes upon her longingly, reminiscing in his mind about last nights activities. He smiles tenderly at her as he speaks. “I distinctly remember telling you to call me Mr. CEO,” says Tyler authoritatively. The smile quickly disappears from his face. “There should be a taxi waiting for you out front shortly. I suggest you hurry and get your things together unless you plan to travel like that.” Tyler snaps his head away from the distraught woman and starts in on his breakfast. The brunette’s brown eyes well up with tears and her lips start to quiver as she breaks down. She hurriedly gets up from the table and dashes back into the house, her high heels clacking all the way. The CEO snorts to himself and seems genuinely pleased with what he just accomplished. “Dumb bitches are always hanging around the day after,” says Tyler, shaking his head. “Fucking vultures and money grubbing whores is what they are. Can’t stand them. If they would just leave in the middle of the night like they know they should then maybe they would get another date.” A couple of bites of his breakfast and the CEO picks up a file that is laying on the table. We get a glimpse of the manila file and can plainly see the PCW logo emblazoned on it. Tyler flips it open and scans over the first page while eating half of his English Muffin. “Turmoil……Nelson and Kannon vs Williams and Steele,” the CEO thinks aloud. “Piece of cake. Kannon destroyed Duke at Supremacy, I destroyed Steele and Snow. Sounds like an open and shut case to me.” Tyler wipes the corners of his mouth with his napkin and flips the page. “Fusion……Nelson vs Williams vs Powell vs Booker. Genius, truly genius,” oozes Tyler incredulously. “I couldn’t have booked a better match myself….” The CEO grins sheepishly and places a hand over his mouth playfully. “Oh, I did book that,” snickers Tyler. “Silly me.” A bite of French toast flushed down by a sip of orange juice. “Big Alan Steele,” starts the CEO with more than a hint of disgust in his voice. “What a fucking leech that son of a bitch is. He’s leeching off of the legacy that I have built with the PCW World Championship. Leeching all of the fame and prestige I have brought to that title. He’s handling stolen goods, and I intend to do everything in my vast power and exhaust every resource to get it back. He has succeeded thus far from keeping it from me, but soon the PCW World Title will be in my possession once again. I must have it. Then all will be right in the world again.” Tyler snarls as he savagely takes a bite from the other half of the English muffin. He chews rather viciously as the frustration within him continues to build. Even after all of his success the CEO is still a hungry, ambitious man that will stop at nothing to get what he wants. The scene fades to black.
