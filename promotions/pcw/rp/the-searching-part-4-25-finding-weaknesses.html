Date: 2-02-02 (lots of 2s there) NOTE: This was supposed to be short! But I still ended up at 15K on notepad! (A good length RP and a half, basically) I may need to go to Long RP Writers Anonymous. Either that or something dealing with shutting the heck up. :P .Song listening guide: 'My Generation' by Limp Bizkit, from that hot dog starfish album thingy. BOBBY STORMBRINGER The Searching? - Part 4.25? - Finding Weaknesses --2-28-02, Thursday afternoon, 2:05 pm-- STORMBRINGER'S VOICE: The last day of February. The NCAA Men's College Basketball Tournament, which is loosely known as 'March Madness' will begin in just a couple of weeks now. Duke University will win, not Maryland or anyone else. My.. 'humble' apologies for spoling it for you. There is another March Madness, and if you are viewing this promo you probably care a lot more about another type of 'March Madness' that is coming. Coming much sooner... in four scant days. This is the name for PCW's spectacular cable Pay-Per-View for the Irish-laden month of March. Hmm. Adequete hype? You really, really think so?? Oh goody. Two men will face off on a special PCW Turmoil this upcoming Wednesday night. They've 'officially' gone at it twice before. Once while one was as sick as a dog with a flu virus and while two other people were contributing to the chaos in the ring. It was Six Finger Cisco's last match in PCW thanks to a buddy of the healthy guy. The End, the fourth participant in hte contest. Hmph. Sounds awfully convienient to me... The scene quickly fades in from black onto the back of a black chair made of leather that may or may not be real. The walls are colored of a dingy gray, and hordes of videotapes are stored in neat little piles, all in their own wooden stands. These stands are littered almost everywhere, only enough room to walk from point A to point B, for the most part. The only other constant part of the room's environment are the boxes. A randomly flashing light eminates from in front of the chair. The only direct sunlight in the room seems to be streaming out from the narrow side windows that are bolted to the opposite walls on the right and the left. After only a glimpse of this while the scene fades in from blackness and Bobby Stormbringer's words, The chair swivels around with a loud squeak to it, The Showstopper's long, screaming red hair flipping thwapping his neck as he turns the sitting device. B.S. leans down and forward, props his elbows onto his thighs, and stares into his brand new state-of-the-art camera. You heard right, brand new! No more fuzziness, and the late fade in was done on purpose this time. B.S. is wearing an purple 'Macho Man' Randy Savage t-shirt like the good old days, and he is wearing black slacks with bare feet. The shades are always on, the Magic cap and watch are on right now. STORMBRINGER: ...terribly convienient. Since The End is your good friend and he was back then, wasn't he, Drakey? Thanks to the stacked deck, you pinned Cisco, One. Two. Three. That is, after he was hit in the face with an 'unidentified flying foreign object'. You must be proud. Drake Maxwell has done a few heinous things. A brutal attack on Mr. Prime Time's tag team partner, Xavier Kannon, and it happened two Sundays ago at Sabogtage. A premeditated two-on-one ambush that may of just gone too far beyond the boundries of wrestling. Most especially too far for Bobby Stormbringer's liking, wrestling purist that he is. There's still a chance that after Drake and Bobby met, that Drake was one of the three men who attacked The Showstopper, tried to send him off to jail. The camera pans farther away to show everything in the dank room, a wide and large wooden staircase a newer part of the shot. The bottom step reaches close to the middle of the room, leading up. Which would lead us to (correctly) assume that this is the unkept basement in the still modest home of The Showstopper, once again in Orlando, Florida. The one true City of Angels. STORMBRINGER: Then of course there was that tag match. We burned the house down in Calgary, stole the show. Of course... I always do that when I'm in Calgary, no matter which organization I've graced my presence with at the time. B.S. shifts to the side and somehow, someway, the advocate for 'feminine-male equality in sport' has managed to uncover one of the many Xavier Kannon/Drake Maxwell matches prior to the sick-f-f-f-fffreak-Mona version of NTICW. Needless to say, both are flying around the ring and destroying one another like scared wombats that have just seen a skunk-haired gremlin. STORMBRINGER: Yet, in neither match did you pin me, Max. Nice table spot on me. Then again, I find The Perfect Storm right through the underbelly of a metal chair to be a tiny.. microscopic bit more impressive than that. No really. I mean it. I could only imagine if both of our respective teams could work the kinks out and get some experience as tag teams for ourselves. It would end up even better. Imagine if we actually had to tag? Wwwow. Another thought of mine is that perhaps Divinity should be worried, what do you think..? Hmm. I hate to say it but you haven't quite pulled even with us for how badly Xavier and I have separately worn you down for Krayzie's benefit. Sometimes even combined for that matter. Now, don't misunderstand. I think if you weren't so damn obessed with Jeremy Howard over the past couple of months then maybe you would of at least had the class to.. 'respond' to my punishment. No worries. You could rationalize it. You could say that you have never deserved to be punished. I tend to disagree. Your mistreatment towards women, to put it mildly. Kidnapping another human being's flesh and blood, just so you can get over with wrestling fans? I don't mean them cheering you, so don't get so ready to bitch me out about being mistaken about something or whatevah. Perish the thought. Something even more difficult than that. To hate you. I know how your mind works, Drake. Why? Because two years ago? Mine worked the exact.. same.. way. When they hate you they pay attention to you. So many of us.. just that simple and rare thing would quench all our thirsts, satisfy us. That's all you really want, isn't it? Attention? I thought so. Well, maybe that and some respect for good measure. For the principle of it. But you showed a part of your hand, Max. You claim you hate Jeremy Howard. Bullsh*t. Pardon the pun. That guy did absolutely nothing to you to warrant such a strong feeling. If for some reason it is true, that you loathe him? Then your anger... you must use it as a weapon. But like in any knifefight, an adversary who knows what he is doing can turn your own knife against you, boy. This is all open to speculation.. whether this calm and even keel persona of yours is just a front for the rage that is 'Boiling Over' inside of you or not. The other is your impatience. You needed a main eventer now, would do anything to get him into matches. It's all about you trying to ride his coattails up this business way too soon, so don't kid a kidder. But you, the buyer of your fame, should beware. Impatience is one the easiest weaknesses to exploit. Don't think I have forgotten, speaking of boling over. Getting along with Xavier at first was a stretch enough. As a former member of Eternal... heh. Max, let's just say that getting along with two of you ol' NTICWers is absolutely out of the question. For your information, the current NTICW doesn't count. Those kids weren't around for that short 'fued'. Although that Powell is certainly on my sh!t list, but still. That's another jackass for another time, just like LeBlanc. I was buried in that place, Cisco as well. We left there to fester under the *ahem* 'leadership' of Cassidy n' Jax while you languished in the spotlight, NTICW a main stay in the Primetime Central Complex. I'm sure they will be. I definitely came to PCW the hard way. Only a relatively small number of people had the opportunity to see any fed I was in, and now? Now so many people will pay in their ignorance of me. Of their flat out ignoring me? That's a big, huge, enormous, tremendous mistake on their part. Not just theirs, Drake. Yours. But go ahead. You concentrate on the rest of Asylum while I sneak in the back door and pin your shoulders to the mat. Before you ever pin mine. Or perhaps a tap out? Strained and pulled lower back muscles from 'The Degree'? Hmph. Whatever I do to you cannot and will not be held against me. If I have to be the instrument of chaos, justice, balance, and random chance against your thin skull then so be it. If you find a way escape me Tuesday, then your punishments are not meant to pass upon you just yet. Just yet. Metaphorically speaking, of course. I know how much you love that sort of thing. Trust me on this next point, because I know. Just to survive Jeremy Howard in a 'Hell In A Cell' first? Don't be a hero. Take my advice. Don't.. climb.. the cage. Not until you take out Howard completely first. B.S. takes a moment to sigh and he shuts the videotape off behind him. STORMBRINGER: Then of course.. you won't listen to me. You never do. So you'll f*ck up and a vet like Krayzie will take advantage. Your obliteration this next week and a world title reign inside of Asylum clinches a successful March Madness for the stable. A stable that the title fits so perfectly for. Asylum. Just like Alliances this past year and this upcoming year, March Madness will belong to the 'Freaks of Nature'. Before I even get to you, Max... there's still the GTT! Don't get distracted now, or we'll all be talking about you as our big uspet of the week, don't you know.. I'm more than prepared enough for Gaines, and victory will be sweet. Although I'm sure the one against Syxx will still feel more gratifying. A victory feels much more satisfying when you defeat someone you know so well. You get my meanin', doncha? I'm sure you do. Even beatdowns of those people are more satisfying. After I get through with Gaines in a couple days I can worry fully about the battle royal that you don't seem ot be a part of. Tough luck. Perhaps by some miracle you can be Continental Champion. If we let you. That would only be if Jeremy would wanted to drop it and get the cruiserweight belt now. I got dibs on Extreme. Assuming of course, I haven't shocked the world come Monday night... but I wouldn't count on that slim chance since Jer is fighitng for his brother. Aww! Did someone miscalculate!? It's all just talk though. All of it true, but talk. We'll see how the next page of our drama unfolds Wednesda- B.S. interrupts himself because he hears footsteps lightly bounding down the stairs. His 'lovely friend', the also red-headed Kira, leans over the side railing and grins a cheesy grin to her Bobby. KIRA: Bobby, hon? The lunch is all ready. Rest your eyes from that scouting stuff, you don't need it by now! Even though Kira yells at Bobby, she does it in the most pleasant, non-serious way imaginable. STORMBRINGER: Heh. Alrighty, babe, I'll be right up. I still need it for Gaines, it was super hard to find any info on the guy. I guess you're right about Maxwell, though. KIRA: Hehe, of course I'm right. Heya. How do you scout a match with twenty guys, anyway? STORMBRINGER: Hmm. You can't, really. You go in with the prior knowledge you have on these guys. My knowledge on Kid Wonder is near limitless, for example. Except I don't know how good that shrimp is in basketball... oh well. If he's not decent at least I can laugh at him for it. Kira York blows a kiss at Bobby and she also snickers at him for what he says. Before responding she begins to make her way back upstairs, without looking at him. KIRA: I'm still gonna beeee theeeere because I don't trust that two-faced b!tch Amanda. Whoa! Pottymouth alert. KIRA: C'mon up and take a break from the camera, babe! Ya need to eat! STORMBRINGER: Okaaay, okaaay. B.S. stands up and neatens up the tapes of Gaines and Maxwell he was using for reasearch, before facing the camera. STORMBRINGER: Heh, what an awesome, silly girl. Anyways, Max. Ever listened to my theme song..? Perhaps you should, the lyrics I mean... #Forfeit the game. Before somebody else takes you out of the frame. Puts your name to shame. Cover your up your face. You can't run the race. The pace is too fast.# And Drake? You just won't last. And baby.... that's no b.s.! B.S. just shuts off the snow-filled TV and walks away towards the stairs since he would never tip over such a new camera for mere effect. The Showstopper has drawn eighth in the battle royal. With three stablemates involved, will that be just enough to get him right there to the very end? Can he 'get over' on Kid Wonder or The Chef, even briefly? Or will such a desire fly right back into his face? Is Bobby really that much better in one on one and triple threat matches, and should Maxwell be concerned about that at all? What about The End? Will B.S.'s legend grow farther, can he defeat Brian Gaines in the PTC Tournament?? Will B.S. go to the NTICW Pay-Per-View, and will brother Randy reveal another culprit in the vicious Jamaica attack there!? The Searching continues.... THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002.
