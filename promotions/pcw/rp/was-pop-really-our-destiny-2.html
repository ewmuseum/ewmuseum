Was PoP really our destiny?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The Road to the Championship…Episode I: Was PoP really our destiny?Part II of II With the last episode of the never-ending tale of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’, we were treated to visions and dreams of alternate futures. We were treated to the items that our athletes could have become if they didn’t walk the same road they had done. Sherwin, had been a professional wrestler for many years… and all of his dreams seem to revolve around being the best that the business could offer, about being the best that was available to both the public and the organizations all around the world. He’d taken his eyes over his ghostly self from an alternate timeline to see the PCW World Champion… and to see all of the success that could have been his. Now, what about Ryan Moore? What could somebody like him have thought of in his time of doubt? Would he have thought about something like winning Championships from a variety of Federations… or would he have simply dreamed about following the career that he’d chosen from the first moment he was asked by a tutor in Junior High? Remember folks, Ryan Moore doesn’t have the same wrestling background that Sherwin has… in fact, NTICW was the first organization that Ryan Moore had actually come to work for like, ever. He was the kid that would sing to himself when the World seemed to be against him, he was the one that would dance whenever a familiar beat started to pound its way out of a speaker system. Ryan Moore had never truly placed his eyes on becoming a professional wrestling personality. Moore had always considered himself to be destined for either… the arts of theatre, musicals… or the glamour’s of a true solo music artist. He’d been successful enough to release a record of his own during the long year where he was without employment. After their previous stint with Primetime Championship Wrestling, he’d scored a record deal with an independent company and looked set to storm into the United States chart scene with a fresh R&amp;B/PoP sound. True, all of this debated the reasoning behind the duo’s decision to venture into PoP… but on the account of Ryan Moore, it was more of the decision to mix PoP with Professional Wrestling. As the ever-cool effect of the screen swaying from side to side began to settle down, we found ourselves standing in the confinements of an empty music studio. Everything had the ghostly complexion that we’d all come to experience in the ‘dream’ that belonged to Rich Sherwin. Items and people looked like they could have been there… but in reality, they were nowhere closer to the destination than either you or I. Everything seemed silent, like a death mist had made its way through these quarters recently… although everything of that kind was dismissed, as Ryan Moore finally emerged from the shadows. “… Hello? Hello? Is anyone around here? Can anybody actually hear anything that I’m trying to say?” Ryan Moore had cupped his hands around his mouth to try and project his voice as far as humanly possible… although there was no response from anyone, or anything. Moore waited for somebody to hear his call, although, as the realization that nobody was going to reply clicked in the back of his mind, the two arms of our other ‘Pretty Cool Hero’ dropped down to his sides, a sigh let out from his mouth. Ryan’s head tilted forward somewhat and he stared down to the floor for a few moments, feeling distraught about being all on his own. “… I wonder if Rich is waiting somewhere around here? Perhaps he’s just waiting for the right moment to jump out or something? You know, those childish little games that always seem… so fun. RICH!! If you’re here, PLEASE answer me!” Ryan called out in desperation, wanting somebody to respond to his pleas… even if that somebody wasn’t Rich Sherwin. The younger member of PCG slowly stepped forward before burrowing his hands deep into the pockets of the jeans he just happened to be wearing. That annoying silence continued to stand rule over everything, coating Ryan and his surroundings in an even deeper layer of muck. Inhaling and exhaling deeply… Ryan finally heard something that may have shown an indication of somebody else being in the complex. What sound was it? What could have been making something like that? Moore walked toward the door of the sound room and grabbed the doorknob, twisting it clockwise before opening it and stepping out into the corridor behind. Ryan could hear the clanging of metallic chair legs being pushed around, a small commotion of people and the mass rustle of goods as individuals began to sit down in their seats and adjust themselves for whatever was going to be happening next. Ryan closed his eyes and tried to think of where everyone could be… where everyone would happen to be in a building laid out like this.Finally, after thinking for as long as he could, Moore opened his eyes again and began to run down the corridor, taking the right option… sprinting through doors, pushing open whatever needed to be opened. After a period of three minutes, the ‘Pretty Cool Guy’ arrived at what could only be described as a ‘Conference Room’. A variety of chairs had been set out in neat fashion, a raised platform stood boldly infront of them all… like, some kind of almighty leader. On top of the platform stood a few tables, some chairs behind them and a table cloth that depicted the face of Ryan Moore along with a few CD’s spinning around his head. Eerily, ghostly images of media workers began to fill all of the seats, each of them looking ready to ask questions to the star and his management team. Ryan stared in disbelief at the amount of people that had turned up… and actually began to smile somewhat. What was all of this about? What was everything here for? Had Ryan Moore done something that he didn’t quite come to know or understand? How should he react about everything? Ryan looked set to walk toward to the stage, to stand upon it and address all of these people as if they were amazing fans of his or what-not. He wanted to get a little recognition for everything that he’d done… for everything that he’d tried to do as both a Professional Wrestler and a musical artist, however… just as the move was about to be made, a whole host of other people began to pour onto the stage infront of them. Business executives, dressed in sharp black business suits, slicked back hair and expensive looking jewellery. “Ladies and Gentlemen, we’d like to Thank you all… for taking the time, and the effort to come here today, as we unveil the announcement of our latest artist signing. For those of you who don’t know this individual, he has come a long way since leaving the World of Professional Wrestling… he has brought himself out of the prejudice that athletes are unable to sing… and he has stepped forward, to prove himself as being a TRUE musical talent, for the ultimate future. Without any future ado or prevention of this momentous occasion, please, may I present to you… Ryan Moore!” After shouting out the name of Ryan Moore, the executive and his fellows burst into a fierce wave of clapping and celebration. As the alternate Ryan Moore appeared from behind a door next to the stage, the people sitting on all of the metallic chairs began to stand and clap too, the musical genius receiving quite the standing ovation. Moore hopped up onto the platform and sat in the chair at the centre of the tables, a large stack of microphones being propped up by an interesting piece of equipment. The ghostly Ryan Moore looked extremely happy to be where he was… which was infinitely better than how the real Ryan Moore was feeling right now. “Everyone… now that the man of the hour is here, I believe that you will have many questions that you’d like to ask. However, due to the schedule that Mr Moore agreed to when coming to sign for our label, he will be travelling to another location for the beginning of recording on his third album… which I believe will be titled, “The Cruel Night Sky…” Ryan… you’re welcome to take everything from here.” “… Why, Thank You kindly. Now people, remember… I can’t be hanging around for too long… because I really have to start the recording of this third album.” The audience began to rustle around and move again, Ryan Moore sitting down at the table with a whole bunch of cameras beginning to flash and flicker around the place. The real Ryan Moore looked up to the table and saw himself sitting there… taking in all of the admiration, all of the joy and pleasure of being a musical artist. “… So, this is the way that I could have walked if I hadn’t decided to walk back into the ring with Rich Sherwin. This is the way, that everything could have worked if I didn’t have those dreams… those aspirations of working for PCW again… those ideals and goals of becoming the first PoP Tag Team Champions in the history of PCW… … We’d done the impossible in the realm that’d been known as NTICW. We’d climbed above everything and taken the World Tag Team Titles. I don’t know what it was… but for some reason, I just wanted to get that little bit more… I just wanted to have a Championship victory that… that… I could truly be proud of. Everyone that’d won these belts before the two of us… everyone, I just wanted to be able to have the same level of respect that they’d come to have.” Ryan Moore turned away from the conference, everything that’d been there, slowly beginning to vanish into the nothingness that it was beforehand. He’d experienced his vision of what would have happened if he didn’t run the route of Wrestling… if he’d just stuck to the music and done the things that he really wanted to do in life. He pushed through a set of double doors and looked down at the floor in a weary state before stopping everything and closing his eyes. In true fashion, the scene began to flicker again, shifting to the left and right before emerging at the point of the breakfast table where Ryan and Rich were both sitting. They arose from their slumber and looked at each other, their dreams and ideas suddenly seeming closer than they’d ever been before. What kind of issues would this spell for the team that’d been so sure they could mix both Professional Wrestling and PoP music? “… After all this time…” “… After everything that’d we’d been through…” “… After everything that’d come to win…” “… and everything that’d we’d come to lose…” “I now… know the answer…” “… To a question, that bears great importance.” “… You’re the one that’s holding me back… you’re the one that’s stopping me from getting my dreams.” Sherwin and Moore both raised a single arm from the table and pointed across to the other. After these visions filled with the delights of what their futures could have been alone, they were filled with the ideas that the other was truly holding them back from what could have been accomplished. How would this come to affect their Tag Team encounter with the World Tag Team Champions on Friday? How would this come to affect their chances of walking away with the Belts themselves? Would Sherwin and Moore be able to focus on the greater points of this whole contest… or would their personal dreams be the very items that prevent glory from falling into hand?
