Pulling Strings - Interlude - Hostage Situations

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15th, 2003 [Welcome to a Los Angeles movie theater, the home to many as a gala, a spacious event where many are only thought of as complete faces in the crowd. The stars are out, and the smiles are forced instead of brought on by what is shown upon the screen. Now sratch that thought of Los Angeles and move yourself to SOUTH CENTRAL Los Angeles. There, you will hear the rambunctious sounds of Jamar Wilson trying to get fine blacks on a Friday night, gunshots when the Crips and Bloods can't find anymore Hispanics to join there groups, and the racial profiling of blacks by the LAPD shown on video tape and in John Singleton.] Dante Jackson: Now this...this is the place for a real movie goer. [Dante's shiny black Ford sits parked in the Cinema Six parking lot as Jason Cunningham shuts the passenger side door along with it. He looks around, trying to catch the sight of any more white people, but finds himself a speck in the sea of black faces.] Jason Cunningham: Um....Dante, I think we should go. I mean sure you have a good time in the Kodak or even in the Shine if they mop up the asiles and made the seats less sticky than they are, but did we have to come to the Cinema Six? [Dante nods.] Jason: But...but...THEY LEAVE THE HOMICIDE TAPE ON THE GROUND! THEY EVEN HAVE A MURAL FULL OF THEM! Dante: And that scares you? I went to a muesem with you, even a GOOD CHARLOTTE CONCERT! AND YOU WANT TO LEAVE HERE?! NO JASE, YOU'RE STAYIN' FOR THIS ONE! Jason (under his breath): Oh God... [Jason puts his hands in his pockets with his head held downward as Dante smiles, wearing a black velour suit and a pair of white and black Nike shoes. Jason sticks close to him, dressed in the same PCW polo shirt and khakis. The crew of blue hooded figures stand at the entrance to the Cinema Six as many begin to exit the parking lot, either with gun totated in arm or white man in tow. A good smile is shown on Jason as he grills one of the men standing at the entrance who shriek back in fear.] Jason: Wow! Dante: What, what happened? Jason: I just scarred those gang members! Dante: That's because you're a pale white man coming to THEIR cinema. They probably went home to get their weapons and stuff to come...well break you. [Jason begins to whimper in fear as Dante pats him on the back.] Dante: Aww, its gonna be alright. Just as long as you don't hear 'break yo self foo'' behind us, then you're safe. Jason: WHY DID WE HAVE TO COME HERE?! Dante: Dude, it was either here, or the Apex Twelve on Crenshaw. Now there, they have the mural of Rodney King getting his revenge on the LAPD, and they stalk on white people just for fun, its actually better than some of the films! [Jason sighs heartedly as they push through the front doors of the Cinema Six, where the ticket counter has her hand already sticking out, waiting for that crisp five dollar bill to land nearly in her hand. Dante walks over, pressing hte palm of her hand as if it were some sick plaything before tapping Jason who looks at the refreshments, his mouth watering in dry anticipation for a drink.] Dante: EWW! Her hand is like....frickin' GOOGELY! Jason: That's called a palm, yours is too when you keep poking it like a retarded stepchild. Dante: Oh, well I'm not going to give her five dollars just to go see Cradle 2 The Grave! You do it. [Jason huffs, telling himself 'fine' at the same time before placing a five in the woman's hand. She retracts back before shrieking out in horror.] Ticket Counter Lady: 2319! WE HAVE A 2319! Jason: 2319?! TELL ME THIS ISN'T LIKE - [Before Jason can even finish his statement, he is implanted into the carpet of the theater by five men in black uniforms who work security. They analyze the five dollar bill, before one of them shouts to the others, forcing them all to make sure that the coast is clear and that they can go back to doing whatever they were doing before the incident. Jason gets up, groggily before hitting the floor once more as Dante begins to drag him into the theater, tickets in hand.] Jason: Monsters.....Inc. [Looking up at the refreshments, Dante and Jason pick out an assortment of gummy worms, chips, soda, popcorn, and two hot dogs before walking into Cradle 2 The Grave. The dimly lit theater is only that way because of the hideous woman in the front row of the theater with a few unpopped zits. Jason sits down as the sea of black once again shows up, nearly frighteneing the man as Dante sits down, enjoying his popcorn.] Dante: Good, but a bit salty I must say. Jason: Salty? That thing has more butter than your total glucose intake! [Dante takes a gander inside of the box, shaking it up before turning back to Jason.] Dante: And just how in the heck did you figure that out? Jason: Its called college and a over-protective mother, get used to it. [Dante could only nod towards that statement, since he did go to Penn State and graduate from there, although he detest Pennsylvania and almost anything that comes out of there. As the two men begin to sit snug in their chairs, the darkness of the movie theater envokes them into short jolts of fright, before the previews pop up on the giant screen located a few feet away. In all aspects of what has gone on inside of the theater, nothing has shocked Jason more, until a wild man dressed in black stands in front of the movie, shrieking and raving like a mad man!] Jason: AH! Dante: Don't worry, its just Kevin the Supermarket Tendant Who Couldn't Work At The Mall. Jason: Well that Supermarket Tendant HAS A MECHETTE! [Soon, gunshots ring out inside of the theater with Jason hitting the deck. He spies the floor for a second, as he hears no shrieking, not 'run for your lives', not even a peep of fear as the men and women sit there, watching the whole thing with a smile on their faces.] Jason: What the.... Dante: Jason, get yo' dumbass up! They just shot the tendant, everything is cool! COOL I SAY! [Jason stands up, checking out the scene before a record scratches, sending most of the audience fairly focused upon Jason as he cowardly smiles for the crowd.] Jason: Hi... Man In Crowd #1: HOLY....IT! THERE'S A WHITE MAN IN THE BUILDING! Man In Crowd #2: AND HE'S GOT A BLACK MAN HOSTAGE! [Dante screams out in horror as Jason points towards him leaning over picking up more popcorn.] Man In Crowd #1: THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?! Man In Crowd #2: HE'S....HE'S GOT HIM DOING - Man In Crowd #3: GOB DABBIT! DAT' BEZ A WHITEY! Man In Crowd #1 & #2: WE PROVED THAT ALREADY! [The third man in the crowd sits back down.] Main In Crowd #3: BORRY! Jason: We'll never be able to see the movie if this keeps up. Dante: You're telling me, YOU HAVE ME HOSTAGE BIOTCH! Jason: THEY ONLY THINK THAT BECAUSE I'M A WHITE GUY IN A MOVIE WITH A BUNCH OF BLACK PEOPLE! [Silence.] Dante: You've done done it now... [Jason whimpers as the majority of the crowd stands up, maces, axes, and the sort of weapons made from the 20th century stand and aim at Jason, his pants officially soiled at the current moment as the scene fades to black.]
