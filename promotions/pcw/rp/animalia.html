Date: not entered [Scene opens inside Flaymin’s hotel room.He sits on the bed and flips through the different channels on TV. His valet, Heather, walks into the room and looks at Flaymin who is busy watching a documentary about the pets of famous celebrities] Heather: What’s that? Flaymin: Famous animals. [Heather points at the TV screen] Heather: And who’s that? Flaymin: That’s cheechee, Marilyn Monroe’s elephant. Heather: Marilyn Monroe? Flaymin: Yep.Apparently, just before she kicked the bucket she bought an elephant.I can see why, though. Heather: They’re tame animals? [Flaymin looks at Heather and shakes his head] Flaymin: No, because cheechee has a big donger. Heather: That’s disgusting. Flaymin: Wait until you see Tommy Lee’s Trouser Snake!They’ve got home video footage of it and everything coming up soon!! [Heather stares at Flaymin with a strange look on her face] Flaymin: What?? Heather: It’s not an animal! Flaymin: Oh yeah?Wait until you see it spit its white venom! [Heather rolls her eyes] Flaymin: I think I want to buy animal. Heather: You mean like a pet? Flaymin: No, like a sidekick.I mean, Michael Jackson and Bubbles the Monkey seemed to have a blast together- I’d like to have a relationship with an animal just like that. Heather: Michael molested that poor monkey, you freak! Flaymin: No he didn’t.He just liked feeling all that fur. Heather: It wasn’t just the fur he was feeling. Flaymin: Ok, so he squeezed its nipple every once in a while- haven’t we all? [The room grows eerily quiet for a moment. Flaymin looks at Heather and Heather simply turns around and shakes her head in dismay] Flaymin: That’s it, I’m getting me an animal. Heather: Are you sure? Flaymin: Yes, I could do with another slave. Heather: But you have to feed it….And clean after it….And nurture it….And get it checked at the vet… Flaymin: No.I’ll let it feed itself.I don’t clean after myself, so why should it have to?Nurture what??And do you think if I got the vet to check the animal AND me, then I’ll get a discount? Heather: You’re an idiot. Flaymin: Eh.An idiot with an animal is cooler than an idiot with nothing. Heather: You haven’t thought this through!I mean, what kind of animal are you planning to buy, anyway? Flaymin: I don’t know.A good one. Heather: What will you do with the animal if it doesn’t work out? Flaymin: Why would it have to work out?Its not I’m going to run it through a maze or anything. Heather: No I didn’t mean it like that. [Flaymin stares at Heather with a look of frustration on his face] Flaymin: Do you even know the first thing about pets? [Heather sighs and begins to walk away] Flaymin: Where are you going? Heather: To get the bail money ready- because we all know you’ll probably wind up in jail after this. Flaymin: Ha!If I get in trouble I’ll train my animal to bust me out.You know, like the flying monkeys in the Wizard of Oz! Heather: Once again…. You’re an idiot. Flaymin: No need for jealousy.I’ll buy a pokemon to ‘nurture’ and feed.Heh, heh, heh…. [Flaymin walks out of the hotel room as the scene fades to black. His destination? The biggest, greatest, loudest, funnest….. (and closest) pet store in town! ]
