Intruder 101 (part 2)

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-14-03 Brianna had just managed to untangle herself from the mess of the ropes that were holding her down to the chair, when the scene had cut out. Her wrists were aching, but she just rubbed the pain away as she looked around waiting for her attacker to sneak up on her again. Feeling that things are clear she cautiously walks out of the hallway and peers into the living room where she sees a man with ruffled black hair talking on a cell phone. Brianna tries to find somewhere else to hide out, but sees another guy come in through the door which causes her to stay where she's at. 'Still no luck?' the man on the phone asks as he covers it up with his right hand. 'No. I checked all of this place, inside and out, and I can't find that damn mouse anywhere. She must be hiding him somewhere.' the guy replies looking very disgusted and tired of not finding what he's looking for. 'Just go back in there and interrogate her until she tells you where it's at.' with that the man walks off and a look of actualization hits Brianna. If he sees that she's not in there, she is going to be in some huge trouble when he finds her. Meanwhile the other man continues to chat on the phone and it seems as though he is fighting his way through a ribbing. 'Boss I'm sorry, but this is very complicated. That mouse isn't stupid, and this lady isn't cooperating...No we haven't found Kramer....Well we know this chick was talking to Kramer before he disappeared....We're on top of it....Marty I promise we got this. We just need a bit more time....Ok gotcha.' he slams the cell phone shut and pushes it into his pocket with a snarl on his face. 'What a bastard.' Just then the other guy comes running in looking distraught. 'What's wrong?' 'She...she's gone.' 'How can that be? Didn't you tie her up?' 'She got away somehow because she's not there anymore. What do we do if she dodged out of the house and is calling the cops right now? I don't wanna go back to jail...I just got out!' the man pleads as a look of worry overcomes him. He starts to rub at his chin as stains of sweat start to seep through his white shirt. 'Don't worry, she didn't leave. I know that much. We'll just search the house, and when we find her we will give her even more of a problem than she started off with.' Brianna can be heard taking a big gulp as she pulls herself into a closet and starts wondering what to do. 'These guys want me because of Kramer. This is just not good. And where is Grimer? They said they haven't found him but what if he's worried. Poor little guy....' she continues to ramble until a small sound is heard from above. She look up and pulls out a pen light from her pocket and points it towards the direction of the sound. Standing there chewing away at a pellet is Grimer! 'I am beginning to understand why they want you. You're smart...smarter than most human beings even! And I promise I’m not going to let them take you, but I want you to know that all of this is too much for little ol’ me.' she stops talking as a shadow blocks the light from coming under the door anymore. Slowly the knob is turned and the closet door is thrown open. Standing there are both men who look displeased with her escaping. 'We found our little stowaway.' 'I say we skin her alive.' the man blurts out with a twisted smile. Brianna holds onto Grimer and slides him into the jacket pocket behind her so the men can’t see him. Brianna then allows them to pull her out as she is drug back to the chair she had just recently escaped. 'Look lady, we just want the mouse and you can get back to life as it was.' 'I don’t really think that’s possible.' Brianna replies candidly. 'So we have ourselves a smart elic eh?' the man with black hair raises his voice losing any and all patience. 'No what you have is a big problem. You think I'm just a helpless girl don't you? I mean first of all I'm in my own house, so I have access to tons of tricks.' she cracks a smile which catches them both off guard. 'What kind of tricks?' the smaller guy asks sounding intrigued. 'I would like to sit here and tell you, but I have things to do.' a big ball of smoke quickly covers the room, and when it clears Brianna is gone! The men go into a frenzy looking all around the place for her, but she’s nowhere in sight. The bigger of the two, and obviously the leader, picks the chair up and slams it against the wall in frustration shattering it to pieces. 'This bitch is really starting to try my patience. Kramer and her think it’s funny to play their games, and prolong our mission, but when we get a hold of them and take them to the boss, I am going to enjoy watching him torture them with everything in the book. And that mouse….once the boss is done doing whatever it is he plans on doing, that mouse is going to have a meeting with Frisky.' 'I thought you said I could keep him? I always wanted a pet.' 'You’re a moron! Why would you want to keep a mouse as a pet, that’s just absurd.' 'But Grimer’s a special mouse remember? Besides I think he likes me.' 'Why would he like you you fool? We have tried to catch him for months, and have driven him away from his owner. If that mouse has as many emotions as brain cells, then he despises you.' 'Maybe we should get back to catching that girl?' 'Right.' With that both men separate and start there search yet again for Brianna. The scene slowly fades to black.... It's a real wonder how Brianna can keep up a career of wrestling with events like these. I mean she has a match tonight, and here she is fighting off intruders in her home. She also has a match on Friday against one of the best PCW female wrestlers of all time, but that's only if she can make it out of her own house in one piece.
