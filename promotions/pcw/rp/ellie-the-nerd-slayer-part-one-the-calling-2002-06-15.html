'Ellie~! The Nerd Slayer' - Part One : The Calling.

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/06/02 On Turmoil, Kannon achieved what he has wanted ever since he first arrived in PCW last December... He won the right to face the PCW World Heavyweight Champion 1 on 1... No Battle Royal like at March Madness, no Triple Threats where he could lose without being pinned... 1 on 1 with the Champion, who after his win against the returning Frisco, will be none other than Big Alan Steele... ...Anyone else in his position would be looking straight ahead to that... Blinkered, but Kannon isn’t “Everybody”.... He has beaten Big Alan Steele before... In a ringside melee that involved Jeremy Howard, the Chef and Eleanor, he got the pin... The World knew it was cheating... Kannon takes it as skill... ....So not only does his own arrogance blind him as to the enormity of the task that faces him, his pride is playing a part in interfering. He never lost the Continental Title, so will not see it going around another man’s waist so on Tuesday he fights Legend Inc. & Snow for a shot at his Title, wanting to win it back maybe even more than he wants to fight Steele.... Oh, and as if all of that wasn’t distraction enough, something... Strange, is about to happen to Ellie~! “Oh my GAWD!!! Like, where did you buy your clothes? Tramps ‘R’ Us?!?!!?” Our shot opens up in the territory of the Prep, their lair from which they prey on the week and attack the needy.... THE CORRIDOR... In front of their lockers stand the 3 Preppy Vixens of the High School... Looking like they were dressed by the same automated “This Season’s Fashionatron 700” Unit... Or maybe it’s just the Cheerleader’s outfit. In front of the lead Prep, barely distinguishable from her colleagues apart from the small difference in hair shade stands what can only be described as... A Geek.... and not one who uses his “geekyness” as a statement... No, this kid is sad and the highlight of his day is probably getting home to play with some Warhammer models... Lead Prep : “And, like, that hair.... What is up with that? Did you like SO use new L’Oreal Geek Grease or something?” She grins at his discomfort and the 2 Preps behind her laugh along at it, and all those in hearing range in the corridor do the same, the poor kid looking more and more conscious of everyone paying attention to it... Lead Prep : “Well, aren’t you going to, like, SAY anything Mr Nerdarama?” The kid drops his head and mumbles... Nerd : “I....Er... I...” The girl tormenting him cringes and waves her hand in front of face, screwing it up in disgust and leaning back. Lead Prep : “Ewwwwwwwzies... Like, what did you brush your teeth with? A sewer?” This is the final straw for the poor child and as a tear runs down his cheek from underneath his thick Black rimmed glasses he runs down the corridor, to the laughs and chuckles of everyone lining it... The 3 Preppy girls stand triumphant and put their books in the lockers... 2nd Prep : “Wow Abby, that last insult SO rocked my World, I, like, SO have to use it!” All 3 of them close their lockers and at the same precise second flick their hair and turn to stride victorious down THEIR corridor, attracting that attention of all the males who stare at them with drooling mouths, and the bitchy stares of the girls, wishing they were like them... Abby : “Ugh, I like, SO have to sit through the boringest lesson eve-” She is cut short a one of the light fittings falls from the ceiling and hits her right on the back of the head... Dropping her to the floor to a huge gasp from all around her. Blood starts to pour out onto the floor as her 2 friends drop down, panicking and crying... 2nd Prep : “Like.. DO something!!! Her hair is all messed up!!!” 3rd Prep : “Somebody, stop the bleeding... It’s going all over her uniform!” Our shot begins to fade out on the motionless Prep, her eyes glazed over and not responding to any of the panic around her... To each generation a Slayer is born,She alone will stand against the forces of Geekyness....She is... The Nerd Slayer *Cue Nerf Herder Theme* “Hello?!?!? Do you, like, speak American?” Our shot opens up on the outside of a small store, in one of those dirty, crummy, crime-ridden cities in America that aren’t Hubbardville where we see the disgruntled figure of Xavier Kannon’s valet and fiancé Eleanor Hall storming out, muttering to herself and due to some unknown reason, very quickly healed from the Executioner’s Chair she took Friday night. Eleanor: “I... Like, SO knew I should have got a personal shopper, I hate talking to people.... In fact, I hate people.... Especially ones I don’t like..” As she walks out she is nearly knocked flying my a bearded man in a Red, Green and Blue checked shirt who just strides along down the street ignoring her, a fact she doesn’t appreciate even though it was she who bumped into him.. Eleanor : “Like.... Excuse me?!?!?!?” The scruffy man carries on, ignoring her whiny wail... Eleanor : “Hello?!?!? Mr “Could I Dress Any More Like A Wife Beater If I Tried”?!?!? It’s me, the girl you nearly hit into... What if I was knocked out and got amnesia, the first thing I could wake up to see was your dress sense and accept it as something I would be seen dead in!!!” She brushes herself down and stomps her feet in frustration.. ... The former Extreme Champion is wearing a Pink, skinny fit “Preppy ‘til I Die” t-shirt with Silver writing on the front paired with sandblasted, flared jeans with Silver sequinned stars running down the right leg. Her long, wavy brown hair with Blond highlights is untied and blows in the slight breeze, and around her wrists are an assortment of bands and bracelets capped off with a chunky watch. She continues to mutter under her breath as she sets off down the street... Eleanor : “I told him this was his promo but no, he had to be all “Legend isn’t bothering to mention opponents or storylines so why should I?” so I had to get all “Well what do you want ME to do?” and he was all “You take the promo, do something with a snide remark about how you should at least MENTION something vaguely linked to current events in a promo...” and now I’m stuck here, having to think of something to do that’s entertaining for the remaining 1 and a half pages of this RP!” The prep navigates the people walking towards her, taking the time to sneer or roll her eyes at the badly dressed ones while thinking of something to do... ... But it appears this “Something” may have found her as a Black car creeps slowly behind her. As she continues to whine under her breath a 16 year old wearing a tight “Blood Bowl” t-shirt and thick rimmed spectacles bumps into her and as she looks up to yell a him her face turns sour.. Eleanor : “Like.... Ewwwwwwwwzies!” The kid looks at her naturally beautiful face and grins creepily, his greasy, thin Brown near Black hair falling down either side of his pimpley face... Eleanor : “Hello? Like WHAT are you looking at... In fact, WHAT are you, you walking advert for the pro-Bullying cause?” His eyes drop and he walks past her to her disgust, and a few strands of his long hair brush against her t-shirt... Eleanor : “Hey! Mr “Taken Out A Restraining Order Against Cleanliness”!!!” As he disappears into the crowd she frowns at where his hair touched that shoulder of her Pink t-shirt... Eleanor : “Ugh... Now I know how those Birds feel when Oil Tankers crash...” As she walk off the camera stays put and turns to show the mysterious Black car once more, this time it’s tinted window is rolled down showing a smartly dressed man dressed all in Black, his round glasses housing dark eyes that stare only at the Prep... Mystery Man : “It is her.... She is the chosen one. Those insults show she has already started to harness her powers...” From within the darkened confines of the vehicle, an English accented voice is heard speaking as the window rolls up... “The Watcher’s Council have been keeping an eye on her or months now... Her insults are quicker and sharper than any I have seen before, and her mocking skills are unparalleled.... She is your Slayer...” The car moves off again we zoom up the street to catch up the fiancé of Xavier Kannon, muttering under her breath at all the people who pass her, each accompanied by a sneer or a smirk... Eleanor : “Poor... Poor... Trashy... Drug Addict... Poor.... Ugh, I don’t even WANT to know what he is... Or her..... My Hubbard, they let you breed?...” Her light Blue eyes, relishing the quick fire insults spewing from her mouth suddenly narrow and she turns around, finally noticing that the car has been following, something she doesn’t look too happy about as she puts her hands on her hips... Eleanor : “Hello?!?!? Do I LOOK like Jaden? Stop curb crawling!” Just as she is about to turn away the window rolls down again and the well pronounced voice of the man in Black we saw previously speaks to her, hidden in the darkness of the car... “Miss Hall, we have been sent to find you..... You are the chosen one and this is your calling...” In response to this her radiant face is a mix of shock and pleasure... Eleanor : “Did Grams finally die and leave me all her money?” “No..... You are the Slayer.... The latest of a long bloodline stemming back to the day the Sinclair Spectrum was invented... You alone will stand between humanity and the nerds, the geeks, and the Internet roleplayers!” He face looks puzzled, seemingly weighing up the pros and cons of this situation, then realising as Kannon is effectively on strike due to not being mentioned in 3 successive promos by Legend Inc. and that she is now solely responsible for his material decides this could be a nice little storyline and inquires... Eleanor : “Are you sure it’s me? Everyone hates Nerds...” “We are sure.... We have monitored you... You have heightened senses... You can “Feel” when a Geek is near, and you have recovery times far quicker than it would normally take an injury to heal...” Eleanor : “Well.... I am feeling good after taking that Executioner’s Chair (OOC: *Angry Face*)... And seeing as I can’t think of anything else to fill Xavi’s TV time with while he is on strike all I have to say is...... Like, WAY Cool!!! I’m SO in!” The door opens and the Preppy valet eagerly hops in as the window closes, accepting her responsibility as has been laid down by the ancients... Well, guys in the early 80’s, to repel the tide of Geekdom... “Um.... If I’m, you know, DOING this thing... Can I get a new wardrobe?” As Ellie~!’s request is heard the car pulls off and we start to fade to Black on it disappearing into the distance... “Sure, you’ll have you own expense account...” Black. “Like...... WAY...... Cool!” To be continued.......
