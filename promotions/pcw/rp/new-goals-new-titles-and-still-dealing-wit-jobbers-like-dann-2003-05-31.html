New goals, new titles, and still dealing wit' jobbers like Danny Boy!

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-03 The scene opens up to Hank and Jenn sitting backstage in Hank’s locker room, both cuddled close together on a black leather sofa. Hank is watching a little television as Jenn is just enjoying be with her boyfriend of years and years. Hank eventually gets bored of watching television and stupid PCW jobbers trying to make a name for them selves, so he flips off the television. Right as Hank turns off his television new PCW reporter Miranda Garcia enters and takes a seat. Hank looks at her as Jenn then looks over at her now and gives her an evil eye.”May the great PCW Superstar help you?””Yeah well my name’s Miranda and I kind of wanted a word with you about your match with Dan Osbourne this Tuesday Turmoil.””Hank always has time to give a little insight about his upcoming match with some no name piece of gutter trash that calls himself Dan Osbourne. Miranda, that’s what you jus’ said your name was right?” (Miranda nods) “Okay good, didn’t want to forget it after two seconds of hearing it. Anyway Miranda, I have faced numerous people like Dan Osbourne and come out on top as the victorious one. If you read on the PCW’s official website and in the recent issue of the PCW magazine you’ll notice this match has been booked because both of us have been on losing streaks as of late. Dan is more used to this then I am of course, I mean I have at least won the PCW Extreme Championship one time in my long-term PCW career.””Thank you for those words, but what I really want to know is what you plan to do, what titles you plan to go after?””You know I’ve never really been fond of the PCW Cruiserweight Championship, but for some strange reason right now I feel as though it’s the next step up in my PCW career. I mean I’ve proven I’m the most extreme the PCW has to offer, I take more than any man in the business and dish it right back out. Now I want to prove I can get in that ring and not use weapons, but use my mind, and my skill to prevail. The current cruiserweight champion is Miss Moonlight right?” (Miranda nods) “Yeah well she won’t be if I get a one-on-one match wit’ her. This is my next step in the PCW and then I’ll keep moving up until I am the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World! Yes my goals are out there, but that’s how you have to be in this business. That’s how you have to show you mean business in the wrestling industry!””What if by chance you lose your match with Dan Osbourne?””Then he cheated, he somehow got a fluke victory, but that won’t stop my goals. It will only put me off a little. Miranda, you don’t want to mess wit’ me, you don’t want to get in my way when I’m focused on accomplishing a given task. Miranda, I’m setting my life on hold until I beat Miss Moonlight until she is bloody and willing to submit! I’m going to batter her so badly she’ll be on her hands and knees handing me that damn title! My goal is simply, my goal is clear, I want to be crowned PCW Cruiserweight Champion! I want to be crowned PCW Continental Champion, and hell I want to be crowned PCW World Heavyweight Champion! By chance if I lose this match wit’ Dan Osbourne, then I’ll jus’ move on wit’ my goals and let Dan have his little celebration, but trust me he won’t win because he can’t win.””You left for quite sometime Hank, why?””My big toe was fractured and I was informed by my doctor that I am in no condition to compete…” (laughs) “Nah, but really my back injury flared up again and I had to have it taken care of, didn’t take long. Actually it took longer than I expected, but I’m back now and I’m ready to kick some major ass in the PCW ring like I always do!””Well your goals sound good, and I hope you accomplish them.” (smiles) “Thank you for your time Hank~!”(smily cockily) ”Oh believe me the pleasure was all yours.” (smirks) “Nah, the pleasure was mine too, thank you for taking your time to interview me.”Miranda gets up and exits as Jenn and Hank go back to cuddling on Hank’s black leather sofa in his PCW locker room. The scene cuts off to black.
