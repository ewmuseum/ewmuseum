Is this the end?

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 Innocence is a word not cared by many, though curse, boo and hiss at you, not caring that you have never left their grace. They go by what the see, they do not know what has really happened, nor do they try to find out. Some may wonder what there kind of people think of feel. Jesus Christ was executed, all because a crowd decided so. They had loved and worshipped him, and then because someone told them to hate him, they did so. People like this do not have a mind of their own; they simply do what they are told, no matter who tells them. They have no will power, though they are loved. Tuesday Turmoil was a night Anton would never forget, for THE Multiple Man had defeated him, though he had not lost his title. Anton’s International Incident teammates, Golden Game aided Anton in retaining his title, though in the process Anton had to feel the fateful heart-ache of defeat, for THE Multiple Man won through disqualification. The thing that hut Anton most though, was the gearing of the crowd towards him, once again they fell short of realizing that he loved them, and that he would always be on their side, would they ever cheer for him again? The heat of the sun shone down like a shepherd looking over his flock. The sky is so blue, you could get lost in its deepness. The day was nothing but perfect for most, though when you are engaged in vigorous training, it is not so perfect. Anton stood in the lone garden; sweat trickled from his forehead like a waterfall. His hair was wet with perspiration, as was his orange t-shirt with the markings ‘PCW’ on it. He slowly raised his hands and put them together, like a Christian would do when celebrating mass, he then took a deep breath, jumped in the air and shot his right leg out as far and high as it could probably go, while doing this, he didn’t make a sound accept his foot cutting through the air, like a knife to an onion. He was in a garden, his own garden in fact. He was in the outskirts of New York City, Manhattan to be more accurate. He was home, the place he was reared, with his family. It got lonely in New Jersey, after his House Keeper retired, so he decided to come home to the people he loved. He felt like he never wanted to step foot in New Jersey again, or for that matter, in a wrestler ring, doubts had been plaguing him for so long, he was but 14 years of age, and already he was in the ring with men twice his age, and twice his size. When he started wrestling, he did it to give his parents the desperate financial support they needed, though when their financial situation was stable, he continued to wrestle, for he had grown a lust for the sport, and for the cheering crowds. It gave him a mighty adrenaline rush, like nothing else could do. Though now, doubts swam through his mind. The garden in which he stood was that of his families. It was a small garden, and there was very little space, as flowers and soil infested it, but there was just enough for Anton to do some well-needed training. Through his Martial Arts lessons, he had learned a new type of Art, which focuses on the mind rather than the body, with little effort you can sweat profusely. It was helping Anton’s fitness and endurance. He had not been booked in PCW much since he had one the Cruiserweight title, and all he gets is constant abuse for not ‘trying’ because he never showed up at PTC Headquarters to train, and no-one ever saw him cut promos, yet what was the point when he was never being booked. Though the training that he had missed would now come back at him, as the next he day would have a match for the Cruiserweight title, against the infamous Rent-A-Hero, and someone who Anton seemed to be fighting constantly. It would be at PCW’s Pay-Per-View, Spring Fling 2002. It would be a brutal battle to say the least, as Rent-A-Hero intended on returning to PCW with vengeance, after being fired previously, he was back and was taking PCW by storm, he was a fan-favourite and everyone was tipping him to take the Cruiserweight title from Anton’s grasp. THE Multiple Man and Anton had faced off three times now, and they would again at the Pay-Per-View. Anton had kept his title from THE Multiple Man for this long, and he hoped he could continue to do so. Though, if he did some how retain the title, he didn’t know how long he would want it. Wrestling was the hardest thing he had ever had to do, and he wasn’t sure he could stick the pressure anymore, for months he had been in the ring fighting against the world’s very best, but he was a fourteen-year-old child. Who else of his age could stick such pressure? The media were hounding him at every turn, he was being nagged and nagged about training, he couldn’t stick it, he felt like just scrapping it all and going back to the thing he desired so much, being a normal kid. What would be hold at Spring Fling? Anton didn’t know, but he knew that the world was coming down on him, is this the end?
