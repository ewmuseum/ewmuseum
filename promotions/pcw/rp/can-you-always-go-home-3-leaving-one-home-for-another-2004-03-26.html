Can You Always Go Home #3: Leaving one home for another

26 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/04 His return to PCW was only hours away. Ryan Knox had spent the last few days spending time with friends from his hometown of Boston, MA and the majority of the waning hours thinking. Was what he was doing the right move? Were his reasons just? At this point it didn’t matter. He was about to leave for the airport, for a long, hard road trip back to glory…or deeper into hell. Knox looked off his back patio out into the vanilla sky overhead. It was an unusually warm day in New England for mid March. As a warm breeze brushed across his face, he realized it was beautiful moments like these that made it harder for him to go into the business of hurting people. The pools of color that painted the sky struck Knox deep inside and made him somewhat sentimental, What could he do about it? Call Boda and tell him he couldn’t come back after all? because the formation of clouds and a warm breeze made him miss the days of standing watching the world pass by with his wife. He respected Boda too much for that. He respected himself too much for that.“Sigh, What’s wrong with me”Knox shook his head in disbelief before turning back into the house and locking his backdoor. Knox grabbed his last bag, an Oakley backpack off his kitchen table as he made his way out of the house. As Knox headed for the front door, he felt chills and his stomach was turning like someone emptied a jar of bees into it. He grabbed the door handle and stopped for a moment to think before leaving.“Am I about to get shot or something? I hope my plan doesn’t crash”Knox had seen movies of clairvoyance before and he wasn’t keen to get on an airplane feeling this way. He was pretty sure Karma couldn’t stick its foot up his ass any further, but why chance it.“Screw it”He opened his front door to leave, only to see his wife making her way to the door. Knox was floored to see her, it had been over a month see he last saw her and the separation and even after looking at her photos for hours upon hours he had almost forget how stunning she was. Amy fully knowing she was going to see Ryan was still almost caught off guard to see him before she had knocked on the door to her own home. She stood in front of him dressed modestly in a pair of jeans and a zip hooded sweatshirt but it was when she dressed down that her true beauty shone through the brightest. The two looked at each other searching for the words but they kept getting lost somewhere between their heart and mouths. Knox could do nothing but stare into her soulful brown eyes. As each instant passed he fell in love with her over and over again. Like most men deep in love, he knew he had to hold back his feelings, things between them were rough and he didn’t want to say the wrong words and run her off. So he kept it simple.“Hi” Knox said with a quirk in his voice.“Hi” Amy responded She folded her arms, part to keep her hands warm and in some way to fulfill what she truly wanted deep down inside, She wished he’d reach his massive arms around her and cloak her in security and make her feel like everything would be alright again, even if it wasn’t true. That’s what she needed. In his heart Knox felt that’s what she wanted Yet, she was the one who left, who quit. He would do anything for another day with her, but ultimately, the ball was in her court.“What are you doing here?” Knox inquired“Don’t I live here?”“I don’t know do you?”She looked away to hide her face from him but only for a moment so she could regain herself before looking back at him.“My sister told me you were leaving to go back tomorrow, I was in the neighborhood and thought I’d see if it were true.”In reality she had driven by the house several times to see, if he had left yet. Trying to build up courage to say the words that would stop him from leaving. She had left him but there hadn’t been a day she didn’t shed a tear because she felt half dead without him. Driving by wanting to intervene in the others life was something both Amy and Ryan were guilty of. Today she finally stepped to the plate and came to see him.“I tried calling you, I left messages. I try all the time Amy. It’s like screaming into a dark hallway, I never get anything back but my own voice”He wasn’t meaning to sound angry or argue but he had become so desperate to have her back that his frustration flowed naturally.“Ok, I don’t know what I was thinking” She turned and began to walk away“Amy!” Knox called out to his wife. She turned back with pools of tears building in her eyes to match the tears he was holding back.“When will you stop running from me?” Knox asked as his insides erupted in his chest. Every emotion known to man boiled beneath his skin. It was a silent scream. Inside he was screaming I love you a thousand times over. But if he uttered the words he was sure to breakdown.“I just came to see if you were really going back. I needed to see with my own eyes” She calmly told her husband fighting back her tears.He had given up the game for a life at home with her and now that she ran from him, he was running from everything else. Running back to a new life at an old familiar place. Deep inside Knox knew all she had to do was say the words and he wouldn’t go, He’d stay right there with her for a string of eternities.“Yea, I’m going back” He confessed“Why? What could you possibly have to prove? Why would you ever want to go back there?”“You wouldn’t understand Amy, I have my reasons”“I wouldn’t understand? I’m your wife Ryan, I’ll at least try to understand”“You’re my wife? Where has my wife been for the last 3 months of my life? Where was my wife when I called begging for forgiveness for whatever I did. I begged, You couldn’t even tell me why you left. You just ran”“Who’s running now?”With those three words, that simple question it felt like she drove a stake into his heart.“Amy, I got to go. I’ve got a plane to catch, I can’t do this now.”The two gazed at each other, resolution could’ve been on the horizon, but Amy was right he was running. “Listen, This is your house too Amy. Please stay here while I’m gone, use your keys, I won’t be back much. For the first time in months, sleep in your home.”“I’ll think about it” She said half cold hearted, it was a defense mechanism to him leaving Knox could do nothing but nod in response, he leaned over and placed a gentle kiss on his wife cheek and got into his Escalade and drove off leaving the love of his life standing watching on him leave on his doorstep....
