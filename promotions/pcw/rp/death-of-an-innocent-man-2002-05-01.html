Death of an Innocent Man

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-1-02 (There lying in bed is the new PCW Rising Star champion Kid Krazie, asleep and dreaming with his newly attained title clutched in his arms. Next to him in his bed is Lu-Lu the shopping cart also off in dreamland.) “It is said that while awake your nightmares and demons can’t harm you! But as soon as you close your eyes they are there and ready!” “I have lived my life for twenty two years, fourteen of which have been tainted by a crime my father committed. A man dying by my father’s own hands! All of which I witnessed with my own two brown eyes!” “Why must I suffer for the crimes of my father? Why must I witness the murder time after time? Here I am asleep yet again and like all the other times I fall asleep I am brought back to that fateful night of May 21, 1988.” “This is the dream that haunts me in my sleep, fourteen years and it still remains embedded into my memory like it happened just yesterday!” (The scene shifts from a peaceful sleeping Krazie to the streets of suburban New York in the evening hours. There on sitting in the parking lot of a corner store in Port Ewen of New York are six white men and little Kid Krazie who is eight years old at this time.) Krazie: “Hey daddy!?” (Krazie is sitting in the bed of a white Ford Pickup truck yelling for his dad who is sitting around another parked pickup truck with five other tattooed white men. These men have swastikas and other tattoo’s representing white power covering their arms and chest. They are wearing blue jeans, sleeveless t-shirts and all are holding a beer in their hand. One man, the biggest out of the group, turns around to Krazie.) Dad: “What is it?” (Krazie turns around and points at a rusty red pinto estate stopped at the red light at the nearby intersection. Krazie’s father and the other five men turn and look as the pinto estate rolls by them.) Dad: “That’s a n!gg*r!” (The six white men stand defiant with their chest puffed out and their fists clinched as the pinto estate rolls past. Krazie notices a young African American kid sitting in the passenger seat and what Krazie thinks is his father sitting behind the wheel. The kid is wearing a baseball cap and tossing a baseball into the air and then catching it on it’s way down. The father and son are laughing and enjoying their time together…. That would all change in a matter of minutes.) Dad: “Let’s get that damned n!gg*r!” (The six white men separate, two guys go with Krazie’s dad and pile into his truck, as the other three run and jump into another truck. They peel out and speed off trying to catch up to the Pinto Estate.) Krazie: “What’s a n!gg*r?” (Krazie’s dad begins to laugh at the question as he points ahead of him at the rusty red Pinto Estate.) Dad: “That’s a n!gg*r!” (Krazie looks in the direction and notices the Pinto Estate still driving at a slow pace. Krazie’s dad leads the charge as he rear ends the Pinto Estate. The pinto estate slams on it’s brakes and comes to a screeching halt. Meanwhile Krazie’s dad stops his truck a few feet back and turns on the blinding lights of his trucks bright lights. The second truck follows suit as it pulls up right next to Krazie’s dad.) Dad: (To Krazie) “Stay in the truck!” (Krazie sits back and watches as the six men climb out of the two trucks and watches as each man picks up a wooden baseball bat from the bed of the truck.) Krazie: “Ok…” (Krazie watches as his father walks halfway between the trucks and the pinto estate. He sees the African American father get out of his car and stand by the door looking back at the six white men.) Dad: “You in the wrong part of town boy!?” (Krazie watches in horror as the six men begin to hurl insults towards the black male all the while taking step by step getting closer and closer to the black man. Soon the six men are surrounding the black man who has fear in his eyes as he looks at the furious white men.) Dad: “Looks like I’m gonna have to teach this here n!gg*r some proper respect!” (The black man begins to question Krazie’s dad but is cut short as Krazie’s dad pops him in the stomach with the baseball bat. Krazie’s eyes pop wide as he witnesses the brutal beating that follows. The black man is laying on his stomach trying to cover up as the six men beat him with the baseball bats! After about ten minutes of the brutal beating the bloody man lays still and unmoving. Krazie’s dad turns his attention to the young black kid in the passenger seat.) Dad: “What do we have here? A little n!gg*r boy! Hey boy why don’t you get out and save your pappy!?” (As Krazie’s dad opens the driver side door of the pinto estate, the bloody black man manages one last burst of energy as he staggers to his feet causing Krazie’s dad to turn back around.) Dad: “What!? You ain’t dead yet!? Damn fellas these n!gg*rs just don’t die!” (As the bloody black man lunges in an attempt to save the life of his father Krazie’s dad swings and cracks the baseball, breaking it in two, against the bloody black man’s face! The bloody black man crumbles to the ground dead even before he hits the concrete!) Krazie: “NOOOOOO!!!!” (Krazie’s dad stands above the dead man with a half broken bloody bat in his hand as the five other white guys stand there nodding their heads in satisfaction.) Dad: “Let’s go….” (Krazie’s dad looks around at his five white friends as they look up at him with satisfaction and a bit of happiness in their eyes.) Dad: “I said… Let’s go! NOW!” (The six men walk towards the trucks throwing the bats in the bed of the trucks and piling in ready to go. As they drive off Krazie, who is still in the bed of his father’s truck, looks back and sees the young black kid hovering above his dead father with tears streaming down his face! The dream fades to nothingness.) “I can remember the kid’s face like I saw it yesterday! The questioning look, the sadness, and the hatred! Oh the hatred! Like throwing a Molotov Cocktail and watching the little flame burst into a huge massive flame reminds me of the look in his eyes…” “I never could talk to my father about the murder, never even brought it up with him! But, I remember after this act of murder my father was idolized by all the other white supremacists!” “It always confused me because my father was no hero! He killed an innocent man with his son watching on in horror! The man died trying to save his son from the same fate that befell him!” (The scene shifts back to Krazie sleeping in his bed as he bolts upright with sweet rolling down from his forehead. He climbs out of bed and walks into the bathroom where splashes his face with some cold water. As he dries his face off he looks into the mirror. He gazes into the eyes of his reflection.) “Fourteen years haunted by one event that I had nothing to do with! The murder of an innocent man just because the color of his skin! A young boy forced to watch his father die in front of his own eyes! But there is one thing that I can’t seem to shake away…” (Krazie smiles as the thoughts cross his mind.) Krazie: “… Satisfaction….” “Watching a man die by my father’s own hands…. Superiority! I look at my father now, like his five friends looked at him on that fateful day….” Krazie: “…Satisfaction…” (The scene fades with Krazie standing there with a smile on his face, as he stares at his reflection thinking of that fateful night fourteen years ago.)
