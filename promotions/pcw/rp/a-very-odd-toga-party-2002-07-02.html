Date: 02/07/02 In the wrestling industry many see valets as simple eye candy... Someone good looking that a wrestler brings to the ring in varying states of undress to keep the crowd entertained... Some however do more, a select few work as managers, helping a wrestler scout his opposition and aid his training.... Eleanor Hall used to fall into the first group... But Sunday changed that. At Gold and Glory, Miss Eleanor Hall became Mrs Eleanor Kannon-Hall as she married PCW star Xavier Kannon live on PPV, and with Scientology’s conservative values out go the low cut short dresses and cleavage revealing tops... Ellie~! have taken a step out of “Valet” and placed it in “Wife”. So now she is married to Xavier, it looks like her attitude is changing... No more does she really not care about the wrestling and only wanting to get herself on camera, now she will be doing everything she can to make her Husband look good... At ringside she will continue the cheating and distracting, but now away from the ring she will be helping with research.... ... Which, with Ellie~! may cause more trouble than it’s worth... “Where’s Greekland?” With those words our shot opens up in the living room of Xavier Kannon’s Hubbardville residence, in the exclusive Kelly Preston development on the upper East side where for the only time since they got back from Kentucky there are no other residents knocking at the door with their congratulations. “You know.. Where, like, Greek people live?” Sitting at a PC is Ellie, now Mrs Eleanor Kannon-Hall who is looking at a map of the World, trying to find the country she believes Greek people are from. As she types away searching for it, her huge Diamond Wedding ring sparkles among the numerous bands and bracelets she wears near to it on her wrist. Eleanor : “Hey... There’s a country called Chile... You know, like the food that poor people and Mexicans eat. Maybe there are other countries named after foods... I’m hungry...” The Prep is wearing small sized light Blue and White striped Argentina football shirt tied at he front to show her toned midriff.. The shirt being worn in honour of Frisco going hand in hand with her search for the country the PCW Heavyweight Champion Big Alan Steele believes is home in part of her quest to know more about Kannon’s career now she is his Wife... And entitled to half of what he makes. Eleanor : “Hey... There’s a country called Hungary too! Xavi, is New Zealand a food?” Laying spread cross the sofa is an unusually apathetic Xavier Kannon, who has been like this since he failed to win back his PCW Continental Title in the 4 man Cage match won by Legend Inc. Xavier : “No.... But you can make one if you like... Pesto with Capers in, there, that is now called New Zealand...” The Scientologist just lays on his couch, looking at the large flat screen TV that is mounted on the wall but not really watching it... Constantly switching channels without paying any attention to what is on them... Dressed rather down in a Black hooded sweatshirt emblazoned with his Green and Purple “XK” logo and a pair of Black worker cut jeans with Purple and Green Scientological runes embroidered down the left leg. Xavier: “And Greek people come from Greece my little Poon Puppy.” The valet hurriedly types in Greece and the map of the country appears up on the screen... Eleanor not seeming too impressed when she finally sees it. Eleanor : “Wow.... If there was, like, an Award existed for crappy looking countries... They would have stopped it ages ago because Greece would have won every year...” With the mouse cursor she scrolls down the other options such as “Religion” “Tourism” and “Passtimes”... On the couch behind her, Xavier finally seems to be taking a vague interest in her work. Xavier : “What is this for anyway.... The Soccer shirt... The computer thing.... I thought you hated computer Nerds?” The Prep continues to type away as Xavier closes his bright Green eyes and rests his head on the arm of the couch, his Red hair falling down his face which he doesn’t even bother to pull from his face. Eleanor : “A computer Nerd away from a computer is still a Nerd.... Ellie away from a PC is still Ellie... And I rule.... YAY ME!!!” With the added boost of her self praise she keeps clicking through what there is before getting back to Kannon’s question. Eleanor : “Oh, and this is me, like, getting involved... I bought this shirt because you’re teaming with Frisco, and now because Big Alan Steele thinks he’s a Greek God I’m looking up Greece... See, I am like SO a good valet... That and I give you great sex and have nice breasts too...” We pull back into the room which is decorated in all White, making it appear even larger than it is... Something complimented by pale varnished floorboards and all Black furniture. On the Black leather couch Kannon thinks about it and nods his head in agreement. Eleanor : “Hmmm.... Greeks used to enjoy Gladiatorial battles... Feeding Christians to Lions.... And Bath House Orgies.... And Theatre....” She turns around to look at Kannon who is facing up at the ceiling, just staring at the vast expanse of White. Eleanor : “Well... I’ve seen Gladiator.... We already did the Christians to Lions at that Festival of Xenu you took me to, that was icky.... As for Bath House Orgies, well, you’ll have to wait for your birthday... Is Theatre like films and stuff?” She looks at him enough to notice a slight, effortless nod behind the arm of the sofa and then turns to type more information into the computer. Eleanor : “Okay... Films and Greek...” She proceeds to type this into an Internet search engine and waits as all the results appear on the screen for her to digest... Her pale Blue eyes look down the lift of sites, sneering at the ones she doesn’t like and showing no emotion to any others until she sees one she likes. Eleanor : “Like.... WA-, no, better than that.. HELLA COOL!!!” Her eyes light up at reading what is on the screen, fidgeting on her chair as she reads it out to herself. Eleanor : “Greek Toga Party, Auditions July 7th at 3pm, Greenwich Road, Queens, New York City..... X-Rated Adult Movie....” Despite what his valet is reading out, it fails to break through the barrier of abject apathy the Gold Patron Meritorious has put up around himself to cope with the loss of his Title. Eleanor : “TOGA PARTIES RULE!!! Wow, and that’s like today... X-Rated, wonder what that is?” With no help forthcoming by Xavier who can now be heard faintly snoring on the sofa she has to ponder it over herself. Eleanor : “Well.... The X-Bomb is kinda spinny so maybe the Movie has lots of spinning in it, you have to warn someone in case they get dizzy... Dizzy isn’t good..... And thank that Hubbard guy it’s an Adult movie, kids are all smelly and they make noise..... Xavi...” She spins around in her seat and looks over to her Husband waiting for a reply. Eleanor : “XAVI!!!” He springs up and out of his slumber, looking around in all directions before his sleepy eyes fix on his gorgeous Wife. Eleanor : “Can I use that teleportation thing you Scientologists developed and haven’t shared with the outside world... You know the one you all use to steal Government Documents from their offices because you all used to get arrested, like, a LOT?!?!?” He nods. Xavier : “Sure...” Then collapses back down on the Sofa for another nap as Ellie~! walks through from the Living room to the Kitchen and opens the door to what looks like a pantry only to emerge not in a food store but on a quiet, rather dirty street in the borough of Queens. Eleanor : “That thing rules.... Not so good when you go to get cereal and end up in Greenland though...” She now stands outside a rather seedy looking establishment with a gaudy Pink Neon sign advertising “Toga Party Auditions Inside” which she looks up at, before taking a quick look at her surroundings.. Which consist of a few “Specialist” Video shop and a few working girls on the corner, the naive valet not really picking up on what kind of neighbourhood she is in. Eleanor : “Damnzzies, I must be late.. Those 3 have already finished their auditions and are waiting for a Cab already...” She then heads up the stairs to the audition in her tied up Football shirt and knee length floral skirt and as she reaches the top, a 70’s esque funk-tinged theme can be heard playing... Rhythmical with lots of Wah-Wah peddle present... Something that could have been written by none other than famous Scientologist Isaac Hayes himself, the man who married her. “Ugh.... Urgh.... Ahhhh... Ahhhhhhhh....” Hearing the noises going on inside the valet’s face looks puzzled before she opens the door and casually wanders into a large hall like room with people dotted around dressed in Toga’s... Suits... Or nothing... Eleanor : “YAY.... Made it... Ho-” Her sentence is cut right off as an extremely well hung naked man walks in front of her, showing it all off for her pale Blue eyes to lock onto and follow on it’s... Or, his path across her... Licking her lips slightly before she closes her eyes and shakes her head to get the image out of her head. Eleanor : “So what if his Toga fell off.....Married woman.... Married woman...” She then wanders into the room, looking around for anyone who resembles a Director, and in her narrow search ignoring the acts of debauchery that are going on around her amidst the Togas. “Oooooooh... Ooooooo.... Ahhh.... Oh my God oh my God oh my God....Ah...AHHHH!!!!” To her left a buxom Blonde with her Toga only left around her waist straddles a man dressed in the remnants of Gladiator gear as a crowd gather around judging them... But exhibiting unparalleled tunnel vision she focuses only on the man getting up from a Director’s Chair. Director : “Good, good.... I like her....” He then turns away from the simulated sex scene to see the grinning face of Ellie~! right in front of him as she gives a little wave. Eleanor : “Like.. Hi, I’m Ellie~!... I’ve come to Audition for your Film... Say, how many A-List celebs will be at the Premier... Not that I’m some selfish fame hungry bitch who is using my Husband for fame, I mean Nicole Kidman is doing that enough for both of us... And all of Womankind...” The Director ignore what she says and just looks up and down her body... From her long, wavy Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights to her pale Blue Bambi-esque eyes to her pouting full lips to her 32B chest and her flat stomach then all the way down her long, never ending legs. Director : “So what qualifies you for a part?” The Preppy valet excitedly reels off her reasons. Eleanor : “Well, at College we had Toga parties all the time, me and my “Sisters” used the sheets from our beds and stuff...” He nods along, a perverted smile crossing his face. Director : “Lots of fresh, nubile young girls in Togas.. Good, go on...” Despite how disturbing his words are, Ellie~! carries on both eagerly and oblivious to all the carnal scenes playing out around her accompanied by grunts, moans and screams. Eleanor : “Oh, and I’ve been doing a lot of research into Greece, you know... Bath House Orgies and things...” The eyes of the perverted Director light up at hearing this. Director : “So how many have you taken on at once?” Not quite realising what is happening around her... The full blown audition for a Porn film, the new Mrs Kannon-Hall takes the question how she interprets it. Eleanor : “Like.. Parts?” The Director shrugs. Director : “I suppose some actresses can call them that...” Eleanor: “Well, in our last High School play I had to play Titania AND Helena in a Midsummer Night’s Dream.” Director : “No, I meant how many Cocks have you taken at on-” Suddenly the Director drops to the floor holding his bleeding face and moaning in bewildered pain as Ellie~!’s Husband, Xavier Kannon wincing as he shakes his hand then holds his knuckles. Xavier : “Nobody asks my Scientological Wife anything like that you debauched Peon...” All around them the moans and grunts of passion have ceased as they watch the Scientologist grab his wife’s arm and start to lead her out. Xavier : “What I Xenu’s name are you doing?!?!!? It’s a good job Hubbard guided my Scientological eyes to the screen!” She looks annoyed at being pulled from the Audition, after not anticipating what the sleazy Director was going to ask her and protests. Eleanor : “Well, I WAS, like, AUDITIONING for the fil-” Xavier : “IT WAS PORN!!!” The Prep stops in her steps, the realisation of how close she came to being sucked into the World to historical softcore Pornography hitting home causing her jaw to drop and her innocent eyes to widen as the shot starts to fade out. Xavier : “You were auditioning to be nailed live on Video for a bunch of introverted, sociopathic perverts to download off the Internet!!!” Eleanor : “Like our handler?” The shot then fades out on Ellie~!’s still puzzled naive face as we hit Black. “Oooops, shouldn’t I of said that?!?!?!?!?!?”
