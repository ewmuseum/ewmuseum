The Training - Chapter 1 - The root of it all

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th August 2002 ***OOC Note: The following RPs are from a series I started while I was back in PWA. I shall be carrying on the series here in PCW so I figured it might help to provide you with the beginning segments of the series. Thanks :-)*** Chapter 1 – The root of it all (Charlotte Lawrence is your average 23 year old woman. Nearly. As a baby she was born in Ireland, but sent over to America by her parents to avoid the tragic war that raged on. A month later she had no parents. They were dead. And in school she was revealed as being illiterate, which led to much torment. This insecurity passed over to her relationship with boyfriend Joe Nero, a cowboy who breeds horses for racing. When she revealed what she regarded as a defect, the expected reaction was for Joe to shove her out the car and drive off into the sunset. Instead, he was very understanding, and the couple stayed together. They’re still together two years later, and although Charlotte has made no attempt to cure the major hindrance, Joe has helped her to work around it.) (It’s a pleasant spring day in Prairie Town, Oregon. In the backyard of the Nero/Lawrence household, Joe stretches out on the grass, comfortable in the serenity of the environment. Next to him lies a closed novel, and the cover reveals that the theme is western; fitting considering what Joe does for a living. Wearing no top and rustled blue jeans, he rests on a towel and stares up at the sky, which contains some fluffy clouds. From the kitchen, situated just behind Joe’s head, Charlotte looks on with a smile. Her hands might be stirring a mixture inside a plastic bowl with a wooden spoon, but her eyes are concentrated on the body of Joe.) ‘Wow, how come I’m so lucky?’ (Charlotte’s mind drifts off into a world of thoughts, mainly rotating around the future. With his soul mate’s attention elsewhere, Joe leaps up off the grass and stealthily creeps into the kitchen and behind his girlfriend. With a quick poke in the stomach, he has Charlotte laughing, and the two tickle each other playfully for a few minutes. Eventually, Charlotte insists if she doesn’t carry on cooking there will be no tea, and the threat of no food is enough to keep Joe at bay. However, he places his hands around Charlotte’s waist and hugs her warmly. The happiness in the room could be felt all the way in Florida.) Joe: I never knew your stomach was so soft. (Sighing, Joe releases his grip and leans back against the work top. Charlotte momentarily places the worn out wooden spoon down on the black metallic surface and spins her wrists to loosen them.) Charlotte: There are many things about me you don’t know! Joe: Yeah. I wish I could stay here and find out more about them, but Joan has called me into the stables this afternoon. Apparently one of our top horses has an infection in the foot and I need to sort it out. (Spinning on his feet, Joe goes to walk out the room, but the next words he hears from Charlotte’s mouth send a chill down the back of his spine.) Charlotte: I think I want to be a wrestler. (With his jaw in the process of hitting the floor, Joe stares into Charlotte’s eyes, giving her his ‘are you mad?’ look. Charlotte carries on smiling in her innocent manner.) Joe: What did you just say? Charlotte: I want to be a wrestler. (Scratching his skull, Joe bends down slowly and lowers his posterior onto a pine chair, before resting his head on a right palm supported by his right elbow.) Joe: Charlie, are you mad? Charlotte: No, but I’m going to turn that way if I don’t get out of this house soon. I mean, the only time I leave these four walls is when venturing to the shops. You’re off at the stables every spare moment, and I need to fulfil my talents. Joe: But, wrestling! It’s so dangerous. Honey, you cannot be taller than 5’7” or heavier than 150lbs. Wrestling is an industry for all the social outcasts, the mental patients too psycho to be considered for government benefits! I mean, have you watched that PCW show recently? There were these guys who got a tractor and destroyed a multi million dollar set! I’m sorry dear, but that is no place for such a pretty woman like you! (In a fluster, Joe walks out the room. The distinctive sound of shoes strutting along the floor can be heard, before the clicking of the front door closing, and a few moments later, the blue Ferrari accelerating down the road. Frustrated, Charlotte violently pulls the cooking apron off over her head and slings it across the room, where it hits a vase, causing the glass object to smash in slow motion down to the floor, where the tiny pieces scatter everywhere. Charlotte merely runs her hand through her fine brown hair. Spontaneously, she walks into the hall, grabs her fleece from the coat rack, and opens the front door. Breathing deeply, the goodness of the May air flows through her arteries. Smiling, she walks off down the road. Time to pay a visit to the gym.) (Charlotte pushed open the door of the ‘Heaven Gym’. Based in the centre of Prairie Town, it was often used for the peasants to mingle, as indicated by men tipping their hats and offering words of greeting as she walked past. However, as she walked along the blue carpet, only one thing is of interest to her. Not the pine beams that extended the floor from the red ceiling, nor the men sweating with towels draping around their necks, but the ring. The ring with two men wrestling in it. The squared circle itself draws her attention, with the super tight black ropes and the grooves running around them, the turnbuckle pads and their unique shape, and the canvas itself which bobs up and down with every little movement.) (The first man dons royal blue pants with black stripes down the side. Hot and sweaty, his hair spikes up naturally. His ‘opponent’ wears red boxing shirts, and his wrists have black tape wrapped around them. Interestingly, both men are bare footed, but Charlotte is not interested in the curves and creases ironed into the gentleman’s clothing. She is watching the art. Without paintbrushes or crayons, the two men are weaving a tapestry worthy of being an article in a gallery. Guy One springboards to the top rope, spins around gracefully before hurtling at Guy Two with such a high velocity crossbody that Guy Two rolls through. Charlie is captured in a trance for several minutes until the two budding athletes roll out the back and head to the showers. She only twitches because someone taps her on the shoulder, Bill the gym owner.) Charlotte: Jesus Bill you want to give me a heart attack? (Bill goes on the defensive, putting his hands in the air simultaneously with Charlotte putting hers down.) Bill: Sorry, I guess I didn’t think statues could move. You’ve been standing there for over ten minutes Charlie, you want something? Charlotte: To be a wrestler. (Dumbfounded, Bill runs a hand through his clustered hair.) Bill: Wait a second. You’ve got the looks of a potential supermodel, a boyfriend who’s raking it in, you weigh 147lbs and stand 5’7”, and you want to be a wrestler? Charlie, boxing and wrestling are not fairy tale industries. (Hands on slender hips, Charlotte stares Bill straight in the eyes, with a fresh look of grit and determination.) Charlotte: Well I ain’t about to spend my life washing dishes and cleaning clothes. Besides, I’m no fairy. Train me Bill and I’ll show you EXACTLY what I am capable of!
