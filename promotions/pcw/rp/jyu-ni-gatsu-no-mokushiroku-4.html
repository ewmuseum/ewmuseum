Jyu Ni Gatsu no Mokushiroku {4}

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December/March [Sunday, December 16, 2002] Anarchy lounged across the black sofa in the living room, dressed in a pair of black sweats and a long sleeved blue shirt with thick blue socks. Her ankle, no longer taped, was feeling better as she pointed her toe up and out to the beat of the music playing. Matrix came walking by, dressed in all black turtleneck and slacks as his trademark. He passed the doorway, but came back with an inquisitive look as he heard the song. “What’s this?” Anarchy looked up from her PCW magazine, “ “Clubbed to Death: Darkside Mix” by Rob D.” Matrix nodded slowly, rubbing his stubble-covered chin. “Nice. Might have to d/l that one later…” Just then Cyclonica stepped into the scene. She gave Matrix an icy glare, sending him shuffling off like a scolded child. Then the Divine Prophetess turned to her half-sister, leaning on her forearms against the back of the sofa as she gave Anarchy a slick smile. “Hey there, Ana.” “What do you want, C-y-c?” Anarchy knew that when Cyclonica called her that there was a catch. That, and the fact that Cyclonica doesn’t make idle chitchat. Anarchy didn’t even look up from her magazine, reading an article about RUSH, PCW, and the mastermind behind the whole merger, Xavier Kannon. Yeah, like that was gonna last long. The Divine Prophetess looked hurt at her. “I just wanted to tell you that Jenna was out in the car waiting for you…told me to come get you.” “Oh yeah,” Ana rolled her eyes as she dropped the magazine. “Gotta go shopping. Rats.” She got up and walked out of the room, taking her time so that she didn’t aggravate her ankle. As she passed by, Cyclonica stuck her foot in the path of that said ankle, quickly removing it as Ana went to sidestep it. Her sister glared at her, which prompted the icy woman to shrug her shoulders. “Hey…sibling rivalry. It’s genetic.” “Screw you.” Ana turned her back and walked out, leaving Cyclonica alone with Rob D playing behind her. That classic icy look came into her eyes again as she folded her arms across her chest. “That’s your ambition, is it not?” Then from below the sounds of loud hammerings boomed. Strangely, it was to the same beat as Clubbed to Death. Cyclonica sighed as she walked out of the living room, her heels clicking against the stone cobbled floors and echoing through the hallways of the castle… … down below, the god hammered his fists into the stone column, his strikes matching the bass of the music from above. Three rapid punches sounding out, each of them with a fierce growl from the lips of the Neo Void God. Then he spun around and rocked a solid kick into its base, the impact shaking the entire room like a miniature earthquake. He stepped back and yelled as he tackled into the post, a heavier rumble quaking the cylindrical training room. Stepping back, he threw his body into it again, another yell of anger and ferocity coming from him. His body reared back one more time, slower, feeling the wear of throwing yourself into pain and suffering again and again. The roar echoed off the walls, and he speared the granite post with everything he had. He stood there leaning against the post, his body heaving for breath, sweat beading against his arms, and his silver eyes closed tight. He stood there…and thought: …We exist…therefore we have no choice but to destroy. People think this is a game…think this is a sport…just some gimmick we put on in front of a camera to entertain them. They’re wrong. This is life…our life…one of many we’ve lived. When we go out there, all that anger is real, all that aggression is real, everything we do out there we do for our own personal purpose. It’s not some knight in armor riding a horse deal…it’s not some stupid multiple personality deal to get laughs because just being ourselves isn’t good enough…we gotta be more than one person…no, it’s not that at all. Hell, it’s not even about being the fatass, or the mummy, or the drunk, or the son of Vampir Nosferaftu. We don’t have to worship Satan, or Hubbard, or Chutlu-Mythos-Bastardos. What- the f-ck- ever. All we gotta do…is go out there…and destroy. It’s what we were born and bred to do. We want that belt…and once we get it, we’re through with all the jackasses and scientologists and the anarchists and the demons and nobodies. We’re through with em all. Once that happens… …we can finally rest… “Taking a break?” Akiyama turned back slowly, jarred from his thoughts suddenly. Standing there, wearing a pair of black gi pants and a yellow tank top was Sonata. She leaned back against the steel door, the lock clicking shut as she smiled slyly at Akiyama. “What do you want?” you could tell the god didn’t hear her coming. Sometimes even his own thoughts were raping him away from reality. The focus for destruction was too great at times…it was a pest. She walked towards Akiyama slowly, her feet padding against the cold stone floor softly as she folded her hands behind her back. The black and blonde ponytail bobbed behind her head as she approached Akiyama and stared up at him. Almond met silver, and the two of them stared at each other for a moment. Then, she waved for him to lean forward. He sighed as he leaned forward, and she leaned close to him. Her breath was sweet and warm as she breathed next to him…and she whisper was soft and seductive…but the words themselves were nothing but insane. “Let’s spar.” The god snatched his head back and glared at her questioningly. “What are ya, stupid or something?” “Do I look stupid?” she crossed her eyes and stuck out her tongue, waving her arms in the air and mocking his question. Akiyama placed his hand on her forehead, stopping her hopping motions instantly. “We’re not kidding,” he was serious, as he always was. That’s one thing you wouldn’t see from the nVg…and that jackassery and tomfoolery. Not like everyone else trying to entertain. “And for your sake, we hope you were.” “Why not? You think I’m not good enough to spar with anymore?” she put up her fists and bounced on the balls of her feet. “Me and you used to spar all the time back when we were in high school. I used to win a few of ‘em too! How many people can say they’ve beaten a god in combat?” Only the jackasses who cheated to win. “We weren’t this big in high school…and we weren’t this reckless with other people’s bodies.” She rolled her eyes, her bouncing stopping as she shoved Akiyama in the arm with her fist. “Whatever. Sound’s like you’re chicken to me!” It was one thing to challenge the nVg…but to taunt him was another. Akiyama stood there with emotionless eyes as the woman flapped her arms, clucking with a grin on her face. He closed his eyes, a sound between a growl and a sigh coming out from him. “Stop that,” the order was flat, but it held meaning. “Now.” She did as she was told, looking up at him with that teasing smile still on her face. “Ah, okay. You’re not a chicken after all.” At least some sense was coming into her…at least one would think that before hearing her next statement. “You’re a DUCK!!” The god’s silver orbs widened in both anger and shock as she waddled around in front of him, keeping back giggles as she quacked. “QUAAAACK!! MOTHER DUCKER!!” She barely saw the arm swinging for her head. Right in mid-quack she caught glimpse of the oncoming arm and dropped to the ground. She rolled back to her feet to find Akiyama standing with his fists raised at his face. A grin came on her face as she raised her own. “Now…that’s what I want from you. Give me what you got…just like we used to do when we were younger.” “You’re gonna get hurt.” If only he were so nice to warn everyone else before it happened. She shrugged as she began to bounce on her toes. “Hurt me big boy. You know you want to.” And with that said…it was on. The god rushed at her with full force. The playful smile on her face lowered, finally realizing what she asked for all this time. All her resources was pushed to the max trying to either block or dodge the onslaught of fists and kicks coming at her from what seems all possible fronts. Akiyama grabbed one of her blocking arms and slung her behind him towards the stone pillar. Sonata twisted around and bounced off the post, coming back and nailing Akiyama in his chest with a dropkick. As soon as she hit the ground a hand latched around her throat. She gagged as Akiyama pulled her up into the air, a sadistic smile on his face as he stared up at her. Her legs flailed as she hammered forearms into his wrist, trying to break his lock on her neck. She suddenly snapped a kick up into the eyes of Akiyama, breaking his hold as he reached for his eyes instinctively. As soon as she hit the ground she let loose with a series of kicks to both knees of the god. She rolled around behind him and booted into the back of the knees as much as she could. All she could hope to achieve was a slight twinge in the vertical base of the nVg. Akiyama turned around, his eyes now staring at her annoyed. That was the last thing you wanted to do. Ask anyone in PCW…more so Kannon… Sonata looked up at the ceiling…it was high up, and the dimly lit room cloaked it in shadows above them. But through that darkness…a flash came through, barely noticeable to anyone not looking up. But she saw it… Just as she turned her attention back to Akiyama, she found his fist flying towards her. By instinct she crossed her arms across her chest, but even instinct couldn’t hold back the Hand Fate dealt her. She was grounded, but that couldn’t keep her from rising up and crashing back into the stonewall behind her. Sonata dropped down to her knees, her hand clutching her chest in pain. She looked up and found the god standing there, fists raised before him. This was a real fight to him. No sparring…no practice…no training. All real. Just like everything he did in that ring. “Get up.” There was no concern for her in his voice, rich with sternness. She stood slowly, her hands going back to her fighting guard. Sonata was not one to back down from anything…no matter how hopeless this was. She was determined, a quality that may just be tested in the future. How determined was she to live during the course of this fight? That’s the question everyone asks themselves in the heat of battle, when fists of divine justice are pounding on them, and they reach down inside to switch on the emergency power, only to find that their resources are tapped out. Then what? What’s left for them? Akiyama had the answer… Death… Sonata came at him with a flying roundhouse kick, popping him in the side of his head. Is that what she really wants? Death? What’s her f-ckin’ problem? Akiyama swung at her, but she ducked underneath and kicked him square in the jaw with an uppercutting side kick. Maybe that’s what everyone wants these days…death. They’re not satisfied with life, so the best they can do is walk right up to the deliverer himself and place an order. And blindly…we provide service. Like a slave. Another kick came from the side, but the god moved out of the way and glared at the girl. She now had bruises on her arm, a welt on her cheek that was beginning to swell. Her beauty was being beaten out of her…even a god should be punished for such a sin. But for Akiyama…there was none to execute punishment. So…manipulation is the entire deal? Everyone’s pulling our strings just so they can get what they want? Cyclonica…Xavier Kannon…SONATA? What do they want from us?? Sonata came in with a kick to Akiyama’s face, but the god didn’t bother blocking at that point. He stood there, allowing her kick to almost touch his face. But she stopped, looking at him with concerned eyes. She could read him better than anyone on the planet could. What is their motives? Why must everyone try and play us for a fool?? “Akiyama?” her voice was soft as she touched his arm, trying to break him from his thoughts. “Daijoubu? You okay? Say something!” No more orders… no more commands… no more rules… someone says we can’t do something, we do it and break their necks in the process for doubting us. We will prove everyone wrong…because this is our time, and we will stand the sole survivor in the grand course of battle! “…anata no wake wa…nan desu ka…” “What?” Akiyama looked up, and found himself crushing Sonata’s head into the stone pillar in the center of the room. Her screams echoed off the walls, but there was no one to hear her…no one to save her. Hopeless…all in all…that was determined before… “Is this what you want??” his growl came louder over her cries of pain, angered and frustrated. “Is THIS THE PAIN YOU ASKED FOR?? Now tell me!! WHAT ARE YOUR MOTIVES??” Then in a flash like lightning…clarity came over him. He snatched his hand back and looked with wide eyes, the woman barely standing in front of him, the blood trickling down the side of her face…the cuts and bruises on her body… the life barely shimmering from within her eyes… That was his destiny…no matter what he told himself. “Sonata!!” he caught her as she fell forward in his arms, no more strength left to fight him, none left to stand. He stroked her hair slowly, calling out her name trying to wake her. “Hey kid…get back up. We’re not done sparring yet. C’mon…” Then words came from his lips… probably the two you’ll never hear from him in this lifetime. “I’m sorry.” And do you know how after you say something, you begin to regret it? That’s what happened here. It was like a knife stabbed him from within his brain, neurological shock quacking through his entire body. His jaw dropped, eyes wide from the surprising pain that he felt…pain he’s never felt before in his life. Then something hit him from behind hard. The god fell forward, his face smacking into the stone pillar loudly. His frame laid on top of Sonata, who was at the moment still unconscious. Strangely, she opened her eyes with an annoyed look rather quickly. “Little help here?” A black taped hand reached out to her, the old friend of Akiyama’s grabbing it and pulling out from under the 309 pound dead weight. She brushed herself off, dabbing a finger at the blood on her forehead and sighed. “Great. Just great.” She turned her attention to the dropped Neo Void God on the ground. “Christ, what’d you hit him with?” Looking back, she found no answer from Cyclonica, leaning against the wall with her eyes closed and her arms folded across her chest. Nearby Matrix stood, a smile on his face and a black taped hand on his hip. Across his shoulder, a brown sack was slung. He shrugged his shoulders and chuckled. “Sandbag.”
