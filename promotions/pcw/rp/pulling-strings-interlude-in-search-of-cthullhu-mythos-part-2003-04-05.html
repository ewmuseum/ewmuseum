Pulling Strings - Interlude - In Search Of Cthullhu Mythos - Part Two; Subsection D

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 5th, 2003 A love/hate relationship... Maybe since the start of their wacky journey, El Romantico and Dante Jackson have crossed paths before, and each time, it would be the Spanish Romantic out with the victory. In a snide twist of thought, Jackson thought he could get Romantico at Turmoil, April 2nd in a freestyle battle in which once again, Dante lost. The voice came back inside of his head, 'Time to get up...' He grunted, before pausing to take time out and open his eyes for the first time in a long time. This was Thursday morning, close to an approximate 7:15 central standard time. The efemminate voice he thought he heard was now a distant neuron gasping for attention. The Lyrical Messiah groaned accordingly, before starring out the window inside of his mansion in Houston. The words enscribed on the doors, the various sayings and nit-pickings of self dignity ate at him, leaping from page to page to his heart, his inner feelings. 'Try what you must, but accomplish what you can.' The long running poem seemed certain to lead right back up towards something by Kipling, Emerson, or one of the long-standing poets of time itself: Witman. Yet, for all thought today, there was only one, the lone thing that raked his mind until it could deal with it for secondary purposes. A title, he'd lost in quick fashion to two men he thought were enemies, but now, advarsaries in a common war against each other. A psychological atmosphere he dared to enter, and he was destined to fail, flat on his face with the anthem of R'yleh. He could dream all he want about getting the title back, but it was all up to karma now, whether or not he wanted the title badly enough to try and pry it out of the hands of the Occult, and the Blind. 'All this honkey-dory shit has got to stop.' he muttered in low tones as he walked along the house, his feet screeching against tiled floors and letting little tink squeaks pop as he walked. The morning air was fresh, good enough to take a large whiff and understand just what happened to be so good about it. So pure, so relaxing. Rocking back on his heels, he briefly chuckled, 'Cruiserweight, Continental, Television.' He thought this out incredulously as if it were monoply, each title a piece where he could add his hotels, his rent sky high, and his bills raking in. He played the board and the board was his. He was the house to everybody else if he actually would come out of this, pain of course from taking this down, but still a champion. Twice the worth. Looking back, he could see the moon fading more and more out of the picture before the Earth was reduced to nothing, just a clear blue sky with clouds partaking in condensation. He began to wonder just how his opponents were trying to concentrate. Vague thoughts of what they were doing began to plague his mind. Even his partner, the man throughout tomorrow he would have to trust as much as anybody else. The first time he'd actually be in a tournament since the days of Playaz Inc. The thing about it is, the man who pushed him out of his last tournament was also in this one. The Overweighter. He quickly scratched his name out of the picture, before fading memories of death in the fourth round would round back up towards his head. Back to the others, he digested. Were they hanging out, discussing strategy? Or becoming the monster as the net game EWR 3.0 tells as a gimmick? To overrun this tournament and become champions without breaking a single drop of prespiration? Bending down, gyrating his hips as he leans to pick up the newspaper, Dante reads the headlines with a throttle of eye gazing. Each word passes before him, and each word becoming closer and closer into a distant memory. The oddest part while trying to think about it was the reasoning. Who would write something putrid, yet be able to read and sent down the throats of every single man or woman who happened to pick it up and take a gander at it? Columnist have no heart, they are like the Tin Man, or the Cowardly Lion in the Wizard of Oz. They have the skill, but not the corresponding vessels to improve on them. An off color pattern of Wartime news captivated the nation as it seemed just about every minute of television was focused upon the tiny little country of Iraq. Dante didn't care, rather looking back at the plexiglass before revolving around more words to speak upon the masses. Time was waning down. The man walked down the halls once more, this time with no emotion as he seemed not to blink even as he trailed his way toward a compy bed. The eyes were fluxed, his head almost in sharp pains every time he took a step. Thump! Thump! Thump! Those were the sounds of feet, smashing against the floor as they rang into his ears like Godzilla destruction of a comic book Tokyo. He twisted his body as he contorts to lay down. And he went to sleep. * * * Inside of a Spanish Villa, the man known to many as El Romantico quietly sat, writing more lines of poetry. His gift though, the burgenoing thought of every female woman gasping, almost dying just to get a glimpse of him was the antagonist to every male who ever tried to date women period. There was nothing about him that seemed human enough, but the Romantic was always in serious need of a place to his own. The villa accompanied him to such a treatment. Tucked away in the California outskirts, the copper tone building sat as a site for the inquisitions, the parties, and the weddings of many blisful Spaniards. But now, as it seems with so many other classical artifacts, it is discarded, taken aback to new and more fancier settings. Even the Spainards, after years of North American conquest, were bound to end up like the America's someday. There's a difference towards the rose carrying wonder however as his hair sits in front of his face. The days of winning the Cruiserweight titles in both Pureosu and PCW were nice things to add on his resume, but yet, none of them struck out to be his finest accomplishments. Before Dante Jackson hurridly lost the Continental title, they were set to even feud upon it. Not wanting to stretch the cosmos with a breath-taker of a feud, but one that would atleast cement both of them as credible. A maid walks in, the garters strapping to her brownish tone as she wears her hair up in a sultry throwback to Dorothy Dandridge. 'Mr. Romantico?' The accent was there, but the English made it seem as if she never really had one in the first place. Romantico grated quickly, 'Yes?' 'The morning papers are here sir,' she said with a quiet whim, 'but I think you'd better try and turn on the television first.' The Spaniard looked back at the woman, wafting his fingers in the air as he sent her back to do whatever duties were needed. Romantico picks up the remote in a quiet gesture, flipping the television as static appears soon as the red power button is pressed. 'Bingo.' was the whisper as gyrating bodies fell along dark and moody silouettes. The Poetic Genius begins flipping around the single channel, hovering around until he finds the Wonder Mike show, discussion about the upcoming Continental title tournament happens to be the focus point of the show. Looking back at the brackets, Romantico tells out in a common roar of embarrasment, 'HIRO SAOTOME AND AVALANCHE?!' Out of all things possible, the grin upon El Romantico's face was only priceless at best. Finding out to know he would be facing Hiro Saotome and Avalanche, two former PCW washouts and complete untalented workers in the system as only a few men could even think that they would even have a chance in this tournament, yet by a string of luck, they've been packed with the undying chance of upset. 'Son of a bitch,' Romantico could only mutter under his breath as cursing was merely below his stature to the people. 'I'm teaming with him...' The him he referred to was six feet four inches, two hundred and twenty pounds, barely stopping at the Cruiserweight scales if his agile appearence wasn't enough. He had been Continental champion before, and now he was going for the double title sweep not seen since Frisco when he won the Television and Continental titles in a deul with Sean Matthews. That was probably the only reason Jackson would enter this tournament, personal gain. Romantico shaked his head as he stalked from the couch over to the telephone. Picking it up from the receiver, he begins to dial numbers, hoping for a response on the opposite line, but gets nothing just dial tone. Ring! Ring! Ring! * * * 'Where am I?' the man rose out in a black surface, looking outward towards the heavens if he could see anything to make sure his sight had not been consumed as he began taking steps in hollow steps, until he looks down, a long slope rising from the ground below as Dante stopped in his tracks. 'The question is rather, where are you?' once more, the efemminite voice spoke from out of the darkness as Dante could only turn around in wonder, thoughts perspiration through his head as each one began sweating down. They lost upon his clothes, his body, and further more on the ground, only to be picked up by someone else. 'I've died haven't I?' The voice humbled, 'No, you are still blinking. You are still entertaining crowds, yet instead of being on stage, you're now on the corner.' 'You're lying!' Dante rushed towards the walls as he stood inside of what Eli Flair called the Wall, and the person called The Faceless. He looked at it suspiciously, block upon cement block stood atop one another surrounding him while the man stood in the middle. 'Would I be?' the Faceless told in a quick jab back towards Dante as he stopped pounding, rather shaking his head as he knew this was a battle he probably could not win. Even if he tried to take this wall down by hand, it would be an endless task. 'I've seen you before, and I'm not here to be taking my chances.' 'Oh, you're referring towards the tournament, are you not?' the smart vernacular was a testament to any being holding you back, just like Norma Jean to Marylin Monroe. 'I might be.' 'Hmph, your answers aren't as forthcoming as you wish they were Dante, they're there as you try and skim through life.' The Faceless reverberated back as the words shot to the Lyrical Messiah as if he were being shot dead in the chest, no Kevlar attached to his body at all. 'Your friend is in Cuba right now almost dead and you have not spoken a single word towards him. You truly are 'one dimensional' as they say you are.' Dante could only shake his head at the diatribe of the Faceless as he could only stare in complete essence, sitting down with his arms folded around his shins. Manic depression was starting to kick in, horribly. 'This would be the time of reflection now would it?' he told her in a low growl, almost as sharp as DMX, but there was no emotion backing it. No substance. 'This 'search' of the Mythos. My mythos. Because I'm a rapper, I'm labeled as generic. Its all a color thing. You try and do things that normally you would hold your own, but yet a white man does it and its called being original.' 'That's why black characters in PCW are their own gimmicks, everything has been taken. And only the redundant are left behind.' The last statement was as almost the truth. Testament of time showed that black men in PCW had chances at gimmicks, but they never came through except the buff over stereotype. Rush Mason - thug, before even voodoo in which he didn't even look as if he practiced it. Rock Sterling - thug. Statuz Quo - thug. A list that goes into the eons of PCW history. 'After all of that, you blame only those of your past?' the Faceless spoke up once more, keeping on that tagging persistant burning of Dante's accomplishments. 'Maybe you're falling inside of their game, their old ways. You used to be a rapper, what happened to that?' 'I still - ' Interrutped. 'See you as a faker. You release these so called 'albums' saying they sell well, but have you negotiated your contract? Have you been in a bitter beef with somebody? Have you made anything conscious towards the people?' Dante steps up, about to roar with all his might, but there's nothing there, the Faceless has him now as he sits back down shaking his head. 'No.' he mutters. 'What was that?' Dante spoke louder, this time with a force as a kick back reaction. 'No!' 'And so now, where will you go?' the Faceless asked with a tone similar to a man he once considered towards a father figure. 'Enders...' 'Oh no, come on now Dante...we're going back to thinking all of this was orchestrated.' Soon, a pounding on the Faceless came, shuddering Dante back as he scrapped his feet along the floor, looking up as the Faceless seemed to be losing its face. For the Poetic Genius hath cometh. * * * 'Dante!' the Spaniard called out as he took steps inside of the house, his drive was more than a quick roam through the hills as Dante happened to be incapacitated. 'Wake up!' He shook Dante violently, but the eyes of the Messiah would not budge an inch, rather flucate as the eyes rolled back. Romantico, thinking quickly for a second tries to believe that indeed his soul had been vanquished, yet Jackson was still breathing. Romantico tried to get his partner back into a living mode, but the mood would take a bit more persuasion. A sharp crack upon the back of Romantico's head sends him tumbling down to the bed with a lone word muttered afterwards. 'Ouch.' Standing over him, Tracy Jackson holds the bat in her hand as she drops it. Dusting her hands off, she stands in a superhero pose. 'Wow! I'm good!' Romantico looked off in the distance, a burnt orange lined against the sky as Dante could only be entrapped in the famed Faceless that every man has. The serene setting as he gets pounded by his own thoughts. Romantico looks over as he sees Dante, crouched as the Faceless stands in front him him. 'Are you ready to choose a path Dante, or are you still wanting to stick to your circumspect life where only I spin your axis?' 'AND JUST WHO THE FUCK ARE YOU?' The Faceless turned around, looking directly into the burning eyes of El Romantico, those same eyes not seen since his wars with Tim Walden over the Crusierweight title a few months ago. 'I wondered when you'd be joining us, Mr. Romantico.' 'Oh I'm not joining this, I'm ending it right here and right now. I think I'm clearly being able to understand just why Dante acts like this, you.' The Facless snickered. 'And how do you plan to do this?' 'Think about it for a second, Dante is out there right now, and here he happens to stand is where will he be after all of this is done? He'll still be the asshole whom I'll hate. The asshole the fans will loathe. The asshole he'll try to become. And the reason why he'll be that same asshole?' 'Because we need it so.' The Faceless looked around as Jackson stood up for the first time in what seemed like ages ago. The time drew closer and closer as he could only watch as he smiled for the first time in a long time. A smile. 'I told you before, I'm my own man Faceless. For every single thing you toss back at me, I'll bring it back, and in one fell swoop, your bricks - crumble. And then, there's no you, only me. I choose the fact that I'll be an asshole for the rest of my life, and though this, being an asshole it means I need no tempting of words to down play me.' Dante chuckled. 'Only the thought of proving them wrong.' Brick slips. And another. Romantico and Jackson look around, as the Wall's integrity begins to fall closer and closer in obscurity. Outward they fall, almost like Berlin as the Faceless only watches in horror. 'No, NO!' 'You're trapped here Faceless. And when this wall goes, so do you.' Frantic is the Faceless as he moves over, trying to climb the still standing piece, but falls back upon them, snapping him down to the crowd as they collapse. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! One last primodial scream as he fades away, gone from Dante's mind. Period. The two men stare at one another as Dante shakes his head as he awakes, looking over at Tracy and then at Romantico, before telling the Poetic Genius, 'You truly do know how to ruin things don't you? 'Between your wife hitting me with a bat and you just talking, I'll stick to easy fuck you.' was all he could mutter. After all that, that's what they had. No title. Just a love for the sport, and a hate for one another. A love/hate relationship.
