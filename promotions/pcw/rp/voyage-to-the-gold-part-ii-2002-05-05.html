Voyage to the Gold: Part II

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 5, 2002 “Are you afraid of Politically Incorrect?” Jack Valentine asked jocularly as part 2 of Voyage to the Gold opened up. Of course, Jack was referring to the tag team champions, Politically Incorrect. The one team that was supposedly an unstoppable force in PCW. The one team that supposedly owned the PCW tag team division. The one team that was a member of the most powerful stable in PCW. The one team that The Heartbreakers had to go head to head with at PCW’s Supremacy. “Of course not,” Johnny murmured with a bit of hesitation. It was almost as if there was a slight hint of fear towards the most powerful team that PCW has ever seen since Da Westsidaz. “And Syko and Miyagawa….” Jack continued. “They don’t stand a chance,” the other half of The Heartbreakers responded with still a hint of hesitation. Books are nothing. Writing and speaking are nothing. It is all about how you perform in the ring. They discovered that they had never been aware before that the Civil War in America was not printed matter but a fact only about ten years old. Of course. They were Chicago natives. They were a little ashamed of my want of tact. Meantime, looking like the conventional conception of fashionable revelers, with their pink opera-hats pushed off their foreheads, Johnny Heart was having some slight difficulty with his latch-key; for the house before which we had stopped was not one of those many-storied houses that made up the greater part of the street. It had only one row of windows above the ground floor. Dead walls abutting on to it indicated that it had a garden. Its dark front presented no marked architectural character, and in the flickering light of a street lamp it looked a little as though it had gone down in the world. The greater then was his surprise to enter a hall paved in black and white marble and in its dimness appearing of palatial proportions. Heart did not turn up the small solitary gas-jet, but led the way across the black and white pavement past the end of the staircase, past a door of gleaming dark wood with a heavy bronze handle. It gave access to his rooms he said; but he took us straight on to the studio at the end of the passage. Johnny and Jack both had been drinking that night, but it seemed that Johnny took the worse of the drinking binge. He kept rambling on about Syko, Miyagawa and Politically Incorrect. Rambling on about how he was afraid to step in the ring with any four of those men. He was bigger than all four of them, yet his fear overcame his size…most likely due to the intoxication and Heart had developed. It was rather a small place tacked on in the manner of a lean-to to the garden side of the house. A large lamp was burning brightly there. The floor was of mere flagstones but the few rugs scattered about though extremely worn were very costly. There was also there a beautiful sofa upholstered in pink figured silk, an enormous divan with many cushions, some splendid arm-chairs of various shapes, but all very shabby, a round table, and in the midst of these fine things a small common iron stove. Somebody must have been attending it lately, for the fire roared and the warmth of the place was very grateful after the bone-searching cold blasts of mistral outside. How Johnny entered this house was unknown. He was just talking to Jack several minutes ago…so he thought. Maybe it was his fear of facing two decent tag teams for the first time in his PCW career that led him to these hallucinations. Maybe it was the thought of him getting gold for the first time in his PCW, or even helping his partner to his first gold ever. Maybe it was just the fact that he was actually afraid of the four men he would face in the next two weeks…or maybe it was just the beers talking. Regardless, he was in an unknown place and had no idea how he got there. Heart, without a word, flung himself on the divan and, propped on his arm, gazed thoughtfully at a distant corner where in the shadow of a monumental carved wardrobe an articulated dummy without head or hands but with beautifully shaped limbs composed in a shrinking attitude, seemed to be embarrassed by his stare. Johnny was really out of it, and by the looks of things, anyone would be worried about a fried in this kind of state. “Incorrect…Syko…Miyagawa,” Johnny uttered in a mumbling voice. “I’ll kill them all…that’s right kill. It’s all about the PINK! Yeah, the PINK! That’s it…” Johnny began to dose off on his little couch that he had stumbled across. He seemed to somewhat know the Pavilion he had come across. The wine had, one would suppose, loosened his tongue. Blunt, too, lost something of his reserve. A woman appeared from the shadows and Johnny sat straight up. “What’s cookin’ baby?” Johnny muttered. From their talk one could gather the notion of an eccentric personality, a man of great style, not so much solitary as difficult of access, a collector of fine things, a model known only to very few people and not at all to the public market. But as meantime they had been emptying their Venetian goblet with a certain regularity. Johnny continued to drink as the women continued to feed him liquor. It was as if she was trying to poison him…kill him so he couldn’t continue on his quest for gold. This was yet another obstacle in the way of The Heartbreakers making their dream of capturing PCW gold. One team member was out of sync and “killing” the team. An almost sad metaphor for the team that is The Heartbreakers. The voices and the impressions they conveyed acquired something fantastic to one’s mind. Johnny was acting very humorous because of his drunken state, saying all kinds of “pickup” lines to the somewhat attractive woman. “Did you know that you’re an extraordinary woman?” He questioned. She just laughed at half of the Heartbreakers and continued feeding him the poison one could call liquor. “You know I really hate The Heartbreakers,” the woman said. Johnny just laughed, not being able to understand what the woman was saying as he just reached for her breasts. “I love you, I truly do…yes, I love you,” the drunken Heartbreaker said. “And I hate you…everything about you. You’re pink clothing, your dumb haircut, your stupid partner, your wrestling ability…everything about you is terrible and I would never get with you in a million years,” the woman said. “One more drink of this and you shouldn’t be able to wrestle my boys next Sunday at the pay per view.” “Hey, wait a minute…” Johnny said as he began to realize what was going on. “Shhhhh,” the woman said. She poured another drink. As Johnny held it in his hand, on the brink of passing out, Jack Valentine busted through a nearby window and an “Olympic type” sound effect played on the screen as if he was there to save the day. “What the hell are YOU doing here,” the woman said. “Shut up…we don’t need BITCHES in our life. Especially ones like you. Come on Johnny…let’s go home,” Jack said. “Heeeyyyyeyy,” Johnny slurred. “I know you.” “Yes, yes, and you are coming home with me…let’s go, you can’t handle yourself right now,” Jack said. “You can’t take him, he’s mine,” the woman said. Jack humorously slapped the girl and she tumbled to the ground. Jack grabbed Johnny’s hand in a very feminine way and wrapped Johnny’s arm around his shoulder. The two walked out of the woman’s apartment and Jack held Johnny up as the two walked off in the moonlight. Stay tuned for Part III
