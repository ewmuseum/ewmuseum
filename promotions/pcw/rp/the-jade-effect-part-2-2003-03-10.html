The Jade Effect (Part 2)

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/03/03 ‘Just like me to leave an idiot in charge of an imbecile’ she thought as she charged down the red carpeted stairs three at a time, ignoring the men struggling to cart her luggage down the hallway towards the door. It turns out that Jade was not quite as nice as she was coming across, that sweet and innocent façade cannot be trusted as far as it could be kicked when she was left alone (and technically she was alone, it could take days to search every room) in a house the size of her ‘smaller’ American dwelling with only a middle aged incompetent fool to safe guard the contents. Now by this point, as you may have guessed, she has become the slightest bit angry. Lacuna liked anger, it was probably the only emotion she could tolerate feeling, it helped her put across her true inner rage without the worry on her conscience getting hold of it when she had finished. Today was the sort of anger that she relished; it was a sort of violent anger that, instead of making her think out a solution, made her break things. She liked breaking things, possibly to too much of a degree. It was during her two-month break from wrestling that she actually discovered the passive aggressive streak. She had found that the gap that was left by not fearing chair shots had turned her slightly insane, but she was better now. Honest. It was five minutes to nine in the morning, and she was heading back to America. This she didn’t want to do, which made her very angry. What made her angrier still was that she had missed breakfast, (which has to be the most important meal because it comes first.) Not many professional athletes would recommend a good fry up at any time of the day, let alone daily before exercise. What made her so much angrier was that she had begun to like Jade. Liking people was pretty rare for Lacuna anyway, especially people who had tendencies to steal from her. She was like that little dog you loved to fuss as a child; pretty cute and loveable until you leave it on its own in your expensive new living room where it will go mad and ruin everything in sight without a thought towards the otherwise. Stupid loveable animals… grr. She hastily grabbed at an expensive looking leather coat from the wall-long coat rack and slung it over her shoulders, hiding her white t-shirt, garbed in the cross of St. George. It was one of her favourite t-shirts, overly large and baggy, it has the greatest lion silhouette on the reverse. The English fashion accessory of the year, maybe she should buy out the company… …No, she wouldn’t let her thoughts wander now, a clear mind was the greatest of tools available to man, or woman. Then again, what could a clear mind help her achieve when all she wanted to do was paint some walls with 100% pure Jade blood. Back to anger now, that red-misted cloud that rested behind the eyes, now she was focused. Jade would pay, no way would she wreck havoc in one of Lacuna’s homes, she was going to pay like Christ himself, no punishment would suffice... “What was I doing again?” she looked into the gilt framed mirror mounted on the wall opposite and examined the coat she had slung on “Oh yeah, going to America, that’s right” So much for focus, hey Lacuna. “Ma’am? Madam Devries?” The call came from Possemthwaite in the hallway. He was one of the men attempting to drag Lacuna’s luggage towards one of the cars “What?” she snapped back, not really in the mood for conversing “When will my convoy be ready to head to the airport? “That’s exactly my reason for disturbing you, my most humblest of employers, we fear you will have to pay excess amounts for transporting this luggage” Lacuna rolled her eyes and sighed “When has money ever been a problem, dimwit” “It has nothing to do with money, Ma’am. The excess amounts I referred to was actually you being arrested” Possemthwaite replied, his voice decidedly shaky “You may wish to leave Steve at home for this trip” “WHAT!” she screamed as she flung open the heavy oak door with ease and stormed to meet Possemthwaite in the hall, her face a picture of grotesque anger. Possemthwaite began to tremble slightly as Lacuna pulled back her right foot, but he recovered as she changed her mind about the almighty shin kick of death, destruction, doom and other fearsome words beginning with ‘D’. This ‘Steve’ of whom she spoke, is her shotgun. So its not really that hard to see the point of view of poor Possemthwaite, he didn’t want to be the guy to lose his job because her luggage was not checked properly by the small team who packed it for her. Little did he know that it was she who put Steve in there. “I put Steve in there personally! How dare you suggest my ignorance in the packing sequence!” She stormed at the thoroughly shocked Possemthwaite “I just didn’t want you to get arrested!” he panicked, raising his own voice in retaliation foolishly “Why would I be arrested? Every kid in America owns a bloody gun! They give them away as bloody presents for opening frigging bank accounts!”she screamed, but began to see the point. Being arrested isn’t a good thing “I’m sorry, but it is actually illegal, after that Twin Towers thing they got a bit jumpy” “No, no, I see your point, put Steve back in his place, I think I have some sort of weaponry over there anyway, speaking of ‘over there’ get the stuff in the car and lets get moving.” - - - - - - - - - -Washington, DCA very long flight later… As she stepped out onto the cold grey gravel outside of her house, she heard the music for the first time. That low, heavy, thumping bass line that penetrated thick walls just as easily as thin eardrums. Brook had driven over to the airport to collect her as quickly as possible and had filled her in on all the details that came to mind. Basically she was having a party. When he said basically, he meant she had everyone from the surrounding three towns and had drained the entire years beer supply in one and a half days. “At least we have no neighbours” she muttered coldly under her breath She looked towards the house with disgust clouding her perfect brown eyes and she shut them again, glancing to the crowd of people, her people, who were idly kicking stones outside the thick oak door. “What the bloody hell do you think you are doing? Why are you not working?” The nearest man was the only one who found the courage to speak to Lacuna who was shaking with an inner rage at the sorry state of her workforce before her. “Er, we have had a slight technical hitch, Ma’am” spoke the crop-haired Jones quietly. He was a big man, ex army, who needed a job. He was employed on the security team. She growled ”What has she done this time, Jones. Why are you all outside” “Technically we are not all outside, most are trapped in the kitchen…”he was cut off by a furious Lacuna, her features quivering. “What do you mean… trapped?” she approached Jones and looked into his chest. He was roughly five inches taller than her, and she was six foot one. “Well, erm, err…” “Are you going to bloody tell me whets going on here, you goon?” She glared at him, but his worried face told her nothing ”URGH!” Finally she couldn’t take his silence any more and she swung her right boot as hard as she could into the groin of the security man, who yelped and doubled over in pain and shock, collapsing heavily to the wet gravel. ”Is someone going to tell me whets going on here or are you all in for a kicking and a firing?” this brought a load, long groan from the fallen security guard, who apparently didn’t want to lose his job as well as his baby-producing abilities. When, all of a sudden a window above their heads opened. “Look! It’s Lacuna! Hey Lacuna! Woo! Up here!” And, above her head, waving frantically, was Jade. A very drunken Jade. Her eyes were glazed and she wobbled dangerously as she leant out of the window. “Jade, what the bloody hell is going on and why are my employees outside?” The bitterness was clear in her voice to everyone except the very drunken Jade, who smiled innocently back down to earth “Oh, they were being annoying and not letting me partaaaaaaaaay woooo!” “So why are they outside?” Lacuna used the ultra polite, ultra sarcastic voice, still to no emotional response to Jade. “I locked them out, I didn’t know you were coming to get drunk of my alchymahol with me, or I’d have got sooooo much more in!” “Just let me in” “Whyyyyyy?” “I do not have time to play your stupid games, just let me in” “Whyyyyyy?” “Don’t make me get Steve out” she bluffed and it worked as Jade dropped her bottle (which smashed on the ground by lacuna’s feet) and stumbled away from the window towards the door “You retards! You bloody morons! How could you let this happen?” she screamed at the dishevelled workers, who turned away in shame. There was little point in arguing with Lacuna at any time, let alone when she is angry. A handful of days before she would face the cruiserweight and TV champions in the same match and she was forced to put up with a hung over Jade as her partner. What had she done to deserve this? This was he first chance to begin taking the world by storm against two of the biggest stars in PCW and it looked to be wasted just because Lacuna hadn’t hidden the beer well enough. Yet, on talent alone she did not doubt the ability of Significant Others, their new tag name. Newbies they are, but not for long. Her thoughts were disrupted as Jade threw open the door and opened her arms wide, calling to Lacuna “Lacunaaaa come and have a little drinkies with Jade-ee!” Lacuna forced her way into the hall and shoved Jade back, before fixing her with a menacing glance, which Jade noticed and began backing off, tripping slightly on the bottom stair “Why you little PIPSQUEEK! I should finish you for good this time! Come here! Lacuna roared and lunged at Jade who, despite being pissed out of her head, dodged nimbly and sprinted up the stairs “Yikes! I’m going to hide in my room!” And she did, leaving a furious Lacuna glaring at her fleeing body with a renewed anger
