The Heat Of Egypt...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday afternoon... (Led Zeppelin’s ‘Kashmir’ begins to play in the background as a gentle breeze kicks up a folly of sand and the cameras slowly pass over rolling dunes of rich golden terrain. A voiceover leisurely grows audible…) Voiceover, typically dark and brooding: “Somewhere about these burning plains roams a band of brave men. Their reasons for being here are unknown, and their sanity is probably not intact to say the least, but they’re here nonetheless. As we pan in, we see them, all nineteen of them, plodding on regardless of the sweltering heat, the consistent battering of sand, and the fact that none of them actually know why the hell they’re here in the first place…” (The vast line of guards stretches back across the desert-like horizon as the cameras slowly settle on a select group of hunched over men, their legs dragging more so than walking as they move onward, sweat inevitably pouring from their faces.) Guard, perplexed: “Why are we here again?” Guard#2, equally perplexed: “(Shrugs)” Guard#1, scowling: “Does ANYONE know why we’re here?” Unknown Egyptian guard: “(Something in Egyptian)!” (The first guard tilts his head to one side, realization sinking in.) Guard#1: “Wait a damned minute! We have NO real EGYPTIANS in this regiment! WHO ARE YOU?! And who the hell are you WORKING FOR?! Unknown Egyptian Guard, shrugging: “(Something in Egyptian).” Guard#2, stroking chin: “Is that so? Sounds like a typical excuse to me, my friend. Almost TOO typical!” (The man pounces, taking the Egyptian down with a clutter of armour! The others watch on as the sun beats down, its rays clearly affecting the Guard’s minds.) Voiceover, still as brooding as ever: “The Egyptian heat has never been very forgiving; it has always been unmerciful…as unmerciful as hell itself! And it can turn men insane with rage…” Guard#4, breaking the brawl up: “What the heck is the matter with you, old chap?! Have you gone completely bloody MAD?!” Guard#1, pushing his comrade off: “Get away from me! All of you…STAY BACK!!” (The guard produces a rustic old knife from his person and swings it wildly.) Guard#7, feeling suicidal: “Now, now…just put the—OWW! That REALLY hurt! I can’t believe you fuckin’ stabbed me, you BASTARD! (Dies)” (A little further back down the line of guards, four robe-clad men carrying a regally decorated palanquin upon their shoulders march on, atop of which, behind silken curtains, reside The Pharaoh Titu Xansiv and Isiy, his charming wife.) Robed figure#1: “I wish they’d stop having sex up there; my shoulder’s absolutely KILLING!” Robed figure#2: “You’re telling ME?! I’m the one carrying the back end of this thing!” Robed figure#4: “What and I’m NOT?!” (The second man cackles cynically, but utters not another word.) Robed figure#3: “Water…must…reach…water…” (The first man kicks his counterpart in the leg. The palanquin abruptly tilts to one side, sparking interest from the guards nearby.) Robed man#1, angrily: “Stop complaining, you big wuss!” Robed figure#3: “Water…must…reach…water…” (Another kick to the shin.) Robed man#1: “Seriously, one more hoot from you and you’re mine, street rat!!” (A moment of silence ensues…But it doesn’t last long…) Robed figure#3: “…Water…must…reach…water…” Robed Man#1: “Right, that’s it! I’ve had just about enough of you, mate!” (He drops his corner of the support pole and stampedes the babbling man to his right! The palanquin goes crashing to the ground, kicking up a wave of sand as it collides with the ground!) Voice of The Pharaoh, angered: “What in the unholy name of Anubis is going on out there?!” Robed figure#2, speaking at first to his companion, and then to his highness: “Oh, shit. Err, NOTHING, milord…nothing at all!” Voiceover: “The heat of Egypt can anger a man. Even a man of its own land.” (A moment passes before the curtains are swiped open and The Pharaoh steps out onto the sand, his expression filed with aggravation and his body confined within the comfort of a dark red robe.) The Pharaoh, staring in disbelief: “What the Duat do you mean NOTHING?! Look at my throne, you fool!” Robed figure#2, confused: “I don’t know what you mean, sire.” The Pharaoh, pointing at the palanquin: “For the love of the Gods, man! Look at IT! There’s nobody at the front, by Ra!” (The robed throne-bearer shrugs.) Robed figure#2: “Not my fault, milord, look over there!” (Pharaoh turns around to find the front bearers brawling in the sand.) The Pharaoh, slapping forehead, sighing: “Oh, for the love of the Gods. Right, you two…get up, c’mon…get up.” (He makes his way towards the men and separates them, dragging them both to their feet.) The Pharaoh: “Now what’s this all about? Don’t you two realize you’re holding us up?!” (The two men look about them, and indeed the guards are watching intensely, stationary and wiping their tired brows.) Robed figure#2, moodily: “Well, he started it.” Robed figure#4, innocently: “Water…must…reach…water…!!” (Pharaoh cocks an eyebrow.) The Pharaoh, baffled: “O.k, I’m sorry, but did you just say that as though you were DEFENDING yourself?! By the GODS! Look, does anyone know what he’s talking about?” (One of the on-looking guards raises a hand sheepishly. Titu smiles, noticing him.) The Pharaoh: “Ah! Come forth, my friend! And tell me what this man sa—” Robed figure#4, defensively: “WATER…must…REACH…water…?!” (Titu stares.) The Pharaoh, not taking an eye off the hooded palanquin bearer: “Guard…what in the name of this great land did he just utter?” Guard: “…(Something in French).” (Xansiv throws up his arms hopelessly.) The Pharaoh: “O…k…where the DUAT do we fine these lunatics?” Guard#13, dumbfounded: “But he’s FRENCH, sir.” The Pharaoh, oblivious: “And your point is?” Guard#13, nodding: “Good call, boss.” The Pharaoh: “Now, get this thing together, my friends, we’re on a tight schedule here so let’s keep moving…WITHOUT ANY MORE FIGHTS! Yes, Johnny, I saw your face.” (Johnny, the random Palace guard, hangs his head, sulking. Pharaoh begins to remount the palanquin, when the voice of a guard stops him in his tracks.) Guard#10: “Sir, what the HELL are we doing out here anyway?” (Pharaoh turns his head and sparks a grin.) The Pharaoh: “I’m TRAINING, my friend! Isn’t it obvious?! I do have a match against that dear old Violence Jack fellow this coming Tuesday, you know!” Guard#10: “More like, WE’RE training! We’ve been walking for hours!” The Pharaoh: “Look, I employ you to GUARD me, hence your title, so do your job and GUARD me, man! No arguing! Just do…your…job! (Moving behind the curtains.) Honey, I’m BACK!” Voice of Isiy, seductively: “Come to me, my love. Come to me…” Guard#10, scowling: “How come he has ALL the FUN?!” (The cameras pan out to the original view we opened up on as the guards plod ever onward into the Egyptian desert…) Voiceover, in a calculating tone: “Sometimes the heat of the desert can be strenuous, cunning even, and the souls of many have succumbed to the warmth of Egypt. Perhaps Violence Jack will be the next victim of Egypt’s heat? Perhaps so…” (The scene fades to gold.)
