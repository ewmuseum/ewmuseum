Dude, watch out for that truck.

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 5, 2002 [The black golf cart with the letters 'TS' painted on the side, putts it's way down Broadmaker Boulevard. Yes, the golf cart, with the max speed of ten mile an hour, is going down a fairly major roadway as if it belonged there. In the drivers seat, 'Heavy Metal' Harry keeps his bloodshot eyes fixed on the road. His long, shoulder length brown hair flickers a little bit from the wind. He wears a pair of leather gloves to aid his grip on the newly installed flame steering wheel cover. In the passenger seat, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy seems most comfortable on their trek down Broadmaker. His blonde locks of hair blowing in the wind, his one million dollar smile radiating off of the bright sunlight of California, and his smooth, tanned skin bring out the gnarly blue in his eyes. There are most bodacious sights taking Broadmaker Boulevard toward California's premiere mall, THE MALL. Tommy smells victory on Fusion and what better way to celebrate then in some totally righteous new threads. He hears that The Gap and Old Navy are having MONDO sales because of Christmas. He could come out of those stores with more than one outfit, probably a bodacious closet full if he doesn't watch himself. There is no better way to get to THE MALL, than by way of golf cart. Enjoy the sights, ponder about the righteousness of life, and come up with a totally gnarly idea for the celebration party when The Tubular One is holding up his newly won PCW Continental Title] 'TOtally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, slow down...your going to get us killed. [Harry looks down at the speedometer and notices that his prized golf cart is only getting up to 7 mile per hour. Harry turns to his former tag team partner and full time bro] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Your right, man. I'll take it down a notch. [Tommy smiles arrogantly and nods his head, happy with the way life is going. It's most bogus that Harry suffered that heinous injury at the hands of Akiyama and The End at Alliances, but it's now opened up an entirely new gnarly world. Tommy has defeated Rush, which is no surprise, and he's on his way to becoming PCW's Continental Champion. It's only been two weeks since his change from tag team superstar to singles superstar and he's already riding the bodacious wings of victory. It really shouldn't come as a surprise though, this is The Tubular One we're talking about. It's not some dime and nickel, totally bogus compadre who doesn't know a wristlock from a wristwatch. Tommy's father, Radical Randy, was a highly decorated wrestler, and even though he died in the right it never made a difference. Tommy was meant to step into his father's radical boots, take up the mantle and Radical, and take the game to a most bodacious new level] TTT: Har, what do you think it's like to be blind? [Harry shrugs] Harry: I don't know, man...I'm not blind. [Tommy ponders for a few moments, trying to put himself in the shoes of a blind man. Not being able to see images or the television. Not being able to watch the waves crash down on the sand, and slowly roll back up. Not being able to watch the bodacious sun rise and set. NOT BEING ABLE TO SEE TANNED BABES OILING UP!! TOTALLY BOGUS, DUDE!] TTT: I've got an idea, compadre. Just stay still and don't freak out or anything. That could be most bogus... Harry: What are you going to do, man? [Tommy chuckles to himself and then covers Harry's eyes up] TTT: Dude...what's it like? [Harry doesn't freak out and seems to be keeping his composure. The golf cart is staying straight on Broadmaker Boulevard, gaining closer to THE MALL at a snail like pace] Harry: It's...like...weird, man. TTT: Explain weird. Harry: Well...I can't see, man! [Tommy arches his eyebrows as if it was a totally bodacious discovery. He keeps his hands over Harry's eyes as a silver BMW comes barreling up behind them] Harry: Silver BMW coming up behind us...at exactly 35...30...25...slowing down dramatically. Goodyear tires...the front tires need replaced, man. [Tommy looks at his brother very strangley] TTT: Dude...that was freakin' excellent!! [Harry takes a fairly sharp turn, almost running into a garbage can. The BMW comes up right behind the golf cart, only inches from it. With the slightest tap of the brake Harry could send this expensive foreign car into them...hurting them severely as well. Tommy, still with his hands over Harry's eyes, looks back at the Beamer] TTT: Hey, I know that dude. That's Gregory!! He's pretty close, dude. Harry: 2.34 inches, man. [Tommy gives Harry that same strange look, then looks back at the Beamer. Gregory Henderson, a professional surfer, has been a good friend of the Cowabunga's since the early days of high school. They used to play gnarly pranks on the other students and even spike the punch bowl at dances...but that's all their relationship has been really. Stupid pranks that usually don't work anyway. Like when they put glue on Mr. Harrington's seat, so that his ten pound chair would be glued to his ass. Unfortunately, Gregory is not the brightest bulb in the Christmas lights, and ended up gluing his crappy goatee to the chair. The totally heinous, smelly janitor had to come up with these giatn hedge clippers and chop off the goatee. Gregory cannot grow facial hair on his chin anymore. If he doesn't shave for 3 days, it looks MOST WEIRD!] Tommy: What in the name of traffic laws are you doing, compadre? [The scrawny, tanned ugly man hangs out of the window with a very retarded looking smile across his face] Gregory: Just going to head down to Pasadena to get my groove on with Sheila Williamson!! TTT: She's a total skank, dude. [Harry runs over a cat...it scurries away into nearby bushes to lick it's wounds] Gregory: No way, bra! She's itchin' for a little Greg action! TTT: She's itching because she has herpes, dude. Gregory: Man!! You put a bummer on everything for me, bra. Hey...why do you have your hands over Har's eyes! He usin' the force or something? TTT: I guess you could say that, compadre. I'm letting Harry find out what it's like to be blind. He's most gifted at this blind thing...it's like he's been blind before or something. Gregory: Well, he sucks at it because he just hit a cat, bra. TTT: Har, you did know you ran over that cat...right? Harry: I was trying to hit it, man. It was grey with a black spot on it's tail... TTT: DUDE!!! Gregory: That's freakin' great, bra! Harry: Yeah, man...thank God Tommy moved his fingers apart. [Tommy shakes his head, taking his hands away. The experiment failed. Harry did a good job at first but then he totally sucked afterward. Talk about bogus] TTT: I guess it truly is most heinous to be blind dude. You do know The End is blind, right? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: That's where the advantage comes in, compadre. I've got two totally bogus guys, Walden and The H.I.T....then this totally blind Celestial dude. That ref might as well hand me the Continental Title! Two bogus dudes and a blind guy...it's like a totally heinous comedy movie. Harry: Yeah, man. [Gregory whips around the golf cart and pulls next to them, in the wrong lane. The Cowabunga's look over at the very ugly professional surfer, who seems to be having a few pimples forming on his nose...must be from the herpes ridden Sheila...ugh...BOGUS!] Gregory: Well, girls, I'm off to go claim new land on Sheila Williamson.... [Tommy shakes his head] TTT: Dude...that land is TOTALLY not new. Gregory: Tom, your really bringin' me down, bra. I'm a man with spirit, a man with charisma, and a man with sexual needs. I have all the tools to tame all the fools, namely unsuspecting chicks. I have the facts, that is exact. [Tommy shakes his head and whispers to Harry] TTT: Dude, is he trying to rap again? Harry: Yeah, man... [Harry looks ahead and then looks back at Tommy with a rather concerned look] Harry: Should we tell him about that large truck coming head on, man? [Tommy looks at his brother once again as Gregory is rapping about Subtraction. Tommy then looks at the large semi that is going head on with the BMW] TTT: Nah...this bodacious rap about subtracting lambs from a vagina is most interesting. [The truck blows its horn, but Gregory is either too stupid or too into his rap to notice the giant truck coming at him. It slams on its brakes sliding forward as Gregory raps about stealing virginity from a school girl with blonde hair....and she's shaved.....on her legs of course] Truck driver: YOUR CRAZY!! [Harry smashes down the gas pedal letting the force of going from 7 miles per hour to 10 really kick in. Harry adjusts his side view mirror, with a decal of Dee Snider, and watches the truck smash into Gregory's precious BMW] TTT: We totally saved that guy, Har. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Now...to THE MALL, I must find gnarly threads to be in at my victory party. Harry: What's a twat, man? TTT: I think it's an Italian pastry.... Harry: Far out, man...we should get those for your victory party. TTT: Totally! [Fade to black]
