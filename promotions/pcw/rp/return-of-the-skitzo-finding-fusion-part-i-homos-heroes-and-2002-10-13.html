Return of the Skitzo: Finding Fusion Part I - Homos, Heroes and Havoc

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-10-02 The dark and stillness of a lonesome small room is disrupted steadily by the drip, drip, dripping of water from a leaky pipe. All around, everywhere, there is nothing but darkness. As the water continues to drip, it slowly becomes fainter and fainter, and drowns out by the sudden beating of a heart. Lub dub…lub dub…lub dub… With the beating of the heart comes the calm, steady sound of breathing from somewhere in the darkness. The dripping from the leaky pipe has faded completely into nothingness, leaving only the beating and the breathing. For minutes more this is all that fills the black void around, when a little voice begins to pip from somewhere. “…It is my destiny…as a soul placed before this magnitude of greatness I must, nay I will prove myself! Be it my task to eat burning coals or breathe the fires of hell I will persevere until my true-…” Suddenly a huge beam of light blasts across the entire room, filling it with pure white light, breaking the poetic mood and revealing all within. A surprised figure suddenly sits up straight, his eyes wide and his heart beating as a door bangs shut and the mischievous giggling of two men stumbling in sends a chill down the sitting man’s spine. Frozen in suspense, he grows angry inside as the peace and serenity is shattered by the invasion of his privacy, in this place of solitude. He sighs to himself and listens as the two men stumble further into the room and pass him, yet somehow fail to realise he is a mere couple of feet to their left. Man #1: You’re so naughty when you’re drunk! Man #2: Oh be nice Rufus, it’s not like I’m completely byronized or anything! Rufus: Byro-…what? Make some sense James, I’m not into all this slang and lingo yet! James: I’ll make a man out of you yet Rufus! From his perch, the sitting man can take no more, and bursts through the stall he was in and roars to a halt before the two shocked men. Rufus: Jesus, Mary and Joseph! Who are you?! James: And what is that unGODLY stench? Man who was sitting but now is standing before two shocked, appalled, and slightly turned on homosexuals: Knowin’ my name ain’t makin’ no damned relevance to ya’ll bum-chums now, iddit? What be you doin’ in MA sanctuary of solitude and enlightenment?! The two obviously flaming homos take a quick look around the room then send a look of confusion and sarcasm towards the man, before one of them giggles and sighs. Rufus: I don’t know about you sire, but last time I checked this wasn’t a sanctuary of solitude……………………it’s the Men’s Shitter! Indeed, all three men are standing in the middle of the Men’s Lavatory, a public lavatory, located just a few blocks from the Oita Big Eye Stadium in Oita City, Japan where Total Selection Friday Fusion was hosted, the same venue which saw the return of Skitzo and the partial downfall of The Chef. While all the PCW stars are headed out of the country for home, Skitzo has stopped off to compose his thoughts in the loos. Skitzo: You boys don’t like ‘rasslin’ I’m guessing? James: If you’re talking about two sweaty men in tight spandex touching each other a lot…no…no we don’t… Rufus: Are you with that PCW lot that are leaving? Skitzo: Well, if this was PWA I’d say yeah, my name be Skitzo and I are the biggest thing to hit PCW since a Bertha porno…but it ain’t PWA and I’m a lowly li’l ‘rassler looking to make a new beginning… Skitzo: How can I get any peace when two bum boys are trying to have it off in my boudoir?! Skitzo: This is a toilet not a boudoir… Skitzo: Coulda told that to George Michael… The two men look at each other shocked, and then slowly inch away to a private cubicle. Skitzo listens as manly moaning rises up out of the cubicle…then turns to leave only to hear one of them screaming like a horrified girl. Skitzo giggles and opens the cubicle door to find them both sitting in a compromising position…with some kind of device bleeping beneath the toilet seat. Rufus: WHAT THE HELL IS THIS?! Skitzo: That there be a bomb, y’know like outta that Mel Gibson/Danny Glover film? James: TURN IT OFF! Skitzo: No… Rufus: Why? Skitzo: Because I fucking despise homosexuals and if you two get up off that seat you die the way you lived…gay and covered in shit… Skitzo: You hate gays? Skitzo: Does it matter? This is a feat even Pranks With Attitude can be proud of…too bad I’m just as witty… Skitzo: Too bad we’re just as witty… Skitzo: Meh who cares, let’s leave the homos to die alone… Skitzo twitches and exits the toilet, watching closely as some construction workers, policemen and a cowboy walk into the toilet…and the entire thing explodes in a shower of excrement and blood. Skitzo: It’s raining’ men… Chuckling to himself, our not-so-with-it hero takes off down another street that has a name he does not understand, heading in the general direction of the closest aeroplane port. Passing a local hotel, Skitzo stops suddenly and turns to see a familiar figure walking out of the hotel, and eyes up the Continental frame of none other than Rent-a-Hero!!!……………………then runs away before the spandex-covered weirdo can make any kind of contact with him. Skitzo: If I wore spandex and got fucked by a walking redheaded STD I’d have killed myself by now… Skitzo: You did… Skitzo: No I damn well didn’t, Mon Cher! Skitzo: It don’t matter, bein’ said that I gots my first PCW match this Friday against-… Skitzo: YOU’SE FIGHTIN’ EVERYBODY BOY! Skitzo: Then I'm gonna kick their asses… Skitzo: ALL FOR ONE… Skitzo: AND ONE FOR THE TROOPS! Skitzo twitches continuously, passing the Japanese folk, who stare back at him as he waltzes past foreign streets and places, none of which makes any sense to him. Trying to figure out the street signs and the buildings around him, Skitzo slowly advances towards the train station, and a ride on the Bullet train to Tokyo. For a day where a man can blow up some homosexuals and run away from Spiderman’s stalker, and not make any note of a contract in PCW or being the sidekick of an intensely insane Goblin, Skitzo feels pretty damn good about himself. Skitzo: I do only wish there be some swamps around here for to go Crocodile Huntin’… Skitzo: Fill Bertha’s dutty ol’ Greasesnatch with some water and you got yourself a dang olmypic size swimming pool! For some strange reason the scene faded out at that very second. To Be Continued, Mon Cher!
