Void Training

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 Introduction: 'Now we play in a valley unknown to most, feared by the rest. What is life until you stood and faced the light, stared it down to it's soul and watched it in all it's splendor? Now try standing in the mirror and looking into your soul. If you can brave this alone then inner peace will soon follow.' The splendors of life are hidden in time, shadowed by darkness, and destroyed by ignorance. Does time really move faster or is it just that human beings have become more unaware? Throwing time away like a child would a used toy that sinks to the bottomless despair of a toy box. What if I told you a plain existed where time held no meaning? You would call me crazy I'm sure but this place does exist. It can be unleashed inside of your mind but most human beings never reach that level of mental power. One man has perfected the entrance of the 'void' if you will. He is known as Akiyama. He uses his void for either pleasure, pain, or training but for now he uses it to help his new friend Jay Sylo train for his up-coming match against The rising star champion Lance Bell, Ripper, and Johnson. Sylo was ready but in ways he wasn't. He still had to face the issue of the beast inside, the one he trained, the one that he thinks he has tamed. Akiyama is almost ready to unleash creatures from other dimensions to help Sylo train...each creature will represent one of his opponents but the catch is if Sylo doesn't make it, he will be trapped in the void for good. We now join the two, Akiyama is getting ready to open the void for Sylo to enter. This will truly be one of the biggest tests in all of the young mans life. (Dark swirled around like an endless vortex, no light could escape it's cold, dark grasp but for some reason two figures could be made out. One slightly taller than the other as they moved forward a small bit. A swirling vortex of blue and silver opened up, continuously swirling. The light revealed a figure with silver hair pulled back in a pony-tail. He wore all black covering every spec of his body except for his eyes which were an almond color. The man beside him had icy blue hair with eyes and goatee to match. He was very ripped and wore nothing more than a baggy pair of ice blue pants with white fire around the ankles.) 'We know you can do this Sylo, remember when you first enter the void you will be slightly dizzy and may feel a slight tingling feeling but since your baptism you won't feel the great sense of pain,' Akiyama said pointing his attention to Sylo. 'I understand,' Was all the Sylencer said. (The void expanded more and more as Akiyama glowed. Sylo stepped through and quickly disappeared but still could hear the voice of Akiyama from above him.) 'Are you feeling ok?' Akiyama asked. 'I am fine,' Sylo replied. 'Level one is coming, be prepared,' Akiyama's voice faded. (The vortex swirled like a whirl-pool of colors. You could never see an ending, it rolled on forever it seemed, all aspects swirled with silver and blue. Out of nothing stepped a large green beast with fire for eyes. He snarled a small bit as Sylo took his fighting stance putting one foot forward and one back. He shifted his hands a small bit putting one to his side, slightly bent and one bent slightly forward. The creature snarled showing it's drool covered teeth. It wore armor which shined from the void. He let out a dark laugh but Sylo only looked back with a smirk. He clinched his fist and let out a loud yell as an aura expanded on him. His muscles expanded and his eyes were full of ice as he stood straight up laughing a small bit.) 'So who are you supposed to represent?' Sylo snarled. 'I represent Steve Johnson but I am known as Salacious,' The creature roared. 'Well...then lets dance!' Sylo snarled once more. (He let our a roar and charged at the beast smacking him down with an axe handle. The creature jumped back up and lashed it's sharp, shiny claws at Sylo but he moved kicking the creature in the back of the leg.) 'Is this the best you've got?' Sylo said laughing a small bit. (The creature roared and jumped up charging at Sylo who continued to battle back. They threw lefts and rights at each other, back and forth until the creature used his long green tail to knock Sylo on his back. He went to stomp but Sylo rolled out of the way and leaped to his feet. The creature charged once more as Sylo let out a blood curdling roar. With one mighty swoop he picked up the creature and drove him straight down into the floor of the void while hearing a loud 'pop') 'Very good,' The voice of Akiyama could be heard. (The floor of the void opened sucking the creature back down into the hole. It quickly closed back up with great suction. Sylo snarled and looked around at the swirling colors once more.) 'What's next?' He asked ready to go. 'Your next opponent will arrive in a matter of moments,' Akiyama's voice replied. (The vortex opened up once more and out stepped a blue creature covered in slimy scales. He had a long spiky tail, each spike was razor sharp. He wore torn leather all over his body and stood around 6'4'.) 'And who do you represent?' Asked Sylo. 'I represent Rip Ryckman and prepare to die!' The creature yelled. (Sylo smirked taking his fighting stance once more. The creature charged as Sylo sprung forward nailing him in the jaw; It had no effect. Sylo snarled as the beast whipped it's tail catching Sylo in the leg. He fell hard as blood began to pour from the open wound. Sylo roared and jumped up nailing the creature in the midsection with various left and right stiff kicks. Faster and faster he drove each foot into the midsection over and over striking the creature with deadly blows. Sylo stepped back as the creature bent over coughing up blue blood. He threw a right hand nailing the creature in the jaw, over and over. Finally with one final upper cut the creature went flying on it's back.) 'Is that all?' Sylo asked. (The creature lunged up but Sylo ducked and grabbed the beast by the tail and drove him down into the void ground snapping his neck as well. The beast laid there as blood ran from it's mouth. Sylo only snarled as the hole from earlier opened up and let the creature drop in.) 'Your doing great Sylo,' The voice of Akiyama could be heard again. 'Thank you,' Sylo replied looking forward. 'But remember if you do not defeat this last opponent you will be trapped in the void for good. We are sorry our friend but that is how the void works,' Akiyama informed Sylo before his voice faded. (Sylo only nodded as the vortex door opened for the last time. The creature that stepped out was orange with spikes covering everything but it's chest, inner arms and legs and face. Sylo snarled as the beast snarled back, their eyes were locked on to each other, moving to the rhythm of the moment. On his shoulder the creature held a title which resembled the rising star title.) 'Let me guess, your supposed to be Lance Bell?' Sylo said smirking a small bit. 'Yes and I am the one who will wipe that smirk off your face! Prepare for your death!' The creature yelled out as he charged at Sylo. (Sylo and the beast collided. Sylo turned to punch but quickly stopped himself so the spikes wouldn't be drove into his hand. The beast kicked Sylo in the gut sending him sprawling against the voids ground but like a quick flash of lighting Sylo sprung back to his feet planting them firmly on the ground. He charged once more and threw a few right hands to the gut of the beast but it did nothing, the beast back handed Sylo as a spike caught him a small bit opening a cut on his face.) 'Your pathetic,' The beast snarled. (Sylo stood up, angered by the words of the beast. He launched himself forward and began driving his hand into the gut of the beast harder and harder. A blue glow surrounded Sylo as he reached around the neck of the beast and raised him high into the air cutting off his air supply. The creatures legs began to twitch and flail wildly but Sylo never let go.) 'You see there is no stopping me, use weapons or cheap tricks because I really don't care....I am the one you fear and you will never...ever forget that!' (With one mighty thrust Sylo drove the beast into the ground snapping it's spinal cord. He stood up and snarled once more letting out a roar to follow up. His eyes stayed locked on the creature who let out screams of pain. Soon the hole opened up and the beast fell in.) 'Akiyama...I have completed my task,' Sylo said into the void. 'We see that. Very good but one task remains,' 'What?!' Sylo said shocked. 'This is your last training session....do well or be trapped forever, we are sorry but this must be done,' Akiyama's voice soon faded. (The vortex door opened except this time all three creatures stepped out, healed and ready to fight.) 'Great,' Sylo muttered. (The first beast which represented Johnson charged. Sylo nailed him with a back hand and shoved him into the creature representing Ripper. Sylo leaped up and nailed a spinning heel kick on both men then turned and charged at the creature who represented Bell but The creature nailed Jay in the arm with a spike knocking him to the ground. All three beasts stood up and snarled as they charged at Sylo. Sylo stood up and tried to back up but the beast charged forward. Quickly Sylo slid under their legs and came up from behind kicking the creature representing Johnson's leg breaking it. The black hole opened up and the beast representing Johnson disappeared. Sylo turned and began to jam his fist in the back of the creature which represented Bell's head. The creature which represented Ripper grabbed Sylo and slung him away. The creature charged and leaped for Sylo but Sylo made it to his feet and slammed it's head on the ground. Sylo stood up and picked up the beast, set him up in a power bomb position and actually delivered the Systematic Shutdown. The beast yelled in pain as the ground opened up sucking the creature in.) 'So it's just you and me once again!' Sylo yelled to the creature representing bell. (The beast snarled but Sylo was ready. He dropped kicked the beast in the head snapping it's neck. Sylo snarled down as the black hole opened sucking the last creature in.) 'Impressive!' boomed the voice of Akiyama. 'I'm tired...get me out of here..' Sylo replied. (The Void's door opened and Sylo stepped out. The colors surrounded him until finally he was surrounded by darkness once more.) (The sun slowly set behind the mountains of an undisclosed location. The birds sang their final songs of day for soon they would rest for the night. The two lone figures stood looking out into the distance, silence was the only thing they had between them.) 'Good luck at march madness,' Sylo said looking to Akiyama. 'Yes we must fight for the world title, the worst part is the fact that we will be number three to enter,' Akiyama replied. 'You have always found a way to win, I know you will,' Sylo said looking into the distance. 'Yes, we usually do but you...you have to fight three men and try to bring gold to the divinity. You are ready our friend but do not underestimate your opponent. Bring the gold home, do it for your fans, do it for the divinity, and do it for yourself,' Akiyama said looking back to Sylo. (He only nodded a small bit.) 'March Madness...it's name fits it because that's what it will be...Madness. I will walk in and unleash everything I have upon my opponents until I walk out with the Rising Star title draped over my shoulder. Time is almost up...may god have mercy on their soul...' Sylo muttered out looking at the fading sun. (The two men watched the sun set, not saying another word as the sun slowly set behind the hills and mountains. Darkness embraced them waiting for time to show all but for now time was up.)
