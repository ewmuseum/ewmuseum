Forming A Plan?

Date: Before The Airship Took Off ‘So how does it work, exactly?’ Gazing at the beautiful, illustrious vision of the new mighty PCW airship, the Cloud Nine, Dorn merely raises an eyebrow behind his dark shades when presented with its greatness. Skeptic is the only word that describes the look on the Astronomer’s face as he contemplates the physics and mechanics of the hulking airborne cruise ship before him. Truthfully, he believes it to be a good idea, but whether it’ll work or not… ‘Have you have been on a cruise ship?’ The man with oil-stained hands beside him inquires, not actually looking at him. The man pulls a filthy rag from his shoulder and wipes his hands down, then stuffs the towel in his pocket. ‘It works a lot like that.’ The Astronomer chuckles to himself and looks to the ground, letting his hand hang for amount before lifting it back up to the engineer, “I can’t say I’ve ever been on a cruise ship. Nor have I ever seen one fly. So you’ll have to explain it to me a little more than that. After all, I’ll be one of the lucky individuals that gets to fly on this damn airborne five star and I’d like to know what I’ll be getting AIR SICK on!’ Dorn’s last statement comes out harsh and abrupt, startling the engineer a little. ‘Well, um…I see. Well, Mr. Tracer, do you have any questions, first off?’ Dorn strokes his freshly shaven and rather pointy chin in consideration of the engineer’s question. Does he have any questions about this flying Ritz? Does he have any REAL concerns about this floating castle of metallic and champagne-smelling wonder? I’ll be damned if I care less, Dorn thinks as a smug grin crosses his face. He even begins to nod and laugh to himself in a quiet, sinister manner. Now obviously, the engineer is still standing there and is now completely overtaken by this little charade. He begins to slowly move away from the silently crazed Astronomer, taking little baby steps and shifting his eyes from his destination to Dorn then back to his destination in a little under a second many times in a row. Needless to say, Tracer notices and gives the engineer an infuriated look, ‘HEY! Where the fuck do you think you’re going?’ ‘I, uh…’ The engineer babbles, desperately trying to come up with a good lie. Suddenly, one hits him and he grips his stomach in mock agony, ‘Oooh damn, I think that chili con carne is finally catching up with me! Goes through me like shit through a goose!’ The Astronomer isn’t convinced, however, ‘Uh huh. You think I’m crazy, don’t you?’ ‘Well, no, I-‘ ‘You think I’m fucking INSANE, don’t you?!?’ Dorn’s voice is getting louder and more infuriated by the second. If the engineer didn’t have to shit before, he does now. ‘No sir, I-‘ The Astronomer suddenly RIPS his shades off his own face and throws them right at the engineer! They bounce on his pug, overweight stomach and go crashing to the beaten black top, making plastic clicking sounds upon impact. The engineer stares at them for lack of a better thing to stare at without getting himself hit, but that doesn’t save him from Dorn’s wrath, ‘JUST TELL ME HOW MANY STARSDAMNED DOORS THERE ARE ON THE STARSDAMNED THING AND IF THERE’S A STARSDAMNED LANDING PAD FOR A STARSDAMNED HELICOPTER ON THE STARSDAMNED THING!!!!!!!’ ‘Yeah…’ The engineer states blankly, still horrified from what he just heard. ‘It’s… just on the top deck.’ Dorn straightens out his jacket and runs a hand through his hair, ‘Thank you. That’s all I needed.’ Seeing his opportunity to escape this horrific scene, the engineer turns and bolts back towards the terminal, his pace astounding for a man of his girth. A confused Dorn watches him go and shrugs, ‘Whatever floats your boat, I guess. Or airship, I should say,’ then turns his attention back to the gargantuan Cloud Nine ship. Though he actually isn’t too concerned by the ordeal, it still amazes him how such a thing was brought about. ‘No wonder my paycheck was so meager…’ The former TV champion stuffs his hands in his pockets and turns away, heading off towards the terminal. A strange smile crosses his face as he departs, what must he be thinking? ‘The bitch will never know what hit her…’ [End.]
