Date: RP #2 For FF House on Haunted Hill Match The lines between reality and hallucination had blurred several minutes earlier. Stepping out into the dour early December evening, Khadafi reflected upon what he had just done. //Pater Nostre, sanctifictur nomen tuum Advient Regnum tuum, Fiat voluntas tua, sicut in caleo et interra\ The slow burn of the morphine that had crawled up the inside of his arm was quickly replaced by the buzz that currently filled his addled brain. His limbs felt heavy and detached; like components of some alien making. His fingers were blocks of wood. His mouth slack and open. He barely remebered how much the dealer had given him, all he remebered was the call from Dante Jackson. That spiteful, arrogant Dante Jackson. How he had read Ali the riot act. Do this, do that. You're making me look bad. You're making Quo look bad. Show up and do your job. But don't do it the way that works, because that way is offensive. Show the people a kinder, happier Ali. One that they won't be afraid to let their kids go see, or maybe even buy his T-Shirt. Sound about right? Yeah, you know it does. That's why Ali had made the call to an old friend of his. A buddy from back in the day when Khadafi had run rampant on RUSH, when Boda was running the joint. Ali discussed some figures with the man and half an hour later he had enough morphine to knock a horse flat. Khadafi had drained the container into the syringe and gleefully stuck it just under his left bicep. Anything to kill the pain in his neck and his chest. The pain that made it hard to breath, to talk, to think. Now, as he stepped out onto the sidewalk, his eyes glazed and bugging, Khadafi began to wander. Ignored were the strange looks that passers by gave him. Ignored were the sneers at his shabby dress, for he had been stripped down to a pair of baggy maroon sweat pants and an old white T-Shirt and a pair of old Adidas kicks, when he had shot up. Ignored was the dull throb in the base of his neck, or the pulsing ache in his chest. Ignored were the dark clouds that lined the heavens, their bellies pregnant with snow. Ignored was the chill wind that ripped through the thin material of his clothing. Ignored was frigid claws of depression, the claws that he had for so long been battling. Ali Khadafi knew that they would be back, but in his drug induced stupor they were, at least for this short amount of time, gone. //Pater Nostre, sanctifictur nomen tuum Advient Regnum tuum, Fiat voluntas tua, sicut in caleo et interra\ All he saw was that face. The face of Dante Jackson. Smiling, laughing, leering, jeering, ridiculing. It floated next to him, always. Not even the morphine could make the face go away. Not even being stoned up to his eyeballs could shut the specter up. Ever since that phone call, it seemed like the reality of the floating head had solidified even more. Always telling him that he wasgoing to be a failure. Letting his fans down. Dragging down the name of Playaz Inc. though the thought of Playaz Inc. being dead and buried had not occured to anyone, save Khadafi himself Throwing dirt on the name on all black wrestlers. Ali was going to be just another stastitic if he wasn't careful. Ali wasn't amounting to anything. Ali had lost to Jamie Celt! Jamie Celt of all people... What had happened to the monster that had resided in PURE? In RUSH? Where was the rage? Where was the anguish? The sound of shattering glass brought people on the run. What they saw was a ver hagard black man and a broken store front display. The glass haloed his feet in a powdered facsimilie of what it once was. His red blood ran down his fist onto the cold cement. Around Khadafi, the wind wipped and the people stared. Several of them asked if he was alright, but they got no answer. His dead, drugged eyes stared through them. He knew he couldn't tell them that he had had enough of Dante's laughing, smirking visage. They wouldn't understand that he had driven his fist through the glass storefront because he thought that Dante had actually been there next to him. Been there degrading him as he walked down the street. Been there trying to shape Khadafi into what Jackson thought the PCW public wanted. He had been there, but he hadn't. Now Khadafi stood there with blood on his hands. Splinters of glass driven deep into his knuckles and forearms. People gawking and pointing. The people he ignored. The pain he ignored. The laughing face of Jackson he ignored. He knew it wasn't there. The blow to the glass had shook that ideal deep into the morphine fuzzed core of his brain. In fact, the srtike had shaken the affects of the drug off almost completely. Ali knew that because each breath was now a battle. Each time he shifted his weight even a little, it hurt enough to bring tears. Yet no tears fell, only blood. He ignored the blood. He ignored the pain. He ignored the people. He ignored...
