That Wasn't Me

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: That Wasn't Me Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 21/11/02 Okay, so Xavier Kannon has done a lot of pretty shitty things, but is he THAT bad? Can you honestly say PCW would be the same without him? “More importantly, do I care?” So he rubbed a few people up the wrong way, made a couple enemies and maybe didn’t tell people EXACTLY what he was going to do. Or something to that effect. “When you think about it, I DIDN’T do more things than I DID.” That’s actually true. You can pin many things on Xavier Kannon, after all, he only claims to be an upstanding Scientologist, not human being. Then again, a man like Xavier would just about resort to anything to justify or excuse himself. “Media, didn’t do that.” The stable that warred with PCW during 2001... Born at Shanghaied by Tyler Nelson, Mr Showtime, Chet King and Dan Hollywood, their take-over will always be remembered, but in it’s existence it was reviled. So much so, Kannon would probably have wanted in. “Nope, not to blame for that one.” But how does what he did stack up to it? Are the Front Office any better, or for that matter, any worse? Is saying you didn’t do something kind of pointless when you did do something equally as bad? Your honour, I did NOT kill him, I killed HIM! You got it all wrong, I stole THAT car! No, I cheated my Taxes LAST year! “Hey, I said I didn’t do THAT!” People detested Xavier Kannon as Champion. With the Phantom Booker and Ellie~! by his side, he could book his own Title defences and only have a Title match when HE chose to. Not the fighting spirit that the PCW fans liked. “There were much worse you know.” So he didn’t defend his Title much more than the 1 defence per fortnight ruling on the belt, could have removed that ruling all together but he didn’t. In fact, the night he lost the belt to Clyde, he was slated to defend it twice. So in his own way, albeit a rash choice... He had some fight in him. “Corey Williams, anybody?” Say what you want about his willingness to fight, but at least he won the thing fair and square.... If you count low-blows as fair, but when you’re fighting in a cage floating in the Caribbean you don’t tend to stick to normal rules. Now if he was just handed the belt, that would be something. He was booed, jeered, despised, detested... Jeered a bit more, but his reign gave some value to the belt. PCW historians don’t try to forget it, fans don’t cringe when they see it in the record books. No newbies will sigh knowing no matter how hard they work, Xavier Kannon would have held the World Title before them. Stewart Hix? Stryfe? Preacher? “See.... I don’t seem so bad now, do I?” He’ll admit to a few rash firings, he’s a red blooded male just like any other guy on the roster... His tantrum’s impacts are just felt a bit harder. Let’s see... There was Tyler Nelson and the Phantom Booker on the same night, part of the special “Fire one bit of PCW’s legacy, fire another for free” promotion... Then there was everyone who got injured that he didn’t want to pay... And DarkStar... “Could of been worse, at least I left some people.” True. So far the fiery Scientologist and his Front Office have resisted firing the whole roster out of annoyance... Maybe they resisted or just decided they’d only have to hire some of them back anyway. Too time consuming, always go with the path of least resistance. After all, he also knows what it was like to be fired. It happened to him by none other than Tyler Nelson. The Commissioner, the PCW Continental Champion in the same boat as the likes of Big Davylicious, Mike Frampton and Kid Krazie. Maybe the Dark Age got it right, stay unemployed and just kill people. “I hereby whole-heatedly apologise for the actions of Tyler Nelson.” Even before the mass firing at the end of Supremacy, DA BOSS~! had been ‘pruning’ the talent base. Under his employment and answering to his call, Dr. Rupert Healey was a consultant to Duke Williams, and selectively fired members of the roster before hiring hem back for a percentage of the money. At least the Front Office found a way of reducing wages without firing them... Even heavy taxation has it’s pros when you think about it. “May I also add, the Front Office is very protective of PCW’s women.” Considering that 13 months ago, The F.O’s Brandi Wilson was being stalked... That also rings true. No longer the innocent victim, having her every move watch by Judas Steele, lurking in the shadows... It is Miss Wilson who is now in control. Where before she was the hunted, she is now the aggressor, knowing there is security posted around every arena to back her up. “And then to hire that stalker? To pay him?” Even Xavier Kannon can tut along to that. “At least I can say I didn’t do that.” Ever the compassionate one. “I’m also happy to report that so far, no PCW fans have been killed by Australians. A statistic we at the office are proud of.” Well, the figures speak for themselves. Since Xavier became CEO at Third Dawning, not a single Aussie has mowed down PCW fans resulting in their deaths. The “0” in that column that all Feds strive for has been achieved. And the fans are really important to Xavier Kannon, and their safety is imperative. After all, the money doesn’t grow on trees. “If the fans did, it’d stop me having to try and be nice to the Peons we have now.” So take time to think. With Alliances around the corner, and names such as Ivan Stanislav and Tyler Nelson returning to PCW, is Kannon really that bad? I mean, he stopped short of making Ellie~! co-Champion at least. Given all that’s gone down, he really isn’t that bad a person. “You can really make anyone sound innocent when the wording is played around with.” Then again, Kannon isn’t the one trying to justify himself. He just doesn’t care. And given the choice, he’d probably do more to make fans hate him. “Now I can’t plead innocent to that one.” But all that is in the past, what goes down at Alliances will be his fault, his doing, his plan. “My fault?” Yes. And there’ll be no passing the blame on. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
