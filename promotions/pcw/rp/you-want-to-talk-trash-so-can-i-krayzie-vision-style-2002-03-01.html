You want to talk trash? So can I..... KRAYZIE VISION style!

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 With the weight of the world placed upon his shoulders, or so it would seem at times for Jeremy Howard, how can he be mentally prepared to face Chet King for the Continental title? The Krazed Lunatic has only a few more hours to prepare himself. But, is he mentally ready for this task? This is Chet King. He's facing the man that went toe to toe with the White Mexican at Shanghaied and still retained his title. Not only does the Krayzie One have to deal with fighting a man as talented as Chet King, but he's also got to worry about Drake Maxwell in the back of his mind. This sick freak would love nothing more than to screw the Krazed Lunatic out of the Continental title just to get inside of his head. But, if Drake Maxwell screws Jeremy out of the Continental title, then he loses his shot at it at March Madness. Just what card is the Fallen Angel going to play before the pay per view? If Drake Maxwell decides to play it, the Krayzie One is going to be ready. That's a message that Drake Maxwell should heed as the scene fades into a dimly lit stage. This scene looks very familiar, although it lacks the usual audience cheering 'Krayzie', an oak desk and a plush blue couch. Its just a stage with a sign hanging above it. The sign, glowing a blue hue, speaks volumes. This is 'Krayzie Vision'. This is the show where Jeremy Howard speaks his mind and holds nothing back! Jeremy: It's become increasingly clear to me that a chosen few individuals do not have a f^ckin clue as to who I really am..... Let me some it up for you people out there that lack brains. I am Jeremy 'Krayzie' Howard...... I am a former two-time World Champion that is not going to back down, even though the masses of today have written me off. Jeremy pauses, obviously seething with anger. Jeremy: Some of you, and there's a large number of you, have no clue what it's like to wake up the day right after being screwed out of the World title, not knowing what the future holds for you now that the one thing you carried with pride has been ripped away from you. You have no idea! Jeremy paces the floor of the stage. There's nothing else on it. Just the Krayzie One. The Krayzie One and all of his anger that continues to build as he keeps talking. Jeremy: As I laid there in my bed back in Elkhart, I thought to myself, what have I yet to accomplish? That's right. That's when the idea to complete the title sweep began. But, it was someone far better than I, that remind me of another task I had yet to accomplish..... I have yet to gain the full freedom of my brother. A very damaging fact to the Krayzie One. On Christmas day, he fought the match of a lifetime, in what he thought was for the freedom of his brother from prison. Jeremy won the rights to Justin's freedom. But, things would take a turn for the worse when Jeremy would learn his brother had 'left' with a dark figure. Only days later, Jeremy would learn that this dark figure would turn out to be Drake Maxwell..... the man Jeremy faces at March Madness. Jeremy: So now, I have my own quest to win the title sweep in PCW in my wrestling career, I have my personal life quest to gain the freedom of my brother. Now, add to that, my good friend Duke Williams is getting his ass handed to him by two of the biggest low lifes in PCW history, being Tyler Nelson and Kid Wonder. What do I do? I come to help my friend like the true friend that I am! Jeremy stops, trying to calm himself down, to no avail. Jeremy: This means, what was once an empty plate of nothing to do, is now my strings being pulled in three different ways. Three different ways! I, myself, had no problem with this. I have taken on alot of responsibilities before and handled them perfectly. But, others didn't see it that way. Jeremy pauses, pacing the floor. A glass of water sits off to the side of the stage, almost out of view. Jeremy stops before it, picking it up. The Krayzie One takes a sip before returning back to his tirade. Jeremy: No. With my fall from grace, I've seeminly become a fading star, huh? A man busts his ass night in and night out, and you all hate on him. This just goes to show the lack of brains of you haters. I willfully demoted myself to fighting for the Continental title, because that's what I wanted! But, not because of my own arrogance and wants. Because I wanted to see guys like Xavier Kannon, Darkstar, Miyagawa and Bobby Stormbringer move up to where I've been for quite some time! Jeremy stops, letting that thought sink in. As the seconds pass, the anger seething inside of him takes prominence on his face. With Drake Maxwell's mind games, Kid Wonder's verbal beratings and the constant backstage views on Krayzie's current status, its getting to a man that looks out not only for himself, but for the very others that look out for him. Asylum. Jeremy: I did this for them. I don't care what kind of garbage Kid Wonder tries to dig up from my history, I went out of my way for the Asylum brothers that have gone out of there way for me! And, speaking of Kid Wonder. Where are you now? Did digging up my history spark some fear in you? I can understand why. You probably come across the tape of what I did you that fateful Fusion. Need a reminder? Whether Kid Wonder wants to be reminded or not, he's going to get it. For, the Krayzie One makes a motion toward the side of the staging area. Moments later, the very same car that Jeremy Howard put Kid Wonder's head through with a death valley drive is pushed out onto the stage. Jeremy, not smiling at all, walks over to the driver's side door of it, staring at the window. Jeremy: You came across this, didn't you? You continuously talk about your greatness and how much of a legend you are. Well, imagine that. I can add another accolade to my list. You do remember how I put you out of wrestling that night? You remember just how Krayzie I really am, now? You should. For, I'm sick of hearing you each and every night, come out and proclaim something you aren't! If you were so d#mn great, then why haven't you did anything other than win the Cruiserweight and TV titles?!?! A good question. Recently, since his alignment with Tyler Nelson, Kid Wonder has taken on a cocky attitude that includes proclaiming just how great he is. Yet, if he was so great, why has Kid Wonder dropped the proverbial ball almost as much as Howard Porter has? Jeremy: I'll tell you why! Because while my stock might've rose and is on the downfall according to some of these brainless jackasses around here, yours never went anywhere just like you! I tell you what, after I get done with Maxwell and his shenanigans, we're going to settle this once and for all! Once again, Jeremy pauses, tryin to regain his composure. That is, because another man needs Jeremy's attention. A man, that while is still fairly new to the PCW scene, has become quite the thorn in the side of the Krayzie One since the first time they wrestled a week after Jeremy won the World title for the second time at Decimation. Jeremy: This brings me to Drake Maxwell. A man this probably more critical of me than any other. Which, I don't get. You, much like Kid Wonder, haven't done a d#mn thing since you arrived here. You've lost title shot after title shot. H#ll, even worse than my aforementioned enemy. And now, you hold my brother like he's some kind of championship going into March Madness. Is this how you get your kicks? By attacking people mentally?..... I sure hope this is not the case..... Because I'm as mental as they get! Jeremy points upwards, making a motion with his hands for something to lower around him and the car he had brought out. It takes only a few seconds before a massive hell in the cell cage begins to lower around Jeremy and the car. Jeremy: This look familiar Drake? Come this Sunday, when we you step into the ring with, this'll be the background. Neither man leaves, until the other man is dead. I know your sick ass is going to try something. I just know it. But, shall I remind you of our match on New Year's day? You sent down Vampir Nosferatu to help you score the win and my World title. What happened Drake? Suddenly, behind where Jeremy stands next to the car within the confines of the cage, a picture begins to roll. On this particular picture, Duke Williams can be seen taking off his headset and leaving the announcers table and rush into the ring. Vampir Nosferatu has just hit a power move on the Krayzie One and is about to place Drake Maxwell over Jeremy's unconscious carcass when the Malice Man hits the ring. Jeremy: That's right Drake! Your plan was thwarted because of my friendship with Duke Williams. But, this time around, Duke is busy. So, whatever your plan is this time, do you think I'd come unprepared? Did you really think I'd just let this go? H#ll f^cking no! This is for my brother's freedom! Try whatever you want. I'll be expecting the unexpected. But just remember..... You will meet a psychotic ending! Jeremy steps away from the car, stepping up to the cage. He reaches out and grabs some of the caging, shaking it a little in a sort of a test. He stops. The camera zooms in on his face. His anger is still evident, but the tone in his voice changes dramatically. Jeremy: This brings me to the last man I want to address..... Chet King.... I have no reason to badmouth you Chet or what you've accomplished. I just want to say... May the best man win..... But, I'll warn you..... My name isn't Kid Wonder.... I don't drop the ball for anyone! The camera stays locked on Jeremy's face, showing his obvious seriousness. In a mere hour or two, the Krayzie One fights for the Continental title. Can this man come one step closer to completing the ultimate goal in PCW history? Tune into Fusion to find out as the scene fades.... to black!
