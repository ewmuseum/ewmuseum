Date: not entered “Kill me!” “So, what do you think?” (In the secret lair of THE Multiple Man, a crisis is brewing like a fresh pot of coffee…) “Mmm… I don’t know. He might just want company to suffer with. He’s sneaky like that.” “Damn you! Put me out of my misery!” (Members of the Entourage cluster around the open doorway to Tmm’s bedroom, all distinctly lacking that look that says ‘I give a damn.’) Val: “What’s he doing? I can’t see!” Keira: ”He’s just lying there, like he’s done for the past three days.” Steve: “Serves him right for watching Frampton promos without a safety rope. Now we can’t even pull him out.” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]!” (Raul boldly shoves them to the side and strides into the room. He’s not three steps in before a dawning look of horror crosses his features, like he’s just swallowed a slow acting poison that’s eating his insides organ by organ.) Raul: “Errk!” (He drops to his knees, then crawls, then collapses utterly. All in five steps.) Steve: (shaking head sadly) “Poor brave bastard.” Keira: “Dumber than a bag of hammers, but you can’t doubt his courage.” Tmm: “Just use the damn gun! Two, no! Three to the head! Glock! Glock! Glock!” Steve: “Seems like a waste of bullets to me.” Keira: “You’d think he’d learn. Watching other people’s garbage is a waste of time. It just kills whatever mockery of a workrate he has.” Steve: “I don’t see why WE have to pay for his mistakes. It should have been painfully obvious that Frampton was a vast pit of suck to begin with. You don’t need to have to kiss the snake to find out if it’s poisonous or not.” Keira: “Parasites have a vested interest in keeping their host alive. If we shoot him, he can’t rake in the bucks for us. If we leave him there, he’ll be fired for no-showing, again no money for us.” Steve: “I prefer to think of myself as more of a pilot fish than a parasite.” Keira: “Pilot fish?” Val: “It’s this little fish that hangs around sharks and scoops up the leftovers.” Tmm: “For God’s sake! Smother me with a pillow! No? FINE! I’ll just lick the power outlet then!” Val: “You think he’s up to harming himself?” (Keira snorts.) Keira: “Feh, as if.” Steve: “Yeah, really. He’s too lazy to bother, even without his will drained by Frampton’s horrificness.” Val: “You sure?” Tmm: “Umm… could somebody roll me over to that outlet over there?” Keira: “Pretty sure.” Val: “Think we could use one of the decontamination suits?” (HV trudges up in one of the afore mentioned decon suits.) Steve: “What would be the point? Even if we stopped up the ears, whomever that did it would still have to see out. One glimpse of Frampton would be almost as bad as hearing him, no matter that skin exposure would be eliminated.” (HV’s shoulders slump in defeat.) Val: “Are you implying that there’s some kind of Frampton radiation at work here?” Keira: “Work is such a strong word, don’t you think?” Steve: “I’m just saying, it would be very hard to counter the sheer… blahness… that Frampton generates.” Val: “Well, we could strap fine literature across the faceplate and guide them with radio, I suppose.” Keira: “When was the last time you saw fine literature HERE?” Val: “Hmm… comic book?” Keira: “Oh, there’s millions of THOSE. Steve, I know you have stacks of the things, get one.” Steve: “Ermm… I’d rather not.” Keira: “Oh, it’s THAT kind of comic, is it?” Steve: “No! Not that, it’s just…” Keira: “Get the damned book. The human slug’s got a match tonight, and we need him up NOW.” Steve: “Oh all right…” (Still grumbling, Steve stomps off, soon returning with a brightly bound folio.) Keira: (eyeing Steve strangely) “Sailor Moon?” Steve: “What?! It’s a collectors item!” Keira: “I’d say I was worried about your masculinity, but that would imply a level of concern for you that I’m not comfortable with…” Steve: (emphatically) “Collectors item! And mind where you put that tape, I’ll want it off after!” (Safely blinded by… ahem… Sailor Moon, and all outside sounds completely filtered out by his helmet, HV takes his first cautious steps into the room as the camera fades out.)
