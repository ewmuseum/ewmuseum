Mute

26 March 2003

Date: 26/03/03 So, she thought to herself, I’m not the only one with a hidden agenda. To Lacuna’s mild surprise (she didn’t like surprise, so she mostly ignored the emotion) she had noticed that Jade had become much more focused on getting her club back from that idiot Mani. Both times that they had met had ended in a major ass kicking of Mani, and Lacuna could see another in the imminent future. But she had her own issues. She ran a hand through her pitch-black hair and sighed loudly, letting the cold air fill her lungs with a sharp stabbing feeling. Above her, the night sky twinkled, or more specifically the sky perforated by the tiny stars twinkled. It confused her that she would glare into the endless abyss, constantly picking out faults with her own definitions. She was a perfectionist of the highest degree, and if she didn’t do it then her mind would probably invent some other little fascination for her obsessive personality. The neatly trimmed green grass below her was beginning to gather dew, and it was starting to soak through to her naturally tanned skin. Hours had passed since she had last moved, and she cared not for the idea of moving any time soon. Her mind was clouded, unlike the dark sky, and it needed a deep pattern of thought to demist it. There were too many things happening at once. She was a simple little girl at heart: one of morals and class. On one side, Jade was dragging her down through the class system into a land of fun she barely new existed. Despite how annoying Jade could be, her tag team partner had been most useful in exhuming a part of Lacuna she barely knew herself into one she wished she was more acquainted with. It was an interesting idea that she didn’t fully understand herself, but that was the way of the world I suppose. But now she had lost the subject, and her mind was drifting back into a familiar pattern of self-examination deep within her head. She would analyse and cross analyse and what for? She didn’t even know the answer to that. This was her second major issue. With Jade around, she had decided that she didn’t even know who she was. Things were changing around her, and it was all going too quickly for her to keep track of. Lacuna was a creature of habit, and didn’t like the changes she was witnessing. She didn’t mind wars or the looming threat of global warming. Poverty didn’t matter to her. All she knew was that she didn’t like the direction she was taking, and the rest of the world could bloody well go and swivel for all she cared. This was genuine Lacuna, or so she told herself. But what was Lacuna now? Had she abandoned all 21 of her previous years just so she could fit in with the ‘lower classes’? It confused her to think too deeply of it, and so she abandoned all track of thought. It was bringing up old memories, and ones she had thought she had laid to rest a long time ago. It was reminding her of the confusion she felt way back when she was young. When her parents died. She blinked quickly and flung her head to the side, hitting it against the wet grass. She didn’t want to be thinking about that now of all times, it was too deep; too intense and would take up all her space for other topics. She knew what would happen because it had happened so many times before. She would start with it, taking the usual route of moping around the house for a while, completely absorbed in thought until she became one hundred percent self absorbed, completely unaware of human activity around her. She would wind up so self absorbed that nothing would matter to her. So much so that she would ignore every aspect of human life. And the scary thing is that this has all happened before. She closed her eyes and let her thoughts change direction, back to that which she felt she could reasonably control. She was quite happy with the results of Fusion, not just that she had finally managed to put a little damage to the relationship of Storm and Windsor, and add a little payback for the early days, but she had been noticed as a contender on the tag circuit. Yeah, she knew about the shot at the titles, but that was an insecure device on to which you could try and hang your beliefs. The fact that the three top teams had featured in the same battle, despite the Significant Others being destroyed, had to be a good thing for the team of Lacuna and Jade. Sounds funny don’t it? She was happy that they took a beating. The reason she was happy was even stranger. She didn’t care that they were destroyed just because the exact same thing happened to those damned Schmoopies. She still hated Storm for their prior match and she still wanted to grind his face into the canvas like the end of a cigarette gets ground into tarmac. She still held him responsible for the beginning of the end of the first teaming of Lacuna and Jade. Teaching him a lesson was all she really cared about. Tessa could go and play with her teddy for all she cared, because she was just an accessory to the crime. She had begun her own little war of that team, her own personal argument that had begun, and would probably end, within her head. Facing, and eventually beating, Tessa was just collateral damage: she was in the way at the wrong time. But then something had changed inside Lacuna, and she decided to exploit their bond like some cheap little tool. She could hurt one and get at the other, and she was doing so. They hadn’t lost the match, and that had to be at least a minor plus. They had given the previous PTC upstarts a run for their money and could well have pulled off the victory in due time. There was more interference and both teams ended up getting destroyed. She was sensing a pattern and didn’t wholly like it much. But, on the same point mentioned earlier, The Schmoopies had taken the brunt of the beating and she had enjoyed this greatly, if she wasn’t unconscious for most of it. She had also managed to mix it with the supposed ‘best’ PCW tag team of all time. They may have won the belt four times but they had also lost it four, and that does nothing but point out their humanity (even if one is a bloody vampire). Jade had been relatively useful in this process. This had led to her final mindful problem. Jade was friends with Storm. Something had to give, and it would be one of them, if not both because Lacuna gets what she wants, even if it does only involve bossing around Yanks in their own country. This would go her way or no way.
