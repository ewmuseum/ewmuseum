The Past, The Present, and Fidel

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/11/02 Time: 3: 46 P.M Location: New York CityScene: Apartment “ The Past, The Present, and Fidel” The Scene opens with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore packing their bags for a little International trip. The Golden Game is going on a business trip to Cuba, to discuss their International Incident campaign. Both Kevin and Juan decided to go, knowing they needed a little time to strategize for there tag team title match on Friday. Juan knows it’s going to be hard to leave Juliet right when the two are in the middle of rekindling their love. Juan wishes Juliet could come along, but it may not be the safest thing, plus Juliet deserves some time to relax after all of the things she’s done for Juan. When Juan is packing his bag, Kevin begins to viciously laugh. Juan doesn’t know what’s so funny, and pushes Kevin in the arm. Kevin tries to calm down before he speaks, but he keeps breaking out in laughter over and over. Juan: What’s so funny? Kevin: Nothing, it’s just one of those times where you think of something funny from the past. You’re swimming trucks your packing reminded me a funny memory. Juan: What memory is that. The smirk appears on Kevin’s face again before he begins telling the story. He lets out hi laughter and begins. Kevin: Remember that time where……. (The scene goes to the past where Juan and Kevin were both bachelors and used to hang out in the beach, when still in the PWA.) Kevin: This so rocks how we don’t have to go to the academy this week. We can go and get a sweet house around this beach, and chill with the ladies everyday. How does that sound to you Juan? Juan: Woman are very good. I like woman. Anything with woman, Juan is surely to like. As you would say chicks are gnarly. Kevin: Totally Juan man. It’s nice to know you Portuguese dudes still dig the ladies. Just wait till I get one of them to settle down and check me out in the ring. They’ll totally dig my big muscles and fighting skills. Juan: What can I say, woman love the muscle, me right? Kevin: Yea, you’re right, about that one let’s go. The two guys go running through the beach and take a swim in the water. Everything is all in good fun, until Kevin spots some fine ladies all alone. Kevin: Hey Kevin, check out those chicks at 5 o’clock. Juan: Oh, what time is it now. Kevin: No, I mean dead a head, but a little to the left. We say five o’clock, because t’s the way the hour arrow points. (Juan scratches his head.) Juan: Oh me see. The two men go running out of the water and approach the ladies. The odd thing is the second the two men run up to the two ladies they see, they go running in the other direction. Kevin: What was all of that about? Hey Juan, is there any food or anything on my face to make me look ugly. Juan looks at Kevin and shrugs. Kevin thinks for a minute or two and then looks over at Juan and notices he’s wearing WHITE swimming trunks that you can see through when they’re wet. Kevin: Juan! Are you crazy, you’re wearing white trunks. (Juan shrugs, as another two ladies go walking by, and whistle at Juan. Juan turns around and winks to the ladies.) Kevin: Juan that was incredible. Your white trunks rule! The ladies dig them look how many of them are looking at you. The two men both purchased white trunks that week, and spent the whole week at the beach. (The scene goes back to the present.) Juan: Ah! Those were the days. That week was probably one of the greatest weeks of my life. I couldn’t even speak English back then. I sounded like a total immigrant. Oh man, I can’t believe I used to be that stupid. Kevin: Don’t worry, you still sound like a total immigrant, it’ll never change. Juan: Shut up. Juan grabs a pillow and throws it at Kevin. Kevin grabs it, and goes chasing Juan around the room. Juan goes running all over with his quick speed, as Kevin trips over a bedpost and falls to the ground. Kevin: Ahhh! My F*CKIN TOE. Juan: See something like that would never happen to me, because I have a golden toe, not a flesh one. (Kevin squints in pain as he speaks.) Kevin: I thought you had a golden heart, Juan. Doesn’t that mean your toe is no longer a golden toe. Juan: Well you see I have a golden toe and a golden heart. Just because I’m the golden heart now, doesn’t mean I’ve lost the golden toe. (Kevin winds up and punches Juan straight in the chest. Juan screams in pain, as he throws his hands in the air in anger.) Juan: What was that for? Kevin: I though you had a golden heart, but I guess you don’t. You’re just the same old not golden feminine man you’ve always been. Juan jumps on Kevin’s back and puts him in a headlock. The two throw each other around in a jokingly manner until they are out of breath. Juan: Who’s the feminine one now? Kevin: You are! Kevin and Juan go back to packing again, and Kevin finds his old overalls and tries to hide them real quickly. Juan: Oh don’t you think I didn’t see that one. Take those right back out, I can’t believe the memories behind those overalls. (Scene goes back to the past where Kevin was wrestling in one of his first matches he’s ever had.) Kevin: Hey Juan, you better look carefully, I’ll show you a few pointers in the ring, but first I have to go get ready. Juan: okay. Kevin goes to get read and comes out wearing a huge pair of sparkled violet overalls, with a silver trim. Juan gives Kevin a weird look, and Kevin shrugs. Kevin: What are you looking at? Juan: I’m sorry, but those overalls you’re wearing seem a little, how do you say, fruity. Any reason you’re wearing them. Kevin: What are you talking about, I asked a guy in the store Too Hot, and he said it looked great on me. Kevin goes out to get in the ring and Juan begins to think. Juan: Isn’t that a gay shop. At the same time Juan speaks Kevin goes running out of the ring to the tune of the audience laughing. (Flashback ends.) Juan: I’ll never forget how stupid you looked that day. Kevin: And I’ll never forget how stupid you look everyday. The two share a laugh until Kevin speaks again. Kevin: Well we better get going we have a plane to catch. And we don’t want to miss our chance of having cigar’s with Castro, do we? Juan: Hell no. The scene fades with the two guys going off and making there way to the airport, where they’ll be getting ready for there meeting with Castro. TO BE CONTINUED……………………………………
