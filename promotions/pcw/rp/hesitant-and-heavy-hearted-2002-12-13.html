Hesitant and Heavy-Hearted

13 December 2002

Title: Hesitant and Heavy-Hearted Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Dec. 13, 2002 Why bother....It was just a useless Fusion match, there was nothing to be gained. I looked out the window feeling rather lower than usual, maybe it was just the afterglow of the buzz from Adrenaline...It was harder to feel much better than those fleeting moments, destroying the shape with every blow struck with the chair, soaring through the air to deliver the cracking impact, preventing medical attention... All with her at my side.....This was my moment, I felt the best in a while, everything was going right... Until he got up. There was no reason, he shouldn't have been able to struggle to his feet that quickly...It was then that I knew what I was in for...Did I? I had him to deal with come Decimation, come the toughest match yet...He was out for vengeance, and that in itself would've been bothersome for me, but I'd made the very error I feared...I grew attached, too much so, maybe she'd noticed it, maybe that's why we didn't seem to talk as much...I was too obvious, I would've attracted the anger of Nathan once more inevitably, maybe it was best that I stayed out of the way for a while...The pain of separation carried me to my current state, vulnerable enough to sit here at my hotel room's table, cradling the Cruiserweight belt in my arms, hoping not to have to deal with the bloody Mullet on Adrenaline until I felt just a little better... I needed confidence, which was odd, seeing how I'd never really had such trouble before, there was so much I'd done...I beat everyone in the World Title hunt recently outside of Xavier at Alliances, I had beaten the Trapezoid on so many occasions and could've been a double champion if not for him...I could've been Cruisernental if I'd made it onto the damn flight, too..I would've been a triple champion if circumstances weren't in my way, maybe...There were always circumstances that got in people's way, no matter how much they tried to avoid them...Maybe the circumstances weren't important right now. The fact was that I had that annoying little tax-evading pest to deal with at this point, the RUSH rebellion personified in the frame of a 157-pound man...This didn't mean I could let my guard down. Syko was smaller than him, and he was Continental Champion for a time, Boca was a weakling and I let him get away with too much in our match, Tomas had flustered me on many an occasion...But none of them had beaten me. The smaller they were, the further they bounced off the mat when I caught them with Penaltyplexes, the easier it resulted for me to finish them off... I was a technical master, I knew how to deal with folks like them. I could win this match and retain the title...It meant I was the best Cruiserweight in PCW, and I wasn't in the mood to change that any time soon...But I wasn't in much of a position to argue it with myself, I'd have another 11 days or so before it really mattered, I only had the Mullet and one of those darn losers that I'd beat before coming out of Fusion to bother with...Then, it was going to be my time. My chance to be the man of the hour, to fend off the Trapezoid as well as I possibly could, to break what was left of his spirit against me, prove who was the better man... ....My chance to get the living crap beaten out of me. This wasn't just an average wrestling match, this was technical hell...A match that gave the best of both worlds, my mild technical advantage that had given me the recent wins in comparison to his hardcore nature, that which had given him so many Extreme Title opportunities. I knew full well that a chair could do greater damage in shorter time than any hold I put him in....I knew full well the magnitude of the damage he wanted to cause... I remembered what was to come Friday once more...8-man tag match, not worth anything to win unless I made the victory...Become the man who should've been champ. Mason'd already done enough to piss me off the week before that I was halfway interested in pummeling him as opposed to my opponents.....Except the Trapezoid. I could attack the arm, it'd keep him out through Decimation if I got him badly enough...I wouldn't have anything to be bothered with....But I wouldn't have a challenge either. I wouldn't be the man of the hour if I just wimped out and prevented him from getting his opportunity, just 'the coward lackey'. I couldn't let him get off easy, but I couldn't hurt him enough to stop him from fighting, lest I just be seen as anything less than I was... ...I wouldn't have to wait too long to try to break him anyway. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
