Interview with the Devil: The Voices (Part 6) RP #1 for Turmoil

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-24-03 All of this stuff happens so quickly in a life and you can’t really understand what the real problem is, even when it comes crashing down on you and literally smacks you right between the eyes. The worst thing that comes out of it all is the paranoia. Something that leaves you so worried about life and keeps you wondering what is really coming around that next corner. Anything could decide to be on the other side of that curb, or in Shane’s case, any kind of trap that Satan has left open for him could be right there. Does he take the chance and make the walk or does he let his fear conquer him? This is one of those days where Shane had to make that decision. Out on a peaceful walk; wait did I forget something? There isn’t a peaceful anything for Shane so I guess you could say it’s an unusual walk. Shane looks very ruffled and hasn’t had a good shave in a few days. His goatee sticking out amongst the rest of the stubble on his face and his clothes look as if he hasn’t changed in several days. Leading us to believe that Shane himself probably hasn’t taken a bath in numerous days, or the guy is just a psycho who doesn’t change into other clothes, but either way you’d still call him a psycho at this point in time. As Shane passes people walking down the street, they all seem to turn and stare at him. Shane begins to grow even more paranoid as he begins to become aware of this, suddenly stopping as he comes to the curb. He stands for a second, closing his eyes, and letting out a deep breath. He turns and noone is around. The once crowded sidewalk is empty and the streets are clear, nothing covers them, only the sound of a single newspaper rolling across the street in the wind is heard. Shane brings his hands to his face as the sweat begins to roll from his brow, “What is this madness?” This question leaves the tips of Shane’s lips and he looks up to the heavens as a tear begins to roll out of the corner of his eye, “Lord, what is this madness?” Suddenly the skies above turn from blue to red; a red so dark is resembles blood. Shane then turns his attention to the streets once more as a loud sinister snickering laugh is heard. The newspaper that was making its way across the street then lands at Shane’s feet and the title of the paper reads: “In A World Full Of Sin, Why Not Try?” Shane reaches down and with a shout he begins to furiously rip the paper into pieces, “Satan!” Shane begins to scream at the top of his lungs, “I’m sick of these games! If you’re going to toy with me…” Shane pauses for a moment as he begins to regain his composure, “You know what?” Shane releases the grip his has on his own palms and lifts his head to look out in front of him, “I’m not going to let you play these games with me,” Shane begins to snicker a bit himself and then he release a monstrous roar, “SATAN I REBUKE YOU!” In a sudden flash of light, everything goes back to normal and all of the people that were once walking the sidewalk are with us again. The cars driving along the street are once again in the street, but now all of the people that are passing really are watching Shane. Some of them confused as to what is going on, while others begin to mock and poke at him. All the while, the people begin to laugh at him very heavily; and Shane realizes what has happened as he continues to walk along even more paranoid than before and a blush of red almost comes across his cheeks from the embarrassment caused by his scene. A voice comes flowing in from a very close distance as Shane continues to walk, “See, I told you Shane. You are weak.” Shane continues walking, almost ignoring the voice, but where is it coming from? “You’re nothing without me Shane and I’ve already shown you how much I can do to you.” The voice continues to belt down on Shane growing closer with every breath. Shane begins to mumble under his own breath as to not bring attention to himself, “Go away! I’ve already told you that you have no power in my life. What more do I have to do?” A loud snickering begins to ring through Shane ears, just as before. The sound almost deafening; Shane turns and cannot find where the sound may be coming from, so he does the only thing he can think of and jumps into a taxi. Once in the taxi Shane releases a long sigh, as the madness could have possibly ended. ~Series To Continue~
