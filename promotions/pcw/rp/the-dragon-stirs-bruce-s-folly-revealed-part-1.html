The Dragon Stirs: Bruce's Folly Revealed (Part 1)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15-16 of 2003 A.D. The bowels of illimitable chaos stretched eternally through three-dimensional space, black currents of matter shifting in perpetual disharmony. Between the rolling black tides rested a lone fog-ridden sphere, cold and barren of life, cast with a beacon of a faint, phantom radiance. Saturated with those elements both composing and sustaining life, this gloomy realm acted as a staging ground for the torture of enemies of the Old Ones’ more nettlesome foes where servitors and the gods themselves would render their enemies with agony and madness. It was also within this remote limbo that Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan regularly met through spiritual incarnation with his dark rulers, those deities of such vast power and dominion that they existed as the embodiment of cosmic principles and laws; omnipotent beings exhibiting the cunning and intelligence of the ordered universe. Where primordial chaos melded into the continuum of merging time and space, basking in the madness of primal infinity, currents of tangible darkness drifted between the conscious dimensions and through unhallowed gulfs where light was taboo and sanity was an element verboten. It was here that Shanahan’s stooped outline wavered in incorporeal form before slowly materializing into flesh made physical, awaiting his conference with the soul and messenger of the Outer Gods, Nyarlathotep. He drew himself up, slowly gazing in a clockwise pivot at his surroundings. A wall of sound bore down on him from the ever-shifting darkness that encompassed the secluded rift in space, its rumbling tone like that of an approaching stampede upon the prairie and reflecting the incalculable stirring of matter infinite. His head bowed in sincerest of reverence, from his lips uttering forth those accursed of syllables. “Ph-nglui mglw-nafh Cthulhu R’lyeh wgah’nagl fhtagn.” The tones echoed in that condensed void and beyond for minutes, reflecting back upon him as if answered by a droning choir of the dead and the damned. The Black Pharaoh had summoned him to this realm for his final instructions prior to the March Madness event. Exactly what the night held for the servant of the black gospel was yet unknown, but the one known as Boston’s Bastard Son hoped the god could shed light on his March Madness fate. Between Skylar Green’s impending annihilation and the World title battle royal itself, it would prove to be a busy evening for the head of the Sect of Black Wisdom. Shanahan became aware of a presence in the realm, foreign and unwelcome judging by its aura. Another abruptly popped up nearby the original, and he grunted. “I do not know who you are,” he snorted angrily without turning to view the culprits. “But you are not welcome in this sacred forum. I assume you’re merely passing through, or you warped into this region by error. I urge you to depart, lest face the consequences of your insolence.” One of the duo let out an audible scoff, the voice of a male. “No error I fear. We are here not of our own accord, but for reasons much like your own. However, our dealings aren’t with your gods. They are with you, High Priest Bruce Shanahan. And we won’t leave until our objective has been seen through.” Flabbergasted at the gall of the still-unseen intruders, Shanahan turned to gaze on the infiltrators. One was a young girl of Asian descent, resting cross-legged and barefoot on a cloud of mist, casually licking at an ice-cream cone. The other, a male of similar lineage and similar approximate age, stood alongside her. Bruce sneered, enraged at the boy’s comments, as the youth began to amble away from the girl to stand alone. “Boy….how dare you defy me and the Old Ones?! You are uninvited for a reason. That is because you are unwanted. Be gone, children. I have no time for your games.” “Then so be it,” uttered the male coolly. “But this is about Skylar Green. And your foolishness in provoking him.” The girl’s voice rang out loud and clear to Shanahan, but not through conventional sound; her words flowed forth through a combination of mental images and what the cultist deemed as a telepathic form of speech. “This is the most horrible error you have made throughout all of your evil activities, Bruce. You have single-handedly wrought possible doom on all of the Earth, and maybe the universe as it is known.” Her words further enraged the already seething Shanahan, who surged forward at the serene youth as she rested on a cloud of luminous mist. Feinting astonishment, her eyes fluttered and she feebly recoiled. Bruce’s hands arced from opposing sides toward the adolescent’s skull. “AHHH!” His arms swept through the air horizontally as he released a gruff shout, watching his bulky arms cross again in mid-air where the girl had been only moments before. He blinked once and sneered, pivoting at the sensation of a surging rush of energy. A vortex of swirling multi-color light spun in a veritable cyclone of material energy, gradually traversing vertically downward to reform the tangible figure of the young girl. She appeared as calm as ever, and still lapping at her ice cream. “No force is needed here, High Priest Shanahan. We came here only wanting civil discussion.” “How gullible do you take me for, you snotty little bitch?” growled Bruce, his back to the girl and her telepathic banterings. “And barring that…..how the hell did you and your little friend get here? This is an isolated realm reserved for myself and the Black Pharaoh……” The girl drew herself erect, her pasty white fingers curled around the cone of ice cream in her right hand. The other hovered in the foggy background to Bruce’s left. “I don’t believe that is the main issue here…” “On the contrary, that is the issue here,” interjected the robed cult priest. “What’s important is who you are and your logic behind being here.” “No, wrong again….the real issue is the question of what prompted you to condemn our entire sphere of existence by lashing out randomly at Skylar Green?” Shanahan’s already contorted countenance twisted into a perverse half-smirk. It was opportunities like this that he relished; the children were playing games, but they were on his terms now. “There was nothing random about my targeting of Skylar Green. It was ordered of me.” “By whom?” Shanahan spun to face his accuser, the tranquil and subtly attractive Asian girl as she licked at a mound of ice cream sapphire in color. Her accusations still had not come in verbal tones, but seemed to reverberate in his mind as if by some telepathic rapport between the pair. “First things first, wench. Your identity and that of your friend. And your means of passage into this most hallowed of dimensions.” She seemed to sigh, though having yet to make a sound. The other came forward from out of the corner of Bruce’s eye, taking the side of the girl. “She is Rumiko, I am Kenshiro,” iterated the boy, resting a hand on the girl’s shoulder. “We are Celestial Oracles, stationed on earth to survey the activities of Celestials.” “I still fail to see your reasoning for breaching this sanctified domain,” spouted Bruce, a scathing demeanor etched across his face as he glared at the pair. “I know of Celestials, and I’ve come to learn Skylar Green is one of those impudent wannabe deities. So factoring that in…..it’s obvious your visit has something to do with displeasure about my appointed quarry.” The one called Kenshiro grinned. “And here you said you saw no reasoning behind our presence here. Amazing deduction, Sherlock.” “No, boy, you misinterpret me. I’m still oblivious to what business it is of two second-rate psychics if I decide to demolish that blind cretin.” Rumiko only sighed, but the tension between the temperamental Kenshiro and the hate-fueled Shanahan only escalated at the mad cultist’s snide comments. Faintly luminescent fog, begotten from a source unseen, continued to drift between the dwellers within the realm. Nyarlathotep had yet to appear, but Shanahan knew even now that the Chaos only lurked beyond the boundaries of detection. His awful presence, though veiled, permeated the small dominion. Shanahan had come to know it by now. He was being tested by his master. The Black Pharaoh was waiting....
