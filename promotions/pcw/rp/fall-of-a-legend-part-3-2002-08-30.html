Fall of a Legend...Part 3

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-30-2002 The shot opens to show an elevator door. The light above it lights up and the tell tale 'BING' is heard as the elevator starts to slowly slide open. Inside the car is a very haggard looking Venneman Pendragon. His hair, still wet from the rainstorm outside, is plastered to his head. His shirt of golden chain mail still glitters brightly, but his eyes show the dull gleam of one who totters on the brink of insanity. He steps wearily from the elevator and looks around. This floor is an oddity. Different trophies adorn the spacious hall, and long unused championship titles are displayed here and there. Pendragon walks through the hall, looking at memorabilia from days gone by in PCW. He smiles at the utter antiquity of it all, although in the grand scheme of things, he realizes that PCW is not very old at all... 'My friend Jon Kano wants me to believe that he is old school. He plays himself up as a bastion of both honor and tradition when in reality all he is is a lost man who wishes for happier times past. Old School. Honor. For myself, EVERYTHING I do is based upon my code of honor. A code long unused in the world. In one point of history, the Knight dominated both warfare and culture. The Knight was symbolic of strength and solidity. Now, the Knight stands for a time when heroes walked the earth. A time when glory and honor went hand in hand, and a victory over an enemy was well earned. The Knight stands for everything that used to be right with the world. And now, I am alone....' He walks to a bust of The Trashcan Man in the PCW hall of fame aisle. He looks at it, lifts it up and looks into the sightless eyes. He smiles wanly as he shakes his head and places the bust back on its stand. He chuckles to himself in amusement as he walks on... 'The PCW Hall of Fame. I guess these chaps are deserving of the immortality offered here. Truth be told though, the names mean absolutely nothing to me. They were here and gone before I ever arrived, so why should I care? I am here to build a new breed of immortality. I care not if I am ever placed beside these men who adorn yonder hall. I care not if the older wrestlers of the federation look at me as an upstart bent upon glory and personal conquest. Truth be told, I AM hell bent upon the gaining of glory. Glory that I attain now is glory gained for the past that everyone has forgotten. I fight for the grand times that were, and for the way that man should be once more, if only he had the heart...' He turns to go back to the elevator only to find that the door has closed and he has to wait on another car. He shakes his head as he presses the up button and he leans on the wall, waiting... WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING IN HERE? Pendragon turns to see a young security guard approaching him, club in hand. Pendragon smiles his most winning smile, but with his eyes the way they are, it doesn't come off quite the way he was hoping... Venneman: What can I do for you, lad? Guard: You can tell me what in the hell you are doing here...And you can tell me why you have that pig sticker there in your hand.... Venneman looks to his right hand, and yes indeed, he has drawn his blade. He looks from the young guard to the blade, and back to the guard... Venneman: I guess I am just raising my solitary lament for better days that seem to forever have left the earth. The guard cocks his head as if to say 'You freak!' and looks warily at the crazy giant of a man before him. He begins to speak a tad more tentatively this time... Guard: Why don't ya just tell me what you are doing in here? It is far past regular office hours, and I am sure that whatever business you have can wait until tomorrow... Pendragon throws back his head and barks a short, abrasive, laugh. He levels his gaze at the guard and speaks in his most commanding tone... Venneman: I am the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. Ye lack the right to question one such as I, when you lack even the modern amenity of a sidearm. That club will do ye little good against my blade should I prove to be hostile... The guard takes a fearful step back... 'I knew the fellow was scared, and with good right. He knew me not, although I am employed by the same company that writes his small checks. I hated to force my will upon the weak, but it had been a long day, and I needed to vent a little. And perhaps the chap would learn the ways of respect...' Venneman: Now...Where might I find the man whom they call Kannon? Where is his office? He must be informed of a serious oversight on behalf of the bookers of this company... The guard shakes his head, disbelieving that the armored giant would be here looking for Kannon... Guard: I do not think the boss would be willing to have a meeting with the likes of you, Lord King or not. You seem like another crazy bastard to me. My man Jon Kano visited a fellow that might be able to help you too...Of course, you might really need it. One of them head shrinks.... Pendragon scratches his head as he stares at the young man who evidently has a pair of brass ones, speaking so defiantly to a man obviously his greater in both strength and size... 'The lad had some stones, I would give him that. But why would Kano visit the head shrinks? I had dealings with them in the past, and those fellows were not to be trifled with, especially by a man with an attitude the likes of which Kano possesses.' *****FLASH****** The scene suddenly switches to a jungle shot. Pendragon and another fellow are tied to what would appear to be sacrificial altars. The man to Pendragon's left is a slight fellow and slightly balding. He is also quite terrified. Pendragon is cool, but the man lets out a scream when some darkly painted men emerge from the jungle. The man at the front wears a multi colored wooden mask and carries two things...In his right hand, he carries Excalibur, while in his left, he carries a tiny human head. The dreaded head shrinks of the Amazon Rain Forest. The leader of the primitives places the shrunken head on the chest of Pendragon's comrade and his fellows bring a woven basket into the clearing. They place the basket on the ground below the prone man's head and start dancing maniacally around the two tied up fellows... Pendragon: Stay calm, Jameson. I have a plan.... Jameson: PLAN??!! What KIND OF PLAN? We have to get out of here! These fellows are cannibals as well as head shrinks! Plus, they have your sword... Pendragon: Exactly...And I swear upon my life as a Knight that they shall not be permitted to use it. The only blood that shall be drawn with Excalibur today shall run from them... The leader raises the blade high and begins a monotone chant. He steps around to Jameson and the blade flashes down toward his throat. In a flash, Pendragon is loose and he claps his mighty hands on either side of the blade, stopping it in mid strike. He then jerks the blade free and slices the leader across the stomach. The man tries to grab himself, but he is too late. Intestines hit the ground approximately one split second before the screaming primitive. The rest of the group stops as Pendragon frees Jameson. The cannibals let out a blood curdling scream for their fallen leader and chase after the two white skins who already flee into the jungle... ***FLASH*** 'No, the head shrinks were not people I would ever wish to run into again. Ruthless in their pursuits, I barely crawled from that jungle with my life. And Kano chooses to see one of his own accord. And people have the nerve to call ME crazy...' Venneman: I believe I must pass upon that suggestion lad. Just tell me where I need to go to find Kannon. I am owed. I am owed my gold that the company took from me. I am owed vengeance upon the dogs who would mock the ways of honor and spit upon the path of glory and greatness. I am owed, and what am I paid? I am paid a match with a self proclaimed legend...Jon Kano... Pendragon shakes his head sadly, finally accepting the fact that perhaps everyone wanted to see him fall. At last, the elevator doors open and Pendragon shuffles in, not waiting for an answer from the guard. He presses the button for floor one, apparently giving up his hopes of a meeting with Kannon.... 'Finally, I realized that Kano was the best I was going to get. Kano, who spewed the names of people he had beaten in his less than stellar career at me as if I were to be impressed. Kano, who thinks my ego too great for the federation. Kano, a Legend in his own mind. What Kano failed to realize is that false Legends, like the men who make them up, do fall. True legends never die. They live on in the hearts of the storyteller and the heart of the very history of man. False legends, like prophets, are tossed aside like so much garbage. Kano believed himself to be a Legend. Claimed he had a reputation to uphold; a LEGENDARY reputation. The only thing he needed to uphold was his own words; words that would soon fall on deaf ears. The fans do not want his version of old school. What they want is a tad grander, a tad more noble. They want the fairy tale. They would want the Knight. That is what legends are made of...' The elevator door closes on Pendragon as his eyes light and he starts to laugh. Fortunately, we are spared the spectacle.... ~FINE~
