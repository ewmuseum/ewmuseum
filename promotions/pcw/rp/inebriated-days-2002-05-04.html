Inebriated Days

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-4-02 (We return to the previous location, the Cleveland HILTON~! where our title sweeping dictator is still relaxing and has just received his shipment of vodka...at only the price of a door, wall, and gunshot to one of his guard's arms. The hallway before the room is shown with people crowding around the obliterated wall and witnessing the Hall of Fame wrestler in his boxers on his bed with 4 guards holding AK-47s standing around him. Frisco, not embarrassed at all, waves to the people who chatter in confusion.) Frisco: 'Hello! Nice of you people to visit me?' (One of the women in the crowds screams.) Woman: 'Guns! They have guns! Oh my God!' (Frisco rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'Here's my gun, you skanked out cumguzzler.' (Frisco adjusts his Argentinean chorizo and winks. The woman gasps and runs away from the disturbing scene.) Frisco: 'Dios mio. I'm turning into Badge. Someone shoot me now!' (The guards turn around with their guns pointed at Frisco. The spectators gasp and scream.) Frisco: 'Hey! I was joking, you morons!' (Frisco calmly directs the guards to put down their guns. They do so.) Frisco: 'Never do that again. Never.' Guard #1: 'Sorry, Jefe.' Guard #3: 'Never again, boss.' Guard #4: 'Lo siento, El Mero Mero.' (Frisco shakes his head and peers at the people gathered around the smoking ruble.) Frisco: 'Why in the hell are you people still here? My guards shot up a wall. Whoopty-frickin'-doo! I bet you've all seen more interesting things than a demolished wall and the future president of Argentina half naked on a bed.' (An old, classy looking woman speaks up.) Lady: 'Before the War, I once saw president Roosevelt at the burlesque house with my friend Emma. He insisted on having his...testicles covered in maple syrup, feathers, and clothes pins and a rolling pin inserted in his behind.' (Frisco points and smiles.) Frisco: 'There ya go. Nothing too see here at all. I am a perfectly normal world leader in a perfectly normal situation.' (The wounded guard comes back with a wad of bloody toilet paper on his arm.) Frisco: 'Excuse me for a sec...' (Frisco holds his hand toward the curious crowd and looks over to his guard.) Frisco: 'Ah, tough guy! Crack open that crate and have at it.' Guard #2: 'You're letting me drink some this time!?' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'You're drinking most of it. It's your job today to get piss drunk and stumble around comically for my amusement.' (Frisco ponders his statement.) Frisco: 'Well, in all actuality, I'll get to see Duke Williams do that tonight, but screw it! You can do a better job, I think.' Guard #2: 'I'll do my best, El Mero Mero.' (Frisco looks back to the crowd.) Frisco: 'Would you like to stick around and watch this? If not, there will be nothing else of interest and you may go on with your business.' (Out of the dozen curious people, 2 young guys come in and sit down. The rest eventually walk off...including one of the hotel employees who was handed a wad of cash by Frisco.) Frisco: 'Excellent.' (Frisco looks at the obliterated door and nobody there to repair it.) Frisco: 'First, someone needs to find a solution to this. We can't very well have people snooping around my room.' (One of the guards gets up and picks the bullet-rittled door of the ground. He sets it against the doorway and wraps it in a sheet.) Frisco: 'Brilliant!' (Frisco claps three times.) Frisco: 'Now, you there, get drunk.' Guard #2: 'Si, Jefe.' (The guard kneels down and opens up the case of STOLICHNAYA~! He whips out a bottle and quickly opens it up. He starts to drink as the two young men cheer him on.) Young Men: 'Chug, chug, chug, chug, chug!' (Frisco smiles happily as the guard knocks back the bottle of quality liquor.) Frisco: 'You two! What are your names?' (The two young men, who appear to be college students, speak to the sloppy political figure.) Charlie: 'Uh, I'm Charlie.' Joey: 'I'm Joey. Hey, you rock, man. Thanks for letting us in.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Anything I can do to inspire the future of America. You gentlemen will be running the government when I am still smoothly running my own empire. You shall be my allies.' Joey: 'Actually, man, I'm gonna be a professional skater.' Charlie: 'And I'm gonna...uhh...I dunno yet.' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'Of course, of course.' Guard #2: 'Whooo! Finished that bitch!' (The guard drops the empty bottle and reaches for another one. Frisco smirks.) Frisco: 'Half of that vodka is on your clothes, you idiot. You have a ways to go.' Guard #2: 'Pssssshhhh! You dunowhatcher talkin' about!' (Frisco motions for a bottle.) Frisco: 'The hell I don't! Gimme a Stoli.' (The guard tosses Frisco a bottle. The pro drowns himself in delicious, intoxicating drink.) Joey: 'Can I have one?' Frisco: 'How old are you?' Joey: '21, sir!' Charlie: 'Yeah, me too.' (Frisco shrugs helplessly.) Frisco: 'Go for it.' Charlie: 'Alright!' (The two guys whip out a few bottles and begin to drink up. The remaining 3 guards look down and frown slightly.) Guard #1: 'Permission to speak, Jefe?' (Frisco nods as he sips on his STOLICHNAYA~!) Frisco: 'Permission granted.' Guard #1: 'Can we have some?' Frisco: 'No!' (The guard hangs his head.) Frisco: 'You job is to protect me. I cannot have all my guards stumbling over eachother and puking all over themselves. You can do that on your off time. You're getting paid right now for shooting any potential threat to my life, not to get drunk.' Guard #4: 'Pero, pero. Mira a Jose!' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Jose's job is to entertain me. Besides, he's injured thanks to you. He can't perform his duties 100%.' Guard #2: 'No, no, no. Yousee, my job is your job to my job...like a blowjob! Ahahaha!' (Frisco chuckles.) Frisco: 'You see what I mean? I only need one. The rest of you just keep watching out for people to shoot...' (The guards point their guns at the young guests.) Frisco: 'No! Stop! I already let those guys in. They're our guests.' (The guards sigh and put down their guns.) Guard #2: 'I fuggingot shot today, eh! Did yasee thastuff? Right in the fugginarm! BLAM! Hahahaha!' (The guard acts as if he got shot again and falls to the floor with his bottle of half-filled vodka.) Guard #2: 'AHHHHHHHH! I GOTFUGGIN SHOT BY YO MOMMA! KA-BANG!' (The two young men laugh as they drink their Stoli. Frisco giggles slightly.) Guard #2: 'FRISSSSCOOOOOOO!' (The guard gets up on his feet as he finishes the second bottle.) Guard #2: 'I love you. You're....the coolest, man. You're the coolest...' (The guard moves forward to hug Frisco, but Frisco keeps him back with his foot.) Frisco: 'Just keep stumbling around like a jackass.' Guard #2: 'Yesir! I shall...stumble around like a jack....ASS! Jackass, baby! Like the show. They go like thisonthere...' (The guard sprints toward the damaged, makeshift door and slams right into it. He breaks through the bullet-weakened wall and falls on the ground. The room breaks out in laughter.) Frisco: 'Ah, the man is a genius.' (Frisco sips his Stoli.) Frisco: 'Damn shame I can't do this everyday.' (Frisco whistles to the drunken guard. He springs back up and almost loses balance as he walks back into the room.) Frisco: 'Hey, cut a promo on Duke Williams for me.' Guard #2: 'Duke Williams?' (The guard cracks open another bottle and begins to chug. The young men continue to look on, merely sipping their own Stoli.) Frisco: 'Yeah.' Guard #2: 'Duke Williams is...yo momma, you stupid...Frisco face! And I fuggedher in the ass, yesserday! She was...like that one chick who you went to the thing with and then you wenta put it iner ass and she said...she said like you donwanna putid in my va-china and then I said yeah, but in your ass first, bitch. AHAHAHA!' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'I remember that. Go on.' Guard #2: 'So when Duke Willis and I went to that place...he tolme somethin'...but then we found her and she told Duke that he wasgonna kick your ass, so I told her to go kick Duke's ass because he's stupid and yeah.' (The guard takes another chug.) Guard #2: 'Then me anthagirl wenover to Duke and I drank a beer withim. He was pretty cool and then we both fugged those girls in their poop chutes.' Frisco: 'I thought you said there was one girl.' Guard #2: 'I ain't done yet!' Frisco: 'Heh. Please continue.' Guard #2: 'I ain't done yet, faggot!' (Frisco rolls his eyes. The guard takes a few more swigs.) Guard #2: 'So after Dupe and I wenta have sex with the...whores, we wenta the bar again. Then we kicked a whole buncha peoples' asses and me and then I stood on the bar and you wannaknow what I said?' Frisco: 'Su--..' Guard #2: 'I ain't fuggin done yet!' (Frisco smirks.) Guard #2: 'You wanna know?' Frisco: 'Ye--...' (The guard begins to cry.) Guard #2: 'Why do you hate me!?' (The guests snicker at the drunk.) Frisco: 'Because you're crying like a bitch instead of entertaining me.' (The guard snaps out of it and begins to speak.) Guard #2: 'Oh, alright then...so what I said when I got on top of the steps wasat I and me we was gonna kick Duke's ass and kick Williams' ass and then kick their asses like whenafter we kicked their asses. I did that promo thayou said tado. I toldto Douche Willis that I was gonna put him in the ring on Friday Fuchscia and I was gonna fugim in the ass in front of the whoooooollllllleeeeeeee audience and that they'd laugh at him and then Frisco was gonna come in and slap him like a girl. Then we'd enslave him likeyoufuggin said!' Frisco: 'Good work. Now do Sylo.' Guard #2: 'I told Sylo the same thing, but you know what I said? I said...what'd I say?' (Frisco shrugs.) Guard #2: 'I said to say ho that me and the ho were gonna go to Syblow and cut his penis off. Haha! Becauseyaknow, when we cut off Syblow's penis and me and the ho went to over there, we put his penis in the glass of water and we put the water where the water for that guy was and he drank the penis! Ahahaha!' (Frisco snickers.) Frisco: 'I see.' Guard #2: 'You donsee nothin'! Because I put Sylo in the sylum.' Frisco: 'You mean asylum.' Guard #2: 'No, I mean sylum, ya fuggin stup...id dumbhead. Sylum is that place in Vietnam where they put the penisless guys that I made. See, whenever a guy gets his wiener cut off, we go there to have sex with prostitutes, but the prostitutes are in the asylum where we put the guys, you know? So I kicked Sylo's ass and then I played ping pong with the Vietnamese prostitute who said 'me love you long time' in that movie...' Frisco: 'Full Metal Jacket.' Guard #2: 'I ain't cold, faggot!' Frisco: 'That was the name of the movie.' Guard #2: 'What movie? You don't know whatcher talkin' about.' (Frisco sips his Stoli and shrugs. The guard finishes off his 3rd bottle and can barely keep his balance.) Frisco: 'Are you sure there's only alcohol in those bottles? You're not holding your liquor well...at all.' (The rest of the guards giggle. Frisco looks over to them.) Frisco: 'Something to tell me?' Guard #1: 'No, sir.' (The guards whisper to eachother in Spanish. Frisco frowns.) Guard #2: 'Sylo's my momma and I put my fist in his fat ass! He died and then I got shot again by you fuggin assholes.' (The guard points all around the room as his pupils grow larger and larger.) Guard #2: 'All...6 of you. And that...giraffe in the corner.' (The guard takes one last swig of the possibly tainted vodka and falls over as he exclaims.) Guard #2: 'BALOGNA SANDWICHES ARE OUR MASTERS!!!!!!!!' (The guard begins to snore loudly as the vodka spills onto the carpet. The guards, guests, and Frisco giggle.) Frisco: 'Well, that was time well spent. I think I shall prepare for my match now.' (Frisco gets out of bed and steps over the wounded, drugged guard as he heads to the bathroom. The two college students poke at the guard's bullet wound as they laugh. The guards happily brag amongst themselves about getting their fellow worker. The scene switches to the outside of the hotel and fades out into the sky.)
