The Mightest Rar

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-03 The gold shined in the bright sun light, sparking its beauty for all to see. There it sat on its resting place atop the dresser. The sun was just rising, the beams hitting the gold through the window. The bright shimmer reflects off the gold and into the eyes of the beholder. With a loud roar he woke, rising from his slumber. Rubbing the sleep out of his eyes he looks across and smiles, the spark of the gold in his eyes. One foot moves in front of the others as he makes his way told the resting gold. He picks the gold up and holds it in front of his face, reading the words inscribed on it. “Tag Team World Champions” A smile crosses his lips. He takes the title and puts it around his waist. Turning to a mirror, he admires the way the gold looks around his waist. He flexes to the mirror showcasing his upper body. A call from the bed breaks his mental focus. “Well don’t you look sexy in your belt, Chris.” Came the purr from Tessa Windsor. Chris looks over toward the bed and sees Tessa lying under the covers, a smile on her face. Chris reaches over to the drawer where he grabbed his title and grabs her title. He tosses it to her. Tessa is caught off guard but manages to catch the title. She crawls out of bed, holding the title. Tessa is dressed in her blue teddy bear pajamas. Chris on the other hand is merely dressed in his boxers with the PCW Tag Team title around his waist. Chris turns to Tessa and takes the title from her and puts it around her waist. “Come on Tessa, we have to get started nice and early this morning.” He told her. “Get started on what?” she asked, still half asleep. Chris just grabs her hand and led her to the mirror. Chris stands in front of it and flexes his muscles, showing an angry expression on his face. “We need to get aggressive!” he tells her. Chris changes pose to flexes his back muscles. Tessa just stares at him confused at what is going on. “Aggressive?” she asked. Tessa grabs Chris to make him stop flexing. He looks at her wondering why she stopped him. “What are you talking about Chris? Aggressive for what?” Chris gives her a smile in touches her nose. “So we can keep our titles silly. We have more enemies then we have friends. So we need to start being more aggressive so we can take out our enemies instead of being the ones getting taken out.” He tells her. Chris turns back to the mirror and practices his game face. “I don’t know about you, but I’m sick and tired of getting leveled by chair shots each week. This week I want to be the one with the chair in my hands.” Tessa looked at Chris a little worried. Sure she saw his point, but she wasn’t exactly the most aggressive person around. She never was before after all. “Uh Chris, I’m not sure if I can be aggressive like you. I mean I’m not sure what I’m suppose to do.” She told him. Chris stopped in mid pose. He didn’t think about that. Chris turned to Tessa and rubbed his chin, thinking about the situation at hand. Just then an idea comes to him as his eyes light up. Chris grabs her by the hand and led her over to the bed. Chris gets her to sit down on the edge of the bed and stands in front of her. “Alright I will teach you!” boost Chris. He takes the title off and places it back on the dresser for safekeeping. “Now the key to being aggressive is a good game face. You have to be able to intimidate your opponent. Like this!” Chris shows Tessa his grin he normally does after laying a person out with a devastating chair shot. Tessa laughs and claps for Chris. “Alright now you try one. Show me your most intimidating face.” He says as he steps back to give her room. Tessa goes a few facial expressions, trying to be intimidating but doesn’t come off as anything other then funny. Chris tries to control his laughter. Finally he speaks up to keep from laughing too hard. “Ok let’s move on. How about a roar of might? Something that will strike fear in the hearts of your opponents. Try that.” Tessa looks around, a hint of red in her face. “What’s the matter Tessa?” Chris asks her. “Chris, it’s embarrassing.” She finally tells him. Chris looks a little surprised by Tessa’s reply. He laughs a little and sits down next to her on the bed. “Come on now, it’s just the two of us in here. Now give me your best growl.” He tells her. Tessa takes a deep breath and lets out her best growl. “RAR!” Chris falls backwards onto the bed laughing. Tessa looks at him, completely red in the face now. She slaps him on the chest. “It’s not funny Chris. I told you it was embarrassing.” She tells him, clearly upset. Chris finally controls his laughter and sits back up. He puts his arms around her and kisses her nose. “You know, you are cute when you are intimidating.” He tells her. Chris stands back up after he kisses her and walks over to the mirror. Tessa gets up and walks behind Chris, putting her arms around his waist. “Chris, how about we just go out to the ring on Friday and have fun? We’ll just do what we always do and wrestle. If we win then we win. If we don’t, well then there will always be another time.” Tessa tells him. Chris smiles and turns around. He hugs her close to him. Friday will come and Friday will go, but will The Schmoopies still be the Tag Team champions? I don’t know, but they don’t seem to worried to me. “You know something, you are right. I need to stop worrying so much. As long as you are by my side I know I have nothing to worry about.
