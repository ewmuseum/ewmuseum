The Mafia Series: Part One.

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 27, 2002. The scene fades into the kitchen of the Jackson Rust residence; definitely a better scene then the hospital, to say the least. Al the Cameraman stands in the center of the kitchen, looking about in shock, as Jackson Rust (himself) walks out from the other room, wearing a plush robe around his body. His hair is soaking wet, sure signs of he just took a shower…yuck. Jackson Rust: Let’s not go through what we did yesterday, huh? Women would die to be in the shower with me, alright? Narrator: *snickers* Jackson Rust: Very funny, just do your job, okay? I don’t need anymore psychological harm done to me like last night at the hospital. Between being threatened with a scalpel, to being slapped about the head with a glove, I think I’ve sustained enough. Al: I agree. Narrator: Shut up, and just do the usual morning promo, okay? Jackson Rust: Uh huh, whatever. Narrator: And…action. Al looks around confused for a moment, before shrugging, and looking back at what he was; which turns out to be a charred toaster, and microwave, combined with some cheap-ass silverware. Jackson Rust: Dude! See, you’re always starting trouble. Narrator: Am not. Jackson Rust: Whatever. As Al continues to stare in shock, and confusion, Jackson notices the telephone, hanging on the wall, with one unread message. Jackson curiously shrugs, before walking to the phone, and pressing the ‘Read Message’ button. “Jackson…it’s Tessa. I see that you aren’t home right now, but I’d appreciate a call from you. I know you’ll listen to me, so that’s why I called. Please call me back whenever you can.” A loud beeping sound emanates from the phone, before Jackson shuts it off. Al proceeds to look like an ass, staring at the blown up microwave/toaster combination. Al: Jack, what the hell happened here? Jackson turns to see Al, looking at the wreckage of charred metal. Jackson Rust: Oh…yeah, that. Well, before you came over, I tried making toast, but the toaster wouldn’t work, so I put the toaster in the microwave. I turned it on, and then a big explosion went off. There’s something wrong with the microwave, I think. Narrator: Yeah, there must be a problem if you can’t microwave your own toaster without an explosion. Jackson Rust: Exactly. Narrator: *sighs* Jackson Rust: Anyway, Al, if you want to sort out the explosion, or whatever…go for it. I have to call up Tessa real quick. Al: But what the hell am I supposed to do? Jackson shrugs, quickly thinking of something. Jackson Rust: Write a letter to the bastard microwave company, explaining how it blew up my toaster. Al: But— Al just sighs, shrugging it off before wandering to a nearby chair conveniently placed several feet from him. Al sits down as Jackson reaches for the phone, dialing a completely random number to fool you all, while still calling Tessa. Muahaha! Jackson Rust: Shut up, dude. I’m on the phone. Narrator: Very sorry. As Jackson waits for the phone to stop ringing, he starts to whistle a tune…until… “Heya, I’m not home at the moment, but if you want to leave me a message, I’ll get back to ya!” Jackson hangs the phone back into its receiver. Jackson Rust: Dammit, she’s not home. Narrator: Awww… Jackson Rust: If you weren’t a loud, booming, heavenly voice of truth and reason, I’d kick you in the nuts. Al: He’s just a narrat— Narrator: Big, booming, heavenly voice of reason and truth. Nothing more, and nothing less. Al: You’re just a narrator! *SMACK* The glove from the last Jackson Rust promo slaps Al about the head, as he tries to fend it off. Rust on the other hand, stands there, watching Al get beaten to a bruised pulp, chuckling, of course. Narrator: I’m a heavenly voice of reason! Al: I’m going to convince you that you’re just a narrator of JacksonCO promos! Narrator: Heavenly voice of JacksonCO, how bout that? Al: No! You’re a narrator! *SMACK* The glove hits him again, and again, as the backdoor slowly opens, and a ratty looking character walks in. He looks around for a bit, spotting Al getting beaten by just a floating glove, as Jackson watches on. He turns around slowly, and sticks his head out of the door. Ratty Looking Character: Jimmy da Snoop says da coast is clear. He steps back into the house, and opens the door wide, as four more men enter, and look around like no one was in. Jimmy the Snoop: Hey! Fat Tony, we gonna rob this place or what, eh? Fat Tony: That’s Fat Tony – Voision One, alright? Jimmy the Snoop: Whateva! Yep, you heard it right…now…I’m trying to pass of Italian accents in writing. Have fun! Fat Tony – Voision One: Sally da Snitch, you see anything suspicious ‘bout this place, eh? ‘Sally’ looks around for a moment, before turning to another man, dressed in an all black fancy suit. Sally the Snitch: Hey, Johnny Tightlips, you see anything other then da two guys and da glove? Johnny Tightlips: I ain’t saying nothing. Sally the Snitch: How ‘bout you, Frankie da Scuzz? Another man steps forward. His suit is filthy, but yet, he maintains a stable role in the Mafia here. Frankie the Scuzz: It looks fine to me. As all of them walk around the house for a moment, Jackson steps forward, and the whole Mafia stumbles back! Fat Tony – Voision One: What the hell?! How did you get in here! Jackson Rust: It’s my house…and I’ve been standing here watching you enter and look around. Fat Tony (Voision One) slaps Jimmy the Snoop in the back of his head. Fat Tony – Voision One: Asshole! You said da coast was clear! Quickly, get da weapons! All of the men reach into their pockets for their guns – but since Steven Spielberg was a co-producer of this promo, they all pull out walkie-talkies, attempting to look intimidating. Jimmy the Snoop: Don’t make us use these! Jackson and Al slowly back up, with the men following them. Unfortunately, the walls do exist in the house, and that’s what stops Jackson and Al from backpedaling. Jackson Rust: Narrator! Do something; smack them with your glove! Narrator: No way! They have walkie-talkies man! Al: So? You’re a booming, heavenly voice of reason, remember?! Narrator: So? Al: *sighs* Fat Tony – Voision One: We gots you corner now. Surrender now, and no one is hurt! The other four men brandish their walkie-talkies, thrusting their arms forward, attempting to scare Rust and Al. Jackson Rust: D-don’t do an-anything dr-drastic here. Within a moment’s time, both Jackson and Al are tied about with ropes that were also conveniently placed around the house. With their arms tied behind their backs, and their legs tied together, they cannot do anything; especially with those lethal walkie-talkies threatening them. Frankie the Scuzz: You dissing the walkie-talkies? We would have guns, but Steven Spielberg says these were better! Right Johnny? Johnny Tightlips: I ain’t saying nothing. Fat Tony – Voision One: Now…we’re going to wait here, until da other two come back! A’right?! Jackson Rust: How do you know we had other people in the house? Al: They’ve been looking around for a few days now. Jackson Rust: And you didn’t say anything? Al: They offered me Jolly Ranchers. Narrator: Oooh! Do you still have some?! Al: No. Jimmy the Snoop: What was that noise? Narrator: Just me. Jimmy the Snoop: Oh, alright. Will Jackson and Al be saved? Or will they crack under the walkie-talkies? Only time can tell. Stay tuned for the sequel to this exciting adventure! Al: *yawn* Jackson Rust: Jesus, help us. Narrator: *sighs*
