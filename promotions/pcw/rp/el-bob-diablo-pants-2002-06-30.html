El-Bob Diablo-Pants

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/30/02 *Whipping, Cracking, and Chopping Sounds* Sandy Cheeks:'HIIIII-YA! Ha, thought you had me, didn't you El-Bob?' *More chopping sounds* El-Bob Diablo-Pants:'WUUU-TAAAA! Thought? I knew I had you! You're going down, Sandy!' Sandy Cheeks:'Don't bet on it, El-Bob!' The scene opens up with Sandy and El-Bob karate-fighting out in the middle of an empty sandy field just outside Bikini Bottom. There are a few rocks lying around, and some seaweed coming out of the ground. Other than that, it's just a sandlot, perfect for karate fighting. Sandy and El-Bob wear their respective blue and red oversized foam helmets and gloves. Sandy:'I see you've been practicing, El-Bob.' El-Bob throws a series of rapid-fire jabs, but Sandy evades them with ease. Sandy:'Not too much, though.' Sandt clocks El-Bob with a standing sidekick that pushes his face into his body. El Bob flies ten feet away and lands on his back. El-Bob:'Whew! Good hit, Sandy. I'll never be ass good at karate as you.' Sandy walks over to El-Bob and extends her hand to pick up her fallen friend. Sandy:'Sure ya will, El-Bob. You just need a little more training. Again?' With the greatest of ease, Sandy pulls El-Bob back up to his feet. El-Bob assumes a fighting stance. El-Bob: *Somewhat serious, though smiling*'Come and get some.' Sandy shrugs and hops up and down to loosen her muscles. Sandy:'Here it comes, El-Bob!' Sandy executes a spinning roundhouse, which El-Bob ducks. El-Bob:'Now, Sandy! Here IT comes; the awesome power of the EL-BOB UPPERCUT!' With Sandy's back to El-Bob, he lunges forward in a Dragon Punch-type maneuver. He uses all of his force and power to punch Sandy in the back of the head. El-Bob:'AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEYYYYYYYYYYYYYAAAAAAAAAAA!' El-Bob lands on his feet and kneels facing away from Sandy. He breathes out heavily. El-Bob: *Panting*'Heh. Heh.' Sandy:'El-Bob, if you're going to call out an attack, you should do it.' El-Bob looks at Sandy. Her back is still turned. He also looks at his hand. It's bent away from the back of helmet. He didn't do any damage at all. El-Bob:'But... I...' Sandy turns back to El-Bob, her guard down. She has a blank yet disappointed look on her face. Sandy:'... You hit me, didn't you?' El-Bob looks sad and disappointed as well. El-Bob:'Uh-huh.' Then he perks up suddenly. El-Bob:'Wellthenletmetryitagain!' El-Bob initiates another uppercut, which Sandy does not block. It flies straight up to the chin of her helmet. Upon impact, El-Bob's entire arm crumples from the impact. Sandy:'Wow, El-Bob. You sure ain't the strongest critter in Bikini Bottom, that's for certain.' El-Bob:'What do you expect? I'm a Mexican sponge!' Sandy and El-Bob laugh and shake hands. She holds her head up high and closes her eyes. Sandy:'Don't worry, though, El-Bob. I've got a good Texas-style workout that'll get you pumped up in no time. It's a lot of sweat, a lot of hard work, and it'll be REEEEEAL painful, but you'll like the results. El-Bob:'Hey, that's great.' Sandy looks down at the phone she's holding in her hands. El-Bob:'Anyway, I would love to take part of this workout you've put together, but I have... swimming lessons. Yeah! I can't be late for those swimming lessons!' Sandy:'El Bob, You live underwater.' El-Bob:'Yeah, so I should learn as soon as possible. Bye!' El-Bob, sitting his single-person couch at home that he called from, hangs up his phone. His pet snail(cat) Gary climbs up on his lap. El-Bob:'Gary do you think I'm weak?' Gary:'Mreow.' El-Bob:'Aw, thanks, Gary.' El-Bob pets Gary's shell. Gary:'Mreow.' El-Bob:'What's that, Gary? You want something to eat? Okay, pal, I'll go fix you a bowl of food right now.' El-Bob tries to get up, but he can't. He pushes down on the couch and swings forward with his legs, but he doesn't budge one bit. Stopping, El-Bob looks down at Gary. Gary:'Mreow?' El-Bob:'Um... Gary -- do you mind?' (Commercial: This RP is brought to you by Mexicans. Tirelessly picking our crops, cleaning our roofs, and maintaining our lawns & gardens. God bless Mexicans.) El-Bob and Patrick Star are over at Squidward's house. He recently redecorated, and against his better judgment, he allows the two to come in and take a look around. Squidward:'And that's everything in the house. Any questions?' Both El-Bob and Patrick eagerly wave their hands in the air. Squidward:'Keeping in mind that I won't answer any questions that can be classified as What is that? because I already explained what EVERYTHING in the house is.' Their hands sink back to the floor. Then Patrick says something. Patrick:'Oh, yeah. Squidward, I had a question that you haven't gone over yet.' Squidward nods his head. Squidward:'I answered all your stupid questions, this might finally be an intelligent one. What is it, Patrick?' Patrick asks in his usual Duh voice. Patrick:'I was just wondering... how come you're named after a squid, but you look like an octopus, but you only seem to have six functional tentacles?' Squidward just stands there wide-eyed and staring at nothing. Squidward:'Um...' Patrick:'I'm just more confused that to be anatomically correct, your ass should be where your head is, and your mouth should be where your ass is.' Squidward takes offense to this comment as a picture of Squidward, ass for a face and face for an ass, appears on-screen replacing the normal picture of Squidward for but a second. Squidward:'Shut up, Patrick! Your mouth would be on your back, and you wouldn't even have a head if this was anatomically correct, so just mind your own business!' Patrick is replaced with a picture of a starfish for a little bit, but it switches back. El-Bob:'You didn't show us that, Squidward.' Squidward looks over at what El-Bob was referring to, but changes his mind about it once he sees it. He snatches it up in his arms and cradles it from view. Squidward:'No, I'm not showing this to you.' El-Bob:'Please?' Squidward:'No.' Patrick:'Please?' Squidward:'No!' *Three Days Later...* El-Bob:'Please?' Squidward:'FINE! Here, I'll show you.' Squidward holds the object out at El-Bob and Patrick. It's like an ant farm. Squidward:'This is a nematode farm. It doesn't do anything, but these nematodes can get easily excited and very aggressive.' El-Bob:'Can we hold it?' Squidward:'No.' *Two Weeks Later...* Patrick:'Please?' Squidward:'FINE! Just don't drop it!' Squidward hands the nematode farm to El-Bob. It slips right through his hands as he grasps it. The farm busts and the nematodes bust out. Nematodes: *Looking around*'Now this is more like it! We're home!' Squidward walks up to the mass of nematodes. Squidward:'No, your home is in that little plastic farm that El-Bob broke. There's another one in the closet. Stay here, I'll get it.' There is a growling sound, and a shadow cast over Squidward. *Twenty Minutes Later* Squidward, Patrick, and El-Bob are sitting out by the road. His Easter Island Head house has been taken over by the nematodes, the three forcibly removed. El-Bob:'Squidward, I'm really sorry--' Squidward:'Nope!' El-Bob:'But Squidward--' Squidward:'Forget it, El-Bob! Nothing you can say or do will change the fact that I'm angry with you. Because of you, I got kicked out of my house by nematodes! Now where am I gonna live?'7El-Bob:'You can live with me!' Before El-Bob can say anything, Squidward takes out a pistol from Lord knows where and shoots himself in the head. El-Bob and Patrick sit by the dead body, dumbfounded. Patrick:'Um... I guess he had a better idea... Hey, you wanna go jelly-fishing?' El-Bob and Patrick get excited. El-Bob:'Do I?' The two run off, leaving Squidward's body. The trash truck comes by. Two fishmen jumps off the back, toss his body in the compactor, and the truck drives off. (Commercial: This RP is brought to you by The End. If you have problems with this RP and would like to file suit, please contact Mr. ShutTheHellUp at the Law Offices of LikeIGiveADamn.) El-Bob has a jellyfish cornered in a rock quarry, far away from the open, grassy (well, seaweed) fields that it's used to. El-Bob:'I've got you now!' El-Bob grabs the jellyfish. I stings him. El-Bob:'Ow! That hurt!' El-Bob recoils and blows on his hand, then goes right back to grabbing the jellyfish, which gets him stung again. El-Bob:'Ow! Why do you keep doing that?' El-Bob just doesn't learn his lesson, and continues to grab at the jellyfish. *STING* El-Bob:'Ow.' *STING* El-Bob:'Ow.' *STING* El-Bob:'Ow.' *STING* El-Bob:'Ow.' *STING* El-Bob:'Ow.' *Four Hours Later...* *STING* El-Bob:'Ow.' *STING* El-Bob:'Ow.' (Commercial: This RP is brought to you by your friendly neighborhood Neo Void God.) (We are NOT friendly. And we had nothing to do with this ridiculous RP.) (Commercial: ... This RP is in no way brought to you by your menacing NVG overlord.) (That's better, mortal. Twit...) Richard:'Lamar, what are you doing?' Lamar:'Impersonating Akiyama. Is it good?' *Richard backs away from Lamar as a black portal of emptiness forms underneath his pudgy friend….* El-Bob is walking to work at the Crusty Crab. He just whistles as he merrily strides along, until a voice haunts him. Haunting Voice:'El-Bob...' El-Bob thinks he was just hearing thinks. He goes on his way. Haunting Voice:'El-Bob...' El-Bob now pretends that he didn't hear anything. He puts his hands in his pockets and whistles and walks faster in a nervous gait. Haunting Voice:'El-Bob..!' El-Bob breaks out into a run. El-Bob:'You won't catch me!' El-Bob runs down the road, but the voice is right there with him. Finally, he stops. He looks around in scared suspicion. El-Bob:'It feels weird. And creepy. It feels like...' El-Bob turns around and points an accusing finger. El-Bob:'SOMEONE'S TRYING TO GET ME TO SWITCH MY LONG DISTANCE CALLING PLAN!' El-Bob sees nothing. He shrugs and goes about his business. Two fishmen in blue suits crawl up out of the sand and brush themselves off. Fishman 1:'Told ya he was on to us.' Fishman 2:'It's better than your idea. Sure, people would agree to the plan when we kidnapped them and tortured them for 7 days.' Fishman 1:'Exactly! What's so bad about that?' As El-Bob continues down the road, another voice stops him. Plankton:'El-Bob?' El-Bob:'Plankton? Where are you?' Plankton:'Over here, son. Come closer.' El-Bob takes a step. Plankton:'A little closer.' El-Bob takes two steps. Plankton:'Just a little more.' El-Bob takes three steps. On the third step- Plankton:'Crap! That's my face!' El-Bob lifts his foot, and looks under his shoe. A flattened Plankton greets him. Plankton:'Hello there, El-Bob. I was just in the neighborhood and wanted to know if you could buy me a Crabby Patty?' El-Bob gets defensive and suspicious, interrogative. El-Bob:'So you can steal the secret formula?' Plankton: *Sighs*'Look, you're stupid enough that I think I can level with you. I always want to steal the Crabby Patty formula, right? So why don't you just let me win this one time, and then you can go back to messing up my plans tomorrow, okay?' El-Bob:'Okay.' Plankton:'First, go and get me a Crabby Patty.' El-Bob runs to the Crusty Crab. *SPLAT!* *SPLAT!* *CRUNCH* Plankton:'No, stop!' El-Bob stops. El-Bob:'What's the matter, Plankton?' Plankton:'Scrape me off your freaking shoe, first!' El-Bob scrapes his shoe on the paved road. *SCRRRRRIIIIAAAAPPE!* Plankton:'Oh! OW! Stop!' El-Bob brings his shoe back up to face level. Plankton pulls himself off. Plankton:'You know what, just forget it. If you're this careless now, just imagine how you'll screw up my plans.' Plankton hops down to the ground and walks back to the Chum Bucket. (Commercial: This RP is brought to you by ideas, as in I've run out of ideas for commercials.) At the Crusty Crab, El-Bob is running around frantically searching all the drawers. El-Bob:'Where is it? WHERE IS IT?!' El-Bob goes up to a pair of fishmen eating at a table and opens their mouths wide. He goes all the way down their throats and comes up covered in food and some other things, but with nothing else. Fishman 3:'That was disgusting. Fishman 4:'Hey, he found my car keys!' El-Bob looks in the trash, scattering it all over. It lands in people's eyes and on their food. Everyone, save for Patrick, slide their food away. Patrick just shrugs and keeps eating. Mr. Crabs comes out. Mr. Crabs:'El-Bob.' El-Bob pays no attention and keeps on his little frenzy. Mr. Crabs:'El-Bob!' El-Bob still runs around all over the place. Mr. Crabs yells at him while tripping him up as he runs past. Mr. Crabs:'EL-BOB!' El-Bob:'Oh, hello, Mr. Crabs.' Mr. Crabs pulls El-Bob back to his feet and brings his attention to the mess he's made. Mr. Crabs:'El-Bob, you're scaring away paying customers! What in the name o' money are you doing?' El-Bob slumps down, then drops to the floor and grabs Mr. Crabs around the ankles, crying. El-Bob:'OH, MR. CRABS, I'M SO SORRY! I LOST THE SPATULA AND NOW I CAN'T COOK CRABBY PATTIES! PLEASE DON'T FIRE ME!' Mr. Crabs pulls El-Bob back to his feet again. Mr. Crabs:'Fire you? M'lad, you're the best fry cook a restauranteur like me could hope for! It's alright if you lost your spatula, just use a grilling fork.' El-Bob acts as if Mr. Crabs is speaking another language. El-Bob:'Grill-- ing-- fo-ork?' Mr. Crabs escorts El-Bob to the back and shows him. Mr. Crabs:'Sure thing, m'boy. Lookie here, this is a grilling fork.' Mr. Crabs presents the cooking utensil to El-Bob. Mr. Crabs:'It's a little different, but does the same thing as a spatula. Here, watch.' With Crabby Patties cooking on the grill, Mr. Crabs flips over two with the grilling fork. Mr. Crabs:'See, it's all in the wrist. You try.' Mr. Crabs hands the grilling fork over to El-Bob. He goes berserk again. El-Bob:'I can't do it! I can't use a grilling fork. AAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!' Mr. Crabs stops El-Bob again. El-Bob:'Look, son, you're going to have to get by. I have to handle the cashier's station because Squidward didn't show up to work today.' The scene cuts to the city dump. Squidward's head is stuck in a pile of trash. His ass is up in the air, with pilotfish eating parasites off of it. Mr. Crabs:'Just try it.' El-Bob slowly approaches the grill with the fork. He picks out a Crabby Patty and slightly pokes it with the fork. It flies up into Mr. Crabs's face. It slides off and lands on the floor. Mr. Crabs picks up the Crabby Patty and puts it in a bun. He walks out to the front. Mr Crabs:'Who wants a Crabby Patty half-price!' Many voices shout 'Me! Me!' Mr. Crabs sticks his head back into the room. Mr. Crabs:'You've got to figure out that grilling fork and keep making me more money.' Mr. Crabs exits the room again, but comes back for a quick moment. Mr. Crabs:'Oh, and El-Bob, no need to tell the health inspector what I just did.' Mr. Crabs goes back to the cashier's counter. El-Bob picks out another Crabby Patty and tries to flip it. El-Bob:'Well, here it goes. I mean, what's the worst that could happen, right?' Cut to an outside shot of the Crusty Crab. All is well for 1.5 seconds, then the entire establishment explodes. The End:'Now, for those of you who are stupid, this cartoon was a depiction of El Diablo, with Spongebob Squarepants representing the so-called Mexican Devil. So, what have we learned from this little cartoon? El Diablo is weak, clumsy, moronic, and unable to adapt to new situations. 'One would think that I've got an easy match ahead of me at Gold & Glory...'
