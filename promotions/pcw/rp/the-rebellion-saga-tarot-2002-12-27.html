The Rebellion Saga: Tarot..

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-27-02 Her presents were unwrapped and the paper drifted through the room like colorful tumbleweeds in a black desert. DarkStar sat by her side and smiled when she opened the last gift, the one thing she truely wanted and needed... Her tarot cards. 'Gavyn!!!!!! These are beautiful!!!' She looked at the ever changing landscape of the tarot cards in her hands, each was different everytime she looked at them, the backgrounds were vivid and the people were living in them, each had their own life, bound to the meaning of itself. The cards were beautiful. 'Where did you get these?' she asked elatedly, 'They are amazing!' DarkStar just smiled, 'It's a perk of my job you can say, when you live in a world of fantasy, things like this are VERY easy to come by.' Realisticly though he had gotten them from his realm, they were just kind of sitting there. It was a nice afterthought, he knew that ever since Talena lost her cards she would need new ones... she may be able to see bits of the future, but the tarot made it easier for her to portray the thoughts from her muddled head. 'Let me do your tarot!' DarkStar raised a eyebrow and sat down in front of their black marble coffee table. 'Sure my pet, what harm could it do?' Little did he know that her tarots could change lives. She giggled and sat down indian style, native american style in a politically correct world, and laid down the first card happily, then the second, then the third, face down with their black glittering backs almost shining (as well as black could do), she flipped the first. 'Lets see if I still have it, this is your past.' The card revealed the Star, a man crouched in the night, he seemed to be thinking as a huge white start shone above his head. DarkStar just stared, he never had any knowledge of the tarot, the occult was Talena's thing. She was very good at witchery, a true Pagan Goddess... DarkStar just got his powers from being evil. Either way is fine really. 'Thats nice...' He waited for her to respond, she didn't. 'Uh.. what the hell does it mean.' 'Someone hasn't been keeping up on his studies.' Talena scolded playfully, 'You see lover,' she pointed upwards, 'The stars are a source of hope, guidance and inspiration. Life in the heavens, as you very well know, is far removed from the petty concerns of earth.' She pointed at the shining star, it was like knocking on air, the card was so real, 'This card reminds us to have faith in ourselves and our future. The stars inspire us to take the first step towards our hopes and... and...' her intelligent speak seemed to be wearing off, making sense made her little head hurt, '..dreams.' DarkStar made a face, 'Dreams?' He had always doubted the tarot in the hands of anyone else, but Talena... well. He had to pay attention. 'What could that one mean?' Talena's eyes changed from sold blue to blue and green again, 'I don't know. There were alot of words.' DarkStar sighed. Talena smiled and flipped the second one. She made a face. 'What?' DarkStar asked, puzzled. All he saw was a purple tower against a dark landscape, 'All I see is a silly litt-...' he was cut off when a bolt of lighting nearly knocked the tower off its foundations with a lifelike crack, these cards were intense. 'Well... this symbolizes your present.' She tapped the card where the lightining damage was. 'This is The Tower, It is hit by a unexpected bolt of lightening which will eventually destroy the enduring Tower.' She frowned again, her eyes normal, her thoughts unclouded, 'You see, the Tower symbolizes something that will always be there, concrete or other wise, the lightning represents how drastic changes can interrupt life without warning.' She looked at the card, 'Most of all, it symbolizes change. Sudden crises and emotional outbursts force us to wake up because of our human heritage.' 'Wow.' 'Yeah, I know.' Talena adjusted her PJs uncomfortably. 'How do you tell all that from a piece of plastic?' 'Majick?' DarkStar nodded, only with these two was Majick NOT a sarcastic answer. 'So... that last one would be the future right...' Talena slightly bobbed her head as a answer. Her slender, pale fingers flipped the card. They both gasped, the card was fiery in itself, a revolving pentagram was surrounded by the five elements of Satanism, Talena nearly looked away, A severed deers head lay there as some sort of sacrifice, the blood dripped. 'The Devil...' DarkStar knew that that couldn't be good, well he didn't know... for some reason Tarots revealed the Death card as a good thing even though he knew how surly Death got when she was having a long day. 'This card is about the personal demons that can confront you,' she began to rattle off everything she could think of, 'addiction, cruelty, dependency, depression, fear, greed, guilt, hatred, hopelessness, ignorance, malice, materialism,; obsession, pessimism, self-doubt, selfishness and everything else.' She seemed uncomfortable portraying the meaning, 'The Devil card shows that you are caught in an unhealthy situation. This card shows that you must examine your situation and yourself very carefully to separate the true from the false. Its supposed to say to stop despairing and feeling sorry for yourself and start believing in your self.' DarkStar blinked idly. 'So.. thats my future according to the tarot cards.' 'Uh huh...' 'Wow. I'm not getting any sleep tonight.' She giggled. DarkStar kissed her forhead. 'Well at least you're happy with your presents.' 'Very! I love you.' Thoughts were eating away at the back of his mind, the tarots were right. 'I love you too.'
