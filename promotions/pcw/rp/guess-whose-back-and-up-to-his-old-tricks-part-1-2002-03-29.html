Guess Whose Back....And Up To His Old Tricks...(part 1)

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29.03.2002 New York City. The city that never sleeps is home to some of the greatest clubs of the western world. From the upscale RnB join “Nel’s Nightclub” on 14th street to the salsa club “Wild Palms” on Broxndale Ave. There is something for every one in New York City. White, black, Cuban or Asian there is somewhere for you to go to. The scene opens up at outside a club called Pulse on Queens Blvd. It is nearly 1am and the club has yet to start really jumping. Outside is a long line of people, mainly black and Latino, but some Asian and white too. All the girls with their short skirts and the men with their shirts. Some are chatting together, some are obviously drunk and singing. The club is quite large, dark brick and large windows, with “Pulse” written along the side of the building in flashing green lights that stand out in the dark sky. There are clubs up and down the road and people walking all around the streets, all dressed up. The line starts to grow longer and longer as more people come to the club. There are two big bouncers at the front door, both about 6’5 each and both probably weighing around 250lbs. One of them is a dark skinned African-American, with his hair cut short and a small goatee. His fellow bouncer is Caucasian, who has short brown hair. Both men are wearing black suits and have dark glasses. All of a sudden the once loud crowd quiets down as a silver Bentley Azure pulls up in front of the club. The crowd are silent in anticipation for which star is driving this $250,000 car. Out of the car step one black man and two white men. Those men are the recently-resigned to PCW wrestler Duzza, his close friend PCW star Red Rock and Red Rocks annoying tag-along manager Boolie. Duzza steps out of the driving seat, Red Rock from the front passenger seat and Boolie pulls his big frame from the back of the car. Duzza is dressed in a pair of baggy white jeans, a white “tp-2.com” T-shirt and has a undone, snow white shirt over it. He has his hair set back in long cornrows and has about 3 platinum chains around his neck. One has a small tag with the initials “M.W” encrusted in diamonds on, one has a platinum cross at the end and one with a iced out dollar sign at the end. Red Rock is dressed in a pair of ridiculously baggy denim flares, a tight black t-shirt with a Metallica symbol on the front and his longish hair hanging down loosely. Boolie is wearing a similar attire. The crowd of people are all staring in awe of Duzza, his iced out attire and his Bentley Azure. Well either that or staring at how Red Rock and Boolie look like leftovers from the 80’s. Before they go into the club, Duzza slowly looks around at the surrounding area, before letting out a long, satisfying sigh Duzza: “ Ahhh, Rock, can you smell that?” Red Rock looks slightly confused, but decides to try and sniffs the air a few times Red Rock: “ All I can smell is smoke, man this place is dirty” Duzza: “ No that fool. I mean success, ahh the great smell of success. After a long time, Duzza is back in PCW, me back in PCW, did you ever think you would see Duzz back in PCW man?” Red Rock: “ No Duzz, I can honestly say I didn’t” Duzza: “ Well, I am, and damn it feels good. I’ma last longer than I did last time, and see if I can teach or these lil n*gga’s how the original G did it” Red Rock: “ That’s great Duzz, but it’s cold out here, any chance we can go in?” Duzza: “ Ha, yeah dawg, let’s go” As a valet takes the keys from Duzza, he walks up to the front door, instead of queuing and goes straight up to the bouncers Duzza: “ Jimmy! Frankie! What’s up boys?” Duzza greets the two bouncers with what is known as daps, before chatting to them Frankie: “ Duzz, we haven’t seen you round here for a long time, I see you got yourself a new set of wheels” Duzza: “ Yeah well y’know, the Pinto wasn’t really attracting the honeys y’know?” Jimmy: “ Haha, yeah Duzz I feel ya, so you coming in tonight?” Duzza: “ Yeah man, I done got signed back into PCW, so thought, hey may as well celebrate it by kicking it at my old joint” Frankie: “ Cool Duzz, umm are those two with you?” Frankie points to Red Rock and Boolie. Who are both sticking out like a sore thumb, both looking very nervous. Duzza chuckles to himself before replying Duzza: “ Yeah man, they with me” Frankie: “ Ok, well Duzz, we’ve got work to do, so hopefully we’ll chat to you later” Duzza: “ Aight boys, nice chatting to you, hey yo Rock, Boolie get over here” Duzza, Red Rock and Boolie all slowly walk through the doors and into the club. The dance floor is packed full. To the right of the dance floor is a line of women, outside a door, so Duzza guesses that, that’s probably the women’s bathroom. To the right of the dance floor is a very long bar, fully stocked with a few people sitting at it. Next to that, are some stairs up to VIP and at the far end of the dance floor is the DJ set. “Shake Your Body” by R. Kelly and Jay-Z plays over the system and Duzza slowly walks down onto the dance floor, the jewelry blinging of his body. At first he moves to a group of fine women and joins in dancing with them, having a good time, while Red Rock and Boolie just to look scared. After about 30 minutes of dancing with the same group of women Duzza looks up to see if Boolie and Red Rock are having fun, but can’t find them anywhere Duzza: “ Aww man, where they gone now… I bet they started a fight with a gangbanger or something” Duzza realizes that Red Rock and Boolie can get into trouble when there surrounded by old white folks so the trouble they could get into, in a hip hop club would be a lot worse. He walks around the club, looking around for Rock and Boolie. After moving to the far left of the club he comes across a small group of women all dancing around a large guy, about 6’8, but is pretty thin, probably not weighing more than 220 lbs. The guy looks African-American and has long braided hair tied back. Duzza instantly recognizes the man as PCW superstar The End. Duzza and End go back to the EW days and have had their fair share of incidents with one another. With Duzza being the one at the end of a beating more of the time. Duzza walks up and taps End on the shoulder. End spins around and looks around Duzza: “ END!” The End: “ Who is this? Duzza? That you?” Duzza: “ Hell yeah dawg, how could you forget this face dawg?” The End: “ Duzz, I’m blind” Duzza: “ Oooh damn… I thought you would of gotten over that by now” The End: “ Gotten over it?” Duzza: “ Yeah man, y’know I thought it was like, one of them ol’ EW-related illnesses” The End: “ EW-related illnesses? What are you talking about Duzz?” Duzza: “ Man, you know how Crimson Flame got that embarrassing rash?! From following Jax for too long, that type of thing” The End: “ O…k, well Duzz, what are you doing here?” Duzza: “ Well this is my hometown dawg! And I’m just celebrating my resigning to PCW, with Red Rock” The End: “ You got into PCW!?” Duzza: “ Yeah dawg, see they can only hold me down for so long. It was only a matter of time before I got back in, anyway what you doing in New York? And if you still blind, then how do you dance in the club without falling over? And how do you tell the fit women from the fine women?” The End: “ What can I say? I just got skills” Duzza: “ Uh huh, well anyway, have you seen Rock or Boolie anywhere?” The End: “ Duzza, how did you lose Boolie? He is 7 foot something, and about 500 lbs, how on earth did you lose a 7 foot gorilla?” Duzza: “ Umm, would you believe me If I said I didn’t know?” The End: “ Well, Duzza, I gotta go do something over there, so err congratulations on getting back in PCW, who you facing in your debut?” Duzza: “ Charlie Loc” The End: “ Oooh, that could be a tight match” Duzza: “ Yeah dawg, I was hoping for Hank, but I hear he quit and took my damn Bentley with him, so I guess Charlie Loc it is, hey it’s a hard knock life” The End: “ Yeah Duzz, well I gotta go, bye” The End quickly turns and walks away, with a women on each one of his arms, making sure he doesn’t walk into anyone. Duzza turns around and continues his look for Rock and Boolie. He decides to check the bar area, but on the way there “Honey” by R. Kelly and Jay-Z came on, so Duzza just had to stop and dance to that. By the time he get’s to the bar, 15 minutes have passed since he spoke to End. The bar is very long and the counter is made out of a smooth, polished wood. The bartenders are all dressed in white shirts and little black bow ties. Duzza quickly spots Red Rock and Boolie sitting at the bar Duzza: “ Dude, why you not out on the dance floor man?” Red Rock: “ Oh, we was waiting for some Metallica to come on, maybe get a mosh started” Duzza: “ A what?! Man, this club plays hip hop, RnB and reggae, no rock music, ok? None. Thankfully. What you see in that crap anyway? It’s just long haired fools shouting over a badly played guitar” Red Rock: “ So?! It sounds great” Duzza: “ Hmm, must be a white thing” Boolie: “ Duzz, you promised we’d go into VIP” Duzza: “ You wanna go into VIP?” Boolie: “ Yes, I ain’t never been in there before” Duzza: “ Well then man, let’s go” Duzza, Boolie and Red Rock are get up from the bar and start to walk across to the stairway leading to VIP. Duzza slowly looks around at the dance floor and bar area before leaving. When, he suddenly notices NTICW wrestler, part time Civil Rights activist and part time homie of Duzza, Jordan Crazy. Duzza: “ Ha, there’s JC, let’s go see him” Boolie: “ What? But I want to go into vip” Duzza: “ A’ight your loss, yo Rock you go up with Boolie and get him in, while I’ma kick it with JC a’ight man?” Rock: “ Ok, Duzza, just be quick” Duzza quickly walks over to the 5’11, 237lb Jordan “Ruckus” Crazy. He is dressed in a pair of black pants, some well polished shows and has a white shirt, undone at the top on and a pair of Malcolm X-type. Crazy is sitting at the bar, his back to Duzza, with a bottle of beer in his hand, he looks like he is drowning his sorrows Duzza: “ J.C!” Jordan Crazy quickly turns around and as he notices Duzza, a wide smile comes across his face, the two quickly engage in ‘daps’ and then start to talk Duzza: “ What up my n*gga J.C?” Crazy: “ Ahh nothing my brother, how are you doing?” Duzza: “ It’s all good round here bro, jus’ celebrating getting back into PCW with a night out on the town man” Crazy: “ You got in PCW? I’m planning on joining you in there eventually” Duzza: “ Yeah dawg, you’ll make it in soon bro, no doubt, just stick In there, look at me, I was in PCW, then I dropped out and went all over the game, but I ended up back in here man” Crazy: “ There’s always hope” Duzza: “ No doubt, anyway bro, why you sitting in a New York bar on your own man? I thought you’d be out then scouting the honeys, y’know what I mean?” Crazy: “ Yeah I feel ya bro, but it’s been a long day, just thought I’d have a beer, then head back to Queens” Duzza: “A’ight bro, fair enough. But I was about to head up to VIP, you sure you don’t wanna come up with me? Chill with the original G for a while, yeah?” Crazy: “ Hey, what’s the worse that can happen?” Duzza: “ Bro, you don’t wanna know” Duzza and Jordan Crazy both laugh and make their way to the stairs that lead up to the stairs. On the way there, a small, thuggish looking brother shoulder barges Duzza. Before smiling cockily and after a small stare down walks away. He is a perfect example of a player hater, who can’t stand to see famous and rich people in his area, flashing their jewelry and with all the girls around them. Crazy: “ What was that fools problem?” Duzza: “ Ahh, just another player hater” Duzza and Crazy walk up to the stairs where Red Rock and Boolie are still standing. Trying to get past the security guard Red Rock: “ Duzz, I’m glad to see you man, they wouldn’t let us in!” Boolie: “ Yeah, he said we wasn’t important enough” Duzza: “ A’ight calm down man, yo Jay, let them in, they with me ok man?” The security guard at the top of the stairs opens the gates and let’s them walk through, they have to go through a metal-detector on there way in. Red Rock and Boolie go through fine, then Duzza goes through and all of a sudden the machine starts beeping away Duzza: “ What the f*ck?” Security Guard: “ Could you please lay all jewelry and other metallic objects in the dish, and college them after” Although not happy about having to take of his jewelry, Duzza does so and then goes to walk through the metal detectors, once again the alarm goes of, this time before Duzza can say anything 3 security guards come up to him and start searching him, suddenly one pulls out a tiny silver hand gun. Duzza’s stares at the gun with his mouth open Red Rock: “ARGH DUZZA, you bought a gun to a club? Why?” Duzza: “ It ain’t mine, I didn’t bring no weapon man, I just got back into PCW, I ain’t gonna go carrying a weapon I’m celebrating!” Security Guard: “ Yeah, what ever, Joe, make a call to the police, get them to take this fool downtown” Jordan Crazy: “ This is Duzz, you can’t do this to him, it isn’t his” Guard: “ Then whose gun is it?” Crazy: “ Well…I don’t know but still..” Guard: “ Let’s get this fool to the front door and wait for the police” Three security guards pick up a kicking and struggling Duzza to the exit of the club, while Duzza is hollering at Red Rock Duzza: “ Rock, take care of my jewelry! Take care of my Bentley! And call my damn lawyer” Duzza is dragged out the front door, where a waiting police car is parked, they stuff Duzza into the back seat of the car Duzza: “ You can’t do this too me, I’m a star damnit! I’m Duzza, Argh, I can’t belive I’m gonna spend my 1st night under PCW contract in jail… Damn” The scene fades to black as a crowd of confused people watch Duzza getting driven downtown in the back of a police car, while protesting his innocence… (To Be Continued…)
