Corrupting the Schmoopies!

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/02/03 “Psychological warfare. It’s what we do best.” Gaz purred as yet another flash of the camera went off. Nerezza looked on in the background with his expressions hidden behind his sunglasses. The magnesium burns reflected off the lenses that shielded his sensitive eyes from the light. Gaz got down to one knee and continued to snap picture after picture after picture. Cheesy aerobic music played in the background to motivate both Gaz and his subject. “I get the feeling this one has an ulterior motive...” the vampire croaked with an air of doubt. He looked across to the backdrop of canvas and studio lighting with a disgusted look across his lips.In front of the camera and basking in the thrill of the photo shoot was none other than Natalia Miyagawa. The Soviet Scarlet was clad in little more than skimpy pink underwear with lacy frills, as well as a set of pink fishnet stockings and matching high heels. She was striking a series of provocative poses, all of which were lapped up by Gaz and his camera. “What could you possibly mean by that, Squire?” The Jester grinned. “Which reminds me, how goes the search so far?” “Well, trawling throughout the internet, I’ve found some 40 fan sites or so based on Tessa Windsor. All the rest are fan-fiction and lemon stories. Too much of this soppy crap and... ugh... never mind. I’ve got around 27 pictures, all jpeg, mostly with each of these fan sites having the same pictures over and over. Damn recycled wording, too.” Nerezza said, looking over his shoulder at the monitor behind him. On a small desk, a computer that looked better off in a museum hummed loudly as the screen displyed another tiny snippet of a PCW promotional picture of Tessa Windsor. “Right. I’m heading off to the Dark Room to develop these...” Gaz said with his face all aglow. Natalia stepped out from behind the lights and backdrop, joining Gaz in the Dark Room. “I want to see how they came out.” The Russian beauty giggled as she closed the door behind the two of them. Nerezza sighed to himself and continued to crawl throughout cyberspace. The gears of madness were turning to produce P.W.A’s method. * * * Hours later... * * * Gaz and Natalia stumbled out of the dark room. Their hair was missed up and Gaz’s outfit was all crooked. What little there was of Natalia’s also seemed a little roughed up. In her left hand is a pair of high heels and in her right, some glossy pieces of paper. “Nice to see that somebody’s actually concentrating on this. I’m your unwilling tag partner, not your butler.” Nerezza growled in frustration., showing a few teeth on one side of his mouth. Gaz, red-faced and a little sweaty, gave a confident and cocky smile in Nerezza’s direction. “Technically, Squire, you’re anything that I say you are. If I feel like putting a leash on you, you can accept it and like it.” He laughed. “Shhh, don’t give our secrets away, Gazzy...” Natalia spoke in her thick Russian accent. Nerezza hissed slightly and pulled out his own pile of paper from the printer connected to the computer. Waving it in the air, it caught the attention of both the Soviet Scarlet and the Emerald Menace. The couple stepped over, both wrapping their arms around the other’s waist. “I think this is what you need. I’ll need you to pick one from what I’ve found.” The Living Nightmare explained, handing over the pictures of Tessa Windsor that he’d printed out. Gaz took them and unwrapped himself from the crimson temptress, freeing up one hand to leaf through the pictures. Examining them carefully, he scanned over each one for quite a while, which produced an annoyed, perhaps jealous look on Natalia’s face. The corner of Nerezza’s mouth upturned slightly as he saw this. Another negative emotion that maybe he could exploit in the same fashion. Gaz suddenly stopped on one photo. “This one. This one will do.” he said, nodding slowly and holding the picture up at arms length. A huge toothy smile filled up his face as he studied the photograph. To his dismay, an long arm stretched out and plucked it from his wandering eyes. “Then if you’re done staring, I’ll get to work” the vampire gruffly spoke. His arm reeled in like a fishing line and sunk back into place. Turning his back on the happy couple, Nerezza brushed his purple hair back over his shoulder and made his way back to the computer. Sitting into a swivel chair that struggled under his weight, he took hold of the mouse and began to maneuvre it around the screen. Shortly, a paint program took up the screen with Tessa’s face being dead middle.“Okay... now all I need is your half of the pictures.” Nerezza said, keeping his back to the couple and instead stretching an arm out in a position that would cause bones to shatter if it was a normal man. “Just don’t start at them for too long.” Gaz giggled, taking the photographs from Natalia and placing them into the awaiting claw of Nerezza. “Hypocrite...” the vampire though as he retracted his arm and flipped up the scanner with his boney fingers. “You two had better get a drink or something... and perhaps some clothes for the lady.” “Shall do, Squire. As a matter of fact, we were just about to disappear for a few supplies. We’ll even pick up something for you. After all, even slaves need to eat. But none of that blood that you need until Turmoil, we don’t want you turning vampire on us now. Call it a safety precaution.” Gaz said in a mocking tone. “JUST FOR YOUR INFORMATION...” Nerezza roared, clearly bing hit in a sensitive place. “...I could slash the both of you apart in this human shape as well!” Natalia ducked behind her male companion as he pushed her backwards a little with a protective arm. “But Rezz... you wouldn’t. Or more accurately, you can’t. Not while you still have your dreams of amassing your vampire army through the gullible fans of wrestling. Now shut your little fangy mouth and get back to work... slave.” The Jester commanded. “...” Nerezza was held in place, not by a physical restraint, but by the looming threat of a career being killed. Specifially his own. As the door to the rented studio closed, Nerezza glanced around the plain white walls of the spacious building and sighed heavily. With no alternative, the Living Nightmare swallowed his pride and began to feed the lingerie pictures of Natalia into the computer. One by one, they appeared on the screen. Nerezza then clicked on another tool and removed Tessa Windsor’s body from the neck down. Finally, he began to erase the heads of the pink lingerie-clad Natalia pictures... * * *Just make the clock’s hands whizz around really, really fast.* * * The door to the studio opened up once again as the giggling pair of Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa slipped in quietly. Sitting at his desk on the same weak-looking chair was the vampire, still tapping away on the keyboard with the reflection of the screen in his red circular sunglasses. Gaz proudly strode over ot the desk and placed a bottle on the side with a loud BANG, grabbing Nerezza’s attention. The vampire jumped a little from the noise and shifted his gaze to the bottle. Behind the sunglasses, his eyes open wide and his mouth screws up in disgust. “GATORADE!?” roared the creature of the night. “Sorry, but we have to promote PCW’s OFFICIAL drink, not some stupid, second rate product like Rezz Bull. Drink it or leave it.” Gaz hissed with authority. Nerezza paused, breathing loudly only to show his discontent. Turning his head sharply back to the screen, the stabbed the Enter key with a boney digit and soon, the printer began to hum to life. WRRRRRRRRRR-PT! WRRRRRRRRRRRR-PT! WRRR-RRR-RR-RRRRR-PT! The attention of Gaz and Nerezza was firmly focused on the sheet of paper emerging. Slowly, the image was cast from the machine onto the paper and dropped. Sitting in the “out” tray was now a picture of Tessa, posing provocatively in skimpy pink lingerie and high heels, thanks to Nerezza and a little help from the computer. Gaz picked it up and began to look it over. “Squire, I must say, I’m impressed! I can’t even see where the necks meet!” Gaz mused with a happy expression. “I had to change Natalia’s skin tone to match Tessa’s. Without the use of a microscope and the brains of a detective, nobody’s going to figure it’s a fake.” Nerezza explained. There was definitely a ton of pride in his voice after completing his work. Reluctantly, he submitted to a laugh of his own. “Now all we need is the final touch.” Natalia said. “Besides, after phase two, the paranoia and suspicion between those two is going to get them completely at each other’s throats.” She held out a pink envelope and sprayed a few squirts of perfume on one side, then took it onto Nerezza’s desk. She was now in her tight pinstripe business suit as opposed to the skimpy clothing of earlier, but still looking just as enticing. Nerezza glanced over to the envelope, curious as to what was being written. “So we now have a picture of Tessa pretending to be in PlayBear. Now what?” the vampire asked. “A stroke of genius, Squire.” Gaz said in a cocky voice and running his fingers thorugh his lime green hair to spike it. “Natalia does a little bit of trickery on the envelope. We make it out that this is a picture that Tessa sent out to a fan that got returned. A reason isn’t needed. Anyway, it conveniently ends up in the hands of Chris, who opens it and finds some unmentionables inside. The accusations and lies begin, the confusion runs and they’ll have less co-ordination on Friday Fusion that the nerves that link the mouth to the brain of George Bush!” “Plus...” Natalia smirked, stuffing the digitally tampered photograph inside. “I’ll add a few things to raise the ante.” “Hm... tell me more!” Gaz ordered with puppy-dog eyes. His facial expression switched to one of lust as Natalia removed the pink stockings from earlier and stuffed those inside the package. “Actions speak louder than words...” Natalia teased. All that the trio had to do was deliver the package when Tessa wasn’t around and make sure Chris was intrigued enough. Can the most insane couple in the business and their unwilling servant corrupt the sweetest pair in PCW? Tune in for the next episode - Same Pranks with time, same channel with Attitude!
