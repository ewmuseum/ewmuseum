7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/7/02 OOC: Sorry about this rp to my readers and fellow PCWers. Between boxing and getting ready to move next week. Life's been hetic plus trying to visit my father in Baltimore in the middle of this. So I didn't produce anything really good with this. Maybe some shits and giggles. Please don't book me for next week though. Once again sorry. (The scene fades to the mean streets of Miami, Florida. Well, okay not really mean but have an imagination! No sunny skies. Just rain pouring and lighting roaring. Now, this is getting boring. But what's that man in the black poncho doing over in the middle of the road speaking with the 15 or 16 year old boy in the black trench coat holding up the an umbrella over his head. As we move in closer we see the boy in the trench coat is after all Violence Jack's brother, Derek. We can't see who the guy in the poncho is but he falls to his knees splashing Derek in the process and lifts his arms up in glory. He tilts his head up reveling the face of Big Daveylicious smiling.) Big Daveylicious: Whoooooooooooooooooooo! I jobbed to Streak! (Derek taps Big D on the shoulder.) Big Daveylicious: What? Can't you see I am taking this moment of true glory and letting myself shine? I mean I was one of the thousands of wrestlers that jobbed to Meanstreak! Derek: Dave, you jobbed to Ryan Streak at the hands of my brother. (Big D's eyes open and look at Derek.) Big Daveylicious: You mean I didn't job to Meanstreak? Whoooooooooooooooo! Derek: Yes, you jobbed to Ryan Streak. Big Daveylicious: Who? Derek: Nevermind. Big Daveylicious: Well, why in the hell am I out here in the pouring rain to celebrate about? Derek: I don't know. How about... (Derek falls to his knees spreading his arms upright in joy smiling to us for the first time.) Derek: I beat up my brother! Big Daveylicious: Wait a minute, son! That's my job! Nobody jumps to their knees and celebrates like I do. Derek: Well, Dave you haven't had much to celebrate about. You got fired and with that lost your gold. You got no naked women. You had a short term relationship with Jaden... Big Daveylicious: Okay, it wasn't a relationship! It was one night of sweet desperate SEX! Derek: ...which leads us to now: where you are hanging out with a 15 year old and humiliating his big nobody no gold brother. Also... Big Daveylicious: STOP IT! HAVE YOU NO SOUL?! Derek: Yes, why bought us matching 'Bestest Friend' shirt with an arrow pointing to each other. (Big D stops and stands up so does Derek.) Big Daveylicious: Derek... Derek: Dave... Big Daveylicious: No wonder you got your ass kicked in streets. You gotta act cool. Get over there in there on the sidewalk. (Derek moves to the sidewalk as Big D kneels down in the middle of the road. He tilts his bleached-blonde head complete with the ever-present Oakleys back and holds the Extreme title in the air with his arms outstretched in joy.) Big Daveylicious: Whooooooooooooooo! I humilated Violence Jack and jobbed to Streak? Derek: Ryan Streak. Big Daveylicious: Uh? Whoooooooooooooooo!...oh crap! (A car lunges towards him. He dives out of the way. The speeding car misses him. He dusts himself off and looks on at the car. He shakes his head as the scene fades to black.)
