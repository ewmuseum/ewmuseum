This Is Really Getting On My First-Rate Nerves

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #4 PPV Our scene opens up on a familiar sight. We see Rush Mason sitting, in a baby blue tropical shirt with baggy jean shorts, on his bed inside of his suite aboard the Wrestle Vessel. The scene looks surprisingly un-interesting. Rush is either asleep or very close to it, as he just lies on his side. However, we soon see that Rush is, in fact awake. “Why in the name of Bon Dieu can’t I get to sleep? Could it be that even the best bed on this little tugboat is sub-par?” Rush’s lovely observation breaks the silence, proving his alertness and the fact that the Voodoo Villain is indeed awake. He sits up on his bed, staring out the large windows all around his room. He sees the shining sun, and figures that it must be about lunch time “Hmm…now where would I find some food in this little shindig? My first-rate stomach is feeling empty.” Rush gets up off of his bed, and slips on some loose sandals, and stretches his arms, while yawning loudly. Without further adieu, Rush opens up the door and looks out on the deck, which seems to be rather full with other people who have come out for some food or for the early afternoon sun. Rush: Hmm…the sub-par morons seem to be out in masses today. It must be the sun. None of the sunbathers and diners near Rush hear his comment, which is probably better that he doesn’t get into even more trouble after his little escapade last night while trying to get some sleep. Walking amongst the tables, Rush spies a small stand for food and drink, and begins to walk towards it. “Getcha Jamaayca Ice Tea Right’Chea!” The loud Jamaican accent of the dread-locked man standing at the food stand can be heard all through-out the deck, and Rush winces as he hears the loud voice resounding from right next to him. Rubbing his ear, Rush steps up to the front of the stand, looking at the board with the choices for the day. Rush:So, do you get a lot of business from the sub-par…errm…the other passengers? The large Jamaican man nods for once second, and then resumes his loud crying and advertising for his products. Rush grabs his ears again tries to speak to the man above his own voice. The man with the accent stops shouting for a moment. “Oh yah mon. I getta lotta costamairs on dis here stand!” Barely comprehending what the other man just said, Rush just shrugs and looks over his choices for food. Looking through it, he decides to just get a chicken sandwich. He also sees the selection of drinks and notices that there is only one choice. Rush: I guess I’ll have a chicken sandwich with the…Jamaican Flat Tea. The man nods and reaches behind, pulling out a plastic container with a chicken sandwich. Rush looks at the not very appetizing looking sandwich, but he is very hungry and begins to eat the sandwich slowly as the man turns his back and starts to prepare Rush’s drink. “Man, you gunta love dis tea.” Rush tries to get a small peek at whatever the man is putting into the drink, but nothing can be seen beyond the man’s tall, lanky body. Continuing to chew his sandwich, Rush looks curiously at the drink as the bartender sets it down on the counter in front of him. Spying something at the bottom of the glass, and winces. Rush: Oh my sweet Voodoo Ass, please tell me this is a tequila and that’s why there’s a worm in it. The Jamaican man grabs the drink quickly and pulls it back behind him, but Rush can see as he sticks his hand in and pulls out the worm, throwing it on the deck where it is immediately attacked by a horde of sea gulls. Bringing the drink back, Rush looks disgustedly at it as the man sets it down in front of him again. “I sweah, mon, I couldn’t find dat ting for da longest time.” Rush finishes the sandwich quickly, but now he’s feeling very thirsty. With a sour face, he picks up the drink and lets a few drops slide down his tongue, and then just decides to take a gulp. Within a few seconds, he spits out the entire gulp onto the floor, and starts rubbing his tongue with a napkin. Rush: What was that?! That sub-par crap doesn’t deserve to go down my first-class throat. The Jamaican man shrugs, and goes over to check a small index card, reading off the list and nodding. “I did de recipe right: One cup Bacardi, one cup palm frond juice, and crushed reefa.” As Rush hears the recipe, he can feel his stomach turning circles. He looks at the man, who continues to mumble to himself loudly about the ingredients of the drink. Rush: Ohh man, my first-rate stomach is feeling surprisingly sub-par. Weaving to and fro, Rush sprints towards the side of the boat, where he begins to spew his first-rate vomit all over the blue ocean. Looking back up from his vomiting endeavors, Rush shakes his head to clear it, and exclaims to no one in particular. Rush:Wow…I feel a lot better. However, just as Rush speaks, the ship comes to a very abrupt halt, which sends the Voodoo Villain flipping onto his back right on the wooden deck. Rush grabs at his back for a moment, but then kicks himself up. “You no-selling jobber!” “Hmmm…” Rush thinks to himself, “I didn’t know Jeff Marx was on the boat with us.” However, Rush has bigger problems in getting this damn sub-par boat back working. He uses a loud voice to announce to people on the deck, very few of whom listen to him. Rush: Sheep, everything is going to be fine. Just keep your little sub-par panties on. Most likely it’s just a retarded Australian with an afro doing this, there’s no reason to worry. And with that, Rush hurries off, while the crowd just looks on, bewildered at the stupidity of this man, but Rush thinks that he is somehow doing a civil favor by helping these far inferior beings. Either way, it’s a good time to fade to black…
