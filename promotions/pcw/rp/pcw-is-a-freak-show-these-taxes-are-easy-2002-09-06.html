PCW is a freak show!!! These taxes are easy.

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/6/02 -The theme to the Adams family plays with a hand popping up doing the snapping. The camera pans back and we see the man snapping is Duke Williams. His presidential snaps continue as he stares at a picture of Asylum. Duke is sitting at his Front Office Desk at 1600 Pennsylvania Ave site of the PCW plaza and the PCW presidential office. Duke just smiles as he looks down on this motley crew of rejects. Williams: To qoute a great line from a great movie. “NEEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRRRRRRDDDDDDDSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!” -Duke lets out a mighty yell using his whole 6’3 frame.- Williams: Talk about a bunch of nobodies!!! Miya has been here longer then me but who’s the President of this fine institution. Ask your self this?? Who’s your daddy???? -Duke sits back and tosses the picture of Asylum in the garbage as he looks over some other papers. Duke reaches into his desk, takes off his shades, and outs on his reading glasses.- Williams: Stupid ass glasses. How are you supposed to look cool with these things laying on your nose? Time to do MY taxes. -Duke jaw drops when he looks over the tax forms written up by his esteemed collogue and CEO Mr. Kannon.- Williams: Holy shit on a rye with butter!! Hoss this is a mess. Wait a minute what is this here? -Duke looks at a head note that reads, “Front Office Personal file under Ezform-XK.- Williams: Well I’ll be a money on crack in the Lincoln Park Zoo!! Let me fine that form. -Duke shifts through about a hundred pages till he finds a royal purple form that reads Ezform-XK.- Williams: Man this is much better the size of print on this page is readable!! What’s this? -A cassette tape is attached to the paper with a note to enjoy. Duke pops the cassette into the player and hits play. A soothing voice is heard.- Voice: Taxes shouldn’t be a difficult process especially for the front office that sets them. So please fill out the easy form with ease and enjoy the soothing sounds of the Hubbardville Phil Harmonic as they perform the instrumental versions of the Front Office theme songs. -The music kicks on as Duke kicks back and looks over the form. Duke cracks his knuckles as he gets ready to rock.- Williams: Lets see is your first name Xavier, Duke, Brandi, or Ellie? -Duke thinks it over before filling in the yes box.- Williams: This is rough. Let me get a glass of water. -Duke sips down some Avon water and gets back to it.- Williams: For have such a prestigious name you get a write off of ten percent. WAHOO!!! -Duke does a little chair dance. Then gets back to the form.- Williams: Do you have a Front Office in the office building? -Duke gets out of his chair and looks out the door to reassure himself that he does have a front office. He runs back to his chair and fills in the yes box.- Williams: Another exemption!! This is so easy! -Duke continues down the list.- Williams: If your city is in a select location your are entitled to an exemption. -Duke looks at the list and panics since there are only three choices.- Williams: Please have Missoula….please have Missoula….please have Missoula. -Duke looks at chose one.- Williams: Hubbardville, Wisconsin…….damn it!!! -Duke starts to sweat.- Williams: The second city is…Sacramento, California???? Boo!!! -Duke starts to get real nervous.- Williams: Missoula, Montana!!!!! YES!!! I get an exemption….I get an exemption…..I’m da man!! -Duke does another victory dance.- Williams: Well that wasn’t so hard and it looks like I’m getting a refund this year!!!! -Duke looks at his desk which is covered with complaints from PCW starts having trouble filling out the tax form.- Williams: I don’t know what they are bitching about it seemed easy to me!! Morons. Its just shows you once again why the Front Office is in charge and not these mental midgets. -Duke looks over the up coming Fusion card.- Williams: Darkstar and Rent-a-homo against Brandi, Duke, and Kannon. It’s a handicap match. Of coarse any match with Darkstar in it is a handicap match. -Duke laughs at his lame joke.- Williams: How many times do I have to beat these two midcarders before they disappear? They are like that fly that keeps buzzing by your head and won’t go away till you smash their brains in. I can do that. If not I’ll simple have them fired or send them back to PWA. Oh damn I’ve been working so hard its time for my paid three hour lunch. I’ll catch you germs later hoss. -Duke gets up and heads out the door.- =w=
