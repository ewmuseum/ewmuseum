Metal Gear Spoofage 2: Sons of Bitches

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/4/02 Four years ago. We had classified intelligence that a new type of Metal Gear was ready for transport. The whole thing stank. But our noses had been out in the cold too long. Along a bridge walks a solitary man. The rain pours down upon him, bouncing off of the slick coat that covers his body and face....he lights a cigarette quietly. After a couple of flicks he realizes he has no chance of actually getting his cancer stick lit in this squall....angrily he pounds the side of his lighter. Screaming at it he shakes the device for all he is worth, then throws it upon the ground and stomps it into pieces. A noise in the man's ear catches his attention. A voice, seemingly yelling at him to get on with the mission....nodding, he sprints down the road. Faster, faster he runs, shedding his coat just as he vanishes totally from sight. Nothing but a green blur now, the mysterious stranger hops the bridge rail and plummets headfirst. Gracefully he dives, reaching for the bungee cord attached to his leg....then panics as he feels that it is not there. The man looks about frantically as he freefalls, flapping his arms like some sort of demented bird. Sadly this does not help either, he crash lands in a heap on the deck of a passing boat, making a huge crashing sound. Marine: Huh? What was that noise? Wandering over, the guard kneels beside the stranger who rests on his knees with his face on the ground, moaning in agony. Marine: Hmph. Clyde Presents: Metal Gear Spoofage 2 - the Sons of Bitches Starring: Many random people Live in terror Shaking off the cobwebs, Solid Snake stumbles drunkenly to his feet. Wandering behind a nearby box, he holds his ear. Snake: This is Snake, come in. Do you read me Bobby? A scene of bushes appears on Snake's monitor. Behind them a fat kid peers, staring through a set of binoculars. Bobby: Little to the right....no, the right! Damn it! Snake: (Sighing) Bobby, come in. Bobby: (Looking around) Oh, hello Snake. Snake: I'm at the landing point, but it looks like my stealth camo's busted. Uh....landing impact. Bobby: Shoo, go away! Bad doggie! I can't watch Amy in the bushes with you humping my leg! .....wait, keeping going. Little farther up..... Snake: Ugh. Hate to say it, but I actually miss Byron. Standing and looking through the downpour, our hero observes as upstanding Marines march back and forth along their assigned routes. Snake: Bobby are you sure Metal Gear's on this ship? Bobby: No don't close the blinds! Bitch! Argh! Snake: BOBBY! Bobby: What? Snake: Is Metal Gear really on this ship!? Bobby: Yeah, I got it from a good source. Snake: It wasn't one of your idiot friends who claim they can hack but in reality just live in their parent's basement using programs they downloaded off astlavista was it? Bobby: .....who told you. Snake: Argh! Bobby: No really, I mean it. It's a new model, designed to mop the floor with all the other models with its abusive power and high pitched whining. Metal Gear Kreller. Snake: (Blinks) We'd better go take care of it then. Bobby: Try heading up to the bridge first. Snake: ......how the hell will a giant robot be in the bridge? Why would I need to go to the bridge? Bobby: Curse my lack of x-ray vision! Snake: What!? Bobby: Just I wish I could see through curtains sometimes. Snake: Bobby, I need you to work with me here! Bobby: No, I said stay AWAY doggie! Ack! Snake: ARGH! In frustration Snake looks up, watching the Marines pace. Then a strange buzzing fills the air. Snake: .....a helicopter? Suddenly a form darts out behind the closest Marine. Dressed all in black, he sneaks up behind the man....wraps a hand around his head and plunges a knife directly into his throat. Snake: Ooh. Shockingly enough the Marine doesn't fall though, instead he turns and grabs at the intruder's collar. The terrorist looks around in panic, then plunges the blade into him again.....again....again. Snake: Oooooh. Still the Marine does not fall. Looking completely dumbfounded, the man throws him to the ground and begins stabbing him over and over, in the face, in the stomach, in the crotch. Snake: Oooooooooooh. Finally the Marine lies still....our mystery man wipes the sweat from his brow. He then yelps as AGAIN his victim reaches up. Snake: (Munching popcorn) Damn. In a blind panic the terrorist stabs the Marine again, over and over until his black suit is covered in blood. Slumping with exhaustion, he then waits as a helicopter hovers overhead, producing a rope ladder. Snake: Katsatka? Bobby: Gesuintite. Snake: Shut up. Down the rope slides several more terrorists, who assist in disposing of the body. Then a huge man hops down, shaking the deck with his sheer girth. Snake: I know him. Solid whips out his digital camera, zooming in on a Russian face. The fellow produces a bear cap, placing it on his head while he barks orders. Bobby: Who is it? Snake: Ivan Gurlucovitch. Bobby: Hm. Snake: Stop acting like you know who I'm talking about. Bobby: .....sorry. Snake: He was the man Revolver Hosselot was to meet up with after Shadow Moses. But of course things got derailed. Bobby: So what's he doing here? Snake: How should I know? On the ship's rail, Ivan laughs heartily. He then makes his way through a door, his platoon of terrorists close behind. Snake: Uh. That isn't good. Bobby: Dammit, running out of batteries for my camcorder! Knew I shouldn't have used the old ones I had in the vibrator. Snake: ......Bobby: ......never mind. Shaking his head in disgust, Solid Snake sprints through the downpour to the doorway in the side of the ship. Entering, he shakes off the water like a wet dog and glances around. Snake: Uh let's see.....bridge.... Scratching his chin, the man ponders which way to go....then looks up, seeing a large signed marked 'Bridge: This Way'. Snake: Well, that'll do it. Snake takes two steps in the direction of the sign, then stops as a loud blaring fills the air. He looks up in shock to see a surveillance camera with a red light blinking frantically. Snake: Crap, I'm getting old. A swarm of guards rush down the hall, alerted by Snake's bungling. He races as fast as he can, following the sign up a flight of stairs to a control room where he slams the door shut. Snake: Phew. Breathing deeply, Snake walks to a large panel located next to the steering column. Snake: Uh..... After a moment of pontificating and head scratching, the warrior relies on his highly trained instincts.....he pushes random buttons. Snake: Hum de dum. Suddenly a series of sparks begin flying from the panel. Buzzes fill the air, crackling, burning sounds....Snake's ear rings. Bobby: Snake, what happened? Snake: (Covering the panel with his hands) Nothing. Bobby: You didn't just start pushing random buttons on the panel, did you? Snake: Don't you have a six year old you could be molesting? Bobby: Actually, yes. Snake: Good. Hey look, there's someone outside! Leaving the helm to fizzle and pop, Snake trots over to the far door. There, he looks out the window to see an odd looking soldier walking slowly. Snake: Hm. As quietly as he can, the hero opens the door while ignoring the flames rapidly building behind him. He then sneaks behind the soldier, hiding and listening as Ivan Gurlucovitch's voice rumbles through her walkie talkie. Ivan: Do not worry Brandi, this is country of liberty. I'm sure you may find many prostitution jobs here.... Brandi: I do not want to be prostitute! Just because grandmother was one you always want me to be one too. Ivan: Oh it was forever ago, can't you let anything go? Brandi: No! Ivan: Dya. You are carrying my grandchild, do you not remember? Brandi: (Holding her stomach) Da, but.... Ivan: No but. I can see the moon, pale as a pizza pie. Brandi: Don't you mean death? Ivan: Da. God, Ivan is hungry. Brandi: ..... Ivan: After mission finished you promise you'd leave the unit. Brandi: But I have no where else to go! Snake: (Pulling his gun) Freeze! Her expression blank, the soldier does as asked. Snake: Throw your hands in the air!Again, slowly Brandi does as she is told. Snake: Wave em like you don't care! Brandi: (Raises an eyebrow) Snake: Yeah. Turn around. The soldier steps to face Snake. As she does, the warrior recoils as a tuft of hair emerges from both of her armpits. In the midst of it, a small flock of baby birds nest and chirp. Snake: What the hell is that!? Brandi: I've been with the unit for the past six months. It leaves no time for grooming. Snake: Still! Brandi: Peeg. Brandi suddenly drops, producing a knife from her boot. She then whips it forward, rotates and windmills her arms in a bizarre dance. Snake: ...... The woman thrusts her hand from the bottom left to the top right, disco dancing with her knife. Then she puts her left foot in, her left foot out, her left foot in, shakes it all about and chucks her knife at Snake. Snake: AGH! At the last second Snake ducks, the knife whizzing over his head narrowly. Brandi: I must give you credit, no one has ever dodged that knife of mine! Snake: You took roughly 60 days to throw it, lady. Brandi: Shut up. You got lucky, but you will not get lucky twice! To Be Continued
