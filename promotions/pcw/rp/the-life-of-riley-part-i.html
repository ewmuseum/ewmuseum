The Life of Riley Part I

Date: 07 03 03 A life of Riley. Something Jade had not experienced to such an extent previously in her life. Something which she was now practicing in its entirety. Being this close to such luxuries had unsettled her awfully; When Jade had been upset by such things she felt the need to binge. Sitting on a luxury queen sized bed Jade now knew that Lacuna knew how to treat her guests. If it was a little pushed onto her, Lacuna had set Jade up nicely. She had been here for barely four hours and had only stopped eating to train a little with Lacuna. She seemed easily pleased by Jade’s progress as they went through the motions. Jade threw an ultra sugary sweet chew bar to the floor. There were wrappers all over the white silk sheet lined bed, the silk linen stained in a nasty chocolate coating, accompanied by a few, still wet beer stains. Jade rests her head back into the plush white pillows and rubs her toned, yet slightly bloated torso. Jade pulls her face as she looks down at her belly button, filled with a black fluff she rolls her eyes and reaches over to the phone. Her fingers almost wrap around the small phone, trying not to move her body too much. “Ungh, so unfair.” Jade mutters as she sits up on the bed, the blood flowing to her head as she gently wipes her hand across it. “Hey Brook. Be a dear and run a lady a bath could you?” Jade smiles until she gets a reply down the end of the phone. “I am a lady damn it! Yes, I don’t care if you are a butler and have more class than me! Yeah? Well I make more money than you so…” Jade blows a raspberry down the phone and sticks out her tongue. “I want bubbles and everything, Yeah I want it now!” Jade slams the phone down on the table. There is no receiver on the cell phone and Jade presses the phone against her ear again. “You’re still here. Go run me a freakin’ bath!” Jade grunts and presses a button as the phone call is ended. Jade falls backwards into the mess and soaks up the high life. Jade was left to dwell o the fact that she was in upper class heaven. This was absolute delight with the cushions and the food, and the drink. Now she had made friends with Lacuna, helped her win her second match in the PCW she knew she was in a good place. That isn’t something a lowly woman like Jade takes for advantage. She didn’t have to work for anything past the occasional training session which actually was doing her body the world of good. The thing about this life is the lack of adventure; excitement was the bets thing for an ample personality. All this could send a girl mad. Who cares? This was a good thing, as a temporary measure only…of course. -[Fade]- Jade has a radio set up in the bath room blasting out full. Jade was shaking her head in the bubbles frantically back and forwards, bits of her hair getting wet through. Jades new NeverMore album was what was booming through the speaker’s. Her arms floated out of the liquid heaven and began playing her magical electric guitar. Jade reaches over the side of the tub and pulls out a little black leather book. She opens the book, which is apparently a journal/phone book. Jade pulls out a black pen and niggles on the top of the pen. Jade breathes out and bite her lip. “Now, what interesting has happened to me in the past couple of days?” Jade mumbled and pressed pen against paper. It read as following, Again, not much with the happenings Diary. I really am at a loose end and it is killing me. Lacuna has gone to England, to escape me and all these loud sounds I make. I’m left with some portly old butler with a superiority complex. He is better than everyone apparently. Well I’ll show him who is boss, the other boss. I’m going to have some fun. At the expense of everybody…of course. I need fun, Jesus I’ll end up like a hermit otherwise; all this money is great and all but that is worth nothing if you can’t blow it and have a little fun. Yours forever Jade. Fun Time Jade stands at the front door of the large American mansion with her arms folded. Seemingly heavy in thought as the huge chandelier style lights hung from the ceiling, highlighting her deep amazing eyes and fantastic plum lips. Brook stood behind her in the hallway looking on in wonder as Jade’s music still blasted stridently through the immaculate rooms. Brook quivered in anticipation of what events this could possibly be leading into. The door bell rang and Jade snapped her hand to the handle. She stalled in opening the door and looked behind her to Brook, he was busily rubbing his hands, a sign of great angst. Jade stepped back away from the door and looked to Brook, her eyebrows raised. “Get the door and be a good Brooky now dear.” Brook muttered solemnly under his breath and stepped towards the door. As he opened it there were a flood of people entering through the door. “Oh no, Lacuna won’t be very pleased about this! I really need to get in tough with her.” Jade pinches Brook by the ear and pulls him down to the ground. She shakes her finger in his face. “Go fetch some beer kegs. We’re in for a long night.” Jade laughed her head backwards as the party followed her into the pool house. Brook however, was on his ay to the phone. To be Continued…
