Two Things I Hate...Liars....And Judges....

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-3-2002 Your in your bedroom...slipping on a long sleek set of black gloves as you look in the mirror, your face flowing like an unstoppable river with pure hatred over two mahor issues.... The Liar... And The Judger... First the 'Liar'....Amy Campebll for those who don't know... She said she loved you...you believed her, and what a fool you were. You did things with and to her that broke so many boundaries, things that made you question at that time if you were TRULY a hero or not. You ignored that irking conscience and you figured your Rent-A-Senses were on too high...your thinking too far into the whole situation... Truth is...you didn't think enough. Face it idiot, she got what she wnated from you...a companion for a while, a good lay, and a heart to hold onto...that was all. She was bored with you the minute she got to know Reno and she had EVERY intention of leaving you at her soonest oppourtunity. She didn't even have the guts to come to your face to say that she wanted him and that she never really loved you...she just loved how you made her feel....she loved that pedestal you put her on... But you, please...You never meant ANYTHING to her and it took her leaving you after that night fo ecstacy to get it.... But when she kissed Reno on Slam!.... When all thsoe fans were CHEERING that 'blssed union'... At that moment you were ready to rip her heart out and Reno's...if they truly had any. All thsi time, behind your back, their love was growing and in an instant...you were cast to the side with no second thought. Your dreams with her, your feelings for her, the PROPOSAL TO HER... She pissed all over them that very night when her lips met Reno's in a moment of passion....and for the first time in your life when you cut the TV off and proceeded to hit the air with all your embroiled anger.... You actually felt what it was like to HATE... And guess what.... It felt good....it felt so good... That hate you felt for them was so intoxicating...so wonderful....it made you wonder what.... REVENGE felt like... You've tasted hate, and your just loving how it feels, your mouth watering as you think about the mutilation fo OSW's new couple... But then you remember...you love her... You can't hurt her...even in your new persona, you couldn't...because the little bit of Rent-A-Hero won't let you... But you NEVER said anything about Reno. So as you douse your fingers in the smooth body paint you mixed yourself in the paint stained sink in your humble bathroom, applying it gently to your bare chest so you can make your new villainous symbol... You slowly plot the many ways you can make Reno's life hell for what he's done to you. But that will have to wait for just a little while... Because we have to get back to the OTHER thing you hate... Judges...also known as...LEGEND INC... He judges you, mouthing off about 'how you've changed', how 'your not a true hero', and how 'he's a hero and he'll make everything right.'Legend is nothing but a judge....never seeing the wrong within him yet somehow finding the wrong in others. You didn't want to become what you've become...but she did it to you, he has NO RIGHT to judge you. For that, you want to make an exmaple of Legend Inc. He wants to 'bring you to justice'...well he'll die trying. So what if your using him as a scapegoat until you can get your vile hands on the one they call Reno... Even the innocent die in small wars. Legend Inc. holds himself on a higher standard then everyone else and he is FIRMLY entrenched in his belief that he is a hero and a legend... To you, he's just short man speaking really loudly on a box.... A man of no real importance who tries to make himself seem big when he's really not. As you add the tiny white legs on the right side of the Venom symbol your painting on your chest, all you can think of is how your going to knock him off that box then beat him into the ground until he realizes he's no judge of you...for he is not God, and only God can judge me. It seems like everyone wants to push your last nuetron and your about sick of it....your still not gettign why the people don't hate you yet, they cheer you even when your trying your hardest to be evil. It's strange...it's as if Rent-A-Hero has never disappeared, like he's been reincarnated in this 'villain' form... No matter...Villain, hero, or not, your still going to terrorize Legend Inc. with all the offense that has made you a mainstay on the roster and put him out of business. The moment he jduged you....the moment he spoke words towards you in an effort to try to sound like he was above you...you knew you had to decimate him...and you will.Everyone talks about his 'momentum'...how he's on a roll after RPG3 and winning the Continental title....wow....seems like he's got everything riding for him huh? Know what your EEEEVIL-Senses tell you? Guys who have EVERYTHING riding for them....have EVERYTHING to lose.... Ask that Rent-A-Hero guy, he could write a Bible-thick story about it. The same way you had so much taken away from you in one feel swoop, you shall do the same to this so-called 'Legend'. the same way he judged you...your going to judge him in the middle of that ring and introduce him to a new side of you. As it seems on Turmoil, it's not exactly a classic battle of good and evil, where the villain is painted in shades of black...and the hero shades of yellow and white...It seems more like shades of grey all around.... Yournot trying to be a hero anymore, you gave up on that when you stopped hearing 'I love you' in the morning from Amy's sweet voice... Your just trying to be something not like what you were...it just so happens they still cheer you, you'll never get why...I mean SLAPPING HANDS REALLY HARD should get you booed shouldn't it? But there's no time to go crazy about it...it's time to end a Legend. If the fans just happen to cheer you as you do, then so be it...if they don't, then all the better. Your not TRYING TO BE A HERO, yet you still....it makes you wonder at time sif it truly was your destiny... But you could careless, right now, you have an evil scheme to put into action and dwelling about what's wrong in your life WILL NOT get that done. So you step out from your bathroom, clad in an all black jumpsuit with black mask, 'Cat Burglar' in bold white letters on the back as if it wasn't mroe obvious. Your scheme is ready to be executed.... What scheme that is you say? Well, it's time to start make Legend Inc. feel what it's like to be missing important things in your lfie...what better then by taking two fo the things he covets with deep care... His workrate.... And his Wrestling God 3 trophy. He'll know how you feel everyday once he comes home to find the two missing.So with that, you race for the door....tripping over the coffee table on the way out...but oh well, your too upset to be embarassed, and you know good and well what time it is... IT'S TIME TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD.... ONE DUMB SCHEME AT A TIME!!You want to bring me down Legend.... You better call the Justice League....sucka...Hey, you had to end the RP somehow, works for Booker.
