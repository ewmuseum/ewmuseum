Whooooooooooo! Tommy Stillinger: Now, I’ll get the director. You just wait here. (Graves pulls on Big D’s arm.) Michael Graves: I don’t know about this. Big Daveylicious: No, damn it! Director: And this must be Big Daveylicious. Big Daveylicious: Your Extreme champion. Director: Really? What company? Big Daveylicious: The PCW. Director: Porno company? Big Daveylicious: I WANTED TO BE A PORNO STAR! (Tommy with a worried look on his face tries to come up with something.) Tommy Stillinger: If you wanted to get laid why didn’t you get a hooker or a female fan? Big Daveylicious: THAT’S IT YOUR DEAD! Michael Graves: Noooooooooooo! You got better things to do? Big Daveylicious: Like what? Michael Graves: Beat Flaymin Aymin? Big Daveylicious: THAT’S IT! Michael Graves: Watch those three lesbians? (Big D lets go of Tommy. He smiles and stares off to the adult actresses.) Big Daveylicious: He’s right. I got to watch those naked women.
