This Isn't Helping!

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/15/03 The sky overhead was cluttered with nimbostratus clouds which as usual brought forth weather which I had hoped would hold off till I was at least off in another country. Yet the moment I got home from Korea after facing Dorn Tracer for the television title it had started pouring and it seemed it would never stop. “Thank god I’m heading off to Afghanistan tomorrow, I don’t think I can stand anymore of this rain.” I said to myself as I looked out the window inside my bedroom and sighed as I saw the rain was picking up. It was another one of those boring days with absolutely nothing to do but sit around. I probably should have been studying up on my opponents for both Friday and Tuesday, but I was sick of always having to do something for either my career or for my family. Today since there was absolutely nothing to do but sit around the house I decided I would make time for myself to just sit back and relax. “Why can’t you come back Chris?” I whispered into the air asking the question that I seemed to ask almost twenty times a day. I had missed Chris dearly and as each day went by reality found itself sinking deeper into me as I realized that it was highly unlikely that he was ever coming back and I would just have to deal with that. Maybe… Hope absorbed into my mind as I reached over to the phone sitting on the small table beside my bed and began to dial up Chris’ cell phone number. “ Nine…two…five…” I thought to myself aloud trying to remember just what his number was. Once all ten of the digits were typed in I waited patiently in anticipation as the phone began to ring. RINGGGG, RINGGGGGG, RINNGGGGG “You have reached Chr…” CLICK! “Stupid answering machine!” I shouted, not wanting to hear Chris’ voice unless it was actually him and not some stupid machine answering for him. I flopped down my bed, stomach first and sat there as my mind began an investigation into the many possible reasons Chris had dissolved from my life. ...Maybe he didn’t want to deal with P.W.A. anymore. I couldn’t blame him. I wouldn’t want anything to do with them either but I’m bonded to Gaz for life now whether I like it or not. There’s no way out without an annulment. Perhaps that is one of the reasons Chris left. He knew that there was no way we could be happily together with Gaz around causing trouble. Maybe he had run off to be someone else to be happy with because he knew he couldn’t be happy with me because of this and since we had both lost to P.W.A. the chances of an annulment went down to almost doubtful... I sighed as I thought about how happy Chris probably was now without me to hold him down. At the same time though I felt though that it was my fault which caused me to resort back to a form of stress relief that I hadn’t used since way back when I was in high school. Perhaps a poem or two would help cheer me up so I began to write down the words as they came to my mind. The oneThe one I’ll never beThe one with the perfect bodyThe perfect looksThe perfect personalityI’m not any of that But I’ll tell you what I amI’m no one specialI’m the ugly oneWith a messed up faceA bitchy attitudeI’m the one nobody wants I really tried to be something desirableI held my emotions in too muchSo then I told the world my thoughtsAnd I became an attention hog.Somebody please tell me whyI’ll never be what anyone wants. “GAH, it’s useless!” I screamed as I realized that this was not helping any and only making me more depressed. I crumbled the piece of paper I was writing on and tossed it across the room along with the pink gel pen I was writing with. “Well I guess I can always figure out a way to get along with Gaz.” I sighed, realizing that it would take a whole day if not more to find the answer I wanted to this dilemma but at least it would keep me occupied. “After all if I’m stuck being Mrs. Green for the rest of my life I might as well try to get along with the insignificant other.” I finished before going to work on thinking on how I was going to survive through Friday, especially if one of the teams going up against up consisted of two of Gaz’s buddies, Nerezza and John Gargano. It was going to be a difficult task to overcome, but I knew I could do it I just had to try, and hope that it didn’t become a three on one situation.
