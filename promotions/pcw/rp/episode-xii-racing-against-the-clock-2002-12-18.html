Soon noticing onyx colored streaks appearing on his battered and bruised skin. Josh slowly raised his head up, having his eyes closed. On both sides of his face, you would notice that he had onyx colored streaks on both sides of his face. Clutched both of his hands into tight fists, as the small cerulean colored orbs were coming straight towards Josh. The orbs were fusing with his body. When all the orbs fused with his body, Josh had yelled outloud. Exploding into a large inferno of energy. Akane and Kenchi shielding their eyes when Josh did that.
