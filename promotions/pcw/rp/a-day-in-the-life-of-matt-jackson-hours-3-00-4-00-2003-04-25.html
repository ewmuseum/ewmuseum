A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 3:00-4:00

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/03 Location: CIA Main Floor, Langley VADate: April 22, 2003Time: 3:00 am Tabby walks down the main hall of the main floor, which the grace and poise of a trained dancer. All around her are glass cubicles. Her green eyes dart from each one, noting the workers that are inside, quietly pounding away on the keys of their keyboards, only stopping momentary to look up at the world outside their glass prisons. Each one is working on a different part of the sleeper cell case. At the head of the row is the oak desk of Matt Jackson, the only missing piece of the puzzle. Running her petite hands through her long brown hair, she can’t help, but wonder where her new crush is. He went out two hours ago to follow up on a lead and nothing has been heard from his since. She just met him, but she is starting to really like him and she hopes that the feeling is mutual. Stopping in front of one of the cubical, she straightens her pink dress and loosens one of the white buttons by the top. The floor is hot and without the air being on, everyone is forced to sweat it out. Taking a step into Brian’s office, her heels click slightly against the concrete floor. Brian’s cubical is much like her own. A little bigger then all of the rest because of their jobs, but nothing to spectacular. In front of her is an average sized desk, with papers strewn all over it. Hanging pictures of Brian and his friends decorate the glass walls. He is typing something on his computer and doesn’t even bother to look up at her. Mr. Donald is wearing a blue dress shirt and black dress pants, he as usual isn’t wearing a tie and the first few buttons are undone. Brian continues not to notice the brunette as he types away, trying to get new information on the sleeper cell, when Tabby quietly speaks up. “Brian are you busy?” Looking up, Brian smiles at the young beauty and runs his hands through his black hair. “Not extremely Tabby. What do you need?” His voice reassuring, trying to make the young girl feel comfortable. Despite his best efforts she is still trembling a little bit. She is always nervous around her former boyfriend, turned boss and Brian knows it. Things between them haven’t quite been the same since they broke up, but Brian is doing everything he can to keep the work environment normal and she is glad for it. That is the reason she is coming to Brian, he is still one of the only people she trusts. “I am just worried about Matt,” she says, staring into his black eyes. Her slender face and big eyes tell him of her fear. “He went out to follow up a lead a few hours ago and he hasn’t been back ever since. It has been almost two hours.” Nodding, Brian gives her a slight smile, trying to put her fears at rest. “I don’t know a lot about Matt, but from what I did hear he knows what he is doing. I am sure he is all right.” Staring at Brian for a minute, Tabby knows he is probably right. “Do you think we should do anything?” She questions, trying to see what the more experienced Brian thinks. Rubbing his chin with his thumb and forefinger, he offers: “I don’t think we have anything to worry about. He probably just got tied up or something. I am sure he will be back soon.” Not satisfied with his answer, Tabby starts to sway from side to side, trying to come up with another reason to get Brian to help her out. She knows how bad it can be to let her personal feelings stand in the way of work, but she just can’t help herself. Matt is just so cute and the way he runs things, has just gotten her infatuated with him. It is nothing more then a crush and she knows that he probably isn’t interested. She doesn’t think anyone would want an insecure, defenseless girl who can’t stand up for herself. She is showing it right here, not making Brian do more. All she would have to do is stand up to him and ask him to help her track down Matt, but she doesn’t want to because she is scared he will figure out her crush for Matt and just blow it off as puppy love. “Did you need anything else?” Brian asks, starting to get annoyed by Tabby. He can tell she wants him to do more, but it isn’t his problem. Matt is nothing more than another hotshot sent down here by Peter Benson to get a quick fix to this sleeper cell. “I am just worried,” she replies, trying to remain strong. “Tabby, I am going to tell you this flat out. Jackson is only down here to try to fix a situation we could all handle without him. All he is, is a hotshot agent who thinks he is great because he is in PCW. What a joke that is anyways. A wrestler?” Clenching her fists, Tabby’s eyes begin to burn holes in Brian. Her calm composer has changed to one of anger and rage now. “What he does in his free time is his business, but I am so glad you are going to help me. We should have broken up a long time before we did.” She tries to deliver the only type of attack she can, but it has no effect on Brian as he leans back in his leather office chair and crosses his arms over his chest. “Well if that is all Ms. Martin, then I believe we have nothing else to discuss.” Turning on her heels, Tabby storms out of the cubical. “Asshole,” she says under her breath, just loud enough to let him make out what she said. He only smiles as she walks away, but he never takes his eyes off of the curvy woman. *** Location: UnknownDate: April 22, 2003Time: 3:30 am SMACK! Matt is awoken, by a stinging backhand to his all ready bruised face. Struggling to open his swollen eyes, Matt stretches, trying to loosen his stiff as a board muscles. He is still tied up in the small room, the dirt floor; wet under his feet due to the puddle of blood and vomit- his of course. Everything is the same as it was just a few short hours ago. His clothes are gone, his body is covered in blood and sweat and worst of all, his arms and legs are starting to go out on him. How am I still alive? That last beating should have killed me, but it doesn’t matter because I can’t last much longer. If these beatings continue, I am going to dehydrate quickly, or just die from blood loss. I don’t want to die and leave Haley alone. I want to protect her and I want to make a name for myself in the PCW. I want to make it to Turmoil and stand toe to toe with the three new comers and show them what PCW is all about, but I am never going to get that chance. I am never going to get the chance to win another match in PCW again. Feeling the rope cut into his wrists more, Matt tries to stand on his legs, but it is hard. His muscles are sore and his feet are cut. Every time he applies more pressure to them, the mix of blood and vomit enters the cuts, sending sharp pain through his body. Even with all his extensive training, this pain is almost unbearable and it isn’t helping matters that is body took a beating last Turmoil against the Overweighter. With no time to recover this is becoming an unwanted strain on his body. “Agent Jackson,” the man’s voice fills the room. “How are we feeling?” His voice wrought with contempt. Moving closer to his prey, the man stares into Matt’s brown eyes, with his opal orbs. His breath is hot and sticky against the dried blood and sweat on Matt’s face. Squinting, Matt tries to figure out who the man is, but he doesn’t get anywhere because of the darkness. The man slaps Matt again, causing his lip to split open again. The taste of his own blood rushes into his mouth. He lets his head hang forward, not caring what the man does to him anymore. Death seems like a better option then taking these constant beatings. I still don’t know what they want with me. They haven’t asked any questions or tried to get information. All they have done is beat me. “What do you people want with me?” Matt screams, his voice barely going over a whisper. It is hard for him to talk and even harder for him to breathe. From the constant abuse, his lungs are in bad shape and there is a good chance if he doesn’t bleed to death, his own oxygen supply is going to be cut off. The man just stares at his victim, whose short brown hair is covered in blood and a mess, his barrel of a chest, is covered in cuts and his angular face, is beaten and bruised. The man smiles at his own handy work, knowing there might be some extra money involved for the punishment he is giving to the ex-Ranger. Everything is going to plan, or at least the man believes it is. “I told you I am going to be the one asking the questions,” the man bellows out, giving Matt another crisp slap. “You almost make my job to easy Agent Jackson. Not only do I get to beat you and break you, but I get to take you to PCW and watch other people beat and break you.” Matt trembles at the mention of PCW. How do they know so much about me? I don’t understand how they got so much information about me so quickly. These men are professionals and this is a lot deeper then I first thought. “That is beside the point though, because when this is all over. You are going to understand why you are here, why we are keeping you here and what we plan on doing with you,” the man tells him with a sickening laugh and a shot to the face. “I am going to leave you with three names Matt, I expect that you won’t know who they are being we haven’t allowed you to see anything at all except for that match you fought at Turmoil. Sivasian Krikorian, Stone Burns, James Edwards. Those are the men you are facing this week, but you won’t be able to stand up to them, look at yourself.” The man smiles as he walks towards the door on Matt’s right. He jerks it open, this time no light floods in, the darkness is only met by darkness. The fresh air removes some of the smell of stale vomit, but it still lingers in the air. They are going to let me wrestle on Turmoil again? It makes no sense; they must be trying to kill me on national TV to make a point. No worries though because Krikorian, Burns, and Edward’s don’t compare to me, even in my current state. That is of course if I can live until Turmoil… 4:00 AM
