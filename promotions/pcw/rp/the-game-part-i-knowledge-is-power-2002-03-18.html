THE GAME - Part I - Knowledge Is Power

18 March 2002

Date: 3-18-02 Song listening guide: ANY song listed below in the RP. LEGAL DISCLAIMER: This promo series is IC with OOC qualties. This promo series may also contain partial nudity, semi-censored mature language, violence so extreme it borders on cartoonish, broken and shattered e-fed rules of engagement, people making embarrassing jackasses of themselves as well as others, lude behavior, grade school-quality bullying, tilde-abusing, and most importantly of all, Kayfabe-breaking aplenty! Would you expect anything less with K-Dub, End, and B.S. in da hizzzz-ouse!? Viewer/Parental/Match Writer discretion is strongly advised. In other words? {Beavis Voice} Huh huh huh. This is gonna be cool! Huh huh huh. BOBBY STORMBRINGER AND PRIMETIME CENTRAL THE GAME - Part I - Knowledge Is Power Then.... B.S., 'The End' Skylar Green, Young Tiger, and Duzza all end up gathering in the middle of the court a few minutes later for a quick Eternal Wrestling reunion that mostly entails of making fun of those sorry sumb!tches Jax and Shane Wethers. Things begin to settle down as more PTCers and more members of the general public file in to this event for charity and take their seats over the next half hour. Ten bucks for the general public, five to those that don't participate in the entertainment. So if a wrestler buys a ticket and then participates? They get a refund. What great motivation! We can fade to black safely for now. Because The Phantom Booker himself repells from the ceiling and his connected cables into his pre-designated spot on top of the rafters. TPB continues to do his best Sting impression, panty hose-on-da-head and all. Now all is right with the world. Until Syko the Goblin decides to show up. Chris Matthews suddenly cups his hands around his mouth and screams for no apparent reason: MATTHEWS: And baby.. that's no b.s.!! B.S. responds by cupping his hands together as well while he chats with Duzza about who the finest half-white / half-black people in the world really are. STORMBRINGER: Heh. Thanks, man! No really- BAH~! You know the rest! And now.... * * * --3-16-02, Saturday afternoon, 5:31 pm-- Our scene fades up from black like all scenes have to. However it does not fade up to the beginning of the Primetime Central charity basketall game like all of you undoubtedly expected to. After flying straight home from his GTT match, Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer has set up an update for his growing legions of marks. Er, fans. This way people can watching the episodes of the basketall game and still keep up on the young man's budding wrestling career. So our scene really fades up on the cluttered basement of Bobby Stormbringer by way of a handheld camcorder of some sort. The picture quality and the shaky footage are the dead giveaways. A basement in Florida, you ask? In return I ask you to suspend your beliefs since yours truly is nostalgic for those old dingy basements from Connecticut, back in the day. The camcorder records in a panning view. Past the moving boxes. Past the hordes of records, cassette tapes, CDs, and more than anything else... videotapes. So many videotapes in wooden stands and bookcases that it boggles the mind. From floor to ceiling. Completely covering three of the four walls with the only exceptions being windows. In Florida they are the kind of windows that you open with a small rolling lever. The shot switches to a close up of these videotapes from the eight-year veteran wrestler and some of the labels are simply incredible. 'Terror.. Mega Wrestling Federation.. 1999 to 2000.' 'Educator.. MWF.. 1999.' 'Trent Storm.. fWo/Thrillfests 3 + 4.. 1999 to 2000.' 'Justin Sane.. fWo.. (year unknown from buyer)' 'Dan Hollywood.. PCW.. 2001.' 'Jacob Radder.. EW.. 2001.' This next one is seemingly a box set. The pen coloring is red instead of black as if it has a special meaning to it. 'Chris Carpenter.. GWF/MWF/Thrillfest 3 winner.. 1998 to 2000. Entire MWF Section of Career.' Back to black ink again. 'Corey Williams.. PWA.. 2001 (Number One Student).' 'Tyler Nelson Pre-CEO promos.. PCW.. 2000 to 2001.' You've obviously gotten the picture by now. The camcorder slowly turns to the right to show Mr. Prime Time himself. Hey. He has the two MWF Primetime Championships to prove it if you doubt the claim. Bobby is skimming his finger through some tapes about three stands down the line. The camcorder shot switches to over his shoulder. The videotapes are indiscernable from this distance however. STORMBRINGER: Ah. Finally. Here it is! B.S. pulls the videotape out of the stand carefully, sleeve and all. He convieniently pulls it up close to where we can see it. The label reads the following: 'Tempest. Thrillfest 4 Tourney win over T. Storm and NAD. 2000.' 'Nad' was only the nickname B.S. gave the drunken fool that was friends of the TF owner. Of the man who should of been kicked out a round or two earlier for his horrid backstage behavior and constant b!tching when there was no place or time for it. THat was how Thrillfest was run, only partially fairly. The owner's friends were pushed to the moon regardless of talent. by the consultation... ...of one semi-retiredBobby Stormbringer. He shortened his name to Bobby Storm at the time in a move to pander to the peasants and the idiots in the locker room that just kept calling him 'Bobby Storm' anyway! Bunch of jackasses. B.S. was prought in at the last minute as a 'Special Consultant to the Owner' since B.S. was forced into temporary retirement after one of several knee surgeries and found himself with loads of time on his hands while recovering. B.S. even signed up for the first round but was forced to pull out by his doctors before he went and did something really stupid. Now after all this time B.S. has caught up to Tempest again on a professional level with him being in Primetime Championship Wrestling and Tempest finally signing up with 'sitcom spinoff' Old School Wrestling. Such a small world we live in, isn't it? B.S. takes the sleeve off and sticks it under his armpit. B.S. takes a green pen out of his tan shorts' pockets and puts it in his mouth. After biting off the cap and spitting it down to the ground, B.S. scribbles on the tape. The additions are bolded. Now it reads: 'Tempest. OSW/Thrillfest 4 Tourney winner o. T. Storm and NAD. 2000.-2002' B.S. looks at the top of the tape and he smiles at the fact that only around a third of it has been recorded over. Then he briefly talks to himself like most other unstable people do. STORMBRINGER: Awesome. Still have ta buy some more blanks but not 'til Wednesday. The Showstopper puts the classic videotape back in its sleeve and he now puts the capless pen back in his pocket. Bobby smoothes out the black 'Dead Aim' Aimz t-shirt from NTICW that he finally got his hands on recently. Of course the clothing is already outdated. B.S. makes his way around a pile of empty boxes and ducks underneath a large pipe. He advances upon his Television and VCR setup against the far wall as we can hear his bare feet smack against the concrete flooring below him. B.S. sets the Tempest tape down on the little stack of tapes he already has on the television. Ones that must of been watched recently. Aimz, Drake Maxwell, Meanstreak, Sebastian Toomes, Zero, and Jeremy 'Krayzie' Howard. Anything from Skylar Green, The End, would definitely be up there if he would of cut a promo in the last few days but he has not done so. Stormbringer pulls out the one labeled 'Zero' and he lightly pushes it into the correct slot on his VCR. B.S. sits down in the leather office chair right in front of the messy configuration. Instead of turning on the TV or starting the VCR he turns back towards the camcorder. STORMBRINGER: Hello there folks. Bobby Stormbringer here.. now this may be a bit strange but what you'll be seeing here will be shown for PCW and for the Global Title Tourney. However if you're watching this as a promo for the tournament, then we've got a long way to go. As long as I am in the Tournament the b-ball game from yesterday is a GTT exclusive. So right here, right now I will do the all-encompassing thing and talk about not only the tourney but also things going down in OSW. Hope I made that clear enough fo' yas. B.S. takes a glass from the side of the stand that the television is sitting on and then he takes a swig from it before putting it back. The liquid is clear as if it is ice water or Sprite (tm). Bobby licks his lips that are dry from the March Florida heat before continuing. STORMBRINGER: First the Tournament. As cool as going head to head with Trent Storm in Thrillfest three was... I've never had an experience like this. Ritchie Syxx, an OSWer and I guy that I've respected for a long time. Then Brian Gaines who was most likely a jobber with way too much damn hype. Sorry kid but there is only one Tyler Nelson. If there wasn't we'd all be in some serious sh!t, I'll tell ya. Then Ben J. Pailthorpe. A former GWF World Heavyweight Champion. So now my career reaches yet another new plateau in these last seven months. Defeated a wrestler who has won a World Championship. Never quite pulled it off before, oddly enough. Even in the Cesspools. That's B.S. and CHZ code speak for Independent Feds that can't seem to stay open, just so you know. STORMBRINGER: Hell of a match, better effort than Syxx put in before. Normal human beings just can't handle The Perfect Storm, however. That Maxwell bastid is just a freak of nature. Must have iron rods in his neck or something. So next I take on another man that made an OSW member fall. Now a free agent and probably the second most talented next to K-Dub, naturally. I'm talking about Zero, or whatever he said his real name was. He and Robert Renfruw are the two guys that have stunned me the most, both with being in the ring and with their promo quality. That's why I joined up. Lots more fame and expanding my knowledge of those outside the 'Big Three'. That being PC-Dub, OSW, and NTICW. Now that CCW is finally on a network they'll challenge to join into that elite group, but that's the facts the way they stand today. Zero will no doubt be the toughest test yet. I wish m' sis Amy didn't pull out to pay more attention to NTICW and now OSW.. or it may of been a family squabble come this Saturday. But still this is the most deserving bracket five final round in my opinion, Zero and I. B.S. grins for a short moment at the thought of hisdream match going down someday, but in a professional and sportsmanlike way.. Amy Campbell versus Bobby Stormbringer. STORMBRINGER: Oh well. I'll have to wait for an interfed PPV when neither of us are very busy. Either that or maybe tag together. It would be up to her since I'm willing to kick almost anyone's arse at any given moment whether the fans like him or not. Or 'her' for that matter. No really. I mean it. B.S. shrugs and shows his impressive Owen Hart-ish fangs in a sneer for a split second. (R.I.P. bro) STORMBRINGER: Ah yep. You're wondering about Randy I bet. The f*cket has disappeared. I mean off the earth. He's trying to sell the family home to the highest bidder. His wife? Nowhere to be seen. Am I surprised? No. But for some reason the bastid loves urning my life upside down at least once every couple of years. I've looked through it backwards and forwards with my lawyers endlessly and our other birth certificates are legit. The odds are too low for anything else. Randy and I are the biological twin sons of Amy Campbell's father. Go freggin' figure. Way too coincidental, man... B.S. sighs to himself at the torment of The Wheel Of Fate before he takes another sip of his drink. STORMBRINGER: Then there's Drake Maxwell and The End. Skylar's been as silent as he is blind and Maxwell..... Max is Max. I don't even wanna hang around with these f*ckers for too long since I got more important things to do. Like Extreme and/or TV belts to win. A sister to protect. A tournament to win. A 'Perfect Storm' to reveal. Not my finishing move... don't worry you'll get it come Turmoil. Just like that Badge is gonna get it. Why can't you people stay out of my business!? Yes, I realize that I am shining brighter than all those around me but that's still no damn excuse. I had Clyde beaten and rather easily. I was surprised at the ease of it but not the fact that it happened. Clyde is yesterday's news, which is exactly what I said before. Badge is the same way. Red Rock? Good idea. Don't come running this way for trouble this quick. I may shake off your ring rust, but I'll also crack your neck in the meantime. Don't worry. You can come back in two months like Valentine has. Until Turmoil then, Badger. Time to dance one more time. You're a big warmup for the Fusion four way I got for the honor of facing Aki. A good warmup. Since you're better than any of those guys in the match. Except me. I'll show you that Tuesday. Enough ranting I have scouting to do. You'll see the b-ball game during GTT hours. Here's your obligitory tease. The special rules, the introduction, and things starting off with.... a bang. Oh yeah. As far as the Tempest tape? Well. Just reminding myself for what Amy's in for. Tempest tore up Thrillfest like no other, and not even Meanie should take her lightly now that she has two years more experience. After all... Knowledge is power. Absolute knowledge is absolute power. And baby? That's no b.s.! Bobby turns towards the television and he turns on the Zero promo from the third round while the scene fades out for the PCW version of this latest creative effort. TO BE CONTINUED.
