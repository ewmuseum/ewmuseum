Revenge Saga Part 10: Reflection...

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/6/02 Crichton sat on the chair by his bed, with his feet resting upon the window ledge. The room is pitch black, except for the moonlight seeping in through the half-opened blinds. The exhaled smoke from is cigarette is emphasised by the delicate light of the moon, as he lifts the half-full bottle of Budweiser and takes a very long sip indeed. He places the bottle back onto the bedside table, looking over his shoulder briefly at the motionless body of Jaden lying a sleep in his bed. A small smile etches its way upon his face, as continues to glare out of the window, contemplating the actions over the last twenty-four hours. Upon finding the remains of the woman known as Ms. Zemoff, Crichton had been in a good mood for the rest of the day. Poetic Justice had taken place, the exact same thing that had happened to Crichton all those months ago had now happened to Fleming and Crichton couldn’t be happier. Crichton thought about the reaction Fleming would have when he found mutilated body of the woman he loved and it made him happy. Crichton realised that this could be conceived as ‘sick’ or ‘perverted’ to have these feelings, but what could he say? Fleming and people like him had made Crichton this way and he planned to punish them all for it. Now that would be poetic justice in its truest form. Could he really leave it at that, though? Could he just leave Fleming to remain alive? After all of his heinous crimes and the misery he has caused hundreds upon hundreds of people. Crichton knew that the only crime Aeryn committed was falling in love with him and she paid the price for this. Fleming on the other hand had murdered and maimed hundreds of people, yet he continued to evade death. Crichton knew that he himself was no angel and would probably end up in the bad part of hell himself, but he sure wasn’t going until he made sure that Fleming was there first. He now hoped that his relationship to the lovely Jaden wouldn’t end as disastrously as his previous one. However, he decided that it was inevitable, since all of the people he knew and cared about had a tendency to die. He intended to keep Jaden as far away from that life as possible, although he didn’t think that he would be successful at that particular intention. When came back from the military compound he had been as evasive as possible, not wanting her to know anything that could potentially get her into trouble in the future. However, from what he gathered she already knew too much. He had only been half-listening when she told him what she had been up to all day, but upon hearing specific sections of her comments his attention was now alerted. Apparently she saw his pictures of Aeryn littered around the place, the only real possessions he had these days. After this she went onto explain her confrontation of Hank Sun, Aeryn’s father and he informed her of what exactly what he was up to. He felt it the right thing to do, to inform Hank of Aeryn’s untimely death. Hank understood the situation and respected Crichton for being a man and informing him of the situation. Crichton also vowed to Hank that he would avenge her death. Hank tried to talk him out of it, but failed terribly. Jaden did have the bare essentials of what he was up to, but he didn’t want to elaborate upon the subject any further.Truth be told he couldn’t decide what to do. Should he attempt to hunt Fleming down and kill him? Or should he wait and let Fleming be filled with the same rage as he? The second idea struck him as the best, but he didn’t want to be thinking about such matters. After his victory at Tuesday Turmoil his hopes were high. The Forgotten Ones, now part of the political machine wiped the floor with both Duzza and Jordan Shabazz and consequently won the match in a record of 12 seconds. Andy Murray very graciously allowed him to pin Shabazz and he added another victory to his belt. Life in PCW was good for him nowadays, in fact his wrestling career was the only constant thing in his life at the moment. He had found real friends within the Political Machine, especially in the form of Jaden. The situation within the ranks of PCW had forced him and Murray to acquire more allies, to ensure their dominance upon the PCW roster. A lot of other stables had been forming over the past couple of weeks and Crichton knew that something was going to go down within the weeks ahead. All the problems stemmed from the roster cut a few weeks ago, whether that was a good decision or not it had caused a great series of divides within the PCW roster and it appeared that everyone on the roster were beginning to take sides. The Forgotten One’s were down to two members and clearly were not strong enough to survive with upcoming war for supremacy. Therefore when Politically Incorrect approached them, he and Murray decided that he did want to be part of the future of the PCW and chose their sides accurately. However, now it was a different story all together, mainly because of Politically Incorrect and their personal war with Tyler Nelson. Soon, the two members of PI, Powell and Lovecraft would be forced to fight one another, for the PCW extreme title. Crichton only hoped that the division in the Political Machines ranks was only temporary, but he knew otherwise. This wasn’t a good thing at all. Nothing was for certain and by thinking about it, and debating every direction the situation could ascend to, was only torturing himself. He quickly shook the thoughts from his mind and began to think forward to his match on Friday against the mysterious newcomer named The Supreme Machine. Crichton didn’t know much about his opponent, but he could safely assume that the task at hand would not be easy, for the PCW roster cut, as the old saying went, ‘cut the fat’ and only the elite stayed. Crichton knew that he was one of the elite, hell after all he’d been through how could he not be? The basic fact was that mere minutes before the PCW roster was fired he had obtained the TV title. Now, even though he had been rehired, the TV title remained vacant, but from his point of view, it was only a matter of time before the title was once again his. The match or matches to decide the TV champion was obviously imminent and it was only a matter of time before the title that rightfully belonged to him, would be back around his waist. Crichton stood from his seated position, as the dawn sun, pierced the blinds, shining upon Jaden’s peaceful face. Crichton grabs his knee-long leather jacket from the back of the chair and slips it on. He had wasted a day at the military compound, fighting it out with Fleming, so he decided to head over to the gym early for extra training. He grabs his bag from the table and heads out of the door, stopping briefly to admire Jaden before leaving the motel room. ************ Epilogue… The military base was nothing less than devastated at the hands of the Thunderbolts. Specifically the Thunderbolts known as Jolt and Mach III. The two Thunderbolts gather around as medics tend to Fleming’s broken ribs, just another resultant of this little battle with Crichton. Fleming watches as other medics swarm the area, tending to other military officials caught up in the carnage, which took place mere minutes ago. As a medic wraps a bandage around the wounded Fleming, a soldier approaches his position from the east; the direction in which Crichton escaped not thirty minutes before. Fleming eyes the soldier warily as he comes ever closer. “State your situation soldier!” Fleming demands, slightly worried at the soldiers urgency. “Sir, we found the charged remains of a woman a couple of meters from the east…” The soldier declares pausing slightly. “Go on!” Fleming demands, attempting to figure out exactly what the soldier is trying to get at. “…I…I think it might be, erm…” The soldier breaks off, trying to figure a way to tell them man, something so devastating. “Spit it out man!” Fleming roars, getting incredibly pissed off. “The body appears to be the remains of Ms. Zemoff sir!” The soldier states bluntly. Fleming was physically taken back the revelation. “Er…Miss…Zemoff..Dead!” Fleming stated, to himself more than anyone else. Fleming pulls himself up, using one of the medics as leverage. “Sir, you shouldn’t be walking…” the medic declares, breaking off when he realises that his words will have no effect whatsoever. “Stow it!” He states “Soldier, show me where her body is!” The soldier nods in agreement. “This way sir!” He states pointing to the east. “John Crichton will pay for his actions! I guarantee it!” Fleming declares, preparing himself for the gruesome sight that was inevitably awaiting him.
