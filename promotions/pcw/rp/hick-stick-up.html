Hick Stick-Up

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.06.03 “No Abner, I don’t think if they changed the named to “Friday Fishin’”, you’d have any better of a chance of defeating Jackson.” “No Abner, if they changed the name of the event to “Cloud-69”, it wouldn’t be any more funny… However, if Flaymin Aymin said it, for some reason, it would be…” “No Abner, the “Agent” part in his name has nothing to do with chemical cleaning materials.” “No Abner, “Agent Orange” sucked…never, ever mention his name again.” “No Abner, if you’re at the top of the roster and start going down, winning the “Setting Sun” title would not be an honor.” “No Abner, that’s five…five fingers on a hand…” Abner: Ooohhh… Abner nods his head, pulling the cell-phone away from his ear for a moment, so as to scratch his head. Abner: Well, alright then…don’t believe I do have any more questions…Cuzin Ed? Looking through the mirror, Abner looks up to see Cuzin Ed nod his head in the backseat. Abner: Yes….uh-huh…yes….yep…uh-huh… Well alright, I’m gonna get headed, k? Good. Alright, yep… Bye. A chattery beep emits from some un-seeable hole in the phone as Abner turns off the receiver, and hangs up. He tosses the phone aside allowing it to fall gently on the cushioned seat of the passenger-side area. Cuzin Ed: Yapper? Abner: Yep. Never shuts his mouth. Cuzin Ed: Bastard. Abner: Bastard. Abner reaches up to unbuckle his seatbelt. After successfully completing the procedure, he reaches back and holds out an open hand to Cuzin Ed. Cuzin Ed: Chocolate? Abner grunts. Through hick-a-communication, Cuzin Ed somehow successfully interprets the response, and within moments, places a specifically requested Dunkin Donut in Abner’s outstretched hand. Abner pulls it back, and within moments, devours the donut, wiping the crumbs away from his goatee, and pleads for another. Cuzin Ed obliges. Abner thankfully requests on after another, until, to Cuzin Ed’s dismay, the box is empty. Like Abner, he carelessly tosses it aside. Abner: Where to? Cuzin Ed: I don’t know. Suddenly, without reason, Abner slams on the break. Their heads buck forward, and regretfully, Abner remembers throwing off the seatbelt, and bangs his head against the dashboard. Cuzin Ed’s more secure than Abner, but still, he’s shaken up. What was left of the coffee now paints the inside of Abner’s windshield, and the radio is decorated in colorful array of donut sprinkles. Cuzin Ed folds his arms across his massive chest. Cuzin Ed: Christ! What was that for? Abner reaches up and rubs a finger across a seer in his flesh. A thin, concentrated stream of blood runs down the length of his face and onto his chest. He unthinkingly absorbs it with his hat, which he, of course, gets tossed aside as well. Abner: Nothin’… Sorry, Ed, it’s just that…well, I’m under a lot of stress right now. Looking back he sees that Cuzin Ed is not impressed. He simply snorts, further wrapping his arms around his chest, and angrily staring his elder in the eye. Abner: Oh, c’mon man! It’s not a big deal… Cuzin Ed: Yeah, well, it is to me. Abner: … Cuzin Ed: Yeah, well, you could’ve killed me… Giving up, he lets out a long overdue sigh. He reaches up and places a steady hand on Abner’s shoulder, comforting his cousin. Cuzin Ed: Look, Ab…it’s just…it’s just that, well, it was only a week ago when you made an announcement to the wrestling world of your intentions. And do you remember what you said? Abner: Yeah, what? Cuzin Ed: You said to take it easy. You said to stop being so competitive, stop being so damn self-righteous, and that that’s what’s wrong with that company…that’s why they’ve gone so wrong. And I for one agree! And now look at you, you’ve given yourself the antidote to yer own damn medicine! Hypocrite! Abner scowls, but he’s right. The last few days, he has in fact taken himself too seriously. He has, of course, taken in his daily fill of beer…the maximum intake of a man of his physical stature…but of course, that’s not enough. Abner: Meh…you could be right… Cuzin Ed: You know I am. Abner: …Yeah, so what’s it to ya? Cuzin Ed: It’s “too me” in that I’m stuck with yer sorry ass on this tour ‘til Cloud-Nine comes, July 13th…well over a month…what is that, five or six weeks? Abner: Yeah, something like that. Cuzin Ed: And I want to have a good time. So, here we are, on the last trip on the open road ‘til the middle of next month sometime, and yet, you won’t enjoy yerself. I mean, c’mon, Ab! She’s yer baby…she’s yer first, only, and most beautiful truck, and this is the last time you’ll see her in weeks! Abner shifts into high gear, rocking the old body as they pick up speed. They round a tight corner, and Abner shifts again, this time maxing her out. Cuzin Ed: ‘At a boy. But to Cuzin Ed’s surprise, instead of going straight (as they had planned), Abner takes another left, and increases speed. Cuzin Ed: What are we doin’? Abner: Going to buy a gun. Cuzin ED: Oh, that’s nice, goin’ to buy a…WHAT!?! Abner peers back at Cuzin Ed through the mirror and gives him a wink. A big, mischievous smile comes over his face, as he slows the car down, preparing to enter a driveway. Cuzin Ed’ eyes get big, and he puts out his hands in a defensive position. Cuzin Ed: Now what a minute now, let’s think clearly now, Ab. Abner: Nah! Do that an’ I probably won’t do it… Cuzin Ed: Exactly. Now Abner, you know that that’s illegal! You don’t want any part of that. Abner: Sure I do. Cuzin ED: Well, I’m sure you do…it’s just that it’s not proper… See, with your felony charge, if you get caught doing somethin’ like this…even attempting to…you’re screwed. Abner: I know. Cuzin Ed: So where’s the fun in that? Abner: The danger. Cuzin Ed sighs, folding his arms over his chest again, knowing that he’s not going to win. Cuzin Ed: Alright, but let’s make it quick! Abner: Done. And true to his word, he swerves at the end of the driveway, and makes a makeshift parking spot between two adjacent vehicles. Abner: You ready? Cuzin Ed: Ready. Pulling the keys out of the ignition, Abner shuts off the car and pockets the controls. He opens the door, and steps out of the car, the crackling sound of pebbles under his boots filling his ears, and momentarily attracting his keen senses as he makes his way across the parking lot. Ed, dragging along behind, is sure to drag as much dead weight as slowly, and as luxuriously as possible. Maybe he can foil the plan. Abner: C’mon, pick up yer feet boy! But probably not. His cousin’s on to him, and always has been. And so as they approach the entranceway to a small wooden hut with a sign above the doorway that reads “Crazy Jimmie’s Firearms” engraved in woodwork, he keeps his thoughts to himself. Person: Howdy. Abner and Cuzin Ed peer up into the eyes of a stranger. A thin, medium-sized man of about their age walks by, with short black hair and deep, piercing brown eyes. Abner tips his hat and does a mini-bow, Cuzin Ed behind. Abner: Mornin’. Person: Mornin’. Cuzin Ed: Howdy. Abner reaches for the door handle, giving it a solid tug. Abner: Ugh! For a man who bench-presses 340, this seems to be a problem. Finally, the creaky old door succumbs to its fate, and Abner steps inside. Cuzin Ed, who is behind him, steps up and begins to mumble under his breath. Cuzin Ed: “Crazy Jimmie’s”…huh. BANG. But the store lives up to its name. Abner drops to the dirt, and Cuzin Ed takes cover behind the nearest rack of mags, as an unknown shooter unloads. BANG. BANG. A harmful mess of pellets goes whizzing past Abner’s head, spraying the wall behind him. They rip at the wooden infrastructure and send deadly slivers flying off everywhere. Abner: Jesus Christ! Suddenly, a man comes into view. He hops around the counter on crutches, dives to the floor, and rolls across to an adjacent aisle, standing and training his weapon on Abner. Besides for the crazy look in his eye, he’s your typical ol’ Vermont hoot. He’s an older man, pushing sixty, with a long gray beard and an aging set of teeth. An old, yellow straw hat sits atop his balding head. Man: Crazy varmints! Abner: We ain’t no varmints, we’re customers!!! Man: Oh… He smiles over at Abner, and lowers the barrel of his long, black shotgun. Tossing the gun aside, he snaps his suspenders in the traditional Vermont greeting. Abner, seemingly un-phased, nods his head and takes a fearless step forward. Abner: Easy there, old timer. The toothless wonder grins, winking back at Abner. Man: Gave you quite a scare, huh? Abner: That you did… …Ed? Cuzin ED? Cuzin Ed: Here. Cuzin Ed appears on the opposite side of the store, rifle in-hand. The old man nods in acknowledgment, turning back to greet the fattened farmer. Man: Uh-huh. I see, you come prepared, eh boys? Cuzin Ed: Damn straight. Cuzin Ed undoes the clip, emptying the bullets, and tosses the gun aside. From his last position, he was clearly in sight of the old man’s back, probably in an offensive hold. Nerd: Wow! From out of the blue, a light-haired, pimply-faced kid wearing black pants, a white tee shirt, and thick black glasses comes into view. He’s on the shorter, side and stands in front of Cuzin Ed, his head coming up to the farmer’s massive shoulders, and looks up in awe. There’s a sparkle in his eye, and as he takes another step forward, a smile cracks his face. Cuzin Ed: Uh…hey… He takes a step back, bewildered by the nerd. Abner laughs at this, and rushes his friend’s aid. Abner: And who might you be? The nerd straightens out his pocket protector, snickering at the two (well, three) rednecks. Nerd: I’m Jason…and is that a Weatherbee Magnum you have? The nerdy boy steps forward and bends over to collect up Cuzin Ed’s arm’s. He holds the long, black rifle on high for a moment, before bending down with it and unfastening the bolt with his fingers. Nerd: Wow! A .273 caliber, model 34-A, Weatherbee Magnum Special! Black stock, plastic casing, five-shot clip with fiber-optic sighting, 1/13-inch barrel twist with a three-ply safety device! How fascinating! Abner turns his head back toward the doorway and eyes his truck. “Is it worth it?” he thinks, letting his hand slide down his tired face. But he can’t help but giggle, turning back to the circus. Cuzin Ed shoots him a look, and Abner nods back in reply. So turning to face the owner, the two boys decide it’s best (dare I say “safest”?) to hurry up, get what they came here for, and leave! Hippie: Hey, dudes. “TTT” Abner thinks, whirling in around in surprise. But nay, it’s only a boy, and a harmless, hippie one at that. Abner: Hippies… He shivers, as the boy leans over the counter, blowing smoke in his face. Abner reaches up and wipes the fumes away, and by the smell of it, he concludes, it’s obviously not a cigarette. Owner: May I? Hippie: Yeah…sure. Sighing, the hippie reaches out and allows the old timer to take a nice, long drag. He’s a tall, lanky, long-haired kid with a thick black beard, and large, glazed brown eyes. He’s wearing a plain black tee shirt and tan colored cargo shorts. Through his clothing, cigarette burns can be seen everywhere, skin protruding through the cloth. He smiles, leans back on the counter, and takes another long, purposeful drag. Abner: Jesus Christ, hippie! What’s a guy like you doin’ working’ in a place like this? Folding his arms across his chest, Abner steps up to the counter. The hippie simply shakes his head, looking down at his feet. Even if he is offended, he’s not showing it. Hippie: Hey, man, a job’s a job… Owner: Yeah, he’s right. Hippie: And if that’s what I gotta do to bring home cash, than I guess, well, that’s what I gotta do… Abner shrugs, lowering his stature and sucking in his chest, sorry for being so unkindly toward the hippie. Abner: Yeah, I guess. Just then, the door opens, and in steps the stranger from outside. As he looks up to meet the guys of those already inside the shop, he lowers his head to the floor, slowly turns and softly shuts the door. He nods his head in recognition his companions. Stranger: Hey. Owner: Howdy. Abner and Cuzin ED: ‘Mornin’. But the stranger obviously feels unwelcome, and hides behind the first row of guns he can make it to, carrying on about his business. Cuzin Ed flashes Abner a quick look, and in response, he simply shrugs. Abner: Well… Looking over at the owner… Abner: …Cuzin Ed, I do believe we’d better hurry up and find what we’ve came for, so as to get on out of here… Cuzin Ed: Yep. I reckon you’re right, Abner. Abner: Wouldn’t wanna cause these gentlemen too much pain… Owner: Well nonsense…I mean, bullshit! Take all the time you want. After flashing the owner a nervous smile, Abner turns to Cuzin Ed, and together, they walk off to the other side of the room while carefully watching their backs the whole time. Nerd: Oh!!! What are you looking for? The nerd pops up in front of Abner, darting across from a corresponding row. Shuffling through the rows, making his way by the tens of long metal racks of rifles, shotguns and handguns of all types, Abner ignores him. Nerd: Hey, if you’re looking for a good handgun, I strongly recommend the Colt .45 dual-action, carbine… He trails off as there’s a loud, painful yell, followed by a an unmistakable thud. Abner: Shut-up! Abner simply picks the young nerd up by the shoulders, and sets him to the side, and continues pilgrimage on to the spot of the commotion. Nerd: Uh…sorry… Abner: Now what the hell is this all about?!? Abner’s worst fears are realized… Well actually, Rosie O’Donnell is not greeting him at his mother’s house with a small bouquet of flowers…but it’s pretty bad. Cuzin Ed stand up chest heaving, and face red. His fists are at his side, though, as Abner notes, they are rolled into tight, rigid balls. Stranger: Nobody move! The odd man they had met on the outside is holding a short, black barreled gun in one hand, and a bag in the other. Abner notices that he has a small dent in his cheek, which has started to swell, as if someone has cuffed him… Cuzin Ed: Prick. Yep. Someone punched him alright…and someone paid, too. A thin stream of blood runs down the back of Cuzin Ed’s neck. The robber must’ve beat him over the head with the butt of the gun, Abner guesses. Stranger: Shut-up! And you…you get over behind the counter with the rest of ‘em! He motions Abner toward the back with his gun, and Abner obediently complies. Rounding the corner, he begins to think to himself “things can’t get much worse”, until, looking down at his feet, sees that the owner, somehow, has gotten in position of another weapon… “Great, just what I need” Abner mumbles under his breath, “…another shootout…” Stranger: What did you say!?! Abner: Nothing’… Stranger: Well shut yer mouth! …And you… He menacingly waves his gun at the hippie… Stranger: Hurry up, and put the money in the bag! Hippie: Whoa, calm down, due! Half-laughing, the wasted hippie reaches forward and presses a button on the register screen, and the drawer flies open. He takes his time, slowly, methodically tossing the cash and a random assortment of change into the bag. He looks up and smiles at the man who’s robbing him, seemingly amused by the whole incident. Stranger: I said hurry it up now! Owner: Now Mr., we don’t want no trouble… Stranger: Then hurry the fuck… Owner: Okay, okay, okay! …You heard him, Jimmie, give the man his money! Hippie: Right. Sweet, dude! Still, he takes his time, and tosses the cash in the bag, being sure to break the rubber-bands of the bundles of ones, putting the bills in separately, one at a time. The man is really starting to get annoyed. But suddenly, from out of the shadows pops the nerdy boy. Stranger: What the…?... Nerd: Wow! That’s a Glock 38., Model 24 Special, eh! That’s so cool… Adding… Nerd: …Too bad it’s not loaded… Looking down at the same time, every sees, to their surprise a metallic black clip, lying on the floor under the man’s feet… Stranger: Uh-oh… He bends down to retrieve it, but it’s too late… So it goes. And now, only a day later, the felony, Abner, is in possession of a fully automatic rifle, compliments of the owner for saving his store… Only in America. But that’s how it goes, and Abner, down for the count as he always seems to be, someway, somehow, seems to have a way of getting out of stick situations. Luck? I think not. Jackson: You’re next.
