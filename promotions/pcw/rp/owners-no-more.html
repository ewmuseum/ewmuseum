Title: Owners No More! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Whatever today is Money. Cash, dinero, semolians... THE GREENBACKS, DAMNIT, THE GREENBACKS! For anyone asking why Politically Incorrect sold 20 percent of PCW to The Phantom Booker, the answer is simple. The cash. The money paid to Politically Incorrect by the pantyhose clad Booker is undisclosed. But, suffice it to say, for Politically Incorrect to sell, the figure must be in the high double digit millions. All ties with the Ultimate Authority have now been severed. The politicians are owners no more. But, their campaign chests are quite a bit more full. You want an explanation, straight from the horse's mouth? Politically Incorrect are ready, willing, and able to explain. As, of course, they prepare for battle. Matchups against The Phantom Phive and The Ultimate Authority are on the horizon. The leaders of The Political Machine feel somewhat slighted. And now, they are ready to execute their plan. Raise havoc. ::A large meeting room in a local hotel is the site for today's meeting. Politically Incorrect sit around a round table. There are Louis Brown, the Politically Incorrect attorney, Katherine Willis, Lovecraft's intern/Love interest, campaign manager Roger Schultz, and of course, Politically Incorrect themselves. The five individuals sit, cups of coffee in front of them, looking as if they are at an important board meeting.:: William S. Powell: So, how does it feel to be ------ million dollars richer? ::For the security of the transaction, the value of the deal is bleeped out.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: It feels tremendous, Mr. Powell! But, I feel that there is something that must be cleared up before we move on... ::Lovecraft frowns briefly.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: For the past few days, I have listened to people talking of Politically Incorrect 'selling out'. We did not 'sell out'. Instead, we made an intelligent business decision. You see, being 20 percent owners was getting us nothing with Kannon and Nelson as majority owners...as testified by that match I had to fight my partner in... William S. Powell: That I should have won! DAMN THAT COREY WILLIAMS! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Anyway, seeing as how ownership was getting us nowhere, I had Mr. Brown here get in touch with Mr. Booker's attorneys..and before long, we had a contract that paid us handsomely, in exchange for our stock in Primetime Championship Wrestling. In business and politics, one must know when to cut one's losses. William S. Powell: Indeed, and that is why we are no longer part owners of PCW. But, it appears that we will face three members of Booker's Phantom Phive...I believe Ricky Thunder, The Supreme Machine, and Ryan Stephens, if I remember off the top of my head. Ironic that we face the charges of our benefactor, and new business partner, isn't it? Reginald V. Lovecraft: It is indeed. Despite our business deal, it must not be forgotten that our main concern is with our own Political Machine. We will take Andy Murray with us into battle against the Phantom Phive. I am sure Mr. Murray is relishing the opportunity. You see, he has come to realize, as have John Crichton, Mark Makonie, and Jaden, that being a member of the Political Machine carries with it certain perks. The power....the prestige...the.. ::Suddenly, Katherine interjects herself, a thought on her mind.:: Katherine Willis: The ability to screw your own co-workers? William S. Powell: I think only Jaden and Crichton have taken advantage of that... ::Lovecraft and Willis both turn to him, stern expressions on their faces.:: William S. Powell: Did you two REALLY have to remind me that I'm not getting laid?! DID YOU?! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes, I did. William S. Powell: I hate you. No really. I mean it. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Okay, you just used a Bobby Stormbringer quote. Kill yourself. ::Suddenly, Powell's eyes light up.:: William S. Powell: Hey, I hit him with the key to Des Moines, Iowa back in NTICW in a Playboy Mansion Match! That was back when I had Damian Beckett as my partner! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Man, that kid's a sucker for his own career nostalgia... William S. Powell: Whawastha'? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Nothing, nothing....anyway, we also get to fight The Ultimate Authority on Friday, in the form of those two Koran thumpers, The Chef and 'The Uzbek Annhilator' Alexis Parshin. I mean...those two preach more than the evangelical Christians from my native Georgia! William S. Powell: They're even worse than those damned Hare Krishnas! Man, I REALLY REALLY REALLY REALLY HATE Hare Krishnas! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Unresolved childhood issues? ::Powell ignores his partner, instead choosing to speak on a more serious matter.:: William S. Powell: Chef, Parshin...you are both members of Nelson's jury-rigged Ultimate Authority. Goeren's injury has already cripped your conglomerate. Now, you face the leaders of The Political Machine. Two men that loathe and despise your boss, and, by association, each of you. The power of Allah cannot save you from two men out for blood and vengeance. Once again, Politically Incorrect fight together, not apart. And together, we will prove what the world already knows, that we are the best tandem in existance in this company. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Indeed......you will see us on Friday Night. And, you will be made to pay in the Halls of Justice. The Ultimate Authority will be found guilty. And, with any luck, we will be able to carry out the death sentence. For, it shall be Election Day! ::Roger Schultz finally speaks.:: Roger Schultz: And, on Election Day, Politically Incorrect ALWAYS triumphs! ::Fade out to....:: Paid for by the friends of TPM.TPM: Three letters, one absolute power. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
