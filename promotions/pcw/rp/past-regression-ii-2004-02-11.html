Past Regression II

11 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 11th 2004 Note: This is a series of three RPs detailing the background of Afwa, and will be a basis of the Afwa/Jay Pheonix feud It stars: Chief Priest Tainafi Malietoa Tautolo 'Towla' Mintola Sailele Afwa Fades in.. Tainafi is seen entering the shack. Seen asleep in his hammock is the savage himself, the future King of Somata, Afwa Kilmanjaro. Tainafi slowly walks over to Afwa, as he does so we fade in.. 'Wake up Afwa, today is your big day..' Afwa ignores Tainafi. Tainafi then goes outside his shack and sounds the large titanium gong, bringing Afwa out of his slumbersome sleep and causing the 500lbs monster to fall out of his hammock, stange as it is a hole in the floor is not made, Afwa then brushes himself off, staggers up and looks more like a walking zombie then a savage. 'Why you wake me, me have nice sleep.. Me been dreaming..' 'The time we have is short, The Tongan Terror could attack us at any moment and we need to find this Spirtual leader and Afwa you have been chosen to go and find him..' 'Afwa tired, Afwa want sleep..' 'Afwa the lives of your people depend upon it.' 'Afwa real mad.. Afwa have nice dream of fishee and pee-puhl.. Afwa not want to go, Afwa want to stay with his pee-puhl.' 'Afwa, we have discussed this before, you have been chosen and today you and somebody of your choice are to leave to find the mysterious caves where the Spiritual leader should be hiding.' 'Me not sure..' 'Now is not the time for second thoughts. Chose somebody whom you think would be best suited, bearing in mind, nobody knows to what lengths this dangerous mission could extend to..' 'Bannanna, me want Bannanna..' 'Do you mean Tautolo' 'Tautolo magic, he be good..' 'Okay I shall have one of the servents send for him. Now Afwa, I must say I am so pleased that you are doing this, as there is nobody better for this task then you.' 'Me want dead cow..' 'Afwa, I have had the servents prepare for you a light lunch and when you find your bearings, I'm sure you can hunt your own food, as I'd rather think taking a Wild Boar with pineapple pieces stuffed in its mouth would weigh you down and you would be easily spotted by The Barbarian Hoards.' 'Me have disguise..' 'Afwa, you want a disguise? I think that is a brilliant suggestion. What is your plan..' 'Me like Bannanna man..' 'Yes I know you like Tautolo, but what would the disguise be..' Afwa looks around, scratches his head and says 'Me be fishee..' 'Afwa this is a serious mission and I hardly think you would do well disguised as a fish, think of something more logical.' Afwa tries to think, yet being as he is like a dumb animal, he finds it very hard and says 'Hmm.. Me not sure..' Tautolo is then seen walking past the shack, to which Afwa looks at him and smiles saying 'Tautolo magic, me disguise...' 'What Afwa?' Tainafi explains to Tautolo what Afwa is saying, Tautolo then smiles saying.. 'I have an idea. If we both disguise ourselves as members of The Tongan Barbarian Hoard, we should have no problem getting past the access to the other side..' 'Won't that be tricky, as they sound different to us..' 'Afwa is stupid, so he'll not speak, I'll do all the talking and if we get stopped, I shall say I am going home until this war is over. And if that doesn't work, then I'll get Afwa to convert them..' Tainafi smiles and says 'Argh, I see..' 'Where my food, me want dead cow..' 'Afwa, we shall have time for that later, now we have to be heading off, as I'm sure this journey ahead of us is going to be a very daunting task, which at the end, may or may not be what we are after..' 'I shall say to you both good luck and I hope to see you on your return.. Oh, before you go, have you got everything you need..' Afwa says in a confused state.. 'Me not sure, Bannana do it..' Tautolo looks to Tainafi, smiles and says.. 'I have the map, with the location marked out. I also have the disguises, here Afwa, think you can put this on?' Tautolo hands Afwa what appears to be a Barbarin Hoards uniform consisting of a silver breast plate, a pair of XXL silver pants, a circular tiatanium shield and the most evil weapon in all of Somata, the double edged sword as used by 'The Tongan Terror' himself. Afwa looks at Tautolo and quickly Afwa puts the uniform on, it barely fits, yet is a bit tight, so Afwa has to not breath in, otherwise it will fall off, which in Afwa's case is a very difficault task as all he does is breath hot air. Tautolo and Afwa are then seen to the gates of Somata, slowly the two warriors walk out of the city and seen behind them are the Chief Priest Tainafi and Sailele who are both saluting, slowly the cameras fade away from Somata and just in the distance we can see the land of Somata. Meanwhile Afwa and Tautolo are seen walking through the desolate cities which the Barbarian Hoards have destroyed, one of these being the capital of the Barbarian Hoard Empire, Tonshu. Slowly the sun begins to set as the day closes.. The shot then opens up the next morning, as it appears that Afwa and Tautolo have been trailing the city for the past day or so. Slowly Tautolo looks over at what he thinks is a place of rest, yet it appears not. The two men continue their ghastly walk to a place which may never exist, slowly Tautolo begins to decrease his pace, turning to Afwa and saying. 'Have you any idea where we are heading..' Afwa with sweat pouring down his face and hunger pains within says.. 'Afwa no read map, me think me lost..' Tautolo sighs and says.. 'Look, give it here, I'm sure I'll be able to find out where we are supposed to be..' Afwa hands the map to Tautolo who proceeds to scour it.. 'By my calculations, we are lost..' 'Afwa hungry! Afwa want eat meat..' 'I don't know what it it about you, but all you seem to want to do every five seconds is eat.' 'Me can't help it, me Afwa!' 'I'll see what we can do, possibly stop at the next town, that is if I can actually find one.' 'Me real hungry..' 'Afwa your belly shall just have to wait.' 'Afwa no like you, you big meanie head..' To quiet the moans of Afwa, Tautolo takes another look at the map, hoping to find some area whre they can at least stop and see where and if they are heading in the right direction. Tautolo spots on the map, that a small reserve is situated near by, that is if all the animals are not dead.. 'There should be some food up here..' Afwa smiles saying.. 'Afwa catch food?' 'Yes you might have too..' Like a dumb child acting out Afwa says.. 'Great, Afwa like to play. Me catch Wild pig and eat it.. Me tear it apart and see it squirm.. Me Afwa, me mad..' Tautolo sounding a bit out of breath says, looking at the map.. 'I think if we walk another ten paces, we shall come to the place known as Pheonix Rock, it is here Afwa, that a reserve full of Wild Animals is located and also the area where we should find the Spiritual Leader..' Afwa rubs his obese stomache and says.. 'Me find food first..' Tautolo just sighs saying.. 'Don't forget Afwa, you came on this mission to find the Magi, for remember the lives of your people are at stake, the lives of the fellow Islanders..' Afwa points to himself and says.. 'Me Islander, me Afwa..' 'Lets walk a few paces then..' Slowly the two men continue in their path to locate Pheonix rock. Up ahead Tautolo spies a broken wodden bridge.. 'We need to get to the other side..' 'Me have idea..' Tautolo looks interested, well anything would be interesting if it would help them two get across to the other side of the bridge.. 'Yes?' 'Afwa be trampoline! Tautolo jump on Afwa stomache and he be like bird..' 'Afwa somehow, I don't think the bridge will carry your weight..' Afwa stamps his feet saying.. 'This my mission.. I do it..' The earth tremours from the stamping of Afwa's size 34 boot has caused the nearby rock formation to crumble and in a sense was good, as it has made a bridge for Afwa and Tautolo to cross the other side of the bridge.. No sooner does Afwa and Tautolo start to cross, a voice is heard coming from the other side.. 'This land is mine.. All who cross shall pay the price.. Death to all who oppose..' Afwa and Tautolo ignore the voice and continue to cross.. The two slowly step off the rock formation and then seen standing before them is a beautfil reserve called De Pineapple Rock. Afwa looks around and can see a few bisons and some wild pigs running around the large open greenery.. Afwa looks up, smells the air and says.. 'Me like, me want to stay! Me Afwa, me found park!' 'Afwa, there is no time for games, we shall have to kill the first animal that we lay eyes on, skin it and eat it alive, like the carnivores we are..' 'Me like Wild Pig, me like to see them squeel as I go for the kill..' 'Pigs that reminds me of those swines your father used to call Americans, they came from America into our land and tried to steal our food..' 'America, where is that?' 'It is a place that forms part of Samoa.. Yet it not that good..' 'Well I like this America, me think we want to go there..' 'Save your people first, bring about the end of The Tongan Terror and then you might get to see America.. Otherwise you might not get to see anything..' Afwa growls saying.. 'Me going to America..' Tautolo sighs, as he knows Afwa will never get there, but tries to play along with him saying.. 'Not yet..' Afwa says it again.. 'Me going to America..' As the two men begin to argue about America, a rather odd looking figure with dark beady eyes the size of saucers, and a rather elongated frame to him is seen emerging from behind one of the rocks.. The figure points a long finger at Afwa and Tautolo, shaking it in their face, as if it was a curse and says.. 'Dare thee to cross the path of the Lorde..' At once Afwa Kilmanjaro raises his double edged sword and goes to cut off the ear of his adversary.. 'Stand back, Afwa deal with this..' Tautolo stops Afwa, as he doesn't know who this is and he could quite possibly be one of The Tongan Terror's men, so to be err on the side of caution, Tautolo decides to talk.. 'No, I think we can talk, he seems okay..' Afwa looks confused as he wants to cause some pain, yet Tautolo won't let him, so Afwa says 'Me no like to think! Me want to play game.. Me want to kill food..' As Afwa speaks of killing for food, the voice from within says.. 'The animals in this area belong to me..' Afwa looks at the figure and pokes at him saying, 'Who you be..' From out of the shadows the face of the figure is shown, he is seen to have razer blade like teeth, which shine in the light, his face is of a devilish white and we can see that his saucer shaped eyes are as black as ebony.. His voice now changes from that of a gracious being to more of a twisted demonic being.. 'I am the Voice from within.. I am The reason that this part of Somata has not been destroyed, I am the direct ruler of the Northern part of Somata.. My Samoan name is Dominiatica Rustica Salum Polombo, but to all those out here, I am known as The Keeper of the Rock, ancient warlord and mystic in the Seven Scrolls of time, Keeper of the key to the Bottemless Pit and the intergender between Heaven and Hell..' Afwa looks up and says.. 'You exist..' The Keeper of the Rock says in a mennacing way.. 'Though I am here now, the time shall come when I have to move on. Somata is becoming a danger zone and it is only by my own killer instinct that I have managed to survive this long and go about my devilsh plot to overthrow 'The Tongan Terror', a plot which you two have stumbled across and now I shall have to kill you..' 'AFWA SHOW YOU! AFWA SHAKE YOU LIKE RAGDOLL!' Afwa goes to grab hold of Lorde De Pineapple's elongated frame, as he does so, he appears to have a pain in his chest.. Afwa clutches at his chest as Lorde just laughs with a deep sadistic tone.. 'FOOLISH INFADEL! Dare thee to try and cause harm to the keeper of the Seven Scrolls. why you shall be cast into the Bottomless Pit and be made to live in the threshold of Heaven and Hell..' Tautolo looks worried 'Now you scare me.. What did you do to him..' The Keeper says.. 'It's a little ancient tao trick, I focused all my energy into causing him harm and since he wanted to harm me, it reversed and he now suffers instead..' Tautolo says to The Keeper.. 'Listen oh Mystical one, we came to seek you out, as we need your help..' With a laugh, The Keeper says.. 'Help, why should I help you..' Tautolo says pointing to Afwa.. 'There is the reason.. You see, you could take Afwa, train him up to have a sadistic brutal nature as you have and lead him in the war against The Tongan Terror..' The Keeper of the Rock shows a wicked grin.. 'The Tongan Terror..' Afwa says.. 'He murder family! He pay for family!' The Keeper smiles saying.. 'So this is an act of revenge, more cunning as the moments go by.. I can see that this is going to make for a very interesting development..' Tautolo says.. 'You help us then..' The Keeper shakes his head saying.. 'No.. I shall help Afwa.. I shall take Afwa.. Afwa, he belong to me..' Tautolo a little confused says.. 'What you mean..' 'Simple. I need somebody to carry out my devilsh plans, I need somebody who has the desire to kill, who has a thirst for blood and who is not afraid of a little pain and destruction.. Afwa, I need you and looks like you need me..' says The Keeper of the Rock with a devilish undertone.' Afwa says.. 'You come back to Somata then..' Tautolo says.. 'We shall all go together, first thing in the morning..' The Keeper says in a dark undertone.. 'Afwa, we shall speak later.. For now rest my tired ones, come, enter Pheonix Rock, the home of the Keeper of the Seven Scrolls of Somata and a true Islander, just like we all are..' The sun then sets once more as the journey to locate De Pineapple Rock andthe Mystical Spiritual Leader has been completed.. As the scene fades out as we can see The Keeper of the Rock saying.. 'There is no rest for the wicked..' All is then black..
