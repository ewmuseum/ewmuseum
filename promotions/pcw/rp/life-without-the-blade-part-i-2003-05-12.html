Life Without The Blade (Part I)

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/12/03 A piercing knock came over my door in the South Korean pad I'd been residing in for a few more days until' Turmoil.. the apartment wasn't much attractive, as the PCW had just thrown me in a pauper-like shelter, feeding myself from what I'd gotten from the airport, which had mostly consisted of deplorable television dinners and frozen foods. I lay on a torn sofa, the main source of light dimmed, and the surroundings of just two windows and a jointed kitchen (with a refrigerator) were at bare sighting. Hopping off of the sofa, I peeked through the eyehole of the door and there I saw who I'd been looking for.. not my friend for a long time, quite frankly not even my friend but still someone of company. His name was Igor Napolitanke, not his born name (Frank Walters) but a changed identity as I positioned him as my accomplice through many sub-rosa murdering. I slid the chain lock that was firmly placed to the side off of the door, and opened It.. I greeted him with the shake of the hand. His apparel and hair seemed heedless as he sported a shabby trench coat, along with string-hanging jeans. His hair was in complete disarray and it looked like it had just been through a fair, as a row of patches accompanied the style of his hair. 'Stone!' he shouted towards me as I looked forward towards the dull, wooden-made doors, where people of mostly Asian descent resided and wouldn't get out what we were saying but still vigilantly closed the door behind him. He patted me on my back, as I lead him towards the living room, which was basically the entire house as the rooms (only two) had all been branches of the living room. The reason he was here because we were discussing the killings he would be pulling off here to get out my point.. I just wanted my campaign to be an epidemic like SARS over the Asian people.. and if it were getting to them it would certainly get over on the world. And the reason I wasn't 'murdering'? I wanted to keep my tradition and slice women with my blade.. but the Athens airport security had assaulted me secretly and stolen my blade.. also, I just didn't want to risk the chance of getting caught (which was always a minor case). I sat him down along with myself and locked eyes as I started off with the confirmed dialogue.. 'Well.. Frank... --' I pertly started before being hit with somewhat of a disturbance from the rude interruption by Frank.. 'I'd rather you call me Igor.. you know so we don't get my OLD name mixed up.' he explained as I gave an understandable nod and continued on from what I was saying.. 'Now.. All you need to do is PROVE my point.. I'm not saying you should go around causing a massacre but all I'm saying is PROVE A POINT.' I stated as he absorbed the information and just incredulously shook his head.. Confused at why he would shake his head, I put effort in to stating several more things but he cut me off, now beginning to rile me. 'You don't need to give me any guidelines.. I'll do what is reasonable.' 'Igor' sneered as I thought in the far depths of my mind.. I looked at him in puzzlement but nodded in approval, as I didn't want to cause any argument revel. We continued on dully for guidelines on what would happen and nothing more. I knew he was a crooked minded soul but he was the only one that would be willing to join or just assist and put out my campaign. 'It won't be difficult, as I'll take them out with this..' he inputted as he displayed his 9-millimeter pistol.. I nodded, even though I never thought a gun was the manly way.. but he was taken out women.. and I was not saying my killings were of defenseless beings but just at ease. He stood up and I lead him back to the door, pocketing his gun and leaving as I pushed the door close and sat back to the sofa before saying.. 'Make a point.' Knowing he didn't receive the information, he would do whatever reckless thing he'd done in the past.. I sat on the sofa, pondering more and more as my Life Without The Blade continued.. and I had to wait for some headstrong, motley like Frank/Igor to pull of my stunts. Also, I would be once again participating in the ever so hectic schedule of the PCW in a tag-team match against a past adversary, Sivasian Krikorian.. I wouldn't cooperate with him but would just go on sleekly by myself... and soon I would prove myself in the PCW as I continuously failed.. But more specifically I would prove myself to the feminine gender. TO BE CONTINUED
