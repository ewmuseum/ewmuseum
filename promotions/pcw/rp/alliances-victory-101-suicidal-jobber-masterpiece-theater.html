Alliances Victory 101: Suicidal Jobber Masterpiece Theater

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.20.02(#4 PPV) “Welcome ladies, gentlemen, and nimrods of the world… The voice of Rush Mason can be heard as the promo slowly begins to fade in from total black. Strangely, Rush’s voice currently has a somewhat soothing sound to it. “To Suicidal Jobber Masterpiece Theater. As the scene is totally faded in, we can that see how very strange it appears to be. Rush is wearing a suit and glasses, as he sits in a large armchair, looking directly at the camera. Rush: I’m your host….Rushington Blake Doogenhowser, your host for the night as we re-live some of the most suicidal jobbers of our wrestling era….and we might even sink to such depths as to go into the details of Corey Williams’ career. The camera widens its view a bit, showing a half-filled or so glass of Wild Turkey bourbon sitting next to Rush, and he sips on it for a moment. He then brings the glass with him, as he points up at a painting of what seems to be some sort of deformed man. Rush: This… Our host points up to the painting, his finger landing on the canvas near a scar on the man’s head. Rush:….was the first suicidal jobber to ever waste the airtime of a wrestling federation. His name? “Wild” Bill O’Malley. Replacing the camera feed on Rush is a picture of said “Suicidal Jobber,” which the painting renders quite realistically. With scars on almost every wrinkle of his face, O’Malley is certainly not an attractive man. Rush: A stereotypical redneck name….perhaps. However, O’Malley revolutionized such acts as getting put through tables from off the top turnbuckle for stupid cheap pops and embedding tacks into his face to entertain the common person. Another clip is shown of O’Malley’s unconscious body lying amidst the wreckage of a broken table, with tacks all over his bleeding body, yet the fans in the audience do look to be cheering. Rush: Sadly…or not, really, when you think about it….O’Malley died of blood loss when attempting to feed his pet piranhas. His body parts are now buried in eastern Wisconsin, where thousands of pathetic white people who have no lives go to visit. With such kind words, the shot returns back to the Voodoo Villain, who is now on his second glass of bourbon, his glasses a bit twisted on his face. However, Rush walks up to another picture, this time a much larger one. The face appears very familiar to PCW fans of the olden days. Rush: This my friends…is a man who was, by all means, the most monumental waste of time in PCW history…and perhaps in the history of the world. Were this promo in a public place, Rush would probably be assaulted, but he continues to badmouth the familiar-looking character in the painting. Rush: Never, before this individual, had one tried so desperately to appeal to the masses and sell himself to them like a cheap whore. Just thinking of this individual makes Rush shake his head, as he finally reveals the name of a man who you would think might be evil in some fashion. However, the reality is the exact converse of this. Rush: The man who has disgraced even the name of suicidal jobbers, not something easy to do, I admit, is…. The suit-clad Mason clears his throat as he looks up at the picture with disdain on his face, as he speaks softly, although his disgust is visible in visage and tone. Rush: …Edward Of course, it’s typical Rush to pick on one who cannot defend themselves, but to pick on a deceased legend of wrestling is asking for an ass-kicking, even if you are the ever-arrogant Rush Mason. Rush: Just think…Ed wasted all of our time as he paraded about PCW, sadly under the misapprehension that people liked him. His puny brain could never comprehend the fact that all of the audience laughed at him…like they would laugh at a retarded clown in a wheelchair. After yet another cheap shot that cuts to the bone of all Ed fans watching, Rush averts his eyes from the painting, staring directly into the camera, continuing to berate the former PCW’er. Rush: Never once did this dismal wretch lay his hands on the World Title, which is quite pathetic in itself. In fact, it was a year ago at this very same Pay Per View that Eddy made his most famous choke….well, second. A video clip shows some of the highlights featuring Ed from the last Alliances, including the World Title match and the highly-touted actual Alliances match. Rush: On that night, Ed had not one, but two shots to win PCW Heavyweight Gold and actually prove something more than the fact that he was an illiterate dumbfuck. But of course….we all know how the story goes… An obviously doctored picture comes up on the screen, with Ed on the Alliances poster from last year, with ‘Dumbfuck’ written across his forehead, a la Al Snow. Rush: Well, maybe he did know it. Anyways, Ed first choked in typical Ed fashion. Faced with the task of winning the World Title, the stupid fucker lost in a match that involved The Homeless Burn Victim, Clyde; Big Retard ‘I R CZECH!!’ Chris Sloboda; and PCW’s Own Trash, Ivan Stanislav. Making a tsk-tsk sound and shaking his head, Rush looks back up once again at the picture, as if really addressing the Hardcore Legend. Rush: Ed, you really fucked that up, but even I didn’t think you could be SO much of an airhead as to lose another shot at the title! You teamed with Pineapple Boy, The Retarded Lunatic, and Purojobbergawa…arguably not a team of exact superstars, but you should’ve been able to win. Who was there to beat…? Clyde? Boda? Ivan? Oh wait…you’d already jobbed to them once before.…in that night. Rush clears his throat, and continues, his voice becoming more full of venom as he spits insults at the painting. Rush: Then, you were so disappointed that you fled….fled to pussy ass OSW. Bravo, Ed. The fans loved you again, because after all, who’s gonna boo a kid with only half a brain? You screwed around with the Vax, smart move. You had to play top cop, do what the fans wanted. Our disgruntled host looks at the cup in his hand and smiles. He then takes one last look at the painting as he addresses the deceased. Rush: And then, your fed of PCW’s rejects did the only good thing they ever did…rid the world of you. Ed, the only time OSW could get ratings was when you died, because you tried to do the ‘right thing.’ And honestly, Ed…you do-gooder dumbfucks have ALWAYS made me sick. Finally, Rush takes a sip from the cup, and then spits the bourbon up at the painting of Ed, its brownish liquid dripping down slowly as Rush looks up and then turns away, while we fade to black… Rot In Pieces -This Year’s Alliances Winner, Rush Mason
