Can you always go home again?/Lessons in PCW History

23 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/23/04 3 am on a cold, brutal Monday…. It’s an hour of nothing, No one goes to bed at this hour and most do not rise at this time. The still of the night is the playtime of wayward and restless souls. Ryan Knox is more the latter these days. Years past his PCW glory days and shortly after the separation from his wife, sleep doesn’t come easy. Tonight is no different. Knox tosses about his king size, down dressed bed, only to slowly swing his legs over the edge of the bed to sit upright. Knox hangs his head as he rubs his face with his hands, clearing away any remnants of the few and far minutes of sleep. Knocks glances over to his bed side night stand, the glaring red numbers of an alarm clock are the only sign of light in the room. The numbers glare at Knox like an old friend in town for a nightly rendezvous…“3:02am” Knox lets out with a sigh.The numbers stare Knox square in the eyes. Reaching over Knox slides his hand under the lampshade of the light on the night stand and with a slight click half of the darkened room is illuminated. As he pulls his hand back, the pit of his stomach falls to his feet as his eyes glance across the photo of himself holding his wife Amy in his arms. This like his terrible sleep habits was just another nightly habit. Each morning he saw her beautiful smile and soulful eyes eating right through him. He had the storybook love and that had slipped away from him, he and his soul mate were now separated. He had given up the spotlight for her. Now that she was gone, He no longer had either of the two loves of his life…. His princess nor the craft that created his name. She wouldn’t understand why he was going back.“Everything I have inspired to be…. Is everything she threw away.”Away from the spotlight Knox, in his own world was not the brash, cocky, life of the party, destructive monstrosity he was in the ring. In his world he was cool, reserved and loving and it was this world that outside the ring Knox built and would die to keep the foundations of from falling… But he had lost his strongest pillar and greatest crutch, the woman he loved.With a strong, deep breath Knox lifts himself off the bed and made his way across the large, white carpeted floor through a doorway. :click:A series of bright, white lights flicker on until they fully light the master bathroom of the Chestnut Hill home. With his recent keeping hours, he was almost surprised to see his reflection opposite him in the mirror. But much like the reflectionless vampire, Knox physical image hadn’t lost anything as years went by. As he stood clad only in royal blue boxers it was impossible not to admire his Herculean like physique. The nickname he garnered in the WHW “The Perfect Specimen” not only hit the nail on the head but drove it right through the wood. With a series of veins that run through his body like roads on a map and a series of well placed scars that marked memories and highlights from his wrestling career. Knox leaned forward placing his hands on the marble sink in front of him to support his weight. As he looked over at the long scar across his left bicep memories of his feud with the legendary Alan Kriegman came rushing to mind. Only the devious evil genius Kriegman would inject a human being with enough ultra-roid to make a human bicep explode. A feud that raged from Knox being a last minute replacement on Kriegman and Chef’s team at Alliances.Feuds, rivalries, Knox had fought every legend PCW had to offer in its early rise to glory. While he had been hungry to get back in the ring and start lining up bodies for a long time coming, it was the recent defecation of PCW’s legends by Jon Kano and his crew of flunkies that had him chomping at the bit more than ever to return. “The only legend in PCW history”?? “The only thing legendary thing about that guy is that at 6 foot 10, 304 pounds; He’s clearly the biggest bag of shit I’ve ever seen.”With all the nonsense that Kano was spewing to the wrestling world, it wasn’t a hard decision for Knox to come back to PCW when he got the call from a former associate Boda. The fact that Kano was going around saying he helped build PCW turned his stomach. Knox along with the likes of Boda, Meanstreak, Kremmen, The Phantom Booker among many others were the stones that built PCW from the ground up. Kano was nothing but a stone that was thrown atop an already established mountain. Kano being inducted to the PCW hall of fame reminded Knox of the underachieving, untalented, nerd in high school who tried out for the team and was given the job of towel boy just so the coach didn’t have to tell him what a worthless athlete he was. Kano deserved to be thrown some kind of bone for sticking around as long as he did.Now Jon Kano brought a new brainchild to PCW in form of the Primetime Players. Yet it was not Kano’s brainchild but the brainchild of Tyler Nelson, former PCW super power who Knox thrashed twice in his PCW career. Once under the guise of “The Phantom Booker” in which Knox defeated Nelson in a viciously brutal enclosed steel cage style match. Nelson bought everything under the sun and bought his way to power but at least when Media Inc. took over it was an original sentiment. Regardless of his personal sentiments for Jon Kano, Knox couldn’t deny that Kano had swerved his way into being the hottest name in pro wrestling and there was something to be said for that. He wasn’t sure exactly what…but he was sure there was something. As someone who had swerved, swindled and backstabbed many a folk in the past, Knox saw nothing wrong with a well placed knife in the back to get ahead.Reaching forward Knox runs the water of the sink in front of him. Leaning forward Knox cups his hands into the cool running water and splashes it up to his face. Knox shuts the faucet grabs the hand towel from the wall and dries his face as he makes his way over to the entertainment center in his bedroom, Knox grabs the remote and sits down in a large love seat located at the end of his bed. Turning on the TV, knocks clicks play on the remote. On comes a taped interview segment of the monster Propaganda. Knox had received a care package from Boda with a note reading…Ryan,These are the most recent tapes of the guys I was telling you about in Jon Kano’s crew. Chris Knox had left off earlier during a Propaganda segment. Knox eyes widen as reviews the tape….**Propaganda takes a deep breath, grabs the iron bar with both hands, lifts it above his head then swings at the wall. But on the wall is Dong Zou, the abducted Chinese immigrant. He is naked except for a pair of wide fronts, which are drenched in blood. As is Dong, he is cut to pieces, he is hanging from two chains that are holding his skinny little body up, he looks like he is about to die. There are rows of cuts dripping down from him. He is crying. The iron bar swings straight into Dong’s jaw. It flies out of place and starts to bounce around like a space hopper. It’s a disgusting sight, Dong is like a five or six long noodles hanging off a wall, drenched in crimson paint. “This will teach these pesky immigrants to stop plaguing over to our country.” Sagar looks at the virtually dead Dong Zou. “Scum!” he shouts at the weeping immigrant. “I will make him fell the wrath of God, Brother Sagar. Just like Blitz.” Propaganda demands as he grabs the bar again for another strike at Dong Zou.**Pausing the tape as his mouth gaped open in utter terror, Knox doesn’t know what to make of what he’s just witnessed. Knox played the segment over again in his head, likening himself to the millions of viewers who watched The Passion of The Christ for the first time. But there was something different about this. What Propaganda had done to this immigrant was not an act of God. Oh no, it was something much more devious and horrifying…“That was a blatant act, homo erotic bondage” Somewhere deep inside Knox found little comfort in making humor of what Propaganda had done to the immigrant. It was vicious and not since the instant before the legendary monster Meanstreak dropped a Dr. Pepper machine on him did Knox have chills like that. What was horrifying to Knox is he considered himself a man of at the very least loose religious faith and if beating a helpless immigrant was something God wanted. Then that loose faith was about to fly out the window. Regardless of the violence the tape held, there was no doubt in his head that Knox freakish strength would match with Propaganda any day. If not, Knox still had those old Balco connections. “How serious can a 7 foot alter boy be taken anyways?”Knox mulled a series of swirling thoughts in his head for a moment….“Maybe Kano was only the second biggest pile of shit I’ve ever seen….”In his head Knox knew he talked allot of shit, even if he was talking to himself. But no one can deny the fact that he can cash every check his ass ever wrote. Clicking play once again the video resumed. As Propaganda’s segment ended, Kris Kanton’s latest segment began to role.“Oh snap!”Knox sprang forward in his seat, with a smile on his face.“I remember this cat. He was one of Scott Naket’s flunkies in sHs. Man, Derrick (Diamond) and I used to beat those clowns around the ring all the time. I can’t believe this guy still has a job.”Knox briefly flashes back to defeating Scott Naket for the Extreme title in only his second PCW match. A Match he was granted because he hit Kremmen with a Hum V to give Naket the win.“We had fun in the early days, Back then you could hit someone with a car and it was fair game”Knox sits back with a reminiscent smile on his face somewhat surprised seeing Kris Kanton’s promo video.“Jeez, nothing like seeing a familiar face. Good for him, sticking around riding coat tails all this time, I guess some things really never change. Let’s just hope they open PCW 2 again for his sake.”Knox shut the TV off and tossed the remote the side. Knox glanced over at the clock to see it was just after 4am. Today officially marked Training Day #1 and the opening chapter of a re-written history. It was time to go to work and point of business number 1?“I think I’ll crank call Boda”To Be Continued…..
