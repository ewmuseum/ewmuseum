Girl Talk [B] - The Neighbor

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March the f-ck Eyes snapped to attention as another gong chimed out in the distance. Sonata looked up, another bright smile on her face. “She’s here! Our next guest!” She jumped up and hurried to the door, leaving Sayaka sitting there on the floor with a cigarette in her hand, a shrug on her shoulders, and dullness on her face. “Great. All we need now is another person.” The sarcasm was strong with this one. Sonata briskly walked out to the gate and opened it slowly, a smile directed at our new guest. Standing there in a red shiny plastic skirt with black shredded leggings, black boots, and a black crop top with red suspenders was a familiar face. “I late or something?” she said, removing the red sunglasses from her face and batting her almond eyes at Sonata. Sonata shook her head; her tight-lipped smile still as effective as her toothy one. “Nah, you’re on time in my book! Come in, you can meet my best friend.” “Boy, that’d be a pleasure.” Seems like everyone got their cynic shot this morning… the woman followed Sonata through the front yard into the castle and removed her boots, looking around the whole time as if she were watching her back. Sound caution as always when treading on the god’s land… but most people were too pompous or foolish to consider otherwise. Fine… let the pain they receive be lesson for them. “Everything fine?” The woman looked up, brushing away a few strands of black and red hair. “Oh, peachy. Just never been here besides the reception for the funeral and all. I’m not too sure Big Void’d be pleased with me being here.” Sonata shook her head, dismissing her idea. “Don’t worry about it. He’s down in the basement training for his big match anyway. I doubt he even comes back up for anything else.” “But there’s a chance?” she stood up, stepping out of the genkan and towards the right hallway. “Boy…I remember someone said chance is the savior of the unsure.” “That sounds like Blitz talk to me.” Sonata chuckled. They turned into the sitting room, where Sonata presented the new guest. “Sayaka Mitsui, I’d like you to meet my neighbor…Mina de Margo.” Her eyebrow arched as Mina leaned down and shook hands with her. “de Margo? You wouldn’t be the same de Margo that runs PURE would you?” Sharp nod as she sat on an empty side of the table. “Same one, ma’am.” “Damn, Sonata. Do you know everyone in the wrestling world?” The hostess giggled, holding her hand to her mouth daintily. “Only Akiyama, Mina, Cyclonica, and Inphino…though I’ve never really met him formally.” “Cyclonica is not in the wrestling world, just for the record.” Mina stated frankly as she took the cup of tea freshly poured by Sonata, muttering a thanks. “What was wrong with her?” Sayaka spit out tobacco smoke with that question. “Let’s just say she was a lot like El Diablo is now…” a refreshing “AAH” came hissing from her black lips after she tasted the tea. “…lot of wasted effort, but doesn’t realize that there’s no talent to work with and cannot possibly hope to survive in a real wrestling match.” “Damn,” the sandy haired woman said depressed. “I kinda liked her too. She had charisma on the mike, and you grew to hate when she was on camera too.” “That wasn’t a gimmick either; in person she can be just the bitch she was on air.” Sonata looked down, shielding her eyes away from the two women ashamed. Sayaka looked up at her friend, leaning forward to try and see her face. “You right there, kiddo?” Mina looked at Sonata and slapped herself in the forehead. “Geez, I forgot about all that. Sorry Sonata…I’ve just had a lot on my mind lately with PURE and Inphino Blitz.” Sonata looked back up at the two women, that textbook smile plastered back on her face. “That’s okay! Sayaka should hear about it all anyway!” -=-=-=-=- There comes a time when you have to stop caring… Stop caring about if you’re really hurting the guy across from you…or if he’s just selling it thick for the kids. Stop caring about if you really did bust him wide open with that fist…or if he bladed before the match. Stop caring about if that act he’s putting on for real…or if it’s just a performance for the fans. Scientology…one of the biggest clown acts outside the other retarded fools in this damn company. We were at the point where we just didn’t care a sh-t about what was gonna happen in that match. All we knew was that someone was gonna get their neck snapped with the ADA, another was getting stomped into the mat just because he pissed us off, and someone was eating a Hand of Fate. We do all those things, and we’ve had a good night. We’re sitting here in the basement training room…just thinking, leaning against the wall on the ground thinking… We really, truly, honestly, sincerely don’t give a f-cking sh-t about being PCW Champion. Looking back at former champions…Kannon, Big Alan Steele, Snow, Stanislav…just a bunch of jokers with shticks backing them up. Shticks and opportunities…two things we don’t have or get. We’ve officially had one shot at the belt, and that was cheated from us by a dumbass referee, then the two March Madness forums it took a whole platoon of monkey faced bastards just to throw us over one top rope… those aren’t defeats…those are just eliminations. But when we’re looking at the possible outcome of this match…we have all reason to believe that that f-cker Kannon, nor anyone else in that match will allow us to walk out with that championship around our waist. Banding together is a human trait…we’ve realized that now. All those alliances we’ve made…they were flawed because they were humanistic approaches to superiority. That’s how humanity views their superiority…through numbers. Front Office, Dark Ages, other flawed stables…number games were always their ploy in victories. And now…it was the biggest group PCW has ever known going against one being in the ring on Tuesday… The group of assorted wrestlers…called PCW. We stood up from our grounded seat, clenching our fists tightly. At this point we knew they weren’t letting is walk out with a victory…so f-ck the title and f-ck them. They will all just have to feel what death is like…at our hands… -=-=-=-=-=-=- A muffled roar sounded out from the inner confides of the castle. The three women looked back, all of their eyes looking in cautiousness. Sayaka looked at Sonata, pointing her cigarette at her. “Does he do that a lot?” She nodded with a smile growing on her face. “I counted thirty nine in one day once. All he does is sit down there and train for his matches…sometimes he goes out running…sometimes he may take me into the city for a fun or something…but most of the time it’s just me and him in the castle all day.” “Heh, at least your home’s not empty.” Mina sipped her tea slowly, Sonata looking concerned at her. “Where’s Blitz?” She shrugged her shoulders at her question. “Never came home after Slam this week. I heard some disturbing things from Shanda Russell about Blitz, so next weekend after I clear things up with the PURE Japan tour I’m flying over there to talk with him…if he shows that is.” Sayaka shook her head slowly. “Man…trying times these days…glad my love life is still in shape.” “How is Force anyways?” Sonata asked her. She waved her hand at her, retracting it to smoke the cigarette in hand. “Force is Force…he hasn’t changed a bit. You’ll have to see him sometime…all of us will have to get back together…one day.” Just then another chime came from the door. Sonata stood up and excused herself from the room. Mina looked at Sayaka with a wrinkled brow. “Who else was invited?” “Beats me…” Sayaka exhaled. “You got me sister.” -=-=-=-To be Continued -=-=-=-
