The Life - 2

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.20.02 His eyes lit up, and then he was back on the road again. Slightly weaving in and out of place, it was all Dante Jackson. Nobody was here with him, not a naive wife on the side of him. A short movement between his hands and thumbs is all that seperates him from the wheel. In the retrospect, he keeps his eyes on the wheel, eying where to go. He looks upward at the signs hanging above him, painted in green and flanked by metallic structures to hold them up. He shrugs, eying the small portion at reads out San Antonio is only 36 miles away from where he currently happens to be. It jerks with him solidly as he moves into a turning lane, the blinker eying towards the right. He turned off the lane, eying upwards a large government building circled around by vast and vast acres of grasslands and short ponds. He drives straight up towards the country club esque mansion, taking in the sights under his dirt ridden fingernails. Jingling the keys lightly, he shut off the ignition with a slight tug. Door opens, door closes, with a thud. A slight whistling tune comes to pass as Dante looks around checking out the various feminine wiles of women around him, all various shapes and sizes. One almond faced woman with short brown hair in braids tied back wearing a Nicole Essex designer shirt and burgundy leather pants began to walk along side Dante, catching a brief glance at him. 'Oh, hi.' she softly muttered. 'Hi.' he responded back, still with a view of the hospital in front of him. It was an exact replica of the hospital he visited as a kid back in New York City, George Washington. The woman nudged on the shoulder. 'You wouldn't be Dante Jackson would you?' 'Yeah, I am.' 'Great, just the person I'm looking for.' she said with a smile. Dante warmed up to her a bit, changing his direction mid-way. 'You're looking for me?' 'Yep, I'm Dawn and I'll be your friend if you'll be mine.' 'Am I supposed to take this for real? Look, I get offers like this all the time, but I'm not an adulterer. Never will be anyhow. I've got a wife at home and not looking to carry any baby mammas on the way.' Milky smooth that announced herself as Dawn scoffed at Dante. 'I wasn't looking for anything of that nature anyway.' She began to pick up her pace, inching farther and farther away from Dante at step of a toe. He changed his mind as he caught the woman by the arm, catching her softly before she stepped and almost broke her heel. 'Wait, let me start over okay? When you're in a predicament that I'm in, you don't get to make many friends. And those friends are always close to being your enemies.' 'True.' 'So you know what? I'll take it like this - what is your occupation?' 'I'm a writer,' she told him nonchalantly, fixing up her shirt. Looped diamond earrings shone from her ears as she wore a Kimberly diamond watch on her right wrist. Didn't seem like writer type accessories from Dante's perspective. Most of the writers he had seen wore plaid shirts and khaki pants. 'Oh, writer?' She nodded easily at it. 'Full degree from Stanford and all. Been out here a good seven months, trying to cover you.' 'Me? What's all the hoopla over me for.' The two of them stopped shallowly admist a short period of what to and when to do things. They looked around, smiling at each other non-chalantly, before Dante looked at her once more, 'What's your story?' 'Um,' she mused on what to say, something usual for anyone that took a step near Dante. He smirked at her, 'No need to be nervous.' 'I'm not, just a childhood stutter.' Dante drew himself a bit back from her, 'Well if you're going to go into Snapple~! like snappages then go that way.' She laughed at his joke, before moving the two began to walk once more. Dawn raised her eyebrows before dropping them back down, 'The Source.' 'You report for them?' Dawn ran her fingers behind her hair, resting her plam along the nape of her neck. Dante slendered backwards, wondering what the writer would tell him next. 'Yeah, its a trip when you're first story happens to be upon a 9x platinum selling recording artist who is also one of the more prominent wrestlers in the world.' 'Yeah, that's me.' Dante quipped. 'The Playaz Incorporated baller.' Dawn blinked a few times, unsure of what Dante meant by that. 'Well, not all of us can be Master P about things now can we? What are you here for?' Dante stopped her, 'Visit my sister. You?' 'Brother who got knifed up. Sad what happened to your sister. April right? I heard straight out of New Orleans that she ended up being hit pretty bad.' Dante placed his hands behind his head, obviously pissed about the entire situation that escalated during the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament back in September. During the course of the month, Dante had known very little about his little sister, close to being his road dogg and partner in crime once his father told him. Now it couldn't come off of his mind. It pained him badly, and Dawn read about the trials so many times before, it was just about as known as a tabloid read out in Supermarkets around the country. 'Bad that you don't wanna talk about it?' she questioned as the two made their way through the double glass doors. Dante being the courteous one pulls the left door open, letting the woman through. 'You can say that.' he told her. Dawn held her head up as she looked down at the sitting receptionist wearing a faded white uniform with her hair tied in a bun. Her pale skin told that she had to have been working her for a good twenty-five years, and had grown from day one to hate her job. 'Excuse me miss,' Dawn began to ask her, disposing of a pen into her Gucci purse. 'do you know where I can find Vincient Browne?' The woman clicked her teeth, roughly polishing her gumline with her salty tongue. She gave a half hearted smile. 'Room 314A.' 'Thanks.' Dante moved ahead of Dawn as she moved out of the way. 'Um, yeah do you know where I can find an April Jackson? She's been here a couple of months and she's pratically a resident.' 'Ah, that girl.' the receptionist told him with a Southern drawl. 'She'll be up a few halls by the B Unit.' 'Thanks,' he replied before making his way towards B Unit. Dawn stopped him before he took his first steps. She sighed, before handing out a piece of paper with red ink inside of the margin. 'Your number?' 'Keep in touch will you?' He smiled, knowing that new acquaintances always brought lady luck upon his side. Its not all that often however, that luck is your guiding light.
