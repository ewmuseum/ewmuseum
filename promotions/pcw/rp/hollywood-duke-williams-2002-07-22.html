Hollywood Duke Williams

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/22/02 -A yellow 2002 Mustang speeds down the highway at 95. The California back drop darts by trying to kwwp up with the car. There is no question of who the driver is, on the front hood in black is the logo =w=. Behind the Wheel the Malice Man is busy on the speakerphone with his agent. .- Williams: Now damn it Shell you know I’m perfect for that action role. Shell: I know your perfect but that bullshit casting agent wants somebody a bit younger. Williams: Younger? -Visibly and audibly upset as his voice raises.- Williams: I can out wrestle, out fuck, and out act any of the kids out there. Where does this guy live? Shell: I’m not telling you where he lives. Williams: Shell who do you work for? Shell: Duke, we don’t want any trouble. Williams: No trouble Shell, where does he live? -The agents voice sounds concerned.- Shell: Lets just say for the sake of argument that I have his address. What would you do with it if I gave it to you? Williams: I’d go over to this age experts house and show him I’m not too old. Shell: Dare I ask how you would do that? Williams: I would beat the hell out of him to show that I can still move. I’d tie him to a chair so he could watch me f*ck his wife every which way but loose. Then I would show him my acting skills by pretending I enjoyed f*cking his old hag. That takes skill Shell. Shell: Ok your not getting his address. Can you come to a casting call on Tuesday? Williams: No Shell you know I have a show. Shell: Oh great who you fighting. Williams: I’m going tp kill Darkstar who has my belt and Clyde who’s just a hump. Shell: Both of them against you? Williams: Yea that prick Ty Nelson thinks he can out fox me. Fuck him. Shell: What about the Legend guy? The kid who couldn’t hold on to the gold. Williams: He gets to ref the match. Shell: Is he going to call the match fair? Williams: Is the pope catholic?? Of coarse he is he has that weakness….what’s it called…??? Shell: Ethics?? Williams: Yea, but not that word. You know like ethics only more biblical and good. Shell: Morels? Williams: Bingo!! He has the weakness of morels. Ha what a loser. He’ll call it right down the middle. Shell: If you beat the two dark dorks do you win any gold? Williams: You would think but the title is not on the line here. Shell: That crap you need me to manage you. Williams: Shell you can’t even get me a movie roll. Shell: I’m trying here Duke. Williams: Not trying hard enough hoss. Shell: Well will see what happens. Williams: good bye Shell. -Duke turns off the speaker as he pulls into the parking lot of lot of the “Tonight Show” getting ready for the midday tapping in which he’s a guest. As soon as the Malice man exits the car a PCW camera crew is waiting for him.- PCW: Duke what do you think of the match Nelson set up for you at Turmoil? Williams: I think it f*cking rocks hoss? PCW: HUH? Williams: Does that kinda grab you by the balls? Did that catch you off guard? HUH? PCW: Yea….don’t you think you’re at a distinct disadvantage? Williams: Did anybody think the Patriots would win the Super Bowl this time last year? Did the odds even fall deeper against them when Drew got hurt? Does the underdog always lose? Fuck no. I’m I the underdog? Fuck yea. Will I lose? Who gives a sh*t. What does this match mean? Pcw: Ahhh Williams: Ahhh your damn right. It means nothing. Will I still get a shot at my title this Sunday? PCW: Yea….. Williams: So I can lose this match and smile, right? PCW: If you wanted too. But…… Williams: Butt, ass, shit, it don’t matter. As long as I walk away breathing. My goal is to do as much damage as possible. I’ll bring a chair in the ring, a knife, a hacksaw, a chainsaw, a god damn seesaw if I have too. What ever it takes to accomplish success in hurting them. I want to cripple them. Destroy them. -The Malice Man gets ready to walk away when the kid asks another question.- PCW: What about Legend Inc as the ref? -Duke adjusts his chrome shades.- Williams: What if he screws me? PCW: ……I……. Williams: What if he screws me? PCW: I…he…I…..won’t? Williams: Shut up and listen kid. What did we just get done talking about? PCW: ummmm? Williams: We talked about the match no mattering. Right? PCW: Yea. Williams: Does Tommy like to play by the books? PCW: Yea? Williams: Give this kid an RC and a Moonpie!! That’s right. Tommy won’t do anything to ruin his reputation as a clean cut, good guy. So again what does this match mean? PCW: Nothing!?! Williams: BINGO!! You are a quick learner. So if I were to knock out Clyde, and Darkstar with a weapon. Do I really need to go for a pin? PCW: ummmm no? Williams: That’s right!! I could then turn my weapon on the referee and lay his ass out. PCW: I guess you could. Williams: Yes I could. And I would do it with style. You know what kind of style? PCW: ummm…..wait……ahhhh……malice style? Williams: Put your wait on it son!! PCW: MALICE STYLE!!! Williams: One more time with feeling. PCW: MALICE STYLE HOSS!!!! Williams: Perfect! Now let me go talk to this big chinned goof. Tell the boys in the back to get ready to be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. -Duke walks into the building.- =w=
