Withdrawal Symptoms

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th May Another match, another loss for Sivasian Krikorian. Is he bothered by it though? I would doubt it. For you see he hasn’t yet had a single one on one match. There has always been someone else at fault if you ask him. He can’t stop another man in the match getting pinned if he’s currently beating the tar out of someone in the corner, can he? Pressure is something all competitors in wrestling feel, something everyone has, and something some use to their advantage. If Sivasian Krikorian can play the right mind games, get into SOCIETY’s, Matt Jackson and Shane Whether’s heads, then he’s surely on for a winner. He can stay one step ahead, one step further towards his dream of conquering the federation and going on to find out who killed his parents, his number one aim for 40 years of his life. Pressure can lead to other things. This seems to combine itself with several other things, however. One such thing could be for a smoker in PCW. Sure, a cigarette now and then would calm someone down, but the staff would be less than pleased. Cigarettes burn inside, they do damage. What would the person more than likely do? Keep there high paying easy, cushdy job, or have a few and keep the tobacco monkey on their back? Obviously the answer is the job, and so they’d give cigarettes up. But like morphine, the viscous and strong painkiller, the body assumes after a while it’s a natural thing, so once you stop pumping tar and coal into your body, it feels unbalanced, something’s wrong, something’s not quite right in the make up of your body. Sivasian isn’t like others, he’s not used to cupping a cigarette between his lips and sucking in it’s putrid filth, he’s more susceptible to hold a gun, a beautifully polished Russian made RF .45 in his hands. To fire it, to feel it buckle into his shoulders. Just like cigarettes, however, things can get bad when you give them up, and with a strict law on foreigners holding guns, things don’t look good for Sivasian’s body make-up. The light flickers and catches the sound blood red couch. Maybe that’s wrong, it’s more of the brown color blood goes when it’s had an hour or two to dry. Hell, that stuffs awful to get our your hair, or under your finger nails. But doesn’t the couch look so inviting, so plush, comfy and big. Just asking, begging you to plonk yourself down on it and let it absorb you’re abnormal body weight. The chair is a longish chair, however, more used to getting laid on. A lovely color, it’s ironic however, that people with problems come here. The room is made deliberately cosy, a small fire in the corner, a log fire, it’s flames used to extract the most out of patients, and the loving cream walls calming them down. The door is guarded, a large, mammoth of a man. A huge black security guard stands, with a pinned up straight jacket. Suddenly, a gorgeous woman with flowing brown hair, and a white scientist jacket walks into the room. She walks over to another fairly large, plush chair and sits down. She picks up a red notepad from an oak desk and a pen, and twiddles it in her teeth. The security guard walks in the room. ‘This one looks big, Mark,’ the woman starts. The security nods and doesn’t speak. ‘Shouldn’t be any trouble for our Security guard supreme!’ she screams in delight as he smirks, showing perfect white teeth. The security guard tips his blue ‘securitor’ hat, and nods, ‘Ma’am.’ ‘Suppose you ought to send him in then, I’m getting great cash for this Mark, fancy a drink after?’ she says. ‘Would love to Ma’am, but I’ve got other things to do.’ Replies the security guard in a deep, booming voice. The psychiatrist rolls her eyes, and then, in a flutter of interested stares down south. Her eyes pop out of her head when she sees the bulge, and she smiles to herself. Suddenly, ‘the soviet sniper’ walks in, and nods. Wearing his usual sniping gloves and black trench coat, he doesn’t seem to have gotten into the Californian way of things. His slicked back thinning hair, however, has some gel applied, and it appears as if minimal effort has been put into today’s appearance. He smiles sweetly towards the woman and she fakes smiles back. ‘Well mister…’ starts the lady, checking out her clipboard, ‘Krikorian, plan to sit down for me, and we’ll see what your problem is.’ ‘Sure thing lady,’ starts Sivasian, lying down on the sit out couch, spreading himself out. Even though Sivasian isn’t the largest wrestler you’ll see, he’s certainly bigger than your average civilian, and his feet peer over the edge of the couch. ‘So, can we take a quick questionnaire please, Mr.Krikorian,’ asks the qualified mental health doctor. Before he can answer, she whips a questionnaire sheet from the desk, and runs her finger threw page after page of questionnaire, as Sivasian rolls his eyes and gulps hard. ‘Ever smoked? The lady asks sharply. ‘Well err….define smoking.’ Repeats Sivasian. ‘Smoking a cigarette, physically inhaling a toxin from a cigarette.’ She repeats, annoying as his apparent inability to adapt to a question. ‘Then yes, yes I have, in the army for many years.’ He replies. ‘Army?’ she questions, ‘Well, I’ll automatically tick the ‘alcohol’ box, but how much of a harsh experience was that?’ she asks him. ‘Oh god….’ He starts. The lady licks her lips in anticipation of a ‘it was awful’ sort of usual army related reply. ‘I loved it! BANG!’ he says smiling at her. ‘Right….’ She says, blinking and sighing at the same time. ‘So, anything happened weirdly lately, dreams…children…wife, anything at all?’ asks the woman, desperate to rush this man through. Siv shrugs and looks at her, ‘Well err…not much, I’ve lose some matches lately.’ ‘You play a professional sport then?’ she asks. ‘Indeed…’ he replies, smirking with pride. ‘Let me guess, soccer? Baseball, basketball maybe?’ she asks, becoming interested in a possible sports star. ‘I err…wrestler.’ ‘Oh…’ is her only weak reply. ‘So, you know that Totally Tubular Tommy guy then.’ She asks? ‘Well not really, he’s more of a vet, me , I’m a rookie, he gets the respect, but I got an autograph for my son once.’ ‘Well,’ she says, ‘he’s quite, you know, hot!’ ‘Listen ma’am, we’re here for me, not your weak sexual distractions.’ Sivasian snaps back sharply. ‘Right, any dreams then.’ She says. ‘None that I can..wait, yes, yes there has been,’ he says. ‘One time when I was dreaming, something to do with me having my sniper rifle, I killed my match opponents for my upcoming wrestling match, then, well…’ he says. ‘Carry on,’ she replies, noting some things down. ‘Well, I shot my mother, but I’ve been getting pains, weird, odd pains in my hands, where I held my gun, and in my shoulder, where it used to recoil back into the bone, it’s…freaky ma’am.’ He says. ‘Well, there is your problem.’ She says. ‘What problem? I don’t have a problem.’ Snaps back Sivasian. ‘First sign, denial.’ She replies, smirking, ‘and then migraines, do you get sharp kind of migraines?’ she asks. ‘YES! YES I DO!’ screams Sivasian, holding onto his head. He leaps from his chair and grabs the woman around the corner, ‘they hurt so much, like a man ripping at my skull with a hammer, it’s terrible.’ ‘And you joined the army for some painful memory, didn’t you Sivasian?’ she says. He nods slowly, he knows she knows, but doesn’t say anything. Instead he sits, staring down at the floor clenching his fist. ‘But it was you’re families fault! ‘ he screams, using his might to tip the lying couch over. ‘Huh?’ she replies, thinking about how she wasn’t taught about this in college training. ‘You killed them! You’re poxy family from the past voted in that president, he wanted to go some with the USSR. But he couldn’t, he made US hurt ourselves.’ He says, his enragement shown in the veins popping out the side of his head. ‘Sorry sir, you’re gunna have to leave’ she replies, ‘you’re getting violent, think claming thoughts.’ ‘Screw you! ‘ shouts Sivasian into the air like a bullet, ‘screw you, screw this establishment, and screw you’re government, in fact, screw this whole country, you drove me to doing this. You drove me mad. You drove me to picking up the gun and killing, and I’ll have to do that again to get rid. It’s like a craving, and you started it!’ Sivasian starts kicking and screaming as the black security guard pulls him away. ‘You’re violent sir, you’ll have to leave.
