The World Is Yours Part 1: Internal Conflict

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/04 The world seemed to pass him by in a blur of mixed emotions and dilute responses. 'Holy Shit' Those were the only words that Shaun Styles was able to express after getting word of the challenge that lays in front of him. Defeating DarkStar was a hell of an accomplishment in itself, he is one of the greats to grace the squared circle, taking him out would prove Styles worthy of competing for the most coveted prize in the business...But does it deem him worthy of holding that same prize? No, the task at hand remains one that might as well be called an impossible one. Defeat DarkStar? It took two tries but here we are. Defeat Boda? No second chances. Ever since reaching word of his next opponent an intense work out has been undertaken by the rookie fan favorite. There was no way Shaun Styles could do it alone, finding no where else to turn he called his best friend, Jade Taylor...who ironically is responsible for this whole wrestling thing himself. The world seemed to pass him by in a blur of mixed emotions and dilute responses as the backwoods of Upstate New York rushed by the racing car. Shaun sat next to the driver seat looking out the window as the wind blew his multi-colored hair in every which direction. His glossy, blue eyes did not seem to make a move as they stayed center on the tinted glass. That was an interest he had held ever since being a child, Shaun Styles was able to sit in a car for hours without moving or making any noise at all. He was fascinated by the world passing him by, he wouldn't miss the opportunity to capture it all visually. In the driver's seat sat the tall figure of Jade Taylor. He was decked out in a brown suit, with a white dress shirt underneath, nothing very extravagant but showing his recent success. The underground boxing promotion has been booming as of late since Shaun Styles arrived in the popular wrestling atmosphere, giving off a great deal of publicity for the gloved combat. Good times were rolling in as he was involved in gathering and organizing all the biggest underground fights since the Thunder/Styles fiasco. He sat concentrated with his brown eyes glaring at the road ahead, still wondering why Shaun would call him after all this time. No sooner would they arrive at their destination, parking the green Jaguar XK on the side of the industrial highway the two front doors opened and the duo walked out. The cold, harsh winter has finally loosened its grip on the New York area in mid-April allowing a fresh breeze of wind to even out the warm air; All combined for the recipe of a beautiful day. They felt the grass wave back and forth under their feet as they jumped the small wooden fence and climbed a small hill to what is unveiled as a small community in the middle of nowhere. It remains a ghost town as not a soul can be seen and the small, victorian homes are clearly abandoned. Jade Taylor: Would you happen to have a reason for bringing me here so early in the day? Shaun Styles shows no reply at first as he finds a small wooden bench with the old layer of paint peeling off. They both sit down and have a clear picture of the highway from which they emerged while they look down off the hill. Taking out a half broken cigarette and lighting it up he appears to be in deep thought as he exhales quite a bit of smoke. Shaun Styles: Do I need a reason to call my best friend? Jade Taylor: Don't give me that bullshit. If you need some money, I'd be glad to help out. With the amount of publicity you've gotten me, I owe you. Shaun Styles: No, that's not it. The wrestling gig seems to be enough to steadily pay Laproni back and pay for my meals. Jade Taylor: So what do you need? Shaun Styles: I wanted to talk to you about something... Shaun Styles seems to be hesitating as the hand holding the cigarette is clearly shaking while he takes a long pull. Jade Taylor: What is it? You can talk to me about anything, you know that, man. Shaun Styles looked up at the sky finding a picture perfect mixture of white dancing clouds in a background of light blue. The memories of spending his summers in this old Indian reservation come back to him as he pictures himself walking down the highway with his small brigade of close friends to a waterfall hidden in the woods. Jade Taylor: Come on, what the hell is it? Jade Taylor was obviously getting aggravated by the silence of his close friend. Shaun Styles: I want out. It took a long pause until Jade Taylor was able to respond. Jade Taylor: What do you mean? Shaun Styles: ...We both know whose fault the J.T. Thunder fight was, there's no way of sugar coating it. You told me get into that ring and you'll take care of the rest. You promised that he'd take the fall, all I had to do was convince Don Laproni to invest as much as he could. I did my job and you fucked up yours. I took the fall for you, man, I took the responsibility. I owe my life to that mob boss because of yo-- Jade Taylor: You're still allive because of me, have you forgotten that? Shaun Styles: I wouldn't be in this situation if it wasn't for you in the first place! Jade Taylor: Yeah, you'd still be eating trash if it wasn't for me. The climax has been reached as the two best friends now looked at each other with fierce eyes, completely in disgust. Shaun Styles: Look...I just want this shit to end. I'm tired of being Laproni's lap dog because of your mess up. I'm tired of trying to hunt down J.T. Thunder for him, I don't want any more part in it. You go and you tell him whose fault it was. I made you a reasonable hunk of change, you can pay him back with that. Me? I just wanna focus on my wrestling career. Jade Taylor let out a loud, sarcastic laugh after hearing the last sentence. Jade Taylor: Wrestling? Wrestling? The only reason you're there is to pay off your debt to Laproni! Shaun Styles: That's how it might have started out but it turned out to be a lot more. I've been training for this match with Boda more than I've trained for any boxing match. It's important to me and I want to be able to concentrate on it. Jade Taylor: You're willing to just hand over your best, shit, your only friend, to Don Laproni after everything I've done for you? Shaun Styles: ...You didn't seem to have a problem with handing me over. That last comment caught Jade Taylor by surprise. His eyes opened up wide as he looked on at the unraveling situation. Without further thought, he grabbed the cigarette out of Shaun Styles' hand, broke it in half, and threw it off the hill. The cherry fell out still lit as it landed on the highway and cars proceeded to run it over. Jade Taylor: You backstabbing little cunt. With the relationship ruined Jade Taylor stood up and walked down the hill in a haste. His tall, skinny figure jumped the fence again as he climbed in the car and sped off. That left Shaun with the question of how to get back. How to get his life on track...
