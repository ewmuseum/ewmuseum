No Concussions, Only Repercussions

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: No Concussions, Only Repercussions Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 23, 2002 It wasn't quite as bad as it looked at first, though you would scarcely believe it from the blood loss..I would probably have to call in some carpet cleaners for the office now, however. They told me when I came to that they'd had to put in 8 stitches, but that was fair enough, didn't really need a hospital bed at this point, but they offered me a well-padded chair... I'd be getting released pretty soon, since there wasn't really much they had to do beyond it, no apparent concussion or fractures..I'd taken worse, they knew it from my occupation and they knew I'd probably be up and about in no time..Which explained why I was back into my office attire so fast..Well, except for the shirt, which was apparently too bloody to wear for now. They told me to take it easy....Ha. I couldn't be bothered to waste the effort on it, I had matches to win and this wouldn't stand in my way. All I could remember was sitting in the office, trying to put in the phone jack, then getting rushed from behind, I think they said that it appeared to have been with the paperweight...Memo to self: Buy styrofoam paperweights. My head was still aching from that one... I didn't remember much past it..I knew that I'd managed to catch him with a swift kick to the balls, but he came back with a charge and that's where it faded out for me...The surprising part was in who'd made the attack. I would've expected this from the Trapezoid, he'd pulled that sort of attack before....I could even have thought Tomas would try it, it wasn't below her...But Nathan? I never saw it coming, he and I had been on pretty good terms before, he'd even stuck up for me after that horrid referee incident..Why had he soured so suddenly on me? What could've compelled him to attack his own co-worker? I knew one possible answer, though it rather bothered me to consider, it was the common link that made for the easiest explanation.. My tag team partner...His girlfriend, though she wasn't very amused when he called her 'babe'. I didn't really think of myself as a threat to his relationship before, I was just a close friend at that point, minding my own business.. But jealousy, like beauty, was in the eye of the beholder. One could become jealous rather easily when one had something to be jealous about, but it always seemed so much less important from my perspective..She was good-looking, certainly, but she was also fickle, as I'd learned from my errors before..I just didn't think of her in that way at the time, there'd been more pressing issues and I was more concerned about keeping her as a strong friend, something that was hard to come by outside of the office now that the fans held disdain for me.. But now, I wasn't really able to think much outside of how I was going to break this to her. She meant a lot to me as a partner and friend right now, but at this point there was the issue of what to do. Stepping aside and getting Nathan off my case wasn't a feasible option, I wasn't just going to let him push me around..However, when she heard about this, he was going to hear about it too no doubt, and he'd know that I had no intentions of giving up my friends for his benefit... Maybe I was overthinking it, after all, he could easily realize that he needed to calm down and take a step back before he acted again or he might end up out of a job..Then it came back to me. The smile...Sadistic as he pulled himself into his stance....Maybe he wouldn't know better. I did, though...I was going to have to use some of that tax money for added security, or at least lock my door a little more often. I could've press charges, after all, the police knew of this, seen it from the blood and the cracks in the wall, but that wouldn't help matters. She'd be caught in the middle even tighter to have me trying to put him in jail, and I didn't want to cause this..I couldn't just step back and concede, but I couldn't push forward and put more strain on our working together...I'd have to tell her, that was certain, she couldn't be left in the dark about this sort of thing or he'd get an ego, a feeling of power and motivation to come after anyone else who he felt was in his way... This was no way to run a business, to have jealous rages go scot-free and leave myself open to further attack. I pulled the cellular phone from my pants pocket, dialing in the somewhat familiar number....A ring...Another...And a third.. 'I'm sorry, but the number you have dialed is temporarily out of the service area. Please hang up and try again later.' I tapped the 'End' button to cancel the transmission..I might as well try and forget it for now..I needed rest to think about this properly, it was too early anyway.. I put the phone back in and let the tension go..I could call later... Nathan wasn't going to stop me from keeping my friend..Nathan wasn't going to stop me from holding my office... And not even Nathan would stop me from holding the PCW Tag Team Championships. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
