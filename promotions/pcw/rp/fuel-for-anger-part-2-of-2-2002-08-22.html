Fuel for Anger...(Part 2 of 2)

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/02 -Note-The bold characters represent Carey writing, and the bold italic characters represent Stephen writing. Drip, Drip, Drip, Drip, Drip… That dripping sound can send a person going mental. Why wouldn’t a company fix such an annoying dripping sound? Then again, this is a prison. If you could recall from last night Carey J. Nowski charged the field at the Little League World Series. It wasn’t exactly a successful attack, but I wouldn’t say any attack on a child is successful. So last night Carey obviously had a mental lapse, and it has cost him a night in prison. I’m sure he’ll be given a hefty fine also. Now he can do nothing but wait until he can be released from prison. He’ll be here for a good while with this annoying drip, which appears to be a leak in the roof. The prison itself it rather crummy, and it seems just right for a prison. The cells have a single bed, a toilet, and a sink. That’s about it. The walls are concrete, the lights are very low, and the cellmates are very quiet. Carey doesn’t seem quite right in a prison cell. All he can do is write on a notepad he has with him. A night in prison is well worth getting my revenge on that kid. Wait…I didn’t even get revenge. Carey lays back on his bed and puts a pillow over his head. He’s lays unmoving for a while, until he bursts out by throwing his pillow. Why doesn’t this damn prison get a new roof? That dripping could drive a man insane. Carey gets up off of the bed, and he prances in circles around his cell. I couldn’t imagine having to live in one of these. I’d go more mental than I already am. Speaking of mental, I wonder what has gotten into me? Things are running through my head that I thought would never happen. Well, I’ll just have to deal with it! If I’m pissed then so be it. Carey sits back down on the bed, and he starts to tap the pen off of the notepad. It is noticeable that he is in another world. I guess prison is another world. What must I do to get people to believe me? I’ve tried aggression, I’ve tried outsmarting them, and I just don’t have many more ideas. I can be violent, but nobody believes me even after that. It’s just getting god damn ridiculous! Nobody in PCW believes that I know the truth. Well one day I’m going to completely snap, and it might even be this Fusion. Yes…this Fusion! Carey gets a deep look on his face, and he gives out an evil little chuckle. This man is definitely going insane. The normal thoughts aren’t running through his mind. It’d be wise to make a guess that Carey is coming up with a plan for Fusion, but who really knows what it could be. The people will finally see my true power and knowledge on Friday. They’ll finally realize that I’m the one who knows the truth. I will hold the PCW and the world in the palm of my hands! Yes, my day will come! Who really needs any of those other people? I can handle everything myself. I know exactly what’s going on. That’s all I need to know. Carey pauses with his writing for a moment, gives an evil chuckle, and once again taps the pen on the notepad. I started my domination on The Pretty Cool Guys and that ignorant referee! This week my domination will continue! A smile comes across Carey’s face as he lays down on his pillow. The notepad and pen slip to the ground, and his eyes flutter shut. So here you have one half of Misplaced asleep in prison, but what has happened to the other half? What is going on with Stephen Cairns? Well, the team is Misplaced, but there actions are similar. They both write a lot when they’re alone, so it wouldn’t a shock if Stephen is writing. Stephen Cairns doesn’t really have much choice of what to do until Carey is released. He’s penned down in a motel room in Williamsport, PA. It’s a small local motel called “The Express Lane Motel”. It’s not at all a big motel, but it’s peaceful. It probably only has twenty rooms though. In one of these rooms are Stephen Cairns, the other half of Misplaced. That room is number I-13. As predicted Stephen is sitting in a chair with a tablet and a bottle of Rolling Rock on a desk next to him. The motel room surrounding him is your typical motel room. It’s a single bed with your sink and counter, television, and a small table in the corner. The bathroom is located next to the sink. It is far from anything special, but as stated before Stephen doesn’t have much of a choice as to what he’s doing. His partner is in prison… My partner is in prison! I never thought that the pudgy little man I pulled off of the New Zealand streets into a cricket game would be in prison. Hell, I expected me to be the first one in prison over here. It’s changed though. Carey is no longer that pudgy little man. He’s not Mr. Fitness, but he sure has bulked up. Ok, so he’s still not physically fit. The man has a lot of rage stored up inside of him though. It’s almost like the tides have turned. Stephen holds a very stiff and serious face as he looks at his tablet. This formally angered man has seemed to calm down over the past few months. As he stated, the tides have turned. I used to be the man who got pissed off easily. I used to be the man who couldn’t control my anger when someone would anger me. Carey taught me some lessons, and he calmed me down a bit. He really did have a big influence on the way I act. I’m still no gentlemen, but I see me more mellow than usual. Carey on the other hand has taken from what I used to be. He’s the man who can’t control his anger. He’s the man who really just wants to take someone’s head off. He doesn’t seem to care anymore, he just wants after somebody. He pauses from his writing, and he sips at his Rolling Rock. It’s tough to tell whether Stephen is upset or concentrating. Strange things have been happening in his life lately. Strange things have been happening in my life lately. The people in my life have been changing. I’ve lost all track of our manager Jackson, and Carey is a new man. It’s not right. Stephen tosses the tablet and pen on the desk next to him, and he finishes his Rolling Rock beer. It appears that he’s wrapping up his night. He gets off of the chair, and he crawls under the covers in his bed. The light goes off, and the television goes on. He’s done for the night. Stephen is asleep, and so is Carey. It appears this tag team has finally settled down for the night. What were we thinking? Nothing can ever go well. A cranky cellmate starts screaming and banging on his cell. This of course wakes Carey up, along with the rest of the cellmates. Finally a guard approaches the yelling cellmate. The normal prison would just tell there cellmate to be quiet, but this isn’t a normal prison. They take the quick solutions! Tranquilizers! That put that guy to sleep real quick! Meanwhile, Carey sits up on his bed with a look of anger. He shakes his head and sits there silently for a moment. Although he really hasn’t spoke for twenty four hours now. He lays back down, but it just doesn’t appear to be comfortable anymore. With a deep sigh, he picks his notepad and pen up off of the ground. I wish that guy would just shut-up! I was finally calm and asleep, but some asshole has to run around screaming. I wish I could just go and tackle that guy. He needs a beating! Anyways, I guess it’s back to my ranting writing! So as I was writing before, my time is coming shortly! This Fusion people will see what a man I can be! I’m not taking bullshit anymore! It has to come to an end! I can’t lose to a nazi anyways. That’s a disgrace to all mankind. He deserves to be beaten, and I will deliver! Yes! What kind of team is a black guy and a nazi anyways? They shouldn’t be teamed together. They shouldn’t be alive! Hopefully I can change that all. These guys are another group who can’t see that I hold the truth. They think of me as a joke, and they just simply put me off! Well I’ve really had enough of it! It’s time to quit putting it on paper, and start putting it into the real world! Everyone will see the truth! I hold to truth! Carey is just about to continue writing, but a police officer approaches his cell. Police Officer: “You can go now! You’ve served your time, and we’ll be letting you know about a fine. Lets not pull any more stunts like this, or you’ll be in here for a lot longer!” The Police Officer opens the cell, and Carey silently walks out. He stuffs his notepad in his pocket, and he follows the officer down the hallway. Drip, Drip, Drip, Drip, Drip…
