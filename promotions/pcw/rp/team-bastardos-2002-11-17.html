TEAM BASTARDOS

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 - 11 - 02 The scene opened on the familiar venue of Gaz Green's apartment. Shroud in the heart of Las Vegas Nevada, high above the density of the streets below it's a setting that has almost became a command post for Primetime Championship Wrestling's dynamic duo, collectively Gaz Green and Nerezza as Pranks With Attitude. Strutting into view, the Emerald Menace shields his eyes from the radiant sun that harshly beats down, magnified by his balcony windows. Clad in a simple pair of shorts, something we'd associate with Cowabunga Connection more so, our defender of the gold, in this case the Tag Team Champion slouches into his leather reclined chair. 'What too do?' 'Oh, what to do?!?' again murmured the once jovial Jester, his body had relaxed but his mind was still ticking over with activity and it was this tug-o-war that troubled the rising star. Peering down at his ash coffee table that sat ominously in the midst of his room, usually used to rest against and would be far from noticeable if it wasn't for the fact that many have slumbered over its surface as a result of its size. Anyway, peering down inspiration beckoned as a notepad sat there, likely a personal belonging of Natalia but it's ownership wasn't the issue here; Gaz now had something to do. T Tournament. To be specific, the Cruiserweight Championship tournament. Another opportunity to adorn gold and show my peers that my contract signing was more a blessing than a burden and now I have a fruitful bout against the under-achiever of Heavy Metal Harry. Fruitful? Well it's almost a bye into the semi-finals but I'm convinced there isn't a roadblock to Alliances2K2, not even the hand of God or the CEO Xavier Kannon for that matter could stall my runaway progress. E Evil. Evil in this case personified by the faction known as 'The Front Office'. A stable lead by the maniacal Xavier Kannon who coincidentally could face me in the Cruiserweight Championship tournament. There has been no love loss between Pranks With Attitude and The Front Office, it has been documented that we are the thorn in their side but that thorn isn't imbedded enough from a personal standpoint. A Alliances2K2. The rookies will look at this event as an opportunity to cement their names but for Gaz 'The Jester' Green it will be a case of running the gauntlet. An ideal night would be to retain the Tag Team Championships and align the gold alongside the acquired Cruiserweight title before taking the team event by storm. The prospect of defeating Playaz Inc, Xavier Kannon and the cream of Wrestle Tattler in one fateful night is far greater than any record I have set in my short PCW tenure but in reality scoring this hat trick would surely place me on a pedestal. .... Ahead of many far more deserving mind you. M Miyagawa-... And not Alexander who manipulated P.W.A and used us as his own private henchmen but his loyal wife, Natalia. Alexander Miyagawa is a name that'll die over time, but through the grim past of the Asylum his wife decided to align herself with Pranks With Attitude, begging the question of 'Why?' Revenge? Spite? Personal Ambition? Whatever the reason, one thing is for certain and that if it weren’t for her involvement in the Championship bout against Playaz Inc, then maybe Gaz Green and Nerezza would still be challengers, rather than Champions. B What kinder letter is B? Bob Vurdo. What do I know about Bob Vurdo? Well he is the Extreme Champion. So was Ellie~! A ATTITUDE. Specifically Pranks With Attitude who really needs to retain the Tag Team Championships because as it stands we are on a collision course with Politically Incorrect’s title reign record. ATTITUDE. Again attitude is something Statuz Quo has injected into the heart of Playaz Inc and his pig ignorance will be his undoing as we head into Alliances2K2, but unlike the last Pay-Per-View 'Red October' there will be no Ellie on hand to table the result in their favour. S Skitzo. What a person to have onboard. He's almost a one-man tag team and has the peculiar habit of talking to himself, conversationally. Skitzo is a veteran of this business and has seen, no, done it all and will certainly be an added boost for the camp of Team Bastardos. Through these positive attributes mind you, there lies one niggling thought, imbedded in the back of my mind - Will his relationship with Syko The Goblin become a factor? T Something this letter spells could define many of the wrestlers backstage but for me 'T' represents-.... Tyler Nelson. Before I came to Primetime Championship Wrestling this man was highly regarded and lost everything he held dear through a simple one-on-one contest against Satan incarnate, yes, he was bullied out of power by none other than Xavier Kannon. However Tyler now represents Five Star Wrestling (FSW) and claims to be the franchise, calling himself 'The Only Six-Star Wrestler In Five Star Wrestling'. To me though Tyler is a stepping-stone to success and even though we have never crossed paths, Alliances could change that. A Arr-... Err-... Not another 'A' Apple. Even wrestlers need to eat and as the parable says, 'An apple a day, keeps the doctor away!' Maybe someone should recite this parable to Redemption Song. R Rezza. Well it's Nerezza. Well it's Nascosto Ireul Nerezza but I simply prefer my pet name of 'Rezza'. What can I say? We've been through thick and thin, from the Academy to New True Internet Championship Wrestling (NTICW) to Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW). A journey I wouldn't change for love or money, it has been a wild ride with many twists and turns but one thing has remained constant and that is simply P.W.A. D DUKE WILLIAMS-.... The protector of the gold, in this case the Cruiserweight Championship. Another sheep of Xavier Kannon's flock, another Front Office member, another thorn in the side of Pranks With Attitude. Now the gold is in his safe keeping I fear the Malice Man may become the Malicious Man and table the Cruiserweight Championship tournament in favour of his Front Office associates, be it Tim Walden, Brandi or even Xavier Kannon himself. O Oblong, well he's a trapezoid but besides the fact that he is named after an absurd shape he remains a threat, not once but twice and quite possibly he may appear in the final of the Cruiserweight tournament meaning we shall square off in a third bout. Remember Gaz 'The Jester' Green versus The H.I.T (Three Match Thriller) S Skitzo-... Again-... Two people contained in the same body, Siamese twins without the deformities, yet partners for life and-.... As the pen scribbled down the remaining comments of the final letter, in this case Skitzo, fellow member of Team Bastardos, the door sprung open, unveiling a picture of beauty-... And Natalia Miyagawa. Waddling in with a hoard of shopping bags, a productive day by the looks of things, Natalia peers over and questions the actions of her client Gaz Green. 'What are you up too?' 'Err-... Arr-... Let me help you with those!' retorted the Conniving Comic, closing the notepad shut, containing his thoughts from the sight of the recently acquired Natalia Miyagawa.
