The Becoming Saga Part 2: Revelations

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th April Entry Date: 10th April After yesterday’s baffling events, I have decided to accept the invitation from the Digital Reality Corporation; I am going to meet this guy at the coffee bar. To be completely honest, I do not quite know what to expect from this because my knowledge of this organisation is patchy to say the very least. I don’t really know what their purpose is, or what they actually do, but hey, it’s worth taking a look. I’m not going to do anything stupid and I am not going to be made to do anything that I do not want to, I’m just going to go down there and see what this guy and his organisation are all about. This could be the start of a new beginning for me … Who knows? - Andy Start Tape The scene slowly fades in inside the apartment block where Andy Murray’s hellhole of an apartment is situated. We are in a long corridor, with many doors at either side. Each door is identical and painted with chestnut tinted varnish. Little golden panels with numbers engraved on them are attached to each of the doors, evens on the left, odds on the right. The wine-red coloured carpet stands out against the cream coloured skirting boards and the cream & wine-red striped wallpaper, which is also decorated with tiny golden crests. At the far end of the corridor, along the small back wall, is a small table that wouldn’t look out of place in the home of a noble, we can just see a small houseplant sitting on the table, with a white coloured payphone sitting behind it. The camera pans along the corridor towards the table … Closer and closer we get, until eventually the camera stops, and turns to the left; we are directly facing one of the many doors, number 204 as the golden number depicts. The small, golden handle of the doors slowly begins to turn, and is pulled open by none other than Andy Murray, as he exits from what is now identified as his apartment, judging by the grin interior beyond the door. Andy closes the door behind him, before turning away form the camera, and locking the door, before sliding the key into one of his pockets. As he turns around and starts to make his way down the corridor, we can see that he is wearing a long, black leather coat, which is opened up revealing a black “Welcome To My Nightmare” t-shirt below. He is also wearing a pair of shades, fading black jeans and a pair of black boots. We hover behind Andy’s large shoulders as he makes his way along the corridor. We can now see that at the other end of the corridor is your average elevator, doors closed. AT either side of the elevator sit a further 2 houseplants, both slightly bigger than the plant that sits on the table. Andy continues to walk slowly down the corridor, his large frame a dominating presence in the empty corridor. Eventually, he reaches the elevator, and presses the glowing red button on the wall. There is a brief “BING” noise as the meter above the elevator doors begins to count down … 26 … 25 … 24 … 23 … 22 … Slowly the red illuminated numbers decrease in value, an expressionless Andy Murray stands patiently, not moving a muscle other than to breath. 21 … 20 … 19 ... 18 … 17 … “BING!” The doors of the elevator slowly slide open, revealing a beautifully furnished elevator suite. Andy steps inside, and turns around to press one of the buttons, when suddenly ... “Hold on buddy!” a voice rings from the other end of the corridor. Andy glances up, and notices an overweight middle-aged man, who has started balding is black hair, running towards the elevator as fast as his stumpy legs can carry him. Andy does not shift his glance from the man as he runs towards the elevator … Where the hell did he come from anyway? Probably from another apartment door. The man finally reaches the elevator, and stops in his tracks as he enters, he slaps his hand against one of the walls, using it for support as he starts to breath heavily: proof that fat blokes have no stamina. Andy takes a step back from the panel which houses the many buttons, as the overweight can stands up straight again. The man regains his stance, and walks across towards the buttons, pondering his choice. “Which floor you headed for buddy?” the man asks Andy, as he slowly turns around to face the much taller PCW Superstar. “Ground” Andy replies as the guy turns around again, and presses the small, illuminated “G” button. There is another “BING!” as the elevator shudders into motion, and begins it’s descent to the ground floor. There is silence between Andy and the man, for a few seconds at least. Out of the blue, the man suddenly turns around, and outstretches his hand towards Andy, looking for a hand shake. ”Ah, you must be Andy Murray right? The wrestler? I loved your work in the PWA! … Oh, I’m Dave by the way,” the guy says. Andy shakes the mans hand hesitantly and slowly. ”How did you know who I am?” Andy enquires. ”I thought everyone would’ve known who you are by now! Someone told me you lived in this building, to be honest I didn’t really believe them at first!” Dave says in an enthusiastic tone of voice, Andy responds with a slow nod. ”Oh, I just moved in across the corridor last night, in case you were wondering” Dave exclaims. One again Andy replies with a nod. For a few moments, there is an uneasy silence between the two, as if one of them, at least, was searching for a thing to say, in attempt to spark up a conversation. Before anything can be said, however, the elevator gradually slows down, eventually coming to a complete halt. The doors slowly slide open. ”Well, this is where we both get off … See you around buddy,” Dave says as he leaves the elevator. To Andy, he seems like one of those incredibly annoying neighbours, you know the kind that are over-enthusiastic 24/7 and take everything way too serious? Anyway, Murray exits the elevator as well, and steps out into the average sized lobby area, which leads out onto the streets. The flooring is marble, and the walls are decorated with the same cream & wine-red wallpaper that was evident upstairs in the corridor – a highly desirable apartment block. The only other people that can be found in this lobby area, other than Andy, are two attractive female receptionists, who stand chatting to each other behind the reception area. Andy starts to walk through the lobby, his heavy black boots echoing around the whole place. As he walks by the reception area, he pulls his keys out of his pocket, and places them down on the desk. ”Back soon …” Andy says as one of the receptionists picks his keys up, and slides them into the relevant cubby-hole on the wall behind. The receptionists continue their meaningless chitchat, as Andy pushes one of the large glass doors open, and exits onto the busy streets of Milwaukee, in the latter stages of the morning rush hour. Andy pauses for a second, and takes a look around. He looks at all the busy people rushing around town, all trying to get to different destinations, and the cars on the street, sitting patiently one after the other, awaiting the green light’s permission to go on their way. His eyes cruise by the construction workers, working on the roadside just across the street, and at the street vendors selling newspapers. ”What a world …” he mutters to himself, as he turns to the left and starts to walk down the busy, busy street. Andy brushes his way past different people all going their different ways, luckily Andy is a large man, any other sized man may have a struggle getting through the constant barrage of pedestrians. Murray sticks out like a sore thumb in this crowd, it’s not every day that you see a 6’7” man dressed entirely in black walking down the street, but luckily for him most of the people are to caught up in their own little worlds to even notice him. Andy knows that the coffee shop is not far away at all, so there is no need to call for a cab or anything. Andy reaches some crossroads, just as a loud buzzer sounds, signalling for pedestrians to cross the road. A flood of people nearly knock Andy off course as he turns the corner, and begins to walk down another equally busy street. This time however, he has a very short distance, and after about 20 seconds or so, reaches his destination. Swallowing hard, Andy pushes the door to “Fabrizio’s Coffee Shop” open, and enters. It was a welcome relief from the hustle and bustle outside, and was a quaint little coffee shop. Decorated with sky blue and white interior, it’s the perfect place to stop off for a break from city life. Andy looks around the shop, which appears to be filled to about half capacity. He continues to look around, until a man dressed in a black suit, with black shades, sitting in the corner, catches his eye. The man looks up, directly into Andy’s eyes, and with a quick head movement, beckons him over. Keeping his eyes fixed on the suited man, Andy dodges between several tables and chairs, occupied and unoccupied, as he makes his way across the room. Andy stops when he reaches the alcoved table that the guy sits in. “You the guy who sent the letter?” Andy asks with a hint of hesitation in his voice. The man again looks up at Andy, and motions for him to sit down. “Please, take a seat …” responds the man in the suit, in a cold tone of voice. Andy does as he is told, and takes a seat across the table from the man. Once Andy is seated, the man pushes a piping hot cup of coffee across the table in Andy’s direction. Andy nods in acceptance, before taking a small sip of the fresh coffee from the cup. There is a brief moment of silence between the two. “I expect you know who I am” the man says, breaking the silence. “Yes.” Andy briefly replies. ”Let me get straight to the point …” the man replies abruptly. ”My name is Clark Simons, I am an agent for The Digital Reality Organisation. Mr. Murray, we have been scouting you for months now, we know things about you that you probably didn’t event know.” ”Oh really?” Andy replies, not buying what Simons is saying at all. ”Yes, really … I think you will find that that coffee is black, with no sugar, you’re favourite” Simons responds, hoping to convince Murray. Andy shrugs. ”Coincidence” replies Andy, still not believing Clark. ”Well, if that is not evidence enough … You live in flat number 204 in the Kingsley Apartment Complex on this block.” ”Yeah … But that’s easy enough to find out” responds Andy, he knows that Simons’s last remark was a lot more convincing. Clark takes a sip from his coffee, before continuing. ”And … We know what happened to your parents.” Andy is motionless, how could this guy know all this stuff about him? ”What is this!? Some kind of sick joke!? Who the hell are you anyways!?” Andy responds, clearly annoyed that this guy knows about Andy’s parent’s death. ”This is no joke Mr. Murray, no joke at all. Believe what I say, for it is nothing but the truth … You’re parents did not die accidentally … They were killed.” ”NO!” Andy yells, covering his ears, he obviously does not want to be reminded of his parents death. Clark reaches under the table, and pulls out a brown document folder. Clark slides it across the table towards Andy. ”There, take a look” Simons says, as Andy slowly opens the folder, revealing a black and white picture of the remains of the Murray family car, after the tragic events. Barely holding his emotions back, Andy notices something that is clearly visible all over the car’s body. Bullet holes. ”You see those bullet holes, Andy? Those were made by the gunshots … You’re parents death was no accident, it was an arrangement,” explains Simons, Andy nods his head in disbelief. Andy is in a near state of shock at the moment, it’s obvious to him that this guy must be telling the truth. ”Who could’ve done such a thing?” Andy enquires. ”Well, if you take a look at the document after that photograph, you should find your answers” Clark responds. Andy moves the photo to one side, revealing a white A4 piece of paper, with the initials “WPA” printed in the centre of the sheet, and the words “World Peacekeeping Organisation” printed below, with a greyscale image of the planet earth in the background. ”WPA?” Andy asks, obviously he has never heard of the WPA. ”Roswell, 1947, a UFO crashed in-tact … Or so we were lead to believe. The United Nations fabricated that whole event to cover up the creation of the WPA” Clark says. ”But what is the WPA?” Andy asks, clearly those last few words from Simons didn’t answer his queries. ”The WPA,” Clark says, ”Is an organisation that was set up by governments around the world, for one sole purpose; liberation. The WPA track down and kill any individual they see as a possible threat to them. They decide who lives and dies, they decide what happens in the world … They are in control. They killed your parents Andy, and they are coming for you as well.” Andy stares blankly at Simons, it’s amazing how just a few minutes can bring forth such shocking revelations. ”That is why we want you to join us Andy,” continues Simons, ”You didn’t know this, but you are facing a daily battle against this guys. They are very close to tracking down your location, and when they do, they will kill you. The Digital Reality Corporation was set up in 1976 as an anti-WPA organisation, recruiting those who they have targeted … And believe me, you have been targeted. Not quite sure what to think at the moment, Murray remains silent, as Clark takes another sip from his coffee. ”Andy, listen to me, joining us will change your life for good, there will be no turning back, but it may save you,” Clark pauses for a moment, to check his watch. ”Listen, I’ve got to leave now … If you are still interested, show up at this address sometime tomorrow …” Simons says, as he pulls out a business car, and hands it to Andy. Clark leaves his seat, and makes his way out of the Coffee Shop, leaving Andy alone. Murray studies the card, it reads: The Digital Reality Corportaion322 47th Street,Milwaukee,Wisconsin. The scene slowly begins to fade out. After these shocking revelations, what does Andy’s future holds? We’ll find out in due course.
