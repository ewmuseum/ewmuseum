Metal Gear Spoofage (part 3)

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/03/02 Don't kill him yet....I want him alive. Leave it to me. I want no more accidents like that Asa Fountain. Or like the several dozen soldiers I've lost to your sheep harem, Argyle. Yes I know. Can you hear me, Solid Snake? A blaring light floods through a warrior's skull. Racking his brain with a shockwave of lightning, pain causing all thought to leave him....Solid Snake looks down to see a trio of despots. Liquid Clyde: There definitely is a resemblance. Don't you think so brother? Liquid smirks, then looks to his right. Then back to Snake....then to his right again quickly, his smile vanishing. Liquid Clyde: Argyle, get the hell off of that sheep! Sniper Argyle: What? Liquid Clyde: Ugh....anyway, I suppose it doesn't matter. I'm off to take care of some launch preparations. Hosselot, DO NOT KILL THE HOSTAGE THIS TIME. Hosselot: I shoot one guy 6 times point blank and everyone thinks I'm some sort of sadist. Whatever. Clyde stomps his way out of the room, leaving Snake alone with Hosselot and Argyle. The latter shimmies over to him, stroking along his chest. Sniper Argyle: Catch you later handsome. With that Argyle shakes out of the room.....Hosselot shakes his head. Hosselot: You have no idea how much that....THING....disturbs me. In any case, we're now going to play a game, Snake. Snake: I'm up for checkers. Hosselot: Shut up. I'm going to run a high voltage current through your body. If the pain becomes too great, just say you give up, and the torture will end. We're going to see what kind of man you ar.... Snake: I give up! Hosselot: .....I haven't hit the SWITCH yet. Snake: I give up! Hosselot: That's cheating! At least let me torture you for a few seconds..... Snake: I give up! Hosselot: (Throwing his remote down) BAH! Get him the hell out of here. A swarm of guards rush the room, yanking Snake rudely down and dragging from the torture chamber to a nearby holding cell. There he is tossed into the corner, where he comes to a rest next to what seems to be a very moldy corpse. Snake: (Staring) Eugh. Making the universal 'icky icky' hand motion, Snake begins to scoot away....then something about the corpse catches his attention. Snake: .....is that Asa Fountain? Examining the body more closely, the warrior barely notices a willowy form sneaking around the edge of the cell. Byron: I'M HERE SNAKE! Snake: (Jumping) AGH! Snake slams his head off the low roof of his cell, falling to the ground in agony and rolling about. Byron: Oops, sorry. Here let me help you up.... Snake: Don't touch me. Byron: What's your problem? Snake: I don't have any problems with your type. I just don't want any part of it, that's all. Byron: What type? Snake: You know....YOUR type. Snake makes the universal 'come see come sa' hand motion, utterly baffling the poor scientist. Byron: Whatever. I'm opening the door to this thing....and....is that a corpse over there? Snake: Wasn't me. Byron: Well, I'll take your word for it. I'll be back later. Snake: I still won't be interested in you. Byron: .....what are you TALKING about? Snake: It's okay, open mind. Byron: Ugh! The man runs away in the opposite direction, leaving Snake to escape through the opened doorway. Onward he journies, out of the building itself and to a large tower....scaling to the top, he peers over the edge. Snake: Be quite a drop if something was to knock me off of hereAGH!!!! AGH!!!!! AGH!!!! Once again almost as if on cue a massive explosion sends Snake flying through the air. He just manages to catch on to the railing of the tower, hanging on with one hand as what seems to be a helicopter rises into the sky. Liquid Clyde: Hahahahaha! Snake: Argh! Using all of his strength, the hero moves from one hand to the other just dodging a barrage of machine gun fire. Snake: Why are you doing this? What did I ever do to you? Liquid Clyde: You live. Don't question the motives of a maniacal super villain! With another dark chuckle, the man with the burned face readies his armament. He then fires, sending exploding hot death directly as Snake who has no choice but to let go, falling into the endless black. Liquid Clyde: .....that was easier than I thought. Down, down through eternity falls a hero. Twisting, turning, sinking into a void, only the sound of the wind whistling by and the mad, mocking laughter of his brother. The ground awaits below him, he senses this....coming closer, closer, closer. Then, a thud beneath him. Snake looks around in bewildered amazement as he finds himself in a large chair. A sense of peace surrounds him as he floats slowly to the earth, with a shake of his head he steps out onto safe ground. Snake: (Touching his ear) Colonel.... Colonel: Do not question the power of deus ex machina. Just go with it. Snake: Gotcha. Leaving behind the savior of so many wandering plots, Snake marches onward into a snowfield. The shuffling tundra blinds him, stinging his eyes but he perseveres....until finally he is distracted by a tiny red dot moving around on his face. Snake: (Waving his hand) Shoo! Go away! Damn kids with their laser pointers. Colonel: Snake you may want to consider ducking. Snake: What, why? Bethany: DUCK YOU IDIOT!!!!! Suddenly a wave of pain shoots through Solid Snake's balls, causing him to double over in agony. Just as he does a bullet whizzes over his head, narrowly avoiding a passage through his brain. Snake: AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! Bethany: QUIT WHINING!!!!!! Tears running from his eyes, Snake drops to his knees and clutches his codec. Byron: What's wrong? Snake: My balls.... Byron: Hm.... Snake: Stop thinking about my balls! Byron: I WASN'T! Sniper Argyle: I can see you perfectly from here. Snake looks on in shock as the voice of the strangely androgynous sniper speaks over his codec. Snake: Where are you? Show yourself! Byron: Argyle, hizzies. Sniper Argyle: Hizzies. Snake: ....you two know each other? Byron: Well, we hang out. You know. Snake: Yeah, I know. Freak. Byron: Stop that. Sniper Argyle: Come my dear, the sheep are so lonely. We want your company. Snake: I'm surrounded by FREAKS! Sniper Argyle: What is his problem? Byron: I have no idea. Sniper Argyle: Well, we'll talk after I dispose of our friend here. Byron: Okay. Snake: BYRON! The codec transmission is cut off as another bullet whizzes by Snake's head. He throws himself into the snow, trying to figure out how to deal with the situation as best he can. Snake: (Thumb in mouth) I want my mommy! The warrior attempts to peek over his sheltering snowbank, but another bullet quickly forces him down again. He clutches his ear again desperately. Snake: Byron, come in! Byron: So yeah, I find that an herbal mix before bedtime really reduces the effect of aging on the skin. Sniper Argyle: I will have to try that....pardon me a second. Another bullet whizzes through the air, catching a peeping Snake on the right shoulder. Snake: AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! Sniper Argyle: So you were saying? Byron: Mix it with a few slices of cucumber, then....little to your right. Argyle swings his/her head to the right just in time to avoid a killing shot from Snake. Sniper Argyle: Thank you. So have you tried the aloe veraARGH!!!!! Argyle is cut off as a remote control missile strikes him/her directly in the back, exploding on contact. The sniper flies into a nearby tree, crashing hard into the snow. Snake: Ugh. Up through the frozen tundra stomps Solid Snake, to the fallen sharpshooter. Blood sprays from Argyle's lips as he/she coughs, speaking hoarsely. Sniper Argyle: I am lung shot.....you cannot save me. Snake: ......first off, I shot you in the back with a missile. Second of all, why would I possibly want to save you? I just shot you in the back with a missile. That isn't the act of a humanitarian. Sniper Argyle: You are a true hero. Just finish me quick. Argyle looks to the sky wistfully, remembering back. Plaintive music seems to fill the air, as he flashes back to happier times. Sniper Argyle: ......I was raised on a battlefiel.... A large BLAM cuts off the sniper in mid sentence, along with the majority of its head. A bit away a very stunned Byron appears, falling to his knees and looking at Snake. Snake: What, she told me to finish him. Byron: (Stares) Snake: What? Did you not hear him say 'finish me quick?' Byron: (Stares) Snake: Fine, be that way. Off storms Snake again, entering a large building and leaving a silently weeping Byron behind. Snake: Lousy freaks. Why I oughta..... Down a set of stairs he marches, into what seems to be a massive blast furnace. Our warrior looks all around, surveying the situation.....to his right, an elevator leads straight down. To his left, a very narrow platform heads to the other side over an extremely nasty pit of molten iron. Snake: .....tough choice. With a shrug, Snake strolls to the right. He pushes the button to the elevator....nothing. Snake: Hm. Again he hits the button....again, again. Still nothing. Snake: .....you're just gonna make me crawl over the burning hot stuff aren't you? Sighing deeply, our intrepid hero walks to the narrow platform on the opposite side of the room. Gingerly he places one foot upon it, then winces as it creaks beneath the minute pressure. Snake: Okay....count of three. One....two....three! Steadily and slowly, Snake eases himself across the narrow beam. He grips the wall behind him with both hands, scooting across ever so slowly. Suddenly a ringing sounds in his ear. Master: Snake, get a load of this email I got forwarded. A nun, a rabbi and a writing desk walk into a bar.... Snake: Now's not the TIME, Master! Continuing on, Snake slinks across the passage....then his eyes widen as a large rat comes crawling from the far side. Snake: No, shoo! Shoo! The small rodent inches closer, easily keeping his balance. He sniffs at Snake's boot, his whiskers tickling his leg. Snake: No! Beat it I say! Ignoring his cries, the huge mouse hops up on the warrior's foot. It then shifts a bit, working its way up his leg. Snake: No, NO! Argh! Not there! No! Solid wiggles and squirms, desperately trying to shake the rat loose as his ear rings again. Byron: .....been there before. Snake: I don't want to know about your experiences with rodents! Especially right now! Byron: I meant dangerous situations! Jeez! Forget it! The scientist signs off again, as Snake dances atop the small platform. Snake: Oh well.....the deus ex machina will save me again. I can't die here. Meanwhile on a tropical island, a large golden chair lounges on a beach. Several hula skirt clad chairs surround him while he sips a margarita and relaxes. Snake: .....agh! Finally in desperation our hero leaps, just catching his fingertips on the far ledge. He then pulls himself up with the last of his strength, sitting on the edge and angrily shaking his leg till the rat escapes and scurries away. Snake: Bah. Crawling to his feet, the legend continues ever onward.....down an elevator, to a frozen chamber. Here all manner of birds assail him, which he attempts to pick at with his pistol. Snake: Shoo! Goddamn animals. I'll kill the lot of you, you stupid little AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!! A box tumbles out of nowhere, knocking the hero off of his feet. From high above a mammoth leaps to before him, sneering and brandishing a huge gatling gun. Vulcan Meanstreak: Welcome, cossack. Snake: Urgh. Snake tries to stand, but Streak merely bats him across the room. Our warrior crashes into a pile of rubble, ending up on top of a staircase leading up. Vulcan Meanstreak: End of the road, cunt. Snake: Can't we negotiatAGH!!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! Again the massive Inuit swats our hero, knocking him straight up the staircase. Snake stands groggily, then sprints up as fast as he can. Snake: Come on, I'll be your friend! Vulcan Meanstreak: Maybe, if you hadn't of killed my birds ya shit. Snake: Doh. Running as fast as he can, Snake heads upstairs as he yells behind himself. Snake: Well, maybe if your birds could learn to use the toilet instead of shitting on the ground I have to walk on..... Vulcan Meanstreak: You really got a deathwish, doncha? Snake: No, I'm just trying to tell you your birds dribble shit everywhere, and STOP SHOOTING AT ME!!!!! Solid ducks just in time to avoid being ripped to shreds by a barrage of automatic fire. He then darts all the way up the staircase to the roof of the building, where he looks out over the snowy wastes. Vulcan Meanstreak: End o' the line. Snake: Uh....uh.... Vulcan Meanstreak: Uh uh what? Soon as I manage to fit this gun through the door you'll be uh uh full o' holes, meat. Meanstreak struggles to fit his weapon through the tiny passage....in desperation Snake feels his pockets. Coming up with a nickel, a movie stub, he groans....then finally finds a spork in his grasp. Snake: (Shrugging) AIYEEE!!!! With a terrible battle cry Snake charges forward, plunging the deadly plastic spoon/fork into the eye of Vulcan Meanstreak. The monster drops his gun, stumbling forward to the edge of the roof. Vulcan Meanstreak: This has become a running gag, hasn't it. Snake: Maybe. Vulcan Meanstreak: Very well....I shall let you in on key plot points. Snake: Come again? Vulcan Meanstreak: The man you saw die in the cell was not Asa Fountain. He was in fact Decoy Octopus, a member of GREYHOUND. Snake: You're kidding. Vulcan Meanstreak: Your ally, Dr. Bethany is actually a serial killer, and she has injected you with a testicle destroying virus. Snake: That bitch. Vulcan Meanstreak: JFK was shot by a pair of gunmen working for the CIA. Snake: I knew it. Snake: And Magnum PI was deemed too much of a niche show, and was thus cancelled. Snake: Bastards. Vulcan Meanstreak: Solve the rest of the riddle yourself. Farewell. With that the behemoth tumbles backwards, falling, falling to mother earth. Leaving behind only a solitary warrior, who turns back the way he came..... To Be Continued
