A Look In The Hall

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/02/02 Champion. What makes a champion? Is it simply the title he carries? The people who would follow that person till the very end? How much does must a person give up to be a champion? The answers to these questions are quite simple. A champion means the very best a company has to offer at any certain level. The World champion is the symbol of the companies very best of all. But every company also has other champions. The men that are to set standards and make the company the very best there is in the world today. The company that is the standard that which other companies try and follow, copy, and attempt to become better than the one that is currently the best in the world. That company above all else is Primetime Championship Wrestling. They have many great champions past and present. But there is one champion now who is by far no where near the standard of a real PCW champion. A man who has stumbled backwards into one of those important spots where he should be setting new standards of greatness. Instead he makes the title he carries and himself look very pathetic and meaningless. But this Sunday all of that will change. Change forever for the better of that title and the greatest company of all time PCW! Steve walks through the big double doors into the building at a very deliberate pace. He wears a pair of black Adidas pants with white stripes down the legs, black Adidas shoes, and a dark blue T-shirt with, “Don’t Be Afraid...Be Convinced!” across the front of the shirt in black letters. His eyes a cold blue set in total focus and determination. He walks through the corridor of the building toward a set of doors to the back that has an “Entrance” sign above them. As he walks toward the doors the camera shifts angles getting the back of his shirt that has, “Reign of Pain Tour 2002-Eternity” in large red letters. He walks to the doors and enters into the next section of the building. He takes two steps inside then stops and looks around the very large room that has very few things in it. But the items in the room are of great significance. The place he stands in now is the Primetime Championship Wrestling Hall of Fame. Each inductee has his own section that are divided and have a bronze statue of the mans head, a huge mural on the back of the section giving the run down of their career’s and their highlights. In each person’s section there are also replica’s of every championship they ever held and for the one’s the held more that once a symbol on each belt representing how many times they held it. There are many sections already set up, but only five currently have these items inside of them. Steve begins to walk through the section. He goes by each section and looks over the things in that section; they’re to keep the man’s accomplishments forever linked to the company they worked for. After he walks past every section he starts over and this time when he stops in front of the first section the camera zooms in to see who he is looking at. The first one belongs to that of PCW great and very first inductee Kremmen. Steve stands there looking over all the things in the section as a smirk forms over his face. The great Kremmen. Very first man to grace these great and honorable halls. The man who had one of the greatest feuds of all time with the “true” Monster of Primetime Championship wrestling. One of the founding fathers of the great company we are now all so fortunate to get to work in. He is the very first ever World Champion. This man brought great honor and prestige to the World Championship. He will always be remembered greatly for all he did here. (Steve moves over to the next section. This one is for a superstar maybe not so well known. One of the founding fathers of Hardcore, Ed.) Now here is a man who truly gave everything he had for the things he wanted and believed in. His battles with TCM are considered not only some of the best ever, but some of the more brutal ever. He is what a champion is all about. Not the most talented or smart wrestler in the history of this company, but he had a great deal of heart and determination. The desire to be the very best in the one great skill he had; hardcore wrestling. He more than accomplished that feat by winning the Extreme title on two separate occasions and bringing glory and honor to the division by giving his all every single time he stepped into that arena. He has something to offer us today in our lesson as well. (Steve walks over to the very next section. He stands there a moment and laughs. Then he folds his arms across his chest. The section he looks upon at this moment is of one of the more entertaining wrestlers in history, The Chef.) The Chef. One of the all time greatest pioneers for this great company. He took part in many stables and groups that had success in one manner or another. But it was again the titles and the way he fought for them and to defend them that is his greatest gifts to this company. He had one of the greatest immediate impacts on the company ever. Imagine getting to slam a frying pan off the head of that jackass the Reverend. That would be a great way to start your career here. And any man who could put up with such an outspoken woman is a tough S.O.B in my not so humble opinion. Look at this list of accomplishments; A TV title reign, Tag Titles, a World title, oh and look what we have here, the Rising Star title. And there was even a Continental title reign. This man is one of the absolute best I have ever seen in this business. (Steve moves on to the next section and a frowns goes over his face. He looks over the Frisco section with a scowl on his face. )Now this is a real load of crap. No doubt Frisco was a great champion and held many titles, but he is one of the biggest ass kisser of the fans I have ever seen. His whole career was about doing what the fans would love to see done, and have them cheer him wildly for it. He is the epitome of what I believe a superstar should never be. He spent his entire career trying to please those ungrateful sacks of shit. He did the absolute worse thing for his career he could have ever done. By attempting to make the fans happy all the time is what cut his mediocre career short. He had the ability and chances to be considered the best of all time possibly. But none of those matters because he sold his soul to those losers that cheered him. He had a nice little feud with a great friend of mine in his career though. Well if you could call it a feud. Can a one sided battle be considered a great feud? I am not sure but sure gave him the title of being that man’s bit#h. Just like Ripper and his two girlfriends are going to be mine on Sunday. I better move on before the site of this crap makes me sick. (He moves on to the very last section with anything in it. The section belonging to the Monster they call Meanstreak. Steve gets a very evil grin over his face.) Now here was an athlete with some class and integrity. He always gave you more than you could handle whether the match was for a title or just because you were unlucky enough to draw a free ass kicking from him. This is the man you would really like to immolate when you talk about being a great champion. I mean lets face it the only other person that would have went out of his way to do what ever it took to win, was Stewart Hix. Those two men would do what ever they had to for victory and the fans never mattered to them in the slightest. That is the only way to be in this business. All you should ever do is go out and take what you want and do what you want, no matter who it pisses off and that is exactly the way I am now and always will be. Soon enough I will make the fans realize that they only thought they have hated some of the men who came before me. They haven’t seen anything yet. Steve turns and walks back toward the way he came in. He leaves through the doors and walks back through the lobby to the front entrance. As he walks out the door he knocks a fan down who is trying to come in through the doors. Steve just laughs at the young man who gives him a dirty look and keeps on walking. He continues to walk toward the parking lot where he has parked his vehicle. Along the way to the vehicle he begins to speak again. Every one might wonder why I went to the Hall of Fame today for our little lesson. I came here to point out to each of my three opponents why they haven’t got a chance of beating me in the match on Sunday. Forget for a moment that there is a title on the line. We will just put that on the back burner for a moment. For various reasons each of my opponents seem to have more important concerns on their minds. Lets start first with the weakest link of the match, the current champion Lance Bell. Bell you got the chance of a lifetime last Sunday when you faced and some how managed to beat Red Rock for the Rising Star title. How you were able to accomplish this is not important to me personally, but goes a long way in proving that the sun will shine on every dogs ass once in a life time. Your one shining moment has already come and gone. The only thing you now have to look forward to for the rest of your career is the great big beating you have waiting for you. Maybe you will get lucky and one of the not so talented members of the match either Ripper or Sylo will get their hands on you and you will get a little bit less of a beating. Then maybe they will not have to carry you from the ring on a stretcher and maybe you could just crawl toward the back on your stomach. Either way you would be wise to not count on being able to hold on to your title. The second loser in this match up is that mentally handicapped Jay Sylo. Oh poor little Jay has such an inner struggle that is taking his mind away from what is more important. Well Jay here is a news flash for you; I do not care about any of it! It doesn’t make a bit of difference to me in the slightest whether it is the old Jay or the new one. I have no problem with kicking either one around the ring and putting their ass out of the title contention. While you go around and thank whomever for showing you the way, and climb your mountains to symbolize your inner struggle and have your little pathetic dreams about warrior battles with evil monsters. Go ahead and keep focusing on those things that neither matter nor will they get you any closer to the Rising Star title than you are right now. That jobber Hank is closer to winning the World Title right now than you will ever be to winning the Rising Star title. And finally we have the biggest piece of elephant shi# in the entire PCW Ripper Ryckman! Ripper I have even more reason for beating you for this title than anybody in the match. To hand you your first loss, to get revenge for you paying off the ref to save yourself in our debut match, and to win my first title at your expense are all great reasons to humiliate you on Sunday night. Ripper you think that having the Pay-Per-View called Madness is fitting for you. Well you are slightly right, it is fitting for the simple fact that your loss will be just another thing in long line of maddening failures for you in your crappy life. As if being ugly, brain dead, and having the woman you love laugh at your advances are not bad enough to cripple your already none extent self esteme, then this loss will be the thing to push you over that edge you live your stupid life on. Sunday night I will make good on the promise I got cheated out of on Tuesday. Sunday night you will tap out to the Tormented Soul and I will become the new Rising Star Champion! Now what a fitting end that will be huh Ripper. You give up to a Tormented Soul. That is what makes this a very fitting PPV match Mr. Ripper. Your poor little tormented mind having to tap out to an even more powerful Tormented Soul! So you can all three relax, stay calm, and just be prepared for the fact that you are all three going to be losers and come up short of your hopes of actually winning the match up. Sweet dreams for now young mental morons. Steve reaches the parking lot where his Tahoe is sitting. He unlocks the door and opens it up. Steve climbs into the seat and turns on the vehicle on. He lets it warm up a moment and then shuts the door and backs out of the parking spot. He pulls the vehicle out onto the road down the road. He makes a right turn at the next corner and the scene fades away to black.
