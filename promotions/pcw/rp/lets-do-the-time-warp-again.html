LETS DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN!!!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: TIME WARP TIME! [I]…And as the clouds darkened, and lighting flashed, the sky beginning to swirl, Bjorguerk the Goblin warlord approached the legendary blade. There was an essence of darkness filling the hearts of all across the land. Today meant the eminent doom of all the Earth and it’s people. They’re soon to have been crowned and rightful heir to the sword; dead. Brutally slaughtered by the men of Bjorguerk, the filth whom would never have faced the fighter for salvation face to face. For many years, he has plagued the Earth with darkness. Building a civilization of the once outcast race of creatures, he took dominion where he deemed fit with force. And now was his hour at hand, the moment that would make him the most unstoppable overlord of the land. Followed by his Goblin minions, who tread by foot, Bjorguerk trotted up the hill upon his black steed, to the location of the stone. “There.” He said, thrusting out his black sword, its tip pointing straight ahead. “My destiny lies within that rock! Forward my minions, it is the darkest hour for those who have purged our very existence so long! Vengeance will be ours!” His army roared to the heavens, and charged onwards up the hill. There was no one to stop them. No one that could. Bjorguerk dismounted, and slowly examined the stone… But more importantly… THE BLADE HELD CAPTIVE WITHIN IT! THE LEGENDARY EXCALIBUR! “Now it will be mine…” Hissed the evil one, reaching out for the hilt of the sword. “Nothing can stop me.” But, Bjorguerk did not obtain it as easily as he thought he might… Rushing at the dark one with fire in her eyes, no longer allowing herself to be the damsel in distress, Sarah lunged, “ARTHUR WOULD HAVE NEVER LET THIS HAPPEN!” she screamed, before being tugged back the binding chains that hold her. The minions of the overlord restrain the lady as the Goblin warlord laughs at the spectacle, truly delighted. “Surely, you must be mistaken… Arthur is dead. His son as well. The blade is mine. You’ve served your purpose, now take her away…” Narrowing his eyes on one of his minions, he hisses. “Kill her.” “MURDERER!” Shrieked Sarah, over and over, as they began to drag her off… Then, cackling evilly, Bjorguerk reached once again for the hilt. But, the heavens clapped with rage, sending a massive bolt of lightning down, striking the blade. Bjorguerk fell back, thrown by the force of the bolt, many of the Goblin underlings shattered by the shock… “NOT SO FAST, BJORGUERK!” He called out from the smoke. “LET THE GIRL GO! I WILL NOT LET YOU SLEW ONE OF MINE, AGAIN!” “No! It can’t be!” Bjorguerk screamed, trying to crawl away! “There’s no way! How is this possible?!” “It is I who returned the sword to the stone…” he said as he stepped out into sight. “And it is only I who will free it.” And as Bjorguerk wailed, the Almighty King Arthur stepped over him, Excalibur in hand. And then… Then… Uh-oh…[/I] “Then what?” “Errmmm… Ze king was run over by poparotzi? Yeah, daz it. And ze awesome Bjorguerk lived happily ever after!” “LIAR!” “No…” “LET ME SEE IT!!” We are reunited with that maniacal master of evil, Syko the Goblin, as he springs offensively at his 2nd in command, the assisting Dr. LA Borodinsky! The old mad scientist slams the hardcover book in his hands shut, and, clutching it tightly to his chest, he turns making a hobbled break for it… Syko the Goblin: “Not so fast!” But, the geezer is not nearly fast enough to escape, not with the agile Goblin on his heels! No really… Syko is clinging to the poor mans ankles with wrapped around arms, being dragged along. Finally, the unfortunate LAB topples, falling to his back, but maintaining a tight hold on the book. STG: “GIVE!” LAB: “Trust me, zere is nothing in it! I swear!!!” Scrambling along the length of the old man, Syko perches atop his chest, and forcibly rips it from his arms. LAB: “VAIT!!” But, Syko is already up, pacing back and forth as he vigorously flips from page to page. STG: “(Rambling as he quickly skims…) Sword… blah, blah, blah… Murderer… …Here we go… ‘And as the pathetic beast wailed for agony, the returned Arthur reeled back his blade…” The Goblin’s jaw drops. Silence. Awkward silence. STG: “DAMMIT! ANOTHER ONE!” Scowling, Syko slams the book shut. STG: “BY GOD, HOW IN DAMNATION COULD THIS WALKING CORPSE ARTHUR HACK OFF MY BRETHRENS HEAD?! THAT’S IT! INTO THE FIRE WITH YOU!!” The Goblin hurls the story into the fire… Blazing, the large bonfire of books illuminates the entire study of Goblin HQ, much to a frustrated Syko’s satisfaction. Well, it isn’t much of a study anymore. The item that once filled the shelves on the walls now fuels the bonfire in a large smoldering heap in the center of the room. LAB: “…Vy?” Kneeling in the dancing glow of the fire, LAB shakes his head in disgust. LAB: “All zat literature…” STG: “Hmph! Literature is for the weak! 1574 books, and yet none dare to tell the tale of the almighty, all-powerful, and all invincible Goblin like they should! Rather, they mock the feeble minds of humans with blasphemy such as ‘Prince Charming,’ and the ‘Damsel in Distress’… Defilement of the TRUTH!!!” Looking up to the Goblin, LAB snorts, utterly disgruntled by Syko. LAB: “You had to burn all ze books?!” Clenching his fist, Syko pulsates with anger, thoughts of Pendragon trying to get the TV Title from him flowing through his head. STG: “Drastic measures are the only answer… To LIES!!!” LAB: “VAT ZE HELL DID YOU EXPECT FROM A BOOK ENTITLED, ‘ZE SWORD AND ZE STONE VII: PAYBACK TIME’ ?!?!?!? ALL OF ZESE BOOKS WERE OF VALIANT KNIGHTS, NOT VALIANT GOBLNS!” STG: “WHAT WAS I THINKING?! WHAT WAS I THINKING?! YOU FOOL! I WAS…” Stopping abruptly, Syko contemplates the question for a moment. STG: “I…?” There is no answer. Not at first. The light of the fire cascades over Syko’s face, as his expression becomes that of sorrow. Sighing, the Goblin drops his head in shame, shaking it in utter dismay. STG: “I suppose I have been a fool… To expect the impossible. All these books, and the searching recently. It has all been me trying to find some kind of loophole.” LAB: “(Quirking a curious brow.) Vat do you mean?” STG: “(Addressing space.) Insolent fools. I can’t be labeled as a mockery for their pathetic existences. Throughout the ages, they have been nothing but walking punching bags.” The old man studies the Goblin carefully, slowly rising to his feet. This is a side of the Goblin he has never seen before. A bit less erratic, but more hateful than ever. LAB can see it his eyes. They reflect the image of the bonfire, as though an apparition of the fire burning within him now. STG: “Who?” LAB: “Yes… who?” STG: “Why… Goblin’s of course.” LAB: “(Agreeably.) Ooohh… Zat seems about… VAAAAAT?!” Utterly disturbed, the elder moves quickly to Syko, reaching out with frail arms. Taking the small Goblin by the shoulders, the old man shakes Syko, like there are demons within that must be rattled out. LAB: “Lord Syko, vat’s wrong!? Zere must be somezing kapoot in your head! More so zan usual, ya?! Ve musn’t let you be sick, I do not vant to be back in ze unemployment line… I mean… I vould be very sad…” Suddenly, Syko breaks free, arms lashing out to force LAB back. Clenching his fists and grinding his teeth, Syko responds in anger… STG: “DAMMIT, YOU OLD COOT! Do you not see what this means? Are you blind to the unavoidable demise of yours truly to that strapping dog of the public, Vennaman Pendragon?!” LAB: “Hold on…” STG: “(Continues, raising his tone.) I WILL LOSE the TV TITLE to that doer of good deeds, simply because of my place in the order of the food chain, and there is not a single thing I, or anyone else can do about it! Since the dawn of time, Goblin’s like me have been nothing more than easy prey for the common man, let alone such a valiant knight like Pendragon!” LAB: “BUT ZIRE, you always have declared supremacy as a Goblin, categorizing ze race as divine! Vat brings fourth such a change of heart now?” The crazed Goblin throws his head back, and lets out an honest roll of laughter, that echoes throughout the study. STG: “Bwahahahahaha! Lies, lies, lies!” Suddenly, Syko’s laughter dribbles to nothing but heavy sobbing, and his legs wobble like rubber beneath him, refusing to support his heavy heart. As he drops down to his knees upon the hard floor, his knuckles drop to ground zero as well… Again and again and again… LAB watches the Goblin as he furiously pummels the floor, but cant find the words to say anything. STG: “I… Tried… to… be… unlike… any… before… me! (He stops punching the ground, allowing his cut up fists a rest.) But, the facts don’t lie!” The Goblin points to the mound of FICTIONAL burning books. STG: “No matter how cunning, or how evil, or how enginuitive… I will never be able to escape the shadow of my ancestors. Primitive, greed guided minds. I am just like them, only with more technology! MY PAST WILL HAUNT ME, FOR I AM BORN! NO MATTER WHAT I DO, PENDRAGON WILL NEVER DIE! WHY?!” LAB: “Uhh…” Throwing his arms in the air, Syko shrieks out in rage! STG: “WHY WONT THEY ALL JUST DIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIE?! WHY?! Why?! Why? Why…?” Syko stops himself, progressively having slowed his tantrum down. Scratching his bulbous head, he asks himself once again, ‘Why?’ His twisted mind ponders the thought, vividly painting a new picture… a new way of viewing the aspect of Goblin existence. Silently, he climbs to his feet, and takes a moment to casually brush himself off. Then, it hits him. Perhaps a smeared sketch in comparison to what it could be at first, it’s enough to turn his distraught frown upside down. Into a maniacal grin. Rubbing his hands together, the evil little Goblin snickers with excitement, teasing curious old LAB with … well… confusion. LAB: “Vat? Vat is it?!” STD: “So… So very simple all along! To escape the past of my ancestors… I must altogether change it!” Scratching his head, LAB responds only with silence and a blank expression. STD: “No time for chatter, old man! TO THE LABORATORY!” And with that, Syko bolts for the door! LAB on the other hand stays behind, rubbing his chin in wonderment… LAB: “Zire… Don’t you zink zat ve should maybe put zat out?” Syko stops himself, turning back to look at his second, who directs him with a pointing finger towards the fire hazard in the middle of the study. STG: “Leave it. As an example.” LAB: “But, It vill surely burn…” STG: “LEAVE IT AS AN EXAMPLE FOR THOSE WHO OPPOSE THE GOBLIN RACE!! NOW, to the laboratory!” Pleading still, the old mad scientists attempts are futile as Syko has already vanished from the room. --- “Muwahaha! It is so perfect, I commend myself greatly!” As though the busy bug has bitten Goblin HQ, frantic work has begun in the laboratory. Sparks flying, theories clashing, and evil brewing… “Pendragon… No! All of civilization is surely doomed! The Goblin’s will reign supreme! Total conquest! WORLD DOMI…” “Zire!” Syko acknowledges the old coot… LAB: “Lord Syko, how exactly do expect zis…” LAB pauses a moment to wave his hand to the heavy chunk on cast iron that Esther, 3rd in command (Secretary, mechanic, manly bouncer…) of Goblin HQ, chisels away at with a torch. Statistically, it has got to weigh at little less than a ton. LAB: “…To work?” More than happy to oblige his scientific assistant with an explanation, Syko pulls out a roll up blueprint, and lays it upon a table, quickly spreading it out flat. The posted is titled ‘Time Machine,’ and is a pretty well drawn out plan for a complex devise. STG: “Well, it all starts out with this little puppy… You see, going back in time to the days of which Goblin kind still had a decent population to mold into something useful, we will become one with them… LAB shakes his head ‘No,’ not that Syko notices… STG: “We will unite them, and teach them of our own technologies, to further advance them above those bastards that would vanquish them for so long! It could take years, but in time, we will have made them into a force reckon with!” LAB: “No… Vat I meant…” STG: “WE WILL BE THEIR KINGS, TRAINING AN ARMY THAT FOR ONCE WILL DEFEAT THE LIKES OF PENDRAGON’S KIND! AND WHEN WE RETURN TO OUR OWN TIME, THE RACE OF GOBLIN’S WILL BE THE RULERS, AND THEY WILL SEE ME, AND PROCLAIM ME AS THE RETURNING MESSIAH WHO MADE THEM…” LAB: “LORD SYKO!” STG: “WHAT?!” LAB: “I am talking about ze time machine itself! How vill zis verk!?” Chuckling, Syko pats his employee on the back in a humbling gesture. STG: “Oh, forgive my, old man… I forget how slow you’re feeble old mind works these days…” Pinpointing the device on the blueprint, the Goblin tries to ejaculate his genius down the geezer’s throat with an intellectual mind. STG: “AsoppositeIonswhichthisdevicewillcreatecompetehurledintospaceatanaproximate speedof7462842.7472 km/persecwitha66.4234degreearctobecomefixiatedwiththeEarth’s orbitalrotationbutonlyagainsttheflowoftataconstantlyincreasingspeedinordertocausethesenseorolltodifusethefrictionlost…” If you can understand, let us know. STG: “…stoptheEarthasitcontinuesmotionthrustingnewfrictionfrom theoppositeionstotryandpulltheEarth’srotationwith…BUTtheflowoftimeandcosmoswould tryandreturnthecurrentsmaintainingthesameasalwaysrotation.TheIonshoweverwouldcontinuetorisein km/persecfightingtheriftsoftimeliterallycuttingaholeintoeverythingthattimecontrolsinour stratacreatingamassivegapingvortexthatwouldserveasagatewaytothepast…” Suddenly, the Goblin is cut off by a slap to the back of the head. Rubbing it in disbelief, he looks to the old man with disgust. LAB: “You nincompoop! I UNDERSTAND YOUR DIGRAMS! HOW DO EXPECT TO LAUCH ZE IONS 7462842.7472 KM/PER SECOND?! Syko rubs his chin… LAB: “Don’t ask, zere is no way! Nothing goes zat fast, and even if zere vas, ve have no access to it! Zis cannot verk!” STG: “Maybe not…” Grinning, Syko nods in good humor, as yet another ingenious idea just pops into his head. STG: “But, we might be able to settle for the next best thing…” --- “Let me get this straight. You let him crash you all in a ’89 Chevy Nova on the freeway because you were all trying to… Go back in time?” Nodding from his hospital bed, Dr. LA Borodinsky confirmed the question of one Tyler Nelson. LAB: “Zat is correct, ya.” Shaking his head in disbelief, Nelson rubs his head for a moment, takes a deep breath, and looks to the old mad man again. Tyler Nelson: “With a 1 ton hunk of metal tied to the damn thing?!” Once again, the old man nods. Putting his hands on his hips, Nelson takes in the scene of the hospital room of the New Jersey hospital. Shifting his eyes over to Syko who lies in his own bed, completely comatose, and attached to a resparator, Nelson frowns. Syko doesn’t seem too messed up on the exterior. Nothing horrible. It’s his brain, and the possible amount of damage its sustained that pisses off Tyler. Tyler: “Did you know he had a ladder match at the PPV?! I thought he hired you to help him… couldn’t you have helped the idiot maintain his health long enough to OFFICIALLY win the damn TV title?” LAB is cornered, and cant really come up with an answer. Not a decent one at least. Tyler cringes for a moment, thinking… Tyler: “Why the hell did you wanna go back in time anyway?” This LAB can answer. LAB: “It’z a long story…” “I HAVE RETURNED!!!” Suddenly, as they both look to Syko’s bed, they see the Goblin himself STANDING atop it, triumphant in his return… Ripping the resparator mask from his face, and the I.V. from his vein, Syko throws his arms up in victory. The Goblin has indeed returned… in a hospital gown. STG: “REJOICE, MINIONS, THE ALMIGHT SAVIOR OF THE ALMIGHTY RACE HAS RETURNED! AND JUST IN TIME IT SEEMS, TO MAKE IT TO THIRD DAWNING, TO SMITE THAT FOOL VENNEMAN PENDRAGON! That is… if he actually EXISTS in this world! GOD SPEED TO THE KING!!” And with that, Syko hurls himself with a magnificent dive… Right through the rooms window, smashing it, and falling dooooown… Both Nelson and LAB stare at the smashed window with mouths agape. Tyler: “…What story are we on again?” LAB: “You do not vant to know…” Fin.
