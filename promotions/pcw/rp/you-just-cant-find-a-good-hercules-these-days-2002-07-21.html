You just can’t find a good Hercules these days!

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st of July, 2002 Our scene opens up with an outlook of the beautiful city of Athens in Greece. The beating sun casts down its rays upon the valley below, outlining the mountains in the distance and the gorgeous city just below the ledge of which we stand upon. Around us, roses and other vivid flowers have been positioned to grow upon the stone pillars around a creamy stone balcony. We pan out a little and we are met with the face of a tall, handsome man, bronzed by the sun and as built as a pro bodybuilder. His long golden locks hand droopily over his broad shoulders as he stands, a golden harp in his hands looking out over the horizon. Hercules: (Sadly) “Oh beautiful, Athens, Hades, dark lord of the underworld, shan’t take you between his wretched claws, for I promise to you that, as your protector, and local hero, I will rise up against that evil God and take his Godly head as my own! For am I not a demigod?” Voice: “Well, no, and no. For you see, you’re in fact an actor filming a corny mythological Greek film, and even if this was real, I doubt Hercules would be able to really kill an immortal…” Hercules turns around to see that a tall, well-built man - of a dark skin complexion, his hair worn in a long flowing ponytail and wearing a silk toga similar to the his - stands behind him; arms folded and a stern look about his handsome visage. Hercules: “Harry, who the hell is this?” A man wearing a chequered shirt and a red cap, worn back to front runs on to the movie’s set. Harry (The director): “John, I’m so sorry, I don’t know who the--…” The Pharaoh: (Proudly) “My friend, you should indeed know who I am! Why, In the name of the Gods, I am ‘The Pharaoh’ Titu Xansiv of the royal federation, PCW!” Harry looks at John and frowns. Harry: “The Pharaoh, huh?” The director strokes his chin thoughtfully. Harry: “Maybe… just MAYBE we could use you in the film? You’re a wrestler, right?” Pharaoh nods. Harry: “Ya’ see, I hear that a lot of pro wrestlers have been going into show biz, lately. You wouldn’t be interested at all, would…you?” Pharaoh frowns and shakes his head to and fro. The Pharaoh: “Good man, I’ve come here today to research the Hercules character, for this coming Tuesday I’m booked in a match against Big Alan Steele, the PCW World Heavyweight Champion!” John (Hercules): (Puzzled) “Sorry if I sound dumb here, I don’t watch a lot of wrestling ya’ see. But, err… what has that got to do with my film debut?!” The Pharaoh: (Cheerfully) “Ah! I was hoping you’d ask that! You see Big Alan Steele believes he’s Hercules: The son of Zeus. So basically I figured that I’d come to the set of this new ‘Hercules’ movie and see what the guy’s really like.” Jon: “And why did you interrupt ME in mid scene?!” The Pharaoh: (Sighing) “Well you made a huge mistake back there, my friend. Hercules is but a demigod, he probably couldn’t defeat Hades.” John winces. John: “Harry, can’t we get this guy off set?” Harry figuratively waves away John’s words Harry: “John, this guy could be the big>/I> thing we need to make the movie’s a sure fire hit! If you’re the professional that you say you are then surly you can see that it could be in the best interests of the movie!” Visibly angered by his director’s words, John frowns. John: “He isn’t even a proper actor! He’s a damned wrestler, for the love of God!” The Pharaoh: (Patting John on the shoulder) “Herc, to be honest I don’t even want to be a part of the film. I just want to go over there and watch in a vain attempt to learn the ways of the great and historical Hercules…” Harry slowly turns and looks back at the production crew. Cameras, and much more equipment used in the creation of a film line the grassy knoll behind the temple in which the film is been filmed. Harry: “Boys, Mr Xansiv’s gonna’ be watching for a while so just make sure he’s alright and treated rightly, OK?” The other members of the crew nod in agreement as The Pharaoh is personally escorted by Harry towards the group of men manning cameras, lighting equipment and other essential elements in the coming-together of the film. The Pharaoh: (Been shown his seat) “Thanks.” Harry nods at the Egyptian warrior and then rushes back over towards John, otherwise known as Hercules. Harry: “OK, straight from the top! In, ONE, TWO, THREE and…ACTION!” Harry moves out of the cameras’ views leaving John to act out his part. Hercules: “No, Athens! Do not worry, for Hades shan’t take you! I will always be there! I will be there when you require my heroic services, I’ll be there when you, my greatly appreciative fellow citizens, need me--…” Unexpectedly, The Pharaoh cuts off John in mid sentence. Titu rises from his seat and walks towards the temple-like set. The Pharaoh: “No, No, NO! This is ALL wrong! John, I don’t want to hear you babbling on for half the movie! I want action, dammit!” Titu rises from his seat and walks towards the temple-like set. The Pharaoh: “I want battles with great and ferocious monsters! I want to see the tests that Hercules had to take all those thousands of years ago! And most importantly I want to see how Hercules fights in unarmed combat! Come now--…” John cuts Titu off and turns to Harry. John: “Harry, I can’t work with this!” Harry looks at Pharaoh, who stands with his arms folded, and a stern look about his face. Harry: “Mr Xansiv does have a point…and you’re acting isn’t all there, I can’t feel Herc’s feelings, man! Put your emotions--… ” John rubs his eyes and then theatrically tosses the gleaming harp to the floor. It clatters with a deep, metallic ring. John: “Ya’ know what? I’ve had enough! This guy’s only been on the damned set for a few minutes and already he thinks he’s running the place! Harry he’s pissing me off! So either you kick this idiot off of the set or I’m leaving!” Harry and the rest of the production crew chuckle under their breath in the way someone thinking: “Hey, you just made a complete and utter ass of yourself!” would… Harry: “Don’t be so stupid, John. Now common, we’ll go and shoot the next scene, this one can wait ‘til a little later on.” John storms off in the direction of a huge red and metallic-black jeep parked on the top of a small hill. The Pharaoh: “What is his problem?!” Harry shrugs. Harry: “He’s always been like that. He makes those sort of threats all the time, but he’s about as reliable to keep to them as a chocolate teapot is to boiling a cup of tea in hell.” The Pharaoh: “I see… Well I was only giving him some pointers! I’d have thought the guy’d have been…well, well pleased, to be honest.” Harry: “He doesn’t really like others taking his supposed limelight.” Xavier Kannon was like that. He hated the limelight been taken away from him, Pharaoh knew that for sure. After all, he’d taken the limelight away from Kannon in the past - back in NTICW - and he couldn’t hack it then as much as he could now. The Gold Patron Meritorious had taken his hate for The Pharaoh to new and unspeakable levels, and for that he’d pay! At the PPV, Wrestle Vessel, Pharaoh would be fighting for Zen Dula…his former bodyguard who Kannon electrocuted, and put in hospital this past Friday on Fusion…the bastard! The Pharaoh: (Solemnly)“I know people like that.” Harry: “Sometimes you just have to bear with them, I guess.” Pharaoh scowls, his thoughts now entirely focussed upon Kannon. No longer was he thinking about his up-and-coming encounter with the PCW World Champion Big Alan Steele and Frisco, nor was he thinking about trying to not think about Xavier. He couldn’t help it. After everything Kannon had put him through…He hated the bastard! Pharaoh shuddered. The Pharaoh: (Tightening his fists) “And sometimes one needs to teach people like that…a lesson.” Harry looked at him in confusion. Harry: “What…do you…mean?” Pharaoh knew what Harry was thinking: “Oh no, he’s going to go and pick a fight with my leading star!’. Well it wasn’t like that at all. This guy, the one playing the part of Hercules, meant about as much to Pharaoh as Zen Dula’s life had done to Kannon. The Pharaoh: “Oh don’t worry, no harm is to come to big Herc over there.” Harry wiped a few beads of sweat away from his forehead. Harry: “Thank God…” The Pharaoh: “Harry I don’t think that I’m learning anything from this visit, so I’m gonna go. Plus, I think I’m beginning to piss John off…” A group of makeup artists are attempting to calm down the huge man, in an unusual way: they’re applying makeup and other artificial colourings and creams to his face. Yet from the looks of things this wasn’t actually calming John down…if anything it was aggravating him more. John: “Get away from me, you fools! I’m as beautiful as I’ll ever be!” Full of himself too, thought Pharaoh. Just like Xavier… Harry: “Yeah, perhaps it would be best if you left?” The Pharaoh: (Watching John with curious eyes) “I absolutely agree.” Pharaoh shakes Harry’s hand before turning away – in disappointment, since he never really learned anything significant about Hercules’ way of fighting - from the set, the arrogant ’Hercules’ and the annoying makeup artists, once again headed towards another PCW event… The Pharaoh: (Glumly) “You just can’t find a good Hercules these days… And with that our scene fades to black…
