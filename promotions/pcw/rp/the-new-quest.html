Date: not entered A New Quest... [PCW Tuesday Turmoil, the 10th of April…A date when something intriguing will happen, a time when a forgotten battle will be relived. For right there, on that fateful night in the Cox Arena in San Diego, just like old times, Xavier Kannon meets The Pharaoh…again. It has been a long while since the old NTICW shut down…many things have changed since then, many things. But among the things that changed at least one thing has lived one, hate. Pharaoh’s eternal hatred for that evil scientologist, Xavier Kannon… Pharaoh hasn’t forgotten what happened the last time these two locked horns. Isiy, Pharaoh’s wife turned on her husband’s back and almost went to the dark side of religion and joined up with Xavier, but Pharaoh managed to keep her…only just. The Cox Arena will once again see the rivalry of these two great athletes as they go at it once again, for old times sake. The Pharaoh versus Xavier Kannon, could this possibly be the last time to get a chance to see these two fight one another? Perhaps, but only time truly knows that…we will see, as time means to, and we will see how these two drive themselves into a frenzy of hatred. Xavier versus Titu…the eternal battle] ..::The scene opens up in the streets of Cairo, Egypt. The bustling cobblestone road of which we find ourselves to be looking upon literally crawls with loathsome men and women of Egyptian Heritage, all dressed in tattered clothing and all appearing to be in need of a good, wholesome meal. Many of these inferiors carry pots, pans and other items of small value in their grubby mitts; cherishing them with everything they have, even going to the lengths of looking about suspiciously at the person standing or walking next to them with great contempt. Suddenly the sound of a royal horn blasts out through the air, sending a metallic rhythm through the warm atmosphere. At that moment all movement in the street halts. The peasants look up, some with expressions of dismay on their mugs, and other seemingly not bothered by the royal noise, to witness a massive, golden chariot come ploughing though the people. Two fierce white horses, dressed in gold ribbons and bangles pull this massive contraption and to our surprise our resident Egyptian God, and PCW TV champion, The Pharaoh rides upon the chariot’s majestic back::.. ..::Pharaoh beams with pride and joy as if reliving the days when he was king of all Egypt as he looks down at the filth below him. He ignores the peasants and continues to smile joylessly as the horses suddenly rear up in the centre of the cobblestone road. The Pharaoh, or Titu Xansiv as he is also know as, coughs a little, as if clearing his throat ready to give a vast speech to the people of Egypt about him, and then without further interruptions our hero speaks::.. The Pharaoh: People of Cairo! I have come here today to tell you something that will seemingly resulting in being of great use to you in the future! It has come to my attention that the city of Cairo, and the country of Egypt for that matter have both fallen into great poverty and ruin. I look about the streets and see peasants roaming like cats looking for their next meal, I see the roads and streets cracked and destroyed by the un-careful acts of others, I see MY former country fallen from grace into a great depression; into a place where only Rats seem to be able to live peacefully, and a place where people like YOU don’t deserve to dwell! My people, I have come to you today to inform you of a significant change that will be coming over this land soon! I, Titu Xansiv, PCW’s TV champion, and former king of this country, will be running for the ruler of all Egypt shortly, and I, my friends, will hope to win your hearts again and give you all the brilliant things that were once on offer here in this formerly noble place! ..::Pharaoh pauses for a second to listen to the reactions of the Egyptians about his chariot’s wheels. The people don’t seem to know what is going on, one man even says::.. Random member of crowd: Who are you? ..::Pharaoh seems taken back by this comment and almost explodes with anger over the man’s obvious ignorance, but he manages to keep himself calm and give the Egyptian Male his answer::.. The Pharaoh: You don’t know who I am? Why I am Titu Xansiv! Former Pharaoh of Egypt! Surly you’ve heard of me! I ruled this very land for almost ten years of my mortal life and now I come back to aid you people in recovering this once glorious place from its terrible poverty and ruin and you ask me ‘Whoa re you?’! ..::The Pharaoh sighs. The crowd murmur and talk amongst themselves, many doubtful voices echo off the walls of the surrounding ruined shops and shackled houses::... The Pharaoh: You people have no idea who I am? ..::All at once the crowd shout out directing their one word answer to his question at Xansiv; who now stands with a displeased look about his handsome face::.. Crowd: No! ..::Pharaoh sighs::.. The Pharaoh: Then perhaps I should refresh your memories! ..::Pharaoh turns to the back of the chariot and we find his mentor Horus and a small group of technicians huddling around something seemingly of dire importance::.. The Pharaoh: (To himself) And they say money can’t buy anything! (To Horus and the men) Ok people! Send up the big screen! ..::Pharaoh smiles as suddenly a harsh buzzing sound fills the air. The people below the long, royal-looking chariot all cower as the noise grows louder and then suddenly stops. Swiftly a large Screen, huge in height and lengthy in width is raised into the humid air by a mechanical arm of some kind into the warm air. The screen buzzes with static and then suddenly the picture fades in to a black and white portrait of The Pharaoh. Grinning proudly Pharaoh is shown wearing his ancient Egyptian crown in all his might, he wears a regal kingly getup of golden jewellery and rich golden bangles, all of which gleam in the grey light of the picture::.. ..::Suddenly the picture begins to gain colour and the crowd watching on from the ground below cry out in wonder; all seem impressed by this amazing ‘thing’ of great amazement. Arrogantly Titu smiles and then turns to the screen to watch what is going on. The screen shows the rolling backdrop of Egypt, a palace of great architecture suddenly fills our view and then as we close in on it Titu is once again shown sitting comfortably on a royal throne of great size and skilled carving::.. ..::Pharaoh smiles as usual and then a beautiful woman walks into the picture…Isiy…the Egyptian tempest walks towards Xansiv and then sits down in his lap. Titu smiles and then begins to speak to her, but his words cannot be heard::.. The Pharaoh: Will you people turn this thing up! Technician: Sorry, sir! ..::The technician runs to the back on the monitors mechanical arm and presses a button three times. Suddenly the booming sound of Titu’s voice, the Xansiv from the video playing that is, can be clearly heard::.. The Pharaoh (from the video): Hello people of Egypt! If you’re watching this then it’s most likely because I, being the man standing in front of you, yes that handsome bugger there! ..::The Pharaoh from the video points down to his real life counterpart below the monitor::.. The Pharaoh (from the video): (continuing) have ended up not being able to convince you of my true identity. Now I know what it’s like, you guys down there must be wondering who the hell I am and without proof of my former job, that being the ruler of Egypt, I’m sure it must be a lot to take in when I say I ruled over Egypt for Ten year…now maybe I can show you a few examples of the thing is did when I ruled Egypt. Come! Follow me! ..::Isiy gets up out of her husbands lap as Titu gets up out of the throne and walks across the palaces floor towards a large window at the side of the throne rooms left hand wall::.. The Pharaoh (from the video): It’s amazing what money can do! Who’d have ever thought that I could persuade Universal Studio’s to help me put this little vignette together for your amusement?? Anyway that’s above the point…look out there! ..::Pharaoh points out of the large window to reveal a land of men and woman dressed in clothes similar to his, all of whom are collecting crops from the banks of the River Nile. Many slaves are there also, building monuments and statues in the image of Titu, the crowd watching grow intrigued and the din of voices all talking in wonder fills the air. The real Pharaoh grins arrogantly::.. The Pharaoh (from the video): When I ruled over Egypt, almost SIX THOUSAND years ago…maybe more, I forget the correct time, but when I ruled, I really ruled! Just like I do now in Primetime Championship Wrestling! Take for example what I have accomplished in the company…former tag team champion, TV champion, life is good, and life is going to get better! For me and for YOU! ..::The crowd cheer::.. The Pharaoh (from the video): Now as my counterpart will have already stated, I am looking to once again reclaim Egypt…to do so I have to grab the hearts of its people again like I did so many years ago… ..::A stern look overcomes the Pharaoh’s face::.. The Pharaoh (from the video): You, my people, my fans, are my new reason to live…soon after I have retaken Egypt many things will happen that you will all benefit from and that I promise! Now please if you wouldn’t mind, I must leave …mainly because Universal Studios want the set for the scorpion king back, but, ah damn I shouldn’t have said that, it makes me sound so cheap! ..::The Pharaoh walks off set as Dwayne Johnson runs onto stage dressed in all-Egyptian garb::.. Random member of crowd: You suck! ..::The people in the crowd suddenly begin to pick up large rocks and hurl them towards the screen::.. The Pharaoh: Someone turn this thing off! The crowd are going mad! (To himself) I didn’t know Dwayne was so hated…wait yes I did! (To the crowd) People, MY people, please, please calm yourselves! ..::A deafening chant rises up from the onlookers::.. Crowd: Die Rocky, Die, Die Rocky, Die… ..::The Pharaoh suddenly beams like a Cheshire cat. He turns to Horus and begins to speak::.. The Pharaoh: I have a brilliant idea! Horus: And what might that be? The Pharaoh: You’ll see! ..::Pharaoh turns back to the crowd and smiles broodingly::.. The Pharaoh: If you want this man to die then I’m afraid I cannot grant you that wish… Crowd: Boo! The Pharaoh: But, I can give you the next best thing! ..::The crowd cheer wildly as Pharaoh stands thinking through his plan::.. The Pharaoh: Now follow me! ..::Pharaoh grabs hold of the chariots reins and tugs them across to the left, the massive white horses turn to face the opposite direction of the street before Pharaoh orders then to set off back down the cobblestone road. The technicians pack away the portable cinema monitor as Pharaoh laughs evilly with the crowd of angry Egyptian peasants following closely behind. Before long Horus comes up to Pharaoh at the front of the chariot and begins to speak with his pupil::.. Horus: What are you doing? And what did you mean ‘I can give you the next best thing?’ ..::Pharaoh grins::.. The Pharaoh: If you want the people, Horus, you have to give the people what they want! And it’s pretty obvious that they hate this Dwayne Johnson fellow! Horus: So what are you going to do? ..::Pharaoh chuckles evilly::.. The Pharaoh: Give them what they want! Now how about you go and ask the crowd some questions you have…I grow bored of your constant questioning…here’s a question you could ask, a good suggestion, ask them why they hate him so much… ..::Horus sighs and slumps to the back of the chariot where the crowd of people are walking steadily behind::.. Horus: Hello, people, you may not know me by sight, but I’m sure you do by name… ..::The people in the crowd look around in puzzlement::.. Horus: I am Horus! The Egyptian God of the Sky! Random member of crowd: You’re crazy old man! (Turning to the rest of the crowd) That guy’s a nutcase! ..::Everyone in the crowd begin to laugh and point at the eccentric old God::.. Horus: I am too the God of the Sky! ..::The crowd continue to laugh and mock the old God::.. Horus: Look I didn’t come over here to be mocked by you ignorant peasants! Now please tell me, why do you hate the one they call ‘The Rock’ so much? Random member of crowd: ‘Cos he made the ancient tongue of our ancestors look like an idiots language! Random member of crowd#2: Yeah! He made Egypt seem inferior! Horus: And that’s why you hate him? Crowd: Yes! ..::Hours looks around in disbelief but then he simply shrugs his shoulders and looks on at the crowd following intently behind the chariot::.. Horus: Good enough reason for me… ..::The scene gradually fades to black and then it reopens up on a shot of the hot Egyptian sun. The sound of clattering weapons can be heard, steel upon steel rings out dimly in the air as suddenly the sound of a mob can be heard droning the sound of hand to hand combat. Suddenly the cameras slowly run down from the sun to reveal the set of a movie being shot. Slowly coming over the horizon we can see The Pharaoh leading his mob of angry Egyptians towards the set. As we look carefully at the people being filmed we find one of them to be in fact Dwayne Johnson. In the WWF stars hand is a large sword and attached to his left arm is a huge shield. All filming comes to a grinding halt as Pharaoh’s mob finally reaches the outskirts of the set. Suddenly a man, wearing a pair of shades, a baseball cap, a pair of white shorts and a sky blue T-shirt comes running up to the mob::.. Man: What on earth do you think you’re doing? ..::Pharaoh is about to speak when the man cuts him off::.. Man: You guys were supposed to be on set a half an hour ago! You’re late! ..::Pharaoh is more than taken back by this comment and frowns angrily down at the man in front of him::.. The Pharaoh: How dare you! Why, I should smite your ignorant body with the wrath of a thousand Gods! I have never been so insulted in my life! Man: What are you talking about, Jimmy? You and these guys need to get into makeup so you’re ready for the next scene! ..::The man suddenly looks at the mob and Pharaoh and smiles::.. Man: You do already look the part though; maybe makeup won’t be needed… ..::He strokes his chin in deep thought::.. The Pharaoh: Look! I don’t know who this ‘Jimmy’ fellow is but we aren’t who you think we are! I am Titu Xansiv, former ruler of ancient Egypt and soon to be ruler of modern Egypt! I hope you know that you have insulted a man so great that he would put you to shame just by showing you his home in Cairo! Man: What are you talking about? The Pharaoh: Look, my good man, why don’t you tell me who the hell you are, and then I’ll tell you what we are doing here! Man: I’m Chuck Russell, the director of this here movie, ‘The Scorpion King’! Random member of crowd: Where’s Dwayne Johnson?! ..::Chuck points towards The Rock who stands looking on from a good vantage point away towards a small mountain of sand in the desert. The crowd suddenly lets out a great boo and words of hatred are shouted at Dwayne::.. Chuck Russell: What the hell is their problem?! And what are you doing here?! The Pharaoh: Well it would seem that my friends have a little problem with ol’ Dwayne over yonder; so I figured that it’d be best if they sorted it out like men… Chuck Russell: Peacefully I’ll hope… The Pharaoh: Of course… Random member of crowd: Kill him! Kill Dwayne! Damn him! ..::Chuck sarcastically looks up at Titu::.. Chuck Russell: Look I can’t let this many people come on set; especially when they want to hurt my leading star! ..::Hearing this the crowd boo Chuck::.. Chuck Russell: Well I can’t! What would Vince say?! The Pharaoh: Vince? Chuck Russell: Vince McMahon! The executive producer! ..::Pharaoh laughs coldly:.. The Pharaoh: What in the name of Duat?! You have Vince McMahon as the executive producer? Good God man! Chuck Russell: Stop it! Stop laughing at me! ..::Seeing that Pharaoh is laughing at Chuck the angry mob of Egyptian’s join in on the chuckle. Chuck suddenly snaps and yells out aloud at Titu and his crowd causing the rest of the film crew to turn and look over at the action taking place on the outskirts of the set::.. Chuck Russell: Get out of here!! You ignorant fool, and the rest of you too!! Get out of here! The Pharaoh: Not until we’ve seen Dwayne! ..::As it happens The Rock hears these words and slowly begins to walk towards the group::.. Random member of crowd: Here he comes! ..::Pharaoh turns to the mob::.. The Pharaoh: Calm yourselves! Calm yourselves, friends!! Crowd: Grrr!!! ..::Dwayne grins revealing his pearly white teeth as he walks towards the ground led by Pharaoh in his golden chariot. In his hand we find a small book of white paper and in his other hand we can see a pen. After a few moments Johnson is in front of the angry crowd who all frown and scowl at the WWF superstar::.. Dwayne Johnson: Hi there, I’m Dwayne Johnson, WWF superstar The Rock! I’m guessing you guy’s are here for autographs? The Pharaoh: Actually- Dwayne Johnson: Ok, ok, so you people don’t want it on this paper…well then, what do you want it on? I hope you’ve brought something for me to sign, or else you’ve trekked across the desert for nothing! ..::The Rock chuckles:.. The Pharaoh: We aren’t here for autographs! Dwayne: Ah! So its pictures you want? Well my publicist isn’t around, but he says I have to charge money for them now, so, sorry guys, I’m gonna’ have to ask you to pay for them. ..::The Rock shrugs slowly and puts on an expression of sympathy. Pharaoh scowls::.. The Pharaoh: I needn’t have my picture taken with you, nor do I want an autograph! To be perfectly honest I don’t want anything from you! It’s these people here that want you for ‘something’! Dwayne: Oh really? And what might that be? Random member of crowd: Get him! ..::The mob jump on top of The Rock as Chuck runs around in horror, screaming and crying::.. Chuck Russell: NOOOOO! ..::Pharaoh smiles as the scene begins to fade on his handsome mug. The sound of punching and fighting can be heard dimly in the background as he turns to Horus who sits smiling at the back of the Chariot::.. The Pharaoh: Our work is done here, let us leave… ..::The scene fades to black::.. The Pharaoh: Its times like these that I feel so good about myself…ya’ know, like ya’ feel that you’ve done something good! Horus: I’m sure Dwayne would have something to say about that, Titu… The End…
