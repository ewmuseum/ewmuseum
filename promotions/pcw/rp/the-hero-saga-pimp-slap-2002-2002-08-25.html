The Hero Saga: Pimp Slap 2002

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 25, 2002 Somewhere down between the allyways and the street corners, a scream is heard. It pierces the quiet night air and raises high up into the dark night sky, illuiminated only by the pale stars in the sky. We see a young girl with a man in a pink hat and matching coat standing over her, she has a bruise on her cheek and is crying, obviously shes a prostitute that didn't pay up enough tonight. 'T-Bone no!' she screamed, her voice crying to the heavens, 'I'm sorry! There were too many cops aro-...' Another hard slap puts her back down and silent. 'Listen here biznatch, we dont do thiz sheet back in da hood.' T-Bone adjusted his silly hat and made sure the feather stuck out as menacingly as possible. 'I dont let biznatches like you get away wifout givin me my propa respect, yo.' T-Bone motioned to his group of henchman, 'Rocko, Smelly, Rocko 2, Percy, Token Large Muscular Black Man Who Knows Kung Fu And Will Most Likely Be The Person A Superhero Would Need To Fight Before They Fought Me... take her out!' The gang advances on the beaten hooker as she crawls back towards the end of the alley, before she can go any further however, TLMBMWKKFAWMLBTPASWNTFBTFM picks her up and pushes her against the wall, undoing his belt... 'Wait, whats that noise?' Music can be heard for no explicable reason, it slowly reaches a crecendo... 'And they say that a.......... HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEROOOOOOOOOO WILL SAVE USSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!!!' Suddenly, the large picture window that was stuck in the side of the building next to the alley shatters, glass flies everywhere as the street toughs scatter away from the hooker, someone lands between them but is hidden by his large flowing black cape, slowly he looks up, a random lightning bolt lights the area as the blue hair and black mask are visable... 'OH NO! ITS DARKWING STAR!!!!!' T-Bone screams, much like a girl, and backs away, Darkwing Star smiles and stands up, his entire overly muscular frame, he speaks in a deep voice, almost in a constant growl, 'So... which one of you touched the lady.' The four normal henchmen who are supposed to get their asses kicked first attack! One by one... Rocko goes flying incredibly far, sailing over the delaware and into central New Jersey somewhere. Rocko 2 yells at the loss of his brother and rushes Darkwing Star. Darkwing laughs and gets behind him, kicking his feet out from under him, he falls face first into the brick wall and... His head randomly explodes!! Gore all over!! The ratings move upwards! 'Pff... I guess he got a little bit... ahead of himself.' Everyone stops to laugh, but Percy, the thug with the comical name, suddenly attacks with the last remaining non large and muscular kung fu knowing person. They both brandish lead pipes and swing them, Darkwing Star is disoriented and ducks them both barely, they continue to try and beat on him ontil he rolls under their legs and pulls them down, he easily lifts one of them up and throws him into a conveniently placed dumpster, the other stands up and goes to fight... but thinks better of it and throws himself into the dumpster... Darkwing laughs and points, making a odd face and cringing... suddenly..... EXPLOSIONS GALORE!!! The dumpster explodes by the mere force of Darkwings mental mind bullet attack! He takes the opportunity to pose, the wind blows his cape and the drama is at a all time high, 'Look! Behind you!' Darkwing glances at the hooker pointing behind him, he turns around just in time to be kicked in the face by the stereotypical large black kungfu ninja guy. 'You forgot about me Darkwing Star... didnt you?' Another hard kick and Darkwing Star goes sliding across the ground. 'No, I noticed you, you were just standing in the corner looking menacing and bad ass, I just figured you were watching.' Darkwing stands up and spinkicks him with the force of a thousand X-Pacs! 'ARGH THE PAIN!' The large man is almost doubled over by the force of the kick. Then suddenly.. The amazing kungfu battle that went on for hours ended... Darkwing Star grinned as he disposed of the bloody remains of the kungfu ninja of deaths body. He looked around for more challengers and saw only T-Bone, the pimp, remaining. 'Now... Now... Mr. Superhero Guy... I.. I'm just a pimp... I'll... I'll give you her if you don't hurt me!' Darkwing Star laughed loudly and put his hands on his hips, striking a pose for anyone watching, he then glanced at the hooker, who was surprisingly attractive, and then back at the pimp. 'Ok... but this isn't over evildoer!' T-Bone sprinted away, his platform boots with the fish in them sloshing loudly. Darkwing turned around to the hooker, 'Thank you so much DarkwingStar! How can I ever repay you!' 'Oh.' Said the hooker, half surprised and half delighted, 'That I can do.' The music begins to play again as the Hooker goes to work on Darkwing Star, the camera pulls far out and into the sky as the music plays us out... 'And they say that a.......... HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEROOOOOOOOOO WILL SAVE USSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!!!'
