The Orchestrated Mocking of ye Old Hoss of the Cliché…

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th August 2002 The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#2 of 3 for Turmoil (6th August 2002)… {Quick OOC Note – this was written yesterday…and finished today, my 6pm-8am Internet connection screwed me over once again. I’m sorry if this entourage of no more than three roleplays seems like sandbagging, but I have got multiple promos on the go here…} Chapter Four: The Orchestrated Mocking of ye Old Hoss of the Cliché… “Alright, let’s get it together, you pitiful excuses for people! We’ve only got another nine hours until the show, and we’ve got to be ready…oh, we’ve got to be damned ready, you lousy bunch of amateurs!. Now then from the TOP, maggots! A-One-And-A-Two-And-A-Three-And-A-Four!! Let’s teach these pussy-whipped Professional Wrestlers on how to be fucking grandiose…do I make myself clear?!?” “Ah…Mister Chris Chase, why if it isn’t a delectable surprise to see that you’re enjoying the likes of some of our more, should I say, choice entertainment. Indeed, the Lubbock Orchestra is certainly a sight to behold, perhaps even worthy of selling a couple to a multitude of our fresh line of souvenirs. You know, Mister Chase, we at the City council know all about your little crusade for humanity – after all, you have been blabbering on about it for a little while now…” “~(Sarcastically)~ What do you have in mind for it? Perhaps you want me to rid your streets of the countless hobos that litter them, or perhaps you want me to play acoustic folk rock to the children without play sets in this city. Or perhaps you’ll want to hire me to replace those infernal benches with something that people don’t find themselves stuck to easily. I don’t suppose that you haven’t quite noticed the meticulous detail that I’m going into to thoroughly shoot down any witticism from a certain hard-headed Malice Man…” “Yes, Yes, I understand, and I wholeheartedly apologize for that incident yesterday. You know, Mister Chase…buying a Souvenir will go towards helping the penniless citizens of this glorious city…” “Do I look like a Mentally-Challenged Tourist to you? Damn representative cretins…” ::.~(Musings of Chris Chase)~ You know, I’m a horrible golf player. I single-handedly crucified my handicap following a slice just off a sand bunker. But enough about golf and my particular ineptitude with the game…I feel like I’ve just been insulted. I’ve never been one for clichés. I’ve hated them, ignored their existence and thoroughly berated anyone as derivative and mind-numbingly useless as Mike Frampton. It’s my major pet hate, it even riles me worse than the superficial joys of Scientology or the habitual twitching and irritating side-comments of moronic sidekicks. Duke Williams is king of the cliché, lord overlord of the stereotype…let’s face it, he’s perhaps too ignorant to realize his mistakes in life, and perhaps too ignorant to avoid making statements that seem all too plagiaristic when compared to the Honky Tonk era in the United States during his youth. It’s an all too similar dilemma to the one from way back in the Chris Chase versus Boda days… And I don’t want to go and bring all that up again… There’s an orchestra playing triumphantly as the scene unfolds in an Amphitheater about 500 yards from the center of the city of Lubbock, Texas. It’s an early Tuesday morning, perhaps bordering on about 8 o’clock. The remnants of the earlier morning mist can be seen out of the small bay window of the darkened Amphitheater, along with the rest of the slightly less than bustling town. I never did take rough nights well, as can be seen from my expression whilst I slump in one of the seats on a precariously perched balcony, high above the performing orchestra below me. Standing behind me is some representative from the City Council, an uptight man with a penchant for higher culture and the cuisine that goes with it…as can be seen with his particularly rotund exterior. He gesticulates wildly as he tries to talk me into the merits of buying souvenirs from the City in general, like he thinks I’m some kind of wealthy tourist. I’m only here to fight the blatant stereotype-ism of Duke Williams, and to justify my credibility as a once and future PCW World Champion – like there was any doubt….:: “I assure you Mister Chase…we here at the City Council do not think of you Professional wrestlers as little more than tourists. If you’d take the time to observe our wonderful training facilities, maybe that’d open your mind a little…” “If you’ve managed to utilize them…then I’ll keep my mind firmly closed. You know, I really do hate your sort…the kind that’s too busy trying to line your own pockets…the cliché of the politician…” “Well…here in Lubbock, we just throw money at Professional Wrestlers. Yep…we certainly do. It’s a gloriously brilliant habit of ours, we can’t help but submit to the awesome tenacity of the Modern-day Professional Wrestling machine, and give our unfailing love towards that species of man that wipes the floor with any other. I tell you what, if you stay right here, I’ll grab a sack of money, and we can drench you in the filthy green stuff…” “~(Acerbically)~That’s Great! Let me go get my “I’m with the Gullible idiot” T-Shirt, and you can get to work with whatever deeds you have planned for the likes of a prominent Professional wrestler that I am…I’m sure that we can have all manner of fun deviating from whatever kind of work that I’ve got planned for today.” ::. In this day and age, you’d think that they’d have banished these types to the Void…Jeez! The Council representative trundles away from me as I sit there, cautiously watching over the symphony act with a keen eye, tweaking the collar of my round-necked T-Shirt with a half-assed twitch. My enthusiasm for this Duke Williams match keeps dwindling by the day, not my enthusiasm to claim a victory, but the enthusiasm for any kind of long-lasting connection with the Malice Man. He’s the master at unfairly judging the character of a supposed Young Punk, there’s no doubt about that. It’s rather dubious, and quite inane to think that he’d ever change that opinion, even if I did pull off my first victory since returning. Duke’s as hardheaded as they come. Stubborn and terrified of anything that could threaten his comfortably idle position in the PCW federation. He cares not for a Saint, a justifiably brilliant individual, and a man with a need to change. Yep…Velocity kills…and a lack of it is reason enough to call about the end of the known World… To sit around idly is to bring about a domino affect that could bring serious repercussions for the youngsters of today’s sport. Not to mention a heavy reduction in the numbers of buxom women – they always did like a dynamic man. Dynamic tendencies indeed. The grotesque notions of slowing down never occurred to me, they never brought about as much as a raised eyebrow. And yet, there’s this unbelievable urge to sit back and do fuck all about it. I mean, why should I fucking bother if the so-called President of this federation sets examples like that? It’s downright fucking ludicrous – verging on the inane. If I was drunk, I wouldn’t think any more of it and pass it off as some hilariously placed nuance in the weave of the Space-Time Continuum, but in this kind of cynical state, where the simplest moron could bear the weight of a scathing rant of the Chris Chase variety…anything’s a possibility… Great. I sigh. I mean, there’s nothing else to do at this point as opposed to watching the orchestra. Perhaps I could bring about an uprising and make something more interesting of this scenario – the crazy monkey people would have a whale of a time involved with something like that. It’s the difference from being eternally bored or eternally enthralled – the interaction between separate entities and the battles that ensue are what make the whole idea of the human race worthwhile. It brings together a plethora of things most wonderfully brilliant, including the likes of cocky Canadian Religious gimmicks who’d end up on the top of the pile at the very end of the day. I snapped out of Never-Never Land a long time ago, and it dawned on me that I was the only person in the Real World. There’s nothing more I’d love to do than to release the rest of my kind from the tortures of being in a world where everything’s black and white…there’s no shades of colour…not even shades of gray. Yes, Chris Chase would save the human race again, like the many times before…it’s the kind of thing that I make a habit out of doing… Rising from my seat, I wince as my knee catches the corner of the chair next to me, sending a soft stinging pain up my leg. It’s not exactly agony, but it’s the kind of thing that would end up pestering me like a spoiled brat when I’m Duke’s age. Clambering down from the set of raised seats that I had been sitting in, I begin to navigate my way down the balcony steps, down towards the stage alcove where the Orchestra continues to perform. The crashing of vitriolic violins and the morose humming of the brass section hits me hard in the chest. One thump to the heart, followed by another…and another. And then…a tap to the shoulder….:: “Chris Chase almighty! That’s where you were hiding for the last few days…you cunning little bastard, you nearly lost me in the woodwork…passing me off for some other moronic sidekick of some other brilliant and wholesomely Saintly Hero. How goes the Duke preparations by the way? Do you think that he’s holding out on the world by not telling us exactly which playmates he done the dirty with. Say, is that an orchestra?” ::.Almost dreading the sight of the person behind me, I turn around sharply after much self-deliberation and such. Once again, Dustin Downey releases a barrage of mis-judged and ill-timed statements to coincide with the death of the decent Chris Chase Roleplay. Yep…you heard me, the handler’s suffering from sleep deprivation, and thus deprived this Roleplay of all Sanity and integrity by bringing back Dustin Downey… Please excuse me whilst we take a commercial break…. {Insert Loud Scream here} Thank you for waiting….:: An Hour or so Later… ……….. Duke Williams needs an attitude adjustment… Biggest Match of My Life? Hardly, I’ve seen bigger matches on a card filled with midgets? You? A God? Please no…I can’t stand the hilarity of it all… How can I not see a match with Xavier Kannon when I’ve already beaten that cretin three times? Jesus CHRIST! ::. ~(Thoughts of Chris Chase)~ Sometimes there’s a line we’re simply not meant to cross, maybe it’s there to stop the likes of myself turning into a raving lunatic and blasting off rants that would give Duke a hard time in comprehending. Comprehension – do you quite understand, Duke? Obviously not. To understand, is to live a life replete with the knowledge that you’re actually going to succeed. I mean, without understanding…where would we be? Would we still be in the primordial ooze, keeping our heathen Neanderthal shapes in the cover of bushes and foliage, picking berries with our bare hands. Duke has a hard time grasping that. He needs to understand the very basic underlying factor in this match of ours…I’m, quite simply, an increasingly brilliant individual…a grandiose spectacle of the modern wrestling world. I live to see those very fans, whom you let down by letting the cash flow go out of control, prosper and continue to live by the word of a devout being. I mean, lets face it…I am adorable, aren’t I? Wouldn’t you say so Duke? But I’m not the one to be a jerk, no matter what that outburst may have proven about my particularly volatile personality. Yes, I may be a bit of a megalomaniac, I may be overly grandiose and theatrical…but that’s no reason to fret. I do it for the greater good, the joys of differentiation from the normal. Someone has to be different, someone has to prove an alternative solution… ~(Normal Narration)~ So says Chris Chase… In this convoluted world of ‘Saint’ Chris Chase, everything seems to be going wrong. The whole despondency following return has been of epic proportions, enough to write a paperback about and almost enough to send our hero into an ever tightening spiral of delusion…the kind of thing that your mother would have warned you about prior to University or some so-called trip of a lifetime… An hour ago, anxiety had finally called at the door of Chris Chase, leaving him as nothing more than a heaving pile of vomiting Canadian. The threat of a moronic interlude, combined with a continuing spiral of mental deficiency, Chris Chase falters and winds up having an anxiety attack on the day that he was due to face Duke Williams. The irony of it all is substantial, especially from a previously mentally stable individual. It’s almost enough to bring in a pack of dancing midgets in for an entertaining climaz for the whole thing…but you know that’ll never happen. Crouching beside a puddle of his own vomit, Chris Chase slumps into a depressing site, a blanket wrapped around his shaking shoulders. Muttering to himself, Dustin Downey and the Council Representative stand behind him, looking down on the removed hero with somewhat worried glances…and SWEET HEAVENS! The Band is STILL playing! Alright, maybe that’s not all too relevant now, but the morose overtones of their performance of ‘The Hall of the Mountain King’ is enough to keep this promotion on the very brink of becoming something all too macabre. With a defiant breath…Chris Chase looks up at the camera and half-heartedly smiles with his pupils dilated, before one last whispered phrase from the former Messiah….:: Chris: Who brought along the dancing midgets? ::. And he collapses once more as a chorus line of midgets parades across the screen….:: To be Continued!
