The Ski Lodge

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/1/2002 ~Can you feel it? Is it just me or has time been wound back about two years and Jon Kano himself is back to his old form? He is going like a bat out of hell, living life in the fast lane like never before. He is like a new man, but is still that man who set PCW on fire two years ago. He has a book that is selling consistently well each week. His PCW merchandise is selling again like it did back in the old days. Kano is churning out interviews and promos like there is no tommorrow. And the part that is truly scary to most or exciting to some is that Kano has recaptured what he had nearly two years ago when he won the PCW Continental Title. He has recaptured his killer instinct. And he is on the prowl for prey, and he doesn't hunt for sport, he hunts to KILL.~ *The scene opens up inside a lobby of a ski resort in the Swiss Alps. The fireplace is lit and the wood is crackling and glowing orange. Jon Kano, wearing a fine sweater and tan khakis pants is sitting on a leather couch near the fire, with a glass of fine French brandy warming in his palm. To his left, sitting next to him, is a lovely blond woman, who somewhat resembles Kano's pregnant wife, Kristin, whom is in Las Vegas at the Kano family villa. She is wearing a white parka, but still her lovely figure is noticable.* Woman: Well Jon, do you want to go up to my room for a nightcap of sorts? Kano: It is only noon. Woman: Figure of speech. Kano: Well no. *The woman pushes herself back from Kano and gives him a puzzled look.* Kano: I have a wife. Woman: So? What she doesn't know won't hurt her. Kano: But I'll know. She is going to give birth to our child in about three months and I love her now and I'll her then. Woman: I suppose that is admirable. I am sure you make her very happy, because I can tell she makes you very happy. If you change your mind, I am in room fifteen. *The lovely, rejected woman gets up from the couch and walks away from Kano.* Kano: Christ, this whole love thing is amazing. I just turned down a woman who most men would blow their load just talking to for my wife and my soon to be mother of my child. It just doesn't seem right, but I know in my heart it is right. But enough of this sentimental bullshit. *Kano takes a small swig of his fine brandy.* Kano: Ahhhhhh, damn Frenchies can't fight a war, but they sure can make a damn good liquor. Tonight I get to follow through on my promise. I promised these two new bloods in MY company an education and tonight an education Amulet and Rollins will receive. Right now, I know what they are doing. They have probably just woken up, wiped the crud out of their eyes, looked at themselves in the mirror, and then IT happened. They realized that tonight, they are going to step into the ring that I built, and regardless of the outcome, they will leave different men. They may walk out, hobble out, or be taken out on a strecher, it is all up to me. I am a ring general once I step over that top rope and that bell rings. It is all by the grace of me if these two survive tongiht to continue their certainly mediocre careers. So these two should just do one thing from now until tonight and in our match, just show me respect and it won't be too painful, but if they disrespect me, then tonight will be a hellish experience for them, I guarantee it. *Kano takes another small swig of his brandy.* Kano: Then on Turmoil, I got the man known as Ali Khadhafi. Who? Exactly. It seems to me like I am a giant and he ain't nothin but a midget in PCW, and this Tuesday I am gonna step on him and not even know it. If Khadhafi knows anything, he knows that I handle my business, its work before play, and between Fusion and Turmoil, I will be having a good time. This Friday and Tuesday, I'll be handling my business extensively and well. And I will be more than happy to show everyone once again what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano still sits in front of the fire and sips his brandy quietly.* ~Do you feel it now?~
