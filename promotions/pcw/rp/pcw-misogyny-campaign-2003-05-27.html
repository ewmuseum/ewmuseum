PCW Misogyny Campaign

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/27/03 'I'll have to discontinue these killings until' further notice.. Well, at least till' I settled some things at my job.' a familiar voice explains, after being accompanied by the boisterous noises coming from receiving telephone. Perched abed is the autodidact of misogyny, Stone Burns.. listening over with his ear, not making out or absorbing the information being released by the caller, the partisan of his misogynistic acts, Elliot Costa who's riled with anger. 'Listen you fucking homo.. Either you do what I say or I'll kill you.' Elliot churlishly pipes, as a popping laugh produced from Stone’s mouth as he shrugs and purses his lips wonderingly. 'That's OK...' Stone brusquely reports, placing the phone over its cradle, ending the conversation and pulling out the cord from behind the cube-shaped box. As all goes quiet, he leans back on the bed, and slightly grins only to be thrown in to shock by Elliot Costa barreling through his motel door, breaking off the hinges, and the door itself. Leaping up from his bed, Stone dives behind his motel refrigerator as Elliot aims his pistol. He kicks over the drawers and stomps with his feet, in effort to tumult the room and get Stone's attention.. Approaching Stone's bed, adjacent and feet away from the refrigerator the Misogynist falls back paces behind, crawling slowly in to the already opened bathroom door.. a shot rings out, alarming Stone who slides in. Elliot looks up at the ceiling, where the bullet hole remains, making a shower of dust fly down. Stone locks himself in the unventilated and wee bathroom just designed with a small tub, a toilet, and a sink sluice, along with a mirror over it. Elliot tiptoes over towards the bathroom door aware of Stone being inside. 'You're going to die soon or later.. so you might as well save your time.' Elliot chuckles, revolving another rapid shot from his pistol now worrying Stone who scrambles in the cramped bathroom.. looking over his right shoulder, he ponders to the shower curtain over the tub.. he walks over, climbing on the topside, and jerking the curtains over him, in a manner of camouflaging himself, although without the similar color on his clothing. Elliot continues cackling as he kicks in the bathroom door, stepping back vigilantly with his pistol out but then advancing forward without resistance.. with not much to look at he came forward towards the tub aperture, jerked the curtains to the side, lucky for Stone not having touch his limbs.. he sticks out his pistol but nothing.. circling around in confusion he is suddenly invaded with an attack. Stone comes over with the curtains and blinds Elliot, who recklessly beings pushing on the trigger.. but failing to hit Stone.. Elliot is brought down to a choke but easily elbows himself out of it, much to the disadvantage to Stone who innocently comes back with his hands in the air as Elliot laughs further with his semi-automatic weapon before him. 'Remember what I said Stone... if you don't do what I tell you.. I'll kill you.. and you actually doubted I would do that.... HUH?!' he bawls, pulling on the trigger but hearing an inaudible click, now much to his disadvantage.. Seizing the moment Stone steps forward tripping Elliot to the back of his head, grabbing his ankle, and twisting at it. Wincing at the agony, adding more to the pain, Stone whips out his blade from the back pocket of his dress pants... Elliot kicks and screams only having Stone stab him in the ankle so deep, it protrudes from the other side... Ripping it out carelessly, he stomps over his body and Elliot, too mutilated to prevent the stomps is further burdened and deemed unconscious. Looking at the blood-trickling blade, he places it back down in his pocket and leaves Elliot there, as the blood rushes out of his sleek black shoes from his ankle, leading up to his dress suit, imbrued in blood. Stone leaves the bathroom, back in to the now brisk main part of the motel, caused by the dismembered door. Fellow residents of the motel peer inside but Stone scares them away as he looks forward. Reaching over to the drawer near his bed, he pulls a paper forward, with a list of names.. He walks over the tipped over drawers, knocked down by Elliot and abandoned his motel room, soon walking down a row of steps leading to the curtilage and exit of the Bainbridge motel. It's around late evening; the moon is in its half-and-half image titled to the side, as Bainbridge continues to experience the frigid temperatures. Strolling onward he reads through the names, familiar names to the television and wrestling atmosphere: 'Miss Moonlight, Tessa Windsor-Green, and Lacuna Debris' The names of his adversaries the following day of Wednesday at Turmoil.. he now is seemingly more involved in his full-time job as he focuses on the names, actuating his misogyny addled emotions. He continues further in to the vacuous streets in the bowels of Bainbridge, readying himself for his match with those three females, assisted by other fellow PCW family workers, SOCIETY and Sivasian Krikorian. 'This is exactly what I came for...' he ends as he grins widely at the seeing of the names, skipping off in to the darkness, dropping the list with the opposition names.. but unseen before, and now at the top are words ENTITLED: 'NEW PCW Victims...' His upcoming three opponents are at clear sight, but there is another row down the straightened paper but it is blurred. All left to be heard is the girlie skips of the misogyny miser, off to meet his PCW associates, and then continuing his newly instated, PCW, misogyny campaign in Miami, Florida.
