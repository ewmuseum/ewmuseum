The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part XV]

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13, 2002 This past Friday on Fusion the feud between Politically Incorrect, Cowabunga Connection and The Heartbreakers heated up. The Heartbreakers made a point to get back at Politically Incorrect for what they did the week before, and Politically Incorrect made a point to stay one step ahead by interfering in The Heartbreakers' match. Cowabunga Connection just seemed to be shoved in the middle of all of this with a match against The Heartbreakers, and a heated conversation, along with some chair shots, with Politically Incorrect. Be that as it may, the war between the PINK! and the PURPLE~! was still burning. When would this never ending story come to a halt and how could it progress anymore from last time. Well, all you have to do is tune in... Valentine stood at the top of the flight of stairs, that led up to the main street. It was a dark night, the handful of wind blown clouds stood out dark against the luminous sky, sprinkled with white gems. But no one noticed, it was late, it was dark, Tuesday night and the good people of the city were celebrating the end of another day of work. Valentine was thankful that he had been able to buy another overcoat to replace the one that he had lost before. It was a good one, mostly wool, a hint of cashmere, a decent collar on it. It kept off the cold wind, and made him look like a million bucks. I think we've all come to realize how much Jack and Johnny care about style. A drunken group of revelers passed, and Valentine melted back into the darkness. Jack, Johnny and Elton were sober and rather regretting it. At a time like this you should be either in bed or drunk, none of the other options seemed particularly appropriate. They were arguing about children’s television programs from when they were young. None of them could name each individual herb. Valentine could, but he kept it to himself. Why had these children’s programs exerted such an influence over his generation. Scooby Doo, Ghostbusters, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles; they seemed to have replaced the classics as the cultural bedrock of the age. What a strange society we live in. A single beep came in over the earphone that he was wearing and he pressed the stopwatch once. Now he would see just how accurate Elton’s guess had been. He had always rather liked Ghostbusters as a child and only recently had he found out that it wasn't real. Yes, he can be a little slow at some times. What were all those other series, a drunk had found him. Drunk Man: “You got five bucks for a cup of tea?” The drunk was swaying peacefully. He had a nautical air, maybe it was just the swaying motion. Jack Valentine: “Sure, there you go.” Valentine could not be bothered arguing. Anyway they had a suitcase full of money, what was five dollars? Drunk Man: “You got another five pence, I could get a cup of coffee then?” By the time that the pair of them had finished the drunk had a small fortune, which was probably about the maximum amount of money he could conceive of. This prompted an excessive display of gratitude. He spat on his hand, and made to shake Valentine’s hand. Then with another sway, he decided to urinate down the stairs, Johnny Heart: “Shit, you can’t do that. Go on, you go and piss in the doorway over there.” Drunk Man: “You’re a good man, you know that, you’re a good man, have you got a family?” And off he went, to vomit all over the shop door. A minute later he was back, The Heartbreakers and Elton were getting a bit bored with all this... Jack Valentine: “How quickly do you suppose someone could run up these here stairs.” The drunk was picking at one of his pockets, he had pulled it inside out. Drunk Man: “These stairs, they’re mighty long, must be, oh a load of stairs. Could take a week to climb them.” Johnny Heart: “No seriously, how quickly could someone run up them, I reckon it could be done in fifteen seconds.” Drunk man: “No, no, take far longer than that, they really is a lot of stairs. I bet you loads of money, take more than fifteen seconds. I’ve got money you know, I’ll show you.” The drunk then started looking for the pocketful of money that Valentine had given him. Jack Valentine: “You see that man running, I bet he climbs up those stairs to here in less than fifteen seconds? Do we have a bet then.” Valentine watched as the figure ran out from the tangle of tenements to the foot of the stairs, a raincoat trailing in the wind. Drunk Man: “You got a bet, I’ve got money you know, enough for a few more drinks. He’s at the bottom of the stairs now, one, two, three, ...” The drunk was leaning quite distinctly to one side now. He was however entering into the spirit of things. Valentine pushed him to one side, the figure was powering up the stairs, both hands pressed on each knee as it pushed the step beneath it, he seemed to positively spring up the stairs. He was taking three stairs at a time, it was like the figure was falling upwards, it certainly bore no relation to a man running up stairs that was for sure. Drunk Man: “... ten, eleven, twelve, Jesus shit, he did it.” The drunk fell over in the excitement. Elton leapt up the last leap and was on the pavement. He was bent double, breathing quickly and deeply. Elton John: “I made that one hundred and thirty eight seconds, what did you get on the stop watch Valentine?” Jack Valentine: “Same here, you were quicker than you thought you’d be Elton.” Drunk Man: “Here you take the money, I’ve never seen anything like it, you won fair and square, take it, take it,...” The drunk handed Valentine back the handful dollars and after much spitting and shaking of hands he headed off into the night. Vanishing into a drunken heaving street full of people. Elton John: “Good to see that you weren’t wasting your time Valentine. If we’re happy with that timing we can call it a night.” Elton punched a palm in the air. Valentine punched another hand into his. The next day... Will Truman was a stickler for details, rules, regulations, he loved them. The more arcane the rules the better he liked them. He enjoyed reprimanding staff for breaking some rule or other. He would not sign anything until it was perfect, no mistakes, anything wrong with it he would send it back refusing to sign it. If you hadn't figured it out yet, Will Truman was the head accountant for the PINK! It had taken Elton fifteen attempts to claim a couple of bus fares once. It had taken a whole day. But of course Truman was right, you had to do these things properly it just would not do to get them wrong. When Valentine put around a note to collect money for the club, Truman had insisted on rewriting it. He was not the most popular person on the PINK! side, though equally he was probably not the least popular person either. Elton did not particularly hold a grudge against him but he was in for the next job against the PURPLE~! and it would be a straightforward job. He had it planned out, in fact he had half figured out how to carry out the job years ago after he had received an official reprimand from Truman for contravention of another mission, with the Village People who were then with the PINK! Elton had been sitting in the bar for most of the morning. From where he was sitting he could see several hundred yards down the road. The bar itself was on a junction. Because of the local fishing trade a lot of the bars around the harbor had licenses which allowed them to open early to tie in with the fishing boats coming into harbor and selling their cargoes. (OOC: Notice the logical thinking...you were wondering why a bar would be open in the morning...smart, aren't I? ; ) It was a plain functional bar. MTV was coming out of the TV which was on a gantry above the far corner of the bar. The bar itself being a solid block. Three sides here in the public bar, and one side opening onto the adjacent lounge bar. The lounge bar was empty. The public bar was not really a lot busier. The public bar however seemed to be a place of religious veneration. Various old men had come in during the course of the morning, bought a half and half sat for the half hour to drink it before leaving to take a bus home. No one had bothered Elton and he had spoken to no one apart from the bartender, a middle aged woman with bleached blond hair. Ten years ago she would have been an absolute babe. Sitting there quiet, almost motionless watching for Truman’s car to come down the road seemed perfectly natural. He had switched himself into neutral, normally he never stopped moving, always working on something, practicing something he might need, planning out some potential trick, memorizing the accountant books and ways into computers round the common and not so common security measures the PURPLE~! might have. This was a huge mission for some reason. A mission that had to be completed by Friday, of course, or The Heartbreakers would not be able to compete in the Texas Tornado Tag Match on Fusion against Cowabunga Connection and Politically Incorrect. Something that was of little concern to other members of the PINK!, but a huge concern for Jack and Johnny who would also be in on this next mission. Normally his hands would be working on something or his mind would be working through some problem. It was a constant hard slog in his job, you had to work mighty hard just to stay in the same place these days, whether it be at the top of the tag team division or keep your job as an accountant. You couldn’t just rely on a good right hook and a convincing manner now, you needed those skills certainly but you also needed to be able to handle computers, electronics, explosives and a million other things. This morning though his mind was just a blank, with one thought, to wait for Truman to appear on that road. He would. Elton knew he would at some stage today appear on the road. A man like Truman was at the top of the tree and had meetings and conferences to attend, no nine to five man him. But somewhere in that schedule he would appear here, on this road. The bartender thought he was asleep. She felt quite sorry for him. A good looking man like Elton, with a sparkle in his eyes. Okay, maybe he's not that good-looking, but she still felt sorry. Just sitting there all morning. It must be some job problem, or staying out of the house to avoid the wife, maybe he was pretending to the wife that he still had a job, when he had been made redundant. Certainly he had not drunk much, but he was not bothering anyone, and he seemed harmless enough. In fact he seemed quite nice. Maybe she would just go over and clean the ashtray, get him talking, see what he had to say for himself. There was still forty five minutes to go before the lunch time rush, when a hardened few of the locals working at the nearby offices came for a lunchtime drink and hot meal. Messengers mostly, but a few more senior staff. Some clever people among them. Maybe he was waiting for someone. Maybe he had worked around here. She could give the make up a touch up, it would not go amiss, and then empty that ashtray. Elton walked slowly and deliberately towards the oncoming car. It was coming up towards the junction quite quickly. As the car reached and passed Elton he turned on his heel. He had an apple in his hand. With a deft underarm flick the apple arced through the air, dropping down through the open sun roof of the car and out of sight. The brakes were slammed on, people were moving quickly in the car, searching for something, with another deft flick he threw the PINK! grenade. It too arced gently through the air and dropped into the car. Elton had turned to run. He bent into the direction he was heading. As he started to run he pressed the walkie talkie in his pocket. Valentine would be in place in one hundred and thirty eight seconds. He would stop momentarily on a busy street. The car’s windows were punched out in a burst of PINK!, that strange rectangular broken glass fell like icy snow. The following car had been the PURPLE~! security guards. They had seen what happened. Their training was to secure the attack site. Ignoring the traffic the security car over took Truman’s car drawing level. A couple of the Village People opened the car doors and poked their bows and arrows, out at some presumed PINK! attackers. Obviously the Indian was there. There was no real gunfire, they were quickly out of their car and checking Truman’s car. Onlookers were starting to gather. This whole situation had an ever increasing potential to do the PINK! damage. The Village People started pushing the gathering crowd back away from the car, smoke was thickening it seemed almost liquid. One of them was using a fire extinguisher to try and combat the flames. At the back of the crowd standing in the door of a bar was the bartender. She had never seen anything like this, what was the world coming to...PINK! and PURPLE~! fighting? What kind of sick joke was this? Lots of feminine men parading around throwing their colors at one another. Who could ever think of something like this? ; ) The second car started to reverse back along the road it had come along. They had seen a running man, from here running north there were only a couple of places you could run to. When he reached the halfway point Elton slipped down a gear, if the pursuing Village People followed him they would get another shot to capture one of the PINK! leaders, if they had not, they had lost the chance. Idiots, they used this route every day, it was not enough to know the layout of the streets, they should have paced out the escape routes, that was what he always did. Then with a renewed vigor he sprinted up the last half of the flight of stairs. He elbowed his way through the crowd. The Heartbreakers were just pulling up in an ugly convertible Ford Pinto. Unsuspecting car for any of the members of the PINK!, he slipped open the passenger door, and Elton climbed in, the car barely stopped. Elton John: “Come on boys nice and easy, but we’ve gotta get out of this place.” The petulant voice of Aerosmith's PINK! rang out on the cassette player, and Elton stretched back, the wind in his hair. The joys of the open road, rock and roll and an open top car. What a day for The Heartbreakers and Elton John! They had attacked their own accountant, Will Truman, but why? Obviously if you saw correctly, the PURPLE~! guards were trailing him, and obviously another PINK! member had defected to the other side. It's beginning to look like Elton and the Heartbreakers are the only loyal members of the PINK! Regardless, The Heartbreakers had completed their mission and were ready for their next task...against Cowabunga Connection and Politically Incorrect. Time would only tell if anything happened between now and Friday, and judging by the past of this 'war,' something is almost guaranteed to take place. Stay tuned!
