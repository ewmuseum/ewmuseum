Opportunity: Sweet or Sour?

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-08-02 Trigger stands outside the house for a few minutes--or hours; it is hard to tell. Eventually, the doorknob turns, and out emerges the sole inhabitant: an elderly man. It seems he is bringing his recycling bin to the curb, when he spots the Infinite Warrior, staring right at him. Trigger stands still like a statue, not a straggler, but not too intruding either. When he has the man's attention, the warrior begins. Trigger: What is opportunity? The man ventures closer towards the street where the questioner waits. He hears clearly, yet is unsure of the relevance to which the warrior asks. Old Man: Excuse me? The Infinite Warrior is fine repeating his inquiry… he is patient, but nevertheless demands satisfaction for his interrogation. Trigger: Opportunity. What is it and what does it mean to you? For some reason--one that he cannot put his finger on, the man finds himself contemplating a response. Why should he answer this nut-job? How can he not? Old Man: An opportunity is a chance that someone gives you… an opening that you can take that will result in better things for you. Trigger nods. Just the answer he expected. Now for the important part. Is opportunity the end of the line? Or will more questions appear, more roadblocks in the way on the path to the meaning of life. Trigger: Why haven't you taken opportunities set out for you? Old Man: Excuse me? Once again it is not the ability to hear, but the ability to comprehend that finds the old man second guessing the words of the Infinite Warrior. Trigger: You're getting older, you're losing strength, and soon you will be in your resting-place. Despite this, you are wise, you are wealthy, and you are free to do as you wish. But you're lonely. You've had opportunities to change this. Why don't you? The man's jaw drops, and his eyes go wide. He quickly paces backwards, until he stumbles over the steps of his porch and falls to a sitting position. Trigger walks step by step towards the cowering victim. The man thinks about yelling for help. What could would it do? Close enough now is the Infinite Warrior that if he intended on attempting a dastardly deed, it would be accomplished. What choice was there now? The man's fear goes away, and he slumps his shoulders and prepares to respond to the query. Trigger is so close now that he can hear the man's heaving breath; smell his fear… taste his anxiety. Old Man: How… How did you know? An acceptable rebuttal. Trigger's knowledge of the human mind seems almost mystical in a sense when one first encounters it. Trigger: I knew by you. I knew by your face, by the way you stand, withered from the world's trauma. I know who you are. Accept it. And answer my question. The man takes the comments for what they are worth, and gives the warrior what he wants. Old Man: I… I don't know. Opportunities arise and fall again. Sometimes I am afraid. Every day that I live my life it becomes harder to take advantage of opportunities, harder to even notice they're there! Opportunities are one thing… their results are another. In a strange turn of events, the man begins to weep, sobbing heavily into his hands. Trigger has his data, and his work is done. He turns, and sets off down the street. The man looks up only at the patter of the warrior's feet along the cement. He beckons his arms out, and Trigger stops momentarily. Old Man: Please… don't leave… help me. Trigger: You have helped me. Now you must help yourself. I must continue my journey. The Infinite Warrior begins to walk again, this time determined to never stop, to never look back until his mission is complete. While he walks to, he talks to the only one that will truly listen… the empty space. Trigger: Age leads to knowledge. Knowledge leads to greed. Greed leads to happiness. Happiness leads to freedom. Freedom leads to opportunity. What does opportunity lead to? Have I come to the end of my quest? Have I found the meaning of life? Trigger: No, no that could not possibly be it. Opportunity… it's not the answer, it is a key! I must take an opportunity, and it will lead me to more knowledge. Suddenly it hits him. Trigger: The PCW World Championship. The most coveted prize in the game… perhaps the most coveted prize in life. How many millions have lay in their beds, late at night, dreaming of even the chance to hold it's golden beauty? And I have this shot. An answer to my desperate desire, my thirst for the meaning of life. Call it a fluke if you will, a mishap, a mistake… but I have the chance. I have an opportunity… He stops once more, clenching his fists tight. He stares up at the sky… Trigger: …and not a soul on earth will keep me from taking it.
