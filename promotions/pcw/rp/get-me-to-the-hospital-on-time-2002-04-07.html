Get me to the Hospital on Time....

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th April 2002 It often starts like this. Of course it is because this is a text based system; just as soon as we work out the visual system with the psycho-tropic drugs in place, we’ll let everyone know. God dammit, I’m getting off track again aren’t I. I’m like a Lesbian scene in a middle of a pay-per-view... no wait, that’s unexpected and quite interesting and educational to watch... this is just unexpected and rather dim. We’re walking through a hospital reception area... ok, you’re not, you are actually sitting at a computer desk in a pair of soiled jeans whilst drinking a glass of Pepsi Cola and eating a Chicken and Mushroom pot noodle. No... that’s me again, sorry. Anyway, Mr Alexander Miyagawa, Mr Jeremy “Jobber” Johnson and of course Natalia’s younger sister, Lara are all walking through the reception area of Anaheim District General Hospital when Miyagawa suddenly spots a flower stand... Miyagawa: You two carry on, I’m just going to get Natalia some fresh flowers. JJ, you know the way, don’t you? JJ: Yep, only been up there a couple times a day since she was admitted... and its TRIPLE J now not JJ. JJ is so like... old hat. Miyagawa: And Triple J isn’t? Why don’t you just add another J to it and be Quadruple J, now that hasn’t been done before. Maybe you could combine the power of the Quadruple with the HIT and become a tag team or something. JJ raises his eyebrow at Miyagawa... while Lara waits impatiently. Lara: Do you two just constantly insult each other, or is it just to impress me, da? JJ and Miyagawa immediately turn to Natalia’s younger sibling. JJ: I thought we had toned it down quite a bit... **turns to Miyagawa** Isn’t that right oh jobber to the great Bad Battio... and if I remember quite correctly... ME. That’s right; you were pinned by me... in under two minutes if I remember correctly. Miyagawa just shrugs back at him as if he doesn’t really give a damn about what JJ just said to him... mainly because he doesn’t. If the Bad Battio thing still got to him after all this time, I think it would shock him more than anyone else... although JJ is the only person who brings up the JJ victory... it would seem like everyone else has completely forgotten about it. Miyagawa: At least you are straight up with your sexuality, you admit you’re gay, and that’s why everyone respects you. JJ looks at Miya as if he just got kicked in the nuts by a big grey donkey. Don’t ask me what the colour of the donkey has to do with the effect of the hoof in the balls, but I’m sure it has something to do with the donkey not being able to do much with his hair that morning.. JJ: No one respects me; Corey Williams gets more respect in a male stripper’s night club. And for the last time... I’M NOT GAY!!!! Lara turns to JJ for a second and grins at him as if she is just about to say something which will make the entire effect much, much worse... which she is about to anyway, incidentally. Lara: It’s ok, just be happy that you’re in America with that sexuality... you would not believe what they do to Gay men in mother Russia... they remove their testicles. JJ looks at Lara stony faced straight into her light green eyes for a brief split second before screaming out aloud... JJ: I’M NOT GAY DAMMIT!!!! Do I wear pink? Miyagawa, Lara and JJ all look at JJ’s pink and yellow Hawaiian shirt that he just happens to be wearing today. JJ: Ok... Do I wear leather?! All three look at JJ’s leather pants that he just happens to be wearing. Nice smoooooooth leather, not the cheap stuff. JJ: Look... I’m just not gay. You don’t see Chris Chase trying to chat me up. I’m a sexy guy, if I was gay, then gay people would try it on all the time. Lara: So when was your last girlfriend then? Da? Do tell. JJ goes slightly red, and then starts muttering as he wanders over towards the lift, dragging his heels all the way. Lara: Ok, I take it back about what I said before about you two fighting all the time... that was fun, da, it was indeed. Miyagawa: Can you just promise me one thing, just one tiny thing... Lara: What’s that? Miyagawa: Don’t get involved with him... if I had JJ for a brother in law... well that would just be like totally freaky. Miyagawa grabs a bunch of flowers, some pink ones... what? He’s a man, he doesn’t know what type of flowers they are... they’re flowers, they’re pink... therefore they’re correct to him... although he does know not to get white lilies; those things are for funerals only... boy he made that mistake once already and does not seek a repeat performance. Anyway, he pays for the overpriced hospital florist’s flowers and wanders over towards where JJ is standing by the lift. JJ: Just one thing about tonight... how do you plan to lift Boda? He’s much heavier than you are. Lara: Tonight? Miyagawa: Oh I have this big match tonight at the Duck Pond later... it’s nothing really. JJ laughs out loud. JJ: You’ve been kicking up a fuss about trying to get yourself booked in a main event of a ppv for eighteen months, and now you have, you say “it’s nothing really”. God damn. Miyagawa smirks over at JJ before hammering the lift call button a few more times to indicate the urgency and the lack of wanting to talk about this particular subject. JJ however, never being the one for subtle let downs, continues. JJ: So seriously, how are you planning to lift him? Miyagawa (bursting into song): Boda... he’s not heavy.... he’s my brother..... Lara: One of my favourite songs, a true communist song about helping the fellow man. Communism rules, didn’t you once loose to Comrade Stanislav? Miyagawa just shakes his head. Miyagawa: I got my ass kicked by Stanislav, and I’m the first to admit it. The guy ripped me to shreds in the ring. But I learnt from that match... I defeated Meanstreak before by playing Cruiserweight with him... I lost to Stanislav by trying to play Cruiserweight and it didn’t work... which is why against Boda I’m not playing Cruiserweight... I’m playing Puroresu. Lara and JJ look slack jawed for about ten seconds. JJ: The difference being? Miyagawa: Oh, you’ll see. And with that note, the lift door opens and Miyagawa, Triple J and Lara usher their way into the lift in order to go visit Lara’s sister, Natalia... her of course being Miyagawa’s wife.
