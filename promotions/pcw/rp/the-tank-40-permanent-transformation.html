Date: The First of the Final Three RP&#039;s of the Tank Series ﻿*********The Tank __40—********* Permanent Transformation The caterpillar enters its cacoonwith dreams of wings and colors and days of floating on heartbeats...How surprising that it’s grown teeth. ~Leigh Donovan Thursday, November 21 - Day 140 Soon after roll call, Harvey Blake leads a crew of half-a-dozen skin heads to Snow’s cell. Theyenter with the same self-assurance that the always seem to have. Harvey is the only one withouta shaved head, but some would argue that he’s bald enough to fit right in. Harvey’s eye patch has always been the item that Snow has found most menacing - completelyblack, save for the blood-red swastika sewn onto it. Snow doesn’t even know if there’s still aneye, however useless, behind that swastika. Whatever the case, Harvey is blind and the swastikaremains where a healthy eye used to rest. To Harvey’s right, of course, is Jake Hooper. Not much older than Snow, Jake Hooper has beenbuilding himself into a physical force. Standing six feet, five inches tall, the young man hasadded at least twenty pounds to his mass since entering the Tank, putting him around the twohundred and fifty pound mark. That’s the same weight that Snow has packed into his six feet, one inch, frame. He’s beensitting on his bed since well before the morning buzzer sounded, the heavy pressures ofupcoming events pounding on the interior of his skull as if about to turn emotion physical andburst through. Of course, Alliances is on the horizon, and by that time, Snow hopes to be a free man again. Hisparole hearing is on Saturday... just two days away. Freedom hasn’t crossed Snow’s mind inquite some time. It’s hard to imagine it now. After only five months contained within the wallsof the Tank, Snow feels like he’s spent his whole life here. Of course, he can’t shake the reality that Vincent Giovanni is still alive and posing as large athreat as ever. Snow hopes that Tim McDonald is working on that problem... then again, he’snever fully trusted McDonald. More immediately, Snow has to worry his battle with J-Thug - tonight. Just hours away, he andthe ultimate of beasts will battle one on one in the tiny caged-in basketball court of the gym. Heknows that’s what Harvey Blake has come to talk about - some of the implications of this fightare inevitable, he supposes. “Up bright and early,” Harvey says with his warm smile - the dress of the devil. The aging mancan sometimes seem so warm. That’s probably why so many people follow him, thinks Snow. “That’s what I like to see, slugger.” “What the fuck are you here for, Blake?” Snow says, his voice as harsh as he can make itwithout raising it. “Tea party,” the Nazi smirks. “Fuck you,” Snow says. “I don’t want you near me, you old bastard.” “Sometimes what you want isn’t important, son,” Harvey says, leaning on the wall oppositeSnow, who’s sitting on his upper bunk. Harvey changes the subject. “You know, I talked toyour room mate yesterday. He’s doing ok... considering.” “Considering?” Snow says with a smirk. “You mind-fucked the poor guy and sent him on akamikaze mission. You knew damn well that J-Thug would smell him coming a mile away.” Inreality, it was a matter of inches, or less than an inch, before J-Thug sensed Leigh Donovan’slingering presence behind him. When J-Thug turned around, Leigh dropped the blade and payedthe price of failure via a severe beating at the hands of the man thought to be the most dangerousin the Tank’s history. “Think what you want son,” Harvey shrugs. “The young man lying in that hospital bedperformed a valiant deed in that room out there. He stood on the platform of his race.” “Fuck you,” Snow repeats an earlier message. “What he did was fucking suicide - and it wasyou who put him up to it.” “A platform for his race,” repeats Harvey. “And that’s exactly what you’ll be standingon tonight.” “Shit,” Snow says with an angry smile. “I fucking knew what this was about as soon as I sawyou and the fucking cue ball crew coming my way.” Snow leans back on the concrete that hisbunk is jutting from. “You can look at this however you want to,” Harvey says in a voice that’s growing lower andcolder. “But sooner or later, you’re going to have to face the facts - when you step into thatcage, you’re representing us all. You may not like it, but you versus J-Thug is the Aryans versusthe niggers.” Snow’s eyes fill with hatred as he stares across into the evil lone eye of Harvey Blake. “Fuck it,” Snow says finally. “I’m not fighting. Find yourself another stupid fuck to put on yourplatform.” “That’s not the way it works, son,” Harvey says, his evil smile creeping back onto his face. “Whether you like it or not, you’re fighting tonight. You’re fighting for everything - your race,your life, even your freedom.” Snow’s attention narrows and every ounce of his heart, body, and soul, rests on that one word:freedom. “What are you talking about?” he asks with a voice that has reluctantly grown quiet. Harvey’s smile broadens. “I have it on good authority that a number of the C.O’s have theirmoney on you,” he says. “If you don’t win, or, as you brought up, don’t show up, I really don’tthink the men who you make hundreds and thousands of dollars poorer will be ready to let youleave this place on Saturday.” Snow is silent. “No,” Harvey says, turning his back to Snow and making his way toward the exit, “I think they’drather keep you around... you know... to give you a proper send-off.” Snow’s teeth clench and his eyes flash with familiar rage. “Just something to think about,” Blake says with a cocky smile as he leads his crew out of thelion’s lair. When they’re gone, Snow sees that J-Thug’s cell is packed with members of his Thug gang. Thursday, November 21 - Day 140 “This is very important, Snow,” says Tim McDonald, holding his cigarette between two fingerset never actually pulling it near his mouth. “This fight can make a lot of people, namely thosewho bet on you, very wealthy. We’ve got money coming in from all over the Tank on this. And,seeing as how the odds are stacked three to one against you, and I run the gambling in thismotherfucker, I can become very wealthy.” Snow stares blankly, still sitting on his upper bunk. He didn’t go to his work detail today, butwithout Geoffery Rankin around, no one will harass him about it. Apparently, every C.O in the prison knows about the fight anyway. “Who’s going to believe that I made thousands in prison?” Tim chuckles. He’s already madethousands, Snow knows - he stole all of Hattoni’s money the day that Hattoni was killed. Hattoni was rich, for an inmate. Tim runs his fingers through his spiky blond hair - it’s less stiff than usual. Snow, lost in hisown mind of stress and chaos, has long-since stopped listening. “You know though,” McDonald says, “after the trouncing you gave that big fucker in the mainroom, I can’t help but be surprised that no one’s betting on you. Well, I can’t say no one. The Nazis all put there money down on you, and a couple of the C.O’s are looking to start acollege fund for their kids if you win. In general though, most people seem to think yousuckered that big fucker.” Snow faintly hears his company, but he’s still lost in his troubles. “Me?” McDonald continues, “I stayed away from making a bet of my own. What’s the point? I’m getting all the action on the gambling in here anyway - well, me and my boys. What’s thesense of risking that? If you win, I still get three times the money I would if you lose.” Snow finally changes the subject. “What about Giovanni?” he asks. “How’s that whole thinggoing. I might be getting out on Saturday, you know - I can’t leave that business unfinished.” An evil smile comes over McDonald’s face. “My friend,” he says, “I think we’ve got that one inthe bag.” Indeed, things aren’t looking good for Vincent Giovanni. At the moment, all of hisfriends on the outside think that he handed the police vital information in cases againsthigh ranking mafia members. In reality, it was Tim McDonald who both supplied theinformation to the police and then pinned the whole thing on Giovanni. A masterpiece,in Tim McDonald’s mind. Of course, the fact that all of the guards have been paid to keepGiovanni from making any contact with the outside world helps. “How?” Snow asks. “That’s not important,” Tim answers. “You’ve got bigger - much bigger - things toworry about right now.” He looks over his shoulder to peer across the cell balcony into J-Thug’scell. The beast is alone now, and his eyes are fixed on his opponent. Thursday, November 21 - Day 140 The gym stinks of men. It’s the smell of well over a hundred men jammed into Unit B’s tinygym. Prisoners, C.O’s, even a few of the psychiatric and management staff trustworthy enoughhave shown up. Each of them cheering in there deepest voice. Masculinity floods the room. From inside the cage, Snow spots Harvey Blake in the front row - someone actually went to thetrouble of setting up make-shift bleachers. “Kill the nigger,” yells an anonymous skinhead, above the crowd. Snow’s eyes snap toward thespeaker, but he’s better off trying to find a needle in a barn - there’s a complete section ofaudience that seems reserved for Nazis. Unfortunately, they’re as close to the cage as they cancomfortably be. The inside of the cage is a tiny basketball court, about fifteen feet wide by twenty-five feet long. Though the space will likely be tight, Snow thinks he’ll be able to use his speed to his advantage,although the bulking up that he’s done since coming to the Tank has slowed him downconsiderably. Of course, he’s stronger than he’s ever been in his life, but only a fool would tryto turn a fight against the imposing J-Thug into a test a strength. “Your white ass is in for a long ride, motherfucker,” an anonymous voice barks at Snow. Whatever the result, Snow knows that the words are true. Win or lose, J-Thug will no doubt gethis shots in. None of Snow’s experiences in a wrestling ring will help him here, in this tiny cube that onemight think designed to rip flesh from bone. One concrete wall to his left, and every other sidedominated by chain-link fence that has jagged points jutting out in spots where the steel hasrusted to its breaking point. This is nothing like a wrestling ring. J-Thug, of course, would be dangerous even in a wrestling ring. His experiences in here, and onthe streets, may well be more than enough to match all of Snow’s training and experience in theprofessional world of grappling. J-Thug is as dangerous as any opponent Snow has ever faced,especially in these circumstances. The man has killed more people than he can keep track ofand injured countless more. His great, great, great grandchildren would never live long enoughto finish his sentence - multiple sentences. Twenty feet away, the beast cracks the knuckles of his right hand while glaring holes in Snow’sface. He’s impossibly large, thinks Snow - the shape of the massive man is something onewould never imagine possible. Not only is he remarkably tall, standing over six and a half feettall, but at mere ounces away from three hundred pounds, the width of the man is breath-taking. “There will be a winner in this fight,” announce Benji Graham, head C.O of Unit B, as he stepsinside the cage to make the announcement to the audience. “Whether or not we’re here all nightmakes no difference. When one of these men gives up, falls unconscious... or worse... the fightwill be declared over. Tim McDonald has informed me to tell you all that as of right now, allbets are off.” Snow can’t believe that Graham is smiling. “Gentlemen,” Graham says, turning his attention to the combatants. “...Or should I sayanimals?” he smirks, sharing a laugh with his fellow C.O’s. “When you hear the lock of thecage, the fight will begin. Not before. Understand?” Neither combatant give a reaction, so Benji Graham takes the opportunity to leave the basketballcage. J-Thug, with a hint of a smile, pulls his white shirt over his head and tosses it aside, baring hisupper body completely. In bold blue letters, the word ‘THUG’ has been freshly tattoo’ed, withinthe last week or two, on his massively wide chest. Snow’s only preparation is to brush a strand of his long blond hair away from his eyes. He’ssurprised at how little he’s feeling right now. He doesn’t fear J-Thug, despite the fact that heknows the obvious threat that the ogre poses. He doesn’t feel anything toward him. No rage, nohatred - nothing like what he feels for his opponents when he steps into a wrestling ring. And then he hears it - they all hear it. CLICK!!! The lock snaps shut and the battle for freedom, from the cage and from each other’s shadow,begins. J-Thug advances first, his eyes never falling from Snow’s face - J-Thug’s left eye is still showingsigns of the beating Snow gave him a few days earlier, though in that case, J-Thug was taken bysurprise. There will be no surprises this time. With a mighty roar, the black monster swings a wild right hand that Snow is easily able to avoid. Instinctively, Snow finds his hands are up and he’s moving around the small space allowed tohim. Unfortunately, J-Thug is a great deal faster than Snow had hoped, and his second punch, asweeping left, lands cleanly on Snow’s jaw. He feels his knees lock beneath him and he staggers back a bit as the crowd roars in approval. J-Thug swings again, this time with his right hand, and the fore arm catches Snow in the side ofthe head, smashing the young man to his right. “Fuck!” Snow curses the power of the beast. But as much as J-Thug is known as the Beast, Snow is known as the lion. When J-Thug returnsto the well once too often, swinging another sweeping right hand, Snow ducks under and slamshis right shoulder into J-Thug’s rock-hard midsection with all his might. Like a strainingbulldozer, Snow manages to push the monster backwards, prompting another mighty cheer fromthe crowd - the fight will not be one sided. “Go down, motherfucker!” bellows J-Thug as he begins jabbing heavy elbows into Snow’s back.With his left hand he grabs a handful of Snow’s thick blond hair while he continues to beat onthe back of Snow’s body with the right. “Ugh!” Snow grunts as he’s finally able to slam J-Thug back-first into the cage. J-Thug lets outa cry of pain, confirming the fact that a few of the chain links had been broken and rusted metalhad pierced the monster’s back. The crowd cheers as Snow leans back and then explodesforward, sending J-Thug’s massive body into the fence a second time. “You cocksucker!” bellows the black beast. He soon manages to grab Snow’s waist, but can’tfind the leverage to push the lion backward. From his lowered position, Snow knows that he has the advantage, but the blows that he sufferedto his back are hurting him more than he’s allowing to show. He’s thankful that he didn’t takeoff his white wife-beater Tank top; if he had, bruises would no doubt be visible already, and thebeast would still be hammering down. “Send him into the fence again!” a man from the crowd bellows - it’s unmistakably HarveyBlake. Because he knows it’s Harvey Blake, Snow refuses to do so. Instead, he musters all hisstrength, and with a mighty roar fitting a real lion, Snow unbelievable lifts the startledmonster off his feet! “Fuck yeah!” cheers an audience member. A second later, Snow lifts the beast completely overhis head and sloppily belly-to-belly suplexes him down on the other side - the audience is awed. Even Snow can’t seem to believe that he accomplished such a feat - he would never have beenable to do something so powerful before his ‘bulking up’ period upon entering the Tank. J-Thug rolls over once on the dirty concrete floor, holding his back in obvious pain. He still hasenough sense to realize the immediate danger, however, and he’s able to scamper to his feetbefore Snow can take advantage of the opportunity. Before a moment passes, he lunges forwardand rams his thick elbow into Snow’s chest, using the momentum to wrestle the younger man tothe floor. Another cheer from the crowd. “It’s all over now, motherfucker!” J-Thug tells Snow through clenched teeth, trying tirelessly toget a clean grip on his hairy neck - Snow’s beard is thicker than usual lately. To aid his efforts,J-Thug begins hammering down on Snow with right hands, keeping his left locked firmly onSnow’s chin-guarded neck. One heavy blow connects cleaner than the others and blood splashes up from a gash opened onSnow’s forehead. A few punches later and J-Thug’s forearm is soaked. “Finish him off!” orders someone in the cruel audience. Obviously, J-Thug attempts to. Onefinal time, he rains a heavy blow on Snow’s completely exposed face, opening another thick cutabove Snow’s left eye. When he’s satisfied that he’s drained Snow of enough resistence, J-Thugonce again applies his right arm to aid his left in choking the young lion out. From his back, Snow desperately sends straight right hands up into the general area of J-Thug’sface, though few are accurate enough to do any real damage to the monster; a few nicks andscratches from scraping knuckles become visible on the monster’s face, but nothing more. With his left hand, Snow is successfully able to defend J-Thug’s massive and obtrusive armsfrom denying him from too much air, though the beasts strength is more than enough tohave Snow coughing ocassionally. They stay in that position on the floor for well over a minute, with J-Thug tirelessly trying tochoke Snow into unconsciousness - or possibly further. The audience is hushed, waiting withevery second for Snow’s arms to finally fall limp. “It’s time to go to sleep now,” says J-Thug as he leans close to Snow, his eyes a mere half footaway from his opponents. He’s too close, and Snow knows it. With an incredible burst,Snow thrusts forward through J-Thug’s powerful arms and slams his forehead directly into thebeast’s nose - it breaks on impact. “Holy fuck!” yells an inmate at the cracking sound that comes from J-Thug’s face as theheadbutt strikes, dead on target. And then, for just a moment, J-Thug’s strength wavers, andSnow opportunistically breaks the monster’s unbelievably powerful hands away from his throatand sends a deadly accurate and quick combination of a single looping right and left to eitherside of J-Thug’s shaved head. The beast topples and Snow rolls out of harms way. When both men get to their feet, it’s a whole knew fight - a war. There is no audience for eitherof them now, they see only their opponent and the visions in front of their eyes. J-Thug, with a crimson river flowing from his nose, attacks Snow with a flurry of lefts andrights, only to find that for every punch he lands cleanly, he receives another just as cleanly. Henever dreamed Snow to be so powerful, even after feeling Snow’s shots a few days ago. Thatman was like a preview of this man, he realizes now. Sometime between the time theyentered the cage and now, J-Thug unlocked the lion that has always lived inside Snow, and forthe first time in his life, he realizes that there is a challenge that he may not be able toovercome - this is it. A beast as terrible and hateful as himself; a lion for his monster. Neither of them hear anything - not even the sickening sounds of raw knuckles on flesh. Thecrowd is virtually silent anything, staring completely in awe at the punishment that each of thesetitans are handing out to, and receiving from, each other. Finally, J-Thug smashes a blow to Snow’s bloody forehead hard enough to stagger the youngman, and he steps in to take advantage of the situation. Snow’s resilience, however, is not to beunderestimated. As soon as J-Thug takes a step forward, Snow fires back and nails the largerman with a heavy right hand powerful enough to make the leader of the Tank’s largest blackgang shaky at the knees. Now it’s Snow’s turn to take advantage. He stares at his opponent with his eyes on everything he hates about the world and himself. With a right hand, he swings at everything that appears to be in the way of his freedom - thebeast staggers back from the shot. He follows with a left hook, thinking of the way the prisonhas all but killed his relationship with his family - J-Thug drops to one knee. Snow hovers over his opponent, his bruised chest heaving from heavy breath. For everythingthat the Tank has made him, a thousand things worse than he hated himself for upon entering theprison, he lifts his right boot and delivers the knock out blow to J-Thug’s forehead... ...It doesn’t land... Mustering what sense he can find in his groggy head, J-Thug manages to dodge the incomingK.O-blow, grabs Snow’s foot, and thrusts his blond-haired opponent mercilessly to the ground. J-thug clumsily crawls over to Snow’s fallen body and attempts to mount him, but finds thatSnow has already gathered his wits and is forced to grapple his way into position. Snow deniesall of J-Thug’s efforts, his teeth clenched and his blazing blue eyes wide and bloodshot - the leftis beginning to close from the swelling beneath the blood-spewing gash just above it. Even onthe floor, their fists fly at each other. It’s a fight of desperation now. Before long, the pair are able to slip from each other’s sweat-and-blood lubricated grasps, andthey regain their feet one more time. Both men are out of breath, unsure if the cause isexhaustion or maybe an injury to their lungs. Snow’s blond hair is black with sweat and hisbeard is read with blood. As they stare at each other under the dim lights of the gymnasium, unable to hear the deafeningcheers of their audience, they almost exchange a moment of genuine respect - almost. Snow is wearing a costume of blood by now. It runs from the several cuts and gashes thatdecorate his face and into his hair where it’s transported to the rest of his body. Again, he seeseverything that he hates - everything that he is. He is the rage that has consumed him for all of these years. It’s no longer part of him, ithas overtaken him completely, and finally in this moment, killed every shred of the man that hehad hoped he could become. The rage and hatred enter his veins and run through his body aseffective as poison, and fill him completely to the brim. There is no consequence for refusal -it’s like a freight train crashing through paper - absolutely and inconceivably inevitable. Theremust be release... I am not a good person I will never be a good person I am the antagonist I AM THE ANTAGONIST! It explodes - everything explodes. Every degree of every measurement of his hatred blowoutward, ripping through him like a million focussed lightening bolts. The target of theinexplicable burst is Jeremy Doris, known to the prisoners of the Tank as J-Thug. Snow lashes out with a superkick so fast, merciless, and violent that it catches the breath ofevery testosterone-spouting lung in the audience. The result is inevitable. The blow crashes cleanly into the left side of J-Thug’s jaw and thebeasts drops as quickly and limply as if he’d been taken down by an elephant gun. His thickbody shouts a sickening thud as it crashes to the concrete floor. There is no motion - there’sbarely breath. His jaw is shattered from the impact of the kick and several of his teeth roll broken and bloodyout onto the dirt of the floor. The way his head tilts to the side allows the blood rushing frominside his mouth a safe exit. Snow’s chest heaves as he stands victorious, his teeth still clenched and his eyes still wide. Hestill feels the after shock of the explosion. He reigns supreme in this place now - king of thejungle. The audience is so silent that a deaf man would miss nothing. And then, when their breathreturns and they regain their wits, they burst into a cheer that threatens to reach through the thickconcrete walls of Unit B. “Unlock the fucking door!” screams Snow as he half stalks, half staggers, over to the exit. Immediately, a smiling Benji Graham rushes over to the door and releases the victoriouscombatant. Graham is half exhilarated by the thrilling battle, and half exhilarated by the factthat he’s just made himself well over two thousand dollars - he was one of the few who pickedSnow to beat a man that outweighed him by fifty pounds. Snow escapes Graham’s pats on the back and tries hard to make his way through the see ofcongratulations and back to his cell. Suddenly, a strong hand whirls him around and he’sconfronted with the genuinely happy face of Harvey Blake. “Victory!” Blake cheers. “You won it for all of us, son! All of us! Twenty years ago, I beat thebiggest nigger in here and won the war for us! You just did the same thing!” Blake may as wellhave signed his own death certificate - he should have known how Snow, pumped full ofadrenalin, would react to such a statement. In a flash, Snow’s fists connect with Harvey’s one-eyed face three times - two rights and a left toseparate them. Blake hits the floor before he realizes what has happened. His companion, Jake Hooper’s eyes flash angrily, but Snow’s daring face forces the large youngman to back down. Instead of attacking Snow, he reaches down to help his leader, the one eyedNazi, to his feet. Snow pushes his way through the crowd, and eventually, through the heavy doors and back intothe main floor of the Unit. He doesn’t quite know why he yearns to get to his cell so much. Hedoesn’t know what peace loneliness will bring him, but it must be better than this ugly room. With the crowd still celebrating all around, except for those especially close to J-Thug, Harveybreathes heavily and utters to Jake Hooper, “That motherfucker just crossed me for the lasttime!” His lone eye blazes, a testament to his vow.
