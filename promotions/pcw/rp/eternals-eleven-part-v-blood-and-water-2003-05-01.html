::~Eternals Eleven~:: (Part V- Blood and Water)

1 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/01/03 Prelude: In the previous chapter Shane and Mikey were in Chicago to meet up with Shane's mafia-loving cousin, Huey Lucio. Shane rejected his cousin when he was younger as he found the mafia a waste of time, but who'd have thought that later down the line Wethers would have taken over power of his late fathers company in London, England? So now in part five of the continuous epic, Wethers meets up with Lucio in the hope he will join his growing Security Team for E-Fest. Wethers knows that Lucio has the power and the right resources to make sure nothing goes wrong at the concert but the question is... Is blood really thicker than water? The streets are becoming darker as the sun begins to descend over the horizon. The streets are now pretty busy as people rush back from work, or out for a night of socialising. Shane turns right at a small junction, walking onto a small street. This street in particular is pretty quiet, surrounded by thatched houses in long rows. Shane walks over to the pavement on the left and towards one of the houses, he follows the foot walk to the front of the house where there are stairs leading down to what looks like a basement. He suddenly hears footsteps behind him, he stops and turns around sharply..but there is no one there, just the odd car that rolls by. Shane continues to stare back over his shoulder for a while before continuing to walk. He walks down the stony steps and towards a blue wooden door at the bottom of the stairs. Shane knocks on the door, you can hear whispers from inside the room, and suddenly a voice can be heard . It sounds like a mans voice, he coughs to clear his throat “Ahem....er....who is it?” “It's me....Shane.” he announces from the outside of the door “Let me in.” the whispers continue, as if there's a discussion taking place “Er....what's the password?” asks the voice. “hmm...let me think,” Shane ponders sarcastically “is it 'open the door before I beat you like a bitch' ?” There is sounds of the door being unlock, suddenly the door opens and a tall, bulky guy stands in front of him with a suit on. The guy is well built and has the shape of a big bodybuilder, he is bigger than Shane and Shane is 6'3. The guy is unshaved and is dressed like a cocktail waiter, but not the sort you'd want to meet on a cold day. The guy looks Shane up and down and opens the door wide for Shane to walk in. He looks outside the door to make sure no one was following as Shane walks inside. The basement is pretty clean, with white painted walls, a large metal table and some chairs a bar with many different liquors, beers and spirits. At the table sits three people, each wearing a different custom designed suit. They All acknowledge Shane's presence, one of them stand up. He has a cream suit, a black shirt and a cream and red striped tie, he holds a cigar in his left hand. The guy is none other than ‘Huey Lucio, Shane’s mafia running cousin. He gained the nickname “Blue Eyes” after Frank Sinatra as his ‘Mr Smooth’ ego made him one of the most calm and collected don on the streets of America. “Shane-o! where'sa you been cuz” yells Huey with his common Italian-American accent, “don'ta you-a know how much i missed ya? Come over hee’ and give yer cousin Huey a hug!” Huey leans forward to hug Shane, but Shane steps back “Huey! What the hell you doing fool?” Shane replies angrily “get off me before bitch slap yo' ass.” Suddenly the two guys at the table and the bar man reach for guys and point them at Shane. Shane stops point blank , but Huey turns around to face the guys, raising his hand “Hey!!! leave it out guys...” Huey replies without hesitation, “this is family ova' hee'. They all put their guns away and quietly sit down “Shane, let me introduce you to Frankie....he runs the mob in New York.” Huey says, pointing over to the far right of the table. Frankie sits at the table with a grey suit, which almost matches his hair. He has a face like a bag of chisels and doesn't smile. Huey then points at a guy who looks to be wearing a white skirt, a blue blouse and make-up. Shane looks disgusted and lost for words “I-i-i-is that guy wearing L-l-lipstick?” Shane asks with a worried look on his face. “Yeah...” replies Huey, “that's Carlos, he runs the mob in San Francisco.” Shane nods his head whilst trying to believe the stupidity “Yeah, it figures.” Shane replies with a heavy sigh. Huey then points to the large barman stood behind the bar “I believe you've already met Francesco.” He says. “Yeah” Shane replies. Huey looks Shane deep in the eyes “Now Shaney...” Huey says calmly “I should let them blow your brains out since you ran me out of my business...that being my protégé', the NIWC. But seen as you are still my cousin and I love Auntie Miranda, I will let it go this time.” Shane frowns and shakes his head “Auntie Miranda died.” Shane reveals to Huey. Huey looks shocked, he falls to the floor as he puts his hands to his face and shakes his head in despair ”oh no...” Huey cries, rubbing his eyes in distress “Auntie Miranda was the only one who understood me” Shane grins and mutters to himself “Auntie Miranda died when we were 4.” Shane adds once again. Huey looks up at Shane “She did?” Huey asks in a not so-convincing way. Shane nods his head “oh..well er....” he mumbles. Huey gets back up “So I hear you need some good security for the concert” Huey asks. Shane nods again Huey reaches inside his expensive suit and pulls out a diary “We are planning on it for mid-June” Shane explains. “Me and the boys..” Huey says, pointing to the guys in the room “..we’ll do it for….say three grand, only cos your family” Shane looks at Huey as if he's crazy. He grins sarcastically “oh yeah sure,” Shane detests, “you charged Alice Cooper less than that for his weekend in L.A. I know for a fact the pigs will be on your back every minute.” Huey cackles “Ah Shane-o, you amuse me.” Huey says confidently “We have everything under control, you see. We have someone that the police want more than anything...” “The Pretzel king?” Shane guesses. “…No” Huey answers. “Doctor Donut?” Shane guesses once more. “No!....” Huey yells impatiently “Saddam Hussain!” Huey leans over the table and picks up a small device that looks like a remote control. It is black and has a few buttons on it. He presses the central red button and a large screen descends from the ceiling. The screen shows live access of Saddam Hussain. “What the f.....? ? ?” Shane says in a bewildered way. “This is our headquarters in downtown Detroit.” Huey informs Shane. “Saddam Hussain's being held captive in a small garage where he will listen to Britney Spears on repeat play 24/7.” “Wow...” Shane says in surprise “they can do that?” Huey nods his head “Yeah,” Huey reassures him, “they're doing it to hostages in the armed forces It's a sort of torture device. After that we will force him to perform Swan Lake on national TV, wearing only a skimpy leotard.” “Yuk!” Shane groans in disgust. “Yeah” Huey adds “and if he doesn't do what we tell him we will cut his balls off, give him titties and send him to Costa Rica for prostitution.” Shane looks on in total disbelief “Damn....” Shane says, almost lost for words, “it must suck to be him right now.” Huey nods his head and watches the screen “Yeah...you could say that.” Huey says with a chuckle. All of a sudden, the sound of swan lake begins and Saddam Hussain looks up at the screen “I will get you damn americaaaaaaans!” Saddam Hussain yells at the cameras in fear.. “Anyway Shane, wanna stay for a game?” asks Huey with a sinister grin. Shane looks down at the playing cards on the table “Sure...” Shane replies with another cocky grin, “I’m good at this game.” As Shane walks over to the table to reach for the cards, Big Francesco runs over to him and taps him on the back “Tag! your it!” squeals Francesco. The four mob members run around the room “C'mon Shane! your it! you gotta catch us!” Huey giggles. Shane shakes his head and frowns “What??? ....” Shane shouts with yet more disbelief in his eyes “you freaks are too stupid to be true. I ain't tagging any of you bitches, how about I play kabaam? It's where I shove my foot up your ass and it goes kabaam!” The four gang members stop and pull out there guns once again, pointing it at Shane “Ah stop with that damn gun thing for hells sake!” Shane orders them, “They aren't even real anyway!” Francesco points the gun against Shane's head “What makes you so sure?” Francesco quizzes. “Erm...the way that is say's 'Toy world' on the side of it.” He reveals. “Dang” Francesco sighs. Shane uses his quickness to pull Francesco's arm away and throw him over Shane's shoulder. Before any more trouble breaks loose, a whole raid of police burst through the door FREEEEEEZE! “What the..?” Shane says as he turns. “huh?” says Huey. “Pigs? ..mmmmm...bacon” drools Francesco, with saliva running down his chin “....mmmm Mcbacon burger.” The police inspector walks over to them “This place has been reported as a scene of organised crime.” The fat Police Officer shouts “.........and the possession of drugs which we will smoke and snort later on. Now don't try run because there's nothing funnier than 5 junkies bouncing off the walls like pinball’s whilst we sit down and have a Joint.” Shane frowns “I'm no junkie you donut eating rectal wart!” Shane complains. The police walk in the room and arrest all of the mafia members, including Shane “I'll be the judge of that.......haha,” laughs the officer. “I've always wanted to say that. Take them away boys” As the police begin to take them all away under arrest, Huey looks back at Shane who is in handcuffs
