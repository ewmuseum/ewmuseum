Can OG help push LeBlanc?

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/02 [i]Scene opens with a close-up of 'The Warden' Jack Riley. The in-car camera zooms in on his face as the PaddyWagon rolls down the street towards the arena. Tonight at Turmoil, the Warden will lead the 'Swamp Dawg' Karl LeBlanc as he teams with Orpheus Grant for a huge tag match vs. Jason Core & The Rush.[/i] Warden: Tonight's the night! Karl LeBlanc and Jason Core will square off once again... for the first time since March Madness. TONIGHT... the Swamp Dawg's bite will be louder than his bark! Driver: Uh, Warden... LeBlanc doesn't bark... he doesn't even talk. Warden: Shut up, dummy. LeBlanc and O.G. is a match made in HELL! No one will be able to stop those two ogres. Driver: What made these two guys decide to tag together? Warden: Destiny... O.G. is the only person I've ever seen LeBlanc talk to. I guess they're homies? Almost like long lost brothers... they speak the same language, they read each others minds. I've never seen such potential in a tag team before. They have the strength, they have the determination, and most importantly, they sure as hell have the chemistry. Driver: Will LeBlanc go back after the Hardcore title? Warden: Does a shark shit in the sea? Hell yes he's going back after the title. He never should have lost it in the first place. That's what I'm trying to tell you... now that he has found a confidant in Orpheus Grant I can see LeBlanc rising to a whole new level. He was never happy before. He couldn't relate to anybody, especially us. I don't think he really likes us 'white folk' too much. Teaming with O.G. is the best thing that could have happened to this young man's career. He focused now... he's ready to re-launch his career. Driver: Man, I gotta piss. I'm pullinh over here at this mini-mart. [i]The two men hop out of the car and enter the convenient store. The clerk recognizes Warden Riley. [/i] Clerk: Hey man! I know you. You're the dude from PCW! I watch you guys three times a week... can I shake your hand? My name's Dante. Warden: Dante, what the hell kinda name is that? Yeah, I'm Warden Riley... what's it too ya? Clerk: I'm just a huge PCW fan. Ya know, I really dig that big black guy you manage. He's a bad motherf*cker. Kinda reminds me of my friend Silent Bob. Warden: You too huh? Everywhere I go I gotta here you morons chastise LeBlanc for not speaking. HE DOESN'T NEED TO SPEAK!!! Clerk: Yeah he does man. Don't you know. I understand you fellas don't really have a background in sports entertainment, but it's all about mic skills man. Warden: Well, coming from a numbnut like you that doesn't mean a whole lot to me. Clerk: I'm just tellin ya. If you wanna put him over with the crowd, draw some serious heat... you're gonna have to teach him some oratory man. That's just the way it is. I've been watching wrestling for years... I know these things. Warden: Gimme a damn can of snuff and shut your mouth. Clerk: What kind? Warden: Skoal, wintergreen... long cut. Driver: Alright Warden. I'm ready, let's roll. Warden: What the hell took you so long... you didn't tell me you had to shit! Driver: Well, you didn't ask. Warden: I've had to stand here for five minutes listening to this pile of crocodile shit spew some cockamamie bullshit out his mouth. Come on, let's go. Clerk: Just trying to help ya out. Hey, even though you're a complete ass... good luck anyway. [i] Back in the PaddyWagon. [/i] Driver: What's on your mind man? You haven't said a word for miles. Warden: Oh, I was just thinking about what that punk clerk was saying back there. Driver: What was he saying? Warden: Oh, that's right... you didn't hear. You were in the john pinching a loaf... I forgot. Driver: Yeah, I missed it. So what happened. Warden: Oh, he was just commenting on the fact that LeBlanc has never spoken. Same old shit everyone else says. Driver: Well, maybe they're right. Look at all the superstars in wrestling. Some of them have a workrate in the ring that absolutely suicks... BUT, they can work a mic. That seems to be what the fans want most. Warden: Well, he ain't gonna talk to us. I sure as hell can't get it out of him. Hmmm... maybe if we talk to OG. He's a little more receptive. Then, maybe he can get LeBlanc to come out of his shell a little bit. Driver: Yeah, LeBlanc's just always so damn bitter at the world. He acts like eeryone's out to get him. If he would just let his guard down every once in a while... then maybe he show some sort of personality. Warden: I hate to admit it, but I'm beginning to think you may be right. I'm gonna talk to OG when we get to the arena tonight. [i] Warden turns and stares out the window... lost in thought. Scene fades.[/i]
