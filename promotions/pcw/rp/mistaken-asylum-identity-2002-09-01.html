Mistaken (asylum) identity

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st of September 2002 (A sheer face of cold, yellowed stone. Its ominous visage freckled with thousands of small barred windows. It truly was the only justified way of describing the place.) (The asylum was huge, even Pharaoh had to admit that. The building was so colossal that it stood out like a pinnacle against the white cliffs surrounding it. Pharaoh and Isiy had travelled to the place because Titu had heard rumours…) (Rumours that involving a certain opponent of his. He’d heard the gossip, and now he wanted the answers. They stood and looked forward, the huge bronze door, blackened with age, was bolted from the inside; or so it would seem since there wasn’t even a keyhole on the exterior. Pharaoh set off up the gravel path towards it, the crunch of the tiny stones under his footing.) Isiy: “I don’t know why we’re even bothering.” (She stopped in mid-step and heaved a long sigh.) Isiy: “He won’t even be here, Titu!” (Pharaoh smiled grimly.) The Pharaoh: “I’m telling you, upon Horus’ good name, we’ll find him here, my love! If I’m wrong…then I’ll eat my…” (He peered about him, wondering what he could possibly ‘eat’ to satisfy his own certainty. Slowly his eyes trailed down his chest.) The Pharaoh: “I’ll eat my bloody loincloth!” (Isiy smiled, but it wasn’t a pleasant grin whatsoever.) Isiy: “I’m not arguing anymore, Titu. If you think he’s here then he must be, mustn’t he? So go on…go right ahead and see if you find the japo-american psychopath inside.” (She looked at the Asylum and began to consider her words.) Isiy: “Well maybe you will find a japo-american psychopath in there, but it won’t be him.” (Pharaoh ceased his movement, the crunch of gravel echoing about the cliff faces of the secluded area as he came to a stop. He wiped a tiny bead of sweat from his brow and shook his head.) The Pharaoh: “I clearly saw it, Isiy, with my own two eyes! I read the damned sign and it clearly stated: ‘Miyagawa – Asylum’.” (He turned on his heels and set off walking again. Isiy jogged to catch up to him.) Isiy: “Don’t you get it?” (She laughed mockingly.) Isiy: “Don’t you get why it said that?” (Titu paid her no attention.)Isiy: “Miyagawa is in a stable called Asylum; he isn’t actually locked up in a nuthouse! Though I dare say he should be.” (Pharaoh waved a hand as though deflecting her words from entering his ears.) The Pharaoh: “Then why exactly are the names of his other stable mates not written next to his?” (Isiy stared at him blankly. A smile grew upon his mouth.) The Pharaoh: “Exactly.” (She sighed. There really was no use in trying to outdo him.) Isiy: “Fine, let go. Come on…” (She speeded up.) Isiy: “The sooner we get there the sooner we leave.” (Pharaoh jogged to keep up to his stubborn wife.) The Pharaoh: “If I’m to defeat this man then I must see him in his home environment--…” Isiy: “So why don’t you just go to Anaheim, then? That’s where he lives!” (Titu chuckled dismissively.) The Pharaoh: “Isiy, we have a home in Anaheim! If he resided near us then I’d know. I for one haven’t seen him around our fair town at all…” (Isiy’s brow furrowed.) Isiy: “Erm, yes you have. Remember, you saw him at the hypermarket a few days ago.” (Pharaoh strained to remember.) The Pharaoh: “Ah, yes…well…the Gods know as well as I do that he could have been institutionalised but days ago! Now, come on; we’re almost there!” (As the couple came closer to the huge door, Titu extended a hand and knocked thrice upon its dark bronze exterior. Moments passed before a tiny slot slid open and pair of tiny eyes gleamed out into the beyond.) Man: “Yeth? Can I help you with thomthing?” (Pharaoh smiled patiently.) The Pharaoh: “Yes, my good man; I was wondering if I could possibly visit a patient. He goes by the name of--…” Man: “Well I don’t know, thir. The thing ith, we have a lot of inthane people inthide the athylum – the man rolled his eyes – “and the public aren’t uthually aloud to enter.” (The man hesitated.) Man: “But I thuppothe I could athk the good Doctor if you’re aloud to enter. Hmm…give me a thecond will you?” The Pharaoh: “But of course…” (Pharaoh leaned towards Isiy.) The Pharaoh: “Excellent! We’re in!” (He grinned smugly as the shuffle of feet slowly faded away. Minutes later the slow stride of the feet returned to the airwaves signalling that the man were reproaching.) Man: “The good doctor thays you can enter under one condition!” (Titu narrowed his eyes.) The Pharaoh: “What’s that?” (The man behind the door slowed his voice almost to a whisper.) Man: “You must never tell anyone you came here today, thir. Remember thothe wordth!” (Pharaoh’s gaze fell on Isiy’s. She gave him a strange glare.) The Pharaoh: “Well…O…k…” (The man’s voice became quite cheerful again.) Man: “Alrighty, thir! Come in!” (The sound of a heavy bolt grinding against something metallic echoed through the valley. The door creaked open to reveal a small, hunchbacked man, his features not all in the right places, looking up at the Egyptian couple. He smiled cheerfully.) Man: “Hello, thir. My name ith Igor, and I’d like to welcome you both to the Shadowmoore Athylum.” (The man extended a hand. Reluctantly Pharaoh shook it and forced a smile.) The Pharaoh: “It’s a…pleasure…to finally be here, Mr. Igor.” (Igor shuffled off down the hallway, his arm beckoning the two to follow. Isiy hesitated at first but the blank expression Pharaoh gave her was enough to compel the Egyptian beauty to pursue the strange man.) Igor: Tho who wath it you withhed to thee, thir? (Pharaoh found that keeping up with Igor was tougher than it looked.) The Pharaoh: “He goes by the name of Miyagawa…” (Igor froze.) Igor: Miyagawa, you thay?! (Xansiv nodded.) The Pharaoh: “That’s right, Igor.” (Igor shuddered. He then took a deep breath of air before continuing down the winding hallway.) Igor: “Ath you thay, thir…ath you thay…” (Titu turned back to Isiy and beamed a wide grin.) The Pharaoh: “I told you we’d find him here, didn’t I?” (Pharaoh jogged up ahead in order to keep up with the strange-looking little man. Isiy sighed silently to herself.) Isiy: “I have a bad feeling about this.” (Igor continued to lead the way until abruptly he came to a stop. Titu looked around his surroundings and found that he and Isiy were confined within a small white room. The walls were padded, and the only items that bore any resemblance to something you’d find outside the asylum’s walls were a circular wooden table and a set of three stubby-legged chairs.) The Pharaoh: “Where are we?” (Igor grinned.) Igor: “Thith ith what we call the ‘talkative room’, thir…” The Pharaoh: “Ah, I…see…” Igor: “In here the patientth get a chance to talk with their vithitorth. It’th uthually a nice environment for them, you know?” (Igor turned his attention to the doorway they entered the ‘talkative room’ via.) Igor: “Now if you’ll excuthe me, I’ll go and prepare …Miyagawa for you…” (Isiy noticed the shudder this time.) Isiy: “Is there anything wrong, Igor?” (Igor waved a hand dismissively.) Igor: “Oh, no, mithtreth. Nothing to worry about!” (He scuttled out of the area.) Isiy: “He seemed a bit…on the edge when talking about this Miyagawa character, don’t you think?” (Her voice indicated that she required some form response from her husband.) The Pharaoh: “Well, I don’t know, do I? Miyagawa’s a nasty piece of work when you get on his bad side and I shouldn’t expect being locked away here would ease stress.” (Isiy took note of the ‘pleasant’ music playing over the asylum’s loud speakers.) Isiy: “Yes…point taken…” (Five minutes or so passed before a tall man wearing a white lab coat entered the room. In his mouth sat a long black pipe, grey smoke ever rising from its interior. The man’s grey, matted hair was tied back in a short ponytail, and both his hands were concealed in his trouser pockets. Pharaoh saw, after looking for a moment, that the man did indeed have both a blue and a green eye and that he hadn’t shaven in some time.) Man: “Hello, my friends. I’m Doctor Shadowmoore; it’s a pleasure to finally have someone back around the asylum. The patients haven’t had many visitors as of late, especially Miyagawa.” (Pharaoh rose from his seat.) The Pharaoh: “Pleased to meet you, Doctor. This is my lovely wife, Isiy.” (The Doc approached and shook both the Egyptian’s hands.) Dr Shadowmoore: “Igor should be back in a few minutes with Miyagawa. Can I ask why you want to see him? Only, I thought all the victim’s families had already visited and given the beast their opinions on his vile acts. Please, I beg of you to tell me you aren’t here to cause trouble...” (Pharaoh’s brow wrinkled.) The Pharaoh: “No? And what do you mean…victim’s families?” (There was a bellow from outside the room.) Voice of Igor: “Calm yourself, Miyagawa! Calm yourself!” (There was a thud and Igor came flying through the doorway! Dr Shadowmoore sprung into action and caught the monstrous man’s arm before it connected with Titu’s face, piercing its skin with a needle.) Dr Shadowmoore: “Do not worry, folks! This will simply calm him down!” (Igor got to his feet, rubbed his forehead, and brushed himself down.) Isiy: “Oh…my…Gods!” (Xansiv looked around frantically.) The Pharaoh: “Godsdamn! That isn’t Miyagawa!” (Igor chuckled.) Igor: “Oh it ith, you can alwayth tell by the way he throwth you --…” The Pharaoh: “No! I mean this ma--…” – he rethought his words – “this thing isn’t whom I came to see! I came to see Alexander Miyagawa!” (Dr Shadowmoore dropped from the beast’s shoulders as it began to wobble wearily from side to side.) Shadowmoore: “But this is Brutus ‘The Brute’ Miyagawa!” The Pharaoh: “I can see that! Come on Isiy, we’re getting out of here!” Igor: “I’ll thow you to the door--…” The Pharaoh: “No thank you, Igor; we know the way!” (Titu shoved Isiy out of Brutus’ reach before ducking a slow punch as the man fell to the padded ground, unconscious.) Dr Shadowmoore: “Hmm – he stroked his chin – maybe that dose was a bit too strong?” Igor: “Oh don’t worry about it, thir. We might actually get thome thleep tonight now.” (The two men shared a laugh before Igor’s face turned pale with fear.) Igor: “Thir! Oh my God! I actually forgot to offer thothe nice people thome of my cupcaketh!” (Shadowmoore patted his co-worker on the shoulder.) Dr Shadowmoore: “Hard lines, Igor…hard lines…” (Back in the hallway, Pharaoh ran for the door and opened it with some struggle. He beckoned for Isiy to get out and then closed it again.) The Pharaoh: “Let’s get out of here before that…thing…catches us!” (He set off running at some pace down the gravel path. Isiy sniffed and shook her head.) Isiy: “Told ya’ so! Miyagawa is in a stable called Asylum! (She laughed mockingly.) Isiy: “Sometimes I wonder about you!” (Pharaoh had enough to think about without having to contemplate being caught by the monstrous man from back inside the asylum. The two matches he was scheduled for this week prayed on his mind for one thing! The first was against Duke Williams, a man he’d never beaten in singles action before, and the second was against the japo-american he thought he’d find here today, Miyagawa. He frowned at Isiy’s words and carried on running.) The Pharaoh: “Bah!”
