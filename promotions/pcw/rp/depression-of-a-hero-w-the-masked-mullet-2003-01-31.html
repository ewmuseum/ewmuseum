Depression of a Hero w/ The Masked Mullet

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Depression of a Hero w/ The Masked Mullet Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 1-31-2003 ..::/::...Our story begins...badly. I mean REALLY BADLY. Like finding Medusa in your bed and a used condom right next to her bad. If you haven't gotten it...things are VERY bad for PCW's very own superhero, Rent-A-Hero. Ever since his auspicious return...he has not only lost EVERY match he's been involved in, but he's also managed to draw the ire of being quite the 'choke artist' yet one more time. Bested by his rival, Totally Tubular Tommy, and beaten in the Continental title contest....crushing his hopes of ever gaining a second Continetal title reign, our hero just sits lacksidasically on the black leather couch of his humble apartment....a shell of a better man. With his Batman mask adorned and his ripped body only brandishing a bear of Underdog boxers and tube socks, our hero looks completely out of his persona. The big screen Sony Vaio TV across from him is blasting the sounds of wild manga, lasers streaking across the screen...and sitting on the glass yin-yang table...is none other than our hero's depression food....housed in a white carton...our hero only raises up from his weary position to consume a few spoonfuls of....MOO-SHU!!! MY GOD RENT-A-HERO...IS EVERYTHING THAT BAD?!..::/::...Rent-A-Hero:'OH QUIET YOU!!'..::/::..Grabbing a book from the coffee table in front of him, our hero hurls it with great force off screen...WALLOPING me in the head as I narrate my own exclamation of pain..::/::..Narrator:'OUCH!!'Rent-A-Hero:'What am I...what have I become? Not only am I a loser. Not only am I a choke artist...but the fans have....have...forgotten about me. I'm just not the hero I used to be. Alas, I'm too full of pride to stop trying...but I'm to weak to give it any more effort. Only moo-shu will console me now...' ..::/::..Our hero dips his sppon into the carton, devouring some of his comfort food desperately in his weakened state when suddenly, loud knocking can be heard upon his door...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'NOBODY'S HOME, CITIZEN!!'Voice:'THEN WHY'S SOMEONE SPEAKIN' TO ME?!'Rent-A-Hero:'Ummm...welll....yea.....ARRRRRRRRRGH!!! I'm coming...can't they just leave a hero be whenhe;s devouring moo-shu.'..::/::..Our hero uproots himself from the couch, an imprint indented upon the couch from his many hours of sitting sedately on the couch. Our hero doesn't stride to answer as he usually would, instead, he drags his feet with his hanging low, clutching his carton of moo-shu to heart as he unlocks the door. He open it wide...to reveal none other than....THE MASKED MULLET!!!! The masked redneck of RUSH fame stands tall before our hero's door, a broad smile transforming to a concerned frown, TMM exclaiming whilest in the hallway..::/::.. TMM:'WHAT IN THE BLUE HELL IS WRONG WITH YOU?! Ya look like yer strung out on that 'crank' or somethin'!'Rent-A-Hero:'Please...don't kick me while I'm down my raging redneck compatriot...I'm just having quite the bad month right now.' TMM:'It looks like you've been kicked enough I gotta say pardner.'..::/::..Our hero just drags his way back to the couch, TMM closing our hero's door behind him as he follows him over to the couch. TMM becomes even more concerned upon seeing our hero curl back into his pitiful ball on the couch, the mulleted one sitting on the cocuh as he exclaims..::/::.. TMM:'COME ON RENT...no time for this foolishness hoss. I know ya've been feelin' pretty bad lately with the losing streak and all lately. I know your feelin' like a fool, a blip, a disaster, a choke artist, a loooooo...'Rent-A-Hero:'Are you done?!' TMM:'Well, I had about five or six more good adject...adja....*struggling with the pronunciation*...adjametives!! Yeah, that's it. Anyways, you gotta get yerself off this couch and head out, show yer face in the world again. Yer lookin' pitiful!' ..::/::..Our hero doesn't even care to repsond, just blankly staring at TMM before dipping his spoon into the moo-shu carton, preparing to spoon out more of the Oriental delicacy before TMM snatches the moo-shu from our hero's hands, yelling in annoyance..::/::.. TMM:'PUT THIS FRICKIN MOO-SHU DOWN FER JUST FIVE MINUTES HERE!!' ..::/::..Our hero's eyes squint up as he turns the spoon with the scoop end up, pointing it at TMM in a threatening fashion as he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Give...back...the moo-shu...' TMM:'No. It's warped yer mind. Reminds me of Uncle Blowjob....got addicted to blowjobs....killed himself when he got one blowjob too many from a drag queen.'Rent-A-Hero:'And that relates to my moo-shu...how?' TMM:'Good point. When I figure it out, I'll let ya kno. Anyways, NO MOO-SHU!!'Rent-A-Hero:'DEATH TO YOU!!'..::/::..Our hero leaps atop of TMM, the two falling off the couch and rolling about on the floor, our hero forcing the spoon towards TMM's face as the mulleted one tries to force his hand back, holding the moo-shu in the other. Ironically, the anime playing beside them is mimicking the mirror image of their tussle, the ninja being pinned down by a crazed Mongol in the anime. TMM forces his foot into RAH's chest and kickflips him over, our hero down on his back as TMM gets up and brushes himself off...::/::.. TMM:'Pardner, calm down.' Rent-A-Hero:'That's it...PREPARE TO GET SLAPPERIZED!!!' ..::/::...Our hero does away with the spoon, leaping high into the air before landing right next to TMM, throwing his hands about in a windmill fashion and slapping TMM weakly, barely even tickling TMM in the process as our hero slowly tires himself out. He continues slapping and slapping until finally, he falls to his knees, at the ankles of TMM, crying....broken down in front of his ew masked friend...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'WHAT HAS HAPPENED TO ME?! I USED TO HAVE FORTUNE, FAME., FANS...AND GUMMI BEARS!! Now all I've got is empty moo-shu cartons and people asking me how they can get Hurricane's autograph. Now...I'm fighting you over moo-shu. Next thing you know...I'LL BE ON CELEBRITY BOXING!!!' ..::/::..Our hero can't help but cry about that fate, feeling like an utter shell of what he used to be capable of. TMM angrily grabs Rent-A-Hero by the arm, jerking him up to his feet and slapping him across the face on each cheek, shaking him as he exclaims..::/::.. TMM:'DAMN IT HERO, your a HERO, not some guy who plays one on TV! Stop all this cryin' and get yerself ta-gether! The Rent-A-Hero I knew wouldn't be crying over these things! The Rent-A-Hero I knew would have a smile, get back at the evil villain....and come with me on a double date tonight, PLLLLLLLLLEASE!!' Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY ULTERIOR MOTIVES, does it LOOK like I'm in any condition to head out on a date. My Rent-A-Charms have fizzeled along with my powers Mullet...I'm nothing. I'm about as much fo a hero as Michael Jackson is black.'...::/::..Our hero trails back over to the couch, slumping back down upon it as he holds his head in sorrow, TMM still trying to motivate him..::/::.. TMM:'COME ON MAN...your a guy the ladies like, and yer charm's not lost! Mabe this'll be whatcha need to get yerself motivated again! Besides...if I can't get a friend to go with her friend, she won't go...and that means ol' Mullet won't get to...you know...' Rent-A-Hero:'MULLET...I can't believe your gonna let her play with your first controller and you don't even KNOW her that well yet. That's just SHAMELESS. Next thing you know, you'll be telling me you wanna swap memory card data!'TMM:'I guess ya don't know. But man come on...ya gotta go. If not for you, fer me?' Rent-A-Hero:'No.' TMM:'A copy of Kingdom Hearts?' Rent-A-Hero:'No.' TMM:'Some 'Mullet His Way' hair spray?' Rent-A-Hero:'No.'TMM:'Fine....a pack of all clear Gummi Bears AND some Trolli Frogs.' Rent-A-Hero:'I'll be dressed in twenty minutes...but I still don't like this.' ..::/::..Our hero raises up from the couch, heading into his bedroom as TMM sits upon the couch, shaking his head and saying to himself..::/::.. TMM:'Pfft...losing streak....if he had grew a mullet, maybe he wouldn't be having this problem.'..::/::..The scene fades, the plans for a date set as the depressed Rent-A-Hero prepares to drown his sorrows in a double date alongside his good friend TMM. Will it indeed be what he needs? WILL THIS DATE BE AN UTTER DISASTER?! HOW DOES TMM GET HIS MULLET SO SHEENY AND SOFT?!
