The Continuing Story of Skylar Green and The Pharaoh

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26th, 2003 'So you want to know how I got involved with The Pharaoh? It seems I was telling this story only last month, but very well. There's more to tell now. First, a quick recap of everything up to the last pay-per-view, which was Supremacy.' Last month before PCW Supremacy, I did The Pharaoh Titu Xansiv a favor after Spring Fling. Coming off a rather uneventful and somewhat of a letdown feud with Chet King, The Pharaoh was in the World Title division rankings but didn't exactly have the momentum that he most surely would have wanted. Totally Tubular Tommy was the PCW World Champion after defeating and injuring Venneman Pendragon, and he frankly had nothing to do. The Pharaoh hadn't solidified his contendership yet, Violence Jack was the Continental Champion after he got himself a DQ victory by having Dorn Tracer and Titu run out and hit him with chairs (which I'm sure that they were all too happy to oblige) and so he wasn't going to be thrown at Mr. Thomas Gordo, and as for myself, I was ready to take the next step but since I had technically lost at Spring Fling I wasn't about to challenge the World Title right away. Especially since all of Tommy's cheating tactics against me to retain the PCW Continental Title when I faced him as the challenger had run their course, and he couldn't use any of his old tricks to beat me. So, I took the initiative and challenged The Pharaoh to a match to determine the #1 Contendership. But not a single match, a series of matches which tested our abilities in different singles and tag team formats. By the end of it, I came out ahead. The first match between us was in which The Pharaoh and I traded styles. He mainly used strikes, while I utilized a high-flying offense (I'm still more agile than I look; The Pharaoh was more out of his element than I was). In that match, I won by disqualification by Totally Tubular Tommy interfering and hitting me with a chair. The second match, a tag match, involved two other wrestlers. I chose my own partner, James Edwards. I detected some potential in the rookie and decided to ask him to be my partner for the match. 'Lunar' was eager for the opportunity, since he had only one other match under his belt, which was a loss in a match that featured the then seemingly-unbeatable Matt Jackson. In that tag team match, it was actually James Edwards who won for our team, pinning The Pharaoh with a School Boy rollup. Yes, almost have to want to get pinned with that for it to work, but Edwards picked up the win for our team. I was ahead 2-0; and James 'Lunar' Edwards arguably got the most important pinfall of his career. That gave him the motivation to continue working and to keep trying his hardest, even if he didn't succeed the first time. It worked for him, despite losing three different matches in a row to Matt Jackson, Edwards faced Jackson in singles competition and defeated him for the Rising Star Title. He makes a better champion anyway; Jackson constantly refers to it as the 'Raising Star' Title. The third match, a submission match between the two of us (myself and Pharaoh) started out being just the two of us. Tommy was the guest referee, which was the bookers' way of keeping him in our sights that he couldn't interfere. Didn't work, however, as Pharaoh won after Tommy put a chair in front of The Pharaoh as I was executing a strike. The Pharaoh was hit with a chair, and Tommy thought he got one over on us. Then we revealed that if Tommy ruined the match, he'd have to fight myself and Pharaoh in separate submission matches. Pharaoh he lost to Totally Tubular Tommy in a No DQ fall thanks to rampant interference on the behalf of Tommy by his brother Heavy Metal Harry; and then I beat Tommy by taking advantage of the ensuing Cowabunga Connection (the name of Tommy and Harry's old tag team before Harry suffered an injury -- one that he reports differently than the head injury that he has listed with PCW) and The Pharaoh and Isiy's wife. That put me ahead of Pharaoh 3-1. A tornado tag team match followed in which I defeated The Pharaoh and Chet King (who weren't exactly in the habit of working well together) with my partner Miss Moonlight. We fared better in a team effort than the other two, who still hated each other even though they weren't on each other's hit lists at the time. It's easy to work with someone when you don't go out every night with the intend of making enemies. Oh well. Some people learn that, some don't. Moonlight beat Chet King to take the win for our team, and I was then ahead by a margin of 4-1 over The Pharaoh. By then, there were some issues brewing between The Pharaoh and Dorn Tracer, the more active members of the Triumvirate (Violence Jack was never quite his same old self after I mentally, physically, and emotionally destroyed him), along with the fact that thanks to the happenings in the 3-match submission series that Totally Tubular Tommy was starting to be more of a problem to The Pharaoh, that a match was signed. It was The Pharaoh and Isiy against Cowabunga Connection, which would reform for one night only. Due to what happened before the matches were scheduled to air, it became myself and The Pharaoh facing Totally Tubular Tommy and Dorn Tracer. Isiy Xansiv and Heavy Metal Harry were then pitted against each other in a singles match. Cowabunga Connection, though the odd ones out in the larger feud between the Triumvirate and myself, both got wins that night with pinfalls over their opponents. In that case, it could be likened to the Spring Fling of the previous year. Boda was the evil one, Miyagawa was the good one, and Chris Chase was just the guy in the middle. But by being the guy in the middle, he ended up winning the PCW World Title. Boda and Miyagawa were so focused on each other that Chris Chase snuck around them and claimed the title. I think Tommy realized that and was hoping for the same. He could defend his World Title while not doing very much. But The Pharaoh is a very vindictive and paranoid person. Tommy was dragged farther in than he wanted to be. In the end, it came down to who wanted to win more. The Supremacy main event; myself, The Pharaoh, and Totally Tubular Tommy as the PCW World Champion. Tommy had the champion's advantage: we had to beat someone. He didn't have to beat anyone. Then again, it was a triple threat match. While The Pharaoh and I wanted to wrestle (I always want to wrestle anyway), Tommy wanted to sit back and let us beat each other. We didn't let him do that, so all of us were active in the match. By the final bell, however, I myself made Totally Tubular Tommy pass out from the pain of my finishing submission. The Pharaoh didn't lose, he didn't win. He was just there. That leads to now. Because of the position that Pharaoh was in by the end of the last pay-per-view, that, to him, is enough to gain a rematch anytime he wants. He's either refused or failed to earn a rematch the right way, he needs to get it by underhanded methods. He must've seen me walking around with the girls, and of course I penned a Thank You speech only hours after winning the PCW World Title. He must've seen that, put two and two together, and then he had his way to get the title: he would blackmail me into it using neutral parties that he would bring into the conflict. It was a despicable act, not just to me, but to my partner for the tag match before which he revealed his plans, Brianna Washington. She helped me to formulate a plan to deny The Pharaoh the PCW World Title, since we both agreed that he needed to win it the right way. Dorn Tracer was getting sick of The Pharaoh's continual failures and wanting to separate himself from him. This has led to a boiling point, in which Dorn stole the #1 contendership from The Pharaoh, and then wrestled a 20 minute match against him on this past Turmoil. The Pharaoh lost twice against Dorn Tracer now, and 'his' #1 contendership has been usurped by an ally. A former ally at this point, I suppose. The Pharaoh is losing the children now because of the contract he signed. If I agree to lose the PCW World Title to him on June 30th, he'll give the girls back. We both already signed to this, and the girls are as good as being out of his 'care', though he's left them at the daycare and not picked them up since this whole thing began. This leads us to Fusion and then Turmoil. This Friday, tomorrow even, I am scheduled to face Dorn Tracer. The Pharaoh will want to stack things in his favor to keep me from losing, and Dorn from winning. He wants the World Title and his agreement is with me. I had an official legal document drawn up and Pharaoh knows that I'll adher to the conditions laid out in the contract. In his mind, he just needs to make sure that I keep the PCW World Title for him to claim. That and he definitely wouldn't want Dorn to win the title before he does. That would make his ego explode. This is where we stand now. Dorn Tracer is my opponent for Fusion, and for Turmoil as well, along with his apparent former comrade-in-arms, The Pharaoh, and anyone whom they can convince to join their team. I'll guarantee that I'll fight Dorn tooth-and-nail to keep this title, whether or not Pharaoh has anything to say or do with it. And I won't take the 6-man tag with Brianna Washington and this Steel Masked Man lightly as well. To everyone I am to face for the coming week and past that, be aware that I'm no less driven to succeed since winning this World Title. In fact, I am more so. I've got three good reasons to win. If you don't believe me, ask them.
