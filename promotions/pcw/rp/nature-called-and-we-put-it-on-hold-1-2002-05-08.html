Nature Called, and We Put It On Hold #1

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/08/02 The camera fades in… The Chef, Bertha, and Manuel are all shown standing by a small stream of water in what seems to be a mountainous region in France called…ah who the hell cares; it’s in France folks. The Chef is wearing a chef’s outfit, green in color, with the Perrier logo in the middle. Manuel is styling some Perrier Kids clothing, and Bertha is strapped into some giant novelty sized Perrier can. Bertha: This f*cking c*cksucking mother f*cking pansy @$$ suite is ho @$$ grabbing f*cking itching my butt fagging giggy, skank @$$!!! The Chef: Quiet B, or I’ll remove you from this commercial and then I’ll wear the can! Manuel, hearing the Chef’s threats, kicks the Culinary Conquistador in the shin. Manuel: Take dis! The Chef: OWWW!!! MY ALLAH THAT STINGS!!! The Chef hops around the area, holding his right leg up. Bertha and Manuel high five each other. Cameraman: Can we get things rolling here? We’ve got an ad with Gerard Depardieu in thirty minutes. The Chef: (Still hopping around) You are still running commercials with that old hack? His last movie was “Bogus” with Whoopi Goldberg for crying out to Allah!!! Bertha: I f*cking dig the b*tch @$$ cow f*cking Whoopster, f*cko! The Chef sighs. Cameraman: That’s great, but can we get rolling!?!? The Chef: Sure thing, once I have a doctor check out this bruise on my shin! Bertha: That ain’t no dick @$$ed f*cking bruise!!! The Chef: Oh yeah!?!? Will that little infidel over there is wearing clodhoppers you insolent cow!!! Bertha: F*cking cow am I!?!? Well f*ck on this f*ck faced mother f*cker!!! Bertha takes a swing at the Chef, who ducks, and rifles a quick kick to the groin of Bertha’s, who falls over onto a rocks, and because of her cylinder shaped can suit, she begins to rolls down the stream! Bertha: F*cking AAAAHHHHHH!!! The Chef: Bertha! I didn’t mean it! It was self-defense! The Chef takes off and chases after Bertha, as she rolls down the steep hill, hit a few jagged rocks along the way to the bottom of the stream. Bertha: F*cking OW! F*cking sunnava!!! F*cking hey! F*cking da-OUCH!!! F*cking hey now! Bertha looks up and sees A WATERFALL in the near vicinity of her rolling path. Bertha: F*cking @$$ humping b*tch nuggeting mother @$$ f*cking you mother f*cking father in the shower queer as f*ck @$$ b*tched fairy ho skank, A$$ CUNT MOTHA F*CKAAAAAAAAAAA!!! The Chef scampers down the hill trying to reach his clone girlfriend. He sees the edge of the cliff with the waterfall overlooking it from a distance, and sees that Bertha is heading right to it!!! The Chef: Allah protect her… Bertha: F*CKING SHHHHHHHHHHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIITTTTTTTTTTTT!!! Bertha rolls over the edge of the cliff and falls Down… Down… Down… Down… Down… Into the water…SPLASH!!! A mushroom shaped tidal wave bursts forth from the pool beneath the waterfall as Bertha and her can make contact with the water. Manuel runs up to the Chef and kicks him in the shin again. The Chef: OWWW!!! Manuel: Mommy is in some deep doo doo sh*t cause of you Chef… The Chef: That still doesn’t give you the right to use a four-letter word like…the “S” word! Manuel: Well Clone mommy says it all the time. The Chef: Well Clone mommy has serious mental problems Manuel! Manuel, saddened by the Chef’s animosity, frowns and begins to sob uncontrollably. The Chef tries to comfort him by kneeling down and hugging him. Manuel though uses the Chef’s vulnerability against, swiftly kicking him in the shin again. The Chef: DANGIT MANUEL!!! The Chef hops around, clutching his right leg again. Manuel: We’ve got to find mommy, Chef… The Chef: I know little guy, it looks like we are about to venture into the French wilderness. Manuel: Yay, a camping trip! The Chef: Not exactly Manuel. Manuel begins to frown. The Chef: We will have to use our animal instincts to venture into the savage zone of nature, and rescue the probably hysterical Bertha before she gives up on humans and begins mating with bears! A tear falls from Manuel’s left eye. The Chef: Aw heck, let’s roast some marshmallows!!! Manuel: Sweet! I love you Chef!!! The Chef: And Allah loves you Manuel, but he’d love you better if you converted…hmmmm? Manuel: Sorry Chef, I’m an Episcopalian ‘till I die… The Chef sighs, but thinks to himself. The Chef: HeHe, when he sees that it is I that controls the graham cracker and chocolate bar supply, he will sacrifice his faith for S’MORES!!! MUHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! The two Alouette men, and I use that term loosely, walk back up the hill and begin to prepare for an expedition to retrieve Bertha as the camera pauses for the narrator. Narrator: Will The Chef and Manuel survive the French wilderness and get back in time to shot a commercial before Gerard Depardieu arrives? Or will everything just suck in the next promo? We’ll find out…SAME CHEF TIME…SAME CHEF CHANNEL!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! The still image disappears as the camera fades to black.
