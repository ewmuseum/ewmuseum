If Operation One fails... try the the second one!

Date: not entered There’s no humanly possible way to deny it… no scientifically way to explore it… and no means of which to obtain any data on it. The item that is being spoken about at this moment in time… is the very “Neo Void” that our own apparent God, ‘Akiyama’ will banish seventy-five percent of his opponents to at the end of every contest. It does make you think about what this “Neo Void” actually is… if it’s a place that you’ll see when walking to the grocery store… or maybe it’s a place that’s just like the slutty little motels that like to find their way on every Motorway Corner? The answer to that question isn’t something that I’m going to be able to give out at the moment… but for the first time, and quite possibly the only time, we are going to see two very well educated men try to find out what the “Void” is… and exactly how to survive it…. After all, the sooner people know how to survive this awesome pit, the better the rest of humanity is going to be in… ten, fifteen… maybe even one hundred years time. For those of you who bothered to watch the last instalment of PCG Action, we saw Sherwin and Moore attempt to get the female referee for the contest to give them something of an unfair advantage. It was a mission attempt that didn’t really manage to give our boys anything they wanted… they didn’t even get any free food out of it all! However, regardless of that small mission… we now move onto this secondary plan. The idealistic nature of this plan has been started… the aim is something that we all have common knowledge of now… but the method is one small piece of data that hasn’t even left the work-shop yet! The scene slowly begins to open in its usual controversially slow fashion, allowing the viewer to see insanely small parts of the scenic view as the picture reaches its full opening. At first, we’re greeted with the kind hearted viewing of a homely fire, burning brightly with its full deck of wood. The picture pulls back from the flames so that we’re introduced to something a little more soothing, that minor detail being the mantelpiece surrounding this fiery wall. The mantelpiece itself is made of the more common oak instead of pine, or anything that’s really substantially deep in value. As the entire display comes into view, sitting on the ‘top’ of the mantelpiece frame, were the replicas of NTICW Tag Team Championship Belts… either side of these belts were small 8x10 framed pictures… the left hand side containing Rich Sherwin’s image, the right hand side containing that of Ryan Moore. Above this small display of their only significant victory within the walls of “New True Internet Championship Wrestling”, we see a large ‘Platinum Record’ display… something that wouldn’t look all too normal in the confinements of the average mans house, but something that compliments any house that homes two of the hottest Tag Team talents this side of the Atlantic Ocean. The scenic view slowly fades out, another image slowly coming into play along with the sounds of ‘Suteki Da Ne’ in its orchestral form playing deeply within the background. Two blue mats are positioned on the floor, our two heros standing high on them, looking across the room at one another with their typical friendly glare. The two men allowed the sounds of the music to swing gently around their bodies, opening up the senses of listening to the soundtrack of “Final Fantasy X”. “Hmmm… Ryan, this music has to be some of the smoothest stuff that I have listened to since that ‘Final Fantasy VII:Reunion’ CD found its way into the CD Player across the room… seriously, I do wonder why we’re never asked to record music for video games of the sort… we’ve already proven to ‘The Heartbreakers’ that our voices are second to none… second to none indeed.”, Sherwin proclaimed, sounding a little disturbed at the fact that both he and Ryan had never been contacted about recording the tracks for one of the many ‘Final Fantasy’ games around.. or any game for that matter. Speaking of which, that ‘Westlife’ Trivia game didn’t even feature any music from PCG… not so much of a missing concept since the game was about those Irish folk, but the concept for them stayed the same… ‘If we’re not making music for you… then we’re not dang happy about anything.’ Ryan spent a few moments to think about an answer that would not only be suitable to the statement, but would allow Sherwin to get back to the task that was originally on hand. “… Hmm… Sherwin, you know, have you ever thought about the fact that both you and I may be… well, how am I going to be able to put this without damaging their reputation… oh well, too bad, I can’t… didn’t you ever come to the fact that we’re just too good to be featuring on their video games? I know its uncool for a couple of guys like us to not be receiving offers from all over the World to appear here.. there… and everywhere, but the fact of the matter is… we’re just tooooo… good, not sweet… just too good for them!”, Moore answered, sounding very confident in every word placed forth. Whether this was confidence that actually existed within the tomb of Moore’s brain was yet to be found… but the chances are, he only said those comments to set a certain somebody’s mind at ease, and allow the more important matters at hand to go through… just like I had stated in the small portion of text above. Rich stopped as Ryan finished saying what was needed and he walked over to the CD Player currently playing the aforementioned track. With one swift motion, he pressed the ‘Stop’ button and pressed the ‘Eject’ button to open up the CD tray. Sherwin picked up the CD and placed it in a case next to the player before closing up and walking back to the mats where Ryan was patiently standing, looking a little interested to see what kind of thing would come next in the day. Rich didn’t do anything for the better part of five seconds, but he did start the conversation going with, “… you know Ryan, that plan we tried to execute with the Woman referee yesterday… that could and should have so totally worked if we didn’t run off for recognizing us right?”, he mentioned, thinking about the Pay-Per-View match if things had gone their way. Slow counts when covered… fast counts when covering among other personal features and the ‘Tag Team Championship’ belts would be as easy as… theirs. “Well Rich… while we could debate what would have happened if we stayed around the scene of the crime when that Woman referee came to identifying us… there is a good chance that we could have had some form of violence carried out to us… or that horrible chick could have asked for something is isn’t money but costs a whole lot more…”, Ryan added, sounding awfully scared and shivering at the very thought of doing the thing that “costs a whole lot more”. Rich tried to figure out what he was talking about at first, not to be taken in the sense of him being retarded or what-not, Richie just doesn’t like to assume that those kinds of favours are the first thing to be spoken about. “Woah… there is no way that you’re talking about the Platinum Record that’s hanging above our Mantelpiece?! There is no… and by God, I mean no way, that the woman in question would have been able to get that… Hell no, that’s a piece of history right there… and by the end of this calendar year, it’ll probably be the most expensive item in the history… of the World!”, Sherwin beamed, thinking that the women would have wanted something that really did have any relevance to her. Geez, nobody would have thought it should have taken a ‘Rocket Scientist’ to remember that this Women wasn’t even sure about who these guys are… and if that was the case, how the hell would she even know about the ultimate Boy Band turned Tag Team in Wrestling History?! Ryan could only manage to look down upon his friend and chuckle to himself as the point that he was talking about didn’t hammer through the cranium that belonged to Rich. “Dude… the extra things that the Women would have wanted… they have nothing to do with the belongings that we have stored inside and around this complex. Do you even remember the way that she was looking at you with those eyes… and the way that she was going to jump down those steps… tackle your ass to the ground and try a little bit of role-reversal in the worst possible way?!”, Ryan cried out, trying to hammer out the danger that both he and Sherwin could have put themselves in should the situation have evolved further than it did on that fateful day. Sherwin looked over to Ryan, cocking his eyebrow up as if to say “Whachutalkinbout Moore?” in the same kind of way that Gary Coleman used to say to the other actors of “Different Strokes” when he played a little black kid called “Arnold”. Moore immediately began to sigh, not thinking that his friend has got the jest of the message, although that was far from the case… as Sherwin smiled, nodding at the same time… before they changed to him shaking his head, his face contorted into a look of fear as realization began to settle in. “Dude… what in Gods name did you think you were doing telling me about all of that… worthless, disgusting information?! That chick… no, I’ll rephrase that into something that everyone is going to understand without any shadows of a doubt… that woman was the most disgusting thing that I had ever come across since… since the time in twelfth grade when some real ugly guy and some real ugly thing started to make out in the canteen!”, Sherwin yelled back, the level of disgust within his voice being easily noticeable. Ryan laughed somewhat at Sherwin’s reactions to the thought of what that ugly female referee would want to do when the lights went out and the party was all but set to begin. “Rich… its better that you found out what that Woman would have wanted now, instead of finding out the kinds of things that she’d want to do when you were in another place… for example, the locker room before or after our big match with Akiyama, Politically Incorrect and ‘The Heartbreakers’”, Ryan informed his friend, trying to get him to understand that it was within the best interests of the “Teams Game Plan” if he were to find out the dirty deeds of this seedy thing before the match… instead of when he’d probably be in a more prone position afterwards. Sherwin nodded his head, just to signify that he got the point of being informed about such a gross act way before it happened, instead of leaving him to find out the hard way. The two men walked into the centre of the mats and stood about ten feet away from each other. “Hmm… you know, I really think its time we started to focus on the real reason why these mats were put out.”, Ryan suggested, looking ready to take on anything that really wanted to come his way and try being lucky. Sherwin stood still, forgetting what the point was of coming here with the mats in the first place. He dropped down to the floor and made sure that everything was in line before getting back to his feet again, this time looking all proud with himself for one reason… or maybe another. Moore looked to him with the “Cocking Eyebrow” making its second appearance today and said, “… what in the Hell made you choose to get on the floor and measure up a pair of mats? Do you really think I care about little details like that? As long as somebody isn’t going to fall on the floor and break their back after contact is made… I don’t care how inline… or out of line these things are.” Sherwin did the one thing that he could do at this point, look to Ryan once again with another confused look. He thought about what to say for a few moments, making facial expressions as if to suggest that he couldn’t bring himself to say the required detail… although, he decided to follow through with it anyway, “… Hmm… well, I was thinking that you placed these mats out for me and the chick that we met… you remember, the one that was from New Jersey calling herself Lucy… I thought these mats were placed down for me and her… to kinda do some interesting stuff.” Ryan’s faced dropped with a look of plain “stale attitude” and his head shook from side to side… in a very slow, very gentle motion. Richie smiled sheepishly and laughed nervously, scratching the back of his head upon such movement from Ryan, “… Wow, I guess I must have just forgotten what the real reason of coming down here was then… hehehe… erm, silly me?”. Ryan still didn’t look like he was in the mood to be playing around, something that Rich was either doing on the account of his recent goofing acts, or on the account that he was a little more retarded than people were made to believe. Ryan stopped shaking his head and looked to Sherwin with a facial expression that was showing a little too much frustration and not enough relaxation. The younger member of the Tag Team inhaled deeply before exhaling and explaining the purpose of the set up, “… Rich, the reason why I have decided that we should come down here…” “That’s majorly sick dude… I knew we were like best friends and everything, but I didn’t think you wanted to take a page out of the ‘Heartbreakers’ book… Sheesh, I thought you knew better than all those other people…”, Sherwin said, cutting off Ryan Moore with his super-sharp input. A decision that really wasn’t going to be the very best thing in the seven year career of the vet Sherwin. “Woah, Woah, Woah, Woah… WOAH!!!”, Ryan shouts out aloud, holding his hands up to prevent any movement from either side of the camp. He looks surprised and very annoyed as Sherwin hops off the mats, thinking that he was preventing anything of that nature from ever being able to take place in that room. Ryan looks at Sherwin all crazy like and begins to talk in a psycho rage, “WHAT THE HELL… WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT? I didn’t lay these mats out for… ewww, that kinda of stuff you sick… sick… sick… F-reak! That’s gotta be the most disgusting thing that I’ve ever come to hear about during the course of the day…” “Uhm… well, I’m sorry and all for shouting that kinda thing out, but its not as if I was going to let you say everything… these mats look like there the kinda things that I would have used if I wanted to have a good time with a chick… you know what I mean? I didn’t think… you were going to use them for anything constructive…”, Sherwin said in his defense, trying to defuse the situation a little and calm down the very shocked member of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys.’ Ryan still wasn’t looking too pleased about the way this situation had exploded from being the set-up for training into something a little more horrible… something a little more disgusting and something that he didn’t care to hear again for the rest of his life. “Rich… in my honest opinion, what you really need to do before you speak, is a little something that I would like to call, “think”… trust me, its so good when you think before you speak, because at least then, we wouldn’t have to worry about ‘escapades’ of this sort, and you’d allow me to say… that the reason why I decided to come down here in the first place, is because we need to understand what the “Neo Void” is all about, and we need to especially understand how to survive it.”, Ryan said in a voice that was relatively calm. All of the pent up emotion that had been in his face just moments before had seemingly cooled off without warning… surprisingly fast too. Sherwin nodded somewhat at the nature of the mats laid before him and walked into the middle, standing ten feet away from Moore again. “Ok… apparently, the ‘Neo Void’ is supposed to be something of a dimension where people are sent to… and a lot of the guys never even make it back to PCW again… I’m not too sure if its something that we’re going to be able to experience for ourselves… but oh well, I guess the only thing to do, is to take-part in something of a home-made simulation and hope it gets us ready for Sunday Night..”, Ryan states to Rich, making sure that every single word spoken found a little home in his brain. Sherwin nodded his head somewhat, getting ready for this little “home-made” experiment to get into action. Ryan stood ready for the first action, but stopped to say a few words to his friend, “Ok Rich… apparently, if we throw ourselves back onto this mat…we should visit the “Void” that everyone has been saying Akiyama will send us to on Sunday.” Sherwin and Moore throw themselves back onto the mats at quite a violent pace, hitting the padded surface. The two men were able to get to their feet without too much hassle… but it looks as though the movement did have some kind of affect on the mental state of both characters. Sherwin looked over to Ryan and said, “… Is this kind of treatment really going to help us do anything on Sunday Night? I mean… can’t you look back to last month on Pay-Per-View when we were going really well until those… Cowabunga Connection jokes eliminated us. Sheesh, but just think… they’re not even going to be in that match on Sunday Night!” Ryan looked back to his best-friend, even looking a little shaken himself. He tried to answer the question that was posed to him by Rich, but fell backwards doing so. Thinking that Moore had dropped back again to try and survive the supposed “Home Made Neo Void” Trip, Sherwin threw himself back again, this time… staying on the floor and looking into the ceiling. The scene slowly begins to fade into the confinements of darkness, the people who bothered to watch this entire segment… left to wonder if both Sherwin and Moore will be able to really get through the “Neo Void Banishment” that Akiyama will more than likely have in store for the two of them on Sunday Night at the “Wrestle Vessel.” With the emotions of all the teams involved still running high… its not a question of whether PCG will be able to win the big one or not… its all down to “Do PCG actually have a chance of walking away the PCW World Tag Team Champions?!” Not even I know the answer to that question… but stay tuned and a little more to the answer may be revealed to you!
