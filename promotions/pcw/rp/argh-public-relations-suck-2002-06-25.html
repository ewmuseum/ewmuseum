Argh!!! Public Relations suck!!!

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/25/2002 ~Oh what a tangled web we weave when we let our mouths overload our asses. There is a certain individual who repeatedly does this at every turn. And it is not just him doing it, it is the whole group he runs with. This man would be 'Controversial' Corey Williams and the group would be the so called Ultimate Authority in PCW. Time and time again, a member of the Ultimate Authority shoots their mouths off or takes an action against a member of the Malice Crew. This would be the unspoken deadly sin. The Malice Crew has always relied heavily on the tightness of their unit. There is not an individual within the Malice Crew, but a group of people working as one. That single reason is why the Malice Crew is the dominant stable within PCW and why Tyler Nelson is slowly starting to go bald. On Turmoil, Kano faces the Ultimate Authority with the Malice Crew teammate Duke Williams and Alexander Miyagawa. At Gold and Glory, Kano teaches Corey Williams how to respect a REAL legend.~ *The scene opens up inside a huge shopping mall in the commons area where a huge platform has been constructed with a banner across the top saying, 'Meet PCW legend, Jon Kano,' on it. On the platform itself is just a table, where Kano, decked out in his traditional Malice Crew gear, is sitting with a huge stack of 8 by 10 glossy pictures of himself to his left. A huge line of people is standing waiting to get a chance to meet their hero, Jon Kano.* Kano (speaking under his breath): Why am I here? Oh yeah, Tyler hates me so he sends me on these god-awful public relations things. *A man approaches Kano with a grin on his face.* Man: Hi, Mr. Kano, I can't tell you what a big fan I am. You are like a hero of mine. Kano: Glad to see you come out. Whats the name, pal? Man: Ross. My name is Ross. *Kano begins to sign the picture, finishes signing, and hands the picture over to the man.* Ross: Well, Jon, can I ask you a quick question? Kano: Yeah, shoot Ross. Ross: Do you really hate the Ultimate Authority that much? Kano: No. I hate them more. I hate everything they stand for. And I hate Tyler Nelson five levels above the rest. Ross: Why? Kano: The head of that group, Tyler Nelson, is out for one thing and one thing only, Tyler Nelson. He claims he is always doing what is best for PCW, but if that is true why does everything he does benefit him? Tyler is a master manipulator. He has manipulated all of these people in the Ultimate Authority, each with their own strengths and weaknesses, into doing his bidding. He promises them the world and delivers Rhode Island. On Turmoil, Duke, Miya, and I join forces to take on three of Tyler's cronies. These three actually have some talent, but think about the hardware Miya, Duke, and myself bring to the table. Miya is a former World Champ, Extreme Champ, and Cruiserweight Champ. Duke is a former Continental Champ. I am a former Rising Star Champ, Television Champ, and Continental Champ. Think about that for a second, the three of us are so vastly decorated and those three wouldn't have sh*t if they weren't Tyler's cronies. So on Turmoil, the Malice Crew and Miyagawa will be standing tall while those three will be laying on their backs wondering what the hell just happened. Ross: Alright, I'll get the line moving now. Thanks, Jon. Kano: No problem. *A man with his son come up to Kano.* Man 2: Jon, this is my son, Mikey. He is a huge fan. Kano: Hi Mikey. How old are you? Mikey: Eight. Kano: Now you don't try any of my moves on your friends, do you? Mikey: No. Kano: Good. Mikey: Mr. Kano, can I ask you a question? Kano: Yeah. Mikey: What are you going to do to Corey Williams at Gold and Glory? Kano: Simply put, I am going to kick his ass. The two of us are going to walk down my ramp, down my aisle, into my ring, and go at it. We will take each other to our limits. We are going to hurt each other. We are going to make each other bleed. We are going to put on a show like none other. We will entertain. We will raise hell. We will probably both put each other in the hospital. But the only thing that will matter, little Mikey, is that in that match, I will be the last man standing. I will go down, but I shall get right back up. I will bleed, but I will stop bleeding. Corey will go down, and he will stay down in a pool of his own blood, wondering what that enormous pain radiating throughout his body like cancer is only to know that it was the deadly duo of the Kanospear and Kanoplex that knocked him down and kept him down. And most importantly, at Gold and Glory, Corey will learn why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano gives little Mikey his autograph and the huge line continues to file past Kano.* ~Welcome to the homestrech into Gold and Glory. Kano is standing tall now, will he be at Turmoil and at Gold and Glory? Only time could tell.~
