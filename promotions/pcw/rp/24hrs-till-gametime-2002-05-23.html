24hrs till gametime....

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.23.02 [fade in] With just twenty-four hours away until we make our debuts in PCW, Ron and myself are inside our training facility at our home in Houston, Texas. I can see Ron at the bench putting the weights on to get ready and start some sets. I walk over to our stereo system and put on some Linkin Park to get us pumped. 'Hey Ron...is this music cool for you or do you want me to throw something else in the cd player?' 'Linkin Park is fine bro. Just crank it up a bit so I can at least hear it,' Ron says from a distance. 'Alright!' I turn the music up a bit and make my way over to the bench. Ron gets underneath the bar and I get behind the bench to spot him. 'Alright man....three sets of fifteen you game Ron?' 'Am I game..what kind of question is that? You know me by now I would think Mike.' 'Your right. Your always ready. So then let's go.' Ron lifts the bar off the bench as I set back and count for him. 'Come on big dawg...you got seven more lets go!' I can see the look on Ron's face that he is taking this very seriously as we always do when it comes to lifting weights and stayin in shape. Ron throws the bar back on the rack. 'Nice work bro...Only two more sets togo.' 'Yeah I gotcha Mike. I'm going to throw these last two sets up like nothing so you can get started.' 'Aight Ron, but don't hurt yourself.' Ron picks the bar back up and starts his second set. I'm looking around the room to see what I can start doin so I don't have to wait on Ron to finish his last set...he seems to be lifting the bench on his own so I don't think there is any need for me to be there. 'Woooooo! I got one more Mike, then it's your go aight.' 'Yeah that's cool, but I'm going to go ahead and start on squats real quick. I don't think you need me to spot you right?' 'I'm good bro...go ahead and do that. When I'm done here I'll goto the powercleans and start there.' 'Sounds good Ron.' I walk over to the squats and throw some weights on. I grab a towel and put it behind my neck so the bar won't leave a red mark....some people may think I'm a wuss, but hey I gotta protect my neck. I grab a weight belt and strap it on real tight. I get under the bar and start my reps. I can see Ron out the corner of my eye lifting the bar up starting his last set. I begin to talk to myself. 'Come on Mike...there ya go....five....six...seven...eight...comeon two more....nine.....ten.....' I walk back and place the bar on the hooks. While I get ready and start my next set, Ron is just finishing his last and walks towards the powerclean bar which is right across from me. 'What the hell was that Mike? Ten reps that's it.' 'Hey man....squating is a whole lot different then bench. That's why I wanted you to do more there.' 'Okay...okay, but still I know you could do more than ten man.' 'Hey you do what you want and I do what I want aight.' 'HAHA. Okay Mike, I don't want to argue with you.' 'Let me get back at these squats and then I'll holla at ya.' Ron grabs some weights for the powerclean as I get underneath the bar once again and do my second set. While I'm doin that Ron begins to talk to me. 'Hey Mike, you know we are only twenty four hours away until our debuts?' 'Man don't talk to me now hold on...' 'My bad. Didn't know you had the bar up already.' Man he knew I had it up cause he was looking at me the whole time. He can be an asshole sometimes but I know he does it just playing around. I finish my sets of squats as he gets ready to do his powercleans. 'Man if I would have lost my concentration, I could have lost control of the bar.' 'My bad Mike, I didn't know you were underneath it yet.' 'I know. To answer your question. I know that we are twenty-four hours away till we make our debuts and I can't wait.' 'Me neither. We've had a busy week this week Mike. First we get the call from PCW about us being hired. Then we drive to do the press conference, and now we are sitting here getting ready for our match tomorrow night.' 'To top that off Ron, we gotta get ready pretty soon as a matter a fact and hope on the plane togo to Atlantic City, New Jersey for Fusion.' 'So you wanna keep working out or what. I mean we only did one thing a piece Mike?' 'Yeah I know. Tell you what. You do that, I'll do the bench and then we need to do some ring work before we get ready.' 'Let's do this.' Hours later we cut back and see Mike and Ron on their ring apron inside their training facility. 'I know tomorrow night is going to be a night that the CC will never forget,' Ron stated. 'It will be a night that the fans of PCW will never forget either. Ron we have a chance tomorrow night to become the tag team champions of PCW.' 'I don't know about you, but I have this gut feeling that it can be done. I want to become the first tag team champions of this new era if you will of PCW. I know it will be a hard first match against Cowabunga Connnection, but we know what tough matches are all about Mike. I'm ready for this!' 'So am I. Let's just hope that CC are ready for what they are about to recieve tomorrow night from Suicidal Tendencies!' [fade out]
