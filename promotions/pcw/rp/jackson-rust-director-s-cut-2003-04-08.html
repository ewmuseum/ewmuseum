Jackson Rust: Director's Cut.

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.04.03. Cheesy music, infotainment-opening credits open up this crap scene – WTF? Voiceover: Ladies and Gentlemen, it's now time for The Hidden Pro Wrestling Show, the show that lets you see previously unseen footage from the world of Professional Wrestling that you will never see anywhere else. And now for your host, it's LANCE LOCKJAW! Some weasel in a cheap suit and perma-grin walks on top a soundstage, surrounded by images of wrestlers from over the years. Lance Lockjaw: WELCOME! As you may have just heard, this is the show where all the outtakes and other things you weren't supposed to see from the world of Pro Wrestling end up: The Hidden Pro Wrestling Show! Don't believe me? Well, let me show you this clip - an out-take from the Corporate Wrestling Alliance, filmed shortly before this year's Wrestle-fest, and the main event pitting the Champion Bulk Boa against the former champion, Big Dave... The scene cuts to Big Dave sat in his locker room. Big Dave: Bulk Boa, you may have got the duke on Monday, but only because you had Big Sexy Chick Magnet in your corner to distract me long enough for you to hit me with your devastating Schoolboy Rollup Pin™, which has claimed so many in your 73 year career. But at Wrestle-fest, it will be just you and me; one on one, and I will reclaim my belt. I will take you, drill you with the Embuggernator, and you will know why they call me Big Dave - because I am BIG, ALL, OVE...I said 'Embuggernator', not 'Embiggernator', didn't I? Fuck, fuck, FUCK! The scene cuts back to the studio. By the way, do you know how hard it is to copy these up for archive? Lance Lockjaw: As you can see, they may be 'Professional Wrestlers', but not so 'professional' when it comes to reading scripts! Canned laughter, while a shot of the audience shows about as much animation as New-grounds when the server crashed. Lance Lockjaw: Next, we have an example of when people take sporting events far too seriously, from a recent XBWFTSF event, Super Duper Extreme Brazil Man encounters some stern opposition... Super Duper Extreme Brazil Man and his valet are sat on a sofa, with Super Duper HARDKORE~! Englishman and Super Duper HARDKORE~! Valet is also present. Hey, do your research! Super Duper Extreme Brazil Man: Eu concordo inteiramente...o burro que aqueles dois cínico os clowns estão dentro para ele. Cameraman: Hey, Boy From Ipanima, RIVALDO DIVED! Super Duper Extreme Brazil Man: Transar você, meu amigo. Cameraman: Huh? Super Duper Extreme Brazil Man’s Valet: Take a wild guess... Cameraman: Yeah, whatever... Super Duper Extreme Brazil Man’s Valet: I see Canada are doing well…... Cameraman: That's it! You, me, right he... Super Duper Extreme Brazil Man dives towards the camera, with sounds of an absolute kicking being heard, before cutting back to the studio. Lance Lockjaw: That goes to show one thing: Never rile up a Brazilian about soccer, especially during the World Cup. That counts double if your country specialises in Ice Hockey. Some fat, trailer park trash actually laughs. A record! Lance Lockjaw: Stay with us after these important messages, for some never before seen footage of the former PCW Rising Star Champion, Jackson Rust. Ad break. I could do some cheap gag about the products, but that's a bit too scatological... Lance Lockjaw: Welcome back. Before the commercial beak, I said there would be some new footage of Jackson Rust that you can't see anywhere else. The reason this footage cannot be shown is because seven months ago, when the promo was broadcasted, it had to be cut in several places due to time constraints, because they didn't want to over-run into the Over 70's Semi Pro Shuffleboard Championships. To recap, this is the promo where Jackson, Al, Steve and Kimberly visited the San Diego Comics Con. The first excerpt features The Ultimate World Championship of Magic: The Gathering... Cut to rough footage. Magic the Gathering #1: So, ith thith where we thine up? Magic the Gathering #2: Yesh. Magic the Gathering #1: Cool beans! Magic the Gathering #2: Don't get too confident - my character hash max shtamina and ish deadly wif hish crosshbow. Magic the Gathering #1: Yeth, well, my tharacter got laid onth. Magic the Gathering #2: Oh my! You're nexsht. Magic the Gathering #1: Hello, my tharacterth's name ith the Illuthtriusth Lord of the Underworld. Tag Boy: Yeah, whatever, here's your nametag, whacko. Magic the Gathering #1: 'Illtrusth Lord of the Underwear'? Magic the Gathering #2: My name ishn't 'Morty Lipshits'! Tag Boy: Bite me. Jackson Rust: Hey, Pete, long time and other clichés. What're you doing here? Pete the Tag Boy: Losing my will to live. Do you know what it's like having to fill in nametags for 40-year-old virgins going through the worst mid-life crisis possible? Jackson Rust: Hmm…no. Pete the Tag Boy: Then you have to sit through their warm-ups. Look over there. Magic the Gathering #1: I am tearing you in half with my Vampire Hate Ray! Magic the Gathering #2: I jusht opened the window, and shunlight redushes you to dusht! Magic the Gathering #1: Aw, crap! Jackson Rust: So, how long has it been since... Pete the Tag Boy: You unceremoniously ditched me? Kimberly: So, who is this guy, exactly? Jackson Rust: Now, thereby comes a tale...unfortunately I can't do the whole Flashback thing, since the Outside Broadcast unit annihilated the budget. Kimberly: Again? Damn, they can be so damn cheap in The Office at times... Jackson Rust: Anyway, when I was getting started in the wrestling biz, Pete always hung around backstage, along with Steve and Al. However, they weren't getting paid because they weren't wrestling, so Steve and Al split ASAP and started leeching off of me, while Pete got lost somewhere in Montana. Pete the Tag Boy: 'Lost'? You told me there was a show there! Jackson Rust: There was... Pete the Tag Boy: A three-ring circus does not constitute as 'a show'. Jackson Rust: Hey, don't fuck with The Greatest Show on Earth - even if clowns scare the shit outta everyone aged four upwards. Besides, we're holding up the line, so see ya. Pete the Tag Boy: Whatever. Wiseass... Magic the Gathering #3: Hello, I'm Charles Manson, the escaped serial killer. Pete the Tag Boy: Yeah, right, go join Voltaire, Bilbo Baggins, Spock and Peter Parker over there... And yet another damn break to a commercial sponsoring Chris Storm brand flavoured condoms. And back from the break. Seamless, isn't it? Lance Lockjaw: As you can see, that was all cut, and went straight into the whole Magic: The Gathering segment of the promo, and the network complained that some geek-nerd-fan boy was stood in front of Kimberly for large segments, which compromised advertiser's policy. But that isn't all that was cut. The later section, which seemed rushed on the broadcast, had much more background. Watch: The camera is in the 'Darkness' section of the San Diego Comics Convention. Professor Goth: With this Melancholynator, I can turn anything into a gothic-tinged monstrosity that will potentially violate copyright, but it will impress anyone that bought the last Type O Negative album. Voice: It was I! Meanwhile... Kid: Look, mommy, POKEMON! Gotta catch 'em all! Mother: Oh great, MORE! Back. Obvious what's going to happen, isn't it? Professor Goth: For example, here I have a Pokemon doll, Pikachu I believe, and will make it Gothic. Observe! He zaps it, and it turns into a small, evil dragon/bat thing, and then flies off. Kid: Look, Mommy! That Pokemon fly! I not see it before. I CHOOSE YOU! Puke n' Chew: ROOOOOOOOOOLF! Kid: WAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHH! Mother: Serves you right, you little bastard. Professor Goth: Wait a minute, this thing is set to 'Evil', that could mean my tests... Japanese Guy: Look, it GOTHZILLA! Professor Goth: Oh bugger... Cut back to the studio. Lance Lockjaw: As you can see, more copyrights were broken in five minutes than on the last Eminem album... Jackson Rust: Excuse me, Mr. Lockjaw... Lance Lockjaw: Yes? Jackson twats him over the head with a chair, causing him to drop down unconscious; fancy how I can do this, eh? Jackson Rust: You owe me $12,000 for the rights to that footage, and failure to pay will result in Tiny paying you 'a visit'. By the way, this whole 'walking into other people's promos' deal sure is tedious... Now, may I just remind everyone that tonight it's match number one in my second run in PCW, and that means A.J. Trinidad, Adam Stryker, and Semore Smoke have the chance to make a name for themselves. Or, to be even more surprising, actually say a word on TV. I know the booking was set to give me an easy ride, but this is ridiculous - my opponents could grow a pair and make their presence known for a change, before I get through faster than the average A.J. Trinidad sex session. But who am I to complain? At least I know the damn rules - I can keep my record the same if I don't turn up to the arena. But, at the same time, I can pick up another one to add to the collection. Now, if you excuse me, since security is on its way, I'll be off. Yes, I know that was anticlimactic, but I'm a busy man. And where did I put my Portuguese dictionary? Jackson takes off, awaiting fines for wasting so much time on set-up and cutting a very short promo, indeed.
