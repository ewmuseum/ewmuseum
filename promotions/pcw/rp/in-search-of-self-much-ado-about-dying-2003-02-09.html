In Search Of Self: Much Ado About Dying

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-02-03 Surprisingly enough, Tommy Gordo hadn’t called about the bitchslap from Samuel L. Jackson. Further investigation into Skitzo’s hotel room revealed a small container filled with LSD and some other tablets. Who brought them or left them there is unknown, however it can be blamed for the previous day which saw Skitzo break his train of thought for the past couple of days and see him terrorize the hotel in which he was staying until Tuesday Turmoil at the Lloyd Center. As he woke up that day, Skitzo wondered why he was lying in a pool of sweat, spit and champagne, and other ungodly fluids. As he got up and walked around his room trying to clear away the bad thoughts, he spotted the small container lying open on top of the minibar. In the corner of the room, the plasma flat screen was on, the 30” occupied by static, obviously the porn channel having been disconnected for over-usage, which is a very scary thought, especially when coupled with the fact Skitzo had woken up in that kind of mess. He emptied the contents of the little container out onto the minibar, reading the front of it as he did so. According to the small label, there were fifteen tablets, “courtesy of the house”. This didn’t register well with Skitzo, especially as he counted out fifteen tablets…now either he had an unknown obsession with drugs…or Mr. Adrian Paul has something he’d like to confess to the public! In any case that left the question on Skitzo’s mind as to what the hell happened that caused him to call out Samuel L. Jackson to do his dirty work. In all honesty he didn’t really care, as he looked back at the television, he suddenly remembered the last conscious minutes of the previous night… --- Skitzo put the phone down and turned the big screen on with a smile on his face. Samuel had called back confirming that TTT had indeed been bitchslapped, and celebrations were in order. As he flicked through the 999 odd channels, ranging from all night 24 hour news, to VH1, to Bondage Bears Go To Bollywood. The entertainment world at his fingertips, and it was all shit! Eventually giving in, Skitzo switched over to one of the many sports channels to catch late night unedited PCW: Friday Night Fusion. At that time, P.W.A. were fighting Storm and Windsor for the PCW Tag Titles, he quickly turned the TV off and fell asleep with no interest in watching the rest of the program. He wasn’t interested much this time around, even though he could scout TTT and learn his tactics, the bitchslap was more than enough to thwart any attempt by the Totally Tubular One to overcome the mighty Skitzo! Mr. Psycho fell asleep thereafter with thoughts of PCW running through his mind, as the day came to an abrupt end. --- A flash of remembrance from the previous night sent Skitzo reaching for the telephone. Slowly dialling, he put the receiver to his ear and waited until the other end of the line was filled with the polite and courteous welcome of a female operator, being that there were so many divisions and positions and whatnot at PCW H.Q., a personal operator was handy. After identifying himself he asked to be put through to booking, and on querying as to the card for Friday Fusion next week, was a bit flustered… Skitzo: ……………What the hell is a Gadget Shop Garbage Match?!……………’A surprise’ does not constitute as an answer y’know……………bah you know me I never complain about clusterfucks, what about it being Valentines Day and all? What if I wanna meet up with my sweetheart and celebrate Valentine’s Day?……………I CAN HAVE A SWEETHEART DAMMIT! Ok thanks anyway…yeah bye… Hanging up the phone, Skitzo was less than pleased. Another clusterfuck was coming his way in PCW, and he didn’t know what to think. Sure he had success in the Alliances Soda Contract match, and Gatorade! now being the official drink of PCW thanks to him was a proud achievement, but being whored into every battle royal got tiring. Being schizophrenic was bothersome at times, but Skitzo never complained, it paid the bills and that’s all that mattered…that plus the fact he was known throughout the world as Mr. Psycho! Today was his fourth day in the hotel and thus far he had killed a sheep and decorated it to intimidate…or resemble TTT as a part of his promotional campaign for this Qualifier win on Turmoil. Wrestling was of course a bigger part of this campaign, but what could he do, broadcast wrestling tapes of himself across the globe? He felt a dead sheep with a surfboard shoved up its ass did more justice to the cause. Apart from that, he had committed some form of fraud – he thought – posing as ‘The Highlander’ Adrian Paul, and also held himself hostage in order to physically bitchslap Tommy Gordo with the help of Samuel L. Jackson. How many PCWers can say they did that in the space of four days?! So today was Day Four. Sunday. The Sabbath. Skitzo contemplated going to Church, but he was already exiled from about 18 other religions, he didn’t want some pissed off Christians tripping him up at the heels. Sunday was a day of rest for many…and the ideal opportunity to cause mayhem for Skitzo, but what could he do? No one else that he knew of was in town, and he didn’t necessarily want to stir up any more trouble in the hotel, at least until he left. He’d been cooped up in his room for too long now, and with the toilet making funny noises and calling him a ‘Nazi Scheister Bastard’ due to Skitzo having flushed the LSD, the One Man Gang was a little worried to say the least. And so, pulling on his outdoor wear of sneakers and jacket, Skitzo left his room for the first time in a long time, making sure the door was locked before taking the elevator the three floors to ground, and signing out…as posing celebrities do. The sun shone brightly on this Sunday. Although a crisp cool breeze filled the air, the sun gave a pulsing warm heat to the people far below. Even walking around for a few blocks Skitzo was bored, looking out for any form of entertainment he could indulge in, be it rat racing or an arcade…anything was better than sitting listening to your toilet telling you that you were the piece of shit! Passing by certain people, Skitzo was given a few glances, and could hear some kids whispering his name as they realised a real life PCW wrestler was in their midst. However, given the fact Skitzo’s last match entailed knocking out a Negro with a 20” black rubber dildo, nobody was too keen on shaking his hand and getting an autograph. He passed the Lloyd Center, looking over the venue that was set to be rocked into the ground on Tuesday when PCW came to town. He passed on going inside, seeing no great attraction other than the PCW ticket information banner running across one of the screens. His attention was turned elsewhere however as more than a couple of blocks down the street he noticed a large, mushroom cloud-like puff of smoke billowing up over what appeared to be a church. Skitzo: HOLY SATISFIED CUSTOMERS!!! THE CHURCH IS ON FIRE!!! Taking off immediately down the road, Skitzo sprinted like a man possessed down the street, leaping over children and slamming old people to the ground on his way to the holy structure. As he neared it however, his look of terror had degenerated into a confused smirk, and his fear of a burning house of Christ turned into the realisation of another…for there before him, as happy as one such as he could be, was Clyde. Skitzo: Clyde? Clyde: Jesus? The Burned Man didn’t even show the courtesy of acknowledging Skitzo, instead keeping close watch over the church as smoke continued to billow up into the building. Skitzo: Very close, but no…what the hell are you doing? Clyde: A favour for the good Lord, fumigating-… Skitzo: Whoa………aren’t there people still in there? Clyde: That’s the favour…some of them were Luke Perry fans… Skitzo: Were? Clyde: Yes, past tense… Skitzo: I see…so they’re all dead at the hands of a psycho maniac burned wrestler with no genitals, an 8-year old lover who held the PCW World Title longer than Big Allan Steel? Clyde: Yes… Skitzo: Ah…want to go get an ice cream or something? Clyde: What better way to wrap up a mass murder than ice cream? So Skitzo and Clyde took off, leaving the fumigation process to come to an end as a church full of Luke Perry fans said hello to their maker. Birds sang their song, happy as could be, and flowers blossomed in little gardens…then turned into spewing torrents of blood and semen with a little Saturday morning children’s TV mixed in as DA MAN! passed them by. Just around the corner Skitzo spotted a mobile ice cream cart, manned by a twenty-something guy with a goofy cap on and a big gay smile. Walking up to the cart, Skitzo perused the menu, while Clyde stared at the man, who was returning the stare with his smile. Ice Cream Man: How can I be of ssservice to you boysss? Clyde stepped back, cocking an eyebrow as the young man smiled at him, giving the Burned Man a small wink as Skitzo stood back up, ready to order. The Demon nudged Mr. Psycho, and leant over to him. Clyde: I don’t like this one, something not right about him… Skitzo: Well whatever you do don’t ask for Amaretto… Clyde: Why ever not? Skitzo: He’s stirring it with his penis…you decide… Looking at the ice cream man with a dissatisfied sneer, Clyde pointed at the tub of Rum Raisin in the hopes it’d have actual Rum in it to cease the pain of existence, then grunted, pointing again at the box of sprinkles. The ice cream man nodded as Skitzo pointed to the tub of Butterscotch. Scooping out the ice cream into a cone, the ice cream man handed each one to Clyde and Skitzo respectively before holding out his hand. Ice Cream Man: That’ll be $2 please… Before Skitzo can hand over the money, Clyde pushes him back, standing in front of the ice cream man, his eyes begin pulsating with a bright red glow, he opens his mouth and unleashes a banshee like screech, and sends a guttural stream of fire blazing out of his mouth and incinerating the ice cream man, the smell of burning flesh filling the air as skin peels and hair burns, until the remains of the ice cream man are strewn across the once delicious pallet of gelatinous delight. Looking quite pleased with himself, Clyde turns and walks away, as Skitzo stands shocked with the money still in his hand and the ice cream almost melted away in the other. Turning and running after Clyde, Skitzo was quite confused as to why someone had to die. Skitzo: Why? Clyde: I specifically asked for sprinkles… Skitzo: SO YOU KILLED HIM?! Clyde flashes a look of innocence before throwing his ice cream away and walking along the sidewalk whistling a happy tune, one that causes an overhead aeroplane to fall out of the sky and crash into the church Clyde was fumigating earlier. Bystanders at this time have completely avoided the two PCW wrestlers as they make their way along the street. Ignoring people as they gaze and point at the two PCWers, Clyde and Skitzo walk along the streets, Clyde gazing at the fluffy clouds and happy little babies, and Skitzo recoiling in horror as he turns them to undercooked Mexican food. Eventually Skitzo and Clyde find themselves in front of a familiar building, and looking at a clock in a nearby shop window, Skitzo sighs. Skitzo: Time is getting on…think I’ll just head back into the ol’ hotel and have lunch or something… Clyde: Don't hop in front of any buses and horribly die or anything.....or do, I don't care… Skitzo: Goodbye to you too… As Skitzo moved towards the hotel, a muffled laugh from behind made him turn around, only to find Clyde looking innocently back at the hotel…the roof of which was a blazing inferno! Clyde: Bah! I was gunning for the whole thing…ah well, can’t win ‘em all… Looking in horror at the roof, Skitzo had no time to think or do as the front doors burst open and none other than the Hotel Manager appeared, angry and bald as ever. Hotel Manager: HEY! My hotel roof is on fire, dammit! Was it that man over there who looks suspiciously like the burnt spawn of Satan and the descendant of every evil known to mankind mixed in with a little Saturday morning Children’s TV who for copyright reasons can’t die because it would mean the death of a thousand and one scantily clad whore nuns from the bowels of an infected rat’s faecally efficient asshole…or was it you, who doesn’t look like he could hurt a child?! Clyde, ever the devil’s advocate, points innocently at Skitzo…who looks back at Clyde in disgust. Skitzo: YOU!…How the…you…FUCK!!! The Hotel Manager clips Skitzo around the ear, dragging him back into the hotel as Clyde walks off, killing things on his way to doing whatever. Hotel Manager: You’re going to put out every bit of that fire and pay for any damages you fiend! After we put you up in one of our master suites as well?! DISRESPECTFUL CUR! Pulling Skitzo into the hotel, the Hotel Manager sealed Skitzo’s fate and brought him to the realisation Day 4 of his stay in Oregon was not to be different, but instead another day of suffering. What could Day 5 bring only time would tell, but for now Skitzo only knew that he was 2-0 to TTT on intimidation, was booked into a clusterfuck he knew he could win but probably wouldn’t because he didn’t want to touch the nasty bastards in the match with him…and he had a whole lot of fire-putting-out to do. Damn that Clyde to hell! To Be Continued
