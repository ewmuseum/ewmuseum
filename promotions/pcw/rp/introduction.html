Date: ??? Location: #1 for Turmoil... If we still need to put that <_< (This was the first RP I posted in PWA. The grade was 87!) 'Where is the money?' 'I-I don't know what happened. I was in the airport and they... they jumped me.' 'In broad daylight?' 'Look, I...' 'Don't play with me Avery. I want to know where my money is, and I want to know NOW.' 'I don't know.' 'Then get it back for me. You better have Fifty thousand dollars before the next time I talk to you,' he hung up on me. I sat in my room of the old, run-down hotel. The hotel was my home for the past year and a half, and wasn't my idea of a dream home. The place was owned by my 'accomplice', a lawyer from here in Miami, Luigi Leone. Luigi was a 'gift' given to me by my boss at the time, Vladmir Cortez. Cortez was not exactly my friend anymore, ever since I lost his money. Damn that Cuban Mafia, a thorn in my side. The good thing was that Vladmir was up in Chicago doing his business dealings, while I was way down here in Miami. The phone rang, causing me to jump up from the torn, rugged bed that I was laying on. I was about to pick up the receiver when Luigi yelled from the other room, 'Got it.' My hotel room door was open all the way and Luigi's office was just next door. The small, two story hotel hadn't been inhabited by anybody other than me in a very long time, so yelling throughout the halls of the place weren't uncommon. Oh, and when I said two story hotel, I meant that it was about fifteen rooms upstairs and then a lounge and check-in counter downstairs. Oh, if only I could get away from this hotel. But no, Luigi thought that it would be a good idea for me to stay cooped up in this stuffy hotel for a whole year and a half. Sure, I went outside to do my small wrestling gigs, but that was hardly a trip. I had to use a different real name, wear a mask, and had to strictly return to the hotel when I was done. How long would it take for my name, Avery Vance, to be whiped away from the world, and more importantly, away from all of the gangsters and mafia members that knew of my troubled attitude? All I need was my one chance. My one chance to join a global fed. If I could join a federation that travels lots and is well-known, I could get back out onto the street. How many people knew my name anyway? Once Luigi told me that it was safe, he said that I could have the name of one of his associates and could have odd jobs to do for them. I could slowly start earning back the Fifty thousand I needed. In the other room, Luigi said goodbye and I heard him place the phone down, walking across his office, and then coming in my direction. Luigi trotted into my room with, surprisingly, a smile across his face. Luigi had shorter brown hair with thick side-burns, that he refused to cut off. Luigi had some glasses and always kept his business face on. Luigi always wore his same blue suit, in case he had to go out for some business deals... or to be a lawyer. 'It's time.' I sat up from my position on the bed and looked at him. 'Time for what?' 'I just got off the phone, with your first business deal.' 'You mean no more hiding?' 'You better believe it. Your gonna need a full wrestling federation to join.' 'Who's the business deal?' 'Paul Baker. Come into my office and I will explain more.' Instead of standing up from my bed and rushing over to Luigi's office like I should have done, I stayed to question him. Besides, the bed that I was now sitting up on was way comfier than the chair in Luigi's office. 'Who is this Paul Baker?' Luigi had already turned away from the threshold of the door to reenter his office. He stopped and turned back into the entrance, facing me. 'Paul Baker is a pretty well-known millionaire. Whether he got his fortunes from stealing, or doing business is debatable.' 'How is he going to find me a job?' Chances are that this Paul Baker guy was a backstabber, just like most of the underground crime lords were. 'Baker own a small bar down on Broad Street. Your going to need to prove your trustworthiness before he will give you an actual, high-stakes job to do. Paul Baker doesn't like pushovers, so you have to stand your ground.' Luigi was firm in his talking. If you took a first look at Luigi, he sort of looks like a nerd. Don't be fooled at his looks however, he can be a hard ass. 'Does he know I'm coming?' 'Of course. You better get going.' And that was it. I would step out of the hotel for the first time in almost two years to restart my life, as a wrestler, and as a conman. Paul Baker was going to change my life... I could only hope.
