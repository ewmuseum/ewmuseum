The Clone Wars Part 1: Intro

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/1/02 The camera fades in… The Chef sits behind a desk in a black leather recliner, smoking a fat cigar, and counting the wads of cash that flourish throughout his newly bought office in his newly bought house. A giant picture of Tyler Nelson drapes over the center of the wall behind the Chef. The eyes of Nelson are locked squarely on the camera with his illuminating yet downright maniacal smile showing forth. The Chef reclines further and further back in his chair, and puts both feet up on the desk, revealing his new Italian made penny loafers. He takes a big puff out of the cigar and winks at the camera playfully. He holds his arms out, practically pleading for the camera to pan around the enormous room. The Chef: I sold out… The Chef takes a few puff of the cigar and continues. The Chef: I did, I sold out, and with Allah as my witness, I feel spectacular!!! If there was a crowd near the Chef, they’d be booing him, but there isn’t, so no boos. The Chef: Some Muslims may look down upon me for what I have done, for I have joined a “capitalist organization”. Some may even speak out against me or make threats against my movement. But I know better, for I am a true follower of Mohammed and the great book of Allah! The Chef whips out a new, leather bound, golden text, high quality version of the Koran. The Chef: Ahem… In the name of Allah who is most gracious, good, and a swell god! The Chef reads a passage from the AL-ANFAL “Those who keep up prayer and spend benevolently out of what they have been given; they are the believers in truth; they shall have from their Lord exalted grades and forgiveness and an honorable sustenance.” The Chef: That sounds a lot like Mr. Nelson to me folks… Right behind Allah, the most gracious embodiment of compassion and goodwill is Mr. Tyler Nelson. Who else has the heart to rescue homeless cats and dogs and ship them over to the Orient into the houses of hungry peasant families? Nobody but Tyler Nelson… Who else has the heart to provide PowerAde to more than 25% of the members of their worldwide sweatshops? Nope, not Kathy Lee Gifford, I’m talking about THE MAN…Tyler Nelson! The Chef pauses for a moment, and then flips through the pages of the Koran to find another enlightening passage. The Chef: The next story I am about to tell is not found in the Koran, but resides within the depths of Islamic Mythology…You see the adversary to Allah in Islamic religion is known as Iblis. He is the being that quarreled with Allah and was sent into the fiery pits of Hell for his behavior. As most narrow-minded Christians today interpret the story, they see the fall from grace as a consequence of the arrogance of Iblis. To them he would not bow to humans…and thus broke Allah’s law… The Chef takes a few puff of his cigar and continues… The Chef: But after further contemplation this Islamic fundamentalist has found a new meaning, and new cause to this story…you see, I feel that Iblis loved Allah too much to bow to humans! I know it’s such a revolutionary idea that you infidels cannot comprehend, and probably never will. His love for Allah was eternal and exquisitely powerful, and the only way he survives this damnation is the memory of the echo from the divine shout of Allah telling him to GO TO HELL! And that is true, sometimes love can “bear and endure all things” like that Looney tune Paul once said… The Chef slams the Koran shut and continues. The Chef: I feel my relationship with Mr. Nelson is the same way. No, I don’t love the man, and I can barely tolerate him sometimes, but I have to admit something…As much as I need him for my spending sprees at Raisin Rack and CuisinArt.Com, he needs me! That’s right you bunch of culinary school dropouts! HE NEEDS ME!!! Who else is going to set up suicide bombings in the buildings of his enemies and competing corporations? Who else is going to keep Allah on his “good side” and guarantee him safe passage into eternity through Allah’s grace? Who is going to provide him with a harem when all of those Asian “Spas” close? ME!!! The Chef pauses to soak in to the aura of intelligent conversation, being wafted through the room faster and more potent than a Bertha fart. The Chef: Black needs white, Allah needs Iblis, and Tyler Nelson needs the Chef!!! The Chef furrows his fluffy black beard and takes a gander at the Tyler Nelson poster behind his desk. The Chef: Sheesh, you think a man with oodles of cash could afford a pack of Stridex once and a while! (Shaking his head) Anyways, now on to the issue of Alexis Parshin, the Uzbek Annihilator… The Chef takes a few Benjamins, folds them, and places them into his newly purchase leopard skin chef uniform. The Chef: I see great things in this Muslim lad; frankly, I see a lot of me in him. No, not literally you homosexual-thinking heathens! I’m talking about the qualities that make up a student of the Koran and a true follower of Allah and Allah’s grace! This guy has got what it takes to become the next big Islamic Fundamentalist in the PCW, and with my tutelage, and Mr. Nelson’s funds, we will terrorize the PCW as a tandem, a Taliban Tandem…hehehe… Suddenly the door of the office bursts open. Big B stands in the doorway, clad in her usual sweats with an “I conquered the 72 oz. Tenderloin at Smokey Joe’s” t-shirt. Bertha: F*cko! You got a f*cking visitor at the ho @$$ f*cking door, b*tch skank f*ck! The Chef: Thank you B, it’s probably some upper class entertainer or media mogul who’s welcoming me to the neighborhood… Bertha: I don’t f*cking think so, f*ck face… The Chef: Then who is it my plump princess? Bertha: Who do I f*cking look like f*cko? A c*ck @$$ f*cking secretary!?!? The Chef: Actually you’re physical stature is probably most comparable to the field of computer programmer or bus driver… Bertha: Yeah! So I f*cking ain’t so secre-f*cking-tary b*otch!!! The Chef: Touché, I guess… The Chef gets up and walks out of the office… The camera does not follow the Chef as it is stuck on a tripod in the room. All we here is a strong sounding whack from outside. CLINK!!! THUMP!!! “F*cking Sweet!” “Thank you my dear, now I will finally get my revenge on the Chef, BAM!!!” The Chef promo Narrator begins his spiel. Narrator: What happened to the Chef!?!? Who is behind this probable attack!?!? (Mumbles)*Cough*Emeril*Cough! We’ll find out, same Chef time; same Chef channel!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! The camera fades to black…
