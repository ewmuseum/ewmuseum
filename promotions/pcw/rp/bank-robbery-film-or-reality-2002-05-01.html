Bank Robbery: Film or Reality?

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st May Just what is up with El Diablo? He seems so much more different than ever before. Has losing his wife really caused all of this? Or is it just numerous occurrences in his life that has finally made this Mexican snap? Whatever the answer is, it is almost certain that Flaymin Aymin had a part to play in this change and this mess. And ever since his Taco Bell commercial aired he has discovered a love for the camera, it seems to be his life now. But just what is it that made El Diablo fall in love with directing? His weakened state of mind? His life falling around him? El Diablo just isn't right in the brain anymore. El Diablo stands in line inside a bank. The line is quite long and El Diablo is growing impatient. There are at least four people in the line ahead of him, and he is stood in the shorter line of the three that the bank has. The bank is a small one; it's more of a local bank than one from a big chain of banks and therefore is very full and busy right now- quite unusually. There are three openings for people to get money from the people behind the counters. The carpet is a grey color and the walls are painted nicely white. There must be at least 18 people waiting in line. El Diablo is wearing his Mexican flag face paint and has his long black hair running loosely down his back. He has on a white vest which strongly promotes his devil tattoo, and some cream baggy denim jeans to go with his black sneakers. And around his neck is a digital video camera not in use at the moment. The weather must be quite hot as sweat drips down from Diablo's forehead. The atmosphere in the bank is that of desperation, as many people are yelling in an attempt to get through the lines quicker and obviously failing. Diablo waits impatiently before yelling something himself. 'Come on, I'm the Metaphor of Magic,' he shouts to the bank clerk behind the counter. The bank clerk just sighs, as he has to go through this horror several days a year. Diablo waits impatiently as the lines in the bank fail to move. He soon decides he has waited long enough and brings the camera up to his face and turns it on. 'This is the scene of a local bank, when the cash machines are out of order and a city of people need their money,' he speaks to the camera as he cycles it slowly around the establishment seeing all the annoyed people waiting in line. One person then leaves the line that El Diablo is stood in, meaning El Diablo can move forward a little closer to getting his money as the room now has one less person in. 'Hmmmm,' he says while thinking, 'This could really make a great movie, if only something dangerous happened now,' Diablo says to the camera whilst having the camera pointed at the electric glass doors. Suddenly two men burst through the door, they are dressed entirely in black and have ladies stockings over their faces to conceal their identity. 'Everyone get on the ground now,' shouts one of the men whilst pointing a gun through his jacket pocket. Screams by women and children are heard as they all drop to the floor with their hands out wide. The second man is carrying a bag to place the money in and is assumed to have a gun in his pocket too. The clerks at the desk stay motionless with their hands under the counters, meaning they could trigger a silent alarm to the cops at any minute. Everybody in the room is down on the floor except for El Diablo, who believes he is making a film. 'I said, down on the ground,' shouts the first man again. Diablo just smiles. 'This is great, only say it like you mean it,' is his response. The man with the gun in his pocket looks at El Diablo, really confused at his stupidity of not getting onto the ground. 'Seriously man, just get to the ground,' the first robber says in a less aggressive tone to El Diablo. Instead though he just stays on his feet still recording what is going on. 'Come on guys, get on with the robbery, otherwise you're just ruining the film,' states Diablo while walking around slowly, trying to capture the suspense from every angle. The second robber approaches his friend and whispers in his ear as the civilians on the floor look on in horror. 'He's obviously a goof, lets just get on with it,' he whispers to his friend. The first robber nods his head and takes his attention off of El Diablo. 'Heads down on the ground,' he yells while using his foot to turn an old mans face down into the carpet. Both robbers approach the counters. 'You lady, get the money out of all the cash registers behind there and lockers, you two stay and put your hands on the counter,' the first robber states angrily. The woman proceeds to start getting money out from behind the counter. El Diablo zooms in close on the robbers with his camera and then tuts insanely until he has the robber's attention once more. 'What?' grunts the first robber as he turns around. Diablo looks back at him without fear in his eyes; as to him this is just a movie he is making. 'You just don't sound that believable,' comments Diablo to the robber. The man raises his arm, which has the gun in his pocket. 'Is a gun not believable?' replies the man as the woman behind the counter fills the large bag the second robber brought with money. Diablo zooms out and steps a little closer to the men. 'Well, lets see it,' says Diablo eagerly, 'Lets see the gun,' he expresses with lust for his movie. The robber looks down at his pocket, and then looks bizarrely at El Diablo. 'No,' he says bluntly, whilst trying to continue with this robbery. The people on the floor are still in fear despite the messy burglary that is occurring before them. 'Keep your faces down and nobody will get hurt,' the first robber shouts to the civilians that are grounded. El Diablo continues filming despite his apparent dissatisfaction. 'Stop!' shouts Diablo. Everyone in the room stops and looks at El Diablo. 'You're supposed to kill someone,' says the Mexican Devil. The civilians on the floor gasp in horror at what Diablo said. The first robber nods. 'You're right,' he says the civilians sheer fear, 'How about, you?' he finishes while pointing at El Diablo with the gun he has in his pocket. El Diablo shakes his head ferociously. 'No, no, no, that is too unbelievable. When does the director ever die in a film?' states and asks Diablo them both. The second robber again approaches his friend and whispers into his ear. 'He's right,' he says. The first robber again nods. 'Ok, what is believable then?' asks the robber to him. El Diablo then walks up to the second robber and hands him the camera. 'Faces down on the ground!!!' shouts the first robber again. The civilians comply. El Diablo then snatches the ladies stocking off of the robbers head and places it on his. 'Now keep filming,' says El Diablo. He places his right hand in his jeans pocket like it's a gun and goes up to the counter. 'GIVE US ALL YOUR MONEY NOW!!!!' he shouts to the lady at the counter. At that precise moment the cops burst into the bank. 'Hey here's one of them,' says one of the cops to the first robber standing back with the stocking over his head. They take him down to the ground and handcuff him immediately. 'And there's the other one,' shouts another cop, looking at El Diablo who has the stocking on his head now. The cops immediately take him down, as the robber with the camera subtly walks away. El Diablo gets cuffed and dragged away screaming. 'But he has my camera, I was making a film,' he yells to the cops as they drag him outside. 'Sure you were, sure you were,' says his arresting officer. El Diablo shouts some more about his camera, as the bank robbery civilians get told they can leave.
