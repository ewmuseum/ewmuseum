Seductive Revenge...Calling Me Yet Again

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-3-2002 Have you mentioned how much your starting to HATE fate? Rewind... PCW Third Dawning... One of the most BEAUTIFUL and redeeming moments in your entire life... As Ric Flair would say...you bled, you sweat, you paid the price...to be the best... Although, Ric never said anything about diving off a 50 foot balcony, driving your plummeting body into the chest of your enemy with every last bit of intensity you could muster...But hey, sometimes you gotta make your own meaning out of things. And you did, you gave Snow and Darkstar the fight of their lives... They may have beaten the living hell out of you so badly that your blood flowed like Niagra Falls, but you made sure you got each and EVERY lick back. For every kendo stick strike, insane dive, and career threatening move...you gained solace... You slowly fed that center of revenge within your soul until finally, with that one dive from the heavens atop of Darkstar... You felt whole again. The months of pain stemming from physical to emotional...the painful feelings that drove you to become the villain you are now...Finally subsided when your hand was raised, and when the the blood was wiped from your eyes....you gazed upon three of the most beautiful sights in your life... Brandi Wilson, the women who even before you met Aimz, stuck with you through thick and thin and always provided you with comfort and support...her dragonskin green eyes eyeing you with concern as she held your broken body in her arms... The PCW Continental Title....the #2 strap in all of PCW. That beautiful gold belt shimmered in the bright ceiling lights, the only reason it didn't blind you was due to the blood already flooding your eyes. This belt linked you to some of PCW's best....from Alan Kriegman, to Trashcan Man, to Legend Inc., to Chet King....and now you could add yourself among the list....the 25th man to ever have laid your hands on this belt and hold it with pride as you grasped the leather strap in your hand....raising it with ANY strength you could muster in your arm to the sky...letting the WORLD know you gave everything you had to claim the title as your own.And finally...the fans. Through thick and thin, those rabid 'Rent-Heads' showed their support for you. Deep inside, you never could get why the average wrestling fan would look at you and open their mouth to cheer for you....you were always used to being the freak, the outcast....the misfit. To feel like you fit in with every outrageous cheer from the PCW fans gave you the strength to go on...the strength to be your best. They loved you dearly...and you had nothing but lvoe for them back. There was no better romance. After that night, you swore it was all over....you got your revenge...you got your title...you got the girl, it was over. It was time to get back to normal, time to stop dwelling over pain and destruction and start getting back to loving life again....The final boss has been beaten and it was your time to walk off in the sunset, the end credits were ready to roll... Then somebody had to F**K it all up and make a sequel! In one fell swoop...all the fighting you did to become whole again, meant absolutely NOTHING! When you embraced Brandi in the ring that night on Fusion....all that revenge and hatred you managed to purge from your body came racing back within you, and it brought a few friends. With that swift kick to your groin, she did more then just send a shock of pain through your crotch.... Brandi opened the floodgates for hatred aaaaaaaaaall over again. You don't know why she did it... You didn't know why she'd sell you, Amy, the kinship you two shared over the past few months, and her fans out...But you had a slight idea.... Xavier Kannon. All your problems seems to lead straight back to him... Big Alan Steele's retirement... Brandi turning on you... The chaos and unrest in PCW.... Kannon is the ultimate super-villain in your mind....And in order for you to be whole again.... In order for you to break THIS cycle of revenge.... You've got to stop him.... End him... Drive a stake through his friggin heart... But you have to start slowly.... And that means first, dealing with Frisco, part of the reason Big Alan Steele is gone. He was a pawn in Kannon's game to oust Steele...And when you play a pawn, your always ready to be sacrificed.... You'll make sure Frisco gets his share of due for what happened to Steele....then it's only a matter of time before you get your hands on XK.... Frankly, if you compare three months ago to now...Same script, different cast. This Continental title....That win at Third Dawning.... Everything means absolutely nothing now....The only time you will only be able to rest easy is when Xavier Kannon is down before you....broken and beaten the way you were at Third Dawning... Today, the road to ending his reign of terror begins.... Revenge has consumed you for the second time.... And the scary part is... It feels better then the last time.And if Kannon needs some proof that he doesn't need to be on the recieving end of a Rent-A-Villain revenge spree..... Ask Darkstar, I'm sure he'll have a nice story on what it feels like to have some sicko crash on his chest.
