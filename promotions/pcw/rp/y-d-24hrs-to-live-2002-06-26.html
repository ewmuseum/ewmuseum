Y.D: 24Hrs To Live

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th June 2002 The story of Charlie Loc seemed to have finally come to the end of the road, not only had the Young Vietnamese Viper landed himself in puddle of blood, but he also seemed to attract trouble and attention! A fight went down and a problem was solved, but the problem itself just lead to something bigger….bigger than anything Charlie could handle anyway!As the 'Khmer' problem was indeed solved in a viscous yet honorable manner, it seemed to have opened up a Pandora’s Box on the young Viper who had just re-entered his home country.Maybe it was for the best as PCW was calling him back to his duties in the states, or maybe it was just his own stupidity that cost him a valuable lesson and possibly his life….How many people does it take for one young 19 year old to learn a harsh lesson? What bigger trouble could Charlie have landed himself in when he took care of the 6’3' Khmer Soldier? Could his first night in Saigon possibly drag on for any longer?Perhaps it was fate or maybe it was just bad luck, whatever it may have been it cost Charlie enough blood and now its delving further….life….or even his soul couldn’t possibly pay the price for the trouble he’s landed himself into this time! One thing that has been learnt, never count the Vietnamese Viper down and out….. Young And Dangerous Pt IV: 24Hrs To Live Dripping is all that could be heard, echoing in a strange dark place somewhere in Saigon, it was though it was almost trying to signify that without it, the place would’ve been forgotten and non-existent. But with the dripping came the darkness and the smell…. Covered by a sheet of thick darkness and accompanied by the constant dripping of a water leak somewhere in the same vicinity came a foul odor! Nothing more could describe the stench other than a mixture of repulsive B.O (Body Odor), an un-flushed toilet and a glass jar of sewage water that hadn’t been opened for at least three years….or to sum it up in one word….Pussy! Whatever the smell may have been, it didn’t seem to make the place seem any more livelier or welcoming as it made it seem more like it was a burial ground that was hidden somewhere deep inside a building with at least 20 bodies all rotting in the same room! As the dripping continued at a constant pace the smell began enough to almost make someone throw their dinner up. No matter which way it was described, which angle it was looked at, there was no way it could be compared to anything nice in life…all it simply seemed like was a dark, disgusting and stinking pussy! WHOOSH~! Out of nowhere a shrill howl of wind sounded out and echoed in the small room as a chilly breeze also accompanied it along for the ride. The room must’ve not been built properly because the wind seemed almost icy, as though it had crept through a small crevice somewhere in one of the walls! It seemed as though everything in Saigon wasn’t up to scratch….that’s if this place was even in Saigon! The only hint of that was the shrill call of the wind and it’s almost icy hand as it began to blow through the room which was almost identical to the bitter cold that was in the city of Saigon. Wherever this room was or whatever this room was, it seemed to be cut from the outside world….the only knowledge that this room actually existed was through the wind that blew through the shoddy walls and the dripping of a leak somewhere, possibly in the ceiling. Suddenly out of nowhere in between the dripping of the leak there was a loud CREAK~! Followed by a sigh and a shiver as it became apparent that there was indeed someone in the room! Maybe that someone was a squatter, maybe it was some poor homeless guy just trying to get a decent nights rest and shelter from the cruel Saigon night, that’s if this place was even in Saigon! But even though the location was unknown….at least there were signs of life! As the wind blew yet again, the howling could still be heard in the distance as the room soon filled with more blowing chilly air. It seemed like Winter had just gained itself permanent residence with the unknown man, possibly trying to force him out of his place of shelter! The man rolled over yet again as another loud creak insured. Just the thought of lying down or even being in the a room that stank like shit, had leaks and cracks and was frozen over like the North Pole is enough to send shivers down anyone’s spine….but then again! The things people will do to get away from the cruel nights! Once again just as the chilly wind was about to blow, footsteps could be heard coming from somewhere in the distance! Their sound mixing in with the still dripping water or whatever it may be and echoing around the room making life seem more apparent. Suddenly the wind began to blow violently from outside the room as the air began to become like ice and the footsteps sounded as though they were coming closer! Maybe the Wind knew what it was doing and tried to build up suspense…then again wind doesn’t even have a brain! But as the wind continued to howl the footsteps continued to slap against the cold hard concrete floor and edge closer and closer to the room before they finally stopped…. As the footsteps finally stopped there was silence….the wind seemed to stop, the dripping ceased and the putrid smell somehow blocked itself out before a rattling of metal broke the silence and everything went back to the way it was! CLICK~! The metal could be heard clicking as suddenly a bright white light flashed in the room before it settled down to a dim yellow color as a light dangling from the roof with a metallic, bent shade around it lit up the room, showing the surroundings. As the surroundings were revealed the option of throwing up began to creep its way back in….as any normal person would’ve thrown up at the mere sight! The room in fact appeared to be a cell of some sort! A wooden, rickety old, rotten bed hung off of a wall on a far side while a toilet sat in the corner of the room on the opposite side, covered in orange rust and with pieces of weird brown objects sticking up and onto the seat. Whilst looking at this the smell began to make a rather un-welcomed re-entry as the festy conditions of the cell continued to reveal themselves! The drip revealed itself to be nothing more than a leak in the roof…only thing was that it wasn’t water that was dripping, this seemed to be some green substance. Possibly liquefied slime or sewage that ranked from another cell above, whatever it was it wasn’t normal! And finally the drafts of cold winds were nothing more than huge cracks in the wall, exposing the cold night from outside the cell and causing an Asian man to shiver in only a tank-top and jeans lying on the wooden bed with a grey torn up blanket pulled tightly over him. As the wind howled once more and the man on the bed began to shiver a voice rang out, high pitched and whiny from somewhere behind the wrought bars that lined the front of the cell. 'You in there…Khiem Jung Loc….the boss want to see you now! Get up before I make you get up! Understand?!' A police officer dressed in a brown Vietnamese uniform was slapping a baton in his hands eyeing down Charlie Loc who was shivering in the cell. The now revealed Charlie Loc seemed to have wanted to leave the cell as he threw the blanket off of the top of him and leaped up out of the bed, his hair covering his face as he chose not to rebel against authority as he so usually does. From underneath his cap the Police Officer managed a smirk as he thought he had one a huge battle as Charlie began to hold his arms shivering as the Police Officer inserted the key into the jail door with a CRUNCH~! And slid it open as Charlie walked out of the cell and into the corridor. Charlie’s feet slapped on the corridor as the smell seemed to un-affect him as the Police Officer could be seen scrunching his face up as he slid the door closed before chuckling to himself and sending his baton directly into the back of Charlie’s knee causing him to drop as he smiled and led the way. Not even feeling the pain Charlie flinched down but quickly recovered and followed the Police Officer down the row of cells which all seemed like third world countries, with appalling conditions such as the cell he was just in before! The floor of the entire cell ward was nothing but fortified concrete which was icy cold under foot as Charlie followed the Police Officer down the corridor and up to a large wooden door his feet finally stopping slapping on the concrete floor. As they stopped in front of the door the Police Officer tapped his baton on his hands and began to smile at Charlie as he looked down, seeing his reflection in the Officers shiny boots before the Officer tapped him on his shoulder with the night black baton and motioned towards the door. Having no other option and wanting to escape the cold Charlie did as he was told and opened the door, coming into a brightly lit and well furnished office, complete with a large desk and swiveling black leather chair in the background. The heat that was being produced in the room was absolutely amazing for something somewhat of a Police Station as Charlie looked around lapping up the warmth and just pleased he was out of the containment cell he was in moments before. Suddenly as he was looking around a stern and cruel old mans voice cut in as Charlie snapped into attention. 'WHAT YOU DOING?! SIT DOWN LA!' Sitting in the leather chair was nothing more than an old man dressed in the same uniform and cap with badges and medals pinned all over his chest as he played with a whip in his hands as he began to talk to Charlie with a look of anger on his face. 'It been a long time hasn’t it La?' The Head Officer replies in a coaxing voice as Charlie finds himself a plastic chair and takes a seat in front of the desk. 'I ASK YOU A QUESTION!' The Head Officer shouts at Charlie as he shakes his head and looks down towards the red carpet on the floor. Charlie still doesn’t respond instead he just nods his head and begins to scratch his arm which has dry blood running down the side of it as the Head Officer seems not to care at all instead he just continues to talk thinking he’s all high and mighty. 'I didn’t think we be seeing you back in Saigon for a looooooong time Khiem! Oh no, we not think we ever see you come back here! You left, the problems stopped and then on your first day back, you manage to cause a riot in the market place AND begin trouble with the Khmer again?!' The Head Officer raises his tone right at the end of the sentence. Still not speaking Charlie just acknowledges the Head Officer by a short nod of his head as the Officer sends the whip slapping onto the table with a CRACK~! 'LOOK AT ME WHEN I TALKING!' The Officer shouts. Finally getting the picture Charlie looks up as the blood he just scratched on his arm begins to slowly trickle down once more as the Head Officer begins to spill, lapping up his authority. 'Well you seem to have sort out a few Khmer tonight Khiem….but you not know what you just done do you? We finally get Saigon clean of Khmer Scum and in one night, you manage to fuck up one of their leaders and spark another war between our syndicates and theirs….YOU STUPID!' The Officer finishes the sentence by adding a few of his own words in Vietnamese as Charlie looks up. 'Well Khiem….we tried everything we you! But whatever we do it seem you just make thing worse for yourself….you want to see your record?! HUH?!' The Officer shouts at the top of his voice before sending the whip cracking down on his desk yet again. Charlie doesn’t even shrug his shoulders as the Head Officer reaches down by his side and produces a stack of about 30-40 sheets of yellow and brown paper filled with all filed paperwork possibly of all the crimes, large or small that Charlie had committed. Although he did them all himself, Charlie seems to be impressed by his achievements as he looks up and a sinister grin crosses over his face as he laps up the warmth of the heating in the office and the attention and reaction the Head Officer is giving him. The Head Officers wrinkled Asian face and gray eyebrows much up together as he slaps the pile with his whip and continues to yell. 'That it Khiem….we told you last time there be no more messing around in Saigon! Your record….they messed up! YOU A CRIMINAL! I not even know how you get a job in the States, but I tell you what! We not going to lock you up and throw away the key…that too simple!' The Head Officer leans back in his leather chair as Charlie perks upright. 'What you doing then? You going to kill me?' Charlie smirks after the end of his comment as the Head Officer grins back. 'No….' The Head Officers voice trails off 'What you going to do then?' Charlie perks upright in his plastic seat and begins to smile getting a decent reaction out of the old wrinkly but high up man. 'You have 24Hrs to leave my city…and this country! You come back….anyone see you here again….anyone sneak you in again….they going to die! So are you! No more Khiem….it over! In 24Hrs if you within the wall of my city, you as good as dead!' The Head Officer begins to laugh like a maniac as Charlie stares back dumbfounded. 'You think you deporting me….' Charlie grins as he begins to play smart again. 'No….' The Head Officers voice trails off again. 'We not paying for nothing! You find your own way out of this country in 24Hrs or we going to kill you~! Now get out of my office!' The Head Officer continues to laugh as Charlie’s eyes grow wide. Charlie gets up out of his seat as the Head Officer throws a huge puffy jacket over the large desk as Charlie grabs it and the old man begins to laugh yet again. 'KHIEM JUNG LOC…….IS……BANISHED
