Date: not entered His name... is Payne...His first name... is Rich...His whole name... is Rich Payne...His Job... to sing songs to the world that nobody had ever been able to hear in its perfected state...His goal... to make sure that nobody around this planet has to listen to the semi-decent trash that comes from his old school rivals at N'Sync... and to make sure that the next generation of PoP Icon cultural peeps are able to withstand the greatness that flows to their ears, otherwise known to the better half of the general community... and the world, as 'The Pretty Cool Guys'... or just 'PCG' for short. Many people around this small world had been recently bothered with the events that would be carried around with Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore.. tuning into their Tag Team match this week on Turmoil, a show which would see the element of Badness springboard from the otherside of the ring and force the PoP duo to attack their own partners, nailing one of them with the 'Pretty Cool Jam' Double Powerbomb... flipping the other off... before of course, leaving the confinements of that relativly small squared circle and making their way... all the way to the very back of the arena. Did anyone see the change of heart coming from the proclaimed 'Tag Team Champions' of Primetime Championship Wrestling?Did anyone come close to seeing the very attack that the 'Pretty Cool Guys' would lay upon their Tag Team Partners when the chance to take all of the fame was removed from their picture?Do you think... that everything would have flowed a little more smoothly for Sherwin and Moore... had 'The Heartbreakers' opted to take that step back... instead of ravaging forward, trying to desperatly get across the finish line and clean up before everyone had finished? Of course, if you were to look at the events of it all... nobody could be shocked to see the sudden movements that came along with this attack. The egos that had size easily comparable to the ass of J-Lo, the egos that had size... easily measurable with the current breast size of 'Glam Model' Jordan... they had taken a beating, alas it was something undeterminable... and something that wasn't done out of spite, but out of friendship and caring... however, thats not the way our future PCW Tag Team Champions saw the mixture of ingridients go is it? Its not the way that the story was supposed to end... but it was now the way that it had finished nonetheless. Looking forward... into the very future that was supposed to be touching us all further down the immediate line... we would see that four-way battle on Fusion, three of the teams that made an appearance on T-Moil, keeping their places and moving forth to the show this Friday... that of course, being followed by what is billed to be one of the... if not, THE biggest 'Pay-Per-View' showcase in the history of 'Primetime'. The big Tag Team Battle Royal that has everyone wondering about who is going to be able to do enough... to not only last for the duration of the entire match... but also, take hold of those Tag Team Championships.... returning the Titles to the pinnicle of Tag Team competition around the world. Now, with that final element of sadness in mind... with the final fact, that both Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore... will be looking to not only take the PCW Tag Team Championships and restore them to their final glory, but to score with plenty of twenty year old girls afterwards, we leave the world of all that is real in these... troubled times... and look forth to the world away from earth, the very place that stars our very own action hero... much like Max Payne, but only about twenty five million times worse. I mean, come on, when you look at all the facts that follow Rich Payne... you can't even mention one good thing about the guy... especially when he can't even nail one single little syringe throw at a bad dude. Tsk... I guess its going to be this way for the rest of our life. Anyway, I think that I have kept you in suspence long either... I think I can see the horrible foam coming from your mouth... and the wet patch around your crotch getting wider in diameter as you wet yourself with anticipation for what should be instore for Rich Payne. So remember folks, when acting out scenes of this nature, be sure that you do infact miss the target... since Sherwins aim is so lame, you have to intentionally miss... he on the other hand, intentionally tries... and misses. Holla...Welcome to the second installenment of the very story that has taken the Nation by the throat and throw it from side to side... shaking that rat bastard like the little doll that it was... like the little pathetic insect that it attempts to be. I see that the actual narrator for the piece didn't bother with the recap information for this installment of data... but thats not really my problem, I'm not here to stand around and tell you all information... that should have been laid forth by the lackey of the installement in the piece at the beginning of the show. However, the time right now... is not the correcto time for me to be bitching about what I should and shouldn't be telling you, because for everyone that cares, I have a Tag Team partner to save from the clutches of Dr Mark Sloan and his evil son, Steve Sloan in the confined walls of Community General. I had come off the back of a close confrontations with my first two guards and the ugly nurse. I was beginning to think that this was the moment my game plan would shoot up to the next level... I was beginning to contemplate if this was going to be the very second in time... that the world was going to force its 'Son of PoP' into raising his standards, taking his collective game and idealistic nature of 'Operation:Moore' to the next level of super stardom. By the time the ugly nurse had fallen down to the ground, I began to walk through the corridor of the first floor, looking around... gazing from left to right... left to right... left... to... right. I was beginning to feel like Max Payne with every step into the world of the unknown. I didn't know what was going to be around the corner, I didn't know what kind of Drug crazied psycho was going to be batting around the walls. It could be something like... 'The Ghost of Crack-mass Past'... or the 'Ghost of Crack period'... or hell, the 'King of Crack Reactions' Dante Jackson! Either way, it didn't take more than a few seconds to understand that anybody under the influence of crack who wanted to get some of me... would surely be a little harder to defeat than somebody who was just standing across the room... looking into my eyes with a glare that could be called 'Trivial'... or 'Visual Harrassment.' I slowly continued down the corridor, taking a right turn at its end with extreme amounts of cautious pounding its way behind me. I was sure that something... or somebody was going to go around and try and nail me tonight... it was the one and only thought that was beginning to stay inside my head... the ever present thought of somebody like Jack Valentine, coming along and doing something that is really very sick and horrible. The very idea of him even being in the same room as myself made me shudder... it had been more than enough of them to do anything over the last few weeks.. that painful loss against 'The Cowabunga Connection' being something that was caused by the patheticness of one certain tandem... not naming any names or pointing any fingers or anyway, we all know who they are. Regardless of that small tidbit of information, I decided to continue down the new corridor, coming to an elevator at the end of the light. I was a little confused as to why an elevator would be placed in such a lame position, especially since it was next to some of the patient rooms... but I guess thats just the way things weren't taken into consideration when the actual hospital was recieving its construction. I pressed the button indicating up and stood back, patiently awaiting the moment that the elevator would come down to ground level and allow me to make something of a worthwhile trip in my quest for Ryan Moore. 'What is this?', I asked myself as the elevator slowly plopped down behind the big metal doors that were obviously here for the saftey reasons of the people... although I don't think my hero, John McClane from 'Die Hard' would have been too bothered about whether the doors were open... or closed... or if the guy was dead... or alive when he was falling down that shaft to his unfortunate death. Standing still with my feet spread shoulder width apart as the elevator came to its complete halt. The doors slowly began to open, pulling back at each side as I looked in with cautious fear.. the suspence was beginning to settle in, having to wait for anything... and everything to even attempt to happen. I carefully took a bold step forward... before taking another... and finally a third, breaking through into the elevator. A loud sigh of relief came from me as I turned around and pressed the button reading '2'. Sure, its not the best idea to simply move up to just the next floor.. but since Ryan Moore could be anywhere around this damned hospital, the only thing I can do is work up each of the different floors and hope that I'm going to be able to find him. The elevator doors closed again and I could feel the contraption beginning to move, shifting itself up the elevator cables toward the second floor.. a journey which actually took the better part of about five seconds. They sprung open again and I walked out into the cold confinements of a new corridor, the immediate surroundings were at a standstill... there was no obvious signs of life... but then again, when it comes to any form of trap, there is never going to be any amount of noise. Like Max would, I reached into the pocket of my pants and fished around for a semi-automatic... amazingly enough, just like in the magical world of TJ Hooker, I managed to find myself a bubble gun... I pulled it out into full view and admired the beauty of its craftsmanship. The pink handle, the yellow body, the little plastic things that come out and around in crazy assed patterns, I placed my index finger around the trigger while holding onto the weapon and carefully began to walk up the corridor, turning left and right at the very sight of a door, kicking the wooden thing in before aiming my weapon around, searching drastically for anyone... or anything. Time began to pass by.. about five minutes or so had flown past at this moment in time, and to be suprise, I was still trying to move up the corridor... I hadn't even made it to the end of the line yet. Thinking about the horrible things that those two scientist freaks could be doing to my best friend, I ran to the end of the corridor and took a left, turning around and looking straight ahead down another corridor and toward the next elevator. The second floor was slowly beginning to look like a joke... everything was so silent... the fact that I'd missed those three syringe shots earlier was beginning to die down and I came to realize that I didn't even need to use them if this current walkabout was going to remain the same for the rest of my mission. I carefully stepped forward, the distance between myself and the elevator getting smaller.. and smaller.. and smaller. With every step I took, the sense of fear... the sense of being along began to settle down and fade away... my steps became more lively, more energetic.. WHEN... 'Yes Dr Mark Sloan...I'll get right on it... hes here and he knows I'm near by... so I don't care if he can hear this radio transmission.... Yes Dr Mark Sloan... I'll be sure to stop him from getting to the fifth floor.' I stopped and took a few paces backwards, my eyes turned to the room where the voice seemed to be coming from... and then, out he stepped... the only man who had been so crude and horrible to 'PCG' the last time we came aboard this sinking ship. The scene panned around him, zooming in upon his face to inform the world... that this demonic character was the assistant from the first few seasons of Diagnosis Murder.. no, it wasn't Amanda Bently, no... it wasn't Jessie Travis either... infact, this was Jack Stewart... the real New York Bad Ass of the hospital profession. One may recall 'The Undertaker' claiming to be the 'American Bad Ass'... but Jack Stewart was 10% Doctor-Dude... and 90% Bad Ass. 'Payne... Max Payne... didn't you once star in a video game that hit the PC, Playstation 2 and whatever other formats it was released on?', he asked, sounding sarcastic. I looked down to the floor and began to wonder why people could ever get mixed up with Max Payne and Rich Payne. I laughed to myself for a few moments before looking back up toward the enemy doctor. 'Max Payne isn't who I am... you *beep* *beep* *beep*... mother-*beep* *beep* *beep*-ing fishface!', I managed to yell out with quite a psychotic backing to it. Jack Stewart was unimpressed by the introduction of who I wasn't and he whistled once, the calling card for all those who are expecting help to come along the way. I looked around my immediate area in a circle quickly, not even being able to find traces of another enemy with the closed vacinity. Jack looked over to me and he ran... I didn't have enough time to react and he laid into me with right hands and a BIG TIME clothesline. I hit the deck as he continued the relentless attacking with boots to my body. 'Rich Payne... Max Payne.. Adrian Payne... it doesn't matter what incantation of that freak you are... to me and Community General, you will always be known as Rich Sherwin... the bastard that couldn't do a thing right!' Jack Stewart looked mad... he looked as though he was constipated... he looked as though he was dying to wack one off... get some action from an 18yr old blonde girl or do something else to a Nomad Sheep-Goat Crossbreed. I didn't quite know what he was getting at or trying to do... but it probably had some sickness to it nonetheless. I remained on the floor, looking up at the lights and then at Jack Stewart as he stood over me. I heard his insane cackling before... *PING* *PING* *PING* *BBBBBRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRROOOOSSSSSSSSSSHHHHH*
