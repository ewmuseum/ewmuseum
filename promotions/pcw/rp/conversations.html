Date: not entered I hear voices… Many… wanting to awake from their sleep… This slumber of ignorance they call doubt…doubt of what is the truth…doubt of what is real, and what they cannot accept as real. I cannot deal with their ignorance any more…fools doubting our status because deep down inside, when they expel our existence, it makes them feel like they have a chance. Pharaoh referred to Akiyama as a god…and Akiyama destroyed him like a god would a mortal. He knows…at least now he does. He knows the power we hold…the power that we can use at will to destroy anyone and anything we desire. And the mere fact that we don’t destroy you all now…is because it is not part of the grand scheme. We are all a part of a universal plan…some scripted to be weak and pathetic, while others are destined to be miserable simpletons who can squeak away with wins. Some…well, just one…one is writing the script that all of you are playing by. And you all will act accordingly…you will act like unbelievers…you will act like you do not recognize the power that is Celestial. So when the god finally destroys you…you will realize with crushed spirit and broken heart…that he is your god…and you cannot stop him. None of you…not a single man in PCW… No matter what you say or think… After all…I’m the one putting those ideas of doubt into your mind… I’m the one writing your lines in this skit to entertain the god… …Or am I? =©= We found followers… A good horde of them…about thirty-six men and women, dressed in silver metallic pants, silver boots, and silver tank tops. They were in hiding…from the government…from their oppressors. They took one look at the god and fell down on their faces…in respect and fear. Cute kids…someone taught them manners well. They were worthless…but they were valuable in a way. Because I knew someone was coming for us…not the government…not the weaklings in PCW…but Inphino Blitz… We had something he wanted…someone he wanted…four friends… Let’s see what he can do… =©= Matrix looked down at the messy workstation, cluttered by technical equipment and CDs. He stood there in the sub-basement of the Department of Divine Examination building, his shoulders hunched over and his head lowered. With his disguise from the airplane finally removed, the black haired young man stared apathetically at the computer surrounded by the junk aforementioned. He was back to work…for the Executives… Dressed back in his short sleeved black button down shirt and black slacks and boots, he heard a beeping from below, a rapid series of high beeps in the melody of “Change” by Deftones. He pulled the black cell phone from his waist and raised it to his pierced ear. “What?” whoever it was, he didn’t want to hear it. Much like no one wanted to hear from Eddie Mandrake anymore. He’s been a waste since he came into PCW…only getting involved with Scorpion. After that, people cared less than when he was actually involved, thus making him less than a zero. “Don’t you “What” me, punk.” It was Reality’s brash voice ringing out through the earpiece. Matrix’s face drooped lower as he rolled his eyes. “I hope you understand the nature of your assignment now that you’re workin’ for us again.” “There’s a difference between employment and slavery, Real.” Matrix countered, picking up a compact disk from his desk and looking at the label across it. “And I guarantee you, I’m only here until I break out of here…which wont be long, I guarantee you.” “Quit squawkin’…the file with the information about your project is somewhere on that sh*tpile you call a desk. Get crackin’…” The phone hung up, and Matrix groaned as he looked at the stack of folders sitting on his desk next to the computer monitor. “At least I’m not Sonata.” He muttered to himself as he walked around the desk and sat down in that dimly lit metal-encased room they called a workstation. -=-=-=-=-=- “Sonata Kojima, 5’8, 139 pounds, Japanese, eye color brown, hair color black, with yellow dyed streaks. Known by the Japanese government to work as a fashion designer in Tokyo…but since we don’t work for the government…we know what you’ve really been doing.” She was still tied to a chair, her arms behind her back and her legs bound to the legs of the chair. She glared up at the speaker, who walked around the office reading from a file. The same office where she and Matrix were kept prisoner was empty, save the speaker and her…as far as she knew. She could only look behind her so far. A long, curvaceous leg stepped into the picture, smooth and bare, with a red high heel at the bottom end of it. Moving up, you could see the upper thighs disappear underneath tight red fabric, clinging to the curves of her hips and torso. A tan manila folder was open in front of the woman’s chest, held delicately by red-nailed fingers. Spice…the Lovely Executive, as she was called…looked down into the folder and smiled at Sonata. “You’ve been a member of the renegade group known as the Turbo Brigade, led by the rogue Sayaka Mitsui and aligned with various vagrants such as Force, former Maxis Inc member Gale, and even Mystic of the Void Elements. And you’ve been quite a thorn in our sides for a long…long time.” Sonata looked away, like she didn’t care to remember any of this. Spice pursed her juicy red lips together in a knowing smirk and read on. “You have a past with a certain someone we want back under containment, Miss Kojima…” circling around the prisoner, she slid her finger along Sonata’s bare shoulder, running against the collar of her black halter top. “You have known the one called Akira Akiyama since youth…and now you’ve become involved with him, helping out an old friend after so long.” “What’s it to you?” Sonata asked, getting tired of the back-story. Spice chuckled as she closed the folder and leaned over her, both her hands resting on her shoulders and her long red hair sweeping against Sonata’s neck. “What is it to me?” she chimed with a smile. “Well…my coworkers and I have a huge interest in the being called Akiyama, as well as the one called Cyclonica. Cyclonica used to be a part of the DDE…until Akiyama came and stole her from us. That girl is powerful…no matter what she may look like.” “I’ve seen…” Sonata agreed spitefully. “Of course you have. Look…I know you may think you know Akiyama…but these are different times. He’s not the same person you used to know. He’s probably not even the same age as you think he is. He’s changed…he’s not human…I doubt he ever was…” Spice’s finger began to trace circles behind Sonata’s ear. Sonata stiffened as the other woman slowly traced a line from the back of her ear, down the side of her neck, and to her collarbone. Things were getting a little…strange. “Whatever Akiyama is at this point…Cyclonica is one as well. Whatever is inside your friend, some of it is inside Cyclonica…and we want to know what it is. We want to help them both before this thing inside of them destroys them from within. It’s making them sick. It’s like a virus within them…and it’s destroying them daily. You’ve seen the things she’s done…you’ve seen what he’s been doing. They’re not normal…and the Executives want them here…” Sonata felt Spice’s finger go up and down the rim of her collar, a scoop neck that allowed Spice’s finger to graze past the top of her chest slowly. Her body was tense as she stared straight ahead, uncomfortable with Spice’s interrogation. “I…want….you…” Oh boy…Sonata’s face was painted the color of shocked, her cheeks flushing suddenly as Spice moved her hand from her collar to her stomach, rubbing it slowly. “I want you…to help me get them back here with us.” “Thank god!” Sonata blurted out suddenly, the color leaving her face. Spice stood up after that, looking at the back of her head with a delighted smile. Sonata shook her head following the woman rising from around her shoulders. “I mean…tough luck! Find yourself another stoolie!” Spice looked down at the ground in defeat. Then she walked around in front of Sonata…and straddled her waist. Sonata’s eyes widened as the two women sat there chest to chest, Spice’s hands clutching her slim waist. Then she slapped her… Sonata turned her head back, her eyes both hurt and angry with the redhead staring coldly into her brown eyes. “You have beautiful eyes…” With that said, she dismounted Sonata and walked out, clicking the lights off on her and leaving the long time friend of the nVg…in the dark… Much like everyone who underestimates Cyclonica…because she is a “manager” as they label her. Because she is not signed as an actual wrestling talent, but has more skill and talent than half the roster…quite comical. Amongst the talent she excels above in PCW…Mandrake is on that list. She has more of a mark in this business than he does…which is bad for him considering she’s only been at this for months… Only months…and already near main event talent…some would say that’s because she’s aligned with Akiyama. Well…you serve the god willingly with your heart and soul…and you will be rewarded in various ways. But besides that…the inhumanity within her makes her better than the rest of you… =©= Oh…you’re back… Seems like you missed all the fireworks. You missed your chance to swoon like a schoolgirl…Inphino Blitz was just here. He stopped by…we traded words…he and daddy traded “words”…and just when we were about to wrap things up, he pulls a gun on us. What kind of good guy is he? We go to all the trouble of presenting his worthless friends before him, and he pulls a gun on us. What an ungrateful little pest. He’s just another reason why these so-called “faces” are nothing but a waste of our time. They go to whatever lengths to get their way…even if it means resorting to weapons and cheap tactics… So right before he leaves…the mongrel has the audacity to turn around and shoot at me. Yes…Inphino Blitz shot at me. That was crossing the line between being sociable and just plain rude. So what did I do? I shut him down…no questions asked. Bullet stopped right at my face…I’m staring at it like it’s a bee that’s landed right on my nose. Then it just falls to the ground. Black Dragon Boy has his ego shattered about now…oh, what…you thought a gun would be such a big deal against two Celestial beings? Shame…mortals are dumber than they seem. Daddy and I had our fun watching him run away with his silly friends…so he can go lose his tournament final to some other flat-scan from Flat-Scan Wrestling or something. We had better things to do at that point…mainly…the both of us had a huge fight ahead of us. Me…I had Edward Mandrake… Yeah…I know…I barely recognize the name myself. But I checked…and yes, he does work for PCW, and yes, he did somehow earn a shot at this Cruiserweight title this Turmoil. I bet the other losers are ashamed that this no-name guy beat them. Hang your heads in shame, Loc and Kramer… It’s no matter though…Ed Man will be a nice warm up for the big picture later in the week. I hope he realizes that he’s about to be beaten by not just a woman… But a goddess… The big deal was on Fusion…the Mall Brawl. The brawl in which both father and me were participants of... I know that last Fusion annoyed him…I can feel his aggravation pumping through his body now as his arms are wrapped around me in his fatherly embrace. He didn’t win the title…but at this point, it could wait. He was at the top of the list for the belt, and no one could knock him from that position. Clyde would get his…this demon was foolish to think he could best a demon god at his own work. Clyde better face fact at this point…he’s been replaced. The raging demon is through…along with his toddler wife… There is a new sick monster in PCW…and he goes by the name of the Neo Void God. A new Dark Age will fall upon this place…the shadows will assemble soon…and there will be no one that can stop the onslaught in their wake of terror. And since Goth is in this season…with the wake of Clyde the champion, Darkstar, and Duke Star the Goblin Goth God…it’s time for Akiyama to go back to his roots… Ladies and Gentlemen… It’s Goth Hunting Season… So says the Divine Prophetess…
