Reunion Pt 1

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/07/02 Where am I?   He finds himself walking through a lush tropical rainforest.  All around him, huge trees covered in flowering vines reach skyward, providing homes to the hundreds of chattering monkeys that he can see leaping around in the canopy high above him.  He is bathed in green sunlight as he continues to stand gazing up at the tree tops hundreds of feet above him.  He notes the constant buzzing of thousands of insects as he slowly becomes more aware of his unexpected surroundings. How did I get here? He turns his gaze back to his immediate surroundings, trying to find some sign, some clue as to his whereabouts but he is surrounded by dense undergrowth that seems to have grown undisturbed for generations.   What am I doing here? A monkey begins calling, almost laughing at him, from somewhere high above.  The buzzing of the insects seems to get louder as he stands confused in the steaming jungle.   I have to get out of here! He begins to force his way through the undergrowth.  The foliage is incredibly dense and his arms are soon torn and bleeding but he doesn’t stop. I have to get out of here! The insects seem to get ever louder as he struggles to clear a path through the jungle.  He can soon feel the steamy jungle heat sapping his strength as he tears through the mass of vines and small trees but he continues on regardless, desperate to find a way out. What was that? Suddenly he stops and listens.  The monkeys are still chattering high above him but he can hear something else, something more familiar.  He pauses for a moment before continuing to make his way slowly through the undergrowth.   There it is again! This time the sound is louder. People? With renewed enthusiasm, he changes direction and resumes tearing a path through the dense mass of vegetation.  His chest and arms are covered in blood from the thousands of scratches that the undergrowth inflicts as revenge for being disturbed after years of peace and his eyes sting from the sweat pouring down his forehead.  The monkeys continue to laugh at him from their branches in the sky as he stumbles on blindly towards the sounds that he can hear in the distance. His arms feel as if they’re on fire as he trips and falls to the jungle floor.  He lays there catching his breath for a few moments before slowly getting back to his feet and continuing to tear a path towards the ever louder sounds.  Suddenly he stumbles and falls through the undergrowth and finds himself in a large clearing.  He picks himself back up and looks slowly around. People! The sun is shining brightly down from a cloudless blue sky as the sound of children playing mixes with the soft buzzing of thousands of insects.  On the other side of the clearing he can see naked children chasing each other around a small group of crude huts where two women sit watching.  The women are dressed in simple dark colored shawls of some kind and one of them seems to be holding something wrapped in the same material.  He feels drawn towards these people and begins making his way across the clearing.  One of the women sees him approaching and calls out.  Two men come out of one of the huts and watch him approaching.  As he gets closer to these people, he notices that the scratches across his arms and body have vanished. What’s going on?  Who are these people? The two men are dressed only in what appear to be loin cloths fastened around their waists and as he approaches one of them raises a hand and calls out something in a strange, foreign language. I can understand! The children run and stand around the women as the two men come out to greet him.  They continue to talk in their mysterious tongue as they walk ever closer. Greetings. I can understand them! We knew you would come. What? The two men reach out and grab him by the shoulders and embrace him in warm welcome.   We knew you would come. Who are you? As the two men lead him eagerly towards the small huts, the women and children look on in amazement. We knew you would return. I’ve never been here before. They said you would return. Who did? They reach the first of the huts.  It is a crude structure with a roof made of thatched leaves sitting on top of four roughly built mud brick walls.  The men point for him to enter through the doorway. Enter, enter.  You must be hungry after your journey. What journey?  I don’t remember getting here! He stoops down and goes through the doorway.  One of the men pushes him towards the bare earth floor. Sit, rest.  We’ll bring you food. He sits down on the cold dirt floor as the children clamber around the doorway, staring at him in astonishment.  One of the men leaves the small hut while the other stands next to where he sits, smiling down at him. We are so happy that you’ve come back. The children all smile at him from the doorway. I’ve never been here before! It’s been so difficult without you.What is this place? The children move out of the door way as the one of the women comes into the hut carrying several bowls that she places on the ground in front of him.  She smiles broadly as she turns and exits the hut.  The man who had left returns to the hut and stands beside the other man before they both sit down.  The woman returns and places more bowls on the floor before leaving again. We asked the Gods and they told us you would come back to us.  They were right. What? The woman returns once more.  This time she is carrying a large pot full of what appears to be steaming broth.  She places it on the floor next to the bowls and sits down against the opposite wall of the hut.  One of the men begins to ladle the broth into the bowls as the other woman that he had seen as he approached the huts appears in the doorway and sits next to him.  He notices that she seems much younger than the other woman.  She is still carrying the bundle wrapped in the shawl.  He notices that it is moving. Eat, eat. A bowl of steaming broth is placed in front of him but his attention is focused on the moving bundle that the young woman is holding next to him.   Eat!  You must eat! He turns and looks at the two men who sit smiling at him. Who are these people? The children are still standing in the doorway, staring and smiling at him. Eat! He looks across the hut at the older woman who had brought the food in. Perhaps he would like some time with his family after being away for so long? What? The two men laugh as one of them continues to pour the broth into the bowls that sit on the bare floor.He must eat first or he’ll have no strength. The children in the doorway giggle to themselves. Family? But he hasn’t seen wife for many months. Wife? If he doesn’t eat he’ll have no strength for his wife I tell you.  Let him eat! I have no wife! He turns back to the young woman sitting silently next to him.  He can now clearly see the dark haired baby that she holds close to her breast beneath the dark colored shawl. But he has never seen his son. Son? He looks again at the small baby snuggled up to the woman next to him. I have no son! He looks around the hut.  The two men are still sitting on his right laughing.  The children are still in the doorway smiling and staring at him in amazement.  The older woman is starting to drink her broth as she sits against the wall opposite him.  The young woman is still sitting next to him holding on to the dark haired baby and his dead tag-team partner, Thunder, stands staring at him in the middle of the small hut. What?  Hey there bud. What are you doing here?  You’re…….. Dead?  Yeah, I know. Then how can you be here?  Who are these people? How should I know?  It’s your dream. What?     To be continued…………….
