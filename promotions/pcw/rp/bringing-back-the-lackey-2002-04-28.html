Bringing back the lackey

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Bringing back the lackey Featuring: Tim Walden Date: April 28, 2002 (The scene opens upon the PCW logo, in blue and white upon a white background...The image stays steady for about 3 seconds, before the camera suddenly pulls out, in a rather cheesy sort of TV effect that one would expect from 2GE+HER or another of those trendy MTV shows, to show that the logo is on the PCW headquarters in Atlantic City, a large white building which sprawls across the lot on-screen..The effect happens yet again, sending the camera's view speeding forward to a full-body view from behind of a dark-haired man in a suit speedwalking toward the doors of the building, the footage pausing as the camera spins 180 degrees around to face the man, revealing himself as the now-infamous Timothy Walden, before quickly spinning another half-revolution to its previous position..Tim opens the doors and continues his brisk pace through the reception room, the camera shifting back and forth as it keeps pace, while a receptionist in her mid-fifties turns toward Tim..) Receptionist: Mr. Walden..Mr. Walden, there's someone here to see you! (Tim stops, ending the meticulous camera-work and salvaging the lunch of a large segment of the nauseated viewers, then turns.) Tim: Who the (expletive) is it?? Receptionist: I don't really know, but he was rather obese and wearing an ugly red w-- Tim: Say no more, either Mona's let herself go even more, or it's just the (verb expletive) man I was hoping for! (Tim starts his brisk walk down the hallway to his right, passing various doors with and without nameplates, while stringed instruments make their presents known on the soundtrack for no apparent reason, before speeding up through a section of cubicles, then slowing down upon nearing the end of the hallway, gradually walking up to the door at the end of the hall, oak with a 'Personnel Director' nameplate, 'Tim Walden' lurking within the gold of the nameplate below it...He turns the knob, and the camera cuts to the inside, the strings coming to a crescendo as the camera zooms from the desk's clutter, sliding deftly over it and into an upwardly concaved climb to his face entering the doorway, pulling in rather rapidly to view the shocked expression..So much for that viewers' lunch thing.) Tim (stunned): Wh---Wh--Where the (multiple expletives) have you been?! Voice (rather low and slow-moving): I asked for an appointment, and they said to wait here... (Tim's expression grows from an open-mouthed shock to a broad grin, as the camera slowly turns around the spacious office..Passing quickly by the various stuff strewn about the walls as it pans to show none other than Tim Walden's famed lackey, the Fake Mexican, in different garb for the most part, consisting of the red afro wig which identifies him easily, but also including a dark grey suit complete with ill-fitting shirt, which cannot hide his ample gut, and a tie.) Tim: MEXI!!! LONG TIME NO (EXPLETIVE) SEE!! (Tim rushes forward and hugs the bum, then points out the chair in front of the desk, which the Fake Mexican immediately takes his position sitting on, as Tim moves to the other side of the mahogany and his plush, executive chair.) Tim: So, would you like to apply for the executive (expletive) secretarial position today? Fake Mexi: Well, I don't know, what exactly does this job entail? Tim: Well, basically you watch my schedule so that my firings don't conflict with my lunches, make sure I haven't accidentally hired any pieces of (expletive), unless it's for the sake of doing mean things once they get here, and call Kannon occasionally to remind him that I (loud expletive) HATE FIGHTING FLAYMIN (multiple loud expletives) AYMIN!! I swear, the little (expletive) is 4-1 against me, and I'm damn sick of it! Every night there's something that cheats me out of a deserved victory, either cheating by some of his friends or the mere threat of cheating, or that time when he threw that big ol' 50-pound sandbag at me just before the show aired...I caught it, but I was damn (multiple expletives) winded from it! But anyway, I'm sick of fighting him, I know that I'll never win a match one-on-one with him for the fact that I assume that he's going to win by some sort of illegal try and it leaves me unable to concentrate on him, so why the hell bother putting forth a (expletive) effort!?! Fake Mexi: Err..What does this have to do with my applying for the position? Tim: Ohh...Sorry about that, I get on a (expletive) rant sometimes. So, anyway, would you like it? It pays 60 grand a year with benefits out the (expletive) wazoo! Fake Mexi: Sure! Tim: All right, just sign right here...And I'll go to sleep now, it isn't like the cameraman actually thought I would do something (expletive) worth putting on camera! Heck, just for that, you're (expletive) fired, cameraman! Voice: Fine, but don't think that you'll get any more of those cool special effects! And I'm stopping the filming for that too! (The scene abruptly fades to black.) ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
