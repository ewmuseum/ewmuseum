Golden Heart Chronicles: GOOAALL~!!!! (Chapter 4 Part 3)

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/16/02 Golden Heart Chronicles Chapter 4 Ending: The first two chapters of the Golden Heart chronicles were the insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we were able to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The first two chapters in the Golden Heart Chronicles are where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. In chapter three and it’s following parts will focus on Juan and his relationship with being a fighter. He puts other things behind him and works on becoming a determined fighter. In chapter 4 Juan focuses on his International match up for the PTC I-PPV. It is composed of his huge Borro (GOAL~!) Bash. Everyone Read and enjoy the final part to chapter three. Time: 1:53 P.M Location: Lisbon, Portugal Scene: Streets (GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAALLLL~!!!!!!!!!!!) Final Part The scene starts right where it left off. Before we left we saw the different groups of PTC wrestlers who gained their own cult following in Portugal. Juan’s bash seems like it is about to be ruined, but will do anything before that happens. Before we go on to, what happened during the final confrontation, let’s see how this all started. The first group that was formed was by Aimz. The scene opened with Aimz getting ready to enter Juan’s bash, when she saw a group of men going off to the bash. Everything would have gone smoothly until Aimz noticed how they were ordering their wives to stay home and clean. They didn’t hit their wives but their reactions seemed a bit anger filled. Of course Aimz doesn’t know Portuguese, but she knows what abuse is when she sees it. So Aimz did the only think she believed in doing. She went up to every one of those guys and handed their Asses to them. Those men received the beatings of their lives all to the hollers and applaud of their own wives. Before Aimz left, one of the English speaking Portuguese women called over to Aimz. Woman: Excuse me, but where did you learn to those moves? With that question came most of the revolution. Aimz agreed to show them moves as more and more women noticed and joined Aimz. Self-defense turned to anger, which turned into violence against all of the Portuguese men. Then came the next group of chaos starters, which is the not so harmful bodacious Cowabunga Connection. Story is the two guys surfed all the way from the states to Portugal. When they arrived they were bombarded by a bunch of teenage boys wanting to learn how to surf. The guys of Cowabunga Connection showed off their moves, and even gave the guys some pointers on hitting on ladies. After this motion, hundreds of Portuguese teenage boys went to get their hear bleached blonde, hit on random girls and say words like “dude”, “narly”, and “bodacious”. This didn’t hold very well with the parents of the kids who have turned rebellious at their parents for not letting them chill by the beach and hand out all day. The boys of Cowabunga are now gaining as many teenage boys as they can to join the wagon. In you thought the other to were bad, well Pailthorpe takes the cake. He’s come to this beautiful land to bombard the English speaking bars. He dares to even talk about that sick excuse of an English national team. The sick part is the limey’s themselves are following him. They are parading the streets for more Englishmen living in Portugal. They’re preaching that they are sick of all of this talk about the Portuguese national team, and are standing up for their soccer team, trying to brainwash poor young Portuguese children to follow that sac religious England team. This gets Juan completely furious, as he knows these young kids know no better, as they are being fed a line of crap from the drunk Englishmen. Next up is Da Westsidaz. They’re campaign started in the streets of Lisbon. Supposedly with just a stereo and a microphone Da Westsidaz raided the streets singing explicit lyrics attracting all types of people. Next thing we saw people were going out and buying baggy clothing. These hooligans are disgracing the respectful Portuguese streets with their strong words and filthy messages led by both Riptah and Duzza. They’re even holding they’re own smaller dance competition trying to compete with the greatness of Juan Borro’s dance competition. It seems Gangsta rap is attracting the Portuguese public who are surrounding Juan using words like “holla”, “trippin”, “aight”, and “muthaf^cka”. The last group is the smaller mob of Xavier Kannon, Tim Walden, Ellie~!, Isaac Hayes, Kirsty Alley, Dave Snapple, and Giovanni Ribisi. This group is daring to bring their ultimate authority to the land Juan runs. To think they have the audacity to try and take over Juan’s homeland. Sure they may be strong, but Juan will not give in. Of course unlike the other groups this one didn’t attract much attention. The only people behind them are a few men trying to get down the pants of Ellie~! With that history known, we continue with the scene where we left off. Juan notices all of the groups and makes a big and clear message to all of them in attempt to save his party. Juan: Nothing is about to go down. I am Juan Borro. This is my homeland and none of you PTC morons are taking it over. That means all of you. I don’t care what I have to do, but this will be settled. The women will go back to their husbands and serve them the way they’re supposed to. The teenage boys will go back to their families and respect their demands. The English soccer fans will go back in the bar where they belong. Never should those men see the day of light in this country. Everyone who is rapping and using vulgar language will stop immediately. We are a peaceful nation who has little ghettos. We don’t need that type of influence on our people. We will continue listening to our normal dance music and forget this rap. Finally, no Ultimate Authority will be taking place in my land, even if the guys think Ellie~! is hot. Juan finishes his announcement but it turns into nothing other than pure commotion. All types of screaming and words exchange through the people. They strippers leave the stripper area joining Aimz rebellion to the dislike of Kwik FX and Calvin Rilley who was having the time of their lives life. The skaters of Portugal are turning into surfers to the dislike of Suburban Uprising who begin complaining. The British are fighting with the Portuguese. Ultimate Authority is shouting orders, and conservative Portuguese are shouting orders at the gang and rap related group of Da Westsidaz, with Loc and Blayze on their side. Barbara Walters and Meredith Vierra are going at it with the Portuguese politicians and Nelly Furtado and Shakira are still trying to see who can make peoples ears bleed first. Juan notices this all from a distance and attempts to put a quick ending to it all. Juan: THAT’S IT! Juan screams loudly as he grabs everyone’s attention. He then takes a deep breath and continues to speak. Juan: This is all too much fighting. We can’t have this kind of fighting in my homeland. The question is what do we do the settle this all. Everyone waits to see what Juan has to say as they talk amongst themselves. Juan: Well I say the only fair way to settle this is to hold a good old dance competition. The crowd laughs at the fact, knowing they’re was going to be a dance competition, but one with real contestants other than a bunch of rebellious groups. Juan: That’s right, Calvin Rilley and Kwik FX, you want your ladies back, then come up here and dance for them back. Calvin and Kwik FX nod ready to be part of it, wanting their ladies back. Juan: Pailthorpe, you want to show your fellow Englishemen that they truly are the best country, then come up here and dance for it. Three cheers come from the English section with beer thrown in the air and Ben throwing his hands in the air, ready to win. Juan: Westsidaz and Blayze. You guys think you have skills on the microphone, well let’s see what kind of skills you have on the dance floor. Riptah and Short Circuit bust a move to show their ready as Blayze gives a little Harlem shake to show he’s game. Juan: Suburban Uprising, you guys think skaters rule over surfers, then why don’t you show the world skaters can get down with their bad selves. Suburban Uprising grab there skateboards and bang the trunks against the ground showing they’re ready to go. Juan: Meredith and Barbara, you think you two know it all and have the ultimate View, then make women here proud and dance for all women kind. Meredith and Barbara look at each other and give one another a high five ready to win it all. Juan: Nelly and Shakira, you two want to know whose voice can make ears bleed quicker, well why don’t we see which one of you can shake it better in front of everyone. Shakira and Nelly stare each other down. Juan: With all of this said. LET THE COMPETITION BEGIN!!!! The Portuguese public explodes in cheers as all of the contestants hit the floor and go to Juan to see who will be split off into each bracket. Juan: All right, competing in the first heat will be Kwik FX, Calvin Rilley, the team of Barbara Walters and Meredith Vierra, Giovanni Ribisi for team Ultimate Authority, and Ben J. Pailthorpe. Okay guys, you know the rules. Five minutes with you guys all on the floor. It’s no hold barred, and the two winners of this semifinal will go on to the final competition. It seems like our monkeys have all flocked, so let me introduce out new panel of judges. The following judges are: Violator, saying “I AM THE VIOLATOR” Big Gooey Sac scratching himself and waving to the crowd Mike Douglas with a big old Howdie The complete Ream Team, who just five minutes ago jobber to one of the monkeys And Hastie and Raine who are seen eating gallons of ice cream Juan: So that’s our respected judging team, so with no more interruptions, let the games begin. The five minutes start Kwik FX takes the camera scene first showing off his skills by doing windmills for maybe of all different varities, then trying a freeze in a grabbing crotch pose. He then spins on his head on top of a Portuguese flag getting a huge pop from the fans. He then grabs Meredith Vierra and attempts to do the Tango de la Muerte with her. The two grab the stoplight as Kwik quickly rises his hands up Meredith’s skirt almost getting the (GOAL~!) before Barbare Walters shows up out of nowhere delivering hurricarana. OOOOOOOOOOOOOOO Barbara grabs the much bigger Rilley and the two start swing dancing. Calvin goes along with it hoping it will get him to the second round, but Barbara thinking smartly turns on him and punches him right in the crotch. OOOOOOOOOOOOOOO Calvin smiles and then unveils his golden cup he’s wearing as Barbara falls off of the stage in pain. Calvin takes over with some extreme breakdancing, but Pailthorpe jumps in and battles Calvin with some mean moves. The two go all out with shooting star presses, handstands, splits, and all other types of moves, but the crowd doesn’t notice them. The crowd is mostly into Giovanni Ribisi doing a dork dance while being mocked by Kannon. The Portuguese people are loving it and even trying to imitate it as it becomes the new fad. Calvin gets mad and tries a variety of wrestling moves into dance steps like a back flip to a worm, but it seems the Dork Dance is hypnotizing the crowd. The timer then sets off and the scene goes to the judges. The first judge the Violator votes for Ribisi and Barbara Walters with Meredith Vierra. Douglas picks Ribisi and Kwik FX. Big Gooey Sac picks Rilley and Pailthorpe. Ream Team pick Ribisi and Kwik FX. The last judges Hastie and Raine pick Rilley and Kwik FX. After the votes are tallied is seems Giovanni Ribisi and Kwik FX will be moving on to the finals. With that finished the next group of dancers take the stage. The timer is set and the competitions starts with Blayze, Short Circuit, Suburban Uprising, Nelly Furtado, And Shakira all competing. The scene first goes to Shakira with the booty shake that puts the Gooey in Big Gooey Sac. Nelly Furtado gets mad and tries to do some custom Portuguese dances trying to favor the Portuguese crowd. The people like it and give Nelly a round of applaud. That is until Blayze and Short Circuit hit the scene. Blayze comes out in flames with a perfect robot into crip walk followed by an airplane spin. Short Circuit checks out the moves and is impressed as the two man both pull a 180 side flip to bridge and pop out at the same time. They then try a little shake and bake with grade A popping all in sync. Riptah and Daisy applaud Short Circuit while chewing on some Peppermint Schnapps. Short Circuit and Duzza are then jumped from behind by the boys of Suburban Uprising who are dancing around like chickens with they’re head cut off. Meanwhile, Shakira and Nelly Furtado get upset by the goofy Americans as they team up and low blow both members of Suburban Uprising. OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO The matchup then turns into a tag competition between the shaking of Nelly and Shakira, against the break dance crip walking style of Blayze and Short Cicruit. The two ladies try and go for the low blow again, but Blayze’s brother jumps in and takes the shot for his brother. OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO The timer goes off and the scene goes to the judges. Violator votes Shakira and Furtado. Big Gooey Sac votes Shakira and Furtado. Douglas votes Short Circuit and Blayze. Ream Team votes Short Circuit and Blayze. Lastly, Hastie and Raine vote Short Circuit and Blayze for the two men to pick up entry into the final. The final four competitors go on the stage, ad Juan meets them all and grabs the microphone. Juan: The last phase will be little bit different as instead of doing your dance you all have to imitate my dance. Now let’s get this started. The timer begins as Juan gets a minute. He does some booty shaking and sexual moves. He even grabs a lady and does some grinding. He then does the Portuguese worm, which is a slower version of the worm. He then controls his crotch to do certain dance on its own. He follows this up the “Si Senor” dance where his legs start going wild in all direction. He then finishes off with a back flip throwing his hands in the air looking victorious. Juan then leaves the stage and the remaining competitors get on and try and imitate it. The attempts are pretty pathetic. Blayze and Short Circuit do it, but a little bit too ghetto and break style for Juan likes. Ribisi tries shaking, but throws his back out and falls to the ground in pain. Kwik FX gets it down perfect, which actually stuns Juan. The timer goes off and it seems Kwik FX is about to win, but right as were about to go the judges, Juna grabs the microphone. Juan: I’ll be judging this one. I saw your dances and I must say it was a good try. But since this is my party, I award the title to none other than myself. I am the Borro Bash Dance Champion. The crowd and wrestlers all boo and become angered. The anger turns to violence, and everyone turns they’re attention to Juan. Juan sees everyone staring at him and makes a huge run in the opposite direction. The scene fades with all of Portugal and PTC members chasing Juan all across Lisbon. Sure Juan is running now, but come tomorrow, there ill be no running for Juan shall prevail once again. THE END The Following promo was paid for and publicized by the Golden Heart Chronicles.
