DC/LX/VI Saga: I hate library's

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/11/02 He was actually slightly scared of death for the first time in years. For so long had he wished and welcomed it, but now he was actually slightly intimidated by it. Everything that he had contemplated of being the worst case scenario was coming to a full, or at least it soon would by what he was reading. The library was old and desolate, silent, as usual, making the computers seem more than out of place, it was hard to imagine how it was still in business with the amount of dust and cobwebs inhabiting it. Nevertheless, he made the most of what was available to him. 'Look for ancient prophecies, mainly with a three digit connection within them leading to something at the end of this year. I can tell you no more.' Those were Jeremiah's words, he had memorised them over and over in his head since he spoke them to him. Now he had finally stumbled on what he had been looking for from them. Ancient Prophecies - they were easy enough to find, there were plenty of them, finding the specific ones was the hardest part. Three digit connection - He didn't really have to take a shot in the dark to even guess that - 666. Leading something to the end of this year - That was the intimidating part. So far, Gideon's startling revelation contained this.... If one begins at August 1, 1914, and advances in time in intervals of 666 days, there appears to be a pattern where some important historical events occur near these 666 day intervals. By reading that sentence it was easy to laugh at it. However his own calculations had proved him wrong, incredibly wrong. Looking over them now, he scanned through various significant events that he had worked out... Beginning with World War 1's beginning, about August 1, 1914, if you advance in intervals of 666 days: 3 x 666 days Jan., 1920. On January 10, 1920, the League of Nations was formed. 5 x 666: Sept. 1923. On September 1, 1923, an 8.9 earthquake struck Japan, devastating the Tokyo area. On November 8, 1923, Hitler's attempt to overthrow the Republican government was unsuccessful, but turned out to be a critical event in his rise to power in Germany. His trial and nine month imprisonment gained him fame, and he used this time to write his book. Note that five is a number associated with Satan-- the pentagon being a Satanic symbol, which certainly fits here. 10 x 666: Oct. 1932. In October 1932, Stalin was consolidating his hold on power in the Soviet Union by purging the competition, and the world was sliding into the Great Depression. 13 x 666: Apr., 1938. In March 1938, Austria was annexed by Hitler's Germany. Thirteen is considered to be an unlucky number, and Revelation 13 is about the Beast, the Antichrist. This was certainly an unlucky time in world history-- it was the year when Hitler's plans for expansion became clear, and it became obvious that Europe was headed for war, and indeed the red horsemen, war, was soon riding. 15 x 666: Dec., 1941. On December 7, 1941, the Pearl Harbor attack, and the U.S. then entered World War 2. 17 x 666: Aug. 1, 1945. On August 6, the Atomic bomb fell on Hiroshima, ending World War 2 shortly thereafter. 27 x 666: Oct. 1963. On November 22, 1963, John F. Kennedy was assassinated. 34 x 666: Aug., 1976. At this time there was a series of disasters, including a new fatal disease, Ebola, appearing in Africa, and the most destructive earthquake of the century in China on July 28, 1976. Chairman Mao died in China on September 9 He sighed as he read through these, turning the page on his notebook was hard, but he had no choice but to continue if he wanted a better understanding of the hell was going on. Even the somewhat ludicrous theories stating that Russian President Putin becoming the Antichrist in 2000 even seemed logical with the statistics supporting it. Also of interest was the pattern of forest fires then in June 1998 in Florida, fires of course having a Satanic association, which may be related to 1998=666x3. Note that the Florida Keys are part of Florida, could the Florida Keys somehow be associated with the Book of Revelation's 'Keys to Hell and Death' But the most startling of all was the theory of this year, 2002. Various pieces of information and statistics he had gathered had led to the small conclusion in which he had written on his small notebook. 2002 - The Four Horseman could ride -Israel started having more problems then with attacks by Palestinian terrorists. 2003 - DC/LX/VI Roman numerals:DC - 600LX-60VI-6=666 He slammed the notebook shut, placed it in the inside pocket of his long black leather trench coat and stormed out of the library, a cold sweat had slowly began to damped the hair around his forehead, he swiped it away violently as he received suspicious and shocked looks from the fellow inhabitants of the library. * * * * The air felt cool on his face outside, the revelations and prophecies that been fulfilled had made his face burn with a mixture of anger, fear and nauseous. This committee, DC/LX/VI - 666. Corny as their name was, were planning something, and obviously had been since the first world war, and whatever they were planning, it was coming to a head by the end of this year. Right now Gideon didn't know if a bunch of men could pull of the scam to bring the apocalypse, but if they couldn't, he didn't know what the next worst thing was, and as uncaring as he was, he wasn't sure he wanted to find out.
