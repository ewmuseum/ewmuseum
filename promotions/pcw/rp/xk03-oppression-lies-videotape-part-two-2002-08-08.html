XK03 : “Oppression, Lies & Videotape Part Two.”

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/08/02 XK03 : “Oppression, Lies & Videotape Part Two.” Location : Warehouse, Location Unknown.Date : The Days After March Madness, Time Unknown. The spotlight still shines in Xavier’s face.. His eyes now acclimatised to it and stay open. His eyeballs reflect the harsh lighting, masking his Green irises behind the lashing of White. The stubble on his chin has grown more and his hair is all astray. Some matted with sweat to his forehead, the rest heading off in all directions. Xavier : “You know... This really isn’t the way to make friends!” Still he jests... With his arms tied and his legs bound he has no other defences than humour and cockiness.... A defeated man has not lost until he can no longer laugh, and Kannon looks like he is about to burst into hoots of it just to defy them. The neck of the Scientologist cranes, the muscles burning with tension as he tries to see around to his wrist watch and try to get an indication of when it is. In this place there are no days... Not even hours and minutes. All there are, are seconds.. Long, lingering seconds in which he may blink when he doesn’t have to just to pass the time... Or spend ten writing his initials in the dirt with his loosest food. “You do not need concern yourself with time Mr Kannon... Or should I call you Gold Patron Meritorious?” For the briefest of seconds his guard drops and his face goes expressionless. They know both him and who he is, and if they had done their research, or someone else had talked.. They would know what he knows... They just have to extract what it is. The defences are back up... His clammy face, moist from the hot light is tilted at an angle to minimise the glare into his eyes as he chuckles... As if to deny any slipping of his mask of contempt. Xavier : “Call me anything you want... As long as it isn’t “Baby”.” Both sides are in different positions. Both sides differ in numbers. Both sides differ in goals. Both sides are frustrated. His captors want to know what is in the memory of Xavier Kannon but are being repelled by his resolve. The passing time spent in the same spot looking at himself on a stack of ragtag monitors has not weakened his joy of mockery. But his frustration revolves around the same thing as the universe itself.. Time. He knows every arrogant comment will only lead to another minutes later... Then another. His path of choice has no end. he does not care. “Do you enjoy it here Mr Kannon?” Their questions come in montone. The five figures disguised by the light in the Scientologist’s eyes all sound the same. Cold, dry and male. He doesn’t even try to see them anymore.. For the last hour he has only stared at himself on the screens in front of him... Turning his head to try and find his “Best Side”.. Or licking the crusty blood from the corner of his mouth, guiding his tongue by the view on the monitors. Xavier : “I’m not one to complain you understand, but I’m not to happy with the “Facilities” here... In fact, I’m thinking of calling my Travel Agent.” Their questions are asked with blank faces. His replies come with a smirk. The game is a verbal form of Cricket. Right now the five are bowling and Kannon is doing a great job in bat.. Turning away their questions in quick fire succession and not being fazed by the ones that do slip through. Between the questions are pauses, used for very different purposes by both sides. For the five they are used to confer behind the column of light that sends colours blotches dancing around the Scientologist’s eyes... For Xavier that are used to resolidy his resolve, his faith. He is only in this place because he is a Scientologist, and that is what is going to get him through it. After all, with the corpse 10 metres to his left having bled out over half an hour ago there is nobody else to get the information from. “Your false bravado does not fool us for one second Xavier... You are not a cold machine, you have a life... You have a career.. You have a GIRLFRIEND...” First wicket taken. On the outside the relationship he has with Eleanor may seem weak, fragile and materialistic.. Much like both of the parties involved... But they are wrong. Kannon cares about few things in life but Ellie~! is one of them... One day he will ask her to marry him... One day they will start a family. Xavier : “Yeah I do.... I don’t think you’re her type though... Not rugged enough.” His reply is weak and they know it. The five could have just asked him repeatedly what he knows and out of boredom and frustration he probably would have said... But this strange game amuses him.. It passes the time and adds variety into the stale surroundings of the warehouse in which he is imprisoned. “Would it really hurt you to tell us what you know Mr Kannon? We did not bring you here from St. Louis to be entertained by your rebuttals.... I know it may come as a surprise to you.” Kannon shrugs as much as he can in the restrained conditions of his tied up state. Xavier : “You mean you don’t enjoy our little talks?” Every question he asks is lost in the dark, dusty and stuffy environment in the warehouse where he is being held.. Never replied to. Ending their line of questioning dead only for another to come back at him. Cat and Mouse. Trial and Error. “How do you know that we NEED what you know? We may already have it... This could all be an exercise in the price you pay for keeping the wrong secrets...” Xavier was not expecting that line. No questions from them have really caused him to think.. But this one has. No longer is it now a “Get the Hubbard Lover to spill” mission in his eyes... If what they suggest is true, a fellow Scientologist died, and one is being held against his will for no reason. They scored an own goal. More and more his internal anger wells up... This is all happening because he practices Dianetics.. because he is a Scientologist. How many other things are? Surely they are not the only ones who think lowly of his religion? Do members of PCW crowds dislike him because he is member of a minority religion. Xavier : “Or how about.. This is because you have nothing better to do that kidnap Scientologists.. Not because you hate us... Not because you disagree with us.... But because you FEAR us...” He likes this. He can work with this. Despite only staring at himself and the White light, Xavier knows that with those words he became inquisitor and now he must follow up. Question, reply. Question, reply... That is how it had gone so far but his question broke that and he will stop them returning to form. He teases them in a mocking voice, his eyes beaming and his teeth shining bright in the light as he smirks. Xavier : “You don’t know what we doooooooo....” Still there is no reply from them. Looking at his scruffy face in the screen he nods... A disembodied image of himself congratulating him on his course of action. Now it is not only his cockiness that taunts them.. It is a hybrid of his own arrogance and his religion.. Working in perfect synergy like never before. They brought him here because he is a follower of Hubbard, if they wanted a Scientologist.. They’re sure going to get one. Xavier : “We kill people.. Yeah, lots of people... Men, Women, Children, Animals... Kill. Kill. Kill.... We shoot, we stab, we poison, we strangle... We bore them to death with Battlefield Earth... That last one was a joke by the way.” Apart from his need of a shower.. Nothing now hints to it being Kannon who is the captor. They are on the backfoot. They are silent. Kannon is telling them how it is... And he’s loving it. Xavier : “Government buildings? Yup.. We break into them. Journalist’s houses? Been there, done that, killed the family! This what you wan to hear? We’re BAD people... Ooooh, bad Scientologists, better find out what they are doing!” He is revelling in it. What can they ask him now? They cannot repeat the question he has just jokingly answered. Their one weapon was their desire for knowledge and it has been jammed by jest. The strength given to him by his sense of being done wrong by has made Kannon the victor. As the Scientologist smirks once more there is a flicker of Black in the dusty beam of White light. Xavier : “That what you wanted to hea-UGH.” The grip of a Black metallic pistol cuts through the column of dust and light on it’s way to Kannon’s chin where it connects with a dull thud followed by the soft sound of the Scientologist’s chin dropping onto his upper torso where it hangs limp. He now sees no light. A screech is heard followed by the sound of something soft dropping on concrete and rolling, His eyes open once more but no longer seeing the blinding spotlight or himself in a monitor... He is met by a clear Blue cold March morning sky... From his viewpoint we see the air make steam as it exits his mouth. The Scientologist groans. Xavier : “I feel like a fucking Tramp...” He is now free... An imprisonment that could have cost him so much more has only done one lasting thing to him. The dirt will wash off. The Blood has gone. The aching in his jaw will go. But the fact he was subjected to it because of his religion would not go away. From now on he was proud of his faith. From now on he was going to preach his faith... And now, he was going to assume the worst of everybody. Non-Scientologists kidnapped him... And there are a lot of them around to be told. He missed Jeremy Howard’s call, but they would see each other that Wednesday. One week later Asylum was dead. In just over a month Jeremy Howard was gone. In under 5 months time Xavier was Champion. And the effects of that night live on.
