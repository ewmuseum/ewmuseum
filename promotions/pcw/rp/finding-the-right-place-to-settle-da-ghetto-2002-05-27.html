Finding The Right Place To Settle...DA GHETTO!

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/27/02 Jordan X Shabazz had created N.I.G.G.A; it was something that he thought could eventually be able to rival the NAACP. He already had gotten Jesse Jackson, Al Sharpton, Rush Mason, and OJ Simpson behind him. All he had to do now was find the right amount of faceless thugs that could, perhaps, add some muscle to the fledging organization. The scene opens up in the beautiful town of Decatur, Georgia. Why is Jordan in Decatur? He had convinced a real estate agent from August that he was interested in buying a building in the area, so the agent has decided to meet with Jordan to discuss possible prices, and to find the right place. Anyways, the sun is shining down on the dirty south like a BIATCH. Loads and loads of children run outside in their shorts and no t-shirts playing in the streets were an open sprinkler shoots out water like Niagara Falls. This is obviously, a rough neighborhood. Several ice cream men are out in full force trying to make a quick buck. Drug dealings are going on one side of the street, while parents are walking their babies on the other side of the street. Jordan X Shabazz stands there, smoking a cigar and surveying the action. He wears a black business suit, and some shiny black shoes. He goes into his coat pocket and wipes a bit of sweat that drenches his entire forehead. He leans on a Cadillac Deville, from the plates it's obviously rented. Jordan checks his watch again, 4:31 PM. A white man, wearing a turd brown, ruffled business suit, comes up to Jordan and offers his hand out for a shake. Jordan shakes his head at the man. 'Strictly business dog, I ain't tryin' to shake nobody's hand.' 'No problem sir. I'm Ross Lockett, Reality real estate. I was the one from...' 'Quit playin' games cracka, let's get down to business whitey, I don't have all day,' Jordan says with a tint of anger in his voice. He was dead serious about finding the right building for his company. This was a brand new organization and he badly wanted it to succeed. Ross Lockett nodded quickly and gestured Jordan towards his clunker of a car. A 98' Honda Accord in decent shape. Jordan shook his head and walked towards the car. He opened the passenger side door and got in. Ross did the same, but on the opposite side of the vehicle. The man began driving through the streets of Decatur, Georgia, the heart of the ghetto. He shakes his head as he realizes how out of place he appears. He's the only white man in the area. Jordan can tell the man is uncomfortable; it makes Jordan grin to himself. 'So, uh, Mr. Shabazz, why did you decide to choose Decatur for this building? You could have easily found one in Augusta or Atlanta,' asks the real estate agent Ross Lockett. Careful not to make a mistake of angering Jordan X Shabazz to any degree. 'It's quite simple Mr. Lockett,' Jordan begins, trying to sound very intelligent and business smart. 'This is the lifeblood of the black man. Not all black men can live in a good neighborhood and not have to worry about having drive bys occur at your house. That's why I'm here, I'm here to bring money into the community so adequate police officers can be hired. I'm here to bring jobs into the community.' Sheesh, he didn't ask for your life story Jordan. Ross Lockett nods, trying to comprehend what Jordan just said. As he turns out of the ghetto, or, the really bad ghetto, and into the semi-ghetto of Decatur, something known as 'Middle-Class' throughout America. Ross comes across a bunch of abandoned high-rise buildings. Ross parks the car, as he and Jordan get out. Jordan stares out in awe at the place. 'Hmmmm..... How much would the cheapest building be? Approximately?' Jordan asks. Ross ponders the question, 'At least $40,000.' 'Whoa! That cheap...' Jordan stared at the buildings. They all appeared to be in bad shape, worn down from several years of rain, and hurricanes. There's one building that Jordan is studying greatly. It's completely broken down, it's the ugliest, and in worse shape than any of the other buildings. There are a few homeless people still sleeping on the ground floor. All the windows have been smashed out, graffiti lights up the walls. It appears as if this is a place where a lot of gang activity would take place. Jordan points towards this particular building, and he says, 'I'll take it.' Ross, needless to say, is completely shocked. 'Sir, Mr. Shabazz, is you sure?' Jordan stared a hole through Ross Lockett. 'Typical white man, typical victim of corporate white America. It's YOUR fault wrestlers like myself are booked in meaningless matches with the likes of Mike Frampton. It's YOUR fault that Negroes like myself, and Rush Mason, can't earn a dime in the business of Professional wrestling... Is that fair?' 'I'm not much of a fan myself, so I'm not somebody to talk,' replies Ross Lockett fearfully. Jordan shakes his head and speaks. 'Yes, of course I'm sure. This is where Negro dreams shall be made. People asked me why I chose to put 'Growing Group' in the name it’s rather simple. The African American race is growing, and they need a leader, that's what I'm here for.' Ross Lockett has obviously is no longer listening to Jordan X Shabazz. Jordan is now in a world of his own, he's fantasying about how great it would be to have white slaves. 'Hmmm... But knowing white people they'd probably try to have an uprising and fight their black slave masters... But knowing black people we'd probably smack them and tell them to sit the fuck down...' Ross is beginning to think that Jordan might be a beer can away from a six-pack, so he tries to get the deal done as quickly as possible. 'Okay Mr. Shabazz, I just need you to sign here, here, and initial here.' States Ross Lockett while holding a stack of papers in front of Jordan. Jordan quickly signed and initialed the papers. As he checked out the building, and pictured the possibility of having N.I.G.G.A emblazed on the top of it, he began to laugh aloud. 'This... This is going to be great!' The enthusiasm is apparent on Jordan's face. This new organization may became his savior! ****************************** 40 hours later. The office had been fixed up! Well... Not completely. But furniture had been put on every floor, computers were in the process of being ordered. There were still holes in some parts of the walls, missing windows, and holes in various spots in the floor. But the thirty-floor building was big enough for Jordan to carry out his business. He was using his laptop computer to set up a website on the Internet for N.I.G.G.A, he had recently spoken with Jonnie Cochran, OJ Simpson, Jesse Jackson, and Al Sharpton, about coming to his office to set up their own little offices within it. Everything was going good... Now all he needed was one key ingredient, workers! Jordan needed a group of loyal, dedicated, and trustworthy people to work for him in his office. He needed several secretaries, and several people that would spend time working on paper work. He was going to have a lawyer for underprivileged, wrongly accused, African American prison inmates, and he was going to have a separate lawyer for the family of the all the black men that get shot for reaching towards their wallets. Jordan had put a full-page ad in the New York Times advertising the chance to work for N.I.G.G.A. He expected people of all races to show up. And they did. He had been through a good amount of applicants, 223 to be exact, and now he was reviewing them, trying to find the right workers for his office. 'Hmm... Business degree from Harvard, no criminal record... Oh shit, what's this I see? He's white.' Jordan tosses this poor applicants piece of paper into a waste bin that is almost full to the brim with papers. 'Are these people serious? They think I'm going to pay a white man to work for me? HA!' Jordan scoffs to himself. 'I've only gotten 30 black applicants, and I've hired all of them already... What to do...' Before anything else happens, the door is opened. In walks a man, he appears to be rather wide, approximately 230 pounds. He stands about 5'10, and he wears a black business suit. Jordan immediately recognizes him as Jake Jackson, Jordan's one time manager and lawyer. He's white, but Jordan still carried on a friendship with him. He saved Jordan from serious prison time. 'Jake, what brings you here?' Jordan asks with a surprised look on his face, he didn't expect Jake to just drop on by. 'You need lawyer services for your new office, and I'm here. Also, I'll help you with those applications,' replies Jake while taking a seat in a used and worn brown leather chair. 'Yes, take all of those of the trash can, having white people work for me gives me a sense of power. Power that with the white man working under this black man... I shall allow the PCW and the world to see why the black man's rise to power...IS...NOW!!!' Jordan begins to laugh loudly as Jake Jackson takes the applications out of the trash bin. Fade to black.
