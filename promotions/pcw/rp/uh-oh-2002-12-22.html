Uh oh

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/22/2002 ~The hours are upon us. Decimation, PCW's annual December extravaganza, is stacked from beginning to end with great matches. One of the most competitive matches on this epic card is the Television Championship Match. The current Television Champion, Jon Kano, and the challenger, one Teresa Tomas, have been trading words for the past two weeks with each day adding fuel to the fire. The question is who will come out on top? Jon Kano has the power and size advantage, not to mention experience. Teresa Tomas, on the other hand, has two men in her corner. These two men, Duke Williams and Abner, have pledged their help to Ms. Tomas. Here is the problem. Will Mr. Kano come out on top even with the odds stacked against him? And another question.....Is Duke going to side with his new found drinking partner or is Duke going to side with Kano, his former friend and drinking partner. No matter what happens we are in for a hell of a match.~ *The scene opens up in Jon Kano's locker room backstage at Decimation. Kano is about half dressed into his wrestling attire and seems to be in a state of concentration or has a hangover that would kill a horse. Suddenly, Cal Seaver, PCW's one man paparazzi, bursts into Kano's locker room with a tape recorder in hand.* Kano: Don't you ever knock, you ass jack? Cal: What did you call me? Kano: I called you an ass jack. Cal: Isn't it jackass? Kano: Everyone says jackass, it takes a special kind of person to say ass jack, ass jack. Cal: Anyway, can I have a couple comments from you, Jon, about tonight's big match. Kano: Does it really matter what I answer with? Cal: Not really. Kano: Didn't think so. What the hell. Cal: Now, Jon, you have eluded to the fact that you and Duke were quite the duo in the past and the fact that he may be turning on Teresa Tomas at Decimation. What do you know? Kano: All I am saying is that Duke and I go back, way back. Maybe he is nostalgic and wants to rekindle this friendship, what time like the present? Cal: So have you and Duke talked about it? Kano: Cal, you must have been stunning as a child. Do you actually think that I would tell you if Duke and I had talked about him helping me out? Cal: Well, I got to ask. Kano: Well I got to shit, but I am here talking to you. Cal: Jon, you are getting a litte touchy aren't you? Kano: You'll know when I get touchy because I will be touching you as in I will spear your ass into that wall over there. The time for talk is over. It is showtime. *Kano reaches into his gear bag and pulls out his Television Championship belt and sets in on his lap.* Kano: Teresa has no idea what she has gotten herself into. I am the REAL LEGEND. I am the Man who Rocketh the Mosteth. I am the reigning PCW Television Champion. She has to beat me, and I don't have to beat her. But most of all, Calvin, the ring is my playground and tonight I will show Tomas why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Cal Seaver gets a little uneasy from the sadistic, almost euphoric smile Kano is now showing.*
