'Bob's Story Time'...Even God Makes Mistakes, Part 5

30 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-30-2002 Once more, the shot opens up into the office of God, or Bob as he prefers. Damn, I’d pay a dollar or two to find out how they got a camera into heaven, but I digress. Bob sits on the same couch as when we last left him, only this time, instead of a book, he holds a television remote in his hand. To his left is a huge bowl of popcorn; Also, a rather large television has been added to the office. Bob smiles warmly as the shot zooms in on him, and he holds the remote towards the camera… Bob: Hello once again, my flock! It has been awhile. Today, we are going to try something a little different. It does require quite a lot of energy to turn the pages in this book into a moving story, so I am going to give you guys the recorded version today. That way, I can enjoy the show myself. Anyhoo, when last we were together, Venneman had the extreme misfortune to have been reincarnated as a squirrel. The boys down in research had made yet another mistake. I don’t know why we pay those guys. As you might have guessed, as soon as Vennesquirrel went the way of the pancake, he was right back up here, and mad as hell…. Bob then points the remote at the television and presses a button. The shot pans around and zooms in on the action. Venneman Pendragon is shown storming from the waiting room at the entrance to heaven. He looks rather pissy in a land of pure white happiness. His white steel armor jingles merrily as he walks though, and his rig ht hand rests directly on the hilt of his famed sword Excalibur. Past angels strumming harps and floating on clouds he strides, straight to the huge white building marked RESEARCH. He pushes on the door but finds it locked. He looks up the side of the building and notices a small video camera pointed right at him… “I am in the realm of God, and yet the bureaucracy is no different than that of my realm, Earth. So much red tape to cut through merely to get a simple task accomplished. Lucky for me, I have a sharp blade…” Venneman draws his sword and stares directly into the camera… Venneman: I know not the ways of Heaven, as I am new here and early as well. All I know is that thou art fucking with the wrong Knight here. The situation shall be rectified…. Pendragon draws back and begins to hack at the door with his sword. Soon, a small gash appears and it only serves to make him swing harder. Just as it appears the door is about to fall, it swings open and a force of heaven dwellers in what appear to be snow white riot suits march out to stand between the enraged sword wielder and Peter, the cleric. They stand sternly, shields and clubs at the ready, unsure of how to handle this threat… Peter: That is quite enough. We have done nothing but try to help you and this is how you would repay us… Venneman: Thou art a fool, and thy help is unappreciated. Thy foolish researchers can get nothing correct…. Peter: WAIT! You asked to be sent back at the same time, same place, and MALE! We accomplished all three. What more can you ask for? Venneman: I wish to return as ME! Can thou not understand that? Yonder world is in a sad state without the Lord King Pendragon! What treachery dost thou plan for the good people of the earth that thou wouldst detain one who punishes the wicked? Peter: I have had enough! Guards! Escort the lost one to the jail. He needs to cool off before we continue… The guards move forward, but Pendragon does not wait for their numbers to fall upon him. He rushes in, his sword a blur in the air. Limbs fly with each swing of the mighty blade, and soon the white ground is littered with bloodless limbs. I guess angels can be hacked, but they do not bleed. Pendragon pays the lack of blood no mind as he hacks apart one angel after another. Peter backs away as Pendragon cuts through the ranks separating them. Abruptly, the shot pulls away and we see Bob, one hand in the popcorn bowl, the other holding a beer that we cant quite make the brand of, laughing hysterically. Bob: Oh my…t took those fellows a while to grow their extremities back. Funniest thing I had seen in a long while…Why, a couple of the fellows were nothing more than a torso with a head hopping around for two months! The fun we had with them. Also, it is always nice to see one such as Peter get knocked down a couple of pegs…Excellent bookkeeper, poor with people he is…. The shot zooms back in and we see Pendragon holding Peter by his throat, Excalibur inches from his face. Peter looks pretty well taken down a couple of notches… Venneman: How long does it take to grow a new limb for you people? Peter: Two months is the norm…. Venneman: Each time thou makes a mistake, I shall take a limb… With that, Pendragon hacks off Peter’s right arm. Peter lets out a scream and immediately reaches over to hold the bloodless stump. Venneman: Now. We will try this again. I will walk into the reincarnation chamber, you guys do what you need to do and send me back as ME! A goddamned wrestler and ruler! Is that so difficult? Peter: … Venneman: He who is silent, is thought to concur… Pendragon leads Peter into the huge white building one more time, and they head straight for the reincarnation wing. Pendragon busts through the doors of the “SEND BACK” lab and pushes Peter aside. He strides over and opens the door of the reincarnation booth, which kind of looks like a white telephone booth… Venneman: Remember…Each time thou makes a mistake, I take a limb! Get it right…. He enters and closes the door behind. Peter nods and Venneman is gone in a huge flash of brilliant white light. Peter: Did you get it right? Technician 1: Better come have a look…. Peter comes over and stares into the monitor…. Peter: Aww hell…. The shot switches to show a well built fellow wearing dark glasses, holding a white cane. He stumbles around on a sidewalk, and the passersby point and laugh at the blind fellow who seems fairly lost… “What is this? Why is it dark? What is this in my hand? They had better not have screwed me yet again…” A child approaches the now blind Venneman cautiously and takes his hand… Child: This way sir….Let me help you! “Good CHRIST!” The shot pulls away and we see Bob once more. A pair of empty bottles lie on the floor, and the popcorn is gone… Bob: Well, that is all for today. My people just didn’t seem to be able to get it right! And I played absolutely no part in it… A mischievous grin comes onto his face as the scene starts to fade…. Bob: Be sure to tune in tomorrow my children…
