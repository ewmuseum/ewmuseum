The Fear and Loathing Saga: Neon Parade...

Date: 10/??/02 We landed in Vegas less then a day after we began this strange trip. (I realize now what a incredible choice of words that really is.) The neon lights were nearly enough to send a healthy man into eplileptic shock just by walking down a main drag. I had called for a limo while we were still flying, during the in flight movie that seemed to have starred Tom Hanks, of course the limo will be charged onto the PCW tab... so there should have been no surprise when the thing pulled up.. 'Holy goddess.' Talena uttered under her breath, the limo was jet black and long... very long... we are talking half a small city block long. It was one of those SUV limos that I have been complaining about since they came into existence, 'Pathetic, who needs something that big.' I would always say... I still stand by those convictions. The thing is monstrous and complete overkill, only used by rappers and people with big heads. It also cost something around 290 dollars an hour. Everyone would see me in this thing, it was easier then spray painting 'DarkStar' on the side of the car in glow in the dark paint. 'Wow, it looks like it came out of P'Diddy's nightmares.' I knew what really came out of his nightmares of course, it was generally always that he would have the same fate as R-Kelly. Absent mindidly taping himself having sex with a ugly teenage black girl. That and Walruses. He was deathly scared of Walruses. 'Gavyn...' Talena was having noticable second thoughts, although this was her idea, 'Are you sure about all this? I'm sure there is some loophole that can get your job back.' Surprisingly I found out there wasn't, if it wasn't for the fans of PCW I would be telling this story in fWo right now. Then you could call me Aimz however, and noone wants that. 'Hun, this is the greatest plan you have ever hatched. Even better then when you stabbed that girl in the chest with a pearing knife.' I kissed her in the middle of the forhead and wiped off my smudged black lipstick with a thumb, my thumb of course. 'Everything is going to work out just fine. Now all we need to do is continue our search.' I looked around grandly, the Neon Cowboy, XXX lit up in seventy different ways, debauchary and drug use, prostitution and politics, all of it came to a head in Vegas. I forget exactly where the center of America is. Last time I checked it was somewhere in the 'heartland' of the United States. That isn't the true center though, Vegas is. Everything comes into Vegas to be drunk and forget. Noone else says a word either... unless there is a price. Then they say everything and anything, some of which aren't true. Vegas is the place where fables are born, people are made, and reputations are tarnished. 'I love the smell of neon.'
