Returning To My Roots: Part II - May The Truth Be Told

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13th, 2002 [I needed to speak to Saul, he was the only one I could trust out there with my words. After leaving his home, we jumped into my prelude and just cruised around before going back to my old home. Funny thing is, my home was only a block down, now we ended up almost 10 blocks away from it. I felt a good heart conversation would work out, so we stopped at this Deli, Saul did not feel like going in so we hit the drive-thru and I just ordered a medium sized Coke while Saul bought a Smoke Meat Combo. I let him savor a few bites before we actually got down to some business, although, it was him who sparked up a conversation] Saul: So playa, explain this PCW thing you was talking about back at the home, how does it feel going back to be the rookie? Blayze: I’d rather leave the wrestling thoughts in the back of my mind for now. Plus I told you already, I am already in the hunt for a title, that Rising Star one I briefly detailed you about. Can we drop that topic for now? Saul: Fine, Fine [I got a bit annoyed at Saul pushing me to inform him on my recent wrestling escapades, I felt today was not time.] Saul: Okay then, what is troubling you? You looked and sounded pretty okay inside, now that we are chilling down the road, you are like the calm and collected silent brother. Blayze: Yes, there is something troubling me Saul: You planning on letting me in on it? Blayze: You seem to ask a lot of questions Saul: Is that a problem? [This is the habit I hated about Saul, when he wants to know something, he has to ask at least a dozen questions. Seeing as I already scared his ass once, this time I had to do something to keep him quiet. I am not going to harm him, just play with his mind a bit.] Blayze: Well… [It was going to be funny yet sadistic in my view but it just had to be done. I jammed my fist on the top of the glove compartment, watching the opening flip down and there it was. A Glock 9 in the compartment, newly bought but of course without ammo (which hidden under the driver’s seat). Saul quickly scattered to get out of the car but I had the door on the child’s safety lock.] Saul: Yo I want the fuck out!! Blayze: Chill. It has nothing in it, I just felt I needed something to shut you up for a bit [He gave me an icy cold stare, I tried to hold my laughter.] Saul: Man I swear, you have issues Blayze: And you question too much[I think that shut him up] Blayze: Saul, I have to understand something and it has been bugging me the minute I drove into the main streets of Compton. The looks, the expressions, the greetings, were all different this time around Saul: Well you know brother, people are like weird and shit around these neck of the woods Blayze: This was more then weird, hell, even Mr. Jenkins saluted me differently. He usually rises from his lawn chair and tips his hat off to me, this time he just glanced over his balcony and nodded. Civilians, walking down the sidewalks and pointing fingers at my car with disgusted looks on their faces. I mean, when the N.W.A. were a hit, they went out and iced up their style up and cruised around in corvettes and low-riders, Compton embraced them. Now look at me, I got my suped-up high powered vehicule, a beautiful condominium in San Jose and a fine array of jewelry, I went from rags to riches. I was a criminal, then seeked the refuge of sports entertainment and paid my dues, why do these people now treat me differently? [I knew Saul was going to answer me with something I was not going to like, just by the look in his eyes] Saul: Because the N.W.A. never sold out [I almost put the brakes to the car but instead continued driving, I was a bit enraged] Blayze: So you, my old school homie, think I sold out to my hometown? Saul: I never said you did, I am just saying it seems like you did [We were now only about 15 minutes away from my old home, so before I relived some memories, I wanted to find out what Saul meant by “it seems like I did”] Blayze: Explain Saul: You were respected on these streets, people knew your face right and left and also because you ended up in the news a few times. Anyways, you did the right thing to get your life together, you got into a business where beating down people for the hell of it, creating a soap opera in the process, but you still fixed yourself up. Okay, you came down to Compton a few times back in like 2000 and even gave some cash to help re-open the Martial Arts/Wrestling Dojo once owned by our late admirer Allah Shaheem. After about the middle of 2001, it seemed your ego just got to you man. You never really called, your brother was in jail for arson and barely gave him a minute of your time, and you had promised these certain upgrades to your city with the money you made but nothing came through Blayze: Hey listen I got my damn brother out of jail Saul: So he could become your bodyguard down in that defunct wrestling fed you were in about a few months ago, CCW..CWC..something like that, whatever. Where is he now? I have not heard from him in a month! Blayze: Oh, he got a job in Haiti, but he keeps it on the downlow. [You think I will tell him I left my brother unconscious in the middle of Haiti, with no passport and no cash? Never] Saul: What was going on in your head, actually, what IS going on in that head of yours Jerome? You now always want to be known as “Blayze”, you never ever mention Compton in TV or Magazine interviews and you have given no aid to Compton. Now you just drive on in here showing off what money you got, flashing your expenses, no wonder people give you the evil eye. You are not the same Jerome Jordan you ere only 2 years ago Blayze: I wish I was not hearing this Saul: Someone had to tell you. I was going to let you know anyways, even if you never brought this up Blayze: You are lucky you are a good friend, or I’d have your brains splattered all over your shirt Saul: I know you are not to fond about my words, but you wanted to know what was going on, I let you in on the truth [Maybe he was right, maybe my ego was getting in the way of certain affairs. Although, could it be that he is jealous of me and just trying to make me seem like the villain? I do not know] Blayze: I do not know what to see, can wee discuss more of this inside the house? Saul: Sure [I turned around the corner and parked right infront of my old home. Saul began to tell me that Darren did not sell the house, he actually worked on it a bit to suit his ways. I began to think as Saul and I made our way out of the car and up the front lawn, just wondering if it is really my fault the town is not on my side. As my hand slowly reached the doorknob, I was hoping that once I stepped into my home, that all the horrible thoughts would wash away. I am just hoping, praying, that Saul is actually telling me all he has to tell, and is not hiding any secrets from me. I turned the knob, pushed the door open and walked right into the past…] To Be Continued..
