P.W.A And The Quest For The Holy Grail (Part 2)

7 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07 - 11 - 02 (Due to the boards reluctance to accept yesterday I sent parts 1 and 2 to The End. However part 1 did finally post but somehow re-directs to Akiyama's Prayer role-play, so in saying that please refer to P.W.A's promo archive) Prologue: We last saw the Conniving Collective better known as Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A) entering into the world of organised crime as they made an after show appearance, ironically at the venue scheduled to hold this weeks flagship show 'Friday Night Fusion'. Tagging along reluctantly however was recent acquisition Natalie Miyagawa as the trio embarked on another Gaz Green caper involving a device that will change society, as we know it. Stumbling into the dwelling of Xavier Kannon, the Jester finds the device in question, the Ebonics-Eradicator that took the rest of the party by surprise. A little too startling, the revelation spurned Nerezza into a frenzy, a frenzy in which he held his partner by the throat, demanding answers. 'It's a what?' roared the Angel Of Fear. The scene opened as the Jester's body dangled freely, held high by the satanic goliath Nascosta Ireul Nerezza. The vampire appeared to be in a rage and his patience was wearing thin, enough so to prompt the drooling fangs to draw closer to that of the slender neck of Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'It's an Ebonics-Eradicator!!!' retorted the Conniving Comedian with a subtle grin, the device in question still clenched firmly, even through the events unfolding. No grin, smile or joke was enough to de-rail the Living Nightmare as he tilted his head and focused on taking a blood donation from his once jovial friend who now stares death or worse, an eternal life of damnation as the living dead in the face. Licking away the excess saliva, Nerezza immediately pounced-.... Nerezza's intimate moment however was thwarted by the untimely interruption of Natalie Miyagawa who bellowed to the devious duo. 'Could you two stop fooling about - We have company!' Dropping the deadweight of Gaz Green to the ground with a heavy thud, the vampire could only conjure up a snarl that would forever imbed itself in the mind of his captor, in this case Gaz Green. 'CONSIDER YOURSELF TOUCHED BY LADY LUCK~!' Breathing uncontrollably and wiping the sweat from his brow, Gaz joined the duet of Nerezza and the Russian beauty Natalie Miyagawa as they spectated a light that was drawing nearer. Creeping along the corridor walls like witches fingers it appears as though our trio of high-profiled celebrities may join another in Jason Snow and his residence with the prison system. 'Err-.... Arr-.... Hmm-.... Well-....' spluttered the Jester trying to respond. 'It's a guard, it's a guard doing his rounds-....' continued the Lord Of Laughter fighting the restraints of his windpipe, abused earlier by that of Nascosta Ireul Nerezza. 'AND YOU NEGLECTED TO MENTION IT!!!' hissed the Spawn Of Evil as he oozed with negativity, almost reaching the end of his tether for the second time within minutes. Diving for a second helping of his partner, his best efforts are again thwarted by the courageous Ms. Miyagawa who threw her body mass into the miss-match combination of both Nerezza and Gaz Green. 'YOU TWO CUT IT OUT!!!' The glow of the torch meanwhile had reached the point of illuminating the corridor occupied by our three bumbling hero's, who have no choice but to seek either shelter or safe passage. Stepping hotfooted from the dwelling of one Xavier Kannon, trampling over the debris of door the trio made their escape with an exhibition of stealth. Passing the glass-paneled double doors from earlier it's a question of retracing the team’s steps without drawing the guard’s attention. Meanwhile however the scene sways back to the dumbfounded stares of an African-American attired in that of a security uniform as he exams the team’s aftermath. 'In da name of Allah, what motherfucking white boyz been playin on ma turf?!?' 'Dis be some fucked up shit-... I'm gonna cap them white crackaz in da knee-caps!' murmured the lowly security officer, unbuckling his pistol from his holster and lowering the intensity of his torch. Returning to the Trickster Team, it appears as though the three-piece party have taken a wrong turning into a corridor without a possible roof escape and the option of entering the public seating gallery, which will be filled to capacity during the PCW spectacle of Friday Night Fusion. 'That's another fine mess you have gotten us into, Gaz!' injected the PCW bloodsucker with a touch of the three-stooges even though his facial expression emits nothing but fury and rage. 'That's it blame the guy in green-....' retorted the Emerald Menace before a Natalie Miyagawa intrusion. 'Stop squabbling-.... STOP SQUABBLING~!' Looking as though Natalie has vanquished the heat between her two traveling colleagues, the view switches and captures the security guard posed to shoot with his pistol at arms length. Gaz and Nerezza usher for their valet to turn to the danger and after a little reluctance, she does with a firm step back and arms held high in what can best be described as a gesture of surrender. 'Oh crap! Nothing like this ever happened with Alex-....' whispered the petite Russian, retorted only by the black officer who's lock and loaded, hopefully not trigger-happy however. 'You'se guyz get them hands on your heads where I can fucking seem de'm, oh don't make me momma's boy-....' Staring at the device still firmly clenched in the Jester's hand, the officer reacts. 'What the fuck be dat white boy - put it on da floor and shut the hell up or I'll be getting medieval on your punk ass, believe that!' continued the hired help, finger still covering the weapons trigger. Gaz lowering the device, used his quick thinking and up-rooted a carpet from under the black officer throwing the individual up into the air whilst gravity played the important role of bringing him back down, with a heavy thud mind you. In the comical moment of the officer nursing his injuries the trio stepped out into the arena with two options, either hide in the public seating arrangement or trip the alarms via the fire exits. 'Them fuckers are gonna pay the ultimate price, my boot having a damn date with their asses, let me tell you fucking white biatches!' cried the guard, rubbing his backside as he returned to his vertical base. Chasing the triple-threat, Gaz Green, Natalie Miyagawa and Nerezza into the arena he corners the criminal collective as they fight to open the fire exit. Raising his weapon again the guard issues yet another warning. 'Get da fuck away from them bleedin doors or I'm gonna pack your scrawny white bodies full of lead. So again, I want to see handz and lots of them, not four or five but six fucking hands raised where I can see them-...' With the speed of a western sharp shooter Gaz fired the Ebonics-Eradicator at the black security guard who simply froze, tapping himself down in the search for any physical damage. With the guard temporary incapacitated Nerezza lifted a lighter to the fire prevention system causing a fog of white powder to be emitted from the overhanging devices, shielding the party as they made their final escape. 'Let's get out of here!' screamed Ms. Miyagawa in bated breath. 'NO NEED TO ASK TWICE!!!' replied the P.W.A combination of Gaz Green and Nerezza in unison. Like a runaway locomotive Nerezza thunderously crashed through the fire exits, landing in a heap on the London street and in full view of the on-coming traffic as the alarms bellowed into the night. Regaining his erect status Nerezza and those too on the PCW pay roll disappeared into the heart of the city of London as the guard emerged from dim visibility of the arena. 'Where have them bloody juveniles gone - And what's with my speech, huh?!?' said the security guard clearing his throat and inspecting his person, startled by the approaching sound of fire fighters and their transportation. With all the commotion that continues the scene concludes by drawing to that of a blank screen, signaling the end to another caper involving Primetime Championship Wrestling's Pranks With Attitude.
