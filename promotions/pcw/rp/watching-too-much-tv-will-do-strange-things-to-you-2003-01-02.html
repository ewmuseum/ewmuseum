Watching Too Much TV Will Do Strange Things To You

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Watching Too Much TV Will Do Strange Things To You Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 02/01/03 “Like.... Boooooooooooooooring” With the whiny voice of PCW’s preppy Commissioner Eleanor Kannon-Hall, we fade in on her in bed channel surfing as the TV bathes the otherwise dark room with light and highlighting her pale skin with the screens reflection. Eleanor : “News? Like, what’s that all about?” Her long mane of sandy Blonde hair is tied back ready for sleep and she’s dressed for bed in a Pink vest top and White and Blue stripes pyjama bottoms, the duvet wrapped like a blanket around her as she sits cross-legged. Eleanor : “Why don’t they have a channel that, like, just shows Melrose Place.... They could SO call it the Melrose Place channel.... YAY THAT!” Sill she flicks through the night time TV, going through all the usuals from obscure comedies to bizarre foreign imports and world sports. Eleanor : “WHOA! They’re, like, playing Tennis on a Table and stuff!” Finally she finds something to attract her attention as a couple of Chinese players belt the little plastic ball back and forth at one another almost too fast to follow. Eleanor : “... Wonder what it’s called.” After a few more seconds pass, so does her interest. Eleanor : “Yawn... That is, like, SO 30 seconds ago!” More channels are flicked through, the Kannon-Hall residence seemingly having every channel known to man, none of which is showing much. Eleanor : “Oooohhh, cartoons!” Eventually, the little prep finds something to match her attention span and understanding capabilities. The enjoyment quickly turns to inquisitiveness. Eleanor : “Like, this is WAY not Disney...” There are no celebrity voices, no Simba the Lion Cub or any classic characters... Instead the sound of Japanese voices blare out during a fight scene with subtitles on the bottom on the screen. Eleanor : “This is, like, the coolest thing EVER!” She sits there perplexed, the show reflecting on her complexion. “What are you watching my little Shag Squirrel?” In the poor light, we make out PCW CEO Xavier Kannon standing in the doorway, watching both his wife and the wall mounted television. Eleanor : “Like, pure YAY in TV form!!!” Walking into the room, Xavier keeps his eyes on the screen as he drops down onto the bed and snuggles up to his wife who seems more concerned with trying to read the subtitles in time. Eleanor : “It’s like, got funny voices, people going RHAR! and words all in one!” Kannon takes the top of the duvet and wraps it around his shoulders, both of the couple now encompassed in it’s warmth as he tries to get her attention by gently running his finger tips down the side of her neck with the arm his has around her petite shoulders. Xavier : “Why don’t you turn of the TV and we can...You know?” Despite his amorous attentions, Ellie~!’s eyes don’t move from the screen, numbed to his touch as Kannon sighs in frustration. Eleanor : “It’s like SO watching people fight while listening to some mad guy WAY ramble on and reading a book all at once.... YAY THOSE!!” Kannon’s eyes narrow in bemusement at Ellie~!’s sudden interest in Anime, and her sudden lack of interest in him. Xavier : “You can watch cartoons in the morning...” With his fingers he slowly pushes the few stray strands of Blonde hair away from her neck before leaning in and softly kissing around it. Ellie~!’s shoulders shudder slightly but she shakes it off, still engrossed in the TV. Eleanor : “HELLO?!? Like, SO trying to watch the cool thing here and stuff!” She nudges her shoulders left, knocking Xavier’s forearm away before resting her elbows on her knees and leaning forward to follow the adventures of some Japanese hero fighting off an army of generic martial artists. Eleanor : “Like... WAY hit them and stuff!” As Ellie~! cheers on the hero, Kannon reaches for the remote control resting on her right thigh but she slaps his hand away. Xavier : “So when did you get into Anime anyway?” Finally breaking her concentration, she looks to him with a furrowed brow, puzzled. Eleanor : “An.....Ni.....Me?” The vacuous light Blue eyes that dominate her face look to him in confusion as Xavier gestures back to the screen where the hero is nailing a spin kick with ferocious energy to one of the villain’s hoard’s chin. Xavier : “Anime... As in that thing on TV you’re watching instead of getting Carnal with me?” Her eyes go from the screen to him, then back to the screen and finally rest on him once more. Eleanor : “Um.... What century do YOU, like, live in? That’s a CARTOON, you know, like Bugs Bunny? WHAT’S, like, SO UP DOC? and stuff?” Rolling his eyes, Kannon just watches alongside his wife, not getting into it at all unlike Ellie~! who’s punching along with the hero and giving her own brand of Japanese commentary. Eleanor : “Why don’t you do this instead of wrestling? That’s like, SO passé and this rules more than Burberry on sale!” Slightly annoyed, Kannon grumbles back. Xavier : “Maybe because I’m not a cartoon?” The little prep purses her lips and nods in agreement, arching her eyebrows and exhaling a sigh heavily. Eleanor : “Yup, that’s why he can do all that cool stuff and you can’t!” Giving up on the matter, Kannon just collapses backwards onto the mattress and pulls a pillow under his head, deciding to sleep it out until Ellie~!’s usually short attention span runs out. Xavier : “Turn it down can you my little Poon Puppy...” With his toes, Kannon tickles Ellie~!’s side as she reluctantly goes to turn down the volume but as she does so a crackle of electricity bolds from the TV to the control’s emitter that encompasses Ellie and then Xavier as it travels via his toes, flowing over them before suddenly they vanish into thin air. “Um.... It didn’t get any quieter and stuff....” To be continued.... ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
