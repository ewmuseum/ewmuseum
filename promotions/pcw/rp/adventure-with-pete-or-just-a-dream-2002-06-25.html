Adventure with Pete or Just a Dream?

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 25, 2002 I want to be a champion. I want to know what it is like to hold a title in my arms, watching it glisten in the sunlight. Oh wait, I already know how it feels – I’ve been a champion. So it is with much chagrin that I am walking alongside this deserted road with Sleazer and Pete Rose thinking about winning the Continental title. After all, I’ve been here before. Not the road, mind you, but this situation. You see, a title can be a wonderful thing. It proves that at one point, you were superior to all of the rest, and vindicates your claim as the best. While you may be sitting there for only a day, the point is that you’re there. No need to cry over spilt milk if you lose it the next day. Back to the travels at hand, though, because the amulet still must be found. After speaking with the notorious black suits about the situation, I’ve come to a decision that we must find the amulet first, before Vader can give it to the shapeshifter and cause all of us a lot of grief with Britney Spears dolls. While some of you may like that perverted sense of future, I don’t. Give me furbys over Britney any day. And what the hell is she doing with NASCAR? Tom Lexian: [kicks the dirt while walking] This sucks. I mean, we’re just walking on the side of a deserted road and we have no idea where we’re going. Do you know Pete? Pete Rose: No, but I do wish you would stop kicking that dirt on me. It’s fairly annoying. These are new jeans, you know? Tom Lexian: [kicks more dirt in Pete’s direction] You’re pathetic, Pete. You claim to be this big, bad baseball player, but you can’t even take a little dirt on you? Where’s your uniform? You used to get that dirty all the time diving into second base. Pete Rose: And first base! Let’s not forget my episodes running into catchers at home plate as well. I don’t mind the uniform dirty. That just shows you work hard. But my god, you can’t get designer jeans dirty. It’s un-american! Tom Lexian: Says who? Levi? C’mon Pete, you know you’re not working hard enough if there’s no sweat on your brow and no dirt on your boots. [Pete was quiet and unattentive. I looked back Sleazer who appeared to dragging a large cactus behind him. He had needles poking into his hands, but he didn’t seem to be feeling any pain. Needless to say, I was intrigued.] Tom Lexian: What in god’s name do you think you’re doing, Sleazer? That’s a damn cactus you’re holding! Sleazer: [puts the cactus down, and begins to pick out the needles out of his hand] I know that. I wanted to put it in my room when we get back. I think it would look absolutely lovely next to my N’Sync poster collection. Pete Rose: Did he say N’Sync poster collection? Tom Lexian: No, go back to brooding Pete. [I knew how much Pete just hated N’Sync. Something about all of those boy wonders looking better than him. Well, good god Pete, maybe if you hit the aerobic stair master, you’d have a better shot at the ladies.] Sleazer: I just love plants…[begins petting the cactus]…OWWW! YOOOOOW! God that hurts! Tom Lexian: Sleazer, look at yourself. You’re just inflicting pain. If you wanted to inflict pain on yourself, why don’t you join PCW and join the Extreme division. You can fight against guys like Akiyama and William Powell. Sleazer: [whispering] I don’t know about those guys. Akiyama looks a little scary, you know, what the mask and everything. Tom Lexian: I hate to say it, but I’ve seen Aki without his mask…[whispering back]…it doesn’t look much better there either. But he is human. Sleazer: But what about all that Neo Void stuff? Tom Lexian: I wouldn’t be on the receiving end of that, but we sure could use that against Melvin the dragon. Sleazer: I’ve been thinking Tom, and that’s a rarity for me, but you don’t seem all that worried about this upcoming match in PCW. I mean, you’re up against three other guys in a cage! A CAGE! Tom Lexian: Sleaze, we are embarking on a mission that could cost us our lives. While it’s true that in that squared circle we could be severely hurt and scarred for the rest of our lives, this mission has our entire futures on the line. Not to mention the fact that the dragon could eat us. I don’t want to be eaten, Sleazer. Sleazer: [shaking his head furiously] I don’t want that either, Tom. [I put my arms around the mismatching bastard and we continued to walk towards the borders of Canada. As I had forgotten, R.L. Stine told us that Melvin likes to fly around there. I had my doubts, as that border was choke full of terrorists using Canada’s amazingly lax immigration policy trying to get into the U.S. I was amazed that no one else has seen this dragon just flying over the borders, but you know those border patrol…in one ear and out the other.] Pete Rose: [looking back at me] Tom, do you think Vader will send any more of his goons after us? Tom Lexian: [shrugs] I really hope not. I don’t want to know what kind of resources he has at his whim, but I’m willing to bet they’re pretty good. [Pete made a strange noise, almost like a whimper. I had never seen a baseball player whimper like this, of course, unless you were Barry Bonds and you didn’t have your armor on. Then taking a Roger Clemens pitch to the arm really hurt.] Tom Lexian: Pete, what’s wrong? Pete Rose: Tom, it’s hopeless. We’ll never find this damn dragon, or the amulet, and DARTH VADER is going to kill us. [he sat down on the ground and began to sob] I’ll never make it to the baseball hall of fame, I’ll never see the inside of Cooperstown… Tom Lexian: [grabs Pete] Pete, you need to get a hold of yourself. Stop being so negative. We’re going to get through this! Animal’s Voice: ROOOOOOOOOOOOAR! [We all stopped talking and began to look over into the distance. The large roar belonged to an animal, and it sounded like a very large animal. One that I didn’t want any part of, and I was sure Sleazer and Pete didn’t either. Now, while Pete and I are seasoned athletes, and Sleazer is, well, Sleazer, I didn’t like our chances. Wild animals tended not to communicate well and used instinct instead of common sense. And no, they two are not the same.] Pete Rose: [stammering] Tom…Tom…what was that? Tom Lexian: [looking around] I don’t know, but it sounded big. Very big. Sleazer: Maybe it was a …rabbit? Tom Lexian: Rabbits don’t roar, Sleazer. They hop around, they eat carrots, but they most definitely do not roar. [I began to see a shadow of a large animal in the distance and began to brace myself] Pete, what does that look like to you? Pete Rose: [squints] Looks like a dark, furry object… Tom Lexian: So it sort of looks like Bobby Stormbringer…but I don’t think he runs on all four legs like that. It’s quick like a cheetah but it’s got the size of a… Sleazer: A BULL! [Indeed, it was a bull. It was almost out of a cartoon with a huge bull barreling down at us at top speeds. All I needed now was a red cape and a bulls eye on my chest. It had two huge horns pointed directly in front of it’s head, perfect for ramming and destroying with supreme injustice.] Tom Lexian: Oh dear, that bull is closing on us fast boys… Pete Rose: Can we run? Can’t we just outrun it? Tom Lexian: It’s a bull, Pete. Don’t you remember basic physics? An animal running on four legs can’t outrun a human on two. Besides, we wouldn’t make it two feet before it speared us. Sleazer: [looks over at Tom] This is it, Tom. The end of the road. I’ll never get to change that awful prom night now, Pete won’t make it to the hall of fame, and you won’t win that gold belt thing that you wanted. [I didn’t want to tell him that it was hopeless, but even an optimist like me knows when you need a miracle. It was at around this time that I began to pray. Not to some third party deity or to some golden idol, but to that one voice that’s always looking down at us. It may have sounded corny, but I never gave up that higher power.] Look, I know I haven’t been all that faithful. And I sure do curse a lot. No really, I do. Sometimes I curse out your name, and that’s not cool. But I try to live a decent life, hammering out a hard living and trying to make a name for myself on this beautiful world. While I can’t complain about life here on Earth, I think I could do more. I think all three of us could do more. We’ve all made mistakes, but we can atone for them, if we’re given the chance. That’s all I’m asking for…a chance. [The bull was a few feet away, and was heading for me first. Of course it was. I closed my eyes, hoping it would be over quick….] YEEEEHAAWWWW! [I looked up, not seeing two horn holes in the middle of my body. Much to my surprise, I saw The Crimson and Nightshard now bull riding and causing the bull to veer off into the side of the road. It was like a rodeo, only I wasn’t watching the TNN network. After a few moments, the bull fell down in exhaustion and my two crime fighting friends leapt off of it triumphantly.] Tom Lexian: [amazed] I don’t know what to say. Where did you guys come from? The Crimson: We’ve been following you since you guys left the café. We were using the Crusader jet. [I look up to see the ultra secret, ultra silent Crusader jet hovering above.] Pete Rose: That’s some piece of equipment, Tom. Tom Lexian: You’re referring to the jet, right? Pete Rose: Uh, yeah. Night Shard: We picked up the bull on the Crusader Radar. Looks like Vader is taking some unorthodox means of getting rid of you guys. Sleazer: A bull? The guy sent a bull for us? Tom Lexian: [shrugs] Could have been worse? He could have sent Jonny Trip! This makes things a lot easier. Now we can take the jet up to the Canadian border and find this damn dragon already. Pete Rose: TO THE JET, BATMAN! A few hours later… The Canadian border never looked so beautiful, at least not from my vantage point. That wasn’t saying much, especially since I had never been there before. But don’t take it from me – take it from a man that has traveled all over this country, all over the world, and still hasn’t seen our natural neighbor, Canada. Though I had to admit that the lower border to Mexico wasn’t much either. The border patrol over there was about as strict as a substitute teacher trying to make it through a fifth grade class. I enjoyed watching all of the people just fly over the border as if a dollar bill had been placed in front of them like a carrot, easing them inside to the United States, but that got really old. Flying overhead the Canadian border, I saw a fairly orderly line of cars trying to make it through the border. Most of them were probably good people, just coming back from a long and well deserved trip. Others, well, were illegal immigrants getting into the U.S. You see, Canada has a fairly lax policy on immigrants getting into their country. Some people just show up without a passport or any sort of identification, and they waltz right into Canada with full rights and privileges. Then they just walk on down to the border and cross into the United States. How do I know this? Because I read my news bulletins that come in. You can’t be a responsible super hero without doing that. And Mr. Bill O’Reilly is one of my primary news sources. So while we’re looking for that pesky dragon, I also have my eyes peeled for any other suspicious activities. Suspicious people usually wear sunglasses too. Pete Rose: [wearing a pair of sunglasses] Hey Tom, what do you think of my new pair of sunglasses? Tom Lexian: [looks at Pete with an awkward suspicion] Pete, please take those glasses off. They make me uncomfortable. Pete Rose: [takes the glasses off] You know what I’ve noticed? Sun glasses make you nervous, big wrestlers make you nervous, porcupines make you nervous… Tom Lexian: [interrupting] Porcupines do not make me nervous. Now where did you hear that? It’s absolutely ridiculous. Pete Rose: [points at Night Shard] Robert told me. Night Shard: [shouting from the cockpit] You big mouth! I told you to keep that a secret. That’s the last time I tell you anything. Pete Rose: It was just too funny to resist. So, porcupines huh? Porcupines were the bane of my existence. I mean, they look nice and all, but when you least expect it, they’ll fire back at you with those damn quills of theirs and stick you right between the eyes. And it hurts. No doubt about it, those quills will leave a mark that you will never forget. I understand nature and how we all need some form of defense, but don’t porcupines have some sort of unfair advantage? Armadillos have armor – why don’t we? Tom Lexian: We’re getting off subject, aren’t we? We’re here to find the dragon and that’s all. Pete Rose: Are we sure it’s a dragon we’re looking for? Tom Lexian: [confused] What do you mean? The Black Suits and R.L. Stine both told us that we’re looking for Merlin the dragon. Pete Rose: [trying to hold back his laughter] Well, it could very well be a flying porcupine for all we know. Pete started to crack up, falling to the floor holding his stomach laughing. Oh, this was funny. Laugh at a man who had a fear. I won’t tell you how much this hurt me, but as I vented my anger, I remembered that I was wearing the famed amulet that only The Executioner wears. EUREKA! I am the Executioner, the man assigned to hunt and destroy all vampires deemed dangerous to the human race. And with the amulet, I had powers that no man possessed. I picked up Pete and began twirling him around my finger. Pete Rose: [starting to get sick] Tom, let me down….I’m sorrrrrry…. Sleazer: [touches Tom’s shoulder] Tom, be nice. He was just joking around. I let Pete go and dropped him on the floor. He keeled over and looked like he was going to throw up. Served him right, too. He needed to understand that no matter how large his ego was or how important he thought he was, always remember that I was the leader. Ever since the beginning of my career, I have been the leader of the group. Never a follower, but a leader. Tom Lexian: I’m sorry, Pete, but this entire mission has got me on edge. We’ve only got one shot at this, you know? Pete squirmed from off the ground and nodded his head. I wasn’t sure if he actually understood or if he was just nodding his head so I wouldn’t hurt him anymore. At a best case scenario, it was a combination of the two. He sat down at a chair far, far away from me and began to tend to his neck which had a few scratches on it. I would have to remember to file my nails next time. Sleazer: [leading Tom away] Why don’t you head over to the cockpit and see if Night Shard or Crimson need any help? I’ll see if I can’t cheer Pete up a bit? Tom Lexian: Since when did you become the counselor type? Sleazer: Come on, Tom. My mom was a counselor, my dad was a counselor…hell, my grand father nearly became a counselor, if not for his obsession with dandelions. Then he just became a gardener. Tom Lexian: So why aren’t you a counselor? Or even a gardener? Sleazer: [sighs] Sadly enough, I hate plants and I don’t like talking all that much. But I know when someone needs to talk, and Pete looks like he needs a pep talk. I nodded and watched him head back to Pete. I wondered what they would discuss, knowing that Pete would just talk about his old playing days and Sleazer would sit there half listening. Not my idea of a good time, but then again, I had to go talk to my jobber friends. The cockpit was huge and decorated with gadgets and gizmos that only me and my jobber friends could have designed. There was no steering wheel or rod, but you controlled the jet with your mind. Night Shard was at the helm at the moment with a large head gear on his head. Micro probes were attached to his head. Tom Lexian: That looks fairly uncomfortable. Night Shard: It could be worse. We could have used that groin navigation device that Thinh wanted. The Crimson: The mind is a very delicate organ. Any slip of the mind could cause the ship to go down. It is better to use an organ used less often… Night Shard: Are you saying I don’t get laid often? The Crimson: [sighs] Did I say that? Night Shard: No, but you’re implying it. Tom Lexian: Guys, stop arguing. We have a difficult task at hand… Suddenly, a red light begins to glow on the dash board and made an irritating sound. Tom puts his hands over his ears, as it made Tom’s ear want to explode. Tom Lexian: [screaming] Jesus Christ, what the hell is that? Night Shard: We’ve got an incoming object on an intercept pattern! It’s going to ram us! A large dragon could now be seen from the cockpit. It was the size of three Crusader jets and it’s wings moved tirelessly. It came dangerously close to us and then rammed it’s tail into the side of the jet as we were moving. The ship rocked to it’s left and we all grabbed something to hold on to. Tom Lexian: This is great. Just great. That dragon is going to kill us all! Night Shard: Well, they never said it would be easy. Tom Lexian: Shut up, Robert. The dragon came in for another pass, this time opening it’s mouth wide. Balls of fire began to erupt from it’s mouth and came hurling towards the jet… Tom Lexian: [closing his eyes] LOOK OUT! ---------------------------------------- I woke up in my bed, dripping with sweat and looking like I had just run a 50 mile marathon. I looked around to see myself alone in my bedroom with no dragon, no Pete Rose, no Sleazer, and no other crusaders by my side. Dragon: Well, I wouldn’t say no dragons. I looked to my side to see a mini-dragon sitting in a chair next to the bed. It had green scaly skin, a large snout and…well, you know what a dragon looks like. Tom Lexian: I still must be dreaming. That’s it. This is all a dream. Ha ha. Maybe I got high last night? Dragon: Whether this is a dream or not is not important. The fact is that I am here. Tom Lexian: To do what? Eat me? Dragon: [laughs] No, you see you have a problem. Tom Lexian: Yeah, I dream about dragons and amulets. Dragon: You have no confidence in yourself. You may think you do, but we both know that you’re dead scared to step into the ring with Kannon, Miya, and Duke, aren’t you? Tom Lexian: Don’t be ridiculous. I can handle all three of them. Dragon: Really? Tom Lexian: Okay, fine. So maybe I’m a little nervous. Who wouldn’t be? Miya is a former world champion. I can change that. That means he’s been the best before. How can I compete with that? He’s proven. Duke was my former stable leader. Not just a mate, but a leader! And now I have to step into a cage with him? And don’t get me started Kannon… Dragon: But you created the obscenity to fight Kannon. Tom Lexian: Yeah, but that’s only because I hate that tilde business. It’s so juvenile. Dragon: Sort of like dreaming about time traveling amulets, right? Listen, you’ve had this problem your entire career. You are brillant in the ring, but when the big matches come, you fold like a deck of cards. Stop doing that! Tom Lexian: Is that supposed to make me stop? Dragon: Well, I guess not. But you know what to do in that ring. Just do it. Tom Lexian: [nods] I guess you’re right. Dragon: [gets up] Of course I am. I am an all seeing dragon. Now, go back to sleep and when you wake up you’ll think this was all a dream. Whether it was or not, you decide. The dragon made it’s way out of my room. I couldn’t sleep that night, and I sat in bed till the sun rose. Another day had come, and I realized, maybe it wasn’t a dream after all.
