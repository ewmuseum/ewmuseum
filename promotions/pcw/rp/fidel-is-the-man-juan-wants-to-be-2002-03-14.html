Fidel is the Man Juan Wants to Be

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/02 Time: 8:16 P.M Location: CubaScene: Parking Lot “ Taking Care of Business.” The scene opens with Juan Borro outside in a parking lot. Juan has had a long day in Cuba and just wants to relax a little. He went over to the bottom floor of the parking lot at the hotel he’s staying at, to grab a little smoke and relieve stress. Juan knows, he has loads of things to take care of when he gets back home. He has four matches, a stable, and a love life to take care of. Juan is thinking about loads of things, when he sees a rather tall Cuban man, run by him with sweat dripping from his head. Juan looks over and watches, as the man is shaking and moving frantically. Juan: Hey man, do you need some help or something, you look like you’re a little lost or something. The man turns around and looks at Juan, with a haunted look on his face. Juan wonders why the man is looking at him that way, but before Juan could speak again, the man goes running the other way. Juan: Hey! What are you running from? Juan goes following the man, as he continues, running farther and farther away from him. Juan stops for a few seconds, and watches to man from behind a few trashcans. The man walks into a large warehouse and Juan follows. When in the warehouse, Juan stays right near the door so he can see what’s going on. Out of nowhere he sees five sports cars pulling up by the warehouse. Juan looks at what’s going on, and sees five men come out, and stand in a line together. The men are wearing all dark clothing and smoking cigars. About five minutes later, a large limousine comes pulling up. The driver comes up from the front, to open the door. Out of the door came Fidel Castro. Fidel comes out in the finest clothing, with a smile on his face. The five other men go right by Fidel’s side, as they all enter the large and empty warehouse. Juan makes sure to get down very low behind a pile of mats so nobody coming in can see him. When in the warehouse, the man who was previously nervous and sweating, comes running up to the group of men, falling to his knees in mercy. Frantic Man: Forgive me my great! Please forgive me! I knew nothing about what was going on, it was all a big mistake. The guards push the man away so he isn’t within a proper distance of Fidel Castro. Fidel laughs, and just points at the man. Frantic Man: Please, Fidel. Have mercy on my soul. Fidel signals for his guards to back off a bit, as he goes toe to toe with the frantic styled man. Fidel Castro: You think you can come to my home, and disrespect my people? I know that’s exactly what you thought you could do. Frantic Man: No sir I’d never! Fidel kicks the man straight in the jaw, sending him to the ground, with blood dripping straight from his mouth. Fidel Castro: Don’t lie to me! Meanwhile, Juan is looking on not knowing anything having to do with what’s going on. Juan thinks of leaving, but decides to stay a little longer. Fidel Castro: Are you going to tell me that you weren’t the man, who attempted to steal my hard earned money. Frantic Man: No, I would never do such a thing like that, you have to believe me, please believe me. Fidel Castro: You know, I’m a trusting man and I usually like to be one with my people, but in the end, evidence doesn’t lie. Frantic Man: Evidence? Fidel signals for the man to be brought over to the other side of the warehouse where a television set is. Fidel grabs a tape and puts it into the VCR, as the man waits to see who is on it. What he sees is a videotape of him taking money from the Castro family vault. He takes his mask off for a few seconds, and none other then his mug shot is shown on there, plain and clear. Fidel stares at him for a few seconds, until the frantic man finally breaks down into pure emotion. Frantic Man: I’m sorry, you don’t understand. You see I needed money and I got with the wrong guys, and if I didn’t pay them they were going to kill me. I have a family to take care of and I need the money. Juan looks on, knowing something bad, is bound to happen to the frantic man. Meanwhile, Fidel is just laughing with a smile on his face. Fidel Castro then walks straight up to the young fractic man, and pinches his cheeks. The frantic man thinks he’s going to be let go, but then Fidel’s expression turns to complete anger, as he twists the skin on the frantic man’s face. Frantic Man: AHHHH!!!! The man then gets out of control as the guards go over to restrain him, and throw him back in his seat. Fidel Castro: There were two things I didn’t like about what you said. Can you guess what they were? The man answers still in pain. Frantic Man: I have no idea. Fidel Castro: Oh, I don’t like people who won’t even try and make a guess I find it just foolish, so take a guess. Frantic Man: I’m not sure is it the part about how I needed the money. Fidel: No, it was actually the part about how you got involved with the wrong people anyway. The second part is you did all this, knowing your family is in danger. If you ask me, that’s pretty stupid. Castro puffs smoke from his cigar in the man’s face, as tears start to come from the eyes of the man. Fidel: What’s wrong, are you scared. Frantic Man: Yes, I am. Fidel goes up to the man and whispers in his ear. Fidel: You should be, because I’m going to kill you. Fidel walks away as the man starts screaming. The screams fade away as Fidel walks away. The screams end with a gunshot, as Juan jumps back in fear. Juan waits until all of the men leave, before he does anything. When they are all gone Juan leaves the scene of the crime, and goes back to the hotel where Kevin is sleeping. Juan opens the door loudly, and Kevin jumps up as he is awakened. Juan comes in all nervously as Kevin shows concern. Kevin: Where were you? Juan: It’s a long story, but I saw Fidel, and he killed this guy for disobeying his commands to him. Kevin: Woah, that’s crazy. Juan: No, the crazy part is the rush I felt when he did it, and how he wanted to be Fidel. It’s weird. I WANTED HIS POWER! Kevin: So are you saying, you want to be like FIDEL? Juan: I’m saying, I want to be in total control. Starting now I shall run things with one thing in mind. And that’s to leave being in total control.
