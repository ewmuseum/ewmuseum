Like Flies Around Shit Part Two: Magnum PI and Parrot Shit...

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/6/03 The almost deafening sound of the pirate airship rocked the entire sundeck, causing various wooden chairs to topple over, due to the sheer power of the vibration. Blind Rage, Warrior and Lusty Linda simply stood there, gazing up at the mammoth flying pirate ship, which was hovering above the domed in a decidedly menacing fashion. This was the worst feeling of all for the superhero team - they were helpless. There was no way for them to actually reach the pirate ship, beyond the massive dome, so they had to wait for the pirates to make the first advance, which could cost some of the lives of the crew. “A rock and a hard place,” Blind Rage blurted, causing both Warrior and Linda to glare at him, confused. Blind Rage and Warrior were still wearing their ‘blending in’ costumes, which comprised of identical red Hawaiian shirts, tight denim shorts and a pair of Jesus sandals. Still with their black masks covering them faces, obviously. Linda glared at them both uneasily. “Damn it, if you two are going to have to get changed,” She declared, “I know you are a couple of tossers, but you can’t got out in the public dressed as tossers.” Warrior and Blind Rage looked at each. “Huh?” they replied in unison. “Its public image guys,” Linda began, “If I am to market our trade to the civilian world, the public need to be assured that they will be saved by serious Superheroes, who will give them piece of mind, for thirty bucks an hour.” “So what are you getting at Linda?” Warrior asked, crossing his arms. “I’m telling you to get your asses back to the cabin and get into you’re uniforms, before this party kicks off!” Linda shouted, “I will not have my clients fighting crime, looking like prime suspects from Magnum PI!” “Hey,” Blind Rage warned, his thick finger pointing directly at Linda, “ Magnum PI ruled!” “Yes,” Linda admitted, “Yes. He did. Back in the nineteen eighties,” She paused for a second. “And he was damn good in bed!” “You’ve fucked Tom Selleck as well?” Warrior asked. “NO!” Linda bellowed, “Magnum PI, you deaf bastard!” Again, Warrior and Blind Rage simply glared at each and rolled their eyes. Then the pirate airship appeared to descend and the noise and vibrations increased to a near intolerable level. “Aw damn!” Blind Rage exclaimed, “Looks like it’s just docked on one of the lower levels!” “We ain’t got time to change, we have to go now!” Warrior declared, already making his way to the stairs. “Where would the Pirates go first?” Linda questioned, as she took up pace behind Blind Rage. “Think about it,” Blind Rage replied, “Their pirates. They’ll go to the casino.” * * * * * By the time the heroic duo reached the casino, the room was nothing more than chaos. Civilians were running around screaming, various PCW superstars had took it upon themselves to defend themselves here and there. The pirates were swinging their swords about in a rather threatening manner, and all of the pirates parrots were shitting over people. The two members of Justice Inc strode into the room confidently, and Blind Rage had raised his hand to call for attention, when two men pushed past them at the entrance and ran deep into the fray. They wore masks, one red and one blue. Warrior and Blind Rage recognised them instantly. They watched as the two ‘superheroes’ took about beating the living shit out of the pirates, in a less than eloquent style. It was nothing more than a bar room brawl. “Look at them,” Blind Rage said, “They fight with no style. Just fists and feet!” The red masked man leapt over the craps table, kicked two pirates in the chest, sending them flying into the rather stern wall, and then dived onto a further three, taking out all three. “Well,” Warrior began, “That was kinda cool, but I could of done better!” Blind Rage tapped his buddy on the back, nearly sending him flying. “Of course you could my friend. We are proper superheroes, these are just pretenders. We have real super powers, they just like to brawl. We will prevail!” The superhero duo watched on as the ‘superhero’ in the blue mask saved an elderly woman from getting robbed by a pirate. The elderly woman thanked him. “Don’t worry ma’am,” the man said, “Justice Co is here!” Warrior and Blind Rage glared at each other and laughed. “Who says that?” Warrior asked. “They’ve been watching too many, camp 60’s superhero TV shows!” Blind Rage replied. By now, the majority of the pirates had turned and fled back to their ship, realising that they’d picked the wrong prey this time. The two ‘posing’ superheroes clear up the last of them, via a few stiff kicks and glass bottle over the head of another. Upon the pirates leaving the ship, the superheroes were surrounded by all of the civilians and consequently thanked for their actions. The titanic duo of Blind Rage and Warrior soundly make their way over to the impostor’s position, only to find them both running away via another emergency exit. Blind Rage looked around intently. “Guess who aren’t here?” Blind Rage asked, with a confident smile upon his face. “Uh – Handbag Warrior and Badger Man?” Warrior replied. “No,” Blind Rage said, glaring angrily at Warrior, “Tobey McGuire and Ben Affleck. You know why don’t you?” “No.” Warrior quickly replied. Blind Rage sighed. “BECAUSE TOBEY MCGUIRE AND BEN AFFLECK ARE JUSTICE CO!” Due to his outburst, the entire room glared at Blind Rage, they then looked at each and then finally made their way out of the casino in search for the two actors, who apparently saved their lives.
