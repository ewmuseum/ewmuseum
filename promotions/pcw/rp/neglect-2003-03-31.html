Neglect

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31 March 2003 Days have passed since we last saw the amusing shenanigans of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Jester trying to take onboard a new persona and with his vampire associate playing judge our host went about masquerading these gimmicks. Hilariously he tried a super hero, gangster and zombie which all failed miserably to impress and now slouched deep into his leather reclined chair our green tailored Jester stares fixated to his television set. “How can that be?” Head bowed into his hands he rotates his neck frantically in a pendulum motion, obviously upset by the footage that continues to play. The Emerald Menace’s head rises from its curtain of darkness so that his eyes may once again witness his dying seconds of a singletons life for now his freedom had untimely elapsed. Tailored in a forest green pair of trousers the matching jacket that accompanies this three-piece suit drapes over the side of the chair as his virgin white shirt scruffily hangs loosely, for now the Jester appears the shell of his former self. “There’s been a mistake?” he mutters to himself as he glares wide-eyed at the video evidence of his premature marriage taken from Tuesday Turmoil. The flicking images show the devious doings of the Conniving Collective as they invade and disrupt the secretive wedding ceremony of Chris Storm and his bride-to-be Tessa Windsor. Strike two for Pranks With Attitude who had already ruined the besotted couples first effort at March Madness but the conclusion of this event left a bitter taste in the mouth of the Velvet Trickster. Strike three will be the PCW Tag Team Championships returning home to their rightful owners but the announcement of Mr. and Mrs. Green has now intruded upon our host’s personal life. ”I now pronounce you husband and wife!” murmurs a dog-collared priest draped in black ropes standing cheerfully within the confines of a drive-through chapel, his head gaping through a hole in the wall. The angelic face of Gaz Green cringes to that of disgust as the television continues with what maybe deemed as the worst day of his life. ”You may kiss the bride!” jovially declares the priest as Tessa and Gaz fathom the events that have unfolded with the organ of laughter in the background being bellowed by the vampire who can’t retain his personal opinions on this matter. The solemn face that now adorns the camera is that of the PCW comic who appears sickened by the aftermath of what was suppose to be just another Tuesday Turmoil. However the events that did transpire have now hit both teams hard and their priorities shifted for now this feud isn’t just about the PCW Tag Team Championships but their intertwined relationships. As it stands, Chris Storm wants to marry Tessa Windsor who by accident is now married to Pranks With Attitude’s Gaz Green who alongside his partner Nascosto Ireul Nerezza wants to reclaim the PCW Tag Team Championships, currently held by Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. As the Jester drowns in self-pity his eyes still rooted to the television set Natalia Miyagawa idly strolls by in the background. Clad in a coat that showcases her trim figure the brunette has two small suitcases in toe as she makes a beeline for the apartment exit. Unnoticed by her client she creaks open the door and mutters her fair-well speech before vanishing. “I’m going out!” she murmurs, acknowledged only by a grunt from its recipient. The door soon closed and through the letterbox a small envelope protrudes before falling down on the cold wooden flooring. The flickering of images on the television set soon fizzles out to leave nothing but a static screen of black and white. In a flimsy attempt Gaz Green reached for the remote control, which catapults to the ground with a thud and the ting that follows is the casing parting to allow the alkaline batteries to escape. “Nothing’s going right for me…” injects the comedian as he reflects further on his situation. Leaning over the side of his leather reclined perch he grasps at the runaway batteries and after some half-hearted retrievals he finally has them in the palm of his hand where he goes about repairing the device. Not the most mind boggling of puzzles he concludes and with a tap of his finger the screen flashes before leaving nothing but a blank piece of glass in the images place. “Maybe Rezz knows some legal loop hole for my dilemma?” Picking his bodyweight up from its seated position he lethargically ventures over to the telephone and taking hold of the receiver he lifts it into place, his nimble fingers waving over the dials like a receptionist at her computer. Several seconds elapse when suddenly the Jester suffered the syndrome of second thoughts, remembering the hysterics of his partner during that fateful night where the Green family took a daughter-in-law. - BANG – The sound of crashing plastic on plastic was that of the receiver being put back in its rightful place. Now already on his feet, Gaz Green roamed aimlessly around his pent house apartment coincidentally situated in his native Las Vegas Nevada and with hawk like eyes he spots the white envelope nestled on the floor and like any curious person he ventured over. “It’s addressed to me?” Spotting the obvious for the envelope had ‘To Gaz’ scribbled along the front. Savagely tearing the envelope open the Lord Of Laughter removed the content of a letter and unfolding it open he inspected, his eyes swaying side to side with the lines of print. To Gaz, Like all good things, they eventually come to an end. Some people would argue that why not sooner but even I would admit that this ride has been fun, as you would put it, a roller coaster. Well in truth I’ve enjoyed my time with Primetime Championship Wrestling and don’t regret either marrying Alex or taking you as a client but the time has now came to terminate this relationship. I could never play second fiddle to another and now you have Tessa to keep the home fires burning. I can hear you now saying you didn’t want it but neither did you want Nerezza holding me captive, Seriously I take things you say with a pinch of salt but I don’t hold any bad blood and wish you luck in all your future endeavours for one day we may meet again. Love, Natalia (Nat) With a huge gulp the Jester digested the news knowing full well that his week had gone from bad to worse and things will likely deteriorate further before things get better. A man renowned for having the last laugh, surely the boot was on the other foot and this news was the dagger being buried a little deeper as he staggered over to his favourite chair where he sat sulking. “Why me?” he winced. Sitting in sombre the scene slowly drew to a close. - The End -
