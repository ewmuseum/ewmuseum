Departing.

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27-05-03 A Brief insight. All through our lives we all have one occupation, which sticks out as being our main occupation for life. We live with this priority until we feel fit to give it up; we work hard at our career to make it as illustrious as we can. We use our occupations to pay for our finances and to provide for our families but some occupations do not pay as well as others. Today in our fervent society there are big demands from citizens to become lawyers, doctors and mainly jobs that have quite the stirring pay load but the one thing that many of these people forget is that we need qualifications from school to be what we want to be. Some people do not know what they want to be and stick with a job until they see fit to leave it. One job that not many people will have experienced and it is mainly pivoted in Spain is that of a bullfighter. You do not see many bullfighters around today but when you consequently come across one it is a real delight. Bullfighters do not need qualifications from school but do need qualifications on their part as they would need to be intrepid and full of valour despite their known size. A Little Spanish Bullfighter. We look at the silhouette of the burning sun as we eventually come across a small, skinny and not very well built figure, his name, Sergio Bullent. His occupation, number one bullfighter in Spain. Sergio is shown sitting outside the old bullfighting arena in the heart of Madrid, his hometown in Spain. The little Spaniard is as always wearing his traditional Spanish white frilly shirt and a pair of black bullfighting trousers with a red belt. “So many incumbent memories come from here. I fought many a bull in this immense arena and was only hurt seriously once in my time as a bullfighter and I find that in itself as a grand achievement.” Sergio renounces as he sits on the marble stool outside of the immensely popular Madrid arena. The little Spaniard then walks cautiously towards a plaque hanging on the wall dedicated to his achievements in bullfighting and fervently reads over the plaque. “This plaque was presented to Sergio Bullent for his commitment to the bullfighting society. Sergio was one of if not the best bullfighter to grace this arena,” “That really does bring back some memories, even though my bullfighting days are not over just yet.” Sergio smiles and rubs his chin. The little Spaniard then walks across the open yard and spots his old friend sitting on a seat in the middle of the yard. Sergio then walks conveniently towards him. “Yo Carlos, how are you these days, Holmes?” Sergio sits down beside his friend and shakes his hand. “Sergio, its great to see you again, my friend. I heard that you where going away for a while and working with a professional wrestling company?” Sergio’s face lights up, “Carlos, that is true but in case you are wondering if I am going to return back to my hometown and my country, I will don’t worry about it.” The two Spaniards get off the pebble bench and begin to walk contemptuously towards the entrance of the old Madrid arena. “Remember the times we had in here Sergio, taking down those bulls.” Sergio laughs and nods at Carlos. “Remember that time the bull caught me on my ribs and ripped the flesh off me, that was sore esse.” Carlos laughs a bit at Sergio’s comments, “How could I not forget, one minute you had that bull pinned to the ground and the next it had its horn stuck into you, do you still feel effects from it now?” Carlos questions Sergio as the two enter the courtyard of the Madrid arena. “Well, from time to time I get cramps around that region, but nothing to be worried about, I’m a tough little Spaniard.” Sergio laughs as Carlos joins in with him. The two men walk out into the middle of the courtyard and admire the view around them. “Say Sergio, how are you going to cope in this wrestling company, your just built of sticks.” The two engage in a bit of banter. “ Carlos, I may be small and light but there is no one who is quicker on their feet or with their hands than me, sure I need to be quick to get out of the way of those bulls!” The two then walk across the courtyard and go into the café for something to eat. Later that Day Sergio Bullent arrives at Madrid airport and looks around him at the scenery that he will be leaving behind. The plane wasn’t due for another hour and a half so Sergio walks into the men’s lavatory to cool off. On his way to there Sergio begins to fill up with tears as his emotions run wild inside of him for he is leaving his hometown. Sergio reaches the men’s lavatory quickly before anyone could spot him sulking. “Have I made the right decision, do I really want to leave my friends and family for a wrestling company. Sure the pay is good but is it what I was always made for, is it my destiny. I get to see new sights around the world on the USO tour and my debut is in Kuwait, where I have never been before in my life, though I do fear for my safety after all of the bombings and terrorism happening over in that part of the world.” Sergio turns on the cold-water tap and puts the plug into the sink. Sergio stares at himself in the mirror while the sink fills up. “Carlos did have a point. I’m very small and not very well built; I could get seriously hurt or even killed by some of the larger wrestlers in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Hell, I think I will be the smallest person on the roster at this moment and already I am booked for a match. The sink fills up with cold water as Sergio puts his face down parallel with the ice cold water and uses his hands to splash water over his face to cool himself down from the humid Spanish weather. “Menolly Xenos, that’s her name. The person I will make my debut against will be this woman. From what I hear she is a very accomplished wrestler in many ways. Apparently she is also making her debut at Turmoil so the pressure will be on the both of us. Maybe I will just be able to pull this one off. Sergio pulls the plug out of the sink as the ice-cold water releases down through the plughole as if to be chasing something to satisfy its needs. “My height and weight though are the only attributes that I entail that worry me. All my family and friends have said that I am too small and that I will be destroyed as soon as I stepped into the ring, but as a true Spaniard I am going to prove them all wrong and make a career for myself in professional wrestling.” The little Spaniard then wipes his face dry as he continues to stare into the mirror situated in front of him. “Maybe I have found a career for myself, bullfighting was fun and full of exhilaration but so is professional wrestling. I only hope for my sake that I have learned enough to make an impact. My life has been full of ups and downs, but now I believe it is taking a turn for the best.” Plane 33456 for Kuwait is now departing from terminal 22. Sergio Bullent rushes out of the men’s lavatory and through a crowd of people. Eventually he reaches terminal twenty two and disappears up through the tunnel.
