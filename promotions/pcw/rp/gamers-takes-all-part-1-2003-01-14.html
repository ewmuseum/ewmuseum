Gamers Takes All...Part 1

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Gamers Takes All...Part 1 Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 1-14-2003 ..::/::..Our story begins in glorious City Mall, it's halls hustling and bustling with activity as people of different races, creeds, and agendas partake of their favorite stores...from Warm Subject to Grababootie & Pinch. The City mall is not void of single store that would attract any citizens within it's bounds...and the same holds true for one VERY special shopper indeed...none other then oure very own spandex superhero of PCW...RENT-A-HERO!! He is walking proud and tall, chest poked out with that all important heroic pride as he passes by random citizens with a few shopping bags in hand. He is decked out in a full body spandex replica of alternate Superman, which would be the black and white version, complete with a matching mask as he traveres through the mounds of people with a smile tattooed upon his face. He looks to the side, noticing the camera before exclaiming loudly throughout the mall..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'TIME OUT!!!' ..::/::..In an amazing turn of events, all actions within the mall stop dead in their tracks...water still suspended in air from splashing by little children, people frozen in the middle of dinners, and conversations frozen in time. All the while, our hero moves about freely as he uses a male shopper as an arm rest before addressing his beloved audience..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Well hello there my Rent-Heads...I'm sure your all wondering why I seem to be so filled with exuberance and pride this very day. Well as it turns out...today is the day I pick up my VERY OWN copy of....NEVERQUEST ONLINE!!'Singing Voices:'HALLEJULLAH!!! HALLEJULLAH!!!' Rent-A-Hero:'That's strange....NEVERQUEST ONLINE!!!' Singing Voices:'HALLEJULLAH!!! HALLEJULLAH!!!' ..::/::..Our hero matches the voices to a group of elderly, unfrozen choir singers amongst the crowd of dead frozen shoppers. Rent-A-Hero folds his arms in annoyance and taps his foot, scolding the elderly choir singers as he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Alright, knock it off ladies...your supposed to be frozen like EVERYONE else! Come on...get freezing.' Choir Lady:'Oh your no fun.' ..::/::..The group of choir ladies froze as instructed, our hero cooling down as he looks back to the camera and continues his speech..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'As I was saying...I'm here to pick up my copy of....NEVERQUEST ONLINE..' ..::/::..Our hero stops mid sentence to catch any singing if any....relieved to find none accompanying the utterance of 'Neverquest Online' as he continues..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'I've already bought the network super online adaptor, Neverquest Guidebook, and Neverquest Elven Language Decoder ring....all I need is this game, and I will experience hours of gaming bliss with other gamers allover the world. Unfortuantely...I'm not pleased with with their inability to add network settings for Krypton...hence I'm just using the City. Nothing can stop me now faithful viewers....the game shall be mine. TIME IN!!'..::/::..All that was unfrozen reanimates itself again, our hero parading through the other customers until he is in front of gamers heaven...GAMERS PARADISE. Our hero drools uncontrollably at the sight of the walls filled with Gamecube, Playstation 2, X-Box, and countless other system peripherals, systems, and of course...games. The store is festively decorated in the esscence of a beach, with foam palm trees and the workers adorned in surfer-type clothing as they proceed to help the customers within. Our hero steps foot within the store, looking up to the heavens and saying..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Thank you God...' ..::/::..Our hero enters the store, immediately going straight to the counter where he finds a husky young man with Coke-bottle frame type glasses and a 'Remember Your Roots' shirt with a Nintendo Entertainment System on the front. He looks unimpressed by the sight of our hero, letting out a slight sneer as he shovels a cheese covered nacho in his mouth from the nacho tray sitting on the counter. Our hero smiles, asking polietely..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'I'm here to pick up a copy of....NEVERQUEST ONLINE...' Choir:'HALLEJULLA...' Rent-A-Hero:'Excuse me...' ..::/::..Our hero grabs a roll of masking tape from the clerk's counter, rushing off screen for only a moment as loud sounds of tape being unraveled screech throughout the store. The camera turns to outside, where we find four choir ladies wrapped tightly with brown masking tape, rent-A-Hero cockily smiling as he twirls the tape roll around his finger..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'No choir lady can hope to stand up to my SUPER SAIEN SCOTCH TAPE POWERS!!!' ..::/::..He places the tape back on the clerk's desk, taking in a deep breath before stating what he came for yet again...::/::...Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY GAME SALES my good man, I hope to attain a copy of the newly released Neverquest Online please!' Clerk:'Well....we've got just ONE COPY LEFT....too bad I've all ready reserved it for myself! So I guess you'll have to wait a little longer my spandex enwrapped friend.' ..::/::..Our hero's eyes blaze with anger...waiting over 6 months for this game to be released, only to be cheated by the clerk. Our hero jumps back, pointing his finger angrily at the clerk as he exclaims, alarming all within the store..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'YOU ARE SUCH A RULE BREAKER YOU ROTUND CLERK OF VIDEO GAME SALES MAN YOU!! IT'S UNSPOKEN GAMERS WORD THAT GAME CLERKS CAN'T RESERVE GAMES FOR THEMSELVES!! INJUSTICE I SAY!!'..::/::..The clerk sits up from his stool, his rotund frame standing tall as he takes his plate of nachos in hand, devouring yet aother as he replies with the cheesiest of breath..::/::.. Clerk:'How dare you try to use the GAMERS RULES, against me! I am also aware that game clerks are aloud to reserve games...AS LONG AS THEY PICK THEM UP AFTER WORK, NO POCKETING BEFORE! Brush up on your rules little spandex boy!' Rent-A-Hero:'I STILL CALL INJUSTICE YOU CROOKED CLERK YOU!! I CHALLENGE YOU!!' *~COLLECTIVE GASP BY CROWD~* ..::/::..The gaming crowd within the store as well as the other clerks gasp in shock at the challenge by our hero. The clerk's eyes squint in a intimidating fashion as he rounds the counter while keeping his eyes locked upon our hero. He stands face to face with him, sitting his nacho plate down as the gaming crowd surrounds the two...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'If I win....I want my game PLUS a copy of Grand Theft Auto Vice City, two memory cards, AND....that clear blue PS2 sitting up there on the shelf....' *~GASP YET AGAIN~* ..::/::..The crowd murmurs and talk among themselves, the stakes high as they eye the bounty of the clear blue PS2 sitting at the top of the 'Hot Games' display case behind the counter. The veryinnards that power the PS2 can be seen within, the blue casing a rarity and usually only available on import...::/:::..Clerk:'That's quite the hefty bounty there my friend...but what will I get in return for this challenge?'Rent-A-Hero:'If I don't win....then I am prepared to sacrifice...'..::/::..Our hero places his shopping bags down, opening one in particular as he unearths it's contents....this prize so rare and sweet that a brilliant light glows from it....a special light only true gamers can see....and the light glows for...A LIMITED EDITION ARTBOOK OF ALL THE FEMALES OF COMICS, VIDEOGAMES, AND ANIME!!!..::/::..*~COLLECTIVE GAMER MALE ORGASM~* ..::/:..Some of the gamers falls to their knees and bow in it's mere presence, other can't help but break down and cry at the sight of such a special book, our hero gripping it with every intention not to lose it. The clerk eyes it with want, and utter lust, knowing the stakes have just gotten higher at its sight...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Lemnar....Ranma....Elektra...Catwoman...Lara Croft...just a few of hundreds of drawn and animated females this book offers...an Alpha Gamer males dream. How bad do you want it?' Clerk:'Your on spandex boy...prepare to lose your precious book. And after I claim it, I will be sure to laminate each and every page before I whack off repeatedly to its contents. Is it a bet...'..::/::..With his coveted Neverquest Online on the line....the chance to own quite possibly one of the greatest games in history....along with the bounty of GTA Vice City, two memory cards, AND a coveted clear blue PS2...there is only one thing Rent-A-Hero CAN say as countless other game freaks watch on, wondering if he'll bet the Holy Bible of animated women against the crooked clerk...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Groovy...' ..::/::..The crowd cheers, RAH and the Clerk staring one another down as the challenge is set to go down within Gamers Paradise....the mall will NEVER be the same again. WHAT CHALLENGES WILL BE IN STORE FOR RENT-A-HERO IN THIS ULTIMATE GAMER BATTLE?! WILL HE CLAIM THE BOUNTY...OR LOSE HIS COVETED BOOK?! WILL NEVER QUEST ONLINE... Lady Choir:'HALLEJULLAH...' WILL THOSE DAMNED LADIES EVER SHUT UP?! FIND OUT NEXT TIME....SAME RENT-A-TIME...SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
