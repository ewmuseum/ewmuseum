OG speaks to LeBlanc

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/30/02 Scene shows the inside of Karl LeBlanc's locker room. Warden Riley paces the floor nervously with a cell phone pressed against his ear. In the corner, a trainer tapes LeBlanc's hands. The Swamp Dawg has a huge dip packed in his lower lip and is spitting into an empty beer bottle. 'OG' Orpheus Grant sits next to LeBlanc, pumping him up before the big title match. OG: Diss is it, brutha. Tonite you git ta f*ck dat mutha f*cka up! Jason Core ain't got shit on you nigga... dat white hairy ass mofo don't deserve no title round his waist! 'Specially no Extreme Title. Look at chew... you da baddest, scariest lookin SOB I eva seen, dawg! Look, I know you don't like hangin wit deez white folks... I don't like it neitha... but you gots ta learn ta deal wit it. Trust me, dawg, da Boss will take of you! LeBlanc sits emotionless. The permanent scowl on his face remains in tact. OG wonders if he's getting through to the stoic canjun monster. OG: Come on, nigga... I know you hear me. Why da f*ck don't you never talk? Listen, you gots to open up man. It's coo bein' a bad ass and all, but you ain't gonna git no where in PCW if ya don't work da stick. You gots ta talk, brutha... you gots ta git on dat mike and let da whole world know what you thinkin'. Let em know who you are, and let em know whose ass you gonna kick and how you gonna kick it, yo! LeBlanc glances up at OG, for the first time making eye contact with someone. OG springs out of his chair and claps. OG: Dat's it biatch! I knew you was listenin'... finally we gettin somewhere. Aight, now... like I was sayin, when you get inside dat ring tonite, I want you to light dat mutha f*ckin Core up! Dis battle done gone on too long... end it man! End dat mutha f*cka... NO prisoners, don't be playin' just tear him da f*ck apart. Git yo belt back nigga... git it back round yo waist where it belongs! Show dat cocksucka what hardcore is all 'bout. LeBlanc sneers and slams his fist into the palm of his other hand. He nods his head up and down and he leaps up from the training table. LeBlanc lets out a roar and pounds his fist against an ice machine. OG hops up and down, shouting with LeBlanc. The two 'niggas' shove each other back and forth. LeBlanc is fired up and ready for blood. Jason Core's blood. Tonight, LeBlanc is primed to recapture his Extreme Title. Warden (talking on his cell phone): Ok, come on in and administer the sedative... I know, I know... I dont want to do it either. I wanna see LeBlanc f*ckin kill Jason Core! I'd like to get a few shots in on that SOB myself, but we have to be somewhat responsible here. If we don't administer a small dose of the sedative before LeBlanc goes out their in public, then we'll ALL be fired! Get in here so we can head out towards the ring... all we need is the absolute minimum requirement. Scene fades to black as Warden Riley flips his cell phone closed.
