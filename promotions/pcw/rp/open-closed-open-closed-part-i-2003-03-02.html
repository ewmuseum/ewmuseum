Open, Closed, Open, Closed part I

Date: 02/03/03 Things, were working. Working! A PCW contract apparently tides you over better than I could have imagined. Furnishings were in their right places. Seats go there, poles go over here. Finally project: Temptations was off the ground. It was higher than off the ground, it was ‘in the air.’ Panic was in the air as what was a shamble turned more into a strip club day by day. Staff were hired, useless bastard’s fired and once again I repeat everything id in order. It feels great. Now all I have to do is deal with the perverts and sit back and relax. After the prep talk and room inspection. -Then- arrange cleaning staff. Finally just sit back and relax and watch things flow into place. Relaxation Jade paces back and forward’s as something close to fifteen people stand in front of the line she treads into the floor. Jade’s arms are rested behind her back and her bubble chin is lifted. She looks over the line, which is a mixture of scantily clothed ladies, two men in black suits and ear pieces and a few casual/smartly dressed men. “You understand what you are to be doing tonight I presume?” The line spoke a comprehensible and enthused ‘Yes.’ Jade nodded her head and the people got to where they were supposed to be. Jade let out a heavy breath and leant against the bar her cheek squashed slightly by her fist. “I’d hoped Carys would have been here for support tonight.” “She’ll be fine. She has a real fighting future ahead of her Jade. There is no need to worry about her; you should be having fun, excited about tonight. Relax.” A black man rested his back against the bar as he pushed a raven colored dreadlock out of his face. Jade tried to force a smile. Damien pushed a little harder. “Why don’t you have a little drink to steady your nerves?” Jade shook her head but smiled anyway. “Hey. Do you remember the first day Carys was in here? That palaver was hilarious when you look back on it.” Jade rested her ass against the bar stool and thought back. “Yeah I do remember that come to think about it. I’m not sure it was so funny though Damien. I bet she doesn’t think it was. I do not however, regret it.” -[Flash]- “OK sit here young one!” Jade ordered, here tone slightly scaring the timid girl she is now talking to. The girl however does as she says and sits down on the four foot bar stool. The chair creaks quietly even under her small frame. Jade stands on the other side of the bar and leans on her forearms, her large cleavage squeezes in between her arms. The girl attempts to concentrate solely on her face. “What is your name?” Jade insists on getting answers from her. She leans forward more. The girl seems awful nervous by Jade’s hindrance. Little beads of sweat form at her brow, it slowly trickles down her face. Jade interrogates her at full power. “Carys! My name is Carys. OK?” The girl, Carys forced her words out in an accent not commonly used in the USA, an accent from London England, mostly referred to as ‘Cockney.’ Jade smiled; almost enthused by the fact that she had finally got the answer from the young Carys “Now would you like to tell me why you’re here?” Jade enquired in a soft tone. “I work here as a stripper. Mani hired me just last week. Say, where is Mani? What has happened to him?” Jade places her head in her hand and sighs resentfully. “How old are you?” Carys looks down at the ground. “18 years old.” Carys mumbled; Jade’s finger extends outwards and forces her eyes to meet with Jade’s. “How OLD are you?” Jade bellows, a dark; almost crimpled figure opens the door. “The girl is 16 years of age!” It was Carys’ father. Jade pulled away her hand. “You are fucking insolent, stupid little bitches like you get their legs broken!” Jade’s fists clenched around the rim of Carys’ jumper and lift her off her chair. “Do you realize what kind of damage that could cause my reputation, my business? Get out of here! Now, you stupid little fucker and DON’T come back, not when you ARE 18, NEVER!” Jade’s rage floods out of her mouth as her chest heaves up and down. Her left index finger pointed out of the door. The young girl was in tears. -[Fade]- Hours had passed. Dirty old men had begun lining up outside to see Jade’s assortment of fine treats dancing around a metallic pole. The front of the building did not lack the extravagance which was inside. There was huge metal rigging surrounding the edges holding spot lights, speakers and Pyrotechnics. Jade did like going over board and this was no different from the norm. The men were chanting like fierce warriors waiting for a meal, or a kill, the shouted ‘Let us in! Let us in!’ Finally the blue spotlights came on and began tracing a make shift stage. Jade was already on the darkened stage and as soon as the lights hit her frame the white pyrotechnics shot up form behind her. Her arms were raised and her head was leant back enjoying the thrill of the moment. The men began jeering as she began to make a little speech. “Now come on boys I am sure you can wait a little longer for a lady like myself to finish a little talk with you all on the rules. I am sure most of you will be regulars here so I think now would be a good time. There will be no touching of my ladies. They touch you and that is if -they- want to. If one of my ladies is harmed in any way I will do some severe harming right back at you. There will be absolutely -no- sexual mischievousness in my club. There are no weapons or narcotics allowed on the premises either. If anyone is seen to break these most basic of rules they will be taken away from the building and beaten into a bloody heap of membrane. That being said everybody have a great night and begin spending!” The fans cheered as Jade cut the ribbon and opened the doors, as soon as Jade herself was in the building the security guards pulled closer together. Jade grabbed herself a glass of champagne and was well on her way to having a night of fun and frolics, sipping away at a large bottle of Moet. Jade made her way across the dance floor and sat tolerantly on the other edge of the bar. She sat in the shadows where she felt most comfortable to waiting like a paranoid junkie for something to go wrong. She knew she would not have to wait long for things to begin messing up. Strobe lights began flickering as Damien, Jade’s chief of security made his way towards the door. “I did not order any strobe lights! Something is terribly wrong here. Look how the colors clash! No way.” Damien let a tall dark muscled figure walk into the club. The light reflected from his head the exact same way it did on his machine gun. “Is that a Machine gun?!” Jade fell to the floor and prepared herself. “Everybody get down to the floor!” She screamed. Following the dark cloaked men were a series of much smaller men. Each had their own barrage of guns at the ready and in full view. Damien grabbed a gun from the large man and grinned, two of his golden teeth showed clearly. “You are a treacherous filthy son of a bitch!” Jade hissed, still not knowing who the larger, more masked person was. Most of the people had fallen to the ground but the ones who took their time were riddle with lead bullets for their troubles. The man who held the machine gun removes his mask and stares into the crowd of dead, alive and severely injured people. “Where are you whore?!” The man screamed. “Shit! Is that Mani?” Jade whispered while she was on her knees. “Hit the light’s guys. Then bring me the skank!” The men shot out every single light which they were capable of and pulled out torches. -[Fade]-
