Date: 05/12/02 Let's have a brief look back at how you and I met... Word had been out for a while that someone was going to make the hit on Angelo Deville. Some decided he was leaking a little too much information out by not only pretending to be a mobster on TV, but also being one when the cameras weren't shooting. Some were just massive fans of Xavier Kannon who disliked the outcome of Alliances. Some were rivals, looking to take care of some 'family business'. Either way, it meant that I would have more than one un-named, unknown opponent this week. Aside from Mr. Psycho's pairing with this mysterious partner, I would now have to contend with another. Information was sketchy at best, costing me whatever the seller set... unless the price triggered a little anger. A lead came up, it didn't leave me much to go on... two numbers to be precise. Forty-Seven. They said you were good. They said you were one of, if not THE best. Smart, cunning, resourceful, a hell of a physical specimen. Hell, seeing things from how you took it, I'm shocked you didn't do better. You waited until it was dark, having the tip-off that Deville was meeting a planted contact somewhere near the park's pond. I was right outside your window, listening to the phone call. Make your way to the top of the old Ice-Cream factory where your equipment was waiting, then intercept Deville while the false contact kept him talking. Perfect plan, I must say. Let me already say that the man who walked out there to talk with Deville is an associate of mine. Your plant is probably pondering how to change his hairstyle back to normal right about now after 240 volts of pure electricity. Depending on if he tried to fight back or not, he might turn up at your funeral. In the meantime, my partner's escorting your target to safety. All I had to do was watch you and follow you then... I slipped into the trunk of your little black car, waiting until the engine started. I'm used to being in complete darkness... I just needed you to stop the car and give me my cue. As soon as your door slammed, I just let myself out... simple to do if you have the strength and patience. I crawled out of the oily mess and listened for your footsteps. As I stood up, you were busy, driving piano wire into some poor soul's neck. I was hiding behind your car, watching you kill that person as they got out of their own car. I saw you take their uniform and their badge, watched as you bundled their body inside their own car and then walked off, cool as a cucumber. I was tailing you, zig-zagging behind as many of the parked vehicles in the parking lot as I could. And that's where we split up. You were so busy, so focused on your mission that you didn't see or hear me. It was child's play as you went in through the employee's entrance, trying to hide that stupid little barcode on your head with your pathetic hat. I was already scaling the wall up the side, ascending faster than any staircase or elevator. It was just a case of the waiting game then. You had a massive billboard as cover for yourself and a nice, clear shot of the park. The night was peaceful and silent. Chilling, but still a sea of serenity. I took a moment to look up at the sky and it's lovely shades of dark blue... then you ruined my moment. You'd have more blood on your hands and now you wore the uniform of a security guard instead of factory worker. Directly in front of you was a small case, containing a rifle. If Gaz and i had swapped places, he'd probably have rigged it with a laughing gas bomb. I decided to parody you from behind. I took out my own little oak case. You opened yours. I opened mine. You begun to piece together your rifle, bit by bit, locking the mechanisms together into place. I was connecting and locking my own weapon. We snapped our pieces together at exactly the same moment, so you wouldn't hear mine. Then when you slid the stock into place, I snapped the blade on. Perfect. You begun to take aim and so did I. One blank bullet inside your gun had already made your mission a failure. And I couldn't really tell, seeing I wasn't the one behind the scope, but if that had been a real bullet, you'd probably have made the hit. But the second you pulled the trigger and nothing came out, you fell backwards and all that sweet, sweet blood began to pour all over the roof, dripping and flowing and - 'REZZ!!' 'Yes, Gaz?' I turned and saw my partner, annoyed at me. I'd been caught red handed. 'Squire, I've told you once and I'll tell you for the last time - STOP REPROGRAMMING MY DAMN GAMES!'
