::~Eternals Eleven~:: (Part III - Rockumentary)

30 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/30/03 The scene opens in a small secluded band room in Leeds City centre. The Three members of ‘Ice Age’; Shane Wethers, Jay Bo’ and Brad Taylor sit in the room opposite a small clueless interviewer. The interviewer is approximately 5’6, thin and looks like he hasn’t had a shave in a number of weeks. His baggy Farrar trousers hang loosely over his scruffy black steel toe boots and his shirt looks like something out of the retro. The band is dressed in their usual attire to maintain the typical punk rock image that they have been so well known to portray. Shane is dressed in black baggy denim skater pants, wearing a white long sleeved t-shirt under his charcoal coloured t-shirt that says ‘Housewife’ in bold pink letters. Jay and Brad are in pretty much the same clothes, apart from Jay’s look a little extreme, which is expected of a lead guitarist. The band sit opposite the interviewer who begins to fidget like a small child in a toyshop, not looking very calm or professional. “Um…er…the…er…..er….interview will begin on the count of three” says the interviewer as he scratches his head. The band looks on with a puzzled expression.“3……er…um….2…….er..” the band nod their head with the rhythm of his countdown, waiting for the last number which he seems to have so much trouble spitting out.“o…….um….er….” the tension grows in the room as the band continue to stare at the interviewer in anticipation of the last number.The interviewer pauses for a moment, taking a breath and wiping the sweat from his for head. “guh….guh…guh….uh….uhhhhh……guh-guh-guh” splutters the interviewer, still making a meal out of his noticeable stutter. Jay glances at Shane with a look of disbelief.“ONE!!!!” he suddenly screams!“Action!” Shouts the relieved cameraman, peaking out from behind the large camera. “Hello, I’m Donny Oswaldonman…” he begins in stuttering tone. Shane leans over to Jay.“he can pronounce a name as queer as that but he can’t count down from three?” Shane whispers as Jay shrugs. “….I’m here with the Yorkshire bad boys, Ice Age. Now for you people who are not familiar with them, they are one of the biggest bands to come out of the United Kingdom since the Spice Girls.” The reporter says with zest. Shane sits forward in an attempt to hit the reporter but his reluctant band members restrain him. “Now boys, it’s been a peculiar year for you both, with so many changes arising you seem to be back in business.” Declares the reporter, “but what everyone is wondering is how are you dealing with the death of your last production manager? We hear he had a great input into your last album hitting platinum”Shane sighs and nods his head, leaning forward. “Yeah it kinda sucks” Shane replies in a baffled tone, “it’s not everyday your production manager falls into his own blender, usually I’d say that’s pretty impossible but since I watched Spinal Tap, its made things very understandable” The reporter nods his head in the usual empathetic way, as if he really gives a shit.“that really sounds bad” the reporter replies, stating the plain obvious.“That’s not even the half of it” adds Shane in a tormented tone, “His remains landed on the floor and the dog ate them”.The reporter gasps in disbelief before replying “His wife must have been devastated” he says, but Shane shakes his head looking into thin air with sorry eyes. “No…the dog ate her aswell”The expression of disbelief on the reporters face grows as he tries to believe what he has just heard. He tries to speak but no words seem to escape from his weary mouth.“Their son wanted to bury something to remind of him of them being together…as one, so they fed the dog laxatives and wait until it shit his parents out” The baffled reporter stares wide-eyed at Shane as Jay leans forward.“But things got worse, the dog seemed to get a sort of high on the amount of laxatives it was given and it ended up eating their grandparents aswell.” The whole room becomes silent as everyone tries to figure out whether to be shocked or disturbed by the unusual fate. Suddenly the reporter changes the subject with a slight hesitation. “Er…anyway” he mumbles, “E-Fest is coming soon and you’ve already announced some of the bands that will be at the concert” The reporter holds up a sky blue poster to face the camera. The poster is full of band names written in bold white letters, the reporter grins with a Cheshire cat smile that even Diamond Dallas page would be envious of. “There’s some great names her such as ‘The Foo Fighters’, ‘Tenacious D’ and ‘Blink 182’. All the ingredients to make the concert bigger than Woodstock!” he declares like some over-whelmed kid with tickets to see the rock show of his life. “Don’t forget ‘Ice Age’,” Shane adds, “those bands would be nothing with out us” The reporter nods his head and agrees “Well guys that should be good…” the reporter begins to state until interrupted by Shane. “Should be?? Should be?” Shane quizzes as if the bewildered reporter had been smoking the good stuff back stage. “This is going to be the concert of the century, me and the rest of the guys are multi-platinum selling artist and Ice Age is about to embark on its greatest time” Shane states in a frustrated tone. “We’ll make Ozzfest like a Brit…”Shane turns and spits on the floor, adding to his statement with emphasis, “…-ney Spears dance class” The Reporter nods his head, trying to calm down the almost fuming Shane Wethers.“I’m sorry if I offended you Mr Wethers, I’m just a little nervous” he announces, “My mom is watching at home and she said if I don’t do good then she’ll let my little brother have my play station” Shane looks the dumb-founded reporter in the eye whilst sighing heavily “You’re a real fag, dude.” Shane replies. The reporter goes red with embarrassment as he swallows hard and tries to cover up his fear. “Er….uh….er….ummmm” he stumbles “would you hurry up and end this interview already” Shane yells, “damn porky pig” “Er…thanks for joining us Ice Age…um thanks” the reporter finishes. Cut! Wethers stands as the backstage camera staff rush around making changes. Shane yawns and looks at his watch as a shadow comes forward from the distance. The face appears to be of none other than Mikey Fishmore, wearing a black pin-striped suit and shiny black ‘Ben Sherman’ loafers. “A’right Guv!” Mikey asks, whilst straightening out his cuff. “What the hells with all these interviews? And why does there seem to be someone always walking out of a Shadow?” Shane asks whilst shaking his head “Anyone would think we are living in a frickin movie” Mikey laughs as they begin to walk towards the door “Kid, you come out with some strange comments” Mikey states with humour in his voice. “That’s what keeps me on the pay roll” he replies with a smirk, “now c’mon… Eternal Eleven is gonna form by itself y’know!”
