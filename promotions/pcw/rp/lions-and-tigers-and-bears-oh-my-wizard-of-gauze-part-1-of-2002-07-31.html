Lions and Tigers and Bears...Oh MY! (Wizard of Gauze part 1 of ?

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-31-2002 (The scene opens to show Venneman Pendragon sitting his horse in what would appear to be an extremely foreign land. He is clad in golden chain mail, as usual, and the PCW Rising Star Title is strapped firmly around his waist. The flowers and trees in the landscape are all over shaped and rather happy looking, and a road paved with yellow bricks seems to stretch into oblivion. Xerxes looks a tad nervous, but Venneman is stone calm, as normal. He looks around and sighs before giving Xerxes a nudge and moving on down the sunlight splashed road...) 'I don't understand it. How do I always manage to get myself into these predicaments? It seems like only yesterday I was like a wee lad once more playing in the sand. And this day has already been distressing enough for a man who should rightfully rule. Fined ten thousand dollars for not putting out the final installment of my promotional PCW tour. Ahh well. That last one will never see the light of day. It just isn't noble...' (*******FLASH******** The scene changes to a shot of the Wrestle Vessel: Swimming pool area. Children play in the pool, and the women wear next to nothing as they sunbathe, hoping to catch the eye of some well muscled and rich wrestler. From off screen, a few bursts of laughter and some crude comments can be heard. What the hell is going on? From the right, Venneman Pendragon comes walking over to the pool. He is clad in a pair of what appear to be Speedo's made from Chain-mail armor, a sleeveless chain-mail shirt, and two bright orange inflatable armbands. He walks towards the pool amidst a chorus of laughter and points from everyone watching. Finally, realizing the taunts and the humor originate with his get up, he turns bright red and storms off, ignoring the raucous crowd. *******FLASH******* The scene switches back to Pendragon atop Xerxes. Pendragon shudders a little as the scene rolls through his mind...) 'At least that little bit of film will never see the light of day. Good thing too. I don't need anything like that to ever fall into the wrong hands. I KNEW the arm bands were too much...Ahh well. It matters not now. What matters is, I am still the champ, and, as is becoming the norm for me, I have absolutely NO IDEA where the hell that I am. I guess I better do a little investigating, although this place seems oddly familiar...' (Pendragon keeps on down the yellow brick road...Damn this place looks familiar. The first landmark he comes to is a man made of metal. Well, a man in as much as it's shape. The figure is rather smallish to be a man, perhaps a large child. The figure seems to be frozen in a position that has it lying on it's back with a look of pain etched upon its face. Pendragon reins up and cocks his head sideways as he looks at the figure.) Venneman: Can you speak? What is this place? Are you in pain? (The metal man points one finger at an oil can that sits on the ground at his feet. Evidently the finger is all he can move. Pendragon nods and jumps down from his horse. He picks up the can and squirts a little in the metal man's mouth. The man works his jaw a hair and begins to speak...) MetalMan: Thank you! Oh my you don't know how long it has been since I last opened my mouth! WOO HOOO!!!! (Venneman holds off on applying oil to any other frozen parts on the metal man, cautious as always...) Venneman: And who would you be? And where in the hell am I? This is ridiculous. One minute I am heading for home after the wrestle vessel pay per view, the next minute I am here, riding down a yellow brick road. Now...Before I let you get up and start moving, you better start talking. (The metal man cracks a smile, damn, he is rather smallish. He then begins to talk in happy tones. If a couple of explanations are all he needs to give in order to move again, he is glad to.) Metalman: First off, I guess I should introduce myself. My name is Psycho, and I am a metal man... (Venneman's eyes light up; finally, something he recognizes...) Venneman: How do you spell that? I know a Syko...And much like you, he appears to have the body and the mind of a small child. An overdeveloped sense of stupidity, but otherwise childlike. Psycho: With a P...I know your predicament; we are not of the same mold, he and I, so you have nothing to worry about from me here in the merry old land of Gauze. Gauze, cause after that attack on Sunday, you have GOT to be wrapped in it. Why the hell else would you be here? Venneman: Sometimes I am merely unlucky. Anyhow...The land of Gauze...Where might this land be situated, geographically speaking? Psycho: Why, over the rainbow, silly. Now are you going to oil me up, or what? (Reluctantly, Venneman applies oil to the joints of Psycho the Metal Man. Psycho climbs to his feet. He tests his limbs before dancing a little step across the shot....) Psycho: Excellent. Now...Ride that way, I assure you I can keep up. (Venneman reluctantly saddles up, and the two head down the road together. If only Venneman were wearing Ruby boots, but he is not.) 'I gotta admit. Psycho was not a whole lot of help; besides, I really didn't trust him. No matter how you spell it, a Syko is a Psycho.' Venneman: So let me get this straight; you say I am dreaming? That after I was attacked on the Wrestle Vessel, I have gone to sleep and woke up here? Psycho: Precisely. Your mind has created a way for you to deal with all of the events that have happened to you recently. Funny that you would pick me to be your guide through this, uh, world. Venneman: Why not? I have been seeing an awful lot of the 'real' Syko here as of late. Seems like PCW management wants us well acquainted. I have dispatched him twice this week already, but like a roach he is damned hard to get rid of. And here you are again.... (Psycho starts to protest, but Venneman waves him off...) Venneman: I know, you are not him. I don't buy thew whole spiel about this being a dream. I remember everything after the PPV. After all, it WAS Syko who attacked me. He stunned me and dazed me a little, but hell! At five feet nothing and a buck o' five he is nothing for the Lord King to worry about. And once more, that brings me back to you. Where are you taking me? (Psycho looks a little nervous at the attitude that Pendragon has unveiled. He looks around to see if anyone might see him, but all that look back are trees. He timidly replies...) Psycho: We knew you would be here. We were, 'told'. There is a man here who controls everything. If you need something, you ask him. Sort of a Wizard. Venneman: A wizard? As in the days of old....Be it Merlin? The world is in dire need of heroes such as he... Psycho: Merlin? Umm, no...Much more powerful. He is unnamed here, and he is ruthless. I'll get you there though. Don't you worry. (Venneman shakes his head at the small man of metal.) Venneman: Look. In do watch some TV, and I am pretty sure that I can find this 'Wizard' on my own. You are free to go your way, Psycho. (A forlorn look comes over the face of Psycho, and he fervently shakes his head...) Psycho: Our way lies together, Venn.... Venneman: Lord Pendragon. Psycho: Ahem..Lord Pendragon...You see, I too seek the Wizard. For though Syko and are different, we do share some things, and I guess we are similar in some traits as well. I mean, though I do try, I cannot wrestle. It is the way that I am, and you have seen that he is that way as well. Venneman: He does possess very little talent. So why do ye seek the Wizard? Psycho: Because I am old, and I need a new gimmick. One that has talent. Perhaps if I can change, the other Syko can change as well. Maybe we can at last be something that deserves RESPECT!!! (Venneman nods. He almost understands although the burden of greatness is heavy upon him. As the two walk on down the road, Psycho starts up a song. Hell, you should have guessed it would be a musical, reader...) Psycho: 'I seek the Wizard...What do I look for? Not a new gizzard...For the time has come for me to change, and for my life to rearrange.... I tire of living in fear, of getting, kicked in the rear... I know that I might have a ball, if only I were not so SMALL!!!! A new gimmick! One that's right! One that might, win the night! The Wizard must help me, for I am but a whelp...ME! The Psycho, the weakling, the putz.......... Venneman: The putz, oh come now...You are not like him. With honor you show me the way! How can I digress, but I now must protest, this song's gone on quite long enough. (And with that, Pendragon kills the musical outburst. He continues on in a gentler tone...) Venneman: I do not think the Wizard can change the fact that you have the body of a small girl. I mean, I am sure in my world Tyler Nelson at one point tried to get Syko to bulk up. Some men are just not meant to be strong, or big, or even wrestlers for that matter. They are better suited for doing that which they are more formed to do...Carry towels...Act as a paperweight....You get the idea. (A couple of tears well up in the eyes of the metal man. He looks crushed...) Venneman: Aww, what am I saying. Perhaps the Wizard will be able to help. You might be turned into a man yet. Psycho: That's it! I was unsure how to put it, but that is it exactly! I want to ask the Wizard to give me the body of a man! I am tired of having the body of a Metal Boy. It is truly embarrassing! Yeah. I want to be a man...Be watchful though, Sire....The trip is long, and the sights shall be odd. And watch out for goblins. (Venneman shakes his head again and heels Xerxes into a trot. Psycho easily keeps pace as Pendragon stares ahead, taking in everything that the strange landscape has to offer.) Venneman: Damn, Xerxes....I don't think we're in Kansas anymore.... (Xerxes neighs his agreement, and the scene slowly fades on the trio as they go deeper into the strange lands....) >>>>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
