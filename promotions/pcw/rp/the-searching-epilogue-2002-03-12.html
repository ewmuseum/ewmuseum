'The Searching' - Epilogue

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-12-02 * * * 'Every search for a hero must begin with something every hero requires. A villian.' -from Mission Impossible: 2 --3-04-02, last Monday night, 10:39 pm, during the main event of PCW March Madness-- SMACK!!! Our scene fades up from black in a rush. Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer also fades up from black in a rush. Courtesy of one cheek-reddening slap right across the face from his wonderful brother. B.S. crosses his eyes and then squeezes them shut as he tries his damnedest to get his blue eyes readjusted to the light. Before he opens them up again he quickly realizes he's in some sort of an office chair. Not only that, but Mr. Prime Time is also handcuffed from each wrist to the resting arms of the chair. RANDY'S VOICE: Wake up G*ddamnit! I bet you would regret missing this. B.S. blinks a few times enough to see.. or not to see.. his surroundings. Everything is dark and the only source of light in the medium-sized room is streaming from a television monitor. By the look of the monitor B.S. can deduce some things while a few tears flow from him during his attempt to get his eyes back open quickly. BS THOUGHTS: I'm still in the arena.. and this is someone's dressing room, because only dressing rooms and staff offices have monitors. But who's dressing room is this..? If it was mine then Kira and Carl would be tied up right alongside me. B.S. squints and looks off to the opposite side of him that Randy's voice came from. No one is there whatsoever to Bobby's relief. B.S. turns his head back towards Randy, confirming his identity. The Showstopper sighs heavily and Randy glances his way for it. STORMBRINGER: Of course it's you. Hello there, brother. Randy pulls the chair he's sitting in right next to Bobby's chair against the wall. Randy sits down and smiles sadistically towards his bound sibling. In five days Randy knows all too well that he will reveal to Bobby and the rest of the world in a unique twist of fate that Aimz (formerly of the NTICW now) is their half sister! Different mothers, same father. In the process Bobby will be let in on the not-so-little secret that the biological father of the Stormbringer twins is not deceased and passed on after all.. but very much alive. On a minor side note, B.S. will also soon learn that in a strange sense he is half-Canadian. Randy idly drops an arm around his brother's neck and shoulders as he points straight to the monitor's screen. His speaking is slurred as if he is chewing on gum. RANDY: Hey look. I think that your meaningless match is down to its last four or five participants. Let us see if your tiny sacrifice pays off, shall we?? Randy reaches over with his free hand and taps a button on the monitor a few times. As a result the volume of the set becomes much louder. B.S. sighs disgustedly at the fact that Randy's got his arm around him like the fool is actually tying to be a true brother. Bobby sees no choice but to play Randy's game and watch along. Mr. Prime Time somehow focuses on the action. Mexi looks at Miya as he starts to stir and boots him back down to the mat. Meanwhile Boda has Snow in the corner of the ring when Chase charges in with a clothesline! However, Boda side steps and Chase sends Snow over the top rope and he lands awkwardly on his left leg. Snow has been eliminated!!! DAVE: Snow looks like he just twisted his left knee there! The shot switches back to the short-haired and long-haired dopplegangers wincing and groaning at the grotesque angle of Snow's leg. Bobby's shock and nausia at the sight is for real. We can assume Randy's wasn't as he just starts laughing and pointing at the screen afterwards. B.S. slowly turns his head in horror at Randy. STORMBRINGER: What in the holy hell is the matter with you!? Chase looks down at Snow writing in pain and with a look of shock on his face as Mexi pulls Miya to his feet and leads him to into the corner and starts to pull him up on the turnbuckle with him. Randy finally quiets himself and then responds after a few seconds of watching the program. Randy looks back at B.S. with sick and almost giddy eyes. Giddy at the coincidence of it. RANDY: That same f*ckin' thing happened to you! What was it, ninety eight or so?? You were eliminated in a battle royal, your second match on TV anywhere ever. You planted wrong on your left leg. Torn ACL in da knee. Torn MCL to boot! Out of it for eleven months after rehabbing like a demon. What a recreation! They even got the leg right. Or is that left? HAH HAHAA! Oh look, there goes Chase. Too bad, seems like a hell of a guy. STORMBRINGER: Whatevah- just shaddup.. brings back bad memories but Snow is the one that's suffering. Hopefully he'll have a more minor injury than I did. But damn did that look nasty! For once, show some compassion for goodness' sakes. Your jokes are still as lame as ever, by the way. RANDY: Shh! Look! STORMBRINGER: Wha- oww! Stop that you nutcase!! Randy pulls Bobby over into a sneak attack of a noogie, of all things! Randy lets him go and finally stops pestering B.S. for the moment. Bobby snarls up at Randy and then looks back to the monitor. Miyagawa is still in there! But wait... where are Darkstar and Xavier!? STORMBRINGER: What the hell- Mexi!?! RANDY: Yyyyyyup. (singing) Asylum is gonna get screw-ewwwed, Asylum is gonna get screw-eww- STORMBRINGER: Shaddup already! What are you? Six years old? RANDY: Eight, I'll have you know! B.S. sighs Dave Kern-style. Speaking of Kern- DAVE: We are down to three men! Boda spins around and looks at Mexi who smiles psychotically before leaping off the top turnbuckle and chokeslaming Miya through the table. STORMBRINGER: NOO! YOU SON OF A BITCH! He didn't even try to win!? The White Mexican and Miyagawa have been eliminated!!! DUKE: Mexi just eliminated himself and Miya to give Boda the world championship! Boda stands in shock at what his friend just did and walks around the ring in a daze as the crowd cheers him on. CROWD: BODA!!! BODA!!! BODA!!! STORMBRINGER: Boda instead of Jason Snow? I think I'm gonna be freggin' sick. No really. I- RANDY: You mean it? STORMBRINGER: ..shaddup I said.. B.S. lowers his head, dejected that his sacrifice for a stablemate came so close to succeeding, but not close enough. B.S. grits his teeth and responds through those gritted teeth, his head still lowered and facing the floor. STORMBRINGER: Mexi.. Mexi he- He ripped me off!! RANDY: What was that, Mr. Ego Time..? STORMBRINGER: Keep it up and I'll bite you. He saw what I did for Miyagawa. I can't believe it! We all had to draw random numbers. He must of taken Nelson's spot. I bet that sumb!tch wouldn't of taken it if Nelson drew number two like Blunderboy did... RANDY: So you are saying that Mexi 'sacrificed himself' for Boda because he saw you do the same thing for Miyagawa. StORMBRINGER: Damn straight I am! Damn straight he did, too. This is insane- what about DS n' Kannon!? RANDY: Ha ha ha, they sucked up the joint big time, brother. STORMBRINGER: What? Why!? RANDY: All three of your punkass teammates got schooled royally by Boda. Your Scientology buddy got thrown out in three minutes! Randy starts snorting and snickering as you can almost hear Ellie~! from off in the distance, screaming: 'And.... Eight.... Seconds!!' STORMBRINGER: No.. no no no NO!! RANDY: Do not worry, brother. I am sure you had no chance of winning regardless. STORMBRINGER: Gee. Thanks. RANDY: You are welcome. Thanks for the 'family bonding'. See you at the next Pay-Per-View. Of course.. and beyond. Time to go back on home to your little b!tch now. Randy gets up out of his seat without warning and he switches off the monitor. This plunges them in complete and utter darkness. STORMBRINGER: Randy? Randy what are you up to now!? Freggin' answer m- We hear the cold metal of Bobby Stormbringer's own sledgehammer slamming into the side of its master's head as we fade to black. Once again our scene coincides with The Showstopper fading to black as well.... {Song Change} * * * --3-07-02, last Friday early morning, 12:08 am, five days ago-- Our scene fades up on a top-down view of Bobby Stormbringer and Kira York lying together in his bed, over the quilt while fully clothed. Shorts and tank tops, the usual Florida gear. Both of their tank tops are white while Bobby's shorts are blue. Kira's are black. Both of the young lovers are just poking eachother in the ribs and trying to get the loudest giggles possible from eachother. Unfortunately every once in a while Kira gets a little too.. how should I say.. 'rambunctious' for Bobby. Some pokes are a little rough and a little too well placed. Bobby grimaces in pain at the last one. STORMBRINGER: Errrgh.. KIRA: Hehe, I'm sorry. STORMBRINGER: Heh. Are not. KIRA: Something Randy did? STORMBRINGER: Nah. He likes to bop me upside the head for some reason. That's Maxwell's Northern Lights into the cage, I think. I got my arse handed to me by him and End. I eliminated myself in the battle royal. But look at the result. Bobby smirks while Kira kisses his cheek and smiles over to him. KIRA: Jeremy is still the Continental Champion. You helped him free his brother. Not to mention because of you and lots of heart on his part, Miya got runner-up in the battle royal. STORMBRINGER: I see what you're saying. Because he was the MVP of the match- KIRA: He got his one-on-one match and now he's World Champion. Thanks in part to you! Kira cutely pokes Bobby in a safe area of his tummy as she says 'you' to him. The Showstopper snickers and recoils at the unexpected poke. STORMBRINGER: (fake whining) Sttoooooopp. He does owe me a TV title shot for that selflessness though. If he can hang on to it. Plus lets not forge tthat Darkstar had his dirty hands in the win. KIRA: I wonder what Talena thinks about Gayvn's change of heart lately.. STORMBRINGER: Hmm. That's a good question. Plus he screwed me over at Turmoil and I have never had NOTHING but respect for that bastid. His wife too. Or girlfriend, whatevah they are. Doesnm't make sense. I guess he finds Xavier more attractive than me and he wants to butt bud- ERR, 'pal' around with him. Hey, that's fine by me. KIRA: Are you calling him... 'bi'? STORMBRINGER: Goths are freaky like that, babe. Nothing would surpsise me now. (Seinfeld impression) Not that there's anything wrong with that! KIRA: Lots of people sure have been trying to use you or beat you up lately. STORMBRINGER: That's alright. I'm The Showstopper. Showstoppers don't forget things. I'll make them all pay. From LeBlanc to Maxwell. From bottom to top. KIRA: There's more than one?? STORMBRINGER: Psst.. Shawn Michaels.. KIRA: Oh! Oh, okay. Don't you mean Kannon at the top instead of Drake? STORMBRINGER: Right. I said from bottom to top. Maxwell's already proven that Kannon's his b!tch. Kira erupts into giggles at the unexpected response. The giggling makes B.S. chuckle a bit and grin. STORMBRINGER: I got Ben J. Pailthorpe next Saturday in the Global Tourney. Clyde next week on Turmoil... that's it for now. 'Bout time I had a lighter schedule for a week or two, at least. I swear to Gawd, if they book me in any more tag matches- KIRA: Didn't you think that Tyler Davidson was going to face you? STORMBRINGER: I think everyone did. But Ben J. ain't a former World Champ for nothin', ya know. Tyler just reminded me of someone. Trent Storm.. KIRA: Another Storm? STORMBRINGER: Heh, yeah. But out of a thousand storms there's only a few that can change history. Trent Storm is one of the few. I know I've been in Primetime Central for a while, but... I still haven't faced anyone with half as much talent as him since Thrillfest. Not Ritchie Syxx. Not Drake Maxwell. Not Ben J. Palithorpe, quite frankly. Not anyone. No really. I mean it. KIRA: I take it you lost..? STORMBRINGER: Barely. I can tell I threw him off his game, but I was still young. What.. twenty-two maybe. I think he may of made it to fWo, for whatever that's worth. If anyone could ground Meanstreak and make him look awful, it was Trent Storm. OSW's Tempest beat him in the finals of the Thrillfest after that one, I think. Either the semifinals or the finals, can't remember which. He's ancient history though.. wonder what happened to him. KIRA: What about Clyde? STORMBRINGER: Aw man.. I'm not looking forward to this. F*ck winning, I gotta survive! Maybe if I invite him to the basketball game..? KIRA: Why would he go? STORMBRINGER: Maybe I'll tell him that a bunch of little girls will be... KIRA: Excuse me? STORMBRINGER: Er, never mind that. A win would be nice against a veteran like that but if I leave healthy enough to play in my b-ball game next week... I'll be satisfied. You are definitely not going to Turmoil next week! KIRA: Awww. STORMBRINGER: Trust me, love, it's for the best. And baby.. that's no b.s.! KIRA: Hehe. STORMBRINGER: What? KIRA: What did you just call me? STORMBRINGER: Well. I uh... wait! No! HAHAHHA!! B.S. is smothered by loving tickles and pokes as we fade to black for good this time due to the sheer freggin' length of this promo! ON TO THE PTC 'STREET' BASKETBALL GAME! THIS HAS BEEN A B.S.
