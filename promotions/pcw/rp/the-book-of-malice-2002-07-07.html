The Book Of Malice

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/7/02 (I know it's cheap to steal your style but it was just that damn good!! I loved it.) 1. American Song writings greatest family spawned the man called Malice.2. Fueled on whiskey and depression his journey was confused.3. A man who walked on the side of dark 4. yet fought for the good.5. Hell’s strongest warrior born again into the light. 6. A man set out on a mission to destroy both the white 7. and the black to leave just the gray. 8. No good or evil just man kind. 9. Greed, corruption, and power is his arch enemy,10. a man who is represented by a black phoenix. 11. The Phoenix and the man of Malice battled long and hard.12. Defeat and victory graced both sides. 13. Many army’s of Greed had fallen in the battles but14. the phoenix true to myth always rose from his ashes.15. The newest incarnation brings with him the five spawns16. of Satan and all that is evil.17. The phoenix has no interest in losing and has brought forth an army 18. that will guarantee victory or complete destruction of all sides.19. Grim sums up the chances of Malice but he against all odds20. has won under pressure in the past. 21. This time an alliances with the side of white is an option. 22. Is the alliance the only option in the war or is it a paper wall.23. Malice is disabled and tortured. 24. Stricken down by the curse of the five spawns of hell,25. the black phoenix spreads his wings over the PCW. 26. The shadow covers all and mighty Zeus is bound to fall. 27. The light warrior and the Malice man must team up to end the curse. 28. Turmoil describes the feud,29. as it fuels it self on principle. 30. Everybody has a different principle,31. complicating the trust of all sides into disarray . 32. The black phoenix will be stopped33. as it will be once again burnt down.34. Will Malice burn it down 35. or will it be a flame burning from with in.36. Once that happens the five spawns of hell 37. will not have the covering of light from the phoenix’s wings.38. Malice will be thrust upon them39. with a shinning light of good.40. The beam will blind them as41. Malice wears his chrome shades. 42. Then the war between the white and gray will be the only one left.43. As the ashes of the phoenix will be buried away for eternity.44. And then it will end. Williams: Now hell hoss I don’t know what I just said but I reckon its kinda like you did. -The malice man emerges as the voice reading the book of Malice.- Williams: If this is about good and bad then so be it. Just remember kid I can get bad as you and do it a whole bunch better. Your young dark and full of rage. I’m old mean, and full of expertise. Chalk it up to the wise and let the war begin. As long as your as Malice thrives in your veins. I own you. That’s the honky tonk truth. -The camera fades on the flying W- =w=
