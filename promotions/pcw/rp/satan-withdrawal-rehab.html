Satan Withdrawal Rehab

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: By Will Otto The last time we saw our hero, he was on Mount Olympus, but the last time we saw Rush he was being forced into going to a meeting of some sort of dark religion by his girlfriend, Melanie. Today, our scene opens up on a blank room; it’s white walls blaring and the floor covered by blue carpeting. In the middle of the room is a circle of chairs where a group of about 11 people is sitting. The man sitting in the middle is a middle-aged white man with a nametag that says Craig. Craig: Okay now Jorge, can you tell the whole group how Satan ruined your life? A young-looking Hispanic man, apparently named Jorge, sits forward in his chair, gulping loudly before he begins to speak. Jorge: Well…I’m sorry… He is having trouble getting started but Craig re-assures him in a partly homosexual voice. Craig: It’s ok Jorge, Satan breaks down even the best of men at times, just do the best you can. Jorge: Okay, well I used to keep my garden in good shape every day and then I became obsessed with vampire worship. I…I killed innocent garlic because I thought it was taking away…from MY STRENGTH! Jorge is almost bursting out now with tears, he is barely containing himself from bawling and Craig comforts, patting him on the shoulders. However, now the camera pans further out to show Rush Mason and his girlfriend, Melanie. Rush: Do you see what I’m saying now? I could become a pathetic helpless moron like this if you make me hang out with those dark jobbers! Melanie: Rush, I don’t care what these people have to say. They’re boring, and at least you have some reminiscent of fun in your mind. You’re not going to get out of this. Rush: But…why do I have to? I’ll NEVER be able to show my face to any respecting black person again if I do! Melanie: Stop being a baby. That guy from that rock group probably worships the devil, and he’s cool. Rush: No, he’s rich and crazy, that’s a totally different thing. Melanie just ignores Rush and quiets him with a swipe of her hand towards his mouth while Craig addresses another person in the group.Craig: Okay, now Penelope, can you share your story with us? A perfectly normal looking brunette twenty something stands up from her chair this time, taking a deep breath and then beginning to speak. Penelope: Well, I’m a pretty normal person, but even I was touched by the devil’s evil. Craig: And how did Satan try and corrupt you? Rush whispers to no one in particular, though loud enough for a mumble to be heard by almost everyone. He speaks in the same voice that Penelope just used. Rush: Well, first he molested my garlic and made me eat chicken. Penelope continues, pretending not to hear Rush snickering, while Melanie sighs, a sigh of irritation that quickly quiets Rush up. Penelope: Well, I missed church one day and… Craig: It’s okay Penelope, we’re all hear with you guarding you with God’s light. Rush mouths “Oh gawd” and begins to feign snoring, but a smack on the left shoulder by Melanie quickly brings him back, though in a worse mood every second that is wasted on these morons. Penelope: Well…later that day…DOUG TROY JOBBED TO HANK~! HANK~! I SAY! Despite her tildation, Penelope nearly breaks down as well at the simple mention of the tragic events. Rush just sits, open-mouthed as Craig comforts the second person in less than ten minutes. Rush: Oh…my…god. Being…overcome…by…boringness Melanie: You see how stupid this is? It was YOUR idea to come here in the first place to prove something to me. Rush: Dark jobber meeting or pathetic garlic-lovers union meeting? Meh, I’ll stay with these people. They’re probably all afraid to pick up forks because they could slip and kill someone, I’m much safer here. Melanie: You know that not all devil-worshippers are terrible people? Rush: Well of course, I forgot how loving and caring those worshipping Lucifer, the angel who defied God himself. They all bring their pet rabbits together with them to the meetings right? Melanie rolls her eyes at Rush’s comment. Craig now addresses another person in the group. Craig: Okay group, we’ve got one last speaker for today, please say hi to him. A black man stands up, looking strangely familiar from somewhere and wearing a golf outfit. Man: Hello well, my name is Orenthal and the devil made me kill my wife, not that I did kill her, but if I had the devil would have made me. Craig: That’s awful Orenthal, can we just call you O- Orenthal: NO! My name’s Orenthal! Rush raises his eyebrows, clearly making some sort of connection in his head. Craig: Is that all you have for us today Orenthal? Orenthal looks up from putting on a golf glove and nods his head and the rest of the group leaves the room, while Melanie and Rush walk out, followed closely by Orenthal. Melanie: Rush, you haven’t proved anything, you’re going anyway. Rush: Aw, what the hell? Are you the Devil’s associate or something? Melanie: No, I just think that you’re closed-minded and boring. You’re a stereotypical black guy, you need to branch out. Finally, they arrive at Rush’s baby blue Range Rover and climb in, while the camera follows Orenthal to his white Ford Bronco. “I didn’t do it!” No, it’s not what you think. The camera shows red liquid spilled all over the trunk of Rush’s Range Rover and Melanie just shakes her head as the scene fades out to black…
