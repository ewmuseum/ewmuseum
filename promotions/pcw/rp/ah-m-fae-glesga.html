Ah'm Fae Glesga!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Insert Date Here (OOC: Ok, I’m pretty sure that a lot of you aren’t going to understand a word that is said in this promo, but that’s just part of the new Andy Murray gimmick.) Welcome, to a new world. Welcome to a world of “Fitba,” “Birds,” and “Chippaes” … A world where battered Mars Bars are a common place, and where bad accents come as standard. Welcome to the world, of Andy Murray. [Slowly but surely, our scene fades in … Awww, isn’t that sweet? We are inside a small apartment, which is … Well, common to say the least. The whole place is decorated terribly, after all, grey carpets and Victorian Style patterned green & white wallpaper doesn’t exactly go well together. And to add to that, the furniture, in this area that looks like the living room, looks like it has been pulled directly from a skip. The 70’s style oak furnishing’s varnish is starting to peel, and the beige coloured sofas are, well, horrible. In fact, the only good thing about the room is the wide screen television, Sky Digital Digibox sitting on top of it. Sitting slouched in one of the chairs, remote control in hand, dressed in a blue Adidas shell suit, sits a tubby guy, standing at about 5’9”. A ned. Welcome to Scotland.] Ned: This is pure pish man … [The unknown ned changes the channel on the television, from what looks like a scummy soap opera, to a football match.] Ned: Bangin’ man! [The ned grins, showing his delight at finding some football match to watch, it is of course a World Cup game … Probably China vs Turkey or something equally as dull. As he gets settled in to watch the game, a door opens, as Andy Murray, former PCW Rising Star champion walks in, wearing a white Nike shell suit. Yes everybody, Andy Murray is a ned. Andy notices his fellow ned sitting watching the “Fitba,” and walks across towards him.] Andy Murray: Mornin’ Rab. Rab: Oh, fit like pal? Andy Murray: Mental min. [Andy walks around the back of the chair Rab sits in, and takes a seat on another right next to him, positioned strategically in front of the television, allowing for perfect vision. Feng Shui anyone?] Andy Murray: So … Fit yee watchin’? Rab: Och, jist the fitba, it’s pretty hummin’ though min. Andy Murray: Aye, I cannae believe England beat Argentina! That’s nae even funny! Rab: Aye, but they winna win the World Cup like. Andy Murray: Aye. [A silence falls between the two, as they watch the World Cup match on the television in awe. From the commentator’s speech, we can pick up that the match is Tunisia vs Belgium. Suddenly, we can hear the crowd eruption on the television, as Belgium slot in a goal.] Andy Murray: Fa scored that? Rab: Errr … Mark Willmont. Andy Murray: Y’mean Mark Williams? Rab: Aye, that’s the one … [Of course, with neds not being to most intelligent species on the planet, they fail to realise the fact that it was Marc Wilmots who scored. Again, a silence falls between the two, as they watch the Belgian team celebrating.] Rab: This is pure mental, by the way. [Translation? “This is great.” Simple? Good.] Andy Murray: I’m gonnae go get somethin’ tae eat, wantin’ anythin’? Rab: Aye, whatever yer havin’. [Andy gets up from his seat, and walks across the room towards an alcove-type area. He walks around the sticking-out wall (Great terminology, eh?) and into a kitchen area, which, surprise surprise, is poorly decorated, the whole thing just screams 70’s out. Andy pulls open a few drawers … Cutlery? Can’t eat that. Plates? Nah … ] Andy Murray: I cannae find the Mars Bars man! [Rab shouts his reply from the living room.] Rab: Phone the chippae then! [Andy looks at his watch, noticing the 11:03am time, why would a fish and chip shop be open at this time of day?] Andy Murray: Alright, where’s the mobile? Rab: Next tae that thing that cleans they dish things. Andy Murray: Dishwasher? Rab: Aye. [Andy walks across the room towards the clapped-out old dishwasher, and notices his “pure bangin’” mobile phone resting on top of it. Andy picks the phone up, turns it on, and holds in the ‘#’ button for a few seconds, before reaching the phone to his ear … Yes, they have the Chip Shop as a quick-dial. The phone rings, and rings, and rings, until finally someone answers.] Chip Shop Guy: Hello, Fish And Chips, how may I help you? Andy Murray: I’d like tae make an order please … Chip Shop Guy: It’s 11am, we don’t take orders this early. Andy Murray: But I’m a pure famous wrestler, by the way. [Most of the chip shop workers would know Andy’s voice by now, as he does visit or phone the place at least twice a day, obviously this kid is new to the shop.] Chip Shop Guy: I’m sorry sir. Andy Murray: Grrr … I’m gonnae hammer yee! [Andy hangs up on the guy, obviously he does not wish a response from him, before shouting across the apartment again.] Andy Murray: Rab, they are’nae open! Rab: Och! [Without nourishment, Andy walks back through the kitchen, and into the lounge a broken man … Or so he thinks anyway. Andy sits down in the seat he was sitting in before, as he straightens his Nike baseball cap.] Rab: So … Fae ye wrestlin’ this week ‘en? Andy Murray: Some wog called Jardan X Shabagger, a wallaper called Duzzer, and another muckah called John Crichton, who’s actually a pure brilliant bloke, by the way. Rab: Gonnae win? Andy Murray: Aye, of course. Jardan Shabagger, he says I look like one of they gorilla things, but ‘a think he looks jist like they big things with horns, fit they called? Rhinos, that’s it. Duzzer is just pure stupit min’ and he has a pure crap haircut, and although Crichton is ma’ mate, I’m still gonnae have tae hammer him. So as far as Tuesday Turmoil goes, it should’nae be a problem pal. Rab: Aye. [A silence falls between the two friends, as they sit and watch the remainder of the football match on the television.] Andy Murray: This fitba’ is pure bangin’, by the way. Rab: Are … [As the single word “Aye” rings through your head, our scene slowly begins to fade out, into black.]
