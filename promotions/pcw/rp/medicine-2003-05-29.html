Medicine

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 29 - 03 'Hold still, you've still got a few holes gaping through your neck...' We rejoin our bad ass and ultra cool Messiah, Dante Jackson as he sits through the lecturer standing above him as he rests in the hospital ward of Miami's Institute of Wrestling Scientists, or The Institute for short. The clothing around the paitents is the same white gown as the doctors, nurses, and aids happen to wear those cyan colored scrub uniforms. One doctor, with the labeled name tag clipped onto his breast pocket stands over away from the cloud of men and women hovering over our hero at this point as he steadily and nerdily jots down notes at a rushed and messy pace. Another doctor whom is atop the pile at this point calls out the doctor. Doctor: 'STEVE! HELP US!' However as the camera switches focuses down towards our hero, the sight of him wearing a Greek god uniform with the sash stripped down past his right pec muscle. He roars in a furious manner as the various doctors and aids try to hold him down. Doctor: (yelling) 'STEVEN BOCA SMITH IF YOU DON'T HELP US, I'LL KEEEEEEL YOU!' The toned down doctor against the white plastered walls smiled at the entire caucus piling up before walking up towards the group and pulling out a small sterile needle from his lab coat and jabs it into the left forearm of the Messiah, sending him sprawling into a daze. The doctor originally standing atop the pile dusts off his lab jacket, before starring a blank hole into the glasses wielding Steve as he smirks to him. The doc mutters things under his breath and low enough for Steven not to even take them into consideration. Steven: 'That's it, take your big nap and rest boyo...' Dante: 'The Messiah never sleeps!' Steven: (blostered) 'Why, why shouldn't the Messiah sleep?' Dante: (boasting) 'The Messiah is a vision, a parallel being from an Act of God concering all which is bad and not good in PCW. Thus, you let me out of my restraints before GOD comes down here and smotes you from the Earth!' Stevens nods, finding a good point in what The Messiah told him, before walking off in uproarious manner, causing Dante to yell out even more in pain. He struggles to release the leather bound straps off his wrists, but they are of no use as they fit snuggly inwards towards a cut position in the bench. Dante: (sighing) 'If only there were some way to get out of here and go to that Expo. I'm sure something's going on, more fishy than that 'fish' they had us eat for lunch...' The Messiah lays back, as the edge of the bench taps loudly onto the back of his head. He curses under his breath, before looking over at his right wrist, peeling his fingers back to atleast try and reach for the buckle to his restraint. He grits his teeth, putting more effort into it before his middle finger reaches the clasp and flicks it away from its position in the belt. He looks over from his free right hand, finding it much more easier to reach the buckle tying down his left wrist as he frees it quickly. As he begins to sit up, the drugs injected by Smith begin to try and take hold, but the Messiah will have none of it. He slugs along down a awkward green colored hall, looking back towards his bunk and the rest of the men and women whom are held captive here by those menacing pencil necked geeks and money men. Dante: 'FREE!' Steven: 'Not so fast, Mister Jackson!' Turning his back around in a one hundred and eighty degree motion, Jackson comes face to face with Steven Smith, his cyan colored dress shirt tucked neatly into his pants. Dante begins to face forward once more, heading upward throughout the halls of the Institute before stopping once more as a few lab coats have made their way down the hall. Dante: (shocked) 'Great Scott!' Steven: (relieved) 'You see now Mister Jackson? You can't escape here, not while you're still apart of the Institute!' Outside, lightning cracks along the blue sky as black puffy clouds surround the building, before fading away once more in a throwback to classic heel lines delivered by bad guys back in the goldern era of colorized film. Dante: 'Why must you keep me here anyway?' Steven shakes his head. Steven: 'Because we are all programmed to do so. It is our mission, our nobility inside of these walls is to benefit wrestlers and fans alike. That is our purpose.' Dante: (scoffing) 'HA! Your purpose is to string together lines of crap for me to wipe my buttocks with, you Matrix Reloaded bootlegger you!' Steven: 'Shut it Messiah, for you shall stay here while we find some way to clone not only you, but the rest of the gentails in PCW, thus creating our own SUPERIOR ARMY and ridding the world of bad wrestlers, such as yourself.' Dante: (confused) 'Army? Do you just have a screw loose or are you with those same Astrologist who help Dorn Tracer? Because if you are...' Our hero grits his teeth, planting his feet sternly in the ground, ready for battle. Smith looks coldly at Jackson, before snapping his fingers, sending in more white lab coats to try and take the massive Messiah down. Steven: (furious) 'GET 'EM!' One loud roar later, the stage is set as one bald lab techy comes at Jackson yelling and screaming at the top of his lungs. It isn't long before the meaty palm of the Messiah slams dead on into the face of the lab man, sending him propelling backwards through the air. Dante turns around, to see two more men charging his way. Taking a wrought bar from along side the wall panel, Jackson brings it upward, scarring the ears with the horrible sound of twisting metal. Jarring the bar off its hinges, he swings it around, knocking both the men ajar and off their feet, before slamming the pole long ways into their backs. The Messiah growls at Stevens, who begins to back up once more, almost in complete fear of his surroundings as there is no one here to help or lead him to safety. He looks over towards his left and finds nothing but sunlight basking through the window. On towards his right, he sees something, a brightly lit red button. Steven: (grumbling) 'You may have won this battle Jackson, but I will win the ARRRGH!' From behind Steven, a cattle prod blasts him in the neck, sending him sprawling down the floor. Standing over him, sending the small little rod sparkling happens to be Tracy Jackson, wife of Dante as she smirks as her latex uniform happens to be a little 'revealing'. Dante: (enthused) 'Wow, thank um...' Tracy: (humming) 'God?' Dante: 'No, your tailor, by god! That is some outfit there, where'd you get it?' Tracy places her hands behind her head, whistling as if she were innocent before Jackson stands up in an uproar, throwing his hands up. Dante: (breathing hard) 'The Little Shop of Superwhores?' The heroine nods, sending Dante crashing to the floor stone faced in embarrasment as Tracy waltz's her way over towards our hero, smacking him back into consciousness. Shrugging off the pain however, Dante picks himself up, walking over towards a blueprint of the structure nailed to the right hand wall where the little red button was. Dante: 'Look, three halls away from here, the exit!' Tracy: (points) 'Look, two floors down, the Hall of Great Female Managers!' Dante: (sternly) 'Exit.' Tracy: 'Managers.' The Messiah stomps his foot down. Dante: 'We'll split up. The teachings of God tell us not to ever raise our voice to women.' Tracy: (smiling) 'Good.' From there, Dante heads towards the exit as Tracy makes her way through the catwalks and down towards the catacombs near the Great Hall of Female Managers. Soon, Dante moves his way towards the exit, before a white hand slaps along his back, sending Dante's nervous system into overload. Dante: (angered) 'BY THE GODS WHAT DO YOU WANT?!' As the camera reveals the man behind Dante, it is clearly evident by his shaking knees and rattling glasses that Jackson is quite aware that he's seen the person before. Dante: (quizzical) 'Are you not the man who led me to that death trap earlier?' Peter: 'I wasn't trying to led you there, I was just hoping that you'd see everything!' Dante: (outraged) 'EVERYTHING INCLUDING DEATH!' Peter: 'Well, you'll be excited to know that we've spliced the semen of Tyler Nelson and Xavier Kannon and made us a super heel!' Peter smiles as the lab tech walks off leaving Dante to stare at him from afar. Dante: (confused) 'O-kay...'
