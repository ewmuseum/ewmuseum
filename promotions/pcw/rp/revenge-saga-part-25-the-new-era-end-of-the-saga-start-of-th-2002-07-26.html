Revenge Saga Part 25: The New Era...[end of the saga, start of the new]

Date: 26/7/02 Revenge Saga Part 25: The New Era… The debate… The incident with Wolverine had gone better than Crichton could have hoped for…he hadn’t been aggravated too much by the experience, but he had no doubts that the change was still imminent…it was still going to happen…it was only a matter of time. The dark impulses had been ravaging his mind for the better part of the year…he didn’t exactly know HOW the dark impulses entered his body…whether it be through a bad childhood, being mistreated by the army, whichever…he knew when he realised these dark impulses were upon him though. That fateful day…before Aeryn died and before the Super Serum was injected into his bloodstream, when he and Aeryn were in the Nevada desert, and Fleming and his goons stormed the diner they were in. One thing lead to another and the diner was destroyed…innocents were killed…a new enemy was made…from then on a battle of good Vs evil had been taken place within his subconscious…there was no clear winner yet…but he could feel the dark impulses slowly getting stronger and stronger…he knew that it was only a matter of time before it happened…before these impulses set up shop and took over for good and he could do nothing about it. The dark impulses had already made him do things he sure as hell wasn’t proud of…and the super soldier serum emphasised this even more so…the serum allowed him to do things now, that he could previously only dream of…that worried him even more. If these dark impulses did eventually take over, the super serum would allow his ‘other self’ to accomplish unspeakable acts…an example of this is what occurred between him and the Avengers a couple of months back…he was able to fight them to standstill. Imagine if these dark impulses had the run of his body. Imagine what could happen then. He could use the combination of his powers, granted from the super serum, and the ‘looseness’ of conscience, to do and again anything he put his mind to. He could maim and kill anyone or anything…especially if nobody could stop him…he was worried about this now…real worried. Was anything worth the risk of allowing this to happen? Was his damned TV title worth that much to him? The answer was undoubtedly no. The better part of his conscience would never allow him to do that…endanger the entire world for his simple ambition. Or was he over valuing himself? Over valuing his capabilities? Or could he be wrong about the dark impulses? He had been making moves that were based on pure theory. What if these dark impulses couldn’t make their way to the forefront of his psyche? What if they were mere glitches within his brain? Glitches that were wholly negative…glitches that wanted him to fail in life and not do well. All of this was pure theory…but what if the last part of the theory was true? He would be giving up the PCW Television title…giving up any such chance of building up his career. On the other hand if he did go into the match with Jordan X Shabazz and if these dark impulses did take over his psyche…all of this waiting for it to happen would be over…all this wondering whether he was losing his mind or not would be finally over…he would know either way. If he didn’t go ahead with the match with Shabazz at Wrestle Vessel he’d always be wondering whether the dark impulses would have become apparent, either during or after the match…and would always wonder whether he could be a big name in PCW…whether he did have the necessary skills to be number one in PCW. ************ Good Vs Evil: The Good Voices In Crichton’s Head… ‘Don’t worry…don’t worry…don’t worry…don’t worry…’ ‘What makes you say that?’ ‘Doesn’t have the power…the power…the power…’ ‘Huh?’ ‘Doesn’t have the power…the power…the power…the power…’ ‘Who doesn’t have the power?’ ‘The other side…the other side…the other side…’ ‘What other side? Shabazz?’ ‘The evil…the evil…the evil’ ‘Too right Shabazz doesn’t have the power he…’ ‘No…the evil…the evil…the evil’ ‘Shabazz IS evil…’ ‘You…you…you…’ ‘I’m not evil…well not THAT evil!’ ‘Everybody is evil…is evil…is evil…’ ‘What in THE name of GREEK buggery, are you talkin’ ‘bout?’ ‘The evil inside you…inside you…inside you…’ ‘You mean my dark impulses right?’ ‘Yes…yes…yes…’‘My dark impulses don’t have the power? ‘Yes…yes…yes…’ ‘To do what?’ ‘Take over…take over…take over…’ ‘Are you trying to tell me that if I go ahead with the match at Wrestle Vessel, the dark impulses will not take over?’ ‘Yes…yes…yes…yes’ ‘Who or what are you?’ ‘Guidance…guidance…guidance…’ ‘I see. Why the hell do you have to repeat everything?’ ‘Goodbye…Goodbye…Goodbye…’ ************ Good Vs Evil: The Evil… ‘Knows nothing!…nothing!…nothing!… ‘What in the hell? Who are you?’ ‘The other side…the other side…the other side…’ ‘Oh great, I guess you’re going to repeat everything too, huh?’ ‘No repeat, echo…echo…echo…’ ‘No shit? You’re the evil aren’t you?’ ‘Yes…yes…yes…’ ‘Oh great…so what pearls of wisdom might you have for me?’ ‘Knows nothing!…Nothing!…Nothing!’ ‘Yeah…you said that already. So say what you have to say then LEAVE…I have to prepare!’ ‘I will prove…prove…prove…’ ‘Prove who?’ ‘The good…the good…the good…’ ‘By doing what?’ ‘Proving I have the power…the power…the power’ ‘This is starting to sound like a Tina Turner song real fast…by doing what exactly?’ ‘Taking over…over…over..’ ‘But…guidance said you couldn’t do that!’ ‘Knows nothing…nothing…nothing…’ ************ The merging… With help of the guidance, he tried to resist the merging at first…but the evil had too much power for Crichton’s good nature to deal with. The evil had to admit that the resistance was good, but it was still crushed nevertheless. The body of John Crichton was now for the evil confront…solely…without question…without a conscience…without fear. For too long the evil had been bottled up within Crichton’s psyche…because of that witch Aeryn Sun…she kept the balance…she kept Crichton sane…now she was dead…and the cap of the bottle was unscrewed. The evil amassed it’s power silently…without delving into confrontation too early…biding it’s time to make a move. Now the time was right…Crichton needed the evil more than anything…he needed the evil to survive and to make him . After all the evil was still essentially Crichton…had the same goals…had the same needs…the evil was just another way of getting these things…a new perspective…a new person.
