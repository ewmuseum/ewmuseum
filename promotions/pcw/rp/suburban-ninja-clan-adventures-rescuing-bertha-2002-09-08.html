Suburban Ninja Clan Adventures: Rescuing Bertha...

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/8/2 OOC Note: Syko and I will handle the Suburban Ninja Clan jointly. They will be used in RPs as characters that will do odd jobs and act as comic relief. This RP is one I wrote for Syko's character timeline, but I'm taking credit for it since I wrote it. :-P 'Remember, the assignment is to rescue Bertha. She's a woman and very large...' Outside of a castle, lit by spotlights shining brightly inside to confuse passing commercial airliners and make the place feel special, the seven Suburban Ninja Clan members stand in the traditional black uniform of the ninja that has been popularized (or in many cases, bastardized) along a moat which must be crossed... Ninja #12:'A moat? I only see these in the movies!' Ninja #8:'Dude, this guy realizes it's the 21st century, right?' Ninja # 31:'It doesn't matter, let's just swim across.' One of the ninjas walks forward and steps into the water. As soon as he gets about waist high, a 50-foot-long blue-scaled water dragon pops out of the moat and devours the ninja in one bite. The others run away from the moat and take refuge in the nearby evergreen woods. Ninja #20:'Frickin A! A dragon?' Ninja #6:'That thing could've eaten us all whole!' Ninja #31:'Yes... luckily, I decided to stop along the way and bring an extra.' The Sensei's best student, Ninja #1, steps out from the woods with his arms behind his back. Ninja #1:'Lesson 19 -- If there is a trap, bring along an extra; the extra will undoubtedly be the first to die.' Following Ninja #1, the ninjas step out of the woods. Ninja #1, much more muscular and a little taller than the others, draws his sword from its sheath and steps forward. Ninja #1:'There is no other way to cross the moat. I must kill the creature.' The another extra taps Ninja #1 on his shoulder. Grabbing his attention, he points over to the left. 150 yards away, a freight truck drives over the bridge that leads over the water to the castle. Ninja #1:'Uh... Right. I saw that. I just wanted to make you all realize it.' He doesn't put his sword away, but waves the ninjas forward toward the bridge. Ninja #1:'There is our path across the water, go!' The ninjas run across the shore toward the bridge. Ninja #1 stays put, clutching the extra by his wrist. The extra turns around, looking at Ninja #1. He, in turn, spins behind the extra and slits his throat. The lifeless body falls to the ground and Ninja #1 kicks it to edge of the water and runs to catch up to his clan. And the head of the Water Dragon slowly creeps up from the depths of the moat... Up ahead, the Ninjas, caught up in the moment, begin a battle cry. Ninjas:'YAAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!' Ninja #1:'SHH! Quiet, morons!' Rushing with great speed and stealth-- Little Boy:'Hey look, Mom! Ninjas!' Ninja #1:'Make a note, the boy knows too much...' Rushing with relatively great speed and stealth, the Ninjas reach the bridge. Using their spike-palms, they scale underneath the bottom of the bridge and slowly make their way across, the Water Dragon waiting for one of them to fall... On the other side, Ninja #1 meets them. Ninja #12:'How did you get here ahead of us?' Ninja #1:'I hitchedhiked.' Ninja #20:'That works?' With that, Ninja #1 holds up his bloodstained sword. Ninja #1:'Who's going to mess with me?' Ninja #8:'He's got a point.' From this point, it's only another 10 minutes' walk to the castle... And so the ninjas stand in front of the castle's high wall. Ninja #1 looks back at his students. They all take their three-pronged grappling hooks off their belts and attached them to the ropes they have tied around their waists. And with speed and precision they throw their hooks up over the wall to grapple onto something. They don't know what it is, but the grip is firm and they start climbing. Ninja #6:'Oh, how to tell her I love her...' Ninja #8:'What the hell is that!' Ninja #12:'Nothing, Number Six is just is just singing Girl from Epanema.' Ninja #31:'Well, tell him to stop!' Ninja #20:'Would you rather he sung the theme from Lavern and Shirley?' Ninja #8:'DEAR GOD NO!' Ninja #1, climbing ahead of the group, looks back at the others. Ninja #1:'SHH!' The others quiet down, but in a very short amount of time Ninja #6 is singing again, much to the annoyance of Ninja #8, who quietly begins to draw his sword while staying in place on the wall... Ninja #6:'Tall and thin and tanned and lovely, the Girl from Epanema goes walking; and when she passes everyone she passes goes--' *SWIPE!* Ninja #6:'AAAAAAAAAHHHHH!' And down he falls the forty feet thet had climbed thus far... *BOOMPF!* Putting his sword away, Ninja #8 looks back up at his disappointed compatriots. Ninja #8:'What? He was getting annoying...' Ninja #1:'Compared to you, I think I'd manage a stealthier intrusion with a marching band!' Sans Ninja #6, they climb to the top of the wall. Crouching perched at the top of the narrow wall, Ninja #1 looks down into the area and sees a large female in a cage. Ninja #1:'Confirm hostage?' The other ninjas look down and see a sign that says 'Bertha,' so they agree with him. They all drop down from the wall and run past the various other cages, the captives all asleep paying no mind to them. Ninja #20:'How many people does he have held here?' Ninja #31:'Should we free them all?' Ninja #1:'No, we're only rescuing the extremely fat female...' Reaching the cage, Ninja #12 removes his lockpick from underneath his right sleeve cuff and skillfully opens the Masterlock keeping her in the cage. She stirs Ninja #8:'Don't worry, Bertha. Syko sent us to free you.' Bertha groans. The ninjas quiet her and lift her up to carry her out. Ninja #12:'DAMN SHE'S HEAVY!' Ninja #1:'You're all just weak.' Ninja #20:'Shut up, it's not like weight training is part of the training regimen. We're ninjas, not Conan!' Though with much difficultly, the ninjas manage to lift Bertha and carry her out of the castle without being detected... Back at the house. Sensei:'YOU KIDNAPPED A HIPPO?!' Ninja #20:'It's female and fat and named Bertha...' Ninja #8:'It was dark, what do you want from us?' Sensei:'I want you to pay the settlement for pending lawsuit from The Renaissance Zoo, that's what!' Syko takes the hippo and begins to leave. Sensei:'Hey, that's... not your Bertha.' Syko sighs and looks at the hippo as he guides it away by a leash. Syko:'I know... but she'll have to do...'
