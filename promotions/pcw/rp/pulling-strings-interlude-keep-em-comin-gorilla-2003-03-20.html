Pulling Strings - Interlude - Keep'em Comin' Gorilla

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 20th, 2003 As the scene opens, it clearly definite that the face of Dante Jackson has changed since the sunlight of Monday morning became brightly apparent in his eyes. As he turns his body upwards to a shoulder resting position, he begins to carress the opposite side as the person close to him seems to be completely unaware of his gentle strokes. The camera pans around as Jason Cunningham sits directly across from the bed of Dante as a smile creaks upon his face. The warm homely smile upon Dante's is shallowly interrupted when... Jason Cunningham: ROAR! Dante leaps from the bed, his eyes just completely bugging out of control as he stands in the middle of his Sealy Posterpedic bed in white pajamas with little rubber duckies running down the side of them as Jason bawls out in laughter. Jason: MUAHAHAHAH! Dante Jackson: What? You hate the fact that my pajamas are better thank your breifs and cold, naked body? Huh? Jason halts laughter for a second at he points towards the opposite side of the bed. Dante: And just what in the blue heck of holy hecks is that thing? Looking rather peculiar, the large hump in the middle of Dante Jackson's bed causes the Lyrical Messiah to kick at it with his foot for a few seconds or more, before bending down slowly and touching the small bundle, before shrieking back in obvious tomfoolery and fear. Jason: Jeez, what are you getting worked up about? Its only... Jason peels the sheet back as the PCW Continental Championship belt lays sprawled along the bed, still buckled as if the imaginary waist of Dante Jackson would fit around it. Dante gives off a coo response before Jason shakes his head, obviously being the only sane and literally serious of the two. Jason: You got scared of your own championship belt? I don't think FRSICO got scared when he felt first a title, now did he? I don't think TOTALLY TUBULAR TOMMY felt that way too. He was rather happy about it. Dante: But that was THEM! I'm still...not quite used to this thing I suppose. Jason: Not used? You've only been champ for 96 hours! Dante looks around, before slamming on a ringing alarm clock that has been buzzing hard inside of his ears for about a good three or four minutes tops. He begins to audibly breathe loudly as the sudden rush of fear comes towards him as he sits back in the bed, hovering and manuvering like a skitzophrenic paitent inside of a padden room. Voice: ....He's started it hasn't he? From inside of the home hallway, the wife, and ever loving presence of Tracy Jackson fills the other open void of the room; moral support. Wearing a black halter cut at the rib cage with a pair of DKNY jeans, the lovable co-hort of many a black man's fantasy stares directly at the Public Relations man before turning her attention towards her husband. Tracy Jackson: He was up all night, looking at old 80's wrestling tapes and it bugged him out badly! Kept me up and Simmone for just trying to drown out that... Dante: Dun..nnnnnn...nah..DUN...DUN...DADUN! Dun..nnnnnn...nah..DUN...DUN...DADUN! Dun..nnnnnn...nah..DUN...DUN...DADUN! Da nnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnn.... Jason: Hm, Ultimate Warrior theme song, better take that Wrestlemania V tape out of the VCR... Before Jason can even make a move, the lady of the house smacks his hand, causing him to retract it back in obvious pain as Tracy gives him a stern look. Tracy: If you're taking those tapes, they're due back Friday before noon at the local Blockbuster. Jason: Nah, nah...I was just (moves eyes around) browsing... Tracy begins to eye him suspiciously as Dante still sits inside of his bed, acting as if the world is a dark cloister of pain just waiting to get at him. She sucks on her teeth before whispering towards herself. Tracy: Awww, poor baby. She pats Dante on the head as he begins to waft an eye towards her, before returning back towards his odd, cold stare into the white walls. A quirky smirk on his face appears as Tracy begins to pull back, taking a few steps out of the room as Dante finally begins to settle back into a comfort zone. Looking around, to see if he's alone, Dante begins to cuddle the Continental title, holding it tightly to his chest as a smile crosses his face. He sighs heavenly, before kissing the metal face plate as Jason walks in, holding a glass of orange juice, unaware of Dante's actions, before spotting him. Jason: Hey Trace was making breakfast in the kitchen so...THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?! Dante struggles to get back into his shocked state as he knows he's been caught in the act. Jason, shakes his head shamefully before taking a seat onto the dresser counter, taking small sips of the orange juice. Jason: So that's why you've been acting strange all morning, all over that stupid frickin' title! Dante: HEY! Don't call Shiny stupid, he hates that. Jason spits out the orange juice he had left in his mouth. Jason: You named it? Dante: Yeah, its Shiny! Jason: Dante, you're not supposed to give a title a name of its own, then you start getting attached to it. Then you go over life and death measures to keep the title in the first place. You BARELY beat those guys at March Madness. Dante: Um, guy, you are so forgetting the fact that I pinned the champion, without having to lay a decent finger on him. That's being psychic to the highest degree possible! Jason: I think you mean telekinetic. Dante shakes his head. Dante: Telekinetic big headache, I won the title, I'm a hero to a bunch of black people who THOUGHT of being champion... He turns to face the camera with a thumbs up and a cheesy smile. Dante: TAKE THAT RUSH MASON! Jason can only look at Dante oddly, before taking his back towards Dante as he begins to take a walk back into the kitchen. His voice is heard from behind his shoulder as he takes more sips of his orange juice as Dante begins to peel himself out of the ducky panjama combo. Jason: Tracy's making breakfast, it would be KEY if you joined us. Dante: Hey, NO WHITE MAN HAS BREAKFAST WITH MY WIFE UNLESS HE'S TRYING TO SELL HER SOMETHING 'kay?! HEY! HEY! I'M TALKING TO YOU! GAH! Feeling ignored just like all the other times you would normally find him, Dante hops up out of bed in a start, tripping over the side as he smashes his kneecap along the floor. Dante: OW! He grumbles, before getting back up as the scene revolves around towards the kitchen of the Jackson's decked out in homely decour, before revolving around into a modernized appeal towards it, just like the home of Jason Snow found in upstate New York City. Jason Cunningham quickly parks a seat right in front of the television as he props his legs up along the wood kitchen table. Tracy slides them off as Dante strolls into the picture, whistling like a migrant worker as Tracy raises an eyebrow towards him. No words are said as the television flickers towards the National Broadcasting Channel as War Coverage begins rather quickly. Ever since 9:47 EST, Wednesday night, the coverage of NBC, just like almost every other news program carrying a program upon a major network has been focused on the tiny Middle Eastern country of Iraq, but in a sense, Dante nor the family really cares about it. Slowly, Dante takes a step towards the VCR as he pushes in a tape, the machine instantly receiving it and loading the boradcast upon the large screen television. Jason: I still don't see why you guys didn't get whatever you're about to watch on DVD. Dante: World Wrestling Entertainment does not make tapes from almost twenty years ago on that little DVD machine. Its rather POINTLESS to have one if all you want are special features and nifty gizmos to make you seem like you're in the movie. If you didn't get a good chance to see it at the show, then don't bother trying to make it better - what I say. Jason: And now you know why black wrestlers are found under rocks instead of bred out in the gyms of America. Dante glares at Jason as the Wrestlemania V logo appears upon the television screen, almost fast forwarded to Howard Finkel's introductions of the Intercontinental (god rest its prestigious soul) championship match between 'Ravishing' Rick Rude and the Ultimate Warrior. Tracy: You cut off the war, just for Rick RUDE?! Dante: Honey, honey. You can't just call the super ego heel just 'Rick Rude'. You have to call him, 'Ravishing Rick Rude!' Jason: Its true, Rude was a badass, and a sexy one at that. Tracy bawls in audible yawns as the Continental champ shakes his head, turning back towards the television as the Ultimate Warrior races down to the ring. Dante: Ever notice that Warrior acts like Violence Jack? Jason: The cult, the face paint, the odd sense to seem like he's on drugs half the time? Dante: EXACTLY! I mean, is there really a Cthulhu Mythos thingy out there now? I mean, I remember back at Penn State when they told us to read about Howard Phillips Lovecraft and August Derleth. I hear they have a little colony in Mexico, or Cuba, or one of those lawn mower places, somebody needs to go check it out. All eyes look upon Jason as he begins to back away from the two Jackson's in the room as he can only mouth words before sighing as a fast forward motion between drinking Scotch on a plane before eying in wonder as the scene fades to white, with Jason in search of the mighty Cthulhu Mythos.
