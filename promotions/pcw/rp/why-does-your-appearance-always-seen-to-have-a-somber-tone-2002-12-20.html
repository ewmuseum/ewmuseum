Why Does Your Appearance Always Seen To Have A Somber Tone

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Why Does Your Appearance Always Seen To Have A Somber Tone Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/20/02 Duke stands in the lobby of PCW New Jersey wearing all black. He wears the solid color because one he feels most comfortable in it and two because of Johnny Cash. Cash is a man who exemplifies a laid back, cool, fucking, badass. “I shot a man in Reno just to watch him die.” That line from the Cash song Folsom Prison Blues repeats in the mind of Williams like an old lady telling stories at the nursing home. In the back of his belt is a Magnum pistol. Not sure if he’s here to scare of kill he proceeded into the elevator to the executive floor. Williams: They want to fuck with me? Want to push my goddamn buttons and try to get a way with that. I’m no wavy fucking gravy hippie I’m an out law from the south. The numbers slowly progress upward on the display atop the elevator. Duke waits patiently to reach the top. Angry, frustrated, and abused, the Malice Man seeks vengeance like the contrary of the biblical Job. He pulls out his piece and points it at the door waiting for it to open. Williams: When I was 22 I ran cocaine down copperhead road. Direct from Columbia to the cities and streets of our nation. I was the third largest cocaine route in the world. This isn’t the first time I’ve walked, dressed in black, with the intent of assignation and murder. It’s the first time in fifteen year since I went straight thanks to a promising career as a pro wrestler. Fear never enters the spine of the man in black but salvation does enter his mind. He was saved by the words of Jesus and a wrestling promoter/preacher in Huntsville, Alabama in 1986. By 1989 Duke was a mid-card wrester in the WWF with a promising outlook. The dark world of his past was buried in gimmick and the re-writing of his history. Addiction invades the mind like a snowstorm blankets its victims. It wages a consistent battle between right and wrong deep inside your mind every single waking hour of your life. Addiction has won the battle many times getting Duke fired from the WWF in 1992 black listing him from American wrestling. Sending him to Japan to shoot fight like a vicious animal fighting for survival. Williams: Fuck. Duke places the piece back tucked into his belt. Back in the states since 1996 the Malice Man has stayed clean and dedicated to his craft. Tonight that all could change as Duke has a kilo of C-cain in his pocket and a revolver in his belt. To go forward with this would me a new life and identify in an other country. In his truck is a one way ticket to Bangladesh and 200,000 dollars in cash. More then enough to flee from the dim witted police. Williams: Malice Time. Duke looks down at his black Movado watch and smiles. The doors swing open and Duke takes immediate inventory of his surroundings like any good hitman would do. Like riding a bicycle the instincts come in full force like a puppy dog greeting his master after a long vacation. One security guard armed, a female receptionist unarmed, a sales executive behind a glass window to the right. BANG one to the head of the guard…BANG next to the sales executive before she can duck for cover…..SLAP to the face of the receptionist to get any information from her before she is executed. That would be the proper game plan but nothing is set yet. Receptionist: Duke your not allowed on this floor anymore according to Mr. Kannon. Williams: Yea but the candy machine down stairs doesn’t have juicy fruit and you know I love juicy fruit. Duke plays it cool like an Eskimo in his living room. He gives the receptionist his puppy dog eyes. The security guard is moving in to the right of Duke. Now the choice between vengeance and salvation rings louder then ever. One quick flash and it all can be over for three innocent humans and four personal payback hits. Receptionist: If you make it quick. Williams: As quick as a sexual escapade with Kannon and Webster at noon. Duke uses his hillbilly charm to make the tight receptionist smile and the security guard back off. Williams: Speaking of Juicy Fruit how’s Timmy Walden? Receptionist: Quickly Mr. Williams and Tony needs to escort you. Williams: What you don’t trust me?? Hi Tony. Tony: Lets make this quick. Duke is escorted down the hallway to the vending machine, which is located in the vicinity of Kannon’s office. Bust in the door and cap his ass is the new plan. Duke pulls thirty-five cents out of his pocket for the twenty-five cent gum. Williams: It says twenty-five cents on the package how can they charge an extra dime. Tony: It’s criminal. Williams: So is this. Duke bashes the side of his fist into the temple of Tony dropping him like an apple on New Years. He quickly moves to Kannon’s door and rears back to kick it in. Duke can hear Kannon in there with his giant assed wife Ellie arguing. Alpha and Omega is about to visit the two un-expecting low life’s through the Armageddon of the man wearing the golden crown whom judges the righteous . Williams: And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals, and I heard, as it were the noise of thunder, one of the four beasts saying, Come and see. And I saw, and behold a white horse: and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer. And when he had opened the second seal, I heard the second beast say, Come and see. And there went out another horse that was red: and power was given to him that sat thereon to take peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another: and there was given unto him a great sword. And when he had opened the third seal, I heard the third beast say, Come and see. And I beheld, and lo A BLACK HORSE; and he that sat on him had a pair of balances in his hand. And I heard a voice in the midst of the four beasts say, A measure of wheat for a penny, and three measures of barley for a penny; and see thou hurt not the oil and the wine. And when he had opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth beast say, Come and see. And I looked, and behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was DEATH, and Hell and followed with him. Its common place for a man who brings death to rationalize it with religion to make it seem acceptable. Wars are fought and the name of God is often used to fuel them. One man may rationalize his wicked ways through Jesus and the Christian bible like The Malice Man does. Some may carry out sick deeds in the name of Allah through the book of the Koran. Others find accountability in the name of Hubbard and Scientology. What ever the out is, God gets the bad reputation in an unjust manor. Duke has said his piece the same line from the book of Revelation he has used before killing so many in his younger years. His legs are aching to kick the door down. Williams: MALICE TIME!!! Like the hand of God reaching up and restraining Duke’s leg he can’t move it. Gun in and hand and death ready to be sentenced all systems go. All systems go, except his leg, which is the leg that would start the party by removing the barrier. Through the desert of life suffering from dehydration Duke has come too far. Why turn back now. Anger fuels vengeance while salvation is reached through forgiveness. Accept the things that I can’t change and forgive those who trespass against us and lead us not into temptation but deliver us from evil. The words of the lords prayer ring in the mind of Duke’s head. Williams: Your day of punishment comes fittingly enough on Sunday. The lords day and the Malice Man’s night. Kannon you’re a lost soul who’s Shepard has come to guide you home. You can take my job, money, and power but you can never take my soul. I have salvation while you have Ellie. The horsemen are coming for you to open the gates of hell and the horse of black will send you there via Whisky Bent and Hell Bound. Duke pulls a black marker out of his pockets and draws his symbol on Kannon’s door. =w= He then throws water on the security guard Tony reviving him. He helps him up. Williams: You passed out on me hoss. Tony: I did? Williams: Yea I was talking about the price of gum and bang you go down. Tony: Fuck. Williams: You ok? Tony: I think so thanks. Williams: Hey I have to split. Duke exits the floor and disappears as the doors close. ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
