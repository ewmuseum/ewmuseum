POLIS! BOLT!

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th August **Ring ring … Ring ring … Ring ring** **Click** Andy Murray: Wha gawn? Rab: Haw man! It’s Rab. Andy Murray: Like min? Rab: No bad … Listen, Ah’m down the chippae, what ye wantin’? Andy Murray: Deep fried Mars Bar, an’ a bottle ‘a Buckie. Rab: Awlright muckah. Andy Murray: We still gonnae go nick some sweeties fae down the Spar? Rab: Aye min … After our food. Andy Murray: Briwliant. Rab: Ah’ll see ye in a minute, awlright? Andy Murray: Awlright. **Click** [Slowly, but surely, our scene fades into the lounge area of Andy Murray’s apartment. Murray, sitting on one of the newly acquired grey leather chairs, slides his mobile phone back into the pocket of his blue & yellow Kappa tracksuit, his hair is today supplemented with 3 scoops of hair gel. His latest Nedly accomplice, Ross Cruickshank (All Rights Reserved), sits on the other chair, wearing a black & white Nike tracksuit, baseball cap as well. Ross is reading a newspaper.] Andy Murray: Haw min … We’re pure gonnae rob the Spar min! [Ross lowers the paper, and glances at Andy.] Ross: Aye min … Pure, mad, mental! Andy Murray: Magic. [Raising his paper again, Ross continues to read though the sports pages. Suddenly, something catches his eye.] Ross: HAW! Andy Murray: What? Ross: LOOK AT THIS MIN! [Andy gets up from his chair, and walks behind Ross’s chair, so that he can read the paper over his shoulder. Ross points out the name of a football team, Buckie Thistle, to Andy.] Ross: Buckie! Andy Murray: There’s a fitba team for Buckfast? Ross: Aye min! Andy Murray: BRIWLIANT! [Andy and Ross share a high five, before Andy walks back over to the seat wear he was sitting. A few moments later, the door of the apartment opens up, and Rab steps through, carrying 3 packages wrapped in white paper, food from the fish & chip shop. Andy turns to see his nedly sidekick, who is modelling a red & white Adidas tracksuit.] Andy Murray: CHIPPAE! MENTAL! [Ross drops his paper, and turns around to face Rab, who walks across the room, hands Andy a parcel, and hands Ross a parcel, before sitting down on one of the brown sofas. Eagerly, the Neds open up their food packages, revealing several fatty based products slathered in batter. They start to ram the food down their foods, as if they had never eaten before. Andy speaks, mouth full.] Andy Murray: This is pure quality food. Rab: Aye. [Within moments, the “meals” are devoured, and the papers have been thrown across the room towards the bin, missing miserably. The Neds lick their fingers, like a bunch of hicks, and lean back in their chairs, passing wind via the rear end, and the mouth. Rab particularly knocks are ripping fart.] Rab: Haw min! Did ye hear that!? Ross: HOHO! Andy Murray: Class min. [For a couple of moments, there is silence between the Neds, before Andy pipes up.] Andy Murray: This is pure borin’ man … Ah’m off down the Spar, lets go … Ross: We’re gonnae nick sweeties fae the Spar now min? Rab: Aye … [The scene fades out, as the Neds get up from their chairs, making their way out of the apartment.] 30 Minutes Later [The scene slowly reopens outside the local Spar (a British convenient store), where our Nedly heroes aim to steal some candy. Suddenly, a dark red coloured Vauxhall Corsa pulls up in front of the Spar. All four doors open, Andy Murray steps out of the driver’s side, Ross Cruickshank from the front passengers, and Rab & Barry from the two back doors. The neds shut the doors behind them, and gather in a circle in front of the Corsa.] Rab: Ho! How come I had tae ride in the back? Andy Murray: Cos ye did. Ross: Cos I wis in the front ya chube. Barry: Awlright, lets get this sorted … Andy Murray: Awlright, here’s the plan … [The group huddle closer together.] Andy Murray: Here’s the plan … We gawn into the Spar … Steal sweets, and bolt … Awlright? Ross, Barry & Rab: Aye. Andy Murray: Lets go! [And with that, the Neds break off, and head inside the store. It’s quite small store for a Spar, and the security system comprises of one of those circular mirror things. The shop keeper looks at the neds suspiciously as they head towards the area where the candy bars are.] Barry: Hoho … [Quickly, they grab at the candy bars, throwing as many into their pockets as possible.] Shopkeeper: What’s going on there!? [They cease.] Andy Murray: Leg it! [And with that said, The Neds quickly rush out of the shop door.] Shopkeeper: I’ll call the police … [But before he can finish, the Neds have left the shop, and are now out on the street. Suddenly, down the road, a police car appears, travelling in the general direction of the Spar.] Ross: POLIS! Andy Murray: BOLT! [Leaving the Corsa behind, probably because they forgot about it, the Neds quickly run down the road, trying to escape the police car, which is obviously not after them, as it cruises at a steady pace. Candy bars fall out of their pockets as they try to evade capture. We cut to an inside view of the police car, a male and female officer glance at the Neds as they run down the street.] Male Officer: What the hell are they doing? Female Officer: Ahhh … You know their type … Male Officer: Yeah … Always up to something. Female Officer: Probably kicked a football in somebody’s garden or something. [The police car slowly pulls away from the Neds, who stop for breath, looks of joy on their faces.] Barry: WE OUTRAN THEY BAWBAGS! Andy Murray: HOHO YA CHUBES! Ross: Briwliant … [And … Fade!]
