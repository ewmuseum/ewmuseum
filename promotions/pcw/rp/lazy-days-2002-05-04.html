Lazy Days

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-4-02 (We arrive on location in Cleveland, Ohio on the afternoon of Friday, May the 3rd, two thousand and two. The skyline of Cleveland sits silently, but strongly ahead. We zoom forward to the faint gray forms of buildings gradually as the details of the city becoming clearer and clearer. The Spring sun shines brightly on the land, illuminating the homes on the outskirts of Cleveland and the surrounding green landscapes. The movement of vehicles can now be seen as we are but a few miles away from downtown. At this moment, the faint noise of a television can be heard. The noise continues as the buildings come closer and cars are seen zipping along the freeways, and the voice of a woman speaking enthusiastically can be heard, but not deciphered. A slight click is then audible followed by a pair of announcers yammering back and forth. Another click is heard and this time gun shots are heard along with the screeching of tires. The view is now watching the Cleveland HILTON~! as it sits with limos and luxury cars in front of it. One particular car of interest is Frisco's presidential limo with its Argentinean flags and machine gun turrets. As this strong, armed vehicle is shown roped off and guarded by men in brown overcoats, we still hear the sounds of the idiot box yapping away. This is interrupted by a verbal outburst from our friendly neighborhood political and professional wrestling savior, who is shown in a pair of silk FRISCACEMANI~! boxers and a bag of CHEETOES~! in his hand.) Frisco: 'Fucking worthless American programming! Why is there never anything good on!?' (Frisco flips the channel which lands on a Duke Williams promo. Frisco cringes in disgust.) Frisco: 'Ugh! To hell with this!' (Frisco shakes his head and drops the remote. He looks over at his hotel room's clock which reads 2:49PM. Frisco yawns and looks at both sides of his bed at his personal guards who stand at attention.) Frisco: 'Good afternoon, gentlemen.' (The 4 men who surround Frisco's bed with semi automatic weapons nod towards the lazy dictator.) Frisco: 'Does anyone here know how my campaign is going? Nobody's called me today and I'm beginning to worry. Surely I have some kind of idiotic convention to say I'll attend, but take the time out of my busy schedule to avoid. It's a very strange situation I am here. Instead of being busy trying to not be busy with such trite, insignificant things, I am forced to not be busy with anything. This is entirely unfair.' (Frisco reaches under his covers and pulls out a royally designed gold back scratcher. He reaches it under his boxers to scratch his left nut. One of the armed guards steps forward and looks at Frisco.) Guard: 'Permission to speak, senor.' (Frisco stops scratching and ponders the request for a second. He then nods and resumes scratching.) Frisco: 'You may.' Guard: 'All of your secretaries are attending a conference back in Buenos on your behalf. They're discussing your stance on the whole proposed 'rematch' with England. Apparently, there is quite an uproar about it.' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'Which part? The actual war, or the enslaving of Britons?' Guard: 'Both, El Mero Mero.' (Frisco rolls his eyes and takes the gold back-scratcher out of his boxers. With it, he reaches over to the table to the side of him to pull his cigar case close to him. He picks up the case and removes the fine import from Havana. Frisco bites off an end and spits it across the room. He then takes out his platinum ZIPPO~! to light up the Cuban. Frisco begins puffing on the thick cigar.) Frisco: 'I knew something like this would happen. They doubt we can defeat those tea-sippin' maricones. That's the product of weak leaders and an even weaker economy. My people have lost faith in our country. Every Argentinean life must be filled with so much hopelessness and despair. God, they need me more than ever.' (Frisco shrugs slightly and blows a thick cloud of smoke into the air.) Frisco: 'Why didn't my secretaries inform me of this meeting?' Guard: 'I don't know. I guess they figured you didn't want to be disturbed.' (Frisco scoffs.) Frisco: 'When have you ever known me to snap at someone for giving me news?' (The guard sheepishly looks up.) Guard: 'Well, uh, besides last week when I told you I forgot my gun at Red Lobster?' Frisco: 'That wasn't snapping, my good man, that was discipline. I had to remind you not to be so stupid. I'm always welcome to hear news, be it bad or good. I need to be in the know, you know what I mean?' Guard: 'Yes, Jefe.' (Frisco nods and puffs away.) Frisco: 'And I thank you for your loyalty. It takes a big man to tell me that my idiot secretaries have acknowledged the fucktards who question my plan. If they don't come back with a full report of what went on there, you have my permission to shoot them all.' (The guard grins.) Frisco: 'Are you up to that?' Guard: 'Claro que si!' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Excellent.' (Frisco continues to puff on his cigar as he looks around the silent hotel room and his guards who stand silent and still. Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'I'm quite bored. Did my secretary of Alcohol, Bagels, and Socks purchase enough booze before he skipped off to Argentina with the rest of those dildos?' (Another guard steps forward. He nods to Frisco.) Frisco: 'You may speak.' Guard #2: 'He left 2 crates of STOLICHNAYA~! in the car.' Frisco: 'Beautiful! Quickly, go to the car and bring up a crate. We're going to kill some time.' (The guards' eyes light up as they bite their lips in anticipation.) Guard #2: 'Yes, sir!' (The guard quickly turns around and heads out of the door, accidentally bumping his assault rifle on the doorway. Frisco rolls his eyes.) Guard: #2: 'Sorry!' (The guard pulls the gun past the door and runs out of the room.) Frisco: 'Dumbass.' (Frisco yawns and spins around to place his feet on the carpet. He rubs his face and goes over his messy hair with his hands. He grabs a brush and strokes his beautiful jet black hair approximately 29 times before checking himself in the mirror and lying back down on the bed with the cigar sticking straight out of his mouth.) Frisco: 'What a day, what a day. No whiny morons bugging me about what kind of speech I should make, who I should meet, where I should take a shit. Maybe being busy is overrated. Relaxation isn't so bad after all.' (Frisco sighs with pleasure and puffs his cigar. The first guard darts his eyes at Frisco and speaks up.) Guard: 'Sir, you do have a match with Duke Williams tonight.' (Frisco frowns and opens his eyes.) Frisco: 'Are you fuckin' kidding me?' (The guard shakes his head.) Frisco: 'Goddamnit! Of course that shaggy, drunken douche would ruin my plans.' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'Why didn't anyone tell me this?' Guard: 'You told your agent to go fuck himself when he suggested you should cut more promos. I think he didn't care to tell you that you had to report to Oxford tonight.' Frisco: 'I tell everyone to go fuck themselves! That's no excuse. No excuse at all.' Guard: 'Yes, Mero Mero.' Frisco: 'Aw, go fuck yourself! Kissing my ass all the time. All you want is to be promoted. That's why you know all this crap.' Guard: 'Si, Presidente.' Frisco: 'Oxford, eh? On the campus? All the way in England?' Guard: 'No, Ohio.' Frisco: 'Oh, good. Less travel distance...but no chance to bring any slaves back. Ah well.' Guard: 'Would you like to know the details of your match on Tuesday?' Frisco: 'Ugh! I have another match!? When will these people stop bugging me with their stupid matches. Can't they see I have more important things to do!?' (Frisco grunts uncomfortably.) Frisco: 'Someone turn the air conditioner up and get me another pillow to put under my ass. I think it's getting bedsores.' (Another guard nods and steps away to oblige.) Frisco: 'Yes, please do.' Guard: 'Well, sir, you face Sylo in Ontario.' (Frisco's 3/4 burned cigar drops from his mouth. Frisco quickly bats it away with a squeal.) Frisco: 'Dear sweet mother of fuck!' Guard: 'Huh?' Frisco: 'I have to face one of these punk kids...in CANADA!? That's just disrespectful on all accounts. PCW is making me go to the shithole that is Canada and waste my time with one of these braindead children who are supposed to be the future of wrestling, but do nothing but disappoint us all. What a way to treat a legend.' Guard: 'I'm sorry, sir.' Frisco: 'Shut up. This is all your fault you know.' Guard: 'Why me!?' Frisco: 'Don't raise your voice to me. You saw what happened to Miguel last week.' (The guard hangs his head.) Guard: 'He raised his voice at you and...' (The other guard arrives, putting the pillow under Frisco's ass, and placing himself back in line.) Frisco: 'Thank you...' (Frisco adjusts himself.) Frisco: 'Yes, and then he blew his own brains out. A troubled young man, I must say.' Guard: 'But why is this my fault?' (Frisco shrugs.) Frisco: 'You aren't shooting enough people. Nobody is taking me seriously enough. If more people get shot, more people know I'm serious about my campaign! See where this goes?' Guard: 'But I--..' Frisco: 'Do you know how many people Jimmy Carter had shot?' Guard: '4?' Frisco: 'NONE! And look where he is now. Forgotten after one disastrous term.' Guard: 'I'll try to do better.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Good. We all have to contribute, you know. This is for the good of our people back home. Soon I will be the president of Argentina and we will turn it into U-fuckin'-topia!' (The door to the room opens up as the remaining 3 guards quickly point their guns towards it.) Frisco: 'FIRE!' (The guard who was assigned to get the alcohol appears in the doorway.) Guard #2: 'No! Wait! Aaaaaaaaahhhhhhhh!!!' (The guards open fire at the split second as the guard ducks down and the bullets rip up the door and walls. Frisco waves his hands quickly.) Frisco: 'Whoa! Whoa! Whoa! Cease fire, cease fire!' (The guards stop shooting and the petrified booze-fetcher stands up with his case of vodka. Frisco frowns deeply.) Frisco: 'Are you guys fucking nuts!? You almost shot the booze!' Guard #1: 'Sorry.' Guard #3: 'Sorry, El Mero Mero.' Guard #4: 'Lo siento.' (The terrified guard looks at his arm with oozes with blood.) Guard #2: 'Fuck! Somebody shot me in the arm!' (Frisco rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'Stop being a baby. This was part of your job description. Put some toilet paper on the wound and crack open the case. I need some entertainment here.' (The guard winces as he brings the case to the foot of the bed and heads to the bathroom.) Guard #2: 'Yes, sir.' (Frisco shakes his head at his reckless guards and waves to a crowd of people who gather around the hole-filled wall. The swiss-cheese looking door falls over as the crowd of guests chatter. The guards look on in embarrassment at the mess they made. A hotel employee frowns at the damage as Frisco whips out a stack of currency from under his bed. He waves it and smiles as the scene fades out.)
