Date: Season I, Episode 14 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason I, Episode 14“Stalking Mossilano”************************************ Location: Marty Verrelli’s Apartment - New York CityDate: January 20, 2003Time: 12:45 pm With her head resting on Marty’s shoulder while they lounged on the sofa, Krista closed hereyes. “Marty,” she said in a far away, soft, voice, “do you love me?” Her voice shook and shefelt tears in her eyes. The question stunned Marty, but he didn’t show it. “Sure,” he told her soothingly. He hoped she was just too close to sleeping to know what shewas saying. “Sure I do.” He kissed her forehead and slowly leaned back further until the sofatook over any labour that his muscles had been performing. Krista’s eyes were closed, and her mind was drifting, but she was far from sleeping. Her small hand reached up and touched his face, and although the tears didn’t recede, she gaveher first genuine smile in days, however small it might have been. *** Location: New York CityDate: January 20, 2003Time: 1:00 pm “Any second now...” The Dragon said quietly as he leaned over the steering wheel of theLumina, staring at the front door of the NY Bank of America. His eyes were like probes,thoroughly inspecting each and every civilian to walk out of those doors. “He’s been in there for a fucking hour,” Snow said from the passenger seat. He turned to look atthe older hitman. “Are you sure he didn’t come out another way?” “Doubt it,” The Dragon said shortly. Snow sighed. “His fucking ride hasn’t moved an inch,” he conceded. John Mossilano’s onlyway out would be through that front exit. “Any thoughts on how we can whack the fucker without having those God damn guardsrecognize us?” The Dragon asked. Snow turned to the older, bald, man. “What if we take them out too?” “Those guys?” The Dragon, for an instant, thought Snow was crazy. “Jesus, kid, they’re armedto the fucking teeth.” “That’s why it would probably be best to get them out of the fucking way before we take out theguy they’re protecting,” Snow said, hardening his eyes on his companion. The Dragon scratched his furry chin and finally agreed. “You’re right,” he sighed. “The onlyway to get to that old fucker is through the guys in that truck. So, genius, any fucking clue onhow we can do that?” Snow looked at the black Blazer about a hundred feet away, where three of John Mossilano’sguards waited for their boss. A fourth guard was in the bank with the Don. Snow thought tohimself and then let out a sigh. “We’ll have to be opportunistic, I guess,” he said. “But one thing’s for sure - there’s no fuckingway we can take him down in front of a crowded bank. We’ll just have to wait.” *** Location: Snow Residence, New York CityDate: January 20, 2003Time: 3:25 pm When she walked through the door, she was met by her sister, Raven, who was smilinguncontrollably. With an unsteady hand, she grabbed Krista by the shoulder, and then couldn’tresist the urge to pull her close and hold her in a tight embrace. “Huge news!” Raven said to her younger sister. “Very, very, huge!” “What’s going on?” Krista asked, half in a daze. “What are you talking about?” “Come!” Raven said, grabbing her younger sister’s hand and leading her to the living room,where she sat her down on the sofa. “I haven’t told anyone yet, and I’m not a hundred percentsure...” “What are you talking about?” Krista repeated, finally coming out of her stunned state. “Ok,” Raven said as she took a deep breath. She couldn’t contain her smile as she grabbed heryounger sister’s hand and closed her eyes. “Krista... ... I’m pregnant!” She was beaming with a joy so pure that it knocked Krista back into her previous dazed state. The younger sister felt overwhelmed by emotion - not good, not bad, but some oblivious feelingthat was exploding from her. And then the tears came. Slow at first, but soon she was crying hard. Raven mistook her tears for joyful ones at first, but soon, she realized that the source of hersisters crying was some deep-set pain. Soon, Krista’s face ended up buried in her sister’s chestas she cried even harder. Raven didn’t understand. “Krista?” she asked, starting to become genuinely afraid. “What’s wrong?” She received no answer, only more tears.
