Tales of A Pharaoh: [Finale]

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/3/02 Tales of A Pharaoh: [Finale] Location: Town with no Name, TexasTime: 9: 35 AM (before Sunday Sabotage) “Akira!” I yelled as I ran towards Akiyama. I had seen him little since the incident at the Café and wished to speak with him greatly… But something was strange about my Friend. He responded with very little enthusiasm. “What do you want?!” he said aggressively. I was taken back by his manner of speaking to me. What in the name of this dreadful planet was wrong with him? I stood there studying my friend. Something was deeply wrong with him, I could tell plainly! “What on the planet Earth is wrong with you, my friend?” I asked curiously. “Never refer to me as your friend, Titu!” he snapped. I jumped back due to the spite that he spoke the words. I couldn’t believe what he had just uttered. “Excuse me?” I said, clearly taken back… “You heard me didn’t you?” he began. “I said Never Refer to me as your friend, for I am the complete opposite!” I stood there thinking about his spiteful words. What was wrong with The Neo Void God? This was not like him at all, that was for sure. “Why do you say these words, Akira? We are Partners, and your words make it seem as though our friendship has come to what I can only call an abrupt end!” I stated. A small crowd had already begun to gather around Akiyama and I. The tension between the two of us was more than clear. “Titu let me make this as clear as possible shall I? You are not my partner any longer, I care not for these titles!” Akira suddenly produced the tag team title belts and tossed them forcefully to the ground. “I spit on these insignificant titles!” before I could stop him the Void God spat on the titles and then kicked dirt from the Texas ground upon them. I gasped, as did the rest of the crowd. “What are you doing and saying?!” I bellowed “Do these not mean anything to you at all?! And after everything we have been through to obtain them!” Akira smirked. “And what have we done to promote them, Xansiv? Nothing at all! We are to have our first title defence on Sunday and already we have held these pieces of gold for the longest reigning time in PCW history! How in the name of my fellow Gods have we pushed these titles to a great recognition? We haven’t, Xansiv, and it is all because of you and your lazy attitude that we have done little to uphold our Titles…or should I say my titles!” I was more taken back than I had been to begin with. Some new and unholy daemon had enveloped the Voltage Fighter and forced his to turn his back on his comrade. “You are more of a Fool that I first gave you credit for, Akiyama!” I shouted before turning back towards the man who had allowed me to stay in his home for the night. I walked a few steps away from my supposed tag team partner, still with my back turned, and then began to walk away from him. “Watch out!” I head a voice cry. Suddenly something hit me. I turned around to see Akira stood with a sick grin on his sadistic face. He had shot me with a bolt of energy, the bastard! I struggled to my feet before shaking off the dirt from my body. “You just made a big mistake, Akira!” I said with a frown. “I doubt it, Pharaoh!” was the reply I received. “Before we do this, Akiyama, tell me where Jay is!” I asked thoughtfully. I had yet to see the blue tipped hair of my other friend and fellow celestial being Jay Sylo. My only thought now was had he turned on me like Akiyama or had he used his sense and gone his own way. I was about to get my answer. “I know what you’re thinking, Pharaoh, and for your information the Celestial knight is with me!” Akiyama laughed with an evil grin. Jay too? What on earth had happened to them? “And what of the bikers we encountered? What happened to them?” I asked curiously. “They have been punished for attempting to challenge Gods such as we…yet now I think that you are no more of a God than these petty people about us.” I ignored his spiteful comment and went on to ask further questions. “What do you mean they have been punished?” Akira arrogantly glared upon me, his hateful eyes met mine and there was silence for a moment. “Some were banished to the void, the remaining three were mutated and set lose upon this land….” The townsfolk made a few hateful remarks, one man shouted, “Kick his ass” to me, to which I smiled. “You have done something evil and un-thoughtful to the land of these people, Akira, why do it?” Akiyama smiled nastily. “Why not? I am a God and I have the power to do as I wish! Do you dare oppose one such as I, Titu?” I thought about his remark carefully. I wanted to battle him, finally I would gain a chance to reveal myself as the dominant God in PCW, but the possibility of an innocent civilians getting hurt was great. “We will battle, Akiyama…” I started to which Akiyama smiled with pleasure. “But not here, leave it to the squared circle!” “You are a wimp, Pharaoh!” he said. “I think not, Akira; for I promise you that we shall have our fight, just not hear…too many are at risk” Akiyama frowned “Very well…” “You do realize that we have to defend the tag team titles on Sunday, don’t you?” I asked. “That I do…but I see no point in doing so…our friendship and partnership is over by a long shot, Pharaoh!” “Then I suggest that we get the match over and done with… quickly!” I replied cockily… It was quite clear that Akiyama had become too arrogant for even me to handle, and to think that I had not seen it coming…I sighed quietly to myself and dropped my head in shame of not seeing such a thing as been imminent… I was about to say something to the void God when suddenly he was gone…as quickly as he had appeared he was gone…only fragments of dirt lingered in the air where he had been standing… “I guess it’s back on the road for me…” I said to myself. “Not before you help out here, Titu!” said the man who to be quite honest I had forgotten about. “I do not know if I have time now, friend…” I stated. “Then you’ll have to make time…” I sighed thinking back to the events that had just happened…Akiyama against me? What had happened? Questions that I would possibly never receive an answer to… “I guess I will…” I said quietly before bowing my head in dismay… The End….
