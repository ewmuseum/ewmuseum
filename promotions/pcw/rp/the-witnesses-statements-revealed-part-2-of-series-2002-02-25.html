The Witnesses Statements Revealed. [part 2 of series]

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/2002 ***Continued from last rp, just to refresh everyone’s minds, since there are those out there.. [Kid Wonder], that can’t remember waking up this morning. Last time we seen Boda, he just pinned Jason Snow in a 6 man tag match on Fusion. To celebrate another ‘moral’ victory over the current World Champion, Boda did a quick interview with Sports Illustrated, then went out for a few drinks. Then, WHAM.... he woke up with a terrible headache, and behind the bars at Boise Correctional Institute. Chris has been lead to a one on one meeting with his lawyer, Brad . Brad has started to fill Boda in on the details of how he ended up in lock up.*** *We open back up in the white room, with a giant mirror on the left wall. Boda is seen sitting in a chair, across from Brad. Boda has his head down, in his hands, and shaking it back and forth. In front of him, pictures of the man that Boda apparently beat half to death. Brad, sitting across from Boda, is on his cell phone, talking to his people back in Orlando. Boda looks up, and grabs one of the pictures that Brad has laid out in front of him* [So, this is the man that seems to have put a stop in my career. I had it all going for me. The fans, the notoriety, and DAMMIT..... I was well on my way to winning the title at March Madness. He1l, I won’t even make it to Turmoil now. Guess Timo has to wrestle Snow and Corey alone. Man.... if it wasn’t for this guy here, I’d have been in that match, and once again, I’d show Snow who the better wrestler is... ME.] *Boda tosses the picture back on the table, and it slides off the other side. Brad hangs up his cell phone, and looks at Chris* Brad: “So..... let me run down the story of what happened, from the witnesses that the Police here have attained.” Boda: “Sure, maybe it’ll help my sudden amnesia.” Brad: “Okay.... here’s the story the witnesses have told the police. Allegedly, you arrived at the Dirty Dick Bar around 11 pm on Friday night.” Boda: “Makes sense. It was after my main event pin over Snow, and after talking with the S.I reporter. Sure, that sounds about right.” Brad: “As witnesses report, you entered the bar, and walked to the bar. Once there, you ordered......” Boda: “A Bacardi and coke. My favorite. Then I went to the restroom.” *So far, it seems that the story Boda remembers, coincides with that of the witnesses. Boda tries to think really hard, about what happened next, because still, it is cloudy.* Boda: “So, I went to the boy’s room.... and, I ran into a man.......” *Brad flips a picture at Boda. It’s the one he saw before, a man, in a Snow t-shirt. Chris nods his head in agreement.* Boda: “Yep, he was in the bathroom, washing his hands. I walked in, and he seen who I was, and got all excited.....’WOW... I can’t believe its Boda. Man, you rock hard.’. Brad, I was real taken aback, because he had on a Snow shirt. So, I asked him....’Hey, how you doing? So, you’re a Snow fan?’. Simple, and not offending. Then he told me yes, he is, has been a fan since PCW2, and what not. He said goodbye, and I went about my business. I mean, come on Brad.... yes the man’s a Snow fan, and yes, I hold 3 victories in a row over him, and sure, come March Madness, I’m walking out the champion.... but, that’s no reason for me to want to beat down a fan.” Brad: “Yes, I know that Chris. I know the PCW World Title is more important to you than that. I know that being THE MAN in PCW holds a higher court for you. But, some one put a man in the hospital, and right now, all arrows point at you. It doesn’t look too good right now. So, what happens after you leave the bathroom, and return to the bar?” Boda: “Let’s see.... I finished up in the bathroom, and headed back to the bar. On the way back, the man wearing the Snow shirt was shooting pool, and asked me to play. When I turned him down, he seemed perturbed, shouting something like....’WHAT, you think you can beat me too, just like Snow? HA’. Something along that line. I just ignored him, and went to drink my Bacardi and Coke. That’s when I woke up here.” *Brad finishes writing the statement from Boda down on a legal pad. He then takes a piece of paper from his backpack, and clears his throat.* Brad: “Ok, Chris.... now, here is the statement from the witnesses. So far, their story matches yours, up to you leaving the bathroom. That’s where it differs. According to eyewitnesses, you left the bathroom, and headed back to the bar. You were approached by.....John Smith..... the man with the Snow shirt on., and asked for an autograph. Apparently, you ignored him, and returned to the bar to drink your Bacardi and Coke. While there, you murmured under your breath something like....’Damn jackass, why would I give a fan of Jason Snow’s an autograph. Bah.’. “ Boda: “Bullshit. I’ve never turned down anyone. Hell, I’ve even given Kid Wonder fans an autograph when asked.” Brad: “Ok... I know that, but again, according to witnesses, you did not. After finishing your first drink, you ordered another, and demanded it to be on the house, something to the effect that you are BODA, and YOU ARE THE MAN. Well, you were told to buy the drink, and that made you mad. So, you still ordered more, and about into your fourth Bacardi and Coke, the Snow fan walks up to you, and pats you on the back, again asking you for an autograph. You shrugged the man off, and pushed him onto a seating position on the stool next to you. From there, you berated him verbally, stating that...’Why in God’s name would I give you MY autograph, when you come to me, wearing that shirt, of the one man, who has made my life miserable the past few weeks, denying me MY gold. Why should I give you MY autograph, while you sit here, worshipping a man that is nothing more than a closet homosexual, that can’t admit that I’M the better man.’. At that point, John Smith, the man in the coma, started to get visibly upset, and you called him ‘A bitch, just like Jason Snow’. So, as the statement here goes, the bartender tried to console the fan, and you called the whole bar a bunch of wetback, cowards. The fan, shot back that you and I quote......’You are a stupid son of a bitch that couldn’t win the World Title if Hank was the champion.’. “ Boda: “Let me guess, from there, I went off?” Brad: “According to witnesses, that is an understatement. You apparently punch John Smith in the face, and Boda Bombed him onto the bar top. You fought back several other patrons, trying to help John Smith, and as Mr. Smith got up, and tried to leave, you grabbed him, and tossed him out the front door and into the parking lot. As you followed him out, amid the screaming of the others, you ripped his Snow shirt off, and spit on it, before chokeslamming the victim onto a car’s hood. From there, the police showed up, and they subdued you. The rest, is that Mr. Smith was rushed to Boise Memorial General Hospital, where he was listed in critical, but stable condition.” Boda: “And I woke up here. Something doesn’t seem right Brad. One week removed from March Madness Pay Per View, and I get arrested for beating up some fan. I just pinned Jason Snow, right here in Boise, and I ‘supposedly’ beat up one of his fans for no particular reason. That doesn’t sit right with me. Yes, I’m a monster, a bad ass in the ring, but, come on.... Brad, you’ve known me since the days I played for The Magic. I’m a lovable person outside the job. Man, you’ve got to help me get out, I’ve got a tag match at Turmoil, not to mention the next change to win the World Title against not only Snow, but 19 others. This can’t be happening, not now, not ever.” *Boda leans back, and spits on the floor, as the door opens, and in steps Sarg. He motions to Brad that the visit is over. Brad stands, as does Chris.* Brad: “I’ll be back on Tuesday morning. We have a date with the county judge. Hopefully, you get your memory back by then, or else, your career, is sunk.” Boda: “Ok. See you then. But, I need to make it to Turmoil.” Sarg: “Oh, about your match at Turmoil...... seems a change has been issued.” Boda: “A change?” Sarg: “Oh yeah, you’ve been unable to follow what’s happening lately. Seems Timo Bolomba jumped ship to OSW, and now, if you happen to make it there, by some miracle, you face Snow and Williams in a handicapped match. If I was you, I’d stay here.” Boda: “If you were me, your wife would be happier. So, Timo left, and I get a shot at beating both Snow and Williams all by myself... sweet. Brad.... get me out of here as soon as possible. I didn’t do that, and you know it. Come Tuesday, some one will spill the truth. I know it. I know some one in PCW has to be behind this. Brad, find out who.” Brad: “All right, see you Tuesday morning.” *Brad leaves, and Boda steps over to were Sarg is. After Brad disappears down the hall, Sarg leads Boda back to his jail cell. What will happen on Tuesday morning? Will some one step forward, and prove Boda didn’t do the things he is alleged to have done? Boda sure hopes so. He’d hate to see his time end like this. The scene fades out as Sarg leads Boda back down the hallways and back to his cell.*
