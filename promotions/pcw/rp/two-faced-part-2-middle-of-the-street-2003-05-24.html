Two Faced - Part 2 - Middle Of The Street

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 24, 2003 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Will you get out of the middle of the street, man?! Your going to get killed! [Harry stands on the side of Route 125 hoping that his brother, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, hears his voice. Because for the past hour Tommy has been standing on the double yellow line of one of the busiest roads in California. Cars roar past at high rates of speed beeping their horns, trying desperately to get this crazy man out of the street. Harry watched Tommy walk out of the house still in his blue underwear, with yellow surfboards on them, and toward Route 125. Harry, dressed in his Iron Maiden T-shirt, isn't totally stoned yet. This is why he noticed Tommy about to sort of commit suicide] Harry: I got dibs on the X-Box then, man! [Harry notices that the words do not phase his brother at all. He just stands on that double yellow line with a sad look on his face. It honestly looks like someone sucked the life out of him but just left a little life in him so that he can function. Harry looks both ways down the road and the cars just whiz by at extremely high rates of speed. He would attempt a rescue mission on his own but it's just far too dangerous to cross the street at this moment and time] Harry: What's wrong, man? Tommy: I'm a greedy loser, dude! Harry: Oh. Tommy: Just go back to the house and I'll be most rad here. Harry: But your in the middle of a busy road, man. How can you be rad? Tommy: Just go home and play some Halo, dude. I'll be there later. [Harry turns around and begins to march back toward the house. Halo does sound good right about now, especially with some sour cream and onion potato chips. Harry does realize that it's only ten in the morning but that's when sour cream and onion have the best flavor. Play a little Iron Maiden in the background while he's blowing stuff up and he's absolute set for the day. As he makes it to a small grass grade he looks back to Tommy just standing in the middle of the road, his arms are extended as if he's waiting for the light of God to beam down onto him. This is just not far out, man. His brother is in trouble and he's thinking about Halo, potato chips, and Iron Maiden. Those things are far out but not when your brother is about to get hit by a car!] Harry: DUDE, Lets just talk about what's bothering you, man! Tommy: I'm two faced! Harry: Wha? Tommy: I'm two faced! Harry: You just have one face, man...it's pretty far out from what I've been told. Tommy: NO! Not actually having two faces, but two faced. You know...like when you act one way but your REALLY another way. Harry: Oh. [Harry still has absolutely NO IDEA what he's talking about. Harry goes back in time, to a place where he was high on pot. Well, he's ALWAYS been high on pot but he is much younger at this time. Tommy was pretty weird at these times, almost like the way he is now. That's when this girl named Ashley stepped in and started talking to Tommy a lot. They spent far too much time together and always went off by themselves into the woods. Harry believes that Tommy just enjoyed sex in the woods with the girl, but he always came back different. He wasn't so evil and weird about things. He was pretty upbeat and always a fun guy to be around. Tommy changed big time during those times and that Ashley girl was always hanging around. Tommy would get happy to see her but also, at the same time, he'd be sort of sad. Sometimes he'd act like he didn't even want to be around her, which just confused Harry further. If you like someone and enjoy their company, why act like you dislike the person? It doesn't make sense, but there is one thing that does make sense...Tommy has been talking to someone lately] Harry: Have you been talking to Ashley again, man? [There is a large moment of silence until a car zooms past with it's horn blaring. Tommy then turns his head a little so that he can see Harry now] Tommy: Last night. Harry: She's not really that far out, man. Everytime you talk to that chick she messes with your head pretty bad. I think that it's best you stay away from her and you get out of that road. Remember, you do need to train for that Skylar Green, Pharaoh match this Sunday! Tommy: I'm training right now. Harry: If you survive getting hit by a car then you can survive them? Is that the training, man? Tommy: No. It's more spiritual than anything. Harry: Well, there is nothing spiritual about standing in the middle of a busy road! Your going to get hit by a car and die, man! Now I'm not a mean person but I'm not in the mood to scrap your face off of the road with a spatula! Tommy: Well, you'll have to have two spatulas to get both of my faces off of the road, dude. Harry: What is WITH you and this two face thing, man! I know it's a character from Batman and I know that it's like these weird siamese twins but what is it with you?! Do you want two faces so that you can get more chicks or something? I just don't get it! [A tractor trailer flies by with it's large horn blaring for the crazy pedistrian to get out of the road. Tommy just stands his ground without even flinching at all. His blue eyes are so focused and in thought that it's no wonder he doesn't realize what's going on around him. To him, it's as if there is only Harry and himself on that road. There is nothing else to get in their way and get in the way of Tommy's dream to have two faces. Well, that according to Harry who still doesn't understand that whole deal] Tommy: You have never noticed the way I've been acting over the past twenty years? Ever since our fathers died things have never been the same for me, dude. I've totally went inward on myself and only thinking that people are truly bogus. I always tried to look for the bad in people and always looked out for the people who only mattered most. Those people were Mom, you, and Ashley. There was nobody else in this world that I was willing to trust or let in my life. Then that's when Ashley let me know that not everybody is totally bogus on the inside. Not everybody is looking to take advantage of me and that people just want to have a good time. I changed, dude. I changed into a person that felt reallly good to me and I figured that this was the person I was truly destined to be. The only problem was that people were taking advantage of me. They were using me for my money and just to make themselves look good to be around me. I then realized that to get ahead maybe I could be less generous, only see the bogusness that is inside of people. That's when things started happening again, bro. Last night Ashley came back to me and she told me some things that really made me think. I REALLY don't know who I am anymore, Har. I don't know whether I'm this totally heinous dude who steps on people to stay on top or if I'm this fun loving compadre who is just down with having a good time. I'm two faced and I don't even know which one to wear! Harry: Where did Ashley come from, man? I thought that she got married five years ago and then took off to the east. Tommy: So did I. She just appeared out of nowhere telling me that things weren't like they were. That her marriage was just a complete joke and that she wanted me in the worst way. I thought that I was just going to have sex with yet another bodacious babe, but for some reason something was holding me back from doing it. I've never really felt like that with another babe, only Ashley. Harry: So, she told you all kinds of bogus stuff, man? Tommy: It seemed bogus at the time like she didn't know what she was talking about, but now that I think about it she seemed to really hit the nail on the head. Harry: Yeah, man. Tommy: Do you remember her wedding day? How she called me to ask how I was? Harry: Yeah, man. Tommy: Well, she told me some things that I never really thought about until now. She told me how the time that she spent with me was like she was already married, had already found her true happiness. I just figured that maybe she was making me feel better about how I was such a good friend to her for so long. Now that I really think about that it's as if she was secretly telling me that I was the compadre that she wanted to marry, and not some heinous sports reporter from New Jeresey. Harry: New Jeresey is just not far out, man. Tommy: Harry, I don't know if I'm going to be able to defend my title on Sunday. We've been doing a lot of totally heinous things to a lot of people and I don't know if I'm going to be able to go on much longer. I think I need to find out which person I am before I really do anything else because I think people are going to end up getting hurt from all of this. Harry: We have hurt people, man. That's in the past and it isn't going to matter when you step in the ring on Sunday. Your going to go out there and your going to beat those two guys. Your going to retain that title and then we're going to find out which one of those faces your going to wear, man. [Tommy sighs and then turns to Harry. A couple cars race past and Tommy then walks back across the street. Just as he makes it to the grass grade Harry is on two tractor trailers zoom past coming so close to Tommy that his blue underwear blow upward almost exposing his private parts. Harry's eyes just bug out of his head as he can't believe what he just saw. Tommy then joins his brother on the grass grade and the two men share a good hug] Harry: I'll tell ya one thing, man. I really liked that one face where you did that Tubular Tyke thing. I loved those retards! Tommy: Bodacious. Fade To Black
