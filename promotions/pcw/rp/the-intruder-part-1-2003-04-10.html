The Intruder Part 1

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-03 RP 1 Turmoil 15th BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The echo of a 12 gage double barrel shotgun bounces off the mountains at her Tennessee home. Teresa stands clad in dirty powdered Levi’s and a worn out brown Winston tee shirt. Her feet are covered in dark brown steel toe boots. In the center of the field behind her cabin is her, her Jack Russell Terrier named Bacon, and the squirrels hiding from the loud shotgun. Teresa leans her shotgun against her Honda Rancher four wheeler, which is parked beside her. Bacon chases a butterfly while Teresa lights up a cigarette. The past month our Top Rope Temptress has been laying low. You can’t really blame her with the nedia hunting her down like the pack of wolves they are. Anyone that knows anything about Teresa Tomas knows she hates questions and she despises interviews. Considering her publicly known temper problem, it would take a very brave and quite stupid human being to approach her about anything and everything. Ask her about her mother, she’ll laugh in your face. Ask her about her brother, you better expect a punch to the nose. Ask her about her love life and the certain individual she has been involved with for the past two or three years and you better run cause this little lady don’t miss when she aims her gun. The final puff of the cigarette enters her lungs slowly. They say nicotine kills. So does alcohol but you don’t see the government outlawing beer. After five seconds or so holding it in, Teresa exhales the smoke and flicks the butt to the side. She places the shotgun on the rack across the front of her ATV and climbs on. With a turn of the key and a push of a button, the Honda Rancher rides to the cabin. Bacon follows close behind. The log cabin is small with only four rooms, living room, bedroom, kitchen and a small bath. She has deer heads decorating the walls in the living room and stuffed small game sitting on shelves in the bedroom. The kitchen is decorated in an old style theme. Antique pots and pans hang from the wall, as well as various utensils used 30 or more years ago. The floor through out the cabin is hardwood. The walls are made of wood as well as the ceiling. Teresa knew something wasn’t right when she parked her four wheeler next to the front porch. The door is ajar and she can hear someone or something walking around inside. “Damn intruders. I’ll show ‘em what happens when you break in someone’s house.” Teresa grabs her shotgun and quietly makes her way to the front door. She pushes it the rest of the way open with her foot and looks around. Nothing. She can still hear the footsteps, but they’re coming from the kitchen. The gun is already loaded so all Teresa has to do is point the double barrels ahead as she slithers her way to her own kitchen. Who knows what weirdo could be stalking her? The barrels enter the kitchen first followed by a man’s scream and glass shattering all over the floor. Teresa peers over her gun and sees a short scrawny man dressed in a business suit and brown hair slicked back. His hands are held up and her favorite beer mug is in millions of pieces by his feet. “Please Miss Tomas. Don’t shoot Me.” The man’s voice quivers in fear. Teresa’s finger resting on the trigger. “Who the hell are you and why are you in MY cabin?” The man kneels cautiously and begins to pick up pieces of glass from the floor. Teresa lowers the gun toward him. “I’m Bobbie from the Nashville Times. Your mother told me where to find you. I’ll pay for your mug. Just don’t shoot Me.” BOOM!
