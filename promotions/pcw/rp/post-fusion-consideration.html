Post-Fusion Consideration...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Recently... [[As the cameras fade in from static, the scene begins to open up backstage after Fusion at the Coleman Center, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. As the cameras focus in more so, they pick up on a dark, shadowed room, lit vaguely with an array of bright candles. Certain areas of the locker room, as has been described, are illuminated heavily, and as the cameras pan about the fairly large room, they show the irate visage of Titu Xansiv. Beside him, as always, are his darling wife, Isiy, and a regiment of guards adorned in ancient Egyptian armour.]] The Pharaoh: “By the Gods! What the Duat happened out there?! Where in the name of Ra were you?!” [[Titu points towards the gathered guards with a scowl. Upon his forehead, he holds a wet cloth, cooling his skull and skin from the chair shot delivered to him from Totally Tubular Tommy earlier in the night.]] The Pharaoh: “Why the Duat did that idiotic excuse for a World Champion have to get involved in my business anyhow?!” [[He pounds the air scornfully, clenching his fists with rage.]] The Pharaoh: “Why, in the name of Horus, Skylar and I were engaged in a crucial match! One which pitted our respective skills and ring-know-how against one another! And then along comes that peasant-like ragtard and destroys EVERYTHING! [[Titu sighs angrily]] The Pharaoh: “You people should have seen it coming and made your way to the ring to take him out! It’s what I PAY you for!” [[Upon these words, a lone guard steps forward, a brave look about his face. His colleagues share a worried glance as the man coughs, rendering Pharaoh’s attention to divert from the mayhem earlier in the night unto him.]] Guard: “But, sir; we can’t be everywhere at once! You wished for us to remain back here with your wife. How were we supposed to know Tommy would get involved in your business anyway? Surely you can understand—” The Pharaoh: “Are there no monitors back here? Can’t you fools see what’s going on out there?” [[The guard’s brow furrows defiantly.]] Guard: “But you INSIST that we don’t watch the TV monitors! You yourself say they’re bad news.” [[A grin slips over Isiy’s mouth as her husband stares at the guard with a stale expression.]] The Pharaoh: “Such insolence—” Isiy: “Oh, shut up, Titu! The guy’s got a point, you know? They were back here ‘protecting’ me! I mean, you DID tell them to!” [[Xansiv scowls at his wife’s mocking words.]] The Pharaoh: “Well, all I’m trying to say is: you could have at least TRIED to keep an eye on me as well as Isiy. I mean, how many of you ARE here tonight?” [[The guard takes a step back, shrugging.]] Guard: “Fifteen…maybe seventeen at most.” The Pharaoh, grinning confidently: “Indeed! Could you have not split into two groups?” [[The guard goes to answer but Isiy sighs, quickly interrupting the armoured soldier.]] Isiy: “And where were your ‘friends’ Bruce and Dorn Tracer in your time of ultimate need? You can’t just blame the guards, Titu.” [[Pharaoh sighs mournfully, realizing his stable-mates were indeed nowhere to be seen when Tommy attacked.]] The Pharaoh: “True. I feel a rift is growing in the group. I must remember to have a word with them when I next see them anyway. This obsession with The Druid is growing annoying.” [[Titu sighs, placing his face in his hands.]] The Pharaoh: “All Dorn ever speaks of these days is this damned unknown stalker! Can’t he see I have more to think about? Skylar Green’s little competitions have yet to come to an end after all. Why, this coming Turmoil I must team with that Ukrainian to face James Edwards and Green himself!” Isiy, quickly: “And that’s not to mention next Fusion.” [[Titu turns to her, confused.]] The Pharaoh: “Huh? What ever do you mean?” [[Isiy takes from her coat pocket a sheet of doubled paper. Unfolding it, she hands the document to Titu. He gasps as he reads through the content.]] Isiy: “Looks like you might get your chance to see to Tommy after all.” The Pharaoh, ignoring her: “BY THE GODS! Skylar AGAIN? Submission match? Bah! With Tommy as the referee?!” [[He screws the paper up and tosses the now tarnished card across the room.]] The Pharaoh: “This grows more and more preposterous by the day!” [[Titu rubs his eyes tiresomely. The guard from earlier speaks up.]] Guard: “Milord, perhaps things aren’t quite AS bad as they SEEM. Take for instance the fact that with Totally Tubular Tommy as the referee for your match at Fusion, you’ll know EXACTLY where he is and what he’s doing. There won’t be any chance of him interfering, will there?” [[Titu strokes his chin thoughtfully.]] The Pharaoh: “True.” Guard, continuing: “And you’ll finally get your chance to defeat Skylar. Plus, you ARE quite the master of submission, sire!” [[The few of the guards giggle. Isiy blushes somewhat. Titu doesn’t quite catch on.]] The Pharaoh: “I see where you’re coming from, soldier, and I like it! By Thoth, I can’t believe I didn’t sign you up as my personal advisor!” [[Titu laughs, as does the guard; although his laugh seems a little forced.]] Guard: “Indeed, sir.” [[Titu runs a hand through his thick mane and laughs slightly, the black hair running gently down his back.]] The Pharaoh: “So, I have Skylar to deal with once more come Fusion? And this Turmoil I must defeat him and James Edwards with the help of that Ukrainian sniper, eh?” [[Titu chuckles much more, but it seems to hurt his still aching skull. He clenches the watery towel to his face again and grimaces.]] Isiy: “Are you Ok?” [[Pharaoh waves a hand dismissively.]] The Pharaoh: “Yes, yes, of course. Just give me a moment here.” [[Titu adds pressure to the raised bump on his forehead and after a moment he continues to speak.]] The Pharaoh: “That damned chair shot near enough took off my head! For that, however, I shall make sure Totally Tubular Tommy feels my wrath! I guess that ‘surfer’ should-be-Hebrew can’t quite remember when I beat him last month!” [[Isiy nods.]] Isiy: “Just remember that on Fusion when he’s the ref. You already beat him, so there’s nothing to worry about.” [[Titu laughs off the comment.]] The Pharaoh: “WORRY?! That’s the least thing on my mind! I already hold a victory over our current ‘World Heavyweight Champion’, so why the Duat should I WORRY about him? Yes, he took a chair to my face on Turmoil, but anyone is strong with a weapon. We’ll see how well he fares when he finally meets me in the ring again!” [[Titu stands from his seat and pushes past the gathered guards. Across the room stands a monument to Ra; a colossal statue shrouded in darkness, but enlightened faintly with assembled candles. Titu kneels before it as the others watch on in awe.]] The Pharaoh: “With the aid of the Gods, I SHALL overcome these obstacles…mark my words.” Isiy: “As you say.” [[Scene fade.]]
