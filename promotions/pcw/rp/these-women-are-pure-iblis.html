These Women Are Pure Iblis...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Harem Troubles Recently, PCW has not seen much of resident goliath, Alexis Parshin, and many fans have questioned whether he deserves his spot in PCW. This Sunday, at the Wrestle Vessel Pay Per View, those questions will be answered, either affirmatively or negatively, and each one will have its consequences… There are two people to be dealt with. First, there is the pathetic insect known as Brandi. The fact that Alexis Parshin is being put in the ring with Brandi is humiliating at that, but for people to actually predict a win from the weakling?! Preposterous! Then of course there is the traitor to all of Islam who has become a hero to PCW’s moronic fans: The Chef. First it was the rolling pin shot, and now the verbal attacks. At Wrestle Vessel, it will be put up or shut up for both Parshin and Chef in one final showdown. “Raghab, please answer the phone!” The booming voice of Alexis Parshin comes over the screen as we see the Uzbek Annihilator sitting on a bed with a hot water pad resting on his head. He has on a large undershirt and military pants, while Raghab, who waddles onto the scene, has on a dark gray t-shirt with a picture of Malcolm X on it and red sweat pants, but still not looking like the usual eccentric Raghab. Moving over to the phone, Raghab picks up the receiver and listens to the shouting on the other end, pulling his ear away quickly. “Errmm…Konstantin, it happens to be for you. It is your wife, Illyena.” The huge man groans as he hears this news, which comes as a shock to most PCW fans, who wouldn’t have known that any woman could marry an individual so hell-bent on evil as Alexei Parshin. Lumbering over to the phone, Parshin sits down as he picks up the phone. “Yes dear?” He tries to sound as convincingly sweet as possible as he speaks into the receiver, certainly not in his normal booming tone of voice, but a softer, more tuned down voice. However, the voice that responds to him is very much less content. “Konstantin Aliyev Alexei Parshin, you haven’t called your pretty little cupcake in over two weeks! You have some explaining to do.” It seems very strange to hear someone, especially a woman, bossing around a man who could easily toss almost any living person around. Even more surprising is the reaction that is received by the woman from the huge man. His voice sounds almost…trembling as he replies into the phone. “Well…Illyena, you know that I have much work to do for infiltration.” This is obviously not a response that is to Illyena’s liking, because the woman quickly shoots back a response to Alexis, “I have work, but you not care? You not love me?!” Alexis squints, trying to figure a way out of his current situation. He puts on his most loving voice, now sounding like a bad soap opera actor, although he probably wouldn’t approve of anyone calling him that, seeing as soap operas are a capitalist creation of the worst kind. “Of course Alexei loves you. It is just that I can only smite so many infidels at a time, dear.” This gets more of a loving response from the supposed “wife” of the Uzbek Annihilator, who just sighs and responds softly. “Having me gone, is hard, no?” Her broken English sounds even more broken through the thousands of miles that the call is traveling. “Yes, it is terrible, Illyena. Now let me get some sleep, I have much work to do.” Illyena agrees and the two give their cordial loving goodbyes, and Alexis breathes a sigh of relief. “Raghab, how has such terrible fate befallen me?” Raghab is not quite sure of Alexis’ meaning and replies in the safest way possible. “I’m not sure what you mean, Konstantin.” As Parshin leans back on the bed, it can be heard creaking under his tremendous weight. “I mean women, are they not a gift and a curse from Allah? Did he not give them for pleasure and punishment?” Raghab nods solemnly, of course not knowing anything on the subject, not being the most attractive or toughest person. Once again, the phone rings, and yet another groan comes from the huge man. Raghab walks apprehensively towards the phone, fearing the same screaming voice of a woman in his ear. But no, he doesn’t hear that, he hears the voice of a different screaming woman. “Konstantin, it is for you again. It is your wife Corenya.” Yes, you heard correctly, it was a different name, because as many of us know, Arab men of power often have upwards of ten wives, in what is known today as a harem. However, the harem of Alexis Parshin seems not to be so successful at the moment, as another loud groan comes from the large man, who has just settled back down. As he walks over to the phone again, the disappointment is evident on his face as he picks up the receiver, this time to a very angry Corenya. “Hello darling how is-” However, Corenya seems in no mood to listen, she is very interested in doing all the talking right now, as she proves when she interrupts Parshin. “Don’t call me darling when I have not heard from you in month! I not interested in stupid excuses! You come home to see me now or I leaving!” Her English is broken as well, but Parshin is used to it, and has grown more accustomed to speaking English then his native language at this point. He listens for a few more minutes, and finally is almost falling asleep from boredom and exhaustion as he hangs up the phone for the second time. “Raghab, please go back to your room, I need my sleep. We have much training ahead.” Raghab nods obediently, and closes the door as Alexis quietly slides under his blankets, thanking Allah for the momentary break from the Iblis known as women… Ring! Ring!
