Therapy

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14, 2003 [The bald man with the mustache sits with confusion on his face. His dark eyes are focused on something in front of him with such concentration that it's hard to imagine exactly what it could be. His tiny glasses hang down so far on the bridge of his nose that it's no wonder he even has a use for them. He leans backward as the confusion seems to fade away and he's now ready to listen. See...that's what therapists do] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Jellyfish. Therapist: Jellyfish? Tommy: Jellyfish. [The therapist seems to be confused yet again. He looks down at his fuzzy brown sports jacket and picks off a few pieces of lint. He looks back up to the tanned, perfect looking Tommy with answers in his mind] Therapist: I believe your lying to me, Mr. Gordo. Tommy: Tommy or Champ will do, dude. Therapist: Tommy. Tommy: I prefer champ though. Therapist: I suppose that explains why your carrying a large golden belt with you. There was no need to bring it here with you, Tommy. Tommy: Well, it's like my American Express card. I NEVER leave home without it. [Tommy smiles brightly showing off the perfect white teeth that a dentist would be proud to see, but hate it at the same time because the patient won't give him good business with teeth like that. The therapist just shifts in the brown leather chair scanning all of the awards he's received over the years for helping athletes cope with their mental bruises. Roger Callahan truly feels that athletes and entertainers endure such brutal mental struggles that nobody would ever understand...except him of course. Many stars have been completely empty inside almost ready to end it all and Roger steps in, and he takes the athlete to a wonderful cure] Roger: Well, that's nice. So, since your the champion of a major wrestling federation, how much pressure is placed onto your shoulders? Tommy: A lot, dude. Roger: Yes, I see. Now with all of that pressure of being the champion your only true fear is of jellyfish. Tommy: TOTALLY! Have you ever been stung by one of those heinous things? Roger: No, I don't go to the beach a whole lot. Tommy: I see why. [Tommy points to the large gut that Roger is sporting. Yes, Roger knows that he is fairly obese and most human beings cover their eyes at the sight of him, but he doesn't need some pretty surfer boy to tell him that] Roger: I think that your hiding many things from everyone, including yourself. Just you being here for the past five minutes shows me that your trying to convince yourself of something which you are not. You seem to have truly gotten caught up in the show and your projecting that into your own character, which is making you seem extremely out of character. Are you following me so far, Tommy? Tommy: No. Roger: Oh...well.... [Roger doesn't really know how else to dumb down his explanation. He could just out right tell Tommy that he's so stupid he's believing himself to be a super villain, which he's not. He may be an arrogant jerkface but far from super villain status] Tommy: This is bogus, I've got babes to see. Roger: That's another subject I'd like to touch on. Women. Why must you surround yourself with women all of the time, but not the same one...different women? [Tommy sits on the leather couch with no emotion wrapped onto his blemishless face] Tommy: You obviously don't get many babes. Roger: One doesn't have to be a 'chick magnet' to be a ladies man. I honestly believe that you need to surround yourself with women to project an image that is not you. Tommy, you are not a ladies man, but you are still just a child who is still figuring out his sexuality. Tommy: Dude, are you trying to say that I'm gay? Roger: Not directly. Tommy: Oh. Roger: You seem so confused about what you want. It's typical in a man your age. You question your decisions and the path that you have taken so far. It may seem successful in financial terms, but is it successful for your mind? Do you feel fulfilled? Tommy: Of course I feel fulfilled, I'm the champion of the world. It's most bodacious. Roger: Did you win it fairly? Tommy: OF COURSE!! [Tommy seems a little defensive as he sputters the last exclamation. Honestly, winning the PCW title was not done by cheating but it was a stoke of luck. When there are ten men in a ring at one time there is bound to be anything that could happen. Tommy was a victim of fate and of being in the right place at the right time] Roger: Tommy, you are suffering a lot on the inside, aren't you? The demons are scratching your heart with their venomous claws! I understand exactly what your going through and I'm only here to help you. Just tell me what it is, tell me what is the thing that makes you this way? [Tommy sits on the couch with his beaming tan face slowly going toward the sad side of things. His blue eyes are as serious as they can get and he looks into the dark eyes of this totally bogus therapist. With sadness weighing on his soul, Tommy can only say...] Tommy: Your totally bogus. Roger: Me? Bogus? Tommy: TOTALLY! Look at this belt... [The therapist looks at the belt] Tommy: ...this means that I'm PCW Champion. I am the totally bodacious ambassador of the Primetime! I've beaten the best of the best to get to this totally radical position in my career. In fact, on Tuesday I'm teaming up with a most bodacious babe to take out two totally heinous compadres...Vendakamps Pentypalms and Rent-A-Jerkface! Roger: Vendakamps Pentypalms? Tommy: Yeah...I don't know where these dudes think of this stuff but that's who is challenging me for THIS gnarly gold. Roger: Ok. Tommy: So why don't we talk about you, dude. We spent all this time talking about all these bogus demons in my head...what about you? Your bald, fat and totally ugly. I bet you have a lot worse issues than I do. You pet the pony don't you? [The therapist seems a little off balance now. His dark eyes are shifting from left to right as the embarrassment covers his face in a crimson red] Roger: Well... *AHEM* ...who doesn't? Tommy: I sure don't. Roger: That's a lie. Tommy: Dude, I'm with so many babes that it's totally unnecessary for a compadre like me to do that. Babes are just like apples...one a day keeps the one hand mambo machine away. I think that you need more confidence in yourself. I bet that you go to the bar and you start to scan the scene. I can see your chubby face right now looking over the bar and those heinous dark eyes of yours are getting all wide. The entire bar is filled with willing middle aged babes who just need a good give 'n' go. To any bodacious dude, like me, it would be childs play. For an ugly compadre like yourself you have to use that personality thing to get you babes... [Roger seems a bit flustered as he puts his hand up] Roger: This session is not about me, nor is it about my sex life. This session is for you and you alone. Tommy: And I can do whatever I want with it. I'm totally free of those cursed demons you were talking about and now it's time to talk about how bogus you are. Roger: I am not bogus. Tommy: Yes you are. I bet that when your in that gnarly bar you just sit in the corner and fantasize about what it would be like. You probably don't even make the totally craptacular attempt for it. Roger: Well, some girls laugh at me. Tommy: Because of that weird mole on your head? Roger: Yes. Tommy: Well, you just wear a rug. It's that simple dude. Or you go to one of those guys...you know....those doctors that deal with the skin... Roger: Dermatologist? Tommy: No!! That's not it! Proctologist is what I was looking for. Roger: That's for....uhhh...nevermind. Tommy: You just go to a proctologist and have that thing cut right off. Then thats one thing they can't laugh at anymore. And if your really serious about getting down with this you could always spend half an hour a day on a treadmill, dude. You have to do some walking, not just stand on it, and you'll be much less fat. Roger: These are things that I already know.... Tommy: Then why are you bitching about it to me? Roger: I never bitched to you about this. You just brought it up because your only avoiding your own problems. Tommy: Pft, whatever, dude. I killed those demons you were talking about. It's all good in the hood now, dude. Roger: Of course. Tommy: Just remember...the ladies enjoy confidence. You walk in there and just talk to them like you do to your heinous patients then you should have nothing to worry about. I shouldn't be helping you because of the entire super villain thing, but I'm feeling most generous...especially since becoming Champion. [A bell rings in the background and Roger picks up a timer from the small desk to his right. He shrugs his shoulders at Tommy] Roger: Looks like our time is up. Tommy: Yeah. I've got to go scan some babes down by the docks. Good luck, dude. Roger: Aren't you coming back next week? Tommy: For what? I'm totally cured. Thanks, Doc. [Tommy walks out of the office with quickness before Roger can say anything] Roger: Oh... [Roger picks up the phone and dials] Roger: Hi, I'd like to make an appointment with Dermatology please. [Fade To Black]
