The Trial...(Part I)

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-17-02 ::Introduction:: With the win over Frampton that Mark has been looking for in a while set him in relief. He now knows what it takes to start to win in PCW and it takes a lot of hard work. If no one knew that already. Now when a javalin came into the ring Mark was surprised. He thought that who could this be doing this to him? He know nows that Ryan Stephens wants to fight him on Fusion in a standard match. Really who is Ryan Stephens first of all? Mark does not know and he threatens Mark by saying he will put a rumor about him and Jaden. But although there is nothing going on between the two and nothing never will because Mark doesn't have any feelings for women these days in his life still. Fusion is the event that Mark shines at every week and this week it shouldn't be any different because Mark is going to go out and do his best like he does ever single week until he receives the respect of people in the PCW. ::End Introduction:: Mark is sitting down in a little room when we see him first. He is sitting in some black basketball shorts without a shirt on and without any shoes on. He looks up and you can see the anger and intensity in his bright blue eyes that are hard to see in the near light that there is shining. There is only a single light above his head that is shining down right on top of Mark's head. 'Who is Ryan Stephens by the way?' Mark asks as he is looking right in front of him concentrating on a spot on the wall that is in front of him. 'Stephens is just a big ego maniac who cares about trying to get people kicked out of the PCW. Who cares about your rumors anyways? They are probably all not true just like the one you just showed that lady. Like about the American History X star, Ed Norton, I had to get that through him before I could use it. So forget that rumor because that is not true.' 'Mr. Makonie please come this way!' a guard dressed in a brown suit just like a sheriff. Mark gets up from the stool and he turns around to be seen by the guard. There are jail bars right in front of Mark and it looks that he is in some kind of jail place. The gate of his cell opens and the guard goes up to Mark to escourt him to the next place possible. He goes through the hallways of the other cells with people grabbing at him hands and trying to pull him towards their cell. But Mark makes sure that he stands right next to the guard. 'Right in there is where you need to get changed.' says the guard who shows him the door to where a orage jail suit is located. Mark doesn't say anything all he does is he just goes into the room and he closes the door in shame. In the room Mark is puttin the orage jail suit on over his legs and then zipping it up to his chest area. He then puts on some black shoes that are laying there right next to the chair that he has to wear for where he is going. He comes out of the room and the guard nods his head. 'Keep on following me you racist man you!' says the guard pointing at Mark to follow his back. Mark still doesn't say anything as he looks at the newly waxed concrete floor. He looks as if he is in shame and regrets for what he did to be here. The guard opens the door to the waiting room that is neatly decorated with flowers and pictures. 'Sit down right there and don't speak or else it will get worse for you.' the guard says as he leaves the room and he goes through two big wooden doors. Mark sits in the waiting room and he sees a secretary looking at her screen and just doing some random typing. She never looks over or anything like that, she just keeps on typing away on her neat little computer. Mark doesn't have anything to talk about so he is thinking about what is going to happen to him. Stephens is back in his mind somewhere, but right now Mark is worried about what he is about to go in here for. He is not thinking about how he is going to shine on Fusion he is thinking about how he is going to survive this situation that he is going to be put in. 'Please follow me Makonie.' the guard comes back and he goes over to the waiting room, grabbing Makonie's jail suit at the chest and bringing him up. Mark still doesn't say anything as he is yanked up by the guard who looks as if he is around 260 pounds weighing wise. Mark follows him again into the trial room in the court. 'Here comes the man who is responsible for this act.' the judge says who is standing up and pointing to Mark as he walks through the double doors. Mark walks down the aisle to the judge and to where his table is at. He goes by the rows of people who are in the jury a lot of them being black as well. Mark looks at all of the people who he passes, some women are crying, others just have their heads turned to Mark. Mark goes through the swinging doors and into the area where the judge stand is at and all of that good stuff in there that people sit at. He goes and sits down at the table by his attorney. He looks over at the other table and there is a white detective and a black man in a blue suit and blue suede shoes. 'Court is now in session!' the judge yells as he sits down and he bangs his little hammer against the wooden piece on his stand. Mark looks down at the table and he is on the inside of his attorney. He looks at the water next and he grabs a cup from the stack. He pulls the pitcher of ice water into a cup and he starts to drink it. He then pours another cup and he downs that ice cold water that is cold as ice down his throat when he swallows. 'Now Mark Makonie you have been convicted of killing an African American in the city of Pasadena, California.' the judge says as he is reading from the trial paper. 'This happened just a year ago when you were still wrestling. I will not let the people tell you the story.' 'I would like to call Henry Walzo to the stand please.' says the white detective as he stands up in front of the whole jury. A man from the jury who has white hair and he is dressed in Hawaiian shirts and blue jeans. He heads past the two swinging doors and to the witness stand. A cop goes over to Henry and he gives him the whole spiel on the bible and then Henry agrees. 'So Henry where were you on the afternoon of December the 12th 2001?' asks the detective making eye contace with Henry. 'Well I was sitting in my bar of course cleaning the counter when we don't have any customers.' says Henry as he is looking right at the jury. 'Then who comes into the door of your bar while you are cleaning your counter?' asks the detective as he looks at Henry trying to get the answer out of him. 'Well Mark Makonie comes into my bar just like a regular guy and orders a beer.' Henry starts to explain. 'Then we get talking and an African American comes into the bar, not thinking anything about this. Then Makonie says a racial comment and everything just let loose from there.' Mark has his head up making contact with Henry as he tells his story. He is saying to himself, that is not true at all, that is not the whole story in how it went down. He takes the water again as his attorney feels worried for Mark about this situation and how he is going to handle it. 'So then what happened after the two started to get into a bar room brawl?' asks the detective putting his arm on the stand resting it. 'Then Mark threw the African American out of the window which shattered onto the street.' Henry explains as he tries to remember what exaclty happened. 'Then I called the police and next I see is the African American laying on the ground with a pool of blood around him. Then I didn't see Mark Makonie after that man was dead around Pasadena.' 'There you have it jury, Mark Makonie is a killer of African Americans.' says the detective coming off of the stand with his arm and to the swinging doors. Mark knows that that is not true and he will prove it when he gets his time to. The attorney is going through his briefcase and fumbling through papers as the detective is still speaking. 'I have no more witnesses to call up at this point.' says the detective as he goes back to his seat. Henry gets up from the witness stand and he goes by the two groups of people sitting down. He smiles as Mark as Mark stares at him going by him. Mark looks back at the table and he takes another swig from the pitcher instead of putting it in a cup this time. 'It is your turn to tell the story Mr. Superattorneythatmarkpaidalotfor.' says the judge as he looks right at Mark's attorney who is still fumbling around with his papers. Mark just puts his head down on the table and he starts to think about the situation that he is in. He thinks about Stephens but only about how the match will be a good one for a Fusion event, but better yet he wants to know how he is going to get out of this predicament. (Stay Tuned to Find Out)
