Chris Is Getting Sweaty

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-16-03 Was the match punishment? It seemed so strange that he was now booked in such a match right after losing a bout. Chris Storm would face the team of Erik Erratic and Stryker. This came after he lost on Friday’s Fusion. Was he being punished? Could that lost really mean that much? He lost before and it was no big deal. After all, Tessa was being rewarded. She is booked in a title match while Chris must go into a handicap match. Something has to give. There must be a reason for what is going on. Only the minds of certain few know what truly is going on. A small dripping noise echoes out. Drip…drip…drip…It is followed by heavy breathing. In and out, in and out, his chest moves. Sweat rolls down his body, dropping to the floor. The blond hair dips down over his forehead, sticking to it. “Yes, yes, this is it. This feels so good.” He says. His voice rippling from his throat, as the words roll off his tongue. His chest moves in and out with each deep breath. His body is shaking now. He closes his eyes, hiding those deep blue orbs of his. “Oh yes, this is how it should be. This is what it’s all about.” He was in pure ecstasy, running on pure emotion now. Every inch of his body was sweating. His was all worked up now. The breathing gets deeper and heavier. His muscles start to get tight. He feels like he is about ready to pass out, but he has to keep going, pushing harder and harder. “Oh…here it comes.” Finally he does it. Chris Storm lifts the large weight over his head. His feet kick apart, holding his balance. He holds the massive weights high over his head as he works out. He holds it for a few seconds the releases it behind him, letting them fall to the ground with a thunderous slam. Chris steps out from it and grabs a towel. He wipes the sweat off his forehead. Taking a short break, he sits down on a nearby bench. “My goodness, what a work out that was. I haven’t pushed myself that hard since, well…” He thinks about it, laughing slightly. “…since a long time.” That has always been one of the strange things about Chris. The only other thing that could get him as worked up as women was training for a match. The idea of pushing his body to the limits and becoming stronger and faster was always a turn on to him. He never understood why though. It was just one of the things he had learned to accept in his life. Just like how the trees are green and the sky is blue. There are things you do not ask questions about. Chris mind wasn’t fully into his work out for once however. It was continuing to drift off into thoughts of this coming Tuesday and his match against Erik Erratic and Adam Stryker. He couldn’t get over the fact that he was booked in a handicap match. His first handicap match in a long time. Now facing two hard opponents is bad enough, especially when your opponents are both former World champions of their respected leagues. Course if there was one thing Chris Storm enjoyed more then anything, it was a good challenge. And that is just what he will be getting from this match, something to push him to his limits. Normally a person would be scared going into a match like this. However Chris is far from normal and he was pumped for such a match. “Only three more days, then I can have my pay back. I get to settle a score one last time.” He says. Chris pushes himself off from the bench. Chris walked over toward where the wall mirrors was. No one else was around as Chris had reserved the gym for only himself. He didn’t want others in the gym to distract him from his training. Not even Tessa. This match was more then just a mere handicap match to him. Chris wanted to win. He had to win. Yet something kept pulling at his conscious. “Was this match punishment? If so, then for what?” he had to ask. It did seem rather strange that he was booked in a handicap match. After all, the Front Office was famous for punishing people with dangerous matches. Chris was just thankful it wasn’t against Two Ton. But still, something was up and Chris wanted to know what. He admired his glistening body as he thought about it. Chris smiled, “The perfect body; the perfect wrestler; the perfect boyfriend; the absolute vision of perfect.” Chris starts to bounce back and forth, from one foot to another. He throws a few punches toward the mirror. “Yeah, I’m perfect. The Perfect Storm!” he says. Chris laughs to himself in a deep voice. He punches his chest as he laughs out. He suddenly feels a tug on his sweat pants. Chris jumps and backs against the mirror. Shocked and surprised, he looks around at who is there. He hears the giggling noise of two little boys. Chris lets out a sigh of relief. He leans over and looks at Evan and Alex standing there giggling. “How did you two little rascals get in here?” he asked them. Chris looked around for a way they could have possibly gotten here. He could have sworn he had let them behind. “Chris, we wanna train with you, so we can be big and strong like you.” Evan says. Chris smiles at the comment. He put his hand on Evan’s head and messed up his hair. He looked over at Alex. “How about you?” he asked. Alex was busy walking around the gym, looking at the different work out equipment. Chris frowned a little. Chris straightened back up and walks over to a set of dumbbells. “So you want to be big and strong Evan? You need to start lifting some weights.” Chris says. He takes a moment to look at his watch. He was pressed for time as it is already. Chris shrugged and let it go. The kids was more important then his training. After all, it was only Erik and Adam. Erik is a whiney little child who hasn’t done anything impressive as of late. Chris knew Adam was any good. After all, if he was then Adam wouldn’t be so jealous of Chris. Then again who could blame him? Evan followed Chris as they walked over to the dumbbells. Chris grabbed the lightest two, little 5 pound dumbbells, and handed them to Evan. Chris grabbed two of the larger weights. He sat down on the bench and cleared space for Evan to sit down. Evan does and Chris shows him how to use the weights properly. The two worked out together for the next few hour, Chris showing Evan how to do it, and little Evan trying his best. Chris just smiled as he watched his son trying to be like him. It was at that moment he knew he was going to win the Handicap match. He has to, he has to make his son proud of him.
