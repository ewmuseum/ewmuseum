Date: 3/04/02 August 1994 It was time to go back into the days of early suburbia to where Teddy and Christopher where trying out new things, those things were how to undo a girls bra-strap with one hand, not trying to make asses of themselves, and also other things that i think would not be aloud to talk about. But we fade back all those years to around August 1994, Christopher Knight stood in his room, with one of the most expensive suits he had to ever wear. He looked in the mirror admiring himself. 'WHO'S A SEXY FLY BIATCH! TONIGHT!' he screamed into, trying to demonstrate what he was. Chris put himself in order he didn't have much to say really, his brother was already out and well he was on his own ready for what he called his 'First Date', he was ready. He grabbed his walet and for some reason his skateboard and walked out the door, at fifteen years old he was ready to take on the world, and....Probably fall flat on his face. He grabbed everything and walked out the door. Suddenly the background music of Blink 182's 'First Date' hit, this may be an adventure of a life time for you all. 'In the car i just can't wait' A picture of Christopher V Knight is shown he sats in a car. With his suit on while his mom drives him to where he is supposed to be going. He hides his head under the dashboard not to be noticed, but little children laugh at him because they know what is happening. 'To pick you up on our very first date' Christopher is now seen knocking on the door of the girl he has affection for, suddenly having a frightened look on his face because of the girl's fath stand over him right at the door. 'And is it cool if i hold your hand' We now see Chris trying to hold the girls hand but failing. She looks around only for him to quickly move his hand away and for some reason to scratch his groin as a diversion. 'Is it wrong if i ask if you want to dance' There we see now Christopher getting down on the dancefloor as his date holds her head in horror and embaressment. Suddenly Chris trys to do the worm but to avail someone just kicks, and he falls flat on his face. 'Do you like my stupid hair' As the girl sits down at a table we are shown the first view of Christopher's hair. A small afro grows on his head with an afro pick stuck and twisted. 'Did you guess that i didn't know what to wear' He now wears after the little change a full black suit only under it to show a Skateboarding T-Shirt and also he wears Vans Trainers on his feet. Everyone laughs at him, even his brother Teddy who has seem to come here, on his SECOND DATE! 'I'm kinda scared of what you think' An action shot of Christopher sweating more than a dog on the summer streets. 'You make me nervous so i really can't eat!' Suddenly we see Christopher with his head down thr toilet hearing the vommiting sound, even with nothing to eat he is there for hours. 'LETS GO! DON'T WAIT! THIS NIGHT'S ALMOST OVER' Then Christopher is seen running away from his girlfriend, actually telling her to run so they don't miss a stupid skateboarding contest next door in a skate park. She just stands there tapping her toes, with her arms folded not beliving what she is seeing. And we see a clock coming up to eleven o'clock. 'HONEST! LETS MAKE! THIS NIGHT LAST FOREVER!' 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' Christopher is then seen writing 'Forver' on his arm a thousand time, also a picture inside his brain with the words 'Forever and Ever', he knows he wants something else thought. 'As you smile i melt inside' Now Christopher makes a joke that she laughs at, suddenly he gets the goffy smile that he has always been known for over his face. Suddenly something starts to melt as it's the hair jell that he used to sprise up his afro. 'I'm not worthy for a minute of your time!' A cardboard cut out of Green Day's....Bille Joe Armstrong is outside promoting their 'Dookie' album. Christopher drops to the floor with the 'We're not worthy chant'. His date taps on the sholder and he stupidly starts doing it to her to. 'I wish it was only me and you' Chris now dreams that he is on a desrted island with his date, but finds out he's in a cafe with everyone around him. 'I'm jealous of everybody in the room' All the men's prying eyes are now turning their attention to Christopher's date. Which he slides under his chair and under the table, and starts to bite his nails in nervousness. 'Please don't look at me with those eyes' We see the lovely eyes of Christopher's date that you can't get away from. 'Please don't that your capable of lies' Christopher than starts to question his date, after he catches her flirting with a few other guys. 'I dreamt the thought of our very first kiss' Use your imigination, g'd damn it. 'Charted it down, probably gonna miss' We see Christopher taking his time, suddenly lining himself up for a kiss. A fly wafts in his date's way as she moves to get away from the fly, he leans forward to much and falls flat on his face. 'LETS GO! DON'T WAIT! THIS NIGHT'S ALMOST OVER' We see the clock now striking as it seems Christopher starts to walk his date home, and he looks at her. But as she wounders what he is doing, he just looks away again. We then Christopher trying to crack a joke with his date again but she just rolls her eyes. A tear then comes down from his eye. 'HONEST! LETS MAKE! THIS NIGHT LAST FOREVER!' 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' Blink 182's Giutar now jams in on time. As the crunching chords and melodie kicks in we are shown all the scenes and more pass through the screen, we have close up's and pictures just stop in motion. Showing the good times and heartbreak Christopher has suffered. 'LETS GO! DON'T WAIT! THIS NIGHT'S ALMOST OVER' 'HONEST! LETS MAKE! THIS NIGHT LAST FOREVER!' 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' Christopher now drops his date off at time. As he walks away his date pulls him back and gives him a kiss on the lips. Without hesitation the young retarded tyke lets it flow. As now she closes the door, Christopher brother stands there folded arms on the other side of the street. Christopher looks back at the door and smiles a goofy look, before running over to his brother smiling and pleasured. 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' 'FOREVER AND EVER' 'LETS MAKE THIS LAST FOREVER' As the song comes to an end, we see Teddy and Christopher outside their home. Teddy looks at him. 'So dude what was it like, did you scored!' asks Teddy. 'You saw didn't you?' asked Chris. 'No!' Chris then starts to get all happy again. 'DUDE! IT RULED!' shouts Chris as both guys jump up in the air 'high fiving' each other. Scene Fades.
