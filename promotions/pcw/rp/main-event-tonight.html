Main Event?! TONIGHT?!

Title: Main Event?! TONIGHT?! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 28th Controversy. This is a concept The Ultimate Authority is not un-used to. The formation of the Authority was controversial. A fired Tim Walden became Personnel Director. A battered and broken Tyler Nelson regained his company. Scientologist Xavier Kannon became the Commissioner. And, up and coming tag team, Politically Incorrect, became tag champs, and heads of the division. However, a match occurs Saturday that they did not create, as such, it is non-title. But still, the opposition is dangerous. On one side, there is the Controversial Messiahs, former World Heavyweight Champions Chris Chase and Corey Williams. On the other, the Malice Crew, Duke Williams and tag competitor par excellance, Legend Inc. Talk about being between a rock and a hard place.. But, Politically Incorrect did not make their reputation on backing down. By hook, or by crook.....the politicians are ready for their first main event appearance. And they WILL not fail. ::A look of shock appears over the faces of Politically Incorrect. It is obvious that, in their busy schedule, they have forgotten about their contest tonight. Their rush was such that they could not even prepare Politically Incorrect One, and instead hopped on a *gasp* COMMERCIAL flight, first class of course. Both men are hurrying towards the arena in a black limo, the Politically Incorrect trademark.:: William S. Powell: How could we have forgotten about a match? We never forget anything! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And, we're in the main event to boot! This is very disheartening! William S. Powell: Don't say that. That's the name of a PCW tag team. Reginald V. Lovecraft: One of the few and the proud. William S. Powell: Damn right, Mr. Lovecraft. The breed is dying out again. Samburo, gone. Misplaced, gone. Golden Game, gone. Fear Factory, gone. Only three teams remain after the mass purge...The Heartbreakers, Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart, the Cowabunga Connection, and Politically Incorrect. Reginald V Lovecraft: And, everyone's finally gunning for us. To borrow from Shakespeare's Julius Caesar, we doth bestride this narrow tag team world like a colossus. The Cowabungas left us that lovely surfboard/memorandum, and the Heartbreakers are talking ill of us in their promotional speeches. Finally, the tag world revolves around us. William S. Powell: As well it should. Reginald V. Lovecraft: However, this is not enough....apparently, the actions of the Ultimate Authority have upset both the New Breed and the Malice Crew. And, in particular, one Duke Williams, who took it upon himself to call me out.. ::Lovecraft smiles. It is obvious that he relishes the opportunity to meet the only man in PCW close to his age.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: We're similar in many ways Duke. My partner's the Midwestern Republican, but I'm one from the South. We're both fortysomethings, both enjoy our smokes and alcohol, and most of all, both enjoy a fight. Under different circumstances, we could be good friends indeed....but you choose to insult me as a member of the Ultimate Authority...as protectors of Tyler Nelson, but really, what IS the Malice Crew? ::Lovecraft's mock quizzical expression changes to one of anger.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: The Malice Crew is and was nothing more than your pathetic attempt to keep power in this company! I may work with Tyler Nelson, but believe me....I am my own man. I own ten percent of Primetime Championship Wrestling, as does my constituent, Mr. Powell. Legend Inc, Jason Core, and The Rush are your lapdogs in your never ending quest to annoy us. THEY are the blind followers here, not us! William S. Powell: During your term as PCW President, merchandise sales fell! Ticket sales fell! PPV buyrates fell! Because you refused to go head to head with the whore that runs the other company. Rest assured that the Ultimate Authority has no qualms about going one on one with Jane Reagan's company and her band of personal whores, but to do that, we need absolute loyalty. Which is something insolent whelps like you are loathe to provide. Reginald V. Lovecraft: And then...Legend Inc....a former IWO Black Tag Team Champion with Mike Extreme. Inc. used to be known as 'Tom Lexian'.. William S. Powell: I take it you made liberal use of our background check capabilities as owners of PCW? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Absolutely. William S. Powell: Ever since March Madness, Legend Inc. has been quite dormant, not saying much, and seemingly content to suck on the bloated beer belly of Duke Williams as a member of the Malice Crew. Legend should be no real threat...and so, it will essentially be Duke Williams representing the Malice Crew this evening. Reginald V. Lovecraft: The Ultimate Authority demands victory on all nights, in all manners, and at all costs. Politically Incorrect will go to any lengths and means necesary to procure victory, ESPECIALLY against Duke Williams and Legend, Inc. Lexian may fancy himself a one man corporation..but when you face Politically Incorrect, you face a political machine. And when you face The Ultimate Authority.....you face a CONGLOMERATE. And you know what that means... William S. Powell: Today....is Election Day! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And, on ELECTION DAY, POLITICALLY INCORRECT ALWAYS TRIUMPHS! ::The scene spirals out to black, as a message appears on the screen.:: Paid for by The Ultimate Authority.We OWN PCW. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
