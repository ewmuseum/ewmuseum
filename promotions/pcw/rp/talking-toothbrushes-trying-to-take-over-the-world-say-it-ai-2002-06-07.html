Talking Toothbrushes Trying To Take Over The World? Say it ain't so!!!!

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th June On Tuesday Turmoil, El Diablo held his very own casting call for his first directed film 'The End' and to be brutally honest it turned out to be a failure, but that is not what El Diablo thinks, no! As everything that El Diablo does is a success is some way, or so he believes. Is that an ultimate ego or just the way a mind of an insane man works? El Diablo walks down the aisle carefully selecting what potato chips he wants and throws them into his shopping cart. 'Hmmm, but do I really want pizza flavored Doritos when I could just buy pizza?' he questions his own choice of food; 'Well, they're in the cart now anyway,' finishes El Diablo, pushing his cart further down the aisle. Our favorite Mexican is out shopping in a Walmarts nearby to where the next instalment of Friday Fusion is due to be. He is obviously trying to catch up on his shopping while he has at least some free time away from his film. The supermarket has many aisles of groceries and several extra stalls inside of it, and many many people are walking around doing their own shopping, giving El Diablo a strange look whenever they see him, and for some reason he doesn't seem to understand. Despite the fact that he is still wearing his face paint underneath his eyes and onto his cheeks. There is no doubt that El Diablo is different than he was before his rivalry with Flaymin Aymin, and perhaps that is what brought him into wanting to be a film director! His film 'The End', as he calls it, is billing it up to be a masterpiece and a blockbuster, meanwhile his 'star' PCW cruiserweight, The End is cautious about it to say the least, and still hasn't said 'yes' to the role. But that is good enough for El Diablo apparently! El Diablo pushes his shopping cart further along and reaches the toiletries section, which he studies with great care. Diablo sees a display of packaged sponges, but doesn't know what they are, so he carefully walks up to them, picks up a packet, opens it and pulls out one of the sponges. He squeezes it tight in his left hand only to see it spring back up to it's old state. 'Wow, springy!' says El Diablo loudly with a child-like smile on his face. He then rolls it up into a ball and grasps it tightly in his right hand, then releases, only to see it return to it's former state again. 'Hey, this is fun! I wonder what American game this is?' Diablo states with a big smirk on his face. At that moment a Walmart employee approaches him. He's shorter than El Diablo, and is a typical teenager with goofy glasses, terrible acne and has horrible ginger hair. 'Excuse me sir!' he snorts at Diablo, while wiping his nose with a hanky. El Diablo looks at him, and studies him a moment. 'What's this?' asks Diablo, while showing the kid the sponge he has out. The kid looks at him in shock. 'You honestly don't know?' he responds in his horrible geekish voice. Diablo stares at him in a way to show he wouldn't ask if he knew, and the kid clearly understands him. 'Well, we don't have these in Mexico!' replies Diablo. The ginger haired teenager simply shakes his head in disbelief. 'It's a sponge!' he answers, in a mocking fashion. 'Sp-unge?' questions El Diablo to a nod from the Walmart employee, 'So is sp-unge as popular as hockey here?' asks Diablo inquisitively. The employee looks up at El Diablo in some serious disbelief, 'What?' the kid asks nearly laughing with shock, 'A sponge is used to wash your body with. You put it in water and put soap on it and it cleans you,' he answers. El Diablo looks at his hand where the sponge is, and then back at the kid. 'You serious?' Diablo asks, being almost as shocked as the kid was a few moments ago. The acne filled teenager nods. El Diablo though thinks back to what the teenager said. 'Oh, I see what this is,' states Diablo loudly to the employee. 'You do?' responds the kid, obviously unaware of what El Diablo means. Diablo nods. 'You want a role on the movie I'm making,' smiles Diablo. 'I do?' asks the kid. Diablo places the sponge in the hand of the kid. 'Yes, you practically volunteered for it!' remarks Diablo, 'Now you'll just love your role,' says Diablo whilst showing his enthusiasm towards his film. 'I will?' asks the kid with an unforgettable expression of surprise on his face. El Diablo turns away to carry on with his shopping, but is interrupted by the kid tapping him on the shoulder. 'Who are you?' asks El Diablo! The kid looks cautiously from side to side. 'Errrrrm, you said I'd have a role in your film?' the kid tries to refresh El Diablo's memory. El Diablo clicks his fingers. 'Ah yes that's right,' he says as he grabs hold of the kids uniform and looks at his name tag, 'Stuart, your role is great, wait for it! Since you love sponges and gave me such a detailed review on what one was, you role is, wait for it! You'll love it, THE GUY THAT WASHES EVERYONE WHEN THEY GET SWEATY!' shouts Diablo to Stuart the Walmart employee. Stuart grimaces. 'And, wait for it! To top that I'll pay you, double what they pay you here!!!' Diablo nods his head, pleased with his negotiating skills. Dave: Wow, he's practically offering to get the kid through college! Jeff: We're not supposed to be here! Dave: Ooops! The kids grimace changes to that of a smile. 'Wow, that means you'll be paying me One Dollar fifty cents an hour,' Stuart says with glee, 'Thanks a lot sir!' With that Stuart walks away with a huge smile on his face. 'Now where was I?' El Diablo rhetorically asks himself. After seeing the sponges earlier, he sees another object that catches his eye in the toiletries section. He walks up to it and opens it up out of the packaging. 'Amazing, it's like a mini paintbrush,' Diablo says with child-like enthusiasm. He's obviously holding a toothbrush, but not any kind of toothbrush an electronic toothbrush. Somehow, you get the idea that this doesn't bode well for El Diablo. 'Hmm, I wonder what this switch does,' says El Diablo in question. He pushes it up and the toothbrush he holds roars loudly and the bristles on the toothbrush start to rattle. 'HOLY SHIT! THIS MINI PAINT BRUSH WANTS TO KILL ME!!!!!' shouts El Diablo so that the whole store can hear him. El Diablo quickly drops the toothbrush, and ignores his shopping cart as he runs away from the electronic toothbrush as fast as possible. Funny how normal and insane El Diablo can seem in the matter of minutes isn't it?
