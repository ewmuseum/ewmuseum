Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 3)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/5/02 Snake do you remember the sinking of that tanker two years ago? Not really. ...... What? A tanker sank, Snake, in the lower New York harbor. Gallons and gallons of crude oil were spilled, so the government built a massive offshore cleanup facility and......wake UP, Snake. Huh? To put a long story short, terrorists have taken over the facility. Unless we come up with 30 billion dollars in cash, they'll blow it out of the water. So screw them. Let them blow it up. Not that simple, Snake. They've taken some hostages..... What sort of hostages? BAD hostages. BAD hostages? Yes. The president of the United States. ...... ......Snake, WAKE UP! Just another five minutes, mommy. Ugh. A lone soldier finds himself propelled throughlukewarm New York waters......bubbles gurgle from his mouth as he yellsand attempts to guide the torpedo he was launched from. Finally he crashesscreaming into the wall of the structure, his dud missile shattering intoa million pieces. Soldier: Argh! In severe pain, the lone intruder crawls out of the water. Shaking off the dampness, he removes his flippers and touches his ear. Soldier: I'm inside the facility. Colonel: Good job. Soldier: Did you know there was no steering column on the torpedo? I kinda sorta got lucky that I wasn't killed back there. Colonel: No time to think about that now, Snake. You've got to get into the Big Shell, rescue the President and neutralize the terrorist threat. Soldier: ....... Colonel: WAKE UP!!!!! Solider: Huh? Blinking the sleep from his eyes, the man stands from his resting spot on the wall. To an elevator he jogs, heading up....up, into the daylight. Colonel: The terrorist's call themselves the Sons of Bitches. Their leader, is Solid Snake. Soldier: But Solid Snake died..... Colonel: Yes, but he's here too. Soldier: And wait a second, I'M Snake. Colonel: Oh yes, I forgot to tell you. We just started calling you that a while ago so you wouldn't feel quite so pathetic about being a skinny little white haired nambla poo. Your real name is Raiden. Raiden: ......I'm not a nambly poo. Colonel: So nambla poo, first head downstairs. There's a backup team of SEALS waiting for you, meet up with them. Raiden: Do they have walrus's too? Colonel: ...... Disconnecting the call, Raiden waits for the elevator to surface then heads down a nearby staircase. There a lone guard patrols, walking back and forth between office cubicles. Raiden: Hmm. Steatlhily, Raiden sneaks up behind the guard....prepares to put him in a chokehold. Just before he can make contact though his ear suddenly rings. Raiden: (Jumping) Ah! Guard: Huh? The guard turns around, Raiden moves quickly behind him and hits his ear. Raiden: What? What is it? Leah: Jack, it's me. Raiden: Leah? Ah! Again the guard shifts, Raiden slips behind him. Raiden: Kind of a bad time. Leah: But like, I missed you and stuff! Raiden: Still....dammit! The guard turns his head to the left, Raiden goes right. Then to the right, Raiden goes left. Leah: Do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: Not now! Raiden yelps as his foe turns, looking him right in the eye. Guard: What the..... A chop to the head puts the fellow down.....he tries to crawl away, but Raiden gets on top of him and hits him frantically over and over. Leah: So yeah, tomorrow's a really special day. Raiden: Ow my eye! Jack is pushed over by the terrorist as he digs his fingers into his eye socket. With all of his strength though the infiltrator flips him over again, choking him with both hands until at last the man lies still. Leah: Talk to you soon Jack! Raiden: Ugh! Limping away from the scene of his crime, Raiden presses on....until another ring enters his skull. Raiden: What!? Colonel: You didn't dispose of the body. Raiden: So what? Colonel: So, it's protocol. Raiden: Protocol? Colonel: Yes. We can't have you attracting flies and guards and such with a corpse. Get rid of the body! Raiden: Where? Colonel: I don't know, find something! The call ends, leaving Raiden to sigh deeply. Back to the dead guard he goes, picking him up underneath the armpits. Raiden: (Grunting) God, what have you been eating? Looking all about, Jack's eyes fall on a set of lockers. He drags his victim over to them....opens, then shrieks as another dead body falls out of it. Raiden: Ah! What the hell? Shaking his head, he moves a bit and opens the second.....another corpse tumbles free, landing next to the first. Raiden: Dammit! Angrily Jack then whips the third open, and surely enough another body falls out to the floor. Raiden: (Holding his ear) Colonel, I'm trying to stuff the bodies into the lockers here, but they're ALREADY stuffed with bodies! Colonel: Your point being? Raiden: Someone's already been here killing guards! I have nowhere to put this guy I knocked off. Colonel: Tough toodles. Find a way. Raiden: You're no hel..... Abruptly the transmission ceases. Jack kneels beside the original corpse, lifting his arm and letting it drop. Then he does the same to the second. Then the first. Raiden: .......what do I doooooooo...... A bit later a guard marches down the stairs, stopping when he sees a rather strange sight. Three guards stand by a table, with party hats on their heads. They all lean against each other somewhat drunkenly. Guard: Wow, party hearty. Heh heh. The soldier walks to the group, slapping them on the shoulders. Guard: Share some of that vodka with a comrade, eh? Getting no answer, the man raises an eyebrow. Guard: Comrade, what is wrongAH!!!! As he tries to shake his friend, the closest body topples on to him. This is followed by the second, then the third, falling on him and pinning him to the floor. Guard: (Trapped) Why me. Nearby, Raiden snickers and makes his way down a hallway. He doesn't get far though before a pain filled scream echoes in the chamber. Raiden: What the..... Taking a hiding position, the young man peers into a nearby room. Seal A: What the hell..... Seal B: What is it? The Seals walk carefully amongst the bodies of their partners, who lay scattered and broken on the floor. Seal A: I don't.....ah my eyes!!!!! The fellow clutches his face, screaming like a banshee. Falling to the floor, he rolls about in agony as his ally looks on. Seal B: I don't.....ah no, no! Not you! Not you!!!!! Firing his gun madly, the Seal tries to take down his unknown assailent but it is no use. Above him a blur of motion whips past....then it lands behind him. Seal B: (Turning) Ah god no! No!!!! A figure stands before him out of his most bizzare nightmares. A very androgynous vampire, wearing a low cut blouse and genie pants hisses....it then grabs him by the hair. Seal B: NO!!!! Slowly the vampire begins to dance. Touching himself in a very inapproriate manner, he gyrates slowly....runs his hand down the front of his shirt, touching his chest. Seal B: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! The second Seal grabs his own face, and in one motion pulls his eyes out of his very head. Over him, the creature stands and smiles. Raiden: (Pulling out his gun) Freeze! Raiden shoots as fast as he can, but the vampire simply dances out of the way of each shot. At the end of his movement he grabs his crotch and thrusts it quickly at him before smiling. Argy: Five today.....or rather six? The bizzare one looks Jack over from head to toe. Argy: Oooh.....definetely six. With a shockingly fast movement, the odd vamp begins to move towards the hapless boy. In a blur the vamp ends up behind Raiden, smiling as the child continues to point his gun haplessly at empty air. Argy: Ah.... At the very last second before horrible things can occur, a voice yells out. Voice: Get down! Suddenly the lights fade in the room. A faint flickering starts up, strobing in time with music that filters out of nowhere.....Argy begins to sing. Argy: Humidity's rising The corpse of a Navy captain rises up off the ground, singing as well. Captain: Barometer's getting low. Raiden: According to all sources, the street's the place to go. The bodies of the deceased navy Seals stand at once and begin dancing slowly. On the far side of the room the stranger grunts and begins shooting at the zombies, to no avail. Raiden: Cos tonight for the first time..... Argy: At just about half past ten.... Raiden: For the first time in history.... Argy: It's gonna start raining men.... The Navy zombies line up next to the gunman, who looks around in confusion. Argy and Raiden break into a dance number, bumping and grinding along. Zombie Singers: It's raining men....hallelujah, it's raining men, amen....it's raining menHallelujah, it's raining men, amen....it's raining menHallelujah, it's raining men, amen....it's raining menHallelujah, it's raining men, amen It's raining menIt's raining menIt's raining menIt's raining men Suddenly catching himself, the man grabs his gun and begins ripping the zombies apart with automatic gun fire. One by one they fall, the room becoming decorated with zombie guts.....when all are down, he turns to Argy. Argy: Oh poo. The vampire's ear suddenly buzzes, he picks it up. Argy: Yeah queen? Queen: Don't call me queen crazy white boy! I gank your ass! Argy: That a promise? Queen: Ack! Get your whiteboy punkass over here now! Argy: Be right there. With that the oddball runs, pushing over the man with the gun. Nearby, Raiden continues to hum to himself in a trance. Raiden: It's raining men.....lalala..... Groaning in pain, the gunman stumbles over....sits next to the far wall and slumps, eying the blonde haired pretty boy warily..... To Be Continued
