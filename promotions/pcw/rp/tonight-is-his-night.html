Tonight is his night...

undated

Date: Meh Fizz flying everywhere, champagne bottles popped open. Glasses clinking together as they are filled. People cheering and congratulating... Streamers are hung from the ceiling, confetti thrown all over the place. Decorations galore, not that the person that they are for can see them to appreciate them. But the thought is there. It's a great time, a great moment. There was a release of tension when the 3-count was called. The whole month leading up to this has been one of anxiety. Rush Mason had tried to stop him, put him on a continuous losing streak. Sometimes he succeeded in costing the match, sometimes he didn't. But Sunday... Shanghaied... Everything came to a head. The match was a battle. And in the End, he indeed came out on top. An Inverted Mexican Stretch Buster; it was all over. And so, the celebration. All for a job well done. It was nearly a month ago that The End announced his retirement from PCW, winning the #1 Contendership to PCW in what was to be his last match. Everyone speculated on whom he would choose to replace him once he retired. And he declared that the following Friday, he would make his choice. And that Friday, he returned to declare himself as the challenger, albeit dropping the moniker 'The End.' The return was at Rush Mason's expense, yes, but that made it all the more sweeter. The first swerve of PCW's New Year and it being built on the back of the former Continental Champion as recently as that Decimation... and the fact that it was not planned months in advance like Mexicano Moe Momar aka Mexi the Great White Mexican Dragon with 100 different name changes to warrant different posers Sandy Wong:'Could we keep the narration from going OOC?' Handler:'Sorry, but the observation is made quite easily. And I'm not even the first person to say it.' Sandy:'Just don't take away from Skylar's day.' Handler:'Very well.' And the fact that it was not planned months in advance like The Dragon's made it the more impressive. And it was a full month of Rush Mason attempting to get back at Skylar, convincing Xavier Kannon to book him in matches that Mason could interfere in, attacking him backstage. Rush Mason not only got the pay-per-view match booked to be for the #1 Contender's spot; he also had the match made a 'Loser Leaves PCW' match. Everyone was unsure if this was a good idea. The last 'Loser Leaves PCW' match in the federation's history was 'Controversial' Corey Williams versus Jon Kano. That was 5 minutes of craptacular action before CCW was sent packing and the crowd cheered louder then that it did when BAS won the World Title. With any luck, Rush Mason and CCW won't get together and challenge Kano and Skylar to a tag match for their PCW spots. But now was the time for celebration... Skylar Green had defended his rank, his title shot, his career. Rush Mason put everything he didn't have on the line, he put his 'life' on the line. And lost. Rush Mason was fired and now what could he do but retire? PCW is considered by both men to be the top federation in the industry. Working anywhere else would almost be an insult to them. Rush Mason would have to retire... Someday, Skylar would have to retire, too... But not tonight. Not with his friends around him. Not with the weight of the world taken off his shoulders, if but for one night. Not with Earth, Wind, and Fire's 'Mighty Mighty' playing in the background. Not with Sandy in his arms and his contract match for the Continental Title coming up this Friday (even if he has to go through Darkstar on the Turmoil Main Event first). Tonight is his night. And he has truly earned it. See you later, Blind One... *End*
