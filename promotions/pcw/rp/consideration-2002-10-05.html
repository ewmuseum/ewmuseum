Date: 05/10/02 “Day by day I get the impression that in living I aggravate his very existence. And if I’m honest, it fills me with such bliss that I feel as though I could become extremely arrogant.” – Titu Xansiv (05/10/02) -==-***-==- The two days of rest I endured at the James H. Hilton Coliseum have strengthened me considerably. I know feel that I am able to compete without trouble against Akira Akiyama, and though my wounds from the unfortunate accident with the lighting-rig have yet to fully heal, I know now that my destiny is to defeat the beast at Red October unheeded Even if I do not manage to take the PCW World Heavyweight Title on that night, I will always know that I was the one who banished the nVg from competing in the main event and therefore destroyed his dream of holding the title after the 6th of October 2002. Everything is almost set now. The competitors are ready and awaiting their opponents and final preparations are being made in The Tokyo Dome for everything to go ahead as planned by PCW staff. All I have to do before the PPV is go and visit Isiy at the Ames State Hospital and fly to the great country where I have my chance to beat Akiyama again. -==-***-==- The rustle of leaves upon the pebble stone drive brushed about my feet as I stood looking over the sanctuary of healing. The Ames State Hospital was large and ominous and I felt small and needlessly helpless outside its colossal structure. It reminded me somewhat of my situation with the God, Akiyama. In comparison to it I was small and weak, Akiyama too was much bigger than me; yet I knew my heart was not cold and serpent-like as its was. Taking in a deep gasp of air, I ventured forth up the driveway and eventually came to the doors of the place, tall and almost as threatening as the building itself they were but I shook off any cautiousness I felt and opened the door…it creaked. Looking around, I found myself in an office of sorts. The main reception, guarded well by a huge pane of thick plastic took my eye, and as I edged closer to it I found that I was under close, methodical watch. “Hello,” I began, “I’m here to visit a patient.” The receptionist, a thin and vaguely beautiful woman looked over my dark-skinned face and sighed. “And who might you be wishing to see, sir?” her voice was almost as cold as Cyclonia’s, but the depth of mind behind these words was nothing compared to that of the Divine Prophetess. “The name is Isiy Xansiv,” I looked around, cautiously. Men and women, sitting upon the steel chairs tightened to the whitewashed walls, glared at me accusingly. I was startled by their threatening looks, but soon overcame the feeling as the woman concealed behind the wall of plastic coughed politely to catch my attention. I turned. “Ward three, isle one, room 4A, sir…” she said, studying my face. “By the way…aren’t you that wrestler…The King of Egypt, or something?” I chuckled, “Actually, I am…but they call me The Pharaoh. Isiy is my wife.” The woman looked down at the files scattered about her desk and absent-mindedly continued to speak with me. “From the looks of her it appears to be a dangerous occupation you’ve taken up, Mr. Xansiv.” Her words sent a shiver up my spine; spite was concealed cleverly within them. I sighed and thought of the moment when Cyclonia beat Isiy down to the mat, a blubbering mess of blood and anguish. “Indeed, it is…” I murmured, a little disheartened. “Indeed it is…” -==-***-==- Room 4A was small and humble. A plant or two had been strewn here and there to lighten the mood of the place and as I opened the mahogany-washed door I saw Isiy sleeping helplessly under the watchful gaze of a few birds from outside the window. I edged further inward and abruptly noticed something upon the table in my wife’s room. A small stone, smooth and pebble-like, sat motionlessly upon the smooth surface. So odd was the sight that I tilted my head in contemplation and wondered what it possible could be. However, it didn’t take me long to forget about it as Isiy rolled over from the right-hand side of her bed to the left. Immediately it became obvious that she wasn’t sleeping. “Titu?!” she bellowed, joyfully. She leapt forth from her bed, not fully clothed, but not fully nude, and hugged me. Her expression of happiness soon changed as she felt the burns and scratches upon my back and front. “What happened to you? …Where have you been?” She asked, her brow furrowing. I sighed, lowering my head as I thought back to the moment when Akiyama turned on his void armour… -==-***-==- …I could see the silver even under the never-ending darkness, and the sound of the crowd was almost deafening. But even over the drowning hum of the people in attendance shouting in disapproval of what just happened, I could clearly make out the huffs of my enemy as he attempted to save us both…or save just him. Moments ago a large piece of metal had fallen upon my skull, and Godsdamn did it hurt… “Isiy…?” I said, dazed and clearly confused. Akiyama grunted angrily. “Oh, it’s you.” I said, trying to move. “What the Duat are you doing here?!” Once again the Neo Void God grunted, this time more wearily than angry, as the weight of the lighting-rig grew on its massive arms. “Fuckin’ Clyde!” I murmured as the God tried with all its might to keep the rig at a good height above us. The thing was huge - in fact it was more than huge, it was colossal. Yet I watched with amazement and, to my own astonishment, admiration as the nVg supported the thing but a few feet over its own frame. For a moment I was sure it said something, but it could have been my imagination, for you see at that exact moment something else hit me across the head, and then all was dark… -==-***-==- “And then,” I continued, “I found myself in the medical-centre of the arena.” Isiy looked into my dark eyes and shook her head to and fro. “Bloody Duat, Titu…” I nodded. “I know…” Getting to my feet, I rubbed my eyes, wearily and turned to her sorrowful face. “I have to go to Japan, Isiy.” I said at length. She nodded, knowingly. “I realize that. But—” She hesitated. “But what?” I queried. “But, I-I…I don’t know if I want to go…Cyclonia’s going—” “Isiy, my love, do not worry of that…creature! I am not forcing you to join me, but I must go. I needed to see you again before I left America, that’s all. I’m not forcing you to come.” “I know you’re not. I just don’t think I’m up to it.” She confessed. “Then that’s Ok. As I’ve said: You don’t have to come with me to Tokyo.” I verified. She rose from the bed and walked towards the window, looking out over the greying sky and the browning trees. The weather was changing again…winter was nearing. “I don’t like leaving you to go by yourself, though.” I sighed, but not mockingly. “Isiy, honestly; you don’t have to come! Look, I tell you what, if you want to then here’s your ticket.” I’d bought her it in case she did wish to journey with me over to Japan. “If you want to come, then meet me in the airport in a few hours, Ok?” “Ok.” She nodded. “I’ve gotta get now. I love you…” Embracing her, I kissed the lips of the most beautiful woman on the earth and smiled. “See you when I get back.” And with that I left the Ames State Hospital, thoughts of Akiyama and his evil ways praying on my mind. I was going to Japan to win…not to lose… -==-***-==- It’s not about the belt anymore, I thought as I walked down the street, It’s about my dignity and standing up to my fears. I needed to beat Akiyama at Red October, and as far as I was concerned, I would.
