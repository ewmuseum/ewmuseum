Date: nVg Multi Character plot Today’s the day… The day of reckoning… We’ve traveled across the ocean all the way to our home country…the home that abandoned us…the country we are enemies of. Lot of effort to come to a country full of enemies…we’re even importing some of our own enemies from overseas so we can dismantle their mortal frames on live television. But before we start with the gaijins…we’re going to put a spot of training in on the Nihonjin. The outside of the Yamoto business center was calm…peaceful. But through it’s soundproof walls the sounds of death were resonating like echoes in the distance. Innocent people were slaughtered…injured…in pain…all caused by two people. Please…we had lost the right to be called people long ago… What we are…is monsters… But we’re Celestial monsters…Celestial demons… Celestial deities… Pharaoh…all he had was a lot of hope and determination… plenty of wishes and dreams of survival and victory that couldn’t fill the IV bag he’ll need after he gets the Celestial crushing he deserves. I saw those same hopes and wishes of survival in the people as they fled from us… I didn’t see much in them after that…except death. The top three floors were already empty…the people escaped or were dead…one way or another. Floor by floor…the building was brought to ruin from the inside…by a plague that Japan thought they could banish from their borders. The people entering the lobby looked up, wondering what was going on above them. Voices massed in pain…screams…cries…wails…sounds that would bring the heart to stop, so that each bit of it could be heard clearly…without interruption. Then another sound interrupted the one scarring their ears as well as their minds… The elevator had arrived… And from it emptied a sphere of fire, like a roaring vomit of flame out the mouth of Satan himself. Those who were still alive after that looked on in terror as grinding footsteps came out of the smoke…footsteps grinding the bones of their fellow man into dust. Heavy…metallic…footsteps… They saw the god in his rampage…in his armor for war… Then they saw the sharp silver stiletto of the Divine Prophetess’s boot pierce their eye… They were not worthy of looking upon him…they were swine… Let us hear your squeals…pigs… “Okay, one more time! What were you doing with Akiyama on that plane?!” In a dark room lighted by one light overhead, Matrix and Sonata sat together on one side of the table. Across from them, two disgruntled officers sat with their arms folded across their chests, questioning them as much as they could Matrix wasn’t the most cooperative, and Sonata was…Sonata. “Who’s Akiyama?” Matrix replied, feigning confusion. “All I know is some terrorist pointed a gun at my dad and sister, and then he pushed them out of the plane!” Sonata looked at Matrix, a bit baffled as well. “It looked like they jumped out of the plane to me.” Matrix glared at her. “Shut up MOM!” “You don’t treat her like she’s your mother,” the black mustached cop raised an eyebrow at him. “What’s up with that?” Matrix adjusted his sunglasses and sighed. “It’s nothing…that’s how our family is!” “He doesn’t love me anymore!” Sonata said, breaking out into a huge sob, her hands going to her face. “You give them life and love, and they think they can treat you like scum!” “Aw geez…” Matrix shook his head, looking down as the cops leaned over, trying to comfort her. One of them handed her a tissue, and she used it to wipe the tears from her eyes before pulling her glasses out from her pocket. Matrix eyed her for a minute…since when did Sonata wear glasses? “Matrix…son?” she said, turning towards him with wide sparkling eyes. “Can you look me straight in my eyes and tell me you love your mother sincerely?” He looked at her, and saw something strange. But he got it… “Mother…I love you.” And Sonata looked back to the two officers…and she ran her finger along the side of her glasses. Just then…a flash emptied into the room, followed by groans of pain. Matrix looked back up to see Sonata standing over the two cops, with their guns in hand!! “You gonna sit there all day and gawk, kid?” she said, tossing a gun in his direction. He grabbed it before looking back up as her surprised. “W…w…what??” She stopped before she opened the door, leading into the hallway. Then she turned back with a smile. “What…surprised? Thought I was just a ditz huh? You’ve got a lot to learn, kid…but let’s save it for later!” Sirens blared as police made their way to the scene. Three cars, screeching around the corner only to be halted by the pile of ruined autos in the center of the street. They sat there, looking dumbfounded as to what could have done so. But then they found their answer…and their end…Akiyama pounced down from above onto the hood of their car, and a single straight arm Hand of Fate put his skull into pieces, along with the windshield. The officer in the passenger’s seat tried to aim his gun at the god…to watch the bullets effortlessly bounce off his frame like the attacks of his opponents come time in battle… Something prevented him from aiming the gun at the god…namely….moi. He pointed the gun into his mouth…and squeezed off, as if he intended to the entire time. The weakness of the mortal mind… Pharaoh was perfect example of it. His wife…that was his last precious treasure in the world. He was nothing else besides her…just a man…just a worthless heap of trash. As much as he wanted to protect her…he could never do so. We always get our hands on her…we always spill her blood…we always drive him into the depths of despair because he is powerless to do anything against it. And Pharaoh…we always stand above you…no matter who your friends are…no matter who you love…no matter what you think you’re capable of…no matter what you’re determined to do. Your attacks will bounce off the nVg like these pathetic peons’ bullets. Speaking of which…three of the shooters were crushed under their own car…and the other three…they dropped their guns and stood still like nice little sacrifices as my pinky opened their throats and sprayed their fluids onto the ground. Their blood…crimson…impure…fresh from their diseased bodies… It was the warm welcome that I had looked forward to ever since I left… The office of purity…the office of sterilization…the snow-white perfect office… Angelica sat there, flipping through the documents of a manila folder, her shapely legs crossed under the tight long white silk skirt she wore. She fanned herself with the collar of the white silk blouse she wore unbuttoned three buttons at the top, and two at the bottom and sighed… It was boring work sometimes…but she didn’t join the Executives for the fame. Sometimes there was action…big drawn out battles escaping barely with her life intact…but the other days…it was nothing but paperwork. Reports, bios, research…just like any other office. Just then…an alarm went off through the building. She didn’t look up…but merely pressed a button on her computer and went back to reading her file, shutting the alarm off as quickly as it came on. Then she picked up a white cell phone on her glass desk and pressed a key. “Illusion,” she said drolly. “Leave the alarm alone.” “Sorry, ma’am,” an apologetic, yet snickering voice said. With that said, she put the phone down and went back to work. But yet again…the alarm goes off. Angelica sighed as she shut it off again and picked up the phone. “Illusion…don’t make me report you.” “It wasn’t me this time!” Just then, the black suited red haired Reality stormed into the room with a look of urgency on his face. Angelica stood up from her desk and stared at him, the cell phone still at her ear. Reality gave her the “important” look. “Akiyama’s is loose…in Tokyo…with the Divine Prophetess.” “Hello??” Illusion yelled through the phone. Angelica stepped from behind her desk and followed Reality towards the elevator. “Shut up,” she ordered curtly. “Get Knight and Spice and meet us in the elevator. Subject number 999 is in Tokyo with the Divine Prophetess.” She hung up the phone and gave Reality a knowing look. “Make sure Shift does his job…” Helicopters pan the perimeter where the our rampage trails…we’re not just on a random path of destruction here. We have a purpose… We have a destination… These men…these men people see fit to be called the world champion…they have a destination set for themselves as well. They all want to go STRAIGHT TO THE TOP! They want to go all the way and climb the Mt. Fuji and roar out their victory for all the country to hear. Post a banner while they’re up there… “Chappy K Jerkoff”…PCW WORLD CHAMPION…BEST OF THE BEST You’re at the top of the world…congrats… But where are we? We’re the ones that helped you get to the top…we’re the ones that made you as big as you are. We’re the ones that tossed you into the volcano while you were yelling like an idiot. Heh…Akiyama put Pharaoh on the map…Akiyama was the one that pushed him into being Tag Team champion…Akiyama was the one that allowed him to obtain the TV title while he took away his godhood…It was Pharaoh’s denied GOD that did all that for him…and his repayment is sacrificing himself so that the Neo Void God may be properly warmed up before taking on three other men in combat at the end of the night. What…a…saint… The god made you Pharaoh…and now it’s time he broke you like the toy you have been all this time…just like he broke El Diablo… And once the final four men are at the top of the mountain…once we’ve assembled to do battle…Akiyama is going to toss them off…one after the other… Every man that thought they were better than the god has one destination that they’re heading for…despite their efforts to choose their own course… They’re all going…to die… “THIS IS THE TOKYO PD!! CEASE YOUR DESTRUCTION OF PUBLIC PROPERTY PLEASE AND GIVE YOURSELF UP!” I looked up as the god continued to walk forward, his armored hands ripping the concrete flesh from the bodies of buildings. Why do they always ask us to stop…they know that their words are useless… But if they want to talk so much… “AKIYAMA!! IF YOU PERSIST, WE WILL BE FORCED TO OPEN FIRE UPON YO---!!!!” Voices choked…their own useless words being the vice on their throats from the inside. I smiled…knowing that there was one less human breathing…one more person silenced before the mass silencing at RO. Anarchy…she was getting silenced in a different…but more fun way… Bondage was fun…it gave a sense of empowerment. It enforced the power normally when you knew you were better than the person being contained. In a way…the god wields bondage over his opponents… they are bound against doing anything against his might…and gagged, speechless in saying anything that can prevent their downfall. That’s how natural rulers go…above the commoners…better than the peasants…master of the slaves… The relationship between Anarchy and I was different. We are equal in thirst for pain…giving it…forcing it on others…a rape of sorts against those who defy us. We force pain into their bodies…and we make sure that the entire process is one slow…excruciating…long… F * C K of suffering… And we’re pros at it… Two pros are going on a date tonight…and once it’s over…one of us will be giving the other the goodnight kiss…what you boys will be wondering…is where will the lips be pressed? A hydrant rocketed into the sky…making contact with the chopper. Fireworks…followed by a rain of fire before the burning mass crashed into the side of a building. Ahead, the god stormed off around the corner, leaving a rising column of water shooting from the ground. I followed, but around that corner laid the end of the road… “Here’s more information from the huge development going on in downtown Tokyo!” An image of the nVg in Void Armor throwing a motorcycle into a store window was shown on the television. His roar is muffled as the anchorwoman spoke. “It’s been said that the man known as Akiyama has been rampaging in Tokyo for about three hours straight, with no end in sight! Police has been helpless in stopping the man they refer to as the NEO VOID GOD! Akira Akiyama, a wrestler with Primetime Championship Wrestling, is said to be an actual deity bent on complete destruction. That woman by his side is called Cyclonica, as our sources say, and she is his ambassador to the human race!” The camera shows the blue and black haired Divine Prophetess glaring off into the camera. Her eyes seem to bore holes through the lens of the camera before everything goes fuzzy. Across from the wide screen television…Inphino Blitz sat on a sofa, dressed in his usual black attire. His eyes stared blankly into the television, as nothing but snow played on it. Snow…how appropriate a name for distortion. “Mina…” he said solemnly, calling for his manager. Mina de Margo poked her black and red streaked head out from the kitchen behind him. “Hmmm?” Inphino stood up, straitening out the lapels of his jacket. “Get your jacket.” The god stood before the Tokyo Dome…the crowded hall of fighting champions swelled with mortals for Red October. He glared silently through his armor mask, peering seamlessly into the structure as he could scan it for his victims tonight. I stood by and watched as he crossed the street and disappeared into the building. I shrugged…no use putting off an ass beating tomorrow when we can give it right the f*ck now… I followed… I was ready to face dear sweet Anarchy… And the god… He was ready for his HOMECOMING… So says the Divine Prophetes… Sonata and Matrix dashed through the city streets, amidst the wreckage, the fires, and the crumpled up pieces of newspaper blowing back and forth. Matrix stopped as he looked around the surroundings. “Aki-man’s been here,” he muttered. Sonata looked back at him with an anxious look on her face. “Come on! We can still catch up with them if we hurry!” Matrix looked up and froze at what he saw… Dead on Sonata’s forehead…was a laser period. Reality stood with his arms folded on the roof of a building. He looked away with a cigarette in his mouth, peering down at something. “Do you have the shot?” he muttered through clenched teeth. A black haired man in a black trench coat nodded, his hands busy with the sniper rifle in hand. “She’s in my sight, sir.” Reality blew out a puff of smoke lazily. “Take the shot. Kill her dead.” TO BE CONTINUED…
