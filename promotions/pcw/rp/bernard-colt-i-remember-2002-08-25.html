Bernard Colt: I Remember

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 I remember the first day that I heard his name… Bernard Colt. A new signee. Ready to take PCW's world by storm. The news reports were all the same. He was a great talent. Perfect for PCW. He was primetime material, or so they said. He would fit right in. I was hesitant to believe such praise… as I always am. After all, why should this newcomer get thrown into the spotlight while I still dwindled in the shadows? I didn't know much about him, nor do I to this day. In my mind, he is an enemy, as all unknowns are. It's better to be too fearful than too unafraid. I fear almost nobody… but I am still fearful. Bernard Colt… As time went on, I saw more of this rookie. I saw as he released promos and wrestled in matches. It was soon clear what kind of person he was. A psychopath. I have no problem with those whom are insane, but those whom believe they are insane for the sole purpose of terrorizing others is an entirely different story. I could tell right away that Bernard Colt was insecure. My excitement was barely confinable. A warrior thrives on the insecurity of his opponents. His foes. His enemies. Yes, although his name was much clearer in my mind, Bernard Colt has remained, and always will remain, an enemy. Has he shown me to believe otherwise? No. The sick, demented mind that lives within Bernard Colt is a twisted one at that. A living hell for those close to him. A repellent for those unbeknownst to him. A future victim for me. I remember the first day that I saw his face… I remember a few weeks ago, taking a look at the latest card for Primetime Championship Wrestling. There his picture was, staring back at me. I am not an arrogant, cocky fool, but do you know what ran through my head when I first got a glimpse of the psychopath known to the wrestling world as Bernard Colt? 'I can destroy him.' It's a wonderful notion in ones mind, to have the assurance that you can defeat another. I imagined his frail body being crushed by the mighty blow of the Infinite Warrior… knocking the insanity right out of him. Damaging his pride as his soul already is. I could imagine his wrestling style. A foolish one, yet one that was effective on unbelievers in the way of the warrior. He had no real talent. But the psychotic frame of mind allowed him to go crazy in the ring, with cheap tactics such as biting and clawing. Bernard Colt feels that he is a traditional wrestler. An All-American. Nationality has nothing to do with one's pride. I remember the first day that I smelled his fear… I am an American, am I not? I take pride in where I live and the freedoms that I enjoy… But it is not all that makes up my character. Does that make me more dimensional than Bernard Colt? Yes. Is he a warrior, or will he ever be one? No. Never. I can smell his fear even as I say this now. A thousand miles away he could be… Millions… Light-years… Yet still I would feel the same as I do now. I have seen his face. I have looked into the eyes of the psychopath. I know what he feels… I know what he fears. Has he looked into my eyes? Has he seen the coldness staring back at him? I am a warrior. He is afraid. He should be. I am a warrior. I will defeat him. I remember the first day that I tasted victory… It is too bad that I have not yet had a chance to make Bernard Colt feel the pain on the outside that he surely feels on the inside. I have not yet had the golden glory of kneeing him in the stomach and bringing him down into the Battle Cry. I have not yet had the opportunity of the referee raising my arms in glory, pronouncing the winner of the match loud and clear, for all the world to hear… Trigger. But the time will come… oh yes. The time will come when I get the 1… 2… 3… Or maybe a knockout. Or maybe the insane Bernard Colt will tap greedily on the mat as I lock on an excruciating submission maneuver. Fate takes many outcomes… but the result will remain the same. I remember the first day that I touched defeat… As previously stated, I am not one to brag. I have long considered the possibility of a loss thanks to Colt… Unreasonable in my opinion, but still possible. How would it be that one who claims himself mentally ill could mentally defeat a warrior? The Infinite Warrior none less. Nevertheless, I must be prepared for a loss. Of course if I do lose to Bernard Colt, I will be the one that has gone insane. I remember the first day that his spirit died… Friday, August 30th, 2002… Bernard Colt's spirit will die. He is very unfortunate, having to step into the ring with me… standing between me and ultimate glory. If I defeat him on Fusion, I will have a shot at the Hardcore Title, a prize I have long sought. The Hardcore title would complete me if I owned it's rights… I will take apart anyone who stands in my way. That anyone right now is Bernard Colt. Bernard Colt must be destroyed… …At the hands of a warrior. I remember you, Bernard Colt, and I'll never forget.
