Mistaken Identity.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, standing in a remote section of grass plains. A slight breeze stirs the long grasses, the low rustle a soothing sound on this lovely afternoon. A few trees lie off in the distance to quell the suspicion that one may have wandered onto the Elysian Fields of lore, reminding one that this is, indeed, one of the few places on Earth untouched by man. Steele, however, is crouched, sweat trickling from his matted hair down his face. A determined look is etched into his features, and his chest heaves and rises from great gasps of breath. The eyes of our hero scan the skies back and forth, vigilantly monitoring as many directions of clear blue sky as he can… KA-BOOOOOM!!! Chunks of sod and rock explode behind Big Al, just as he dives out of the way! Steele rolls and comes up in a crouch, a thick forearm raised over his forehead to protect his eyes from the shower of dirt as it rains from the sky. A huge smoking crater lies just where he had once stood. SKREEEEEEE!!! Steele’s head snaps left milliseconds before he again leaps aside… KERPLOW!!! Breathing heavily, Big Al slides baseball-style and brings his momentum to a single knee, again his face the definition of perfect concentration, seemingly unaware of the shower of earth around him. Another streak of smoke in the sky forces Steele to flip backwards, another explosion rocking the area he once stood, the concussive forces propelling him further back. Steele manages to twist himself into a forward dive, tuck and roll from the extra propulsion, as he comes to stand in the prairie. Licking his fingers calmly, he reaches down and extinguishes a small flame on his toga with a sizzle. There is a crackle on a walkie talkie hidden within the many folds of Steele’s custom designed warrior’s toga, and our hero whips it out… the walkie talkie, that is. Talkie: (crackle!) “End of phase one… commencing phase two… over!” Big Al’s muscles tense upon hearing the news, as he tucks away the communicator. A few silent moments of respite follow, before the rumbling of a cargo plane overhead. Within seconds the air is filled with white silk parachutes, men covered head to toe in black gear aiming large, shoulder mounted weaponry at his person. Steele grimaces and gets into a battle ready position… Contrails fill the sky, all the lines streaking towards our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele. Bellowing out a manly warcry, he sprints forward and leaps through a great heave of earth from a missile touching down right before his feet. The force pushes him up and horizontal, making him somersault forward to maintain his momentum and continue moving… … for stopping would surely mean death. Landing hard, his sandals dig into the grass, his hand touching down for balance. Standing up to run, he comes face to face with a man in black launcher pointed directly into Steele chest from about 30 feet away. A small standoff before the man slightly tenses his arm… Big Al throws himself backwards, bending at the waist as the rocket rushes through the air, the smell of burning fuel and exhaust enveloping Steele as he narrowly averts a deadly situation. Big Al continues his motion and rounds off his body. He charges for the man ahead, arm held back to strike, when another missile strikes just to the right of him, blowing him awkwardly in a sideways flip. Big Al reaches out an arm and manages to control his fall a bit, tucking in his shoulder and continuing to roll across the prairie as another rocket explodes next to him. The rocks shower him as he looks up, anger evident in his eyes. Grabbing one of the larger stones, he leaps up and whirls around, whipping the rock across the plains, catching a man full in the chest, knocking the wind out of him. Big Al rushes over and takes the offensive, running full tilt and exploding a vicious uppercut into the black helmet of the man. The helmet splits into three portions as the man is lifted off the ground, flopping hard to the earth, his eyes rolled into the back of his head. Big Al reaches down and grabs the missile launcher and runs full tilt to the right, a group of the men chasing him and trying to circle him in. They begin to spread out and out flank him to contain Steele. Big Al grunts and stops, planting a foot and springing back into the group! Caught by surprise, most dive out of the way, but one is granted a taste of Steele’s brand of justice, flipped around from a wicked clothesline that nearly decapitates the man. Click! Big Al’s eyes open wide as he hears the sound of a launcher leveled at his body. Turning just as the missile is released, he desperately swings the stolen weapon at the whistling projectile… FLOOOM!!! The launcher meets the rocket and predictably detonates on impact, the force of the blast sending Big Al flying backwards out of control. Sliding across the field on his back, he shakes his head, the blast deafening him and disorientating his senses. The world rotates at odd angles as Big Al staggers to his feet, slapping at his head to shake out the cobwebs. Feeling rather than seeing the rockets as they approach, Big Al manages to leap into the air as huge explosions rock his former residence. Pulling up a portion of his toga and wiping the grime from his face, Big Al grins wanly at the advancing horde of men in black. Big Alan Steele: (to himself) “Tis not going very well… time to use my head!” Al rises up to his full impressive height of 6’5”, haughtily looking over towards the men advancing. They seem to pause a bit, unsure of exactly what he’s doing. Big Al throws his head back and… laughs? BAS: “Ha ha ha ha! Well done, gladiators! You have given me quite the pause in my training! But I fear your reserves run dry whilst my strength remains! So, I say to you, come! And by the gods I’ll give you what for!!” The men halt for but a moment, despite the spurrings of a commander. BAS: (roaring) “HI-KEEBA~!” Steele rushes forward, belting out his warcry, arm clocked back like a snake ready to strike. The men seem transfixed by the scene for a minute, this huge man rushing superior numbers, and soon they realize the gravity of the situation. Stream after stream of panic-shot missiles scream forth at Big Al, who maintains his run until the very last moment, leaping into the air at the first of the men. Implanting his head into the first one’s midsection, he drives forward, gang tackling a large group, rolling through bringing down devastating elbows into their helmets. Another group off to the side doesn’t dare fire, lest they disintegrate their own men, charge at the Big man. Crack! Smash! Ker-plowie! Ooof! Ouch! Big Al finishes off the last one with a wicked foot sweep that makes the man crumple up in pain. His eyes catch something in the corner of his eye, and he rolls left, grabbing a weapon in his roll, and comes up on his back, aiming it into the chest of the last man, the final opponent also white-knuckling his own rocket launcher. Steele smirks as the man’s eyes are in a state of fear. Click/click! Both men look at their empty weapons and toss them aside. Big Al arches himself and springs up onto his feet, slowly and confidently getting into a balanced fighting stance. He rubs his thumb against the side of his nose and smiles at the cowering man in black, who unsteadily also adopts a more battle tested stance. Big Al makes a motion with his hand to ‘come,’ and the man gulps and nods… Before running off screaming across the field. Big Al straightens up and scratches his head in confusion. Suddenly, the sound of a helicopter rumbles through the silence of the smoking field. Big Al quickly turns to face the new menace as it thunders it’s way at low altitude. A ladder dangles from the chopper, a pretty brunette with her hair pulled back in a tight bun clutching at the lower rungs, a bullhorn in a free hand. Steele grins and waves, recognizing his trusted manager Sue Saksnussum. But his grin slowly fades as he notices the dark looks on her scorn-filled visage. The helicopter drops Sue off right next to Al. She whips the bullhorn to her face and screams into it, the horn aimed directly at our hero at extreme close range. Sue: “WHAT THE _ _ _ _ ARE YOU DOING?!?!” Big Al’s head whips back from the loudness. He puts a finger in his ear, trying to rub away the pain. BAS: “By the gods, shrill Queen of the Harpies! You could deafen one with your device of sound amplification!” Sue: “GOOD!! Now answer my question!” BAS: “Why, I am merely training for my upcoming match! I see no wrong in my actions!!” Sue: “Look, I do the scheduling around here. If I had wanted you to take on the US army’s new gyro-jet division, I would have put it on you day planner. Instead, I put ‘work out at Gold’s Gym.’ So, tell me… what are you doing out here?!” BAS: (huff) “Gold’s Gym? Hardly the proper manner in taking on a devious and powerful super-villain the likes of Kannon X!! I must be ready for his implements of Doom and Destruction!!” Sue: “Wha-? What are you talking about?! Xavier Kannon?” BAS: “Indeed!” Sue: “You buffoon! He’s no super-villain! He’s just some guy named Kannon! Heck, I think he’s a religious nut.” BAS: “Er, you men he is not a man-cyborg with catastrophic weaponry of mass destruction built into a perfect soldier body capable of destroying all in his path, namely cannons of all shapes and sizes?” Sue: (shaking head sadly)“I gotta get you out more and keep your contact with super-villains more minimal – your thin shreds of realty are snapping like the elaborate bra straps on that mammoth woman Bethany! This isn’t like a Megaman game where the name of the opponent actually indicates what they are!! Still, you did have more moves than a convict in a U-Haul… but next time, leave the training regimen to me. I would like to get you to matches in a single piece.” BAS: (crossing heart) “You have my solemn oath.” Sue: (sigh while waving for the chopper) “Alright. Guess we should head home. Can I get a Hi-Keeba?” BAS: (thumbs-up) “Hi-Keeba!”
