Counted out and left for dead...the fools!TT#2

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-17-03 Adam walks into the garage of his estate. He flips a switch as the lights come on. Just standing in the doorway for a moment he gazes into the garage. Taking a couple of steps in he comes to what appears to be a pile of rubble and metal. ”Heh, they said you had been destroyed. If only they had known how wrong they were about you and about me.” he thinks to himself remembering his near fatal accident from this past summer. As the light turns green Adam kicks his bike forward and gets slammed into from the side by a car speeding through the light. Adam turns just as the impact is made. He slides up the hood and crashes into the windshield. He hits with a sickening thud and rolls off landing onto the asphalt. A pool of blood gathers at his mouth as his eyes slowly close. ”That…was a bitch but the reaction of those around me was even worse.” he remembers as he bends down and picks up the charred license plate. He holds the license plate in his hand and runs his fingers along the number seven. The only real visible number left. The black comes off on his fingers. He drops the license plate back down to the pile before taking a seat. Adam slides down a wall and sits with his knees in his chest. He’s within kicking distance of the pile, his head hanging down. ”Counted out and left for dead…the fools!” Adam kicks the rubble in frustration. He leans his head back up against the wall as he extends his other leg out. ”I’m through being counted out as a washed up has been or someone who never was, people are going to once again know the name Adam Stryker. Not only that, they will know the fear that was once attached to it as well. Starting with Chris Storm, one by one they will all fall as I build my legacy…” Adam reaches out to the gas tank and pulls the Harley Davidson insignia rather easily from it. He holds it up to eye level and examines it closely. We shift to Jaslene’s point of view. She’s sitting in the kitchen flipping through a magazine as the phone rings. Jaslene doesn’t move as the phone keeps ringing. ”Oh come on Adam pick up the phone already!” she says to herself still flipping through the magazine. Four rings. Five rings. Six rings. Finally Jaslene pushes her seat back and rips the phone from the wall in frustration. ”WHAT?!” she screams into the phone. ”Hey Jaslene is that any way to greet a friend?” a voice responds with a laugh. ”Oh sorry AJ! I thought Adam was going to answer the phone, I didn’t mean to yell at you like that.” Jaslene says, ”I don’t really know where he is right now.” There is silence as Jaslene sits back down in the seat and starts to flip through the magazine once again. ”Well?” AJ says after a few more moments. ”Well what?” Jaslene says nonchalantly. ”Where is? Could you possibly go look for him?” AJ responds with a little frustration in his voice. Jaslene lets out a sigh, ”Fine! I guess I could go look if that’s what you really want me to do.” ”Thanks.” is AJ’s reply. Jaslene gets up and heads out of the kitchen as we switch back to Adam’s point of view. He’s still sitting in the same position with the license plate in his hands. After another few minutes he flips it back onto the pile. Dust rises as it hits. Adam gets up slowly and in a fit of rage just lets into the decrepit pile of rubble. He swings his right foot into it and connects sending metal everywhere. ”GOD DAMN IT ALL!” he yells out, ”What the hell is wrong with me?” Switching back to Jaslene’s point of view she comes out of the bedroom with the phone still in hand. She walks past a white door and stops. ”Hm…I wonder if he is in there?” she thinks to herself. She reaches out for the door knob as she pulls the phone back up to her ear, ”Hold on a sec AJ, I think I may have found them.” Jaslene doesn’t give him time to respond as she drops the phone back down to her side “Adam” Jaslene asks quizzically pondering if there’s any life left in the shattered mass of a person she sees before her. “Wha, Who, Huh?” Adam responds through his daze. “Oh, sorry Jas, guess I got lost in a thought from the past. Anyway, what is it?” “Um, yeah,” Jaslene pauses while sifting the image through her subconscious, trying to connect with Adam and his memories. “A.J. is on the phone for you.” Grabbing the phone from Jaslene, Adam offers a soft sigh, “Thanks Jas”. Jaslene turns and saunters out of the garage a little mixed about Adam’s demeanor as he slowly raises the receiver to his ear. “A.J.?” Adam reassures himself. “Yeah buddy, it’s me, and I’ve got some good news!” “Oh yeah?” Stryker asks although not totally concentrating on the reality yet as he peers through the glimmering rays of sunshine out the window into the sky. “Adam? Everything alright man?” A.J. hints to the obvious. “Well, I guess I just got lost in the past for a moment, events I have pushed to the farthest columns of my memory,” Adam responds with yet another sigh. “I guess I haven’t totally come to grips with certain things that have happened in my life.” “That’s understandable man. Things in this industry happen so fast, and life moves at such a rapid pace that we often times never have the chance to fully have closure with what happens outside of the business.” “Yeah, tell me about it” Adam’s chuckle resonates through the phone. An awkward silence is held as Adam again drifts out the window to the clear blue sky scattered with satin, soft, clouds. It’s strange how a cloud in the sky can look like so many different things to so many different people. Almost a trained response to perceive what your heart wants to see through all of the gray in life, a lone white cloud offers a beacon of hope and happiness. “Adam!” A.J. yells through the telephone. “Sorry man, guess you just caught me at a moment of weakness,” Adam openly admits. “Anyway, yeah, what’s your big news?” “Well, I don’t know how big it is, more so good news than big I guess,” A.J. replies with a hint of excitement in his voice. “Well, make with it then!” “We’re flying into the states this week, should be there by Thursday.” A.J. says, almost yelling into the phone causing Adam to move the ear-piece a distance from his head. “We’re, flying in?” Adam questions “Yeah, Amira, myself, and I need to find a way to get the kangaroo on the plain.” ”You are going to try and bring Hank with you?” Adam says in shock, ”How are you going to pull that one off?” ”I don’t really know to be honest with you but we will figure it out of course.” Adam walks out of the garage with the phone still in hand.
