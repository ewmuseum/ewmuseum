I Have A Dream...

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/07/02 There are many words bounded around to describe Xavier Kannon. Arrogant. Conceited. Asshole. But one not used, that is probably just as true as those 3, is Idealist. Everything he does, no matter how malicious, devious, or underhanded, is for a reason... Obtaining it will add to his vision of how things should be... His “Ideal” view. After complaining about what he called “Oppression” in PCW against him, he went on the warpath.. But while he waged his war, something good came out of it. Hubbardville. While most of Kannon’s failures and problems were his own fault, there were real people being held down because of their religion. He both used them to justify his own actions, and helped them by aiding the founding of a city where they would feel safe. Now the next target for his “Ideal” existence has been selected.. And for probably the 1st time in his life, there is something he wants more than anything else on the face of Hubbard’s Green Earth... And that is the PCW World Heavyweight Championship... He dreams of it. He desires it. He craves it. He will do anything to get it... What he will do if he wins it is another matter all together. “Men.... Women..... Children...” Our shot opens up in Juliet Lewis Square in north Hubbardville, the site on which the building of the city was planned, and on which it’s founding was officially declared upon construction before all it’s soon-to-be residents. “.... Fellow Scientologists....” The camera is hovering high above the section of the city, showing a mass gathering of Scientologists unseen anywhere before by the public eye.... They stand shoulder to shoulder, chest to back in the Square, with all the roads coming off it backlogged with human beings trying to get to the large mass of fellow Scientologists. It is night and the sun as disappeared from the Wisconsin sky to leave only bright Orange streetlights and a giant lighting rig to illuminate those gathered who fill the mild night air with a constant low frequency murmur. “Fellow Scientologists.” Cutting from the overhead view we are shown PCW superstar, Gold Patron Meritorious in the Church of Scientology, and they Mayor of Hubbardville... Xavier Kannon. His bright Green eyes fill with pride as he looks out at the huge gathering before him, a slow confident nodding motion being carried out by his head, on top of which his mane of long Red hair has been tied back in a braided ponytail. He is standing on a stage, at the foot of which stands a thick rank of Hubbardville Security to keep the public away from the dignitaries on the stage. Standing in one long line behind Xavier are his wife and valet Eleanor Kannon-Hall, Hollywood A-List Scientologists, Tom Cruise and John Travolta, the star of Cheers, Kirstey Alley, Actress and star of the sit-com Dharma and Greg, Jenna Elfman, actress Juliet Lewis, and singer and voice of South Park’s Chef, Isaac Hayes. They all hold hands in solidarity as Xavier stands right in front of the microphone, gazing out into his fellow Hubbard followers. Xavier : “I have A DREAM!!!” These simple words, strung together invoke a rush of emotion from this before him, just like they did for Martin Luther King when he spoke them... His declaration sending shivers down the spines of those gathered in the Square, who were already hyped enough. Behind the Mayor, the line of famous Scientologists nod their heads to back up his words, while Xavier takes a moment to adjust his Black tie that he wears with a Purple shirt and Black suit. Xavier : “I have a dream where every Scientologist.... Man, Woman or Child.... Black, White, or Yellow..... Will be able to play alongside Christian children, Islamic children, Jewish children.... Even Hari Krishna children...” His words strike a note deep within the crowd, who all moved to the city of Hubbardville to escape the oppression of the outside world, and who pray for the day when they will be accepted as a normal religion by society... None seeing it is people like Kannon himself who stops that happening. Behind him Ellie~! nudges John Travolta and nods to him... Eleanor : “Hari... Krishna.... Children.” They both turn and look eyes front to the back of Xavier who is flicking his Green eyes from face to face in the crowd, his face of defiance quickly melting to one of disgust and defeat. Xavier : “But it will never happen...” He solemnly drops his head and shakes it, as behind him the line mimics his motions perfectly, all looking upset as they admit a very weakly thought defeat... As soon it becomes clear why they are there. They are not there to speak. Or offer any opinions. Not even for the good of Scientology. They are there to back him up with their body language, the followers who look up to them never thinking about doubting what such a collection of their idols believe. The heads in the crowd are all looking up at the stage, their eyes full of hope, all praying to Hubbard that Kannon will denounce his last words. Xavier : “Because they do not want THEIR children to play with YOUR children.... They only want young Scientologists around them so they can POISON their minds, trying to tell you that Scientology is not a proper religion... Like the PEONS that they are.” Eleanor : “That....... They...... Are!” Kannon’s eyes narrow and his head slowly turn to Ellie~! who quickly lowers her head as to avoid his Green eyes meeting her pale Blue pair. In the Square before Xavier, the collective crowd all turn and nod to each other... Taking in Kannon’s words as truth, buying what they are being told by Scientology’s upper ranks to further distance them from the outer world and fuel their hate and mistrust of others. Behind him Eleanor mutters to herself, in ear ranch of John Travolta. Eleanor : “I have a Dream? Wow, Malcolm X should sue his ass.” The star of Battlefield Earth whispers back to her. John : “Actually it was Martin Luther King.” The Prep looks deep in thought for a moment, scratching her head that hosts her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights. Eleanor : “What movie was that from again?” Travolta looks at her still, a little puzzled and still whispering. John : “No movie... He was a real life person.” This comes as a revelation to Mrs Kannon-Hall. Eleanor : “Like.... NO WAY!??!?!?!” Her outburst again distracts Kannon who glares right at her, silencing her immediately and she darts into a standing straight position, mouthing “Sorry” to him then gesturing to him to turn around. In front of him the jam packed Scientologists in Isaac Hayes Square are waiting for more. Xavier : “I have a dream..... A dream where I can walk into work, into PCW and not have announcers oppress me... Claiming I have done vicious acts... Lies... PURELY because I read the works of Hubbard rather than their out dated BIBLE!” He then shows a face we have never seen before... Downturn.. Glum, almost as if he is sulking. Playing up to the crowd and trying to get his fans and fellow Hubbard followers to feel some empathy with him. Xavier : “But it isn’t going to happen.. They’ll NEVER accept a Scientologist. Even though I only do the best I can for that company, they’ll never look past the fact that I’m a Scientologist.” He sighs overly loudly and in a very animated manner he shakes his head, the row behind him doing the same in perfect time.. The words and actions seen on the stage impregnating themselves into the brains of the Hubbardville residents. Xavier : “And I have a dream.... Where I walk out of Wrestle Vessel with the PCW World Title around my waist, sending a message around the world that they can TRY to hold us down, but in the end it will be OUR actions hat shall triumph!” He may be cold and calculating, but Kannon is also charismatic and his words stir up something inside the crowd and they are no longer a gathering of the oppressed and cheated... But the hopeful and optimistic as they hang on his every word. Xavier : “..In Hubbard’s name.... I promise you.... THAT one, WILL happen.” He nods confidently as the show begins to fade to Black... The rank of Scientologists behind him walking forwards and applauding as they incorporate him into their chain of solidarity. “KANNON! KANNON! KANNON! KANNON! KANNON!” The Mayor looks down upon his people, pretending to wipe a tear from his eye and as our picture is nearly on Black we see the trademark smirk morph from his lips. “KANNON! KANNON! KANNON! KANNON! KANNON!”
