A Special Day (1 of 2)

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-09-02 Time: 2pm Thursday-=[We start off the day in the RoC household like usual. This time however, we don’t see Adam RoC in site. Instead we see his wife Shannon in the kitchen with her good friend, Carolyn. Carolyn has just entered the room as Shannon is chopping onions beside the kitchen sink.]=- Carolyn: Girl guess what! Shannon: Hey Carolyn, what’s goin on? Carolyn: I got two tickets to Brian McKnight this Saturday! Shannon: WHAT?! Carolyn: That’s right, WKYS was giving them out all week, and I finally got some tickets! Shannon: You got to be kidding me! Carolyn: So what time should I pick you up? Shannon: Just pick me…you know what? Saturday is our Special Day. Carolyn: And? Just celebrate it on Sunday. That man won’t care if you celebrate it one day later. Shannon: You don’t understand. Adam is so sweet when it comes to our Special Day. On our 3rd Special Day, he rented a whole entire park so that we can have a quiet picnic under the sun. Carolyn: Didn’t it rain that day? Shannon: yea but… Carolyn: And didn’t he leave early because he forgot a basketball game was on? Shannon: But still… Carolyn: And didn’t he… Shannon: Aight shut up Carolyn! The point is he can be sweet when he wants to. Carolyn: And I could lose wait if I wanted to, but as you can tell, I just don’t want to. Shannon: Everyone always see the bad side of Adam, I always see the good side. -=[Thug roC walks into the room with some sweats on and a gray hoody. He roCies an empty orange juice bottle as he heads to the refrigerator for some more]=- Shannon: Hey Sweetie! Thug roC: [mumbles]. Carolyn: Sweet huh? More like sour. Shannon: So what are you doing today? Thug roC: Today is my off day, I don’t have to train. I don’t have to do any paperwork. I don’t have to do anything at all. So I just want to relax and not be bothered. Shannon: Oh. -=[Thug roC starts to exit the room, but remembers something and turns around]=- Thug roC: Oh yea, don’t make any plans for Saturday. Shannon: Really? Thug roC: Yea, I’m golfing with Machine all that day. So I need you to be here that night cause then “it’s on.” Shannon: It is? [To Carolyn]. Told you he can be sweet. Thug roC: Yea, it’ll be on alright. I need you to let the satellite guy in so he can turn our Dish back on. I can’t stand basic cable. Thanks hun. Carolyn: Awww, satellite TV on your Special Day, that’s so romantic Shannon: No, that’s just a plan so that I won’t guess what he’s really planning. He just trying to make me thing that he’s forgotten. But I know… -=[We see Clare walking in through the back door and slams it behind her cutting Shannon off. She then digs in the freezer and grabs a tub of ice cream]=- Shannon: What’s wrong with you? Clare: Men. I can’t stand them Carolyn: Well don’t go eating every time you’re mad at some boy. You’ll end up like me. You see this gut, that’s Robert Clark –1989. See these Thighs? That’s Eugene Clemens of 1991. See these breasts…well my momma gave me these. Clare: Whatever -=[Clare walks out with her tub of ice cream]=- Carolyn: That girl is too skinny, I can’t even make a decent broth out of her. Shannon, are you sure you won’t rethink this? Shannon: yes. I love me some Brian McKnight, but I know my Adam has something planned. Carolyn: I swear, you put too much faith in that man. If my husband ever told me to wait for the cable guy, I would kill him. Shannon: That shows why you’re not married. Carolyn: The way I see it, you’re gonna be stuck in this marriage forever. But Brian will only be here for one McKnight. -=[Carolyn starts making her way towards the door]=- Carolyn: And if you think Tug RoC actually remembered your Special Day… Shannon: He hasn’t forgotten Carolyn! -=[Shannon closes the door behind her]=- Shannon: Gawd! I hope he hasn’t forgotten! Time: 4pm Thursday-=[We fast forward in the day where Thug roC has a small putting exercise setup for himself. He is in the living room with a putter in hand and the small 8 foot strip of green to practice in. He has the putter up to him like a mic as he does his own commentary]=- Thug roC: [In quiet golf announcer’s voice]. It’s behind another intense day with intense heat, and even intense rainfall. The intensity is in the air as the green is as bumpy as the back of Michael Clark Duncan’s head. And it’s come down to the two must handsome men in the PGA. Adam RoCker and Tiger Woods. If I was a betting man, I wouldn’t put my money on the big tooth fella this time. Here it is, the final put for the million dollar master’s championship, and that gawd awful green jacket. -=[In he background we see Shannon and her oldest son Michael (16 years old) talking to each other]=- Shannon: Ok Michael, there’s your father. Michael: Come on mom, he’s putting. I’ll end up in trouble. Shannon: Do I have to remind you that I gave you life? Michael: But he can take it away! Shannon: Just go in there please. -=[Thug roC is about to put when Michael approaches him]=- Michael: Hey Dad! -=[The sudden disturbance cause Thug roC to miss his putt completely.]=- Michael: What are you up to? Thug roC: Nothin, I just gave away my master’s jacket, that’s what I’m up to. Michael: I don’t get your fascination with this stupid game. It’s so boring. Thug roC: Boring? Do you know how much concentration goes into golf? Do you know how hard this game really is? Golf is the most mentally challenging game there is. Michael: Let me try. Thug roC: I said it was “mentally challenging,” not “for the mentally challenged” Michael: I can do it. Thug roC: What you think you can just walk up and just pop the ball in the whole. This isn’t sex ya know. This is very scientific. You have to understand all the factors like the gust of the wind, the straight line which you must hit the ball on. Everything factors into every shot -=[Michael hits the ball into the hole easily]=- Michael: You’re just thinking too much. By the way, what are you doing this weekend? Thug roC: I’m going to noneya Michael: Where’s that? Thug roC: Noneya-business. Why what are your plans? Michael: Nothing. Thug roC: Good, let me make you some plans. Plan on washing my car and cleaning the gutters this Saturday. Michael: Oh, I just remembered that I have to read to some orphans. Thug roC: Too late… -=[Michael walks off with his head down into the kitchen where he is approached by Shannon]=- Shannon: What he say? Michael: He says I have car wash duty, thanks a lot mom. -=[Michael walks off]=- Shannon: Never send a boy to do a women’s job. Time: 10pm Thursday-=[ We find Thug roC on his computer late at night while Shannon is above him massaging his shoulders. She’s still trying to get his plans for the weekend out of him, but so far she has had no luck]=- Thug roC: Damn, someone keeps out bidding me for this Muhammad Ali gloves. Shannon: So what? Thug roC: So what, baby these are his actual gloves. These are from the thrilla in manilla. The best fight except for that celebrity fight between Tod Bridges and Vanilla Ice.Shannon: Are you going to come to bed? I have something for you… Thug roC: Wait a minute. What’s going on here? Shannon: huh? Thug roC: Did you wreck the car again? Shannon: No. I just wanted to show you my love. Seems like just yesterday that we got together. Thug roC: Yea, I guess so. Shannon: How long has it been? Thug roC: About a week since you last wrecked the car. Shannon: No I mean since we got married. Thug roC: I dunno, just feels like forever! -=[Shannon gets upset and storms off]=- To Be Continued...
