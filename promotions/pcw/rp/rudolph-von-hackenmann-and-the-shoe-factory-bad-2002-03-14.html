Rudolph von Hackenmann and the Shoe Factory (Bad...)

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/02 Sunday Sabotage, March 10, 2002. The PCW Tag Team division was reformed, with the Golden Game, the Controversial Messiahs, and Metallyx getting a few teams to join them. These teams were joined by some signees from NTICW, including Rudolph von Hackenmann and Mason Creeper, Fear Factory! This Sunday, they wrestle against fellow recent signees the Cowabunga Connection. Can Rudolph and Mason coexist for long enough to survive until Sunday, nevertheless defeat Totally Tubular Tommy and Heavy Metal Harry? Only time can tell this, but know one thing. Since his recent signing in PCW, Rudolph has had some time to devise some plans to get revenge on the scientists in Austria who scorned him so badly. What does he have in store for them this time? (The scene slowly fades in outside of a shoe factory in the middle of the night, when suddenly, the door slowly creaks open. Out of the door falls a bodybag, and Rudolph von Hackenmann soon walks out. He looks over at the camera with a scowl on his face quickly, looks down at the bodybag, and looks back up at the camera.) Rudolph: Would you stop following me around? (Rudolph walks down the dark street with the camera still following him. He finally looks over at the camera again with an angrier look on his face.) Rudolph: Can you not take a hint? (As Rudolph continues down the street, the camera still follows him, hiding behind anything he can, like bushes, benches, and mailboxes. He hides behind a mailbox and Rudolph sees him. Rudolph growls and runs, and after about five seconds, we can no longer see him. The cameraman is apparently running after him now, since the camera moves speedily down the streets, dodging people and bobbing up and down. The camera points to a window on the side of a house and sees Rudolph von Hackenmann being attacked by an old man. The cameraman slowly opens the door and the camera moves inside, showing the action closer. The old man beating the crap out of Rudolph is almost six feet tall and has large muscles. He quickly turns to the camera and starts to walk closer.) Man: Why are there more people in my house? I am seriously starting to get a little angry! (The man swiftly reaches his hand out and pushes the cameraman down, knocking the camera out of his hands. We can hear Rudolph screaming and things cracking in the background, but all we can see is the blue carpet and the dirty white walls. Finally, we hear the door open and hear cracking on the concrete outside. The camera quickly moves up and out the door, and a hand reaches out and slams it behind him. Rudolph slowly gets up off the ground, this time with the camera pointing at him. He shakes off the injuries he has forgone and an evil grin slowly comes across his face.) Rudolph: This guy needs to pay... And so do you! You following me around is what got me into this mess in the first place! (The camera turns away from Rudolph and starts to move very quickly. After about a minute, there is no movement other than that of the pedestrians on the street. The camera turns around to see that Rudolph has disappeared. The camera then looks up to show the Shoe Factory sign... Slowly, the camera ventures into the shoe factory, and still moving slowly, the camera ventures to the back of the main room, avoiding various machines. Through a door the camera travels, just to reveal Rudolph new laboratory, with experiments on the left side and shoes on the right side. Rudolph is standing in the back of the room, and cranking can be heard. The camera zooms in to show that Rudolph is putting something into a shoe. Rudolph continues to work hard, silently, as the camera watches him, shaking the whole time, as the cameraman fears being noticed. After about two minutes of watching and waiting, Rudolph finally finishes what he was doing. The camera swiftly moves behind a box full of shoes, and pops up to show Rudolph von Hackenmann holding up a pair of tennis shoes sideways. Inside the shoes are two spikes bolted to the insole. Rudolph lets out a horrific, ear-piercing laugh.) Rudolph: Hahahahaha! This will teach that little bastard! (Rudolph stops laughing, and he carries the shoes out of the factory, with the camera following him all the way. He conceals the spikes by pressing the shoes against his body as he walks down the street. He sits next to the man's house for about two minutes until he walks out. Rudolph and the camera slowly sneak into the house. Rudolph grabs a pair of white tennis shoes off the floor and takes them, replacing them with the shoes he rigged with spikes. The man then comes through the door. Rudolph jumps behind the couch, and the man looks over at his shoes.) Man: Hey! What kind of crap is this? (Rudolph pops out from behind the couch, running at the man. With a swift punch, Rudolph knocks him cold, and he hits the floor extremely hard.) Rudolph: A job well done... (Rudolph slowly walks out the door as the scene fades away...)
