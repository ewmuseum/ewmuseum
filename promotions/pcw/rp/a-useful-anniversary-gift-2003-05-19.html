A Useful Anniversary Gift

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/19/03 It was the Saturday after the Fusion where I had been forced to tag with the man whom I was unfortunately married to, yet everything had gone much better than I had assumed. We managed to gain the win even if it was through just as much fluke as our marriage and somehow I got away without having a prank pulled on me once more. Things seemed great although the minute I got home I stumbled across a package on my doorstep from none other than my insignificant other. It wasn’t all that large, but it was wrapped up neatly in pink wrapping paper with a card attached to it. “Oh great I can only imagine what this could be.” I sighed as I picked the package up and walked into the living room, taking a seat on the couch before very cautiously tearing open the package from the man who gave me the surname I despised. “AHHH!” I screamed as an opened package of green hair dye came spilling out of the package onto the white tank top I was wearing. I should have expected something like this from the Prince of Pranks but at the same time I was thinking perhaps he went through a change of heart but then again how likely was that? I grabbed the card which was attached to the gift and used it’s envelope to wipe up some of the mess. As always though my curiosity seized hold of me and I ripped open the envelope to see just what was placed in there. Inside of it I found a homemade card from none other than the jester himself which read on the outside “To my oh so loving wife”. I opened the card only imagining what kind of message would be inside and sure enough there was another one which read “Happy two month anniversary!” “Two months!” I gasped considering that two months was two months longer than I had wanted this to last. I tossed the card to the side and picked up the green hair dye which was the same tone of green as Gaz’s hair. The green dye that had leaked over the top onto the side stained my hands as I walked towards the trash can to throw the unwanted anniversary gift away and out of sight. “WHAT ARE YOU DOING?” Screamed a voice into my ear that I had recognized all too well. Surely enough I turned to the side and Mini Gaz was peering over my shoulder with widened emerald eyes as the gift that was given to me by the man he was created after was about to be thrown away. “I’m getting rid of this crap, what do you think I’m doing?” I responded to the mini green jester without much remorse considering that his presence served only more of a reminder of the man I couldn’t stand being married to. “DON’T DO IT!” Screamed the smaller jester once more as his eyes grew wider as my hand hovered over the plastic trash can. “Why not? I mean what good is hair dye that I’m never going to use?” I said, confused as to what was so special about this hair dye that caused the smaller version of Gaz to cringe every time I got closer to the trash can with the dye in my hand. “Just think of what you could do with it sweet cheeks!” Mini Gaz replied as he breathed a sigh of relief as I moved my hand away from the trash bin to listen to what he had to say. “Dye my hair the same color as Gaz’s?” I questioned confused as to why I would want to do such a thing considering I hated the man. “Even better.” Mini Gaz smirked before continuing on. “Dye someone else’s hair green.” His smirk grew upon completion of his sentence. “Just who’s hair were you thinking of?” I questioned as the temptation began to consume me as I thought of the number of possible people I would love to pull such a prank on. “Well there is A.J. Trinidad, you’re facing him on Tuesday and Friday and well let’s just say it would send a message out to him.” Mini Gaz suggested but as usual I thought about what the consequences and decided that pulling such a trick on A.J. wouldn’t be the greatest of ideas. “I don’t think I really want to mess with him, he seems like the type to only be fueled by their anger.” I answered as the thought of A.J. beating me down and humiliating on both Turmoil and Fusion filled my head, causing me to shudder in fear and move on to another possible person to pull such a prank on. “How about Manuel Labor?” The smaller jester offered another victim, this one seeming more reasonable but at the same time it seemed rather pointless considering I didn’t have much reason to pull such a trick on him since the only thing he was one of my three opponents on Turmoil. “No that’s pointless. Why do I need to play such games with him?” I argued back, not seeing much point in pulling on a prank on someone whose mind I really didn’t need to get into. “Then in that case the only person left would be….” The smaller jester started up before I cut him off quickly before he could mention the name I was almost positive was next. “OH NO! There is NO way I’m pulling a trick like that on Stryker. The last thing I want is my head smashed in with a steel chair on his behalf once again. It was bad enough when he did it before just to get to Chris.” I replied as I remembered back to January, when I suffered a chair shot delivered from Adam Stryker for the simple reason that I was an ally of Chris whom at that time he wanted to make suffer. The last thing I wanted was to be at the end of another one of those chair shots. “In that case, how about the usual victim of your new attitude?” The jester suggested, seemingly out of ideas for possible victims. “My mom?” I questioned, not sure if he meant her or someone else, but then again who else did I prank? No one from what I could remember besides the one time I painted El Romantico’s locker room pink simply to get under his skin. “Of course.” Mini Gaz replied as if it were a given. “After all does she not deserve it with all the hell she has put you through?” “You know, this actually sounds pretty good.” I replied as the image of my mother looking in the reflection of the mirror and seeing that her hair had been turned the same color green as Gaz’s ran though my mind. I chuckled slightly and then realized that once again she was the perfect person to be the victim of my prank. “Then quick, get to work before she wakes up!” Mini Gaz commanded from his station on my shoulder as I rushed to the bathroom to replace the contents of the shampoo bottle with the thick green hair dye. Surely enough just as I had finished pouring the last drops of hair dye into the shampoo bottle my mother had arrived downstairs just on schedule to take her shower. I scurried out of the bathroom quickly but quite unfortunately just as I had reached the door my mother was waiting there to get in. “And you didn’t even flush the toilet or wash your hands? My god no manners, no sanitary awareness, what kind of child did I raise? A failure if you ask me.” She ranted on as she glared down at me with the blue eyes I had inherited from her. She must have thought I was in there to go to the bathroom but that was great since that meant that she had not caught me. All I had to do is play along with her rant and act unsanitary and it would lead to her once again being humiliated by me. “Oops I must have forgotten.” I played the fool as I slapped myself on the forehead and walked back into the bathroom to do both of the tasks she had accused me of forgetting. “Good, now get out! I have to take a shower.” She commanded as I finished cleaning up my hands, hoping she wouldn’t spot the green dye that had washed off my hands and now stained the sink and the water used to wash it off. Instead of coming up with a witty response to her attitude I simply walked away in a chorus of silent giggles in anticipation of the screams that would come from the bathroom the minute she found out that the shampoo she was using wasn’t really shampoo at all. I paced around the hallway outside the bathroom for what seemed like hours but what was in reality fifteen minutes before I heard the sound that confirmed that my prank had been successfully pulled off. “AHHHHH, Tessa I am going to kill you!” She shouted with an emphasis on the word kill before busting through the bathroom door and glaring at me with a look that told me I better know a way to fix this, but instead of paying it much attention I turned my ears up for the words which would surely follow this look. “You better know how to fix this!” She threatened as she indicated what she was talking about by pointing up at the mess of short green hair that sat upon her head. “Oh I do, but I’ve got to run mom. Got a match against Stryker, Labor, and Trinidad I’ve got to get ready for.” I said before dashing towards the front door and making an exit as Mini Gaz sat on my shoulder with a satisfied smile. “See, you’re representing the Green family greatly! Good job sweet cheeks!” The jester said as he assured me with a pat on my shoulder. Instead of smiling though I looked at him not wanting to hear about the Green family. Sure I was now part of it now, but it wasn’t by choice and the last thing I wanted was to be reminded once again that I was now a part of the green family. “Green…bleh…who says I’m doing this for the Green family? As far as I’m concerned this is simply to show my mother than until she starts respecting me she’s going to continue to get no respect from me.” I said not caring anything about the Green family part of my pranks. “If you say so sweet cheeks, but we all know you really do love being a Green so you shouldn’t deny it.” The devilish jester said before making his exit. I rolled my eyes in disgust at his comments before laughing if off. If there was anyone in the world who didn’t want to be a Green it was me, but I guess until the day Gaz decided that he was sick of it too I would have to learn to live with it which might be forever considering Gaz had seemed to be using this marriage to his benefit and making the best of it simply to make my life a living hell.
