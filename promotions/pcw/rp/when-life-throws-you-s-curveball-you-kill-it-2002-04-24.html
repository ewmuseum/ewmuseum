When Life throws you s curveball, you kill it!

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/02 The following RP is narrated by Feletia. Here’s her take on an average night with Badge. Enjoy. Here we go again. I ask Mike for one night alone. One night where he and I could relax and just be with each other and it turns into this. Things we’re going well, Mike is kicking Kramer Wild Wing’s @ss like normal, when out from nowhere Orpheus Grant and Karl Leblanc come out from the back and attack Mike causing him to lose the match. I would have tried to stop the attack, but there were two of them, and I decided that I would stay away from the beating I would receive. I mean what woman would stand a chance against two guys? Maybe Ellie, but I don’t know if I would consider her a woman. I’ve heard she has bigger testicles than her boyfriend Xavier Kannon does. So here I sit, riding around a sh*tty town like Fargo, Mike in one of his spells of rage, looking for something bad to do to someone. “Hey Feletia, let me know if you see any black people around here.” He tells me. “Ok” I tell him I will, but I definitely won’t. There’s no way I am going to help Mike hurt anyone. I could just read the headlines tomorrow: “Ex- police officer kills black man with his bare hands.” That would look great. That’s all we need. Another paper blaming the police for all of the brutality towards black people.) “Is that a black guy?” he asks, slowing down the car, and pointing across the street.. “No hun, it’s just a white guy with a black sweatshirt.” I tell him. Thank goodness it actually is a white guy. Hope fully Mike won’t be able to find anyone, and we can go back to the hotel. Some might say, “Why would you stay with a man that you knew killed people?” It’s not that easy. I’m not going to leave him just because he makes some bad decisions. What would have happened if Hillary Clinton had left Bill? She wouldn’t be a senator right now, that’s for sure. In this messed up world you have to stick with your man, or they will get worse. Say what you wants about me, but you can’t deny that I give my undying love to this man. He’s still the same guy, just has a few things going on in his head. I notice him beginning to get frustrated. Good maybe we can go back to the hotel. Then the worst possible thing that could happen, happens. As we drive down the road, we spot a black man walking down the street. The only black guy in all of Fargo, and he had to pick tonight to go out for a walk. “Ah ha!” Mike exclaims slamming on the brakes. “Swamp Dawg is toast!” I try to stop him, “Mike you know that isn’t Karl LeBlanc don’t you?” I ask him. He turns and gives me a look that scares the sh*t out of me. I’ve known Mike for a long time but I had never seen him look like that. He was gone. There was no stopping him. You could see that Mike wasn’t there anymore. As absurd as this sounds, In his messed up mind he actually believes that this black man is actually Karl LeBlanc. As Mike exits the car he walks over and opens the trunk. From there he yells out to the man... “Hey Karl! Yeah you! I need some help with my car, could you help me?” The man is hesitant at first but starts to head towards the car. Oh how badly I wanted to tell the man to turn around and run away, but I knew if I did, I would be the dead person instead of this man. It’s cowardly I know, but I fear death. I haven’t exactly lived a good life, and if there is a hell, I would probably end up there. As the boy gets close to the car I finally get a good look at him. My God it’s just a boy. He has to be no older than 19, coming over to help out. “Hey thanks for helping Karl!” Mike says as the boy approaches the car. I open the driver’s side door and cry out to Mike... “Come on Mike, it’s not Karl! Look at him it’s not LeBlanc! It’s just a boy!” Mike hears my plead and walks over towards me and kicks the door shut in my face. I watch as Mike grabs one of those big black mag-lights. He says something to the boy and points toward the back of the car. As the boy bends down to check it out, Mike raises the light over his head and clobbers the boy with it. I Try to close my eyes, but the sound is ringing through my ears. I open my eyes and look at Mike. His face has a look of pleasure on it. A look of lust. Again he raises the big flashlight over his head and strikes the boy. And again. And again. I flinch every time I hear the sound of the flashlight smashing into the boy’s skull. After five or six deafening blows, it turns to silence again. Then Mike returns to the drivers seat of the car, dumps the bloody mag-light in the back seat and we take off. Mike then bends over towards me, I flinch at first, but only briefly, as I realize that he just wants to give me a kiss. I lean toward him and he plants one of my forehead. As Mike and I head back towards the hotel, I glance over at him and notice that he is finally back to normal. Finally his awful look is gone as he looks peaceful. The things you put up with for your man. (Scene fades)
