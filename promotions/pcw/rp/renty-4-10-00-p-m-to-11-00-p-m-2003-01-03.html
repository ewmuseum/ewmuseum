Renty-4-10:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m.

3 January 2003

Title: Renty-4-10:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m. Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 1-3-2003 The Following Is Taking Place Between The Hours Of 10:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m. ....This Is.... Renty-4 4... 3... 2...1... 10:01 ..::/::..Our story begins back in the state of the art Rent-Cave, where we left our PCW superhero and his holographic helper, Wonder Woman V2, in a tense state of affairs. Our hero's fists are clenched ever tightly, the adrenaline building within him as he has his eyes upon the mysterious person that had him frozen in his track only a mere hour ago...the camera pans around, leaving our hero and Wonder Woman behind as it turns completely, stopping on a face quite familar to you all....because it IS the face of someone familiar to us all...RENT-A-VILLAIN!!!He stands tall, his Green Lantern costume sporting black where the green should be and vice-versa, the exact opposite of the way Rent-A-Hero is wearing the traditional colored Green Lantern outfit. He has ALL the features of our masked hero...yet he seems more coniving, more intense...more evil. His crimson red eyes stare holes throuh our hero as he stands with a murderous smile upon his face, smiling heartily as he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Did you miss me?' Rent-A-Hero:'I thought you were...were...'Rent-A-Villain:'DEAD?! Ohh Renty....you didn't really think you could escape me did you? You HONESTLY thought you could run from your counterpart? The versa to your vice? The ominous darkness to your brilliant light? Never. But I wouldn't call this the time for a family reunion....I call this the time to DESTROY YOU.' ..::/::..Wonder Woman floats from the side of our hero, to right in front of the face of his evil counterpart. In a desperately inqusitive voice, she asks..::/::.. Wonder Woman V2:'Your involved with whoever it was who stole Rent-A-Hero's blue dot....aren't you?'Rent-A-Villain:'Guilty as charged.'..::/::..Our hero steps closer, stopping about 4 feet away from his eternal nemesis as his aqua blue eyes tighten in intensity, our hero filling his voice with all the courage he can possibly muster as he exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Reveal whoever it is that put you up to this Rent-A-Villain....if your apart of me, there HAS to be some good in you. Stop this madness before it gets out of control.'..::/::..Rent-A-Villain laughs maniaclly, amused by the statement of his counterpart. He cuts the distance between them in half by another 2 feet, replying to our hero in a threatening manner that would scare the shell of of an armadillo..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'My whole life...I have realized I am nothing but a clone, a copy....of something inferior, something weak. I'll feel so much better when this whole thing is over...when your destroyed and gone from my sight FOREVER. Then only the superior Rent-A-Whatever will stand. Or....I can just seal your fate right here in this crummy 'Rent-Cave' of yours and save you 24 hours of agony.' Rent-A-Hero:'You can call me pathetic...you can call me inferior...BUT NOBODY TALKS SMACK ABOUT MY RENT-CAVE!!! WONDER WOMAN, DETAIN THE INTRUDER!!'Wonder Woman V2:'Activating detainment un...'Rent-A-Villain:'Rent-Cave, access code 'Shizznittlebam'...shut down all systems and proceed to wipe all data from systems. No overide' Wonder Woman V2:'Wait...HOW DOES HE KNOW THE PA...S...CO...' ..::/::..Our hero is dazed that his holographic helper has phased out within the blink of an eye, all the systems in the Rent-Cave shutting down abruptly one by one as Rent-A-Villain just laughs at his 'pulling of the trump card' on Rent-A-Hero. Finally, all the systems shut down except for the lights. Rent-A-Villain smirks, cracking his knuckles as he approaches our hero and stands face to face, eye to eye with him..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'You think your so smart don't you? Don't you realize that I'll ALWAYS be one step ahead of you, Hero. We are a mirror of each other...but this side of the mirror is going to kick your ass!'..::/::..Our hero tightens his spandex gloves, looking to Rent-A-Villain with a straight face and a surplus of bravery in his heart as he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'I'm a mirror for you too.....hehe, I knew I'd get to pull that line off from that Star Trek movie.'Rent-A-Villain:'Idiot.'Rent-A-Hero:'Villain...let's make this the last time one of us rises from the dead. One shall stand....the other shall fall. Now if your ready to get it on, I say....groovy.' ..::/::..After a few seconds of dead air, that tense moment that can be cut with a knife....Rent-A-Hero and Villain each throw a respective right hook, both catching each other in the jaw as they fall to the floor...the two of them in unison exclaiming..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero and Rent-A-Villain:'HOLY SOUR JAWBREAKERS!!' Meanwhile...10:47 ..::/::..When we last left the lovely Rosaria.....we found her contimplating with VERY little time how to save herself and the children from danger as a group of mysterious intruders were attempting to break their way into the video room of the Rent-A-Dad's orphange. We return just as the door bursts open, light flooding the room as a group of four masked henchmen in full black body suits and skeleton masks flood the room, securing the area as someone else enters behind them. The person behind them all flicks the light switch on, that 'person' revealed to be a beautiful Hispanic women in an all black combat suit with a combat rifle in hand, her eyes soft green and her hair the darkest of black with ONE silver streak in the front. She looks about, noticing the room empty of all the children that had been there before AND Rosaria....only a TV playing and the huge quilted rug still in place. The woman seems quite suspicious of the entire scenario, looking to her henchmen and ordring in a mild tone..::/::.. Woman:'Henchmen...search the area. Goon, Thug you take the east area. Fall Guy, Ruffian...west. I will handle the north.'Henchmen:'YES LADY KINTARA!'..::/::..The henchmen and Kintara spread out, looking throughout the room as they proceed to ransack everything from the closet, to the video tape shelf, to the toy bin...relentless in thier pursuit of completing their mission...::/::.. !!~Sneeze~!! ..::/::..All the henchmen and Kintara spin around, alarmed by the sneeze as they all have their eyes glued to the other closet next to the door they just burst through. Kintara takes the lead, quietly signaling them to flank her as she approaches the closet with slow, small steps. Upon reaching the closet, Kintara pushes back her silver bang and holds her gun at ready as she places her hand on the knob. Slowly turning it...she is not even given the chance to pull it open as she is suddenly BOMBARDED by a grup of kids, tackling her to the ground as they yell and scream with all their might...::/::.. Kintara:'GET THESE BRATS OFF ME NOW!!!' ..::/::..The four henchmen attempt to help Kintara, who is now having her hair pulled furiously and her feet stepped on as she screams in utter annoyance. As the henchmen attempt to pull the children off, Rosaria rushes from the closet with a set of weights on a metal straight bar, tossing them at the four henchmen as they topple over to the ground, pinned by the excess weight as they kick and scream for freedom..::/::.. Kintara:'LAZY HENCHMEN?! WHAT DO I PAY YOU FOR?!'Kids:'GO ROSARIA!! GO!! GO!! GET RENT-A-HERO!! GO!!'..::/::..The kids continues to kick, bite, and scratch away at their would-be kidnappers, Rosaria following the encouragement of the kids and racing out of the video room to retrieve Rent-A-Hero..::/::.. Rosaria::'I've got to get Ren...'..::/::..The moment she exits the door, her chest slams into the chest of a spandex covered figure, Rosaria looking up as it appears to be RENT-A-HERO standing before her. She hugs him in the deepest show of relief, pointing to the video room as she exclaims..::/::.. Rosaria:'RENT-A-HERO, RENT-A-HERO, THE KIDS AND I NEED YOU!! COME ON, I'LL EXPLAIN IT ALL AFTER YOU....wait....your eyes...' ..::/::..After a good look into 'Rent-A-Hero's' eyes, Rosaria begins to slowly back away, the spandex figure devilishly smirking as he inches closer and closer to Rosaria as she backs away..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'I believe we haven't met...Rosaria is it? I'm Rent-A-Villain...you musthave mistaken me for somebody....dead.' ..::/::..The scene switches to the Rent-Cave....now a smoldering and smoking mess. All the equipment is in shambles from what must have been a brutal battle...and laying face down under a hulking piece of sheet metal...is our fallen hero. Unable to hear the screams of Rosaria and the kids as his battered body remains unconcious..::/::.. 5... 4...3... 2...1... 11:00..... ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
