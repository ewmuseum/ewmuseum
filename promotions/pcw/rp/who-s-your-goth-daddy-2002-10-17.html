Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Who's your Goth Daddy???? Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/17/02 Dateline: Thursday October 17, 2002Time: 7:56pm CST Place: The evil Goth Lair of Duke WilliamsOccasion: Continental Goth Celebration party. -The usually empty secret Goth Lair of Dukestar the Goblin is hopping with life as a party takes place in his honor. It’s a motley crew to say the least filled with black haired freaks, pierced monsters, vampires, and even shape changers, and a few Gods. Duke’s mortal servant Roseanne Bar is escorting him through the crowd intruding him to his constituents and peers.- Roseanne: Come on over this way I want you to meat the oldest and most famous vampire in the world. Dukestar: I hate meeting people. -Duke towers above most the Goths as he stands 6’3. His hair is put back in pig tails and his make is white and black. The shinny continental title rests over his creepy Goth God shoulder as people pat it when he walks by.- Roseanne: Come on this party is for you. Dukestar: Marlon its good to see you. -Duke bumps into Marlon Brando the famous actor and president of the shape changers. He is wearing a bright red moo-moo and looks really fat.- Brando: Its an honor to be here. Dukestar: Help your self to the food table we have baby brains, priest nuts sautéed in dead nun vagina, an assortment of blood types. Brando: Thank…thank you….I will check that out. -Brando pats the belt as Duke and the massive man part ways. In the distance Duke spots Zicor spilling goats blood on the carpet.- Dukestar: HEY…hey…hey this is a Goth party not a pig sty hoss. Watch it you dark walking bastard we have standards in this humble Goth God lair. Goats blood is a bitch to get out of the carpet. Zicor: Sorry Dukestar. Roseanne: Come on you need to meet the president of the vamps. It’s a big deal. Dukestar: This party is a headache. Roseanne: Not as big of a headache that you left Rent-A-Loser when you nailed him in the head. -Duke smiles an evil goth smile.- Dukestar: Damn right!! I am truly a God that Akiyama ain’t get dick on me. Roseanne: I bet he would like to get his dick on you. Rumor is he’s the God of the Rainbow Goth God collation. Dukestar: The homo gods? Roseanne: Yup. Dukestar: Damn no wonder he never touches that hot piece of ass he hangs with. Roseanne: Yea that’s probably his “best friend”. -The two laugh as the shift through the party to find Regis Philbin standing in the corner.- Regis: DUKE HOW ARE YOU BUDDY!!! Dukestar: Regis thanks for coming all this way. I never thought to see you in this kind of crowd huh? Regis: I love the atmosphere its GREAT!! Dukestar: I’ll be right back with you Regg I have to meet some vampire dignitary. Roseanne: Ahh Duke he is the vampire president. Dukestar: Regis? Regis: You mean you never knew? HA HA HA HA -Regis Philbin spins around and when he turns forward he hand fangs and a black cape. Nothing else has changed his face and everything looks the same but with the fangs he looks just like a vampire.- Dukestar: Damn!! Your like super man. Regis: I do my best killer. -Regis shakes Duke’s hand.- Regis: Pardon me but I’m going to get some goats blood. Dukestar: I never knew….it was right in front of me and I never knew. Roseanne: You really need to finish up that “Idiot’s God to Goth and Vampire History”. Dukestar: I was reading today that Anna Nicole Smith is a zombie. Roseanne: Why did you think she slept with a guy about to die. Come on. Dukestar: I tell you its not easy being a God. Roseanne: I don’t pity you. GilbetTheGoblin: Hey we’re out of cheese covered whore nipples. Roseanne: So run down to Satan’s Super Saver Store and grab some. GilbertTheGoblin: OK -The midget goblin in a waiters outfit waddles off.- Dukestar: I swear you can’t find good help anymore. Roseanne: Damn right. -The party as a collective whole starts chanting for a Dukestar speech.- Party: Speech….speech….speech!! -Duke calms them down by waving his arms around as he makes his way to the head of the room.- Dukestar: You dirty pedophiles, murders, freaks, goblins, Goths, wiccans, Satan worshipers, and clergy men, thank you for humbling this great CHAMPION Goth God today!!!! -The crowd erupts in a giant cheer.- Dukestar: Unlike the Goth wrestlers of the past I’m the real deal. No bullshit from me just Malice and vulgarity!! -Another pop from the crowd.- Dukestar: Now that I have been elevated to the statutes of Goth God I control all. When you control all you win all. This piece of gold symbolizes the beginning of GOTHDOM in PCW!!!! No longer shall our kind be oppressed. We will stand up and reign supreme over all the norms out their. We have powers they don’t understand and we will use them to gain what I.….we want. I am the president of the Mortals of PCW and the Goth God of all those who walk on the dark side. -The crowd cheers.- Dukestar: I am a God and I will not drop this gold at mall brawl. I hear mortals like Kano ranting on their past….big f*cking deal. This two time continental champion don’t give a damn. The rest of the roster can bicker about which one of them is going to take up this immortal one but they all shall fail. They are wasting their valuable breath since I am their king and they don’t even know it. GOTH FOREVER!! -The crowd goes nuts as Duke drinks down some red stuff.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
