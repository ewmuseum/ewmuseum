Lexington SOCIETY (RP #2 for Fusion)

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 22, 2003 Begin Transmission: (Michael L. Chang leads his massive charge SOCIETY down the street, in Lexington, Kentucky. Heads begin turning everywhere, as the blonde haired, blue eyed piece of perfection soaks in the scenery. His training as an assassin has taught him well to look carefully at his surroundings..) “Mr. Chang,” SOCIETY inquires, “Why are we here in this city?” Chang looks over gravely at his companion before responding, “I want you to understand what you will be doing for a long, long time. You will not be at home in the Falklands. You will be here, living amongst your inferiors.” (SOCIETY sizes up the situation, looking at the denizens of the city, all with their own quirks…all falling short of that unrealistic goal of perfection.) “There are many I must destroy here,” he said, his voice showing less emotion than it would take one to swat a fly. Chang is quick to squash that idea. “No, you must pick your spots. Killing is not the answer we want. Sometimes, it is better to use an open-hand slap than a closed fist. ESPECIALLY in your dealings in Primetime Championship Wrestling. NO KILLINGS! Is that understood?!” (The tone in Chang’s voice would lead one to believe he is on the way to a SOCIETY beating. However, Michael is the big man’s confidant, the one man he trusts, and the only person on earth he would allow to speak thus. “Yes, sir. But why, may I inquire?” “Killings attract attention. Of the negative variety. Killings ARE necessary to advance the cause of the Order, but NOT in the squared circle. You will defeat your opponents, and lay them to waste, but NOT kill them. That is the type of display that inspires fear in both opponents and onlookers alike. And you know what Machiavelli says about fear.” “It is better to be feared than loved,” SOCIETY said, the maxim having been driven into his mind for years now. (Chang leads SOCIETY over to a small park, and taps him on the shoulder.) “I need to go make a report back to home. I will leave you here for about five or ten minutes. Nothing rash, understand?” (He nods slowly, as Chang walks off for the shade of a nearby tree.) “As expected, my first two opponents in Primetime Championship Wrestling are not conformists. Sivasian Krikorian… when I first read of you, I was full of promise. A military man, typically about as close as the regular world gets to conformity in today’s day and age. Yet now, you are a rogue, split from your force out of a desire to kill Americans. Although you do not appreciate the finer points of conformity, I can appreciate that, we really are not that different from one another. We are both assassins, both carefully honed to a deadly edge. The difference is, that you need your rifle to execute your judgments. I…I do not.” (SOCIETY allows himself a rare smirk, leaving it to the listener to figure out his meaning.) “And then, the Ahtumoth, some archeologist who believes he can go against the whims of SOCIETY. An Egyptian. I was already horrified enough that we have some non-conformist in PCW who believes himself to be a Pharoah, but now, I also have you to deal with. Freaks such as yourself must be taught the meaning of conformity. And, with my skills, I am more than capable of doing so.” (The gigantic man goes to sit down on a bench, but in a rare instance of being caught off guard, the bench folds under his 350 plus pounds, breaking, and depositing SOCIETY on the ground. He stays in his makeshift seat on top of the broken bench for several seconds, not knowing what to do, before he finally gets to his feet, and dusts himself off, scowling.) “That is why I can never rest on my laurels here. I may be immensely strong, and immensely powerful. I may even be perfect, but even perfection can be assaulted. On any given day, mere mortals can come close enough to be deity-like. Close enough, perhaps, even to defeat me. My guard must always remain up.” “Indeed it must.” Michael L. Chang was finished with his call back to base. “How was your conversation,” SOCIETY asked, well trained by his job for this kind of small talk. “Excellent. The Order is looking forward to your debut. For my sake, and yours, you had best not dissapoint them.” “I will not,” SOCIETY said, “You expect me to?” “No, I absolutely do not. But remember, you are the culmination of millions of dollars of research and development by the Order. They would like to see that their investment has borne fruit.” (SOCIETY analyzed the situation quickly.) “So, in other words, failure is not an option.”“Precisely,” Chang said, with conviction. “Precisely.” “In that case, you have no need to fear. The Ahtumoth and Krikorian will come face to face with the Divine Warrior of the Order of Society and Logic. And, at my hand, they will CONFORM…to SOCIETY!” (Chang nods, and leads SOCIETY out of the park, and down the city streets of Lexington, as we fade to black.)
