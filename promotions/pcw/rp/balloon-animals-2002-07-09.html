Balloon Animals

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/09/02 Kramer's first ever title defense for the Extreme Championship is just around the corner. He has the tedious task of taking on Rush Mason, the self proclaimed Voodoo Villain. Since this match was signed just last week, all Kramer can do is hope that he is up to par. Though Rush isn't the best in the business, he does possess a great deal of talent. But with the support of the fans, and KFC chicken, Kramer will prevail. At least we hope so right? The scene opens up to the loud sound of a POP!. The camera pans around and we notice that we are inside of a mall. People are walking around all over the place, wasting money on expensive gifts for themselves and family members. We hear another loud popping sound which causes the camera to spin around and focus in the direction of the noise. Standing there looking rather frustrated, wearing a white smiley face shirt, and baggy black pants, is Kramer Wild Wing! In his hand he holds a long pink balloon. He places both hands on it and starts to bend it into different shapes. He turns the end of the balloon once....twice.....POP! Kramer: Arghhhhhhh! Can you please show me again Dinko? Standing across from him is a man dressed in a clown outfit. He has the big fluffy multi-colored wig, striped outfit, and humongous shoes that all good clowns wear. He looks frustrated too as he holds his slumped head with his hand looking like he is trying to massage away a headache. Dinko: Look maybe you are just not cut out to do this. Maybe not everyone can make balloon animals sir. Kramer: But I almost had it that time. I was making a cute little poodle. Dinko: So that's what it was. ((he says sarcastically)) Kramer looks at him with a scowl on his face. Kramer picks up another balloon to the dismay of Dinko. Kramer works feverishly to produce an animal this time making a lot of high pitched squeaky noises with the balloon. The end result is a tangled up mess. Kramer stares at it from every angle possible trying to figure out what it resembles. Kramer: What do you think it is Dinko my man? Dinko: It's nothing! Now go away! Kramer: You know I came here to learn a new trade and this is the treatment I get? Dinko: A trade? Making balloon animals is much more than a trade! I didn't sit through four years of CCU for nothing. Kramer: What's CCU? Dinko: Clown College University. Kramer: You wasted four years at a Clown College? Dinko: I am appalled by your disrespect. Those were the best four years of my life I'll have you know. Because of CCU I now know how to make kids smile, and I know how to brighten people's moods. Kramer: I don't know. As a kid I was always scared of clowns. Ever since that incident with FreakNasty The Urban Space Clown…… The mention of that name results in a terrified expression on Dinko's face. Kramer: Something wrong? Dinko is speechless. Kramer: Cat got your tongue? Dinko: I ummm…..did ummm….did you say FreakNasty The Urban Space Clown? Kramer: Yes. You know him or something? Dinko: Know him? The whole clown world knows him. Kramer: What's so special about the dude? Dinko: Well think about why you didn't like him. Kramer: He was just a freaky person. I mean he had those fangs with black and white hair. Dinko: Listen....get in touch with me sometime and I will give you the full scoop on him. Dinko shudders as he hands Kramer a business card and walks away, not bothering to bring along his cart of balloons. That is a good thing for Kramer though as he takes the handles and rolls it through the mall. Kramer: I'll give it back to him.....sometime or another. Kramer laughs a bit as he continues to walk towards the exit. Before he can make it out, a group of kids crowd around the cart asking for balloon animals. Not wanting to upset the little children, Kramer tries his best to comply with thier requests. Each kid asks for a different animal and they look livid at the results. Kid: You suck at this! All in unison the kids kick Kramer in the shins as they walk away. Kramer limps a bit aching in pain. Not wanting another run in like that, he rushes outside. The scene slowly fades to black. Even if Kramer fails he will still have a sense of joy within himself. The Extreme Championship marked the third title that he has held in PCW which gives him the notion that he has accomplished a lot. He may not have the best win loss record, but he tried hard to win when it counted most. So going in to Turmoil he will try his best against Rush Mason. In return for all of his hard work, he hopes to make it out with the title, and make it through another day as a PCW champion.
