Dashing Through the Snow

10 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 10th Powdery white drops falling from the sky, collecting on the ground and piling up. As beautiful as the white dust was, it caused more harm then beauty was worth. Driving down the road and seeing cars overturned, piece of shattered glass covering the road, and bent trunks were reminders of how Mother Nature had used its beauty as a tool to beguile the people of the northeastern part of the United States into thinking that on this snowy day nothing could possibly go wrong. After all what harm could these soft white flakes that melted with your body heat possibly cause? Obviously a lot more than people estimated. And as their car slides off the road, taking the most innocent lives on this earth, it made them realize how wrong they were to underestimate those flakes. The more I thought about it the more these frozen drops of moisture reminded me of the friendship triangle between Chris, Jackson, and me. The friendship and I seemed so innocent, but quite obviously it was causing more trouble than I could have ever imagined. I couldn’t take the senseless fighting between Jackson and Chris anymore, but I was not about to let go of the friendship I had with Jackson, just because Chris did not approve of it. But at the same time I couldn’t take having Chris angry at me any longer. It had torn a hole in my heart and quite obviously Christa’s as well. I had to get back to Chris’ apartment and put patches over the wounds that were formed as a result of my close friendship with Rust. The only problem is how I would tell Chris that I wasn’t going to stop being friends with Jackson because of him. And how would I tell him that I was sorry for walking out on him and leaving Alex in his care with no signs of returning. If I had thought it those thoughts were weighing me down before, it was nothing like the feeling of a ton of bricks falling onto my shoulders at once as I pulled into his apartment driveway as Christa continued to nap in the backseat of the car. It was now or never, and I certainly would not allow never to be the choice I made, so I stepped out of the car and walked over to the other side pulling the sleeping Christa into my arms and made my way carefully through the snow that measured up to my knees, towards the entrance to the apartment Chris lived in. The TV fizzled as the screen jumped. The black and white fuzz fought on the screen. The signal from the stations was out because of the snow. I hit the set on the roof but it did no good. There was no television while the snow was still falling. I didn't care to watch it anyways. I just sat down on the couch and stared at the television. Was this what my life turned into? Just sitting on the couch watching nothing? It just seems like I just wasted all those years training and work for nothing then. Things for me were on a crash course straight down. I should just admit it to myself. 'Without Tessa, I'm nothing.' I told myself. I let the words roll off my tongue. My head fell into my hands when I said it. I didn't want to believe it but I had to. I need her back. I should crawl back to her and tell her I'm sorry. That I'm so stupid for not trusting her. My lack of trust has completely ruined our relationship. Just because I couldn't trust her and her friendship with Jackson Rust, I may have lost the most wonderful women I have ever met. I can't let it go. I need to get her back. But I made such a stupid mistake. She could never take me back. As I continued my way to the door the bottom my legs had numbed. I took another step forward stepping to quickly to find myself falling sideways into the snow, allowing Christa to tumble from my arms. THUD We both found ourselves face first inside of the snow. Christa woke up screaming as I sat there feeling a slight pain creep up my ankle. I’m sure it would hurt a lot more once my ankle thawed out and the numbness faded, but until then I wouldn’t worry. I simply reached out to Christa’s arm pulling her up out of the snow as she dusted herself looking at me wondering how she got to back to the apartment parking lot. “I thought you were mad at Chris, Tessa.” She exclaimed grasping tightly onto my hand to keep her own hand warm with the combination of heat emitting from both out bodies. “I may be upset, but I still love him to death.” I said pulling the door open to the apartment feeling a breeze of warm hit me with relief. Christa simply responded with a smile as we walked down the hall and pressed a button on the elevator causing it to reply with a beep and then the opening of its door. I was in sorry shape. I looked like a mess. My hair was a wreck, bags hung under my eyes. I was a sorry sight. I couldn't sleep. I couldn't eat. I could hardly drag myself out of the bed in the morning. Training for my matches? That was out of the question. All I had was enough energy to cook for Alex and some for myself, and keep him company. But I don't know how long it will last really. My energy is being drained out of my body. I mean I am just sitting here on the couch watching the spots on the television. Normally I would be watching the snow with Tessa, but she's gone. I couldn't help but sigh. Alex walked into the room and looked at me on the couch. I guess my depressed mood was affecting him too. I really didn't want him depressed. He was the only thing I had left. But it was still painful to look at him. He was a reminder of her. He had her hair, her eyes. Kami, I missed her badly. I should tell her I love her. That I want her back. That I can't live without her. But.....I won't. DING Like music to my ears the elevators bell rang one last time to inform me I had arrived on the floor which kept the apartment of Chris Storm. Leading Christa by the hand we walked down the dim hallway. With each foot I could feel the tension building up. Would he want me back? Or was I better off staying back home in Massachusetts. The butterflies were building in my stomach. What if he didn’t want me back? It would kill to have him say no, more than a knife to the heart. But the best I could do is gulp down my fear as I stood in front of my door and reached out banging my fist up against his apartment door. I am now sitting on the couch and do nothing. I have to do something. I should call her. Talk with her. At least hearing her voice would make me feel better. I don't know what to say to her, but I just need to hear her voice. I picked up the phone and started to dial her number. Still unsure of what I was say to her. There was a knock at the door. I looked at the door but brushed it off. It was no doubt no one important. Besides I wanted to talk to my Tessa. I needed her sweet voice falling on my ears. Who ever was at the door could wait. It may have been only a few seconds but it seemed like minutes and still no answer. I felt rejected. Maybe I should just walk in, but then the thought crossed my mind that maybe if I walked in I would come across a sight I would hate to see. The sight of Chris Storm and Ashea together again. Maybe not as a couple, but just the sight of them hanging out together and exchanging jokes made me sick knowing the type of woman she was when he wasn’t around. I waited for a few more seconds but still no answer. I simply leaned my back up against the door and slumped to the floor where I curled my legs up laid my head inside of the palms of my hands, an obvious sign of the frustration and pain I was feeling at the moment. Ring....ring.....ring....It rang and rang but no answer. Maybe she wasn't home? I hung up the phone and slumped down in a chair. I guess I just wasn't meant to hear her sweet voice. What if she wasn't home because she was out with Jackson Rust? I could just see them two out at lunch enjoying themselves. Tessa complaining about me and Jackson telling her how wrong I am for her. All the time he would be thinking about how he could 'lay' her. It made me sick. I hated to think like that. Tessa wouldn't do something like that to me. But....we have been fighting a lot lately. She even slapped me backstage when I tried to tell her I was sorry for hitting her with the steel chair. 'I need some air' I told myself as I quickly got out of the chair. I looked around for Alex to see if he wanted to go with me. I guess he must have heard my words because he was already getting his gloves and coat out of the closet. I grabbed mine and put them on quickly. I would take Alex for a walk through Central park and clear out my head. Get those thoughts of Tessa and Jackson out of my head. Even if it is just for a moment. I reached for the door and turned the knob. I flung the door open and just as I was about to step outside Tessa falls into the room and lands on her back. She must have been leaning against the door. I jumped back shocked to see her. I just looked down into her surprised eyes. 'Tessa...' I lifted my head up off the ground and looked up at Chris. He was bundled up in his jacket as was Alex who stood behind him. “What took you so long, or do I not want to know?” I said pulling myself off the ground as Christa slipped in silently behind me, pushing the door shut. Christa looked at Alex whom looked up at me and over at Chris who stood beside him. The tension could be felt from miles away. The only thing I could think in my mind was the possibility of him making plans with Ashea on the phone and then bringing Alex along with him to meet her and have Ashea take a place in his life as his adoptive mother. Well I guess that explains why she didn't answer the phone. But what was she doing here? I thought she was still mad at me. I cringed when I heard her words. Already she must have been thinking I was with another woman. I thought I had earned her trust. I guess I was wrong. 'What are you doing here? I thought you up and left. Or did you finally decide you should see Alex again?' I told her. My words were rather cold. I was getting upset from the fact that it seemed she didn't trust me. No doubt Jackson Rust pumped her up to make her believe I was sleeping with different women each night. So she finally broke down and had to come and check up on him. “What do you mean ‘What am I doing here.’? Is it wrong for me to come see someone I care for now? Or do you not want to see me any longer simply because I needed space. Does that really make me that bad of a person? Am I suddenly not the Tessa Windsor you knew and loved anymore?” I snapped, showing my obvious feelings about the question that had slipped from his mouth while wondering in my mind if suddenly he no longer he cared for me, if I had made a big mistake on walking out that cold December night. I simply stared into his cold blue eyes and the tension grew. It was a mistake and I knew it, and I would be the one who would have to pay a heavy emotional burden. Damn my bad choices, damn them to hell.
