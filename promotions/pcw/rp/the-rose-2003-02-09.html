The Rose

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-9-03 RP 1 Fusion 14 This first promo begins in a floral shop somewhere in Boise, Idaho. Less than a week away from Valentine’s day and would you know she will be defending her belt on the day set aside for couples to once again remind each other just how they feel. “Can I help you Miss Tomas?” The desk clerk asks Teresa politely. She is an older woman in her 40’s with a green apron and the name ‘Ida’ sewn in the center. Teresa silently chuckles at the irony and approaches the desk. Behind her are various selections of roses, carnations, and other flowers, both real and fake. “I am looking for one flower for a special person.” A smile crosses Ida’s face obviously pleased to sell a flower to Teresa Tomas. She’s already thinking of where to hang the picture she plans to take of the famous wrestler. “I see. How special is this person? Is he a wrestler as well?” The bell on the door rings signaling someone is entering. Teresa doesn’t look back. She leans on the counter with her elbows resting on top. The desk clerk named Ida smiles a greeting to the customer then returns her attention to Teresa. “Yeah, he’s special alright. Let’s just say we have a date in the ring come Friday. I want to get him something to remind him how special this Valentine’s day is.” This time Ida nods realizing what and whom Teresa is talking about. On Valentine’s day, Teresa will be defending her Television Title with her recent archrival, Tim Walden. The man has been a thorn in her side since the day he returned to PCW with his ridiculous taxes. Now, he’s just after her belt. Hasn’t he learned by now that his destiny doesn’t consist of winning the TV belt? One would think time after time of not winning the gold; he would give up the race. Ida places a single blood red rose on the counter. On the stem are several thorns. The desk clerk holds the rose delicately by the base of the bulb. Teresa nods in approval and picks out a green vase. “Would you like a card to go along with your rose?” Teresa thinks for a moment then grins with an idea in her head. “Yes, a card will be perfect.” This scene fades and returns with the rose resting on Tim Walden’s desk with a tall card leaning against the vase waiting for the Taxman to read it. The card reads as follows. Dearest Tim, This rose was chosen special just for you. It is a reminder of how special Friday is. You know, Valentine’s day. Friday is also our date in the square circle. Please be a gentleman and don’t be late. As a reminder, I have given you this rose. The color of the rose does not only symbolize love, but the blood you will shed, blood red. The pedals are delicate to the touch; such as will your body when I am done. As you look at the beautiful rose, you want to touch it. You want to take it out of the vase, but wait. There are thorns; such as you are a thorn in my side. Here is what’s so special about this rose Timmy. It represents the TV belt. MY belt. You want to hold the belt but you can’t. You want to hold the rose, but you can’t. There are thorns on the stem and I am your thorn. In order to hold the stem, you must cut off the thorns. Can you find your scissors? Love always to hate forever, Teresa Tomas
