A Few Good Bisexuals

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th April An intriguing week in the life of El Diablo to say the least. A devastating brawl with Flaymin Aymin, finding his wife in a sexual position with another woman, and rescuing the Taco Bell Chihuahua, and being made spokesperson for the great Taco company. Put all this aside, and there is Spring Fling 2002, a hair vs mask match against Flaymin Aymin, and with all these personal problems that both men are living through it should be a great match. All this brings us to now. El Diablo and Angel are seated in an hotel in Anaheim, California, the location of Spring Fling. Their hotel room is small, two rooms, one being the main room which stores a bed, a chair, a television and a chest of drawers, the other being the bathroom with only a toilet and sink in it. Not very upper class for El Diablo, but that is the way he likes it. Angel wears a long black wig to hide her bald head after what Aymin did to her. She has on all of her usual cosmetics and wears a black bathrobe obviously prepared to go to bed and sleep on some things, and no I don't mean Jenna Jameson like her exploits on Fusion showed. Angel is lying down on the bed but her eyes are wide open rather than shutting them to get some shuteye. El Diablo sits opposite her on the rather uncomfortable and unpadded chair. He wears; maybe for the last time his precious mask, his long dark hair flows through the roof of the mask and down his back. Unlike Angel he is unprepared to go to sleep, as he still wears a black t-shirt and some long black shorts, but no shoes, this indicating he plans on going nowhere. In his hands is a book, a book that reads '101 ways to tell if you're bisexual' You can definitely tell where this promo is going. 'No, I don't prefer pussy than a good cock,' responds Angel to one of the books questions. El Diablo stares across at her. 'So you're saying I don't?' retorts Diablo looking extremely unhappy. Angel sighs. 'I didn't say that,' she says angrily. 'It was insinuated,' replies Diablo promptly. Angel sighs again. 'Next question,' she says trying to avoid the last one. Diablo shakes his head, 'Fine,' He reads a bit more of the book as Angel remains lying down with a look of disgust on her face at being interrogated by her own husband. El Diablo suddenly smiles sinisterly and nods his head. 'On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate me in bed?' asks Diablo with an evil smile. Angel looks at her husband in horror. 'You really want me to answer that?' she replies. This unnerves El Diablo who simply nods. 'Lets just say, more than your penis size,' she responds. El Diablo grins almost as large as the Grinch. 'I said 1-10 not more than ten,' grins El Diablo. Angel shakes her head. 'Come on honey, we both know that isn't true,' replies Angel, almost completely breaking El Diablo's heart. He looks at her in disgust and then looks down at his crotch. 'I'll pretend you never said that,' he yells at her. She remains on the bed lying down unphased by what her husband has to say. El Diablo reads more of the book and then looks up at his wife. 'Who's a better lover, myself, Flaymin Aymin or Jenna?' asks Diablo inquisitively. Angel immediately looks up at him. 'The book doesn't say that,' is her response in a stunned voice. 'Answer the question,' presses El Diablo. Angel leaps up and heads towards the seated El Diablo, she starts trying to take the book. 'Let me see that passage,' responds Angel, while violently trying to take the book from her husband. El Diablo proceeds to sit on the book, he then stands up and pushes Angel onto the bed and then peers over her. Angel is now very frightened. She struggles to say a word, 'W-w-what do you want from me?' Diablo smirks. 'Who is the better lover?' he asks once again to his frightened wife. Angel in fear still avoids the question. 'I-I don't know,' she replies, 'What do you want?' questions Angel. El Diablo quits peering over her and retreats back a little but remains standing. 'I WANT TO KNOW THE TRUTH,' he yells at her in a violent verbal attack. Angel sits up, no longer in fear. 'YOU CAN'T HANDLE THE TRUTH,' she screams back at him. A few moments of silence pass, as the tension in the room is tight. El Diablo then suddenly has a realisation. 'Oh, I see what it is,' he says to her. She looks utterly puzzled. 'Now I know,' Diablo says again. Angel stares at her husband in confusion. 'Know what, what is?' she asks still confused. 'The plot, Angel,' he says in anger to his wife, 'The plot,' Angel then stands up. 'What plot?' asks Angel still in the dark. El Diablo smirks and turns his back on her. 'I knew you'd deny it,' he says, 'Aymin's plot of trying to turn you bisexual,' states El Diablo to his wife. Angel stands there in shock. 'WHAT?' she screams? 'He's trying to convert you into his and Jenna's sex slave,' he says to Angel's utter shock, 'It's all so obvious now,' finishes the Mexican Devil. A thud is audible as Angel drops onto the bed in shock and utter disgust that her husband would think that. 'He's been successful too,' he says in his typical Mexican accent, 'I'LL KILL HIM,' shouts Diablo at the top of his lungs. He marches into the bathroom and slams the door shut behind him. Angel remains in shock for a few moments before getting up to her feet and walking over to the chair. She picks up the book and starts flicking through it. 'Now where does it say that question,' she says to herself.
