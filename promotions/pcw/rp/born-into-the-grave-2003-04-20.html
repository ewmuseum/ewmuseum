Born into the Grave

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/20/03 'Sadness.' 'I am actually sad.' 'Though mildly I assure you, know that remorse rests in my heart.' 'For what I must do.' Somewhere in the midst of an all consuming darkness, the familiar voice of a demon echoes. His voice is almost plaintive. The voice of one deep in thought. Though also the voice of one resigned to his fate. The cursed voice of one with that oh so terrible burden. The burden of precognition. 'I know where our future lies, Gavyn.' 'Though I need not say.' 'Our destinies, layed before us not by us, but by the useless and blind hand of others.' 'The darkness that consumes me is but a shadow compared to that blindness.' A chuckle from the monster, though throughly devoid of humor. Sadness. In his own, terrible way. 'Yes, I laugh at the very thought of it.' 'For what purpose are we here?' 'Do we have a purpose?' Anger. Though again, mild. Dulled by the tinge of resignation. 'I will tell you that much.' 'We serve no purpose.' 'We exist to entertain those blind, unknowing cretins with our eternal dance of death.' A brief thud. The sound of a fist hitting flesh. Frustration. 'Why do we continue?' 'Why do we keep entertaining them?' 'They ask us now to end this charade.' 'But I ask, what lies ahead for us once it is completed?' 'When either you are gone, or I am gone.' 'Will our services even be required?' Another slight laugh from the all consuming blackness. 'I, of course, know the answer.' 'One of my many curses, is that I know the answer to all.' 'But, I may not change the course of fate.' 'Even to one with my....abilities.' 'It would be as attempting to block a raging river with a pebble.' Contempt. 'And so, we contine on and on and on.' 'We are not needed.' 'We are discarded.' 'In an age where the petty gods of destiny have grown senile and afraid.' 'They are afraid of us, Gavyn.' A deep sigh. 'Truth.' 'Do you wish to know the true reason we are pitted against one another?' 'It is because, left unchecked, beings such as us could never be stopped.' 'We have no equals, Darkstar.' 'And that is our true curse.' Somewhere in the distance, a crack of thunder. 'We are the pinnacle.' 'There are none greater than us.' 'And, the petty gods could not bear to allow us to roam free.' 'Yet, we cannot die.' 'You know this as well as I do. For we have killed each other before.' Another laugh. Irony. 'So what is left to do with us, if we may not perish?' 'Why, they pit us against each other for eternity, of course.' The disgust rings clear in the demon's words. 'And when our rage cannot contain the evil therein, they come up with creative new suggestions.' 'Exile.' Another sigh. 'But what exhile? You know as well as I, the only 'exile' for us is destruction.' 'A destruction from which there is no return.' A brief moment of silence, broken only by another clap of thunder as a soft rain begins to pour down. 'Wonder if I can do it?' 'Wonder if you can do it?' 'Wonder if we are capable of taking one another to a place where even monsters such as us may not return? 'I assure you, Gavyn.' 'We are.' Resignation. 'And so, I leave you with these words.' 'Whether I finish you, or you finish me.' 'Ask yourself this, in your heart of hearts.' 'Is there any escape from this?' 'Is there any escape from the cruel hand of fate?' 'That hand which guides us from the moment of our births?' A bolt of lighting finally illuminates the room. In that brief moment, we behold the spector of Clyde. He sits, coated head to toe in blood with what appears to be a small babe in his lap. A weak cry escapes it's lips, as the demon raises his blade. An expression of pity crosses his face, but it is twisted. Twisted, deformed by pure and hopeless insanity. 'No, Gavyn.' 'We are born into the grave.' The lightning fades as quickly as it came, as the terrible sound of steel entering flesh rips forth, silencing a small voice eternally as we mercifully fade to black......
