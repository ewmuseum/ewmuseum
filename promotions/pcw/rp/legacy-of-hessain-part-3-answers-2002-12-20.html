Legacy of Hessain: Part 3 - Answers

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/12/02 Recap: After being reborn as a vampire to take revenge on his Murderers, Hessain has been told that they were merely hired hands. To complete his retribution, Hessain has been guided to the mighty Pillars Of Nosgoth for answers. * * * Legacy Of Hessain: Part 3 - Answers I approached the pillars from the south. The Pillars Of Nosgoth were 9 ivory white structures that appeared to stretch into heaven itself. Each one was supposed to represent the life of the land... and indeed, they were in ill health if the vermin I'd met were to walk freely. The pillars themselves exuded an awe, with their origins in mystery and their purpose in question. But Morgazius' words were an enigma, as was the greeting I received. Fading in and out of existence was an apparition, with hair as red as mine and a most peculiar trait - as she turned and weaved throughout the marvelous structures, she bore two sides to her... As she showed her left side, she was a maiden with an eye of warm brown, clad in a pure white gown and gloves. The only thing missing was a crown that would have signified her proof as one of Willendorf's high princesses, beautiful and glorious. On her other side as she traversed the Pillars, she almost literally transformed. Dressed in the attire of the ladies of the night, her baby-blue eye did nothing to try and exert any innocence, as her clothing was barely suitable for swimming in. Indeed, she was both a lady of a palace and a brothel in one contradicting body. Spectral as she was, she had not seen my coming yet. Instead, she merely floated as though bound to the Pillars themselves, muttering to herself. As I drew closer, I listened to her words... 'Skitzrapto, your madness has shattered our dreams and blinded you...' she wept from her sexually explicit side. She glided towards me, causing me to draw my blade in an instant. 'Keep your distance, or I'll send you to the hell they call New Minas, spirit!' I yelled. She shook her head and backed away. 'There is nothing left of me to fear, vampire. I am only a shadow of my former self. Aimziel, the balance. I represent the JD and coke, the Vodka and Rezz Bull, the Pink and the Eminem.' 'And the virgin and the whore?' I questioned. 'Nay, nobleman, else my left side would be Byron.' she replied. 'But I can divulge the answers you desire.' 'I desire no more than a cure for this dying body!' I commanded the wraith. The look in both her eyes led me to fear her next words. 'There is no cure for a condition of death, only the possibility of being redeemed. Will you accept this offer?' Her eyes and soothing words seduced me. I nodded slowly, yearning for the release from this state. The Pillar of Balance glowed in front of me. At the time, I knew not why. 'Then you must first complete a task. Go forth and cleanse Nosgoth of the scourge that now resides in it like a parasite. These Pillars are the representations of the magical circle of nine that has been sworn to protect Nosgoth since before anyone can recall. But a traitor within this circle has poisoned it. My murder at his hands has sent Skitzrapto into a frenzy, spreading a wave of psychic and mental destruction throughout the circle. Now, each of the circle has his or her own designs on Nosgoth, spurred on by a depression implanted into their heads. If his shouts of corruption are not silenced, this land shall perish...' the specter cried. 'BAH! I care not for this land. When my wrongs have been righted, I shall ascend to above!' I roared. Such arrogance from the phantom... 'Then you shall fester along with Nosgoth. Beware, Hessain... beware the UnsPOEken...' she whispered. And with that, she unraveled into the night air like the ghost she was. I took a second to glance at the Pillars, then felt unexplainably drawn to them. The Pillar of Balance, Aimziel's charge, seemed to be decaying slowly with chips and cracks forming all the time. I then examined the others, wondering at their height and purpose. On the far left was the Pillar of the Mind. As I ran a gauntlet down the gigantic cylinder, my mind surged with thoughts of madness and depression. I quickly yanked my palm from it's corrupting surface. I was twisted enough as it is. Pressing on, next was the Pillar of Conflict. With a name such as that, I dare not touch it. Located on it was a crest, a red ruby. I recognised it as being symbolic of Magnumek, the protector of the circle of nine. Then I realized the horrible truth. To end my curse of vampirism and as a side effect, restore balance to Nosgoth, I would have to heal each of these pillars by killing the member of the circle of nine that was linked to it. Although the Pillar of Balance was glowing, yet still looking as though it were on the verge of death. Alive, yet much like me, undead. And much like Nosgoth, so close to cataclysm yet still hanging by a thread. But alas, my musings would have to wait. My mission, as it were, lay east. The Fortress of the Mind as Morgazius had spoken of was in fact the mountain retreat of the Mentalist. It would appear I had an unarranged audience with Skitzrapto. If indeed his mind was the cause of Nosgoth's decay, then it was my duty to separate it from his body...
