Through The Eyes Of Harry - Part 4 - Later, Mom

21 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 21, 2003 [MILF. Mom I'd LIke TO..well, the idea is there. Tommy and Harry's Mom qualifies as a perfect MILF, espeically since she's extremely single and EXTREMELY rich. Tommy gave her some extra cash so she could start her image consultant service and it just took off from there. The elite of entertainers come to her for advice on how to capture an image or to change their image due to totally bogus scandals. The beautiful woman handles a lot of things, but manages to ALWAYS keep her cool under every situation. It's one thing that Harry inherited from his mother, but unfortunately Tommy did not. She stands at an intimidating six feet tall and the sexy, black high heeled boots she wears only add to her height. Her tanned skin without a blemish and with little make up, because she DOESN'T need make up for her beauty. She's slender, but not like a stick figure. She has just enough meat on her bones. Her bright, fully white smile melts hearts...something Tommy inherited from his mother, and unforunately, Harry did not. Today she isn't smiling, not one bit. Her stern face, still looking sexy, pierces though her sons like a hot knife. The Tubular Tiki, Tommy's bodacious coffee house chain, is going at full force late in the morning. Workers dressed in Tiki outfits scurry in the background to get the people their coffee or gnarly specialty drinks that Tommy 'apparently' came up with himself. She sips her coffee and doesn't even need to say a word as to why she suddenly showed up all mad. Harry, in his sober state, knows EXACTLY what's wrong. As he looks at Tommy, who doesn't seem to be too worried, Harry's worry only deepens] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Mom, I understand why your upset but... Mom: Shhh! [She won't have any of it from Harry. Her anger is more projected toward Tommy, who is the man mostly behind all of the heinous things that have happened. She is mad at Harry too, but just because he never really tried to stop Tommy. If she were smoking bowl after bowl she wouldn't be able to do much either!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Mom, your being most bogus today. Is Enrique that Mexican florist dude not up to par? Harry: Come on, Tommy, that's just gross. Mom: Harry! [Harry puts his arms up in front of him to signal that he'll be quiet. It's at that moment that Tara, a Tubular Tiki employee, brings Harry a 'Gnarly Green Tea'. Harry just looks at it with confusion, knowing full well that he never ordered anything. He had a bunch of Capri Sun before he left the house] Tara: Your daily drink, sir. Have a good one, Harry. [Tara looks amazingly BEAUTIFUL! How could Harry never notice her before?! Her long black hair, pale skin, eyebrow piercing, and the voluptous body. My God! She's a total babe and he's just had her bring him a Gnarly Green Tea?! What in the name of Judas Priest is wrong with him?! Sheesh...] Mom: Your brother is right...that's gross. Enrique will come later... TTT: Is that a fact? [Tommy has a sick smile on his face, looking to Harry for a confirmation of his attemtped joke. Harry, being the sober man that he is, just looks at his brother as if he has boogers flowing out of his nose] Mom: Ugh, your such a pervert. I just wonder where you get it from. TTT: Televison. Mom: Sure. That's what they all say. TTT: That's what you said to me when I asked why there were three dudes in your bed. Mom: That's different. TTT: How is that different then what I do?! Mom: I'm allowed. TTT: Because your a woman?! Mom: No, because I'm your mother and your my son...that's why! [Tommy looks to Harry with that 'Help Me' look. Harry just shrugs knowing full well that he can't say anything to object that. What mom says...it goes!] Mom: What is this crap about you cutting funds on The Tubular Tykes Foundation?! I don't really watch the news or read newspapers but they sent my check back and informed me! The letter was VERY nasty to tell you the truth. If I wasn't so PISSED at you I would have taken offense to some of the things they said about you. [Tommy is immediately angered] TTT: What did they say?! I'll have them beaten with surfboards immediately. Harry, get the boys ready! Harry: We don't have boys. TTT: Yes we do. They are in Venice Beach, training in surfboard combat. Harry: Your retarded. TTT: Only on the weekends. Harry: Fuzzy Wuzzy Was A Bear. TTT: Eat poo. Mom: ENOUGH! [They immediately stop their bickering] Mom: How could something like this happen? Why would you just stop a wonderful foundation like that? Wait...you don't even have to answer that. I think I know what is happening here...it's this NEW ATTITUDE that your throwing around. You think that since your wearing a gold belt that you have to prove that your some kind of man with great power. Is that it? TTT: No...I'm a super villain. [Tommy's mom is just bewildered as she freeze in mid stride to take a sip of her coffee. It's as if she can't believe what she has just heard from her son. Harry can see it in her blue eyes, the fire burning behind them. It was only months ago that the two of them were able to patch things up and put the odd past they had with each other behind them. Now, with Tommy's decisions, it's sort of resurfaced again. She's going to scream at him, at his place of business to be exact, and she's going to try her best to make him feel like he's an eight year old child again. She always did that, even to Harry. She would always take them back to childhood and remind them that they are just paranoid children. She only did it to cover up her sexual encounters with men, but she'll use it for other things. Especially this situation] Mom: A super villain? TTT: Totally. I rob from the poor and give to...me. It's quite bodacious. Mom: Thomas Gordo! I may be very free spirited and casual but I cannot condone such actions! You've destroyed something so special like Tubular Tykes so you can thicken your pockets?! Don't you find that a bit absurd?! TTT: Mom, your using bogusly large words. It's hurting my head. [Harry just shakes his head at his brother who is totally oblivious to the fact that he's about to get disowned] Mom: Tommy, you are a self centered, greedy son of a bitch. Your NOTHING like your father, and the way that your going now, you'll NEVER be like him. You can win all the titles you want and beat all the wrestlers you can, but you will never be at your fathers level...especiallyl not the way that your doing it. Harry: Tommy, that's what I've... Mom: Harry! Shut up! If you weren't so god damned STONED all the time then you would be able to step in and stop some of this bullshit! You could have stopped him from ending Tubular Tykes! I guess if I were a pothead who just sat on his fat ass I'd probably just go with the flow JUST LIKE YOU! Harry: Mom... Mom: Don't do it...just let it go. I don't want to hear it, not anymore. [She grabs the coffee and gets up from the table without taking a second look back. Harry watches her walk away, through the line of people, and disappearing for good. He can't believe the look on her face, the way it contorted in such anger. She really meant it and she is going to stand beside her decision. It's sad. It really is sad. It only makes Harry come to the ultimate conclusion that he IS a loser. That he has wasted away his life by smoking pot and masterbating. Is that really a way to live a life? To go around and do absolutely nothing with it? He's lost and so is his brother. He looks confused, what else is new. It probably won't even sink in that she is disowning him either. He's just going to think she's upset and that he'll buy her some diamonds. She'll come right back over and do nothing but beg for forgiveness. Or he'll just feel that she owes him for the money that he gave her so she could start her business. That is enough to warrant forgiveness all the time, according to Tommy] TTT: That was TOTALLY BOGUS! Lets go to the park and do some jogging. Harry: Sure... [Fade to black]
