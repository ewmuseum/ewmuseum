Rent-A-Hero & His Adventures In...MACK-O-LOGY?!

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Rent-A-Hero & His Adventures In...MACK-O-LOGY?! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 2-27-2003 ..::/::..Our story begins within the bachelor pad apartment of one Rent-A-Hero, obvious by the strewn EGM, PSM, and Superman comics all over the floor. The big screen Sony Vaio TV is blasting with color and surround sound as “Vampire Hunter D” is played in all it’s anime glory across the 52 inch screen…and laying on the black leather couch across the room, in nothing but his Underdog boxers and a Green Lantern mask and covered by a Care Bears blanket…is PCW’s only superhero and defender of all that is right, Rent-A-Hero. He looks to be content and happy in his slumber, a small dribble of drool running down a closed grin of our hero as he lays in the comfort of his own home..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Yes….zzzzzzz....yes Superman, I’ll gladly take your cape….zzzzzzz…..” !~RING~! !~RING~! !~RING~!..::/::..Our hero leaps from the comfort of his couch, in groggy alert as he looks about in all directions while throwing fists everywhere, hitting only air as he exclaims in a panic..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”NOT AGAIN….YOU CYBER NINJA’S SHALL NOT REBUKE ME….FEEL MY WRATH!!!” ..::/::..The phone rings yet again, snapping out of his daze. His eyes immediately shoot to the Get Smart fashioned shoe-phone sitting idly on the glass nightstand near the couch. Wiping his eyes, he grips the phone and slides the sole to the side, taking in some air before answering..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Hello…this is the residence of one Rent-A-Hero…may I ask who’s calling? What? FOR REALS?! SUPER SAIYANS OF SMASHERING….I GOT A SHOT AT THE CRUSIERWEIGHT TITLE THIS FRIDAY ON FUSION?! AGAINST EL ROMANTICO?! HOLY GOBS OF GOLDAGE….thanks for the heads up. Talk to you later.”..::/::..RAH hangs up the phone, his face filled with a burst of exuberance as he clenches his fists…thinking of the gold he has a strong chance of putting back around his waist for the second time. Snatching up his remote from the yin-yang glass table and cutting his TV off with one touch of a button, RAH says in his broad, heroic voice..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”THE SLEEPING HERO HAS RESTED LONG ENOUGH!! I’ve sat idly by for too long and watched my peers and younger talent pass me by….and I’ve let myself become a mere shell of myself in PCW. No longer can this happen. I MUST slay the love beast that is El Romantico, and take the PCW Cruiserweight title back into my possession for a second time. With that blow struck, I will have the momentum I need to show the PCW that JUSTICE is back in town….and it isn’t on vacation anytime soon! Then….I shall draw power from the Cruiserweight title and add it to my Kryptonian endoskeleton, using it to topple that brash and cocky Totally Tubular Tommy! But first…I have to study my wily opponent for battle. For me to best El Romantico in combat…..I MUST BECOME HIM!! TO THE PENTHOUSE!!” ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero strikes a pose before dashing out the door to the outside….obviously forgetting the need for CLOTHES when traversing into the outside world. He is promptly reminded as a random tenant yells from off camera..::/::.. Random Tennant:”HAHA…all those “SUPERPOWERS” don’t make up for the fact that you got a VIENNA SAUSAGE in your drawers!” ..::/::..Promptly, the apartment door opens yet again, Rent-A-Hero bursting through and slamming it shut behind him with a reddened face of embarrassment. As he makes his way to the bedroom, Rent-A-Hero says to the audience with a “hint” of embarrassment..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Mental note….after long-winded monologues…make sure you have a suit on.” Later On…. ..::/::..We now find ourselves on the VERY top floor of the apartment complex inhabited by Rent-A-Hero. Now adorned in a full body Flash costume, our hero takes in a deep breath before reluctantly knocking on a cherry oak door labeled “69” in gold numbers. Our hero’s wait is short, as the door opens, revealing a BEAUTIFUL caramel tone woman answering the door in a crimson red lingerie set, hugging her body just right as her red silk robe sits on her body untied. Our hero is lost for words at the moment as she asks..::/::.. Woman:”You’re here for something?” Rent-A-Hero:”Well…um…well…see…I….yea….umm….err……I….umm…..Darrel…yea.” Woman:”Oh, you’re here to see Darrel…come in.” ..::/::..She opens the door wide, standing aside for our hero to enter as he slowly steps in. He is mesmerized by the sexual atmosphere of Darrel’s penthouse apartment, soft R&B music playing and fruit incense filling the room as he looks about, his apartment a STOREHOUSE of beautiful women walking about in fine Victoria’s Secret lingerie..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero: *Looking up* ”Well whatever the secret is Mr.Narrator man….it’s out.”..::/::..Still caught up in the atmosphere, the caramel woman in red lingerie makes her way to the back hallway and disappears to the left. Shortly, she returns with a handsome man of ebony skin, a black silk robe covering his body as he rubs his hands, approaching Rent-A-Hero with a smile on his face..::/::.. Darrel:”Yo Rent, how ya livin?” Rent-A-Hero:”Good my friend. You have a plethora of beauties in your base of operations? Must be your costume!” ..::/::..Darrel laughs, turning to the woman who answered the door and whispering something in her ear. His soft words make her giggle slightly as she heads to the back, Darrel motioning Rent-A-Hero over to a red velvet couch. The two take a seat, Darrel picking up a wine glass from his nightstand as one of his beauties promptly fills his glass with wine. Taking a sip, he asks..::/::.. Darrel:”So what brings you by? Pointers? I already told you Rent…just keep the costume bottom on when your hitting it, and you’ve got the BEST protection since Trojan Spermicide my boy.” Rent-A-Hero:”NO NO NO DARREL!! You are sadly mistaken my friend…see…I have a big Cruiserweight Title match this Friday and my opponent is El Romantico. He’s something of a ladies man and well…to beat him…I need to know his ways. So I was hoping…maybe…you can teach me your tricks?” ..::/::..Darrel raise up from the couch, sipping his wine while pondering the request of Rent-A-Hero. He snaps his fingers, and a beautiful Asian woman emerges from the hall to take his glass away. With yet another snap, she returns, rubbing his shoulders firmly as he stands in thought. He eyes Rent-A-Hero in all his spandex glory before finally saying..::/::.. Darrel:”Man I don’t know….it takes a special kind of man to be a mack. Someone smooth…with charisma…with showmanship. If I teach you, you gotta leave all that “HOLY VIRGINITY” crap alone. You have to ditch the spandex for silk, the monologues for macking…and the Playstation controls for fine ass ho’s….can you do that?” ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero gives it some serious thought….pondering if he can really leave behind what he knows to delve into a world he doesn’t. After a few seconds, our hero jumps to his feet and exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”IF IT MEANS GETTING THE CRUSIERWEIGHT TITLE….HOLY PLAYERS OF PLUTO DARREL…I shall become a…A MACK!!”Darrel:”Damn, I got some work to do…it’s hard enough that you’re a white boy…but we REALLY gotta get you past this comic book crap.” Rent-A-Hero:”HEY, graphical literature is essential in the life of a mack…isn’t it?” Darrel:”Only if it starts with a “P” and ends with “layboy” ..::/::..With that, the scene fades with our hero preparing to delve into the world of “mack-o-logy”.
