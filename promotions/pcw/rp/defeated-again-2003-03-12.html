Defeated Again

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/12/03 A blackened sky illuminated by several burning balls of gas. Staring at this sky I sat beside the man who would soon be my husband, Chris Storm. Chris kept me wrapped up in his arms as we stared through the large transparent window inside our bedroom, admiring the beauty of the nighttime sky. “It’s so beautiful, isn’t it Chris?” I asked as I rested my head up against his well built chest. Just being in his arms made me felt that no matter the odds I would always be safe with him around. The only problem was that on Friday I would not be allowed to have him by my side to assure my security provided Gaz and Nerezza had a plan ready to put into action even if Nerezza himself was not allowed at ringside as well, but that was still another three days before I had to worry about, so why not enjoy the moment with the person I loved the most on this earth, my fiancé, Chris Storm. “Of course Tessa, but you are even more beautiful.” He smiled as he ran his hand through my light brown hair before providing even more comfort for me with a simple kiss on the cheek. “You are the…” “CHRIIISSSSSSSS! TESSAAAAA” Cried the voice of four year old Evan from down the hall, interrupting my sentence before I could finish it. “Want me to handle it?” Chris questioned as he slowly lifted me off of him, placing me on my side of the bed. “Sure.” I answered as I crawled under the white cotton sheets making myself comfortable in a matter of seconds with my head resting on the pillow and my eyes partially closed. “Ok, I’ll be back soon Tessa bear.” He said as he pulled himself off the bed and headed off down the hall, closing the bedroom door behind him. As the footsteps traveled further away from the door I allowed my nosy ears to eavesdrop in on what was being said down in Evan’s bedroom out of curiosity. “Chris I can’t sleep. I think there is a monster or something outside the window.” Young Evan cried out of fear as he pointed towards the window towards the tree’s that became still as the wind stopped blowing. Chris did his best to comfort young Evan by answering, “Don’t worry it’s just the breeze causing the branches to move, just like the wind makes your hair move when you are out there.” Realizing that it was just a typical four year old fear of the dark I turned my ears off and closed my eyes, burying my head deep into the pillow. Sure I probably should go and help Chris try to comfort Evan, but if Chris did not want yet another struggle to pull me out of bed in the morning than he would understand that I needed to stay in bed and get some sleep. Just as I was about to fall asleep the door creaked open once again. “I got him to sleep.” The tall figure that walked in through the door said as it walked over to bed and crawled under the sheets, making itself comfortable beside me. “Thanks Chris.” I answered as I rewarded him with a kiss on the cheek. “A kiss on the cheek? I think I deserve a little more than that.” He said as he wrapped his arm around me pulling me closer to him so that my chest was pressing up against his as his hands found their way under my shirt and now ran up and down my back. “Tough luck.” I teased in a response filled with giggles. My giggles were interrupted though by the opening of the door once again. Looking down I expected to see Evan but instead the person I saw was much taller than the four year old boy. As my eyes traveled further up I realized the person was Chris, but that would mean… “KAMI, WHAT IS GOING ON HERE?” Chris shouted from the doorway looking down at bed in which me and some other guy were wrapped up in sheets cuddled close to each other. “Chris how could you be there if you are right…” I cut off my own sentence as I realized just who the man beside me was. It had been the same man who had been taunting Chris and me for nearly a week now, and the man who could easily be mistaken for Chris. “Ein” Growled Chris from the doorway as he stomped towards the bed, his eyes glazed over in anger, whether it was at me or Ein I wasn’t sure, all I knew is that one of the two of us was going to pay even if I had no clue that I was with the enemy. “Heh, admit it Chris. I’ve defeated you once again. When will you finally realize that I’m the better of us two, even your precious little Tessa can’t resist me.” Ein taunted as he climbed out of the bed with a smirk and casually walked by Chris, leaving the room without a fight. Looking over at Chris I realized that his emotions had overtaken him once again as the only thing he could do was clench his fist while looking at Ein walking down the hall without a worry in his mind, knowing Chris wouldn’t dare to attack him. “I’m sorry; I didn’t know it was him I swear.” I answered in a soft and almost inaudible voice not knowing whether Chris would even forgive me for what has just occurred right in front of his own two eyes. Instead of answering Chris looked to the ground with tearful eyes. I realized that Ein was right. He had defeated both Chris and I with a simple mind game the moment we were both on our own. It made me wonder, could P.W.A possibly defeat just as easily with mind games of their own? I didn’t dare to think about it any longer I simply looked up at the emotionally charged Chris who looked ready to cry and began to cry tears for both him and I.
