Hypothesizing Kid Krazie

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/10/02 Munchausen's By-Proxy. This is a disease, a mental condition in which a mother purposely makes their child sick, gives them too much or too little medication, and forces the child to go through unnecessary surgical procedures. Such an experience is devestatingly traumatic to the child that learns the truth. So much so that some of them block out their memories of the past. So much so that some may even lose their sanity. The following is a hypothesis dealing with the current mental condition of one PCW wrestler and former Rising Star Champion, Kid Krazie. Kid Krazie has no memory about his past. His first memories are of him meeting his Food Lion shopping cart (it could at least be Piggly Wiggly, but noooo) and falling in love with it. Such a history is obviously an attempt on the part of the subconscious mind to cover up whatever bad experiences Kid Krazie may have dealt with in the past. But such a bad story? Yeesh. Kid Krazie has no other name. That's all he's really known as. Yet, despite the fact that his memory might reach back all of a few months, he apparently knows that he's from Houston, Texas, and is almost twenty-two years old. While one could speculate that he merely looked at his license and discovered this information. True, Kid Krazie could have taken up residence in Houston after his memory loss. Perhaps, but to them, I pose this question. If Kid Krazie could find out his hometown and birthdate from his license, why would it not divulge his real name? In the Houston listings, indeed in the entire state of Texas, there is no listing of anyone named Kid Krazie, so this being his legal name is not an option. One can only assume that Kid Krazie merely made up this information, and that he may not even be as old as he says he is. Kid Krazie's account of his memory loss is as follows, attained from his file biography that he provided to PCW. 'Kid Krazie while walking out from his neighborhood Randall's grocery store slips in a puddle causing him to crash down to the concrete ground. Upon waking from his state of unconciousness it is noticed he has experienced some head trauma. 'As he walks from Randall's that fatefull afternoon with amnesia he sees a dented shopping cart sitting next to Randall's dumpster. He begins talking with this shopping cart as he finds out she is 28 years old and her name is Lu-Lu. She is originally from Kroger Island and she stands approximately 3 feet tall weighing 11 lbs. of soaking wet steel. 'They walk out of Randall's parking lot together that fateful day man and cart together at last. 'His past is clouded in confusion after the fall he took. Maybe some day his memory will be back but until then don't look at his past look at the present and maybe even a little of his future!' This is hardly believable. His subconcious mind, however lacking in imagination, has created this myth for Kid Krazie to live his life by. Look at his story a little more closely. 1) Kid Krazie slips in a puddle causing him to crash down to the concrete ground. Now if this is actually true, this would be quite embarassing for Kid Krazie. To think, a man who regularly springs off of 1-inch thick steel cables, to perform acrobatic feats, slipping in a puddle of water while simply walking out of a store. Laughable. 2) Kid Krazie wakes up, and it is noticed that he has suffered head trauma. Head trauma, was it? I believe that Kid Krazie's mother damaged his mind by afflicting him with several childhood illnesses, leaving him bedridden. Think about it, Kid Krazie is not very muscular, and must rely on a low-impact high-flying offense. I would suggest that pre-adolescent immuno-deficiencies are responsible for Kid Krazie's relative lack of strength. His head trauma is not physical, but mental. While it is no fault of his own for whatI believe he has suffered, his story could be better. 3) Kid Krazie meets and courts a shopping cart by a dumpster. To think that a human would so forget that interlocution with inanimate objects is not possible, yet maintain his speech abilities, is hardly believable. Add to that the fact that if this part of the story were true, a possibility that I reject, it means that Kid Krazie is inconsiderate and selfish. Think about it. A shopping cart from all the way out on Kroger Island, sitting next to a dumpster out back of a grocery store? Kid Krazie, you daft twit, that was a man's home! Lu-Lu isn't your girlfriend, it's a homeless man's version of a mobile home. I can see that. Some poor man leaves his shopping cart out near a dumpster while he scroungs around inside for scraps of food, and some crazy guy comes and steals his cart. AND IT'S A FOOD LION CART! Now, I said that I believed that Kid Krazie's mother suffered from Munchausen's By-Proxy. I am not without my own evidence, and in this case, it's all physical. I've interviewed many people about Kid Krazie, and this is what they agree on. A) Pallor complexion on otherwise tanned body. Considering that the face is exposed to the tanning rays of UV light more than his body is, only a lingering health condition could cause such an anomaly in skin tone. B) Lack of muscular physique. Now, I understand that we can't all be muscular, but Kid Krazie has one of the smallest muscle masses in PCW. He relies on a highi-flying offense since he's so small. But really, Kid Krazie is 215 pounds, but not much of it seems to be from muscle. I would suggest that Kid Krazie has a binging eating disorder, in which he ingests large amounts of food to calm himself during emotional fits. Who knows, he may need to gorge himself all the time in order to feel 'normal.' C) Tattoos What's the big deal? That is what some would ask. What I would say is that Kid Krazie subconsciously yearns for self-mutilation, stemming from all the things that his mother did to him as a youth. Why? I don't believe that it's because Kid Krazie subconsciously feels that he deserved what happened to him. No, instead I propose that this self-mutilation is rooted in an early desire to make himself as non-handsome a child as possible. He thought that the more he mutilated himself, the less anyone would care if he got ill, and in turn the less attention his mother would get for 'treating' him, meaning an end to his suffering. Of course, I could be completely wrong. Kid Krazie's mother might not have done anything to him. But the great thing about my hypothesis is that since no one but Kid Krazie knows, and he can't remember, I might as well publish my information as fact. At least I'm offering something more than 'I slipped and fell and now I don't remember.' And so, there is my hypothesis on the mental condition of Kid Krazie. He seemed special enough to do this for. Hiro Saotome is normal enough. Kramer WildWing isn't important enough to analyze. And myself, well, whatever my mental state the one thing for certain is that whether it is Charlie Loc or Sunshine holding the Cruiserweight Title, I will be challenging them after Tuesday Turmoil. I hope you've enjoyed this little pseudo-scientific anti-intellectual spiel I've gone on. And if not, screw you, I've got a title shot to win and a movie to do.
