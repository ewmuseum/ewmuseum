The Bitter Cold - Part 1 - Cold is Bogus

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 26, 2003 [The East of the United States has been taking a brutal pounding this winter. Cold, Ice, and Snow have been in the forecast since early November and it hasn't let up even going toward the month of March. Many people in the east haven't seen their lawns in months and have gotten used to using their four wheel drive ALL THE TIME! For a bodacious compadre who lives in California and has never experienced snow before, it's quite a sight to see all the gnarly white stuff. But once that dude steps outside and feels the bitter cold, he'll immediately jump back inside the warm airport] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: TOTALLY BOGUS!! [Tommy is dressed only in a blue and yellow silk shirt, blue jeans, and tennis shoes. He doesn't have a winter coat of any sort, well...because he was too stupid to even think of taking one with him. He figured the weather in California was going to be the weather in New York City. His brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry slowly comes into view with two suitcases and two gym bags hanging off of him. He wears a large black winter coat, black leather pants, and, underneath, black long underwear. The Heavy Metal One was prepared for the bitter cold especially since he's been sober for about five days now. It's a far out feeling to actually experience reality and know what is going on for once. He knew Tommy would forget about the bitter cold of the East, and he didn't bother to remind him. It's his punishment for the way he treated their mother the other day. She wanted answers and Tommy just gave her a bunch of his stupidity. It's no wonder she isn't returning their calls and having her secretary scream curse words at them] TTT: Dude, you go outside and see if the limo is here. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: There is a guy outside with a sign. [Tommy squints his eyes to outside and does notice a husky guy in a black suit with a sign in his hand] TTT: What does the sign say? [Harry rolls his eyes and sighs] Harry: It has your name on it. [Tommy squints his eyes again and notices the words on the sign that say: 'Totally Tubular' Tommy'.] TTT: Well...I'll be damned, dude. [Harry just shakes his head as he slowly struggles closer to the door with the large amount of weight on his body. Many people pass him getting out to the cold so they can get to their meetings or hotels. It just so happens that Tommy is going to be doing both of them in only a few short hours. He has to get to the Plaza Hotel in forty five minutes and then that gives him an hour to get ready, and then he must be at 5th Street Plaza by 3 P.M. to talk about the Tubular Tiki expansion. At the rate Harry is moving, which is just as fast as a turtle, they are going to be VERY late] Harry: Do you mind helping with these bags? TTT: No. [Tommy just stands there looking rather frightened of the cold outside. Harry walks through the automatic door as the limo driver immediately comes over to assist PCW's most far out manager] Limo Driver: Hey there, buddy. Harry: Hi. That's 'Totally Tubular' Tommy in there. Limo Driver: I know, mac. He's the only putz who would wear a short sleeve shirt while it's only in the single digits. [Harry just laughs as he helps the limo driver pack the car up with their luggage] Limo Driver: Is he just gonna stand there? [Harry just shakes his head and walks back inside to see what Tommy's problem is. Obviously it's the freezing cold that is frightening The Tubular One] Harry: What's wrong? TTT: It's cold out there, dude! I'm going to turn into one of those totally heinous icicles if I go out there! Why didn't you tell me that New York was this freezing cold? Harry: It's only five feet from the front door to the limo, Tommy. You won't freeze to death or anything. It'll be say, I promise. [Tommy nods his head with fear still in his blue eyes] TTT: Har... Harry: Yeah? TTT: How about you go to the car, leave the door open and I'll run and dive into the limo. [Harry just chuckles and nods] Harry: All right, Tommy. If that's what will make you feel better. [Harry walks back out into the bitter cold, as Tommy thinks of how brave and bodacious Harry is to do that. Granted he looks like he's all puffed up as if he were on some totally heinous steroids, but it's still most brave of him. Tommy watches as Harry gives him the thumbs up to come on out. It's now or never, Tommy thinks to himself. For some totally bogus reason his life begins to flash before his eyes. He remembers his father telling him about temptation and how it's easy to fall into the hands of evil. Money, he said, is the root of all evil, son. Tommy's father could have become a VERY rich man, just like Tommy is right now. 'Radical' Randy could have been a very successful wrestler but he never sold out. He was content with the money he was making just from wrestling, and that was it. As Tommy stares out at that limo, knowing he has to face that bitter cold, that EVIL COLD, he feels that he's let his father down. He didn't listen to him and he did everything that his father told him not to do. It's like Anakin Skywalker being breed to be one of the most bodacious Jedi warriors of all time, but then turning to the dark side because he is totally weak. Maybe Tommy should have paid more attention to Star Wars and he wouldn't have found himself where he is today. But would he have won the PCW Continental Title? Would he truly have gotten this far in his wrestling career WITHOUT being a sell out/super villain?] TTT: Ok... [Tommy bursts into a full speed run as the doors barely open in time and he leaps as if he were Spider-Man and makes it into the limo. Harry quickly slams the door behind him as Tommy shivers like crazy just from the half a second of being in the bitter New York cold] TTT: This weather is TOTALLY BOGUS! Harry: We need to get you some winter clothes. TTT: Totally. Driver, I need you to step on it. I'm supposed to be at some totally heinous meeting in an hour or so. The faster I get out of there, the faster I get to the bodacious babes of NEW YORK CITY! Limo Driver: Yes, sir. Can I ask you a question? TTT: If you have to. Limo Driver: Can I get your autograph? [There is a few moments of silence in the car] Limo Driver: It's for my son. He's a big PCW fan and it would be really cool if he had your autograph. TTT: I don't give out autographs. Limo Driver: Why? TTT: Duh, dude. I'm a super villain. Limo Driver: Oh. [Harry looks up at the driver, who seems a bit upset at the denial. Tommy could have SOME compassion and have given the guy an autograph just for his kid. Just because he's playing this super villain role doesn't mean he has to totally deny people EVERYTHING!] Harry: Hey...I need you to sign this ten grand check. TTT: Bodacious. [Tommy scribbles his name on the piece of paper with his blue eyes full of joy. He can probably already hear the sounds of the money being deposited into his bank account. Granted he was told by his father that being greedy is wrong, it doesn't matter now. The cold didn't kill him and he can't help it that he's filthy rich. It just comes with the gnarly territory of being a super villain and a totally bodacious wrestler] Harry: Here ya go. [Harry hands the signature to the limo driver] TTT: DUDE!! You totally tricked me! Harry: Of course I did. TTT: You know, I'm really getting tired of this sober thing. Harry: The only thing I'm getting tired of are these distractions. You could have someone else handle the Tubular Tiki expansion. I think YOU need to start concentrating on Fusion. Especially after what happened with Rent-A-Hero and everything...not like it was HORRIBLE, but it's enough to take you off of your game. We need this win on Friday. IT'S AGAINST SNOW! THE PCW CHAMPION! TTT: I can totally trash that dude, don't worry about it. Last week you were all worried about me not beating that Venom Pentagon dude...and what happened? Harry: You won. TTT: TOTALLY!! Harry: Well, Snow is a lot different from Venneman Pendragon. TTT: Your right. Snow is holding something that is going to be mine at March Madness, that's the only difference. Harry: Ok. TTT: DUDE!! HOW COME WE'RE NOT MOVING?! Limo Driver: Traffic. TTT: Tell them to move. Limo Driver: Sure. I'll get out and tell thousands of pissed off New Yawkers to just move so you can get to your meeting. TTT: Well...are you? [Harry and the Limo Driver roll their eyes as Tommy just sits there and waits] Fade to black
