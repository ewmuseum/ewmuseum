For those two seconds... I am free!

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/3/02 When you fight and fight and fight and just cannot get there, it can be devastating, though is some how, after you gave all you could, and won, finally, that can be the most momentous feeling you could ever experience. For when effort is put into something, it states that much sweeter. For nothing but your own blood sweat and tears and be as sweet. Life is it’s own reward, we must learn to live our own and not in the shadow of others, life begins now. PCW March Madness, the venue of the biggest match in Anton the Kid’s PCW career. He would go into battle against three other men, in a hellacious tables match, to determine the number one contender for the cruiserweight title. It was surely a massive night for everyone involved, all the greats would be participating; fighting to win, and Anton would be among them. His opponents were; Kramer Wild Wing, former PCW cruiserweight and television champion, a tough foe for anyone, Ganker, new in PCW, but already experienced and youthful, lastly, Red Rock, former PCW Rising Star Champion, again a tough opponent. Four men, one ring, tables galore, it was a dream match for some, a nightmare for others. Anton went into the match, the adrenaline rushing through his body at a fast pace, he had been anticipating the match for so long, he didn’t mind putting his body on the line, for the reward would be great. He went in, he tried his best, and he succeeded, he took out Red Rock, he took out Kramer Wild Wing and he took out Ganker. He then had the right to be named to number one contender for the Cruiserweight title, it was truly a great honour to have such a ranking, especially for Anton, he then knew what it was like to be highly appreciated, as the crowd cheered for him, it nearly brought a tear to his eye. Though all the success he had received was thrown away by the vicious Asian scumbag, Charlie Loc. A man that was apparently in PCW a development fed, that Anton was in a while back, though then it was no ordinary development fed, it was a threat to the very high ground PCW stands on. Though since the firing of former PCW President, lane Bishop, it has fallen to pieces. Loc and Anton had never had a confrontation before that moment, Anton didn’t know why Loc did it, be he would surely have a chance to claim his vengeance. A great day it was for Anton McGonnell, he was in hospital for three hours after March Madness but had been out for quite a while, and had was about to learn what Martial Art he would next learn from his master, Tsunami Chow-Lee, the greatest Martial Arts teacher to ever grace the face of the earth. Anton was extremely excited about having the chance to learn his next Art of Martial Combat. He had already achieved a Black belt in Judo, though he had some, previous experience of Judo, before Tsunami took Anton under his wing. The bright New York sun glazed onto Anton’s shiny, gel infested hair. He wore his usual Martial Arts attire of a black coat with an Asian symbol in orange and red on the top right hand side. He also wore matching black bottoms that were quite baggy, as well as what looked like black slippers. He also had a Nike Kit bag on his left shoulder; it was blue and black and was quite big. Anton walked across the Manhattan path on the busy morning. He turned into a side alleyway and almost immediately entered a door on the side of the alleyway, which led him into a small corridor. He entered another door into a hallway, then another big modernized black one. This room was like an office. It was quite small; there was a desk with many papers and pens on it, and a black leather chair behind it. On this chair, sat a quite small man with black hair, and signs of grey coming along the sides. Around the walls hung pictures, of many great Martial Artist, and some of Tsunami fighting himself, when he was in his prime. “Ah, hello Anton-san, I see you have arrived, I would like to congratulate you on your great win at March Madness.” Tsunami said, with his wise voice. “Thank you Master Tsunami, tough I was rather annoyed that I lost in the final of that Judo tournament, though it showed me that I am not supreme and will not get me big headed, I realize my opponent was a great fighter, I just hope I will have the chance to defeat him one day.” “That is good to hear Anton-san., I am not at all disappointed that you lost in the final, for winning is not everything, you must realize that.” “I do indeed Master Tsunami.” “Good, I will now reveal to you the next course of study in your Martial Arts training, your next art of combat will be…” The sweat rolled down Anton’s forehead, he knew he shouldn’t feel nervous, but it felt very big to him, for he loved Martial Arts, and needed it against the much bigger opponents he competed against in PCW… “Karate!” Said Tsunami. “The most well known Martial Art in the world, it is practical, yet it can be deadly, it is of course the most popular, though I am sure, my teachings of Karate will be much different than you see at local classes, for I am not called the greatest teacher of Martial Combat for nothing, I shall teach you everything of the Art, and you will be supreme. Karate involved a lot of high leg work, which you are well capable of.” Tsunami said, inspirational as usual. “I promise you Master Tsunami, I will heed your every word, and will soon be a master at the Art of Karate, just like you.” Anton said. “Good, now go and get ready, today you shall learn the basics of Karate.” Tsunami said while he got up from his leather chair. Anton bowed to him, he bowed back. Anton then left the room and entered to hallway again, he turns to a door on the right and enters it. The room is a small locker room. It was three seats on it and one locker-wardrobe. Anton sits down on one of the seats and begins to stretch. The next day would be a big one for him, a very big one; in fact it would be the biggest one of his PCW career. For he would compete in PCW Tuesday Turmoil, his opponent would be the Multiple Man, and the reward would be the coveted PCW title. For the Multiple Man had defeated Chris Chase for the title, and now Anton would have to take on one of PCW’s best talents, Anton only hoped he could receive the reward he so desperately wanted. Anton had searched for a long time the previous night, looking for information on The Multiple Man, but he could find very little, it again added to The Multiple Man’s mysterious nature. It would add to the trouble Anton would have in the match, he scanned through all of his records, but could find very little. It would be a lot harder to scout him in the match now, but Anton would not let such an opportunity pass him by, he would go into The Multiple Man like a speeding bullet. Though The Multiple Man was not all of Anton’s troubles, he would also compete on Friday Fusion, angst the treacherous Charlie Loc, the man that had ruined Anton’s celebration after winning at March Madness, and out Anton in hospital for hours, Anton would take great pleasure in executing his vengeance. Loc was not as mysterious as The Multiple Man, Anton would easily dig up his stats, and he would sure as hell be ready for Loc, for he couldn’t let him get away with what he did. Though, for now, Anton preferred to concentrate on his massive Cruiserweight title match, against the mysterious Multiple Man, a match made in heaven, a match of dreams, a match that Anton was desperate to win, for all his life, since he was three years old and watched his first PCW Friday Fusion, he dreamed of, one day winning his favourite title, the PCW Cruiserweight title. He loved the title do much because it was held by greats like Kid Wonder and Miyagawa. He loved the way they flew across the ring so gracefully, flying without wings and defying physical limitation, it was an amazing site, and Anton dreamed of one day living it. Now he had the chance, and he wouldn’t pass it up for the world, it was all he wanted, he refused to give such an opportunity away. He, himself, loved flying through the air, hitting his opponent with a mighty impact, for those two seconds, you are free, he loved feeling that, nothing could compare to it. As he sat, lacing his boots and getting ready for his Karate lesson, he thought, if he could hit his favourite move, the mighty Leap of Life, glide through the air, for two or less seconds, and feel what freedom really is, then, nothing would compare with it, it would be the greatest moment of his life, for if he some how won, and the crowd cheered for him, and the adrenaline rushed through his body, and he could get p onto the top rope, hold his head up high, and his Cruiserweight title belt up higher, then he would feel true freedom. And if he wins, he will remember those two seconds in the air, when he was free!
