Equal Warriors

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-10-02 The sun and the wind once had a huge battle, to see whose wrath was mightier. The two most potent elements in the universe clashed. They were evenly matched in all aspects. What did this mean? They would call it a draw? Oh no, such titans would never give in. So… in the end, it came down to heart and determination with that…the sun prevailed. I fought with every bone in my body. I gave it my all, yet the woman prevailed. The deceitfulness of female bond led to my failure. It was a cold day for me. Though it would not be repeated. Teresa Tomas may indeed have one this round, but the war was far from over. As for Semore Smoke on Turmoil… well, it is time for me to get back to my winning ways, and what better match to do it than an Extreme title match, and match where anything goes, but only one can walk away with the title. “Take it down! Yeah, just a bit more.” “Hurry up with those planks!” “Has the carpenters arrived yet?” These were the words heard around the busy building in the middle of Tokyo. It was indeed the building that would be the new Celtic Main Japanese base. Jamie stood on inspecting the re-development of the building. On his head was a yellow hard-hat. He wore a grey t-shirt and black, baggy bottoms. His hair was not visible under his hat and the hat shadowed his eyes out, though you could tell they were gleaming with excitement. Beside him stood two people. One was his best friend Gerald; he also wore a hard hat. He stood quietly with his hands in his pockets watching the developments go on lazily. On the other side of Jamie was the head of the development project. He was an old oriental man. He wore a red hard-hat unlike everyone else, and had a black jacket on him and blue prints rolled up in his left hand. “When should this be finished?” Jamie asked the Head of the project, while still facing the building. “Well, withal the furnishing and extra construction we have to do it could take 4 days.” He said in weak English. “Okay, that’s great.” Jamie replied solemnly. Jamie began to walk closer to the action. Gerald came with him, the two continued to look up. “Aw man, this is a great move for us Gerald. With this our international strength will triple!” Jamie said almost excitedly. “Yeah, it’s looking good Jay. This expansion idea of yours really is working out.” Gerald replied. “Yeah, now all we need to watch out for is gangs around here getting word of our presence before we get established. “ Jamie said apprehensively “I don’t think that’ll be much of a problem, we have kept it pretty quiet.” Gerald replied. Jamie looked around, not missing a singe gap in the streets and alleyways around. “I dunno, there seems to be a lot of suspicious people hanging around here.” Jamie replies almost paranoid. One of the construction workers looks over at Jamie. He is close to him though he keeps his head down. “As you should be Jamie-san, as you should be.” The young oriental man says into himself. He then walks away, still staring at Jamie but with his head down. Jamie shivered. He looked at the man and got a cold chill. “Wh… who is he.” Jamie said. He began to walk over towards him. The man spotted Jamie’s suspicion. He let go of the brick he was carrying and is fell to the ground. The man then ran it an extremely fast speed. Jamie followed, at equally a fast speed. “Wow, those guys are fast.” An American tourist said as he past the two. Jamie ran and ran as fast as he could, he began to gain on the Japanese man. Though he turned a corner and by the time Jamie reached it he was gone and out of site. “Damn it!” Jamie said as he kicked the wall in frustration. “Who was he?” Jamie asked himself. As he walked back to the building he wondered who the man was. Though he knew he would not get distracted, for this man could not get in the way of Jamie’s match of Wednesday, for he had won the NTICW World title when it was known for being extreme, Smoke won it when it was a half-ass wrestling fed where the referee’s let nothing go. Jamie intended to prove who the better champion was.
