Midgets and Chicken Nuggets

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/03/03 - Repost The broken down '72 green Chevy Nova pulls into it's familiar parking spot at Wendy's in Cleveland Ohio. The doors open and cautiously, Pete peeks his head out of the opening. He scans the parking lot looking for something, and satisfied whatever he's looking for isn't there, he reaches up to the visor and pulls out a pair of Groucho Glasses. The ridiculous nose and face of them barely fit his large head. He gets out of the car, and walks over to the other side to help General Tso out. Pete: Well, looks safe to me. Put your disguise on. Let's go eat. Tso: ...? Pete: What do you mean? Tso: ...! Pete: Oh, yeah. They've got those nuggets, little dude. Tso: ... Tso hops out of the car, and is shaking a little bit. Pete grabs his hand and they start walking to the entrance. A buick pulls into the parking lot. Pete watches it as the 3 men in the car stare back at him. He picks up Tso and runs into the restaurant. Other than a few employees, and an older woman sitting in the back, chain smoking Pall Malls, the place is empty. Pete walks up to the counter. The Cashier, a teenage girl with beautiful long hair smiles at him. Pete nods as he smiles back at her, looking into her blue eyes. Maggie: Hi Pete! Hi Tso-ey! Pete: Hi Maggie. How are you today? Maggie Good, thank you. What can we get you today? Pete: I'll take a Number 1 with no pickles, a Number 3 with no lettuce, a Number 5 with no mayo...what did you want Tso? Tso: ...! Pete: Oh yeah, 6 packs of chicken nuggets. Well, better make that 10. Maggie: Ok. That'll be $34.14 Pete reaches into his back pocket and pulls out his wallet, as he does from under his jacket, his Extreme title slips down into view. Pete pays it no matter and pulls out 35 dollars. Maggie's eyes move downward, catching a glimpse of the Extreme belt. Maggie: Oh my god! You're wearing that thing in public? Pete: Yeah, It's mine isn't it? Maggie: Oh my god! That's like, so cool! Was it hard winning that? Pete: Are you kidding? All i did was fall on some guy. I don't even want the thing! Maggie: Oh my god! I love PCW. Have you ever met Snow? Pete: I talked to him once... Maggie: oh My god! tell me all about him! Is he as cute in person as he is on TV? Pete: Well I... Maggie: You've got to tell me what you talked to him about! Pete: I walked up to him, and said hello, and he said 'Where's my fucking coffee?' Maggie: Oh my god! So you like, had a moment with Snow?? Pete: I...guess... Maggie: Oh...my...god! Quickly, three trays of artery clogging goodness are on the counter. Pete hands the tray of Nuggets to Tso, who scrambles to a table, and begins chowing down the miniscule delights. Pete says his goodbyes to Maggie, and grabs the two trays. He walks over to where Tso is sitting, and sits down himself. He grabs a napkin from the napkin holder and puts one on the table, and grabs another and tucks it into his shirt. He unbuttons his jacket, and puts the Extreme title on his shoulder. He grabs one of his three sandwiches and opens it. He pulls off the wrapper and starts eating with wreckless abandon. Lettuce starts flying out of the sandwich, and drips down onto the title belt. One sandwich down, as another is unwrapped, and begins to be eaten. Pete shovels fries into his mouth while Tso continues to eat his Chicken Nuggets. Tso: ... Pete: What? Tso: ...! Pete: I did? Damnit. I gotta be more careful with this thing. Pete grabs a napkin and wipes the mayo off the Extreme Title. he looks at the napkin and shrugs, placing it in his mouth. Tso looks at Pete and shakes his head. Pete simply shrugs and starts to eat again. Tso, has seemingly lost his appetite and gets up, and walks to the bathroom. He opens the door and sighs, looking at the toilet that's at least a foot too high for him to reach with his stubby little legs. He quickly moves, ricochets like a beach ball off one wall, kicks out from the corner, and executes a perfect back flip landing on the toilet seat. He looks around and giggles slightly, as he pulls his small zipper down, and takes a leak. The midget smiles as he relieves himself... Unaware of that danger that lays ahead... DUN DUN DUUUUUUUUUUUUN!
