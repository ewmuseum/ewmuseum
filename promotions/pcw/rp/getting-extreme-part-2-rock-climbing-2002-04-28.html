Getting Extreme - Part 2 - Rock Climbing

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 28 [Last we left the Cowabunga Connection and their newly acquired friend, Manfred, they were on a trip to go sky diving. It didn't really go as planned, with Manfred wimping out and then Harry and Tommy had themselves a little fight over how they were going to handle the upcoming Extreme Title match. Manfred stuck his nose in to break up the small fight that was about to break out, but ended up being pushed out of the plane. Fortunately he had his parachute on. Harry and Tommy made up and then dove out of the plane like two thrill seekers only can] [The scene opens up on a large mountain like canyon. It goes completely straight up, and the person that climbs up to the top of this canyon can truly say that they are EXTREME. Sounds of grunting, screams of fear, and hatchets clanging into the rock can be heard in the distance. The camera moves down the rocky gorge and shows three men at the BOTTOM of the canyon. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, decked out only in blue and yellow shorts and rock climbing shoes, keeps pounding the sharp climbing tool into the rocks. 'Heavy Metal' Harry is clinging out a small part of the bottom, trying to begin his climb, but keeps falling off the rock and welps in pain. He wears his usualy Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. Obviously Harry is not equipped to go rock climbing. Manfred is always in his tuxedo, with his odd twitching black, bushy mustache, he just stares up the gigantic canyon] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: This is going to be a most gnarly climb, dudes. If we conquer this thing then we'll be known as the most extreme compadres in history. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: People climb this canyon all the time, man. [Tommy slams the hatchet like tool into the rock] TTT: Yeah, but they have never done it in a tuxedo and cowboy boots, dude. [Harry looks down at his cowboy boots and smiles brightly] Harry: Yeah, man. [Manfred still stares up at the canyon] TTT: Did you hear about Misplaced getting fired, dude? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: That's a totally bogus thing, compadre. I mean first Samburu just leaves, then Metallyx falls off the face of the earth, Jobber's Inc. quits. That was the first bogus wave of leaving, and then came the second which almost wiped out the entire tag team force, dude. Harry: Tag Team Killa, man. TTT: Maybe there is, dude. Suburban Uprising left, but that was because we TOTALLY embarrassed them and took the number one contendership. Then Fear Factory is fired, but that's also because we gave them gnarly beatings that they couldn't take any longer. Now Misplaced is fired, that is totally bogus, but gnarly in a way. Harry: Why's it gnarly, man? TTT: Well, because with those dudes gone that is two less compadres in the Cincinatti Airport battle for the Extreme Title. That saves our energy from having to take out two extra dudes. [Harry smiles again] Harry: Yeah, man. [Manfred is still staring up at the top of the canyon] TTT: Now with Misplaced gone there are only three tag teams left, dude. Us, The Heartbreakers, and those totally bogus dudes, Politically Incorrect. Harry: Politicially Incorrect does suck, man. TTT: For sure, dude. In a way that's good because it makes our trek to becoming the most bodacious, gnarly, and radical tag team ever in PCW, much easier. Then if you look on the other hand there really isn't much competition to have and it'll get completely heinous after a while. I mean who wants to keep wrestling Politically Incorrect night after night, making them look totally bogus. Harry: I would, man. TTT: Yeah, just to make those dictators look like a couple heinous dudes. [The Cowabunga Connection doesn't notice but Manfred begins making his trek up the canyon. Tommy just keeps slamming the hatchet like tool into the rocks, paying no attention to the Lurch-like man who healed him from his illness of one week] TTT: There are a lot of veteran PCW guys in this match, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: All these guys who have already held many championships and who have accomplished a lot of bodacious things in their careers. They hold a big edge over us, dude. That's why we are going to have to stick together no matter what. Harry: Of course, man. [Harry goes to try his shot at climbing the same rock in front of him, but he once again falls off and onto his back] Harry: OOF...man.. [Tommy giggles at him, still slamming the hatchet into the rocks next to him. From doing this there are giant holes all over the place. Apparently Tommy doesn't notice as he still thinks about the rough Extreme match that he and Harry have coming up. It's going to be a challenge all right, a huge one] TTT: Dude, I noticed that The Rush is in the match. It'll be like an old NTICW reunion. Harry: Crunk sucks, man. TTT: Yeah, you are right about that. Crunk does sound pretty bogus, but Rush is a bodacious guy once you get to know him. Harry: Did we talk to him in NTICW, man? TTT: I don't think so, dude. But anybody who is against that bogus Ultimate Authority is gnarly in my book. [Harry looks unbelievably surprised at the mention of Tommy having a book] Harry: You have a book, man? TTT: Not personally, but I've read Curious George before. Harry: Curious George is far out, man. TTT: Yeah, you certainly like monkey's don't you, dude? Harry: I spank mine when it's bad, man. [Tommy looks extremely disgusted now] TTT: That's too much information, compadre. In speaking of NTICW guys...Jay Sylo is in the Airport battle too. He's a most gigantic dude. [Little pebbles and rocks tumble down in between Harry and Tommy. The camera looks up showing that Manfred is more than halfway up the canyon, knocking bits of rock down to the bottom. The Cowabunga Connection pays no attention to the falling rocks as they are too taken back by their upcoming match on PCW's Tuesday Turmoil] Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Like I said before, there are just a lot of big dudes in this thing. That's why I was stressing that we need to stick together through this thing until it's just you and me in the end. If anything bogus is happening to you I'll be there to bail you out, and I expect you to do the same. Harry: Damn skippy, man. What about Chef, man? He's like a far out guy who's won a lot of titles here in PCW. TTT: I totally agree, dude. The guys that we mentioned are compadres that we're going to really have to keep our eyes on no matter what. If we let them out of our must radical sight, then it can only spell heinous things for us. [Harry doesn't quite catch what Tommy is trying to say] Harry: What, man? TT: I totally agree, dude. The guys that we mentioned are compadres that we're going to really have to keep our eyes on no matter what. If we let them out of our must radical sight, then it can only spell heinous things for us. [Harry still doesn't catch it] Harry: Can you speak English, man? [Tommy seems to be a bit upset by this, but he'll give it another shot. He speaks very clearly, pronouncing each word with preciseness] TT: I totally agree, dude. The guys that we mentioned are compadres that we're going to really have to keep our eyes on no matter what. If we let them out of our must radical sight, then it can only spell heinous things for us. [Harry then smiles] Harry: Ok, I get it now, man. [Tommy stops whacking the rocks and looks around him, feeling that they are missing something. Every since Manfred has started living with them he's gotten so used to having two other people around him...well, three if you count when Jessica Alba comes over for a night of Yahtzee. Tommy notices that Manfred isn't around] TTT: Where did Manfred go, dude? [Harry looks around him, seeing no one else in sight] Harry: I don't know, man. Maybe he shrunk. TTT: You think so? Harry: It could happen, man. Just like in those really cheesy sci-fi movies from the 50's, man. [Tommy begins inspecting the ground, hoping to find a shrunken Manfred. Harry just stares forward with his bloodshot, stoned eyes. He thinks about that Cowabunga sandwich thing that he and Tommy could have with Jessica Alba. She'd like it, he's sure of it. What woman wouldn't mind two giant, hard coc--] TTT: I can't find him, compadre! Maybe we stepped on him and he's dead. That would be totally bogus to kill the dude that healed me! Harry: Yeah, man. [Then a large rock, about the size of a baseball, falls down between the two men. They look up to the top and see a gigantic figure standing at the top. The man is leaping up and down as if he had just won the lottery or something. Harry and Tommy both look at each other in confusion as if who this person could be. Then they see the tuxedo jacket float down between them and they slowly begin putting two and two together. Unfortunately, Tommy gets three instead of four] TTT: Dude! That was Manfred's jacket! I think that bogus dude at the top killed Manfred!! [Harry looks down at the jacket, then up at the large man still doing a dance. Harry looks at the jacket again and does his math now. Finally someone is able to come up with four and solve this damn thing] Harry: No, man. That's Manfred at the top of the canyon. [Tommy looks up, and does notice that the giant looks familiar] TTT: Well, how come he didn't say anything, dude? Harry: He's mute, man. TTT: Oh yeah. [Manfred keeps his celebration going at the top of the canyon, showing that he is truly extreme. He begins breaking it down with the Running Man, showing that large Lurch-like white guys can dance. The scene then fades to black on the dancing Manfred]
