Nightlife: The Henchmen for the Dynasty is Found

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/9/03 Nightlife With lights of all colors reflecting off haunting faces and unfamiliar persona. Men and women dressed for the fittest with gimmicky personality and subhuman traits. It’s a playground for the freaks and oddities and a chance at love for the trivial and contemporaries. With so many blends of attitudes and personalities that you find yourself in a maze for the mind. It’s that first second when you open the doors and all eyes are suddenly planted on you as if royalty had just stepped within its throne. You take three big steps before a deep breath. Within those three steps you see the transvestites, the rich, the poor, the punk, the junkies, the foreign, and the mysterious. All with one thing in common: Their need for the nightlife Nightlife is a funny thing. It allows you to take off that suit you wear every day for work and put on a completely new person. When night comes your no longer the same person all your friends and family know you as. You’re suddenly another character in this huge drama. You play that role as if it’s your real life, because you wish it could be your real life. I come to this place escaping everything that ever haunts me in the regular hours of my waking day. Here I am king and everyone else is just a simple role in my kingdom. I am a Romantic poet and wrestler by day, and a Casanova villainous king by night. In Spain it isn’t about who’s the smartest or best looking. People don’t follow you based on who you are. People follow you based on the money, power, and strength you’ve obtained through the years. I own this place as if I had etched my name in the fine print during the ownership struggles. But I am not an owner. Winking twice at the DJ to make him play a song at my request, or raising my arm in the air for a complete silence through the building does not make me an owner, but yet a greater force. These people do not dare to take the risk of disobeying me, or going against my in authorization. No one ever said they couldn’t, but at the same time no one ever said they could. To my left and right I had nearly a hundred men willing to follow me off a cliff. An offender to the words I preach or motions I take would not last any longer than a day. So I never thought much of anyone questioning the power I held. I had the mark of a Euro to protect me, and the mind of a businessman to elevate me. If force were to be taken, I would not be able to be defeated. I was at a level where the only thing that could bring me down was I myself. The nightlife was here to make my head bigger and bigger, and to elevate my ego to a point where there was no turning back. That was until one man tried to put an end to the Romantico dynasty. That one man found himself in a predicament he may have never expected to be in. I was sitting in my usual custom-made leather tapestry silk lined throne, when the music suddenly stopped. I rose from my feet and looked up to see what the commotion was. At my side was the owner of Lacura, which was one of the common clubs I let my power be felt in. What is this disturbance? A man is trying to hold a protest with a group of radicals within the building and is ruining my possessions. Should I call the police? There is no need; it will be taken care of shortly. I slowly walked over to the crowd as men and women stepped out of the way when they saw I was within ten feet of their vicinity. When I finally made my way through the crowd, the men who loyally fight by my side clinched their fists and got ready to go in. HALT! What is wrong Romantico? I shall handle this myself. I stared at the group of men in red and black, and then saw their leader in the front, wearing a long black bandana with a long cut extending down his face. What is it you make such a ruckus over here in this palace of nightlife? I protest again the racial segregation of the Moorish people of Spain. Clubs just like this one have turned our people away and taken in all types of oddities and sexual deviants. I demand equality or else peace will not be heard. Peace shall be heard, and you shall leave for you are in a place where you are not wanted. Find yourself the door and leave us all forever. Romantico turned around and began to walk away when he heard a word not familiar to his vocabulary. No! The whole place turned quiet as Romantico turned around and looked at the man. Excuse me. I shall not leave until I have spread my message. I stared once again at the man noticing more than before the long scar extending cheek to cheek. Tell me how you go that scar on your face? I used to be a bullfighter until the prejudice of this country through me out of their leagues so more of their own could be given the chance to bullfight. If an animal could make such an impression on you, do not let me make on of my own? The man then suddenly walked right up to me and went nose to nose with me in the center of the club. Is there something you are contemplating doing. The bigger and more defined barbaric looking man through a wild swing at Romantico. Romantico calmly caught the punch and just stared at the man laughing. What came next was something Romantico did not laugh about. Instead of being discouraged the Moorish man swung away with his left hitting Romantico right above the eye. Romantico was stunned as he held his hand to his eye and felt the blood trickle down. I WILL NOT BACK DOWN! The more screamed for his cause as Romantico finally realized that this man was not looking for violence, but yet equality. What is your name young man? Savito! Well Savito, put down your signs, yield your protest, and join us. Savito looked on spectacle as Romantico extended his hand. After a few moments of silence Savito shuck the hand of Romantico, as the music went back on and everyone enjoyed the nightlife together. Only time will tell what will come of Savito and Romantico.
