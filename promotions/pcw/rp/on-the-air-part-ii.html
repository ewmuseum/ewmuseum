On the air...Part II

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-7-02 ::Introduction:: With the amazing match that Mark had on Turmoil he had some confidence that he can actually play with the top notch wrestlers of PCW. He had the match in control until The Chef stole a victory away from Mark. But I guess that is what happens in the wrestling business, the match can go either way at some point of the match. Mark is not mad because he was taught some things in the match that he fought in. He got to fight against the new people of the Ultimate Authority in The Chef. The Chef is a good wrestler I will give it to him, but he is beatable and hopefully when Mark gets his chance again he will beat him. But now he is focusing on his upcoming match tonight with a new face in PCW, Ricky Thunder. A lot of people expect to have him as a top notch star in the years to come, but to Mark he is just another rookie who has to prove himself to the wrestling business. ::End Introduction:: Mark flew back from Philly to get back on the air with Dennis Miller in California. Mark hopes to talk more about his wrestling background then what is to come in wrestling. Mark knows that Dennis does not know anything about wrestling, probably only football, but Mark will work with him on the air and see where it goes from there. “We have a familiar face back in the studio today.” Says Dennis as he is talking in the booth by himself waiting for Mark to come. “He was here just a couple of days ago and has come back to talk to the whole world about his career and to answer all of your fans questions.” Mark goes into the sound proof door and he enters the studio where Dennis is still talking into the microphone. He signals to Mark to have a seat right next to him by his microphone he was on just a couple of days ago. “Well Mark we all know that you had a match on Tuesday Night and you did pretty well in that certain match you were talking about on Monday.” Dennis says as he is looking at Mark as he fixes his mouth getting ready to talk. “You were in control of the match all the way through and then you just let it go. You went flying over the top rope and bam The Chef pins Kano and he gets the pin. How did you let that one get away from you like that?” “Well Dennis sometimes you just loose your groove that you are in, in the middle of the match and then sometimes you just get screwed over and let the match get away.” Mark says as he is thinking back to the match and how it was like. “I was in control and then that throw just that very throw was all that was needed to have enough people out of the ring for the win. I just have to be prepared next match when I do face them all that I cannot get screwed again.” “Now people said that you lost, but you really won. How is that?” Dennis asks as he is really uncertain of what people mean. “It is very simple how I won that match.” Mark says trying to explain to Dennis how he learned a lot out of the match. “I got a lot learned out of the match that I lost in. I am new here still as people say and I am still learning and rising in PCW each and every match. Everyone learns a lot from a match as if it is a new hold or how much pain they can suffer. It is like all the Americans today out in the working field except for all of the African Americans that are blown off their asses, but it is like you go out to a job and you learning something new everyday. It is just amazing how much you can accomplish and look back at from just one match and put it to your next one.” Dennis keeps on speaking about Mark a little more and Mark just sits there and stares down at the counter. He thinks about what he just said and how me meant what he said. He does learn a lot even if he does lose matches. Mark knows it doesn’t matter on your win loss record it matters on how you perform and how the fans like you. How much energy you get when fans are booing or giving you mixed reactions is what it is all about. “So with all of the stables going around and people going to this and people leaving that, how do you see your stable stacked up to all the rest?” Dennis asked as Mark catches the last of his speech that ends on a question. “Well the Political Machine we have a lot of talent, Jaden who is probably going to be a great women’s star in PCW in the months to come. We have Murray and Crichton who were a tag team, but are now single wrestlers. I don’t know much about them, but Murray has to be good if he was the Rising Star Champion at one time, but hopefully I will get to know them better. Then there is Politically Incorrect, Powell and Lovecraft, the tag team who brought this whole stable together and who are going to take it to the next level. I hope that I get Political Machine recognized with my matches and the wrestler I am. I hope to destruct some hell on PCW in the months to come. I know that I haven’t been very dominating, but when Gold and Glory comes and after that I am going to be a new person and there will be a new era I am going to enter.” With that said Dennis is sitting at his microphone trying not to kill himself with yet another speech that Mark gave. But it was Dennis who asked Mark the question and it was Mark who gave a detailed answer. Dennis takes a deep breath and there is silence across the radio waves as Mark is just sipping on the glass of water that is right next to him and he then puts the purified water down on the counter. “Now I have heard a lot of people talk about Ricky Thunder across the air waves.” Dennis says as he breaks the dead silence of the air. “I have heard that he is going to be “the next big thing” that will hit PCW along with some other wrestlers in PCW. What are your thoughts on him and your match tonight?” “Ricky Thunder, the same wrestler that came into PCW and said I was going to be the first victim?” asks Mark to Dennis as he looks at him and into his eyes. “That is the only Ricky Thunder I know of Mark.” Says Dennis, as he knows what he is talking about because of his research. “Ricky Thunder has come into PCW thinking that he is going to glide through me.” Mark says as he is trying to think back to Ricky’s first promo that he did in PCW. “He said I was going to be the first victim that he has had in PCW. Well that is not true since he is just a rookie in PCW like me, but he has not showed anyone anything about himself to prove that he is the next big thing. So what he has cut a few promos that are decent, but how will he fair up in the ring? Are his skills up to date like mine are? Well we will just have to find that out and it doesn’t matter if he is part of the Phantom Phive version 2 because that doesn’t matter in this match. It is a one on one match in which we are going to see which two rising star’s are going to be better than the other one in the near future.” “Yet another detailed answer by you Mark and we will take a question from a caller now.” Dennis says as he is relieved that he is going to the callers. Dennis goes over to the phone box where the incoming calls light up a red light that indicate what line they are on. They are all filled up as they all want to know more about Mark and about wrestling in general. It is like watching TRL on Mtv and seeing all of the lights light up on the box where Carson presses but that doesn’t matter right now as I am only giving you a visual. “Hello and you are on the air with Mark Makonie!” Dennis says as he screams into the microphone after pushing line eight and the red light goes off. “Yes Mark, how did you come up with your gimmick?” A guy with a deep voice asks as he talks into his phone clearly. “When I was young I was picked on a lot.” Mark says as it might be emotional to talk about his past, but he can deal with it. “It was not really when I was a kid, but as in my high school years. I was a geek in high school with the chess club and playing in the band. I did all of that geeky stuff like study and was in the top ten percent in my class. Then I went on to college, which I didn’t know was going to be a mostly African American culture school. I roomed with an African American and was around them for a while until I met a guy who was Jax. He explained to me what a Neo-Nazi was and I wanted to get back at the culture for what they did and wanted to do it where it was legal, wrestling.” Dennis looks as if he is going to fall asleep as he hears Mark talking into the microphone not changing his voice up at all or acting excited about what he is talking about. Dennis looks up at Mark with a heavy glaze over his eyes as he is trying to speak into his microphone, but can’t seem to get the words that he wants out of his mouth. “Well I think that we should take another call, what do you think Dennis?” Mark asks as he knows that Dennis is having troubles finding words to get out of his mouth. “Yes another call we will take.” Dennis says in a tired voice and he is still hunched over in his chair like he was. Dennis gets up and he pushes another button to get rid of a red light. He then goes out of the booth and he goes into the lounge. Dennis can’t stand to be around Mark as he is going to get some coffee from the Magic Chef coffee pot in the lounge. “You are on the air with me Mark Makonie and ready to answer you question.” Mark says as he speaks happily into the microphone, a first from what we have heard him speak of all night. “Holla Makonie!” says a voice over the phone and Mark knows that is a language of an African American. “What da ya think ya are doin with my home boyz that are in tha fed?” “You and the rest of the African Americans don’t deserve to have phones to use.” Says Mark as he has a mean look in his eyes as he doesn’t have a smile or frown on his face. “You are a disgrace to this country as it has been shown with the R. Kelley scandal. An African American can’t get enough play from a woman that he has to go and get some little girls to pose for him. He then looks at it every night and probably whacks off just like every one of you African Americans. I leave you with that today and hope get in touch with you all again.” Mark takes off his headphones and he heads out of the room as he flips a switch to get it off the air. He then storms out of the booth just like on Monday having a lot of things on his mind at the moment. He is going to catch a plane to his event tonight on Fusion. He hopes that he will win tonight although he has been busy throughout the week to do promos. He just hopes that he can work in the ring at his best and he hopes that someday he can get all of the African Americans dead or sent back to their country.
