The Assassination Series Part One: Damn Pirates...

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/5/03 The towering mammoth, Blind Rage barged his way through the police barricade and gazed at the sight before in awe and splendour. The crowd of civilians had begun a rather inspiring Justice Inc chant, because the realised that a fight was imminent. Blind Rage glanced behind him, realising that Warrior wasn’t with him for some reason, so he ventured back to the police barricade to see where he was. Upon reaching the barricade again, he discovered that Warrior had been held up by the police. They had refused to believe that he was superhero and figured that he was just some lunatic in a suit. Blind Rage couldn’t blame them for doing such a thing; after all, Warrior was a lunatic in a suit. Hearing reassurances from Blind Rage seemed to ease the police officers considerably and consequently Warrior was allowed through. Upon seeing the sight before him, Warrior’s draw visibly dropped. Upon hearing the unconfirmed reports on the radio, they were understandably reluctant to believe their ears, but stood before the giant pirate ship put a new perspective on things. According to the report the pirate ship appeared in the docks early that morning. Blind Rage eyed the ship up intently. There was no sign that the ship was actually a pirate vessel, except for the black skull and crossbone flag. In the olden days this would signal a crew of pirates, but they had to be sure before they charged on board and accused the lot of them of being pirates. However, the ship itself seemed to be old, with no visible engine and the same design of the ships in the early 19th Century. This was indeed a mystery. Warrior shot Blind Rage with an understandable uncomfortable look. They then take a couple of steps forward, before being stopped in their tracks. Something was coming down the wooden plank, which was balanced in between the shore and the edge of the boat. Blind Rage strained his eyes to see what it was. It was a pirate. The pirate stopped half way down the ramp, put his fingers in his mouth and whistled. With the whistle came more of them, swarming out of the various cabins and then charging across the wooden plank onto the shore. Blind Rage noticed that each of the pirates had an over-sized kind of sabre sword. Blind Rage sighed. Pirates. No superheroes, just damned pirates. He confidently walked forward toward the pirates, causing the leader of the pirates to point in Blind Rage’s vague direction and bark some kind of order. Warrior guessed it to be the word ‘charge’. He was correct. Warrior hesitantly held back a little from Rage and quickly took out both of his laser axes. This one was going to get messy, there was no doubt about it. Warrior watched on as the first five pirates reached Blind Rage. The first one was simply shrugged aside, flying about 10 feet backwards and falling into the water. The second one that attacked actually landed a blow with his rather large sabre, which subsequently shattered upon hitting Rage’s super tough hide. Blind Rage then hit the pirate in the jaw. The pirate flew backwards and collided with the other three, who were knocked out instantly. Then two more of the pirates piled on top of Blind Rage, whilst another two pirates headed for Warrior. Warrior flicked the button on the side of his laser axe and as a resultant the two green, axe-like lasers sprung from the metal batons. Warrior blocked the first blow and kicked the pirate in gut, following up by bringing the base of the baton down, hard across the base of his neck. He then quickly swivelled around, slicing the second pirate in across the chest. Meanwhile, Blind Rage had convincingly threw the two pirates away from him and taken several others out in the amount of time it had taken Warrior to dismantle two. Now there was only the lead pirate left, who looked decidedly worried and had begun to stumble backwards. “Ooooo-arrrrrr…take it easy boys…t’was only a diversion!” the pirate murmured. Blind Rage and Warrior glanced at each other. “A diversion? From what?” Blind Rage asked in wonder. “It’s him!” the pirate declared pointing behind them, Blind Rage and Warrior glance around and see a maniacal-looking man, leaning against the black, iron, lamp post. The man is wearing a full length blue suit, with a bullseye painted on the top of his mask. The man smiled. “Guess who I am.” he simply said. Blind Rage already knew who he was. He had read newspaper articles about the man and how he constantly got his assed kicked by Daredevil. What could he want with Justice Inc? he thought to himself, speculatively. “Well come on.” the assassin prompted. “I haven’t got all day!” “We know who you are!” Blind Rage declared. “What we want to know is; what do you want with us?” Bullseye laughed in a crazy fashion. “That’s the question…isn’y it?” “We know…that’s why we asked you, dumbass.” Warrior replied. “Don’t worry about it Warrior…he’s just trying to be dramatic. Typical super villain.” Blind Rage added. Bullseye sighed. “Do you two morons want to stop ATTEMPTING to be funny, while I get to the point?” “Oh…the super villain is getting in a rut…we’d better listen to the maniac’s plans.” Blind Rage prompted. “You may wish to mock me now, but when you read this…” Bullseye trailed off, as he three a knife, which pieced a note. “You won’t be so jovial.” The knife was thrown with excellent pin-point accuracy, indenting itself into the grown by Blind Rage’s foot. Blind Rage soundlessly bent down and retrieved the knife and upon extension the note. He read the note with fierce concentration, his expression morphing into concern directly from anger. He handed the note to Warrior and grimly looked up at where Bullseye was to find him gone. This didn’t surprise Blind Rage in the slightest. Somebody had employed the deadly Bullseye to take Justice Inc out of the equation. Blind Rage figured it must have been that owl-guy who had employed that disgruntled midget. The note informed them that two of their number had already been captured. Badger Man and the Handbag Warrior were tough individuals, despite their slightly comic monikers and Bullseye had captured them with relative ease. The note also informed the two remaining free members of Justice Inc where The Handbag Warrior and Badger Man were being held. Blind Rage knew that this was intended as an obvious trap, but what else could they do? “What are we gonna do Rage?” Warrior asked, handing the note back to him. “We go to the address on the note.” Blind Rage replied. “And walk into Bullseye’s trap.” Blind Rage nodded grimly. “Indeed.”
