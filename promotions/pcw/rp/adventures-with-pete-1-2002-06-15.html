Adventures with Pete [1]

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 15, 2002 [The scene opens to Tom Lexian, sitting out in a field, tying his shoes. These aren’t your ordinary shoes either…these are new Nike Awesomes. What makes them so different? They cost twice as much as the former pair. Go figure, huh? After lacing up his shoes, he looked up at the beautiful sky and smiled. It was a good day to be traveling. He would be successful today. That’s when he heard a startling sound behind him, and he turned around quickly, coming face to face with a…Tomb Raider?] Lara Croft: [stretching out her arms] Awwww….it’s sooo early. I haven’t even had a chance to shower yet. Tom Lexian: [surprised] Aren’t you….? Lara Croft: Oh god, please don’t start with me. It’s far too early for this now. Tom Lexian: Too early for what? Lara Croft: You know….Oh my gosh! You’re Lara Croft! I love you! Oh my gosh! Tom Lexian: [shrugs] I can see how that can be annoying. I get that all the time… Lara Croft: [smiles] ….really? Tom Lexian: [dejected] Ok, no. But I wish I got it all the time. I want people to see me and to rush to my side, screaming for my autograph and clawing over each other, just to get a whiff of my cologne. Lara Croft: If that’s your dream, you are pretty pathetic. Tom Lexian: You’re not the first to say that to me! I’ve been rejected by loads of women before… Lara Croft: For some reason, I completely believe you. Now, can we cut this out and get down to business please? Tom Lexian: Hey, you dropped on me, remember? It’s your show. [Tom sat down again, admiring the view from the field. As he did, Lara sat beside him, revealing a small scroll. She unraveled it, and inside was a picture of a very special object.] Tom Lexian: [in awe] Whoa…that’s the Amulet of Orion! It’s more awesome than I first believed… Lara Croft: Uh, yeah…awesome. Anyways, I believe this is what you’ve been so anxious to acquire, is it not? Tom Lexian: It is…[Tom rubs his hand on the scroll, touching the picture of the amulet. As he does, he presses harder against the paper, trying to get a cheaper feel through the paper on Lara’s leg.] Lara Croft: [slaps Tom] Behave yourself! Tom Lexian: [rubs his cheek, embarrassed] Sorry, Lara. I couldn’t help myself. Those eyes…those lips….that hair…[his eyes dip lower]…wow, those two, huge… Lara Croft: [takes out her gun, sticking it in Tom’s face] Say it, and I’ll be giving you a few more eyes to stare at them with. Get it? Tom Lexian: Got it… Lara Croft: Good. [puts away the gun.] Tom Lexian: You know, I’m curious. How exactly did you find out what I was planning? I mean, it’s not like I announced it or anything. [Lara reaches in her pocket, bringing out a small poser. On it has Tom’s face plastered on the front, and in big, bold letters reads, “I am announcing that I’m looking for the Amulet of Orion. Go me!” Lara looks back at Tom, who is already blushing.] Tom Lexian: Okay, maybe I did announce it. So sue me. I didn’t think anyone was going to take me seriously, I mean, no one knows if this thing exists anyways. Lara Croft: Oh, believe me. It exists alright. I was sent by someone who wants to see it found, but is too lazy to go look for it himself. Tom Lexian: And that person would be? Lara Croft: [laughs] No you don’t…I’m not at liberty to say, and besides, you don’t need to know. All you need to know is that someone is ready and willing to help you. Tom Lexian: [smiles] Lucky me. Lara Croft: Indeed. Now, this Amulet that you seek will not be easy to find. It can be found in the belly of a dragon, in a place far, far away. Tom Lexian: Far, far away? Could you be a bit more specific than that? Lara Croft: I wish I could. Even we don’t know the exact location of the amulet, either. If we did, we would have gone out to search for it ourselves. Let me be a bit more honest with you…this is a dangerous mission. Many have gone on it, and not returned in one piece. I, myself, am scared to even be helping you. Tom Lexian: Is there a reason why it’s so well protected? Lara Croft: Nimrod, it’s extremely powerful. They can’t just have anyone running around with it. The results could be catastrophic. Why did you want the amulet again? Tom Lexian: I need it to be able to go back into the past and train harder. Lara Croft: Couldn’t you just train now, in the present? Just train harder… Tom Lexian: No, no…I need all the time possible to train. You see, I'm a bit superstitious. Lara Croft: I see…well, I can help you with that. The amulet is located inside of a great dragon…a very large, angry dragon. You’ll have to find him, because he’s constantly moving around and eating…well, people. Tom Lexian: I’d like to feed Xavier Kannon to that dragon. Lara Croft: [confused] Who? What? Where? Tom Lexian: Ah yes, I forgot – you don’t know who I’m talking about. Xavier Kannon is this guy I'm supposed to fight. Well, him and this other guy, Snow. Big scary fellow, uses the tilde key a lot. But anyways, I wouldn't mind seeing him gobbled up at all. Lara Croft: If you’re so confident, why do you need to go back into time to train? Aren’t you confident enough to be able to train by yourself and to win the match? Tom Lexian: It’s not that simple. I need something to reassure me that I can be the best that I can. If I don’t have it up here [taps himself on the head], then I won’t have it in the ring. Lara Croft: Well, I hope that you’re ready to fight a dragon for it. As I said before, it won’t be easy to get the amulet. You’ll have to slay him to get access to it, and even slaying him will be nearly impossible. Tom Lexian: How impossible? Lara Croft: They’re betting a million to one odds that you’ll get the amulet at Reno. Tom Lexian: What the hell…they’re betting on my success during this mission? What kind of a city is Reno? Lara Croft: [shrugs] An entertaining one? Anyways, the odds on you returning alive is even slimmer, so I won’t quote those to you. Let’s just say that if you want to make some quick cash, you might want to bet on yourself to win it all. Tom Lexian: Are you going to make any bets of your own? Lara Croft: Sure I am. Why, I have twenty bucks riding on you getting killed by Sunday night. Tom Lexian: [shocked and surprised] What?! I can’t believe it, Lara. How can you just bet against me? Lara Croft: Sorry honey, but you don’t stand too much of a chance against that dragon. He breathes fire, he can fly…hell, he’s a lot sexier than you too. [Tom frowns, placing his face in his hands. How can he compete with a dragon? He probably was everything that Lara just described him as. This definitely wouldn’t be easy…] Tom Lexian: Well, if it’s all the same to you, I’ll still give it my all. Lara Croft: As I expected. Well, we’re not going to leave you empty handed, Tom. We have some clues for you. See a man in Reno named R.L. Stine. He might have some information that might be useful for you. Tom Lexian: Okay. What if he doesn’t know anything? Lara Croft: Then you’re in the shitter, bud. We have no clues other than that. [gets up and starts to leave.] Tom Lexian: [gets up suddenly, wiping off his pants.] Hey, hey, hey…what’s the hurry, Lara? I’m not going on this mission any time soon, and…well, I was hoping to get to know you better… [Lara smiles, patting Tom on the back. Tom hoped that she’d agree to have dinner with him tonight, and hopefully he’d make some head way on an obviously perfect relationship. Forget the fact that he already has a girlfriend….] Lara Croft: I think you have enough to worry about. You see, we’re not letting you go on this mission alone. Tom Lexian: Oh? You’re not? Lara Croft: No silly! We have someone who we think can be perfect for your quest… [Suddenly, a large, fat, Caucasian man appears from the sky, falling right in front of Tom. Tom looks at him oddly, as he looks remarkably like…] Tom Lexian: [in shock] Pete Rose?! Pete Rose: [rubbing his head] Shit…that was one hell of a ride… Lara Croft: You two behave yourselves… [Lara Croft disappears in a flash of light, blinding Tom for a moment. He can’t believe he’s been stuck along with Pete Rose, a gambler and a renowned cheater in the sport of Baseball. Sure, he was a great hitter – but how many assholes beat against their own team? In the last few moments, Tom hadn’t realized that Rose had slipped behind him, checking him out.] Tom Lexian: What the hell are you doing? Why are you checking out my ass? Pete Rose: [looking up] What? I’m not checking out your ass! Hell, I wouldn’t check out my own ass. I was just…sizing you up. Tom Lexian: [crossing his arms] Start making more sense, bud… Pete Rose: Okay, okay…well, I wanted to see how big you were and how strong you were so…so I could… Tom Lexian: Go on… Pete Rose: [slumps his shoulders] So I could figure out whether or not I should bet against you or bet in favor of you… [Tom suddenly slaps Pete on the nose, causing him to fall to the ground. Pete is stunned, but suddenly wields a bat…] Pete Rose: Oh…you’re asking for it now, Lexian. I may be old and guilty of betting, but I can still swing a bat… Tom Lexian: How the hell could you bet against me? Pete Rose: You let Lara bet against you! Tom Lexian: You’re not a fine ass woman that I want to get laid with! Pete Rose: I can be! Tom Lexian: [pauses] Ewww, gross. Anyways, haven’t you learned your lesson from your past? You got banned and busted from baseball because you bet on games. Haven’t you learned that it’s wrong? Pete Rose: [defending himself] They never actually convicted me on any crimes… Tom Lexian: Please don’t bullshit me, Pete. This is just pathetic. I can’t believe they have named you my sidekick for this mission. I’m going to risk my life to look for this amulet, and you’re over here betting against me! Pete Rose: Hey, I have a lot of great attributes that you could use! Tom Lexian: [sighs] Okay fine…elaborate a bit on that. Tell me why you can make a great sidekick for me, Pete, and maybe I’ll keep you on my team. Pete Rose: Well, besides having a great reputation, I also wield my trusty bat, just in case we run into problems with mean ol’ bad guys. [Tom doesn’t look impressed.] Pete Rose: [hastily continuing] ….uh, AND…uh, I always have a ready supply of bubble gum for your chewing pleasure. [Pete Rose pulls out a pack of Bazooka bubble gum, and begins to chew it. Tom steals the comic, much to Pete’s chagrin.] Tom Lexian: [reading the comic] Look Pete, you’re going to have to do better than that if you want to be on my team. Now, I want you to look me in the eye and tell me that you can be an aide for me, not a hindrance. Pete Rose: I can’t look you in the eye when you’re staring at that damn comic all the time. Tom Lexian: Oh sorry…[puts the comic away] Okay…tell me what I want to hear… Pete Rose: Tom, I can be your knight in shining armor. Tom Lexian: [throws up his hands in frustration] Dude, this isn’t some homosexual shit, okay? This is serious. If I’m going to put my life on the line, I need to know that I can trust the people that have my back. Now, tell me you can do it… Pete Rose: I can do it… Tom Lexian: [shouting] I can’t hear you! Pete Rose: I CAN DO IT! Tom Lexian: [shouting even louder] I CAN’T HEAR YOU! Pete Rose: [face is turning red] I CAN DO IT! I CAN DO IT! Tom Lexian: Show me the money! Pete Rose: Show me the money! Tom Lexian: I love the asian man! Pete Rose: I love the asian man! He’s so handsome and charming and… Tom Lexian: [pats Pete on the shoulder and starts to walk off.] Congratulations Pete…you’re still my teammate. Let’s head out of here, I’m starving… [So the scene fades with Tom walking off to a nearby restaurant with Pete Rose following closely behind. For some reason he keeps slipping on his bat and falling into the dirt. Tom laughed and laughed…oh, a new friendship was born.]
