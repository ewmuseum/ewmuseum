Hot 97, Tha Morning Show!

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/02 {::Two more days until Red Rock & Lance Bell go face to face. Lance has his game face on and is ready for anything that comes in his way. But for now, Lance, Juju, Flava, Kyle and James are heading somewhere in New York. Yes, New York. They left Idaho for the day due to how boring and stiff it got up there. The five are going to be on one of the best Radio Stations in the world, yes, Lance and the crew are heading to a station that plays nothing but Rap & R&B, yes, they are heading to Hot 97.::} {::The scene opens to Lance leaning his back against a black limousine, he has a black sweat shirt on with a hoodie, it is pouring cats and dogs outside. He is impatiently waiting for his friends to come out so they can do a little promotional gig for PCW on Hot 97. Every minute or so, Lance looks down on his watch and looks back up at the door to see if anyone is coming out. Right off the bat he sees James, Flava and JuJu. They run out of the house and run inside of the limo due to the rain. Lance is waiting for one more person, and that is Kyle...::} Lance:(yelling) KYLE, WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING, WE GOTTA GET OUT OF HERE C'MON! WE ARE GOING TO BE LATE FOR THE MORNING SHOW! Kyle:(yells back)HOLD UP DUDE! Lance:(mimicking Kyle's voice under breath) hold up dude... idiot. {::Ten minutes later.....::} Kyle:(walking out of the house) Ok, I'm ready... Lance:God man! Took you long enough... now lets go! {::The two head into the limousine as the limo driver, wastes no time and heads off to the Hot 97 station. On their way there, they have a little conversation about the show and what will be asked of them...::} JuJu:Finally, I get to surround myself with smart people, other than you idiots! Lance:Ju, just because they are your same skin color doesn't make them smart people or us 'white people' idiots. JuJu:Well then, you must not know, Star & Buck, and Miss Jones, then they have Crossover Negro Reese and Miguel! Lance:Yes, JuJu, I know all of them. All star does is humiliate people all over the country.... he brings controversy to the world.. JuJu:Man, whatever, all I know is that this show is going to be the best one yet, the only person I feel sorry for is Kyle. Lance:Why Kyle? Kyle:Yeah, why me? JuJu:Well, Kyle, you may have the most trouble because your easy to pick on... I mean look at you, blonde hair, blue eyes, a guy who always says Dude all of the time, around a bunch of black disgruntled people? YO ASS GONNA BE CHOPPED UP ON THE RADIO STATION... Kyle:Well, you'll have my back won't you? JuJu:Heh, lets not comment on that... but you won't be alone... you'll have James The Third who's gonna get chopped on as well... oh and Flava.... man all of ya'll gonna get it on that show except for me and maybe Lance. Flava:Man, they ain't gonna go after me son, I'm to nice... my Flava can't be touched... Star & Buck betta chill before I bust four caps in they ass.... fa real. JuJu:Flava, your not understanding me.... you are a white man trying to be black. Your not gonna cut it, I mean c'mon, you have gold fronts my friend... then you have cornrows... it's not going to work. Flava:Well, you MAY be right, but you know what would be funny? Lance:What would be funny Flava? Flava:If JuJu, after talking all of this smack, gets played out the most on the show... Flava:I'll give you so many reasons WHY that won't happen, but I'll give you on. One reason to why Star & Buck won't play me out is because I am the representative. Flava:Say what? JuJu:Well, it's like this, I embody of what an everyday, African American should do. James:(With British Accent)And what to them do you embody Mr.JuJu? JuJu:Shiiit, I embody an everyday African American, that's mackin girls and pimpen hoes... Lance:Your just an idiot. We'll see what happens when we make our way to Hot 97. After that, tonight I got my match versus Bob, and then we head to Canada on Sunday versus Red Rock for the Rising Title and then, get this, I have a four corners match against Brock Newbludd, Jon Kano and then against 'Amazing' Mike Storm. James:Again? Now you must beat that low life Jon Kano and two others? You have a busy week ahead of you. Lance:I know, I gotta tone it down for a bit... I'm traveling the world now... Kyle:Are we there yet limo driver person sir? Driver:Um, no, we are not.. Kyle:Damn dude, how long is it going to take? JuJu:Man, I don't know why your so excited to get there! You know what's going to happen to you... but, I'm not going to speak of it anymore, I'll let take your own embarrassment. Lance:Yes, please stop, it's getting really annoying. (turns to Kyle) Don't worry Kyle, if they start to jump on you, we got your back, and when I say we got your back.. WE ALL, have your back, right JuJu? JuJu:Um.... (hesitates) y...yeah.. Driver:We are here! Lance:Good, ok guys, remember the mission, have fun and PCW... nothing other but those two ok? {::The limo driver pulls up to a reserved spot for visitors. He parks the limo and Lance and the crew step out of the limo. They look around at the building and they see the Hot 97 logo to the right of the brown looking building. They walk in as they are met by the visiting woman. She gets their names down and then, they are searched by security guards and then they are shown to where the morning show is being held. They look up and see the ON AIR logo in red. They walk in as the first one to see them is Miguel, the shows producer. He shows the five men to their seats as Star turns around and chuckles as Buckwild is in the back half asleep and Miss Jones is on her two way talking to some friends. They are on commercial, but a few seconds later they are off of commercial and they are back on the air....::} Star:This is Hot 97, This is the Star & Buckwild Show featuring MS.Jones. We have our guests for the day, PCW's wrestler named Lance Bell and his little hoes (laughter in the studio) Lets get this thing crackin my negro's..... I'm going to love this show today (smiles) {::As the camera pans around looking at the five friends they look a little bit afraid. Not knowing what they have just gotten themselves into. Everyone in the studio begins to laugh as Star seems like he is just warming up.... the scene fades as Lance begins to speak....::} To visit the real Hot 97, go to http://www.hot97.com
