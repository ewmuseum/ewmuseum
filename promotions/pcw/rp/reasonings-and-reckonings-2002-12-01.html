Reasonings and Reckonings

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec 1st, 2002 The asphalt dissappeared under the tires at an atonishing rate as the big Italian V-twin rang forth at a furious pitch. The saguaro and prickly pear flashed by like streaks of green in a sea of tan. Even in the month of December, Arizona's highways were prime territory for a relaxing 140 MPH romp on the Aprilia. Even though the temperatures were beginning to dip, a set of black and red heated leathers kept Kevin Sandusky warm as he tucked the 1000cc machine through the corners and wrenched the the throttle in the straights. Oh yeah. Kevin Sandusky. You'd know him better by the name on the personalized plate of 2002 Aprilia Mille. Ebola III He knows this desert and these roads like the back of his hand. He ought to, he was raised here. It was these same desert roads that his stepfather Jose made him run for miles and hours on end. Out amidst the diamondbacks and tarantulas, in the glaring eye of the sun, his stepfather relentlessly prepared him for his destiny. To show the world the true 'art' of Lucha Libre. Not that comedic parody that some people want you to believe is 'lucha'. Not the idiodic ramblings of the false icons whom seek a cheap pop and a quick buck by making jackasses out of themselves, but the artistry and tradition and honor that are part and parcel to being a true luchadore. Soon the entire world would see, the time of reckoning growing ever closer. But now even more could witness the true art, thanks to the checkbook and vision of some. Many had asked why Ebola III turned on his federation, why a man so commited to tradition could willingly see the traditions of an entire federation be destroyed. Why? Because RUSH more than any other had allowed the mockery of Lucha to happen. Because the fans of RUSH were so sold into the lies and deciet that they would not change. Since RUSH and it's fans didn't have perceptiveness or conscience to right their wrongs, then why should they be allowed to continue to exist. It all came down to a very simple question when the offer was made for Alliances. Would Ebola III be able to deliver final justice and retribution to the pretender whom called himself El Spiffy by destroying him and taking his title? From the moment Kannon nodded his head in approval, the path was set. El Spiffy would pay, the fans of RUSH would pay, the entire federation would pay for having allowed such a disgrace to have occured. Soon the Syndicate title's curse of derelict champions would be broken. Quo? El Spiffy? All they had ever done was make a joke of the Syndicate title. That would all be over soon. The time to talk and try to verbally sway the people had ended. Now all that remained was to end the disgrace that was El Spiffy. To beat him till he had no pride, embarrass him till he had no will, then take his title and leave him with nothing but a 'mask' of regret. The moment of reckoning grows closer with every mile that passes behind the black and red blur that speeds across the desert floor. Revenge and redemption on his mind, Kevin Sandusky squeezes the throttle even tighter.
