Date: 27-03-03 Again he found himself pacing back and forth across his hotel room. Again, Gynaeoco Joe was situated on the dresser table, troubled by Skitzo’s state of mind. With his sexual frustration quenched for now, and his trust in pornography shattered all that was left to do now was conquer the Young Tiger. He had been thinking for a while on how to defeat such an…adversary. “Pinning” him wasn’t an option, this one was feisty and he’d need a good beatdown to keep him down. In every match, every wrestler knows there’s a chance he or she might lose. How to combat this problem? One method would be to think like his opponent in order to be one step ahead, but imagining he was a whiny redskin with no talent didn’t appeal to him as much. Intimidation was the key. Skitzo could begin by blasting “Run To The Hills” by Iron Maiden into the arena and emerge as a gun-toting cowboy, scaring YT back into the reservation where he belonged. The only downside to that plan was insulting hundreds, maybe even thousands of Native Americans who one reason or another may be watching PCW television. That in itself didn’t bother Skitzo; he could take the abuse he dished out back, but when it was for the sake of this petty runt, well that didn’t sit well with Mr. Psycho. In the case that intimidation wouldn’t work, what else was there? Sure, Gynaeoco Joe and Skitzo’s 20” black rubber dildo would be on standby, in fact those two items were all he basically needed, as well as to simply show up. Friday would be a special day for Skitzo. It would be the day his name was added to the history books, as he ties with Dan Hollywood for the longest Extreme Title reign. To contemplate this sent shivers down Skitzo’s spine, for tying, and even breaking a record – once he obliterated Young Tiger – would be a great feat for someone such as Skitzo. Of course breaking a title reign record wasn’t much these days, but considering the Extreme Title had been whored around more than even the World Title during the New Breed run, this would be a feat worthy celebrating about. A whole 41 days he had kept the title for, and tomorrow would mark the 42nd. After that he’d be GUARANTEED to last 46 days as champion! How could he guarantee himself a victory on Fusion? It wasn’t enough to just waltz in and kick Young Tiger up the ass, swing the dildo and leave again; he had to have an advantage walking into Fusion, and thinking to himself as he watched Gynaeoco Joe floating in the fluids of his jar, he realised what it was. Dan Hollywood, the only man to do what he has done already, lost his title after 2 days…the answer was so simple it kicked Skitzo right in the ass! Skitzo: SWEET JESUS I’VE GOT TO GET A COLONIC! Or not…one more time. Skitzo: I’ve got to study Dan Hollywood’s extreme title reign! Much better. In order to study the title reign, or more importantly the match Hollywood lost, Skitzo had to first purchase a copy of PCW New Dawning 2001. Normally this would go off without a hitch and he could simply come back to his hotel room and review the tape, but this was Skitzo, he couldn’t do anything like any other normal person. Strolling down the street, he wore his cut-off denim shorts and a gator-skin waistcoat, no need for a shirt on a fine day such as today was. Stopping outside a local HMV, Skitzo went over in his mind buying the product and leaving, simple as that. In his state of sexuality he couldn’t afford any fuck ups, especially not this close to Fusion. Walking into the store Skitzo was greeted with the refreshing blast of warm air from the vents above the main entrance. The aisles themselves weren’t very busy, a couple of people around each genre of music picking out something new, while a middle-aged man flicked through some country albums and a couple of sneaky looking teenagers were quickly nabbed by security as they hid copies of various DVDs inside their jackets. Chuckling to himself, Skitzo headed for the VHS/DVD section to find what he was looking for. One of the receptionists and the two teenagers being carted away looked over at Skitzo, the teenagers wasting no time in addressing the Extreme Champion. Hey you Man-Dyke! Get da fuck out of here! Yeah mang, go kill chu’self, aiiiight! Skitzo: You two assholes are in enough trouble as it is! I hope you know how to squeal like a piggy boys, SOOOWEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!! The security guard chuckled at Skitzo and winked, before dragging the two boys into a back room, slamming it shut behind them. Skitzo proceeded to the VHS/DVD section, and began looking through the ‘Sports’ category, trying to find what he was looking for. Failing to see it, he moved on to ‘Wrestling’, where funnily enough he immediately found the PCW PPV and ‘Best Of’ tapes and DVDs listed. Looking through each one, he saw Supremacy, Decimation, Wrestle Vessel, March Madness, New Dawning, and Spring Fli-… Skitzo: EUREKA! I found it! Grabbing the New Dawning 2001 videocassette, Skitzo rushed back to the reception and slammed it on the desk where the receptionist took it and priced it, before looking at Skitzo with a glint in his eye. For a few moments that was all he did until Skitzo sighed and took action. Skitzo: Skippy, if you hurry up and sell me this, I’ll get you tickets to see Fusion… Immediately zapping out of his trance, the receptionist rang the video through the till and bagged it, handing it to Skitzo and smiling. Receptionist: $19.99 please… Forking over the cash, Skitzo took his purchase and rushed out the door, only to be followed by the receptionist, who leaned out of the store worried. Receptionist: WHAT ABOUT MY TICKET?! Skitzo turned back, having completely forgotten about his deal in the space of a few short minutes. Skitzo: JUST TELL THEM I INVITED YOU! The receptionist nodded to himself and went back into the store, as Skitzo jogged off up the street back to his hotel room. Leaving Gynaeoco Joe by himself for too long was a bad idea, especially in this warm weather. He may get too warm and be sick…as only a vagina in a jar can be. As he entered the hotel, the first thing he thought of was that while he had a video, he didn’t have a TV. After inquiring at the front desk, he was directed to the bar and entertainment room where a 21” TV and VCR sat in the corner, inviting him to stick the tape in. Sitting it on the TV, Skitzo suddenly remembered his precious vagina, and after a couple of minutes re-entered the room carrying Gynaeoco Joe under his waistcoat. Pulling a chair up to the TV, Skitzo inserted the video and watched as the main titles came up, and the action began! Dave Kern: Hello everyone and welcome to Primetime Championship Wrestling’s special Pay Per View telecast, NEW DAWNING!!! I am Dave Kern, and I am joined, as always at my announce position by my partners in crime, Steven Smith and Jeff Marx!!! We are coming to you live from the jam-packed TD Waterhouse Arena in Orlando Florida, and ladies and gentleman, tonight will usher in a new era in PCW history. Looking at the video case, he spotted the Extreme match around halfway down the card listed, and began fast-forwarding, passing Doug Troy and Macaber Payne, the TLC Tag Title match with NAS, Flames Of The Earth, HIT and Nuke, Boda and Morozov, The Red Army and Da Westsidaz…past the few promos until finally he arrived at the match he was looking for… Dave: Yes, we have millions of Spanish-speaking fans in the Americas and Europe....Anyway, we all just witnessed Nikita Morozov successfully defend the Rising Star Title and a clash between sHs and The Red Army. Unfortunately for Morozov, this defense is said to be his last. Due to a rule violation, Morozov is no longer allowed to hold the Rising Star Title and it is now vacant. Jeff: Let's just hope it's not claimed again. Rookies suck. Dave: I'm sure it will be. We have many hungry, young talents ready for a title. Alright, folks, up next we have our Triple Threat Extreme Title Match!!! Skitzo: Finally, time to see how NOT to lose the title! Watching on as Dan Hollywood battled his adversaries in JJ and Flaymin Aymin, Skitzo paid careful attention, noting down the particulars of the match. Skitzo and Gynaeoco Joe watched as Flaymin Aymin torched his opponents, Hollywood brandished his bronze award statue menacingly, and all of a sudden people begin interfering! Skitzo: FUCKING TYLER NELSON! Why can’t he just direct his troops back up his ass in the locker room?! Gynaeoco Joe bubbled quietly. Skitzo: Yes Joe, the interference in this match took away the spectacle of the use of weaponry and ingenuity of the champion who is looking strong even as the interference-… Hollywood, in desperation, tries to grab Aymin from behind, but the Australian pyromaniac quickly turns around SCORCHING THE HOLLYWOOD NATIVE IN THE FACE!!! Buffy screams hysterically from the outside. She climbs into the ring, and embraces the burnt skin of Hollywood. Dave: Get out of there Buffy! Aymin lets loose another BARRAGE OF FLAMES PUTTING VEGAS’S HAIR ON FIRE!!! Skitzo: Ok never mind… Skitzo found himself ducking and duking, throwing shadow punches at the television, taking in every ounce of detail as pinfalls were broken as the end of the match approached! Hollywood grabs Johnson by the hair, and ushers him to the floor with a HEADLINER!!! Hollywood covers….1…..2….SAVE BY AYMIN!!! Jeff: Who’s that coming down the aisle!?!? Dave: Buffy!!! Steven: The Vampire Slayer? Dave: No!!! Buffy Vegas!!! Buffy, with her head bandaged, runs down the aisle wielding a fire extinguisher! She slides into the ring and SPRAYS AYMIN WITH IT!!! Crowd: Buff-EEE!!! Buff-EEE!!! Aymin staggers back to the corner, covering his eyes with his hands! Hollywood seizes the opportunity, kicking Aymin in the gut and setting him up for a Planet Hollywood! Meanwhile. Johnson gets to his feet, sees the pin and sprints towards Hollywood. Steven: GO JJ!!! Buffy jumps up on the apron with a fire extinguisher and let’s loose with some spray towards Johnson. Steven: DUCK!!! Buffy let’s loose with the extinguisher, by Johnson ducks leaving Hollywood to be SPRAYED BY VEGAS WITH THE FIRE EXTINGUISHER!!! HE DROPS TO THE CANVAS COVERING HIS EYES AND AYMIN WHO HE WAS HOLDING FALLS ON TOP OF HIM! The ref counts…..1……2…..3!!! Winner and new PCW Extreme Champion: FLAYMIN AYMIN!!! Skitzo paused the video and rewound it, looking back and watching as Jeremy Johnson sprayed Dan Hollywood, causing Aymin to fall on top of him for the three count. He watched the footage again and again, and with an expressionless look on his face, turned the TV off, removed the videotape and turned around…taking Joe with him as he walked out of the room and into the front lobby of the hotel. From there he walked out into the street, and stood on the curb, looking at the traffic as it rushed past. Joe began bubbling, obviously distressed. Skitzo: A fire extinguisher… Another car passed and Joe bubbled again, more profusely. Skitzo: A kid confused about his own sexuality and a pyro Aussie… An 18-wheeler passed this time, rumbling along as Joe bubbled furiously at Skitzo. Skitzo: FOR $19.99?!?!?! Skitzo cried out, throwing the videotape into the middle of the road and watching as a little girl riding her pink tricycle rather innocently passed by, running over the videotape and barely making contact when… KABOOM!!!!!!! The cassette exploded and the little girl flew up into the air in a cloud of ashes and blonde hair and ribbons, finally combusting in a shower of blood and hitting the street again in the form of little red raindrops. Gynaeoco Joe was speechless, and then bubbled once more. Skitzo: No I will not taste the rainbow! You sicko! I paid $20 to get tips on how to retain my fucking title from that squaw and what do I get?! Flaymin Aymin and a fire extinguisher and a gay rentboy! NO WONDER HOLLYWOOD LOST!!! After ALL of that trouble I get absolutely nowhere, and now I owe that kid tickets to Fusion, I need to prepare for my match, and………pick up my thong from the dry cleaners! Gynaeoco Joe bubbled continuously for two minutes. Skitzo: You’re right Joe, as long as I’ve got my assertiveness, my good looks, my wrestling ability…and a 20” black rubber dildo I’ll be just fine! And so, after wasting a day for nothing on the road to yet another defence, Skitzo retreated back into his hotel room, pissed off but ready, ready to keep what was his and look good doing it! Time for the shit to hit the fan as Skitzo vs. Young Tiger rolled right around the corner! The End
