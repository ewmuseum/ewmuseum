Schizophrenic Couchmania

Date: AKA Trying To Be Skitzo And Sucking At It “Ack! We’ve got a match against Skitzo!” The voice of Rush Mason opens this promo, which is very much different from your run of the mill promo. It’s still a dumb spoof, but it’s a different type of dumb spoof. “Skitzo?” The voice that answers back is also Rush’s, and this simple, yet stupid spoof, takes place with Rush just sitting on a couch at home, icing his knee, propped up on the couch. He seems to be….uhh….talking to himself. “That stupid ass schizophrenic?” “HEY! Not nice, we’re a nice person!” “Not you again! You’re the one who got us in trouble, it was YOU that made us lose that match on Adrenaline!” “Non, cela m'était! Je suis desolèe.” “Who let the French guy in, he’s stinking things up!” “It came with the whole Voodoo bit that this idiot picked up.” “Not cool man. I don’t dish on you guys just because you’re all Cthulu followers or-” “Scientologists!” “WHAT?! One of you is a Scientologist?!” “It was him!” “Me?! I’m the one who always gets blamed!” Rush’s left hand swings at his face, but his right hand blocks, his face changing emotion with every statement. “Please, guys, violence isn’t the answer.” “Oh great, that pacifist bitch is here.” “C’mon, hol’ me back, hol’ me back!” “Bring it you little woman, I’ll take you any day!” “Those two are at it again?” “When are they not?” “Leur lutte ennuie tout à fait.” “Hey Frenchie, scram and take a shower. You’re not wanted.” “Aimeriez-vous un smackdown français, biatch?” “Ever heard of the French-Indian war where we destroyed your people? I’ll do it again.” “Peut-être je ferai seulement a mis une malédiction de Vaudou sur votre famille!” “What did Frenchish man say?” “You shut your damn mouth about my mother!” “NEVER! She’s a dirty tramp!” “Uhh…he said something about putting a Voodoo curse on my family…” “My family is your family, or did you forget that we’re all stuck in this one body, Napolean? “Qui allez-vous appeler? Ghostbusters!” “Please, stop fighting! It hurts me!” “Is he singing the Ghostbusters song?” “Yeah that’s right, I called your mom a dirty tramp!” “Only as dirty as that filthy twat you came out of!” “I’m huuuuungry.” “Shut up, fatty.” “We’re in the same body, and therefore the same size, dumbass!” “This yelling is hurting my ears!” “I’m about to hurt that bitch over there, except not just in his ears!” “Oh bring it you coke-sniffing maggot!”“Who told about the coke?! It was just one time when I was in Miami and I was young!” “Ce jeu de rôles muet a continué assez long, n'est-ce pas?” With that, Rush takes a very deep breath, leans back, picks up a magazine and remarks to no one in particular as the scene fades to black… “So, we’ve got a match against Skitzo?”
