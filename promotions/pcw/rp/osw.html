Date: RESULTS TO MALL BRAWL RIGHT HERE!!!! 8:00 PM, eastern. Friday, the 18th of October, 2002. Grandberry Mall, Tokyo, Japan. The 3rd annual PCW Mall Brawl will take place here, tonight. In fact… Right now. The scenes you are about to see are the future as we are to know it. What happens here, will happen. Lest someone might be able to change the influxes of time, this will be YOUR fate. Veiled in shadow, a faceless silhouette melts into the leather exterior of his comfy chair, gazing upon the dim glow of a monitor before him. The frilly cat wound into a purring coil upon his lap rouses as he strokes it ever so gently with the back of his fingers over its crown. His hand stops as he softly hushes the feline, “Shh, now Bobo…” And as his cat settles back into a relaxed position, the shadow chortles menacingly under his breath, continuing, “Soon, Bobo… Soon, they’ll be mine.” Piercing the black, his eye emits an infrared glow the takes aim upon the animated monitor… TV: “And things look ready to go…” We cut to within the large Japanese mega-mall… Dave Kern: “Looks as though the P-C-W officials have taken their positions, and are ready to let this pot boil!” Jeff Marx: “…Violence! Bloodshed! Jobbers getting marauded! I love it!” Having taken their respectful rolls, the P-C-W commentary team prepares to oversee the spectacular event from a safe locale. They hit the screen, watching their monitors, Dave suddenly turning to the camera, and giving a big thumbs up. DK: “Ready to roll!” And, thusly the scene cuts right to the mall again. Herded into agonizingly close quarters, the battle ready roster awaits the official word to take onslaught to a new level. Officials nervously move from competitor to competitor, questioning how much longer they can control the fierce warriors revved up to capture the P-C-W Continental title… “Psst! Hey! Meacham!” The respectable head officiate of P-C-W twists his attention to a large potted tree, diverted from the situation at hand… Cut to a circle of roster members surrounding the End, shoulder to shoulder, trapping the blind man. He scratches his head, and takes slow steps towards… Snow: “WATCH WHERE YER GOING, BAT BOY!!!” The Chillin’ Villain shoves the End back, a sly grin spreading across his face. As the End catches his stance, finding balance, he shrugs it off. End: “Sorry…” Another shove sprawls the End to the floor, El Diablo trying to contain his laughter. Cut back to Ref Meacham, whom has been hooked by a whisper from the decorative bushes… Ref M: “Who’s there?!” “Psst! Over here…” Meacham shifts from the bushes, to the marble pillar not too far off. Blindly, he is lured closer, until he locks his sights onto a figure in a trench coat, leaning casually against the structure. Motioning for the Ref to come closer, the mystery man lifts the wide brim of his hat, revealing a shadow cast smirk. Ref M: “…Er-” ???: “My intentions are quite clear. This column here doesn’t seem sturdy enough to hold its own… maybe you should take a look.” Confused, but not about to abandon proper safety precautions, even before such a brutal match as this, Meacham inches his way towards the stranger until… CRACK! …As in, Ref Meacham is CRACKed across the back of his head with a bat. Smugly, the Goblin snickers, removing his hat, and dropping it to the floor. With a twirl, he removes the coat, as though a cape. Syko the Goblin: “Muwahahah! How utterly foolish can one be? Far too easy.” Spinning the worn old baseball bat in her hands, Esther nods agreeably at a job well done. Wait… Kicks Meacham in the side. He doesn’t move. Job well done. Syko the Goblin: “Now, where’s LAB?” “(Sigh.) Coming, Zire…” Emerging from behind another pillar, and trudging into view, LAB comes to the Goblin’s side, disguised as a P-C-W Referee. The old man glares at Esther, who shakes her head in silence at him, fighting a smirk. LAB: “Vy couldn’t she do zis?!” Syko the Goblin: “BAH! Nobody would fall for it! Women cannot be trusted as officials!” LAB: “But!” Syko the Goblin: “Keep it down! We mustn’t give wind of our coo…” WHOOSH! “LORD SYKO! I HAVE RE-” Syko, Esther, and LAB: “(Holding fingers to mouth.) SHHHHHH~!” Hushed into silence, a newly arrived ninja shamefully drops to one knee, bowing his head. Ninja #16: “(Whispering.) A thousand apologies, master!” Syko the Goblin: “Hmph. Status report.” The ninja, who seems to have just entwined with the scene from nowhere nods, and pulls out a rolled up scroll, promptly pressing it to the marble floor, and flattening it out. A blueprint of the mall… Ninja #16: “My superiors have mapped the best possible route to overtake the enemy forces one by one…” From up on the 2nd level balcony, a native guard spies on this villainous group suspiciously through binoculars. Suddenly, his walkie-talkie crackles to life, diverting his hawk eye view. “(Something in Japanese.)”Translation: “Shigetsu, are we supposed to get shipments today?” Quirking his brow, the guard unsheathes the walkie-talkie from his belt, responding… Shigetsu(Guard): (Something in Japanese.)Translation: “Hm? No, all shipments were cancelled… The mall is under total lockdown.” The guard looks through the binoculars again, the pack of odd balls still camped out of view. Guard: Translation: “I have discovered what I believe to be a pack of American roadie tourist that have intruded the mall. I might need some help.” WT: Translation: “I need you here. A shipping truck arrived, and they are refusing to… GAHHH!” The communication device reverting to noisy static, the guard quickly forgets about the villains, as he sprints off to the unknown problem at hand… Syko the Goblin: “…And the rest of the clan is lying in wait for the ambush, right?” The lone messenger of the clan looks to the floor, sheepishly. Cut to designer clothing store… PENETRATED! Sensei: “Stuff your bags to the brim!” We cut back from the Suburban Ninja Clan looting, to Syko’s demanding glare. Ninja #16: “Yeah… something like that.” Syko the Goblin: “Excellent… We must be stealth now, for the prize is too great to allow any mistakes.” And then, the bell rings. Dave: “AND THIS MALL BRAWL MATCH IS UNDERWAY!” TOTAL CHAOS ENSUES IN THE MAIN FOYER OF THE MALL! Jeff: “WHICH JOBBER WILL GET PICKED OFF FIRST?!” Scratch chaos. Total unity is more like it. As in, the entire roster unites in a dog pile onto Kannon!!! 123! XAVIER KANNON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!!!! NOW TOTAL CHAOS ENSUES!! --- Dave: “My God, I think this may be the best Mall Brawl to date!” Jeff: “I hate the world.” Whilst all the hand to hand warfare rages throughout the mall, a groggy Scorpion stays to where he perceives to be seclusion to catch a breather… Wiping the sweat, and blood from his brow, he spots a bench. A bench which doubles as four benches, and a large potting point for a small tree. Scorpion: “Yesh!” Assuming he’s all alone, the unsuspecting low-carder plops down on the bench with a heaving sigh. “Step into my parlor said the spider to the fly…” Scorpion: “Huh? GAH!” The young P-C-W upstart is startled, but his reaction time is too slow to avoid a pair of hands… So, he just grabs the hands, and yanks the small assailant from the vegetation that fills the soil! The maniacal master of trickery is unveiled, and launched fiercely into the solid marble floor!! “ZERE’S ZE PIN!!” LAB scrambles out from the greenery behind Syko, his Ref disguise serving it’s purpose well enough. Excited by his awesome luck, Scorpion pounces upon the leveled Goblin, making a sloppy cover… The old man drops to make the count. 1 2 TASER TO SCORPION’S NECK! The evil Doctor nudges Scorpion’s body with his toe, flipping him off. LAB: “(Sighing.) Ze first time ve didn’t have to retreat during zis match…” Syko the Goblin: “Victory… is… mine…” Meanwhile… Dave: “So much devastation, thus far. I’m sure if there was a crowd, BladeD would be receiving a standing ovation ri-…” Jeff: “JOBBER.” Dave: “But he held his ground against 5 men for half an hour!” Jeff: “AND?!” Dave: “(Sigh.)” On the metaphorical battlefield, the number in this match has drastically plummeted to those hell bent on survival. Skitzo: “DIE!!” Skitzo: “NEVER!!” The split minded mishap struggles against his own hands that constrict around his neck, falling to one knee… Finally, his eyes bulge and a roll back, and he limply falls to the ground… High above, Duke whateverhisnameis dangles with one grasping hand hooked over the railing of the 2nd balcony. Smiling down upon him, the cynical shade of green, Gaz Green that is, offers his hand. Duke falls for the act of mercy, shaking hands with a mousetrap before falling freely into a pretzel cart below. The End blind walks into him, and they both tumble over the edge, The End landing on top of both Duke and the Jester. 123. His fall cushioned, the End rises from the wreckage, casually brushing himself off, before strutting away. The match continues… “Garbage can want garbage.” …An empty corridor of the mall? “Shh…” “Don’t shush me!” A filthy trashcan hops up, and slowly tiptoes… FILTHY TRASHCAN?! Two glinting orbs within the can scan the area thoroughly. Trashcan: “The trap is set, now to spring…” A clamor of hasty feet rushing towards the can causes it to drop dead, and lie in still wait. Trashcan: “Down! Someone’s coming!” “SHH!” “STOP THAT!” “SHHH!” The can trembles, rocking to and fro quite noticeably. But, that doesn’t stop the stampede of cloaked figures who pass by, knocking over the can in their rush. The can hits the ground rolling, and Syko along with his trusty assistant LAB spill out. The Goblin mounts his ally, shaking him vigorously by the colar. Syko the Goblin: “WE HAD THEM DAMMIT!” LAB: “Who vere zey?!” Syko the Goblin: “WE HAD… …I don’t know…” LAB: “Zen shall ve follow?” Syko the Goblin: “I like your way of thinking.” Dismounting, Syko helps old LAB up, who turns to follow… Syko the Goblin: “WHERE do you think you’re going?” LAB: “To foll-” Syko points to the tumbled trashcan, a stern look chiseled upon his demanding face. Back where the real action is… Dave: “Who in the…?” Those remaining in the competition find themselves stopping abruptly, turning to see these veiled newcomers, letting their fists fall, and their brows quirk. 25 at least, stand before the 15 or less still standing in this match. Snow: “Who are…-” As the tallest stepped forward, his beaming eyes shining from deep within his hood, he bellowed, “What is anything?” Sliding his hood back slowly, he grinned as the eyes of those before him widened. “You will learn whom it is we are, for we are your enemies. We are O-S-W.” Everyone goes blank. End: “What?” Following in the skull masked one’s footsteps, she came forward, unmasking her shell, “We are OSW.” She smiled, letting her red hair dangle freely. Darkstar: “Is it just me… or are these weirdoes jabbering like a pack of unshaven terrorist?” “Silence!” he called fourth, pulling out a- Chef: “Repeat that please?” As if a fish caught at sea, the Chef is caught within a projected net, “Ha!” snorted the assailant. Confused as they are, the random jabber too unsolvable a riddle to decipher now, the P-C-W boys and girls still know to pull off the gloves. Violence Jack: “ARE WE GUNNA LET SOME LOSERS FROM O-S-W PUSH US AROUND?!” -5 minutes later.- Well, with all the P-C-W boys and girls properly restrained in cuffs, and those whom were eliminated earlier, and forced to leave now being herded back in under similar circumstances, it’d seem this new force O-S-W has won. “Yes, we have taken the ‘ratings.’” He said without emotion into the cell phone. “Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da. Da.” He continues… “P-C-W is no more.” Coming to his side, a delicate woman takes his hand, “We have completed the objective.” “Yes… err… Delicate Woman, we almost have. But, first a head count.” He replies. Also, he is stern. And he replied. “Tempest.” She hisses. “And we have everyone except for one.” Well, their dialect makes no sense to us normal people, but… Their intentions: very clear. The small woman moves over to a trashcan, and kicks it over. Spewing some of her illiterate rhetoric, she tugs out the unconscious form of… Scorpion. “Da. That is all then.” He says, and he is like a robot. A red one. Winner: ………..O-S-W?
