Date: 17-09-02 Loneliness… is it pain? Is it anguish? Is it torment? Does it rip you up inside and then compressed you into nothing but a speck? Is loneliness an emotion or just an excuse for something you want to hide? It is doubtful anyone can answer such questions. Though loneliness is not usually alone when it is eating away at one’s mind, soul and heart. It is usually accompanied by such things as; depression, desire, pain, fear and… boredom. Friday Fusion saw Jamie Celt’s first win in PCW. Due to the unfinished bout he had with Trigger on Turmoil, you could considerate to be his debut. He fought one Cecelia Verucana. Verucana was a great competitor to say the least. She had taken some of the very best in PCW to their knees. Jamie stepped into the ring with her, knowing very well what he was in for. He battled and battled then finally, it looked like he had it won. Though fate would once more discontinue Jamie’s luck. Teresa Tomas came down to the ring, and repeatedly slapped Jamie with leather belt, before rapping in round his throat and choking him. Jamie won the match due to disqualification, but the damage had been done. Teresa then said she did this for one reason… because she wanted to win the number one contender’s match for the TV title on Turmoil, and she would not let anyone stand in her way. Though, the mistake on her part was… it just made Jamie want it more. The sun shined through into the large room from the balcony on the right side of the room. Jamie sat facing north. His eyes were locked in the television in front of him, yet his mind was not. The room he was in was large; it doubled as a main living room, and a kitchen, though the kitchen was sprawled away in the southwest wing of the room. The room was currently clean, though Jamie had only recently checked into the apartment, and whether it would stay that way would be questionable. Jamie wore nothing but a dark green dressing robe and a pair of blue slippers. His hair was messy, and his eyes seemed droopy. It was easy to tell he was just out of bed. The time was 8:17 am. Jamie was in his hotel suite in the heart of Milwaukee. He had just turned on the television, the television automatically went onto CNN, and Jamie watched, but did not taken in, the current events happening around the world. Jamie tilts his head, trying to get into the program. Though the thoughts that consume his mind overpower him, to the extent that he gets aggravated and picks up the remote control in one swift movement. He then automatically presses the ‘mute’ button and all sound in the room now ceases. He throws the remote over on the others side of the brown, cotton couch. Jamie lifts his arm and puts them behind his head. He looks up at the ceiling and engages in deep thought. To an extent, he misses life in Boston; he missed his friends, his life style. He wanted to do something about it but he could never leave his love of wrestling and PCW. PCW was his big break and he just could not screw that up. Even when he won the NTICW World Title it was not good enough for the critics, though now in PCW he has a chance to really make a name for himself. To shine, and that could start on Tuesday at PCW Turmoil. He would be involved in a 4-way match, and the winner would receive a shot at the PCW TV Title. It was the break Jamie needed, and he just could not afford to let it go by. Teresa, Tomas, Flaymin Aymin, and the mighty Venneman Pendragon. These three opponents would not be ones Jamie would ask for if he had a chance like this, though no matter the challenge, Jamie had to try. He had studied all three of his opponents down to the last detail. He learnt their styles, from Teresa’s highflying ability to Pendragon’s powerhouse style. He did not feel confident about his match, though he felt prepared, which is all he could possibly ask from himself. Though, a lot more was on Jamie’s mind. He still felt the urge to return to Boston. He wanted the thrill of gang wars, and saving the innocent again, it was such an adrenaline rush. He also wanted to be around his friend’s and family again. Though he knew he would have very little time for such things. As he continued to look at the ceiling he awoke from his trance. He then slowly got up from his couch, then stretched himself, and yawned. He went slowly out into his balcony; the door to it was already opened. He looked down to the busy streets of Milwaukee. Sounds echoed through his ears of everyday life. People in suits went to work as cars were tooting their horns loudly, at the insensitivity and immoral values of others. There was so much on these streets, things that should be sorted out. Jamie thought tiredly… “If the Celtics were in places like this, it would be kept in order, no crime, no immoral, and no one to boss civilians around like slaves…” a thought then entered Jamie’s head. It was a thought that immediately brought a smile to his face. He left the balcony and went back in to the comfort of his hotel suite. Though he did not stop at the couch. He went straight of the right corner side of the room beside the door. There stood a small, nimble, wooden table with nothing but a red, stylish phone on it. Jamie picked the phone up and began to dial numbers at a rate you would think only touch-typers could go. He waited for several seconds as the phone dialled. An anxious, yet excited look covered his face. “Hello.” Jamie said into the phone. “Jamie?” Came the voice from the other line. “Yeah Dad.” “Hey Son!” Jamie’s father, Anthony said loudly. “Look Dad, I have a great proposition for you, one you are sure to be interested in.” “Go on…” His father said curiously. “You know how you always wanted to expand the Celtics, right?” Jamie said with excitement in his voice. “Yeah.” Anthony replies still curious. “Well, I thought, if I took a few of the guys with me around on my PCW travels then we could go from city to city expanding the gang, making new allies and helping people out.” Jamie said with a great finale like sound at the finish. “What a great idea Jamie…” As the conversation dragged on Jamie finally felt like things were on the up an up for him, on all fronts. Though all his attention would have to be focused on his three opponents at Turmoil, for a name is nothing without glory.
