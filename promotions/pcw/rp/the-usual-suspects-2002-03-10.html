The Usual Suspects

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/02 OOC Note: Err…*waves*…(sighs) I’ve been playing way too much Metal Gear Solid 2…that and I’ve had Exams all week… The World Conquest Chapters… RP#1 for Sabotage and #1 for Fusion… Location:A Police Department/Wherever. Time/Date: 16:00/ 9/10th March 2002. Weather: Not known due to interior location. Episode Two: The Usual Suspects… “I’m INNOCENT COMRADE! I tell you…Shalashaka is innocent…how many times to I have to tell you this, you freakish Moronic thug! I tell you…Liquid will hear about this, LIQUID!!” “Ah Shaddup…Damn Revolver Ocelot, tell that one to someone who isn’t a blindingly awesome moron. Now then guys, lock this cretinous Russian felon up…and we can have the Identity parade for those Chris Sloboda assault incidents on Sunday and Friday. Yeah, and fetch me some donuts too!” “Yes Sir…err…Mister Downey. Introducing the suspects for the Chris Sloboda incident: Shaq~! Mark Malloy, Marky Mark the Midget Mark, Essa Rios and ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase! Please stand up against that chart, NOW!” “Que?” “Quiet Rios…you’re lucky I haven’t brought you in for inproper use of the Spanish Language!” “Que?” ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ Holed up ever since I decided that beating some sense into an unentertaining cretin at the very end of March Madness and Friday Fusion, holed up in a claustrophobic Police Cell…awaiting the punishment for the supposed “assault charges” placed upon me by an anonymous benefactor. Not Boda…that’s for sure, heck, Boda wouldn’t be smart enough to keep me away from him in that fashion…no, not at all. After being eliminated at fourth place in the March Madness World Title match, by the aforementioned Boda…I watched my long time arch-nemesis win the PCW World Title without him actually lifting a finger other than to eliminate yours truly. The Jackass. Of course…that’s enough reason to give him a good couple of shots around the head with a handily placed Steel Chair…before staring the coward down after being forced from the ring by his cohorts. And to make things ever increasingly worse at that time, by the end of Wednesday I find myself suddenly stripped of my PCW Cruiserweight Title…thanks to said Boda-Boy. Come Friday Fusion, despite his World Title loss being humiliating enough, Boda and new World Champion Miyagawa found themselves taken aback by the sudden outburst from New Breed, as Corey and I pummel the two former leaders of two now defunct stables off into the void of oblivion. I can’t say that Boda didn’t deserve at least that…sure, I know he’s the Uber- Big Boda Daddy now, but that’s not going to stop ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase from exacting some revenge on a man who reigned for three days, THREE DAYS! Subsequently, I find myself arrested by a police force led by a turncoat idiot…and here I am…finding myself subjected to an inane identity parade… So, in order to get to that match tommorrow night, I’ll need to find some way out of here…or at least hope for a minimal or Public service type punishment…so I can go about the single-handed destruction of my opponents, and the rebuilding of New Breed…short-handed after I accidentally eliminated Jason Snow from the Madness Battle Royal…sealing his fated as a doomed World Champion….:: Dustin: ~(Yelling)~ Alright Jenkins, bring in the witnesses from Sunday night’s incident…NO…I don’t WANT a PLAIN Donut! I want one of those Puff pastry type thingies with the jam filling… Essa Rios: Que? Que? QUE? Dustin: ~(Pulling out a handgun of some form)~ Quiet you! You Stinking Mexican Cruiserweight! Don’t you have any knowing of your right to remain SILENT? Jeez…and people call me dumb… ::. ~(Normal Narration)~ As the Title Credits roll and Matthew Good Band’s ‘Under the Influence’ begins to schizophrenically blare…we’re introduced rather abruptly to a scene deep within the bowels of a detainment center in Saint Louis, Missouri…the setting for the gone and finished PCW Pay-Per-View, March Madness. And, as an angry Russian madman with a transplanted arm is ushered away by a pair of hat wearing men in blue…we find a quintet of hand-cuffed individuals being led to stand in front of a height scale that takes up the majority of the wall…of course, you’ll have seen one of those before. And, with a frustrated expression on his face, stands ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase…a supposed ‘Asshole’…~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ HEY, YOU CAN’T SAY THAT!…~(Normal Narration)~ Alright… ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, an arrogant young Canadian with a conceited streak…dressed in convict clothing. He’s frowning at his captor… ‘Deadly’ Dustin Downey, who also just happens to be his own road agent and former Tag Partner. Dustin leans against a wall with a blissfully ignorant smirk on his face as he spots a pair of witnesses being led to their booth… Pushing himself off the wall…Dustin greets these two with open arms…one being Ivor Borischev (Russian Madman who had captured and Tortured Chris during the PCW Euro Tour, now Talkshow host) and Sho Funaki (One half of the World’s Greatest Tag Team *cough* Kaientai!)…both of which have been associated with ‘The Messiah’ in the last 9 months….:: Dustin: Welcome Misters Borischev and Funaki…to the Identification Parade of the suspects for the Boda Fiasco on Sunday night’s March Madness. Now, with fair warning…each of these felons have motives for attacking Boda…so thus, you’re gonna have to decide very carefully who attacked Chris Sloboda on the night of March 3rd… Funaki: inDEED~! ::. As Funaki’s moronic catchphrase lives up to its moronic label…Borischev, the Muscle-bound yet poorly adapted to combat Russian, slaps himself in the forehead, gesticulating slowly yet with purpose as he explains the torture that he’s obviously been through as the fed up expression on his face tells….:: Borischev: ~(Slaps self in forehead)~ Argh…Downey, do you realise how many times I’ve had to hear THAT today? Well…I’ll tell you American…TOO MANY DAMN TIMES! Why Mother Russia would crumble even more if these irritating Japanese folk overrun our wonderful if cheap country. How do I stop this idiot? ::. With a moronic smirk…Dustin shrugs…turning his back to both the big Russian and the Diminutive Jap….:: Dustin: Try the “Off” switch…you know these crazy Japs, they’re full of gadgetry…unless you’ve already tried that. In that case, try to gag and bind him to a wall or something…and we’ll charge him for misconduct of using the word ‘Indeed’… Funaki: No ‘Indeed’…inDEED~! Dustin: Gah…well…never mind. Would you two please take a long inspection of the felons, and identify the assailant? Err…I mean now, so you two take a close look at these five guys… ::. And with those last few words, Dustin turns away from the pair of ‘witnesses’, grinning moronically as he takes his position beside a large crate of jam-filled donuts. He lifts one up to his mouth, pausing as he watches Funaki and Borischev pace in front of the five suspects, frowning as their eyes pass over each, each of whom standing as close to the wall as possible. Yet, at the right hand end, there stands a frowning Chris Chase…almost looking as if he’s trying to convict himself. His sharp brown eyes, those cocky eyes that constantly flitter when he makes those confident witticisms of his, look from side to side…probing the room like a ‘Grey’ to an alien abductee. Borischev’s hulking shape blocks the camera at this point, as he rubs his chin…eyeing Chris with little respect at all. Of course, you’ll remember that Chris twice fooled this big Russian…and maybe this’ll be third time unlucky for ‘The Messiah’…or maybe Dustin’ll cut into a song and dance act just to please us fans – which in any case would get downright irritating after half a minute of it….:: Borischev: Comrade Downey, I think we’ve found our felon…the person who despises Boda, who unwittingly caused the collapse of the Snapple franchise in Canada… ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~Of course, we couldn’t go any longer without a rant from some young Canadian Ego-maniac, an egomaniac with a dream. The unknowing hero of millions by punishing a lowlife for his deeds against humanity and Tim Walden. The handcuffs bite into my wrists as I struggle to wrench them off, whilst being berated by the witnesses sent to condemn me to a life of repaying Boda for all those superficial ‘sins’ that I’ve committed against him. There is a cold breeze wafting through the room as I suddenly return from the depths of self-despair…bridging the gap left after the whole incident on Fusion….:: Chris: Go me! No, really…let ‘GO’ of ‘ME’! Can’t you see that this is some moronic rouse to keep me from obliterating Sloboda on Sunday? Dustin! You sold me out! You sold New Breed out! Where exactly is Corey anyhow? ::. Pulling himself from his donuts, Dustin swaggers over to my position, as my muscles strain as I try to free myself from the shackles that subdue my angry streak….:: Dustin: So…it was you after all, Chris? Well, you couldn’t fool me…for I’m Downey…SUPERCOP! Chris: Look, obviously you’re having a Badge moment Dustin…it’s valid, given the fact that you’ve also wanted to be a Hardcore Legend, a Pirate and a Country bumpkin. Now, as far as this whole Boda-Boy assault goes…I admit it, I punked him out…TWICE! But who can blame me…he’s going around saying that my win over him was tainted…back in PCW2, yet he can’t seem to remember the fact that he needed YOU to beat me! So Dustin…set me free... ::. With a discerning eye, Dustin stares at me, directly to my face…glaring at me as if I’d somehow become some moronic billboard advertisement that he’d suddenly become undoubtedly partial to. Borischev crosses his arms in a huff…shaking his head as Funaki taps him on the shoulder. I watch, as Borischev subsequently sends the little Japanese man through the nearest window with a first class ticket…snorting typically as Dustin walks ever closer to me. The Breath, I can smell the breath of Dustin as he gets closer…the breath that smells like a rather strange mix of funnel cakes and shaving foam…Ugh! And as if this whole thing had become quite pedestrian in the last two seconds, it takes a different turn as Downey gives me a strange look…before slapping me on my wrist….:: Dustin: Alright you…I hope you’ve learnt your lesson. Now then, be a good little Supposed Saviour and smack me around the head so I can get back to being the normal Dustin… ::. Of course, when opportunities such as these arise, I must be obliged to take advantage of them. With a subtle flick of the wrist, I wallop Dustin over the back of the head, sending him down to his knees, and then into a crumpled heap on the concrete floor, as the scene gradually begins to fade out… The last few days are now a blur for me…ever since I smacked Dustin around the back of the head to bring him out of that crazed state of his…I can’t say that I can remember anything before that moment…or at least between that moment and the end of PCW Friday Fusion. Sitting upon a park bench in an unknown small country town, I sigh as I view the rather pedestrian world around me. The cliché of people going about their usual daily routine is ever apparent here, their blur diffuses in front of my static position viewing the park ahead of me. There, Dustin Downey is slightly out of place…wielding a baseball bat in a term of Little Leaguers, waiting for the pitch. It comes, and the whiffle ball used smacks the former PCW2 Cruiserweight Champion in the face for the eighteenth time this afternoon. He howls and drops to the floor as the boys that make up the rest of the game walk away shaking their heads… Taking the swift opportunity to eke my way through commuters of all sorts, I find myself walking onto the green and actually helping my former Tag Partner to his feet…before smacking myself delirious after figuring out that I was actually being a good guy for a change. Have no fear, I hear I’m still an asshole in 68 countries…so that leaked on your damn bonfire, didn’t it? Damn cretins, you think you know it all, don’t you? Well, you’ll never quite be as subtly brilliant as… ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase…I mean, who had the guts to take on a seven footer? And who beat said Seven footer to a pulp? I rest my case your honour…CASE DISMISSED! And yet another aptly used witticism goes to Chris Chase….:: Chris: You never did get over your fear of whiffle balls, did you Dustin? Damn, you’ve actually brought me into a near-flashback state…accursed Dustin. You know, I hate it when that happens… Dustin: Eh? Flashbacks make me queasy, so why on earth would I want to bring back memories of promotions past. Besides, that was ‘Mr. Hardcore’ Dustin Downey…a hardcore legend in every respect. Why, I’m a proverbial Ed… ::. That is all well and good…until you really have to point out the obvious….:: Chris: Dustin you cretinous fool…Ed never wore Hot Pants on his head! Dustin: So that’s why I haven’t been able to hit even one ball yet! Thanks Chris… Chris: Don’t mention it. No, really…don’t mention it. I’ve got a reputation to fulfil… Dustin: Oh…and what would that be? ::. To be Continued…Fade to White! And you can Justify …that! Gah, that was shoddy….::
