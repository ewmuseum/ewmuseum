Meet Adam The Anarchist

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 17 [The scene opens in a wonderful suburban neighborhood just outside of Los Angeles, California. Beautiful, hand crafted townhouses that are each two stories and almost half a block long. The upper middle class fill the homes with their Mercedes, Cadillacs, Expensive SUV's, and high priced sports cars in the driveways. The clouds part, shining a rush of light down onto the suburban neighborhood. A laborador retriever, golden, trots down the paved quiet street and then suddenly comes to a stop and sits down. A young man, who looks to be in his mid twenties walks up to the dog. The man wears a black T-shirt with a bloody pentagram on the front, a pair of black jeans, and black boots. His long black hair hangs down in his face, covering up his pale face. The two beings stare each other down as if they are about to face off in a pure massacre that could leave both wounded for years to come. The dog snarls and growls, trying to make his opponent feel fear but this very gothic and dark young man does not feel any fear. The man's lip snarls upward sending out a growl of his own, trying to intimidate the dog but his attempt fails horribly] Gothic Man: For...Anarchy... [The young man now holds a Louisville slugger in his hand and begins bashing a nearby mailbox that reads 'The Johnsons' and has a painted family portrait. The young man bashes the box into little chips of wood and dented metal. The young man's face is contorted in such anger, as if the mailbox had done something so horrible to him. He stops for a few seconds, then snorts up a large chunk of mucus and spits a giant green, bloody spit booger on top of the wreckage. Then he stomps on it some more, the anger still not wiped from his face and then he stops. Only a few seconds go by and then he spits another giant spit booger on it, then stomps on it some more, and then once again spits on it. He's out of breath and he's out of boogers and spit. He looks down at the dog, whom looks extremely satisfied, and then nods at the mailbox. The dog then begins to urinate on the broken, spit filled mailbox. Once the dog finishes the young, angry man then turns his back to the camera, unzips his pants, and begins a urination session of his own. He finishes and smiles with satisfaction, finally able to let the anger pass by him, for now] Gothic Man: I...Love...This...Game... [As the young gothic man and his dog are about to walk further down this peaceful suburban street, a blue 2002 Mustang comes barreling down the street blasting the most bodacious music of all time, The Beach Boys. The angry man and his dog stop and take notice immediately as the car comes to a screeching halt next to them. Inside the car is none other than PCW'S most bodacious tag team, THE COWABUNGA CONNECTION! 'TOTALLY TUBULAR' TOMMY sits behind the wheel wearing a pair of Oakley sunglasses, decked out in his Hawaiian dress shirt, blue and yellow swim trunks, and black sandals. He smiles brightly over to this angry, disgusting man] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: ADAM, what in the name of bogusness are you doing to your own neighborhood? Adam: Anarchy... TTT: Of course that's what it is, dude. We are driving down through here as a shortcut and we see broken mailboxes covered in spit and piss; our only conclusion is that Adam The Anarchist has most bodaciously struck again. [The man named Adam The Anarchist smiles brightly, taking great pride in his actions] Adam The Anarchist: I...rule... [A Steeler's football helmet rises upward, frightenly with a body attached to it. That body is of 'HEAVY METAL' HARRY. Harry still wears his gift from Jerome Bettis from a week ago, and he wears it with great pride. Harry also still wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. He smiles, obviously just finishing up taking a monster bong hit] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: You still have your dog, man. Adam The Anarchist: Yeah...Buster...rules...too... TTT: Gnarly. [Adam pets his dog and the golden Lab wags it's tail in approval of it's master's affection] Adam: Where...are...you...coming...from...? TTT: We are coming from Memphis, dude. We had to do this totally bogus radio interview and I ended up storming out because the dude was just being extremely heinous in a most bogus way. He had the nerve to say that we couldn't beat The Heartbreakers or The Pretty Cool Guys, talk about the bogusness of all bogusness. Adam: What...a...moron... Harry: Yeah, man. Adam: I...could...burn...his...house...down... TTT: That's a most prestigious idea but I don't resort to that kind of thing, compadre. [Adam then pulls out a large bottle and begins chugging a large amount of it. He sighs in relief, feeling the fluids go through his body. Tommy quickly catches the writing on the side of the bottle and it reads 'PooFast: Got Poop Stuck in the Chute, Just Drink This And Poop'. Tommy laughs to himself] TTT: I notice that you still have that constipation problem, dude. Adam: Yeah...I...need...help...because...I...have...a...narrow...rectum... Harry: You should unnarrow it, man. Adam: That...will...hurt... Harry: No it won't, man. Adam: Yes...it...will... Harry: I'm tell you that if you get an operation you won't talk one word at a time anymore and you'll be able to poop freely, man. Adam: I...will...light...you...on...fire... Harry: PooFast works, man. [Adam hops in the back of the Mustang and his dog, Buster, follows behind him] Adam: How...fast...is...this...car...? TTT: Really fast, dude. Adam: Good...because...I...have...to...POOP...!! [Tommy puts the pedal to the medal to get his good friend, Adam The Anarchist, to the toilet] TTT: How's things going at home, dude? Adam: Horrible...my...dad...got...a...promotion...and...he's...head...executive... TTT: That's totally GNARLY! Adam: No...it...isn't... TTT: Why? Adam: Because...I...dispise...executives...because...its...totally...against...what...I...believe...in... TTT: Oh. Adam: How...is...wrestling...going... TTT: Totally heinous, dude. All of those PCW head dudes, Tyler Nelson and Phantom Booker, are being totally bogus to us. They go back and forth between each other not even putting better wrestlers ahead of them. Talk about most bogus egotisical maniacs. I mean we are the greatest tag team to ever grace PCW, and actually GIVE it a tag team division and they don't utilize our most bodacious skills. [Adam is doubled over now, trying his best to hold it inside of him. He knows deep down inside that he shouldn't have drank so much of the PooFast, but it's too late for regrets because he can feel about three days of human excrement building up right on the edge of his rectum, ready to burst at any moment. If he keeps talking to Tommy and Harry he can keep his mind off of it] Adam: That...sucks...Want...me...to...kill...them... TTT: No way, dude. I don't think it's possible to kill dudes like that anyway. Harry: I got a new bong, man. Adam: Cool... TTT: Harry, we can't worry about the new and improved Jerome Bongus because we have to concentrate on the situation at hand and that's our most heinous week against The Heartbreakers and Pretty Cool Guys. Adam: They...all...sound...gay...to...me... TTT: Yeah, they all seem that way but Harry and I are equal opportunity winners. Gay, Straight, or Bi we'll most bodaciously kick ass, take names, and then righteously take the victory like the greatest tag team that we are. [Tommy takes exit number 54, as Adam soon realizes that Tommy isn't going to his home, but he's going to Santa Monica to hit the club scene. You do not hit the clubs with an anarchist who has to poop] TTT: The talk around PCW is that since The Heartbreakers were the last tag champs that they are the most prestigious team to defeat in the tag team division. Once we do that and prove that we are the uncrown tag team champions of the world, it's time that we most bodaciously move on to The Pretty Cool Guys who have been considered to be the biggest threat to be the next PCW tag team champions. Adam: Yeah...thats...interesting... Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: I have my high flying and technical skills to guide me through that ring where Harry has pure power and brute strength to help make us most bodacious in a far out way. Harry: Right on, man. TTT: We have the greatest tag team arsenal in all of PCW and it's not being utilized by the great powers of PCW. I think after this week of Cowabunganess that they will take notice and finally realize that you can't hold down tubularness. Harry: Yeah, man. Adam: SO, your being held down by a higher power, and aren't we all. [Tommy and Harry turn around with fright in their eyes] TTT: DUDE!! Your not talking one word at a time!! Harry: Yeah, man. Adam: I know. [Tommy and Harry begin sniffing the air and both can catch a hint of poo in the air] Harry: Are we driving through farm land, man? [All around them are clubs, clothing store, and restaurants. Adam has a look of pure guilt on his face] Adam: I'm sorry but I couldn't hold it. [A puddle of brown sits underneath Adam, seeping onto the car mats and the leather interior. Tommy slams on the brakes, throws the car in a parking space and then he and Harry leap from the car. They run down Rodeo Drive, escaping the awful smell and look of Adam's mess] Adam: It's the worlds fault that I am cursed with a small rectum. I can't help it that I'm this way, but they continue to shun me anyway. That is why I cause anarchy, that is why I am ADAM THE ANARCHIST!! [Buster jumps out of the car and begins following the Cowabunga Connection] Adam: Awww.... [The scene then fades to black]
