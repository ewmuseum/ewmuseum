Snow melts but Malice stays Evil!!!!

23 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Snow melts but Malice stays Evil!!!! Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/23/02 Dateline: Wednesday October 23, 2002Place: The evil Goth Lair of our God and President Dukestar The Goblin Mission: Trash talk Jason Snow in Evil Goth manor. -In the deep dark lair of Duke Williams some compare to hell others compare it to a teenagers basement room. Sits a man of pure evil and darkness not just a man but rather a God. An Evil Goth God and on hell of a bass fisherman. He wears nothing but black to symbolism all that is wrong with the world, that and it’s slimming on the camera. He sits in his throne made from the bones of murdered hobos stabbed in the heartland. The bones are covered with a plush leather skin. In his hand is a messed up Rubics Cube which he fidgets with frustration.- Dukestar: My favorite thing about being an Evil God is I get to read the history books and find out how Evil is saturated into our society on a daily basis. -Duke reaches over and picks up a book.- Dukestar: As you can see in Dick Daleys book “The history of Goth inventions.” One of the key tools we have used over the past thirty years has been the Rubics cube. A cube that is designed to confuse, frustrate, and create rage in any soul. What a marvelous invention. Now you many say but Dukestar Boy Gorge did all those things too. -Duke lets out one of his evil Goth laughs so frightening it makes your little toe duck under his neighbor toe. Truly that EVIL.- Dukestar: But seriously what the cube does is it makes you eventual cheat. I don’t care if you’re a blue haired church going saint or an innocent young mind yet to be exposed to the curl world. You will tear off the colored stickers and rearrange them to look as if you solved the puzzle. -Dike lets out another hardy laugh. Did I mention it was Evil and totally Goth.- Dukestar: But enough about the twisted world I rule lets get down to bloody tacks if you will. When I think Snow I think the purity of winter which covers out world with a white cleansing power. A cold that sends trees, bugs, and birds, into a deep somber. In another words it leaves me greatly joyed to watch the world die for those few short months. -Duke takes a bite out of his Evil snake carcass which looks a lot like a Snickers bar.- Dukestar: When I think Jason Snow I truly find myself vacillating between sincere like and dislike. On one hand he can be as dark as a deep holler but the next week as light as a beacon in the night. But really folks lets face the fact I have been summonsed and transformed into a GOD while he sits in the pokey and rots. I am a President of a massive company while he is a resident of an institution designed for incarceration. Is he still in prison??? -Duke looks over to his mortal servant Roseanne Bar who is watching the Price is Right on TV. She just shrugs her shoulders in indecision. – Dukestar: So helpful thank you. -Roseanne offers up her middle finger.- Dukestar: ahhhh in EVIL Goth edict that is a sign of respect. No really it is. Anyway being as I’m a God and I’m busy doing God thinks I don’t have time to check into the details of Jason Snow so if he’s out of the slammer good for him. Fact is I’m a God and he isn’t. Roseanne: Yea but hasn’t he been world champion before and you haven’t? Dukestar: Keep talking and you won’t be paid this week. Roseanne: Sorry. Dukestar: As my servant pointed out……….. -Duke stares into the camera for a moment.- Dukestar: Is my black mascara smeared? -Roseanne peeks over the couch.- Roseanne: Yes. Dukestar: How am I supposed to be an Evil Goth God if my evil Goth makeup is smeared? Hold on turn that damn camera off. -The camera cuts off and when it returns we see Duke in the same chair with his makeup fixed.- Dukestar: Yes it is true Snow is a former World Champion and I’m not. However I’m no longer the cowboy. You see that gimmick died in the early eighties and I never caught on. Even image consulting meetings with the Phantom Booker never resulted in a gimmick change. But now I have changed. Not only has my gimmick evolved but I’m now a god. Tell me Jason Snow how do you beat a God who is unwilling to lose? -Duke sips his Evil witches brew which looks a lot like hot Java.- Dukestar: You see I’m not only Immortal I am also unbeatable. Since becoming a God I have won the Continental title and I’m yet to lose….. Roseanne: What about Mall Brawl? Dukestar: Hey shhh -Duke throws an Evil pillow at Roseanne.- Dukestar: At Mall Brawl I decided on my own to let another Goth kid win the title. In exchange to pay homage to me he changed his last name to Goblin. Roseanne: He…. Dukestar: Ahhh Roseanne: But…. Dukestar: Silence Roseanne: I… Dukestar: Tom Arnold. -Roseanne just yells as she goes back to watching The Price is Right.- Dukestar: Take away my good looks, take away my styling Goth cloths, take away my wonderful verbal skills, hell even take away my God powers and your still left with your boss Jason. -Duke lets out his less Goth like more corporate laugh.- Dukestar: So be warned that I will have to tax you if I lose. You’re bad PR for the federation with you sitting in the prison and all. Shame on you driving our ratings down. PCW used you when needed but now we moved on. Join the current Goth craze or get out. This Friday I will display my Goth God powers when I send you Whiskey Bent and Goth bound then I’ll tax your ass. That my friends is the honky tonk goth god evil Front Office Truth, hoss. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
