Champions: Lost but not Forgotten

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 27, 2002 OOC: This was inspired by some of my old feuds with the Dark Ages members. I don't know why I felt inspired, but I was. :) The solemn tones of an old cathedral clock have announced midnight -- the air is thick and heavy -- a strange, death like stillness pervades all nature. Like the ominous calm that precedes some more than usually terrific outbreak of the elements, they seem to have paused even in their ordinary fluctuations, to gather a terrific strength for the great effort. A faint peal of thunder now comes from far off. Like a signal gun for the battle of the winds to begin, it appeared to awaken them from their lethargy, and one awful, warring hurricane swept over a whole plethora of tag teams, producing more devastation in the four or five minutes it lasted, than would a half century of ordinary phenomena. It was as if some giant had blown upon some toy figures, and scattered many of the tag teams before the hot blast of his terrific breath; for as suddenly as that blast of wind had come did it cease, and all was as still and calm as before. Jack and Johnny awakened, and thought that what they had heard must be the confused chimera of a dream. However, they saw bodies lying on their bedroom floor, bodies of PCW tag teams, and the bodies of themselves. There above the bodies was none other than Akiyama, laughing at the debree. Lights flashed about the building, and various room doors opened; voices called one to the other. There was an universal stir and commotion among the inhabitants. Johnny Heart: “What’s going on Jack?” Jack Valentine: (Screaming over the wind) “I don’t know Johnny...I don’t know!” Johnny Heart: “Are we dead?” Jack Valentine: “Hold Me!” Voice of Akiyama: “Soon enough, the whole division will be dead. Rest in Peace Heartbreakers.” They trembled and turned to sleep again, pulling their PINK! covers over their heads. They awoke to the glistening glimmer of the PINK! sunrise shining through their bedroom window. Both of them were dripping in a pool of their own sweat. They turned and looked to the other, knowing that they had the same nightmare...or was it a nightmare at all? Could it have been reality? After all, the man is god-like. But no evidence of any event as the one they had pictured was at all left in the room. Jack Valentine: “What just happened Johnny?” Johnny Heart: “I was just about to ask you the same thing?” Jack Valentine: “Was that a dream?” Johnny Heart: (Nervously) “It had to be...nothing like that could really happen...could it?” Jack Valentine: “No, of course not...not in a million years.” Johnny Heart: “But we did have the same dream...so how do we know it wasn’t real?” Jack Valentine: “A lot of people that are close have the same dream Johnny...that stuff just...ahem...happens.” The scene shifted to black and white where a strange howling noise came from the throat of this monstrous figure, Akiyama, and it seemed upon the point of rushing upon The Heartbreakers. Suddenly, then, as if some impulse had seized upon it, it uttered a wild and terrible shrieking kind of laugh; and then turning, dashed through the window, and in one instant disappeared from before the eyes of those who felt nearly annihilated by its fearful presence. Johnny Heart: (Screaming like a little girl) “Oh my God! Oh my God!” Jack Valentine: (Realizing that it was just a picture in his head) “What, what did you see?” Johnny Heart: “I saw him...it was like the nightmare all over again.” Jack Valentine: “Who did you see?” Johnny Heart: (Pointing silently to the television...as if he was in a scary movie.) On the television showed Akiyama beating the two tag teams in the ring on Turmoil senseless. It then showed the monstrous figure laughing over the debree, just like in their supposed nightmare. Jack Valentine: “You saw Akiyama...” Johnny Heart: “Yes...and he’s soooo scary!” Jack Valentine: “Wait a minute...I think I know why we had the same dream Johnny.” Johnny Heart: (Holding a blanket in fright) “Why?” Jack Valentine: “Because we left the TV on again...we probably heard the scene playing out in our subconscious, so we dreamt about it...that’s all.” Johnny Heart: (Putting the blanket down) “Oh...I knew that.” Jack Valentine: “It’s so simple. We’re nervous about this match on Sunday, mixed with the emotions that were felt listening to the TV during the Akiyama scene.” Johnny Heart: (Still nervous, but trying to cover it up) “Obviously.” Jack Valentine: “There’s really nothing to worry about...I mean, sure nVg is a scary man, but nothing more. We’ve encountered scarier than him in our careers, haven’t we?” Johnny Heart: (Laughing) “Yeah, like Pretty Cool Guys...boy, they need a Pretty Cool Makeover.” The pair laughs and then all of the sudden the scene shifts to black and white again. This time, blood rushed down the walls and all of the tag teams in the match on Sunday appeared to be hung by a noose. Akiyama of course was standing there with rope in his hand. The scene came out of black and white again and this time Jack screamed like a girl. Jack Valentine: “Ahhhh!” Johnny Heart: “What is it?” Jack Valentine: “I saw him too.” The pair grabs each other and hugs in fright. They notice the cameras and then brush it off like they aren’t really scared. Jack Valentine: (Clearing his throat in a very manly fashion) “Ahem. We’re not afraid of Akiyama.” Johnny Heart: “Nor are we gay.” Jack Valentine: “Who said anything about being gay?” Johnny Heart: “Um...no one...I said nor are we...yay?!?” Jack Valentine: “Oh...ok. Well, the fact of the matter is this. We’ve got to get these delusions out of our head and we’ve got to get focused on this match, fearless.” Johnny Heart: “Fearless!” Jack Valentine: “Strong, and determined.” Johnny Heart: “Strong and determined!” Jack Valentine: “Most importantly though...stay the hell away from Akiyama!” Johnny Heart: “Good idea.” Johnny rushes over to the TV and turns off the clip of Akiyama causing carnage over the tag team division last week on Turmoil. As Jack and Johnny walk away from the television, the camera zooms in on the VCR and picks up that there was no tape in there of Turmoil, nor was the TV even turned onto PCW television. So was this really a dream? Or was it reality? What wonderfully different impressions and feelings, with regard to the same circumstances, come across the mind in the broad, clear, and beautiful light of day to what haunt the imagination, and often render the judgment almost incapable of action, when the heavy shadow of night is upon all things. There must be a downright physical reason for this effect -- it is so remarkable and so universal. It seems that the sun's rays so completely alter and modify the constitution of the atmosphere, that it produces, as we inhale it, a wonderfully different effect upon the nerves of the human subject. Akiyama was not human. He was a god. Did he or did he not show his face in the house of The Heartbreakers? Did he plant those delusions in their heads? Maybe the camera was mistaken, and it all really was a dream. Regardless, all teams are alive and well, and The Heartbreakers are heading in to Gold & Glory with their heads held high...well, at least when they’re not looking at Akiyama.
