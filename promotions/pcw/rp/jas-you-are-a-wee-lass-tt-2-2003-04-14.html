Jas you are a wee lass...(TT#2)

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-14-03 A door open as steam comes billowing out Adam Strykerappears with a towel around his waist. He’s drying hishair with another towel. Jaslene is sitting on theedge of a bed that sits in the middle of the room. Shelooks up to his clean shaven face and smiles. 'There, you look much better now.' She giggles. 'Ah yeah, I feel much better too.' He respondschecking himself over in a mirror. 'It’s simplyamazing how good one can feel after a hot shower and ashave.' Jaslene nods. Adam smirks as he looks at her in themirror. 'What about you?' Adam asks turning to face Jaslene.'Are you feeling any better now?' 'A bit yeah.' Jaslene says rubbing her head, 'Thoughmy head is still throbbing.' 'What did you expect?' Adam says with a laugh, 'Withthe amount you drank I’m surprised that you are stillawake.' 'Adam!' Jaslene exclaims, 'I didn’t drink that much! Ionly had about half a bottle before you came bargingin.' 'Jas you are a wee lass, half a bottle of straightVodka is the same as me drinking two full ones.' Hepoints out tossing the towel in his hand toward ahamper. The falls just short of the open hamper as Adam walksinto a closet and begins to go through his wardrobe.Jaslene gets up off of the bed and walks to thedoorway of the closet and stops. She turns aroundquickly as she notices Adam has dropped the towel thatwas around his waist. 'So…uh…are you feeling ready for your match Tuesday?'Jaslene asks as she walks back into the middle of thebedroom. 'I suppose so, why do you ask?' comes the reply fromthe closet. 'Well you haven’t so much as stepped foot in the ringsave an attack on Trinidad and that small segment youhad a couple of shows back.' Jaslene calls out takinga seat in front of a mirror and picking up ahairbrush. 'Eh details, details Jas.' He calls back. 'Meansnothing. It’s like riding a bike you know? Why I betyou could step back into the ring and becoming theRising Star Champion in a matter of weeks.' 'Uh, Adam it’s the Shooting Star Title now but you’reprobably right. Dorn Tracer ain’t got nothing on me.'Jaslene says with a smile as she starts to brush herhair. 'Yeah that’s true.' Adam says as he comes out of thecloset dressed in a pair of black sweats with a graymuscle shirt on. 'You’d probably bitch Dorn around thering like nobody’s business.' Jaslene laughs as Adam stands behind her. He placeshis hands on her shoulders and begins to rub. Jaslenesighs as she puts the brush down and closes her eyes. 'Have you made contact with the Pharaoh yet?' Jasleneasks. Adam stops rubbing her shoulders for a moment, 'No.Honestly I don’t know if I want to either. He seems abit odd to me but the again a man who thinks he is aPharaoh from ancient Egypt would seem a bit odd toeveryone.' 'Hm…that’s a good point you have there Adam but…'Jaslene trails off. 'But…what?' Adam asks bending down to look her in theeye. 'Come on Adam!' Jaslene slaps him in the forehead andsticks her tongue out him. 'HE’S YOUR PARTNER! Lasttime I checked in order to win tag team matches,especially four way Tornado Matches, one needs to beable to work with said partner!' 'I may have to work with him but that doesn’t mean Ineed to interact with him that much.' Adam repliessticking his tongue out at her. She stands up and punches him in the stomach lightly.He pushes her away in a playful manner before headingout the sliding door leading to the deck. The sunreflects off of the pool in the corner of thebackyard. He stands at the railing and places his armsdown on it. He leans over the edge and takes in a deepbreath. 'Ahhhh…' Adam sighs. Jaslene comes to his side and looks out over thebackyard. She places her hand down onto his and wrapsher fingers around his hand. Jaslene squeezes andsmiles as the wind begins to pick up. Her hair blowsin the breeze. 'This is nice don’t you think?' Jaslene says turningto face Adam. He just nods and continues to look out over hisbackyard. Jaslene looks disappointed as she returnsher gaze to the scene in front of her. 'How do you think this is going to go over?' shecalmly asks brushing her hair back behind her ear. 'Badly…' Adam says softly trailing off. Jaslene takes a step back and looks at Adam. He turnsand meets her glare. 'What?' Adam says reaching out and grabbing hershoulder. 'Did I say something wrong?' 'Adam, what is wrong?' she asks moving in closer tohim. 'You are always confident going into a match. Nomatter what! You have never been like this.' 'Well Jas…' Adam says looking down into her eyes,'Maybe I’m just coming to my sense that I’m not thegreatest wrestler out there. You know? I’m gett-' She slaps him hard across the face as she pulls awayfrom him. The outline from her hand appears on Adam’sface as she rubs her hand. Adam stands there stunned.He pulls his hand up to his face. 'Ouch…' he says as he begins to rub his face whereJaslene had slapped him. 'That really, REALLY, hurtJas! What was that for?' 'Stop moping!' Jaslene yells at him, 'I don’t like tosee you acting this way and I know this isn’t naturalfor you so just stop it!' 'Jas…I…' Adam starts but then just trails off as hestares into her eyes. 'Adam you are a damn good wrestler,' she says onceagain wrapping her arms around him, 'That much youproved in the DWO. You carried that whole organizationfor the longest time. It’s just that now you havebecome a small fish in a large pond. I can understandwhy you would have your doubts but don’t.' Adam chuckles, 'Heh, you make it all sound so simpleJas.' 'That’s because it is that simple.' She replies with asmile. 'Now I ask you again, how do you feel aboutyour match tomorrow?' 'Much better now…' Adam says as he bends down andkisses the top of her head. The two turn and face the backyard again. Adam restshis head on top of Jaslene’s.
