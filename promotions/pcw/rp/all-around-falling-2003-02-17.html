All Around Falling

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-17-03 Depression, suffocation, drowning in your own little world of problems; it is one side of the human spectrum. One the other side is joy, gladness, and utter happiness. It is two extremes that everyone has suffered at one point in their life. However, it is in life that we remember only the worst. Think back long and hard when you were your happiest moment, when your heart was dipped in a world of joy and glee. Now think back to a darker time. A time where you felt like you could end it all, where life didn’t seem to matter as much. Which was easier to recall? Sadly, for most people it is the time of great depression. It is human nature to allow one self to get so depressed and wrapped up in such a small event. We magnify the problem and let it leech on us. It slowly builds until we can no longer seem to handle it. There must be a way to find relief from such misery yet at times it seems so far away, even if it is right under our very noses. “Something has been affecting Chris Storm as of late but he won’t say what it is. There is just something different about him. He doesn’t seem to be his normal self as of late. What could it be though? He is a man with everything a person could want, a beautiful fiancée with three wonderful children. He has a great place to live and incredible cars to drive. He is a famous star appearing on television. Little children all over the country and even the world look up to him. Yet, he seems to be so depressed as of late. As if there is something behind the scenes suffocating him. What could it be that has affected him so greatly? I just can not put my finger on it.” A rather rugged man stands, leaning against a brick wall. He is filthy, wearing an old red trench coat and torn green jeans. His grey hair is matted and unwashed, as it hangs over his face. Old black eyes that can tell a thousand tells look out across him at another brick wall. This man is clearly in an alley way. He leans his head back against the brick wall. His hand moves toward his mouth, a bent cigarette in his grasp. He takes a drag from the cigarette and lowers it once more, blowing the thick smoke out across the alley. This man is known as Arizona. He is a former body guard for Chris Storm back in his WIW: NG days. The two was rather good friends at one point. Chris knew the one person he could depend on was Arizona, and that led him to tell some of his darkest secrets to him. It was Arizona that Chris first admitted his love for Tessa Windsor to. Arizona, however, was a bum living off the street. What money he earned protecting Chris was blown on female monkeys for his manager Cheech, a stuffed toy monkey. Arizona had been watching Chris as of late however. He’s been noting the strange behavior affecting Chris as of late, concerned with his friend’s state of mind. Arizona pushes off the wall and starts walking down the alley. A ting sound followed faintly behind him. The chain hooked to his pants attaching to his manager Cheech dragged on the ground. Arizona made his way to the other end of the alley way. There he could see the light shining between the two tall buildings. As he walked he continued to take inhales from his cigarette. “My friend Chris, what is troubling your mind? Could it possibly be cold feet for you impending marriage? Do not fear this as it is natural for all men to go through. Maybe it is your handicap match that has you so worried? You know full well that Erik Erratic and Adam Stryker is no match for you. I have seen you enter harder matches and exit without a mark on your body. Perhaps it is your Tag Team title match coming up? Clyde and DarkStar is no match for you and your beloved fiancée. You two shall reclaim your titles and become two time champions, for your partnership goes beyond that of mere wrestling. Alas my dear friend, what is it that ails you so? Hmm, perhaps I should investigate more.” He says as he peers out from the alley way. He looks out ward a video store. Through the glass window you can see Chris Storm looking at different movies. He has a sad look on his face. Chris puts down one movie and walks off. Inside the store, Chris is all alone, just one lone clerk behind the counter. Chris is slowly browsing through the store, looking at the different movies to rent. He lets out a low sigh as his feet brush across the carpet. He walks to the new release section and stands in front of a wall of different movies. He rubs his chin as he looks at the different movies available to rent. “My Big Fat Greek Wedding” he says as he looks at the box for the movie. He lets out a slight sigh as he grabs the box and looks at the back of it. A frown comes to his face and so he puts it back. “That just doesn’t seem like something I want to watch right now. But look at them, so joyous and happy. Can my marriage be like that? I doubt it; I am not the marrying type. I mean look at me.” Chris looks over to where a movie poster is hanging on the wall. He looks at his reflection in it. “My face looks like I’m a stupid hick. I always have that stupid grin on my face.” He says with an annoyed look on his face. Chris turns away from the poster and looks back at the movies. Chris looks over at another movie, “The Bourne Identity”. He flips it over to look at the other side of the case. After reading the back, he tosses the movie back against the wall, not caring if it falls into place. “Someone else that can not even remember their past huh? He was lucky, at least when we discovered his past he had one. What about myself? What does my past hold for me? Was I a bastard or just left alone to die? Maybe they saw that ugly mug of mine and decided to skip town.” He says with spite in his voice. Chris gets sick of looking at the movies and starts to leave the store. His eye is caught by something however. Chris turns his attention to the adult section of the store, the bright neon sign with “XXX” on it. Chris looks to the counter to notice the clerk has his attention turned to a movie on the television. He sneaks into the adult section behind the curtain. Looking around, he finds himself surrounded by adult movies. Covers filled with naked women with oversized breast, something that never really turned Chris on. Chris lets out another sigh, disgust with letting himself come in here. He turns to leave but sees it. It’s one of his movies he made while he worked as a porno star. This was the first time seeing one of his movies since he had tosses them out for Tessa when he was moving out of his apartment and into her home. Chris leans his head against the cold metal shelf used to hold the movies. “A porn star, a violent wrestler, an egoistical manic, a Shinto priest who can not even remember his own past; what a great catch I am.” He laughs to himself. “What does Tessa even see in me? What do I see in her? It’s sad, I’m engaged to her yet I can hardly remember what even attracted me to her. I bet she can’t remember either.” He lets out another sigh, biting his bottom lip. “This marriage won’t last. It is nothing more then a joke. We think we are in love but what do we know? We are just kids, me at 26 and her at 21. Yet we think we are ready for marriage? How could I have been so stupid to ask her to marry me? Now I have ruined her life for sure. She is going to be stuck with me until we finally decide we should be divorced. Then she will be so depressed and the kids will be stuck in the middle. I’ve ruined an entire family’s life just by letting my emotions get the better of me. How could I have been so stupid?” he shouts, his voice getting higher and higher as he speaks. Chris’ anger gets the better of him and he punches the wall. The movies fall off their shelves and shatter on the floor. Chris doesn’t even bother to look at them. The clerk behind the counter looks toward the curtain and shrugs. Chris’ fist is balled up tight as he gets madder and madder thinking about it. His entire fist is red now. His muscles relax and he lets out one finally sigh. His head drops down. “Listen to me, questioning everything. I use to be so sure. Now I can not make a single decision without questioning myself thousands of times. How can I expect to win in the PCW when I can hardly handle my own personal matter? Erik and Adam are going to walk all over me. I am going to fail Evan. I can just see it now.” Chris reaches into his back pocket and pulls out his wallet. Opening it up, he sees some pictures. Chris flips through them until he comes across one of little Evan. A smile actually comes to his face. Chris takes it out and holds it, looking at the picture. “Evan, remember when I told you that I would defeat Erik and Adam for you? I do. I can still remember that smile on your face. You looked so proud of me then. At that moment I actually felt like your father.” Chris stops, getting choked up from his words. He puts the picture back into his wallet. Chris holds his head up high and walks out from the curtain into the main store. He starts breathing in and out, deeper then normal. “I promise you Evan, I am not going to win for myself. I am going to win for you. I will make you proud of me son, I promise.” Chris lets out another long sigh and marches out of the video store. The cool winter air hits his body, a refreshing blast of energy. He still wasn’t sure how he was going to win, but he knew he had to. If he couldn’t keep his promise to his son Evan, then what kind of a father would he is? Well, we will find out on Tuesday, now won’t we?
