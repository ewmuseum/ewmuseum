Dealing with Generic Network Personal

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th Dec, &#039;02 (We open up to what appears to be some kind of boardroom. The room is surrounded by strong looking glass walls, allowing the room to view out onto a beautiful blue ocean, with beams of sun hitting the water creating a superb sparkling golden effect. Inside the room itself, the floor is covered with a wonderful white carpet. And in the center of the room, there is an attractive antique wooden oval table, which the people in the room are sitting around in their luxurious leather chairs.) Man: I hate these glass windows, the sun gets in my eyes as it’s bouncing off the ocean and the glass, it’s horrible. Woman: I hate this white carpet, it’s so boring, you think that whoever made chose the carpet could of chosen a better color. Man: I also hate this table, it looks so old that I think it might collapse any second now. Woman: I also hate these chairs. Leather just irritates me. (As the two people sitting up one end of the table complain about the room, another man further down the table, wearing an expensive suit and some gold and silver jewelry who appears to be in charge, pipes in.) Head Man: Yeah I know, this is the worst room that we’ve ever used as a boardroom. What a waste of thirty million dollars! I don’t think we’ll ever use this room again. But nonetheless, we’ll have to use it today in discussing whether or not we should renew The Chat King Wrestling Show. (We then see the entire table which consists of the man and woman we saw talking at the beginning, the man who appears to be in charge, another two men and another woman, and PCW wrestler, Chet King, who’s wearing the Armani suit we saw him wearing at Turmoil this past Tuesday.) Chet King: What’s there to discuss? The show should be bought back immediately, no questions asked. Head Man: Well we have to consider whether it will draw ratings. Chet King: Of course it’ll draw ratings, it was a ratings winner towards the end of its last stint. Head Man: That was due to Mexi co hosting the show without you, and without Mexi, we feel that the show has lost a lot of it’s drawing power. Chet King: I admit that Mexi was good, but I think the show can still draw big with me hosting the show solo once again. (The lady who has yet to speak starts to laugh uncontrollably at this.) Chet King: What? Woman #2: Hahaha, did you just, hahaha, say that the show can still get big ratings with, hahaha, you hosting the show solo? (Chet King seems a bit offended by this.) Chet King: Yes, why, what’s wrong with me? Woman #2: Don’t be offended by this, but you’re annoying. Chet King: I’m annoying? Why? (One of the men who has yet to speak, chimes in.) Man #2: Because you seem to keep talk talking and you never shut up. (The other man who has remained silent until now, starts to talk as well.) Man#3: Because you seem to continually use cliché’s that no one wants to hear. Woman #2: Because your voice gives me headaches. Man #1: Because you talk about topics that I couldn’t care less about. Woman #1: Because your wrestling style looks pathetic. Head Man: Because half the time you don’t what your talking about. Man #2: Because you seem to have a bit of an ego. Woman #2: Because you constantly seem to live your life by remembering your past glories. Man #3: Because, when your show was on, you talked about how you think some wrestlers need to change their styles, yet you’ve never even been world champion – so it’s not your place to tell people things. Woman #1: Because you give people advice when it’s not your place to give. Man #3: That’s what I just said! Woman #1: I was just reiterating the point. Head Man: Because your manager is annoying bisexual woman. Man #1: Because your….. (Chet King jumps out of his seat in frustration.) Chet King: Ok, ok, ok, ok, ok! I get the point! You all think I’m annoying! Man #1: Not all of us. What gave you that impression? (Chet King lets out a sigh of frustration.) Chet King: If the problem here is that you think I’m annoying, why don’t you tell me what can I do to stop being annoying? Woman #2: You could study the speech pattern of one of your fellow PCW wrestlers. He has a very interesting, and not at all annoying way of speaking, what was his name again, uh… Chet King: Duke Williams? Woman #2: No. Chet King: Xavier Kannon? Woman #2: No that’s not it either. Chet King: Tim Walden? Woman #2: No, no, no! It’s, umm… Chet King: Totally Tubular Tommy? Woman #2: Yes that’s the one. He’s very entertaining when he speaks, not at all annoying. (This brings a look of shock to the face of Chet King.) Chet King: You want me to talk like TTT, the man I’m facing on Tuesday Turmoil, the man who says dude and bodacious and heinous? Woman #2: Yep! Chet King: You mean to tell me, that you want me to talk using some of the most annoying words of all time, in an attempt not to sound annoying? Woman #2: It sounds like you don’t appreciate Tommy’s fantastically exciting use of the English language. Maybe if you did, you’d understand how not to be annoying. Chet King: You’ve got to be kidding me. I hope no one else in this room shares this belief. (The members of the room start to murmur amongst themselves.) Chet King: So you’re all basically saying that The Chat King Wrestling Show won’t get put back on the air, unless I talk like TTT? Head Man: Look, Chet, we don’t expect you to just change your whole persona around to become as intellectually stimulating and entreating as Totally Tubular Tommy, but we would like to see you become less annoying. Chet King: So you’re saying that the show will be coming back on air? Head Man: On the conditions that you aren’t annoying, and that you draw ratings. Chet King: I guarantee that show will draw ratings. The show will be fast paced, with high profile guests, there won’t be a dull moment. Head Man: Do you have any guests in particular lined up? Chet King: I had a few discussions backstage at Turmoil with some big names in PCW, and they’d all be happy to appear on the show. Head Man: Well, despite the fact that we haven’t managed to secure Mexi to come back to the show for this season, we will give the show another shot. You better not blow it Chet. Chet King: Trust me, I don’t blow anything, unlike you and your little adventure with generic network man #3 over there (Chet points in the direction of Man #3, while the man in charge and Man #3’s faces go red.) Head Man: Yes, well, we were drunk, and these things happen, but it’s all in the past. Man #3: Yes, it’s all in the past. (The man in charge is clearly embarrassed by all of this.) Head Man: I think it’d be best if we took a short lunch break, and when we come back we can discuss what time slot we’ll put the show into. (The scene fades out as the Head Man, the fellow network personal, and Chet King all file out of the room with the knowledge that The Chat King Wrestling Show is coming back on to the air.)
