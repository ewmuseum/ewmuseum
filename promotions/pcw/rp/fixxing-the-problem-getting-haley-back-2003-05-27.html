Fixxing The Problem...Getting Haley Back

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/27/03 Some say that last night was in the making for a month, there was no way Matt Jackson wasn’t going to walk out of Supremacy, with the Raising Star Title. Others claim that it was nothing more then a good run that’s going to end…end soon with a loss on Turmoil to James Edwards. The man that got closer to him then anyone else in the Raising Star Tournament, having a record similar to Matt’s, but with only three major flaws. Flaws that aren’t in Matt’s record, flaws that he put in James record, not once, but three times pinning him and claiming the win. Claiming the win like he did last night, raising his Raising Star Title over his head, proclaiming that he was the best of the new people and that no one was going to stand in his way, but now he is faced with his first title defense. Forced to step into the ring with James Edwards again and risk losing everything that he capped off last night…losing the one thing that he has yet to do, beat Kid Wonder’s record for the longest undefeated streak, five more wins and it will be Jackson’s…five more. After the record is his, it’s hard to tell what’s going to happen, how things are going to end up, but that’s just life…fate. This is almost bitter sweet, winning the one thing I have worked hard for, but losing the one thing I worked harder for…losing Haley, just when I had gotten her back. Makes the events of last night useless. Beating the three other men didn’t have the same impact that it should have, it’s just not the same without Haley by my side…sharing in the victories and the record breaking. Sitting snuggly against the red padded back of the bench in the booth, Matt crosses his arms over his barrel chest, his grey suit wrinkling up into a thousand little creases, as he stares at the man in front of him, his brown eyes, cold and full of hatred and anger, as the Raising Star Title, sits carefully on his knee, the leather straps touching the white title floor. He continues to stare at the man, tilting his head back to get a better view, of the middle-aged man standing in front of him, wearing a white suit, with his thinned grey hair combed over to hide his balding head. His nearly six foot five inches of height continues to take slow small steps toward the booth, his skull looking like the anointed king of some royal court and his voice filled with the wisdom of prophets. Reaching the booth, with just a few massive steps, the distingued attorney, glides into the booth seat across from Jackson, like a graceful swan. He unbuttons the grey sport coat and smoothes out his dress shirt, before out reaching his massive arms behind him and leaning back, never taking his opal orbs off of the Raising Star Champion, giving the piece of gold at his knee a few glances. Upon his arrival in the seat, a waiter in a generic uniform runs over and begins to say something, but is quickly cut off by the waving of the man’s large hand, not even getting a first look for either of them, much less a second. The man who is much larger then the 5’8” agent, quickly places his hand back behind his head, letting a concerned look come across his stone face, which is visibly noted, by the wrinkles and small scars that are about his face…showing that he’s been through a lot in his sixty years on this planet. Showing that he’s been through thick and thin and still come out a head to be the famed attorney that he is today. The Chameleon flicks a piece of his spiky brown hair out of his face, and places his hands back on the small wooden table in front of them, letting one rest in the other, not balling them up. There is no smile on his black and blue angular face, which is a surprise considering he just won his first PCW Title and a few days ago; he was named a graduate of the PWA. None of it matters right now, because his only concern is Haley…not the titles, not his match with Edwards…none of it. “I am sorry we had to meet under these circumstances champ,” the man begins, letting his eyes fall to the Raising Star Title again. “I figured I could offer my help, being that I got you into this situation and for the fact it’s my daughter. I am sure you can handle it alone, but I want to give you whatever type of support that I can.” “It’s not your fault,” Matt replies, letting his eyes softly scan the small breakfast restaurant, with the posters of breakfast foods and specials hanging on the walls. Older couples casually sitting in booths similar to theirs, drinking coffee and discussing the morning’s news, waiters and waitresses passing by every now and then with hot food for waiting customers and hoping they can do it quick enough to get some sort of worthwhile tip. His eyes fall back to the man seated across from him, noting that the man hasn’t moved an inch, and at the same time, noticing where all the exits are and the best way to escape in case of a problem. “I should have never taken the paths I did Jason,” Matt continues, his voice full of anger and hatred, trying to hold it back. Letting Hilton know he was speaking with a cool head. “If I hadn’t joined the skinheads and lied to them they way I did, none of this would have happened. I shouldn’t have just tossed around PCW and things, like I did. I just got careless and this is all my fault.” “It’s not,” he barks, letting the words roll of his tongue and slamming his fist down on the table to help get his point across, causing Jackson’s small glass to sake on top of the small saucer it’s located in. Jackson jolts back, careful to make sure that none of the dark liquid inside spills over onto his suit. “I put you in this situation, not knowing the kind of guy you were. It’s my fault Matt and I am going to help you fix it. I respected what you went through to keep my daughter…don’t get all weak on me now.” Knowing he is right, Jackson slowly lets the smell of breakfast fill his lung, as he takes a deep breath and exhales, pushing everything out of his mind…his match, his title, and everything else. Taking a few more breaths, he slumps down a bit in the seat, letting the Chameleon completely take him over, forcing him to prepare of the inevitable, which is to find Haley. “Everything is fine…I am not getting all weak on you,” Jackson coolly says, letting his voice cut through all the other noise in the air. Bringing a shock to the face of Hilton, knowing that this isn’t the man he was just talking to a few seconds ago, it’s someone else, the man he wanted Matt to become…the man that’s going to find his daughter and make sure she’s safe. “Basically, I will search around and hunt the bastard down trying to find out everything I can before Wednesday…from there I will go to Turmoil, have my match with Edwards, keep the title and then I will get out of there and keep looking. Only stopping for Fusion and Turmoil, everything has to be wrapped up by June 7th though, at that time…we step onto the PCW Airship to Tour for a month. If you want to help me, so be it, but I am not going to let you hold me back,” Matt bellows out, letting a small smile curl over his white teeth. This is what he enjoys the most…the hunt. That’s why competing for the Raising Star Title, was so fun, it was a hunt to prove that he could get it. Now that he has it, he doesn’t want to lose it and force the hunt to start all over again. He doesn’t want the hassle or worry again, that’s why he doesn’t plan on losing to Edwards at Turmoil. That’s why the belt is going to stay firmly around his waist. “I understand completely,” Hilton replies, trying to convince Jackson that he can be a help. “Just name what you need me to do and I will do it and we will have her back in time for that air tour. Money, power, and anything else you need I have Matt. I think you know that better then anyone else. Nothing will stand in our way of getting back the one thing that means the world to us.” “Okay, but I believe I have everything I need right here,” Matt says, opening his grey jacket, to reveal two leather shoulder holsters, each with a black Berretta 9MM firmly placed inside. Shutting his jacket, he looks back to the older Hilton with a smile. “I will find those skinhead bastards and when I do I will send them to the moon…faster then I can finish off Edwards.” Looking down at the glazed white tabletop, Jason stares at it for a moment. Trying to gather his thoughts…trying not to let Jackson know about his feelings, hating the death of people no matter who they are. That’s why he never had Matt killed, because it’s not in his system…the operative on the other hand, has no problem seeing a body fall at his feet, it almost brings him more joy then when he sees an opponent fall. Bringing his eyes back up to meet the agent’s he nods and offers: “Do whatever you have to do…I don’t care, I just want to make sure that Haley is safe and I know she will be in your hands. I got everyone into this mess, but I thank you for getting us all out of it Matt.” He reaches out one of his massive paws to shake the much smaller petite hand of the agent. Jackson meets his hand and firmly squeezes it, as he stands up and grabs the Raising Star Title, from his knee. “Everything will be all right…I promise Mr. Hilton,” Matt tells him, letting the look of worry fade from Jason’s face some. Matt walks past the sitting Hilton, towards the glass door at the front of the restaurant. Taking a few large steps, he reaches the door and spins back around. “I will be in touch Jason.” Without another word, he pushes open the door and walks out into the hot morning air of LA. Letting the breeze, blow against his tan skin. Hilton continues to sit in the booth, letting out a sigh…he buries his head in his enormous hands and feels the single tear fall from his eye, which he quickly wipes away to make sure no one sees it. “Be strong Jason, everything is going to be all right…he’s the best”
