Chapter XVIII: The Train To America….

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-4-03 ::Introduction:: Arizona has come and gone with Makonie showing up to the Turmoil card, the first card in PCW he has been to in over two weeks. Makonie got back into the swing of things with an ambush on the spics with the soap products. Now its going to be a week later and he has to face to trashy women in the ring and one Ultimate Warrior wannabe. There is no way that Makonie is going to let a white trash woman win this match. Women don’t deserve to win at anything especially in PCW with it being a man’s sport. Now lets go back into the past to look at Mark Makonie’s way of life. ::End Introduction:: Chapter XVIII: The Train To America…. The manure smell lingers in the air as the hay in the corner of the no lighted box train car is shown. There is not a sound in the box besides the hay moving on the wooden floor of the box car. There lay fat hogs sleeping their nights away like all animals do, thinking of the sues that they are going to end up laying so they can have little piglets. A dark head pops up from the corner of the hay where all the rustling has been made. Makonie looks around in the box car as he doesn’t remember where he is at. The blood rushes towards his head so as he got up too quickly. The brain is dizzy as Makonie’s head starts to bob around since he can’t look straight. He tries to think but the pain is going right through his white boned skull and straight for the nerves. Makonie shakes his head to try to clear this sort of dizziness, but that doesn’t work. It just makes it feel like it is going to burst into millions of pieces with the flowing of blood staining the wooden floor boards. Mark likes back down in the hay as he was before but his head is uncovered. His body is as hot as a fat man’s armpit as the hay is keeping it warm from the cool night in Northern Mexico. Mark’s heavy eyelids come down over his eyes so that his brain can settle down for the night. The heavy red blood starts to flow towards his feet now as he lays down for a little bit thinking about what he is going to do in America. Mark can see words floating around with what people have said. A lot of them are Spanish words that he doesn’t even know the meaning of because he can’t even speak the fake language they carry out with one another. People’s voices are heard in the back of the brain as the blood moves evenly so that he can think normally now. His nerves in his brain are calm and are receiving normal signals like everyone else’s. . “They are the people who are taking all of our jobs away and why? Just because they can speak an international language. They don’t deserve to even have a job since they can’t even speak English. Plus you have already mentioned a couple of more reasons to target at.” The quote can be heard over and over again in the back of Makonie’s mind as he remembers the meeting that he has with his best friend. Makonie’s heart starts to race faster and faster as he can remember the meeting with Jax. He can remember his knees getting weak and they were shaking beneath him. He was nervous to see if he was going to find a Nazi still “What in the hell am I doing in my life that is so special?” Mark asks himself as he can’t think of anything to dream about. “There are those damn spics that are fucking driving me nuts and the coon people who always just drive me nuts, but I need help. I can’t do all of this alone since they can just gang up on me.” The thoughts never crossed the twisted mind of Makonie as to how big all of these communities are. He didn’t realize how much he can be beated down on and how much he can be hurt throughout these events that he is pulling in Mexico and even in the states. There are probably guards waiting in the states for Makonie to come across the border so they can jump him from all the shit that he gave them. “Who cares about numbers?” Makonie says to himself as he is thinking. “We have the white community that out populate every other minority in the world, but yet we can’t take out the lower ones? Why the hell not? So fuck numbers, fuck all the spics, fuck all the coons, and fuck people who are against Nazi’s.” A knot builds up inside the stomach of Makonie as he thinks about what he can do to everyone of these communities. These people don’t deserve to be in the states or even in the world so why should they be? The excitement is building up and the anticipation for crossing the bored and having the light to guide him on his way to his perfect place. The hogs across from Makonie are rustling the hay as he lays there with his eyes closed and thinking himself into sleep. The train sounds like the breaks are put on as the wheels squeal against the steel tracks of the railroad. Makonie feels the sudden slowing down of the train as he opens up his eyes quick, but all he sees is the side of the box car. He moves his head up as he looks over at the hogs and can see them moving around in the hay making noise as the train comes to a sudden halt. Makonie can hear feet moving around from the outside as it sounds like guards are talking amongst themselves. Can Makonie get out of this situation or not? Find out next chapter!
