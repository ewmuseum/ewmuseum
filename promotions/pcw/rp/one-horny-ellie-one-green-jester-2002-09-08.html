One Horny Ellie~! & One Green Jester

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/09/02 “I know you’re angry... But I’ll, like, SO make it up to you... I still have THAT outfit from Fusion...” Our shot opens up on the tantalising face of the PCW Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall as she and her husband walk up the path to their Hubbardville home following their traumatic trip.. The trip that resulted from Ellie~! trying to prove she could prank, and nearly killing Kannon in the process. Beside her the bedraggled CEO of PCW still looks none too happy, but the teasing little smile never fails to bring him round. Xavier : “I’ll hold you to that my little Poon Puppy, you have a long night ahead of you...” Ellie~! blushes and giggles. Eleanor : “You’ll be holding me to something else soon, I hope.... Tiger...” She runs a finger down his chin, the nail of which is intricately varnished with Pink and Silver. Xavier : “Urgh.... That PEON has done nothing!” The Scientologist pushes the door open to show there are still bits of plaster in the hallway, he turns back to the door and looks above it to see that there is still a huge hole in the interior. Eleanor : “Like........SO WATCH OUT!!! The prep suddenly scurries towards the Scientologist and tries to push him out of the way... Her 115 pound frame not budging Kannon an inch... He looks at her puzzled while Ellie~! points up. Eleanor : “It’s, like, going to fall!” As the light fixture starts to creak and drop down a little, the Gold Patron Meritorious’ eyes grow wide and just in the nick of time he grabs his wife and pulls her away.. Both of them ending up on the floor as the elegant chandelier comes crashing down. Eleanor : “Ooooh, not waiting for the bedroom...” The prep purrs as Kannon looks up at the hole in the ceiling where his expensive antique used to be. Xavier : “I could have died..... For the THIRD TIME in two days...” He gets to his feet, helping Ellie~! up after him and she clings onto the Scientologist, ready to make nearly killing the CEO up to him. Kannon not looking too willing at this second. Eleanor : “Well, it’s done already... Now, let’s get upstairs and knock a light fitting out the old fashioned way.” She winks at him and takes his arm, guiding him around the Spike Pit she created the day before, and reaching the stairs where Ellie~! skips ahead to kiss him, holding the rail with one hand. Eleanor : “Something to get you staGGGYYYAAAAAHHHHH!!!!!!!!” The little prep convulses as her hand seems unable to release itself from the rail.. Her long Blonde hair frizzes up and heads north, standing on end... Where she has Kannon held by the wrist conducts the voltage and the same thing happens to him before Ellie~! finally releases and couple go flying across the wooden floor and into the wall where they land entwined. Xavier : “I’m starting to think you should just make it up to me here... Before I DIE.” With her hand starting to smoke, Ellie~! tries to pull her hair back down, looking at Kannon’s that is also stuck out wildly. Eleanor : “Um.. Hello, I’m assuming YOU won’t be the one having their body grinding against a floor SO in need of a varni-” She sees Kannon’s eyes gesture to the rug. Eleanor : “And don’t even get me STARTED on Carpet Burn!” The Scientologist groans and slowly starts to pull himself up, finally regaining some feeling in his limb’s extremities as his wife pulls herself up using him, before whispering something un-heard in his ear that perks him up. Xavier : “I knew I married you for a reason...” The lusty couple continue in their quest to reach the bedroom, leaving the shattered chandelier and the electrocuted rail, Kannon muttering about the electrics. Luckily for the couple, on their blinkered rush to the marital bed they skip the tripwire... For now... Eleanor : “For.... A... Reason!” He scoops her up into his arms and carries her up the stairs and with an outstretched foot he pushes open the double doors that lead to the lavish master bedroom.. And the item of furniture they are looking for.. Their bed. Xavier : “You know, you have at least 3 near fatal acts to make up for.... “ Kannon pushes the doors closed behind him, and with the couple face to face, their noses touching, he walks her back towards the bed and pulls her White, Purple and Black geometrically patterned top up above her head to show a Purple padded bra underneath. Eleanor : “Oh.. I’m sure I’ll, like, SO find ways...” He holds her arms up above her head as he kisses the side of her neck then nibbles at her ear , and the top is seen falling down to the floor behind the prep, freeing her hands to un-zip the Black hooded sweat-top that her man is wearing, showing off his toned chest. Xavier : “Let’s make a new rule, my little Fuck Ferret.. All pranks will be saved for the bedroom...” As his arms move around his wife’s waist, his top drops down to the floor and Ellie~! then goes to work on his belt as he undoes her Grey skirt, sending it dropping to the floor.” Eleanor : “Like... SO agreed!” Her leg reaches up and wraps itself around the Scientologist’s waist and allowing Kannon’s hand to run along from the calf to the top of her thigh before he grabs her other leg and lifts her off the floor, her legs wrapped around him and the couple collapse onto the bed. Kannon pushes his preppy wife up the bed, Ellie~! unbuttoning his Black jeans while he slides her left bra strap off and starts to kiss her shoulder.... Then her pale Blue eyes drift off her husband for one second. Eleanor : “Er... Xavi?” As she points up from the bed it takes Xavier a few seconds to get his attention from kissing the top of her left breast to the ceiling.... Ellie~! slides the strap back up while the Scientologist cranes his neck up to look at the ceiling. Xavier : “What in Hubb-” On the White ceiling is spray painted a familiar logo.... A capital “A” in a circle.. The Asylum symbol. Xavier : “-ard’s nam-” In a split second, Ellie~! shrieks and the bed snaps together like a Venus Fly-Trap, entombing the couple as their muffled cried are heard. “FUCKERS!!!” “I, like, can’t get into your boxers...” “No my little Poon Puppy... I said FUCK-ERS...” They continue to struggle, the 2 sides of the bed nearly being prised apart but not allowing more than a hand out as our shot fades to Black. “ASSSSSSSSSYYYYYYYYYYYLLLLLLLLUUUUUMMMMMMMM!!!!!”
