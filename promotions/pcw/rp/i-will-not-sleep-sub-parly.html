I Will Not Sleep Sub-Parly!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #3 PPV Our scene just opens up on the world famous Wrestle Vessel. It seems to be nighttime and the huge ship is illuminated by thousands of lights. The ship also is very much awake, even in the late hours of the night, which is evidenced by the large amount of noise on all decks and the number of people out, either gazing at the stars with a loved one, or trying to figure out how to make the mood of the night exactly perfect. “Bon Dieu, don’t you sub-par beings ever go to sleep?!” Obviously there are other things on that man’s mind. That man, as we see while the camera zooms in on him, is none other than PCW Extreme Champion Rush Mason. Rush is sitting in his fashionable boat wear, which includes a tropical looking shirt, open over a wife-beater. “Man, this boat gives new meaning to the phrase sub-par.” Muttering to himself about the sub-parity of this boat, Rush sits up on his bed, which is in his large suite on the boat. The room looks large, but not very full, seeing as it is intended to sleep multiple people or a family. Sitting up, Rush notices that the walls of his room are vibrating from all of the commotion outside. Rush: Do you people not understand what sleep is? Some of us first-class citizens actually need some sleep! Rush almost spits in disgust as he says the words and looks at the vibrating wall. He shakes his head and gets up from the bed, looking very irritable as he does. Looking at himself in the mirror once before setting out, Rush looks happy with himself. Rush: Well…at least one person will look more attractive than a farm animal out there. Smiling at himself in vanity, Rush turns and heads out the door, immediately hearing the huge amount of noise as he opens the doors, and covering his ears suddenly, as the door to his room slams shut amidst the chaos. Usually at this point, Rush would begin to whine or something, but his words wouldn’t be heard over here anyway. “Do you think you’re better off aloooone?” The beats of Alice Deejay rage on the deck, as many people around Rush’s age and younger dance, some sexually, others just enjoying friendship. Rush looks around and shakes his head in disbelief. “Talk to me! Ooooooohh! Talk to me!” More of the outrageously lyrics come over the sound system, as some people even sing along. Rush just looks extremely pissed off now and walks over to the DJ Booth, where a young Hispanic male is mixing rather well. He doesn’t notice Rush until Rush puts his hand down right in front of the man. Both men have to yell to be heard at all. Rush: Say Cheech, do you think you could turn off el musico? My ears are el hurting from el shitty music. Rush gives a fake smile to the man, who is not impressed at Rush’s terrific grasp of the Spanish language. The man looks back at Rush, and-trying to keep his concentration on scratching- nods his head and goes back to his DJ’ing duties. Rush speaks once again to the man, this time in a much more annoyed tone. Rush: Okay listen here El Taco, some of the citizens with more than sixteen individual brain cells would like some goddamn sleep. Now you can go live la vida loca somewhere else…or I can help you leave. The Hispanic man this time totally ignores Rush except for a hand motion that is known by any normal person to mean ‘get out of here.’ Rush chuckles to himself as he sees this, and suddenly grabs the man by his collar, which immediately sends the music to a scratching halt as everyone turns towards the booth, seeing Rush and the DJ. Rush: Okay, now since you’ve ruined one of the two jobs you’re qualified for, you can go do the other one and clean the pool. Comprende, hombre? The dancing crowd lets out a big ‘Awww’ as they want their night to continue, and some even throw curse words up towards Rush, who responds to them all as they walk off towards their rooms for the night. “Hey fuck you buddy!” “Go find your boyfriend, you prick!” “I HAVE NO PANTS ON!” Yes, as we can see, some of the dancing crowd has already been to the wonderful world of alcohol tonight. Rush: Trust me people. I’m doing you a favor. Maybe if you sleep some more, your children will look less like gargoyles than you lot. If the dancers were not all dead tired, most would probably try to kick Rush’s ass, but most need a drink anyway, and head off to the ship’s bar to catch that one last drink before the last call as Rush stands triumphantly in front of his “conquered land.” The DJ snarls at him as he walks off, the lights in the area finally going off in what is now the wee hours of the morning. Rush: How nice of you. I’ll tell El Diablo you said hi, because you’re all related anyway. The DJ is a small man, and probably better for Rush, because the DJ certainly hasn’t taken kindly to Rush’s racial epithets, but just throws some curses in Spanish as he slinks off. Grinning a small celebratory smile, Rush walks back towards his room and opens the door. Rush: My leadership powers are striking. You’d think that I’d at least get a restaurant chain named after me, but of course the sub-par beings don’t even know what’s good for them. Falling down onto his bed, exhausted, Rush doesn’t even bother to undress into more comfortable sleep wear, but just fluffs his pillow once (because the Voodoo Villain can not have a sub-par pillow!) and puts his head down. His eyelids are just about to shut when… “Oooooh Yeaaaaaaah…” “ Oh yeah!! Take me for a ride!” The sounds can be easily recognized as the sound of terrible quality lovemaking. Rush, probably a master in this sort of thing, hears the sounds as he goes to close his eyes. He tries to continue his sleep, but the awkward shouts stop any attempt at sleep for the Extreme Champion. Rush: I must’ve been some real ass in my last life, like Flaymin Aymin or the Pope or something. Very irritated once again, Rush gets up off his bed for a second time in the night and walks out the door, this time letting it slam loudly on purpose. He pinpoints the location of the sound, and bangs loudly on the door, at first only hearing whispers from inside. “No, C’mon honey, this is the perfect night.” “You just wait my muffin, I’ll be right back.” Rolling his eyes at the sappy language, Rush waits, tapping his foot in irritation as he sees a smallish man open the door. Normally the man wouldn’t seem small, but he seems very small in the presence of Rush, a 6’4 260 individual. “Yes sir, may I help you?” For a moment there is silence as Rush just looks at the man, blinking. Finally Rush opens his mouth and out comes his frusteration and agitation. Rush: Okay, listen here Peewee. I’m trying to get the sleep that I, as a first-rate citizen, deserve. The man tries to cut in, but Rush dismisses him with a swipe of the hand, and continues his verbal trashing. Rush: If you need to continue your sub-par sexual activity, then find another room somewhere, preferably on a different continent, because I’m not interested in hearing this bimbo fake an orgasm just to make you happy. Both the woman and man look shocked at this, and Rush turns to leave, while the man starts to yell after him. Rush doesn’t even look back, but just raises his left hand and extends his middle finger towards the man. He opens the door and shuts it quietly, very content with the night’s work. Rush: Rush Mason-2; Sup-Par Citizens-0 As our scene fades to black, we see Rush fading off into a deep restful first-rate sleep as a woman’s voice can be heard amongst the blackness. “How’d he know that I was faking it?”
