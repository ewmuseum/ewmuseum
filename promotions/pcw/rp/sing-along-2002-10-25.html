Sing Along

Title: Sing Along Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/25/02 -Duke Williams dressed in all black with kiss style makeup dances and sings as he vacuum’s his evil Goth lair. Its time to do some cleaning even Evil Gods need to be tidy. Dukestar: I'm too Gothey for my love, too Gothy for my love Love's going to leave me I'm too Gothy for my shirt, too Gothy for my shirt So Gothy it hurts And I'm too Gothy for PCW, too Gothy for PCW OSW and DWO And I'm too Gothy for your party, too Gothy for your party No way I'm disco dancing I'm a Goth God, you know what I mean And I do my little turn in the ring Yeah, in the ring, in the ring, yeah I do my little turn in the ring. I'm too Gothy for my car, too Gothy for my car Too Gothy by far And I'm too Gothy for my old cowboy hat Too Gothy for my cowboy hat, what d'you think about that I'm a Goth God you know what I mean And I do my little turn in the ring Yeah, in the ring, in the ring, yeah I shake my little touche in the ring -Duke shakes his ass as he vacuum’s and sings along. In the mist of ass shaking he realizes the PCW camera is on sending a feed back to the office. He quickly ceases his ass wiggling and gets serious as he changes to a less homo Goth remix.- Dukestar: ahhh I like Goth butts and I can not lie You other vamps can't deny!!! Death Baby got back! Oh I see the camera is on. Witness as this machine I use sucks up souls trapped deep in my carpet. You may say but Dukestar that is a vacuums cleaner. -Duke lets out one of his patented evil Goth laughs as he unveils the brand label on the side which obviously has been tampered with.- Dukestar: You see it’s a really black and deck her. So evil but yet it looks so simple, that’s the beauty of it. You norms never see it coming cus you think it’s a common house hold appliance. When in reality it sucks your soul out. I have the new model that even works on really fat people but I haven’t read the instructions on how to use that creature feature. -Duke lets out a random evil howl.- Dukestar: So later tonight I go head to head with the great white hype know as Snow. So normal even the normal people say damn that dog is normal. -Duke shakes his head.- Dukestar: How can you live being so f*cking plain?? Your like yogurt, hoss….the plain stuff…not even Vanilla just the flavorless goo….not even a berry or two….nope just plain yogurt. -Duke makes a sour face thinking of plain yogurt.- Dukestar: A man of your blandness if no match for a walking, talking., singing, swinging, Goth God. -Duke flexes his Goth muscles.- Dukestar: You see I’m the in thing hoss. You’re a chapter in PCW’s past. Sorry about that but it’s a fact. As a matter of fact it’s the Goth God Front Office Honky Tonk Truth, Hoss. Be ready to be sent Whiskey Bent and Goth Bound. -Duke goes back to siging and cleaning.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
