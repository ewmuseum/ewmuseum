Date: Joint RP -- Pharaoh #1 for Friday Fusion *A cool morning as Sandy knocks at the door of a stately Anaheim, CA manor. Sandy is dressed in a yellow sundress and her hair is down to the shoulders. She spared the make-up today, so her pale cheeks show. Skylar is simply dressed in jeans and a black long-sleeved T-shirt. His hair is tied in a ponytail.* Sandy:'It was nice of him to invite us to his house.' Skylar:'We're cool. And we're both celebrating wins at the PPV. #1 Contender's in our respective divisions. You bring the fruit basket?' *Sandy nods, the basket in her hand. But the fruit is wax.* Sandy: 'I couldn't get real fruit...' Skylar:'That's okay, he has people serving him anyway.' *The sound of footsteps from within litters the air before an audible 'click' interupts and the door flies open to reveal Titu Xansiv, wearing his usual Egyptian garb, standing in the doorway, a smile about his face.* The Pharaoh:'So glad you could join us today, Skylar! Do come in, the both of you.' Skylar:'Thank you for having us.' *Skylar extends his hand for a shake with Pharaoh. It is well-met. Pharaoh guides Skylar into the house with his free hand. Sandy hands over the basket of wax fruit.* Sandy: 'I didn't know what kind of fruit you like, so I went for something decorative.' *She reaches into her purse and brings out a bag of candy.* Sandy:'But I figured everyone likes chocolate, so I picked up this bag of Hershey's Kisses!' *Handing the bag over to Sandy, she lets herself in.* The Pharaoh: '....Thanks. So, Skylar, finally secured your rightful position as the Continental Title's number one contender I see! We both have great victories to celebrate!' Skylar: *Shrugging modestly*'Indeed.' *Skylar's head drifts around.* Skylar:'Well, I can't see anything, but this is the first time I've really been to your house so why don't you humor your guest with a grand tour?' *Pharaoh sighs* The Pharaoh:'Do I really have to, Sky? I mean...there's nothing to show really, aside from the secret catacombs, shrines, and tombs preserved for my death---' *Pharaoh scratches his head nervously.* The Pharaoh:'I said that outloud, didn't I?' *Sandy and Skylar nod their heads. Pharaoh stamps his foot on the ground and snaps his right fingers.* The Pharaoh:'Dammit!' *In Heaven, Baby Jesus cries. Skylar pats Pharaoh on the shoulder while Sandy has her back turned, looking at the sculpture of Nefertiti on Pharaoh's credenza. He makes a bit of a joke.* Skylar:'It's alright, as long as those catacombs and stuff don't lead to buried treasure or anything, your secret is safe with us.' *Pharaoh's eyes dart left and right shiftily.* Pharaoh:'Yeah... of course not...' *Skylar nods once more and then goes on in an innocently ribbing tone.* Skylar:'Good. Now is there some place we can sit down, or did the Egyptians not invest in such things as chairs?' *Titu chuckles, wafting a hand down a hallway to the trio's left.* The Pharaoh:'Of course, of course. Follow me.' *Titu leads the couple down the winding corridor until they come to an abrupt stop outside a huge arch-shaped hole in the wall. The three make their way into the lounge and sit down on the golden couches scattered about the room.* The Pharaoh:'This is the lounge, obviously. We don't have a TV, sorry-- Oh...I apologize again, my friend...it slipped my mind.' *Skylar gives a casual, unoffended shrug.* Skylar:'Meh, I can still listen to a television. And Sandy likes watching certain shows.' The Pharaoh: Well pardon my ignorance, but I don't really watch TV, it's not how things were in acnient times, you see. *At that moment, Isiy strides into the room, her arms folded over her ample bossom.* Isiy:'You don't like anything that isn't somehow related to your precious ancient Egypt.' Pharaoh cocks an eyebrow, staring at his wife, but his voice meant for Skylar and Sandy. The Pharaoh:'And here comes my charming wife.' *Skylar acknowledges Isiy with a gesture.* Skylar: *Nods* 'Isiy.' *Isiy returns the gesture.* Isiy: *Nods*'Skylar' *Sandy nods at Isiy as well to greet her.* Sandy *Nods*'Isiy' *Isiy gives the greeting in kind.* Isiy: *Nods*'Sandy.' *And a random white man, dressed in black pants and a red shirt, with brown hair and a fair complexion, gets in on the greetings with his own nod.* John Boy: *Nods*'Isiy.' *Isiy cocks her head to the side in confusion.* Isiy:'Who are you?' *Pharaoh stands up and tackles the stranger to the ground.* Pharaoh:'Those guards must be sleeping on the job again. GUARDS!' *Two overweight Irish guards walk in, dressed in Egyptian robes and wielding spears. They escort out the man who wandered into the house.* Pharaoh:'Stupid union...' *Sandy fidgets around on the couch trying to get comfortable.* Sandy:'Whose idea was it to have solid gold couches?' *Isiy stares at Pharaoh, who blushes lightly.* Pharaoh:'WHY DOES NO ONE BELIEVE ME THAT IT WAS A MISTAKE?! I meant to order the color gold, not solid gold' *He sighs, throwing his arms up in frustration as he falls back onto the solid gold loveseat.* Pharaoh: 'Nobody believes me! Bah!' *With a laugh, Skylar shrugs and shakes his head.* Skylar:'Must have ordered from the same furniture dealer as Tyler Nelson. He tends to get those two things confused.' *Pharaoh shrugs.* Pharaoh:'Perhaps so.' Isiy:'So what brings you two here?' Pharaoh:'I invited them.' Isiy: *Softly claps hands together once*'Okay, anyone want something to eat? The slaves--err servants are just cooking somthing up.' *Skylar shakes his head and right hand to decline. Sandy politely declines verbally.* Sandy:'No, thank you. We had breakfast earlier this morning. We're fine. I'd take some juice, if you have some. Pineapple?'
