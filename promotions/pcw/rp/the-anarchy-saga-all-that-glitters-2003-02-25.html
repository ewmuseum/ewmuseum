The Anarchy Saga: All that Glitters..

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-03 Stop me if you've heard this one... A senator walks into a bar, he sits down on a stool and looks at the bartender, 'A Gin and Tonic, and have you seen a man about this high, this wide, black hair?'. The bartender nods and says 'Yeah, that chap in the back, he came in about a hour ago and hasnt said a word to us since.' He gives the Senator his drink and goes to the other side of the bar. The senator walks to the man in the corner and begins to talk to him about enviormental policy/war liberties/ civil liberties... anything, the man in the corner tells the Senator what he wants, he wants to chop down old trees for a minimart/bomb Iraq/eliminate free speech in the media, the Senator gives a non-commital answer and the exchange is made. The senator carries a briefcase with a uncountable amount of money in it, all in a days work and in exchange for his word that the man will get one supporter, now here comes the funny part... This is a true story. Politicians practice this all the time. Call them bribes, kikcbacks, call them whatever you want. It is the same thing. Corruption. Its rampant in our apparent utopia (as the republicans would like you to believe) and the government that supports it wrongly. How can the ideals and worries of millions of people be decided by 50 white men in a air conditioned office somewhere, especially when they take more bribes then we make honestly in our lives. Corruption is what we call it. Its what happens when a generally good person does something bad, and continues to do it, usually for money. The Senator could very well put a idea into existence that would wipe out hundres of thousands of people, that could start a war. In which case the nation falls apart and so many people die that blood flows through the ocean like a oil slick. This is what our wars are fought for, for this monopoly on government that these middle aged idiots have over the American sheep. Right? True? None of it. Corrupt and wrong is what this 'democracy' is, they even lied about the name. Democracy would mean that all of the people decide what they want, what we are is a Republic. A Republic is a Dictatorship ran by a hundred other irrelevant yes men salivating over the money they can make when they sell you out. Do you think they give a shit about you or your family or your well being? Not very funny, is it?
