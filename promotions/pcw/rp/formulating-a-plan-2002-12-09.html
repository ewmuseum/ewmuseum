Formulating a plan

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-02 31-11-02 Bob Vurdo sits at his table in the little office out the back of his new enterprise KFC. He looks across the desk at one of his young goons who nervously stares at the gun laying on the table. He knows Bob Vurdo doesn't call you somewhere and then just leave a gun laying on the table by accident. This young man still has nightmares about the task he had to do for Bob Vurdo not long ago. This young man was responsible for the murder of two black woman and one young black boy. Bob Vurdo smiles at the youngster in an encouraging fashion. 'All right Peter I have it all planned out for you.... It will be a breeze.... In and out in not even a minute.' Bob places his hand on Peter's shoulder and smiles at him again. Peter manages to force a weak smile back at his boss before Bob continues. 'I have the camera's covering every part of this joint apart from one corner. The South West corner is security free so you won't get your shot on every paper in the country by morning.... That's not the hardest part though.... I don't want the Fed's up my ass with all this whacking going on so I want you to kill the guy, take his body out to the car and drive off.... You dispose of the body how you see fit.' Bob licks his lips and pauses for a few seconds. 'No one will know anything happened coz the guy I got working at that hour is a good actor and will be able to say he was too intimidated to inform the authorities.' Bob looks into the young mans eyes to make sure he is listening carefully to the next part. 'Make sure no one else is around and strike when you want to but don't let G-Dawg get out of there alive.... Stay outside the joint.... Take a few days if you have to just make sure you kill him without getting noticed.' 'What if he is with his friends?' Peter is obviously worried about the plan but Bob has it all covered. 'That's the thing.... These black obviously aint that smart and he comes in here most of the time without his friends and at odd hours.... I been watching through some of the old tapes.' Peter nods his head in approval to the well thought out plan. 'Remember to whack him between 9.30 and 12 cause that's when our man is at the counter.' The young man nods again. 'Take this gun and after you use it dump it somewhere with the body.' Bob slides the gun across the small table toward Peter. Peter picks up the gun and puts it in the inside pocket of his dull brown trench coat. 'This will be a big blow as G is there leader.... Make sure you get it right.' Peter gets up from his chair and walks out of the small office. Bob Vurdo knows that this is a risky mission but he has planned it out well. If Peter can pull it off then he will be a made man inside the Vurdo family. Being a made man in the Vurdo Family is a big thing as it means you have protection from nearly anyone in the whole of America. It also means your getting paid a little more to do your job well. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Current Date ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob Vurdo sits across the small table in the middle of the small office in the back of the KFC from Tony. Tony has just arrived and when he rang he said he had some good news. It's been two days since the murder of G-Dawg the leader of the blacks which went perfectly to plan. Tony has a smirk on his face as he looks across at Bob. 'We have defeated the black men.... They are as good as gone without their leader.... They have no one smart enough to run things now.' Tony smiles at Bob before continuing. 'I don't think we have to worry about them now.... They have no economic power and without that they're nothing.' Bob smiles 'That's great news.' 'Now lets get down to business.' As always Tony goes back to what comes first the business. 'Prostitution has hit the roof. We are making double the money we were just three months ago which is a fuck load of money. I still don't know why the influx has happened to rapidly but we shouldn't really worry about it. This is great fucking news Bob.' Bob smiles as he listens to Tony Bob is all about wealth and power. Tony continues 'The drug market is also steadily rising. We are selling a lot of Cocaine at the moment due to the increase in Amber Hills population. The new marketing campaign by the council has obviously made us a lot of money Bob and I think we should capitalize on this.' 'How?' Bob is a greedy man and always expects to know possible ways to make money. However he is very smart in the way he goes about it and never rushes into anything. 'We need to get the authorities working for us as much as possible.' Bob curiously looks on. 'Well the local election is coming up and I think it would be a good idea if you went for it.' Bob Vurdo smiles 'You know what.... That's a great idea.... I am sure I WILL make a great mayor.' 'Okay, Okay well let's get a plan together on how we will go about this.' Tony then reaches down beside him and pulls a briefcase up to the table. 'I have all the papers here on how to apply and what you have to do.' Tony opens up the briefcase and pulls out a blue folder and opens it up on the table. Tony begins flicking through the clear sleeves filled with various pieces of paper until he reaches the one he's looking for. It is an advertisement which reads 'Think you can be the next mayor?' In bold text at the top of the page. Bob turns the folder to face him and begins reading as Tony pulls out his cell phone from his pocket. He begins punching in a number until Bob interrupts. 'What are you doing?' 'I'm ringing up to apply for you.' 'I don't know just yet give me a day or so to read over it and have a good think about it.' Tony nods his head in a way that suggests he knows what Bob is saying and agrees with him. 'Okay.... But we haven't got much time to come up with a plan so lets start one now and you can think about it overnight.' 'Why are you so enthusiastic about this?' Tony is very rarely so quick to get into things which makes Bob suspicious. 'This could bring big things for the Vurdo family Bob and I'll be damned if we are going to miss out.' 'Just give me a day to think it over then we'll start planning.' ***
