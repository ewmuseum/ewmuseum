Jigen, the first.

Date: #1 The world would pass around him, but he would remain the same. That has become a valid fact in these past few years of being in the public’s eye. It was only three years ago that anyone had heard of the name Inphino Blitz and had no clue of who he was. Now, there is nowhere that he cannot travel without someone having heard of the Black Dragon Master, his accomplishments, and his life. Blitz desires for the live he had lived, which is why he spends most of his time a recluse, on air few times, fewer interviews and appearances. It was definitely apparent how he wished to live his life, spending all his free time in his solitary home near Sobama beach on Sado Island, Japan. The island itself is one of exile, little populated with peace and quiet. Some often say that it was the fast-paced society of Japan that drove Inphino to Sado… others said he banished himself there … “for the sins he had committed”…So now he sits… alone in the back room of his training hall. At the back of the wall sits a small Buddhist shrine, a single stick of burning incense sending thin wisps of smoke into the air. And before that shrine he sat… thinking… pondering… clearing his mind. There’s been too much cluttered since becoming a wrestling star, and there’s never enough time to sort everything out. Most wrestlers worry only about when to travel, their accommodations, or their next opponent… but Inphino cared nothing about any of them. Mina took care of arrangements like that; leaving him with an abundant of time for his own ventures. Mina told him that Brian Arson of the Primetime Players had accepted his open challenge, and she had accepted it for him. Normally Mina would hold out for the greater gain, like Kano or Propaganda, but like fools they had ignored him. Arson made a wise decision in challenging Inphino, and when those two stepped into the ring he would be rewarded for it. Instead of lifting weights, or practicing his martial arts, or strategizing, Inphino meditated. His mind needed to be in order so he could accomplish anything. He believed that his defeat to Propaganda was only because he had no focus. The same mistake… would not be made again. Blitz sat with his knees folded beneath his own, his black pants pressed neatly with palms pressed face down on his thighs. His muscles could be seen through the tight black shirt he wore under the dim lighting, shadows clinging to the rips in his arms and chest. And his black hair was pulled back in a ponytail, as to not distract him from focus. Everything was in order. All was calm…A gust of wind blew for a moment, the candle flickering atop of its slender length. His eyes opened slowly, watching it return to stillness. He could do the same, but something else was disturbed.Inphino walked out of his training hall and stood in front of the doorway. The garden was in order, fountain trickling into Koi pond, rock garden untouched. This area was clear. From there he treaded barefoot onto the oak porch and reached for the sliding door of the parlor. His hand hesitated for a moment, eyes narrowing as he stared into the milky white screen. Then with one motion he pushed the door open… and found nothing but a darkened room. His nerves lowered to an ease, a soft breath blowing from his lips. He walked into the dark parlor and moved towards the kitchen. In the background his motions could be heard softly, porcelain sitting on the counter, water pouring into a cup. Inphino walked back into the darkened parlor and stopped in the middle of the room. The table was at his side, and only part of the room was visible by the light of the moon entering from outside. He remained looking ahead at the wall, his brow furrowing as his hand slowly raised and put the white cup to his lips. “You have no place here.”From the darkness a white cloud of smoke started to trail into the moonlight. Inphino didn’t turn towards the intruder, but lowered his cup to his side. “Where is Mina?”The person had no words for him, merely sat there as smoke billowed steadily into the light. The muscles in Inphino’s jaw began to shift, his jaw setting as his teeth clenched. “Answer me.”Suddenly there was the sound of porcelain breaking, a tinkling sound as the pieces clattered against each other and fell to the hard wood floor. Whoever this intruder was, they had succeeded in doing the one thing many have never, and that’s draw fluid emotion from the stone known as Inphino Blitz.“So… it seems you still have a shred of humanity left in you. How quaint”The voice of that woman was so familiar. It was so smooth, so hypnotic… so cruel. It could only belong to one woman. Inphino looked towards her, fists balled up at his sides. His right hand was moist from the water spilt from his crushed cup. “I won’t ask you again, girl. Where is Mina?”Slowly she leaned into the light, and was revealed for who she was. Her long locks were pulled back in a ponytail, save two thick bangs hanging at the sides of her beautiful face, dark blue with black tips. There was no cigarette lit, yet the smoke that floated into the air was cold vapor seeping from the pouting lips… of the Divine Prophetess. “Your manners are despicable, Inphino. You have a guest, and you will not sit with her? And I thought chivalry leeched for dear life off you.”Inphino had no intention of being gentlemanly to her. She after all was not a woman, but an unholy demon spawned from pure evil. Cyclonica was not natural, anyone could tell by her abnormal behavior. When she saw the stone gaze in his eye, Cyc merely smirked, tilting her head to the side coyly. “I think you should sit and listen to my words, Black Dragon Master… that is, if you care anything for that poppet of yours, Mina.”That was the single condition that would hold Blitz at anyone’s mercy. Cautiously he sat down in the chair in front of her, scooting close to the table with his hands in his lap. There was no reason to trust her, but after hearing what she had to say he had no choice. “Now is not the time to toy with me, oracle,” he warned her. “I have a serious match to prepare for.”She looked at him like his words were shocking. “Oh? I thought you had to fight Brian Arson?” Before he could respond, she smirked and folded her hands in front of her on the table. “Yeah, I think the series of events about to unfold in front of you are a lot more important than a match with Arson. This is a matter of life and death… pretty much MY life and YOUR death.”That last remark brought a twinge in the eyebrow of Blitz, a threatened curiosity jumping in his spine. “You are familiar with my father, correct?”A nod, “The god, as you two so claim…”Now she nodded. “I stand by my beliefs, and I’ll thank you not to mock them.”“You’re also a certified psycho.”She was set to respond in spite, yet she smiled slickly back at him, wagging a finger as to scold him. “That’s PSYCHIC. Anyways, my father has your woman, and if there’s anything you wish to do about it, you’ll come with me.”“How much truth do you think your words hold? Fact and fiction make no difference in your eye.”She agreed with a nod of her head. “Yet how can one sort the two if they are believed to be one or the other? What distinguishes between the two is belief, and whether you believe in a lie or believe in the truth it makes no difference. What does matter is that my dear father has your dear friend, and from what I’ve seen in my dreams, I don’t like the looks of things.”Blitz watched the expression change on her face, going from a snide superiority to despair and defeat. Then he understood her urgency. “Ah, has yon father left you for another woman, moreso mines?”Her eyes shifted downwards slowly, lower lip pouting. It was almost delicious, watching such a beast of a human at a low point instead of being on top. “The fact alone is hurtful enough, yet for Akiyama to take Mina of all people… that’s like a knife in the back, am I right?”Cyclonica looked up with her teeth clenched. “You have no idea…”He nodded, folding his arms across his chest. “So… in order to get what we want back, we have to work together. Is that it?”The Divine Prophetess nodded, yet a crafty look came into her eye as she leaned back into the darkness of the room slowly. “Or… we could let the swap be and play our options…”Inphino’s eyes glanced down to his lap, and then back across the table at Cyclonica. He was definitely not interested. “Remove it… now.”She stood up from the table, stepping into the light of the moon. Her black leather pants shined in the light, and her own tight binding black corset cinched her torso almost tortuously. Her ponytail whipped around as she looked back at him, that cold stare returning to her eyes so classically. “Have it your way, monk. Shall we give chase, then?”
