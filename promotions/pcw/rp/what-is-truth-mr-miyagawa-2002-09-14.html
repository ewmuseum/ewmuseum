What is truth Mr. Miyagawa?

14 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/14/02 -The Malice man sits at his desk with the official seal of the PCW presidency on the front. He smokes a big cigar as he stares into the camera with a cocky smile on his face.- Williams: Riddle me this Miya……. -Duke stands up grabs his crouch adjusts then sits back down.- Williams: As the colored kids say, whoop there it is. The question you propose in your Legend Inc style rambling is accurate. Although a tad juvenal in delivery for this humble man of the world. Comic books? Don’t you have something better to do like maybe throw your self in the river and die? -Duke shrugs his shoulders.- Williams: I don’t know just a suggestion. I implore to reach the caliber of dialogue needed to speak with the President OF PCW. Do so or you will receive the ignorance tax. -Duke takes a big puff on his cigar.- Williams: Although your question may be sound it is a head of the game. The first question that should be asked is not who will defeat evil but rather who is the evil? Who is the bad guy? Wasn’t Batman nothing more then a psychopathic mercenary taking the law into his own hands? Not living by the constitution of our great land but rather driven by events that took place in his youth. Now Miya I don’t know what the priest did to you as a child but it isn’t my problem hoss. -Duke talks with his hands as he always does.- Williams: You see the Joker was nothing more then a capitalist looking to make a living for himself. He employed hundreds of men in an industry that generated revenue and commerce in downtown Gothlem. Batman acting out not with justice but rather with vengeance taking the law in his own hands killed a great community icon. Putting hundreds of young men out of a job leaving them nothing for their families. So Mr. Miya who is the bad guy? I think….I think….your implying by your actions and statements that you feel the Front Office is evil or the bad guys rather then the saviors of PCW. Is it really that way? Real…good word. -Duke laughs.- Williams: You my friend live in fantasy. You are a fantasy wrestler talking about fictional characters and unrealistic scenarios. I on the other hand live in reality. I live with the fact that PCW has to pay bills. The fact that PCW has to generate interest in order to win ratings and that ever important advertiser dollar. The fact that I am responsible for the employment of hundreds of people. I am a true American Hero as is the entire Front Office. Your like a kid waiting to get an expensive toy for Hubbard-mas you just do not understand value. -Duke sighs and shakes his head.- Williams: So again ask yourself WHO is the bad guy? Perception and truth are two different worlds aren’t they Mr. Miyagawa. The fans perceive you and your cackle of misfits as men fighting the system. The fans perceive the Front Office as a bunch of crooks trying to hurt PCW. But what is truth? The truth is you kids hold as much value as a some loud, obnoxious, rock band, hell bent on destroy social ills by swearing on a record to brain wash the youth. In reality…there is that word again Mr. Miyagawa…in reality the truth is they are doing much more damage to the system as a whole rather then helping it. They are getting nice and rich off of the whole outlaw image aren’t they Mr. Miyagawa. Or should I replace they with you? -Duke smiles.- Williams: I noticed in your last pithy presentation of ponder you managed to plug your new T-shirt. -Dukes eyes get bigger to accent the irony.- Williams: The truth is you make a lot of money off of ignorant kiddies buying your apparel. That is fact isn’t it Mr. Miyagawa. So really your banshee behavior is nothing more then a marketing ploy on your end. Hey hoss its cool to be mental and it sells. Kid I see right through you. Your what, about 22 years of age? I’ve been in this business for almost as long as you have been breathing. No tricks you can pull on me that I haven’t concocted in my youth. I’ve live on the wild said and I know you ain’t living long like that. So lets get down to facts once again and the fact is you’re a hypocrite. I’m not afraid of you because your not nuts your rather intelligent. I know your nothing more then a guy trying to shill his name for a few dollars. Oh yea you play the whack job with the best of them pal, but your no Jeremy Howard are you hoss? You like me are nothing more then a salesman. I accept what I am, you don’t. -Duke takes a sip of water sitting on his desk.- Williams: You know the crazy gimmick sells almost as good as the redneck one. I was able to act them both out at the same time. Ha Ha Ha Damn am I good. -Duke smiles at him self.- Williams: So how do I rectify our dispute? How do I get paid back for the damage to my car and more important my reputation?? How do I make you see the light like a whore in church???? Pain, maybe. Mental anguish, appealing. Malice, now your right on. -Duke lips snarls up like Elvis in the mist of a song.- Williams: Mr. Miyagawa I have played nice for as long as I can. Not anymore. The hit is set and the pain is forthcoming. Fearing me is wise but I can’t make those decisions for you. I will act as the electro shock therapy to set your mind back to reality. Prozac is a joke compared to some good old fashion Malice. You can quote me on that. So to come full circle as I like to do in closing. The answer to the question who has the power to defeat all that is evil? The answer is “The Malice Man” Duke Williams. Its just in your mind your you may see it a little different. I however speak with fact and you can not dispute fact can you Mr. Miyagawa. -Duke puts out his cigar is the camera pans in on Duke’s logo.- =w=
