In the Heat of the Night

2 June 2002

Date: June 2, 2002 A black van wonders through the night to the gas station where an anonymous call was made a few minutes ago. The woman walks to the van and climbs in the front passenger seat. 'What did you think you was doing. How did this all happen?' 'It's a long story and I just want to go home!' 'I don't think you should go home right now. The police might already be their. How bout you wait until this thing blows over. You are always welcome at my house,' he says, placing his hand on the woman's leg caressing it, rising his hand up her skirt. She is too tired to realize what he is doing. The cameras come from behind the passengers in the van (the driver and the front seat passenger) to the front of the van. It is none other than Stable members John Crichton and Jaden. 'Hey since I am going to be staying at your house I need to stop past PCW, I have an extra set of clothes their.' John puts an intriguing grin on his face, thinking I can get another shot at Jaden since Big Daveylicious and Jaden broke up a few days back. 'I can do that babe,' he says making a U-turn to head to PCW. Jaden falls asleep in the car and John can not take his eyes off her. Her beauty intrigues him. He sees her as an angel sent by God to him, so precious, pure, and loving. He begins to lick his lips and think of the continuous things he wants to do with Jaden, however, he knows it will be hard to since the Stable started. As they pull up to, the PCW Jaden is still sleep. He wants to take advantage of this moment but he cannot. He knows how women are about the whole rape situation, especially an African/Latin-American Woman of her age. “Jaden, we are here, he says rocking her by the shoulder!” “Umm-huh, oh. Thanks Davey,” she says oblivious, deaf of what’s going on. This is the most sleep she has hand in a while. “Davey, I am not Davey,” John thinks in his head…”maybe this will work all out for the best!” As they enter the PCW Arena, they are all alone, not a soul in sight. “John I really want to thank you for all that you have done for me. You have been a complete gentlemen. You have not even hit on me all night,” she says, with burning cheeks, but she is also appreciative for that. Jaden then give John a friend kiss on his side of the face; however they both wanted more as Jaden and Johns body took as one. Their lips were uniting as one. At that moment their mind, soul, and body went to a new level. Nevertheless, they did not know they where being watched. They had an unexpected visitor… “What! No, this cant be happening,” he says, in his mind, as he remembers what Jaden said to him earlier. “I AM NOT READY FOR A RELATIONSHIP AT THIS CURRENT MOMENT…I DON’T THINK I CAN GET BACK WITH YOU EVEN BECAUSE MY ANGEL NEEDS ME MORE THAN YOU, MY BABY. However, in a since he does not care about the baby, all he wants is Jaden. His eyes turn green with enviousness, as he watches his one true love and John roll around in the center of the PCW Arena ring, unclothed and sharing a moment of passion. He does not know what to do other than leave… “Enjoy your life Jaden because I am out of it, FOREVER!” The scene ends with John Crichton and Jaden Lopez enjoying each other company in the center of the PCW ring, hearing the springs of the ring screech and rattle. The moans of the two ends this episode entitled, In the Heat of the Night!
