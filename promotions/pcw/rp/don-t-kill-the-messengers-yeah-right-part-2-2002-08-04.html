Don't kill the messengers?...Yeah, Right(Part 2)

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 4, 2002 [Hot, a very simple adjective, meaning to have a high temperature. Basically, think Texas, think Northern Texas. The sun is somewhat low in the sky, the heat lines that head across a lonely stretch of highway are like a river. Constantly flowing, constantly moving. A 2000 Lincoln Continental is heading down that highway. The speed of it is rather academic…60 in a 55 zone. Pretty much the norm for anyone in the U.S. The car suddenly stops and turns into an airfield. The security guard steps out of his booth with a clipboard and heads for the driver’s side window. The guard seems rather out of shape with a stomach the size of a cantaloupe. He waves the car onto the infield, and he heads back to the booth to open the gate. The car, drives onto the tarmac. The car eventually comes to a stop just near a taxiway heading to a hangar. Two men step out of the stopped car. One man wearing a three piece business suit, the other wearing less-than-casual attire, with a tank top and jean shorts. The suit man looks at his watch.] Tank-top man: When did the plane leave? Suit man: Two hours ago. Tank-top man: It should be here by now. Suit man: Well, give it time, alright? Maybe he got held up at customs. Maybe the plane was delayed due to traffic. Tank-top man: Maybe he got arrested. Suit man: That’s not funny. [The Tank-top guy looks about the grounds as the Suit guy grabs something from the glove compartment of the Continental. A pair of binoculars comes out of the passenger side window within the Suit guy’s hands. He places them on his eyes and looks around the sky. The tank-top guy takes a sip from a bottle of water. The suit guy locks his direction due east.] Suit man: Here he comes! [A Lear jet makes its way from the east with its landing gear down. It touches down with no problems. It hits reverse thrust and the brakes. The Aircraft’s speed slows considerably. It taxis off the runway, and stops near the Continental. It applies the parking brake, and the door opens up. Out steps our villain, Eddie Mandrake, wearing a black leather jacket, black jean shorts, Nike shoes, and a white shirt saying “Got Blood?” He looks about the area, and, with his bag, he heads down the steps of the aircraft onto the hot tarmac. Both men approach Eddie.] Suit man: Hello Edward, My name is James Gallagher; I am your staff representative to the office of PCW. If you need anything, let me know. Along with me is my assistant, Steve Folio. Steve: Hi Eddie. [Eddie just nods, loads his bag in the trunk, and enters the car. Steve and James look at each other and enter the car as well. It begins to leave the airport and head back to the highway. Inside the air conditioned Continental, Eddie just stares out the window, James has his briefcase, and Steve…well…He is trying to crack open a Pepsi™ without pulling the tab. James finally speaks up after a few minutes on the highway.] James: We received your signed contract just recently via UPS. You are a very interesting individual, Edward. [Eddie just nods in agreement.] James: Um, we are heading to the Holiday Inn Lubbock Park hotel. It’s a great hotel, talent stays on the fourth floor, and the staff stays on the fifth. You have to get your own ice and drinks. Room service is already paid…GAHH! [Steve managed to open the Pepsi™ can with his tooth. The cola spilt all over James’ pants, and he winces as the stains set into the fabric. Eddie just continues to stare out the window.] James: Steve, I’m sending you the bill. Steve: I DID IT! I OPENED AN ALUMINUM CAN WITHOUT USING THE TAB! WHOO-HOO! Eddie: You must be very proud of yourself, jackass. I’m surprised you still have a job. Steve: HA ha…Hmm…. [James looks at Eddie with a mixed reaction. Half was served perfectly for what he did to his suit. The other half was not so good because of what he said to his friend. The car passes a sign saying, “Welcome to Lubbock”] James: That was a little rude Edward. Eddie: Look, once we get to the hotel, I need to go to downtown. I have errands to run James: What errands? You’re a wrestler for PCW now. What errands can you possibly run? Eddie: I have errands to run in town, which is all you need to know. James: Well, make sure that you are on time for the monday talent meeting. The office members do not appreciate it when rookies are late for meetings or shows. Eddie: Yeah, yeah, I’ll be there. [The car enters the hotel’s parking lot.] Steve: Well, here we are. Hey Eddie, you want to get a beer? [Eddie, with one of the most deadly looks, looks at Steve. Steve simply backs away as Eddie exits the car with his bag in tow. He closes the door behind him, as the car speeds to a near-by restaurant.] Eddie: Geez. What a freak. [Eddie shakes his head, and with his bag, enters the front door of the hotel to check in.]
