SHP's return!

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/9/02 'HEY, I NEED SOME HELP OVER HERE!' comes a scream from the dock. The ocean breeze and saltwater irritates the nose of the vampire laying on the back of a huge crate. Below him stands The Jester, leaning with one elbow on the wood. Both are in their usual attire and stay hidden from sight. The dock workers try to assist the safe movement of a massive containment unit that rocks back an forth on the end of a crane. 'That's the one, the grey metallic thing.' Says the vampire in a calm tone of voice. 'Looks like that scene in Jurassic Park where they try to get the first Velociraptor in. Are we going to feed one of the workers to it?' replies Gaz, picking at his custom smart suit. 'Nope. Let me call the shots on the pranks from now for a while. Inside that cage is 800 lbs worth of black and white furry lawsuit, waiting to happen. I need a distraction from you. You get the workers attention, I release the beast.' Nerezza explains, laying motionless and relaxed, looking up at the blue sky through red glass. 'After that, HIDE.' 'What's in that crate anyway?' Asks Gaz, opening a small container that resembles a glasses case. He removes a clear glass capsule that has a yellow liquid floating inside. He proceeds to shake it up inside and bubbles begin to form, fizzing ominously. 'That contains what we stole from NTICW before we were fired for use of our weapons after the Extreme Regime was banished. Speaking of which...' Nerezza stands up on his crate and reaches inside the inner pockets of his flowing, sleeveless red trenchcoat. He removes a leather scabbard that curves in shape like the edge of a Cutlass. The living nightmare unzips it and folds it open, revealing a series of small cylinders, one of which has a groove cut into it. As he pieces each together, they lock into place with a satisfying SNAP! Gaz looks up, recognizing the sound instantly. As the final part screeches into place, it creates a high pitched noise that makes Gaz cover his ears in pain. 'Ah, it's good to be back...' Nerezza says, ramming the last part home. It glistens in the bright sunlight and the reflection forces Nerezza to turn his eyes away as the shine penetrates his lenses. 'You talk as though you were that thing...' Gaz mumbles, staring at the construction. A metal pole almost as tall as Nerezza himself stands up, supporting a curved blade at the top. 'Presenting, the all-new, improved fully metal version...' Nerezza grins with childish glee. 'I just couldn't come to PCW without my scythe...' 'Great. Your toy is with you. Now can we get along with the prank?' Gaz says, looking at the bubbles and foam inside the capsule. Nerezza stops rubbing his cheek along the top of the blade and focuses on the task. 'My bad. Now, when I give the signal, you get their attention.' the red-clad wrestler commands, leaping off the crate and onto the ground, scurrying away and hiding amongst the other items littering the dock. Gaz merely waits, looking at the fizzing mixture inside the tube. A ray of sunlight soon hits his face, prompting him to look up. On top of the crane and masked from view by the crate itself was his partner, using the metallic object in his gloved hands to signal with a reflection. Taking his cue, Gaz then throws the stink bomb in the direction of the dock workers. The glass breaks apart on impact and scatters the yellow liquid with a popping noise, spreading a disgusting smell over the dock. Nerezza clamps his nose but the dock workers fall prey to the stench. Even the seagulls disperse under the intensely sickening aroma. The workers spot the Emerald Menace and quickly begin a barrage of language as foul as the stink, abandoning their posts and running towards the prankster. 'Let's go for a jog, fat boys!' Gaz taunts and turns on his heels, drawing the large men away. Nerezza gets to work picking the lock on the front of the cage with claws, un-noticed by the group of sailors. A particularly huge mountain of a man gives up, puffing and panting as his legs fail to carry the enormous weight they support. 'Back to ze cage!' he yells in a thick german accent. His colleagues halt the chase and return, following the presumed leader. Nerezza quickly grabs the top of the crane for safety and holds his scythe tightly. With one swipe, he severs the only thing holding the cage off the ground, forcing it to make a premature landing. The massive structure slams into the ground and the door comes off the hinges, jamming a look of terror onto the faces of the workers. 'IT'S LOOSE!' yells the German, as from the cage wanders an animal... black, white and covered in thick body fur, sporting a pink scarf tied round its neck. The panda bear sniffs the air and spots the group of petrified beholders in front of it. With a snarl, it lumbers forward and makes a beeline for the largest man it can see. 'RUN, BUSTER BIGGS, RUN FOR YOUR LIFE!' screams one of the smaller men, already taking his own advice with the rest of the crew and scattering across the dock like ants running from a wave of boiling water from a kettle. Gaz watches from a distance while Nerezza hangs from one claw, surveying the mayhem from the top of the now-abandoned crane. His eyes watch cruelly from behind the red lenses and he giggles in anticipation as the slobbering bear makes his way closer and closer to the motionless victim. Making his way back down, he listens as the man's panic-ridden screams fill the salty air. At the bottom of the crane, a bemused Jester waits for him, safely concealed behind the dock's numerous cargo. 'So you brought the Sexual Harassment Panda over here to set him free on an unsuspecting member of the public workforce??!' Gaz exclaims. '.....yeah, that's about the thick of it.' Nerezza grins, reveling in the distressed cries. 'And I thought I was disturbed' Gaz grumbles, shaking his head in mock disappointment. The two walk away, with Nerezza seeming to bask in the sounds of the man's only shrieks... 'Not ze pants.... NOT THE PANTS! AAAAAAAH!'
