Duke....Me And My Jaw REALLY Hate You!

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/04/02 Ellie~!’s prologue : I hate Duke Williams, I hate Duke Williams, I hate Duke Williams! Everything was going so well, Xavier had won the Continental Title, he is the Commissioner and the Ultimate Authority could, like, tell everyone what to do...Add to that Walden sending us our very own lackey and everything was, like, SO great....Until Duke Willaims decided to stop being that old fat man at that table thing and became that old fat man wrestler again....I hate Duke Williams, I hate Duke Williams, I hate Duke Willaims..... “Owww......Owww......Owww....” Our shot open up in h living room of Xavier Kannon’s Beverly Hills residence where he is lying back on the Black leather sofa holding a bag of ice to his jaw. Xavier : “I think that redneck, hillbilly sack of shit broke my broke my jaw....OWWW!” His jaw is visibly swollen after the hard punches levelled at him by Duke Williams, who Sunday night was drawn out of retirement by Xavier’s taunts, actions and ridiculing of his legacy. The Scientologist is wearing a Black “Ultimate Authority” t-shirt with Gold lettering and a pair of his Black, worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green embroidery snaking down the left leg to his bare feet that are resting on a footstool. Xavier : “Well he may think that Sabotage meant something, he may think people care that he dragged his fat ass out of retirement and is risking permanent injury every time he steps in the ring....But to me it just proved how truly idiotic he is!” The Gold Patron Meritorious goes to chuckle but winces at he pain in his jaw and holds the ice pack tighter to his jaw, looking in discomfort but not enough to stop his attack on PCW President. Xavier : “Well.....DUKE....As soon as you put that old age ass of yours back on the active wrestling roster....You plumped that flabby behind right into my duristiction! I am now free to book you in any match I want! I can book you in a 3 on 1 against Jay Sylo, Akiyama and Karl LeBlanc and watch the 3 biggest men in PCW tear you limb from limb!” ......I think Xavi hates Duke about as much as me....Duke may insult me a lot but that is just because he’s jealous of how great I am, he hits Xavier because he hates him, and one day Xavi is going to hit him back.... The Scientologist manages a smirk through his mask of discomfort as he envisions the 3 giants of Primetime Championship Wrestling throwing Duke around the ring to finally finish off the former Continental Champion’s career. Xavier : “Or I could book you in a 4 on 1 against the New Breed and see them hit the Quadruple Superkick on that wrinkled old head out yours until you become comatose.....But Duke, despite the fact I COULD do that....I’m not going to....” He takes a second to pause and opens his mouth as wide as he can, trying to loosen up his jaw through the swelling as h keeps the ice pack pressed against it. Xavier : “....I’m not even going to book you against Mike Frampton.....In fact, I’M NOT GOING TO BOOK YOU FULL STOP!“ As soon as he finishes shouting he screws his face up in pain and mutters obscenities under his breath in frustration at his sore jaw. ........See what Duke has done to him? Williams is so scared that Xavi’s wise words will convert all those idiots who follow Crunk to, like, Scientology that he hit his jaw to try and silence him...But it, like, SO didn’t work..Well, it kinda did as it hurts like hell.... Xavier : “You see Duke, wrestlers pride themselves on how often they get matches....I myself, at Turmoil when I make my 1st successful Title defence against Frisco will be competing in my 8th straight match....While you Duke, after Sabotage, can look forward to 8 straight shows of doing absolutely nothing! Unless that is....I decide to find a match Worthy of you!” His hate for Duke Williams is evident on his face as a permanent sneer is plastered on his face as he talks about the PCW President and thorn in his side as Commissioner. Xavier : “Perhaps I should bring back some of the.....Quote Un-Quote.....TALENT you signed before we managed to find a capable Personnel Director to face you. Duke v lance Bell anyone? No, I don’t think so! Duke taking on Avalanche? Well...Before I was Commissioner that really COULD have happened! Duke against Damien Xaviers.....Well, I’d laugh until I couldn’t breath at that on but because of you and your uncultured fighting style...My jaw is a bit saw.” The Continental Champion leans his head back and closes his eyes, holding the ice tighter to his swollen mandible. “I hate Duke Williams, I hate Duke Williams, I hate Duke Willaims........ I........ HATE...... DUKE....... WILLIAMS!!!” Into the White coloured, minimalist styled living room storms Xavier’s valet Eleanor Hall, also looking none too pleased at Jeremy Howard’s replacement as most hated male. Xavier : “So do I my little Shag Squirrel, so do I....” The Scientologist mutters as Eleanor paces around the White carpet, wildly gesturing with her hands and sneering as she harshly speaks through her teeth. Eleanor : “He is directly flouting my authority as Commissioneress by ruining a perfectly good match with his stupid brawling of stupidness, all that after he, like, still made the match go ahead after we SO tried to cancel it!” “I GOT IT ELLIE~! I’M BACK FROM BULGARIA AND I’VE GOT IT!!!” Dropping down ono the carpet exhausted as soon as he enters he living room is a dishevelled looking Jordan X Shabazz, prospective PCW wrestler and all round lackey of the 1st couple of Scientology. ......He, like, looks kinda familiar.....I think he might be some guy off the street I gave money to so they would leave me a lone...Anyway....Whay is he in the house...... Eleanor : “Er.....Who is this again? He looks familiar....” Ellie~! looks down at the man who is panting for breath on the floor, gagging for breath and holding his head in his grubby hands. Xavier : “Um...It’s Jordan X Shabazz...You know, he came to the house on Sunday, said he’d do anything to be a PCW wrestler?” She still doesn’t seem enlightened as to who the scruffy and close to passing out man by her feet is. Jordan slowly pulls a hand out from under him and in his grubby palm he holds a bright shiny Silver ca of Tab Clear. Jordan : “Got drink.....Ellie~!.....Flight from Bulgaria diverted to Texas....Hitched to 30 miles away.....Walked.......Duke......Mocking Snapple~!.....Says Ellie~! was a C-.....Something no nice.....Do I get my tryout match....” His head drops down onto the carpet and only his outstretched hand shakes where it holds the chilled can of Soda. Xavier looks down at the rookie, not looking too happy. Xavier : “When he recovers remind me to have him shampoo that carpet.....And de-flea it by the look of him...” Eleanor looks down a the man and sees the can of Tab Clear in his hand and slowly squats down to cautiously take it from his hand....Being careful not to disturb the scruffy looking developmental talent. .........Hmmm.......I know that Soda, I was thinking about it the other day.....I could have sworn I ordered some from the shops...Must have been wrong, anyway, I’ll have his...I think we own him.... Eleanor : “Is that Tab Clear? Wow...I haven’t seen that for years! Think he’ll mind if I have it?” Kannon sighs at his valet, not remembering she sent him to Bulgaria just to get her 1 can of the clear cola. Xavier shakes his head and reclines in his seat, finally taking the ice pack away from his chin. Xavier : “Okay, now you have your soda and Shabazz her is taking a well earned rest the Duke Williams situation has to be addressed. Not only does he viciously attack me....Now he is disrespecting PCW’s OFFICIAL soft drink AND it’s Commissioneress.....And if these rumours of that redneck promoting Crunk are true then the Ultimate Authority will need to take action.” As Ellie~! takes a sip from the Tab Clear, Jordan starts to stir on the floor and mumbles. Jordan : “Diss.....Ellie~!.....Duke Williams did.....Said.....” Jordan’s head again drops down onto the White carpet and Xavier sighs. Xavier : “Great, that’s going to grind the dirt in even more! Don’t worry yourself over him my little Poon Puppy...I’ll take care of Duke soon enough, and with more people like Jordan joining us we will revolutionise PCW!” The Scientologists rises to change his ice pack and Eleanor follows him out into the hall leaving Jordan sprawled out on the floor, exhausted and shivering now he has come back around. Jordan : “Cold....So Cold.....Need PCW contract.....Do anything......Beat up anyone.....So cold...” Again his head drops heavily down onto Xavier’s carpet as the shot fades to Black on his broken and tired body.
