Second Chances

9 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/04 The cold, dreary night had set in on the metropolitan New York City as a slight drizzle fell from the sky. The harsh winter refused to pass by, all the while sending its piercing winds to April. Not a soul can be seen outdoors but a faint silhoutte of a short, built figure. He walked down the abandoned street without a worry in his mind keeping his eyes still on the road ahead. Stopping in front of a large warehouse he gazed up at the structure and nodded his head side to side. 'No turning back now' Pushing the metal front door open with his palm a loud screech is heard interrupting the silence of the night. Surprisingly the inside of it is vast emptiness covered by the metallic walls. Just a big soundproof box... The man pushes his head back bouncing his hair out of his face revealing himself as the Extreme Champion of PCW, Shaun Styles. Docked in plain street clothes, an old jeans jacket covering a white shirt and blue denim pants he walks further into the darkness. 'Hello!?!' he calls out into the empty complex, his voice bouncing off the walls...No answer. From behind a large brown bag is thrown on Styles covering his head. He struggles to get free when another set of hands ball into a fist and knock him down on the tiled floor. All that is seen is black until finally he lifts his heavy eyelids open once again after what seems to have been a week. The first thing he sees is Don Laproni standing in front of him pacing back and forth with his hair slicked back and a leather suit carressing his figure. His two bulky handymen, Tony and Young, are standing side by side behind him with their hands crossed. He attempts to get up but notices that he is tied down to a chair. 'You should've kept the bag on, it was an improvement.' A smile graced the don's face as he said this while looking at Shaun's now beaten up face. 'We've noticed you had some recent success in your wrestling investment lately.' 'What can I say? I'm blessed.' Shaun retorted with a slick look on his face. It was immediatly erased as Laproni used an open hand to smack Styles. 'Don't get wise with me, punk. I asked you to come here for a reason. I see you've had a couple of victories under your belt and one thing I know is that victories don't come cheap. You owe me a shit load of money my friend and that's the only reason you're still alive. Please...Give me another reason.' Shaun Styles reaches into his pocket with his one free hand and takes out an envelope. Don Laproni snatches it away from him in a heart beat and tears it open. Looking through it he finds a wad of cash, looking satisfied he cuts the ropes from the chair and Shaun Styles gets to his feet. 'I'll be expecting more.' Laproni says as he turns around and walks out of the abandoned warehouse. No turning back now
