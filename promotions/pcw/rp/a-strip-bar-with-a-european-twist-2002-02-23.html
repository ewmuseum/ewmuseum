A strip bar with a European twist!

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/02/02 The scene opens up on a dark night a dark rainy night. It looks like a desolated sleezy part of town and it is lined with bums, strip joints, porno shops, liquor bars and lots of drunks and all round a load of shifty un trustworthy people. An aging hooker on the street corner stands in a red latex mini skirt and a revealing top, two men walk by her and she gets their attention by revealing her leg. The two men are Red Rock and Boolie, Red Rock is wearing a black jacket with Metallica patches on it and some black jeans and Boolie is dressed in a blue AC/DC shirt. The aging hooker approaches the two men with a suggestive look and tone of voice.Hooker: hey fellas lookin’ for a good time, if y’ar I’m your lady!Red Rock: WOOO HOOO PUSSY! WOOPAH! Boolie calms down the testosterone pumped Red Rock by blocking his path with his forearm and shaking his head.Boolie: Red…you haven’t even looked at her face yet, you never look at their faces and you always complain…or scream the next morning! Now look at her face! Red Rock shrugs and slowly rolls his eyes up at the hooker and looks at her ugly face, it is wrinkled and pale and her eyes are bloodshot!Red Rock: URAGHHA! OH MY GOD! You look like a freaking clown how much make up do you need? Get away from me you disgust me! Hooker: kiss my ass buddy! Red Rock and Boolie cringe and walk away with look of disgust at the aging hooker.Boolie: I think we’ve walked in to the bad part of town Red!Red Rock: what makes you think that? Red Rock and Boolie look around and witness three men mug a bum a petrol bomb flies of their heads in to a near by window, people running out of bars on fire and a 16 year old girl with 2 kids.Red Rock: OOOOHHH! I see it now the explosions and stuff gives that impressionBoolie: yeah and all the porno clubs and other places of pornographic styles! Check out that place Boolie points over at the window of a shop across the road. In the window is “dwarf sex 24/7!!!”Red Rock: which way is it back to the hotel…I think these bums are beginning to realise with have money! Boolie: DON’T say that out loud they’ll come near us…. you know about my phobia of bums! Red Rock: yes I know if they touch you, you scream and beat them to death so I think we should get the hell out of here Red Rock and Boolie begins to slowly walk forward trying not to disturb the crime. They walk on for about 5 minutes when Boolie suddenly stops outside a small building with red and blue lights flashing.Red Rock: Boolie…c’mon lets scram before we get mugged, I don’t think they take kindly to us in this area. Red Rock looks at Boolie and Boolie has a big happy grin across his face.Boolie: Look Red this is soooooo cool! Red Rock: it doesn’t matter I don’t want to be here it’s scaring me…WHOA! Red Rock just sees the sign and it reads “FREE LAP DANCES on our opening night!!!”Red Rock: sweeeeeeet! That does say FREE lap dances right?Boolie: it sure does unless I forgot how to read…again…Red Rock: well what are we waiting for lets get our free lap dances and get the hell out of here! Red Rock and Boolie grin and walk through the doors as the scene fades out to black. Scene2: Strange strip bar> The scene reopens inside the strip bar. Red Rock and Boolie look around and see a lot of German WW2 memorial stuff. All the men are blonde and have blue eyes and their hair swept to the side. Most of them are getting lap dances yet they show no emotion. The woman also looked like they are having no fun.Boolie: I don’t think these people are having much funRed Rock: mmm…maybe the lap dances are crap and that’s why they’re free…Boolie: well you get what you pay for lets go find a seat! Red Rock and Boolie find an empty table near the back of the bar with all the blonde men watching them slowly sipping their drinks. Red Rock: you know there is something weirdo about these guys in here, they’re all blondeBoolie: I’m sure it’s nothing weird, and I’m also sure it has nothing to do with Dubya Dubya 2Red Rock: OH MY GOD! Red Rock jumps out of his seat and begins pointing with a shocked look on his face as his chair falls backwards on to the floor. On his way down he tables the table out with him making empty glasses fly everywhere!.Boolie: what? What happened? Why did you just go mental?Red Rock: they…have Hitler’s head in a frigging jar on the bookshelf! Boolie turns around and looks at the book shelf and his usual clueless expression quickly turns in to a screwed up look of shock before he begins jumping up and down screaming.Boolie: GAAAAAAAAHA! NAZI IN A JAR! Red Rock: mmm…all the blonde people…all German WW2 stuff, the lack of emotion from all the people…Hitler in a jar…OH MY GOD WE’RE IN A NAZI STRIP JOINT?Boolie: ah crap what a disappointment! Lets get out of here! Red Rock and Boolie try to walk out when a large German places his hand on Red Rock’s shoulder.Red Rock: oh dear…German Man: was it you who threw thiz at my head? Red Rock turns around to see a large German man with Glass in his head and bleeding heavily yet showing no emotion.Red Rock: DUDE you’re bleeding! Aren’t you in pain?German Man: yes I am in great pain can you not tell?Red Rock: err…. no. Well sorry about the glassing you an all but this bar suck and we’re off… Red Rock and Boolie try to leave but two more large German Men stop them in their tracks.German Man: you cannot leave yet my friendz you have not accepted your free lap dance from our fine females. Boolie: we’ll pass thank you, Red here has to wrestle tonight and we can’t be late.German Man: but you have no choice, you HAVE to have the lap dance! Red Rock and Boolie’s eyes widen with fear and the simultaneously gulp. The German man bangs his fist on the table and the glasses clink.German Man: SEND IN THE WOMEN SEND IN HELDA AND HELGA! The room begins to shake and Red Rock and Boolie begin to look very nervous. Beads of sweat run down their head and round the corner both dressed in tight red dresses walk two exceptionally large German chicks with very hairy legs too much make up and stringy greasy blonde hair. They both also resemble pigs with their pig noses and cheeks. Red Rock and Boolie’s jaws drop to the floor and a tear rolls from Red Rock’s eye.Red Rock: hey Boolie…that hooker outside just seemed a lot more inviting…Boolie: WHAT DO WE DO? THEY’RE GONNA MAKE US TAKE A LAP DANCE FROM THOSE…PIG WOMEN! Red Rock gulps.Red Rock: don’t worry I have a plan…Boolie: QUICK tell me now! Red Rock runs his fingers through his hair and takes in a deep breath.Red Rock: RUN AWAY! Red Rock and Boolie push away the three German men and make a break for the exit. Both men scared out of their wit trip over each other and crash to the floor.Red Rock: NNNNNNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! The three German men pick Red Rock and Boolie up to their feet. Boolie looks as if he is crying and Red Rock is screaming in fear.Red Rock: NO, NO! PLEASE NO, OH DEAR GOD NO! SAVE ME JESUS! MUHAMED, BUDDA, SOMEONE SAVE ME! NNNNNNOOOOOOOO The German man tapes Red Rock’s face up and he still makes small mumbling noises whilst Boolie is blubbering like a little kid being dragged to Summer school.German Man: enjoy your free lap dances men…BUWHAHAHAHAHA! Hey look at me I laughed that have not happened to me for years. Red Rock and Boolie are dragged in to the back kicking as the two ugly German chicks follow with grins on their faces as the scene fades to black. Scene3: The horror The scene reopens with Red Rock and Boolie walking out from the back. Both men are as pale as a milk bottle and they both have utterly blank faces and their jaws hang loose. It is clear they are in shock.German Man: you are free to go now my friends please come back againGerman Man2: I think they enjoyed them selves HansGerman Man: I think they did they look as joyful as we do after a lap dance. The camera shows shots of Red Rock and Boolie’s blanks faces of shock once again. Red Rock and Boolie slowly begins to stumble out on to the street. The German’s slam the door behind them.Red Rock: that…was disturbingBoolie: I will never ever be able to sleep ever again…Red Rock: I think this will require therapy Just as Red Rock and Boolie finish explaining their trauma a familiar face appears round the corner.?: AW MAN! Just as I was beingin’ to trust cracka’s I see you two walk out of a Nazi Strip joint! I’m disappointed!Red Rock: DUZZA! Err…it’s not what it looks like we were tricked!Duzza: mmm hmm yeah sure and I’m big foot. Well I don’t know why I’m in this promo I guess I’m here to make you boys feel awkward how’d I do?Red Rock: pretty well, I was scared I thought you were going to bust a cap in ma ass!Duzza: are you mocking me? What did I tell you about mocking me boy! Red Rock gulps and begins to run away.Duzza: GET BACK HERE CRACKER FOOL! The scene fades out to black with Boolie shrugging and following Red Rock and Duzza.
