A little lesson in BUMPING.

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/1/2002 **continued from last promo, Boda has just entered The Funkin Conservatory, home of Dory Funk, and the school where Boda learned to wrestle. Dory has a class of about 12 students in session at this time, and Boda suddenly interrupts them** ‘Whoo, all right. Dory, you’re the best, Old Man.’ shouts Boda. *The entire class of 12 stop what they are doing. Dory, whose in the ring with one of his students cracks a big smile without even looking at Chris.* ‘Don’t you have a World Title to be winning this weekend?’ responds Dory. *Chris walks towards the ring, as Dory climbs out, leaving his student in the ring, and the rest looking back at Boda. As the two walk towards one another, the audible murmuring of the class is heard* “wow, that’s Boda from PCW.” “Shit... you know who that is?” “What in the hell is he doing here?” *As Dory and Chris finally come toe to toe, Chris hugs his mentor, trainer and friend. They share a laugh, and a smile* ‘Of course I have a World Title to win. But, I couldn’t win it without seeking some guidance from my mentor.’ Boda tells Dory. ‘Guidance? From what I’ve seen, you my friend, are on a tear in PCW as of late. What possible guidance could you need?’ questions Dory. ‘Spiritual, mostly spiritual Dory. But, listen,’ Boda puts his arm around the shoulder of Dory, and walks back to the ring with him, ‘I don’t want to interrupt your class here.’ , Boda looks around at the students, 12 young men and women who are trying to break into the business, ‘So, what say I help you train them, in how to take a bump properly.’ ‘Are you sure? I mean, there are 12 of them here.’ retorts Dory. ‘I’d hate to see you injure yourself before Sunday.’ ‘Me, injure myself?’ Boda throws a jab at Dory, who moves out of the way, ‘You’re the old man here. And besides, I’m facing 19 men at March Madness, this could be my pre-game warm up, so to speak.’ ‘Okay, let’s do it.’ Dory gets in the ring, and is followed by Chris, ‘Okay, you are all in for a treat today,’ Dory addresses the class, ‘We are graced by none other than a former Conservatory graduate, and quite possibly the biggest name to walk out of here. Many of you know him, because I heard you murmur his name as I walked by, but incase some of you live under a rock..... This here is Boda from PCW, and the man that will be the next World Champion.’ *Chris walks to the ropes, and does his usual pre-match posing in the ring. The wetbacks all cheer the star. Boda steps back, near Dory, and addresses the students* ‘You know, as I look down at you all sitting there, all with dreams of becoming a professional wrestler,’ addresses Boda, ‘I have one thing to say..... GOOD LUCK. It’s a hard business, and believe me, it takes alot of time, and alot of skill to get to the top. I should know. I’ve gone from job squad president, to PCW main event, top face, mainstay. I’ve developed a fan base, something I even thought would never happen. Of course, all of it will be for naught if I fail to win the world title, again. I’ve got a string of bad luck when the title is on the line, and even though I am the biggest man, pound for pound in PCW, but, that and a quarter will get you a bag of chips to eat after jobbing match after match. So, I came here, today, to talk with Dory, talk with Marti, and hopefully gather that last little bit of self-confidence I need to do my best this Sunday. But, I wanted to address you guys, and girls,’ Boda looks at the two young women in the class, ‘and give you a bit of advice, and I’m going to run all of you through the course on taking bumps, PCW style. But, be fore warned, I’m not going to take it easy on any of you. I will make you all bump, and you will all take it like the men, and women that you are.’ *Chris steps out of the ring, and grabs a chair from ringside, and tosses it into the ring. Again, Boda steps back over the ropes, and into the ring. He points to one of the students* ‘Hey, come in here. Get in the ring with me.’ Boda tells the young students, who looks reluctant to get in, ‘Come on, I’m not going to hurt you. Dory... these kids soft nowadays?’ *Dory shrugs his shoulders, as the student climbs into the ring. He walks towards Boda, but stops about 5 feet from him.* ‘Kid, I’m not going to hurt you. Pick that chair up, and hit me in the back with it.’ Boda commands the young, nervous student, who shakes his head no. ‘What? Why won’t you hit me?’ ‘Because you are Boda, and you are the man that should be World Champion. I don’t want to make you mad, by hitting you with the chair.’ says the wetback. Boda laughs in response. ‘What’s your name kid?’, asks Boda. ‘Freddy, actually, Frederick O’Hollywood.’ replies the kid. ‘Let me guess, your Japanese.’ Boda sarcastically responds, ‘So, being the Irish powerhouse you seem to be, let me ask you why you got into wrestling?’ *Freddy spews off some answer about money, fame and power. This causes Boda to smirk.* ‘Wrong answer Freddy,’ replies the Big Bad Boda Daddy, ‘If your not doing this for yourself, because you like to, or because of the love of treating the fans to a show, then you are in the wrong business. Now, Freddy, let’s move onto the bump part of today’s lesson. You watch PCW right?’, in which Freddy nods yes to,’ Well, then you know that this Sunday, I face nineteen others for a shot at the World Title. 20 men, all beating each other up for one reason Freddy. The World Title. There is no other reason, other than that 20-pound gold belt. Now, in a match with 20 wrestlers, some serious bumps are bound to take place. So, Freddy, let’s role-play a bit here. I’ll be me.... of course, because no one can do me better.....and you can play the part of Legend Inc. Now, Freddy, as you may know, being you seem to follow PCW, Legend Inc, Tom Lexian, is a new member to PCW. Thus, he has a lot to prove. But, this is what will happen to Mr. Lexian when I get in the ring on Sunday.’ *Boda walks over to Freddy and clotheslines him to the ground, picks him up, and delivers The Boda Bomb to the wetback. Boda then tosses Freddy over the top rope and he lands with a thud at the feet of the rest of the students. Freddy rolls around, moaning, as Boda looks at the first of the two ladies in the class.* ‘So, you want to be a superstar wrestler. Well, there is a call for women wrestlers in not only PCW, but also other feds out there. Come on in, and let’s talk,’ Boda requests of the young lady, as he parts the ropes for her. The long legged blonde gets in the ring, and first thing she does is gives Boda a hug, ‘Well, thanks. I take it you’re a fan?’ Boda watches as the young girl shakes her head yes, ‘Ok, honey, tell me your name.’ ‘Jennifer’ shyly answers the young lady. ‘Don’t be shy. That’s a trait that will get you no where in the world of wrestling. Speaking of which,’ Boda smiles as the blonde tried to relax around him, ‘why do you want to be a wrestler?’ *Jennifer talks about her love of wrestling growing up, and that is was the only good memory she has of her parents, who died horribly in a car wreck two years ago. She feels it’s like a tribute to her parents, manly her father, if she becomes a successful female wrestler* ‘That’s a beautiful thing. So, to you, you have a goal, to make your parents proud.’ replies Boda, as he walks around Jennifer, never taking his eyes of her, or hers off his. ‘How many bumps have you taken in Dory’s class?’ Jennifer replies with alot. ‘So, you’re not afraid of having a man throw you around, and beat you down? Well, you remind me of another wrestler I will face this weekend. Kid Wonder. Maybe you’ve heard of him, sorry if you actually like him. Well, Jennifer, let’s pretend, for a moment, that you are Kid Wonder, and you are waiting in the ring for me to enter, I step over the top rope, and you run at me, trying to eliminate me right away’ *Boda steps out of the ring, and Jennifer waits in the ring, looking almost quizzical. Boda climbs on the apron, and steps over the top rope, like he does whenever he enters a match. Dory tell Jennifer to attack Chris, and try to eliminate him, by sending him over the top rope. Jennifer runs in at Boda, who ducks a clothesline from her, and he spins around, and clotheslines her over the top rope, sending her crashing/bumping, to the floor below. Boda leans on the top rope, and looks down at Jennifer.* ‘Nothing personal Jennifer,’ Boda tells the girl he just clotheslined over the top rope, ‘But you make a perfect Kid Wonder... you sucked almost as bad as he does. Good job, you really went for the reality there.’ *Boda continues his little lesson with the next nine students. One by one, he gets them in the ring, and gives them advice on making it in pro-wrestling, then metaphorically assigning them a name of one of the 19 in the main event at March Madness, and proceeding to show them how to take a bump, like the previous two. Dory watches his students learn a lesson, the hard way. Finally, Boda wipes away some sweat from his brow, and looks out at the remaining student still sitting. Boda has chosen to pick this kid last, for one particular reason* ‘So, you’re a Miyagawa fan? You think Miya is going to outlast me, and eliminate me on Sunday to win the title?’ Boda barks out to the young kid left sitting. He’s wearing a Miya t-shirt, and nods yes to the questions by Chris. ‘Well, junior, let me tell you something, and you can go home, after Dory schools you some more today, and email your ‘hero’ and let him know I said he’s the one that sucks, not me, like he so frequently likes to say about me. And, that I took it easy on one of his fans, because, I’ll just show his fans how well their goggle wearing freak flies as I send him not only over the top rope, but I‘m going to toss him into the 5th row of seats around ringside. Now, I’d like to see Miya take that kind of bump. But tell you what.... if you want to come in here, and take a bump, in the name of your idol, I’ll be glad to assist you. Just as well, too, because I’ll not only be eliminating him from winning the title this Sunday, but on Turmoil, I’ll break down any hopes he has of still being the champion.’ *The last student, the Miyagawa fan, decides to not take a bump. And Chris just laughs it off. The kid is apparently afraid Chris might actually give him a harder bump to take than the rest of the class. As the rest of the students take a seat around ringside. Dory slides in the ring, and talks to them all, telling them that the class is over for today, and that tomorrow, they will be learning submission moves. The students all get up, and thank Boda for the bump lessons, and leave the training room of the Conservatory. Dory pulls in a chair, and sits as does Chris. The scene fades out as the two start to talk about life, wrestling, and the pursuit of GOLD.* **to be continued**
