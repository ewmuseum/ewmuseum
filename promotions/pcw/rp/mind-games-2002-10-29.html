Mind Games

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-10-02 Bob Vurdo is scheduled to meet Tony down at the docks. The meeting is to decide what to do about the black men that are moving into Amber Hill. There are more men coming every day and every one of them joins the gang that seems to be building quite quickly. This gang is suspected to be led by none other than Ron Jackson. Ron Jackson is a smart man who can lead the black men in Amber Hill to ownership of the city. Bob Vurdo now has a problem which isn't what he needed he has enough problems at the moment. The only reason Bob has decided to make the trip out of the house for the first time is simply for revenge. Ron Jackson has hurt him in a way he never thought he could be hurt. He has never felt this way about anyone and to have her taken away has put him in more emotional pain than it is possible to imagine. ------------------------***---------------------------- Ron Jackson sits on the front steps sharing a joint with his other black friend. They are the leaders of the new black gang in Amber Hill. 'I got that whore back there if ya want a turn?' Ron is an evil man with no care for anyone other than himself. It's inhuman to treat any person like an object but what he is doing is as low as it goes. 'Why do you have it in so bad for this girl anyway?' 'She's Bob 'I don't run things no more' Vurdo's girlfriend. She is our ticket to money here.' 'How are you going to get rid of all of them through just one girl?' 'That's the catch.... He tries to move the hole family but because they got their own families here and have been here for a long time they won't want to move. So Bob is so crazy over this girl he becomes a ruthless killing machine and kills them out himself. It's only a matter of time before Bob Vurdo is killed and the family collapses. By then there will be no one experienced enough to take over.' 'Your one smart fucker.' The other black man is quite impressed by Ron's plan. If this pulls off for Ron he will be the biggest member in that gang and will finally be a respected rich man. 'You don't have to tell me that.' Ron smiles before taking another drag of the joint and handing it to his friend. His friend takes a puff before standing up. 'Now can I have a look at this girl that Vurdo fella is so obsessed over?' 'Sure follow me.' Ron stands up and leads his friend through the small house to a dark bedroom. The room is quite bare with only a bed and it is also quite cold without carpet. Ron walks over to her and turns on the light beside her bed. Rosanna is tied to the bed by her hands to the top end and her legs to the bottom. She is covered with only a sheet and is shivering from the cold room. Her face has two bruises on both sides of her face and she also appears to be missing some teeth from her once perfect mouth. She has spent a lot of time crying but her eyes can't cry anymore. Never had she imagined such a horrific experience and now she has to live it. She would rather be dead then go through what she has had to in the last few days. She has been beaten, raped and generally treated in a barbaric manner. Ron spits on her face hitting her in the eye as she shakes her head to get the flem off from her eye. Ron's friend is quite disgusted by the way he is treating her. 'What the fuck are you doing?' The black man can't believe what he is seeing. 'She's fucking trash now and besides the worse we treat her the more angry Bob will get.' 'No one deserves treatment like that though man.' 'Fuck off. You want to fuck her or not?' Ron acts like he is the smartest man alive and the other man doesn't like this he is mortified from what he has just seen. He can't even begin to imagine what this innocent girl has had to go through. 'This is fucking wrong.' Ron turns to him and looks at him for a second. He is angry by the fact the man is questioning his leadership. Ron pulls out a gun from his pocket and without even blinking shoots the other man four times before turning back to Rosanna. Rosanna can't believe what she has just seen. Ron has a sadistic emotionless look on his face making him look like the devil. Ron pulls down his pants reveling his boxer shorts before heading over to the bedside table and turning out the light. --------------------------***---------------------------- Bob has his white shirt and long pants with braces on. The braces have gun holders attached each holder carries a gun for Bobs protection. Bob sits across from Tony looking quite depressed. The rage can be seen through his eyes as they look at the ground of the enormous docking bay. The large wooden shack is relatively empty apart from the table, the chairs and the television. Tony knows what Bob is going through and knows how much pain he must be in so he decides to make this as short for him as possible. 'Those black nigger fucks are moving in like a plague.' Bob doesn't even acknowledge Tony's speaking although he is listening to him he doesn't have the energy to reply. Tony knows this so he continues with what he has to say. 'They have already taken away some of our business through drugs. We need to kill them off as quickly as we can or they could take over.... It's a race against time now.' Bob nods in agreement with what Tony is saying. 'I know how much you want your revenge Bob but we can get Ron when we get the rest of them. Believe me I want to kill that black fucker myself.' Bob turns his head and looks at Tony as Rons name is mentioned. You can see the rage in his eyes as he stands up from his seat without saying a word. Bob pulls a gun out from his holder and aims it at Tony's head. 'Where you in on it!!!' Bob is obviously full of rage he is standing over Tony breathing quickly and staring down at him. Tony has a confused expression on his face as he looks back up at Bob. He can't believe what he is hearing. 'Where you?!' Bob moves around the table and closes in on Tony still holding his gun aimed to his head. 'No.... I wouldn't.... I couldn't.' Tony is quite scared of Bob at the moment who knows what he could do with that gun in his hands in the state he is in right now. 'It makes perfect sense now you cold hearted mutha fucker.' Bob turns the gun sideways and braces himself to pull the trigger. 'I wouldn't do that to you Bob.... I wouldn't do that after all you have done for me.' Tony is sliding down his seat trying to move away from Bob who has let emotions take him over. Bob drops the gun from his hands before turning his back and walking off. 'If you are I will find out.' Bob walks to his car and starts it up before driving off. Tony is quite hurt by the fact he doubted him about something so big. Then again Bob has let the emotions take him over and now he is just a human time bomb waiting to explode on the right or wrong person.
