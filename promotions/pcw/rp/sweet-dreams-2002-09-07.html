Sweet Dreams

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07 - 09 - 02 'This scene is giving me the Children Of The Corn vibes!' gloated the jovial voice of Gaz 'The Jester' Green. Standing before the camera was the slender physique of the Asylums eye candy known as Anarchy. Her blue hair fluttered in the gentle breeze as she stood in a catalogue pose that allowed the home audience to bask in her choice of wardrobe currently a chain mail vest and loose fitting trousers. Standing along side her was one half of the dynamic duo, Pranks With Attitude's very own conniving comic Gaz Green who fashionably adorns a three-piece business suit that colour co-ordinates with his velvet dyed hair. As always his hair is firmly held hair in a spike whilst his eyes remain sheltered beneath sunglasses and both parties feel quite contempt to watch a series of children walking in two flowing columns. 'Breath taking really' sighed Anarchy. The camera panning out allows us to see both Asylum members looking over their flock, high in the mountains of a place known as 'Gazville,' which happens to be sign posted with a plaque. The children all dressed as various PCW personalities walk along a cobbled pathway, passing buildings in the process they appear drone like, their actions rather monotone. 'The camp could do with a lick of paint - But now my pockets have been bled dry by the Front Office we'll have to make do with the current facilities!' roared the Lord Of Laughter towards his comrade. 'Gaz, how do you plan to take me shopping then?' retorted the dizzy diva. 'Huh!?! Look at the progress we've made....' interrupted the emerald menace with an out-stretched arm. Each of the children have had a PCW character bestowed upon them as they continue their journey, watched over by the companies recent acquisitions of Gaz Green and Anarchy. The two youngsters leading the pack have the uncanny resemblance to PCW's first couple of Xavier Kannon and Ellie, whilst the others masquerade as other members of the active roster. 'So what’s with all the Area 51 secrecy?' questioned Anarchy. The children continue to waltz on without supervision, heading towards to a wooden hut set aside from the rest of the complex, hidden from prying eyes. 'Well Anarchy you have heard about these Cults where they brain wash simple minded folk who who haven't the will to oppress their captors absurd ideas. Children appear to be the heart of these issues, fuelling the media with gossip of pedophilia and kidnapping so as a precaution we at Gazville decided to keep this on a need to know basis.... Trust the sexually deprived faiths of the Catholics to draw attention!' proclaimed the former NTICW superstar with a subtle smile. Anarchy nods her head in approval of the statement issued by her work colleague. The devious duo step hotfooted into the village, stopping momentarily to watch a class in progress. An adult voice roars over the silence, responded only by the children still in full portrayal of their PCW counter-parts, sat at simple desks looking on with keen interest. 'What religion do you follow?' 'I'M A PRANKOLOGIST' 'What if I said you can't be?' 'I WOULDN'T LISTEN TO YOUR HALF HEARTED PLEADS' 'Who do you idolise?' 'GAZ 'THE JESTER' GREEN' 'Interesting.... Prankology can only grow, you are the future of this fine religion!' bellowed the teacher as he stands erect at the front of his class. Anarchy looks at Gaz with amazement as he responds with a cheeky smile. Looking rather egotistical Gaz basks in his accomplishment as the founder of Prankology. 'Your not making Genetically....' said the female competitor before an untimely interruption from her co-host. 'Genetically Engineered Super Children, heaven forbid~!' injected the Jester. 'No, actually I was going to say Genetically Modified foods, I'm kinder famished' The Asylum members cut this short de-tour of conversation short and looked in at the class once more. 'What do you watch?' 'I WATCH ANYTHING COMEDY RELATED' 'What if I told you that Norman Wisdom was a failure and Ali G is a fraud?' 'I WOULD SAY - WHAT DO YOU KNOW, HUH!?!' 'What if I said Gazville is a figment of your imaginations?' 'GAZVILLE IS FAR MORE BELIEVEABLE THAN SAY.... HUBBARDVILLE!' 'Do your parents force Prankology onto you?' 'NO, IT'S FREEDOM OF CHOICE. WHY SIT READING A MUSTY OLD BOOK WHEN YOU COULD BE MAKING OTHER PEOPLES LIVES MISERY!!!' 'This lesson has surpassed even my high standards and I can only surmise that graduation is right around the corner - good, good' said the teacher looking pleased with himself as his facial expression turns to a blatant look of being smug. Anarchy stands motionless, taken back by the whole episode. 'This is.... WOW, we could sell them over ebay!' joked Anarchy with a smile, showcasing her pearl white teeth. She bounces around in glee until her face centres on the stern look of Gaz 'The Jester' Green as Anarchy tried to compose herself. 'EBAY?' groaned the Prince Of Pranks. 'Sorry...' replied Anarchy flickering her eyes in a flirtatious manner. Suddenly the image draws black and re-opens in the bedroom of the jovial Jester as he awakes from his deep slumber under the abuse of his partner Nerezza who stands over his co-worker / friend in an intimidating stance. The entire event had been a dream, but Gaz seemed pleased to have slept about his own utopia even though the truth of the matter was that Gazville would be forever locked in the imagination of the PCW comic. 'Time to get this show on the road' hissed the Angel Of Fear. Nerezza standing tall with his claret red trench coat draping his broad shoulders, pale complexion and sunglasses adorning his facial features he awaits his comrade’s reaction. 'What a dream!' 'I now know what it feels like to be a World Champion....' laughed the comedian, contemplating over his dream. 'Forget that, it's time to get dressed junior!' groaned the behemoth as he dragged his P.W.A partner from the comfort of his crypt, the scene finally drawing to a closure.
