Through The Eyes [6] The Son

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22.12.02 Previewing the Pay Per View, once a month this type of a spectacle comes upon us with the glitz, the glamour and the spotlight of a new star ready to be born. Over the years, Primetime Championship Wrestling has brought to you the gore, the spacious arenas packed and filled to capacity, not to mention the champions crowned. For the end of the second fiscal year, and third overall year in the history of PCW, Decimation is always shown throughout the public, this year from Skydome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. For years, my dad would reflect on how things would be if you ever had the chance to become a champion, but he never told me about whether or not wrestling was in my blood. I had to search for it, until on a balmy September night, I found my answer. He was the Northest Entertainment Wrestling's Heavyweight champion, and for a year - he held the title until he met my mom and then the two of them decided to have me. After that, he covered for wrestling, even stuck with it for a few house shows and then ended up having my sister April without telling me or my mother about it. It was though trying to figure out how in the world could a father do such a thing to his son, but I guess you just figure it out quick and then don't say another word about it later. I could see my mother on Thanksgiving, straighing out herself by lifting up the form of turkey and then allowing all of us to feast. April was on the opposite end of the table, with Eric - my father on the right and Pamela - my mother on the left of him. Tracy was on my left side and April's new found boyfriend Clint happened to stick his nose on side of her by dragging a chair up to the table. Replicas of titles that Dad had won, from the United States, the Cruiserweight when he was wrestling for LoW, Legends of Wrestling in Uthica, and that shiny World title, cased and framed up, next to a picture from the event some twenty odd years ago. I envy that championship, and for everything it was worth, I wished to hold onto one of my own. The Tag Titles that I was set to win a month ago later didn't satisfy that need of respect, prestige. Its hard trying to play keep up with your father, the wrestling legend throughout New York, Buffalo, places like that. Leaning back in the chair, I dug into the food, quietly munching down on every single bite until it was all gone, not a single piece to be left over for a pet or another to stab their knife in. I'd just gotten into PCW barely fresh, in August. Teamed with many friends and enemies, before long knowing that I was to become something. Then, the incident occurred. During October and mid-way through November, I never knew what was to be in store for me if the title or the opportunity to win one was to arise, but I saw what in a tournament. The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, put on for the first time by the Primetime Network, a supergroup of intergrated federations and I came in - only on thoughts of how I won, how I had beaten the current PCW World Heavyweight champion in Wrestling God 3, how I placed fourth in the un heard of Last Man Standing contest put on by the Fans Wrestling Federation a month later. What was I to think, that my father would crush those hopes and dreams by telling me I had a sister, and that he'd slept around on my mother. Why did he do it? I'm wondering that as I reflect right now. 'Dad,' I asked him at the table, cleaning my plate. 'why'd you hold your tongue to not tell me about April?' He quickly rose from the table, making his way towards the kitchen sink, trying to cower away from me. He held his hands over the sink, the warm water splashing over his palms like a rushing Niargra Falls. 'Son,' he finally began to speak up. 'It was something I just did, couldn't let you go off without knowing.' My ears felt like they were burning, my throat dryer than a desert and the heat of Phoenix during summer. Did I want to let my anger just go ahead and let it all run? Nope, I needed more answers for that. 'Shush,' I whispered to him. 'You cost me a chance to become a star, for what? Just for your little name call to make it out to the public? What kind of shit is that Dad?' 'Something for you to understand. I didn't want to - ' 'Hurt me? You've hurt me more than everybody else. Gaz Green could throw me through a pit of lions, a goddam bowl of acid, but I'd survive. You crushed whatever chance I had for success Eric, you fucked it all up.' 'You're here to be pissed over something that happened to you? You can't remember what happened to me can you? They took my career, MY LIFE away from me!' 'No, you took it away. Just to hold up the lie.' 'You selfish, heartless prick.' he retorted to me, each word stabbing me with accuracy, 'You blame me for things, never blame yourself, do you? Maybe it was because of me that you lost, so what? It was your emotions that lead you to lose in the first place.' I sighed, 'It wasn't my fault. It wasn't my fault - it never is! I'm perfect dammit, not a single thing can touch me!' Storming out of the room, I took Tracy by the arm, quietly dragging her out of the house as we hopped into the car, driving away from the family and away from my father, running away from the truth. Running away.
