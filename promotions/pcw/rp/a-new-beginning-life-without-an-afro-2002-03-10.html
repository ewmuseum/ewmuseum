A new beginning: Life without an afro

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/02 Ode to an Afro You were there when I startedBack when I was mentally retarded. Like a vibrant, bushy shrubYou’d sway in the windAnd growl like a cubFearless like tonic and gin. You were there to help me scoreWith all of those whoresConnecting with every woman in siteWho would gleefully stare At my glorious hairBefore running away in fright. But now that you’re goneEl Diablo must dieBecause messing with me is utterly wrong.There is no escapingNo place to hideHe will be scared shitlessAll through the night. However there is something you really must knowFor I have been writing this odeWearing nothing but silky panty ho….err… (forget that line). [Scene opens backstage at Friday Fusion shortly after Flaymin’s afro has been shaved off by El Diablo. In stunned silence, Flaymin stares at the mirror- specifically at the few centimetres above his forehead where his afro once stood. He rubs his head in disbelief while he shakes his head in utter shock simultaneously. Jenna walks into the room and sits on the couch.She flips open a magazine on the coffee table unaware that Flaymin’s got a shinier head then a bowling bowl. Flaymin looks at Jenna before turning back to the mirror once again to stare at his head] Flaymin: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRGGGGGHHHHHHH!!!! Jenna: What’s your problem? Flaymin: MY HEAD!! [Jenna looks at Flaymin’s head and smiles] Jenna: I see you’ve been using that moisturiser again. Flaymin: Forget the f’n moisturiser- look at my hair! [Jenna looks at Flaymin’s bald scalp and begins to shudder] Jenna: Oh…. My…..G-O-D!What happened? Flaymin: El Diablo, that f*cking Mexican moron let loose with a razor. [Suddenly there is a knock on the door. In to the room walks a cameraman and ‘People Like Us’ Reporter Robert Burton. Robert looks at Flaymin’s bald head and raises an eyebrow] Robert: What happened here?It looks like you lost a fight with a lawn mower. Flaymin: Shut up and get out, Jenna and I are conducting crisis talks. Robert: But we still have to shoot the final segment for The People Like Us documentary profiling the professional wrestler. This won’t take very long and before you know it Jenna will be free to rub your crystal ball of a head for good luck.] Flaymin: Are you being humorous? Do I look like I’m having fun here?Does my face say ‘Come on down and crack some jokes?’Am I some sort of Carnival Freak Show with a flashing neon sign above my head? Are you being humorous? Robert: You said that one already. Flaymin: Yeah I know, I just couldn’t think up of any other rhetorical questions. Robert: Nevertheless we are still going to have to shoot the final segment of the documentary right now. Flaymin: Can’t we do it tomorrow, because I’m REALLY not in the mood. Robert: Sorry buddy.This fourth instalment of the documentary profiles the backstage area after a match or ‘incident’, not when you’re having a rendezvous in a cheap brothel with a hooker named Bambi. Jenna: I remember having sex with Bambi. [Robert rolls his eyes] Robert: We’re not talking about a real deer. Jenna: Neither am I, it was a dog with fake antlers glued above either ear! Flaymin: Ewww Robert: Moving right along…. [Robert reaches into his pocket and pulls out a small notepad.He flicks through a couple of pages until he comes to a page with highlighted questions on it.] Robert: Right, first off- what do you tend to do after a match? [The question falls on deaf ears as Flaymin is busy wiping the tears from his eyes as he stares at his bald head in the mirror] Robert: Flaymin? Flaymin: I’m sorry what did you say? Robert: You seem very sensitive about your sudden hair loss.Do you get emotional after every trip to the hair salon? Flaymin: WHAT HAIR SALON?????I HAD MY HEAD SHAVED WHILE I WAS PASSED OUT!! [Robert wipes the flying spit from his face while Jenna puts her hands on Flaymin’s shoulders to keep him calm.] Flaymin: Sorry about the sudden sea spray. Robert: All is forgiven.But you really need to start answering a few of my questions instead of beating around the bush. Tell me now, do you speak to other wrestlers after you’ve had a match? Or do you just exit the arena as soon as your wrestling commitments have been fulfilled. Flaymin: No I tend to wait until the main event is well underway. Robert: Why is that? Flaymin: Because those idiots leave their lockers wide open…. If you catch my drift. Robert: You steal the personal belongings of other wrestlers?How barbaric!! Flaymin: It’s only natural.If someone leaves a bag open full of Armani suits and chocolate candy bars, then that it is more then a welcoming invitation to take whatever you want. [Robert shakes his head in disgust] Robert: And you wonder why someone shaved off your afro… Flaymin: Is this documentary over?I need to book an appointment with a hair transplant guru. Robert: Don King? Flaymin: No, Vin Diesel. Robert: Huh? Flaymin: He’ll put me on the ‘Fast and Furious’ hair re-growth program. Robert: Riiiiiight…. I think I can wrap up the documentary on my own from here.Thanks for participating in the People Like Us documentary and be sure to see it on TV when it airs. Flaymin: *sarcastically* Yeah… I can’t wait…. [Robert and the cameraman exit the room. Jenna stands in the corner of the room scratching her stomach and back] Flaymin: What are you doing? Jenna: This dress is making me itchy.I’m going to put a different one on. [Jenna begins to strip.She takes off her dress and throws it on the floor. Totally naked, she walks over to her bag and pulls out another dress. Flaymin grins as he watches Jenna stand there totally naked.Slowly his eyes begin to wander down her body…. Flaymin: Say, when was the last time you shaved your err…. ‘bush’. Jenna: It’s been a while.Why? Flaymin: That looks like one fine bush.Matter o’ fact, there is enough hair in that bush to form a mini-afro…. Jenna: What are getting at? Flaymin: Oh nothing. [Flaymin picks the razor up off the floor and turns it on] Flaymin: By the way, you wouldn’t happen to have any glue in that bag would you? Extra strong glue…. [Jenna looks down at her bush and then up at Flaymin’s bald head.She then raises an eyebrow when she see’s Flaymin’s razor] Jenna: You gotta be kidding… Flaymin: It’ll only take a minute, I promise!And think of the extra breathing room down there!! Jenna: There isn’t enough hair down there to form an afro!! Flaymin: Don’t worry, I’ll shape it into a Mr T Mo-hawk. [Jenna closes her eyes as Flaymin crouches down and begins to shave off Jenna’s bush in a vague attempt to re-create some hair for his head….] [Scene fades to black]
