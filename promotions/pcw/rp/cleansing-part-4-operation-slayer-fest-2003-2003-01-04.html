Cleansing (part 4) - Operation Slayer Fest 2003

4 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04 January 2003 Prologue: During the 'Cleansing (part 1)' we witnessed a darker side of comedy as the Front Office Jester of Gaz Green entered what best could be described as a hornets nest of vampires, its location a grim backdrop of a nightclub known as the '6th Gate'. Vanquishing one of these un-dead beings Gaz and his accomplice Natalia Miyagawa acquainted themselves with a fellowship of vampire slayers called 'Blood Bath' who have since been offered the bounty of former Pranks With Attitude member Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. With a luxurious contract laid on the table our next chapter in this ongoing saga sees the possible conclusion as 'Operation Slayer Fest 2003' turns from theory to practise, headed up by the ruthless band of the Blood Bath collective. An overcast sky looms of the pitiful backdrop of a run down area. Buildings stand crippled and ridden in decay, absolute squalor for someone living the celebrity life yet Nerezza utilises this grim backdrop to shelter from the public eye. Almost a re-enactment of Bosnia you would assume the sky would light up in fireworks or the appropriate gun fire however, through the dank, damp dwellings that litter this side of town we capture a mysterious black transit van parked up. Oozing with curiosity the scene suddenly changed to a more appropriate view of the interior where those plotting the downfall of Nerezza sit in silence. Each member of Blood Bath make their final preparations, New Blood adjusts his hands-free microphone whilst Bane adds the final touches of camouflage paint, creating a Brave Heart-esque facial mask. 'Remember silent but violent, no witnesses-....' murmured the distinctive voice of the Corporate Jester. Long gone is the velvet business suit we have came to hate, now adorning black overalls with carefully positioned padding and heavy builders boots, the Front Office Entertainment Manager could easily be an extra in an espionage flick It seems apparent that Gaz Green will over-see the charade up close and personal, possibly adding insult to injury as the very demise of Nerezza may be at the hand of the only human he had ever deemed a friend. 'Time to lock and load guys!' snarled the figurehead of Blood Bath, the man known only as Gunn. From a fabric sports bag the five-man party dived in like a pack of hunger driven wolves, all of which retrieving a variety of weapons. Bane seemed content with silver coated knuckle-dusters that had a formidable line of spikes whilst New Blood gleefully inspected a standard harpoon gun, however it's projectiles were the key to his glee as he held aloft silver-tipped stakes that had been grafted for aerial use. The rest of this ruthless band of mercenaries each accepted their own array of weaponry and after a few child-like moments the van returned to its desolate yet eerie silence. Gaz peered down at his watch as the second hand pivoted around the clock face, the moment of truth looming. 'You know what to do so I suggest being a little professional and-... Happy slaying gents!' joked the Jester as he wrenched back on the barrel of his shot gun, a click indicating the weapon was armed and ready the vehicles doors slid aside. 'DOUBLE TIME~!' roared Gunn as his personal flock flooded out onto the open street like a SWAT team. Each man took up their own attacking position, hiding and moving seemed to be the way forward as the five-man party ushered closer to a desolate building that looked to be under demolition. Coated in vandalism, coloured paint and an apparent disregard for a persons home the building was quite the sight, an entrance lay open as the door swayed ajar on one hinge. Firstly Judgement sailed through the opening, his stake held aloft rather triumphantly while New Blood followed suit, it wasn't long before the welcoming corridor of this dwelling had been laid under siege by this fellowship of vampire slayers. 'Something doesn't feel right?!?' murmured C.B. (Carl Black) raising his thermo-goggles and inspecting the premises, his teeth chattering heavily as his nerves seeped through his tough man portrayal. 'Keep it to yourself or we'll all be spooked-....' retorted New Blood as peered over his comrades shoulder, meanwhile though Bane continued to be the real factor as he stood a pillar of strength, unfazed by the murmur of second thoughts. Waltzing past, Gunn headed up the group, his hand tightly grasping the knee length sword that he has running alongside his leg. A few hand signal gestures later and the infantry slowly moved closer, trying desperately not to disturb the debris of the building or to make any unnecessary sounds that would alert their target as the vigilante group drew up to an unmarked door. 'You do know that's the stairs right?' questioned the Emerald Menace. A few grunts and nods later and the door was slowly drawn open, an unnecessary creak seeped through but nothing substantial. The door rolled to one side, low and behold a flight of wooden stairs that would surely creak under their heavy weight and quite possibly jeopardise the mission, however this flimsy obstacle was the only route accessible and after a few tense moments of niggling Gunn gave the all clear. 'Tread lightly-....' whispered Gunn at the foot of the stairs, ushering for New Blood to go first and soon a queue mounted as the tension-followed suit. - CREAK - Nothing bellowing but an early warning, yet its low volume offered the up tight party a chance to breathe a sigh of needed relief. Traipsing up the rest of the rickety stairs, the numbers soon mounted atop on a small walkway that leads to the second and final floor, however the mass of muscle Bane stands at the bottom knowing the stairs would struggle under the lighter men, him, they'd perish. 'BANE - BANE - BANE - COME ON!!!' silently called Gunn as he glared down at his associate who's reluctance was wrote across his face, smeared in his camouflage mask so to speak. Shutting his eyes the giant stepped onto the first platform, literally on tip-toes he further climbed under the watchful eye of his Blood Bath brothers who hail encouraging comments quietly when suddenly-.... - CRASH - A thunderous howl of pain and the behemoths leg vanishes into the rotted wood of the stairs as he becomes incapacitated. Cringing and holding back further cries of both anger and frustration, the sweat seeps from his forehead; obviously any sudden movements could cause the walkway to cave in costing both the teams exit and more importantly a colleague. 'AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH~!' Unable to control his rage Bane screamed and with some gesturing hand movements he indicates for the team to strive on without him, waving frantically the course of action saw Gunn lead an explosive attack, obviously any cover had been blown thus a hit and hope contingency. 'Right - Come on, forget the element of surprise!' hissed Gunn rather energetically as he lead his band of merry men into war whilst Gaz Green rubbed his forehead in disbelief, his master plan floored. Languishing behind with a dumbfounded expression the Jester watched in horror as New Blood threw his body weight through a door, the occupancy of Nerezza, one thud after another, when finally the door caved in and the assault began. A shower of smoke and the rustling of debris that litters the floor, the team storm the premises but to no avail as the room lay bare. 'WHAT THE FUC-....' solemnly cried Judgement throwing down his weapon. Inspected further by the Jester, indeed the vampire continued to prank the prankster as he evades another foiled master plan by his former tag team partner. Suddenly though a trap springs and dangling on a rope, foot securely fastened in a lasso New Blood whales as he fights the impossible, gravity. Trying to retain his amusement even after such a set back, more was to follow as the television set flickered into life, a snowy picture of black and white lines crackled. 'I'd say he's got your number on this occasion, huh!?!' injected Gunn raising his eyebrows sarcastically whilst he smirked with his bristle-ridden face. As the television continued to spook those present the suspense had long since past when without warning the arrogant mug of Nerezza filtered onto the screen, chuckling he openly emits his hysterics towards the thwarted efforts of Gaz 'The Corporate Jester' Green. 'BOO~!' jovially screamed the image of the PCW behemoth, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. 'GOOD EVENING WESTERN MOOK! Anyway-.... Gaz 'The Traitor' Green, your so predictable, I mean I dangled myself as bait and you continually fail to get the job done. Now that the jokes are all put aside, it will be YOU facing a darker side of comedy, I plan to have the last laugh in this game of cat and mouse-....' continued the amused Nerezza as the image twitched from the poor recording. 'Now, the hunter... becomes the HUNTED!!!' concluded the Living Nightmare before the picture distorted and fizzled out, returning to its original glare of snowy emptiness. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!' retorted the Prince Of Pranks Gaz Green like a spoilt child throwing a temper tantrum, kicking at the bare floor and grinding his teeth in pure disgust the Blood Bath watch-on collecting their thoughts. As the child-like antics of Gaz Green seep through and the surrounding fellowship of vampire slayers reflect the image joins that of Nerezza's and draws to a timely closure, firstly an eerie silence, then the image fades to black, closing another doomed venture of the Front Office Jester. - The End? -
