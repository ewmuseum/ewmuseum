Totally Tubular Mommy - Part 4 - The Journal

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 28 [The scene opens on a leather note book with the initials 'TTT' across the center in a plate of gold. The black leather is slightly cracked near the corners, showing that it has been through many years of abuse. The book slowly opens to show some of the milk colored pages have been torn out of the book and that different colored pages have been inserted, maybe the 'TTT' person was writing something on the road. So many words are written through out this book, signifying that maybe this is some sort of journal or memoirs] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy, one half of the Cowabunga Connection, sits at his beautifully hand crafted oak desk. The perfectly crafted desk was finished in a dark varnish that shines under the desk lamp that Tommy has illuminated. The former professional surfer looks rather depressed and tired as the shade of the desk lamp hits his face. His bright blue eyes don't have the twinkle in them that they usually do. There is something on his mind, something that has plagued him for so many years. That something is his mother] [He flips through the pages in his journal and stops at a very early entry...] August 21, 1986 I had a gnarly good time today. Me and Harry ran around on the beach for hours pretending that we were He-Man. He didn't want to play Skeletor but I made him. I went back home and I saw Mommy with this strange man. He was a big man with giant super duper muscles that looked like He-Man's. He-Man wore clothes though, which is totally rad, but this guy was nakie. Mommy was nakie too but I couldn't tell too much because this other guy was covering her. I shouldn't have watched but I thought he was hurting her. She was screaming and yelling for a guy named Shaun. I grabbed my He-Man sword and wacked the guy in the head. It was TOTALLY RAD because he fell on the floor crying. I never made another person cry before, I did feel bad, but he was hurting my Mommy! He rolled over and I saw something that I didn't want to see...his pee pee. What a small pee pee it was too! I remember looking at mine earlier in the morning and my pee pee was MUCH bigger than his. Mommy was really upset about what I did. She yelled at me and told me to go drown in the ocean. So, I went out to the ocean and let the current push me in to drown. Harry ran down after me and pulled me out of the water before I could drown. He then told me all about how we use our pee pee's as swords and we have to slay women with our swords, pee pee's. That's how we make babies and show girls that we are the dominant sex. I got lost at that point and realized that the guy was just slaying my mother with his pee pee and trying to make babies. That man was trying to make me a BABY BROTHER!! I always wanted a baby brother since I can remember. I felt bad and went to talk to my mom and tell her I was REALLY sorry. When I went back she was getting slayed by a pee pee again, this time it was a DIFFERENT guy! I asked why there was another guy slaying her and for some reason the man got mad and left. He had a much large pee pee than that other guy I knocked out. Mom got really mad this time and dragged me to my room. I am so sad right now. I could have a baby brother, but I messed it up. I always mess things up. [Tommy sighs loudly to himself, going back to that hot summer night. He remembers those guys like he remembers yesterday. All they were looking for was fun and he screwed it up for them. It was hard coping with the fact that your mother is a raging slut] [Footsteps can be heard behind Tommy. He looks back to see his tag team partner, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, coming up behind him. Harry is dressed in a Bon Jovi T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. He has a very concerned look on his face as he approaches his partner and best friend] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: The toilet is clogged, man. [Tommy looks at Harry with a scowl on his face. He thought Harry was going to offer words of wisdom like he used to when they were kids, but instead he talks to Tommy about the toilet? And how in the name of bogusness did it get clogged?!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, how did you clog the toilet? [Harry shrugs] Harry: I just poop, man. I'm no plumber. [Tommy shakes his head in frustration. Harry used to he so smart and such an easy person to get along with. Now, after years upon years of marijuana smoking, Harry has become a giant burnout who has a fried brain. You almost feel sorry for him because he's like one of those mentally handicapped children you see on commercials for charity foundations] TTT: I'll call the plumber later, compadre. [Harry nods with a smile] Harry: Far out, man. [Tommy then begins flipping through the pages on the journal again. He comes to a section in the center of the book that has many torn up yellow pages. They seem to be phone numbers and Tommy smiles at the sight of them] TTT: Those were the days... [Harry peaks over Tommy's shoulder and sees the numbers] Harry: You have Cecilia Harris' number, man!! TTT: She was a bodacious babe, dude. [Harry looks out of the window that is beside Tommy's desk. He then looks back to Tommy] Harry: LOOK AT THAT EAGLE!!! [Tommy quickly turns his head to the window, hoping to catch a glimpse of the eagle. Meanwhile Harry reaches his hand around Tommy and snatches up the number for Cecilia Harris. He smiles devilishly like a small child. Tommy then turns back to Harry who quickly wipes the smile from his face] TTT: I didn't see an eagle, dude. Harry: It was just an airplane, man. [Tommy looks at Harry suspiciously and then just forgets about it, going back to his journal...] APRIL 6, 1994 My 16th birthday was TOTALLY GNARLY!! Harry got me a tiny replica surfboard of the one I use. It's custom made which probably cost the Harrymeister a lot of cash. He's totally bodacious for doing that. My mom took off early in the morning though so she wasn't there to attend my most triumpant bash, or so I thought. She showed up halfway through with a group of dudes dressed up in sailor outfits. I don't know if she went to a Halloween party in April or what, but these dudes definitely looked like sailors. She rushed to her room with them and it was only shortly after that that everyone at the party could hear the sounds of moaning and pounding. Even with The Beach Boys blasting at a most ear friendly volume everyone could hear those sounds. Talk about totally bogus of my mother to have a massive orgy during her son's 16th birthday party. All of my most rad friends were totally spooked out as to why my mother would do such a thing. I felt crappy and Harry offered me hits from his bong, BONG VON BONGI. I took down bong hits like Love Was A Bad Name and like I Was Wanted Dead or Alive. I felt totally bodacious in a far out way after those hits and went on enjoying the party. Then things got hairy...not like my most gnarly compadre...but like that stuff that grows out of your head, and pubic regions. The sailors left with smiles on their faces...and then some of my friends started going into my mom's bedroom. IT WAS TOTALLY BOGUS!! MY FRIENDS WERE DOING MY MOM!! It was like a line for a most excellent amusement park ride. She was servicing half of my high school and they weren't thinking twice about it. I may have been totally high but I knew that I had to stop it. Before I could protest Cecilia Harris approached me and wanted to see my room...so I did. May I say...I am no longer a virgin. TOTALLY TUBULAR!!! [Tommy smiles to himself after his first sexual experience. Especially one with that bodacious babe Cecilia Harris. But his mom still plagued his mind and why she did those things was totally beyond Tommy. How can you just sleep with his friends from high school without thinking about consequences? It was totally bogus!] [In the background Harry can be seen on the telephone] Harry: Hello? Is this Cecilia, man? [Harry pauses for a few moments and then has a look of anger on his face] Harry: What do you mean this is her boyfriend?! I'm her boyfriend, man. [There is a much longer period of silence as Harry begins looking at himself in the mirror] Harry: Yeah...this is Harry, man. Cecilia, remember that time in US History when I gave a presentation on George Washington. He nods his head a few times looking a bit confused at times, but when isn't he confused? Tommy looks behind him wondering who in the name of Judas Priest is he talking to on the phone] TTT: Who are you talking to, dude? [Harry covers the receiver at the bottom] Harry: Cecilia Harris, man. [Tommy's eyes bug out of his head as he runs out of the room for a few moments only to return holding the cordless phone from the kitchen, he listens in] Cecilia: ... Harry your not my boyfriend, Ok? Harry: I am too. Remember under the bleachers at the Homecoming game when you told me that there was nobody else in the world you couldn't be without? [Cecilia sounds as if she doesn't even remember it happening] Cecilia: What are you talking about, Harry?! That wasn't me!! I was with Greg Velmire on the homecoming float all that time. [Harry now seems extremely confused, thinking back to that day and now remembering who it was] Harry: HOLY FARTBUBBLES, MAN!! I was talking to the janitor, Mr. Kelliher. He has moles all over his face...and he was a guy, man! Cecilia: Yeah...but now that that is cleared up I have to go. My baby needs attention. Harry: Do you breast feed your baby? Cecilia: Yes. Now I need to go, it was good catching up Harry. [The phone goes dead] TTT: That was totally bogus, dude! Harry: That was far out, man. She is into breast feeding. We have been best friends, almost like brothers really, since I can remember. We have that over every other team in that ring and we can work together. People question Harry's intelligence but he's a most rough compadre and can take it to people in the ring. The Heartbreakers, Pretty Cool Guys, Dark Messiahs, Suicidal Tendencies, Misplaced, Akiyama...all those bogus dudes don't have the bond that we have and they will all go over the top rope. I hope that on Sunday night Harry and I return to that hotel room carrying the PCW Tag Team Titles. Honestly, winning those titles will wipe everything away that is most plaguing my soul.
