Date: not entered Well…you win some, you lose some… I…was successful. I gained not only the PCW Cruiserweight title…but I also gained a sister in Anarchy…and a mother… Mother…never really thought about who she could be…or if I even had one at all. But she exists…and is with me now… But…I also lost a part of me…a part of what made me… C E L E S T I A L . . . Half bred? Only 50% Celestial? And 50% human?? This perfect vessel is tainted with the diseased chromosomes of the weak and filthy? I am not whole? Ugh…I feel sick… Finding that out was worse than if I lost the match against Anarchy. But nothing was worse than the god… and the divine sin committed against him. He destroyed everyone in his path…only to have some mortal screw him in a quick count. Leo…he got what he had coming to him…but there’s going to be a lot more coming to him, as well as everyone when the god comes calling. Duke Williams…you saw just how weak you really are. You saw that your mouth is only good for threats…but your body isn’t capable of backing them up. You sent the god Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound…but immediately you were shown your Absolute Destiny Apocalypse! The nVg stood up like a child had managed to trip him on their jump rope. A weak child you are…Williams…with childish words…and childish strength. And El Diablo…this has been the second time he’s found destruction from the god’s hands at a pay per view. Two straight shows in a row…two straight ADAs upon the Mexican Devil. Well….mortals don’t learn much after being beaten by the god…brain damage and all… But if the newly turned bad boy wants another round with the god…he can come and get it just like he got it at Red October…and just like he got it at Third Dawning… But there’s one more man who will learn that you do not escape so easily from Celestial destruction… Clyde… We must applaud you…it took real guts to poke the god in the eye. And not only that…but to sneak away with a win that you fell into…real guts there. We’re going to see how many guts you have this Fusion…because if you think you can pull the same crap you pulled this Sunday…we’re going to rip off your finger, as well as rip out your internal organs. Heh…as for Tomas… … There’s no way an American Woman can be considered a threat against the CELESTIAL GODDESS…the Divine Prophetess…and the PCW Cruiserweight Champion… So says…the Divine Prophetess… =©= Last Night… Sonata stared at Matrix as he watched her with frightened eyes. She stood there, dressed in her yellow stretch jeans, black leather heeled boots, black halter top, yellow matching jean jacket, and her black and yellow hair tied in a ponytail. With laser-point accessory marked dead between the eyes. “What are you staring at?” she asked impatiently. She’s really shown her true colors since the Tokyo Police captured them when they arrived to Japan. She took both of the guards watching them out, and broke them out of the precinct without notice. Matrix wasn’t sure if she even was the same person he had met the first time. “Don’t…move.” He said. Well…moving would be a smart thing, considering targeting requires immobility. He reached into his pocket and pulled out his cell phone…that amazing gizmo of advanced technology. It was by no means a Palm Pilot of Death…but it was pretty neato keen if you ask around. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Reality flicked away his cigarette and glared over at Shift. “Isn’t she dead yet?” The sniper named Shift waved a black-gloved hand back at the Executive. “Patience…is a virtue.” “So is me kicking your ass!” the red haired man snapped, pulling a fresh cigarette and a lighter from his open black suit jacket. “Now kill the b*tch dead!” Shift sighed, his aiming right eye going back into the scope. “Fine…you wanna rush art…we’ll rush art.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Matrix pointed his cell phone at Sonata Kojima and ran it over her body from where he stood, about five feet away from her. “Okay…when I say duck…you duck.” She rolled her eyes. “What’s going on with you?” “You really want to know?” She nodded, the laser dot moving back and forth across her forehead. “YES!” Matrix looked up from his cell phone with a smile. “You have a laser target in your face, which means someone’s about to blow your brains all over the sidewalk.” She stared at him for a moment blankly… “No…really…what’s going on?” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Shift licked his lip as he squeezed the trigger. The gun fired… But Sonata Kojima stayed standing. Reality looked over the edge of the rooftop below. “Dammit! You missed her!” “That’s impossible!” Shift protested, looking up from the scope on his sniper rifle. “I never miss!” Just then, another Sonata was shown running down the street. Reality and Shift watched as Matrix pressed a key on his phone and ran off behind her. The image of Sonata that Shift was aiming at…disappeared in thin air. “Arrrgh!” Reality said, turning around and rushing for the stairs into the building. “They duped you with a hologram!!” Shift followed, lugging his rifle behind him. “Where’d they get the technology for THAT??” “Matrix is former DDE! He was in charge of technological events! He’s a f*ckin whiz with computers and sh!t like that!!” “Fine time to tell me this now!” Shift said, following Reality down the flight of stairs. Reality suddenly stopped, whirling around and aiming a electric taser nightstick at his heart. “Shift…” he said slowly, deeply, and threateningly. “DON’T…raise your voice at me.” Shift nodded quickly before Reality continued down the stairs. =©= Footsteps stomped heavily through the backstage hallways of the Tokyo Dome. Huddled steps…coming down hard against the black tiled floor with a purpose in mind. The march led on around a corner, past PCW staff until they stopped at a single black door… Celestials In bold silver letters… A fist came knocking down on the door with authority…bold move to come calling to the god like that. Just like it was bold for Leo Michelli to slam his hand down on the mat so rapidly against the god. He will be dealt with…indeed he will… A woman answered the door…a blue haired woman with icy blue lips. She peered out of the room with ice blue eyes at the nine combat-geared armed Japanese officers stood ready to fire. She was dressed in a ice blue tank top with black leather jeans, the tank tight and spouting a developed chest. She looked young…but she was built like a full-grown woman of maturity. “May I help you gentlemen?” This…was Glacia…known to PCW as Mikado Kusanagi…also known as the mother of the Divine Prophetess… Standing before her…at the point of the pack…was Ushio Kuonji, commander in chief of the Neo Void God Expulsion Task Force. “Ma’am…I need to speak with Akira Akiyama…NOW.” There was no tolerance in his voice as he ordered her to let him in. She blew what seemed to be a puff of white vapor from her lips before stepping aside, opening the door wide for the soldiers to walk in. Entering the dimly lit dressing room, they find the god sitting with his head lowered, glaring at the ground. Standing by his side was Cyclonica and Anarchy, both of whom leaned against the wall with their arms folded under their chests. They both didn’t look pleased with the way the end of the night went…despite the gold draped on the Divine Prophetess’s shoulder taken from her now revealed half-sister. “Blasphemous insects…” Cyclonica muttered. “How dare they think they can dive out of harm’s way that easily! That Leo will pay for his sin…and Clyde will come to feel his destruction soon enough for the narrow escape of Apocalypse!” Anarchy gave a short nod, either agreeing or still mulling over the father she just found. Cyclonica turned her attention to the Nihonjin standing in their doorway with weapons pointed at them, a scowl on her face. “What is the meaning of this?” she snapped. “You dare to come calling upon the god in this drastic time of his fierce anger? He will crush you all like bugs!” “We have a warrant for the arrest of Akira Akiyama for the crime of treason, murder, fraud, and crimes against the country.” Ushio barked, holding up a document in his hand. Cyclonica glared at them for a moment before turning to Glacia. “You let them in?” Cyclonica’s mother shrugged her shoulders, another wisp thought to be cigarette smoke blowing form her lips…but she wasn’t smoking. “They had guns…what could I do?” The Divine Prophetess eyed her blankly… “You are so new around here…” Anarchy watched as her sister pushed herself from the wall and looked to Ushio Kuonji. “Look, mortal…I don’t know what makes you think you can walk in here and DEMAND that the NEO VOID GOD just give himself up…but you’re WAY OUT OF LINE!” Ushio stepped up, half his officers taking aim on her while the other kept the rest of the room’s occupants at bay. “This is a direct order from the Emperor and Prime Minister themselves.” Cyclonica stepped toe to toe with the man, towering over him by about three feet. “I don’t care if it came from L Ron Mother F*cking Hubbard…there’s nothing you can say that’s going to make the god submit to your will pigs!” “We’re holding your friends in custody…the sandy haired kid and the black and yellow haired woman.” She scoffed at them. “You can KILL THEM for all I care! You are not--!” Just then Akiyama pushed past her, glaring down into the eyes of the Japanese Defense Force Commander Ushio pulled out a pair of handcuffs, finding the size of the god’s wrists to be completely larger than the cuffs can handle. He shrugged as he motioned for the god to step forward into custody. Cyclonica watched as Akiyama walked off with the soldiers, Glacia and Anarchy watching silently as they close the door behind him. “Well…” she muttered, picking up the Cruiserweight title. “Now that’s just Divine…” T O B E C O N T I N U E D
