Rhyno Logic

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th April 2002 BST or the 7th EST We rejoin the collective known as... ok, we rejoin Miyagawa as he walks down the corridors of the Duck Pond here in Anaheim, California. He would be quite proud of being in the stadium where the Mighty Ducks play, but as he all saw at March Madness, Miyagawa happens to be a St.Louis Blues fan. Mainly because the ducks suck. The ducks suck and they have a lot of unspent cash. Where the hell is the logic in that? Rhyno logic. He walks down the corridor some more when he hears a crash at the end of it... he walks up and the place opens out into the staff car park section... where there is a coke machine. He hears raised voices. Voice: GORE!!! GORE!!! DAMNED MACHINE!! GORE!!!! GIVE ME A CAN OF PEPSI!!! GORE!!!! GORE!!! Miyagawa walks over towards here a man with really really greasy hair in a black wrestling singlet is attempting to spear a poor unsuspecting Pepsi Cola machine while a guy in a light brown suede jacket and black baseball cap is screaming “Gore” at him. Miyagawa looks around for either Al Gore or some blood, but neither are present. He walks over towards the duo and stands about a meter away from the Pepsi Cola machine which is still taking quite the battering. Baseball Cap Guy: GORE!!!! GORE!!!! The greasy wrestling singlet wearing gimboid spears the Pepsi Machine again and then backs up and does it yet again for still what appears to be no valid reason at all. Miyagawa: Um... you do realise that all drinks machines are free tonight on Duke Williams orders, right? The singlet wearing grease ball stands up straight and walks right up to the machine and hits a button... out popping the plastic bottle full of Pepsi... the choice for the next generation. Miyagawa: Sorry guys, my narrator is sponsored by Pepsi. The grease ball shrugs at the man wearing the pink shirt as if he’s some sort of Chris Jericho Japo-American clone or something. The baseball cap sticks his hand out and snatches the can of Pepsi from the singlet wearing, who promptly gets another can of Pepsi, the choice for the next generation from the machine. Miyagawa then also gets a can of Pepsi, the choice for the next generation from the machine as well. Baseball Cap Wearer with the Ponytail and the crappy suede jacket: Hi, I’m Paul Heyman and this here is the meanest, the toughest son of a bitch in all of California at the moment he is... Miyagawa holds up his hand to Heyman, who shuts up. Miyagawa: I know who he is. Nice to meet you Chyna, gotta admit your bitch tits are even bigger then Reverends, I’m impressed. So how’s life treating you then? Heyman and “Chyna” look at Miyagawa completely slackjawed. “Chyna”: Can I Gore him now boss? Heyman: No, this is one of the people we’re been looking for... you see, RHYNO and I are out here teaching people logic... Rhyno: Rhyno logic. Miyagawa opens the can of Pepsi, the choice for the next generation and takes a heartily gulp of its cool, crisp, clean taste before saying out loud. Miyagawa: So let me get this straight, you are not Chyna. Rhyno: No. Miyagawa: Is that not currently, because you’ve an operation of some sort... I mean it’s not like its Chyna’s first is it? Rhyno: I am not, nor ever have been Chyna. I never want to be Chyna; I never even want to meet Chyna. I am not Chyna. Miyagawa: So you’re not Chyna then? Rhyno: For the last time, no I am not Chyna. Miyagawa: What’s with the “Y” thing then? I mean I can understand not calling her “China” because I think the communist government would be a little annoyed... but what’s with the... Heyman: Contract issue, anyway, we’re missing the point here. Miyagawa: There’s a point? Heyman: Yes, of course there’s a point. There’s always a point. Miyagawa: There was no point to Rhyno’s interference at No Mercy last year during the Tag Team match. Heyman: There was a point. Miyagawa: No there wasn’t. Heyman: Was there a point in you kidnapping Clyde then? Miyagawa thinks for a second over both situations. Miyagawa: Good point. Heyman: If you’d like to follow us, we’ve assembled several people for you to meet once again. Miyagawa: Do I know them? Heymen: I’m sure you’ve met them all at least once before, you see... we’re going to demonstrate the principles of Rhyno logic. Miyagawa: Is this going to be like a lecture of Scientology, because I really have better things to do... like watch paint dry or something, that’s much more interesting. Or perhaps arrange Syko’s “Welcome Back to PCW” party. Heyman: Come on, there’s punch and pie. Miyagawa raises his eyebrow as the offer of punch and pie seems to have peaked his interested. Miyagawa: Punch and Pie you say? Heyman: Yeah, punch and pie. Miyagawa: Ok. Let’s go, but there better be some decent pie... what type of pie do you have? Heyman: You don’t want to go there. Miyagawa: Go where? Heyman: Gimmick infringement, you really don’t want to go there. Miyagawa: That coming from a man who hands out animal based gimmicks as if they were candy? Heyman grins at Miyagawa and Rhyno. Heyman: So you have heard of me then! Miyagawa shakes his head as he follows the grease ball in a wrestling singlet called Rhyno... or Chyna depending on your suspicious nature and of course the man they call Paul Heyman... the even greasier ponytail wearing cos he’s otherwise completely bald, baseball cap wearing, crappy suede jacket wearing... Miyagawa: My narrator really fucking hates you Paul. Heyman: I’m sensing that. Rhyno: I’m not Chyna. Miyagawa: You said that already. Rhyno: You didn’t believe me. Miyagawa: True. The trio walk on, out of the car parking era as they each drink their Pepsi... ok, that ought to do the sponsorship deal... I can now admit that they’re all drinking Snapple and not Pepsi at all. Anyway, they wander forward to a room. Miya and Rhyno walk up the corridor and Paul Heyman leaps from behind them both. Heyman: DOOOOOOOOR! DOOOOOOOR!!! Miyagawa: Why thanks Paul *Walks thru door* You’ve got a real problem with that... Heyman: I know... GORE!!!!!! DOOR!!!! My ass is SORE!!!!!! Your wife is a BORE!!!!!!!!! I need mental help. Miyagawa: Well I would prescribe a healthy dose of Therapy. Heyman: I’ve seen your Therapy and I don’t want anything of it. How many people did you choke out on Fusion exactly? Miyagawa: I lost count... I might have been eliminated, but that sure was fun. Miyagawa grins like a Cheshire cat and then looks into the room properly at last... and sees about ten people of various shapes and sizes... he begins to remember a few faces from here and there... particularly one... Drake Maxwell. One of the little guys steps forward with a British Accent. Brit: Hi there. Maxwell steps forward immediately afterward. Maxwell: Hi there. Brit: I just said that. Maxwell: I know, I just reused it. Hi there. Brit: Eh? Maxwell: I just used it again. The pair step backwards into the crowd as greasy old Chyna... erm... Rhyno steps into the room. Miyagawa: So what’s with all the jobbers? Do you want me to train them or something? Hey! What’s JJ doing here? JJ waves from behind a much taller guy trying to be noticed. JJ: I’M NOT GAY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Heyman, Chyna... Rhyno and Miyagawa all look at each other in confusion trying to work out who just called JJ gay. JJ: Wait a second... damn. Miyagawa: So why are all the jobbers here then Heyman? Heyman grins at Rhyno and signals for him to speak. Rhyno: It’s simple... it’s Rhyno logic.... you see at some point in your career you lost to each person in this room... and tonight you have a chance of becoming PCW World Champion again... now according to Rhyno Logic, that makes each and everyone one of these people here the Number one Contender to the Primetime Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Title. Miyagawa: Eh? Rhyno: It’s simple... Miyagawa: I heard what you said. I just still have to say... eh? You dragged me all this way to tell me some shitty little story about how you’re so big and dumb and have this cack handed view of logic, plus you drag a load of old farts here into a room to try and put me off my game? Heyman and Rhyno look at each other for a second before looking back at Miyagawa. Rhyno: Well... yeah. Miyagawa looks at the men in the room and starts picking on them one by one. Miyagawa: Let’s take Maxwell for example... Maxwell: For example... Miyagawa: Will you stop copying me? Maxwell: Copying me... Miyagawa: I mean it. Maxwell: It... Miyagawa punches Maxwell in the face, then dodges an identical punch back at him. Miyagawa: GORE!!!! GORE!!! Rhyno instinctively dives into the chest of Maxwell into a spear, smashing him into the wall, knocking the plaster and wallpaper for six, and Maxwell unconscious to the mat. Miyagawa looks at Rhyno as he stands up and grins. Miyagawa: Roll over? Fetch? Would you like a Rhyno-snack? Heyman: Only I can give him his Rhyno-snacks... and they’re in the car. Rhyno: Aww... Heyman: Ok, I have one. Rhyno starts to beg like a dog and Heyman pulls a small biscuit out of his pocket and throws it to Rhyno who catches it out of the air with his mouth. Miyagawa: Ok, now this is just mentally disturbing. Rhyno and his master look at Miyagawa for what he just said, wondering if he meant them. The fact that Miyagawa was all the time locked on Bad Battio probably means that he wasn’t. Miyagawa: You brought Bad Battio back... Christ... now stand back... wait I know a second... Heyman, do you have a key for this room? Heyman: Yeah. **Holds up key in his right hand** Miyagawa snatches the key out of his hand and dives out the door, before locking it on the outside, locking the nine other people inside. Miyagawa: Let’s see... there are nine people in there including Chyna... GORE!!!! **Thunk** Miyagawa: GORE!!!!! **THUNK* GORE!!!!! **THUNK* GORE!!!!! **THUNK* GORE!!!!! **THUNK* GORE!!!!! **THUNK* GORE!!!!! **THUNK* GORE!!!!! **THUNK* Miyagawa breathes deeply trying to get his breath back. Miyagawa: Wow, now I know what Slade feels like after a sentence. Miyagawa unlocks the door and out wanders Rhyno, looking a little confused. Miyagawa: Nice one Chyna, that’s all the skeletons in my closet removed. Rhyno: I’M NOT CHYNA!!!!! Miyagawa: Of course your not, of course your not. Now if you excuse me, I have to go defeat the double Chrises. Not a pleasant thought, but still... someone has to do it... oh and Chyna... Rhyno: My name is RHYNO. Miyagawa: Close the DOOOOOOOORRRRR!!!!! Miyagawa grins madly as he wanders away leaving Rhyno standing there looking rather bemused next to the open door in which Paul Heyman, Drake Maxwell, Bad Battio, Jeremy Johnson, Brock Newbludd, Jake Keeton, Stewart Hix, Mike Storm and Johnny Vain all lie unconscious when suddenly he hears... Miyagawa: SHIT!! I FORGOT THE PUNCH AND PIE!!!
