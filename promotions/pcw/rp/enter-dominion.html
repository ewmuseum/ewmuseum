Enter: Dominion

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-4-03 It has always been my forte to display one side of the spectrum. I’m a man with two completely different sides, meshed together as one. Very few have been able to interpret me for what I really am. Angel in my pocket, devil by my side. I’ve lived by that code all of my life. And yet, very few have been able to break into the mold and dissect me. Hell, I’ve put all the hints out there. Frankly, I’m tired of waiting. It’s about time I explain. The first half of my famous phrase is a reflection of myself. Eric Wolfson. Angel in my pocket. Simply put, I’m not all that bad of a guy in reality. I don’t go around looking for trouble and I certainly don’t *want* to hurt people. That’s just the way it goes down. People push, push, push and ask for it. They ask for me to pull the ace out of my sleeve. And when I can’t take anymore, the devil by my side comes forth. Dominion rules with an iron fist. ****** [ The room was actually calm for a change. What was more surprising, however, was the fact that the entire business was quiet. On a day like today, a business like this one was usually bustling with people. You know how it is. Everyone wants to be in show business in some way, shape or form. And that’s where one Larry Jones came in. Anyone who was going to be successful in any sort of profession in front of a live crowd – or in front of a camera, for that matter - was going to need an agent. More on him in a moment. ] [ Eric Wolfson sat patiently in his seat. He’d been waiting for quite a while, enough time to even find an entertaining beat emanating from the octagonal fish tank next to him. He shuffled in his seat, tapping the glass with his right middle finger. Finding absolutely no entertainment in the act, he finally leaned farther back in his seat, crossing his arms. ] [ While he was sitting within this tranquil setting, Wolfson found himself deep in thought. He had just made yet another huge career move and while such a thing never bothered him before, this time was a little different. He was twenty-seven years old now. He’d be entering a brand new promotion and this one was a far cry from where he’d come. Primetime Championship Wrestling was a whole lot more commercialized than Wolfson was used to. Don’t get me wrong, Wolfson had gone through his fair share of commercialization and wasn’t by any means camera shy. The only thing was, he’d have to get used to playing by a whole new set of rules. His language would have to be scaled back greatly, for one. ] *CRASH* [ Wolfson’s head jerked toward the direction of the sound, as the shattering of glass cut through the silence, almost enough to make him jump. Larry Jones stood in the doorway, his eyes widened and his jaw lowered. ] “Uh, buddy…” Wolfson started. “You ok, man?” [ Larry gained his composure, slowly making his way into the room. Larry began to slowly walk past Wolfson, who was seated near his desk, keeping his eyes glued to him. Wolfson, on the other hand, merely rolled his eyes as he figured the situation out. ] “What do you think?” Wolfson asked. “Go ahead, say it.” [ Larry seated himself, rubbing his eyes. ] “What the hell happened to you?” Larry asked. [ Running his hands across the top of his head, Wolfson shrugged. ] “What?” Wolfson responded. “You don’t like it?” [ Shaking his head, Larry blinked hardly. ] “Nah, it looks good.” Larry replied. “Just… It’s *very* surprising. Caught me way off guard is all.” [ Wolfson chuckled to himself for a moment. Larry did have a point. Wolfson was always the guy with the long blonde hair and rock star look. Now, with his short black hair and goatee, he had to agree that it was a huge change. ] “It was time for a change.” Wolfson admitted. “Sure, the chicks totally dug the long hair, but let’s be realistic here. I’m married. Off the market. And besides, with my new outlook on the business, this style fits me better.” [ Leaning back in his seat, Larry tossed his feet up onto the desk in front of him. He crossed his arms, narrowing his eyes. ] “Oh?” Larry asked. “And what is that all about?” [ Wolfson shrugged, leaning forward closer to the desk in front of him. ] “I’m sick and tired, Larry.” Wolfson admitted. “For so long now, I’ve been putting myself on the backburner for the betterment of my promotions. Putting the new guys over, playing the hero for the masses, etcetera. I’m just tired of it. I’m tired of being the guy everyone comes to when they need help. It’s about time I start taking what is deserved to me.” [ Larry nodded, taking a sip of the coffee he just picked up off his desk. ] “And when did this come about?” Larry asked. “Because, I just talked to you about two weeks ago and you didn’t mention any of this. ] [ Wolfson nodded as well. ] “Let’s just say I had an epiphany.” Wolfson challenged. “I’ve got this brand new road to look forward to now. Thanks to you, I’ve got Primetime Championship Wrestling to look forward to. And there’s no way I’m being the good guy this time around.” [ Larry shrugged, setting his cup back down on the desk. ] “Fair enough.” Larry admitted. “Speaking of PCW, what do you think?” [ Nodding in satisfaction, Wolfson replied. ] “Not too shabby, old man.” Wolfson replied, chuckling. “You’ve outdone yourself this time.” [ Dusting off his shoulder, Larry smirked a little. ] “Was there any doubt?” Larry asked. “I mean, you did say you wanted something a little bigger this time around, right?” [ Wolfson smiled. ] “And that’s what I got for you.” Larry continued. “Primetime is a fitting name for this place. The truth is, they rule in the ratings and if getting back into the mainstream is what you want, this is the place to do it.” [ Leaning back in his seat again, Wolfson allowed all of the possibilities to fill his mind. ] “Friday Fusion.” Larry started. “Not only did I get you an exclusive contract with this place, but I got you a title match at the upcoming Fusion event.” [ Wolfson’s eyes widened. A grin covered his face. ] “You??” Wolfson asked. “Larry, did you not catch the previous Fusion event? This Dorn Tracer cat… I apparently got under his skin without even trying. It’s more than obvious that this guy took my words to heart and wants to take care of me in the ring. Sorry, pal, but I got this one myself.” [ Whatever the truth may be, the point was that there was the pressing matter of a title shot at hand, and Larry had to get his client ready. ] “Whatever the case may be…” Larry began. “You’ve got to be prepared for this thing. It’s not a simple one on one. You’re looking at a four corners match.” [ Wolfson rolled his eyes, letting out a deep sigh. ] “C’mon, partner.” Wolfson replied. “This is me. You know better than anyone that I can hang. One corner, two corners, three, six, ten… Doesn’t matter to me. Point is there are three other guys in this thing that I’ve got to dispose of. Simple as that.” [ Larry removed his feet from the desk, picking up a piece of paper in the process. ] “Jade, Cozette du Pres and of course the Champion himself, Dorn Tracer.” Larry read. “Those are the guys you’re looking at this week.” [ Wolfson allowed the information to sink in. ] “Heh, Tracer…” Wolfson thought aloud. “That friggin’ guy has a few screws loose. I mean, there I am, Larry. I just got done meeting with one of the PCW suits, I’m standing there waiting on another to see me, reading my paper and there that wanker is. Up in the rafters hollering out some crap like he’s a god among men. I mean, while there’s nothing wrong with thinking you’re a notch above the rest, he’s taking this a little too far. Either this cat is playing some sort of character, or he’s completely insane. I hope for the latter, actually. Someone needs to slap him back into reality.” [ Larry held his hands in front of him, as if to motion for Wolfson to slow down. ] “Wait a second, Eric.” Larry requested. “You’ve got to think of the bigger picture here. Forget about that guy as a single being. You’ve got to take these other two into account just as much as him. Tracer is the Champion, but Jade and Cozette du Pres are just as much of a threat.” [ Taking his shades off of his face, Wolfson peered at his long time friend with sarcastic eyes. ] “Oh, I’m taking them all seriously, don’t doubt that.” Wolfson admitted. “I’m just saying. This guy talked a lot of smack Tuesday night. That’s not going to go unanswered. I think I’ll have a little bit of fun with this Dorn Tracer character, Larry. Let a little sunshine into his life, dig?” [ Larry had been holding this back since first getting a glimpse of the upcoming Fusion’s card. Finally, when he couldn’t hold it in any longer, he opted to fill Wolfson in on a little bit of news. ] “By the way.” Larry started. “Remember how I told you that PCW was a little more of an inter-gender federation?” [ Wolfson nodded, shrugging. ] “Well…” Larry continued. “Don’t the names Jade and Cozette stand as a little odd to you?” [ Wolfson paused for a moment, looking off to the side. ] “I dunno.” Wolfson replied. “I just thought they were a couple of nancies.” [ Larry grinned. ] “Try women, Eric.” Larry corrected. “You’re facing two women Friday night.” [ Looking on in shock, Wolfson’s jaw dropped a little. ] “Women??” Wolfson asked. “You’ve *got* to be kidding me…” [ Larry laughed a little to himself. ] “I kid you not.” Larry admitted. “I told you this thing was inter-gender.” [ Wolfson looked around the room for a few moments, trying to get a grip on the situation. Finally, he paused with a smirk slowly forming on his face. ] “The hell you say.” Wolfson replied, joyfully. “Heh. This thing just got a whole lot easier. I mean, what happens if they break a nail?” [ Wolfson began laughing. Larry on the other hand, stayed quite serious. ] “Don’t even think about it.” Larry interjected. “Trust me, any woman who can make the PCW roster isn’t any woman you want to be taking lightly. Especially these two. They’re looking to make something of themselves in Primetime. They’re just as hungry as you are.” [ Leaning back in his seat again, Wolfson smirked arrogantly. ] “Ok, Lar’. Wolfson replied. “Go ahead, give me their info.” [ Looking back down at the paper in his hand, Larry cleared his throat. ] “Cozette du Pres.” He began. “Her twentieth birthday just passed about two weeks ago. She’s pretty much new to the business, but very good at what she does. She relies strongly on her submission training, so you’ll want to keep an eye on her if and or when you get on the mat with her.” “Check.” Wolfson replied. [ Glancing up at his friend, Larry let out a slight sigh. ] “Jade.” He continued. “At twenty-three years of age, she’s an accomplished competitor having captured an Intercontinental Championship in a previous federation. Her fighting style is a lot like yours, a mix of technical and power, and she likes to give people shots in the pills, if you know what I mean.” [ Shaking his head, Wolfson snickered to himself. ] “What woman doesn’t?” Wolfson asked in a rhetorical fashion. “Go for the manhood, that is.” [ Raising his eyebrows as to completely agree, Larry smiled before continuing. ] “And then the Champion himself.” Larry digressed. “Oh, by the way, your Rising Star shot has now officially been changed to a Shooting Star shot. For whatever reason, the PCW brass bought into Tracer’s campaign and renamed the title. It’ll hold this new title for as long as Tracer holds it.” “Is that so?” Wolfson asked, intrigued. “That stupid Shooting Star thing actually has some life to it?” [ Larry taps the paper with his free hand. ] “Believe it.” He responds. “If someone beats this guy Friday night, it’s back to being the Rising Star Championship. Until that happens, however, get used to the Shooting Star name.” [ Nonchalantly, Wolfson shrugs his shoulders. ] “No problem.” He continues. “All I have to do is pin his shoulders to the mat for three seconds and that’ll be that. Simple enough.” [ Standing up from his seat now, Wolfson grabs his jacket from the seat next to him and slips it on. ] “Once again you’ve outdone yourself.” Wolfson admits. “Don’t miss Fusion. This Shooting Star business is about to be even shorter lived than the XFL was.” [ Wolfson turns to leave, however, Larry stops him. ] “Oh, I completely forgot.” Larry says, abruptly. “Eve called this morning, apparently she couldn’t get you on your cell…” [ Turning back around, Wolfson nods. ] “Yeah, that’s Team Mobile for ya.” Wolfson admits. “So what’s up?” “You’re to meet her at the airport tomorrow morning.” Larry replies. “She’s in.” [ Grinning, Wolfson turns and walks out of the room. ] To be continued…
