The REAL Heartbreak Hotel

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/02 On and off. It’s been nearly a year since Jack Valentine has been in his prime here in PCW. After starting off his career with a victory over the legendary Vampir Nosferatu, things were looking up for Valentine. He moved on, proving impressive in several key matches against several PCW Hall of Famers and winning the rights to be number 1 contender for several championship titles. Then, a black plague of injury struck the career of Jack, causing him to miss months of action sending him plunging back to the bottom of the ladder. After unsuccessfully trying to crawl rung by rung back up to the top, he was struck with another bit of career cancer, severely injuring his knee just a few weeks after his meager return. Now, Jack’s career is nearing the year mark in about a month and he has accomplished a little more than nothing. He has gained no respect in PCW and is fed up with being a nobody. All his life he has excelled the competition, completely destroying them in every aspect of life. He has succeeded in winning bodybuilding competitions, demolished society with his devilishly handsome looks and deserves to carry the arrogant aura that he possesses today. Yet, he is hackneyed with the lack of respect that he gets. He is back in PCW to give it one more try at gaining that respect, and this time his injury prone career will not get in the way of him getting to the top…at least in his mind. With that said, the camera pans into what seems to be some sort of set up set made out to look like a shrine to Jack Valentine. The background is a large pink curtain with the words “The REAL Heartbreak Hotel” written in flashing neon red lights across the curtain. Jack is sitting in a large, high-backed leather chair wearing his usual feather pink boa, a white headband wrapped around his short, yet perfectly gelled hair. He has on a nice satin robe with pink sparkles gracing the lining of the robe. The camera zooms into the exquisite and unblemished face of Jack Valentine as he throws out a smile with his sparkling white teeth and a wink of his eye. Jack Valentine: “Welcome…” He throws out another sparkling smile. Jack Valentine: “Welcome to the first ever edition of the REAL Heartbreak Hotel, where I, Jack Valentine…known to you all as The Heartbreaker will be taking you on a journey through the life and times of the greatest man who ever walked the face of this planet…who’s that you ask? Well let me tell you a little bit about this man because I will be introducing him very shortly as the first guest on the show.” Jack gives another million dollar smile to the camera and pulls out a pink envelope from his back pocket. Jack Valentine: “This man who is about to come out is a legend killer…a destroyer of greats. He has done it all and then some in the world of PCW, but has been out of action for several months now…” There are several children who are at the stage listening to the taping of this show and they begin bickering amongst themselves. Children: “Who could it be? Meanstreak? Ed? I can’t wait…” Jack Valentine: “This man has defeated 2 hall of famers single handedly and has taken out several stables without any help from others…” More bickering comes from the area of the children. Children: “Stanislav? Newbludd? I’m so excited” Jack Valentine: “Allow me to go in the back and walk hand in hand with this man out here to the show…it is such an honor to have him here today…” The children begin shuffling around and jumping with excitement in expectation of seeing someone that they know and love from PCW. A legend if you will… Jack walks behind the pink curtain and a loud drum roll begins to play from a sound machine operated on the side by one of Jack’s scrubby looking friends. An announcer’s voice takes over the PA system, or what seems to be a real announcer…the camera pans around to see Jack Valentine with his hand over a microphone pretending to be an announcer. Jack Valentine: “Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages. The REAL Heartbreak Hotel proudly presents to you the greatest superstar that has ever step foot in a PCW ring…” The drum roll becomes louder and a guitar string blares across the “perfectly” pink set. The guitar string is followed by a very familiar cord, known well to little “heartbreakers” all across the world. It is the sound of “Kickstart my Heart” by Motley Crue. The little children begin to wonder what is going on. They are curious as to why Jack Valentine’s music is playing. They were expecting some sort of superstar to come out and be on the show. All of the sudden, Jack lashes open the curtain and holds his hands jointly, high above his head as streamers and balloons come flying down from the ceiling as he waves humorously at the small children that are there to watch the show live. They begin booing at the top of their lungs but Jack’s friend drowns out their sounds by sending out waves of laughter and clapping from the sound machine. As Jack sits down in his chair, he acts like someone else is in the chair next to him. Jack Valentine: “Wow…I am speechless…it is such an honor to have a wrestler of your caliber here on my little show. I am amazed and enthralled by your talent and I wish I could be like you in every aspect of life.” Jack gets up out of his seat and sits down in the other seat as if he is answering the comments that were just made by himself. Jack Valentine: “Why thank you kind sir…it’s a pleasure to be here on this great set. Might I say you’re doing a fine job as the host of this fine program.” Once again Jack extracts himself from the seat and switches spots again. Jack Valentine: “Well, now that you’re here, let’s get down to business. How does it feel to be the greatest superstar that PCW has ever laid its eyes on?” Jack switches seats. Jack Valentine: “I’m flattered that you would say such a nice comment like that…but I don’t know if I would say that I’m the greatest. Even though I did pin superstars such as Ed, Trashcan Man, Vampir Nosferatu and The Chef.” He switches seats again. Jack Valentine: “That’s right…you did defeat those hall of famers…and without any help from anyone else I might add…” All of the sudden the little children decide to speak up. Children: “That’s bullcrap…you cheated every time and you know it!” Jack Valentine: “Shut up you little brats…SECURITY! Get these snot nosed punks out of here…” Jack looks over at his friend who is operating the sound machine. Jack Valentine: “Remind me to edit that out later tonight before this airs…” His friend nods. Jack sits back down and continues his embarrassment of an interview. Jack Valentine: “Now Jack, you’ve been burdened with a career of injury, yet you still remain at the top of your game…how do you do it?” He switches seats. Jack Valentine: “Well, it takes a lot of perseverance and determination. I have to work really hard, and it’s just good to know that I have the fans on my side and they are behind me a hundred and ten percent.” A cameraman off to the side is heard laughing at Jack’s foolish statements. He whispers to another cameraman. Cameraman: “That jackass hasn’t worked a day in his life…he’s too busy combing his hair.” Oblivious to what the cameraman just said, Jack continues the interview after switching seats once again. Jack Valentine: “Gee whiz…you are a role model for myself and everyone else in this proud country of ours. Now, you return to PCW this Friday to face someone named Mike Frampton. This guy was apparently a big superstar in FSW and has a great tag team career here in PCW alongside of Big Daveylicious…how does it make you feel to wrestle someone of this caliber in your big return match?” He jumps seats yet again. Jack Valentine: “Who?” Jack pulls out a file and begins filing his nails…after blowing on them for a little bit, he begins speaking again. Jack Valentine: “FSW means nothing to me…I’ve wrestled women that are better than those wrestlers if you can believe that…” Jack, back to his old ways of thinking that women are no good for anything more than sex, blows on his nails again after the derogatory remark. He throws the nail file down on the seat and switches chairs once again. Jack Valentine: “Wow…confidence of a superstar.” The cameraman whispers behind Jack’s back again… Camerman: “More like confidence of a jobber…” The camerman chuckles as Jack continues the bogus interview. Jack Valentine: “You also are in a 10-man battle royal at Spring Fling for the Rising Star title…a title that you’ve fought many times for, but have never won…” He jumps seats and picks up the nail file again, and goes to work on his nails. Jack Valentine: “I could care less about that belt…why do you think I’ve never won it? Don’t you think I would’ve won the belt if I had wanted it…I’m Jack Valentine…Picture Perfection…Grade A Heartbreaker. I do what I want, when I want.” The cameraman whispers once again. Cameraman: “Yeah right…that guy couldn’t win a belt to save his faggoty ann, pink wearing ass!” This time the cameraman was a little too loud and Jack overhears him. Jack Valentine: “Cut! Cut! Cut!” Jack Valentine stands up and looks at the cameraman who is still laughing at Valentine. Jack walks over and yanks the headphones right off of the ears of the cameraman. The cameraman turns around in shock as Jack grabs him by the hair and tosses him onto the set. Jack begins destroying the set as he tosses the poor, defenseless cameraman around. Jack plants a huge DDT on the cameraman through the table. He then shatters a glass window by tossing the cameraman like a rag doll through it. Jack Valentine: “I’ve had enough for today…let’s go.” Jack grabs his friend who was operating the sound machine and tosses his boa back around his neck like a diva, puts on his sunglasses and walks out with his nose held high. The scene fades to pink and the words “This has been a Heartbroken Production…” fade out in black on the screen.
