PLAY THE GAME

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-23-02 {The scene opens up with Kevin Klaymore and Juan Borro, the ever-impressive Golden Game, inside Raul’s barn working a little in preparation for their tag match. Kevin and Juan are tossing a saddle back and forth, improvising in the lack of technology. The sound of a sweat suit rubbing against itself is faintly heard over the naying of the horses. Panning over to Juan, you can see the sweat over his brows and the want in his eyes. Looking closer into the eyes, the only thing visible is the heart, want, and determination in him. Realizing Juan is going to do whatever it takes to win the camera quickly pans over to Kevin. Wearing his holy jeans and an unbuttoned shirt, he wipes off the sweat on his forehead quickly so he can catch the heavy saddle moving at high speeds. As he catches it he drops it down as Juan walks over to Kevin.} Juan (panting): So Kev…. What next? Kevin: You see those two hundred-pound wooden posts over there? {Juan looks over in disbelief in the size and weight. Then he points over to them confirming Kevin’s choice.} Juan: Ya mean those things over there? Kevin: Yeah man we have to be able to pick them up and throw them down. Politically Incorrect, is what I’m pointing at bud. {Juan begins to curse obscenities in Portuguese.} Kevin: Hey dude calm down, even you have to get your strength and stamina up. Juan: Kevin we’ve been at this all damn day we deserve a break. {At that very second Fidel stands beside the door and begins to watch Kevin and Juan train for their political battle.} {Kevin looks at his watch and before he could decide on anything Juan speaks over the constant mooing and naying of the cows and horses.} Juan: Well I can go longer and harder than anyone out there. I am the Golden Toe Juan Borro! I am the toughest jugador de futbol ever. I can run with the so-called big dogs. Kevin: That’s the spirit Juan. Let’s get this party started. {Fidel looks on at the unbelievable heart in these two. Even though they are so far away from any kind of training facility, they are training to get better and better even though they are the best right now.} {Kevin grabs a post and hands it to Juan who strugginly hoists it over his shoulders and begins a few mile run. Kevin grabs one and tries for a second and barely gets it over his shoulders. He then follows Juan in the run.} Fidel: Those chicos have heart. The corazon de luchando. Even the small Juan, he is more determined than anything is. {The scene then cuts to Juan and Kevin running through the stables and around the pastures to become even better.} Kevin: Look at this Juan. {Kevin hurdles over a small fence with ease but doesn’t see the other fence right in front of it.} Kevin: ha Mr. Soccer golden to-ohhhhhhhhh shit! {Kevin trips over it and falls right on his face. As he rolls over on his back one of the posts barely misses his head. Then the second lands millimeters from his family package. Then it suddenly falls down, first on his jewels then all the way from the stomach to his head.} {Juan drops the post to go see if Kevin is all right.} Juan: Kevin man you ok? Kevin: Hey Mandy when’d you grow a mustache? Juan: Kevin this is Juan? Can you see me? Kevin: Yeah I can see all three of you. Now will the other two leave and please speak one at a time. Juan: Oh boy… Kevin: … and pick up that phone Golden Heart. Juan: So Kevin you ok or what? Kevin: Yeah I’m ok just a little dizzy. {Juan falls down to the ground cracking up laughing over what just happened.} Juan: hahahahahahahahahahahahaha that was just too wahahahahaha funny man. Dude too bad we didn’t have a camera. You’d be all over European funniest videos. Kevin: Shut up man. I meant to do that. Why else do you think I’d go through all of that just to supposedly trip and fall? Juan: Because you tried to show off like the cocky son of a bitch you are. Kevin: Show off? Well I’ll show you show off. {Kevin gets up and chases after Juan. Juan being the quicker of the two quickly runs back to the house.} {Inside the house Juan quickly runs into his room where Juliet is showering. He quickly takes off his clothes and gets inside with her hoping Kevin won’t find him. But Kevin, seeing Juan jumping inside, gets an idea.} {Kevin goes over and grabs a bucket and a glass. He run downstairs fills the bucket up with ice and the glass with cold water. He then runs up the stairs and slowly walks into the room. He sees Juliet in a robe and motions for her to keep quiet.} Kevin: Shhhhhhhhh {Juliet nods and motions for Kevin to go on. Kevin goes into the bathroom and dumps the glass of cold water into the shower followed by the bucket of ice.} {Out of nowhere a lady shrieks.} Mandy: I swear to god if that was Kevin and Juan with their practical jokes I’m gonna kill them. {Kevin looks over to Juliet and sees Juan under the bed. Kevin runs out of the room and tosses the bucket and glass over at Juan. Mandy runs out of the shower wrapped up in a towel looking for the culprit. She looks and sees the bucket, the glass, and Juan hiding under he bed laughing.} Mandy: That’s it Juan I’m gonna kick your ass! {Juan looks up with a scared look on his face and tries to run. Mandy grabs him by the leg and puts him in a leg breaker.} Juliet: Go easy on him Amanda I still need him for tonight. Mandy: I’ll just hurt his leg a little just so he won’t try that shower stunt again. Juan: I didn’t owwwwwwwwwwww do it. Juliet: Sorry Mandy but Kevin did it thinkin you were Juan in the shower. Mandy: KEVIN KARLOS KLAYMORE VEN ACA AHORA! VOY A DAR UN PUNTAPIE TU CULO! {Scene cuts over to Kevin in his room with a scared look on his face and the sound of Mandy walking through the house screaming obscenities trying to find Kevin.} {Scene cuts over to Mandy finding Kevin. She gives him a little kiss then a hard punch to the shoulder. As she leaves Kevin rubs his arm and thinks how lucky he is to have friends and so much more. Even though he regrets living in the US he realizes that he would have never met Mandy. And never of met Juan or get into PCW. Isn’t it funny how one can despise one thing but thank God for it at the same time? GOLDEN GAME ARE GETTING BUSINESS DONE MENTALLY AND POLITICALLY.} {Scene pans over the ranch and soon fades to black. Golden Game and International Incident are making an impact in this so-called game of life.}
