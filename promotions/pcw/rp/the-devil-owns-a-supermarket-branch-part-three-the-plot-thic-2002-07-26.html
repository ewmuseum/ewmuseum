The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Three: The Plot Thickens…

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th July 2002 The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#2 for PPV (28th July 2002)… Chapter One: The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Three: The Plot Thickens… ::.~(Musings of Chris Chase)~ I twiddle my thumbs for the 1345th time today, sighing like I’ve made it a profession. Being dragged through a bush is bad enough, but being dragged through a bush whilst being lectured on the same thing over and over again is enough to make me pull a Dustin. Yeah, if this loudmouth doesn’t stop soon, he’ll be eating Mahogany for the latter part of the day. Yep, we’re still here, still bored to death – like we’ve been forced to sit through re-runs of Xavier Kannon promos for an entire day. We’ve all been subjected to the legally inclined whining of one Quinton Flynn, legal representative of the Wal-Mart Corporation, and a guy that Mister Kannon should find some level ground with… Not that that’s a good thing… For those of you who’ve just joined us (Where the fuck have YOU been?), let me briefly recap on the whole situation so far. A week ago, Dustin Downey was charged with the unprovoked assault on Wal-Mart employee Harry Good, and subsequently I’ve been politely co-opted into representing the idiot’s defense – for the greater good, apparently. I’ve always wondered about that, the greater good, exactly how great is it? That people have to continually make sacrifices for the greater good, if it was ever all that great, wouldn’t it mean LESS work? (Sigh - Writer). It’s for the greater good of this Sunday’s Pay-Per-View though, come to think of it. Sunday’s not all that far away, in fact, it’s pretty damn soon. So soon in fact, that I can already smell the blood stained breath of that vile guy known only as ‘Clyde’. Once again, I face the guy for nothing other than the pride of our respective beliefs – or for the sheer joy of pummeling the heck out of each other in the depths of a huge pit dug into a beach. Me versus the ever-evil Clyde. Come to think about it, isn’t ‘Clyde’ a river in Scotland? Never mind… This court case, particularly the prosecution, has dragged on for too damn long…nearly a full day of non-stop, in-your-face…tedium. It’s positively enough to make the more brightly astounding and brilliant individuals on this world kick the living crap out of the prosecution’s representative…but being that we’re in a bribed court room, even the slightest notion of mutiny would bring a ton of trouble piling on our very foreheads. I twiddle my thumbs again as the scene finally fades in for you people out there. Thankfully enough for you, you don’t exactly have to sit through all this, unfortunately for me, I do. The collar on my shirt seems a little more stretched than usual, my hair’s a little more out of place than usual…and Dustin’s slightly more deranged that usual. Enough to the extent of biting holes out of the desk whilst wielding a table leg like a broad sword, yelling profanities only Cap’n Dustin would know. Flynn paces in front of us, pointer held in his left hand, indicating to an another anatomy chart with much aplomb….:: “Psst. Yes, you…the one with the pipe organ…” “What? Would you keep quiet in case that moron notices us here again and attacks the bailiff? Remember fool, if he sees us now, the guy downstairs will fire us…literally! We can’t blow it now, his plan’s almost complete!” “Yes, Yes…I know that…but…” “Are your tap dancing shoes firmly attached, I mean, it’s hard enough keeping them on with these stupid frickin’ claws! I’m glad they stuck me with the portable pipe organ, the most vile and evil weapon ever wielded by a minion of Satan!” “Funny, that Table leg held by Dustin seems particularly dangerous, what if he goes after us with that?” “What do you think? We run you moronic cretin, didn’t they teach you ANYTHING at the Satan’s Minions Orientation Seminar, or did you fall asleep during the whole damn thing? Wait…don’t answer…I think Flynn’s about to finish…GET READY!” ::. Appearing to give up with the whole Cap’n Dustin act, Dustin gets down from the table and slumps into his chair, with some weird grin constantly affixed to his face. Not that that’s any different from the Dustin that I’ve known for well over a year now. Even Quincy, the overly worked-up judge, seems to faltering to the affects of the over-enthusiastic Flynn – the British court representative of the Wal-Mart Corporation. The whole thing is particularly reminiscent of the state of PCW affairs after I was crucified back in October of last year. Everyone degenerated into a completely lifeless state of existence, and the spirits of once rampant fans began to drop. Do you want that on your conscience again, Chase? That’s what happens when things become over-clichéd and trite, over-used and abused like illegal substances and loose women. Symbolic clichés aside, work must be done to preserve what was once sacred to those that mattered… The right to blabber at length about god knows what… Yep…I’ve gone and confused you all again, it’s not the first time – after all there was that time in Kentucky that I’m not fond of explaining for the twentieth time. I peel myself off the desk, which doesn’t quite hurt as much as it sounds – as much as you doubters would like to see me suffer. Sure, I’ve had a vacation, that doesn’t make me any less brilliant or even somewhat subdued. You’ve caught me in one of those moods that makes me seem like an arrogant asshole. Whoopee! It’s about damn time I seemed slightly dynamic for a change, remaining static is for the birds on marble statues and exaggerated #1 Contenders for titles well above their grasp. And you know who you are… So then, at last Flynn seems somewhat near finishing, after the twentieth excruciating hour that made my crucifixion look about as painful as petting a kitten….:: Quinton Flynn: Now, if you’d pay attention people. The Anatomy of Dustin Downey. Notice the abnormally positioned eyes, the particularly pointy ears…the incredibly sharp teeth. Is this a creature that should be considered normal? I don’t think so… ::. Dragging herself up from the depths of despair and boredom she’d been subjected to, Quincy hammers the desk feebly with the overly sized mallet of hers, staring down at Flynn with a half gone out, yet piercing glare. She takes one look at the anatomy chart, and for once, gives a decision that doesn’t seem tailored to the whims of Wal-Mart….:: Quincy: Flynn…that’d be a Doberman you ridiculously overpaid cretin, now sit down before the horde of mutant gophers at the door decide to feed on the remnants of your over-boiled brain. Now can we please get Chase up here before the rampaging horde of Satanists decide to burn this bloody place down! ::. Obviously the judge’s beginning to feel the mind-numbing effects of the inane and pointless banter, a typical trait of Dustin’s if I remember rightly, if at all. Flynn frowns upon Quincy’s sudden swerve against his ‘bore tactics’ and proceeds to fold up the anatomy chart which clearly shows a dog plastered all over one side of it. Go figure. There’s a huge rumbling amongst the ever-growing number of Satanists that make up the attending crowd in this fiasco as I rise from my seat for the first time in twenty hours. It’s enough to wear out a lesser being…but when was I ever lesser? So, here I am…blatantly promoting the merits of being myself in the ever-typical Chris Chase manner, that ever-continual cycle of saying that I’m better than at least 75% of the Global Population. Funny, I never felt that that was pointless at any point, but then again, I’m not thinking on a level with what I used to be like. It’s almost like consciously stating that I just might be average, which is hardly normal for an inhumanely labeled person such as myself. They called me ‘the Messiah’, they called me a ‘Saint’, and for what? For another opportunity to live in an imaginary world… Maybe now I’m fighting myself, beating myself like the self-hating jobbers that used to surround the free-form idiots such as myself. How frickin’ ironic. It’s hazy to say the least, translucent by any means. I’m no transparent guy; I’m too damn confusing to be easy to see through. Even voicing out complaints like my enemies seems to fail miserably – not that I didn’t expect it to, mind. So, here, I find myself bringing my good sweet self into the limelight for the first time in say, twenty hours. The atmosphere’s what I’m used to…those damn Satanists are getting rowdier with every step I take forward….:: Quincy: Well, Mister Chase. The defense? I mean, we haven’t got ALL day you know. Satanist: GET AWAY YOU FREAKIN’ GOD BOTHERER!! DOWN WITH JESUS! DOWN WITH JESUS! DOWN WITH JESUS! F*CK OFF MIIIIIISTER SAINT! ::. If I wanted this kind of reception, I’d have played bass and sung hymns at a Slayer concert, if I could bring myself to doing something THAT downright foolish. There’s a cold breeze blowing through, a cold breeze that brings some relief to my heated neck. I thank the person who bothered to open the door or at the very least a window…at least they’ve done more for this court than Flynn ever did. As I greet the crowd with a silent wave, a snort comes from Flynn, followed by an incensed glare from the attorney on the Wal-Mart side of things. Gathering myself together, which is easier done than said, mind. There’s a wad of papers in my right hand, stuck to the layer upon layer of sweat that’s built up in my palm ever since the first time Flynn managed to mention Dustin, which was the first of many if I’m not mistaken. Poking a pondering glance around the room, with my chin held with my free hand, I begin to question defending Dustin for the umpteenth time….:: Chris: Your honor…I call Dustin Downey to the stand… ::. And I do the predictable. Dustin rises from his seat and takes the stand, swearing his oath before the court and God himself. If only the toaster was here. My slightly deranged pal’s tired, we all are. Heck, let’s all be tired together. After all, being tired could ruin my chances of royally punking out ANOTHER Satanic jobber, although the number of matches that I’ve actually had since returning from that little vacation has been nothing more than two. Never fear though Chase fans…there’ll be much, much more of me to come…post Pay-Per-View, when the news comes that Chris Chase has once again foiled the forces of evil. But let’s leave that for now, let’s forget about all that hubbub when it comes to watching me make a complete Chase out of myself in front of the court….:: Chris: Ladies, Gentlemen and Satanists…you think you may just have seen it all. You think that my overly moronic comrade here is guilty thanks to the whining brought upon us by this Quinton Flynn. It’s nothing more than the clichéd propaganda brought on by Tyler Nelson, it’s repackaged garbage which is taken more swiftly than Xavier Kannon in the ass of a Ream Teamer… Satanist: SCIENTOLOGY AND CHRISTIANITY SUCK! UP WITH SATAN! ::. Will this day ever end? Will I be forced to listen to the chantings of Satanists forever? A minute long silence follows, as the Satanist that decided to yell that is subsequently beaten by his cohorts for some unknown reason as they begin to cheer. Maybe I’ve got more influence over these types that I thought, could it be possible that I’ve converted these fools to the Chris Chase way of thinking. Well…no. Dustin’s got a crucifix in his mouth…again, the origin of which is quite unknown to me. I’m not always the first to know these things. I slap my forehead…and continue despite the fact that Dustin’s obviously trying to mock me. Again, I shrug this off….:: Chris: Now, suffice to say Dustin, acting like a complete lunatic isn’t going to convince this jury that you’re innocent of any wrong doings in the past, in fact, I’m having a hard time trying not to slap you around the back of your freaking skull… Dustin: Is that ethical Chris, I’m sure that these types are pretty sensitive about violence and whatnot. After all, these are Satanists Chris…they don’t want to see Dustin slapped around the head, or a rampaging horde of midgets tearing the place down. They want to see unadulterated children’s entertainment; it’s the kind of thing that makes you wonder why they didn’t bring JJ here… ::. Ah…Vintage Downey, how freaking relevant….:: Chris: Probably because they’d eat your staff buddy Dustin, you know, it’s standard active Satanist policy to desecrate everything within reach of their clammy little hands. JJ is desecrated enough to withstand that kind of attention, do you at least understand that my slightly moronic and irrelevant pal? ::. This kind of conversation is reminiscent of the good old days of the Chase/Downey team that was High Impact, the team that jobbed Ivan Stanislav to Shawn Prodigy, the bane of serious teams the world over. Unfortunately, its relevance has nothing to do with the court at all, and obviously Quincy gets a little more than peeved when faced with the possibility of even more inane banter than she could shake a Meanstreak sized stick at. Sure, it might insult the Satanists to some degree…but it doesn’t truly bring about the real reason why Dustin attacked that innocent Harry Good, if you could call an intern that bangs hygiene staff that. Thus, Quincy nearly swallows her mallet in reaction to the repartee that brought the Satanists to a standstill….:: Quincy: ~(In a positively ashamed way)~ Mister Chase! If you two have nothing better to do than argue on the effects of Satanism on some former PCW jobber, then you’ve got another thing coming. If you can’t come up with a satisfactory line of defense in the next ten minutes, then I’m afraid I’ll have to sentence Mister Downey… Dustin: Again with the Sentencing? Jeez…you could have picked a better time to do this, how about 10 pm on next Saturday? If you bring the wine and I’ll bring me, we’re sure to work something out… ::.Almost as if the whole world had been subjected to the words “Tim” and “Walden” (Which would have possibly resulted in a mile high mountain of vomit), the entire world shudders at the simple thought of Dustin hitting on a tired old judge. Well…at least it’s something under these circumstances. Too bummed out to think of a half decent defense, I let loose the most terrifying weapon known to man…the truth, after slapping my forehead for the second time in the space of five minutes….:: Chris: ~(Sighs)~ Dustin, could you please describe exactly what happened during that incident last week? Come on, I’m running low on promotion time as it stands, can’t you at least be a little considerate towards the needs of an Anti-Satanist? Lead Satanist: DID YOU HEAR THAT, MY BRETHREN?!? LET’S KILL THE SAINT AND GO FOR A CHOIR BOY BURGER AFTERWARDS!! WHAT DO YOU SAY?! Rest of Satanists: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! UP WITH SATAN!! ::. Yes, dealing with Satanists is more fun than a barrelful of rabid weasels…NOT. Holding my arms up in protest to this, my piercing stare does nothing to phase the lead Satanist or his horde of cronies. However, Dustin looks down at me, and the court…perched up on the stand for all to see. For once in his life, he seems able to put together a thought or two, trying to remember why on earth he’d jump an defenseless vendor after being served forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts and a packet of cereal. After what seems like an eternity filled with the ranting of angry Satanists….:: Satanists: DOWN WITH CHASE! DOWN WITH CHASE! DOWN WITH CHASE! ::.Yes…there’s got to be some way of getting shot of that. Thankfully, Dustin finally comes too, after nearly exhausting himself trying to remember that very day that will lie in infamy for the rest of ETERNITY! (Coughs) I have no idea why I just said that, it sounded like a good idea at the time. – So, it’s time for Dustin to reveal the truth, once and for all…before the Satanists bring about a barn-burning catastrophe….:: Dustin: This story is one of fear, accomplishment, and mind-numbing side-plots that lead to nothing but eventual chaos. It’s complete with intrigue, hamsters and a thirty story coke can filled with goat urine…sorry, I mean goat’s blood… Satanists: INDEED! Dustin: And it goes a little like this… Two hours later… Chris: So what you’re telling us was that during the course of paying the goods, you were mocked or could that be welcome by a chorus line of demon minions wearing tap dancing shoes and playing pipe organs. And knowing not of how to cope with it, you went for them, inadvertently attacking Harry Good here at the same time, because they were sitting on his shoulder? ::.Two hours later and we’ve progressed no further other than attempting to come to terms with the ridiculous story of Dustin’s. It’s reduced a plethora of Satanists to tears, and failed to even mention the thirty story can of Goat’s blood – which is probably the reason for a horde of blubbering Satan botherers. However, the sound of pipe organs fills the air as I look at Dustin with that ever cynical stare of mine. Dustin himself nearly reduces himself to rubble upon hearing this….:: Dustin: ARGH! They’ve come for me again! “HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE’S A MINION! A LOUSY, STEALING, ROTTEN LITTLE CUR! NOT SO STRANGER…BUT HEEEE’S A MAIMER! DUSTIN DOWNEY!A MINION!HE’S A MINION!A MINION!HERE TO KILL THE GOOD BOY!A MINION!HERE TO SAVE HELL FROM THAT CHASE GUY!A MINION!A MINION! A MINION! A MINION!A MINION!SATAN’S BUTCHER BOY!A MINION!HE SOLD HIS SOUL! NOW HE’S GOT A GOAL!A MINION!TO MAIM THE SAINT! AND TO SING LIKE A TOTAL BUFFOON!A MINION! DOWNEY A MINION! DOWNEY A MINION! DOWNEY A MINION!” Dustin: NO…I DON’T WANT TO BE A MINION! I’M A FRICKING MORON!! ::. And once again…Dustin does the unthinkable – well, considering that he’s done it before, it’s not exactly unthinkable is it? For now, this is Chris Chase signing off… Fade to fucking White!.:: TO BE CONTINUED!
