...school daze Part:II...

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28.02.02 'To get hype before a game, you drink Gatorade, to get hype before a match you think and become your enemy.' CVI: Back in The Day - Part Two She always was open to comments by others, speaking her mind at different functions across campus, but it seemed that for me a mere freshman to try and tackle the cutest sophomore there, it was a challenge to play games with the people you knew and girls, was no exception. Many people on campus heard of my stunts with the skateboard trying to impress people left and right, and when it came to class work, I was a down right nerd to it. You know, more of the Tom Lexian kind, nerd, but cool around everyone else. The message was clear soon as I heard the voice of the Senior Guidance Counselor tell me. 'College is not a joke.' he said and I prematurely believed him for telling the truth in the matter. The Brown auditorium looked more like a place where you would find teams of twelve with five on the court trying to wrestle down a bouncing ball and put it through a little hoop. But no, it housed rows of chairs and a bulletin board placed on the left side for current school related events. There were students, lined up where there were supposed to be benches for the students, faculty, and audience to watch the games and I puzzled myself with the look. She sat in the first row, her red hair quickly bunched up with the ends of her pink sweater. I looked at the piece of paper that happened to list all of the classes I had and Historical Findings and Loopholes was first, followed by Integrated Physics, and then for third period Journalism, and finally for fourth period I think it was English, but I mostly slacked off there. 'To get hype before a game, you drink Gatorade, to get hype before a match you think and become your enemy.' As the story goes, I could hear the voice of a person, who closely got in my face for no reason. Football jock, you know those types with block shaped heads and chips on their shoulders. Gavin Newport, captain of the lackluster football team. Yet you knew when he was in one place, he had cronies. A red head with long hair who took up some type of cult religion. Another who had dark short blue spiky hair with a black leather jacket over his body. This was a group of freaks larger than a Lollapalooza concert. I'm a freak myself so I cannot doubt them for being what they were. One walked up to Amy, 'Hey, you think we can go out sometime? I really would like to see your pretty smile on a daily basis.' Newport asked her. 'No, and that'll be my answer til' eternity.' she told him, giving off a smirk from his friends. As I was looking down there, I felt my head getting hot and if this were my prize and this greasy black hared idiot was walking on property with my name on it. Soon I started to walk down the stairs and go talk to them, but a hand grasped on my shoulder, one of my partners had held me back, with a cool head. 'Don't do it man, a lotta dudes trying to get at her.' he told me, I looked at him out the corner of my eye. 'I'm going to let this one slide, for now.' I took a seat down into my chair, not looking at the girl in the front, but at a sight of my own personal conquest. A list full of questions waiting for answers, something like in my present now with Jeremy and Bobby, they all want answers to their questions, and I'm just the man to give it to them. Seems pretty though, like a flower in bloom - my rise to the top. CVII: Old School SUCKS~! My black hair crept over my face as the pencil scratched its lead along the brittle faces of the notebook paper, doodling little drawings of my mind while the teacher continued to make no sense whatsoever about the pointless moments of history. No one cared about history, it's what impact you left in history. Soon class was over, the rushing of people out of the doors were kind of odd enough to not even know who was coming out or going in. Taking my stuff into my arms, I accidentally bumped into her while she was walking out. There was a need for an apology, but really she looked at me with a look of anger, I apologized but I think I was getting nowhere fast with it. 'Watch where you're going!' she told me with an emphatic response to my apologetic pleas. 'You could have made me hit this desk!' 'Look, you're probably one of those spoiled children from the Hamptons aren't you?' I asked her with some wit added to it. I knew it was something that could prove useful. 'Well, yes but you're a plebeian! I mean you aren't even worthy of my presence. What will happen when I tell Daddy I met a weakling from the hard knocks.' she told me, I shrugged it off. 'Once you get out of this pampered spoiled bitchy stage in your life, you know life isn't handed to you fairly. Believe me.' I told her chagriningly, not actually know what she would say in response. I could see Gavin coming back with the corner of my eye. His dirty black colored Yankee's cap seemed to be back in style since the Yankee's were beginning their return to baseball dominance. The black letterman jacket he received in his junior year hung on his body tightly as a pair of blue jeans with white ensemble made the color code of everything perfect. He looked at me as if I were another pint sized nerd he'd pick on back in high school. 'What in the hell are you looking at scrimp? I think you need to have a refresh in I'm The Man 101.' he told me, a dirty smell of burnt cigarettes were on his tongue and teeth, giving off instant bad breath. It ran through a mind of his that nothing could penetrate unless it was something boasting his own ego, or some sex. Either or, this wasn't his place to be in as a bubble thought flew over my mind of knocking his block off, the kind of recreations you see on Ally Mcbeal. 'And you are?' I asked of him, I could tell by his voice, all of the thoughts in his mind and my were on getting Amy Falan around the arms of myself or him as if she were a prize. 'You should know, you know of our great football team?' he asked me another question. You wonder, ask a person a question, they respond with a question and you cut through the bull with another question. 'Football team? After this season, I don't think N.Y.U had a football team.' I joked with him, his friends laughed as he looked around as he grimaced a hard look to them they let their smiles disappear. 'Are these your bitches?' I asked him. 'You could say that.' he told me, I knew that could have forced one of them, if they had the balls to just punch him in the mouth and make him lower than what I already found him as. 'Well, you see I'm new here and you see, I couldn't help but notice that you happen to look like somebody I know...are you Howard Stern?' I asked him, once again feeding the comedy right down their throats and making his cronies laugh. 'Grr.' he said, I knew it was something I saw a pissed off guy on steroids do, I think this was the secret to muscularity. 'Well it was nice meeting you Howard, and you too Ms. ?' I looked at Gavin while mentioning Amy, I knew it was going to be funny if she didn't tell me and hid her emotions. 'Falan. With a long a.' she told me. A smile crept over my face as she told me this although I could help but notice the fact that Gavin happened to be a total pushover when it came to intelligence. Just like Jeremy Howard, trying to be head strong combat type trying to get his son back. Just like Duke Williams before him, Miyagawa saved his ass. Yeah, that was my past for the second part... CVIII: The Mall Harper's Mall was packed full of people walking around on a Thursday afternoon, many were getting different outfits, others jewelry. But it was different with me, sitting down enjoying me McDonald's fries, a King Burger from Burger King, and a soda from Sprite, someting I'd not take from Snapple, for sometimes it has its moments in the sun, and sometimes it doesn't. I guess when you do things to leave an impact, you actually leave a blueprint for people to follow. History, it's something not in our DNA, but learned...
