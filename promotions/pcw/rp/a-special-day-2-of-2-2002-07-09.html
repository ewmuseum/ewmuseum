A Special Day (2 of 2)

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-9-02 Time: 10am Friday-=[We find Shannon on the phone in the living room early in the morning]=- Shannon: Yes Carolyn, if you want to give the ticket away then go ahead and do it…Don’t worry about me, I’ll just be here waiting for the satellite guy to get here… -=[Thug roC walks in with something under his shoulder]=- Shannon: Hey hunnie. Thug roC: Hey Shannon: Where are you going so fast? Thug roC: I got to go to the bathroom Shannon: What’s under your arm? Thug roC: I told you I got go to the bathroom Shannon: [Back on the phone]. See I told you Carolyn, I just saw him walk in with a Tiffany’s box…He ran upstairs to hide it….I’m goin up there to find it…bye! -=[Shannon hangs up the phone and jets up the stairs. She is seen entering the master bedroom where she peeks around the corner to make sure that nobody is in the room. She enters silently and sees Thug roC’s jacket on the chair. She hurries to check it but finds that it’s already empty. She then goes to the dresser and looks under all of Thug roC’s clothes, but still finds nothing. She hurries over to the bed and starts looking between the Michaelresses when she hears Thug roC coming out of the bathroom. She then jumps on the bed and acts like she has been laying there the whole time. Thug roC comes out the bathroom while brushing his teeth]=- Thug roC: Hey hunnie, what are you doing up here? Shannon: Just relaxing Thug roC: Oh ok. I guess there’s nothing suspicious about that. -=[Thug roC turns and goes back into the bathroom leaving Shannon to start searching even more. She gets down and looks under the bed. She seems to have discovered something when Thug roC is coming back out the bathroom. Shannon acts like she’s exercising this time]=- Thug roC: What are you doing this time? Shannon: Push ups… Thug roC: I thought you were relaxing. Shannon: I got tired of relaxing, I’m doing push ups now… -=[Thug roC then places his foot and forces Shannon down farther]=- Thug roC: Well if you’re going to do them you got to go all the way down. Shannon: Urgh. Thug roC: Well you know what, can you stop that for right now? Shannon: Ok, but why? Thug roC: I got something for you. Shannon: You do! Thug roC: Yea, I knew you thought I’d forget about it, but I didn’t Shannon: See, I was telling Carolyn that you wouldn’t forget. She wouldn’t believe me, but I had faith in you. -=[Thug roC goes on the other side of the bed while Shannon rambles on not noticing what he’s getting]=- Shannon: You know what, I want it to be a surprise. I’m closing my eyes now! Thug roC: Umm…if ya want. Shannon: I knew you’d come through for me Terrel… -=[Shannon puts her hands out to receive her gift as Thug roC puts a full trash bag in her hands]=- Shannon: What’s this? Thug roC: This week’s trash. I know how made you get when I forget to take the trash out, so I made sure to remember this week. -=[Shannon gets upset and storms out of the room]=- Time: 1pm Friday-=[The whole family is sitting at the kitchen table eating lunch. Seems to be a normal moment until Shannon figures that she should get some more information on this Saturday.]=- Shannon: So, who are you golfing with this weekend? Thug roC: Just some buddies of mine. Shannon: Like who? Thug roC: The usual, Machine and Rush. Shannon: What time… Thug roC: [Cutting off] Pass the butter. Shannon: Sure…When are you leaving? Thug roC: Around noon. Pass the bread. Shannon: Yea…Hope you don’t forget anything Thug roC: Oh you’re right Shannon: I am? Thug roC: Yea, I need some salt. It’s over on the counter -=[Thug roC gets up and walks over to the counter as the kids all start giggling at their mom. Shannon get up and sort of follows Thug roC as he gets up]=- Shannon: No Terrel, I mean like something else. Something really important *hint hint* Thug roC: Something important? Shannon: Yea, about Saturday Thug roC: Oh Shannon! How could I had forgotten?! Shannon: yea… -=[Thug roC Suddenly hugs Shannon]=- Thug roC: The fight is Saturday! Shannon: … Thug roC: Thank you for reminding me! I didn’t even order it. With all these satellite thoughts, I completely forgot. I’m going to order it right now. -=[Thug roC rushes out of the room]=- Shannon: [Under her breath]. I’m gonna order you a can of whoop ass in a second… Time: 10am Saturday-=[We find Shannon just coming out of the shower. She is now dressed and wonders where all the kids are. She goes downstairs and its empty there too. She then opens the fridge and gets a can of Sunny D to drink. She guzzles it down while going outside to water the plants. She opens the back door and…. SURPRISE! -=[Come to find out that this was all a plan. The whole family is out back all dressed up. Chairs are out along with a minister standing and waiting. Shannon has no clue on what’s going on. Thug roC steps up to explain]=- Thug roC: Well Shannon, to stop you from bickering, bitching, complaining and all the other stuff about forgetting our Special Day, I went ahead and planned for our vows renewed. Shannon: Terrel you shouldn’t have! Thug roC: It’s the least I can do. So go on up stairs so we can get this party started. -=[Shannon, along with some of her friends run back in the house to get dressed. We see Thug roC’s bodyguard, Machine is also there]=- Machine: Aight RoC, what’s the real story? Thug roC: I found my old calendar last night and it happened to have this day circled and I had no clue what for. So I went ahead and guessed that it was an Special Day. Machine: How did you know it wasn’t her birthday? Thug roC: …. Machine: You are positive that today is your Special Day and not her birthday aren’t you? Thug roC: … Machine: OMG -=[We then go upstairs in the house where Shannon and her friends are talking]=- Carolyn: I meant to ask you, what exactly is so special about today anyways? I asked your no good husband, but he didn’t feel like telling me. Shannon: Today’s not special. Every now and then I go into his office with a red marker and circle a random day on his calendar. That way he thinks he forgot something and has to buy me something big. I was hoping for a flatscreen TV or something. But I guess this will do. Carolyn: O Ok -=[Scene fades to black]=-
