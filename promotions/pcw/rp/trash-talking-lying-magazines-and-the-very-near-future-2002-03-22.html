Trash talking, Lying Magazines, and the very near Future!

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/03/02 Trash talking, Lying Magazines, and the very near Future! The scene opens up in the lounge of The Palace, Anaheim, California…As expected we find our hero and resident Egyptian God The Pharaoh, his mentor Horus, and his beautiful wife Isiy sitting talking. Pharaoh bears a harsh frown upon his brow and we can probably guess why…there was obviously failure in Horus’ “great” plan to hold up the restaurant that he so stupidly proposed. Isiy sits with a frown too, but hers is nowhere near as angry looking as Titu’s. Horus on the other hand sits smiling looking over a magazine in his hands. The Pharaoh: Nice idea of yours Horus! It almost got us damn well arrested! Pharaoh’s words are filled with livid fury. Horus calmly lifts his head up from reading the magazine in his hands and smiles. Horus: The point is, we didn’t… The Pharaoh: And what if we would have done? We could have gone down for a long time. Pharaoh sighs and shakes his head to and fro. The Egyptian God then reaches behind him and produces one of the guns that we saw in the last promo. The Pharaoh: This! Pharaoh pauses and with amazing Godly strength snaps the gun in half. Four small silvery bullets hit the floor with echoing thuds. The Pharaoh: (continuing) Will not do! We are not terrorist chefs Horus… Pharaoh voice begins to calm a little… The Pharaoh: And besides I have a lot more to think about than your CRAZY ideas… Horus: I see… I now see the error of my ways, Titu…I am sorry… The Pharaoh: It is quite all right, my old friend and Godly mentor. Horus smiles. Horus: In future your training will be a little less silly, and a lot more intelligible… The Pharaoh: Training in the gym, mat wrestling, new Godly powers, I expect? Horus: Indeed, Titu! The Pharaoh: Excellent… Horus gets up from his seat on the couch and walks towards Pharaoh; still holding the magazine tightly in his hand. Horus: There is something in here about you! Pharaoh looks on in puzzled. The Pharaoh: There is? Horus: Ah yes, it speaks of you and your feud with Akiyama, well the beginning of it, that is… Pharaoh snatches the magazine away from his mentor’s hands and begins to read through the article Horus speaks of. At length Pharaoh speaks. The Pharaoh: Poppycock! It seems that I am the underdog going into Spring Fling! Or so it says here! Horus sighs. Horus: Yes, it does, the magazine clearly states that… The Pharaoh: Then it seems as though things need to be said and done…and I think the perfect time to do so is this Sunday on Sabotage! Horus smiles broodingly. Horus: What are you going to address? The Pharaoh: Everything! Including the fact that I am not the underdog in this match! I am clearly the one favoured to win the TV title at the Pay-Per-View, yet this simplistic Mortal magazine states differently! When will the media learn that they are both un-liked and un-respected? Horus: Never? The Pharaoh: Exactly! Pharaoh continues to read through the page. The Pharaoh: And here! It clearly states that they believe Ben J. Pailthorpe will manage to defeat me this coming Friday on Fusion to claim the number one contenders spot to the TV title! How dare they put out such an idiotic lie! Is it not plain to see that this newcomer from GWF is nothing in comparison to my brilliance? Once again Pharaoh sighs. The Pharaoh: I simply cannot believe anything that these foolish Mortals have written in their publishing. MORTAL FOOLS! Horus begins to laugh. Horus: Then perhaps you need to prove to the world that you can take any opposition that is thrown at you… The Pharaoh: Oh don’t worry… I plan to! Pharaoh grins egotistically. The Pharaoh: And it all starts this Friday on PCW fusion! Horus: I’ll take it that you are confident of winning your match then? Pharaoh looks at the great Egyptian God of the sky sarcastically. The Pharaoh: Win? By my Godly name, Horus, I shall dominate! I am destined to pick up this victor on Fusion and then go on to claim the TV title as a prize for my hard work at spring fling! Things are looking up for the Jewel and nobody is going to tell me otherwise. Pharaoh smirks proudly. The Pharaoh: Just you wait! All of you shall see, I promise! Big Davelicious, Ben J. Pailthorpe, Bobby Stormbringer? You say their names now with so much pleasure but come Saturday morning the world shall see all three of these Mortal idiots in a different light; for they will have been defeated like so many others by the greatest of all the Egyptian Gods- Horus frowns. The Pharaoh: Well perhaps not as great as you, but you get the point! I shall defeat them all, and then? Pharaoh pauses and smiles evilly. The Pharaoh: (continuing) Then Akiyama will have his turn to go one on one with Pharaoh pauses… The Pharaoh: THE PHARAOH! The scene fades on the face of Titu Xansiv, still a devilish grin is present on his lips.
