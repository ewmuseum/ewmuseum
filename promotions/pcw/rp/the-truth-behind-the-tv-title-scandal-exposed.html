Peace….Tranquillity….Comfort… This is the lif…… Jenna: AAAAAAAAAYMIIIIIIIIIIIIIN!!!!!!!!!! [Jenna’s shrieking voice penetrates the calm, soothing atmosphere. [Jenna starts waving her hands around wildly and pointing her finger in Flaymin’s face] Jenna: Oh noooo!!Oh don’t tell me you just said that! Flaymin: Would you like to say it again? Jenna: I want some damn toilet paper. Flaymin: Sheesh. [Flaymin looks around his recliner for some paper but only manages to find a PCW Magazine. He hands the magazine to Jenna] Flaymin: Here, wipe your ass with this.Preferably on the page with the El Diablo photo on it. [Jenna takes the magazine and gives Flaymin a nasty stare. She flips through the magazine and suddenly comes to a halt at one particular page] Jenna: This is odd. Flaymin: Yeah I can’t believe your reading that crap either. Flaymin: Hold on a sec, I held it for 40 days too. Jenna: That’s what I was thinking. [Flaymin reaches into the side of the recliner and whips out his trusty Canon F-402 Scientific Statistical Calculator. [Jenna begins to dial the phone number into the calculator. She holds the calculator to her ear for almost a minute] Jenna: Dammit, no answer! Flaymin: They must know we’re on to them! We have them cornered now!! [Jenna throws the calculator over her shoulder] Jenna: Forget the phone, let’s go there and protest in person! [Flaymin quickly drinks his Chardonnay and gets up off his chair] Flaymin: I’ll go start the car.You go and put some clothes on!
