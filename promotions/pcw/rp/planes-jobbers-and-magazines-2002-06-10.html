Planes, Jobbers and Magazines.

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th June 2002 A hard day at the office is what confronts some people when they go to work. Maybe a slog down the mines if you lived thirty years ago and in Yorkshire; but alas not the people of today. The paperless office is the way of the future, but we now use three times more paper through printouts than we did when that was first said. A computer the size of a room that once decoded a German War Code could now be happily sitting in the palm of your very hand. The moral of the story? Things always change. Some for the good, some for the bad, but life in itself is ever changing, expect for the single fact. Change will never change because it will always be change. But some things never change, and that is what we try to explain here, so I hope you have your skull screwed on the right way around because there are going to be things so subtle in this that even I won’t recognise them. Yes. This was completely irrelevant. What? I thought “Most Irrelevant Intro” would be a new award... just hopefully it won’t result like the time I started off the “Longest Promo” thing. Anyway, we star off boarding a plane at L.A. Airport, it’s a United Airlines flight and an exhausted Miyagawa rushes onto it, having been late for it already. He flashes his boarding pass at the woman at the gate and gets onto the plane, the woman shutting the gate right behind him. He gets up the little connection corridor just in time as he shows his boarding pass again, and is ushered into business class. He stows his bag in the locker above his head and the stewardess closes it immediately afterwards. He sits himself down and straps himself in as the airplane begins to taxi towards the runway. He breaths a sigh of relief as the stewards go through the standard safety explanations, Miyagawa already knowing them off the top of his head. He reaches into the pocket on the back of the seat in front of him and removes an in flight magazine, palming through it non-chaletly in order to pass the time. He glances over to the person sitting between him and the window and is surprised to find out who it is. Scott Hall looks back towards him unhappily. Probably because he hadn’t had a drink yet. Maybe because he blew his last chance at a decent job with large pay packets. Or then again it could just be because that little tassel in his hair at the front looks really stupid. Probably the third one, because I know that would make me upset. Hall: Whatcha looking at? Miyagawa snorts in retort and goes back to reading the magazine... or rather, looking at it again since they only replace the things once a month and Miyagawa flies about the country about twice a week. Hall: No, seriously, whatcha looking at? It’s the silly tassel of hair at the front of my head isn’t it? It’s always the tassel... damn kids teasing me... Perfect taking the piss... and now look at me? LOOK AT ME! Miyagawa glances over to Hall from the magazine. Hall: DON’T LOOK AT ME! Miyagawa shakes his head and glances away as Hall covers his face with his hands trying to hide from everyone who was looking at him... which was actually just Miyagawa but never mind, eh Scott? Miyagawa (not looking up from the Magazine at him): I just have to ask you one question... why did you do it? Hall: What? Start drinking again? Miyagawa: No, why did you cut off Michael Hayes’ mullet. Hall: Have you ever cut off a man’s mullet? It’s great fun, here, watch. Hall pulls out a pair of scissors from a pocket and reaches over the seat in front of him and grabs a woman by the hair and cuts a huge lump out of it. The woman screams and turns around horrified as the steward’s rush towards Hall when they see he has a pair of scissors. Steward: He’s armed! Hall: Whatcha talking about? This is a pair of scissors. Suddenly a plain clothes security guard gets up on the other side of the plane and pulls out a gun aiming it at Scott Hall. Guard: Throw the weapon to the floor and put your hands on your head! Hall: What? But this is a pair of scissors! Miyagawa (still palming through the magazine): Bah, they wouldn’t even let me bring a pair of chopsticks onto a flight from Japan. It’s still that bad... like you could hold up a plane with a pair of scissors or chopsticks. Hall throws the scissors into the aisle and the stewards grab him by the arms and pull him over Miyagawa and start dragging him very roughly down the aisle. Hall: But... You can’t do this! I’m Scott Hall dammit! Steward: You can’t be Scott Hall... you’re sober. Miyagawa shakes his head in disgust as he take’s Hall’s former seat in order to get the window view just after placing the in flight magazine back in it’s little pocket in the back of the chair in front of him. Miyagawa: I might have to use the mullet chopping thing though on Tuesday. That would certainly be amusing. And with that this story ends. But the moral of the story? Things always change, but everyone expects them to stay the same.
