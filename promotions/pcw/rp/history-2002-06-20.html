History...

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/20/02 Three opponents. Three challengers. Not that I have a title, but by far of all the participants in this match I am, by far, the highest up on the ladder. Sure, Jordan X Shabazz and John Crichton are going to wrestle for the Television Title, but dollars to doughnuts that the average PCW fan knows me more than they know some guy I call “Prey” and a black man that is upset that he was looked over for the part of Malcolm X in favor of Denzel Washington. And El Diablo... well, we all know that in his mental state that he’ll be in a mental institution before a title picture. But for now, why don’t you sit back and let me regale you with the history I have with each one of my opponents. First, Jordan X Shabazz. We were supposed to be in El Diablo’s movie together. He kept trying to saying that I’m all funny and personable. Don’t listen to that idiot. I’m not a friendly person. Shabazz would say -- “Can I say something?” End:“No.” He wouldn’t shut up, instead he would still try to get in words -- “Please?” End:“No.” For some reason, Shabazz just doesn’t catch a clue easily; maybe he does drugs or something (apparently Sunshine and or Loc are loaded with the stuff in their bloodstream, they might as well be pushing) -- “Pretty please?” End:“Shush, before I remove your larynx!” I do my best to stay away from him. My partial black heritage seems to be reason enough for this twit to wish to pal around with me. End as Shabazz:“Come on, man! Us Black people have to stick together!” End:“I’m half Japanese, you know that, right?” End as Shabazz:“I can look past that!” I wonder if Shabazz even realizes that the only reason why he hasn’t yet achieved success in PCW is not because of lack of talent, but because he can’t see himself as any more than a black man. He keeps saying that the rest of the world is discriminating against him? Laughable. Jordan discriminates and alienates himself, and the sooner he realizes that, the sooner he’ll climb the ladder. I had a match involving Jordan X Shabazz over in PURE. He was on my team. As I expected, everything else in the world stopped when Ali Khadafi came out to pester him. Jordan left the match to fight Khadafi (and I do mean fight, since the man has not one actual wrestling move in his entire repertoire of moves). All I asked of Shabazz was to stay focused on the match until the ending bell, and he couldn’t even do that. Frankly, I’m surprised the Malice Crew puts so much faith in a man who’s own agenda comes before the team. I’m one to talk about my own agenda, but I’ve never asked to be trusted by anyone, never tried to pal around with anyone to get ahead in the federation. Enough about Shabazz. Anything else I could say would be compliments, and I don’t want to do that. On to John Crichton. We knew each other before PCW. We were together in PWA for about a week before I left. Crichton had already been there for some weeks before I arrived. I had my sight then; it was funny to watch Crichton. You see, back then, he wasn’t quite bright. In fact, he was sometimes seemed slam-drunk, as he slurred his speech the PWA headmasters beat the hell out of him so much. It was interesting to see Crichton. He always carried around about 10 pictures of the ‘John Crichton’ character from Farscape, which he would shove in your face whenever he spoke. He would have them displayed on the PWA screen when he did in-ring promos. Now he seems to have developed and grown a little more out of his old PWA persona, but I’ll never forget that his file photo in PWA was not his own, but that of a conversation between “John Crichton” and “Scorpius.” Meeting Crichton for the first time was an awkward experience. End as Crichton:“Who’re you?” End:“I’m The End, new student.” End as Crichton: *pushing picture up into The End’s face*“I’m John Crichton. I run this school.” End:“No, I’m pretty sure that you’re not the principal.” End as Crichton: *showing another pitcure, covering up view of Crichton himself*“None of that principal stuff, I own this place. I’m the best wrestler in here, and don’t you forget it.” Of course, The HIT regularly tossed Crichton around the ring when he got out of line. Crichton even wore a face-sized picture of “John Crichton” over his own face, he was so caught up in those pictures, which he probably used to hide his own shame in being such a terrible wrestler and overall person. As I said, he showed the pictures as he spoke, and listening to him speak was an ordeal in and of itself, because the greater extent of his speaking ability was the phrase, “Now ain’t that a bitch?” And I mean, after everything. In the cafeteria... Lunchlady:“What’ll ya have?” End as Crichton:“I’ll take some of that meatloaf and peas. Now ain’t that a bitch?” While in class... Headmaster:“You do remember how to properly fall from offensive maneuvers, don’t you?” End as Crichton:“Yeah. Now ain’t that a bitch?” Even talking to his mother... Crichton’s mother:“Are you getting enough sleep, Johnny?” End as Crichton:“Of course, Mom. 8 hours every night. Now ain’t that a bitch?” Of course, after that usage of the phrase directed at his mother, Crichton was pulled into the ring by the ear, spanked, and grounded for a week. He’s never been the same after that public humiliation. But who wouldn’t change or just their whole personality just to shift attention away from the fact that his or her mother literally beat their ass in the ring? And that’s about all I can say for John Crichton. I haven’t kept up with him recently, but what reason is there for me to do so? Finally, El Diablo. The Mexican Devil. He’s like a chihuahua compared to me. Smaller, more sickly, shakes all the time. El Diablo was coming in as principal of the PWA as I was leaving it. I didn’t try to talk to him much, he was crazy even then. Like when he tried to send PWA students to PCW before they were ready, to get them to capture the PCW Title from Stanislav and bring it back to him. End as Diablo:“A sad day in PCW it is when I can’t even get in to a federation that a big dumb Russian rules with an iron fist!” End as Ken Kaze:“A sad day when a Mexican can’t get work as a lower card jobber, indeed.” End as Diablo:“Quiet! For that, your next match in PWA is against Two Ton!” End as Ken Kaze:“Crap!” End as Drake Maxwell:“What does this have to do with us?” End as Diablo:“Plain and simple, I’m sending you all to PCW to bring me Ivan Stanislav’s head and the PCW World Title on a silver platter.” End as Ritchie Syxx:“I’m Ritchie Syxx. Who’s Ivan Stanislav, a Russian butcher?” End as Diablo:“Close, he’s the PCW World Champion and a supporter of communism.” End as Hessian:“Ah, yes. The dark lord wants Ivan. I’ll take his head, alright, but I won’t be delivering it to you, little man.” End as Diablo:“... yes. Well, the Red Army is strong as ever, but I still expect you to get me that belt!” End as Itagaki Freeze:“Don’t you think that’s a little dangerous?” End as Diablo:“Don’t worry, I’m going to have Akiyama kill you.” End as Itagaki Freeze: *shrugs*“Oh, well.” End as Young Tiger:“You realize, El Diablo, that sending these kids to fight Ivan Stanislav is a suicide mission?” End as El Diablo:“That’s it, you’ve earned yourself a ticket to PCW, Ramirez. All of you will bring me the PCW World Title or die trying! You hear that Crichton, die!” El Diablo also aspired to a film career at this point, but for some reason the only videos he ever shot involved hiding cameras in the women’s locker room. Pervert. Well, that’s about it for my history with these characters. There’s probably more that I’m forgetting, but I’m not worrying so much about the past. I want to ignore them and look to the future. Perhaps you’ve learned a little more about my opponents. If so, then that is all that I hoped for in speaking with you. Good day.
