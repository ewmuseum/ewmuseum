A New Ball Field

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/01/03 We open up to a table with screws and bolts scattered all around, and pieces of metal in a big pile near the end of the table. Two soft looking hands suddenly come into the picture, with fingernails emblazoned in a dark red coloring. The camera cuts back away from the table slowly revealing a female with reddish brown curls rolling down her face, wearing a simple black shirt and black pants which fits perfectly against her slim, toned body. She reaches into her right pocket and pulls out a shiny flathead screwdriver. She looks down at all of the odds and ends and picks up a screw and two pieces of metal. 'This is going to be a piece of cake.' she says lightly as she sticks the screw into the hole of one of the metal plates. She takes the second plate and pulls it up. As she does this the screen goes black for several seconds and then returns. The room, which was only illuminated by a small light earlier, is now completely lit up revealing a light pink carpet and white walls. The camera zooms back over to the table which now has a big bulge in the center covered by a black cloth. In the distance we hear muttering as two voices get closer and closer. The door on the far right opens up with a bit of a squeak as the woman reenters with a tall slender man by her side. 'Thomas I promise you will like this.' the woman exclaims excitedly as she rushes over to the table. 'Listen hun, I'm sure it's nothing less than perfect, but I doubt I can really help you out.' Thomas replies as he lets out a huff in his blue suit following the woman reluctantly to the table. 'Just look at it. That's all I'm asking.' With that she pulls the black cloth off of the table to reveal a large shiny square shaped metal box. 'Wow it's a box.' he says sarcastically. Instead of replying to that uncalled-for comment, the woman presses a big black button on the side and steam instantly shoots out from all sides causing the man to jump back in surprise, getting his suit all wrinkled up. As the steam starts to die down the top of the metal box lifts up with two long rods holding the top up. Then the top of the box starts to spread out even further creating what looks like wings on the sides. A light buzzing sound can be heard as the box starts to violently shake before bursting into flames. The flames are small, but Thomas still runs over to the fire extinguisher and puts it out spraying white foam all over the place. The fire is finally depleted, and during the process Thomas somehow got some foam on him, which makes him very irate. 'Dammit! This is a brand new suit!' he said as he brushed away at it feverishly trying to prevent a stain. 'I'm sorry. I thought I really had it this time Thomas.' the woman says sympathetically as she walks over towards him. 'Brianna you have said that several times! Now look, you're a bright girl and everything, but sometimes I think you overestimate yourself. Just stick to the simple things like toys for kids...' 'NO! How am I supposed to get better if I don't expand my creativity? I will get this Thomas, and when it goes on the market and flies off the shelves, then I will happily rub it in your face.' Brianna walks over towards the table and leans against it for support. She places her right hand up at her forehead looking down with tears threatening to overtake her. 'You do that hun.' With that he leaves the room and shuts the door on his way out. Brianna, in a fit of anger, lifts up what is left of the metal box, and throws it against the wall leaving a huge dent. Then she just drops to the floor and keeps her hands over her face as she begins to cry due to major frustration. *Ring...ring...ring* The phone begins to ring, which forces Brianna to sit up and stifle the tears. She walks out of the room and enters a hallway, which too has a pink carpet and white walls. The camera zooms down towards a small table and sees the cordless phone jiggling as it continues to ring. Brianna runs to it and picks it up just before the noise stops. 'Hello?' she says while sniffling and wiping the water off of her face. The conversation continues for several minutes and the whole time Brianna's frown is transformed into a huge smile. Obviously she likes what she's hearing. 'You're joking right? You mean I'm in? I made it?!' Brianna hangs up the phone and there is a second of silence as the news all sinks into her head. Then she starts leaping around like a madman laughing and smiling up a storm. Overcome by excitement, she stops to catch her breath before running down the steps in the middle of the hallway. Brianna takes a right to a room, which has a 'Keep Out' sign hung crookedly on the door. 'Milo! Milo I have some great news!' She bangs on the door. The door slowly creaks open and an averaged sized man stands there in his boxers with his dark brown hair ruffled all over his head. 'Do you mind, I was sleeping!' he retorts with a sullen look on his face. 'First of all it's two in the afternoon so you should be up and enjoying this beautiful day. Second....(long pause) I was accepted into PCW!' she waits for a happy response and then the man starts jumping up and down with Brianna. 'Big sweaty men putting you in holds, girl you're living my dream!' Milo says as he walks with Brianna to the living room, after reaching into his room and sliding on a pair of pants and shirt. They sit next to each other on a light pink couch. 'Now since I made it in PCW my life is going to change! I will be making lots of money and I will be able to start on new projects. And I can afford better parts and then I can sell the stuff I make to huge corporations.....' Brianna goes on and on and Milo sits idly by taking in every word. He then cuts her off to throw in his own views. 'I'm really happy for you Bri, but you're my best friend and I don't want to see you get hurt. Granted, you have been training and stuff for a year now, but I don't think you are ready for the type of competition PCW is going to throw at you.' With that Brianna stands up in a fit of rage and starts to walk away before Milo stands up and grabs her arm, which she quickly pulls away. 'How dare you not support me Milo? I thought you of all people would be there to push me along and help me fulfill my dream. Instead you assume that I am too precious of cargo to go in there with a bunch of muscle heads. Let me tell you something pal, I may not seem like much on the outside, but when I step in that ring nothing seems to phase me. I turn into a completely different person, a stronger person.' Brianna pelts out with clenched teeth. 'It's not that I don't support you Bri....' he tries to apologize but is cut off. 'You just don't think I can handle it. I was there when you came out of the closet right? I was there to help you through the tough transaction, and I accepted you for who you are. Now all I am asking is for you to accept me for who I am. I am a fighter Milo, a wrestler! Instead you treat me like that jackass Thomas who doubts that I have the ability to create anything but toys for kids!' Again her eyes are flooded with tears as she stares at her longtime friend. 'Don't you compare me to him Bri! I am nothing like him. I am just looking out for your well being.' Milo continues to plee his case. 'Worry about me all you want Milo, but I am a professional wrestler, and all of my hard work and training is finally coming to play.' She walks away and exits out the front door, leaving a very sorry looking Milo standing there without a motion in sight. The scene slowly fades to black.
