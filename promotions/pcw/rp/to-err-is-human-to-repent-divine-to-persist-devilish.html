Date: not entered Cyclonica: Darn it…if I hear Duke say Akiyama was exhaled from Japan one more time…Exhale means to breathe air out…not to cast out!!! Exhaled must be his vocabulary word of the week! =©= Welcome back…we’re standing now back in the same mall as before…in the same city…as before…in front of the same fountain…as before…D@mn, repetition is contagious, isn’t it? Thanks a lot, Duke. =©= =©=Akiyama and Cyclonica stood in front of the fountain looking around the area. Over Akiyama’s shoulder was a sack, which was in fact thrashing around as if someone was inside of it. Good thing we know better now, don’t we? =©= Cyclonica: We’re back…at the mall. This is the site where we shall unveil our plan. This is where we shall unveil… =©= She looked to her left and right before looking at Akiyama. =©= Cyclonica: Project Malice! Voice: Let me out of here!! Why’d you knock me out!? =©=Akiyama punched the sack, drawing out a whimper and a little less movement. Cyclonica continued on with her explanation like the guy in the sack wasn’t there. =©= Cyclonica: You see…there’s one thing about Duke that makes him stand out from those other guys…he thinks it’s talent, but that’s a load of Malice Crap. No… it’s the beard. The unshaven features of the fool makes him look rugged in an otherwise trimmed society. So therefore to harness the rugidity of Williams…we shall harness the power of…the beard! AKIYAMA! UNLEASH PROJECT MALICE. =©=Akiyama swung the sack from his shoulder and dumped the contents out onto the floor. Out tumbled Seibzhen…dressed in a cowboy hat, black jeans, black boots, and a black shirt that said “MALICE MAN” with an arrow pointing up at his face. Speaking of his face…it was covered in black hair. He looked around frantically at his surroundings. =©= Seibzhen: Where am I?? What am I doing here?? =©=He reached up and found himself a surprise…=©= Seibzhen: Why is there a beard on my face??? Cyclonica: Quit squawking and listen to the plan, Baka. We’ve done some alterations to your face to make you look like Duke Williams…hence the new beard fully grown on your face. Seibzhen: Where’d this come from anyway? Cyclonica: Well… Seibzhen: Wait a second…where’s my dog? Where’s Snowy??? Cyclonica: Don’t jump ahead of the story! While we were preparing to fit you with the beard, your dog fell into the black paint. Seibzhen: Snowy had an accident with the black paint??? Cyclonica: Accident?? No, that was planned out. After he was dipped black, we shaved him and glued the hair to your face. Notice the sheen of your newfound facial hair…and those fleas and ticks…authentic Malice Man. Seibzhen: SNOWWYYYYY!!!! =©=Cyclonica booted the man in his chest, causing his head to bounce back against the ground. =©= Cyclonica: Stop sniveling and do what I say. =©=He stood up and glumly stood before the Divine Prophetess. She looked him over and nodded slowly. =©= Cyclonica: Yep…you look raunchy enough…this is what you’re going to do. You go wandering into the mall…see how people react to you. We’ll observe from a distance…got it? =©=He nodded slowly, in which Cyclonica jabbed him in his pec with her fingernail. =©= Cyclonica: Do you understand me, BAKA? Seibzhen/Baka: Yes… =©=He walked off slowly as the two Celestials watched him. Akiyama sat on the edge of the fountain and glowered at the Duke look alike. Cyclonica shrugged her shoulders at the god when he looked at her. =©= Cyclonica: Don’t give me that look…I know this is a little unorthodox…even for us. But this is Duke Williams…half-assed job for a half-assed jackass. =©= The two gave follow to Baka (formerly known as Seibzhen) down the alleyway. He walked awkwardly amongst the crowd, getting huge stares from the people, mouths agape and everything. A trio of girls in blue and white school outfits sat gabbing amongst themselves about their usual teenage shenanigans…that is, until Baka crossed their paths. They all stopped and stared as the Baka/Duke strutted past them. He tipped his hat to the girls. =©= Baka: Hossettes… =©= As he passed, their eyes followed…mouths still gaped open. =©= Girl A: That’s one Malice-Motha--- Girl B and C: SHUT YOUR MOUTH!!! =©=Girl A shrugged at their reprimanding. =©= Girl: A: I’m just talking about Duke Williams! Girl B and C: We can dig it! =©=Cyclonica and Akiyama watched as Baka began getting into the part, his strut getting more wild and his imitation of a redneck getting more and more crude. He even stopped to grab a bag of tobacco out of a shop so he could spit on the floor. The people were whispering amongst themselves, pointing and saying, “It’s the Malice Man!” and things just as similar. Walking past Victoria’s Secret, he attracted a group of rather…unpleasant looking women dressed in tight attire. =©= Woman: Hiya Duke! Baka/ Duke: Hey there, hot mama! What can the Malice Man do for you? Woman: Well…first you can cough up the money you owe me for that blowjob I gave you! Black Woman: And you can also pay the clinic bills for the Crabs you gave me!! Baka/Duke: What? Huh? No...wait..there’s a misunderstanding here… Latin Woman: Nothing misunderstood about the fire you started in my house when you fell asleep while smoking weed and fell into the whiskey!!!! =©= As the three prostitute-type females surrounded Baka with accusations of actions committed by the real Duke Williams, two mall cops stood on post eyeing the whole situation. The taller one looked down at his deputy with a nod. =©= Tall Cop: Do you see what I see, rookie? Short Cop: I think so…but that can’t be him. He’s too small to be him. The papers said he was a bigger guy. Tall Cop: Look here, son…when you become chief of Mall Police for countless bravery in the face of runaway shopping carts and keeping the Goths in order as they terrorize the mall…THEN you can tell me if or if that is NOT HIM!! Short Cop: Goths? They just stand in front of the arcade and brood! How’s that cause for danger?? Tall Cop: DON’T YOU TALK BACK TO A COMMANDING OFFICER! DO YOU COPY ME ROOKIE??? Short Cop: Yeah…all right…don’t pop a blood vessel, pops. Tall Cop: THEN ROGER ME BACK ON THE RADIO WHEN YOU COPY!!! =©= The shorter, much younger cop sighed as he pulled his walkie talkie from his belt and switched it on with a squawk. =©= Short Cop: ROGER, COPY, OVER!! Tall Cop: Now…let’s go get our MAN!! =©= The two cops approached through the forming crowd and separated the hookers from Baka the Malice Man. Baka smiled as he breathed a sigh of relief. =©= Baka: Thank you, kind sirs. I don’t know what came over those women. Tall Cop: Duke Williams…you’re under arrest for usage of Marijuana. Baka: WHAT??? I’m not Duke Williams!! Tall Cop: Do you take me for a fool?? You look like Duke with that grimy beard…talk like Duke… =©= Leaning in, his nose wrinkled as he frowned down at him. =©= Tall Cop: And according to the scent of bad pot and cheap whiskey…you even smell like Duke!! =©= Baka whipped his head around towards Cyclonica and Akiyama. The Divine Prophetess was looking away innocently as she stuffed a paper bag into a nearby trash receptacle. He pointed to them accusingly, trying to save his own neck while nailing the real perpetrators. =©= Baka: It was THEM!!! THEY MADE me look like Duke Williams!!! =©=The two cops looked up ahead the walkway to find Akiyama and Cyclonica standing there…with an entourage of Gothic teens lurking around behind them. The Tall Cop rubbed his chin as he glared at the black-dressed group. =©= Tall Cop: So Goths…we meet…AGAIN!! Short Cop: Geez… =©=The Tall Cop approached the litter of them, staring up at the massive Akiyama and trying to be as authoritative as possible. =©= Tall Cop: Excuse me, son…but do you know that man right there? =©= Akiyama looked over at Cyclonica before looking down at Baka. Then silently, he shook his head. Cyclonica smirked as Baka’s face dropped. =©= Cyclonica: Akiyama…there’s no use lying to the officer. We know that man…no FRICKIN doubt about it. =©= Baka’s face lit up as Cyclonica nodded to the officer. =©= Tall Cop: You do?? Cyclonica: Yep…that’s Duke “Malice Man” Williams! He tried to get us to hold his weed and whiskey while he talked to those whores, but we said “No way! That stuff’s not cool!!” Goths: Yeah!!! Tall Cop: Let’s go, buddy!!! =©=The two cops grabbed Baka and began to usher him out of the area. Of course…he resisted…and of course…he got sprayed down with mace like he was a fly on the wall. Cyclonica nodded approvingly as they began pummeling him with their nightclubs to subdue him. =©= Cyclonica: Ah…nothing better than the warm feeling of watching police brutality. But you know what’s even better? Watching the n-V-g totally make a bigger fool out of an already extreme fool! And that’s just the way it’s going to be! Duke Williams has no chance at all, no matter how many women he screws, no matter how much whiskey he drinks, no matter how much grass he blazes, he will never truly be….CELESTIAL! And that means he will never be enough to even skin the knees of the Neo Void God!! =©= She slapped Akiyama on his chest, causing his eyes to flash with a hint of rage. She glared up at him with her own black eyes matching his. =©= Cyclonica: Akiyama!! Fusion…Show Williams what Malice really is…make him feel the hatred that has been cast upon you as well as the wickedness you hold for others …by making him scream…in THE DARKNESS…Duke…he’s nothing but a pebble in the temple of greatness we’re going to build to your name!! His blood will be mixed with his bones to lay out the foundation for your rise above GODHOOD!! =©=The Goths behind them all agreed with Cyclonica’s speech with their own low comments and nods. Akiyama looked back at the group from before with his dark eyes wide and hollow. Cyclonica pointed a finger at them, her own eyes not looking very friendly. =©= Cyclonica: I thought I told you pansies to STOP FOLLOWING US! =©=They knew the drill…they fled, Akiyama chased…they tripped, Akiyama beat them within an inch of their gothic dark lord loving lives…just like High School, huh? Cyclonica looked back as they dragged away a bloody Baka through a crowd of mall shoppers. She nodded at her surroundings and sighed in relief. =©= Cyclonica: Yes sir…you can’t get good carnage like nowadays…as they say… “To Err is Human…to Repent, Divine…and to Persist…DEVILISH. It is because of humanity’s foolish errors…that they are indeed the DEVILS. SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS.” =©=
