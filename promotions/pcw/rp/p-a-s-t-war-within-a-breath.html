P a s t / War Within A Breath

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today The past… It comes back to haunt us too many times. Remember the time when you tripped and fell in the lunchroom in high school? You fell face first into your mashed potatoes and corn. Everyone laughed at you…and from then on you were a klutz. Or how about the time when you were out on your first date, and when you came back from the bathroom she was gone? Didn’t even put in on the dinner either…cheap b*tch. Here’s a nice one…how bout when you were at the prom, and when you returned to your table with the punch she asked for, she was off dancing with one of the jocks? Bet that one had to hurt, didn’t it? Then as you got older…it got worse… Falling in your lunch became screwing up on the job…which led to you losing your job. Just because a guy drops a few tons of bricks on someone’s head they think you’re not careful…sissies. Your date leaving you to pay for lunch turns for the worse….she leaves you, that hasn’t changed…but she’s left with your wallet, and your car keys…and your clothes. And it was your idea to go to a hotel…dumbass. And how about finding your date dancing with someone else? That technically doesn’t change… she’s still dancing with someone else… it just hurts harder when you walk into your bedroom to find your wife doing the mattress waltz with the cable guy. It hurts…probably more than any pain you can feel in your body. Emotional scars don’t heal as fast… I heard some whore say that recently… But physical scars…they sure make pretty artwork. But the past…it’s still there…haunting you…haunting your mind like ghosts of memory. You can’t get rid of them…you never will. That’s the great thing about knowing how to inflict emotional scars…they’re the gift that keeps on giving. Anarchy…there is something about her past that is most definitely coming back to catch her right in the ass. It could be my boot…it could be something else of mines… I haven’t decided yet. But what she needs to know is that our battle…it’s not about some belt…it’s not about who wins or loses… It’s about what you learn during the match. I want to know if our fun together in that freezer was good for you as it was good for me. I want to feel your mind…feel what you felt when you had all the filthy human blood drain from your body. I want to know how you felt when you felt that new Divine Blood pump inside of you. I want to know if you shivered…I want to know if you felt goose bumps. I want to know if it made you w e t… It’s a divine feeling…I know for a fact. This darkness flows through me all day every day. The god was busy at the moment…throwing something around the alley where I stood thinking. I left him to his fun while I concentrated harder. Those Japanese backstabbers wont let us back into the country…and we need to get there so the god can destroy the rest of those PCW flat-scans in the main event. I need to get there so I can get the Savage Tranquility. That book is crucial to the outcome of everything past, present and future… Heh…past… There it goes again. Only the god is powerful enough to have to deal with all these men in one night. Aside from those children in the main event, Akiyama had to instill the fear of god into both the old thorn and the new thorn. Inphino Blitz and Pharaoh… Inphino Blitz just didn’t know when to curl up and die…much like the thing Akiyama threw around was trying to do now. Unfortunately for them, curling only exposes your spine easier…makes it quicker to paralyze. That seemed like a perfect punishment for Blitz… I can imagine the despair running through his mind that he cannot move his body…he can never train his precious martial artist frame again. Reduced to being spoon-fed and diaper changed by that neo-goth Mina de Margo. Mark my words…either at this show in Japan…or in the near future…Inphino Blitz will meet his Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. But Pharaoh…I just couldn’t understand how they’d allow this man into Japan and not the nVg. Pharaoh was more a failure than the god…first, this man isn’t a god; hasn’t been since Akiyama beat it out of him the first time they met. Second, he’s not a prince of Egypt. He’s no Moses, he’s no Ramses, and he’s no Tut! He’s just some dumbass delusional mortal that thinks he’s better than his own kind! He has nothing to back himself up with. At least the god has actual GODHOOD establishing his superiority. He has the moniker…PHARAOH. Oooh…after hearing the name, I have the sudden urge to exalt his name. Pharaoh should be honored though…the god holds him high among the other mortals. Maybe not as high as the body Akiyama just heaved into the air now…but pretty frickin high if you ask me. Pharaoh is considered the Neo Inphino Blitz in the eyes of the god. And Inphino is considered THE main enemy of the nVg. That’s an honor, Titu. Go get a plaque made and put it on your wall. “#1 ENEMY OF AKIYAMA.” Tell your friends. It’s a grand accomplishment. But Pharaoh…he knows he’s going to fall. He always falls. No matter how many times he tries to step against the god with his peon-like skills and his hopeless determination, the god will drive him into the earth…a lot harder than the guy that got dumped headfirst into the side of the trashcan just now. Akiyama was just killing time here…no need to put on an exhibition. What do he have to offer anyway? He don’t hold any belts…he don’t hold anything that we’re looking for. All he is…is a wall in the way of our destruction. And he knows for a fact…it takes only one HAND OF FATE to break the walls down. He’s a warm up…a practice match before we move on for the title. Something to get the killing juices flowing. A snack before the main course…that’s what he is. It really makes me sad that this is the preliminary match that Kannon is giving us before the PPV main event. Kannon must want the god to move on…Kannon must want to receive his Absolute Destiny Apocalypse after evading his beating all those times from the nVg. It’s the Universal Reverb there…duck destruction so many times, and inevitably, you’ll find yourself destroyed when you’re not looking. No one sees the ADA coming…no matter how much they try to prepare for it. The god had the man pressed against the wall now, putting his fist to his ribs over and over again. The repetition of his grunts as blood leaked from his mouth made me yearn for my own personal toy. My dear Anarchy has her friend Jenna…I’d like to take a turn on the young girl myself. Maybe after we have our date at Red October, she’ll let me come over and play with her girl. Me…I want a doll of my own. I think Isiy would be a nice candidate for being my torture doll. She meets qualifications: weak, innocent, defenseless, tender, and full of life. It would be ecstatic to hang her from the ceiling and drain all that life out of her slowly… I would swoon from running my hands through her hair and pulling at it, tearing locks from her scalp and hearing her whimpers as I sit on her back. And her mind…her mind is fresh…V I R G I N … that would be a sweet mindf*ck. It’d be intense…tight…probably painful for her, but that was her problem, not mines. If the god beat her lousy husband of hers hard enough…maybe she’d be alone…and then I could take her for my own. And then Anarchy and I could trade dolls…dress them up…play with them…make them bleed. The sound of wheezing caught my attention. I looked over to see the god pressing his foot into the chest of the man. For the first time I took notice as to the identity of the man. He was dressed in a tattered t-shirt and brown jacket with ripped blue jeans and dirty sneakers. His face was caked in grime and littered with hair. He was a nobody… Just another bum… Akiyama should be fighting other people…people like Kannon, Blitz, Pharaoh, Miyagawa…those kinds of people… Wait…what’s the difference? Just like this slob…they’re all nothing but bums. A lot of bums going at it on October 6th…three bums and a god…the odds were never evenly matched in their favor.I snapped my fingers, drawing the dark eyes of the god towards me. I waved for him to walk on over, and he complied. He stood before me, silent and menacing. I patted him on his massive chest and nodded slowly. “We’re going to Japan.” His hands closed into fists as he nodded. I knew he was ready for what I had planned… We were going to war… Our enemy…an entire country… We would beat them into submission just for the opportunity to do so to Pharaoh. When we finished with everything…when everyone who stands in our way have had their lungs punctured by broken ribs caused by punishment…they will find real war within a breath… Each gasp for air becoming the thin line between life and death… Let the war begin…
