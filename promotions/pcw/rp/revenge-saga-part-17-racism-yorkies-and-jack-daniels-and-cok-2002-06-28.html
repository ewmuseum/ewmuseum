Revenge Saga Part 17: Racism, Yorkies and Jack Daniels and Coke...

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 17: Racism, Yorkies and Jack Daniels + Coke… Each and every time he dealt with The Thunderbolts the sight of them always daunted him, even though he was in total control of the situation. The five of The Thunderbolts stood before him, upon the green grass of his weapons testing sight, at the back of the Zemof mansion. The Thunderbolts were all fixed in conversation of they’re own, and do not notice Fleming, as he exits from the security door onto the patio. He took a minute to look upon his troops and had to admit that he was very impressed by them. He knew that with the help of The Thunderbolts nobody would oppose him, much less John Crichton. Truth be told Fleming had no ambition to rule the world, even though aligning The Thunderbolts almost granted him that ability. He wanted the death of John Crichton pure and simple, her had no lust for power, and he was no megalomaniac wanting to take over the world. The Thunderbolt known as ‘Jolt’ is the first to notice Fleming looking down upon them, she nudges the others to make them aware of his presence. Fleming: [Walking down the steps of the porch, down onto the grass] Hello my friends. Atlas: Why have you called us here Fleming? Fleming: I have another business proposition for you. Moonstone: You realise that will cost money… Songbird: …A lot more money than the US Military would grant for official business. Fleming: It’s not official business… Mach 1: No shit sherlock, just tell us the deal, so we can decide. Fleming: [Smiling] Always to the point, hey Mach 1? Mach 1: Well you know me! Fleming: When you saved me from the explosion at the military base, created by our mutual enemy, the infamous John Crichton, you said to contact you if I wanted to do business. Mach 1: Yeah. Fleming: Well I want to do a little business. Moonstone: Well fill us in on the details, like what you want us to do? How much are you going you pay us? How are you going to pay us? Fleming: Well this morning… ************ Crichton: I’m sure! So do I have any chance of scoring? Woman: Maybe…but not with me. Crichton: [Nodding in understanding] I see. Crichton had been in this bar for far too long, hell he knew that because he was trying to chat up random women that serve behind bars. The longer he left the confrontation with Jaden, the more it played upon his mind. He had been thinking of possible arguments he could use to questions, possible things he could say to her and possible things he could call her. He had gone over the events of the previous night in his head too many times to count and he couldn’t see one thing he had done wrong, hell she was the one who burnt pictures of a loved one out of petty jealousy. Regardless of the facts, he very much doubted that he could live with Jaden now, he had become too emotionally attached and he had to save the relationship, because he had no desire to feel the same pain he felt for Aeryn when she was torn from his grasp for the very last time. He had very little J.D. and Coke in his glass, in all rights he should have finished the drink more than fifteen minutes ago, but he was simply dragging it out, busy thinking about his lousy situation. He doesn’t realise it, but the voice of Jordan X Shabazz, his rival, his enemy, his threat, is heard in the background. The noise is quiet at first, but then gets louder and louder, as Crichton looks up from the drink to see the woman from behind the bar playing with the Television remote, watching Shabazz’ latest promo. Woman: Aw god, I hate this guy. Crichton: Who is it? Woman: Jordan X Shabazz, he pisses me right off! Crichton: Me too. Why does he piss you off? Woman: Well he’s a cunt isn’t he. Crichton: [Eyes widening at the realisation of the woman’s latest words] That’s a bit strong isn’t it? Woman: No, I think he’s a racist! Crichton: Really? Woman: Yeah. Crichton: Don’t you just hate it when black people are racist about other cultures, such as Asians for example, even though they have been the brunt of such racism for a number of years. Woman: I believe the correct term John, is ‘African-Americans’. Crichton: Oh, I’m so sorry. Woman: Yes, and don’t you just hate it when ‘African-Americans’ are racist to us ‘Caucasians’ and doing they declare that they are ‘standing up for their rights’ or ‘paying us back’ even though the majority of the country are no longer racist. Crichton: In fact by the year 2010 only 30% of the population of America will be Caucasians, the rest will be a mixture of Hispanic, African-American’s and various other ethnic communities. Woman: Hmmmm, interesting. This Anti-Racism Advertisement was brought to you by Nestle’s Yorkie. ‘No Girls Allowed’. ************ Fleming:…. So what do you think of my proposition? Fleming scanned the faces of all of The Thunderbolts before him, looking for any confused or negative looks, so that he could determine whether any more persuasion was needed. All the faces before him appeared to be utterly emotionless. Well I am dealing professionals now! He declares silently. The Thunderbolts kind of huddle up and begin to discuss the matter. After about fifteen minutes they seem to come to some kind of consensus, as they break from the huddle. Mach 1 one approaches Fleming. Mach 1: We’re not entirely sure we want to be a part of this. Fleming: [Nodding] Well you should realise that as soon as Crichton composes himself further, he will be coming for each and everyone of you. He won’t confront you as a group, he will get to each and everyone of you individually, and then he will kill you. Atlas: [Laughing] He’s one wimpy guy, how could he possibly harm us? Fleming: [Smiling] Before you say anything too brash, you should realise that he has defeated The Avengers before, on a number of occasions. If you work with me, we can all strike him before he is fully ready, before he realises his destructive potential. Plus you will be paid handsomely for your efforts. Mach 1: How handsomely? Fleming: Come and see for yourself Mach 1, the suitcase of money is in my office. Mach 1: [Nods in agreement, as Fleming makes his way over to the security door] The rest of you stay out here. This won’t take long.
