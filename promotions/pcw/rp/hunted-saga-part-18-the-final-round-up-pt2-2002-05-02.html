Hunted Saga Part 18: The final round up pt2

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/5/02 The Hunted Saga Part 18: The final Round Up pt2 The mood was indeed sombre in the car as Crichton and Aeryn drove away from the Tuesday Turmoil arena in Cincinnati, Ohio, there was no particular reason for this mood, it was just how they preferred it. Crichton was in a particularly happy mood, and was thinking about the events of his previous match against Orpheus Grant, Kramer Wild Wing and Karl LeBlanc. He finally got the revenge he had been waiting for, since more than a week prior. Crichton played the events back in his for the hundred time… Crichton then gets into the ring and clotheslines KWW down to the mat. Crichton pulls KWW back to his feet and whips him to the ropes, he hits OG who is standing on the apron and knocks him onto the outside. KWW stumbled back towards Crichton, who puts KWW's head between his legs and lifts him up spins around and piledrives WildWing into mat. Dave: The Winonna. He goes for the pin. ..1 ..2 ..3 Dave: He managed to beat two men. Crichton quickly gets out of the ring as OG rushes in and roars. WINNER: John Crichton and Ben J. Pailthorpe via pinfall. Dave: The Forgotten Ones win. I'm sure Orpheus Grant and Kramer Wildwing won't forget this. In theory he should never have lost to Kramer Wild Wing in the first place and there’s a good chance he wouldn’t have, except for the interventions of Grant and LeBlanc, that was the very reason that revenge on all of those involved was essential to Crichton’s ego. But now the match was over and he began looking forward to his match on Friday Fusion, against rival for the TV title a number one contendership match against Jason Core. If he was actually successful in beating Jason Core he would have another shot at Pharaoh’s TV title. It really got at him that the last match he had with The Pharaoh was inconclusive; there was no winner, a double count-out. Crichton knew that he had a good a chance as anybody and he intended to take the chance. “So Aeryn how far is it to the Oxford from Cincinnati?” He questioned, feeling that near forgotten feeling of excitement. “Just a couple of miles now. How do you think you’ll fair against Jason Core?” Aeryn asks intrigued at the thought of the match. “Well I think that I …” He trailed off as the explosion at the side of the car knocks them off road into forest land. Crichton hastily brings the car back on road and looks through the rear view mirror to find the cause of the explosion. He realised that with his luck and the kind of people, who were already after him, that explosion was no damn accident. What he saw could have been a shock to a normal person, but he’d seen this kind of thing before. It was someone wearing some kind of Hi-Tech battle suit, based on some kind of plane. It was not like the one Ms. Zemoff wore in their last encounter with the rich industrialist, this suit was much more advanced. Crichton had to wonder what other kind of dangerous enemy he was now encountering, he assumed that the government was willing to pay great deal to get him back, so in theory it could be any bounty hunter after him. Crichton managed to swerve the car to the left to avoid another exploding projectile, fired by the person in the suit behind him, but he knew that he could dodge these projectiles forever and he had to act. Without warning, he takes the car off the road to the left of the road and into forest foliage attempting to shake the guy in the suit. Crichton doubted very much that he had got away from the danger, but it was better than staying on road. This way, they would be a much more difficult target. Crichton was doing well; dodging tree’s here and there until upon the horizon, coming closer and closer. Then before Crichton’s very eyes the man grows taller and taller. Crichton rubs his eyes in amazement. “Good god! I know I’m not this drunk, again!” he states, unable to stop crashing in the man’s massive leg and then being embraced by the warmth of unconsciousness. ************ Slowly, ever so slowly, flickering wildly at first, Crichton’s eyes open. He tries to rub his eyes, and realises that he can’t move his arms, he also realises that he can’t move his legs. However, it’s not that he is physically unable to move them, it’s just that he is strapped down to something. His view hadn’t give him much more of a clue of what was happening, he could only see the clear blue sky, but he could also tell that he was moving. That led him to two conclusions; the first was that he now knew that he was right after all, someone was after him. The second was that whoever was after him had been successful in their attempt. The only thing left to do now was figure out who? And also worry whether Aeryn was okay or not. He figured that the guy in the advanced plane suit and the guy that grew before his eyes to over sixty feet were working together that was without question, but who were they working for? If they were indeed super hero’s as Crichton suspected they were loyal to the government, so therefore he would be going back to be experimented upon, what he had been fighting to resist for the best part of three months. On the bright side, if they were superhero’s who captured Crichton that means they wouldn’t have hurt Aeryn, which was the only good thing he could think of in his situation. Then above his head the guy in the advanced mechanics flies into view. “Ah, your awake now are you Crichton?” The guy in the robotic flying suit questions rhetorically. “Who the Hell are you? What do you want with me?” Crichton questions. “Don’t play innocent with us Crichton, we know what you’ve done and we’re taking you back to the authorities.” The man answers. “I haven’t done anything! What has it got to do with you anyhow?” Crichton questions. “We are the Thunderbolts, we do good for money! The government hired us to catch scum like you!” Mach 1 states “Hey wait a minute! You know the Avengers right? They let me go a while back because they realised that the government are up to no good!” Crichton replies. “The Avengers think they’re so superior to us and they couldn’t even take you in! Hahahaha priceless.” Mach 1 jokes. “What did you do with Aeryn?” Crichton asks. “Don’t worry yourself boy, she’s okay not that you’ll ever see her again!” Mach 1 taunts, flying out of Crichton’s view. Crichton thought back to the incident with the Avengers about a month ago. They were his only way out of here and if Aeryn was not captured in the process he hoped she would remember… Crichton then felt something grab him by the waist and lift him up into the air effortlessly. Crichton suspicions were confirmed as the Fifteen foot man stands there with Crichton captured. “Look I’m telling the truth! And I’m sure you guys have something better to do!” Crichton pleads. The one called Captain America steps forward, with his hand on his chin obviously pondering the situation. “Well Mr. Crichton, if you are telling the truth, it is the Avengers obliged duty to leave you alone, however if you are indeed lying, well were gonna kick your ass.” Cap debates. The one dressed in Black steps forward. “Well if he is lying we could always track him again!” The Black Panther suggests. Cap nods in agreement. “We’ll let you go for now, and if you are lying we will be back!” Cap declares. The fifteen-foot man places Crichton gently onto the floor and then the Avengers exit the area in their jet, specifically built by their benefactor Tony Stark. Crichton wanders back into his motel room, to be greated with a warm hug from Aeryn. ************ When Aeryn emerged from the warmth of unconsciousness to find herself utterly alone. Her last memories were of Crichton lying unconscious next to her and then some men talking outside of the crashed car. They mentioned the name ‘Thunderbolts’ and that’s when Aeryn lost consciousness. Thinking about it, Aeryn remembered the Thunderbolts from the news; Superhero’s for hire I think the journalists dubbed them. Aeryn decided that there was only one way to battle a group of superhero’s; with another group of superhero’s. Aeryn knew exactly where to get some too.
