Road to Triumph

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24-09-02 Can two wrongs make a right? Logic would surely say such a thing is preposterous, yet it happens. Everyday we see to things that go wrong turn into something great! It will not happen to you a lot, so, when it does, you must do everything you possibly can to make use of this bizarre chance you have been given, or else it will just pass you by, like so much that already has. “Flight 109 from Atlantic City, New Jersey to Davenport, Iowa has now arrived on Platform 9.” A grey –double-door stood lifeless, though noise came from every direction around it. Then suddenly, the door came alive as it was pushed open. Men and woman of every age and attire came though the door in a mad rush. In the huge crowd walked a young man of about 5’11 in height and had a strong build. He wore a green sweatshirt saying ‘Boston Celtics’ on it; he also wore blue, baggy jeans. This person was of course Jamie Celt. On his right shoulder was a black sports bag. His hair was spiked up with gel and his eyes seemed droopy and tired. As he came through the grey doors he looked around at the huger airport. There were shops and promotional posters were ever he looked. In the middle of the large area were three lines of seats. Very few people were there; some foreigners who were asleep were the only occupants for over five minutes. Jamie continued to look around the vast ocean of people. It was obvious that he could not locate who or what he was looking for. After searching for nearly a minute he eventually gave up and made his way over to the row of seats. He set down on the second from the end seat. He looked around one last time before resting in the seat, looking down at the ground beneath him. He thought loosely about his upcoming week. It would be a week that could well lead to big things for him, it was the break he wanted, and the break he needed. He could finally prove that he was not just an average rookie of the game that you see every day. He wanted to prove that he was something special and would be for years to come! After several lonely minutes of sitting on the chair, looking at people come and go, people that he would probably never see again, he got up and stretched his arms out. He then let out a big yawn. He turned around and once again searched for someone. He stood of his tiptoes to get a better view, though still he could not find who he was looking for. When he realized this he went back down to his normal level and began to look at all the shops there were around the airport, out of sheer boredom. As he looked one store in particular caught his eye. He smiled at the sight of it. It was a PCW Merchandise shop. Since he had nothing else to do he made his way over to it. The store was very modernized. It had a blue and white theme. Inside the shop looked a lot bigger than what Jamie thought. Merchandise of all PCW wrestlers was there, selling by the minute. Jamie looked around; he saw so many pieces of what people may consider ’memorabilia’ and what others may consider ‘a collectors item’. Jamie stood, quietly. Some people seemed to recognize him though they did not approach him. Maybe it was due to the realization that he was in deep thought. First, he saw a Flaymin Aymin t-shirt. It said on it ‘World peace is only a six pack away’. This was Jamie’s first opponent of Tuesday, he was tough, yet charismatic, a crowd pleaser if you will. He was surely one to be reckoned with. Jamie did not fear him, yet Jamie did not treat him as a nine contender for the match. For that match in particular was no small one. It was a number one contenders match for the PCW Extreme title, it was a chance Jamie could only dream off, and there was no way he would let it fly by. He had arrived it Iowa just then a day before the match, and he felt ready. He then shifted his head over to a Hardcore Hoefel shirt. It said on it ‘Can you beat me? No, so f*** off’. This was Jamie’s other opponent on Turmoil, one that was once called PTC’s best. He was the crème de la crème of PTC and now he was in PCW, Jamie knew what he was capable off, and Jamie also new that he could take what he had in PTC and make it into something even better in PCW. That’s why Jamie was concentrating on him heavily. He was an opponent to take seriously… to say the least. Jamie’s eyes then shifted to a top shelf where a PCW Cruiserweight title replica belt was on display. This was the prize Jamie so desperately wanted. And he would have his chance of Friday Fusion. He knew he had to give that match his all, for he would do anything to be called the PCW Cruiserweight Champion. When living in Boston as a child he loved watching cruiserweights, they were the most exciting of them all. He wanted to follow in the footsteps of such legends like Kid Wonder and Miyagawa to claim the title. His head then moved towards a Charlie Loc sweatshirt. This was the only man he would have to go through the get the title. Charlie was probably he best cruiserweight in PCW. Jamie knew what he was up against and would not take Loc lightly. He then saw a t-shirt of Anarchy. The current PCW Cruiserweight champion, Jamie wanted the title of her so badly. She was one of the best women PCW had to offer. Then finally a poster of Teresa Tomas hung up on a wall. This woman had attacked Jamie on several occasions. Why? Jamie did not know, but he would surely want to claim vengeance on Fusion, and had every intension of doing so. Jamie walked out of the shop knowing that this week could make or break his career, and he would stop at nothing to achieve triumph.
