Stuck at a gas station?

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-6-02 The sun is shining bright, the birds are singing, and Brandi is late for an interview with PCW’s Tiffany Jennings. She had stopped off at a gas station to buy a soda on the way. Now she hurries out of the small building, and to her black 2002 Camaro. She digs through her purse, looking for her keys. Unable to find them, Brandi grows frustrated and drops the contents of her purse out on the asphalt next to the car. She scatters them across the ground, finding anything from makeup to loose change, but no car keys. Brandi- I don’t have time for this! Brandi quickly shoves her things back into her purse and runs back into the gas station. She comes back out a few minutes later. She’s still without car keys. Back at her car, she leans over and peers through the window. There are the keys, laying between the two seats. How they ended up there and not in Brandi’s purse is a mystery to her. Brandi- Damn! Brandi goes through her purse once again, finding her cell phone and dialing Tiffany Jennings number. Brandi- Hey Tiffany. It’s Brandi…Yeah I know I was supposed to be there ten minutes ago. I kind of…locked my keys in the car…Yeah…I’m at the 76 station right down the street…Ok, see you soon. Brandi hangs up the phone and puts it back in her purse. She tries to open the drivers side door with no luck. Then she walks around to the passenger side and tries that door. It’s also locked, but the window is open a little bit. Brandi sticks her hand inside and tries to reach the door lock. It’s to no avail, as the window isn’t open enough. She walks over to a nearby curb and decides to have a seat while she waits for Jennings to arrive. Much to Brandi’s relief, Tiffany Jennings had offered to meet Brandi at the gas station. Brandi didn’t have a problem with this, but trying to have a serious conversation in a 76 station parking lot may be a bit odd. The gas station attendant pokes his head out of the store and sees Brandi sitting alone on the curb. No one is inside the store, so he walks over to her and has a seat next to her. Attendant- No luck with the keys, huh? Brandi- Nope. They’re locked inside. I feel like an idiot for doing that too. The man looks at Brandi and laughs. Attendant- Well, you shouldn’t. You’d be surprised how many people do it. I see it a lot around here. Brandi is unsure if the man is serious or if he’s just trying to make her feel better, but she nods anyway. He looks over at the car, then back over at Brandi. Attendant- Well, it’s rather hot out here. I can go call you a locksmith and you can come wait inside where there’s air conditioning if you’d like. Brandi nods and the man starts to go back inside the gas station. Brandi stands up and looks up and down the street. There’s no sign of Jennings. She tries the doors on her car one last time, knowing that unfortunately they won’t open, then she goes inside the building. The attendant has already called a locksmith, and is sitting in a room behind the counter. He’s watching a television and drinking a beer. Brandi isn’t sure if he’s supposed to be drinking on the job, but she decides it’s best not to ask him. When he heard the bell attached to the door ring, the man looked up and motioned for Brandi to join him in the room. She does now and is surprised to see that the room is rather inviting. In the corner of the room is a Ms. Pac-Man game. There are plenty of green plants, and even a small fish tank on one side of the room. Attendant- Feel free to make yourself at home. The only spot to sit in the room is on the small loveseat and is awfully close to the man. Brandi decides against it, and turns her attention towards the Ms. Pac-Man game. The man notices her looking at it and motions for her to go play. Attendant- Feel free to play. It’s rigged so it won’t cost you a thing. Brandi nods and goes over to the game. The bell rings from the door, so the attendant gets up and walks into the store. Brandi is glad to have him gone, but he soon returns with Tiffany Jennings. The man produces a folding chair from somewhere and opens it up next to the arcade game before leaving again. Jennings takes a seat next to Brandi. Brandi doesn’t bother to look up from her game, which she is getting quite into by now. Brandi- Hey Tiffany. Tiffany- Hi Brandi. Is someone coming to get the keys out of your car. Brandi nods. Tiffany looks as though she feels awkward by Brandi’s unwillingness to talk. She clears her throat, which gets Brandi to look up from her game for just a second. Tiffany- Well, what is your prediction for your match against The End? Brandi- What? You want to know if I think I’ll win? Tiffany- Well, yeah. That is kind of the idea. Brandi shrugs. Tiffany looks confused. Tiffany- Perhaps it wasn’t a good time to interview you now. Brandi still doesn’t look up from her game. She quickly jerks the joystick back and forth. Her face is frozen in a look of concentration. Brandi- Why do you say that? Tiffany sighs. Tiffany- Well, who do you think will walk away the winner on Fusion? Will it be The End walking away with his Cruiserweight title still wrapped around his waist, or will it be yourself walking away with the gold for the first time? Brandi- Honestly, I have no idea. I don’t want to make any promises because people will expect me to hold true to them, and I’m not quite sure I’ll be able to do that. I’ve never had a match with the man, so I’m not quite sure what to expect from him. GO! GO! Go, damn you! Damned ghost!… Brandi’s sudden outburst leaves Tiffany looking shocked. Brandi clears her throat and her face glows red with embarrassment. Brandi- Opps. Sorry about that. The ghost got me…So, anyways. Yeah, I’m not going to say that I’m going to lose on Fusion. I’m also not saying that I’m going to lose. I think that too many people get full of themselves and their abilities and they mess up. I don’t want to be one those people. I can’t afford to be one of those people. All I know is that I won a shot at his title, and that I plan on giving it my best shot. Hopefully I’ll win, but if I don’t I’ll have to try again. Tiffany mumbles something to herself, but Brandi doesn’t notice. She’s caught up in her game again. On the screen the two Pac people have just met up and are sharing a kiss after being chased by the ghosts. Tiffany notices this and it prompts another question. Tiffany- Well, what about your involvement with Rent A Villain? Will it play any sort of factor in your match? Perhaps we’ll see some interference… Brandi- If I do win this match, it will be by myself. I don’t want, nor do I need, help from anyone. Tiffany- Are you sure? A few people seem to feel strongly that because of your relationship with Rent A Villain, he will somehow try to help you win Cruiserweight gold. A combination of having Ms. Pac Man devoured by a ghost, and of Tiffany’s accusation makes Brandi angry. For the first time, she looks up from her game and straight into Tiffany’s face. Brandi- Look, I said he won’t get involved, and I meant he won’t get involved! Just forget about what a few people think, will ya? Tiffany looks startled by the tone of Brandi’s voice, but she wisely figures that it’s better to just drop it. Tiffany- How are you feeling right now? Brandi- What kind of question is that? Tiffany- Well, how are you feeling? After what Snow did to you and RAV? About your match with The End? About your career in the future? Brandi- I’m feeling great. I have a match that I can use greatly to my advantage. It can help out my career greatly. I’m doing well after what Snow has done to me. I just feel… Brandi decides not to describe the pain she’s been getting in her neck and head in fear that PCW will make her see a doctor before they allow her to wrestle The End. Tiffany sits, waiting for Brandi’s answer. Brandi- I feel great. That’s all. Just great. Tiffany doesn’t seem too happy with Brandi’s answer. She seems to know that she was about to say something else, but thought better of it. Tiffany- Are you sure? Brandi- Of course I’m sure! Tiffany gets up from her chair and starts towards the exit. She watches Brandi closely. Now she’s unsure of what to expect from her. She’s had a few outbursts, which is unusual for her. The attendant pokes his head into the room. Attendant- The locksmith’s here. Brandi looks up from the game and nods at the man. Brandi- Thanks. I’ll be there in just a second. The attendant waits outside for Brandi. She loses her final life on Ms. Pac Man, and walks with Tiffany outside. A gross looking man with greasy, uncombed hair is standing by Brandi’s car. Tiffany- Well, it was…interesting talking to you today. Thank you. Brandi- Hey, no problem. Brandi walks over to her car, where the man has just got the door open. Tiffany Jennings is left standing with the gas station attendant. Locksmith- Well, it’s all ready to go. Brandi runs to tell the locksmith thank you and realizes that he’s staring a little south of her face. She clears her throat, and the man looks back up. Brandi- How much do I owe ya? Locksmith- For you, not a thing. Although you can come have dinner with me tonight. Brandi gets in her car and pulls a twenty dollar bill out of her purse. She hands it to the locksmith and starts the engine. Brandi- I uh…thank you for the offer, but at this point and time I’d have to decline it. Have a nice day. Brandi throws a little wave over her shoulder to he gas station attendant and Tiffany Jennings, before happily driving out of the 76 station.
