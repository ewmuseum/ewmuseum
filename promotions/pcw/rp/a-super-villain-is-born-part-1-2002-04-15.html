A Super Villain Is Born Part 1

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 15th, 2002 (The scene opens as Mike Frampton sits at a grungy round table. As the camera sweeps around the table many prominent faces are seen. The Riddler, Dr. Octopus, Penguin, Mr. Freeze, and The Joker are all there. Mike Frampton sits at the head of a table. He wears a latex covering over his hair making him appear bald. He wears a black suit. The Riddler, Dr. Octopus, Penguin, Dr. Freeze, and The Joker all stare at Mike with fiendish grins. Mike smiles back and begins.) Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Gentlemen, I have one simple question for all of you. How are we going to kill Rent A Hero? The Joker: Personally I don't see how this has anything to do with us. We want to kill Batman. Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Already taken care of. (Mike Frampton pulls a string and the body of Batman falls down onto the table.) Dr. Freeze: I'm going to need a cold compress, STAT! Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Shut up you wanted him dead. Now then... Dr. Octopus: Eh hem. Before I help I want Spiderman dead! Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: ... (Mike pulls a second string and the body of Spiderman flops down on top of Batman.) Dr. Octopus: Alright then. Penguin: Damn it Lex, I need payments! Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Listen, my men are hijacking a truck full of Highliner Fishsticks tonight. Penguin: Excellent. Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Now may I continue. (The Riddler raises his hand.) Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: What is it now Riddler? The Riddler: Killing Rent A Hero is so last year. Couldn't we just trap him in a giant maze? Penguin: Or brainwash him. The Joker: Or make him join a chorus line. (Everyone turns towards The Joker with a confused look.) The Joker: I mean, killing is fine. Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Now then, honestly men, does anyone really object to killing? (The Riddler and Dr. Octopus raise there hands.) The Riddler: Why do we have to kill him? Dr. Octopus: Couldn't we just put him in a coma? Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: No we are super villains and we must kill him. The Joker: Seriously a chorus line would take away his credibility as a superhero. (Again everyone turns to The Joker. He falls silent.) Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Joker, one more word about Rent A Hero and a chorus line and we will kill you too. The Joker: I'm just saying... *BANG* (Penguin puts his smoking umbrella down.) Penguin: That takes care of that. Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Now then gentlemen. How do we do it? The Riddler: With a big floppy chicken. *BANG* Penguin: I never liked him anyways. Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Anyways what I was thinking was... (The real Lex Luther opens the door and walks in.) Lex Luther: Hey guys I was just killing Superman and... heeeeyy, who the hell are you? (Luther points at Frampton.) Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Why I’m Lex Luther, who the hell are you? Lex Luther: No I'm Lex Luther. Dr. Octopus: I'm confused. *BANG* (Dr. Octopus hits the ground.) Penguin: I hate people who talk during the good parts. Dr. Freeze: Yes, me too. *BANG* (Dr. Freeze too hits the floor.) Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: Penguin, stop killing everyone. Penguin: Meh!*BANG* (Lex Luther drops to the floor after being shot by Penguin.) Mike Frampton/Lex Luther: I'm just going to walk over here now. (Mike runs out the door as Penguin takes a shot at him laughing.) Penguin: I still want my fish. (Fade to black.)
