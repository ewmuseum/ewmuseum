Interview with the Devil: Self Talk (Part 5 of a series done in another fed, DO NOT count toward match)

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-21-03 All of the darkness in the world comes pouring over me and I can’t stand it sometimes. Yet, I’m supposed to be such a great man of God and then again, Satan runs my life so much that it isn’t funny. It’s beginning to get hard to handle some of the things that are going on, and I’ve been trying to confess this to many people. Those that believe in me will hear nothing of it and those that will listen only try to bring me back up with support and no actual help. I’m about to go nuts on them all and just throw the towel in and submit myself to the ways of evil. No! I will not let that happen, I’m better than that. Then again, am I? Stop it Shane! Quit questioning yourself! You have nothing to fear, but fear itself. That’s what gets me the most though, it is the fear; it drives me so much sometimes and then it comes rushing over me like a sudden flood and I can’t quite seem to take the effects of it all. Thusly causing me to want to give in so easily, but I can’t. Yes, you can! You could give up with such ease, but you’re not going to because you’re not going to let those thoughts leak in to your mind. Or am I? Of course you’re not. How could you? You’re fighting for the right things in life, aren’t you? I’m not even sure anymore, when I started I felt as if I were doing all of the right things for this business, but now I just don’t know. Confused? Of course I’m confused, just about as much as the next person walking by me. I can definitely tell you that every single person who comes by me notices a change in me. A sense of fear that covers me like a soft blanket. Then take that blanket off and toss it in the trash where it belongs. It’s not as easy as you would think. Yes, it is. You’re only letting it be more than it actually is by acknowledging it. No, you’re wrong. You don’t know what I’m going through, all of these problems, you wouldn’t understand a single one of them. Of course I would, I am you. You’re talking to yourself right now; I know exactly what you’re going through. How is that possible? I’m not insane; I can’t be talking to myself. Of course you’re not talking to yourself, because you are an idiot. I’m in your subconscious and I know more about life than you’ll ever imagine. But if you’re me and I’m you, then we have to know the same things. Not in the least, Shane, you know nothing because you don’t allow yourself to know as much as you possible could, but soon, you will know everything; or you will fall and become your fears. For this is the way things have been written in the book, just be patient and use your wisdom to come to a conclusion on the things in your life.
