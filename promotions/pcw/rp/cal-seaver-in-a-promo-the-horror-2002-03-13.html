Cal Seaver in a Promo? The Horror!

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th March 2002 We’re backstage at Tuesday Turmoil prior to Turmoil itself. We’re in Miyagawa’s locker room in fact in a rather drab and boring section, although we havn’t done it for a while so we’re hoping that foul judgement will not be cast down upon us nor shall we be turned into turds. I meant toads. Honestly, I did. Anyway, we’re backstage with Miyagawa as he finishes off changing into his ringware... he’s already got one of his shirts on and his pants, and of course his socks... in fact he’s just tying up his boot laces right when there is a knock on the door. Miyagawa: Come in! The door opens and stands there none other than the on-the-payroll-but-hardly-used-since-the-Blood-Brothers-left interviewer Cal Seaver wearing a nicely made black suit. Nicely made that is if you particularly like nylon in a huge fashion. Or huge fashion mistakes, it’s completely up to you. Cal: How’s it going champ? Are you nervous about facing Boda again? Cal points his microphone towards Miyagawa sitting on the office chair in front of the rather theatrical dressing table with mirrors and lights and even... makeup... Cal looks at the makeup and adds one and one together and deduces that it’s Natalia’s. Cal: So where’s Natalia? I didn’t realise she was gracing us with her presence here tonight at Turmoil. Miyagawa looks up at Cal now that he’s finished tying the lace on his right boot, and looks down again and begins to tie the left boot up this time. Cal: I guess you must be a pretty busy guy now Champ with all the endorsements and stuff coming in. But at least you managed to keep hold of the belt for longer than Boda, which must be some comfort, right? Cal looks around the dressing room some more, it’s pretty nicely done out with a shower over towards the back and even some towels with Miyagawa’s initials on them... either that or AIM is sponsoring this event in order to boost it’s image. Cal: That reminds me of the time that you and Meanstreak were double booked in the same locker room, and Streak walked in and found you pissing in his shower. That was funny. Cripes, that was friging’ hilarious. Cal looks down at Miyagawa and finds that he doesn’t seem to be all that forthcoming with answers to any of his questions. Cal: And of course you’ve probably already started looking ahead to Chase on Fusion to try and show him who’s boss, right? Although it’ll be the first time in a couple of weeks that you won’t be able to mention the Ass-Ass-Ins in a promo, right? Silence for ten seconds. Miyagawa finally looks up at Cal. Cal smiles sheepishly knowing that this is possibly the worst interview on record in the entire history of PCW. Miyagawa: No Cal, I’m not nervous about facing Boda again, but let’s be blunt, I’m already looking past Boda and onto Chris Chase who is going to be getting a taste of what the World Champion is all about. Yes Cal, Natalia is here, but she’s popped out because she had a crazy craving for peanut butter and lime jello, don’t ask me, I’m not even particulary struck with Peanut butter although I hear that the Phantom Booker loves Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups. Cal: Really? Miyagawa: Yes Cal, I have managed to keep the belt longer than Boda but only because Boda had a total cow of a booking week. That’s why I’m giving him this rematch because he’s had some time off this week only having to kick the crud out of Chris Chase on Sunday just like I’m going to do on Friday. And no Cal, Meanstreak ADD’ing me while I was only wearing a towel was not amusing. It hurt a great deal, and one day I will choking him out but it’ll be when he least expects it. Cal: But he’s in OSW now, and PCW contracts state that you can’t leave while you PCW Champion because of an amendment after the Stewart Hix incident. Miyagawa: Yes Cal, the Phantom Booker really loves Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups, and no Cal, I did not know that about the PCW Contracts. Silence ensues once again as Miyagawa finally answers all of the interviewers questions. Miyagawa: Steve Jabowski was finally signed by NTICW. Did you hear that? Cal: No, I didn’t. Miyagawa: You’re not exactly much of a reporter then are you? No wonder no one asks you to interview them any more, you’re just a great big... what’s the word... ah I know... foo’. I know what we can do to liven up your act a little bit more, would you mind wearing one of my t-shirts? Cal: Sure, I’d love to wear a Miyagawa t-shirt, I always have to wear these crappy nylon suits that Duke Williams gives me. He said to me “Hoss, here’s a dollar fifty, go get a wardrobe” So I went to this charity shop and said... Miyagawa stands up and picks up a discarded t-shirt from the floor and places it over Cal’s head, covering his face completely. Cal (slightly muffled): What exactly do you think you are doing. Miyagawa: Shut up buttfinch and let me talk to the camera. Cal moves his head to where the sound is coming from. Miyagawa: Don’t move your head buttfinch, the t-shirt will fall off. **Turns to the camera** Act One Scene One. This is how it all begins... Miyagawa wins the World Title from an overrated undertalented overstated Gimboid like Chris Sloboda. Scene Two... Miyagawa gives the Gimboid a rematch because he’s so great and then beats the fatty’s ass once again for the whole one... the two... and even the three. Act Two begins with the opening scene of Chris Chase screaming like a little baby.... Miyagawa tilts his head upwards towards the ceiling. Miyagawa: Waaaa waaaaa waaaaaa New Breed sucks... waaaa waaaa and I’m stuck in it... waaaa waaaaa waaaa.... what can I do now that the only talented guy in my stable has left? Waaaaaaa waaaaaa I want my Mummy... waaaaa I want a title.... waaaaa. He tips his head back down towards the camera. Miyagawa: You get the idea, and you know Chris you’ve got a serious problem there, it’s called being a Gimboid. And if you went to any doctor he’d suggest a serious course of Therapy. And you know what else? I’m going to give you that course of Therapy on Friday night at Fusion... and you know why? Cal: Because you’re... Miyagawa grabs Cal by the head and slams his skull into the wall before pulling him back into camera shot and carefully straightening out the t-shirt. Miyagawa: It’s Because... I’m... Excellent.
