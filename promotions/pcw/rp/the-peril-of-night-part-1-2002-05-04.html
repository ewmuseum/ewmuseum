The Peril of Night part 1

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/05/02 Here it was, this is what it had all come to. The OSW and the Queen of Extreme tournament, they were all in her past now. This was her future, and what a future it promised to be. The illustrious PCW had opened their doors, as the pride of Prime Time Central, how could she not simply be exhilarated by the flashes of both glory and defeat that enclosed her mind with their visions of it all. As she sat upon the bench simply attempting to comprehend the meanings and possibilities that were seemingly unfathomable, yet they had led her to this well of never-ending success. What lay in store for her during this unforeseen endeavour and what would people see her as now? Would she be seen like all so many of her newfound colleagues, to be no more than a fraudulent actor with a perm and a nice smile, or would she be seen for what she really is. The ravaging beast of darkness that got by with any means necessary, purging, maiming and destroying simply to get by. Only time could tell. She sat there, a broken person. Without the vibrant and luscious luxuries of companionship or even an acquaintance to ease both the pain and the happiness that she felt. Her pain dwelling from her personal life, the voice of her conscience had been silenced by one more omnipotent with evil than she could ever have imagined and her endless struggle to rid herself of this, yet she also felt a faint brush of glory, raising her lowly spirits in a vain attempt to incubate the miniscule soul that continuously diminished within her. All of this woven in the lattices of her mind, the tangled web of her thought and maze of her mentality, she could not begin to unwind the fibers of melancholy that had made her so sad, for it had been so long since she had seen the innards of herself. Some might say too long. Attempting to break the lowliness that brewed within her empty heart, she looked to the sky, to the stars and beyond. Seeking some sort of reconcile from the pains that lashed at her troubled soul with the whip of depression. Bright shimmering specks lit up the sky, filtered by clouds of purple shade and grey shadow. She felt enclosed by it all; it made her feel so small. The stars and their beauty, like the dreams she had longed for as a child, she wanted to be there, to feel the magic and by simply sitting there within that moment, many moons could come and go without her comprehension, as she was lost in a realm of desire and yearning. The craving to be whole again. Breaking off from the enticement of the heavens she shook her head, as the shades and strands of violet hair rolled from her shoulders and sailed in the mysterious wind that blew past in erratic gusts. As she turned she saw a figure, wrapped in a cloak of shadows it stood, still as the night itself and simply looked out and beyond over the empty, silent hills. The scope of his vision looking with hers, was she dreaming? For in the dead of night, none but her would come here to seek their reconcile, what was the meaning of this. She stood with curiosity wanting to learn more of this enigmatic entity, but as she stood, blinking and wiping the dew from her eyes to clear the images of her mind, she opened her eyes once more, but saw that the figure had gone. She stood for a moment to wonder and to fathom what had happened, surely it must have been her mind, the trickery of her visions displaying her longings, but perhaps not. For she had seen it move, like no other and as she had observed the figure, she had still felt the night, with the blistering cold wind invigorating her senses, surely it was real. Once again in a vain attempt to reiterate her mental awareness of this fellow lost soul, she wiped her eyes, scratching at them with her nylon sleeve and chaffing her lids, as she desperately tried to bring back the image to her head. For maybe she had finally found another like her, what she had wanted for so long would finally be with her, a friend. In the heat of the moment she simply decided to pursue in hope of blundering her way through the relentless battering wind of the night and to find this entity, which had enticed her to meet with it. With nothing to carry she simply turned toward the gravel covered path, wearing her black shoes without high heels she began stride with power toward her ever nearing goal and closer to the being that was her aim. Her violet and purple strands wavered along the enigmatic gusts of the nightly blustering tide that like knives slashed at her silken dress. The lengths and lace of her blouse were ripped asunder as she continually pressed on, trying to ignore the tides of daggers that cut at her skin, clothes and eyes, but it matched her and continued to force her back. Overpowered by the seemingly living breeze, she struggled; the sky was a grimace reflecting the pain caused by the force that ebbed from the ongoing battle between the great natural evils. Her face was expressionless, it seemed almost as if this was effortless, but although she would not show it, she was in grieving pain. Aggravated by the winds she could not surpass her eyes turned from mellow observing to a piercing stare of blades, that almost seemed to invoke an inner power that sliced effortlessly through the wind as she raised her arm and marauded on following her one path. As the winds subsided leaving only a chilling yet mellow breeze, she went on, now in the stance of a march, with her eyes locked upon the distant path. There were trees of evergreen, as far as her eyes had seen, the purple clouds and greys of shadow spiraling above her, merging and become a filthy brown, mirroring her mood. The green grass of spring luscious and filled with promise of a looming summer, which she despised, she hated it all and simply kept her eyes from wondering toward the fabricated beauty of this forest clearing. She neared an opening into the forest, her march, now once more became an enduring lunge with every step her speed increased with the desire to find this man still, she could sense that somebody was near, or perhaps something. The power of it’s evil was more than worthy of her infatuation and had drawn her into its web of mystery, for why else would she have endured such hardships to reach it? She stood now, at the threshold between moonlight and the darkness that lay beneath the canopies of nature, with a deep breath and a blink of her eyes, she overstepped this line drawn by nature and stepped into a realm, which she must blunder through on this step to reach her goal. She had come so far but would she be able to go any further? Or would she simply abandon her chance to meet another that truly was of her kind. The hurdle was there, would she leap and cross the threshold to what had been the source of her dreams and desires for so long, or would she simply fall back to the way she was, lonely and despising of herself once more. Only time could tell...
