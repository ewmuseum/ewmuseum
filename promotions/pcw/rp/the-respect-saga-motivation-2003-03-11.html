The Respect Saga: Motivation.

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-03 'Gavyn,' Talena muttered, she wasn't really that happy with her lovers actions as of late. He had been distant and almost... dim too her recently, gone for great lengths of time without any notice. No warning at all. 'What has gotten into you lately, you have been making us very cross.' She gave her motherly scowl, her doll hanging limply in her arms. 'I've been soul searching Talena, my dear, soul-searching,' and it had been true, he had been. DarkStar had gone to the ends of the earth and back to try and figure out what his problem was, it was so simple that he was surprised he hadn't realized it before. He wasn't nearly as important as he should be. He was a two year veteran in PCW who got no respect compared to other guys, it sickened him, and what was the excuse? Laziness? Talent? Nothing... just general favoritism. Two years. He turned his back on his federation pride and his 'country'. All because of this. 'Soul-searching? I could have done that for you.' Her piercing blue eyes showed that she could have, DarkStar looked away, 'This was how I wanted to do it, and I have decided my next plan of action.' He sneered. The black lipstick curling up, Talena loved that look and nearly jumped him there. 'What! What is it puppy! I want to know!' She begged like a six year old presented with a clue at what her birthday present was. 'It is really quite simple.' He stood at the kitchen counter now cutting a apple, 'You see, I am pushed into the back of these idiots minds, the bookers, the fans, the talent, the people, the americas, and yet i'm torn on if it should even seem important.' The cutting got more intense, Talena herself was mesmorized by it. 'My agenda is seperate, I want to be the star in PCW, a dark star, the first real one they have had despite what Clyde would tell you. But, the US... the underground needs me to take up the flag... and for the longest time I couldn't think of how to combine my duties.' He slammed the knife down into the counter, it stuck straight up, about a half an inch in. Talena jumped at the sound and nearly fell over a chair. 'Now, the whole world will have my name blessing their heathen tongues. Humanity will worship me as their fucking misanthropic GOD once again.' He bit a slice of the apple, smiling. 'Daddy has come home Talena.' Talena jumped in the air and clapped happily, her expression saying it all.
