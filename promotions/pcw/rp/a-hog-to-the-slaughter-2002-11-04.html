A Hog to the Slaughter

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/04/02 'GRUAAAAAAAAAARGH!' In a dank and dreary and darkened hotel room, a voice groans in anger. His eyes a shade of red, though bloodshot instead of their usual fiery hue, he stares at his television. 'Ooh, my toenails have gotten funky.' Into the room strolls the homicidal eight year old known as Bethany, looking from the tv to Clyde. Bethany: ......Anna Nicole Smith, Clyde? Clyde: Yeehoebra. On the screen the formerly attractive woman turned pile of goo parades about her house. Anna: Lalala. Bethany: Dear god, that's one girl who should NOT be wearing a cheerleader outfit. Clyde: Yeehoebra. Bethany: Speak english, I can't understand you when you sit there muttering like Sideshow Bob!!!! Anna: (Looking down) Ooh, dropped a penny. Clyde: (Eyes widening) No, NO!!!!!! As the beastly woman bends over, Bethany panics. She first tries to cover Clyde's eyes, but the flames licking forth from them burns her palms. Bethany: Yeeouch! The psychopath blows on her hands, then looks at the tv again. As Anna Nicole slowly folds upon herself like the breaching of some terrible whale, Bethany leaps. Bethany: UNGAAAAAAH!!!!! Hitting the television with her body, Bethany succeeds in knocking it over. Both her and the set hit the ground with a thud as Clyde shakes his head sleepily. Clyde: Where am I? Bethany: BAH! As his lover dusts herself off, the burned man glances at the fallen monitor. Clyde: Anna Nicole Smith, Bethany? Bethany: You were the one watching her! Clyde: Was not. Bethany: Were too! And you know what happens when you see stuff like that, you fucking zone out and go insane! Clyde: Meh. From her position on the ground, Anna's voice shrilly rings forth. Anna: Man.....let that be a lesson to yall, shave once every year or so down there! That's just gotten NASSS-TY. Clyde: ARGH!!!! Clyde stands, marching across the room and slamming his face into a nearby wall. Then over to the other side, slamming his face into the other wall. This pattern repeats for some time before Bethany shakes her head. Bethany: I'd unplug the TV but you'd just find some other horrible show to drive yourself nuts with. Not answering, the demon merely continues bashing his skull off the hotel plaster. Bethany: Hell you might force yourself to watch El Diablo promos, then we'd REALLY be up shit creek. Clyde: (Slams his head harder) Bethany: But what can we do? The little girl scratches her chin....then her eyes seem to light up. A malicious grin crosses her face..... A dancing cartoon leads us into a frightening sight, a very drunken fat girl spinning in a circle. Anna: Welcome....ooh, I think.....I don't know. The camera follows the rotund princess through the chambers of the home as she stumbles about. Nearly tripping over her own feet, Anna looks all around. Anna: (Slurring) Whee....I love having cameras around. Cause they....yeah! And.....um....what was I saying? The starlet blinks, staring into space. Then her attention is caught by something to the side. Anna: Food. Haven't eaten in....yeah. Three minutes. Giggling, Anna Nicole prances off camera. We follow her through the chambers of her highly overpriced estate, entering a door.....to find her resting upside down in the bathtub, kicking her feet frantically. Anna: (Muffled) Help! A hand reaches past the camera's view, grabbing her tubby arm and yanking. With a mighty effort Ms. Smith wrenches free, crashing to the floor. Anna: (Giggling) That wasn't the fridge! After a dainty belch, Anna begins crawling in the other direction. Making her way to her feet, she grins at the cameraperson. Anna: I feel pretty with cameras around.....hehe! Finally after an eternity the young woman reaches the kitchen. Reaching for the refrigerator door handle, she nearly falls again but catches herself. Anna: I'm going on a diet soon. Heeeee! Belching again and hiccuping, Anna yanks on the door. It flies open....she then yelps as a form comes sailing out of it. Bethany: DIE!!!!!! The cameraman backpeddles in shock as what appears to be a little girl latches onto Anna Nicole Smith's face. The rotund girl shakes her head weakly. Anna: Hehe, that tickles. Bethany: Huh!? Producing a knife, Bethany tries to take a swipe but Anna simply lifts her off with the other hand and looks at her quizzically. Anna: Yer a feisty chicken leg. Hehe! Once again burping, Anna Nicole gives a hearty drunken laugh as Bethany's eyes water. Bethany: What the hell have you been eating, roadkill? Anna: Maybe. What's it to ya? Bethany: AGH!!!! Kicking with all her might, the little girl manages to free herself from the grasp of Ms. Smith. She crawls between her tubby legs, spinning and glaring at her. Bethany: Now you die! Again Bethany charges, leaping through the air. However as her head strikes Anna's massive gut she merely rebounds, flying across the room and into the darkness where another voice grunts. Clyde: (Off camera) Ow! Bethany: (Off camera) Stop standing where I'm crash landing! Clyde: (Off camera) You were the one who wanted her killed. Finally after a long moment the towering visage of the demon known as Clyde emerges. Anna Nicole Smith looks on drunkenly, then finally smiles. Anna: I know you.....you're that thing from Wishmaster! I met yooou 'fore. Clyde: Bah! As Anna turns and reaches up towards a high shelf exposing much more crack than is necessary, Clyde stalks towards her. Clyde: Believe me, I don't want to be here. It was the girl's idea. Bethany: (Dusting herself off) Well, if you didn't insist on watching godawful shows like this we wouldn't have to! But you do, so our only course of action is to kill the fatass. Anna Nicole finally grabs hold of what she was seeking, a bottle of hooch high above. Falling from exertion, she slumps with a goofy grin on her face. Anna: Fuuuuuuny. Heeee! Taking a swig, Ms. Smith drools as the burned man looks at Bethany. Clyde: Well, want to try again? Bethany: You do it! Clyde: Bah! Shaking his head, Clyde whips out a knife of his own. Raising it upon high, he prepares to stab....then stops and sniffs the air. Clyde: Is that beached whale I smell? Anna: Flaaatery. The drunken former actress rolls like a log, her mounds of flab rebounding along the floor. She crashes into Clyde's knees, causing him to buckle. Clyde: Aggggh! Stumbling, the demon tries to keep his balance but fails. Falling on his face, he groans as his lover looks on. Bethany: Yeesh. Sighing deeply, Bethany takes her knife again and closes the gap towards Anna. Bethany: This'll hurt you way more than it hurts me. The girl swings her blade, sinking it deep into Ms. Smith's shoulder. She grins evilly....then stops as she notices Anna chuckling. Anna: Ticklyyyyy! Get me mooore hooch. With an absent swing of her meaty hand, Anna Nicole knocks Bethany clear across the room. She hits the sink, slumping to the ground painfully. Bethany: How? How!? She didn't even feel that! Clyde: (Standing slowly) Look at her, she's obviously got so much alcohol in her system she wouldn't feel the impact from an elephant stampede, let alone your little knife. Bethany: Feh! What'll we do then? With a mighty effort Anna Nicole Smith stands again, pirouetting about like the world's largest dancing hippo. Anna: I fly thhough the air wit the gweatest of eeeee! Clyde: Hm. Clyde turns around and opens up the cabinet beneath the sink. Rummaging about, he produces a bottle of bleach. Bethany: Well, it's worth a shot. Bethany reaches into her dress, bringing forth a syringe. She hands it to the demon, who opens the top on his canister then fills it to the brim. Clyde: See how you like this. Rising again, Clyde charges at Anna. However just as he reaches her the extremely large girl snatches the needle from his grasp. Anna: Gimme! Anna Nicole Smith spins the vial around, her eyes lighting up happily. Then she jabs her own wrist with it, injecting herself full of the toxic substance. Anna: Wheeeeeee! The soft stuff's fun sometimes. Clyde: ...... Bethany: ...... Anna: I want HARD stuff though! Gimmmmme! GIMMMMEEEEE!!!!!! Anna throws herself on the ground, throwing a temper tantrum as our murderous pair looks on. Bethany: Well that was a bright idea. Clyde: Better than yours. Bethany: Was not! Clyde: Was too! Bethany: Whatever, now what do we do!? Anna Nicole looks up from her position on the ground, slowly licking her lips. Anna: Hungry again. Clyde: Um..... Bethany: RUN FOOL!!!!! Bethany dives and knocks Clyde out of the way just in time, as Anna snaps her jaws forth with inhuman speed. Both the girl and the demon hit the ground hard, but on the flip side not eaten. Clyde: Good lord! The burned man looks on in shock as Anna's head begins moving towards him, snapping its jaws. The remainder of her body stays still, merely being dragged behind it. Anna: FOOOOOOOOD! Bethany: NO!!!!!! Hopping up, the little girl runs for her life yanking a dazed Clyde along with her. The pair sprints out of the kitchen, up a flight of stairs as behind them horrible skulching sounds ring forth. Anna: (Muffled) Foooooood......FOOOOOOOOOD! Clyde: Nice idea to come here, sweetie! Bethany: Shut up and run!!!!! Moving as fast as they can, Clyde and Bethany escape through a nearby door. Slamming it shut, the girl rests against it....then jumps in terror as the wood of it cracks behind her. Clyde: Holy crap. The demon looks on somewhat amazed as the paneling of the door vanishes bit by bit. Anna Nicole Smith's face appears behind it, her teeth gnashing about as she appears to be eating the very wood itself. Anna: Hunnnnngry! Clyde: (Nodding) Now that's impressive. Bethany: Come on! Grabbing Clyde again, Bethany drags him through the bedroom and out of sight. Finally the wood gives way, the beast known as Anna Nicole hefts herself through. Anna: Heeeeeeee! Hauling herself in bloblike fashion, Anna follows the scent. Through the far door she crawls, out to the balcony.....then stops short. Anna: Huh? Ms. Smith's eyes grow wide as she sees what looks to be an old and enfeebled man, dressed in a fine Armani suit. Anna: POOKIE!!!!! J. Howard Marshall looks up through cataract coated eyes, shaking and trembling. Marshall: Oooh....I'm not long for this world..... Anna's former 90 year old husband reaches into his pocket, mopping his brow with a 100 dollar bill. Her eyes lighting up, the portly golddigger rises like a cobra. Anna: I love you money!!!!! Charging with arms outstretched and paying no mind to the fact Mr. Marshall should be dead, Anna stumbles towards him.....then looks around in confusion as he vanishes into thin air. Anna: Uh....AAAAAH!!!!!! Letting loose a horrible shriek, Anna Nicole Smith topples over the balcony. Down, down she falls, flapping her flabby arms like a rotund bird but to no avail.....high above, Bethany and Clyde look on. Bethany: ..... Clyde: ....... Bethany: ...... Then at last a horrifying 'SKULCH' sounds out as the mammoth's body hits ground zero. The demon glares at Bethany once again. Clyde: Had to pick her, didn't you. Bethany: Oh shut up. Let's go! Grumbling, the psychopathic child makes her way away soon to be followed by the burned man. We pan over the balcony to the mass of shrubbery below, deathly calm and still.....which shifts slightly, ever so slightly. 'Foooooood......'
