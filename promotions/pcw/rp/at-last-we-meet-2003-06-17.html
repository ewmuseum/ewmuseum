At Last We Meet

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17.06.03 We see a frantic and panicked Abner pacing up and down on the sundeck of the airship. Underneath the green, tainted cover of his John Deere cap, a sweat has started to form. He looks concerned, but determined as well, and storms around in the blazing sun as it beats down onto the open part of the air ship. Abner: Expect the unexpected…expect the unexpected He mumbles those words repeatedly until he stops pacing around and stands still, itching his chin and goes deep into his thoughts. Abner: Right, for the last two weeks, this masked German dude has been into my locker room, so chances are…that…he’ll… He trails off at the end of the sentence and mumbles to himself while staring at the wooden slats in the floor. Moments later, a light bulb has seemingly gone off in his head as he clicks his fingers. Painfully, he produces a small bottle water of H20 from the other hand, and takes a sip, shivering in light of the medicine. He cringes, and then looks back up at the camera. Abner: I’ve got it! I’ll get that German freak good! He wants to mess with mah beer…mah lovely, sweet beer and he wants to trash mah locker room? Cause lord, only AH trash mah locker room! Abner pauses to throw the water to the floor. Abner: Well, I’ll fix it so he get the beatin’ of his life!! I don’t care how big he is!! The redneck rubs his hands at the prospect of finally getting his hands on the German perpetrator that has toyed with him for the last two weeks. He then storms off with a focused and determined expression, intent on getting revenge. The shot fades out but only for a few seconds. And then, returning, the scene shows Abner down a deserted hallway, pumping himself up as he clutches his fists and shakes his arms vigorously. He’s pacing up and down a few yards as he mumbles a few more words before composing himself. Abner: I’ve got the jackass this time, oh yeah. He won’t know what’s hit him when I beat him down, down, down… Stopping to clap his hands in rhythm, on beat with each threat, Abner: …like the government mule he is! He then barges into his locker room, but doesn’t turn on the light. Instead, he closes the door immediately behind him. As he takes position behind the doorway, darkness ensues. Abner (whispering): I’ll stand behind this here door, and when that freak walks in, I’ll bash him a good ‘un! A few moments pass and all we can see is Abner’s feet lit by a tiny gap of light shining through at the bottom of the door. Abner sighs as he waits, patiently, but at the same time, impatiently. Abner: Looks like I’ll be here all night. A nice cold one would pass the time… You can here footsteps moving across the floor. There’s a silent pause… Seemingly, the redneck has just realized that he hasn’t actually got any full-alcoholic beer left, as you here the sound of a refrigerator door slam shut. Abner: Damn that German! Damn him all ta hell! Oooh, he’s really gonna get it now! More moments pass, until some heavy breathing can be heard. It’s starts off as a slight murmur, but gets louder with every second. Abner: What in the hell is that noise? Somebody’s dog in here? …Darth Vader? Abner can be heard tip toeing around, as his boots tap against the carpet. There are more moments of darkness and silence, before a large gulp can be heard…seemingly Abner has found something… and he doesn’t like it. Abner: That…that…be…ur…a…gas mask? Within seconds, a commotion can be heard and the lights flicker on. The redneck jumps out of his skin to see the gas masked German standing right there in his room! Abner: You!! He points an accusing finger at the foreigner…literally. But before he can make any offense though, the German launches a hard right and Abner falls against the wall. The masked man then clamps his right hand around Abner’s throat and tightens the grip as he brings him closer to own face. The redneck is spluttering and coughing as he fights for air, and moments later, the masked individual hurls Abner across the room. He smashes the metal lockers, thus creating a loud hollow thud, and goes crashing, sliding down to the floor. The German pauses and stares down at Abner reaching for his throat, before pulling him to the center of the room with light ease. He then reaches into his coat and pulls out a folded German flag, flapping it in the air to straighten the gold, black and red coloured item out. Without hesitation, the giant gently places the flag across the prone body of Abner. The German stands over the flag-covered Abner before departing the room, slamming the door behind him. The camera then pans down to see the German flag covering a motionless Abner before slowly fading out. We fade back to ringside in the presence of a shocked David Kern….and an eating Jeff Marx. Jeff rips open a package of twinkies, and tossing the wrappers aside, immediately shoves the cakes into his mouth. Dave: My word! Jeff: Mmm….delicious… Jeff rubs his tummy, cheeks bulging as he continues to chew. Dave: No! The attack…this is….this is getting very, very- Jeff cuts him off. Jeff: -BORING!!! Dave shakes his head. Dave (ignoring Jeff, who’s opened another package of pastries): Well folks, regardless of what my companion here thinks, I think we’ve got a feud on our hands… Do I smell something for Cloud Nine? Jeff: Nope, that was me… Dave sighs as the camera fades out.
