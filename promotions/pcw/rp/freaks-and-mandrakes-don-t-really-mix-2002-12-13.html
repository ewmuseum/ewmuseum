Freaks and Mandrakes don't really mix

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 13, 2002 “Doug, what the hell is this?” Eddie inquired as we begin on this adventure. We are some sort of commercial complex. In front of a red door, stating in a gold plate on it about three quarters of the way up “Freak Anon”. We see Eddie and Spider standing in front of the door awaiting some sort of move toward the door. “What do you mean?” Spider asks for clarification “Well, I’m standing here, in front of a place called ‘Freak Anon’…What are you trying to say?” “Well, the fact that you need true mental help is there, and if the anger management thing isn’t going to work, then this is the next best thing. There are people like you in here, masochistic, genetic freaks, and people into hardcore things…” “Whoa…you mean to tell me that I’m in a support group for retards??” “That is a very ignorant thing to say, however it isn’t. In here there are people who have been traumatized by events that have turned them into some sort of freak, whether it be mental, physical, emotional, anything like that” Spider explains. “Ah, I see now. Well, let’s get this over with” Eddie concludes as he enters the room, with Spider close to follow. Eddie enters the room to see only four people. One of them is larger than Spider muscle-wise. He doesn’t even have a neck! The suit that he is wearing looks to be holding on for dear life to try to prevent from ripping from the seams. That man is setting everything up. There is one in a chair, wearing a very large white suit. He is rather short, and is wearing bandages a white mask, very similar to The Phantom of the Opera mask. A third woman is in the room, she looks to be very muscle bound and almost seven feet tall. She almost doesn’t even look like a woman…more like a she-male. Eddie’s eyes expand, and quickly does an about face, right into Spider. All Spider does is point in the opposite direction and Eddie continues to walk in. The suited one approaches them. “Hi there, my name is Scott, are you here for the meeting?” “Uh…” “Yes, He is. My name is Doug, and this is Eddie” Spider interrupts quickly before Eddie has a chance to speak. Scott extends a hand, and just looks into a cold dead stare. Scott draws the hand back. “Well, we have a variety of things that go on here, like team bowling, and a few other things…this is just an information session to get everyone acquainted with everyone else” Scott explains. Eddie turns to Spider “This is supposed to help me in my match tomorrow, how?” Eddie asks. “This will get you in touch with that inner freak that you have, and to channel the fact that you are a freak into a tiny bitter ball, and release it upon all the participants in the match” Spider explains. “Uh…huh…This better be worth it” Eddie says as he has a seat. Spider grabs a coffee from a near by table, as Scott stands in the middle of the room with the other two freaks. Spider sits down, away from the circle. Scott begins to speak. “Alright, welcome everyone to our first ever meeting of Freaks Anonymous. Here is where we gather to sit in the midst of other freaks, away from the leering eyes of John Q. Public. Today, we will just go around the room, and introduce everyone to everybody. Just to get the ball rolling, my name is Scott Steiner, and I am a freak…It all started back when I was wrestling in WCW, and they wanted me to grow massively huge to contrast my brother and tag team partner, Rick. So, I took a serum that the U.S. Army, in co-operation with WCW were working on…virtually undetectable to standard drug tests. I took it, and in a few months…my muscles grew muscles. My peaks reached all time highs. Since all I care about are my freaks, and my peaks, let’s move on to you in the white.” Scott moves to away from the center, and the bandaged one stands up…slowly, but surely, he gains a vertical position. “Hi, my name is Jamie and I’m a freak…” “Hi, Jamie” The she-male, and Scott say, Eddie remains motionless in his chair. “…Well, hmm…where to begin…My full name is James Calloway. Yes, as in the real name of the Undertaker. I am his Real brother. Not that big ass that you see on the television. I was the inspiration for that character, and that mother fucker, Vince McMahon is making money off of my pain! The premise behind the character is true…when we were kids, Mark was a pyromaniac. He enjoyed fire, its warmth, its glow; the fact that is was a symbol of both life and death. His obsession carried him too far one day, and it cost me a good portion of my body, which still hurt even years after the incident. Because of my brother, I am now scarred for life…made this way, while he grows rich from endorsements. My hatred for him grows everyday he wrestles in a WWE ring. That’s about all of my pain that I wish to share…” “Dude, did you not know Stop, Drop, and Roll?” Eddie asks sarcastically. Spider just looks at Eddie with disgust. “Excuse me, but that is very ignorant, maybe you want to share your story?” Scott Asks in a perturbed tone of voice. “Fine, I will. My name is Eddie. I am a freak because of my mental stability. Of course I may not weight 300 pounds and have size Double-D breasts…or have acid burns on over seventy-five percent of my body…or I may not have a medieval BSDM fetish like our friend Scott…” Eddie turns to Scott for a brief moment… “Or maybe you can explain the chain-mail?” Eddie states as he turns away and continues. “Nonetheless, I don’t remember how exactly I became this way, but I’ve read the accident reports at my previous job, and I had a six by six timber fall on my head from twelve feet up. Since then, I then became someone…no…something different. My life changed dramatically…I would go to accident scenes and laugh at the victims. I would do heartless and sadistic things…things I would never normally do…but then, one day, I took my first life. I spread the parts of the body all over the country, and it kept going from there…more lives gone…with a simple flick of the wrist. It finally came down to me making money off of it…and I became a bounty hunter. For two years, I did the dirty work for employers both good, and evil. I stayed away from Terrorist bounties however; even I have ethics in regard to whom I get paid by. Then came PCW…and now I am here, and several people…mainly law enforcement and employer types have approached me and said, ‘you need to control yourself, you must learn calm’ and other things of the like. Going form a profession where being angry didn’t matter, to where it did is quite hard. However, seeing you people here…make me hate what I have become! Doug, let’s bail.” Eddie quickly walks out of the office, Spider looking up to see Eddie leaving. Spider drops the coffee and quickly follows. “Eddie, what the hell are you doing?” Spider requests an answer for. “Look, I just had an epiphany…I will go to my anger management session on Sunday. Reason being is that I won’t be even near the arena for the doctor to assess me. However, I know how to control it.” “You couldn’t control a television! Look…Wait…you said that you Want to go to the classes?” “Yeah, I am seeing that anger has consumed all those people in there, and they made themselves that way…I am not going on that route. Regardless of the receipts I owe Amulet, Green, Vurdo and the Dark Gimmick. All the other are simply Pylons! If I eliminate the biggest threats in that match…I may have a good shot! All you would have to do is trust me to it!” Spider looks out a nearby window, and sees a tractor trailer jack-knife at that split second he looked out the window. “I have a feeling that you’ll be eating my words through an I.V. later on that night, but I’ll leave you to your own devices for this one…do what you feel is best.” Spider accepts. “Alright….Extreme Title….HERE I COME!” Eddie exclaims as he bolts down the corridor. Spider stays behind for one moment… He better have a plan…otherwise the other wrestlers will eat him alive. Spider thinks to himself as he chases after Eddie down the corridor, as we end this adventure.
