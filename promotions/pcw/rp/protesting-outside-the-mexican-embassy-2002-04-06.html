Protesting outside the Mexican Embassy

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/4/02 [Scene opens outside the Mexican Embassy.The embassy itself is surrounded by a large black gate, which stretches right around the building to keep out unwanted visitors…. And protestors. Inside the big, black gates are half a dozen armed security guards.The Mexican guards march up, down and around the embassy keeping a watch for anything out of the ordinary. Flaymin Aymin suddenly approaches the main gates outside the Mexican Embassy.He looks at the big, black gate and begins to bang on it. A nearby security guard notices Flaymin and walks over to him] Guard: Can I help you? Flaymin: Is this the Mexican Embassy? Guard: That it is. Flaymin: Well can you open the damn gate? Guard: What is your business here? Flaymin: Protesting. Guard: Sorry Chico, you are going to have to protest from outside the gates. [Flaymin nods his head and shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: Alright, suit yourself. [The guard walks off and continues his lookout duties. Meanwhile, Flaymin zips open his bag.He pulls out one of those ringside-wrestling chairs and unfolds it. He places the chair directly in front of the main gate and sits down on it.Flaymin then pulls out a bottle of beer and the latest edition of ‘Tractors Monthly’ from his bag.Finally, Flaymin sits back and relaxes- reading his magazine and knocking down some booze. Time rolls by and Flaymin sits quietly in front of the main gate, while the security guards inside the gate keep a watchful eye on him. After a while a small kid approaches the main gate and tries to get past Flaymin. Flaymin stretches out his arm and pushes the kid backwards] Flaymin: Yeah??? Kid: What? Flaymin: The Mexican Embassy has been closed off until further notice. Kid: I don’t care, I’m just here to….. Flaymin: I bet you wanna know why. Kid: Uh no. Flaymin: Well I’ll tell you why! El Diablo a.k.a The Midge Mexican Moron is a menace to society.I believe it is my duty keep out other Mexican people who are a threat to the livelihood of my afro.I just can’t keep letting Mexican wrestlers shave my hair off you know.El Diablo must be stopped and more Mexicans in this country is not the answer, thus it is my mission to guard the Mexican Embassy from all Mexicans wishing to enter the country. Kid: If you’re trying to keep Mexicans OUT of America, shouldn’t you be guarding the Mexican BORDER and not the EMBASSY? Flaymin: Eh.The border is too far away and I’ve got better things to do then wave away incoming Mexicans aaaaaaaaall the way down south in the middle of the dessert. Kid: That sounds fascinating.But I’m only here to deliver lunch to my da… Flaymin: Gimme that bag!! [Flaymin swipes the paper bag from the little kid.He sticks his hand into it and pulls out a sandwich. Flaymin spins the sandwich around, analysing its features.Finally he looks at the kid and grunts] Flaymin: Tuna.Blah. [Flaymin unwraps the tuna sandwich and takes a big bite.He chomps on the sandwich and then winces in pain at the aftertaste of the bad sandwich] Kid: Aren’t you going to give it to my dad? Flaymin: Forget your dad.I’m surprised there isn’t a ‘Toxic Waste’ label on this crap. [The little kid begins to whimper] Kid: But it was my dad’s sandwich! Flaymin: Look kid, does your dad like finely processed food? Kid: I guess so. Flaymin: Good. In that case I’ll vomit the sandwich all over him later today. Heck, the vomit will probably taste better then the sandwich….. and smell better too. [The kid begins to cry uncontrollably.He then storms off weeping and howling loudly. Flaymin sits back in his chair and picks up his Tractor magazine and continues to read it. But before he can get halfway through the article on ‘Turning your Petrol Tank into a Beer Keg’ a dirty man in raggedy clothes approaches Flaymin. Flaymin looks up from his magazine and raises an eyebrow. The man staggers closer to Flaymin before he suddenly faints and passes out on the floor. Flaymin looks at the passed out man and gently kicks him in the ribs] Flaymin: Out of all the places to die, he had to go kaput in front of me. [Flaymin starts banging on the big, black metal gate behind him to get the attention of the guards. The guard hears the banging and approaches Flaymin] Flaymin: Hey buddy, call a hearse….. or an ambulance…. or a forklift.Whatever you do, just get this stinking carcass out of my face. [The guard looks at the body of the man and shakes his head.He then turns back around and continues his guarding duties] Flaymin: Cruel, heartless bastard. [Suddenly the passed out man on the floor begins to move.Flaymin’s eyes grow wide and he clumsily falls off his chair] Flaymin: What the hell?!?! [The tired, haggard man slowly crawls across the floor to get near Flaymin. Once close enough, he opens his mouth and struggles to let out a few words to Flaymin] Man: Please….help me. Flaymin: Do I look like a member of the Red Cross? Man: Please…. I must get into the embassy. Flaymin: Yeah? Man: My family…. They are being deported…. I need to speak to the ….consulate inside…. the embassy. Flaymin: Gee, I’d love to help you but….. no. [The exhausted man looks at Flaymin with a questioning look on his face] Man: What do you…mean? Flaymin: No Mexicans get in.No Mexicans get out. Man: But my family… they are being deported! Flaymin: Here’s a half-eaten Tuna Sandwich, it should keep you busy until you get back to Mexico. Man: But… my family!! Flaymin: Yeah we’ve all got family, but you really should consider yourself lucky. Man: You will let me into the embassy? Flaymin: No, I don’t give Tuna Sandwiches to just any homeless bum.You should be proud of yourself. [Flaymin proudly nods his head in approval. Without an ounce of energy remaining, the man just slumps back on to the floor, unable to move] Flaymin: Get up you lazy hobo, you’re family is being deported!! [The man remains fainted on the floor. Flaymin looks at the body and shrugs his shoulders. He then gets up out of his chair and shoves the half-eaten Tuna sandwich into the mouth of the passed out man] Flaymin: This should stop him from dying. [Flaymin reaches into his bag and pulls out another bottle of beer.He inserts the top of the bottle into his mouth and violently rips off the bottle cap with his teeth. He spits out the bottle cap and begins to chug down the beer. Before he can finish half the bottle, three men in dark suits approach Flaymin.Two of the men are quite large and are wearing dark sunglasses with earpieces connected to their…um ears. The third man, a middle aged, Latino looking man with slicked back hair advances towards Flaymin. The three men look at the passed out body of the man on the floor with the tuna sandwich in his mouth. The middle-aged Latino man points at the body and looks at Flaymin] Latino Man: Who is this? Flaymin: I dunno. Who the hell are you? Latino Man: I am the Mexican Ambassador here on a goodwill visit. Flaymin: Yeah?Do you wanna Tuna Sandwich? Latino Man: Tuna… Sandwich? Flaymin: You’ll have to stick your hands into the mouth of the hobo and wipe off some saliva but other then that I’m sure you’ll enjoy it. [The Latino Man gives Flaymin a strange stare] Latino: Uh…. no.A Tuna Sandwich does not interest me. Flaymin: Well it’s not going anywhere in case you change your mind. [Flaymin picks up his Tractor Magazine and continues to read from it while drinking the beer in his hand] Latino Man: Excuse me senor, but you are blocking off the main entrance into the embassy. Flaymin: I know. Latino Man: Would you please move? Flaymin: I really would love to let you into the embassy buuuuuuut I couldn’t give two flying f*cks. Latino Man: Excuse me? Flaymin: The embassy is closed until further notice.Thankyou for your concern and I hope you have a great day. [Flaymin puts on a cheesy smile and waves his hand while the Mexican Ambassador frowns in retaliation] Latino Man: What is your official duty here today? Flaymin: ‘Mexican-Keeper-Outer-Guy’ [Unimpressed, the Mexican Ambassador offers Flaymin a stern warning] Latino Man: I will offer you one final opportunity to move out of the way. Flaymin: NO, I’m offering YOU one final opportunity to get out of my way- you’re blocking the damn sun!!How is a man supposed to get a tan when a greasy ambassador is blocking your view? [The ambassador looks at his two bodyguards and nods his head. Without hesitation, the two bodyguards grab Flaymin by his shirt and toss him out of the way. Flaymin gets up off the floor and charges back at the security guards, tackling both of them to the ground as the scene fades to black…..]
