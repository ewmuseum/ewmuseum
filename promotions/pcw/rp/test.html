Date: not entered Déjà vu. Rain dripped down Violence Jack’s worn visage, into his eyes and the curledcorners of his mouth. Before him was situated the largest cathedral inPhiladelphia, and potentially his last source for answers. At his side,shielded from the rain by an umbrella, stood Jared Solomon and Chris Havenstorm. Havenstorm cast a somber gaze to the still and silent Jack. “You better get this over with, VJ,” stated Havenstorm. “Don’t take that damnedMexican lightly……look where it got me.” This comment initially garnered no response from his partner, but after severalseconds an eerie smirk crossed the ruffian’s face. “Nah, I ain’t worryin’ about it. You’re just incompetent, Chris. Heheh.” Havenstorm had learned to read to Jack’s expressions and actions, and it wasobvious the sarcastic veteran’s remarks were in jest. “Quite a pity we never got the opportunity to decide which of us is the betterman, VJ. But I think we all know it’s pretty rhetorical….” “The only thing rhetorical here is how you always choke when the pressure’s on. HAHAHA!” Havenstorm couldn’t help but crack a sheepish grin in light of the wisecrack. All the same, at least Jack was in a joking mood. As of late his personalityhad been quite volatile and his reactions were unusually impulsive….even forhim.It was obvious Jack’s psyche was ravaged, as in the midst of one of his ‘spells’he had even told Chris he felt he deserved restitution of some nature from themedical community or otherwise. Hell maybe the church should be obligated toreimburse him for his recent spiritual anguish, or so he thought. In all theirtime as a duo, he had never heard his partner entertain such a peculiar theory. Due in part to the fact Jack had always been what one would define as a heathen,and the very mention of a higher deity often brought a scowl to his face and acurse from his mouth.Arguably his in-ring game hadn’t suffered as much as anticipated, but even sohis normally colossal morale remained intact only as result of hisever-increasing streak of victories. Pila hadn’t even shown up to the arena forthe first round, and thankfully Anton had put up a credible resistance beforefinally proving unable to handle VJ’s nearly inhuman stamina and break his loftythreshold of pain. Drake had suffered a similar fate to that of the PCWCruiserweight champion. But if he failed this weekend and resolution continuedto elude him, one could only guess the effect it would inflict. “Marvelous ain’t it?” spouted Jack with rain trickling down his left cheek,interrupting Havenstorm’s soliloquy. “Just a few weeks ago we surged into theopening round of this thing claiming we’d run roughshod over the competition,and every man in the company laughed at us. They called us loons, egomaniacs,brash two-bit ‘indy’ worms. Look now, huh? Saturday another one bites thedust. I get to stamp out a Primetime Central icon!” He turned on his outer foot to face his escort duo, his jacket and hat soaked inthe storm. A clap of thunder rumbled ominously in the distance, a flash oflightning illuminating the heavens above and the surrounding street amidst thedark of early night. The downpour further intensified, as the umbrella-shroudedduo could now barely distinguish the silhouette of their comrade through thedriving rain. “We have made our mark, Chris! We have stepped up to the f*ckin’ plate andeliminated everything those corporate fools have tossed in our path! Ya thinkI’m goin’ to go cower under some damn rock like the rest of this f*ckin’establishment because I’m against some past-his-prime PCW megastar?! Do ya?!” Havenstorm twisted his head to gaze at Solomon, whose eyes remained set on theforeboding shadow before them. “Dammit Havenstorm, I’m f*ckin’ talking to you!” Lips pursed, the larger of the infamous Cruel Intentions tandem slowly rotateshis head and eyes in the direction of his outraged compatriot, who held aclenched fist raised to jaw-level. “I’m in this damn contest to make a statement! For years I’ve busted my ass,destroyed wannabe after wannabe, and I never got so much as a godd*mn inkling ofreceivin’ any credit or respect! I wander into Primetime Central, hopin’ forsome honest competition! And ya know what I find?!” Through the rain Solomon and Havenstorm can distinguish the quick, erraticbreathing of Jack as his rage overflows yet again, his tone baleful. “I find the same bush league misfits and dismal rejects I’ve been destroyin’for years! I’ve given these freaks a glimpse into the macabre and twisted worldthat I live and crushed their disposable heroes like soda cans! And still Iget no respect!” “Jack!” interjected Havenstorm, in attempt to quell his partner’s wild fury.“Let it go! These things take time!” “I’ve given those motherf*ckers time!” snarled Jack with sufficient force tocause even his long-time ally to flinch. “If patience is a virtue, I’m a saint! How many icons do I gotta conquer?! How many of their coveted champions must Idemolish?! It’s absurd!” Chris raised an arm and spread his palm outward as a gesture to calm his partnerdown. “You’ll have that respect when you win this competition, Jack! Calm down!” “Don’t you get it?!” bellowed the infuriated VJ. “There is no f*ckin’retribution for me in this life! No matter what I do, who I maim, how manygolden straps I carry on my shoulder….I can’t win outside the ring!” “That’s bullsh*t!” erupted Havenstorm, charging out from beneath the umbrellainto the drenching rain and facing down his partner who trembled withirreparable ire. “I suffered through the same existence you did! Jack, we’renot living under that sort of oppression anymore! You’ve been recognized timeand again, beaten the best in our profession and been involved in some of themost epic battles this side of Normandy Beach! You’re being delusional!” “F*ck you! You don’t know me, Havenstorm! I saw my best friends gunned downright beside me when I was seventeen, and by some celestial act of Godly scornthose very same bullets missed ME! That’s not salvation, that’s punishment! You’re in this business because you wanted to be….for me it was either fight in a ring and get paid for it or fight on the street just to make it through another wretched day! And the only reason I didn’t give in is because I didn’t want to give the satisfaction of my death toaddicts and gangsters!” “How’s this any different?” shouted Chris in retort. “How were those addicts andgangsters any different from these punks in the tournament? Are you going tolet them beat you? Are you going to just let them into your head and beat you before they even have the chance to do it in the ring?” “No one is going to beat me!” yelled Jack. “NO ONE!” He raised a fist and drew back, prepared to unleash his now-brimming rage on his astounded partner. He swing is wild, striking but thin air as Havenstorm sidesteps the attack. Faintly, he detected a blow strike the back of the head, viewed Chris’s perturbed face through the raging downpour, and all went black. * * * “Ugh…what the hell?.....” His head reverberated with a throbbing ache, the world still in a semi-cloudyhaze. He was still wet from the rainstorm and his clothes clung to his skin,but he now found himself in some well-furnished office adorned with seeminglypriceless old relics, a trio of majestic oil paintings and decorations ofreligious significance about the brown walls and wooden cabinets. Atop the deskon the side of the room was a bronze chalice (seemingly for decoration), withpictures of various Biblical figures setup as one would do with family pictures. Hanging over the chair behind the desk was a painting resembling that of the onein Father Craven’s office, depicting the image of Christ preaching in the Middle East. As Jack slid his feet off the couch he was sprawled across, the door on the eastside of the room creaked open. Emerging forth was a man in the traditionalrobes of the clergy, a glass of water in hand. Taking notice of Jack’sconsciousness, he expressed a sincere smile and a brief nod of the head. Hiseyes were a light blue, kind and genuine with a keen wisdom portrayedwithin. “Ah,” he chuckled, “it seems our ‘patient’ has awakened.” “Where am I?” mumbled Jack, clutching at the back of his head with a grimace. “St. Joseph’s Cathedral,” the clergyman responded, passing the glass to VJ.“Your friends, Mr. Havenstorm and Mr. Solomon, brought you in.” “Oh man….” murmured Jack, recalling the brawl that had nearly erupted previousto his falling unconscious. “My anger….” “Your friends asked me to relay their sorrow for what occured. You were quiteout of control, and had become irrational. When you attacked Mr. Havenstorm,your other friend snuck behind you and used the hilt of his umbrella to knockyou unconscious, sheerly for your own protection. They carried you into this church and then into my chamber per my request.” Jack brought the glass to his lips, the liquid coursing down his throat in parched gulps. The clergyman echoed a hearty laugh. “HAHA!! For a man drenched in rainwater, you certainly are thirsty!” Jack deposited the empty glass on the desk, tongue passing over his lips to absorb the moisture. “Well it sure ain’t a priority of mine to drink any of this Philly rain!” he cackled heartily. “This town’s worse than Boston….I’m surprised the stuff didn’t disintegrate me!” The clergyman cracked a mild grin. “Well I’m not one to dispute that this fair city has a problem with air pollution…..but by the same token it has been too kind to me to warrant any direct insults.” VJ pulled himself up with a grunt, strolling to the ample desk and withdrawing a small portrait of Joseph to look upon. The minister took pleasure at seeing the inquisitive gleam in his guest’s eyes. “Joseph, Mr. Shanahan. Earthly father to our Savior, what a glorious title! Even the most ordinary citizens can do incredible things when the Lord decrees it. As the Good Book says……‘The meek shall inherit the Earth.’ “You believe that?” inquired Jack in a questioning skeptical tone, refusing to elevate his eyes from the picture. “I do not question the word of God,” came the reply in a matter-of-fact air of certainty. “It is not my position to do so. He has blessed me time and again, and for that I will infer Him no offense.“Bruce, do you know what the shortest verse in the Good Book is?” No, Reggie, I don’t. I’m not a holy man, and never have been in all honesty…..” “ ‘Jesus wept.’ It is the shortest verse in the entire Holy Bible, Bruce, but italso has among the deepest bearing of any phrase in the entire book. JesusChrist, the Son, was a motivator. He preached the gospel at a time when suchactivity brought persecution. He found a lesson in every situation, be itnegative or positive. But there came a time when even the Son of God wasappalled by the state of affairs surrounding him. He was aghast by the sin of man, their lack of faith in him following the death of Lazarus. He broke down and cried….’Jesus wept’, John 11:35. VJ replaced the picture in its original stand before raising his eyes, with head still drooped, to meet gazes with the priest. “I truly apologize for any inconvenience, father,” he said ruefully. “But I’m here for a reason.” “And what would that be, my son?” “I have come to meet with Bishop McCarver. I was told he could help me with some rather strange dilemma I’m locked in…..” “Say no more, Mr. Shanahan,” replies the kindly old pastor. “Your search for answers ends here…..or so I hope.” With a smile he props one arm atop the desk, lowering his finger to point to a plaque resting on the edge of the desk. Bishop Reginald McCarver VJ lifted his stare to look upon the concerned countenance of the man he had searched for. He felt a wave of shame, cursing his indecent behavior and lack of faith in front of a well-respected clergyman. “I am sincerely sorry, Bishop McCarver,” Jack uttered in contrite discourse. “I can’t imagine what you must think of me right now…..” “Poppy-cock!” snickered the jovial church elder. “In all honestly, I was quite pleased when I heard a man in your situation chose the Church as his source of comfort. It beats all the artificial relief issued by those wackos in the medical community. Sometimes things happen science cannot explain, and those incredulous cynics dismiss anything their glorious ‘wonder drugs’ fail to alleviate. I am like a shepard trusted with tending the flock of the Lord….and it delights me to see one of those sheep returning from a disobedient absence.” Taking heed of the relaxed smile across the lips of his visitor, the bishop shrugged. “And call me Reggie. No formalities are necessary in this office.” He spun on his revolving chair, drawing a key from a drawer near the base of the desk. He stood up and drew back the portrait of the sermonizing Christ, revealing the contours of a metallic safe. He brought up the bronze key and gave it a twist in the lock, a click confirming its fit and the door swinging ajar. VJ made out a short stack of documents and manuscripts, with a small collection of hardback novels lined against the rear wall. The bishop leaned in and deftly removed one of the shadow-veiled volumes, pivoting to place it atop his desk and revealing the title for his guest’s eyes to observe. It was one Jack had heard of, yet never even believed existed. And even in light of his little knowledge, a shiver ran down his spine. The Necronomicon “I’ve heard of that thing,” muttered Jack as the bishop dropped the forbidden tome on a manilla folder atop his study table. “Holds magic spells to wake the dead and I heard it was written in blood and covered in skin.” His statement was met with a fervent shake of the head. “Bound in human flesh, inked in mortal blood…..ah, the touch of Hollywood. ‘Tisnothing more, Bruce. In reality, this unholy tome was written by a man namedAbdul Alhazred, also called the ‘Mad Arab’. And was done so in nothing morethan parchment and normal ink.But that doesn’t take away from its truly blasphemous nature, my son. It’sexistence was sheer speculation until its discovery just over a hundredyears ago…..it was said to contain the secrets to performing unholy ritualsdirected at resurrecting the dead and conjuring slumbering demons and devils. But upon its excavation, there was no outward sign of that being the case. It appeared to be a historybook, a record of evil deeds and arcane diagrams with no real relevance. Disturbing yet harmless. We, the Church, acquired a copy and had it wholly translated. And our fears were confirmed.” Bishop McCarver clasped his hands, extending both forefingers and placing themto his lips. “On the outward appearance, it’s nothing but a collection of irreligious storiesand accounts of evil rituals. But our scholars who have delved further into itsmeaning have found references much more sinister than anything previouslyconsidered. There are metaphors and references to other accursed volumes thatwould aid in fleshing out the significance of particular passages that on thesurface appear to be mere babble. We discovered a cryptic code…..one thatsubstantiates all former beliefs of it being a ‘spell-book of the damned’, so tosay….” “Pardon the pun, Reggie,” said Jack, “but you’re scaring the Hell outta me…” “Heheheh. That is my intention.” The bishop smirked casually, somewhat putting the Boston resident at ease. “There exists a theory, Bruce,” continued McCarver without pause. “One said tobe condemned by the vast majority of the holy church. But it’s one many of ushave started to consider in light of the rampant decline of morals in thiscrumbling society…..and other events not often made public. This theory statesthat humanity is nothing but a blundering race of Johnny-come-latelys whoconstantly stumble amidst the terrible secrets of unknown Ancients…..Ancientswho once dwelled upon this Earth with all the power of Gods. And some arguethese beings still slumber in a state of torpor in ancient cities in theuncharted depths of the oceans. And only mankind’s ignorance keeps them fromrealizing what exactly they have discovered.” McCarver’s upper lip curled as he slowly paced to the opposite corner of theroom. Jack placed a hand on the black leather cover of the tome, practically feeling the evil of its contents radiate outward. “Father Craven informed me these beliefs are outlawed in the Church, Reggie.” “Ah yes, of course…..Father Craven’s statement that the Church denounces the belief. I bet it sounded similar to ‘My very entertaining of this hypothesis is one thatcould get me banished from the priesthood…..belief in such a far-fetchedphilosophy is taboo in the church.’ True?” Jack replied with a fleeting nod. “He told you that because our condemnation of the belief is something of anofficial cover……an issue so confidential that even my own announcing this to you is grounds for reprimand, but as I see it this is obviously an exceptional case. In reality the Vatican does uphold a similar belief, Bruce, even though they publicly disavow the very notion that a cult worshipping such a ‘fairy tale’ subsists in modern society. But considerthis: how would you feel if the premier force of spiritual guidance on earth,the Pope, came out and acknowledged the existence of yet another deity in asimilar category as the Lord and the Beast? Most people, even the most loyalbelievers, would dismiss it. They would call John Paul and the currentadministration crazy. Even the church is engaged in politics, Bruce, and hasfor centuries. It’s simply bad publicity for such a vital piece of informationto be recognized out of the blue. Elder Gods existing beyond the stars andoutside the confines of our own universe, warring in an eternal jihad with thelikes of Lucifer and the Lord, Our God? Add to that at this moment, the idea oftheir having an ‘agent’ currently in a millennia-old state of torpor at thebottom of some unknown ocean trench just waiting to be revived? As I said, evenfor the most devoted of followers it would be a bitter pill to swallow. Especially with a case like yours. This very copy of the wretched Necronomiconwas delivered to me from the Vatican itself. Oh yes, my son, they acknowledgethe reality of this unpopular theory……but they do so only in the shadows andoutside the public eye. It’s similar to the exorcisms that John Paul wasrevealed to have performed in recent times. He was the subject of a great dealof public ridicule, but the fact of the matter is that he did what needed to bedone.” “What do you mean I’m an ‘exceptional case’?” Bishop McCarver tilted back in his cahir, resting his folded hands behind his head as a make-shift pillow. “You’ve been blessed…..or should I say, cursed…..by your visions. It’shappened, Bruce, such testimonies have been documented in the annals of theVatican. Sometimes common men become…..how should I say this, um…..’mediums’. Mediums on behalf of those ancient sleeping Gods. You channel their desires anddreams as sort of a vessel. All proverbially of course, it’s obvious thosewrestlers in your dreams aren’t currently hanging crucified and eviscerated inthe halls of some vile cathedral of the depraved.What makes yours even more unique is that you appear to be particularlytargeted, and by just a minion no less. And thankfully, by a lesser minion.” “Lesser?” “There are levels of demons, my son. And no powerful high-ranking demon, or‘vampire’ in this case, would resort to focusing his efforts on a professionalwrestling organization. He’s the equivalent of a child…..old yes, but a far cryfrom an elder or antediluvian. Because of that he is beatable.” “He doesn’t serve any “Elder God’, Reggie. He claims to be a disciple of Satan as I recall…..so does that giant ex-apprentice of his, Hessian.” “He may not even know who he truly serves. He may believe it to be himself, someunnamed sire, or as you say, Lucifer. The only reason I am skeptical to believe his service lies to that of Satan is the content of your dreams. Speaking with Father Craven about the descriptions you gave on the symbols present throughout your revelations, I’m apt to believe otherwise. They match glyphs and idols found in that despicable tome you see on my desk, ones used in cult rituals for the purpose of resuscitating their foreign gods.A common belief is that the Beast himself was taught his ways by these Elder Gods….and the fallen archangel only rebelled against his master due to the conniving wishes of these extraneous deities. That particular notion the Church does not believe, but there are ‘conspiracy theorists’ claiming this as the truth.” “Regardess of all that,” interposed Jack, “I imagine there’s no theory on how do to destroy a ‘minion’ who dabbles in professional wrestling…..” McCarver chuckled at the pun. “There is no difference than any other demon. Unfortunately I fear thisNosferatu character had gorged on many souls already. You don’t need to destroy him in a literal sense, as this is a vastly different forum than is typical in spiritual warfare…..just defeat him in some manner. And considering professional wrestling is the trade in question, do so in that particular forum. And I believe to do so, you must get by this ‘White Mexican’ fellow.” Jack nods, that particular reality finally registering. It was something he haddisregarded in light of this recent conflict. “I am not what one would call a scholar on the subject of professionalwrestling. But I think it goes without saying, as it does in every othercompetitive sport and activity, it would be unwise to look past this Mexicangentleman….” “Gentleman?” scoffs Jack with an outward display of amusement. “White Mexican? The guy’s a lot of things, but he ain’t no gentleman!” “Just the same,” continued McCarver, “I feel it is rash to focus on Vampir, oranywhere at all beyond this current contest. I spoke this morning with FatherCraven on your situation, and he informed me your cohort Christopher Havenstormalso neglected to focus on this ‘White Mexican’ and faced a setback. I fearyou’re the only one still standing in this competition of yours who realizeswhat’s going on. And on the outset, it seems this ‘Nosferatu’ looks at you ashaving a bulls-eye on your chest.” “I’m used to it,” mumbles Jack, hands on hips and gazing somberly out the windowas the rain patters on the glass pane. “He won’t be the first who’s made it apriority to knock me off.” “It appears you’re focused, Bruce. Father Craven also had informed me you’requite the accomplished wrestler…..hopefully you can persevere through thissituation and regain your life back, so to say. I fear there is little I can dobeyond offering my encouragement and condolences for your current state ofaffairs. Please realize however that the Lord Our God is forever watching, andhe will do all he can to protect and safeguard you.” Jack cracked his knuckles and stood, shaking the hand of the bishop. “God watch over you,” replied McCarver with a warn and candid smile. “Anytime you feel you need guidance, do not hesitate to let me know. And if you want, we shall say a short prayer together….” VJ, appreciating the gratitude of the venerable Catholic clergyman, returned the handshake firmly and with a grin of his own. With that, the Boston native left the office of the bishop and rejoined his comrades for the travel back to his hometown, and final preparation for the upcoming match with White Mexican. * * * March 22, 2002 Entry 9 My mind some sort of holy battleground?! I had hoped my visit to Bishop McCarver would bring solace; instead I learn I’ve ‘graduated’ to becoming the pawn of some jihad lasting before human beings were on the same evolutionary level as amoebas! For now I focus on the task closer at hand; I must concentrate my efforts on White Mexican. This has become more than a quest for respect and my own redemption. I feel as if I’m battling on behalf of every agonized soul fallen in this tournament. Vampir’s victory last year, and present hold on the belt we all have fought (and most perished) for, have presented him with the status of overseer. The fate of all those vanquished in this contest lie in his hands. He must be defeated. White Mexican…..ah, with my attention now rediverted on what must be done and the ante upped even further, I have become infinitely more dangerous. I have little more to say on the subject than that. Mexican is a fallen star in the Primetime Central empire. He is a joke with an expired 15 minutes of fame which continues to this moment on borrowed time. Tomorrow night, I put a stop to it. The bar of excellence is about to lifted in this decrepit company. And it is going to be elevated by the bastard son of Boston Massachusetts. Lord, giveth me the strength to crush the opposition escalating before me, and layeth upon me the valor to triumph in the face of all trepidation. Let the fire in my eyes light my way, and let the bridges I burn in my wake destroy my adversaries. I have lived a life of self-centeredness, but now the time has come to fight for something more significant. Layeth thy hands upon me, Father, and grant me with thy omnipotent strength. I have sinned, Father. And again, so shall be done tomorrow night. Forgive me. But it must be done…..and for that, I believe no forgiveness is necessary….
