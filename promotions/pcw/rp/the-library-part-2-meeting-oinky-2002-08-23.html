The Library - Part 2 - Meeting Oinky

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 23 [Ghosts are totally weird. Are they haunting you because they don't like you, or do they want you to do stuff for them? It's such a bogus way to not know what is up with a ghost. Like that little compadre in the Sixth Sense. The ghosts just scared the crap out of him and made him feel most heinous about the situation. With time and the help of that gnarly dude, Bruce Willis, our little compadre was able to control the bogus ghosts before his eyes and help them out. It was a most happy ending, to a most BODACIOUS movie] [In the Los Angeles Public Library it's become quite a different story. A stress filled librarian steals two pigs in the dead of the night, and then proceeds to eat them alive. Nobody knows exactly why she did this or why she would just pignap some pigs and eat them. Most people wonder why she didn't just go to the supermarket down the street and get some bacon, but this tormented librarian went 50 miles out to a farm to steal pigs. I guess sometimes you have to go to the source yourself to get good eating anymore] [The Cowabunga Connection has decided to turn over a new leaf, and that's study. The Tubular One knew that studying their newly acquired opponents, Makonie and Duzza, that they could maybe get a jump on them. The two men learned that Makonie was some totally bogus Nazi dude who didn't like black people. With a quick trip to the library the duo learned so many bogus things about Nazi's and the horrible things that they have done in the past. Things got heated in the library with Tommy and the cute librarian. In fact, it got quite intimate between the two. That's when Harry saw something...something so evil and frightening, that he ran....] [Harry, dressed in his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots, runs through the aisles of books trying his best to dodge the evil he saw earlier. Those red eyes pierced his heart, as if they were telling him that he was going to die. It was a ghost coming back for some revenge and Harry was it's primary target] Harry: Running sucks, man. [Harry finds a cart full of books and tosses them off the cart. He then lies down underneath the cart, covering himself with books hoping that they will hide him from this vile beast] Harry: I'll blend in, man. [Harry sees the very tall, thin woman round the corner with a butcher knife in hand. Her dark red eyes scan the dark aisles, searching for her prey. Her brown hair is up in a tight bun, her face is extremely thin and wrinkly...like she had just risen from the grave. Her floral print dress is torn in some places and there is blood dripping from her mouth. Her shoulders hunch over as her head snaps back and forth, scanning the area. Harry's eyes grow in fear. His breathing becomes rapid, as he tries to control it as not to give away his far out hiding spot. The librarian beast stands in front of the cart, with Harry only able to see the librarian's legs] Evil Librarian: Rrrrr.... [Her saliva sort of gurgles along with the roar. Harry is trying to do his best to keep his body from just shaking in fear. He places slowly lets a book slide over his face, only seeing a picture of a long haired man being impaled on a stake. Wait...that dude kind of looks like him? That's weird] Harry: Huh, man? Evil Librarian: GRAW!!!!! [The disgusting decomposing face of the librarian is only inches from Harry as she grabs at him. Harry immediately rolls from out of the cart and begins running again, but this time the creature is a lot closer. The woman has the butcher knife extended only a few feet away from PCW's resident stoner] Harry: I'm not a pig, man!!! Evil Librarian: RAH!!! Harry: I'll take that as a 'Shut Up', man. [Harry quickly uses lightning fast speed to stop himself and juke toward the fiction aisle. The evil librarian keeps going straight, losing Harry for enough time so he can get distance in between he and the psycho. Harry hides is a dark space, able to see most of the area, but nothing really able to see him] Harry: This is not far out, man..... [He breathes heavily, trying to get the air to his lungs] Harry: Why would some crazy dead librarian chick want to slice me up, man? What did I ever do? [Harry begins thinking of all the bogus things that he has done in his life. His childhood memories come back to him faster than he thought that they would. Third grade comes to mind and so does Rebecca Green. He liked her a lot and ended up shaving her head into a mohawk to show his appreciation for her. Unfortunately she cried nonstop and ended up moving to the east coast] Harry: That wasn't that bad, man....Just a mohawk. [He then nods in agreement with himself when eighth grade comes to mind. Gary Stacy really wasn't a popular dude, not in the least. The dude wore rainbow suspenders, Teddy Ruxbin T-shirts, and played a lot of Dungeons and Dragons. Harry put fast drying glue in his underwear during gym class, and ol' Gary ended up with underwear glued to his package. Talk about TOTALLY BOGUS!! Gary got it surgically removed, but it took a few...ahem...inches off] Harry: Ouch, man....that was not far out. I'll have to apologize. Evil Librarian: GRAW!! [Harry flies backward, smashing through a door as the Librarian swings down from the ceiling like some kind of super villain. Harry stumbles down a long staircase, his body smashing against the walls, and finally making a giant thud at the bottom.....] MEANWHILE.... [The Tubular One and his newly found babe, Valerie, finish their kiss. The loud sound of someone falling down the steps is heard and it startles to couple] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: What was that? Valerie: It was probably the ghost in the cellar. [Tommy has a look of fear on his face as he remembers the myth of that bogus libarian lady] TTT: Is it that evil librarian babe? Valerie: NO!! That is just some stupid story that the locals around here made up. I've worked here for years and I was told nothing of that sort ever occured. But pigs did get slaughtered here, and those are the ghosts that I'm talking about. [Tommy looks more confused then scared] TTT: There can be pig ghosts? Valerie: Yeah... TTT: GNARLY! [Valerie wears a very sly smile on her face, as she rubs her hands on Tommy's muscular chest] Valerie: There is a back room, nobody knows about it except me. We can go back there and you can show me exactly how that YAHTZEE is done. [Tommy smiles and looks all around to find that Harry is missing from his chair. Tommy's mind goes back to that loud noise he heard coming from the distance. That could have been Harry!] TTT: Where did Harry go? Valerie: I don't know. He'll be fine. He probably just went outside to leave us alone! See what a good friend you have! [Tommy smiles brightly and nods] TTT: Yes, he is most bodacious in a far out way. It's time to go and get some things out of my system before this gnarly match at Third Dawning. [The two begin their way toward the back room as Valerie has pure excitement on her face] Valerie: Trust me, your going to get A LOT out of your system, Tommy. TTT: Tubular.... IN THE BASEMENT [Harry pushes himself to his feet, shaking the cobwebs out. His eyes are a bit more blurry than usual, and his double vision is at an all time high. The basement isn't even really that large, especially for an extremely well known library. The are many generators and ventilation systems that line the basement. Books scattered about window sills and stacked on desks. I guess these are the forgotten books that nobody wants to read] Voice: OINK!!! OINK!!! [Harry looks confused as he can hear the distant oinking of a pig. What in the name of Judas Priest would a pig be doing here? It's a library basement, not a farm] Voice: OINK OINK!! [The sound is getting louder and closer to him. But there are no footsteps, or pig steps in this case. Just the eeire echo of oinking] Harry: Hey pig, man. Voice: OINK! [Harry kind of laughs to himself] Harry: The pig answered me, man. Are you into Twisted Sister, man? Voice: OINK!! OINK OINK! Harry: FAR OUT, MAN! [Harry laughs and begins looking through the books. 'Catcher in the Rye'. Harry remembers having to read that book in high school, and how he used his copy to smash spiders with] Harry: I've got my spider killer back, man. [Harry places to book in his pocket with a hazy smile across his face] Voice: OOIIINNKKKK!!! [Harry turns around to see a giant pig face right in front of his. He leaps backward, slamming into a pile of books. He decides to hide underneat the books, hoping that they'll cover him up and the evil pig face will go away. He peeks out and it's not like an ordinary pig...it's a pig but it looks strange. It looks like a human being dressed up in a pig suit. It's just standing there Oinking like a crazy person] Harry: STAY AWAY FROM ME, MAN!! Pig: OINK!!! Harry: I mean it! Pig: OINK OINK!! Harry: I'll make bacon out of you, man! Pig: OINK!!! [Harry slowly climbs out of the book pile and gets closer to the pig. He holds a hand out to show that he means no harm toward it, even though he just threatened to make bacon out of it] Harry: This is really weird to me, man. I've never really been attacked by a dude in a pig costume, man. Pig: OINK!! [Harry looks shocked for a moment when he realizes that he can understand the Oinking. He hears an Oink, but for some reason in his mind it comes out as perfect English to him] Pig: OINK! (The lady is coming!) Harry: I know, man. She was chasing me down the stairs earlier. (OINK OINK!), man. Pig: OINK! (Your lucky she didn't rip open your stomach and start eating your intestines) Harry: Yeah, man. (Oink, man) [The stairs begin creeking loudly as the tall, thin woman makes her way into the basement. The butcher knife is drawn and her shadow seems 150 feet high. Harry and the pig hold onto each other, hoping that each others comfort will save them] Harry: I love you, man. (OINK, man) Pig: OINK!! (I love you too) [The knife slams down, and there is blood everywhere!] Fade to Black
