Date: again.... | I awoke to inhale nothing but weed smoke reigning in thick grey clouds which blood shotted my eyes. Followed by a cough and almost suffocation, it all outro-luded after getting use to it. Thin rays of light floated in a dim line across the moving intoxicating fog with a wooden fan spinning slowly above. | 'Wake da fuck up! Don't you kno' we goin' to Big momma's funeral today?!' Dressed down in her best sunday dress; black see through top matching velvet black pants with black high heels topping it off of course with the over sized sunday hat, Buuday brought to my presence. 'Wut da fuck u talkin' about? I don't kno' no damn Big Momma.' Either that, or the weed was fucking up my mind. 'I got some business to handle anywayz, ya'll go off wit out me.' Turning my head away from Buuday to not be turned to stone by the ugly, evil looking she would have gave me, surprisingly, 'Oh ok,' with such innocence came out after an exit from the room. | Something wasn't right... I should have been thinking about that, but the allowance for my closed-attention would pay me back in full later... | *1 Hour Later* | Nice beautiful flowers draped over a elegantly curved coffin lies ahead of several rows of benched filled with family members made widows, feeling lonly without the one they called 'Big Momma.' The preacher, on the other hand, stood before the coffin on a higher level. Choir ready-ing themselves for another couple of hours to see apart a person they don't know, plus members who give off the impression of looking sad but know good and well they could give a fuck. So the preacher went on talking as if he were God's lips. | 'Today, is a holly day, because today we this is not a moaning but a celebration.' Looking over the sea of crying people, 'Big Momma was a good and kind lady.' Meanwhile the preacher spoke, Buuday had her only sly conversation to the woman sitting next to her, 'Big Momma was a good woman. I remember the time she gave birth to Larretta, got her off drugs, and her abusing husband.' Continuing, 'Yea, she was the one that put me back in school to get my edu-ma-cation plus get me off da ebonicz. Yup, sho' did, hmm-uh.' Ending with a sassy mum created from the throat. | The woman just shakes her head at Buuday. | | *ring* The call head finally came. It was time for us to do the thing. *ring* I couldn't pick up the phone, my body locked up on me. Muscles, thoughts, emotion, all locked by an unvisiable time stopping force. *ring* Ok... pick up the phone... Nothing... Was the burden awaiting me on the other side to big for even me? *ring* Suddenly, I answered the phone with a jerk. | 'Wut up.' Laid back, calm, I kept it gangsta. 'Hey sugar, this is Big Momma, remember me? I baby sat ya'll one day. Is Kendra Williams there?' Odd... 'Big Momma? Didn't you die? Plus, who da fuck iz Kendra?' Perplexed questions, the signal of shock. I made it that obvious. 'Died? Well, not that I remember. Why would you think I had died?' 'Becuz Buuday is at yo funeral.' 'Buuday?' 'Don't u kno' Buuday? I assumed dat iz who u were callin' fo.' 'No, I was calling for Kendra Williams. She isn't there? Oh my goodness, I'm going to be so embarrassed if I got the wrong phone number.' 'Wait wait wait, I'm fuckin' confused my damn self.' Putting a pause on the matters at hand, 'Diz Kendra, iz she ghetto as fuck, weave queen, chocolate black, and big ass?' 'Why no, Kendra was a saint after I taught her to speak proper english and leave those hood ways.' 'Yea, u got da wrong fuckin' number.' *click...dail tone* Wasn't the call I was waiting for, so I put a wrapped something around my arm tight, found a vein, and injected more heroin. Nerves calmed, brain dead state. *Two hours later* 'So with the power of ol' might god, I would finally like to say farewell to Barbara Jenkins.' Bowing his head to show fake remorse, everyone else drains their eyes dry. 'Barbara Jenkins? Who da fu-... God damn... I don' went to da wrong funeral! Let me get my ass outa herre!' Immediately, rushing through the row of bent legs, Buuday took off running out of the church. | *ring* Reaching over, I picked up the red phone and held it to my ear, high as hell. | 'Yea'' 'You ready?' 'I'm ready.' 'Letz go take those bitches out.' I had done it already, what's another kill? '...Letz do da damn thing.' -to be continued
