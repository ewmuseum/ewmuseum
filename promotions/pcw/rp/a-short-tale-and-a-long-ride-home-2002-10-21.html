A Short Tale and a Long Ride Home

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-21-02 RP 3 Turmoil 22nd The sound of hoof beats wakens Teresa from her slumber. She sits up and first realizes she is moving. Second, she hits the top of her head on what appears to be a ceiling of sorts. She rubs the sleep out of her eyes smelling the sweet scent of morning dew. When her eyes finally focuses, she realizes she isn’t in her hotel room anymore but in the back of a carriage. A small carriage. Out the window of the door, she watches trees pass by. To the front is a man dressed in a dark blue suit similar to what you would see in the pictures of a fairy tale book. She looks down and finds herself in a white and light blue dress with lots of ruffles and layers. The dress is short sleeved and has a V-neck. What in God’s creation? The man driving the coach hears Teresa’s mumbling and turns his head back towards her with a smile. Aye Lady Teresa, it’s good to see you awake. How are you feeling? Teresa gives the man a confused look. I have a headache. Did you just call me Lady? Who are you and what am I doing here? Why am I wearing this dress? Where are you taking me? You don’t remember? That nasty fall you took must have affected your memory. To answer your questions, yes, I did call you Lady Teresa ‘cause that is who you are. I am your coachman, William Tucker. You are wearing that dress because it is your favorite and I’m taking you to your keep. The carriage moves along the narrow dirt road through what seems like the middle of a forest. Teresa sits back in her wooden seat and sighs. This is too strange to be real she thinks, but while she’s here, why not have a little fun with it? Another hour passes and they finally reach the gate to a large mansion. Two men in armor stand guard. They open the gates allowing the carriage to enter. Teresa continues to watch out the window of the door, now in amazement at the size of the house and the beauty it holds. The carriage finally comes to a stop and Tucker climbs out of his seat and opens the door for Teresa. She takes his hand remembering how they do it on TV, and he leads her toward the door. Little is said between the two. Teresa is still sorting the pieces of this strange puzzle together while Tucker continues to do his job. Just inside the door, a tall muscular man dressed in fine clothing with short black hair meets them. Teresa begins to think it odd that no one has welcomed her home considering Tucker had told her this was her home. Tucker introduces Teresa as the Lady Teresa and she wishes to see the Lord of the house. The tall man nods giving Teresa a smile then begins to walk down a hall. Teresa takes this opportunity to grab Tucker’s arm and squeeze it tightly. I thought you told me I lived here. If so, why didn’t he recognize me? Tucker places his hand over hers patting it as if comforting her. The tall man returns. He speaks in a deep voice telling Tucker that Lord Timothy is ready to see Teresa. Teresa doesn’t release Tucker’s arm, waiting on a response. Instead, the tall man pries Teresa’s fingers off Tucker’s arm. In return, Teresa throws her fist to Tucker’s jaw, then gives the tall man a knee to the groin. She then feels something hit her head and she blacks out. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~@~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ In the meantime, King Roland is speaking to his most loyal, trustworthy knight, Sir Patrick. Worried about the welfare of his niece, Lady Teresa, he proposes an offer to Sir Patrick. You have known Teresa since you were a child. The two of you have grown up together and I trust no one more with her than you. I am positive the demon possessed, Lord Timothy has her captive inside the walls of his keep. If you go there, rescue and bring her back, I shall reward you greatly. As long as Teresa is brought home alive and well, that will be reward enough. The brave and noble Sir Patrick disappears in the horizon on his steed in search of daughter of the King’s only sister. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~@~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Teresa wakes up again. This time she is lying in a bed stuffed with feathers. It feels so soft and the blankets covering her are so warm she doesn’t want to move. She manages to sit up rubbing her head. She remembers waking up before in the carriage and entering the large house. Looking around it seems she is still within the walls. She is now convinced Tucker was lying and this is certainly NOT her home. Sitting in a chair in a corner is a short man dressed in very fine clothing. Looking down, Teresa isn’t in the blue and white dress, but in a long pink silk gown. Her baby blue eyes notice the short man. The man stands and approaches Teresa. He sits beside her on the bed. The tips of his fingers touch her face and slide delicately down her neck and toward her chest. My Lady Teresa. A fine lady you are. Ever since we were children, I dreamed of having you as my own. Now I have you, my life is complete. Teresa grabs the man’s wrist and forces it off her chest. She stands up. Who are you and what are you talking about? Why am I here?The short man grabs Teresa’s arm and forces her to sit back on the bed. He then takes her hand and kisses it gently. I am Lord Timothy and this is my home. I have had you rescued my lady, from your evil uncle, the King. Tucker has spoke to me of your memory loss. It’s a pity things had to have happened this way. Lord Timothy then traces his fingertips along side her earlobe and down to her shoulder where he then slips his finger under the shoulder strap of her gown and slides it off her shoulder. Not only is Teresa confused, she is angry. Who does this Lord Timothy think he is touching her and speaking to her as if she belonged to him. She smacks his face. He smacks her back. She pushes him off the bed then slides off the bed as well. While he’s still on the floor, she picks him up by the hair of his head and forces it into a hard wooden dresser. Teresa does this repeatedly until his blood drips onto the floor. Her hand then grasps his throat and she forces him against the wall. Her face comes inches from his. As he sees the mad temper glare in her eyes, she sees the fear shine in his. Listen to my words. I belong to no one but myself. Give me one reason why I shouldn’t break your neck right now. Instead of answering her, Lord Timothy yelled for the guards and Teresa presses harder on his neck. Suddenly, the door swings open and a man in full armor rushes into the room. Teresa drops Lord Timothy leaving him gasping for like. She stands her ground keeping her eye on the man. And who do you think you are? The man, obviously a knight takes off his helm revealing himself as Sir Patrick. I am Sir Patrick. Don’t you recognize me Lady Teresa? I am here to bring you home. Sir who? I don’t know a Sir Patrick. I know who you are. You are my opponent Tuesday night for the Television title and this is some sick joke and I’m not laughing. If you‘ll excuse me, I’ll dress and find my way home myself. Sir Patrick takes a step back as Teresa passes him. He thinks she is delusional due to whatever Lord Timothy has done to her. He dare not think about it. Once she is dressed, he picks her up and throws her over his shoulder. Despite her refusal and pointless attempts at kicking and punching, under his metal armor, his body is unharmed. As Teresa is carried through the house, she sees bodies scattered about. Outside he places her on the back of his white horse and mounts as well. She could jump off and run, but where to? Teresa holds on to Sir Patrick who she is obviously confusing him with Venneman Pendragon, the man she is facing Tuesday night. About three hours of body aching riding; something catches Teresa’s attention. She turns her head to watch a large green Ogre walking by with a donkey following. C’mon Donkey. We don’t have all day. I need to save the Princess and get my swamp back. Hey, don’t get your underwear in a bunch. I’m coming. Teresa blinks thinking she must be seeing things. She rests her head on the knight’s back and finds herself drifting off to sleep.
