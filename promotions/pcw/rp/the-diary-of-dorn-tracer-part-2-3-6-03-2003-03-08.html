The Diary of Dorn Tracer - Part 2: 3-6-03

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 - 8 - 03 Written: 3-6-03 Good evening, journal. I’ve quite a story to tell, if you’re willing to listen. But first, an inquiry: Have you ever had two distinct sides of your life collide for supremacy over your very being? Sounds rather flattering, doesn’t it? Well, being an experienced individual in such a thing let me be the first to tell you that it’s a living nightmare. Being stuck by a mile wide flaming asteroid wouldn’t be quite as bad. Sounds rather harsh, does it? Personally, I’m rather worried. I wake up in a cold sweat every night, jolting up out of bed after having these horrible, gut-wrenching nightmares of burning bodies and contests of wits. But how could this be so, journal? The contenders aren’t of mighty proportions, that of which the Earth has never seen before… well, at least, one of them isn’t. My Goddess, the Creator and Destroy of All, En’Flarim, and my best friend Jessica Carter have been at an indirect battle of control over me. It’s wracking my mind and it’s been difficult to concentrate on the coming tide of battle, but allow me to explain: As you know, I am of course an Astronomer. I hail a group of ultra-powerful, celestial beings known as the Tiamats. The leader of the Tiamats, born of negative energy and chaos, is En’Flarim. She is I, she is we, and she is everything. The all-powerful control of life and death, mastering entire universes with a shake of her palm. Or at least, she was. She lost most of her power in a climatic battle with a being known as Orion, but that’s a tale for another day. Now, she has come to me, claiming that I am the Supreme Being known as the Lunis Superia. Quite a title, is it not, journal? She’s given me the strength, endurance, and iron will to succeed against all odds. She gave me the soul and spirit to win the PCW Rising Star Title and stand up against the blasphemous masses, declaring, “I, Dorn Tracer, am YOUR Rising Star Champion! I am an Astronomer!” She has done so much for me, even to the point of turning my life around. I used to lay in a run down motel bed, doing favors of questionable ethic, wondering when Death was going to find me and whisk me away from the nightmare I had to live through. I had little money, no job, and found myself feasting on the remains of whatever the guy next door was willing to give me. Things definitely weren’t going my way. I was soon having visions of a great dragon, visiting and telling me great things. But, the wonderful visions ended with me being consumed by a great maw of fire. I would wake up, terrified and dumbfounded as to what to do. Then, she came to me… in reality… Taking me away from it all and imbuing me with new strength, En’Flarim guided me to victory and golden splendor. What price was there to pay? Nothing, as far as I know. I must serve unquestioningly and never fail. Easy, is it not? That’s what I thought too; especially for someone like me with an undying will to succeed and prove the masses wrong through attacking them on their own ground. But apparently, someone DOESN’T think so. Jessica Carter has been my best friend for as long as I can remember. I met her during a stay in the Primetime Wrestling Academy, where I was trying to refine my skills as an Astrological warrior. We had a misunderstanding to begin with, but we soon grew to know each other very well and she’s always been there to listen to me. She knows my deepest, darkest secrets that I dare not bring up, for fear I will drive myself to insanity again. Her happy, stalwart attitude brings a smile to my face as I sit here and picture it in my head. She’s pretty, and understand, and very helpful when I need it. And sometimes when I DON’T need it, for that matter. Like recently. She has been pestering me about ‘getting me help’ for a problem that she explains as ‘zealot-like brainwashing.’ BAH! She’s so much smarter than that, why would she conceive of such a thing? She claims I’m brainwashed to En’Flarim, doing her bidding like a robot born of flesh and bone. I’ve told her many times that my ventures in PCW and beyond have been of my own accord, and En’Flarim has given the strength to do so. She doesn’t believe me, and continues to try and ‘help’ me. She took me to dinner, let me stay at her home, and even considered being my valet so that she could show me the error of my ways. As if she was anyone to do so. En’Flarim is my Goddess and Master, yet Jessica contests that. She’s bad-mouthed En’Flarim and even told me that I should completely forget her. I cannot believe Jessica is so foolish as to suggest such things. En’Flarim shrugs her off as mere mortal, not taking in anything that Jessica has said about her. Surprisingly, I find myself torn between my Goddess and best friend. But why isn’t the decision easy? En’Flarim is the all too obvious answer, but yet, something brewing deep within my mind sends an electric signal of “HALT!” whenever I consider brushing off Jessica’s efforts. What is this that I am feeling, journal? Is it uncertainty? Hesitation? Or possibly guilt at denying my best friend her right to be concerned? Bah, pure rubbish. Couldn’t be anything like that. I’ve nothing to be ashamed of, or guilty of for that matter. My life is as bright as a bursting supernova. The Shooting Star Title continues to sit around my waist, and the chance at the ultimate prize looks just over the horizon. A chance at the PCW World Heavyweight Title has made itself apparent in the form of a huge, 30-warrior battle royal. En’Flarim has told me that this is my chance to make a true impact. But I’m so nervous… the prize is as contested as the planets themselves. Entire races have been driven to bloody destruction as a result of greed and great power in the form of artifacts or territory. The PCW World Heavyweight Title is both an artifact and a territory; only those deemed of having a worthy soul are allowed to throw their mind, spirit, and body into the whirling, chaotic fold that is the Heavyweight Title division. I am as such, for En’Flarim tells me so. It shall be an exhausting, deadly battle of epic proportions… and some may not make it out, journal. But as long as I stick by En’Flarim and not pay any heed to the blasphemous claims of Jessica Carter, I can succeed. I must succeed. I WILL succeed. Woe unto those who stand in my way, journal. The Astronomer will burst through the ranks like a mighty meteorite, crushing those beneath me and tossing aside those above me. Volcanoes, oceans, and the very core of the Earth itself will buckle when my power reaches its full potential. The PCW World Heavyweight Title is mine. And journal, that… is a fact. [End.]
