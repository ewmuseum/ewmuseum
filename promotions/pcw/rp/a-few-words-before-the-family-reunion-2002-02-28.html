A few words before the family reunion

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 He did it. It might not have been flashy or very Hollywood-esque, but the Krazed Lunatic got his son back and a measure of revenge. Keith Lipowitz thought he was in total control. He thought there was no one out there that could stop him from sacrificing the Krayzie One and his two year old son. Keith Lipowitz never planned on Darkstar or Miyagawa showing up. And, much to the dismay of all the haters out there that think Asylum has no unity, they helped the Krazed Lunatic get back his son. So, what does a father do with a two year old that's never actually seen him before? The answer is easy if you're Jeremy Howard. As the scene fades into the inside of Alexander Miyagawa's yellow hummer, the Krayzie One and his son can be seen sitting in the backseat, with Darkstar taking the front passenger seat and Miya sitting in the driver's seat. Jeremy, his adrenaline still flowing from the evil ordeal back behind the old church, holds his son tightly close to him. Miyagawa: Where to, partner? Jeremy lets out a yawn, showing signs of fatigue from his quest to get his son back. Jeremy: My house. Jessica is probably worrying her little body to death. Darkstar: Well, she's got a great present headed her way. Jeremy smiles. Jeremy: Yeah. I just hope that Stormbringer and Kannon made sure she was safe from anyone else that might have my name on there hit list. Miyagawa: She's fine. I talked to Stormbringer before we got to the church and he said she was alright. Just worrying a little about your safety. Jeremy shakes his head. Jeremy: She was worrying? Sh!t, for a second there, I was worrying about my own safety after I got hit in the back of the head with the crowbar a second time. It took a white van blasting the side of Jeremy's BMW X5, two crowbar shots to the back of the head, a large number of men and various other attacks to subdue Jeremy Howard. But, in the end, it still wasn't enough. They had him down and seemingly out. Yet, when you back Jeremy Howard into a wall, he explodes off it like a ticking time bomb that went off. Such was the case back at the old church. Darkstar: Just what did happen back there? Jeremy: Where do I begin? Jeremy situates himself in the backseat of the hummer, while checking the seatbelt on Troy. It tight around his little waist, albeit it being illegal that his son is not in a car seat. Although, if Asylum were pulled over by a police officer, it wouldn't mean anything to the Krayzie One to lay him out. An officer of the law or not, Troy's going back to his mother and that's final. Jeremy: I left Jessica at home, because it wasn't safe for her, and headed down here. I couldn't remember how we got that alleyway where I began to chase the black Trans Am, so I searched down a few streets. Jeremy pauses, keeping his eyes watching out of the window for any kind of surprise that might happen. This man isn't taking any chances. Yet, nothing happens. Jeremy: I finally find the alleyway after about a hour or so. I turn down the alley and bam. A white van blasts me right in the driver's side door. I hit my head on something, I can't remember and went out cold. Miyagawa: Airbag go off? Jeremy shakes his head, 'no'. Jeremy: I think the BMW was a dead duck after the car chase. Then, I wake up in this dark room, tied to a chair by my arms and legs. I couldn't break the rope. It was impossible. Thick stuff. Suddenly, Lipowitz and his ass mongers enter the room. To my left, I can see the circle of fire, burning. Darkstar: Those people have no clue how to be goth. Miyagawa: You going to give them a few lessons? Darkstar: I think I already did. There's no doubt about that. To some, it might be humanly impossible, but the man they call Darkstar blew up the old church, sending the person this occult called the Higher Being to his death. The proceeding moments in the life of Darkstar can only be called unbelievable as he literally took out an entire occult with his mystical powers, thus helping the Krayzie One get his son back. Some people might not understand just what Asylum is about. But, when a member really needs help, the best help they can ask for, is Asylum. Miyagawa: Jeremy, as you were saying? Jeremy, with his arm around his son, gives two year old Troy a tight squeeze before resuming his story. Jeremy: So, this Lipowitz guy comes in and we have a battle of words. Sometime during all of that, I must've pissed him off with something I said, because I got cracked in the back of the head with a crowbar again. Darkstar: It's a wonder you still have your head on your shoulders. Jeremy: Tell me about it. Next thing I know, I'm trying to fight all the pain while I'm being dragged outside. They get me outside and Lipowitz decides to have another verbal battle with me. Again, I must've said something he didn't like, because thats when he revealed Troy in a cage, no less. Miyagawa: Someone should revoke his parental rights. A grin crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: I already did revoke his parental rights. Miyagawa: True. Jeremy looks out of the window, while he recounts the final moments of his standoff behind the old church in Terre Haute, Indiana. As Jeremy stares out of the window, he spots a green sign on the side of the road. Indianapolis: 31 miles. That places Jeremy a little under four hours away from Jessica and seeing her beautiful face light up at the sight of her son being returned to her. Jeremy: Lipowitz reveals Troy and has four his men take him into the circle of fire where the cross is. I don't understand this man sick fetish with burning the cross, but it really bothered me. When he had his man start to set Troy on fire, something inside of me clicked and I was ready to take on all those guys by myself..... Jeremy pauses to catch his breathe, then.... Jeremy: ....and go down in a blaze of glory if need be, but two men that I'd trust my life with showed up. Miyagawa: I told you before, being your friend might get me into some unwanted trouble sometimes, but I'm always there for you man. Always. Ever since Jeremy entered PCW, his friendship with Miyagawa has been there. Never once, in the entire time he was leading Greed Inc., did he lay a hand on Miyagawa. While he might've been fulling the fans and the rest of PCW, he was not about to risk his friendship with the Japo-American superstar. Two men helped Jeremy Howard out when he was a rookie in PCW, one of those being Alexander Miyagawa. Therefore, there's two people in PCW that Jeremy Howard would go to the ends of this Earth for. And, it's apparent, one of those two men will do the same for the Krayzie One. Darkstar: You know I have your back. You believed in me, when ever I didn't at times. This was my pleasure. Then there's Darkstar. The Krazed Lunatic was an eyelash away from losing his World title because of underhanded tactics by his opponents, but Darkstar hit the ring and showed his true friendship to the Krayzie One in helping Jeremy to retain. Jeremy was even the man that helped at Darkstar's 'funeral'. There friendship might only be in its beginning stages, but its fast becoming one that Jeremy is greatful for. Jeremy: The next time you guys EVER need ANYTHING, consider my services there. It's the least I can do for two men that were there for me when I needed them the most. One man might have more enemies than red blood cells in his body, buts the number of true friends he has that matters. For Jeremy Howard, the number of true friends that he has always seems to rise when he needs them the most. And, with an all important chance at winning the Continental title on Friday Fusion and the chance to gain the freedom of his brother at March Madness, a good question might be, who will it be this time? Drake Maxwell might be evil, but Jeremy Howard is loved by many. The scene fades out, as Jeremy continues to hold his son tight in his left arm, as Miya continues down the road toward Jeremy's house in Elkhart, Indiana four hour away.... to black!
