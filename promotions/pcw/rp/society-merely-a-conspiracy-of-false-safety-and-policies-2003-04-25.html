Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 25 - 03 SOCIETY… The pure representation of what reactionaries and conservatives wish us all to be. They advocate the concept of “Change is bad” and “Everyone must be the same.” Pfft… I can tell you exactly what’s wrong with that. And why I detest this so-called ‘society’ so much. Early on, before the 1950s, American society was living under a heavy weight, caused by the fear for their own weakness in trying to confront a Totalitarian state. Modernization was coming in full swing following the atrocious acts of World War II, and that meant a new age of Democracy. However, a stream of cold sweat dripped down the spine of the American government after the debacle concerning Franklin D. Roosevelt’s strangely long reign as “President” of the United States. Citizens as a whole weren’t overly concerned due to the success of FDR’s reign… they were blind to the fact that their country had technically fallen into the hands of a single man alone. This was obviously a quick change of pace, but why did the American people choose to go with it? Ignorance. Advocating normality. No opposition of the government’s decisions. Henry David Thoreau once said, “That government is best which governs the least.” His reasoning was his claim that the American people demand lessened political interference and want nothing but their own free will to work of off, while the people in power take care of the trivial details. This is the liberal heart of America beating to life, and with Modernization over the horizon, that same liberal heart soon became clogged with fears of Communism and abrupt change. Some consider change to be that ‘point of no return’, and that’s why many fear it. America no longer called for the chance to make its own decision and once again started sucking off the tit of its so-called ‘democractic government’. In their blind, beady eyes, a group of well-educated and talented men are running their country fluidly through thought-out decisions and the popular vote. How little the people really know. Their very fore fathers were the ones that totally denounced the foundation of a democratic, normal society: And they did it in secret. Called ‘The Order Of Cincinnati’, a group of men under George Washington established a deal stating that if the country were to fall into internal or foreign-based crisis, the Constitution would be suspended and a single man would be declared the official ‘ruler’ of the United States of America. How democratic. But yet, the people know nothing of this. I doubt the stupid blasphemers even realize that there’s WORLD SOCIALIST PARTY vying for a pseudo-totalitarian government running about on their land! After years of fighting the Socialists and Communists, the Americans are letting them in. But you know what’s funny? You’ll never hear of them. Americans will always hang on to their ‘democracy’ and ‘state of normality’, because that’s all they know. It’s the same way with religion. You’ll never have a Satanist President, nor will you ever have a Wiccan senator. These people will TRY to run for an office that should be rightfully theirs, based on support, but the American society of normality would never allow it. It would throw the nation into chaos. They’ve been whipped into accepting whatever the higher-ups throw down their throats as ‘normal’ or ‘right’. The New World Order conspiracy looms over the horizon with George W. Bush now in office, and threatens to envelop the world under one multi-national policy where the U.N. has the final word. A proverbial dictatorship. The government wouldn’t give a holy fuck what the people thought, because in their eyes, the mind of the people would bring chaos. Hitler was a person. He had a certain state of mind. Look what happened. Very interesting. What would happen if say… ASTROLOGY would become the official religion of America? Would ‘society’ accept it? Absolutely not, and that’s a good enough excuse to rip society apart and rebuild it into a more tolerant, justified group of people. What about UFOs? How come the government constantly denies these things? Because of the Illuminati. The Illuminati is a 200 year old secret organization bent on world domination by exploiting outstanding events and enveloping the weak minds of the people into believing that they truly have things under control. Same with the New World Order. And society is dumb enough to believe it. They think what they have is there because the government set it out before them, and if it were to change, then all chaos would break loose. That’s why people like me, Astronomers, and other minorities bent on change are so discriminated against and looked down upon. Sound crazy? It shouldn’t. People know it’s true. Project HAARP. The New World Order. The Illuminati. And society itself. And SOCIETY represents everything that the Illuminati represents. Screw the Illuminati. Screw the New World Order. Screw George W. Bush. And screw you, SOCIETY! It’s time for a change. Woe unto those that think they can stop it.
