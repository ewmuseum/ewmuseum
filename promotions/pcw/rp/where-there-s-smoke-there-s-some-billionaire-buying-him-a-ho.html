Where there's smoke there's some billionaire buying him a house in order to get him to help him fight Jon Kano.

undated

Date: Now Mind games are fun, especially when played with the mindless. You see, whilst Smoke is most certainly a very well-conditioned athlete, even at his under-developed ages, they are somewhat lacking in cunning. Semore Smoke is not only new and inexperienced, but he's also a little slow anyway. It is most certainly sad to see a young man with potential's mind go 'up in smoke.' But that's the way it goes, and Corey can do what he does best and use his surroundings to his advantage. In Corey's mind he is the pioneer of the new era, not the corner stone, but rather the centre stone. In Corey's mind he will be the centre piece, in the spotlight, Corey will be to the new era as Tyler Nelson was to the old one.... It's just a matter of getting the new era started. More than two months ago PCW restarted in what was not meant to be a reset, but rather an evolution in a new era. Did it work? Has anything really changed? The same old stories and as the same old faces are being rehashed. Tyler Nelson is still there as the CEO now with the 'new version' of Dark Ages. Frisco returned to a shot at the title, as did Legend Inc to a Continental title, Duke Williams is still the President and is continuing his quest to reclaim the title that made him. Chris Chase returned, no longer a messiah but now a saint, wow, how humble of the cock-sucker to take a step down. And Big Alan Steele is still the PCW Champion, that man who rode the heat of Corey's title reign to ironically enough become the man everyone toted as the 'pioneer of the new era,' now where'd I hear that before? What Corey sees that other people don't is that though Big Alan Steele has not been in PCW long, before then he had a long and healthy reign in the now-defunct CCW. In fact, people have even called him a CCW legend. Now, Corey has nothing against legends, the real ones that is, but the problem is that people assume a legend has to be someone who's been with the company a long time, And with this misconception comes the loss of the idea of a legend being someone of actual talent. This type of so-called legend Corey DOES have a problem with and has continued to, the most recent example of which is a man named Jon Kano. Their hatred for each other has transcended everything; time, place and character, just when was the last time we heard Corey talk about controversy? The contrast in their characters, their clothes, their wrestling style and their attitudes towards the fans all are meaningless in the end. When the bell rings these two have only one thought in their mind: one must fall. This is not one of those 'feuds' in which two guys have a partially homosexual tiff and then a match and then the next night forget about it. Neither of these men will move on until the other is no longer able to, move on that is. And though there will be many mental and emotional battles have been won on both sides, eventually the ultimate physical battle must be fought and one must fall. Semore Smoke, the ultimate wild card. This former NTICW champion prospect despite lacking in intelligence most certainly has it in the skill department. Both Kano and Corey have seen this and both immediately set in a plan to use this kid's skill to their advantage. Whilst CCW may not agree with the way Kano does things, he knows that Kano is not an idiot. After you bleed in the same ring as a guy you tend to learn a lot about him. Corey learnt a tiny bit of respect for Kano, as did Kano for Corey. In fact, the only thing stopping them from being best friends is the fact that they hate each other with an undying passion. They unknowingly hated each other since CCW debuted a year ago, they hate each other now and they will hate each other ten years on, the only way for the passion to die is for one of them to die. Kano struck first, a plot so subtle no one even saw it, Kano went on about how Smoke taking out CCW was doing him a favor and congratulating the kid, Smoke however rejected the thanks and later challenged Kano to a match. CCW tried to get Smoke on his side for a triple threat match but Smoke again rejected. Since then both men have gone out of their way to get the kid to join them, using various types of logic, lies and promises. So the question remains, which way will Smoke go? ::Our scene opens up at a real estate agent's office in New York. Photos of various houses are lined along the walls on either side of the room and in the glass cabinet which acts as a counter near the center of the room lying parallel with the back wall. Behind that counter are a bunch of cubicles in which we can see a bunch of different desks.: 'Name?' 'Corey Williams, but I don't want you to put it in my name.' ::Our camera moves forward and we pan around the front divider of the cubicle and see Corey Williams dressed in a bear fur coat and black leather pants like usual. Across the cubicle on the other side of desk sits a woman in a business suit with hair slicked back into a pony tail with a red scrunchie. As she adjusts her large framed glasses we notice that she is wearing a name tag which reads 'Eileen.' Eileen looks up at CCW with a confused face.:: Eileen: Lemme get this straight, you want to buy the 34 Smith Street Deluxe Mansion, but you don't want it for yourself!? Corey: It's a gift, (Corey considers his words carefully before disclosing them) for a 'friend'. Eileen: But are you sure you want to buy it without having them at least coming here? I mean, most of the buyers at least go check out the house, but your friend isn't even going to see his house and you've only seen a few photos of it. With such an...expensive purchase don't you think you should at least check it out? Corey: It's big and it's expensive, that about covers my interests. Eileen: But what about you're friend's interests? What make's you think he's gonna like it? Corey: Well, you see that patch of grass right there.... ::CCW points towards a huge path of grass that can be seen behind the house on the aerial photo.:: Corey: That would be the perfect place for my 'friend' to grow his...umm....er..... daisies. Eileen: Is he into gardening? Corey: Yes, I guess you could say that about my 'friend', he certainly loves tend to his plants every day. Eileen: So you're absolutely sure? Corey: Of course I'm sure! You're meant to be trying to swindle me, not talk me out of the deal. Now start doing your job or you're fired! ::Eileen is startled, less by Corey's out burst and more by his words.:: Eileen: Fired? Corey: Yes! Don't you know that I happen to own this goddamn agency, I bought it like a week ago or something. Bargain price too. Eileen: Really? ::Eileen suddenly takes of her glasses, lets out her hair and unbuttons the top button of her blouse before smiling at CCW seductively. CCW smiles back, not because he is spell-binded by her looks but because he loves to watch the effects his power has on people.:: Eileen: Well then, let's suck... I mean let's close this baby up so we can go to dinner shall we? Corey: If you just asked me out on a date then you can forget it, I don't date employees...........Ok, I do, but not the ugly ones. ::A look of disappointment from Eileen lasts only as long as Corey will allow it to, for the actually somewhat beautiful woman suddenly remembers that her job is on the line.:: Eileen (after clearing her throat): Well, all of your papers are in order, I just the name of the man who shall be the new owner of this house. Corey: The name of my 'friend' is... Semore Smoke. ::Eileen writes this down, surprisingly enough, not on any official document but rather on a napkin.:: Eileen: Uh-huh, and uh, what is his current address? Corey: I don't know, do I! Do you think I have time to remember these petty details! Eileen: That's ok, it's not important anyway. And finally, what are your friend's turn ons? ::CCW simply smiles at the desperate looking Eileen.:: Eileen: Turn offs? Corey: ....You're fired ::Eileen's eyes widen in shock and she begins to stammer.:: Eileen: But, but, but but.... ::CCW grabs a pen from the desk and signs what appears to be his name on the paper on the desk. He then begins to stand up.:: Eileen: But, but, but, but... Corey: Yep, fired. ::Once on his feet, Corey picks up a top hat that seems to have been lying on the hat rack just outside the cubicle the whole time. He places the top hat on his head before turning back towards Eileen.:: Corey: Strange whore, it's been a pleasure. But now I must bid you adieu... ::Corey curtseys Eileen and then turns and leaves, leaving Eileen completely shocked. CCW opens the door of the building and exits through it. On the streets, he adjusts his hat for a moment and then reaches into the pockets of his bear fur coat and pulls out a pair of gloves. He places the gloves on his hands and rubs them together before looking up towards the camera which just 'happens to be there.':: Corey: That's right Smoke, that big ole mansion I bet you just always wanted, and it's yours. There's plenty more where that came from. You see, I can offer you things that Kano couldn't achieve in his wildest dreams. I can offer you money, cars, houses, power and authority. But the best thing you can have is freedom. The freedom to be yourself, the freedom to not give a shit about what the fans think. The freedom to beat some guy up coz his laughter pisses you off, or because you don't like the poofy pants he wears to the ring. The freedom to not just be another player in the game, but to be the guy who takes things to the next level, and then just when it looks like everyone has caught him up, the level after that. I am giving you the chance of a lifetime Semore, the chance of a million lifetimes, a billion lifetimes! But whilst the rewards of taking that chance are greater than you could imagine in your brightest dreams, let me warn you right now, the consequences of not taking that chance are worse than you can imagine in your darkest nightmares. You don't want to be my enemy Smoke, nobody does. It's like I always say, 'the only reason people hate me is because I want them to.' And you can guarantee that if you actually CHOOSE to be my enemy, I will make you hate me most of all. ::And with that chilling message, our scene fades to black (not plaid).:: Let's recap shall we: In this bid for his power, Semore Smoke is getting money and houses from Corey Williams, and homosexual lovin' from Jon Kano. Whilst homosexual lovin' may not exactly be a good thing the fact is it beats what CCW and Kano are getting in return so far, a big fat cold shoulder as Smoke continues to reject them. Both of them are trying to 'use' Smoke to their advantage, but right now neither of them seems to have much of an advantage, right now the man with the advantage, the man holding all the cards is, ironically enough, the 'ultimate wildcard' Semore Smoke. Maybe he's not so damn mindless after all. !The End!
