Flashdance III - Part Two

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 2nd - Pre-Sabotage. OOC: This is a little shorter than it should have been, I’ve got a lot of work on my hands, so thus I could only pile about two and a half hours work into it…meh! However, at this rate, I still should have at least 7-8 RPs up for the PPV. The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals…… RP#2 for Turmoil and #2 for Spring Fling… Location: Da Tavern – Mecca for Tired and downright foul Comics and irritable patrons, (Insert Turmoil Location here)… Chapter Five: Flashdance III – Part Two of Three – Tired Jokes Mean Zilch “Wait, Wait, Wait…I’ve got an even better joke…so lay down the rotten fruit, and at least wait until I’ve pulled this one on ya. Now then, why did the chicken cross the road? To buy a magazine that would end up strewn all over his bedroom floor, ripped up into tiny pieces and eventually thrown into the trashcan, of course! Now, is that a nifty gag or what? No…NO…NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” “Boo! Get of the stage you frickin’ moron! Jeez…I tell you, these patrons are brutal…that hapless comic just had his eyes filled with the pulp of rotten fruit and vegetables from the last two acts. Who in their right minds would actually hire these cretins as comedic entertainment? Meh…it’d probably be the same idiots who thought that Boda and Miya-What-err had decent career prospects in the PCW…wouldn’t you think?” “Hmph, the poor schmucks, they don’t honestly realise what they’re getting themselves into. But if one thing has to be asked: Is your life one constant frickin’ promo? Every waking moment, you seem to be mocking your opponents with shameless abandon, and to be quite honest Chris…it occasionally gets a tiny bit tiresome!” “Tell me about it…I mean, had I the control that I’d usually have over my exploits, I’d have probably stopped mocking my opponents about 8 months ago. BUT NO! Apparently it’s good friggin’ business, and it pays handsomely mind…” “Someone’s a little high on their own supply, don’t you think?” ::. Lame gags and jibes. That’s what my opponents, top draw opponents mind, have degenerated this little conflict into. They’ve brought up the same tired old stuff, time and time again…and it’s just slightly getting on my nerves. Boda comes up with something wholly unoriginal, Miyagawa catches on…and the two chase each others tails until both their wives give up and enter the world of smut. Sitting on the fence is a young, original entertainer…heck, I embody originality. I choose not to enter with the same entrance theme of about a hundred other wrestlers the world over, I choose not to come up with gags that a thirteen year old would have used a thousand times already.Heck, I’m 23 years old, possibly the youngest in the Spring Fling Mainevent…and yet I’m still surrounded by the bratish types of Slo-Jobber and Miya-Boda. That aside, I’m carrying the frickin’ Mainevent of the Pay-Per-View for my sake! All this Chris Chase merchandise and R&D funding would have to go into something…and what could be more appropriate than an event at which I win the most Prestigious title belt in the business, raising it up a notch or two from the jobberific likes of the freakin’ slacker that holds it at the moment… That’s not to say that I haven’t forgotten the former stage performer who now thinks of himself as the equivalent to Hercules, the spawn of Zeus – Big Alan Steele. Steele and I go quite a way back, back to the days of the Circle of Justice…those blinded post-High Impact, pre-New Breed days that will churn in my soul forever more. It was back then when I was crucified by the Dark Age, called a jobber by Jeff Marx, and generally abused in that sense. And blindly, I stuck my neck out on more than four or five occasions for the deranged idiot…a lackey in any sense of the form. But I’m no lackey now, I’m my own man…the last remaining active survivor from New Breed…the last remnant of High Impact, the most impressive former PCW2 wrestler that ever emerged from that federation. And now I’ve first got to deal with a man who managed to beat Meanstreak on a fluke, and one who’s currently waring with the likes of the Chef. Come to think of it, weren’t both of those two in the #1 Contendership match for the PCW World Title? Of course they were! And who won that match perchance? Once again, the indie Rock overtones of Matthew Good Band’s epic “Failing the Rorschach Test” impact the scene with much volume…shredding the boos and jeers of the comic’s audience to shreds as the camera gradually pans around the scene, the grimy interior of a tavern not far from the Turmoil base of operations. A far cry from the cleaner, yet smuttier location of a strip joint in Manhattan…I smirk to myself with a frown developing on my face as I glare out of the window out at the blue skies and green grass that border this dingy underworld of Boda-like filth. Not exactly my ideal choice of a place to recupperate before my clash with Steele, but for the opportunity to thoroughly berate my opponents, it seemed to frickin’ tempting to leave it be. And besides, I’ve been to these kinds of places before…and although those visits usually ended up in Dustin puking over some Biker who subsequently threw the both of us out, this time is bound to be different. After all, the closest we have to a Dustin in here is nothing more than a voice-activated Cardboard cutout….:: Matt Good Lyrics: Hey Alice…I’m Caving In, I’m Caving in. I know it’s not allowed but sometimes I fantasize. I’m peeling off my skin, enough to fill it up again. Hey Rabbit, into the pavement, I’m caving in… Dustin Cutout: More funnelcakes please! Say, remember when you scoffed seven of those bad boys and puked over that black man and spilled his drink, only for him to throw us both into the swimming pool? ::. And that’s probably enough to get me thrown out…although it wasn’t my idea to bring along that abomination. You can blame that on Serenity, my accompaniment for the next week, month, whatever. She stands beside me along one of the walls of the bar, watching the hapless acts put on by the poor comedians hired by the Tavern, having rotten fruit thrown at them by two dozen spectators….:: Spectator #1: Get of the DAMN stage you freaking moron. Chicken jokes are so 1950s, you damn retard. Tell us something that hasn’t already been used by Boda a thousand times! Comedian: ~(Dodging volleys of rotten fruit)~ But I don’t know any better, I’m only a beginner, a hapless beginner…a useless rookie. Sheesh, as if this audience couldn’t be any tougher… ::. Serenity smacks me firmly in my left arm, taking my constantly mobile attention into her own hands, grasping it firmly. I avert my head away from the stage, and straight into her face…as she smirks deviously. I figured as much, as she begins to highlight a plot to either thoroughly berate my opponents or embarrass myself. Not too impressed with the latter, I begin to shake my head in disagreement….:: Serenity: Poor schmuck…you know Chris, with your avid knowledge of Boda, Miyagawa and other random jokes, you could proverbally bring the house down. After all, they aren’t half as tired as these half-witted jibes… Chris: Oh please, Boda jokes are in the very least entertaining. For crying out loud, do you really want me to kill this crowd or what? These people aren’t hardcore Chris Chase fans, they aren’t as strongly willed as the average Professional wrestling fan. How do you expect me to joke about Boda to about 2 dozen virgins to the Professional Wrestling contingent, really now? After all…Boda’s nothing more than a useless excuse for a living embodiment of a sleeping pill…if you take in more Boda than you’re able to…then it’s nothing more than putting yourself than a coma. Just because I have very high levels of Boda tolerance, it doesn’t mean that these down and homely folk are going to have even a quarter of that. Do you realise that you’re bringing about the mass coma induction of these poor folk? I don’t want to be responsible for that! ::. Besides, it might end up being twisted into some bizarre form of putting Boda over…and I’m never going to be likely to do that, not as long as the flames of hell continue to roar…and the Boda fans continue to snore. Miyagawa jibes would have the adverse effect on the populus. Instead of turning the entire population into a comatose state, we’d be faced with a bunch of Miya-clones, raving lunatics who run around all over the four corners of the world…claiming that they can kill people and taking on large former Soviet Soldiers whilst throwing reproductions of Ricky Flame through a wrestling ring. Besides, hardly any of that has any relevance. I think this as I plant myself firmly into an empty seat…smirking as arrogantly as usual, beside the cardboard cutout of Dustin Downey, who grins in a blissfully ignorant way….:: Dustin Cutout: Say Chris, have you seen Ricky Martin run by here? He nabbed my pair of leopard skin leather pants! I’m going to KILL that Puerto Rican FOOL! RICKY MUST DIE!! ::. I sigh to myself, reminded of the time that Dustin was sent to jail for beating Ricky Martin up. How anyone other than myself could remember that is actually beyond me. With a sigh, I smack the cutout, which firmly remains in its place…as if it had been reinforced somehow….:: Serenity: Oh…I forgot to mention that I had the boys down town reinforce the cutout, after all, it’s nothing more than cardboard. So now it has three strips of steel running along the back, holding the cutout firmly in place. You never know what form of schmuck is likely to wantonly destroy the cutout… ::. I can think of one person who’d be willing to get rid of, burn and thoroughly destroy the cardboard embodiment of a hamster loving excuse for a former tag partner. Heck, Dustin’s my buddy after all…but his accompaniment sometimes borders on the tiresome, you think? My thoughts don’t lie with such darn trivial matters, instead, Turmoil had come faster than anyone could ever realise…the duration between Fusion and Turmoil was about as long as Boda’s career, or at least his future career after Spring Fling. Besides, Turmoil stands as a chance to prove what I’m capable of…against a respected opponent, with absolutely nothing on the line. And with these copious amounts of raw, unconstrained talent…how can I lose? Despite Miyagawa’s best efforts, I’m still pretty much on that hot streak…I’m still highly impressive of late…and I’ll do anything to justify that….:: Chris: Well I can think of at least one person who has something against the existence of the cutout…ME! Why would I want to be accompanied by Dustin still, if I have the chance to not be followed everywhere by the guy? I mean, heck, having fans follow me to the four corners of the world doesn’t bother me…it just bothers me that at least one person in this world has to make moronic side-comments to everything I say, and most of the time it happens to be Dustin… Serenity: Don’t you just love the throwing of rotten fruit at hapless and useless schmucks? Why I could just do this all day! ::. I slap myself firmly on the forehead, cringing at the sound of the comment above. Only there’s one thing stopping it from being a moronic piss-take gesture usually subjected by Dustin. It’s actually relevant. For you see, I look up…only to find Serenity actively involved in lobbing pieces of rotten asparagus at the comedia on stage, who has subsequently broken down into a state of disrepair. Leaping up from my seat, I grab the Dustin cutout and Serenity’s arm...before dragging her out of the tavern – kicking and screaming and lobbing small pieces of fruit at me….:: Ten Minutes and lots of Fruit thrown later… Serenity: WHY’D YOU HAVE TO GO AND DO THAT…Y-Y-YOU SCHMUCK! I WAS HAVING FUN FOR YOUR SAKE! PUT ME DOWN DAMNIT! ::. To be Continued…Fade to White….::
