Resting After The Storm...Bringer?

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-12-02 The helicopter touched down softly on the helipad…if it could be called that. Rather than touch down in Mexico City for Turmoil, Skitzo had decided it would be best to head straight for Sacramento with his mystery partner, whom P.W.A. would surely not be pleased to see. Skitzo: Like I give a Damn Skippy what Count Dragula and an Acme reject think about me, Friday is going to see their judgement day once they get a look at my partner! Ain’t that right BOYO!!! From inside the cage, a gurgling roar sounds out, and Skitzo grins wide from ear to ear. A ramp opens from the helicopter and Skitzo drives the buggy carrying the cage out onto the ground. As The Mental One prepares to happily drive off, a man in a navy suit exits the helicopter and stops him. Skitzo: What do you want? I’ve got some vermin need exterminated! Man: Mr. Williams you have remember, what you carry in this cage is extremely dangerous and should not under any circumstances be let loose-… Skitzo: YADDA! YADDA! YADDA! I heard it all before Joe, I know what I’m doing now if you’ll just feck off, I’ll be on my way! With that, Skitzo pushes the man out of the way and skitters off on the buggy towards the busy streets of Sacramento, California! People and children wave to him as he drives along the street, causing a little traffic to build up but not enough as to frustrate anyone. Skitzo: We should really book into a hotel or something for the week before Fusion… Skitzo: There’s a perfectly good cage behind us, we can use that for the duration! Skitzo: If you want to go sleep with the monster then be my guest but don’t wake up wondering where your torso has gone and blame me! Skitzo: Well there wasn’t much of a journey from Louisiana to here; I think we best just get a half hour’s rest before heading out and doing our thing! Skitzo: Yeah I’m thinking a half hour sounds good, especially after rescuing that little minx Ellie~! on Adrenaline, OOOOOOWOWOWOWOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Putting his foot to the floor, Skitzo races at 4pmh down the road to the nearest hotel, while the beast in the cage begins to get a little restless in the cage. As he pulls to the side of the road next to a little quiet looking hotel, more people begin to pass by, saluting the Gatorade-preaching schizophrenic. Looking at the entrance to the little building and the big steel cage, Skitzo scratches his head in confusion, before a sinister twinkle glistens in his eye. The receptionist gasps in horror as the metal structure cascades through the front window, and the sound of 12 Skitzos fusing back together forces her up out of her chair in a rage. Pulling open the front door of the hotel, she looks out only to be bombarded by Mr. Psycho, who leaps past her and grabs hold of the cage, looking around suspiciously before resuming a normal pose. Skitzo: I’d like to book a room please… Receptionist: ARE YOU FUCKING CRAZY MAN YOU JUST CAN’T BARGE IN HERE AND THROW A GODDAMNED BOX THROUGH THE WINDOW AND EXPECT TO JUST GET AWAY WITH A GODDAMN ROOM DO YOU UNDERSTAND WHAT I’M SAYING YOU KNOW YOU BETTER ELSE I’M CALLING THE COPS ON YOUR ASS SO WHADDAYA GOTTA SAY FOR YOURSELF YOU PIECE OF SHIT YOU BETTER HAVE A GOOD EXCUSE FOR BREAKING THAT WINDOW OR BY GOD I WILL CALL THE COOOOoooooppppssssssssssssssssssssssssss-…zzzzzzzzzzzzzzz… Skitzo pulls back his arm and smiles as the trapezius nerve hold drops the receptionist immediately, sending her into a deep sleep. Skitzo: I want to look up her skirt… Skitzo: No you pervert we need to keep a semi-low profile here… Skitzo: And destroying a hotel and taking out a receptionist is the way to do that? Skitzo: Let’s just pick a room key and get some rest! And so Mr. Psycho grabs a random room key, tossing a cheque to the downed receptionist to pay for everything…including the damage caused as he implements his Gatorlicious manoeuvre into moving the cage into his room, luckily enough the nearest one on the ground floor. As he planks the cage down Skitzo hears an ungodly roar and looks at the side of the cage to see none other than Bobby Stormbringer trapped fully under the cage bar his head! Skitzo: I thought they killed you? Bobby: You can’t kill what you can’t see!! Skitzo: Covering your eyes does not constitute as invisibility… Bobby: It did when I made love to AIMZ!!! Skitzo: Again…that was a horse’s arse… Bobby: CURSES!!! Ohh, I just realised I am trapped underneath this cage…help me out fare schizoid? Skitzo: Why should I? I could do everyone a favour and kill you… Bobby: Oh be nice, life is hard enough after I get banged hard by batty Irish boys named McGonnell, give a guy a break, eh? Skitzo: Hmmmm…do you know who is in the cage? Skitzo: He’s very special you know… Skitzo kneels down beside Bobby and whispers into his ear the name of the occupant. Immediately Bobby’s head begins thrashing about and he screams to high heavens. Bobby: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!! LET ME OUT OF HERE!!! I CAN’T BE HERE NEXT TO THAT BEAST!!!!! LET ME OUT!!!!!!!! GRAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!!!!!! GET FIRESNATCH!!!!!!! GET HELP!!!!!!! GNAAAAAARGH!!!!!! Skitzo promptly grabs an electric wire and a cup of water from the dresser adjacent to the door. Tipping the water on Bobby, Skitzo takes the electrical wire and jams it into his eyeball, and watches as Bobby Stormbringer explodes in a flurry of red hair and whiny crap. Stranger yet, immediately he yawns and heads over to his bed as though nothing had ever happened…and closes his eyes for the resting period ahead… Skitzo: P.W.A. are gonna be sorry… Skitzo: They best have a grave ready, they’re dead… Skitzo: Nerezza especially… Skitzo: Ah well……………NIGHT NIGHT EV’RYBODY!!!!!!!!! The End…or is it?
