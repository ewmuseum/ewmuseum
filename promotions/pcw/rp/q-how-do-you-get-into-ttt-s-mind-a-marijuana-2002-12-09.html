Q: How do you get into TTT's mind? A: Marijuana

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th Dec, 02 The last time we saw Chet King, he was hanging out at a beach and met a surfy named Derek. The reason for Chet doing this, as the beach is not his usual scene, was in an attempt to get in the mind set of his opponent for this coming Tuesday Turmoil, Totally Tubular Tommy, as Chet’s philosophy is to beat your opponent, you must be able to think like your opponent. Chet felt TTT mind worked like that of a surfy, and thus to get into TTT’s mind he needed to hang out with some. We left off with Derek offering to take Chet to the local surf club, to ‘chill out’ with some friends of his by the use of weed. Chet had to think hard about this offer, as Chet isn’t a drug user but he realized that this may be the best possible chance to think like TTT, and that’s why he accepted the offer. The scene opens at the surf club where we see in a corner of the room a yellow couch in front of a small coffee table, where four men sit. One of the men is Chet King who is wearing a Chat King Wrestling Show t-shirt and a pair of blue board shorts. Next to him is Derek who is wearing just a pair of black board shorts, and next too him is a Jamaican guy wearing a t-shrit with a picture of a hemp leaf and black board shorts.. Finally on the end of the couch is another local surfer wearing a white t-shirt and green board shorts. In this guy on the ends hand is a Gatorade (Plug for PCW’s official soft drink!) bottle with two holes burnt into the top of it. One hole has a section of hose in it, and a small cone made out of a soft drink bottle sitting on top of the hose. Derek: Chet dude, you like smoking the funky green shit? Chet King: Actually I don’t smoke at all. I can’t afford to. I’ve got to keep my health intact for work. Derek: Is work all you think about dude? You’ve got to learn chill out bro, and look at things from a bodacious point of view. It’s awesome! Chet looks confused, not fully understanding what Derek just said. Chet King: I will have some weed and “chill out” with you today, but only to get in the mind of Totally Tubular Tommy, not because I want to, as I condemn drug usage. Derek: Right on dude! The local surfer on the end of the couch then reaches onto the table and takes a bit of marijuana out of a wooden bowl situated on the table. He then sticks the marijuana into the cone , and places one of his fingers onto the second hole. Local Surfer: Wanna light this skunk up for me dude? Jamaican: Sure mon. The Jamaican pulls a lighter out of his shorts, flicks it onto the cone piece as the local surfer places his mouth on top of the bong and inhales as the marijuana smoke flies through the bottle. As he finishes he lets out a bit of a cough. Local Surfer: Dude, that shit is radical! Jamaican: Mon, let me have a hit of it. The local surfer hands over the bong to the Jamaican. Jamaican: Respect brother. The Jamaican sets everything up, then takes his hit and as he finishes he lets out a big smile. Jamaican: That is some awesome whacky tobaccy, mon! Derek: Dude, I want some, this should be like totally far out and bodacious! As Derek takes the bong, the Jamaican and local surfer start laughing mindlessly. Chet King: What’s so funny? This comment causes the two to keep laughing further. Derek: Dude, you just got to have some of this funky stuff, then you’ll know what these dudes are laughing about. As Derek takes his hit and smoke leaves the chamber, his eyes grow wide. Derek: Whoa! Dude! This is gnarly! The Jamaican, who is still laughing, looks at Chet. Jamaican: It’s your turn now, mon. Chet looks reluctant as he takes the bong from Derek. He turns to the others for support, but they just laugh. Chet King: Well if I want to be able to think like TTT I guess I better do this. Chet convinces himself to do it as he grabs some weed from the bowl on the table. He then places it in the cone piece and takes the lighter from Derek. He places his finger over the second hole then prepares to put his mouth over the bottle and light up. Chet King: Here goes nothing. Chet then takes his hit, which causes him to drop the bong and go into a fit of coughing. Derek, the Jamaican and the local surfer just laugh at Chet. Chet King: That is harsh. Chet starts to revive and stop coughing as the others continue to just laugh at Chet. Chet King: Dude, that is really harsh. Chet’s eyes suddenly open wide. Chet King: Dude, did I just say dude? Chet then starts joining in the laughter of the others. Chet King: Whoa dude! This stuff has done it! My mind has like totally turned into the mind of Totally Tubular Tommy, like totally! Derek: That’s awesome dude! Jamaican: Yah, that’s awesome, mon! Local Surfer: That’s cool bro. Chet King: It’s gnarly! The four men burst out into uncontained laughter once more. Chet King: You know, my mind is working like TTT’s, and dude that means I can work out what he’s thinking and how to out think him. Local Surfer: You said think two times in the one sentence, that made me think! This absolute random, non sensical comment brings forth a bout of hysteria from the four men. Derek: Dude, that’s the funniest thing I’ve ever heard, you should like totally be a comedian. Local Surfer: That would be so radical. I’d be totally famous. Chet King: Hahaha, I’m already famous. Jamaican: Don’t lie, mon. Chet King: I’m telling the truth dude. I’m like a wrestler and I got to fight like a guy called Totally Tubular Tommy, who’s like you guys, meaning he’s like stupid or something, hahaha! And I’ve also got to like fight guys called Tim Walden, Tommy again, Rush and this guy called Dante Jackson who like pissed me off the other day dude! Local Surfer: Whoa! Chet King: I know dude, and I get to team with these gnarly dudes called The HIT, The End and Snow. They’re all radical dudes! Hahaha! Local Surfer: Whoa! Chet King: And I’m also famous, because, like, I’m a chat show host, which is why I’m wearing this t-shirt with the name of my show on it. Local Surfer: Whoa! Chet King: Hahaha, it’s so cool. Local Surfer: Whoa! Once again the men have just started laughing again as Derek just finishes taking another hit from the bong. Jamaican: You know, mon, I was on a chat show once, the Jerry Springer show actually. Chet King: Was it awesome? Jamacian: Mon, they got me to play the role of a husband who was angry that his wives brothers dogs sisters best friends owners nephews teachers cats enemies owners fathers husbands doctors plumbers mailmans friend was cheating on their lover! Chet King: Dude, you mean the Jerry Springer Show is set up? Jamaican: I think so, mon, unless I really was angry that my wives brothers dogs sisters best friends owners nephews teachers cats enemies owners fathers husbands doctors plumbers mailman’s friend was cheating on their lover. Chet King: I can’t believe it dude, this is so trippy, I always thought that show was real. At least I know my show will never be fake! The men sit there for a moment contemplating Chet’s last comment, until they all end up laughing. And as these men laugh, we leave the scene, safe with the knowledge that Chet King now knows just how Totally Tubular Tommy thinks!
