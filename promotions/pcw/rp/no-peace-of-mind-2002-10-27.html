No Peace of Mind

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.27.02 The abandoned Love Box warehouse, South Chicago… “…76…77…78…79…80…” -A single bead of sweat trickles down the bridge of Rich “The Renegade” Rollins’ nose, hanging on the tip for a single moment before splattering somewhere on the cold concrete floor. With his fingers laced behind his head, Rollins performs sit-up after sit-up, his steady blue eyes concentrated on some unseen figure in the distance, totally focused. The city is quiet in the industrial district once the sun goes down, the occasional squawk of a car horn off in the distance wafting in through the skylights above. Listening to some inner voice, The Renegade’s mouth moves ever so slightly, barely forming words here and there but never uttering one. His eyes never waiver from their distant gaze, locked on to some ethereal object that can only be seen using the mind’s eye. In the safety of this silence, he pushes harder.- “…98…99…100…101…” -His muscles bulge with effort of his 255-pound body pulling on his bare midsection, every sinew of his being oblivious to everything except the pain… the struggle… the ring… - Lacey: Rich? You still awake? -Dressed down in one of The Renegade’s old Black Sabbath tee shirts, Lacey Reinking emerges from his “bedroom,” an old manager’s office converted into minimal living quarters. Her long strains of shimmering blonde hair lay in a tangled mess atop her head, but with the effortless motion of her hand, the tangles unfurl around her fingers to smooth out around her shoulders. Squinting in the darkness of the storage area, Lacey finally notices Rollins in his intense frame of mind.- Lacey: You’ve got to love a man with stamina… done but not quite finished, right Rich? -Laughing, Rollins slumps onto his back, laying on his crossed forearms.- Renegade: Gotta love a woman with a sense of humor, too. (he looks at her) I’m just doing my thing, Lace. I always train from about midnight until I get tired, I suppose. Usually do this for an hour, then I sleep. Lacey: (grinning) Not when I’m around, you don’t. -Rollins doesn’t respond, only smiles. Her very presence seems to emanate a warm sense of comfort, a feeling that most definitely puts a smile on The Renegade’s face. But the look in Rollins’ eyes… Lacey knows that look. Despite his best attempts at hiding it, Lacey knows Rich Rollins’ pain. She knows it all too well because she was part of it.- Lacey: You’re thinking about your brother, aren’t you? -The smile on his face fades back to his familiar rock-hard scowl, his eyes looking away from Lacey.- Renegade: No. Lacey: You’re lying. He goes on trial for murder next week, Rich. I know how you feel, believe me, but you have to let it out. -A dead silence descends over the both of them. A handful of heartbeats pass in the darkness before Rollins answers, his words sending an eerie chill down Lacey’s spine.- Renegade: Jason killed innocent people. He deserves to die. -Ignoring Lacey’s startled look, The Renegade steels himself once more, hunching forward to his knees and back down again, continuing his sit-up marathon. His eyes return to their stoic state of being, blankly seeing a world that does not exist.- “…114…115…116…” Lacey: Dammit, don’t ignore me like that! Shutting me out isn’t going to move mountains, Rich. And the problem in your head… in both our heads… is more than we can manage alone. If you won’t talk to me, than at least listen, okay? “…120…121…122…” Lacey: (swallowing her anger) You’re a gifted man, Rich Rollins. You’ve got a life that people can only dream about, and have done things and gone places I’ve only read about in brochures. I mean, you were in Israel last Friday, you fly to Rome in two days, next Friday you travel to Switzerland… look at what you have, Rich. Your brother was jealous, and that’s all. He hates you because you did what he couldn’t, and that was make a difference with his life. He killed people…(she pauses, pushing the tears from her throat)… he killed people because it was a step above what you do, Rich; he tried to outdo you in the only way he knew how. He’s insane, end of story. You are not like him, do you hear me? Rich, look at me! “…134…135…136…” SLAP! -Her face flushed with frustration, Lacey watches the red mark on Rollins’ cheek fade in, forming the impression of her small hand. Her hand stings, but not nearly as much as her heart.- -Because in spite of it all, Rich Rollins continues his work.- “…139…140…” -She watches him for a few moments, wiping a tear from the corner of her eye. The Renegade’s mouth moves once again, forming words that have no sound or meaning save to Rollins himself. An inhuman suffering distorts his stony visage, a suffering that is lined by the bitter daggers of guilt.- “…149…150.” -The Renegade finally stops, collapsing onto his back and heaving for air. His stomach burns with the searing fire of exhaustion, but he pushes it aside, albeit not easily. Lacey just continues to look into his dark eyes, eyes that seem long since dead.- Renegade: I tried to help them, you know. -The words are soft, but in their quiet murmur is a fear that Lacey had never heard from his lips until now. It frightened her.- Renegade: Mom and Jason both; I tried sending them money, but all I ever got was a big “Fuck you!” from Jason. He thought I abandoned them. (he laughs, strained due to the pain in his stomach) I couldn’t watch mom cash in one more welfare check and keep my sanity, Lace. I mean, who could? All I wanted was to help… and then it wasn’t wanted. So how does Jason repay me for a nine-year guilt trip? -Rollins turns now, looking into Lacey’s sympathetic eyes with difficulty. A single tear rolls down his right cheek, the first tear to fall down his face since before he can remember.- Renegade: He slaughters innocent men and women, just to get back at me. That’s my blood, Lacey. He’s my kin, he shares the same genetic code as me, and he fucking snaps like a twig. When will my turn come, Lace? (he pauses, the words sinking into his own ears) Where’s my breaking point? I don’t have a family anymore, and all I have to fill the void is wrestling. My job allows me to physically abuse my opponents… (whispering)… to beat them until they can’t move. Lacey: Rich, you… Renegade: (interrupting) I don’t have the capacity to keep it all straight, Lace. If I dwell on my brother and his… (he swallows hard) crimes, I take an aggression to the ring that I don’t want. If I’m not careful, I’ll end up right there with him. A murderer. -The finality of his words strikes Lacey hard, driving a needle the size of a fist straight through her heart. A thousand different expressions of sympathy flood her mind, but she knows he doesn’t want her sympathy. No man feels secure about his weaknesses, most of all Rich Rollins. He’d likely get even more upset if she started offering emotional support, so she responds with the only sure-fire answer available.- -Wrestling.- Lacey: (trying to be cheerful) So, any plans on paying back Mark Michigan? I think he was a little too cocky to swing… Renegade: (coldly) I might kill him. -Her words die on her lips at his icy retort, the look in his eyes as chilling to match. Another uncomfortable silence stretches between them, but this time is broken by The Renegade.- Renegade: You see, I can’t juggle my life like this. I have to make the choice between my fucked up personal life, or wrestling. Guess what? I choose wrestling. I have a four-way match on Tuesday, a three way dance next Friday, and all in different countries. I have five different opponents to worry about right now, and one man to focus on. Skylar Green is the only person I care about right now. My brother isn’t even on the radar, and you know why? (pause) I don’t want to do something I may regret. -Her mind numb from thought, Lacey just watches Rollins’ hard features watch her, unsure of what to say or do. Looking down to the small towel The Renegade is sitting on, a small electric clock flashes the time 1:03 by his side. Picking up the clock, Lacey fiddles with the buttons and sets it back down, rising from her crouched position.- Lacey: Well, I better let you get started. Renegade: Get started with what? Lacey: (motioning to the clock) With your working out. It’s daylight saving’s time, and technically you’ve only been here for three minutes. You should get started. -Turning on her bare heel, Lacey disappears back into the darkness of Rollins’ bedroom, closing the door gently behind her. The Renegade watches her leave, his tongue tied. Looking down at his clock, the time 12:04 flashes at him harshly, taunting his very sanity.- -With nothing else to do, Rollins lays back down and begins to count.- “1…2…3…4…” ~Fin~
