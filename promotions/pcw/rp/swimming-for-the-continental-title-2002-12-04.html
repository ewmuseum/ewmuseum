Swimming For The Continental Title

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 4, 2002 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude! Gimme a ride to the pool! ['Heavy Metal' Harry, decked out in his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots, is kicked back in the driver seat of his far out golf cart, The Twisted Mobile. With the new installation of a bitchin' CD Player, Kenwood of course, Harry can jam out to Twisted Sister with the greatest of ease. Tommy has already leapt into the passenger seat wearing only his yellow speedos. His tanned muscular body flexes as he holds onto the roof of the golf cart] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Far out, man. [Harry slams on the gas pedal as the small tires make a tiny screeching sound...] TTT: Nice peel out job, compadre. Harry: Thanks, man. [The golf cart travels down a twenty foot path, for a grand total of twenty seconds] Harry: Last stop...the pool, man. [Tommy looks over to his brother and former tag team partner with a one million dollar smile] TTT: Thanks, dude! [Tommy slides out of the passenger seat and dives into the pool. Harry throws the cart into reverse as to not fall into the pool with his ride...again. Tommy resurfaces halfway into his swimming pool, which is shaped like a surfboard by the way. His blonde locks are now matted down with water as he paddles his way toward the opposite side of the pool in record time. With Tommy's gnarly endurance and stamina he can swim around the pool for hours on end without even getting tired. His body is a totally bodacious high performance machine, he has to make sure it stays at the top of its game. Anything less is totally heinous] TTT: You have to admit, that was a most EXCELLENT victory over Rush on Fusion! Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: You were there at ringside, cheering me on...just like in the NTICW days. In staying with that righteous tradition I kicked the living surf wax out of Rush, just like in the NTICW days. Pretty gnarly beginning to a pretty gnarly singles career, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy emerges himself under water and pushes off the end of the pool and begins his set of butterfly strokes. His entire body feeling the burn from the most difficult stroke. In speaking of stoke, Harry hasn't done that for a very long time...swimming, of course. Tommy goes back and forth for many repititions, as Harry watches on feeling most dizzy from it. Tommy's mind moves in every direction as he pushes himself to move faster, keep good form, work his bodacious muscles. The Continental Title is what has plagued his mind since defeating Rush on Fusion. The Chef is gone, which means the title is up for grabs and how gnarly is it that The Tubular One is entered in the four way dance for the title. The End, Tim Walden, and H.I.T are the men that will be in the opposite corners, and all are very respectable opponents. They can be respectable all they want but they will never match up to the bodaciousness that it takes to be Tommy. All three men have fought hard to get to this point, but they will fall short to the sheer Tubular facts...Tommy is SOOOO much better than them. The End seems to be the only real problem in all of this. Not because of his skill, but because of his bogus attitude] TTT: Harry... Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Do you happen to know where my blue speedos went to? [Harry pauses for a few moments, looking Tommy in the eye the whole time] Harry: I sold them, man. TTT: What?! Harry: I did laundry last night and some chick was talking to me, man. TTT: You gave my speedos to a babe?! Harry: She gave me 50 bucks, man. TTT: Those are my favorite speedos, compadre! How am I supposed to stay in a bodacious state of mind for my big match on Friday? Now I'm just stuck with the fact that some babe is out there with my speedos... *sigh* ...I suppose its gnarly. Did you at least wash them first? Harry: No, man. TTT: DUDE!!! That's totally disgusting! [Harry smiles and shrugs] Harry: Sorry, man...she was pretty persistent. [Tommy shakes his head with a disgusted look on his face. Maybe he should ask for new speedos for Christmas. In speaking of Christmas, as Tommy begins doing sets of the breast stroke...something he knows a lot about, that reminds Tommy of The End's bogus attitude. How he blew Tommy off because of the high magnitude of his Christmas wishes. When a man goes out to the ring and just hands out wishes to totally heinous dudes, you expect to get your wish made. The End just left Tommy standing there with nothing, only to ride off with Harry in his golf cart. He just wanted an easy victory on Fusion, which he's going to win anyway, but sometimes it's good not to put all that effort into it! It's totally heinous!! Then The End didn't give up Sandy Wong for Harry. Tommy was being totally unselfish, giving to his bodacious bro, and The End had to babble on about human beings not being slaves?! Now that drew the totally bogus line with that blind Celestial compadre! Fusion is going to be most interesting now...just wait until what happens] Harry: Do you think I'm attractive, man? [Tommy stretches out his arms, just finishing up his gnarly set of the breast stroke] TTT: Your totally beautiful, dude. [Harry gives Tommy a very confused look] Harry: Can a guy be beauiful, man? TTT: Of course a guy can, dude. Harry: But doesn't that make him gay, man? TTT: NO WAY! That's heinous, your going in the wrong direction. Your a most beautiful dude who knocks the babes out with a one-two knock out punch of Harry charm. Harry: Do I really have charm, man? TTT: You know it. [Harry smiles and nods to himself. The faint sounds of Twisted Sisters 'No Evil' playing in the background. His long brown hair hangs down just past his shoulders as, it's obvious, Harry is pondering about something] TTT: You have a bodacious babe on your mind? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Who? Harry: Sandy Wong, man. [Tommy shakes his head, not upset that Harry has a thing for her, but upset about The End blowing off that Christmas wish] TTT: Har, I'm sorry that whole wish thing didn't work out. I thought it would be full proof, but The End is TOTALLY BOGUS! Harry: Yeah, man. I thought that was my chance to be able to wine and dine sandy, but I thought maybe it didn't happen because I may be unattractive, man. [Tommy looks like he just sucked on a sour lemon] TTT: Dude, how can you go and think something like that?! That's heinous!! BOGUS!! All totally negative things fall into that category, compadre. The End is a bogus dude trying to keep the lovely Sandy Wong away from you, the much more bodacious man. [Harry nods reassuringly] Harry: Yeah, man! TTT: And once that totally gnarly Continental Title is around my waist, Sandy Wong will be coming to you begging for a shot! [Harry nods and throws his fist in the air, circa 80's rock concert style. Harry looks behind him to make sure he steers the golf cart correctly, but unfortunately the Twisted Mobile ends up barreling into the pool...again] TTT: Bogus, dude... Harry: Yeah, man. [Fade to black]
