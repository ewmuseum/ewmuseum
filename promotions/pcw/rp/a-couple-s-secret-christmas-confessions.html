A Couple's Secret Christmas Confessions

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, December Twenty-Second, 2002. The scene opens with Ariz and Misery walking down a sidewalk at their current location. The location of the sidewalk in Windsor is not really important. What is important is that the sidewalk exists, is in Windsor, and the couple are walking down it... RUSH Network Champion. A title that I have never held before. Unfortunately, though I came quite close, I was not able to win that title on Turmoil... “You did a good job on Tuesday, Riz,” Misery says to him. “I did fairly well I guess,” Ariz replies. But not well enough and that is what matters in the end. “Too bad that that guy had to start hitting you the way that he did,” she declares. “His name is Matt Hunt, dear,” Ona states. “I don’t care what his name is,” Misery yells. “The point I was trying to make is that he should not have hit you with that...that...sausage!” “Das Goldene Bratwurst, honey?” he asks. “Yes! He shouldn’t have smacked you with his bratwurst!” she exclaims. There is a short pause, during which Ariz kind of gives Misery an odd look. What she just said really did not sound right. “What?” Misery inquires. “Nothing,” Ona replies as he wipes the odd look off of his face. As he finishes his very short sentence, the couple arrives in front of a large building with windows made of stained glass and large doors. “What are we doing in front of a Catholic church?” Riz asks. “I thought you might want to go in and pray or something,” she declares. “It is Sunday, you know.” I do not know why she wanted to come here. She knows that I have not been inside of a church since my mom died. I have never liked churches anyway. There is just something about the people that go to them that kind of freak me out and the churches themselves look creepy. “I don’t want to go in there,” he murmurs. “Besides, mass is probably over anyway.” That should keep her from continuing to try to get me to go into the church. “Well,” Misery says angrily. “I’m going in. You can either go in with me or sit out here all by yourself.” Grr...I sure do hate it when she is stubborn. She slowly begins to head into the church by herself, leaving Ariz standing alone outside the church. “Alright,” Ona replies reluctantly as he catches up with her. “I’ll go inside with you.” The couple enter the church, which looks similar to just about any other Catholic church in the world. Misery looks around, seeing only a few people and a priest or two. She then takes a look at the confessional. “Say,” she declares as she walks over to the confession booth, “I’m going to go in here.” “Do you have sins to confess?” Ariz questions as he sees her go towards the confessional. “Not really,” Misery responds quickly as she opens up the door to the booth. “I just feel like going in here.” “Alright, whatever,” he states. “Do what you want.” While Ariz sits on one of the seats in the church, Misery goes in and sits down in the confessional. She then opens up the little window that allows her to speak with the priest that is sitting on the other side of the booth. “Forgive me father for I have sinned,” Misery mutters as she crosses herself. “How long has it been since your last confession?” asks the priest. “It has been far too long father,” Misery replies. “Far too long.” “How have you sinned?” the priest inquires. There is a bit of a pause as Misery thinks. “Well, I have been having sex with a man without being married to him,” she confesses. “That’s all of the sins that I can remember.” “May your sins be forgiven, my child, “ states the priest. Misery closes the little window, stands to her feet, and comes out of the booth. Ariz gets up from his seat. Well, that was an extremely boring wait. “Have fun?” Ona questions. “I guess,” she declares. I wonder what she told him. It could have been anything, from cheating on me to doing drugs. Guess it wouldn’t hurt to ask./b> “So, what did you confess?” “That’s for the priest and I to know,” Misery states, “And you to find out. Anyways, it’s your turn.” Damn it. I knew she wouldn’t tell me. I gave it a shot though. I guess it’s my turn to confess now. Ariz walks over to the confession booth. Misery walks over to the chair where Ona was when she came out and she sits down on it. “Whatever,” he says as he enters the confessional. “Might as well go in here.” Ona takes a seat in the booth and opens up the window allowing the priest, who is still sitting in the booth, to communicate with him. “Forgive me Father, for I have sinned,” The Rizmaster mumbles while crossing himself. “And how long has it been since your last confession?” the priest queries. Ariz takes a moment to think about the question. Hmm...Now that I think about it, I cannot remember the last time I confessed. I guess it was too long ago. “I can’t remember, Father. It was a long time ago,” he mutters. “That’s alright, my son,” says the priest. “Now, what is it you want to confess?” “Well Father, I have committed adultery. Not once, but twice,” Ona admits. The priest asks him, “Anything else, my son?” “No, Father. That’s all I have to confess,” Ariz replies. “May your sins be forgiven, my son,” declares the priest. After closing up the window, Ariz exits the booth. Misery stands to her feet. “Now that that’s over with,” Ariz declares, “I have to be in Jefferson in time for Turmoil tomorrow. Let’s go.” The couple begins to leave the church. They start walking up the sidewalk, back the way in which they came to the church. Time to head to Santa’s Village, Jefferson, New Hampshire for the big Carnival of Carnage III match for the PCW Tag-Team Titles that I, along with everyone else in PCW and RUSH is involved in on Turmoil. After that is a match with Stimpo on Adrenaline, which is part of a Mini-Tourney for a RUSH Network Title Shot. “You’re going to do very well this week, Riz,” Misery says excitedly. “I can feel it. You’re going to kick ass in that Carnival of Carnage. Plus, I know you’re going to beat up Stimpo on your way to the RUSH Network Title.” “I hope you’re right...I hope you’re right,” Ariz mutters as the scene fades to black.
