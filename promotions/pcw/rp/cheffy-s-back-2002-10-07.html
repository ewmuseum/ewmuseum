Cheffy's Back

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/7/02 “Guess Who’s Back…Back Again…Cheffy’s Back………WHO REALLY CARES!?!?” The camera fades in… CUT!!! The camera peers back and we see a constructed kitchen set with the standard black and white tiled floor. A giant wood fire oven stands in the center with a chrome-colored refrigerator and a counter with a cutting board to the left of it and a sink and garbage disposal unit to the right. A colander hangs above the sink, changing the light sent from a stage light into many tiny little lights. The many lights look like hundreds and hundreds of stars decorating an infinite cosmos. A stagehand moves the stage light and changes the direction of the many lights, having them spin around and around to create a dizzying image. A few female back up dancers dressed as French maids walk around mindlessly as the director walks up and confronts the video’s star. Director: What is it now Alouette!?!? The Chef, dressed in his usual white chef’s uniform and hat, guzzles a Perrier and mouths off to the director and producer of his new video. The Chef: I’m tired of spewing out these Eminem lyrics! The Chef’s cloned life mate Bertha Stewart, standing and gorging herself behind the snack table salivates at the mere mention of the chocolate hard candy. Bertha: MMMMMM, M&M’s… Director: What’s wrong Cheffy? The PCW management wants you to come back in style and sell those miniature replica rolling pins on the Shopzone. Producer: And we feel that nothing will get you more “over” with the PCW fans and remind them of who you are better than a little musical parody of “Without Me”! The Chef: Good idea guys, but I think I know how to acquire a fan base… “Oh really, then why do we have 2,000 extra replica rolling pins just collecting dust in some warehouse in Hong Kong?!?!” PCW Shopzone President Dickey Quickfingers walks onto the stage and pat a few of the Chef’s female back-up dancers on the ass before finally confronting the Culinary Conquistador. The Chef: I should have known you were behind this Quickfingers! You’re a miserable penny-pincher with nothing better to do besides bossing PCW talent around. You’re like a Hotpocket without it’s artificial cheese and meat filling; you’re without a soul… Quickfingers: Am I really? The Chef: Believe you me, I could tell you were in on this project. The floor tiles aren’t waxed, the caviar is a bunch of fawn droppings painted black, and the maids DON’T EVEN HAVE FEATHER DUSTERS!!! The budget on this video was probably less than the last Beethoven movie! Isn’t there 3 now? Bertha: I think f*cking four, f*ck @$$!!! The Chef: Thanks B, (turns to Quickfingers) She’s my resident movie expert… Quickfingers: Get to the point you washed up…COOK!!! The Chef snaps. The Chef: COOK!?!? Well sir, I didn’t go to Le Cordon Bleu for eight years just to be a standard COOK!!! I am a MASTER CHEF, and you sir, are a culinary school dropout!!! Quickfingers: Heh, would a culinary school dropout….CANCEL THE PRODUCTION OF YOUR NEW T-SHIRT!?!? The Chef quickly changes his tone after realizing he might not grace a PCW T-shirt. The Chef: I’ll be good… Quickfingers: Too late, PIERRE! You’re a washed up has-been, an old fogy from PCW yesteryear, you’ve got no fan base or following; frankly I’m surprised you were reinstated!!! The Chef lowers his head in shame. Quickfingers: Management ordered me to star you in this video to boost fan interest in your reinstatement, and believe you me I protested and protested, but it was to no avail. The Chef: You hear that Bertha, someone out there really likes me!!! Bertha swallows fifteen donuts and answers. Bertha: I’m f*cking ho @$$ mother f*cking @$$ humping b*tch @$$ f*cking sh*tty as f*ck c*ck sucking ecstatic, f*cko… Quickfingers: I suggest you finish this abomination and return to you midcarder ways or you’ll tick off what few supporters you do have left! Bertha: (Angrily replying) I suggest you f*cking shut up before I f*cking f*ck you up! The Chef: Bertha, I’m very delighted you finally decided to defend me, but he’s the man that green lights T-shirt production!!! Don’t take your frustrations out on him!!! Bertha, ignoring the Chef’s pleas, knocks what food was left on the snack cart off and rolls up the sleeves on her “I Love Milwaukee” sweatshirt. Bertha: Wanna f*cking go b*tch @$$!?!? Quickfingers steps back a few paces, and then turns to the director and producer of the video. Quickfingers: Guys, help me out! Director: (Sobbing) *Sniff* *Sniff* You called my video an “abomination”!?!? I thought you loved my work!!! Quickfingers: I lied, I actually found you’re work nauseating, that’s why I hired you…TO SINK THE CAREER OF THAT CULINARY COCKSUCKER!!! Producer: You rat bastard!!! How dare you misuse such an unprecedented combination of talent!!! Quickfingers: Yeah, unprecedented in that you were the first producer and director to ever agree to film something with that no-talent French piece of crap!!! The Chef: Watch your tone Dickey, my wife has blubber, and she’s not afraid to use it… Bertha nods and approaches the Shopzone curator. As she inches closer and closer, Quickfingers begins to sweat heavily. Quickfingers: It’s…it’s getting hot in here… Bertha: Really? Well it will get pretty f*cking hot when I f*cking lodge your piece of cow sh*t f*cking skank s*cking off ho banging queer as f*ck @$$ in f*cking between some layers of my c*ck @$$ed blubber, biotch!!! Bertha stands right by Dickey Quickfingers, almost sandwiching him in the corner he’s backed up into. Quickfingers: *Gulp* Maybe…maybe I’ve underestimated…all of you…why don’t we… Bertha: (Interrupting) Why don’t you f*cking go for a b*tch @$$ f*cking ride, ho skank!!! The Chef: Bertha, NO!!!But the Mastodon doesn’t listen to the Chef’s pleas. She lifts up Quickfingers over her head into a gorilla press, and the Shopzone president soils himself immediately upon lift off. She spins Dickey around effortlessly, and IMPALES HIM ONTO THE COLD, HARD, UNFORGIVING FLOOR WITH A SIT DOWN PILEDRIVER!!! Director and Producer: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOoooooooooo!!! The Chef grimaces as he sees Quickfinger’s head bounce off the floor. Bertha stands back up and wipes her hands as if she had just taken out the trash. Quickfingers squirms on the floor, drifting into unconsciousness. The Chef: Well, I guess since ol’ Dick is out of it, we can go back to doing my video for my many fans! Director: It’s great that you have a change of heart Chef, but…I’m afraid I’m going to have to decline your offer. The Chef: What!?!? You can’t be serious!!! Producer: Yes, we’ve noticed a new specimen that really has a chance of getting over with the fans! The Chef: And who might that be?!?! Both the director and the producer immediately point at Bertha. Bertha: Hey jerk @$$es, it f*cking ain’t polite to f*cking point! The Chef: You can’t be serious!?!? Both the director and producer nod. The Chef: You are, aren’t you? They nod again. The Chef: Well that’s just great… I’ve now been upstaged by a monkey in a leotard, a walnut, the brain of Walt Disney, a Brawny paper towel, and now the clone of my wife!!! Director: A paper towel? The Chef: Yeah, that’s the last time I bet on Bounty to remove large amounts of liquid from my tabletops! Bertha: Yeah, it ain’t the f*cking quicker c*ck @$$ed mother f*cking picker ho b*tch f*ck upper like it f*cking says! Director: God, I loved those stringed together cuss words! Producer: She really is something, isn’t she Dan? I see her as the next Carnie Wilson… Director: I was thinking more along the lines of Sandra Bullock, but you make a good point, she is fat… The Chef: Ahem! Aren’t we supposed to be talking about the rejuvenation of my career!?!? Aren’t those my dancers in maid uniforms!?!? Isn’t that my…what the? The Chef watches as the “Cheffy Show” sign is taken down to make way for a bigger “Bertha Show” sign. The Chef: I hate Hollywood… Director: Bertha, can you dance? Bertha immediately lights up. Bertha: I sure f*cking can!!! She steps back and, then begins the most uncoordinated break dancing combination ever performed. Bertha: Too f*cking legit…too f*cking legit to f*cking quit…f*cking HE HEEEEEEEOOOOOOOOWWWWW!!! Director: HA!!! This is pure gold! Producer: She’s like James Brown, with a touch of Roseanne! Director: Soul Train Awards here we come! The Chef sighs a Dave Kern like sigh as he watches his portly princess steal his spotlight. The camera mercifully fades to black as Bertha and the “Berthettes” begin a dance number.
