Another one bites the dust

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-25-02 The atmosphere of the casino, lights, the people, the money, it intrigues me. It sucks me in and it doesn’t let me pull away until the closing. Rolling the dice, playing the hand, pulling the slot machine, it makes up a night of life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness. People call my winnings luck I call it talent and common sense of the game. Luck is a myth, I possess traits of a legend and that is stronger then any myth created. Dealers fear me, owners fear me. As I walk up to the blackjack table you can see the fear in the dealers eyes. Sweat pouring off the forehead. Trembling of the lips. Quiver in the body. They know me; they fear me and they know that I’m a force that they can not match and that they could lose their job if they lose to me. Three tables with one dealer at each table, they know one will be pick while the other to two will be saved. But be rest assured that someone’s lively hood will be on the line. A crowd begins to form behind Scorpion now. The crowd is anxious as to who will be picked. Inch by inch, step by step. Scorpion begins to peer ever so closer to an opponent of his choosing. Scorpion turns his head to the first dealer. Who was quivering now suddenly smiles towards Scorpion’s direction. Scorpion steps right into the face of the dealer, he looks directly into his eyes, forcing an intimidation to flow through the man’s body. Scrawny, very easy to manipulate. Could be an easy match. Scorpion backs away from the first dealer and as Scorpion turns away he shows a slight bit of relief. Scorpion turns around to the second dealer who is straight faced, motionless, trying to intimidate Scorpion. Their eyes cross paths and the intimidation flow into one another begins rejuvenate into their veins trying to weaken the other. A carbon copy of a man that wants to be just like me, a man that will fall. He thinks he can play this game then so be it, we shall see how he fares. The eyes continue the lock. No sign of emotion between the two. There faces motionless. Scorpion rapidly turns his head to the third dealer who is less occupied and shakes when Scorpion looks directly into his eyes. The terminable of the third dealer is very visible. Scorpion begins to laugh at the man. Scorpion: Why waste my time. Consider yourself saved. Third Dealer: Thank you…. Scorpion: Shut up! Third Dealer: Yes sir. Scorpion then slowly walks to the middle of the first and second table. Scorpion: I’m always up for a challenge and the second table seems more like a challenge presented. Congratulations. A sigh of relief comes from the first dealer. The two other dealers are very lucky men. Scorpion walks to the second table where a crowd forms a ring around them. Scorpion takes off his black leather trench coat. The dealer shuffles the deck not losing eye contact with Scorpion. The dealer takes the top card from the deck and puts it face down in front of Scorpion. The dealer then takes the next card and puts it face down in front of him. The next card in the deck is face up in front of Scorpion. An eight. Good solid number. He’s got a five face up. Let’s see what’s under here. An ace. One or an Eleven. Let’s go with the eleven that would give me an eighteen. Solid number. Second Dealer: Are you getting hit? Scorpion: No. Second Dealer: Then show your hand. Scorpion: Eighteen. Second Dealer: I have seventeen. Winner is Scorpion. Continue to play? Scorpion: Yeah, first one to three wins. Second Dealer: So it is sir. The dealer shuffles and gives Scorpion and himself their hand. Dam it a six face up card. I got a six face down as well. Scorpion: Hit. Second Dealer: Yes sir. Scorpion takes the card the dealer hands him from the pile. Nine. Twenty-one. Aren’t I perfect? Second Dealer: Show your hand sir. Scorpion: Twenty-one. Second Dealer: Nineteen. Winner is Scorpion. Two to zero. One more win and I out of here. The dealer shuffles again and gives Scorpion and himself their hand. Face up is two. Dam it. The face down card better be good. Face down is a five. Total are seven. Scorpion: Hit. Scorpion gets dealt another card. Three. Total of ten. Scorpion: Hit. Nine. Now that’s the card I’m looking for. Total are nineteen. The lights above the table appear to be brighter then before. The sweat above both men’s forehead is evident. The dealer takes a small handkerchief from his pocket to wipe his forehead. The crowd around the table becomes more anxious as there mouths are left open in anticipation for what the hand of both men will be. Second Dealer: Eighteen. The crowd gasps with the hand the dealer has shown. Scorpion stares at the man with a stone cold look. Scorpion: Nineteen. The crowd applauds with amazement for Scorpion. The dealer and Scorpion shake hands and Scorpion gets up from the table with people trying to shake his hand. He shakes the people off taking his money to the winnings area. They call that luck. I call it a mix between common sense and talent. Call it what they want, but this streak will never be broken.
