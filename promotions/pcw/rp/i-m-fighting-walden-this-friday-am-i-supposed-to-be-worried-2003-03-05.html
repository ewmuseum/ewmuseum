I'm Fighting Walden This Friday? Am I supposed to be...WORRIED or something?

5 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: I'm Fighting Walden This Friday? Am I supposed to be...WORRIED or something? Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-5-2003 We interrupt your usual offbeat, Rent-A-Hero promo to bring you a special news bulletin.... Geez, I can't believe I'm putting off learning how to be a 'mack' just to convey this public service announcement to Tim Walden....Tim, you are what we like to call a 'parasite'...NO NO, not that dumb finisher Syko The Goblin uses, but the kind of parasite like say, Savage Dragon comic book villain, Horde. I mean your constantly miserable and at war with the world and your YET to stop griping about trivial issues. So what do you do my miserable tax snatching friend? You just leech onto a host and make their lives HELL-O~!, tormenting them and and verbally lashing them until you feel the job is done....unfortunately, unlike the superviallin Horde... Your a REALLY sucky villain. I mean COME ON, you go on these meaningless tirades like your Dr.Doom or something! You WISH you had supervillain skills the likes of Red Skull, Dr.Doom, Kingpin, and the like. Heck, you make a henchman from BATMAN....(not the cartoon series or the movie, but the OLD SCHOOL version with the cheesy theme song and sound effects mind you)....look like a criminal mastermind.In the fight against good and evil Timmy, usually the hero has to find SOME challenge in the villain he's facing. I can certainly name countless villains I've faced in my tenure where I've had to prepare WEEKS ahead of time.Darkstar.....Clyde....Xavier Kannon... Dark Gimmick... 'Totally Tubular' Tommy... El Romantico... Frisco... And then....there's you. I mean, your BEST superpower is the ability to cause grown humanoid men's minds to cave in with annoyance due to your CONSTANT griping about who you've jobbed to, or people who got belts ahead of you, or why someone's ahead of you and your not. That's probably the reason you became 'Mr.Tax-Man' right? So you can take hard-earned money from innocent citizen after citizen because you grew tired of people snatching what LITTLE respect and dignity you had. Tim....I'm a hero with a mass of victories and losses against top-tier villains in this federation and I must say, each and EVERY fight was a grueling experience.... Then my Rent-A-Senses point me to PCW.com and I find I'm facing YOU this Fusion and all I can say is... 'YOWZA'S OF BOWSER! Am I being punished or something?'It doesn't take a Kryptonian plutonioum scientist to figure out that I have NO respect for you whatsoever in terms of skill or character. You make a big deal about making people's lives heck...making these big promises of pain and torture, and you've actually PLOTTED your attacks on various stars. But the most entertaining part as always...is when you fail. To WATCH you try to be some kind of criminal mastermind is like trying to watch Dick Dastardly's dog, Mutley, attempt to hold the world ransom with a plot on the scale of Galactus' from Fantastic Four issue #1236.... In layman's terms...KNOW YOUR PLACE Walden. We all have a niche we can be classified under Rent-A-Hero....superhero Clyde....sadistic sicko with an insatiable bloodlustPete!.....the goofy, loveable underdog we all love to cheerXavier Kannon....the greediest mastermind to EVER hit PCW since Tyler Nelson... Tim Walden....LACKEY!!Your overstepping your humanoid boundaries Walden, because lackey-dom is the life for you. LOOK AT YOUR QUALIFICATIONS?! Your spineless, a 'yes' man, you can't seem to get things done on your own, and your SUCH a hateable weasel. Your a lackey with honors. You'd put Destro from G.I. Joe to shame!Do you ever see a nameless henchman in any comic you read trying to do the supervillain's job....OF COURSE NOT YOU FOUL FIEND! So with that in mind, you need to take this piece of advice from the ONE and only Rent-A-Hero... When your not riding another villain's cape, you are nothing. Your plans are worth about as much as a headless Gummi Bear and your doomed to fail when it really counts. This Friday.....there will be no 'classic battle of good vs. evil'... It's just the superhero vs. a faceless henchman, and you KNOW what happens to the henchman. Get this routine through your head before Friday Tim....KICK, WHAM, GRAVITY DEFIER, because that's about as far as the match is gonna go lackey boy. I'd piledrive you, but you'd have to have a spine for it to do any damage! All you are is just a roadblock to the IMPORTANT villains out there.... The Kannon's...The Violence Jack's... The 'Totally Tubular' Tommy's....EVEN the Dorn Tracer's.... You've always been Bullseye trying to do Kingpin's job, Walden. If you think Friday will be your 'big chance' to prove to the world you are a superior villain....think again. You will recieve the obligatory.....!!~SPLAT~!!.....after your beating and fade off, plotting plans destined to fail yet again Walden.Nobody needs to call Miss Cleo for her to tell them your going to lose this Friday....since when did anybody ever worry about Superman getting beat by 'Random Henchman #5'?! On a side note, I hear MexiDragon....or DragonMexi...or UndeadMexiDragonMan is bad again, I'm sure he'd LOVE to have a serviceable lackey again Walden, don't you think?Now if you'll excuse me, I'll be 'training hard' for our match!(If you call playing Playstation 2 'training.) I'll see you SAME RENT-A-TIME....SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL....Not like I have to tune in to know how our battle is going to end however. So gripe on that, TAX-MAN! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
