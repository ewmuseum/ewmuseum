Date: I don&#039;t know what today is... Sheets of rain were clashing down upon the ground in layers about layers. Thunder crackled and lighting brightly lit up the sky. It was very cold as well, and typically not a good weather to be out in. Eddy was inside his hotel, sipping tea and watching TV. He was in warm clothing, with a blue short sleeved over his black long sleeved. He has black sweats and was sitting on the bed, neatly laid out with a forest-esque design. He sat the tea down and began to recall his last match against El Romantico. 'That whole ordeal was truly confusing…To be honest. I came in trying my hardest, yet I still didn’t win the title. But I won the match…', said Eddy, talking to himself out loud. Eddy sipped the tea, which was very weak in Eddy’s opinion but he didn’t argue. He tried to be calm, and he never got angry. 'I won, yes, I did…Yet I didn’t win the title. I thought I had the chance to win until he smacked me over the head with the chair…'. The camera zoomed in closer to his forehead, which was a shade of red. 'Sure, I won, I feel proud for that. But it was by DQ. Even though I thought I could’ve won anyway, I just never like to take a win like this. I didn’t really get any good on my part…' Thunder crackled loudly in the background as Eddy flipped through the channels to spot something good. With nothing that caught his eye, he sighed and flipped off the TV. He reached on the wooden night stand where his cell phone was and dialed. He was calling to check his messages from home. He wanted to know who was calling, who had called him, ETC. 'Hello. Please enter your digit code.' Eddy pressed three numbers and waited. 'You have….2….messages.' 'Hey! Eddy! It’s Kirk, with Bradley in the background….*glass breaks and cat screeches*…..Yeah, anyway, we’d like to say you did great on your match although you should’ve won the title! You won though be happy about that….Bradley! Stop trying to milk the cat……Anyway, see ya.' *BEEP* 'Eddy…it’s me', said a mysterious voice Eddy recognized. Eddy paid attention more. 'We….have your sister.' Great, it’s the cops! 'This is Frankie Robertso.' Eddy froze. The man who sold drugs to his sister. 'Your sister is here, she’d like to say hi….Melissa?' Eddy was shocked as a female voice came on the phone. 'Eddy, get me out of this! Please! They’re beating me and….OW! Please, please, please, I beg you Eddy, I won’t do drugs anymore, just save me!' His sister screamed and she was tugged away from the phone on the other line as Frankie Robertso came back. 'There you have it. Come to Fusion, and you can have your sister…' Eddy sighed in relief. 'But it’s not going to be easy.' *BEEP* Eddy didn’t know if we was scared, shocked, relieved or angry. But whatever emotion it was, he knew he had to get to Iowa in order to see his sister. Even though it was pouring down so hard, and Eddy felt comfortable, he was to run off. He flipped off everything, lifted his backpack up and began to walk outside. Not packing an umbrella, he lifted his backpack over his head and dashed to the lobby to pay his bill. He opened the door, and walked in, backpack dripping. He walked up to the clerk, a middle aged Hispanic man with a small ponytail and a black beard and slapped the bill down, and told him to cash his room out. Confused, the man nodded and Eddy walked back outside again and into the pouring of the rain. He ran quickly to his truck, having trouble getting his keys out and using the wrong keys, at about the 3rd key he got it and opened the door, soaked and soaking the seat he was in. He turned the ignition, and began off to the airport. He made huge splashes as he drove fast over puddles, as the puddles usually splashed onto his windshield. He turned on his wipers and continued driving. Later… In the airport echoing area where announcements were made echoing on a pretty empty night for the airport, Eddy just walks in, dripping wet with his hair soaked and his backpack dripping looked up at the TV’s above him to check for any Iowa flights, or anything close. Of course, due to the harsh raining, everything was delayed. Eddy was in this situation more then once in his life. He had always waited for flights, but in the end in works and he got on the flight. But he the worst gut feeling it wasn’t tonight. Maybe he was just too pessimistic at the time, however, he sat down on the bench without assuming anything and waited for 30 minutes. It felt like 3 hours to Eddy, but when it was all over, it happened: 'Ladies and gentlemen, all flights are resumed.' Eddy was happy inside, until he heard the next words. 'Except for a few exceptions.' He hoped Iowa wasn’t one. He was tense for the next few seconds waiting for the woman to state the flights. 'The flight to Portland, Oregon, has been cancelled.' Eddy heard a man say 'Damnit!' and turned to see a man in a brown jacket and black jeans with a black cap. He began to storm out. 'The flight to Raleigh, North Carolina has been delayed.' Eddy waited, he didn’t know if she would say Iowa. '….And finally…' Eddy stood up. '…The flight too Salt Lake City, Utah has been delayed.' Eddy sighed. No Des Moines, Iowa. He felt relieved, and went up to buy a ticket. The clerk was a middle aged red head woman with diamond ear rings and a uniform that every staff member of the airport had. 'Hello. I’d like to buy for Des Moines, Iowa.' The lady began typing up on the computer. 'Well, you are a bit out of luck, the Des Moines flight is going to be off in 5 minutes.' 'What? Will there be anything before Friday to Des Moines?' The lady typed up again. 'There’s one Saturday, but nope, nothing for Des Moines except for today.' Eddy was tense. 'Can you give me the ticket now? I really, REALLY need to go.' 'If you can run your ass off to the plane, I guess I won’t stop you. But it’s 4 minutes until take off.' Eddy pulled out his wet cash. 'Here…', said Eddy, slapping down the money and making that counter wet. The lady looked oddly at the money but sighed and took it, then pulled a ticket out. 'Well, run your ass off, you have under 4 minutes to go.' He ran up the hallway, backpack falling off his shoulder and he has about 1:30 seconds left. Eddy then saw his worst site: The bag check. He threw his backpack on it, on waited for it to be scanned. He waited, then once is was out, he ran through the scanner and passed as he dashed up to his flight, seconds left of the minute. Eddy nodded and ran full speed to the gate for Iowa. Full speed. He was a bit confused to find the gate, which took a minute off. 3 minutes. Eddy realized he was going into the wrong gate, and saw that Des Moines was all the way across the airport. 2 minutes. Eddy ran like a fool to the gate and ran right through, showing a security guard the ticket. 1 minute. 'Oh God…' Then, Eddy tripped, falling to the ground. Eddy noticed his shoe was untied, but didn’t care. He jumped up again, then looked outside. His plane was flying off, taunting him as it flew away.
