The Bellzier Chronicles: Of Dreams and Nightmares [Part Four]

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 27, 2002 DreamLandTime Unknown Ah water. The drink of the gods, nature’s loving nectar. It sure does taste like syrup. Oh, ahem, excuse me. Well, I didn’t know the scene had faded in so quickly, otherwise I would have made myself a bit presentable. Still don’t know what’s going on? Let me elaborate a bit for you. The scene has just faded into a secluded forest clearing, where there is a small water fall draining a large river into a lake. I, Tom Lexian, am standing beneath the waterfall, bare butt naked, trying to take a shower. Yeah, so this isn’t exactly what you want to be seeing at the moment. Well, here’s a newsflash: This isn’t something I want you guys to be seeing at the moment either. Hah! NEWSFLASH: TOM LEXIAN HAS BEEN ILLEGALLY FLASHING FLYING SQUIRRELS NEAR WATERFALLS! Damn newsflashes. Anyways, just as a bit of a recap, I’m still stuck here in Dream Land. Man, that really pisses me off. I really hate it here, you know? Eh, I’m sure you do. Everything here seems to be able to walk, talk, and carry on a semi-intelligible conversation with each other, which is just so damn annoying, I think I’m going to rip all of the hair out of my head. Now, you’re probably wondering why in the hell I’m taking a shower under “this thar” waterfall anyways. It’s quite simple, my dear Sherlock – I was dirty. From the second I took a horrific fall down a treacherous hole, to the time I was thrown into a lake of Jello, from the time I captured a Jello warrior, I was just a dirty, dirty person. Too bad dirt wasn’t as lame as it is back in my own world. Here, it just sits on you and collects. I was half expecting the stuff to jump off of me and grow intelligence somehow. Ah, the joys of life. Taking a shower wasn’t exactly my own original idea. Traveling with Sheri, apparently the smartest girl in all of Dream Land, and a Jello Warrior, has made me some sort of a superstar. They still didn’t know the whole story about me, but I was going to change that. I assumed I would if I would get any more of their cooperation. Aside from that, they flat out told me that I stink and that I needed to change my clothes or take a shower. So I did. The place look fairly secluded, so I felt relatively safe. That was, until a talking dolphin made my acquaintance. “Ahhh! You’re naked!” A dolphin said, poking its head up from out of the water. Hmmm….a perceptive little mammal, isn’t he? I turned around, realizing that I was still standing there stark naked. Not a good time to get bashful, then again, what do I have to be worried about? It’s only a dolphin. “What exactly are you looking at? Don’t you have some sharks to go beat up or something?” “Sharks? I hate sharks! They’re so mean and they think they’re better than everyone just because they have teeth. What people forget is that we have teeth too! See!” The dolphin smiled at me, showing off a nice pair of pearly whites. I examined the more closely. Damn, those were really nice. He must have had dolphin braces or something. “Look, you have nice teeth and all, but could you leave please?” I asked, rubbing soap over my arms again. “I would actually like to have some privacy while I’m taking a shower…” The dolphin scoffed and batted some water in my direction. “All of you humans…you think just because you have protruding genitalia that you are something special. Let me tell you something – you’re nothing special to me, okay? Actually, you’re below normal. “ Is he insulting my manhood? Yes, yes I think he is. Grrr…time to morph into angry mode. “Are you trying to insult me?” I demanded. “Do you want to make me mad? You don’t want to see me when I’m angry, bud.” “Oh, tough words from the man who’s naked under a waterfall. Heed some advice from someone who’s actually been in Dream Land longer than a nightfall. You see, it’s obvious to me that you’re an outsider. You stand out here more than an elephant in a snake exhibit at the zoo. I’ve seen a lot of you guys around, and frankly, I don’t like it.” “What others?” I asked, seeming more curious than ever. “You people don’t belong here, no more than I would belong in your world. But of course, these damn power struggles just keep occurring, and they think rather than fighting amongst themselves, they’ll bring off worlders in to do their own dirty work. Boy, that’s just plain lame.” “But that’s the whole point, isn’t it? To be as lame as humanly possible….” It seemed all too obvious to me. “Well, I’m not human, so that rules me out. But things have gotten way too lame for me around here. I mean, I’m always explaining what this world means and how to live in it, and that’s just plain lame. I can’t stand it anymore.” “What is this place about, anyways?” Pushing buttons sometimes gets you answers. Whoo, let’s see how far this guy can go… “FIND OUT FOR YOURSELF, YOU BLOW HOLE!” And with that last bit of defiance, the poor homosexual, talking dolphin swam away from me. I guess the poor guy couldn’t take the “Mutha Fuckin’” heat. Oh well, I guess I pressed the right button after all. I shrugged it off, going back to my shower and getting off all of this lame dirt off of me. “Scrub scrub…scrubba dub dub…” I began to sing to myself. Zzzzzzzelm! I looked around startled, but couldn’t see anyone around. Would I ever be able to finish this shower? “Wha…what was that? Who is there?” Zzzzzzzzelm! Boom! Crash! “Ahhhh! I heard that! You can’t hide from me! Come out, come out, wherever you are!” I picked up my bar of soap which I didn’t use. “I have soap and I’m not afraid to use it! C’mon!” I was anxious, nervous, and scared all at the same time. I was looking around my surroundings wildly, as if some dangerous creature was about to spring upon me and steal my shoes. Wait, haven’t we gone over this before? Oh well. This wouldn’t be the first time I’ve been this deliberate, but it was something about being naked under a waterfall that makes you a bit more paranoid. That’s when the big eyeball zoomed right in front of my face. It was a giant eye, with two little wings on it’s sides, and it just looked at me. I didn’t know what to say at first. I wanted to scream, but that would have been rather rude. Instead, I just stood and stared at it as blankly as it stared at me. “Tom Lexian….You owe me your life, and now you must pay your debts. Come to the castle and you will be given instructions on how to fulfill your end of the debt. Do not come, and you shall dye!” “Dye?! “ I screamed. I turned around and began running back to the forest. The eyeball blinked for a moment. “Dye? Oh, uhm, I meant, die. Not dye. Come back here!….ahhh, screw it.” I ran and ran, not really realizing that I was now running through the woods naked. Was it different than being in the middle of a waterfall being naked? Well, at least at the waterfall, I wasn’t getting perverted cat calls from the local animal population. A frog bellowed at me. “Ribbit! Ribbit! Whoa, show me some skin hot daddy!” A rabbit sneered at me. “Hop! Hop! Nice legs! You must work out!” And a lion screamed in shock. “Roooooooar! My eyes….oh dear lord, my lion eyes!” I didn’t stop running until I made my way back to the encampment where Sherri and the Jello warrior had been waiting for me. Needless to say, they were more than shocked to see me. Sherri stood up from where she was and looked at me with utter disbelief. The Jello warrior unsheathed his jello sword, and I thought he might try some funny business with his weapon. Sherri stood up in surprise as she saw me coming. “What are you doing? Why were you running? And why the hell don’t you have clothes on?!” The jello warrior ventured an explanation. “Don’t be stupid! It’s obviously some clone of him, designed to track us down and kill us! Except they were so lazy that they didn’t even bother putting clothes on him! Kill him!” Wait, don’t do that! Damn paranoid Jello warriors. “No, no. It’s really me. I was taking a shower when I got scared off….” I stopped to catch my breath and began talking again. “I was being chased by this big, ugly eyeball….and it was spying on me when I was taking a shower!” The Jello warrior and Sherri exchange glances, and they suddenly have scared looks on their faces. My, that is never good. Sherri and the Jello warrior exclaimed together: “OH NO! IT WAS AN EYE OF COVERDALE!” I shrugged. “A what? A who? Eye of what? “ “You don’t seem to understand,” Sheri said with an intense look on her face. “That was an eye of Coverdale. That means he’s found you, and he’s found us. Oh god, we’re in big trouble now.” “Look, we need to figure things out,” the Jello Warrior proclaimed. “Let’s all sit down and this fellow can tell us the entire story, so we know exactly what’s going on.” Everyone sits down, and suddenly has a stick and marshmallows roasting on a fire. “I must say, Jello, that you are rather calm and sensible for someone who has Jello for brains.” The Jello warrior blushed, which means some of his face just melted off. “Awww, you’re just saying that.” “Okay, well, here’s my entire story. My name’s Tom Lexian, and I am a professional wrestler. I first got stuck here last week, chasing this damn talking squirrel from my world and into this world. I chased him into this door, which brought me here and in front of some talking rabbit named Fearful. We were actually stuck when we were trapped by Tiger-Man, but I somehow was transported back to my world the second I was running out the back door. I had my match and everything, and when I was walking out of the arena, I suddenly wound up here. I was falling down a great big hole, and I landed in some jello lake.” The Jello warrior cast an evil look my way, and I decided not to mention anything Jello related for the remainder of my story. “That’s where I find myself now, trapped in Dream Land, and trying to get out.“ Sheri just shook her head. “Boy, are you screwed.” “Yes, yes you are. No one escapes Dream Land unless they have help. We cannot do it. We’re just regular Dream Land residents. Only the powers to be, i.e., King Coverdale or Maples the Caterpillar really have the power to send you back.” “Wait one hog washed second here…” I began to say, getting upset. “Are you trying to tell me that I’m fucking stuck here? Stuck with animals that talk and annoy me? Stuck with inanimate objects that have feelings and share them with me on a daily basis? Stuck in a place where my real life counterparts are now maniacal, loose cannon maniacs?” Sheri looked over at the Jello warrior. “You see! I told you he wasn’t as stupid as you thought!” “Intelligence and in breeding are two separate issues.” Inbreeding? What does that have to do with anything? I raised my hand to get them to stop speaking. “Look, we’re off the topic. I need to get out of here. When I left the arena, they were talking about possibly putting me into a main event. I need that! I need attention! I need money most of all, because my god damn pet cactus spends so much money on mineral water!” “I don’t know what else to do. If King Coverdale really is trying to recruit you, then he’ll get you, no matter how hard you try to avoid him. He always gets what he wants.” The Jello warrior had a point there. “But he knows he can’t beat Maples the Caterpillar by himself,” Sheri admitted. “Shit, who can beat a guy that can turn rain into Jello anyways? That’s why he wants your help, and you’re probably not the only person he has brought into this world.” “But what can I do to help?” I asked, some fear littered in my voice. “I’m just a pathetic wrestler, not some war ridden soldier!” “Look, it’s probably time for you to find out the truth about King Coverdale. He ruled over these lands with a very fervent nature. In other words, he was aggressive, but in no way a ruthless tyrant like Maples.“ “Maples isn’t that bad, but he’s always doing lame things like turning rain into Jello or wood into Styrofoam, just for the sake of spiting someone.” Sherri said. “Coverdale left Dream Land because he thought he had lost his lameness,” The jello warrior continued. “He thought he had outgrown this world, but anyone who spends any amount of time here, knows that leaving it would be extremely difficult. Now he’s back, and he wants it all back.” “And that caterpillar isn’t probably willing to give it back…” I finished. “Not bloody likely,” Sheri replied. “Then again, who knows? Maples has a knack for doing things unexpectedly.” “We’re still off the topic. How do I get out of here?” Everyone exchanged glances, and stayed oddly quiet. I knew then that they had no clue how to get me out. I would be stuck here, a prisoner in Dream Land, for the rest of my life. That would be even more depressing. But suddenly a voice called out to me, like an angel from above…. A talking, clapping squirrel dropped in from a tree. “I think I can help you with that…” I was in shock. It was him, that son of a bitch that stuck me in Dream Land in the first place. Would I kill him? Would I strangle him? Oh gosh, I really hate this place now. I really do.
