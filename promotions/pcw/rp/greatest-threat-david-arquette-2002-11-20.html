Greatest Threat, David Arquette

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-20-02 G.O.D: Dr. Jenkins! I’m scared! Dr. Jenkins: I am too Gene, I am too. [The two sit on the bed cowering in fear. Shivering with great fright.] G.O.D: What are we going to do? [The great doctor bows his head in shame. He looks up at the television set and watches as the pictures flash back onto his face.] Dr. Jenkins: Maybe… [A tear begins to form in the corner of his eye. It swells up and starts to gain water as the lower lip of Jenkins begins to quiver.] Dr. Jenkins: Just maybe… [The tear falls down using the crevices of Jenkins’ face and cheeks as a slide.] Dr. Jenkins: Maybe he’ll go easy on us. [The thought of the man Jenkins is talking about sends G.O.D. into a wild burst of moans.] Dr. Jenkins: It’s going to be okay Gene! [Jenkins tries to comfort the giant, but to no avail. He keeps sobbing and then launches himself backwards against the hotel pull-out. Sobbing with tears the size of rain drops he cries out.] G.O.D: But, but, but why? Why does… why does… he have to be… be in the match? Why? Why? Why! WHY! Dr. Jenkins: Shhh… shh… It’s going to be okay Gene. [The giant is only slightly eased by this as he stops screaming, but remains crying.] Dr. Jenkins: We’ll figure something out. He must have a weakness. He must! [The great doctor peers at the TV with evil narrow eyes. The evil man on the television screen staring back at the two on the pull out.] Dr. Jenkins: I’m going to get you. [An idiotic laugh from the television is his only response.] Dr. Jenkins: I’m really going to get you. [G.O.D. cries out, raising his arms in the air with madness.] G.O.D: He is the ultimate! How can anyone stop this God? [The camera focuses in on the television screen. It shows pictures of Scream, Scream 2, Scream 3, and Ready To Rumble on it that involve the infamous, indestructable, deadly, evil, sinister, unstoppable, David Arquette.] G.O.D: How can anyone beat him?! Dr. Jenkins: I don’t know Gene, but someone will. Someone has to! [He stands up.] Dr. Jenkins: Someone will defeat that man. If he is a man at all. Maybe he’s like some sort of half-man, half-animal beast thing, I’m not sure. All I know is that he is amazing. He was put in this match to scare us. Maybe, just maybe he won’t show. Maybe that small cold heart of his will warm just enough to give the rest of us a chance. Maybe we’ll finally be able to hold our dream true if that man, or man-beast, let’s us. We can only hope that he will have a smile on his face and not the hard looks that he shows so often with his deadly intentions. [G.O.D. sits up, his eyes red from tears. He sniffles a few times.] G.O.D: Dr. Jenkins? Dr. Jenkins: Yes, Gene… G.O.D: What if he isn’t nice and sweet? What if he just wants to destroy everything in his path? What if he’s coming as… [G.O.D. gulps.] G.O.D: The Law? [Dr. Jenkins bows his head for a few seconds. He shakes it and then brings it up, looking more serious than ever before.] Dr. Jenkins: Then may God help us all. [G.O.D. roars and moves to the TV fast. He smashes his hand down on it and it frizzes. That evil dastardly laugh laughs on and on and on. It just keeps laughing, drilling it into your skull. Laughing and laughing and laughing till it just can’t laugh no more!] G.O.D: DIE DEMON FROM HELL! BACK WHERE YOU CAME FROM ARQUETTE! BACK! [The Genetic Oriented Drone lifts up the TV, pulling the cord and sending a few sparks upwards. He lifts the already turned off television and slams it to the ground, shattering it. The two just look at it, unspeaking.] Dr. Jenkins: Gene… G.O.D: Yeah, I’ll clean it up. Dr. Jenkins: Good… G.O.D: Damn you David, damn you.
