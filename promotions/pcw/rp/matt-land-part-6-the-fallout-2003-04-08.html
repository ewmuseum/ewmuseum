Matt Land, Part 6- The Fallout

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/8/03 “You fucking failed. How could you fail?” Matt demands, in a cold calculating voice, delivering a right hand to the man’s long face. He follows it up with another hard shot to the man’s mouth and he begins to pace over the dirt floor of the CIA’s warehouse in Washington, DC. Behind him are a few boxes that are starting to rot and the room is lit by a small amount of moonlight that is peering in through the windows above. Sitting in front of him, tied to a small wooden chair is a monster of a man. Almost double Matt’s size, he sits in a white suit, his mouth bloody and a few teeth are missing from the beating he has received at the hands of the ghost. Crossing his arms over his chest, Matt stands staring at the man. He is still angry with Jason Hilton and the situation that took place a few hours earlier in the hospital. That is part of the reason he decided to extract revenge against this man and his failure so soon. He needs to burn off a little steam to make sure he doesn’t go back and slay Jason, for being a complete ass. “I gave you exact instructions on what to do. I told you who to bribe and how much to give them. The only thing I didn’t do was the job myself,” Matt yells at him, punching him again. “Sure you got me the match with Akiyama, the match to which I was going to remove him from the picture for Xavier, but I never got a chance because my half beaten partner was pinned. The pin was counted by Violence Jack would should have just stood there and watched him pin her!” Matt punches him again, this time knocking out a few more teeth and causing blood to spill from his hand. He takes a step back and straightens his grey suit jacket and focuses his blazing brown eyes on the man. He goes back to pacing around the floor, kicking dirt up on his black dress shoes and the cuffs of his grey pants. He rubs his thumb and index finger over his chin and kicks a few small rocks over to the loading gates off to the right. He is beginning to want to just kill the man for his insolence, but with Sarah Clarkson in the small foremen’s office to the right, he didn’t want to draw attention from her and make it so she wouldn’t give him any information. She is the next one on his list to give a beating to, once he is done with the man in front of him. “I think I pretty much made it clear what would happen if you failed Tommy,” Matt barks at him, causing sweat to build up on the brow of the middle-aged man. His silver hair is all over the place and his green eyes, look like emeralds in the field of blood. He knows exactly what is going to come next, that is his death. He messed up and he knew the brash CIA Agent wasn’t going to stand for it. He knew all the stories and rumors, before he decided to help Matt and now it is exactly like he heard. “Right now I could be in LA, getting ready to strap the Continental Title around my waist. Instead I am forced to stand here and punish you for failing me. “I gave you money and pretty much anything else you asked for. I even arranged for you to be able to leave the country without any hassles, all you had to do was help me and the rest of the agency in our mission to destroy PCW. Now I am back at step one and that doesn’t make me happy.” Matt kicks the leg off of the wooden chair, causing it to tip and fall to the side. With one quick motion, he kicks the man in the face and then again. He stops for a second and looks down at his victim, his face covered in blood and dirt. “You didn’t think I wouldn’t check the records Tommy? I saw that you kept all the money for yourself. For whatever reason, I am not sure, but you honestly didn’t think by doing that you would be able to make it out alive?” Matt paces in front of him, peering down on the man. Reaching into his jacket pocket, he makes sure that his automatic is firmly in place and ready. “What is it like to know you are going to die?” Matt calmly questions him. His composer is cold and remorseless now. Taking another person’s life has never meant anything to him; it was just another way to further the legacy that was Christian MacManus. He is one of the few men that actually will pull the trigger to get his point across. That has been the way it always was and the way it always will be. “You are rather quiet for a man who is about to lose his life. I figured you would have had some grand explanation to as why the tournament wasn’t fixed. Or at least why you fucked me over,” Matt sneers at him, baring his white teeth, like frenzied animal about to pounce. He doesn’t though, he just keeps pacing, surveying the man before him. Tommy Conklin is the man he hired to help him fix the PCW Continental Tournament, but in the end the middle-aged man stole his money and tried to make a run for it. Matt wouldn’t be the ace of an agent he is, had he not checked all his tracks and that is exactly what he did. He found out Tommy’s plan. Stepping up and kicking him in the face, Matt knocks out his remaining front teeth. Filling his mouth full of dirt and causing dust to fly up everywhere. He was keeping his emotions out of it though, he made the mistake of bringing them in the last time he talked to Clarkson and it almost cost him big. He isn’t going to make the same mistake twice, but it doesn’t matter if he does, because Clarkson is remaining alive because she has information. Tommy is remaining alive because the ghost is too lax to pull his pistol out and shoot. He is just waiting for the right time to strike. “You know Matt, you really are a cocksucker. Trying to take things that you shouldn’t have. I didn’t bribe those judges because you didn’t deserve to be in that Tournament,” Tommy blurts out, spitting out the blood and dirt that is in his mouth. “You are nothing, but a coward and you are just proving your actions by doing this.” “Tommy not a good time to try to take command of this little session. I do believe that is what got you in trouble in the first place. You thought you could one up and cross me. Didn’t work because look at where you ended up.” “Fuck you,” Tommy screams, allowing his words to linger in the room for a second. Reaching into his jacket pocket, Matt pulls out his silenced Beretta pistol. With a quick wink at the beaten man, he smiles and pulls the trigger. ‘Don’t ever cross me,” he tells Tommy, watching the final breaths fill his lungs. *** “She is doing a lot better today Mr. Hilton,” a strong vice starts in. “She should be waking up and anytime and most of the swelling seems gone.” “Thank you doctor,” Jason replies, shaking his hand and seeing him out the door. He pushes his grey hair to the side and sits back down in the wooden chair next to his daughter’s hospital bed. She is still not showing signs of consciousness, but the famed law professor remains optimistic. His believes his princess it much like him and she will be able to fight her way through this, she will be able to come out a stronger person because of it. Pulling out his cell phone from his white suit pocket, Jason begins to dial in the numbers ever so easy. His pale blue eyes completely focused on the task at hand. Taking a deep breath, he pushes the send button and places the phone next to his ear. Waiting silently, listening to the small bit of static that comes through the rings. “Hello,” a voice booms out from the other end of the phone. “It is Jason. Is everything fairing well in court with the order?” Hilton asks, cocking his eyebrow, trying to figure out if getting an order to keep Matt Jackson away from his daughter is the right thing to do. He wants to protect Haley, but he knows this isn’t totally Matt’s fault. Haley is old enough to make her own decisions and he wouldn’t always be there to look out for her. “Not really Mr. Hilton. It is almost impossible to get information on this guy. No matter where we go, everything is buried so deep in a hole it is unobtainable. There is something about this guy, I am going to keep digging, but it isn’t looking good right now.” “How impossible are we talking here,” Jason asks, becoming more interested in what is going on. His feelings towards Matt are changing and he is starting to believe it would be in Haley’s best interest if he gets the order.“I can’t be certain Jason. I am talking to get the deep shit on this guy we would have to take it past Capitol Hill. Unless you have a few big favors that you haven’t called in, we aren’t touching this guy. He is a goddamn ghost. His past doesn’t exist; it is like he fell from the sky and his present is nothing more then the name Matthew Jackson. He just appeared a few years ago here in the United States, some rich family took him in.” “How can’t he have a past? You are missing something here Adam and I want you to get to the bottom of this. Talk to this rich family that took him in, do anything and everything. I just want to make sure you get the order and he stays away from Haley,” Jason bellows into the phone. Clenching his teeth, he slams his free hand down on the arm of the chair in frustration. “Look I am doing everything in my power, but it is going to take time and that is something we don’t have a lot of right now. If you can push the trial back a month or so I think we will have the firepower that we need. Until then I just don’t see this happening though.” “We don’t have time!” Jason screams, trying his best to keep himself composed. “Haley is going to be waking up soon and she is going to want to rush right back into PCW with him. The best I can give you is until Friday, because that is when the next event is, if you can’t get it done by then I am not sure what we are going to do. “I am going to do everything I can Jason, but this guy isn’t your run of the mill Joe Blow. This guy has secrets and they are being kept in a lock box I am not sure we want to open.” “I want him stopped, do whatever you have to. If you find out anything more report back.” Placing the phone on the table next to the bed, Jason Hilton takes his daughter’s hand and squeezes it tightly. Pushing her blonde hair away from her face, making sure they remaining dried blood isn’t there anymore. “I am going to do everything in my power to protect you Haley, he won’t be allowed to hurt you anymore.”
