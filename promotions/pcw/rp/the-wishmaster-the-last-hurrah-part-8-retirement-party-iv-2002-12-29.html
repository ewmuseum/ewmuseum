The Wishmaster --- The Last Hurrah, Part 8 (Retirement Party IV)

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/29/02 Dante Jackson straps the helmet to The End's head. It looks like a metal pasta strainer with large bifocals in front. The device is locked in tight, and hooked into the back of The End's head. The blood drips down the back, the hooks going deep into the optical center of his brain. Dante Jackson has a smock over his Fubu shirt and pants to keep them from getting dirty. Mani Shigeru was right, but is hard to get out. The End:'What is this?' Dante:'I stole it from out Kriegman's car. Should give you your sight back.' The End:'It's a good thing I'm retiring, you're trying to ruin my real-life gimmick.' Dante:'What are friends for?' Having hooked in the machine properly, Dante flips the switch to power it. It sends many volts of electricity shooting through his optical nerves to stimulate them. Mani Shigeru, Nerezza, Sandy Wong, Jackson Rust, and Inphino Blitz have gathered around and seeing if this machine works. Dante:'See anything?' The End:'I don't know. My mind's eye has been filling in images for so long...' Jackson Rust hands The End an autographed picture of him. The End tosses it away in fright. The End:'DEAR GOD! I must be able to see, I wouldn't imagine something so hideous. Rust? That's what you` look like? Plastic surgery, man!' Rolling his eyes, Jackson Rust walks away from The End. The next two people who stand in front of him to allow him a look are Inphino Blitz and Mani Shigeru. Mani:'.....' The End:'Yeah, I see you. You could have at least picked a mask that matches the color of your clothes. Inphino... good to see you. Heh, I can say that without irony now.' Sandy Wong comes into his view. She looks very pretty, more so than he imagined. Sandy had a nice heart, End frankly didn't care what she looked like. But her fair countenance... she's beautiful by any standard. She was always beautiful to The End, even though he never saw her. But part of him, the human part, the nature in him to try to put faces with the voices in his head... he now has one to go on, a whole figure of a finely shaped woman whose outer beauty is only surpassed by her inner beauty. The End:'Sandy...' He can't say anything more. The woman he knew as little more than a half-deaf fly on the wall in January, the woman he came to know as a friend after Wrestle Vessel when circumstances forced them together following the pay-per-view, the woman he began dating a few months ago and the woman he's fallen in love with... He just stands up and gives her a kiss and a long embrace, the only appropriate expression of his feelings. Dante:'Hey, everybody, End can see!' Everyone turns to The End. Even Asa Fountain. His rhinestone pants glimmer so ridiculously bright that the glare shorts out the optical device and it blows up on The End's head. The End:'Argh! Dammit!' Dante Jackson holds up his hand and waves it in front of The End's eyes? Dante:'How many fingers am I holding up?' The End, his eyes as blank as ever, flips him off. The End:'I dunno, how many am I holding up?' Dante:'You ain't gotta be like that.' Dante takes off the remaining pieces of the strainer and tosses it away. He turns to the group. Dante:'False alarm.' Then he adds sarcastically... Dante:'Thanks, Asa.' Asa, who hadn't even been paying attention, just waves back at Dante with a nod of his head and his giant fro. Asa:'Fo sho', Cat Daddy. Any time.' Now that The End is blind once more, Alan Kriegman takes the opportunity to walk up and put the pen and contract in his hands. Kriegman:'Simple enough, Skylar--' The End:'Xavier.' Kriegman stops, the supergenius thinking he's heard wrong. Kriegman:'What?' The End:'My name is Xavier. Xaiver Kannon.' Kriegman:'What the hell, when did this happen?' Giving a shrug with a smirk, The End pats out the smoke in his hair while answering Kriegman's question. The End:'Friday, 5 PM.' Kriegman:'And why on earth would you change your name to Xavier Kannon?' The End nods his head to the left to the RUSH roster members, Chris Matthews, Tyler Valentine, Stimpo. The End:'A going-away present to them. The Resistance. And anyone else who doesn't like the owner and CEO. Remember my Christmas Wish show?' Kriegman:'How could I forget? I planned so many ways to destroy the set and you with it.' Though a disturbing statement, The End isn't surprised and keeps going. The End:'Anyway, Matt Elia wrote a letter asking me to help out with the Resistance. But truthfully there was nothing I could do for him. I felt bad, I couldn't fulfill his wish. But I wanted to make it up to him somehow, do the next best thing.' Kriegman:'So you changed your legal name so that when you retire they can see the headline Xavier Kannon Retires from PCW?' A nod of his head. The End:'It's the little consolation I could give. They'd like to see that happen, and it's the best I can do.' Alan Kriegman yawns at the story. Linden Young checks his watch. Kriegman:'Well, listen, that's all very well and good, but I've got other places to be and you've got to get some sleep before you job your last match at Adrenaline. So just sign this contract giving away all rights to the name The End and I'll be out of your hair.' Kriegman notices the smoke. Kriegman:'Was your hair on fire?' And then he looks down to the floor. Kriegman:'My vision magnifier! It's ruined! Who did this?' Nerezza points to Dante Jackson. Alan Kriegman reaches into his pocket and takes out the PALM PILOT OF DEATH~! Kriegman:'Linden, check the contract signing. I've got something to take care of.' Linden Young shows the contract to Sandy Wong, who confirms that The End would just give up the name, nothing else. The End signs on the line, agreeing to give up the ring name of 'The End' after his match at Adrenaline. Kriegman was decent enough to let him use it one more night. As Linden Young stops Kriegman from killing Dante Jackson and thus getting all black people in the US and the ACLU to file suit against him, the Pharaoh walks up. He's dressed as usual in his Pharaoh's ring outfit, and lack of a shirt as always. He's tried to buff up since he left but he's not too big. But he lifts Scott Steiner above his head and threw him out of the window, so he's still strong. Outside, Steiner sits up exposing his head and shoulders out of the bushes. He scratches his head. Scott Steiner:'You can't do that to the Big Bad Booty Daddy!' Pete sits up much the same way, right beside Scott Steiner. Steiner:'How'd you get out here?' Pete shakes his head and shrugs his shoulders. He looks up at the 240 pounds black man wearing gray sweats, typing at his computer while all his relatives talk meaningless gossip at the dinner table 15 feet away. Pete:'I dunno. How'd I get out here?' Handler:'I don't feel like explaining or giving another flashback. Just make something up.' Pete:'Okay.... Um... I said hi to Tempest.' Bob Vurdo sits up out of the bushes. Steiner:'What are you doing out here?' Bob Vurdo:'Hiding from Jamie Celt.' Duzza sticks his head out of the near window. Duzza:'Ha!' Duzza goes back in, but then sticks his head back out to laugh like Statuz Quo. Duzza:'LOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOLOL!!!' Little Richard walks by in a purple suede suit. He slaps Duzza on the shoulder. Richard:'Shut up!' Duzza tosses Little Richard out onto Scott Steiner. Richard lands in a pin. 1..... 2....... 3! Michael Cole:'Little Richard is the #1 Contender to the World Champion, Triple H, and they'll go head to head at the Royal Rumble!' Tazz:'God, I hate my job.' Back inside, Pharaoh talks with The End. Pharaoh:'It is a shame you should retire now.' The End:'Everything comes to an end.' Pharaoh:'Yes... but now, when you were reaching the top of PCW. The Continental Title was within your grasp!' The End:'Says the man who has a shot to get a singles match with the World Champion.' Pharaoh:'Heh. Well, it's a testement to my success. Ride Akiyama's train to the top, then get injured by the man who will be in the World Title scene. Come back and take his place. That's how you ascend p the card!' The End gives a snicker. Pharaoh:'Your last match is on Adrenaline... It's a #1 Contendership match. What happens if you win?' The End:'I'm counting on winning. I should at least win my last match. Once that happens, I'd give my place to whomever I feel is a worthy championship contender.' Looking out through the crowd of people, Pharaoh surveys the wrestlers (PCW) in attendance. Pharaoh:'Any in mind who you would give that spot to?' The End gives a nod. The End:'Anyone in the match with me would be considered as that Adrenaline match pretty much IS the Continental division. Other names I'm mulling over include Mani Shigeru, Skitzo, El Romantico and Josh Starwind, Chris Storm...' Pharaoh:'He's not a jobber is he? The last one.' The End:'It remains to be seem, but he does have quite a tradition to break.' The two share a laugh. Pharaoh:'Well, I wish you good fortune. May the gods smile upon you whatever you choose to do now.' The End:'Thank you, Titu.' Pharaoh:'Any time... uh, Xavier.' Pharaoh gives a shudder. Pharaoh:'Wish I didn't have to call you that.' The End:'Heh.' More to come? Of course! Come back for Part 5!
