Stupid sidekicks... can't take them anywhere...

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/2 Today is the day. The Misfits have waited long enough. It is now time for all of them to get their drivers' licenses. Richard already had one, but it expired, so he must retake the driving test. For everyone else, this will be their first time. Everyone is excited, save for Slade, who's taking this a little too seriously. He's got on racing gloves, a NASCAR body suit... the whole nine yards. Drek is dressed as he always is in his wolf's fur tunic and bearskin boots. Lamar has a drastically over-stretched Ultraman Shirt that has each fist bulging out thanks to the 350-pound leper's man-boobs. Richard's got on a green T-shirt and purple shorts. Looks like the Joker on Spring Break. Scene 1: Bus Stop With Juju the Albino Iguana in tow, The Misfits, accompanied by The End, must wait for the bus to take them to the DMV, where they'll take their tests and get licenses. The few other people that need to get on the bus all stand as far away as they can; Lamar hasn't bathed in a few days and the people can't stand it. Even the Misfits find Lamar's odor a problem. Luckily, it's raining at the moment. The Misfits push Lamar out into the rain and toss him a bar of Zest. Richard:'Don't come back under until you've scrubbed up.' Lamar takes off his shirt and starts washing. A freight truck passes by. The driver catches glimpse of Lamar in all his obese man-boobed horrendous glory. Truck Driver:'AAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHH!!! MY EYES!!!' The truck skids out of control, flipping over five times. *Bump-thump, bump-thump, bump-thump, bump-thump, bump-thump* It stops rolling, but continues to skid down the road. *SSSSSSSSSSSSSCCCCCCCCCRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH!!!* The truck crashes into a tree. *CRASH!* Next, a woman screams in fright. *AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!* The truck explodes. *KER-BOOM!* And finally, an agitated cat meows and hisses for no apparent reason. *MMRRRRREEEEOOOWW!* Lamar, splashed with water, has fallen down. The impact has caused his stomach to fall off. The mass of flesh wiggles alongside its owner. Just then, a hippie'd-out 60's Volkswagen van drives up. It stops by Lamar and a group of earth-conscious tree-hugging environmentally-conscious slackers jump out, looking like rejects from the Austin Powers auditions. Hippie 1:'Look, a beached manatee!' Hippie 2:'And it just gave birth. Quickly, we have to get it back to the coast!' The hippies toss Lamar and his disembodied gut into their van and head for the brackish coastal waters as quickly as their badly maintained van will take them. How fast? A quadriplegic could outrun this van. It sputters down the road, every other car in the world lapping it two times over, honking for it to get off the road. The Misfits just sit sheltered from the rain under the bus stop. Slade:'Okasdf mawen nui df?' Richard:'No, Richard is sure that they won't pay attention to him when he tells them that he's not a manatee.' They sit still and silent for a while. Juju minds himself eating flies that land near him. The other Misfits just sit there, with other people coming under the rain shelter now that smelly Lamar is gone. Speaking of Lamar, Drek asks one important question. Drek:'Kin Lamar swim?' End:'No, Lamar's afraid of any body of water larger than those little plastic kiddy-pools.' They all shrug. For about ten minutes they continue to sit at the bus stop as if nothing happened, everyone just minding their own business and hoping that's the last they see of the little piggie. Finally... End:'Ugh. Let's go after him.' The driver's test will have to wait until tomorrow. The End and his Misfits stand up and drag their feet as they walk after the hippie van. It's THAT slow. Scene 2: Bus Stop, Part Deux Lamar's all back together, and The Misfits are back at the bus stop today with The End, having missed their opportunity to take the driving tests yesterday. Everyone's here again, but it's not rainy today. Also, the Misfits made extra sure not to let Lamar out of the house without bathing, so there's no problems with hygiene. In addition, he's now wearing clothes that don't cut into his skin and cut off oxygen (even worse, revealing all the things that he shouldn't be revealing). Everyone else is pretty much the same, the other Misfits having time to go home and change yesterday before chasing after the van. When they returned, it was but a block away from its previous position! But enough about that, on with the story. Slade:'Mklez, huisd oawnjuh ijwea?' End:'I need each of you to have a driver's license. I have certain... plans for my opponent, and it requires that you al can legally drive a vehicle. And for some reason, I feel the need to clarify that there will not be a yellow hummer involved.' Drek:'Richard, dun'na ye olreddie 'ave a license?' Richard:'Kinda. Mine's expired, so if I ever got pulled over, I'd be fined. And while they were at it, they'd go ahead and search my vehicle.' Slade:'Ooiasdf omrwer, iuchb werb.' Richard:'You've got a point. I'm a 30-year-old white man. They're too busy looking for Arabs and Hispanic Muslims, aren't they?' Juju:'Wonk!' Lamar:'Look, the bus is here!' The city bus adorned with Hooters advertisements arrives and takes the Misfits to the DMV. Scene 3: The Bus While on the bus to the DMV, a crazy white police officer driving a rich black man's Jaguar drives alongside the bus. He yells at the driver, tries getting in front of him, but nothing deters the driver from ignoring the officer. Finally, he has the owner of the car that he has commandeered write a note on a sheet of paper that says 'Bomb on Bus.' The man writes the note. The wind catches the note and flings it to the bus windshield. Reading the note, the bus driver slows down. Officer:'No!' The officer breaks the driver's side door of the car clean off the hinges to pull the car next to the bus. The bus driver opens the door. Officer:'No! Don't slow down! There's a bomb on the bus, I'm coming on!' The driver nods his head. Bracing for the jump and telling the jaguar's owner to take the wheel- Officer:'Take the wheel.' Rich Black Man:'Why wouldn't I take the wheel? It's my car, dammit!' The officer jumps to the bus and climbs inside. Meanwhile, the owner of the car is so busy paying attention to the crazy white police officer that he doesn't mind the road ahead of him. He turns his head and his face goes pale. Rich Black Man:'AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!' The jaguar skids out of control, flipping over five times. *Bump-thump, bump-thump, bump-thump, bump-thump, bump-thump* It stops rolling, but continues to skid down the road. *SSSSSSSSSSSSSCCCCCCCCCRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH!!!* The car crashes into the median in the center of the highway. *CRASH!* Next, a woman screams in fright *AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!* The car explodes. *KER-BOOM!* And finally, an agitated cat meows and hisses for no apparent reason. *MMRRRRREEEEOOOWW!* Back on the bus, the passengers are wondering what's going on. The officer, who's in plain clothes since HE'S NOT EVEN ON DUTY tries to calm everyone's fears. Officer:'Everyone, I ask that you please not panic, but there is reason to be alarmed. There is a bomb on the bus.' Everyone yells and screams. Passengers:'Oh my God! We're all gonna die!' The officer leans in and converses with the bus driver. Officer:'The bomber is sick. He works in weird ways. This particular bomb is programmed to activate when the bus's speed exceeds 90 miles per hour. When the bus then drops back to 80 miles per hour, the bomb will explode.' The bus driver looks at the officer like he's crazy. He's a poor black man, and has no time for this. Driver:'Foo' ass cracka! Sit down! Don't you know the speed limit's 70?' The officer scratches his head. Officer:'Oh.' Everyone on the bus laughs at the officer. Back at his hideout, the bomber, who has installed cameras in the bus to see and hear everything, realizes his mistake. Bomber: *Snaps fingers*'Damn!' The officer sits down next to a passenger named Annie and cries. Everyone's taking this in good humor, surprisingly, but the Misfits aren't. Richard:'What the hell was that?' Slade: *Shrugs*'Hmph.' Lamar:'He's funny.' Drek:'An' t'day oov all days, I forget me axe.' Juju:'Wonk!' Scene 4: At the DMV Teller:'Next please?' Richard steps up to the counter. Richard:'Richard would like to retake Richard's driving test.' Teller:'Um... I don't know what you're talking about.' Richard swirls his hand around, not that that actually helps the counter attendant to understand. Richard:'Richard was a new driver's license.' Teller:'Look, are you yankin' my chain? Get out of line, we're got customers.' Richard:'RICHARD is a customer and would like a license to drive.' In the line next to Richard, a hospital employee talks with the teller in front of him. Orderly:'Hey, can I cash this check?' Teller:'No problem, I'll just have to take out $5 for the non-account fee.' Orderly:'No problem.' Richard looks back at the Misfits. Except for Lamar, all of them look angrily at Richard. Drek:'We leave ye' in charge of directions ONE TIME and you bring us to a bank?' Richard:'It was an honest mistake. I thought this was a bank.' Slade:'Opoawef oindk j oi haeoia jfnasdjnb hu fkj' Richard:'There could be many DMVs with First Union for a name. We don't know!' The Misfits shove Richard out of the bank and go about their day. Another opportunity wasted.
