17 again and brutal

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 30, 2002 (The Scene begins at the motel where John and Jaden are staying. Jaden is in the doghouse after her old boyfriend came into town. They must have been talking for a while for him to know about John and Jaden’s relationship.) …One Hour Later Knock! Knock! There is a knock at the door followed by someone trying to turn the doorknob, with repeated knocks. “Wait a got damn minute!” John says. He is still furious with Jaden and her little friend. “Jaden I swear that better not be him!” John does not think about looking through the peephole. He goes straight to the door and opens it giving the person standing outside heat. While opening he says, “Who is it!” As the door opens, he sees a black, shinny, 9 millimeter in his face. “It’s me and who the hell are you!” John tries to shut the door, avoiding being shot. He begins to speak with an Arabian dialect, “Sorry me speak no English! Good-bye!” “Hell To The Nah… move motherfuc…” The gun carrier tries to pry the door open with the gun. Jaden is in a state of shock; however, she gets a glance in at the person. It is an older person, with grayish black hair and caramel brown skin. “Grandma? What are you doing here and why do you have a gun?” Jaden says puzzled. “Put the gun down.” Jaden’s grandmother pushes John out of her way and enters the motel room. “Are you alright baby? Did Romeo come past?” “Who the hell is ROMEO! Jaden, how many people are you messing around with?” “John shhh… no one!” “A 17 year old girl like you does not need to be fooling around with a 22 year old. Get you stuff you moving back in with me!” “Woah, go back 17?” Jaden looks at John with her little puppy dog eyes. “Oops, I think I forgot to tell you!” “Yea damn right! Are you telling me I have been sleeping with a 17 year old girl.” “16,” Jaden says backing behind her grandmother knowing she still has the gun. “My birthday was the other day; I just turned 17!” “Jaden how old is this lil’ white boy?” “Old enough ma’am!” The grandmother begins to looks around still swing her black 9 millimeter in the air. “Who are you talking to? Jaden come on get your shit and lets go NOW!” “Why do we have to go? I am fine right here with John!” “The hell you are. I will help you pack!” Jaden snatches the gun out of her grandmother’s hand and stands in front of the door with tears running down the side of her face. “Oh, no I am not going anywhere! I will kill myself before I go with you or I might kill you.” She says turning the gun around from her head toward her grandmother. To Be Continued
