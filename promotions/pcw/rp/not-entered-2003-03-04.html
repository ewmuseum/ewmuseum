Title: Not entered Featuring: Tim Walden Date: March 4, 2003 Let's see...Make scathing tirade against crowd and pathetic brawling rednecks? Check.. Take measures to prevent that lout Duke from blindsiding me again? Check... Get the best shots in when Tomas eventually shows up in a blind rage? ............Okay, now THIS was the depressing part. Two out of three...Generally not considered bad, but when the third entirely undermines all that the first two have attained, an exception is usually in order. I had devoted my minutes on that night to berating the fans for their decision to ignore my plights in favor of their bias toward her, and she tore down all that which I had built up with my efforts through a lightning-quick attack, the specifics of which were probably planned in advance for best efficiency of attaining her goal, to catch me off my guard with just the right moves to bring me down... Who was I kidding? I got beaten down, and I didn't even get in a shot, let alone the better end of it. This had to rank among my least proud moments alone already, I had beaten her how many times before, only for her to continually take advantages over me now..This time, I just didn't have the fight in me. It wasn't even up to my standard, like something was missing..I took the shots and returned nothing, constantly reeling from the impact, throwing me off..I didn't even manage to reverse her quick try for Southern Comfort, and I was left with nowhere to go. Nothing to do, no way that reaching the ropes would make an iota of difference to her, no opportunity to escape at this point, no other way to leave the hold than to either pass out after a long, pointless delay or tap out..No evasion of the facts as always, but this time, it worked against me... I tapped out. Not for the first time, still not in a match, but all the same affecting my psyche. How could I possibly win the war if the battle could end so quickly? What sort of message had been sent to the people was obvious, that their hero was too great to let me get her down now, whether it was an improvement of her skill or just the devaluation of mine...I knew it couldn't be so soon a difference from my performance mere weeks ago, just coincidental nature in combination with my own mild depreciation if there was any...Right? I had another chance. The official PCW rankings listed 5 contenders to the TV Title, and being #1 as I tended to, it only seemed natural that I fight the remaining contenders to assert myself...Three men that I'd had pasts with, but with Nerezza fighting for the Office, two out of three might not be so bad after all.. It was just a matter of who I had to compete with. Kano...That damned waste of flesh, he held TWO unfair victories over me in the past in addition to his entire self-inflated ego, which convinced himself that he was not merely a Continental Champion by the will of TPB two years back, and not merely a lucky sap who got to fight a TV Champ on the brink of injury as well as winning the belt once by the sheer suckiness of Mr. Showtime, but instead put himself into the belief that he had EARNED these, one of the many beliefs he held that heightened his delusions of grandeur... That he really was good enough to compete on the level of the major players, that he hadn't had competition on the level of Ultimate Warlord for the Continental and Television reigns so short you could fry an egg by them, that he had EVER given me instruction that went beyond 'Uhhh...hit 'em real hard', that the sorry excuse for a man who relied on brute strength and the same old maneuvers every night would take credit for any of my success, much less consider himself a legend for the mere fact of having spent more than two years fighting people who weren't of any matter to the real legends and never having put up a decent battle against anyone of substance..If only I'd thought to tax fake retirements, PCW would never have had any financial problems in the first place. ...He needed a reality check, and it'd have to come tonight. Him and Chet King..I wanted to beat Chet, and it was rather obvious. I already had a victory over him, having won our match a couple of weeks back, but it came only through copious interference from my co-workers. There wasn't as much pride in it as I had hoped for, but at least here there was the psychological advantage I'd gained from drilling it into his head on his own show soon after..I looked forward to fighting him once more, however, his skills would fit right in for a nice battle. And if I could take care of him tonight... There would probably be room to deal with Teresa in the near future again. To fight the match I knew I was fully capable of fighting and join the ranks of the two-time Television Champions, alongside a series of wrestlers whom I either outmatched or felt capable of at one time..But the first thing would have to come first.. Then Rent-Boy. If anything could put a bee in the fans' bonnet, it would be winning over the man who had possession of my old belt, and no less on his own ground of battle in front of them...It had been months since we last met, and longer since we truly fought tooth-and-nail. One thing hadn't changed though... He still didn't quite seem to grasp the concept of necessary evil. Death...Poverty...Disease....Taxation. Taxation was just another cause to fight against from his perspective..Soon he would have to suffer for his views though, for while he made sure to be prompt in paying his taxes, he spoke too negatively upon them and I still held no interest in mercy upon the youth who had prevented me from attaining Rising Star gold in the past...Soon he would be trapped in my clutches, never to escape himself from the strict enforcement of taxation, merely to reach the same point that everyone else who dared challenge our authority reached eventually... The point of no evasion. ...Wouldn't he? ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
