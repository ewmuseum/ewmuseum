Reclaiming Jim Surge

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th September 2002 Jim Surge shakes his head slowly as his fork clinks listlessly against the ceramic plate from which he eats a very healthy-looking garden salad. Ben Nighthorse Campbell looks over to his tag-team partner and half-brother with a smirk, knowing just how much the cowboy hates eating anything that is remotely healthy for him. Despite Surge’s outstanding work ethic in the gym, it is always a chore to get him to respect what he puts into his body nourishment-wise. “Delicious, eh?” Ben says as he is unable to control his mirth. “Are you going to get some plastic surgery on those lips, asshole?” Surge scowls back. Jim drops his silver fork to the table and watches it as it seems to fall in slow-motion, a slight rattling noise coming at the moment of impact and then an uneasy silence. “Why don’t you get up and take a walk?” asks Misty, pulling her long black hair back into a ponytail so that it stays out of her face while she eats. “You don’t seem to be enjoying your salad!” “Maybe that’s a good idea Mist,” Surge responds, standing up slowly from the table and grabbing his black Stetson hat before making his way out of Redemption Song’s hotel room at the Marriot Residence Inn in Portland, Oregon. Misty’s eyes stay glued to the cowboy like a tractor-beam until the door closes. “I’m worried about him Ben,” Misty says as she stuffs a forkful of tofu into her mouth. “He’s been in a funk ever since Tom left.” “Tom Core is an asshole,” Ben replies, not even looking up at the gorgeous woman who used to be Jim’s girlfriend and manager when Surge was an independent wrestler. “We’re all better off without him. He’s only in this business for money and nothing good ever comes from that. WE have something to prove. WE’RE the ones who will make the World Tag-Team Championship something to strive for again!” Misty nods in agreement. “Still, look at him,” she says in a genuinely concerned voice. “He’s not even thinking about Turmoil.” “Do you think we really need to be worried about that match? We’ve just come off a two-month long unbeaten string and ended the careers of 3 other tag-teams,” Ben says proudly as he straightens out his chest like a cock competing for the same hen. His mini-moment is disrupted by a rapping noise on the door coming from the outside. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! “I’ll get it,” Misty says as she gets up. Ben cannot help but watch Misty with his jaw open as she walks over to the door, from her black leather pants up to her skin-tight “Redemption Song” t-shirt before turning his attentions back to his own soup and salad. Misty opens the door and it’s Jim Surge. “I forgot my room key,” Surge says with a slight smile, knowing that Ben and Misty are going to give him hell for being a dumbass. “HA!” Ben exclaims as he points over to Jim with his fork and shakes his head in amusement. Misty starts giggling as well as she moves out of the doorway allowing the Tombstone, Arizona native to reenter the room. “I know, I know,” Surge says taking off his hat and tossing it onto the couch. “I forgot the friggin’ room key AGAIN. Go on, let me have it!” Jim slumps down onto the couch and turns on the TV. “I didn’t really feel like taking a walk anyway,” he says without a trace of a western drawl. Ben gets up from the table and sits down next to his half-brother. “Man, we’ve been though a whole hell of a lot these past few months, especially with the deaths of Marty and Mandy, and now Tom’s gone and....” “Don’t mention him please,” Jim responds without turning his attentions away from the television set. Jim keeps flipping channels until he spots the U.S. Open Tennis Tournament on the tube and leaves it on to watch Pete Samprass try and make it into yet another Grand Slam final. “I don’t think that’s too healthy Jim,” Misty chimes in from back at the table where she has returned to finish her lunch. “What the hell is this a group therapy session?!” Surge says, the western drawl creeping back into his speech with his agitated tone. “Jesus! The guy was our manager, now he’s not. What the hell else is there to say about it? A couple of days ago y’all told me not to dwell and now all you’re fucking talking about is Tom Core!!!” Ben shoots a look over to Misty who just shrugs her shoulders in a way which says to Ben: “I don’t know what else to say!” Ben lets out a deep exhale and turns his attentions back to Jim. “Jimbo, what are we going to do to Misplaced on Turmoil?” “Wrestle them!” the cowboy responds in a bitterly sarcastic tone. “Wrestle them?!” Ben says as he takes off his handmade leather headband and rests it on the coffeetable in front of the television. “Okay, what have you done with the real Jim Surge? The Jim Surge who would say something like: ‘we’re gonna pound those sacks a’ crap so hard that their asses are gonna fall straight out of their mouths and their eyeballs’ll be at the bottom of the river?’” “I would never say something like that,” Surge says, staying focused on the tennis match as Samprass wins the second set, again through a tiebreak. “Okay... now I know something’s wrong,” Misty chimes in from the table. “Shut up little missy,” Jim says, glaring back at her in a way meant to suggest that he was annoyed at being interrupted from his television watching. “Jim,” Ben says, standing up and standing in front of his half-brother with his arms folded. “Did you hear what they said about the PCW World Tag-Team Championship at the last Turmoil? They said that the TELEVISION TITLE is worth more than the tag straps!!” “Yeah, so, they’re right,” Jim says, trying to crane his head around Campbell so that he can see the television. Suddenly, Misty hits the power button from the remote control that she has back at the table and Jim slumps back in the couch. “Okay Dr. Phil,” he says looking up at the Apache Ghostrider, “lay it on me. Tell me what’s wrong with my life.” Ben shakes his head negatively. “What’s gotten into you man? The PCW World Tag-Team Championship! The quest begins in just three days and you’re here watching tennis! The same belts that the Blood Brothers wore! The same belts that Da Westsidaz held! The same belts that you’ve been craving ever since your first day in the Primetime Wrestling Academy! Isn’t that why you jumped at the first chance to could to drop OSW’s Most Extreme Team competition and come here?” “Don’t waste your breath on OSW,” Surge says, traces of emotion finally creeping back into his voice. “They’ll learn soon enough that the real talent is here in PCW, and PCW is where we’re going to stay!” “There you go bro,” Ben says as he nods his head affirmative. “Misplaced has no chance against us, realistically speaking,” Surge says as he strokes the two-day old stubble on his chin. “Maybe we’ll end their tag-team careers as well!” “Actually,” Misty pipes back in, “word on the street is they’re thinking of starting solo careers.” “Of course!” Jim says emphatically, “That’s because Redemption Song is now in the tag-team division. I wouldn’t be surprised if a few teams suddenly ‘split up,’ if they don’t we’ll split them in friggin’ two inside the ring. Who the hell says hardcore is dead?!” Ben smiles and looks back over to Misty who returns the smile with a relieved look. “Listen Jim, if you want I’ll try to find Tom,” Ben offers as he sits back down on the couch. “Like one of your Ghostrider traces and whatnot?” Jim asks, seemingly interested in the Apache’s proposition. “How exactly does that work again?” “It’s more of being able to travel or ‘ride’ the Great Spirit while asleep,” Ben begins, “like what you white folks call ‘lucid dreaming.’ Inside the Great Spirit, you can essentially find anything if you know where to look, including things like the spirits of the deceased and the souls of the living.” “The trance state is a different medicine and requires more time and actually takes time to recover from. The Ghostwalk trace enables me to get very quick glimpses of the future. But the glimpses are usually so short that I have to try and piece it together like a puzzle. Many times I’ve been unsuccessful, especially since we’ve started living in cities.” For the first time since signing contracts with PCW, Ben Nighthorse Campbell looks distressed. And this time Jim looks back at Misty with a concerned expression. “I think perhaps it’s time to reclaim Ben Campbell,” Jim says. “How about a trip to the Oregon coast? It’s supposed to be beautiful and nothing but sea lions.” Ben smiles back at his cowboy half-brother and shakes his head in disbelief. “For a white dude, you’re okay,” Ben says before quickly adding “and YOU, you’re not too bad yourself!” in Misty’s direction. “Road trip!” Misty says as she gets up from the table. “I’m driving!” (to be continued...)
