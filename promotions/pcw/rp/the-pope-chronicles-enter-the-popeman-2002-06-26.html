The Pope Chronicles: Enter the Popeman

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th June 2002 It is a dark night. Rather than a bright night, like what happens in Norway. Norway blows. Sweden sucks. But it depends on the amount of cash you pay those hookers. Anyway, back to what I was gibbering on about before. It is a dark night, one of those nights which chill you to your bone. No, not that bone. I was talking about bones in general. Pervert. Anyway. A light beams across the dark night sky, illuminating the skyscrapers of... whatever city it is we’re in. Let’s call it New York, since all comic book cities in fact mean New York... like Gotham City... and Smallville... no... Wait, that’s New Jersey. Anyway the light beams across the night sky showing in the middle... catholic cross. I’m now waiting for the makers of Buffy the Vampire Slayer to sue me because of this intro. Regardless we cut to the Miyagawa mansion on the outside of the city where Den is wearing a dress... a pink dress... and he appears to have breasts. You know men who have breasts are called... oh... SHE’s a woman. Damn... anyway, she is standing by the window with its flowing drapes when the light with the catholic cross is seen. Den: It’s the sign. ALEXANDER! It’s the sign! Miyagawa rushes into the room, dressed in a fine black suit looking quite natty and all dressed up. He walks over to the window and slaps Den on the ass as he does so. Den doesn’t take too kindly to that and punches him in the face, knocking Miyagawa down to his ass. Miyagawa: What was that for? Den: Partially because you just slapped me on the ass and partially because I’m dressed in what I can only describe as... A FROCK. My employment doesn’t cover wearing frocks. If it did, I would never have signed up. Miyagawa stands up slowly; dusting down his suit as he does so... and of course looking warily at Den in her lovely pink frock. Miyagawa: Look, now is not the time. You said you wanted to be involved with my wrestling career, did you not? Den: I said I wanted to accompany you down to the ring. I never said that I wanted to wear a frock. Miyagawa: Your next costume is leather, will that do? Den: Is it a skirt? Miyagawa: It’s a close fitting bodysuit. Den nods in agreement that it is much better. Den: It’ll do. **pauses for a second** so what lame assed crap have you got now that this sign is all high budgeted flashing in the sky? Some crack induced Eninem rip-off music video or some such? Miyagawa: Whoa... guess who’s on the blob this week. Den: Do you want me to punch you in the face again? Miyagawa does his best of answer her question while ignoring her threat. Probably the best thing for his health anyway. Miyagawa: TO THE POPECAVE! Miyagawa points over towards a bookshelf. Den doesn’t move... and instead just looks rather annoyed. Den: The what? Miyagawa: The Popecave. Den: *sigh* that’s what I thought you said. Lead on McDougal. Miyagawa: You mean MacDuff. Den: You want another punch in the face? Miyagawa: Ok, it might be McDougal then... Miyagawa sprints over as best he can towards a bookshelf and pulls a copy of the bible off the shelf and slides it back on there. A secret passageway opens in the door revealing two fireman’s polls. Den: You know most modern facilities have lifts. Miyagawa: I was going for classic. Although I do have the lifts as well. Den: Good, because there is no way I am sliding down a fireman’s poll. Miyagawa snorts with laughter. Den: What? It would make my thighs sore. Miyagawa snorts again with laughter, barely holding it all in. Den now catches on. Den: Just open the lift door or something already will you, you jackass. Miyagawa calms down and pulls a copy of Jean Paul’s Audio Bible off the shelf and places it back. Another secret passageway is revealed; this time is a lift and opens quickly. Miyagawa and Den walk into the lift and Miyagawa pushes the button. Miyagawa: To protect the world from devastation... to unite all... Den: Just don’t go there. Miyagawa: Ok. What about “DEATH TO SCIENTOLOGY!” Den sighs audibly as the lift door closes and we leave the duo for the moment as the camera on this particular “Rent-a-Hero” fades out.
