Walking with Fire

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-27-02 Life can be funny at times. One minute you are on a high, the next you are on a low, then back high again. It's a very ending rollercoaster. For Chris Storm it's at a low. Made worst by the fact that in his mind, he might lose the one thing he cared about more then anything else. A new threat has came up in his life and it's in the form of Jackson Rust. Chris has made himself believe that Jackson Rust posses a threat to his relationship with Tessa Windsor. Now he finds himself in a match where he can get his hands on Jackson Rust and cause him pain. But Tessa Windsor is also in the match. He doesn't know if he will be able to strike her or not. He will just have to wait until the time comes though. Chris is walking through Central Park, looking at the snow covering the ground. The snow had stopped falling a few minutes ago though. But the white powder still blanketed the earth. Chris kept his head down as he just watching himself kick the snow up. 'I don't want to lose Tessa. She means too much to me. I can not let myself stand back and watch her be swept away by another man. Damn that Jackson!' he tells himself. Chris cusses himself out below his breath. Chris doesn't watch where he is going and accidently bumbs into something. 'Ouch' Chris loosk up and sees he bumped into a women. She is quite attractive at that. Wrapped up in her pink winter clothes. She looks up at Chris slightly annoyed. Then she sees who she bumped into. Her eyes light up like a Christmas tree. She jumps on him and gives him a big hug. Chris looks around trying to figure out why this women is hugging him. She lefts go and looks at him with a big smile on her face. She looks surprised and happy at the same time. Chris was about to tell her he was sorry when she interupted him. 'Chris Storm! I can't believe I ran into you here. That's amazing. Wow you look great!' she tells him. She looks him over with his eyes. Chris gets a little worried, hoping this isn't another stalker. She flashes him her emerald green eyes and a shot goes off in Chris' head. He remembers her. Chris tries to think back from where he remembers her from. Then he remembers what it is from. His eyes grow huge, he has to step back. The women looks proud as she can tell he just remembered who she is. 'Ashea Titious?' he stutters out. 'from Babysitter Got Back?' he ask. She just nods her head. Chris' gives her a big hug now as he remembers who she is fully. He gives her a little kiss to the cheek. 'Oh my goodness, how long has it been since we last talked?' he ask her. She frowns. Ashea takes off her mittens and rubs her hands together. 'Not since you left the business.' she tells him. 'Why did you leave Chris? I thought you was having fun.' asked the porn star. She looks up to Chris with a sad expression all over her face. Chris gets a sting of guilt. He was her best friend in the business. They was in a few films together. In that time they talked to each other all the time and became real good friends. However Chris left that life behind and the two stopped talking to each other. Looking at her sulking like that, Chris grabs her hands and holds him. He gives her a smile. 'I left because I found true love. The women of my dreams walked into my life and I knew I could not continue that life I had.' he told her. Chris thinks about Tessa and his smile grows and grows. Ashea looks at Chris and has to smile too. 'Do you miss that life?' she ask him. She bites her bottom lip. Chris isn't even looking at her anymore. He's staring out into space, thinking about his sweet Tessa. However he hears her words. Chris turns back to look at her. 'Not for a minute. I would not trade just one minute with Tessa for years of being in porno movies.' he responds honestly. Ashea has to smile at that reply. 'She is a very lucky girl then.' Ashea tells him. A sudden chill blows through the park. Ashea shivers at the cold. Chris looks at her with concern. 'Look my apartment isn't very far away. Why don't we go there and share stories over coffee? Tessa is there. I would love for you to meet her and the kids. They are wonderful.' he ask her. Chris gives her a puppy dog face hoping to pursade her to agree. Ashea looks at him and laughs as the silly expression on his face. She gives him a playful slap to the shoulder. 'Ok it's a date.' Chris holds out his arm for her, which she gladly accepts. The two walk off to Chris' apartment, sharing tells of what they have been doing since they last saw each other. They walk up the stairs toward the apartment. Ashea looks over to Chris and sees him looking worried. Ashea stops him before they get to the door. 'Chris what is the matter? You look concerned about something?' He looks around trying to avoid the question. But it's dragging on his mind. He knows he can not lie to Ashea and tell her it's nothing. And he needs someone to talk to. 'You know how I am a wrestler? Well I signed with a company called PCW. And they booked me in a match against Tessa Windsor. And Jackson too. Two of my friends. I don't know if I will be able to bring myself to striking Tessa. No matter the situation.' he explains to her. Chris leans up against the wall trying to figure out a solution to his problem. He just shakes his head. 'But look that is my problem. I will deal with it myself. We have coffee and stories to share inside.' he tells her as he opens the door. A breeze of warm air fills the hallway as the door opens. Chris holds the door open for Ashea as she walks in. Chris shuts the door behind himself. He looks around for Tessa be can not seem to see her anywheres. 'Tessa!' he calls out. Tessa sticks her head up from the couch. She has the phone in her hand. 'Look Jackson, Chris just got home. I'll call you back later.' with that she hangs up the phone. Chris' temper starts to rise as he sees she has been talking to Jackson Rust. He lashes out at Tessa. 'What are you doing talking to him? He's our opponent this Tuesday!' Tessa looks at him surprised. Chris walks up to her and sees she has been crying. His anger breaks down. However Tessa lashes back at Chris. 'He's not our opponent. He's our friend.' she tells him. Tessa notices the other women in the apartment. 'Who is she?' she ask while pointing at Ashea. Chris looks back over at Ashea. He had forgotten she was even there. Chris walks back over to Ashea. He introduces her to Tessa. 'This is my friend Ashea Titious. I ran into her in the park just a few minutes ago. We use to do porno movies together.' Tessa jumps off the couch from that last statement. She marches up to Chris. 'You brought home one of the porno girls you slept with! Chris how dare you.' she yells at him. Although trying to be quiet so she won't wake Alex or Christa. Chris thinks about what Tessa said and lets out a good laugh. Ashea laughs too. Tessa looks at them both like they were crazy. 'Tessa, we never had sex if that is what you are thinking. Ashea is a lesbian!' Chris informs her. Ashea winks at Tessa and blows her a kiss. Chris lets out a laugh. Chris goes and sits down on the couch. Tessa sits down beside him and Ashea takes a chair opposite the couch. 'Tessa keep Ashea entertained why I go fix us some coffee.' he ask her as he walks out of the room and into the kitchen. Ashea looks over at Tessa. Tessa gives her a smile. 'So you are Chris' boyfriend. I don't know why he would date someone like you.' Ashea snaps at Tessa. Tessa looks at her surprised. 'What did you say?' Tessa askes her. 'You heard me. Because you wrapped Chris around your little figure, you made him quit the business.' she tells Tessa. Tessa gets up ready to slap her when Chris walks back in with the coffee in hand. He looks at Tessa standing up, ready to slap Ashea. 'Tessa, what's going on?' he ask. Tessa just sits back on the couch and gives Ashea a cold stare. Chris hands everyone their coffee. Ashea takes a sip of the coffee. 'This is really good Chris. I bet you must cook everything around here don't you?' Ashea says. Chris just smiles and rubs the back of his head. He closes his eyes and Ashea gives Tessa a dirty look. Tessa gets up from her seat and puts down her coffee. 'Don't look at me that way! I don't what you are trying to suggest to Chris.' she tells Ashea. Ashea just looks around like she is innocent as a lamb. Chris opens his eyes and looks at Tessa surprised. 'Tessa, please try to be a little more polite here. Ashea is my friend.' he tells her. Tessa just looks at Chris. She tries to plead with him. 'But Chris! She insulted me while you was getting coffee. And just now she gave me a dirty look. She's trying to suggest I don't do anything!' Chris looks over to Ashea, who is acting like she is shocked to her Tessa say all this. Chris shakes his head and looks back at Tessa. 'If you are jealous of Ashea, you have no reason to be. I already told you that Ashea and I never slept together. I promise you it's the truth.' he tells her. Angry at being called jealous, Tessa storms off into Chris' bedroom. Chris gets up and excuses himself to follow Tessa. He walks into his room to see Tessa sitting on the corner of the bed upset. He sits down next to her and notices his black box is laying out and all of the items inside are laying on the bed. He jumps up from where he is sitting. 'What is all this? Tessa have you been digging through my stuff!' he howlers. Chris starts to gather his stuff and put it back in the box. Tessa looks at all the adult movies and naughty magazines. 'Chris I thought you gave up that life. Why do you still have all that stuff saved in the closet?' she ask him. Chris just shoots her a look while he putting the box back in the closet. 'What were you doing going through my closet in the first place?' he questions her. 'I was just putting away your Santa suit and I needed to make room for it. I didn't think you would mind if I moved around some of your stuff.' she tells him. But Chris is very mad now. Was she digging up dirt on him so she could tell Jackson about it? Is that why she was calling him? Letting Jackson know all of his dirty secrets before the match? How could she betray him like that? He loved her more then anything and now it looked like she was trying to make him lose to Jackson. Maybe it is true that she now loves Jackson instead of Chris. Ashea walks into the room to see the damage she has caused. Ashea smiles as she sees Chris and Tessa fighting. 'I think I should leave Chris. You still have my number so call me sometime. We can go out to lunch.' she says as she leaves. Chris doesn't even pay any attention to her. He finishes putting away the black shoe box and stands up in front of Tessa. 'I want you to leave my room now.' he demands. Tessa looks at him with those sad eyes. She tries to say something to him but Chris turns his head and looks away. Tessa just lets her head fall as she walks out of the room. She shuts the door behind her. Chris collapses on the bed. He lays face down on the matress. He rolls over onto his back and looks up at the ceiling. 'How could she invade my privacy like that? I thought she trusted me more then to do something like that. Maybe it is my fault for keeping them. But it's a part of my past. I need it. I need to remember my past. I can not afford to let myself forget anymore then I already have. If I have no past then how can I have a future?' he ask himself. Chris just stares upward, looking off into space. He looks over at the door and gets up. Chris walks over to it and locks the door. He goes back to the bed and sits down. 'I just want to be alone for now.'
