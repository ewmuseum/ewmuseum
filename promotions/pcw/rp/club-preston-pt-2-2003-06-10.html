Club Preston (Pt. 2)

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.10.03 After receiving his room on the Cloud Nine air ship, Tyler realized that it was especially bland. He has come to the conclusion that he will transform his bland room into a small scale comedy club. He is preparing to decorate the unfriendly confines of his quarters to the heavy beats of rock music. The rock music blares as Tyler begins to settle in his home away from home. Just as he pulls the first pale blue T-shirt from his duffel back, there is a heavy thud on the door. Tyler doesn’t hear it at first, getting it confused with the beats of the music. He hums the tune to himself and begins to hang his clothing up. The thud on the door gets more extreme, jogging Tyler’s attention over to it. Tyler rolls his eyes. He flips down a lever on the panel to kill the music. The constant thud continues. Tyler shuffles towards the door with an irritated look on his face. He swings open the door to reveal a bell hop, decked out in his signature, scarlet red jump suit. “You have all the stuff I asked for already? I knew that there was great service on this ship but wow, that was quick,” Tyler says with a huge smile on his face, satisfied that his needs are finally being fulfilled right at the moment. The bell hop looks confused. Tyler glances at the bell hops empty arms and notices that his assumption was incorrect. “I’m sorry, what stuff are you talking about,” the bell hop says in a frightened voice. He doesn’t especially like the fact that he is upsetting a wrestler that is a lot larger than him. Tyler smiles and pats him on the back to show him that he is harmless. “Never mind kid, I got you mixed up with someone else. What can I do for you?” Tyler asks in his most friendly voice, as to not convey a hostile vibe. The bell hop looks down at his shiny black shoes and toes at the carpet. He doesn’t want to look Tyler eye-to-eye. “Well Mr. Preston, I don’t want to be the one to ruin any fun, but there is strictly no loud music to be played on the VIP floor. There are guests that are trying to sleep at all times of the day. We mustn’t disturb them,” the man says shyly. Tyler’s eyebrows jet down over his eyes in anger. “It was just some simple music to get me in the mood to get this room in shape. Sorry if I want to look at little more than white walls for my entire stay here,” Tyler says in an accusing voice as if this is the bell hop’s entire fault. The bell hop backs up a little as Tyler rests his huge paws on his waist. “I understand sir, but there are other people staying close to this room that will be easily disturbed. They have already contacted the desk, informing us that they do not want any sort of disturbance during the duration of their stay,” he mumbles under his breathe to exhibit to Tyler that he is being forced to do this, and it is not done under his own free will. “They requested that huh? Tell sir, who are some of the people staying around this vicinity?,” Tyler asks with a cunning smile spreading across his face that is now harboring stubble from a few days of not shaving. “I’m sorry Mr. Preston, but I can not give that kind of information out. Here on the Cloud Nine we are supposed to keep everything one-hundred percent classified. It’s to protect the celebrities and the wrestlers all the same,” the bell hop quickly replies as one of his rehearsed lines that he was trained to say. Tyler shakes his head in disapproval, and the young man backs up even further. “I think that you got me all wrong. The only reason that I want to know who they are is so that I can catch them later to apologize. I truly don’t mean to be a disturbance, and it hurts me a great deal to think that I may have been a nuisance during my first days here on the ship,” Tyler says with a smile and a sweet tone in his voice. He smiles innocently, to the point where the bell hop actually thinks he is speaking the truth. The bell hop smiles and breathes a deep breathe. “Oh, thank goodness. I thought you were one of those guys that would go find the people and beat them up. Sorry Mr. Preston, I truly did assume too many things about. Anyway, we have a fair amount of people staying around your room. There’s System of the Down, John Gargano as you may know, Silvio Rochel..” The bell hop says until Tyler puts the tip of his index finger over his lips to keep him from continuing on with his rant. “Silvio Rochelli huh? I suggest that you go right over to Rochelli’s room and tell him that if he can’t handle a little noise, then he has the wrong air ship. We aren’t going to cater to the every need of a stupid rookie,” Tyler says with pure disgust as he nudges the bell hops head back in a hostile fashion. The bell hop once again looks frighten as he temps the idea of just running away. He stands his ground and faces Tyler straight in the eyes. “This is not my doing Mr. Preston. I know that you may be angry, but every passenger here on the Cloud Nine gets the same treatment, whether they are a rookie or not,” the bell hop says as he winces. He holds his eyes tightly together as if Tyler is going to haul off and hit him. Tyler almost wants to, but continues giving him a piece of his mind. “I don’t care what the rules are; I’m not stepping around this punk. He walks in to my territory and thinks he can start making rule for ME to follow? He’s got another thing coming to him. You just go down to Silvio’s room and tell him that if he doesn’t like my music, he can run back to the Academy of wrestling where he came from, and still belongs,” Tyler screams out as he slams the door right in the cowering bell hops face. The bell hop stands in place for a moment to figure out what really happened. Tyler breathes deeply on the other side in frustration. ‘Silvio Rochelli, that guy is everywhere. He’s the only thing that they spoke about in the Academy of wrestling. He was all the hype as his ass makes his way over to the PCW. There has to be an end to this craze. That’s right; he’s just a ‘fad’ and nothing more. If I can just put up with him for a few more months at the most, he will disappear just like every other fad in the business. I remember the Overweighter, he was all the rage just a few months ago. Yes, now I’m seeing clearer, Rochelli is just a present time Overweighter. He’s a punk!” Tyler’s mind wanders as he throws a small temper tantrum over the inability to play his music loud. It seems to him that no matter what he wants to do, there is always some one in his way. ‘I bet once I create ‘Club Preston’ in this room, Rochelli will be the first one to try to get in. He always has to hone in on what other people work hard to perfect. Everyone paves the way for that little bastard and he just travels the easy road. I would like to see him break his back once in a while like John and I do. Instead, he just sits there and everyone drools over him, handing him anything that he wants. If he thinks I’m handing him this match, then he is wrong…dead wrong,’ he continues on, traveling further and further from the task at hand. He is supposed to be planning a way to transform his average living center in to a small scale comedy club, but his mind has other ideas. Tyler paces around the room, throwing a piece of clothing back in to the closet every now and then, but in the mean time, waiting for his supplies to create his comedy club to arrive. As he paces more and more, his mind continues to wander. He doesn’t want to focus on the hostile topic of Silvio Rochelli and Matt Jackson, but at the same time, it is inevitable. ‘Whatever happened to Matt Jackson being my buddy? He’s the one that got me in to this business. We’ve been in and out of the same organizations for years. Now he’s going to betray me at the drop of a hat. That sounds like him. I faintly recall a time where he was actually modest, but those days are obviously on the books for good. Would it kill him to pay at least a little respect to the men that do the grunt work in this organization? There would be no top of the card for him to star in if it wasn’t for men like Warrior and myself,’ his mind blazes, thinking of Matt Jackson’s arrogant face as he claims he is the rising star that everyone has been waiting for,’ his mind grinds at him as he eggs it on by staying idle for so long in his room. ‘A very good secret agent is Matt Jackson, but let’s just see if he can solve the mystery of his own murder to take place on Friday,’ his mind continues, causing him to laugh out loud. He covers his mouth, ashamed that he would laugh at such an evil thought. He tosses around more clothing until there is a soft knock at the door. His eyes open wide as he bolts to open the door. He presses his body against the smooth, cream metal of the door. He peers through the small peep hole in the top center of the door. He sees a magnified image of a second bell hop wielding various objects. It is the things he has asked for to complete ‘Club Preston’. ‘My stuff!’ his mind exclaims as he tries to contain himself in the presence of an unknown man. ‘I hope everyone is ready to laugh, because ‘Club Preston’ is starting to take form. As a great creation is born, a ‘fad’, Silvio Rochelli, will die,’ his mind says cunningly as he prepares to open the door and delve himself in to hard work on his project. The scene fades out to black.
