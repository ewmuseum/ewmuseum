In Search Of Self: Night Of The Living Sperminators!

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-02-03 Buddy lay asleep in the back of the Audi, while Skitzo sat in the driver’s seat, his ankle tiring as it remained suspended over the gas pedal keeping the acceleration at an even 68mph. A half hour ago or so Skitzo stopped at a gas station, filling the tank while Buddy crafted an origami swan from pages of the Playboy magazine. Glancing at him from inside the station, Skitzo paid for the fuel, and then quickly paid a visit to the next-door toilet cubicles. Hanging between two of them was a condom-vending machine. Not being able to resist the urge, Skitzo threw in all his spare change, around $13 worth, and emerged with his pockets bulging with many packs of ultra ribbed Durex. Leaving the men’s cubicles, instinct swerved him into the women’s cubicles, and surely enough five minutes later he emerged with five packs of Tampax. As he walked back to the Audi, he also spotted a soda-vending machine outside the station, and putting $5 into the machine, took 10 cans of Dr. Pepper back to the car, and got in the back seat. Skitzo: Buddy; do you know how to drive? Buddy: Alas, never in my travels have I come across an automobile, William. I could try if you-… Skitzo: Never mind…I need this car… Climbing over into the driver’s seat, Skitzo threw the condoms and tampons into the back seat, carefully placing the sodas on the seat so as not to fizz them up, which would ruin the plan. Driving back out onto the road, Skitzo made sure he was a good few miles from the station before parking on the verge and grabbing one condom, one tampon, and a can of soda. Buddy: What in heavens are you doing? Skitzo: Gonna have some fun…EXTREME fun! Buddy didn’t have to question this, as he knew already what Skitzo was and where he was going. Earlier in the day Skitzo had explained the PCW concept, and where he stood this Friday. Although Buddy too had no idea what a Gadget Shop Garbage Match was, he knew of Skitzo’s great interest in the bout. “You can triumph over adversity, William. When David defeated Goliath that was a show of strength, in the heart and soul as well as the muscles. You can reach your goal if you truly believe you can, and if not…you could always drive a couple of nails through your dildo to give it that extreme edge…!” Those were Buddy’s only words pertaining to the matter, before he turned on the radio and astonished Skitzo by finding a radio station he shouldn’t have, although Skitzo didn’t object, simply settling in and listening to Big Al on 98.5 WACF introducing “Who’s Your Daddy?” by Toby Keith. Turning the radio up, Buddy ‘jammed’ as only he knew how, while Skitzo drove on towards the gas station. Now, Skitzo was parked on the side of the road, Buddy feeling tired and readying himself for the aforementioned nap, the plan was put into action. Opening a can of soda, Skitzo worked one of the condoms around the rim, until it was stuck fast around the can. Shaking the can, Skitzo watched as physics took place before his eyes, and the condom fastened around the rim began to inflate and erect as the pressure from the fizzing of the soda left the can and entered the condom. To complete the ensemble, Skitzo tied one of the tampons around the condom, and taking a black marker pen from his kitbag, drew a little happy face on the head of the rubber. Skitzo: Eeexcellent… Twenty Minutes Later Buddy, having just woken up, tried to maintain eye contact with one of the condoms as it wobbled in the breeze. Skitzo had driven along, having passed Baker a few hours previously after the 6 or so hours of straight driving at around 70mph from Portland, faster when he knew there was no chance of traffic cops being around, which is why it was possible in the first place. Taking Interstate Highway 95 to State Highway 19, Skitzo finally parked the car at the side of the road and took off down the road a mile or so more with Buddy and the Condom/Tampon/Soda hybrids. Hiding in the bushes, they waited…and watched…and waited. A few hours passed and they waited some more, watching…until finally the roaring of a truck engine careering up the road came into audio range. Taking two of the soda cans each, Skitzo and Buddy waited, as the 18-wheeler suddenly speeded past they slung the cans, watching as one hit a wheel, two hit the back of the cab, and one flew through the window! An amazing stream of Dr. Pepper and Spermicide flew out the window as the truck driver’s wailing echoed, and the truck skidded on, failing to stop as the prospect of them being aliens invade the driver’s head, forcing him on. Laughing like a wild pair of loons, Buddy took another three condom cans and lined them up in the middle of the road, pointing to a soft-top Porsche with the top down making its way along the road. Surely enough it was filled with college students drinking and laughing. Hiding in the bushes Skitzo and Buddy watched as the Porsche hit the cans, and a wave of Spermicide and Dr. Pepper flew over the hood and into the car, soaking the students who screamed, the driver picking bits of condom out of his face. Buddy: What amazing creations! Skitzo: Yes…they will henceforth be known as…Sperminators! Smiling, Skitzo threw two more at a cow over in a nearby field, watching it take a fit as the cans exploded in its face, blinding it with Spermicide and aluminium. Now Skitzo and Buddy were left with one can, the sun was setting and night was approaching, time for the big finale. Skitzo: What can we do with this one? Buddy: Watch my friend… Placing the can in the middle of the road, Skitzo watched in amazement as Buddy stood back, closing his eyes and raising his arms. A bolt of lightning struck the condom suddenly, but instead of exploding, it began growing…growing…growing…sprouting arms and legs until finally it stood at 7ft, the happy face turning into a devious frown. Buddy sat down, tired, while the 7ft Sperminator simply stood awaiting some kind of instruction. Skitzo: Whoa man…how the-… Buddy: I can walk on water too ya know… Suddenly it clicked with Skitzo. The Porn-abusing, Country-loving, masturbating, sadistic Sperminator-growing Buddy that Skitzo had given a life was something otherworldly…but what? It stuck with Skitzo as Buddy jumped back into the bushes, swiping his long hair out of his face and watching as the Sperminator’s tampon began twirling extremely fast, until the Sperminator actually began floating into the air! Skitzo watched in astonishment as eyed up a nearby Toyota…and instead of smashing into it…flew off into the sunset! Skitzo: WHERE’S HE GOING?! Buddy: Carpe Diem… Skitzo: Kill whose carpets? Buddy watched as the final fragments of sunlight disappeared behind the horizon along with the Sperminator, and slapped Skitzo on the back, winking at him. Buddy: I can make my way to the State Park from here, thank you for all your help William…God be with you, my blessed brother… Unable to utter a single word, Skitzo watched as Buddy walked off into the darkness, and soon he took off back to his rental car waiting at the side of the road as he had left it. Nine of the Sperminator’s had caused grief for someone in some form, and for shits and giggles Skitzo saw that as a symbolic representation of the Gadget Shop match, being that of course the giant Sperminator could be related to him. Life worked in mysterious ways, and today was filled with mystery as Skitzo got back into his car and started off for Boise, another 5hrs away if he drove slowly. Turning on the radio, Skitzo was surprised to hear a news bulletin being read… …It was confirmed today that an explosion which occurred at the White House no more than a few minutes ago was the work of what witnesses say was a 7” can of Dr. Pepper, connected to which was what onlookers described as a giant condom and tampon with a frown…President George W. Bush is said to have suffered severe trauma and a broken nail, while Colin Powell has demanded War be declared on Iraq after this explosion from what the Secretary of State was one of Saddam’s Weapons of Mass Destruction…yes the 7” floating Condom/Tampon Soda Can bomb…more on this when and if it breaks…” Skitzo didn’t know what to think, instead he was busy picturing Buddy in his smock, drinking his urine and making origami animals…who in the hell could be so eccentric and weird to like Country and Western, Sperminators, Playboy, Smocks, and Mast-… Skitzo: ………JESUS H. CHRIST!!!!!!!!! ………………Must’ve been Jesus then. With that mystery solved, Skitzo proceeded on his way to Boise for the taping of Friday Fusion, where he knew he could go the distance once more and prove everyone wrong, taking his dildo with him to annihilate everyone from the Wee Tigger to his buddy Pete! No matter what happened he had already secured his place in the March Madness rumble on Turmoil, and while that wasn’t his main concern at the moment, the revelation that a Sperminator had almost killed the President and Iraq were getting the blamed was the only thing driving him at that point… He had been in turmoil for the past seven days. From dead sheep to fraud to terrorism and bitchslaps to dead ice gay ice cream men to fumigated churches to burning hotels to killing paedophiles to riding with Jesus incarnate and creating the greatest invention of all time: Sperminators, Skitzo was ready to take his success on Turmoil to Fusion and make a schizophrenic impact with everyone…leaving this trail of devastation behind him. Ah well, all in a week’s work for Skitzo! The End!!!
