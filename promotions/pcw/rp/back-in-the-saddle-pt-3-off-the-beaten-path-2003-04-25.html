Back in the saddle pt 3 - Off the beaten path

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-25-2003 BAM! The flames shoot out from the barrel, projecting the molten lead towards its target at incredible speeds. The bullet burns a hole in the man’s shirt then begins to dig into his flesh. It tears through the tissue and sinew, cauterizing the wound until finally striking bone. It shatters through the shoulder socket and mercifully escapes to the open air. The man spins around with the impact to his shoulder and falls to the ground. For what seems like an eternity, the man struggles to get to his feet. Fighting against the burning pain, he begins to reach for his pistol on his hip. Before he is able to, however, he is struck once more, this time in the chest. The impact this time sends him flying through the air, only to land unceremoniously on the ground. The townspeople begin coming out of their hiding places and head towards the fallen man. They keep their eyes on the man still holding the smoking gun. We find ourselves in the presence of our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne looking to be a little out of place. Instead of his normal attire of Armani suits, he wears a dusty brown poncho over equally dusty shirt and denim jeans. Instead of loafers, boots, fully equipped with spurs that glimmer in the sun are found. Under a cowboy hat, eyes squinted by the suns glare, pierce through the air to meet the gaze of the man across from him. The sun bears down with no remorse, waves of heat bouncing from every surface. A Tumbleweed bounces across the road. A raven flies over head, silhouetted against the sun. Dan holsters his pistol and pulls a cigar from his shirt pocket, lighting it and placing the lighter back into his jeans. The undertaker signals from the fallen man that he has taken his last breath. Nervously at first, then rushing in an emotional sprint towards Dan, the townspeople rush to their hero. Hands stretch out to touch him, children tug at his clothing, and young maidens kiss him, all in appreciation of his recent accomplishment. The terror has been ended. The bandit thwarted. The town is safe again after the countless years of the man’s reign of terror. Without a word, Dan turns and saunters to his steed. Grasping the reigns, Dan mounts his horse with one fluid motion. Turning around he urges the horse to walk. The townspeople stand in awe as he rides into the sunset. They know in their hearts that he will never return. Many of them drop to their knees in prayer, thanking the Almighty for this gift, however brief. Their days will carry on in peace. Thanks to one man. “What the hell is that? That has absolutely nothing to do with your story. What does a gunfight have to do with your return to the wrestling ring, Dan?” “Nothing. It was just a dream I had last night. It’s hard to keep all these flashbacks in order.” “Oh… my… God.” Fade to black
