Date: Today Freak and Igor let the young woman go once they found a proper hiding place from the Archangel Andariel. The distant voices were still heard from the scene of the incident, even though Freak and Igor were both hiding out in a small bunker located at the end of a long alleyway. Freak was distressed, the Archangel knew of his weakness, and until nightfall, he was vullnerable to Andariel. Igor knew of his weakness as well, but he would not let his master fall because of mere daylight, Igor knew the streets well and could keep him well hidden. Freak was greatful for his apprentice in the dark arts, and has great hopes for him to carry on his ways if Freak passes. Igor carries knowledge of the Afterworld, and not much people ever find out about that until they die. His religion is basically lost, and only the good side is treasured. If it was not for evil, then the human's civilization would not exist. Freak beleived this, because he was the Messiah Of Darkness, and he preeched his lost religion to his tag partner, and now apprentice, Igor Castro. The two were both silent, not saying much since the retreat from Andariel. Freak sat with his arms crossed, deep in thought of his next move. He sat still, with his eyes closed and head bent down. His hair droped over his face as he concentrated on his mind. Igor just sat and stared at him, curiously. He was sitting on a chair but a meter away in the small bunker. Silence grew more once the activity going on outside soon died. Igor could hear the flourecent lights above, giving the delicate hum that filled the little room. Igor shifted his hand to rub his bald head, Igor was much shorter then Freak, he being only 6' while Freak being much taller at 6'6. Igor had a more childish look too him, but strong for his youth. He was muscular, but depends more on his swiftness of feet to win wrestling matches in PCW. Freak was much more bulk then him, having large arms stretching his shirt and wide thighs that were noticeable through his jeans. Igor admired his strength as a human, but admired his strength as a demon even more. Once night falls over the earth, Freak becomes imensely powerful. His body changes, he becomes super fast with reflexes faster then any living being on earth. His strength increases too, if he were to go against his honor in wrestling and summon his powers during a match, he could overpower anyone in PCW. But, Freak would never do that, he even has his limits of power. 'Do you think that you could teach me about the black arts to pass the time?' Igor said starting conversation. Freak broke his concentration and moved his head up to face Igor with his dark eyes. 'Certainly, but... are you ready?' Freak answered Igor's question with another question. Igor's stomach tightened and his mind was whipped with fear of not being able to be taught the arts, 'Ready? Of course, you took me as your apprentice, I am ready!' Igor asked anxious. 'For you to be ready is your desicion, and yours alone. I only picked you because of your past, your mind, it is so damaged and corrupted, similar to mine. I am just going to tell you that once you start training, then you can never turn back. You will know how it ends, you can see the way the judgement works before you are sent to the Afterworld, you will have some black energy in you, and will be decided immediately, and I know where you will end up.' Freak explained in his dark and scratchy voice. 'The Underworld, the hell of The Afteroworld.' Igor finished the sentence. Igor's face had a sense of depression, realizing what he was getting himself into. 'You could suffer a false punishment, if you start training, and then quit, you will go to the Underoworld, and suffer as a mere reincarnation (-Reincarnation- Once your fate has been decided, and go to heaven or hell, you will be spend 100 years reincarnating, and the more good or evil you are depending on where you end up, you will reincarnate into a stronger being. ex: Freak being the Messiah Of Evil, automatically made him the strongest reincarnation of evil, the vampire. Andariel was a good person, a real christian throughout his life, and he was reincarnated into the strongest reincarnation of good, the Archangel.), when you could finish your training and become a vampire, like I.' Freak told Igor with a complete serious tone. 'The question is, if you start, will you finish?' Freak stated. 'Yes. I will complete the training, and return to the living world and we will both reunite them, and once again Satin shall rule.' Igor answered. 'Excellent!' Freak said with great enthusiasim. A smile appered on Igor's face, as he prepared to get started with his training. Igor waited for this moment since he first learned the truth about the universe. Once he could harness his power, he might be as powerful as his master, Freak. 'First, your mind must be clear of thoughts... Once your mind is quieted, you must meditate on important events throughout your life to make your reason of life noticeable. Every life form has a purpose, find yours!' Freak instructed. Igor sat in his chair comfortably, and began to slow his breathing, he kept concentrating on peace of mind, so he could begin. Momentarily, all his hopes, his stress, his concerns, dissapeared from his mind. 'Now, concentrate on you recent events of your life.' Freak said as if knowing what Igor was thinking. Igor went into deep concentration, determined to gain his goal. H ebegan thinking about how Freak is teaching him, about Andariel, about PCW. His mind, once empty, now full of thought. 'Are you ready?' Freak asked once again. Igor nodded his head slightly. 'Good, now think about the bad things throughout your life, things that you hate, things that you dispise!' Freak said. Igor began to channel his thoughts, releasing the good ones, and thinking of the bad. He came into veiw of his drunken father, he came into the veiw of Andariel, he thought of his opponents in PCW, of how bad he wants the tag team championship and of how badly he wants to have entrance to the Afterworld. Igor's two medalions began to glow, and it was visibke through his t shirt. Freak became aware, and a smile appeared on his face as Igor had his head down while concentrating. 'Unleash your anger! Give in to your hatred, except your black heart!' Freak yelled rising Igor's momentum. Igor's head rose, and he opened his eyes into a dead glow. Black, evil energy fountained from his eyes, and he was feeling the power that begun to surge through him. Suddenly the door of the room opened, and wind blew in knocking Freak and Igor to their feet. Andariel walked in with his long thin wings taking most room in the small bunker. Igor's concentration was broken and he too fell to the floor. 'Ha, you can not hide from me, I could sense the energy build up from the other side of town!' Andariel yelled as one of his six glowing wings reached towards Freak and grasped him by the leg and dragging him outside, slamming against a brick wall. Andariel took his attention to Igor that was just getting up from Andariel's surprise attack. 'You will suffer the same fate as your master!' Andariel yelled at Igor while racing towards him and punching him in the ribs causing him to literaly fly to the other side of the room. While Andariel was distracted, Freak recovered quickly, and summoned magic from his medalion, which was much weaker then his summoned power when nightfall hits. He possessed some power through a medalion in the daylight to dsefend himself, but unfortunately, it was not powerful enough to stop the mighty Archangel. Freak pressured the negative energy into the palms of his hand and released it towards Andariel. Once it reached him, it evaporated momentarily. Andariel, in his gold full-body armour turned around and faced the injured Freak once again. Andariel was unaware of the current time, the sun above was already a yellow colour and the sun was setting fast. Freak was aware, so he fled. Andriel jumpd high over the buildings in the alleyway and spread his large and graceful wings and took a birds-eye veiw over Freak running down the empty streets. towards a park that had many trees. Can the sun set fast enough for Freak to summon his true power and defend himself against the mighty Andariel? to be continued...
