Origin - Primetime Championship Wrestling

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th April 2003 The Dark Wrestling Alliance nothing more than a distant memory, Primetime Wrestling Academy the Genesis for both the Jester and the tag team banner he headlines beneath and New True Internet Championship Wrestling really a step in the wrong direction. The Dark Wrestling Alliance was associated with New Blood, arguably a different gimmick but hidden beneath the surface of the same face New Blood was another personality and therefore pays no mind to the origin of the Emerald Menace. Dead and buried, New Blood was succeeded by the green tailored Jester. “We’ve heard this before!” For those of you who have paid attention and followed the series then indeed the above revelations are nothing more than a prologue for the events to come but for those who decided to ignore then consider this brief summary the dissected past of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Jester. The Dark Wrestling Alliance, ridden in corruption and mismanagement waved goodbye to their first ever Double Gold Champion and as his back was turned their waves turned to that of ‘Good Riddance’ thus the closing of one door and the opening of another. The bright light at the end of the tunnel was certainly not Heaven but career salvation as the deceased New Blood was welcomed into the Primetime Wrestling Academy. Now under the guise of Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green the opportunity to learn from the elite of the industry was far more appealing than applying the little knowledge of his trade in another promotion that would eventually fold. “I’ll spare you the details…” Indeed. The Jester’s tenure with the Academy was mentored by Old School Wrestling’s goliath Hessian who can also take further credit in the birth of what would become the most dominant tag team of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s Third Age, Pranks With Attitude. With New True Internet Championship Wrestling resurrected the time had came for the Jester to be placed under the community microscope as both members of the Conniving Collective were shipped across. “New True Internet Championship Wrestling would send a cold tingling shiver down the spine of the most upper-lipped Britain so imagine what nightmarish thoughts still haunt a person who once participated there?” As you can guess the less said about NTICW the better but the dynamic duo’s involvement there did attract interest from a number of high-profiled scouts and when their contracts were terminated by mutual consent then it was a case of doing ones homework. The time had come to evaluate what Wrestle Online had to offer? Primetime Wrestling Circuit wasn’t even a twinkle in its creators eye back then as he was pre-occupied with his current venture in Old School Wrestling, the bastard company of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Literally Old School Wrestling liberated its rival’s roster and by doing so had created an existing fan base but if truth were told, the company never once showed an interest in Pranks With Attitude as a unit or individually and why would they? They had already strengthened their tag team division and during that process they had already overlooked the fellowships application so it was a case of pursuing other avenues for the Jester and Vampire collective. A company that had come forward with an offer was RUSH but they lacked the credentials. First and foremost RUSH had no tag team division and their offer was for the duo to participate as singles competitors, which may have elevated them both as household names but the true niggling doubt lay that RUSH was considered by the masses as the trailing promotion. Lagging behind the duo were not prepared to make the same mistakes as they did with New true Internet Championship Wrestling and so the only alternative interested in swooping was PCW. “The rest as they say is history!” The ink of the contract had long since dried when both the Emerald Menace and Living Nightmare debuted at Primetime Championship Wrestling’s Third Dawning Pay-Per-View as they injected themselves into a Fatal Four-Way Elimination match. Making no immediate impact the duo soon reversed their misfortune by aligning alongside fellow debutant Anarchy and the company’s king of the cruiserweights Alexander Miyagawa under the newly resurrected ‘Asylum’ banner. Headed up by Alexander himself the duo were following in the footsteps of greats as Asylum during previous incarnations homed household names like Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon. The latter at this point had been installed as the promotions alpha male with his Championship victory over the departed Tyler Nelson and ironically his stable entitled The Front Office would face Asylum in the Main Event of Tuesday Turmoil only two weeks after they had debuted. Pranks With Attitude was taken old school and by that I’m not making any reference to the competition (Old School Wrestling) but the beating they suffered at the hands of their Front Office counterparts. What doesn’t kill you makes you stronger and the parable couldn’t be more right because the team like a Phoenix rose from its own ashes and captured the PCW Tag Team Championship for reign number one of five. In saying that, short and sweet would apply here too for two weeks later the Champions would lose those titles to their nemesis’ Playaz Inc in what would then fuel a heated feud between both parties. Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo (Collectively Playaz Inc) continued to have a strangle hold of the gold for a number of weeks with P.W.A playing second fiddle behind. The frustration not only seeped into the team but the stable of Asylum and eventually the four-piece group disbanded bitterly mind you as the Vampire caved in Alexander’s cranium, coincidentally forcing him into early retirement. Courtesy of Alexander’s wife, Pranks With Attitude reclaimed the titles making them two-time Champions and over the months things jogged along. “Until…” Playing a heinous prank on CEO Xavier Kannon would cost the dynamic duo as they were stripped of the gold after successfully holding their prize for a record fifty-three days, eclipsing the previous record held by Media’s Politically Incorrect. However in retort the Scientologist booked the pair to face off against one another as he booked them to compete at the looming next Pay-Per-View. With a week to go the duo were scheduled to face the conceited CEO in a match and as it concluded the Jester had shown his true allegiance by joining forces with the depleted Front Office who had lost the insolent Duke Williams. Pranks With Attitude were finished and at the Pay-Per-View the Angel Of Fear Nascosto Ireul Nerezza beat his backstabbing former friend and partner to a near inch of his life. The feud wouldn’t dissolve there as the Jester wormed a rematch for the next Pay-Per-View entitled Shanghaied where through trickery and deceit the prankster won, claiming full ownership of the brand name ‘Pranks With Attitude’. Soon came Tag Team Championship reign number three. In a special ‘Pot Luck’ match the Champions Josh Starwind and El Romantico fell short of retaining and the Front Office were crowned in their place as Xavier Kannon captured tag gold for the first time in his illustrious career alongside the man titled ‘The King Midas Of Tag’. Primetime Championship Wrestling’s alpha male however lost his half to the Living Nightmare a week later thus unintentionally reforming the Trickster Team and courtesy of a little revenge the Vampire played a huge part in the loss that would coronate Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm as their heirs. “The rest as they say is history… Again!” The titles changed hands quicker than Bobby Stormbringer can change identities and announcing the whole separation as nothing more than a swerve saw the Vampire inducted into the Front Office. The titles in the meantime bounced about between Storm/ Windsor, The Dark Age and Pranks With Attitude until finally the untimely marriage accident that set up the bout for Spring Fling. An historical night that not only crowned new Tag Team Champions but also in doing so placed them onto the same pedestal of Blood Brothers and Da Westsidaz with five PCW Tag Team Championship reigns apiece. “From Origin to Origin – Primetime Championship Wrestling that about covers the career of Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green and his handiwork that is Pranks With Attitude” - To be continued -
