Tumbling the Giant?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Just now... Tumbling the giant? It’s so utterly easy for the cunning, little munchkin. His clever confidence of devious traps, pulled from an endless bag of goodies, makes him a dangerous threat to giants of all kinds. Emerging from his hole, cut out of the molding along the wall at floor level, the mischievous little man licks his lips, barely checking to see if the coast is clear. Hypnotically drawn out from his haven by the seductive aroma of… A reassuring sniff does indeed confirm it is cheese. Scanning the area: No, not the table, or the counter, and definitely not the sink. Wait, target: Located! The refrigerator, normally an impenetrable fortress, but now its gate hangs open a mere inch. Some careless fool has left defenses wide open for an invasion, and there’s no saving the vittles now. Persuaded by his first, and foremost instinct, he follows through on the orders his grumbling stomach gives him. Running in place, his feet trying to find some traction of the slippery tile floor, he works up a cloud of dirt before bolting forward. Weaving through the legs of the dinner table, and the four chairs as well, naturally, he blindly scampers off to his destination. It’s a tight squeeze, even for a puny “rodent” like himself, but he manages to suck it in, and immerse himself within the cold conditions of the new environment. Shivering, his intuition demands action quickly, hopefully before his small body submits to the cold. With the door open, the fridge light remains on, giving him the advantage of sight, and smell. It doesn’t take long for the tiny man to make it all the way up to the top shelf, where his deepest desire awaits him, ready to fill his need… Bright as gold, the bacteria bred delicacy illuminates his excited face, and it’s a matter of nanoseconds before he greedily drives his hands into the substance. The triangular block of cheddar dwarfs him, but he manages to lift the whole mass of it overhead, envelop a corner in the mouth, and drive the entire wad of golden delight straight down to his bowels. His torso, having taken the shape of the cheese, breaks the balance his legs support him with. Breaking at the knees, he falls to his rump with a sigh of satisfaction. “What I need is some good, rich, pure, uncut cheese.” Small tremors beneath him warn him of the approaching behemoth. This isn’t good… “What the…? Oh no, the door is open! My food’s going to be spoiled!” Swinging the door wide open the rest of the way, a towering mass of a man bends over, to examine the fridge. Eyes darting from produce item to produce item, until they snap they’re aim upon the empty plate where there was once over a pound of solid cheese, but now a single morsel. Boda very much wanted that cheese. Boda: “(Growling, frustrated with the sudden predicament.) My cheese, damn it!” Other than the empty plate, the top shelf shows no signs of the perpetrator, not in sight at least. Furious, his only solution in mind is the strip search the entire fridge, literally tear the thing apart. It’s then that, out of the corner of his eye, he sees something scampering across the tile floor… Boda: “HUH? A Syko! C’mere you little…” A trickster by nature, evading the big, bad Boda was an easy get away, but it’s staying away that counts. And this little cat burglar is not getting away without a clean chase. Score one for the Goblin, though, as Boda attempts to pursue, falling flat on his face. The old tie the shoes together by the laces trick. The backyard doesn’t deem Boda successful, either. Syko watches from around the corner of the house, snickering, as Boda attempts to walk, but steps on a rake causing the wooden handle to come up, and smack him in the face. There are a lot of rakes, so this could take a while… Back in the house, Boda lies in wait over a mouse hole in his living room, frying pan in hand. An easy set-up for Syko to creep up from behind, sporting a weapon of his own. Busy with anticipation, Boda never expects a whooping from the rear. Getting a bit creative, Boda tries the robotic “Girl Syko” robot trick. Moments later, he’s kissing a “Girl Boda” robot, right before it explodes. Boda buys a ferocious cat. Paper reads the next day that the cat turned up in a river. Dynamite is strategically placed in all the mouse holes, only to have it returned while he is at a safe distance, fingers in ears. Boda tries, Syko outsmarts. It’s just how it goes. Size does not matter, but rather it is wit that wins the war. Right? Quite cocky with his accomplishments in outsmarting the dimwitted Boda thus far, Syko decides to have a little fun. The standard chase scenario goes into action, and of course Syko stays on step ahead, setting a trap for Boda. Waiting patiently for Boda, a smile wide from cheek to cheek, the Goblin is not quick enough to escape Boda this time. Boda: “I GOTCHA! NOW, SYKO… I WILL CRUSH YOU PUNY BONES!” Within the clutches of Boda, Syko doesn’t stand a chance. Boda squeezes the hand held victim within a clenched fist. Syko turns blue, and his organs feel as if they are about to splatter out. The support of his skeleton begins to collapse, his frame; no match for Boda’s amazing strength… It’s all over… “AHHH!” No, anything but that! Sitting up, the Goblin finds himself drenched in his own sweat. A nightmare, worse than any he has ever beheld before, branded permanently into his train of thought, perhaps a cause of horrible flashbacks in the near future. There’s a limit to what a normal man, or Goblin, can take. Syko the Goblin: “BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOODAAAAAAAAAA!!!!” That night, I was severely reminded of my greatest handicap in life. Size. TBC
