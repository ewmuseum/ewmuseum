Easing Traffic Congestion

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Easing Traffic Congestion Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 22/01/03 “Eugh, like, HELLO?!?! Important people WAY trying to go somewhere important and stuff!” With the whiny and frustrated voice of PCW’s preppy Commissioner we open up on a shot of total gridlock, cars packed in like Veal calves on their way to slaughter with the road blocked for as far as the eye can see. Cutting from the tapestry of automobiles on asphalt, we see the stressed face of Eleanor Kannon-Hall, looking out the window of the Front Office’s Limousine at all the surrounding traffic, looking resentful that they’re even on the same stretch of road as her. Eleanor : “HEY! That guy there is, like, SO old! Maybe we can ask him to die early so we can WAY steal his spot in the queue?” Beside her, looking a tad calmer is the PCW CEO, Xavier Kannon. Reclining lazily with his arms crossed over his chest and head dropped back, he closes his eyes to try and block out the sound of car horns and his wife’s moaning. Eleanor : “Um.... Mr. Driver Man, can’t you, like, fly or something?” Not surprisingly, through the tinted glass dividing screen, the Grey-capped driver turns his head to the right and answers back with a ‘No’, not pleasing Ellie~! who drops back into her seat in a huff. Eleanor : “Hmmph... Some driver YOU are!” Relaxing beside her, Kannon opens his left eye and looks across to his wife in his usual peculiar way. Eleanor : “Okay, next Tuesday we are SO having Turmoil at our house, all this travelling blows to the highest order of blow!” Groaning, Kannon closes his eye again and fidgets, trying to get himself comfortable to sleep through all the ambient noise. Seeing Xavier starting to nod off, Ellie~! looks around for entertainment but finds none forthcoming so again kneels on the back seat looking out of the window. Eleanor : “WHOA! You, like, SO have to see this!” Despite the enthusiasm in her voice, Kannon’s attention can not be grabbed, much to her annoyance. With an evil little smile crossing her Pink lips, Ellie~!’s brain turns over. Eleanor : “... Duke in a monster truck is WAY about to ram us!” Eyelids springing open, Kannon flies out of his seat and towards the window with a look of terror on his pale face, only to see nothing but still traffic and his wife giggling uncontrollably beside him. Eleanor : “You are, like, SO easy!” His giggly little wife still cracking up, Kannon, holding his chest drops back into his seat and starts taking deep breathes, mumbling under his breath. Xavier : “That’s what they said about you...” Ellie~!’s face is now beet Red after her laughing fit, the little prep calming down and dropping back into her seat once more, boredom returned. Eleanor : “Like.............. SO.............. Bored!” His breathing now regulated, Kannon returns to resting, not entertaining Ellie~! much to her annoyance. Scurrying across the leather seat towards him, Ellie~! watches his face, eye closed and oblivious to her presence. Eleanor : “Like, wake up already...” Her words do not stir him so instead she moves her left hand in front of his face and starts waving. Getting no reaction, with her Pink and Silver nailed index finger, she flicks his nose. Xavier : “Uh?” Coughing as he’s pulled from his sleep, Kannon growls at Ellie~! who just grins back at him with her innocent little smile before dropping her head onto his lap, snuggling up to him and trying to get to sleep herself. “HEY! SHE’S SUCKING HIM OFF!!!” A yell comes from the pick-up truck parked to their left, the checked-shirt wearing occupants winding down their window to hoot as from their view, Ellie~!’s nap looks like something else. “WHOOOOOO! CHUG ON IT, SLUT!!!” Her face turning sour, Ellie~!’s head raises up and looks to the truck through the window, the two men in the front punching the air, cheering her on while Kannon doesn’t stir. Eleanor : “Ewwwwwwwwzies, they look like icky Duke people!” The 2 men continue to hoot and cheer, opening their mouths wide and moving a gripping hand back and forth in front of it to simulate what they thought she was up to. Winding the window down, Ellie~! looks to them with distaste. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?! I’m not Teresa Tomas!” Shaking her head, Ellie~! rolls her eyes as the men continue, getting on her nerves as they point down to their respective crotches. Eleanor : “You, SO shouldn’t make me angry....” Her pale Blue eyes looking to them with determination, the little prep continues muttering. Eleanor : “... You wouldn’t like me when I’m WAY angry and stuff!” The warning doing nothing to dissuade the 2 hicks who don’t let up with their suggestive words and actions, Ellie~! grabs the Limo’s door with both her little hands, wrists adorned in all manner of bands and bracelets, then rips it clean off before throwing it at the pick-up truck. “WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOA!!!” Her little foot stamping down on the concrete, Ellie~! steps out of the Limo, her face showing rare anger. Under her skinny fit Pink t-shirt a muscle probably bulges somewhere. Then, throwing her head back, she yells in her preppy little voice. Eleanor : “PREPSTREAK, LIKE, WAY GO RAAAAAA!!!!” She beats her 34B chest before stopping, wincing. Eleanor : “Owwwwwwwwwwwwzies... Like, SO not doing that again!” In the pick-up truck, the 2 men hold their hands up in defence, apologising over and over again. Eleanor : “PREPSTREAK... SMASH!!!” Effortlessly, Ellie~! grabs the pick-up truck in both hands and heaves it into the air, throwing it over 2 lanes of traffic and into the ditch before leaping on the spot and applauding herself. Eleanor : “YAY PREPSTREAK!!!” Suddenly, speeding into the gap left by the pick-up truck, a Stationwagon tries to screech to a halt before hitting the preppy little Commissioner but it’s too late, the car smashing into her, compacting itself and as Ellie~! itches her leg wondering what just tickled her. Looking around she sees a twisted heap on metal and scratches her head. Eleanor : “Was that, like, there before?” Shrugging, Prepstreak Ellie~! looks at all the traffic in front of their Limo and scurries over, grabbing the back bumper of the vehicle and tossing it 10 feet up into the air before landing with a crash in the lane to the right. Eleanor : “Like.... SO.... RAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!” As the Monster Known as Prepstreak cuts a swathe through the gridlock, tossing cars left and right the shot fades to Black, only the crashing of metal and glass against concrete being heard. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
