The Next Mayor Of Hubbardville?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/07/02 (#2 for PPV) Wrestlers crave many things. Most just want a good career, an uppercard spot, maybe some Titles to have under their name, possibly headline a Pay-Per-View... This is also the case in PCW. Amidst those who wrestle to please Allah, or fight with the confidence of the Void to back them up, most are just honest workers trying to make their way.. Well, there is also a bunch of sadistic killer... And then, there are the greedy ones. They desire more than just money.. Any wrestler can earn money through independent bookings and merchandise sales... These wrestlers crave POWER. While Duke Williams may be President, he is not a greedy, he is there for the people. It is those typified by Tyler Nelson such as Xavier Kannon and Frisco who relish power. Frisco wants it in his country, while Kannon has it in PCW. Exploiting his shares he bought with Tyler Nelson to the fullest he has become almost impervious to punishment, he attacks and injures people with barely a shrug of the shoulders... But now he is turning his attention away from wrestling in his pursuit of power.... For while the PCW World Title slips toward his grasp, so does something he wants to own just as much.. If not more... ... He WANTS Hubbardville, he helped make it reality, now he wants to make it HIS. “Look at it...... Isn’t is just.... So far beyond words?” Our shot opens up on the famous L. Ron Space Needle in downtown Hubbardville just off the corner of Wearenotacult Drive and Harikrishnasdie Walk where the giant structure seems to protrude into the clouds themselves... The perfect place to view the futuristic and glistening cityscape below. “It’s certainly... Well, like.... THERE...” Looking down at the skyscrapers threatening to break into their eyeline the city can be seen in all it’s splendour... The lush Green around every block is host to groups of young Scientologists playing while in the offices above them the older followers of Hubbard work to keep up the religion’s vast reserves of money.... And right through the middle the majestic River Hubbard flows, the high midday Wisconsin sun glistening on the surface. “And soon... It shall all be mine.” Pulling back from the windowed dome we see the 1st couple of Scientology themselves, Xavier Kannon and his wife Eleanor Kannon-Hall looking down at it all, a definite look of fondness for once being seen in Kannon’s usually cold Green eyes. Eleanor : “So then when you die it’ll be.... Like mine... YAY ME!!!” He looks to his enthusiastic young wife with raised eyebrows to which she just shrugs and starts to point to various landmarks. Eleanor : “I’d paint that Pink... And get rid of that, we don’t need that.... I’d make that a Mall... Oh, and I’d move the river to there...” As she plans the city in her little Preppy mind, Xavier breaks away, breaking her concentration and the camera turns with him to see the L Ron. Space Needle has become something rather different, it’s become his campaign room. He looks at it in awe, nodding his head in agreement at his surroundings. Xavier : “So.. Where do I stand?” Before where he stands, the whole room has been decked out in his colours of Black, Purple and Green... Tables are set up in rows that house phone after phone after phone, all manned by men or women dressed smartly and wearing a “VOTE XK” rosette. On the far wall there is a giant White board where 2 names are written at the bottom.... KANNON and RIBISI.... Both at the foot of empty columns that await their individual statistics. Campaign Worker : “Well, the feelers have been send out and we’ll have an indication of where the A-List stand anytime so-” The man is then cut off as one of the men dealing with the phones throws his hands into the air and yells out in jubilation. “WE GOT TRAVOLTA!!!” After a few brief seconds of taking the information in, the room explodes into applause and cheering at having Hollywood’s main ambassador for Scientology onside and there is a constant hum of conversation around the top of the L. Ron Space Needle. “I thought he was already a Scientologist?” Kannon’s concentration is broken as Ellie~! has grown bored of planning his city and wandered like a lost lamb back to his side, looking at all the workers running around. Eleanor : “Or did they only just find out?” Xavier turns to his wife and looks at her oddly, wondering if she actually has a clue what he is doing... Or she for that matter. Xavier : “No my little Fuck Ferret, it means that John Travolta is backing me in my quest to become Mayor of Hubbardville, something that Hubbard himself would champion me for...” Ellie~! looks from him to the room and back, then again, looking confused. Eleanor : “You’re running for Mayor?” Kannon slaps his hand on his forehead and Ellie~! raises her eyebrows, nodding in approval at Kannon’s quest. Eleanor : “So, like... Who are you running against?” He points over to the East wall where their are campaign posters for both himself and Giovanni Ribisi, the 2 candidates in the race for power in Hubbardville. The Gold Patron Meritorious then sneers at the sight of the goofy actor who he loathes and says his name with utter disgust. Xavier: “Ribisi.” Eleanor : “Want me to help?” Kannon then looks from his wife to the assembled team of elite campaign managers who he has drafted in to aid him. Xavier : “No.. You’re okay my little Fuck Ferret, let them do all the work and you can enjoy all the benefits once I win.” Eleanor : “You sure? I can run a campaign you know... When I was running for Prom Queen I had the closest rival’s brake cut.. One fireball of a car crash later and DAH DAH!!! Ellie~! was Queen!” Where most normal men would look in horror at such a revelation, the Scientologist just puts his arm around Ellie and kisses the top of her head. Xavier : “I love you.” “RIBISI GOT ALLEY!!!!” Another of Kannon’s campaign team leap up from their seats with the bad news.... While Kannon has Travolta, Ribisi has gained the support of his female equivalent. Xavier : “WHAT?!?!? Why has she sided with that dufus?” One of his campaigners nervously weighs in with a possible reason. Campaigner : “Er.... Remember that Ellie~! said she looked like a Moose who went on the Elvis Cheeseburger diet?” His wife sniggers and covers her mouth to disguise it as Xavier turns to her. Eleanor : “Ooooops.” “HEY XAVI!” Suddenly all the eyes in the room leave the Prep, much to her liking and they focus on the man who has just walked into the top of the L. Ron Space Needle... Standing out like a sore thumb amidst the smartly dressed suits of Kannon’s campaign team... The man is none other than GIOVANNI RIBISI. Kannon scowls. Ribisi : “I thought, seeing as this is a friendly competition and all we could share this place as a campaign headquarters... Wow, you got a lot of people.. I just have my Mom opening male.” He is met with stony silence by all the people on the room. Xavier : “Of course you can Giovanni...” His answer is met by even more stunned silence as his fellow Scientologist wanders into the room, taking a good look around. Ribisi : “Wow, you’ve got a lot of cool stuff...” Still, all Kannon’s campaigners have stopped what they are doing and look at him with cold, blank looks... But seeing how he is a complete Moron he fails to pick up on it. Xavier : “Hey, Giovanni, come and look at this... The trees in Jenna Elfman Gardens look like an image of Hubbard!” The star of the Mod Squad rushes over to the window, eager for a look and he cranes his neck in all angles to try and see it... As he presses his face onto the glass, Xavier walks back towards the centre of the room, Ellie~! in tow and he then nods to one of his team... Giovanni : “I don’t see it...” The staff member nods back and reaches behind him to a big Red button which he presses. Giovanni : “Are you su-ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!” The glass panel he was leant up against drops out of it’s fixings.... Shortly followed by Ribisi himself and both hurtle from the top of the L. Ron Space Needle to the ground 400 feet below.... Xavier : “Ooooops.” *UMPTH* As they hear the sickening thud it acts as a catalyst for the team to start work again and within a second or two the room is alive with the sound of frenzied campaigning as we fade to Black. “Someone fix that window... It’s causing a draft.” “Causing.... A..... Draft!”
