Friendly Lacuna

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/04/03 Her chest heaved outwards as her muscled worked overtime in lifting the weights again. It had been a long time since she had been so overly engrossed in training that the outside world had been shunned, but today was such a day. Her brown eyes were hidden under the thin skin of her eyelids as she pressed upwards again, hoisting the weights up and locking her arms, before bringing them down again. Great, yet another promo set in a gym, where someone shows off quite how much they can lift. I’m so engrossed. End sarcasm. The world around her had ceased to exist. She hadn’t had contact with the news outside of her grounds for a good few days now, and she hadn’t left the safe security of the land she owned for the same amount of time. Such matters seemed trivial know, she just didn’t care any more. She mumbled something under her breath as she pushed the weights up again. It was impossible to make out her worlds as they were hidden under layer upon layer of hidden contexts and Lacuna-speak. She probably didn’t know what she said herself. She mumbled and grunted as if she had something important she was trying to express without the medium of words, yet in fact, she hadn’t a clue she was even doing it. She thought so much that sometimes it spilled outside of her head and into her speech without her doing so much as batting an eyelid. Her raven black hair was coated in a thick layer of sweat, and hung limply from her head, pulled down from the bench towards the immaculate tiled floor. She had been doing this for some time now, and she was clearly aching. Her arms where shaking violently with the effort as she pushed forth the weights towards the ceiling yet again. Her eyes were still closed and her purple lips pursed tightly in deep contemplation. “Why are you doing this?” from across the room, Brook had been watching Lacuna. He was perched on the end of a similar bench, but had not touched the weights. His eyes roved over Lacuna like he was transfixed: nearly as much as Lacuna was with her own thoughts. Lacuna was transfixed. She ignored the presence of Brook (now that she was aware of it, and she was aware of it, just pretending that she wasn’t) and continued with the constant lifting and pressing. She had settled into the routine now, and it was beginning to come a bit easier. Her arms were still shaking violently, but she was ignoring the pain and working through it. After all, she had far too much to think about at the moment, Brook stood gently to his feet and, in quite a reversal, clomped his booted feet heavily across the tiled flooring with a childlike nature. “Why are you ignoring me?” he called out across the room, his voice echoing loudly. “Hello? Person talking to you here! Earth calling Lacuna, come in you rich English snob!” Slowly, Lacuna hung up the weights and sat up smoothly, turning her whole body to face the balding Brook, who was still crossing the room loudly. “She called me that” Lacuna’s voice was cold and calculated; as if every word she spoke had been planned prior to speech. “She will pay for her remarks” “Who’s she?” enquired Brook as he stopped next to Lacuna and sat down, scratching his head in confusion “Are you obsessing over Tessa again? What has she done to you anyway?' “No” she spoke, still calm as anything. “She has or has had absolutely no bearings over my feelings as far as I can ever remember” “So… why was it you went psycho about her then?” Brook was confused, which was quite a rarity in a man who ran his life like clockwork. He didn’t have much time for confusion, or even thinking outside of his daily routine. It had even been quite an intense battle with his conscience to go down to the gym and work out what Lacuna was up to. Now he wished he hadn’t. Brook had been Lacuna’s only friend for quite some time now, and the only member of her workforce she would trust with anything beyond the right to breathing. He had felt the regrettable twang of pain as she began to slip away from his sometimes overly tight grasp, but he knew that the only thing he could do for her was to let her enjoy herself, even if it wasn’t to his personal tastes. “I didn’t. Go away.” She was so calm; so calm that it was nearing the verge of being eerily rude. Brook had seen these moods so many times that by now, he had developed a routine to work through them, as with everything important in his life. All he could do was let her get on with it, and not do anything to antagonise her. “As you will” he stood to his feet and adjusted his black and white uniform so as to keep the one thing that held him apart from the sweat coated, attractive figure of Lacuna; class. He had half expected Lacuna to call him back; it had been this was so many times prior, but it was not to be today. He reached the exit and turned to watch Lacuna. She was being very strange today; something had definitely clicked. She had not been her usual self since Turmoil, and he doubted that she would again until she had finally got whatever issues she was grappling with straightened out in her head. Brook had left her, and she was alone again, back within the solace of her own personal cocoon. This was the way things were meant to be for her at the moment, as her mind fought to take control over the ravaging, pillaging hordes that were setting fire to her sanity and raping her thoughts. She was angry: angrier than she had been for a long time prior to today. She was so angry that it was hurting her to think, and thinking was what she had always done best. It was ruining her and she knew it, but she would not let it drop; there was no way that Lacuna was going to let this get the better of her, and so it had to be sorted out as soon as possible, probably marking Fusion would be a good idea. People were going to have to pay very soon, because Lacuna doesn’t like it when she is on the wrong end of a tongue lashing, especially that which involves her getting beaten up first. She had been insulted by that ditz Hilton and her sharp tongue, and she had been beaten by the chair of the Yankee-Mick Matt Jackson, and she would make them pay for it, because she was really, really not in the mood. She would show them what a ‘posh snob’ was capable of, and she would prove that little girls who need to re-sit school and learn the proper use of the language cannot succeed in this industry; her industry. Hilton was about to be on the wrong end of the wrong Lacuna.
