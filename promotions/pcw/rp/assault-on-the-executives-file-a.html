Date: not entered “Those who will not learn by words…will learn by actions. Those who cannot teach by words…will be taught by actions.” ---Savage Tranquility, Page Undetermined. These days grow tedious for me… All I do now is sit…and wait… Sit…and wait… This task of mines has become droll…much like the entertainment of watching Akiyama cut the lifespan of each mortal in PCW with each punch he delivers tends to get a but dull. Sure…violence is what makes the world go ‘round, but there’s no fun when the NVG gets to constantly maim the same faces over and over again. But much like those insipid humans in front of their televisions and in the arena…I sit…and watch… We sit…and watch… But unlike those beer swilling insects, I sit and watch because it is my job…they sit and watch because it is their entertainment to repetitively watch the GOD OF PCW© cause harm to the same men each time he steps out into that arena. Sometimes I wish I could break protocol and just change a few things with this world…make it more of a live show instead of eternal reruns. I am waiting…for what, you would ask? I am waiting for my exodus… I am waiting for my savior… He will come… …And with him…anarchy will crash down from the heavens and blood will spill from above. My time here is finished…I will be free…no more duties…no more obligations of destiny…no more servitude to fate…no more listening to what THEY say… My god will deliver me. I have prayed and dreamed for this day. Of course this realm you call earth will never be the same again if I am turned loose upon it alongside the Neo Void God. Prophecy has been made by me…it will come true indeed… So says the Divine Prophetess… The vapors floated from the mug into her nostrils. Rich…strong…robust…excellent qualities for any double mocha latte. It was about three in the morning, but the office was still buzzing with activity. No…there wasn’t an emergency in the office…these were normal working hours. This department worked round the clock, not because they wanted to, because they had to.Her skin was tanned…perfectly. Some would say that perfection is not achievable…but somehow, she managed to have her entire body the color of rich, tan goodness. There were only two colors that highlighted her skin tone. One of them was black…jet black…like her hair. Her hair was like a riverbed reflecting the sky, shimmering of darkness. It was smoothed back and tied in a bun at the base of her head…perfectly. Yet another exhibition of her perfection. The other color that highlighted her…was white. The white silk gripped at her skin like it was trying to caress her. It was pure white…from the spaghetti straps on her smooth shoulders all the way down to the hem of the dress. Even her heels were pure as snow… That face…she was heavenly…like an angel…but there was something more… Her facial features may have been beautiful beyond comparison, but her eyes were hollow…lifeless…like everything to her was flawed, distasteful, and beneath her. That didn’t stop her from smiling at the taste of that double mocha latte. Behind her, night sky was her backdrop as the shade was drawn from the bay window behind her. The moon shimmered down on the sea behind their office building, the silent crash of the tide against the rocks. Panning out from the window, the entire office could be seen as bright white. Maybe it was the lights magnifying the whiteness, but it was like a poster for sterilization. The desk was pure steel, and her leather chair was white leather. She was the White Queen… Her hand clacked across the computer keyboard while holding the mug in the other hand. She brought up a file upon her screen and stared into it, studying the information. One specific word came across the screen… “Akiyama” A knock came at her door. Her head lifted up towards it. “Enter.” She cooed in an elegant, debonair voice. The doors slid open, and a man stepped in. He was dressed in a black suit, with black sunglasses and a black silk shirt and a blood red tie. His reddish brown hair was dangling over his face, unkempt and ragged. But his face…it was purely professional. He stepped into the room and stood before her desk, his hands behind his back. “Have you heard anything from the Divine Prophetess yet?” his voice was demanding, yet seemed a bit cocky. “Nothing…and it’ll remain that way until one of us goes down there and sees what’s wrong.” She was curt, a hint of annoyance inside her voice. The man looked up a bit before looking into her face again. “I told Illusion to go check on her, but he’s too scared. He took one step inside and ran out in three seconds. The others haven’t been too eager to venture inside after that.” She sighed, taking another sip of the fresh brewed coffee. “Why haven’t you gone down to check on her?” He looked as if he were shocked at her proposal. “Me? I can’t go down there. I’m too…um…important! As the leader of this department, I must stay up here to rouse morale.” “Last time I checked, Reality…” she replied drolly. “…I was the one running things around here.” “That’s just because you’re talented in organization and other things that work towards running a tight, Executive ship.” He said, his mouth grinning. Rolling her eyes, she looked back at her computer. “At least you could show your appreciation by doing me this one task. Sometimes I don’t even know why I deal with you all.” “Thank you, Angelica.” He said that in the sweetest voice he could, only coming out with something that sounded like a gay child. “Anything on the Project Z situation?” “I’m analyzing the data as we speak.” She said. “This most certainly isn’t a case we can crack in a few days or so. This case could very well wreck the fabric of this world, not to mention the universe. The bad thing about this is that it doesn’t just deal with Subject Number 3-9.” Reality stepped forward, taking more interest in her words. “What do you mean? From what we had learned, Akiyama was the only one a part of Project Z!” Angelica turned her monitor around for him to read. “From my research, the subject was designed to have the ability to awaken dormant I-Genes within mortals, giving them slight superhuman and abnormal abilities. From what we have surveyed, Subject Number 3-9 is awakening these I-Genes and ordering them to completely take over the host’s DNA structure.” “Are you saying that he’s making people into Inphinites?” Reality seemed to have trouble believing this fact. Angelica nodded slowly. “Indeed. Our subject has made modifications to his initial programming from creation. Now he is obtaining abilities that totally surpassed his parameters. He is developing new ways of harnessing the Universal Negative Energy and using it as he sees fit.” “If he’s abusing the UNE, that could have drastic results.” She nodded in agreement. “Another thing, is that he could be able to obtain access to the UPE as well.” Reality exhaled deeply, letting the information sink in. “If he can get the positive and the negative charges…” “Chaos.” She said, completing his thoughts. “Our goal is clear.” He nodded, his face looking determined more than ever. “Subject Number 3-9…must be neutralized.” Suddenly, there was a rumble from below…large enough to send shivers up the building. Reality and Angelica looked around as the shivers began to subside. Then there was an electronic ring of a cell phone. Reality grabbed the device from out of his suit jacket and held it to his ears. “Reality…” he answered, looking concerned. “…Who’s here? NOW? Notify Illusion and Spice of the situation and rendezvous with Angelica and me on the thirty-ninth floor!” Putting his phone away, Angelica stood to her feet. “What’s going on?” Reality stood next to the door, where he looked back at her with a grim look. “Subject Number 3-9 is at our front door.” Reality and Angelica stood on a techno-style elevator, about ten feet long and ten feet wide. The walls and the panels were silver, with circuits etched into the metal. A light beamed down on them from the ceiling above. Outside, it sounded like they were moving at least 90 miles per hour. The speed suddenly slowed and stopped under 3 seconds, and the doors opened. Standing outside the elevator were three other people, two men and a woman. One of the men was dressed in a white shirt, with a silver vest, shoes and slacks. On his hands were black cut off gloves. The smaller man of the two was dressed in a yellow Zoot Suit with a matching fedora and shoes. The woman with them was wearing a red tight mini-dress with red heels. They all piled into the elevator as it took off again. “What’s the situation, Knight?” Reality asked sternly. The largest man out of the group, about seven feet tall, turned towards a keypad on the wall and began imputing numbers. “At 0300 hours, there was a report sent in to me speaking of a large man dressed in black with silver hair rampaging the gates of the outer perimeter. Knowing of the knave thou spoke of earlier, I immediately recognized him on the surveillance monitors.” he finished imputing the code, and a panel popped out with a steamy hiss. “It was most definitely the knave thou spoke of.” “Fine, we have Subject Number 3-9 on a warpath outside our outer perimeter.” Reality reached for the five briefcases on the panel and began handing them out to the people in the lift. “We are to neutralize him, not terminate. That means you, Spice.” The red haired vixen shrugged innocently as she took her case. “Is it always my fault if the target dies?” “Just when you start making out with him,” the yellow suited man replied with a grin. “Which is about 95.9 percent of the time!” “Hmmph!” she huffed, folding her arms across her chest. “Can I help it if I have an overactive libido?” “By the Celestials, I wish you could.” Angelica muttered. “Stun him, Executives, we need him alive.” The lift began to slow…approaching its stop on the ground floor. In unison, the corporate looking group all placed sunglasses upon their faces and stepped out into the war zone. “GET THOSE REINFORCEMENT WALLS UP OVER IN THE WEST SECTOR!!!” “WE NEED BACKUP OVER AT THE EAST WALL!! WHERE IS THE EMERGENCY SQUADRON!?!” “HE’S STILL ASSAULTING THE MAIN GATE!!! GET SOME MEN OVER THERE NOW!!!” Angelica ran her eyes over the outer perimeter wall. There were cracks webbing along the surface of the walls, made from indentations from some force outside of it. There were small fires burning along the top of the security wall and on the ground, where armored soldiers were hurrying to put them out. A crashing sound was booming at the front gate, pounding in a repetitive fashion like a drum. “We art lucky thine outer perimeter is reinforced by titanium, lest this knave have infiltrated out grounds.” Knight remarked. Reality shook his head as he took lead of the group. “That titanium isn’t holding for long though, ladies and gents.” He looked over towards a soldier running by with a fancy assault rifle in hand. “You; tell them to open the outer gates.” “Open them??” he squawked in fear. “But sir!! Opening the gates could result in—“ “I didn’t ask for a bloody explanation!! Just do what the hell I asked!!!” The soldier ran off towards the gate with a glum look on his face. Reality looked back at his cohorts and nodded. “All right, Executives,” he announced. “Time to go to work.” As the outer gates creaked open, the five Executives whipped their briefcases out in front of them. They all knelt down on the ground, setting their cases flat in front of them. Opening them, they all pulled out a various weapon of advanced variety. Knight pulled out something that looked like a tin can and a nightstick combined, but after pressing a button, it extended into a lance. Spice pulled out a whip covered in barbwire and cracked it at the ground. Illusion began whipping out a variety of high tech guns, strapping them inside his suit jacket and carrying two mean looking rifles in each hand. Reality pulled out a metal nightstick and pressed a button on its handle, charging volts of electricity through it. Then he slipped a smaller taser inside his suit pocket. Angelica closed her case, wearing a pair of metal spiked claws on her hands. Illusion took the point, aiming his guns at the gate. “I’ll send off a beginner blast on him. Then the rest of you dash in and neutralize him.” The nozzles of his rifles began glowing with a white light. The hum of the machines grew louder and louder as the energy charged up within his weapons. The guards dove out of the way as a small smirk spread out on the yellow suited man’s face. His knees bent down as his fingers pulled the triggers. A wide wave of laser fire shot out from his rifles with a high-pitched scream, blazing with force directly the target outside the gates. The sky went white as the explosion hit on mark. With eyes shielded, the other Executives stormed in towards their target to neutralize him. …But there was no target… When the smoke was cleared out, and the vision was clear again, all that was left was a large circle pattern cut in the grass, exposing the bare ground. Illusion ran up with his rifles draped over his shoulders, an apologetic look on his face. “D@mn…” he muttered in awe. “I was set on Neutralize.” Spice glared over in his direction. “You sure it wasn’t Vaporize?” Before Illusion could snap back with a response, a ring came from Knight’s coat. He reached in and held the cell phone to his ears. “Knight…” soon, a surprised look came over his face. “He’s in Sub-basement Omega??? Do whatever thy can to halt his progress!! We shall be there at once!!!” Angelica’s face grew a bit displeased at the turn of events. Looking to Illusion, she brushed past him to head for the building again. “Did you even hit him, Illusion?” A hurt look came over his face as he ran behind the others quickly. “HEY! I’M NO SNIPER!! I’M JUST WORKING WITH THE TOOLS GIVEN TO ME!!!” ===To Be Continued===
