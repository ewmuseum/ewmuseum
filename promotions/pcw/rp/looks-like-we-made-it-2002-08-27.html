Looks Like We Made It

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/08/02 “What do you mean you can’t open it? IT’S A DOOR YOU STUPID PEON, HOW HARD CAN IT BE?!” Our shot opens up outside the CEO’s Office in PCW’s New Jersey headquarters where the couple of Xavier Kannon and his wife Eleanor Kannon-Hall stand, waiting very impatiently while a hapless PCW employee tries all the keys on his chain to try and open the door. “I’ve never had to open this door before Mr Kannon, sir... I don’t know what key to use.” The new CEO and owner of PCW sighs and shakes his head at the flustered male employee in his seemingly mid-50’s decked out in Black trousers and a Black PCW logo emblazoned polo shirt. The Scientologist raises his eyebrows and directs an insultingly toned piece of advice to the staffer. Xavier : “Did you try the one with CEO’S OFFICE on by any chance?” The bright Green irises lock onto the employee’s frustrated face as he fans through the keys in a token gesture to show none are labelled for the office. Eleanor : “By...... Any...... Chance?” With the 1st couple of PCW casting their authoritative shadows over him, the employee continues about his task of un-locking the office that Xavier will now call home after his victory on Sunday. A look of mild disdain finds it’s way, as usual, onto the Gold Patron Meritorious’ Lion-esque face, now with a thin beard covering the lower portion of his face that perfectly accompanies his mane of Strawberry Blond hair that he sports. Xavier : “By Hubbard, man... You are dressed like a Peon of the most idiotic order.” Ignoring the cruel words of his boss the man continues to try key after key after key in the lock... All of them either not fitting or not turning the lock over. Each failed attempt bringing a sigh from the CEO and a shake of the head from Ellie~! Xavier : “What possessed you to come to work in a POLO SHIRT, man.... You look like a washed up middle aged Golfer.” The CEO checks his watch in a gesture who’s sole purpose is obviously to frustrate the worker, who finally replies, not looking up from the lock as he still fiddles with it on his knees. “They’re the Uniform, Sir... We were told to always wear the brand logo.” This news brings a look of shock and bemusement from Kannon who looks to Ellie~!, only to get a shrug in return. Xavier : “What weak minded, finished and fired, left-over remnant of the past would have possibly passed such a Peonic rule as that?” The question is as rhetorical as you can get, Kannon taking another pop at Tyler’s tenure as CEO before being dethroned by the Scientologist just 2 days ago. Eleanor : “Um.. Wouldn’t that, like, be Tyler Nelson!” As always with Ellie~!, it’s hard to tell whether she is being sarcastic or not. Not minding that, Kannon continues to observe the employee going about his work. Xavier : “When you finish the child-like, suitable for children aged 4 upwards, put the right shape in the hole task... Send out a memo telling people to stop wearing those Hubbard-awful Polo Shirts.” Another key is tried. Another key is discarded. “I’m not in the Administrative side of thi-” Before the man even finished explaining to the new CEO that he isn’t in a position to send the memo he is cut off. The few words he did manage to get out causing a sneer to be aimed in his direction from Kannon’s wife. Xavier : “I SAID... When you are done with that, send out a memo saying to stop wearing those Polo Shirts.... That clear?” The man bows his head, realising he will have this to put up with until he quits.... Enough to probably make most men suicidal, but at least Tyler’s un-humanitarian actions of the past have hardened the staff a little. “Yes, sir.” The Green eyes of the Hubbard follower focus on the key ring, now nearly 3/4 of them have been tried to no result and Ellie~!’s fidgeting has become most distracting. Eleanor : “How long have we, like, been here? It feels like 75384 years.” Though not quite to her exaggerated lengths, Kannon also tires of the wait. Xavier : “4 minutes my little Poon Puppy.” She lets out a loud, annoying sigh and looks up at the ceiling as Kannon tires of waiting for the key to be found. Xavier : “Stand aside Peon, unless you want my most Scientological of feet impacting on your skull.” The man in the Polo Shirt heaves himself up just as Kannon walks into position and levels an inch perfect kick to the door, sending it flying open with damage to the lock and door frame. Xavier : “Get me a new door.... And send that memo.” The couple walk into the CEO’s office as the poor employee trundles down the hall, wondering what substances he will have to abuse to get him through his job. Xavier : “Urgh.... This whole room SMELLS of him.” Now standing inside the huge office that now belongs to him, Kannon’s face is screwed up at the left-over aura. Eleanor : “Who? That fat man in the Polo Shirt?” The Gold Patron Meritorious walks across the $50,000 rug and around the $20,000 desk who’s paper-weight alone costs well over $500, to the windows where he pushes one open. An action that causes him to cough and splutter. Xavier : “Eugh....” His face screwed up and eyes watering, the CEO slams the window shut so hard the blinds shake. Eleanor : “Ewwww... That smells like.. Like...” Both of their faces clearly show disgust as the Scientologist hunts around for some air freshener... Not looking too happy at all. Xavier : “IT SMELLS LIKE FUCKING NEW JERSEY!!!” The body language of Xavier makes it appear that he is trying to force the smell from his nostrils using all the muscles in his face. The other side of the desk, Ellie~! ponders the situation over. Eleanor : “Wow, and we’re like, in Jersey too!” After aiming a brief WHAT glance at his wife that is met with her blank Bambi look, Kannon bellows out through the broken door to anyone who can hear him. Xavier : “SOMEONE WHO CAN UNDERSTAND ENGLISH AND ISN’T DRESSED LIKE A MORON GET IN HERE!!!” He starts to pace around the room behind his desk as Ellie~! looks confused, asking of herself. Eleanor : “Does he mean me?” The Scientologist’s call is answered as a young woman pokes her head through the door’s remains. Her Blonde hair in a neat bob and Black rimmed stylish glasses adorning her face... She is met with an icy glare by Eleanor. “Yes, Mr Kannon...” Gesturing with his hands to the whole room, Xavier levels his demand at her. Xavier : “I want air from Hubbardville in here... I don’t care how you do it, but I want MY air, in MY office!” This bizarre request leaves the young PCW employee wondering what to say. “How do you mean... Bottled air?” The temperamental CEO offers no additional information to help her out as he strokes his beard, checking out the so-far unexplored areas of the office like a Lion exploring the savannah. This provides the chance for Ellie~! to fend her off. Eleanor : “He SAID... He wants his air!” Her wide eyes glare patronisingly at the employee who just bows her had in defeat... Seemingly the pattern of the day. “Yes, Mr Kannon. I’ll arrange it.” A smile has now come to Kannon’s face as he drops back down into the comfortable Black leather chair and gestures for Ellie~! to come to him. Xavier : “Well, my little Shag Squirrel...” She sits on his lap, both surveying the CEO’s office in front of a view over Atlantic City. Xavier : “.... Looks like we made it.” The pair smirk as those simple words bring images rushing into their mind. All the vile acts they have perpetrated to get to where they are, all the people’s careers they have stepped over and all the lives they have ruined. Eleanor : “We........ Made........ It!”
