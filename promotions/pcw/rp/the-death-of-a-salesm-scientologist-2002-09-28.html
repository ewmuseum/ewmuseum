The Death Of A Salesm-...Scientologist?

28 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/09/02 “Why all the talk about my will, my little Shag Squirrel?” Our shot opens up in the kitchen of Xavier Kannon’s Hubbardville residence where the PCW Champion it rooting through the cupboards looking like he’s just got out of the shower. His long mane of Strawberry Blonde hair is damp, taking on a darker colour while he only wears a towel tied around his waist. “You know, for if Clyde, like, SO kills you or something...” The mention of the Demon’s name makes the PCW CEO scowl quietly and his cupboard fumbling takes on a more aggressive manner. Behind him we then see Ellie~! sat at the table, leaning forward on her elbows as she presses the point. Eleanor : “What if, like, he sets you on fire and you go all...” With her little hands the prep mimics flames dancing all around her, swatting them and silently yelling. Eleanor : “.... Crispy and dead!” He lets out a sigh and shakes his head while his preppy wife plays about with the Toaster Pastry in front of her, dressed only in a Pink robe, her light Brown hair with Blonde highlights cascading untamed down her back. Eleanor : “Because you can’t, like, marry me and give me loads of stuff and then die... Because then I won’t be able to keep getting... Um...” She hunts about for the best word, scratching her head while her pale Blue eyes look up to the ceiling. Eleanor : “.... Er....” Looking to her in a bemused manner, Xavier opens his bright Green eyes wide and tilts his head. Eleanor : “Like... Stuff!” With a box of Muesli in his hand, Xavier walks over to the breakfast table and drops down into one of the seats, watching Ellie~! take little nipples at her still hot breakfast. Xavier: “Trust me, my little Fuck Ferret... My great and noble life will not be ended by some Circus Freak.... It would take a God to kill someone as highly evolved as me.” Ellie~! raises her head and “Ahhhh”s as if she understands while Kannon pours some milk onto his serial, hoping his wife is now put at ease. Eleanor : “So Akiyama’s going to kill you then...” She thinks nothing of it as Xavier coughs out his mouthful of Muesli and stares right at his oblivious wife, lost in her own world of after death finance. Eleanor : “I guessed he might do that after you SOhit him with that Chair, like, seventy five million thousand gazillion times...” The CEO just shakes his head as he wipes him mouth with a napkin. Eleanor : “Well, whoever kills you, a will would kinda rule.... Especially if I got everything...” Her eyes then acknowledge him, looking right at his as her voice adopts a more serious tone... Well, about as serious as she can manage. Eleanor : “I am, like, getting it all... Right?” He hesitates in answering. Eleanor : “RIGHT?“ Finishing his mouth of Muesli, Kannon answers in an appeasing manner, not wanting to anger his wife... Or rather, get her in a sulky fit. Xavier : “Well, SOME would naturally go to the Church of Scientology.” The prep looks confused as she finally takes something that could be considered a normal sized bite from the Toaster pastry. Eleanor : “What’s that?” The PCW Champion has to take a moment to weigh up if she is actually being serious, with her naturally sarcastic tone he often struggles to understand some of her meanings... He then looks around, seeing if their is anything else she is referring to before sighing as he realises she did inDEED just ask what Scientology was. Xavier : “Er... That Religion I follow, my little Poon Puppy.” Her memory is triggered and she remembers it, before leaning across the table to Xavier... Only her husband seeing the view presented by Ellie~! leaning before him with a loosely tied robe. Eleanor : “Does Scientology do....” She wraps her arms around him and whispers something into his ear that sends his eyes widening and his jaw dropping before Kannon hurriedly tries to regain his composure. Xavier : “If they did.... That would create much the scandal...” Sitting back down the prep pulls her robe tightly around her and crosses her arms, looking miffed. Eleanor : “Than they, like, SO don’t deserve any of your money after Clyde burns you to death of drops a big metal thing on you or if Akiyama throws you into the big dark whole things.... And stuff!” Finally annoyed with the talk of his painful, brutal and apparently impending demise, Kannon pushes his bowl away from him and looks right at his wife, the Scientologist’s body gesturing sternly. Xavier : “Look, I am NOT going to DIE! Not from CLYDE, not from AKIYAMA... Not from ANYTHING!!!” Normally something as resounding as that would close the conversation, but then again, Xavier married Ellie~! who looks back curiously again. Eleanor : “Not even from a Plane Crash?” With her Toaster Pastry she simulates a plane going down complete with the BOOM and hand motioned explosion... The PCW Champion looks for a second at the crumpled breakfast before snapping out of it. Xavier : “LOOK! I AM NOT GOING TO DIE!!!!“ Ellie~! leans back in her chair to emphasise the volume and directness of her husband’s outburst, speaking in a softer, sympathetic tone. Eleanor : “Wow, you’re like SO taking dying badly!” Tired of hearing about him dying, Xavier gets up from the table and dumps his bowl in the sink, looking back to Ellie~! more direct than ever, speaking in his clearest, most concise voice. Xavier : “I... Am NOT.... Going..... To DIE!“ Still his words don’t get through to her as she starts to pick little bits off the crushed Pastry that was used to simulate the crash. Eleanor : “I think I, like, read about some guy saying that before lightning struck him and he.. Well, died, I think!” Growing more stressed by the second, he holds both his palms to Ellie~!, gesturing that he’s had enough before he heads to the kitchen door. Xavier : “I’m not going to die.... I don’t need a wi-” On his way out, his form suddenly lunges forward.... *THUD* .... And his head smacks onto one of the kitchen units as his wife keeps picking at her breakfast. Eleanor : “You kinda, like, fell over there...” Down on the floor, Kannon rolls over onto his back and holds his bleeding forehead before looking down to his right foot where Ellie~!’s bag’s handle is wrapped round his foot. Eleanor : “Wow, you’re REALLY in a hurry to do this dying stuff, aren’t you!” The Scientologist groans as he tries to get to his feet, Ellie~! looking back to her Toaster Pastry as the shot fades out to Black. “I’ll call the Lawyer before you stick your head in the oven or something...”
