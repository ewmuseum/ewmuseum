Family Matters pt1- A Shocking Revelation

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th March After more than a week in hiding from Flaymin Aymin, The Mexican Devil is ready to come out just a little bit more. He is aware that if Flaymin catches up to him he will be in some serious danger but he is prepared to take that risk. The only lingering question regarding that is why? After finally laying to rest the past in releasing Pedro's ashes that he had kept ahold of for near nine years, Diablo had come to the realisation that he does not really know who his family is, he does not really know where he came from and that he doesn't really know who he is because of this. It's a painstaking thought to realise you do not have a family anymore and that you are lost in the ocean of chaos that is life, all alone. So, what is El Diablo's new journey? Simple, to discover his heritage, to discover where he is from, to discover who he is and finally to discover what family he has. El Diablo is inside a long winding library, looking up and down a shelf for what seems to be a specific book. The library is a very large one and must hold host to near tens of thousands of books- wow! That is very thorough. The library is well decorated too, with a lovely blue carpet across the floor, white gloss painted walls and marble stairways and statues, which pay homage to how old this library really is. Even the bookcases are made out of an old hand carved wood, with gargoyles carved in at the start and end of every bookcase, it is quite a wonder to see, a very beautiful sight. The shelf that Diablo is examining very closely is one under the label of 'Family'. A strange label to have, but then again in a library, which hosts so many books, there will be some new and different sections and this just happens to be one of them. After a few moments of searching through the entire contents of the shelf, he finally pulls out the very book he had been looking for. As he pulls it out, to separate it from the rest of the books a little glow can be seen, like it is the holy grail of all books he has been looking for. With a gleam in his eye and a smile as wide as a double decker bus, El Diablo sits down at the nearest table. Even the tables at this library are hand carved and have beautifully zigzag patterns in them as well as the gargoyle features at the ends of every table. Only the chairs aren't hand carved, and that is especially evident on El Diablo's chair as the price tag is still attached to it t one of the legs. The Mexican Devil places his new found holy grail of a book down, the title of which reads 'Mexican Family Trees' and below it is a cute little drawing of a tree which has branches holding young Mexican children, all the way up to old Mexican grandparents. It is a very apt depiction. With a sigh and a quick left hand through his bright red hair, Diablo opens the book up at the index and immediately goes to the letter 'D' to start to find any information on the Diaz families. As there are so many Diaz families this could be quite a time consuming task, but one El Diablo feels he must take to find out whom he is. TWO HOURS ELAPSE The Mexican Devil is still looking through the book and with a frown on his face. It seems the day has been a bust. But El Diablo's pessimism soon vanishes as he sees a beautiful Mexican woman approaching him. She has long black hair that runs just past her shoulders, a beautiful olive complexion and by her tight clothing a great physique. She stands at what must be about 5'5' and at a weight of around 102lbs. She is a beautiful sight to see, and better yet she is heading towards El Diablo. Diablo starts to look nervous for a second and by looking down at his left hand and seeing his wedding ring, he panics and quickly removes it, placing it in his black denim jacket pocket. The woman then stops right in front of him. El Diablo looks at her from head to toe, from her black high heeled shoes, her tight blue denim jeans and her tight white T-shirt to her ebony locks of hair that curl just slightly at the bottom. 'Excuse me, I think I might be of assistance. I'm...,' before this woman finishes her sentence off El Diablo gets to his feet and offers her to sit down. The Mexican woman takes the seat, and El Diablo starts to lay on the charm, with the impression that this lady is just another wrestling groupie. 'Yes I know,' Diablo comments without letting her finish, 'And you know who I am,' The woman smiles, 'Well actually, you're my...' Again The Mexican Devil interrupts her, as he goes to sit down just opposite this young woman, 'Hero. I know,' he assumes arrogantly, 'I'm Raul and you are?' he asks the beautiful woman who can only be in her mid twenties. 'Patty,' she replies. Diablo smirks innocently, although we all know he is a devil in disguise. 'Patty? That's a beautiful name,' he comments in passing, 'Patty, you said you could be of assistance? Do you work here?' Diablo asks, feigning an interest in her life. Patty shakes her head. 'No, but I can help you with what you're trying to look for,' she replies in a beautiful Mexican accent. Diablo looks a little confused, but goes on with the game while trying to look at Patty's cleavage. 'Just slide me that book over,' Patty requests. El D complies and slides her the book over. She leans forward to get a good look at the book, leaving Diablo an ample amount of time to stare at her cleavage. She is very well endowed in the chest area. 'Ah a Diaz, just as I suspected,' Patty remarks while turning a few pages. El Diablo really does look bemused now, surprised by the fact she seemed to know that he was a Diaz before even looking in the book. He scratches his chin in thought, wondering how well educated this woman really is. Patty turns a few pages and then stops on a certain page, she smiles a beautiful smile and slides the book back over to El Diablo. 'I believe that is the page you were looking for,' Patty states to him. Diablo examines the page, and indeed it is the right page. All the way from Great-Grandfather Diaz to Father Diaz to his generation. 'How'd you know?' he asks the beautiful woman, clueless. She smiles again. 'Take a closer look,' Patty prompts Diablo. He takes his eyes off of Patty and back to the book, and on further examination he finds that along the same line as himself and 'Pedro' is another name, 'Patty'. Diablo looks up at Patty again and then down at the book once again. Suddenly The Mexican Devil leaps up in surprise and has a very disgusted and repulsed look on his face. 'EWWWWWWWWW! I'VE JUST BEEN HITTING ON MY SISTER,' he exclaims loudly. Loud enough to the get the attention of the librarian. 'SHHHHH!'
