Misogyny Consequence (Part I)

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/21/03 (OOC: A continuation of my last PWA roleplay and just some fill-in information on how he gets in to the PCW, etc.. A brief piece.) The irruption of night darkness didn't come to much of a surprise as Stone's 'days' began to deteriorate. The horizontal and vertical tandem of police vehicles indicated the presence of the Bainbridge Precinct. Within the confines of the facility sat Stone, brushing his hair vigorously. Before him stood a man by the name of Gentry Clark, sporting an exquisite, navy colored suit, with his hair slickly pushed back. He was Stone's public appointed lawyer and profusely piped over his mobile phone. Finally, a light smile came across Gentry's visage, he placed his pair of elbows on the ivory steel table and began.. 'Here's the deal.. Your so called 'homicides' have no clear evidence linking it to you. To make it much more clear, they cannot find the bodies of these women reported. Usually they would take you in to trial, and all that bull shit but i've got you out of this WHOLE ordeal.' Stone confusingly arched his eyebrows, and drew his head back. 'What kind of lawyer are you?' Stone interrogated. 'I am the lawyer/attorney helping you. That's all..' he replied as Stone approvingly nodded and continued to sit quietly, while Gentry placed his palms on his waist and added a haste tap with his shoe. 'Well..? What are you waiting for?' he questioned, Stone brought his attention back over to his 'attorney', spreading his hands in puzzlement. 'What do you mean 'What am I waiting for?'?' he returned 'You can leave.' he claimed while a somnolent Stone jumped, not literally at least. 'I CAN?' he inquired just rendering a languid nod from his lawyer, pointing towards the closed door. Stone stood up, unpremeditatedly shaking his lawyer's hand, turning the knob to the exit door only to be brought back by his lawyer, with a furled missive. 'One more thing.. Here's some information on where you'll be going from here.' he stated. Stone snatched it away from his grip and left. He strolled past a horde of security officers, office doors, and empty imprisonment's, all leading to the main lobby where the bustle of shackled criminals, and rushing lutanents actively went on letting the Misogynist, Stone Burns lead his way out through the exit, and down a diminutive row of concrete stairs. He freely yet evilly grinned, inhaling the hair joyously, while pulling the missive given to him by his lawyer in front of him and flattening it for visible viewing. A line of words accompanied the pale paper as the italicized letters, aligned in the center read.. 'PCW' And beneath it read a long description of locations, only producing a questioning grimace across his face and ended.. 'PCW?'
