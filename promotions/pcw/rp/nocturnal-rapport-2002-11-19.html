Nocturnal Rapport

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/02 A vast plain stretched forth, young and beautiful. Flowers danced in all directions, singing as a tall man wandered through. A gentle breeze wafted through the clear air, bringing about the smell of a distant waterfall, pleasant and mild. In the distance a forest rested, clear and full of life. And a quick glance around revealed bunnies were hopping everywhere. Yes, bunnies. Clyde blinked. 'You must be kidding me,' he muttered, reaching out and snapping his claw-like fingers around a fluffy white creature's neck. It squirmed and made some sort of bunny noise; and Clyde dropped it, wringing his hand and scowling. 'Or not.' He looked around. Everything was green, blue, pink, or some other disgusting pastel color. There were fluffy white clouds in the sky that looked strangely like poofy armchairs, and there were little children running around in what appeared to be Easter clothes singing childish songs. Bunnies were hopping, birds were singing, bees were buzzing, and Clyde was in the middle of it with a horrified look on his face. He walked forward, crushing the perfect posies and daisies on purpose with his black leather boots. He looked behind him with a smirk, only to find that the flowers had sprung up again. 'Last time I get high before bedtime,' he muttered, 'Now I'm hallucinating.' He looked around himself once more. Bunnies were now pairing off and running off to separate meadows. He noticed that the children, who were originally in a circle, were also pairing off. Except for one. Clyde blinked again, squinted, and then went into semi-shock as a white blur ran and crashed right into him. 'Hiya!' 'Who in hell are you?' Clyde asked, shoving the little girl in the white, ruffly dress off of him. 'You don't recognize me?' she sounded hurt, 'after all I've done to you?' She sniffled. '…Oh dear god.' 'Hihi Clyde!' 'I'm higher than I thought.' 'Whaddya mean?' Bethany asked, her big amber eyes glistening over with tears of neglect. '…Why are you dressed in white?' 'I love white! It's my favorite color!' Clyde felt woozy and sat down hard on the soft, plush grass. Bethany plopped right in his lap and he pushed her right back out. 'You're mean! But that's okay, I love you anyways.' She snuggled up to him. For her efforts, the little girl received a shove. However this did not deter her in the slightest, she merely charged forward and tackled the demon to the green grass with a mighty hug. “Begone!” “Hehe!” With a giggle, Bethany straightened out her dress and wiped it clear of dirt. “What is the meaning of this?”, snarled the burned man. “You’re in heaven, tra la la!” From off in the distance the platoon of small children ran towards the distraught monster. Forming a circle and joining hands, they pranced and danced around his form. “Heaven....bah! No heaven.....God has no.....”, he sputtered and coughed. However the dancing kiddies would not be denied. “Tra la la, shish boom ba!” A sound suddenly ripped apart the shenanigans....from far away, Clyde could hear a siren. 'Maybe it's an ambulance,' he thought, 'coming to save my poor soul from an 8 year old in a white dress and these wretched little....' The sounds were getting louder, and Bethany had suddenly gotten much more mushy. She had climbed back into his lap and was now tracing circles on his shirt, humming a sappy love song happily. Clyde for his part was feeling funny....warm all over. Fuzzy. And everything was growing blurry. One minute Bethany was clear, and then she faded out for a second. He noticed that every time she came into focus, she was getting closer…and her little red mouth was getting rounder…the siren was getting louder and louder, she was getting closer and closer…until.. SMACK 'Wake up you dumbass!' Something was hitting him very hard on the head. 'Urrgh, what?' Clyde blinked and started at a half naked Bethany staring at him, the bottom of her hair singed and looking furious. She hit him again across the head with the frying pan she held in her hand, and then she bit his hand like she was a rabid dog. 'That's the last time I sleep next to you!' She shrieked, and Clyde noticed that his boxers were practically burned off, as well as his covers. 'What happened?' he asked dumbly, as though he didn't know his own self. 'Guess what happened!' The little demon-girl screamed, 'just guess!' 'You'll wake the whole hotel up.' 'You already did!' Bethany didn't seem to want to shut up, 'what with your hormones and all!' Finally, Clyde got it. He looked at Bethany, her singed hair, and then down at his singed boxers. '…Oops.' The little girl arose from the bed, storming around the corner. The burned man simply looked at the scorched ruins around him, smiling a bit. 'Heh.' Finally after a long while Bethany returned, dragging a fire extinguisher behind her. The demon merely looked on quizzically. 'The fire's died down dear, no need for....' 'Oh it's not for the fire,' the child snarled as she hefted the canister over her head with a mighty effort and hopped straight up onto Clyde's stomach. 'Ah, crap.' Resignation entered the burned man's voice, he sighed as the steel object speeded towards his skull, causing him to crash into the eternal darkness once more......
