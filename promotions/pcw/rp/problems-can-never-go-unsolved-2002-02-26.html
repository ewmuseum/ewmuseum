Problems can never go unsolved

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/2/02 Through the ages, many greats have come, and with one denial, they fall, with one failure, they fall, with one mistake, they fall. No one can foresee the future, tell who will make it in this world, but we are not to judge, it is up to the higher being… God. We are over shadowed by his power, though one can make it, we must try, give it our all, we must get back up and fight until the end, nothing should bring us down. For when you wake up, old and withered you will sit down, and realize… you have wasted your life, are you happy? You must ask yourself that, for life is short… no matter how many times you fail; there is always time to change. A match made in heaven for one an everyday fight for the other. The Mexican Devil, versus the Kid. Based on view, you would believe it to be the Devil, who can judge? For no one can base upon names or attributes alone, one must watch and realize. Underdogs are not always weaker. Anton McGonnell had perspiration falling from his forehead like a waterfall before Friday Fusion, there was so much at stake, so much he could grasp. He went into the match, with the crowd on his side, the cheered for him, the adrenalin was immaculate, it was intoxicating, and he loved every second of it. A figure then appeared from the crowd and hit Anton, he took off his covering attire, and revealed himself as the Mexican Devil… El Diablo. Diablo attacked Anton kicked him, anything that could be done. Though Anton fought back, with everything inside him, heart and adrenaline brought him through. He had the power to win it all. It was everything he could think about, winning and going on to March Madness with a spot in the World Title match, but he was not to be so lucky… as usual. The reached Tag Team Champion… Akiyama and The Pharaoh, known combined as… The Divinity, members of the stable International Incident, they attacked el Diablo due to a silly row, and Anton was the one to pay, he lost by disqualification, and lost any hope he had of become PCW World Champion… For now. The time has come, for so, long, Anton has been rejecting it, but he must confront it. Ever since he left NTICW, and joined PCW, he hasn’t had time to take it all in, yet so much had come, and nothing had left. Could that all change… Anton walked up the long steps, he panted as he walked. He made his way up to steps leading to the PTC Headquarters main building. The time was 9:30 am. He was usually on of the first PCW Superstars in at Mornings, but today he was quite late as he had Martial Arts tuition to do with his knew trainer… Tsunami Chow-Lee. He was, in Anton’s opinion, the greatest Martial Arts teacher to ever grace to purified soil of God’s earth. The outside of the PTC Headquarters Main Building looked extremely modernized. It was black and had a PTC Banner on it. It looked almost metallic as the sun shone down on it. The day with good, there was few clouds floating in the sky and the sun was shining brightly. Anton finally reached the top of the steps, the entrance doors spanned over about 20 feet horizontally which meant there was plenty of room of different people to come in and out at one time. Anton walked through these doors and entered an extremely large Reception room. There were many people here, about two hundred or more, most were wearing business suits and few wrestlers could be seen, though this was not the place where wrestlers usually ‘hung out’. These people were mostly agents, lawyer, endorsers etc. There was a couple of large reception area where about fifteen men and women in all were serving the needs of the people around the room. Anton quickly made his way through this area; he went to the other side of the room where the elevators were situated. There were about five elevators in all, and there were many different floors, Anton pressed the button for ‘PCW Floor 1’. Once the elevator arrived. The insides of these elevators were quite luxurious, the red walls were covered with different patterns and pictures, and there was soft music playing in the background. There were only two other men that entered the elevator with Anton. One was wearing a plain black jacket with a black tie and white shirt, and had a bald head besides from around the sides were there was a bit of brown hair. The other man was wearing a blue suit and was carrying a brown suitcase; he had light brown hair and looked about 55 year of age. Anton did not speak the whole way up towards his floor, as he didn’t have much to talk about with men in suits. Eventually Anton got out, he was the only one getting off at this particular stops, which meant the men were probably going to endorse or ‘negotiate ’with some of the smaller feds in PTC, as this was the main floor for PCW negotiation. Anton looked around briefly, the room was extremely modernized and looked fantastic, there were luxurious carpet floors and fascinating banners etc. all around the place. This was the floors main reception area. Anton made his way to the left where he entered a corridor, it was quite wide and there were a lot of doors on each side. PCW Star names such as ‘Boda, Snow and The Pharaoh’ were seen by Anton’s transfixed eyes as he made his way passed them. He eventually reached a light brown door, which said the words ‘Anton The Kid’ on it. He took a key from his left pocket and put it in the door, he give it a turn and the door sprang open. Anton, who was wearing a blue ‘Official PCW’ t-shirt and a pair of black, baggy, Nike Bottoms, looked around the room, it looked as it always did, very, very cool. He loved the way everything around the building was so rich, modernized and luxurious. It made him feel special. He threw his Nike Kit Bag, which he had on his right shoulder, onto a red seat, which sat near the door. He then took a seat and stretched his neck. Tomorrow would be Tuesday, the day of PCW Tuesday Turmoil, Anton had a match scheduled against the man who had defeated him on Friday to claim a place in the March Madness Battle Royal where the PCW World Title was at stake. Anton was extremely annoyed about this, but hoped it would be an opportunity for him to get a good match at the Pay-Per-View such as a Rising Star tie match, that may have been pushing his luck a bit, but we can all dream. He was surprised the final card wasn’t up at this stage as it was less than a week away. Anton also had the Global Title Tournament to worry about, though that was now his last priority because he thought PCW was much more important, and with the Pay-Per-View coming up, he would much prefer to concentrate on it. Anton then thought for a minute, what if the Pay-Per-View Card was up? He decided the go out to the notice board area to check this. So he got up and walked towards the door, opened it and walked down the corridor, he then went up to the notice board area, and there is was, the card for March Madness 2002, it was the finalized card, and Anton hoped he would be on it. He scrolled down with his finger, and then his eyes brightened. For he had indeed a match at the Pay-Per View.Table Match#1 Contender to the Cruiserweight TitleGanker vs. Anton the Kid vs. Red Rock vs. Kramer Wild WingHe was ecstatic, finally he was moving up to the Cruiserweight division, and if he could win and March Madness he would get his dream of a PCW Cruiserweight title shot. Though his opponents would be no pushovers.First was Ganker, he was a high-flying 16-year old, only two years older than Anton, he would certainly be a big challenge, despite being around the same size and weight as Anton. Then came Red Rock, the former PCW Rising Star Champion, he was a real talent and would surely be the PCW Cruiserweight Champion at some point, but Anton didn’t intend on letting it come anytime soon. And finally came Kramer Wild Wing. He was just back in PCW, after being out for quite a long time. He was a 33 year old, and very experienced, all-round competitor who was a former PCW Cruiserweight and Television Champion. He would be an extremely tough opponent.Despite all this, Anton would train his best and could only pray for victory. He had a lot to contemplate and he wanted to get it all cleared up. He had a lot to contemplate and he wanted to get it all cleared up. Though he decided he would go into his locker room, instead of standing around the notice boards all day.He walked back towards his locker room, and closed the door after him. He sat down on his red couch and out his head in his hands.For so long now, he had been rejecting his problems, before he came to PCW, his life was a complete mess, but he had decided at that point to leave all these things to the side, while he tried to build up a solid career for himself. Though, in the short month he had been back in PCW, after hid accident, he had achieved quite a lot, wins over Juan Borro and Vampir Nosferatu. Though with that came loses against Bobby Stormbringer and El Diablo. Though he knew he could make it in PCW, he currently had an even record, which was good for a rookie such as him.Though now, it was time to confront his problems, he had left so many things behind when NTICW closed, such as; the nightmares he once had, which went unsolved and also, the strange people he kept meeting, as well as who actually tripped him when he had his accident that put him out of PCW competition for a month. He would have to investigate it all, for they were all solemn matters, and Anton had a feeling they were all linked in some way. It would surely be a tough task, Drake Maxwell had done a lot to help him back in the day, but now he was so busy as he was a big PCW Superstar, Anton didn’t know if he would have time to help Anton. It was up to Anton himself now; he would find it all out, what it meant. Despite this, a lot of concentration still had to be directed at his match against El Diablo on Turmoil, his GTT match against WWA superstar Violence Jack on Saturday, and his PPV match against Red Rock, Ganker and Kramer Wild Wing on Sunday at March Madness. He had a busy schedule but would try his hardest all-round. It was after all his life, wrestling that is. And no matter what his personal problems were, he still had a career to live. He is suddenly awoken from his thoughts by a ringing sound; he realizes it is his cell phone. He takes a small black phone from his right hand trouser pocket and presses a button.“Hello?” Anton says.“I know you want answers, but will you find them?” Comes, what seems like a disguised voice from the other line.“Who is this?” Anton asks in a scared but demanding tone.The only answer he receives is the tone of the caller hanging up.Anton’s knees shake, thousands of thoughts enter his head, what was that voice? What did he mean? How did he know what Anton was thinking? These were the sort of questions the entered Anton’s mind, and the fact that he didn’t know them, scared him more than anything.“What shall come next, oh cruel superior being?” Anton insightfully said to himself.That was a good question, what would come next for him, so much had happened, he didn’t know if he could take anymore. Though he would deal with it if he must, for the crimson blood burned brightly inside him, he would stick by himself, as a famous man once said there is only… One Love, One Life, but Anton is Forever!
