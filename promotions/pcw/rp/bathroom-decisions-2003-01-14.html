Bathroom Decisions

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-14-03 The door slammed shut. Silence dawned on the room. The room was filling with heat. I had to escape before I did something I would regret. I leaned against the door and pulls out my wallet. I removed an old, faded picture from it. There is a slit down the picture where it appears to have been stabbed. I looked at the picture and slowly fell to my seat on the ground. I don't know why I ran into the bathroom, it just seemed like the only place I could escape. 'Mom...is it really you?' I asked. I let out a sigh. I couldn't know. I don't even know why I kept this picture. I just found it in the apartment building and kept it. But I just had this feeling. I can't explain it. It's similar to when I met Tessa Windsor. Yet it's different. I couldn't explain it so I just stared at the picture. 'Mom, where are you? I need you now.' I said. I needed her? Ha, I never needed her before. I never even knew her before. So why do I need her now? -sigh- But I did. My life is going down hill fast and I don't know what to do. I need some advice. Then it came to me. I could see the little child in front of my eyes. His name was Evan. He was only about seven years old. He was so young yet he was all alone in life now. I could feel a cold shiver come up my spine. I shook it off. I felt bad for the kid. I really did. It's never easy when something like that happens to a child. But why did Tessa want to adopt him? Was she really ready to be a mother? Would that make me the.....I can't even finish that sentence. I can't even think about it. I don't know about Tessa but I'm not ready for it. 'I can't be!' I shouted out loud. My heart was racing. Sweat started to drip on my forehead. I could already feel the butterflies in my stomach. They where the size of elephants. All I could think was 'I'm not ready.' The more I thought about it the madder I got. I was the Perfect Storm, Chris Storm. I can handle anything. I have won countless awards and honors. Yet I was not ready to be...to be...-sigh- I still can't say it. How can I? More so, how can Tessa expect me to be one? 'I never had one.... Not that I could remember anyways.' I said. The words hung in the air. Reminding me of the life I never had. The experiences I never got to share. 'Maybe I should...' nah. No way. Just because I had a rough childhood; well I think I did, I can't remember; doesn't mean I should go and adopt every child who is having a hard life. If we did that we would have an apartment full of kids. We are already full. Tessa has to sleep on the couch dispite the fact I tell her over and over again to sleep in the bed and I'll sleep on the couch. She never listened to me though. She was set that she was sleeping on the couch. Yet if Evan was going to stay with us, where would he sleep? It all just didn't make sense to me. There was a knock at the door. I stood up and stared at the door. The knocking came again. I wanted to be alone. I wasn't going to talk to anyone. 'Go away!' I shouted. They kept knocking. 'I said leave me alone!' Then I heard his little voice. 'But mister! I gotta pee!' he said. I just stared at the door confused. I slowly unlocked the door and openned it slowly. I found see the little child doing the boy dance as he hoped back and forth As soon as he had enough room, he bolted into the room and quickly went to the can. I just shut the door behind him and stared in confusion. Evan goes about his business. FLUSH......ZIPPPP He turns to the sink and washes his hands then dries them on a towel and turns to me. The whole time I simply stares with amazement. I sit back down and leans against the door. I stare at the kid eye to eye while sitting down. Evan reaches forward and touches my face. He puts both of his hands on my cheeks. I can feel his small hands exploring what my face is like. I reach out and touch Evan's face also. Evan finally steps back and looks at me. 'Hi, I'm Evan. Who are you?' he asked. 'My name is Chris Storm.' I told him. I was just blow away by all of this. I'm meeting a person for the first time ever and I might be his.....no way. It's just to weird. 'Nice to meet you.' he says as he extends his small hand out. I shook his hand. A smile made it's way onto my face. 'Can I ask you something?' he says shyly. I stared at him. My interest was peaked. 'You can ask me anything.' I told him. He just stared down at his feet and put his hands in his pockets. 'Are you going to be my new daddy?' he finally asked. My heart stopped. I stopped breathing. How am I suppose to answer that question? What can I possiblely say? This is a life changing decision. I just stared at him. I put my hand down onto the cold ground. I felt something under my hand. I picked it up and looked at it. It was the picture of the women who I thought was my mother. I looked at it and him, back and forth. -sigh- I know what I have to do. It's time for me to stop being such a small fish and finally stand up and be a man. I stood up and held the picture by his head. I just smiled at him and put my hand on the top of his head. 'Evan, I'm going to try everything in my power to be your dad. I promise.' and with those words I bent over and kissed him on the forehead. I opened the door and let him walk out first. I looked over at Tessa and smiled. She gave me a warm smile back. One problem was fixed. But we was a long way from having our relationship the way it was.
