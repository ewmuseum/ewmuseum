Inside Eau De Toilette

16 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/01 The camera fades in… The viewer is taken inside the doors of Eau De Toilette and into the heavily decorated, French style restaurant. A Louis XIV era chandelier hangs above the central plaza with a giant white marble fireplace to one side and a collection of impressionist paintings on the other. The tables are made from the finest French wood, and covered with beautiful white cloths, delicately embroidered and stitched. The chairs are wood, Victorian style, with comfortable velvet cushions on the set in a wide array of colors and patterns. Two giant mirrors line the opposite sides of the restaurant, showing all that takes place in the eatery during with one simple glance. In the kitchen, the collection of Le Cordon Bleu graduates and Guatemalan migrant workers are shown whipping up the finest in French cuisine including quiche, brioche, escargot, and cream pastry. A cheerful hostess greets the patrons as they enter the bistro. Bertha: Welcome mother f*ckers to the best f*cking b*tch @$$ c*ck sucking @$$ humping queer as f*ck piece of sh*t f*cking sh*tty @$$ French f*cking restaurant in the f*ck your father in the shower f*cking c*ck sucker state! The Chef is shown walking from table to table asking questions and pretending to care for the patrons of his restaurant. Patron: There is a fly in my soup! The Chef: No sir, that is just a big piece of pepper, yeah that will do…wait, did I just say that out loud? Nevermind, I’ll get you another bowl! The Chef goes to retrieve some more soup for the picky customer. The Chef: (Upon entering the kitchen and making sure the door is firmly closed) Ok, I need some Chicken and Stars pronto! A migrant worker walks up to the Chef with a confused look. Employee: Senior Chef, It displeases me very much to say this, but I think we should at least switch to Progresso over the Campbell’s soup… The Chef grabs the young mestizo man by the collar. The Chef: Listen you little infidel, the day I switch to Progresso is the day I require my employees to wash their hands after they come out of the bathroom, OK!?!? The Chef sets the man back down. Employee: Yes Senior Chef! The migrant worker scurries away and finds a can of Chicken and Stars.The Chef walks back out into the dining room and notices a very familiar face sitting at the table reserved for him and Bertha with a bunch of groupies. The Chef: So, we meet again… Emeril: BAM!!! I’m here, I’m not queer as you can see, and I’m going to be your most critical customer yet! BAM!!! Groupie: Emeril, can you tell me that story when you beat the Chef in a sword fight? The Chef: But I beat Emeril in that fi… America’s most popular chef interrupts the Chef. Emeril: Again? Well, ok, just for you Betsy… Groupie: I’m Patsy! Emeril: Whatever, whoa, you know what, I never noticed you had such nice tits, I mean, BAM!!! They’re really huge!!! Groupie: Oh Emeril, you’re so articulate… The Chef: Cut the crap you rancid piece of sausage, how did you slip by my Bertha clone!?!? Emeril: Easy, I promised one of your migrant workers I would aid his radical group in a coup against their country’s oligarch dictator and he let me in through the back door. The Chef: Damn! Well now Mr. Elian Gonzalez will wish he never made that deal with you! The Chef turns around and marches back towards the kitchen. Emeril: Listen Chef, I’ll spare telling the public that you are holding America’s favorite Cuban boy in the restaurant under the confines of debt servitude and the consequential humiliation, for a price… The Chef turns around and looks at Emeril with an angry stare. The Chef: What’s your price…? Emeril: I want a Bertha clone! The Chef: You want a Bertha clone!?!? You can’t handle a Bertha clone!!! Emeril: Listen Chef, give me a Bertha clone, and I’ll leave you alone. These girls are great for physical relationships, but I need a companion. I need someone to hold me when I don’t feel like saying “BAM!” and to tell me that my NBC sitcom wasn’t cancelled because I’m a horrible actor. I need a clone… The Chef sighs. The Chef: You know Emeril, you are my greatest rival, and my mortal enemy, but I can’t deny you a happiness you can easily reach with the right amount of bribery… Emeril: How much!?!? I’ll pay anything!!! The Chef: Well I’m a newly converted Islamic Militant with a moderately successful restaurant, so money isn’t an issue. I need a favor… Emeril: Wait; I’m not going to be doing any suicide bombings am I? The Chef: Nah, I’ll save that for the up and coming young men with a promising educational and intellectual future. Emeril: OK, then what do I have to do? The Chef: I want you to…SAY YOUR PRAYERS!!! SHALALALALALALA!!! The Chef jumps over the table, screaming sort some of gibberish, and lunges towards Emeril, landing on top of him and pounding on his face with left and right hands! Emeril’s groupies try to pry the Chef away from their man, but they quickly give up after they realize who’s standing behind him. Bertha: The only f*cking thing I hate more than a f*cking loser like Emeril is a bunch b*tch @$$ f*cking whore skanks who’ll f*cking s*ck cock and ride d*ck to get anywhere in this mother f*cking life! IT’S F*CKING BERTHA TIME!!! The clone effortlessly lifts one groupie up over her head in a gorilla press fashion and tosses her onto a neighboring table, disrupting yet another pleasant dinner conversation. Bertha then takes the other two groupies and smashes their heads together in a classic double noggin knocker! Bertha: Time to take out the d*ck @$$ trash! The clone pushes the Chef off Emeril, grabs the TV chef by his shirt and hoists him up to her face. The combination of popped blackhead pus and sweat runs down her forehead. She grins at Emeril, exposing her yellowish/brown teeth, covered in layers of corn, tootsie roll pieces, and discount fried pig ears. Emeril: God you’re beautiful… Bertha: Nice try f*ckface! I know my natural other, the original Bertha, didn’t care for you that much, except for the brief affair you had, and beat the f*cking sh*t out your worthless piece of b*tch @$$ carcass whenever she f*cking could. So let’s not f*cking waste our precious pansy @$$ time… Bertha drops Emeril back on the ground, grabs his legs, and begins to swing him around. With each spin, she swings him faster, gaining more and more velocity. She finally lets Emeril go and the Food TV’s highest rated chef flies out the front door of the restaurant shouting, “I’ll be back, BAM!!!” as he exits. The Chef walks over to the Bertha clone and gives her a huge hug. The Chef: Praise Allah, you didn’t fall for his seductive nature. Bertha: I know the old f*cking Bertha made that mother f*cking mistake, and I ain’t gonna let an @$$ cunt like Emeril do that f*cking again! The Chef, overwhelmed with love for his clone, hugs her tightly. PFFFFFTTTT!!! A large blast bellows out of the clone’s behind. Bertha: I think you f*cking hugged me too hard, f*cko… The Chef: (Wafting away the toxic fumes) Remind me not to do that anymore… Bertha: It’s a f*cking deal! The couple walks back into the kitchen holding hands. The patrons that stayed in the restaurant during the fight quickly split after getting a whiff of Bertha’s womanliness leaving the restaurant unoccupied. Elian Gonzalez pops his head out from the kitchen and shouts, “Adios Enemigos!” The camera mercifully fades to black…
