Pyramid Wrestling Gym

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Bah... Our scene opens up on a view of a vast sign that reads ‘Pyramid Wrestling Gym’. It’s a bright sunny day around us and birds can be heard humming and singing in the trees nearby. The city that we reside in is in fact that of the bustling metrologies of Indianapolis, Indiana, the very place that this weeks PCW Friday Fusion will air from. The early afternoon air flows gently past us as we stand back watching this brightly coloured sign, its very design printed upon a jet-black background, and the classic ‘Eye of Horus’ symbol acting as a backdrop for the notice’s words. We pan out a bit and find that standing, as though admiring some great marvel, are non-other than The Pharaoh, PCW’s TV champion, his new advisor and one hell of an ugly cretin, Wimlog Mogre and The Pharaoh’s gorgeous wife, Isiy. Upon Pharaoh’s right shoulder we find his gleaming TV title belt. The Pharaoh: I like it! Good work, Wimlog! Pharaoh goes to pat Mogre on the back but quickly reframes, the beastly man is quite a frightening sight to behold, after all. Wimlog: Thank you, Masster…it’s surprising how eassy you can buy out places like this…I hardly had to flash more than a few thousand dollars-… Pharaoh gasps. The Pharaoh: What?! You paid ‘a few thousand dollars’ for this old shackle hut? Wimlog: Yess, Masster. You DID tell me to go and purchase a place to set up as a wrestling gym. You seemed more than eager after I told you that the best way to get the edge over someone is to play his or her own game… Pharaoh sighs unconvinced by Wimlog’s words, but knowing that he has money to burn anyway he simply lets this action, that of spending just over three thousand dollars to buy this old gym, slide. Isiy looks up at her husband doubtfully… Isiy: Well are we just going to stand around outside all day, or are we going in to see how the new students are coming along? Isiy has grown quite impatient of standing outside, as she has been doing since the cameras first began to roll. Pharaoh smiles and then looks down at Wimlog, the hunchbacked freak, with angry eyes giving the new advisor a soundless warning of not to spend Titu’s money stupidly again. The Pharaoh: Of course, my love. Follow me… Pharaoh leads Isiy towards the gyms entrance door and swiftly opens it. Inside many men and women workout and practice wrestling moves and submission holds in a huge ring located in the main halls center. Pharaoh turns to Isiy… The Pharaoh: Welcome, my dear, to the Pyramid Wrestling Gym! Mogre begins to giggle… Wimlog: The perfect way to piss off Jason Core! Pharaoh and Mogre share a humoured chuckle. For, you see, before the cameras got here, back in Cairo that is, Mogre and Pharaoh were in deep discussion over the number one contender to Pharaoh’s TV title, Jason Core. Pharaoh happened to mention the fact that Core owned a wrestling gym and Wimlog, being the intelligent yet ugly person he is, figured out a way to annoy, anger and more importantly mock the former Extreme champion better than ever before…by opening a wrestling gym to imitate Core’s persona! The Pharaoh: I must hand it to you, Wimlog, it was a brilliantly laid out plan! Jason must be having a fit sat at home watching this right now! Wimlog: I’m sure he is, Pharaoh! Pharaoh, accompanied by his entourage, slowly walks towards the squared circle positioned in the halls center. Pharaoh grins arrogantly as he walks and then as they all rear up to the rings apron he stops and throws his arms up towards the men and women working moves in the ring. The Pharaoh: Please allow me to introduce you to my five top students! All action in the ring halts as the students see the cameras and The Pharaoh looking up at them. The Pharaoh: This here is a young prospect that we here at the Pyramid Wrestling Gym have to offer. Let me introduce Katie McLeary! A young woman, only in her late teens strides over to the ring ropes and revealingly slips out between them simultaneously revealing a bright pink thong to the cameramen, Pharaoh, who takes no notice and Wimlog, who snickers girlishly. Katie: Hiya everyone! I’m Katie Mc-… The Pharaoh: Yes, yes, we’ve already introduced you, Katie; you may simply stand there and be quiet. The young lady folds her arms over her chest moodily and stamps her feet in frustration, performing quite a pathetic excuse for a tantrum. Katie: That isn’t fair! Why can’t I have air time like you?! Pharaoh sighs in frustration. His time to cut his promo is running out quickly and he knows it. Katie’s interruption is not well appreciated at all with him. Katie: I’m being kept back here in Pyramid Championship Wrestling! It’s not fair, not fair at all! Pharaoh has had enough of her whining. He sighs again and then clicks his fingers. Suddenly out of nowhere two huge Egyptian bodyguards, all dressed in classic Ancient Egyptian garb loom out from the shadows of the gym and, both taking one of the young smutty teenagers arms, lead her off the premise. Isiy: Bitch! The Pharaoh: Agreed! Now onto the second biggest prospect we have here in PWG, let me bring out, The Destroyer! At these words a monstrous roar fills the gyms air. Many of the students look up from their training to witness a huge beastly man stride over to the ropes of the ring, step over the cables, and jump down in front of Pharaoh. He breathes heavily as his long thickly greased hair sways from side to side. Destroyer: PREPARE FOR TOTAL DESTRUCTION! Pharaoh cowers back from the titan-sized dark-gimmick monster. The Pharaoh: Good Horus, Destroyer! What have you been eating? Your breath stinks, my stupendous friend! Destroyer looks hurt by Titu’s harsh words. Destroyer: Prepare for Total destruction? Pharaoh looks up into Destroyers near-tear-filled eyes and sighs. The Pharaoh: Which one of those guy’s in the ring is it who knows what this guy s talking about? No answer comes from the students in the ring. The Pharaoh: Which-… Pharaoh feels a tug on his loincloth and looks down; there stands his new advisor as we expected, Wimlog. He calls for Pharaoh to kneel down so that he is his size. The Pharaoh: What? Can’t you see I’m in the middle of cutting my promo? Wimlog: Look, Ssire, Katie was the girl who could understand Destroyer…you just threw her off the premises… Pharaoh stands back up to his normal height and looks around shiftily. He then pats Destroyer on the shoulder. The Pharaoh: Look, big fellow, I think I know what’s up with you…it’s the fact that the truth hurts…your breath really does stink…I’m sorry it had to come out in that manner though, perhaps I should have broken that tragic piece of news to you with a more sympathetic approach…take him away, Mogre… Wimlog leads him off the scene to care to his upset feelings. Wimlog: Come on, I know how nasty the Masster can be sometimes; he calls me ugly all the time! And look at me! I am NOT ugly! Destroyer suddenly seems to have a complete change of emotion and begins to laugh furiously…Pharaoh turns to see what all the commotion is about. The Pharaoh: What’s happening over there, Mogre? What’s so funny? Destroyer: Prepare for total destruction! The Pharaoh: What? Suddenly a voice from outside the PWG complex shouts… Voice: He said, ‘Mister Wimlog reckons he isn’t ugly’… Everyone in the gym begins to laugh at the eccentric comment…even Pharaoh and Isiy share in the laugh. A tear grows in the corner of one of Wimlog Mogre’s blood shot eye. The Pharaoh: Katie? Is that you? There is a brief pause of silence and then the voice sounds out again. Voice: Yes…it is me…and I hope you come to your senses and have me back in the gym, Titu! I am the best prospect in wrestling, you know! Suddenly Pharaoh clicks his fingers again and the same two guards from before charge outside to deal with the rude teenage girl. Pharaoh smiles as the two guards return moments later cracking their knuckles and featuring grim grins about their fierce visages. The Pharaoh: Anyway, back to the introduction of my top five students… Isiy: You mean four, right? I mean you did just throw that bitch out of here…so she isn’t a member of the gym anymore… Pharaoh nods thoughtfully, taking in his wife’s words. The Pharaoh: True, true…you have an excellent point, my dear Isiy! Pharaoh turns behind him where Wimlog sits alone in a corner of the gym sobbing… The Pharaoh: Mogre! Give a note to my head guardsmen that THAT scumbag girl shan’t be aloud back in the gym! Is that clear? Wimlog looks up and nods, not daring to say a word in case Pharaoh and possibly the rest of the gym once again insult him harshly, and that includes Destroyer… The Pharaoh: Anyway, as I was saying! Let me introduce you to the final three members of MY Pyramid Wrestling Gym… Pharaoh beckons the final three students standing in the ring to make their way over towards him. They stand along side the ropes and wave to the cameras lining the ring’s apron. Pharaoh smiles as he names each one from right to left… The Pharaoh: Here we have Trev Senour… Pharaoh comes up close the cameras and whispers… The Pharaoh: We all think he’s gay…he seems pretty obsessed with his best friend, and usual tag team partner Pete! Pharaoh moves away from the camera and continues to introduce the last two people leaning across the ring’s ropes… The Pharaoh: Next up we have Pete J. Jackson! He is possibly our best performer…and last but by far not the least we have the Mexican legend… El Rippo De Torro Le Guarrda El Sombrero … We focus in on a small Mexican man; he’s good looking with long black hair down to his shoulders, which can be seen hanging loosely from underneath a sombrero style hat. A pair of maracas can be found in his hands and he shakes them wildly as the cameras focus in on him. El Rippo De Torro Le Guarrda El Sombrero: Viva Le Mexico! The Pharaoh: Yes, yes, my Mexican friend! Clam yourself! And thus concludes the round-up of PWG’s finest… Silence fills the air…a cricket beetle or two can be heard outside…Pharaoh looks at Isiy with stern eyes… Isiy: What? Oh yeah! Well, Titu, that’s very impressive but haven’t you got something you’d like to say to finish of this RIVETING promo?! Isiy’s words sound very unreal, and it seems as though Wimlog, now fully composed again, stands in front of her with a cue card…Pharaoh grins… The Pharaoh: Well yes, Isiy, I do indeed…Jason Core…they say that mimicking someone is the finest form of flattery…Ha! The person who said that was more than stupendous! By Horus, I have not set up this entire promotion rip-off of YOU to make you feel good, why I have done this to prove a point, and that is whatever you do I CAN do better! This Fusion, J-Core you will come no further to tasting TV title gold than John Crichton did last week, his emotions ran him wild and thus I still hold onto my title! The same went for Akiyama, I defeated him fairly at Spring Fling and I aim to do so again this Fusion! The cameras Focus in on Pharaoh, who now stands, arms folded over his amply toned chest and a wicked grin smeared upon his handsome mug. The Pharaoh: I am a man of sportsmanship though, and so I wish you good luck, my friend, but what is luck when you’re facing a man like me Jason, luck is nothing more than useless…remember than… Pharaoh stares up at his pupils in the squared circle. The Pharaoh: Now though I must help finish off the training of a few of my students…it would seem as though I am doing a better job than you are, Core… Pharaoh turns from the cameras and slides into the ring…he grapples with Pete J. Jackson as the scene begins to fade to black…
