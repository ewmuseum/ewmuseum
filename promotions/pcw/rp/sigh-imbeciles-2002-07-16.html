*sigh* Imbeciles!

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16, 2002 Live a day in my shoes, and you'll see what it means to be a true hero. Not the sports athletes that you dream about making that last second shot at the buzzer, or the quarterback who throws that touchdown at the end of the fourth quarter just as the clock runs down. No, that's not being a hero. I'm sorry, but neither is a burnt up ol' hag like Clyde nor the piereced up sleazy like DarkStar. *Lexian steps towards the camera, extending his amulet to the view of the audience. He flashes it to remind everyone of the power he could possess, and how he chooses to wield it.* Heroes? Them? Didn't I see them both on Hollywood's feared and despicable? Despite the contrary, I am not crazy. People want to live in a world where shit doesn't happen, and point one man here who hasn't almost been run over by a car and you'll realize that the most unrealistic scenario is often the one you are presented with. So you don't want to believe in all of the hocus pocus. Don't want to believe in vampires? Okay, well, the next time you have two fangs diving into your neck just keep telling yourself in a slight whisper...'It's just not real.' *Lexian smiles, shaking his head in naive disgust* That will help keep the bleeding down as he or she is sucking your life force from you. Nice, huh? Glad to see you're using that realistic sense of direction again. But besides all of the much too obvious forms of evil out there, how do you attempt to explain terrorists, or dictators, or rapists, or just criminals in general? Hell, Syko the Goblin sure as hell seems evil enough to me. Last time I checked, the guy was a person, or at least close to one. You want to tell me that evil doesn't exist? Oh, I see - you only see the evil that you want to believe in. That's fairly convenient, don't you think? Let the heroes take care of all the 'bad stuff' that you people don't want to deal with. Let them put their lives on the line for your sake of reality. *He just shrugs, perplexed in his position to be the hero or the villain. He wants to be a hero, but does the world want him to be one?* I never said I wouldn't fight for you, but don't pretend like you don't see it for what it's worth. Whenever I step into the ring, I see the fans that know what Legend Inc. is all about. It's not the fame, not the money, not the damn dollar bills with the dead presidents on them. Hell, it's not even the girls, although they are nice perks when Nat's not around. But in a sea of individuals, there are a select few that choose to stand on their feet, clap their hands and salute a man who is willing to fight for them. You want to cheer for Duke? Sure, he works for the blue collared worker who works that 9-5 route and plops down in front of his television to be entertained. But he is by no means somehow superior to me. I respected him, but he seems to think that he has the RIGHT to claim that the Continental title is his. *shakes his finger* I don't think so. Duke, if you wanted this title, you had your shot at Gold and Glory. But don't think for a second that you can leave me high and dry, getting my head handed to me by the Dark Age, and have me forget about it. No, it won't work that way. And now we're teamed up again this week? Jesus Christ in heaven, there must be a damn conspiracy against me. Look, take your head out of the sand for just one second and realize something...you want my title? Make sure that it's still around my waist after Turmoil and you'll get your shot, because you know, just like I do, that if the Dark Age gets their grubby little hands on it, there will be hell to get it back. I think thats something both of us can fight for. As for DarkStar and Clyde, well, I think we've gone down that road before. I think Bethany has a thing for me, but hell, I think she has a thing for Duke too. They go on and on about how it's time for a new Dark Age and how they're going to get the titles. You guys aren't getting anything, and with all of the internal turmoil that DarkStar seems to be going through, I'm sure his head's not in the game at all. I think I've forgotten about Syko...oh, poor little goblin. Well, Syko, there are better things in life than being a Continental champion. You could be the cherry in Xavier Kannon's drink, the dent in Miyagawa's porsche, or a nick in Big Allan Steele's shoe. You see, your life is complete now. *He throws his hands in the air, as if waiting for an answer.* I'm going to Turmoil with one thing in mind, and that's retaining the Continental title. One way, or the funken` other. Try to stop me. *Walks away from the camera, smiling the entire time.*
