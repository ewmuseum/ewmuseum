The... re-DEBUT~!

22 April 2003

Date: 22/04/03 Pretty Cool ChroniclesChapter #2: The… re-DEBUT~! The… re-DEBUT~!…Yeah, that sounds about right… the, second debut of the most unsuccessful Tag Team in the history of Professional Wrestling, yet the second debut, of the most successful Pop stars currently in Professional Wrestling. You know, it almost sounds ironic to be the absolute poopest at one thing, but like, the absolute greatest at something else… which, unfortunately for me, doesn’t seem to mean much anyway. Well, after the incidents that ran alongside the little incident at Raccoon City, Ryan Moore and I found ourselves arguing over the song that we should have sung to escape the apparent ‘Death Pit’ that we’d positioned ourselves in. It took a good few hours to come to some form of ending conclusion, but we came to the conclusion, that… the best song to sing, probably would have been, Michael Jackson’s “Thriller”… so, perhaps, the next time you’re viewing our electric promo… you could possibly, I don’t know… throw “Thriller” into your CD Player and just pretend that the “Backstreet Boys” dance we were doing, was actually relevant to that. Anyway, I never seem to find myself staying on the point of something for very long, so… from here on out, you’re going to discover the reaction to the re-DEBUT of both Ryan Moore and myself. So, take it easy… stay cool kids… and, uh… keep watching. -- Production Start Production Date: 21/04/03Current Location: The Bachelor Pad of… Rich Sherwin The scene slowly, but ever gracefully begins to open in the confinements of narrow corridor, painted white in color with nothing but doors preventing the wall from being seventeen thousand feet of brighty whitey space. Ryan Moore and myself stood at the front door to my own personal apartment, a Bachelor Pad that was typically reserved for those “House Parties” and “Record Launches”. I felt rather hesitant about using this place for something other than the average party scene… although, something in my blood told me that everything would be OK. Dressed in a pair of red three-quarter lengths, black trainers and a red T-Shirt… I looked quite the piece and had been attracting the attention of the public for something of the entire day. Ryan on the other hand, had decided to wear something a little more conservative. He opted for a pair of knee length shorts and an English Soccer shirt, which, strangely enough, was from his favourite side… West Ham United. I pushed my apartment key into the lock on the door and slowly twisted to the right. The locks on the door clicked to signify that they were opening and I pulled down the handle before pushing ahead. The door open widely and the pad that belonged to myself, and myself alone… was now to be viewed upon by the eyes of people who didn’t have any actual intention of throwing a party. Walking through the door, I threw the door keys across to the other side of the room, looking to get them in the armchair that was there. They landed in the centre of the seat with something of my primal precision, a key skill that’d come in handy with my old days as a professional Frisbee Champion. Ryan followed me into the apartment, his face lighting upon as his eyes came across all of the exceptionally cool things I owned. “I guess you’ve heard about the plans that the people at ‘Primetime Championship Wrestling’ have for us?” I asked Ryan intensively, looking for a few answers to the questions that were still swimming around at the back of my fragile little mind. The tone of my voice was somewhat calm, but it was enough to break the attention of the young warrior, forcing him to turn around and look me in the eye. “… Well, I’ve heard that we’re supposed to be wrestling in some form of a… Triple Threat contest, or, perhaps it was something of a Three Way Elimination…” he began, his voice slowly beginning to drift off into the lost space that was spent time. My face slowly cringed as he ran through every little individual fact all over again, checking to see which of the two things he’d said was the correct one. After a few moments, he continued, “Hmm, I believe that we’re going to be wrestling in some form, of a Three Way Elimination contest… with, Lusty Inc and Limp Bizkit’s Second Album.” I looked around the immediate area and laughed somewhat, actually happy to hear names of teams like those. For some reason, I felt somewhat invincible… after being told that the worst we could face was a couple of lovesick puppies and a couple of, severely degenerated music albums. Fred Durst had never been anything that I couldn’t personally handle… so, perhaps two of his music albums would do something that could have me on the ropes for something more than half a second. “… Oh, wait… no, I think I got the names of our Tag Team opponents wrong, Rich.” Ryan began, stopping again at the end of that line before thinking for a few moments and continuing, “… Ah yes, I remember now… I’d just used those two previous names to remember what we were going to be facing. Apparently, Justice Inc and Signifcant Others are going to be the two duos that try to make us slip over at the first hurdle.” My right arm raised upward from my side without any voluntary motion before my hand came fwapping back, slapping across my face with quite the intensity. I muttered the words, “…Owww… Owwwwaowww…” before running the hand down that very face, sighing loudly at the same time. Now, I wasn’t very impressed with the handling of information given to me by my Tag Team partner. “… I can’t believe, that of all the things possible to get wrong in this world… of all the things that you could have possibly got wrong, what appeared to be a couple of teams that may have been looking for nothing more than a little International recognition, has now turned into a couple of teams who look like they could cause some major damage to our precious sexy bodies!” I said in a tone that was actually louder than normal. The tone of voice reflected the kind of annoyance that was climbing around the veins of my body, but there wasn’t enough to push me into the Realm of actually being really mad. “Rich, you don’t have to worry about the teams that we’re going to have to face in the matter of twenty four hours on Wednesday Turmoil… seriously, you don’t have to worry about any of that stuff.” Ryan said to me with an enthusiastic ring to his voice. Part of me thought that the young lad might have had some kind of a plan in the back of his mind… but, part of me reminded myself not to get my hopes up, as these kind of plans always seem to fail when Showtime comes through. I mean, another ingenious plan that we tried to use to get anywhere, was to butter up the Lesbian Referee a few days before our enormous Tag Team Title match against a variety of different Tag Teams… … Unfortunately for us, nothing seemed to go our way and we’d been destined to fail from the ring of the first bell. Anyway, I tried to convince myself not to get too excited about the potential plan… but, regardless of how hard I tried, my excitement level just continued to increase. Ryan soon said, “… Well, because of the fact that this is nothing more than an Elimination Tag Team bout… we could, if we’re lucky, start fooling around with the Porn Star on the outside of the ring… and THEN, when one team has been eliminated… we jump in, and like… totally score the win!!” The idea actually sounded like music to my ears for a few moments, it was like one of those Pop songs that just wouldn’t fade away, regardless of its biological age. However, like every good story, as the idea continued to run rampant around the insides of my head, it sounded worse… and worse… and then some more. “Ryan… did you ever think about what could happen if, Justice Inc are too busy watching us get it on with their managerial figure… and then, totally lose everything within the space of five seconds. Our chances of winning would probably be made a lot worse or something!” I called out aloud, sounding as serious as I possibly could to make sure the message stuck deep within the cranium of Ryan. He walked further into the inside of the apartment and slumped down in one of my sofas. His hand shot up and formed a ‘V’ shape with his index finger and thumb over his chin. My right eyebrow cocked up into the air, my gaze turning to Ryan as he remained seated, a possible million and one thoughts bursting everywhere. “Hmm… I never really did stop to think of that outcome, especially since I just assumed those two people would just get on with their contest… and like, kinda leave us to just get on with everything that was usual. Those teams would have beaten each other to within an inch of their lives and then we would have just… I don’t know, jumped and pinned them both at the same time, perhaps?” he answered, in something of a mellow tone too. Still, regardless of the calm and collected attitude that he was trying to throw across, I still didn’t want to have to rely on that plan… it was just too damn dangerous! I found myself walking deeper into my apartment moments after that thought had run through my mind. Slumping down in a chair opposite Ryan, I glanced over to see exactly what he was getting up to. The ‘Boy Wonder’ fiddled around with his fingers, his eyes looking down to the carpet, seemingly creating some kind of piercing hole… or, maybe that was just his stare of intensity whenever active thoughts were running in and out of his head? “… Ryan, are you feeling alright?” I asked, sounding sincere with my question. He didn’t respond and continued to look down to the floor. I really began to think that this was going to be THE turning point in the life of Ryan Moore… that this was going to be the ultimate changing that would see the boy, take his steps into the light that was adulthood… and devise plans that were, not only extremely cool and radical with their execution, but PERFECT in every sense of the word. After a few intense moments of looking down to the floor, he clasped his hands together and returned his gaze to me, the look of a ‘Thinking Man’ spread right across his entire face! “Richard… Sherwin, I think that we’re going to win against both Justice Inc, and Significant Others, with the most convincing of victories in the history of ‘Primetime Championship Wrestling’.” He said in something of a sinister tone. I leaned closer to find out exactly what kind of plan this was going to be, as the scene slowly began to fade out into the uttermost confinement of darkness. -- Production End Well… there you have it people, another episode of the Pretty Cool Chronicles has passed and we’ve ended on the elusive moment that Ryan Moore told me the plan that we were going to use against both Justice Inc and… those other two peoples, in their stunning contest against us in the matter of one day’s time! You’re all going to find out exactly what kind of plan THAT just happened to be, when Turmoil hits the airs… but, before I go, remember to spread the word of Love and Free Music! Remember to buy your Pretty Cool Guys CD!!!
