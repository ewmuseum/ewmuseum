'Let Me Teach You Something Hoss...' (Features Duke Williams)

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/24/02 OOC Note- Before anybody IM’s me telling me the ending doesn’t make sense, just realize that Duke drives to Jordan’s hotel first. Say, I’m not one to complain, but for some reason, I don’t feel as if some people care about the PCW the way I do. Sure I just joined the PCW less than a week ago, sure I still haven’t wrestled in my first official PCW match, but, it seems as if some guys don’t understand the prestige that I carry being here in the PCW. I waited my turn for the chance in the PCW, I waited in the PWA and in the NTICW for months on end, waiting in the wings, waiting for all the slackers in the PCW to get canned so I could show them what I was made of. It took me four months. Well, I promise everybody, now that I’m in the PCW, there’s going to be hell to pay for the slackers… Sure they might not be afraid of me now…But just give it time. Give it time. As my match with Mark Makonie at the PCW Pay Per View, the more excited I get. The feud that we had in the NTICW was unequaled in it‘s intensity, these cats up here in the PCW don‘t know about that, they‘re too good to watch the NTICW apparently. Well, it‘s all gravy, because on Friday Fusion, I make my debut in the ring. There are many critics, many people who still think that Mark Makonie is a better wrestler than me, even after I defeated him, well, I guess I gotta prove them wrong. Prove the haters wrong… The scene opens up in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. The home of all that is good beer. We zoom around the city, moving from landmark to landmark. The sun shines today, as seagulls and birds fly throughout the sky. Small little rain drops are formulating despite the sun shining. Down into the city we go. We see many homeless men, with signs that read ‘Why lie…Need Beer.’ And many other sites. Though Milwaukee wouldn’t be considered a mega metropolis, it stills has a lot of action going on throughout it. We now go through the slums of Milwaukee, nasty one story houses, the paint is peeling on the outside, their doors are broken. We now move across the street from an apartment slum to a small bar. It has ‘Justin’s’ written across the marquee. We now enter the bar, it looks rather empty. Not too many people roaming about, and there are plenty of empty tables. We see two large looking men at the counter of the bar. The bar smells like a mixture of cigarette smoke, and beer. The bar isn’t even a quarter full. We go to the counter and we see the same two large men we saw earlier. One of them is larger than the other. One is of African American descent while the other is Caucasian. The Caucasian is dressed in a red flannel shirt with a black shirt under it, a black Stetson, and some blue jeans. The African American is dressed in what appears to be baggy khaki pants, some white Nike Old Schools, a Doo rag, and some Basketball looking wrist bands. We now go to the Bartenders view, and we see that the two men sitting at the front of the bar are none other than PCW wrestlers, and Malice Crew members, Duke Williams, and Jordan X Shabazz. Duke has a brown colored drink in front of him, which I would assume is some sort of Jack Daniels drink, while Jordan, the manly man that he is, has a half empty glass of sparkling water. Duke chugs his brown colored drink and he taps the table as the bartender goes to get him some more. Duke looks at Jordan as Jordan chugs the rest of his sparkling water and he taps the counter for more water. Duke looks at Jordan with a look of disdain on his face. What am I doing here in this country ass bar? It’s the land of rednecks in a predominantly black area, no wonder why this place is so empty.. Duke Williams: “Jordan, remember on Sunday when I saved your ass hoss?” Jordan X Shabazz: “Of course I remember, how could I forget? It was the best night of my professional wrestling career.” Duke Williams: “Well, now that you are in the Malice Crew, and now that you are in the PCW, you are going to have to act like a man.” Oh God of Crunk please save me. There’s no freakin’ way he’s going to ask me to do what I think he’s going to ask me to do. Jordan looks at Duke as if he just said ‘Rush Mason isn’t God.’ Jordan has a look of disbelief on his face. He sips his sparkling water and he speaks. Jordan X Shabazz: “Duke, sparkling water does me fine, after getting drunk and getting my physical, I don’t want to ever drink again.” Duke Williams: “Come on Hoss, drink some alcohol. You first hang over is a bitch, but just drink here, I’ll teach you how to take a proper shot. I’ll also teach you how to be drunk, and not show it.” Damn right it was my first hangover, and if I have anything to do with it, it’ll be my last. Sure, call me a pussy, a fan, a phony, a fake, whatever. Alls I know is that I ain’t dealin’ with no migraine headaches before my first ever wrestling debut. Jordan has a skeptical look on his face. Jordan X Shabazz: “Duke, I think sparkling water is all I’ll have.” Duke Williams: “Damn it Hoss.” Duke stands up and he shoves Jordan’s sparkling water off the table. Duke taps the table as the bartender goes and he fills up a large glass of bourbon. I swear to God, if Duke Williams couldn’t kick my ass, and if he didn’t save me on Sabotage, and if he didn’t make me a member of the Malice Crew, and if I didn’t know him, and if I had twenty of my homies with me, and if I had a baseball bat with me, I’d like to just swing for the fences at this moment. Jordan X Shabazz: “Can I have it with orange juice?” Duke Williams: “Damn it Hoss, you’ve gotta be a man to survive in the PCW. Drinking bourbon with orange juice is for pussies. And pussies aren’t in the Malice Crew. You understand me? Obviously he hasn’t heard of gin and juice… to quote a great man ‘Ugh, white people.’ Jordan X Shabazz: “Yes sir.” Jordan looks at Duke, and it looks like he’s about the throw up. The man is Jordan is coming out as he tries to chug the bourbon down. He gets about half of it down when he spits it back out on the table and he begins choking. Jordan X Shabazz: “Damn, freakin’ *Cough* Crack *Cough* ers *Cough* and their stupid ass drinks.” Duke Williams shakes his head. He looks disappointed and ashamed at the same time. The bartender is now reading a magazine and is paying no attention to Jordan and Duke. Duke Williams: “Let me show you something Hoss.” Duke taps the counter as the bartender fixes yet another drink for the already almost inebriated Duke Williams. The bar has now emptied leaving only Jordan, Duke, and the bartender. Duke chugs down a large glass of bourbon. It doesn’t faze him one bit, he looks at Jordan, and smiles. God damned crackas. They can drink anything and not feel it burn in their throat, he’s probably just playing it off, tryin’ to make a n*gga feel bad… There’s no way he could chug that down without screaming or choking… Duke Williams: “Listen Hoss, don’t sweat it, just chug down the drink, and don’t even think about it. It tastes great when you don’t think about it.” Chug down the drink? What the f*ck does this playa look like? A 40-year old overweight, low income making, trailer park living in, redneck? Ah hell, might as well prove this wrestling Icon wrong, he IS my partner, and he DID help me get into the PCW… Jordan taps the counter like Duke did before him. The bartender fixes Jordan a hard bourbon. We see Jordan’s gulp as the drink is placed on the table. Jordan tentatively sips the bourbon to get used to the taste. He looks at a grinning Duke Williams, and with all his strength, all the power of Malcolm X and Martin Luther King Jr. on his shoulders, Jordan gulps down the entire drink as Duke stands up and begins clapping. BOOYAH! That’s for all the haters out there who say I’m a wussy and what… Jordan X Shabazz: “Oh God Of Crunk.” Jordan begins running towards the rest room. Before he makes it, he vomits all over the bathroom floor. We hear the groans of anger are heard as the bartender rushes towards the rest room. He sees Jordan moving out of the restroom and he lets him by. Bartender: “OH MY GOD! I JUST GOT THIS PLACE CLEANED AFTER FOUR YEARS, THIS’LL COST ME ANOTHER FORTUNE!” The bartender turns around and he angrily stomps out of the bathroom. He walks towards the bar and he looks at Jordan and Duke. Bartender: “Okay, I want you outta my bar now! Pay up for all them drinks, and another $80 to clean the floors.” HOLD UP! Time out! This cracka must be crazy. He thinks that I am going to give a dollar that I could easily donate to the NAACP to him? I swear to God, white people are the craziest people on this planet. That’s why I fight for our rights, because if I don’t, then the white man will step all over us. Jordan X Shabazz (To Duke Williams): “Duke, since you taught me about your culture, let me show you mine.” Jordan runs towards the door without leaving a single penny on the table. The burly bartender who is dressed in biker clothing runs towards the door and he begins yelling obscenities at Jordan X Shabazz, who was safely hiding behind the corner. Duke and him already planned the routine. Duke would come walking around the corner and then they would go their separate ways until Friday. While Jordan ran, Duke walked out of the bar. There was nothing the bartender could do, it was DUKE F^CKING WILLIAMS FOR RUSH’ SAKE! Duke met up with Jordan around the corner. Duke had a grin on his face. Duke Williams: “That was great hoss. I’m proud of you. I didn’t expect you to drink that bourbon, hell, I know plenty of people that have trouble swallowing liquids that burn the throat…And Ellie~! Isn’t one of them.” Jordan begins laughing as the two men walk to Duke’s pickup. They are still in the ghetto, but the sun is out, so nobody will get mugged at this moment and time. Jordan looks at Duke. Jordan X Shabazz: “Well, I think it’s time you learned Ebonics.” Duke Williams: “E what a what?” Jordan X Shabazz: “Ebonics.” Jordan said the word with pride. It was the specialized language that most black people spoke but very few white people did. And when they did, they usually used out of date ‘98 East Coast Hip Hop slang. You know, that’s when it was Cool to be black. Duke Williams: “Okay hoss, I taught you something, now you are gonna teach me something.” Jordan nods as they walk past Duke Williams’ car and into the streets of Indiana. Jordan X Shabazz: “First off, you’ve got to get into the mind set of a black man.” Duke Williams: “Mind set?” Jordan nods again. Jordan X Shabazz: “Yeah, the mid set. First things first, you have to get the walk down.” Jordan begins to walk like one leg is shorter than the other, AKA as ‘The Pimp Walk.’ Jordan looks back nodding at Duke Williams. Duke begins cracking up and not grasping the concept of walking like a cripple. Duke Williams: “I’m sorry Jord, I think we should just get right to the meat and teach me some jive.” Jordan X Shabazz: “Jive?” Duke Williams: “Jive.” Jordan laughs as the two men turn the corner of the curb. Duke Williams: “Actually, I’ve got a question for you Jordan.” Jordan X Shabazz: “And what is that?” Duke Williams and Jordan turn another curb, they essentially walked in a circle throughout the block. They are now nearing Duke’s pickup truck. “If you are a Civil Rights Activist, why do you speak in slang? Don’t you think that speaking like an intelligent person with a high IQ would improve the African American’s status in this world?” Jordan stops in stride. He ponders what Duke Williams told him. He nods at Duke. Jordan X Shabazz: “You know what? I think I’ll do that. I never really thought like that before, but the more I think about it now, the more I realize that you are right.” Duke nods. “That’s my job. Giving my commentary on things…And whipping ass.” Jordan sighs. “White people, can’t live with them, and YOU CAN LIVE WITHOUT THEM!” The scene fades to black with Jordan X Shabazz and Duke Williams entering Duke’s truck and going to their separate hotels…
