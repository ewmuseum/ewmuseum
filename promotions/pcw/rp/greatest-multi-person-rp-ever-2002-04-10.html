GREATEST MULTI-PERSON RP EVER!

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/10/02 The End:“It’s time for a victory celebration! Yes, despite the fact that his match is on Friday Fusion, and that today is only Wednesday, The End is so assured of his victory that he is already having a party about it. Join in, won’t you? Today, I celebrate my win over Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc. It truly is a great thing, how I can be so good.” Handler:“Um… two things, first, shouldn’t you wait until AFTER your match to celebrate? Second, what about Darkstar? He’s in the match as well.” The End:“Are you kidding? We all know that I’ll have to carry his short, fat ass in the ring.” Handler:“He’s only 3 inches shorter than you, and not that much heavier.” The End:“You’re going to ruin my moment here; look, I’m better than everyone else in that match. I know it, they know it, you know it… the reader knows it, isn’t that right, Reader?” Reader:“Nope.” The End:“Shut up! Anyway, handler, we’ve just been talking back and forth without any proper scene set-up or anything. Get with the RP writing and stop making this a bland script!” Handler:“I would so kick your ass if you were a tangible character…” The End:“Just get to writing, I’ve got a party to host.” Handler:“… Fine.” The End hosts his party in the same hotel where Usher shot his “U Don’t Have To Call” video. Oh look, they’re still dancing around. Get the hell out, this is a private party! Err-hehmm. Anyway, let’s take a look around and see what’s going on. The End is at the only one at the bar; everyone else is out and about, mingling and talking with the other people. The End, even though it’s his party, resigns himself to sitting at the bar, drinking virgin strawberry daiquiris. Wuss. The End:“I don’t drink alcohol and you know it.” … Anyway, The End finishes his drink and asks the bartender to give him another.” The End: *Snapping his fingers at the bartender*“Keep, I’ll have another daiquiri.” The bartender is wearing a black apron and navy shirt and pants. His hair is short, barely stopping at the shoulders, but is still long enough to be tied back in a ponytail. The bartender turns around, and wipes off a glass while speaking. Bartender End:“I’m sorry, I don’t have anymore.” The End:“What are you talking about? I’m the only PCW worker who bothers to order virgin drinks (except for maybe Rent-A-Hero, but the door guards will keep him out).” Rent-A- Hero End comes up in the Spider-Man costume Rent-A-Hero wears and places his order. Rent-A-Hero End: *Slamming a fifty dollar bill on the counter*“Good bartender! I require another delectable daiquiri drink to delight my Kryptonian taste buds. Virgin as always, much like myself. I must remain sober, for after this party I must attend to a legion of evil super-villains that have had a grand-spanking booty-whipping of Rent-tastic proportions! Villains everywhere are wary, and they do tremble at the mere mention of such awesome powers and awesome weapons of awesome might; weapons and powers such as the Yo-Yo of Mild Discomfort, and my Super Saiyan strength and Kamehameha Wave of Destruction, as well as my good old-fashioned Biz-nitch Slap O’ Doom~! Can’t forget that little doozy. So, good citizen, I shall gladly be taking another strawberry daquiri, and this time please do not skimp on the syrup.” The bartender shakes his head in disbelief at what he just heard. “He can’t be that stupid, can he?” thinks the bartender in one of the few spots of internal monologue in this entire RP. Bartender End:“We don’t have any more of the syrup for the daquiris. You and your gracious host here drank it all.” The End looks at Rent-A-Hero End (please, End, just go along with my text and don’t argue) with a confused expression. The End:“How did you get past the guard monkeys?” Rent-A-Hero End:“Guard monkeys?” The End:“I don’t know, whatever my handler calls those freaks that are my player-controlled fans. They were supposed to stop you at the door.” Rent-A-Hero End:“There was no one at the door, except for one man resembling the Kingpin who insisted upon my wearing a party-hat. While it was fun at first, the novelty soon wore off and I gave the hat to a group of men sitting in the corner over there wearing exactly the same outfits as the one at the door..” There’s no one at the door, except for Lamar End. He’s directing people inside. He’s supposed to keep the riff-raff out… Inside, we see Slade End, Richard End, Lexicon End, Kay Fabe End, and Drek End playing cards with Darkstar End, Rush End, and El Diablo End. Everyone has folded their hand except Drek End and Darkstar End Drek End:“Royal Flush, spades.” Darkstar End takes out a knife and holds it up to Drek End’s face. Darkstar End:“I could kill you right now.” Drek End:“A knife. Well, I’ve got a huge battle axe.” Drek End pulls his axe from under the table He doesn’t hold it at Darkstar End’s face, but rather just slings it over his right shoulder in a cocky, nonchalant way.. Drek End:“Perhaps you’d like to take a closer look?” Darkstar End tosses the knife away behind him, the knife hitting Duzza End in the eye. Duzza End:“F*ck! My eye! I saw that, mutha f*ckin’ cracka! Yo’ betta hope I don’ git sight back in dis eye o’ itz gon’ be yo’ *ss! Dumb*ss punk b*tch cracka!” Jojo the Albino Iguana escorts Duzza out of the hotel so that he won’t get blood all over the floor before calling him an ambulance. Sitting at the bar wallowing in self-pity, El Diablo End is guzzling beer after beer. He asks for one more, but the bartender won’t comply. El Diablo End: *Banging his left hand on the bar*“Gimme another beer!” Bartender End: *Shaking head “no” while serving a Bloody Mary to Andy Murray End*“You’ve had enough, man.” El Diablo:“Come on! One more beer!” The bartender whistles over at the Giant Sexual Harassment Panda End, which steps up to El Diablo End. El Diablo End decides this isn’t a fight he wishes to get into and steps away. The melancholy El Diablo walks back to his room. El Diablo End fiddles with he keys to try to open his room door. El Diablo End:“I can’t believe I freaking lost my match with Aymin! Now I have to lose my mask, which is the whole key to my freaking lucha aura… I won’t be able to go back to Mexico with pride, losing my mask in America! “And then, to make matters worse, the bartender cuts me off! I only had 24 beers! I paid!” It is at this point that something comes to his attention. The door is unlocked on a key card, and El Diablo has been trying to use his house keys to open it. El Diablo End:“Dammit!” The door opens for El Diablo End before he can reach into his pocket for his key card (wouldn’t do any good, he traded it to a kleptomaniac for money to get his 20th beer). Inside, Brianna Banks, Erika Michelle Barr, and Jamie Bergman (you wouldn’t believe how long it took me to find a site that gave me the names of celebs/porn stars without providing the pics that wouldn’t gotten me kicked out of the computer lab and lost my job forever), their naked bodies wet from the shower, greet the forlorn El Diablo. Porn Stars:“We saw how bad you felt after you lost the match at Spring Fling… would you like us to cheer you up?” El Diablo End steps inside of the room and removes his shirt. El Diablo End:“I am married, but what the hell.” The End and Tim Walden End are debating reparations. Tim Walden End:“What the *expletive deleted* are you *expletive deleted* talking about? Reparations are *expletive deleted* good and will *expletive deleted* help the *expletive deleted* *expletive deleted*. It’s owed to the mother *expletive deleted*!” The End:“I disagree. The government doesn’t owe blacks reparations because it was not responsible for slavery. Also, the money would have to be derived either by asset redistribution, or hyperinflation of the economy. Not to mention that it would cause unemployment and social unrest.” Tim Walden End:“Who the *expletive deleted* cares? I’m Canadian anyhow! At least my *expletive deleted* handler is….” Another game of cards, this time involving Jeff Marx End, PFIK End and BAS End. Jeff Marx End:“Ingenious idea from The End, impersonating all of the characters in this RP so that he wouldn’t have to ask handlers’ permission to use their characters. I think this went off well. And I raise $40.” PFIK End:“Even when being impersonated by another person, you can’t help but break kayfabe, can you? I’m out.” BAS End looks at his cards. His face is stoic in its cold blankness. BAS End:“I shall raise you a whopping $500!” Jeff Marx decides that BAS is bluffing. He slaps his cards down to the table. Jeff Marx End:“Two Pair, jacks over eights!” BAS End smiles and puts his cards down on the table softly. BAS End:“Straight, diamonds up from 3. I shall be taking the money, thank you.” Jeff Marx End, in disbelief of his loss, walks off to find someone to complain to. Finished with his argument with Tim Walden End, The End is leaning with his back to the bar, surveying everything that’s going on. The End:“You know what, handler?” Handler:“What?” The End:“When I have a real victory party, remind me not to invite any of these people.” Handler:“Very well, but you’ve still got to get ready for that match with Loc and Stormbringer.” The End: *Shrugging off the last comment*“If I’m not ready now, I won’t be ready. But I feel ready, so that’s good enough.” Handler:“If you say so.” The End:“Now, could we please end this debacle? Akiyama End is starting to banish everyone and I don’t feel like dealing with this.” Handler:“Okay.” *Fade to black*
