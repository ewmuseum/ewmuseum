Welcome to the house of Kano

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/2002 *The scene opens up outside of a huge, extravagant house on a cliff overlooking a large city. This house is as big as some neighboorhoods in America. It is made of solid brick and has a large garage attached to it on the right side. Suddenly the large front door swings open and out steps Jon Kano. Jon Kano is dressed in baggy blue jeans, tan Doc Martin boots, and a black t-shirt with 'PCW' written across the chest in red lettering in a style like the letters are bleeding.* Kano: Hey, welcome to my crib. C'mon in and I'll show you around. *The scene fades out and then fades back in inside of Kano's kitchen. This kitchen has stainless steel everything with marble counters and black and white tiling. In the center of the kitchen, is a large stove and oven. Jon Kano hops up and sits on one of the counters.* Kano: This is my kitchen, which I really love. It is one of the rooms in this house that I actually helped design. It has luxury and is to the point, which I think reflects my personality. I love the refrigerator because of its triple-wide stainless steel doors. That refrigerator cost thirty-two large alone. These marble counters cost almost seventy. These aren't those cheap, run-of-the-mill laminated marble countertops. These things are solid marble. Anyway, lets go to my paraphenalia room, as I call it. *Kano walks out of his kitchen and takes an immediate right and through a door into a huge room. This room is pretty simple. It is littered with Jon Kano's career items. It has pictures on the wall, programs, flyers, videotapes, DVDs, posters, stand-up posters, and various other items.* Kano: This is my favorite room, hands down. I love to just come in here and crash on that couch and ponder my future. I love this picture here. *Kano points to a picture of himself and Boda sitting a bar somewhere and talking.* Kano: I don't know why I like that picture so much, but I do. It is just Chris and me bullsh*tting about something and his wife, Stacey, took a picture of us and I got a copy of it. It was just me and him having a good time. I like this one, too. *Kano points to another picture where he is in the ring wrestling a man that is unfamiliar to most PCW fans.* Kano: That was my last match in a fed that I wrestled in before I came to PCW. It was a rather large fed based out of Tokyo, Japan. That match took place in the Tokyo Dome. Everyone knew it was my last match because I wanted to come back to the states. After the match, I got a standing ovation from the seventy-five thousand fans in the arena that night. That picture brings back a lot of good memories of wrestling over there. There is only one place I like wrestling more than over there and that place is Caesar's Palace. Anyway, lets go out to my garage and we will take a look at my toys. *The scene fades out as Kano begins to leave the room and fades back in inside of a huge eight-car garage filled with eight different cars. Kano walks over to his trademark Lincoln Navigator.* Kano: This is my baby, my 2001 Lincoln Navigator. I am getting rid of this thing next week and getting a brand new one, like I do every year. The one next to it is a 2002 Nissan Altima. If you have the means, I strongly recommend picking one up. It has a lot of nice features and is my favorite car to just drive around town. Next to my Altima, is my 2002 Lincoln Continental with twenty inch Assassin rims on it. Next to it is my 1964 Cheverolet Impala that I am restoring. I am about half way done now. I plan on going all out on this thing, hydraulics and all that other good stuff. Next to my Impala, is my 2001 Dodge Viper. I love to beat that car. Yes, my beater car is a sixty-thousand dollar automobile. Jesus, Karl Marx is probably spinning in his grave right now. I am the ultimate capitalist pig, aren't I? Next to my Viper, is a 2001 PT Cruiser, which I HATE. The car is too damn slow. I have put about forty miles on that thing, and I hate it. Next to my pile of sh*t, is a 2002 Chevy Corvette. I just got it, and I love it already. Lots of fun in that car. And finally, is my 2002 Ford F-150 XLT Off-road 4x4. That is my truck to drive around my property. I spend a lot of time out here washing and waxing and buffing and staring at these cars, so I also have my security center out here. I can see just about everything on my property on these televisions. *Kano points over to a stand which holds about ten mini-tvs, each with a different picture of Kano's estate.* Kano: I like to see if anyone is intruding on my property and I like to know who is at the door before I go and answer it. It is a good policy to get into, especially if you have a very large estate like I do. Its a very nice feature to this house. It is like a covert operation to watch everything that is going on in my house or around my house at all times. *Kano sits down on a stool, and lights up a cigarette.* Kano: So Friday, I have a man who wants to think he is 'Controversial.' He isn't controversial, I AM. Who has caused more controversy than me? Not many people. I mean I used to like this kid. I thought he coulda been a contenda'. He was part of the New Breed, a group of young wrestlers who had just entered PCW and wanted to make names for themselves. It was a nice principle. I can't help but remember about when I was at that point in my career. This Corey Williams fellow was in a match with me a few months back and he called me a PCW legend. He gained a lot of respect from for that comment, but now he is a little cocky bastard. He calls me an old man? Well sh*t, how long was I out for? Last time I checked, I was only 26 years old. I may give the impression that I am older because I have been wrestling since I was 20, and by the time I was 22, I was a legend. I had this industry transformed. I look at my career and I know I can wrestle for another 20 or 25 years. With all I have accomplished in 6 years, the sky is the limit on the next 20. Corey needs to recognize that I am not some run of the mill, end of the era legend. I am a REAL LEGEND who wrestled in front of 100,000 fans and defeated Alan Kreigman. I can't wait until Friday to show you and every single fan in the stands that I still got what it takes, that I am in my prime, that when I come to the ring I am not able to be beaten by my opponent but only by myself, and most importantly, above all other things that is true about me, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano puts his cigarette out on the floor of his garage.*
