And They Say a Hero Can Save Us

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/02 Tales of the Golden Toe: The Golden Heart as passed and the Golden Toe has come back again. The Golden Toe is the old Juan Borro, the fun loving foreign soccer player who did his all to help others. Through the tales of the Golden Toe, Juan gives us insight into present experiences with his life and duties and encounters with people. The Golden Toe has seen his calling, and that calling is to do good be good, and be a power for good. No force will stop Juan’s GOAL~! Time: 2: 23 A.M.Location: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Bedroom “And They Say a Hero Can Save Us.” The scene opens with Juan Borro sleeping in his bed-cuddled warm and tight. Juan can’t seem to get to sleep, he just keeps thinking about his match with RAH. In a way Juan wishes he could be like RAH. He then thinks back to a hero that was in his life. It didn’t take long as one person stuck out in his mind perfectly. The sequence comes to Juan. He was ten years old back then and was just about to make his escape from the orphanage he lived in. He was there almost five years when he and an older boy around sixteen decided that they would leave and never come back as long as they lived. Juan was thrilled about the situation because anything could be better than the terrible ordeals he’s gone through in the orphanage. Juan packed his bags and planned the escape. An escape from an orphanage wasn’t a hard thing. These people weren’t paid so they could care less if you left at any time. It depended on the will of us the children if we wanted to leave. Legally they had to be eighteen, and if they were found roaming the streets at a younger age we’d be put back or brought to an institution. Juan didn’t care about that at all though. All he cared about was that he finally found someone to escape with and the two of them would live off the streets like the greatest warriors in the world. They’d be like Rob Hood and take from the rich and give to the poor. The person making the voyage with Juan was named Filipo. Filipo was one of the most popular kids in the whole orphanage. If anyone needed some food or had to get extra supplies Filipo would work his magic to get it for them. Filipo was also tough. No one ever messed with him, because they knew they couldn’t handle him. He was never even seen crying. Filipo was one of a kind, all of the girls wanted him and all the guys wanted to be him. All of these reasons were why Juan wanted to be him. To Juan Filipo was his hero. Some people call their heroes a parent, but Juan never had steady ones so Filipo was the closest thing he had to a friend and a hero. He hopes that later that night Filipo could be as perfect in their escape as he’s always been. Juan wouldn’t trust himself with anyone else. Their were some kids who said Filipo was no good and was a huge liar, but Juan never believed a word about that, because he knew the truth, or at least he thought he did. It’s in the middle of the night and Juan sneaks over to Filipo’s room. Juan does the special knock and Filipo answers quickly letting him in. Filipo: Do you have everything ready to go? Juan: I sure do. What will be our first step? Are we going to try and sneak through the front and make a run for it. Filipo: No that’s not needed. Juan: Oh then what are we going to do? Filipo points at the window and smiles. Juan nods his head scared senseless of heights back then. Filipo: Don’t worry it’s pretty safe. We just have to tie toe a few yards, then use hangers to slide on the phone lines. Once we clear the fence I had stash of boxes set up this morning and we’ll jump on those. It’ll hurt a bit, but it should take away a lot of the impact of our fall. Juan: Let’s go then. The two get started with Filipo being brave and going first. Filipo completes it with pure grace not even making a single mistake. He then whistles over to Juan. Juan takes a deep breath and starts. He steps out the window and takes small side steps. He then reaches the line, puts his hanger out and soars through the sky. As the air breezes through Juan’s face he feels free almost as if a new life is about to begin. The air is thick and if Juan would have stayed there for a few more seconds he’d be in a land of euphoria, but he had to make the jump. Juan made the jump a little off and would have landed straight on the floor if Filipo weren’t there to break his fall. Juan: Sorry I couldn’t time the drop. Filipo: its fine at least we’re out of there. Wave goodbye because we’re never going back to that place. Juan makes his last goodbye thinking even more about how great Filipo is saving him from the hard fall and triumphing over evil to escape with him by his side. Juan knows now that the hard part is gone everything will fall in place. Because Heroes don’t fail, they always prevail. Juan and Filipo were on their own at last. They walked through the streets all night and just told jokes and hung out. They found a comfortable spot in an alley, right near an abandoned couch. Filipo even found a heater in the back of a pizza restaurant that would keep the two of us warm. It seemed like their first night would be one to remember. Nothing was bad enough to make them go back, the stars were sparkling and we felt more comfortable than he had in years. Everything was finally back to normal and Juan couldn’t be happier. Juan and Filipo then went to bed and had a great night sleep. They didn’t wake up until about 11 A.M the next day. Juan: Hey Filipo is it too late for breakfast. Filipo: No way. We’re going to have a huge buffet. It’ll be the best meal we’ve had in years I guarantee it. Juan was extremely happy as the two went to a diner and ate all they could eat. Juan was stuffed and couldn’t even look at another piece of food. The waitress then came with the check and everything had just changed in a matter of seconds. Filipo just stared at the bill in complete amazement. Juan: What’s wrong? Filipo: I’ve made a huge mistake. It’s been so long since I’ve paid for a meal that I forgot all the prices, I didn’t even think it would be half of this much money. Juan glanced at the bill and in just one meal seventy five percent of the money they had saved was gone. When they leaved that diner, both of them felt completely depressed. There was so little money left they couldn’t even have lunch. By the time dinner came along the two were starving and begging for money. They didn’t get much and they each had a roll of bread, not nearly anything that could satisfy them fully. That night nothing was the same. They weren’t warm and they didn’t have a comfortable bed to sleep in. That night when they were all asleep Juan heard a trashcan get knocked over. When he woke up and checked he saw Filipo with his bags packed leaving. Juan: What are you doing. Filipo didn’t look proud, but told the truth. Filipo: I can’t take this anymore I’m going back. Suddenly, Juan’s interpretation of Filipo completely changed. Juan: How can you do this? We said we’d never go back no matter how hard it got. Are you a quiter? Filipo: No, but I know we’ll die if we stay like this. Why don’t you try and do this for a few more days, I’ll guarantee you’ll come crawling back. Juan didn’t respond, his emotions said it all. He just looked the other way and never turned around. Days and years went by and Juan never went back. In the end the only hero Juan had to look up to was himself. This leads to the conclusion that heroes aren’t needed. Heroes just help give us guidance and encouragement, but in the end they’re not all they’re cracked up to be. Everybody could do the same things all on there own if they tried making heroes completely useless. Juan had a person he thought of as a hero and maybe he was, but nobody is perfect and a hero is no different. In the end were all the same. And at Supremacy Juan will show everyone that him and RAH are no different. Juan WILL win HIS cruiserweight title again, and this time he doesn’t plan on losing it.
