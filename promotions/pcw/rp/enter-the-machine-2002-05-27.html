Enter The Machine

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/05/02 A shining black limousine roars out of the car park of the PCW Pavilion in Atlantic City as two policemen on horseback clear a path through the mass of fans that have gathered to catch a glimpse of the PCW superstars as they leave after Friday Fusion.  As the limo slows to allow the policemen to move the crowd out of it's way, some of the fans put there faces to the heavily tinted windows, straining to get a look at the lone occupant. Who is it? Can you see? It's him. Who? That CCW guy, Supreme Machine The fans push closer to the huge automobile, hoping to catch a glimpse of the five time CCW Heavyweight Champion.  One of the mounted policemen turns back and tries in vain to get the fans to step away from the limo but still more of them cram in to get a closer look.  Before long the limousine is totally surrounded by hundreds of fans who begin a familiar chant. SUMA!  SUMA!  SUMA!  SUMA! Just as the situation seems like it's going to get out of control and turn into a riot, the mounted policeman in front of the limousine somehow manages to finally clear a path and the huge black car races away from the wildly cheering fans.  In a matter of seconds the limo has disappeared into the night leaving the two mounted policemen to deal with the over excited, still chanting  crowd. As the limousine speeds away from the scene of Friday Fusion, the shot cuts to the interior of the car where it's lone passenger, The Supreme Machine, sits back relaxing on one of the luxurious leather seats, sipping nonchalantly on a bottle of Perrier and flicking through the channels on the small television screen that is set in mahogany just in front of him.  He is dressed in loose blue jeans and a tight fitting black T-shirt that highlights his huge pecs and biceps, while his mane of jet black hair is tied neatly back in a long ponytail.  He continues flicking through the channels while taking the occasional look out of the darkened windows.  He takes another sip of the Perrier as he finds a television station that is showing the highlights from Friday Fusion.  A broad smile appears on his face as he watches the replay of him laying out PCW CEO Tyler Nelson. Yet another smash hit. The Supreme Machine puts down his Perrier and settles back into the leather seat to watch the show.  As the highlights package finishes, he takes another sip of mineral water before switching the television off with the remote control that he places beside him on the back seat.  He takes a moment to gaze out window at the passing scenery then turns to face the camera. So I suppose you're all wondering what I'm doing here in PCW right?  Well, before I get inundated with questions and accusations I'll lay it all out in the open for you. He sips on the Perrier before continuing. Where do I start?  Well seeing as I'm dealing with ignorant fools, I'll start by telling you who I am.  Now I'm sure there are some amongst you PCW peons who will know exactly who I am, but for the rest of you that have been living with your heads in the sand for God knows how long, I'll enlighten you.  I am a five time CCW Heavyweight Champion.  I am the only wrestler to be inducted into the CCW Hall of Fame.  I am a legend.  I am The Supreme Machine. He chuckles softly to himself as he goes on. I can see it now.  Some of you fools are going to say something along the lines of "well so what if you were a champion in CCW.  This is PCW.  CCW counts for nothing here".  Well let me just say to you that I don't care about any of your reputations either.  Sure, some of you have held titles before but I'll let you in on a little secret... you only had them because I wasn't around to stop you.  I couldn't care less who you are or how good you believe yourselves to be, because all that counts for nothing.  I haven't come all the way to PCW to just make up the numbers.  No, not by a long shot.  So take this as an advanced warning.. I get what I want and I'm yet to face anyone who has been able to stop me.  If there is a belt in any federation that I haven't held, it's only because I didn't want it.  Now I know that there have been wrestlers that have come over from CCW before me, your current champion Big Alan Steele is one I believe, but next to me they are nothing.  Kensuke Suzahara, Jason McAllister, TVO.... They all pale into insignificance when compared to me. He stretches out along the length of the back seat. Now as for the why.  I'm sure that there's more than a few of you wondering just what the hell I'm doing in PCW.  Well there's a number of reasons but I'm only going to let you morons in on a couple of them.  Firstly, I'm here at the request of The Phantom Booker.  I met him a few years ago when I was just starting out in professional wrestling and he helped me out on more than a few occasions, so when he contacted me to come over to PCW I didn't hesitate. I always remember those that give me assistance, just like I never forget those that are stupid enough to get in my way.  The Phantom Booker tells me PCW's ratings are down and that he needs help in bringing them back up so here I am. The second reason is even simpler.  I have grown tired of CCW, there are no more challenges left for me there.  Sure, there are titles I didn't win in CCW but that's just because I didn't want them. I don't care to soil my hands on CCW's mid card dross when I can come to PCW and destroy the small number of mildly talented wrestlers that are plying their trade here.  And mark my words, for destroy them I shall. The Supreme Machine sits back up straight and takes a sip of his Perrier as he switches the television back on.  He flicks through a few channels before settling on a sports channel that is showing motorcycle racing. The rest of my reasons for coming to PCW are my private business.  You've been told all that I'm prepared to divulge at this point.  All you need to know is that I've arrived in PCW and I won't be going anywhere in a hurry. He watches the television for a few seconds before switching it back off.  He takes a deep breath and turns and gazes silently out at the passing traffic. What happened to your buffoon of a CEO, Tyler Nelson, and the rest of his pathetic little cronies on Friday night is nothing compared to what's going to be happening once I actually set foot in a PCW ring.  What are they called again?  Ultimate Authority?  What a damned joke.  Xavier Kannon? Mark Makonie?  I seriously hope that these pathetic excuses for wrestlers aren't the best that PCW have to offer.  A Scientologist and a ignorant white supremacist?  Bah!  If it wasn't for The Phantom Booker I wouldn't even be wasting my time on these ridiculous idiots. He shakes his head as he reaches for the intercom that connects him to his chauffeur who is hidden behind a black, sliding panel. How long until we get to New Brunswick? The chauffeur's voice comes crackling back from the front seat. I'm not sure Sir, we've hit a little traffic but I doubt that it'll be more than a couple of hours. OK.  Let me know when we're nearly there, I have some business to attend to as soon as we arrive. Yes Sir. The Supreme Machine relaxes back in his seat and turns back to face the camera. So you've all been warned.  Ultimate Authority... He chuckles softly again. Sorry, they are just so pathetic it's funny.  Ultimate Authority, Big Alan Steele, whoever.. I don't care who you are.  You get in my way and you get hurt.  I know that you've probably all heard that before but this time it's for real.  This time it's me telling you, so I suggest you all pay close attention because I won't be warning any of you again.  Get between me and what I want and I promise that I will walk all over you and stamp your bloodied carcasses into dust. The camera slowly zooms in on The Supreme Machine until his steely grey eyes dominate the shot and appear to burn through the lens. I will dominate in PCW just like I did in CCW.  I AM the greatest wrestler of all time and I WILL defeat each and every one of you......BECAUSE I CAN!    .
