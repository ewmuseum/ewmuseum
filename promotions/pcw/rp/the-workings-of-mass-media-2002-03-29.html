The workings of mass media..

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The workings of mass media.. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 29th, 2002 Propaganda. As any true follower of history knows, it may be the most potent force in the world. The Red Army, through careful censorship and propaganda, managed to turn Russia and its satellites into the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics, one of the most tightly run and strict empires in the history of the world. While the United States has traditionally stood for democracy, it has not been immune to using propaganda. During the Civil War, President Abraham Lincoln delivered the Gettysburg Address. While actually doing nothing to free slaves, it sent a powerful message to the world. In short: The U.S. does not condone slavery. Yet, the border states, still in the Union, were practicing slavery. Contradiction. Such is the art of propaganda. Saying one thing....meaning the other, to persuade people of some view or ideaology. And, while the Soviets may have left PCW, one master of propaganda has arrived. Roger Schultz. William S. Powell's campaign manager. You don't get someone like William S. Powell elected without using gallons of propaganda. It becomes quite necessary. And, that is why Roger Schultz is at a Des Moines television studio, the same one William S. Powell tore through in a rage in his first days in Primetime Championship Wrestling. The reason? Quite simply....Roger's here to get a commercial ready. A propaganda commercial, to promote Politically Incorrect....and why they should be tag team champions. He has to hurry though....it's 3:22 PM Iowa time... ::Sitting in a television production room in Des Moines, Iowa, Roger Schultz is beginning to panic. He's having problems figuring out the answer to a rather distressing problem....:: Roger Schultz: Goddamnit all to hell! HOW THE HELL DO I MAKE THESE TWO POLITICAL IDIOTS LOOK LIKE GOOD POTENTIAL PCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS?! ::Schultz sits down, poring over pictures, film, and other media from the political and wrestling careers of Politically Incorrect.:: Roger Schultz: I mean, I love those guys...they're great..but they aren't exactly the nicest sons of b*tches around...I mean, their alliance with Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon has involved the destruction of an old folks home in order to build the Des Moines First Church of Scientology....oh, quick note... ::Roger takes out his Palm Pilot and stylus, and quickly begins scribbling notes down:: Roger Schultz: Groundbreaking ceremony for Scientology Church...April 3rd. I'll have to get Kannon on the line and explain that...may as well make it open invitation too.. ::Schultz manages a smile, and packs the palm pilot into his suit coat.:: Roger Schultz: William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft are consumate politicians. This isn't a bad thing...it just, well, means that their morals are a level lower than that of most people. It's a product of the system really. If people knew how to use recall properly, they could get rid of the idiots populating the system...but, not only do they NOT use it...they have a further problem. There is no such thing as a non-idiot running for political office. ::Schultz sighs loudly:: Roger Schultz: Powell and Lovecraft both mean well.....but it is as they say, absolute power corrupts absolutely. Once in power, it is human nature to crave that power. And Powell has used it...and abused it. In his organization, Lovecraft has used it...and abused it. Why am I telling you this, you may ask? The answer is simple...because almost everyone watching this will have forgotten by later tonight. Human memory and attention span is rather low..we promote violence and hate..we glorify anti-heroes...hey wait! That gives me an actual idea! I know how to promote these two now.... ::Schultz grins happily..as the scene changes to one familar to Politically Incorrect, a limousine. Lovecraft and Powell are on their way to the ARCO Arena, getting ready for the evening's gauntlet match. Both men appear to be getting ready for the match with an ice cold Snapple. NO, THE SHAMELESS SNAPPLE PLUGS WILL NOT GO AWAY. Thank you.:: William S. Powell: Just think Reginald...we're a few hours away from the biggest match of our lives. Reginald V. Lovecraft: I know....on Sabotage we had a huge opportunity....but we blew it, plain and simple. We had The Executioner's Chair ready to fry..and in comes II. In came the goddamn Chef, and his TaliBANG on my partner here... William S. Powell: Bastard blindsides me Reginald! I couldn't see him coming! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Regardless....it does not matter anymore. We have many other teams to go through tonight. For all we know, we could be first out. We could also be the team coming out right before Golden Game. We have to be prepared to wrestle ten minutes, twenty minutes, hell, maybe even an hour if it is necessary. That's what it will take..Powell, can you handle it? William S. Powell: Of course! Why do you ask that? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Because, several of the interns have been telling me you have, shall we say, stamina problems. William S. Powell: I DO NOT!! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Sure you don't......regardless......PCW must prepare for new tag team champions. Because Sunday will be Election Day! William S. Powell: And on Election Day, Politically Incorrect ALWAYS triumphs! ::Fade to the usual...:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically Incorrect!Next PCW Tag Team Champions ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
