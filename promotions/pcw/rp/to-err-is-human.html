To Err Is Human...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Humanity’s not perfect…Not by a long shot… Every frickin day that they exist in is a mistake… From dawn to dusk…fault, error, screw up, “Whoops!” Even what they are successful at they mess up eventually. Just a never-ending cycle of blunders and mishaps…an eternal vaudeville performance for those up high… But don’t worry…no, don’t worry…you can’t correct yourself no matter how much you try to. Failure is in your genetic code…right down to the tiniest chromosome and blood cell. Why do you think that humans grow old…grow sick…feel pain…die? Their bodies physically fail them…just as their mind does daily. And it is all just a matter of time before complete failure delivers humanity into extinction. This Friday coming, a mortal takes on an immortal...human versus Celestial Being…Man against God. Duke…Williams… The Malice Man himself. He’s so successful in PCW…leader of the Malice Crew…The Great Radio Star…PCW legend, respected by allies and foes… Well…maybe not foes… Duke’s got it all… Still…failure’s still running through his veins… =©= The scene begins on a worn down neighborhood somewhere in one of the major US cities. Cars zoom by at a quick speed past a trash cluttered alley. Graffiti creeps along the edges of the dingy red brick building as we slowly move in towards the entrance of the alleyway. =©= Duke Williams is a talker…we all know that. It’s not uncommon to hear his voice gracing a PCW event more than once a night. He’s a commentator for crying out loud…he’s paid to do so. But it’s his mouth that gets him into trouble… Actually…his mouth is the tool that gets him into trouble. The mouth is the physical tool of fault…where the mind is the mental tool of error. It is the DNA of his species itself that brings him to failure. His words are what lead him to rocky situations…it’s hard to say that he chooses his words carefully before vomiting them out. It’s no matter…because even the most moving of speakers…even the most talented of orators… …stutters… =©= Standing at the edge of the alley is a young woman. Japanese descent…with long ice blue and black hair trailing down to her thighs. She looked to be about eighteen or nineteen years old. Her eye catching form was wrapped in tight black vinyl pants, knee high black leather heeled boots tightening around her shapely calves, a light blue PVC halter top, and over that a black mesh tight shirt. She stared down into the shaded alley through light blue reflective lenses, scanning for something or someone. The steel heel of her boot clicked against the asphalt as she entered into the rat’s den. =©= Tired…Worthless…Washed up… …A nothing past prime… Duke Williams? No….no frickin way… But he is seasoned…experienced…meaning he’s been hanging around this business for a long @ss time. That means it’s about time the Malice Man took a lengthy vacation of sorts. To the Void? Nah…not really… Akiyama doesn’t do that anymore…not as much as he used to…Duke has nothing to fear about banishment. Even if the Neo Void God could banish Duke Williams now, there’s no guarantee that with his big mouth he’d be silenced. He prides himself on his vocal capabilities…it’s done so much for him in his life. While Akiyama’s not the one to silence him…the bout that is soon to come should be an indication that sometimes silence is golden…and in this case, silence is everything… =©= She walked slowly, being careful not to tread in waste beset not mentioned. It looked like the trash hadn’t been removed in months. Was that a Christmas tree? Please don’t say that’s a worn out Easter basket in that can… ugh. She, on the other hand, had no response to her environment. Her face stayed in two phases: One, beautiful, and two, emotionless. It was nothing for her to step through that decrepit back alley. The things she did to get the job done…=©= =©= There was a fence at the back of the alley…looks like the end of the line for her. Around the boxes and dumpsters she had found what she was looking for. Her arms hung by her sides as she stared hard into what she pursued. =©= Is Duke even a good guy? It’s hard to tell, really. One would think that good guys had morals…yet Duke seems to have a low level of morals. Infidelity…adultery… drug usage…vulgarity…lewdness…is this what humanity considers to be proper conduct of a good guy figure? A “face”? When Akiyama was around, he at least held a twisted sense of honor, courage, and so on. Duke’s popular status merely proves the error of humanity within themselves. They pick the men they can relate to the most in their grease induced laziness than to actually use their brains and think about things on a logical scale. Duke’s not one to fight a foe like the nVg…he likes to face the ego-masturbating semi-psychotic gorillas instead of intelligent and otherwise strategic foes. Not into the technical style, are we? Too bad…not everyone’s as underprivileged in the wrestling area as Duke Williams… Akiyama’s the thinking man’s fighter…and from Duke’s last appearance, he’s doing some thinking of his own on this match coming up. For once, he makes a good choice on something. He’s said that the ones who dressed in black to make a statement got beat up…jocks made the friends and such. Spoken like a true blockhead jock… shows how much he knows about Akiyama anyway…but as humanity proves yet again…if you aren’t clear on details, make an assumption about what you don’t know. If you need to know…Malice Man…Akiyama knows where you’re coming from…he was surrounded by jocks in his youth…when he was on his school’s judo and wrestling team. Not really any of your business though…but it is Akiyama’s business to make sure that you realize the error of your ways…your human ways… =©= Just like he was the last time he was seen…same shiny black vinyl pants with the multitude of zippers crisscrossing all over his legs…same black tape running up his hands and forearms… same black see through mesh shirt too. One could only wonder how long he’s been here. =©= =©= The woman squatted down next to him and stared into his face. His eyes were closed…and his face was in a serene appearance. Duke Williams had called him ugly…funny how he was making facial judgments on a male. She traced her ice blue fingernails over his cheek. Then she trailed down his throat down to his chiseled chest…and stopped. She had felt something that shouldn’t be there. She felt it over and over…and made mental note of what it could be. Strange…=©= =©= Then she grabbed his black nailed hand and held it. It was warm…another thing that drew her right eyebrow to rise. She let his hand plop onto the ground as she stood back up to her full six foot height. He lay slumped against the fence, his head lowered, his hair mussed and hanging over his face…his ponytail coming undone and lounging over his shoulder. What a waste…such potential should be put to good use…=©= =©= And it was her job to make sure he used it…=©= It’s times like this that Akiyama’s position over humanity is nothing more than great. Why would anyone want to be a part of this flawed species is more confusing than Duke trying to decipher a dark gimmick promo. Don’t take it the wrong way…this will be a great match…this will be an excellent match…the Neo Void God versus the Malice Man in the squared circle… Duke’s test of his talents to see if he really is back into tip top shape…the same goes for Akiyama as well… …A lot’s happened to him since Supremacy…he’s not the same as he used to be… “I don’t dig that dark crap…” Don’t worry about it…Akiyama’s the last of a dying breed…but that makes him just as dangerous…that means he’s fighting to survive… And as you should know by now…survivability takes priority. Duke Williams…the essence of humanity… The essence…of error… So says the Divine Prophetess… =©= Her fingers slipped the sunglasses off her face, finally giving us a clear view of her eyes…and afterwards, you’d ask her to put them back on. =©= =©= Pure…black…darkness…=©= =©= Her ice blue lips curled into a small grin as she peered down into the nVg. =©= Cyclonica: Rise and shine, Neo Void God…we’ve got ourselves a Malice Man to maul. =©=
