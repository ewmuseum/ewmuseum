He's in a better place...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Fusion #1, Turmoil #2 -=- Dedicated to my grandfather…God bless his soul-=- It is true… We are responsible for many a lives taken from this realm. But even so…sometimes natural order does the job for us. Natural order…time… It’s all a matter of time… That applies not only to our victory…but it also applies to the end of this life. When you’re immortal, you don’t think about the possibility of death…it’s like folklore to beings like us. A metaphorical boogeyman to keep us in our places… But we fear it not…we wield it as a tool against the weak. Death as our punishment is one thing…but death as a punishment to someone who did not deserve it…is a different matter altogether. I felt his soul rising from this realm and moving onto the next. Free…without burden from this world anymore. It was almost…serene, peaceful and harmonic to my cold demeanor. But the bliss that it showered my persona sent me a dream…a dream of unique proportions. In that dream I walked sterile halls of a hospital, with nurses and doctors rushing back and forth to tend to the critical lives of their patients. Then I heard a beep…long, droll, steady…a droning wail crying out the end of a life. F L A T S C A N The electronic wail of the heart monitor echoed through my mind, my psyche amplifying the sound to roar and shatter the inside of my head. My eyes closed, hands clutching my temples as I looked up to the sky…and screamed in rage. The continuous beep melted away… A ringing telephone took its place. I found myself standing against the wooden railing of a balcony. I was in the exterior corridor of an apartment building. The phone came from the inside of the apartment I stood next to, but I could hear it like it was right in front of me. Looking inside the window, I found a young black man in khakis and a gray turtleneck answer the phone. His voice was low… meekness his guise. As he heard the news, I could feel his essence take a change… For the better…or for the worse… I cannot tell that myself. The scene whirled around me, and I found myself holding the phone. The receiver was pressed to my ear, and on the other end I heard a familiar voice. “Sugu raku ni shite yaru...” I will soon end your pain… The phone fell from my hand as I turned around. I found the wispy haired black man standing in the doorway to his bedroom, his head lowered as he stared down at his palms held out before him. I knew mortals dealt with death in many different fashions…but how come the way this mortal was taking it was affecting me so hard? He disappeared around the corner of his room. I followed, and found him staring in the bathroom mirror, his blank emotionless face looking eerie and ghostly. But it was not his reflection I saw from him… In the mirror was Inphino Blitz. He matched expressions perfectly. Then…something rose up within the man. Once tranquil, his entire body surged up, his muscles flexing as he shook the walls with a tremendous roar. Insanity flared within his wide eyes as his hands were stretched out at his sides. And the reflection in the mirror was once again different… I saw my father…Akiyama… The Neo Void God roared in a similar fashion in the mirror reflection. I watched the man go back and forth from peace to chaos…Inphino to Akiyama…Aura to Void…over and over and over for hours. He cleared his mind… only to flush more rage through it. He stilled his body… only to explode forward with martial arts violence. He silenced his voice…only to break out in crazed laughter. This man’s mind…this man’s soul…they were evolving… All for the death of a loved one… Finally…with every bit of energy spent, he collapsed on the ground. I watched as his body heaved for breath, sucking it in like a sponge took up water. He looked so helpless in that position…unguarded…weak… Why couldn’t I kill him then? Then I asked myself…what reason do I have to do so? I found myself kneeling in front of the man as he sat up on his knees and glared at the ground. I placed a hand on his shoulder and nodded slowly at him, though he looked straight through me. I wished not to harm this person… We may be monsters…we may be demons to you people… But we are only those beasts to people who draw it from us. “Sorrow is a dark serpent of despair against the heart, mind, and soul. How you befriend the Black Dragon…is the dilemma I place upon your shoulders.” Cold lips pressed into his forehead as I rose up over him. I know not from where the words came from…but I felt they were comforting enough to aid the man in his time of need. I watched the man rise up, his bushy hair drooping over his eyes slightly as his head began to rise from its lowered position fixed on the ground. His eyes…they were hollow…empty… But his lips…they were pasted with lunacy. He was determined… He was BALANCED I found the environment bleeding around me…and I watched next at a higher altitude from a apartment balcony as the man, jaded by a family member passing, walked out into the streets a new man. Reborn…more than human, but less than Celestial… On my left, Inphino Blitz crouched on the wooden railing, peering down silently at the man walk down the cold night street, cars parked along his path. To my right I felt my father’s presence. He slipped an arm around me, embracing me lovingly and safely. Inphino was his sorrow…he was, and forever will be the Void Element of Sorrow. He brought peace. Akiyama was his anger…the Celestial monster possessing his mind and body with his will. He brought chaos. Me…Cyclonica…I was his stability. Amidst sadness and madness…I brought harmony between the two. The three of us…together…silent…peaceful…. brought together by one being… “Marquette…” Then I awoke… I was staring up at the moon, from my bedroom window in the castle. This realm came back to me… Father had to deal with men Turmoil and Fusion… all men who had their reason for fighting against him. It would be up to them…whether or not they want to wait for natural order…time… Or the Neo Void God… So says the Divine Prophetess… Rest in peace, grandfather…
