'...God I hate Asians...' Corey's Secret Weapon

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: I don&#039;t know ::Our scene opens at an airport, as the camera fades in we see an Asian man rolling his luggage out of the arrivals gate. He searches around for something or somebody and eventually finds it when his eyes fall upon a woman, also Asian. The man leaves his luggage cart and runs towards the Asian woman, the two meet and embrace.:: '....God I hate Asians' ::Our camera swings around to 'Controversial' Corey Williams, with one foot on a bench in front of him and the other on the floor, he leans forward with a disgusted look on his face.:: Corey: Look at them, making 'ooh'ing and 'ahh'ing sounds, acting all happy as if there is race superior to them. It makes me sick how some people can avoid the truth, avoid the brutal and controversial truth. Ignoring the ABC factor, the scientifically proven fact that says that all men of Australian, American, British and Canadian descent are smarter and athletically superior. ::Corey flexes his bicep and inspects it with great interest.:: Corey: I mean, that's the reason why Asians have to invent all those machines, coz they're neither smart nor strong enough to do things themselves. ::It's so strange how Corey's logic works, just as you're about to open your mouth to argue a point against him, he argues that very same point to HIS advantage.:: Corey: And they're not very good at making things in the first place! The only thing they're worse at than driving cars, is MAKING cars. And if anything you ever own breaks, ever, you can guarantee that if you turn it over and read the label on the back it will say 'Made in Taiwan.' They're a waste of space to tell you the truth, these Asians should be replaced with Americans, decent, hardworking Americans. I mean, for Chase's sake, most Asians can't even speak English! What's the point of life if you can't even put a goddamn sentence together? ::Corey watches in disgust as the man goes back to get his luggage cart and he and the woman walk off, arm in arm. Our camera again focuses on the arrival gate, and we see the last of the people coming through, a woman this time. She has bright red/orange hair and is wearing a woman's business suit. She pushes her luggage cart forward and her eyes search for someone, much like the Asian man. She finally finds who she was looking for and our cameras swing around to reveal that that person is actually Corey Williams himself, grinning evilly. The woman begins to approach CCW, Corey has had only one purpose for women in the past, and this woman is too ugly for it.:: Corey: Ahh, here comes my secret weapon now..... 'Corey! Good to see you again.' ::The lady is, of course, Ms Pauline Hanson, the World's most controversial politician whose One Nation party invented the policies 'Asians go home' and 'No hand outs for Abos' which for some reason are still yet to become law in Australia.:: Corey: You too Ms Hanson, I trust your flight went well? Pauline: Nah, it was f*cking shockin' ::Corey is a little taken aback by the distinguished politician's language, little does he know that she was formerly a Fish and Chip shop owner.:: Corey: Well I -uh, I'm sorry to hear that. Let's go then, shall we? ::Corey holds his arm and Pauline takes hold, the two walk off arm in arm.:: Pauline: So, tell me about this friend of yours. Corey: He's name is Isawa Miyagawa and he ain't no friend of mine, Isawa Miya-frikken-ching-chong-gawa! And until Chase and I happened, he was the World Champ, of course he didn't actually earn the belt in the first place, after all, what Asian has EVER earnt anything? But when he was World Champion, he was a disgrace to the very people that a World Champ should represent, America. Pauline: So he's of Asian decent? Corey: Don't let the bastard fool you like he does the fans as he goes on about this Japo-American crap. Japo-American? There's no such damn thing! And when I look in to his eyes, those sly, slanty eyes of his, I can tell that he's 100% Asian. Just another wanna-be lying his ass off to get to the top of this company. Just another try-hard trying to be as cool as the guys who reinvented the word cool, New Breed. So to answer your question Pauline, yes he's an Asian, a nip, a noodle-sucker, a gook, a goddamn small-c*cked, slanty-eyed motherf*cker! Pauline: Yeh, I've noticed that you guys seem to be having the same problem that we Australians are having, Asian Invasion. And I'm thinking of starting a One Nation Party branch up here in the States, whaddya think? Corey: It sounds like a 'bonza' idea Pauline. You know, all I hear is people talking about the War Against Terrorism, Osama Bin Laden this and Osama Bin Laden that, but everyone is ignoring the problem brewing at our very own shores. The War Against Asians is coming, and when it does, we're gonna need people like you on our side Pauline. ::Corey and Pauline exit the airport through some sliding doors.:: Pauline: I just hope I'm not too late. ::Pauline looks around nervously at a bunch of Asian youths hanging out out the front of the airport.:: Pauline: So, where are you parked? ::Corey laughs.:: Corey: Why, where all PCW HEADLINERS are parked.... ::Corey points to a large black limo right out the front of the door:: Pauline: You mean you get this for free just for working at PCW? Corey: Actually.........no ::Corey opens the front door of the car and pulls out a black chauffeur's cap, which he places upon his own head.:: Corey: ....I'm doing this for a little extra money. Pauline: .....Oh. ::There is a short awkward pause, for any sane person would expect Corey Williams, a PCW HEADLINER, to be making millions and millions of dollars, instead of less money than Jeremy Johnson, who has wrestled one match for PCW in this entire year.:: Corey: Well, hop on in. ::Corey opens the passenger's door for Pauline and she steps in. Corey moves back to the front door and gets in, closing the door behind him. We hear the engine of the limo rev up and then the sleek black limo slowly begins to roll away. Our cameras cut inside.:: Pauline: So, is there anything else I need to know about this Asian. Corey: Well, he has a wife, she's Russian. Recently she was put into hospital when Boda gave her the 'Bounced Czech' onto a car, kinda ironic really, of Isawa Miyagawa and his wife, I thought Miya was the one most likely to be involved in a car accident. I guess it was his fault anyway, he couldn't protect his wife from Boda, mind you, I'm not surprised; the Japanese were just as bad at protecting Hiroshima! Pauline: Corey.... that was cold..... keep up the good work. Corey: And what’s with the big fuss anyway? I mean, I can see why he cares about his pregnant wife so much, but don’t most Asians flush their babies down the toilet anyway? Pauline: Damn Corey, you’re on a roll. Corey: What can I say, (Corey shrugs) I'm good at what I do. ::Corey grins and raises his eyebrows. We see the car slowly come to a halt at an intersection, once the car is stopped Corey takes his hands off the wheels and turns to the camera.:: Corey: Miyagawa, I'll admit it.... You Asians, despite being inferior, are good at some things, like your video games. But this Tuesday ain't gonna be no video game, it's gonna be all too real. Because this Tuesday when you and Boda come up against THE single most dominating stable in ALL of PCW, you will, like most Asians, come up a little........ short. ::Corey bursts out into laughter at his own controversial joke.:: Corey: BWAHA! Short! God I just-- SCREECH! SLAM! ::The screen and the limo are shaken, we see Corey and Ms Hanson thrown forward, Corey hitting his head on the dash board. Corey sits up, rubbing where he hit his head.:: Corey: DAMNIT! That's gonna come out of my pay! ::Corey gets out of the limo and turns to inspect the back of it. Our camera zooms in on it and there we see a pink Hyundai slammed into the back of it, with a scared and confused Asian man sitting in it ranting something in a foreign language. Corey sighs and wipes the blood off of his forehead with his right hand.:: Corey: .....God I hate Asians.... !The End!
