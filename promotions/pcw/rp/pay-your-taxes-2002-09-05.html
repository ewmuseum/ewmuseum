Pay Your Taxes

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/09/02 Static. “Attention PEONS!” Our shot cuts to the face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon. His long, Strawberry Blonde hair is tied back in a ponytail and he’s wearing a pair of thick rimmed glasses. Xavier : “To many of you, calculating your taxes can be a daunting task.. Especially as most of you have the cranial capacity of a brain damaged Laama...” The camera pulls out to show he is dressed in a White and Blue pinstriped suit with Red braces... Doing his best to look like a member of the I.R.S. Xavier : “But, with the help of this informational video, I will help you fill out your Front Office Tax self-assessment form...” The CEO holds up a booklet to the camera for a few seconds before placing it neatly back down on his desk. Xavier : “Now maybe you’re wondering WHY you have to pay your Front Office Tax?” He scratches his head in deliberate bad acting to put the point across. Xavier : “Now, I could go on to tell you all the resources that this Tax will pay for, but instead I’ll just say you pay it or we’ll fire you.” The camera zooms out once more to show Kannon sitting at his huge Mahogany CEO’s desk and that Ellie~!, with her hair tied up, dressed neatly in a skirt suit with thick rimmed glasses and a clipboard in hand, is standing beside him. Xavier : “Now, with the help of my beautiful assistant, Ellie~!, I will show you how to go about filling your form in....” Ellie~! tries to resist doing a little pose for the camera but gives in. Xavier : “For this exercise, Ellie~! shall be playing the role of your average PCW wrestler... Ready, my little Poon Puppy?” With these words, the little prep hunches her shoulders and screws up her face.. Doing her best Neanderthal impression. Eleanor : “I’m... Like, SO a PCW wrestler.... ANTI-YAY ME!!!!” Kannon slides his glasses down his nose and looks into the camera with his Green eyes. Xavier : “Now, first you will have to fill in the section marked NAME found in the top left corner...” Ellie~! picks up the form from the desk and stares at it, trying to find the section.... She turns it around then opens it up trying to find it as Kannon sighs and continues. Xavier : “Now, you can either fill in your WRESTLER name or your REAL name... Whichever you think you can spell...” Beside him Ellie~! finally finds the “Name” section and holds it up in front of her, pointing to it merrily. Xavier : “Now... Being the generous people we are, the Front Office has implemented a “Familly or Couples Tax Break”, where those eligible will only have to pay 38.75%...” Ellie~! looks around the form again and still can’t find it... After a few more seconds of hunting she grabs a pen and writes “THING” on the front page before turning the booklet around and pointing to it, proud of her initiative. Xavier : “So be sure to fill that section in.... For example, if I had to fill it in I would write Ellie~! and tick “Married”. While Chris Chase would write Corey Williams and tick “Life Partner”. Kannon chuckles as Ellie~! pretends to fill in the form, waving her pan all around the booklet. Xavier : “Next, you fill in your Salary... This is where I sense the biggest problems will be found...” His wife goes back to her wrestler impression, scratching her head and looking idiotically dumbfounded... Even more so than normal. Xavier : “To fill in Salary... You look at the number in the right hand box of your pay cheque, the one next to the $ sign...” Ellie~! now picks up a cheque from the desk and looks blankly at it from all angles.
