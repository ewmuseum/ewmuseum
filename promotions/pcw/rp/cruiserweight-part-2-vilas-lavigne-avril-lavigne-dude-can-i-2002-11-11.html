Cruiserweight?! - Part 2 - Vilas Lavigne. Avril Lavigne? Dude, Can I Date Your Sister?

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 11 [The poster of Avril Lavigne will make any horny man to stop and take a gander. Her blue eyes radiating that intangible 'it' that some people have. Her growling smile showing that rebellious nature that she shows so well. She's a beautiful girl that is making passerby's stop to take a look, and that's just her freakin' poster] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I'm in love, man. [Cowabunga Connection's 'Heavy Metal' Harry stands in front of the poster with his bloodshot eyes in amazement. Many babes catch his eye and are certainly far out, he does live in Calfornia after all. But for some reason Avril Lavigne is different, very different. The way she wears her clothes, her style, her personality. It's coming through perfectly to Harry right now. She's beautiful. She's perfect. She should be his. They would be the perfect couple. Two rockers on a road, not knowing where it's taking them. She's selling out arena's with her far out music, while Harry sells out arena's with his far out wrestling. It'd be perfect. Avril will be his, man] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: She's pretty hot, dude. And she's legal. [Harry's brother, Tommy, has always been there. Through the bad times and the good times The Tubular One has made sure that nothing bad happened to Harry. They only found out that they were brothers a few months ago, but they knew they were brothers the minute they became best friends all those years back. That's why they are the best cohessive tag team in PCW, and the best it's ever seen] Harry: She's so far out, man. [Harry stands in awe, wearing his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. Tommy stands next to him, looking from the poster to his brother. Tommy wears his Hawaiian button down shirt, khaki cargo shorts, and black sandals. His blond hair is spiked up today, trying something different. Ever since he's recovered from sexual addiction he's found himself trying gnarly new things. With his hair. With his surfboard. Now he's going to try new things in the wrestling ring. Could it be for better or worse? Only time will tell. The man who is going to be changing his style in the ring is Harry. He recently was entered in the cruiserweight title tournament. Harry is no cruiserweight. Not even close] TTT: Dude, I just figured something out. [Harry doesn't care. He is only focused on Avril. Yummy] TTT: The dude your facing in the first round of the tournament is named Vilas Lavigna. Lavigna. Lavigne. DUDE. That's totally weird, dude! [Now Harry is paying attention] Harry: Are you saying what I think your saying, man? TTT: Totally, compadre. [Harry stares at Avril's beautiful face, tilting his head to the right like a little puppy would do] Harry: Then Avril Lavigne is the sister of Vilas Lavigna, man. TTT: Dude... [Downtown Los Angeles is totally gnarly, dudes. Who wouldn't want to be there? Yeah, there is a lot of smog and pollution but it's one of the most excellent trademarks of the United States. Calfornia kicks major booty. The Cowabunga Connection makes their way down Main Street in the downtown district. Tommy is searching for the new bodacious gym that just opened up as Harry is thinking of how he can ask his opponent, Vilas Lavigna, for his sister's hand in marriage] Harry: Do you think I should immediately ask him when we get to the arena, man? Or should I ask him after I win? [Tommy shakes his head, knowing full well that the name similarities between the two is most weird, but Harry is really taking this thing too far. There could be a lot of compadres named Lavigne in the world, it doesn't necessarily mean that they are related, but it is weird that they happen to run into that gnarly poster just as Harry is about to take on that Vilas dude. It's like it's some kind of totally bodacious sign sent from the head dude above. Or maybe they have both gone completely mental] TTT: Dude, you have never met Avril in your life. I think that you two should date before you go rushing into marriage, compadre. Harry: No way, man. We were meant for each other. I can give her everything that she's ever wanted. TTT: What does she want, dude? [Harry blanks out for a few moments, staring at his brother with nothing behind is eyes] Harry: Ummm, well....ME, MAN!! [Tommy shrugs, already knowing that's what he was going to say anyway. What else could he say? Avril Lavigne already probably has everything she wants. It's not like they have hung out with the bodacious babe before. Great!! Now Tommy is thinking about what a long night of Yahtzee playing would be like with Avril Lavigne!! He is a dude in recovery from sexual addiction. He can't be thinking like that all the time. Yes, it's human nature, but it will eventually engulf his soul making him go back to his addicted ways. It's not that hard to do when your an elligible, good looking bachelor like Tommy] Harry: Can you imagine what it would be like to make love to a woman like that, man? [The words cut through Tommy's heart like sharpened razors. Freshly sharpened at that. No, wait. Not sharpened razors. More like a hot poker used by a large black man that stands at 7 foot 1 inch tall. Yeah, it feels just like that] TTT: Dude, I can't imagine stuff like that. It will only drive me to go back to my totally heinous sexual ways again. Harry: Oh yeah, man. Sorry. [They stop at a very busy intersection, waiting for the 'Walk' signal to pop up. It's the corner of Main Street and 5th Avenue. Such shops as Janga's World of Spiders, Butterscotch Heaven, and The Gap line the block. Tommy wants to stop in Janga's Spider shop to check out the new tarantula's that they got in, but the spiders will just freak Harry out. He'll come back another day...maybe tomorrow when Harry sleeps in until 4 in the afternoon] TTT: Dude, this cruiserweight thing is not going to be easy. Harry: I know, man. TTT: These compadres may be a lot smaller than you, but they can probably run circles around you. Your going to have to hit the gym and get your agility up to a major high, dude. I'm not talking about just getting on the treadmill either, which you think is agility training. I'm talking about getting into some most EXCELLENT stretching techniques and learning some totally bodacious martial arts. Harry: Martial arts, man? Like Jackie Chan? TTT: Totally! Harry: I'll be able to jump around like that crazy Asian man...ummm...man? TTT: Without a doubt, my headbanging bro. Harry: I bet Avril wouldn't be able to resist me one bit if I could jump around like Jackie Chan, man. Not only would I be a far out rocker, but I'd be an athletic dude who could protect her. I could protect her from her totally non-far out brother, Vilas. TTT: Dude...I wasn't thinking of it like that, but, sure, it could be used like that. [Another busy intersection is approached by the most dynamic duo since Batman and Robin. Main Street and 7th. The home of Freaky Frank's House of Love, for all those sexual toys needed for the ladies. Starbucks Coffee, the owner a sexual addict himself. Then there is Gerhardt's Gym, the gnarly place that Tommy has been searching for. It's not large at all, in fact, it's only about 4 feet by 4 feet. What kind of heinous gym is this? The newspaper said that it was state of the art, concentrating on agility and stamina. This was supposed to be Harry's salvation. The training to get him ready for his cruiserweight action. Madness, dude] Harry: Is this the place, man? TTT: I guess so. Kind of bogus, don't you think? Harry: Yeah, man. [The two men enter the glass door. Tommy pulls the door and it doesn't open] TTT: It's locked, dude. [Harry pushes on it and the door immediately opens] TTT: Dude...it's magic. [Harry points at the small sticker above to handle] Harry: Push, man. [Tommy shrugs off the stupidity moment and enters the tiny gym. The green carpet is soft, you could probably bounce around on it like a bodacious trampoline. There are posters up of pressure points and judo throws, but other than that the place looks empty. It kind of has a musty smell, like someone cut the cheese or something. Pretty bogus] Harry: Did you cut one, man? TTT: No way, compadre. If I'm going to let one loose your going to know about it. [The two men explore around the place, not that there is much to explore really. Harry begins looking at the pressure point poster, taking in the art of making a man give up just from one touch. Harry notices one point just behind the shoulder blade. He uses his right hand and places it in the spot...then squeezes] Harry: OW, MAN!! [Tommy rushes over to his brother and tag team partner] TTT: Dude, whats wrong? Harry: Pressure...point...man. TTT: Dude, just let go. [Harry lets go of the point and immediately has the feeling in his left shoulder again. He sighs with relief] Harry: Thanks, man. [Then a door whips open from the back of the gym. A very muscularly cut man walks out wearing a green wifebeater and green workout pants. The guy has to be almost seven feet tall. His long black hair is tied back in a pony tail, accenting his face, which Tommy thinks isn't a bodacious thing because the dude is most ugly. He looks like one of those totally heinous trolls from the Lord Of The Rings] Trainer: WHATS UP!!! [Tommy and Harry are taken back by the man's enthusiasm in his craptacular greeting] TTT: Hey, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. Trainer: ARE YOU LOOKING FOR TOP NOTCH TRAINING?!?! TTT: Damn skippy, compadre. Trainer: YOU LOOK IN FINE SHAPE, SIR!! [Tommy smiles, flattered by the comment] TTT: It's not for me, dude. It's for my tag team partner. [The Trainer looks Harry over, and he doesn't look impressed at all] Trainer: LOOKS LIKE I HAVE MY WORK CUT OUT FOR ME!!! TTT: Dude, Harry is in most excellent shape. We just need to get him more agile. He's competeing in that totally gnarly Cruiserweight Tournament for PCW. [The Trainer looks extremely shocked] Trainer: CRUISERWEIGHT?!?! [He pauses for a few moments] Trainer: CRUISERWEIGHT?!! [He pulls up Harry's Twisted Sister T-shirt to reveal the pale gut that Harry is actually proud of] Trainer: HE LOOKS LIKE A POT BELLY PIG!!! [Harry is offended] Harry: Hey, man!! [The Trainer pushes Harry against the wall, right next to the list of judo throws. The Trainer stands there for a few moments in a Kung Fu stance, maybe harnessing his chi or something. Who knows what this crazy compadre is doing] Trainer: GIVE ME YOUR RIGHT LEG!! Harry: No, man. Trainer: GIVE IT TO ME!!! [The Trainer's veins bulge out of his neck and his face begins to become extremely red. Harry then slowly lifts up his right leg, the trainer seems to take it gently] Trainer: NO PAIN, NO GAIN!!! [The trainer then immediately pushes the leg back to Harry's shoulder. A loud crack is heard, followed by the agony of Harry] Harry: YEEAARGHHHHHHHHHHH, MAN!!!! [Tommy's face contorts in agony and he didn't even feel anything] TTT: Dude...do it for Avril. [Fade to Black]
