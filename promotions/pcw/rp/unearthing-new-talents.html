Unearthing new talents!

Date: not entered OOC: This is loosely based on a true story of my idiotic ways :) [Scene opens in the Sony Music Studio where Flaymin and Michelle Branch have been recording their upcoming duet.Flaymin sits in the production room, gazing intently at all the flashing lights and switches on the production table.He reaches over to press his button only to have his hand slapped away by Michelle] Flaymin: What was that for? Michelle: I know all about your troubles with expensive equipment.Do you honestly think I’m going to let you screw up a $20,000 Production Table? [In response, Flaymin mumbles underneath his breath] Flaymin: Bitch. Michelle: What was that? Flaymin: I said… you must be rich. [Michelle raises an eyebrow and then shakes her head] Michelle: You need to cheer up a little.The music industry isn’t all wham, bam thankyou ma’am- it’s a slow process. In the meantime, why don’t you grab a beer from the mini-fridge behind you? [Flaymin’s eyes boggle open at the mere mention of beer.His head swings rapidly around to face the mini-fridge only to hear a loud ‘cracking’ sound emanating from his neck] Michelle: What on earth was that? Flaymin: I think my neck muscles just cracked. [Michelle looks at Flaymin with a surprised look on her face] Michelle: I never knew you could crack your neck muscles. Flaymin: I never even knew I had neck muscles. [Flaymin attempts to repeat this remarkable feat one more time.So once again, he swiftly swings his neck to one side only to hear another ‘crack’ sound] Flaymin: Now this is cool! [Michelle hops off her chair and tries to do the same thing] Michelle: Alright, show me how it’s done. Flaymin: Sit down you crazy fool, not anyone can crack their neck muscles.Do you know how long it took me to master the art of neck crackin’? Michelle: You just started doing it a minute ago! Flaymin: That’s not the point! Michelle: Then what is the point? [Flaymin pauses] Flaymin: What are we talking about again? [Michelle sigh’s and walks over to Flaymin] Michelle: Come on, quit fooling around and show me how its done! Flaymin: Right.All you need to do, really, is just spin your head really quick] [Michelle nods her head.She then stands in the centre of the room and closes her eyes to concentrate. Suddenly she swings her head around violently, only to have her body wobble around in circles until she uncontrollably crashes into the wall, banging her head against the hard brick wall] Flaymin: All this time I’ve known you; never did you mention you were a head banger! [Michelle winces in pain and grabs her head.Slowly, trickles of blood to seep out of her forehead] Michelle: Oh my god!I’m bleeding!!Call an ambulance!!Now!!Quick!!Now!!Ambulance!!Now! Flaymin: Quit your yapping and let me have a look at it. [Flaymin inspects the gash on Michelle’s forehead and grins] Flaymin: Congratulations, on behalf of the deranged, mentally challenged and physically abnormal I now declare you officially hardcore! But hey, let me demonstrate my neck cracking abilities just one more time for you. [Flaymin calmly tilts his head over to one side to unleash yet another deadly cracking sound] Flaymin: I’m starting to enjoy this.Here, I’ll do it again! [This time Flaymin snaps his head backwards, once again unleashing another vicious cracking sound] Michelle: My turn!My turn! [Michelle, wipes the blood off her forehead and stands in the middle of the room once again] Michelle: If I end up flying towards the wall, make sure you stop me this time. Flaymin: Gotcha. [Michelle rolls her shoulders forward and then backwards to warm up a little. Suddenly, she tries to crack her neck once again.This time she trips backwards and slams her head on the production table behind her.Michelle falls to the ground and clutches her head in pain. Flaymin drops the beer in his hand and dashes over. He steps over the fallen body of the injured Michelle Branch and looks at the damaged Production Table] Flaymin: The table!!!You just damaged a $20,000 table!! [Angered by Flaymn’s lack of compassion, Michelle grabs a hold of Flaymin’s leg and digs her teeth into shin] Flaymin: Aaaaaaaargggghhh!!! [Flaymin shakes his leg aggressively to loosen the grip of Michelle’s fangs. However, Flaymin also loses his footing and comes crashing down on the $20,000 production table behind him.This time, the table shatters and comes crumbling down to the floor. Shocked, Michelle looks at Flaymin…..Flaymin stares right back….] Flaymin: It fell…. It broke…. I wasn’t here. Michelle: Ditto. [Michelle raises her head and listens closely to the approaching footsteps down the hallway] Michelle: Quick, someone’s coming! [Michelle, gets up off the floor and makes her way to the door. Flaymin does likewise, only to fall down in a heap on the ground clutching his neck] Michelle: Come on!!We have to go!! Flaymin: My neck!Its all stiff and I can’t move it.DAMN THAT NECK MUSCLE CRACKING!! [Michelle looks at the door and then looks back at the ailing Flaymin.She pauses for a moment to consider her options] Michelle: Sorry, you’re on your own! [Michelle darts out of the room, as Flaymin slumps to the floor holding his damaged neck] Flaymin: Someone call a chiropractor!!!!!! [Scene fades out]
