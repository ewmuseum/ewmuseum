Just Another Day

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/03/02 Gold and Glory sure was a night to remember. New champions were crowned, blood was shed, two lovers were united in holy matrimony, and a PCW fan favorite had died. Though it wasn't one of the best things on the card, Kramer Wild Wing had achieved something so little people had thought possible. He had beat all odds to become the new Extreme Champion! In the end it was down to him and The Multiple Man, and due to a lucky fall through the roof of a trailer, Kramer got the pin. Of course many people say the win was undeserved considering it was sheer luck that he landed on TMM. Even Kramer believes it was a lucky turn of events, but he will prove himself when he defends it. Now in just a day he gets to face John Crichton for the Television Title. What happens if Kramer succeeds in beating him? Well he becomes a double champion, which is extremely hard to do in PCW, and he has one of the best weeks in his life! The scene opens up in what looks to be someone's back yard. The birds are chirping away, and the sun is beating down on yet another hot day in Florida. The thing that really catches are eyes, is the gigantic swimming pool in the yard. It has to be at least ten feet in diameter and eight feet deep. The camera changes its position and is now directly over the swimming pool. We see the faint silhouette of someone swimming near the bottom, but we can't make out who it is. Suddenly the person comes up a bit further in the water, and we see a huge tattoo that reads 'Wild' (if you don't remember he got it after a long night of partying a few months back). Instantly we know it's Kramer.....but since when does he have a pool? With his air supply running low, he swims to the surface and peeks his head out gasping for air. He swims to the edge and climbs out of the pool onto the deck. He drips over everywhere as his bright blue swim trunks stick to him like glue. He takes a seat in a lawn chair and sprawls out, trying to make the best of this humid day. A few minutes pass by of peaceful bliss, until it is ruined by the sound of Jason running up the steps. Jason: Kramer, you have some people on your porch wanting to talk with you. Kramer: About what? Jason: What do I look like? Your fricken servant? Get off of your butt and go ask them yourself. Kramer stands up and looks at Jason with a sneer on his face, upset that he doesn't get a chance to relax. Ever since he won at Gold and Glory everyone and their brother have been calling him. Before Kramer leaves he looks to Jason who has his back turned towards him. Jason walks over to the edge of the pool and peers over the water. Kramer gets a huge smile on his face as he rushes over and knocks Jason in which causes a huge splash! Jason resurfaces and swims to the edge of the pool to climb out. Jason: That wasn't the least bit funny Kramer. Kramer: Oh believe me, it was! Kramer walks inside through the back door, grabs a towel and dries himself off as he makes his way to the front. Before opening the door he peeks out through the window to see about ten people all crammed at his doorstep. He lets out a long sigh as he pulls the door open. As soon as he does he is blinded by the flashing of cameras. Reporter 1: How does it feel to win at Gold and Glory? Reporter 2: Do you know anything about Juan Borro's death? Reporter 3: Any sex scandals happening behind closed doors of PCW? Kramer takes a step back but realizes it isn't the best of ideas, as the reporters take a step forward. Not wanting them all to come trampling in his home, he steps forward and blocks their paths. All of the question asking and camera flashing continues. Finally Kramer speaks up as he has had just about all he can take. Kramer: Stop asking me questions!! Don't you people have someone else more important to bother like Xavier Kannon or Snow? Reporter 1: Yes, but they won't answer our phone calls. And the last time we showed up at their doors, the most unspeakable things happened. Kramer feels a little sympathetic for the reporters as he realizes it must be a tough job bothering the hell out of people. The noise has miraculously gone down, but the microphones are still raised waiting to hear from Kramer. Kramer: First I feel good about winning at Gold and Glory. It has been about seven months since I last held a title, and it just feels good to have some gold around my waist. Second, I have no clue what happened with Borro unfortunately. I just feel sorry he had to go out like that. Third, I know of no sex scandals. Besides no one ever tells me anything anyway so I am always left in the dark. Finally, if I win on Turmoil, and I add another belt around my waist, I don't want any of you coming around here! The last thing I need are you nuisances to society, coming up and bugging me all of the friggin time. Honestly it can drive a man insane! Kramer stops his ranting and raving and closes the door as the questions start up again. He makes sure to lock the door, and he pulls the blinds down as the reporters try to peek in through the windows. He walks back outside thinking about what he had said. He knows deep down inside he likes the attention from the reporters, but when they come to your house and constantly call you, you get a bit upset with them. As he walks back outside he sees Jason sitting in his lawn chair. Kramer looks at him for a second and then dives hit first into the pool. He comes back up and turns to his back and just lets the water move him around. Jason: Finally got rid of the paparazzi ay? Kramer: No. They are still out there. Jason chuckles a bit. Jason: Oh I forgot to thank you earlier. Kramer: Thank me for what? Jason: Buying this swimming pool. Now we can have pool parties and invite the ladies. Kramer: Jason let's be honest. What ladies do you know that you could invite besides your bald headed girlfriend and your mom? Jason: How do the other wrestlers get celebs to hang out with them? Kramer: I thought you had connections? I mean you are an actor. Jason: Eh......I have an idea....we should move out to Hollywood and then we would see stars practically everyday! Kramer: Try no for one hundred Alex. ((pauses)) I just realized something. We see each other every day and you are always over at my house. You know people are going to start talking if they haven't already. The last thing I need is for someone to think I'm gay. Jason: I'm gay right now. Kramer's eyes bulge as he jerks in the water to face Jason. Jason: I meant the happy kind of gay you moron! Kramer looks a little relieved as his mind goes back to wondering about what will happen on Turmoil. The scene slowly fades to black.
