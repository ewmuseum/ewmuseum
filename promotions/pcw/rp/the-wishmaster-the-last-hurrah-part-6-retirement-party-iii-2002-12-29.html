The Wishmaster --- The Last Hurrah, Part 6 (Retirement Party III)

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/29/02 Back to The End's Retirement Party, where Dante Jackson, Vanilla Skillz, Snoop Dogg, and Dr. Dre are all onstage giving their own version of Dre's 'Forgot About Dre' in a Dante Jackson-produced remix called 'Forgot about Dante.' Dante:'Ya'll know me still the same ol' DBeen low keyHated on by these cracka's Got no cream, no albums and no Ben's, no Bent's and no groupiesNo yachts, no jets, no waterfront propertiesGot they panties in a bunchCause they need welfare mo' than meGot my pad fulla titles and albums, suckasThey be mad cause they all now my butla'sDustin' and cookin' and cleanin' my clothesJust like to think of them as dry hoes' The End is talking with Luis Sangre over in the corner, the latter taking a break from serenading the women with Spanish songs. Sangre is dressed in black slacks, a button-down, black shirt with red dragons on it, and a white wifebeater. Sangre:'And remember that time in Argentina where there was the layover for our flight because the government fell apart and no one else's money was any good?' The End:'Yeah. And then we were caught in the middle of the riots and to just go with the flow we started smashing windows and looting stores.' Skillz:'Nowadays everybody wanna talk like they got something to sayBut nothin comes out when they move they lipsJust a buncha gibberishAnd muthafuckas act like they forgot about DanteNowadays everybody wanna talk like they got something to sayBut nothin comes out when they move they lipsJust a buncha gibberishAnd muthafuckas act like they forgot about Dante' Sangre: 'Haha! Yeah... Anyway, we brought you something.' Sangre hands The End a DVD case. Dr. Dre:'Bitch ho PleaseGet on you' kneesBout to smack you in the face with deezWho you think brought you the o' G'sEazy-E's Ice Cube's and D.O.C's and Snoop D O double G'sAnd a group that said muthafuck the policeGave you a tape full of dope beatsThe bomb weed stroll through in you hoodAnd when your album sales wasn't doin too goodWho's the doc that he told you to go seeYa'll better listen up closely' Sangre:'That's Up In Smoke, the DVD. And Sister Ash gave you Creed tickets.' End:'Sister Ash?' Sangre:'She's the nun at the bar getting plastered.' End: *Pretending that surprises him*'Ah.' Snoop Dogg:'All you niggas that said that I turned popOr the Firm flop ya'll are the reason Dante ain't been getting no sleepSo fuck ya'll all of ya'llIf ya'll don't like me blow meYa'll are gonna keep fuckin around wit meAnd turn me back to the old meYa'll know the D O Double GGot ass in one hand and the other holdin' HennesseySippin' on gin and juicecuttin' looseSit down, muthafuck, dis ain't duck duck gooseCan't match what I did,The hoes I been wit',The rhymes that I spitNow back up, clean up,Yo leg drippin' wet and you startin' to smell like shit.' The End walks away from Luis Sangre and runs into Sandy Wong. The End:'Sorry.' Sandy:'That's okay, I've come to expect it. Who was that man in the corner?' Skillz:'Nowadays everybody wanna talk like they got something to sayBut nothin comes out when they move they lipsJust a buncha gibberishAnd muthafuckas act like they forgot about Dante.Nowadays everybody wanna talk like they got something to sayBut nothin comes out when they move they lipsJust a buncha gibberishAnd muthafuckas act like they forgot about Dante.Nowadays everybody wanna talk like they got something to sayBut nothin comes out when they move they lipsJust a buncha gibberishAnd muthafuckas act like they forgot about Dante.Nowadays everybody wanna talk like they got something to sayBut nothin comes out when they move they lipsJust a buncha gibberishAnd muthafuckas act like they forgot about Dante.' The End gives a casual shrug. The End:'I have no idea.' The End walks past Hessian, puts the DVD case in his hands. Hessian immediately drops the case as it burns his hands. Minion:'Creed merchandise?' Hessian:'Yeah...' Minion the Imp spits on the DVD case, his acid spit dissolving the tickets and DVD. Minion wipes his mouth and gets spun around and kicked in the gut by Logan, the Hardcore Dwarf. Logan, in jeans and black T-shirt, picks him up, powerbomb! Bubba McCoy, dressed the same as Logan, walks up to watch. Bubba McCoy:'Let's get ready for midget-fighting!' Ivan Stanislav, standing near Hessian, snaps the red suspenders over his black shirt, making his loose black slacks wrinkle slightly. Stanislav:'Compared to Praporshik Stanislav, all men are small.' Hessian:'I heard that.' Logan stomps down of Minion the Imp before he catches the foot and pushes him away. Coming back to his feet, Minion throttles the charging Logan and gives him the IMP CHOKESLAM OF DEATH! Dave:'A chokeslam! Logan is out of it!' Jeff:'You expected anything else?' Minion covers Logan for the pin, with OSW referee Fontaine counting. 1..... 2..... SAVE BY MINI-ME! Raves:'What the fuck?!' Smith:'That Mini-Me is so cute!' Mini-Me picks up Minion and gives him a groin kick. The Impbeef is unharmed. So Mini-Me settles for biting his eyes. Logan gets up and together Mini-Me and Logan kick Minion in the gut, doubling him over for a double-team brainbuster! Argyle:'Mini-Me is making an appearance to avenge his loss to Minion on an OSW pay-per-view earlier this year!' But while Mini-Me and Logan walk away, General Tso, Pete's midget friend, slides in and pins Minion. 1.... 2.... 3! Winner and NEW MIDGET FIGHTING CHAMPION: GENERAL TSO! Inphino Blitz and Mina de Margo shake their heads at the happenings. Blitz:'Times like this I think I should've just gone back to PURE full-time.' Mina:'But that's where BladeD is...' Blitz: *Shudders*'Nevermind.' Meanwhile, Rent-A-Hero and Pete are arguing. RAH:'I brought Twister first!' Pete:'But I brought it second! Use mine!' RAH:'But did you also bring a Playstation 2?' Pete:'Are you kidding? I got the PS2 version!' Pete holds up the disc, and RAH's (whose outfit of Spider-man open up shirt, with a black T-Shirt underneath, Venom print baggy pants, Spiderman 2099 mask clashes like the Israelis and the Palestinians) eyes light up. RAH:'Good Gotham! Put the disc in!' Stephen Lee is trying to hit on Tempest over by the buffet table. Tempest is wearing sparkly red hoodie and flower embroidered jeans. Stephen Lee has on black baggy pants and a black wifebeater with pizza sauce stains. Lee is getting coaching by the two guys on his shoulders that only he can see or hear, Frank, the Evil One, is wearing a black tuxedo and his dark hair is curly and wavy. Bob, the Good One, is wearing a white tux and his immaculate blond hair is neatly slicked back. Lee: *At Frank prompting.*'You look like you were poured into those pants. May I have a drink?' Bob:'No, no, you're doing it all wrong! Like this.' Lee: *At Bob prompting*'You are more beautifaul than any flower, or sunset in the world. Give me the pleasure of your company for a date?' Tempest backs up from Stephen Lee. Tempest:'You... might wanna talk to him about that.' Stephen Lee turns around to Ivan Stanislav's chest. He looks up at the Russian Bear, frowning with a growl while cracking his fists. Lee:'Uh... What do I do now? You guys gotta help me, you got me into this!' Frank and Bob:'Um.... You're on your own. Bye.' Stephen Lee is punched in the face and he falls to the floor. Turning his head to the side, he sees Bob and Frank running away through the crowd, dodging around the legs of the guests and trying not to get stepped on. Stanislav pulls Lee back up by his shirt, which is ripping under the weight. Lee:'Uh... would it at all make you go easy on me if I said I disapproved of that new Mega Mall they opened up in Russia?' Stanislav:'THEY DID WHAT?!' Tempest:'Uh-oh... You shouldn't have mentioned that....' At the PS2 station that RAH has set up, Stephen Lee's manager Yoshikiro has his PFIK CAW about to perform the Buzzsaw Kick to the Duzza CAW and win the WWE Undisputed Championship. Duzza is mashing the buttons like a madman. Kraig Carter, in Sherlock Holmes detective outfit, is standing there watching. Duzza:'Reverse! REVERSE!' PFIK successfully hits five Buzzsaw kicks in a row and then pins Duzza. Referee:'1...... 2...... 3!!' Duzza throws the controller on the ground. Duzza:'Dammit! This CAW couldn't do nothin'! It's messed up!' Angelo Deville walks up in his white pinstriped suit, laughing. Deville:'No, you built it perfectly.' Duzza:'Shut up!' Yoshikiro:'{Something in Japanese.}' Kraig Carter:'What?' Blitz:'He says he must go sweep up Stephen Lee, who just received a Red Scare from Ivan Stanislav.' Carter puts his hands up. Blitz waves him off. Blitz:'Stop it. Tonight is about The End's retirement, not our issues.' Alan Kriegman walks by. Kriegman:'He won't be The End for long!' Kriegman steps over the broken table in front of him by stepping on Stephen Lee. Lee is knocked out through the table. He was outside when Stanislav warned everyone not to chase Tempest. Lee's two small guys on his shoulder, Bob and Frank, talk with him as he's fading out of consciousness. Frank:'That was stupid.' Frank:'Yeah, I gotta agree...' There's still a lot left to be happening! Alan Kriegman has to get The End to sign away the rights to the name, Dante Jackson has a gift to give The End, and there are presentations that must be made. Come on back for Part 4 of however-many-parts-the-handler-decides-to-do-in-this-series! Bye!
