Misogyny Stricken (Part II) - Stephanie?

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/03 Stone sat home through to the broken record of news anchors reporting the death of a woman by the Candice Wright, a misogyny victim happening to be the slayings of the blade wielding lunatic, Stone Burns.. but of course they didn't mention his name seeing that the murderer/ (or as Matt Jackson says he is) killer was unknown. The misogynist packed his duffel bag with the raiment's he has stored within his two-star hotel in Tuscaloosa, Alabama. He readied himself for a rather lengthy air flight for Yemen as the PCW started off its USO tour. Stone shoved his blade in to his tux side-pocket, shouldered his duffel bag, advanced onward towards the door, and exited. He didn't mind to lock the door behind him as it was his last stay and that it wouldn't harm him financially since the PCW had him covered. Before him was a sleek, visibly untouched, black exterior of a Jeep Grand Cherokee, Stone's rented SUV. He pulled a key, the top covered in black lather, and dangling before it like the feet of a small child on a chair were a few buttons controlling the car doors. He clicked to the far right side and the audible sound of the four-door car locks indicated the opening. Feet away from the vehicle, he walked through a passageway through the early evening, as the moon got bigger and bigger through the warm Bainbridge, Georgia. The surroundings and neighborhood were seemingly dormant and void as not a person or automobile was seen in sight, excluding the parked cars. He walked passed the passenger seat, and around the front of the vehicle and in to the driver's door, opening it in to the driver's seat. The interior was the attractive, black, smooth leather layers as the stick shift maintained a glistening and well-polished image. Stone tossed his duffel bag in to the two of four seats in the back, as he placed the single key in to the hole, rendering the loud revving of the engine. The vehicle screeched off from the conduction of Stone who abruptly stomped on the gas, and left through the area empty of street lights. Stone swerved a few corners, finally ending up in to a freeway, bereft of the usual mass amount of cars. The evening became rather dreary as a large gray cloud formed in to the sky, as a slight sprinkle of rain came down in to a harsh pounding. Stone activated his windshield wipers, but the scene in front of him was still rather blurry even with the thick, bright car light. Now his automobile windshield was deluged in a pool of ongoing rain, as the wipers attacked back quickly with their fierce left and right movement only making the rain succeed with the amount pouring and the vigorous landings. He didn't bother to yield as the weather got more and more vicious.. It withered for as he passed through a tunnel, from there slightly seeing that green back grounded sign pointing right saying 'BAINBRIDGE REGIONAL AIRPORT' and he continued on as the rain distorted his vision. He closed his eyes, and turned right with the steering wheel. No harsh bumps or hits. Stone opened his eyes again and... - - - 'CRASH!' Stone's vehicle collides with a figure, deflecting off of the windshield instantly cracking it. The misogynist abruptly stops the car, pushes the door out and looks to the side where the figure had landed. A woman it was.... He devilishly smiled, and looked around for witness’s but.. none. Stone gaited forward, and kneeled down on the blood caked woman, soaked in both the mixtures of crimson blood and clear rain. Stone examined her face, as a few cuts accompanied her nose and eyelids. She seemed dead, but Stone held her wrist browsing for a pulse as it continuously went, indicating her presence. Examining her face, he stopped, and pondered. The expression on his face was a rather thunderstruck grimace as he stood up and looked down again, shook his head, looked down again.. 'It can't be.' he muttered to himself as he profusely shut his eyes but still seeing the same unchanging image.. 'Stephanie?' To Be Continued
