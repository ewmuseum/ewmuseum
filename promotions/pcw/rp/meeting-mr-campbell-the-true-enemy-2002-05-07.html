Meeting Mr.Campbell...The True Enemy

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Meeting Mr.Campbell...The True Enemy Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 5-7-2002 ..::/::..Our story begins as many days have passed and many things are on the mind of our young hero, trying to handle two situations to the best of his ability even though they will be the most hard and grueling tasks of his life. First, the defense of his PCW Crusierweight title against Juan 'The Golden Toe' Borro. These two men have been seething 'desire' for the PCW Crusierweight championship that our hero holds and with 2 singles losses to the Juan, our hero is more determined than ever to not only retain his title...but also get one LARGE win to make up for those three blemishes to Juan. Second, the first and most important meeting between Rent-A-Hero and perhaps his greatest adversary...the father of his lover Amy Campbell...Richard Campbell. This enemy is the MOST intimidating to our hero due to the sheer fact that he must make this enemy an ally...and that his power comes psychologically, not physically...but being 6'4 and a solid 265 isn't a minus either. Richard seems to have an innate human hatred for our spandex enwrapped hero, his first respone to seeing our hero upon saying 'hi' when both he and Aimz came through the door of their Nova Scotia after the long flight was a harsh, 'Look...Aimz brought home a giant condom...freat pick sweetie,' with a sarcastic smile to add salt to the wound. Since then, our hero has been using all his power to attain the trust and respect of Mr.Campbell, but that is a task easier said than done...and the continuation of his relationship with Amy hangs in the balance...with that said, we now go to the outside workshop of Mr.Campbell, a meager little wooden shed which houses a peaceful sanctum within for the blue-collar Nova Scotian. Hockey memorabilia litters the room from posters to loose trading cards strewn over the workbench of Mr.Campbell and he keeps this workshop furnished with a large green couch, a wide refrigerator within walking distance of the couch, and a 36' TV sitting on a makeshift stand of wooden boards...the perfect getaway for the average man. Shortly, the silence is broken in the workshop as Rent-A-Hero, dressed in his Iron Fist replica garb, complete with golden yellow punching gloves and ninja mask and the stout yet built Mr.Campbell enter the workshop, our hero wincing and letting out cries of pain while holding his head as Mr.Campbell shakes his head in shame. Rent-A-Hero immediately topples onto the aforementioned green couch, his childish cries of pain a lot more hilarious then they are crucial as Mr.Campbell goes to his workbench, crouching down as he rustles around within it's contents, various items rattling around as Mr.Campbell lets out a tired sigh, thumbing through his scruffy mountaineer-style beard as he says casually to our hero, the two of them beginning their 'bonding' time together AKA 'torment' time in Mr.Campbell's case, since this morning..::/::..Mr.Campbell:'I'm sorry Rent-A-Hero...it's not like I MEANT to hit you with the baseball.' *Snickers* 'If anything, those 'Rent-A-Senses' of yours shoulda kicked in when that ball was headed rigth for that noggin' of yours.'Rent-A-Hero:'I'm sorry Mr.Campbell...I guess my powers were failing me, but you DID tell me to look the other way...but I saw no Green Lantern.' Mr.Campbell:'Oh shut your mouth and quit your whining. Now let's see what I got for ya here...oh yea, this should heal ya up nicely.' Rent-A-Hero:'Whatever you have and a warm hug from my love rabbit shall cure ANY ailment this hero has sir.' Mr.Campbell: *Quietly in an almost murderous voice* 'Hug....my daughter....hugging...you...condom boy...must...suffer....must...pay.' *Speaks normally as hard as possible* 'Any...blood?' Rent-A-Hero:'I'm seeing some drippage of the crimson fluid from my forehead, yes sir. I'm sure some good old fashioned alcohol will clear up and disinfect this wound.'..::/::..Mr.Campbell grabs ahold of something within the cabinet, moving over to our hero and checking on him. He moves our hero's hand off his throbbing forehead, adjusting his thick glasses to get a good look at the wide gash in our hero's forehead just below the blonde hairline of Rent-A-Hero, that part of his mask moved down so Mr.Campbell can assess the wound..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'I see you've found some alcohol good sir..'Mr.Campbell:'Yes siree the best alcohol money can buy...good ol' Jack'll clear ya right up.' ..::/::..Mr.Campbell brandishes the large bottle of the hard drink, Jack Daniels, the glass longneck bottle half filled with the weak orange glossy drink. Our hero strikes a confused look towards Mr.Campbell, twisting off the cap of the drink as our hero says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Sir...I don't think that's the proper alcohol for this......'!!!!~GYAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHH~!!!!..::/::..Mr.Campbell abruptly interrupts the correction by Rent-A-Hero as he allows the Jack Daniels to generously flow over the face and open wound of our hero, the wound and his eyes burning in an undescribeable agony as he falls off the couch and rolls around, rubbing his eyes instinctively as he lets out more and more screams of pain, Mr.Campbell just standing there in his faded blue jeans and tank top and taking a long sip from the bottle of the Jack Daniels..::/::..Mr.Campbell:'If he wants my daughter...he's gonna have to earn her. On a side note, Duke Williams would be proud.' *yelling over Rent-A-Hero as he continues to roll around* 'Hey...if you can find some of that Clorox bleach sitting over there, that'd be real good to stop the burning...' *quietly* 'Oh this is too much fun...ain't that right Jack?' *Looks to the bottle like their having a coversation* 'What...drink you...oh no I cou...yes I could.' *Quickly knocks back a sip as Rent-A-Hero continues to roll about screaming.*Later That Day At A Local Bar.....::/::..It's later on in the day and things look to be a bit calmer between both the torturous Mr.Campbell and our protagonist, Rent-A-Hero. They both are sitting comfortably at a local Nova Scotian bar, Mr.Campbell enjoying a nice frosty mug of cold beer while our hero sits across him, his eyes bloodshot red from the previous happenings as he takes a sip of his bright red Tahitian Treat from his bere mug, amazed as the drink travels through the many swirls in his plastic twisty straw. Mr.Campbell, sits the beer down, his voice loud and domineering enough to overpower the old-school rock in the background as he looks over to Rent-A-Hero and says..::/::..Mr.Campbell:'Well son, how about you tell me about yourself....where do you come from, what do you do?' Rent-A-Hero:'Well, I'm a test tube failure genetically enginnered by a mad scientist for destruction and devastation who somehow showed compassion and was later stranded in a cold, cruel world by himself...turning to comics and cartoons, I soon molded myself after them and became a walking, talking, breathing hero who swore to bring justice to the world and into the wrestling ring while at the same time, making spandex a fashion statement yet again and shamelessly promoting myself at all times....' ..::/::..That response only draws a baffled eye from the father of Amy, Mr.Campbell setting his drink down abruptly as he scoffs repeatedly in an effort to find the first out of a pleathora of things to say to Rent-A-Hero. Our hero justs looks to him, awaiting an answer with that bright smile of his as Mr.Campbell finally is able to get SOMETHING out, the stress caused by the answer about to make him lose more hair on his already thinning head..::/::.. Mr.Campbell:'I always knew you either were a crack fiend or a test tube baby...but you weren't supposed to prove me right?! And what's this business about you 'shamelessly plugging'?'SHAMELESS PLUGGING ALERT!! DON'T SAY I DDIN'T WARN YOU....IT'S COMING...IT'S COMING...I'M TELLIN' YA IT'S COMIN...FINE THEN DON'T BELIEVE ME...KEEP READING...GO AHEAD...YOUR NOT HURTING MY FEELINGS.... :( *SNIFF SNIFF* ..::/::..Our hero answers Mr.Campbell in an extroverted tone, looking towards the camera at times while speaking as if this were a cheesy infomercial on a shopping network..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Shameless plugging? Why whatever could you mean? I mean, PCW Supremacy practically sells itself! With a killer line-up of PCW superstars ranging from myself, Miyagawa, Xavier Kannon, and many others, it's well worth the price for tickets or ordering it on pay-per-view for the GOSH-DARNED SWELL PRICE OF...$21.95!!! I mean, that's a STEAL seeing as how I, Rent-A-Hero will be putting on a five-star classic in the ring with Juan 'Golden Toe' Borro for the PCW Crusierweight title...even though we all know the outcome will be a beaten down soccer player brougth back to the justice of REALITY...the reality that he will NEVER beat the PCW's only superhero on PCW Supremacy in what will be the GREATEST CRUSIERWEIGHT TITLE MATCH OF ALL TIME. If I were a fan, I'd get tickets for the ENTIRE family to come out just to watch me KAMEHAMEHA BOMB Juan Borro into oblivion alone, the many other spectacular matches just ADD to the value Mr.Campbell! So if I were you, I'd look into buying PCW Supremacy so you could see the best of the PCW superstars and of course me, Rent-A-Hero, retain my coveted Crusierweight title against Juan Borro in what will be, A BATTLE OF THE AGES I TELL YOU....and no I am not using this extriverted voice nor am I overhyping certain words just to emphasize why you and many other people should buy PCW Supremacy only on pay-per-view...oh...by the way, my throat is parched..oh hack hack, sure wish I had something to relieve the demon stamping about in my throat...'..::/::..Mr.Campbell just watches on at our hero, frozen in shock of the utter stupidity of the man who is the boyfriend of his beloved daughter as he shamelessly continues his hard plugging. Conviently, one of the young bartenders approaches Rent-A-Hero with a fresh and unopened can of 'Tahitian Treat' sitting neatly on his tray, holding it so the camera can get a good view as he turns his head to the audience also, our heor feigning shock as the drink is brought to him before the waiter exclaims, lookign at the camera the whole time as he sounds like he is dryly reading from some cue-cards..::/::..Bartender:'Rent-A-Hero...it seems like the demons of dehydration are pummeling you at full force today....have no fear, because TAHITIAN TREAT will destroy that insatiable monster they call 'thirst'! Here, have a sip.' *Winks to the camera as he makes sure they get a good look at the drink* ..::/::..Mr.Campbell's mouth is wide open, 'idiot' being the only thing that comes to mind about Rent-A-Hero as he takes the drink from the tray, popping the top as it makes the trademark 'KRRZZZTCH' sound of soda cans. The camera then takes a deep close-up on our hero, everything moving in slow motion as he raises the drink up and guzzles it merrily...finishing the drink in one fell swoop before making a satisfied 'AHHHHHHH' loudly, wiping his mouth while lookign at the camera and exclaiming..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'E-GADS GOOD BARTENDER, THIS DRINK IS HEAVEN SENT!! NO OTHER DRINK HAS THE PURE SMOOTH, FRUITY FIZZ OF TAHITIAN TREAT!! IT'S SO TAHITIAN-EY AND FILLED WITH SUPER SAIEN SWEETNESS THAT'LL DRIVE ANY KRYPTONIAN TO A HAPPY BUZZ!! NOT ONLY WILL I HAVE TO SERVE LAGE HELPING OF SMACKERING SAUCE TO JUAN BORRO AT PCW SUPREMACY for only $21.95, smacking him about like he's a two dollar lady of the evening and I'm his body solicitor in an effort to retain the sweet sweet PCW Crusierweight championship....but this drink is SOOO good, it makes me want to compete with the pleathora of men who wish to establish their soda on top of the PCW world. No drink can top the the RADICAL RED RUSH FLAVOR OF TAHITIAN TREAT and I shall topple all men who stand in my way of promoting and making this drink the OFFICIAL drink of the PCW. So from this day forward...I shall stand by TAHITIAN DRINK...THE ONLY DRINK THAT'S TAHITIAN-EY...whatever that tastes like!' *Smiles wide as the waiter and himself pose for the camera*..::/::..With both shameless plug spiels done by our hero, Mr.Campbell tosses back his beer with no breather, intaking all of the devil's juice and slamming his mug down with authority as he looks to the camera, a look of utter seriousness and intesnity on his elderly face as tiny 'anger' wrinkles begin to form, Mr.Campbell saying..::/::..Mr.Campbell:'My...daughter...this...guy....NOT GONNA HAPPEN. I see I'm gonna have to take drastic measures. This call for....'Operation:Rid-A-Hero'...mark my words....THIS WILL END!!!' *Pauses* 'I said...THIS WILL END!! D*** IT, DON'T I GET ANY EVIL MUSIC OR SOMETHING?'! ..::/::..I usually don't do this, but what the he**, it is a Rent-A-Hero RP...there's been weirder..::/::..Mr.Campbell:*evil music plays* 'There it goes. HAHHAHAHAHAH!! HAHAHAHAHAHAH!!! HAHAHA!!' *COUGH COUGH* *HACK HACK* 'Da** beer...drank it too fast....HAHA *cough*' ..::/::..With that said and the impression left, it seems as if Mr.Campbell is ready to set a plan into motion to break up Rent-A-Hero and his one and only, Amy Campbell. Who knew that our hero's greatest enemy, would be from the blood of the one he loves. WILL RENT-A-HERO BE ABLE TO HOLD ONTO AMY?! WHAT IS THIS PLAN MR.CAMPBELL HAS FOR OUR HERO?! WILL RENT-A-HERO SUCCEED AT THE PAY-PER-VIEW?! WHAT DOES TAHITIAN-EY TASTE LIKE?! FIND OUT, SAME RENT-A-TIME...SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
