The Neo Void Prep

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Neo Void Prep Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 27/10/02 “So... Er... Like, what does the Void actually DO.. And stuff? You know... What is it?” Our shot opens up on the frustrated face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon as he looks over the monthly sales figures of PCW Merchandise, a task made all the harder by the presence of a bored and curious Ellie~!... A BAD combination. The Scientologist sighs and squeezes his pen a little tighter, turning his fingers pale. His head does not move up or make eye contact as he replies, hoping a few vague answers will make her give up. Xavier : “It isn’t anything... It’s anti-STUFF as you would put it.” Naturally, this is above the little prep’s head but she ponders it never the less while painting her toenails Pink on the couch. Eleanor : “Ahhhhhhhhhhhh... So.... What is it again?” Kannon scowls as his focus is broken again and he moves back to the top of the column, stress starting to show on his face... And in his hands where the biro he’s holding cracks between his fingers. Xavier : “It’s the collection of all the negative energy in the Universe... Something like that.” Pulling her leg up to her face she blows her toenail, taking her husband’s words in one ear and out the other, passing through the “Do Not Understand” checkpoint on the way. Eleanor : “So if I’m out shoe shopping, and like all “I’ll SO never find a good pair I don’t already have!” and being all negative Ellie~! and stuff... That makes the Void?” Her way of putting it bemuses Xavier who’s still working out which lines sold best over the month. Sighing he finally looks up to his wife who looks back with her energetic pale Blue eyes, hoping she’s right. So Kannon just agrees. Xavier : “Yes, my little Poon Puppy... Your shoe shopping is the source of the Void.” His wife appeased, Kannon returns to the finances while Ellie~! looks suitably pleased with herself. Eleanor : “Cool... So when he decides he doesn’t want to team with you and, like, SO breaks you in two for eliminating him from that Mall Brawl thing...” As Ellie~! rambles on, Kannon’s bright Green eyes shoot open, his pupils dilating as the preppy Commissioner reminds him Akiyama won’t be exactly friendly. *CRACK!* His body tenses up and another biro is cracked in his hand as the thought of an enraged nVg as his ally runs through his Scientological mind. Xavier : “Oh Hubbard...” The eyes of the Scientologist stare at the page but not at the words and numbers, his ears taking in the ramblings of Ellie~! as she continues on about controlling the Void. Eleanor : “.... And when he’s doing that punching thing that breaks people in half or the thing which hurts, and that other on that goes, like, WHAM! and all that other painful stuff...” Oblivious to her husband’s nerves, Ellie~! carries on with hand actions while Xavier is becoming increasingly more pale. Eleanor : “...I’ll be, like, HELLO?!?!?! I, like, SO control your Void so stop hurting Xavi before he dies and stuff!!!” She smiles, looking pleased with herself as she gets back to decorating her toenails. Eleanor : “See? You have Ellie~! to protect you now!” The terrified Scientologist mutters under his breath, his face White and his hands shaking. Xavier : “Xenu save me...” Finally the glow of thinking she controls the Void dies down, and the curious looks returns to her freckled little face which looks back to Kannon, tilted to the side. Eleanor : “So.... What is it again?” A growl resonates from the CEO as he tries to block out all thoughts of Akiyama being angry with him and returns to the accounts, desperately hoping it can distract him from what lays ahead. Eleanor : “Like, the Void, I mean...” All Kannon wants is a bit of peace and a stress free day, something he isn’t exactly getting while Ellie~! questions him and Akiyama weighs heavy on his mind. He mutters under his breath in an irritated tone. Xavier : “It’s the thing that exists between your ears...” Not hearing his mutterings, the little Commissioner keeps looking to him, eyes wide open and waiting for an answer while her husband just engrosses himself in PCW business. Still she waits for an answer, only the odd blink taking her gaze from her husband. Finally she tires. Eleanor : “Well... If Ellie~!, like, controls this Void thing then I’m going to find out about it!” Her enthusiasm doesn’t penetrate the sphere of concentrations that Xavier has built around himself. Eleanor : “I’m, like, SO the NEO VOID PREP!!!“ She rises chirpilly from the sofa and strikes a pose, enthusiastic about her new vocation as she skips off towards the hallway... Stopping only to muse upon a small point. Eleanor : “I, like, wonder what NEO means? Probably, like, Latin for YAY! or something.... YAY NEO!“ The ditzy Commissioner skips off into the hall and after a few seconds the sound of the door slamming shut behind her is heard, to the relief of her husband who can finally break out from his self-imposed trance. He leans back in his seat and breathes a deep breath out, locking his fingers together and pushing them backwards to send a crack all the way along his knuckles. Xavier : “I had no idea the Bertha “WHAT THE FUCKETY FUCK ARE YOU LOOKING AT YOU FUCKING SHIT FACED CUNT!?!?!” t-shirt would sell so well....” With another pile of sales sheets looming, the Scientologist casts aside the first spreadsheets and gets his head back into the figures, the shot fading to Black as his eyelids creep closer together and his head rests on his hand. The Scientologist drifts off into slumber as our screen is Blacked out.... *SLAM!* “The Void.... Like, WAY rules!” He is yanked out of his sleep by the slamming of the front door and the happy callings of Ellie~! who stumbles into the lounge, anchored at each arm by handfuls of shopping bags. Kannon raises an eyebrow. Xavier : “I thought you were exploring the Physics and Dimensional Dynamics of the Void? Or does inverse reality have Malls now?” Ellie~! looks innocent and thinks of an excuse... Her body language and facial expression denoting that she’s thinking of the said excuse... Rather obviously. Eleanor : “Um... Er... I, like, SO used my powers of the Void to, kinda, summon me shopping!” She drops a few bags and holds up 2 Gucci ones she has left. Eleanor : “See... I SO pulled these from the Void!” Dropping his pen on the table and pushing the stack of papers away, Xavier gives her a suspicious look. Xavier : “Riiiiiiiiiiiiiiight...” Ellie~! picks up on his scepticism and narrows her eyes, waving her hands before her. Eleanor : “Don't make me, like, send you to the Void!” Kannon smirks while Ellie~! double-takes her unsuccessful Void Banishment and the shot slowly fades to Black on the preppy Commissioner re-trying her Banishing techniques. “Er.... Abracadabra.... Hocus Pocus..... Like, SO get Banished!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
