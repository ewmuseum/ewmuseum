Reliving a nightmare.

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 His eyes, his fiery eyes bore down on me. His hands, his powerful hands wrapped around me. I couldn’t escape those eyes. I couldn’t escape those hands. Would I ever be free from him? Could I ever escape him? I wasn’t sure I’d ever find those answer. I wasn’t sure if anyone could ever find those answer if they had gone through what I had. Not with him. Not with that madman. Not with that psychotic freak. Nobody could escape those unanswerable questions… especially not me. I woke up in a cold sweat that covered my entire body. Jerking awake I found the clock sitting across from me. It’s glowing numbers staring me in the face reading 3:21am and I had just awoken from another nightmare. The same nightmare I had been having from time to time since that awful night. A nightmare that hadn’t woke me from my sleep in months. It was gone only to return to me again tonight. I sat up in bed with fear gripping my soul and playing tricks with my subconscious mind. It was making me relive the worst night of my entire life. Making me relive each passing second that seemed to fade into another pain stricken minute. It was as if I were stuck reliving a nightmare, a nightmare that I can’t seem to forget. As I sat there flashes of what had happened began to come back as if they were happening at this very minute. Him getting out of his car and walking across the street holding two roses, one red and the other white. He carried a bottle of wine in his other hand. He wore a look of pleading and forgiveness on his face. The entire scene began passing before me as if in slow motion. As he stepped up onto the curb I dropped my purse in fright. He didn’t seem to understand I was afraid. I wanted to run. I wanted to hide. I wanted to get to a phone and call for help. But fear had me in its clutches and I couldn’t move. As if fear kept me here to face him, a psychotic freak. Why couldn’t I get away? Why couldn’t he understand I wanted nothing to do with him? Why had this man come into my life? Why me? Why now? Why? So many thoughts ran together in my mind. I wasn’t sure which were real and which were derived from the fear dwelling inside of my heart. I only knew one thing at that moment. That I was going to die. That he was going to kill me. I could see it in his eyes. As if he were possessed by some greater force, that blazed raging hot within his eyes. I began to shiver as I looked over to my now husband lying next to me in bed. He was still asleep, my jerking awake and cries of fear hadn’t wakened him. I tossed back the covers and placed my feet on the soft carpet that lined my bedroom floor. The darkness of the room caused the fear inside of me to jump up in my throat. I carefully made my way to the bathroom were at first I felt as though someone was going to jump out at me. As I flipped on the light I realized that it was just my mind playing tricks on me yet one more time. I turned the water on and splashed my face. As I grabbed a towel and patted the water away from my eyes I looked up at the mirror. As I did so I jumped back in fright as his reflection peered back at me. Within an instant his reflection was replaced with my own. But it was enough to send an ice cold chill through my body. I grabbed two towels from the cupboard and wrapped one around my shoulders and the other around my waist. I then sat on the toilet as more scenes from that night flashed before me. He stood just a few feet from me holding out the flowers. Offering them to me as if the last few weeks didn’t mean anything, as if I never told him to stay away from me, and as if he hadn’t noticed I was scarred stiff from fright. I noticed the look on his face as I refused to take the flowers. His eyes flared to life as if telling him what to do and say next. “Take the flowers.” He said. “They’re for you.” Saying it as if I hadn’t realized they were for me. Still I refused to take the flowers. Struggling to speak I blurted out. “I don’t want your flowers.” “But they’re for you.” He replied. Again I refused them. “I don’t want your d@mn flowers.” I found strength within me that I hadn’t realized I had. A strength I didn’t know I could ever find. He lifted the wine bottle. “I brought wine. We can celebrate together.” Celebrate, what is this guy, some sort of freak? “Celebrate what?” A smile came over his face. “Us being together again.” His face distorted by the light shining down from the moon and stars above, it was as if he had forgot everything. “There is no us, least of all together.” I blurted out in a rather harsh tone. Why couldn’t he realize I didn’t want any part of this? That I wanted no part of him? That I wanted no part of, US? Why couldn’t he understand? I know for a fact I didn’t do anything to lead him on. I didn’t smile; invite him over, not a d@mn thing to make him think there was an us or that there ever would be an us again. So what then? What made him think that we’d ever get back together? “But I love you.” The words come from his lips as if a death sentence. You what? You love me? How can you love me, you don’t even know me? If you loved me then you would never have attacked that guy at the bar? You love me my @ss. “I don’t care whether you think you love me or not. I don’t love you.” The words spewed out of my mouth before I even knew what I was saying. They as well had a great affect on him as well. He stumped back as if the words at hit him at his very core. I shook off the flashes as the urge to vomit came up into my throat. I leaned over and hurled into the tub. I heard someone in the hallway. A chill went up my spine but I was relieved when I heard my husband’s voice. “Emily are you alright?” “Yes, dear.” I called out. “Are you having nightmares again?” He asked. As if every time I woke up in the middle of the night I was having a nightmare… which I did. “No I’m just sick.” No use in him worrying.“Would you like me to get you something? Crackers or pepto?” “No thank you I’ll be fine. Go back to bed.” I just wanted to be alone. “Are you sure?” He was kind but persistent. “Yes, I’m sure. I’ll be fine.” I heard his footsteps as he walked away back towards the bedroom. I wiped my mouth with the towel hanging across my shoulders. As the scene of that night began to play again in my mind. I had caught him off guard. I think he truly believed that we would get back together tonight. He hadn’t even taken into account what he would do if I refused his offer. He looked up at me a fire blazing behind those big blue eyes he had. A smirk came over his face as he began to laugh. What he was thinking I couldn’t tell. What he was going to do I couldn’t predict. What he did I’d have to live with! The wine bottle came crashing down on the left side of my face. The bottle hit shattering into a million pieces upon impact causing blood to begin running down the side of my head even before I had hit the ground. The last thing I remember before passing out was him standing over me. He dropped both roses onto my chest as he spoke. “In love as in death.” His words ringing in my ears, as I was sweep off into a world enveloped in darkness. What had happened next I was unclear of, whether a neighbor had heard the noise we had made or if one of them had seen what happened. All I know was that when I came too paramedics where standing over me. The pain I felt was unbearable. I looked from face to face of the people standing over me. All of which were unfamiliar except one. Why did they allow him to stand over me? Why didn’t someone get him away from me? Did they not know he was the one to do this? Did they not understand what he was capable of? That’s when things began to get strange. For I heard one of the officers tell another that they had the suspect in custody in the back of a squad car. That’s when the one I had thought done it just disappeared. As if he was swallowed by the darkness of the night. He vanished into thin air. At the time I was certain it was my imagination playing tricks on me. But now, I’m not sure of what I had seen. Flashing back to my senses I realize that my fear may never go away. I had thought it was gone. That I had gotten over that night. That I had gotten over what had happened. That I had gotten over him, the one they call Ripper. But that all changed tonight. I was sitting on the couch reading a book that I had wanted to finish. While my husband was sitting over in his recliner watching television, a pro wrestling show. He enjoys the shows from time to time. I hardly ever pay any attention to the programs but tonight something said caught my attention. As I was finishing a paragraph one of the announcers mentioned a name. A name I hadn’t thought about in a very long time, Ripper Ryckman. I set my book down in mid sentence and looked up at the television. Thinking to myself that it can’t be him, not on national television, on my television at home, and where I’m suppose to feel safe. But it was him. He was now a professional wrestler in PCW I think it was. I didn’t say anything to my husband. He knows the story but I never mentioned any names to him. He had no idea that the man he was watching was the one that attacked his wife before he met her. I sat and watched the match which was all of a minute long. He had won via disqualification when his opponent hit him with a low blow, serves the bastard right. Then what I say was him and his opponent utterly destroyed the referee of the match. He then looked down at the man and spit on him. The camera caught a close up of his eyes. The same eyes that stood over me and blazed with pure hatred and disgust, those eyes looked the same tonight on the television. Those eyes brought back all of my fear, all of my fright, and every single thought that ever ran through my mind about that madman. And tonight the dreams returned. As I sit here reliving it all, only one thing comes to mind. Will I ever be free from his clutches? Will the man known as the Ripper ever free me from his grasp?
