Date: Chapter 14 I had it all in my head perfectly. I knew when Project 84 would take off! “Told you I knew where you were. Have fun on Cloud Nine.” Bursting into my hotel room where I was spending a few nights, I grabbed immediately the notes on Project 84 and read the excerpt from the pages. Although Project 84 is a highly deadly mission that should nto be attempted by a beginner, there a still a few crucial missing pieces to putting the whole thing together. First of all, to get the desired impact on the mission, thousands of gallons of Airplane fuel is needed to pump into the small chip for more power. Such airplane fuel is commonly not found around and is farely rare. How the fuel will be gained is unsure at this time. For Project 84 to work, Henry Claval, Clair Rivence, and Sven Neirlinger needed a ton of airplane fuel, only found in a big airliner that goes on long flights. To go along with this, how did whoever put the note on my hotel door know where I was or anything about Cloud Nine? First off, Clair Rivence had told me in our little AIM chat the other day that she could track me down as fast as she wanted and could gain any information on me. She must have made a few phone calls to find out my room number. Along with that, she must have read about Cloud Nine and PCW's enormous plane. Knowing that the Cloud Nine airplane used a ton of fuel, Clair must've decided that she would scare me with the note, and gain the fuel in the mean time. Once I was in the air on Cloud Nine, Project 84 could be free to set off and I wouldn't have any chance at stopping the plan. I was only about one hundred or so miles away from where the enormous PCW plane was being kept and being refueled for the take-off, a week away. As fast as I good, I jumped in my car and drove to the scene. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I quickly drove off to the prohibited place where the PCW plane was being kept. Nobody except airplane staff and PCW personel were allowed into the area so I flashed my VIP pass and proceeded into the field where the airplane was. The jet was like anything I had ever seen. It was big and huge with hardly any windows on the side of it. I could only imagine what was inside for me to keep myself entertained during the month it would be in air. Oh course, I couldn't go into the actual plane, just into the area where there were washrooms and a place with a pool table. I fixed my eyes ahead at the huge fuel tanks that were on the back of the plane. Gettting the binoculars out of my side pocket, I focussed intently on the fuel cylinders. There it was I had seen them. There was a girl in a black business suit whom I recognized as Clair Rivence easily. She wore a focused look. Beside her, was a man. From the pictures I had seen of Henry Claval, I was pretty sure it was him. Claval was wearing glasses. I stared shocked at what the two were doing. In plane view in daylight of everybody around, the two cons had a small container and were gathering fuel into it to fill the container. There was a pump re-routing the gas into the container. The only question was, where was Sven, my brother. I turned to my side and saw the worker in which had let me into the area. I quickly ran over to him and asked him. SyN: Excuse me, who are those people over there? I motioned towards Clair and Henry. Worker: Those? They are part of the jet refueling team. Why? SyN: Oh, it's just because... All of a sudden, a bullet reached out and hit the worker in the neck. The man dropped to the ground, lifeless and lay there with blood dripping out. I turned around quickly to see the face of my brother Sven on top of a semi truck, rifle in hand. We stared at each other back and forth without word, looking at each other intently. This was it... The final show-down!...
