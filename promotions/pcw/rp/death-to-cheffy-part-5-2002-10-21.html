Date: 10/21/02 The camera fades in… We see a still image of Jackson yelling out a request to one of his henchmen to open the cage containing a hungry and irritated Macaulay Culkin, whom Jackson probably kidnapped after filming the music video for Jackson’s song “Black or White”. Narrator: Thank god we’ve finally managed to make it to the final part of this snore-fest. The last time we left the Chef, Bertha and him were trying to thwart the evil scheme of Michael Jackson. Unfortunately both B and Cheffy ran into some trouble…let’s watch! The still image begins to play… Jackson: UNLEASH THE CULKIN!!! Suddenly the cage with the Home Alone star drops to the ground, breaking itself open and letting the malnourished and mistreated child star out. He smacks his cheeks with his hands and lets out a skin-crawling shriek… He turns and looks at Bertha’s leg, which looks as if it was chock full of cottage cheese. Culkin: Food… The Home Alone star charges at Bertha and LATCHES ONTO HER LEG, BITING AND NIBBLING AT HER FLABBY FLESH!!! Bertha: Get the f*cking f*ck off of me you piece of sh*t f*ck tard!!! The Chef immediately chases after a fleeing Jackson. The Chef: Come back and fight like a man, Jackson!!! But the Thriller himself manages to scurry away, shouting in a victorious tone, “HE HE HEEE!!!” The Chef looks around the giant dining hall and sees a giant centerpiece in the middle of the table. The Chef: Manuel!!! The Chef recognizes the hog-tied child and rushes over to save him, BUT SUDDENLY A GROTESQUE CREATURE COMES SWINGING DOWN FROM THE CEILING!!! HE LANDS ON THE TABLE RIGHT IN FRONT OF MANUEL AND LETS OUT A HUGE ROAR!!! The Chef: Gah!!! I know you!!! You’re the ELEPHANT MAN!!! But the creature doesn’t answer the Chef; it slowly approaches the Sultan of Sautéing and lets out another giant roar!!! The Chef: This is going to be quite interesting to say the least… The Chef backs up quickly to a corner of the room as the Elephant Man approaches and gets closer and closer. The Chef can smell the rotten breath of the sideshow freak as his hideous faces comes ever so near to the face of the Culinary Conquistador… The Chef: Bertha!?!? Little help? The camera fades to black… Narrator: And now on to the final chapter in this sub par Chef series! The camera fades in once again… The Chef crawls into the corner of the dining hall as the Elephant man aggressively moves forward. The Chef: Bertha!!! Ber-THA!!! The Elephant Man moves even closer to the Chef. He places his deformed hand over the quivering Culinary Conquistador’s neck and is just about to squeeze when WHACK!!! “Out of the f*cking way Lord F*ckington!!!” BERTHA STEWART CLOCKS THE MONSTER OVER THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH THE BODY OF MACAULY CULKIN, WHICH SHE IS USING AS A CLUB!!! The Chef: My hero…I mean…heroine!!! Bertha spins the body of the Home Alone star around like a baton and then RAMS CULKING HEAD FIRST INTO THE STOMACH OF THE DAZED MONSTER!!! The Chef: That’s it, now sauté his @$$ in a rosemary, tarragon, and allspice vinaigrette!!! Bertha then TOSSES CULKIN ACROSS THE ROOM and the Good Son star flies face first into A STUCCO WALL!!! The Chef: Who puts Stucco on a wall, seriously!?!? The Chef then stands back up, whips out a cheese grater and GOES TO WORK ON THE FINGERNAILS OF THE ELEPHANT MAN!!! The Chef: One manicure for a culinary school dropout coming up!!! The Elephant man squeals in pain as the Chef grates his nails and skin against the cheese grater! The Chef: Say Uncle! Say it! Elephant Man: RRRRRRAAAAAAARRRR-OOOOOOOOFFF!!! SUDDENLY BERTHA STEPS FORWARD AND KICKS THE ELEPHANT MAN SQUARELY ON THE TESTICLES!!! The giant freak grabs his crotch and falls face first onto the floor with a giant THUMP!!! The Chef: Hey, I was going to finish him off! Bertha: Sure f*cko, that’s why you needed my f*cking b*tch @$$ mother f*cking help, f*ck face mother f*cker!!! The Chef then remembers why he came to the mansion in the first place. The Chef: MANUEL!!! The Sultan of Sautéing darts past Bertha and makes his way to the table, quickly jumping on top of it and untying his sobbing adopted son. The Chef: Hush Manuel, you’re going home! “NOT SO FAST, HE HE HEEEEEE!!!” Bertha: F*ck!!! The Chef and Jackson both glance at Bertha with disappointed looks. Bertha: Ahem, I mean…holy f*cking @$$ humping c*ck @$$ed mother f*cker piece of sh*t queer as f*ck b*tch @$$ mother f*cking f*ckety f*ck f*ck!!! The Chef: Better…you were saying Jackson? The Chef: Why was your children’s show Christmas-themed? After all you are a Jehovah’s Witness…right? Jackson: Chef, you fail to understand the economic factors both foreign and domestic that force me to detach from my faith and pursue a show that suits the majority of the USA children population’s beliefs and mythology. I was going to take you to Thailand where we could be a happy couple and I could shower you with gifts and…YEEEEOUCH!!! Jackson is KICKED AGAIN!!! Manuel: Mommy! Daddy! Wake up! Manuel’s pleading stirs both the Chef and Bertha from their boredom-induced slumber. The Chef: Jackson, you fiend!!! Bertha: Do I f*cking smell steamed broccoli? Bertha wanders off into the background, following her nose as usual. The Chef draws a rolling pin. The Chef: Have at you, you rancid piece of poultry! The Chef takes a swing as Manuel backs away, Jackson ducks the attack, doing a splits on the floor and hopping back up, JUMP KICKING THE CHEF IN THE FACE!!! The Chef: GACK!!! Jackson then takes his bell and CLOBBERS THE CHEF OVER THE HEAD WITH THE JINGLING OBJECT!!! Jackson: Jingle (DING!) Bells (DING!) Jingle (DING!) Bells (DING!) Jingle (DING!) All (DING!) The (DING!) Bertha crawls up to Jackson and manages to place her bum right over his face. Bertha: F*cking broccoli always does this to me…f-f-f-f-f-f… Bertha feels a rumble in her tummy. Bertha: f-f-f-f-f-f-f-f…F****************CCCCCCKKKKKKKKKK!!! PPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPPFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT!!!!!!!!! A HUGE GUST OF BROWN WIND PASSES THROUGH THE HOLES IN BERTHA’S SWEAT PANTS AND THE BODY OF JINGLE JACKSON CONVULGES AND SQUIRMS, TRYING TOM FREE ITSELF FROM THE GUST OF SHITTY AIR!!! Jackson finally stops squirming, and lies still on the ground… Bertha: He’s at f*cking peace now… Bertha closes Jackson’s eyes and picks up the KOed Chef, slinging him across her shoulder. She grabs Manuel by the hand, and yanks him away as she stampedes out of the house. She stops on the front porch. Bertha: The first one was for f*cking Manuel…this is for f*cking ME!!! PPPPPPPPPFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT!!!!!!!!!! She lets out another horrifyingly stench-filled fart and the foundation of the mansion begins to shake. The Chef finally wakes up. The Chef: Holy Hollandaise Sauce!!! What is that smell!?!? Bertha: F*cking broccoli with a piece of sh*t touch of f*cking Frank’s Red Hot Sauce… The Chef: Which once in your bowels is a lethal combination… Bertha: F*cking true, f*cko, f*cking true…. The Chef: Is Manuel ok? Manuel: Hi Chef! I dis many years old! He holds up two fingers. The Chef: Great…I’m glad to see that everything worked out just fine… ***Handler’s Note: Everything DID NOT work out just fine. The Chef lost his job at Kid Net due to his addiction to Pepto Bismol, which he did not report during the interview process. The odor around the Jackson Mansion has yet to go away, so police and emergency crews have yet to retrieve the body of Jackson and survive the trek back out of the mansion alive. Manuel is resting quietly and safely at home, but this ordeal has left him with a very unhealthy aversion to broccoli. Macaulay Culkin was last seen somewhere in the Canadian wilderness, wait…that’s the Sasquatch… The camera fades to black as Bertha carries her two main men down the garden path towards the catering van.
