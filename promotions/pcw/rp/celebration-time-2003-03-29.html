Celebration Time

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 29, 2003 'I can feel it coming in the air tonight Hold On I've been waiting for this moment all my life Hold on' [The sound of Phil Collins fills the room giving it a somber moment. The beautiful, slender women have slowed down their dancing and it almost looks as if it's all being done in slow motion. The men dancing with them and watching seem hypnotized by their beautiful movements. The stobe lights and spotlights throughout the immense room have slowed down as well to the point where time itself has slowed down. A blonde haired man sits in a large wicker chair with a small grin on his face. His tan complexion is so deep that he must have been born that way to have such the perfect skin. His blue eyes filled with a sense of satisfaction, not even really looking at the women dancing in front of him. Every now and then the lights pass his face and it radiates through the room to all the others. The guests of the party look to this man in the wicker chair, raising their glasses toward him. For the blonde haired man is the guest of honor on the ninth floor of Hotel California. The busty women smile and shake their bosoms toward the man hoping to capture his attention. They won't catch his attention at this moment as his mind is elsewhere, which is extremely rare. The tanned man is wandering to a place of where his loyalty and personal drive are located. Why this blonde surfer man does what he does. He looks up to the ceiling of the room and thinks of the man who inspired him to do what he does. The man wonders if his father is proud of him up there in the depths of heaven. Maybe if he watched the match with God, pointing out the bodacious moves that his son was distributing to his opponents. Then once victory was achieved, did his father smile? Did he nod in approval? This surfer boy will never know the answer to that question. He'll never know for sure if that's exactly what happened, but he can only let his imagination give him the answers for now] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy, the new Primetime Championship Wrestling World champion, sits proudly in the wicker chair. Phil Collins has faded out and the people erupt in cheers at the wonderful come down from the high energy that was pulsing through the place. Tommy smiles wide at the sight of all the people who have come to the party. That they have come over to celebrate Tommy's biggest victory in his entire career. He battled through nine other men, the best that PCW has to offer to date, and he claimed victory over all of them. The man, who only a week earlier lost his Continental Championship, rose above the adversity and won the title that means the most in the sport. As the people clap he revisits the feeling that he got when the referee slammed his hand down for the count of three. The wave of warmth that entered his body just like the late morning sun during a gnarly surf session. His emotions were at a most odd confused state as he didn't know whether to smile, cry, or just jump up and down like a totally radical madman. The crowd may have been upset, they may have been booing, but it still remains that The Tubular One has achieved one of his most precious long term goals in life. He proved, to every totally heinous compadre who said he wouldn't make it, wrong. He jumped up the ladder like that gnarly superhero Spider-Man and sucked those championships in with that most excellent web juice stuff. Through Skylar Green, Rent-A-Hero, and Venneman Pendragon...Tommy proved that he's the best of the best. He is PCW's general now. The Champion of the world] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Does the belt make me look fat, dude? [The scene turns next to the chair to reveal Tommy's younger brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. He has a grin on his face as well, but not from the victory, but because he had a special substance get him to that stage. He's once again decked out in his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. To Tommy it's been a while since he's seen Harry in those duds, but to Harry it just felt like yesterday he had them on. The long, brown haired man looks down to his bodacious brother and shrugs to him] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I don't know, man. [Tommy stands up from the wicker chair and looks down at the belt, sucking his stomach in like an insecure woman] TTT: I think this belt makes me look bogusly pudgy. Harry: Now that you stand up you don't look so bad, man. The belt is much bigger, that's all. [Tommy feels around on the belt, squinting his eyes as he does so. His thoughts are very deep as to whether or not the belt makes him look fat. If it DOES make him look obese this belt will immediately have to go to PCW for a totally radical make over. No wonder that totally bogus dude Snow looked like he was as large as The Overweighter...it was the damn belt] TTT: Maybe your right, dude. This DOES feel heavier than that other belt. Much more gold on it, of course...but I just look pudgy. Did you exchange any of my bodacious lotions this morning? Sometimes when the wrong chemicals touch my most excellent skin I get puffy. Then I start to resemble that bogus Stay Puff marshmallow man from that Ghostbusters movie. Harry: I didn't touch your lotions, man....well.... TTT: Well? Harry: Well, I might have used the Peppermint Slime for...ummm...reasons, man. [Tommy smiles and nods] TTT: I completely understand, dude. [Tommy then looks back toward the crowd of dudes and dudettes. All of the gnarly movie stars who have taken time out of their busy movie making schedules. The musicians who have stopped touring or recording new music just to be a part of the bodacious festivities. Sports stars taking time from training regimens to enjoy themselves for a night. All of this courtesy of the new PCW Champion. The man who is to represent the wrestling world as their totally gnarly spokesman. Honestly, who is BETTER than 'Totally Tubular' Tommy? Nobody, really. He should have had the title a long time ago] Jerry (Tommy's agent): Tommy, give us a speech. [The people at the party begin tapping their glasses and chanting 'Speech' at the guest of honor. Tommy acts embarrassed by all of the attention, but he paid Jerry a couple extra thousand bucks to start the speech chant. Tommy knows that there is nothing better than standing up in a room full of rich people and impressing them. To let them know that he's better than them so that way they keep coming back to him for things. That he's the biggest dog in the yard, and this is EXACTLY WHY he's the Champion now. It's speech time, dudes] TTT: First I would like to say welcome to the party. [The people cheer and raise their glasses to Tommy] TTT: I hope that you are all having a bodacious time, because I know that I am. I'd like to secondly address to all of you totally gnarly people for not turning your backs onto me. There have been a lot of totally heinous things written up about me in the papers. They have called me an evil, greedy dude who left handicapped children out in the cold. Well, I did do that but they forgot to mention that I gave all those totally retarded children free passes to those water parks that are going up!! [The crowd all nods to each other as if free passes to a water park makes up for destroying a childs school and home] TTT: Ever since I was a little compadre, watching my dad lacing up his boots before a match, I wanted to be a wrestler in the worst way. When he died in the ring I went numb and somehow must have forgotten that bodacious drive to be a wrestler. I did, however, go on to become the most gnarly surfer in the WORLD! [The crowd of people clap in approval of Tommy's great surfing accomplishments] TTT: Thank you. As I finished up dominating everything in the surfing world that's when that wrestling drive came back into my totally radical system. I immediately began training and got my mind focused on becoming the absolutely bodacious ever to grace the ring. The sport is in my blood and I didn't have a problem picking it up. In fact, I was what everyone called a prodigy. I had to go look that up in the dictionary, because I thought they were making fun of me, but it means that I'm just extra bodacious. [The crowd laughs] TTT: I entered Primetime Wrestling Academy with thoughts of one day entering the hallowed halls of PCW. That one day I'd be up there winning championships and wowing the totally radical crowds. Unfortunately, I had to pay a few dues on the way there because I was immediately thrown into a little place called NTICW. There I worked hard against so many dudes and dudettes who are now in PCW or OSE right now. As that era came to an end I was thrown out in the trash for no reason what so ever. I was forgotten by everyone and everything....except for one totally bodacious man. [The crowd sort of breathes in all at once to find out who this special person is] TTT: And that's 'Heavy Metal' Harry, my brother!! [The crowd claps for Harry and he just nods with a stoned grin fixed on his face] TTT: NTICW soon reformed and they signed us back up. It was there that I went undefeated and was on my way to dominating that place as champion when PCW gave me the call of a lifetime. They wanted me. They just didn't want me... [Tommy puts his arm around Harry] TTT: ...they wanted my bro too. We began our tag team legacy as the Cowabunga Connection and tore through every team that they ever threw at us. Once again, all these totally heinous politics started being thrown our way and we never truly won the PCW Tag Team titles. After nine to ten months of being one of the greatest PCW tag teams ever Harry suffered a back injury from those totally bogus dudes Akiyama and The End, known now as Skylar Green. I then began to learn the folly of my ways as a wrestler and it was that I did not embrace my true self. I was not accepting the person who I truly had become and was turning into. I, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, was a super villain and I had to start ACTING like it! I did. Skylar Green then found out that I'm not just some totally dumb surfer and that I can get things done. I claimed the Continental Championship as mine soon after the ending of the Cowabunga Connection and that's when it truly started to begin. Now...I'm the World Champion. It was a feat that my father, Radical Randy, was never able to capture during his career. I just hope that he's proud of his son for finally being the first in the family to capture the most gnarly gold in all of the wrestling world!! [The crowd claps loudly for the new champion as Tommy waves and smiles to them. He fakes a few tears for the sake of the bodacious babes in the crowd. They'll all flock to him later, telling him how sweet his speech was to his brother and his deceased father. Tommy will then sleep with many of them to give further celebration for his victory] TTT: Har, this champion thing is going to be a BREEZE!! Harry: Yeah, man. Fade to black
