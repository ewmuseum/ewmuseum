Coping with Villainy - Part 2 - Shai's Family

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 23, 2003 [Shailyn was raised in a most uptight Christian family. They believe in values and, most of all, their strong belief in God. They always taught good in their daughter and made sure that she was to spread good everywhere that she went. It wasn't until she met 'Totally Tubular' Tommy that her Christian values began to be questioned. She wondered why she was even holding herself back, not experiencing things while she was still young and full of life. Tommy was the one who taught her that, and got her to break through the wall that her parents put up. Now she feels so free and liberated that her life seems to have so much meaning now. It's like everything is clear and she can see that her future is going to be bright. A future with The Tubular One] [As Tommy sits in the living room, staring at the large portrait of Jesus Christ on their mantle, he begins to question why he's even there. He has a Pay Per View match in which he should be training for, even though Rent-A-Jerkface isn't even that much of a threat. It's mostly the stress of impressing those totally bodacious people that come to see him each and every week. They may boo him, but they respect him. The crowd respects that Tommy is the Continental Champion and that he's the BEST wrestler in PCW. It's only a matter of time. Now if he wasn't sitting in Shailyn's parents living room, he could get much closer to that goal. He doesn't understand why Shai wanted this. It would have been easier just for her to come over, give him a most excellent massage, and then they could just get on with the shagging. But now the gnarly super villain has to really work to get some tonight. Yes, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy has to work to get his poontang tonight] Shailyn's Dad: So, your a professional wrestler? [For some reason Shailyn's Dad, Bob, reminds Tommy of a blond Billy Crystal. Just the look, not the personality. The man sits across from Tommy like he's some kind of detective holding The Tubular One underneath a bright light] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: That's right, dude. I've got a most important match coming up this Sunday. I have to defend my Continental Title against some bogus dude named Rent-A-Jerkface. [As Tommy finishes Shailyn's mom comes into the living room carrying a platter full of freshly baked cookies. They look most delicious as Tommy grabs some of them off before she has a chance to sit them down. Shai's mom looks just like the usual housewife. Permed hair, large dresses, and a floral patterned apron. She always wears a smile across her face and everything is 'cute' to her] Shailyn's Mom: Tommy, you certainly are hungry! Don't worry, though, because there are plenty of cookies...JUST FOR YOU! [Shai's Mom, Marie, brushes Tommy's crotch and she sends him a very wicked looking smile. Tommy stops chewing on one of his cookies as he seems frightened. He then looks over at Bob and begins chewing again. Bob begins talking about some God stuff as Tommy just ignores it and leans over to his brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. Har sports a bag of ice on his head from that nasty fall he took for the tree earlier] TTT: Har, she just touched my crotch, dude. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Really, man? TTT: Yeah. Harry: Do you think she'll touch mine? TTT: GROSS! [Sitting diagonal from Tommy, Harry, and Shai is her older brother, Mikey. He's the one with the little daughter who enjoys the company of Mittens, the family cat. Luckily, Mittens is a fighter and avoided Harry's attempt at murder. Mikey is the typical older brother. Pissed off, overprotective, and gay] Mikey: Yeah, eat up. Maybe when your done you can try and kill our cat again. Harry: That cat is crazy, man. Mikey: You tried to rip it's head off. Harry: No way, man. TTT: Dude, I'm a super villian. Those are the types of things I'm supposed to do. [Everyone just looks at Tommy as if he just morphed into the Tin Man from the Wizard Of Oz] Mikey: Your retarded! Little girl: No, daddy...he's a stupid head. TTT: Your a stupid head! Mikey: Don't talk to my daughter like that, scumbag! Bob: Everyone just hold on a second!! Lets cool off for a moment. Let the steam off and see what's left of everyone. Ok...breathe in and out. Let the power of God running through your veins and lets talk out this disagreement like gentlemen. [Everyone is silent as Harry is really the only person in the room making noise. His loud crunching on the cookies is making it tough for anybody to really reflect on the situation at hand] TTT: This is a most awkward situation for me, dudes and dudettes. I should be at home, training for my match, but instead I've decided to come here...to be with you lovely people. Mikey: I thought you said that you were a super villain. TTT: I am. Because I'm lying. Little girl: STUPID HEAD! TTT: GRRR!! Bob: Tommy, where is it that you'd like to be? [That's a really vague question that runs through Tommy's mind. There are an infinite amount of places that he'd like to be right now other than Shailyn's parents house. In fact, he'd rather be sitting in a room with Rent-A-Jerkface than be sitting there with Shai's family. The tension is already bogus with the attempted murder of the family cat, Mittens. It's not going to make it any better with Tommy just blurting out all of the places where he'd rather be. But, Tommy has always stuck by the rule of being honest with people no matter if he's a super villain or not] TTT: To be honest, Bob, I'd much rather be at my bodacious beach pad with your daughter. She's giving me the most excellent massage of my life. Then when all is through she'll put on that most excellent lingerie I got her for Christmas, model for me, eventually strip, and then we'll play Yahtzee until Shai falls asleep. Mikey: I'm going to kill you... Harry: And I want Marie to touch my crotch, man. Mikey: NO! I'M GOING TO KILL YOU!!! Little girl: STUPID HEAD!!! [Mikey leaps up and attacks Harry, who has a mouth full of cookies. The entire family piles on top to try and stop the two from tearing each other apart, and that's when Tommy's cell phone decides to ring. He pulls the tiny Sprint PCS phone from his blazer pocket] TTT: 'Totaly Tubular' Tommy, resident super villain. [The voice on the other end seems to be confused] Voice: Super villain? TTT: Totally, dude. Tommy's agent: Tommy, your not a super villain. TTT: Dude, I totally am a super villain. It's great! Tommy's agent: Tommy, you CAN'T be a super villain. You do realize that you have very respectable businesses running and I don't think customers would want to be buying things from you if your a super villain. TTT: Who cares, dude. Being a super villain is totally bodacious. I get to say whatever I want, do whatever I want, and most of all I get to HURT whoever I want. Including the ever so heinous Rent-A-Jerkface. Tommy's agent: Well, that's why I called, Tom. I wanted to make sure everything was in order for you on Sunday. I really would hate if you had any doubts about Rent-A-Hero. He's a tough opponent, granted on a losing streak, but he's one tough customer. TTT: Greg, it's taken care of already. I'm a bodacious super villain and he's the super hero. Everyone knows that the villain always comes out on top. [Nothing but silence on the other end of the phone] TTT: It's not a losing steak he's on, Greg, it's just him being a loser, compadre. What kind of superhero loses about every match he wrestles? It doesn't make any sense. Tommy's agent: What's going on in the background there, where are you? I hope your not having one of those wild parties at Hotel California!! TTT: No, I'm at Shailyn's parents house, dude. Harry hit on her mom and now Shai's brother is trying to kick Harry's ass. Only it seems to me that good ol' Bob is laying quite a gnarly smackdown on his own son. It's pretty radical you should see it!! Tommy's agent: Tom, you ARE KILLING ME!! JESUS! Listen, get your super villain ass OUT OF THERE and go home. You have training to do and I'd like it not to be having your stoner brother fighting people. TTT: Ok, dude. [Tommy hangs up his cell phone and places it back in his pocket. He calls over to Harry] TTT: Dude, we need to head back to the beach house. I've got some most excellent training to get to. [Harry crawls out from underneath the pile with the cookies still in his mouth. The two men exit the house as Harry's face can be seen contorted with a smile] TTT: Dude, what's up with you? Harry: Mpmph, ljdl...man. TTT: She touched your crotch? [Harry holds up a piece of paper] TTT: DUDE, YOU GOT DIGITS!! AWESOME!!! [Tommy gives his brother a high five] TTT: Ok....now where did we park the car? To be continued...
