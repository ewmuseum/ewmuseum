A Time in the life of.... Ryan Moore

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered The last time we had decided to follow a member of the elusive band 'PCG', we found Rich Sherwin walk into a Karaoke Bar and switch off the player while the song from their most-archest rivals 'The Heartbreakers' boomed out. The heated battle between these two teams is something that hasn't really had the kind of climax that so many of these wrestling fans would like to see, and with other various things happenning in the careers of both Teams, one can only wonder when we are going to see the 'Ultimate End' to a dispute that may just exist until the very end of time! Sure, Sherwin and Moore don't have to worry about fighting their own personal battle on the side... but unfortunently, the two members of the Pure PoP-tastical team see their minor look-over as being nothing more than a look of disrespect... and better more, an opportunity to take the win without too much... if any hassle. For those of you who are wondering what in Gods name is being spoken about right here... both members of 'The Heartbreakers' are fighting the 'Good Fight' against all those who oppose the color 'Pink'... something that is the color of various garments, books... and womens... erhm, yeah, I think you'll get the picture. As stated before, in the last 'PCG' segment, Rich Sherwin was the character whom we had decided to spend all the time with... however, today, we look over at the other member of the 'Pretty Cool Guys'. Once more, for those of you who are still living with Osama Bin Laden in a cave, we're talking about Ryan Moore. Will this youngster find himself in as much 'Trouble' as his Tag Partner? Will this kid do something to anger the world... or even anger the masses of Pink who will, without any doubt be cheering Johnny and Jack onto victory this Friday Night at Fusion? Nobody can place their fingers down on anything until the action has been carried through. The scene slowly began to open up inside the cosy confinements of a record store. Keeping in tune with the supposed 'serious' vibes that were being carried along with the 'Musical Match' this coming Friday Night, the picture was quick to open to its complete state. The layout of the room was pretty typical for a store of its kind with racks of Albums to the left and the singles to the right. In the space that existed between both sets of racks, there were some smaller ones in 2 column, 4 row fashion. Once more, albums on the left... and yes, the other materials were hanging rather high on the right. The visual looked over to the counter at the far end of the store where a relativly slim man was standing. He was dressed in a white T-Shirt, some black pants and a bandana. This individual looked to be in his early thirities and looked like quite the 'Alternate' being. The visual span around almost immediatly after, just in time to watch the store door open and hear the little bell chime twice. Ryan Moore was the character that stepped through that very door... dressed to the full in the type of street-clothes that would even make the most fashionable of PoP Stars take notice and blush. The relativly slim guy behind the counter saw Ryan and silently squealed in delight. He opened up the wave of conversation with the first line... a line that most people would hear at a fast-food like restaurant. 'Welcome to Good Burger... home of the Good Burger... can I take your order?', he asked, sounding rather pleased to see somebody with as much fame as Ryan walk into his store. Moore looked around the inside of the store, rolling his head around a last time before meeting the stare of the assistant with his own. He cracked a rather faint smile, just to show that he a) Came in peace and b) Was well mannered. Curious to discuss something that was related to a combination of both wrestling and Music, Moore walked over to the counter... a place where he greeted the bewildered assistant with a gentle 'appreciative' responce. The tone of Moore's voice was somewhat softer than usual, namely because he wasn't too sure on this guys 'Mental State' following the hearing of a Fast Food welcoming in a record store. 'Er... Eh... Erm, hey there.... I'm Ryan Moore and I understand that this is one of the coolest stores around the immediate area.', he said, continuing to hold up a false smile... and a false sense of 'Awareness' in the company of a possible psycho-killer-freak like the dude standing opposite him. The next few moments of 'conversation' between the two men was nothing but a harsh silence... not something that you'd really hear when it came to the silence between 'everyday' conversation, but the kind that really began to show the levels of 'UNcomfortable Nature'. The dude behind the counter side-stepped to his right and walked around to the other side... now meaning that he was but a few steps away from our heroic PoP-Icon. 'You know... we sell all kinds of music in this here location... from Rock... to Metal... to Dance... to PoP... to 'The Hear...'', the assistant began before Ryan held up his hand in a true method of stealing from WWE Employee, 'The Rock'. Moore hadn't been too bothered about the kinds of music that were on offer these days... although his attention did suddenly perk up again at the mention of the music that he makes. However, his attention really got to its boiling point when he heard the names of his arch-rivals partially spoken. Trying to keep cool, he turned to face the employee, slightly shaking with the vibrations of his own anger. 'EXXXXXCUSE ME.... would you care to go on a little more about those... nasty, horrible, no talent, thieves?!', Moore responded in a manner that would even make somebody like Mike Tyson fall down and weep like a little girl. Of course, the Mike Tysons that we were talking about... were those who didn't box for a living of have voices that go all high pitched. The assistant was a little shaken at the tone behind Moore's voice at first... but soon came back to reality after just imagining that he was no more of a loud-mouth biatch than Miss Jenny Lopez. 'The... Heartbreakers are all but set to release their first single within the next few days or so following their victory against 'The Pretty Cool Guys' on Fusion this coming week. Everyone is totally set for Heart and Valentine to take the win... infact, they're so set for the outcome that nobody is taking bets on anything other than a clean sweep for the H-Breakers.', the assistant proclaimed, sounding pleased with himself for even knowing anything near that amount of knowledge. Ryan began to scowl a little, hating every mention of the words that were so close to spelling out another 'W-I-N' for the 'Heartbreakers'. It seems as though the whole world is against the real artists for this contest.... but somewhere, somehow... there has to be somebody hoping that both Ryan and Rich would be able to do the business against these fools... and show them why singing is left to the real perfectionists and nobody else! 'You know what?', Ryan began, his voice taking a turn for the better, leaving the world of pain... the world of hurt... and the world of a possible 'Pretty Cool Guy' loss somewhere in Africa. He looked around the store for another few seconds before making eye contact with the assistant again and continuning his little speach, '... you and the World all claim that 'The Heartbreakers' are going to be the ones that walk out of Friday Fusion with that record deal. You all... its like you don't even look at the way that WE... and WE ALONE eliminated 'The Heartbreakers' from that stunning Tag Team Battle Royal just a mere three weeks ago at PCW Pay-Per-View 'Gold and Glory'. Its like you don't remember that... so I'm going to be kind and refresh your memory... WE HOLD ONE OVER THEM! A legit win against those fools... and then Rich Sherwin holds an individual win over them... remember the Fatal Fourway that came... hmm, just a few days before showtime?' The assistant looked at Ryan Moore with a confused look, one that was clear to show that he only supported 'The Heartbreakers' since they really made themselves known in the world of PoP by taking a supposed recording chance out of the hands of 'PCG' and subsequently put it on the line for them both this coming Friday night. Ryan didn't look impressed as the assistant seemed to fade off into being nothing more than a backstage puppet. He looked around the store for somebody else that could hold conversation with him... somebody that actually knew something about 'Wrestling', the 'Heartbreakers' and 'Primetime Championship Wrestling' because it was painfully obvious that this interesting fellow... didn't know anything about the three topics listed. 'Dude... I can't believe you totally tried to give me the low-down on my very own match against Heart and Valentine this week... when, you totally know nothing about anything in those fields.', Ryan stated, sounding rather disgusted to even be talking to such a low form of life. The visual looks around the immediate area again, Moore turns away from the assistant and begins to walk to the exit of the store, his head held low and his face showing a little more 'emotion' than usual. We follow Ryan as he reopens the store door and steps out into the street. What could be happenning now we wonder? Ryan turns to face the source of the visual, but looks around once or twice to get accustomed with his surroundings. After a few moments, he slowly begins to get this show on the road, '... Well, it seems that another day for the Greatest Boy Band in all of the World is coming to an end... I spoke to some totally uncool Loser who thought he knew stuff about my GRAND match this comnig Friday and it has really made me feel disgusted to have done so. I've been told by this same loser that the world is expecting to see those... 'Other Team Players' take the contract... but I'm damn sure that all the 13 and 14yr old chicks that followed both Richie and myself through NTICW will be around to cheer us onto victory! However, while I choose to squander on that note, I think its about time I do something for the masses watching me from their homes, I think its time I give you a reason why PCG will do so well after WE win the recording deal this Friday!' Ryan takes a few steps away from the visual device, making sure that his entire body is in the shot and that its placed around the mid-point. He looks down to the floor and allows his arms to flop down his sides before standing alone for a period of about ten seconds. We wait patiently for something to happen... and then, some groovy music begins to play along in the background. It reminds us all of... well, nothing that we'd ever been able to hear before! Moore starts to dance along to the track before the singing can get underway. 'Oh... Baby, you mean so much to me... I try to hide it but you'll always see... I, like to hide, but its wearing pretty thin... Ooo Yeah..... ooo yeah-e-yeah...', Moore sings out aloud at the top of his voice... before calming down into a state of silence and looking into the visual for one last time. He sheepishly waves to all of the adoring young female fans that may be catching this interesting show before walking off out of the scene. The image fades out slowly to black as we reach the end of another Chapter in the PCG Story. Do Rich and Ryan have what it takes to defeat 'The Heartbreakers' and chase their dream this week? Do they have all of Kirk Angel's three 'I's' to get all the way through this epic battle and take the contract that should be theirs? Its been stated once already by the opposition narrator and now its going to be stated again by me... this could be one of the very matches of 2002! Be sure you catch it... this FRIDAY NIGHT... on PCW FUSION!
