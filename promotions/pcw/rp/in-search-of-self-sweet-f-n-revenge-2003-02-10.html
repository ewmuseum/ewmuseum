In Search Of Self: Sweet F'N Revenge!

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-02-03 Monday. This was the last day at the hotel for Skitzo. Turmoil was just around the corner and his fun-filled four-day fuckupalooza was coming to an end. Albeit the hotel manager had consistently tried to ruin him, Skitzo didn’t really give a fuck. The fire took a good hour to put out, and the roof took even longer to wash clean of smoke and soot. The hotel manager never liked Skitzo anyway, and somewhere deep down he probably knew that Adrian Paul a.k.a. William Williams was a.k.a. Skitzo…but he’d rather punish than persecute. This man was a stuck up bastard. Traipsing back into his room late in the evening, Skitzo collapsed into the Jacuzzi and let the bubbles wash away the pain and dirt of the day. Where Clyde went after he didn’t know, all he knew was that the little fucker of a hotel manager had to pay, and he would…oh yes he would. He rubbed his hands a lot. He closely resembled a greasy car salesman, the kind always clasping their hands, counting the money as you buy some heap of shit that only cost $3 to put together in the first place. He was bald too, but he had one of those “Honest, I’m not bald” hairdos, long stringy hair combed over the top from the middle, he looked like a prick but his money convinced him otherwise. From the pursed lips to the dingy cheap suits to the lack of a wedding ring, anyone could tell he wasn’t married, and rightly so, anyone who wanted this little fucker had to be after his money. In any case he was an excellent target for a classic Skitzo prank. Sure, P.W.A. were the Princes of Prankage, but Skitzo had his own little sick twisted flavour of prank, and the hotel manager would get a taste on this fine day! As he got dressed for the day, a knock came at the door and Skitzo answered it to find none other than the little sack of shit himself standing there, still rubbing his hands. Skitzo shot him a dirty look, to which the manager replied with a cheesy grin. The tension was thick in the air as the manager walked into the room, without so much as by your leave, and began inspecting what looked like a very tidy room for Skitzo. Hotel Manager: What’s been going on here then… Skitzo: What? Hotel Manager: The drugs…one of our cleaners found a plastic container filled with LSD yesterday while you were on the roof. Care to explain, Mr. Williams? Skitzo: It wasn’t mine…I found them here after I woke up yesterday, and I didn’t pack them! Hotel Manager: So what were they doing there? Skitzo: I don’t fucking know!!! I didn’t want to touch them any-fucking-way! Skitzo took umbrage, tapping his foot and crossing his arms. As he was about to point his finger at the manager and make another proclamation, he suddenly remembered the events of the last day. The insulting toilet, the fifteen tablets…and a look of sudden realisation spread across his face. Skitzo: Hold on a cotton-picking minute…I flushed those LSD tablets yesterday…how then did someone find them AFTER I disposed of them? Hotel Manager: ………………………Erm………we only found the container and it was clearly labelled LSD! Skitzo: You just told me you found the container filled with LSD… Hotel Manager: I mean-… Skitzo: You planted those drugs didn’t you…and you wanted me to take them because then you could sue me or some shit, DIDN’T YOU!!! Hotel Manager: I’ll have you know my establishment is not the forum for drug trafficking! How dare you make such accusations! Skitzo: You my fatheaded friend are a liar and a bastard!!! What if I told you I had proof and I had planted my own little camera in the room that caught you or one of your cronies planting LSD in my room? Hotel Manager: I’d tell you that you’re a liar… Skitzo: Wanna call my bluff, cream puff? The hotel manager looked somewhat nervous, rubbing his hands together a little more vigorously, and before he could utter another word Skitzo advanced on him, careful not to indulge in anything that could be considered harassment, and instead rested both hands on the hotel manager’s shoulders, staring him in the eye. Skitzo: I don’t know what your problem with me is…but get a goddamn grip of yourself and leave me alone. I have a lot of friends in high places I could’ve recommended this place to, but do you just want me to leave? The hotel manager didn’t really know what to say at this point. His original plan to vacate the hotel of Skitzo’s presence had failed, and now Mr. Psycho was on to him. For the past four days Skitzo had been in search of self, trying to figure himself out while he prepared for Turmoil and the chance to be in one of the most prominent clusterfucks in PCW’s tradition. The March Madness was a chance to shine yet again, like he did at Alliances and in the Alamo Match to win his soda contract. He seemed to prosper most in matches where the odds were shy against him, why should a singles contest be any different? Skitzo was going to try as hard as he could anyway, like he did at every other event, even if he lost why should that mean he couldn’t put on a good show? The problem with this hotel manager was that he didn’t care what happened to Skitzo, he just didn’t want his operation getting screwed up by an idiot who just wanted to cause trouble for the hell of it. Staring into Skitzo’s eyes, frustration building up inside caused the hotel manager to just mouth a ‘Sorry’, turn and walk away, shutting the door behind him as he left, slowly and sheepishly rubbing his hands together still. Situations like that got to him, everyone against the schizophrenic, that’s how it would be on Friday when he and some other “extremists” went head-to-head for the title in another clusterfuck. How could the odds be against a man who’s more prolific in battle royals than any other AND comes with a lethal 20” black rubber dildo? That thing nearly killed Nerezza in vampire form, imagine what it’d do to people like Young Tiger and Jon Kano! Ok, so Pete! might swallow it in all the wrong places, and Brianna Washington might swallow it in all the right places, but any way you looked at it this was in the bag for Skitzo. It was the same with Turmoil; he was rested up and ready to perform. Although he was ready to wrestle and so forth, there was no guarantee he could win…as long as someone ate dildo it didn’t really matter. For the moment however Turmoil and Fusion seemed like a million miles away, for the moment…Skitzo had a slick little rat bastard who needed his ass handed to him. So he took off down the hallways, nodding at people politely on his way to the lobby. He never met the hotel manager again, and what staff he did lay his eyes on, they were careful to avoid him, obviously the entire establishment was against him, but Skitzo had a plan. A potato in the exhaust pipe of the manager’s Oldsmobile ’88 would suffice, that was…until he hit the ground floor and passed the staff room, hearing a familiar voice… “-…Yeah I’ll see you in about an hour…I love you too baby, aha…daddy loves you darling, now put on your sister……………Becky? Hiya, it’s daddy…yup I’m in the hotel…yes sweetie there’s lots of people here, aha…look darling daddy has to run, I’ll see you in an hour…ok, love you…byeeee…” Skitzo skittered out of the way as the hotel manager put the phone down and ran out of the staff room, doing a little dance as he ran up a small flight of stairs leading towards the lounge. Spotting a little business lounge opposite it, Skitzo sprinted across the lobby, leaping up the stairs into the lounge in time to see the manager leave the other lounge again, heading back to the staff room. Moving into the business lounge, Skitzo spots one man with a homosexually inclined goatee and a laptop hooked up to one of the many PCs in the room, downloading games or pornography or something. Skitzo: How did I miss this place?! IT’S SO OBVIOUS WHAT I MUST DO!!!
