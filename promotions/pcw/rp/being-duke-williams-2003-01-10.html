Being.... Duke Williams

10 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 - 01 - 03 A gentle breeze ushers-by kicking up particles of sand on this dusty open road that passes through this dank wooden town, a town missing a nametag but I'd bet it's right in the heart of Redkneckville, namely Texas. Wooden buildings litter either side of this central road, a solitude little town with more activity coming from the elements rather than its natives, a town best described as a baron waste land. 'I'm gonna be rolling into town like a runaway locomotive and leaving an impact similar to that of TNT sweet cheeks!' murmurs a distinctive voice merely a few feet away. Parked on the outskirts a Cadillac sits ominously roaring its twin-cab engine, as the driver and his passenger converse between one-another, scheming and plotting this sort of discussion would belong to but one party 'The Conniving Collective'. Twitching his foot over the vehicles accelerator, jerking the bonnet in the process, we capture the dynamic duo in all their glory as the camera basks over the wardrobe of Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'Hold on to your hat baby... The Malicious Man is coming home, hoss!' cried the Emerald Menace as he sarcastically portrayed himself as Primetime Championship Wrestling's Duke Williams. Adjusting the peak of his cowboy hat, preventing the glare of this intense sunshine to bother him further we can see that the Jester has lost his usual velvet business suit in favour of blue denim jeans with a huge glistening belt buckle, a chequered shirt and spur boots that only add to the masquerade. The final touch however was the key to this charade as the clean-shaven face of Gaz Green had been subjected to thick bristled hair and more importantly the cowboy whiskers that give the whole Duke Williams awe. 'Gaz, why are we dressed like Spaghetti Western extras?' retorts the sweet and innocent voice of Natalia Miyagawa as she rolled her eyes. Following up with a sigh her client finally replies, still however in his rugged tough man voice he offers his valet a valid explanation. 'Darling if I'm to be a Champion, then those childish pranks got to be left in the bedroom, it's time to get with the programme and that means a whole new serious side to Gareth Green you hear....' The Soviet Scarlet absorbed the explanation solemnly, her face telling a thousand stories, its biggest message being 'HELP. 'Gareth Green?' questions the separated wife of OSW performer Alexander Miyagawa. 'And why not? Gareth is my real name and Gaz seems like a city slicker with a damn cell phone....' The automobile blazes back into life and with a thunderous roar of the engine it moves forward under a dense cloud of black smoke. Strolling into the heart of this flimsy town, everything appears desolate as the windows remain boarded up and business seems over for the day, well for most, a melody playing draws the attention of the Jester and his foot once again hits the accelerator. Parking outside the establishment Gaz Green peered up at the nametag, which reads, 'WILLIAM'S PUB 'N' GRUB' 'This be looking like some fancy joint hoss!' injected the masquerading Jester as he left the confines of his Cadillac, accompanied as always by Natalia Miyagawa they both waltz through two panels of wood and into the bar. Wooden flooring, wooden walls, the place was a termites heaven. A hoard of people sat in conversation as they converse over tankards of ale, the more formal of these natives sat under a cloud of smoke, puffing and wheezing they played a hand of cards as coins hit the table surface with low pitched thuds. Other people meanwhile were strutting their stuff on the dance floor accompanied by the bellowing Wurlitzer Juke Box that plays the distinctive 'Big Balls in Cowtown' by Asleep At The Wheel. Soaking in the atmosphere Gaz gave a little smirk, curling the corners of his mouth to that of a smile he proceeded up to the crowded bar whilst toying with his false whiskers he dragged the excess baggage of Natalia Miyagawa by the wrist as she fought with great reluctance. 'LET ME GO... LET ME GO... LET ME GO...' 'Excuse me sir but the little lady would like for ya to leave go of her slender wrist, huh!?!' said a spluttering voice from behind, ignored by the Jester the gentleman’s retort soon had the Front Office Entertainment Manager's full attention as he placed his hand on his shoulder. Washing his lips with saliva, stroking his moustache and grinding his teeth to the gum, finally the Corporate Jester turned to greet his assailant who was a short, stumpy gentleman wearing a shirt that was a size too small and a pair of braces that tested his trousers elasticity as they rose the legs to the point where his socks were revealed. The stranger did indeed sport a pair of ankle-tappers. 'Obviously son-shine you don't know who you be messing with... Hoss?!?' hissed Gaz Green in reply. The unknown man squinted his narrow eyes and looked at the PCW Jester dumbfounded for several minutes, rubbing his hands into his beer soaked shirt he looks over to his drinking partners for help in this matter but they offer nothing more than a gaze of intrigue themselves. Like a Mexican wave the glasses rose and the content was swallowed, everybody at this time had ceased and were looking over to see where this dispute would be resolved, be it gentlemanly through conversation or the usual punch first, ask questions later technique. 'You be Mr. Williams off the tele... You'se look a little bigger on the box...' stated the man with a smile as Gaz merely nodded with an up and down motion, extending his hand to gesture further. 'A trick by the cameramen hoss... You be right though... You be looking at the Malice Man Duke Williams right here hoss... The man who will beat the dipshit Xavier Kannon at Anah... Shanghaied!' continued the Lord Of Laughter still in full portrayal of his opponent for Sunday Adrenaline, the man who has been granted the opportunity of taking his PCW Tag Team Championship. A few grunts and groans filtered and the handshake commenced when suddenly the stranger began to have a seizure, jerking violently the hands remained locked as the camera peered down unveiling the infamous hand buzzer 'The Most Electrifying Move In Primetime'. Natalia watched the scene, something she has witnessed a thousand times before, even being the one to utilise the move on several occasions as the onlookers stared in disbelief. 'HEART ATTACK!!!!' screamed Gaz Green at half-mast. Suddenly another of these natives looked down noticing the dodgy device sparking and fizzling as the victim yelped in pain, his colleague soon retorted something that would spurn this pleasant setting into that of a bar-room brawl, Pleasantville was about to be hit with chaos and carnage courtesy of the Conniving Collective. 'What be that thing Mr? This be your fucking fault....' The man peddled back before unleashing a punch that breezed past the beautiful face of Natalia Miyagawa as her eyes widened from the near miss, suddenly there was contact. The crash was from the clashing of body limbs but Gaz Green watched unscathed as he positioned his victim in the way of the knockout blow causing the poor native to slouch to the ground holding his fragile jaw. Giggling Gaz toyed with the assailant to try his luck a second time when without warning a clenched fist shot forward but utilising his wrestling knowledge he soon reversed the attack to that of a judo throw as the man went hurtling into the wooden bar. 'That be my husband you....' screamed a women as she lunged forward swinging a leather hand-bag, her attacks flawed as Natalia kicked out her foot and the female tripped over almost decapitating another bystander. 'YOU FLIMSY BITCH!!!' mourned the bystander nursing his head. Suddenly the bar erupted into chaos as drinks flew up into the air, tables literally were over-turned and the place escaladed into a slugfest as punches were both thrown and taken. Friends hit friends, families were forced to face one-another and no matter creed, colour, religion, you became a target as the bar turned to that of a senseless blood bath. - Meanwhile - Rubbing his hands in glee and accompanied by a more jovial valet, Gaz Green tilted his cowboy hat as he extended his hand to open the door to the Cadillac in a gentlemanly fashion. Racing over to the drivers side the car soon roared to life and the duo vanished into the sunset as another devious prank was fulfilled, this time under the mask of Duke Williams who waits in the wings with his own retort as the mind games of Adrenaline come to light.
