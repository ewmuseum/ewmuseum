It's a conspiracy....

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-03-02 *Scene opens up with Ricky Thunder plowing down the highway with reckless abandon, as a street sign says 34th street exit. He swerves off of the exit and heads off down the ramp at about 60 miles per hour, at the bottom of the ramp he blows through a red light and swings a left in front of the crossing traffic. Ricky pulls up in front of Nations Bank and leaves the car parked on the curb, where he jumps out and heads inside. He walks up the sidewalk and enters through the huge glass front doors. Once inside he notices a small old lady in line, and about 3 tellers who are doing absolutely nothing. Ricky heads over to one of the women's windows and slams the check down on the counter* Ricky: I need to open an account. Teller: Well, sir, you need to go get in line behind that woman there. Ricky: Bullsh^t. You three are standing here spouting off at the mouth not doing a damn thing and you can't open my account? Teller: Not now. Not after you talked to me that way. Ricky: I want to speak to the branch manager. Go get the damn manager. GO! *The young lady backs away from the counter as Ricky is standing there his fist clenched around his check glaring at the terrified young brunette. She steps into a side office and about 10 seconds later a rather large man wearing a pin striped shirt appears. He stares across the counter at Ricky neither of them speaking at first, then the man finally breaking the silence.* Man: Is there a problem here sir? Ricky: Don't talk to me like that. You want to intimidate someone you can go call your kids into the room, but don't you ever try to intimidate me like that again. Man: Look... Ricky: NO! You look! Here is the bottom line. I have money, I want to open an account. You are paying these whores to stand around and do absolutely nothing! This is bullsh^t. I am not going to wait behind some 75 year old woman who is probably going to have a damn heart attack while standing there in line just to open an account, you can kiss my ass! Man: Out! Get the hell out of my bank! *Ricky dives across the counter as the man takes a step back, security rushes from the surveillance room and grabs Ricky's arms. The pull him to his feet and drag him out the door. As they are pulling him out he yells out across the bank* Ricky: This ain't over you old bast^rd. You want to cross me!? Fine! You think you are Shabazz? You think you are Tyler Nelson? You think you are The Chef? You can kiss my ass! I will stomp you from one end of this building to the other. You're nothing! You're.. *Ricky's final statement cut off as he is shoved out the front door of the bank. He spits at the doors and straightens his clothes out before he heads down the sidewalk where his car is parked. When he gets to his car he notices a parking ticket on his windshield. He snatches it off and reads the $75.00 fine. He wads it up and throws it on the ground with disgust. Suddenly from across the street comes the ticketing officer.* Officer: What's going on? Ricky: Nothing. I am leaving. Officer: I'm sorry, did you say you were leaving or littering? Because I heard you say leaving I thought, but I know I saw you litter. Ricky: Give me a break! Jesus Christ! Will it f^ckin shut you up if I pick it? *Ricky opens his car door and grabs the wad of paper on the ground. He slams his car door shut again as the officer peers down over his sunglasses.* Officer: I thank you kindly for not littering my streets. However, here is your fine for doing so in the first place. *Ricky snatches the ticket from the officer, grumbling all the while.* Ricky: $125.00!! Are you f^ckin crazy?! *The officer whips off another ticket labeled disorderly conduct* Officer: There is another $60.00 to add to your collection. Anything else before I go? Ricky: Yeah, if your going to fine me for littering then damn it, I am going to litter. *Ricky dumps the tickets right back out on the ground and glares up at the officer, who has his hand on his nightstick. The officer takes a step back from the car and glances at the tickets on the ground.* Officer: Could you step out of the car sir? Ricky: No. Not without a reason. Littering is not an offense to bother getting out of the car over. Disorderly conduct, is not an offense I can be arrested for. As for a parking ticket, it will merely get you laughed out of the courts. So I beg you to please step away from my vehicle and kindly kiss my ass. *Ricky pulls the car from the curb and takes off down 34th street back towards the expressway. The cop standing in the middle of the street staring at him just shaking his head. Ricky pulls onto the expressway and guns the engine in the old Shelby as he makes his way up the road a couple miles further. Ricky reaches over and grabs his cell phone lying in the passenger seat. He dials in a number and listens as the phone starts to ring.* Ricky: Yo, what's up man? Oh yeah? Cool. Hey I am about 25 miles away I guess, want to give me some directions? Yeah about 20 miles from Manhattan. Aight, a right off the freeway, and a left at the second light. Ok what building? Cool, I will be there in about a half hour. *Ricky tosses the cell phone back into the passenger seat as he looks down at the speedometer reading 85. He drops the Cobra into 5th as he punches the gas and the tires squeal. He races the engine, swerving in and out of traffic, as the speedometer races to 125. Ricky continues barreling down the highway until he sees the sign for Manhattan. He cuts across 3 lanes of traffic as he jumps of the exit ramp. He skids to a stop at the light at the bottom of the ramp. When it turns green he is all over the gas again, spinning tires taking off. He blows through the first red light and fishtails around the corner of the second one. Ricky continues punching the gas and shifting gears until he is in front of the building. He slides the car sideways in the middle of the street as he turns around and parks in front of the building. He gets out of the car and head up to the doorstep ringing the doorbell. Finally a familiar voice is heard on the intercom, the a buzz that lets him in. Ricky enters and makes his way through the house, which has been heavily designed in antiques, following the sounds of the ring. He heads upstairs, where he encounters SuMa beating the hell out of his sparring partner. Ricky enters the room, and all the action stops.* SuMa: What's up Ricky! Glad to see you made it! Ricky: Man this day has sucked! What the hell is wrong with New Yorkers? SuMa: Didn't you say that about New Jersey as well? Ricky: No, there I said what the hell is wrong with Americans. Sparring Partner: You don't like it, get the hell out of the country! Ricky: You little bast^rd. After the day I've had you dare talk to me that way? You don't know... SuMa: Relax Ricky, maybe he's right. Maybe we should try to adapt. *SuMa moves across the ring swiftly nearly beheading the sparring partner with a clothesline, and he then begins stomping away at him.* SuMa: Or maybe you should learn when to shut the hell up! *SuMa grabs the guys and throws him out of the ring. He sits down against the turnbuckle as he grabs a drink of his water.* Ricky: Man, first the bank wouldn't open me an account. I dunno why either, just being assholes! The this cop tried to ticket me for everything under the sun. I'm surprised he did write me a ticket for the gray clouds in the sky! I am disliking this country more and more every day SuMa. If it weren't for my obligations... SuMa: Obligations? You don't have any obligations. You signed a contract for PCW. You want out, then leave. Quit! Walk away! Be like your brother Ricky, walk away before it's your time to. Live the remainder of your life wondering what could have happened, what will happen, what might have been! Go ahead Ricky, WALK AWAY! Ricky: So you too! You are going to sh^t on me today too eh? I don't need this sh^t! Maybe I will walk away, maybe I will quit before it is my time! But something I won't do is leave before I get my revenge on each and every person who has put their filthy hands on me so far. This Friday I start with Mark Makonie. He will be the first victim. He will not however be the last. For you see, as I continue to fight, Tyler Nelson continues to throw people in front of him, much like that of a human shield. However, I will not leave PCW until I have made a mockery of him and entire bunch of groupies. SuMa: Now that's more like it! Quit b^tching about everybody and DO something! Ricky: But that's not all. You see, another small problem we have is that of Miyagawa. SuMa: Here we go.... Ricky: I single handedly eliminated everyone from that match on Fusion. Had it not been for me, Miyagawa would have had no chance. How does he repay me? With a cheapshot. What the camera did not see, is what happened off screen. With Miyagawa's blatant use of a chair, he was able to pin me. If he hadn't done so, I would have made short work of him and I would have totally obliterated Lovecraft. Everyone knows that! SuMa: A chair? I didn't see the camera cut away long enough for him to use a chair. Ricky: I know what happened SuMa I was there, you weren't! SuMa: Riiiiiiiight. Anyhow... Ricky: You don't believe me either? You know what it don't matter, fact of the matter is, when I am done with Ultimate Authority, it will be Miayagawa's turn to Feel The Thunder!
