Empire Saga™ Part Five: Making another enemy and Gaining more Territory…

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/8/02 Empire Saga™ Part Five: Making another enemy and Gaining more Territory… [{The camera opens up within the familiar office of ‘The Chief Anarchist’ John Crichton, focused upon the doorway, amply guarded by two of Crichton’s familiar bodyguards. The camera then begins to pan around to the right, past the bullet-proof glass window, past the extravagant green rubber plant in the corner of the room, past the lavish picture of dogs playing poker, only stopping at the sight of Crichton’s lavish mahogany desk and his equally lavish leather chair. However, it is not John Crichton sat in the leather chair, but an old, Mediterranean man, wearing an expensive-looking grey suit and smoking a rather large Cuban cigar, idly blowing smoke in no particular direction.}] Man: How long will he be? Henchman Reginald: I have no idea Don Rico Darnelli, he is in his personal gym training. Don Rico Darnelli: Well can’t you go and get him? This meeting is very important to both parties! Henchman Reginald: We informed him of your arrival Don Rico, but John Crichton hates to be disturbed during training. Don Rico Darnelli: [{Rising angrily from the leather seat, pounding the mahogany table furiously}] That is unacceptable! [{Immediately after this statement the door leading in/out of the office swings open…all heads turn to see John Crichton stood there, still wearing his knee-long leather jacket, with a white sweat towel draped over his shoulder.}] Crichton: What is unacceptable Don Rico? Don Rico Darnelli: Oh…uh…nothing…John [{Sitting back down in the leather chair.}] Crichton: Good to hear. Apparently the rumours had even reached the tentative ears of one of the most influential Mafia bosses in Chicago. He had no idea how the rumours got out…he figured he must have some kind of leak within the empire…it didn’t bother him that much because it help business, it persuaded people to cave in to his demands before any kind of physical violence was necessary…in fact he was planning to use the very propaganda technique to help him if his usual ‘persuasion’ techniques failed. The only thing was, the rumour escaped earlier than he suspected…this troubled him greatly. He had a spy in the midst…there was somebody within his Criminal Empire who is either working for someone else or is being paid off by someone else. Either way he had to do something and do it fast. An example needed to be made, but the question was how? And to whom? [{Crichton walks over to his desk and stands there for a second, simply staring at the Don. Don looks around to both henchmen, obviously confused at Crichton’s actions}] Don Rico Darnelli: Wha…what’s wrong? Crichton: You seem to be sat on MY chair! Don Rico Darnelli: [{The realisation of his actions obviously just come to him as he prompt stands up from the seat and begins to back away, graciously}] I’m sorry I didn’t realise… Crichton: That’s okay…It’s an easy mistake to make I guess. [{Don Rico hesitantly moves around the side of the mahogany desk and sits upon the less comfortable wooden chair, directly opposite John Crichton.}] Don Rico Darnelli: So shall we get down to business? I assume that is why you have called me here today. Crichton: Business? Yes…it is business in some regards. Don Rico Darnelli: I don’t like the direction in which this conversation is heading! Crichton:[{A smile developing upon his face}] And so you shouldn’t! Don Rico Darnelli:[{An angry look developing upon his face}] Do not provoke me John Crichton! I am not a fool, I know you seized this power from the incapable Mr. Freeze, but I assure you that I will be no pushover! Crichton:[{His smile widening}] I see my business as a few leaks here and there! I also see that you know of my…reputation. Don Rico Darnelli:[{The realisation that he let slip more than he intended to dawns upon him.}] Yes I have…certain sources. Crichton: I’m sure you do. Don Rico Darnelli: [{Quickly changing the subject}] Now, why did you bring me here this day? Crichton: I have a very simple business deal for you. Don Rico Darnelli: Go on. Crichton: Well, you put all of your territory under my control and I shall let you live… Don Rico Darnelli:[{Shaking his head in disbelief}] What? Crichton: Oh yes…and you will give me the name of your informant within my ‘organisation’. Don Rico Darnelli: Who the hell do you think you are John Crichton? Crichton: You just answered your own question my friend. [{Without warning Don Rico furiously rises from his wooden seat and reaches inside his suit for his weapon. Crichton boots the desk so hard that it flies into Don Rico’s gut, forcing the weapon out of his hands. Crichton then slowly rises to his feet, grabbing Don Rico by the throat and hoisting him easily up into the air.}] Crichton: Now listen to me! I’m not asking or allowing you luxury of a decision in the matter one way or another I WILL own your territory and I WILL find your informant, but this way you and your Organisation will continue to survive…and I will take out the majority of your opposition allowing us to rule this town! So what do you say? Don Rico Darnelli: Argghhh…O…k…a….y… Crichton:[{Releasing his grip on Don Rico’s throat}] Good…welcome aboard. Now if you would like to tell me the name of the informant, we will be done here. Don Rico Darnelli: His name is Joel Samuel’s… Crichton:[{Looking at Henchman Reginald}] Call a meeting for tonight…[{Reginald simply nods in responds and ends off to make the arrangements}] …I’ve got a little demonstration for the troops!
