The Pretty Girl Scout Diaries-How To Impress The Ladies

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-3-2002 ..::/::..A chorus of electronic beeps and mechanical whirs lead us into our story as we come to find none other than PCW's only superhero, Rent-A-Hero, sitting on the edge of a long metal medical table with his shirt off and his left arm strapped to a heart monitor sitting on what appears to be the doctor's cluttered desk, the electronic beats steady and without pause or overlap, symbolizing a healthy heart. With his feet dangling over the edge and kicking aimlessly like a little kid in a high chair, Rent-A-Hero looks about the doctor's office in utter curiosity of the tools and instruments about him, interrupted at that instant by a middle-aged doctor with a thick goatee and a his head shaved low until only miniscule nubs of hair poke up. The badge on his snow white doctor's coat reads 'Dr. Andrews' and in his hand is a wooden clipboard in which he is filling out as he enters, while at the same time listening for the heart monitor's beats for any abnormalities. Rent-A-Hero sinches the nose of his Green Lantern mask and fixes it lower on his face, his eyes lined up better through the holes now as he looks to the doctor with that pearly white heroic smile of his and asks..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Good doctor, what's information do you have implying my condition? A superhero must keep himself in prime shape and health good man.' ..::/::..Dr. Andrews listens for the beeps just a few more times before and follows each one with an unseen mark on the clipboard before looking up to our hero and answering..::/::..Dr. Andrews:'Well first of all before I tell you anything, I really didn't know what to put on your health form seeing as how you have no health card or I.D., so what is your real full name?' Rent-A-Hero:'First name 'Rent'...middle initial '-A-'...last name 'Hero'. That should do it for ya good man. I'm sorry about my I.D. probs by the way, they were left on Krypton during my rush to escape from my dying planet...' *sigh*' Dr. Andrews:'Yea....riiiiiight.' *Marks 'head trauma' and 'dillusional' on Rent-A-Hero's health form* 'Anyways, you healed up just fine and you seem to have no physical pain from your match with Juan and the fight that took place later. Not a single bone split on your body, you are in peak physical condition, and your bodies internal balance is just fine, that'd give you full clearance to continue your wrestling Mr.Hero. Although, I did find ONE abnormality with your blood...' Rent-A-Hero:'Must be my Kryptonian blood throwing off your mere mortal observation machines. Or MAYBE, just MAYBE it's the fact that my blood still burns with wanting to get my hands on Juan one more time. Sunday, he earned my respect.....a feat even harder than getting the helmet off of Juggernaut like on the Uncanny X-Men #45! But he construed and twisted that respect sealed with a handshake by attacking me yesterday and I will NOT forget that. I have every intention of making sure me and Juan lock up yet again....for he is beginning to become the Dr.Doom to my Reed Richards, The Hulk to my Thing....but he will NEVER be the Kryptonite that leaves me beaten in the ring. Next time me and him meet, the outcome will be in my favor and he will begin to have NO idea what kind of force he is dealing with. He closed a chapter on Sunday with that handshake just to open up a completely different one doctor....and now this chapter has some spills, thrills, and chills just ready to take him to the next level...and when I bend the laws of gravity to beat him 1..2..3, I shall reign victorious, but for now, he is just a lingering villain on the brink of a destined defeat. I've got bigger fish to fry, and that fish is in the form of the PCW Crusierweight Title....' Dr. Andrews:'Seriously...there is something STRANGE about your blood. Not strange enough to keep you from competing, but there's a weird strain within it that concerns me.'Rent-A-Hero:'Your observations are quite intriguing to me Dr. Andrews, although it woulda sounded better coming from George Clooney and all, he's like the greatest doctor in the world....'Dr. Andrews:'DOCTOR?! The guy PLAYS a doctor on TV! By no means can you qualify him as real!'Rent-A-Hero:'Now now there mere mortal, don't be jealous and grow green like the Incredible Hulk just because you can't perform triple bypass surgery and still romance the lovely Dr.Childers within a 60 minute timespan. If anything, I'd say your gonna have a lot of buisness after Spring Fling, because two bodies are gonna roll in by the name of Anton the Kid and The Multiple Man. Those are the two main bosses hoping to stop me from claiming the coveted PCW Crusierweight crown so sought after. All three of us shall climb a stairway to Valhalla and only ONE can descend down to the world of the normal with that belt in their hands. TMM and Anton are just wannabes...they are just phonies who believe they have some special power or ability to put them above the rest, when in reality, they're just as normal as the average Earthling. Normal? Rent-A-Hero? Those two words have NO place together, that's like saying 'Osama Bin Liden-Civil Rights Leader'. I soar above the clouds, save those in need, absorb insane amounts of inhuman pain, and I save the day all before the ending credits for whatever PCW show I'm on come on. My Rent-A-Powers are coveted and almighty and they will be what allows me to climb that ladder rung by rung, outdoing the two evildoers who have tarnished the Crusierweight title, and snatching that ladder down and making it mine. I have ONE mission coming into that pay-per-view....to SAVE the Crusierweight division. No better way then by giving that beautiful gold belt some meaning again...and the start of that meaning will begin with the SUPER-SAIEN DECIMATION of The Multiple Man and I2's Anton The Kid. Two evildoers beaten in one stone and saving the Crusierweight division to boot...I'd call that a good night for a superhero.' ..::/::..Dr.Andrews assists Rent-A-Hero with the removal of the heart monitor device strapped to his left arm, finally getting it off as Dr. Andrews sets it on his desk. Rent-A-Hero pulls up the hanging upper body part of his Green Lantern full body spandex suit and puts his arms through it, sighing in complete comfort at the tight feel of the material before zipping it up, the spandex once again hugging his ripped body as he exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY OXYGEN BLOCKAGE, now I'm feeling wizzer!' Dr. Andrews:'Well Mr.Hero, your free to go, just take care and expect a call about that blood soon...I'm gonna look at it immediately when you leave. Wait wait....what are you doing tonight?' Rent-A-Hero:'Well...I intended to go to my new place of residence and recount my comic book collection, sort my spandex replica costumes, and dub a few manga tapes. Nothing but fun at my place huh?' *smiles proudly as if his night was something to brag about.* Dr. Andrews:'Right....so your doing nothing....how would you like to go out on a date Mr.Hero, I know this great girl just LOOKING for a date and you seem right up her alley.' ..::/::..The thought of a date somewhat stimulates the mind of our hero as he strikes a stance of heavy thinking, knowing that a part of him would love to get out and meet new citizens, while the other still wishes to hold out for his one true love, Aimz. He thumbs his chin lightly while still in deep contemplation before asking..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Well good doctor, what is it that she does for a living pray tell?' Dr. Andrews:'She's a girl scout leader actually, me and her know each other from college. She's very nice, polite, pretty, and she can make a mean batch of cookies.' Rent-A-Hero:'GASP!!! Even....the cookie that sets my heart on fire like the Human Torch....PEANUT BUTTER DELIGHT?!'..::/::..Dr. Andrews simply nods in agreement with Rent-A-Hero's question, making our spandex enwrapped protagonist shiver uncontrollably, falling off the edge of the table and leaping back upon it qucikly to save face, replying..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'I meant to do that, I was just testing your eye reflexes good citizen, HAHAHA.' *nervously scratches and quickly changes the subject* 'Well...a part of me really dosen't want to have anything to do with women right now, but I figure it's just a harmless night of fun and how evil can a woman be who makes PEANUT BUTTER DELIGHT COOKIES!! She has GOT to be a Saint. I accept your date prescription Dr. Andrews.' Dr. Andrews:'Great, I'll have her call you then Mr.Hero, trust me, she's a winner.' Rent-A-Hero:'And thank you again good doctor, now I must race home and prepare for my interaction with the opposite sex.....question is, should I impress her with my overtly large selection of video games or my neverending and bottomless knowledge pertaining to the Star Wars universe?! AWAY I GO DOCTOR...WHOOOSH!!' ..::/::..With that said, our hero strikes a pose reminiscent of an Olympic track runner at the starting line and dashes out the office, making stupid whir noises as he does. Dr. Andrews just smiles and shakes his head at the 'character' he has just met as he goes to his desk to continue his business..::/::.. CLANG!!! BANG!!! OOOOWIE!! ..::/::..That loud sequence of sounds alarms Dr. Andrews as his head shoots up with alarm. He shortly hears the blaring voice of Rent-A-Hero as he exclaims from what seems to be down the hall..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'SORRY GOOD CITIZEN!!! I'm sure you'll only need one liver anyways! AWAY I GO!!' Later On, At Rent-A-Hero's Apartment......::/::..The door to none other than our hero's apartment opens wide, our hero striding through with two plastic bags in hand as he kicks the door lightly, making it shut with a light slam as he walks towards his blue leather couch in the center of the living room, which is littered with various PSM and EGM magazines on the floor. He sets the bags down on his yin-yang crystal coffee table and digs into one of them eagerly as the rustling of plastic accompanies his searching, pulling out a red and white book reading, 'How To Impress Girls'. Our hero promptly plops down on his cushy couch, managing to ignore his large entertainment set-up consisting of a 62' Sony widescreen TV, DVD set, VCR, X-Box, PS2, AND Gamecube, which would be enough for the average electronic buff to have a proverbial orgasm for. He thumbs through the book until he is on the first chapter, reading aloud in the confines of his apartment..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Thank you for buying 'How To Impress Girls', the sure-fire number one book that'll have the honey of your dreams in the sack with you in no time...WIZZER, this book will get girls to wanna go camping with me!! *Reads again* The first step in impressin a girl is your speech. No girl wants you to approach her with a lame speaking voice or worse yet, a lame line. The best way to overcome this is to look in the mirror and practice your speech carefully so as not to make a complete idiot out of yourself. I mean, it's not like your a nerdy spandex wearing idiot who thinks a 'G-Spot' is a power-up in an X-Box game...HEEEEEEEEY, what are they trying to suggest?! *Reads again* If you are, then you know we're just playing with you. *Rent-A-Hero regains his smile* Now go to the nearest mirror and look at yourself...say something that will get the girl interested in YOU. Interest is key.' ..::/::..Our hero approaches the hall mirror which reflects the whole length of his body, our hero looking at himself and trying his hardest to look serious, his eyes construing into a forced drunk stupor as he eyes himself and says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'So member of the opposite gender, how about those interstellar standard numerical values eh? No no no! Hey, your looking quite splendid.....wanna slay some villains with me? NOOOOO, I don't want them caught up in my work anyways. HOLY BOOTY SHORTS YOU GOT A ROTUND BACKSIDE, WANNA GYRATE?! GYAHHHH this is NOT working for me!'Book Passage:'Should you not be able to make those words flow out properly, sometimes it's all about your 'atmosphere'. Sometimes a change in style can aid you in those maic words being able to seep out to the female you intend to bag for an intense night of headboard breaking.' Rent-A-Hero:'Why would anyone want to destroy headboards with a woman....heck, why would a woman even COME in a guy's bedroom? Earthlings and their strange customs. Anyways...change of style eh? I don't think it'd hurt. Let's see what the closet has for me....TO THE RENT-CA....I mean my closet...*sigh*...I hate apartment life.' *saunters to his bedroom* In His Bedroom... ..::/::..The meager bedroom of Rent-A-Hero looks like a complete warzone, more game magazines and large piles of clothing littering the floor, the only things in order being his oak nightstand and his king sized bed with Superman sheets fit tightly over it with imprints of the OTHER Kryptonian hero flying on them. More and more clothes begin to fly from the closet of Rent-A-Hero, the housing place of his various superhero costumes, until finally the constant throwing stops. Silence overtakes his bedroom before he finally leaps out, decked out in baggy FUBU jeans, multiple silver chains, a Fat Albert tank, and a DKNY blue skully cap, his Green Lantern mask still on of course as he throws his hands about like a rapper in a video and stepping about like he's got something caught in his crotch, saying with overexaggeration..::/::.. Rent-A-Rapper:'Yo Yo shorty, how bout' you come to the crib with me and we get up on some PS2 and fight some super villain, na(know) wha I mean? Bling bling, everytime I ride my bicycle, bling bling.' *Rings bicycle bell* 'GRRRR NO, THIS IS NOT ME!! AND I GOTTA TAKE THESE PANTS OFF, I'M ALMOST STARTING TO GET....*Gulp*....COMFORTABLE IN THEM!!' ..::/::..Going back in the closet, more rummaging and searching can be heard before Rent-A-Hero shoots back out, wearing a pair of black boots, tight black wrestling bottoms, and wearing a balding cap on his head as he looks about with paranoid eyes and says..::/::..Rent-A-Austin:'Girl you wanna go out? What? You want me to take you somewhere? What? You'd like a romantic evening for two? What? A shindig? What? A romp? What? A little private time? What? Well that can be arranged, cause RENT-OOOO SAID SOOOO!!! NO NO NO!! I don't want her thinking I'm DEAF or anything. Geez this outfit is drafty..::/::..Our hero dashes back in the closet, tossing off the clothing and shortly, reemerging from the closet with tight leather pants, a 'Disturbed' shirt, and his golden blonde hair spiked up high above his head in a pleathora of directions. He plays with the metal ball chain arund his neck and says..::/::.. Rent-A-Rocker:'Yo girl, I say we like jump in my van and go see some Disturbed! THEY FRIGGIN RULE!! DISTURBED IS GAWD, YEEEEEEEAAAAA!!! ROCK ON FOREVER!!! WOOOOOOOO!!! NO no....this is too hyper...even for me. I don't care if the pants rule the school, I still hate this look.' ..::/::..Our hero enters the closet once again rummaging through more clothes and tossing things about, still searching for his 'look'. Unbeknownst to him though, his answering machine which sits next to his lightsaber phone on his coffee table, happens to have three messages and with the autoplay feature activated, the machine starts up and rewinds itself before playing. The messages are unheard by our hero as he continues cursing and yelling, still searching for the proper look for himself..::/::.. Message 1-The Girl Scout:'Hey Rent, this is Jeanie, the Girl Scout Dr. Andrews set you up with. I'm looking forward to our date and I'll be dropping by later today s we can do something. Sorry I called so late, but trapping bears and tying knots can take so much out of a girl. I had some much fun getting on my knees and getting them dirty just to please my girls! (Take that how you wanna :p) Anyways, see you later Rent and I'll be sure to bring the cookies!' !~BEEP~! Message 2-Amy 'Aimz' Campbell:'RENT, I had to call...I thought about things and all and after that song and everything, I can't stop thinking about you. I HAVE to see you today and I WILL be by. We have to talk about US. I miss you and I think we really need to try to make things work again....I still care about you Rent. I'll be by later, BYEEEEEEEZZZZ!!' !~BEEP~! Message 3-Dr. Andrews:'RENT, CANCEL THAT DATE WITH JEANIE AND ANY OTHER GIRL FOR AWHILE!! I FINISHED THOSE TESTS ON YOUR BLOOD AND YOU HAVE A RARE GENE....THE X-CHROMOZOID!!!!It's rare gene in a few and you are one of the few carriers. It gives the possesser uncanny body strength and agility in general and other affects, but it has an incredibly dangerous cataylst....that gene can do you NOTHING but good UNLESS....YOU HAVE SEX!!!A certain sexual chemical stimulates the gene...then it becomes a virus Rent, and there's no telling how dangerous it will become. You have to contact me immediately!! Thank you...and be careful!!' !~BEEP~! ..::/::..The machine ends and this life changing revalation is revealed, but our hero is still in the dark about it as he continues breezing through clothing when suddenly, he hears knocking against his hardwood door..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'MY PIZZA!! Their speed is uncanny like the Flash, let's just hope their heat is that of the Human Torch! TO THE DOOR!!' *dashes and trips up on a pile of clothes* 'I meant to do that...' ..::/::..Our hero walks towards the door, wearing only a pair of Spiderman The Movie boxers, with scenes from the movie imprinted on them, as he approaches the door and opens it wide......our hero is in PURE shock to find a lovely brunette in a black top with a green sash over her shoulder covered in various badges, a short green and black plaid mini-skirt, long slender legs, and a pair of black clogs. Obviously this would be Jeanie, who drops the cookies in tupperware from her hands and stands in shock of the barely naked hero, her eyes probing his heavenly body as our hero stands shocked that a woman is seeing him this intimately. All Jeanie can mouth at the sight of our hero is..::/::.. Jeanie The Girl Scout:'Oh....my.....God.' ..::/::..Within a matter fo seconds, Jeanie falls back and slams against the floor hard, fainted with her eyes closed. Our hero looks to the camera, a more confused look on his face than usual before saying..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'That is a good first impression.....right? Right?' ..::/::..With that said, our hero tries to revive the lovely girl scout as the scene fades, the three important messages missed by Rent-A-Hero looking to have a huge effect on his day.
