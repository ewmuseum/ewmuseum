Cover Girl

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/12/02 We zoom in on a face set in stone, yet still flesh. Its expression does not waver, not in the slightest...indeed, it is resolve personified. 'No.' Around we go to another face, this one in a state of despair. 'Why you gotta be like that man?' In a small, darkened room stands Scourge and the Violator. Cheap camera equipment lines the floors, wires crisscrossing in every direction....a makeshift studio from the looks of things. And across the room, a very irritated looking demon. Clyde: I'll put it another way. Hell no. Next to a tripod, the behemoth known as the Violator shakes his mighty head. Holding a small box in his hand, he implores to the killer. Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge: Come on man, we'd do it for you! Still Clyde remains steadfast, his arms folded across his chest. Clyde: No. I am not wearing a schoolgirl uniform. I don't care what excuse you have for it. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Scourge holds his hand to his forehead, looking very much the angst ridden director. Scourge: Look man, we've got our first official match in PCW coming up! We're facing....uh.... The bestiality lover turns to his tag team partner. Scourge: Who're we facing? Violator: Prepare for total violation. Scourge: Oh yeah. Scourge returns his gaze to Clyde, who stares at him blankly. Scourge: Cowabunga Connection, man!Clyde: ....who the hell is Cowabunga Connection? Scourge: Man, don't you know anything? They're....uh.... Once again the freak turns his head. Scourge: Who are they man? Violator: I am the Violator. Scourge: (Slapping his forehead) Oh yeah! They're these guys, and they're like surfers man! Clyde: ....point being? Scourge: So like, this is our debut. So you've gotta put on the dress. Once more the burned man stares at Scourge. Then finally, after a long moment.... Clyde: No. Violator: (Groaning) Prepare for total violation! Clyde: You still haven't explained why in the hell you would need me to wear a schoolgirl uniform. Scourge: Well, it's like this. Cowabunga Connection did this thing where they were in a school, see. So we wanna spoof them for our promo. But like....we need someone to wear the dress and play the schoolgirl. Violator: (Nodding) I am the Violator. Clyde: ....so why don't you have Violator wear the dress? Scourge shakes his head in frustration. Scourge: Why you gotta be like that man? Look at him, he's not gonna fit in this thing. Clyde: ....so why don't you wear the dress and leave me the hell alone? Scourge: It doesn't match my shoes. Clyde: (Slaps his forehead) Scourge: So come on man, you want us to win our debut match don't you?Clyde: No. Both the Violator and Scourge look on blankly at Clyde. Clyde: ....what? I don't care if the two of you job to Big Gooey Sac and Phoenix. Violator: I am the Violator. Scourge: I know, that's cold man. Clyde: Hmph. Well if you'll excuse me.... Suddenly what sounds like a muffled explosion is heard from somewhere outside. Clyde: ....the hell was that? Scourge: I dunno man....we're in Wisconsin, they probably test surface missiles for the hell of it. Or something. The killer shakes his head, smirking somewhat. Clyde: I really should go see for myself. Pardon me, boys. Clyde exits stage left, leaving Total Violation to stand in shocked silence. Violator: .....prepare for total violation. Scourge: Why's he gotta be like that man? Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge: What the hell are we gonna do now man? We gotta have SOMEONE willing to put on a dress for this moronic spoof.... Both men look to the floor....then slowly to each other, their eyes lighting up with an idea. 'That's it, work it!' A camera flashes, snapping off picture after picture. Scourge kneels, tilting the device this way and that...finally he looks over, giving a thumbs up. Scourge: You brilliant man! Across from him on the stage, television's Ricardo Montalban twirls. His skirt twists to and fro as he strikes various poses. Montalban: Yes, Tattoo. I am a pretty girl! Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge snaps a few more pictures, grinning broadly. Scourge: With stuff like this we can't lose man! That match is ours! Violator: Prepare for total violation! Oddly enough a buzzing sound rings out, causing Scourge to pause. Scourge: Dude, was that you? Violator: (Shaking his head) I am the Violator. Scourge: Dunno. Anyway, we.... Once again the strange noise rings forth, louder this time. Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge: I don't know what it.... Suddenly the door to the makeshift studio flies open, bouncing off the wall. A shadow falls over the set.... Scourge: (Looking on) Oh....shit. Bethany: MOTHER FUCKERS!!!!! In stomps the homicidal eight year old, swinging a chainsaw larger than she is over her dainty head. Her shrill voice pierces the air, even over the rumbling of her weapon. Montalban: What is the meaning of this, Tattoo. You make me feel less sexy. Bethany: BASTARD! THAT'S MY DRESS!!!!! Violator puts his hands on his hips, looking at Bethany. Violator: I am the Violator. Bethany: I stole it, okay? Not like the girl would have been needing it anymore with what was left of her. So shut up. Violator: (Shrugging) Prepare for total violation. Scourge spikes his camera, looking on angrily. Scourge: How're we supposed to do a spoof if you kill us? Dammit, think man! The star of Fantasy Island, decked out in his schoolgirl uniform looks on. Montalban: Tattoo, perhaps it is not wise to taunt the child with the killing device. Bethany: SONS OF BITCHES!!!!! DIE!!!!!!! Bethany charges, swinging her weapon madly. We zoom in on the eyes of Scourge and Violator, huddled together in terror, as their screams ring out and we mercifully fade to black.....
