Date: Part 1 It’s the pursued one’s fear that he can smell… Following the trail with much haste, the creature spots a sprinting silhouette in the distance. Charging through the thick brush, it drives though branches and twigs, snapping them easily in its wake. In no way slowed down by the greenery in its path, it makes much gain on the prey ahead, and soon they both break out into the open where the bright moon shines down upon them. Close enough to hear the silhouette wheezing, the creature lunges blindly… Not nearly close enough to detect a quick change of direction by the hunted. Missing, it lands in rough manner, sliding on the ground face first, but scrambles to already frantically driving feet. The pain only invigorates the hurt more. There is a powerful, yearning desire within its frame of mind to seek out this… this… Panicked, the victim dumbly looks back, forfeiting warning of obstacles ahead, and trips over a small rock. By the time that he is stumbling from knee to foot, trying futilely to get away, the pursuing beast is already at his heels, nipping away. A sudden boost of adrenaline drives him up, and his eyes clench shut while his legs bolt for one last good dash. --- LAB: “Vat in ze vurld?!” He had come in so abruptly, slamming the door hard behind him, that he caused them both to jump. We are taken by surprise by Syko the Goblin, soaked in sweat, as he disrupts peace within his haven (GOBLIN HQ). Pinning the door shut with his back pressed against it, the Goblin looks to his two allies with a cascade of terror across his face. Syko the Goblin: “(Gasping.) …Demon… Monster…” Confusion fogs perception immediately. The front lobby of Syko’s HQ begins to envelope in an uneasy feeling. Esther rises from her desk slowly, looking to LAB, then the panicked Goblin. LAB: “Vere have you been? Vat’s going on here?” Syko snaps his bloodshot crosiers at the old, scientific madman. STG: “INFIDEL! I said…” (BAM!) Pounded hard from the other side, the door is jutted from its frame, jolting the tiny Goblin’s base in turn, but he manages to keep it shut! LAB: “VAAAT?!” (BAM!) Another pound!! This time Syko almost is bowled down by the door! STG: “HELP YOU BLITHERING OLD FOOL!” The old man doesn’t hesitate. Teaming up to cover the door, they manage to keep out whatever is behind the door for the moment… (BAM!)(BAM!)(BAM!) LAB: “Vat is zis thing?!” STG: “Some sort… (Grunting in strain.) …of monster… …He’s after me!” (BAM!) LAB: “But, Vy?” As the two struggle, Esther slides open the top drawer of her desk, and with-drawls a solid, slightly worn, metal bat. Esther rolls over her desk and approaches the immediate situation with a steady head for action, the bat cocked to swing. Calmly, Esther gives a wave to the boss and her partner to move. (BAM!) LAB: “On ze count of three! …one…” STG: “EVERY MAN FOR HIMSELF!!!” Showing no regard for the others, Syko removes his weight from the door, leaving it for LAB to hold, which doesn’t last very long, and shoves past Esther. He retreats to her desk, and dives over it to the other side, and flips it over to it’s side scattering everything from its surface. (BAM!) From behind his new-found barrier, Syko works up the nerve to peek over just in time to see… LAB: “AHHHHHH!!!” …His eldest employee catapulted by the forced open door across the lobby, whizzing right past Syko’s head, and into the cheap wall that explodes into a cloud of plaster powder, ultimately halting his flight. Esther prepares for a line drive, but suddenly stops. A blank look of confusion crosses her face, and dumbfounded, she lets the bat fall to her side. Syko would scream… if he wasn’t so stunned with terror. Esther scratches her head, and looks back to Syko with a face that just screams, “derrrrrr?” Waddling in, its green head pivoting about in gaze upon the area, a rather normal looking Mallard Duck enters. It SEEMS pretty harmless. STG: “……(Deep breath.) KILLLL IT!!!!” The Duck’s primal instinct activates upon the detection of Syko. Springing off webbed feet with abnormal height in its leap, the soars at Esther with a hiss! Landing upon her grey mop-top, it bounds to into air with another mighty leap, and torpedoes its way right into the Goblin! STG: “AWAY BEAST!” Pounced upon, the Goblin shrieks, struggling with the rabid duck… STG: “Get off me! I command you… BLARG! Somebody help me!” The scene fades out to the image of Syko mounted by an over joyous duck that vigorous uses its tongue to paint the Goblin’s face with a sloppy coating of saliva. So, there are many questions to be answered… For one, what is the connection between Syko and this Duck? Also, how might this play in with the match Wednesday night? Bah! It probably wont! To most certainly be continued…
