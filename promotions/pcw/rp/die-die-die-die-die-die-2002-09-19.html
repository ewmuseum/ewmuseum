Die Die Die Die Die Die

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/09/02 “I HATE him....” We are shown the frustrated face of PCW Champion Xavier Kannon, still stewing after the events of Turmoil where even with fire and explosives, he was unable to halt the Demon known only as Clyde. Xavier : “Well, Turmoil will just be the rehearsal.... Clyde will DIE.” “Clyde..... Will..... DIE!“ Beside the Scientologist, as always, we see his wife and valet Eleanor, her hair seemingly recovered from the scorched torment it suffered on Tuesday night with exploding closets and plastic explosive face putty. Xavier : “Time to see what all this can do. If fire doesn’t work then we’ll just have to get a bit more CREATIVE!” His eyes look dead ahead, the trademark smug grin appearing on his face as Ellie~! seems to be into it too. Eleanor : “Bit..... More.... Creative!” The camera pans away from the couple to show that are standing in what appears to be an underground weapon’s testing facility, most likely somewhere in the extensive underground network that teems with life below Hubbardville. Xavier : “Somewhere there is something that will stop the Demon, so we might as well have fun in looking for it...” In the vast plain White room are various contraptions set up. False locker room doors, holes in the ground... Anything you can think of. Xavier : “Begin the tests!” The little prep seems excited at the prospect of the weapons test and eagerly fidgets, waiting for them to begin. Eleanor : “YEAH!!! Like... SO begin.... The.... Tests!” From the door adjacent to them, a man in thick fire-proof overalls walks out, seeming a little bit nervous as he walks to one of the doors, held up by about six feet of thin board wall either side. Xavier : “What are you waiting for, Scientologist.... There are lethal contraptions to be tested!” He cautiously edges towards the door and grabs a hold of the handle to a beep that startles him... Nervously the test subject looks around to Kannon and Ellie~! who both just point to the door. Eleanor : “Um... HELLO?!?!?! Just, like, open it and die already!” With the prep’s words not exactly encouraging him, the Scientologist pushes the door open to show nothing behind it. “Huh?” *THUD* However, above him the top of the door frame plummets down and impacts on the man’s head and knocks him to the ground... The sound of machine mechanism kicking in is heard as the top of the door frame starts to raise again and a glimmer of metal is seen. Xavier : “Hmmmm.... If making him a human fireball doesn’t kill him, a bash to the head isn’t going to get us very far....” His terrified eyes reflecting the gleam of the metal, the heavily padded test subject scrambles away from under the door frame just as a sharp blade comes crashing down, making the door one intricate Guillotine. Xavier : “.... I stand corrected!” Beside him, the prep’s vacuous pale Blue eyes look at the blade, transfixed as it raises back up into the door frame, leaving the contraption once again looking totally innocuous. Eleanor : “You...... Stand..... Corrected!” The tester gets to his feet, startled and breathing heavily as he dusts himself off only to see Kannon pointing to the next contraption that resembles a bathroom stall. Xavier : “I’ve heard good things about this one.” Ellie~! doesn’t look quite as enthusiastic. Eleanor : “But it’s, like, a toilet! Ewwwwwwwwwzzies.” The already terrified Scientologist walks slowly and cautiously towards the bathroom stall and screws his face up in fearful anticipation as he slowly pushes the door open, it’s creak only adding to his nervousness. Xavier : “BOOOOOOOOOOOOM!“ The tester jumps out of his skin as Kannon fakes an explosion sound, laughing along with his valet as he holds a hand up to he scared stiff test subject. Xavier : “Sorry... Sorry... Do carry on.” Beside him, the little prep giggles at the sight of the pale and frightened Scientologist. Eleanor : ”Do......... Carry........ On!” Very slowly, very nervously and very cautiously the tester walks into the stall and takes a seat on the toilet... His eyes darting around the stall wondering where the threat to his life will come from... Then, he fidgets, adjusting the pressure on the seat. *CLICK* Xavier : “Pressure change activation... Nice!” Eleanor : “Pressure... Change... Acto-..... Akso-.... Um.. YEAH!“ Suddenly, from behind the tank, restraints whip around to hold the terrified test subject in place.. From the bottom of the toilet feet restraints then emerge and the man is now held tightly. Xavier : “Hmmmm, good so far....A bit clinical, needs that ‘Sick’ edge.” With the struggling Scientologist trying to unsuccessfully free himself, something lowers down around the man’s neck, his head held firmly in place while the terrified eyes watch the ring descend. Xavier : “Intriguing...” The ring descends around the test subject’s head to show that it contains 4 needles, each filled with a Silver liquid.. Most probably Mercury. Eleanor : “Cool, Silver is SO the in colour!” Seeing the collection of Mercury filled syringes surrounding his neck, the test subject struggles to free himself until the contraption resets itself, raising it’s toxic crown and relinquishing the terrified tester’s form. “That’s it.... I’m DONE!” He brushes off the couple with his hands and staggers to the door, almost hyperventilating. *CLUNK* “ARGH!!!!” A metal trap suddenly clamps itself around the man’s ankle... The spiked jaws protruding into his flesh as the tester hops on his good leg, barely able to lift the heavy trap off the floor. Eleanor : “That, like, SO has to hurt!!!” Xavier : “He’s a Scientologist.. With the strength of Hubbard the pain will not worry him.” The heavily padded tester wails out as he hops around, his face Red and pained and blood soaks into his clothes around the wound. “IT HURTS... OH IT HURTS... IT HUUUUUUUUUUUURTS!!!!” Still he hops around the testing lab full of dangerous contraptions until he manages to hop somewhere else... Through the floor. *SPLASH* Kannon and Ellie~! look to each other as the man suddenly disappears through the floor and is heard splashing into a pool of some kind. Xavier : “Hmmm, hope it isn’t too deep, that trap’s a bit of an Anchor.” Eleanor : “Of... An.... Anchor!” “AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!!!!!!!!” Following the splash a fizzing is heard and steam starts to rise out of the hole to the concern of the couple. Xavier : “Um.. I think we might want to get out of here my little Poon Puppy.... Testing looks like it’s called off.” Eleanor : “Like... So.... Agreed!” Covering their noses at the smell of the fumes emerging from the pit, the couple make their hasty exit into one of the elevator shafts as the shot fades out.. Only the fizzing and screaming of the hapless test subject being heard. “IT BURNS..... OH IT BURNS!!!!!!”
