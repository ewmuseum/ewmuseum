Ten Things I like

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-30-02 The views expressed are those of Scorpion and not those of PCW, thank you. Well I did a top ten list of things I hate why not do a top ten list of things I like? Only seems right and I don’t want everyone to think that I hate everything only a couple things, that’s all. So I decide to put together my top ten lists of things I like. Now I feel like one of those cheep countdowns that VH1 has on there channel all the time. #10: Sports Sports are a major thing for me; either betting on them in pools or watching all different sports it’s all good. World Cup, World Series, Super Bowl, NBA Finals, Stanley Cups, Masters, U.S. Open, Strongman competition, it doesn’t matter the sport I love watching them. Sports are the only thing in the world that mute out any woman. Your wife can come home and talk about how she found a table cloth on sale and the voice of John Madden on a Sunday afternoon can control your attention while she is talking, it’s unbelievable and I thank the man that invented sports television. Twenty fours of sports, I love when they run out of stuff to air and they put on the strongman competitions where these men from Germany are pulling eight wheelers ten to twenty feet. Sports are the only thing that can grab the attention of a man with a small attention span. Unless there is a naked woman on a pole in my living room everyday of the week and every hour of the day doing what she does best. So there are only two things that could really grab my attention then. #9: Music Music has changed a lot in the past forty years, we started in the sixty’s with the living legend of the guitar playing, Jimi Hendrix who could make the guitar talk. The Beatles grab the hearts of little girls and created some of the best song writing in the seventy’s. Led Zeppelin created “Stairway to heaven” a eleven minute song that proclaims a story which songs to day very rarely do unless it’s about pimping hoes and taking drugs. Lynard Skynard was talking about “firebirds” and going back to his sweet home in Alabama. Eighty’s were led by the Punk revolution. The Sex Pistols created Anarchy and not giving a dam that the hell they pissed off. The eighty’s ended with crappy teenybopper shit that made Jimi Hendrix turn over in his grave. Ninety’s was the hip-hop revolution, with LL Cool J, to the late Tu Pac and Notorious B.I.G. Ninety’s was also the alternive music revolution with great bands like Nirvana and Pearl Jam. The Ninety’s ended with crappy music like Brittany Spears and N’Sync. Music is a driving force in my people. I personally enjoy listening to Led Zeppelin and Jimi Hendrix. Music has always been a great asset to the world and now you don’t need to pay for it anymore, downloading music is so much easier and if the over paid artists don’t like it then to bad. #8: New York New York means a lot to me. I’m a New Yorker by heart. Despite what people say about New York it’s still one of the greatest places to visit in the entire world. There are so many cultures in New York. New York is stronger then ever now since September 11th, 2001 and the New York sprit will never be broken. New York has a lot of great things. The Yankees, Rangers, and the Madison Square garden crowd that is consider one of the best crowds in the entire wrestling world. Many people love-visiting New York, it really is a beautiful place. I’m proud of where I’m from and I don’t care what anyone says. #7: Cars I love the starting of an engine in a corvette. I love the horsepower of a camero. The speed of muscle cars blows my mind and I crave for the speed every time I enter a fast machine. I’m not necessarily a big fan of the cars in the “Fast and the Furious” but I respect what these guys have done to the Mazda’s and Honda’s. Because there is really nothing much to work with and they put a shit load of money in those cars to make them look good. But it still cannot compare to something that is one percent real and that’s what I love about the Corvette’s, Camero’s and Ferrari’s. Those things can turn on a dime faster then a prostitute running from the police. It is truly breathtaking to watch these things move. #6: Women Women, love or hate them. You just got to love them. They’re the only things that can get men to there knees. Shore once you marry one it can be a son of a bitch because all they talk about is how wonderful Martha Stewart is and how Oprah changed there life. Woman might spend most of their days at a mall shopping or a friend’s house, but who can complain? That gives you a good eight years to pop in that porno and not get yelled at. Hell if it wasn’t for women, there wouldn’t be Playboy and I know that would leave a lot of men around the world very pissed off and I can’t bare to think where the world would be today if we didn’t have Playboy. Just think, we might still be living life like Beaver Cleaver where women stay in the house all day, not leaving. Now that would most defiantly suck. I also thank malls around the world for being around when we really need them. Your watching a football game and your wife is bitching about how Oprah stopped her book club. All you got to do is hand her a flyer from a store in the mall and wham she’s gone. The mall is like bait in essence, it reels women in. Thank god we have women, or we would be doomed. #5: Alcohol Besides sports television what is the only other thing that can mute off your wife? Answer: Beer. Coors, Budweiser, Miller you name it, it does it. It’s like a medicine that takes men to a whole different world. A world where all the women are naked and everything around you is made out of candy. There is also an unwritten law around men around the world that if you can drink a twelve pack of beers and still stand then you are a god. Alcohol is the driving force of a man’s bloodstream. Shore it screws up your senses but who really uses them anyway? I’m oblivious sometimes to what senses if any I have. The only one’s I know I have are sight, hearing and taste that’s it and Alcohol reassures what senses I have when it blurs my vision, makes a weird ringing noise in my ear and I can taste the bear in my mouth. So not only is Alcohol one hell of a beverage but it also is it’s own form of a medicine…. and that’s why I love it! #4: WrestlingNow how can I leave my day job off this list? Wrestling is the greatest thing in the world. I mean where else could you kick someone’s ass and get away with it? Maybe jail, but you got that whole Bubba thing and “bitches” so I don’t know. But if I had to pick a job it would most defiantly be wrestling. Wrestling has been taken to new standards today. Hardcore and Extreme is part of wrestling while Bruno Samartino wrestling is the past. Wrestling has really evolved over the past couple of years into one of the greatest businesses in the world. I’m proud to be a wrestler and I’m very proud to be with the PCW. Wrestling is a beautiful sport. Wrestling to me means three little things, blood, guts and glory. #3: Money Where would most of us be today with out money? Money is the driving force of all greed and the driving force of all countries economies. Money makes me live the way I love, buy the stuff I want. Money helps keep my family safe and money gives me all the abilities to give my family everything I want. Earning money is nice, but making money is so much nicer and that’s why I love being the “King of casinos.” Money is makes people happy and if it makes me happy that’s all that matters. But as long as I know that my family is financially good then that makes me very happy that I know that my family will live a good life cause we have all the essentials to make us live peacefully and that’s all that matters to me. Money is also good when you know it’s in your pocket. #2: Playboy Where would magazine’s be today without Playboy? What state would men be in today without Playboy? I shutter to think where we would be today, what steps we would be missing, the bodies, and the women… The only time men saw this much silicone was in are local pharmacy magazine, so it was a big jump in the process of the perfect male. Playboy has been the defining moment in teenage boys “young beginnings” so it has served its purpose to be one of the greatest elements ever created for entertainment. Now I know a seventy-year-old man getting it on with a young twenty-year-old female is quite disgusting but you got to feel for Hugh Heffner, once you turn seventy would you want the action he is getting? He truly is a American hero and Playboy is truly a American sport. #1: Casinos Casino had to be number one on my list; Casino’s gave me the name Scorpion. Casinos just like wrestling, put money in my pocket. The casino is like a second home for me. From the slots to the black jack I love it all. The atmosphere takes my breath away. The people dressed up like Elvis. It’s all-good. Being able to defeat your opponent for money is oh so sweet and that’s what draws me into these places. That’s why casinos are at the top of my list. Now that I have put together ten things I like, hopefully people don’t think that I hate absolutely everything in the world. I don’t hate everything, I just find most stuff annoying.
