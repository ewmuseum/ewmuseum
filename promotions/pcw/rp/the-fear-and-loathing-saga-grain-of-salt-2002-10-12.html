The Fear and Loathing Saga: Grain of Salt...

12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-12-02 ‘Really, I don’t think that you could be anymore naïve my love.’ My words echoed off of the empty hallway in the bowels of the arena. Talena looked at me with a vision of despair and mass confusion. Minutes ago, I was forcibly was dragged out of the arena by various henchmen and rent-a-cops because of a drug policy violation, a bogus addendum to a non-existent rule that was hatched by the genius of Xavier ‘Hi, I’m a assfuck’ Kannon. I knew there was no drug policy, I’ve been smoking pot for the majority of my stay in major league wrestling federations… hell, I’ve been smoking pot since I was old enough to inhale. That wasn’t the point, unlike most workers I’ve seen enter these hallowed halls, I don’t need to put my love for drugs in the forefront of myself. I have far more interesting highs and lows to speak of. I’ve had more highs and lows then if I had a life of meth abuse. Drugs weren’t very important to me, I was busy deciding what I was going to do to get back into PCW. I began to run over how I would just brutally murder Kannon and hide his corpse, or maybe hang it from the oak tree in my front yard, when Talena interjected in a way only she knows how… ‘That ignorant little assuming freak!’ She pouted and stamped her boot-clad feet on the marble floor. She was talking about Kannon. ‘Who does he think he is!’ ‘Well… he thinks he is God, I wonder when someone is going to reveal to him that his religion was made by a guy who wrote overrated books.’ She agreed with me of course, Hubbard was a terrible writer, especially compared to someone like Hunter S. Thompson. Make a note of that name, if you don’t know who he is you will probably need a crash course. You see, Thompson wrote the Fear and Loathing books. A very intricate and well-written story of two writers and their love for drugs. This was very important. Remember him. I was hoping she would have a plan of some sort, she was basically my attorney during these whole situations. Talena was always like me, she had… how do I put this nicely… personality issues. By that I mean she has many of them to have issues with. She’s a risky attorney to have. Sometimes she could be arguing a beautiful case for you, other times… well… she’s babbling about chocolate and bears and clouds tasting like flowers or whatever the fuck her mind is engrossed in at the time. It really has some interesting points occasionally. ‘Gavyn, I do have an idea…’ I looked at her as I saw security coming to push us out into the parking lot, I fleetingly wondered if my Porsche could itself become a projectile large enough to cause some damage to Xavier’s little vehicle. ‘Does it include death? Making Kannon look bad? Fire?’ She shook her head, blowing in her hands to warm them, although I noticed it wasn’t very cold out. I started to realize that she wasn’t blowing, it was a sniffing motion. ‘Not in the least, as your attorney, I think it should be brought to your attention that I added something into your contract last year that you might find rather interesting.’ I raised one of my pierced eyebrows while she started to giggle, a bit of white powder drifted off of her right hand. I couldn’t help but smile. She took coke like it was a pixy stick. A expensive Pixy Stick. But one none the less. ‘Since when were you my attorney?’ She ran her fishnet clad arm across her nose hesitantly, ‘Since I realized you didn’t exactly have a good brain for law.’ I opened my mouth to argue but really had nothing to say, she was right, in high school I nearly failed Business Law, I only passed because of a run in with a insane asylum near the end of my Junior year, the teacher felt bad for me and passed me so I could get out of it. She giggled more heavily this time as she sat down on the ground, the salt shaker in her back pocket rolled out and to a stop under my foot. I picked it up and put it under my nose, taking a steady hit of the white powder inside. I smiled as everything began to move with trails behind it rather quickly. ‘So… what are you talking about?’ My words slurred together as I glanced to the right, security had weapons, there were to many of those little buggers for Talena and I to do anything about. We walked back to the car on our own power, I looked back at the expanding and contracting bright green arena, I could have sworn it wasn’t green though, whatever. I needed another hit. Talena still hadn’t answered me, I realized that she was trailing behind me, her doll in her right hand… this wasn’t the time to discuss business. She swayed back and forth, I started to look for my contract, I knew it was in this damn car somewhere, I just wished it would stop moving so fast. ‘The American Dream, puppy… that’s what I put in your contract, the American Dream.’ She was insane. I smiled and loved her all the more for it.
