What Ever Happened To Jordan Crazy?

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.06.02 Every person, no matter what sex, race or creed sometimes needs to get away from it all. The average person probably goes into forest or the mountains, to get away from everything to do with life in the new millennium. But Duzza isn’t like everyone else, when Duzza needs to get away from it all, there’s only once place he goes. Back home. When enough is enough Duzza packs some clothes and a basketball, some CDs and drives from his Hampton mansion into the studio apartment in downtown Harlem. And recently things have gotten to much for the PCW star to handle. There is the fact his career in wrestling is going downhill, most of his friends have deserted him and to make things worst, his closest friend in the music industry RnB Don R. Kelly has been arrested with career damaging charges of child porn. Now people close to the situation know R. Kelly has been set up, it’s blatantly obvious but sadly not everyone can be as close to the situation as Duzza is. Not everyone will ever know the real truth. Sadly the majority of people will only know the lies they read about in the newspapers or see on the television. Duzza prays every night that they won’t send R. Kelly to jail, but in the back of his mind he knows there is a large chance that the king of RnB could be going down. And to top it all Duzza has been booked in a 4-way match with close friends John Critchon and Andy Murray and formed Duzza associate Jordan X Shabazz. Now you’d expect Jordan and Duzza to work together in this match, but sadly things have changed now. For whatever reason Duzza and Jordan aren’t as close as they used to be. Maybe it was Duzza’s close friendship with R. Kelly that sent Jordan over the top, maybe Jordan figured he was too good for Duzza who knows… The scene opens up in a dimly lit studio apartment in downtown Harlem. It is about 11.30pm and the only light is coming from street lights and the moon, shining through the large window at the side of the ring that looks over the town. There are two people in the small apartment; one is of course Duzza, while the other is his wife Brittany. Brittany is sat down on the couch, while Duzza is staring out the window watching the world pass him by. He is dressed in a pair of baggy white slacks, a baggy white plain T-Shirt that is only a few inches away from covering his knees and some untied Timberland boots. His hair is set back in long cornrows, that reach the bottom of his neck. For once Duzza is void of any jewellery. Brittany: “Malek…what’s wrong?” At first, Duzza continues to look out the window, but eventually he turns to speak, resting himself on the windowsill. Duzza: “Huh? Nothin’s wrong” Brittany: “Malek, don’t lie” Duzza: “I ain’t lying, I done told you ain’t nothing wrong” Brittany: “Whenever you drag me up to this damn apartment in the middle of Harlem, that means something is wrong, now if it’s the R. Kelly thing then…” Duzza: “It ain’t the R. thing… well that ain’t all of it” Brittany: “So there is something wrong…tell me” Duzza: “You remember when I told you I wanted to the sell the homes and all the cars and all the jewellery and just go buy a island in the Caribbean or something?” Brittany: “It was only in the car on the way here two hours ago honey” Duzza: “Yeah well… I wanna do it. I’m sick of this shit man” Brittany: “See, I don’t understand. Sick of what? We’re living it up” Duzza: “Naw, your living it up. I’m living in hell, it’s like that song when Ja Rule goes ‘I’m living in Hell but living wealthy’ That is exactly how I feel” Brittany: “I still don’t understand” Duzza: “What? You don’t speak English? You don’t understand the words coming from my mouth? Can’t chu hear me?” Brittany: Don’t use the tone with me Malek Williams! Now talk to me, don’t talk at me. What is bothering you” Duzza: “I’m sick of all this celebrity shit, I miss just being able to kick it back in the hood on the couch in front of the tv, or just chillin at the basketball courts, I miss being able to walk on the streets without someone recognising me…” Brittany: “Then why did you get into wrestling? Or try to get into music?” Duzza: “Because I had to get out of the ghetto” Brittany: “But we’re in the freaking ghetto now Malek, you keep coming back” Duzza: “I did all this stuff for you, everything I did was for you, so you could live the life you deserve, I didn’t want none of this…” Brittany: “Yes you did, you wanted all this. I know you wanted it, don’t act like you didn’t. What’s really buggin you? You have a match with Jordan and two white guys, nothing to worry about there” Duzza: “Yes there is…” Brittany: “What? What’s to worry about there, you and Jordan are boys” Duzza: “Were boys…” Brittany: “What? What did you do to him?” Duzza: “Man, I ain’t done shit to that fool, he turned on me!” Brittany: “Nah, you been boys before I knew you, you musta done something Malek” Duzza: “Nah, see this is how it goes down. I was watching some PCW tv yeah, and on comes my boy Jordan and I was thinking ‘ahh ma nigga, glad he’s doing good for himself’ and then he talks about me and he goes ‘we used to be close’ and some other shit bout me pushing him out of a car on the highway…I admit I did it but still, he didn’t have to tell the world” Brittany: “You pushed him out a car!?” Duzza: “He had it coming…anyway, he turned his back on me, after all the shit I did for that fool! Man, back in the day he was gonna prove his love for some bitch by jumpin’ of the Brooklyn Bridge! Guess who had to climb up there and stop him? Me, that’s who” Brittany: “For real?” Duzza: “Yeah for real. Man, I tell ya, Jordan done sold out. I miss Jordan Crazy. My nigga Jordan Crazy. That was a real brother, down for me like I was down for him. Ax him about that bullet in his shoulder, some thugs tried to steal his bike, I got my ass kicked getting his bike back!” Brittany: “Your still boys though” Duzza: “Nah, we ain’t boys, he turned on me! I should of seen it coming. He’s just another money wanting bastard from the hood. I mean, when he changed his name from Jordan Crazy to Jordan X Shabazz, jus’ cause some white boy dissed his name I didn’t agree with his decision, but y’know I stuck by the brother, I stuck by him. Then he got his ass into PCW and I was happy for the brother… I wasn’t happy how he got in though, kissin ass n shit, he was making black folk look bad, but he didn’t go through with it. And we was cool still, I robbed a toy store for the brother! I miss Jordan Crazy… Jordan X Shabazz is a prick” Brittany: “Malek, don’t say that about him. Your old friends, you can patch up your differences” Duzza: “I didn’t even know we had any differences till he said we had differences! I mean, I still got love and respect for my man…but he’s changed. I’m willing to settle our differences he is…But he ain’t the same man no more” Brittany: “Yes he is” Duzza: “Naw, when he changed his name he changed everything. Back in the day Jordan always had a bottle with some kool aid in it, now he won’t touch the stuff. He’s become a white man in a black man’s body. I see my Dante trying to make him black again and I hope Dante succeeds, because Jordan needs the help…” Brittany: “And just to think, a month ago you was predicting Jordan would be the first African American champ of PCW” Duzza: “Yeah well… Jordan ain’t black no more…he’s white. He’s Caublasian, that’s what he is” Brittany: “Well, he’s also your only chance of getting out of this match without getting another beating” Duzza: “Girl please, I’ma be aight. I don’t need Jordan X Shabazz i need Jordan Crazy!, but he’d best stay out of my way in that match. That’s for damn sure. And he should never have dissed R. Kelly…”
