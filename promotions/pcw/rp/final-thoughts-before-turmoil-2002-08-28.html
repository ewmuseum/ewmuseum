Final thoughts before Turmoil

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-28-02 Her green eyes openly quickly and dart around the room. Soon, she remembers where she is and sits there, letting everything sink in. She is wet with sweat after awakening from a dream. More so a look at reality than a nightmare. Her neck is stiff, and her back hurts. That’s no surprise. She did fall asleep leaning against the wall. Amy lies close by, head resting against Brandi’s shoulder. Brandi carefully rests the small child’s head on a nearby pillow and waits as she settles comfortably into the bed. Brandi walks over to a nearby chair and has a seat, crossing one leg over the other. She rests an elbow against the armrest and lays her head against her palm. Her eyes wander around the room. They’ve been asleep for quite some time now, as it’s almost light outside. She doesn’t even remember going to sleep. She and Amy had just sat down to watch a movie. A few feet away is a large couch. Rent-A-Villain sleeps restlessly atop it. He’s been asleep off and on for awhile. He won’t be the same for quite some time after his brutal match at Third Dawning. His body is wrapped with bandages. Brandi looks at him lovingly. I’m sorry. I never should have asked you to drop out of the match. I knew you could do it, I just didn’t want to see you like this. I guess…I was being selfish. She sighs, wishing that he could hear her thoughts. She felt so bad for ever doubting him. Perhaps this was her fault in some way? Perhaps she only pushed him farther? You never would have asked me to drop out of a championship match. Never. Tonight, when I go out there, I know you’ll give me your full support. That’s one of the reasons you’re so great… A rustling beside her takes Brandi’s attention away from Rent-A-Villain and to the sleeping Amy. She isn’t used to a bed of this size, and she’s making sure she uses every inch of it. When Amy settles back down to sleep, Brandi gets up and walks over to Rent and sits on the floor beside the couch. I know I wasn’t right in asking you to give up a chance at the one thing you’ve worked so hard for. The one thing that you deserve so much. I just wanted, no I guess I expected, you to throw away everything you’ve been through. I almost threw everything we’ve been through away too. Brandi reaches up and lightly grabs Rent’s hand, taking it in her own. She sits there and stares up at him for awhile. He seems so at peace. So happy with everything. Brandi strokes his hand and is surprised to get a small squeeze in reply. She looks up, but the evil villain shows no signs of being awake. He lays there motionless. Brandi must have imagined it. I…love you. Brandi jumps when she feels a small tap on her shoulder. She turns around and sees Amy standing behind her, gripping her teddy bear in one hand and rubbing an eye with the other. Brandi smiles, turns around, and lightly kisses RAV’s cheek before getting up and walking back to the bed with Amy. Amy- What were you doing? Brandi- Nothing. Just checking on Rent. Amy- He’s gonna be ok, right? He just has some owies? Brandi looks in Rent-A-Villain’s direction before turning her attention back to the child. Brandi- Of course he will. Amy seems very pleased with her answer. She hugs the teddy bear tightly, and jumps backwards onto the bed, giggling. Brandi- Shhhh. We don’t want to wake him up, do we? Amy shakes her head, and Brandi lays back down on the bed beside her. Amy- When are we leaving? We have to go soon, don’t we? So we can get to the show on time and not be late? Brandi- Wow. So many questions already. Yeah, we’ll be leaving soon. You all ready to go? Amy nods. Brandi- Good. I have to wrestle once though, so maybe you can stay with Renty while I do that. Ok? Amy nods once more and Brandi closes her eyes. One more shot. That’s all I have. With any luck, that’s all I’ll need. It would please Amy. I know that. She’d get to wear the Cruiserweight title. She’s more of a champion than any of us, anyway. All she’s been through, and she’s still smiling. That’s all you can do really. Smile and hope for the best. Her eyes pop open when she feels the small arm thrown across her shoulders. She looks beside her. Amy lays there, eyes closed, bear clutched in an iron grip. Brandi smiles. She’s found that since she’s been with Amy, she’s been smiling a lot more lately. Kids just seem to have something about them. They’re so loving and forgiving. Oh, Amy. If I could, I’d take all your pain and lock it away somewhere. I know you’ll never show it, but I also know that it hurts you. It’s hurt me too, but you have to stick it out. Things can only get better. The child squeezes closer to Brandi, as if she can read her mind. Brandi moves closer to Amy. She’s whispering something, but she can’t quite understand what it is. Amy- I love you… Before Brandi has a chance to respond, the child falls asleep. Brandi- I love you too, Amy. Brandi smiles, and closes her eyes once again. Why does she need the Cruiserweight title? She has everything she needs right here. With that final thought, Brandi drifts off to sleep.
