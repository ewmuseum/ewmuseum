Sci-Fi Theatre(Part 4)

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 15, 2002 [We return from commercial to see Spider away from the set, the camera looks around for him, and they find him at the food table grabbing some donuts.] Director: Spider, we are on! Spider: Huh? Oh, we are? Damn, welcome back. I’m just grabbing some snacks for the next part of the movie here. Oh, it’s getting good now. Eddie eyeing the capitol of Arrakis, Arrakeen. Will the Executrix punish him? Let’s find out! [He hits that dreaded play button again, we then return to Arrakis. The palace of the Ordos looks to be getting older as they stay in there. However, the tanks and troops remain ever vigilant.] Narrator: The overseer filed the report as planned praising the Sergeant of Alpha Company, and his Executive officer for the reclaiming of the city of Arsunt. However, the Overseer saw to it that the forces were replenished on Arrakis. Now, six months after the first attack…The overseer has his back against the wall… [In his office, Eddie is going over some paperwork. All is serene, until Lieutenant Citadell, and now Staff Sergeant Snowball walk into the office. Both men wearing old spice stained armour.] Citadell: Sir, we need to talk to you. [Eddie looks up.] Eddie: Sure, have a seat guys. [Both men sit down in the chairs near the desk.] Citadell: Sir, the men are getting anxious. Of course, a lot of them are new, and I’m sure you do not want to risk them. However the older guys have been waiting for the last six months to attack Arrakeen, and you have not made any aggressive movements! Snowball: I totally agree with him sir, we need action, and we need to attack! Eddie: I really do not like the tone of your voices. However, I have been planning the right attack pattern to nail Rabban’s balls to the wall. We should ally with the Fremen. Citadell: Are you crazy? They have been raiding us for the last four months! They’ve been calling for your head!! Eddie: We should inform them about Rabban, the threat that he provides. See if you can find the one they call Muad’Dib. Snowball: …….Citadell: You have got to be kidding me? Eddie: I am not. Find him. Bring him to me. Snowball: Sir, I respectfully decline to follow that order. Citadel: As do I, sir. [Eddie pounds his desk in anger.] Eddie: You two have been too dense to come to me with any sort of plan to attempt to take Arrakis for ourselves, and now when I come up with one, you say it is unacceptable?! What are you to judge my actions?! Citadell: You sir, have been behaving irrationally ever since we took back the palace! The reason why I haven’t taken control of the mission as of yet is beyond me…Maybe because the Mentat likes you, or you are favoured by the Executrix, or a bit of both…but the point is, that we NEED TO ATTACK NOW! *BOOM* Snowball: What the hell was that? Radio: (Static)…FREMEN ATTACK! AND THIS TIME, THEY ARE HEAVILY ARMED!!...(Static) Eddie: Standard defence, I’m on the way down. [Eddie and the two rush from the office to the ground level to see the Fremen attack a section of a shield wall. The Ordos warriors are fighting with incredible savagery. Eddie runs to the front lines…] Eddie: WAIT! HOLD YOUR FIRE! HOLD YOUR FIRE!! [The Ordos warriors stop immediately, and the Fremen stop briefly as Eddie approaches one of them.] Eddie: Look, I need to talk to Muad’Dib. Man: You’ve found him. [Eddie turns to the source of the voice. He sees a man wearing a Water Reclamation battle suit, that most of the Fremen are wearing. He is about the same size as Eddie, in all respects.] Eddie: I need to confer with you, an idea. Muad’Dib: If it’s about the retaking of Arrakeen…forget it. We will not ally ourselves with someone so insidious. Eddie: Hear me out. If you take the throne of the Empire…You can slice off from the Spice that you harvest and give us ten to fifteen percent, and you will never hear from us again, except in council. Muad’Dib: But how do you know that I will take the throne of the Empire? Eddie: We put two and two together. When we regained our base here there were Imperial Sardaukar guarding the way into the base…Whether Harkonnen and Corrino have made an alliance is irrelevant. You take over Arrakeen, the Empire will be yours for the taking. Man: He lies, Muad’Dib! Eddie: Look to the future…if indeed you are the creature of legend… [There is tension on the scene, The Ordos are ready to kill the Fremen and Vice Versa. Muad’Dib closes his eyes, but opens them after a few seconds.] Muad’Dib: If we ally with you, then Arrakis is doomed, nonetheless, you are correct. Eddie: How about our other proposal? Muad’Dib: That may be satisfactory, depending on the events of the next few months. However, we will talk about that, then. In the meantime, inform your superiors to the plan that you want. Then, contact me in exactly three months time…by then, the Empire will be mine. Eddie: Very well, we are agreed. [Both men extend a hand, and they shake on the verbal agreement. We then return to the missing set, to see Spider munching on some donuts and watching a monitor.] Spider: Wow. I wonder how this is going to turn out. This is getting incredibly weird. However, I didn’t write this thing. Stay Tuned for the conclusion! [Fade to commercial for Right Guard Xtreme.]
