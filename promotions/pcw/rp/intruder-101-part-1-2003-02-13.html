Intruder 101 (part 1)

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-13-03 The small creature ran freely on top of the desk as Brianna watched on not sure what to do. Her eyes followed Grimer, but she had never really had a pet in all of her life, due to her busy schedule, so this was something new for her. What did he eat? Was there anything special he needed? That's all Brianna could think about as her mind wandered back to the moment when Kramer had run out of the cafe obviously escaping being caught by the burly men who had stepped inside just after he made a swift exit. 'What am I going to do with you?' Brianna asks in Grimer's direction as he continues his attempt at exercise. He runs to the corner of the light brown desk and leaps off onto Brianna's bed which is nicely made with big plush purple covers thrown on it without a wrinkle in sight. Grimer makes a soft landing and shows his liking for the softness by making little squeaking noises. He runs over the bed and steps right on top of an envelope and stands there looking at Brianna with his black beady eyes. 'That's the envelope Kramer gave me right before he left....maybe it has some clues as to what's all going on inside! Why didn't I think of that before?' she leaps up and grabs the envelope lifting up the flap and pulls out a folded white piece of paper. 'You are one smart little mouse.' she gives Grimer a little pat on the head as he continues the exploration of her bed. If you are reading this it means that things have gotten a little hectic in my life and I have entrusted you with the one thing that I care for most. First let me say that I am sorry if I have brought you into a situation that you are either not prepared for or don't want to undertake. But I promise, if you keep Grimer happy and well taken care of, I will repay you. Don't worry I have not left for good, and will be coming back once the coast is clear. Until then I have to just hope and pray that you have it in your heart to take care of him and do this for me. Brianna finishes reading and a look of confusion is now on her face as she still isn't sure what kind of trouble he's in. All she knows is that he is having some very hellacious problems. She looks on down the letter and thankfully it shows the foods that Grimer can/can't eat, as well as a few other things she had needed to know. 'So I guess for the time being it's going to be you and me huh buddy?' Grimer doesn't really seem to care as he has found a nice warm spot on her pillow where he is curled up. 'I think this is going to be fun.' Brianna stands up and turns out the light to give Grimer a little peace and quiet. She then descends down the steps and stretches before walking into the kitchen feeling a bit parched. A nice cold bottle of Aquafina water is waiting for her in the fridge which she greedily takes before popping off the cap and gulping down the liquid inside. She looks refreshed as she takes the bottle with her to the kitchen window as she looks out into her side yard which is dimly lit as it is nighttime. At first things are ok, but then she notices a shadow zoom past which forces her to jump and drop the bottle into the sink. Instead of picking it up Brianna pulls her hair back behind her ear and walks out of the kitchen and to the front door. Before she opens it she grabs a bat sitting by the door and a jacket, which she throws on. Slowly she turns the nob and then proceeds out of house to investigate. 'I’m not scared…this is probably just my imagination acting up.' she whispers to herself as she rounds the corner of the house nearing where she had spotted the figure. She takes a few steps and hears a crunching of leaves which causes her to turn around quickly. As she does a figure leaps out and jumps on top, overpowering her and sending her crashing to the ground very hard. The bat was knocked out of her hand so now Brianna can only rely on herself. The figure pins her hands to the ground making it hard for her to swing and knock them off. 'Get off of me!' seeing no other option, Brianna starts screaming hoping someone will come out and help her. The figure notices her tactic and quickly covers her mouth with is hand which she quickly clenches her teeth in causing him to grunt in pain. He stands up and pulls her up with him and hits her hard in the side of the face before tossing her over his shoulder. The lights dim briefly and come back on. We see Brianna tied to a chair with a gag in her mouth and a dark bruise on the side of her eye. She looks a bit dazed as the camera pans around showing that they are still at her house. 'What…what happened?' she notices that she can’t move and instantly falls into a state of fear, until she remembers her advantage. Brianna manages to reach into her back pocket and pull out what looks like a lighter. As she flicks the wheel a thin piece of metal comes out with a little circular saw at the end, and it begins to quickly rotate. She puts it against the ropes and it quickly cuts them away freeing Brianna! She leaps up and a look of revenge crosses her face recalling the confrontation earlier. At that point she thinks it has something to do with Kramer’s predicament and is worried that they think she’s connected. If life wasn’t already enough with a big wrestling show coming up, now she has to fight off an intruder, and quite possibly her life!
