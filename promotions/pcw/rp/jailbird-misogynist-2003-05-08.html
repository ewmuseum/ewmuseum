Jailbird Misogynist

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/03 (OOC: Beginning to write in the first person, just experimenting for a few series and if it goes well it'll be official. And this series is inspired by the Edgar Allen Poe piece called 'The Pit & The Pendulum', not really related but in some way with the imprisonment of my character.. anyway, try to enjoy =)..) EXPOSITION: A few Yemeni (women) misandrists, against the misogynist Stone Burns happened to have a ear on the PCW USO tour, eventually leading them to the baleful misogyny driven, blade wielding lunatic, Stone Burns. They'd administered a kidnapping just after the conclusion of the recent Turmoil. Many staff members and affiliated PCW crew had witnessed the kidnapping but were advised before the tour began, not to engage in any physical contact with the foreign populace. The women instantly gained seizure of him with the assistance of the Sana pedestrians, happening to be there at the time, and willingly helping them because of there issues with Americans. The misandrist enclaves made Stone a prisoner in their remote village in the confines of Sana. Locking him up in a home constructed imprisonment. A scarce amount of food, and faint to no light was the punishment that the misogynist would have to bear for his 'behavior' towards women. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ I've seen no light for hours. I have a bare visual of the room: rock solid walls with the void of windows, and a bolted shut door leading to my escape but as any outlandish style of policing, they fettered my hands to my back with these ponderous, steel-made shackles. My hands were drum-tight and I began to lose feeling in them but still stumbling around through the spacious prison. 'The door' had opened and closed or that's what I had heard many times as multiple Yemeni women harlots came and went, and dropped what seemed to be a bucket and a goblet with a scarce amount of food. I'd crawled around in search for the food, and finally tipped over the supposed bucket and let my hand out in scrutiny for the goblet and obtained it a few inches later. The dim light coming from beneath 'the door' and from the squared middle gave me an indication about what I would down. The goblet had nothing more than stale and contaminated water. But it seemed to do no harm as I sipped it, and the urge to quaff it in one gulp came to mind but I rationed it because of my thought that it would be the first and last drink. I placed it down on the ground, which was quite visible to my eye. The ground had well-designed masonry of unidentifiable letters, and next to them unclad women.. I winced at the thought of that but rose my head and got back to my meal. The bucket had nothing more than a jagged piece of bread, in my case it was rather tasty and tendered, seasoned with some kind of substance and spice. But I seemed to be deceived by the taste and I completely forgot to think if it were to be poisoned. I spit it out but it made me want more as a few bites in the bread were left but I garnered my hunger and sipped the water. From the back of the 'door' I heard sounds of feminine voices, as if they were scheming of what to do with me. I crept as the tight shackles on my feet prohibited me to stand, and went onward slowly, as the light from under the door made more the room much clearer and visible. As I got near the door, I tried to push forward with my hands throwing them up over my head but the pain overcame my body and they fell back down on my spine. To the side of me were a mass amount of rodents that I'd never seen before especially a muskrat that frightened me with it's gesturing but I looked away. I brought my chest up and it bulked out and I had firm grip of the knob of the door with my mouth and all of my teeth. Turning the knob I felt like my body was being whipped with steel chains and I whimpered slightly turning the knob and finally having it opened. The door creaked open, luckily not being expected by anyone that might've guarded the door. I jerked it to the side with my chin and opened widely with a loud wooden cracking sound. It all turned out to be a ploy as the metal bars drained in to the hard floor obstructed me from crawling out to my escape. Instead, the worst was about to happen as the walls were caving in and all I thought was... good that I had all this hate for women. Their possessed minds would actually crush a person for a specific behavior and act. Coming closer and closer I prepared to be sandwiched but it suddenly came to a halt and a horrible looking Yemeni women came before the metal bars, and looking to the middle right where a key hole unlocking the door-like structure she placed a key and unlocked the door smiling with her chipped teeth, shaded in a fiend color of brown. Her hair was strewn to the top and it looked similar to a mullet, she looked down on me and blurted a few words I could not understand, most likely in her language but what the fuck did I care.. all I was thinking about was me grabbing my blade and cutting her in to pieces.. but before her she had a glossy knife, and she stroked it with her hand and cackled.. She dragged my body through the pavement, scratching it from top to bottom but I didn't dare to feel agony from a woman's act so I kept it inside as she brought me out to the center of a garden, elaborate flowers of all types and the rich green grass filled the air. It was midday afternoon and the sky was as unusually yellow as that woman's teeth. She left me there and called out to the side of her as I lay there on my back, holding in the pain from my hands.. and abruptly came a herd of woman in a tandem, dancing and smiling.. they approached me; all holding knives and I just shut my eyes, and pursed my lips. I was feeling the water that I had earlier drank and it began to make me ill and I should've known not to down such dirty liquid. They all winded back with the knives all in the process of kicking me in a vicious way but still coming to no pain in my part. The first women came down, and she and the other looked the same as the women who had came for me in the imprisonment and I winced at the look of her face. She slid down my body and I just shivered at the feeling as she began slicing my thigh. The others seemed to be eager to attack me so they all simultaneously started slicing the parts of my body, more specifically my thighs and the top of my legs. I felt pain but not so damaging physically yet still painful. Finally, after minutes of torture as I put it they stopped and thought to have done much damage by slicing my thighs and legs.. although the parts were quite bloody and agonizing in the depths of my body I expected more. Thinking it was all over, they frolicked off cheering, as the same women who came and obtained me dragged me back through the grass burning my skin and on to the pavement platform, and feet away was the door back in to my cell.. she send me in by a rough kick, locked the metal bar door right after shutting the other door that I had earlier opened. I looked around in to nothingness as the only source of light, which came from the bottom and middle of the door started to die out in a way and the goblet had tipped over, spilling out all the contaminated water inside, even though I didn't seem to like it a dire thirst continued to come over me. The bucket with the bread had now been gobbled up by the rodents and flies roaming in the cell and I just waited there.. but coming to mind was my Fusion match and how it would be 24 hours or so away and I was still in Yemen, beaten up by a bunch of harlots, who'd dressed me up in a torn up toga... I will get to Fusion because those harlots aren't thinking well enough... and I will avenge as much as I can. Lastly I aided my cuts, but every time I touched each one it gave off a slight burn in to my body so I left it alone and pondered off in to the darkness and nothingness of the cell. TO BE CONTINUED
