The Birth Of Hubbardville : The Calling From...Um...Somewhere...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/04/06 Ellie~!’s Prologue : Okay, like, first off...Xavier is SO not scared of Duke Williams! Just because that redneck beat the hell out of him once, doesn’t mean he’ll do it again! Soon Duke Williams, he will hit you for every comment you have made about me...Calling me a bitch, a slut and a Cum-Ne...EUGH....I HATE DUKE WILLAIMS! Anyway, even if Xavier isn’t fighting you on Sunday he’ll beat that little friend of yours because, like, the Ultimate Authority is SO the only group of people who wear suits!..... “I still don’t get why, like, people insist on driving on these SO middle of nowhere roads late at night! I mean, like, they only end up saying they have been abducted by aliens or they saw a headless man....You know, nerd’s stuff...” Our shot opens up inside Eleanor’s black jeep Cherokee which is, as she pointed out in her usual whiny voice, driving along a deserted road late at night. The headlights only show the road and the tall trees that line it thickly either side. Xavier : “We will not be abducted by aliens, although if anyone was it would be a Scientologist given our levels of wisdom beyond anything else seen on this plane of existence.” The Scientologist is wearing one of his black hooded sweatshirts with “XK” emblazoned on the font in flaming green and purple. With this he has a pair of his trademark black worker-cut jeans with purple and green Scientological runes embroidered down the left leg to where a pair of black, purple and grey Skechers adorn his feet as they work the peddles. His valet looks to him as if he has missed out something glaringly obvious and given the very, very slim chance of anything appearing in front of them he looks to her, his facial expressions prompting Ellie~! to explain. Eleanor : “....And they would, like, SO take the preps as well! I mean, we are like a genetically enhanced super race or something...Like, um, that...” The PCW Commissioneress is wearing a skinny fit pink t-shirt with Snapple~! across the chest in silver and a pair of sandblasted blue jeans with silver sequinned stars down the legs. Her long, wavy brown hair with subtle blond highlights is neatly tied back and as usual, she wears a large array of bands and bracelets around her wrists. .......Being a Prep is great. You’re, like, automatically better than anyone else and you don’t even have to work hard to get it, which is SO cool because working for things is, like, work!...... Hearing what she has to say the Commissioner turns to look back at the road. Xavier : “Yes Ellie~!....They’d take the preps...” As usual, the naive valet fails to pick up on the sarcasm and looks rather pleased that the Gold Patron Meritorious agrees with her. Eleanor : “I can’t wait to get to Fusion, they you could, like, book yourself in a match with Duke because you are, like, the Commissioner to my Commissioneress and then once and for all you can, like...Beat him up for all the horrible things he said to me....” At the mention of Xavier getting in the ring with Duke Williams his head jolts around and he looks right into his valet’s pale blue eyes, with what seems a look of fear on his face. He tries to speak but no words come out prompting Eleanor to stare back curiously. Eleanor : “Said.......To........Me?” ......A lot of people say that Xavier is scared of Duke Williams...Like, how stupid can they be? If he was scared he’d be thinking of ways to avoid him and acting all strange when Duke’s name is mentioned....Some people are, like, SO DUH!...... The Scientologist is going pale colour in the face as he gulps at Ellie~!’s suggested prospect of having him get into the ring again with Duke Williams, making him think quickly for an argument to kill the suggestion. Xavier : “Um...I would...But....Um.....I can’t....” Even Eleanor, who admittedly isn’t the most worldly of people looks at him, confused by what appears to be reluctance. Xavier : “It’s a rule that PCW has...Yeah, if you fight someone in an eight man tag you can’t fight them in singles again for a month, and because me and Duke had that...encounter on Sunday, a match is surely out of the question.....” He breathes a sigh of relief and settles back into his seat to concentrate on the road. Eleanor seems to understand and does the same, reclining her chair back before her eyes narrow and a look of confusion appears on her face. Eleanor : “But don’t you fight Legend Inc. on Sunday....I think that was, like, him in that match too wasn’t it?” As her preppy voice states the fact Xavier again wriggles in his seat, feeling almost claustrophobic under Ellie~!’s unintentional questioning in the confines of the jeep. Xavier : “Um....That wasn’t Legend Inc.....It was, um.....RYAN PHILLIPPE....They look identical! Yeah, that’s it....So I am free to fight Legend at Sabotage....Phew...” He wipes his brow and lets out a sigh of relief, but this still doesn’t get his valet of his case as one answer leads to another question. Eleanor : “Wow! Ryan Phillippe wrestled in PCW.....Did he bring Reece? That woman knows how to condition....” The Gold Patron ignores the question and after about a minute Eleanor stops staring at him, prodding him, and “huh?”’ing him for an answer and collapses back into her chair, folding her arms and muttering under her breathe. Eleanor : “Ooooh, my name is Xavier....I’m a Scientologist.......I don’t have to answer my valet’s questions......Na na-na na na....” Using his super increased spiritual enlightenment, Xavier blocks out his valet’s muttering and he just looks at the deserted road ahead of him. The road is still empty, the car is quiet....Nothing is really happening....It’s just a normal quiet drive at night.... Xavier : “Hmmm.....It’s 22 : 48....” Ellie~! looks at him with pure confusion and peculiarity on her face, her jaw dropping. Eleanor : “But there.....Are, like.....Only 1 to 12 on clocks!” Suddenly their is a blinding flash of light that emanates from just in front of the windshield and floods the entire screen with the brilliant, glowing white. “Where am I? You can’t do this to me! I’m a Gold Patron Meritorious you know! I can have you arrested for religious prejudice!” As the lights fades down we see Xavier standing amidst a vast expanse of white. Where he is seems to have only one dimension and the white stretches as far as the eye can see all around him. The Commissioner turns 360 on the spot, scanning the void around him. Xavier : “Okay......If this is some kind of alien spaceship then I perfectly understand you beaming me up here....I know my Scientological brain is far more evolved than those mere humans down there but I must tell you, you will learn very little about my wisdom by probing my anus! So are we agreed? No anal probing?” ......Okay....I was, kinda not here so I can’t really comment....Great example of minimalism though...... There is no reply and despite the vast expanse he stands in, his voice echoes all around him, something which seems to please him. Xavier : “Wow...I actually DO love my own voice....Wait....I’ve read about places like these....I bet it’s the Government.....WELL I WON’T TELL YOU ANYTHING ABOUT THE UPPER WORKINGS OF SCIENTOLOGY! YOU’LL HAVE TO KILL ME LIKE THE THETAN’S KILLED THE LAST GUY TO LEA-.....Um.....Maybe I said too much....” The obviously flustered Scientologist starts to stroll around but nothing, even the horizons seem to change as h looks all around him. He suddenly stops and looks down to his fete before stamping as hard as he can but no sound is produced. Xavier : “IF I FIND OUT YOU HAVE SOMETHING TO DO WITH THIS DUKE YOU ARE IN TROUBLE! THE ULTIMATE AUTHORITY MAY NOT BE ABLE TO TRANSPORT PEOPLE THROUGH SPACE AND TIME TO ANOTHER DIMENSION BUT WE’LL...WE’LL...DAMN IT WE’LL HIT YOU A LOT!!!!” Kannon starts to stomp around, looking furious and insecure at the situation, one he doesn’t have control over for once like he does in PCW. “WE CHOSE YOU FOR A REASON GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS KANNON” A voice booms out of nowhere at a near deafening volume and Xavier immediately reaches for his ears and cringes as the intensity of the voice blows his long auburn hair back. The Scientologist squirms. Xavier : “Um....Can you turn down the volume or something before my Scientological ear drums explode?” “DO YOU NOT TIRE OF THE OPPRESSION SCIENTOLOGISTS MUST SUFFER IN THE WORLD” The voice is now a little quieter, something Xavier acknowledges with a slight nod as he mulls over the question, still slightly un-nerved by the situation he is in. Xavier : “Not really, it gives me an excuse when I lose, I can say that the referee was oppressing my beliefs by counting fast or something...It comes in rather handy with all the fuss flying around about religious tolerance....” “WE HAVE A MISSION FOR YOU TO UNDERTAKE XAVIER. YOUR NAME WILL GO DOWN IN THE HISTORY BOOKS AS ONE OF THE GREATEST EVER SCIENTOLOGISTS AND THIS WILL BE YOUR CROWNING ACHIEVEMENT” The Scientologist is now staring straight up to where it seems the voice is coming but he can only see white. He takes a second to digest the words before looking a bit confused. Xavier : “You mean I wasn’t going to be already....” “YOU WILL GIVE THEM REFUGE. YOU WILL BE THE MAN RESPONSIBLE FOR GIVING EVERY OPPRESSED SCIENTOLOGIST A HOME” As the voice booms again Xavier is now on his knees, tapping on the floor and seeing if he can produce any sound. Xavier : “I do that already, I even have Jordan X Shabazz in my trunk, giving an oppressed man a ride and all! Um....How many people do you think I will have to house again? I only have about 17 spare rooms and only 1 TV.....” “YOU WILL BUILD A GREAT CITY XAVIER. IT WILL BE A SHINING JEWEL AMIDST A CRUMBLING SOCIETY WHERE ONLY SCIENTOLOGISTS CAN LIVE TO ESCAPE THE NARROW-MINDEDNESS OF THE OUTSIDE WORLD” Xavier : “Can Ellie~! live there too? Or I’ll never here the end of it....” “GO NOW GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS AND BECOME THE MAN ALL SCIENTOLOGISTS LOOK UP TOO. THAT ALL SCIENTOLOGISTS GO TO IN THEIR TIME OF NEED. GO AND BUILD..........HUBBARDVILLE....” Another blinding flash of light is seen and Xavier has disappeared from the other plane of reality before another flash brings the now befuddled Scientologist back. “OH.....IT IS IN WISCONSIN BY THE WAY....WE BUILT IT FOR GIOVANNI RIBISI TO RUN BEFORE HE MADE THE MOD SQUAD....AFTER THAT, WELL.....YOU KNOW...” The now familiar flash of intense light happens and we cut back to the jeep where Xavier slams his foot down and brakes sharply causing the tyres to screech and Jordan X Shabazz to fly into the side of the trunk where he is travelling. “OWWWWWWWWWWWW” Ellie~! looks to him with a quizzical look as she holds where her seatbelt dug into her. Eleanor : “Um....Like...OWWWW!” The Scientologist looks around, almost startled. .......Wow....He really looked a little bit...Well....Freaky, and he really cares too much about time and everything. Time isn’t that important when you think about it, it’s just kinda...Well, there! Now flossing...THAT is important.... Eleanor : “What’s up? Someone mention you fighting Duke again?” The Commissioner then looks down at the clock on the dashboard and stares at it agasp, his bright Green eyes wide open. Xavier : “It was 22 : 48.....Now it is 22 : 59...” Eleanor : “Well, actually it says 23 : 00...” He notices the clock has ticked over and sighs, before pulling off again to continue their journey to Fusion. Xavier : “Oh, and after Fusion we are going to Wisconsin...” In his head he runs through the events of the last 15 minutes in his head, knowing that he has a great task ahead of him in creating Hubbardville, a Utopian society for all Dianetics followers as the show fades to black. “What’s a Wisconsin?”
