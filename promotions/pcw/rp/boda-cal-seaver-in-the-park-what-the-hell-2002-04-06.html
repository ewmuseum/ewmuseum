Boda, Cal Seaver... in the park? What the hell?

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/06/2002 Boda: “36 hours. That’s all the time left. No more time for dreaming. Only for the nightmare to become an ugly reality. Especially for you Mr. Chase. For Miya... this past month’s been a living hell. He pissed me off and I taught him a lesson about life. As for you... The Messiah.... I can not teach you that lesson. For, in your life, everyone that you love, either leaves you.... Or fucking dies.” Cal Seaver: “Must you use that language?” Boda: “Shut up Cal.... this is my time. I can speak any way I want to. What are you going to do? Call in that little Jerry McGuire puke kid. Go ahead.... I beat down a pregnant bitch, what makes you think I won’t slap that little four eyed prick around?” Cal Seaver: “What has gotten into you? You’ve never been like this.” Boda: “Whatever. Just do your job, before I make you do it.” Cal Seaver: “...............” *Our scene opens in a park in downtown Anaheim. City that will host Spring Fling. Current hometown of Alexander Miyagawa. Site of the beating that will take place Sunday night. Blood will spill. 2 men will go home empty handed. One man will retain what is rightfully his. In this park, sit Cal Seaver, PCW reporter. Next to him, almost overshadowing him, sits the current PCW World Champion. Boda, non-challantly sitting on a park bench, watching the world go by. Boda, sitting there, answering questions from the ever questioning Cal. Of course, this has already started on the wrong note, as far as Boda is concerned.* Boda: “Cal.... you are not going to sit here and piss me off, are you? Because, if you do, I’ll simply bounce you off the sidewalk, and take my title belt, walk over to the Anaheim Memorial Hospital right over there, and pay a little visit to Natalie Miyagawa.” Cal Seaver: “Speaking of Natalie.... are you going to give the blood to save her life. Save her child’s life? After all, you are the reason she’s in there.” Boda: “I told Miya this, and I’ll tell you this. If he spills my blood..... she can have it. If not..... oh well. And, I suggest Miya and Chris Chase are comfortable with hospitals, because after Spring Fling.... they both are going to be needing a long hospital stay.” Cal Seaver: “So, you plan on hurting your opponents?” *At this point Boda stands up, towering over the awkward Cal.* Boda: “Cal... look at me. Tell me what you see.” Cal Seaver: “I see the PCW World Champion.” Boda: “And.” Cal Seaver: “And.......” *Boda grabs Cal’s face, making him looks directly at him* Boda: “I mean, LOOK at me. What do you see?” *Cal finally gets the picture.* Cal Seaver: “I see 6’11 350 pounds of pure monster. I see a man that intimidates others without speaking. I see a changed man, with much more anger to release. I see.......” Boda: “All right, shut up, that’s enough. Now, you described me to a T. Now, let’s look at Chase and Miyanotgonnawinna.” Cal Seaver: “Okay, lets.” *Boda sits back down, next to Cal. He kicks back, laying the World Title across his chest, for all to see.* Boda: “First..... there is Chris ‘I might be a Messiah, but I still like to take Corey in the ass’ Chase. He’s what? 5’1... maybe 120 pounds soaking wet. He’s.....” *Cal quickly corrects Boda’s obvious mistake* Cal Seaver: “Actually, he’s 6’1, about 227 lbs.” *Boda smacks Cal in the back of the head* Boda: “Interrupt me again, and you get hurt. Correct me again, and Lord have mercy on ya, because I won’t. Got it?” Cal Seaver: “Got it.” Boda: “So, like I was saying......He’s talented, that I will give him. If you call being a whining, arrogant piss ant a talent. He’s got style..... maybe Grease was once hip, but come on..... that movie has been over done. I mean.... Cal, besides that.... why does he deserve a title shot?” Cal Seaver: “Because you made a deal with him, and you gave him a title shot.” Boda: “True, and do you know why I did?” *Cal simply shrugs ‘NO’.* Boda: “Because......I believe that PCW is an equal opportunity employer. I mean, they hired guys like Duzza, and OG and Kramer Wild Wing. They hire idiotic Batman loving, Superman worshipping, pajama wearing freaks. So, why not give the better of the two homosexuals they hired, an opportunity of a lifetime.” Cal Seaver: “Homosexuals?” Boda: “Damn Cal..... are you blind. Haven’t you watched Miyagonnadropanotherbigone as he showed the proof, over and over again. It’s blatantly obvious. Only problem, is lately, they are missing the pivot man. Snow, being he’s all laid up, with a bum knee now. Thanks to Chase of course. So, there you have Chris Chase and Corey Williams left. Bang, the two ‘special’ people that PCW hired. I gave the better one, Chris Chase, an opportunity. I gave him his fifteen minutes. Why? Because I can, and because................” *Boda looks around, before looking right at Cal, with the evil look in his eyes.* Boda: “Because...... I WANTED TOO.” Cal Seaver: “Wow.... you wanted to face him? Why?” Boda: “Ever since PCW2, ever since I stepped foot into the Deuce, all I heard was this ‘Chris Chase is the next World Champion.’ ‘Chris Chase is the future of this fed.’ ‘Chase is as good as Boda.’. For months in the Deuce, that’s what I listened to, that’s what I heard. And, after I lost the chance to be PCW2 Continental Champion.... I knew I had to prove myself better than him. Even after the fateful ‘recruiting’ scandal, I knew I had to better myself. So, I DID. Look at the records. Read the bios. Chris Chase...... Former PCW2 Continental Champion. Former PCW Cruiserweight Champion [thanks to me]. Impressive? Not really. I mean, it’s not bad..... neither is Chris Chase. For some one that was as highly praised as he was...... I’m not impressed. Now, Cal... let’s look at my accolades. BODA..... [clears throat].....PCW2 Tag Team Champion. PCW2 World Champion. PCW former 2 Time Tag Team Champion, former [one of the best] Continental Champion, former Extreme Champion and 2 Time PCW World Champion. What’s that speak for about my changes at Spring Fling?” Cal Seaver: “Good, I guess.” *Boda plops the belt onto the ground.* Boda: “Good... I.... guess? Cal, do you not know how to use that god given brain of yours. Good I guess is not the answer I was looking for. It’s the wrong answer. You are the weakest link ..... good bye. No Cal. It speaks volumes about my changes. I’ve more than proved myself the better wrestler. I’ve proved myself the bigger wrestler. I proved myself to be a greater champion, the real future of PCW2, and soon to be PCW. I’m up here.... [Boda raises his one hand, up above his head.]... and Chase is still down here.....[Boda lowers his other hand right by his Johnson.].....Yet...................” *Boda punches the tree right next to him* Boda: “Yet....... that little cockroach still talk shit. He claims to be better than me. He mocks me. He disrespects ME. No one disrespects Boda. Ivan tried it... where’s he at? OSW. Why.... because IVAN FEARS BODA. Mexi disrespected me. He’s in the curtain jerker division with Tyler Nelson. So, I gave him his 15 minutes, in order to finally beat into him... that he’s no better than me. Time to teach him about respect.” *Boda snorts, and lets out a growl. He looks visibly upset.* Cal Seaver: “So, Chase is more of a lesson, than actually an obstacle. Got it. Then, why Miya? Why this sudden obsession with him?” *Boda grabs his neck, and cracks it. Cal quivers at the sound of the vertebrae in Boda’s neck snapping, crackling and popping. * Boda: “Now, Miyaswifeisgonnadie, well he’s a different story. Miya and I are old school. Matter of fact, if I remember right..... me and Miyajackass are the only remaining wrestlers from the beginning of PCW’s existence. At least, we are the biggest stars from PCW’s beginnings. We are OLD SCHOOL. But, believe it or not... we’ve never gone at it, sans one Tag Match me and the other old schooler [Kano] beat Miyalossers ass with a forgotten partner. So, I ask you Cal... why not Miyagoingdown. I want to see who the better one is. All the long, we’ve been heading in different directions, heading for an eventual collision course, and guess what. It is demolition derby time. Me and Alex have to butt heads, and why not now. Why not while the biggest prize in the land is on the line. Because it makes sense. Two future hall of famers. Two warriors. One goal. The Gold. The Japo-American against the Ameri-Czech. Old School vs Old School. I want to be the one synominous with PCW.” Cal Seaver: “Well, I need to finish this up. Any last statements?” Boda: “Yeah.....Miya.... hope you bring everything tomorrow night. Because, remember.... it might save your wife’s life. And Chase.....it’s spelled R...E...S...P...E....C....T. Learn it.... BOY!!” *Boda simply gets up, and heads out into the park. Cal grimaces at the thought of being the ones that angered Boda.*
