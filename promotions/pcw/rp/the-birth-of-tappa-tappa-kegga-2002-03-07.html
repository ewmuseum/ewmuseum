The Birth Of Tappa Tappa Kegga

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-07-02 **Theme Music: 'Three Is The Magic Number' from Schoolhouse Rocks** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, It's official. I've got the stuff to get the CWS back up and running. I'm the PCW Extreme Champion. And Fusion...Fusion's gonna be something else. I'm the happiest I've been since I lost you, darling. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [Our scene opens up in Jason's apartment. Mark and Joe, Jason's friends who rescued him from certain embarrasment last week. The two fraternity boys lounge lazily on the couch, watching static on the TV.] Mark: I can't believe Jason won. Joe: Yeah, he's like a real star now. Mark: I always thought he was just some kinda weird dude who lived next door. Joe: Yeah. [Silence for a brief while.] Joe: You wanna watch some porn? Mark: ... [Suddenly, the strong form of Jason pushes the door open. His arm is bandaged up, and Jason looks pretty badly banged up from his match at the PPV. But is that getting him down? No way.] Jason: Guys, what's up? Mark: Nothin'. Joe: Yeah. Jason: Anyone call? Mark: Nope. Joe: Uh...wait. One guy. Mark: Oh yeah. Something Inc. Some telemarketer. Jason: Oh, it's probably not important then. I talked to everyone I needed to talk to today. Mark: Cool. Jason: So... [Jason plops down on the couch.] Jason: You guys seem like you don't do a whole lot. [Mark and Joe look at each other.] Mark and Joe: Nope. Jason: Good. Mark: Why? Jason: I'm gonna keep you occupied for a little while. Keep you from going on the streets. Joe: Huh? Jason: I'mm reopening the Core Wrestling School soon, guys. [Mark and Joe give Jason a totally blank look.] Joe: You need janitors? Jason: Not exactly... [Another blank look from the frat boys.] Jason: You guys are gonna be enrolled in it. [Joe and Mark's eyes get really wide.] Joe: We're gonna be professional wrestlers? [Jason nods.] Jason: You guys are gonna learn a real trade. [The boys just sit in stunned amazment.] Mark: This...this is the opportunity of a lifetime. [Joe looks at Mark, and smacks him upside the head.] Joe: We're gonna have to be wrestlers? Jason: Well, you don't have to. Mark: It'll get you chicks, Joe. [Joe's look of disappointment turns to joy.] Joe: Wicked... [Jason stands up, and turns off the TV.] Jason: So come on boys, pack your things. [The guys look stunned.] Jason: You're spending your Spring Break working for me. We're going to Fusion. [The fratboys look stunned.] Joe: We're going to PCW's Friday Fusion!? Mark: Really? Jason: Yep. [They exchange a look.] Joe: Can we get drunk first? [Jason slaps him upside the head.] Jason: No. Not until we get to Texas. Mark: Alright! Jason: We've gotta make a pickup when we're there...and you guys'll get to see two of the most fantastic technical wrestlers in the business wrestle. [Another blank look from the fratties.] Mark: Aren't you wrestling Steve Johnson? Joe: Isn't he fired? [Jason sighs.] Jason: This ISN'T going to be easy. [And with that, Jason and the two boys leave to head towards Texas and Fusion. On the way out, Jason is heard talking to the boys on his way out...] Jason: Hey, do you guys know where I can get a cowboy hat around here?
