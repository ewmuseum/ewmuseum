Giver Of Life

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 20th One o'clock, the moon is still and the stars sparkle. Calling me closer I bring my eyes only to discover the false accusations of the sparkling lights in the sky. Two o'clock, my eyes grow heavy but I remain awake. The night is taking it's tole but I refuse to give in to the noctural evils surrounding me like so many others. Three o'clock, the hour of Satan has arrived. Insulting the holy trinity as it moves slowly by. The longest sixty minutes of the day come and go in what feels like an eternity. Still no sign of he who I dare wait for. Four o'clock, a knock on the door. *Tap**Tap**Tap* I walk closer to the sound of knocking. *Tap**Tap**Tap* How they grow louder. *Tap**Tap**Tap* I reach the door but nothing is there. My mind grows weary and everything is not what it seems. Five o'clock, the sun begins it's slow asscension from the other side of the world. For a being who does not sleep it shines brightly. I made it through the night but still no sign of he who I await. Six o'clock, *CRASH* A bird flies through my window ending it's life. As the life giver I breathed new life into the dim witted flying entity. I see someone over the hill. I run to meet them but it takes longer than it should. Seven o'clock, I reach the hill and it is he. He is in a much different way. His hair gray, his beared grown long. He falls down. I get onto my knees. His heart is racing. Why now? WHY TODAY? I must save him. Eight o'clock, he is dead. I saw the life being sucked away from him in an instant. To watch a man die is a beautiful thing. His eyes grew wide and his blood ran cold. It was his time, now it is mine. Nine o'clock, the noose is tightened. I hang breathlessly. The world around me grows dark and I wait in pain. *CRASH* As I reach the end all around me goes dark, I hear voices. Ten o'clock, *BEEP**BEEP**BEEP**BEEP**BEEP* I live, what shame I have brought upon myself.Surviving my own attempts at death. Perhaps there is a reason for my life. Perhaps I should stay. Just perhaps, I meant too. Eleven o'clock, I lie in bed wondering my future. Do I live or do I die? Do I stay or do I go? What can I do but save lives and end them? What t he fuck am I here for? I have the two most powerful gifts. The gift to give life and the gift to snatch it just like an oncoming vehicle. Twelve o'clock, I awake, drouzy I find myself alone. Was it a dream? Why am I home? Was that a prophecy? One thing I know, this was no act of man, this is but an act of God. An act ofsalvation. *epilogue* These last twelve hours have brought upon thought after thought after thought, each calling me, ME, to save humanity. I am not the person that fate calls upon. I am the person that the world need remove. I am no longer Mike Frampton, I am the giver of life, killer of men, possesser of power. I no longer give life, I take it, all but my own.
