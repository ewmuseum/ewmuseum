The Interrogation...

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within a dark area. It is totally pitch black dark and you have no way of seeing where you are and you can’t even see your hand in front of your face. After a few seconds of darkness, the light in the room comes on. The room is actually a cell, there are no windows and only one light in the room that is controlled by a switch just outside of the cell. Semore Smoke walks into the room or cell and walks to the center where you see a figure sitting in a chair under a black veil. Smoke pulls back the veil and reveals that it’s AJ Trinidad’s pet kangaroo, Crespo. Crespo seems scared as Smoke stares at it with a smile on his face. Smoke walks around the seated kangaroo and just intimidates it further with the silence. Smoke makes a complete circle around Crespo and stops, he then speaks... Semore Smoke: Well, if it isn’t AJ’s best friend Crespo? Yes, I know who you are and I hope you know who I am. {{{{Smoke is now tapping his finger on the kangaroo’s head and it’s obvious that the kangaroo is getting angry over the tapping, it opens up it’s jaw an snaps at Smoke’s finger, but Smoke pulls it back just in time...}}}} Semore Smoke: Whoa boy! You wouldn’t want to do that again, or you’ll go another four days without food or water. Now, you see, you’re collateral to me because AJ Trinidad wrote a check that his ass can’t sign. And I’ll prove that whenever I get my next chance at him. But until then, you’re mine, not his... And you’re just being used to motivate Trinidad to win the PCW TV Championship, a title that I’ll be glad to take out of his hands later own. Now, do you have anything to say for yourself? {{{{The kangaroo just stares at Smoke as Smoke’s grin gets wider. Smoke looks at the Armani suit that he’s wearing and sees that Crespo is staring at his pocket the whole time. Smoke reaches in his pocket and pulls out a joint and Crespo gets excited at the site of it...}}}} Semore Smoke: Oh yeah, I saw you smoking a joint one day on TV with Trinidad. So you like to toke up as well? Well, I couldn’t oppress a fellow toker like that, I could beat you, starve you, and even kill you, but I could never deny a toker a joint. Why not let you have this one? {{{{Crespo jumps in the chair and the chair bounces up and down as he’s excited. Smoke sticks the joint in Crespo’s mouth and then pulls out a lighter. Crespo shows a hint of a smile as Smoke lights the joint. Crespo takes a long drag on the joint and then spits it out as he begins to cough from the joint. Smoke looks at the kangaroo and laughs...}}}} Semore Smoke: Hahaha! Oh come on! It’s not that bad! AJ Trinidad thought Oregano would be good for me, and I thought the same. I just hope that you realize that paybacks a bitch after this. {{{{The kangaroo looks sad now over the fact that he hasn’t had a smoke in days and it doesn’t seemed phased on the fact that it hasn’t eaten in days either. Smoke walks around the kangaroo again and continues to have a smile on his face that doesn’t look natural, looks almost forced, but it’s a smile nonetheless…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, AJ Trinidad will have the biggest decision to make this Friday, but he won’t know about that until this Tuesday night when I unveil my plans with you. Ha! Your friend will wish he had never crossed the Smokester and his band of thugs. {{{{Smoke grabs the black veil from the floor and throws it back over the kangaroo who squirms a bit but just settles down as the restraints tighten up on the chair. Smoke picks up the joint that he had given to the kangaroo is puts it out in the palm of his hand. He then throws it into a wastebasket that is across the cell…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, don’t squirm now, the more you move, the tighter those restraints get, and before you know it, it’ll cut the blood off to your hooves and they’ll just rot and fall off. {{{{Smoke turns around and walks through the door of the cell, the camera shows him standing there outside the door and he closes the door behind him. You see Smoke’s face through the window in the door as he continues to smile. You see Smoke reach over somewhere beside the door and he pulls something down, the lights in the cell then go off, and Smoke walks away while the scene fades into the black abyss of the cell…}}}}
