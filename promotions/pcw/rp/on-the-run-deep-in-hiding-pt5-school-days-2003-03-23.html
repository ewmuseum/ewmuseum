On The Run, Deep In Hiding pt5- School Days

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd March Being back home in Puebla is a difficult task for El Diablo. He's already laid to rest his past by scattering Pedro's ashes away, and being met by a familiar face in Carlos, an old primary school friend. All this being a way for El Diablo to reach a new beginning and start again- at least in some aspects. So, to grow upon that, today El Diablo is making a visit to his old high school- the scene of a very lonely childhood for him. There is nothing quite like visiting an old school, just walking back down and through the halls and corridors triggers so many old memories you never thought you had. The memories can be good or bad, but either way you feel the same about them, you miss them, you miss that old high school feeling, waking up every morning to head to that building where your life moved forward. Of course the reasons for missing them are very different, you miss the good because you wish you could go back to life when it was so simple and then you miss the sad because you wish you could go back and change past events. Thus is life. So now The Mexican Devil heads back into school, the school where he suffered dearly. El Diablo's feelings are clearly mixed, but it is something that needs to be done. It needs to be done so El Diablo can move on with his life and make a future for himself. A future of greatness. 'Ah, Raul I'm glad you can make it,' a tall and slender woman in her late thirties greets El Diablo. Diablo squints his eyes as he first enters the main entrance of the building, trying to decipher who that woman is. 'Miss Rodriguez?' he questions to try and find an answer of whom the woman is. The woman smiles and pushes her long dark brown hair back. 'It's Mrs Garcia now actually,' she responds proudly. Diablo smirks in knowing he was right with his guess. 'Congratulations,' Diablo says meaninglessly. Raul looks at Mrs Garcia directly in her deep dark brown eyes, reminiscing about the days he spent as a teenager lusting after her. She's still very attractive even for her age, and El Diablo has definitely noticed that. Mrs Garcia, then known as Miss Rodriguez was their English teacher, and a very good one at that. Maybe it was because Diablo lusted after her for his entire time in her class that he became so good at speaking English. Raul did try to impress her, and impress her he did, as his English now is perfect. Mrs Garcia of course did not know he looked at her like that back then, but he seems to be looking at her like that currently, despite being married to Angela. El Diablo notices that she is wearing a blue blouse and a long shin length blue skirt with black high-heeled shoes. A complete contrast of clothing he is used to seeing on attractive women, after all his wife does tend to dress a bit sluttish. 'Here come this way,' Mrs Garcia grabs him by the left arm and starts leading him around, 'Take a look around, things haven't changed much since you were here, but our decorating of this school has,' she states with a little smile. Diablo aptly follows her, making sure to look at her ass every moment she isn't looking back at him. Diablo is wearing a loose white vest which clearly shows his muscular physique, and some long cut off blue denim shorts with brown sandals. 'You know Raul, you are the most famous person to ever come to our school,' she comments with a beautiful smile. Diablo smiles back, proud of the fact that he is considered famous in his hometown. Just looking around the school as Mrs Garcia leads him, is bringing back memories to him. The corridor where Jose got a broken nose because a door was accidentally slammed into him, the science lab where Patty was set on fire as a joke, of course it wasn't funny as she ended up with second degree burns and then the Principal's Office where Raul would end up going to a lot in his duration here. Raul really wasn't that much of a trouble maker, he just got involved with the wrong crowd of people and ended up taking the blame for them. They are the kind of friends he had to live with having while growing up, no wonder he was so close with this brother Pedro. He gets snapped out of reliving old memories by Mrs Garcia telling him to take a seat just outside the Principal's Office. Diablo does as is told, just like old times. He looks up at her and smirks. 'Am I trouble again Miss Rodriguez?' he asks innocently. Mrs Garcia laughs softly. 'Please just call me Pilar,' she says while taking a seat next to Diablo, 'Juan just wants to see again,' El Diablo looks confused. 'Juan?' he asks completely baffled. Pilar plays with her hair and looks back at him with a smile. 'Sorry, Mr Dominguez,' she replies. Diablo gulps. Mr Dominguez was the Principal he had to meet on several occasions when he was taking the blame for the things his 'friends' did. His mother and father were never happy about having to come in and see the Principal on an almost weekly basis; so just by being in trouble for his friends he was in trouble with his parents very frequently. 'Oh it's ok,' Pilar Garcia says reassuringly as she rubs Raul's shoulder, 'He's much calmer now and is glad to see you made a success of yourself,' she finishes, still rubbing Diablo's shoulder. Diablo's thoughts are running wild as she rubs his shoulder. 'Yeah, I'll believe it when I see it,' he replies sternly. Pilar doesn't say a word and they continue to wait. Raul can't help but glance over as Pilar's skirt is revealing part of her thighs; he is amazed at how attractive she still is at her age. He knows looking at her is wrong, but he cannot help himself. Angela would not be happy. Diablo nervously looks over and places his left hand on her thigh, but to his surprise Pilar leaps up and looks at him disgusted. 'What are you doing Raul?' she interrogates him. Diablo stands up and looks mortified that she is reacting this way. 'WHAT?' he yells, 'YOU'VE BEEN GIVING ME SIGNALS EVER SINCE I GOT HERE!' Diablo protests. Pilar takes a step back and pulls her skirt down. 'I have no idea what you've been thinking Raul, but that was never the case,' Mrs Garcia argues back at him. Diablo now looks angry. 'YOU DRESS UP LOOKING HOT, SMILE AND LOOK AT ME SEDUCTIVELY AND THEN START CARESSING ME THEN SAY THERE WAS NOTHING THERE?' he yells loudly. Pilar now looks very uncomfortable. 'There never was,' she replies firmly. Diablo steps forward and has a menacing look in his eyes. 'Growing up I found you to be very sexy and would beat off over you, so don't say there was nothing,' Raul speaks vilely. Mrs Garcia takes another step back, only for El Diablo to take several steps forward and grope Pilar then tries to unbutton her blouse. 'HOW DARE YOU?' she screams before slapping him. Diablo stops as Mr Dominguez rushes out from his office. 'WHAT'S ALL THE COMMOTION GOING ON OUT HERE?' Juan yells. Raul turns around to look at him. 'I HATED YOU MR DOMINGUEZ, YOU ALWAYS CAME DOWN HARD ON ME. I HATE THIS SCHOOL, I HATE ITS TEACHERS AND I HATED ITS PUPILS. YOU ALL BULLIED ME,' he yells in the face of Principal Juan Dominguez. Pilar buttons her blouse up and is shocked to see this side of Raul while Mr Dominguez goes red in the face at Diablo's comments. 'Raul Diaz, I might have known it was you causing all this trouble,' he says in an evil calm voice. Diablo smiles maniacally. 'YOU WANT TROUBLE? TRY THIS!' Diablo retorts back angrily. With a swift movement he nails Mr Dominguez in the jaw with a hard right punch, causing the Principal to fall to the ground. Pilar quickly rushes over to him, with blood rushing from his mouth and nose. 'JUST GO YOU INSANE BASTARD,' Pilar yells at Diablo. Diablo looks around one last time at his school and spits on the floor. 'I HATE YOU ALL,' Diablo shouts loudly. Before he flees he picks up a seat and throws it through the library window in a fit of rage. 'I'LL SEE YOU ALL IN HELL,' he shouts one more time. The Mexican Devil then looks around insanely at the damage he has caused, making sure to take one last look at his beloved Pilar Rodriguez Garcia before running away as fast as he possibly can.
