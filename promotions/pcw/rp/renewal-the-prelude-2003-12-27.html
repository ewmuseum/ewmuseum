Renewal: The Prelude

27 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27/03 Ring!!!!! Ring!!!!! Ring!!!!! Ring!!!!! Ring!!!!! The almost empty room was silent for a long time up until now. There haven't been any visitors since Shaun Styles dissapeared from the face of the earth after the closing of the X. No phonecalls or anything of the sort. Today's his lucky day, he was going to recieve both. The annoying tone of the old telephone was enough to wake up Styles from his slumber. The lights in the room were turned on to reveal a pretty sorry excuse for a residence. There was a small bed draped with dirty sheets and a towel for a blanket. Next to it stood a small table where the telephone rest. Well, used to rest but now was ringing off the hook. If only he could afford an answering machine... Other than those two items the room was pretty bare except for a mini-fridge sitting on the floor opposite of the bed. A rusty window showed a breath-taking view of a brick wall. Shaun rushed over to the phone stepping on his dirty laundry. Finally the ringing was over as the former PURE Trinity champion held the phone to his ear and sat on the creaking bed. 'Hello?' Shaun Styles voice didn't seem much like it's old self. It grew a cold, raspy tone, the kind little children fear a ghost of having. 'Shaun! It's me, Jade!' The other line boomed with excitement as a faint smile grew on Shaun's face. He laid flat on his back, glad to hear the voice of his old friend, Jade Taylor. 'It's been a while since we talked.' Styles didn't show it but he was more than happy to hear from anyone, especially someone who's been with him through everything that's happened. 'Yeah, it has...But listen, I was able to get you a job.' The voice grew to such a pitch a bystander could've easily heard it. 'Really? This better not be the same as that last deal you got me. What fed was that? ThReat? I never even got to make my debut there, they closed right after I joined.' Shaun shows a lot of dissapointment from his last statement. He's doing well to hide his true emotions, eagerness to see where he will go next. 'You have nothing to worry about when I tell you where you're off to...P...C...W.' There was no reponse for a little while. 'You're shitting me.' Shaun Styles is at last able to find the words as they escape his body. 'I shit you not, my friend. I'll be right over to discuss the details.' 'It's a date.' A smile, going from one ear to another, found it's way to Styles' face. He was starting to look like his old self again...
