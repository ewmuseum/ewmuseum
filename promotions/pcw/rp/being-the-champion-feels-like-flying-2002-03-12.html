Being the Champion Feels Like Flying

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 March 2002 Flight A127 from Anaheim, California. That’s what the camera is currently on. I just figured you might want to know that. Did you also know that Sydney is not the capital of Australia? No, it’s not Melbourne either. I’m getting off the point again, aren’t I? Well I guess I had better get back on it then. Natalia and Miyagawa, having checked out from the hospital where Natalia was having her regular medical checkup, have made their way to the Airport. Oh well, I’m not having a particularly good day. I was fired from the National Lottery of Zambia you know. Someone said I wasn’t African enough. I said that I was a monotonous voice that had no cultural dialect. They said something in African that I didn’t stand, then Michael Caine arrived and told them to “Stop throwing those bloody spears, at me” and then I was fired. Life sucks. So in other words, I’m having a bit of a bad day. Luckily though, for Natalia and Miyagawa, life seems to be on the up. Miyagawa was warned about nausea at altitude following the concussion at the hands of Chris Sloboda, but no effects seem to be forthcoming. Maybe it’s due to the fact that he’s completely rat-arsed at the free champagne in first class about this British Airlines 747. So let’s go right now, down to the cabin. Miyagawa sits there, next to his dear wife, who is currently asleep in the reclining chair next to him. Miyagawa meanwhile is drinking yet another glass of champagne, and his eyes already seem quite, quite glazed over. He’s wearing his a black t-shirt and jeans with one of those crappy airline blankets thrown over his legs. Miyagawa looks over Natalia happily and then looks at the guy across the aisle from him (Miyagawa is in the aisle seat to the right side of the plane, while Natalia is in the window seat). He looks at the balding business man sitting to the side off him across the half meter gap and suddenly. Miyagawa: SHUT UP! MY WIFE IS SLEEPING! Natalia sits bolt upright and the business man looks pretty damned startled. Natalia looks at Miyagawa and looks... not exactly furious, but pretty annoyed. She leans over in front of him so she can speak to the guy across the way. Natalia: I’m sorry, but my husband has suffered a recent head trauma and they said alcohol wasn’t good for him... obviously he must have chosen to forget that small fact. Miyagawa: I’M NOT DRUNK! Natalia: So why exactly are you shouting then? Miyagawa: I’M WORLD CHAMPION! I CAN SHOUT IF I LIKE! IN FACT I THINK I’M THE WORLD CHAMPION OF SHOUTING! Natalia: Move your legs, I’m going to go for a walk. Take some painkillers and try to get some sleep. No, don’t... you’re not supposed to take painkillers and alcohol are you? She looks at the shouting wrestler on his way to kick Boda’s ass once again. Natalia: Screw it, take them anyway. Worst case scenario, you’ll throw up, and at least then you’ll appreciate what happens when I get morning sickness. Miyagawa looks at her and grins drunkenly while getting the pills from his trouser pocket and taking some, washing them down with the champagne while Natalia steps around his legs and gets into the corridor. She walks down the corridor slowly, just basically trying to stretch out her legs a little after sitting so far through the two hours this flight has lasted so far. She walks along through first class until she gets to the toilets, when suddenly she feels a tug on the back of her blouse which she is wearing over the top of a pair of jeans. She turns around and a six year old girl is sitting there in a huge first class seat. Well actually, it’s the same size as Natalia’s and Miyagawa’s, just with the girl being much smaller it just looks huge. The little girl wears what could be best described as a typical English summer dress for a schoolgirl. Natalia crouches down so that she can speak to the little girl, who’s hair is done up in a pair of pigtails. The girl’s mother is sitting next to her, but seems to be asleep. The father is no where to be seen, but there is an empty seat behind Natalia, so she presumes that is where he was sitting before he got up. Natalia: And what can I do for you, little miss? Young Girl: I’m going to live in Oklahoma. Is it big? Natalia: I don’t know, I’ve never been there before. My husband has though, he said he went there last year as part of a tour. Young Girl: A tour? Is he a clown in the circus then? Natalia: Sometimes I think he is, but no. He’s a professional wrestler. Young Girl: What does that mean then? Natalia: It’s a different form of entertainment, but with the same principles. The young girl nods as if she understands, but probably doesn’t understand the long words. The girl does notice the accent in Natalia’s voice however. Young Girl: Where are you from, you don’t sound American? Are you a Muslim? Natalia: That’s because I come from a small town just outside of Moscow, in Russia. Deep in the heart of Russia, you could say. And no, I’m not a Muslim. Young Girl: I was born in Hong Kong. Daddy finally sold the business and now we’ve moved back to America. I’ve never been in Oklahoma before, so that’s why I was asking you see... Mummy just says nice things about it, but she says that about everything. Natalia: You’re a very lucky girl, I never knew my mother that much, and my father was always a little abrupt. I still phone him from time to time, but I haven’t seen him since October. I sometimes miss him, but not often. You’ll learn as you grow up that you’ll adapt to circumstances at a surprising rate. Six months ago I couldn’t speak English. I guess I’m just a quick study, because now I can speak nine languages. Young Girl: Nine? Natalia: Da, nine. Russian, Latin, English, German, Spanish, Mandarin, Japanese... hell, my husband is half Japanese and he can’t speak more than three words of the language... and a couple others. Young Girl: Wow... well I only speak Mandarin and English. I was learning Japanese at school, but I guess now I have to change schools too. So where do you live now? Natalia: Well mainly I live in California. It’s nice, my husband and I live with a couple of friends in a small renovated gym out there. Occasionally we even get paying customers, but not more than twice a month. But we’re always flying out... Airline companies must love us, we fly almost everywhere these days. It’s only when it’s within three or four states that we bother to drive. Young Girl: So you lived in Moscow, and now you live in Anaheim? Why? Natalia: I met my husband when he was in Moscow. He was rather down, and was nearly an alcoholic... I helped turn that around. It was something similar to love at first site. So you just believe in those fairy stories, because they do come true. It was almost like he rode up on a white horse and rescued me from a castle... although he actually fell out of the sky on a parachute into the field next to our house before passing out from the cold. Natalia nearly looses balance after crouching for so long and leans forward to grab the edge of the arm rest to keep herself steady. Young Girls: Sounds... erm... Natalia: Not exactly the fairy tale is it? But it’s closer to a real life one then you might expect. Don’t worry about moving, because life has a habit of grabbing you by the arm and dragging you along when you least expect it. Just we have to move about a lot because of the continual wrestling tour. It’s a good job he gets well paid otherwise we’d have to drive everywhere. It’s one of the perks of being World Champion I guess. Young Girl: Oh, I know wrestling. People like Jeremy Howard and Hank and so on. JJ is fantastic, I miss him... I sent him love letters, and he replied as well. He said next time he was in town he’d come and see me, but I guess I had better send him my new address then. Natalia: Hey, a little PCW fan eh? You might know my husband, Alexander Miyagawa. Young Girl: Is he ok? Boda really beat him up badly. I didn’t think he was going to get up at all. Natalia: He’s surprisingly resilient, either that or just plain stubborn. I think it’s stubborn more than anything else really. But he claims to be fine, he just banged his head a little bit and has a few stitches. He’s insisted on giving Boda a rematch just to prove who the better man is on this weeks Turmoil. Then he’s got a non title match against Chris Chase on Fusion. Young Girl: I’m sure he’ll win. As long as no one as big as Ivan hits him anyway. Is OSW as good as PCW anyway? Natalia: Haven’t got a clue. I’ve been told that they’re pretty damned good, but I’ve never watched a show. And no matter what Miya says, I know he hasn’t either. So pretty much he’s going to participate in a match where he knows nothing about all but one of the opponents. He said it was a good thing, because it means he can hurt anyone he wants without caring. Young Girl: Isn’t that a little bit stupid? You’ve got to know the compaction if you want to beat them. Natalia: Me and you think alike, you know? The Young Girl smiles at Natalia broadly, showing that she is in fact starting to loose her milk teeth. Natalia reaches forward and shakes the young girl’s hand, before realising that she never found out the girl’s name as she stands up after crouching for so long. Natalia: So what’s your name? Young Girl: Bethany. Natalia grins at her knowingly, not even mentioning the possible joke. Natalia: It was nice meeting you Bethany. I’ll get Miya to sign something for you and I’ll bring it along later. Bethany: Thanks! And with that, Natalia continued on her little walk about the 747 aircraft, even seeing the horror that is known as Economy class on long haul flights. She did manage to avoid the even bigger horror, that is known as... trying to have a crap during air turbulence. Lucky for some. And with that, this promo is done.
