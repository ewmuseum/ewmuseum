A trip to the mental asylum...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens outside the mental institution where El Diablo is currently being held. The white building is surrounded by lush plants and a large green lawn that emenates a sense of serenity and tranquility. A taxi pulls up outside the building and out steps Flaymin Aymin. He wipes the stench and the dirt of the taxi from his shiny yellow suit and walks into the building. Inside, a half dozen mentally challenged individuals wander around- talking to themselves and screaming abruptly for no reason in particular. Flaymin walks over to the reception where a middle-aged woman in a red outfit sits behind a desk] Receptionist: Can i help you sir? Flaymin: Yes, i'm looking for a.... 'friend'. Receptionist: Name? Flaymin: Flaymin Aymin. [The receptionist taps Flaymin's name into her database but fails to uncover any results] Receptionist: I'm sorry but there is no one of that name currently residing here. [Flaymin looks at the receptionist in confusion] Flaymin: What name? Receptionist: Flaymin Aymin. Flaymin: That's me! Receptionist: I didn't ask for YOUR name! Flaymin: Lady, you said 'name' and i assumed you wanted MY name. Receptionist: Why would i want your name? Flaymin: How the hell am i supposed to know. Receptionist: I'm beginning to think that you might feel right at home in a place like this.... Flaymin: And i'm beginning to think that the last time you had a shower was 1984. [The bemused receptionist stares at Flaymin] Receptionist: So now you're insulting my appearance? Typical male bastard. [Flaymin runs his hands through his hair as he attempts to make sense of what is currently transpiring] Flaymin: LOOK, all i want to know is what room my 'friend' is staying in. That's it! I don't want to know why you're slow.... I don't want to know why you look like the hobo that collects cans down the street... I don't want to know what breed of cat you skinned and ate for breakfast... All i want to know is where EL DIABLO is! [The receptionist gazes at Flaymin with disgust] Receptionist: If you seriously expect me to help you after you just cowardly insulted me- than you've got another thing coming. Flaymin: FINE! I'll find him myself. [Flaymin turns away from the receptionist and heads to the nearest door. However when he opens it, a flashing red light above the door begins to flash and a wailing siren fills the entire room for about 5 seconds. Once the flashing light and the siren have ceased, Flaymin looks at the door in bewilderment. In the corner of his eye he notices the receptionist laughing hysterically] Receptionist: I'm sorry sir but you're going to need an access card to get into there. Flaymin: Aaarrgghhh!! [Flaymin bangs his fist against the wall and stares furiously at the unhelpful receptionist. Suddenly a large man, wearing a white uniform approaches Flaymin] Man: Can i help you sir? Flaymin: Do you work here? Man: Yes, i do. I take care of the patients. Flaymin: Good. Because that bitch of a receptionist you have over there deserves to be fired on the spot. Man: What receptionist? Flaymin: The one over there. [Flaymin points at the receptionist. The man shakes his head and smiles] Man: That's no receptionist. That's Dorris- she's been locked up here for three years! Flaymin: What? Well then why does she have her own desk? Man: You mean that crappy piece of IKEA furniture? That stuff is scattered all over this place. Flaymin: Yeah? And then how do you explain that computer she was using to access the database. Man: Oh that... Why don't i show you. [The man takes Flaymin over to the desk where Dorris is sitting. He picks up the so-called computer and hands it to Flaymin. Flaymin looks at it only to realise that it is nothing more thana cardboard cereal box] Man: I don't know how you can confuse a ceral box with a computer and mistake a deranged lunatic for a receptionist.... Maybe i can show you a pamphlet and we can get you to fill out an application form. [Flaymin looks at the Application form for the Mental Institution] Flaymin: Is this a competition? [The man rolls his eyes and then speaks in a soothing voice] Man: Yeeeeess..... it is a competition! You win a 3 month stay in room 203. Flaymin: Where's that? Man: Upstairs and to your left. Flaymin: And i don't have to pay any rent, right? Man: Nope. Flaymin: Nor are there any costs for food? Man: Nope. Flaymin: Cool! I could use another free apartment! [The man mumbles underneath his breath as the scene fades to black] Man: 'Apartment'..... yeeeeesss....
