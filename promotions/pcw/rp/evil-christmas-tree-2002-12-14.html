Evil Christmas Tree

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 14, 2002 [Have you ever had your body stretched so bogusly that when you woke up the next morning you moved like a robot? Well, for the most Tubular dude in PCW, he is experiencing this pain first hand. After what happened on Turmoil it was safe to say that Tommy was lucky just to get out of the match with what he did. That TOTALLY bogus Chet King had to heinously cheat his way to a victory. He used the ropes, dudes and dudettes! He broke the rules and he should be punished for his bogus actions. Tommy's body was stretched like a gumband and he could already feel his muscles torn at that moment. It didn't help that the ever so heinous End came out and nailed Tommy with his finisher. How can someone that heinous, that bogus, nail a compadre as he's just recovered from a most grueling battle? Only The End would do something as heinous as that. So, Tommy is forced to walk around just as stiff as his surfboard, benind over as if he aged fifty years, watching how he twists his body. His workout sessions have suffered greatly from the large amount of soreness that rips through his bodacious body. Tommy is upset. No, he is ANGRY at what happened on Turmoil. The cheating, the bogus compadres taking advantage of the situation. Harry even got nailed by Chet King, and Harry is recovering from a broken back. A most heinous injury and that bogus Chet King had to try and hurt Harry further. Cheater, savage, and TOTALLY BOGUS! He'll pay come Sunday when he'll get another shot at him in a bodacious tag team affair. Rush Mason will be Tommy's partner...unfortunately. The dude who Tommy defeated easily only a few weeks back, who now holds the gnarly Continental Title, and who is NOW Tommy's partner on Sunday. If Rush can't cut it against the radical wrestling of Tommy, how's he going to cut it with Chet King and The H.I.T.? He won't. So, it looks like our Tubular hero is going to have to take matters into his own hands, and single handedly win that tag team match on Adrenaline] ['Heavy Metal' Harry has been in far out spirits lately. Christmas is on it's way and he's going to be getting the most bitchin' gift a guy could ask for....Sandy Wong. Once Tommy takes out The End at Decemation and wins the Continental Title, Sandy Wong will be all for Harry. On the soft mattress with the Twisted Sister sheets and comforter. She'll even wear this far out letaher outfit that Harry bought for her, all red and radiating of sexiness. She'll be equipped with whips, chains, and scented oils. Harry will prefer Peppermint Slime the most because it has such a history with him. Having that beautiful Asian woman rubbing him, touching him, and pleasing every far out bone in his body. He doesn't want anything else for Christmas, because nothing else interests him in the least. Just her and the beauty she brings with her. A far out Christmas for a far out dude] [Bob's Christmas Tree emporium has served the Los Angeles Community for so many years. It never snows, and it never seems like winter in the City Of Angels. But Bob somehow manages a way to give that Christmas feel to his fellow Los Angelesians by way of his specialty Christmas trees. It's been a most gnarly legend that Bob actually grows them in his backyard, right next to the field of marijuana he has. Harry claimed he saw it once, and smoked bong hits with Bob. It doesn't matter what else he grows, because he has TOTALLY BODACIOUS Christmas trees and the former Cowabunga Connection needs one. Tommy wears a grey sweatshirt that reads 'Tubular Surf', a pair of khaki cargo pants, and black Vans tennis shoes. It's a bit chilly these days but Tommy can handle it...at least for the day. Harry is decked out in his usual wardrobe of a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. His eyes as bloodshot as ever as he touches the pine trees as if they were completely foreign to him. He lightly touches one of the pine needles and takes his hand back quickly like a frightened animal. He peers at the tree closely as if it had been a first time at seeing a pine needle. Tommy looks over at his younger brother with confusion and distaste] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, what in the name of evergreen trees are you doing? 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I think it's alive, man! [Tommy shakes his head and browses through more of the trees. He needs the perfect size for the living room. Nothing too large and nothing too small. Just get that right amount of size and perfection that the Tubular One desires] TTT: Dude, what do you think about this one? [Tommy stands next to a nice tree that is just a few inches taller than Tommy and pretty fat around the bottom. Harry looks over at it and begins studying the pine needles as if he were some sort of needle scientist. He looks like one of those far out monkeys who pick the bugs out of a fellow monkey as he looks up at Tommy every once in a while with such an interested look in his stoned eyes] TTT: It's just a tree, compadre. I just need your opinion on whether or not this will fit in our righteous living room. [Harry touches the needles and all of them immediately fall off the tree leaving it bare and dead. Both men look at each other in shock and quickly scurry away from the tree] TTT: We didn't do that.... Harry: Yeah, man. [The small field isn't too crowded on this Saturday morning. It seems most people have already grabbed up their trees, because of that bogus holiday Thanksgiving falling so late in the month. That damn holiday screws everything up, dudes and dudettes. It's just one of those transition holidays to make you get pumped for the most radical of them all, Christmas. Which makes Tommy think of The End, the bogus wish making dude who HUMILIATED Tommy on Turmoil. How he just denied Tommy his gnarly wishes and treated him like he didn't matter. Nobody has ever done that to the Tubular One. Nobody just walks away from him without giving him what he wants. The End did just that only a week earlier. Now he's thrown himself to INSULT Tommy, call him things that he is TRULY not. HE'S NOT GAY!! NOT GAY!! Dudes and dudettes need to know this vitally piece of important information. The End was attempting the make Tommy out to be bogus, to be gay. The End is just insecure and needs to push what he is onto the most bodacious and gnarly dude...this being Tommy. It just really bugs him!!] TTT: BOGUS! [Tommy pushes down a tree as it snaps in half. Tommy and Harry look at each other with the same shocked look as before and then they scurry away from the area. They pass a couple happy families celebrating their purchase of the tree. The children's little faces so lit up that they are going to get to decorate the tree on this chilly California day. They are going to countdown to Christmas, and when they get bodacious presents. Tommy feels the rage building up as HIS Christmas wishes weren't granted. That Continental Title should have been his and The End has to spout bogus things about morals and doing it on your own!! WHAT KIND OF HEINOUS CRAP IS THAT?! It's just babble! Nothing but pointless babble] TTT: I am TOTALLY going to kill The End after what happened on Tuesday, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: That Boca dude slapped me on the butt!! Nobody slaps me on the butt except TOTALLY, TOTALLY bodacious babes! I accept nothing less than that. The End made me look like a most bogus dude in front of all of those fans, MY FANS!! The fans that paid their money to see the Tubular One perform! He insulted me and they laughed at me, AND NOW MY FANS THINK I'M A HEINOUS JOKE!! Harry: Your not a joke, man. TTT: Thanks, Har. Harry: Your just gay, man... [Harry giggles as Tommy stops walking and stares at his brother with angry eyes] TTT: DUDE!! For the last time, I AM NOT GAY! That is so heinous of you to say that. [Harry laughs to himself, rubbing the tears from his bloodshot eyes] Harry: I know, man! I know. I couldn't help myself. [Tommy rolls his eyes and keeps moving along to find that PERFECT tree. It's just so tough to find it with all of the good ones picked from already. It seems that Bob has a most bogus crop of them this year, maybe Tommy could help him with that. He spies one that is a little bit shorter than him, but it's fat all around like that bogus talk show lady Rosie O'Donnell. This could be a most bodacious buy] TTT: Harry, look at this one!! It's gnarly! [Harry looks at the pine needles, touches it lightly, and jumps back waiting for the needles to fall off, but they don't. He smiles and nods to Tommy, that this is the one, that this is the far out tree that will grace the Tubular living room] TTT: All right, grab it and we're going to get out of here. [Tommy limps through other trees, keeping his back stiff. The Totally Tubular robot sturggles on as Harry investigates exactly how he's going to be able to pick up this tree. At the base, or just hug around it like he would the far out Sandy Wong? Decisions, man...they freakin' suck] Harry: Ok, man... [Harry hugs around the tree and the pine needles begin stabbing him in his face] Harry: Ouch, man. [He tries going into it a little higher, with his head cocked backward. The needles end up stabbing into his crotch] Harry: OUCH, MAN! [Harry then breathes heavily as if he just carried in an entire furniture set, and looks at the tree like his worst enemy, like Tommy would look at The End. That's when Tommy stolls back through the piles of trees like a robotic penguin] TTT: Dude, what's taking so long? Harry: The damn tree keeps stabbing me, man. [Tommy rolls his eyes and looks up to the sky as if asking the Lord Almighty for some mercy. Maybe just give his brother a most bodacious clue about life] TTT: Here, I'll carry it... [Tommy reaches in and gets stabbed in the face, just like Harry did] TTT: ARGH...bogus.... [He then lets out a frustrated sigh, examines the Rosie O'Donnell-like tree and moves back in. He tilts his head back, just like Harry did and he hugs around, getting pine needles to the Tubular crotch] TTT: HEINOUS!! Totally....heinous....ouch..... [Harry nods and points at the evil tree] Harry: I told you, man. [Tommy looks at the tree and suddenly he notices a most bogus transformation. It begins to take a form of The End!! That's bogus...REALLY bogus! The stupid mask, those HEINOUS blind eyes looking all blank and stuff! He's probably laughing underneath that bogus mask! He's taunting the Tubular One, humliating him in front of all the other trees. The trees are LAUGHING AT TOMMY! HE'S NOT GAY!! He's not some bogus dude that can be laughed at, he's 'Totally Tubular' Tommy the best wrestler PCW has to offer! STOP LAUGHING AT ME, COMPADRES!!!] TTT: DIE!!!!! [Tommy attacks the tree and punches it wildly. His right hand slamming into the tree with such vengence that the pine needles begin scratching up his hand and blood is running out. Many fathers and boyfriends run over to pull this madman from the tree, save it's life before it doesn't have a chance to spend Christmas with a nice family. Tommy lashing out like a wild man, pushing the men away looking at them with those wild blue eyes that used to have such happiness in them. 'Calm down', 'Take it easy', and 'It's just a tree' begin to filter through Tommy's brain and he can feel the adrenaline fading away, his imagination going back to normal. The tree lies on the ground torn to shreds, it's not going to be saved. The tree is broken, done for, and will never have another chance. Tommy smiles to himself and lets out a chuckle. This is exactly what is going to be ready for The End at Decemation once Tommy gets his hands on him] Harry: That was totally far out, man. [Tommy waddles past the people with his robot walk and heads toward the exit. People stare at him with fear and with hate. The evil man who ended the Tubular Tykes, who took away such a nice program from mentally challenged children. The man who attacked a Christmas Tree cold heartedly, just like he ended the program. The evil surfer that heads toward his blue and yellow Mustang with a sick smile on his face. Thoughts of heinous things happening to The End rushing through his head...that's when Tommy sees a sign. A white sign with red writing...which Tommy sees as the blood of The End] Harry: Check it out, man....Santa is going to be at Wal-Mart!! [Tommy smiles wickedly as he puts the top up on the convertible. The shadows slowly drift over him as he lets out a wicked laugh....then the roof collapses on his head] TTT: OUCH!!! [Harry laughs] Harry: Now what are the chances of that happening at THAT exact moment, man? HAHA!! [Fade to black]
