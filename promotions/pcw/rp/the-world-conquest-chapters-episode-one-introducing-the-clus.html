The World Conquest Chapters: Episode One - Introducing the Cluster Situation...

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/03/2002 OOC Note: Gah…School and Computer problems have killed my workrate for this week…although that said, I’ve managed to break the 24 RP barrier for this month…which is practically an RP a day… The World Conquest Chapters… RP#5 for March Madness and #1 for Turmoil… Location: St. Louis Roller Rink…St. Louis, Missouri, near a Puppetry store. Time: 13:00. Weather: Not known due to interior location. Episode One: Introduction to the Cluster situation… “*PFFFFFFFFFFT* (Cough) Ahem…(Microphone Wail) AH DAMNIT! WORK! (Cough) Ahem…Welcome to the St Louis Roller Rink, Ladies and Gentlemen…Children of their teenage years. We’d like to give a special welcome to a couple of surprise guests, residing here until the media buzz has subsided…Give it up for ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase and his manager Dustin Downey!” “Well…that’s OUR cover blown. Thanks to these buffoons, we’re going to be hounded by those dogs of the media…on subjects such as that of politicians and Russian Madmen…to Scientology of all things. Why on earth didn’t you pay the guy not to announce our names in public, Dustin?” “Well don’t look at me…I’m just a puppet, and I don’t even have pockets. If you want a Dustin with pockets, check under the table…” “AND YOU CAN JUSTIFY THAT!” “Is copyright infringement the ‘In’ thing nowadays, or have you gone completely nuts? STOP STEALING MY FREAKIN’ CATCHPHRASE DUSTIN! Jeez…give a moron a collection of half the PCW roster in puppet form…Christ…I hope you don’t incur any more costs upon this whole operation… ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ It’s been a while…granted that the last time you saw me was in a useless and downright inane promotion dictating that each and every person should cleanse themselves after going head to head with Xavier Kannon…damn Scientologists. Anyhow, that aside, there is a little matter of a Primetime Championship Wrestling Pay-Per-View in the next twenty-four hours that strictly grabs my attention by the throat…parading itself around as if it somehow managed to emulate yours truly in a precise way. March Madness is deemed as an event that could make or break the federation or a particular person’s career. Fare well…and the rewards are bountiful…for lack of a better example, last year’s winner, Frisco, became PCW World Heavyweight Champion…before eventually fading away to allow other talents to push their way to the top. Does Jeremy Howard ring a bell? However, as much as I am the uber-confident guy, there is one slight problem with the whole affair. It’s a clusterf*ck…and boy do I hate them…from personal experience, of course. My whole Love-Hate affair with the Cluster began long before Chris Chase and Dustin Downey were ever associated as tag partners or former tag partners…in the PCW2 no less. The inferior (When compared to its PCW Counterpart) PCW2 Cruiserweight Title was up for grabs in a Tables, Ladders and Chairs Battle Royal…the winner of which had to climb that lengthy climb up the ladder, if only to either fall off…or take the title in their grasp. And after spending the entire match unconscious outside of the ring, the winner just happened to be a certain Dustin Downey. If I remember rightly, Dustin has been the primary cause of my Cluster woes ever since that day, for example; being eliminated in the Tag Title Battle Royal, managing to eliminate us both in the Extreme Battle Royal…before then eliminating me in a further one or two clusters. Sheesh, you’d think that by now he’d actually be making a hobby out of it… Now though, things are different, if only in the slightest, least obvious sense. Dustin is, by all means, my manager…a friend devoted to my personal whims and desires…assisting me in the finer points in the life of a Professional wrestler. This is despite the fact that I currently do it better than Dustin ever did, which defeats the point in all actuality….:: Dustin Puppet: But Chris…otherwise you wouldn’t be able to mock all your key opponents in one fell swoop…thus negating existence and my chance to get some Chili Cheese Fries. Do you see where I’m going here? Chris Puppet: AND YOU CAN JUSTIFY THAT! ::. ~(Normal Narration)~ The scene opens, rather grandly, upon an immense Roller Rink…or rather within an immense Roller Rink, in the very nucleus of St Louis, Missouri…no less. A clock stationed on a wall reads One o’clock in the afternoon as the camera slowly pans down…revealing the extent of the room. Slowly but surely, increasing increments of body can be seen filtering onto the camera…the fed up and slightly beaten face of Chris Chase can be seen resting upon his angled arm…sighing as he twiddles his thumbs. On the table he is sitting at, there are a number of puppets sprawled across the table…along with another couple of puppets that seem to be in constant motion at the side of the table. Underneath the table is a squashed Dustin Downey, who constantly alters the twang in his voice to suit that of the two puppets being used…the Dustin Downey puppet…and of course, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase puppet. Slamming the table, Chris frowns at the very sight of these…kicking Dustin….:: Dustin: ~(From under the table)~ OWIE! What’d you have to go and do that for? Can’t you see that I’m trying to get you over here? Jeez…some Messiahs don’t know when to quit being so self-conscious and watch out for people trying to help them… ::. Waving the Chase puppet about…Dustin once again proclaims the cry known to many as Chris Chase’s Catchphrase…~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ And a DAMN good catchphrase at that! ~(Normal Narration)~ Ahem…Chris sighs once more, as he is faced by the small representation of himself….:: Chris Puppet: AND YOU CAN JUSTIFY THAT! ::. Chris slams the table again….:: Chris: ~(Frowning)~ Will you stop that now? Please, you think that you’d give me just a little time for thought, time to ponder just how I’m going to tackle this Battle Royal tonight…hmmmm…so, Spectacular entrance…eliminate Kannon…ya-da-ya-da-ya-da… ::. Flicking off the Dustin Downey puppet, Dustin fumbles blindly on the table…searching for yet another puppet to toy with in an amusing, yet highly annoying way, whilst ‘The Messiah’ racks his thoughts, folding his arms behind his head with an arrogant smirk on his face. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ My eyes search the room, as I look for a tonic to my dilemna of the constant niggle with Battle Royals. It’s annoying, you know, when there’s nothing on hand to whisk you away back into the blissful arms of self-confidence. Teenagers whisk by behind me on the rink, skating with so much freakin’ joy that it’s obvious that they’ve finally forgotten all about computers and Professional Wrestling. I wish I could forget the ridiculous and downright stupid act of some William S. Powell and his “buddy” on Friday night…spoiling yet another key New Breed victory over Afwa Kilimanjaro…~(Normal Narration)~ With his hand finally in the puppet that he’d been searching for, Dustin begins to wave it around beside the table…emulating the voice of its donor….:: Xavier Puppet: Well HEEEEELLO! I’m Xavier Kannon and I’m REALLY STUPID! In fact, I’m so freakin’ dumb, I even managed to let Dustin Downey buy shares in the Snapple~! brand. And if that’s not enough…I can’t ever beat Chris Chase… ::. Of course, what’s an Xavier Kannon puppet without the….:: Ellie~! Puppet: Can’t…Beat…Chase! ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ God kill me now. With all this insane rambling by my cohort Dustin Downey going on, there’s basically little time or space for preparations of any sort. And whilst Dustin continues to poke fun at everyone and me via the medium of a puppet show, whilst children of ages between eight and eleven begin to gather around him, I’m approached by some vendor from the cafeteria area of the rink…looking suitably steamed at the great noise that Dustin’s managing to generate….:: Chris Puppet: Oh for MY sake Kannon…won’t you stop babbling about who knows what. You’re never going to win this PCW Cruiserweight Title I tell you…and you’re going down at March Madness…I tell you. After all…you are faced with ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, the singularly most talented and awesome person since the creation of sliced bread…and if that isn’t enough…I’m a better rebellious Religious boy than you! AND YOU CAN JUSTIFY THAT! ::. Why…he’s even managed to break out some of the most moronic combinations of catchphrases and character niches since the time that he and I brought together the likes of Chet King, Ed, Jay Storm, the PCW Announce team, Clyde, Bethany and Bob the Chair to search for a rare variety of Abominable Snowman. Which in any case, turned out to be the Hardcore Isoceles Trapezium in a Yeti suit…after he had a little too much of that Himilayas Brand Whisky….:: Snow Puppet: Cretins…Morons…Imbeciles…I’m SUPERIOR DAMNIT! OH MY GREATNESS…IS THAT APPLE SAUCE!? Bow down for your PCW World Champion…and your DADDY! Clyde Puppet: Bah! ::. ~(Normal Narration)~ Ahem…whilst Dustin continues to rant at great length with the puppet characters…Chris nods and grunts to the whims of the vendor, who stands above the Celestial Cowboy dressed with a suitably funny looking contraption of the hat variety. The vendor is of a skinny build, and looks like High School Student lost in the world of Roller Rinks and Fast Food. With a conceited grin on his face, Chris rises from his seat…whilst Dustin continues to witticise…paying no attention to ‘The Messiah’. With a strut in his step, Chris strolls over to the side of the rink…watching people pass by whilst Dustin can be seen in the background…STILL handling those puppets of his….:: Tyler Nelson Puppet: YOU’VE JUST BEEN CANCELLED! MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAAHAHA…HA…HAHA…HA…*Cough*…HA!…Meh, I really need to work on that laugh… ::. And upon hearing this, Chris slaps himself in the forehead, pulling his hand across his relatively unblemished face with a long aggravated moan…driving his knee into the thin wooden walls that line the rink….:: Bobby Stormbringer Puppet: And Baby that’s no B.S! Dustin Puppet: WHOOOOOOOOOOO~! I AM JESUS! WHOOOOOOOOOO~! ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ March Madness is literally hours away…and this guy decides to wind me up, decides to make me even more exasperated than I already am? You know Cretins…I’d like nothing more than to throw myself in front of a ninety mile an hour train at this precise moment in time…but to save you from the lack of decent entertainment other than Xavier Kannon fouling up every three minutes…I won’t. I mean, heck, it isn’t because I haven’t got the guts, you complete buffoons, it’s because of the implications that it might have on the rest of the known world. With Chris Chase, the world is certainly a more balanced and more entertained place. Balanced? Why…there only has to be so many slaves to work knocking around on this planet, so do I have to be one? Heck no…I’m not prone to the blackmails of Tyler Nelson such as a certain Kid Wonder that I know quite a lot of. And surely…I’m no lackey to Duke Williams either…unlike some certain Legend Inc. fellow that I’ve come to know a lot more of late… ~(Normal Narration)~ Tired with the whole affair with the puppets and his ‘Hey I’m a Moron’ routine, Dustin pulls himself from underneath the table…to the disappointment of about twenty children gathered there. Muttering, he takes and shoves each and every one of those puppets into a polythene bag…before making his way over to Chris with nothing less than a blissfully ignorant expression on his face. Running a hand through his short hair…tosses the bag of puppets into the wall…before standing beside Chris. Chris himself smiles cockily as he watches a bunch of cretins skate around, bumping into each other like a swarm of ninnies. Amongst all this chaos, sits a Roller Skate wearing man of Religious standing…calmly watching as the rest of the world goes by…at this, Chris snorts arrogantly….:: Dustin: Well…that was fun for about ten minutes. Say, Chris…what are you looking at? You know, I don’t quite think that those mothers over there will appreciate you staring up their nubile daughters…look…they’re frowning at me already… ::. With a swift flick of the wrist, Chris smacks Dustin around the back of the head…causing the ignoramous to crash into the side wall of the rink….:: Chris: Need I remind you that you’re just spent ten minutes teaching eight-year-olds how to say the word ‘DAMN’? Dustin: Well of course not…err…you just did, didn’t you Chris? You know, I hate it when you contradict yourself…by doing things such as that… ::. With a confident sigh, Chris nods acknowledgingly…continuing to frown at the man nestled in the very center of the rink with his focused brown eyes….:: Chris: You see that Dustin…I hate THAT! Those goody-two shoes cretins who think that a religious gimmick is something involving the burning of incense and avoiding the odd Cruicifix or two. It never bothered me until now though…the main reason being is because that guy looks exactly like I do… ::. Upon closer inspection, the man…indeed…does happen to look quite a lot like the Chris Chase that we’ve become accustomed to of late. Similar clothes, similar hair style…almost identical cocky smirk. It’s uncanny to say the least, which at least in Chase’s opinion…sucks more proverbial ass than Xavier Kannon for crying out loud. Chris smacks the wall of the rink…continuing to frown with some intensity in his manner. Turning his back from the cross-legged man, Chris glances down at Dustin with a confident grin….:: Dustin: Well, Chris…my Messiahrific friend…how goes the preparations for this Clusterf*ck tonight? Chris: Well…thanks to your insane ramblings from earlier…they’ve been sucked into the foreboding vortex like the camera in that planned promotion to the Mystery Vortex. I tell you Dustin, though, as much as it seems like I haven’t prepared so much…really, these last couple of days rest after that idiot Powell decided to take exception to me have proven that I can bounce back even for the most horrendous of events. I’ve never trusted these cluster events, and never will do…especially if the fact that the last time I had a World Title Match that Boda won it becomes an omen for this one… ::. And with the mention of Chris Sloboda…Dustin frantically goes searching through his bag…before yelling in triumph as he pulls out the Boda Puppet….:: Boda Puppet: Because I suck. I SUCK SUCK SUCK SUCK SUCK and SUCK! And what’s more, Chris Chase rules! Chris Puppet: AND YOU CAN JUSTIFY…THAT! ::. Chris sighs, shaking his head at the whole idiocy of the whole thing….:: Chris: Gah…this world keeps on getting better and better…Not! Please…will someone please Shoot me now…Now… ::. And so…as Dustin continues to fling around puppets of all forms and varieties with moronic aplomb…this episode comes to a close. Chris sighs, looking around him at the chaos that surrounds him…smiling cockily as the scene eventually fades out…and the credits roll…and the band plays…and the ship sinks…and Walden jobs uncontrollably…and Dustin comes up with yet another moronic phrase. Sure…don’t kill me for that, just thank me when you’re sitting there at 2 Am in the morning. And you can Justify…that..::
