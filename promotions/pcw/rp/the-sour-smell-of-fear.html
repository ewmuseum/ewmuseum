The Sour Smell of Fear...

Date: not entered Can you smell that? Its stench is like aroma therapy… soothing to the nostrils and the body. It relaxes, stimulates….invigorates. It’s a smell that’s long lasted in this federation…the god can smell it radiating off these filthy humans. It’s a smell that seeps through the pores of mortals…like sweat from their perspiring bodies. They cannot smell it…for they’re not at that level yet. It’s like the burnt roast in the oven…charred black from overcooking. And though you cannot identify it …deep down inside you know what that tang is … -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Well, doc…what is it?” A man sat in the small, cramped examination rooms, with the mini-bed with the annoying paper sheet. He was dressed in a pair of black slacks and a white undershirt, his pale skin clashing with the darkness of the black slacks. He ran his hands through his brown hair nervously as the doctor, a graying man with thick glasses and a beard, entered the room with a clipboard in hand. “Calm down, mister. I just got in here.” He said with a chuckle. “And we still have a matter of fixing all the paperwork out.” The man’s head shifted a bit, looking around the room as if he were being watched. “Ok, doc…go on with it.” The doctor nodded as he pulled up the rolling stool from the counter in the corner of the room. He took a pen from his coat pocket and prepared to write. “What’s your first and last name?” He hesitated before answering… “Richard Seibxzhen.” Dun-dun- DUUNNNNNNNNN! Yes, Richard Seibzhen…former Neo Void Lackey and all round nice guy. Last time we saw him, Akiyama and Cyclonica had burned down his house, shaved his dog after puking on it, and dressed him up as Duke Williams and got him arrested for drug charges. Now lets see what our favorite friend has in store for us. “Age?” “30, sir.” “Cause for appointment?” his eyes shifted around the room, an embarrassed hue growing upon his face. “Um….I have a….stch…..” The doc cocked his head towards him, his eye brow raised. “What was that?” “A….er….um….smlll….” “I couldn’t hear that. Could you please repeat yourself?” Seibzhen sighed. “I have a bodily odor…and it wont go away.” The doc smiled at him, the wrinkles in his face creasing up like folded leather. “You don’t have to be embarrassed about that, son. That’s why you come to a dermatologist. I’m here to help. Now…how long has this been happening with you?” His eyes wandered to the ceiling, his mind recollecting the answer to the question at hand. “Um…I think it’s been about…two months.” “Two months?? And you’re just coming to me now?” “Well, my fiancé just stopped sleeping with me because the smell’s gotten that bad!” “Yeah, I know what you mean there…” the old doctor looked away, a grimace growing slowly. “Lousy ex-wife.” Richard looked at him concerned. “Uh…doc?” He looked back and nodded. “Sorry, I get distracted sometimes. Where were we?” “My smell?” Richard sounded desperate for help. You could understand why too, if you could smell the stench vaporizing off of him. “Right….now, what do you think is causing this smell?” “I don’t know…that’s why I’m here.” “Oh…right.” The doc scratched his head as he read through his notepad. “Well then, are you stressed?” “Just as stressed as any red blooded America in this time of war on terrorism.” He replied with a smile. The doc gave him a blank state before jotting something down on his pad. “O….kay. Are you maintaining a steady balance?” Seibzhen nodded. “I eat my daily amount of fruits and vegetables a day, along with a half a gallon of spring water.” The doc checked something off on his pad. “Well…at this point all I can say is that you have a glandular problem. We’ll probably have to run a few tests before we can get a conclusive decision. “ He nodded. “Anything to get rid of this smell.” “Good. I’ll get my nurse in here to prep you for the tests.” He got up and walked out of the room, leaving Richard Seibzhen sitting silently upon the examination table. It was an awkward, nervous silence…one that most people take on when they’re dealing with something serious…a silence that Akiyama most hears from his opponents before doing combat. Richard broke out into a hum, slowly at first, then building up into the full blown hum. Then he started muttering the words. “My neck…my back…my neck…my back…” He slid off the table and started dancing to the beat in his head. His rear waggling and his hips jutting in and out. His arms swing wildly in the air as his head rocked back and forth. “RICHARD SEIBZHEN??” “AUUUUGH!!!” he jumped at least two feet in the air before landing of the examination table. The paper sheet crumpled erratically as he frantically scurried around, trying to gain some sort of protection. Then as he looked upon the person who called his name, he let out a scream of terror. Standing there in a full blown nurses outfit…with her hair pinned up behind her….was Cyclonica. “Hiya, Rich!’ she said with s sick look on her face. “Long time no see.” “HOW DID YOU FIND ME???” he howled at her in shock. She waved her hand at him as she closed the door behind her. “Oh…you’re not that hard to track you know. We can smell you…” “Do you know what it is?” he asked her quickly. “Oh yes I do…indeed I do.” She walked closer to him, her black leather stiletto heels clicking along the hard floor. “That smell you’re covered in…is fear.” His eyes widened at the answer he received. Like he couldn’t believe it… “W….w…what?” She nodded slowly, like she actually wanted to help him understand. “Fear…you know, being afraid…SCARED. It leaves a trail for miles…distinct and unique only to you. And that’s…why I’m here.” She reached up for him, causing him to jump at her closeness. She let out a hiss of delight at his reaction. “Yep…you’re not alone you know…there are plenty others out there that fear the god…some even fear me…” Cyclonica stepped back towards the equipment table and looked over the items before her. Richard gazed on in horror as her hands ran along the edge of the counter…she made simple movements eerie. “There are a lot more others….chefs, devils, scientologists…infinite warriors...you wouldn’t believe the hideous smells coming from them. Their fear isn’t as strong as yours is…but you’ve had time to let yours fester. Don’t worry about it though…their fear shall grow to be just as large as yours…then you can start a support group or a softball team or whatever you losers like to do to bond” Richard watched as she turned back towards him. In her hands was a syringe, filled with a clear fluid that secreted out of the long needle. She tapped the peak of it with her nail, testing its durability before smiling sickly at our boy Seibzhen. “How does a nice eye full of rubbing alcohol sound right now?” Richard screamed at the top of his lings as Cyclonica closed in on him. Just then, the doctor came walking in, knocking the syringe from her hands. The vial crashed to the ground as she glared up at him. “What’s going on here??” he asked worried. Cyclonica shrugged. “Cold stethoscope.” “Oh…I get that every day.” He grinned at her sheepishly. Seibzhen shook his head. “She’s lying!! He’s here to kill me and harvest my soul for the VOIDDD!!” Cyclonica and the doc stared at him blankly for a second. Then he laughed as he walked out of the room. “Crazy bird…hahaha.” The door snapped shut…and the Divine Prophetess got back to work. “You tried to tell on me you little child??” she reached into her nurse’s pockets and pulled out a slew of scalpels , wedged between her fingers like throwing knives. “Men filled with fear do desperate things in hopes of refuge from their fear…you will learn as well as everyone else that there is no escape.” Seibzhen jumped off the bed and tried to rush past her. With a flick of her wrists, she set the blades flying through the air. And like a circus trick, the blades hit mark on the edges of Richard’s pants and the sleeves of his tank top. He stood pinned against the wall helpless as Cyclonica pulled a scalpel from her blouse. She pressed the blade to his throat before licking the tip of it lightly. “Yes…I wonder if that stench of fear is on the inside as well as the outside...” “What’s going on here?” Cyclonica looked back as the doctor stood with his hands on his hips angrily staring at them. Richard looked relieved at this savior before him, but his eyes dropped again when he saw the predicament he was in. Cyclonica, however, sighed. “God…” “Don’t you “ god” me!” he snapped back at her. She shook her head. “No…” he nail pointed behind him. “God.”Before he could turn around, a snap slapped through the room. He collapsed in a silent heap of pain as Akiyama stood in the hallway/. Cyclonica nodded to the god before backing out of the room next to him. “We have your scent locked on…and it wont be long that we will come back for what is ours...so says the Divine Prophetess.” Seibzhen cried softly as he realized that the nightmare of Akiyama would be one that he never woke from… …and the stink of fear… well…the fear was as obvious as smell of bad casserole… …Sour…
