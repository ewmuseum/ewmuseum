Reunion Pt 2

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/06/02 So then Shane just turns up out of nowhere?  That's some pretty weird shit man. Ricky Thunder sits on a dark brown leather lounge facing The Supreme Machine who sits on an identical lounge on the other side of a small coffee table.  They are in a large room with polished wooden floor boards and two massive windows that offer breathtaking views of New York City.  The walls are covered in art works, mainly in the surrealist style, and a few photographs. You been smokin' weed before bed or something? Of course not! Ricky Thunder laughs as he takes a long drink of steaming coffee from a black mug that he puts down on the coffee table.  He is dressed in a simple black jumper and a pair of blue jeans.  He stretches his legs out and puts his feet on the coffee table. Hey!  Feet off the furniture!  That coffee table is over a hundred and twenty years old! He quickly puts his feet back down on the polished floor. Jesus, it's only a coffee table!  Anyway, can't you afford to get a new one? The Supreme Machine laughs as he shakes his head in exasperation. You just have no appreciation for the finer things in life Rick.  Your brother didn't either. Man, it's a coffee table.  Get over it. It's not just a coffee table.   This coffee table........ ....is just a coffee table.  Sorry man, I'm not into it. Ricky picks up the mug and takes another drink of coffee.  He watches silently as The Supreme Machine stands up and walks over to one of the huge windows.  He seems strangely troubled as he stands, dressed a white T-shirt and a pair of black track pants, staring out at the late night traffic, thirty two floors below. So....... this dream thing seems to have freaked you out man.  It's not like you. The Supreme Machine remains silent as he continues watching the world go by, far below. Maybe it was a flashback from a past life or something? Very funny. Well, fuck man, lighten up.  It was just a dream.  That's all. The Supreme Machine stays staring out the window with his back to Ricky Thunder. It didn't feel like just a dream.  Maybe it means one of your family is gonna get in contact with you?  That's nothing to get freaked out by. Suddenly Ricky Thunder bursts into a fit of hysterical laughter. What is it? I was just thinking.  Maybe you've got some love child out there looking for you.  You haven't been getting up to anything on the road have you?  I know some of those groupies can be quite..... persuasive. Can't you ever be serious? Hey, I am SuMa!  Some of those women that hang around backstage damn near raped me after a show one night! How could you ever have survived? Oh, you know how it is...... you gotta do what you gotta do. Ricky Thunder laughs loudly as he takes another sip of coffee and then stands and joins The Supreme Machine at the huge window. But seriously, relax man. I can't Rick.  That dream was so vivid. Man, it was a dream.  That's it.  Fuck, you should be more worried about your PCW career after Tuesday night.  What was with that anyway? The Supreme Machine runs a massive hand through his long black hair as he watches a man and woman arguing in an apartment building nearby. Don't worry Rick, that was just a small hiccup.  That's all. I hope so.  I know you can do better than that. Don't worry.  I will. The Supreme Machine watches silently as the man and woman begin exchanging blows.  Ricky Thunder notices them too. WOO HOO!  Hit him!   I'll put ten dollars on the chick kicking the crap out of her husband.  What you reckon? The man soon overpowers the woman and knocks her to the floor.  He looks around and sees the two PCW superstars watching him so he pulls the curtains across the windows. Damn!  I just lost ten bucks! The Supreme Machine walks away from the window and sits back down on the leather lounge.  He picks up a remote control from the coffee table and turns on a huge television that hangs on one of the white walls.  He flicks through the channels until he comes to the World Cup football game between Portugal and the USA. I never knew my family.  Did you know that? Ricky Thunder comes over and sits opposite. That fucking dream again?  Come on man, get over it!   Watch the game.  Who do you think will win? The Supreme Machine looks blankly at the big screen. USA.   I can't get it out of my head.  I guess the fact that I don't remember my own family just makes it all the more disturbing. Ricky looks at The Supreme Machine in surprise. USA?  No way!  I'll bet you another ten bucks on Portugal.  They're gonna flog the US. The CCW Hall of Famer shakes his head as Ricky relaxes back in the lounge. What do you mean you don't remember your family?  I never knew you were an orphan. I was taken from them when I was a baby.  I've never seen or heard from any of them since. Well then maybe it's like I told ya before and one of them is trying to find you. Maybe. They both look up at the television as The United States scores it's first goal. DAMN!!!   Ricky Thunder jumps to his feet. That's it!  I'm out of here before I lose anymore money. He looks angrily at the television before turning and walking across the room towards a wooden door.  The Supreme Machine gets up and follows him. Man, you've gotta learn how to relax.  Have a herbal tea or something, I don't know.  Yeah, perhaps you're right. Hey, how's about we both go out now for a few beers or something?  That'll relax you. No, I'll pass thanks. The Supreme Machine opens the wooden door and the two of them walk into the foyer.  Ricky pushes the button and the elevator arrives almost immediately. OK then, suit yourself.  I'll catch you later then.  Remember man, chill out. Ricky walks in to the elevator and waves as the door slide closed.  The Supreme Machine waves back and walks back into his apartment. He is about to sit back down on the leather lounge when the intercom from the lobby buzzes loudly near the wooden doors.  The Supreme Machine crosses the room and talks into the handset. Hello? There is no answer. Hello? He waits for a few moments before placing the handset down and turning to walk away.  He only goes a few paces when the intercom buzzes again.  He turns back and picks up the handset. Hello?  Who's there?  Ricky? All he can hear is the sound of traffic passing outside his building. Is that you Ricky?  Stop being an asshole! He slams the handset back down but stays near it this time.  Within a few seconds it buzzes again. Look!  Whoever is there just FUCK OFF! The Supreme Machine leaves the handset off the hook and goes back to watching the World Cup soccer.  He watches in silence for a few minutes before there is a knock on the wooden doors that lead to the elevator.  He stands up in surprise. What the hell?  No-one can get up here with out me letting them in! There is another knock as he hurries across the room.  He stops at the door. Who's there?  Ricky, is that you? He listens but there is no answer. Who is it? Still there is no answer.  He waits for a few moments more before opening the wooden doors.  There is nobody there.  The small foyer is empty.  The Supreme Machine turns to go back into his apartment when he notices a blue envelope stuck to his door.  He takes it from the door and opens it as he goes back into his apartment and closes the door behind him. What the hell? He pulls out a piece of blue paper and begins to read it.
