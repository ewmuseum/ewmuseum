Global Conquest

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-10-02 The Messiah was sent from the heavens above to spread the word of the Lord. And now Christianity reigns supreme throughout the religious world. Many people now try the method of ‘spreading the word’. Some are successful, some are not. Though just like the Romans stopped Jesus’ teachings… the Japanese wanted to do the same. It started with me being lonely, travelling all over the country. Sometimes even travelling to other countries, like now. Jamie Celt walks down the dreary, dark streets. People look it him, oriental people I might add. His head looked up into the sky. I loved life back Boston, fighting with the Celtic gang; there was nothing in the world like it. He walked on down the solemn street; thugs roamed the streets looking for prey. They did not touch Jamie though, due to his strong physique. Though I also love wrestling. It is something I doubt I could live without. The pure adrenaline I get from it is intoxicating. Somewhat like gangsterism as a matter of fact. He wore a blue denim jacket and jeans. Beneath his denim jacket was a plain white t-shirt. His hair was spiked up as always, and his blonde tips blew in the cold night air. Then it came to me… why not bring the two together? I called my father, the head of the Celtic gang in Boston, and I told him! I told him my plan to expand the Celtic gang to new areas! For years the likes of the Mafia and Triads were bringing in cavalry from other parts of the country, and world for that matter. It was the biggest plan of my life, if it did not work, I would have disgraced my family. Jamie turned into a long, narrow alleyway and continued on. So, I took twenty members of the Celtic gang, all close friends of mine, around on my PCW travels, we have been to the likes of Iowa and Wisconsin. And yes, the plan did work! We opened shops and the likes there to employ Celtic members. Our plan was working like a dream, though now I set my eyes on even higher things, beyond my imagination… until I heard of Red October, for my plans turned to a Global scale. Jamie then came to a fleet of steps. He quickly walked up them. At the top, connected to a brown, industrial building was a green door. It was locked but Jamie took a small wire from his pocket and inserted into the door. He twisted it for a minute, and then the door sprang open. “Wha…? Who’s there?” A shocked voice cried in the dark, yet large room. His voice echoed into Jamie’s ears. “It’s me.” Jamie quietly called back. “Oh damn, Jay, you should have said something’” The now relieved voice cried. Jamie rolled his eyes and reached into his inside jacket pocket. From it, he pulled a small torch. He pressed the ‘on’ button on the torch and the room sprung to life. Jamie flashed it in the direction of the voice, there stood three other men. Jamie began to walkover towards them. “Hey Leo, Joey, Dan, whattup?” He asked casually. Jamie then turned the torch around and the finally saw a light switch on the other side of the room. He quickly went over a flicked the switch. A single light came of in the centre of the room, though it was a very strong light. He then noticed a few tables and chairs in the room, at the right side. “Okay, let’s get to business.” Jamie said. He turned of the torch and made his way over to the nearest table, the others followed, they all then sat down. “Okay, this building should be okay for the Celtic Tokyo headquarters.” Jamie said looking around it. “Don’t you think we should have just asked the owners to let us look around instead or breaking in?” Dan asked. “What’s the fun in that?” Jamie replied. Jamie then looked at Joey. “How much will it cost?” He asked. Joey looked around the room. “Well, in this central area, it’s a prime location and it’s a big, well-built building… I’d say, $300,000, or so.” Joey responded, whilst still looking around. Jamie thinks for a moment. “Okay, that’s not bad. Dan, get in touch with my father, tell him to send the money.” Jamie said. “No problem Jay.” Dan responded. Jamie got up from his eat and looked out a large window onto the busy streets of Tokyo. People past by the second. “Now all we have to do is build our powering Tokyo, and flew our influence.” Jamie said. Leo got up from his seat. He walked over beside Jamie. “That is not gonna be easy Jamie. There is some high power gangs in this City.” Jamie looks at Leo. “We are the Celtics! We will not be defeated, have we ever lost a gang war?” “Well… no.” Leo responds, hanging his head. “Exactly. Besides, the streets of this place are crawling with the Irish Catholics. And anyway, Tokyo was once heavily swarmed with the Irish Mafia, which is now defunct, we are sure to build a strong-hold here.” Jamie said confidently. Jamie put his hands behind his back and straightened his posture. “Yes, we will conquer Tokyo, it will be our first global conquest, and then… it will just keep getting better.” Jamie smiled at his words. “Well guys, you’ll have to excuse me. I face Teresa Tomas in the Tokyo Dome tomorrow. I have to win, for I will not let myself lose to a schizophrenic woman!” Jamie said determinately. He knew Tomas would be a threat, he knew this well. Though He would not let her defeat him, he wanted the Cruiserweight title, and after losing his chance last Fusion, he would not do so again! Teresa had attacked Jamie on several occasions… why? He did not know, though he did know that he would not stand for it, his vengeance would be at Red October! Meanwhile, on the other side of Tokyo… A young oriental man comes into a luxurious looking room. “ Sir, Sir! We have received word that the Celtics have arrived in Tokyo Sir!” The young man said. In front of a window is another young man, he is well built ad has a rather hansom face. He looks out the window with his hands begins his back and has a straightened posture. He smiles eagerly at the news. “ And so it begins Jamie, and so it begins.”
