Justin...... You're home now....

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-02 To call the last three days in life of Jeremy Howard anything but explosive, would be an understatement. The Krazed Lunatic went through and won a hellacious hell in the cell match for the rights to his brother, to getting sucker punched by his supposed friend three days removed from the previous. Just when he put out one fire, another one arises. With all of his personal problems seemingly solved, just how is Jeremy going to deal with his new problem? Never once did the Krayzie One have to ask Xavier Kannon for help in his battles against Drake Maxwell. Kannon was always willing and ready to assist Jeremy. But, just what was Kannon's motive during all that? With his actions last night on Turmoil, the Krazed Lunatic has to be feeling like he was used. Because, now that he's taken care of Drake Maxwell and won his brother's freedom, Kannon has no more use for the Krayzie One..... Does he? Hopefully, come Fusion, that question will gets its answer. For, the Krayzie One has already stated that he fully plans to confront his former friend about his actions on Turmoil. For Jeremy, this is becoming an all to coincidental occurence in his life. It was only six months ago, he could call Jason Snow a friend. Now, the Krayzie One gets to live with the fact that that friend robbed him of the World title. Only a month ago, Xavier Kannon was his friend. Come Fusion, will it be known that Kannon wants Jeremy's Continental title? If Scientology wants to keep existing, it'd better be the wrong answer. But, before fans get there answer as to just how far this friendship has been split apart, the scene fades into a blue 2001 Pontiac Aztek cruising down the street. In the driver's seat, wearing name brand blue jeans and a plain white t-shirt, is former two-time World Champion and current Continental Champion, Jeremy Howard. To his left, sitting in the passenger's seat, is the man that survived three and a half years of prison life and two months of hell with Drake Maxwell, Justin Howard. Justin: How in the world did you get enough money to just buy an Aztek outright like that? Jeremy's brother seems in disbelief as to how his brother can afford another vehicle after he trashed his black BMW X5 chasing down a Trans Am to get information on Troy before he was found. Jeremy grins. Jeremy: It's a long story that I'll tell you someday. Justin: How long does it take to get to mom's house from here? Jeremy: Ten minutes. Justin: Well, you could start the story..... We do have alot of catching up to do. Jeremy just maintains his grin. It's going to be quite hard telling his brother that he sold out to one of the greediest men in the world, in Tyler Nelson, then stole millions of dollars right out from underneath his nose so that he could finance getting his brother freed from jail back then. What Jeremy did, while understandable and somewhat honorable, still hurt the feelings of some. Even Jeremy's own family didn't know it was all a ploy, until he stated so afterwards. Jeremy: Maybe some other time. Right now, you should just concentrate on seeing mom. For Jeremy's mother, its been all most four years since she's seen her youngest son. Four years. A visit to her son in prison would have been too detrimental to this woman's health. So, for four years, she's had to live with the fact that her baby boy sat in a prison cell, rotting away. Its an unbearable thought, but this woman is only ten minutes away from seeing her son now and she doesn't even know it. Justin: I can't wait to see her. She's going to absolutely flip when she sees me. Jeremy: I'm just happy that I was able to fulfill my promise to her. In the time leading up to his showdown with Drake Maxwell at March Madness, the Krazed Lunatic was a busy man. He fought off evil occultists to get his son back, he beat Drake Maxwell in a hell in the cell match for his brother's freedom and he strengthened his love for Jessica. For the first time in four years, Jeremy Howard has his whole family with him. Safe and sound. Justin: What promise was that? Jeremy takes a deep breathe while he drives. In front of the Krayzie One, sits a street sign. Oakwood Avenue. Jeremy takes a left, turning down the side road. Jeremy: About a year and a half ago, mom fell really sick from the stress of her youngest son being taken away. I paid mom a visit in the hospital and told her that I'd do everything I possibly could to get you back. Justin: And you did. Jeremy knods his head, agreeing with his brother. Jeremy: I'd have had you back sooner, if it weren't for that piece of trash, Drake Maxwell. Justin: I think after that ten foot drop from the cage through those tables will change his mind about ever doing something so stupid again in his life. Jeremy turns his wheel to the right, pulling into an unfamiliar driveway. Its not the normal apartment complex Jeremy's mother has been known to live before. No, this time its a white modular home that serves as her home. Jeremy stops his Aztek, looking to his mother's home. Jeremy: Well, time's a wasting. It's time to really complete this family reunion. This time, its Justin's turn to take a deep breathe. Justin: Man, I've waited for this day for four years..... Lets do this. Jeremy grabs the handle on his door and steps out. Justin does the same. The two brothers turn toward each other, lock eyes, then begin to make there way toward the front door of the white modular. Neither man says a word. The two brothers, Justin being all of seventeen now, can feel his heart racing. A four year seperation from his mother is about to come to an end. After what seems like ages to a tense Justin, the two brothers reach the front door. Jeremy: Ready? Justin shrugs. Justin: As ready as I'll ever be. Jeremy grabs the screen door and pulls it open. Then, the Krazed Lunatic knocks upon the white door a few times. Knock. Knock. Behind the door, Jeremy can hear some sort of noise. Moments later, the Howard family reunion becomes complete when Jeremy's mother, Christine, opens the door to the sight of her youngest son, Justin, standing before her a free man. Justin: Mom. Christine stands at the door, feeling a sort of shock come over her. Christine: Justin? Justin knods his head, thus melting the heart of this thirty nine year old woman. Her youngest son is home. Christine: My baby is home! Jeremy steps to the side, allowing his mother and brother to embrace, as tears stream down her face. No longer does she have to stay awake at night and worry about her baby boy. He's alive and well and in her arms right now. Now, he's not sitting in a prison cell, wondering if he's going to live or die each hour. Now, he's not alone in a dark cell, praying that one day he'll be free from the wrath of this mad man whom he doesn't even know. The only thing these two have to think about is recovering from the last four years. For, Jeremy Howard lived up to his promise to his mother. Justin Howard is a free man. Jeremy: I don't mean to be rude mom, but I have to get heading out for Fusion. Christine: So soon? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jeremy: I have to confront a friend or former friend as to his actions as of late. Christine: Ah, I see. Jeremy: Justin will stay with you until I get back..... I don't think anyone will try a thing, being they know who Justin's brother is. Christine shakes her head, feeling some comfort in what Jeremy said. Jeremy knods to his family then begins to turn away to return to his vehicle. But, before he can make it off the steps, a certain someone has something to say to him. Christine: Jeremy. The Krayzie One turns around as he takes a step down onto the first step. Jeremy: Yeah? Christine moves over to where he stands before hugging tightly like a real mother does when she wants to show her oldest son just how much she appreciates the hell he put himself through just to bring his brother back home to her. Christine: Thank you so much son..... You make your mommy so proud. I love you. The Krazed Lunatic doesn't say a word. Rather, like a real man would, the former two-time World Champion and current Continental Champion returns his mothers hug for the entire world to see. In the background, stands Jeremy's brother, Justin, watching on with a loving smile. The Howard family reunion is complete. Instead of just standing there and watching, Justin decided to join, creating a nice family hug as the scene fades..... to black!
