Coming Apart? Never!

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 14 - 03 I wish they’d just shut up… Dorn thinks with a sigh as he sits slouched down in a whicker chair while his so-called stablemates bicker like children over fallen ice cream. The Astronomer just runs his hand over his face as if to wipe himself clean of the civil war taint and sighs once again, wondering when it’s all going to end. They’ve been arguing for quite some time now about the same thing: The conspiracy against them. They feel, with the feeling mutual with Dorn, that the big wigs of PCW have been booking them to fail from day one, fearing that PCW may succumb to their righteous might. And in a sense, Dorn agrees. ‘YOU KNOW IT’S TRUE, BRUCE!’ The Pharaoh roars at the top of his sand-crusted lungs, his eyes raging with the fury of the desert itself. ‘I can’t wait for the day that they rally the entirety of PCW’s roster against us and assault us like animals in the middle of the ring!’ Waving his arms about as if painting the picture for all who can see, Pharaoh is the calmest martyr right now. Recent events have been plaguing his mind, as has PCW’s bookings of his stablemates and him. ‘Tell me I’m wrong!’ Violence Jack, adorned in his mystical black cloak, merely scowls at the comment and scrunches his nose in disgust. ‘Your mindless raving is disturbing, Titu. Can you not get a hold of yourself? You’re a danger to us all if you don’t.’ ‘That’s EXACTLY what he said to ME…’ Dorn chimes in, staring at Pharaoh from the corner of his eye. Pharaoh gives him a funny, unbelieving look before turning his gaze back to the Baron of Brutality and Continental Champion. ‘Look, Bruce, I know we can overcome any obstacle that the infidels throw at us, but that’s ONLY if we cooperate. We’ve been pseudo-enemies lately and it baffles me to no end.’ Pharaoh’s tone is somewhat pleading, if not desperate. PCW’s Power Stable has been somewhat rebelling lately, even to the point of the members fighting each other physically. Of course, no bad blood spawns from such an event, but if it happens enough, then it very well may have a long-lasting effect on the team. Dorn had to fight Violence Jack last Tuesday and that didn’t make either of them very happy… then Pharaoh accidentally hit Dorn with a chair and Dorn gave him the Zodiac to pay him back. It’s definitely been a crazy time for the stable. Violence Jack waves his hand dismissingly and looks up at the ceiling, ‘I fear not PCW’s attempts to break us. I fear OUR attempts to break us,’ Then he looks back down and stares directly into Titu Xansiv’s eyes, ‘If this team is going to last, we need to get our shit together. With Chtulu’s dark blessing, such a thing can happen. But it must begin immediately. Friday rapidly approaches, and you and Dorn are on a team…’ ‘Let’s hope he decides NOT to smack me in the face with a chair again.’ Dorn spits snidely, scowling in Pharaoh’s direction. The dark Egyptian merely shrugs. ‘And maybe he’ll sane up a little and quit seeing Druids everywhere,’ Pharaoh retort causes Dorn to burst from his chair and make a beeline right for the Egyptian Madman, but Violence Jack catches him in mid-sprint and picks him up onto his shoulder. The Astronomer thrashes about, trying to get loose, but the Continental Champion dumps him right back into his chair and holds his down by his shoulder, looking him straight in the eye. ‘Calm yourself! Have you not been listening? We must put aside these petty differences or else we’ll be destroyed! Do you understand?’ Violence Jack inquires to Dorn’s heavily panting visage, from which he gets a nod. He lets loose of the Astronomer and glares angrily at Pharaoh, who’s standing calmly with his arms folded across his chest. ‘You too, Titu. You have to put aside your anger towards Dorn and work with him on Friday, or you’ll both fail and embarrass us. We’re the ultimate power in PCW right now, holding more cards than anyone. Even Totally Tubular Tommy has nothing that can stand against us. Dante Jackson is a mere fly on the wall, Skylar Green can be disposed of, and the Druid is your problem, Dorn. I know you can handle it.’ The Astronomer nods and strokes his chin, ‘Speaking of… I’ve been thinking about challenging this so-called Druid to a match at Supremacy. What do you think?’ ‘You plan on de-masking him?’ Pharaoh asks with a hint of skepticism in his Egyptian voice. ‘Of course.’ Violence Jack folds his cloaked arms across his chest and nods, ‘Good. One problem taken care of. Shouldn’t be taken with much consideration, as we all have other matters to attend to. Can we count on you in the darkest hour, Dorn?’ At that, Dorn sort of rises from the chair and grips the arm, a look of confusion on his face. He stares at Violence Jack through his shades and cocks an eyebrow at his teammate, trying to figure out what he means. ‘The darkest hour?’ ‘Dark tides ahead, Astronomer. It’s time we become more brazen with our attacks on PCW. Can we count on you to be there?’ Pharaoh asks, smirking somewhat at the thought of the future. Dorn still sits for a moment, as if he’s actually debating on whether or not to join them, but he finally stands up and reaches his hand out. ‘Only if I can count on YOU to be there as well…’ At that, both Pharaoh and Violence Jack reach their hands out as well and form a tri-bond that will defy PCW history. Three zealots; One target. Can PCW withstand the might that is the Triumvirate? For their sake, they had better hope so. [End.]
