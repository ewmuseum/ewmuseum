Harry's Decision - Part 4 - Mom

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15, 2003 [Things have not been peachy keen between the brothers bodacious and their mother. Ever since that whole 'statue incident' things between the two have been terrible. The beautiful mother of Harry and Tommy tried to convince her son that his ways are bogus. Tommy didn't even listen, he just kept checking out the bodacious babes or thought about another way to make some cash. Harry, who was sober, tried to make sense to his mother about Tommy's condition. He didn't want his mother angry at them when things were starting to come together for him. Only now she his disowned Tommy and doesn't even acknowledge to people that he's still alive. It's really a shame that things have come to this, but hopefully she'll have enough goodness in her heart to listen to Harry, her other son. When Harry found outhe was Tommy's brother and that a woman he wanted to have a hot night's passion with was his mother, he was never truly sure if she loved him as much as she did Tommy. Just the way she always treated him when they were together, as if Harry were this mistake getting in the way of what was important, which was Tommy. Even when he was high and in a haze he felt those feelings. Harry remembers those feelings even in his sober state, which tells him that they must truly be strong] ['Heavy Metal' Harry stands in the lobby of 'Gordo Incorporated' and stares at the gold plated elevators. He's not sure if he's making a rational decision going right up and seeing his mother like this. He's not really sure about a lot of things at this point, but he is sure that he is leaving California to pursue a new life. Harry is dressed in a very sharp looking suit and tie. Nice gray blazer, red tie, white dress shirt, and gray trousers that match the blazer perfectly. With his long brown hair pulled into a perfect pony tail, he looks physically ready for this meeting. Unfortunately, his mental side is very unsure of it. He looks over to the revolving doors in the front and notices that Tommy isn't there. Harry stretches backward and looks behind a small plant by the doors and there sits Tommy behind the plant] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Tommy, why the hell are you behind the plant? [Tommy, dressed him a sharp looking suit and tie himself, sort of cowers behind the plant like a frightened child. His bright blue eyes look paranoid as they keep looking up in the corners of the lobby, just by the elevators. Harry glances that way and notices a security camera and he sort of chuckles to himself] Harry: Are you worried about them seeing you on the camera? 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: TOTALLY! Harry: Do you think our mother is hovering over the security just waiting for us to walk in here? [Tommy is silent for a few moments as he plays with one of the leaves on the plant, and then he looks back up at his younger brother with a sadness in his eyes] Tommy: Yes. [Harry sighs and grabs Tommy by his arm and begins dragging him through the lobby. Many secretaries and mail clerks watch the odd looking scene. A five foot, ten inch chubby man dragging a muscular six foot, four inch man through a lobby. Tommy waves at the bodacious babes with embarrassment on his face as Harry's dark brown eyes are fixed on those gold plated elevators] Harry: You just need to work through this foolish pride of yours and tell her that your sorry. It's not really as hard as you think that it is, but I wouldn't blame her if she didn't accept your apology. [The elevator comes as a group of executives gets off in the lobby and the dynamic duo enters. Harry presses the '5' button and the doors slowly come to a close as Tommy nervously stands in the corner looking up at the numbers. It goes from 'M' to '1' and then from '1' to '2'. This is most bogus because the elevator is moving much too fast. Elevators aren't supposed to be like one of those gnarly transporters from Star Trek. Elevators are supposed to squeak really loud and take a long time, in fact, they are supposed to get stuck so you don't have to face your most ANGRY mother. But now it's on '4' and it takes a halting stop at '5'. The doors open to the large open waiting room and both men can feel their stomachs going into their throats] Harry: It has to be done. Tommy: You know. I'm going to stay on the elevator...put on some Axe body spray...and see if I can bag a bodacious executive babe. You go work your rad magic! Harry: You coming with me. Tommy: Harry, I don't need to be with you! I don't WANT to be with you right now. I just want to go back down into the lobby and try to score with some babes! Is that so hard for a man to ask? [Harry rolls his eyes as he remembers the arguement they had the night before. Harry had broken Jerome Bongus and rejected the weed Tommy tried to give him. Tommy was just crushed and looked like a part of him was killed when the 'other' Harry went away. Harry understands what Tommy is going through, sort of losing someone, but he really hasn't. That 'other' Harry is still inside it's just he doesn't need to be out there anymore. Harry tried to explain it to Tommy but he just stared out to the beach with that hypnotic blank stare he has. He is not a deep man by any means, but when he looks out to the waves it's like something inside of his soul has a connection to them. Everything becomes clear to Tommy when he watches the ocean work it's magic, and after riding those waves for so many years it's no wonder he has a cosmic connection with them. It was a battle to get Tommy to even leave the house, but it was a turning point in the war because he's only twenty feet away from the goal] Harry: You can get some babes AFTER we see mom. It probably won't even be that long, so lets just DO THIS! [Harry once again drags Tommy across the marble floor, heading toward the receptionists desk. Marla, their mothers secretary since day one, immediately notices them and hits a buzzer of some sort. Harry stands perfectly erect wearing a very warm smile on his face, hoping that it will help smooth things with Marla. He wishes that he could remember what kind of lady she is like but he doesn't. He does notice the she has a very pretty face, with very little makeup, and by her sparkling mini-skirt and tight blouse she likes to advertise her womanly assests, not share them...right away] Harry: Hi, Marla, can you tell my mother that we're here to speak with her. [Marla would usually give the two men a tongue lashing and enjoy it, but there is something rather odd about the pair. Usually Harry is sort of stumbling around, looking at the lights in the ceiling as Tommy leans on the desk and hits on her. Tommy is hiding behind the large spider plant and Harry is asking her nicely to see his mother. What is wrong? One conclusion that she's come to is that this is a VERY TERRIBLE trick] Marla: You'll see your mother when hell freezes over, stoner boy. Now get out of my face. Harry: Marla, you don't understand! This isn't a trick or anything. I don't know WHAT my mother told you what happened between all of us, but all that Tommy and I know is that we are very sorry for how we acted. Tommy was being an immature brat that day at Tubular Tiki and...well...mom just didn't understand me at the time. I've changed and she didn't even let me tell her. [Marla looks confused as she peers over at Tommy, who slowly peeks his head out from behind the plant] Marla: Why doesn't Rain Man over there admit this? Harry: Because he's scared. Marla: Your telling me that the confident, good looking 'Tubular' Tommy is afraid? [Tommy peeks his head back out and smiles brightly at the couple of compliments that were thrown his way] Tommy: Gnarly. Harry: Yes, he is. Both of us have come her to speak with her, to maybe put all of this behind us. Just getting Tommy here was a very brutal battle in itself, I need some kind of gift for that, Marla. [She looks at her desk, the intercom especially. She then looks back up at the very handsome looking Harry. He HAS changed. And if this were some kind of trick it would have completely been blown by now, because lets face it...neither of them are Einsteins. She presses the intercom and sighs] Marla: Mrs. Gordo, your sons are here to see you. [The is just silence and then the sound of breaking glass is heard coming from behind the large oak doors just behind Marla. Everyone flinches from the sudden sound and then looks at the door with widened eyes] Marla: I guess that means no. Harry: Yeah... [Harry feels his heart drop down, feeling it break into a thousand pieces. His mother has never seen him sober like he is right now. She never got to experience what her 'other' son has to offer to her and now that it's here she won't even give him the time of day. Tommy pats Harry on the back] Tommy: It was worth a shot, dude. [The words really do help Harry as Tommy's quirky voice sounds deep and sincere, which is quite rare these days. Well, not if your a naked lady statue, a bodacious babe, or a pile of money. But, it's a start for him] Harry: Wait... [Harry turns back toward the door and stands right in front of it. He tries to open it but it's locked, which he figured it was. He stares at the finish on the oak door for a few moments, looking at the texture of the wood. He knows she's in there and that she CAN hear what is going on outside. If she doesn't want to see them, then at least she can hear them] Harry: Mom... [The is nothing but silence, which Harry knew he would get] Harry: I know that your angry and I know that your hurting inside right now, but you need to listen to what we have to say. [Harry motions for Tommy to step up to the door. Tommy waves his hands up, objecting to do so. Harry lets out an angry sigh and grabs Tommy by the arm and pulls him in front of the door. Marla sort of giggles at it] Tommy: Dude, you've got a forceful grip. Harry: Say what you need to say, bro. [Tommy sighs in front of the door and goes to open his mouth, something that he's good at, but nothing comes out. He then looks down to look for the words, opens his mouth, but still nothing comes out. His face shows a bit of confusion as to why he can't say what he wants to say...is it that hard? To be sincere that is? Maybe it is. Tommy can feel the warmth in his heart as he does remember the good his mother has done for him over the years...and he doesn't have to search for the words anymore] Tommy: Your the most bodacious lady of all the ladies out there in the world. What I did was totally bogus...heinous...a TOTALLY BUMMER! I always listened to what you had to say and it was usually dead on sight...and I'm ready to listen to you again, mom. I just want you to know that I'm sorry and if you still don't talk to me anymore...that's rad...but at least now you know how I feel about it... [Tommy just looks down at the floor now as Harry puts his hand on Tommy's shoulder. Harry smiles and knows that it came from the heart, another rarity lately in The Tubular One. Now that Tommy has his piece out of the way it's now his turn] Harry: Mom, it's Harry. I bet your standing in there right now wondering if there is something wrong with my voice. I bet your wondering why my vocabulary has gone from a second grader to a high school graduate...well, it's a simple explanation. I stopped smoking weed. I'm completely sober and I have a strong grip on reality. [Harry pauses for a few seconds just trying to keep his composure. There is so much to say and he doesn't want to ramble it together in a total clusterfuck] Harry: I tried to tell you that day at the Tubular Tiki but you just wouldn't let me get any words in. I was never really given a chance. It did hurt me but I'm sure not as much as you were hurt that day. See...the past few weeks I've had this FEELING that maybe you don't really even love me. That I was a 'mistake' between you and my father...that it was just supposed to be one night of pleasure that turned into the miracle of concieving a child. The way you've supported Tommy and have shunned me out so that your 'real' son can go on with his successful life. Just leave me behind him to support him, and be there for him so that maybe YOU don't have to be there. [Harry pauses again knowing that he has to let the words sink in on her. In fact, he needs to let them sink in on HIM. Some of his words he never knew that he really felt, but when he said them it's like it suddenly stirred them from their peaceful sleep] Harry: I love my brother. Before six months ago he was always my best friend in the entire world, and I viewed him as a brother. Knowing that we were related, that the same blood flowed through our blood it just made everything feel so GREAT! It's just like it was meant to be. I WILL support Tommy, especially now. Continental Title defense. World Title Battle Royal. I'm there for him...until afterword.... [Another short pause] Harry: I'm leaving California, mom. While in my sober state I've come to grips with a lot of suppressed feelings deep in my soul, and I'm going to listen to them. I'm moving to New York City right after the March Madness Pay Per View to pursue a career of my own...maybe make you proud, make my father proud. Tommy is already doing that...and I think it's time for that 'other' kid to give it a shot. I just wanted you to know these things...maybe you already knew them, but I've never gotten a chance to tell you. That is until now. Mom..... [He pauses for a few moments knowing that the words are hard to say...but he means them] Harry: ...I love you. [Harry then turns from the door as he puts his arm around Tommy. They walk to the elevator brothers in arms, just happy that they have each other. The elevator door opens and the brothers disappear from view] Marla: That was SO beautiful... [The oak doors open and the beautiful, tall mother of the Cowabunga Connection stands in the doorway with tears pouring down her face. Marla stands up and consoles her boss] Marla: Is there anything that I can get you? Mom: Yes, Marla...get the car because it's time we put somethings to rest. [Fade to black] To Be Continued
