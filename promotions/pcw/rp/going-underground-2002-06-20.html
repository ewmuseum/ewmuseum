Going Underground.

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/06/02 Xavier Kannon can be described as somewhat of a hothead.... In some people this can be used to their advantage, in some it is a hindrance. Hindrance only STARTS to describe how it effected wrestling’s only Scientologist on Tuesday night as Turmoil aired from Washington D.C. After another bitterness fuelled rant at PCW for having to qualify for a Continental Title shot, his current PCW Nemesis and Tormentor Miyagawa stepped out to interrupt him.... In order to cut off Miya, the Gold Patron Meritorious decided to up the stakes on their already standing wager... If Kannon DID NOT win his match that night, Miyagawa would be the Best Man at his wedding, scheduled for a few hours before Gold & Glory... ...Thinking that Miya would then try to cost him the match in order to sabotage the wedding to the woman the former World Champ has affectionately called “Arse end of a Cow faced woman” Xavier debuted his Cradle Piledriver... THE HALLMARK... To try and injure the Japo-American.... And although it did stop Miya from costing him the match after the Stable run-ins that the PCW fans have come to expect, the soon to be Best Man spiked Kannon with this Reverse Death Valley Driver, leaving the Scientologist unconscious as the show went of air... ...But Kannon will see Miyagawa plenty before his wedding, and once after it as at both Fusion AND Turmoil they will be on opposite sides of the ring.... Then in a Cage at the PPV.... The man escaping first winning the Continental Championship that Xavier won over two months ago. “No he IS NOT my Best Man! He’s a Moron.... A Loon.... He’ll probably bring along Pimply Faced Intern Kid or Syko the Goblin or another one of those FREAKS he hangs around with!” Our shot open up on a busy carriage on the New York Underground where we see wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon shouting into his cellphone, combating the poor reception and checking the display to see how many bars of signal he has left. “Hangs...... Around.... Wi-...OOOOOOTH!!” Beside him stands his valet, Eleanor Hall but not for long as her bored pose with hands on hips doesn’t have good balancing properties and as the train takes a corner the prep goes staggering back into a crowd of commuters, not that Kannon notices as he shakes his phone, trying to get a better signal. Xavier : “No I didn’t make him my Be-...Okay, well I KIND OF did.... I lost a bet and now he’s taken on the role!” As his face shows the evident stress the loss of his wager to Miya is causing him we hear the familiar voice of Hollywood icon John Travolta on the other end of the phone, sounding very crackly due to the poor reception. “So now I’m not your Best Man and someone you don’t even like is? He isn’t even a Scientologist!” Behind Kannon we see Eleanor pull herself out of the crowd she fell into and adjust her Denim jacket and fix her long, wavy Brown hair. Kannon is now shouting into his phone hoping that John can here him, attracting strange looks from the fellow passengers. Xavier : “BUT DON’T WORRY, I HAVE PLENTY OF TIME TO TAKE HIM OUT BEFORE NEXT SUNDAY.... IF HE IS STILL WALKING AFTER FRIDAY THEN I HAVE TUESDAY’S MATCH.... WITH A VERY SPECIAL REFEREE!” His face flashes a trademark smug grin in the direction of Ellie~! who will don the referee’s shirt... Or whatever variation of the colours she wants to wear on Tuesday. She looks back to him, grinning. Eleanor : “Yeah, I’m like, SO special! YAY ME!!!” He raises his eyebrows at his naive fiancé’s words then scowls at those passengers that give her strange looks, sending their eyes shooting off in other directions when his Emerald Green eyes lock onto them. Xavier : “So we’ll stick to the plan John, YOU, not that Japo-American Peon will stand at the top of the aisle with me as I marry my gorgeous bride...” Ellie taps a man reading his paper on the shoulder, getting his aggravated attention then pointing to Xavier with giggly pride. Eleanor : “I’m his gorgeous bride you know.... Me!” His moody face turns away and he pulls his paper back up to mask him from the Prep who just sneers at him. Eleanor : “Moooooooooooooddy!” She returns to her man’s side, leaning around his shoulder so the sides of their faces touch and she talks into the phone as Kannon tries to at the same time. Xavier : “Yes, so I’ll se-” Eleanor : “Hey John..... John? John? Hey John... It’s Ellie~!... Hey!” Kannon scowls and pulls the phone away, causing Ellie~! to pout at him to jest him further.... Seeing the Scientologist isn’t in a playful mood she turns to another of the cramped and stressed passengers and twiddles her hair. Eleanor : “Do you like my top?” As she fishes for compliments from strangers, the Scientologist continue his rather broken up conversation, continuing to shout to combat the weak signal. Xavier : “YEAH...... HE’S A JAPO-AMERICAN.... YES, I KNOW HUBBARD HATED JAPS.... YOU’RE RIGHT J.T... NOT MANY KNOW THE PSYCHLARS FROM BATTLEFIELD EARTH ARE MEANT TO MIMIC THE JAPANESE SOLDIERS AND HOW THEY TREATED P.O.W’s...” His rather racist comments attract some glances from those unfortunate to share a carriage with him and Eleanor, he just stares coldly back at them, the majority not wanting to cause a scene and looking away again. Xavier : “ANYWAY, THIS LINE IS ABOUT AS CLEAR AS PIMPLY FACED INTERN KID’S COMPLEXION SO I’M GOING TO HANG UP.... I’LL SEE YOU FOR THE BUCK’S PARTY ON FRIDAY...BYE.” He hangs up and drops the phone back into the pocket of his Black worker-cut jeans before running his fingers through his long, Auburn hair, sliding a tie off his wrist and tying it in a tight pony tail. The Gold Patron Meritorious looks with a sneer at those sharing the tight confines of this train with him.... Single mothers and pushchair, old men resembling tramps, businessmen with their oversized papers and tourists with their huge rucksacks.... He sighs and shakes his head, muttering under his breath. Xavier : “....And there I was thinking being locked in a Cage with a half Japanese moron, a drunken smelly hick and a four eyed wimp was going to be taxing... I’m locked in Society’s speeding dumpster...” The train begins to slow and the bright lights of the platform come into the view as the vehicle comes to a stop before the familiar hissing of the doors is heard followed by the sounds of men and women getting up and moving towards the doors. Xavier : “Some idiots will leave and to keep up the Neanderthal equilibrium more must enter... The cycle of ret-” As he mutters his rather jaded social commentary, a Blond haired backpacker turns to the door and knocks into Kannon’s shoulder with his huge rucksack.. Xavier : “Watch it PEON!“ The man who appears to be of European decent doesn’t realise he just jolted Kannon, the Scientologist just sneers as he sees the man step down onto the platform and head to the exit. Xavier : “I hope you walk in front of a bus and have your unbelieving bones knocked from your troglodyte skin for daring to hit into my Scientological shoulder...” As he continues to observe the scene around him with a disgust filled face, Eleanor joins his side again, smiling and beaming like a child. Eleanor : “This place is.... Like SO cool!!! All these funny people to talk to, like, that woman has 6 kids by SIX different fathers! That’s like one each.... And that man in the Grey suit let me try on his hat.... But then I dropped it and someone stood on it and he got a little angry...” We pan across the carriage to see a frustrated business man in a smart Grey suit trying to reshape his Bowler hat by punching the foot print out from the inside. Eleanor : “So... If John is your Best Man TOO... Does that mean you’re having TWO Best Men seeing as Miya is as well... Although if he’s playing such an important role at the wedding maybe we should start calling him Alexander.... Or Alex.... or Xand-” As she speaks the Scientologist’s eyes look to her in disbelief, wondering how she can possibly want to accept Miya into their Wedding plans... Xavier : “NO!!! I am NOT having TWO Best Men!!! I’m having ONE!!! John Travolta!” As his voice is raised he again attracts the attention of the other passengers who are standing deep into the carriage at this apparently busy time... He widens his eyes and snaps at them, trying to make it out that HE is the normal one. Xavier : “Yes, that’s right, JOHN TRAVOLTA! I bet the only way you PEONS would get to meet him would be camping outside his house!” As they shake their heads and mutter insults at him, he turns back to Eleanor, addressing the situation. Xavier : “And HOW can you possibly want him near you after e called you, I quote... “An Arse End of A Cow Faced Woman“!?!?!?!?” One of the women sitting down below him looks up at his face, light going into her eyes that has reflected off him not something the temperamental Scientologist likes as he sneers and mutters to her. Xavier : “Although the cap does indeed fit, this time, and probably only this time you weren’t wearing it...” She mumbles something that sounds like “Asshole” under her breath as Kannon looks back to his fiancé with pleading eyes. Eleanor : “Yes, but he’s the Best Man, it’s his job to make jokes and say little things like that! Like that weird slammy movey thing he did on you, he was just playing around with you, like Best Men do... It’s, like, their job or something...” Her innocence leaves the Gold Patron Meritorious speechless and he slaps his forehead, hoping that he can program hate for Miya into her vicious side by Tuesday... His head shaking comes to an end as we reach their station... Xavier : “Move...PEON!“ ...And Kannon makes his way to the door, looking back for Ellie~! as the shot begins to fade out. “I might stay on here, this man has a Dog and he’s kinda old so if he dies before his stop he said I can keep it!” The chirpy voice of the future Miss Eleanor Kannon-Hall is heard from the crowd but with the stress of the past days and upcoming weeks out Scientologist isn’t in a joking mood. Xavier : “ELLIE~!.... Don’t make me cancel the Pre-Nup!” “O............K..........” As we get to near Black his scalded valet trundles out and onto the platform, taking the arm of her man as they follow the Way Out signs... “Do you think he’ll bring that little Goblin guy with him? He’s funny... All GNAR!!! and stuff!” The sound of a desperate Scientologist groaning is the last thing we hear above the footsteps fading out.
