Fishing

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/02 We open up to a very beautiful view of a lake. The water is sparkling from the glint of the sun, as the low winds brush harmlessly against the trees. A little white fishing boat, that appears brand new, comes calmly down the lake. Sitting inside is none other than Kramer. He is wearing a silver fishing hat with fish hooks all around it, an ironed white shirt accompanied with a dingy looking olive colored vest, and a pair of baggy silver colored pants to top it all off. Overall his outfit really doesn't go together which only proves that Kramer has a weird sense of style. He leans over the edge of the boat to peer into the water looking for the best spot to find his first victim. Kramer pulls up a black fishing pole and reaches his hand into a bucket of worms. He selects the biggest and juiciest one as he hooks the sucker which is squirming all over trying to get free. Kramer brings the fishing pole back and then launches it into the water. The fishing line goes straight into the water and the action is all over for now as we just sit and wait. Minutes go by and nothing. Kramer who is growing a tad bit impatient, starts to whistle while tapping his fingers on the fishing pole. Out of the blue his eyes light up and he stands up as he starts to reel in the line. Hooked to the...well the hook is a piece of laminated paper. Kramer pulls it off of the fishing hook and reads it aloud. Kramer: You are dangerously close to getting your ass fired! ~PCW~. Man! My inactivity has finally caught up to me. He takes the paper and tosses it into the air and as he does it magically disappears. Kramer pulls the fishing line back far and tosses it back into the sparkling water. He feels the tug yet again and feverishly reels it in. This time he drags from the depths of the water, a black leather boot. Kramer raises his eyebrow. Something from inside catches his eye so he pulls back the tongue of the boot, as far as it can go and pulls out a note reading. 'Property of the Mafia, DO NOT TOUCH!' Totally ignoring this Kramer reaches in and pulls out a little locked box resembling a small treasure chest. Kramer: How am I suppose to open it if there isn't a key? Kramer turns the boot upside down and out comes the brass key which clinks to the boat. Kramer bends over and picks it up and takes a seat as he pushes the key into the lock. He hears a little click and he pulls open the lid. Inside there is an array of sparkly diamonds almost filling the entire box. Not really caring too much about the diamonds Kramer closes the box, puts it back into the shoe and throws it into the water. The camera pans around and we see some Mafia members dressed all in black holding guns pointing at Kramer. But as soon as he throws their property back into the water they put them down. Member: It's alright he put it back. What Kramer doesn't realize is that that was nearly ten million dollars worth in diamonds, but hey we never said he was the sharpest tool in the shed. Feeling disappionted in his findings thus far, Kramer kicks the side of the boat in frustration. Kramer: Are there even any fish in this lake!? Wait I got an idea. Still fully clothed Kramer leaps into the lake and goes under water. The camera shows a shot of him in the water going to the bottom. Finally he reaches it and we see a tiny cork. Kramer pulls it out of the lake and hurries back to the top for a breath of air. He climbs back into the boat soaking wet as the water in the lake starts to drain. It takes nearly 15 minutes before its finished. Kramer looks around to see if there were in fact fish in the lake. Kramer: Dead body.....old car.....another dead body.......Elvis statue.......a FISH! Kramer spots a small fish which is flopping all around slowly dying. Kramer climbs out of the boat and walks over and picks it up. Kramer: It sure is a small fellow though....hmmm.....oh wait. He snaps his fingers as he gets yet another idea. He reaches into his pant pocket and pulls out a little vile of green liquid. He pours a little dash on the fish and it starts to grow! By the time the growth spurt stops, Kramer has to use two hands to carry it. Kramer: Now how am I suppose to get out of this joint? I'm not dragging this boat all that way. Hmmm.... Yet again with another idea! Kramer reaches into his pocket again and this time pulls out a remote control. He points it to the water and presses rewind. As he does this the water starts to fill back the empty banks. He sits in the back of the boat and starts the engine. Like a flash of lightening he bolts through the water going at high speeds. Finally he reaches his destination area. He pulls the boat onto shore and pulls it up a tiny hill. He takes the fish out and puts it in a freezer in the back of his truck. (which is what he is standing by. Its a black Ford) Before doing anything else he hops onto the back of the truck with his feet dangling off the edge as he pulls out his cell phone. He dials some numbers and waits. Kramer: I wonder if I got any messages. Voice: You have one message. Kramer changes over and it switches to the message. Jason: Kramer I just wanted to remind you that you have a match with Rent A Hero. Well actually its not reminding you because I never told you but you do have a match. Oh and did you know that you are walking a thin red line in PCW? They are ready to boot your butt so you better do good! Well thats all. Kramer puts the cell phone up and a smile etches across his face. Kramer: I get the luxury of taking on a superhero!!!! This is totally cool. I always wanted to be a super hero...I remember once in the third grade I wore a Batman costume. I made the mistake of trying to jump off of the roof thinking I could fly, but I fell and broke both of my legs. His smile suddenly fades as his childhood memory fully resourfaces. Kramer: I had to spend eight months and double leg casts!! I guess I wasn't cut out for it. I guess not everyone can be a superhero. A frown comes across his face as he looks down to the ground looking ready to cry. The scene slowly fades.
