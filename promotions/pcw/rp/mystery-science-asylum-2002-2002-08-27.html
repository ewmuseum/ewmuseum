Mystery Science Asylum 2002

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 August 2002 Asylum. The very word conjures up images of a lonely erection of stone and steel, isolated from society. Home to the twisted and a place to shield mainstream people from the rejections of sanity. How ironic that two of the most diseased minds ever to stalk Mother Earth roam freely rather than within the confines of a straitjacket. Yet the cries of the lunatics and mentally disturbed have attracted the free. The prayers and sorrow of one man have finally been answered. Finally, a face will bring him back to the harsh reality of life... The squeaky, rusty gates of the cell slid open with a grating noise that made all present clench their fists and screw their eyes shut. A long hallway stood before the pair known to the wrestling industry as Pranks With Attitude. It seemed almost like a derelict tunnel. Water dripped from the ceiling onto the leather, red trench coat that adorned the shoulders of one of the two. Pat, pat, pat. The dull brown brickwork and darkness forced Gaz Green to remove his almost trademark green sunglasses to see better. Nerezza's circular red lenses remained in place. The behemoth seemed to find solace in the darkness and dank surroundings. The only sound was of Gaz breathing and the dripping of the water onto Nerezza's coat Suddenly, the gate behind them screeched shut again and one by one, a trail of lights burst into life, progressing further and further into the tunnel. As if on cue, a barrage of screams and rattling exploded from all sides. While the sudden cries of insane agony would shake a normal man to his soul, Nerezza merely jumped initially while Gaz seemed to revel in the chaotic din. A smile crept along his mouth as the waves upon waves of strangled squeals and moans of perpetual torment swam through the thick air. Stepping between the men, a podgy black man with a thinning hairline and bushy beard took point and turned his back to the horrors of the cells. His grey uniform was a contrast to the gaudy colours of the two grapplers and the constant wails of the imprisoned banshees still took their toll on his ears. 'He's right at the end, we thought he'd be more comfortable in here!' he yelled, trying to make himself heard above the cacophony of shouting. 'Do not touch the glass, do not approach the glass! You can pass nothing but soft paper, no pencils or pens, no staples or paperclips in his paper! Use the sliding food carrier, no exceptions! If he attempts to pass you anything, do not accept it!!' 'What's so dangerous about him? When he taught me how to wrestle, he was just a film buff with a little fetish for wrestling hardcore style...' Nerezza raised his voice above the wails. 'On the afternoon of July 8, 1981, he complained of chest pains after having a chair driven into it at a house show and was taken to the medical room backstage. When the doctor leaned over him, he did this to him.' The guard handed Nerezza a small photo that made even the vampire lean backwards in fright. 'The doctors managed to re-set his jaw more or less, remove most of the turnbuckle nails from his heart and save one of his eyes. His pulse never got above eighty-five, even when he had delivered twenty-seven stabbing wounds in rapid succession with the other end of the mic.' Nerezza advanced forward before being stopped one more time. 'We put a chair out for you...' Nodding, Nerezza marched past him with ignorance to the yells. Facing Nerezza was a corner - as he turned it, he froze in his tracks. Gaz strutted behind, then also stopped as the scene before them looked like something only a movie director could have created. A winding corridor, lined with yellow, mildew covered walls stretched out and from either side, hands reached through cold bars in desperation. Long fingernails projected from each digit and in some cases, the decoration of dirt and blood mingled. As the vampire marched down the hallway, Gaz slowly shuffled behind. While Nerezza looked straight forward, disgusted with the wretches of humanity and unwilling to make eye contact with any of the rejects, Gaz stared long and hard into the suffering, tortured eyeballs of each prisoner, drinking up the anguish and sorrow that each man groaned with. He looked inside the cells to see the condition of each man who was not exhausting their vocal cords. Inside one, a shadow of a human being was bound in a straitjacket, standing in his head and clapping his feet together over and over. Amused, the Emerald Menace withdrew his attention from the lost soul and turned to his right to see another set of bars uninhabited by desperate claws. Inside this one, a skinny, long haired, old, bearded fellow simply repeated 'I am Merlin... I am Merlin... I am Merlin...' to himself over and over. 'GAZ!' snapped Nerezza, standing at the furthest reaches of the corridor. 'Sight seeing comes later. We have an old friend to greet.' Gaz finally dragged himself from the sideshow of horrors and gradually closed the gap between himself and his partner. As he caught up, the pair needed to turn one final corner. As they did so, the lights went out and once more, they were plunged into darkness momentarily, before a single bulb illuminated only the most immediate of surroundings. Gradually, the horrific tsunami of insanity died down and the rattling ceased... The spotlight revealed a solitary chair, set out as the guard had noted. In front of the chair stood an audience of one. Behind a glass screen stood a man no older than 25 and maybe younger. His hair was a bright red and his eyes were a dull green. He was dressed in a standard blue jumpsuit that was uniform and his number was sewn to the chest. Decorating his cell was a series of charcoal sketches and paintings. Behind him was a bed, messy and unmade. For some reason, he bore a somewhat amused smile on his face, despite only having just realised who his visitor was. 'Is that... Nerezza? Well hello, Nerezza...' he said in a voice that sounded West Virginian in origin. 'It has certainly been a long time, Timoth-' Nerezza's greeting was interrupted by the prisoner, this time in a sharp Canadian accent. 'Ah, ah, ah, nobody uses the real name, not even the guards! It's Big Mack now, BIIIIG MACK!' he spoke through gritted teeth. Nerezza stood up, looking around the cell. Gaz happily plonked himself onto chair and made himself at home. Clearly, the grin on his lips indicated he had a plan. Nerezza continued his greeting with the mysterious inmate. 'Big Mack... it's been a while now since we both last saw each other. I take it you've been keeping well...?' Big Mack didn't answer. Instead, he raised his head and inhaled deeply. Exhaling, he smiled. 'You smell of ... blood... and sometimes you wear Lynx Apollo... but not today.' he deduced. 'Very Good, Doctor Mack...' Nerezza smiled. 'Aw, come off it, everyone saw the time you were in that commercial as Random Sweaty Wrestler B!' Gaz exclaimed. Nerezza tried to ignore the Mystery Science Theatre style commentary of his partner and tried to change the subject. 'Did you draw these?' He said, gesturing at the sketches on the walls. They were scenes from various matches in wrestling history, victorious pinfalls and moments of great high spots that still stun the people who saw them. 'That was the cage scene from 1998...' Mack explained, pointing out the outline of a falling, slightly overweight man as a much larger wrestler threw him from a steel construction to the floor below. 'And this is from back in the eighties... when giants collided.' 'All that detail just from memory?' Nerezza questioned, genuinely in awe of the art. 'Yeah, they only show those clips ten times each week...' Gaz muttered. 'Memory is what I have, Mr. Nerezza, in place of a view.' Mack said, turning his back and marching towards the stone wall. 'I've been in this room for eight months now, and I know they'll never let me wrestle again. What I want is a television where I can see a Suplex, or some Chair Shots... I want to be in an indy federation far away from this Boxing program they force me to watch.' 'BAH! Boxing is rigged and fake, we ALL know that.' The Jester cried out. Both Nerezza and Big Mack nodded in agreement slowly. 'Gaz, a word...' Nerezza jerked his head to one side, indicating for the smartly-dressed smart-ass to follow. As they rounded the corner, Nerezza began to explain. 'That was the man who taught me how to wrestle back when I was just a human. He was a movie maniac but in particular, he liked the workings of Thomas Harris' book adaptations.' '...Hannibal, the Cannibal?' Gaz looked with a raised eyebrow and quickly sidestepped a drop of water that threatened to ruin his classy suit. 'I don't expect him to talk to you, but I have to be able to say we tried. I had to know what happened to him. Maybe I could learn more from him...' Nerezza said with a guilty tone to his normally deep voice. 'You dragged me into an asylum to meet a nut-case who showed you how to wrestle in hopes that he'd teach us new moves!?! I'm not going in there, he'll eat me!' Gaz tried to stifle his frustrated anger. 'I've been hanging around with one psychopath since early May who's got a taste for human flesh, isn't one enough? I'm outta here!' Gaz strode down the corridor, then stopped halfway to peer into more cells. Nerezza sighed and returned with his true intent revealed. Facing the madman once more, he cracked his knuckles and drew himself up to full height. 'So... you think you and I would have a fair match these days? I've gotten better since we last met...' the living nightmare proposed. He slid a small sheet of paper into the sliding drawer and pushed it through. Big Mack picked it up and began to run over it with beady eyes. 'Primetime Championship Wrestling...' he said, almost indifferently. 'Are you questioning my abilities?' 'I'm just seeing if you're still up for it... maybe you and I could test each other out. We're both so much more than we once were...' he spoke in a low voice, trying to tempt the lunatic into whatever it was he had planned. His hopes were shattered by the slamming of the sliding door, making him jump in fright. Mack leaned closer to the glass. 'The last Prison Guard once tried to test me. I ate his spleen with some baked beans and a nice Lemonade.' At that point, Mack let off a horrid, rippling noise from his rear that forced Nerezza to duck as the sound rang out. Prrff-frfp-frfp-frfp-frrfrffp! 'God-DAMN, I can't even breathe and I can smell that!' The vampire crawled along the floor and gradually brought himself to his feet as he came into the same corridor that Gaz was in, still staring at a man inside a cell that would scurry around his cell, trying to avoid a rotating light. He remained in the only shadowy area of the room and constantly ran in circles. 'Gaz, we're leaving.' Nerezza said, patting him on the shoulder to get his attention. 'Hold on, I have a plan...' Gaz announced, extracting something red and flat from his neat jacket pocket. He raised the object to his lips and blew hard into it, inflating it slowly. A few more times inhaling and exhaling heavily and he had filled it to maximum capacity. The Lord Of Laughter examined the rate at which the antisocial ran around, then slipped the whoopee cushion inside. One more revolution was made before the man stepped on it, freezing solid as he looked around to see what was going on. Before he knew it, he was caught in the light beam and started squeaking loudly, causing the other inmates to begin their demented chorus. The guilty party quickly made their way to the exit, leaving Nerezza's former mentor and the deranged man with a fear of light behind. 'I take it you enjoyed that riot of screaming as applause?' Nerezza scolded Gaz. 'Applause? Surely, not. All of that squealing was music to my ears...'
