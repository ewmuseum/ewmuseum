A Goddess of Crunk

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/6/02 The scene opens up with Rush sitting at the kitchen table of his living room in Greenville, North Carolina apartment. The kitchen looks like that of a single man in his 20’s. The counters are cluttered with various food related objects. However, there is a small amount of order. The dishes are washed, and only a few are in the sink. The floor has a new looking sheen to it, as though it was just washed. Rush sits at the table, in a gray wife beater and baby blue University of North Carolina basketball shorts. He’s got a Dunkin Donuts cup, and is taking infrequent sips of it, while going through the paper. Suddenly, a sight that has not been seen in Rush’s apartment in a while can be seen walking out of the bathroom. A dark-haired, white, youngish looking woman walks out of the bathroom in a large PCW t-shirt, Rush’s most likely, which falls down almost to her knees, covering her panty-clad ass, legs, etc. (everything that Anton the Kid has never seen) There is nothing outstanding about her, yet she is definitely beautiful. She sits down at the table across from Rush, which causes our Crunk as Hell God to look up from his paper. Rush: You seem awfully disappointed for someone who was so loud last night. What’s wrong? Girl: You don’t love me. You just wanted me because I was pretty, and you wanted to get me in bed. Rush: No I didn’t. I mean, I do think you’re beautiful, but that wasn’t why I want to spend time with you. Melanie, you’re the first girl I’ve had time for in a long time, and the first one I’ve met that can deal with godly proportions of an immortal such as myself. Melanie, which is the girl’s name and if you didn’t figure that out, well then I’m not sure how you read it in the first place, giggles at this comment. Melanie: Well, that’s good. But I’m telling you, I’m not a fan and I never will be. I’ll come to your matches, but I’m not gonna walk down to the ring with you and all that nonsense. Rush: That’s fine. If you did, you’d probably turn on me like that bitch Amanda did on everyone in PCW. Melanie: Are you calling me a bitch? Rush: No, of course not. I would never- Melanie: Oh I’m so glad that we can share our feelings like that. In that case, when I said I was a virgin, I was really lying. Rush: Who said we were sharing feelings? HEYYY! What the hell do you mean you’re not a virgin? Melanie realizes that there is only one way to get out of this situation, the most famous woman escape technique in the book. She’s going to confuse him like hell. Melanie: So, you’re calling me fat? Rush: What?!?! What’re you talking about? I was asking why you- Melanie: So you love me? Then let’s get married! Rush: Wait, what? When did I say that? I mean not that I don’t like you but- Melanie: You hate me! It’s ok I see it now, I’ll just go. Rush: STOP! You’re confusing the hell out of me. Mission accomplished for Melanie, and hopefully Rush has put the question out of his mind for now. Rush is now up out of his chair. Rush: I do not love you, yet. You are not fat. You’re pretty and smart and funny. Now, do you believe that I like you a lot? Melanie puts on a sad look on her face, thinks for a few seconds, and then replies with a smile. Melanie: Okay, I’m fine now. Thank you, my Crunk as Hell God. I can’t wait to watch you on Sunday, when you finally kick Xavier Kannon’s ass for turning on you as a tag partner. Rush: Well…ummm he didn’t actually turn on me. Melanie: But you wouldn’t stand to be around Eleanor? Rush: Nope, it’s not that either. Melanie: Then why in the hell did you ditch him as a tag partner? I mean, he’s the #3 ranked wrestler from the fan polls, and he’s already had a Cruiserweight title reign. Rush mumbles something, an audible but totally undecipherable mumble that Melanie cannot hear. Melanie: What’d you say Rush? I can’t hear you when you mumble like that. Rush mumbles the exact same way as before, now it is obvious that he wants to drop the subject, but Melanie insists on hearing what Rush has to say. Melanie: Rush, you have to speak up, I’m sorry I couldn’t hear you.Rush sighs and says what he has been saying the whole time, only this time out loud. Rush: Black Attack. Black Attack is why I ditched Kannon. Rush tries to explain but Melanie’s loud racous laughter drowns out any chance of that. Rush rolls his eyes and sighs as Melanie is now on the floor lying down, laughing her brains out. Rush: Haha. Make fun of Rush for being in the jobber stable. Real mature of you, Mel. Thanks for being so appreciative of my hard work. Melanie: Whoo. That was pretty good, Rush. Thanks, you certainly brightened up my day with that little real life humor. Rush: Very funny there Mel. I’d like to see you make decisions that are as tough as that one. People thought it would be a very good idea. Seriously. Melanie: Okay, I’ll make a decision right now. You and I are going over to that couch over there, because I’m still tired. Rush: Why do I have to go over? Did you forget that I am a God? Melanie: Rush? Rush: Yes, goddess of Crunk? Melanie: Stuff it. Rush: Yeah, love you too. The two walk over to the couch, and Rush lies down on his back. Melanie lies down, placing her head on Rush’s broad, strong chest. He kisses her head, as she drifts off into a quiet nap. Rush: Now this is the life. I got a girl again. I get to face Kannon and The End, two new opponents who may teach me a thing or two, but the Crunk as Hell God will end up victorious. Rush begins to slip off into a nap-like state, but suddenly the phone rings, interrupting him. He reluctantly gets up, gently placing Melanie’s head down on the couch, her body warm to the touch. He gets up an answers the phone with an annoyed voice. Rush: Hello? Yes, this is Rush, what do you want? I’m kinda busy. Voice on Other End: Mr.Mason, it’s Jonas Rafter from down at the lab. We spoke a few days ago about your animal creation?Rush: I seem to recall this somewhere in my godly mind. What did you call me for? Jonas: Well, it’s hard to explain, but I’ve got something you might like very much. It’s a very big strive in science today but you should see for yourself. Rush: Okay, well I’ll be down there in about two hours. Right now I’ve got some stuff to do. Jonas: That sounds good. Rush hangs up the phone, ready to get back to cuddling with the sleeping goddess of Crunk. Hey! Even gods need to cuddle sometimes. So, with a new girl and a better outlook on life, Rush lies down hugging his new girlfriend close to him, sleeping off into sleep, getting ready for a trip to see Jonas Rafter. So, now is a perfect time for us to fade to black…
