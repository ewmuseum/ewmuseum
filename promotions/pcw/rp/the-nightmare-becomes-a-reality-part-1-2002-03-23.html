The Nightmare Becomes A Reality? (Part 1)

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.03.02 [Tape Delayed. Taped: Sunday, May, 19th, 2002. The scene opens in Duzza’s Hamptons mansion. A large white building, with three floors, built on a 3 acre plot of land. There are electric gates at the front of the long drive that leads to the mansion. At the mansion, there is a large three car garage, even though Duzza has more than 3 cars. Two of the doors are shut, but one is open, exposing a large gold Lexus. Outside on of the closed garage doors, there is a parked black BMW series 7. The mansion is surrounded by large pine tree’s, to the far left, there is large basketball, as Duzza, just like his idol R. Kelly cannot sleep at night without playing basketball during the day. The camera moves into Duzza’s mansion and into the master bedroom. With a king-sized, with red, silk sheets on it, a large TV in the corner, a bathroom en suite, a walk in closet for his wife’s shoes and a balcony, looking over the ocean, Duzza’s bedroom is a far cry from the 1 bedroom apartment he grew up in, back in Harlem. Duzza is laying in bed. The night before, there was a big “Oh my God, Duzz got resigned to PCW” party in a club in Harlem. Of course, Duzz was there, living it up, sipping on the Crystal till the early hours of the morning. Duzza, is laying on the bed, with the sheets all crumpled around him. He is wearing only a pair of baggy white boxer shorts and nothing else, exposing his muscular body and the cross tattoo on his right shoulder. He has his hair set back in long cornrows, that run past his head and down his neck. After laying motionless for a few moments, the alarm clock which is situated on a small table next to him, rings loudly. It is easy to tell that Duzza is defiantly now a morning man. He quickly, jumps up and looks at the alarm clock with disgust. 2 seconds later the alarm clock is on the floor, knocked of it’s table by Duzza. Duzza, while mumbling something then gets out of his bed and walks into the bathroom which is connected to the bedroom. A few moments later Duzza comes back out of the bathroom and walks into the large walk-in closet. He puts on a pair of baggy white pants, a tight R. Kelly ‘TP-2 tour” T-shirt and how has a platinum chain around his neck. He then walks over to his massive stereo. He would put on the radio, but he’d probably just hear someone unknown DJ dis his man R. Kelly. So instead he decides to put on a cd. That CD is the “TP-2.com” by R. Kelly Duzza then sits back on the bed, to put on some socks. It is then he notices his hand. Duzza’s face pales as he looks at the back of his hands. The camera zooms into the back of Duzza’s hand. They look pretty normal, apart from in the centre there are some white splotches Duzza starts to look panicked. He rubs at the skin, hoping that the white will come off. But it doesn’t Duzza: “What tha fuck?” Duzza quickly runs to the bathroom and washes his hands under the tap, but still the white blemish on his hands remains Duzza: “Malek chill..it’s probably nothing…yeah, it’s nothing… but what if it is something? Hey yo, wifey get up here…please” Duzza’s ebony queen/wife Brittany walks into the bedroom, a confused look on her beautiful face Brittany: “Oh my God. You haven’t have you?” Duzza, shame faced, shows his wife the back of his hands. At first, she doesn’t know what to say. Brittany: “I think we’d best call a doctor. But don’t worry, you’ll be find, probably just a little skin blemish, nothing to worry about” Duzza: “Let’s hope so” Brittany picks up the phone and dials a number Brittany: “Hi, this is Brittany Williams, I’d like to book an appointment with Dr Hughes, please…. Yeah, it’s an emergency…. I need one now… ok, see you then” Duzza: “Well?” Brittany: “We have an appointment in 30 minutes, so let’s go now” Duzza: “Ok, go pull around the jeep with the tinted windows, while I go change” Brittany: “Duzz, it’s like 100 degrees out there, just go in what you got on” Duzza: “No….i gotta change” The scene fades, but then re-opens in a upscale neighborhood of New York. All the houses are painted white and look exactly alike. All with the same one door garage and mowed lawn. Only one house looks different. It has long steps leading up to it and has people going to and from it. This building is the doctors surgery. Somewhere, where the rich and famous who are around the New York area go for various things. Duzza is no different. The black jeep pulls up in front of the doctors. And Duzza and his wife step out. Duzza is now dressed from head to toe in black, he has a black t-shirt, baggy black jeans and has a black beanie on. He also has a pair of black gloves on. Duzza looks around to see if anyone is looking at him. He thinks the coast is clear and starts to walk up the steps, but quickly spins back around Duzza: “Aww shit!” Brittany: “What is it?” Duzza: “At the top of the steps, the n*gga in the white shirt, I think it my man Jordan” Brittany: “The wrestler?” Duzza: “Yeah… shit, we gotta go, ‘fore he see’s me” Brittany: “It’ll be fine, he won’t notice” Before Duzza can reply, Jordan X Shabazz, hollers at him Jordan: “DUZZA! That you?” Jordan, is walking down the steps towards Duzza, dressed in a white t-shirt, white shorts and is carrying a small brown paper bag Duzza: “Heh, hey bro” Jordan: “Of all the doctors surgery’s you come to the same one as me, imagine that” Duzza: “Yeah imagine that. Well I gotta go” Jordan: “Hold up, player why you got on these damn gloves man? It’s burning out here” Duzza: “Errr….no reason…what’s in that bag?” Jordan: “Nothing…not a damn thing” Brittany: “C’mon Malek, we gotta see the doctor” Jordan: “Damn Duzz, there something wrong with you?” Duzza: “That’s what I’m hear to find out…” Jordan: “Well, I ain’t doing nothing, how bout I just come lend you some support?” Duzza: “Nah, bro, I’ll be fine trust me” Jordan: “Duzz, it’s the least I can do, I mean what’s the worse that can happen to you? What? You gonna turn white?? Hahahahahaha, I’m too funny” Duzza: “Well…that’s what I came to the doctors for…” Duzza removes his gloves and shows Jordan the white skin blemishes on his hands. Jordan looks at his hands with a mixture of shock and humour. Jordan: “Duzza..is…turning…white! Hahahahahahaha, your become a cracker!” Duzza: “Shut the fuck up, before you get smacked the fuck up” Jordan: “Heh, I’m sorry it’s just, you hate white folk, and now you might become one, you could have Micheal Jackson disease!” Duzza: “Dude… your not helping the situation here” Brittany: “I think we should go in” Duzza: “Yeah…and let’s see going on” The scene starts to fade to black as Duzza, his wife and Jordan X Shabazz all walk up the steps to the doctors surgery…
