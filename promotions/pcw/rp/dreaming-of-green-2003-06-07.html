Dreaming of Green

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/06/03 He runs but he knows not what from. Sweat matts his short black hair to his forehead and his eyes dart from side to side as if seeking out an invisible man somewhere in the dense undergrowth. As the man is revealed we see it is PCW's Extreme Champion Manuel Labor, fully dressed in his ring attire with the Extreme Championship fixed firmly around his waist. The Lucha Legend appears to be running through a forest, the bird calls echoing through the moisture filled air as Labor burst through endless foliage. Small cuts begin to appear on his hands as vines flip back and Labor is forced to throw his hands up to defend himself. The skin though is not enough and small welts begin to appear and bleed. Staring down at his hands Labor pauses but not for long as a noise unheard by anyone else spurs him on to continue running. Wiping his brow, causing Mauri style blood streaks to from on his forehead. As he burst through more and more foliage the pressure seems to be building in Labors eyes. He keeps looking over his shoulder before checking back and then repeating the process. The end result to all seems inevitable. After about thirty seconds and at least two more cycles of the shoulder checking as Labor turns his foot catches in a root and he is sent tumbling. As Labor falls the screen splits in two. In one half we see the Lucha Legends eyes fly open with fear, his pupils shrinking to the size of pins and sweat pouring from his brow. In the other half of the screen we see a cardiograph of Labors Heart rate spiking out of control. Overriding both of these images is the sound of a racing heartrate presumably Labors. This is all we hear as Labor's slow mo fall continues until THUD. Labor hits the ground on the edge of an opening, sending a dust cloud up into the air as he quickly pulls himself to the middle of the circle before an unnatural silence befalls the whole area. As Labor looks around he can see that he has found himself in a totally cleared circle. Peering around nervously he hears a rustling and as his head shoots round like a shotgun the rustling appears behind him. As Labor spins around from the forest a green mist begins to appear until he is standing ankle deep in it. As the mist continues to rise a crazy giggling is heard, HAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHA.................................... Manuel rears up from his bed the sheets sticking to him due to the sweat and wiping his brow rises towards the sink. Labor- God dammit whats with this dreams its just another match, fair enough its with two other champs but I have beaten El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin. I can do this. They will know thae meaning of hard work and we will see if they bleed Green. The camera fades to black as Labor glugs rapidly at his pint of water.
