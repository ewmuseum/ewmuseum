10 Things I Hate About Gaz

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/03 While sitting around the house this dreary Wednesday afternoon I began to ponder the answer to a question both my mother and Evan had popped at me. Why did I hate Gaz Green so much? It was a question worth asking on their behalf but a question I wished they had not asked on my behalf. There were many reasons I disliked Gaz and if they wanted to know, I’d let it be clear to them why he would never be the one to move in and make himself home with us. I grabbed a baby blue gel pen that was resting conveniently on the coffee table as well as a small note pad and jotted the simple heading 10 Things I Hate About Gaz at the top before letting my mind go to work. 1.) His pranks never end no matter what the circumstance. 2. That damn hand buzzer of his and the fact that I’ve seemingly been shocked more than anyone by it. 3.) The fact that he doesn’t seem to find this marriage because he enjoys tormenting me with the fact that I too am a Green now. 4.) He ruined what was supposed to be the happiest day of my life, not once but twice. 5.) He seemingly won’t go away no matter how hard I try to get away from him! 6.) He continues to refer to us as Team Green ever since that damn match where we had to tag together despite the fact that we are anything but a team. I placed my pen down on the paper as my mind went blank. I had run out of reasons. But there had to be more reasons than the ones I had listed for me hating Gaz this just didn’t seem like enough. But what else could I list? He was a prankster everyone knew that, but why hate him for that? Everyone had a bit of rebellion and need to prank in their blood; some just hid it better than others. But then there was always the several times where he had knocked me out with multiple weapons, yet still I couldn’t find much reason to hate him for that either considering that for something as prized as the PCW Tag Team Titles even I had sunken as low as to almost using a weapon one time in order to retain the titles at Spring Fling, before being intercepted by Gaz and a rag covered in chloroform which cost both Chris and I the titles. “Damn it there has to be more than six reasons I hate Gaz!” I shouted as I buried myself further in my seat on the couch forcing myself into deeper thought. “Aww come on sweet cheeks, you can’t hate Gaz that much if you can only think of six reasons.” I heard the voice of Mini Gaz whisper into my ear before the smaller version of the prince of pranks appeared on my shoulder and made himself at home as he tried to convince me that the real version of him was not such a bad guy. “There are more than six reasons; they just aren’t as obvious as the other reasons!” I retorted, not wanting to admit that I was indeed having trouble getting past six reasons and just might not be able to list even one more. “Sureeeee.” Mini Gaz replied in a very sarcastic tone which was telling me that I shouldn’t bother because he already knew that I couldn’t think of more than six reasons of why I hated Gaz so much. “Alright fine, I can’t think of anymore than six reasons but those six reasons are good enough reasons to hate him as much as I do!” I argued back knowing that I probably looked like a fool not being able to list more than six reasons why I hated the person whom served as one of my biggest rivals simply because I was given him last name which is something I never wanted. “You know I think you have a thing for the jester. Perhaps that’s why you can’t get past six reasons.” The emerald green elf chuckled as if he had revealed a secret crush that I had not wanted anyone else to know. “WHAT? THAT IS THE MOST RIDICOLOUS THING I’VE EVER HEARD!” I shouted back perhaps in not the best response considering that almost every time a secret that was not supposed to be discovered is found it results in the same type of squeal that was coming from me, but I couldn’t help but squeal considering what was coming from the little jester’s mouth was far from the truth. “Oh it may seem ridiculous but it’s true, it’s damn true and you know it better than anyone my dear Tessa Bear.” Mini Gaz said before making an exit knowing very well I would be angered with his words but even inside my mind I found me questioning myself. Did I really not hate Gaz as much as I thought I did and was there something about him there actually a good side to him that I was refusing to see? I wanted to think about it but it bothered me too much to think that I might actually like him in even the slightest ways and I refused to think about it any further especially considering I had my match against The Order and Stone Burns to think about which was much more important considering that I would be facing Sivasian Krikorian not only on Turmoil but Fusion as well.
