Cannonball Run 5, Takin' the lead, and losin' the cops...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Two weeks ago. {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into another dark night on the highway. Cars are scarce on the highway at this time of night, but one single car is seen driving down the road at an alarming speed. The car is of course the blue 2000 Dodge Viper, which is owned by Semore Smoke. You can hear the music blaring from the vehicle as he speeds down the desert highway and luckily to us, Semore Smoke isn't singing to the song this time. The song playing over the radio is one of Smoke's old favorites. Semore Smoke just happens to have a thing for every type of music and one of his favorite genres happens to be comedy. The song playing over his radio right now is a song from the Weird Al Yankovic record, 'Bad Hair Day'. The song is 'The Night Santa Went Crazy', which is number twelve at the end of the album. But… as the scene enters the car… our wishes are ended, and Semore begins to sing along to the song starting at the second verse… Semore Smoke: 'The workshop is gone now, he decided to bomb it, everywhere you'll find pieces of Cupid and Comet, he tied up his helpers, and he held the elves hostage, and he turned poor Rudolph into reindeer sausage! He got Dancer and Prancer with an old German Luger, and he slashed up Dasher just like Freddy Krueger, and he picked up a flamethrower and he barbecued Blitzen, and he took a big bite and said 'It tastes just like Chicken!' The night Santa went crazy, the night Kris Kringle went nuts, now you can't hardly walk around the north pole, without stepping in reindeer guts!' {{{{The End stirs from his sleeping state in the passenger seat as he turns to Semore Smoke and looks at him with a confused look on his face. He can't really see him of course, but is looking where Semore Smoke is sitting. He then says to him with a tone of annoyance in his voice…}}}} End: Why do you have to sing that annoying song? {{{{Smoke hits the stop button on his CD player set up that he installed himself as a custom job to the car. He then looks at End, but shuffles his eyes back and forth from The End's face and the road. He then answers End's question…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, I happen to like that type of music. In fact, that one song happens to be one of my favorites. It's funny, that's why I like it. End: Well… I don't like it, anyone who can make fun of Santa Claus like that must not have much of a conscience. Semore Smoke: You're not turning into Bernard Colt on me now are you? End: Nah, I just have some morals that are pretty much all. To tell you the truth, I don't enjoy Bernard Colt at all. He preaches about being a true American, but he himself doesn't have much when it comes to being moral. So what, he doesn't smoke or do anything like that, tell me who doesn't, other then him. {{{{Semore just turns his head and looks back at the road for the duration of the drive. He looks at the road sign that he passes a few seconds later and smiles. He then announces with hype in his voice…}}}} Semore Smoke: Only five miles until the California state line! Woo! {{{{End and Smoke then celebrate their drive. Semore takes a joint out of his pocket and sticks it in his mouth. He then lights the joint and begins to puff on the joint, letting the smoke ease it's way out of his lungs. End doesn't mind his smoking, and in fact, he even offers to take a few hits off the roach near the end in celebration. The scene changes from the inside of Semore's Dodge to the inside of the brown UPS DHL van that D-Kay Calyx and Joey Flash are driving. In the driver seat as usual is Joey Flash, and sitting in the navigation spot is D-Kay Calyx, they're still surrounded by cops as they get their escort through the state of Nevada. But hopefully the plan that D-Kay announced earlier will get them away from the cops, that way they can break into a sprint for the money. D-Kay looks at a sign that they pass and smiles, he then squints ahead, and sure enough, after they go over the horizon of a hill, he can see the lights…}}}} D-Kay: Dude! Just one mile from Las Vegas! See the lights! This is gonna be so fuckin' awesome! The Circle K we'll be pulling into is just another mile away, it's right there in fact, in front of the Caesar's Palace. {{{{D-Kay points at Caesar's Palace and sure enough, a Circle K sign is seen in front of the famed casino and hotel. D-Kay waves out the window to the cops as they pull into the driveway of the Circle K. Joey pulls the van over and parks it in the parking lot beside the convenient store. D-Kay runs out of the van and walks up to the cop car behind him that followed them into the parking lot. He walks up to the cop car and the cop rolls the window down on the passenger side so D-Kay can stick his head through the window and talk to him. D-Kay does so and he tells the cop…}}}} D-Kay: We're gonna get some quick drinks in here, and we'll be right back. Ok? Cop: Sure, go ahead, I'll be right here. {{{{D-Kay smiles and nods, he then pulls his head back through the window and walks away from the cop car and the van for the last time. Joey and him both enter the store together and they walk to the back of the store. In the back is another door that exits to the other end of the convenient store parking lot. They exit through that door and right there parked in the driveway is an old 1978 Cadillac. D-Kay walks over to the blue Caddy and opens the driver side door. He sits into the car and pulls a set of keys out of his pocket. He sticks the key in the ignition and turns the key, and sure enough the car starts. Joey smiles and opens the passenger door and sits down into the car. D-Kay closes his door as does Joey and D-Kay puts the car into reverse and pulls out, he then pulls straight and out into the road again, they both then drive through Las Vegas, with smiles on their faces, as they head towards LA and the money that awaits the winner of the race. The scene fades on the Caddy driving off into the nighttime lights. It then fades in again on the red van that is familiar already to the leaders of the race, Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan. Driving in the technological wonder is Ivan himself and navigating on a virtual map is Alexei. All of a sudden a red light begins to flash in the car as systems start to fail…}}}} Ivan: What!? What's going on Alexei? {{{{Ivan's deep Russian accent is hard to understand, but you can understand his meaning. Alexei's accent is no better…}}}} Alexei: All systems are failing sir. Damn pieces of shit! We're now visible! Ivan: Damn…. The heat censor screen is fading as well. Cut if off, I'll have to cut on the headlights… for the first time in this race. {{{{Alexei does so and the screen turns to his normal windshield view. Ivan then hits a switch in front of him and cuts the headlights on. As soon as he cuts the lights on, another red light goes off, and the computer is repeating this word over and over again…}}}} Computer: SMOKEY! SMOKEY! SMOKEY! SMOKEY! SMOKEY! SMOKEY! SMOKEY! SMOKEY! Ivan: Shut that computer off. I know a cop is behind us. {{{{Sure enough, a cop hits its flashers and then it's siren and is signaling for the speeding Russian van to pull over. Ivan hit's a switch again, and the scene shows the license plates on the back of the van switches to a normal United States license plate with 'Russian 1' written across it. Ivan then pulls the van over for the cop and he stares at Alexei as the cop gets out of the car…}}}} Ivan: Damn computer shit failing on us Russians… Let me guess… Made in Japan. Alexei: Yes sir. Ivan: Damn Japanese shit! {{{{The cop walks up to the front of the van and Ivan goes through the normal set up that any pulled over person would go through. The scene fades on Ivan and then reopens up within Semore Smoke's blue Dodge Viper. Sitting in the front seat, Smoke sees a set of flashing lights in front of him so he slows down. He rolls the window down lets the weed smoke out of the car just in case. He then continues going the speed limit and even cuts the music down again, which he had cut back up. As he drives by, he realizes that the cop pulled over someone, and the person that is pulled over is driving a red van, in fact. He then smiles as he looks at the two people in the driver seat, he and End look at each other with the same look and they high five each other. Smoke then says to himself and End with a smile on his face…}}}} Semore Smoke: Who's in the lead now, bitch? {{{{The scene leaves the Dodge and as Smoke clears the cop's site, he starts to speed again. About two seconds later he approaches a sign that says 'Welcome to California' and you can hear the celebration. The scene fades on the taillights of Semore Smoke's speeding blue Dodge Viper…}}}}
