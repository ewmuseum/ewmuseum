JJ: I'm Not Gay!!!!! versus Everyone Else: Yes you are.

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th April We cut back to the continuing madness that is Miyagawa’s turmoiled and corrupted life. Boy would that intro had sounded good over a year ago, but now it’s kinda out of place and sucky. Kinda like Chris Chase in a main event at a PPV... but we’ll get on to that again later. Miyagawa, Lara and Triple J are standing inside the elevator waiting for it to arrive at the floor at which Natalia is staying in her ward. Hopefully of course after the PPV tonight, Chris Sloboda aka known as Boda will donate three or four pints of blood to Natalia this evening allowing her to have the operation to save their baby. JJ turns to Miyagawa abruptly. JJ: Where’s Steve? I haven’t seen the old man Jabowski for ages. Miyagawa shrugs back at him simply because he’s not sure where old man river himself is. Miyagawa: Haven’t got a clue, JJ. **Triple J scowls at him because Miya left out on of the J’s** He’s pretty busy with NTICW right now... for the first time in his life he has his own life. We cut to Steve Jabowski himself... he’s sitting at his PC. He stares at a green surfboard thing on top of his midi tower and watches the green light flash for no reason. The camera zooms close up on him and shows the beads of sweat of pure annoyance bead from his forehead. He wipes them off in a single swipe from his hand. Steve: BASTARD! He grabs a screwdriver and slams it handle side down, onto the PC Midi Tower making lovely twanging noises. Steve: Will you… *TWANG* FUCKING WORK… *TWANG* YOU PIECE OF SHIT… *TWANG*… A young lady walks in, blonde hair and big... erm... eyes. And her breasts aren’t bad either. Steve is wearing a muscle shirt and a pair of jeans. The young lady is wearing some sort of waitress uniform and has a badge which reads “Cindy” on it. Cindy: Ah, connection problems? Steve swivels in his executive chair and throws the screwdriver like a throwing knife, right into the wall in front of himself. It stands out of the wall looking menacing. Cindy looks at the screwdriver sticking out of the Summerslam ‘88 poster… right in Macho Man Randy Savage’s forehead in fact. Cindy: That will be the “yes” answer then I take it? Steve just makes a growl at him. Cindy: Try phoning technical support. Steve: I did. Cindy: So, what did they say? Steve: Tell told me to delete some stuff and then complained when I didn’t write down all the information from the stuff that I was deleting. Then they passed the buck and said it was down to the phone line. Cindy: Did they say that they would sort it out? Steve: Yes. But they don’t work on the weekends, so I have to wait till Monday or Tuesday. They said to phone Tuesday if it still wasn’t working. Cindy: Ah. So what’s the big problem, just wait till Monday or Tuesday? Steve: But you don’t understand... Miyagawa is in a main event tonight in his first ever main event at a PPV. Cindy: Haven’t you heard of “television”. Just call the cable company and order it, no problem. Steve shakes his head, almost disappointed at Cindy wanting to take the easy way out at this point. Steve: But if I watch it over the web on their website I can send emails backstage... I can email Miyagawa. Cindy: Don’t you have his mobile number then? Steve sighs with annoyance at the modem kicking up the fuss. Steve: No, that’s not the point. Watching a pay per view on the web is new... it’s gimmicky... and I... Cindy shakes her head also in annoyance. Cindy: Gah... men are always the same. Gadgets. Did you ever ride that motorised scooter that you bought? No. Was the electric can opener any better than the manual one... no. So why don’t you just watch it on the television and phone Miyagawa on his mobile if you want to speak to him? Steve completely ignores what the young lady just said to him. Steve: I am going to have to make a pact with the devil themselves. Cindy: You’re going to join Fedwars? Steve: No, I’m going to have to sign up for an AOL account. Steve and Cindy both shudder at the same time at the mention of the name of the devil’s own pride and joy... AOL **shudder** Cindy: Can’t you just wait till Tuesday? You can watch it on the Television. You can phone him on his mobile. Don’t do it Steve... don’t sign up for...A...O...L... They both shudder once more at the mention of that terrible name... the name of pure unadulterated evil... Steve picks up a computer magazine from the floor near the desk and bends it length ways (so he’s not bending the spine) and finds that he can’t bend it. Steve: Ah here we go, an inserted CD... time to... sell my soul... Cindy: But... but... we have the television... we have his mobile number... don’t... it’s not worth it... I’ll even pay for the sign up cost for the PPV. Steve looks at Cindy. Steve: Well if you’re gonna put it like that, the phone is over there and I’m sure you have your credit card handy. Cindy crosses her arms in annoyance. Cindy: It was all a ruse wasn’t it? Steve grins at her. Steve: Yep, and now I get to watch Miyagawa become PCW World Champion after beating Boda and Chris Chase for absolutely nothing at all... I rule. Cindy: God I hate you dad. Steve: Hey, I’m only doing my job. Cindy: Being a bastard? Steve: Nah, I’m “The Bastard”. Get it right. Steve grins as the pan out back to the three people in the lift. JJ: So no idea where he is at all then? Miyagawa: Nope, none at all. He said he might watch the PPV on the internet or on the TV if he can con his daughter into paying for it. JJ: Steve has a daughter? Miyagawa: Steve has three. JJ: And he never introduced me to any of them? Miyagawa: He said he didn’t want to risk you stop being gay and dating one. The lift echoes... as they are about to find out... JJ: I’M NOT GAY!!!!!!!!!!
