Rule #1 in Psychiatric Warfare...

26 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 26, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within what looks to be the back of a limo. The camera pans the back seat of the limo and you see a TV, VCR, DVD player, CD player, and other house hold items. As it pans you see seated in one seat one of Semore Smoke’s guards. Across from that guard as the camera now zooms away from the TV that is showing the latest news on the war in Iraq you see the second guard of Smoke’s. The camera then turns dramatically around and you see the face of PCW’s terrorist, or at least Trinidad’s terrorist, Semore Smoke himself. Smoke turns the TV off with the remote in his hand and then looks out the window as he’s obvious just driving around time to try and pass time... Semore Smoke: Well... guys, what can you tell me about this place? {{{{The guard on the left speaks up first as the lead guard...}}}} Guard: This is little Mexico New York, sir. You can tell because of all the Mexicans that are around. {{{{Smoke nods but looks kind of disappointed...}}}} Semore Smoke: I know where we are dumb ass. I just want to know if there is any family I can meet with here to talk to about... well... you know. {{{{The guard looks surprised and then answers...}}}} Guard: Oh! Well, there isn’t anyone here to talk to, Jones pretty much owns this part of town, why did you want to come here anyway? Semore Smoke: Ever heard about psychiatric warfare? {{{{Smoke turns away from the window and stares at the guard he’s been addressing lately, waiting for an answer...}}}} Guard: Yes sir I have. Isn’t it where you, the soldier try and get to the enemy not physically, but mentally? {{{{Smoke nods with a smile on his face...}}}} Semore Smoke: Yep, that’s just about it. Well, I’m here as a former soldier from Fort Bragg, the 82nd Airborne division that is. And I’d like to tell you that psychiatric warfare was one of my favorite styles of waging war. You can defeat your opponent by just driving them crazy with mind games. Well, that’s what we’re doing here, we’ll get into the head that is El Diablo, The Mexican Devil. {{{{Both guards look at each other with huge grins on their faces...}}}} Guard: Oh! Makes sense boss. Just like how you’re driving Trinidad crazy with you having his favorite kangaroo, Crespo. Semore Smoke: Precisely. You see, first off I’ll play with him for a bit, then we he does what I want him to do I’ll play around some more and he’ll wish he had never pissed me off. But you see, like any style of war, psychiatric has to be planned out precisely to work. And I’m hoping that once I get inside the head of El Diablo, I can dismantle any and all knowledge he has of himself, his people, or even PCW. Stop here! {{{{Smoke stares up ahead yelling at the driver of the limo and the drivers stops the limo upon request. Smoke stares out the window and smiles as he has found what he was looking for. Smoke opens the door to the limo and steps out of the limo, he motions for the two guards to stay in the limo and closes the door behind him. He walks forward and the camera zooms in on what he was looking for, a poster of El Diablo posted on the side of a store with several young kids around it cheering, many of the kids are dressed in El Diablo shirts and other such PCW and El Diablo merchandise. Even one is wearing the new Semore Smoke shirt that has a pot leaf on the front of it and it reads “Got Pot?” below it. Smoke steps forward to the group of kids and does so with a huge grin on his face. The one little Mexican boy wearing the Semore Smoke T-shirt turns and sees Semore Smoke and screams out with a smile on his face and in an excited voice...}}}} Boy: Everyone! It’s Semore Smoke! {{{{Smoke walks up to the group of kids and tones his grin down to a smile...}}}} Semore Smoke: Hey kids, I was just driving by in my limo and I noticed that you all had PCW T-shirts on and were gathered around an El Diablo poser, what’s going on? {{{{The Semore Smoke fan speaks up first...}}}} Boy: We’re just watching the Pay Per View video that a friend of mine taped from catching March Madness. I can’t believe you’re here though! {{{{Smoke nods his head and shakes his head in both agreement and shocked that he’s actually got one or two fans left. Smoke gets down on one knee in front of the boy and points to the poster...}}}} Semore Smoke: Now, why do you guys love this man? {{{{Smoke was talking to everyone, but the boy is whom he expects to answer as he also seems to be the oldest of the kids there as you look at them more and more...}}}} Boy: Well, he returned at the last Pay Per View just like you and he’s Mexican and so are we! {{{{Smoke nods his head in understanding and his face reeks of understanding of what the little boy is saying. But then Smoke looks at the boy and the kids with a serious look on his face...}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, did you know that El Diablo is actually the Devil? {{{{The kids laugh at first, and so does the boy...}}}} Boy: Ha! Of course he is, that’s why his name is El Diablo! {{{{Smoke shakes his head showing that they don’t understand what he’s saying...}}}} Semore Smoke: No, what I’m saying is that he’s THE Devil. You know, the enemy of God from hell? He’s here on Earth to steal the souls of all of you, but I’ll be stopping him this Friday and he won’t lay a hand on you here. {{{{The kids look afraid now and they stop laughing. The boy that Smoke is talking to in front of him looks around at the ground for a few seconds and then stares back up at Smoke...}}}} Boy: Mr. Smoke, I’m glad you told us this, before we started to worship the Devil, us Catholics wouldn’t want that to happen! Semore Smoke: Yeah, you wouldn’t, now wish me luck this Friday! {{{{The kids stop frowning and smile at Smoke a lovable grin...}}}} Kids: Good luck! {{{{Smoke turns around and walks away from the kids, an adult comes walking up to Smoke and has a piece of paper and a pen in his hand...}}}} Man: Mr. Smoke! Can I have your autograph? {{{{Smoke turns to the man disgusted...}}}} Semore Smoke: No you can’t! Get away from me! {{{{The kids behind him didn’t hear him and are still in awe that Smoke showed up in front of them. Smoke walks up to his limo leaving the man he just passed shocked in what Smoke just answered with. Smoke opens he limo door and steps into the limo, he smiles to both of his guards and says...}}}} Semore Smoke: Number one rule, turn his people against him, hence, his own race and fans. Next we’ll hit the second rule of psychiatric warfare... {{{{The limo drives off with Smoke smiling along the way...}}}}
