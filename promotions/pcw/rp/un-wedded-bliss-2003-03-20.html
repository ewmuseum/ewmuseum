[Un]Wedded Bliss

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 20, 2003 We’re not a petty man. We’re willing to forgive anyone for his or her sins against us pending he or she wished to be forgiven. But some things are unforgivable. All of these people are against the god, and for what reason? Because they think they deserve something that none of them have the god given right to even glance upon? We think not. They all want to fight against us because the threat of destruction just got a little greater. The situations bubble across the seas, and back on the home front people are getting jittery. Terror alert is high, be wary. Death and destruction should remain in someone else’s country, not ours some think. Well…sorry to be a bother but we’re making a special delivery to select individuals. Chet King, Rent A Hero, Venneman Pendragon, Xavier Kannon, Triple T, Clyde, El Diablo, Violence Jack, Skylar Green… Nine individuals…all looking to take out one man for the win. Will it be us…will it be their god? Like we said earlier, we think not. Our castle stands empty now…all it’s inhabitants now gone. And the structure itself in shambles from the fight that happened on Christmas between so called friends of our daughter called the Executives and ourselves. Three of them against one of us…given our track record you’d think we were able to stomp them out with ease right? Dead wrong. Matrix…that’s Cyclonica’s computer dork friend…he had some patch in our head that prevented us from even touching one of those Executive goons. We couldn’t do anything but sit there and take a triple team beating or our head would start exploding with high-pitched buzzing and migraines. Kannon tries injecting mercury in our blood and thinks that can do something effectively…he should’ve came to these guys for results. We’ll never forget their faces for the rest of our lives…kinda hard to forget someone who mercilessly tears into you while you sit there helplessly. People remember our faces for a long time in their dreams…we’re what nightmares are made of. We walked through the door, stepping over a piece of rubble and looked out into the courtyard. The pond was in a mess from the fight, the plants burned or wrecked. “SONATA!!” Stupid girl…where was she? The black and blonde haired woman came rushing out from across the courtyard wearing a pair of blue jeans, a black apron, and a white long sleeved shirt. She stopped in front of us and bowed deeply, looking up at us with a tired smile and streaks of flour across her face. “Yes, sir?” “Are you finished with dinner yet?” She shook her head, her smile lowering. “No, sir. It’ll be ready in about ten minutes.” F-ck, was she slow. Speed slow, not El Diablo slow, that idiot thinking he can come back to a warm welcome. All that fuss, all those f-ckin videos and when he comes back he picks a fight with sh-tty Flaymin Aymin. Wow, big f-ckin impact made there. “F-ck…we were walking for at least an hour there, and you’re still not finished with that sh-t?” Instantly she went down to an apologetic bow. “I’m sorry sir, very sorry. I’ll finish as quickly as I can for you.” “Yeah, yeah, just don’t burn the sh-t.” we slapped her on the rear as we walked past her. We knew she was smiling as we stepped into the courtyard…ever since she betrayed us with Cyclonica and Matrix in December she’s been nothing but the good traditional Japanese wife…all except the wife part. Screw if we’re getting married. You think we’re some sappy pansy ass like Chris Storm or Xavier Kannon? Their pussy whipped asses are getting a different kind of whipping come next week in the ring. We were tired and bitter and pissed off with the entire company. We don’t care about Jeremy Howard co-owning it or whoever else f-ckin co owns it. We hate the people that run the sh-t, we hate the people that work for the sh-t company. If we gave a sh-t if it imploded from within or if it became the best company in the f-ckin world, we’d put a personal hand in the complete destruction of the joint. Then we wouldn’t have to deal with bitches like Kannon whining every week about something not going his way, or dumbasses like Triple T appeasing to the dope heads and pot smokers. Kicking aside a piece of the crumpled wall, we shook our head slowly. Yeah, destruction was our thing, but we dished it out. We didn’t take it well. Not physically, but mentally. When someone gets the advantage to the degree where we feel we’re on the defensive, that’s f-ckin wrong. A team of people want to come against us to take us down, that’s f-ckin wrong. Anyone think they can drop us down with a weapon, that’s just f-ckin wrong. But rest assured…all that will happen during the big title match. And if something goes wrong during that match, we’ll be ready to snap… “Dinner!” Sonata poked her head out from the corner across the way, a polite smile on her face. We waved her off, spitting in the trashed pond before heading over. Eating…we used to remember when we only had an appetite for chaos. Now we’re dining on food… Shows what happens when you realize what you’re really not. -=-=-=-=-=-=- “How was your day?” Sonata wrapped some noodles up with her chopsticks and slurped them down. She was good at that… we looked up from our bowl of rice and teriyaki with a wrinkled brow. “Day was day. Leave it alone.” And with that we went back to eating. This wasn’t some social time, it was eating time. You wanna talk, you go to Chet King. He likes to run his big ass mouth all the time about sh-t that don’t concern him. It’s called being nosy…mind your own business. “Oh come on now,” she said smiling. “Something interesting must’ve happened to you.” Sh-t…wouldn’t leave well the f-ck enough alone huh? “We ran for an hour, trained all morning, sat around here with you for god knows how long. Some day huh?” She chuckled as she sipped her tea. “My day was pretty nice. I did the laundry, and after that I tried to straighten up a little around here. Got most of the rooms on the west wing cleared out of the debris. We nodded while shoveling rice in our mouth. “Yeah, remind us to tip you at the end of the week.” Another laugh from the girl…like everything we f-ckin said was so damn hilarious. So cheerful like that spandex wearing goofball hero…he’s been more of a waste around here than we have…no one cares he’s the pussyweight champ or f-ck ever. “Duke Williams dropped the belt…” we blurted out between chewing. Sonata looked up at us with wide eyes. “Who?” “Duke Williams…old ugly ass bearded no talent whore who won the title at March Madness. He retired and dropped the belt.” “Oh…” she nodded slowly. “Didn’t you say that would happen?” “Predicted it all by ourselves.” Nodding, we reached across the table and grabbed the tofu. “F-ck a Divine Prophetess.” Her hands went to her face in laughter, holding it back as we pulled a piece of tofu off the block. Popping it into our mouth, even we couldn’t help but smirk at the comment. Hell, we get funny sometimes… we just don’t live our lives like a f-ckin comedy act like the surfer, or the hero, or even “DA MAN” sometimes. “So what’s happening with the belt?” she asked us. “Ten man brawl for it. No rules, one pin.” “And you’re in it right?” she grabbed the bowl of miso soup and poured a bit into her own bowl. We looked up at her with an annoyed look from that question. “Hell yeah. We would wreck the whole party if we weren’t. We’re a part of the world title no matter what those f-cks think they can do. They can have all the little petty feuds they want, but in the end it’s all coming down to who’s got the most talent.” “And no one’s got more talent than my god!” Sonata beamed with support in her smile, looking across the table at us with admiring eyes. “F-ck yeah…” we looked up and met her eyes for a second, leaning back and nodding slowly. “…f-ck yeah…” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=- It would have been easy for us to forget everything that happened. Sonata was happy just being with us, living in the castle alone with us and being the textbook wife. All day she hummed and sang and cleaned and cooked and whatever else sh-t she did around here when we weren’t paying attention. And never did she complain about anything. All she cared for was being here… And us…we didn’t give a rat’s ass if she stayed or fled with the rest of the people that lived here. The End left when things got thick too…shows how much of a friend he was. As soon as Alliances was over he moved his blind ass out with his bitch and now look where they ended up. She’s DEAD and he’s got nothing. None of that would’ve happened if he stayed in touch with friends. His own damn fault anyways for letting a bitch like V-Jack whore him out. We thought of the way we treated Sonata as punishment for what she did, but she was enjoying herself…so was it punishment in that case? That’s like if we ADA one of those dumbasses in the match and pin them, will it be punishment if they get back up and ask for another one? It’d be questionable if it was or not…we’d still ADA them either way. We’d ADA anyone again for getting back up from the first one…audacious persistent no selling bastards. It was about 11pm when we sat on the ledge looking out at the moon from the bay window in our bedroom. Stripped down to a pair of black pajama pants, we leaned against the wall thinking about the big match. Yet another chance for us to take what we deserve…and if it became yet another screw job and someone who doesn’t deserve the belt takes it we swear…the war on Iraq is gonna seem like a spat compared to the destruction we unleash on that man. The water shut off in the bathroom, and a few minutes later the black and blonde haired woman came padding across the room in a black towel. “Whatcha doing?” she said cheerfully after a yawn. Before we answered she plopped down on our lap, snuggling back and draping our arm over her. Tenacious little slut wasn’t she? “Thinking.” “About the big match?” “Yeah…” She nodded, looking out at the night sky. “You know I’ve been thinking myself.” “Yeah? Don’t that beat f-ck all.” She tapped me in the arm teasingly before continuing. “No, I was wondering…ever since we got back together, I wanted to ask you why you’ve been talking in plural form.” Mother f-cker… no one’s asked us that in a while…or ever as a matter of fact. “Well…” damn…we had to remember why we spoke like this. The reason’s probably changed since then. We knew it wasn’t some goofy ass gimmick shtick like being a medieval knight or a walking HP Lovecraft novel…it was much deeper than that. “Well?” she looked up with waiting on her face. “Hold your damn horses. We gotta remember why we talk like this.” She pouted and leaned back, waiting patiently until we got it again. “Well…back when we were with the Void Elements trying to become the whole master of the Void and all, our mind kinda got f-cked over by the mystical sh-t they did. Then when we took on the name of Neo Void God…we just started talking like this, like it was a part of the name. But in our minds…it does feel like there are more than just one person up there.” “How many do you have?” she curiously asked with a yawn. “Uh…there’s the one you know, the one you grew up with. We think he’s pushed all the way in the back, the nice guy who’d give the shirt off his back for you. Then there’s the apocalyptic voice, the one that pretty much takes over in the ring and says destroy everything around us. He has a lot of say in what we do. Then there’s the god voice…he tells us that we’re superior, that we deserve to be above the masses, that we deserve to rule. We believe he’s true sometimes…other times he’s just annoying.” “Sounds like that one has a superiority complex,” she yawned softly and chuckled in the same grade. “Heh, yeah he might. Then there’s the thug, the yakuza style voice that knows how to take care of business. He plans mostly everything, from who we attack, what body part we attack, and when we attack. He’s the organized one, most efficient. There’s the martial artist in us that spits out some Blitz –shit every once in a while, but we ignore him. But then…there’s one voice that we can’t label. It doesn’t even sound like our other voices…it’s like someone’s up there, and he tells us things about what it means to be the nVg…what it takes to be a god…what we must do to reach that complete godhood.” “What does it mean…to be the nVg?” her tired voice murmured. “…destruction.” That’s all we could answer with…because as far as we knew, that’s all it consisted off. Thus far we haven’t done anything but destroy people…though we might have been a little more ahead of the game if our daughter hadn’t side tracked us with that jackass Celestial scam. Right now we were demi-godly… and if we wanted to become whole we’d have to figure out just what being the Neo Void God was. Light snoozing buzzed into our ears. Sonata breathed softly as she leaned her head against our thick bicep, her moist skin sticking to our chest. We stroked her damp hair a few times before kissing her on top of her head…leaning back and closing our eyes to rest ourselves. She betrayed us…yet we still love her… So all this anger and hatred and rage for being betrayed by her…it’d have to be channeled onto the next available vessels… Poor…poor fools…they’ll never win the belt now…
