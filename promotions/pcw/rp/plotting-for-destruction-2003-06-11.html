Plotting...for Destruction

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/03 'Success is the sole earthly judge of right and wrong.' “You will see him fall!” Silver yells, slamming his fist down on the table to emphasise his point, the two men in front of him jolt back in their seats in fear, each dressed in a cheaply made grey suit. The others in the restaurant, dressed in lighter attire, pay no mind to the table, as they are too caught up in their own thoughts and conversations. Waiters and waitresses pass by, dressed in generic threads, each moving quickly, to get their food to it’s desired location, in hopes they will receive a tip. Most in the area can’t be made out, due to the dimmed lights and the lack of light the candles provide. “Everything is now in motion and soon…you will all see my genius,” Ronchelli barks out, brushing off his black Armani suit, trying to make sure everyone knows just how important and rich he is, the reality of it all though is that no one cares and soon they are just going to forget about him…passing him off as just another face in the large sea of greatness. Dinnertime has passed on the sixth deck of the PCW Airship and late night is quickly approaching. The two associates of Silver stare on, disgruntled that they have to listen to this whole speech again, knowing exactly the outcome. It hasn’t changed the past times Ronchelli has barked it out, but he’s paying them for their services, so they are going to please him. “Silver, are you sure this is the right move though?” the man to his left asks, his voice soft and relaxed, knowing the explosion that’s forthcoming. “This man is a professional…we can’t just charge in there and shot the place up, it will draw to much attention and you can’t outsmart him, he’s far to well off for that,” he stops for a minute, trying to judge Ronchelli’s reaction, reading his face like a book, as he peers into his cold eyes. “All I’m saying is we need to be careful here.” Silver gages his partner for a minute, running his hand through his black hair. “I thought you would have figured this out by now,” he sighs, losing interest in the conversation, faster then a fourteen year old boy does in just about everything. “You think they give the title “Coming Storm” to just anyone? They give that title to the best and brightest stars like myself…the future.” Silver enjoys talking about how good he believes himself to be and it gives me more joy each time he does so. His cohort just looks past him, not wanting to hear anymore of the achievements of Silver, he’s grown tired of the man repeating the same worthless facts over and over again, about how he’s better then Matt Jackson and how he’s going to crush the man, because he has the desire. The two don’t believe a word of it, knowing that it’s just Ronchelli’s way of trying to make himself look better then he really should be considered, it’s just his way of getting past that fact that he doesn’t have the talent needed, but he can put on the mask to make it look like he does. “Sir, I fully understand the reason you were given the title, but I’m just not sure you have the experience needed to beat this man,” he says, his voice full of resentment and dismay, but he keeps looking interested. “This is a very serious matter, you are planning on killing a man, who for all we know might not exist…this is his game Silver and you are still learning the rules.” “I’ve have enough of your bane talk you son of a bitch,” Silver says matter-of-factly. “You don’t have eyes for the grand plan and for that I apologize, to you and everyone else, because the true genies it holds, is like the man that created the tire, it’s that good and you should almost be thanking me for letting you play a part in it,” he tells them, a small smirk painted on his face. “You need to stay on your toes,” he begins. Leaning over the glossy wood table, knocking the glass salt and peppershakers, paying no mind to the crashing sound it makes in the relatively silent room. “We are going to strike very soon…the time is getting closer and very soon, he will be nothing more then a faded star.” “Have you made the proper preparations for the charge sir?” the man on his left asks, hoping the answer will be different then the one he knows Silver is going to give him, but he just nods knowing that they are getting paid and the future of their jobs depends on this whole plan. “Of course I am prepared…who in the hell would ask a question like that…to Silver Ronchelli,” his voice booms, sending the men across from him into the state of fear they were in previous. “You think Matt Jackson is going to crush Silver? IT’S NOT GOING TO HAPPEN! I have studied Matt, I would almost go as far as to say need be I could copy his style…we don’t have anytime to waste, tonight part one of Operation Rotten goes into play!” ~>……
