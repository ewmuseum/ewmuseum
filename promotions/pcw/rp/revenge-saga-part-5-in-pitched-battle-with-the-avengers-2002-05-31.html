Revenge Saga Part 5: In Pitched Battle... with the Avengers

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/5/02 Revenge Saga Part 5: The Avenger Encounter… Since early that very morning, Crichton had been challenging himself in the most extreme ways known to man. He had strolled into a biker’s bar, stood on a table and declared it a car bar. This however, turned out not be a challenge, much less an extreme challenge. The brawl was over very quickly and at the end of it only Crichton was conscious, never mind upright. His second extreme challenge ended moments before the Avengers arrived. The battle with an entire karate class was more what he expected the extreme title match on Friday Fusion to be like. For it had the main components of an extreme match, the sneaky tactics and the foreign objects. Still the challenge wasn’t up to much, perhaps if chose to fight Karate class, whose average age was above 16; he might have received a better challenge. Crichton’s knee-long leather jacket complimented his body greatly, appearing to be skin tight, yet allowing him the space to move freely and therefore not hinder him when action was necessary. He stood arms by his sides, unconscious bodies, in the not too distant background, waiting for the inevitable battle to commence. He surely wasn’t going to give himself up and go peacefully with them, at least not before implementing his revenge upon Miss Zemoff and Dan Lyons. Plus a surviving a confrontation with ‘Earth’s Mightiest Heroes’ would be an extreme challenge that is almost impossible to accomplish. Crichton was thrown out of his train of thought as he realised that the Avenger known as Captain America was saying something. Crichton simply looked up, now fully withdrawn from his thought, and gave look, which forced Cap to repeat his last statement. “So Crichton, we will give you one final chance, to come with us quietly, then we will be forced to take you down.” Cap stated. Crichton simply looked at the four heroes in front of him, not saying a word and not changing the bland expression upon his face. “We realise that you feel you need to extract some kind of revenge upon us, for the death of Aeryn, but we’re doing this to help you! Crichton your wanted for a number of crimes and your the prime suspect in a murder case involving a well known army General.” The Black Panther states. “We want to help you as a somewhat feeble way, of making up for our involvement in Aeryns death…” Iron Man trails off. These comments did cause Crichton to change his expression, from a very indifferent look to that of bitter and twisted rage. “NO! You don’t get to think about that name or that person, never mind speaking of her!” Crichton declares, very animated indeed. “Crichton, please calm down! I wasn’t there when it happened, but think Crichton, would Aeryn have wanted you to do, some of things you have done recently?” The Scarlet Witch almost pleads. The last comment hit Crichton hard, a kind of feeling he never wanted to experience again, he turns to hide his feelings from his probable foes and punches the wall hard, knocking a few bricks out of place. Then mysteriously his mind clears the feelings away and replaces them with only the familiar thirst for revenge. “We believe that the super soldier serum is affecting your mind, turning you into a twisted being, whose sole intent is to gain revenge. The death of Aeryn provided the serum with a vessel to house this thirst for revenge. We believe when we cure you of this, your mind will return to normal and you will no longer be a malevolent being.” Cap explained carefully. However, the words spoken by Captain America never reached Crichton, the serum clouded his hearing and judgement. He had no knowledge of Cap’s last statement, the serum restricts his perception and in essence controls him. The key to breaking this seemed to be getting over the death of Aeryn and it appeared that the serum’s grasp upon Crichton would never be broken. Crichton turns from facing the wall, back to facing the four Avengers stood before him, with an almost sinister look upon his face. “I came here simply to test my new found abilities and that I have. I wish no further harm to come to the innocents that are in this room, therefore I suggest that you evacuate the building before this gets…rough!” Crichton states, genuinely concerned for the lives of the pupils lay unconscious before him. Captain America simply nods in response. “I guess your right, Iron Man would you do the honours?” Cap replies. Iron Man nods then takes to the air, via rocket power from his armoured boots. He then presses a few buttons on the panel on his wrist. Crichton simply walks to the left, edging out of the way of the unconscious pupils and they’re teacher. Iron Man places his hands in front of him and a light blue, substance exits from his hands and physically lifts the teacher and the pupils off the ground and carefully shepherds them out of the door. “Magnetic field!” Iron Man states in explanation. Once the innocent people are evacuated out of the battlegrounds. Crichton draws closer to his enemies, as Iron Man lands just behind the other three Avengers. A smile is etched upon his face. “Now don’t any of you hold back on me now! This is a challenge for me and expect you all to give it the best you have. It’ll train me up for my extreme title match that I have coming up!” Crichton states cockily. “I’m afraid you will not be making it to any such match Crichton! You will be coming with us, when this little dance is all over!” Captain America states. “I’d like to debate that actually!” Crichton replies sarcastically. “For the last time Crichton, please, think of Aeryn she wouldn’t want this!” The Scarlet Witch pleads in one last ditch effort in the hope of avoiding a confrontation. “I TOLD YOU NEVER TO MENTION THAT NAME!” Crichton explodesd in pure fury, launching into an attack aimed at the Scarlet Witch. Caps reactions are fast for an old man, as he places himself between Crichton and his fellow Avenger, and using Crichton’s own momentum to take him off his feet and judo throw him across the floor. Cap attempts to follow this up with a boot, but Crichton regains his composure and rolls out of the way just as the boot comes down and inpacts with floor. Crichton jumps up to his feet, and throws a haymaker punch directly at Cap. The blow impacts upon the star-spangled Avengers jaw, sending him sliding ten feet across the floor, only stopping as he impacts with the wall. With this The Black Panther pounces arms first at Crichton, resembling his namesake greatly, Crichton thought. However, Crichton composed himself after briefly dealing with Cap, and grabs the incoming Panther by the wrists then placing his foot in the Black Panther’s stomach and using the pounces’ momentum to fall back and flip The Black Panther over. The Black Panther flies through the air resembling an arrow from a bow, but is abruptly stopped by the wall. The Panther falls to the ground in a heap, leaving his body imprint, firmly fixed upon the walls plaster work. Crichton sprang to his feet, as quickly as the Black Panther pounced, with a smile of his face as wide as the building itself. He’d just taken two of Earth’s mightiest heroes out, cleanly, without using any kind of dirty tricks. If nothing else from the battle so far, he had discovered that he was ready to tackle the rich industrialist known as Miss Zemoff and he was damned sure he was ready for the extreme title match on Friday Fusion. Crichton is snapped out of his daydream, by falling bricks, one catches him on the foot, forcing him to dive out of the way of the rest. At first Crichton suspected that Iron Man was the cause of this, but when he raised his head and saw The Scarlet Witch, exorcising her powers, Crichton was put back into place. He really didn’t want to hurt a woman, but his very survival depended upon it. Crichton dived out of the way as the Scarlet Witch forced another section of the roof to collapse upon Crichton’s former position. At the end of his dive, Crichton grabs, one of the wooden sticks, left over from his battle with the karate pupils. “Damn I hate to do this but…” Crichton declares before launching the stick at The Scarlet Witch. Crichton was aiming the throw at anything, he was just doing what he did best, relying on his instincts and hoping for the best. The stick flew through the air and hit The Scarlet Witch right between the eyes, knocking her unconscious instantly. Crichton slowly stands from his keeling position and rubs his coat down a little. “That is enough!” Iron Man begins, his voice booming, “I’ve held back, hoping that you would come to your senses, but it seems that it is out of the question, so I’m forced to put you down!” His voice getting louder and louder. With that Iron Man, once again takes to the air, and begins to barrage Crichton’s position with a series of repulser blasts. Crichton’s heightened agility saw that the initial few did not touch him. Then Iron Man lowers himself, to Crichton’s height, while he’s still regaining his composure and balance from the repulser blasts, then fires one large uni-beam from his chest plate. However, Crichton turns and moves out of the way of the blast, before anything got near him. However, the blasts impact upon the wall, knocked him over and created a massive whole, as it sliced through the bricks and the cement. Iron Man then slowly raises his right arm, aiming the repulser blast directly at Crichton’s head. Knowing that he had no chance of out manoeuvring the blast simply sat there and waited for the inevitable to happen. Then, a red, white and blue blur pushed the ‘Golden Avengers’ right arm in another direct as the repulser blast exits the iron-clad glove and blows yet another hole in the wall. “What the hell are you doing Cap? You heard him yourself, he wanted to be takne down!” Iron Man states. “I know, but doing it by lowering yourself to his level that’s just…” Cap trails off. “…epidemic! He’s gone.” The Black Panther chimes in. They all look towards where Crichton once was and find nothing, but air. “He must have escaped through the hole! Panther see if you can see him!” Cap demands. The Black Panther goes to the hole and looks about, he comes back in a matter of minutes. “No sign of him and nothing to track of him!” Black Panther informs “He caught a lucky break this time, but next time he won’t be so lucky!” Iron Man states storming off.
