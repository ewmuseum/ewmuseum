Head Vs Heart

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered We start with an excerpt from Friday Fusion: ~!Corey: Whoah, whoah, whoah! Look Kano, I'm trying to do the right thing by you here. Maybe you oughta think about this first, I mean, just a few days ago you had no career to defend, but now you've got you're future to look after. You've gotta be thinking about your wife and the security of your future. You've gotta be thinking about the Hall of Fame committee, do you think they're gonna admit some guy who made a huge comeback only for it to be ended when he came up against his younger, more skillful and better looking counterpart? Why dpn't you just save yourself a tonne of embarrasment and walk away with your career intact? Who knows, maybe you'll be able to get home in time to make sour love to your wife before Matlock starts. Williams turns his back and grins as Kano thinks this over. Kano turns to face the entrance and then, with his head to the ground and much disgust from the audience, climbs through the ropes and stands on the apron preparing to leave. However, once he hears the crowd's disgust he looks up to them, his eyes widening. His head begins to move around the crowd, focusing on various individuals who combine to form this sell out crowd. Slowly the people begin to rally behind Kano once again. Kano begins to point up into the audience in the same manner as before, with the same passion as before. 'Kano' chants begin to start up and Kano begins to nod, a small smile coming on his face. Then, with the whole crowd behind him Kano turns to get back into the and... WHAM! Jon Kano goes flying from the apron and into the air before landing with a sick thud on the solid concrete. Corey slips the brass knucks off his right hand and places them back where they came from, the briefcase in the hand of Corey's lawyer, with a sick grin. Williams reaches into the briefcase once again and pulls out a gold plated pen. He unfolds the contract that is still in his left hand and then turns to face the crumpled body of Jon Kano. Pen and paper in hand, he leans over the ropes and yells as close to Kano as he can, as if hoping to penetrate Kano's state of unconsciousness with his words. Corey: Hey Kano! Did you actually think that I was SCARED of you? Did you think that I was AFRAID to put my measurably more successful career against yours? Well I got news for you pal, I wanted this match just as much as you. And whilst my original signing was NOT official.... Williams puts the pen to paper and signs his name above the dotted line, just next to the crossed-out invalid version of his name. Once finished he throws both the pen and paper at Kano's lifeless body. Corey: ....This one is! Williams begins to pace around the ring once again. Corey: So this Sunday, Williams Kano retirement match WILL go on. This time there is no coming back Jon Kano, this time if I win your time will FINALLY be over. And then it will be my time! Mark my words Jon Kano, WHEN I beat you, it will mark the dawn of a new era. WHEN I win, I will no known as 'controversial,' I will no longer be know as 'the pioneer of the new era,' no no. WHEN I win this weekend Jon Kano I will forever be known as the KING....OF THE NEW ERA! Williams holds his arms out wide and closes his eyes for a moment, taking in the boos with much enthusiasm. Once quite finished he leaves the ring and stands over Kano's body. Corey: Hey Kano, seeya Sunday; at the crack of dawn. He drops the mic onto the body of Kano as 'The Future is X-Rated' by Matthew Good-Band surprisingly enough hits over the PA system.!~So there it is, Corey Williams and Jon Kano. For nearly four months Corey Williams has tried to end Jon Kano's career. For three months Kano has continued to survive like he always does and has tried to redeem his first ever PPV loss. However, the ultimate challenge has been laid down. The words that come out of very few mouths in PCW. Retire match. This is it; the first man to submit or be pinned has their career ended. Both Kano and Corey could have their futures die in an instant of three seconds, but that is where the similarities end. Jon Kano is a war ravaged veteran; he has been through it all. Corey Williams is a young lion; he is ready to go through it all and in fact looking forward to what he considers 'it all'. However in Williams' eyes 'it all' is the simple glory of being a professional wrestler. He does not know of the hard work that Jon Kano has put in, the hardship and personal sacrifice he has endured. If Jon Kano's career ends on Sunday, then all of PCW will unite in celebration of one of PCW's true legends. He'll go home to a ticker tape parade and spend his days as a loving Husband and Father. In fact, according to Corey by ending Kano's career he is doing him a favor and bringing him one step closer to a spot in the PCW Hall Of Fame. If Corey Williams' career ends on Sunday, no one will remember him. He will be the man who spent four months preparing for greatness only to fall short of his own hype by losing to Jon Kano's. He will be the man whose career was cut tragically short before he could reach his full potential. Corey Williams will still have his billions; he will always have his billions. But by looking at the billionaire's history, including the founding of CCW Enterprises whose sole purpose is to bring Corey Williams to greatness; we will see that Williams has only ever used this money to further himself along his chosen path. Even the limos, the houses and the swimming pools have always been for supporting his image. Williams' goal has always been to be famous and on everyone's mind, if he can't have that then he has nothing. It is quite obvious that Corey Williams, the new school of PCW, has more to lose than Jon Kano, the old school of PCW. It is hardly a fair gamble. It is hardly do or die for both men, for Kano died a long time ago: this match is his last chance at rebirth. Some might even say that Kano is actually fighting to get his career back from Williams, who stole it in order to add more wood to his fire. At first Williams was forced into this match by Jon Kano as Kano took hold of the future, knowing that he must get rid of Williams in order to get his name back in PCW. Williams was pissed off and rightly so, just as he was about to proclaim himself the King of the Third Era his glory moment was stolen from him in similar fashion to the way he stole Kano's career weeks earlier. But thanks to the expertise of his lawyer, Corey managed to worm his way out of the match. No longer was he forced to defend his career and future of glory, rather.... He chose to of his own free will. Though the signature of Corey's that was forced onto the contract by Jon Kano was deemed invalid, Corey did something entirely strange and signed the contract validly, thus recreating the match for Sunday. Williams then exclaimed that he wanted the match just as much as Kano wanted it, only he wanted it on his own terms. It seemed that once again this was an act of sheer egotism, but is it? When Williams realized he didn't have to wrestle in this match, his values and philosophies were conflicted, on one hand he could save his career on the other he could end Jon Kano's. The logical thing would have been to take the lesser gamble and choose to preserve his own career, after all that has always been the most important thing to him, however Williams decided to go for it all and try to take Jon Kano to the grave. Not out of love for himself but rather out of hatred of Kano. Williams has always claimed that his indifference towards Kano was strictly business; however his disproved this on Tuesday as he chose Kano over himself. This four month ball of hatred towards Kano has been building up and up and up, and much to everyone's surprise, it even overtook Corey's ego. Corey Williams hates Kano more than he loves himself. This was a simple truth that came to Williams after he realized what he had done. He had jeopardized his shining career for a man who he had always claimed to be nothing. This small fact frightened Corey more than anything else in his life. Living on the streets of New York each day with gangster’s and cops after him never scared him, neither did the death threats he received from his racial and sexist comments as 'The Controversial One.' Kano's existence has got him full of fear. Why? Corey's narcissism is something he prides himself on, after all, as he often points out, it's well justified. But Kano stole that from him, Kano made his ego second best. Corey's ego is what fuels his ambition and desire for success. In short, no more ego, no more career. Williams also prides himself on the ability to manipulate others, to make them so obsessed with their hatred of him that their own pathetic lives are put on halt when he does something. The very fact that someone has manipulated him into hating them so much that his career revolves around them is not a feeling Corey enjoys. Kano may enjoy surviving, but Williams does not. He must be the focal point of everyone's lives, including his, or else he does not want to exist at all. He must be flamboyant, charismatic and sometimes even controversial, this is who he is. If Williams' hatred of Kano stops him from being those things, then he will see himself just as he sees Jon Kano, nothing. When it comes to his career, Corey Williams will have an all or nothing mentality. When it comes to this moment of desperation, Jon Kano will have an all or nothing attitude. Corey Williams is all head, Jon Kano is all heart. Who will win?
