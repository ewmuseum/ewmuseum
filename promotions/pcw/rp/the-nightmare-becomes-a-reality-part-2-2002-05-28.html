The Nightmare Becomes a Reality? (part 2)

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28.05.02 The scene opens up in the centre of the doctors surgery. As this is a doctors for rich people the lobby area is completely different than what you would find downtown. There are, large comfy chairs, relaxing music playing in the background, Bonsai tree’s are sitting on windowsills and there is even a small pond with fish and a tiny waterfall in it. Duzza his wife and Jordan walk into the lobby area. Duzza and Jordan find somewhere to sit, while Brittany goes to the reception area. Jordan: “So…how’s life?” Duzza: “I woke up, with white patches on my skin… how do you think my life is?” Jordan: “Aww Duzz, don’t worry about it” Duzza: “Imagine it happened to you… what would you do?” Jordan: “Well, do you mean before or after I cut my hands off?” Duzza: “Your not much help here man” Jordan: “Ahh bro, I’m just playing around” Duzza: “Yeah, well if you ain’t noticed I’m not in a cheery mood” Jordan: “Ok, point taken. It wasn’t fair of me to make fun of the fact that your turning white!” Duzza: “You just want a punch don’t chu?” Jordan: “Haha, I’m sorry it was too easy. I mean, of all the people in the world, you turn white! There are 6 billion people…well like 4 billion if you don’t count white people, and you turn white! What are the chances of that” Duzza: “Ok…now your starting to annoy me” Jordan: “Sorry man…we’re boys, I remember when I was just a lowly PWA’er and we first spoke, and we been speaking like every day since man” Duzza: “Yeah I remember man” Jordan: “You, along with my bro Drew and my boy Rush, I look up to y’all y’know and I don’t wanna dis you heavy bro. I’m just playin” Duzza: “Aight bro, I understand. Just this is a touchy subject bro” Jordan: “Ok man….i got ya” Duzza’s lovely wife/ebony queen Brittany walks over from the reception area and sits down, next to Duzza and takes hold of his glove covered hand. Brittany: “What, you boys talking about then?” Duzza: “Err nothing” Jordan: “Yeah…so..Duzz, hear you got a match against that neo-nazi” Duzza: “What?! You talking bout that cracker you beat up?” Jordan: “If you mean, Makonie then yeah him” Duzza: “Makonie? My little pony? Whatever, he’s a fool. He can take that white supremacy shit and shove it up his ass” Jordan: “I hear he shoves something up his ass every night” Duzza: “Ewwwww” Jordan: “What?!” Duzza: “That’s nasty man. I don’t wanna know about some crackers anal sex loving private life, geez” Jordan: “It was a joke!” Duzza: “Well, it was still nasty..eww eww eww” Jordan: “What now?!” Duzza: “Mental image…eww now I got a mental image of him…argh thanks a lot Jordan” Jordan: “Man….mental images of a gay nazi and your turning white! You must be having a bad day” Duzza: “Yeah….and it’s gonna get worse…I can sense it” Just then a announcment comes over the tannoy Voice: “Dr Jones is now ready to see patient Mr Williams” Duzza: “A’ight, that’s me, be back soon bro” Jordan: “ok man, good luck” Duzza and Brittany turn from Jordan and walk into the doctors office. The scene fades to black then re-opens in the office. The walls are painted white. In one corner there is a small bed. On the walls there are cabinets of medicines on one shelf and medical books on the other. The doctor is a average sized, average looking white guy, with dark brown hair and glasses is sitting behind a desk.. He greets Duzza with handshake and shows them a seat. Doctor: “Ahh Malek and Brittany, how is the happy couple?” Duzza: “Not too happy about being here, that’s for damn sure” Doctor: “Ok, let’s make it quick then, how can I help?” Duzza: “Well..this is kinda embarrassing but….well…err…” Brittany: “Just tell the man Malek” Doctor: “Yes, Malek, tell me” Duzza: “Well…I woke up with….white patches on my hand” Doctor: “Ooooooh” Duzza: “So doc, can ya help me?” Doctor: “Ok, well remove your gloves and let me see the skin” Duzza removes his gloves and shows the white patches. The doctor has a look of concern on his face Duzza: “What? What is it?” Doctor: “Hmm… I’ll need to run some more tests before I make a decision” Brittany: “So that means there’s a chance that…it could be…Michael Jackson disease?” Doctor: “Well, we in the medical industry tend to call it vitiligo, but yes….there could be a chance he has it” Duzza: “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO” The scene fades to black as Duzza starts to see his life pass before his very eyes.(End of Part 2)
