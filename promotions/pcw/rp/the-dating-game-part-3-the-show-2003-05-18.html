The Dating Game Part 3 (The show)

18 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/5/03 Director: And we are live in 5 4 3 2 1 The camera’s whirred into life as Richard the presenter of the show ‘the dating game’ pranced down the stairs, his suit had clearly seen better days as the creases showed, his black hair which had been dyed glistened under the lights of the studio. The crowd was cheering frantically a few random chants of ‘woo woo woo’ could be heard which did not help the nerves of James Edwards who was sat in the first seat of three, next to him were the other celebrities who had been signed up for the show, Henry Winkler and David Schwimmer. It was an interesting mix and one that James was not sure how to deal with, he was facing the Fonz…well a twenty year older Fonz and the most annoying man on television who somehow got to kiss Jennifer Aniston nearly every week for seven years. James shot an evil glare across at David who simply smiled a half hearted smile in the direction of James, the camera’s picking up every movement as the applause finally died down allowing Richard the presenter formally known as Luthar to begin the show. Richard: Hello and welcome to a special celebrity edition of the dating game, behind the screen behind me we have three of the biggest celebrities in the world and backstage we have one lucky lady who will narrow them down to one. The audience broke into a rapturous applause once more, Richard lapping it up like a dog who was being praised simply for fetching a stick, all he was doing was reading off an autocue and the audience was eating every word of it. James’ head sunk a bit taking in Richard’s words ‘three of the biggest celebrities in the world’, there were crimes about fraud, in his hay day maybe the Fonz was one of the worlds biggest celebrities, maybe in the mid nineteen nineties David Schwimmer could have been considered a major celebrity but never would he ever been considered a celebrity all he did was fight people twice a week if he was lucky enough to be booked, there was going to be a riot if he was picked, celebrity pfft yeah righ. Richard: So without further adieu let’s bring out Michelle. The crowd breaks into cheers again like a batch of trained monkeys as a small, slim and extremely sexy brunette takes a delicate step from backstage before walking slowly down the curved steps her hair bobbing up and down as she took the steps upon reaching the bottom she pulls down her incredibly small black dress slightly before hopping up onto the stool that is positioned next to Richard who is clearly having trouble controlling himself with a small speck of drool creeping down the side of his mouth. A few wolf whistles from the crowd and continued cheering cause Michelle to blush slightly and lower her head trying to pretend that she was not there, or at least she wished that she was not being filmed. Richard: Ok settle down folks we all know she’s a beauty. Michelle I’m sure you’ve watched the program before, what you need to do is ask our celebrities a question and then judging from their answers pick which one you want to be your date for the evening. Michelle nodded clutching tightly to her pre-written questions that the shows writers had probably spent the best part of an hour working on, similarly behind the screen James, ‘The Fonz’ Henry Winkler and David Schwimmer were all glancing at ‘their’ answers which had been scribbled on a piece of paper, trying to memorise them a look of disgust filled James’ face as he read his supposed answer to question number one and shook his head, he had signed a contract he had to read this rubbish, the guys in PCW would never be able to look at him in the same light again. Richard: Ok Michelle take it away with your first question. Michelle: Hi guys. Henry & David: Hi Michelle. James glances sideways to look at Henry and David shaking his head from side to side not believing the puppet show that he had become a part of in the name of money. Michelle: My first question is to all of you. If I was to invite you home for coffee after our date and maybe more, what would be your coffee and what would you do? The camera pans round the screen to reveal the celebrities sitting there all preparing to deliver their respective answers, the camera zooms in on the chiselled face of Henry Winkler first who winks mischievously at the camera much to the disgust of James who lets out an audible tut and sigh. Henry: Well Michelle first of all I’d just like to say I wouldn’t need a coffee as I’m already high on life like this one time I was so high on life I jumped over a shark on my motorcycle yeah, so here’s what I’d do we’d forget the coffee and then and put you on the back of my chopper and BAM…eyyyyyyyyyy Henry once again reverts into his old persona of the Fonz as the crowd cheers at the catchphrase which once made him famous in the nineteen seventies but now has little more effect than making him look like an old letch. The camera focuses on Henry for a bit more until it pans right and is met with the face of David Schwimmer who grins like a moron into the camera giving a little wave as he does so. David: Well…errrrrr, I spend a lot of my time in a …errrr coffee house so I’d know exactly what sort of errrr coffee to get you and then maybe we can open a bottle of wine and we can just talk and maybe become good friends. The crowd lets out a slight jeer after not getting the smut that they had prepared themselves for resulting in a small smug grin to form on James’ face as the camera pans left once more focusing in on James’ face his green eyes glowing brightly until he glances down at the piece of paper and sighs heavily. James: Hi Michelle, I’d have an espresso so that when my lunar module hit your black hole…you’d see the Milky Way. The crowd breaks out into hollering and cheering as the smuttiest remark of the show so far had been said resulting in James burying his head in his hands mumbling something incoherently as the camera zoomed back round in front of the screen to show the blushing face of Michelle and the smug sly grin of Richard. Richard: Well now you’ve heard the answers you must choose. Will it be number one the man who claims to have jumped over a shark on a motorbike? Henry: Eyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy Richard: Will it be number two some moron that clearly didn’t read his script and just wants to be friends with one of the most attractive women I have ever seen. James glances over at David leans in and quietly whispers in his ear. James: Ha I kicked your ass. Richard: Or will it be number three the man who thinks that he could fly you to the moon with his lunar module? The choice Michelle is yours. The crowd breaks out into a frenzy of voices calling a variety of numbers from the options available the majority shouting out number three, James’ number. Richard beckons the audience to quiet which they do as the camera zooms in on Michelle as she is forced to make her final decision. Michelle: Well, I’m going to have to go with the common choice here and go with… number three. James stands up triumphantly, maintaining his charade for the cameras, which he knew, would be on him at the present moment in time. Composing himself he steps out from behind the screen and hugs Michelle who looks less than impressed. Michelle: Who are you? Richard: That’s all we have time for today on the dating game be sure to join us next week when we get to hear all about Michelle and James’ date, I have been Richard Noblesman and I will see you next week. Michelle: No seriously who is he? Director: And cut. Great show people, that’s a wrap. Michelle looks stonily at Richard for a while before looking James up and down noticing his physique a bit and that whilst he is not a world famous celebrity, he’s not that bad of a looker. Richard: We’re so sorry Michelle if the contestants had stuck to the script then you would have chosen number two, his name is James and he’s… Michelle looks the now blushing James up and down once more before interrupting Richard. Michelle: He’s my date for this evening, come along James. With that Michelle clasps James’ hand before leading him off backstage leaving Richard alone on the set calling out after the two of them Richard: I’m sure you’ll have a great time together, that James, he’s the best damn rodeo rider there is!
