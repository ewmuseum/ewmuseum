Hanging Out At The Mall

16 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 16, 2003 [THE MALL, is a gnarly new mega complex filled with tons of bodacious stores. Five levels of greatness. A food court that could give any man a heart attack. A bitchin' arcade with that totally excellent boxing simulation game...you know...the one where you put on the gloves and box. It's a RIGHTEOUS workout. Babes, dudes, and bums walk around the mall trying to take away all their most heinous problems. Don't deny it, dudes and dudettes, you go to the mall to get away. You buy that bodacious MP3 player because your depressed about your job, family life, or just depressed because it's gnarly. Then there is 'Totally Tubular' Tommy the guy who hasn't spent much time with depression, because there really isn't any need to do it. Ever since birth he's had a fairly easy life with his father being a successful professional wrestler, money wasn't exactly a problem. Then the surfing thing, which is a gift, took Tommy to new heights and gave him the vast fortune that he has today. He still has cash pouring into his bank account and he doesn't even know how it's happening. Could be the profits from Tubular Tan or Tubular, For Men. Could be the gnarly profits of the Tubular Tiki coffee houses, that have now expanded to two states. It could be those bodacious stocks this Wall Street compadre was babbling about, but he's been hearing heinous things about that lately. Money is not a problem, and neither are babes. He's always had a most excellent companion to share his romantic needs with, just something that came with the territory. A professional surfer with good looks and a great personality...of course something bodacious was going to come from it. Whether people like it or not, Tommy did work for what he has now. His father paved the way for Tommy to have top notch education and surfing lessons...then The Tubular One took care of the rest. Now he's going to make up to his father, even though he is passed away. He's going to wrestle at a professional level and engrave the name of Tubular into the perils of wrestling history. When they say 'wrestling', 'Totally Tubular' Tommy will come out of their mouths. Then shortly thereafter, they will talk about the radical compadre who produced the Tubular One, Radical Randy] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: I'm thinking that I should go most 80's retro for my new threads. [Harry smiles and nods his head. Sporting an Iron Maiden T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots...this scruffy long haired man with the nickname of 'Heavy Metal' will only agree with Tommy's 80's suggestion. There will be a mystery opponent this Friday and nobody knows who this person can be. Tommy just knows that whoever this totally heinous compadres is, that he's going to go down big time. If it's a former PCW Champion who has set many records, then so be it. Tommy will only cruise through him with the greatest of ease showing that he truly is the greatest World Champion in the history of PCW.] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [The two men make their way past Kay-B-Toy Store, Spencers Gift, and a bodacious Japanese pottery place. The name doesn't matter, just that it has bodacious Japanese pottery with half naked Asian women on them. That's right, half naked Asian women] TTT: It's going to be a most busy time for me, Har. I've got a mystery opponent on Friday. It doesn't really even matter who it is dude because I'm just going to walk right through this compadre. All this person will be to me is just some totally bogus gnat flying around and all that I have to do is crush it. [A group of teenage girls giggle madly as Tommy walks by with his tanned chest exposed. His Hawaiian dress shirt buttoned up a little bit. Tommy knows this, and finds a very weird satisfaction out of babes enjoying his body. He's doing them a favor, really] Harry: Did you see that one chick with the tight belly shirt on, man? TTT: Of course I did, but they are way out of our age range, compadre. [Harry holds his head down feeling like a scolded child. Age range or not that chick was far out in a major way...just a night of smokin' out of Jerome Bongus and doing the horizontal mambo on Harry Junior and she'd be in love with him] TTT: This looks like a gnarly place... [Tommy stops at a store called, Gadzooks. A couple bodacious babes, who work there, are at the entrance looks...well, bodacious] Blonde girl: Hi, there. Is there anything in particular that your looking for? [Tommy gives the girl one of his one million dollar smiles, showing his perfect white teeth] TTT: I need something 80's retro, babe. I'm going to need to be most pimped out for my victory party after Friday Fusion. [The girl smiles and guides Tommy through the women's clothing. Mostly skimpy tops that barely cover the breasts, showing off their stomach's, showing the skin, which leads to men thinking about sex right away. Which makes Tommy think...if women REALLY don't want men to be pigs then why with the skimpy clothes? If women just show off skin and look sexy how can a compadre not think of sexual activity? It doesn't make sense...as Tommy finishes his thoughts the blonde girl with the cheetah-like tube top and tight blue jeans that are work out on her ass, shows Tommy T-shirts and ripped up blue jeans that are on sale] Blonde girl: This would go SOOO good on you. These TIGHT blue jeans with a studded leather belt, mmmmm, you'll look so sexy. [Tommy looks at the jeans in the mirror, putting them over his legs and smiles. He gives himself a thumbs up as Harry looks at the torn blue jeans with an orgasmic stare. He's found his Graceland!!] Harry: Far out..... [Tommy disappears into one of the dressing rooms where his head and feet can only be seen. He throws his Hawaiian shirt on the blue door to throw on the AC/DC T-shirt. Harry looks through the discount rack and has found a treasure. Something that must have been overlooked by some poor sap who truly doesn't know style. Some unlucky soul forgot to grab the rare Twisted Sister 'Evil Has No Boundaries' Tour T-shirt!!! It will make Harry's collection of T-shirt's complete and finally he can fully represent Twisted Sister in his cover band....even though he is the only one in it so far] TTT: Harry, how do I look, dude? [Harry can't look up from the far out Twisted Sister T-shirt, the missing gem from his collection] TTT: Dude...how do I look? [Harry CAN'T look up and Tommy doesn't understand it] TTT: HARRY!!!! Wake up, dude! [Tommy is knocking on Harry's hard head, trying to get him to look up. That's when Tommy notices the Twisted Sister symbol on the T-shirt and now realizes Harry's stare. He has finally found that one totally bodacious tour T-shirt that he's been looking for. It's about time because Tommy was getting most tired of hearing about it] TTT: Congrats, compadre. I'll buy it for you...now how do I look? [Harry slowly looks up, trying his hardest to keep his head up. The images of Dee Snider slipping from his mind already, he can't let it happen. His head falls downward and he begins staring at the shirt again. Tommy sighs and looks up to the ceiling wondering why he even bothers. The blond girl who greeted them decides to offer her services] Blond girl: You look SOOO sexy!! I bet you could wrap bubble paper around yourself and look sexy...mmmmmm. [Tommy thinks about that for a second, and then he nods, knowing that he could make that sexy. Just so Harry didn't try and pop the bubbles, that would be most bogus] TTT: I'm totally taking this...I'm going to be truly pimpin' it tubular style at my victory party!! [The blond girl nods with his right index finger hanging on her pink, lucious lower lip. Tommy, who is decked out in his torn blue jeans, black studded belt, AC/DC T-shirt, and black spiked wrist bands, has no clue that this babe is digging on him] Blonde girl: Mmmmm, I could just eat you up like Campbell's soup. [Tommy isn't paying attention, he's too busy looking at himself in the mirror. He notices his large bicep muscles stretching out the bodacious AC/DC T-shirt, making him look much larger than he already is. The babes are going to be digging on him at this party. He could probably have ten or twenty babes going at once. THIS IS GOING TO BE BODACIOUS!] Harry: Twisted...Sister...man...... TTT: Babe, your looking at the greatest PCW Champion of ALL TIME!! Blonde Girl: MMMMM.... [Fade to black]
