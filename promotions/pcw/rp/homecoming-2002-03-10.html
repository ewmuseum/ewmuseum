Homecoming.

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/10/02 The door opens inside to Stately Steele Manor, where we rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he makes his entrance into the foyer. Trusted manager Sue Saksnussum stumbles inside, hair a mess. Both show signs of battle in his torn toga and various scratches covering their exposed skin. Steele takes in a deep breath and tosses aside the ‘Abandoned’ sign that once was nailed to the front door. Exhaling, he chokes on the noxious smells inside. Big Alan Steele: “By the gods! First attacked by the hoary denizens of a lawn gone evil and now, once inside the once plush confines of my hedonistic masterpiece I must fill by lungs with the stench of death!” Sue: (brushing twigs out of her hair) “Yeah, that lawn is a mess. I thought the will-o-wisps highly unnecessary myself… I thought you hired someone to take care of that?” But the big man does not immediately answer, his steely eyes locked in the next room. BAS: “You there! Make haste from my abode at once or suffer the wrath of Big Alan Steele!” A long haired and grimy man hurried gathers up a few belongings from around a crackling fire in a metal barrel and dashes past the two and disappears into the green beyond the door. Sue shakes her head sadly as she closes the door to avoid hearing the mannish screams and the snapping of bones now coming from the ‘lawn.’ Sue: “Man, I can’t stand that Shawn Prodigy!!” BAS: “Like vermin looking for shelter, I dislike him not for his survival instincts. He simply has less to work with that most humans.” Sue: (holding nose and waving hand in the air) “Well, it’s gonna take a good couple of weeks to get his smell out of here.” BAS: “Alas it is unfortunate, and despite all the shortcomings, tis good to be home again!” Sue: (sarcasm) “Oh yeah, I mean what a joy it is to come home to an empty house smelling like dead bodies with a lawn that could take out an entire Hvitos tribe. Much better than our vacation in Barbados.” BAS: “Vacation?!?! Twas merely a slight relocation of my training and justice imbibing tendencies! Crime was down over 400%!” Sue: “Coulda swore it was to recover from the injuries you sustained from that fall Chef forced upon you.” BAS: (distant look) “Wherever I go I spread the goodness of Olympia… its just that the criminal element in Barbados was more conducive to my, er, weakened state.” Sue: (sulking) “I wish we were STILL in Barbados.” BAS: (frowning) “Well, I doubt we would be welcome after your small cigarette incident…” Flashback to a gorgeous hotel resort… in flames. Sue: (indignant) “Yeah, well, it was their own fault. They ran out of Newports.” BAS: (pointing a finger at her) “And thusly is why you shall never again seek solace in the smoke of tobacco products! Besides, you set bad examples for the little children at ringside PCW events!” Sue: “Bad example?! Why, because I enjoy the occasional smoke or two during your matches?!” BAS: “No, because you pass them out like hard candy.” The front door opens wide and one of Big Alan Steele’s personal dance troupe, known to PCW fans as the Graces, falls through the opening, raising her head and throwing her hand to her forehead in agony. Steffi: “I made it! I am… alive! You know, the lawn could use a mowing. I thought you had someone take care of that?” BAS: “But I hired a man of great competence! I know not the reasons of his failure.” The blonde and beautiful Grace gets up and dusts herself off, her histrionics over. Steffi: “Well, I personally thought the man-eating Venus Flytrap was particularly in bad taste. What’s for dinner.” Sue: “We don’t know yet, we want everyone to get inside first.” BAS: (rubbing chin thoughtfully) “Perhaps I should go back into the forest of Death that has become my front lawn and save the others?” Sue: “Naw. This’ll be a good chance to thin out the cast. Besides, you have a match to train for! Juan Borro, one of Chef’s good ole boys, is just around the corner.” BAS: (grinding teeth together) “And soon he shall know the bitter taste of brimstone, once I send his bloodied carcass to Lord Hades domain…” The door opens again and two more blondes scamper through into the safety of the Manor. Huffing and puffing, Cindi and Jodi look wet and covered with grit. Jodi: “Hey, was there any reason to put a quicksand marsh where the driveway was? Who did the landscaping while we were gone?” Cindi: “Yeah, and what IS the deal with the trees and their vines that snake around you, trying to strangle you? It could really use a mow out there…” Sue: (impatient) “Yeah, we know.” BAS: (thoughtfully) “I must have a talk with that Greens Keeper…” Once again, the door opens, this time four blondes scurry in. Steele closes the door behind the frightened four… and as soon as the door is shut tight, a small explosion on the top half of the wood sends splinters flying through the room, causing everyone to duck in self-preservation. The bloodied head of the Sado-Masochist, Big Al’s crime fighting partner, sticks unceremoniously through. Sado-Masochist: “Let me in! Let me in!!” Big Al grabs a hold of S&M’s ears and roughly pulls him through the door, tossing his limp body to the side with a casual shrug. Sounds of wild beats and jungle sounds roar through the gaping hole. The girls run over to him and begin tending to his many, many wounds. S&M merely groans. Terri: “I think it was the shambling mounds that finally got him!” S&M: “The lawn could… could use a mowing…” Sue: “Yeah, we know it. Damn, I could use a cigarette.” BAS: (sternly) “No nicotine ever again! Tis the demon’s drug!” Sue: (yelling) “But how will I make it through your matches! I’m usually bored stupid at those damn PCW shows!!” BAS: “Perhaps you should tend to researching this dastardly Juan Borro that I shall be facing within the fortnight... but no carcinogenic agents!” Sue: (whining) “But I love them!” BAS: “No more! Hi-Keeba!” Sue leaves in a huff to the kitchen area. Big Al shakes his head sadly at her as he helps up the Sado-Masochist. S&M: “Damn, Big Al. Did you know that there is a whole tribe of dryads out there?! What the heck is going on around here?!” BAS: (calming tones) “All questions shall be answered and all fears allayed. Though, I wouldn’t have to go far for training anymore…” Candi: “Still, it would be nice to go up the walkway without being attacked by wood sprites.” They group makes their way to the kitchen, where Sue is busily working on something. BAS: “How goes the research?” Sue: “I would, but I just realized that there are more important matters to attend to than catching his promos; something more exciting and more immediately pressing to our future. Like reorganizing these bullion cubes. Just seemed more important.” Kelli: “Oooo, I hate it when the chicken gets mixed in with the beef!” Sue: “Shut up. Say… weren’t there eight of you Graces before?” Suzi: “Um, I don’t know. None of us can count that high.” Sue: “And I can’t say I ever really noticed. Oh well, better get back to those bullion cubes.” BAS: (flexing muscles) “And I suppose I should head down into our training facilities?” Sue: “(shrugging) “Couldn’t hurt, I guess. Just don’t over-do it. Remember, it IS a former soccer player, and a finesse one at that. This shouldn’t be too hard. Just hit him and he’ll start rolling around like he was shot by a CIA sniper.” BAS: “Need help sorting?” Sue: (scratching head) “Naw. But I keep having this nagging feeling we’ve forgotten something…” Cut to Barbados, where a beautiful blonde with a slight tinge of red in her hair looks around t5he beach in desperation. Gerri: “Big Al? Sue?! GIRLS?!?! Where is everybody?!”
