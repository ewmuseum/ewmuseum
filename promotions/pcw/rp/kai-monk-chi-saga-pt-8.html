Kai-Monk-Chi (Saga pt.8)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th November Deuce 000 Deuce § Kai stood there in the darkness with his back turned to Cami liu. His head lowered. Why did he feel like he lost a piece of his heart? He had turned down many other fighters who had asked to him to train them. There was the girl from Japan, who was the most persistent. She would constantly follow him around professing that she was in love with him, and wanted to learn from him. He did not feel any remorse like this when he politely explained to her that he was not ready to take on any students. Why was telling her so hard for him? After what seemed an eternity passed, Cami liu straightened and wiped her eyes. 'I am sorry to have bothered you Kai,' she said softly, trying to regain her composure. 'I did not mean to burden you with my problems.' 'Good luck to you on your travels.' she said with a tear strained voice. She started to walk to the door on the opposite side of the room. Kai felt as if a part of him was leaving that he would never be able to reclaim. He knew she wanted to know the knowledge to take revenge for her father. She was full of pain and sadness, and this driving goal to destroy Radar. 'I know it is wrong to follow this path, it only leads to destruction.' He mused to himself. 'But I can't stand the thought of her getting hurt.' He had caught a glimpse into her soul when he gazed into her eyes. Aside from the pain of losing her father. He saw his own likeness in there constantly alone and on the move. However, where he had steadfast friends like Ben, he felt as if she had none. She was alone, and now he had turned his back on her when she thought he would be able to help her. 'Ca...Cami liu... wait!' he said, walking after her. She stopped just short of the door, and turned to face him. 'I.... I will teach you all that I know about the mastery of chi. But.... you must promise me that you will not throw away your life seeking revenge.” Cami liu's eyes started to water again. She ran and jumped at Kai giving the startled Shotokan fighter a hug and kiss on the cheek, and then burying her head into his chest. 'Thank you Kai!!! I knew you would help me!' she started to cry still holding her arms around his neck with her body pressed against his. He slowly brought up his arms to embrace her. If it was not for the darkness that they were standing in, you could see Kai turn red with embarrassment. Here he was holding this beautiful woman he just only met. Nevertheless, he felt relieved that she was happy now. Meanwhile.... Ben was collecting his money from the bookie, since the fights were abruptly cancelled due to most everyone tearing out of the door after Kai. 'Come on Dan! We got to go find out if Kai is alright.' Ben said heading for the door. 'Of course he is alright!' Dan said angrily. 'He is always ALRIGHT! Sheese... Hey! Wait up!' Dan and Ben left the building, Dan was still grumbling about how the night went. 'Well, I don't think we will be able to hail a taxi around here.' Ben said. 'I had to pay the driver who dropped up off an extra $200.00 just so he would. Looks like we’re walking back.” The two fighters walked down a couple of streets, still searching as they went for their lost comrade. When they were perhaps a mile away from the arena, they saw Kai leaving a building...'but wait!!' Ben thought to himself. 'That guy must have hit me harder than I thought! Is he with a girl??' he said without thinking. 'What? Oh, man!! WHAT IS IT ABOUT HIM?' Dan said rather loudly. 'Why do all the chicks flock to him?' 'Quiet Dan!' Ben warned. 'Hey! Kai! You okay pal?' Ben shouted as he ran up to him. 'I am okay Ben.' Kai replied to his friend. 'That was quite a crowd that came after you.' Ben laughed. 'Yes, I managed to elude them all except for this young lady.' Kai said stepping to the side. 'Hi! My name is Cami liu,” she said shaking Ben's hand. 'Ben... Ben Thompson' he replied. 'You are quite a skilled fighter Cami liu, your fight was spectacular! In addition, you managed to corner this guy here. That is a difficult task in it's self.' Ben laughed punching Kai's shoulder. 'Yes, it was tough indeed.' She said looking up at him and giving a squeeze on Kai's arm she was holding in her own.' Kai was now turning a brighter shade of red. Dan noticed Kai's uncomfortable situation, and decided to turn up the heat on him a little. 'Hey! Kai! Why are you tur....'*OOMMMPHHH! * Dan was cut off from Ben's elbow smashing into his stomach. 'What was that Dan? We didn't quite hear you. This is an old friend of ours. He holds the record for the fastest Knockout... his own.' Ben snickered. 'HA...” Dan said sarcastically. I am heading back to my room now. Dan said walking off. 'You staying anywhere around here Cami liu?' Ben asked turning his attention back to her. 'I have a room over close to the airport.' She replied. 'Well, We have the top floor over at the Ritz, you are welcome to stay there if you like. Airport hotels are so small and noisy. Besides, it looks like you two have some 'things' to discuss.' Ben said with an evil grin towards Kai. 'If it would not be to much trouble.' She said with a smile and another squeeze on Kai's arm. 'Nope, no trouble at all. C'mon, we'll head back and grab a cab over to get your things. Then grab some dinner. I don't know about you two, but I am hungry!' Ben smiled. They finally made it back to the hotel and had gotten Cami liu settled into one of the adjoining rooms of the top floor and were ready to go eat. Dan declined to go saying he had other things he wanted to look into for the night and left. The three went to a small little restaurant that was located a block down the street from where they were staying. Kai was quite during most of the meal as Ben and Cami liu talked like old school chums. 'So, you wish to learn the ways of the Chi mastery?' Ben asked sipping his wine. 'Yes, that is why I have been looking for Kai here for a couple of years now.' She replied glancing at Kai. 'Well, I don't think you will find a better teacher anywhere. When we trained together with the Grandmaster in China, if it was not for my old buddy here, I don't think I would know it either.' 'All the other lessons we were taught I picked up on ok...' Ben continued. '...But when we started to learn that, I just could not do it. Kai picked up on it fast. It surprised Master how fast he was able to wield it. I thought I would never learn it, but Kai took the time to help Me.,” he said with a smile towards Kai. 'I don't think I would find a better teacher anywhere either,” she said also smiling at Kai. 'When I was entering the different tournaments, I had heard some pretty incredible stories about the man who beat Saga.' 'If you will both excuse me, I need to get some air and retire for the night.' Kai said as he slowly got up. 'Okay, pal. We'll see you tomorrow morning then.' Ben said as he took another drink. 'Good night Kai.' Cami liu called out to him. 'Good night Cami liu' Kai replied, as he walked towards the door. She followed his every movement to the door until he disappeared into the night. Ben politely cleared his throat, which snapped her back from her dreamy gaze. 'You know, he really likes you.' Ben said putting his glass back down on the table, which was instantly refilled by the waiter. Cami liu blushed at this. 'I've known him almost all of my life.' 'When we first left the dojo to travel the world years back, we meet many women who tried to capture his attention, but he never gave them a second glance. It's always been the 'Fight' for him. I really can't blame him, he was raised in a Shaolin monastery in Jinan.' 'He is very naive about the world, but he has so much passion for the 'Fight' I've always wondered if anything else mattered to him. There have been many who have asked him to train them, and he has refused them all.' 'Re... Really?' she asked. 'Yup! Somehow you are special, you have gotten to him somewhat. You do not have to answer this if you do not want to, but...please excuse me for being blunt. I've seen the way you look at him. Do you like him?' Ben said taking a large gulp of wine. 'I...' she turned a deep shade of red, adverting her gaze from his. 'I'm sorry to have embarrassed you.' Ben apologized. 'You don't have to say anything. I just see so much of him in you.' ' I... I am attracted to him,” she said slowly, looking into her glass. 'I don't know what it is about him, I've heard all these stories about what a fierce fighter he was, but he is nothing what I expected. He is gentle and so unbelievable shy.' 'I know...' Ben replied. 'Well, it's getting late, we better head back.' Ben paid the bill and they both headed back to the hotel.
