Kano is here, Nelson is queer!!!

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/2002 ~What the f*ck is going on in the world today? Has there ever been a question more asked in human history? Probably not. This question is rhetorical, it can never be answered because there are too many reasons for each individual's actions. Take, for instance, the case of Andrea Yates. This woman drowned her five children, with the oldest being eight years of age. The whole world was shocked by this crime last summer when was shown on the news. No one could believe how this woman could kill her own children. What reason did she give? If she killed her five children, Satan would be vanquished from her home. Now her trial has begun, and her husband is trying to defend her. I don't care if she was possessed by the devil himself, she should still fry. She killed five people whom she gave life. This bond between a mother and child is supposed to be an unbreakable bond, yet this woman broke it five times over in a violent and malicious way. There are people in third world countries starving to death because there is not enough food to go around in that particular country, while in America, you look down every street in America there are fat people, not just chubby, but disgusting fatbodies. This is not a shot at America, just a statement of the facts. In the history of PCW, there have been many undeserving PCW World Champions. People like Stewart Hix, The Preacher, and Stryfe come to mind as people who have taken the PCW World Title and soiled its good name. Jon Kano is not one of these people, mostly because he possesses the skills needed to be not only a PCW World Champion, but a LEGENDARY PCW World Champion. He has the in-ring ability. He has the charisma oozing off of him. He can hold a crowd in the palm of his hand at any time he chooses. Why shouldn't Kano be the World Champion? Good question.~ *The scene opens backstage just a few hours before PCW's March Madness (Yes, you still have time to order, only on Pay-Per-View) goes on the air. Jon Kano is seen entering the backstage area, as he has just arrived. Kano is dressed in a vibrant blue Armani suit with a black silk tie and black Gucci shoes. He is carrying a bag strapped onto his shoulder and is wheeling a luggage bag on wheels behind him. Jon Kano is headed towards his locker room until he is stopped by Cal Seaver, in all of his K-mart dressed glory, and with a microphone in hand.* Cal: Hey, Jon. I was wondering if I could get a few words for PCW Magazine. Tonight is a big night in the careers of twenty men involved in the battle royal for the most prestigious title in the wrestling industry, the PCW World Title. Kano: Sir Calvin of the Seavers, let me stop you right there. First, it is a big night in the careers of nineteen men. I have a strong feeling that our beloved CEO, Tyler Nelson, is not going to make here tonight. I think he is a little busy with taking it up the ass from Bruno the prison pimp and all. Cal: Mr. Nelson is going to kill you when he gets out. Kano: My cousin works at the Department of Justice, he doesn't think Tyler is going to be a problem for the civilized world for the next fifteen to forty years. So that leaves nineteen men that I have to go through. Cal: You seem confident. Kano: Damn straight. I am so confident that I have actually been calling my old Army buddies and trying to get them to come here tonight to watch their old buddy become an international superstar. I called one of my best friends in the Army, he got out about two years ago. He resides in Morocco and is a spearchucker. Cal: Oh my God, I can't believe you just said that. Kano: What? Cal: Spearchucker has a racial tone to it that is demeaning. Kano: Oh that, well he is a legitmate spearchucker. He chucks spears for the Moroccan government. I hope he can make it. Cal: Shouldn't you be concentrating on the match? Kano: Oh, don't get me wrong. I am supremely confident in my abilities to win this match, but I am still very concentrated on this match. I look at this match and I can't think of a time when there has been this much talent in the ring at once, granted most of the talent is vested in the bodies of Chris Sloboda and myself. But Akiyama has a good shot as does Snow, but other than those, I don't think any others have a legitimate shot at winning. It is either going to be Snow, Akiyama, Boda, or myself. Any of us would make great champions, but I would truly be the greatest of all time. I have worked hard, I have played by the book, and I have made more sacrifices for this company that anyone else has. I deserve it. I had a dream last night. I was walking on the beach with God. And he was telling me to keep on doing what I am doing and that I will get what I deserve. I asked what he meant by that and he said the PCW World Title. I asked him when, and he said he couldn't tell me but it would be in the near future. So even God himself backs me. My dream ended with Him telling me that I rock. I woke up this morning and for the first time in a long time I was excited. I was down right gitty. I could only think about if tonight was the near future. If tonight is my destiny to become the PCW World Champion. I'll tell you one motherf*cking thing, if I win tonight, there is nothing that is going to take the title away from me while I still breathe air. But maybe I am getting ahead of myself, if I don't win tonight, I'll take it like a man. I won't sit and pout that I didn't defeat eighteen other men. All I want to do is if I cannot win tonight, I want to be one of the last men in. If I can just do that, then I will be able to finish respectably and get another shot at the title down the road. Cal: Is there anyone you are concerned about? Kano: Boda. I am afraid that it is going to come down at the end to the two of us. We each want to win very badly and will stop at nothing to realize our dream of being the PCW World Champion. I already have talked to Boda and I told him that I love him like he was my brother, but if I have to, I will knock him the f*ck out of the ring. He told me he would do the same to me. We both know this is business with the two of us, and neither one of us would take it personal, but I am still concerned that it would happen this way. If it would happen, that is just the way the cards fall and Boda or myself would have to accept it. Simple as that. But I truly do believe that tonight will be my night. Cal: Why? Kano: Because, simply put, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano leaves Cal Seaver and continues his trek to his locker room.* ~What is going on? Kano is. Tonight could very well be Jon Kano's time to shine.~
