...hospitals in canada blow...

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2.24.02 'Smile, for I am at the top of my game and you are at the end of yours.' LVII: First Person Perspective Shoney'sCanada Alberta, Canada8:00 AM (Breakfast) Ka-Ching! 'The total comes to $7.47 in Canadian currency.' 'Great.' Morning had sucked, waking up to the simple of a bellhop's idiotic questions of how was my night. I could have smacked him in the face but really I could have done much worse. The crazy people are in corporations holding society back, not on the streets creating mass murder mysteries and killing babies. Arriving at Shoney's around eight o'clock in the morning, I felt a light breeze tap against the shoulder portion of my raincoat, as drizzle poured down from the heavens and I was left to fend off of it. The flipping of bills from my hand almost felt like I was losing power over what I owned. Minutes after I dropped the bill into the waitress' hand, I knew I was losing a part of myself, and I loved every moment of it, from the first point I got into the restaurant Sunday morning to the time when I will leave. Really, it's fundamental that you get a good breakfast before starting your day. Sunday, the Sabbath maybe one of the more holy days in the calendar system and by most believers in the faith, Sunday is cheerished by laying on your butt and doing nothing. Soon it would be Monday morning, a day after Sabotage, a day after The End and my soon defeat of Xavier Kannon and Bobby Stormbringer (Nostradamus moment by predicting the future). Funny, once it was thought that Scientology would replace many of the lead religions known across mankind. Scientology's creed is simple, for everything to be equal to all men, yet when the Rush became an honorary Scientologist, he broke a creed. Who's fault is that? Xavier Kannon and look where he is now, out of the GTT within one round. Looks like Kannon had to find out the hardway, never forget a small man whose name will be known off of you. As I dig into the scrambled eggs with a metallic fork, I see the patrons inside enjoying their meals, conversing with one another as a clamor of echoes flow into my ears. Seemingly that when you die, people don't talk, yet they revisit the site from which they die. The one guy that dies in a car crash, sees his vehicle flipped over on the top portion and smoke bellowing out from it as if it were a pissed off dragon. You have to understand that when you're a celebrity, everything comes different. Such as social status... 'Soon as everything I hear about Xavier Kannon is dimished, then I can get back to what is important.' The whimpering of a little boy came out of nowhere as if it were some shorted reaction. Childish manners nag you at every turn and this point was no exception to that rule. Just like the Kevin Costner movies, '3000 Miles to Graceland', 'Waterworld', 'For Love of The Game', and the upcoming 'Dragon Fly' it stank. Somewhere in the distance of America and Overseas, I could hear the voice of Jeff Marx ring out, 'He's shot down ever one of Kevin Coster's last films!' Soon I would be on the road, teaching at a Calgary Sunday School, teaching people the evil of Scientology and all other religions not of neo-voidism. Well into business matters, there is the man known as The End on the week's end. Once again the Global Title Tournament spans into another round and I am mist the thick of it. Cut down from seven days and now to one to speak on Sabotage, two to speak on Turmoil and now it's my ball game to own that after the abysmal showing from Miyagawa the last time we faced. Television.... Scooping a piece of sausage into my mouth and let my jaw bones and teeth break it down, it was something figurative on my mind. Not that the fact that I have to face one of my friends in battle on the weekend, but that a whole chance to prove myself against these fellows, against my enemies, and against myself. The plate was deserted as a small portion of eggs were left out since I was too full to eat them. Climbing up from the table, I dropped down a $5 bill Canadian for the waitress as a tip. Walked out the door, with the sun shinning heavenly over my body and the light coming closer and closer to my eyes. Looking downward into a new, I could see the end of a car, flipped over on it's back straining to get up. Police crews were everywhere trying to hold everyone not involved in the scene back with yellow tape blocking them from entrance. I could see the crawling body of a young man, high in height and wonderous in facial expression. He had a gash on his head the size of a knife to a man's chest. He was trying to blink, but there was nothing left of his eyes at the moment, a shard of glass made his blindness possible. Right now, I could use a drink... LVIII: Transportation? Anyways, back on the scene at hand as an ambulance pulled up behind the flipped over Chevy and loaded the blind man into the portion where they could work on him and neuralize his serious injuries. Somehow, I felt a relation to The End in some point. If he hadn't been hard headed trying to get a bio chip out of his arm, then he would not be blind. Maybe he could have been a somebody wherever he would end up after Eternal Wrestling, but he chose PCW, and I chose him to further in the plans of Jeremy Howard's mind. Yeah Jeremy Howard - the same man who happens to be gunning for the Continental title and when he does win, I'll be there to collect the benefits and happen to hold two titles at once, at the pity of Miyagawa. He can bring up The Rush and I'll bring up Bad Battio so were even there. Canada seems to be a home for Miyagawa I mean they act alike, both are boring, corny at making attempts for laughter and happen to fail at almost everything including when it comes to hockey. They got lucky with the women, but I think Super Mario ( Miyagawa) will get his veteran self spanked by the Americans' and there will be not a miracle of victory...but a victory of just being better. When the ambulance pulled out of the scene, everyone back out and went back to their normal duties, but not forgetting what happened to them on this street today. Deciding to skip a school since maybe, teachers won't give much of a care since I wasn't a Canadian star, I would go to the hospital and try to get more information upon the blind man. Either two methods of transportation: One is walk but that'll take thirty minutes and I'll end up too late for visitation. Canada has real up-tight people when it comes to lateness, you could have sworn Lance Storm was the King of Canada. The other method, driving was quicker easier on my feet, but harder on my wallet. I went with my gut and decided to hail a cab, just for converstaion periods since talking to yourself happened to be a real pain. Soon a whole fleet of cabs came up to the sidewalk I was at and a fist fight almost broke out between them. As I shook my head and started to laugh my butt off, I quickly hopped into a undisclosed cab that they hadn't seen coming to pick me up. 'That fight back there must have been a real doozy for all those guys to be in it.' I said to the cabbie up front, not knowing who he was or what he looked like. All I got back from him was silence. 'You one of those 'rasslers' aren't cha?' he told me in a gruffy voice. I could tell he was Texan by the way he said wrestlers, but down there, everything is nice - the women, the business(except Enron), and the food. 'Yep,' I punched the word out as if I were asked the question a thousand times before. 'You aren't one of those guys that thinks wrestling's fake do you?' He shook his head no. 'The name's Alvin, and right now I figa, you want to get to the hospital to visit the boy who got his eye gutted.' he said smirking with a space in his tinted yellow teeth that could be a version of the Void. 'Ever follow the PTC?' I asked him. 'Yeah, follow my favorite wrestler around as he heads that company that attracts major attention.' 'Duke Williams?' That was the answer to his statement, I'm sure of it. Sure enough I was right, and he began talking about Duke for what it seemed for days, like they were good friends back in the day or something. I dozed off, like many would in this situation and started to just blankly put out inside of a Void Realm. After I heard the screeching of the car to a stop, I knew it was time to go visit the blind... LVIX: Blind Hospital This hospital seemed to be run by the blind as no one seemed to wonder who was coming in or going out. It was uncanny for people to be blind 24/7 or even acted like it, but I didn't want to feel being blind anyday. I know The End wouldn't like me for saying that, but when it comes down to business then who do you want on your side? Exactly myself. Note that was a rhetorical question since maybe anyone could become #1 Contender at this point. Soon as I stepped out of the car, I could see a few people waiting back by the front entrance doors, trying to catch a peek at who was under the mask of blindness. I snuck under, playing it by the rules of espionage and taking a dirty doctor's coat and portraying myself as the doctor. Moving close to the body, I could see a point of blonde hair matted on top of his head. A drip of blood started to pour onto the wheeling bed and once they led him to the operating room, I knew there was nothing that I could do. And that's part two of my day, I guess with The End as my teammate, I couldn't be more in a place of entrapment since The End happens to be my tag team partner for Sunday. When you face friends, everything goes out of the window. And when you have a chance to gain closer admittance to the title then you have things. So here I am, ready for Miyagawa, Jeremy Howard and ready for the combination of Kannon and Stormbringer. Sitting at my computer typing away, while they sit on revenge, puny jokes, and social reform. How does being a prep suck, I mean you can't get friends because everyone finds you annoying and such a b*tch. How does Eleanor Hall live with herself? It's like the only things in the world that will survive after a nuclear meltdown is roaches, Cher, and Eleanor Hall's Annoying Voice and Sense of Humor. I think they call it being picked by an idiot who lost to OSW trash. That's it for now as I wipe the floor with Xavier Kannon and Bobby Stormbringer. Keep Ya Head Up,D. M.
