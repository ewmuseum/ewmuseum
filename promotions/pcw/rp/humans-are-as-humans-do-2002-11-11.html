Humans are as humans do...

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/11/02 'I used to come here all the time... back when I was a kid, not even in double digits of age. I'd sit on this little rock here, just watching the waves and listening to the seagulls. Cute little seaside town, it was... then I got a job in wrestling and Canada called me.' It's the dead of night. Nerezza it perched on top of a pillar, one of many that line the waterfront. In front of him, a sea of sand and shells guards the ocean. A sea in front of a sea. The salty air stimulates his nostrils as he breathes in, his mouth even begins to water a little at the edge. The decorative bulbs of all colours illuminate him, but in his vampire form, grey and cold, he looks like an immense gargoyle in a red cape. The few people who are about think nothing of the silent and immobile giant. A stray mongrel barks at him from below, but the swaying of the tide holds his attention in a moist vice. 'And my opponent calls himself a romantic... how typically human. Romance to them is another word for lust. A cheap form of charm before they attempt to make another child or kill off the chances of that happening. Romance is a feeling, one that humanity has cheapened to February 14th and Anne Summers catalogues. Is what I feel here, now... romance? A love for this place where I spent summer, where sunglasses weren't needed and Punch & Judy was considered entertainment?' Out to sea, Nerezza sees a light, bobbing up and down, probably belonging to a boat. 'They don't know what it's like... to be a true beast and yet retain man's emotions. Why couldn't they understand? What caused man to break away from Nature's design and only be brought back by what they consider a disease? And why am I the ONLY one to reject the organized ranks of vampires, running free from them instead? Am I to be lonely for the rest of my life... or forever?!' The questions bombard Nerezza like a military barrage. The dog gave up and wandered off, leaving the monster on his throne. 'Gaz and Natalia seem rather cosy... Alexander's blind ambition cost him his wife. Even Kannon, as much of a rat as he is, enjoys life with Ellie. All these couples... and the only time I kiss a girl is seconds before her throat is shredded apart by me.' Nerezza gazes down into his hands. The 4-inch fingers curl ominously towards his face and the thick, leathery skin wrinkles a little. A speck of liquid drips onto them. It's clear and salty, but not sea water. The hands begin to shrink as more and more rains down upon them until finally, Nerezza's human hands are back to their normal shade of flesh. 'Must be time to feed again... Brandi was sweet enough but Walden was hardly a snack.' The vampire tries to hide a sob, but his fists ball up and rest on his shoulders as his eyes disappear below his arms. 'At least if I'd stayed with the council, I could have seen more of my kind... I brought this on myself the same way Alexander did. It's only justice that I stay alone...' The lonesome vampire looks up and around. The dog that became wary of his true self had even left him. The beach was deserted and only the lights that swooped up and down across the pillars reminded him of the world he'd set out to accomplish. A world where humans didn't exist, just nature's animals. No industry, no mass-marketing, no war. A place where the food chain was balanced and the top of it only killed what it ate. A time like when the dinosaurs roamed and everything was just... perfect.But it meant a place without art, a place without medicine, a place without Rezz Bull. The vampire blinked. 'In my self-righteous behaviour... have I devoted myself to a belief that defies my OWN wishes? Am I going to go on a mission that will rob me of what I want? If I go about doing what I set out to do... tricking humanity through the media into killing themselves... won't I be just as bad as them? Nerezza turned around on his stone column, dropping to the floor with a thud that echoed along the beach to the cliffs. The winds carried it back to him and the sea air once more brought back memories of times gone by. 'If man kills himself... no blood will be on my hands. Until the day that they make the one weapon that will kill them all... I'll be as human as them. Perhaps, I'll try some of that cheapened romance... when daybreak comes. How appropriate that France is the next stop. I wonder if the city of romance can spare a companion for me...?'
