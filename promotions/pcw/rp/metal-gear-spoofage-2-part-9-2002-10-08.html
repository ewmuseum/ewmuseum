Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 9)

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/8/02 Lying face down on top of a battered and smoking bridge, a young man drifts in and out of unconsciousness. Raiden: (Groaning) Uggggggggggh. Above him a helicopter whirls, its blades slicing the air. From out of the doorway of it, a face peers mired in disgust. Snake: Raiden, get up! Raiden: No get up, need sleepy. Bobby: Raiden, you want mint for pillow? Snake: (Glaring) Shut up! Bobby: Sorry. Raiden: (Crawling to his knees) Can I go home now..... Snake: No, you still have things you need to do. Raiden: (Somewhat sarcastically) Why can't you do them? You're Snake after all. Snake: Well, uh.... Suddenly a rock finds its way out of the copter, clonking squarely off Raiden's skull. He falls to his knees clutching his head. Raiden: Why'd you do that for!? Snake: Let's just say every time you start thinking too much about silly things like plot points I'm going to hit you with a rock. Okay? Raiden: (Tearing up) Okay. So how are you here though? Snake died! Another rock, nailing Jack in the forehead. Down he goes to the bridge. Raiden: Mommy! Snake: I have plenty more where that came from. Raiden: Okay, okay! Can I at least ask about Bobby? I KNOW I killed him just two chapters agoAGH! Yet another stone, bouncing off Jack's melon and straight off the bridge to the waters far below. Snake: No. Raiden: Uuuuuugh. Okay, okay, where's the president then? Snake: How should I know? Raiden: What good are youAGH! STOP HITTING ME WITH ROCKS! Snake: (Holding another rock) Stop pissing me off. Raiden: (Clutching his head) Owwww! Sobbing softly, Jack kneels and phones in to the Colonel. Colonel: Raiden....are you crying? Raiden: (Sniffling) No. Colonel: What is it then? Raiden: Metal Gear's already active! Colonel: That's bad. But it isn't too late for you to stop the terrorists from launching a nuclear strike. Raiden: Oh yeah, and that Pliskin guy is really Solid Snake. Did you know that? Colonel: What, that wasn't part of the script! Raiden: Huh? The Colonel begins thumbing through a mysterious booklet, scanning quickly. Colonel: No, nowhere in the script! What the hell? How are we supposed to..... Raiden: Colonel? Colonel: (Hiding the booklet behind his back) What? Raiden: What were you reading there? Colonel: Foxdie in aisle four. Raiden: WhaYEARRGGGH!!!!! A wave of intense pain flashes through the testicles of Jack. He doubles over in agony, holding his crotch. Raiden: What....the....hell...... Colonel: Raiden, stop asking silly questions and go find the president. He should be somewhere on strut 2. Raiden: God it hurts.... Colonel: Stop whining! Hobbling away, Raiden inches across the remnants of the bridge towards the next strut over. Seeing a hole too large to leap across, he swings himself over the edge of the railing and begins shimmying across. Raiden: Don't....look....down..... Sweatdrops form on the brow of the hero. He hangs in place for an eternity in time....his head seems to vibrate. Then finally with an agonized cry he looks down to see the endless water waywaywaywaywaywaywayWAY down below. Raiden: No..... Looking up again, Jack continues his trip across the rail. At about the halfway point a seagull dives in...circles a couple of times, then perches directly on top of his skull. Raiden: (Rolling his eyes up) No, shoo! Go away! Ignoring his pleas, the bird marches and hops on his mass of white hair. It then begins pecking, pulling out strands and garnering yelps from Jack. Raiden: No, shoo I say! Go away! Snake: He probably thinks your hair is some kind of nest. Raiden: What can I do to stop him!? Snake: Well, you have to admit your head is pretty nestlike. Raiden: Shut up! Snake: Don't make me break out the rocks again. Raiden: (Glancing down) No, NO! Continuing onward, Raiden whimpers as the gull pecks away. Leah: Jack do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: Not now! Leah: Tee hee! But seriously, do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: ARGH! Onward, onward he crawls. Finally the seagull stops pecking, looks up briefly and takes flight. Raiden: Thank god. Then a stream of yellow liquid courses from above, bouncing off Jack's head and soaking his hair. Guard: (High above) Dah, should not have drank 12 pack of Mountain Dew. Raiden: Aaaaaaaaaaagh! Doused in the spray of urine, Jack crawls on and gargles in disgust. Finally he reaches the end of the ledge, pulling himself up. Raiden: (Wringing out his hair with wide insane eyes) I want to die. Still dripping, our hero makes his way through a nearby door and into a hallway. He then stops abruptly as he hears a voice from around the corner. Kriegidus: (Through a radio) Brandi, Snake is here! Brandi: My father had some unfinished business with him. Kriegidus: Be that as it may. We can't change the plan now. Brandi: And why not? Kriegidus: Because! It would be too much trouble. Brandi: Too much trouble? I could go and kill him right now. Kriegidus: No. Brandi: Why ever not!? Kriegidus: Just no. Don't make me hit you with a rock. Brandi: Bah. Where is Hosselot? Kriegidus: Not here. Brandi: I don't trust him. Kriegidus: Don't talk like that, he's one of our own! Brandi: One of our own? He kicked my father down a ladder to his screaming and horrible death! Kriegidus: Stop being so petty. Brandi: Petty!? Kriegidus: Well I need to go check on the unit. Brandi: I'll head back then. Kriegidus: Uh, the bridge is destroyed. Brandi: What about the helicopter? Kriegidus: We found it in the bottom of the harbor, no idea what happened there. Brandi: Alright, my unit will begin pulling out. We'll take the oil fence. Kriegidus: Not yet, the intruder is still at large! Brandi: He has luck on his side.... Brandi stops suddenly, sniffing the air. Kriegidus: What? Brandi: I smell something. Kriegidus: Agent Aimz is dead, Brandi. Brandi: I know that. It smells like peepee. Kriegidus: ..... Brandi: What? Kriegidus: Nothing. Listen, the conformation is in one hour. Keep the president from killing himself with snack foods until then. Around the corner Raiden raises an eyebrow curiously. Raiden: Snack foods? Brandi: (Turning) What was that? In a panic Jack dives behind a stack of nearby crates, causing a huge crash. Kriegidus: What's going on? Brandi: I do not know. I will turn the electric currents on. The female soldier produces a remote control, pressing a button. The floor before her begins to pulse with high voltage. Kriegidus: Is this really necessary? I doubt the president has the IQ required to turn the doorknob. Brandi: Still. Oh yes, before I go....we leave for Russia when all this is finished. I want half the money. My soldiers, you will take care of them. Kriegidus: (Cracking his knuckles) Gladly. Brandi: I meant provide for them! Kriegidus: But of course. What happened to rebuilding mother Russian though? Brandi: The old bastard is dead. No need for senile cold war stories anymore. Kriegidus: It's your life. Brandi: I'll say this again as well....do not betray us! Kriegidus: (Darkly) The feeling is mutual.... Raiden: (Snores) Brandi: (Turning) What IS that sound? Raiden: (Groggily) Huh? Wha? Jack looks up briefly to see Brandi closing in on him. He looks about frantically, trying to no avail to find another place to hide....at last though she shrugs. Brandi: Feh. Grunting, the woman makes her way out of the room....Raiden stands. Raiden: Hm. Walking over and examining the electric floor, Jack holds his chin. Slowly he extends a foot, tapping it to the panel....screaming as voltage courses through his body. Raiden: YEAAAAAAAAAAAGH!!!!!! Convulsing, our hero launches across the room and slams hard into the wall. Colonel: (Shakes his head sadly) Raiden: (Smoking) Uuuuuuuuuurgh. Colonel: Raiden you'll have to disable the control panel. Raiden: How? Colonel: Well, there's a circuit breaker right behind you. Maybe you should try hitting it. Raiden: (Looking) That's actually a good plan. Jack opens the box on the wall....looks on for a second, then grabs the switch. He then convulses as the urine drenching him from head to toe causes a jolt of electricity to pulse through him. Raiden: (Shuddering) Eeeeeeeaaaaaahahahahaha! After a few seconds Raiden flies again, landing on the electrified floor. Raiden: AAAAAAaaahahahahaha! Again he launches forward, grabbing onto the circuit breaker to steady himself and again getting shocked for his efforts. Raiden: AAAAAAAAAAAgodhelpmeheheheheahahahahagah!!!!! And again our hero sails through the air, this time landing in a heap at the doorway just past the trapped floor panel. Colonel: Well that's one way of doing it. Raiden: When.....will....the....hurting.....stop...... Slowly Jack crawls to his hands and knees....fumbles up to the door, which he opens. He then looks on wearily. Raiden: (Opening his eyes slowly) Mr. President? In a tiny chamber, president George W. Bush himself sits eating out of a bag of Rold Golds. Bush: Uh....hello. The strain of these two words causes a pretzel to go down the wrong pipe. He waves his arms frantically, turning blue as Jack rushes the chamber and wraps his arms around his torso. Raiden: No....breathe! BREATHE DAMN YOU BREATHE!!!!!! To Be Continued
