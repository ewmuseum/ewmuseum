It was nice to see that a father and daughter can still communicate with each other in this cold day and age. The two Celestials appeared from the oak double doors leading down into the basement, and stepped out into the courtyard. The air was crisp that morning, a bit chilled, but still pleasant enough to walk unprotected in. Cyclonica padded across the cold stone on bare feet, while Akiyama’s boots clomped after her. When they entered the kitchen, Boca was sitting at the table sipping on a new mug, this one black with a white wavy design on it. End and Sandy were already finished with their dishes, and were walking out just as the Celestials entered the kitchen. End stopped parallel to Akiyama…the two of them facing each other. Akiyama, with a stern gaze towards the Blind Ninja, gave him a slow nod of his head. End obviously couldn’t see the nod…but somehow he responded with his own nod. Sandy stared at End as he walked on past the god, confused on what just happened.
