Total Domination; Short and 'F*CKING SWEET!'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now ::Just when Corey was beginning to doubt himself, New Breed and everything he believed in, something like this happened. Something wonderful, something extraordinarily good. The Deuces and Asylum split up, they did exactly what Corey and the rest of New Breed had been saying from day one. Attitude adjustment? That goes out of the window now, for since when did the best in the world need an attitude adjustment? Our scene opens up at Corey's new home in Melbourne, Australia. We go through a quick tour of the house, as we see it has a number of deluxe bedrooms, all with en suites. A huge spacious living room complete with stereo system, surround sound and big screen TV. Two stories, wooden floorboards, a roaring fireplace, bear skin rug on the floor, an endangered species of course, the place of Corey's dreams. And that is where we find Corey, in the house of his dreams, lying on the bearskin rug, beside a roaring fireplace. He is wearing nothing but a pair of jeans, giving us a view of his chiselled physique that has come about from seemingly a lifetime of partying. Oh well, I guess somebody has to be the 'lucky ones'. Corey sighs, breathing in the clean Australian air. He gets to his feet and grabs his bear fur coat, which just happens to be lying on a nearby couch. He puts the bear fur coat on and walks into the kitchen of his newly made dream home. He opens the fridge and inspects it, before coming up with his conclusion.:: Corey: Hmph, we're out of Vegimite. ::Corey looks up to the camera and grins.:: Corey: Oh well, nothing can spoil the good mood I am in today. You see, because on Wednesday the 6th of March, New Breed's prophecy came true and we finally fulfilled our goal. That being to be known as the world's greatest stable to ourselves....check, to the wrestlers.... check, and finally, to the fans........ CHECK! ::Corey pulls a tub of 'possum jelly' out of the fridge and closes the door.:: Corey: You see, we've always known we were the greatest stable in the world. But of course we knew, after all, we are superior. ::Corey places the tub onto the kitchen bench.:: Corey: And it wasn't hard to convince the wrestlers either, I mean there's only so many ass-kickings they can take before they have to accept the truth. ::Corey walks over to a cupboard and opens it, he pulls a loaf of bread from out of it.:: Corey: But how to convince the average man, the idiot in the street.... ::Corey places the loaf of bread on the bench, next to the Possum Jelly.:: Corey: That's the problem. You see, you idiot fans are very good at ignoring the facts, when we defeated Management you said 'What about the Wild Deuces. And then we beat the Wild Deuces time after time, every time you'd say 'but you cheated' or 'you used a low blow', until we finally beat them once and for all, and then you said 'But what about Asylum?' ::Corey opens the tub of Possum Jelly:: Corey: So then came Shanghaied, and which stable had the belt? New Breed damnit! And then what did you say? 'Timo Bolamba is a one-man stable!' So we went to face Timo, and he ran like a little bitch to OSW. And finally, we had established ourselves as THE greatest stable in the world..... or so we thought. 'Asylum and the Wild Deuces are in great form and have new members.' Asylum!? Wild Deuces!? We just beat those guys!!! ::Corey undoes the tie on the end of the package that contains the loaf of bread. Corey: And I hate to admit it, but for a while we thought that we were going to be beaten, not by Asylum or by the Wild Deuces, those guys never threatened us, but by the fans and their own stupidity! ::Corey bends down and opens a cupboard below him, he pulls out a plate and then stands upright again. He puts the plate on the bench.:: Corey: And it pissed me off, it pissed me off tremendously. After all we had done, all the obstacles we had overcome, we'd go to the arena and hear chants of 'Boda' or 'Miyagawa', guys who had done nothing! ::Corey opens a drawer at waist height, he pulls out a knife and then closes the drawer.:: Corey: And then it happened, the moment that will be forever remembered by PCW fans, not because Asylum and Wild Deuces split up, they were a waste of space anyway, but because it was that moment when the New Breed became the first stable in the history of PCW to gain TOTAL DOMINANCE. Voted as the best by ourselves, voted as the best by our peers, and FINALLY, voted by the best by the so-called fans! ::Corey takes out two slices of bread from the loaf and places them on the plate.:: Corey: For not even the people, as dumb as they are, can deny the truth this time. You can't say 'what about Asylum' or 'what about the Deuces', coz this time, there is no Asylum and there is no Deuces! ::Corey sticks the knife into the jelly and shows it to the camera.:: Corey: They, much like the species of possum used to make this jelly, are EXTINCT! ::Corey spreads the jelly over both slices of bread, and then puts on slice on top of the other.:: Corey: New Breed make history once again, confirmed by the very fans that hate and despise them. ::Corey lifts the possum jelly sandwich off the plate and shows it to the camera.:: Corey: And that piece of irony, much like this sandwich, is F*CKING SWEET! ::Corey takes a huge bite out of his sandwich, and we see the bliss in his eyes, the bliss at the controversial taste experienced by the few rich enough to get their hands on the jelly made of these extinct creatures. Corey doesn't see why, after all, being eaten by a great PCW superstar like himself has to be the highpoint of a possum's career. Especially when that certain PCW superstar, is at the pinnacle of HIS career. So their goals are complete, but that's never enough for New Breed. They have been known to set the bar higher than everyone else, leading PCW forward as the others struggle behind, having to settle with being nothing but New Breed copycats. And with Asylum and the Deuces divided like the Red Sea, it leaves a path for the New Breed to come up the middle and regain the leader's position. So the only question left is, how will New Breed shock us? The scene fades out.:: !The End!
