Sorrow

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-12-02 A light breeze whistled though the air was warm. The sky was clear and was lit up by the bright moon and stars. People passed Jamie's eyes, people he may never see again, people he did not care about. Strange, you could pass someone a thousand times over and never know it. You could have met people who became famous or even legendary, and never know it. Though, Jamie knew a man that he would never see again, and that was guaranteed. That man's name was Christopher Keily. He was a good man of 32 years. He had a family, a young family. He did not deserve to die. He was born into the Celtic gang, he was raised in the Celtic gang, and he served in the Celtic gang! It was not a choice, not an option! He had no other alternative. Frustration and anger loomed in Jamie's face; never had he felt anger like he had at that moment. As he thought about the innocent man that was murdered just to send a warning. What kind of warning was it? A letter, a phone call, even vandalism would have been sufficient warning, why must they end a man's life in order to send a message. Funny, how the world works, is it not? Chris Keily was sitting down in a chair of a warehouse. He was supervising that night. He was sweaty due to a basketball game he had had before his shift, he had not had time to go home and change. Maybe if he did things would have been different, maybe he could have stared at his wife and children one more time, and smiled, but no. That was not to be, simply because the Vurdo Mafia had returned. It's a small world isn't it? Jamie wrestled in the same federation as a man that in part had to do with the killing of a Celtic Gang member. The police however did not know this, they were too ignorant to realize. And of course, the Celtics could not tell them this, otherwise an entire new investigation would be opened concerning the Mafia/Celtic gang war of 1990, which was the biggest Boston had ever seen. The Celtic's won that war, Jamie was only seven at the time, though he remembers clips in his head of the traumatic battles that took place. His house was often broken into, Jamie was afraid for his life as grown men of Italian origin stared him in the eye, though they did not kill him, it was against the rules of a gang war and the Mafia, being as 'high class' as they are rarely broke those rules. The war was basically over who would rule Boston, who would have most of the territory within the city and who would be the 'top gang' their. After the Celtics won, the Mafia left the City for another City near Boston called Amber Hill. Though now, it seemed they were returning. In fact they made it clear they were returning, they made it clear in the most brutal and vulgar way possible. Jamie knew the Celtics had a fight on their hands, the Vurdo Mafia were their biggest challenge to date. Though, passion burned in the Celtics souls, they wanted to defeat the Mafia more than you could possibly imagine, they would do it for Chris. Jamie raised his left arm and pulled back his sweater sleeve. He looked at his watched, it read '11:21 pm'. He had been sitting on that bench for over three hours, and had not budged once. The breeze blew his hair to the left slightly, it was as ever spiked up, his blonde tips seemed to be drying and falling down towards his scalp, it was obvious he had not 'gelled it' in a while. Not many people roamed Parkinson's Street that night, only gangsters and petty crooks, looking for their prey. The street lamp that towered over Jamie's head seemed to act as a spotlight on him, as everyone who passed looked at him oddly, or maybe he was just paranoid. Really though, he did not care. A familiar voice echoed behind him. 'So that's where you are.' Jamie tilted his head slightly to the right; he looked at Gerald for a second, and then turned back. 'Yeah, this is where I am.' He replies mockingly. Gerald takes a seat beside him. 'You know Jamie, there is no point in sitting here, waiting for something to bring Chris back, because the simple fact of the matter is he's not coming back, and the only god damn thing we can do about it is make sure his death is avenged and I'll be damned before I let you sit here, feeling sorry for yourself when you, more so then any of us have a job to do.' Gerald's harsh words strangely comforted Anton, almost like motivation. 'Thanks.' Jamie replies with a smile. Gerald did not really understand what he meant, not sure he wanted to either. Jamie got up from his seat and began walking down the road. 'Where are you?' Gerald shouts to him. 'I got a lot to do, a war to fight and a match to win.' Jamie shouts back. Jamie did indeed, on Fusion he had a number one contenders match for The Cruiserweight title against El Romantico, Teresa Tomas and Vilas Lavagne. It would be no easy task, but he had claimed his first win in a long time the previous week, and knew he needed to continue winning. Though that match was not all that was on Jamie's agenda for Fusion, not all at all.
