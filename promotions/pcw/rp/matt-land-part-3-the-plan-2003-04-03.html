Matt Land, Part 3 - The Plan

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/03 “So you still don’t want to talk?” His voice full of anger and contempt, Matt Jackson slams his fist down on the metal table, his brown eyes burning a hole into the young lady in front of him, as he pulls out the chair directly across from her. His black suit matches his personality and he crosses his arms across his wide chest. There is a little pain, once they come to a rest. His body is throbbing from Turmoil and the attempt of Jade and Lacuna took take him out of the match. They weren’t able to overcome the ghost and Sarah Clarkson wouldn’t be able to either. “There aren’t any disqualifications,” he sneers at her, letting a smile curl on his thin lips. He knows she doesn’t understand the joke he makes, but it makes him feel a little better after the anger he felt at Turmoil. Sarah stares blankly at the highly trained agent, holding back the tears that are trying to come out. She is small and weak and not even close to the physical presence that is sitting across from her. Her body is beaten and her once nice dress is torn from the constant abuse over the past few days. Her face is caked with dirt and even if she could muster a smile, her normal white teeth would be red with the blood that has been spilling from her lips. It has been hell on her, but she isn’t ready to give up to him just yet. She still is holding out, waiting for the right moment to fight back. “I won’t tell you anything,” her voice hints at the lack of sleep she has been receiving, and her eyes explain it further. Her black pupils stand out in the sea of red that surrounds them, glancing around the room she calls home, with the white brick walls, emotionless just like her captor. The only things that are even around her and in the center is the small metal table that she is cuffed to and the chair she is tied ever so tightly to. She brings her eyes back on to the ghost and stares at him unblinking, trying to tell him that she isn’t ready to give up. “I am not scared of you anymore. You have given me your worst and I am feeling nothing of it yet.” “Is that the case?” His eyes meeting hers, noticing how blood shot they really are. “You are no longer scared of that man that is going to kill you? I almost just wish for a moment, you could see your father and ask him of the fabled ghost. I know he has heard of him, too bad you get to see it instead of hearing about it.” His voice is full of truth and he backs it up with a smile, showing his perfect white teeth to her. His stone face makes the point even clearer to her, making sure that there is no doubt in her mind, that he is going to end the young rich poshes life. Sarah does everything she can to hold the tears back, she doesn’t want to seem weak and make her threats meaningless. She looks down at the metal table, taking a deep breath trying to decide how to continue the chess game. The stalemate is going on for far to long and if she hopes to get out of this situation, she needs to gather some momentum quickly. Her mind and body are worn out though and coming up with a plan seems almost unbearable. Matt did the job of breaking her, even with the fact she won’t show it. She isn’t the same, this is a new Sarah, four days ago she was naive to the world and now in a rush, she is forced to learn it all- to survive. She brushes the mess that is her hair out of her face with her free hand and once again brings her eyes to Matt’s. “You know that my dad has someone looking for me. It is impossible that he would go this long without taking notice of my missing. He has access to some of the best agents in the world and they will fine me,” she tells him, trying to put confidence into her statement. It isn’t present because she knows that there is a small chance anyone will be able to save her now. “You believe someone is going to save you? Beat the best at his own game?” Matt continues to sit back in the chair and stare at her, his hawk like eyes scanning his victim with precision. The only thing left for him to do is swoop down and take the mouse out of existence. To take her life, too add another name to the list of countless faces that he has put before his own feet. She means nothing to him, except for another person standing in his way of getting what he wants. “You aren’t the best! You are only some mediocre half wit that happened to be in the right place at the right time,” she says, letting her British accent play on each of the words. “If you were as good as you claim, then you wouldn’t be beating on some defenseless girl for information.” The cocky smile brightness at the sound of her words and he starts to methodically laugh. Almost as if he is mocking his opponent and any opponent he has ever stood face to face with. Teresa Tomas and Akiyama’s faces skip through his thoughts, like a stone skipping across a lake, rippling his thoughts and causing Sarah Clarkson to sink to the bottom. These two were trying to stop him from getting what he wants. Not information, but power and control. “You are nothing Ms. Clarkson,” he declares, as if he is stating war on her comment. “The only reason you are here is because there was no one else. You don’t fit, nor do you belong. It is simply that there is no one left. You are the only one who knows information about Andrew and that is why you were picked. You might be defenseless, but it is a price you pay for being in the position your father is. He will fall at my feet, just like you soon will.” “That is where you are wrong. You don’t understand how crafty and intelligent my father can be. Maybe you should have looked a little more in depth at him before you screwed with him because if you aren’t careful. You are going to be the one falling at his feet.” Her eyes once again meet his, trying to let him know she isn’t fearful. Her face though plastered with dirt, shows that she believes her statement to be true and that the man holding her against her will might soon be dead. Matt’s smile grows with contempt at the challenge she places in front of him. “You believe your father is going to kill me?” His smile only grows larger, as he kicks the chair out from behind him and makes his way around the table to the young lady. “No one has ever stood over my body in triumph, even when I was at my worst, no man was able to stand atop the mountain I built.” Matt’s eyes begin to ignite, like a match being thrown into a puddle of gas. “I did my research on your father and I am prepared, I knew exactly what I was getting into. It is just too bad you didn’t get the same curtsey, but you will soon learn.” “You will soon tell me everything I want to know,” he hisses, into her ear, raising his right hand and striking it across her slender face. Leaving a stinging red mark to remember him by, he turns on his heels and walks out the small metal door. Sarah is once again alone and the crimson liquid is pouring from her lip. The table is now stained with the fresh genuine English blood. *** “Matt what are we going to do about the Tournament?” Her voice is full of worry, as she watches the drawings of the brackets for the thousandth time. Her head has come to rest on his barrel of a chest and his black suit provides just enough comfort. Probing him with her icy blue eyes, she awaits a response. Nothing is said; Matt just continues to lie on his monstrous bed, looking around his elegant room. Off to the left is the strong oak nightstand, which seems like it has held its place ever since the dawning of time. Next to it is the bath, the massive amount of porcelain that could crack and falter at any second. Teresa Jane Tomas, he says her name with a hate. She has been nothing, but a thorn in everyone’s side ever since Matt had first heard the name. She is a person much like himself, always wanting something more; never content with quitting, and most of all someone you can’t trust. He knows how serious of a threat she presents, but not anything that brings him great worry. Akiayma, his voice is wrought with hatred for Teresa’s partner. The sound of his name makes Matt’s blood boil. He just like his Southern partner has been a thorn in everyone’s side- mainly one Xavier Kannon’s. Neither of his opponent’s strikes fear into him, by all accounts they should, but Matt could careless about their pasts. They haven’t made an impact on him and that is all he cares about. They aren’t going to stop him from his goal. They aren’t going to stop him from taking what is rightfully his. “We are going to win it,” he finally replies to her. His eyes locking on to hers and like an open door, he peers at her soul. He can see the fear that she has going into Sunday, her face tells him everything, as he strokes her blonde hair away from it. Haley shouldn’t be in the position she is now and for the most part she has been forced into it. Following Matt into everything isn’t the best life for her, but it is the best he can provide. He would do his best to protect the young princess that is cuddling next to him. “Isn’t the competition going to be like stiff?” She asks, twisting her hair on her finger and cocking her head to the side, like a puppy looking for something from its master. Matt runs his hands through his brown hair and his ever so cocky smile returns. His mind is completely focused on his two foes and Clarkson is in the way back of it. She will soon trip up and be the next face that he buries in his mind, as another senseless victim. A person who doesn’t have to be there, but made the dreadful mistake of crossing path’s with the CIA’s best. “Competition tough? Two words that don’t belong together in the PCW,” Matt tells her, pausing every now and then to laugh. She doesn’t understand exactly what he is planning for the tournament, but it is going to ensure that they win and he is able to claim the Continental Title as his own. He would be able to fulfill his destiny and propel the mission forward. “We will win by any means necessary,” he says, letting an evil smile form on his lips. Soon everything will be revealed to the world and by that time it will be to late. *** Darkness descends on the Washington, DC countryside, alive with nocturnal beasts and the trees swaying from side to side, Jackson creeps though the woods boarding his estate. Startled birds fly from their perches; and owls jump to life, sending out their alarms of noise and if with help from a co-conspirator, they become silent once again. Matt is unable to be seen, as his black suit makes him unnoticeable in the darkness- in his friend. “You have done a good job so far,” Matt whispers into the night. The wind begins to pick up and the trees start to rustle, drowning out the words just spoken. His devious plan is finally coming into full effect and this was just some work to make sure everything was in order. Silently the ghost starts his trek into the night. Out of the darkness, a man is produced and more noticeable then Jackson. His physical dominance can be felt, as soon as he appears behind the agent. His white suit makes him stand out in the dark and his silver hair shimmers in the moonlight. His eyes dart around to make certain that no one else is around, even though he knows that no one would be allowed to get onto Jackson’s property. The agent has the top of the line security. “Did you get your payment?” Matt questions the man, not turning around to face him. He knows better then to fear a man, who to the best of his knowledge is no threat. He casually crosses his arms over his massive chest and cracks a smile, as he waits for the man’s response. Feeling inside his jacket with his elbow to make sure his automatic is there, just in case. “Yes I did and I am sure you are happy with the results up to this point?” The man’s voice, full of confidence, as he leans against a tree, playing the whole thing off, like he is the one who is in charge. Of course he isn’t, Matt is the one who brought him in on this job and he was the one who was going to take him out if needed. Agent Jackson is always the one in control and he plans on keeping it this way. “I am very pleased with the work you have done so far. The first round match against Akiyama and Tomas is exactly what I wanted. I need to prove to Xavier, that I can remove problems for him and make everything run smoothly. Akiyama is just one of the people that happened to cross him. So you are doing us both a great favor by helping out. Were either Skyler Green or Jack the least bit knowledgeable to what went on?” “Never, they don’t have a clue that I rigged your names to be pulled like that It was quite hard, but as you can see I completed the task,” he responds. Trying to make himself sound like the important missing piece in this puzzle that Matt was putting together. Little did he know that he was all ready in place, just like all the other pieces and the only one that was missing was the Continental Title. “Very well, here is the rest of your money,” Matt says, pulling an envelope from his jacket pocket, scrapping it against his pistol, to reassure himself that it is there. “Just make sure you get to the refs now, I know the location is secret, but I should have it for you soon. Remember this is important and if you screw it up, I will make sure you don’t live to pull anything else.” “Everything will be done like you asked Agent Jackson and the Continental Title will soon be in your hands,” he assures Matt, who pays no mind to him. Turning on his heels he lungs into the darkness back towards his house. His cocky smile comes back across his lips, thinking about what he has just done. Getting one step closer to the downfall of the PCW. You want to stop me? Try as you might, but can you? I think not, I am the best, the brightest, the trickiest, I am Matt Jackson.
