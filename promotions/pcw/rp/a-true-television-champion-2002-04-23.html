A TRUE Television Champion!

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-23-02 **OOC NOTE: Thanks for the break guys. Back to RP'ing for me! More than half this Rp was typed with one hand.** **Theme Music: 'Learn To Fly' by Foo Fighters** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, I'm a little busy right now. Got a big match against a tiny Egyptian. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [The scene comes up on the sunny pre-summer sun hanging in the sky in Tallahassee, Florida. It pans down to see a beautiful blonde woman walk into A.J.'s Sports Bar on Tennessee Street.] Sandra: I dunno if this'll work... [In Gilchrist dormitory on the FSU campus, Mark Farmer and Joe Stephenson bang on a door, both armed with...clipboards?] Joe: This is retarded. [Mark slaps him upside the head.] Mark: Shut up already! [Nick North walks into the FSU Leach center with a clipboard of his own, shaking his head and staring at every well-formed woman who struts through the gym.] Nick: Dumb assignment, but a great view... [We see brief shots of the rest of the CWS. Eddie Russo at Vinyl Fever. Kyle Lamen at the Tallahassee Mall. Jamie Hunter in a men's bathroom at said mall. Pegleg Paula and Jacques of Arc out at the beach (pale Jacques in a speedo. Ugh!). Chris in the center of FAMU's campus. El Pequeno Hombre Del Fantastico is at the Hispanic Student Union of FSU. Each student has said clipboards. Why? We go back to Joe and Mark, who're furiously knocking on some student's dorm room door.] Joe: Hey, yo! Survey time! [The door swings open, to reveal the dirty, dingy settings of the average freshman college student's dorm room. A pimply-faced freshman kind is standing in the doorway.] PFFK: Yeah? Mark: Hi, we're representatives of the Core Wrestling School, The Malice Crew, and the President of PCW. [As the PFFK's eyes bug out, and the Tappa Tappa Kegga boys sniff the air, the scene cuts to Kyle Lamen in the Tallahassee Mall. Kyle is calling out to random passerbys, and drawing a fairly sizable crowd.] Kyle: Frickin'-A~! You can decide what you want to see on Primetime Championship Wrestling, by simply filling out this frickin' survey! [A middle-aged man, complete with beer gut and toting a five-year old son walks up to the Crown Prince Of Punk Rock.] Man: What're you talking about? [The scene cuts again, to Sandra Davis, standing near the bar at A.J.'s, recieving LOTS of attention.] Sandra: Basically, there're just some questions about people in PCW...title belts, pushes...that sort of thing. [The scene cuts to the CWS's 'Man On The Street' The Violator. He is on a street corner by Popeye's, with his clipboard like all the others. A homeless man approaches.] Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!?! [We cut back to Emo Eddie at Vinyl Fever, who's cornered a guy with a 'Because...I'm...Excellent!' Miyagawa t-shirt on.] Eddie: Like, what do you think of the New Breed holding the world title and passing it around like a joint? [The camera cuts back to Mark, Joe…and the PFFK sitting on PFFK’s bed, smoking marijuana out of a fluorescent lightbulb-bong.] Joe: Isn’t this stuff bad for you? [PFFK nods.] Joe: Cool. [We see Chris Andrews talking to a small circle of FAMU students.] Chris: Who’s your favorite PCW superstar, currently? [A number of different murmured names come up…but the majority vote is:] The Rush! [We shoot back to Kyle Lamen at the mall.] Kyle: Would you rather see good wrestling matches, or Corey Williams banter about ethnic slurs and stuff? [The crowd looks on angrily as Kyle holds up a picture of the ugliest man in pro wrestling, “Controversial” Corey Williams. Our scene goes back to Sandra Davis at the bar.] Sandra: More T&A? [A rousing chorus of “YES!” as Sandra wiggles a little in her short skirt. Finally the scene goes back to Kyle in the mall…as he stands on the stage usually reserved for Santa or the Easter Bunny.] Kyle: Ladies and gentlemen who are fans of Primetime Championship Wrestling…a new day has come! Finally…your television Champion will be the true Champion of Television! He adheres to the Malice Crew philosophy of wrestling not for fame, not for glory…but for YOU! The PCW’s loyal fans!! So when you see J-Core wrestle on Fusion…put all of your support behind him! Cheer your hearts out! Because a win for Jason Core is a win for everybody!!! [The crowd cheers appreciatively, as mall security runs out and Kyle is forced to make his escape, drawing this promo to a close.]
