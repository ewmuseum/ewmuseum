Whispers from white padded walls: Pt2(Ignore the other one. This is the real one)

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/12/02 The night was a bleak dark shade, but it was the alley that reeked of future travesties and doom lurking not so far away. Jacob Riley was drunk as usual, a bottle submerged in a brown bag never dropping more than 5 inches from his black stubble mouth whilst singing songs that symbolised rebellion from his childhood. He was a wealthy man, business man, but a man with a wicked addiction he refused to openly confess and face. Jacob Riley was an alcoholic, Jacob Riley was The Seven Bell's bar best customer and Jacob Riley was a man about to witness the short events that would alter his mind for the worse forever. The alley was a shortcut back home, at least he thought so. He forgot a lot of important things when he was drunk, including the way home. Yet the smash of something up ahead of the long narrow dark alley cut of his alcohol fuelled thoughts. He looked up ahead, breathing heavily, his eyes wide. Another smash, followed by some laughter, followed by a shrill painful cry. 'W-w-w-w-w what...who-who's there?!' Jacob slurred out. Then ahead he saw a tall man stumbling, clutching his chest before slumping to the litter and trash filled cold floor of the alley. 'No, please' he cried. Two figures then came into a barely visible view. They were clearly young though, and they were laughing. The man was in pain....and they were laughing. To Jacob Riley, these two men who represented the youth of our generation, were driving this country all the way to hell. Jacob had seen enough, and was ready to yell at them. He hated kids anyway, never had time for them. Just as he was about to though, one of them drew a blade. He could tell by the way the moon flickered and reflected off it. In one quick swift motion one of them then drove the blade right into the throat of the wounded man. 'Good God' Jacob spoke softly to himself. The now dying man gasped for air but found nothing but wisps of it, before he slowly faded from this earth. 'You get the wallet' 'Damn right' Jacob, horrified, dropped his bottle of precious alcohol, sending it smashing to the ground. That was more than enough to get the attention of the two young fiends. 'Well well well, what we got ere'? A spppppppppy?' 'Looks that way to me' shouted their voices as they walked up to him. 'N-N-N-N NO! ' Jacob slurred. 'Here' he drew his wallet and tossed it on the floor ahead of him. 'Take it, it's yours' 'Why thank ye kind sir' one of them spoke maturely while the other chuckled manically. 'But we'll be taking more than your wallet tonight.' 'N-no....please?!' Jacob hopelessly turned to run, but fell almost amusingly. He turned his head from the floor and looked over his shoulder behind him, the two fiends had broken into a jog to reach him. Their night of theft and murdered showed no signs of ending soon. That was until HE came. They were 15 yards away, easily. Then that haunting Shade dropped down before his vision. He swooped down from the shadows and landed between Jacob and the two fiends. Jacob always remembered him as a Demonic Angel. He didn't know whether to feel relieved or more afraid. Slowly and sadistically he thrust his long black leather trench coat out, before tilting his head upwards to look at the two confused and clearly shocked and scared fiends. 'Gentleman........ a little late to be wandering the streets, wouldn't you agree?' Silence from both of them. 'Is that a muted yes or a silent defiant no?' 'N-Y.....who the fuck are you?' 'I am nothing more to you than A Shade gentleman. An avenging sinister paladin. Nothing more.' 'Avenging?' 'You committed a crime gentleman. As far as I am concerned God often works to slowly to carve out his punishments, he is often idle in trying to maintain his mysterious ways. I kindly take it upon myself, occasionally, to place myself in a position where I avenge the crimes he amusingly watches.' 'WE-errrr...... that guy we ...... he mugged us first, it....' 'It was total self defence' Gideon Poe merely looked at the two with his enchanting blue eyes, emotionless and impatient. 'Long have I wandered this world. Controlled by a puppet maser hell bent on turning me into a deluded instrument of pure evil. My plans for my life are of nothing anymore, apart from to defy him. By making things right by others....I feel ever so slightly that I gain a little more sanity and faint drops of purity by doing so.' 'W-..what does that mean?' Jacob looked on from the dark shadows with intrigue and horror. 'Tonight gentleman.........you will be my drops of purity.' Another brief tense silent fell, full of thoughts of what was set to come. 'Thy soul shall find itself alone 'Mid dark thoughts of the grey tomb-stone' More confusion, and more fear. 'Spirits Of The Dead....Edgar Allen Poe' With that, The Avatar Of Death swooped upon them. Jacob Riley watched, in horror. What others saw didn't matter, but what he saw, in his severely drunk state sent the man quite simply insane. He saw a demon emerge... He saw death's new angel strike down... He saw bloodshed and gore decorate the alley wall... He saw a man take pleasure in obtaining what he defined as his small drops of purity ... This man was not of any normal human mind or purpose. The sound of bones being snapped effortlessly.... The sound of blood dripping from deep seething wounds.. But it was the cries. Not cries of two boys who just murdered a man for his wallet, cries from young boys who were sorry for their crimes, and writhing in pain. They were cries from boys who were being tormented by The Avatar Of Death. It was those cries that sent his mind into a downward spiral. 'Have you finished being a hero now?' croaked a voice. The head of The Shade turned to greet the figure of Jeremiah O'Barr. Jacob Riley slowly rose to his feet, fear absorbing him. The cries of the boys still ringing in his head. 'Made a new friend I see?' Gideon turned sharply to where the Elderly Evil Jeremiah O'Barr was pointing, pointing at Jacob Riley from the mist of the alley. Gideon then turned to face the now delusional insane gentleman staring at him wide eyed.. All Jacob could do for speech was yell in muted gasps. His voice had been dissolved into the ether. His mind, his body, were both transfixed and hypnotised by those deep blue eyes of this Angel Of Death. Then there he sat, he slumped back down to the floor of they alley. A voice now, Gideon's voice, spinning around in his head. Spinning along with the boy's cries as Jacob began sobbing, tears streaming down his eyes as he crouched up into a small ball onto the floor of the cold alley floor. The next time he awoke, he would be greeted by the soft white walls of Winterbourne Asylum.
