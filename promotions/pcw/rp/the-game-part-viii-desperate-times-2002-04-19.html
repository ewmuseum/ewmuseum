No more b!tching about my HTML now, ya hear? ;+) Song Listening Guide: 'Take A Look Around' by Limp Bizkit, from Mission Impossible: 2. #I know why you wanna hate me...NOW I know why you wanna hate me!'Cause hate.. is all the world has even seen lately!# BOBBY S. CAMPBELL THE GAME - Part VIII - Desperate Times.... Then.. PCW Wednesday Turmoil.. April 10th.... *edited* 'Don't worry Bobby... we're not going to kill you just yet. You haven't suffered enough....' says Darkstar. The Dark Age hauls the crucifix to an upright position. BS faces his girlfriend. Talena looks to him, then to her... kicking the chair out from under Kira, causing her to fall and the noose to tighten around her neck. Clyde speaks to his prey, unfeeling. 'Don't worry Bobby, she'll be better off...' 'Better off than you in any case,' Badge says, on the brink of laughter. Darkstar chimes in again to agree, 'Yes, she isn't going to suffer nearly as much as you're going to.' With that Bethany emerges from the black, carrying a gasoline can. She douses the volitile liquid on BS... Badge produces a match. 'Nighty night,' Badge says. Badge brings the match to BS's body, but Badge's hand is grabbed by another hand. Zoom out to show that the hand belongs to none other than former PCW Heavyweight champion, Stryfe! 'What the hell?' exclaims Dave Kern from inside the arena. Stormbringer's eyes widen at the sight of the former champ. Badge, Clyde, and the rest of The Dark Age stare at their former stablemate. Stryfe takes the match away from Badge. Then, before it goes out, Stryfe tosses it at Stormbringer causing him to erupt into flames. Clyde grins a very evil grin at the return to the dark side of his former buddy, Stryfe. Kira's eyes watch dimly from her hanging position as Talena sings softly. 'Come on baby light my fire... mwehehehehehehe!!' And now.... * * * There was once a man by the name of Robert Maxwell Stormbringer the second. Now that man's name is Robert Stormbringer Campbell. The last name is now the middle one. You see, in secret this... 'man' went through the legal system to change his name, and not for the first time. The first time was purely cosmetic, to cut the 'bringer' at the end of his name off, to appeal to the masses of inferior lifeforms that were either too lazy, too ignorant, or too stupid to call this 'human' anything but 'Bobby Storm.' The second time was to, of course, change the name back to what it was in the first place. This 'person' who called himself Bobby woke up from his slumber one day to realize that what other people think of you is completely irrelevant. Meaningless. Besides the point. Not your friends. Not your mangy mutt of a canine. Not even your family. Nothing that occurs outside of one's own being counts in the least. So you may ask, 'What are all these reactions..? Then what good are they really for?' They are sweet fruit. Candy for the senses. Since after all, nothing is more stunningly horrid than total silence. Nothing. As recent as early March on these Christian calenders, this 'man' learned of a rather twisted tale. He is fortunate enough to have a twin brother. Or unfortunate, depending upon your own particular point of view. We could go on for hours and hours along their own skewed history. However, we will not. Once again it is not relevant, at least not at the moment. They are both twenty-five years of age. Both will turn twenty-six on October the thirtieth. The day before Halloween. Quite appropriate, don't you think..? This 'twisted tale' that seemed to be cut straight out of a soap opera was this. That a rapidly rising female in the professional wrestling world by the name of Amy Campbell was actually their long lost sister. Revealed by the sinister Randy, unsurprisingly. The most shocking thing of all was by all accounts... it still seems to be true. Most disturbing may be that even though he will never admit it to anyone now, Bobby was most likely very attracted to and infatuated with 'Aimz' from afar, despite his passionatte love for Kira York. Especially given his propensity (pattern) to be very weak and hormonal, so to speak, when it comes to redheaded members of the opposite sex. That would also include women usually known to the masses as 'Mona' and 'Jane Reagan.' But after all, they do say that all good things... must come to an end. 'B.S.', as this so-called human being is often called, is about to be paid a very special visit. There's no need to speak any further on the subject. Time to be the proverbial fly on the wall, as it were. First one of these redheaded woman trouble-makers... then another. Let's get this show on the road.... * * * Oh wait. In case you were wondering. THE GAME began as follows. Newbludd drafted Charlie Loc, much to the chagrin of Bobby Stormbringer. Duke Williams showed up as a special guest referee, alongside Tim Walden and Jason Core. B.S. convinced The End to draft a family member in Aimz. THE GAME was on. First, it must be mentioned just how utterly stunning one Kira York looked in her skimpy cheerleading outfit. Yow! All red and orange. The (at that time) redheaded Kira wore a skin tight long-sleeved cotton top with the shortest skirt you've ever seen! Underneath? A garment that could double as tight underwear that doesn't leave too terribly much to the imagination. Cutie pie face as always, not to mention a fairly ample bossom and plenty other curves to match. It was rumored that Young Tiger collected the dragging and drooling jaw of The Showstopper and gave it back to him. At some point, at least. In other words? Smokin'! Also, do you REALLY care about what Boolie and Munkey looked like after that!? I didn't think so. B.S. quickly scored the first five straight points and also gained three steals in the process in a clear message to all of PTC that wrestling was never his true calling. Basketball was. After jaw jacking intensely with Badge for a moment (due to Badge's return to PCW and interference in Stormbringer's match with Clyde at the time), Honorary Coach Brock Newbludd wisely called timeout to cool off Stormbringer. In a shocking move, B.S. took himself out of THE GAME to let his sister play. Finally, Zero managed to hit The Phantom Booker in the shoulder with a spitball. It was a nice career while it lasted, though. Team Heel's first points were inexplicably scored when Juan Borro kicked the basketball through the hoop from three-point range! Badge somehow resisted throttling him. Already TPB started in on the long-winded 'Boooodddddaaaa' chants since the former two-time PCW Champion hadn't done much until that point. Aimz and Kid Wonder ran circles around Team Heel on the perimeter. Whenever one penetrated (poor choice of words..?), he or she would just pass the ball to Boda and it was Dunk City! Makonie scored the next two points on nice medium range shots, making the score a total of 7-3. Borro sat down at that point to let his foot be attended to, while Loc was put into THE GAME. Duzza and The Rush, 'House Entertainment' for the night, played and scratched such selections as 'One Mic' from Nas, 'Bad Boy For Life' by P. Diddy, 'Girls, Girls, Girls' by Jay-Z, 'Intergalactic' by the Beastie Boys, 'Criminal' by Eminem, among many others. Loc made a pestering effect immediately on defense, coming up with several steals. Also a few cheap elbows into the ribs of his old rival Aimz now and then never hurt any one. At least not Loc. B.S. became infuriated at seeing this and the former high school All-American started coaching from the sidelines, much more loudly than the usually reserved Skylar Green, 'The End'. After a Makonie three-pointer (which gets your team the ball back right away), Badge got an embarrassing windmill jam right over Kid Wonder. Borro in for Makonie after a hard foul on Double M by Boda. Team Heel gets their fifth point in a row after Borro hits an angle bank shot from fifteen feet in the left corner. This time with his hands! End calls timeout without hesitation. The score is 7-6, Team Orlando (plus the redheaded hottie from Nova Scotia. Close enough, right..?) holding onto the lead by the skin of their teeth. In the timeout Boda completely goes off, complaining about needing to be 'fed the ball' and that Aimz was dragging the team down. It was obvious that the b!tching lit a fire under Aimz and she seemed to gain more focus. K-Dub demanded to be taken out. When End obliged and put B.S. back in, K-Dub went over to the scorekeeper's table to kid around with his sist- ERR, his girlfriend and scorekeeper, Amanda. After a few minutes of everyone yelling at K-Dub NOT to reset the game clock to zero every time Duke Williams blows his whistle, play finally continues unabated. The teams were Boda, B.S., and Aimz against Badge, Loc, and Borro. Aimz feeds the ball to Boda in the post. Despite Loc's bothersome double team, Boda dunks all over Badge with relative ease! Boda looks up to the rafters and he seems to be much more interested in trash talking with Phantom Booker than Badge! TPB doesn't bother to do much more than grin at him. Of course, B.S. is right over to pick up the slack with insults right in Badge's ear! Core and Walden have to separate the crooked cop and the intellectual elitist while Duke just enjoys his small bottles of Jack Daniels that he brought along. After Security Chief Jay Sylo threatens the lives of Violence Jack, SyN, and Adrian Voltage up in the crowd, that pretty much ends their argument. Red Rock and Argyle, meanwhile, just discuss the finer points of what else? Sheep. Loc scored points seven and eight for Team Heel on layups, while their dominant player, Badge, scored the ninth point for his team on a very long three-pointer with Aimz AND Stormbringer in his face! However, between flawless three-point-range shots by Aimz and Boda crushing all those in his path down low in the post, the halftime score ends up 11-9 in favor of Team Orlando. All those who need it make quick trips to the concession stands or the restrooms. Meanwhile, all of the team members make their way back to the respective locker rooms, except Bobby Stormbringer who is still the Master Of Ceremonies. The Slam Dunk Contest would be next. But right now? Now let's get this show on the road. Still paying attention..? If you are, then it is high time to talk about yesterday... * * * {Song Change} --4-18-02, Thursday, 1:10 pm EST-- The first scene based in the present day in quite some time fades up on the modest home of PCW's Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer... at least that's what 'they' still think of him as. That and The Showstopper will only be a member of the recently downtrodden wrestling company for two more days. Two days that will pass in a mere blink of an eye. A medium-sized television in a large but simple wooden entertainment center is what can first be seen. A VCR rests on top of the TV set. A Sony Playstation 2, a Nintendo Gamecube, many video games, and a bunch of DVD movies all complete the electronic (plus expensive) ensemble. A framed work of art is hung on the wall above it, as well as a white hands clock. A beige coffee table sits in the middle of the living room and only a remote lays upon it. The PTC Promo Channel is on the TV with very low volume. It appears to be a promo from current two-time PCW World Heavyweight Champion (and someone that B.S. used to mark out for as a fan), Jason Snow. The shot quickly pans to the left in a semi-circle to show B.S.'s valet and girlfriend Kira York sitting on the dark evergreen sofa on the other side of the room. Now the simple computer/dining room can be seen in the background, deeper into the Orlando home. On each side of the couch two black endtables with gold trim can be seen, with a matching lamp on each of them. Kira appears to be leaning forward and watching the Snow promo... but she isn't. In reality she is zoning out and looking completely distressed. Wet streaks make a trail south of her baby blue eyes where tears used to be. She hugs herself tightly for some form of comfort and gently rocks back and forth. Just what in the f*ck do you THINK happens when you see the person you love most on this dustball of a world crucified, burned, and left for dead!? I hope you're not surprised because trauma is a part of every day human life. Some people can just deal with that fact better than others, that's all. Not to mention the fact that she herself was bladed and hung for virtually no other reason than being in a relationship with Bobby 'Stormbringer.' Luckily for Bobby he is one of the chosen. A select few. 'The Perfect Storm' will provide ALL to those that choose to accept it like he has. To live by the universal laws of chaos and try to wade through all challenges and adversity that come before him. Some challenges are just far too difficult to master when you are a mortal such as he. Sometimes challenges come too close together. Too many in number to juggle successfully. This is when 'The Perfect Storm' bestows special... 'rewards' to its most dedicated pawns and subjects. Mr. Prime Time fits into the 'too many to handle' category at this moment. You may think that those challenges are being fired, suffering technical problems with editing his promos, his concussion, the attack he suffered from New Age, trying to keep the Syndicate from falling apart before they can ever mesh together in the first place... Once again. Like usual. You would be wrong. There is a gentle but rapid fire rapping on the front door of the modest home for this young and tired couple. Kira doesn't move. She doesn't even look up. The scene loops itself over and over around the mind of the no longer naive lady from the New England area. The heat of the flames. Her voice leaving her in an instant. The warm blood dripping off her chin. The screams of terror. The rage at the cowardice of it all... At the helplessness of it all. For one instant when Bobby looked at her then she could tell. There was a mixture of guilt and something else in his face. Something that said: 'Everything will be just fine. I'll see to it.' BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG!! Kira jumps right out of her seat and to her feet, her heart leaping with her. Instead of just knocking on the door with a fist, whomever was on the other side now seemed to be kicking the door! Kira opens her mouth to respond but a squeal filled with pain is all she gets for her troubles. Her windpipe was still damaged. She could speak again, but she would have to endure what could only be described as a sharp knife being impaled halfway into her neck if she talked. While rubbing her neck she despondently walks over to the front door, her shoulders hunched. Finally, Kira opens the door to the unwanted outside world while looking down at the ground in front of her. Baggy green khaki pants... black boots? Kira looks up, her nose wrinkled in confusion. When she does she nearly gasps in surprise. 'A-Amy..?' Kira says weakly. The shot changes to one right behind the nineteen-year-old brunette to show that the person behind the door is in fact Old School Wrestling's resident redhead, the forementioned Amy 'Aimz' Campbell. A black mesh Hardy Boyz-like t-shirt with a sultry red top underneath completes the warm weather outfit. For some reason, she also wears a ratty friendship bracelet on her left wrist. Three things are impossible not to stare at when you see her. Unless you're a family member, of course. Her green eyes. Her red hair. And errrmmm... I think you can guess the third thing. Even more easy to see than those right now, however, is the concerned look plastered all over her face. So worried that it may border on straight up panic. A black rent-a-car of some indeterminate model is also seen parked in the background behind Campbell. Kira sees this expression and she immediately wipes her face clean of the old tears with her shirt sleeve. 'Is he here? Is he okay!?' Aimz nearly screams. Aimz barges into the home (even though she's always welcome) and Kira steps aside, wringing her hands together. Aimz whips her head from side to side and grits her teeth. Kira slowly shuts the door behind them and leans against it before mustering up the strength to say something. 'Bob. Bobby... down.' Kira clutches her throat and quite literally growls at the need to struggle to say simple words. Despite the pain, she spits out the end of her sentence. 'Downstairs! Owwwrrr...' Aimz turns around to the slightly taller Kira and she sighs sadly, shaking her head. 'Damn. I'm sorry... I forgot about your throat. I heard what happened last week. Are you two okay??' Kira shoots Amy a nasty look. She has the innate ability, thankfully, to be able to speak with her eyes just about as well as with her speech. The look seems to say, 'Does it really look like we are 'okay'!?' Aimz lowers her eyes and apologizes on instinct, her mind still hell bent on her brother by the same father and different mothers. 'I'm just concerned about you two. I know Clyde and them other guys can be real f*ckin' *ssholes.' Kira takes a deep breath to calm her nerves and she nods to Aimz. Then she waves her hands in front of her in a gentle gesture that seems to say, 'Everything's alright.' Aimz grits her teeth in a jolt of rage against those who hurt Kira and Bobby before wrinkling her forehead and talking at a frantic pace again. 'Kira... you said Bobby's downstairs? Why isn't he in the hospital!? Now I know it was just business between 'em, but I mean he got set up by that slut-b!tch Mona-' Aimz is cut off by the sternly shaking head of a glaring Kira. Kira isn't glaring really at Amy Campbell, but rather the young woman is just bitter and upset at life in general at the cards she and her 'boy toy' have been dealt for the last month or two. It's safe to say that something that pisses The Showstopper off to no end has happened. Innocence has been lost. Beautiful, precious innocence... gone for all time and there isn't a freggin' thing to be done about any of it. Kira walks off to the right, to the smallish hallway between the dining room and the living room. Instead of going to the bathroom or the couple's bedroom, she takes a short right and decends a couple of steps down the stairwell heading down, as opposed to the other staircase leading up towards the modified attic. Kira looks behind her at a confuzzled Aimz and waves her on with a weak smile. Aimz nervously smiles back and follows her most likely future sister-in-law on down to the house's depths.... Where Amy Campbell will be only the third person on Earth to learn of a wild and unimaginable secret. Where she will learn of a secret that has around a fifty fifty chance of completely changing not only her life, but also the future of her entire family line... Forever. The truth of 'The Perfect Storm' will be revealed once and for all because desperate times... call for desperate measures. TO BE CONTINUED. THIS HAS BEEN A B.O.T.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002.
