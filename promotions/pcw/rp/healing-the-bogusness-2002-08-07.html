Healing The Bogusness

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7 [When you see bodaciousness, you want nothing more then to go and grab for it. Because Bodaciousness is really all people need out of life. That's why compadres are here on earth, to experience life and all of it's bodaciousness. Shailyn is total bodaciousness and The Tubular One went for it. He held her by his side like the angel that she deserved to be. He seemed so happy with her around. All he wanted to do was kiss her at least just give her a totally gnarly massage, but she wasn't up for anything like that. Shailyn, the good Christian virgin girl, was saving herself for the one she truly loved and would marry. The Tubular One calmed her and told her that maybe she should at least practice on some dudes. So that way she is most tubular to the special dude she wants to get hitched with. The gnarly celebration was totally intact as the Cowabunga Connection begins it's road to the PCW tag team titles. Everyone was having a most radical time until...that's right...Tommy caught Shailyn at the party with another guy. Not just any guy. Not like some attractive actor or another rival surfer, but the ugliest man she could have picked...RANDY RAGGAMUFFIN!!] [Bogus cannot even begin to describe the situation between the Cowabunga's and Randy Raggamuffin. They all used to be very good friends until that creative nickname, Raggamuffin, came about. Then Randy slowly phased himself out, becoming the joke of the group. He didn't want to be the joke, he wanted to be super cool just like Harry and Tommy were. Every chance they got the Cowabunga's would slam him down, make him feel less than nothing. Was it on purpose? Not really. It's just the way that they are with Raggamuffin. The professional skateboarder wept like a little school girl about most of the things and only vowed revenge on his two former friends. Putting Ex-Lax in Harry's eggs and somehow locking all nearby bathromms making the Heavy Metal One suffer a pain that no man should feel in his rectum. The Ragster then went beyond all duty to even try and recruit fellow skateboarders, Suburban Uprising, to dethrone the Cowabunga's from tag team competition. It didn't work. Now his latest attempt in hurting the Cowabunga's, take away the one thing that Tommy can always get...GIRLS!!] [With Friday Fusion only around the corner, Cowabunga Connection may be in dire straits for their title shots. Harry sounds like David Arquette when he screams and Tommy has locked himself in his room to fall into a dark, deep depression. The Heartbreakers seem to have an easy victory on their hands if things keep going the way that they are] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy lies in his blue and yellow decorated bedroom just staring up at the ceiling. His tanned face is concealed behind a large amount of stubble, from days of not shaving. The blue silk covers have been thrown to the floor and the yellow comforter is the only covering that remains on the bed. The television is smashed in, his stereo has been thrown to the opposite side of the room, and his surfboard THANKFULLY remains in perfect condition] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: In My Room....In My Roooooom. [The familiar Beach Boys song echoes through his mind and out of his mouth. He'd put it on the stereo but it seems that he smashed it off of the wall across the way. Suddenly there is a loud knock at the door. Harry's voice echoes through the blue and yellow oak door] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: The therapist is here, man!! [Tommy slightly raises his head and looks at the door with anger] TTT: I DON'T NEED A THERAPIST, DUDE!!! Harry: You need to get that out of your head, man. Come on out, listen to this guy and we can headbang to some Twisted Sister! TTT: NOOO!!! [There is a few moments of silence, then Harry finally chimes back in] Harry: Then I'll talk to him about my David Arquette problem, man. TTT: GOOD!!! Harry: Later, man. [Tommy looks back up at the blue and yellow door and suddenly realizes that he's never been that pissed off at a door before. It felt kind of good. Gnarly] [Outside in the stylish living room, Harry leaps onto the black leather couch and lies down. He's ready for a far out session with this cool guy joe therapist. The very short, balding therapist takes a seat adjacent from Harry with a clipboard in his hand. The wrinkles and lines in his face show he's seen a lot of stress in his life. They crinkle in confusion as he wonders where Tommy is at and why the weird metalhead has suddenly taken the couch over] Therapist: Ummm, where's Tommy? [Harry shrugs] Harry: He doesn't want to talk, man. [The therapist then looks behind him toward the direction of Tommy's room, then back at Harry] Therapist: All right. Well, I think I'll go back to my office now. I have plenty of clients to attend to this evening. Harry: No way, man. I've got a problem. Therapist: Hmmmm, I suppose I could listen to you since the session was paid for in advance. [Harry smiles, happy that he is finally going to get help with his David Arquette syndrome] Therapist: So, what is on your mind, Harry? [Harry breathes in and breathes out deeply, and then goes on to explain his situation] Harry: We had this far out party at Hotel California this past weekend to celebrate our win over Pretty Cool Guys, man. I need to sleep because all I smell is 2000 Flushes Blue, man. I'm knocking and knocking and this far out chick opens the door with a bedsheet covering his naked body, man. She drops it and says, 'Take Me', man. It was just a totally psychadelic experience that I handed her the sheet back and she covered up her far out naked body, man. Does that make me gay, man? [The therapist just nods his head not really sure that understood all of that. He just shrugs] Therapist: Well, that could mean a lot of things, Harry. It could mean your gay and you don't have any sexual feeling toward women. Maybe your even repulsed by women so you wanted her to cover up. Or it could mean that you were being a perfect gentleman, not wanting to take advantage of the poor soul. Harry: What would you have done it that situation, man? Therapist: I would have boinked her!!! Harry: Oh, man. Therapist: I get the feeling that that isn't the problem. Harry: It's part of it, man. Therapist: Go on, Harry. [Harry then breathes in and out, as if trying not to pass out or throw up. This is going to be tough to say] Harry: I'm feeling a lot of pressure lately because of our upcoming title shots against The Heartbreakers, man. We've beaten them before and we've been far out in every way possible, man. It's just with Tommy all depressed about his girl knocking boots with Randy Raggamuffin.... [The Therapist interjects himself with a giggle behind his interjection] Therapist: You mean that really ugly skateboarder?! Harry: Yeah, man. [The Therapist then holds in the laughter and motions to Harry to keep going] Harry: To say the absolute least, I'm feeling a total bummer, man. Then with the bedsheet thing and her large boyfriend in a G-String calling me gay. The whole 2000 Flushes Blue incident...I started screaming like David Arquette. [The Therapist now works off his laughter and turns serious] Therapist: David Arquette...that's pretty serious. Harry: I know, man. Therapist: This is a proven syndrome, Harry. Harry: Cool...man... Therapist: I don't really see it truly effecting you that bad though. [Suddenly a tomato smacks the sliding screen door and a scratchy voice is heard in the distance] Scratchy voice: YOUR GAY!!!! [Harry leaps up from the couch and points at the door with his index finger. His voice screeches EXACTLY like David Arquette] Harry: I'M NOT GAY!!!!!!!!!! [The Therapist looks in absolute terror] Therapist: Sweet Jesus....your INFECTED!!! [Harry's face drops in horror] Harry: Not far out, man. Not far out at all. [The scene now switches back to The Tubular One, lying in his bed. His happy go lucky smile totally wiped away, FOR GOOD! No more being bodacious in a far out kind of way, it's only time for bogusness in a most heinous kind of way. You can't just sit around and think that everything is gnarly and radical. What the hell is Tommy thinking?! When it comes straight down to it, the world is TOTALLY bogus. Look at all the violence. The divorce rate. The economy. It's all totally bogus because all the people in the world are bogus. Tommy tried to make a difference, he tried to show the world that the world can be totally far out in a bodacious kind of way on the other side of the tracks. People just took his words for granted and used them to better themselves. Shailyn certainly did it. Raggamuffin certainly did it by stealing her, knowing full well that Tommy had something going with her. Bogus, all of it!] [On Tommy's back deck a very dirty, ugly homeless man stands there. Tommy locks eyes with the fat bearded man and thinks that maybe he's that totally bogus Satan dude coming to take his soul] Homeless man: NEED...FOOD!!! [Tommy gets up from his bed, only in his blue boxer shorts with little yellow surfboards on them. He opens the sliding glass door slightly and gives the homeless man a very tired look] TTT: Can't you see I'm trying to wallow in my self-bogusness, dude? Homeless Man: Yeah, somethin' like that. Listen...I got my pecker shot off in the war and I need some cash so I can go get a new pecker!! TTT: WHAT?!?! [The homeless man thinks about that but then realizes that his drunked mind spit out the wrong thing] Homeless Man: NOOOOOOO!!! I mean I got my right hand shot off in the war and I need to get a new set of BALLS!! [Tommy looks at the Homeless Man's right hand and sees that it is perfecty intact. His left hand is perfectly intact as well. Tommy really doesn't want to know about the balls] TTT: Dude.... [The Homeless man then thinks about it once again. WAIT A MINUTE!! You can't have balls on your hand. You can have balls IN YOUR HAND, but not as your hand. That's REALLY messed up. But...you need to be missing your hand to need a new anything. Damn that BOX O' WINE HE GOT AT THE MINI-MART!!!!] Homeless Man: Listen. I'm f*cking hungry and I need some cash. [Tommy shrugs and walks over to his wallet. He pulls out two crisp one hundred dollar bills and hands them over to the Homeless Man. The guys eyes bug out of his head as he begins doing the electric slide on the deck and then turns it into a beautifully performed moonwalk. Tommy looks pretty impressed by this old, dirty, and bearded Homeless Man] TTT: Totally gnarly, dude. Homeless Man: ME?!?! NO WAY!! YOUR TOTALLY GNARLY, SON!!! You just gave me 200 bucks!!! I would love to just kiss you right now! [Tommy looks disgusted] TTT: No way, dude. [The Homeless Man DEFINITELY had too much Box O' Wine] Homeless Man: Right. Listen, I'm gonna help you out. I can go buy me a life time supply of Happy Meals now so I'm in some debt to you. TTT: It's all right, homeless compadre. I'm going to go back to wallowing in my self-bogusness. [The Homeless Man just shakes his head as Tommy begins his trek back to the waterbed] Homeless Man: She still wants you, Tommy. [Tommy's eyes bug out of his head as he turns to the Homeless Man] TTT: WHAT?? Homeless Man: The girl that you yearn for. She wears the chasity belt of truth and uses the whips of her words to stop your most deep sexual urges. Not many girls have done that to you, in fact, no girl has ever done that to you. You get your sex easy, all night, and sometimes five times a night. You animal. [Tommy smiles and nods] Homeless Man: The Raggamuffin only despises what he cannot have, so he must destroy everything that is beautiful. He will succeed if you let him succeed, Tubular One. You cannot let the evil swallow you into the shadows!! You must rise up and say, 'DUDES!!! I'm Tubular AND YOUR NOT!!!!' Your going to be ONE HALF OF THE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS BY FRIDAY!!! YOU WILL DESTROY PINK!!! YOU WILL GET THE GIRL!!! YOU WILL EAT CAKE!!! TTT: CAKE IS BODACIOUS!!!! Homeless Man: Yes it is. [Tommy looks down to the floor and can feel the bogusness leaving his body. Bodacious feelings have returned to him. It was like some kind of disease that infected his gnarly cells, but he just needed a little bodacious medicine to cure it!! The Homeless Man has cured Tommy!!! He's BODACIOUS AGAIN!!! Tommy looks up.....and the Homeless Man has disappeared] TTT: Who was that dude? [The scene then moves to below the deck, where the Homeless Man lies with his legs behind him. Pain ravages his face] Homeless Man: I...fell....down. [The scene then fades to black]
