A Year In The Making - Aimz (Part 2)

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21 Jan 2003 Recap: During the first outing in this saga we were acquainted with various talents across the Wrestle Tattler community, be it Primetime Championship Wrestling's alpha male Xavier Kannon, RUSH's booze fuelled brawler Brock Newbludd and Old School Wrestling's satanic goliath Hessian. However neither of these headline stars were at this graduation ceremony to receive recognition as the honour had already been bestowed upon four deserving individuals in Angelo Deville, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Amy 'Aimz' Campbell. Angelo Deville Former 'Five Star Wrestling' performer who as the current Universal Champion is regarded as the hottest free agent on the open market, the once protégé of Wrestle Tattler great Tyler Nelson. Nascosto Ireul Nerezza Three time PCW Tag Team Champion, numerous awards, once a member of Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A) and currently the only vampire residing in the Wrestle Tattler community. There isn't a thing you could say that hasn't already been said, the product of the Academy's Hessian era. Gaz 'The Jester' Green Again, another former member of Pranks With Attitude, Gaz Green is himself a three time PCW Tag Team Champion. The Jester recently found himself inducted into Xavier Kannon's private circle known as 'The Front Office' alongside the likes of Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson. Amy 'Aimz' Campbell Former 'Old School Wrestling' performer, Aimz was once the stable mate of World Champion great Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes in The Elite and has even wrestled for that covenant prize. One of the most respected females in the industry she may have left the Wrestle Tattler community, yet missed she will certainly not be forgotten. '...Speaking of which... I will now hand you over to Amy 'Aimz' Campbell...' The roar of Hessian seems warm and hearted as the crowd stare at the behemoth as though he were soft yet before Amy Campbell could take to the stage the goliath concludes in a more familiar tone. 'Oh... And all go and fucking rot in the dankest pits of hell!' A cheer erupts as the spotlight locates that of Amy Campbell who looks the picture of health as she stands to attention, momentarily soaking in the positive reaction of her return before heading to the altar. Toying with her infamous red hair she steps onto the platform passing former wrestling colleague Hessian and even taking the time to snarl towards his obsessive Imp, Minion. Smiling she looks out to the congregation, her eyes roaming those present she finally begins. 'If you can bare with me... I have prepared a speech...' Dropping her hands into her cargo pants she rummages about for a few seconds drawing a gleaming smile from Minion who mutters 'Firesnatch~!' under his breath as he rubs his purple hands in glee. A few wayward seconds pass and Amy locates the piece of paper and unravelled it so she can begin. 'Working in the Academy gave me time to fine tune myself for the journey that lay ahead and more so it gave me an opportunity to meet many of you I have come to deem as friends....' informed the Cruiserweight as the crowd clapped in appreciation. 'The Academy was quite the ride... So too was the opportunity to showcase Aimz on a bigger stage in Old School Wrestling, where I was given the chance to wrestle alongside Sebastian Toomes, Jane Reagan, Seth Masters and even Trevor Hawke....' murmured the red-headed female as she pointed out her former stable mate, Civil Disobediences very own Trevor Hawke. Giving Amy Campbell the thumbs up, she peers down at her speech once more to conclude. 'Firesnatch~!' muttered Minion. 'Indeed....' retorts his mountainous client Hessian, growling from beneath his skull fronted facial mask. 'Firesnatch... Firesnatch... Firesnatch... Firesnatch... Firesnatch!' Suddenly Minion in his crippled condition dived from his wheel chair like a bloodthirsty hound, grasping his small hands around the leg of Amy Campbell before rotating his hips in a sexually suggestive manner. Up and down, left and right, the Imp held on for dear life as Aimz tried desperately to dislodge him, Hessian waving his head in shame as the purple headed warrior bounced about screaming 'Firesnatch~!' The charade turned the crowd into a hysterical mess and laughter broke out from all four corners of the room. With Minion The Imp bucking as though he were straddled to a horse, those watching felt it necessary to comment. 'Oh good... Xavier you like so brought me to a pantomime and I thought this was going to be some boring wrestling function... YAY...' injected the ditzy diva as her husband held his head in shame, almost a re-enactment of Hessian's earlier retort. 'What’s going on?' questioned the artist formerly known as The End, Skylar Green. 'Damn I wish Quo could've made it... He would say something to the tune of... Typical of some white cracka to make a mothafuckin fool of de'm selves, holla back youngin!' joked Dante Jackson as he watched the shenanigans of the stage escalate to the point where Hessian intervenes. On stage the former 'Cultist' snags his valet by the back of his dirty robe and wrenches Minion off his victim of Amy Campbell, obviously to her relief. Aimz controlled her breathing after the ordeal and looking over to the bemused crowd with a look of embarrassment before returning to her cut off... No, untimely interruption, she picks up where she had left off. 'Err... Arr... I'm sure there was a sign outside stating no pet's allowed!' began Amy Campbell as she recomposed herself to conclude her acceptance into the honouree graduates list. 'Besides those I have worked with I would also like to take this time to give a special thank you to those who have aided me during this time... So... A BIG thank you to my family and friends, especially... Bobby Stormbringer for his support... What?' roared the former OSW Cruiserweight Champion, angered. Infuriated, her eyes glazed over with rage, the diva snarls once more down the set-up of microphones. 'IS THIS SOME KIND OF SICK JOKE?!?' 'I THOUGHT I LEFT THESE STALKER JOKES WHEN I SIGNED FOR FWO!!!' 'THIS SHIT ISN'T FUNNY NO-MORE....' Almost at the end of her tether, Amy broke down in a flood of tears, her make-up running like a cascade of colour she finally snaps and sets hotfooted from the premises as those in attendance watch in aw, star struck. Her departure somewhat unexpected, those watchful eyes sway back to the built up pedestal at the front where Hessian stands dumbfounded, no contingency except that of continuing the show as he announces the next graduate. 'Well Amy's departure was like her OSW career... Short and sweet... So as we bid the Firesnatch good-bye our next graduate was a hand groomed student of mine and maybe one day 'The Malice Man' Duke Williams will actually pronounce his name correctly... NEREZZA~!' The vampire stands erect, almost blinded by the piercing white spotlight that shines down, before proceeding towards the altar to say his piece. - To be continued -
