Redneck Special Forces (part 1)

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 23, 2002 Have you all heard the joke, that in recent attempts to find and drive the Taliban and Al Qaeda out of the mountains of the Afghanistan, the United States Government, sent in the elite force known as the Redneck Special Forces. It was funny, we all had a laugh, aside from one man, who saw the joke, and it’s message as a bright shining beacon, and a true answer to the problem, that man, George W. Bush. The House of Congress December 12, 2002 All eyes in congress burned into the man at the podium, as he made his proposition. Murmurs and gasps flew through out the large speaking hall. Eyes were widened, and sniggers were made, as George W. Bush proclaimed his newest task to try and attack terrorism. By terrorizing it before it had the chance to terrorize us, or something like that. G.W.: Now, ya’ see that’s mah plan. Sure, it has a few things to be fiximafied, but ya get the general drift, right? Random Senator: Right… Sir, have we mentioned that you are a complete jackass? G.W. Bush scowls, and whispers into his lapel, and slowly the black clad members of the FBI, swarm around the random senator, and pointing their Uzi’s at him, and slowly guide him out of the office. G.W. just looks up and gives a nice, sweet, smile, and puts his hands in his pockets. G.W.: So, Any other questimitories? The senate was quite, and G.W. nodded his large, pumpkin-esq. head, satisfied with his speaking prowess and he simply strolls out of the senate, whistling Dixie as he goes to take a siesta under his desk in the oval office. After the Nap, the refreshed G.O.P sat down on the floor, in his little cowboy pajamas, a crayon in hand, as he searched to finish the schematics of his master plan for War on Terrorism. He made his list of potential candidates, and grinned, holding it up to the light, but just then, he realized something wasn’t right. G.W.: LAURA! Ah need help with mah new War on Terrorism Plan! Laura Bush shuddered, and her left eye twitched, just ever so slightly, she smoothed down her dress, and sighed, remembering that it was only a few more years, until she was out of this hell. She smiled sweetly, and called up to her husband. Laura: George, dear. Why don’t you just watch some TV? I’m busy right now; the girls are drunk again, and are now throwing up all over. G.W.: Did the police catch them this time? Laura: No! They managed to stay away from the police. G.W.: Well, then give ‘em some pot, for bein’ Daddy’s smart little girls. G.W. nodded, with a grin, then picked up the remote control to his TV, and flicked over a few channels. Humming to himself as he looked for that certain channel to watch, while he worked on his schematics, in crayon, of course. Then, he hears is the call that strikes terror into the hearts of all villains everywhere. “Dudda dudda dudda dudda BATMAN!” George W. Bush slowly came to his feet, the crayon falling from his sweaty palm, and a small glimmer of.. Well.. Something, that normally only comes into his eyes, when he orders a deed of mass destruction, or another death penalty in Texas. The glimmer which often could be mistaken as a glimmer or shard of intelligence, that most people think G.W. Bush is incapable of having, which, they would be correct, it was just gas. The gas was quickly expelled in a giggling fit of the President of the United States of America, in the utmost dignity, of his position. G.W. continued to stare at the screen of the television, in a trance, at least until he started hacking and coughing at his own Texas sized stench. G.W.: Euraka! No.. Wait.. Ekrua? Naw, that ain’t it.. dag nabbit! A kind aide stepped in, and offers some kind advice. Aide: Eureka, sir? G.W.: Yes! Eureka! G.W. Bush nodded his head soundly, a pleased look on his face, and turns to the kind aide. G.W.: Oh, yer’ fired. Ya’ll gotta remember ta’ never correct the president. The aide’s face sours, and he grumbles. Aide: Well, I just got screwed. G.W.: Ah ain’t any dern Demmercrat! Slowly the Aide was taken a hold of by the Secret Service, who escorted him down the hall, and to the electric chair Bush had set up in the green room, for entertainment purposes. There was a screech, and a power surge, and Bush skipped happily back to the TV, the plastic cowboy hat being kept in place by the elastic string that was under his chin. He went back to watching his epiphany, in a state of oafish awe. G.W. slowly reached out placing his digits on the warm screen of the colour TV. G.W. Bush: Tha’s what ah’ need! He bounced up and down on his bottom, resuming to giggle like a madly, as Adam West, knocked out the Joker, and the animated “BIFF!” Followed, and Bush clapped his hands, taking in the original, campy, Batman show. G.W. Bush: Tha’s what I need! He repeated himself, in high-pitched glee. G.W. Bush: Ah’ needs a man to strike fear inta’ the hearts of the Tale-bhan! I will find that person, n’ he will lead the Redneck Special Forces into Afghan.. Afghanima.. The terrorist place! Somewhere, in the middle of Canada, after Decimation.. The Masked Mullet blinks, and cocks his head over to the side, a confused look on his face. Vanilla Skillz, glances over to his friend, as the doctor tapes him up, and in between his uncomfortable squirming, and laughing as Venessa tickles the back of his neck with a loving caress, he manages to ask a question. Vanilla Skillz: Yo, Dawg.. Y’all seem distracted, what’s the dillio? The Masked Mullet blinks, and looks upward, at the hospital ceiling; he reaches up, and lightly scratches the top of his mighty hairdo. The Masked Mullet: Ah’ dunno Skillz, ther’s jist this disturbance in the Mullet.. Vanilla Skillz: Is it lice? Skillz scoots over on the doctor’s table a bit, to get away from Mullet, Venessa giggling lightly, covering her mouth with her hand. The Masked Mullet: NO! IT AIN’T LICE! S’just.. Ah’ have a bad feelin’.. A very bad feelin’
