Wind, Snow, and Kissing: sounds like there’s a storm-a-brewing!

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/3/02 Rain, Wind, Snow, and Kissing: sounds like there’s a storm-a-brewing! The scene opens up on The Pharaoh pacing back and forth in the lounge of the Palace situated in Anaheim, California. Pharaoh suddenly stops dead in his tracks and looks forth out of a distant window. Tiny raindrops have rushed over the transparent surface and a strong wind is present in the air outside. It howls and growls and lashes the raindrops up against the windows strong wooden frame, dampening it to the hilt. Titu sighs and then suddenly swings around to find standing behind him his wife Isiy, arms crossed over her chest and a worried expression painted upon her beautiful face. Isiy: What’s the matter, my love? Pharaoh turns around so that he is face to face with his wife; he then places his arms behind his back and sighs sternly. The Pharaoh: Is it not obvious? Isiy: No… The Pharaoh: Take a look outside, my dear… Isiy moves her concentration away from Titu and looks now towards the window. The glass is now completely covered in raindrops. Isiy: It’s only raining, Titu…what’s so bad about that? Pharaoh sighs. The Pharaoh: Come now, Isiy! Is this not a sign from the Gods? Think about whom I must face this coming Friday Fusion and then tell me why you think I’m worried … Isiy stands for a moment contemplating Pharaoh’s words. Eventually she comes to her own conclusion. Isiy: Well you face Big Davelicious… Pharaoh cuts in. The Pharaoh: Do you really believe that I am truly bothered by the one named Big Davelicious? NO! I have defeated him once already along with that ignorant six fingered freak, Cisco! I am worried about the one they call Bobby Stormbringer! Isiy: But Stormbringer is nothing in comparison to you, Titu, why should you be worried about a minutely skilled competitor such as he? Pharaoh turns back to the window. The Pharaoh: Take a good look outside, my dearest. This weather, is it not something that one would call the beginning of a storm? Isiy: Well yes, I suppose one would call it that… The Pharaoh: Then why can you not see the reason for my worry? Stormbringer is actually one the best technical wrestlers in PCW. He is one of the fastest growing superstars in PCW and this Friday I, along with Big Davelicious and a mystery opponent must fight him for a chance to become number one contender to that Rat, Akiyama’s TV title! Look at the weather Isiy; the gods have brought this storm upon us as a sign of things to come! This is a warning for me not to underestimate Stormbringer! Isiy frowns. Isiy: You worry too much about too little, Titu. What of this mystery opponent? In my opinion he is the one you should be most worried about! With Stormbringer you can study him and his movements but with this mystery man you can do little to protect yourself from him! Pharaoh smiles. The Pharaoh: I guess you are right enough, but Stormbringer is, in my opinion, the one that I need to watch out for. This storm, it is a sign I tell you! A sign of things that could be! I must prepare for him…and the mystery opponent too, I guess! Isiy: I see little reason why you are worrying about him so much, my love…he is highly overrated! The Pharaoh: That is true… Isiy: And he thinks he’s funny, when in actual fact…he isn’t! The Pharaoh: Good point… Isiy: And to be perfectly frank…well…he sucks! The Pharaoh: Another good point… Isiy smiles in victory of completely summing up Stormbringer as she sees him. The Pharaoh: But he is still someone to be monitored carefully…in our match I shall make sure that I dispose of him quickly, followed by Big Davelicious and then the mystery opponent, whoever he or she may be! Isiy walks towards her husband and places a caring hand on his immense, brown shoulder. Isiy: Now you’re coming around, darling! Things aren’t that bad… The Pharaoh: Oh it isn’t the fact that I cannot defeat Bobby, because I can, it’s just this storm! It is very rare to see such a fierce storm brewing in California. It troubles me…much like Stormbringer’s ignorance. Isiy: Yet surly this storm cannot be a sign from the Gods to warn you of a man such as the so-called “Showstopper”. He’s hardly worth causing a storm of this magnitude. Outside the window the storm rages on, much worse than a few moments ago. A chill air suddenly takes an effect on the rain, seemingly freezing much of it. Now large and fierce snowflakes pelt the windows of the palace and the surrounding houses. With a frown of great sarcasm Pharaoh turns to his wife. The Pharaoh: Then why is it snowing outside? Isiy: Because it’s winter? The Pharaoh: Winter has long left us, Isiy, and this is California we’re talking about! This is a work of my fellow Gods! Pharaoh sighs and closes a pair of red and maroon curtains over the window just as a booming roll of thunder floods over the land and a spike of lightning hits a tree outside tumbling it to the soggy ground with a crash. The Pharaoh: Damn Stormbringer! To get a chance to destroy that Demigod Akiyama I must first go through him and judging on the events taking place outside things do not look too great on me coming out of that ring victorious. Isiy: This event may have nothing to do with your fellow friends and Gods; it may simply be a coincidence. The Pharaoh: That is true enough. But I cannot take any risks. I must prepare myself well for Stormbringer this Friday if I am to claim the number one contenders spot to the TV title! The Egyptian Gods are warning me not to be so cocky on this occasion and I must take their Godly advice. Isiy: Do as you wish, Titu, but I truly don’t think you should worry about an inferior Mortal such as Bobby “The Showcrapper” Stormbringer. The Pharaoh sternly looks down upon his wife’s pretty, grinning face. The Pharaoh: Do you have something against Stormbringer that I do not now of? Isiy: No… The Pharaoh: Then why on this dreadful planet have you been insulting him non-stop for the past ten minutes? Isiy: Well I don’t like to see you getting upset like this… The Pharaoh: I aren’t even upset, I’m just worried, that’s all… Isiy: Then why not go and see Horus and ask him if this storm is being caused because of Stormbringer? Titu smiles arrogantly. The Pharaoh: That is in actual fact a fairly good idea, Isiy, my love! Isiy smiles proudly. Isiy: Thank you… The Pharaoh: Yes…then I must go now! I have Horus to see! Pharaoh walks across the room towards a huge door on the right hand wall of the lounge. Isiy: You’re going now? The Pharaoh: Yes! I have no time to lose! Isiy sighs. Isiy: Then wait for me! And is Carmoon coming along with us? Pharaoh stops his advance on the door. The Pharaoh: Why exactly are you coming? Isiy: Because I’m your wife and I want to come along, I hardly get out of this house! Pharaoh sarcastically looks at his wife. The Pharaoh: Isiy, how in Duat can you say that you don’t get out? You’re a Goddess in the unholy name of Osiris! It isn’t my fault that you don’t use your teleportation powers to visit exotic places now is it?! Isiy: Why are you so snappy lately?! Pharaoh sighs and shakes his head. The Pharaoh: Take three guesses, my love! Isiy is about to when Titu cuts in on her. The Pharaoh: Akiyama pissed me off! I lost My Tag team title! And now the Gods are giving me signs that I might not be able to defeat Stormbringer so that I can face that piece of scum Akira Akiyama. That’s why I’m so “snappy” lately… Isiy: Well you don’t have to take it out on me! I didn’t ask the Neo Void God to piss you off, I didn’t cost you your tag team title and I am not one of the Gods who might, and I repeat might, be sending down this storm as a sign that you can’t beat Stormbringer! So back off, Titu! Pharaoh walks towards his wife and hugs her, picking her five foot six inch frame up off the ground. The Pharaoh: Well what can I say? Pharaoh smiles. The Pharaoh: I’m sorry, my lovely Egyptian Goddess! The two share a passionate kiss and then Pharaoh looks deep into Isiy’s eyes. The Pharaoh: Come then…let us both go and see Horus! Carmoon is not needed… Isiy smiles excitedly as Pharaoh places her feet back down on the floor. The scene fades as, holding hands Pharaoh and Isiy leave the lounge…
