I brung the weed to my lips once more, puffing on it before watching smoke ooze out from my mouth in black intoxicating clouds. The sun beat in from the window making lines of neon-light across my brown intimidating body with 'Piru' etched out on my chest. Buuday and Sheeca were lord knows where, I could give a fuck at the time. Ring...Ring...Ring... | 'Hello?' Like I wasn't quite fully in my body I pulled my shit together to reach over and answer the red phone which sat on my oak wood dresser. 'Who da fuck iz diz?' All voices that day came new to me. I was completely out of it. 'Wut? Man, you need to stop smoking that sticky-icky-icky shit partna'' Still in an extreme state of perplexity he continued to talk on about some things that didn't really stick in my mind. 'Blah blah blah, Blah blah blah, Blah blah blah...' It all mumbled like a school teacher on a Charlie Brown cartoon, I didn't understand it, but like the kids on the cartoon, I just nodded my head and acted like I knew. 'Wait, who da fuck are you again?' After that all I heard was 'FUCK YOU' and the sound of the dial tone. Soon following, I let the phone slide out my hands hitting the red carpet floor below with a Thud! | High as hell, my mind started to ponder away. Thinking up shit that makes no sense to a sober person but funny ass fuck to a higher than a motha fucka person like myself at the time. | | Feeling like the room was spinning in circles, I wiped the sweat which was seeping from my forehead at a extraordinary fast pace away and took another puff of my weed before setting it back on the ash tray which sat on the dresser besides me. Apart of me felt like picking up the dirty drawls, shirt, pants, and all other accessories worn on the naked body that laid around the room but then again I knew my black ass wasn't going to get up and do shit about it. A breeze brung the noise of Atlanta streets into my room while at the same time pushing forward the wooden blinds making a clacking sound as the blinds would soon come back and hit the wall because they were to big to hang inside the window area. As I sat there I could here the front door swing open, allowing Buuday and Sheeca to laugh historically at things ghetto woman would laugh at. | | By that time I knew, 'this bitch was trying to kill my ass.' But I couldn't let that concern me now. I had Piru business to take care of. The dimmed now turned orange/red light from the setting sun projected barely bright blindly light off my platinum silver 20 inch dubs as I cruised down the street sipping on Gin and Juice. Broke down apartment buildings pictured on the outside with ghetto kids running ramped, red brick building up the structure, thugs thugging, and old people still trying to get their thug on all flew like a flip book through my heavily dark tented windows. I turned on Piru street and pulled up to my boy's house, Darnel. | | Approaching my Benz with abrupt steps and a guilty mask; Darnal carried himself. Laid back in my black leather coated seat, head tilted towards the dark tented window at which came down stealth like. Stopping short enough to see half my face. I passed the poison, he exchanged for money. Before all the smoke dispersed in flowing waves, crawling to the clouds above, my window crushed visible light putting my second oxygon(weed smoke) in an entrapment.
