The Return of Momma Tomas

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-2-03 RP 1 Turmoil 4th It’s Saturday morning. The neck brace has been off for a day now. Luckily the injury was only a major sprain. The doctor had said if she hadn’t been as physically fit, the neck would more than likely be broken. During her time off, Teresa has been spending time with a said friend in Louisiana. Though their secret isn’t much of a secret anymore, the couple attempts to keep their relationship on a low profile as much as possible. Our Television Champion wakens to the smell of bacon, eggs, and coffee. A smile forms on her face thinking Scott had woken before her and is now downstairs cooking breakfast. To her dismay, when Teresa opens her eyes, there lays Scott sleeping like a baby. If Scott is here, then who is downstairs? Quiet as a mouse, Teresa slips out of the king-size bed. Her and Scott had stayed up most of the night watching movies and she doesn’t want to wake him because she knows he will try to keep her upstairs. She tries to make as little noise as possible as she inches her way to the closet incase the intruder decides to make a quick get away out the back door. She grabs her 12-gauge shotgun out of the closet. Just like American Express, she never leaves home without it. The aroma of the food grows stronger once her feet reach the last step. The kitchen is to the right and her steps are made softly. Who knows what crazed lunatic has broken in the famous wrestler’s house just to cook breakfast. The double barrels of the shotgun are the first to enter the kitchen followed by Teresa. “OK, stop what you are doing and don’t move or I’ll blow your head off.” Teresa speaks these words before her eyes can see the old woman glaring at her with a hot greasy spatula in her hand. “Momma?” “Teresa Jane Tomas, put that thing away before you take someone’s eye out. Haven’t I taught you not to point guns in the house? Now run upstairs and wake what’s-his-face. Breakfast is ready.” Teresa slowly lowers the shotgun while staring at her mother. The poor lady is in her sixties and still is kicking as if she was forty. Her gray hair is hidden beneath a light brown hair color. She wears a homemade light blue dress with white trim. Around her waist is a beige colored apron she obviously bright with her from Tennessee. A pair of glasses rests perfectly over her wrinkled face. Once she had scolded her only daughter, Momma Tomas turns her back and turns a slice of bacon. The 12-gauge shotgun is now leaning in a corner. Teresa turns back toward the door only to be met with two arms wrapping around her; a warm hug and a soft kiss good morning. “Ymm, fixing me breakfast Baby? Smells good.” To his surprise, the response came from inside the kitchen and not from Teresa. “Thank you Sugar, but I’m not your Baby.” Scott looks down to Teresa with a suspicious eye. She answers with a shrug. The two step into the kitchen and takes a seat at the table. Neither knows how or why Momma Tomas is here. “Momma, how did you get in?” Teresa asks while pouring everyone a glass of milk. Momma Tomas pulls a key from her apron and places it on the table beside Scott. “I found it under your place mat. I’d find another hiding place if I was you. And what in heavens did you do to you hair boy?” It’s been months since Momma Tomas seen Scott. He has cut his hair shorter and a goatee covers his chin. He combs his fingers through his dark brown hair and opens his mouth to speak but Teresa interrupts. “Momma! Don’t start on him please. This is his house and not yours. I’m glad to see you, but why did you come?” For a moment, Momma Tomas takes her eyes of Scott and pours herself a cup of coffee. She waits to sit in her seat before answering. “I watch your shows Hun. I saw what Brandi done to you and that Walden boy done afterwards. As a mother, it is my job to make sure you are well taken care of. And that is exactly what I intend to do.” “How long are you planning on staying Mrs. Tomas?” Scott asks cautiously before taking a bite of his eggs. He knows ever since he has been involved with Teresa, the woman has not thought highly of him. The old woman is old fashioned and set in her ways. If it was up to her, Teresa would still be making ties with Jack Slayter but we all know that isn’t happening in the new future. “As long as it takes Mr. Taylor. However long it takes for the doctors to say her neck has healed 100 percent, I’ll be right here. Since you’re around, I might as well make sure you’ll take care of her when I go home.” Scott picks up his glass of milk and takes one long drink trying desperately not to say what he is thinking. Perhaps he’ll never understand the grudge this woman has on him. In the meantime, Teresa is eating quietly. She learned as a child it is better sometimes not to say much when Momma Tomas is in one of her ‘moods’. Out of the blue however, Momma Tomas says something that causes Teresa to nearly choke on her toast. “Teresa dear, why don’t you get to know that Duke feller a little more. He seems to be a nice man. You’ll have to bring him to Tennessee to meet me sometime. I’ll cook..” “MOTHER!” Teresa bursts out. Momma Tomas gives her daughter an innocent glance and Scott leaves the room.
