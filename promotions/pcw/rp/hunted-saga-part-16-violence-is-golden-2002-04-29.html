Hunted Saga Part 16: VIOLENCE is GOLDEN

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/4/02 Hunted Saga Part 16: VIOLENCE is GOLDEN… Last time… Shun-Tu steps forward. “I really should have realised that you would not be taken easily John Crichton, after all you have managed to evade the army for a very long time as it is. You got lucky, because I underestimated you. I assure you that will never happen again.” Shun-tu states menacingly. “Fan-fucking-tastic.” Crichton shouts out at the top of his voice. Then without warning a deafening noise filled the bar and the whole front of the pub gave way, under blinding yellow-orange flames. ‘This really is one of those days!” Crichton thought. To be continued… And now… The cause of the panic, the confusion, the overall mayhem was an extremely explosive charge, the cause Crichton had not been able to witness yet. He had however figured that it must have been something extremely powerful, to create such destruction. The blast however had given Crichton and Aeryn the distraction they had prayed to the heavens for. The Chinese Triads were the closest to the wall when the blast hit and the resultant of this was the sheer force knocking them off their feet. Crichton took his chance and quickly led Aeryn to the bar doorway, cursing all the way. “Fuck! Fuuuucccckkk! FUCK! Why does this shit constantly happen to me?” Crichton cursed in his bid for freedom. “What the hell are you talkin’ about we’re home and dry.” Aeryn stated. “Hell no, you really expect whatever the hell blew that wall up, isn’t here for me! Come on use your head! He couldn’t have helped us because we have no friends!” Crichton replies in a slightly raised voice. They finally clambered past a crap load of rumble to the cold night air and find themselves faced with their ‘rescuer’; a ten foot metal device, which seemed to have a human controller operating it from the inside. Crichton stared at the face and realised that it was none other than Ms. Zemoff, the rich industrialist. It began to fall into place for Crichton now. Ms. Zemoff developed weapon systems for the United States army and therefore had all of the cutting edge war technology. This metal device she was currently operating must be the by-product of her work. “Aw…bugger me senseless and call me the Bugger King [That one’s for you Cassius Deamont] , that’s Ms. Zemoff, I told you we had no friends!” Crichton stated before pushing Aeryn away as he dodged a blast from the hefty metal device. “What the hell are you doing, you crazy metal bint! You can’t kill me, you need me to bargain with the army!” Crichton shouts at the metal beast, rolling away from yet another energy blast, which impacts upon the floor, spewing concrete into the air. A mad, metallic laugh can be heard coming from the metal monster. “Very perceptive Mr. Crichton, but what you fail to realise is that, these blasts I’m barraging you with now, are simply stun rays, to render you unconscious.” she explained, much to Crichton’s dismay. “The thing is with you is Ms. Zemoff, is that your far too dramatic, especially entrance wise.” Crichton commented. Crichton dodged a few concrete projectiles, before ducking another one of Ms. Zemoff’s energy blasts and diving behind a brick wall. “Oh hell I’m screwed! How the hell am I gonna get outta this one?” Crichton mutters to himself, searching the area for some kind of weapon to potentially use against Zemoff. “You can’t hide forever Crichton! I will catch you and I will hand you over to the army, so why not just give up now!” Ms. Zemoff taunts, an obvious attempt to break Crichton’s morale. “Not so fast lady!” the dark husky voice started “He’s coming with me!” the voice continues. Crichton briefly raises head above his brick wall barricade just enough to see Dan Lyons, the diner owner out for revenge against Crichton, standing there with two hefty looking guns pointed straight at Ms. Zemoff, Ms. Zemoff turns to face the new contender in this little game. “And who might you be?” Ms. Zemoff questions, refusing to be intimidated by the man. Lyon’s laughs to himself a little. “I’m the guy telling you to back off and leave Crichton to me!'” Lyons yelled obviously loosing his very short temper. Ms. Zemoff smirks a little, shrugging slightly. “And what exactly do you plan to do if I don’t, ‘back off’?” Ms. Zemoff questions, her thick Russian accent almost making the words incomprehensible. Lyons shakes with anger. “Don’t tempt me woman!” Lyons shouts his anger obviously reaching boiling point, simultaneously clicking the safety cap off both of his guns. Ms. Zemoff smirks slightly once more and lowers her head a little. Then with out warning, an obvious tactic to catch Lyons off guard, charges at Lyons stun Blaster’s blazing. However, Lyons is not an amateur and reacts quickly to Zemoff’s attack, diving out of her path, while making sure none of the stun blasts connect. Once landing abruptly upon a soft grassy area, he quickly gets his bearings in order, before discharging lethal shots of his very High-Tec weapons. Most of the shots fly by the extremely quick Zemoff, in her equally High-Tec robotic suit, but one of the shots catches her suit on the left shoulder, changing her trajectory and forcing her to crash through a heavily cemented wall. Lyons held position for a second, making sure there was no further movement from Zemoff. Happy that there is no movement, he quickly pushes himself up off the ground and makes his way over to Crichton’s last position, never taking his eyes off the sizeable hole in the building wall. Once confident that Zemoff wasn’t getting up he fixes his gaze upon the wall, in which Crichton was hiding behind and silently held position. He savoured the moment a little, before taking aim and unloading his guns upon the formidable presence of the wall, believing Crichton was still hiding there. The wall however, proved no opposition as the gun charges tear the wall apart and leaving nothing more than rubble and smoke. ‘If Crichton was behind the wall’, he silently mused ‘He isn’t breathing now!’ *** As soon as Crichton recognised Lyons familiar voice and familiar frame, he decided that he’d better get out of the area. In truth he didn’t want to fight Lyons a bit and the recent dark impulses compounded the problem too, he simply didn’t want to lose control again. All that Crichton needed was a window of opportunity, for he and Aeryn to escape undetected and therefore unscathed. When the battle between Ms. Zemoff and Lyons erupted, he knew that this was the chance that he was looking for and he took it. He grabbed Aeryn by the hand and escorted her down the back alleyways opposite the cover of the wall. And although Lyons quickly won the battle, Crichton had managed to get to safety in the allotted time. *** Lyons waded through the rubble and the smoke in search of his hapless victims. However, he was becoming more and more disappointed with every inch of the area he checked. He soon came to realise that Crichton was no longer there and he came to realise why nobody ever called him the sharpest knife in the draw. He also realised that escaping while both of his enemies were engaged in battle was the clever thing to do, so Lyons had figure why he didn’t figure this out in the first place. He already knew the answer before he asked the question. Both hatred and rage were clouding his judgement, something he knew he had to shake if he ever wanted to capture Crichton, something he also knew was not going to be easy. Lyons looked up from the rubble and scanned the area for any signs of Crichton, anything which would suggest the where abouts of his prey. At first the up turned trashcan in the opposite alleyway did not register, but the more he thought about it the more it clicked. He couldn’t decided whether it was more instincts or desperation, but he decided to search this alleyway nevertheless; it was all he had. *** Luckily on the way the bar, Crichton had decided to park his car in a ‘better’ part of town, somehow knowing that the bar was a rough place to be. He never imagined it was this rough though. Since the panic of escaping the two maniacs, he had managed to get his bearings and now was heading in the vague direction of his car, still using the alleyways. The alleyways reminded him of the time he lost his memory and found himself in the piss covered alleyway. He shuddered at the thought; he had never been so vulnerable in his life and he obviously didn’t like it one bit. What he needed to do now was keep his and Aeryn’s morale up and basically take their minds off yet another life or death situation. So he thought about his centre, the sport he loved and cherished; Wrestling. “So Aeryn, am I booked for any matches in the PCW this week?” He questions almost catching Aeryn off balance. “Wha…erm…yeah on both Tuesday Turmoil and Friday Fusion!” Aeryn answers quickly. Crichton’s face is covered with shock. “What both events? Who will I be facing?” He asks. An angry look etches itself upon Aeryn’s face. “Look Crichton, I know you regard your wrestling career as ‘top priority’, but you will have no wrestling career if we don’t get out of this one alive!” Aeryn states, her tone of voice complementing the anger look upon her face. Crichton’s jaw almost drops in shock. “I’m just trying to keep our minds of the situation to ease tension, just go along with it and everything will work out fine.” He replies in a soothing tone. “Okay, alright, erm…on Tuesday we’re in Firestar Center in Cincinatti and it will be you, Ben Pailthorpe and Andy Murray against Orpheus Grant, Kramer Wild Wing and Karl LeBlanc.” Aeryn states. A thoughtful look etches itself upon Crichton’s face. “Oh fantastic a chance to get a little revenge! I remember losing to Kramer Wild Wing a couple of weeks ago, when those two thugs Orpheus Grant and Karl LeBlanc came down and beat me while I was helpless. Well this time the numbers will be evened out and their school boy attacks will not work, when they witness the might of the forgotten ones.” Crichton states, with pictures of graphic violence enter his head contained his three opponents and a double barrelled shotgun. “Er…Crichton are you okay?” Aeryn asks in a concerned voice. His rage subsiding and his dark impulses kept in check he nods a ‘yes’. “And on Friday Fusion?” He questions. “Your fighting Jason Core for the number one contendership of the TV title.” She simply answers. Crichton’s face lights up with joy. “Oh hell yeah, another chance to face The Pharaoh again for his TV title. As you know the last match was a draw and if I beat Jason Core I have the opportunity to show who the best man really is!” Crichton states with delight. Yes well Crichton, you have to beat Jason Core before get that title shot though reme…” Aeryn trails off as concrete is blasted up twelve meters in the air just a foot in front of Crichton. Crichton looks up to see Lyons standing at the end of the entrance, gun aimed directly at Crichton’s head. Lyons then moves forward little. “It’s always a good thing to let your prey know who their predator is before you kill them!” Lyons states in his typically husky voice. Crichton sighs a little. “Do you have a super villains handbook you memorise or something?” Crichton questions sarcastically. Lyons smirks a little. “Insolent till the end eh? I’ve got to admire that!” Lyons states. “I admire the last time we were in this situation, when I shoved my foot up your ass!” Crichton replies, his dark impulses itching to take over once more. Lyons takes another step forward, to the point where he is almost face to face with him. “You know, I’m going to kill you first and make Aeryn watch you slowly die, much in the same manner you forced me to watch my wife and daughter die!” Lyons states lifting one of his High-Tec guns up to high level and pointing it directly at Crichton’s chest. Crichton had no doubt Lyons had the balls to pull the trigger and closed his eyes and waited for the inevitable to happen. Instead of being blown away however he simply hurt a grunting in noise. He opens his eyes to see Lyons falling to the ground and looks up to see the damaged armour suit of Ms. Zemoff hovering above his position. He decided that he wasn’t going to wait for her to fire first this time and to take action. So he swiftly dives to the floor, grabbing one of Lyons’ High-Tec guns, forward rolls and then fires a few shots into Zemoff’s general direction. Despite her damaged suit, Zemoff manages to dodge two of the shots, but the third and forth shots did not miss. The first shot that connected hit her in the right shoulder and the second connected shot hit her in the chest. He couldn’t help wondering whether she survived the crash or not, but he wasn’t going to wait around to find out!
