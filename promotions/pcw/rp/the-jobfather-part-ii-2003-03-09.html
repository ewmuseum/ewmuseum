The Jobfather - - Part II

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09 March 2003 As many already know the Front Office’s wealth has came from taxing the workforce and this insurance has subsequently made the stable the most powerful entity within Primetime Championship Wrestling. Besides the wealth of the roster at their disposal the stable also homes a wealth of talent, firstly headed up by the dirtiest player in the game, Xavier Kannon who quite possibly can be the only person gracing Wrestle Tattler who can say that they have done it all. World ChampionGrand Slam WinnerPCW OwnerRUSH Owner His list of attributes would make any potential employer drool over such a résumé. Backed up then by two of the industries veterans in Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson, anyone even thinking of toying with such a well-oiled unit would require more guts than brains. Worryingly more so, the Front Office doesn’t stop there. Amongst this flock also is the greatest tag team since the ‘Third Age’ in Pranks With Attitude, the Jester and Vampire collective, individually known as Gaz Green and Nerezza. The Front Office has thwarted all challenges under taken but as we witnessed earlier a new threat has surfaced and instead of rallying against the stable as a platoon, this new force has cleverly singled out the Jester. Maybe they believe the Emerald Menace to be the weakest link; usually a pillar of strength however his first encounter has left the first chink in the armour. The scene opens within a typical office. Artificial lighting rains from the ceiling, wooden desks litter the floor and writing utensils are in there thousands. Scribbling away in the background we can clearly make out the former Cruiserweight Champion of Tim Walden who sits opposite the Front Office Personal Director of Ms. Brandi Wilson who appears somewhat engaged. Conversing over the telephone the scene appears business as usual but snugly tucked away behind a screen sits the Corporate Jester who is typically tailored in his green business suit. “Why me…” muttered the Jester. Waving his head from side to side he looks a shell of his former self. Granted he appears without direction, no tag team championship wrapped over his shoulder, banished to Primetime Wrestling Academy and narrowly making it to March Madness via a legal loop hole, installed by his stable mates wife. However all this misery could be put down to one conversation with a stranger, the words of which tormenting the once jovial Jester, “Right to business – It’s quite simple; we want you to speak with your newfound friend Mr. Kannon and put forward our case. Our is that of loss of earnings under his tyranny and we want a slice of the pie, call this Operation Pay Day… Squire…” Gaz knows very well that the Scientologist wouldn’t listen to such a case; after all he made a living from making our peoples lives miserable. The most charitable moment Gaz could reflect back on was the money he waged to two tramps that fought in a barbaric moment of madness, no way would he simply dip into his own pocket to satisfy a bunch of unemployed punks. Even if they were mentally bullying one of his own. “Well I haven’t heard anything since. Maybe they were just messing with me!” murmured Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green again. His face perked ever so briefly, a smile came and went as he camouflaged the idle threat as merely someone giving him a piece of his own medicine, a prank. It made perfect sense; after all he had instigated so many on people that it was simply a matter of time before the role was reversed. He accepted that no news was good news and that motel room incident was a cleverly hatched prank to throw him from his game plan, it could be anybody from the roster. Two Ton – The liposuction incident would spurn anybody into revenge.Chet King – Possibly took the Jester’s words from Tuesday Turmoil a little to heart.Dante Jackson – Long standing rivalry.Skitzo – With so many personalities, one may very well be a conceited prankster.El Diablo – Head of the ‘I Hate Pranks With Attitude Foundation’Chris Storm – The whole impersonation incident. The possibilities are endless, over a eight-month tenure, Pranks With Attitude have trod on the toes of so many people that your best bet would be to write the names of those involved and pull out a winner via the lottery. But the perpetrator did suggest being a former employer; however there have been a few of those too in an eight-month period. Pen at hand the Jester scribbled those who have since vanished from the spot light onto a piece of paper, possibly not to continue the mystery further but to pass away the time as he sat at his enclosed desk in solitude. “Well there’s been a few!” murmurs the Lord Of Laughter as the nib makes contact with the surface of the paper. Suddenly his thoughts manifested onto the paper as the names mounted, names of significance and some that would challenge a true veteran. - Mark Michigan, Rich Rollins, Ali Khadafi, Statuz Quo, Kai, Alan Ackart, Amber Token, El Diablo, Jamie Celt, BladeD, Charlie Loc, Big Alan Steele, Misplaced, The Black Dragons, Eddie Mandrake, Duzza, Heavy Metal Harry - Fixated in thought the Jester doesn’t notice the introduction of the behemoth that is Nascosto Ireul Nerezza who peers down over his shoulder, eyes swaying to the lines of writing. Grunting to indicate his presence, Gaz Green turns to the second half of Pranks With Attitude and without so much as an exchange of pleasantries a conversation erupts. “I can see your stalker is still toying with you!” hissed the vampire. “Not really…” retorted Gaz half heartedly. “I was merely passing the time away. I was reflecting, here we are, eight months into our contracts and we have already lost our rookie label even though we are relatively new to the business ourselves!” concluded the Jester, making his point clear. “How very true. I only called by to see whether you wanted to hit the gym? March Madness looms my friend, indeed it’s a no lose situation so we must grasp this opportunity with both hands-… Talons, both talons…” said the Living Nightmare, curling the corner of his lips to that of a smile. “I’ll catch up with you! I firstly want to check-up on…” injected the Jester before Nerezza’s untimely interruption. “HER!!!” The cloaked nosferatu pivoted on his foot and turned making his exit. Once again left to his own amusements, Gaz Green reached over to the button clad telephone when suddenly a melody emerged from beneath his emerald business jacket. The polyphonic tune of Offspring’s Original Prankster erupted, it was apparent somebody was ringing his mobile. Reaching in he removed said article, the casing trembling in his hand as he placed the device to his ear. “Natalia just phoned to… What the, where is Natalia… These games have to stop…” Gaz silenced himself as he listened intently, obviously something was wrong. His facial expression oozed with worry as the other line took control of the conversation; the Jester finally ending the call after several minutes had since elapsed. Clutching his pen once more he scribbled onto a piece of paper, instructions? An address? Really, who knows, one thing was certain however; this person was beating the prankster at his own game, emotional warfare. - To be continued -
