X-Files Conventions are Gnarly

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 23 [The scene opens on a long stretch of lonely road. The hot sun blazes down onto the asphalt giving it that very musty-like smell. As the road stretches on you can see the road almost sizzling in the air from the rays of the steaming sun. The scene pans out and shows a beautiful, brand new 2002 red Mustang convertible. It's on the shoulder of the road with smoke coming from the hood. Two men lean against the side of the car, obviously ticked off about the situation that they are in. The man on your left is, one half of PCW's newest tag team, The Cowabunga Connection, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. He wears a blue tank top shirt that has yellow lettering, which reads 'Tubular Is As Tubular Does'. A pair of khaki cargo shorts and black sandals accompany the tank top shirt. Tommy seems comfortable in the heat as he just casually looks around with his bright, blue eyes. Being from LA your entire life and growing up on the bodacious beaches you tend to get used to the heat. On the other hand, the man on your right is not enjoying this heat. The other half of the Cowabunga Connection, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, roasts in his all black attire. Black Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. The sweat pours down his brow, making his long brown hair look freshly showered. Harry holds a can of Coca Cola in his hand, taking sips from it, hoping that he won't die of thirst out here on this road] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: We've been in more bogus situations, dude. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [Just then a roaring engine is heard barreling closer and closer to their broken down Mustang] Harry: I think it's aliens, man. TTT: No way, dude. Aliens only show up in Arizona. Harry: Oh yeah, man. [A big, shiny blue tow truck flies up the road and slams on the brakes, almost running over the feet of the Cowabunga Connection. The door opens and a bald, fat man emerges with sweat and oil stains on his face. He wears a tight, white T-shirt with even more oil stains and sweat, blue jeans that are two sizes too small, and black mountain boots. Tommy then looks over to Harry with a frightened look on his face] TTT: Maybe you were right about the aliens, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [The tow truck driver approaches the men, looking them over. The blonde haired pretty boy looks like he's going to be a problem. He's just another one of those rich LA punks who think that ol' Bob the tow truck driver will fix everything. Well, he'll fix it but its going to be coming as a very expensive price. Its time to stop taking crap from these pretty boy, rich punks!] Tow Truck Driver: All right, boys. Looks like you've got an overheated engine here. Shouldn't be driving so damn fast down this road in this heat. TTT: Of course, dude. [The tow truck driver rolls his eyes] Tow Truck Driver: Pop the hood. [Tommy seems confused by that order. He looks to Harry hoping that he'll have an answer, but Harry is still freaked out that this tow truck driver is an alien. Harry is thinking that maybe this guy is Chewbacca in disguise, or maybe Chewbacca when he shaves his body. He looks better with all the hair, man. The tow truck driver seems impatient] Tow Truck Driver: Well? [Tommy then shrugs, and grabs the Coca Cola out of Harry's hand. Tommy then pours the remaining liquid all over the hood of his new red Mustang. The tow truck driver seems to be absolutely taken back by the action, looking at Tommy as if he has lobsters growing out of his ears. Tommy then tosses the empty can inside the car, so not to litter, and smiles approvingly] TTT: The hood has been popped, compadre! [Harry seems upset] Harry: I'm going to die of thirst, man! [The tow truck driver then seems to be absolutely frustrated with these two morons. How can someone be so stupid to not be able to pop a freakin' hood? Looks like he's going to have to take care of this one alone. He'll just get rid of these two morons for a while so his work can't be disturbed] Tow Truck Driver: Guys, the LA convention center is only a mile up the road. Why don't you guys just wait there until I fix up your car here. [Harry and Tommy look to each other, shrug, and then begin to walk to the blue tow truck] TTT: I haven't been to the convention center in years, dude. The last time was that surfing convention where I played Yahtzee with Yasmine Bleeth. That was TOTALLY GNARLY! Harry: Yeah, man. [The tow truck driver now has a look of anger on his face. These punks think that they are going to get a ride there? They want him to fix their freakin' car and now they want a freakin' ride! They are FREAKIN' crazy! So freakin' freak them!] Tow Truck Driver: Sorry, fellas, but you have to walk. [Harry and Tommy then turn to the ugly tow truck driver with horror on their faces] TTT: Walk?! That'll kill us, dude! Harry: Yeah, man. [The tow truck driver shrugs] Tow Truck Driver: It's only a mile. [The scene then fades. A new scene opens up on the large, shining letters that read 'LA'. The scene zooms out showing the silver, metallic sign that reads 'LA Convention Center'. A large banner above the sign says 'X-Files Convention...TODAY!'. The scene pans downward to the main entrance, which is flooded with many skinny, pimple faced young men. This large group of young men are probably the least likely to get laid in life. They all wear X-Files memorabilia, talking frantically about their favorite episodes, and talking about how they all deserve to get Agent Scully in the sack] Fanboy # 1: Dude! Robert Patrick sucks in the new episodes! X-Files has TOTALLY gone down hill since that happened. They need to bring back Mulder to make me even enjoy the storylines. The only reason I watch it is for Scully. Fanboy # 2: Your so wrong! The show always will kick BUTT! Scully is hot...I agree. [Then two of the most attractive men at the convention approach the entrance. Both men are filled with sweat and are hunched over. The Cowabunga Connection seems to have made their mile long walk, but just barely. Harry leans against a nearby pillar looking up to the sky] Harry: Take me away Twisted Sister God...uh...man. [Tommy then puts his hand on Harry's shoulder, trying to console him] TTT: The walk wasn't that bad, dude! In fact, I believe it was most gnarly. It gets us righteously ready for our upcoming match on Sunday. [The two men then look at the large crowd in the front entrance and suddenly realize what they have gotten themselves into. Harry looks at the pimple faced fanboys, seeing them with their video tapes and comic books. Tommy looks really confused at this point] Harry: I think its more aliens, man. TTT: I don't think so, dude. I think this is a most bogus cult. [Harry looks at Tommy with even more fear] Harry: Do you think they'll make us drink Kool Aid, man? TTT: Probably. [Just then the doors of the main entrance open and the horde of nerds pours inside the building. They are ready to meet cast memebers who appeared just on a few shows or played extras. A few security guards shuffle the Cowabunga Connection inside, they are too freaked out to fight it off. Somehow they are the escalator to the main floor, thats where all the X-Files action is taking place. The large room is filled with autograph booths, trading cards, comic books, and the back area is dedicated to many people talking about their conspiracy theories. The PCW tag team watches as the dorks run to the booths working their magic, enjoying what makes them happy in life. Tommy looks to Harry, who seems mesmerized] TTT: Ok. We'll turn around slowly and then we'll bodaciously walk out. [The two men turn and a large muscle man wearing an X-Files T-shirt stands in their way] Large X-Files Fan: You two look like you don't belong here. TTT: Of course we don't, dude. I'm too Tubular to be here and Harry is just too...ummm...I'll have to think about that, but he doesn't belong here either, compadre Harry: Yeah, man. Large X-Files Fan: Well, since thats the case. I'm going to make you gentlemen...STAY! [The man pushes Harry and Tommy further into the anarchy that is the X-Files convention. Many on-lookers laugh, hoping to turn these men into believers. Harry and Tommy begins objecting to this] TTT: Dude, this is totally heinous! We don't belong here so we'll just leave and go back to our car. It's broken down but we'll go back to it. Harry: Yeah, man. Large X-Files Fan: Your going to be just fine here, gentlemen. Your going to listen to our fellow brothers preach about how our government is corrupt and how they are truly holding back information about alien activities. They don't want us to discover the truth and help make this world a much better place, because that would mean they lose their money and power. TTT: Wait a minute, compadre! I think I know what is going on here. This is totally a bogus thing to do. Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry then looks confused and looks over at Tommy] Harry: What is going on here, man? TTT: Its that Carey Nowski compadre! He's one half of Misplaced and he has this most bogus obsession with X-Files and conspiracies! He set this up so that we would be here to witness this total heinous stuff. Then he's going to make us believe the same way he does so that way we don't go to Sunday Sabotage and be most triumphant over them. Now how's that for a conspiracy, dude? [Harry looks at Tommy with a look of confusion, and really a look of just not caring] Harry: I have no hair on my nuts, man. [Everything in the convention center stops and comes to a dead silence. The large man with the X-Files shirt on smiles and shrugs] Large X-Files Fan: Me too. Various people from the crowd: ME TOO! [Tommy looks disgusted and the large man pushes them on. They go past all the card booths, the comic booths, with all the nerds look on with satisfaction on their faces. They are going to turn these men into believers whether they like it or not. What a feat with would be for two dumbasses to be believers. With their fame in PCW they'll be able to help spread the word even more. The two men are pushed in the front of the stage area in the back. This is where the most action is going on. Many of the nerds are preaching about conspiracies and how they are being held back from really knowing the truth] Conspirator: ...I was there, I saw it! They landed in the field behind my house and took me into their spaceship. They told me that the world I lived in was doomed if we did not change our ways. They told me to help bring peace and nobility back to the earth so that we can move on in harmony. Nobody will listen to me, nobody will believe me! [Tommy turns to Harry with a smile on his face] TTT: They probably gave him on of those bogus probes, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [The conspirator picks up on that comment and stops his speech. He then gestures toward the two, Misplaced, men] Conspirator: So, you must be the non-believers. I saw you outside and could tell right away, but you going to be believers. In fact, why don't you come on this stage now and tell us about a conspiracy, or an event that has made you think there is more going on than you think. [Harry and Tommy march to the stage, both smiling the entire way up. They take to the podium and begin] TTT: First thing is first, dudes. It's most TUBULAR to be here! Today you'll be gnarlified by what we have to say. Because what we experienced was a most bogus act, and it most be stopped. But first I'd like to talk about how we're going to take out Misplaced on Sunday, show the world that they are TOTALLY bogus and then take another step closer to becoming the tag team champions. Harry: YEAH, MAN! [The large crowd is silent, obviously not really knowing what the hell Tommy is talking about. Tommy then shrugs and figures that he'll go on] TTT: This afternoon we were on our way to a most bodacious lunch party. We heard that Jessica Alba would be there in all her most gnarly glory. [The group of horny young men cheer at the name of Jessica Alba] TTT: Yeah, her. Well, I was hoping to get in a few games of Yahtzee with this bodacious babe, but our car broke down. It's totally bogus because I just bought that car a few weeks ago. So you can only imagine the heinous feelings that I have. [Nobody in the crowd understands because their moms brought them to the convention] TTT: This tow truck driver came and he was the most bogus looking dude that you'll ever meet. He was bald and fat, and he didn't have any teeth. He had all these oil spots all over him. He kind of looked like...an alien! Harry: Yeah, man. [The group of X-Files fans begin to have a large commotion, believing the tubular one's ranting] TTT: He was a most bogus alien, but he's going to fix my car so I had to make peace with him. [Harry then takes control of the microphone] Harry: This guy is weird, man. He had hair in places that a man can't grow hair, man. In fact, this guy is believed to be Chewbacca....SHAVED, MAN!! [The crowd murmurs to each other with disbelief. Tommy nods his head with a smile, thinking that Chewbacca was a bodacious wookie. Just then the tow truck driver storms into the stage area, with keys in his hand. Everyone in the crowd gasps, knowing exactly who it is....ITS CHEWBACCA!] TTT: There he is, dudes!! IT'S THE BODACIOUS ALIEN CHEWBACCA!! [The tow truck driver shows pure anger on his face] Tow Truck Driver: I am NOT CHEWBACCA! You pretty boy, rich scumbag!! [The large crowd of fanboys attacks the wookie, or the supposed wookie, and the Cowabunga Connection has the keys to their mustang. They then quickly exit the convention center and hop into the car. Tommy smiles to Harry, who is giggling to himself] TTT: Now we can still make that luncheon with Jessica Alba, and let the Yahtzee begin! Harry: Far out, man. [The scene then fades to black]
