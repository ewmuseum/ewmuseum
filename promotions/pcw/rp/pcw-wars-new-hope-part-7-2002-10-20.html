PCW Wars: New Hope Part 7

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 - 10 - 02 Officer: 'To your stations!' Officer: 'Come with me!' said the uniformed gentlemen towards a group who have been assigned as the search party, their first order of the day is too strip the Millennium Eagle apart in the hope of extracting a Rebel force. A line of Stormbringers march towards the pirate ship in readiness to board it, while other troopers stand guard, weapons clenched tightly, anxious to unload on the poor souls who occupy her. As a handful of Imperial Stormbringers enter, Lord Pharaoh and the Grand Moff Tarkannon wait patiently outside. Officer: 'There's no one aboard, sir. According to the ships log, the crew abandoned her just after takeoff. If I maybe permitted to comment sir, I believe this is a decoy since the life pods have been jettisoned!' Tarkannon: 'But you weren't permitted to comment, were you? Lord Pharaoh what have you to say on the matter?' Darth Pharaoh: 'Hmm-... I believe the crew used this vessel as a decoy and jettisoned during the voyage, taking with them the droids!' Officer: 'But....' Darth Pharaoh: 'But-... What-... Err-... Zip it. com-... Hush! Now there's a good boy, run along and organise a scanning crew. I want every part of this ship checked!' Officer: 'Yes, sir!' Darth Pharaoh: 'I sense something... A presence I haven't felt since... Err-... Lunchtime, must be indigestion!' The combination of Darth Pharaoh and Moff Tarkannon exit the hangar in conversation. Another officer brings forth a scanning crew who enter the craft via its extended ramp way and vanish into the depths of the vessel, away from prying eyes. A few crash sounds startle a trooper who stands guard and he calls with concern up the ramp way. Trooper: 'Stop goofing off up there - Lord Pharaoh will turn your minds to putty and you know what that means....' Gaz's Voice: 'Wheel chair confined Steven Hawking wannabes?!?' Trooper: 'Who's that? Dave you sound, well, gay?!?' said the Imperial Stormbringer as he boarded the Millennium Eagle. The heavy footsteps suddenly ceased, replaced by the sound of a blaster being fired. The entrance became bathed in smoke and from the darkness emerges Gaz Solo and Alexander Skywalker in Stormbringer uniforms. Cherezza has been bounded together and masquerades as their prisoner as the party exit the hangar. Gaz Solo: 'Cheeky bast-... Do I really sound gay?' Cherezza subtly lets off a howl as his way of chuckling towards the comments muttered earlier by the Imperial Stormbringer, prompting the Jedi apprentice to take a cheap pop of his own. A. Skywalker: 'Between masturbating and gay jokes, you have quite the repertoire... Anyway between his howling and your trigger happy fingers, blasting everything in sight, it's a wonder that the whole station doesn’t know we're here!' Entering the gantry room, which has been left unattended, R2 rolled up to the terminal and worked his magic. Suddenly the layout of the battle station appeared and most notably the exact layout for the location of the reactors, co-incidentally those that power the tractor beam. R2 D2 blurted a number of random beeps, translated by the droid Rush-3PO. Rush-3PO: 'Damn there's seven of these reactor things but my little compadres here tells me that it is only necessary to take out one... If I may suggest something dudes, we send the old white man on this suicide mission, after all he be the most expendable, huh!' The team nods their acknowledgement just as the Jedi Knight Kenobi entered, unbeknownst that he is about to play the pawn in this daunting task against the Empire. C. Loc Kenobi: 'So what did I miss?' A. Skywalker: 'Err-... Arr-... Well-...' Gaz Solo: 'I'm going to say this as subtly as I possibly can - OLD MAN YOU GET THE TASK OF KNOCKING OUT ONE OF THOSE REACTORS BECAUSE THE REST OF US WISH TOO LIVE!!!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'What-... And I don't even get a say in the matter. What happened to the Jedi method of choosing-...' Gaz Solo: 'Which is?' C. Loc Kenobi: 'ROCK, PAPER AND SCISSORS!!!' Charlie Loc faced off against the reluctant Gaz Solo and their hands swayed up and down. On the third jerk of their respective wrists, both party's brandished their choice, in this case Charlie Loc opted for paper whilst the smuggler Gaz represented scissors with two parted fingers. As Charlie Loc starred with a dumbfounded expression, the R2 unit bellowed a series of whistles and beeps, drawing the attention of his traveling companions. A. Skywalker: 'What is it?' Rush-3PO: 'My main man R2 here tells me he has found that bodacious babe from the video footage! Her sweet ass is being detained on level five, a detention block.... And you must get your white asses in gear 'cause she's scheduled to be terminated!' Gaz Solo: 'Does someone want to fill me in?' A. Skywalker: 'No thanks - I'm straight as they come!!!' Gaz Solo: 'This is bull shit - Between listening to the comedian farmer here and having to rescue some silly convict, I'd personally take my payment and run' A. Skywalker: 'This isn't any she, we're talking royalty, we're talking Princess Anarchy... She's rich, powerful, beautiful, single...' Gaz stands erect and motionless drooling over the white surface of the floor. Almost bathing the walkway he snaps out of his trance and agrees to venture on, after all this mission could eradicate a number of problems in his life, pay off his debt and proof to the others that he is far from gay. Gaz Solo: 'Well, you've talked me into it, what's the plan fearless leader?' A. Skywalker: 'Leader? Charlie, you can be the leader, any ideas, plans you wish to share with the group?' Looking around they find the rugged Charlie Loc has vanished, the likelihood is that he has gone to takeout one of the said reactors, which will grant their release. Rush-3PO: 'It be like working with bleeding dummies... Here use these electronic cuffs on da Wookie and waltz up to level five with your prisoner, none of deez white boys will none the wiser!' A. Skywalker: 'Arr - And what will you two droids be doing while we're sticking our necks out?' R2 beeps his response, translated by the English speaking Rush-3PO. Rush-3PO: 'So true dude - Dis be a white mans problem, so me and the trashcan here are going to make like a tree and leave, in other words, we're gonna get the fuck outta here!' The droids vanish behind a cylinder door whilst Alex and Gaz head out into the open. Trying to look inconspicuous in their armoured suits, they wait for a vacuum elevator to arrive. Troops, bureaucrats and robots bustle about ignoring the trio completely. Only a few give the Wookie uneasy glances as the elevator finally arrives and all three members enter. A. Skywalker: 'I can't see jack-shit in this helmet?' The elevator stops and the doors part . The trio step out and find themselves approached by a grim looking officer. Gaz Solo: 'This isn't going to work?' A. Skywalker: 'Well its kinder after the facts now... What do you usually do in when faced with similar circumstances?' Gaz Solo: 'Well it pleases me to inform you that I have never been stranded in an Imperial battle station, but usually I say what the hell before unloading with my blaster, why?' A. Skywalker: 'WHAT THE HELL~!' Instantly Gaz began to unload on the officer who falls to the ground in a heap. His three supporting guards grip their weapons to retaliate but the nimble fingers of the Rebel Alliance make short work, as the death count of this series begins to rise once more. Cherezza not to feel left out, tore the victims limbs, painting the quarters crimson red and hitting this particular segment with a fifteen rating, hmm-... A. Skywalker: 'EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWW~!' Gaz Solo: 'The Princess is being kept in.... One of those cell's!' A. Skywalker: 'No shit brains, but which one?' Shrugging his shoulders Alexander scurried down the corridor, peering into the captives holding cells, trying to find the elusive Princess Anarchy. During his disappearance the intercom sprung into life with a harsh toned individual looking for answers, responded by the smart mouth of Gaz Solo this conversation had trouble all over it. Intercom Voice: 'What happened?' Gaz Solo: 'We just massacred your guards bitch, that’s what happened. So what you going to do about it, huh?' Intercom Voice: 'Listen up stupid, I'm not going to do anything but the squad of Stormbringers I've sent will most likely put enough holes in you guys to strain tea with your dead corpses!' As the officer on the end of the intercom blurted laughter, Gaz took forth his blaster and destroyed the control panel and the surrounding cameras that have already captured footage of this slaughter. Gaz Solo: 'Farmers boy can we like hurry up! We're about to have some company and me and the Wookie would love for you to participate this time....' Ignoring the pleads of his comrades Alexander blasts the locking mechanism to one of the holding bays and the shutter vertically opens unveiling the Princess asleep. The young boy stands dazzled by the Princess's beauty that turns to see the assailant, his face seeping through the smoke she lowers her jaw to speak. Anarchy: 'Pee-wee, tell your Lord Pharaoh, he can go too hell!' Alex removes his helmet and addresses the youthful senator. A. Skywalker: 'Oh, the uniform - Babe I'm here with Charlie Loc and the droid, don't fret 'cause the cavalry is here!' Anarchy: 'And you’re my night in shining armour? Yeah right, well I should thank you for making the effort but this isn't how I imagined my rescue!' A. Skywalker: 'Well 'SORRY' next time you can let down your bunched hair and I'll climb up the side of this battle station or how about I ride the Wookie during your rescue, slaying the dragon on my way in' Anarchy: 'Err-... I don't want to burst you bubble but dragons like sooooo don't exist, are you delusional, too many bumps to the head?' Waving his hand in manner of 'Forget It' the young Skywalker returns to the aid of his colleagues as they fend off the advancing Stormbringers, allowing the scene to change to that of a conversation involving Darth Pharaoh and Grand Moff Tarkannon. - To be continued -
