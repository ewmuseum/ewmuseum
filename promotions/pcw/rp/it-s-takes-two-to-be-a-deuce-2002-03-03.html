It's takes two to be a Deuce

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/2002 ‘Get me to the Kansas City Police Station, pronto.’ Boda barks to a cabbie as he climbs into a yellow and checker colored Kansas City Cab Co. cab. ‘Sure thing pal, get in.’ says the cabbie back. *The yellow and checkered cab drives away from the hotel that Boda spent last night in. A sleepless night it was, though, as he had not only the upcoming PPV on his mind, but also his buddy, his confidante, Mexi, was hauled off in a fit of rage last night. Of course, Boda has always wanted to do to Tyler Nelson, what Mexi did last night. Beat on him, beat him like the dog he is. But, did the Mexican go too far? Did his rage cross the line when he tried to break Tyler free from the police, and he in turn got arrested? A lot of unanswered questions remain. Is this the beginning of the end for Tyler Nelson and Mexi? What affect will this have on the upcoming PPV? Does Boda even care that Tyler is locked up, and now seemingly out of the PPV?* [one less obstacle to hurdle] ‘Say, buddy.... why you in a hurry to go to the police station?’ questions the cabbie. ‘Well.....’ Boda leans forward, reading the name off the placard in the front window, ‘Eddie, if you must know, I’m going to go visit a friend that got locked up last night. Going to see if I can get him out on bail, or something.’ ‘Ah.... good plan. So, what he do?’ asks the nosey Eddie. ‘Long story, and, really, not to be rude..... it’s none of your business.’ retorts Chris. *So, that seems to have shut up our cabbie Eddie. He focuses on the main goal, getting Boda to his destination. After a short five-minute drive across the city, Eddie pulls the cab up to the front of the Kansas City Police Station. Boda pays the $20.00 fare, and gives Eddie a rather large tip. Chris steps out of the cab, and walks up the concrete steps leading to the main lobby entrance to the station. The police station is not unlike the ones we see on TV shows like NYPD Blues and Third Watch. The large front doors give way to a larger lobby, with a reception area for visitors near the elevators. Boda walks past the benches where people are waiting, and even some bums are sleeping. He approaches the reception area, which is occupied by the desk sergeant.* ‘Hey there, I’m here to visit a friend of mine.’ Boda says to the desk sergeant, with out really looking at the man’s eyes. ‘So, where can I find Mexi?’ *The front desk sergeant looks up from his Police Weekly Magazine. He looks at Chris, and questions him* ‘Are you Mr. Mexi’s council?’ ‘Not really, but, I do owe him a favor,’ speaks Boda to the half-interested sergeant. ‘You see, last week, I to was arrested for some bogus bullshit, just to keep me from competing this weekend and winning the World Title. But, much to the dismay of the unlucky 19 others in the match, I was released after it was discovered Mexi’s ex-wife framed me and I was let go. So, in some way, I need to return the favor to him. Get my drift?’ ‘I sure do,’ responds the astute sergeant. ‘And the answer is no. Only lawyers are allowed to see Mexi or Tyler Nelson. Under strict enforcement from the Commish. Sorry, and, have a good day.’ *Boda walks away dejected. Now, not only can he not go in, and see Mexi, but, the sergeant didn’t seem to like the story of how Boda has to return the favor for Mexi’s help this past week. So, now, that his plans have been destroyed by a simple no from a man with a Glock 9MM on his person, Boda heads out and looks for a cab ride back to the hotel.* ‘Yo, cab.’ Boda waves his right arm in the air, hailing the cab that is pulling over to him now. Boda opens the rear door, and looks in. ‘Take me to the Marriott?’ ‘Sure thing. Hop in’ responds the cab driver. As Boda starts to climb in, something grabs his attention. ‘You know what.... I won’t be needing a ride now,’ Boda informs the cabbie ‘I’ve solved all my visitation problems’ *Boda steps away from the cab, and walks across the main street. Chris returns about 5 minutes later, carrying a brown bag in his hands. Chris walks into the lobby of the K.C Police Station, and marches right up to the front desk. The sergeant looks up at Chris, and smirks.* ‘The answer is still no,’ barks the sergeant, ‘you cannot visit that prisoner. Only lawyers are allowed.’ ‘I know....’ Boda smugly responds, ‘but, what if I gave you the contents of this bag?’ Boda puts the brown paper bag on the counter. ‘You can have the contents inside, just for letting me visit my friend.’ ‘Listen pal,’ the desk sergeant stands from his chair, his belly hitting the desk, ‘no amount of money can make me break the rules. No amount.’ *Chris opens the bag, and the smell of the contents are enough to stop the sarge in mid sentence* ‘I ain’t talking about Benjamins. I’m talking about 12 warm, glazed Krispy Kreme donuts. Fresh from the oven.’ Chris waves his hand over the bag, sending the smell floating towards the sergeant. ‘So, what you say, could I visit my friend?’ *The sergeant looks into the bag, and takes it from Boda, and sets it under the desk. He picks up the phone, and calls the holding area* ‘Hey, Dave.....There’s a visitor for the wrestler we arrested last night. I’m sending him to the visiting room.’ Sarge hangs up the phone, and looks at Chris. ‘Okay, you’re going to walk down the hall, and hang a left, and go to the end of that hall, and take the elevator to the basement, and once you leave the elevator, the visiting room is straight ahead. Thanks for the donuts.’ *Chris nods at the sergeant, and heads down the hallway to the next hallway, and to the elevators. Boda heads down the elevator, to the basement, and to the waiting room. There, Chris is led to a round table in the visiting room. He sits, and waits, and waits, and waits, and just before falling asleep, in walks a officer, and Tyler Nelson.* ‘What the hell,’ shouts Boda, ‘that’s not who I came to see.’ ‘Please, Chris’ demands Tyler, ‘lets talk. I need to see anyone, and you’ll do.’ ‘Sure, why not,’ Boda motions for Tyler to sit. He does, and the officer leaves, and brings in another inmate, to visit with his lawyer. The inmate is a rather large African American man, with a patch over his right eye. The African American smiles at Tyler, who in turn freaks out, and tries to hide his face from the man. ‘A new friend Tyler? So, what can I do for you? Besides laugh at you, ridicule you and mock you not only for Mexi kicking the shit out of you last night, and now, you’re as much a threat to me not winning the title as Chris Chase is. Meaning ZILCH, NOTTA.’ Boda says mockingly. ‘YOU STUPID JACKASS.....’ Tyler is visibly and vocally upset, ‘How dare you.... I’m Tyler Nelson... I own you... I will KILL that Tequila drinking buddy of yours....’ ‘What’s that Tyler? You like big black men to take you from behind?’ Boda responds to the berating from Tyler with a little egging on, ‘How’s your ass feeling today Ty?’ *The big African American inmate, reaches over, and pats Tyler on the ass. Tyler jumps from his seat, and lands ass first on the floor.* ‘Okay, okay.... stop it. This place is the worst. They will stop at nothing to make a man a woman.’ Tyler gets back in his seat. ‘Boda, listen, I don’t do this very often, hell, I don’t do this at all..... but you got to help get me out of here. You owe me. I got you to stardom in Media.’ ‘HA.’ laughs Boda. ‘You used me in Media, than cancelled me when you felt I was no longer needed. You cancelled my ass, and sent me to PCW2, you OPENLY GAY NIMPHO.’ *The big African American inmate smiles, and winks at Tyler.* ‘Dammit.....’ Tyler is getting nervous at this point. ‘Stop that. Listen, any thing you want, just get me out of here. How bout I guarantee you win at March Madness?’ ‘Hmm....’ Boda thinks about the offer from Tyler. ‘Are you bribing me? First you fire my friend Mexi, then you can’t take the ass beating from him, like a man, and now, you want me to help you out of jail? Why should I help you, and not Mexi? Give me one reason.’ ‘Because I’m Tyler Nelson.... I’m PCW. I own it all. And you have to answer to me, just like Mexi will.’ Tyler is getting irate again, apparently not taking the ribbing and name calling from Chris to lightly. ‘I tell you what, you help me out.... I’ll forgive Mexi for how he’s treated me lately.’ ‘Damn, that’s worth it all together. Here.’ Boda takes a card from his pocket, and slides it across the table, to Tyler. Tyler picks it up, and reads it, then throws it on the table again. ‘BODA, YOU THINK THIS SHIT IS FUNNY?’ Tyler blows a cork this time. ‘A FUCKING GET OUT OF JAIL FREE CARD FROM MONOPOLY. FORGET ABOUT IT, I’M GOING TO KILL YOUR FRIEND, AND THEN, I’LL MAKE YOUR LIFE A LIVING HELL.’ ‘Well, I offered to help,’ laughs Boda. ‘Don’t worry Tyler, after I win the World Title, and walk through the other 19, wait, sorry, make that 18 wrestlers, I’ll say a special hello to you during my interview. I just hope the officers in here will let you at least watch on Sunday.’ ‘You bastard. How dare you talk to me that way. I don’t let Mexi do it, and I won’t let you do it either.’ an angry Tyler states. ‘You think the beating Mexi got last night, before I got arrested was bad..... just wait till I get out, and I get my hands around him again. And this time, I’ll blame your stupidity, for mistreating me, for the damage I deliver upon him. And as for you.... I’m going to personally see that you never, NEVER win the World Title.’ ‘Like that would be anything new.’ Boda breaks kayfabe for a moment. ‘Fuck you, you greedy bastard. The Wild Deuces are here. Boda and Mexi are the real deal, and in 10-20, when you get out of this hell, WE WILL STILL BE the top dawgs in PCW. And, you want to know why.... because Mexi.....’ ‘Don’t say it Boda, don’t say it.’ Tyler grunts. ‘CAN.’ Boda finishes the saying that grates at Tyler. ‘Now, Officer, take this has-been Commissioner, the man that Mexi will BEAT down, next and every chance he gets, and get him out of here. And, please, bring me the guy I came to visit.’ *The officers grab Tyler, and they drag him off, kicking and screaming. Tyler yells at Boda, trying to use his powers, but he cannot because of his little legal problems. As Tyler vanishes out of Boda’s view, he sits there waiting, and waiting, and waiting again, till finally, the hacks bring in Boda’s little buddy, Mexi. He looks well, almost pleasantly happy. He walks in, and sits in the seat where Tyler just sat. Him and Chris shake hands, and sit back to chitchat. The subject heads straight to......* ‘How we getting you out of here?’ immediately asks Boda. ‘You need to be at the PPV. And watch me win the title man. I mean, after walking through, and beating everyone else I face in that match, it’s not going to be as worth it if your not there to celebrate with me.’ ‘Chris, you know me, you know me well by now.’ Mexi confidently tells Chris. ‘You go into March Madness, and do what you do best. Beat down Kid Wonder. Slap around the three New Breed bitches. Make Miya realize just who the hell you are. Show the newbie, Legend, Inc, that no one messes with Boda. Make Wild Deuces proud by sending International Incident back to the countries they came from. Make Asylum realize you are just that much crazier, by taking no shit from anyone tomorrow night. Taking no beatings, and not giving any inches in that match. This match is yours Chris. The title, is waiting for you. Go to St. Louis, and win it, and prove everyone wrong about The Big Bad Boda Daddy. Go out and make the world know who the best man in PCW is..... YOU. I’ve gotten everything under control here.’ ‘Okay. That’s all I needed to here. Now, now, I’m ready to rumble.’ Boda reaches his hand out to Mexi, and they shake hands again. ‘Sunday, is my time to shine, and I’ll be as bright as gold on a sunshiny day. Bet on it.’ *Chris stands and watches as the hacks come to take Mexi back to his holding cell. Just as Mexi reaches the door he turns back to Boda and flashes him one of his trademark smiles. “I’ll accompany you to the ring Sunday Chris, you can bet on it.” Mexi says with a wink of an eye and quickly disappearing through the door and out of site. Chris makes his way back to the lobby, and waits outside for a cab to hail as the scene fades to black.*
