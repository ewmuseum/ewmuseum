The Hubbardville Ellie~!s

11 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/09/02 “Um....... Like..... HELLO?!?!?!?!?!?!” With the loud, annoying and whiny voice of PCW’S Commissioner, Eleanor Hall, we fade into a picture of her slightly peeved form. The prep’s hands are on her hips as she stands on lush Green grass with White lines across it, decked out on a Black and Green mini-dress. Eleanor : “THIS is the reason you couldn’t hire a crack team of Geneto.... Geppet-... Genetat-... NERDS to make me a Dodo?” Pulling away from the prep it is shown she is standing on an American Football field, the grass seemingly unused for any sporting events so far. Beside her, as always is her husband, the PCW CEO Xavier Kannon wearing a baggy Black football jersey with Ellie~!s written across the front in Gold. Xavier : “Er.... I’ll do that for your birthday... But look, isn’t is just awe inspiring?” With these words the Gold Patron Meritorious gestures outwards with his arms and the camera complies, pulling far out to show a state of the art football stadium in downtown Hubbardville... The L Ron Hubbard Memorial Arena, where Fusion will be held, seen in the background. Despite it’s grand scale, Ellie~! seems un-impressed as she looks around, her lip curled up in the corner out of apathy. Eleanor : “Shouldn’t there, like, be a roof or something?” The Scientologist sighs and shakes his head as Ellie~! shields her eyes and looks up at the Wisconsin sky. Xavier : “No.. This is a football field. The best in the whole country... A miracle of architecture and engineering, and more importantly... Home of the....” He excitedly gestures to the football jersey he is wearing. Xavier : “THE HUBBARDVILLE ELLIE~!S!!!” Her doe-esque, vacuous pale Blue eyes look to her name in Gold italics on Xavier’s jersey and tries to raise an apathetic smile. Eleanor : “Er... Yay me?” Despite his valet’s apparent lack of enthusiasm for his new venture, the Scientologist paces about on the touchline, looking out into Hubbardville’s latest construction. Xavier : “Imagine every weekend, 1000’s cramming into Kirstey Alley Field to watch the Hubbardville Ellie~!s in action. The Cowboys, The Rams, the... Um... Other ones named after Animals!” His wife’s eyes narrow as she looks at him, her head tilted. Eleanor : “Um... Don’t you have to, like, join their club or something?” This seems to stop Kannon’s momentum a bit as he realises he may have overlooked something... Something which he now tries to cover up by holding a hand up to Ellie~! Xavier: “One second....” He turns his back to his wife and pulls his Cell Phone out and hitting the speed dial. Xavier : “Duke, hey........ Let’s say I have a Football Team and want to, well, play Football.... How would I do that?” He pauses and turns to Ellie~!, giving a thumbs up. Xavier: The NFL.... Right...... You got their number?...... Oh, Blackmail? Sure, do what you feel is best...... With his BABY-SITTER? Wow, that’s some real dirt........ He said yes? Great.... One second...” The Scientologist lowers his phone from his ear and looks back to his wife who’s eyes are wide open in a “So you had it all sorted, huh?“ look. Xavier : “See.... We’re in this NFL thing....Oooooh, the players!” He gets back on the phone and gestures from the sideline to the tunnel where 7 men come running out.. Ellie~! eyeing them up and licking her lips until her eyes hit Kannon’s and she snaps out of it. Xavier: “Duke, you still there?.... Right, I got some players ready too....” The 7 men line up, 1 looking a little out of place. Xavier : “I have.... Wait, turn around will you...” The line all turn around in unison to show the names on the backs of their Black and Gold Hubbardville Ellie~!’s jerseys. Xavier : “Right... I have FARVE... Brett Farve the Quarterback? That’s the thrower, right?........ Okay, then there’s BRUCE... Isaac? Yeah, probably. So what does he do? Run.. Okay, we need running...” As he runs off the team names to Duke he gets a peculiar look from his wife. Eleanor : “I think he, like, SO caught something from me...” Kannon remains animated on his Cell Phone talking to Duke. Xavier : “MOSS.... A catcher? Yeah, that’ll be useful as we have a thrower and all.... Then we have, er... STRAHAN... Yup, we got him... Defensive End... Like you used to need when you walked into the Chase/Williams’ locker room?..... Alright, next is GRAMATICA... A kicker? You have those?” The 7 men standing before Kannon have looks on their face that can easily be translated as “What have we got ourselves into....” Xavier: “Okay, we got URLACHER.. Linebacker... Okay. Last up is IVERSON.... Well I don’t know him either.... I dunno, tall guy.. Skinny, think his name was Alan..... BASKETBALL?.... Can’t he play both?” The line chatter amongst themselves as Ellie~! looks over them with a sneer, seeing them as a threat. Xavier: “What do you mean I need more? 40?!?!?!?!?! I spent MILLIONS AND MILLIONS just getting 7... And one doesn’t even PLAY football.... Hold on...” He drops his arm in dejection and sighs, looking at the row of puzzled Football players lined up before him as Ellie~! giggles at his dilemma. Xavier: “Right.. Which of you can play 2 positions? On offence AND defence?” They all shake their heads and mutter to each other, brushing Kannon off with their hand gestures as they walk back to the tunnel. Xavier : “Duke, I’ll call you back...” The Scientologist bellows after them, furious. Xavier : “WHERE ARE YOU GOING?!?!!? YOU’RE HUBBARDVILLE ELLIE~!S!!!!” His wife looks insulted as the men disappear down the tunnel, Ellie~! putting her hands on her hips. Eleanor : “I am, like, SO the real Hubbardville Ellie~! YAY ME!“ Kannon shakes his head and walks off the field with his head in his hands as the shot fades out on the impressive, and now seemingly useless, stadium. “Maybe I can give this place away in exchange for Dodo DNA....” “YAY! I think I’ll call my Dodo “Dead”!”
