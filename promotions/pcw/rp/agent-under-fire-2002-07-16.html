Agent Under Fire

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th July 2002 We find ourselves in a dark badly lit room surrounded by people wearing dodgy black leather jackets and sunglasses. Because they’re wearing sunglasses in the dark, you put the two and two together and realise that therefore they must in fact be gangsters. A lone gangster stands in the middle of the group and suddenly takes off the sunglasses, throwing them to the floor as the camera pans in. It’s Miyagawa. He suddenly pulls two guns out of holsters around his belts and then... Miyagawa (in a really, really bad Italian accent): Come on you bitches, let’s go kill some mofo’s. Suddenly from off camera a loud snap is heard and a groan from the mobsters coincides with the lightning being massively increased and... Bloke: CUT! What the hell was that? That’s not Italian! Miyagawa: I told you I can’t do accents. Can’t I be just American? Look at me! I’m half freaking Japanese, why the hell do I have an Italian accent? The camera cuts over to another camera, showing that we are in fact on a film set and the man screaming at Miyagawa is in fact none other than the lord of all things grungy and artistic... no wait, it’s actually the worst actor in the world, Quentin Tarantino. A man on a mobile phone and what appears to be an extremely expensive suit wanders onto the set and walks right over to Miyagawa. He turns of the phone and slams shut its cover before placing it inside his jacket pocket. Man: Don’t worry Mariah darling, you were great. Miyagawa (annoyed): My name is Alexander Miyagawa. People call me Miya or Miyagawa. People do NOT call me Mariah. Do I look like I have several successful albums with Sony and a head condition? Man: Albums, what a wonderful idea... I’m thinking Miyagawa sings Country. Miyagawa: Duke Williams already did that. Man: Maybe Miyagawa sings Charity Christmas Songs... that could be a good little earner for you. **shakes his hands from side to side** Anyway, I just got off the phone with Sobe and they said it would be a great idea for you to do a photo shoot. Miyagawa: But I don’t like Sobe anymore. Powerade is much more...mmmmmm.... delicious if you catch my drift. Man: I do hear you, but Powerade weren’t willing to pay my sort of moolah if you catch my drift, yah? So I’m thinking we do the Sobe photo shoot on Wednesday. Miyagawa shakes his head as in “NO!” with a definite certainty to it. Miyagawa: On Wednesday I will be spending time with my wife. Man: We could bring her along. Miyagawa’s eyes narrow at the Agent. Miyagawa: No. Leave her out of this. I’m already annoyed enough that she’s insisting she comes to Turmoil. I don’t want an incident similar to the last time she did. There always seems to be some ten foot tall jackass attacking my wife. It gets rather repetitive after a while when you’ve had Boda and Ivan tracking her down. Man: Somehow I don’t think “Miyagawa the Man who gets his ass kicked by big people” will sell your videos nearly as well as “Miyagawa the Living Legend”. Miyagawa shakes his head and walks away, off the set and past the camera while the Agent does his best to keep up while hopping over the camera cables. Miyagawa: I’m no living legend. Man: Are you dead then? Miyagawa: That was a pitiful attempt at a point; but a point nonetheless. Man: I’ve been getting some people to work on a new catchphrase for you. Miyagawa: What’s wrong with because... I’m... Excellent then? Man: It’s so old school to only have one catchphrase... people have three or four now, and that is the route to success. Miyagawa shakes his head once again in annoyance. Miyagawa: BUT I AM OLD SCHOOL!**Pauses for a second as he looks at the people looking at him for shouting** Just not in the Old School Wrestling type of way. I’m not quite ready for the wrestler’s retirement village quite yet. Man: What about... That’s the Bottom Line because I just said so? **Miyagawa shakes his head** If you smell what the Gawa is cooking? Miyagawa: That might work for Chef, but not for me. Man: Wot? Miyagawa: What? Man: No, that’s the catchphrase... WOT? Miyagawa: Gah... Don’t Imitate, Innovate. The man stops and steps in front of Miyagawa stopping the PCW wrestler and points at him with enthusiasm after Miyagawa just said that. Miyagawa: Oh hell no...
