Humiliation: What Corey Fears Most.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now 'Three Kanoplexes! Three fucking Kanoplexes!' ::As our scene fades in we see and irate Corey Williams pick up a dustbin and throw it against a wall, creating a loud bang. Our camera stays focused on the wall, and after our eyes adjust to the dimmed lights we finally make out what is on it, diplomas. Not just one, or two or even three but 10 diplomas. We hear the voices of two men, one of which is CCW, as our camera swings back around to the middle of the room.:: 'Hmm...thank god these rooms are soundproof.' CCW: Soundproof? Really? But why does a shrink needs sound proof walls? Shrink: Well to stop my secretaries from listening in. Besides, you're paying me 10 grand for a session, I've gotta spend it on something. But enough about me, we're here to discuss your problems. ::The camera finally shows the full scene, we see Corey Williams lying on a huge leather couch in psychiatry. Opposite the couch is a smaller, thinner couch, where a distinguished looking man sits in a business suit, his right hand stroking his chin in deep thought.:: CCW: Ok, but I've never really been much good at talking about myself. ::Lie of the century, even the psychiatrist can't help but smirk. Corey glares at him but the psychiatrist quickly stifles a laugh and moves on.:: Shrink: So you were talking about these 'Kanoplexes' that pained you, but you seem in pretty good condition today. CCW: It wasn't the Kanoplexes Doc, it was the laughing that followed it. I can still hear them laughing at me. ME, Corey Williams! People are meant to quiver at the sound of my name, not laugh! My face should bring about the perfect mixture of hatred and respect... but NOT a perfect mixture of shits and giggles! Shrink: Ah, I see. So this is not a physical pain, but rather a mental pain... Now we are getting somewhere. CCW: This wasn't just pain Doc.... this was FAR worse. Shrink: Sorry, I don't follow... Pain is the term generally used to describe all the negative feelings that your body produces, both mental and physical. CCW: Yes, but pain doesn't even begin to describe this, this was the ultimate... Shrink: How? CCW: This was.... humiliation, complete and utter humiliation! Shrink: Ah, so you have a fear of humiliation do you? CCW: Fear? Never! I fear nothing! I fear no one! I-- Shrink: Alright, alright, I get it. But surely you must have done something to this man to deserve this kind of treatment Corey. CCW: Mr. Williams. Shrink (correcting himself): Sorry, Mr. Williams. CCW: Well, that's the thing I didn't even touch the poor bastard. You see, Jon Kano's problem is that he's an ignorant piece of shit. I just simply mentioned the truth to him and he went beserk! It was INHUMANE I tell you! I mean, I'm not a violent man or anything, but I'm pretty sure that if Kano had've wanted to settle his problems with me man to man I could have taken him. I knew it, he knew it, and so he came at me FROM BEHIND! ::Just by looking into Corey's eyes we can see that he's told himself that story so many times that, despite the fact that he knows deep down in his heart that it's a lie, he has now convinced himself that it's the truth. But in reality it's quite the opposite, it was Corey who charged at Kano from behind after Kano had outwrestled him.:: Shrink: Hmm... sounds like this man is having quite an effect on you, is there any chance we could get him to come in here to join you for a session? CCW: We're feuding, not getting married!!! Shrink: Sorry, I didn't mean anything by it, I just thought that maybe it'd be good for you to talk through your problems rather than just resort to violence. CCW: You're not... ::Corey holds his right arm out straight and rotates his wrist quickly, moving his outstretched hand from side to side whilst meanwhile screwing up his face in disgust of the thought.:: CCW: ...are you? Shrink: No I'm straight with a wife and kids actually, but enough about me, we're here to discuss your problems. Look, I think the best thing for you to do is to just avoid this 'Kano' for a few days and allow things to cool down. Try and keep your mind on something else for a few days. ::Corey thinks for a moment before an idea slowly creeps into his mind. He speaks, slowly to begin with but as he gets a clearer view of CCW: Well, I do have matches against Rent a Hero and Snow coming up this week.... Shrink: Yeh! See, that's the spirit, get back into the game for the love of the game! CCW: I can show these people that CCW is still in business! Shrink: Yeh! CCW: I'll prove that I'm more than just a gimmick, I can wrestle to! Shrink: Yeh CCW: I'll prove to everyone that Jason Snow is nothing but a washed-up over the hill has-been, like Kano! Shrink: Ye-- What? CCW: And I still have to get Snow back for dropping me for the spandex-wearing loser, although I'm sure they're very happy together..... Shrink: No, I don't think that's very producti--... CCW: In fact, heard that Rent A Hero was pregnant with Snow's baby.... Shrink: Look, perhaps you and this 'Snow' character should.... CCW: ...But the poor loser's spandex suit cut the circulation to the baby off and it died. Shrink: Well now, that's hardly a reasonable comment to m-- CCW: ...Babies, that's just another example of how Snow's over the hill. Superstars don't have kids, just ask Tyler Nelson! Shrink: Well it's hardly something worth fighting ove-- CCW: I'll put my hands around both of their necks and squeeze until they just stop breathing! ::CCW begins to get up off of the couch.:: Shrink: Wait! ::For once CCW actually hears the psychiatrist.:: CCW: I feel ten times better Doc, you're a genius! Shrink: But wait.... ::It's too late, Corey quickly exits the room, running to go put his mind and body to good use, the destruction of the Snow, Rent a Hero and ultimately Jon Kano. The Shrink looks on, with nothing to do but complete his sentence to the empty room.:: Shrink: ....can't we just talk about this? ::The scene fades to plaid, the last thing we see is the worried and uncertain eyes of the Shrink; just what exactly has he done?:: !The End!
