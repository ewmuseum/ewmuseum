Broken Burden

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.3.02 I find myself in a verdant fraction of a pasture. Across a freezing landscape ducks etch their vivid V-formations in the evening light. Down at the edge of the marshes, reeds, their stems weighted down with ice, stir uneasily in the bitter wind of the sunset. The rut of farm carts, set like iron in the frozen ground, gleam like steel as the afterglow lingers in the sky. “My life has always been a living hell for as long as I can remember.” My deep voice breaks the melancholy of the silence; breath hanging like mist and a thick steam rises from the shoes. The light of the meadow is all but bright, so I am masked behind shadows. The dying sun stains the marshes redly and ice glistens in the potholes of the streets. My skin begins to feel cold and clammy as the downpour is devoured by the ground beneath. The sun is now completely obscured by the dark clouds and the moon has come out from beneath the sinister billows and glistens down on the torrent of rain. Why am I hated by this society? I do nothing wrong, yet you all hate me for what I am. Trapped in this mind…banished from a beautiful life. Never gracing the chance of a good life, for everything I undergo is mistaking. Doing the possible is all but possible. I am not what anybody wants me to be. Conceived at birth, I was a defect, and now I live the flaw every day. Why was I put here? I ask myself the same question with the same feeling every single time. To feel the agony…to deliver self ruin? I watch as the glimmering slab of metal escapes from my wrist, dropping to the ground beneath. Watching the crimson fluid submerge from the wound and broken veins, I await the expectations. Why won’t it just end? How much pain will I received before it’s over? I have nothing left…now take me. Take me from this existence and destroy my being. Why must I live like this with nothing to subsist for? My hopes have all vanished from the human eye; however my punishment is this life. Misery is what I live by and misery is what I shall die by. There is no stopping what I will go through, for the world has already introduced me to what it will become. The cherry fluid seeps from the lesion bit by bit, before coming to a halt. The scars resting on my wrist give the impression of swelling up from the new score and the warm liquid begins to trickle down my forearm. As exhilarating as it may be to harm oneself, isn’t really exciting as it is to others. They have taken away everything possible from me. You have done the same, because you are them. Call me a freak…it doesn’t seem to bother me any longer, yet you and your children take pride in doing so. You say it with a laugh and turn your head…but you do say it well with intent. I bring my stained forearm out of the obscured light, and watch as the color seems to blend in with my skin. As much as you want to assume that I am hurting myself…you are completely in the wrong. You don’t know me. Why should I take your advice? Aren’t you the same type of person like the next and last? ------------- The scene fades into a lone country road, made of shabby tar and gravel. The natural world’s lovely songs are heard. There is a brick house across the way of the road…it’s a loner, for there is no other house in sight for miles. A brown station wagon sits in the driveway and all is in pure harmony perceived that nothing can go wrong. Inside of the house is a very different chronicle…and that’s where we take you. From the front door in there is no furniture, no tables, or anything whatsoever. All that is arranged inside are shadowy curtains and fresh black highlighted walls, turned inside out to keep out the luminosity from the sun from the daylight hours. Look to the left and there is more curtains and those are the ones that you will go through. Come on…go through…don’t be frightened. Through the curtains you draw closer on what would be the dining room. It is empty just like the last room, and still dark, with the exception of for the semi-open door on the right. All is hushed in the dwelling…until the door throws open instantaneously, and a man of average build walks through it. The patch of brunette hair waves as he walks and his clothes are enclosed by a leather apron of some sort. He takes the apron off and pulls the murky pelt gloves from his hands. He is slightly over-weight, but he looks as if he is the happiest man on the planet. As he exits through the curtains he lays the accessories on the floor, and whistles a lively tune of melody. BOOM! The sound comes from inside of the curtains. The front door has slammed…and then a click. The door has been locked and the house is now ensured with safety of a break-in. Through the door…down the steps…it’s the cellar. The steps are tainted cement, with fractures in almost each stair, showing the age of the residence. Reaching the bottom, the basement holds all garnishes that any basement would hold. Tools line the dimly lit walls, hung by rusty nails. A furnace rests in the middle, surrounded by a small container of petroleum. The entire cellar is neatly decorated, with every speck of dust wiped up, almost as if it is inspected thoroughly each day. That doesn’t answer the question of why there is no furniture in the house, though. What is with this guy? Is he some sort of freak of nature that wishes to sleep in his basement? This is more recognized than the floors of his house. Everything is just so foggy with dark colors. Past the furnace rests a variety of abnormal gears for a usual human-being. Along a small domestic board is an assortment of metallic objects, all parallel to one another. From small shears to penknives, they glimmer in the weak glow of the cellar. BOOM! The door slams yet again. The unchanged chap that walked through the curtain a few minutes ago saunters down the flight of steps of the vault with his usual whistling routine. His apron and gloves are back on over his attire and he smiles an eerie smile as he walks behind the cellar beside the tinny items. His gruff voice breaks the peace while tightening the right glove. “Now, you didn’t think I forgot about you…did ya?” The man says with a beam on his face, allowing his colorless teeth to show. He pulls a black shroud from the table that was out of view displaying…a woman? An average woman with blonde hair and brown highlights, with her eyes widened with fear. She stares at the man in dismay as he reaches for one of his metallic objects. “Now, this won’t hurt a bit,” he says with a grin as he pulls the penknife up. He pulls a portable ceiling light to get a better look at the female. She sports a black halter top and the rest of her body is buried by the black veil. Her eyes twinkle in the light with fear and the black duck tape around her mouth shifts up and down with groans. “Shh…It’s ok. I’ve done this before, you should know that,” he says as he wipes the end of the penknife with his black gloves. He bends down toward the girl’s abdominal section and slowly embeds the knife into her midsection, as she makes out a funneled scream. He moves it slowly down to her belly button, exposing blood from every direction. “Why do I do these things to people like you? I’m just normal, baby…I just like to hurt people. It feels nice to cause people misery…for the rest of their lives.” ------------- Why do I show you these things? What’s this have to do with me? It’s what you do. It’s what your kind does. It’s the way you are. It’s the way you are to people like me. Does that woman deserve what she is about to receive? How’s about this one…do I deserve what I live? Killing without hesitation, often without thought and reason, has become a rule, and, at least to some of you, the act of killing has become a source of thrills and enjoyment. You are all murderers. You’ve taken everything from me. I have no mother because of you. I have no father because of you. You have sent them from this world. Is it pure destiny? Hell no...You just wanted to cause misery. It’ll teach me to ever fall in love again. Trust you again? Hell no. Become friends with you? Go to hell. I need nobody but myself and my friend, misery. I found the letter…the one that my mother wrote. Why did she commit suicide? Why did she take her own life? It was because of you. You shot my father. You made her do that. The words still run through my mind...the words that I found on that bloodstained piece of paper. “Please refrain from any type of sorrow. If you feel like crying save the tears for tomorrow. I made the decision to end my life for no particular reason. None at all, I just got tired of living. That's all. Life lost its flavor to me, everything started looking so bland. It felt so pointless living in a world smeared with bullshit. Everywhere I walked I'd be stepping in it. I feel that for myself death will be better than life, suffocation better than breath… I'm just tired of being ethical in a completely unethical world. I'm tired of being told to take the straight path when I'm the most crooked person I know. I no longer want to live in a world where I'm afraid to love who I choose to love. I'm no longer interested in a world where war of skin color is a bigger battle then WWI and WWII put together. People hate people for what they were, and not what they are, as I have found out. I refuse to live in a world where trees are illegal and cigarettes aren't--A herbal essence versus A pile of toxic shit. I refuse to live in a world where the presidents oral recreation is a bigger deal then the poverty laying less then 420 feet from the white house itself. A world where millions and millions of dollars are shredded on a daily basis-now tell me what kind of sense does that make? A world where hate has become the basis for living, a place where happiness no longer exist. I can no longer live to die. I'd rather just not live at all. With death I’m looking for a sounder way of living. Be glad for me because I am no longer suffering--Be glad for me because I have found complete peace by now. Live on and live strong. Let bravery stand across your chest since it missed mine. Learn to love people for what they are, not what they were known for. Wipe your tears, I'm dead...it's over. I have no fears.” I show no tears, for they are no longer needed. I care for no tears. I don’t want to live anymore. She didn’t want to live for me any longer. She didn’t want a life. She made me find misery. I’m dead in my eyes. In your eyes, I’m nothing….a freak….misery torn. I look at my crimson stained forearm and hope for serious outcomes, to no avail. The punishment is this life. I can’t die…I’m invincibly a freak of nature who has a world of misery forever……I don’t want to live anymore……. End
