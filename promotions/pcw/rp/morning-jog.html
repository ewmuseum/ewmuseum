Morning Jog.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered We rejoin our… whoa! No time for introductions! “Hurry… HURRY!!” screams the frantic female voice over the two-way wrist communicator strapped to the beefy forearm of our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, “The countdown is almost up!!” Big Al sprints as fast as he can through the hazy morning streets of San Jose. Arms pumping furiously but carefully, as he holds a cardboard box with a handle as steady as he can. Sweat pours off his face as he holds his wrist up to reply. “How much time do we have available?” “It’s set to go off any minute!!” “BY THE GODS!!” The early traffic is light as Steele hurriedly crosses the street. Suddenly, a whiling cloud of almost transparent figures engulfs him in a mist of fists and kicks. “The oriental assassins are upon me!” “Ninjas?! Where the Hell did they come from?!” “By Zeus’ beard, where do they EVER come from?” Steele blocks a kick with his forearm, flipping the box from his right hand to his left in a single, deft maneuver. Spinning backwards, he sweeps his leg out and snaps it around the back of the ninja, whipping the assassin’s legs out from underneath him and continuing forward, stepping on his face. Steele fights his way through, dodging perilous punches and intense kicks as he makes smooth transitions with the cardboard box to maintain its stability. “Only one minute left!! Where are you?!” Big Al huffs as he swings upwards with a wicked uppercut, connecting and sending a cloaked figure flying off into the arms of Morpheus. The box switches hands and he turns with a savage elbow, knocking back two ninja with the single blow. With his arm still in follow through, he yells into the communicator. “I am at the corner of Maplewood and Palm!” “You’re too far!! You’ll never make it in time!!” Big Al screeches to a halt and makes a hard right in avoidance of a new set of ninja, hell-bent on stopping him in his desperate mission. As he dives directly in front of a bus, narrowly missing contact, the ninja don’t prove to be as lucky, blasted by the city conveyance like pins in a bowling alley. “What of the Masochist?” puffs Big Al between gasps, “How has his mission gone?” “Predictably.” “Bah!” “They caught and brought him down at the Parson’s Place.” Cars slam on their brakes and lay down rubber in order to miss the street combatants. One halts directly in front of Steele, who quickly places the box on the hood of the car and vaults it like a pommel horse, flipping sideways and thumping two onrushing ninja with simultaneous kicks as he dismounts, spinning and retrieving his precious cargo without looking. BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! A horrified Steele looks down at his wrist communicator. The wretched beeping can only mean one thing… “Steele!!” the voice on the other end screams, “The alarm just went off!” Time is up. “I heard!” “It’s all over! Game over man! Game over!!” “I… I can make it… but you have GOT to buy me some more time!!” Big Al continues hustling down the streets, the denizens of a vast ninja underworld continuing their assault on the Greek demi-god. His toga has been shredded into tatters. His feet ache from his sandals slapping at the pavement. His chest burns with lactic acid buildup. But stoically he keeps on, hoping against the odds that he may somehow pull this one off… “It’s… it’s… moving, Big Al!! It’s heading right for us!!” Steele tosses the box into the air, it spinning in the morning sun. Roaring, he rushes three ninja bearing nunchaku, arms raised crossed across his face. The wooden implements crack like gunshots off his forearms, but he ignores the pain and explodes forward, his double-backfist combination sidekick sending the cloaked warriors spinning away. He catches the box and tucks it under his arm, like a running back in football. Stiff-arm held out, he effectively plows a path through a particularly nasty section of shrieking oriental assassins. “I need more time!! Give her a spoonful of instant! At this morning malaise, she shant know the difference!” “I dunno… I’m scared Al! I don’t want to go NEAR that thing! Not this early…” “DO IT!!! I NEED MORE TIME!!” “Okay…” A phalanx of ninja drop lightly to the ground in front of Big Al, their black outfits blending into each other, harassing Big Al’s perception of numbers. Steel glints in the sunlight as swords are drawn. “Hi-Keeba!!” Steele dives forward, but short of the ninja. In his tuck, he gently places the cardboard box on the ground and rolls forward in front of it, launching himself forward again as his sandals catch pavement and becomes a human missile, crashing into the wall of ninja and effectively rendering them unconscious. Steele quickly rises and turns… and watches as a ninja makes a mad dash for the package!! “Big Al… she took the instant crystals, but I don’t know how long it’ll last!!” Steele looks around quickly and grabs the feet of one of the smaller ninja. Then, with a mighty ‘Hi-Keeba’, he twirls around like an Olympic hammer thrower and slingshots the black form towards the ninja and the box. The flying human crunches into the ninja just as he grips the cardboard handle on the box. The force of the impact jolts through ninja and package, but his grip holds firm as it is the handle that gives, rending off the box as the ninja is tossed aside by presumably his compatriot. The box tilts dangerously on its side, top coming undone. Big Al’s eyes go wide as he rushes in a desperate attempt t stop it. “Noooooooo!” A single Styrofoam cup dislodges and hits the ground as the box rights itself. The plastic top comes loose and the black ichor spills onto the sidewalk. Steele quickly retrieves the box and tucks it away, cursing at the loss of precious fluid. “Big Al! The Masochist DID IT!” a cheering female voice cries over the communicator, “He made it with the beans!! He doesn’t look so good, though…” “Then quickly!” urges Steele into his communicator, “Brew a freshened pot before we are all doomed!!” Steele turns the corner and smiles grimly, seeing the front gates of Stately Steele Manor just up ahead. His smile quickly fades, however, as he sees a street teeming with ninjas. His eyes dart around quickly, looking for an escape route as getting through the gate would take too much time. “It’s not brewing fast enough, Al!” “It has to!!” “And she’s getting restless! Ands starting to shake! I don’t like the looks of it! You have to make it, Big Al! You are our only hope!!” Steele races down the sidewalk as the assassins up ahead prepare, eagerly unsheathing the many implements of death of the ninja. Steele grimaces, imagining their bloodthirsty smiles under their cowls… When suddenly, a plan forms in Big Al’s head! Noticing the traffic up ahead, he grabs a garbage can of a nearby neighbor and hurls it into oncoming traffic. A black sedan slams on the brakes, in an effort to avoid the trashy mess. A local delivery truck behind the sedan also hits the brakes, while turning slightly to the right to avoid a collision. Steele leaps onto the sedan and runs up it, leaping onto the cab of the truck. Silver flashes through the air as shuriken and darts imbed themselves into the car and truck, narrowly missing our Big and Bold hero. But one hits its mark, ripping through the box and severing a Styrofoam cup in half, scalding hot coffee pouring onto the thigh of Steele. He roars in surprise and pain but manages to leap off the truck, over the ninja, onto one of the pillars holding the swing gates in place. An assassin follows, leaping onto the other pillar. The two jump almost in unison, Steele aiming to hit the sidewalk to home running; the ninja aiming for Steele with a long katana. Big Al’s eyes widen in shock as he catches the oriental warrior in his peripheral vision just in time. The ninja arcs towards him, screaming, with the sword poised over his head in a strike… but Steele manages to twist sideways, grabbing a cup of coffee and flinging it at the ninja. The container explodes boiling-hot liquid over the face of the ninja, who reacts by dropping like a stone kite to the ground, rolling and screaming as he holds his face. Big Al grabs the last remaining cup and discards the box, sprinting through the open door. With only seconds to spare, he crashes into the kitchen, a fearful vision of trusted manager Sue Saksnussum, her face twisted into some Medusean visage, wielding a large butcher knife, the graces cowering in a corner. Sue throws her head back and shrieks feraly, lunging forward… Steele reacts instantly, throwing the coffee cup into the air. Sue’s head snaps to attention, and her autonomic reflexes work to perfection as she catches the cup, tears off the lid, holds the cup about a foot above her gaping maw, and pours the steaming liquid directly down her throat with no hint of pain or loss of fluid. Everyone waits fearfully as she wipes her mouth and sighs contentedly, smacking her lips, her features beginning to return to her normal, pretty self. “Ah! Oh man, how I love Dunkin’ Donuts coffee in the morning. You know, I’m just not myself until I have some coffee…” Nervous titters about the room as Steele sags against the wall in relief, wiping sweat from his brow. “We have duly noticed,” he says dryly. “Yeah,” says Grace Steffi, “Do we really have to go through this every morning? Can’t we just stock up on coffee now?” “We would, but she ends up drinking it all, no matter how much we store away,” states another Grace. “Besides, there’s nothing quite like fresh coffee,” says Sue, the color returning to her face, “Yup! Nothing to get my day moving like a few good cups of coffee. And NOTHING tops off a cup of coffee like a good, Carolina smoke!” Sue reaches into her robe and pulls out a cigarette packet, wrinkled and worn. She turns it upside down and taps at it. And again. And again. Turning it upside down, she closes an eye and peers inside, looking for a cancer stick… We cut to Big Alan Steele, hurriedly pumping his arms as he sprints as fast a he humanly can, a carton of Marlboros in his hand. “Hurry… HURRY!!” screams the frantic female voice over the two-way wrist communicator strapped to the beefy forearm of our Big and Bold hero, “She’s about to have a nic-fit!!!” Ninja drop to the sidewalk in front of him, taking up fighting poses. “Hi-Keeba!!”
