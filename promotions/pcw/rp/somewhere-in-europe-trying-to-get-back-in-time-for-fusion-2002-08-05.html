Somewhere in Europe, trying to get back in time for Fusion...

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/05/02 End:'I feel rumblings on the ground.' Richard:'Richard doesn't. You must be imagining things.' The near empty streets are stomped dirty with dust that still hangs in the air as though large groups of people have been running through quite often. The sun bakes the Misfits, along with The End and their new traveling companion Sandy Wong. She says it's a shame that PCW released her in favor of the larger-breasted Tiffany Jennings, but did she really ever do anything save for a brief period of fawning over Akiyama? Drek looks around at the exotic architecture and asks what everyone else has already asked. Drek:'Wheh-are w', anyway? This doesn't look like th' Unite'id Stets.' Slade checks the local map he has in his hands. Slade:'Ayun moskticho.' Sandy:'What'd he say?' Richard:'I think he said 'Pamplona'.' Sandy:'Pamplona?' Drek:'Lexi, yoo knoow were thah' is?' With a upset shrug and a rushed, disappointed sigh, Lexicon smugly answers his fellow Misfit. Lexicon:'I'm the Master of the English Language, not an atlas, jackass! And don't call me Lexi!' Richard:'You're just upset that we tricked you into jumping off of the cruise ship the other day.' Lexicon:'Yes, and I was very lucky that I landed on that adolescent Beluga whale.' Sandy:'Indeed; the waters of the Atlantic are too warm this time of year for Belugas to live in. They usually stay up north.' Lexicon:'Whether it was a Beluga whale or some fat woman in an inner-tube, makes it no difference. The important thing to consider is that I am alive and well. And I reiterate that I have no idea where Pamplona is.' Although it hurts to breathe, let alone speak, The End doesn't give it much thought as he jumps back into the conversation. End:'Pamplona is in Spain. I've heard of it before because the town is supposed to be famous for something, though exactly what that is escapes me at the moment.' The End stops in his tracks, while the others move ahead of him in their walk through the town. End:'I swear that I still feel rumbling on the ground.' Lamar, who hasn't said anything up to this point, feels a sharp pain in his left arm. Lamar:'Ow!' Lamar's arm falls to the ground, impaled with something of a pike. Lamar gets several others hurled his way. Being as slow and fat as he is, he can't really dodge them and they all hit their marks, though nothing is a fatal hit. Lamar:'What's happening?' Some people, adults though they look like teens, are spearing Lamar. The person who threw the first pike rushes up and takes the pike and the arm. Lamar:'Hey! I need that!' The people are all shouting, 'Toro!' and attacking Lamar with their firecracker-looking pikes. Drek (who brought his ax today) puts it to good use by standing in front of Lamar and growling menacingly. The stupid people continue to throw their weapons at Lamar. Drek, thoroughly annoyed, comes to his friend's aid. Cutting a swath of destruction-- End:'Drek! No ax.' Drek:'Dahmmit! Yoo ne'er leh' me 'ave any foon!' So, Drek hands his ax over to Lexicon. The axe is so heavy that Lexicon drops it right to the floor, nearly cutting off his own foot! Bashing a swath of destruction, Drek rushes into battle, 0punching and kicking and biting and thrashing every attacker in sight. Even the ones who were just watching and shouting, 'Toro!' like idiots. In the meantime, The End, who can't see the fighting anyway, doesn't bother to cheer on Drek like the rest of the Misfits. Instead, he occupies his mind with other matters. End:'That rumbling's getting closer. And I just know I heard about Pamplona somewhere.' Drek lifts one of Lamar's attackers up onto his shoulders in a Fireman's Carry and drops him to the floor with the DR-DVD (Drek Redaxe Death Death Valley Driver). The people, though being attacked in retaliation for their spearing of Lamar, pretty much ignore Drek and continue with their unexplained aggressions toward Lamar. Drek catches a pike aimed for Lamar's head, snaps it in half, and then charges the one who threw it. Drek hoists the man up and slams him back-first with a sidewalk slam. A round of high-jumping (they need to be) clotheslines follow as Drek finishes of the stragglers. And the final attacker gets a powerbomb from Drek. With the locals incapacitated, Drek wipes his hands of them. Richard and Lexicon go and congratulate Drek for his actions and battle prowess. Slade, on the other hand, is taking the pikes out of Lamar and bandaging up his wounds so he doesn't, you know, die or anything. Lexicon:'Where did you learn that?' Drek:'I am a warrior of the dwarven order of Mallox! We are trained to be deadly proficiency in all weapons and hand-to-hand combat.' Slade:'Iounerfj sljk nvsduif klejf eorn lsj.' Sandy:'Yeah, guys! Come here and help!' Richard:'Yes, it would be faster if we helped you pull out those things, put we're in no hurry so get back to work!' The End interjects. End:'Not in a hurry? I've got to defend the PCW Cruiserweight Title on Fusion against Brandi of all people, and damned if I lose it because none of you people can book a return trip correctly! I tell you, this is the last time that you guys come with me anywhere!' Sandy Wong calms The End down. Sandy:'Sky! You know you can open up those stitches again just by raising your voice. Try not to talk, I know it hurts when you do.' Everyone continues walking through the town, lost. Slade tries to make out the map, but he can't read Spanish and the map is more of written instructions than anything else. The End could read the map if he had eyes. Oh well. Drek whirls his ax around like a pencil between the fingers. He nearly comes close to hitting Slade, too busy trying to decipher the map to notice much else. Lamar is trying to stick his arm back in place. Lexicon just takes in the local atmosphere. The buildings are all of Spanish-Moor design. They reach high into the sky, and are built to provide shelter from the sun during the day, and provide shelter from the cool wind at night. Lights, lanterns hang on wires going between the buildings, but since it's still the middle of the day, nothing is lit. Everything is made of old clay brick, but the town has been renovated to become a tourist attraction, so there's a vintage look, but everything is safe and far from falling apart. A few children and some older persons pass by, running and cheering. They're all dressed in various clothing. Whatever they're celebrating is not a formal-dress event. Some wear white from head-to-toe, others are in navy blue shirts and green pants. They're all thoroughly enjoying themselves it seems; they run through the streets with excitement on their faces, hopping up and down with the universally understood 'Yay!' coming from their happy lips. The Misfits get into the spirit as well... well, Lexicon does anyway, as he chases after the women. Lexicon:'I know not what special event you celebrate, but I must admit that I am very pleased and excited to join you in this Festival of Bouncing Mammary Glands. Wow, these things are nearly the size of Brandi's!' The Pamplonans don't understand Lexicon, so he's free to say what he wants with impunity. Lexicon decides to push his luck, approaching an attractive young woman with tanned skin and brown hair in denim overalls and a white T-shirt, hoping that her only word of English is 'yes.' Lexicon:'Excuse me, ma'am, but may I grasp your buttocks? And could we just skip any local customs of foreplay and courtship and just get right down to the sex?' A smile from the woman brings a warm feeling to Lexicon… in his pants. He turns around to the other Misfits and gives them a thumbs up. Lexicon then gives himself a self-high five, sure that's going to score tonight with some vacation sex. Five minutes later, Lexicon wakes up. He notices that he is flat on his back, blood trickling from the side of his mouth and an errant tooth lying about twelve feet away inside of a flowering pot filled with oregano. Lexicon:'Wha?' Slade:'Pok perojkp mfskdn; kjneraw liaud.' Lexicon:'SHE WAS AMERICAN?!' Sandy:'Well, Puerto Rican-American, if it helps.' Lexicon:'No it doesn't! Now I have to go back to America, and there'll be a sexual harassment lawsuit waiting against me, and she'll sue me for every dime I-' Richard:'You?' Lexicon:'- Every dime my wife has!' The End gets down on all fours and puts his ear and hands to the ground. He's obvoiusly not involved with anything the Misfits have on their minds at the moment. End:'There is definitely some sort of tremor activity. It seems to be coming this way from the West.' Drek:'Yoo're oover-re'ahktin'.' The End:'Look-' Drek:'Yes, ah'kin. Yoo try i' soometime.' Sandy:'Stop fighting, you two.' Drek:'Meh'k me.' Sandy grabs Drek by his left ear and twists. Drek screams aloud in pain and falls to the ground in the fetal position, sucking his thumb and rolling back and forth. Sandy is rather shocked by his reaction. Sandy:'What happened?' End:'You must've grabbed his ear. Don't do that. It's like grabbing young Goku by the tail.' Sandy:'What is he, a Ferengi?' (Star Trek fans rejoice at the use of the comparison, while everyone else scratches their heads and curses at me for using that quote. Realizing that the last few sentences came off like a Rent-a-Hero RP, I go back and change the wording a little.) End:'You must've grabbed his ear. Don't do that. It's like kicking a man in the testicles.' Sandy:'Oh.' Richard kneels down and pats Drek on the shoulder. Lamar:'Hey!' Lamar turns around and gets a stone thrown square between his eyes from some schoolchildren. He stumbles back, and shakes the ground when he falls. The surrounding buildings vibrate violently, and some of the foundations crack. Loose objects topple to the ground. The Misfits lose their footing and fall to the ground. When the extremely localized earthquake passes, everyone gets up from the ground. Lexicon:'You know, young man, it's bad enough that we get charged for you as FIVE PEOPLE whenever we fly anywhere, but could you try not to fall down when we're with you?' Lamar:'It's not my fault, they threw rocks at me!' Lamar points over at the schoolchildren, all of which shout, 'Toro!' mockingly at the black-clad Lamar, his hair shaped into horns this morning with liberal use of coconut oil that also attracts many flies to Lamar's head. Drek:'Thah'ts ih'! IH'S CLOOBERIN' TIME!' Drek leaps into the fray for the second time today, punching and kicking and stomping, powerslamming and suplexing. Bodies fly left and right, children scream in horror as the 'bully' attacks them relentlessly... Or... End:'Drek! They're kids…' Drek:'Dahmmit!' Drek puts his hands behind his back and lets the children throw rocks at him at The End's implied request not to injure them. The rocks don't hurt Drek; his dwarven skin is tough enough to withstand a rockslide, and 300-degree temperatures. Still, it's annoying, like when a person is being pestered by a swarm of gnats. Drek:'How ahm ah supoosed t'be a dwoorf bahd-ahss when a'kint bea' oop kids?' The children all cease their stone-throwing and run. Lamar steps forward once they're all gone and flexes his incredibly flabby arms. No muscle appears in the mounds of fat hanging off of his arms. Lamar:'I sure showed them! We're safe now.' Richard smacks Lamar across the head, knocking off his nose. Meanwhile, a large group of people runs by. Richard:'Idiot.' Slade:'Asdoijefniuk fiusdfphwe.' Richard:'RICHARD DIDN'T MEAN TO SEND US TO EUROPE, IT WAS THE WRONG DAMN TICKET!' The rumblings get closer. Everyone can feel it now. Sandy:'Lamar, would you sit still, please?' Everyone looks over at Lamar, who's just leaning up against a phone poll. Lamar looks at them and makes a pouting face with his hands on his hips. Lamar:'What'd I do now?' Drek:'No, cahn't be 'im.' The rumblings get closer… closer… Roaring is heard from around the corner, and a thick cloud of dust approaches. Stragglers from the group past are finally turning the corner and run past The End, Sandy Wong, and the Misfits. The End snaps his fingers. End:'Now I remember what Pamplona is famous for: The Running of the Bulls!' Richard:'Bulls?' Sandy:'Bulls?' Lexicon:'Bulls?' Drek:'Bools?' Slade:'Nmcwontidnu?' Everyone starts running as the large group of bulls rounds the corner. The bulls are fast, as fast at the Misfits. The End and Sandy Wong manage to be the fastest runners, with Slade and Drek right behind them. Richard and Lexicon lag behind. Slade:'Uwiepkoasd adsouifn iure okoa?' Drek:'Lamar oolred'dy goot trahmpled. 'Ave t'coome bahck foor 'im la'er.' In the back, Richard gets poked in the back by a one-horned bull (they always use lame, old, or deformed bulls for these things, not that the bulls don't manage to catch victims). Lexicon accidentally gets hit with some of the spears that people from the windows at the bulls. Lexicon:'Ow! CANNOT YOU PEOPLE MAKE BETTER AIM?! I HAVE MORE THAN ENOUGH PROBLEMS TO DEAL WITH AS IT IS WITHOUT WORRYING ABOUT BEING SKEWERED BY MY FELLOW HUMAN BEINGS!' Lexicon looks over at Richard, panting so much he can't speak in a straight sentence, but speaks with determination. Lexicon:'AS GOD *pants* AS MY WITNESS, *pants* *pants* I SHALL MAKE IT MY PERSONAL MISSION IN LIFE TO SEE TO IT *pants* THAT YOU NEVER *pants* EVER *pants* BOOK ANOTHER VACATION FOR US FOR AS LONG AS YOU LIVE!!!' Richard gets catches two horns each in either ass-cheek. Like in the cartoons, it prompts him up into the air, and back on the ground, running a little faster, but not by much. Richard:'F*CK!'
