Come here! You got something I want! An epic car chase!

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-02 I got more bang for a buckStep to me, you'll get f^cked! Just how much more can Jeremy Howard handle/take? A man can only handle so much, before he succumbs to the pressure of it all and folds in defeat. But, this man is the Krazed Lunatic. If there was ever a man that could handle the fight for a precious two year old son, a quest for the Continental title and the battle of a lifetime for the freedom of his baby brother, Its Jeremy Howard. But, how much can he handle/take? Almost four years ago, Jeremy had his brother unceremoniously ripped away from him by the former Governor of Indiana, Stan Hinkle, because Jeremy beat his son in an amateur championship match. Now, after fighting the uphill battle all those years, the Krayzie One has his chance to secure the freedom of his brother on Sunday at March Madness against the man that kidnapped eighteen year old, Justin Howard, as he was leaving the Plainfield Correctional Facility that held him. Three days after the Krazed Lunatic lost his World title belt to his former best friend, he made a stunning proclamation. Jeremy Howard is going to shoot for the championship title sweep in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Three days from now, he has his chance to move one step closer to that impressive goal when he takes on Chet King for the Continental title in the main event at Friday Fusion. Add to those two battles, the continued fight to get his son back from the foster parent that accepted Jeremy's son from the new Governor of Indiana, Richard O'Bannon, and one might think that Jeremy Howard is about to fail. But, this is Jeremy Howard. When everyone else is thinking that Jeremy Howard is down and out, this man is plotting his strike. And, when he strikes, the Krazed Lunatic strikes hard. The only question remains, just how hard is he going to strike when its time for someone to meet a psychotic ending? Jessica: Don't let him get away! To those words from the lovely Jessica, the scene fades into the Krayzie One slamming on the gas of his black BMW X5. In front of the Krazed Lunatic, is a black Trans Am. The black Trans Am bursts out of its parking space and out into the busy road. Its not long before Jeremy pulls up alongside the Trans Am, slamming into the side of it. Innocent cars suffer from the chase, careening into cars alongside the road, with one even going through the front of a store. Jeremy: Oh shit! Jessica: Watch out! Jeremy clenches his steering wheel tighter, regaining control of his car. Jeremy: This should make a great bed time story to tell Troy. Jessica pays no attention, instead she grabs anything she possibly can to stay safe. Jeremy chases the black Trans Am down an alleyway, knocking trashcans and the like down as he blasts by them. Finally, the Trans Am breaks out of the alleyways and into a public park. Jeremy: I need some driving music. Jeremy can't see her, but Jessica, wearing a blue watery looking bodysuit that clings right to her body, turns to him with a quizzical look in her face. Jessica: You want music right now? We're going almost a hundred miles per hour in a public park and you want music to drive too? Jeremy: Yeah, some Disturbed. Jessica: You're unbelievable sometimes. Jessica, holding onto a handle above the passenger side door with her right hand, reaches over to the CD player in the BMW with her left hand and hits the play button on Jeremy's Disturbed CD. Moments later, 'Voices' by Disturbed, the official theme song of Asylum, begins to blare out over Jeremy's speaker system. Meanwhile, the chase continues to heat up in the public park, with Jeremy only a few feet behind the leading Trans Am. Jessica: To think, Chet King is probably at home right now relaxing before the big Continental title fight. Not Jeremy Howard. He's doing a hundred miles an hour through a public park! Jeremy clenches his steering wheel as he gets right up beside the black Trans Am. Jeremy uses his BMW as a battering ram, choosing to slam into the side of it. The impact of the hit, causing the Trans Am and the BMW to split part, if only for a second. That second part, both vehicles slam into a number of cars in the parking lot of the park. Jeremy: Are you b!tching? Jessica: Can you blame me? I'm scared off my ass. Jeremy: If this Mr. Curry knows where Keith Lipowitz is, then I'm going to force him to tell me! Jeremy slams into the front driver's side of a Ford Escort and the front passenger's side of a Pontiac Grand Am, shoving them out of the way. Jessica: That's obvious. Jeremy: Just sit tight. I'll get this f^cker! The Trans Am and the BMW meet back up, slamming into the side of each other. The chase suddenly makes it out of the park and onto ten lanes of highway on the outskirts of Terre Haute, Indiana. Neither the man in the Trans Am or Jeremy in the BMW X5 are willing to give an inch, which causes each of them to take out a few lightpoles along the way. One of the lightpoles bends awkward and smashes rear passenger's side window out of the BMW. Jeremy: Shit! Put this on! Jeremy reaches into the backseat, while driving, grabbing a blue flannel jacket. Jessica takes it and places it around her cold arms. Jessica: I'm getting scared, honey. Jeremy: You have nothing worry about. I'd be more scared for what I'm going to do to Drake Maxwell than anything stopping us from getting back our son. Suddenly, the two vehicles come to a dark tunnel. The Trans Am and the BMW slam into each other numerous times, which sends them also bouncing off the side of the tunnel. A light can be seen at the end of the tunnel. It's fifteen feet away. Ten. Five. Two and a half feet away. Finally, the Trans Am and the BMW reach the light at the end of the tunnel. The Trans Am, obviously knowing what he's doing, takes an immediate left, leaving the highway. Jeremy: Motherf^ck! Jessica doesn't say a word, she just watches the Trans Am to see where it goes. Sitting in front of Jeremy, blocking his way to getting any closer to the Trans Am, is an accident site. Jeremy stops the BMW, sighing. Jessica: He went into that church over there! Jeremy: How the h#ll am I going to get to it now? I can't go in reverse. Cars zoom by, some beeping because Jeremy's stopped his car in the middle of the road. Jeremy: F^ck this. Hang on! Jessica complies, because Jeremy doesn't give her anytime to challenge him. Jeremy punches the gas, building up his speed. The BMW gets up past a hundred as Jeremy closes in on the accident site. A hundred and twenty. All of the sudden, in one single motion, Jeremy whips his steering wheel toward the left, ramping off the side of the highway. The black, beat up, BMW smacks the top of a Greyhound bus, then crashes onto the pavement. Jeremy: You alright? Jessica knods her head, 'yes'. Jessica: I've only got a few bumps and bruises...... I think. Jeremy hits the gas on the BMW. Surprisingly, the sport utility vehicle goes, albeit a bit sluggish. Jeremy: Come on, just get me to my son and I'll never ask a thing of you again. Jessica: It's that church! Jeremy follows the length of Jessica's arm to see where she's pointing. Jeremy creeps the BMW into the driveway of a run down, old church. An eerie feeling comes over the body of Jessica as she looks up, into the sky. The light of the sun is starting to fade out, with darkness upon them. Jeremy, ever so confident, heads toward an opening a little bit away from the church to hide the BMW as the scene fades... to black!
