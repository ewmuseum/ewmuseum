Will Akiyama's fans like what Chet has to say?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [The scene opens up in the grey room that Chet has been using over the last couple weeks to voice his opinions over certain members of PCW. He’s sitting on the black chair in the corner of the room, and begins to talk.] Chet King: Akiyama thinks I’m sticking my nose in business that’s got nothing to do with me? Talk about one of your all time hypocritical statements. Aki, you’ve got no clue what is and what isn’t my business, so keep your little opinion to yourself. My business is whatever I make my business, and right now Aki I’m making you that business. I guess it was bound to happen that you’d have a slip of the tongue and let your opinion out, considering that every time I turn my TV on I see you rambling for hours. I want to give you a challenge Aki, next time I see you on TV can you keep everything short and to the point? Don’t crap on for hours, and don’t let Sonata crap on for hours either. Just see if the both of you can keep everything short and sweet next time, and if you can then I’ll give you a prize. The prize will be that you can touch the soon to be mine World Championship, just for a second, which will be a great prize for you as you’ve never come close to touching it, and you never will again. I’ll even let you have a picture with it, so you can pretend, many years down the road, to your grandchildren that you once were the PCW World Champion. I’m sure though your grandchildren will realize just how far fetched that idea would be, but you could try anyway. Aki, if I really was nosy I’d be wanting to find out how you got thrown in to the main event on Turmoil. It couldn’t be because you’re a good wrestler, because, well, you’re not. Aki, I know you think you’re a higher being, but most mortals seem to be able to defeat you, which really doesn’t say much for your claim of being a higher power. Take March Madness for example, us mere mortals knocked you over the top rope, but if you were really a higher power I would of thought you would of survived and gone on to win the world championship. But no, you couldn’t do it just like you won’t be able to do it at Turmoil. Also, if you were a high power then you wouldn’t ramble. Like I said before everyone is sick and tired of your rambling. If you were a higher power you’d know that no one wants to listen to your crap. No one knows what you’re going on about. You just ramble, ramble, ramble. No one cares what you have to say Aki, and if you were a high power then you would know this! [The door to the grey room in which Chet is filming his promo opens up. In to the room walks Cindy Bi.] Chet King: What are you doing here? I’ve still got much more to talk about. Cindy Bi: Look Chet, you know yesterday how I was telling you that there are some people that fans enjoy seeing you insult, and there are others that they don’t? Chet King: Yes, of course I remember. Cindy Bi: Well Akiyama is one of the people that some fans don’t like seeing you attack. Sure there are lots who are enjoying it, but there are also lots who aren’t. Chet King: Why wouldn’t people like this? Surely people want me to let their opinions, of being sick and tired with Aki and his rambling, be known. Cindy Bi: Believe it or not, Aki does have a fan base, and they aren’t going to be happy. [Chet laughs] Chet King: Hahaha! That’s a good one Cindy! Aki having a fan base? Hahaha! Cindy Bi: I know you might find it hard to believe, but it’s true. Chet King: Well I can tell you that I don’t want people who are fans of Aki to be fans of me as well, because people who are absolute morons aren’t the type of people I want supporting me. Cindy Bi: But Che… Chet King: But nothing. Like I said yesterday, if I’ve got people on my shit list, such as Akiyama, then I’m going to tell things the way they are. And I’ve told the truth, Akiyama is a rambling fool who thinks he’s a higher power, but in actuality he’s a weak mortal who despite his size would have no chance if he fought me one on one. Cindy Bi: Look Chet, if Akiyama’s that bad then he’s not even worth your time, spend it on assholes who deserve it like Xavier Kannon or TTT. Chet King: You know what Cindy, you’re right. Kannon and TTT, both deserve to get their little bitch asses humiliated. Cindy Bi: Okay, lets go get something to eat then you can come back here in a couple hours. Chet King: Alright, but just let me remind Aki of my challenge to him. If you can stop rambling Aki, just one time, then I’ll let him touch my soon to be won world championship as that will be the closest he’ll ever get to it. [We leave the scene as Chet and Cindy leave the grey room.]
