Title: Last Stand In Open Country #1 Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 01/3103 -No fancy details, long drawn out stories, or cute tails. Just a man and his anger and plenty of trash talk. Going out the way he came in and nothing more. The man is Duke Williams and he sits in front of a camera and trash talks. He is dressed in all black as usual.- Williams: One week back and you’re in the main event? You think your something special Jack? You think so? Huh?? You want to talk about hard life and hard times. Cus hoss I got your hard life right between my legs. Duke grabs his crotch. Williams: Whoops. Did I get you angry you over rated fudge packer. Did I hurt you feelings? You going to cut a fifty-minute promo explaining a piss you took when you were two that changed your life? Come on give me all you got Jacky. A table sitting next to Duke has a bottle of whiskey on it. He pours himself a nice, big, glass of amber gold. Williams: Come on Jacky I know there’s nothing in the world you’d like to do then come back and prove to PCW just how good you are and how over rated I am. I’m sure you can get a damn T-shirt made up explaining just how violent you can be. Duke laughs into the camera. Williams: But you know something kid, violence is nothing with out drive. Random acts of violence are meaningless. But violence driven by passion makes all the difference in the world. You got drive Jacky? What makes you tick. Me it’s as clear as a dribble of jizm on your ladies face. It’s Malice. Duke wipes the smile off his face. Williams: My streak of Malice has been tame for a long time now. But I’m retiring in a month so why hold back? What do I have to lose? Maybe a few teeth but I’m sure they’ll be falling out in a decade or two anyway. So Jacky what do you have on the table? I return match. Big dick baby. Duke lights up one of Punch cigars. Williams: You like to beat on guys when they’re not looking. Boy are you a big man taking down Sky. Somebody tame this wild bastard he’s out of control hoss!! The business has never seen anything like him before. Come on you pathetic honky bald fucker. Your nothing but a nothing jive Jack. Duke gets angry. Williams: Bring you best game. Bring you family’s best game. Bring the presidents best game. Because outside of God himself nobody is going to stop me in the month of Malice. Call in the guard, the reserves, the terrorists, the assassins, my mother cus hoss I’m dangerous baby. You ever been Whisky Bent and Hell Bound Jacky?? Yea well Tuesday you will be. Where going to do it Malice Style hoss! =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
