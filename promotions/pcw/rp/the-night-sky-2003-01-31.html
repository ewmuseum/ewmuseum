The Night Sky

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-31-03 (The majestic evening sky has fallen upon the land and the countless numbers of stars beam in the sky like beckoning lights, drawing you in to see what is beyond. A cool evening mist hangs in the air, complemented by a brisk breeze. Trees sway to and fro gently, emitting a nice, soft whistling noise. All is very peaceful tonight, which makes it a perfect night to do some sight seeing.) (In a empty, rolling field, Dorn Tracer stands at the apex of a small hill, staring up at the endless blackness hanging above his head. An overwhelming feeling washes over him as he tries to get a feel for what his life is all about. You can learn a lot from watching sky. Entire chapters of histories can be explained from small, subtle patterns in the stars. Yes, the night sky is a more than just a time of day… it’s a sage, a storyteller, and a beauty.) (The Astronomer wears no expression on his cold face; only his trademark pair of dark shades. The tail of his trench coat sways in the breeze, but other than that, nothing on him gives any movement. He stands rigidly, just staring…) Dorn: Beautiful… simply beautiful. Everything is so perfect at night. I wish it would last longer than 10 hours. (He gives a long, sorrowed sigh and shakes his head at nothing in particular. Dorn crosses his arms across his chest and continues to gaze at the night sky, as if he’s looking for an answer.) Dorn, softly: Oh beautiful, burning stars above… what lies in store for me? My simple life has run a course of tragedy, anger, and sorrow. Is that my destiny? Am I to be fooled by the positive happenings of the present? Tell me, oh night sky… They will not answer you, Dorn… Dorn, sighing: Yes, I know… It seems if only my illness induced prayers attract their attention. Don’t be so foolish. They’re always listening, they just rarely answer. Have a little more faith. Dorn: Faith… What is ‘faith’? (The Dragon Goddess, residing in psychic form within Dorn’s consciousness, replies in a rather irritated way. A strict, unforgiving being, En’Flarim has been whipping Dorn into a formidable fighting force, try to deter from the emotional mess he once was.) Don’t act as a vagabond, my warrior. You know perfectly well what faith is. It’s what keeps you and I alive. (Tracer lowers his gaze and gives a puzzled look, as this is certainly news to him. He hadn’t known that the amount of faith people have in Astrology keeps HIM alive as well, as it does the Tiamats.) Dorn: Really? Of course. You are bound to me now. You feed off of my energy and live as I do. If people were to lose enough faith in me to kill me, you would go with me. Dorn, darkly: … I see. Why did you not tell me before? It matters not, Lunis Superia. Do not think we’re at risk, for we are well on our way to the exact opposite. Your success, though still in its infancy, holds good things. (Dorn gives a slight nod and stares blankly at the forest below the hill, watching as the beautiful greenery moves from side to side in the evening breeze.) Dorn: So that is my purpose? I am to keep you and me alive? Partially. It would certainly do us both some good to continue existing. Worry not, for I can sense you are nervous. The odds are with us. Dorn, rather angrily: I don’t see how! Apparently, the very Tiamats I once looked up to are out to kill me. You chose that path upon joining with me, Dorn. (Knowing that’s COMPLETELY untrue, Dorn grits his teeth and begins to fume angrily. His nostrils flare and he lets out a violent outburst, swinging his arms.) Dorn, loudly: YOU JUST TOOK OVER! I DIDN’T CHOOSE! I sensed in your heart that you were accepting me. I am your Goddess, Dorn… your everything. I am your life, your death, your very being. You must have faith in me, or you shall simply kill yourself. (Tracer gives an anger sigh and drops to one knee to the grass, feeling overwhelmed. Things get more and more complicated as he goes along. His past has become to creep up on him and the future is looking very bleak. He has only the present to hang on to, and even that isn’t positive in its entire aspect.) Dorn, sadly: This is too much for me… I’m not even weeks into this and I already feel like my body is being ripped apart. My past wants me to forget this ever happened and by the sound of it, the future isn’t looking that great. You have a lot to work for. Your career as my champion is going fine. In fact, the trophy we seek is just over the horizon. Look up… (The Astronomer lifts his weary gaze to the majestic evening sky and sees something beautiful. A true spirit lifter in his eyes… he can do not but smile as a lone shooting star blazes through the sky, quick as lightning. Its ascent is nearly vertically, and suddenly, he knows what his Goddess is talking about.) Dorn, distantly: I understand now… I’m like a shooting star… Of course, for you are the Lunis Superia. You are going to be our hero and champion that saves Astrology. This task should be of no burden to you at all. Dorn: I can already feel the weight lifting, having seen that. I will not disappoint you… Good. Your future awaits you, and your greatness has no limit. If you serve me unquestioningly and achieve your goals, I shall induct you into the Hall of Legends… (Upon hearing this, Dorn’s expression slowly melts into shock and confusion. His mouth slowly drops upon and his eyes draw inward.) Dorn: The… what? The Hall of Legend. An eternal showcasing of immortals. Humans call them constellations. Dorn, shocked: You mean the constellations in the night sky are actually figures from the Hall of Legends?? Indeed. Tartorus, God of Battle, is there. Orion, Earth’s mightiest Hero, is there. Virus, the one who subdued Tartorus’ rage, is there. Desima is there. All have achieved something that no other could. And should you save our dying faith, then you shall find a place in the stars. NO ONE will ever be able to take that away from you. Not even I. (The very idea sends a tingling down Dorn’s spine. It’s an overwhelming and exciting thought, almost unreal. He takes a deep breath, trying to get a feel for the situation, and exhales slowly. His nerves are shot from the mere THOUGHT of that, and he can do nothing… not even speak.) But you MUST succeed. Failure is not an option, and will only lead to your demise. Even though I could easily resurrect, I would choose not to if you were to fail. Do you understand? (Dorn swallows roughly, trying to get his thoughts in order. Almost without thinking, he nods.) Dorn: Of course, my Goddess… The night sky itself shall be your throne if you were to succeed. The rewards are limitless. You will finally be accepted for being a follower of the True Way, a way long forgotten in the minds of fools and blasphemers alike. You must change their ways for me, for Astrology… and for yourself. (Tracer nods once again and sits down in the grass slowly, resting his tired knees. It’s all very confusing, but sounds as if it’s the right thing to do. Is this path right for him? Will it truly lead to great things? Is he going the right way? Only this Friday will decide that. He’s to do battle with Eric Erratic. And it’s for the recently reinstated Rising Star Title. His first true challenge. If he were to win the title, it would bring more prestige and meaning to Astrology. It would convince the masses that this is no false religion. Everything Dorn stands for is riding on his ability to succeed and to gain these trophies… to achieve respect… To win.) [End.]
