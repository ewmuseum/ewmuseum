Mall Rats

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st June 2002 We open upon a black screen slowly fading into view. The screen is black and white, just like in the old movies. We are unable to make out fully what is being recorded. In the bottom right hand corner of the screen reads 13:41 p.m. This time slot is placed in a clear white text. The picture begins to come into view. Even though it’s still a bit blurry we can recognize that there are a lot of people walking through what appears to be some sort of indoor place. Then, the camera comes into full view. We recognize the place as a typical city mall. The camera we are watching it through is one of the many security cameras around the whole place. Through this camera we can see a couple of pet shops to the left hand side, a good few food courts to the far right and a huge gravel stone fountain. There are a lot of shoppers walking separate directions upon the part of the facility. Out of the blue, we can see two men running from the bottom left corner of the screen straight upward. One man, with blonde spiked hair continuously turns to take a brief look behind him. The two men begin to pick up the pace as a further four men appear from the same place, also running in the same direction. The newer four men are all dressed in sky blue shirts, black pants and navy blue caps. Two of the men are blowing whistles. These four security guards, for some reason are chasing the first two men. The camera suddenly switches to another security camera. This time is appears to be facing in the exact opposite direction as the first. The original two men are seen running again but this time toward the camera. Spotted in the far distance are the four security guards still chasing them. The two men stop and catch their breath back assuming they have lost the four security guards. The two step around a corner (still inside the mall) as they begin to talk. The security camera zooms right in on the pair of mischievous mall rats. Shockingly, the two are immediately recognized as Primetime Championship Wrestling tag team, The Price Brothers. Chet is wearing a black T-shirt, and a pair of black jeans. His brother Michael is wearing a blue polo shirt and a pair of white pants. Chet is crouched over trying to catch his breath back as Michael seems to be shouting at him. Since this is only a security camera we are unable to hear what they are saying. The camera darkens out as the scene comes to an end. We reopen outside the mall. It’s a bright and sunny day somewhere in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. From this position we can see the large front of the mall. The doors are glass as are the windows. There are signs posted everywhere on the glass windows. The floor outside the mall is gray gravel. A large stone water fountain, very similar to the one inside is set in front of the mall. Two shadows appear in front of the camera along the floor. After a couple of second, two men appear in front of the screen. The man to the right has spiked blonde hair and is recognized as Chet Price. The man beside him has short brown hair and is recognized as Michael Price. Both men are looking at the camera. Chet: “Hey there, we’re the Price brothers. And today we’re gonna tell all our fans about our upcoming match this Tuesday on Turmoil against Pretty Cool Guys. The match is gonna take place right here in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Just a couple of miles away is the place where we’re gonna fight; Philadelphia’s own First Union Center.” Michael: “That’s right. Now even though Pretty Cool Guys overrate us in size, we can still take them down. Rich Sherwin for example, he’s 6’3”. He’s taller than both of us but still smaller than his partner, Ryan Moore. Moore is 6’4”. But, I hope or the sake of Pretty Cool Guys that they don’t underestimate us. If they’re wondering why, these can ask Politically Incorrect. They made that mistake, PCG shouldn’t.” Chet: “Politically Incorrect continuously said how they where easily going to beat a couple of rookie’s straight from the academy. They strongly believed that because we’re rookies that we’d be an easy ride; a pushover if you will. God, were they wrong.” A deep male voice from behind the camera speaks. Man: “Before you go any further, let me ask you this. We just saw the tape form the security camera’s in that mall. Why were you being chased?” Chet and Michael begin to laugh. Michael: “I was just simply trying to dive from an escalator into a near by water fountain. The damn security guards shouted and gave chase to my brother and myself.” Man: “How did you get away?” Chet: “The main security guard chased us into the Gap ©. He tripped over a stool or something and fell into a heap of clothes. The other three guys stopped chasing us to help him. We got outside and here we are.” Michael: “You see, we don’t mean to cause trouble. Well I don’t at least. It’s all down to this disease we’ve unfortunately been infested with. The disease is known as Jackassenitus and causes my brother and I to do crazy and stupid things just like before.” Chet: “Anyway, we’ve explained all that and now I wanna get onto the subject of the tag team tournament. We’re in this round robin tournament where the winners receive the tag team championship titles. There are so many great tag teams in the tournament. But hey, what do we have to lose?” Michael: “Exactly, and that’s…hey!” A hand grabs Michael and Chet from behind on their shoulders. The two spin around and to the shock of them, there stands the head security guard. He has short black hair and a blue shirt. Guard: “You guys! Come with me!” As the security guard drags the two brothers away, Chet shouts to the camera. Chet: “Until next time! We are The Price Brothers!”
