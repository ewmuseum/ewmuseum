Sippin' On Hennessey

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th March 2002 March 3rd, 2002. A Fatal Four Way match decided the winner of the Cruiser Weight Number One Contender! Four men in one ring and one huge hard fought match with the eventual victor being none other than Anton The Kid! Everything that he had achieved in that match was both leveled and destroyed in a matter of seconds by none other than the self-proclaimed Asian Gangster, Charlie Loc! The whole hard work of the match went up in smoke due to the one man who made his first impression on his so-called 'New' home! Mouth’s were left on the floor as Charlie Loc not only proved he was a hard hitter, but he proved that he has balls…Sizing up his opponent for his debut on Turmoil, Charlie is writing the first chapter of many! He’s out to prove a point…No matter how big or how small someone is, never under-estimate them! 'BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP~!' A car swerves hard to the right, missing a man by a matter of inches and it seems that no one could really care! In every direction life seems to continue without a pause to even reflect on what just happened! Seems to be a part of life here, no one could care less what happens as long as it isn’t to them! Them being the citizens of New York! To them it would be nothing more than just another road accident in their fine city and just more paper work for New York’s finest to fill out! Doesn’t sound to inviting but then again neither does death! The man who has just had his life saved from a force greater than his own swaggers backward and hits his heels on the curb of the sidewalk, swinging his arms around like some sort of retard to gain his balance! CRASH~! He falls backwards hard onto the sidewalk, the bottle of alcohol smashing through the momentum of the fall and spilling its precious liquid, forming a rather large puddle at the young man’s feet! 'What The Fuck?!' A look of anger crosses the man’s face as he pushes himself to a seated position while the alcohol trickles down off of the sidewalk, into the curb and then to a storm drain. As he turns around to see who he thinks has pushed him, the first thing that comes to his eyes is the broken bottle of alcohol and the expression on his face quickly turns into one of sadness. The drunken man literally springs to his feet, making people on the sidewalk stare at him in a weird way as he clutches a thick chunk of his hair and begins to mope before sinking back onto his knees and watching his happiness for the night slowly trickle it’s way to the deep, dank New York sewage system… 'Hey man, are you alright?' A passer by stops walking and looks down onto the crying man and is greeted by a somewhat angry look. He backs up a little as the man pushes his air out of his face and wipes a tear from his eye before leaning back onto his feet and adjusting his pants, revealing his identity! 'You think I’m alright?! HUH?! You think this be ok?!' The man who has now revealed himself as Charlie Loc snaps back and advances forwards towards the bystander who throws his hands helplessly in front of his face and stumbles backwards through the crowd of walking people. 'Holy Shit man! Your Charlie Loc!' His eyes grow wide and the look of fear vanishes from his face and he reaches one of his hands out to shake that of young Charlie’s! Charlie’s screwed up facial expression doesn’t change as he just stops and looks down at the man’s outstretched hand. He stares for a minute or so and both men seem to be looking very puzzled. People begin to walk around the two men and the soft murmurs and muttering starts up. Neither of the two men seems to notice…. Slap~! Suddenly out of nowhere Charlie grabs ahold of the bystanders hand and pulls him up close to his face before raising his other fist and gritting his teeth. The bystander winces and leans back a little as Charlie begins to shake. 'Calm down Charlie! It’s ok man!' the bystander manages to squeak out a reply but it still doesn’t seem to chill him out. Instead the look of anger on Charlie’s face just seems to intensify and Charlie pulls his face closer to the bystanders, so close that the bystander can even smell what he’s been drinking! Charlie grunts and his words become muffled. 'You tellin’ me to 'Calm Down' Huh?! You think this something to be calm about! HOW YOU KNOW MY FUCKIN’ NAME?! HUH?!' The bystander’s face screws up as he leans back further and turns to the left, the smell of the alcohol making him feel somewhat sick! 'It…it…it’s ok! I saw you March Madness, you know the huge PCW show? You kicked ass….i' Charlie’s eyes widen and he raises his fist higher above the bystander’s face. 'YOU THINK I NOT KNOW WHAT I DID?! HUH?! YOU THINK BECAUSE I DRUNK I NOT NO NOTHING?!' The murmursand muttering cease as the people walking by all seem to suddenly walk a huge circle around the two men and keep their heads pointed down as Charlie continues to yell. 'YOU KNOW WHO THE FUCK I’AM?! HUH?!' The bystander replies matter-of-factly, 'Yeah…your Charlie Loc! You rule man!' Charlie first raises that little higher before yet another confused look hits his face. He lowers his hand and tilts his head looking strangely at the bystander before a huge toothy grin crosses his face and he slaps the bystander hard on the back causing a loud slapping sound and a gasp from the man he’s embracing. The two embrace on the sidewalk as the passer’s by are now looking at the two with very weird expressions on their faces. 'Man, I think you rule and all, but I can’t….BREATHE!' The man screams the last word and Charlie let’s go of him stumbling backwards and tripping over the curb once again, landing flat on his ass on the road. The angry look crosses back over his face yet again as he flicks his hair back and catches a glimpse of the alcohol, now trickling it’s last eminence down the storm drain. The bystander covers his mouth and slowly creeps forwards towards Charlie who has somewhat forgotten about the push and is now once again moping over his spilt alcohol! 'S-s-s-sorry man! I..' The bystander stutters before being cut off by one of Charlie’s moans. He continues to creep forward and stoops down on the curb into a squatting sort of position as a tear falls from Charlie’s face, making a splashing sound as it hits the rest of the liquid. 'WHY?! IT CAUSE I ASIAN ISN’T IT?!' Charlie screams before crawling and turning around to sit up on the curb. He continues to sob as he closes his legs and puts his hands over his face. The soft sobbing sounds can be heard as the onlookers don’t seem to care too much anymore and Charlie is once again left alone in his misery. The bystander gets up and sits next to Charlie on the curb and wriggles around to get himself comfortable. 'It’s ok man, it’s just Alcohol!' The bystander pats Charlie on the back as Charlie raises his head and wipes a drain of snot from his nose before replying in between sobs. 'What you mean 'Just Alcohol'?!' Charlie sniffs again. A smile crosses over the bystanders face as he continues to pat Charlie on the back. 'You work in PCW, you earn huge money! Why don’t you just buy some more?' The bystander waits for Charlie to reply but is just greeted with a series of sob’s and sniff’s. 'Ok…maybe not! But hey, look on the bright side! Your going to have plenty of money once you’ve hit it big! Right…?' The bystander still receives no reply. 'Maybe I should just change the subject then?' He stops patting Charlie on the back and the two men sit there on the curb motionless. The sound of traffic and scuffling feet and Charlie’s sniffing begins to pick back up. The cold New York wind also begins to blow as the bystander looks dazed and confused and removes his hand off of Charlie’s back and begins to twiddle his thumbs. All of a sudden the bystander’s face lights up and he looks towards Charlie before asking his question. 'Hey Charlie, I’ve been watching you ever since you pulled that shit off in the NTICW! It’s great to finally see you hit the big time man! I thought it was awesome when you took out that Anton The Kid…' The bystander is a about to finish his sentence when Charlie stops sobbing abruptly and looks upwards towards him with yet another angry and teary glare in his eyes. 'What you say? You think Anton The Kid better than me?! HUH?! YOU THINK HE BETTER THAN ME?! THAT WHAT YOU SAYING?!' Charlie begins to yell at the bystander yet again but the people walking by don’t seem to notice nor care… 'No man, I was saying that you really proved a point when you…' once again the bystander is cut off mid-sentence and the yelling of Charlie picks up once again. 'YOU WANT TO SEE WHAT I DO TO YOU?! HUH?! YOU THINK ANTON GOT IT BAD, YOU SAY HIS NAME I MAKE YOU GET IT TEN TIMES WORSE! MUH-' Beeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeep~! The beeping of a car’s horn off somewhere in the distance cut’s Charlie last words off…. Charlie turns to the side and spits onto the road and onto the wheel of a yellow taxi. A muffled yell can be heard from the driver as in return a cigarette is thrown out the window and lands directly into the last remnants of the trickling alcohol….Charlie looks and once again he breaks down. 'Geez man, you were really attached to that alcohol weren’t you? What was it? Bacardi, Tequilla, Jack…' yet again the bystander is cut off as Charlie looks up with tears still in his eyes. 'HENNESSEY!' Charlie begins to cry even louder than before as his head returns back onto his hands and the moaning and wailing can be heard echoing on the streets. The bystander scratches his head. 'Damn, that stuff ain’t cheap either! I see where your coming from Chuck!' He shakes his head and goes back to twiddling his thumbs like before. Charlie lets out a muffled reply from between his hands. 'You think I not know that?!' 'Well, I’ll try and change the topic here…Umm, let’s see! Oh yeah, your making your debut against that latin guy Reeco…Reeco…Reeco, well I can’t remember the rest of his name, but don’t worry Chuck you’ll clean him up anyway!' The bystander smiles and pats Charlie on the back again. Charlie stops sobbing for a moment and lets out a clearer reply. 'What you say?' 'Chuck, sorry I meant Charlie! Honest mistake,' The bystander let’s out a sigh of relief. 'Not that, what you say about Latin guy?' Charlie looks up to face the bystander sitting next to him with a serious look on his face. The tears are gone from his eyes as he wipes his nose and waits for a reply. 'Oh, about that guy…Ummm, Reeco Suave! That’s it Reeco Suave!' The bystander nods his head and smiles at Charlie before seeing his expression and turning it back into a serious one… 'YOU THINK HE GOOD TOO HUH?! I WARN YOU BEFORE WHITE BOY! YOU NOT FUCK WITH ME, BUT WHAT YOU DO?! HUH?! YOU DO IT ANYWAY!' Charlie gets up as the bystander winces and throws his hands back in front of his face. 'I HAVE BAD NIGHT AND ALL YOU DO, IS TALK TO ME ABOUT TWO WHITE BOYS I NOT EVEN LIKE?! HUH?! YOU THINK THIS A JOKE?! HUH?! DO YOU!?' Charlie grabs the bystander by the neck and drags him to his feet. The bystander begins to choke and fumbles around in his pocket before replying a somewhat cut-off sentence. 'I..I…I…m…so…rry! Le…t…go….i’ll….get….yo..you…some…He…' 'YOU WANT SOME HELP?! I GIVE YOU HELP WHITE BOY!' Charlie screams at the bystander who throws something onto the ground at Charlie’s feet before grabbing the hands which are around his neck…Out of nowhere Charlie lets go with one hand and sends it flying into the bystanders face. A scream can be heard from the sidewalk behind the two men and footsteps can also be heard clattering on the cement. Blood splatters onto the curb and mixes with the remaining alcohol as Charlie laughs before sending yet another hard right to the bystanders face. 'I GIVE YOU HELP WHITE BOY!' Charlie laughs sadistically. Charlie flicks his hair back and then pushes the bystander into the road as the smell of burning rubber and screeching tires fills the New York air. He leans down and picks up the object the man threw at his feet and opens it up seeing a wad of notes and signifying that it’s his wallet. 'You help me good White Boy! I go buy another bottle! It seem my night not so bad after all! Sweet Dreams!' Charlie chuckles to himself before tucking the wallet away in his pocket and walking off through the crowd of onlookers with blood stained onto his fist. He disappears into the thickness of the crowd and sirens can be heard. A car door can be heard slamming as the citizens of New York finally seem to take notice of what was going on… A drunken psychopath had just wrecked an innocent man’s life for the next few months all because he was willing to help! He was just trying to be a good citizen but no one else seemed to stop and care…. All it’s resulted in is just more work and toil for New York’s finest and a shit load more paper work, and they wonder why police are never around when you need them…. The first chapter of many has just been written in the book of Charlie Loc. Not only has the man proved he can still fight when he’s drunk, but he’s given off the impression that he doesn’t take shit from anyone! Not much is for certain but one thing is for sure, PCW won’t be the same once he’s been through and done his deal…no way will it ever be the same! Perhaps it’s just a small fire burning on the end of a match, or maybe it could be a fire about to burst into a fire ball! Who knows? Only time will be able to tell it’s tale….
