Coming Back Down the Road

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.05.04 (RP#2 TT) Five months. It’s been five. Long. Months: Five months since the grueling match between Abner, and his German foe, Matt Hunt. Five months since his heroic victory over his archenemy. Five months since the greatest night of PCW’s later life. Five months since Abner was promised a shot at the Television title. Which he never got. Five months since he was promised a shot at the Hardcore title. Which he never got. Five months since he was promised a match at Cloud Nine. It never happened. Five months since he was promised a no-holds-barred, all-out, street rule Cargo Hold Death Match. It never happened. Five months since, after being defeated at the hands of PCW’s favorite redneck, a tired, beaten and disheartened Matt Hunt would be forced to strap on a parachute, and step off the ship, plummeting down to the ground from thousands of feet above earth, humiliating him into retirement, and getting him out of Abner and PCW’s hair. Forever. But that didn’t work out. Five months since Abner would have returned home, a champion, having just completed his first major PCW tour, and a sweeping victory over 'The Masked German.' But that just didn’t work out. Five months since the fall of an empire. Though it gave birth to many, smaller new ones. Five months since the stars fell out of the sky. Crashing into the earth, leaving craters, but also giving birth to new, foreign forms of life. Five months since the rulers of an empire took their last breath. But not before ascending to the heavens. Five months since the extinction of the dinosaurs. But not before leaving their prints, and we will forever walk in their shadows. Five months since the fall of the greatest wrestling federation ever to hit the waves, PCW, causing a riff so big and so deep, that no other fed in history could replace it, and none ever will… Until now... ---------- Abner yawns, stretching out his arms, and twisting his toes. His long, slender feet stick out from under the sheets, and the gaping ‘O’ of his mouth widens as he lets out a yawn, straightening his crooked back. He rolls back over in bed, bringing the pillow over his face. He closes his eyes, and tries to go back to sleep. But he has no such luck. So sitting upright, with a dirty, hairy hand, he reaches behind his back and scratches his bottom side with his fingers, itching at the tingle and poking at the grit. He’s sore. His lower back has brought him pain over the past summer. Though his scars from yesteryear have all but faded away, now and then, there’s still a pinch of venom, shooting up his spine and making him cringe in discomfort. But compared to other times, this is nothing. 'Huh', he grumbles, looking down at his hand. 'Poison ivy, must be...' He shrugs, tossing off the covers and getting to his feet. After being in the woods for so many months, it doesn't surprise him. But the last he knew, he was never allergic to it. Well, maybe he's going soft. Shame. He slowly makes his way over to the window, peering out at the golden morning sun. 'Beautiful day,' he mutters, pulling down the shades, mockingly. He storms out of the room, and starts off his day with a nice hot cup of fresh Green Mountain coffee. 'Ah!' he exclaims, cupping the top of the mug with one hand, balancing its base with the other. After a quick shower and a shot of whiskey and Coke, he sits down at the table to read the morning paper, freshly thrown in by the paperboy…right into a puddle. 'Asshole,' he mutters, shaking his head while he lights a cigarette. But nonetheless, he takes a seat at the table and unfolds the morning paper, albeit covered in mud. 'Ah yes,' he says, 'let’s see what’s in the news. Let’s see what’s in the fuckin’, lying-ho news...' 'Headlines,' he reads, ' ‘Three Killed in Car-Trip On Way Home From Canada.’ 'Well, that’s certainly fascinating,' he adds, sardonically, 'I’m glad they were on the way home from Canada, because, boy, if they were killed on the way home from somewhere else…well then…well that’d be a different story! 'Oh, and here’s something interested: ‘Stock Market Down Again; Economy Takes Another Hit’. Well surprise, surprise…didn’t see that comin’. I mean, hasn’t been too often in the last three years that something bad has happened! And Christ, add insult to injury, why, the same time the stock market goes to shit, so does the economy…What a coincidence! Go figure, huh? 'Jesus Christ!' He slams the paper down on the table, spilling coffee everywhere. Shaking his head in disgust, he clears it off, and pours himself another cup. See, it’s been almost four long months since Abner’s had a decent paying job, and he’s a little on the edge. Restless as ever, and just as foul-mouthed. These times are rough. Making his way into the bathroom, Abner stops in front of the mirror and stares into the eyes of a beast, his reflection seemingly increasing the intensity his scars, both mentally and physically, ten-fold out of proportion to what they really are. Abner pulls back his hair, now long and dark, streaked with thinly curled strays of white, and blinks his eyes. Deep and green, without a glint of happiness. He sighs, relieved. He may feel like he's getting old, but still, he's a young man, and he's got a long way to go. A long way. He turns around, fingering a puple welt on his lower back. It's a bruise he recieved while working out in the woods. A loggging accident. Logging being what he's done since he's left PCW, and other oddball jobs here and there. So shaking his head, Abner reaches into the overhanging medicine cabinet and pulls out a razor and shaving cream. His thick, prickly mustache is starting to bother him, and on more than one occasion he's been inclined to itch. So he sets to work immediately, treating his rough skin as if it were a sworn foe. The razor slips. He cuts himself. Swearing under his breath, he pulls back and lets the razor drop to the bathroom floor. He sighs, peering into the mirror noting the blood trickling down his cheek, staining the white cream a dark pink. It hurts. So, in order to releive the burning sensation, he wipes off his face with water, letting a thin stream of blood, cream and tainted water run down the drain. 'Damn!' he screams, seeing that the job has not yet been completed. In random spots around his face, the hair still thickly grows. He shrugs. He assumes that he'll have to wait 'til later. And so he leaves the bathroom in a hurry, but with a quick glance back in the mirror, he notes the new, fresh scar, and his heart sinks into his chest. Suddenly, from somewhere in the kitchen, a phone rings. He rushes out. No, the living room. He rolls his eyes, swiveling his feet, and turning in the other direction. Only to find that, actually, it's coming from his bedroom... Muttering under his breath, he makes his way into his room, stepping over a pile of clothes and begins to fumble around, looking for the source of the racket. 'That's funny,' he whispers to himself, 'I coulda swore I had that thing disconnected months ago...' Finally, exhausted, he sees a thin cord sticking out of the bed, and pulls it back...only to find the phone disconnected at the other end. 'Damn mut!' he screams, shaking his fists in the direction of the dog house, outside his bedroom window, '...stupid son of a bitch!' But then, he cocks his head sideways, perturbed. 'Wait a minute...'. The phone's still ringing. So diving into his closet, he wrestles free a pair of dirty barn pants, tosses aside a stained brown boot, and lifts up the phone, putting the reciever to his ear. 'Hello?' He stops for a moment, his eyes gone wide. As the words flow through his body, penetrating his head, he stands, stunnded, not believing what he's hearing...nor the voice he's hearing it from. ---------- He forgot about the PCW cell phone, issued to him when he originally signed on slighlty over over a year ago. But surprise, surprise. Just when you think something's over, and you're ready to move on, it comes up again and bites you in the ass. And sometimes, though, it feels good. Real good. But that's the way things goes. So after feeding his dogs, packing his bags, and calling his mother to say goodbye...again...Abner's all packed up, and ready to go. He calls up an old girlfriend (well, kind of...but we won't go there quite yet...), and instructs her to watch after the place. He says, after the initial inception, he should be back home for a few more days, with just enough time to clean up the place and say his proper goodbyes, before he's off to the ring, and off to compete in the federation that he once loved, PCW. And so he hits the road, wind at his bak, ready for more adventures... And with Abner, would you expect anything less?
