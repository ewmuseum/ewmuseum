The Last Saga: They Should Always Pay Attention

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-17-03 There is a city, its on the east coast, and the people refer to it as Philadelphia. It is where millions of people have been born, millions raised, and millions died. It is a city like almost any other. Sure, it has its differences on the outside, the skyline for one is more magnificent then your normal city. The people in it are different, but vaguely the same. The inner workings of the city ran the same. The streets, the money, the murders, the corruption, the poltical graf, the theft, the government, the circuitry, it all came down to the same infastructure that every single major city has based itself off of since the 1900's were a new born baby. During the day it ran like a well oiled machine, and at night it glittered like constalations long forgotten. The city breathed and never died. It couldn't. It was almighty Philadelphia, the original capital. The main difference is that there is a single star on this beautiful constallation that is like no other, it shines like darkness, and is dark like light. It is the Dream Lord, the Anarchist, he has many names. Many personalities. Many different sides to himself. The problem though, is that even as an Anarchist his past haunts him. He tried to change, he tried to make himself more acceptable in a way. The Anarchism was a way to get noticed, to champion his cause even further then he could as the misunderstood Sorcerer that he was. So he threw his Dream Realm aside and let it fall into disrepair, he let the people dictate what they thought like they had hundreds of years before deities came into existance. He picked up the Circle-A and carried it on his back. And noone cared. It would be hard to say truely why noone cared. They should have. The problem wasn't him, however, it was his medium. How could one get noticed in the place he was trying to use as a soap box. The soap box had long since rotten, noone listens to those who stand upon it. Primtime had become the Graveyard Shift, and although it wasn't his fault, he was the scapegoat. His views were ignored, his talents thrown to the ground, and his respect non-existent. It made him want to vomit, so he did, he vomitted himself into hiding, where he ignored the idiotic rantings of the World Champion and the even more idiotic rantings of the President. He locked the doors to the Drazail Mansion and he sat there with his beautiful wife for hours, days, then weeks on end. Waiting, waiting until everything got better. 'It never would.' Were his own words one night, Talena stirred curiously. He didn't follow it up, he just stared in his own little world, thinking and plotting. Clyde wasn't even a problem, they had always said they would end their feud in PCW once and for all before one of them left, and they would. There was one problem though. This wasn't PCW. It was a shell, a self-parody, a sickening display of idiots running around with one of seven belts that dont even matter to anyone anymore. The people like DarkStar had been getting phased out for nearly a year and they didn't even notice. Clyde and DarkStar were to battle eachother one last time on this mortal plane, and noone cared, because noone cared enough to pay attention to it. Its like how you can look at a city, like Philadelphia, and understand it exists. You know everything you have ever heard but that was it. You have never been there. You just hear things from other people, so you make a judgement about it and move on. Thats what PCW is now. People don't look inside the city anymore, they don't open the windows and listen in. If they did they would know what was going on in that large converted church when DarkStar was thinking, and if they had known then maybe there wouldn't have been a problem. Maybe if they werent idiots and would just have listened to him instead of what everyone else was saying, there wouldn't be a problem. They would have also seen a young priest walking down a uncongested back street of Northern Philadelphia in the unseasonably warm weather, they would have seen his black robes and his white collar, and they probably would have went 'I'll be damned, I can see between the lines now.' and they would have seen him strolling without a care in the world, his bible in one hand and his rosary in the other. If they were DarkStar, they could have seen his personality, and how he was a champion for HIS cause.. the souls of the people. They would have seen that he became a priest recently because of all the ills in this world. And Maybe, they could have warned him. 'Father, forgive me... I am about to sin.' The priest heard the voice and turned around quickly, he saw nothing. 'I must be hearing thin-..' His thought couldn't be finished, a thick cord of red beads tightened around his neck, it was his own rosary that had been in his pocket only seconds prior, it began to lift him off of the ground, his face turning a shade of blue as his feet kicked feebily in the air nearly three feet off the ground. He couldn't fight back. There was noone holding the rosary. Laughter filled the street as a tall man in a trench coat with ice blue hair stepped out from the shadows, a girl hanging off his shoulder. 'Gavyn, don't be cross.' She laughed that shrill, fear inducing laugh, it was like a time machine. Talena's eyes were almost glassy, as if they saw nothing... or everything at all. 'Oh, I won't be.' The priests eyes nearly bulged out of his head, in his brain he saw pure Evil, not the typical angry anarchist, but pure and unhidden evil. As if the Morning Star himself had... no... 'It couldn't be? Is that what you are thinking?' DarkStar finished his thought, the Priest wished the rosary would kill him now. 'No, I'm not Lucifer.' DarkStar put a black cigarette in his mouth and lit it, 'He is much more subdued then I am. More about the revenge on God and all that stuff. Green eyed monster I guess.' 'This isn't about Him.' Talena said, 'This is about the stars. All the pretty stars.' DarkStar nodded, humoring her, 'This is my own revenge, Father, not revenge on God though. Nothing against him, he is just doing his job. You see, father, it takes alot to get noticed now a days. I tried to let my true self show through and ease humanity into seeing their downfall gradually, I made my hatred for the American government known in public.. but they didn't notice, they were too fucking stupid to notice.' He took the cigarette from his mouth and lowered it a little, his black-nailed hand cupped it, presumably to keep the wind from putting it out. 'So, i'll make them notice. I'll make them notice like they should have nearly two years ago when I first gave them the chance.' A fire grew, and grew, and grew in his hand, nearly all the air was out of the Priests lungs. 'So I'll use you as my own little Post-It Note, father.' His hand raised and the fire ball that was once his cigarette flew out, hitting the man in the chest and ingulfing him in seconds. He stiill hung in mid-air by his Rosary, irony I would guess. 'What pretty colors.' Talena's eyes lit up like a childs on Christmas. DarkStar took the woman in his arms and embraced her. 'Like old times, right pet?' Talena smiled and reached down where most of the Priests blood had emptied from his charred (and still mostly flaming) body, she rubbed her fingers in it and looked at it admiringly. 'Like old times, the City will burn into more pretty colors, won't it?' DarkStar looked one more time at his handy work, at his first kill in months. 'Everything will burn, Talena, EVERYTHING.' Yes, they all would have known... if only they paid attention.
