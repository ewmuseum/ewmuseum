Conference Call with The Spice Girls

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin had infiltrated the mansion of Ginger Spice.Whilst there he managed to convince Ginger that the world needed ‘Girl Power’, but then a new dilemma reared its head…. How will he convince the rest of the members to rejoin the Spice Girls? [Scene opens in Geri’s mansion as she storms towards her office with Flaymin in tow. Flaymin however seems to be more interested in the scenery that surrounds him] Flaymin: Wow, I never knew Geri-ville had so many statues of naked men…. Geri: What else is a girl supposed to do in her spare time? Flaymin: *cough* Singing lessons *cough* So anyways, where are we going? Geri: Come with me- I’ll take you to my conference room. [The two walk down the long and stretching corridor and into Geri’s conference room. The room itself is quite large with a gigantic table in the middle of the room surrounded by expensive recliner chairs and a fireplace in the corner] Flaymin: Wow, what do you do in here? [Geri just rolls her eyes and replies with a sarcastic tone in her voice] Geri: I sit around sculling beer and cracking nuts on my forehead. [Flaymin looks around at all of the statues and erotic photo’s of naked men that are scattered around the room] Flaymin: When you say cracking ‘nuts’…. Geri: *sigh* NO, I’m not talking about testicles. Flaymin: Oh… I knew that.Its just that with only one nut left, I’m quite protective of it. [Geri gives Flaymin an odd stare before she starts typing some names into her laptop.Almost instantly, the names of the remaining Spice Girls pop up on the laptop with their respective phone numbers. Geri dials the numbers of all the other Spice Girls and presses a button on the phone that allows the dial tone to be heard from the speakers situated around the room] Flaymin: So what’s happening? Geri: I’ve called all the girls up for a little conference call. [Flaymin suddenly clicks his fingers together] Flaymin: I get it now!Conference Calls….. In ….. The Conference room!! Smart, very smart. [Geri briefly considers the thought that maybe letting Flaymin into her house was a big mistake. While the phone continues to dial, Flaymin walks around the room looking at all the ancient statues of naked men] Geri: You sure do like those statues, don’t you? Flaymin: Yep, do you think I could get a miniature one of me so I can hang around my neck? Geri: Let me get this straight:You want a mini statue of you posing naked… so you can hang round your neck? Flaymin: Neato, huh? Geri: Uh…. Suuuuuure. [Before this enthralling conversation can continue, Emma Bunton a.k.a Baby Spice picks up her phone and speaks into it] Emma: Hello? Geri: Hello love, its me- Geri. Emma: Seinfeld? Geri: No. Emma: Jeri…cho? Geri: Who?No dammit, its Geri as in Geri the chick formerly known as Ginger Spice. [Flaymin quickly chimes in with his own description] Flaymin: You know, the namesake of Geri-yoga! Emma: And who’s that? Flaymin: I’m Flaymin Aym- Mel B: ‘ello, who’s this? [Flaymin is abruptly cut off by Scary Spice who has also just picked up her phone and joined the conference call. Geri: Great, we now have three of the group members.We’re just waiting on Posh to get off her back and Sporty to pull her fingers out of all those dykes. Flaymin: Is Posh having back pains? Geri: What? Flaymin: Why else would she be on her back? Mel B: Think about it, love. [Flaymin ponders this riddle for a moment….] Flaymin: Oooh right… Emma: You get it now? Flaymin: Yep…. She should know better than trying to pick up heavy boxes by herself. [A collective groan is heard as Geri looks at her watch, waiting to get the conference underway. A moment later, Mel C a.k.a Sporty Spice comes on the line although she seems busy screaming at someone else in her home] Mel C: SHIELA, GET BACK ON YOUR KNEES AND DON’T GET UP UNTIL I FIND THE WHIP!! Uhhh…. Hello? Geri: Hello Mel, its Geri. Mel C: Seinfeld? Geri: No, you freakin’ slut! [By this point Geri is furious.She reaches over to a nearby statue in the room and slams it on to the floor only to see it shatter into a thousand pieces] Flaymin: Dammit woman, you just ruined my favourite statue. [Flaymin storms towards the door in an attempt to leave the room] Geri: Where do you think you’re going? Flaymin: I’m going to find some superglue so I can put the statue back together. [As Flaymin exits the room, Posh Spice i.e. Victoria Beckham comes on the line] Victoria: Hello, who’s this? Emma: Hello darling. Victoria: Oh hi Emma! Mel B: Hi Posh! Victoria: Hi Mel, how are the kids. Mel C: Long time no see, Victoria! Victoria, Tell me about it, Mel! [Finally Geri decides to greet Victoria] Geri: Hello Victoria, how are you? Victoria: Geri? [Geri sigh’s in relief, thankful that someone has finally remembered her] Victoria: I just love your whole stand-up routine, Mr Seinfeld! [This time, Geri’s face turns a shade of red.She bangs her fists on the table and then tries to tip over another statue only to be stopped by Flaymin who has quickly rushed back into the room to save the naked male statues] Flaymin: Get a grip, woman!Leave the poor statues alone!! Mel C: So what is the meaning of this conference call? Flaymin: Ahem, allow me to introduce myself.I’m Flaymin Aymin and I’ve come here on a very special mission to…… [Scene fades out…………] Tune in next time for the thrilling conclusion to The Spice Girls Saga!
