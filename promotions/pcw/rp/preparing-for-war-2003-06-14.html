Preparing For War

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 14 - 03 The faint smell of gasoline and clean steel fills the air, breezing the air and carried by the heavy-duty power of the industrial-size air conditioner in the ceiling. Weapons of various types as far as the eye can see in the large showcase room, ranging from rifles to grenades to fist-grip knives. The dusty, concrete floor is littered and dotted with various display cases, each holding a different instrument of war. Despite this, the place seems innocent enough. The only person in what appears to be an army-navy store is a grizzled man smoking a short cigar and wearing a gray army tank top. His hair is buzzed and an angry look dons his face. He’d probably like some customers. And he’s just about to get one. Bing! The little bell attached to the front door gives a little ring, signaling an incoming customer. The man straightens up and holds his head high as the customer approaches, adorned in a familiar black trench coat and pair of shades. It can only be one man. “Hello,” Dorn Tracer states flatly, his eyes gazing about at his surroundings. “You the owner of this place?” “Yes, I am.” The man behind the counter retorts, watching Dorn as he searches about. “Can I help you with something?” The Astronomer doesn’t answer as he sifts through the odds and ends of military equipment. He picks up grenades, gun holsters, packages of bullets, and torso armor. The man behind the counter raises an eyebrow at Dorn as he searches about, apparently on the hunt for something specific. “Can I help you?” The man repeats again, not shaken a bit by the annoyed look Dorn gives him in return. “I don’t know. Can you?” Though slightly irritated by this arrogant man, the cashier doesn’t bat an eye, “I can try, sir.” “I guess that’s the best you can do, then?” Dorn inquires, wearing a smartass grin on his clean-shaven face. Why he continues to taunt the rather large man behind the counter is unknown, but he obviously doesn’t see a reason to quit. “Well then, I’ll take it. I’m preparing for a war, and I need the proper equipment. Nothing subtle, but nothing that will completely destroy where I’m at. Know what I mean?” “You’ll have to be more specific, sir.” “This is no ordinary war I’m going in, you understand. This is much more DANGEROUS war. A much more PERSONAL war. Not one fought on charred grounds and in leveled towns, but one fought man-to-man. A war fought between two single minds out to out do each other, you know what I mean?” Tracer explains, with a burning look of hate in his crystal blue eyes. The proverbial fires dance within his pupils, signifying his distaste for whomever he’s talking about. “And for that same war, I need supplies. Tools of destruction. Something to combat my rivals unique weaponry. CUSTOM-MADE weaponry, at that.” “What do you mean?” The man inquires, his burly arms folded across his chest and a thick eyebrow raised in puzzlement. Dorn sifts through a various pieces of sniper net, not really taking any notice of the odds and ends. “It’s hard to explain. Let’s just say it’s very difficult to prepare for. My rival’s means of war are like no other.” The man nods and purses his lips in mock ponder, as if he’s really thinking about helping this arrogant prick. To further this act, he even begins to look through his merchandise, faking a search for something to help Dorn. The Astronomer, however, isn’t faking. He continues to rummage about, hoping to find what he’s looking for before the next century rolls around. Becoming a little annoyed by Dorn’s arrogant, annoying presence, the man speaks up, “Just what kind of war would you be fighting to be looking in here for nothing in particular?” “Just exploring my options,” Dorn retorts flatly. “I see,” The man continues, “Well, I don’t know if you’ll find what you’re looking for, but good luck. I could probably be of more assistance if you’d explain better…” The Astronomer seems to freeze in mid-motion, his head the only thing moving as it slowly twists around to look the man dead in the eye. The man’s a little taken back by this sudden strange gawking from Dorn, but keeps his jaw level. Even when Dorn approaches him and stands right in front of him, he doesn’t budge. “You can never possibly know the demon I face. This cretin is probably the worst thing to walk the Earth.” “How so?” “Such an evil presence. It’s caused me nothing but pain for WEEKS on end. An innocent victim like myself has had to endure what I would like to call complete torture. Sneak attacks, mind games, and even physical harm. Take that times quite a long duration and you’ve got yourself a war. But I won’t let it bother me any longer. I’m preparing to FIGHT BACK!” Dorn’s triumphant call echoes about the store as he stands proud, his chin high. However, the man only shakes his head, “Whatever. It can’t be that bad.” Dorn gives him a horrified look, almost like he’s seen the ghost of John F. Kennedy, “How can you say that? You don’t even know her or what she’s capable of!! She’s monster!! A MONSTER, I SAY! A NO GOOD LYING MONSTER!” “She?” The man inquires with a small grin. “DAMN RIGHT!” Dorn cries, slamming his fist down on the nearest table. “They’re the worst kind! Fucking beasts!! They’ll torture you and smack you around until you go insane, and it’s illegal to do anything back! Well SCREW THAT, come Tuesday AND Cloud Nine, that bitch is mine! She’ll finally get hers… mark my words!” And with that, without even waiting for the man’s heckling response, Dorn storms to the front door and flings it open. He disappears into the afternoon sunlight, leaving the man with only one thought. ”Just say no to drugs…” [End.]
