Kidnapped?!

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/02 With the founding of the new Church of Crunk, there’s bound to be some setbacks and problems. Rival religions will do whatever they can to try and take down the following of the Crunk as He!l. As Martin Luther King Jr. had threats made against him for his new ideas about equality for all races, Rush will have these same threats made to him because of his new church, which may seem like blasphemy to some other churches. This is made ever more of a problem because Rush is PCW superstar. So, with crazy fans and church followers, Rush’s own safety must always be on his mind. The scene opens up in Rush’s large apartment in Greenville, NC. The shot, at the moment, is on the spacious bedroom of Rush, and as usual at the beginning of promos in Rush’s apartment, Rush is sleeping on the large king-sized bed that is lined up along the far side of the wall. His snoring can be heard over the daily chatter from outside. On the nearby night table, his alarm goes off with the annoying “bleeeeeeeep bleeeeeeep” that means it’s time for Rush to get up. Rush reaches around, trying to hit or grab the alarm clock, but is unsuccessful in his sleepy state. Very reluctantly, Rush gets up slowly out of his bed. Walking around in a zombie-like fashion, in baby blue basketball shorts and a wife-beater, he heads to the bathroom to get ready for what will be a strange day, as is almost every one. But he can’t even begin to believe how strange a day it will be. Rush plays the radio while in the shower, blasting “Give it to Her Hard” by Tantro Metro & Devonte, which is the crunkest song in the world not done by Petey Pablo. (End of music promoting.) After Rush’s rousing rendition of the song in the shower, which can only be compared to the screech of a cat if you gave it a titty twister, Rush gets up and goes back into his room to get into a fresh set of new clothes. No, the camera doesn’t follow him, you sick freak. When the camera finally does come into Rush’s bedroom again, he’s in his stuff and is ready to go out. He’s wearing a black sweater-like vest with just a wife-beater under it, with dark blue jeans, over white T-Macs. As he heads out the door, with the keys to his Range Rover in hand, he grabs his wallet and shuts the door behind him. He walks through the carpeted halls, and stops to push the button to go down to the garage on the elevator. He waits for a few moments, humming “Give it to Her Hard” out loud. Finally a loud ping means that the elevator is there, so Rush gets in. Inside the elevator, he sees that the button for the garage level is not lit up, so he just decides to go down to the ground level. After about five minutes, with no one else getting onto the elevator, Rush steps out onto the ground level and goes over to the where the receptionist usually sits and ignores people, but she isn’t at her desk at the moment. The only other way to the garage is a set of stairs that is accessible from outside, so Rush, rather irritated at this but still wanting his vehicle, heads out towards the revolving door in the front of the apartment. He steps through it, and finds himself outside in the brilliant sun of a day that seems like spring. Rush goes around to the side of the building, and heads towards the stair set that leads down to the garage. He quickly steps down them into the large, open garage. Off in the farthest corner to the northeast is his Range Rover. He walks fast over to the car, but a man steps into his way. Man: Sir, do you think you could tell me how to get to Flowers St.? Rush: Just go out of the entrance, and- Rush didn’t see the man who stalked him from behind. The man stands over Rush with a lead pipe in hand, and he cackles evilly. The two men lift Rush into the back of a large SUV. They look around before closing the back door, and jumping into the ride. They quickly go in reverse, and then speed out of the garage. Rush’s mind now seems to be playing games on him. On the outside, he can feel the sickness rising within him, and the bile build up in the acids, and tries to erupt from his throat. However, inside his stomach feels tingly, just like his head, but it’s not painful. In this black void of dream, pain and happiness are melded together and he doesn’t know where his body is, but his soul seems to have lost the will to live. He feels empty. However, he must put this feeling aside, because he knows that he was attacked, but where is he now? What is the pain that he feels from the outside? AGGGGH! Rush: AGGGGHHH! Rush screams out in pain, as he feels the cigarette of one of the men on his skin. The man takes away the cigarette, but he just looks on, with a blank look on his face, at Rush. Rush: What in the fuck was that for? And why the hell am I in the back of some huge cat? Because I just had the weirdest dream. Man: You wouldn’t wake up. If you hadn’t woken up, we would’ve just killed you, because we’re not going to waste time with you. Why are you in out car? Well, you’re in our car because you’re now our property because we kidnapped you, and if you don’t follow whatever we say, then you will die very painfully and slowly, so I suggest that you stop your moaning, or we’ll stop it for you. Rush gulps and watches on as the two men go back to their huddled conversation. Rush tries to catch parts of it but he only gets small part. “Master…we…no time…he….we…died…good thing.” Rush tries to decipher the small bits and pieces that he can hear, but he can’t make anything of the scrambled messages. Rush sits back, trying to think of any way to fight back, but he can still see the lead pipe in the hands of the second man. Rush: Where are we going? Man: Well, I suppose you’ll know that when we get there, won’t you? Rush: Hey, you’re the ones that kidnapped me, the Crunk as He!l God, and- Man: You WILL NOT refer to yourself as a God!!! Rush: And why wouldn’t I refer to myself as exactly what I am? You’ve already taken me away from where I live; I think I don't have to put up with your blasphemous ways. Man: You are no god. In fact, you are barely good enough to be called a mortal. You portray all that a god is not. And when you meet OUR god, you will see what true power is. Yes, he will show the one way…the only way. Rush: Okay, so let me get this straight: You’re not going to tell me where we’re going, you’re denying the fact, which by the way is true, that I *AM * the Crunk as He!l God, and you won’t tell me who your master is. The two men laugh at Rush’s comment, but Rush just raises his eyebrows, with a questioning look, towards them. They both now have fire in their eyes, as the one who has talked to him the whole time, replies in a strange voice. Man: Oh, you know our master. Hahahaa, you know our master well. But he will show you. He will show you everything. He will even show you, before he disposes of those godly ways of yours. HAHAHHAHA! The other man does something very strange at this point. He knocks the other man in the back with the lead pipe, blacking him out for a moment. Rush just watches on and now looks very confused. Rush: Are you on my side? The man lowers his head, seemingly in shame, and doesn’t respond for a moment. Finally a low, grumbled voice emits with the answer. Man: No, I’m not. I don’t like to see the deaths, but I’m caught in the web now. There is nothing I can do for you now. I’m too deep. I’m just too deep… The man trails off into soft sobs. Rush leans back into the uncomfortable position in the back of the vehicle. He can’t bear to look at the man, who continues his soft crying in the seat above him. He slips off, once again, into sleep… By the time Rush wakes up, the first man from before has woken up, and strangely, he is just talking to the second man like nothing ever happened. This time, Rush cannot catch any part of their conversation, so he just sits in the back and pretends to sleep, trying to save up his energy for whatever may come next. He feels the car slowing down, and finally is jolted a little bit, when it comes to a complete stop. A small click is heard, as the back door unlocks. The two men come to the back and raise it up, letting Rush step out. However, as soon as he steps out, he is hit with the lead pipe again, and he crumples into a heap. The driver, who can now be seen, is a large man and he picks Rush up, following the other two men. They are outside of a large house that uses Greek architecture. Columns are on both sides of the entrance and the stone building has a slanting roof on the sides, with an arch in the middle of the roof. The gray stone steps leading up to the building are now covered in shadow, and night has fallen on wherever this is. The three men, and Rush’s prone form, head up the stairs with no noise at all, silently advancing towards the door. The man who talked to Rush in the car is wearing a suit, while the one who talked to him in the garage is wearing a long black trench coat. They open the door, and walk in as cold air rushes through, past them. Inside the doorway is an ominous sight. The walls are lined with torches, and in the firelight, dried blood, caked over in layers, can be seen on the walls, splattered from side to side. Some is fresh and red, while some of it has dried into a sickening brown shade. The three men make no note of it, and walk down the silent corridor, soft padding sounds from their feet onto the stone. At the end of a corridor is a giant circle like a large amphitheater. In the middle, there is a black robed figure, and all around are more people dressed like him, but with their hoods down. The three men bring Rush’s figure over to the man in the middle of the circle, and lay his body before this man. Man: We have brought him to you Master. We have stolen him from his world, the world of mortals who believe in him, and brought him here for you. The master, the man clad in all black and his hood up, puts his two sleeves together and kneels down to look at Rush. He then rises after an inspection of the unconscious man. Master: Good work, now be off with you. This man and I have much to discuss. After I’m through with him, he will know who is the TRUE god around here. The three men leave, as do the people all around in the circle. It takes a few minutes, but finally the Master is alone with the body of Rush. Master: You..will…call me God. The Master turns away from the camera, and pulls his hood down, but the only thing that can be seen is the back of his hood, hanging down on his robes. He cackles loudly, and the shot goes up to the full moon, clouds passing over, as the cackling can be heard below. The evil may rise, if nothing is done. But nothing can be done for now, so we fade to black…
