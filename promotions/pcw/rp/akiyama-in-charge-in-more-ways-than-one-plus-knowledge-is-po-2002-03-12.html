Akiyama in Charge in More Ways than One; Plus... Knowledge is Power

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/12/02 The scene opens inside of a long hall, longer than conceivable by the human brain. The hall contains countless books that written on every imaginable subject, shelved and organized in standard alphabetical order, but each book, each subject, being so large and thoroughly researched that reading but one book would take an entire human lifetime. A bright cascading bluish-white light radiates down from an unknown source about the hall. The hall itself has no walls, no floor, no ceiling, just emptiness with the exception of the books and the shelves. Here in this hall stands Akiyama, his celestial form the same as his ethereal form, wearing the familiar Neo Void Armor. Akiyama:“We don’t wear the armor anymore.” Handler:“Yes, well at the time I’m writing this, I can’t contact your handler to ask what you’d be wearing, so just accept it for now. When I can, I’ll ask what you wear in the celestial realm.” Akiyama:“Fair enough, we agree.” Handler:“Just asking – since when are you allowed to talk to me OOC?” Akiyama:“We figured since that blind guy gets to do it so much, we should to. And frankly, we do a better job of it.” Handler:“Whatever, just get back in character and wait for your cue line, okay?” Akiyama:“Very well.” Here in this hall stands Akiyama, his celestial form the same as his ethereal form, wearing the familiar Neo Void Armor. Handler:“Wait, your handler’s on, he just got a new SN. Let me contact him. Akiyama:“You better fix that.” Handler:“I will! Gimme a second.” Akiyama:“Daft tool.” [detail withheld]: Hey. [detail withheld]: Hey [detail withheld]: I’m doing an RP where Akiyama takes The End to the celestial realm. What would Akiyama wear there? [detail withheld]: Same as always. Black zippered vinyl pants and a black mesh short-sleeved mock tee. [detail withheld]: Okay, thanks. Handler:“Okay, you don’t wear the Neo Void Armor, I’ve got your proper attire.” Akiyama:“That’s better, we wish to be represented properly.” Handler:“Would you let me get on with his RP?” Akiyama:“We’re just screwing with you. Don’t take it personally.” Handler:“Geez.” Here in this hall stands Akiyama, his celestial form the same as his ethereal form, wearing black zippered vinyl pants and a black mesh short-sleeved mock tee. No shoes were specified, so I’m going to assume he’s not wearing any. Akiyama:“We wear shoes, twit.” Handler:“Would you let me do my thing?” Akiyama:“You’re not doing too good of a job with it. We believe it would be best if we took over here.” Handler:“You can’t do that!” Akiyama:“Oh no?” Akiyama opens up a portal to the Void in the computer lab in which he works as a lab assistant. The other students on campus, working hard on their papers and diligently replying to inane e-mail gossip with their friends, are oblivious to the handler being sucked into the black hole of nothingness. They don’t even hear his screams as he is ripped out of his own reality, and into the empty darkness that is only the Void. Akiyama enters the computer lab from the doorway at the corner where the left and front walls meet. He moves past the printer desk at the side of the door, and sits down at the vacant computer in the third row, Computer 9 of 12. The students in the lab busily type and tack away at their keyboards, unaware that the NVG is in their presence. Akiyama:“We’re writing this RP now, so if you don’t like it, GO SCREW! As for the rest of you, keep reading…” Neo Void God Akira Akiyama, with his latest recruit The End, transcends the ethereal realm as he so often does, glad to be rid of the stench and corruption of Earth and back in the purity of the Celestial Plane. Akiyama, from the central hub where all Gods and Angels arrive to the Celestial Plane, takes his young charge deep into the core of the universe, where no mortal has ever gone before. The trip is quick, though infinitely long, as there is no conception of time or distance in the celestial realm. All space is linked together, and one has but to think of where they wish to be in order to be there. Akiyama’s Void operates on similar mechanics, which is why he is able to use it for travel by using it as a medium to traverse the ethereal realm. Within seconds that last an eternity, Akiyama and The End, who is wearing his ridiculous gold mask, silver shirt, and white pants that look like they are split in the crotch… the man has no fashion sense. Akiyama:“Your handler gave you no fashion sense. That’s one of the things we’ll need to correct in the Void Baptism.” The End:“It’s not his fault. I’m blind. I don’t know what I wear from day to day. I could put on a dress in the morning and I wouldn’t know.” Akiyama:“So maybe we should get rid of that blindness.” The End:“No, it shapes my perspective on things.” Akiyama:“What perspective you can have being blind.” The End:”Stop being mean.” Akiyama:”We think as a god we’re entitled to be mean if we so choose.” {At this point, Akiyama thinks about adding in a mention to Zurick. And so it happens… Now back to the regularly scheduled RP already in progress.) Within seconds that last an eternity, Akiyama and his protégé The End stand in front of two large wooden doors stretching 20 feet tall and 10 feet wide. Akiyama but wills the doors open, and so they make themselves ajar, allowing the Neo Void God and the Nephalim to enter. The doors open into of a long hall, longer than conceivable by the human brain. When the doors close, there are no doors, just a hall that stretches forward and back seemingly forever. The hall contains countless books that written on every imaginable subject, shelved and organized in standard alphabetical order, but each book, each subject, being so large and thoroughly researched that reading but one book would take an entire human lifetime. A bright cascading bluish-white light radiates down from an unknown source about the hall. The hall itself has no walls, no floor, no ceiling, just emptiness with the exception of the books and the shelves. Akiyama moves over to the shelf, guiding The End to follow him with a waving hand gesture. The End cannot “see” Akiyama per se, but follows the motion by some extra-sensory perception (made easier in the celestial plane due to the lack of the distractions of the corporeal realm such as noise, confusion, large crowds of people, and the like). “Welcome to the Hall of Knowledge,” Akiyama says, holding his arms outstretched at the shelf their right. “As you know, knowledge is truth, truth formed from perspectives based upon experience and learned facts. If one’s knowledge is but one truth, then the Hall of Knowledge is THE one truth, being all truths, all perspectives. Everything that ever was and ever shall be is recorded in these volumes.” The End shakes his head and whistles a low-pitched note. “That pretty much kills the free-will notion.” “Not exactly,” replies the NVG, “for the Hall of Knowledge is but a blueprint for the course of history. For the entire lifespan of the universe has a script from beginning to end, a plan of how everything should happen. However, the script is not enforced, and so deviations can and do happen.” The End feels the books. In fact, in the celestial realm, he can indeed see the books. “I can see here….” He silently utters. Akiyama crosses his arms and smirks as he looks down at his feet there being no floor to stare at. “The conditions and afflictions a celestial endures in the ethereal realm do not follow one to the celestial realm.” “What happens,” The End begins, “when those deviations occur; when the script doesn’t go according to plan?” Akiyama shrugs his shoulders, his head turned to the left tilted on his shoulder, still looking at his feet. “Nothing,” he answers, “and everything.” “I don’t get it.” “There is Fate. He determines the order of the universe; he maintains the scripts and checks to make sure that they do what is supposed to happen. Fate’s a terrible guy to hang around with. He can always tell when you’re lying. That and he cheats at poker, damn him.” “My question?” The End returns with a smirk of his own. “Everything that ever can happen will happen and does happen. There are infinite realities, each one different because of one mere detail not going off as planned.” Akiyama holds up his right hand with the index finger outstretched. “Imagine a world different from this one for the mere fact that Little Debbie makes yogurt instead of snack cakes.” The End ponders a thought for a second. It is unrelated, and he soon returns to the main discussion. “And which reality do we exist in?” Akiyama takes in a deep breath, though breathing is not required in the celestial realm. His massive muscular chest expands and shrinks with the air Akiyama merely believes that he is inhaling. “The celestial realm is the only universal constant; it remains unchanged no matter what the reality of the corporeal realm. But the ethereal realm we exist in… so far as we know, that is the realm that is the closest to the script that Fate maintains.” “Punk Club” by the Utah Saints begins to sound in the ears of the Neo Void God and The End. The music has no origin, it seems to be all around the two. The End looks around for a music source. Akiyama just stands against the shelf with his arms crossed. “Where’s that music coming from?” asks The End. “That?” says Akiyama. “That’s just Fate. That’s his theme music.” “I recognize this. Utah Saints… Punk Club. This song goes on forever.” “Just like the course of history.” A puff of odorless smoke appears between The End and Akiyama. It clouds everything in sight. The smoke quickly dissipates, and standing in that spot is a being with long white hair, unblemished young face like that Sean Astin wuss; wearing jeans, black cowboy boots, and a brown leather jacket. Black biker gloves cover his hands. The End looks over to Akiyama. “You say I don’t have any fashion sense?” Fate looks at The End with disapproval. “You’re not much better,” he spits. “Don’t fight, you two,” Akiyama interjects. “I heard my name,” Fate says, looking over to Akiyama. “What’s up?” “I was telling my young friend here about you,” Akiyama says, pointing to The End. Fate leans to The End, and whispers to him, “If he said anything about me, a beached dolphin, condoms, and liquor, it’s a lie.” The End takes two steps away from Fate. Akiyama laughs. “I was telling him about your duties,” the NVG says. Fate nods his understanding and his voice gets louder. “Ah, yes,” he begins. “I make sure that the plan for the universe is followed in at least one reality. If the plan is not followed, and fate (the concept) no maintained, then all realities will cease to exist, having nothing from which to branch off.” “Is that a very hard job?” Fate laughs and shakes his head. “Not really, the universe polices itself in that right. But I still get to be immortal and do almost anything I want.” The End thinks for a moment. “Punk Club,” being an extremely techno-mix, continues playing. “Let me ask you some questions,” asks The End. “Shoot,” replies the Fate. The End picks his words carefully, whatever in his head very important to him. “Do you know everything in the main reality that I exist in?” Fate nods in the affirmative. “May you share that information with other Celestials?” Fate shakes his head and adopts a “Yes, but” sort of tone. “I can,” Fate says, “but I can only shed light on things that have already happened. I can’t give out information about the future.” “Great, that’s okay.” “What do you need to know?” Fate asks, genuinely interested. The End takes a second to answer. “I really want to know…” A pause in speech; Akiyama and Fate don’t know what The End is going to say, and they’re interested to hear it. “I want to know who my father is.”
