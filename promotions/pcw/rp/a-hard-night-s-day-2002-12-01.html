A Hard Night's Day

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: A Hard Night's Day Featuring: Tim Walden Date: December 1, 2002 It was morning...The day after Alliances...The first thing I remembered as I got up from the bed, this was the day I finally taught the Trapezoid a lesson...I wasn't feeling all that shabby for having gone through hell, but I still ached a little, guess victory acts as a bit of a natural anaesthetic and all. How many times had I beaten him? I didn't really think about it so well, taken a lot of pain and celebrated pretty hard afterward...I knew I'd gotten the better of him, however...It was too early, I knew well enough that, never got any decent thinking done in the morning anyway...Looked to the Cruiserweight Title laying on my suitcase... ...Well, at least one thing became apparent quickly. I was the PCW Cruiserweight Champion...That strengthened my self-confidence right there, the fact that I won the belt myself, one-on-one with the HIT, sweet revenge for every little attack, screwjob finish, missed opportunity, the agony he had put me through, emotional damage healing over in one grasp of championship gold... But it wasn't enough. I was now well awake, well enough aware now that he wasn't finished in my books. I might have gotten the better of him there, but he wasn't the sort to just quit because he'd lost, he and I still met twice more that night alone...It was all too apparent that there were still problems to be dealt with, he had still cheated PCW out of its rightful tax money, as had Tomas, and they still had done too much to just get away like this....But we could keep the pressure as long as necessary..Whoever got the TV Title shot would be able to take the belt from the Trapezoid..I felt relaxed for the time being, and I headed into the shower to take advantage of my somewhat ignorant bliss.. Flash forward to shower's end, as I pulled the towel around myself before sliding my socks on, then looking into the mirror with a continuing confidence...This was the new Cruiserweight Champion, one of the winners of Alliances, the next Champion of some other title hopefully come next week....Not the World Title, of course, it'd been Xavier's plan to win all along that had gotten us victory and besides, he owned the company, and probably not Continental gold either, being that Duke wouldn't be inclined to accept something below me... This left the same choice of shots to split in most cases. I could take the TV shot or the Extreme...I sat back to consider my options, still focused on my reflection. Beat up the Trapezoid again? Ehh, on one hand, this could be a nice opportunity to knock it into his head that he wasn't cut out to fight me...But the title was mine before, was it worth trying to get it back? HIT-Walden once more I was not in the mood for at the time, it'd just be another case of the same old, and after HE became a 2-time champ, it was tarnished...Maybe I could be Extreme Champ, it wouldn't be that tough to beat Vurdo or Amulet...In fact, it was all too easy of a task, but the belt wasn't all that valuable in lineage, so I might as well not be stuck fighting with a great effort for it..It was just the next step to a possible eventual Grand Slam, best get it while the getting was good.. That was what I could do. Brandi was capable of outwrestling the Trapezoid if need be, but I was a little worried still from what had happened last night, with the three having been counted in his favour as he got a little taste of victory...It was a rather cheap win though, she'd gotten double-teamed and attacked by Clyde throughout....But she told me she'd be all right, just go score the win, it was better off if the World Champ was out of the running anyway...She sacrificed herself for my good...No, for the team's good, but I was still going down in history with a pin on the World Champion to punctuate my series of pinfalls throughout..... My mind wandered for a few moments on the night's events, as I got up and left briefly, grabbing the day's clothes out of my suitcase before heading back into the room..They cheated us out of the tag titles, solidifying his ability to ruin my title shots and yet still keep crowds on his side, but I still got him the next time, I survived the Percent Error off the ladder and got the title eventually...I remembered nullifying his progress in the Alamo match, as I pulled the slacks up to my waist... Then it hit me, as things tend to do....Memory suddenly kicking in just when I'd gotten on top of the world, knocking me down a couple pegs...Brandi, soaked through and right in front of me...If I'd had any sense, I'd've turned away immediately, but the male instincts held my attention, like a train-wreck...A hot, drenched train-wr--What was I thinking? I shook my head, relieving it of that thought for the moment..This was just what I didn't need, I couldn't let myself start thinking of her in that way or things would just get more complicated... As if it wasn't threatening to grow awkward enough already...I could still keep my cool there, but I was now fast growing fearful that it would be irrelevant..The view had sparked arousal, and though I tried to hide the fact as best I could, it may easily have become evident, whether to another wrestler or the cameras sending the activity in the ring into millions of homes across quite a few countries....She hadn't seemed to have found out, but the world may have just watched me watching her and the results thereof...And that world probably included Nathan. So, there were two possibilities here.... Either I could probably get on with life as it was beforehand, none the wiser, and I would be able to concentrate on my matches....Or, just maybe, I had just aroused the ultimate wrath of a man I had come to fear, a psychotic boyfriend whose attack based on mere jealous suspicion had put me into hospital while simultaneously having the potential to lead to a horrible tension with my closest ally, all for the fact of tension in my tights. I would probably be well off to get security, if not for that it would only cause more suspicion should it be the best case scenario...It wasn't anything to worry about yet though, I shouldn't have started panicking, maybe it wasn't that easily noticed...I looked back across the room at my one great distraction... The Cruiserweight Title. I could make it not merely my goal, but also use it as a tool of avoidance of reality. If I just focused my efforts on the belt, I could put off any thought to the repercussions of Alliances until something really did happen...This was a good solution, but it couldn't keep me from being still a little worried about going to the office...Just pretend that nothing's wrong, think about your matches....What a time to be unbooked. At least there was a contendership match for the title... ...I shouldn't really be happy about that normally. Who was fighting in it? That El Romantico guy and Virus Latoya, or whatever his name was, something I didn't care to overthink...Wasn't he like from Thailand? They were both relatively new, so hopefully I wouldn't have to try too hard to beat them...However, trying too hard was what I was looking to do now, so for once, in my mind it was pretty obvious...Whoever wins would be facing dire consequences, battling a man concentrating on the match as best he could out of hope to get away from the stress...He'd be lucky if it blew over quickly, I could relax a little then and perhaps he wouldn't be unfortunate enough to suffer the brunt of my exertion.. But right now, it wasn't just a matter of hanging onto the gold which I held in my hands...This was a matter of keeping my sanity intact, I had to plan for dealing with one of those foreign crazies, fighting each other for love of a woman, and further aspirations to gold.. Twas a shame that I'd have to rain on their parade... ...Even the winner would have to lose. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
