Where was Mark after PCW?

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27-1-03 ::Introduction:: (Makonie back in PCW is a wonderful thing to the e-wrestling community. Now finally people can see what they have been missing with the put downs of other minorities. People haven’t really seen any hate in the e-wrestling world since Makonie has exited PCW. In those last couple of months it seems as if a lot of things have changed. Makonie didn’t come back to PCW to prove anything to the fans. He came back to prove it to himself and to everyone who didn’t think he could make it. With all of the peace people hating Makonie from the last time he was in PCW what are they going to think of him now since he is back? On Fusion this week Makonie is going to be able to show everyone how much they have been missing and how much he may bring back to the ring.) End Introduction:: The camera pans in the scene as Mark Makonie is sitting on the street corner in a back yard alley. Mark is wearing a pair of black leather boots with black eyelets and black laces that go all the way up, but are untied. He has on his black jeans that are cut at the bottom to fit around his boots as well as his wife beater that is not tucked in to show his ripped body. Mark is sitting there stroking his beard thinking about what he has gone through in the past couple of months since he has been out of PCW. “The past couple of months since that damn nigger broke my jaw my life has changed.” Makonie says above all of the street noise out on the main drag. “The coon put me out of action with a surgery that the PCW didn’t cover. They fired me before they could pay and that was uncalled for and now they want me back. What was the change in their mind?” ::Flashback:: The streets are dark with a spotlight showing the Confederate Flag above a town hall in an unknown Southern city. Mark is strolling the streets with the tape around his jaw from the surgery that he just had. Too many thoughts are going through his mind as he is walking in the same attire as he usually does. “What was PCW thinking when they fired me?” Mark asks to himself as he can see he is the only person out this late at night on the streets. “It was that entire coon’s fault. He didn’t do the best for the team like I did. Did they think I enjoyed tagging with him? Well it was the worst couple of months tagging with him.” Mark goes past a restaurant that shows a newspaper clipping in the window. Mark bothers to stop to see what that says in interest. As Makonie reads the clipping he gets a smile on his face as he gets done with the article. He stands in amazement to look at the picture as well that goes along with the article. “The city boys attack once again.” Mark says with excitement in his voice. “I am glad that someone has taken over command here in this little town. That the white community has taken action and followed the path that I set for them years before.” Mark thinks about his past and in his early teen years when he first started off being a Nazi. There were those nights they the boys would go out to raid the black community with shaving cream and even with guns. Mark backs away from the glass window as he rubs his jaw and realizes how sore that his jaw is. “The gas station hold up was the first robberies and Nazi related events that I did in my past.” Mark says as he is rubbing his bald head as well. “I remember that like the back of my hand.” “Look at this cracker trying to act all Nazi and shit.” A voice says in a distance ahead of where Makonie is walking to. “You have that symbol shit on your left pec and think that now you can go around and torment the whole nigga nation?” “Do you know who the hell you are talking to?” Makonie shouts in the distance as he can’t see anyone that was talking since all the lights have mostly been burnt out on the streets which leaves a chill of fear loathing around the streets. “I know I am talking to a small ass motherfucker who thinks that since he can shave his head and walk like a nigga hater and dress like a nigga hater that he is one.” The voice says as a shadow is leaning up against a steel pole which is dripping with water from the heat of the night. As Mark walks closer to the voice he doesn’t say anymore since he is trying to do an element of surprise and to first off see who is up against the pole. Mark’s thoughts about his doctor bills, and about getting fired from PCW is gone and is now focused on the man with the deep voice who he can’t hear. Who whenever he speaks sends a chill down the spine of Makonie not in fear but in worries of who is speaking to him. “Whoever the hell you are you don’t know who you are speaking to.” Makonie says as he is getting close and can hear the man breath as he is breathing hard as well as see the smoke come out of his mouth and rise to the dark black sky. “Oh is that so?” Asks the man who is inhaling on a marijuana smelling joint. “Then how do I know your Nazi fuckin name is Mark Makonie?” Mark thinks to himself that he wonders how he did know his name? Well Mark was just fired by PCW and so that is how maybe some people would know since the white guy who hated all was know out of wrestling. “You see my name on the television.” Mark replies in a strong set of voice. “No you weak ass bitch that is not it.” The guy replies as he then takes another hit of his joint and finally throws the roach on the ground. “I know you for being that white ass guy who thinks that he is better than all of the niggas around in this place. You are full of yourself and you have got something coming to you.” “Well in those words then if I have something coming to me, you might as well bring it now since I am not going to let coons again tear me apart again.” Mark says as he is now face to face with the coon who he is talking to.” “You asked for it.” The guy says as his voice carries throughout the streets of the small city. Mark is eye to eye with the man as he can see the black man’s black pupils surrounded by a dark brown color around them. Mark is not looking around to anyone else as he then gets knocked on the head with a wooden baseball bat as the bat cracks down the barrel and through the handle. Mark falls to the ground like a sack of potatoes and blood starts to come out of the mouth of him. “Mark you said bring it to you so that is what I did you nigga hatin bitch.” The black guy says as he and his other guy take off in the night as the lights in a nearby apartment flicker on. Mark lays there knocked out cold beginning to have blood run down the middle of his chin to the neck of him. Mark’s head is as well bleeding down the front as a couple of streaks of hot blood run down the middle and sides of his forehead. The blood is starting to stain the gray cement on the sidewalk as Mark is still not moving an inch as in later days will finally realize how much pain can hurt. ::End of Flashback:: How can Mark cope with what the past couple of months have left him? How will he come back and make sure this doesn’t happen again as it has since no one will believe his beliefs. In days and months to come Mark is going to find out how hard it is yet once again to live in the wrestling world and give it his all in the real world.
