Heros and Fairytales?

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/04/03 ************************************************************************************************************************Journal Entry 23/04/03 Heros and Fairytales? The hero of this story, as in many of the stories told throughout creation, is never the intended character. Look deeper, and it's always someone else. Whether it be his faithful sidekick or his worthy opposite, the hero is the one that sacrifices himself in spite of the odds given to him. I have never considered myself the hero, and will never take that mantle in time. My thinking and actions are not of what heroes are made of. I can't really think of the last time I saw a hero, anywhere, except in the comic books and cartoons and animes. You see them there everywhere, sacrificing themselves for someone they barely know, but they do it because it's right. Ideals like this are not meant to be outside that world. It's survival of the fittest, my friends, dog eat dog. Hunt or be hunted and all that jazz. Heroes simply cannot exist in a world that does not need them. And it's really not so much that we don't need them...they cannot be found. The world is a harsh place, and to be someone like that...well...it just can't happen. I don't wanna bust anyone's bubble or anything, but truth be told, I'm more of a hero than anything you'll see in Superman. I tell it like it is. I hunt. I defend, retaliate, am honest and dont steal for my way. Loyalty and friendship is fought for. It's like a friend once told me: once two people hate each other, more than anything in the entire world, only then will they be able to trust each other. It makes sense only to the people that have done so. In truth, the true heroes out there are the ones that show their children and siblings that yes, this world will mess you up and yes, this is a bad time to grow up in. But you know what? That's also the harsh reality of it. There's no such thing as a 'Face' wrestler out there. People that claim to be 'Face' will also do anything to win a match, just like a 'Heel', bottom line. Even Rent a Hero used chairs during his matches. I'm not a hero, folks. I'm a realist. The glass is half empty dammit, 'cause it is, and unless you plan on filling it up, don't tell me otherwise. Wrestling is all a game, that's all it is. A game. Its a thrill. Its the claim to fame everyone wants. Does a hero want to be remembered? No.Wrestlers do. That's why there's a freakin' Hall of Fame, people! Wake up and smell the cigarette smoke! Hero's cannot exist in the world we play in. No one is above the other person. When you step into the ring everyone is equal in what they can do. Sure some are more experienced than others and some are willing to go down roads others might be unwilling to venture down.I want the challenge of a lifetime, and I'll do what I have too to get it. Call me an asshole. Call me what you will, but you know what I am? I am somebodys HERO. You're damn right I am. PCW shows everyone what's up around here. It teaches your children everything you don't wanna show them. You don't want them to learn about violence and sexual content and nudity and drugs and all that other good shit the world spits to them? Then don't let them out of the damn house. Heroes tell the truth, tell me this world doesn't suck? Tell me this place isn't harsh? Thought so. It sucks, and you all know it. I know at least every single grown up out there had told his or her child never to grow up, to enjoy it while they can. We tell them why. We tell them the truth. That's what heroes do. We can't help that they listen. The truth is, and this doesn't go out to just the kids, but to everyone out there: More then likely, you all will die without having made the slightest impact on this world. And as time goes on, you will be forgotten and even further down the line, some will question your existence. With the truth in hands, people, run with it. Why bother living in a world that will shit on you anyways? It never made sense to me. Maybe come friday, when I will fufill a dream, things might make sense. Maybe this Young Mexican will become a hero or maybe he will just grow up. ************************************************************************************************************************
