The Sleeping Dragon...

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/08/02 October 4, Friday Fusion at the MGM Grand in Las Vegas, Neveda. The time is roughly 7:30 pm. There are people waiting for the show, but it hasn't started yet. In fact, no one was at ringside save for some lingering wrestlers and crew. The End was scheduled to wrestle on Duzza's team against Rush and Mark Makonie. Duzza was seeming to for an alliance with the Uprising, but Rush wasn't too fond of him, while at the same time, The End didn't like Makonie anymore than he was against Rush Mason. But before that could happen, there was some business that had to be taken care of... Anyone who saw the match saw The End getting beaten around. But what they didn't see was what happened before the show ever aired... The End is walking to the ring from the back in a relatively empty staff parking lot. There is determination shown upon his brow and he clenches and unclenches his fists as he walks with brisk pace to the ringside area, where he was scheduled, as declared by CEO Xavier Kannon on the previous Fusion shortly before his win over Frisco, that there would be a punishment match forced upon him. And The End had no idea what he was stepping into, just like last time. Hearing short, fast footsteps behind him, The End turns 180, but continues walking, backwards, to his destination. Sandy Wong, wearing the usual silver spaghetti-strapped low cut blouse and black pants. She catches up, running behind him. Sandy: Sky! She stops, slowing down to catch her breath, but the urgency is still in her voice. You don't have to do this! The End: Booked in some punishment match, might as well get it overwith. I won the last one, remember? Sandy: Yeah, but you still got beaten to a pulp. The End stops walking and just stands there over Sandy. She slows her walk as she catches up and hugs him, her face buried in his chest. Sandy: I don't want to see you get hurt is all. The End shakes his head, his hair flicking to and fro with his new mask that doesn't cover his head allowing it to breathe. The End: Don't worry, I heal quickly even if something does happen. No need to worry. Just stay back here until I'm finished. The End walks away. Sandy stands there for a moment, watching after; but soon she leaves. Though not on the air and not even wearing headsets, Dave Kern and Jeff Marx are sitting at the ringside commentators' desk, ready to watch the pre-Fusion punishment match that Kannon set up for The End. The End really didn't do anything wrong, other than working for both PCW and PURE at the same time. Funny, until Inphino Blitz upstaged Kannon and beat up Akiyama, that had never been a problem... There are fans in the crowd, but nowhere near capacity to the sold-out arena that PCW had managed with its last free cable show before Red October overseas in Japan. A total of 50 people occupied their seats, mostly in the front row to just get a feel for being right up by the ring before security and the rightful ticketholders forced them to move back to the nose-bleed section where they were supposed to be. But tonight, before Fusion started, these fans would get either a treat or a travesty, depending on how much they liked The End... Marx and Kern sat at the table in matching black tuxedos. Marx eats a Caesar Salad, his dinner for the night. Kern is drinking some red wine cocktails before the cameras go on. Jeff: Punishment time. Dave: I don't think The End deserves any of this. He wrestles well, and for free! He's every owner's dream worker! Jeff: Be that as it may, he's going to be handicapped in more ways than one after tonight! The End walks down to the ring and slides in. He steps past the referee, Ibe Black (already in the ring per Xavier Kannon's orders), and moves to the far corner. Dave: What could our CEO possibly have booked for The End to do now, before he has a scheduled tag match later tonight? And The End's opponents, all three of them, walk down to the ring without their music playing. The lack of music is to trick The End, perhaps he won't hear them coming. He doesn't at first, being too far away up the ramp. But The End is close to the commentators' table and he hears Dave and Jeff perfectly well. Jeff: Uprising versus The End? Playaz Inc., along with their manager Jerome 'Blayze' Jordan, approach the ring. The End, sensing their proximity to the ring, bounces off the far ropes and charges to the other side of the ring, jumping clear over the rope with a high angle shooting star press! Dave: Frailty! Both Playaz Inc. go down. The End picks up Dante and throws him into the ring. Statuz Quo is quick to get up and clubs The End in the back with a forearm. He turns End around and whips him to the guardwall. The End springs onto it and jumps back off with the Cyclotron! Jeff: Meh. Climbing back into the ring, The End is met with a kick to the gut from Dante Jackson as Ibe Black calls for the bell. Dave: WHAT?! Jeff: Quo's getting to the ring now, might wanna take advantage. Jackson whips The End to the ropes. Anticipating a clothesline, The End ducks it and goes behind Jackson with a rolling prawn hold... Dave: He pulls it back up into a wheelbarrow suplex! Jeff: And there's the other one. Statuz Quo gives The End a flying forearm as he rises. Quo pulls End to his feet and shoves him into the corner, then begins to stomp down on The End. Dante Jackson soon gets up and moves in to help out. Jeff: Black Attack is beating up the black ninja. I'm opposed to Black Attack, but I don't like The End. What do I do? I know, watch The End get beat! Pulling The End up out of the corner, Playaz Inc. whips him to the opposite corner. The End slides to a stop, and when both men attempt to follow in with a double clothesline, The End bridges into an evasion and kips back up to his feet. Jackson turns around faster and charges The End. The End kicks him in the stomach and grabs his head. Shared Vertigo! Jeff: Can't he just do a regular DDT? Statuz Quo attacks The End by punching him in the face as he stands up. A whip to the ropes, reversed by The End, Quo comes back with a running tackle. Dave: ENOUGH! Early advantage, The End has to hit and run in this match. Jeff: You think? Jackson comes in and grabs The End from behind in a rear waistlock. The End flips out of a German Suplex attempt, into an inverted facelock. The End spins around into a Stunner. Dave: One Last Time! Leaving both members of Playaz Inc in the ring, The End steps out to the apron. With Statuz Quo trying to get to his feet near Dante Jackson, The End springs up onto the top rope and executes a corkscrew shooting star spin... Fans: OOOOOOOOOOOHHH! The End lies on the mat, his head cracked from the VICIOUS chair shot that Rush Mason delivered to the flying End. Rush Mason picks up The End and sets the chair on the ground. Jeff: Blackout. Whatever. The End folds over onto his stomach from Rush's move. Playaz Inc recover from The End's attacks and stomp him into the mat. And they are joined by Blayze, who accompanied Rush out. Dave: The End shouldn't have to go through this. Pulling The End back up, Playaz Inc crashes his head into the mat with a big Stuffed Piledriver. Jeff: Well, another face getting his face kicked in is always good, so let the marking begin. LAY THE BLACK-DOWN ON HIS BLACKANESE CANDY ASS! Blayze is out of active competition, but Playaz, Inc. pulls The End up to his feet for Blayze to hit an Argentine Backbreaker Rack Slam. Dave: That's Thug roC's move... Jeff: And Blayze's whole function was to replace Ham Hock, so it's okay. With The End held down by the soon-to-be tag team champions, Rush Mason climbs to the top rope and with the assistance of Blayze as a launch, jumps off onto The End with a high body splash. Jeff: Ha! Bet he wishes he wasn't a Cruiserweight now! Dave: That was a big body splash from the 260 pound Rush Mason! While Rush Mason mounts The End and punches him in the face, Dante Jackson and Blayze kick him at the sides of the head. Statuz Quo is standing back, calling over to him that he wants another shot at The End. Rush and Blayze back off while Dante Jackson pulls End to his feet, though The End is hardly standing on his own. Jackson locks The End in a front facelock and lifts him up for a suplex, but turns it into a Falcon Arrow, which Statuz assists halfway down in a powerbomb. Jeff: Well, he's already lost two matches in one night. I think I'd like to see more of this trend... Dave: How can you take pleasure in another's suffering? Jeff: It's remarkably easy if you'd grow a backbone and have your testicles descend. Rush Mason kicks The End onto his back and places a single foot on top of The End. Ibe Black counts the pin with a smile on his face. Black: ONE! TWO! THREE! Fans: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHHH!! Rush looks down. The End's right arm is thrown into the air. Picking up The End, Rush Mason lifts him onto his shoulders for the Shadow of Greatness. The End slips out! Playaz, Inc. and Rush immediately pound on The End with kicks and punches as he tries to fight them all off. Numbers get the better of him, that, and he gets hit with another chair shot. Dave: Down on his knees, The End is getting kicked all to Hell by the Uprising. Jeff: He should be going down. What's wrong with him? Dave: I for one applaud that tenacity! The End starts to stand up. The Uprising punch and kick harder, but he still gets up. Almost to a stance, The End releases and loud yell and The Uprising members go flying away. The bright white light dies down. It came from The End. An exertion of his Celestial energy in desperation. Though not knocked out, The Uprising all fight to their feet with confused and panicked looks. Not knowing what to do, they randomly start punching at air. Jeff: Would someone explain to me what's going on? Dave: I think The End used his Celestial powers to make the Uprising blind! Jeff: No, I think that harlot Sandy turned the lights up too high. And of course Mr. Blindy McAsswipe wouldn't be affected... The End rolls out of the ring and walks slowly up the entrance ramp while Jeff shudders at the mentions of both Jane Reagan and Tyler Nelson. Ibe Black seems to have been spared the effects, and counts the ring out. The End doesn't come back, he just goes right out of the ringside area. Though they don't know it, The Uprising wins this Punishment Match... It was welling up within me... A feeling like there was something deep inside about to burst out. Not literally, but I could feel it... as though I wasn't the only one inside my head. That there was another voice... not like the one I heard that led me to the PWA contract that sent me on my way to the career I now continue in, even though I lost my sight because of it... But a different voice... almost demonic. Not articulate at all... just said 'Kill them...' And I could feel it taking over. Almost like that Linkin Park song, 'Papercut.' On a side note, I have no idea how that song related to Anton McGonnel... It took everything I had to fight it off, even at the risk of continued assault from The Uprising. I've heard that voice before... back when I had the visions of my parents, Sandy, the Misfits... that I slayed every one of them. There's something going on inside of me and I don't know what it is... Whatever power I used before Fusion was out of desperation, just like in the match with Rush Mason at Red October in the Tokyo Dome... The End, just before giving up and tiring out, seems to sense his impending danger, and literally bursts out of the hold before RUNNING STRAIGHT UP THE SIDE OF THE STAGE ABOVE HIM, AND DIVING OFF NEAR THE TOP, NAILING RUSH MASON WITH A BLINDINGLY FAST SPEAR.... End rises up to his feet suddenly and swings his arms out violently, as a literal purple ball of psionic energy explodes in a radius about four feet wide, sending both The End and Mason crumpling down to the concrete floor in pain.... 1… 2… 3… 4… 5… Dave: Okay, they're both dead. Just forget it. 6… 7… Suddenly, something seems out of place. A lone woman appears from behind one of the decorative plants in the Tokyo Dome's lobby, with a knife in her hand. Upon closer inspection… 8… …It appears to be Sandy Wong, The End's beloved friend. She elegantly carves the knife gently into the flesh of her own arm, and a small flow of blood trickles out slowly… Dave: Uh…? 9… It seems to spark something in the end. Sensing that Sandy is hurt, he springs to his feet immediately, and actually hovers in the air for a moment before landing on his feet. 10! A concussion blast emanating from my palm... running up a sheer wall? Hovering? I'd not done anything like that before. But it wasn't me in control, really. At least until the end. All throughout that fight with Rush Mason it was like I was watching it. Whatever it was, had control of me, and it wanted Rush's blood. It wanted to taste Rush's blood. But when Sandy approached me and put her hands over mine... then ran it down her arm... something stirred in me that I had to get up and help her, screw the match. I remember now, I was in my right mind. It wasn't so much a hovering as it was just an exertion of my energy forcing me away from the ground in time not to be counted out... but didn't even worry about Mason, Sandy was my concern... I knew she had more wrong with her than just a cut arm... As the feed cuts to commercial, Sandy Wong collapses in The End's arms. He catches her and runs her back to the trainer's table. The bottom of her shirt is a little more red than if she just cut her arm. Setting her down on the table, pushing aside the dead body The End took out earlier in the night, The End takes off his glove and places it on Sandy's stomach. There is a little too much give to it. Sandy's breathing goes shallow as she lay prone on the table. He pulls up her blouse a few inches below her breasts and places his hand down to her stomach again. A rush of blood meets his hand... The End takes his other hand and takes Sandy's pulse. It's getting harder and harder to detect. Sandy reaches up and places her right arm on The End's near left arm with which he cradles her head. Her vision goes blurry from tears and she begins to cough up a little blood. Sandy: I heard... Kenshiro say that you... do better when a lo-- loved... one is gonna die... The End shakes his head, his eyes tearing up for the first time in a long, long time. The End: Why'd you do this? ... I could lose the match. I don't want to lose you... Sandy smiles at The End, not expecting him, normally avoiding such a subject as his feelings about her, to be so vocal. The End: Your life's more important to me than my career... She shakes her head weakly, The End supporting her head in his hand as the blood from her mouth trickles down her right cheek, falling on his hand... Sandy: Well *coughs* your... care-- *coughs* career is more im... portant to me than my life.... That's when I did it. Wasn't even so much thinking about it, it just happened... I just put my arm back down on her stomach where she nearly gutted herself, and did it... The End's entire body begins to glow a bluish-purple as he takes both hands and places them upon Sandy's mortal injuries. He presses down just hard enough not to hurt her and breathes in as deeply as he can. Exhaling, he releases a low grunting chant of some sort as the glow slowly drains from his body down his arms to his hands. Sandy glows as well, her eyes pulling themselves closed. Her breathing slows as The End transfers this aura to Sandy. Before he can finish, it just completely stops. The End doesn't let up, the glowing field of psionic injury still moves from his body to Sandy's. Behind him, Cowabunga Connection are standing and watching him. They wait for their own match with Playaz, Inc. which is scheduled next on the card as soon as the production crew lets them know it's time to go out to the ring. TTT: Dude... And The End finishes, there is no stir in Sandy. Heavy Metal Harry's head sinks to the ground with a shake. HMH: Bogus... Cowabunga Connection step forward and pat The End on his shoulder, but he shrugs them off. Standing over Sandy, he just waits. TTT and HMH look at each other, just wondering how long The End is prepared to stand over the corpse before moving on. But then, Sandy opens her eyes and sits up! TTT & HMH: WHOA! Though she's still covered in her own blood, her wounds are completely healed! She turns and swings her legs off the table, and then gets up. Sandy: What happened? I was dying... The End doesn't say anything, he just embraces her tightly, pulling her into the air. She laughs, breathing normally. Sandy: Sky... you're gonna get my blood all over your outfit! The End spins with her a few times, but lets her down. He places his hands over her stomach, and aside from being sticky from blood, there's nothing wrong that a shower won't fix. Cowabunga Connection marvel at what they just saw, but their music is starting and they have to get to the ring to win the Tag Team Titles from Playaz, Inc. As for The End and Sandy Wong, after a scary episode between them... well, they didn't really have any plans, but Sandy suggested that he busy himself and check out Syko and Bertha's wedding later in the night. The End decided against it and left after his own match. That was all well and good, for Shodan keeps doing impersonations of him anyway, and The End really didn't feel like a repeat of his EW debut... And Sho the Oracle watches over everything from the Celestial Plane, nodding his head. Rumiko and Kenshiro are also watching in the orb of energy Sho has created to watch over The End. Sho:'I always said that there would be a hybrid with the potential to be more powerful than even the Celestials. A human heart filled with compassion as well and anger and apathy, and a body endowed with the powers of the Watchers...' Kenshiro:'You mean the Sleeping Dragon?' Rumiko:'Never had that even been remotely near coming true... even some Celestials figured it to be an old rumor or ghost story, a joke at best...' Sho:'The End is the Sleeping Dragon... and it is now awakened...'
