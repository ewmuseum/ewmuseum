Dressed in a black Phat Albert shirt with bagging Fubu jeans down to the fresh so clean Lugz boots. His platinum silver chains jingle around his neck as he softly shakes his head remembering his mother words; 'hold your head up on those rainy days.' A half ten inch thick marble stone with the words, 'Please don't morn my death but only thank the lord I don't have to suffer anymore. Even though I never got to see you my last day of life, I just want to say, I love you.' chiseled in below 'Barbara Johnson-' with her life time date apparently missing as no one quite knows. Guilt buries lil' Thug in emotional disaster as he looks down at his mother's grave site only wishing he was there for her final words. Mud spews up from the ground and runs down the slight hill as a lament song, (I Cry) by Ja Rule ft. lil' Mo, softly plays in the back ground. You see I'm tryin' to pay my damn respects to my damn dead mah fuckin' momma! Get da fuck outa here! Are you some kind of damn idiot?! Ya stupid ass bitch! Damn...' Lil' Thug looks at Buuday as she continues to stand there not knowing what to do. 'Wut da fuck, get da fuck outa here! Damn, iz ya stupid of sumn?!' |/| Buuday quickly turns off the radio. |/| 'Um, uh, I'ma just head back to da limo.' Feeling incredibly stupid Buuday points in the limos direction and makes off towards it. |/| Shaking his head unfathomable about what Buuday's thoughts were, he turns back to his mother's grave. Unable to bring up something as it has been to much time between them, he stares in a trance at the water splash onto of the tombstone and pours along the sides in cords of streams. It's funny how the rain running down a tombstone can remind of such great memories. Memories of your life seem to all by a play of the past as you wake up the next day after next. Then one great memory sticks in mind, the day their dad left them. |/| 'I still remember the tyme that cracka wannabe of a man left us. Left wuz wit nuttin. -Lil' Thug smirks that devilish smirk once more. I never noticed it till now, but it wuz fo' da best.' ::-:: Barbara disregards the letter that summons great pain upon her life and opens the freezer. A gust of slightly cold air spills out of the freezer hitting Barbara in the face cooler her sweat from the awfully damp and hot apartment. She pulls out the only thing that sits there, bacon, and shuts the freezer down. As soon as she shuts the freezer door the letter is knocked away from the freezer and makes a rustling noise as it hits the ground. Barbara with her short, stringy, black, and gray hair tied back and secured in a ponytail by a purple hair band, her sweet brown eyes lock onto the letter. Barbara sets the bacon on the counter and picks up the letter but all she can make out is, 'Dear Barbara.' She pulls her glasses from out her pocket and breathes onto the lens before wiping them with her gown. Then she makes out the rest of the horrific, heart-breaking letter. ::-:: *Note: I don't know how to put this so I'm just going to put it like this. We have been together for ten years. Everything that has came our way we have dealt with it together and got through hard times. I love you with all my heart but I can't stand this anymore. I've been supporting this family since day one but I can't do it anymore. I can't stand to watch you all suffer and your disappointed faces when I come home with a small amount of money or none at all. I'm sorry but I just can't take this anymore. So it pains me to say this but I'm leaving you all... ::-:: She puts her open hand to her chest in shock then drops the letter. Barbara begins to panic only will the words burned into her mind. She crouches down and covers her mouth as tears begin to pure from here eyes. The glasses fall to the floor and crack as she just sits there looking at the letter crying. What once started out as a good blessing day, suddenly turned into a damned horrible day. The tears begin to roll of her check and wet her gown, as she cannot control it. Then a faint sleepy voice is heard approaching the kitchen. ::-:: 'Mommy, where are you.' ::-:: Barbara tries to reply but only silence comes out as her word are replaced with tears or pain. The voice is heard louder and louder as the little boy approaches the kitchen in search for his mom. Then turning the corner, the boy finds his mother heart break and in tears. He runs to her and kneels at her side trying to comfort her in her time of need. ::-:: 'Mommy, what's wrong? Why are you crying? Please mommy stop, you're making me sad too.' ::-:: Tears come to the boy's eye not being able to take watching his mother in pain anymore. He wraps his arms around his mother and they both begin sharing their pain. Just a few seconds later the two other boys in the bed wake up rubbing their eyes from sleepiness. They both throw the thin green cotton blankets off them and hop out of bed thinking it's another exciting day to play. With big grins on their faces one would question why they are so happy seeing has how they have nothing. AJ, the smallest wearing these fuzzy gray cotton night cloths pants with no shirt and the other Trez, the middle child, wearing just plan white underwear. Both with smooth chocolate brown skin that matches their perfect little light brown eyes that light up the room everywhere they go. Seeing as how Trez and AJ got along the best, they were too alike meaning they often get into brother-to-brother fights, which does happen. But as Trez and AJ play carelessly they began to hear cries coming from the kitchen, which is just around the corner since their apartment is so small. Then they stop playing and look towards the kitchen. ::-:: 'Who's crying?' The six-year-old Trez says puzzled 'I don't know. Let’s go see.' ::-:: The bowlegged five year old AJ says as he makes his way to the kitchen with Trez following right behind. But when they turn the corner a huge tall black figure to them, turns the corner shaken up with her eyes red from crying. She looks down on AJ and Trez then kneels down before them. Her hands are shaking and her face is drained from all happiness. AJ and Trez look at one another scared thinking they are in trouble then their mother breaks the heartbreaking news to them. ::-:: 'Now, I don't know how to tell you this but your father walked out on us. He left us and he ain't coming back.' ::-:: Trez and AJ confused at first finally get it and look at their mother who is kneeling their emotionally hurt. ::-:: 'Momma, where did here go? Is he coming back?” ::-:: The mother replies stern. ::-:: 'I told you he isn’t coming back you hear! He's gone and he isn’t coming back any more! He left us with nothing! I should have known when he was gone in the middle of the night but I guess I was too blind to see.' ::-:: Barbara look at her children who stand there not knowing what to do then looks up at the destroyed ceiling and begins to cry while shouting out here pain. ::-:: 'Please lord! No! How could this have happen to my babies and me? Why must I be the one to live such a live? I don't deserve this! Lord, please help me! Bless me with you good will, please Lord help me!' 'I remember it like it wuz yesterday. You lying on da floor with fear crushin' you within'... Ur death stroked da beginnin' of my life. Like genesis, I wuz reborn into da man I am today standin' before you speakin' true as only a true gangsta can.' ::-:: The gang never achieved that goal though. They only got to an alley trying to out run the cops. It seems somebody heard the gun shot and called the police and before you knew it they were surrounding the place. When the cops showed up Marcel and some others got AJ and fled trying to keep his mangled body from cop's eyes. So unfortunately for AJ, he ending up dumped behind some dumpster late at night in an alley just before Marcel and his gang got caught. Three days later, AJ was found by a lady who was on her way to throw something away in the dumpster. He was said to be in critical condition and if was he wasn't found in the next couple of hours, he would have been died. AJ slipped into a coma for two and a half months. Just laying there in a state of unconsciousness his mother finally gave up hope of them returning and let the god lord put her soul at rest. The hospital buried here body January 13th, right on her birthday. She was 67 when she died. On her tombstone it said, 'Please don't morn my death but only thank the lord I don't have to suffer anymore. Even though I never got to see you my last day of life, I just want to say, I love you.' Underneath all that on the smooth black marble tombstone was a red rose. It was a cold after noon when AJ arose from his coma. Light gray clouds covered the sun and a chill breezed throughout. It was approximately 6:13pm when AJ opened his eyes. He lied there wearing nothing underneath of soft white blanket covering his nudity. AJ slightly begins to move muscles at a time feeling his bones pop all over his body. Two doctors walk in the room and see this then immediately tend to him. As they are doing all these things to him, he looks down and notices a red rose just like the one he gave his mother, sitting on his lap. A tear rolls down his cheek, now realizing he has no one in the world that he can depend on. AJ slowly closes his eyes again as the world goes dark for him once more. Time progresses, people change, and he is now out of the hospital. It has been two years since his emotional break down that has left him with depression. The rain purrs from the sky as he stands right in the middle of it. Rain rolls down his body soaking him as he stands there wearing an old dirty torn white shirt that match his torn at the knee jeans that a so faded that are becoming white. His hair all unbraided looking wild as he stands there staring helplessly at the ground. The lights from the liquor store hit AJ from the right casting a shadow on the ground to the left. Thunder rumbles as the skylights up then fades back into darkness. As AJ stands there he hears a ghetto voice that brings back old memories. He slowly turns around and there stands Buuday to his surprise. 'AJ?' She says as her then high heel boots make a thud on the ground as she walks closer. Her black panty hoes rolls all the way up to her thighs wearing a tight black leather skirt that shows off her dangerous curves and her bootylicous butt. Buuday wears no braw as you can her breast bounce up and down with his short red leather shirt. Things were looking good once more until the club burned down. After that they didn't know what they were going to do next until Sheeca's lotto ticket. ::-:: 'The memories stick like glue 'n da back of my mind. Sometimez, I can't sleep at nyte so I drink myself to sleep. Nothin' wrong wit' a lil' Courvoisier here and there... right? I mean, you did it. Wait, I guess ya didn't cuz ya didn't have enough money to support us! My brothaz and I carried you! We were da one's out there bustin' our asses to make you proud of us! Are you proud of me now?' Staccato, lil' Thug smokes his blunt faster than usual. |/| Smoke eases from lil' Thug's nose and mouth as he begins to relax. Flicking his blunt off to the side letting the rain put it out, lil' Thug throws his umbrella and embraces the rain purring down from the crying heavens with open arms. Rain drops stain his cloths as they quickly invade and wet his cloths cooperating as one.
