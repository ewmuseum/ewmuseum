The guitar kicks in, Dee Snider snarls over the rough guitar, and the glass shatters. That's when the door whips open and Harry is standing there with pure ecstasy in his eyes. It's like a drug addict finally getting a fix after a day of not having any. Harry feels the metal coursing through his veins, his favorite band of all time rocking in his brothers room at an ear shattering level. Harry's cowboy boots smash through the glass, as he air guitars his way all over the place] Harry: YEAH, MAN!!! YEAH!! WOOOOOO, MAN!!!!! The sand seems endless as it points to the light tower that has held for so many bodacious nights with bodacious babes. A perfect place for romance, privacy, and Yahtzee. He notices two women near the lighthouse, knocking a beach ball back and forth. It seems like the two are having a most bodacious time with one another. Two beautiful, tanned babes bouncing a ball back and forth, enjoying the warm sun. This is California after all, it doesn't snow here. It's most warm at all times, giving dudes and dudettes the freedom to get out there and enjoy life. Harry can't be smooth talking these babes, it's absolutely impossible! Unless, Harry was able to somehow benefit from Tommy's horrible downfall at Applebees. Maybe Tommy played Twisted Sister TOO LOUD and it sparked something inside The Heavy Metal One, a hidden talent that was always there but just too lazy to use. This is bodacious, but yet it's totally bogus at the same time] Harry: Check out what I found on the beach, man. [The two women giggle like school girls, absolutely in love with Harry already. They had only met him two minutes ago and now they seem like they want to marry him already. This...is weird] Blonde haired woman: Harry is SOOO funny! Oh MY GOD! Brown haired woman: FOR SURE! Plus, he's sexy as hell. I want nothing more than to just rip that Twisted Sister shirt right off his back. [The two women chuckle together, hugging on each other. Harry's eyes bug out wide toward Tommy. Both men are pretty perplexed at this whole situation. Tommy is rejected the day before and now Harry is getting the bodacious babes. Harry is getting what Tommy should be having, but shouldn't he be happy for his bro? Well, he is...but he feels most left out. It's coming at such a horrible time as well.
