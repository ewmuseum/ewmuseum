The Publicity Stunts Part One: 60’s Campness, Carlifting and Adam West

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/6/03 The local mall was buzzing with people. It was hard to move inside because the crowd had come to see their favourite PCW superstar - Lusty Linda. The ex-porn star turned wrestling agent had arranged for a publicity stunt to showcase some of the skills of her clients, in order to gain further publicity for their superhero company and to shed light upon the teams illustrious wrestling career. The mall was filled, mostly, with bald, middle aged men who knew their porn stars and needless to say the two mail members of the Justice Inc team were not impressed. They stood at the centre of the mall, at the edge of the giant show circle. Around the circle was an impenetrable safety barrier, policed by what appeared to be the majority of the police force - the male portion. Linda stood at the centre of the two superheroes, occasionally wandering over to massive, shiny, red pickup truck for a barely legal pose. The truck had two rather large handles on the roof of the van, ready for the massive publicity stunt. “I can’t believe she booked this damn stunt right before our title rematch!” Blind Rage muttered to Warrior. “Just grin, bear it, and make sure you don’t injure yourself. Simple.” Warrior replied. “Easy for you to say.” “Well you’re the one with super human strength…you should see what she has in store for me!” Blind Rage watched as Lusty Linda sexily strode over the bare wooden stand, which had been placed at the other side of the truck, to receive a mic. She then runs her hands smoothly along the handle of the mic, to simulate some kind of mastrurbation. “Hello lad…well Gentlemen and people from all over the world, welcome to the Justice Inc Strange Stunt Segment…” “GET YOUR TITS OUT!” came a voice from the crowd. Linda just giggled. “You know I only do that on a Tuesday…and on a side note, buy my new video in all major porn stores now, only $156.99!” “Somehow…” Blind Rage whispered, “I just knew she’d get that pitch in there somehow!” “Relax,” Warrior replied, “This will be over in a second and then we can get back on the road.” “We shouldn’t be here, assign around, we should be preparing for our match!” Blind Rage added. “…no Clive,” Linda shouts, “the stunt won’t involve me and any type of ping pong ball!” Linda sighed, “Without further ado I present to you…the one…the only…BLIND RAGE!” The crowd looked on with great indifference as the mammoth superhero strode into the centre of the circle. He looked around grimly and began to warm up. “For those of you who aren’t aware of what will be occurring, that includes you Clive, my man Blind Rage here, will lift this red truck up into the air and leave there for a second or two.” There was a brief gasp from a few members of the audience, but the majority of the crowd was still transfixed with Lusty Linda. Blind Rage took a last grim look around the crowd, before taking the handles on top of the car firmly. Because he towered over the car, he had a fairly decent strong base to lift from and consequently he lifted the truck up with considerable ease. However, when the truck was lifted into the air, the truck and Blind Rage were hoisted from the ground and became stuck to the roof. Blind Rage is hanging down a decidedly surprised expression etched upon his face. “I’m kinda stuck up here…” he bellowed. Warrior glanced up at him. “Get down here you sill assed…all you need to do is let go of the damn rails…you’ll survive!” Blind Rage sighed. “I know that dumbass, but I’ve been glued to the handles!” “What in the hell!” Warrior cursed, as he noticed two fairly big headed their way. The two guys pushed past a lot of the crowd and simply threw the clearly inept police officer out of the way, before barging through the security barrier. Both guy’s were wearing jet black jogging costumes with lime green question marks bolded placed at the centre of the chest. The two guys step aside to reveal a much smaller guy, who is wearing a lime-green suit with matching hat, a black shirt and tie, along with some kind of cane. Essentially he looked like a Leprechaun. Warrior recognized the man as The Riddler. “Now that we’ve got Blind Rage out of the way, it looks like we have time to play.” The Riddler chants walking up close to Warrior. “Riddle me this, Riddle me that…” WHAMMO! Warrior cracks the disturbed villain across the jaw with such force, he slides across the floor upon landing. “Shut the fuck up!” Warrior exclaimed, tenderly massaging his fist, “I really hate that guy!” The two massive guys simply look at each other growl then angrily make their way over to Warrior. “Oh…here goes!” Warrior commented, dodging a punch by one of the guys. SOCK! Warrior hit back with a left hand to the guy’s jaw. POW! The other guy dotted Warrior on the jaw, forcing him back against the barrier. WHAM! Warrior kicks the advancing thug in the gut, then PUNCH! An upper cut sends the Riddler thug down. “Whoa!” came a voice from the crowd, “Whoa!” Warrior turned to see the legend known as Adam West leap over the guard rails and confront him up close. “Listen up son…and listen up good.” West said, “You can’t just be goin’ around stealin’ material willy – nilly!” “Huh?” Warrior simply replied. “All the POW!, SOCK!, WHAM’s!” Adam West replied. “They’re all copyrighted bitch!” BIFF! Adam West decks Warrior with a swift right hand. “Prepare to hear from my lawyers!” “Good go damn!” Warrior replied, watching Adam West walk away. “What else can go wrong?” That’s when Warrior noticed the Riddler. The fallen villain retrieves a metallic device from the inside of his breast pocket and laughs to himself. “I’ll still have the last laugh!” With that he pressed the button, which caused the obvious magnet at the top of the mall to switch off, sending Blind Rage and the red van to the ground in a heap. “Ah damn…” Warrior exclaimed. “Blind Rage ain’t gonna be happy about this!”
