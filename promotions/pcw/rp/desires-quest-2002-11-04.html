Desires Quest

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 4th, 2002 Desires QuestVilas Lavagna It wasn’t uncommon for someone like me to be hunted down and brought forth to explain his actions in front of the high council of monks that lived in are capital. For someone like me it was more of an adventure then anything and what really made the hunt so special was the fact that I could simply become the hunted or the hunter. I was always being questioned about everything I looked for, everything I believed in and everything I had come to respect. But one man can’t stand alone in the quiet of the night. He must rise up and above the calling that was brought down onto him and shout out to the world “I AM NOT A SLAVE!” That’s all I ever was to this people. A vast nation filled with wild dreams and beautiful sights and I was trapped waiting for my turn to leave. I was alone and with that I took things my own way. No one cared what anyone thought at the time. It was all what the church said and what people did. If the church said to obey this God everyone would agree. Those who didn’t were punished in ways that I don’t wish to recall. But I must tell my side and let the people know that the prey will move on and survive. No more will the hunter be the only man aloud to live his own life in such grace and luxury. The prey will turn around the ways that the Gods had foreseen and take back what was rightfully theirs. “He has spoken to me and has shown me the way. He will lead us into the future and we shall be free for all the evil the surrounds are lives.” Of course the only thing that brings evil is the passion and desire a group have. Groups of people are ignorant and unfaithful, but one man has the power to control that group and bring them to justice. But also one man can take a group and be the mastermind behind a plot of hate and deception that even fears the great Gods them self. But that is where he enters. The one man with the desire to live on his own, in his way and with his own beliefs. Is it possible for one man to go in one way and never return? Can this one man survive on his own or is his destiny to be prey all his life? I kid you not when I say beware of this souls ability. Life behind this wall is very dark and very cold and one loose stone can bring the wall down, but so does a man that lives for his God instead of himself. Always doing well for others and never being aloud to worship his own side and his own mind. Surely we can all see that the Church is the most powerful cult the world man even see. With the ability to portray imaginable beings they can steal any chance at a families well being right from under them. Using their minds as ponds in a worldwide game of chess these kings of gods have the ability to control thousands if not billions at one time. Who can actually stop them? The one man with the desire to be on his own. The man who throughout history has spoken times and time again about the ability to be your own self. Normally the church will allow anyone to have their own thoughts but when they cross path war and death break out. One man will speak out. One man will survive. With or without help he will become much more then just simple prey. He will become the greatest hunter this world will known and love. Millions versus one are better odds then being apart of the millions. It still comes down to one thought versus one thought. It’s better to have your own ideas then to be a follower. _______________________________________________________________ His eyes squinted as the bright light filled the empty room of a little cottage just outside the capital of Thailand. He shuffled his way into the room staying on the carpet placed at the door so he wouldn’t drop water on the floor. She came in behind him of course with the heavy rain gear but how was he suppose to know if would be this bad tonight. She shocked her umbrella outside and pulls it back into one small bundle and placed it back into its proper holder. She pulled back the hood on her glistening jacket and released her long silk black hair. It fell to her back as she took the rest of the coat off and hung it up to dry. He of course didn’t notice at the moment. His mind was on something far away from this little cottage. “I warned you before about going to the temple,” she spoke softly, sending a sparking glare towards him as he stood by the roaring fire with the flames shouting up and cracking on the wood. He simply shrugged it off and watched the seemingly ends array of light hail from the fire below. He pounded his head hard on the mantel that just seemed to jet out of the wall. The normal few pictures and the little china doll laced the mantel and provided a place for his throbbing head. She softly pushed the words through her lips and whispered, “I know it may be hard on you, but not all the people see you the same as before.” He couldn’t care what she had to say at the moment. He grabbed one of her dolls and held it in his hand ever so slightly tilting it into the light of the fire. The light caught the small figure and sent a glow through the inside. She slowly walked up and gave him a little startle. She grabbed the small doll and looked at it in her own hand. She placed a towel around hid shoulders and took his jacket. “Your lucky they didn’t catch you tonight. They warned you before about disturbing the temple,” she continued to speak as she hung his coat with the others. She gracefully sat into one of her chairs and proceeded to glaze at her book still sitting on her coffee table. She grabbed the book in her left hand and continued on from were she left off before. The only light was coming from the fire and he was blocking it with his body. But she wasn’t reading anyway, but only waiting for him to start spilling hi story on the floor. “There was nothing you could have done their today. In fact you only made things that much worse for yourself,” she voiced as her eyes grazed the pages looking for the place of last halt. He finally turned around and looked at her as she read her book, not even paying attention to him or the depression mood he was in and asked, “What should have I done?” She calmly looked away from her book and into his eyes and simply added, “nothing.” She calmly went back to her book as he stood still and watches her ignore him again. He turned around and looked back into the roaring fire. The heat poured the sweat from his face and it continued to run down his shirt. She calmly read on not ever looking what kind of mood he would be in. He wiped his face and threw the liquid into the fire, sparking a few flames that rose to his face, scorching the air that was beneath him. “It wasn’t my fault this happen. For once I had nothing to do with it,” he said with a sort of worry in his voice. This caught her attention. Denial was never apart of his mental state so he wouldn’t lie about this. She placed her book down and walked over to him, putting her arm on his shoulder. The two gazed into the fire and looked at each other through blinding eyes. “What really did happen at the temple,” she asked as a look of concern flashed over her pale white skin. He looked at her almost in tears and took her hand and gently pulled her away from the flames. “Sit down and I will explain what truly happen.”
