THE CHEWBACCA DEFENCE (PWA vs. P.W.A part 2)

12 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 - 11 - 02 Prologue: After achieving what many regard as 'The greatest successful Tag Team title defence in history' Gaz Green emerged home in his native pad of Las Vegas Nevada only to face yet another uphill struggle. From the inhumane stairs challenge put forth by business associate Natalia Miyagawa after a series of bickering and squabbling, the Jester was in-line for more bad news. Escalading for a number of weeks and confirmed in a writing was that Primetime Wrestling Academy were willing to press on with legal action over the initial use, P.W.A. It appears the Academy aren't thrilled at the idea of the initials being associated with Pranks With Attitude and have filed a lawsuit. However the story has taken a twist as the dynamic duo of Nascosta Ireul Nerezza and Gaz 'The Jester' Green counter-sue on the grounds that the Academy has had free publicity from the teams growth and success. The scene opened with the luxurious apartment set in the heart of Las Vegas Nevada and more importantly the property of Gaz Green. Again nothing appeared out of place, Natalia Miyagawa sits peering over to her agent with interest as Gaz initiates the teams tactical counter-strike. 'Yeah that'll be great squire!' murmured the Jester as he spoke over the telephone. Responding to the opposite line with a series of nods as both parties immersed themselves in conversation, a conversation centred on that of this recent obstacle concerning the identities of the PCW tag team and that of the Academy. 'Thanks again - Speak more later - Goodbye!' concluded the Emerald Menace ending the call. Failing to lower the receiver, Gaz again bashed at the keypad to the confused glares of Natalia, obviously searching for answers she politely asked. 'What are you doing?!?' 'Ringing Nerezza. I've just booked us an appointment with Johnny Cockran and funny enough he's a big fan of Pranks With Attitude so he's thrown us to the front of the queue!' 'Hello, Nerezza-... Don't speak, just listen. I've just booked us an appointment with Johnny Cockran over this P.W.A issue with the Academy and he assures me that we have a good case. Anyway as much as I'd love to stop and chat, you have the task of swinging by in that heap of-...' the concluding Gaz was momentarily interrupted upon by the behemoth, possibly over the reference towards his car. The thunderous response at the Jester's cheap pop caused Gaz to remove the receiver and place it arms length. A few minutes of ranting and again the line silenced allowing the Conniving Comic to finish what was started by concluding this conversation. 'Yeah I know it’s a vintage automobile, classic even, the point is you have an hour to get over here!' Crashing down the telephone receiver whilst Nerezza continued to murmur, the current collective of P.W.A consisting of Gaz 'The Jester' Green and the picture of beauty Natalia Miyagawa set hotfooted from the Jester's party pad and across to the elevator. With the horizontal doors closing the camera watched as the numbers descended and the sounding of 'Dings' mounted, finally coming to rest on the lobby, fifteen floors below. Time has long expired since we last saw Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa. It appears as though Nerezza has collected his cargo and the trio of Primetime employee's have made the daunting journey across town to the offices of the renowned 'Johnny Cockran'. Sitting in a grim waiting room with the strong odour of wood, most likely from the various furnishings was the devious duo of Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Nerezza, alongside that of their recent acquisition Natalia Miyagawa. Almost on the brink of losing their sanity, well on the verge of boredom they sat twiddling their thumbs, waiting a date with the same man who sold the innocence of O J Simpson. Tapping away freely on a keyboard, eyes fixated on the monitor, a young receptionist continued to work at pace. With a high pitch beep from the office's inter-com system, the clerk pressed a button and the distinctive voice of the lawyer filtered over. 'SEND IN P.W.A's MR. GREEN AND MR. NEREZZA, PLEASE~!' Looking up with a smile and the flirtatious playing of hair the receptionist politely asked the group to make their way to the end of the corridor, which ominously stretched to their right. 'Mr. Cockran will see you now! Along the corridor - Second door in, on the right-hand side-....' 'Cheers sweet cheeks!!!' retorted the lady killing Gareth Green with a cheeky smile, showcasing his pearly white teeth and an accompanying wink, which was caught by all in attendance. Dragged reluctantly by the mountainous goliath of satanic origin Nerezza, the triple threat of Gaz Green, Nerezza and Natalia Miyagawa homed in on the office of their representing lawyer. With long strides and almost re-enacting Reservoir Dogs in the process the trio stall abruptly in front of the door, before gently tapping for entry. 'Please - Come in!' murmured a voice from behind the two inches of wooden door, an invitation taken up by the trio of PCW superstars as they descended upon the premises. On entry they witnessed the infamous Johnny Cockran slouched in a leather reclined chair, back turned he swung around to greet his newly acquired clients with a warm welcoming smile. 'Hello-.... Please, take a seat!' Dropping over five hundred pounds of weight into three separate chairs the conversation kicked off almost immediately as Johnny Cockran displayed exactly why people regard him as the best in this particular field. 'As I understand Primetime Wrestling Academy who you represented approximately three months ago have filed a law suit against you on the grounds of copyright infringement' injected the budding defence lawyer to the impressed glares of those listening. 'EXACTLY~!' hissed the Angel Of Fear Nerezza. 'Well as we explained earlier-... It would be wise to counter-sue the Academy on the grounds of using you over this three-month time scale as free publicity within the wrestling circuit. One hitch is that we would have to prove in court that student applications have risen since your departure, information which isn't usually made public!' continued the African-American, fingering through his excessive notes and documentation. 'So how long can this take squire!' asked the Lord Of Laughter. 'We should be talking days and hopefully this'll be the case. However for the time being I shall ask you to re-evaluate your name-....' said Johnny before a harsh intrusion by the raging vampire Nerezza. 'Re-evaluate? You mean change our name?!?' 'Not exactly - The Academy have made the mistake of filing this against 'Pranks With Attitude' and haven't stated the P.W.A infringement so until we can gather our resources I suggest playing with these three initials!' replied the clever businessman, a temporary resolve until Pranks With Attitude can counter-sue the Academy, tactically known as a swerve. 'So we could basically call ourselves-... People With Arthritis-...' questioned the petite Russian drawing some obscure glances, notably at the name mentioned. 'Hmm-... Well-... Yes, you could use a combination of names from the letters of P.W.A and providing it wasn't Pranks With Attitude then you will not be liable. Once the information comes to light on the student applications then we could resolve this matter in your favour, otherwise it's Operation SW!!!' said Johnny Cockran, whispering the latter half of his comments, especially the reference to Operation SW. 'Operation SW?' asked the inquisitive Jester, Gaz Green. 'Operation Star Wars-... The Chewbacca defence-... Very top secret' answered the lawyer, sealing his mouth with a zip effect. 'Well I shall inform you when this information has reached my hand but until then I suggest taking my advice and sitting tight' concluded the former O J Simpson defendant. With a series of handshakes and departing gestures the trio left satisfied that this minor infringement maybe settled. Smiling and looking rather jovial the door slammed shut with a heavy thud, closing another chapter of the on-going Pranks With Attitude saga. 'Till next time-....'
