Celebrity Allotments #2: Can I Dig It, Sucka?

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st May 2002 We return to the only patch of shrub land in the middle of Los Angeles. The Celebrity Allotment welcomes one and all as it usually does. Syko stands there minding his own plot... well actually he’s sitting on some plastic garden furniture while eating a pizza, but hey, that probably does come under “minding” in some sort of regard considering that minding usually means watch but staying well out of it. He sits there while Miyagawa wearing one of his latest t-shirts keeps digging. It’s looking pretty good now if you are into allotments... the shed is up and free standing with a little plastic greenhouse stapled to the side of it. There are defiantly rows of unknown plants starting to grow and Miyagawa is wandering along them with two watering cans (one in each hand) watering the row right now as I very well speak. One is a red can, and the other is green... and as Miyagawa explained earlier it’s that way so that when he gets some weed killer later on, he can only use the red one in the weed killer so that when he waters plants that he doesn’t want to get the weed killer, such as the potatoes, then he just doesn’t use the red one at all on them. Simple, eh? Syko shoves another slice of pizza into his mouth while sitting there watching Miyagawa working. He sits there in one of his t-shirts... actually it’s his only t-shirt with “Enter the Goblin” on the front... and I think you can guess what’s on the back. He appears to have a thought, which is always nice. Syko: Hey Miya... when are we actually going to make a profit out of this, we don’t actually seem to be getting many customers or anything. We should get a big sign... like one of those billboard posters... maybe we could get adverts in Times Square... or perhaps we could sp... No, you, this is your stupid idea... you could sponsor Supremacy with this. Miyagawa grins over at the Goblin as the water finally runs out in the watering cans. He wanders over towards the white plastic table that Syko is sitting by on the white plastic chair and sits down on another equally plastic and equally white chair also next to the table. Miyagawa: Nice idea, but considering that it would require me throwing a lot of money at the PPV just to hear Jeff Marx shill my garden veggies, I think I’ll pass. Syko: Even if Marx said something like... MIYAGAWA’S GROWING CARROTS~!!! CARROTS~!!!! CARROTS I SAY~!!! Syko grins at his Jeff Marx impression... they funny thing is that his voice never changed which probably means that Syko thinks that he naturally sounds just like Jeff Marx... which is not only disturbing but a morbidly disgusting image in my mind of Jeff Marx and Syko the Goblin being somehow related. God I need my brain washed now. Miyagawa hears a noise behind him and glances over his shoulder at the small car parking area of the allotment... well I say car parking area, it isn’t really... it’s just the part near the gate. A black car pulls up there and out steps... Booker T.Booker is dressing in a black shirt and a pair of black jeans which is a nice change from seeing him in either a pair of black or a pair of white underpants... and thank god Goldust isn’t with him... it’d take Miyagawa and Syko a good few months to clear all the gold crap off the Allotment. Syko: What’s he doing here? Miyagawa: Didn’t you know? Booker and I go way back... I helped him dig out the space for his swimming pool. Although I kinda panicked when we found Picasso the Ninja Turtle in a fridge about six feet down in his garden... still... the turtle did maim the Big Boss Man, so it’s always good. Syko: Wasn’t that when you went to the Wrestling Asylum and Steve got ravaged by old women? Miyagawa sighs out loud for a second. Miyagawa: Ah... the good old days. I remember back then... when Hulk Hogan thought he was Kurt Angle... when Marty Jennety walked around claiming he was already dead... ah... those were good days. Booker T makes his way over to the little conference with the plastic table and chairs. He stands next to the duo on their little garden chairs. Booker: Yo suckas... what’s up? Syko: Sucka? Sucka? I’m no Sucka! Booker: Whoa sucka, calm down. Miyagawa: Syko, he calls everyone a sucka. Live with it. Syko: But I’m not a Sucka. I’ll prove that to everyone. No one will call me a sucka... or a Psycho... damn that Xavier Kannon goon. I’ve been away practicing my wrestling and now I’m ready to come back and win at a pay per view... yes that’s right... win. Booker: Win at a Pay Per View? Damn sucka, I haven’t done that for ages. Miyagawa: Well I’m about fifty-fifty wins and losses at pay per views, this time last year thought it was quite different. I guess I can thank Kensuke Suzajobber for getting my wins-loss ratio back to level standards. Booker: Ok suckas, less talking about you, more talking about me. Celebrity Allotment, suckas. Miyagawa: Ok Booker, its two hundred bucks and you can grow whatever you want? Booker: Can I can dig it, sucka? Miyagawa and Syko grin as they feel their wallets expand all of a sudden. Miyagawa: Well you have to supply your own tools, but yes, you can dig it... sucka. Booker: Sucka? Sucka? Tell me you didn’t just say that... tell me you didn’t just say that. Syko interrupts with a retort. Syko: You didn’t just say that. Booker: Thanks sucka, I thought I didn’t... I wish people would take this here sucka some more literally more often, the amount of suckas who think I’m referring to them. Syko: Are you sure that your not related to Mr. T. Booker: Only people I dislike call me Mr.T, you can call me Booker, sucka. Syko: Cool... thanks Booker, sucka. Miyagawa stares at Syko as if to say “stop trying to piss off our first customer, you silly goblin”. Booker: Ok, so suckas will you sort everything out that I have to fill out? Miyagawa: Yeah, just come back in a couple of days and you can pick a plot. Miyagawa thumbs towards the large patch of barren wasteland in the middle of LA. Although the duo must have put some effort in because you now notice that they’ve even hammered little plaques with numbers on them into the ground at certain intervals. Booker: Well suckas, that will be a pleasure. Hey suckas, what’s that? Booker points off in the distance and both Syko and Miyagawa turn their heads... at which point Booker grabs the last slice of Syko’s pizza and shoves it into his mouth before turning and running back towards his car. Syko and Miyagawa turn back and see Booker getting into his car. Miyagawa: Nice bloke. Syko goes to pick up the last piece and then sees that it’s vanished. Syko: BOOKER T... I’M GOING TO KILL YOU!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Miyagawa frowns. Miyagawa: Nice Mexi impression. I’d stop before that catches on. Syko: Bah. Miyagawa: Phantom Booker. Syko: It’s not fair, I can’t do anything right. Miyagawa: And that’s Darkstar. You need your own persona, you really do. Miyagawa grins in an evil manner towards his friend, the goblin. Miyagawa: Where’s those bottles of Evian. Syko now has a chance to grin back at Miyagawa in an evil manner. Syko: I drank them. Miyagawa: You drank them? Now what am I going to water the allotment with? And with that we leave the duo for the time being in their shrub land in central L.A... When will Miyagawa admit this is his retiring plan? When will Syko get a girl? When will Booker T get a decent gimmick and stop tagging with homosexuals? All these and more questions will be answered in the next intriguing episode of... Celebrity Allotments Episode 2: The Turnip Wars.
