Why Me?

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/18/03 Tick Tock Tick Tock Tick Tock The only sound to be heard throughout the house is the clock resting on the kitchen wall. Around the kitchen table the I am surrounded by the four people I love most, yet instead of the usual commotion there is complete and utter silence as the room fills with tension. We all stare at each other, our faces emotionless until finally the oldest child Alex, breaks the silence. “Tessa are we going to have to live with our mom?” He questions as he wraps his fork around the spaghetti that sits on his plate. Instead of picking up and bringing it to his mouth though he twirls it around his fork nervously and stares at his food, having no appetite because of the thoughts lingering inside his young mind. “I don’t know Alex.” I sighed as I looked down at the plate that sat before me as I was overcome by the same feeling that was terrorizing Alex and forced to drop my fork, unable to look Alex in the eye knowing that if I did he would be able to see that I was expecting the worst. “Take you away? What are you talking about? Silly boy you have not seen her in ages, why would you live with her?” Chris questioned from his seat beside me, looking up from his plate and staring over at Alex who still had not taken a bite from his plate. “She was here yesterday!” Alex snapped, obviously feeling rather upset that Chris was not there to witness it and perhaps feeling that Chris should have been there for him if Chris really cared for him. “What?” Chris shouted out as his fork fell from the grasp of his hand in shock. He must have realized what this could mean for him and I as far as the kids were concerned, hopefully it would not bother him as much as it did me. “Don’t worry about it Chris, I’ve got it handled.” I said to Chris as my appetite returned causing me to jump into the plate of food before me with both fork and knife. “No, I want to know what’s going on.” Chris demanded with a stern look, not blinking an eye as if he would not do anything until he got the answer that he had wanted and felt he deserved. “We’ll talk about it later Chris.” I said, standing up from my seat and walking off in the direction of the living room to get away from the conversation I had wanted to stay far from. The only thing I could think to myself is that this couldn’t come at a worse time. I had a match with Lacuna on Friday, and with P.W.A, around god only knew how bad it could become, and now this. It seemed that the world was turning its back at me all at once and the only lifesaver I had was the PCW Tag Title I held onto. “Tessa please….” I blocked out the rest of what he was saying from my mind. I loved Chris to death, but at the moment I wanted to keep my mind far away from thoughts of losing two of the people who meant the most to me considering I had helped raise them for the past six of their lives and yet it stood a chance that they could be taken away from me just like that by a person who had abandoned them in their time of need. Why me? That one question ran though my mind as I flopped down on the couch and let my mind go to work. It made no sense to me. How could someone as sweet as me have to go through such shit after all I’ve done for others? Why would it make sense to take away two children who I have practically helped raise on my own in the past six months? Why did I have to be the victim of P.W.A.’s continual pranking? And most of all why did this all have to happen at once? Instead of searching for answers that would satisfy my mind I thought of something else. I thought about the way Chris had been acting ever since P.W.A. had crashed our wedding this past Sunday. It had brought back a Chris I had hoped never to see again. It had brought back a violent and careless Chris. No longer was caring and loving for the children and I through cuddling and hugs a first priority, instead it was showing his care by bashing someone’s head in with a steel chair. It was a side of Chris I had never hoped to see again. Just seeing the case containing his own personalized steel chair brought chills down my side. If he had used that chair against me before surely he wouldn’t question to do it again, or use it in a much worse manner on someone else, which made me question on whether or not I should tell him the truth about how Christa and Alex’s mom had come to take them back and would stop at no lengths to get what she wanted, or if I should just tell him she just came to visit. Letting out a stressful sigh I sunk deeper into the couch as my eyes closed and the stress sunk deeper into both my heart and mind. What was I to do? I had absolutely no clue.
