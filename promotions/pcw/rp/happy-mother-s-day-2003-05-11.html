Happy Mother's Day!

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/11/03 (O.O.C. Note- Props to Gaz for giving me the idea for this roleplay =D) When I woke up this morning I saw quite the unexpected sight. Beside me on the table beside my picture lay a vase filled with roses. “Aww how sweet, someone thinks I’m special.” I thought to myself as I looked over the flowers and reached over to the card that was tucked inside of them. Sliding my finger under the slight opening and peeled away the closing of the envelope and pulled out the card which was neatly tucked inside it. Maybe… My mind thought back to Chris and the great times that we spent together. Maybe he has finally found his way back to me and things could finally go back to the way they were, the way I liked them. It was a time when it seemed no matter what kind of burdens fell upon us we would always rise to the top and overcome them, but that time was over now and now I was on my own and surely enough for Chris to come back would be asking too much and just as I had expected the outcome of the card showed that as it read “Happy Mother’s Day”. “Oh Evan you’re such a sweetheart.” I whispered to the card since Evan was not present. Then I suddenly remembered that I had not gotten my own mother a mother’s day gift. But then again did she really deserve one for all the hell she had put me through in this past year alone? Certainly it didn’t seem that way to me, after all who would buy a gift for someone who constantly put them down and always told them that they were not trying hard enough, but at the same time I had my good conscious telling me that she was my mother and just think of what she must have went through to get me on this earth. I closed my eyes figuring that it would help me concentrate better but instead it brought me to a cartoon like atmosphere that could only be created through a daydream. “What the hell?” I muttered to myself wondering what on earth was going on. I stood there in a field of flowers as the sun beat down on me. It seemed like heaven but I knew it was too good to be real. As I took a seat on the grassy domain I found myself sinking even more into an unreality as two creatures nested themselves on my shoulders. One of them was gowned in a white robe with a halo floating above her head and looked exactly like a miniature version of myself while the other was quite the opposite and donned a forest green suit with his green hair spiked up and quite mischievous grin settled on his lips. “Gaz?” I questioned the creature who looked quite similar to the man whose last name I now shared because of a prank that had gone quite wrong. “No no no sweet cheeks you’ve got it quite wrong. You see my name is Mini Gaz.” He announced quite proudly with broadened shoulders and brightened Emerald eyes that gave him a slight tone of innocence. “Give me a break.” The smaller version of me replied to the creature who called himself Mini Gaz. “You are both no different. He’s a trouble maker, you’re a trouble maker. He’s quite hated, you’re quite hated….” She continued on with her comparisons as I turned my attention away from her pretty much able to predict all that she would say. My interest was instead focused on the smaller version of Gaz who was now tinkering with the hand buzzer attached to his hand. Seeing that he was not doing anything too interesting I then reverted my attention back to the field around me as I went back to the thought that had brought her here in the first place. “Does my mom really need a mother’s day gift?” I sighed as I searched for the answer to my questioned. The question must have triggered an alarm off in the head of both creatures because within a second both of them stopped what they were doing and scurried their way up my shoulder towards my ears, Mini Tessa on the right one and Mini Gaz on the left. “Of course she does!” The smaller version of me exclaimed as if it were sinful not to listen to her and side with not getting my mother a gift. “Oh she does, does she?” The smaller jester commented nearly heaving over in laughter. “Yes she does.” Mini Tessa replied to Gaz’s fit of laughter in a serious tone, not seeming to like the way Gaz was joking around about the situation. “And why is that?” the smaller Gaz inquired “Since when does someone buy a gift for someone who puts them on guilt trips and make them feel like absolute crap?” he argued his point back at Tessa who seemed to have blocked out all the bad things this woman has done. “BECAUSE SHE’S HER MOTHER!” The little Tessa shouted back at Gaz, forgetting that she was right next to my ear therefore slightly deafening me in that one ear which I wasn’t too pleased about. “So?” Was the simple reply that Gaz came up with, obviously not caring who and what this woman had done. He was simply here to point out the bad in her perhaps because he could relate well to it. “So there.” Mini Tessa pouted like a child, obviously frustrated with having to argue with a man that she couldn’t stand. It had reminded me a great deal of myself when dealing with the actual Gaz this past Friday. “You know I think I have an idea that will keep you both satisfied.” I replied with precarious grin crossing my face that worried the both of them as I dissolved from their reality back into my own world back at home. -A few hours later- “Tessa can you hurry up please?” My mother shouted from the bottom of the stairs calling up to me inside of my bedroom where I was getting together the gift that I decided to give to her to show her how much I appreciated her. “Almost done mom.” I replied as I grabbed the gift that was wrapped up inside of a box that was shaped for carrying an outfit or two from a clothing store nearby. “This better be a good present for all the crap you’ve put me through.” She snapped, quite obviously becoming impatient with all the waiting. “Oh trust me mom, you’ll love it.” I beamed as I dashed down the stairs with the present tucked safely inside my arms. Despite the smile across my face my mother simply scowled at me and snatched the present from my grasp the moment I reached the bottom of the stairs. “Thank you.” She replied quite unpleasantly not bothering to acknowledge my generosity with a hug or anything. “Happy Mother’s Day.” I proclaimed in a cheery voice before taking time to reach out and embrace her in a loving hug. She simply pulled away and scampered off to the couch in the living room with her present. I followed behind as she began to eagerly tear her way through the paper like an animal tearing apart its prey. “I hope you like it.” I said as I watched on from the living room doorway. “I’m sure I….. TESSA YOU LITTLE!!!!!” She jumped up from her seat shrieking in my direction as she jumped up from her seat on the couch and tossed the box filled with dead flowers and insects to the floor. “I love you too mom.” I said sarcastically before breaking into a fit of giggles. I made a dash for the stairs and up towards my room so that I could avoid the series of screams in my direction that would surely follow once she got a hold of me, which was if she got a hold of me. Once inside my room I jumped onto the bed which still had yet to be made feeling quite satisfied with my actions. Call it stress relief if you must, but to me this was nothing but revenge. If she wanted to continue to hound me about how bad of a child I supposedly was then she could do so, but that would mean only more rebellion on my behalf. “You know Gaz I have to give you some credit; you are quite a crafty jester.” I said to the image of Gaz that was floating in my mind to remind me that if it wasn’t for the smaller version of him than I probably wouldn’t have gone through with such a great idea. “Tessa I can’t believe you…” I heard a voice inside my mind say before the image of myself all dressed up in white robes popped into my mind once more. “You do know she’s your only mother don’t you?”“I do realize this.” I replied to the angelic version of myself only feeling a slight tinge of guilt. “Then why did you do it?” The smaller Tessa questioned me looking at me as if I had committed crime with her baby blue puppy eyes standing out the most which were telling me how disappointed she was in my actions. “It’s quite simple; I needed to get into the mind of a jester before Friday.” I replied with an answer that didn’t seem to be too reasonable to anyone but myself. “But Tessa you only have the Green last name, you’re not actually a Green!” My good conscious argued back trying to get me to see how incredibly evil my actions were in her eyes. “Oh but you don’t understand, I am a Green and I better get used to it if I want to win this Friday otherwise I’ll just end up with my face drilled into the mat once more and quite frankly I don’t know about you but I don’t want that happening yet again.” I replied not the least bit concerned with the smaller Tessa’s opinion. “Fine then, but if you end up getting your face drilled into the mat because you were stupid enough to trust him then that’s your fault.” My conscious retorted pretty much hinting to me that if I got my ass kicked it was going to be my fault no matter what. “Who said I trust him?” I argued back, wanting to make sure it was clear that I was not allied with Gaz Green in the least bit; I was simply just trying to play on his level so that I may walk away with the win I wanted on Friday. “Oh trust me, if you keep this up pretty soon you’ll find yourself having no problem with the Green last name at all. But my work here is done so take care of yourself because if you make a mistake your pretty much on your own now.” The fairy type creature said before disappearing into thin air after that reminded that served more as a warning. “Great job sweet cheeks!” The miniature Gaz proclaimed, taking the good fairy’s place on my shoulder. “Don’t let her words get to you she has no clue what she is talking about.” He said in reference to his nemesis, the miniature Tessa. “Thanks.” I replied not all too sure as to what I should be saying to him considering he was nothing more than a smaller version of the man I loathed yet was forced to tag with in the ring on Friday. “Don’t worry you’ll do a great job representing the Green name on Tuesday and Friday as long as you keep acts like that up.” He said as he patted me on the shoulder before disappearing back into thin air. “Ugh Green.” I said to myself not too happy with the last line of the smaller jester, sure I wanted to win but did I really want that win to be credited to me as a Green? I let the thought linger inside my mind for a few moments before sitting up so that I may go downstairs and get myself something to eat since I had yet to eat that day. I realized that as long as I was a Green I would have to learn to accept it and that was all there was to it even if I wanted out of this marriage more than an inmate wanted freed from his cell in jail.
