The Tape - part II

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/03 The loud smashing of plastic on plastic hammered away onto a wooden desk and spilled all over the floor. Forming a small mountain on the desk was a pile of videotapes… specifically, the very same tapes that had been sent out not so long ago from the same factory the desk sat in. Sitting behind the desk and staring out from behind a purple mask of hair, Nerezza’s red eyes examined the top one. “All the same… all ignorant…” Nerezza wheezed with disgust. “You’re really going to do it, aren’t you…?” Gaz shuddered, looking over his partner’s shoulder. “Surely this isn’t the answer, Squire, I mean, genocide in the name of a pet peeve?” Nerezza rose out of his seat, extending an arm out and smashing it through the plaster of the office, sending a spray of white chunks over the floor. “If you had an itch, would you not scratch it? If an insect stood in your path, would you not crush it? If something irritated you, would you not deal with it?!” snapped Nerezza, bringing his arm back and dusting the white power off his glove. “I suppose, but this is…” Gaz began. “This is me scratching my itch, and… incredible…” Nerezza gasped, brushing his hair from out of his eyes and revealing his stretched jaw. Parting his lips in amazement, the vampire showed a row of pointed teeth. “Someone… obeyed… heh. It seems, Gaz, that one shall be spared.” “Thankyou Rezz, honestly, at least one person’s going to come out of this alive…” Gaz sighed in relief, looking at the tape. “Wait… that one’s not…” “But it was… I can tell. One shall be spared. The others… heeheeheehee….” Nerezza began to laugh, his smile splitting across his face completely, literally from ear to ear. He turned to Gaz and showed him a face full of glee and teeth. “Everyone will suffer.” ~ Night time had fallen on the house that the twins, Donna and Darren Ray lived in. From Donna’s room, the sounds of “Rock Is Dead” leak out into the hallway while Xavier Kannon made his entrance on one of her many PPV tapes. In Darren’s room, the walls were lit up with the light of the TV screen as he played on a Basketball videogame. Just a normal night. “Okay kids, lights out!” came a voice from downstairs. “Yes, dad!” came the chorus in unison. The usual routine began then, with the lights going out almost synchronized. Then a shout rang out from Donna’s room, a cry of shock. The lights went back on and Donna Darren running from his room. Donna’s hair was slightly spiked and steam could be seen rising from her and her bed. “What happened?” Darren asked, frightened. Donna didn’t say a word as she reached for her glasses, only pointing at her bed. Laying on the mattress was a small, grey circular object, polished like metal. On closer inspection by the twins, it turned out to be… a hand buzzer, with the area around it wet. A few leaves and specks of dirt and gravel sat around it. “Whoa, don’t look at me, I didn’t put that there… I’ve been playing all night!” Darren stammered in defense. Donna looked at him, still silent, then ran out of her room and across the landing, into his. Darren followed behind, puzzled. When they both reached his room, Donna slowly reached out with a trembling hand and whipped off the blanket on Darren’s bed. Sitting beneath the covers was the same object, awaiting its unsuspecting victim in a small, dirty puddle. Day 5 had ended very, very badly. ~Day 6~ “…and with the score tied, both team have a lot to play for in the coming games. Now over to you, Nicole.” “Thanks, Alex. In this week’s wacky news, are aliens beaming random objects into homes across the USA? That’s the opinion of several people in the small town of Ricetown, South Carolina. Locals have reported the objects, which seem unconnected, appearing in their homes with no apparent reasoning behind them. Either we’re witnessing the work of reverse cat-burglar or those things that people left alone for a few seconds that ran away are having guilt trips and coming back. For SWBO news, I’m Nicole Flowers.” The TV screen faded to one single dot as the twins looked at each other in disbelief. “It’s happening everywhere.” Darren grimly admitted. “And it’s getting worse.” Donna sighed. “First all that muddy water, then the hand buzzers, then the tropical fish started bleeding… it’s like some kind of apocalyptic sign.” “Maybe some kind of warning…” Darren suggested, scratching his head. “Like, do something or else.” “Or else…” Donna thought aloud, trying to remember where she had seen the phrase before. And then it struck her. Donna jumped off the sofa and pelted up the stairs as fast as she could, bursting into her room and dashing to her bin. Among the screwed up pieces of paper and food wrappers, she found the slip of paper that came with the Pranks With Attitude video. Turning it over in her hands, she found something even more odd. The paper was completely blank, with no ink on it other than the places to fill in details. “…blank…” Suddenly, a mad laugh came from nowhere behind her. Donna yelled out loud and spun around, holding her hands protectively in front of her face and screwing her eyes shut. After maybe a minute, she slowly opened them, expecting to see a ghost in front of her. Instead, a Gaz Green figure lay on his back with the button on his back pressed in, activating his laugh. She breathed a sigh of relief and made her way to the shelf, picking up the fallen toy and placing him back on his feet. “Gaz, you have to learn to stand up…” She whispered to the figure. “Sure thing, sweet-cheeks.” It replied. Donna dropped it like a hot brick, yelping. Before she had a chance to do anything else, a sudden battle cry boomed from behind her as the entire shelf full of figures came to life, throwing themselves at her. Donna was knocked off her feet as hoards of PCW figures bombarded her, clinging to her clothes and weighing her down. She tried to get up, but found herself pinned to her floor by all of her figures. All hope seemed lost as the manic laughter of Gaz Green rang out again, this time coming from straight in front of her as he stood on her chin. Frozen with fear, he eyes bulged wide as the figure’s face remained still, but the rest of the body moved. “Die laughing, sweet-cheeks!” the voice giggled as Gaz’s plastic avatar leaned over her face, then spread her lips apart and stepped inside her mouth, laughing all the way. Donna screamed as the toy slid into her jaws, then sat up suddenly, covered in sweat. All around her was the darkness of her room. She was sat in her bed, breathing rapidly and shaking. Suddenly, she clamped her hand over her mouth and poked around the inside with her tongue, feeling for some sort of trace of the plastic intruder. “…” Spinning her head over to her left, she took a glance at the clock. 03:41 “Day 7…” she dryly realized.
