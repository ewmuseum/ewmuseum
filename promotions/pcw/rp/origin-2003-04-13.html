Date: 13th April 2003 Origins, Genesis, the Beginning call it what you will but everyone gracing the Wrestle Online community and more importantly Primetime Championship Wrestling had an agenda for being there. Many claim that they wish to one day adorn World Heavyweight gold whilst others merely wish to etch their names into record books and Hall Of Fames but this changes from each superstar to the next. The world of professional wrestling isn’t just the bright lights and living the celebrity life for this would be the average day to a Las Vegas native but behind the lavish is a grimmer reality. The constant travelling hits you like narcolepsy yet you’re ordered to perform and even at half-mast your expected to have a surge of adrenaline for that twenty minutes of in-ring presence. Granted your families are very well looked after financially and even those talent less individuals who defied the odds and climbed from the after birth bucket would generate a salary much better than any dead end job they would endeavoured anyhow. However does a salary fill in the void of missing your child’s first word or that day when they first begin school? With all that said none of the above really entails the Jester for he has neither child nor wife. Many would argue that the green tailored Jester is actually a married man but the marriage is nothing more than a sham, a charade that allowed the team of Pranks With Attitude to capitalise. Now at Spring Fling the combination of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor Green will run the gauntlet against arguably the most dominant tag team of the Third Age in Pranks With Attitude. As many would know the former four time PCW Tag Team Champions haven’t always been dirty and underhanded but fan favourites and were once adored and admired. A pairing that was unknown during their Primetime Wrestling Academy era to a talked about unit of New True Internet Championship Wrestling and finally the cheered Jester and Vampire collective to enter Primetime Championship Wrestling. The rest as they say is history as greed seeped in and now we have the conceited former Champions who remain loyal to only two entities, the Tag Team Championship and former Front Office stable mate and PCW alpha male Xavier Kannon. Before fame, fortune and fate there isn’t a great deal recorded about either the Jester or his Vampire accomplice but digging deep I plan to share the Genesis of Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green and unveil his path from straight ‘A’ student to Evil Super Genius on the brink of etching his name in stone. Ever so typical but the man brought into this world as Gareth Green was your normal High School student. During them days there wasn’t any dyed green hair or green tailored business suits and during this era there was no clear cut that the tormented student would turn the tide and become the tormentor. Actually the Emerald Menace was the black sheep, the ugly duckling always wanting to be the swan but being the butt of everybody’s jokes opportunities were fading fast. Then during the mid eighties the popularity of wrestling surged and like many of his age he soon began taking a keen interest. One would argue that these were his initial steps to stardom? Now there’s a difference from watching wrestling to actually performing, theory into practise but not disheartened by his tormentors the young student prepared both physically and mentally for his calling. Unless his number was ex-directory then his calling simply never materialised and the years of school faded fast for the question of ‘What now?’ loomed. Would the boy with a dream merely abide like a sheep and follow the rest of the flock to College or would he pursue that dream and in doing so gamble away his life? Please, if he had gone to College then I conclude now and forget writing another five hundred words but as we know the student opted to defy the odds… However in a twist of fate, his career path had taken a turn for the worse as employment within the industry immediately dried up and in the middle of this recession small training facilities blossomed. Tuition didn’t come cheap however as these business ventures only catered for those willing to part with what little people already had and little being the operative word. People slaved away for their opportunity including Gareth Green and the man chasing a dream became renowned for working odd jobs as he desperately tried to salvage a career that hadn’t even started. As popularity continued to soar, so did the necessary tuition fees and the summit of the mountain faded to the point of being almost unobtainable but then luck seeped in and so the story takes a turn for the better. Missing out on a opportunity to watch a travelling promotion due to work commitments the prankster in making later met up with one of it’s talents by chance and the door opened. Working as a bartender in a pitiful social club, the usual smoke infested squalor, Gareth Green watched as a mountainous individual sat quenching his thirst while the punters took stabs at his occupation. It came to fruition that the gentleman was a wrestler on the same tour the Jester had missed and his presence was the catalyst to fuel the punters anguish. Anyway one of these infidels attempted to attack the gentleman but quick intervention from Gareth Green saw off the threat and in return, the wrestler, later unveiled as Double MJ offered the lowly bartender the opportunity to work the circuit. One would laugh for Gareth Green had now been reduced to a lowly ring constructor but it did however offer the real taste of a wrestling environment and in fairness was a foot through the door. The seasons passed as he worked as a ring constructor for a number of months, assembling the ring for each show when by chance the promoter of this promotion offered a proposition with regards to an angle he had in mind. The idea was for Gareth Green to take a number of bumps from the companies leading heel, which never disheartened for his moment had finally came. The night came and the backdrop was filled to capacity as the companies biggest heel trash talked members of the roster and staff alike when on queue he took out his built up frustration on the construction boy. Being tossed about like a rag doll isn’t everyone’s jollies but that was the Jester’s initial use when finally he snapped and hit the thug off his feet with a Cross Body, which coincidentally spurned the crowned into cheers but like all good things, they come to an end. Ascending the turnbuckle could arguably be the worst or best decision the young Gareth Green could’ve made, he was pummelled but his bravery earned him a spot on the roster. All that was missing was an alias, and so the era of New Blood was born… Grasping the opportunity in both hands New Blood become a prominent part of the company and sky rocketed up the corporate ladder. His ascension up the ranks was then rewarded further by his inclusion into a tag team featuring the man who made his dream a reality, Double MJ. Things were looking up but like the parable ‘Thing’s that go up must come down!’ Like a downward spiral his career alongside Double MJ was plagued by misfortune and courtesy of a high profile angle the team split causing a rift between the two parties. The idea was then put in place for New Blood to feud with Double MJ and conclude at the Pay-Per-View, the very same spectacle that never materialised. In turned out that the company was in turmoil and its prospects were being shipped out for financial reasons, unknown to the rookie that his opponent had terminated his contract prier to their showdown. Double MJ had parted company and taken up residence in a local rival known as the Dark Wrestling Alliance (D.W.A) but the loss of his mentor was the least of his problems as Gareth Green, New Blood call him what you would was to made unemployed. - To be continued -
