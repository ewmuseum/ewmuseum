Front Office Christmas

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29 - 12 - 02 Christmas Christmas is the one-day set aside on the annual calendar where everybody, no matter of creed and colour, nor religion gather together in celebration. Issues are put aside for this one-day, broken bridges mended and generally a festive season where people rejoice, whilst reflecting over the last twelve months and their significance. However-.... Why do we actually celebrate Christmas? Many would argue that this celebration reflects the birth of the Messiah Jesus Christ and this one-day shows our homage towards him. Other people use this festive season to show human emotion by giving presents; even people with built up hatred and a general dis-taste for those other than themselves. A further question would be who would stoop so low and actually personify these bad attributes, which other than evil incarnate Xavier Kannon. So how would someone like CEO Xavier Kannon celebrate Christmas? Primetime Championship Wrestling's alpha-male is disliked by the majority and liked by few, that few his personal circle he calls 'The Front Office'. A man who has sworn his life to Scientology, is the picture of power and to anger him further, has just came off a defeat at Decimation, a loss suffered by the thorn in his side Duke Williams. None of this answers the question above but maybe the events below will-.... A dazzling lighting display accompanies the bellow of music indicating a party. Christmas decorations litter the walls of this spacious room, debris of party poppers litter the floor and through the thunder of the speakers, low murmurs of conversation filter. The elegant furnishings suggest an owner/s with taste, someone who appreciates the finer things in life, in fact the scene looks to have a spark of similarity as though we have see this setting countless times before. Hitting like a thunderbolt, it is apparent that this cosy home is the Hubbardville love nest of CEO Xavier Kannon and Commishineress Eleanor Kannon-Hall. - THUD - The crash bellowed from the white washed door of the dwelling and from the glitz and glamour of the party within emerged a suit-adorning butler who professionally strutted to the door, opening to unveil the Conniving Collective of Gaz 'The Corporate Jester' Green and Soviet Scarlet Natalia Miyagawa. 'Whoa-... Last time I was here I was somewhat engaged in war with the owner!' murmured the Emerald Menace towards his beautiful valet who's glittery dress glistened in the lighting. 'It's nice to see our taxes are well spent!' sarcastically replied Ms. Miyagawa, her eyes roaming over the elegant furnishings. 'This way sir-....' issued the butler as he lead the two person party into the home of Xavier Kannon. Walking from the front door, through the living quarters, the trio embark to the back end of this colossal home where a party is well underway, the sound rocking the buildings very foundations. Entering the darkness of one room, the lights jerked into action as they roamed the four corners at an high tempo, the random flashes unveiling activity as various people danced whilst others littered the refreshment stand. 'Mr. Kannon will be with you shortly, please feel free to make use of the house!' said the butler with his strong English accent, pivoting he vanished into the density of the crowd leaving the recently acquired Front Office Jester to the company of his valet. 'Well Natalia, I bet Alexander never treated you to a celebrity bash!?!' asked the Lord Of Laughter as he swept his arm around the slender waist of Natalia, who didn't thwart her clients advances but before she can at-least retort his statement the familiar voice of Xavier Kannon filtered with an interruption. 'And how is Alexander these days, I mean dropping down a league and having the embarrassment of airport security questioning him every time he walks through a metal detector, something you had a hand in Gaz!' The duo turn to greet the PCW owner who stood alongside his most prize asset of former cheerleader Eleanor Kannon-Hall. The two established diva's snarl at one another whilst the Front Office members of CEO Xavier Kannon and Gaz Green pleasantly exchange a handshake. Their inter-linked hands separate and another hired help waltzes past holding a silver tray, which the quartet bombards, dislodging the array of champagne glasses. 'Alexander is a sorry subject, similar to that of Decimation I would think?!?' retorted Natalia Miyagawa, a little sarcasm from her part as she hinted towards the CEO's slight setback in his quest to defeat former stable mate Duke Williams. 'Err-... Arr-... Well-...' stuttered the Scientologist, lost for words, however saved by the intervention of his loyal Jester Gaz Green. 'A minor set-back I can assure you. Personally I think Mr. Kannon has had his fair share of good luck, exchanging the has-been of Duke Williams for an improved model, the signing of his very own Corporate Jester thus strengthening The Front Office!' The group turned to silence as they drank the content of their glasses and as the camera peered across the dance floor, we witness the conversing of fellow stable mates, Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson. The Financial Reclamation Executive rambled away to his colleague, the Personal Director unaware of the presence of Gaz Green who frantically waved but to no avail as the conversation deepened. 'Ellie my little fuck ferret, would you mind taking Natalia on a tour of the house while I speak to MY Corporate Jester?' pleasantly asked the Scientologist, lowering his head to give his wife a farewell kiss. The two female valets hand in hand walked in conversation, lucky it wasn’t one dragging the other by their hair thought Gaz, which spurned a smug smile as Xavier Kannon crashed his hand on his Front Office partners shoulder, catching the former Tag Champion a little off-guard. 'Gaz now that we're associates, tell me, have you gone from banging Alexander Miyagawa's head against a concrete wall to banging his wife?' questioned the inquisitive Wisconsin native, his eyes locked like cross-hairs on the smug face of Gaz Green who retorted with a vague statement that only fuelled further speculation. 'I don't kiss and tell-....' The duo smiled and chuckled shallowly towards one another. Even though a bond had been established between the pair, a rift from eons ago still lurks yet the two superstars share respect for one another thus their involvement within The Front Office. 'Oh, I have a gift for you squire!' injected the green tailored Jester. Squeezing his hand into the inside of his emerald business jacket, Gaz emerged baring a small petite gift that had been wrapped in glistening foil paper, accompanied with a golden string, a professional job one would say. Stretching out his hand with whilst retaining his smug facial expression, Kannon accepted the parcel and like a child on Christmas day threw aside the coverings revealing a small box with the bold lettering of 'Rolex'. 'What a thoughtful gesture-....' said Xavier Kannon. Opening the small box, the Scientologist gazed over the elegant gift, which was a band of gold with diamonds encased around the rim and the efficient second hand that pivoted full circle. Closing the box under a warm smile, a photograph moment, maybe not level par to the one he adorned when defeating Tyler Nelson but still a rarity, Xavier Kannon then replied. 'Mr. Green I too have a gift for you-... Actually I have a title?!?' The Jester's eyes lit up like an Anime character, wide and glassy, the silver tongue soon followed as the former Pranks With Attitude member when into overdrive, his sentences a chattering mess of excitement. 'EXTREME CHAMPION - Maybe not, the title is a little below a two time Tag Team Champion, however its a stepping stone, a foot through the door-....' 'CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION - Well Tim Walden currently holds that and he is far deserving to retain that pristine, unless of course he vacates the title where I maybe considered' 'TELEVISION CHAMPION - Hmmmm, its been held by some of the greats of Primetime Championship Wrestling-....' 'CONTINENTAL CHAMPION - Now we're talking!' As the Jester reflected over the pristine of PCW, Xavier Kannon merely watched on quite jovial. As Gaz was about to murmur the words of 'WORLD' the CEO interrupted and finally concluded the concept of his gift, Gaz a mess of anticipation, his body trembling at what lay in wait. 'Actually the title I bestow upon you is-.... FRONT OFFICE ENTERTAINMENT MANAGER!' roared the former World Heavyweight Champion, Gaz stood in awe, his mouth ajar, his eyes capturing a glint of glee as he reflected over his newfound role within the company. Clashing his glass against that of Gaz Green's, the devious duo celebrated the festive season with the announcement that P.W.A's former prankster had now been promoted within the company to that of FRONT OFFICE ENTERTAINMENT MANAGER. With a sip from they respected glasses the music continued to bellow, people continued to dance and rejoice and all appeared well as the scene closed from the comfort of Hubbardville's first couple's Wisconsin home. - The End -
