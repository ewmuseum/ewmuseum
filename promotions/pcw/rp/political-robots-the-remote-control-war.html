Political Robots & The Remote Control War

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Ladies, Gentlemen, and Scottish People...I&#039;m Baaaaaaaack Our scene opens up on the apartment of Rush Mason, newly refurbished since last seen. Now there are strange idols and other figures all over the apartment, including some African and Haitian flags hanging in various parts of the apartment. Rush: Ahh…now that this has the homely feel of Bon Dieu’s own abode. As Rush refers to the almighty creator of the Voodoo belief, he flops down onto a leather couch, which is rested against a wall that holds a Haitian flag directly above Rush’s head. He grabs the remote control from the coffee table that holds more than one strange-looking magazines featuring idols like the ones in his apartment. Rush: Well damn…it’s 12:02. What the hell is on TV at 12:02? The Voodoo Villain begins to channel surf, flipping through multiple Girls Gone Wild infomercials and re-runs of M*A*S*H. Rush: Ugh…late night TV. Continuing to flip through the TV, Rush finally stops on the hip-hop and R&B countdown show, 106 & Park. The two co-hosts, AJ and Free, are both standing at the edge of the stage. AJ: Yes, thanks to the great people in the office, we’ve got a huge performance from rap’s hottest rising star! Rush mutters to himself while he watches with boredom on his face. Rush: As big as your forehead, you overgrown ape? Free: Yes, here he is….ANDY MURRAY! Rush looks on in disbelief and then throws his head back with disgust and boredom. Before the terrible performance can start, Rush changes the channel, landing on C-SPAN, where a congressional hearing of some sort is taking place. Rush: Of course; Congress: The legal form of country clubs where white middle-aged men can pretend to work. Un pays fou. The French words for “crazy country” sound strange coming out of Rush’s mouth, but most likely have somehow to do with his brainwashing and converting to Voodoo beliefs. On the TV screen, senator Trent Lott is addressing the panel in his most exciting robotic, droning voice. Senator Lott: Thus, in conclusion, the Pledge of Allegiance is very unconstitutional and should be banned from public schooling. Rush rolls his eyes while he watches on, kicking off his shoes. Rush: You’ve gotta be kidding me. Instead of focusing on keeping these terrorists, we’re whining and bitching about some moronic issue that no one will ever remember. This country is starting to be disturbingly reminiscent of a John Crichton promo. Rush shivers at the thought of Andy Murray’s friend droning on, in even more of a boring fashion then Trent Lott’s looooong pronooooooounciationnnn offffffff wooooooords. Trent Lott: In faaaaaact, God is unconstitutional. Ah think all religion should be baaaaaaanned. Whahhhhh don’t we just make the officiaaaaal religion worshipping robots, because they’ll all control us soon anywaaaaaay. If we- Before another long tedious speech can come from the Senator’s mouth, the channel is thankfully changed, switching over to MTV, where MTVNews is just coming up with its “ten to the hour, every hour until you figure out this isn’t real news” update. The ever preptastic Gideon Yago comes up onto the screen. Gideon Yago: Jennifer Lopez has finally announced it: She and husband Chris Judd will be calling it quits. Rush’s eyes start to close, becoming heavy as a result of the endlessly boring reports. Rush: Resist…Urge…To…Watch…Porn Gideon Yago: Her second marriage therefore has ended for the 34 year old Lopez, who is once again the most eligible bachelorette in the country. Rush begins to slap himself on the side of his face to keep himself awake as he mutters to himself. Rush: Most eligible bachelorette? That’s like saying Jaden is actually respected for her wrestling ability. The Voodoo Villain snorts a laugh as he yet again changes the channel, landing now on some sort of CNN-like news channel, that has over a thousand “BREAKING NEWS” stories per day, each one of them immensely important, even if most happen to be the extinction of the pygmy ass-gobbling Polynesian gnat (you get our drift). Rush: Well, just dandy. Yet another escapade with Morons Inc. Rush’s sarcastic comment comes just as a shot of the Bush cabinet members appear on the screen, at a conference of some sort as the caption’s title reads Confused Man Violated White House. Rush: Oh my Bon Dieu, they’ve figured out that- Very afraid that the government has actually figured just how inept the president is, Rush listens closely, but his idea is shot down when he hears that the story is about a man flying in restricted air space. Of course, there is a reporter with poofy hair including a seasoned analyst to talk about this “BREAKING NEWS” situation. Reporter: Jim, can you give us any idea of what happened today at the conference? Analyst: Well I can Bob. Apparently the man who entered the “no fly zone” was “confused and apologetic” when he learned of this infraction. This would not be a normally annoying sentence, but the analyst has taken the liberty of making all quotation marks with his fingers, making his appear slightly more intelligent than an amoeba, a small child, or George W. Bush. Rush: We need two people to tell us that someone who flew over the White House was a moron? That’s like needing three people to explain a referee’s three count on Andy “Verbal Vacuum of Nothingness” Murray. Once again, the channel is fortunately changed, finally landing on “Politically Incorrect” with Bill Maher. The only guest that can be seen is John Travolta. Bill Maher: Now John, obviously the light has recently been shed on religious extremists. Do you think that any followers of Scientology would do such a thing as this? John Travolta: Well, I really don’t think so. Most of the members are rich white men like me who live in Beverly Hills. Both men and the audience share a laugh at this as Rush just blinks, finding very little humor in this. Rush: I thought that the Lifetime channel was purgatory television, but this may just take the cake. Bill Maher: That’s true, would you expect something like that from a lesser-known religion? John Travolta: I think we can just call them inferior; we’re all adults here who know the reality Bill, aren’t we? Rush’s face now is devoid of any emotion, just shaking his head at what he sees on his television. Rush: Resist…Urge…To…Become…A…Terrorist Bill Maher: Yeah, like would you expect a Voodoo follower to do something crazy like try and black magic everyone in New York City? Both men laugh once again. John Travolta: No, they’d never afford the plane ticket. Remote Control: Click!
