Past Regression I

15 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th January 2004 Note: This is a series of three RPs detailing the background of Afwa, and will be a basis of the Afwa/Jay Pheonix feud It stars: Chief Priest Tainafi Malietoa Tautolo 'Towla' Mintola Sailele Afwa The setting is that of an archaic period, a time when the islands of Samoa were being destroyed by the likes of The Tongan army, especially that of the barbarian hoards led by the mighty 'Tongan Terror'. Seen all around are burnt out shacks, bodies of various shapes and sizes, some with limbs, some without, and others with just a skull, some with nothing at all. The atmosphere all around us is that of anarchy and war, the scorching heat beaming down upon the shores of Somata radiates the cameras view. In the distance a battle march can be heard playing, reminding us what savage men these around us once were, though the scene has been dropped a little what with all these deaths. A voice can be heard crying out in the distance, mourning for the dead and weeping for the walking wounded. The scene can honestly be described as the remains of a battleground, weapons of various forms, especially sabres and cutlasses, along with heavy titanium circular shields can be seen on the ground in amongst the dead and the dying. The only light, apart from the radiant sun is seen coming from one of the few remaining shacks, though it does look like it will be in for a state of repairs as the grass curtain protecting the windows from the rain is in tatters, torn to pieces by the hoards in their attempt to cause destruction and what was once a wooden door is now nothing more then a pile of ash, as it seems the fire was put out before much damage was really caused. Seen inside the shack is the tribal clothing of the Somatan Chief Priest Tainafi Malietoa. Tainafi slowly walks to the front of the shack, stands outside and basks in the radiant heat that is emanated from the sun. A titanium gong can be seen outside the shack. Tainafi using one of those metal sticks sounds the gong, instantly the people of Somata stop what they are doing, thus showing their respect for their Chief Priest. Slowly a group of tribal warriors, or what is left of the Somata tribal warriors gather around the shack, though one rather ambient tribal warrior is not there, for he is in the Crypt, mourning the loss of his parents, he is of course Afwa Kilmanjaro, son of the King of Somata and prince of his people thus due to his parents death, he is now the rightful heir to all of Somata. Tainafi with a deep voice speaks to his crowds. 'Citizens of Somata, today a tragic incident has occurred. The sinister plot to destroy our land has been made manifest and the armies across the northern side of Samoa are raging against us. Day by day and more of out people are becoming massacred at the hands of 'The Tongan Terror' and his Tyrannous armies of the Shankti tribe. No matter what we do, the lives of those dear to us can not be brought back, Somata is slowly becoming extinct, soon all of us will have no choice but to leave, for the lives of our people and your own depend upon it.' As he finishes, from amongst the crowds a young warrior by the name of Tautolo 'Towla' Mintola can be seen shaking his head at what Tainafi is saying. He then in a bold voice says, 'What about the war, can't there be peace' Tainafi smiles, saying, 'Peace is a word that these bloodthirsty dogs do not comprehend. All they know is how to kill, it's in their ancestry and as time goes by, unless we can somehow find a way of putting a stop to all of this, then soon it shall become unsafe for me to even be talking with you.' Another voice this time from a warrior called Sailele in the crowds says, 'What can we do?' At this, Tainafi sighs, then looks as if he could easily give up, though he continues. 'Their armies are too strong, and our supplies are running out. What we need is someway of dragging them down with us. What we need is somebody who could help us through this bloodthirsty massacre, have you any ideas, where such a person can be found?' Suddenly in another outburst Tautolo pushes past and gets to the front saying to Tainafi. 'Sire, there are rumours that in the West cost of Tonga there is an old spiritual leader who people say has the power to unleash the demons of Heaven and Hell and holds the key to the bottomless pit.' Tainafi turns to Tautolo and says, 'Who is this man?' Tautolo sighs. 'That's just it sire, nobody has ever found him. Some say he is located in the West, some say the East, and nobody has yet to see him though. He could in fact be a mythical creature dreamed up by our people in order to protect them.' One of the few remaining prophets shouts out: BELIVE AND THE TRUTH SHALL SET YOU FREE. 'I'll not employ what men I have left to seek out a being that could not even exist. Yet if this man has the power to unleash Heaven and Hell and holds the key to the bottomless pit, then this task can be achieved by only one.' Tautolo in an inquisitive tone. 'Who sire, who do you trust enough to enter such a dangerous quest.' 'There can be nobody better for this then the Samoan God himself.' Tautolo can't believe it. 'You can't. He.' Sailele says, 'Yes, he is not in a fit state. After the sadistic savage way that the barbarian hoards under the guidence of The Tongan Terror debauched his parents, carved a mark into his own flesh and brutally slaughtered them in front of him, clearly he is not the correct delegate for this task.' Tainafi shakes his head. 'That is the reason why. This will help Afwa come to terms with those who slaughtered his family and in the end will force the savage in Afwa to unleash the beast and devour the flesh of those not so great as him. Afwa has inside him passions that will one day thrive on the sheer fact that all we see before us now, or what is left of Somata shall belong to him. Afwa is a real prince among us all and tomorrow morning, Afwa shall go on his journey to find the mysterious Spiritual leader and I just hope for the sake of Somata he can do it. For the fate of Somata it now rests in his hands.' Tautolo looks puzzled, saying, 'What if he can't, what if this magi doesn't exist?' Tainafi looking a bit perplexed but does not say he is, instead says, 'Think of the negative points to life and that is what you shall end up living for. The positive is what is the key to all of this. We need to all stay focused and think positive about the situation here in Somata, for one wrong move now and the entire village could be theirs and all that the great Kilmanjaro dynasty represent shall be worthless and the deaths of Afwa's parents shall mean that the barbarian hoards have won.' Tautolo says. 'Never. Never will they become the victor. The battle is far from over, the war is just beginning, and I don't know about you but I am no way near ready, if ready at all to just hand over what last remains we now have. Who is with me, who will fight these Barbarians? Who will bring down the mighty Tongan Terror and avenge the deaths of all our people. Who will stand and fight for what they believe in. Who will stand fight for Somata, who will fight for our Island, for like one Islander to another, that is what we all are.' Slowly we can see entering before us now the heavy built frame of a man who has a build like an Ox, strength like a bear and the courage of a lion. His face shows a few tears from his previous mourning, but apart from that the one we know as Afwa Kilmanjaro, now King of all Somata is seen standing before us. Afwa in his usual self like manner speaks saying. 'Afwa, I be Afwa.' Tainafi looks at Afwa and looks as if he is about to cry. 'I am sorry Afwa, but I need you to help us. I need to tell you the plans.' Afwa snarls, 'Me Afwa, me no make plans! Me go chase Wild Boar and tear head! Me Avenge death of family!' Tautolo looks at his best friend and says, 'Afwa, the barbarian hoards day by day are getting stronger, all ready they have captured the eastern provinces, soon they shall have conquered half of Somata but before that happen, Afwa we need your help. Will you help the people of Somata, will you help your people?' Afwa snorts, 'Afwa, he help nobody. He help himself! Afwa want feeding, give Afwa some FRESH MEAT!' Tainafi is quick to rush to the aid of Afwa, ordering the servants to bring Afwa some raw meat for him to devour. 'You heard him, now do it! I want to make him as comfortable as I possibly can, for tomorrow morning, Afwa, you shall go on a journey, a journey to seek out something which many say is a myth, yet Afwa, I have faith in you, I have faith in what your parents did for Somata and I know that if this Spiritual Leader does really exist then you shall find him, bring back the key to the bottomless pit and send The Barbarian Hoards and The Tongan Terror deep down, never to return.' Afwa looking puzzled says, 'Me Afwa, me confused.' Tainafi turns to Sailele and says, 'Sailele, do you have the map of where this leader is supposed to be.' Sailele slowly takes out a piece of parchment, which has over time worn away, yet we can somewhat see a route and a location where this leader is supposed to be. Afwa takes a gaze over the map and sees a large ebony black mound with a skull on in the middle of the map. Around the skull a blood red circle can be seen and the name Pheonix Rock. Afwa scratches his head and snorts, the air being so fierce that the paper parchment blows out of Sailele' hands and onto the floor. Afwa looks and is a bit scared, so with hesitation he says. 'That be the place?' Sailele says, 'Afwa, Pheonix Rock is a place where not many have lived to tell the tale. Those who walk past the Pheonix Falls, the ebony black rock is said to give out a vibrating sound, causing them to shake and fall off the mountaintop to the death below. Afwa you are a big person and one slight slip, and the rock could crumble with you on it, all I can say Afwa is good luck, as I'm sure if anybody can save us it is you, for you are the monster, you are The Samoan God, Afwa!' Afwa again look scared saying, 'Afwa he scared! Afwa he no like heights.' Tainafi tries to reassure the big oaf saying, 'Afwa, it shall be ok. Every step of the way, the spirits of your fathers shall be there to guide you. They will guide you on your path to the ultimate destiny. And now Afwa, I suggest we all get some rest as tomorrow could be the start of a very long conquest.' The scene slowly fades out as for today the tribe of Somata is about to get some rest, tomorrow, who knows what might happen. Slowly the sun sets upon us as the scene fades to black.
