The Double Cross

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/10/02 ***1pm*** Bob Vurdo and Rosanna have just pulled up outside Bob's large house. There are a few cars in the driveway as there is a small social meeting for Bob to meet the new recruit and for the rest of the family to meet Rosanna Bobs new girlfriend. Bob and Rosanna walk on the path through the garden toward the door where Bob stops and turns to Rosanna. Rosanna is wearing a moron dress and her hair is slightly wavy as it hangs loosely on her shoulders. Bob is wearing a black business suit that makes the two quite well dressed for the gathering. 'This is very important so be on your best behavior.' Bob is very serious as he says this and is also slightly nervous. First impressions last with these men and if they don't like Rosanna then it will make the relationship very difficult. Rosanna nods her hair and smiles at Bob before placing her hand on his right shoulder. 'What are you so worried about.... Just relax.' Bob nods his head and turns to the door before straightening himself up. Bob opens up the door revealing the empty lobby of his house. The room has four doors going off it and a large staircase in the middle of the room that leads up to the next floor. There is a door on each side of the room and then two doors at the back of the room on each side of the staircase. Bob and Rosanna make their way to the door at the back of the room to the left of the staircase. They open up the door which leads into a hallway with a door at the end that leads into the backyard and two doors on the right hand side of the corridor. They head toward the door which leads to the backyard and as they approach they can hear voices. They open up the back door which reveals a large perfectly mowed yard with luscious green grass. In the middle of the yard is a table set up under the shade of a marquee. At the table sits Tony, Sammy who is in a wheelchair and Ron Jackson talking to each other. As they hear the door open their heads turn to see Bob and Rosanna. When Ron and Bob's eyes meet they don't part. The two stare at each other in disbelief as Bob begins quickly walking toward Ron. Bob reaches into his jacket and pulls out a handgun as he walks up to Ron. A few metres away from Ron he aims it to his head and stares down at the black man. 'What the fuck is going on?' Tony can't believe what Bob is doing and either can Sammy. 'Please man it was a misunderstanding.' Ron is backing down to Bob who has a gun aimed straight for Ron's head. 'Who the fuck am I?' Bob has an elevated voice as he says this he is full of anger from the way he was treated yesterday. Ron goes to answer but Bob interrupts 'I'm Bob Vurdo I run this town.... I am the man no one wants to fuck with!' Ron nods at Bob in a scared manner. 'I didn't know...' 'Don't claim you own nothing until you get your facts straight you fucking ghetto bastard.' Ron looks extremely intimidated by the gun which is held aimed at his head. 'Whoa Bob let it go whatever it is.' Tony is trying his best to cool down the situation, he can see the look in Bob's eyes and he knows this is serious but he doesn't want to see Ron dead. With one quick swift movement Ron stands up and pulls out a gun and aims it at Bobs head. The two stand just metres away from each other with guns aimed at each other. Both men have the look of rage in their eyes. Both men are ready to kill the other without a second thought but they both know they can't get shot themselves in the process. Tony pulls a gun out of his jacket and aims it at Ron. When it comes to taking sides Tony will always be with Bob Vurdo. Sammy does the same and just as the tide seems to have turned three gun shots are heard from behind the men at the back of the yard. Bob and Tony both fall to the ground and hide behind the nearest objects being chairs. Bob takes a few shots at where he thought Ron was but realizes he has also moved from his spot. Bob rolls over onto his stomach to look for Rosanna and as he does he sees two black men disappearing into the house. Rosanna is not to be found and because the gun fire seems to have stopped both Bob and Tony get up from the ground. Bob doesn't see Rosanna and realizes they must have taken her. He drops to his knees on the ground and begins to break down crying. Bob hasn't cried since he was a young boy and this is obviously quite a serious situation. The only person other than his mother he has ever loved has just been taken by three black men including Ron Jackson. The images of the three men he saw outside the hospital the other day keep flashing through his head. He is in another world.... A world of chaos where nothing goes right. Why did his one true love have to be taken away from him? Why does he have such bad luck? Is it Karma? Bob is the leader of a large crime family so that has to be bad karma and what if Karma is real Bob would never have anything good happen. The fist time he ever noticed Ron was when he made a sexual remark about Rosanna. Bob can't help but help stupid things jump through his head such as rape. He can see it now the three black men each having their turn with her while she is tied down to a bed. That's what black people are like Bob thinks to himself who knows what they could do to Rosanna. Rage takes him over and he stands up and begins running toward the back door of his house. Bob has never been seen so emotional before there's no telling what this could do to the Bob's psychology. 'Sammy's been hit.' Tony is yelling to Bob but Bob doesn't hear it he is in his own world now. Bob disappears into the house as Tony looks over Sammy. Sammy is still breathing and is still conscious in his wheelchair. He has a lot of blood staining his shirt from the shot which hit him in the lower left area of his upper body. Blood from internal bleeding starts pouring out from his mouth as he looks at Tony. Sammy's eyes are half there and half not there with his eyelids half shut he looks over Tony who seems to be beside himself after the tragedy. 'Your not going to die Sammy! I am not going to let you die dammit!' Tony puts his hands to his head to think about it for a second. He moves over to his friend Sammy and puts his hand on his shoulder. 'You are not going to do!' Tony has sworn to himself he will not let Sammy die today. 'Call a fucking doctor! Someone call a fucking ambulance!' Tony is yelling out but no one is there to hear his cries for help. 'I'll be back in a second hang in there Sammy.' Tony taps him on the shoulder one more time and takes a look at him as Sammy nods in a half conscious fashion. Tony runs down to the back door and into the corridor. He opens up the first door to his left which is a bedroom. He runs over ot the bedside table with a phone on it and dials 991. 'This is 911 please state your emergency....' --------------------------------***6.00pm***------------------------------ We see Tony sitting on a couch staring at the television with his head in his hands. He is beside himself and is thinking of the event that's transpired earlier today. He could have done more for Sammy if he had acted earlier. Bob disappeared after he started chasing after the men that have Rosanna. What if Bob is dead as well it will mean the collapse of the Vurdo family and Tony will become a sitting duck for the many enemies he has. 'In local news, today at 1.20pm a suspected local Mafia figurehead died in hospital after being shot. It is believed this is an act of gang warfare and we are urging all residents of Amber Hill to watch out for suspicious situations.' The voice of the news lady interrupts Tony's chain of thought. As he hears this a tear rolls from out of his left eye he didn't know for sure that he was dead until now. 'Those black fuckers don't know what they have got themselves into.' The tone in which Tony says this shows that he means every word he says and there is justice to be had.
