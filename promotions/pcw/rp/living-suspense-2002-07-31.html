Living Suspense

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st July Around the corner is El Diablo's match against Chris Chase, Akiyama and Rent A Villain, and what a match it should be, and El Diablo is exactly is just where he should be on the road to preparation for such a huge match. But where is this road taking him? What strange twists will occur as he gets ready for Turmoil? The scene opens up on a street somewhere, which is practically empty except for a few pigeons roaming them and some birds resting on phone towers. The street is in the middle of nowhere, which is quite evident by the lack of buildings around and just a field of grass opposite the paved part. Even though it's an empty street there is a road and some parked cars on it, perhaps parked cars of people that work nearby. It looks a nice place during the day, like now, but at night it looks like it could be a different story, as their are no streetlights around at all. But for now it looks peaceful and very un-industrial except for the cars and the telephone box at the far end of the street. Then enter El Diablo! Diablo is wearing a white vest and black shorts, as well as black sneakers and white socks. He also wears his Mexican face paint and has his long black hair out loose, as well as a bottle of water he holds in his right hand. Diablo is sweating heavily, from what looks like to have been a very strenuous work out. He wipes his head with his left hand and shakes off the excess sweat like a dog would after being bathed. 'Whew! What a work out,' says El Diablo to himself. But then just a little bit behind him appears Angel, who seems to have run a little bit but walked most of the way. She is wearing a tight black T-Shirt that is tied at the bottom so her pierced navel is visible, a short black skirt and a pair of white sneakers. Her dyed black hair is tied behind her and she looks quite tired. 'Did you really have to bring me along?' asks Angel, panting away. Diablo looks back at her and holds his hand out for her to grab. 'Because I thought you wanted us to do more things together,' he says as Angel takes his hand. Angel simply sighs. 'I do, but I didn't mean I want to do everything with you!' comments Angel in a soft way to let him down gently. El Diablo nods and then smirks. 'Don't you like working out?' asks Diablo to his beautiful wife. They start to stroll down the street hand in hand as the birds fly above them and some walk in front of them on the pathway with a mad look in their eyes. 'Of course I do honey. But this is your match, not mine! And right now I don't feel that desire to exercise,' replies Angel in an attempt to motivate El Diablo for his match. 'Well I have that desire right now! I need that desire and adrenaline as that is what will get me to my match on Turmoil and help me be victorious! It is a match I must win to further my career,' Diablo says with passion in his voice. Angel smirks to herself for succeeding in motivating her husband for the Tuesday Turmoil match against Chris Chase, Akiyama and Rent A Villain. They continue to walk and as they do, they notice that some of the birds are beginning to act crazy. Bird after bird start fighting with one another and crowing rather loudly and frighteningly. Angel takes a hold of El Diablo's hand tight, as they begin to walk faster to try and avoid the birds. Diablo looks around cautiously, and rightfully so as just as he does that a magpie pecks right onto his foot, causing Diablo to yelp in pain. As he does yelp in pain, the birds start to mimic the yelp and crow even louder. 'Shit, we best get out of here,' whispers Diablo in panic to his wife. But it is almost as if the birds can here him as they start flying circles around their head. Angel screams loudly and El Diablo tries to swat the birds away from around their heads with his hands, but he soon gives up in favor of running for cover with his wife. They desperately sprint away, trying to avoid the birds attacks, but as they run the birds just start coming at them in larger groups and attempt to peck away at them. 'Quick, in here,' screams Angel to her husband. They quickly force their way into the empty phone booth and slam the door shut behind them. For extra safety Diablo even leans against the door to stop any birds from making their way in. 'What the hell is going on?' shrieks Angel. Both Angel and El Diablo pant heavily to catch their breaths fully from running away from those birds. 'I don't know,' answers Diablo in worry and looking behind him to make sure they are safe, 'But this does seem awfully familiar,' continues the Mexican Devil. Angel nods. 'You're telling me,' she says while pointing to across the street. Across the street there is a building and inside of it, a man is dragging some heavy object across the floor inside of a trash bag. El Diablo looks at it and panics. 'Shiiiiiiit!' he shouts, 'Why does this kind of stuff always happen to me?' he moans whilst biting his fingernails. Almost immediately after he says that a bird crashes into the phone booth, which causes a scream from Angel. She jumps right into the arms of her husband and places her head on his shoulder. 'I'm sure we'll be ok,' says Diablo, trying to reassure his nervous wife. But in reality El Diablo is just as scared and nervous as she is. At that point another bird crashes into the phone booth and slides back down the glass after causing a crack in it. Angel grips to El Diablo tighter than before. 'This seems all so familiar, but I just can't put my finger it,' states Diablo, while making sure he keeps the door closed. Angel raises her head and looks up at her husband. 'I don't care what is now! We're gonna DIE!!!!!!!!' she screams as tears stream down from her eyes onto her cheeks. Diablo suddenly lets her go and heads towards the telephone. 'Brilliant idea Raul,' Angel says as she begins to wipe her tears away. El Diablo picks up the receiver and begins to type in three key numbers, '999' As Angel watches on in hope, she sees the look on her husbands face go from joy to despair. He then drops the receiver and drops to the floor himself, with his head in his hands. Angel places her hand on his shoulder, which causes him to look up at her, whose look of hope is now the same look in his eyes. 'It's dead! There is no way out,' mutters Diablo agonisingly to his wife. She begins to cry, but it is barely audible over the amount of crowing that the birds are making. 'I got it, this is familiar to those two Hitchcock films The Birds and Rear Window,' comments Diablo to his now weeping wife. But before she can respond a few lighting bolts occur and suddenly everything around them goes white. It's almost like they're travelling in time.=============== 'Nah it'll never work,' says a voice. Soon a piece of paper is screwed up and thrown into a waste paper bin. But soon the voice is very recognisable, it's the voice of El Diablo. A hand is then placed on his shoulder. 'It was a good attempt, I'm sure you'll do better next time,' Angel says to reassure her husband. El Diablo sighs. 'It ripped off too many movies though,' states the director wannabe El Diablo. Angel hugs him and kisses him on the cheek. 'You'll do better next time,' she says softly. Was that all just a script from one of El Diablo's movie attempts? *shudders* The horror of it all. Lets just hope he can wrestle better than he can write movie scripts!
