A True Hero

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/15/02 'WORK FASTER, BITCH!' The shrill sounds of a female voice once again pierces the air.......we pan down from a calm blue sky to an overgrown garden. Weeds shoot in all directions, covering the ground........bushes long neglected shoot their branches in all directions, resembling large trees and making this seem like less a garden, but more a jungle. And suitably enough, in the center stands a little girl dressed in tan khaki shorts and holding a whip. Bethany: Faster! Faster I say~! At the far end of this garden is none other than the legendary Johnny Cash. His suit of black now stained with spots of red, he tolls in the burning sun. Cash: You know I'm gonna get you for this eventually, right? Bethany: Blah blah blah! Work! Cash grunts, swinging his machete and trying his hardest to make headway against the vines that seem more like steel cables........when another voice, deeper and more fierce catches his attention. 'GRUAAAAAAAAHHH!!!!!!!' Bethany: (Glancing over) Clyde I told you to stop eating so much cheese! Leaving her newfound slave, the evil little girl makes her way into the decrepit house serving as her and her lover's home. At first she sees nothing.........scanning the room, squinting her eyes even further than their usual squint, she at last though makes out a faint bit of illumination. Bethany: There you are. Bethany marches to the television, her tiny form blocking it off. In front Her hands on her hips, she looks down at Clyde. Bethany: What the hell are you doing? The demonic killer known as Clyde looks rather worse for wear.......his hair is mussed, large bags droop from underneath his eyes. He stares a hole through his lover, seemingly unaware of her presence. Clyde: Arglebargle. Bethany: Speak english, dipshit! Cash: (From outside) Hey man you got baby panthers living out in this thing, you know that? Clyde does not answer.......merely continues staring at the TV. Finally in exasperation Bethany turns, looking at the images it holds. Bethany: ..........Lexian and Duke promos? Clyde: Agghbalaba. Drool hangs from Clyde's mouth, his head wobbles limply to and fro. The little girl grips him by the hair, yanking him up rudely. Bethany: You're doing it again, watching crappy promos! Remember the last time you did that, you watched Miyajobber's first 80 page monstrosity? You aged about 102 years, Clyde! You couldn't get it up! I'M NOT BUYING YOU VIAGRA AGAIN!!!!!! Clyde: Balamamaba. Bethany: ARGH! In anger Bethany plops down on the couch next to Clyde.......he limply falls over, landing in her lap and still staring at the TV. Bethany: (Shoving) Get off me! Our killer flops to the other side, his head bouncing sickly off the side of the seat. Clyde: Agggggh. Bethany: Can't say I blame you. What the hell's the deal with Lexian anyway? All of a sudden he's talking to Captain America and battling the Hulk? Clyde: Barglesnart. Bethany: I swear some people can't differentiate between reality and fiction. And besides, Captain America always was a crappy hero anyway. The dimunitive bitch goddess stares at the television........hitting the button on the remote control. Tom Lexian's picture fades out.......Duke Williams replaces him. Then Duke fades out, to be replaced by Lexian. Duke........Lexian.........Duke. Bethany: (Yawning) We need to switch cable companies........ Bethany blinks, sleepily........slowly closes her eyes. Abruptly though she is awakened by a creaking at the door. Bethany: Huh? Wha? Clyde: Lamama......... Ignoring her dazed boyfriend, she sits up.......and gapes as a figure bounds into the room. His hair puffs out from a white headband, his gaudy outfit displaying a plunging v-neck. He whips his head back and cackles. Man: I'm Black Lightniiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiing! Bethany: ..........you sure are. Black Lightning looks quickly to the left, then to the right. He then snaps his head down to look upon Bethany. Black Lightning: Foxy momma! I'm here to show you what a real hero is like! Our superhero takes the girl by the hand.........begrudgingly she allows herself to be led. Out into the yard they go, but she finds it is now strangely altered.........a city spreads before her. Bethany: ..........what the hell? Black Lightning: Aha! Ahaha! Aha! Bethany: You okay? Black Lightning: Aha! Yeah! Just like laughing like this! Ahahaha! The hero jogs down the street, throwing his arms.........again Bethany follows. The pair passes a small convience store........the owner of which stands outside, holding out an apple. Black Lightning: (Taking the apple) Ahahaha! Thanks m'man! Bethany: ........uh........ Black Lightning: See, when you're a true hero the people in the ghetto love ya! Bethany: Right......... Suddenly Black Lightning stops, causing Bethany to run into his back and fall to the ground. Bethany: BITCH! Black Lightning: Aha! Haha! Aha! I smell evil doing in the air! Bethany: How the hell can you smell anything through all that afro sheen? Black Lightning: Come on! We be on our way! Once more Lightning trots through the streets..........rounds a corner and gasps. Black Lightning: Sweet Christmas! Bethany: What!? Black Lightning: Those guys are stealing watermelon! Bethany looks on blankly........then finally shakes her head as her partner rushes to the attack. Black Lightning: (Leaping forward) I'm Black Lightniiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiing! The pair of criminals, fruit dripping from their mouths merely glance up. Crook: We bought this melon fair and square, slag off. Ignoring them, the superhero points downward with both arms. Black Lightning: Eat justice, crackers! Power begins to surge through Black Lightning's arms.........electricity wraps around him, buzzing across his body. His hands begin to glow........a burst of power erupts. However instead of incinerating the watermelon thieves his gauntlets explode, sending him flying through the air straight over Bethany's head. Bethany: ..........dammit! Shaking her head, Bethany follows the flying hero........at last she finds him laying inside a smoking hole in the ground, only his platform shoes sticking out. Black Lightning: Aha.......ahaha........bleeding internally.......haha....... Bethany: And I guess you want help. Black Lightning: Call a doctor for Black Lightniiiiiiiiiing.........aha....... Bethany: NO! I hate you! You're nothing but a 70's throwback horribly stereotypical black superhero! I'm shocked the NAACP hasn't torn down your house by now, dragging you into the streets and beaten you to death with your afro pick! You're a waste of skin, even more so than Tom Lexian! You aren't a hero, he isn't a hero, YOU BOTH SUCK!!!!!!!! Black Lightning: I can taste my spleen, ahaha.......... Bethany: GRUAAAAAHHHHH!!!!!!!!! Bethany whips out her knife and begins plunging it into the fallen token black hero.........again, again she stabs. Gradually in her murderous haze her vision blurs.......... Bethany: Die.........die........die......... .........and she awakens to find herself sitting atop Clyde on the couch, stabbing him repeatedly. Bethany: DIE! ..........huh? Finally the little girl ceases.........the burned man lies moaning with blood dripping from his body. Clyde: Uggggggggh......... Bethany: ..........this is all Lexian's fault! Stupid nonsensical promos! Bethany chucks her knife at the TV, nailing the image of Legend Inc right between the eyes and shattering the glass. Cash: (From outside) Whoa momma a cougar! Down boy! Bethany: DAMMIT! Our evil princess screams in frustration, getting up and reaching into the broken TV to retreve her knife. She then stomps towards the sound of Johnny Cash in the backyard jungle, waving the blade maniacally as Clyde continues to drool and we mercifully fade to black..........
