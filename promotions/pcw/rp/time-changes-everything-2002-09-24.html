Time changes everything.

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/24/02 -Duke sits on top of the Hubbardville water tower over looking the perfect Wisconsin town. Duke is smoking a cigar as he soaks in the cool fall air moving into the reign. Duke looks into the camera and speaks.- Williams: Johnny you and I are both professionals. To be honest I’m glad to see you back. I’m sorry you’re going to have to start 0-2 but hey life’s a dance ain’t it? -Duke looks over the pumpkin patch sitting in the distance.- Williams: I didn’t really want it to come to this. I asked you for a favor because I could trust you. You betrayed my trust. Now look at you your as pathetic as a man who’ s hand falls asleep while he’s jerking off. -Duke smiles.- Williams: What exactly makes you a legend? How can you call yourself that? I’ve been collecting title belts from different feds for the last twenty years. I’ve slaughtered giants, defeated evil, I was the first wrestler to swear on camera, the guy to chip away Nelson’s power, a hero to the fans, an American icon.. What makes you a legend Jon Kano?? You rock.? Really? I’ve seen a lot more rolling then rocking hoss. -Duke takes a big drag of Punch Cigar as he watches a top heavy women jogging down the street on this cool fall morning.- Williams: You know what the difference between her and a sewing machine is? -Duke laughs to himself.- Williams: I sewing machine only has one bobbin. -Duke laughs out loud.- Williams: Back to the “legendary” Jon Kano. You know as well as I do Jon that it’s a jungle out there. You have to look out for number one with out stepping in number two. My vision is still there Jon, your is slipping. You just come around to realize the fans are a waste of time? -Duke shakes his head.- Williams: A tad behind the times Jon. You see kid I’ve reinvented myself time and time again. I’m a leader while your a follow. I keep up with the happenings and make things happen. I’m hip and jive word holla back youngen!! -Duke throws out some gang signs he doesn’t understand.- Williams: There is two ways to do it. Be stubborn and stay the same. Or change with the times. I’ve changed Jon while you’re that same old loser. It’s a pity really. -Duke watches as the fire department washes there truck with Perrier water.- Williams: From greatest to latest. Blade I see your one of them kids who don’t mention. Your afraid to go word for word with the fastest mouth in wrestling. I understand hoss. I understand that you don’t understand what you’ve gotten yourself into. I understand that your nightmare are probably full of Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. IF not they should be and that’s the Front Office Truth. =w=
