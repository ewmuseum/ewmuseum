So the saga continues

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-1-02 RP 1 Turmoil 3rd We begin inside the cab of a new model silver Silverado. The same plain pick up truck our uncouple couple rented when they first arrived in Canada. They are on their way to yet another hotel before Teresa’s big four way match with Romana, the Russian chick that caused her the match with Anarchy on Fusion, Cecilia, the woman that came in to get her a piece of Anarchy, and finally, Anarchy, herself. All this for the number one contendorship for the Rising Star Title, the first step in being someone in PCW. Teresa stares out the side window as Jack does the driving. She thinks of her match coming up and what may or may not happen in the future. She wasn’t expecting to fight on Friday’s Fusion, but since she did put Charlotte out for a while, the big shots in the office decided to drop Teresa in her spot for the contendor match. Teresa couldn’t have thought of a better plan. Now once chick is out of the way, she has three more to even have a chance at the belt. Teresa’s thoughts not only are on her career. She continues to think about Scott a lot though she does good to keep it in. When they first met, she was simply a rookie manager in an indy fed. The same fed she met Jack, but we won’t go there. For a time she managed both Jack and Scott. They were even a tag team for a short time. Best of friends. Then Scott turned heel. She was torn between her best friend, Scott and her then boyfriend Jack. This situation was similar to the Hogan/Savage era back in the WWF 80’s and Miss Elizabeth was torn between Hulk Hogan and her husband, Randy Savage if they were married yet at that time. Things have changed a lot as we can see. Teresa isn’t seeing either man but somehow is on the road with Jack when she would rather be sitting next to Scott. No words are exchanged in this silent drive to a Hotel Hampton somewhere still in Canada. The sun is unusually hot and the rental truck decided to lose its A/C. Teresa has her long blonde hair pulled in a ponytail. Jack is sweating bullets. When they finally make it to the hotel, claim their reservation, and step into their hotel, Teresa heads straight for the shower. Jack places the luggage next to the wall and scopes the place out. Luckily for Teresa, the hotel room is actually two rooms divided by a single white wooden door. The colors are in shades of blues and greens. Each room has the basic dresser, nightstand and king size bed, a bathroom and a closet. After taking a few minutes to check out the place and Teresa’s suitcase, Jack sits on Teresa’s bed listening to the shower. His light blue eyes move toward the bathroom door as the usual manly thought roam through his manly mind. He dares not attempt anything. If Teresa sends him back to Texas, he is screwed. Then Teresa’s cell phone rings. Teresa doesn’t hear her phone. There’s something about a hot shower that relaxes you and Teresa is so relaxed, she doesn’t want to get out. Finally after forty-five minutes or so of just standing under the showerhead as the many streams of hot water beat against her chest and run down to her toes, Teresa decides it’s best to get dry and dressed before Jack comes in to check on her. He knows better than to jeopardize his own career. The water turns off and Teresa pulls the white hotel towel from the hanging rack and wraps her hair in it. She then takes another and wraps it around her body and steps out of the bathroom. Once she looks toward her bed, she sees Jack laying back on it making himself all nice and cozy. His white snakeskin boots are lying beside the bed and his hat lying over his midsection. Yes, he is clothed. Jacks hands are behind his head and he is staring at her with a grin. Teresa walks past the bed for her suitcase and returns to the bathroom shutting the door behind her. “That’s not funny Ace. Keep dreaming” Jack sits up in the bed returning his black Stetson hat on his head and walks to the closed bathroom door. “Your phone rang earlier. I hope you didn’t mind me answering” Teresa slips her jeans over her hips and buttons them at her waist. She reaches for her bra inside her suitcase but somehow cannot find it. “Well, who was it? If it was the Office clan again, did you tell them not to worry I’m not backing out on my match Tuesday?” Jack looks at the closed door confused but at the same time amused that she doesn’t have the slightest idea whom called her. “What you talking about Teresa? You know they wouldn’t talk you out of a match unless they have something up their sleeves.” “C’mon Jack, haven’t you heard the talk in the locker room? Practically no one thinks I have a chance at the title. Sorry bud, I don’t kiss ass to get what I want. I kick ass” “Well Teresa, you do have a point, but that’s not who called.” Teresa now opens the door, fully dressed in a black Budweiser tee shirt and loose fitting Levi’s. The towel is off her head and her wet hair is hanging down her back. She reaches for the comb out of her back pocket and begins to comb her hair while looking at Jack. “Well, Don’t just stand there like an idiot. Tell Me.” Jack lowered his head pretending to be sympathetic and fakes a sigh. “It was Scott”. Teresa’s eyes light up though she tries to hide her emotions. “Scott? Why the hell didn’t you knock on the door? What did you tell him?” Jack reaches for Teresa’s hands. She tries to pull them away but he holds on tighter while staring straight at her pretending to be all serious. “Teresa, I’m sorry. I told him you were in the shower. He said it was ok and that he hoped he’d get your voicemail anyway. He said that he’s doing fine and he’s engaged.” Teresa stares blanking at Jack. Using all her strength, she jerks her hand out of his and rushes to her cell phone. Jack runs after her and grabs her arm. “Teresa, don’t. You’ll only hurt yourself if you call him. He’s over you Hun, now it’s time to get over him.” Teresa glares at Jack with a wild flaming stare. “Who’s the bitch? I’ll whip the tar out of her. Don’t you think I won’t.” Jack steps back releasing her arm. He knows she would take it hard, but the outcome isn’t quite what he had expected. He just knew she would do the usual female thing and cry on his shoulder, then he’d console her and boy would he ever. Thing don’t always happen your way. “It’s a waste of time Teresa. She’s just some crew girl. He didn’t tell her name. And besides, you have your big match in a few days. Remember what you always said? Business before pleasure? Business is business and all that?” Teresa closes her eyes taking a deep breath. Jack’s words were a sword right through her heart. The more he keeps talking about Scott, the deeper the sharp edge grinds. “I’m a tough girl Jack. Remember that.” Teresa picks up her phone and dials Scott’s number. When she gets the busy signal, she presses the end button and throws the phone. It misses Jack’s head by a hair and hits the wall falling to the floor in three pieces. “Oh damn. Time for a new phone. You’re right Jack. Business first. Then I’m gonna really kick some ass.” Jack watches Teresa storm out of the hotel room door and slamming it shut causing the mirror above the dresser to rattle. He reaches in his back pocket and pulls out a white lace bra with a girn. That was her only one too. Poor thing, mad torn, hurt and no bra for support.
