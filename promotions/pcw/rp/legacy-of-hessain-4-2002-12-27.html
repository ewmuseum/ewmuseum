Date: 27/12/02 Legacy of Hessain Part 4: Disguise Recap: Hessain has been charged with the mission of killing Skitzrapto, Nosgoth's resident psychic. After Aimziel was murdered, her lover, Skitzrapto, went insane and unleashed a psychic attack on Nosgoth's most powerful sorcerers and fighters. As a result, their magic is now being used to poison Nosgoth. Hessian's priority is to cut the madness at the source... Skitzrapto's head. * * * My quest, as Aimziel had charged me, was to find the madman Skitzrapto and to put him out of Nosgoth's misery. The journey would take me from the pillars to the West, to Nosgoth's armpit - Steinchencröe. I arrived in the town during the day. The sun, which the Necroprankster has protected me from, would give no happiness to this area. Foul, abundant with vermin that walks on both four legs and two, Steinchencröe carried a distinct odour in the air. Nevertheless, I had to pass through these dull and horrid streets to gain way to Skitzrapto's keep in the West. I hungered for blood like the little fledgling I was and as much as my body ached, I would not dine on such dirty plates. However, inside the dankest corner of this cesspool, I saw an oddity. Sitting against a grey stone wall and covered in filthy brown rags that barely covered enough of the caked flesh was someone outcast from the outcasts... grinding a tree branch around and around in endless circles inside a sickening stale pot, this man looked up at me, recognising what I was instantly. How could he not? My fangs and dead skin were immediate clues as to my true form. But instead of running away, the senile man simply grabbed a rat from the floor. In desperation that turned even MY stomach, he clamped his teeth around the beast's neck and the rat jerked once with a squeak of pain... then went limp. Tossing the rodent away, he spat the head directly into the pot in front of him and looked up at me briefly, stirring the ghastly mixture. 'You were expecting me ta run in fear, vampire?' he wheezed. 'I've nothing left ta lose...and ye may take as much of my blood as ye can handle.' 'I care not for anything of your disgusting veins, foul one.' I replied to the stench before me. 'You have absolutely nothing of interest to me.' 'Oh, I beg ta differ. There's a little trick ye can use when ye need to gain an audience...' cackled the lunatic. With a wave of his hands, he surrounded himself in an illusion that beguiled even my eyes. Sitting in the same place and position as the peasant was a nobleman like myself. His white face looked as smooth as silk and the matted locks of ungroomed hair sprung into curls of sandy golden blonde. The rags that clung to him were whisked away to be replaced by exquisite lace and royal attire. Even the tree branch had transformed into a golden scepter, encrusted with rubies and emeralds. 'What is this trickery?!' I stammered, taken aback by this sudden magic. 'One day, good sir, a gypsy stopped unto me and offered me a trade. He told me that if I surrendered to him all that I own, I would be able to leave Steinchencröe with ten times what I owned. Sure enough, my cart was his and his magic was mine. I merely wait for King Shigeru to pass through this place where I may pose as one of his men, stranded. Remember, vampire... there are always those who'll talk to you...' and with that, he transformed back into his ragged, sorry state. 'As long as ye know how ta look...' Curious as his babblings were, it was also incredibly convenient. The Gypsy camp lay on my way to Skitzrapto's keep, to the town of Vasserbunde. Saying my farewells to the stinking man, I quickly rushed out of the pit they called Steinchencröe. Upon reflection, perhaps the town should have been called Stench-encröe. But nevertheless, Vasserbunde and the gypsy camps would call my name next. My mouth was beginning to ache with the lack of blood. The gypsies, full of superstition and paranoia, would finally have reason to speak to travelers about the fearsome vampire menace...
