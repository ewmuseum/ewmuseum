My motives and existence is for Celestial purposes only…not to bid any sort of personal desire into the lesser beings’ lives. But I was tired of watching…I felt like participating. I also heard some of the higher-ranking Celestials mention him as a hindrance to their entire existence. They were the top of the godly ladder…and they talked about something being a burden to their existence? That power intrigued me. I wanted to know more. It wasn’t long before I was scanning every detail of information that passed through my universal sea of knowledge, obtaining little by little of the entity known as the Neo Void God. I learned of his purpose…of how he came to be…of how he can become stronger…of how he can become higher than even the Celestials. Celestials in fact were nothing except a classification of gods, not select to a certain region, religion, or mythology like Egyptian, Greek, or Pagan gods. Celestials existed with the universe…and existed with all forms of the classifications. But Akiyama…he clearly wasn’t the same as the rest of the Celestials. After processing the text of the Pages of Miyuki, the Celestial goddess of universal information, I found that myths written of the Neo Void God told him to be a hybrid of both the universal collective of positive and negative energy, or the Aura and the Void. This came to be true when I learned of Akiyama’s Celestial parents…Offering and Commission, the Celestial goddess of the Void and the Celestial god of the Aura. His mortal parents proved to be merely the providers of the physical essence…the Celestial parents provided the mental, spiritual, and overall Celestial essence of the being entirely. It was only after a certain period of mortality did the nVg finally come aware of his godhood and claim his birthright. The myth of the Neo Void God also told of him being an adaptive creature…where the gods were given their powers from birth, and maintained the same level of power throughout that seemingly eternal lifespan, the Neo Void God had the ability to increase his powers continuously in his existence. He could do it through force, by claiming a critical part of an individual’s essence… Just as he took Pharaoh so called “godhood”… …Or he could do it by faith, where an individual could offer a piece of them to him to increase his abilities. Either way, it has been determined: The nVg does not need your faith to exist. That rule exists for all Celestials, but Akiyama enforces it. Another piece of the nVg mythology is his purpose. It’s a question that has baffled many people as they plan on taking out the Neo Void God…a foolish thing to try no matter who you think you are. Upon many occasions, the god has made his purpose known that he wishes to destroy this universe; to bring it into a universal harmony. The details about it are uncertain…to you people, anyway. I know just exactly how he’s going to do it. I leaned all this information about this creature…this being made out to be the proverbial monster in the gods’ closet. But someone opened the closet…for some strange reason…and now the monster was out…and there was nothing that anyone could do to stop him. I wanted the Neo Void God as my own… That power…it was possible that it dwarfed my own. I was not imprisoned because I was some dinky telephone psychic. I was the best in existence…pushing out about 999 pounds of gravitational pressure per square inch. If I desired, I could bring down a skyscraper in seconds with my mind alone…level cities in a matter of hours…topple countries in a matter of a few days… Maybe I’d get to try it out someday…maybe on this wretched country that decided to imprison me in this basement for most of my life. “Cy…” I knew that voice…it was Matrix. Matrix was the computer genius working with the Executives in the Department of Divine Examination. While he wasn’t exactly an Executive like the rest of them, he was an important person in the DDE. He was in charge of maintaining my containment in the third sub basement of the DDE building, along with any other projects tacked onto the job. He was young, about 19 years, and already deep into things most mortals never dreamed of. I could see out of the frosted glass separating me from the outside world a tall man wearing all black with short black hair.
