Stones.

Date: not entered We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he grinds his teeth together in anger, his eyes aflame with rage. His hands balled into ever-tightening fists, he drinks in the bitter scene before him, bile rising in his throat in response. “BWAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Now the tables have been turned, Big Alan DWEEB!! He that was once the hunted, has gained control, now holding the fate of that which the hunter holds most dear!!” The voice sounds like stones grating against each other and managing to spew out words in a gravelly manner. The limited light glistens on the giant black granite headed man on the platform, who throws his head back in obscene laughter, each cackle sending shivers down the spine of our hero. “Zeus had warned me of such days…” growls Big Al. Deep within the secret lair of The Moa, a cavern carved from the hills surrounding San Jose, All Hades has broken loose. After a long and involved chase through downtown San Jose, foiling a bank robbery, Big Alan Steele has battled his way through entire armies of big-headed minions, looking as if they had just stepped off the shores of Easter Island. They possessed incredible strength, yet lacked normal balancing capabilities possessed by most humans, leaving the city of San Jose littered with giant, black heads groaning in pain. But The Moa’s huge head also contained an enormous brain, and while he had originally underestimated the tenacity and determination of our hero, Big Alan Steele, his massive melon effectively created a series of traps and escape routes to inhibit Steele, and allow him to regroup back inside his hillside lair. Inside, tremendous battles erupted, with Big Alan Steele’s brashness coming to a head-on collision with The Moa’s subterfuge, with each time, Steele’s brute strength proven the better matched between the two. But now this. Steele puts his hands to his mouth, yelling across the huge chamber. “Hang on, trusted manager Sue Saksnussum!! I shant let you fall to your impending doom!!” The brunette struggles to bring in the breath to yell, her body held in the grip of the Moa… by the throat. She manages to get enough air into her lungs to force out an audible sentence. “I didn’t expect you to, you brainless sack of doorknobs!! If you let me get killed, I’ll make it my business to see you DEAD!!” The Moa’s stony lips slide back into a malicious grin. “Delicious! I wish this moment could last forever, but unfortunately, all good things must end.” Moa holds out his huge, sculpted right arm, his grip around the neck of Sue tightening as he suspends her off the ground and holds her out off the platform. Steele eyes follow the move, and for the first time, he notices the floor around the platform. Blood-stained spikes gleam crimson on the chamber surface, forcing our hero to gasp in alarm. “Help! Save us!” “Omigod!! Don’t let us die!!” Moa grins as he looks purposely to his left and at the chord wrapped tightly around his hand. The chord stretches up towards the ceiling and dangles through a pulley, attached to a cage. A cage filled with small, precious children, their chubby little faces streaked with tears as their cage dangles precariously over a roiling vat of flesh eating acid. Steele again gasps in alarm. Moa sneers at Steele. “Did you really think you could defeat me?! Did you really think that I would allow you to gain an upper hand? Now, no matter what my fate, yours shall forever be sealed!! For you must choose! Your long-time trusted manager, Sue Saksnussum? Your ground on reality? Confidant and one true friend?” “Or do you force her to make the ultimate sacrifice, one dying for the many? Is one life worth the lives of twenty fresh-faced pre-schoolers, the very future of our world? How many of them shall become doctors? Or scholars? Or find a cure for cancer?!” “I miss my Pokemon!” a snot-faced rat whines, rubbing his runny nose against his sleeve. Moa snarls, and lets the rope slips just a tiny bit, jerking the cage. The children scream in fear, the smell of the acid wafting up to their cage. Steele’s eyes go wide, as his limited brain power smokes into overdrive. “Some Lord of Massive Craniums you have become!,” scoffs Steele, as he slowly steps forward to the platform, decreasing the distance, “For it is the weak mind that must relegate themselves to the use of hapless victims to entangle on of justice, as is I!! For all of our battles on this very day, never had I imagined that you would stoop so low as to allow innocent victims become a part of our extremely personally rivalry, begun this day! Hi-Keeba!!” “Bah! Don’t be so foolish! I am merely following a trend that has begun ever since the very first inception of the superhero! It is my God-given right to force you, the ones you love, and hapless bystanders into my plots of perilous and unlikely situations that you, yes you, the hero, must extricate EVERYONE from, thus allowing me just enough time to escape via secret passageways that only I may traverse, thereby continuing our feud another day!! I fully expect you to save them all… or at least most of them! And it is that simplistic brilliancy that shall allow me to escape!! It’s as beautiful as it is deranged, and you have only YOURSELF to blame!! BWAHAHAHAHA!!!” Steele has inched his way ever closer to the platform, his stalling technique working perfectly. Moa hasn’t even noticed. But now, Steele stops, taking in every possible aspect of the situation. The Moa’s stone face curls into a sneer, as he looks down on Big Alan Steele. “So now, I have drunk in enough of the pleasure in watching you squirm under my thumb! Now, you choose! Shall you choose your one true… or the future of the nation?!?!” Big Al tenses in anticipation, “By the gods, you’re mad!! Possessed by the demons of Hades!!” Sue’s eyes are bugging out and turning crimson from lack of oxygen… but she’s a heavy smoker, so lack of oxygen isn’t a real problem. Big Al has seen that look before, the few times he disappointed her in the ring with a sub-par performance. Pure, unbridled anger, capable of destroying everything in its path. Her eyes fixate on the giant head of The Moa… The giant head rolls back in laughter. Time seems to stand still as Steele sharp eyes watch Moa’s hands flinch, setting the plan into motion… We now re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he sits on a nearby park bench next to his trusted manager, Sue Saksnussum. Seemingly thousands of swirling blue and red lights illuminate the night as the San Jose Police Department pulls clean-up duty on the rampage that was Moa vs. BAS. Sue calmly sips at a coffee while simultaneously smoking a cigarette, no small task, while Big Al is debriefed by one of SJPD’s finest. Steele looks uncomfortable, wringing his hands in what looks to be shame. “So,” The young officer presses, “What happened next?” Big Al breathes deeply, adjusting the collar of his toga. “Well… er… tis an admittedly difficult situation to discuss. I mean… the children…” Sue fails to suppress an evil giggle, garnering the consternation of both men in her presence. She sighs and drains the last of her cup before speaking. “Criminies, sometimes I think all you men are nothing but a bunch of pansies.” The blue boy scowls at the insult, “Ma’am, I hope you realize the gravity of the situation. This is no laughing matter!! Tonight was a great tragedy!” “What manner of sins must a man commit in a single lifetime to earn such a terrible burden such as mine!” bemoans our bohunkular hero, “If there was only more that I could have done!!” The sympathetic officer pats Steele on his shoulder (like rock.) “It’s okay, Big guy. You did all you could…” Sue stands up, her face twisted into a condescending scowl. “What?!?! I don’t GET IT!! We WON!!” Big Alan Steele buries his face into his hands. “But at what cost!! AT WHAT COST!!!” A team of paramedics exit the cavernous lair, The Moa crying out in high-pitched screams of pain, his face chiseled into a slab of pain. His one eye cracks open, and focuses on the three at the park bench. In a girlie voice, Moa squeals at the top of his lungs as he clutches at his groin. “YOU’LL PAY FOR THIS, IF IT’S THE LAST THING I EVER DO!!! MY FUTURE CHILDREN!! WHY?! WHY?!?!” Sue blows out a plume of smoke, a slow grin spreading across her face. “Looks like he wasn’t all made entirely out of stone after all.” Sue glances over her shoulder in satisfaction at Steele and the officer, both suffering through sympathy pains and clutching at their batches. Sue again frowns. “Will you guys knock it off? Everything is fine!! Big Al did something suitably heroic and saved the kiddies… and I made it easier on him by taking out Moa.” Steele winces in pain yet again, “Tonight’s victory was a shallow one, in that many rules of noble combat were broken… as well as other notables.” “Ahhh!!” The young cop clasps his hands over his ears, “Can we talk about something else?!” A female officer comes over and grins appreciatively at Sue. The two share a female moment and cackle with glee, exchanging high fives. “You go, girl!! All the other female officers are proud of ya!!” Steele hangs his head in embarrassment, the male officer clearing his throat and trying to get back to the matter at hand. “Okay… so, uh, how does this relate to you refereeing the Malice Crew and Authority match tomorrow?” “It doesn’t,” Sue replies, “So let’s not pretend it does. Now c’mon, Al! I’VE got some celebrating to do!!” Steele breathes deeply, and lets out a long, regretful sigh, “Hi-Keeba!!”
