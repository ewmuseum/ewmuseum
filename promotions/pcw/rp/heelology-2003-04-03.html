HEELOLOGY

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03 April 2003 Primetime Championship Wrestling has seen it all. From the complexity of orchestrating a master plan in a bid to swallow the largest entity in the wrestling industry to the washed up airtime allocated to its cretins who do more time rambling than they do performing. The lowest of these would bark nonsense week in and week out on how they have either been oppressed or ridiculed, concluding that they will wreak revenge when in reality the only thing that wreaks is their constant drivel. It is this type of talent, a word I use loosely that brings the product down. Even those fortunate to climb from the depths of the afterbirth bucket can be entertaining. Even those with a brain rivaled only by that of a dinosaur can work a crowd, be it in a negative or positive manner but then there are those who appear transparent in front of twenty thousand wrestling fanatics who can only muster an eerie silence. For these there is no Phoenix scenario where from the pits of jobber hell they will be reborn into productive products but the agonizing green mile of knowing that their careers are nothing more than ticking time bombs. Primetime Championship Wrestling has both welcomed and waved by this type of individual but now the company has an arsenal of competitors that could steam train the outside competition. There isn’t a single piece of deadweight roaming the locker room, a horse shafted the last of these, another suffered the indignity of live liposuction and from the aftermath the company now has a credible roster. The fans don’t love a Champion, they love to see a Champion fail… More importantly they love to see a face fail for all their jeering is nothing more than camouflaged admiration. Admittedly they cheer and spurn on their favourites but their allegiance is only to put them on top of a pedestal so high that the only route left is to nose dive. Their admiration is nothing more than a catalyst for the conceited heel for their chants are subliminal messages to these heels, messages demanding the next heinous tactic. Every heel no matter of stature within the company has tried something different from the next yet those more prominent in the Main Event scene have no choice but to go that extra mile. Kidnapping Hostile Take-Over Sickening and malicious injury Even the buying out of another company only to abolish it out of spite. Not the most common but something the wrestling world witnessed when Primetime Championship Wrestling engulfed the assets of RUSH and later abandoned for the product had run its course. In some cases these heels have been known to murder in cold blood and the company idly stands by for these mishaps bring ratings and it’s evidence can be swept under the carpet by two words, ‘Occupational Accident’. If what I preach is fabrication then explain why your knight in shining armour, by that I mean the faces of the roster eventually fizzle out and succumb to the dark side? The heroic face has its perks but how many solid faces have remained loyal to their alignment and not jump ship to the idea of being a heel. Being a heel opens far more doors, you tend to test the boundaries of the law and maybe it’s this adrenaline rush that fuels the greats. However there are all kinds of heels. There are those whose idea of generating heat is by the use of profanity and in exceptional cases there are those who have the ability to mastermind and fulfil devious acts of a grander scale. It is these players who can deem themselves, Evil Super Geniuses. The genesis of this concept was founded by Alan Kriegman who arguably is the greatest heel mastermind to lace a wrestling boot. A brain that dreamt and created the infamous stable known as the Think Tank, the man who wormed his way into power as President of Old School Entertainment and ruled with an iron fist. Most importantly the person who recently liberated the Universal Championship from the strangle hold of Pro Wrestling Circuit’s resident mobster Angelo Deville. Difficult shoes to fill one may say but filled they were. For Tyler Nelson stepped up to the batting plate and picked up where his predecessor had left off. Another role model for heels everywhere, the Greediest Player In The Game like the man before him bullied his way into power under the banner of Media. Leading a consortium Tyler bought Primetime Championship Wrestling and in doing so took control of the World Heavyweight Title. A man that has been around the block and then some, firstly he seized control of Primetime Championship Wrestling and in doing so made his own personal playground before being overthrown by his very own protégé. Another case of the student surpassing the mentor but before his illustrious legacy fizzles out Tyler Nelson had take his product over to Five Star Wrestling with similar effects and after the promotion had run its course the Greediest Player In The Game had already made the transition to Old School Entertainment. Achieving what many others had failed, Tyler retired one of the businesses greatest faces in Kid Wonder yet his thirst for more hadn’t been quenched and so the GREATEST HEEL Tyler Nelson now hopes he can make that a double retirement as he goes against arguably the GREATEST FACE Ivan Stanislav. However if Tyler Nelson was such a great in his role of a heel, what happened to the man who dethroned his rule of tyranny? Quite simply his protégé achieved everything his mentor had and possibly more. Xavier Kannon plotted behind his mentors back, biding his time and his time came after the Scientologist retired one of the greatest faces ever in Jeremy Howard at Spring Fling. It was this one victory that fuelled Xavier Kannon into making a beeline for the World Heavyweight Championship and at Third Dawning he stripped Tyler Nelson both of his title and company. Not impressed? Well consider the fact that he also lined his own pocket with funds allocated to the workforce and formed one of the greatest stables of it’s era in The Front Office, Xavier Kannon accomplished what no other heel before him could and that was to swallow and digest another company. Worryingly more so were his motives, for he done this purely to prove that he could. Now what does all that needless information has to do with a Jester? Well all the above are role models for the heels of tomorrow and arguably each has surpassed the next, so the real question is ‘Who will carry the torch when Xavier Kannon parts company with Primetime Championship Wrestling?’ Well it isn’t something that’s happening anytime soon but that fateful day shall come and all the attributes of these great heels I see in myself. Yet to showcase them to a wider audience I’ve covered the fundamentals in kidnapping, injury and have even married my nemesis to simply toy in her personal relationship. The advantage I have over many others in similar circumstances is that I have already had a successful business relationship with PCW’s alpha male and like he this journey starts with the capture of the covenant Cruiserweight Championship. Come Friday Fusion, Triple Threat, Pharaoh versus Dante Jackson versus Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green could quite possibly be the first signpost in the long journey of becoming the next Xavier Kannon.
