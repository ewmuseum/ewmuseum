Maybe Alive, Maybe Not

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/02 There is actually a need for some recap now. The last we saw of Rush was a terrifying, shocking ending to a strange day of events. In escaping from his kidnappers, Rush tried to make an almost impossible swerving turn in a speeding BMW, which resulted in the car swerving, flying through a guardrail at a high speed, crashing through the large forest, barely missing a lake, sliding down a steep rock slope, and finally smashing into an outcropping of rocks, smashing head-on into the rocks. Now Rush is presumed dead, though his body has not been found, and his location before he died is not even certain. Back at PCW Headquarters, a conference room is buzzing, with mostly reporters and a few obscure faces, all talking amongst themselves, waiting for any news. They haven’t heard the news yet, but they’ve heard that an important announcement is coming from PCW on this night. Lots of news anchors are getting make up done on them, testing their microphones, or something else getting ready for the show. It’s known to most of the people in PCW, and many of these reporters who cover sports, that recently Rush was recently given the right of godhood by fellow god, Akiyama. Apparently the kidnapping has something to do with this. The small crowd quiets as the Pimply-Faced Intern Kid walks through the large security doors, followed by two very large security guards, who step in front of the door, blocking all access to it. The PFIK steps up to the podium, with a grave look on his face, a strange sight for such a look on a young person’s face. Everything is silent in the room except for the rolling cameras. Everyone’s eyes are on the young man, as he tries to gain composure to speak to the crowd. He takes a deep breath and addresses the small crowd. PFIK: Welcome, and thank you for taking time out of your busy schedules to come and hear this special briefing straight from the police dispatches in Maniwa, Ohio. There was a death in the PCW family reported earlier this afternoon. A gasp goes thorough the audience. Whispers go around the room about who the deceased might be. As PFIK tries to regain control of the discussion, the room quiets down once again. All ears are on him, waiting for a name to roll off of his lips: Krayzie? Clyde? Miyagawa? Possibly even Snow? PFIK: Rush Mason, known to PCW fans as the Rush, was killed in what is being called a fatal car accident. Those words stun, and bring a rise from the small crowd. Reporters raise their hands to ask questions but PFIK doesn’t take any, but continues to address the crowd. A boy, of eleven or so, who came to hear about the signing of an important new superstar, or a match for March Madness, cries off in a corner. No one comforts him, as he is there alone. He sobs quietly, and the PFIK looks at him, their eyes meeting for a moment, and then PFIK goes back to his speech. PFIK: Much like the killing of Daniel Pearl, Rush was kidnapped. The kidnapping took place less than 48 hours ago, and is suspected to be work of religious fanatics, possibly even Satanists. No major suspects have come forward or been discussed yet, as most people are still in a state of shock. The cameras continue to roll, as many of the reporters, grim-faced, turn towards their cameras and begin to give small monologues to go along with the report and the footage that just went out on many cable channels. The PFIK waits until the reporters have stopped their talking, and once again addresses the saddened crowd. PFIK: We at PCW, though Rush was relatively new, are very saddened by this incident and our deepest condolences go out to his brother and cousins. On the PCW website, Rush’s roster page will be turned into a memorial for the fallen wrestler. Still shocked at the terrible news, many of the reporters shut off the cameras, not wishing to bring any more pain to fans or family members. However, one reporter asks a very bold question. Female Reporter: This doesn’t seem like the right time or place, but is PCW still continuing with the March Madness Pay Per View? PFIK: We will be continuing our normal schedule of programming, as we think that would be best for grieving family, fans and wrestlers. We also believe that Rush would’ve wanted for us to continue in such a hard time. The reporters sit back down, jotting notes onto her small notepad. The PFIK looks around, for any more questions from the media. A balding male reporter stands up, to ask another question. Male Reporter: With all due respect to Mr. Mason and his friends, he is not nearly a big enough part of PCW to cancel a Pay Per View event for that is already sold out. However, is there any chance of his death being anything other then a drive gone wrong? PFIK: Police will be investigating in the coming weeks. One other car, stripped of plates and any identifying items, was found in the woods less than a mile away from the crash site. However, the crash site itself cannot be investigated yet, because tonight is very windy and the wind makes a trip down the rocky slope very dangerous. The reporter sits back down, he too scribbling away on a small pad. PFIK looks around for any final remarks or questions, and seeing as there are none, he silently steps down from the podium, heading back through the doors that security still has blocked. However, this time the camera follows him as he makes his way down the hall. Coming into view of the camera is NTICW wrestler (and Ron’s personal biatch until 2004) Semore Smoke. PFIK walks up to Xavier Kannon’s lemming who is wearing a black unbuttoned button-down short sleeve shirt, black loose-fitting pants, and tan Timberland boots. PFIK: Semore Smoke, it’s good to see that some people are still around here on this tragic day. Were you closely associated with Rush? Semore: Well, no. Rush was, however from the Dirty South and representing North Carolina up in PCW, so I’ve gotta show him some love now that he’s gone. I mean, he kept it gangster with Black Attack, but I wasn’t really a big fan of his new stuff, all this godly crap. But I’m not gonna speak badly about him now that he’s dead. PFIK: Is there anything you wish you could say to Rush if he was here right now? Anything that you never told him and wanted to? Semore: Nothing particular. I’d tell him to cut the divine stuff, because all it’s going to do is make him look silly. I suppose I would’ve congratulated him as well for making it up to PCW, where I hope to go someday. PFIK: Thank you Semore. For the rest of the 30 minutes scheduled for this week’s PCW recap, we will be talking to friends of Rush behind the scenes, or any wrestlers who have something they wish to say to his family or anything they think about the dearly departed. As PFIK walks into a large, open locker room, a few superstars can be seen around the room. The first one he goes to talk to is Ben J. Pailthorpe, wrestler in GWF and commentator for RUSH Wrestling. PFIK: Ben, were you closely associated with Rush? Ben: It’s a conspiracy, goddamnit! PFIK: What’s a conspiracy? Ben: Rush isn’t dead, you fool! This is all a cover-up! Rush is being snuffed out because of his godly ways and PCW is trying to hide it! If you can’t see it, you must be blind. PFIK: Don’t you think that’s a little outrageous? I mean, Tyler Nelson has done some crazy things, but do you really think he would get someone to kill Rush? Ben: It wasn’t Nelson, it was someone else… Ben has some very insightful thinking towards the recent death of Rush Mason. In fact, some of it may not be all wrong. However, the fact that he is a conspiracy nerd makes PFIK walk away, while he mutters something about the moon landing. PFIK spots another person to talk to, none other then an infamous killer himself. A man who was involved with NTICW right before it shut down: The Quiet Killer, Corg. He is wearing no shirt, but he is covered with black pants and black wrestling boots at the moment. PFIK: C-c-Corg, how nice of you to pay your respects to the deceased. Did you and Rush become close in NTICW? Corg: No we didn’t. I became a champion, and I suppose we shared that bond, but nothing else ever allowed us to meet, therefore we never talked much. He was arrogant, just like all of them… PFIK: Don’t you think that’s a bit harsh, especially for a man who is presumed dead after an awful auto accident? You can’t have a little bit of mercy for a man who just died? Corg: We all die, that’s life. The dreams I have are different then yours, but I am no different than you. I will die, you will die. Except, one can only truly appreciate life once having taken it from others. Only a killer will ever understand what it means to die… PFIK, scared of the gothic warrior backs away as Corg starts to chant that last sentence to himself. PFIK backs up until he is far enough away to run, but he backs up right into a figure not seen for a long time, none other then PCW favorite Argyle. PFIK: Argyle? You’ve come to pay respects to Rush Mason? Argyle: Actually I was coming to hype a comeback I was thinking about doing, right after I die. PFIK: Excuse me?! Argyle: Well, see I’ve figure it out finally. If you die, your popularity skyrockets. Look at Akiyama and Darkstar. They both came back from death to be mega-popular. So if any jobber can pull it off, then why not me? PFIK: Sir, the reason they “came back” from death was because they never really died. It’s impossible to come back from death. Argyle: Not true, my acne-faced friend. I’m what you would call a nephalim. I could if I wanted. PFIK: Is that even a real word? Argyle: Who knows but that’s what I am. Just wait. After Rush comes back from the dead, I’m gonna be next in line. I call dibs on the next death angle! PFIK: Argyle, Rush is really dead. Argyle: Ok, sure. Well I gotta plan my last shot at glory, adios. PFIK shakes his head as the other man walks off. He then turns around to see another familiar figure, PCW Wrestler The End, Skylar Green. PFIK: Well, Skylar, you’ve obviously come to pay respects to Rush. You were involved in his godhood approval. Do you consider yourself one of his better friends with the federation? End: No. I have, and always will be neutral. The fact that I watched him means nothing. However, his death brings back painful memories of my own. PFIK: Not this nephalim stuff again. End: I think you would find it surprisingly believable with a katana at your throat. I have died, and I lived through it. Not everyone can be so lucky. Many are stuck with only one chance on earth, and they squander it like fools. Pathetic… PFIK: Well that’s very rude of you, but we’ve got almost no time left and I’d rather not just berate you. End: Good, because I hate cleaning the blood off my sword. PFIK gulps and walks away, turning right into the huge chest of a huge man. None other then the Neo Void God stands right in front of him, towering over the young reporter. He breathes heavily as he stares down at the small man. Akiyama: Are you going to ask us what we think of Rush, our very own creation? Do we scare you little man? PFIK, visibly frightened and shaking, replies with a very meek voice to the Japanese giant. The fear is growing in PFIK as evidenced by quivering knees. PFIK: Well, Akiyama sir, what are your thoughts on the death of a man whom you must’ve considered at least a good acquaintance, if not a friend? Akiyama: We find Rush to be humorous at times, bland at other times. However, the Void has taken him now that he has fallen into darkness, he may need a guide. Time spend in the void changes one. We know from experience. PFIK: That’s very interesting. But what do you mean, he may need a guide in the darkness? Akiyama: We mean what we say. We know the darkness as well as anyone. We have returned from the same darkness as our crunk friend, and now we may have to rescue him from a terrible trip. PFIK: Ok, I’m not sure what that means, but thank you for taking time to talk to us about the loss of Rush. Akiyama walks away, without replying to the PFIK, not looking back at the smaller man. Akiyama talks to himself as he walks off down a dimly lit hallway. Akiyama: To help him, we would have to find the darkness where he resides. For all darkness is not the same darkness. Maybe we will find him in our own Void. Maybe not. Maybe he is alive, Maybe not… The scene ends with these words echoing throughout the hallway with the steps of Akira Akiyama down the hallway, as the scene fades out, into nothing but black…
