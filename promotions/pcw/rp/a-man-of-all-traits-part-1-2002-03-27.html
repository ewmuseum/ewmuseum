A Man Of All Traits Part 1

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 27th, 2002 (Mike is seen sitting with Michael Graves at a circular table in a recreation hall. A magician is on stage and wows the audience with his ability to pull off spectacular illusions. Mike watches intrigued. Graves too looks on in amazement. Mike turns to Graves.) Mike Frampton: Man, I am sick and tired of being a simple wrestler. I want to do something new. Something like that. (Mike points to the magician.) Michael Graves: A magician eh? Why not? I always wanted to dress up in those sequence dresses and assist a magician. Mike Frampton: Stop making passes at me Graves. (Mike slaps Graves on the back of the head.) Mike Frampton: However I am going to need a partner. (Graves smiles at Mike.) Mike Frampton: Alright fine you can be my assistant. Go rent that video tape that was on fox with all of the tricks revealed. Michael Graves: Yes sir! (Graves rushes out the door as the scene fades out to white. A moment later the scene fades back in to Michael Graves who is with Mike Frampton. Many props are laid out infront of them such as a box with a redline down the middle, knives, chainsaws, saws, a box of doves, and a guillotine. Mike picks up the box of Doves.) Mike Frampton: Now Graves, we will start off the show with the simple releasing of the doves from an empty box, do you get it? Michael Graves: Yes I do. I hand you the box with nothing in it, then I switch it very quickly. Mike Frampton: Good job, afterward we move onto the sawing of you in half. (Graves swallows hard.) Michael Graves: You sure you know what you are doing? Mike Frampton: Of course I do! Never question a magician. Now, get in the box. Michael Graves: But... Mike Frampton: GET! (Graves slowly climbs into the box. Mike shuts the top.) Mike Frampton: Do you have your feet curled up? Michael Graves: Yup, cut away. (Mike begins to saw as the scene fades out to white. A moment later the scene fades back in to the recreation hall. A sign reading 'Witness the magic of Frampton' is posted at the back. A crowd of people wait for Frampton and Graves. Graves walks out onto stage in a red sequence gown. The crowd laughs. Graves grabs a microphone.)Michael Graves: Ladies and gentlemen, presenting, the master of illusions, the original magician, the worlds first wrestler illusionist, the first... (Someone throws a cup at Graves.) Michael Graves: Mike Frampton! (Frampton walks out onto the stage to a few applause. He picks up a box. He opens it to show it is empty.) Mike Frampton: Now it is empty. (Mike closes it and opens it. The crowd screams. Two dead doves flop out onto the stage.) Mike Frampton: Who knew you had to feed magical birds? (Graves takes the box.) Mike Frampton: Now ladies and gentlemen, I will saw my partner in half! (Graves steps into the box. Mike pulls up a chainsaw. He tugs on the cord. It begins. He puts it onto the box. Graves screams.) Michael Graves: STOP! FOR THE LOVE OF GOD STOP! (Mike pulls back the chainsaw which is stained with a single drop of red.) Mike Frampton: Did I cut you? Michael Graves: No! You hit my ketchup packages from lunch. (The crowd boos.) Mike Frampton: Ladies and gentlemen moving onto my final act. I will chop off my partners head! (Graves moves towards the guillotine. He slips and his foot enters the head hole. He pulls down the lever by accident and the blade falls down. Graves pulls his foot out but his pinky toe flies off. Blood splatters everywhere.) Mike Frampton: Ladies and gentlemen that has been our show. (The curtain comes across the stage.) Mike Frampton: GRAVES! (The scene fades out to white. The scene then fades back into an empty recreation hall. Graves and Mike are again sitting at a table. Graves foot is wrapped in a cast.) Michael Graves: Maybe you could be a comedian because that was a damn funny show! (The scene fades out as Frampton slaps Graves.)
