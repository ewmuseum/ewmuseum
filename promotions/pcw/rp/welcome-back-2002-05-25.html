Welcome Back

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/25/02 The scene opens up to the cackling of a furious fire blazing against the calm wind. The sun has cowered away, and the moon stands out glowing, taking its place. Many faces are gathered around the fire, celebrating. The camera pans around and then stops on a man wearing no shirt, long, tattered, baggy blue jeans, and an old fashioned Indian headdress which stands at least two feet tall. It's Kramer, whose eyes reflect the fire making him look deviant. He lets out a battle cry and drums an open hand across his mouth several times as he tosses a handful of papers into the fire, feeding it. Besides the noise of the fire, and Kramer bellowing out, a booming shout can be heard which breaks up everyone's concentration on the fire, including Kramer. Voice: IT'S HERE!!! The crowd that was once gathered around the fire, now rush all in the same direction with looks of excite emblazoned in their eyes. Kramer somehow cuts in front of the pack and stands like a statue in front of them, before they can enter their destination, which seems to be the large wood cabin, located behind Kramer. Kramer: Remember everyone, don't let this turn you all into savages. No matter how you slice it, everyone gets the same amount; at least that's what we were promised. Now you may all pass. May the times be good to you my dear friends. Kramer steps out of the way and the people all crowd into the cabin, but what for? Our minds are racing endlessly as Kramer continues to stand just looking around. He makes a hacking sound and launches a huge glob of spit towards a tree several feet away. The missile hits and splatters all over. He then turns and walks into the cabin. As he slowly pushes the doors in our eyes are engulfed with a lot of activity! We can tell that this must be some party due to strobe lights flashing, music blaring, and people socializing. Kramer pushes into through the crowd of people giving multiple high fives and shouts along the way. He then pulls his way towards the food and beverage table. Normally at parties you expect alcohol right? Well not at a Kramer party. Instead of alcohol there are bottles of water, various soft drinks, and of course a giant bowl of red punch with lemon halves floating along the top. Kramer, after a minute of deciding, grabs a bottle of water and jumps back into the crowd bobbing his head to the music. A familiar short, stubby, figure steps up towards Kramer and taps him on the shoulder. Kramer whips around and sees Alexander who looks slightly rugged with an untucked white buttoned down shirt, and the 'I haven't shaven in days' look. Kramer: Jason? May you look terrible. Jason: Well I've been through hell the past week, so I think I deserve to. Kramer: Follow me into the 'Quiet Room'. Jason follows Kramer through the crowd, getting pushed and punched a couple of times with elbows. They go to the far back of the cabin to a door. Kramer pulls it open and steps in with Jason at his heels. It's a nice little room with a curtained window, red and white rugs on the floor, a spinning computer chair, as well as a big brown couch. Kramer jumps towards the spinning chair and goes flying around the room. Jason sprawls out on the couch and sits holding his head. Kramer, having enough fun for the moment, stops the chair and parks it a few feet away from Jason. Kramer: Did you by any chance happen to get a call from PCW headquarters? Jason looks over and gives a stern no. Kramer: You neither huh? Jason: I don't see why you didn't make the cut Kramer. I mean you aren't that great, but you are decent. You are the type of wrestler people pay to see get beaten up. Kramer nods as the last thing does make sense. Jason: How could they not want the attention that us being associated with KFC brings? Kramer listens on trying to keep a burst of laughter from gushing out of his mouth. He also waits to see whatever else Jason says, before he tells him the truth about being in PCW. Jason on the other hand looks near enraged as he talks on. Jason: Besides if they didn't want your Indian ass, the least they could have done was hire me to be a manager for someone else! Instead I get pushed aside and thrown out having to fend off tax collectors..... Kramer: Uh Jason. Jason: WHAT!? Kramer: It's only been a week. You can't already be broke. Jason: Well I will be in the future since I'm out of a job. Kramer: Jason I have a confession to make. As he says this he starts to giggle a little. Jason: What's so funny? Kramer: Well, the truth is.....I did get back into PCW. And as a matter of fact my first match up is on Turmoil and its for the Cruiserweight Title. Jason jumps up and starts strangling Kramer. Kramer flails around and finally throws him away towards the couch. Kramer jumps to his feet rubbing his neck as he looks at Jason. Kramer: What the hell was that? Jason: You let me go on and on like that and you didn't tell me? I was down and you didn't have the decensy to tell me that we still have a job? Kramer: It sorta slipped my mind. Jason's frown flips upside down into a smile. He then charges at Kramer, nearly knocking him down with a hug. Jason: I have a job!!! I won't be poor!!! The five second hug fest finally ends, and Jason tucks in his shirt making him look presentable. Jason: So who is this unlucky bastard that you have to go up against? Kramer: Well its a tournament type thing. There will be three matches where its two on two action. Then at the end of the night, the three winners battle it out for the gold. Jason: Your first opponent would be? Kramer: The Multiple Man. Jason: Well Kramer, buddy, pal. You understand what you have to do right? Kramer: Ummm..... Jason: You have to kick his multiple ass all over the ring, and then move onto the triple threat match and kick their asses too! Kramer: Sounds like a plan to me. They both walk out of the room and back to the party which is still jamming. Kramer walks over to the music and turns it off. Instantly he is booed. With a deep booming voice he yells. Kramer: May I have everyone's attention please? The dancers stop, the chattering stops, and everyone's eyes become transfixed on Kramer. Kramer: First let me thank you all for coming to my 'Welcome Back To PCW Party'. It wouldn't have been a blast without you, and I want to thank you for the support. Before you get back to having a good time I have something to say. On Tuesday Turmoil, I start wrestling again. Now on that day I have the chance to obtain some gold that I once held, the Cruiserweight Title. As you all probably know, it's been a while since I have accomplished something so great. My mission is to win that belt and prove to the world that I still have what it takes. Now I know it won't be easy, because there are great athletes involved, but I am going to give it my all even if it means pushing my body to the limits. With that being said, LET'S PARTY!! Kramer blares up the music and jumps into the crowd who gives him cheers and handshakes. If this was a welcome back party, how did Jason find out? Well to make a long story short, he was picking up empty pop cans to trade in for money, and he spotted Kramer's car, so he went in. The scene slowly fades to black.
