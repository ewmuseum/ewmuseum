The Long Journey to Discover....pt4: Rallying

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th Oct To say El Diablo is a complex being is an under statement. What man in his right mind would label everyone and everything corrupt? Ok, stupid question, El Diablo would, but why? Is everyone truly corrupt in some form or another? It's quite the possibility, and perhaps people don't even know it. Who would admit to being corrupt? Who could say with a straight face that they aren't corrupt? When people lie are we being corrupt? In some cases that is a form of corruption, but how can it be judged when something is corrupt and something isn't? That is for sure one difficult question to answer, but that is El Diablo's job! He determines who is corrupt and when they are being corrupt. Is it a hard job? Of course it must be. Why? Because by that, the truth is easy to find, and if the truth isn't pretty, it can be guaranteed that the person hiding the truth will hunt down the discoverer of the truth and eliminate them, much like governments do. Frightening in a way isn't it? But that's how it all works.----------- A low lighted room hosts a friendly poker game between El Diablo, his wife Angela and his two security guards Kurt and Leroy. But really, how friendly can a game of poker really be? Especially when large amounts of money are on the line. The room is very small, and barely holds the table they're playing on in there let alone the chairs they are seated on. The room has no wallpaper on the walls, or paint for that matter, just hard concrete walls with chips in them, and the ground isn't much better. 'I'll raise you $500,' says Kurt while he places his poker chips in the middle of the table. Kurt isn't much of a 'bluffer' in poker and that is why he'll always wear sunglasses when he plays- maybe that makes him worse though, as sometimes he has trouble reading the cards and will say he has a 'full house' when really he has two jacks and no queen. 'I'll see your $500 and raise you $500,' Leroy smiles. Leroy is a card shark. He really knows his way around a poker table, so anytime he'd raise you, you just knew that maybe you were in for a bit of trouble. Or in for a huge dent in your wallet and worse of all Leroy knew he was good. 'I call,' comments Angela, intent on taking the money. Angela really is not a card player, but loves gambling and loves money. She's also a welcome distraction to look at when playing cards, and will sometimes show cleavage to make the other players lose concentration. It often worked. 'Three aces,' El Diablo says as he lays his card hand down. El Diablo is not perfect at poker, but he's not poor at it either. Sure he doesn't have a good poker face, but he knows how to play and how to lure his opponents into defeat. Much like he does in wrestling. 'Four aces,' Kurt proudly shows. 'Damn, only two aces,' Leroy slams his cards down. 'Three aces,' Angela quips politely. It's obvious they're all cheating, but really there is no need in it, if it's only going to be a friendly game. But they all cheated anyway, without any strong logic or reason to back it up, well, except for the big heap of money in the middle of the table. And the money must quite obviously be all El Diablo's anyway. Think about it: El Diablo works for PCW and gets paid by them, Kurt and Leroy work for El Diablo and get paid by him, Angela is his wife and therefore gets money from him, so this whole poker game was all based upon El Diablo's money. 'Haha, I win,' Kurt exclaims happily and pulls the money from the center of the table into him. Well, the poker chips representing the money anyway. In the meanwhile El Diablo is seething. This is noticeable by his heavy breathing and red face. He must be angry that he lost. 'Hun, what's wrong?' Angela asks with strong concern at her husband's behaviour. She places her hands on one of his, but he doesn't even notice it. Instead El Diablo stands up and tips the table upside down in one fast motion, with the table edges only just missing contact with the other three, thanks to their quick reactions to stand up when the table started to move. Underneath the table lies cards, beer cans, poker chips and uneaten potato chips. Luckily nothing breakable was on there. 'WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH ALL OF YOU?' yells Diablo in rage, to his three compatriots, 'WHY THE HELL ARE WE PLAYING POKER IF ALL WE'RE GOING TO DO IS CHEAT?' he continues, still red in the face. Angela looks at the floor in shame, but Kurt and Leroy remain standing and eyes locked on El Diablo. 'It's just in fun,' responds Kurt trying to be calm, but uncomfortable in the current situation. The Lord of Mexico almost laughs, but instead gets an evil smirk on his face, almost like he is plotting something. 'Fun? FUN? Cheating your friends is not fun, cheating others is! That is you're damned lesson of the day. Learn it, obey it and never screw around with me again,' Diablo barks out the orders and then looks at Angela, 'Well except you, you're welcome to,' Angela looks up and smiles a bit, whereas Kurt and Leroy still aren't happy. El Diablo is almost like a dictator here, barking out orders for his generals to follow and to understand. Teaching them what is right and wrong in his own warped mind, is El Diablo the next Adolf Hitler? Surely not, but the similarities are there and the foundations are going to be built upon every day until El Diablo finally takes out all his aggression and does something insane. The day is sooner than he thinks. 'Cheating on each other is not good for us. It makes us as corrupt as society. Do you really want to be like that? Do you really want to be like everyone else?' the dictator El Diablo asks his generals. They all shake their heads and listen on to El Diablo. 'We do not want to conform and we will not conform, we are not like everyone else, we are BETTER than they are. We know the truth, whereas they do not, we know what we believe in is true and we have better values than they have. WE are not a corrupt machine, we are not a government, we are not slaves to the government like all those other vermin are, we are not slaves to the agenda, we aren't afraid to think for ourselves, we aren't afraid to question authority. Fact is, we know we are in a noble battle, and we know we will win,' rants Diablo in passion of his beliefs. His allies are now upbeat at this talk and begin to smile knowing what he is saying is true. 'We are not corrupt! Governments are, PCW is, and Clyde is, but not us. Worse of all, Clyde knows he is and continues to deny it, so now I must win to be able to face him at Alliances and kick him out of this corrupt organisation known as PCW!' finishes El Diablo in anger. Now motivated, El Diablo walks out of the poker room almost in a march with his troops behind him, leading them into a long battle in their War against PCW and corruption.
