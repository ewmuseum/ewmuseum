The Ill Effect. Part 2 of last RP

Date: 04/02-03-04-05/2002 FLUSH **Boda opens the door, and looks rather ill. So, the big bad-ass pushes the camera away, sending it tumbling to the floor in the hallway. We see Boda climb onto the bed as the scene fades out.** *This is where part one left off, last promo. Now, onto part two.* ‘The Ill Effect’ **Our scene opens where part one left off. Boda, after eating at Outback Steakhouse, was feeling a little under the weather all of a sudden. Could of been the bad food, could of been the fat chick hitting on him in the elevator. Or worse.... it could have been a case of the Chase-Promo-Itis. In any case, Boda, the World Champion mind ya, is in bed, tossing and turning. His stomach in obvious torture. On the nightstand next to the Champ, lay an empty bottle of Pepto and a half empty bottle of NightQuil. Suddenly, Chris Sloboda stops tossing, and comes to rest in the center of the bed, in an awkward fetal position. He starts to snore. This leads us into the REM mode** fuzzy video feed representing one falling into a dream *It’s a bright and early morning. The dew on the grass glistens. The red barn in the distance comes alive with the mooing of the cows, the neighing of the horses. A rooster screams...........* COCKA DOODLE DO....................COCKA DOODLE DO.................... *The rooster crawls back into his pen, as a light inside a two story farmhouse comes on. The light is in the kitchen. A shadow walks past the window, giving us very little detail as to who the farmer is [as if we don’t know]. After a short five minutes, the light goes out, and the back door opens. Out steps Boda. Only, Boda is not in his usual attire. No, he is in blue overalls. He is wearing a straw hat, and has a piece of hay sticking from his mouth, like a cigarette. Farmer Boda lets out a stretch, and a growl. He scratches at his nuts, as most men do in the morning. Boda sniffs the farm fresh air* Farmer Boda: “Well, it’s going to be a beautiful day. Time to get to milking my girls.” *Farmer Boda walks towards the red barn. He opens the front doors, and the rustling of the cattle can be heard. Boda walks to his prize heifer, and pats her on the ass.* Farmer Boda: “Okay Natalie..... I need some milk from ya today hun.” *Boda kneels alongside Natalie, and tugs on her utters, and swishes some cow fresh milk into the awaiting bucket. This goes on for a bit, when suddenly, a noise startles Boda. He looks up from Natalie's underneaths, and looks out into the barn. Silence* Farmer Boda: “Hmm..... wonder what the hell that was. Oh well..... come on girl, give me some more.” *Boda returns to pumping the milk into the bucket, when the noise is heard again.* BAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHBAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHH Farmer Boda: “Natalie..... sounds like Corey is waking up. Guess I’ll go check on him now.” Natalie: “MOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO” Farmer Boda: “It’s okay girl......” *So, there we have Farmer Boda finishing the milking, and he puts up his bucket. Again, the noise is heard* BAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHBAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHH Farmer Boda: “Son of a bitch, Corey sounds upset. Wonder if the coyote got into his pen again.” *In a flash, Boda gets to his feet, and heads out the backside of the barn. He stops short, and is stunned by what he sees. Quickly, he backtracks into the barn, and reaches into one of the horse pens. He produces his 3030 rifle, and loads it with a slug.* Farmer Boda: “I’m a get that bastard now. I’ll make him bleed good too.” *Farmer Boda takes the rifle, and heads back to the outside of the barn. He pulls the rifle into a shooting position, and takes aim. He gives a loud verbal warning, then fires a single shot.* Farmer Boda: “Got em.” *Being safety conscious, Boda walks back to the barn, and puts away the rifle. He wipes some sweat from his brow, and heads back to the backside of the barn. He walks out into the goat pen, and looks at his prize winning goat.* Farmer Boda: “Corey.... you okay?” Corey: “BAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH” Farmer Boda: “I can’t believe the nerve of this sick bastard. Did he hurt you boy?” Corey: “Bah” Farmer Boda: “Now, what the hell should I do with the remains? I know.... let me feed him to the alligator down at the lake.” *Farmer Boda reaches down, and picks up the victim of his expert shooting skills. As he picks up the limp body, something falls to the ground, unbeknownst to Boda. Corey ‘bahs’, as Boda walks towards the lake. It’s a short 500 foot walk to the muddy water of the farm lake. As Boda humps the deadweight of the ‘trespasser‘, he stops at the 6 foot chain-link fence, and plops the body up and over. * Farmer Boda: “Pfffff.... heavy little rascal.” *As soon as Boda stops speaking, a flash and a splash is seen and heard, as a large 8 foot alligator jumps up on the land, and snatches the remains that Boda tossed over the fence.* Farmer Boda: “Hungry little dickens” *Boda watches as the gator finishes off his special breakfast. Then, after a nasty alligator burp, Boda cheerfully walks back to the farm, and to check on his goat Corey. But, drama hits as Boda walks around the corner of the barn, and we see a police officer standing near the goat pen. Boda slowly walks closer, as the officer says good morning.* Farmer Boda: “God morning. Anything I can do for you officer.....” Officer: “Oh, right. My name is Officer Alexander Miyacopper. And I was patrolling the area, looking for an escaped mental patient from the Looney bin down town.” Farmer Boda: “Really? Wow. I didn’t hear anything about that before. So, what was this guy locked up for?” Officer Miyacopper: “Well, he likes to rape goats in their little goat asses.” *Boda’s eyes shoot wide open. Did he kill the goat raper?* Officer Miyacopper: “So, seeing as you are a local farmer, I was patrolling your area. I thought I heard a gun shot.” Farmer Boda: “Actually, you did. I shot one of my horses. Had to put her down. Broke a leg. You know how that is.” Officer Miyacopper: “Yeah. That’s always a tough thing to do. By the way..... you have a pretty goat here. What’s his name?” Farmer Boda: “Uhmm... Corey. Corey Williams!!” Officer Miyacopper: “Odd name for a goat. Reminds me of this lad I once knew. Thought he was a talented wrestler, but he sucked about as much as a ..... well.... a goat.” Farmer Boda: “Yeah..... I see. So, can I help you?” *Officer Miyacopper bends down, to pet Corey. The goat gives a little ‘bah’. Boda is getting increasingly nervous.* Officer Miyacopper: “So, you haven’t seen anyone out these parts then? Well, let me give you a picture, and you take a closer look.” *As Officer Miyacopper stands back up, he picks something up off the ground. [perhaps what was dropped before]. He reaches in his breast pocket, and produces a picture.* Farmer Boda: “God.... that’s an ugly son of a bitch. No wonder why he has to rape goats. No woman in her right mind would get with that. Does he have a name?” Officer Miyacopper: “Yes. His name is Chris Chase. And, he’s a dangerous man. You sure he hasn’t been around here?” Farmer Boda: “No. So, I’ll be seeing you later.” *Boda starts to head into the farm house. When Officer Miyacopper stops him again.* Officer Miyacopper: “So, being as I found Chris Chase’s identification here on the ground, inside your goat pen, I take it that you where lying to me.” *Farmer Boda turns around, and Miyacopper shows him the I.D he found on the ground. Then and there, Farmer Boda breaks down* Farmer Boda: “All right. It’s true. I came out this morning. Corey was making an awful racket. I got my gun, and I saw that man, Chris Chase, fucking my prize goat Corey Williams in the goat ass. So, I shot the son of a bitch. I mean......come on Officer..... he was raping my goat in the ass. And he was trespassing. I had to shoot him. HE WAS RAPING COREY WILLIAMS. Please..... please don’t arrest me.” *Officer Miyacopper calms Farmer Boda down.* Officer Miyacopper: “Don’t worry about it. Just come down to the station, and fill out the paperwork and make a statement. Hell, you might even be toted as a hero.” Farmer Boda :”Really? Sweet.” *Farmer Boda and Officer Miyacopper head towards the patrol car. Officer Miyacopper asks Farmer Boda one last quick question.* Officer Miyacopper: “Can I ask one more thing, before your statement? Wh.............” Farmer Boda: “I fed him to the alligator.” Officer Miyacopper: “Oh. Let’s go.” *Farmer Boda and Officer Miyacopper climbs into the patrol car, and it drives off down the dirt road. The dust in the air thins out and we are back into Boda’s hotel room. He suddenly wakes up, in a cold sweat. He sits up, and looks at the alarm clock. He lets out a belch* Boda: “Damn... that’s the last time I eat a full rack of ribs at 11 o'clock at night.” *The scene fades as Boda drifts back off to sleep.*
