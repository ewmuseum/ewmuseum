Hmmm really? This is how it really is!

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/06/03 Ever since the dream where I had seen the brutal and bloody death of my sister, my already perilous sleep had diminished to a few simple stolen hours here or there even then my dreams were plagued with the returning memory of my sister, the end ultimately the same every time. The woman who meant the most to me in the entire world face down in my shower with a large chunk of debris wedged firmly in the back of her neck the blood flowing like water down the drain her very essence dripping away slowly. It wasn’t just that, which was getting to me though it was the terribly nasty things that some young upstart had said just a few nights ago. I believe he went by the name of Silvio Rochelli and I am scheduled to face him on Fusion for my Rising Star Title That there is the problem, it’s my Rising Star Title. Every morning I wake up and see it hung on the wall, the light glistening off it causing a mini sunrise in itself. Every morning I look at the title and see my name staring back at me and I know that I earned it. I put myself through the mental anguish of bringing myself to face Jackson just three days after being beaten by him for a third humiliating time. As I stepped into the ring with Jackson for the fourth time, the time I rose above it all and managed to bring his ‘reign’ down to a simple three days. I was not bothered about the title, I just wanted to beat the man who had put me through so much. Nearly a month later and the rising star title still hangs beside the bed assigned to me by PCW on the airship that has been my home and my prison for the past few weeks, everyday coming face to face with people you know are vying for my title. Each one of them a contender in their own right, the latest in the long line was Silvio Rochelli a man who speaks an awful lot more than he wrestles from what I can gather a man who once again deemed it fit to resort to simple name calling in an attempt to put the wind up me. His words are still fresh in my mind yet they aren’t worrying me, more something to keep me amused during my periods of insomnia. The idea of a man being so narrow minded that he refused to see past his own wallet. When I was Silvio was but an afterthought, someone who was wasting his time down in the Primetime Wrestling Academy I was in a bout, which had me teaming with the current world champion. Skylar Green. The man had hand picked me to be his partner despite my fledgling career, which at that point consisted of just one match a loss to the agent Matt Jackson. It was in that match I pinned the Pharaoh something that in recent weeks only Dorn Tracer the number one contender to the world title had been able to do. However Silvio may argue that it was before my time as rising star champion and that it cannot be counted as a defence, fair enough the man would have a point. So I sit and ask myself a question, a question, which initially I had doubted myself until the reality of it dawned on my mind. How exactly have I stripped credibility from the title? A quick look at the evidence would suggest that I have not. I have indeed been a fighting champion, never once feigning injury to avoid a match, never once turning down a challenge. I even went as far as issuing an open challenge for my belt at Turmoil, a match that ultimately ended in a mass brawl with SyN, Debris and Burns. Even the most sceptical fan could not say I was not a fighting champion, so what was it that was making me strip the credibility from the title? Was it that fact that I was booked in the main event to face Totally Tubular Tommy and was close to pulling off an upset victory until SyN decided to interfere once more in a brutal act of Jealousy? Was it the fact that the loss to Tommy has been my only loss since claiming the Rising Star title including a successful defence against one Matt Jackson. Jackson a man that we both knew very well a man who I was eventually able to overcome, beating the best in the division to claim the title, yet a man that Silvio seems to have problems with. On numerous occasions he has claimed to be the one to take out Jackson, the man who finally eliminated the agent from the ranks of PCW. The last time I checked Silvio may be winning the mind games but when it comes to stepping in the ring Silvio is being bitchslapped more than Hank ever was. Silvio had his chance to take out Jackson last week but ultimately he failed, Jackson is now the number one contender to the cruiserweight belt and Silvio is nothing more than an enforcer for the bout. I am an honest man and can say without a doubt that I struggled to defeat Jackson, every time I stepped into the ring with him in the back of my mind was the thought, you’re going to choke again. Now though my demons have been vanquished Jackson I know is a talented individual but at this early stage Silvio isn’t anywhere near touching Jackson and if he fails to come close to him…how does he think he can take me out, be the one to ‘bring credibility’ to the title. Everyday I hold the title I become a small step closer to Dorn Tracer’s reign, to breaking that reign and being thrust up the ladder in PCW, with every victory comes that little glimmer of hope. I’ve heard the whispers backstage begin, about that pointed headed dude who might be the one to take away the record. The one with the big grin who if his luck holds out might just be able to pull it off and it excites me, knowing that I have a chance to be recognised forever, until some other new blood comes along and takes my spot, I know it’s a long way off but it’s there a glimmer of light in the distance. A light that if I were to just reach that little bit further and little bit harder I could clasp between my hands and make it mine.
