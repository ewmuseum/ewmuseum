A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Loneliness: Matt

2 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/2/03 Loneliness isn’t something a person ever wants to feel…to be a part of. It’s something that no one ever wants to feel…to have. Human nature is to crave social interaction and to constantly progress through it. Every one needs interaction to live…cases have been reported where children never had interaction except for receiving food, but when they were found…it was to late, they died because they didn’t know love…interaction. Stone Burns is quickly becoming a social outcast…missing out on love. Slowly becoming the textbook case of a serial killer….a murder. The only thing that sets him apart is the fact that he doesn’t have the number of kills most of the monsters in today’s society have…falling just short of being the next big one. Failing to realize his mistakes…his shortcomings. He can’t deal with his own problems like a man…he brings a knife into the equation and tries to kill his way out of problems. Thinking that it’s going to help him train for PCW and strike fear into his opponents…strike fear into me. It does nothing, but make me wonder exactly where his parents went wrong…how did he end up like this? Fear isn’t going to come in the form of killing defenseless women…from wanting to kill me. I am not going to fear a knife…when I carry a gun. I am not going to fear a kill count of three when mine surpasses it and I am not going to fear a man who isn’t anything more then a pretend wrestler…a failure. Stone Burns isn’t a man…he is a mere man living in fear. Slaying his wife because he didn’t want to be alone…slaying another woman because he was feeling alone. Trying to bring himself comfort in someone else’s pain. I know better then anyone…pulling the trigger far more then I want to remember. My motivates were different, but in the end it doesn’t matter…we both have killed and will probably kill again…I am better though. I am a killer…he is a murder. -Christian Michael MacManus *** Location: UnknownDate: May 3, 2003Time: 7:00 am I don’t know how much more of this I can take…my body is tired and broken. My girlfriend is in the hands of a nazi and there isn’t anything I can do about it…my world is spinning out of control and the only part I have in it…is that I am allowed to watch it and hang on for the downward spiral of a ride. Tied snuggly to the wooden chair is the agent…Matt Jackson. The binding wrapped tightly around his barrel of a chest, isn’t allowing him much movement. He is glad for being able to sit down for a change…even if he is tied to the chair. His legs were growing weary and his wrists were almost cut down to the bone. The small room is in the same condition it has been in for days…dirt floor covered in blood and vomit…his blood and vomit. The rest of the room is covered in a blanket of darkness and it’s hard to tell exactly how small it is. Matt knows if he could reach his feet out of the cold bucket of water they were in, he could probably touch the walls, but them being tied to the rickety legs of the chair isn’t helping him much. Letting the sweat trickle down his body…like drips out of a syringe, Matt just quietly sits in the chair. His whole body screaming out in pain, sweat into open wounds…bruised and beaten. There isn’t anything he can do about it though; he is just forced to tough it out…to embrace the pain and take it as another trial in his unrest to get back to his princess. Standing in front of him is the man who is his vice…his enemy. Nameless is all he is known as, but to Matt he is nothing more then a crony to Jason Hilton. His slicked back black hair and tainted breath are the only things the operative knows about him. Fiddling with a camera the whole time, letting his tee shirt and jeans rustle as he works…the man pays no mind to the man in front of him. Throwing his head back, Matt removes his bloody brown hair from his angular face…letting a few drips of sweat fall into his bloodshot brown eyes. He gazes upon Nameless, getting his body ready to take another beating…more punishment. I don’t even know how I am still able to compete in the PCW…my body is a disaster area, fit for federal aid. I’m not going to leave Haley though; I will tough it all out and when I finally get the chance…looking Jason Hilton in the eyes – I am going to make him feel remorse for everything before I finally send him to his grave. Stepping forward…the man’s unbearable hot breath touches Matt’s bare flesh, like a knife piercing through him he shutters, almost vomiting. Letting a smile uncover cracked teeth, Nameless checks the knots and than backs up when he is satisfied with his work. Reaching into the back pocket of his tattered jeans…Nameless pulls out a taser and runs his long fingers over the smooth black plastic. Giving a psychotic laugh, the man steps back up to Matt and leans close to his bleeding ear. “You just won’t learn,” Nameless whispers…making sure his voice is full of firm resolve. “You guys aren’t going to win…it’s going to take a lot more to topple me than a couple of beatings,” Matt replies…letting a small smile reveal his bloodstained white teeth. What am I saying? The beatings are almost more then I can handle…remaining strong is the only thing I have to hold on to…the only thing I have to keep my ship from sinking, that and wrestling. The only place I can take all this pent up aggression out in. Stepping back a bit Nameless stares into Matt’s eyes attempting to tap into his soul. “I know they won’t do anymore…that is why you aren’t hanging from the ceiling anymore. I would have thought a master of interrogation would have known what I was doing.” Looking at him dumbfounded Matt slides down in the chair a bit…feeling the rope tighten, cutting off his air supply a bit. He doesn’t care its momentary relief from the stale vomit smell in the room…the pain it causes only takes his mind away from another beaten part of his body. His mind can’t stay on one part though; the chair gives splinters to his naked body, the sweat falling into gashes and countless other knacks that are currently paining him. Grinning Nameless offers: “Let me show you exactly what I mean Matt…unless of course you tell me what I want to hear right now and we can end this all now.” Shaking his head in disgust. “Beat me…break me…crush me, but nothing is going to change my answer. I am not going to leave her…just because you guys are trying to force me too. The only way I am going to turn my back on Haley, is if you kill me,” Matt yells out…his voice cracking and broken. I want to remain strong, but it’s hard. When I try to show my strength to them, only weakness comes out…probably due to the fact that my body doesn’t have strength anymore. I have lost my will and I am beginning to waver. I must stay on path! “That is what I thought you were going to say…I didn’t expect anything less from the great agent,” Nameless hisses…letting his spit graze Matt’s ear, making his stomach turn at the mere notion that this thug’s filth just touched him. “Do you worst,” Matt says…hardening his voice. Showing no signs of wavering here, as he fights his urges to scream out in pain…his urges to give up the most important thing in his life…the only thing that has ever mattered too him. “I think I will…” Using Matt for support…Nameless flings himself up from his crouching position. Flinging the taser forward…he sends it straight into Matt’s exposed flesh, sending hundreds of volts to rush through out the ex-Ranger’s body…smiling all the while. Without a sound, Matt grabs a hold of the flimsy wooden arms of the chair…feeling the voltage touch every inch of his body…every orifice of his beaten body. Biting down hard, his teeth crunch together like to titan’s in battle. The water at his feet only causes the rush to move over his body quicker…letting more pain emanate. Pulling the taser back…Nameless surveys his work…laughing at the burn marks he placed on Matt’s chest. Quickly reasserting his dominance with a stinging backhand…letting the crimson liquid flow from Matt’s swollen lip. Struggling to keep his eyelids open…from falling into blackness Matt says. “Is that all? Making his lips form a shit-eating grin, throwing Nameless into a complete rage…making his eyes almost pop from their sockets. “You want more you son of a bitch?” He screams…letting his angry fill the room, cutting the quiet mood in half faster then a chainsaw through a board. “I told you…if you want me to dump Haley, then you are going to half to kill me,” Matt answers…the sarcasm in his voice is laid thickly. With a fail swoop Nameless plunges the taser into Matt’s chest once more…forcing the agent to grip onto the arms of the chair once more. Splinters fill his hands, but it doesn’t take his mind off of the electricity that is storming his body right now. There isn’t any stopping this time…Nameless is being relentless, holding the device to Jackson harder then before. His hands shake and his knuckles turn white from gripping the taser so hard. Thoughts of, “I hope you die…you bastard.” Fill his head…fire dances in his eyes at what is sure to be the impending death of Matt. Jackson’s eyes fall shut and his grunts come abruptly to a halt…making Nameless pull the taser back, a look of disappointment painting his face. Matt sits in the chair; silently breathing as Nameless watches his chest heave up and down. “Today isn’t your day to be sent to hell,” Nameless whispers…turning on his heels and walking out the door to the right of Matt, slamming it shut hard… Matt’s world goes dark and blank…once again he fades to loneliness. I am going to die alone…never getting to hold Haley in my arms again. 7:00 AM
