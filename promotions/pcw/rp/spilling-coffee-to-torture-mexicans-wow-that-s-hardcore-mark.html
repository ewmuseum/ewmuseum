Spilling coffee to torture Mexicans! Wow, that's hardcore Mark!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 March &#039;03 The scene opens with Chet King sitting on a chair staring directly down the barrel of a camera. Behind Chet there is a bizarre sight. There are clowns, animals, super models, billionaires, politicians and more clowns all having what looks to be a party at some sort of under water mansion made of gold. Chet King: These days most people when doing promos are having adventures getting up to all sorts of antics. There’s not really much trash talking these days, just adventures and antics. Suddenly the background behind Chet just disappears and is replaced by a grey wall. Chet King: Not me however. I’m not in the mood to go on any adventure in my lead up to March Madness. I’m just ready to do some good old fashion trash talking. I thought to myself who could I talk about? Then I turned on my TV and I saw none other then Mark Makonie spilling coffee. Mark I see you’re down in Mexico at the moment, spreading your beliefs of white power by spilling coffee over a restaurant. Just make sure you can get to LA by Sunday so I can slap the taste out of that bigot mouth of yours. *Sarcastically* Let me congratulate you on that spilling of coffee. That was the most hardcore thing I’ve ever seen. You thought those Mexicans a lesson. They thought they were better then white people? Well everytime they think that, they should just remember that coffee could be spilt on their floors, and then they’ll realize not to mess with white people. I mean that was just ingenious. How did you ever come up with such a grand concept like spilling coffee on floor? Not even the most evil, sick and twisted people in our world could come up with such a horrifically terrifying yet ingenious plan. I think that everyone owes you a debt of gratitude Mark for spilling that coffee on the floor. It truly was a noble, heroic and extremely brave thing to do. Seriously Mark, if you’re that much of a little Nazi bitch, go beat some Mexicans up instead of spilling coffee. Beat them up good Mark, go ahead bitch. They won’t sue you Mark, they’ll be too afraid that you’ll come back and spill coffee. Heh, seriously though go and beat them up. I’d love to see the part where whoever you’ve decided to fight beats you down and then cuts that swastika right off of your chest. Mark can I just ask you a question? At March Madness, are you going to do like your hero Hitler did? What I mean is when I’m nearing in on you, just like when the Russians were nearing in on Hitler, are you going to jump over the top rope and eliminate yourself from the rumble, just like Hitler committed suicide? Or perhaps you’re going to go in with a more hardcore attitude. Perhaps you’re going to threaten to poor a bag of coffee on the floor of my house if I lay a hand on you! I was just wondering if it was that Jax friend of yours that inspired you to come up with such an incredible scheme. He seems like a really smart guy who seems like he could come up with an idea such as coffee spilling. He also seems like the kind of guy who likes manipulating young men like yourself. He seems to not only enjoy manipulating you into following his beliefs and becoming a neo nazi. He also seems to be the kind of guy who would use young men like yourself for other purposes, if you catch my drift. I’m sure you do, as I’m sure you’re very aware of it. But nonetheless, if it was Jax who invented that clever plan of spilling coffee over the floor to push forward the message of white superiority, then tell him he’s a genius! In all seriousness, you make me sick to my stomach Mark. Fuck you, fuck Jax, fuck Hitler, fuck white power, fuck neo nazi’s, fuck the KKK, fuck the national socialists, fuck white supremacy. Fuck all racist bigots out there. Mark if I see you in the battle royal, you better do what Hitler did, and fuck off. But hey, no hard feelings Mark. After all I’m still in your debt for coming up with such a great idea such as spilling coffee. You taught those Mexicans a lesson Mark! When they go to sleep at night I bet they have nightmares about the coffee. You showed them Mark!
