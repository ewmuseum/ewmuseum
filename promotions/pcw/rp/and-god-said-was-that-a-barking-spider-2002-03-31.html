...and God said, 'Was that a barking spider?'

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/31/02 The camera fades in… The Chef is shown sitting on a seat in a bus that is roaming the crowded streets of some bustling American Metropolis. He speaks to absolutely no one, not even moving a facial muscle to express any sort of human feeling. He looks at his newly bought Rolex, courtesy of Osama Bin Laden’s checking account, and looks towards the back of the bus towards the lavatory. He looks back to the watch and sees the long hand ticking towards another completed minute, at the 2… Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock… Tick Tock… The hand moves closer and closer to the 12. A small drop of sweat falls from the Chef’s forehead. He quickly wipes it away, and glances back at the watch. He grits his teeth nervously and and taps his fingers frantically on his knee. He looks around the bus again, noticing the women with a young child resting on her lap, the businessman with his copy of the Wall Street Journal, the poor fellow with a plastic bag full of cheap aluminum cans. The Chef wonders to himself… “I can’t imagine the sheer horror that will take place in just a few moments; a horrible act so vile and so wretched that Osama Bin Laden will sternly denounce it. I want to be away from it, but yet I cannot run away, for it will always linger in one way or another.” The Chef watches the long hand continues it’s journey towards the 12; it’s past 4… “Do the people of the world really deserve this? Does infidelity reap such a terrible harvest? Shall I sacrifice myself to this ungodly power!?!?” The Chef eagerly anticipates the coming of the next minutes with extreme eagerness. The long hand reaches 6… Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock….Tick Tock…Tick Tock… “If I let this happen, who knows what chaos it will bring forth. A thousand plagues for a thousand years couldn’t equal the sheer demonic fervor of what will happen! Please Allah, give me a sign!” Suddenly the bus runs over a mammoth pothole. The tremendous shock causes a Catholic priest sitting in the front row to accidentally toss up his Bible. The Good Book almost faithfully lands unexpectedly in the groin area of the Chef’s body, causing quite an emotional stir. The Chef: ALLLLLLLAAHHHHHHHHH!!! Why have you forsaken my Prairie Oysters!?!? The Chef choice words cause a nun in the seat beside him to move up three spots to sit beside a man that would pass at Billy Idol’s twin. Nun: It’s so nice to be around honest, good natured Christians, unlike that piece of Taliban Trash over there! Billy Idol look-alike: Sister, your narrow mindedness disgusts me! The leather-clad rocker stands up and moves over to share a seat with a Courtney Love look-alike, they lay eyes upon each other and as Dean Martin would say, “That’s Amore!” The Chef winces in pain from the Bible Blow, and looks at his watch. The hand just passes 8… “Man, this is a long minute! I could have prayed to Allah three times by now. I would have had to pray at least two more times, but at least I’d get the majority out of the way.” The Chef looks towards the rear of the bus again. “Come On…” Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock… The long hand approaches the 9 and continues it’s magical journey. The Chef sits and twiddles his thumbs, trying to past the time. “It’s too late now, I can’t stop it from coming. It’s going to reach me everyone else here; we cannot escape. Will it incapacitate me? Yes… Will it kill me? Hopefully no…” The long hand reaches 10… “What am I saying, I don’t want this to happen! I don’t care how many infidel lives it will ruin, I’m not going to put up with it any longer!” The Chef rises up and walks towards the rear of the bus. He passed the young mother, the businessman, the old woman, the poor man, the circus midget, a man wearing a grocery bag (presumably Scott Naket), and every member of the New Kids on the Block except for Jordan Knight. The Chef: Hey Donny! New Kid: Sup… The Chef continues his journey towards the end of the bus. Suddenly, a loud rumbling is heard from the back… The Chef: I am too late! The Chef rushes towards the back, the rumbling gets louder and louder, the long hand reaches the 11… The Chef spots a door near the end of the bus, he tries to open it. The Chef: The weapon, the weapon is here! The Chef tries to open the latch but the door is locked. The Chef grabs an old woman sitting near the back of the bus by the collar and questions her. The Chef: How can I get the door open!?!? Old Woman: I’ll never te-ell! The Chef: Jumping Jambalaya woman! This isn’t a Michael Douglas movie! This is real life, and you are all going to… Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock… Before the Chef can finish his sentence… KAAAAAAABBBBBBOOOOOOOOOMMMM!!!!! In a rush of water and a nutty aura of smoke, the whole rear of the bus bursts and sends all the passengers flying in opposite directions across the county area. The Chef lands in front of a stop sign two blocks east of the explosion. The Chef: Why…why….why….? The Chef looks down at the ground, as if he was ashamed for his actions… The Chef: Why did I let Bertha eat at Long John Silver’s!?!? WHY ALLAH!?!? WHY!?!?! Suddenly a rhinoceros er…Bertha falls from the sky and lands right by the Chef, soaked from head to toe in brown water and poo. Bertha: Damn I could barely f*cking fit into that lava-f*cking-tory, f*ckface! The Chef sighs, saddened that his promos have mainly become ways to demonstrate the awesome power of Bertha’s sphincter muscles. Big B pats him on the back, leaving a few brown handprints on his outfit. The Chef: YUCK!!! It’s staining my Islamic Militant Garb! Bertha: You’ve got f*ckin camouflage, it’ll f*cking blend right in, b*tch @$$! As Bertha continues her nasty finger painting adventure on the Chef’s clothing, the camera zooms out to see the same bus, now broken in half. People continue to drop from the sky, demonstrating the mind-boggling power of Bertha’s blast. We hear the Chef's and Bertha' voices one last time... The Chef: I've got one question B: Why do you synchrinize your potty time with each hour? Bertha: It's f*cking cool! It's like a mother f*cking rocket launch or some-f*cking-thing... On that day, even the lord of Heaven pinched his nose shut, for such a foul stench was never smelt before in the world, and thanks to the friendly folks at Long John Silvers, will be imminently felt again. The camera fades to black…
