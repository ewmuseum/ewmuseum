The Dust of the Ages

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20/02 Happier times. Do you remember them? Everyone was innocent once. So what brings us to this path in life? I'm asking you this. Imploring. Come with me now, as I go through my life. Your life. This is your life, Mr. Kannon. And mine as well. Me, my memory isn't exactly clear. Various events, garbled, broken images that I cannot be certain are even my own. It isn't a good feeling, Mr. Kannon. But I live with it. I've lived with it, for over two thousand years. I will live with it eternally. Long past the point you are bones, dust. Resting in the earth....I'll live on. My pain will live on. Forever. Walk with me through the garden of memories. Can you see it? It shines on the ground as we walk, like a precious diamond. It is only through the closer inspection that we can determine whether in fact the memory is 'good', or whether it is 'bad'. I pick it up, sparkling and glittering. Look with me....look into it as I look into it. For the moment, drop your feeble, horribly feeble and trivial affairs. Look into the truly important things, Mr. Kannon. The murky cloud inside of it parts....we look beyond. Inside of it, a field of green...the white clouds, pure, purer than the souls of either you or I drift above. A family there. Is it mine? Is it yours? A past life? Future life? Impossible to tell. Neither here nor there, regardless. A woman, we can tell from her shape. Though her face is blurry, preventing us from discerning her identity...she could be anyone. But the truth of the matter, the cold truth. She is everyone. Walking with a child....again, is it yours or mine? It doesn't matter. We're the same, Mr. Kannon. Isn't it funny? I'd laugh, if I didn't already know what was to come. Inside the sparkling memory, deep inside, the child dances with the mother. Lightning cracks over the horizon. The image shifts. She's laying on the floor, motionless. Splayed, broken. Lifeless. Tragedy. Pure and whole. Untainted tragedy. Pull back. We don't need to witness any more, Mr. Kannon. I've seen it all before. Too many times. Broken dreams. Broken lives. Over and over again. The same old story. Repeated ad nauseam. So what is my point, you may ask? Look around you. Look to the ground in front of us, in this, the realm of the souls. Look to the glitters. Count them. Go on. I have an eternity to wait. It may take you that long though, Mr. Kannon. For every one of them is a broken dream. A broken life. You're walking on them. They are the stones which line your path of deceit. You trod them underfoot. As do I. They stretch on, on. As far as the eye can see. They are the sky, they are the earth. They are the air you breathe. So many. Uncountable. And I ask you this, Mr. Kannon. As I look into your eyes. At the scope of this, all this suffering....what is your life? I'll tell you what it is. Petty. Pointless. Without worth. You are another dream, Mr. Kannon. Another broken dream, trod underfoot and forgotten. When I take your life, no one will mourn. When I reduce you to the dust of ages, no one will remember.
