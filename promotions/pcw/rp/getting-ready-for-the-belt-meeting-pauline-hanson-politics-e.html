Getting ready for the belt, meeting Pauline Hanson, politics equivilant of Steve Austin. Setting the foreigners in their place second.

Date: Stardate 10.4324433 ::As the scene opens, once again our camera is following Corey's black limo. Instantly we know we're in Australia, because Corey can't afford a limo anywhere else. You see, only Corey would be as controversial as to have a storyline in which he is rich in one country but poor in another. And speaking of controversy, can you feel it? It is in the atmosphere. Very few men and women are chosen to be controversial, to have the ability to sense what the crowd is feeling and known just how to give the controversy junkies their fix. It is even a rarer occasion when two of these people come in contact with one another, for when it happens, the amount of controversy is paramount. The controversial atmosphere can be cut with a knife, and yo Momma will orgasm five-fold. Today is such an occasion, and as the limo slows to a halt, we feel that atmosphere. We can feel it as the life-force of two controversial human-beings come closer and closer together, until they become one, only not in a sexual way because one of these controversial beings happens to be really ugly. The door opens, out steps a handsome man, the man of your dreams, the man known as the controversial one, 'Controversial' Corey Williams. He is wearing black leather pants and a black t-shirt with a syringe on the front reading 'Come get your fix....' As he walks past the camera, we manage to see the back of the t-shirt reading '....controversy junky.' Corey walks on and the camera zooms out to finally find out our location. As the camera zooms out a pans up, what we see a huge construction site, which we can see is nearly complete. And when it is complete, it will become a mansion, the new Australian home of Corey Williams. Corey no longer walks on, instead he stops and his eyes pan up in the same fashion as the camera did. He removes his sunglasses from his head, and shows emotion. The only time Corey usually shows emotion is when he's talking about controversy or opponents, but generally that all comes under his favourite subject, himself and New Breed. And as much as this house will soon be his, it isn't right now, and he didn't build it or have anything to do with the building. So as Corey removes the sunglasses that keep people guessing as to what he is thinking under that fake Hollywood-style smile, we see for possibly the first time in his life, that he is impressed. That he is finally impressed with something that isn't him, finally pleased with someone that a member of New Breed's work. And then he walks forward to the person who's work this is, who deserves his impression, and when we see that person, we realise why they were able to make Corey impressed, able to get past his ego and controversy. Because it is an egomaniac and controversial person themselves, it is Pauline Hanson.:: Corey: Pauline! This is great! Pauline: What did I tell you, you know before I became the World's most controversial politician I wanted to be an architect. Corey: But I thought that before you were the World's most controversial politician you were a fish and chip shop owner. ::Pauline is surprised, she didn't expect Corey to actually know anything about her, she thought that she would be able to make things up to suit her own ego, much like Corey often does.:: Pauline: .....That too. Corey: And these workers, I can't believe they've built a mansion in only a few weeks. Pauline: Well, nearly built it anyway, it will be finished by the time you get back from March Madness. Corey: Still, that's record time, what construction company did you use again? Pauline: Actually it's a fairly new one that I had a hand in creating, it's called the AWCC. Corey: Really? And what does that stand for? Pauline: The All White Construction Company. ::Corey is amused, he turns to look back up to his mansion and the people working on it, and that's when he notices that they're all white.:: Corey: They're great! Pauline: Yes, they're very big on the 'ABC Factor.' Corey: ABC factor? I've never heard of it. Pauline: Well it's quite simple, it's why some people are better than others, because they have the ABC factor.A is for Australian and American.B is for Britainand C is Canadian.The four big English-speaking white nations, the four greatest races in the world. That was until all this 'immigration' came about, now we've got wogs and nips and Abos and niggers roaming the streets at will, shoot them I say, shoot them all. Corey: Ah, now I know what you mean. Much like the ABC factor will take place in the main event of March Madness. The ABC factor will leave only the god-chosen rulers, that being English-speaking whites left in the ring. Well I guess that automatically eliminates, Akiyama, El Diablo, The Rush, Juan Burro and that Bubba-Ray Dudley look-alike Chef. Not to mention Miyagawa and the man who puts the SLOB into Sloboda, Boda. These guys are even worse, they know about the ABC factor so they pretend to be American..... and that's just sad. ::Corey shakes his head, disgusted by those men. Not only is 'American-Patriot' a poor gimmick to begin with, but it's probably a good idea to at least be American to begin with! Hell, even Chris Kanton bothered to be American!:: Corey: And it also eliminates Tyler Nelson, for his skin is not white, it is green. And not with greed, but with envy. Tyler Nelson is slowly losing his power. As the old generation gets taken over by the new, an old, desperate man is clinging on desperately to keep his spot in the sunlight. But to use a cliché Tyler Nelson, it's OUR time now! This is a warning going out to William S.Powell and Lovecraft, the team collectively known as 'We stole the name of Corey's finisher and old entrance music and besmirched it by naming it after our jobby team of crapstastic geriatrics.' Whenever, wherever, I don't care if it's in the ring or while you're watching Matlock, YOU WILL BE TAKEN DOWN. Because you DON'T MESS WITH THE NEW BREED! And we WILL HAVE OUR REVENGE! ::Corey stares at the camera psychotically, noticeably breathing in and out Pauline: For Chase's sake, calm down Corey. Corey: Hunh? ::Corey turns his head to Pauline, drawn out of his trance.:: Pauline: You heard me, take a chill pill Phil. Corey: What? Pauline: Go on a vacation or something. Corey: What you say buoy? Pauline: I'm just saying, if this Tyler Nelson guy is so scared of you, why bother talking about him? ::Corey's eyes move from the camera, to Pauline, and then back to the camera.:: Corey: No, I don't think you're getting it. Pauline: I mean, why bother getting so worked up about him? Corey: Well you see..... Pauline: I mean surely he's not worth fussing over if you're as good as you say. ::Corey's eyes shift from Pauline, to the camera and then back to Pauline again. This lowers his voice, trying to talk so that only Pauline will hear, but not the camera.:: Corey (under breath): No you see, he's not really jealous it's just part of an angle. ::Pauline stares into space, dumbfounded.:: Pauline: Please explain! ::Corey hesitates, eyeing the camera nervously again as he prepares to break kayfabe once again.:: Corey (under breath): Because I want to get myself over and... Pauline: Please explain! Corey (under breath): I'll try and do that by have attacking a guy who IS over.... Pauline: Please explain! ::Corey sighs, maybe it's time to go for a simple approach.:: Corey (under breath): Because wrestling not real.... Pauline: Please explain! ::Corey slaps his forehead, ah to hell with Kayfabe. He looks up at the camera.:: Corey: Ladies and gentlemen, politics equivalent of 'Stone Cold' Steve Austin, Pauline Hanson! Pauline: Please explain! ::Pauline begins to smile, as she too has to break her act and return to the land of the living.:: Pauline: How many was that? Corey: Five. Pauline: Damn! My record is seven. ::Corey can't help but smile, for even in all her stupidity and ugliness, Pauline Hanson is controversial down to her very last bone.:: Corey: Well ya just gotta try harder. Now, about this house, I know this is kinda last minute, but I'm about to be in the main event of a Pay-Per-View, and you, like everyone else, must know that CCW ALWAYS fires up for a Pay-Per-View, a time to display my controversy LIVE to the rest of the world. And one way or another, CCW ALWAYS wins at a Pay-Per-View. So there's more than a good chance I'll be coming home with the title belt. Which is why I'm asking you to add a title belt room for my new house. I already have one at home, but this one will be BIGGER AND BETTER! And at the centre of it all, the piece de la resistance..... the World Championship Belt, under ownership of its new champion, CONTROVERSIAL COREY WILLIAMS! Pauline: Sure thing, it will be ready by Monday. Corey: Just like that? Pauline: Just like that, never under estimate the ABC factor. Corey: Great, now all I gotta do is eliminate the other eight non-superior English-speaking whites, and it will be all up to New Breed. Pauline: And what happens there? Corey: Well, although Snow seems to think Chris and I are gonna lay down for him, or in this case throw ourselves out, there's little to no chance of that happening. I'm afraid it may be go time between the New Breed. And whilst Snow may be superior, and whilst Chase may be cocky... NEITHER of them are CONTROVERSIAL! So, in short, you're looking at the next PCW World Heavyweight Champion! ::Corey is on a roll, but maybe he takes it too far with his next comment.:: Corey: Now can you dig that.... sucka? ::This one stupid comment sends Pauline back into her chance in an attempt to save what's left of the ratings of this promo.:: Pauline: Please explain! ::Corey smiles.:: Corey: Seeya Monday, Pauline, gotta go back to the 'land of the free' now, although I don't know where that expression came from. If it's the land of the free how come I'm poor as HEEL over there? Pauline: Please explain! Corey: Exactly, I want someone to explain that to me! Pauline: Please explain! Corey: All right going now Pauline, I have controversy junkies who are relying on me for a fix. ::Corey walks back, back to his limo. He gets in it and the chauffeur turns the engine on. The limo drives off, to March Madness, to where history is made and the future begins, to where CCW will become World Champ! Fade to plaid.:: !The End!
