The final score(Part 5)

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20, 2002 [Fast forward a few minutes now, back at Caesar’s Palace. In the office of “The Chair”, the steel briefcase of $10,000,000 is sitting on his desk. The room stands still, with the dust floating around the room, and the room is absolutely still. A small sound breaks the silence. Ding The elevator door opens, and out comes Eddie Mandrake, laced with sweat, as he runs into the room. He pulls out his Beretta and runs into the office. He makes his way in with no sort of trouble at all. He reaches the desk, but the chair turns around showing the Monster of a man sitting there with a twelve-gauge shotgun. He is also laced with sweat.] Monster: Surprise, Eddie. [Eddie just stares down the mouth of the shotgun. More beads of sweat appear on both of their foreheads.] Eddie: You know, we can kill each other with one shot right here. Monster: I know, but you know something, it wouldn’t be as fun. [The monster puts the shotgun down. Eddie, somewhat confused, lowers his weapon. The monster stands out of the chair, and walks out onto the floor.] Eddie: You know, when I saw that pin yesterday, I wondered what it was…and now I know. It was a CCW Pin. Monster: So? Eddie: I know who you are, Eric Anthony Clark. [Eric cracks a half smile] Eric: Heh. So, it clicked in. Eddie: Former Head of CCW Security during the Spider Era. You used to take out the trash, and now you create it. [Eric’s smile fades] Eric: No, it’s the government that is the trash now. They are a bunch of rednecks and hillbillies who don’t know what the hell they are doing. Especially now with this corporate responsibility law they put in. What the hell is that? Eddie: It just means your boss will have to post any sort of embezzlement that he does now. Eric: I thought America was the country where you could do that sort of thing…Greed Rules here doesn’t it? Eddie: How would I know? I’m Canadian! Eric: As am I for about a quarter of the time. However… Eddie: You’ve fleeced several casinos in the US and Canada and you say that what you do is part of your pursuit of free enterprise? Eric: Yes. However, my employer has stated that the rules of employment are quite specific. You must perish. Eddie: Try it! [Eric charges in with a jumping kick, but Eddie ducks it, and the two go at it, Eddie blocking most of the offensive onslaught that Eric is providing. Eddie is being forced back into a wall, while Eric continues to punch and kick his way in. Now against the wall, Eric winds up for a punch, Eddie ducks as Eric follows through leaving a good sized hole in the wall. Eddie sees his moment, and begins to wail away on Eric, but Eric just shrugs off the shots, and delivers a massive side kick to Eddie’s head. Eddie flies across the office, and when he lands, he slides across the tile floor, and crashes into the wall opposite of his previous position. Eric cracks a half smile again and his walks toward Eddie, who is clutching his abdomen, and spitting up blood. Eric stands over the slumped visage of the bounty hunter; he slowly crouches down to his level.] Eric: So…here, the great Edward Mandrake, one of the best bounty hunters in the world. Here he sits…defeated, and finally dying. [Eddie, during the time Eric was talking, grabbed a short piece of steel stud that came loose when Eddie crashed into the wall.] Eddie: …Not quite [Eddie takes a swipe at Eric’s face, and it connects leaving a massive gash near his right eye.] Eric: GAAAHHH! AGH! [Eric drops to the floor, and Eddie gets up, leaving the money behind. Eddie just gets to the elevator. Eric gets up, showing the grizzly damage that was done by the stud. The right eye looks close to falling out of the socket.] Eric: MANDRAKE!!!! DIE!!! [Eric rushes the elevator door, and Eddie calmly hits the close door button. Eric reaches the door just as it closes leaving a massive impact sound when Eric collided with the door. Eddie while in the elevator takes his cell phone out. He hits a speed dial button.] Eddie: James? I’m out of the bounty hunting business forever; I’ll see you in Norman. [Eddie hits the end button. He walks out of the elevator and gets into his car. The bellhops, despite the beatings the previous day have loaded Eddie’s car with his stuff. Eddie gets into his car, and starts it up. He checks one of his bags looking for something to give the bellhops for their trouble…and he notices that fifty bucks is missing!] Eddie: That’s it. Any Hell-hop that tries to carry my bags will be KILLED! [Eddie sees one crossing the canopy of Caesar’s Palace…An evil grin appears on Eddie’s face, as he peels out of the canopy hitting a bellhop with his WRX. Eddie just keeps on driving out of Las Vegas as he heads into the sunset.] THE END
