Lord of the Amulets: Farmer Maggot

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Skitzo and Kaldorn are introduced in this rp! “Uh-oh.” Sam didn’t get to finish his sentence as all of a sudden, two dogs started barking at us, coming straight for us. About five feet away, the dog got ready to pounce on all of us. The look on the dogs face wasn’t a happy one. The dog looked mean and hungry. The dog barked and lowered his head, ready to pounce. We all turned to run the other way when we saw the other big, black dog, ready to pounce as well. The snarl was scary and not nice. “Hutch, Fang, down!” The dogs stopped short, almost in a full jump and turning around and walking to a man, coming our way. The man had long, black, hair down to his shoulder. He didn’t have a very happy smile but it wasn’t that mad. “What are you kids doing here?” He snarled at us as his two dogs, Fang and Hutch sat by his side, still snarling at us three. We all stood pretty much as still as possible until Sm Ramey finally worked up the courage to speak. “We didn’t, uh... mean any... harm sir, it’s just that uh... we were just taking, uh... short cut and we didn’t know... this was.. Uh, your field.... uh.... sir.” Sam’s voice was shakier than a vibrator. He finally finished spitting out what he had to say and then looked at the man, half scared to death. “As long as you had no intentions to steal some of my mushrooms, I’m okay. Why are you taking a short-cut. Where are you headed?” “Brickethollow.” it was Pippin who finally could talk now. All I could do was stand there, hoping that the Farmer wouldn’t charge us or anything like that. “I see. My name’s Farmer Maggot. People around here call me Skitzo. Let me feed you, there is paleness in your face.” Skitzo gave us a small smile and turned with his two dog following him. The three of us quickly caught up to him as he said. “Don’t worry, my two dogs won’t hurt you unless I command them. They bark loud but there soft inside, although there face doesn’t always show it.” I immediately hears Sam breathe a small sigh of relief underneath his breath. Pippin also started with ease. Skitzo’s house was just a couple hundred yards away. His house was in the middle of nowhere with a gravel road leading out back onto the road and a mushrooms field all around that was nicely blooming for the season. Skitzo’s house was a regular, old house. The house was poorly taken care of and there was no sign of a Miss Maggot around. “Sit down, I’m just making some Mushroom soup.” “Ths is all too much.” said Sam, sitting down on a couch. Me and Pippin sat next to him, relieved to have some rest for our feet after walking for so long on the dirt. A few minutes later, some beautiful, hot, mushroom soup was laid on the table on neatly with four plates. We all sat down and pigged out on the soup, without conversation. When we were nearly down, Skitzo started talking. “You say your going to Brickethollow, eh? You have to cross the ferry, about ten miles away. How are you planning on getting there?” I looked at Sam and Sam looked at Pippin. Sam was the one that answered the question. “Well, we’re gonna walk.” Skitzo’s jaw basically dropped but he quickly hid it and said cheerfully: “Nonsense, I have a wagon that I can take you in. Don’t believe in cars myself, being a farmer but it will get you there faster than walking.” All three of us looked at each other and smiled. “Thank-you!”, I put in. By the time we were all piled into the wagon that would take us to the ferry, it was dark out and the only light we had was a lantern that Skitzo had brought along to help us. The wagon moved slowly along the main road which we were now on. Every so often, it would shake to the side and almost go off the road, in which Skitzo would have to shake it back onto the road. The dock of a lake soon came into view, even in the darkness. The moon’s reflection bounced off the water, making it sparkle. We arrived at the dog and got out of the boat, ready to leave. “Thank-you for the ride. It helped us!” I told Skitzo, smiling. “And the mushrooms you gave us will supply us with enough food to last till Brickethollow!” put in Pippin. “It has been my pleasure. Stay out of trouble!” I turned to Skitzo to give him a final good-bye. “Skitzo, good-by...” I stopped suddenly, turning my attention to behind me. Ramey and Palmer turned and listened behind us. The sound was that of soft footsteps. The steps weren’t human steps. They were horse steps! They were the steps of a huge, black, horse. “The black riders!” sputtered out Sam. “The who?” asked Skitzo. “What do we do?” interrupted Pippin, ignoring the question that Farmer Skitzo asked. The footsteps were getting closer and closer until they were loud. Everybody was to stiff and couldn’t move. Even Skitzo, who didn’t know about the black riders. “Get down!” whispered Sam. We obediently got onto our knees or ducked down, just as the horse came over a small hill. The horse wasn’t black at all! The horse wasn’t white but a sparkly silver colour. On the horse was a figure. The face couldn’t be seen but all of a sudden, the figure waved at us. “HI! Guys! Pippin, Sam, and Mark!” As the figure came closer, it was seen that the figure was none other than Merridoc ‘Kaldorn’, the person who was supposed to be taking care of the house in Brickethollow! He rode up to us as we got up out of our crouch position. “Why are you guys ducked like that?” “Bye, all. I must be off.” Not even staying to say hi to Merry, Skitzo hurried off on his wagon, back towards his house. When he was out of sight, Kaldorn repeated. “Why are you crouched like that?” All three of us looked at each other and I said. “Long story. Let’s just board the ferry and we’ll explain on the way to Brickethollow!”
