Parent Time: Very Strange

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/25/02 The scene opens up with a bright green Volkswagen Beetle traveling down an interstate with absolutely nothing surrounding it. The road is near empty so only a few cars pass by time to time. Inside the Volkswagen is Carey J. Nowski, Stephen Cairns, and Jackson “Hoe” Pallish. Outside it is looking like a storm is coming. The wind is picking up and the clouds are getting darker. Along the highway they pass a sign that says a town called Bellville is two miles away. I’d say that Misplaced is heading to Belleville, Pennsylvania, which is where Carey’s parents live. You have to wonder what a conspiracy nerd’s parents are like. I guess we’ll soon find out. Meanwhile, Stephen and Carey are talking about this upcoming Friday Fusion. Stephen: “…I don’t care, we better win this week. We need this to keep our career’s going. You better give it your all.” Carey: “Silence! I always do my best my young padawan learner! The force is with me!.” Stephen: “Shut up please. Don’t act like a fucking moron now, it’s not a good time. You always have to bullshit the day before a match. Also, how are we going to get to Jacksonville by tomorrow night?” Carey: “We will get there young one. You must look on the positive sides of things.” Stephen just shakes his head, and they continue down the interstate. Carey takes exit 42 to Belleville. They go onto this road of…nothing again. Finally, they hit a small neighborhood with many ordinary houses lined up in rows. As they’re driving they come up to a neon green house. Yes, it is a neon green house. To Stephens surprise Carey pulls into the driveway of this neon green house. Carey: “This is the place. They must’ve done some painting. Last year it was hot pink. I like the look.” Stephen: “This is ridiculous.” Jackson: “It looks like somebody jus’came an pissed on yo’ mama’s house. They need some designing skills.” Stephen and Jackson continue to gaze at the house while Carey approaches the door. He rings the doorbell, and after a few moments an old lady opens the door in a wheel chair. She’s holding a poodle. It also appears that she has shaved the poor poodle bald. Carey just smiles. Carey: “Grandma Stevenson! I thought you died?” Grandma chuckles and starts to tug on the ear of her poodle for some reason. Grandma Stevenson: “Of course not sonny. I only went in a coma for about 17 years. Did your insane parents tell you that I died?” Carey: “Yeah, they did. So how’ve you been since your coma that I never knew about?” Grandma Stevenson: “Just wonderful! I have this great poodle, and I saw god during my coma. It’s really amazing. I didn’t picture god having such a big beard. There were dancing candy bears to, but god ate there heads off. Oh boy, I need to take my pills. Come on in!” Carey once again just shakes his head, and he waves the other two in. They all step into a house with a wretched smell. Inside the door is a narrow hallway that is painted in leopard spots…purple and pink leopard spots. Jackson: “Damn Carey, what dat’ smell be? Smells like mama made a bad koon’ or something.” Carey: “We don’t eat raccoons here Jackson. The smell is just my fathers laboratory downstairs. He doesn’t like to clean it. He says the smells remind him of when he was a child. He spent his childhood in a mental institute. He’s weird.” Stephen: “Anymore “he’s” in store for us? Your parents certainly have a unique sense of style. Did they get trapped in the 60’s?” Carey: “Actually, my mother did. She refuses to leave her life from the 60’s. It’s rather strange. Wait until you see the living room…” Before they could finish talking Carey’s mother comes running down the hall…in 60’s clothing. She skips conversing and runs to give Carey a hug. It appears that she is a lovable person because she hugs Jackson and Stephen. After she is finished she grabs Carey and just looks at him. Carey’s Mother: “Honey Mustard! It’s really you baby! How’s my sexy swinger been doing?” Carey: “Fine mother, we just came down to visit you for a while. It’s been so long since I’ve seen you. I also notice that you’ve redecorated the house again. It looks very…shagadelic.” Carey’s Mother: “Finally you start to come into the 60’s! I’m glad you’re getting the groovy talk down baby…” Carey: “Actually, I just watched Austin Powers. I remembered that word for some reason.” Carey’s mother walks away from Carey and goes to greet Stephen and Jackson. Carey’s Mother: “I guess you’re Carey’s hip friends? Welcome to my pad! I’m Angela, but you can call me Angie cutie!” Stephen: “Aren’t you married? I mean…nice to meet you Mrs. Nowski.” Angela: “I said call me Angie you stupid bitch!” Stephen: “Excuse me? Did you just call me a stupid bitch? You do realize that I’m a professional wrestler, right?” Angela: “Are you Carey’s partner in that wrestling game? It’s ridiculous! Such a fake game!” Once again they’re interrupted by Carey’s father coming from his basement laboratory. He has on a white lab coat and a black t-shirt underneath. He runs and attempts to hug Carey, but Carey moves out of the way. His smell is unbearable. Carey: “Sorry dad, you’re just smelling foul today. Guys, this is my dad Marty. Marty, these are my friends.” Marty: “Dudes! What’s up? What a bodacious day we have here.” Stephen can’t help to chuckle because Carey’s dad sounds as stupid as that Cowabunga team they face on Fusion. Stephen: (chuckling) Nice to meet you Marty. I’m Stephen Cairns. I’m also Carey’s tag team partner for wrestling. You remind me of our opponents.” Marty: “Awesome! You better beat those bogus dudes tomorrow! If not I’ll so totally beat you down to a pulp. My dudester of a son doesn’t lose.” Stephen once again can’t help but laugh at his comment about Carey not losing. Of course, Carey is a loser. Meanwhile, poor Jackson is still standing in the middle of the hall, and he hasn’t yet been noticed. Angela finally looks at him. She takes a long look at this ghetto farmer. Angela: “Baby look! It’s Billy Bob Jay-Z! That’s hip baby! Grrrrrr!” Carey: “Mother please, you’re not being very nice. He’s sensitive about how he is, and he won’t hesitate to snap on anyone. Not even you mother.” Angela: “Oh don’t worry about me honey mustard! I’ve been taking a groovy protection course at the YMCA. It’s awesome!” Carey: “Can we just go into the living room please? I think we all would like to relax for a while. We’ve been traveling all day and we have to get to Jacksonville by tomorrow night. So we need rest!” They all make there way down the strangely colored hall into the strangely colored living room. The living room is neon orange. Yes, it’s neon orange. This family must be big on bright colors. Anyhow, the family of a conspiracy nerd, a bodacious scientist, and a swinging mother all accompany a cricket player and a black farmer into the living room. What combinations we have here. The living room is neon orange with black inflatable furniture and many neon paintings on the wall. They all take seats on the furniture. Carey: “Mother, why did you tell me that grandma was dead? She was just in a coma for 17 years. It’s completely different!” Angela: “Grandma is alive? Really? Where is she sweety?” Carey: “Are you drunk mom? She’s the one who greeted us when we came.” Angela: “Well I did have a few margarita’s, what of it asshole?” Carey: “Mother please stop. You’re creeping us out.” Grandma comes strolling into the room with her poodle again, and Angela jumps up in shock. Angela: “Mom! It’s really you! How have you been? How did you live?” Grandma Stevenson: “What are you talking about devil woman? I’ve been here for 6 months now! Go play with your satin toys devil woman!” Angela: “Yes mother.” Without further conversation Angela walks out of the room and up the stairs. In comes Carey’s father though. Marty: “She’s being so bogus tonight. Please forgive her dudes. So you guys must be pretty tired, huh? How about you go get some rest for a while? We have two bodacious bedrooms upstairs where your guests can hang for a while Carey. So go ahead up!” Now Stephen and Jackson quietly make their way up the steps and to the bedrooms. It’s now Marty, Carey, and grandma Stevenson in the living room. Marty: “So Carey, how’s your life been? Pretty bodacious?” Carey: “It’s had ups and downs, but I’m doing alright now. We have a match tomorrow against two surfer guys. They’ve beaten us before, but I think we’ll get them this time.” Marty: “That sounds pretty gnarly. Good luck dude. You know me and your mom are always here for you. You’re a bodacious kid, we couldn’t ever forget you. Anyways, I’m going to go check on your mom. She’s acting bogus tonight. You remember the house, so just get comfortable!” Carey: “Thanks dad. We all have a flight to Jacksonville at 5:00 AM, so we’ll be out of here in no time. We have to beat these bogus Cowabunga guys!” Marty and Grandma Stevenson make there way out of the room and up the stairs. Carey stays on the inflatable chair and turns on the television. Not much is on so he goes to the reliable sci-fi channel. After so long he falls asleep and all is quiet in the Nowski house.
