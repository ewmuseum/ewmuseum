A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 12:00-1:00

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/03 Location: Matt Jackson’s Apartment, New YorkDate: April 21, 2003Time: 12:00 PM Matt walks into the living room of his apartment. He is still dressed from work: a grey suit with black dress shoes. His brown hair is still spiked up and unmoving from the gobs of gel he put in it this morning. His pistol rests casually in its holster inside his breast pocket of his jacket. A badge rests on his belt, snapped in place by a clip on the back of it, the gold flickers as he moves, letting the light reflect off of it. The wooden floor cricks as Matt moves across it, making his way to a leather couch, he plops down and rests his head in his hands. Sarah Clarkson is no more, I got Peter the information he wanted and then finished the job I started. No one is going to be any wiser and now I have more time to focus on the PCW, on doing what I always planned on doing. He stretches his whole 5’8” frame across the couch, listening to the leather squeak under him. Reaching into his jacket pocket, he pulls out the automatic and sets it on the coffee table next to him, next he snaps his badge off and sets it next to the pistol. Sitting up a bit, he works his jacket off and tosses on a recliner across the room, watching it fall and twist, until it finally comes to its final resting spot. Kicking off his shoes, he props his feet up on the end of the couch, deciding here would be the best place to sleep tonight. He knows that Haley is all ready asleep and he doesn’t want to wake her. Placing his head back in his hands, he begins to relax on the couch. Look down towards his feet; he notices a blanket, folded in an orderly manner. He pulls it up over his legs and rolls over to his left side facing the room. He grabs a pillow from the floor next to the couch and places it under his head. She has had a ruff month and with everything coming to a stand still in our case against her father I think it will be good if she gets some rest. Everything will be back to normal for her soon. Letting his blue eyes scan around the room, he sees the fireplace in front of him with the recliner to the left of it and on the right is the entertainment center. Behind is head is the walk way into the kitchen of his tiny apartment. This one room apartment has been his haven ever since he retired from the CIA for the first time. He could probably afford better now with his PCW contract and the money he is bringing in from the CIA, but he likes this place. Slowly his brown eyes haze over, bringing him closer and closer to his much desired sleep. He just wants to forget about Spring Fling and the loss that they took. He knew that Haley probably wouldn’t be able to keep up and it was a risk that he was willing to take. He needed her to be there for him and she was; now it was up to him to finish the job. He hadn’t been taking his duties to destroying the PCW as seriously as when he first came in. Jade and Lacuna got him away from that and then because of them he almost had to spend a night in jail. I still can’t believe that car bomb didn’t work. All she had to do was get in the car and start it, but no the little posh bitch messed that up. They are nothing more then faded memories now, things of the past and I am not going to dwell on them. I am just going to let it die and move on to big and better things. Kannon made a major mistake in giving up his power. His eyelids heavy, Matt starts his drift into sleep. He emerges himself in the blanket and rolls onto his other side, letting his body face the window that was behind him. His body falls limp and he enters sleep, his breathing slows down and he is now unknowing of what is going on around him. The ringing of his cell phone awakes him, which he left in his back pocket, jerking up; he reaches back into his pocket and pulls out the black phone. Flipping it up he presses the talk button and waits. “Jackson,” Matt says, his voice tried and groggy. “Matt, it is Peter. We have a small problem down here and we need you to come in right away,” the DCI replies. “Can’t this wait until morning?” “No you need to come in now,” Peter says, his voice generally concerned. “I will be there in ten.” *** Location: CIA Conference Room, Langley VADate: April 21, 2003Time: 12:30 PM Walking through the sliding class door, Matt grits his teeth in anger as he makes his way into the conference room. The round oak table stands in front of him, with the 25 leather chairs all around it. For a change there are only two people waiting for him in the room. Peter Benson the Director of Central Intelligent, a man in his late forties, who looks more like he is in early sixties. He is full of life and by his actions you would think he was young, but his grey hair tells other wise. Next to the DCI is Edward Matthews, the CIA’s information master. The short, fat, balding man knows everything anyone wants to know about, that goes on in the CIA. As, Matt makes his way towards them Edward’s hazel eyes stay focused on Matt. Benson casually sits at the head of the table, smoking his pipe and looking over some documents that sit under his watchful black eyes. This better be important if they pulled me away from some much-needed rest. Ever since Spring Fling, I have been working my ass off to get rid of all the loose ends that came from the Clarkson problem and now I need to get ready for Turmoil. Who knows what kind of problems the Overweighter is going to present. Not that I am worried of course, but I really don’t want to have to waste my time like we did with Lacuna and Jade. Pulling out his chair, Matt sits down and rolls back up to the edge of the table. Resting his elbows on the table, he lets his head rest in them as he waits for Peter to start the meeting. He takes a few deep breaths to get the sleep out of his mind and tries to focus on the DCI. Leaning forward, letting his black suit wrinkle a bit, Peter scribbles a few notes down on a pad of paper and then looks up at Matt. He removes his thin glasses and puts them on the table. “Well Matt I know you weren’t to keen on coming in here. I know you have been doing a lot of stuff over the past few weeks, but I needed my best on this one,” Peter begins, “There has been something going down for a while and I am not sure how we are going to deal with it.” Matt cocks his head to the side, his eyes questioning Peter. His hawk like eyes then go to Edward, seeing the overweight man looking over some papers, he skims back over to Peter. The ex-SEAL only meets his glance. “It would help if I knew the problem and then maybe I could tell you how to attack it,” Matt demands, his voice telling the whole story of his lack of sleep and his edginess. Peter slides a folder over to Matt as he nods his head. Flipping open the brief, Matt crosses his arms over his barrel of a chest and starts to skim the contents. Reaching a part, Matt’s eyebrows rise and his face goes white. He shuts the folder and looks back to Peter, who is still nodding his head, letting Matt know this is the serious of the serious. “It is that serious Matt,” Peter tells him, his voice resolved and hard. “We have no clue what we are going to do. Right now I think the best plan of action is to put you on the floor and make you the acting chief for terrorism..” “How exactly did you guys find out about this sleeper cell? I mean I thought these things were meant to be impossible to find.” “We got a tip about it, nothing else is known. Everything in that folder is all we have to go off of,” Peter says, looking down to try to hide his disappointment. Matt holds up his hand, almost waving it at the DCI to get his attention back. Rubbing his hands together, Matt stares blankly at the table for a minute, gathering his thoughts. “I will get down on the floor and start piecing some information together. Other then that I have a few other ideas I will try, but with what we have to go off of this is going to be hard Peter and we don’t have much time. There is only one reason they wake a sleeper cell.” “I know I just hope you are able to do something about it.” I hope I am too, I never thought about what would happen if there were another threat of terrorism on the United States. I am going to have to do everything in my power to stop him though, I might not have much information to go off of, but this is like a cancer and it needs to be operated on soon. *** Location: CIA Main Floor, Langley VADate: April 21, 2003Time: 12:45 PM Matt swaggers into the Main Floor office of the CIA, looking around he notices all the workers slowly moving around, showing their tiredness. Each one looking like the next except for a different color tie or blouse. To his right and left there is a row of cubicles and straight about 20 feet is his desk, moving past all the cubicles, his eyes dart from side to side trying to get a general idea of who is doing what. The hard cement floor makes his shoes click as he walks over it. As he passes, each of the employees’s eye their new boss. Most of them know exactly who he is and though they don’t know the situation they know it was serious enough to bring him in. A few whisper to each other as they stand outside the glass boxes they call offices and others just peer out the small opening to get a glance of him. Once at his desk, the famed agent pulls out the office chair and collapses into it. His desk is neatly arranged with his computer in the center, a cordless phone next to the computer and a few pens and pencils by the phone. Pulling a piece of paper out of his pocket, he dials the extensions of all the people Peter told him to get in touch with, each getting the same thing. “Meeting in five minutes at my desk.” After making all the calls, Matt opens up the folder about the sleeper cell and writes a few notes down on a piece of paper. Looking down at his metal wrist watch, he notes there is still a bit of time before and meeting and starts to go through the file more in-depth, highlighting things in thinks are important and getting as many notes as possible. He is only taken away from his work when two people walk up to his desk. A man, who seems to be in his early twenties, he reminds Matt a lot of himself. His black gelled hair shines in the light as he gets closer to the desk and his lambent eyes sparkle. Next to him is a woman, who seems to be in her late twenties, probably just a few years older then Matt. Her shoulder length brown hair bounces and her heels click on the cement as she walks. “Hello Mr. Jackson, I am Brian Donald,” the man says, reaching out a hand to greet his new boss. “I am the data analyzer.” “Nice to meet you Brian and you are?” He questions looking over at the stunning brunette who has now stopped in front of his desk. She looks amazing in the pink dress with white buttons and slowly she pushes her hair from her face and looks at him with perfect blue eyes. “I am Tabby,” she tells him with a smile. She is amazing and that smile, the way it forms over her slender pink lips. I am glad I decided to help Peter out. “I pretty much just get the information, sift through it and then bring you the important details.” “Well now that the pleasantries are out of the way we can get down to work. I am going to be frank, we don’t have a lot of time and this is going to be tough to crack,” Matt barks out, making sure they understand the seriousness of what he is telling them. He knows they are going to be in this for the long run and he wants to make sure they are ready for it. “I took a few notes and have a few leads I want you to start on Brian. Tabby, I want you to get me a list of all the terrorist groups in the US that have had activity over the past six months.” They both nod in agreement as Matt looks down at his notes to make sure he hasn’t missed anything. “We don’t know when this guy is going to strike so we need to move quickly. I am going to put a few calls in to some old friends and see what I can’t dig up and then we will all meet back here in half and hour.” 1:00
