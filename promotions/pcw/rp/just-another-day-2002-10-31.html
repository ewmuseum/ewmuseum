Just Another Day

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31.10.02 “Tell me your joking honey” says Brittany, Duzza’s lovely half black half Asian Queen. Duzza: “ I am for real, this is gonna be good, hey Jimmy c’mon down son” The scene is the large kitchen of Duzza’s Hampton mansion. The walls are covered in peachy tiles, obviously not Duzza’s taste. On the far side of the kitchen, there is a massive window, looking out over Duzza’s large garden. On the right side there are three large brown cupboards and a door leading to the outside. To the left of the kitchen stands three more cupboards and a large breakfast bar. Brittany is leaning on to the work station. Dressed in a pair of white tight pants, a tight ‘Duzza rules’ T-shirt and her hair set back in a long pony tail. Duzza is standing opposite Brittany, sitting up on the breakfast bar, dressed in a pair of very baggy black jeans, a ridiculously baggy white ‘TP-2.com’ T-shirt and his hair set back in cornrows. For once he doesn’t have on much jewelry Duzza: “ C’mon son, show ya momma how good you lookin’ for ya first day of school” Slowly a small African-American child walks into the kitchen with his head facing the floor. He is dressed like he has just walked out of the ghetto. He is wearing big Timberland boots, a pair of baggy jeans, a T-shirt so baggy it’s touching his knees, a big black puffer-jacket nearly touching the floor and a big black wooly hat on his head. He looks miserable, he looks like a true gangster A small tear trickles down Duzza’s cheek, at the sight of his son dressed like a mini-gangsta. It is a tear of joy Duzza: “ I’m, I’m jus’ so damn proud” Brittany however does not look happy at all, she has a look of shock on her face Brittany: “ Duzza you cannot make our son go out dressed like that” Duzza: “ Wha’? Why not, he look like his pops don’t he” The child, Jimmy, does not look happy at all, in fact he looks like he’s having trouble standing straight. Duzza looks down at the child, he looks slightly confused Duzza: “ Something is missing, hmm, what is it? Wait I know, a gun” Brittany: “ WHAT! Now Malek, hold on, ain’t no way my baby going to school on his first day dressed like some thug and carrying a weapon!? What you trying to do to poor Jimmy?” Duzza: “ A’ight a’ight, no gun, c’mon Jimmy, we gots to take you too school” Brittany: “ Err Malek, how about I take him?” Duzza: “ Nah girl, you stay here, I’ll take him” Duzza picks up the keys which are in a small disk-like thing near the door, while Brittany gives Jimmy a kiss and his lunch and heads out the door. Duzza walks into the large 3-car garage. He looks at the Black Bentley, then to the black BMW jeep, then the red Ferrari. Duzza decides to go with the jeep, he puts Jimmy in the passenger seat, gets in the drivers seat and slowly backs out Duzza: “ So Jimmy, ya like your new look?” Jimmy: “ Err, well…” Duzza: “ C’mon son, out wit’ it” Jimmy: “ I look like you Dad…I look like a fool…” Duzza looks insulted, he looks at Jimmy’s clothes, then looks at his own Duzza: “ What do you mean you look like a fool? You look like a gangsta son” Jimmy: “ Exactly Dad, I look like a fool” Duzza: “ Your mother done poisoned your brain ain’t she” Jimmy looks confused Jimmy: “ Poisoned my brain? What does that mean dad?” Duzza: “ Don’t worry son, don’t worry” They slowly pull up to the school. The school is on a very busy road. Cars are parked everywhere. There is a steel black railing running around the entire place. The actual school looks like a old Church. The walls are painted red. There is a massive white door, leading into the building. Duzza and Jimmy slowly go through the door. At first Jimmy doesn’t want to go to his class dressed like a thug, but after Duzza gives him $5 he soon happily runs to class. Duzza looks at his son with pride in his eyes, he then slowly turns and goes to head out of the school. It is then that a fine young lady comes up to him. She is dressed in a short black skirt, and has a small brown fur coat covering her top. She also has a furry hat on. She is black, but of a light complexion. She is very sexy, probably about 25, maybe 26. She walks up to Duzza and places a hand on his muscular chest Lady: “ Hey, your that Duzza aren’t you?” Duzza smiles to himself, there are few things he likes better than fine honeys recognizing him in public Duzza: “ Yeah yeah, that’s me, how can I help you miss?” Lady: “ Please call me Jess” Duzza: “ Ok then, Jess, how can I help you?” Jess: “ Well, I was just wondering…was it embarrassing to get beaten up by that white guy on tv the other week? I mean I know it’s fake and all, but damn” Duzza: “It is not fake like you think honey and what white guy anyway? I don’t remember taking a beating from no white boy for… a good month” Jess: “ Oh, well how are you know? You all healed up”? Duzza: “ Yeah I’m fine” Jess: “ Well how about you and me go and get to know each other better?” Jess starts rubbing Duzza’s arm slightly Duzza: “ Err how you mean? Go out for a drink or somethin’?” Jess: “ Oh we could skip that and go straight to the bedroom, you know you want to” Duzza: “ Ha, well that’s a great offer but. I’m married…see ya” Duzza quickly walks away, Jess looks disappointed, while Duzza actually looks pretty happy. Probably thanking the good Lord for his amazing good looks which attract the honeys Duzza walks out of the school and gets into his jeep. He quickly spins the car around and pulls away. He gets onto a straight road, with streets everywhere, he turns on the radio, the R. Kelly song ‘The Worlds Greatest’ comes onto the air. R. Kelly is probably not the first artist you would think a thug like Duzza would listen to, but Duzza and R. Kelly are very good friends and Duzza has made some music with the guy and Duzza is one of the few people to have stood by R. Kelly during his recent problems. Duzza starts singing to the chorus of ‘The Worlds Greatest’ when his cell-phone starts ringing, he looks for somewhere to pull over but can’t find one, so he just answers the phone while driving Duzza: “ Yo yo, who this?” Statuz Quo: “It yo n*gga Stat” Duzza: “Hey ‘sup dawg?” Statuz Quo: “I gots to speak wit’ you man, ‘bout your match” Duzza: “ My match? What yo on ‘bout dawg” Statuz: “ Wit’ Ali against that HIT cracker” Duzza: “ The HIT? Oh yeah I gots a match wit’ that mother f*cker ain’t I, ha hard to believe it ain’t it, them putting this n*gga and Ali in wit’ a cracker, they crazy punks ain’t they, I’ll kill that guy” Statuz Quo: “Yeah well…I hope so, ‘cos since me n Dante lost our belts, well dawg, you gotta rep Playaz Inc to the fullest dawg, for real. And I got a bad feelin’ bout this match dawg, I think they gonna do something, management that is, and they’ll make us lose dawg, make Playaz Inc look even worse ” Duzza: “What? Nah dawg, you trippin’” Statuz Quo: “ I’m serious man, Max said it’s gonna happen man” Duzza: “ Max! Max!? The Max who always going ‘ain’t nuttin’ wrong wit’ me’ while he is twitching? That Max?” Statuz Quo: “ No fool, Old Chess-Club Max” Duzza: “ Oh it’s straight up then” Statuz Quo: “ Exactly, that’s what I’m saying. See putting you n Ali in a match wit’ a cracker!? They know you gon’ kill him, it’s a perfect opportunity to embarrass us dawg” Duzza: “ Well you know I will be laying a beating on that punk. I can’t believe they even letting a me and Ali fight that fool, that ain’t right. But you know Statuz, thanks for hollering at me, you might be right, for once” Statuz Quo: “ I was shocked as well, so what ya gonna do ‘bout it man, can’t let them get you” Duzza: “ You wanna know what I’m gon’ do, I’ma holler at Ali, and then I’ma go get my…” At this very moment, Duzza notices flashing lights in his mirror Duzza: “ Oh F*CK” Duzza quickly puts down the phone, pulls over and places his head in his hands as the police officer walks up to his car…
