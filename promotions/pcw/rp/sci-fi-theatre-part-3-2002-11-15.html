Sci-Fi Theatre(Part 3)

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 15, 2002 [We return to the Bridge of the Enterprise, along with the host of this edition of Sci-Fi Masterpiece Theatre, or would it be Disaster-piece theatre. Spider is yelling at one of the stage hands…] *CLICK* Stage Hand: Wuh-oh. [Suddenly the Bridge of the Enterprise violently shakes in one direction, sending Spider flying across the bridge, from the Captain’s chair to the Science station…then from there to the Engineering station, and back to the Captain’s chair. Spider is left in a heap at the base of the chair.] Stage Hand: Sorry. I pressed the Photon Torpedo Hit button with my ass. [Spider’s hand slowly reaches up and hit’s the play button on the chair, as the view screen returns to the movie. We are entering a Space shot with the dropships now on the way down to the planet of Arrakis. We enter one of them to see the troops getting their chemical weapons ready, taking great care not to discharge it inside the ship. Eddie and the Lieutenant Citadell are sitting near the door. Eddie looks a little bit green.] Citadell: Is this your first drop, Overseer? Eddie: Yeah, and I hope it’s my last as well. If I ever return to Draconis 4, it’ll be in victory, and not in defeat. Citadell: I hope so as well. It would be a shame to lose such a benevolent governor. [Two minutes pass as the turbulence gets worse and worse as the dropship passes through thermal layers, on the way to a local spaceport.] Eddie: Pilot! Can you take us to the spaceport of Arsunt? Pilot: Sure thing…I’ll inform the other transports as well. [The pilot radios the others of the change in destination, and they all acknowledge the change, and they all change direction to head to the city of Arsunt. Within five minutes, they all land within the city. The heavier transports take a little while longer to land, but the troop carriers have landed, and the Ordos troops begin their assault. Missiles are firing from the dropship, bullets whiz by the troops, some piercing the arm armour of the troops. Some rounds penetrate and hit the troops themselves, causing them to drop. Eddie’s transport is on final approach…The troops are waiting to dispatch themselves. The dropship lands at last and the door swings open.] Eddie: GO GO GO! [The Troops that are with Eddie and the Lieutenant, run out with reckless abandon and begin to dispatch their chemical loads onto their enemies. The enemy troops cough and eventually drop to the deck, dying. They try to fight back with firing their rifles, they drop the rank and file of the Ordos troops rather quickly...] Citadell: Our troops are dropping like flies sir! We need the heavies! Eddie: PILOT! HOW LONG UNTIL THE HEAVIES ARRIVE?! Pilot: Approximately one minute! Eddie: This thing will be over in one minute! Tell them to open the hatch as soon as they hit fifty feet from the ground. Pilot: We are not authorized to do that, sir. Eddie: If you don’t I’ll tell my guys to drive through the hatch! [The pilot looks at the Overseer for a moment…] Pilot: Alright. [The pilot gets onto the radio and begins to communicate what he wants done, of course the other pilots all ask for confirmation. The pilot confirms the order, and the other pilots acknowledge. Some radio chatter over the Ordos lines report something disturbing.] Citadell: Sir, we have lost Delta Company! Eddie: Shit! [Suddenly, there is a quiet whistling…which grows loud right quick, which then turns into a mighty loud explosion with sand being kicked up and large bodies flying through the air, most of them are Harkonnen troops. Then another explosion sends more of the dug in Harkonnen troops flying.] Citadell: What the hell…? Eddie: The heavies! They’re on the ground. Tell the troops that we are going to win this! Tell them to advance onto the Harkonnen position. Leave no survivors, Scorched Earth policy in effect! Let’s move! [Eddie grabs a pulse rifle from the rack and runs down the ramp of the dropship and sprints to the front lines. Firing his rifle in controlled bursts, the shells hit the cratered ground near the path of where Eddie’s running. Eddie runs into a hand-to-hand combat session between the Ordos’ Alpha Company and some Harkonnen troops. Eddie quickly helps them tear through the Harkonnen troops as if it were a bad Kung-Fu movie. He meets up with the sergeant of the Alpha company, who is a tall black man, who is really muscle bound, and has the same radioactive green glow in his eyes like the Lieutenant.] Eddie: With me men, we must seize the palace! [Then, as if on cue, men in black suits come over a sand dune…they all carry an anti-infantry chaingun!] Alpha Sarge: SARDAUKAR! FALL BACK!! [They all retreat behind a small sand dune, as they fire wildly into the sand, killing many Ordos troops before they reach the dune. The sarge, and ten others make it back.] Alpha Sarge: You know what we could use right now… Eddie: What? Alpha Sarge: An entire Sietch community of Fremen armed with sniper rifles. [The firing stops no sooner than he said that.] Eddie: Sergeant…That was too much of a coincidence… [Eddie and the sarge look over the dune to see the Sardaukar, felled by unknown means.] Alpha Sarge: Either way, I’ll take it. Citadell(Over Radio): Harkonnen troops are in retreat, all remaining forces converge onto the base. Eddie: Excellent. Let’s head in. [Eddie and the troops that he are with enter the palace. The neo-gothic architecture still remains, despite the scandalous graffiti that the Harkonnens put over the logos of House Ordos.] Alpha Sarge: Damn man, I knew the Harkonnen were pigs, but this is completely disgusting. Eddie: I feel like I’m going to vomit. I think I remember where the control room was… [Eddie heads down a few corridors most of them covered in the same graffiti and blood stains from the previous attack that drove the Ordos off of Arrakis. They enter the control room, and they see that everything has been relatively untouched.] Alpha Sarge: Whoever locked this door did a fine job. Eddie: I tend to agree. [Lieutenant Citadell, runs up on Eddie and his new found company.] Citadell: Overseer, the grounds are secure. Eddie: Grand, begin taking casualty reports, I have to report to the Executrix soon, and your name will feature prominently in my report, and yours too sergeant? Alpha Sarge: Snowball, sir. Citadell: Thank you sir. [Citadell runs off again. The remnants of Alpha Company begin to sit down and rest, as Eddie looks out the window of the command centre.] Eddie(Thinking): Next stop…the capitol of Arrakis, Arrakeen. [We then return to the set, as we see Spider beating the hell of the stage hand who hit the rocker panel with his posterior.] Spider: Mother…Fucking…Son of a…BITCH! [Spider looks up at the camera.] Spider: We’ll be back in a few, stay tuned. [Fade to a commercial for PCW’s Friday Night Fusion.]
