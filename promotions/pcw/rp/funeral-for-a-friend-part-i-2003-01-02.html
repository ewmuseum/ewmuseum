Funeral For A Friend (Part I)

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/03 Funeral for a Friend My emotions are behind me, but I wear my hurt on my sleeve like a medal. Some people go through their whole life without having anyone believing in them. I know pretty well, because I though I was on of those people. I thought I’d have to go through everything by myself in life, and that no one would truly care about me from the day I was conceived until the day I died. That’s when he showed up… It was a dark night in the summer. There was a smooth mid drift on this beautiful evening and everyone was out having fun. I was out, but at that point in my life, I didn’t know the meaning of the word “fun”. Go long, Hugo. The cheering and enjoyment is as present as the air we breath on this beautiful day. Unfortunately all of that was about to come to an end. The ball slipped through Hugo’s hands as his friend passed the ball a little too far. The ball had rolled into an alley and Huge volunteered to get it. As he ran after the ball, he tripped on a piece of plywood on the ground and cut his knee up pretty badly on a cinderblock near it. Just a few feet a way I happened to be sleeping in the precious comfort of an old mattress someone had thrown out. I heard this boys yells for help and quickly awakened. This alley had been pretty undisturbed for the short time I slept there. I was at a low point in my life, and had no shelter. Previously to this I had the Poetry Corner to read at and they’d pay me enough for low end living, but it was still a living. Now I had to fight for anything I could get. And all I could get was this street alley. So after the shouts of the child, I decided I’d go over and offer whatever assistance I could to this child. I was not a many of many words, because I pretty much had nobody to talk to. Do you need help? The child turned around and responded completely in distress. My leg, I hit it and now it won’t stop bleeding. I need help. I felt no more need for words as I took off the shirt on my back and place it on the floor. I grabbed the vodka that was keeping me warm through the colder nights and gently rubbed it on the child’s cut. Afterwards I tightly tied the shirt around his leg to help prevent the blood flow. The child would have been right on his way, if someone didn’t come along and jump to blindside ignorant conclusions. Hugo, where the hell are you? You’re going to get it when I find you. As I looked over at the child I saw a look of fear in his eyes. His father circled into the alley and saw his son and I sitting down, as I helped him get to his feet. Hugo, what the hell are you doing near that bum. Get over here, before you catch something. I cut myself and he helped me from bleeding. Enough of that bullshit; get over here before I beat the shit out of you. The father grabbed his son by the arm and yanked him over followed by a hard slap in the back of the head. Now get in the damn house. I have to settle some stuff with this garbage here. He didn’t do anything dad, he just helped. The father returned the sons words with a hard slap across the son’s face. Leaving a red indent. The second the father’s hand made contact with the skin of his son, I remembered words I heard spoken not too long ago. *Flashback* “I think of the strength and happiness I have now, but everytime I feel at my highest I remember the things I failed to do.” Isabella said shaking her head in a disappointing manner. “And what was that?” Romantico replied, wanting to hear what his friend had to say. “I always remembered when my father would lay his hands on my mother and make her scream in pain. Then I’d just stand their absolutely defenseless, doing absolutely nothing to help the case. I’ll never be able to go back in time and make him pay for what he did.” Isabella said as a tear flowed from her eye. “Maybe you couldn’t stop it for yourself, but in the future, maybe you can. The future is the only thing we have left worth caring about.” Romantico replied as he comforted the fellow orphan with a hug. *Flashback finished* Now it’s time for you to get what you deserve you dirty bum. I looked the man in the eyes as he undid his belt and took it out ready to fight. As he was about to attack a man came out of his house and walked over to see what was going on. Hey you two stop this right now. Shut up old man, I have to take care of this bum. Do you like it? What the hell are you talking about you homeless slob? Do you like the feeling you get when your wife and son scream for mercy through the pain you put on them? How dare you talk to me that way? The man then raced toward me and lifted his arm flinging the belt in my direction. I just stuck out my hand and let God do the rest. I caught it in mid air and held it firmly without ever taking my eyes off of the man. You never answered me. You are one crazy piece of trash. The man then went to punch me as I dodged every time. He tried to yank the belt loose, but I wouldn’t let go. He grabbed the bottle of vodka on the ground and broke it on the ledge. He then jabbed it at me, trying to stab me, but I held my hand out and blocked it with all my force. The glass went right through my hand as it bled all over the ground, but I never let go. Apologize for what you’ve done! I ain’t sayin a damn thing to you. I continued as I head butt him in the face and knocked him clean out. The spectator’s looked on in awe as I had just wiped the floor with this large man. I walked away bleeding, but it didn’t phase me. The man who had come out to try and break up the fight then came up to me. Son, I might just have a career for you. He handed me a card that said “Underground League of Spain”. I accepted and thanked him without taking much consideration. Your bravery is a virtue. Those were the words that the man I now stand over the grave of said to me just four years ago. That was the day we met, but it wasn’t the last day we shared such a powerful bond. TO BE CONTINUED…………
