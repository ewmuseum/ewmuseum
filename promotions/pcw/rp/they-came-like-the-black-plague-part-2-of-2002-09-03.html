They came like the Black Plague (Part 2 of ?)

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-3-02 Where we last left Scorpion he was taking his in-laws bags up stairs to the guest room. Since then Scorpion has proceeded to walk down stairs and into the kitchen where his wife is making some lunch. Mr. and Mrs. Waffle your bags are now in your room. Mother in-law: Oh that’s nice Jeff, thanks a lot. That stuck up bitch… Father in-law: So Jeff, Stephanie here tells me that you recently were signed to a big time wrestling promotion? Yes I have, I just joined Primetime Championship Wrestling. Mother in-law: Have you ever thought of doing another thing besides wrestling? No I really enjoy what I’m doing it’s a really dream of mine since I was a little boy watching wrestling on Saturday Mornings. Mother in-law: That’s nice. Anyway, Stephanie remember that Robert you dated in eleventh grade? Stephanie: Oh yeah Mother, I do. What about him? Mother in-law: Well I ran into to his mother in the store one day, what a wonderful woman by the way and she told me that he just got a big time job in the City as District Attorney. Stephanie: What’s your point Mother? Mother in-law: I’m just telling you that you should have played your cards right. Think about the lifestyle that you could have had. Gigantic home and beautiful clothes… Stephanie: What’s wrong with my clothes? Mother in-law: Well dear, they are not very, well there not something a lady should be wearing. Stephanie: It’s tank top and jeans. Mother in-law: Exactly. Stephanie: And Mother why would I want to live a miserable life knowing I don’t love the man. I like my lifestyle now, I love my husband he treats me right and my son is my top priority. Mother in-law: Well dear what will you do when Jeff gets hurt or fired? Stephanie: PCW treats our family like we are family. Father in-law: Now dear what your Mother is just trying to say is that being a District Attorney is a much better job then a wrestler. Stephanie: To my Mother it’s a greater job. But to my family we think wrestling is a better job then District Attorney. Mother in-law: How much longer can Jeff do this? He could be fired tomorrow and end up working for a landscaping business. It’s like I’m invisible, they are disrespecting me to my own face! Stephanie: Don’t you worry about us Mother! You always have criticized everything I’ve done because I haven’t done it your way. Mother in-law: Well just look at your sister she married a man that is a CEO for a big time company. They live ravishing don’t they? Stephanie: But are they really happy? Ask them that and personally I don’t care what my sister does because she was always kissing your ass. I think I should go…c’mon Toby let’s go watch “Blue’s clues” Stephanie: It’s all right Jeff as far as I’m concerned this conversation is over! Mother in-law: I as well do believe that this conversation is over. Excuse me as I go to the power room. We have a power room? My Mother in laws walks out of the room to go to the “Powder Room” a.k.a the Bathroom. As I stand there I notice my wife very frustrated at she takes the hotdogs out of the boiling water. Stephanie: Dad I can’t take this anymore! Father in-law: Take what? Stephanie: Mom telling me how to live my life! Father in-law: She wants the best for you. Stephanie: No she doesn’t, she wants me to live my life by her standards, going out to ball, buying Gucci clothing, going to fancy places. She needs to understand that isn’t me. I prefer eating at the pizza place down the street, shopping at a mall and watching my husband wrestle. Father in-law: I understand that dear. But see it from her point of view. Stephanie: I have had enough of seeing her way. I’m my own person and she doesn’t realize it. I’m fed up with playing by her rules. My mother in law walks out of the bathroom and back into the kitchen. Mother in-law: Is lunch ready? Stephanie: Yeah. Let’s eat I’m hungry. Here Steph let me take that. Stephanie: Thanks Jeff. I take the plates with the hotdogs out to the dining room table. We all sit down ready to eat our hotdogs. There is a dead moment for about five minutes without any conversation. There is a lot of tension in the room and I’m in the middle of it. Father in-law: So Jeff you play any golf when you have a day off from wrestling? No not really, I enjoy playing Basketball and Baseball. Father in-law: Oh. You enjoy getting all sweaty like that? Sure I guess you could say that. But I also like the sport. Father in-law: Oh that’s nice. Mother in-law: Jeff is this wrestling business safe for your family? I mean your putting your body at risk every night. Did it ever occur to you that you have a wife and a eleventh month old son? Stephanie: MOTHER! I’m doing what I love doing most. How many people can say that? I’m a professional at what I do and yes I can get hurt but my family understands that I’m living out a dream while finically backing my family. It never really crosses my mind. Mother in-law: I’m sure if you quit wrestling today your father in-law can get you a top job at his company. Stephanie: MOTHER! How rude can you be? I appreciate the offer but I enjoy what I’m doing and down the road I could be making the same amount of money that the top dollar job gets. I get money from my contract, merchandising, public appearances, autograph signings and being on Pay Per View shows. So I have no problem with the money I make. Mother in-law: Why wait for the money when you could get it now? Stephanie: Mother that is it. I can’t stand being in the same room with you right now. Father in-law: Dear get back here please. Mother in-law: What is her problem? Pardon me; I have to go calm down Steph. Father in-law: All right Jeff. As the scene closes the noise of Stephanie crying gets louder as Scorpion walks up the stairs to comfort his wife.
