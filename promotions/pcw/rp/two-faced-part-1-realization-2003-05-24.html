Date: May 24, 2003 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Get your hands out of my pants. [That's so utterly strange. It's as if there is some sort of turning point hitting Mr. Thomas Gordo and he's realizing what is wrong with his life. See, even though he is rich beyond his wildest dreams, completely successful in all the things he loves to do, and not to mention PCW Champion, he feels that his life is dramatically missing something. How can a man with practically everything be missing something out of his life? He has the looks, charm, and charisma to land any woman that he damn well pleases. He can buy anything that he wants at any given moment. But at this point in time he doesn't feel like doing any of that. This is why he's uttering the words 'Get your hands out of my pants'. Because he can't figure out what piece of the puzzle is missing] Woman: Well, are you sure about that? [Tommy sits in the backseat of his 2002 Mustang convertible and looks deep into the woman's hypnotic blue eyes. He fights the urge to smile because she is so bodacious looking] Tommy: I'm totally sure. [The blonde woman smiles and then looks down toward Tommy's crotch with a giggle] Woman: I believe someone else would disagree with you. [Tommy joins this lovely woman in staring at his crotch. It's quite obvious that his twig and berries are ready for action just as they should be all the time. Physical stimulation and mental stimulation are two separate worlds, Tommy thinks to himself. It's quite obvious that Tommy isn't in the mental state of having a radical sexual encounter, but according to his sexual parts he's ready to go. It's nature to have an erection when there is a beautiful woman half naked in the back of your Mustang. Only really gay men and other non-gay women wouldn't be aroused by this woman] Tommy: Well, that compadre just doesn't know whats good for him sometimes. [The woman seems a bit offended by the last statement and she tries to take one more look at Tommy. What is it that she likes about this dumbass? The looks? The money? The power? Maybe it's an inter-mixture off all of these key ingredients. This blonde haired, blue eyed woman looks at Tommy and realizes that without the money, power, and looks, he'd be nothing but a useless human being. His personality is like a summer blockbuster action movie. It gives you what you want right away giving one an instant state of satisfaction, but in the end all a person really wanted was a good plot. Tommy didn't have a good plot, just tons of explosions to get the motor running] Woman: I think maybe I should get going then. Tommy: You don't have to leave, bodacious babe. It's just that I won't be up to sexual activity. [Tommy smiles brightly, showing off the pearl-like teeth in his mouth. The woman, at one time, would have found the smile to be mentally stimulating but now she sees through the disguise that Tommy wears. She is beginning to see him for who he truly is on the inside. It's pretty god damn ugly inside there, that's for sure. If she doesn't bail soon she might end up chucking up that Italian dinner that Tommy took her out for] Woman: Well, sexual activity is what I was looking for tonight. So, why don't I leave you here in the backseat with your thoughts. Maybe you can dream about that shaggy brother of yours and maybe bust a-- Tommy: WHOA! That was TOTALLY BOGUS! There was absolutely no need to go after my brother like that. He never did anything heinous to you, so there is no need to bring the heinous onto him. [She just stares at Tommy in confusion and truly wonders how he even got through life. How can anybody really understand what the hell that he says? Maybe a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle could decipher the language he speaks, but regular people in these times can only be confused by what this surfer is trying to communicate. This guy is one of the greatest surfers of all time? He's the PCw World Champion? How much did HE sell his soul for?] Woman: Tommy, your a f**king loser. You prey on weak and helpless people to gain more on your ego and your bank account. You use your money to buy people and power. Then you use women to make you feel better about yourself. Did you hear that? YOU USE WOMEN! You use them until they can't serve your purpose any longer! Tommy: But I don't want to have sex with you, babe. Woman: Yeah, I know that. You just wanted to have somebody to talk to because your probably REALLY scared about something. You don't want to talk to your brother about it so he doesn't think that your a wuss. If you talk to a woman about it then that makes you look like a sensitive man and THEN you'll get that sex that you TRULY wanted in the first place. [As the blonde woman is gathering her clothing and putting it back on, it leaves Tommy to ponder over what she has just said to him. That he uses women? That's totally a bogus statement if he ever heard one before. He doesn't use women, he frees them. He takes women from their heinous environments and gives them something that they have been craving their entire lives. One night with Tommy and it makes all of their worries go away for quite sometime. In fact, Tommy believes that he serves to be some sort of totally gnarly muse to women. Once they feel the bodacious foreskin they become transformed into these successful women with the feeling of knowing what they want out of life. How dare this babe come in his car and tell him all of these bogus lies!] Tommy: Hold on, totally heinous babe! I don't use women! [As she is finished buttoning up her blouse she smiles so gently. Her face looks so peaceful and happy. She holds out her hand and pets Tommy's face] Woman: I've known you for a really long time, Tommy. You've known me for a really long time. I'm pretty sure the things that we say about each other are going to be true. Tommy: Well, it's not. Woman: I think you need to realize that you have the worst case of denial that I've ever seen. Tommy: I'm so totally not in denial. Woman: Ok, whatever you say. [Ashley Jameson has ALWAYS been around Tommmy. From the time that Tommy was in kindergarten she was always calling Tommy a 'stupid head'. She never truly stopped calling him that even two decades later. Through the years they've shared some pretty strong feelings with each other and have been nothing but truthful with each other. Ashley always saw through Tommy and his evil ways. In fact, she was the creator of the 'New Tommy' when he entered PWA all those years ago. She told him that he needed to be, 'Less of a greedy asshole'. Tommy didn't understand that too well and she just molded him into the man that he is supposed to be. She knows this from one night she spent with him while they were teenagers. The way his eyes looked so innocent and so hurt. She could tell that he was giving, thoughtful, and fun. That Tommy was an absolute riot to be around. He made sure that everybody was happy and having a good time. He shared his money. He didn't just get with girls to have one night of passion, he truly wanted to get to know them. That could be why she turned him from good boy to bad boy. She created it and then she destroyed it. It could have been jealousy, but she's in that denial thing too. There were just too many women in his life and too many of them breaking his heart. At this moment, she is sorry that she brought back that evil Tommy all those months ago. She was just tired of seeing these bloodthirsty women trying to take advantage of his good will. Or maybe she was tired of seeing Tommy falling in love with other women...and not her] Tommy: So, you don't want to have sex now? Ashley: Nope. [Ash is just sitting in the backseat with her petite hand on the door handle. The two of them share a look and for just a moment she can sense that good Tommy. The way his blue eyes are shining underneath the star light could be what it is, but for some reason she can truly sense the goodness in him. He didn't want to ask for the sex, that's just what he thinks is evil to do at the moment. Which...it was. But the way he asked it was as if he forced the words out of him mouth] Tommy: Don't leave me. [The words pierce through Ashley like a hot knife. They are the words that she could hear him trying to say all night. When they were at the restaurant and he was talking about his Pay Per View match it was as if he were saying, 'Babe, don't leave my side tonight. I need you more than ever right now. Not because I need someone to vent to but because I truly love you. I want you there because your supposed to be there by my side, your supposed to be my wife. I can't lose you Ashley and you can't lose me. So, tonight just stay with me'. Well, she believes that the words are SOMETHING like that, but she could be a little off] Ashley: Tommy, do you remember that one night when we were sixteen? Tommy: The night that you told me I was a greedy loser? Ashley: HA HA! Yeah... Tommy: I remember that. Ashley: I just wanted that night to last forever. Tommy: Because of the sex? Ashley: No, not because of the sex but because of the way you held me. Tommy: I could do that right now, babe. Ashley: No, you couldn't. That's why I have to go now. Tommy: Come on, Ash, don't leave me. Ashley: I have to. Tommy: Why? Ashley: If I stay then something bad will happen and we'll both end up regretting it for the rest of our lives. You know that it will happen too. [Tommy sighs and realizes that he hasn't felt his heart ache like this in a REALLY long time. In fact, the last time his did do this was when Ashley told him that she was going to be getting married. They were only twenty, it was far too young to get married. He remembers sitting in the tiny apartment, being sore from his wrestling training, and how he so badly wanted to run and stop that wedding. He wanted to stop that heinous dude from ruining her life, but she seemed to be so happy with him. It was like she found her ultimate happiness that people search for their entire lives. Who was he to run to that chapel and stop her from reaching the ultimate happiness? It would have been totally bogus in a heinous kind of way, that's what it would have been. So, he just laid in the bed, staring at the cracks in the ceiling, and dreaming of becoming the greatest wrestler in the world] Tommy: Nothing bad is going to happen and I think that YOU know that. Ashley: Goodbye, Tommy. [The door opens and she trots through the empty parking lot. Before Tommy can even move she disappears into the darkness. He knows there is absolutely no use in trying to go after her because she'll only scream at him and he HATES that. She's going to be gone for a while and he curses himself for letting her go now. Well, he does have a Pay Per View match to concentrate on. Skylar Green and The Pharaoh at the same time. It's going to be pretty brutal, but it's nothing that 'Totally Tubular' Tommy can't handle] Fade to black
