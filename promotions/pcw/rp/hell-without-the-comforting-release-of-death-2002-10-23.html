Title: Hell Without The Comforting Release Of Death Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 23/10/02 “I can’t believe I let myself be roped into this...” Our shot opens up on the disgruntled face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon, a snarling and frustrated figure. Xavier : “I think I would rather be at OSW’s Slam! than stuck in this gutter of humanity...” As the camera zooms away from the Scientologist, we see the words every sane person dreads plastered over a banner. A phrase that sends a shiver down the spine of most men.... And women... And Apes.... “SCIENCE FICTION CONVENTION” Xavier : “Wait... Make that an OSW Pay Per View.” He cringes as his bright Green eyes truly take in the sights before him. Grown men dressed up as Klingons, well, wearing Black wigs and holding cupped hands over their foreheads... Women dressed up as Borgs and unseen creatures sporting Cybermen outfits from Dr. Who. Xavier : “If I were Christian, which thank Hubbard I’m not... This would be hell without the comforting release of death beforehand.” A hand is runs over his stubbled chin as the thought of turning back weighs heavy in his mind. The Scientologist’s hair has taken a drastic change too, the Strawberry Blonde died an earthy Red once more with a long braid. His eyes search around as if he has lost something. Xavier : “Ellie~!?” The bright Green eyes again search around him, a look of worry crosses his face at the loss of one of the few things he cares about. Xavier : “Please don't let her be lost in a building full of Nerds... The Therapist’s bills would bankrupt even me...” Finally he locks onto the beacons that are Ellie~! pale Blue vacuous eyes and he breathes a sigh of relief, until she emerges from the crowd and his stubbled jaw drops. “You like?” As he wife emerges through the sea of Nerds, she is sporting one of the Black and Red outfits from Star Trek : The Next Generation, the suit hugging every curve of her body. Eleanor : “I’m not sure who the designer is, but they, like, have this hella cool Gold logo!” She plays about with the Gold Federation badge on her left breast as she walks back over to Xavier... Her appearance drawing many bouts of dribbling from more than one area of the body as the Nerds stare at her, the Scientologist’s growling just enough to get them to divert their gaze. Xavier : “Where did you go?” Ellie~! shrugs and runs her hands down the outfit slowly to admire it as the sound of a Geekboy fainting and dropping to the floor is heard. Eleanor : “I thought seeing as you dragged me here I might as well do a bit of shopping, they have these cool bits of Sliver jewellery that you, like, SO stick on your face too!!! Why are we here anyway?” The CEO puts his arm around his wife and guides her on their path, not looking too comfortable with the looks the woman he loves is getting from the boner wielding virgins that frequent the convention. Xavier : “They needed someone to man the Battlefield Earth exhibit... And through begging, attempted blackmail then finally a hefty sum of money they got me to do it.” At the mention of money, Ellie~!’s light Blue globes that make her eyes flash dollar signs.
