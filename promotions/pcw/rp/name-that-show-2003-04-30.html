Name that show

30 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-30-03 We find ourselves in the presence of our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne, and a group of his friends. Mikey, Dan’s trainer and best friend, Molly, the love of his life, Jack, one of Mikey’s friends, and his wife, Liz. They are at the annual state fair, having a great time. Both Molly and Liz are carrying armloads of stuffed animals. Mikey pulls off a good portion of the cotton candy he’s holding and stuffs it into his mouth. “Mefts gribe dat mworeraror!” Dan, obviously confused, “What? Dude, we can understand anything you’re saying. Try pointing.” Mikey points towards a nearby roller coaster. “Oh, you want to go ride that roller coaster? Sounds good to me. Let’s go!” With that, our group heads over to the somewhat run down roller coaster. The group piles into the cars, they are the only ones on the ride and the operator seems somewhat surprised by the influx of business. “I can’t tell you folks how surprised I am that anyone would still want to ride after what happened last time.” Dan inquires, “What happened last time?” “Um… time for the ride to start. Have fun!” The operator hurriedly activates the ride, sending Dan and his companions into the mouth of the dragon designed entrance. Half way through the ride, the coaster speeds up. It seems to be moving at a dangerous speed. “We seem to be moving at a dangerous speed, guys.” “Thank you, Jack.” The coaster rockets over a small hill and into through a brightly-lit tunnel. Mikey’s eyes widen as he exclaims, “I know what this is! Alright!” As the coaster barrels through the light, the group becomes more frantic. Then, just as quickly as it happened, the coaster breaks through the light into a lush green area and comes to a stop. Mikey excitedly hops out of the car and begins to search the area. The rest of the group calmly exits and takes comfort that their ride is over. Or is it? “What’s going on here?” Mikey, with a new-found vigor not seen since his youth, runs over to Dan, “Don’t you recognize it? The run down ride, the tunnel of light, this whole experience.” Dan doesn’t seem to register. “I don’t recognize any of this, man.” Before Mikey can explain what’s going on, Molly screams in surprise as a small, elderly man seems to appear out of nowhere. Again, Mikey seems to know exactly what’s going on. “Are you the Dungeon Master?” The small man looks at Mikey in astonishment, clears his throat and responds, “Oh you are indeed a wise one, but I’m not exactly who you think I am. I am not this ‘Dungeon Master’ of which you speak, but I’m close.” “Well then who are you?” “Mickey Rooney.” “Whoa! Now just hold on a minute! I thought I knew what was going on here and then you show up? I want an explanation.” Dan, surprised by Mikey’s take charge attitude, crosses his arm and waits for the explanation. Mickey Rooney begins to speak, ”Well, as Mikey has figured out, this is a familiar situation. My world is in need of your help as it was once before. You five were chosen by fate to save us. You will each be given a mystic item to aid you in your upcoming quest.” “Exactly what kind of quest are we going to be sent of, Mr. Rooney?” “Well, Mr. Osbourne, for starters, call me Mickey.” “I can’t do that. It gets too confusing trying to keep Mikey and Mickey separate. I have to think of my readers, you know?” “Point taken. Well, you r quest is as timeless as I am. You must stop the evil Viniger’s plot to take over this world.” Mikey steps forward, “Don’t you mean Venger?” “No. Viniger is his younger brother. Venger got whacked a few years back by the first set of adventurers. Viniger took over almost as soon as his brother fell.” “So what happened to the Dungeon Master?” “He lost his life in the final battle.” “So how did you get the job?” “What’s with all the questions, Mikey? I got the job, same as you did. I rode that stupid roller coaster. I tell ya, when I get back, I’m having that thing shut down. But anyway, on to your mystic items…” “I’ve already figured it out for you. I’ll take the bow, Molly gets the cloak, Liz will definitely get the club, Jack should get the hat, and Dan gets the shield.” “Well you just got it all figured out, don’t you? Well, you’re wrong. Most of that stuff was lost in the final battle.” “So what do we get then?” “Well, the only two items left were the shield and the club. You were correct in your choices though. The club shall definitely be given to this beautiful and volatile lady.” Mickey hand a large wooed club to Liz. “YEAH! That’s what I’m talking about!” The group steps back from the overzealous Liz. “An excellent choice, if I do say so myself. The shield, as Mikey assumed will, in fact, go to Dan. However, upon the loss of the last wielder, the powers that be decided to give the next person not only the shield, but also a sword.” Dan accepts the sword and shield from Mickey Rooney. “And what am I supposed to do with this stuff? I’m a wrestler, not one of King Arthur’s nights.” “That’s the great things about these weapons, the give the wielder the instant knowledge to use them properly. Just as the club gave Liz the aggressive nature of a barbarian.” Jack begins to chuckle. “The club didn’t do that. That’s how Liz always is.” Liz smacks Jack upside his oversized head. “Okay then. Well, let’s hand out the rest of these items.” A few moments later. “Hey, I thought you said these were all destroyed,” exclaimed Mikey, now the wielder of the mystic bow. Molly places the cloak on her back and clasps it around her neck. Dan and Liz try to regain their composure from the hysterical laughter brought on by the ridiculous outfit that came with the magical hat Jack is now wearing. “I just said that to shut you up, Mr. Know-it-all.” Undoubted by Mickey Rooney, Mikey basks in the glow of his superior intellect. Dan steps up, breaking the growing tension between the two men. “So where do we find this Viniger, and how do we stop him?” Mikey puts a hand on Dan’s shoulder. “Don’t worry about it, Dan. I can lead us through this. I know enough about this stuff that I can get us through.” Mickey Rooney, obviously agitated at Mikey’s presumptuous nature, turns and fades into the horizon. “Your funeral.” Will our intrepid adventures be able to find and defeat Viniger and save this world? How will they find their way back to their own realm? Will Dan be able to get to his match on Friday? All this and more will be revealed in our next installment.
