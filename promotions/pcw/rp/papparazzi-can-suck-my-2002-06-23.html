Date: 6/23/02 “WHITE NINJA………IS A WOMAN” There is a point in every man’s life where he thinks of himself as some type of super being. Until a few days ago I think I thought of myself that way, but now it’s changed. Things change people, and one thing is finding out someone is on a higher level then you, but when a WOMAN is on a higher level then you, things become blurred. Publicity loves these types of things. They use their lack of human emotion to drill into your mind, but sometimes you’ve just had enough. The air is smoking and the temperature is mild. Summer is in the air, and the soothing senses that get me past the day are living it up. Being alone soothes the soul, helps you think of all the things you’ve ever done good in your life and all the things you’d change about your life if you could go back in time. You enjoy it after awhile, but you wish their could be someone there to share your stories with. *Puff* Smoke embalms the air, glances in both directions, children laughing parents chatting everything in harmony. *Screeeeech* Breaks sound and my neck turns in one quick rapid motion. The sound of a truck gets you paranoid and as I expected, it was nothing other than the Paparazzi. Sure you look at them first and you think these are innocent mothers and fathers who are just trying to make a living. You can say that all you want, but I have one word for these people, RUTHLESS. Media is like Satin and it seems these people are his subjects, but a man like myself isn’t going to give in to these people. They’ve chosen the wrong person to disturb today. Foots stomping heard behind me, but I clear it out of my mind. One ear and out the other I say, because they don’t mean anything to me. I just keep moving along and taking in the atmosphere around me. Excuse me Juan can we have a word with you. Sure that’s what the initial statement was, but in my head I heard it more along these lines “BLAH BLAH BLAH BLAH BLAH”. You think they’d realize I don’t want to talk, but no. After that little incident, the head honcho two hundred seventy five pound I never worked out in my life wannabe lover of hers comes over and tries to impress her, by getting me to speak to the reporters. Of course I’m not that stupid, I’m not going to give this guy the edge so he can have a chance of getting laid. Just remember children; this guy brought it on himself. Hey mister can you just take five minutes out of your day to talk to us about the recent happenings in PCW. *Hand on shoulder* POP! That’s right I decked him. I didn’t want to be so violent, but what was I to do, first this asshole tries to make like I’m doing some injustice by not speaking to reporters, and then he dares to stick his butter scotch dairy dipping fingers on the fine silk exterior of my five hundred dollar shirt. What came next really put me over the edge. Like expected they all run over to the big fatso lying on the ground, taking pictures to see if there was any injury on him that they can sue me with. Then after their done acting like his friend they step on his back to pursue me once again. There was one of the guys, that I must say was quiet brave with the statement he made, but bravery always equals stupidity and don’t try to tell me otherwise. Leave him alone, he’s still acting like he has something stuck up his ass because he lost to a woman. A woman! I said responding in my very very kind tone of voice. I try not to show them, that what they say gets to me, but this time I had to make an example. I’m sorry sir, but would you like to come over here and say that to me. Why so you could strike me like you did to the other defenseless man. Actually, Yes that’s exactly what I’d like to do to you. And what do you mean defenseless, I see we all have two hands right here, that’s all it takes to fight. It’s not my fault you people don’t take care of your bodies. No need to be a smart-ass man, at least I didn’t lose to a woman. Oh but your scared to come next to a man who weighs less then you and lost to a woman. Don’t turn this into a who is tougher then who conversation, because I’m too mature to sink to this level. From that point on I just pictured this guy with a penis as a head, because he acted like quite the dickhead. You know what, I’ll give you money hungry self absorbed leeches something to put in their paper. Plus, I’ll make it extremely easy on you all, because I’m going to give myself the interview. Excuse me Mr. Borro I have an itchy cunt and I’m just dying to know if you realize you lost to a woman. Yes, I do realize it, thank you so much for bringing it to my attention. Do you feel intimidated by woman now? Well my mother was a woman and she spanked me with her shoe, but other than not very much. What kind of respect do you have for Heaven Rayne, knowing that for so long she lasted with both you and Charlie Loc? Well for the record she got hers, but I guess her ass distracted me. I just wanted to tap it. Thank God the white ninja was a woman, because if she wasn’t I might have needed to spend a few extra bucks on a psychiatrist. I do have respect for her; she must work hard on her tight buns. Would it be fair for me to call you a male chauvinist? You just called me it so does it really matter. How do you think the PPV will turn out, especially with your retirement coming up? Do you think you’ll win? No I think I’ll lose, what kidn of question is that. I’d like to see someone say I think I’m going to lose. This must be the question you reporters ask when you run out of ideas. And you make like I don’t know about my own retirement, are you all retarded. Now comes that time when you all get up in my ass, trying to get in my pants or something. You snap photos and put catchy little captions under it saying “Borro Intimidated”. Then you think people will read it, but anyone who is really a fan of mine knows all you people are pure shit. Or at least they will once they see this promo. What’s your problem? You people are my problem, but you know what, out of the goodness of my heart, I’ll give you something to put on the front of your articles. *Turns around and pulls down pants, showing his ass.* EXTRA EXTRA READ ALL ABOUT IT, BORRO’S ASS HITS THE FRONT PAGE! Scene fades to black.
