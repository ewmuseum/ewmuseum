Y.D: Home Sweet Home

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th July 2002 After leaving all that had happened in Vietnam behind him…or rather a couple of thousand feet below him and a few hundred kilometers behind him, Charlie Loc had somehow officially been able to close the Pandora’s box which he had opened and put an end to one of the longest nights in history!Little does Charlie know that he’s just escaped one time zone and fate and delved head first into another one, meaning his long night will continue its run and the trouble is maybe far from over!Although the Vietnamese Viper was able to somehow elude the authorities back in Saigon, the chances of him eluding them in New York seemed pretty thin….How much more trouble was possible for Charlie to land himself in? When will the night ever end?! And just how much more luck does he have left….it has to be running low!Yet again time will tell its tale, as for the Vietnamese Viper and the hero of the story hopefully he can get back home on his own…without the assistance of a Police Car! Young And Dangerous Pt VI: Home Sweet Home Darkness surrounds everything, and with darkness usually comes some form of silence…but not in this case! As the darkness seems to be stretching out as far as the eye can see it is accompanied by a soft roar or rather a sweet purr of a rather large engine, which can be heard coming from somewhere nearby. As the pistons rotate and the engine rev’s the purring continues and soft, hypnotic vibrations or rhythms rather can be felt running through the floor or whatever it may be beneath. Wherever this place is it seems that it’s one large Massager, as every sound that it makes, every rhythm is produces, and every last spec of darkness that covers it like a blanket seem relaxing and make the environment feel somewhat peaceful…. BOOM~! BOOM~! BOOM~! Suddenly there are three large bangs followed by a series of smaller ones as the rhythm in the floor is disrupted and the engine turns from its soft purr into a giant roar as it fights whatever it was to get back in control. Nothing can be seen but from the sounds of it, everything is alright and back to normal. The Engine is purring away like before, the hypnotic vibrations are running through the floor and the darkness still envelopes every last inch around…but still something doesn’t feel right! It is said that people can sense when they are being watched….that they can feel the presence of another form in their vicinity in certain circumstances….this is no exception! Although the scene has returned to normal, there is a sensation that something is lurking in the darkness! Not the sensation that people receive when they were children, not fear…but something more along the lines of curiosity. An old saying states “Curiosity Killed The Cat” and most horror movie’s or “Stabber-flicks” as they are so called prove that Curiosity can kill but even though that thought is hovering around, the curiosity still remains! Nothing can be said or done to change that…. “Uhhhh…” A soft groaning noise can be heard coming from somewhere on the floor in the darkness as there is a shuffle before the sound of the engine overtakes it and returns everything to the state that it was in before. The moaning and groaning begins again as this time a shuffle follows which is then followed closely behind by a rather loud BANG~! As something is dropped which then clatters around on the floor of wherever this place is. They also say that whenever the hairs on the back of your neck prick up that there is someone from another time and place standing close to you…watching…waiting for something! It is almost the same sensation as fear but there is no human urge to resist it. Somehow, someway the mind mentally prepares people for what they possibly might face. Probably apart of the human defense mechanism but for some reason this time it’s not kicking in. “…..” Somehow over the loud purring of nearby engine, breathing can be heard. Not raspy breathing which is seen on the movies, but relaxed breathing as though someone has just awoken and is still a little drowsy. Suddenly whoever or whatever is breathing does something as a small blue, fluorescent light casts a faint glow throughout the darkness revealing a little of the surroundings and causing the thing to shield its eyes as they are not yet adjusted to the darkness. As the thing shields its face a part of a tattoo is revealed in the light as the man slowly removes his hand from in front of his face and looks down at the light which seems to be nothing more than a watch. The now revealed to be human peers over the small light as his long hair falls in front of it casting shadows where the light hits the walls of the setting as he seemingly reads the time which is none other than 8:00pm whatever the date is….. Finally the person stops staring at their watch and turns away, releasing his hand from the button which controls the light and plunging the scene back into complete darkness as there is a loud thud as the man seemingly falls back onto something hard and passes out…or falls back asleep whichever he may be doing. A few minutes pass of relaxing purring and vibrations of the good sort as the mystery man who is lying in what would appear to be the back of some sort of transportation relaxes and everything returns to the same way it was in the beginning…peaceful and relaxing. …..BOOOOM~! Suddenly there is a heaving exploding sound followed by a loud grinding noise as a squealing can be heard and the vibrations through the floor become ecstatic and violent as the engine seems to switch itself into overdrive, beginning to roar yet again. The silhouette of the man can be seen bolted upright as everything begins to rumble and roar all around as the darkness is disturbed and turns from a peaceful, relaxing paradise to a roaring, violent war-zone. The grinding and roaring continues for a few more minutes before everything comes to an abrupt holt, sending things crashing around in the confinement that the man is in as the vibrations through the floor begin to fade as the engine can be hear slowly powering down. As everything slowly begins to fade into nothingness the darkness once again begins to seem peaceful…but there is a stir in the atmosphere as the person who is confined inside is restless. Suddenly everything becomes eerily silent….the darkness seems to be the only thing that keeps it from being a dream to a reality, then VROOM~! All of a sudden there is robotic grinding noise as light begins to creep in behind where the mystery man is as the door to the storage confinement is slowly being opened or lowered rather, letting the light take over the darkness and causing a blinding effect for a few seconds. As the light adjusts the scene becomes clear as an Airplane Cargo Hold as surrounding the familiar looking man who is sprawled on the floor are a few suitcases with clothes spread around him. Around that there are piles upon piles of suitcases and other luggage, all bound up…. How the hell this man even made it into the Cargo Hold is amazing. How he survived is a miracle as most Cargo Holds are airtight and the chances of survival within them are limited, so it is obvious this man needed to get away from wherever he came desperately. The man who is lying sprawled on a pile of suitcases slowly lowers his hands from over his face and springs to his feet. His huge puffy black jacket and silver jeans flopping around in the sudden burst of wind as he quickly dives behind a set of suitcases as to not be spotted…. The only thing is that the man reveals himself to be the Vietnamese Viper himself…Charlie Loc as it would appear he managed to stow away from Saigon and make it safely back to his home country. “Don’t Count Your Chickens Before They Hatch” is an old and wise saying and one that would be used in this case, because if Charlie had any thought that he was home free….he was wrong! Clank~! Clank~! Sounds can be heard followed by voices as footsteps become clear as two men enter the Cargo Hold at the entrance, dressed all in black suits and sunglasses and holding flashlights. “The little bastard shouldn’t be too hard to find, then again those Asians are probably small enough to fit into even a toilet bag!” The posh American voice of one of the men can be heard as they both chuckle at the comment before the flip on their flashlights. “How much were we told we’d get for this slant eyed freak?” The other dark haired agent asks his counter part ass he shines his flashlight off to one side of the Hold before moving over to investigate. There is a clanking noise as the other Agent moves to the opposite side of the Hold and begins to shine his flashlight looking around before replying. “I got told we’d get $1,000 for sending him back…plus a bonus from the boss.” Behind the shadow of his flashlight the Agent can be seen smiling as he replies back with a chuckle. “Well lets hurry this shit up, it’s nearly 8:30 and I want to get home before Springer starts!” Both men begin to chuckle to each other as the search around the Hold obviously looking for Charlie as he pokes his head around the corner of a pile of suitcases in the middle of the aisle as he ducks back around. Taking a deep breath he looks back around and notices the Agents are either side of him and making their way over to his hiding place. Knowing he can’t take any chances of being caught he removes the watch from off of his wrist and launches it to the back of the Hold, causing it to clatter and both Agents to look. “I think we found him!” One of the Agent smiles and nods as both men reach for their pistols at their waistsbefore shining their flashlights in the direction of the noise and advancing past Charlie’s position. Knowing that it’s do or die Charlie dashes around the corner of the hold and sprints down the ramp making a clanking sound as the Agents yell and give pursuit. As he makes it off of the ramp Charlie is greeted by a cold blast of chilly New York air and welcomed home by the darkness of the night that seems to be never-ending. Looking around quickly Charlie darts off to the terminal and through the sliding doors before being pursued by the Agents. Giving hot pursuit, chasing young Loc into the terminal the Agents begin to yell and flash their badges as people scream as they wave their guns around and Charlie disappears into the crowd. The sea of people at the Air Port is amazing as Charlie somehow manages to outrun the two Federal Agents and escape into the crowd of about a Million and One people who are all terrified of the Agents holding badges and guns. Whatever the case Charlie seemed to have escaped the grasp of the law once again but still continues to run knowing that if he stops there’s a good chance he might be caught…. Finally making his way to the entrance, where he makes an untimely exit, Charlie bumps into a couple sending them bowling over in the doorway and clothes and luggage flying everywhere as all heads turn to see Charlie run through the large sliding doors of the Terminal. The yelling can be heard by the couple as the male helps his girlfriend back onto her feet as Charlie wastes no time and opens the back of a yellow Taxi’s Cab and slams it behind him. The driver, an old looking and rather fat white man adjusts the rear view mirror to see Charlie who is laying out of breath on the backseat as he squints his beady eyes to try and get a good look at him before he asks him a question in a gruff Brooklyn voice. “You got money kid?” He continues to look at the back seat as Charlie reaches into a pocket of his thick jacket and smiles his sinister grin. “How this for money white boy?!” Charlie leans forward and presses a knife to the drivers throat as his eyes bulge to his head and he throws his hands up in defense. Charlie slinks back down in the back seat keeping the knife to his neck as he orders the driver where to take him. “China Town..and step on it muh-fucker or I going to make sure your smile as big as your gut!” His whiny Asian voice is followed up by a chuckle as the driver gulps and adjusts the golfing cap on his head as he places his hand on the wheel and puts his foot down on the accelerator. “China Town? Y-y-you got it kid!” The driver manages to stutter as Charlie smiles in approval keeping the knife to his neck as they pull off onto the main road with New York City in front of them. As the taxi begins to disappear down the freeway Charlie can be heard screaming from the backseat as the radio can then be heard pumping and the driver puts his pedal to the metal and they speed up down the highway. The Air Port slowly becomes further and further away as Police Cars can be seen rushing towards it with their sirens blazing not knowing their criminal has escaped yet again. They say “Darkness Be My Friend”, it indeed is Charlie’s friend as the Vietnamese Viper has managed to elude authorities once again and slip off into the darkness of the night unknown and walking away with nothing on his mind. Being labeled the Vietnamese Viper is something, but living up to the name is something else as Charlie Loc has proved once again he’s nothing to be judged, trusted or approached as the story of the Asian Gangster finally comes to an end….or this chapter anyway. Wind picks up as New York City shines like a beacon in the night sky, the night which has managed to last a good 17hrs! As the lights blink it looks amazing and everything seems peaceful, or it may seem peaceful! The Vietnamese Viper is back in town and no doubt trouble is not far behind…..
