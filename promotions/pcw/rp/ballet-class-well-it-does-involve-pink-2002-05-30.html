Ballet Class? Well It Does Involve PINK!

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 30, 2002 Visions of sugarplums danced in their heads...literally. Several female ballerinas lined the full-length mirrors in the back of the room, bending and tugging in every which way. Their tight pink leotards clung to their body like white on rice. The girls had their hair nice and tucked in a bun, and their makeup glistening off of the reflection of the mirror. Ballet Teacher: (Stretching as well) “Ok girls, hurry up and finish your stretching for today’s class. Today we will be learning about the five positions of the feet and we will also be learning a little bit about the barre. Remember girls, ballet is an artform and is not something to be taken lightly. Everyone please take your positions.” The girls gracefully come away from the rail over by the mirror into the center of the room. They line up in a large square with the teacher in the center. Ballet Teacher: “Now girls, All leg movements begin and end from the five basic position. The dancer should attempt to center their weight equally between the feet. Keep your muscles in your thighs and buttocks tightened.” The teacher prances around the room to check each and every girl’s position. Ballet Teacher: (Grabbing one girl’s ankles) “Put them like this...there you are.” She continues onward to the other girls, commenting on them one by one. Ballet Teacher: “Good...excellent...could use a little work...outstanding...you’ve got potential, but that’s about it.” The teacher paused and looked around. She then glanced at the clock with an intolerant look adorning her face. Ballet Teacher: (Addressing all of the girls) “Aren’t we missing someone.” Almost on command, the two large entrance doors swing open and in come Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart, making a fashionably late entrance. Not only that, but they are dressed in large pink tutus surrounding their waists. The teacher gives them each a stern look and they toss their pink bags down and hustle over to their places in the square. Ballet Teacher: “Do you have any idea what is going on right now boys?” Jack and Johnny both look at each other and shrug their shoulders. They then look around the room only to check and see what the girls are doing. All of the sudden Johnny tosses his arms, with clenched hands, up into the air over his head, shifting his feet into the position the other girls have their feet in...Jack is soon to follow. Ballet Teacher: “What in God’s name are you doing?” Johnny Heart: “A Plié?” Jack Valentine: “Good word Johnny.” Johnny Heart: “Thanks Jack.” The two Heartbreakers smack each other on the butt before the Ballet Teacher quickly interrupted their “mini-celebration.” Ballet Teacher: (Shouting) “That is not a Plié!!! Now, be quiet and pay attention before I kick you both out.” The Heartbreakers both put their hands up as if to signal that they mean no harm. The other girls in the class laugh at the situation as they realize that their ballet teacher is a real bitch. Ballet Teacher: “Now girls...” Jack Valentine: “Ahem...” Ballet Teacher: (Rolling her eyes) “Excuse me...now PEOPLE...remember to keep the knees straight and in a direct line with the center of the turned out feet. Never push your foot out beyond the knee as this can cause problems. Keep the head centered, neck free of tension and eyes straight ahead.” The girls...and boys move themselves into position again. The girls in the class get themselves into perfect form, with their feet perpendicular and the heads up...their eyes straight ahead. Jack and Johnny on the other hand struggle to find the right position. Johnny tumbles over out of his lack of knowledge for what he is doing. Ballet Teacher: (With the other girls laughing in the background) “Could you please tell me what you are doing?” Johnny hops back up and gets into his same position as he did when he entered the room. Jack chuckles and the other girls in the room begin to hysterically laugh. The ballet teacher is not amused however and gives Johnny a smack on the wrist, catching a bit of his tutu as well. Ballet Teacher: (Arms folding) “May I ask why you two are here in the first place?!?” Jack Valentine: (In a humorously serious manner) “We just figured that ballet would give us an extra advantage in the wrestling ring, making us more flexible and giving us a better mind set.” The ballet teacher seemingly agrees with Jack and starts to continue class before being interrupted by Johnny. Johnny Heart: “And we just like looking at all of this PINK!” The girls in the class snicker at Johnny’s immature manner towards the teacher. Jack reaches out his hand for Johnny to high five and the teacher smacks it down once again. Jack Valentine: “Okay, okay...continue with your little class.” After things calm down in the ballet room, the teacher returns to the center of the square. Ballet Teacher: “Now, everyone head over to the barre.” The girls prance over to the barre and place one arm on the ballet bar. Jack and Johnny humorously goof around, tip toeing like little fairies over to the bar...of course not to the teacher’s delight. Ballet Teacher: (Huffing) “All dance training begins at the barre. Here the dancer executes a series of exercises carefully designed to prepare the body for the exacting demand of the classical dance. Each exercise is meant to strengthen specific parts of the body. These exercises done correctly, develop the dancer gradually, giving the ability to achieve a balanced and controlled movement. Does everyone understand so far?” The girls nod in agreement and the teacher continues down the line looking for Jack and Johnny. Jack and Johnny seem to be in a trance looking at themselves in the mirror, not flinching for a second, but throwing out a random pose every now and then. Ballet Teacher: (Shouting) “BOYS!” Jack and Johnny jump and then nod in agreement like the rest of the girls. Ballet Teacher: (Continuing her lesson) “The support of the barre frees the dancer from the strain of maintaining control of the entire body before the muscles are sufficiently prepared. Barre practice remains an important part of the dancer's technique throughout professional life. It is always used as a preparation to every class and performance. The Barre should never be tightly gripped, leaned on or pulled on. Each exercise should be performed slowly and simply. ” As this is said, the girls begin stretching more on the barre. However Jack and Johnny begin pulling hard on the bar, ripping it off the wall. The ballet teacher has a stunned look of shock on her face, but the other girls just laugh at the situation. Ballet Teacher: (Pulling on her hair) “Did you not hear a word I just said!?!?” Jack Valentine: “Um...” Johnny Heart: “Pink?” Ballet Teacher: (Flailing her arms) “I’ve had it with you two...get out...out of my class!” Jack Valentine: “But...” Johnny Heart: “We didn’t even get a lesson in...we wanted to be prepared for our extreme title shot this Friday and round 2 of the tag team tournament.” Ballet Teacher: “This is ballet gentlemen...not a wrasslin’ ring. Please pick up your bags and remove yourselves from the building!” Jack and Johnny both shrug. They go and grab their bags and the girls all say goodbye to The Heartbreakers, some even throwing a phone number and the handsome pair as they exit. With the pink bags strapped around their shoulder, the Heartbreakers solemnly exit the building and head for their pink Caddy...tutus and all. Jack Valentine: “Well, at least we got to see some PINK!” Johnny Heart: “Yeah, but I kind of wish we would’ve learned more.” Johnny tries to pose again in the same humorous way he did before. The ballet teacher looks out of the window at the two and violently slides the blinds shut as the scene fades to PINK!
