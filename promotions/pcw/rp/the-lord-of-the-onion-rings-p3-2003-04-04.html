The Lord Of The Onion Rings (P3)

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/03 (The camera opens up. You can see The Overweighter, Throwdough and Sam Ganja walking up a path. They have climbed half of the mountain. The Overweighter has been knackered since the first 10 meters. His shirt is soaking wet from all the sweating he has been doing. They reach a hut that is by the side of the path. The Overweighter wants to have a sit down and get a drink. So they walk up to the hut, it says on the side of the hut Enter If You Dare. The Overweighter being stupid like he is just ignores the sign and walks in. The hall way that they walked into was covered in dust and had no light coming in from anywhere. There are pictures up on the wall, when you look closer you can see through the thick dust. There is a mountain with a building on top of it, The Overweighter walks over to it and wipes it. His eyes start to pop out, his mouth opens and he is amazed by what he has seen.) The Overweighter: That’s Big Mouths! Throwdough: Yeah that’s the place The Lord of The Onion Rings lives. The Overweighter: Why Big Mouths!?!! Sam: The owner of Big Mouths gave away the company to The Lord of The Onion Rings. Throwdough: He closed down Big Mouths and started taking onion rings from everyone. The Overweighter: Why! Why did it have to be Big Mouths! Right we are going to get up this mountain and we are going to give this Onion Ring guy a piece of me! Throwdough: Let’s get out of this hut then! The Overweighter: …After this rest. (The Overweighter walks over to a door. He goes to open it but it doesn’t open and he walks right into it. Bang, his face smacks it. He tries to open it again. It’s not opening. He decides to lean against the door while turning the handle. The door doesn’t open and surprisingly the door doesn’t break either. The Overweighter stops, he scratches his head while trying to figure out how to open it. The door has not locks on it so it can’t be locked. He walks back so he can have a run up and kick the door. He goes to run but Throwdough walks over to the door and opens it. The Overweighter already started to run without seeing Throwdough open the door. He runs straight into the other room. He runs into a load of cobwebs and it’s all over him. He is fighting to get them off but it’s not working. Throwdough and Sam are standing in the doorway laughing at The Overweighter.) The Overweighter: Ah! Get it off! Get it off! Throwdough: Ha, ha, ha. Okay wait a minute. Sam: Overweighter sir what’s that big black thing on your head? The Overweighter: A Spider AHHH! (The Overweighter starts to runs around in circles panicking like mad. He is hitting his head as well, he sees a frying pan on the side so he picks it up still going mad and hits himself in the head with it.) Sam: You missed. (The Overweighter stops and hits himself in the head with the frying pan again. He then starts to rapidly hitting himself in the head with the frying pan. Throwdough sees a fridge and thinks the only way to calm him down is to show him some food. He opens the fridge without knowing The Overweighter is going mad again and running around like a headless chicken. He runs straight into the door of the fridge just as Throwdough opens it. It hits The Overweighter. He stops going mad, the fridge hit his belly it couldn’t hit his face because his belly comes out like he has got a rubber ring around him. He looks at his belly and makes a weird face. His cheeks puff out and his belly gets bigger. All of a sudden The Overweighter goes a gigantic burp.) The Overweighter: BURRRRRRRRP!!! Throwdough: Wow that stinks! Sam: What did you eat!?!! The Overweighter: That feels better. Sam: Sorry Overweighter that’s my mistake, it’s your hair not a spider. (The Overweighter looks at Sam with evil and atomic hatred burning in his eyes. He wants vengeance on Sam.) The Overweighter: Run…Now! (Sam gets scared and starts to run out of the front door of the hut. He begins to run up the hill.) Throwdough: Your not going to hurt him, are you? The Overweighter: Maybe depends of if I think up a good revenge. Throwdough: We better get going and try to catch up with Sam, his probably near the top by now. The Overweighter: Oh well. (The Overweighter and Throwdough walk out of the hut, they begin to walk up the path. They walk awhile but then they stop. They have a choice of two ways of going, left or right. They both look at each other and they both have confusion in their eyes.) The Overweighter: What way are we going to go then? Throwdough: What one do you think Sam took? The Overweighter: Don’t know, one side has shadows and dying trees on it. The other has fresh and healthy trees with fruit on it… He could have taken either. He might have taken the dark route because he thinks we would expect him to take the bright route. But he might think that and then think we might think that he would do that and he then would take the bright route. Throwdough: It’s like poker, it’s it a bluff or is it a double bluff? The Overweighter: Yeah, We could split up? Throwdough: If we do your going the dark route. The Overweighter: Okay we will both go that way. (The Overweighter and Throwdough take the bright and beautiful route. Are walking down the path and every step they have it gets darker and darker. Until it’s so dark they can’t see where they are going very well. They carry on going but Throwdough walks into something sticking out the ground quite high.) Throwdough: Ouch! The Overweighter: What? Throwdough: I walked into something. The Overweighter: I’m standing on something weird… I think it’s a load of flowers? Throwdough: What’s that thing? (There is a bright light floating over them. It’s like a ball of light. It’s the size of a mini football (Mini Soccer ball).) The Overweighter: I think it’s an orb. Throwdough: Orb? The Overweighter: Yeah it’s a think that spirits create when they want to me noticed or trying to communicate with someone or something. Throwdough: You mean ghosts? The Overweighter: Yeah… Throwdough: … I think this is a bad time to tell you this but never mind! I have heard stories about a graveyard in the mountains, it was a dark Graveyard and it’s never light. But there are open graves everywhere and if someone falls in it the grave would fill it self. The Overweighter: I think we are standing on someone’s grave… Throwdough: … What are we going to do? If we carry on we might fall in a grave but if we don’t were stuck. The Overweighter: We need a form of light! Throwdough: What about the orb? The Overweighter: You can’t use that we would have to catch it and that’s impossible. Throwdough: I have a match in my pocket, would that do? The Overweighter: It would burn out to quickly, I have to warn you. I feel a fart coming on. Throwdough: That’s a brilliant idea! Well done you’re a genius. The Overweighter: Finally someone notices my talent! I have been telling people for years but no one believes me. Throwdough: Okay, try and build up that fart of yours. So I can light it, if you build it up enough it will light up are way through the graveyard! The Overweighter: Okay, but if you need that then their no need to build it up. Throwdough: Okay, bend over and face the way we came in so your ass is facing the way we want to go. The Overweighter: Okay, I will tell you when I am going to fart. (The Overweighter bends over and starts to squeeze as hard as he can. All of a sudden he does a huge fart.) The Overweighter: Now! (Throwdough lights the match, Overweighter farts and it makes a huge explosion. It lights up the whole graveyard and there is a clear path in front of them to the exit of the graveyard.) The Overweighter: Ah it burns! Throwdough: Okay, I know the way follow me. The Overweighter: Okay, give me something to hold on to. Throwdough: Grab my shirt. (The Overweighter grabs his shirt and they both begin to slowly walk across the graveyard in the dark. They get to the other end and it starts to get lighter and lighter as they carry on going. They get to total lightness, they see footprints on the muddy floor.) The Overweighter: Could that be Sam’s footprints? Throwdough: Yeah that’s about the size of his feet. (They follow the footprints, they lead them to The Lord of the Onion Ring’s home. The Overweighter looks up at the building. He sees the Big Mouths sign. He takes a massive gulp.) The Overweighter: We are here, are you ready to go in? Throwdough: … The Overweighter: You don’t have to come in if you don’t want to. Throwdough: Okay, see you later then. (Throwdough runs away.) The Overweighter: I was joking!!! (Throwdough is already gone. Overweighter walks over to the building, he gets to the steps and he begins to walks up them towards the door.) The Overweighter: Here we go, be ready for anything. (The Overweighter goes to open the door. Suddenly the door opens on it’s own. The Overweighter walks in, it looks like the same Big Mouths Overweighter likes to go to. He walks over to the counter. The building seems empty. All of a sudden a big man walks into the room from the back. He is wearing a cloak, he rips off his cloak and you see his face.) Lord Of The Onion Rings: I am the lord of the rings, what is your purpose in my home? The Overweighter: Your, your, your…Dorn Tracer! I have a match against you for your title! Lord of The Onion Rings: I’m going to slap your silly… Spotty Teenager: If you don’t wake up and get your order! (The Overweighter opens his eyes, looks at the spotty teenager with a weird look on his face.) Spotty Teenager: Sir, here is your order. You wanted onion rings, here they are. The Overweighter: What happened? Spotty Teenager: Nothing, okay I know they took longer than I said but here they are now. The Overweighter: Oh good. (The Overweighter gets up and walks over to the counter. He picks up his onion ring and walks out. He walks past an old lady by the door of Big Mouths.) Old Lady: Ha, ha, ha, ha. (The Overweighter walks around the corner and the camera fades to blank.)
