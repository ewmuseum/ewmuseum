Three From the Past......The Gathering Storm

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-14-2003 The scene opens into a large open field. The moon illumines the area, and a pair of men stand in the darkness, the larger of the two holding a torch. The shot zooms downward and we are met with the visage of Arthur Pendragon, decked out in a newly forged set of shining golden armor. At his waist is strapped a newly forged sword of black steel. His companion is the Ancient One, but Arthur knows him as Merlin. Merlin looks quite pissed as he surveys the field, and Arthur seems a tad nervous… Merlin: Damned Lionheart…His feeble mind was able to grasp that the man he was to destroy WAS his progeny. DAMN HIM TO HELL! Arthur: Thy will brought him here, can it not send him hence? Merlin: Nay, Arthur. I control the soul, but the body must be slain before I can collect mine due…If only he hadn’t been so pigheaded about the whole affair. Venneman Pendragon MUST be destroyed. I WILL rule as I have always been meant to, only now it will be I who wields the power directly. I’ll be a puppet master no longer…Mark my words, Arthur… Arthur looks at Merlin quizzically, but shakes his head of whatever thoughts had crept up. Merlin, though, rubs his hands together and reaches beneath his cloak and pulls out a wooden staff…(Again, NOT a sexual reference)…He mumbles some words over the wood and the staff begins to glow with a blue light… Merlin: Thou knowest that alone, you would fail as well, does thou not? Arthur:…. Merlin: His skill with the blade surpasses even yours, and Excalibur has been his for years. It has forgotten thy touch, young master. And so, to leap this particular hurdle, I will fashion for thee an army, and thou shalt lead it against Richard and Venneman… Arthur: Is that not a tad much, Merlin? An army to do battle against but two men? Merlin: When you have lived to be as old as I, you learn never to take chances… With a wave of his hand, Merlin silences Arthur before he can speak. He then points the staff towards the emptiness of the plain, and waves it in a circular motion…Almost immediately, man shapes begin to form out of the very air. Men on horseback, men carrying pikes, men with short swords on foot, a couple of men with rifles even. As they come into being under the full moon, they stare around bewilderedly… Arthur: Good Christ Merlin….What hast thy done? Merlin: As I resurrected thee, so too have I brought forth an army of the dead, but not quite. Each man here was destined to die a death on a battlefield somewhere in history. What I have done is whisked them away to here, one instant before they were to meet their maker. Arthur: Then these men are mortal? Merlin: In every way…Now, pay attention and thee shall learn why these men shall fight for thee… Merlin takes a deep breath, and as he begins to speak, it would seem that he grows tenfold in stature. Certainly his voice is now that of a giant as it booms over the countryside… Merlin: MY LEGION!! STARE NOT AS IF YOU KNOW NOT WHAT HAPPENS, BUT HEAR ME NOW AND LEARN THY FATE! I AM MORE OR LESS WHAT YOU WOULD CALL A WIZARD…A MAGICIAN; A SORCEROR EVEN. I HAVE SAVED THE LOT OF YOU FROM DEATH, AND ALL I ASK IN RETURN IS THAT YOU FIGHT A BATTLE FOR ME!! The group of men stare at each other, still confused, and horse whinny with nervousness in the night…. Merlin: A SIMPLE TASK IS WHAT I HAVE IN STORE…DEFEAT TWO MEN FOR ME, AND THEE SHALL LIVE IN THIS TIME, TO DO WHATEVER YOUR HEARTS DESIRE. FAIL ME, AND YOUR FATE WILL BE THE ONE THAT I SAVED YOU FROM. YOU WILL RETURN TO THE INSTANT YOU WERE SAVED TO LIVE OUT THE FINAL SECONDS OF YOUR PATHETIC LIVES. WHAT I ASK IS BUT A SMALL PITTANCE FOR HAVING SAVED YOU. WILL YOU FIGHT??? The men, only having Merlin’s foulable word to go on, engage in a group “shrug” before raising their weapons and engaging in a group…. Men: HURRAH! Merlin smiles and turns to Arthur… Merlin: You see? When it is life that one is fighting for, man will do anything…ANYTHING!!! HaHa….Hahaha…..MwaHa….Mwa HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Arthur: Good Christ… The scene changes as Merlin continues his maniacal laughter… ******************FLASH****************** Dawn broke bright over the horizon, a strange thing in England, normally so drab, so gray. Perhaps better days are indeed on the way. At least, that is the hope. The shot comes around from a beautiful shot of the horizon and sets upon a group of three men, gathered round a small fire. Richard, Joseph and our resident hero, Venneman Pendragon. Pendragon: Mighty fine breakfast, my Lord… Richard: When one spend years at a time trying to reclaim the Holy Land, one learns the art of making breakfast. Even if the one in question is in fact the King of England. Pendragon: The Crusades…That must have been a glorious and hallowed time to exist, my Lord… Richard merely stares at the longing Pendragon. His look turns to one of sadness and regret. He shakes his head and his massive shoulders slump. Pendragon looks at him questioningly, and Richard finally speaks… Richard: ‘Twas not a war I would gladly fight again, young Sire. Countless men lost their lives, and what was the reason? Pendragon: Infidels held the Holy Land. Is that not reason enough? Richard: We called them barbarians; uncivilized. The Moors held the same god as we Christians. We just never allowed ourselves to understand it…I most of all. I became obsessed with the task of driving the moors from Jerusalem. The need of it consumed me. I lost many compatriots in the futile efforts of reclaiming the lost land; I lost many friends. Had I to do it over again, I would forego the missions and opt for a more peaceful solution… Pendragon: Aye…Peace. I have ne’er been one for peace. It seems that trouble always has a way of seeking out the last noble. It matters not. I live for the purpose I derive from battle. The begging of the opponents, the fear that men feel when they look into mine eyes. THAT is important… IMPORTANT. THAT IS WHAT RULES THE LORD KING. VENGEANCE. I SEEK VENGEANCE UPON THE WICKED. THAT IS WHAT I DO… Pendragon: Is that truly the way that mine mind works? Joseph shakes his head while Richard merely looks at Pendragon… Richard: What art thy speaking of? And who to? Pendragon: ‘Tis not of any importance…Perhaps it would be best if we were on our way…We have serious business to attend to. Joseph: He speaks truth… The two knights turn to stare at Joseph who until this point has been rather quiet… Joseph: HE already knows what has transpired between the two of you. HE has probably already concocted a scheme to destroy the both of you…And you drone on spouting loads of mindless drivel. What is the matter with the two of you??? Richard: The small one does have a point, Pendragon… Venneman rubs his chin before rising and walking swiftly to Xerxes, who waits patiently while munching on some grass… Pendragon: If the man is a true mind, he will raise an army. He will know that combined, thee and I art quite the force to be reckoned with… Richard: Hmm…two men against an army…Let us hope he thinks not the way thou dost… Joseph: If he does, the nobles will be there to help. We are seven hundred strong, and word has already gone out that we are to meet at Stonehenge. Already a detachment of cavalry is riding hard from behind us, trying to catch up to us. If we make the same time today as we have made the past two, they shall overtake us by nightfall, and then we will fear not this…Army… FEAR? HE BELIEVES I FEAR???? Pendragon glares at Joseph, rage in his eyes…. Pendragon: I fear not the army…. Richard: Aye…In fact, what I fear the most is that THY army is made up of halflings such as thee… Richard chuckles and in an instant, Joseph has drawn a knife from somewhere about his person and is holding it at his throat. Richard cannot help but let his surprise show… Joseph: All things are not as they would appear, my King…Never judge, lest ye be wrong… Pendragon: So thou art fast. How will thee handle thyself if this is to come to blows? Joseph: I can handle myself. You worry about thee… Pendragon shakes his head… Pendragon: That half and half talk that thou engages in really grinds upon mine nerves, Joseph… Joseph pulls the knife down and makes it disappear up one of his sleeves before smiling at Pendragon… Joseph: Than I shall keep it up….Lord King… Pendragon: Bah….Bastard. Let us saddle up and take out leave of this place….I feel eyes upon mine back… *************FLASH************* Merlin looks into a small mirror and smiles. He has been watching nearly every move of team Pendragon. He shakes his head in what appears to be a mix of both admiration and loathing… Merlin: Aye…You are a smart one. Why would I risk failure by not raising an army? And now I know that thou will have one to command as well. This will be a battle for the ages. Pendragon against Pendragon for the fate of all mankind. If only you weren’t so damned headstrong. YOU would be the one I would place just beneath me in the new world that is to come… Merlin shakes his head with mock disappointment… Merlin: But, alas, I doubt that thee would be inclined to be leashed by a wizard. Oh well. I guess my plan will have to go forward as it goes forward. There is no place for a man such as thee in MY future…. Merlin shakes his head once more as he puts the small mirror beneath his cloak and turns from the shot as the scene fades to black… TO BE CONTINUED
