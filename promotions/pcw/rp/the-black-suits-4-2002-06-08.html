The Black Suits [4]

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 8, 2002 Wrestling Locker Room 11:07 am It was a startling move...perhaps a bit unexpected. No, let me rephrase that - it was unexpected. And now, Agent Chopsticks and Agent Sourpuss were laying on the ground, a bit pissed, and a bit embarrassed. Now, I know what you're thinking, 'Embarrassed? Professionals like Chopsticks and Sourpuss? I don't believe it!' Yes, I too, your oh so intelligent narrator (when Tom Lexian is in the scene, all 'I' 's are referring to himself. I take a nice little break.), could not believe my eyes when I saw their cheeks form with red blush and their heads down, as if they had tripped in front of a very attractive woman, or man, if that was their thing. I'm not THAT omnicient. Perhaps they had been so cocky, with their armed guns and their spiffy suits, that they had forgotten that Tom was a bit clever. They had taken him as a dumb jock that knew very little about information, the world around him, or even how to make a decent distraction. When he yelled out, 'Sporty!' they both turned around as if the flying butterfly man had been directly behind them, ready to bite their heads off and take the crystal for himself. Agent Chopsticks is a very bright, young man, but he had fallen for the trick and felt a bit stupid. He had been trying to prove to Sourpuss for the past few months that he didn't need an attendant or a teacher, and that he could handle himself on the open road. He had a lot of respect for Sourpuss, but he was continually getting the feeling that Sourpuss was like an overbearing mother to him, that didn't want to let go of the reigns. It was an unfortunate feeling, and hurt the student - teacher bond they had. Agent Sourpuss was picking himself off the ground, feeling around the ground for the gun that he had dropped. He felt naked without it, since it was one of the only forms of protection that he had. He was bordering forty now, and his physical strength and prowess were leaving him. Back in his twenties, he remembered lifting weights, boxing, and sorts of other activities to test his strength. He was proud of his build, and he knew that he was a great athlete. That all changed when he joined The Agency, however, and soon his mind was just as an important weapon as the body. It was an odd feeling to finally appreciate the smarts and intellect that he did possess, although that mind of his soon overtook most of his daily activities, and he let his physical prowess disintegrate into nothing. It was a combination of broken skills and old age which kept Sourpuss from being the strongmen he had always been. Perhaps it was pride that kept in the hunt - a pride that told him that he could still be an agent. Sourpuss found his gun, saddled underneath Chopsticks, who was still looking for his own weapon. Sourpuss told him not to bother, as he had seen Lexian take off with it after he had tackled them both. He was smart - now he had a weapon to use besides his muscle. Not only that, but the kid had some brains too, and that made him even more dangerous. Sourpuss tried to convince himself that Lexian probably didn't know how to use the gun, but he couldn't quite convince himself fully. It wasn't that hard to fire a gun - you just squeeze the trigger. Although, that still requires being able to aim and decipher the target properly. That could be hard considering that the target would be a six foot man-butterfly who could fly and had amazing agility and speed. That was his target, although he probably didn't know it yet. Chopsticks got to his feet, rubbing his hands together which was now dirtied from being pressed to the floor twice. Once, being tackled by Lexian, and a second time, by his friend who followed right behind him. That was two embarrassments in one day - not a good start for the case. Once again, they had forgotten about the man standing behind Lexian during the entire confrontation. They didn't think he was going to be much of a factor, considering that he was probably scared shitless after what they had heard them tell Lexian, but they had underestimated him just as they had underestimated Lexian himself. That was two underestimations - very dangerous. The second tackling caused them to completely lose track of where Lexian had run off to, and now they had no clue where they should start searching. The longer the crystal was on him, the sooner Sporty would find him and take it by force. Not a great proposition. 'What are we going to do now?' Chopsticks asked, brushing off his suit. There was a brown streak going down his back, probably where either Lexian or his friend had stepped on him on their haste to escape, but Sourpuss wasn't going to say anything. 'We find Lexian,' Sourpuss replied, sticking his gun back into his shoulder holster. He checked his jacket in a similiar fashion that Chopsticks had, but he was enamored to find out that it wasn't stained. Bully for him - now he didn't have to do laundry when this was all over. 'He couldn't have gotten very far, unless he headed for his car, which would have been a smart move.' 'We didn't see his car when we came into the arena though...' 'That doesn't mean he couldn't have been parked somewhere else,' Sourpuss replied. He tried to put himself into Lexian's shoes - where would he go if he was being chased by two guys in black suits and a killer butterfly? Sourpuss just couldn't figure it out - it vexed him. Chopsticks looked visibly upset, as it looked like he really missed his gun. Sourpuss would have offered him his gun, just to make him feel more secure, but he didn't want to get caught with Sporty without a weapon in hand. Sporty may have been a butterfly, but he could break his arm with one quick move. Sourpuss couldn't even do twenty push ups nowadays - how the hell was he supposed to fight an alien bug without a weapon? Besides, he would still be able to grab him an auxilary weapon from The Agency's weapon depot when they got back, but first thing was first - they had to find Lexian before Sporty did. Sourpuss and Chopsticks made their way out of the locker room and headed into the hallway leading out of the arena. As they left, they were immediately hit with the scent of fresh air. It amazed Sourpuss how bad a locker room could smell, and how many guys could stand it and actually wanted to spend more time in there. Well, that was their problem, not his. If it was up to Sourpuss, he would be able to work outdoors all the time. Unfortunately, his assignments included space trips as well, and there is no smell in space, only vaccum and more vaccum. The reason that it was so important the crystal be returned to the Agency, was that Lexian was in grave danger every second he held on to it. There was some sort of odd connection that the crystal had to Sporty, which wasn't all to surprising considering that the crystal held so much power for him. But Sporty could actually home into the crystal, and can actually pinpoint its location. Sourpuss was afraid Sporty would find Lexian, before they could catch up with him. And knowing how head strong Lexian has been so far, he might wind up on tomorrow's newspapers. Not that Sourpuss would be entirely sad to see the guy go - he did completely embarrass him. The sun was now directly overhead Sourpuss and Chopsticks as they walked outside of the arena and into the general public parking lot. The lot was sparely populated with cars from wrestlers and/or attendants who were currently working in the arena. As expected, Lexian's car was nowhere to be found, although they hadn't seen it in the lot when they first arrived. They walked into the sidewalk, looking around for any clues that might lead them into the right direction to where he might have gone. Things were looking suddenly bleak, as nothing short of, 'Lexian went this way! Go here!' was giving them any hints on the mystery. Chopsticks sighed as well, almost being able to read Sourpuss' mind and realize what he was thinking - They were fucked. Ahhhhhh! CRASH! BOOM! Screaming, shouts of horror, pleads for mercy....that could only mean one thing.... Sporty.... Chopsticks and Soupuss looked over to their left, seeing a McDonalds restaurant down the street. They looked closely to see that the window had been shattered open, and there were people screaming, trying to get out of the restaurant. Sourpuss squinted, and he was able to see a dark figure flying about inside of the restaurant. He didn't have hawk quality vision, but he was willing to bet good money that it was their boy, Sporty, causing the havoc inside of the restaurant. And where Sporty was causing mayhem, Lexian and the crystal probably wasn't far behind. Sourpuss took the gun from out of his holster, taking off the safety. Chopsticks looked longingly at the gun, as if he wished he had his weapon too. Instead, he bent down and grabbed a wooden shard from the ground, which was the size of a bat. It probably had been broken off from a larger piece of wood, but any guess was as good as his. They nodded at each other and sprinted over towards McDonalds, and those golden arches suddenly meant more to him than just cheap greasy food.
