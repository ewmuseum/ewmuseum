Waiting, Wanting, Desiring! Part 3

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th Oct After finally getting to Japan, having escaped six insane gas station workers and having thought about nothing else but Red October, El Diablo is taking a break to take his wife out to a restaurant. Having only been in Japan for a few days, El Diablo has already had quite the adventure and that adventure will no doubt carry right the way through till the end of PCW's Red October, where he could leave as PCW World Champion. His wife Angel, meanwhile, has had a torrid time in Japan, having to run away from crazed Japanese workers, a car breaking down and a long haul flight seated next to El Diablo, who has a terrible fear of flying. Mini Flashback 'Is it over yet?' asks Diablo. Angel looks at him, 'No,' 'What the hell is that?' he asks. Angel sighs, 'Turbulence,' Diablo fiddles with his ears, 'My ears hurt,' Angel puts her head in her hands Flashback Over Imagine having to put up with that for several hours! It's simply horrible. But nonetheless El Diablo is taking his wife out to make up for the horrible time they have had so far on this makeshift vacation. Angel, as always is dressed up beautifully, she has her long black dyed hair down and loose and is wearing minimal make up. She shows off her stunning figure in a tight but long blue dress and high heels. El Diablo however, looks quite the fool. Tuxedos have never suited him, yet he still chooses to wear one, purely in the mood of the evening. And being in Japan, where else is there to eat but an authentic Sushi Restaurant? Of course neither El Diablo nor Angel have ever eaten sushi before, so lord knows how this event will turn out. One thing they hadn't taken into account was the fact they'd have to remove their shoes on first entry, and how ironic it is that El Diablo's feet stunk horribly from all those work outs in preparation for his match with Snow at Red October. Angel is too kind to point this out, so instead remained quiet. Of course they had been expecting tables, and yes they were right, but they didn't anticipate them to be on the floor. Stupid foreign customs Burn in hell 'I don't like my feet near my food,' whispers Diablo to Angel. Angel tries not to laugh at that statement as she looks around the establishment. The Sushi Restaurant is quite large and has several 'tables' around the room, all separated by what can only be labelled as 'thin curtains' Curtains made out of paper that is. Paper burns. You shall die The kitchen is at the right of the room, and on the walls are Japanese masks, which look quite interesting, especially to El Diablo, as it is wide known he has a love for masks. 'This place is insane,' states Diablo, 'I'd much rather be back home than here,' he finishes. Angel simply nods at his statement in agreement. Before El Diablo can say anything else, a waiter approaches them with menus. 'Hallo, my name is Kenta, and I will be your waiter this-a evening,' he says while handing the menus to them, 'Just make your choices now, ok?' Diablo starts to turn the map upside down, as he really doesn't understand it's lay out or what it means. In the end he just points to several pictures and says he and Angel will both have them. 'Ah, excellent choice, sir' responds Kenta, as he takes the menus away from them and heads back to the kitchen. Diablo rubs his forehead in frustration at how this evening is turning out. 'I hope the food tastes better than it smells,' says El Diablo to his wife. Angel has to force herself not to laugh as she knows that smell is just his feet. 'I hope so too,' she says reassuringly, while fixing her hair. Suddenly El Diablo feels a shooting pain going up and down his leg, which causes him to start rolling around on the floor manically, and in the process he wrecks the table they were supposed to eat on. 'AH, AH, AH, CRAMP, CRAMP!' he screams in pain. Several waiters rush out and pull him to his feet. 'Are you ok?' asks one of them. Diablo nods softly, 'Just some leg cramp,' The waiters smirk. 'We'll get you some-a chairs,' another says, 'Oh and the cost of the tab-er will be on your bill,' he finishes. Diablo simply nods and sits down on the chair a waiter has already got for him, and Angel sits down on one after getting up. Obviously, El Diablo feels embarrassed, but doesn't let it show and just goes on with the evening. Whereas Angel is bright red at her husbands actions. Idiotic restaurant Time to burn it all down After waiting a few moments in silence, Kenta the waiter comes back with a tray full of raw fish and places it on a stool in front of Angel and El Diablo. 'Enjoy,' he says, while waiting for them to have a taste. Stupid fool! Go away 'Well here we go,' comments El D to Angel. They both pick a piece of sushi up off the tray and taste it! Both their faces have that disgusted look on it, but El Diablo goes further by spitting the food out on the floor. Kenta is shocked. 'Bleh, p-tooey!' moans the Mexican Devil, 'That's horrible,' He then wipes his tongue with his tie to get rid of that vile taste. Vile food? How I loathe thee Kenta doesn't move, but the door from the kitchen do as the Head Chef walks out with an angry look on his face. 'You do not rike my cooking?' the Head Chef says in his deep voice. Diablo coughs and spits on the floor, which only adds insult to injury. Meanwhile Angel is just watching on, although she is just as disgusted with the 'food' as her husband is. 'It is horrible! It makes me want to vomit,' protests El Diablo about the sushi. The Head Chef grunts with anger. 'I'll show you vomit! Your own vomit,' Diablo looks confused, but that look soon turns to fear as the Head Chef of the restaurant pulls out a samurai style sword from his pants. Crazy motherfuckers 'HOLY SHIT!' shrieks the Director of Dictation. El Diablo leaps up with amazing speed, and grabs Angel by the arm as they prepare to run away. Behind them the angry Chef follows with a sharp sword. 'Come here, bitch!' he screams at them. Angel and her husband exit the restaurant and just keep running without looking back. After several minutes of running they come to a halt somewhere and gasp for breath. 'Are you crazy, El?' Angel asks her husband. Diablo pants for breath. 'Just look at it this way, at least we didn't have to pay,' he answers with a smirk. However that smirk soon disappears. Fuck 'OH DAMMIT!' he yells. Standing in front of them are the six gas station workers that were chasing them earlier. 'GET THEM!' one of them shouts. Again El Diablo and Angel set off on a long run for safety. 'I hate this country,' whispers Angel as they run away. Insane bastards Burn in an abyss
