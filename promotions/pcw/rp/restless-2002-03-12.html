Restless

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th March 2002 'Flight 219 To Oklahoma Is Now Boarding In Gate 27! Could All Passengers Flying Direct From New York To Oklahoma Please Board In Gate 27! This Is The First And Final Call! The Flight Will Be Departing In Exactly 10 Minutes!' The voice booms on the P.A system above the hubbub of the New York airport. Amidst a flurry of people walking around of all cultures, all skin colors and all nationalities is none other than Charlie Loc! To the untrained eye he is just another face in the crowd, but to someone who watches and follows celebrities, his a true diamond! More announcements get called over the P.A system as Charlie looks look’s above him at the huge screen with the Gate Guide and Flight Schedule above him before reaching down and grabbing his single, yet huge black suit case and walking off into the thick of a huge crowd of people moving to the same side of the airport as him! For a moment he disappears into the sea of faces, suit cases and bodies but then re-emerges at the front of the crowd and makes his way onto the escalator traveling up to the second floor of the New York National Airport and then on his way to finding his gate. It’s amazing that anyone can even breathe at the airport as everyone is so packed and confined in such a small place. Thank God that half of them get on a plane and travel outside the state…then again, as that half leave, twice as many come in on return trips or departures so it ends up as complete chaos yet again! Charlie looks down at the gold rolex sitting atop his left hand and checks the time, before looking up with a scared expression and checking to see if he’s near his boarding gate! 'Flight 219 To Oklahoma Is Now In The Final Minutes Of Boarding, We Will Not Wait For Any Late Passengers As We Are Already Off Schedule! Sorry For Any Inconvenience!' Once again the ‘perfect’ voice booms over the P.A system and cuts through the crowd’s of people’s talking like a knife into butter… A drop of sweat forms on Charlie’s brow as he reaches up with his hand and wipes it off, before checking the time yet again and then glancing upwards at the gate numbers with a very shocked look on his face. As he stares up yet again he notices that he is indeed standing in front of Gate 27 but due to all the people surrounding everything he can’t find it! Frantically he looks around trying to find his boarding Gate before it’s too late! His usual 'Ice-Cool' attitude is somewhat broken as he really looks as though he’s beginning to stress as he scans his eyes over the endless amounts of people massing up in every direction! Suddenly a reflection glints in his eye and he turns his head sharply to see a quiet attractive brunette flight attendant checking her watch and grabbing the red rope to pull across the Gate Entrance to signify the closing of the gate! With a huge burst of speed Charlie wrenches both himself and his suitcase a million miles an hour across the crowded airport, nearly bowling an old lady over! He doesn’t bother to apologize, instead he just runs up to the flight attendant huffing and puffing, well out of breath! The Flight Attendant is just about to draw the rope closed when she hears Charlie’s panting and turns around to see him red-faced and doubled over trying to catch his breath! 'Well it looks like you’ve made it just in time Mr….'The Flight Attendant stoops down to be at Charlie’s eye level as she finishes asking the question. While gasping for air all Charlie can manage to say is 'Loc' before he nearly collapses on the floor! 'Oh my God! Charlie Loc! Are you ok? Here I’ll get someone to take this for you and I’ll organize a nice glass of Champagne for us…I mean you on the plane!' The Flight Attendant try’s hard not to blush but drapes her arm around Charlie and snaps her fingers for someone to pick up his luggage before drawing the rope across the gate and escorting him down through the Gate and into the plane…. Just as Charlie and the Flight Attendant disappear around the corner a huge, built looking, middle-aged man appears dressed in a red suit. He cracks his knuckles and struts over to the luggage before trying to pick it up…to his dismay he can’t! A few laughs can be heard in the background as the man gets a red face as he struggles to drag the suitcase off and onto the plane….. *** 'Here you go Mr. Loc!' The Flight Attendant produces a pillow and motions for Charlie to tilt his head forward while she places it behind his head. When she’s putting it behind him, she notices the bleached-blonde rat’s tail that has been plaited at the back of his head and she gets a 'sexy' glint in her eye. She takes her time to plump the pillow up before an exhausted and flabbergasted Charlie can’t take it anymore and just leans back, hitting his head on the pillow with a soft thumping sound. Charlie begins to shake his head from side to side and lick his lips as he clearly isn’t looking in good condition at all! The Flight Attendant gets a worried look on her face and does up his belt buckle as the familiar beeping sound comes on to alert the passengers that the plane is ready for take off! 'You really don’t look to well Mr. Loc, were about to take off right now, but I’ll make sure that as soon as were in the air, I’ll tend to you! Matter of fact, I’ll do more than just…oh there I go again! Just sit tight Mr. Loc!' Once again the flight attendant blushes before having a good fiddle with Charlie’s waist and making sure his belt is tight, before moving off to be seated for the take off. 'This Is Your Captain Speaking, We Will Now Be Taking Off On A Three Hour Flight To Norman Oklahoma! Arrival Time Is Expected To Be Approximately 6pm! We Hope You Enjoy Your Flight, Thank you From The Captain And Crew~!' The Captain’s voice can be heard loud and clear over the plane’s P.A system before the transmission from the front of the plane cuts out. The engine begins to make a whooshing sound as it gears up and the familiar clicking sounds of people doing up and fastening their seat belts fills the Cabin of the first-class passengers. As the seat belts continue clicking, Charlie slumps down in his huge blue armchair and looks as though he’s going to pass out when a little kid, probably about five to six years old pops his head up from above the seat in front of him, his blonde hair and blue eyes make him look like a typical American male…. As the kid looks around from over the top of his seat his eyes do a full circle before coming to a stop on Charlie Loc. As the kid sees the Superstar his eyes light up like a Christmas Tree and he quickly ducks down and begins to talk to his mother. 'Mommy! Mommy! The guy from the T.v is behind us! The guy from the westling is behind us! Mommy! Mommy! Can I talk to him? Pweeeeeese?' The little boy’s voice is almost that of the kid from Adam Sandler’s movie, Big Daddy. The mothers head pops up over the seat and takes a look at Charlie and her eyes grow wide as she slowly sinks back down. Charlie seems unaware of everything that’s going on. 'The man looks as though he’s nearly dying! Come on, you’ve got three hours with him! If you want to talk to him, make sure you don’t get too close! You don’t want AID’s now do you?' The mother replies in a stern almost whispering voice. 'No Mommy! I’ll be a good boy! I pwomise!' The little boy says as you can hear a smooching sound. As the mother and the boy finish kissing each other on the cheek you can see her hands reach out the side and pull out the built in earphones and put them into her ears. The little Kid pops his head over the seat just enough so his eyes and nose are showing and he begins to talk. 'Pssssssst, Mr. Westler! Awre You Okay?' He whispers as Charlie just lets out a groan. 'Pweese answer me Siwr!' The Kid asks him again this time in a louder voice and is just met by yet another loud groan. The kid’s head pops down out of sight and he can be heard speaking to his mother again. 'Mommy! Mommy! The westler won’t talk to me!' The mother can be heard sighing as she pops her head over and her eyes grow wide with shock! She lets out a shrill scream as Charlie is laying there in the seat White as a ghost. 'SOMEBODY GET THIS MAN A DOCTOR! I THINK HE’S GOING TO DIE~!' Darkness begins to take over the surroundings…. *** The soft sea breeze begins to blow into Charlie’s hair as he wipes his eyes to find himself sitting on a beach with crystal clear blue waters, the Sun shining down upon him and beautiful white sands that stretch as far as the eye can see. Charlie is laying sprawled in the sand topless with a pair of black and white boxing shorts stretching down to his knees the only thing keeping him warm. He begins to scratch at his stomach before running his fingers along the scar and then leaning back and flipping up onto his feet…. As he springs up he looks around as though he’s hallucinating and quickly looks from left to right. He begins to back up and the sound of a crashing wave causes him to turn around….he jumps up as the cold water touches his toes and he quickly runs onto the sand. His face grows with a look of confusion as behind him is a city, looking like something out of a proposed building site, complete with cars and people bustling up and down the boulevard….but there’s something wrong with it! There’s absolutely no noise what so ever coming from the people or the traffic and there is no one else on the beach other than him! Just as the thought crosses Charlie’s mind he looks down and notices a set of footprints leading off next to the outline of his body where he just picked himself up from before! A worried look crosses over his face as he follows them and then out of no where a voice can be heard and he jumps half a mile! 'Charlie, I missed you!' Standing behind Charlie was the attractive Flight Attendant that had escorted him onto the plane just moments before he had entered…wherever it was that he had entered! Her sweet voice almost matches her looks, she is wearing a pink polka-dot bikini, a grass skirt and a pink and white flower in her hair, looking like a Hawaiian….except with a Spanish background to her! She embraces Charlie and begins to talk to him yet again. 'Charlie what’s wrong? I thought this is what you wanted us to be like? Just you me and the beach! That’s what you told me you wanted Charlie!' Charlie scratches his head before putting his arms cautiously around the girl. 'Where…where…where am I? I not remember being here!' 'Oh Charlie! You told me this is all you ever wanted! You and I to be here, together! Your just like….your just like….your just like…' The girl pauses and begins to cry into Charlie’s pectorals as he gets a confused look on his face. She continues to sob as Charlie holds her close and begins to talk to her. 'What happen? Who you think I like? C’mon you know you can tell me!'Charlie really has no idea what’s going on but he decides to just sit back and go with the flow as this gorgeous young woman continues to cry into his muscles. Her tears running down each and every crevice. 'My last love…Reeco!' She finally answers the question and Charlie’s eyes grow wide and suddenly he is full of rage. 'What Reeco’s last name? I might know him!' Charlie asks her a question but tries not to sound to over excited about it. 'Suave…that’s why my last name is Suave! We divorced Charlie! Your treating me just how he treated me!' She continues to cry harder than before as her tears run off of Charlie and soak the ground. The waves crash in the background as Charlie pulls the girl off of him and looks her in the eyes. 'I going to get Reeco if it the last thing I do!' The girl gets a sad smile across her face as she embraces Charlie yet again….'Oh Charlie!' The Darkness begins to take back over yet again…. *** Charlie snaps his head and the old man standing in front him leans back. Charlie begins to rub his eyes as a huge crowd of people has former around him on the plane. The little boy, his mother, the Flight Attendant and a whole bunch of other people are all crowded around where he is sitting with a gray haired, old man with glasses and a bushy gray mustache is standing in front of him. 'Charlie Isn’t it?' The doctor adjusts his glasses and looks at Charlie. All Charlie can do is nod his reply, he seems to weary to do anything else. 'Well Charlie, you suffered from a mild Asthma attack but you’ve been through the worst of it and you should be fine given an hour or so to rest!' The doctor smiles as he replies and Charlie manages a weak smile. The doctor continues smiling before turning to the flight attendant and trying to have a private conversation which is just able to be heard while the little Kid in front of Charlie begins to stare at him with a big smile on his face. 'Your Wude Mister!' The Kid pokes his tongue out as Charlie slouches down and looks out the window of the now landed plane… 'He should be fine now, I have no idea what was going on when he fainted though! Who’s Reeco?' The doctor can be heard asking the Flight Attendant in a soft voice. 'I have no idea, but if he’s ok then everything should be fine! Thanks for all your help doctor!' The Flight Attendant smiles at the Doctor as he smiles back. He shakes his head and begins to tsk, before walking off. His feet clunking on the floor of the plane. 'I don’t know what was going on in his head though…' The people begin to move off as the little Kid stops paying attention to Charlie and the Flight Attendant moves over to him and places a small card in his lap. She kisses him on the head and whispers something before walking off. 'Goodbye Charlie!' Charlie picks up the card but the words are blurry as he is still yet to be fully woken up! He slouches his head down against the window and looks outside into the dark night of the city of Oklahoma. Rain can be heard and seen pattering down on the runway as Charlie slowly opens his eyes before closing them and thinking of what lies ahead as the rain continues to fall into the night…..
