Friday After Dark...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (At the residence of Skylar Green and Sandy Wong; Friday, February 7th, 10:02 pm EST) *Thump* Tania opened her eyes, waking from her sleep. The room was bright with the street lamp outside sending orange light in through the closed window shades. The textured ceiling was light and dark with the shadows of the mounds of plaster covering up part of the illuminated portions of the ceiling. Tania just looked up at the wall with her dark brown eyes, not stirring from her bed. 'Quizás era mi imaginación,' she thought, her 13-year-old body huddled under the white and pink Powerpuff Girls sheets. When it was late and she woke up, sometimes her imagination played tricks on her. One time she thought there was a grandfather clock coming out of the ceiling. But her waking hallucinations didn't have sound. *Thump* 'Allí es otra vez.' She hid under the sheets, hearing it again. She was too smart to think there was a monster, but she wasn't a stranger to home invasions, it had happened before. Whenever someone broke in to her house back in Harlem, they were just looking for some valuables. They'd try to sneak in, take what they could, and leave. *Thump* Tania looked over at her younger sister Rafaela. Her brown hair covered her fair-skinned face completely. But she was snoring, so she was asleep. Tania and Ines had gotten used to it, they could sleep through Rafaela's snoring. Ines... 'Debo mirar en en Ines,' thought Tania, whose 3-year-old sister occupied the room across the hall. She'd have to leave the room and risk being seen by whomever was robbing the place to check on her, but Ines was easily frightened and if she made a lot of noise then whomever was there would have to shut her up. She snuck out of bed, careful to conserve movement so not to wake 7-year-old Rafaela. She wore a blue B2K shirt (odd because even B2K seems to have an aversion to wearing shirts, especially the lead singer) and some simple pajama pants with pink hearts on them. Her light skin glowed orange from the light and her short hair swayed from the air-conditioning blowing it as she passed under the vent. Opening the door, she could hear voices, 7 distinct voices, coming from downstairs. The nanny had left, she wasn't below. They were doing something. 'Douse the drapes, the furniture, everything!' 'Did someone check the back yet?' 'No, you go.' 'I've got my hands full with the gas tanks.' 'You two, in the back.' Tania snuck over to the next room and gently though quickly picked up Ines, carrying her back over to her room. 'Ellos vienen...' Indeed, they would be in the back rooms shortly. There wasn't much time, she had to get her and her sisters out of the house. She went over to Rafaela and woke her up by covering her mouth and nose. 'Nnn!' was all Rafaela could say. Her eyes opened wide. 'Shh!' Tania whispered into her sister's ear. She was to take Ines and go out the window of the first story house, and run to a neighbor's house. Tania would take a moment to make up the bed and toss away some clothes. If it looked like someone snuck out, they might be pursued. Out in the family room, the 7 cloaked men, their red robes covering every inch of their bodies, drip out the last ounce of gasoline. The family room is decorated with various rereations of works of art from such artists as Picasso, Renoir, even an enlarged Norman Rockwell. The center of the room has a coffee table, surrounded by a Presidential Burghandy leather living room set. The couch is placed in front of a 40-inch flatscreen television that is mounted on the wall and surrounded by a DVD tower and a few game systems. The two accompanying loveseats flank the sofa on opposing sides of the table adjacent to the table. Around the room, shelves with sculptures from ancient period Chinese, Greek, and Mayan cultures line the walls, the shelves and sculptures themselves soaked in the gasoline. In the back, the girls' room door is opened by one of the men. Nothing, it's a guest room with stupid girlie sheets. The bed is made up nicely, there's nothing around. Looking into the next room across the hall, it is the same, only the sheets are simpler red bedspreads. 'This place is going up like weed at a Jay-Z concert.' 'Jay-Z sucks.' 'Why do you think there's weed there? They get people high enough and anyone's entertaining.' A voice comes from outside, a bald man with no robes walked out of the house with a camera in front of him, held by another one of the robed figures. 'We're ready.' Hiding in the bushes, most of the neighbors away for the weekend, the three sisters watched as their new home goes up in flames. Tania cradles Ines in her arms, the young one crying into her shoulder. Rafaela is herself in tears, crouching in the bushes. Her face is rosy. '¿Por qué quema nuestra casa?' Tania couldn't answer that. No one expected something like this to happen. The house was burning down, done by complete strangers. But the pain was all too familiar. In one way or another, the girls had lost two homes before, the security of a place to call their own taken away. All alone, hiding a block away, they could only watch their home crumble to the ground and the smoke rise into the air. The robed figures stayed to watch their handiwork, and they didn't leave until there was the sound of sirens in the distance. First they lost their father, then their uncle... now they were losing their home and quite possibly their new family in Skylar Green and Sandy Wong. Tania's vision blurred by her own tears, she thought that they had done something. She thought it was their fault. The sisters had done something, angered God that he was always punishing them. They hadn't had a moment's peace since they were born. It's like they were cursed. Tania seriously considered just living on the streets. At least, she reasoned, they wouldn't have anything left to lose. But she looked at her sleeping sisters and held them in her arms. However jaded she might be by the situations her life always thrust her into, her sisters deserved better. They deserved a home and a family who loved them. She had to try... And so, leaving her sisters on the ground to sleep, she ran out of the bushes and flagged down a police cruiser as it was driving away....
