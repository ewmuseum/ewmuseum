A Third Fearful Day for Fear Factory

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/02 fizz. fizz. fizz. crackle. snap. pop. (The scene slowly fades in to show Rudolph von Hackenmann in his laboratory, with everything in place. He is wearing his average clothing, but he has white latex gloves added to his ensemble. He is seen working diligently on some kind of chemical in a test tube, mixing and stirring, mixing and stirring. Rudolph finally stops his mixing and stirring after about one minute. He turns around and looks at the door to his laboratory. There is a bloodstain on the door, but no longer is there a dangling dead body.) Rudolph: I still cannot believe that the body of the man who turned the owner of this place in was hanging on the door to my laboratory, and that I was gone when it happened! This is some crazy stuff! (Rudolph walks out into the main room, slowly opening the door to his laboratory. The hole in the floor where Rudolph fell through is now boarded over with new planks, and the floor is uneven. Suddenly, the phone upstairs starts to ring. Rudolph runs to the right side of the room and quickly opens the door. Running up the stairs the phone rings for a third time, and a fourth time, and a fifth time, and a sixth time. Finally, just as Rudolph enters his kitchen, with its black and white tile and average kitchen appliances, the answering machine kicks on. The police chief is talking.) Chief: Mr. Von Hackenmann! The Shoe Killer has escaped! I would recommend being extremely careful, since you reside in HIS shoe factory... (A look of fear comes across the face of Rudolph, and he slowly backs out of the room. He runs down the stairs and quickly swings open the door to the main room, running right into Mason Creeper as soon as he arrives. Both men fall on the floor, and Rudolph rolls around, trying to get Mason's filth off of him.) Mason: Watch where you're going? (Rudolph gets a little aggrivated.) Rudolph: I didn't make you stay in my residence! You can leave at any time! Mason: No! We are a team, so we should act like a team! (Rudolph frowns angrily.) Rudolph: We're a team! Not brothers! Mason: We're pretty close! (Rudolph gets up off the floor and walks away from Mason, ignoring that idiotic comment. Mason gets up and walks upstairs, and Rudolph walks back into his laboratory. Rudolph angrily opens the door quickly, and quickly becomes horrified, as the Shoe Killer stands in front of his whiteboard, with his bloody axe in hand. The killer runs toward Rudolph, swinging the axe as him. Rudolph barely ducks under, and the axe slams through the door, cutting a huge hole in it. Rudolph quickly runs and jumps out through the hole. The killer opens the remainer of the door and chases after Rudolph. Rudolph, scared, runs toward the main door, but it is locked. He turns around and looks in horror, as the killer slowly advances on him. The killer talks to him in a soft, but angry, voice.) Killer: Give me my factory back, or I'll have to kill you for it! (Rudolph, despite being scared to hell, replies boldly.) Rudolph: Over my dead body! Killer: That can be arranged! (Suddenly, a huge wooden head pops out from the wall.) Wooden Head: NOOO!! Cliché! Help! (The wooden head vanishes into thin air, as the killer creeps closer and closer to Rudolph, who is still in complete shock. Rudolph quickly runs away from the door, toward his laboratory again. He runs in and grabs an empty test tube. He throws it at the killer.) Rudolph: Shrink you bastard! I've perfected my shrinking potion! (The killer swings his axe and shatters the test tube. The Shoe Killer laughs at Rudolph.) Killer: You must be forgetting, I was an evil scientist too! I know every trick, and I took care of that problem! (Rudolph now looks on even more fearfully. Suddenly, a huge gerbil busts through the door. The killer turns around and hacks all four of its enormous legs off, and it falls to the floor, writhing in pain and bleeding all over the plank floor.) Rudolph: You bastard! You killed my huge gerbil! Oh wait... I'm next! HELP! HELP! HEEEEELP!!! (Suddenly, from the right side of the room, the door to the upstairs opens, and Mason Creeper comes through, carrying a pistol. He points it at the Shoe Killer.) Mason: Man, it's been a long time! (Mason pulls the trigger, and a bullet cruises into the shoulder of the arm holding the axe. The axe falls to the floor, slamming into the killer's foot. He lets out the loudest scream ever heard by man.) Rudolph: Nice shot Mason! You saved my ass today! You make sure he doesn't escape, and if he tries, kill him! I'll call the cops! (Rudolph runs off to the right side of the room, and Mason moves right next to the Shoe Killer. Mason stands with his gun pointed right at the head of the killer for almost five minutes, before sirens are heard. Suddenly, two police officers come into the room and handcuff the wounded killer. Rudolph comes back down from upstairs, and Mason takes the gun away from the killer's head. One of the officers lifts the axe out of the killer's foot, and the other one drags him off.) Rudolph: Damn, I got lucky today! (Rudolph takes a deep sniff.) Rudolph: This place smells like a freaking slaughterhouse! I better get it cleaned up. Mason clears his throat.) Rudolph: Yeah, thanks Mason! I owe you big time! Mason: Thank you for finally realizing what a valuable asset I am to have around! (Rudolph, unamused my Mason's final comment, leaves the scene with a frown, while Mason laughs. The scene slowly fades away, with Rudolph's head still perched on his neck, and his factory turned into a graveyard for enormous gerbils. The Shoe Killer has been sent back to prison, and the town is safe, thanks to Mason Creeper...)
