Missing: Chapter VIX: Back At The Church

undated

Date: Chapter 9 (To writer. Hold on. There'll be a few more from me. ;)) There it was. The same house I was at, only a few days before. It still looked the same. A basic one story, white house. There was a rock path leading up to the front door and beside that, a one car garage. I parked beside the drive-way, not wanting to actually park in the drive way, for fear of someone knowing who I am. Sure, I had only talked to one person last time, Clair Rivence, but who knows who could have been watching me. I slammed my car door closed. It was a rented, Pontiac Sunfire GT. A 98, but with not to many miles on it. It went quite fast and the sun roof at the top really made it worth while. The black colour, I didn't mind, but I would have prefered a red or blue, something lighter than black. Since it was night time, I wondered if anybody was still awake. I checked on my watch, 10:09. Would Clair be asleep this early? As I walked up the rock path, I noticed that there were no lights on in the house. It was total silence all around me. To the left of the priest's home, was where the church was. The church was red brick with stain glass windows heading down the side of the church that I'm sure looked nice in the day time. As I got up to the entrance of the home, I wondered if I should do this. I had to! Before knocking on the door, I peered in the living room window that hung beside the door. There was no TV on and no lights on in the kitchen, which I could see through the window. Could nobody maybe be home? With a deep breathe, I looked at the door. After a few seconds of silence, I knocked on the door, fairly hard and waited. Silence... Silence... Silence... My dreams started to shatter. Had I come all the way to Germany, my home country to find nothing. Most people when they came to there home country, were anxious to see there parents or siblings. Well, guess what? My father had run away right after I was born. My mom couldn't handle me and my brother both so she sent me on an immigration ship when I was seven or eight, I can't remember. My brother, Sven, was raied by my mom in Germany, I'm assuming. In his adult age, he does evil and it a full-fledged terrorist. What was with my family? I decided to knock one more time on the door before me. KNOCK Silence... Silence... Silence... She wasn't home. It was useless. Before leaving though, I headed down the rock path that led to the side of the house and the back yard. I went around the house. The backyard was fairly large. There were a few trees. The huge backyard merged with the church back lawn and there was a fire pit. I guessed this was where church events in the summer were held. There was a back door, I walked up to that and peered in the window. Right in the window was the kitchen I had seen earlier through the from window. Leading from that was a hallway to the bedrooms. No sign of life here either. Saddened, I turned around away from the house and something caught my eye. A door. There was a partially opened door leading out of the back of the church. Quietly, I stepped over to the door, about thirty or so feet away. I slowly opened the door. It squeaked once but I didn't care. I was too mesmerized at what I was finding. There was a light from either a computer screen or a TV on downstairs. There were about twelve steps leading down to a room of some sort. In the room were a man and a women! They were talking... And I was listening...
