Try To Find Some Empathy With Corey.

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/03/02 The shot opens up on the sun-drenched campus of Albuquerque University in New Mexico. On the lawns inter-crossed with Grey stone paths the students that fraternise the campus sit about and talk in the afternoon sun. “Wow. it is like So cool to be in college again...Do you think there are any fat chicks from the country I can pick on? It’d be like being 19 all over again!” With these words Xavier Kannon’s valet Eleanor is seen walking into shot, the Scientologist himself following no too far behind her, taking in the sights. Xavier : “Okay...But AFTER we do what we came here for...This is quite important...” The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing a long, Brown leather coat that stops just a few inches above the path. Under it her has a black silk shirt, left open to show a Black round neck t-shirt with Asylum RIP written across the chest in Purple, a sarcastic message to Krayzie and Miya who shared the stable name with since Shang-Haied 2. With this he has a pair of his signature Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and intricate Purple embroidery depicting Scientological runes snaking down the left leg to where a pair of Black, Grey and Purple Skechers adorn his feet. Ellie~! looks deep in thought, trying to remember why they are actually there again. Eleanor : “Oh yeah...What we are here for.....What is that again? Just to refresh my memory...” Ellie~! is wearing a Brown suede jacket and matching suede skirt with a hem line just above he knee. Under the jacket she wears a light Pink t-shirt with Snapple written across her 32C chest in white. The Gold Patron Meritorious sighs at his valets words but then miles it off, shaking his head and putting it down to her preppy naivity. Xavier : “Well.....As you know I’ll be teaming with Corey Williams on Tuesday... This comes as news to Ellie~! who interrupts him. Eleanor : “Well, actually I didn’t know....Did you tell me?...Anyways, I’ll remember it now...Corey...Williams...Okay, I think my head got it... As usual with his valet, Xavi doesn’t take I as an insult that she doesn’t follow his wrestling career that much, he does the same with the Shoe Designers that she reels off to him. Xavier : “Right...Well, as I’m sure you will have noticed, Corey Williams is a little on the Controversial side...” He looks to her in hope that she was both listening and knows what he is on about...But it looks like he is out of luck as Ellie~! looks back with a furrowed brow, still trying to remember if Corey is the man Xavier was talking about a second or so ago. Eleanor : “He is?” Again the head of the Scientologist shakes but just like L Ron Hubbard when he introduced Scientology to the world in the 1950’s Xavier is not deterred. Xavier : “Yes...He is! Anyways, I am teaming with him to face Miyagawa and.....You know who...” Even though the names of the PCW roster usually draw a black in the mind of Ellie~! the name You know who causes a sneer t cross her face even more harsh than the ones she usually reserves to those she despises. Eleanor: “You mean the boyfriend of that skanky, manky slut Jessica.....” Xavier nods as Ellie~! scowls at the thought of Jessica, the woman she scuffled with twice at Fusion. Eleanor : “Well next time she comes anywhere near me it won’t be like the altercations we had on Friday, it will be more like when I felled the mighty Greek God Big Alan Steele in one chair swing....” A gleam returns to her eyes as she thinks back to the shot with a steel chair she gave to an exhausted and battered Alan Steele to give Xavier a win on Friday. Eleanor : “Yeah...I can just see that bitch picking her hair of the floor now....” As the visage of hate starts to fade off her face, one of confusion takes it over.... Eleanor : “Er....Who is Miyagawa?” In this second Xavier’s hopes that Eleanor was finally starting to take an interest in PCW are shattered, but seeing as she is Ellie~! and it is to be expected he just shrugs it off. Xavier : “The one I knocked out by breaking a Snapple bottle over his head!” She giggles at the reminder of the incident that cost Miya the Television Title. Eleanor: “Oh yeah....That was funny! H was stumbling about holding his face with blood and glass in his eyes...That was, like, SO hilarious!!!” His valet’s wicked side returns a smile to Eleanor’s face. Xavier : “Well...I suppose I can tell you this now...I had been saving it as a surprise but now seems as good a time as any seeing as you are in the mood so to speak...” Eleanor looks around at the crowds of Students sitting on the lawns either side of the path they are strolling down, looking uncomfortable. Eleanor : “Shouldn’t we...Um...Save that for later? I’ll save public acts of...Well...That to Jessica, which she usually follows up with public acts of being given $2!” For a few seconds the Scientologist looks confused, then finally gets on the same page as Ellie~! due to the Jessica / Whore analogy. Xavier : “No..Not that!” At finding out that it wasn’t what she assumed Ellie~!’s face is filled with youthful curiosity. Eleanor : “Well what is it then? Tell me!!!” Xavier : “Okay my little Poon Puppy, you are going to love this! On Friday, it’s you and me....versus Jeremy Howard and Jessica!!!” With this news Ellie~! stops and jumps up on the spot, clapping her hands in enthusiasm and a broad smile crossing her face. Eleanor : “YAY ME!!!” She then leaps forward and hugs Xavier who is almost taken aback by his valet’s lack of apathy towards PCW. Eleanor : “Thank you, thank you, thank you....I will like, SO not let you down, I’m gonna beat her around real good while you SO totally destroy her greasy haired husband!” She relinquishes her hold on Xavier and gestures with her head down the path to a dumbfounded Xavier who is still standing still. Eleanor : “Um...Weren’t we meant to be going this way for something?” These words snap Xavier out of whatever he was in and he takes his valet’s arm as they continue walking along the path, attracting the attention of some crowds of students both through who they are, and Ellie~!’s loud outburst of satisfaction a few seconds earlier. Eleanor : “You still haven’t told me what we are here for? Are we going to the Chemistry department to get Acid to throw in Jessica’s ugly face? Or maybe to the library to get a Hibbert bo- Xavier : “Hubbard.” Eleanor : “What-EVER!...Book to smack Jeremy and Miyathingy over the head with like the Chef did?” The Gold Patron Meritorious shakes his head at her guesses to her annoyance, in the end she looks up to him with her eyes wide open and a So WHY are we here? expression on her face. Xavier : “In order to get some Chemistry going with Corey I am going to make the effort too understand where he is coming from, and in return I hope that he will decide to embrace the teachings of Dianetics...For after all we are the most Controversial of religions!” They wait for a group of Students to bundle out the door before they walk into the corridor of the Social Sciences department. Eleanor : “What? So you are changing you name to Corey?” Ellie~! stills seems to have no idea what Xavier is talking about when he says understanding Corey. Eleanor : “Wait...You aren’t going to shrink yourself and go inside him like in the film where you see through there eyes for a whole day? because if what I heard about him and Chase is true then that is something NOBODY should have to see....Well, apart from the guy who pays $5 to do it to Jessica!” Kannon works out what his valet means and a disgusted look crosses his face that he has to shake his head rapidly to get out of his mind. Xavier : “No...Definitely not that...But all will be revealed soon!” They head through a set of double doors and end up in a large hall where a dozen or so tables are laid out and manned by students from each course housed under the department heading. International Relations, Social Studies, Religious Studies and many others. Eleanor : “What....Is he a student here or something?” Kannon scans around, looking at the names on each desk before looking confused and walking to the desk in the centre named Information, Ellie~! trundles along behind him, still looking confused also. Eleanor : “He did a degree in Information?” Xavier gets to the desk and smiles politely to the woman behind it. Xavier : “Hello.....I can’t seem to find a subject I came here for....” Woman : “What would that be?” Kannon decides this is time for a dramatic pause as both the Woman and Ellie~! look at him, wondering what his answer will be. Xavier : “I am looking to do...A PhD IN CON-TRO-VER-SEEEE!!!!!!“ The woman looks at him with a blank expression on his face, while Ellie~!, unexpectedly remembering what she was told a few minutes ago, grins in recognition of what Xavier is on about. Woman : “I’m sorry...We don’t run that course!” Kannon seems shocked by this revelation and looks back to her. Xavier : “What do you mean you don’t do it...It’s CONTROVERSY! It’s all around us, it drives the World....It governs our very Society! An example...George Bush is a retarded Texan hick who is merely a puppet...See...CONTROVERSY! How can you not do any form of further education in it?” She stares up to him, slightly worried by the Auburn headed eccentric that stands above her. Xavier : “How can one such as I hope to become as Controversial as the God of Controversy himself Corey Williams if there is no means to educate ourselves?” Woman : “We-” Kannon cuts her off, an agitated look now crossing his face. Kannon : “That was a rhetorical question...” Eleanor : “A.......Rhetorical.......Question!” Ellie~! is now standing beside Xavier, sneering at the woman who is still confused by Xavier’s request. Something seems to click within Xavier as a grin starts to form and he nods his head. Xavier : “Oh, I know what his is all about...I get it...” He is almost laughing out loud as he speaks, although his face does not portray an emotion of joy. Xavier : “....It’s because I’m a Scientologist isn’t it? Go on...Don’t insult me by lying....My belief in the teachings of L Ron Hubbard means that you don’t want me on any course here so you closed the only one I am interested in!” The woman manning the information desk is lost for words at Xavier’s accusation and looks uncomfortable as the 1st Couple of Scientology loom over her, waiting for an answer. Woman : “I can assure you Si-” Out if instinct Eleanor thrusts her palm out and stops it an inch away from the woman’s face to stop her talking while Xavier looks offended. Xavier : “Do not refer to me as SIR...I am Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon from the Des Moines Church of Scientology in Iowa!” The woman stutters, wondering what to reply to his with. Woman : “I can assure that we have an unbiased entry sys-” The Scientologist gives her no time to explain herself, his face already contorted with anger, the kind usually reserved only for talk of Jeremy Howard. Xavier : “Don’t even insult m by saying that you are not prejudice...THE WHOLE WORLD IS PREJUDICE!!!” He walks away from the desk and heads towards the Religious Studies desk as the people in the hall watch him after his outburst. He locks eyes on the man behind the course’s desk. Xavier : “YOU! HOW MUCH SCIENTOLOGY IS COVERED IN YOUR COURSE?!?!?” Them man slides his chair back in response to Xavier’s aggressive question as he looks around, trying not to make eye contact with either Xavier or Ellie~! who both lean over the desk looking right at him. Man : “Er...We don’t cover it...Er...The syllabus doesn’t treat it as a Religion...” Xavier shakes his head and stands back up straight, looking out to the people in the Hall who have averted their attention to him. Xavier : “DID YOU HEAR THAT? NOT ONLY ARE THEY STOPPING ME DOING THE COURSE DUE TO RELIGIOUS PREJUDICE....BUT THEY THEN TELL ME THAT WHAT I BELIEVE IN ISN’T EVEN A RELIGION!!! DO YOU SEE WHAT I HAVE TO PUT UP WITH?” The people just look at the 1st Couple of Scientology blankly, not saying a word. Xavier : “WILL NO-ONE ELSE NOT SPEAK UP FOR US?” Still silence. Xavier shakes his head and starts to walk towards the exit, turning around in stereo with Ellie~! as they reach the door. Xavier : “SOON YOU WON’T HAVE A CHOICE BUT TO LISTEN TO US! TRUST ME WHEN I SAY THIS PEOPLE, SCIENTOLOGY AND CONTROVERSY ARE TAKING OVER AND THERE IS NOTHING YOU CAN DO ABOUT IT!!!!” Eleanor : “Do.......About........It!” As Xavier storms out the doors, Ellie~! gives the people watching in the Hall one last sneer before turning round to nearly catch the door that is swinging closed in the face. Eleanor : “Hey! No.......Hit......Ellie~!” She pushes the door back open and follows him out as the people in the Hall look on in disbelief as we fade to Black.
