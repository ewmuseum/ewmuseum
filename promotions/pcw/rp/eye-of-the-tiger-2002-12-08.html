Eye of the Tiger

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/08/02 A button clicks into place on an old CD player. A device inside whirls, preparing the music within.....we pan back a little to see a tall man kneeling beside it. Clyde: Hm. The demon stands, turning around. He taps his fists together, which we now see are covered by red boxing gloves. Clyde: Alright, let's go. Across the ring stands the not so dynamic duo of Scourge and the Violator. With gloves on of their own, they look on in confusion. Scourge: What we doing here man? Violator: I am the Violator! Clyde: Training. Scourge: Training!? The bestiality lover scratches his head, as Clyde shucks around and jabs the air to Eye of the Tiger by Survivor. Clyde: (Punching) Yes, training. The burned man ducks and punches, boxing his shadow. Clyde: I've got an upcoming three on one battle coming up. Duke, Kannon, Diablo....they all want me dead. Then there's the small matter of Peons with Autism..... Violator: I am the Violator. Clyde: I don't care what their names are, I just want them dead! Now come on. Clyde hops forward, sticking out several jabs that pop off Scourge's nose. He swats them away girlishly, moving around. Scourge: Stop that man, I AH!!!!! A massive haymaker decks the freak, his head bounces off the canvas painfully. Clyde: (Tapping his gloves) I can't believe I lost to you once. Violator: I am the Violator! Furious at the abuse to his partner, the behemoth known as Violator charges. He takes a mighty swing, which Clyde sidesteps. Clyde: Come on. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Violator swings again, but again his incredibly slow hook meets nothing but air. Clyde: I need to train here, not be amused. Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!!!! Angrily Violator hops up to the far end of the ring. He grazes his foot off the canvas, then takes a large jump forward. Clyde: Eh? Violator takes another jump, gaining ground. Clyde: I don't get it. Bethany: IT'S THE BALD BULL ATTACK YOU TWIT, DIDN'T YOU EVER PLAY NINTENDO!?!? Clyde looks to his eight year old lover at ringside, placing his gloves on his hips. Clyde: No, I didn't. Bethany: URGGGGGGGH!!!!! The Violator leaps once more, preparing a mighty swat. Still looking at Bethany, the demon sticks his glove out causing the monster to jump right into it stomach first. Violator: I am the..... The catchphrase dies on the big man's lips as all the air leaves his lungs. He collapses into a heap at the feet of Clyde. Clyde: Competition I say, competition! Bethany: Grr! In a huff, the little girl stomps towards the locker room. Clyde continues to shadow box over the corpses of his comrades, then stops as he sees a middle aged (yet holding up rather well) woman marching towards him. Clyde: Hm, interesting. The lady rolls into the ring, facing down the demon. Kathy Lee Gifford: Alright, put em up! Clyde: Very well. Kathy Lee sticks and moves, jabbing Clyde's face ineffectively. He looks at her somewhat dumbfounded. Kathy Lee Gifford: Yeah, I'll get you! You'll be sharing a glass coffin with Frank! Clyde: Bah. Nonchalantly our serial killer throws a punch, connecting with Ms. Gifford's chin. Her head comes completely detached, bouncing off the turnbuckle. Clyde: (Turning) Come on Bethany, you can do better that that. Bethany: URGH! Again the girl storms off, leaving Clyde to box to the music. Clyde: (Jabbing) Huh....ha.....ha.....uh? And again he pauses as a line of people step out of the locker room towards him. Clyde: Bethany. That's the best you can come up with? None other than TV's Ricardo Montalban hobbles into the ring, surrounded by a platoon of midgets. Ricardo: Yes Tattoo, we will serve up a heaping helping of pain! But don't worry, you'll soon loosen up to the experience. Clyde: Ugh. The line of wee folk begin circling Clyde....he tries to punch at them, but finds he can't reach. Clyde: (Jabbing) Ugh.....ugh..... Ricardo: Soon we will feast on your flesh....some parts more than others. Clyde: DIE!!!! Infuriated, the psychopath charges straight through the pile of midgets. Stepping on tiny arms and legs and heads, he makes a beeline directly for the old man and swings with all his might. Clyde: GRUAAAAGH!!!!! The punch connects, causing Ricardo Montalban to do a complete 360. He then wobbles dizzily. Ricardo: This is not the best fantasy I have ever had. Ow. Down goes the elderly pervert, his flock of Tattoos swarm to his aid. Clyde again glares at Bethany. Clyde: Cute. But you'll have to do better than that. Bethany: Alright. Clyde: (Raising an eyebrow) Alright? Bethany wastes no more words....instead she slides into the ring herself, pulling the gloves off the now deceased Kathy Lee Gifford and slipping them on herself. Clyde: You're going to take me on? Bethany: Damn right! Clyde: Well this should be interesting. But I'm afraid you're overmatched. Bethany: Bah~! The insane child leaps at Clyde, who merely sticks a fist out and knocks her out of midair. She springs to her feet though, again lunging and again eating fist. Clyde: These Sylvester Stallone Learn Boxing and German While You Sleep tapes really pay off, eh? Ich bin ein schlussel! Bethany: You just called yourself a pen you dipshit! Clyde: .....oops. Bethany groggily stands, shaking off the cobwebs. She dusts off her dress, then looks on evilly. Clyde: What's so funny? Bethany: I know something you don't know. Clyde: And what's that? Bethany: That for a demon, you never learned rule number 1 about fighting. Clyde: I probably did, I just stopped caring. Bethany: ......well, be that as it may. The little girl reaches into her pocket, producing a remote control. At this Clyde's CD player begins to shake.....the voice of Kenny G resounds. Clyde: (Holding his ears) Agh! What madness is this? From behind the demon, Scourge and Violator stand. On the other side of the ring the headless corpse of Kathy Lee Gifford does as well, stumbling over and picking up her skull. Bethany: Mwaha. Clyde: What ARE you doing? Lastly Ricardo Montalban stands, surrounded by his midget harem. Slowly they walk together, standing around the small form of Bethany who cackles. Bethany: Rule one....no one ever said you had to fight fair. Clyde: ......bumsen sie mich roh. The entire brigade charges Clyde at once, tackling him to the ground and beating him senseless. He vanishes beneath an onslaught of fists, teeth and other body parts unmentionable, as we mercifully fade to black......
