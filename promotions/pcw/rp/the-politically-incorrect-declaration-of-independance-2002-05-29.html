The Politically Incorrect Declaration of Independance

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Politically Incorrect Declaration of Independance Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: May 29th Authority. The Ultimate Authority. It's goal? Achieving money, power, and respect. It's philosophy? 'First you get the money.. Then you get the power...' Then, you will be respected. A simple philosophy, subscribed to by men of all times. An ex-convict, Karl LeBlanc. A career jobber, Tim Walden. A street thug, Orpheus Grant. A scientologist, Xavier Kannon. A power-mad CEO, Tyler Nelson. And, two corrupted politicians, Politically Incorrect. LeBlanc, Grant, and Walden are all gone now. Prior to Tuesday Turmoil, The Ultimate Authority was down to four men. Afterwards, just two. Why? The politicians grew tired of being second rate under their stablemates. The greatest tandem in PCW proudly stands together to oppose The Ultimate Authority in PCW. Tyler Nelson and Xavier Kannon may own 70 percent of PCW. Nelson may be CEO. But, conditions seem ripe for an overthrowing of the old regime. Kannon and Nelson face assaults from Miyagawa, the Malice Crew, and Phantom Phive. Nelson desperately needs aid, and he is now looking for those who will still follow him. Reginald V. Lovecraft and William S. Powell are now men without countries so to speak. They are alone now...in a world of stables. Can Politically Incorrect stand alone? Lovecraft hopes to prove yes this Friday. In his first singles match...he will bring reform to the Extreme division. In the only way he can, that of course being the POLITICALLY INCORRECT way! ::Still in the lovely hole of the United States that is New Jersey, Reginald V. Lovecraft sits alone. Miss Katherine Willis is not even there, as Lovecraft seems to be desiring to make a speech alone. He sits, hands folded on a ledge, looking directly ahead of himself, as deadly serious as ever.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: When in the course of PCW events, it becomes necesary for one team to dissolve the stable bands which have connected them with another, and to assume among the skies of PCW the equal standing that the laws of talent and ability entitle them, a decent explanation should be given to declare the causes for our seperation. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Politically Incorrect holds these truths to be self evident...that all men are created equal, and that they are endowed by their Creator with the unalienable rights to life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.. :: A tear comes to The Governor's eye, as he appears to be fighting back emotion.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: In 1776, the forefathers of this great nation stated the above in a Declaration of Independance from Great Britain. Ever since then, its words, ideals, and meaning have been cited by countries worldwide who wished to throw off the shackles of tyranny, and, at long last, strike their own path in the world, to stand up and be counted as man, woman, and child, not as subjects. :Lovecraft smiles as he gestures to his cuff. On it are cufflinks of the American flag, made from 14 karat gold, rubies, opals, and sapphires. They are one of Reginald's most valuable and prized posessions.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: On this day, I cite the Declaration, for once again, an oppressed people have risen up to rebel against tyranny. Politically Incorrect have seceded from the evil empire that is The Ultimate Authority. And, believe me, our grievances against King Tyler Nelson are numerous. ::Putting on a pair of spectacles, he begins to read from a typewritten document he has at his side. Reginald V. Lovecraft: He hath refused to submit to the laws set forth by himself. He hath forbidden his fellow owners to pass legislation for the good of the whole. He hath hired Neo Nazis into PCW against the boundary of good taste. He hath had Commissioner Kannon book himself into a World Title Match that he was undeserving of. He hath used us as pawns in his game for domination of this company. He hath fired one of his fellow owners in an effort to get his stock. He hath subjected his followers to harsh beatings at the hands of other groups. ::He smiles, and goes back to recitation from memory.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: In every stage of our oppression, we have attempted to assert our equality. We have even attempted to petition our fellow Ultimate Authority members, but they have not seemed willing to support us. Therefore, we must hold them as we do all people. Enemies in battle, friends in peace. WE, THEREFORE, DECLARE OURSELVES TO BE A FREE AND INDEPENDENT TAG TEAM, CAPABLE OF FIGHTING IN MATCHES OURSELVES, AND DOING ALL THINGS THAT TAG TEAMS DO! ::Reginald gasps from the efforts of delivering such a powerful speech.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: You have just heard Politically Incorrect's Declaration of Independance from the Ultimate Authority. We are now on our own. You know, it is a bit ironic....how like America Politically Incorrect is.. :He stops, allowing the audience to ponder that point: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Despite Politically Incorrect often being accused of being corrupt....we are simply following the most American of doctrines. The concepts of laissez-faire capitalism and social Darwinism state that the business, economic, and political worlds are all conducted under 'survival of the fittest' rules. In essence, this means...THERE ARE NO RULES. ::Lovecraft rises to his full height. His crimson suit is of the finest quality. Every inch of Reginald is tailor made for public appearances. He confidently speaks.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Corruption and bribery are legal under these economics theory...politics is a giant battle of often illegal activities. You know....extreme wrestling is really quite similar to politics. It involves tactics that are usually illegal, but are perfectly permissable in the environment in question. Graft is my steel chair, bribery my lead pipe, and patronage my brass knuckles.....with no rules, I am quite a versed man indeed. It is this versing that will make me Extreme Champion. Now, if you will excuse me, there is business to be taken care of.. ::Lovecraft rises, and turns to leave, as we fade to...:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically IncorrectIndependant, Once Again ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
