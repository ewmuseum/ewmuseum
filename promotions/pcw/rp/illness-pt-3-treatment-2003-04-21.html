Illness pt. 3: Treatment

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-21-03 People of all colors and backgrounds gather together, all seemingly annoyed, impatient, angered, or passed out. The joys of the hospital waiting room, god only knows what bacteria and sickness float around in the air inside of this room, and quite honestly, I’d rather not know. Never a good magazine, that fucking balding, fat ass, trailer trash, dick hole has taken the last ESPN the Magazine and I’ll be damned if I’m going to read a fucking Home and Garden print! God damned Latin American woman is watching some fucking sappy soap opera on the Spanish channel, and no, despite my last name and my Spanish back ground, I can’t speak the shitty language. What annoys me most however is not the cold coffee, and certainly not the two aforementioned circumstances, it’s the fact that there are five nurses standing over there talking about their fucking worthless lives, sharing stories about their families and the one loner who has no family keeps bringing up her retarded cats! “Sometimes I wish I just had a fucking gun to shoot myself in the head,” whispers A.J. Trinidad to himself while pinching the bridge of his nose in between his thumb and forefinger. “I don’t even know why the hell I’m here.” A.J. peaks over his right shoulder, the slowly stands up. Well dressed in a navy blue button up long sleeve Polo shirt which is uncuffed and rolled up only once, black pants that fit as they were made to, black shoes as well, he brushes off the wrinkles from his shirt and turns to the right to walk away. “Where the hell do you think you’re going?” Asks Amira holding up her hand and pressing her palm into his chest. “You promised me we’d get this checked out, we’ve already been here for almost two hours, and I’ll be damned if you’re going to sneak out now.” A.J.’s bottom lip pouts out and he returns to his seat silently murmuring some unpleasant words under his breath. Then, finally, the ultimate ray of light in the waiting room; a nurse with a clipboard. Everyone in the room’s heads turn in unison with a growing look of plea on their faces in hopes of being the next name on the list of what has to be an infinite amount of names. The air grows heavy with anticipation, you could here a needle drop if there were one at this precise moment, but there wasn’t, then the portly nurse looked out over her wire-rimmed bifocals, “Harry Balls, the doctor will see you now.” A pole like skinny man stands up obviously embarrassed by his own name, when he should have been more embarrassed with the sweat pants and white T-shirt with yellow stains on the underarms. He coughs once sounding almost fake before turning his attention to the nurse, “It’s Harry Balles”, he replied almost angrily, accentuating the Bal-les. “I’m sorry sir, the doctor will see you now,” the nurse responded calmly. Everyone else in the waiting room breathed out a sigh of contempt as A.J. looks back down to Amira who has since taken the seat next to him. “Where did you disappear off to?” asks Trinidad if for no other reason, just to make conversation. “I went and peaked on the Internet at your E-mail to see if you received an assignment this week for Turmoil,” answer Amira with a hint of concern in her voice. “Anddddddddddddd?” A.J. questions apparently sarcastically. “And, you have another four corners match, against Smoke, Dante Jackson, and Dorn Tracer.” She said while bracing herself for a negative response from A.J. “Another god damn four corners match? Christ I feel like I’m being type cast here in PCW.” Trinidad continues. “It was funny enough to be written as a woman last week at the PPV, that had me rolling around on the floor for the better part of five minutes, but another four corners match, Jesus.” “Well, A.J., you could look at the good side of a four corners match.” Amira added trying to shed some light on the situation. “I mean, at least you’re booked in a match, and it’s down in the card a ways. It’ll be a good opportunity for you to get some exposure.” “I don’t really care at this point, just as long as Smoke brings Crespo back to me, I have his damn dismissal papers,” Trinidad says with a large smile growing across his face. “I can’t wait to see that old boy again, and if that stoner harmed one hair on his funny looking body, I’ll, I’ll, I’ll…….. Amira looks over to A.J. expecting him to finish his sentence but instead sees him slumping over in his chair clutching to the side of his head. A.J.’s complexion has gone to pale and small beads of sweat start to build just under his hairline as Amira darts up to the desk alerting the nurse of his situation. “Nurse! I need to get him in right away!” Amira exclaimed pointing over to A.J. “Yes, I know, everyone needs to get in”, the nurse began with an acerbic tone before looking up to A.J. who has somehow managed to stand up and begin walking towards the desk. “Oh my god!” The nurse immediately grabbed the telephone, dialed a quick two-digit number and was demanding emergency personnel to the vicinity immediately. Just as she hung up, two men came barreling through the door with a wheel chair in tow. “What happened!” The first doctor demanded. “I don’t know,” responded the nurse visibly shaken. “He was just waiting here, then he took a turn for the worse. I think this is his wife here; maybe she’ll be able to tell you more. The nurse nods over towards Amira as the doctors load A.J. up and sprint with him through to swinging doors, disappearing with Amira following closely.
