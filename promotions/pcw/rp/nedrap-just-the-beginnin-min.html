NedRap: Just The Beginnin', Min

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A short while before you read this. [In true PCW fashion, or camera slowly fades in from black, into the black and white, monotone view of a security camera, located in an average sized, Glasgow grocery store. There are about 4, small isles, which depict the fact that the shop is hardly a superstore. At the counter, a stereotypical old Asian guy with a grey moustache sits, reading a copy of the daily newspaper. There are about 5 different people in the shop … a big black lady, two 10-13 year old Glasgow children, and everyone’s favourite neds, Andy Murray and Rab. We can barely make out Andy and Rab, who are browsing through the magazine rack at the far end of the stores, both dressed of course in shell suits and baseball caps. Our view suddenly changes, to a full colour view of Andy and Rab, as they look through the various magazines.] Rab: Min, how don’t we get a magazine? This one’s pure mental. Andy Murray: Ach, awl we need is the essentials, ken? Rab: Aye, so that’s Sunny Delight, Mars Bars, Walkers Crips and Tizer? Andy Murray: Aye. Rab: Mental, ah’m gonnae go get that then. [Rab walks off around the aisle, as Andy continues to look though the magazines. He picks up a copy of the latest edition of “Max Power,” the definitive motor vehicle/scantily clad women magazine. Andy, who for the record is the proud owner of a Vaxhaull Nova, flips through the pages, not stopping to look at any particular page at all, before placing the magazine randomly in front of the “Teletubbies” magazines. Andy then walks away from the magazine rack, towards a “Pick & Mix” section. With a confused look across his face, Andy looks at the hundreds of practically identical sweets, before shrugging, and walking towards the counter. Dodging past the children, he is caught out by Rab, who gets their first, arms full of crisps, sweets and drinks. Barely able to hold them on his own, Rab stumbles forward, and drops them all on the counter, startling the Asian gentleman.] Shopkeeper: Oh … Is that all, sir? Rab: Aye, hurry up an’ add it up. [The shopkeeper starts to count the hundreds and hundreds of items, after about 5 seconds, Rab grows impatient.] Rab: Baw bag, I aint got all day, hurry up min! Shopkeeper: I am trying my hardest, I am but one man! Andy Murray: Oi you ya chube, dinnae give Rab cheek or I’m gonnae pure hammer you, ken? [The shopkeeper grimaces, before starting to pile all the stuff into a single carrier bag, not really caring how much it is worth. He holds the bag up, and passes it to Andy, the bag nearly bursting with all that crap that is in it. Andy grabs the bag, holding it at his side.] Shopkeeper: That will be … Err … £20 please sir. Rab: Twenty quid? Ah’m no payin’ that ya chube. Shopkeeper: Make that £10 then. Andy Murray: That’s more like it. [Rab reaches into his pocket, and pulls out 20 50 pence coins, awkward bastard. Rab drops the coins on the counter, leaving the shopkeeper to gather them together. Andy and Rab turn away from the shopkeeper, and walk down the aisle, before exiting the store onto the suburban Glasgow streets. Andy’s motor, a dark blue Vauxhall Nova, sits on the street, paint rusting at the edges. Far from your ideal car.] Rab: Where we goin’ now min? Andy Murray: Lets go down the fitba’. Rab: There’s nae fitba’, min. Andy Murray: Aye there is, Pollok FC are playin’. Rab: Brilliant. [Andy pulls open the drivers door to his car, and throws the bag into the back seat, as he fastens his seatbelt, Rab walks around the front of the car, and enters through the passenger door as Andy slams his door shut, and turns the ignition key.] Rab: MIN! I just had a pure brilliant idea, by the way. [Andy looks at Rab.] Andy Murray: Fit min? Rab: Well, ye know how we’re pure famous and all? Andy Murray: Aye. Rab: Well, I was thinkin’ … Andy Murray: What were ye thinking’? [Rab swallows, before continuing, as if he expects Andy to reply violently.] Rab: We need to sing one of they rap songs. [There is a pause between Andy and Rab, Andy looks like he could lunge forward and smack Rab at any moment. Rab looks a little uneasy.] Andy Murray: THAT’S PURE BRILLIANT MIN! [Rab breathes a sigh of relief, as Andy and himself share a high five. They laugh for a few moments.] Andy Murray: Min, how do we rap? Rab: You know like they black guys on the tele? Andy Murray: Who? Like Ian Wright? Rab: No, they ones that sing. [A puzzled look crosses Andy’s face, before he realises what Rab is talking about.] Andy Murray: Oh, aye. Rab: Jist like that, min. Andy Murray: Pure mental! Rab: Aye! But lets go tae the fitba’ … Andy Murray: Aye! [With that, Andy places his foot on the accelerator, as the neds drive off into the distance. Andy and Rab doing a rap!? God help us!]
