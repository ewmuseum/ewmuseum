Startling Discovery?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-29-03 5 “One…” “Two…” “Three…” A voice calls out in a monotonous tone. “Four…” “Five…” The voice grows softer and softer until it’s no longer audible. Heavy breathing can be heard. “Six…” “Seven…” The soft, monotonous voice is replaced a rougher, deeper voice. Suddenly a light flashes on as a door can be heard opening. We are back in the same small hotel room as before. The alarm clock next to the bed reads, “5:30” Adam is lying on the ground at the base of the bed. His feet are tucked underneath the bed. The legs of his mesh shorts fall as he bends his knees. “Eight…” “Nine…” the soft voice calls out. Jaslene steps into the room from the bathroom with a toothbrush in her mouth. She pulls a bathrobe around her and ties the knot in front of her waist. “Ten…Eleven…Twelve…” Jaslene continues between strokes with the toothbrush. She walks over and takes a seat on the bed. “Why are you doing this again?” “Because Jas…ugh…it’s my morning routine…ugh…and it’s especial-ugh-ly…important today…ugh…because of my match.” Adam strains to reply not stopping, “I do this…ugh…every morning.” Jaslene yawns, “I know, I know but it doesn’t bother me when we aren’t on the road. I just don’t understand why you put yourself through this.” “Have to…ugh…stay in shape…ugh…to further my career…” Adam replies. He stops and rests on the ground. “Oh so now you are done?” Jaslene asks getting up and walking to the bathroom. “With pushups and sit-ups I am.” Adam replies sitting up and moving out away from the bed, “But I still have to run.” “Run?” Jaslene says reappearing at the doorway of the bathroom, “but we are in MOBILE, ALABAMA! Where are you going to run?” “I don’t know,” Adam answers her, “but I’ll figure it out. As long as I know how to get back here I’ll be fine.” “Just take your cell phone with you…please?” Jaslene pleads as she lies back down on the bed. Adam laughs as he gets to his feet. He takes a pair of black jogging pants off of the back of a chair. He slides his right leg in and looks to Jaslene. She’s giving him the evil eye. “You’re serious?” Adam asks slowly sliding his other leg into the pants, “I think I can handle myself if something were to happen Jas.” “Please Adam? For me?” she asks him again. Adam picks up a black sweatshirt off of the table. “Heh, you know I can’t say no to you.” Adam says sliding the sweatshirt over his head. The letters AWR are printed across the chest in big red letters. He pulls the hood up over his head. Walking over to the bed and leaning down, he gives Jaslene a kiss on the cheek. With that done, he heads for the door. “Adam!” Jaslene calls out just as he grips the doorknob. He turns back to face her. She is kneeling on the bed with a cell phone in her hand glaring at him. “Nice try but I think you forgot something.” She says as she tosses it to him. He catches it and smiles, “Of course I did, how silly of me. It’s not like I was trying to leave without it or anything.” “Uh huh.” Jaslene says with a stone cold look in her eyes. “Thanks babe, I’ll be back in a bit.” Adam says depositing the phone into the front pocket of the sweatshirt. “Bye!” Jaslene calls out as he opens and walks through the door. Adam lets the door close behind him. ”I wonder what that was all about?” He turns and heads down the hallway. Nearly every person he passes offers a ‘Good Morning’ in one way or another. Be it nodding, hand gesture or just simply saying, “Good Morning!” with a smile. Adam simply nods in return trying to keep his head down as much as possible. He comes to the front lobby. There’s not much activity taking place. There is one person behind the main desk and another sitting in the lobby watching the morning news. Adam continues out to the front entrance. Before he can open the door, another man opens it from the other side. “Good Morning.” The middle-aged man says with a smile holding the door open for Adam. “Thanks.” Adam replies walking through the door. He is greeted by a dark and murky morning. A light rainfall slowly comes out of the darkened sky. The sun is just barely sticking out over the lines of trees in the distance. Adam takes in a deep breath and exhales slowly. ”This should be good.” he thinks to himself. He turns and begins to jog off. -Meanwhile back in the room- Jaslene is now dressed in a pair of mesh shorts and a ’Got Stryker?’ PCW shirt. She’s kneeling down in front of Adam’s suitcase again. This time she pulls out the same black bag but also a notebook. She looks puzzled at the notebook but curiosity gets the better of her as she takes it back to the bed. She takes a seat and opens it up. August Two Thousand and Two, Had a horrible match with Jackson Rust for the DWO Title on Travesty. It was insane, PPV quality type match. I’m in so much pain right now it’s not even funny. I went to Dr. Bruckman again today, he was able to get me some Codeine. I don’t like that as much, it makes me too out of it to really do anything. I told him that but he was telling me it’s a pretty standard pain killer. Jaslene reads a bit more before flipping a page of so. “My God…” she says stopping on a passage. Late August Two Thousand and Two, the aftermath of that Ladders/Scaffold match is worse than I thought. My head is still ringing, the Codeine has done nothing. I’ve tried taking some Percocet but they aren’t working either. If the pain doesn’t go away soon I’m going to have to pop some Vicadine. I’ll do anything to keep the pain away. I can let anyone down, everyone is looking to me to carry this company and I have to follow through. “He’s killing himself!” Jaslene exclaims, she drops the book and it flips toward the end. She picks it back up and reads some more. Late April Two Thousand and Three, took another shot to the head last night in a match. My head is still ringing. I don’t know how many more of these I have in me but I took a few Vicadine and I’m feeling somewhat better. Bruckman thinks that my insomnia might be due to taking too many of these painkillers. I doubt that though since some of these are meant to help you sleep. I think he thinks I’ve got a problem. He told me that he wants to see me cut back to which I replied to do so would in effect end my career. That didn’t go over too well. Tears begin to fall onto the pages as Jaslene quickly drops it to the floor so as not to give Adam any evidence she had seen his journal. She drops her head into her hands and begins to cry. “I…I had no idea…” she sobs, “I can’t let this go on but I’m powerless to stop it…” -Back to Adam- He’s still jogging, the rain has damped the shoulders and the hood of his sweatshirt. “Another match with Smoke, what are the idiots who book the shows thinking? I’m much too good of an entertainer to be wasted on a stoner. It’s fucking ridiculous.” The clouds begin to break allowing the rising sun to shine through. Adam reaches up and pulls his hood back. “One thing is for certain, Smoke is going to know some real pain. The shit he has pulled on AJ in the last few weeks isn’t going to help him now. It’s only going to fuel the fire. Smoke is alone, no one is willing to help him out. Jackson doesn’t care one bit about him, he was thrown into that tag match against his will…at least that’s what he told me.” The sign to the hotel can be seen just on the horizon. “We’ll know soon enough if I do have a reason to be in the PCW. I have been shaky since I came to PCW and I need to prove to myself that I belong.” The hotel is now just a few hundred feet away as Adam picks up the pace. “Not long now Smoke, only a few hours remain until we find out just what you are made of and just what I am made of. I know I’m the better man, now I must prove it to the rest of the PCW.” Adam stops right at the front entrance. He bends over and takes in a few deep gasps of air. -Back in the room- Jaslene is sitting on the edge of the bed, everything is clean and she is trying to stop herself from crying. She wipes the tears from her eyes just as the door opens. She quickly reaches down and turns on the TV. Adam steps into the room and notices that she is crying. “Hey, is everything all right?” he asks pulling his sweatshirt off, “You look upset…have you been crying?” Jaslene points to the TV, “It’s the movie…I can’t help it.” Adam walks over and looks to the TV. “Jas…you are watching Austin Powers Three.” He says sarcastically. “I know but Beyonce Knowles is just absolutely terrible it brings me to tears every time I watch this movie.” She says through a couple of sobs. Adam reaches down and picks up the remote. He changes the channel to ESPN. “Here watch Sportscenter, it’s not quite as bad as that, I’m going to take a quick shower.” He says handing the remote back to her. He bends down and kisses her on the head before walking off toward the bathroom. “Christ…” Jaslene sighs. The scene fades out as Jaslene falls back on the bed and continues to cry.
