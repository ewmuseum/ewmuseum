Title: Our Feature Presentation: Act I Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Today again ::The 'CORRUPTION MAN!' Title screen and opening credits fade quickly..as we pan through the city....the city of Des Moines, Iowa, although made to look much bigger and majestic than it really is. Schultz elbows Powell.:: Roger Schultz: Poetic license? William S. Powell: Yes. Now shut up, and watch the damn movie. ::Back to Des Moines...it's skyscrapers....as we slowly pan down to street level. It is nighttime, and a beautiful full moon is high in the sky. The narrator begins to speak again.:: In Des Moines, Iowa...goodness has forever attempted to penetrate all facets of life. Organizations such as the National Organization of Women, the NAACP, and *gasp* even the BOY SCOUTS OF AMERICA have attempted to quell the evil that reigns in this unfair metropolis. As a matter of fact, there is one right now... :On street level, a grandmother stands at a busy street corner, shivering in the cold dark of night. Her cane is old and worn, her face wrinkled with the signs of age. She is clearly a damsel in distress. Suddenly, from out of our field of view, comes a Boy Scout, about seventeen years old. He wears the full Boy Scout regalia, and carries 'Brainteasers for Mensa Members' in his right hand.:: Boy Scout: Hello there! My name is Byron! Can I help you across this street ma'am? Grandmother: Why certainly, sonny! It's wonderful to see someone doing some good in this city! Poor Boy Scout. Poor grandmother. They know not what awaits them. For, with the speaking of the word 'good', come several interesting side-effects.. ::In the sky, a giant letter 'C' lights up. It's brightness is stark against the night sky.:: The Corruption Symbol flashes high over Des Moines! It is only a matter of time, before it is spotted...and then, may whatever deity you believe in have mercy on the Boy Scout and grandmother.. Byron the Boy Scout: Don't mention it! Here... :He takes the old woman's arm, and gently begins to escort her across the street. Suddenly, from out of nowhere, a loud, booming voice screams out.: 'CORRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRUUUUUUUUUPPPPPPPPTTTTTIIIIOOOONNNNNN MAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAANNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN!!!!!!!!!!' ::A red Cadillac limousine pulls up to the street corner, nearly hitting the Boy Scout and grandmother.:: THE CORRUPTICADILLAC HAS ARRIVED! THAT CAN ONLY MEAN ONE THING! ::Odd voices begin calling out.:: Is it a stockbroker? Is it a Enron executive? ::The narrator speaks.:: NO! IT IS............CORRUPTION MAN!!! ::From behind the wheel, Corruption Man speaks to his loyal sidekick, Filibuster Boy:: Corruption Man: Did we hit them, Filibuster Boy? Filibuster Boy: No sir! We just missed! Corruption Man: Blast it! We're going to have to do this on foot then! ::Corruption Man is first to emerge from the vehicle. He wears a red and black body suit, with a pulsing blue 'C' in the center of the chest.:: Corruption Man: THERE WILL BE NO GOODNESS ON MY WATCH, YOU VILE BOY SCOUT YOU! ::The boy scout seems quite perturbed by Corruption Man's appearance.:: Byron the Boy Scout: Hey! What the hel-- err, what the HECK is wrong with you? I'm just trying to escort this kindly old lady across the street! I can get a HELPFULNESS MERIT BADGE for this, sir! ::At the sound of Byron's speech, Corruption Man begins to fall, hands at his ears.:: Corruption Man: GAAAAAHHHHHH!!!! HE SAID THAT EVIL WORD!! FILIBUSTER BOY! QUICK! MAKE HASTE WITH THE DUCT TAPE! ::Filibuster Boy hurriedly scampers out of the car, duct taping the boy scout's mouth firmly shut.:: Filibuster Boy: AT YOUR SERVICE, CORRUPTION MAN! ::The grandmother quickly attempts to get away, well, as quickly as she can. Instead, Filibuster Boy simply trips the elderly woman. Her body falls, teeth chipping against the asphalt, a pitiful cry arising from her lips.:: Grandmother: My hip! I think I broke my hip! Help! Help me! ::The two villains turn away callously from the elderly woman, as Corruption Man issues his trademark cry: Corruption Man: TO THE TOWERS.......OF CORRUPTION!! ::Both men hop into the Corrupticadillac....as they speed off back to the Towers of Corruption. However....it is in a different place that we head to.....for.....deep in a Des Moines apartment.....we find the RENT-A-CAVE!, with the Tobey McGwire played Rent-A-Hero:: Rent-A-Hero: Holy nogoodniks, Sexual Innuendo Girl! Corruption Man and his sidekick are at it again! :Sexual Innuendo Girl stands at his side. She is a rather fetching redhead. However, her goals seem to be counter to the usual of Rent-A-Hero...:: Sexual Innuendo Girl: Can't someone else take care of it? I have something I'd like to take care of now.. Rent-A-Hero: And what would that be, my sweets? ::Before she can get her answer out, the infamous beginning to the 'Trojan-Man' jingle begins, and out comes Ron Jeremy, looking reasonable like Snow would if he was dressed up in a Trojan Man outfit.:: Rent-A-Hero: My partner in do-gooding! What are you doing dressed up in that costume! Trojan Man: I've decided to go into business! I will help damsels in distress with.. romantic problems. Rent-A-Hero: Romantic problems? ::At this point, Sexual Harassement Girl begins muttering under her breath:: Sexual Harassment Girl: Would that include helping a completely lame Hero who probably doesn't know what sex IS?! ::Trojan Man chooses to ignore her...:: Trojan Man: See, I've decided to use the whole 'Women orgasm when they see me' thing for the powers of ROMANTIC GOOD! Rent-A-Hero: Or WHAT?! Anyway, it doesn't matter. Corruption Man and Filibuster Boy have continued their path of badness! AND WE MUST STOP THEM! QUICK! TO THE RENT-A-CYCLE!! ::Rent-A-Hero and Trojan Man go off, leaving Sexual Innuendo Girl alone.:: Sexual Innuendo Girl: Why couldn't we just go to the Rent-A-Bedroom? WILL CORRUPTION MAN AND HIS SIDEKICK KEEP THE STREETS SAFE FOR EVIL?WILL THE SUPERLATIVE SUPERHEROES END THEIR QUEST?FIND OUT IN ACT II! ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
