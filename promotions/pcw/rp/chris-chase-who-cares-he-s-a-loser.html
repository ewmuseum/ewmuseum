Chris Chase? Who cares!! He's a loser.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: With American Pride -The Malice Man wears a suit and a cowboy hat as he walks through the offices of PCW. A pesky young reporter named Alvin Austin tracks down the Missoula muscle man and corners him. Duke adjusts his shades and talks to the kid for a few moments before the audio is kicked in.- Austin: This is Alvin Austin reporting for the PCW Palace were I’ve tracked down the president of this fine company he is Duke Williams. Duke how are you feeling? Williams: I feel like I need a damn drink hoss. Austin: Can somebody get this man a cola. Williams: Make it a whiskey Skippy. Austin: Very well we’ll get you some booze. Now Mr. Williams what are your thoughts on stepping in the ring with the Saint Chris Chase? Williams: My thoughts? Who’s booking these show Mickey Mouse matches man? Austin: I’m not sure. Williams: I thought you were a reporter? Austin: I am. Williams: Then do some investigations hoss. Austin: Will do sir. Williams: How do I feel about this match? Austin: Yes. Williams; He’s on a mission from God? Austin: That’s what he says. Williams: Does he think he’s Elwood Blues? Austin: I’m not sure. Williams: Cus he ain’t no damn Blues Brother. If he’s on a mission from God we can solve this fast. Austin: We can? Williams: We no, me yes. I am God so if he’s doing my work for me then is’s as simple as sucking on a nipple. Shut your damn mouth, lay on the mat, let me pin you. A f*cking work of art if you ask me. Praise be to Duke!!! I’ll swim on water, turn wine into urine, and eat your ladies fish. What more proof do you need? Austin: I’m convinced. Williams: You know what else bugs me kid? -Duke adjusts his shades.- Austin: What’s that? Williams: This f*cking hump is in the biggest match of his life and he’s talking about Xavior Kannon. Austin: Perplexing. Williams: Yea, per-like-you-said! This guy shouldn’t worry about a match he will never see. Austin: He is a former world champion. Williams: Isn’t that a bite in the balls hoss? -Duke puts a thumb to his nose.- Williams: What a dark day in PCW history. What a f*cking joke with a bad punch line poncho. This guy being world champion is the equivalent of saying Carrottop is funnier then Carlin. If this f*cking mook thinks that was anything more then a cute gimmick champion, he needs to wake up out of never-never land. Snap out of it Chase. You need to worry about the here and now not where you will never be again. You dig hoss? You catch the words flying out of my mouth. Cus hoss I don’t buy into the Chris Chase hype. I don’t find you or any of the other young stars very impressive. You bore me son. Where did he disappear to on the “vacation”? -Duke turns to Alvin Austin.- Austin: Not really sure. Williams: Do you care? Austin: I can’t say that I do. Williams: You catch my drift Chase? Nobody f*cking cares. Where are they now? Who gives two strokes on the old flesh pony. You see Chase you are just what I need to get out of my slump. An example of a poor excuse for a champion. A kid from the now generation. You want it now and don’t care about the order of structure. F*ck you and your ways Chase. -A man hands Duke a drink.- Williams: Well hell hoss I was ready to wrap things up but now they we got drinks lets keep going. Grab a seat son. -Duke and Alvin both sit down on some plush leather couches.- Alvin: So you’re going to hurt him to send a message? Williams: Is that your final answer? Of coarse I’m going to hurt him. Like God sent a message to Moses with the Ten Commandments so will I. -Duke puts his hands in the air like he’s possessed then grabs a PCW travel pack pamphlet sitting on the coffee table.- Williams: As a matter of fact God has just sent me the Top Ten Commandments of Malice from the home office in Wahoo, Nebraska. Alvin: God has an office in Nebraska? Williams: What else does that state have? Alvin: I got you. So what is the top ten? Williams: I don’t know there is nothing in here but travel tips. Alvin: The lord works in mysterious ways. Williams: You ain’t kidding there is a great coupon in here. Alvin: So is that? Williams: F*ck no that ain’t it. Chase isn’t allowed to talk about Kannon but I sure can. This hump wants out to OSW adios motherf*cker pack your bags and get out. Alvin: What about Ellie? Williams: If that little walking/talking glory hole wants to push her commissioner powers then this President will get presidential and that’s not my style slick. Alvin: You’ll go head to head with her? Williams: Kid she’s a pro at giving head so that would be tough but I’ll do my best. Alvin: But first its going to be Chris Chase. Williams: Turmoil things will heat up when Christopher goes Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. Alvin: That’s the truth. Williams: The honky tonk truth. =w=
