What does Vince McMahon want with my Sportscenter showcase?

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 28, 2002 Joy....rapture....happiness. Three words that continually describe the emotions that come with victory. Ask yourself a question: How do you feel when you win? It doesn't matter what you win at - it could be a game, a puzzle, hell...even a victory on an exam is pretty exciting. It is the feeling of accomplishment when you realize that you have succeeded at something that you have worked so hard for. It feels sweet. That was the scene when Germany defeated the U.S. in the World cup game. And yes, it was on tape delay. But it felt oddly exhilarating as he watched it in his apartment and that good, German player had that goal. Tom even had the nerve to stand up and shout, “Gooooooooaaaal!” Natalie promptly slapped him on the back of the head for not having any national pride. ....for Tom Lexian, it meant he was four million dollars richer. Words could hardly describe the reaction of Tom Lexian as he watched the game anxiously at his home. The game meant more than just money - it meant success. With this victory he was two steps closer to getting on to national television and having his story told to the entire world. The Legend Inc. story. Robert “Night Shard” Yarney had been by his side while they watched the game. He had just gotten back from his day out with his son and had just heard the news about the loan, the bet, and the bid to be on the SportsCenter Showcase. Needless to say he was a little skeptical - ok, he was very skeptical. But being the friend that he was he would help Tom out now. After the game they received a call from Jimmy the Fish to come pick up the money at a local Mickey D's in the morning. From there they would head down to a local ESPN News Media building where a special 'meeting' would be held. The scene opens to Robert and Tom leaving the McDonalds with a briefcase in hand. A very heavy briefcase. * * * Robert Yarney: [struggles to hold up the briefcase] Geez, this thing is heavy. Legend Inc.: Well what do you expect? There's four million dollars in there in bills. It's not supposed to be light. Robert Yarney: Well, bills are light, aren't they? I mean, they're made of paper. Legend Inc.: [sighs] Oh my god...Robert, what the hell is wrong with you this morning? You've been making dumb remarks all day. Robert Yarney: [laughs] I'm just playing around, Tom. It's all this time I've been spending with Brad. Legend Inc.: How old is he now? Three? Four? Robert Yarney: [proudly] He's three years, four months, and six days. He's headstrong, just like his old man. He just won't take no for an answer. Legend Inc.: [sarcastically] Isn't that how you had Brad in the first place? Robert Yarney: [hits Tom in the shoulder] Don't joke around about that. That's my son you're talking about. Legend Inc.: [opens up the car door and gets into the drivers seat] You know I'm just poking some fun at you. I love the little guy. It's like having a little Night Shard around all the time. He really looks up to you. Robert Yarney: [gets in the passenger side] Yeah, I noticed. But that's really a good thing. I mean, Brenda kept him away from me so long I thought he'd might not even remember me. Legend Inc.: So he asks a lot of weird questions? Robert Yarney: Oh, he's curious about everything. He keeps asking me what I do for a living. Legend Inc.: Are you going to tell him? Robert Yarney: Tell him what? That I'm a wrestler, or at least used to be? That I hurt people as my vocation? I'm sorry, but I don't want my son going down my path. Legend Inc.: [keeps his eyes on the road] I didn't know you felt that way about our sport. I didn't think you were ashamed. Robert Yarney: I'm not ashamed. I've learned a lot through this sport. Pain, agony... Legend Inc.: ...victory, friendship.... Robert Yarney: [looks at Tom] More pain and agony than victory and friendship, buddy. But for all the wrong that has happened to me because of this sport, I still wouldn't give any of it up. I'd do it all again. Legend Inc.: But you wouldn't want Brad to follow in your footsteps. Robert Yarney: I'd rather him do something else. Legend Inc.: Maybe he could be a banker. Or maybe a correspondent to ESPN. Robert Yarney: [laughs] Maybe. I still can't believe how you're trying to pull off this scam. Legend Inc.: It's not a scam. It's a plan. Robert Yarney: Tom, you loaned out two million dollars under the surname 'Tee Howard,' you then bet it on the World Cup game and doubled your money, and then you're going to try to be the highest bidder in some Showcase auction and be on the show. I mean, does that sound ridiculous to you? Legend Inc.: [admittedly] Ok, I was a bit disbelieving at first too. But the plan is already in motion. This really could give me a lot of exposure. Robert Yarney: Are you really that worried about Miyagawa, Kannon and Duke? I mean, you could take those guys out no problem. Legend Inc.: That's what I thought about Frisco, and look what happened there. Robert Yarney: You tried to wrestle his style. You beat yourself. Legend Inc.: The last thing I need to hear is how I beat myself, alright? I'll take care of business this sunday, don't worry. Miyagawa is going off this hero thing himself. Something about the Popeman chronicles? [shrugs] And El Dukie always has a plan, but he keeps talking smack about me. It’s alright, he’s always been the better instigator. Robert Yarney: What about Kannon, dude? I don't think he's a pushover. Legend Inc.: Oh, Kannon? The former champion? He never really lost that title, but I doubt his head is in the game. He wants more, and BAS is holding what he wants. I doubt he’ll be much of a challenge. Robert Yarney: I know you're ready physically, but do you really have to go through with this Sportscenter thing? I mean... Legend Inc.: Sean, I have to do it. Mentally I need to get over that double DQ from last week. Mentally...I'm not confident. Well, I am...but I need something to reassure me. I need to reassure myself that people know who Legend Inc. is and that I will go in that ring and take care of business. Robert Yarney: Well I hope you trust this Joker person, because he could be setting you up for a huge screw job. Legend Inc.: I know. But for now I have to trust him. He hasn't swerved me yet. Robert Yarney: So this News Media building we're going to...how do we know it's actually holding the so called 'Bid' for the show? Legend Inc.: [hands Robert the note] I got this under my door this morning. It's a note from the Joker. Robert Yarney: [reads the note] He's pretty specific in here. He gives the exact location of the bidding and what to do.....[reads on]...who's Mr. Vitale? Legend Inc.: You don't know who Dick Vitale is? Robert Yarney: [shakes his head] Should I? Legend Inc.: You obviously don't watch college basketball very often. Dick Vitale is an announcer for ESPN and he does college games. He's probably one of the most energetic men I've ever seen on television. Robert Yarney: The note says to go to talk to him before the bidding. Do you know why? Legend Inc.: [shrugs] Only the Joker knows. I would think he would have contacted me in person by now, but he's nowhere to be found. Robert Yarney: Man, that guy is weird. What exactly does he look like? Legend Inc.: You mean besides the green pants and the brown trench coat? Robert Yarney: Yeah. Legend Inc.: I'm not so sure. He's some white guy with dark brown hair. But he's always wearing shades and some baseball cap. Last time it was a Texas Ranger's cap. Robert Yarney: So he's a big A-Rod fan then? Legend Inc.: Bah. I don't know. He's a definite weirdo with a capital 'W.' He just better know what he's doing or else... Robert Yarney: ...or else what? Legend Inc.: [looks at Sean] Or else we take care of it, like we always do. Robert Yarney: [looks forward and sees the ESPN building] Hey, there it is. * * * Tom parked the car a few blocks from the building. The parking meter was only two hours but Tom was pretty sure that he and Robert would be in the bidding for more than that. Tom had other ideas than rushing out here to refill the meter though... Tom went into the back of his car and grabbed a bat and a plastic bag. He began swinging at the meter until the head of the meter popped off sending change raining to the rain. Some homeless folk ran over and picked up the loose change that was sprawled on the pavement. Tom ignored the scene and placed the plastic bag over the meter to make it look like it was broken. Well, it was broken now. Tom and Robert made their way into the building which was only three stories high. It was not a studio, but a correspondent news headquarters for the area. Since Northern California hosted such teams like the Oakland A's, Oakland Raiders, SF Giants and Niners, and many more, it was only common sense to have a news team here all the time. As they walked into the main room of the building them saw a lot of athletes hanging around. Stars such as Derek Jeter, Carlos Delgado, Evander Holyfield and....Vince McMahon? What was the icon of professional wrestling doing here? And what did he want? Tom just shook his head. This wasn’t good.
