In dreams we find salvation...

15 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/01/02 Ya'know I dream sometimes. Yeah, not about spilling the blood of someone who's decided to try and set off the short fuse that has become my sadistic nature. I mean real dreams, I'll even go as far as saying happy thoughts. When you travel nearly all of your adult life all you can do sometimes is dream. There's guys and girls in PCW and RUSH, tough people, been doing this longer than I have. Travelled up the same highways, caught the same flights, put up with the same old shit airplane food and become honourable members of the mile high club just to kill the time. Yeah, they've done this more times than they care to remember. But they've grown old. They've wizened up to the world. They've become aware that there is a world outside this industry and that the people they have come to love and care for live outside of it. They live their dreams, whether it's eventually retiring with the traditional wife and two kids living in a plush house with the freshly mown lawns with the white picket fences just outside Beverley Hills, financial security and the comfort of life's benefits all included in this all American dream package. Or just simply having the freedom to take up next to someone who they've been in a relationship with that for once has lasted longer than their last piss they took in the men's urinals in their local bar. Knowing they've bled in the ring enough to earn the right to no longer work for a living. Yeah, those are things I dream about. 'Fucked up in the head' as I've been dubbed, even I can't help but get that warm feeling inside when I dream of these little things. I think they call it hope... That's usually what wakes me up though, that feeling, because let's face it, hope leads to nothing but a disappointing anti climax. So before hope hits, I try and make the most of that time when dreaming. I've got a woman, one I love. After all the years of bullshit and deceit I still find it hard to trust people in these dreams, but not her. Whoever she is she's tamed me, God knows how but she did, but that's why I love her. We've got a house, a damn big one too. Her idea, everything in the house is, furniture, decorating, the whole lot. It's just to keep her happy. I'm content with the large bed I sleep and nail her in every night. The rest of the house though, it's all her field and territory. Then we've got a kid, in the dream I can't decide whether to name him after Axl Rose, Steve Vai or Kirk Hammett. Maybe soon I'll name him after all three. He's about 4, I used to feel awkward with him calling me Dad, not anymore though. He's got my deep blue eyes and hair, and that's it. The rest comes from his mother, including his defiance. One other thing he's got from me though is the talent to make a guitar speak poetically. Sure, it's a kids electric thing that plays old classics by The Beatles when you press certain buttons and all other crazy noises. Yet he knows what he's doing with it. Yeah, he's gonna be a rock star maybe. He's gonna go through the same shit that we all go through on the road in this industry. He's gonna be as defiant as his old man on the road though, and he'll hang in there. Then......... Then hope sets in. I look at the people around me, living their dreams that are waiting back at home for them. Jesus............... I fucking loathe them.
