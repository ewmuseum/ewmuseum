Confessing Fusion

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 15 Even Snow can’t really believe that it happened, but he is once again the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World... funny, he thought the journey would be harder, from the outset. But now, just a week after returning to PCW, he is once again the champion... whether he deserves the title around his waist is another story. Of course, there are more deserving champions - Snow will never admit that. Aside from Snow’s controversial second championship win, there was other big news to come out of Friday Fusion. All rumours that had New Breed disband were discredited completely, as New Breed showed up in full force, even bringing along a brand new member - Deadly Dustin Downey. Downey had been a long time tag team partner, and friend, of former PCW Champion Chris Chase - apparently, that friendship remains well in tact.... So what does this mean for Chris Chase? Why did he so willingly give up the Greatest prize in all of wrestling to Snow? Only time will tell... Priest: “Are you Catholic, my son?” We can’t see much, as our view is not well-lit... Snow: “Catholic? I’m Superior!” Snow’s tone is harsh - he often takes offense to labels that don’t address his “Greatness” and “Superiority”. It soon becomes obvious that we are with Snow inside a cramped Catholic confessional. A small light from the tiny window gleams onto Snow’s face - the light comes from the other side of the booth, which is occupied by a priest... Priest: “But your voice, my son - it seems so... ... familiar...” Snow: “Well it should, damn it! I am, of course, the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World!” Priest: “The... what did you say?” Snow: “You heard me, damn it!” Priest: “The PCW Heavyweight Champion of the... ... ... PCW, as in, the wrestling organization?” Snow’s tone becomes sarcastic... Snow: “No! PCW as in, Priests that Can’t Wu... uh... ... ...” When he finally admits that there is no colorful insult to tack on, Snow raises his voice with the priest... Snow: “OF COURSE IT’S PCW THE WRESTLING ORGANIZATION!” Priest: “...And you’re Jason Snow... I’m familiar with your work.” Snow: “Well of course you are! I mean, you are a man of God, after all! And I, being the Greatest man that God has created in the last couple of millenniums, would obviously be studied by priests and other religious bastards the world over!” The priest doesn’t know how to respond to this - Snow is getting quite used to that reaction by now. People never know quite how to take his insults - usually they don’t quite untangle his web of words for at least a dozen seconds. The priest decides to hurry this along - he is by no means star struck by the PCW Champion... Priest: “Well... do you have any sins to confess?” Snow: “Sins? Why in the hell would I have sins? Didn’t I already explain to you about my Superiority and Greatness?” Priest: “No man is without sin, Ja- er... my son.” Snow: “No peasant, like yourself, maybe. But we Superior Individuals are of a higher breed, you know. One might even refer to us as... a new breed.” In the streak of light that is smeared across the champions face, we can see him smirking at his pun... Snow sees a shred of his own cleverness as pure brilliance. We can hear the priest sigh... Priest: “So you don’t think you’ve done anything wrong?” Snow: “Of course not!” Priest: “Not even at last Friday’s Fusion? ...I did watch you know.” Snow: “Fusion? Of course I didn’t do anything wrong at Fusion! By Greatness, I did something right! I did something GREAT! I won the PCW Championship, damn it!” Priest: “And you don’t feel the least bit guilty about how you won it?” Snow pretends to be confused, though we all know that he’s not. Snow’s knows better than anyone that his match with Chris Chase on Fusion was a sham. Still, Snow goes with the same old story... Snow: “Chris Chase and I had one of the most epic battles in PCW history!” Priest: “The match lasted four seconds!” Snow: “Take it from me - afterall, the mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!! ...Some Great things have happened in four seconds.” Priest: “What does that have to do with anything? One minute we’re talking about your pathetic excuse for a wrestling match, the next your talking about orgasms!” Snow: “Well... for Greatness sakes, how would YOU know what it feels like in the ring? Sure, the match may have only lasted a matter of mere moments, but you have no idea the... uh... uh...” Snow tries to think something up as quickly as possible... Snow: “The mental battle that was going on! Damn it, our minds don’t work like the minds of peasants, you know!” The priest is sick of this now. He doesn’t want to listen to Snow anymore - who would. Snow’s ego would wear on the best of pacifists... Priest: “You’re being ridiculous. Why did you come to confession if you claim to have nothing to confess!?” Snow: “Well I thought that this was a concession booth! Is that so wrong? No! Hell no! That’s not wrong at all! In fact, since I’m the Superior Individual here, I demand you get me a hotdog!” Priest: “A...” The priest stammers... Priest: “A hotdog? You want a hotdog? A HOTDOG!?!?” Snow: “Damn it, I saw a big bowl of water when I came in here. Would it be too damn hard for you to boil it in that!?” Snow just bought himself more bad publicity than he will ever need. Snow, who is a religious man at heart, obviously doesn’t know much about the Catholic Faith. We can almost see the priest’s face reddening on the other side of the wall. We know what’s coming next... Priest: “Out! OUT! Get out of the house of God! OUT!” Amazingly enough, Snow is shocked by the priests reaction. A moment later, we hear the door on the priest’s side slam shut, signalling that the man of the cloth has made his exit. After a moment of silence, Snow thrusts his hands into the air and exclaims... Snow: “Fine! I’ll make the hotdogs!” As the Killin’ Villain stands up and makes his exit, the PCW Camera Man decides that it would probably be best for Snow’s image, and the image of PCW, if he stopped filming. And with that, the scene fades to black...
