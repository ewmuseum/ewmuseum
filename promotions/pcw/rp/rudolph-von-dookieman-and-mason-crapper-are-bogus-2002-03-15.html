Rudolph von Dookieman and Mason Crapper are bogus

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15th [The scene opens one the large words that read 'Patterson Port-O-Potty'. In the background many loud sounds can be heard such as drills, jackhammers, and insanely, loud beeping. Over all of that sound you can hear a faint sound of a farting noise and right behind the farting, splashing sounds. The scene begins to zoom out showing more and more of the brown Port-O-Potty. Behind the moving toilet is a large construction site, obviously some sort of skyskrapers going up. Cranes, wrecking balls, and bulldozers all line the area, ready to destroy and rebuild. Then a long, brown haired man runs to the Port-O-Potty and begins trying to open the door, but its not budging. The man turns and looks around at the construction site. He wears a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. His eyes are bloodshot and glazed over like ever before as he bounces back and forth trying not to make any false moves. One half of the Cowabunga Connection, 'Heavy Metal' Harry leans into the door and begins his plea] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I need to use the bathroom, man. [A very deep and rough voice is returned] Deep and rough voice: Shut up! I'll be out in a minute. Harry: You don't understand, I really have to go, man. Deep and rough voice: Listen up, pally boy. I'm in here takin' a huge dump and if I let you in here I'll still have to go. Now hold your freakin' horses and I'll be out in a minute! [Harry's face droops in disappointment and pain. He can feel his waste building up and wanting to come out of his body. He holds his stomach, leaning against the potty. The feeling just suddenly came over him, as if someone touched him with a magic wand and it all appeared. As he leans harder the door opens, smacking the stocky, Twisted Sister listening man in the back of his head. Harry falls to the ground, then sees a fat guy wearing an orange hard hat coming out with a satisfied smile on his face] Construction worker: Its all yours, pally boy. [Harry quickly enters the potty and then he stops dead in his tracks. The smell is just awful! Whatever came out of that construction worker had to be something alien! Nobody should have a smell like that, even if you are letting out excrement. Harry begins gagging a little bit, desperately wanting to just sit down and let it all out. He coughs now, bending over trying to get the smell out of his mind. He can't do it!] Harry: Not far out. Not far out at all, man. [Then from behind Harry another man appears. This man wears a PCW T-shirt, blue swim trunks with yellow lining on the waist, and a pair of black sandals. The man looks almost like a greek God with his perfect tan, perfect smile and teeth, and the bright, blonde hair that shines even in the darkest of places. Thats right, you've guessed it, the other half of Cowabunga Connection, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: What's wrong, dude? Can't you go? [Tommy approaches the potty now and he stops, holding his nose. His face contorts into a very disgusted looking face] TTT: BOGUS, dude! Did you do that? [Harry looks back still coughing and gagging] Harry: No, man. TTT: That is the most heinous smell ever! How can you even stand in there, compadre? [Harry comes out of the potty, holding his stomach] Harry: No way, man. We have to find someplace else, man. TTT: Yeah, dude. I can't believe you just jumped up like that. I've never seen you move so fast. It was a most bodacious site. Harry: There is nothing bodacious about this, man. [Tommy then shrugs and begins looking all around. They are in a small suburb like community with little knick knack shops all around, a neighborhood grocery store, and a very 50's style cafe which the two men came from, or should we say, Harry ran. Tommy then scratches his head still not sure how this sickness hit Harry so hard] TTT: I just find it most bogus that you suddenly got sick. I think that there is a most heinous conspiracy against us since we joined the PCW. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Weird things have been happening to us since we found out that our opponents are Rudolph Von Hackenmann and Mason Creeper, Fear Factory. Those dudes are extremely bogus, we should know, we were in NTICW with them, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy then looks at the construction site. The steel beams all connected together in a framing, almost in the shape of what this structure is going to be. Tommy smiles feeling that building things is TOTALLY bodacious. The smile fades as he then realizes what could have caused this tragedy] TTT: Dude, remember when you got up? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: I turned my head and started talking to a bodacious babe, and wasn't paying attention to your food. I bet those bogus opponents of ours could have slipped something in your food. Talk about HEINOUS!! Harry: Yeah, man. Stupid Fear Factory. TTT: Totally. More like Rudolph Von Dookieman and Mason Crapper. Harry: Ha Ha. Yeah, man. Rudy Poo and Turd Creeper. TTT: Ha Ha. Gnarly!! [Tommy laughs and then looks over to the diner, and frowns. He realizes that Fear Factory couldn't have gotten far if they put something into Harry's food. They could be watching them now like a couple of bogus peeping toms] TTT: We have to think back to the diner, dude. Retrace our steps and make sure we saw Rudy Poo and Mason Crapper there. Harry: Ok, man. [The two men pause and Harry taps Tommy on the shoulder] Harry: Can we find a bathroom first, man? TTT: Oh yeah! That would be the gnarly thing to do. TUBULAR FLASHBACK! ------------------ [The scene changes over to the inside of the 50's style diner. There is just large clouds of smoke all around as many sit at the grill area eating their breakfasts and drinking their coffee. At the booths, which are pretty empty most people enjoy their large portion breakfasts and orange juice. The waitresses are dressed in long dresses and wear those ridiculous dark red aprons that say 'Milky's Diner'. 'Heavy Metal' Harry and 'Totally Tubular' Tommy sit at a booth in the corner. The counter tops are an odd shade of puke green and white, not giving an appetite appealing look. 'In My Room' by the Beach Boys plays lightly over the jukebox beside the men] Harry: Why did you have to pick the Beach Boys, man? TTT: Because they are bodacious! Harry: Should have picked Quiet Riot, man. [The waitress then walks over with two large plates of food and sets them down in front of Harry and Tommy. The woman has her long, black hair tied back in a hairnet so not to have her nice locks in the fattening food. She is very attractive with very little makeup on. Her breasts perk out nicely from the dress that all the waitresses have to wear. By the looks of it it seems that she was someplace very cold. She smiles at the two gentlemen who seem out of place in a small town like this] Waitress: A super pancake special for the hair metal guy and a delicious eggs deluxe for the surfer. You boys have fun now. [The waitress begins walking away as Harry and Tommy both watch her nice, shapely behind shaking back and forth. The two men then smile to each other and nod] TTT: Now that is a bodacious bottom. Harry: Perfect rhythm, man. [The two men then begin digging in to their meals. Harry has a sort of orgasmic look on his face as he chows down a piece of pancake. Tommy is very slowly about comsuming his meal. He places a napkin in his lap as to not get any on his gnarly swim trunks and to help the cleaning people out at the diner. Tommy then looks out of the window on his left seeing the construction site across the street, he thinks about the upcoming match, Cowabunga Connections first ever PCW match] TTT: Are you nervous about our first PCW tag match? [Harry looks up at Tommy with his mouth full of pancake. Harry can't exactly talk so he just shrugs happy to fill his munchy's craving] TTT: What is it with putting us up against bogus scientists? I took Rich Cannon to school at Magic Kingdom Massacre and now they have us with that Rudolph Von Hackenmann dude. Then there is that other heinous guy Mason Creeper. Talk about a boring tag team! [Harry finally swallows his mouthful of pancakes] Harry: Yeah, man. I'll be back. I need more syrup, man. [Harry stands up from the booth and walks out of the scene. Tommy then looks back outside, thinking that stopping at this small town and their way to the Sunday Sabatoge event was a gnarly idea. Its not often that he and Harry get to experience small towns since they are always on the beach or partying in Los Angeles. The waitress with the very rhythmic ass walks by again, smiling at Tommy as she passes. Tommy then turns around taking in the unbelievable site of the waitress. What rhythm she has, and what a Yahtzee player she must be] TTT: Tubular... [As Tommy is distracted, a dark skinned man in a red Independent trucks T-shirt walks up to the table. He pulls out a canister of some sort that reads 'Poo Fast'. The black haired man with the skateboarding shirt on is none other than Harry and Tommy's most hated California enemy, Randy Raggamuffin. The evil skateboarder who is tired of being called Raggamuffin and wants to get revenge on Harry and Tommy. Raggamuffin, real name Randy Raggagu, begins pouring the syrup like liquid over the pancakes with an evil smile on his face. He looks to make sure that Tommy isn't paying attention, and just like clockwork, Tommy is looking at the ass of the waitress] Raggamuffin: Take a load of this, Twisted Sister boy. [Randy then looks left and right, then disappears from sight. Harry comes back with a container full of syrup in his hand and he sees that there is syrup already on his pancakes] Harry: Where did this come from, man? [Tommy whips his head back around, looking down at Harry's plate. Tommy shrugs] TTT: The waitress I guess. [Harry smiles and begins digging into his newly watered pancakes. His face has a large smile on it, satisfied as if he'd just had a long night of sex. Tommy begins taking small bites of his scrambled eggs, satisfied himself with the work of this gnarly chef] TTT: We need to come up with a righteous catchphrase, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Its got to be something that catches the gnarly fans attention and relates to our most bodacious personalities. Its gotta just grab them, smack them around, and then kiss them goodnight, compadre. [Harry's face then lowers as he feels something brewing down below. He then looks at the half eaten pancakes and pushes them away just suddenly feeling very full. Tommy just continues] TTT: It has to be original because if we rip something off it won't be original, right? [Harry can feel his stomach bubbling like crazy as his insides begin pushing madly. He begins sweating, his face turning beat red] Harry: Gotta...go!! [Harry leaps up and runs across the diner to the bathrooms. He tries pushing the door open but it won't budge. The rhythmic assed waitress smiles at Harry] Waitress: Sorry, hun. The bathrooms are closed. Harry: No, man...no. [Harry runs back to the table, trotting back and forth in place] Harry: I need to potty, man! TTT: Totally bogus. Don't do it here! Harry: I need to find a place to potty, man!! [Tommy looks outside and spots the construction site. He sees a fat man in an orange hardhat walk in the Port-O-Potty just at the entrance of the site] TTT: Port-O-Potty at twelve o'clock, dude! [Harry's face contorts as if he's about to cry like a whining child] Harry: I can't wait until twelve, man! I have to potty now! TTT: No, dude! There is a potty out at the construction site!! Go, compadre! [Harry sees it and smiles, seeing his salvation. He looks at Tommy and nods] TTT: Gnarly. [Harry then runs to the exit like lightning and disappears behind the door. Tommy laughs wildly as everyone at the grill looks at the exit wondering where that rush of wind came from. Tommy looks out and sees Harry run to the Port-O-Potty, banging on the door. Bummer, its occupied] END OF TUBULAR FLASHBACK ----------------------- [The scene now shows Tommy standing against a line of bathroom stalls, looking at his bulging biceps in one of the mirrors above the many sinks. A pair of feet sits underneath a stall clear across the bathroom, as water splashing sounds are heard] Harry: AWWWWWWW!! YES, MAN!! [Tommy giggles to himself finding a lot of humor in Harry's misfortune. Rudy Poo and Crapper were no where in site and none of the townspeople have seen then through the day. Tommy did find skateboard tracks from a leaking fire hydrant and knowing that only one man could have done this, Randy Raggamuffin. Tommy pounds his right fist into his left hand now. Raggamuffin will pay dearly for his bogusness. Tommy doesn't know how, but he will. Tommy then smiles thinking of a most righteous catchphrase] TTT: Dude, I've got a killer catchphrase. Harry: Ok, man. TTT: WHATCHA GONNA DO WHEN THE CONNECTION GOES COWABUNGA ON YOU!! [The bathroom echoes out the word 'Cowabunga' for a few moments and then it gets really quiet. Then the silence if followed by a couple farting sounds and more splashing. Then Harry gives his review] Harry: That sounds kind of familiar, man. TTT: It does. Hmmm... [Tommy goes into thought again. He smiles and snaps his fingers together] TTT: I've got it! If you smell what the Cowabunga is cookin'!! Harry: Familiar, dude. [Tommy nods realizing that sounds so oddly familiar] TTT: This catchphrase stuff is most hard. Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy scratches his chin, smiles and fixes his hair in the mirror] TTT: When Rudy Poo and Mason Crapper hear the Quiet Riot, they will go...for...THE WIPEOUT!! [The stall in which Harry was in opens, the toilet flushes, and Harry appears with a stoned out smile on his face] Harry: Far out, man. [The scene fades to black]
