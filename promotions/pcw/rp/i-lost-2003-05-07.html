'I...lost.'

7 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 7, 2003 (All is quiet. The city streets of Yemen at night are not exactly bustling. In fact, only one man stands on them. Coincidentally, he happens to be the only six foot, ten inch tall man in all of Yemen. SOCIETY is out on the mean streets of Yemen, attempting to rationalize what happened mere hours ago.) “I…I lost.” (The words are foreign. For a man of his size, his ability, his…magnitude, losing is something that is supposed to occur after months and months, in a battle of immense importance. That is the way it was for all SOCIETY had studied before even stepping foot in PCW.) “Before I came here, I plotted out my career path. I studied hours of film, and found the men I wished to emulate. Akiyama, the Neo Void God, a man who left destruction in his wake and terror in the hearts of his opponents! Ivan Stanislav, the Communist champion who tore through this company as if it were nothing! And Meanstreak…the man who DEFINED Primetime Championship Wrestling for over a year! THESE were the men I aspired to, and I have FAILED!” (SOCIETY’s bellowing is bootless, his words echoing into the night sky of Yemen. The massive man’s hands extend upwards, genuine agony in his voice.) “This is NOT what I spent my entire life training for on the Falklands! I expected to come in here and run ramshackle over everyone and everything! I had learned the wrestling maneuvers! I knew how to put a crowd in fear at the sound of my voice! I was READY! And my debut went perfectly, yet this did not! Why? I honestly have not found any explanation why. And nor do I expect to. For Shane Wethers, there will be hell to pay in the extremely near future. Know this. You may have defeated SOCIETY once, but there is only so much time that you can survive being different. Your nonconformity will be your downfall, be it this week on Friday Fusion, next week on Turmoil, or at the pay-per-view extravaganza. I WILL get my hands on you again, and the results will be the rough equivalent of your music playing! A cacophony of violence!” (SOCIETY shakes with emotion as he speaks, pouring his soul out to the unheeding sky.) “But, I must move on. Not all will conform immediately. It is a fact of life. It would be foolish for me to have believed that I would never fall. That fall may have occurred much earlier than I would have liked, but it occurred. Now, the measure of SOCIETY will be my ability to get right back up after that fall, and set myself back on the proper path.” (Taking a seat on the dusty curb of the street, SOCIETY looks around him. In the dark, little is visible except the road itself, and the sand extending out for miles beyond the small community.) “Tommorow, I will get on a plane, and continue flying towards my next destination. Essentially, the only thing different between my arrival there and my arrival here in Yemen before Turmoil is that now, I have one loss. One win, and one loss. My third contest is thus of utmost importance. If I win, I will have two victories and one loss, and my chances of advancing on to Supremacy are good. If I lose, I will have one victory and two losses, and those chances diminish greatly. And so, without exaggeration, I can say that this is my most important contest yet.” (His voice falters, cracking slightly, as the unexpected happens. A tear forms in SOCIETY’s eyes, a rare sign of humanity in the superhuman. A rare chink in the armor. An odd glance at the man whose name is classified, rather than the Divine Soldier of the Order of Society and Logic.) “I am through with letting down those who depend upon me. By losing to Shane Wethers, I let down the Order of Society and Logic. I let down Michael Chang. I let down this world’s hopes to have a proper leadership! I USED AN EYE POKE IN THE RING! With all of the weapons at my disposal, with my power and size, I used an eye poke…this truly has been a bad night. For some reason, everything fell apart last night! Now, it is about putting everything back together again. And that occurs with Mr. Robertson in the ring at Fusion.” (Reaching up, SOCIETY obliterates the tear from existence with a simple touch. Any sentimental emotion is gone from his face, as the usual stoic anger of the Conformist returns.) “Robertson, you cannot imagine the danger you find yourself in now. Before, you were simply an opponent that would be made to conform, no more, and no less. Now, however, you are a roadblock. You are standing between me and my spot before a national pay-per-view audience. And for that, you will suffer. For that, Christian, you will pay! As your so-called savior did, you will die for the sins of the society you live in!” (Rising from his seat, SOCIETY turns, headed back towards a tall building that can only be presumed to be his hotel. Before he goes, he looks over his shoulder one final time, back out into the Yemeni night.) “I lost…but I WILL win again. And that win shall come at your expense, Robertson. My time is now. Primetime Championship Wrestling, your conformity by force is STILL imminent! And there are no choices left, save one. And I’m sure you all know it by now.” (As he reaches the door to the hotel, SOCIETY flings it open, and then closes it forcefully, headed off to his room.) End Transmission.
