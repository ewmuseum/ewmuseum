Date: not entered ----------OOC NOTEThe names of The Dark Messiahs have been mixed up deliberately. It is not a mistake on my part.---------- Gold and Glory...Glory and Gold...Why couldn't they have named it Platinum and Hit... get it? Platinum Hit... hehehe... gets me all the time, but really, why couldn't this International Business have named their June Pay-Per-View something that was a little more universal? Why couldn't they have named this show something... that would relate to the PoP Icons of the world too? Its all good having a show called 'G&G' for the people who will walk away with Championship Gold... but in our game... in our buisness, its all about getting your record to become a Platinum Hit... its all about your singles and albums to become Platinum... so why couldn't we have something along those lines? The Pay-Per-View show itself is closing in... closing in very closly indeed... with under a week to go until it officially takes place! However... no show of this size is going to be a show unless the build-up for it is beyond all the general comprehensions of life... the build up for the team known as 'PCG' as they go into the MASSIVE Tag Team Battle Royal... is beyong all human knowledge and understanding.. Tuesday 25th June 2002..Tomorrows date... and the first step towards the show itself... and by God, the build up for the Battle Royal here is really beginning to kick off... the MAJOR, SUPERSTAR, ICONIC, AMAZING, FANTASIC, HEROIC star Rich Sherwin and his Tag Team partner, Ryan Moore... team up with... those 'Heartbreakers' to take on the double fixture of 'Cowabunga Connection' and 'Misplaced'... what kinds of action will happen here? 'The Heartbreakers' cost 'PCG' their match against the 'Cowabunga Connection' last week on Fusion! Will this be the opportunity for revenge... or will the boys be able to get along with the match at hand before moving onto 'Gold and Glory'... taking the PCW Tag Team Championship Belts back to their beach house in Malibu? Nobody knows for sure... but everyone does know that the contest will be very... very big! Friday 28th June 2002Two days before the show itself... the first 'Pay-Per-View' event since officials at 'Primetime Championship Wrestling' had decided to reset the roster and bring in some new faces. On this fateful night, what are we going to be treated to with regards to all of the teams involved in this weeks big Battle Royal? Rich Sherwin v Jack Valentine v Totally Tubular Tommy v Mike Stone Another contest featuring four teams who have worked their way through the rankings in the past few weeks. Three teams represented here in this bout make their mark in the 8-Man Tag just three nights before. What kinds of action will everyone be looking for tonight? Will anyone here suffer injuries hampering their chances of winning on Sunday Night? Its been said that nobody likes 'PCG'... but will anyone be able to take their game plan to the next level and eliminate them? So... thats the end of it for the week before the show. Thats the end of the week as we look ahead for Sunday 30th where the Tag Team Titles and many other Championships will be decided. 'The Pretty Cool Guys' have been hailed as being one of the favorites to lift the Championship belts... but will they be able to do such a thing? Do they have it in them to go all the way through? Sure, both Sherwin and Moore have been able to score victories against Misplaced and The Price Brothers... even going as far as to end the careers of 'The Price Brothers' with that extremely sweet victory. Tonight however, we won't see the 'Pretty Cool Guys' trying to become 'dudes'... we won't see the boys trying to learn the art of 'Fusion'... we aren't going to even see these two nice guys go out of their way to do something constructive for the community! Instead, we are going to go back to the very place that contained these boys for a few episodes of their past life. Does the name of 'Community General' ring any bells? Well, for the majority of people... I wouldn't be suprised if your responce was, 'Wha? What the hell is Community General? Why the hell is a General Community being brought into the equation?' Heh, for those of you who are answering the question with that very expected responce... 'Community General' is the place of work for the very cool Doctor Mark Sloan (... and no, not 'The Specialist' Mark Sloan from FWA :-D.. ). Infact, that very place was also the very place where 'PCG' first made their real sufficient challenge against the Tag Team Division of 'NTICW'. So... with that very long intro... with that very long piece of information that has probably flown over your head instead of into your head... I leave with the story as it unfolds... outside the walls of 'Community General' Hospital as both Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore walk into the complex... enjoy.. Hello folks... and welcome to yet another edition of the 'Story that never ever gets older'... with your host for tonight, the very cool... the very talented and the very intellectually advanced, Rich Sherwin! Now, many of you people will wonder what the 'Pretty Cool Guys' are doing around a hospital... and alot of you may be wondering why people like to insult the intelligence of us... and yet won't even dare to do anything about a couple of people who are unable to tell a General Hospital from one of those Psycho-Doctor joints. Some people... how they see themselves as being above the law.. above the general census of the world and get some of the very small things wrong. Sometimes, I thank people like my mother and father for teaching me whats right and wrong... and for teaching me the small differences in life... You know... the general changes... like, the difference between Pounds and Pennies.. the difference between Black and White... oh, the difference between Rap and Crap... although it has been said that the line for that final topic is far too close for anyone to really state it as being one or the other. Mike Steel and Daniel Stone... you two boys, I really feel sorry for you... you know, I really feel sorry for the fact that you had to walk into some... place and get some sleeping pills for a sore throat... but really, I don't even want to know the real reason for that Sore Thorat... I'm sure somebody like Madonna would be able to give us all some expert advice on the real meanings behind Sore Throats. Gah... I've been rabbiting on too much about other things and not even mentioning the current situation at hand.... phew, calm down... breathe in.... out... in... out... thats it, I'm all cool and ready to get on with the real matters at hand. The whole scenario begins outside the doors of 'Community General'... it looks like a general hospital, myself and Ryan Moore have decided to come along to this production wearing items of clothing that really hit back at our roots of PoP... and not those awful clothes that I had attempted to wear when it came to infiltraiting that 'Dude Party'. The two of us walked onto a black mat infront of the doors and allow them to automatically slide open... ** Swwwwiiiiish ** It was one of the sounds that I'd always wanted to hear throughout the course of my life. Watching Star Trek had made me deperate to hear the sounds of their automatic doors and what not... just to feel like I was in the future a little. The two of us stepped through and walked straight ahead down the entrance corridor down to the reception desk. Ryan had walked about two paces ahead of me and he reached the desk first... real elementary stuff there for all your brains... two paces ahead, means he finishes at the end of the line before me. Well, regardless of that, he slams his hands down upon the desk, trying to look like one of those White Gangsta boys that think they are actually worth something in the Black community. The Nurse on the other end of the desk looks up from their computer at Ryan.. at first, the PoP Icon is taken back by something. I didn't know what it was until I made my way over to the desk itself and looked at the women. At first, I was like, 'Yeah man... this chick has been able to make Ryan Moore turn away somewhat... shes gotta be a hottie and a half!'... instead, that pre-determined judgement was something that would only exist in the world of all that is freakin' fake! I looked over the desk at the women and smiled sharply... before looking away also... being turned off to the max by her damned ugliness and a spot on the end of her nose. 'Excuse me Miss...', I started to say as she turned her head into my directly and smiled a smile that really should have been on the face of Christina Aguillera and not her. I couldn't bring myself to look at the nurses face again... and so I just looked down the corridor at the front of the building and continued my line from there, '... do they really make cubicles for three here?' I started to laugh a little... all by myself of course. Ryan Moore stepped away from the desk and walked over to the waiting area, shaking his head from side to side at the total lameness of the material that I had used. The ugly... disgusting woman that was seated behind the desk looked at me sternly... I of course, still couldn't bring myself to looking over into her eyes. She began to wonder what I was talking about.... mumbling something about how idiotic I was to say something that had no real meaning. Meh, it was obvious that somebody of her calibre hadn't even been spoken to before... but I couldn't blame anyone for not talking to her... damn, Mike Steel would probably pick up something like this... and so, I decided to do what any other gentlemen would do, show her the meaning of my joke. Closing my eyes, I turned around and looked into her direction... she looked shocked as my arm raised into the air and pointed down at her. 'YOOOOOHOOOOO... are NOT ONLY THE UGLIEST THING I HAVE EVER SEEN.... BUT HOW THE HELL CAN YOU FIT YOUR BODY... YOUR FACE... AND THAT FRICKIN' UGLY SPOT INTO THE SAME ROOM?!?!! HOW THE HELL CAN A CUBICLE FIT THREE PEOPLE?!?!?!', I screamed out at the top of my voice... making sure that every single word that followed from the hole was making sufficient impact upon the ugliness of such a woman. She looked around the room for a few seconds, looking at her friends behind her before getting up from her seat and running off in a crying frenzy. A few seconds later... I opened my eyes... opened them wide and stared at the empty seat that was now infront of me. The friends of this nurse looked at me with stern looks... I could sense that I had done something wrong... but at the same time, I felt the same urge that told me I'd done the right thing in preventing males like myself from having to look at something as ugly as that. Would people like 'Rent-A-Hero' be truely honored by the way that I'd been able to get rid of something like that? I'm thinking that he'd be proud that one of his citizens had done something worthwhile for the community... and by God, everyone had been informed that I wasn't going to do something beneficial... wow, it really is true when it comes to being a hero! 'Rich-A-Hero'... 'Sherwin-A-Hero'... or maybe... 'PrettyCoolGuy-A-Hero'? Either way, I think our friend would be superly happy to see that the lowest member of society had been able to do something... but then, I could have defeated our spandex clad warrior by showing the woman to him... and making him cower by its awful sight. 'Thats the man who insulted me!', I heard a sobbing voice scream... my head turned to the direction of where it was coming from... I saw the ugly woman chugging down the aisle toward me. She was closely followed by Dr Mark Sloan and his Copper son, Steve Sloan. Hmm... scratch everything I said about being a hero and scratch the plan that I made about showing this thing to RAH sometime... if Mark Sloan and Steve Sloan are able to look at and help such a beast... then its not going to be any kind of challenge for 'Renty'. 'Ryan...', I began to call as the trio of people got closer to my location. He got up from his seat in the waiting area and looked over to me with a confused glare in his eye. I walked over to him in a calm fashion... realizing that in all of the movies, the heros in the scene have all the time in the world to run away, '... Mark Sloan and his kid are coming up the aisle... I mean, I totally insulted that ugly thing... and now I think shes come back to eat us for dinner.' 'She has come to eat us... I thought you could only eat a woman?!', he exclaimed, thinking about it in THAT manner. I shock my head in disgust and simply began to run away from my gormless 'Tag Team' partner. He remained still and started to think about what I meant as the trio of personnel finally reached Ryan. Both Mark and Steve Sloan took him by an arm and began to cart the superstar away into the back of the Hospital. I couldn't believe that Ryan was still trying to figure out what I had said... even as he was being carted off into the darkness of the complex. This hospital was slowly beginning to turn into something that had never been seen before. The Dark Messiahs had now lost their little battle against 'The Pretty Cool Guys'... deciding to mock them for their skills... but being countermocked at the way that I know the difference in 'Hosptials' around the whole damn world!... Ok, so maybe I hadn't really been able to countermock in the way I liked to... but still, at least I got the damn place right. What could come next... would it be the heroic action for me? Would I go all the way and rescue my friend from the jaws of both Mark and Steve? Nobody knows... hell, not even I know how I'm going to go around this thing! All I do know though... is that I'm going to need Ryan Moore for both this weeks telecast of Turmoil... and the Pay-Per-View show this coming Sunday! Tune in next time... to see exactly what course of action I will take against... 'Community General'.
