Does The Fabric Of Time & Space Come In Any OTHER Colours?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/07/02 (RP#1 for FF) When it comes to holding grudges, Xavier Kannon is one of the best.... Usually renowned for his quick temper and short fuse, snapping at the smallest thing.. When he takes his time to plan revenge he can be one of the most cold, calculating and downright evil people around... And right now, he has a good reason for revenge and a mind so clouded with repressed rage that the consequences of his actions do not matter to him. After mummifying the Pharaoh in bandages and blood at Fusion, Xavier assumed it was safe that Titu would not be at Turmoil... So he took the opportunity to slur and abuse Egypt even more by calling out any Egyptian from the back, a challenge answered by the Pharaoh’s Bodyguard, Zen Dula.... But must to his anguish, the Pharaoh returned and caused him to lose the match. With his desire for revenge suppressed as he still had to battle Rent-a-Villain, Xavier has yet to release his anger.... Something that could cost the World dearly. When a silently enraged Xavier Kannon, with his naive wife not the woman to stop him, decides to test of some of Scientology’s darkest contraptions in order to gain his revenge not only Pharaoh could pay dearly.... Time, space.... And reality could too.... “It doesn’t really have any special features... You know, like CD Player, Cup Holder, Airbag and Anti-Lock Breaks as standard.... It’s just your run-of-the-mill Time Machine....” With these bizarre words we open up in one of the TOP SECRET Research Labs in the City of Hubbardville, Wisconsin.. The home of Scientology where amongst the walls of computers, animal cages, and tanks full of L Ron Hubbard clones maturing we see the couple of Xavier Kannon and his wife/valet Ellie conversing with one of the Scientists who carry out the clandestine... And most likely, highly illegal Scientological experiments. All 3 of them are gazing at a strange contraption that best resembles a Purple and Black moving and flashing hole in the lab’s floor that seems to have a gravimetric force as all the small items in the room and the dust gets sucked into it. Xavier : “Well, I was hoping for an In-Time/Space Continuum Movie but I’m sure it will suffice for the task at hand.... Are the co-ordinates set?” Beside him, Ellie~! has dressed for the occasion in her best Scientist’s outfit of White lab-coat and Black rimmed glasses, her hair tied up and a selection f pens in the coat pocket... She is looking at the hole with her pale Blue eyes wide in excitement. Eleanor : “Co......... Ordinates......... Set?” The Scientist is briefly interrupted by a fellow co-worker who briefs him on the status of their Biological Warfare department before returning to Xavier and Ellie~! nodding at their question. Scientist : “Yes... It is all ready for you to use Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon, it as been set to Ancient Egypt, the Rammesses the Third era, the probes and trained talking reconnaissance Monkeys have been sent and confirm the location and place on the time line.” The Scientologist’s face breaks into a smug grin, excited at the prospect of what ever it is he has planned in Ancient Egypt, the place his arch nemesis the Pharaoh believes is home... The enthusiastic face of Eleanor then quickly changes as, for a change, she has a thought... Eleanor : “Um.... I, like, have a question...” She even raises her hand as she speaks and the 2 men turn to her, gesturing with her expression for her to share he inquiry with them. Eleanor : “What if I, like, you know... Meet myself in the past and then there would be, like, SO 2 of us and I might explode or something... I’ve heard that happens...” As she speaks, the Scientist looks to Kannon who’s expression reassures him that this is a regular Ellie~! thing.... He puts his arm around his wife’s shoulder to assure her things will be fine. Xavier : “Fear not my little Shag Squirrel, we are going to Ancient Egypt, so unless they have Mid-Nineties Shopping Malls there, I think you’ll be safe... Ready?” The now calmed face of Eleanor looks down at the hole, still not quite happy with something. Eleanor : “Does it come in any other colours?” The Scientist looks at her with a blank expression on his face so blank it is almost caving in on itself through lack of any expression at all as Eleanor points to the Purple and Black vortex where crackles of electricity flicker. Eleanor : “I mean.. Black hasn’t been Black for, like 3 years now... And the Purple just doesn’t really scream “TIME TRAVEL” to me.... Do you have it in Silver and Pink?” The Scientist is lost for words as Xavier steps in to speed up proceedings, impatient as he runs through his plans for the foray into the past in his mind. Xavier : “I’m afraid Time and Space portals don’t come in a choice of size or colours my little Fuck Ferret.... So can we get on with it now?” She sighs and shrugs, still not comfortable with the colour but putting it to the back of her mind as Kannon turns to the Scientist. Xavier : “We shouldn’t be long... The undoubted wisdom of Hubbard doesn’t usually take long to sink in...” He then crosses his arms and jumps into the hole of raw energy on the lab’s floor before Ellie~! leans over and looks into it, muttering to herself under her Preppy breath. Eleanor : “Well.... Here SO goes, like, nothing!” The prep then tentatively steps into the hole, dipping her toe into it nervously. Eleanor : “Hehe... That feels funn-YAAAAAAAAAH!” She is then sucked down into the hole and as we follow her in we see her falling through a huge Black and Purple tunnel, random flashes of light happening all around her as pure, raw, energy flows around the perimeter of the time vortex. “HOW ARE YOU FINDING THE TRIP MY LITTLE POON PUPPY!?!?!” Below her we see Kannon falling, looking up at his wife as they are propelled back into the past using the state of the art device from the Research Labs of Hubbardville. The Prep shrugs, seemingly not overly enthused at rocketing through time and space itself. Eleanor : “It’s not bad I suppose...” There is then a sudden flash of blinding light and as the White fades out into a picture we see 2 sets of legs sticking out of a sand dune. “Okay... Let’s remember that upright in the Time Tunnel is upside down in reality...” We then see Xavier pulling his head out of the sand and shaking the grains out of his long Red hair and from his clothes before he grabs Ellie~!’s feet and pulls her out so she can spit a mouthful of sand, no looking too happy about the landing. Eleanor : “I know it’s, like, SO rude to spit but I was NOT swallowing that! It tasted all icky and felt funny...” In his Scientological mind, Kannon tries his hardest to suppress the joke his subconscious is screaming to say... Just managing to resist blurting it out.... Eleanor shakes her hair then looks around them... Looking horrified. Eleanor : “IT... LIKE..... SHRUNK US!!!!” Her pale Blue eyes are wide open as she looks out over the vast expanse of sand all around them, the Pyramids of Giza clearly visible to their east.... Xavier looks puzzled by his wife’s outburst. Xavier : “Huh?” She points to the Pyramids. Eleanor : “THOSE SAND CASTLES ARE HUGE!!!” The Scientologist’s Green eyes roll up into his head at his naive valet’s words as he helps her up to her feet and reassures her. Xavier : “Fear not my little Shag Squirrel... Those are the Ancient Pyramids of Giza... We aren’t small, they are just big.” His words calm her a little but she still seems curious. Eleanor : “How big?” Xavier : “REALLY big!” The Prep is still not convinced as she looks down at the awe inspiring examples of masonry. Eleanor : “Bigger than a house?” Xavier : “Yes.” Eleanor : “Bigger than TWO houses?” Xavier : “Yes.” Eleanor : “Bigger than the Earth?” Xavier : “No Ellie~!.... Not bigger than the Earth.” The Preppy valet of the Scientologist folds her arms and sneers at the giant structures. Eleanor : “So they’re not really THAT big then are they?” The Gold Patron Meritorious shakes his head and decides they had better get on with their task, taking his unimpressed wife’s arm and leading her down the sand dune as the shot starts to fade out. Eleanor : “So.. What are we, like, DOING here anyway? Do we need a new Rug for the hall?” We see the couple disappear down the dune and into the sun as we finally hit Black. “No.... We’re hear to convert Rammesses to Scientology thus sending reality and much of the Earth’s existence crumbling all around us.” “Isn’t that kinda.... Like, dangerous?” “Pharaoh started it....” To Be Concluded....
