'Keep Primetime Championship Wrestling PURE'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: now ::Our scene opens up out the front of the PCW Headquarters, Atlantic City, the place where it all happens. The scene seems peaceful enough, until we zoom out to see a bunch of people gathered round a large white stage out the front. Our camera fades into the situation that is gathering down below.:: 'We won't stand for it anymore! We want PCW to please explain why they, of all the people in this nation, are supporting the Asian Invasion. I watched it happen in Australia, the Asians slipped into the country unnoticed and then the next thing you know they have there own restaurants! I try to stop it for the love of my country and everyone calls me a racist!' ::The speaker is, of course, controversial Australian politician, Pauline Hanson. Leader of the One Nation party and believer of the theory that 'they should all be f*ckin' shot.' She speaks through her megaphone, her temper flaring like her bright red hair.:: Pauline: Now you people here in America have a chance to learn from our mistakes, a chance to stop the Asian Invasion of America! ::The crowd of racists, Nazis and KKK members alike cheer:: Pauline: And it all starts here, for no other place in your entire country can sway the public's opinion like Primetime Championship Wrestling. So I say, that the time is now! ::Against the crowd, or rather mob, cheer their approval.:: Pauline: So right here, right now, we start the bid. The bid for two new laws to be introduced into the American constitution. 'Asians go home'... ::The crowd cheers again.:: Pauline: And 'No handouts for Abos!' ::The crowd go to cheer but stop, confused by the last word.:: 'Uhh, I think what Pauline meant to say was 'No handouts for Abbos!' ' ::The crowd cheers as Corey Williams enters the camera shot and gets onto the megaphone, whilst Pauline is looking a little confused herself.:: Corey: Look, Pauline is right, we cannot allow these slanty-eyed bastards to take over what it rightfully ours. These Asians are sly too, the other day I was looking through the PCW website and I came across a wrestler named Alexander Miyagawa! That's right, Isawa Miyagawa has changed his name to try and pass off as an honest, decent American! ::There is a reaction of shock from the crowd and then some chatter between themselves.:: Corey: Exactly! It's disgusting! And for the life of me I couldn't understand why he'd do it, that is until I heard about PURE. PURE is a new fed in which all Asian wrestlers will be forced to wrestle OTHER Japanese wrestlers 24 hours a day, 7 days a year, in short, it will be hell. No charisma, no promos, just two nips going at it. And Miyagawa, one of the smarter nips but a nip nonetheless, saw it coming and tried to ride under the radar of the PCW officials. ::The crowd begin to boo Miyagawa:: Corey: But we, being the Americans that we are, were too smart for Miyagawa's dumb tricks! We saw him for what he truly was! ::The crowd cheers:: Corey: Nothing but a wanna-be gook! ::Crowd cheers again:: Corey: But then why isn't he in PURE right now? Why hasn't he been taken from the supposedly Asian-free environment of PCW? ::There are again some low murmurs from the crowd, as they try and answer Corey's question.:: Corey: I'll tell you why! Because he's best friends with Jeremy Howard, who just happens to be best friends with Duke Williams, that's why! Duke Williams used, no ABUSED his presidential privilege to keep his boyfriend's friend in PCW. And it is a crime, a crime committed by Williams, Howard and Miyagawa himself, a crime committed against the fans of PCW! ::The crowd boo, our camera spins around to get a few shots of the people, with protest signs like 'Keep PCW PURE' and 'Miyagawa Phone Home'.:: Corey: That is why I speak for ALL of my PCW fans when I say that we request, no DEMAND, that Duke Williams send Isawa Miyagawa to PURE.... that is of course, AFTER I beat his ass silly tonight at Turmoil. And furthermore, we DEMAND that guys with dicks less than three inches long no longer be allowed into PCW! ::The crowd go wild, we see protesters burning pictures of Miyagawa.:: Corey: And we won't take no for answer! So everybody say it with me! Hell no, Asians must go! Hell no, Asians must go! Hell no, Asians must go! Crowd (with Corey): Hell no, Asians must go! Hell no, Asians must go! Hell no, Asians must go! ::The crowd begin to flood the gates of PCW headquarters, thirsting for blood due to Corey's speech. Corey stands back on the platform with Pauline, both have satisfied smiles on their faces.:: Corey: ….People are such idiots. ::Our scene fades out.:: !The End!
