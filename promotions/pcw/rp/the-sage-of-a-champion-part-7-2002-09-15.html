The Sage Of A Champion(Part 7)

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-15-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 7” Chapter 4: A Sidetracking Sent Straight From HellDay 6: The Insane One Has Been Chosen ::The scene picks up the afternoon after BladeD’s day of bowling. He was going to go about the day as normal because he won’t go back to any kind of work till tomorrow, but the only problem is that earlier in the day he received a phone call. This phone call was from Charles Banks himself. After that phone call came a phone call from General Powers, so basically BladeD had to go to a meeting. Banks wants BladeD to forget about all of this BattlePowers stuff and General P. has problems of his own. BladeD is basically caught in between the middle of all these problems.:: ::BladeD went from a day of relaxation, to a day of stress. It is amazing how he lives this way, but that is how he has been living since he was 17. He lives a life a radicals, day in and day out. Only this time it is something more then normal. He hasn’t been put in a positive yet where he could cost millions of people their lives… Yet only time will tell us what dangers are ahead:: ::The scene picks up in the NMA Of NYC.:: General Powers: BladeD. I know you are in a battle for BattlePowers… but now we are in some big trouble. BladeD: WTF now? General P: You see BladeD. You know that you are one of the most valuable men in the country. BladeD: Yes…. Go on. General P: Well, that is where the problem lies. See. We can’t go on like this…. You being the valuable person you are should have a lot more respect towards Mr. Charles Banks. BladeD: What are you talking about respect? I give him respect… General P: Apparently not or we wouldn’t be having you two “fighting” over BattlePowers… How about this? Either you drop this crap, become Co-CEO or President, whatever it may be, and go on with life…. This is your General commanding you. BladeD: What if I refuse?General P: BladeD!!!! Have you lost your mind?!?! I am commanding you!! How about this? Either you listen to me or!! Or!! Or!! BladeD: Or what General? General P: Or I will make sure you are demoted all the way down to enlisted and never rise back up ever again! That is what! BladeD: WTF? General P: WTF is right! So either you listen to me or you will be saying “WTF” all the way down to enlisted. BladeD: That is blackmail!! General P: That! Sir! Is the only thing I can do! ::BladeD turns his chair around and for a while there is a huge silence… General Powers’ isn’t filling out any paper work or anything… They just have their backs turned to each other wondering what to do about all of this. BladeD’s is about to make the difference between lives. Because if he accepts, then millions of lives will be put in danger, but if he doesn’t accept the infantryman himself will see the last of his days of a man in power… Suddenly, out of nowhere, the moment of silence is broken. Only BladeD or the General doesn’t break it. It is broken by a knock on the door:: General P: -- shouting in a loud and angry voice -- Come in! ::The door slowly, but surely opens… BladeD begins to feel weird because it is taking so long for the guy to open the door… BladeD first thought is that it is just some kind old man who isn’t able to move fast, but once the door is half-way open and the man walks through BladeD’s jaw drops to the fall and not a word comes out of his mouth… General asks BladeD who it is and gets no answer.:: Unknown Person: -- in a low, yet gentle voice -- Ah.. Hello BladeD. I am guessing the General is behind you. ::General Powers slowly turns around and as he is turning around he has a weird look on his face, a face of someone not believing something, a face of confusion, a face of disapproval. Seconds later the General finally gets all the way around and once he gets all the way around he looks up and he is in complete shock and surprise. He now has the same face on has BladeD, yet his is a little different because he has more of a look of fear. General Powers rubs his eyes:: General Powers: I don’t believe. This can’t be. There is no possible way Athens Mir is standing in front of me. Athens Mir: Yes. It is me you speak of and yes I am standing in front of you as we speak. General P: But that can’t be… It just can’t be… you di…. ::Athens interrupted him:: Athens: You two thought I had died, but actually I had never died in the first place. Back in 2000 when you two thought the war was over and everyone had died… that was not to be true…. I had no pulse, but that was just for a short while…. When I pass out… most of the time I go into a coma and my body half way shuts down leaving me without a pulse… When my body was found I had a pulse at the time, so I was rushed to the hospital. I was in a coma, so I had no idea what was going on, but I was told that I was taken care of while in this coma and my family was told that I was most likely make it, it would just take a year or two before I would come too. BladeD: So I am guessing sometime this year you came too. Athens: Well you could sort of say that… Right before this year started I came too… Like December the 20th or something of that sort. It has taken me this long to learn how to speak and walk and all that stuff again, but here I am. General P: So what are you going to do? Try to kill us? Athens: That is what you would think, since we were on opposing sides, but do you remember much about that war general? General P: Yes, Mir. I remember everything about that war. Athens: Well see. I wasn’t against you actually… I was on there side just for the main purpose of backstabbing. BladeD: That’s what it was! General P: What BladeD? BladeD: Back during that war… well before it had started. There was a rumor going around. Everyone was saying not to shot Mir because he wasn’t against us. He was for us… But the story was that a lot of the guys on the field liked Mir and that Mir was to be killed. We had two groups. One group going around saying that he shouldn’t be killed because he is really on our side… One group was going around saying that he was against us and should be killed. General P: Interesting…. Keep going. BladeD: The true story I had heard was this… and Mir after I am done… correct me if I am wrong. I was told that Mir had plans of going in and doing a job similar to something I would do. He was going in as a spy for our team. He was going to get “The Book Of Violent Ray” which if you guys remember “Violent Ray” was a machine similar to the one of “Metal Armour” only this one was much powerful. I was told that you(pointing to Mir) were going to fight this war like normal and uses the skills you have to get a medal of honor and stuff like that, so you could basically have the power to go around wherever you wanted, whenever you wanted, so that you could retrieve this book and take the machine… using it against them… I don’t know much after that. :: The scene cuts from there. The story is really beginning to pick up and it is one that you probably would one to get a little more of, so be patient and be ready for the next episode in this sage of a champion that is about to take a bigger twist then just Athens Mir. Stay tuned!!::
