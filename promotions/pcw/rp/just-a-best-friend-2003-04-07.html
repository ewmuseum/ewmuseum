Just a Best Friend?

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/03 The sun starts to set as the little hand on the clock hits six. All is quiet in the house, it seems as though everyone had nothing to say. But I am sure everyone had plenty on their mind that they wanted to say, they only problem is they didn’t know how to express it. Christa and Alex were gone, which left Chris, Evan, and I the only members of the Windsor-Storm household. Ding Dong Our ears perked up at the sound of the doorbell as it echoed throughout the house. Just as a child would Evan ran to the door in anticipation of finding out who was there standing on the other side. Surely he must have had hope that he would swing the door open and see Christa and Alex on the other side, I had the same hope but the figure seemed much too tall to belong to a child so the little hope that I had diminished to nothing as I walked up beside Evan and pulled the door open before he could. “About time you opened the door.” The voice on the other side said in the tone of my mother’s usual pessimistic voice. Surely enough it was her who came strolling through the front door, handing me her black fur coat as if I were her slave. “What are you…” I began to question before being cut off by her as I expected I would be since she vocally insisted that she be the one who asks the question and give the demands while I just stand there and wait for her to provide me with the information that I needed. “Is it wrong for your only mother to pay you a visit once in awhile? I thought I taught you better than to ask questions.” She answered my question with a scold as if I still lived under her roof and was supposed to abide by her rules seeing as though she was my mother and that’s what all so called good daughters do. “Who was it?” Chris questioned as he approached it, obviously not noticing my mother whom was standing beside me, but before I could answer the question for him my mother was already shooting him dirty look and ready to go off on him now. “What is he doing here?” She shouted out in disgust, obviously not aware of the fact that Chris and I had lived together for quite awhile now. She looked over at me with her intimidating blue orbs which glowed with anger, demanding an answer. “He lives with here with me and Evan, mom.” I answered casually, trying to hint to her that her beliefs no longer meant anything to me now that I was on my own. Inside my mind I hoped that she wouldn’t ask where Christa and Alex were just incase she hadn’t know that they were once again in Allison’s custody. “Evan? You mean you’re living with not one, but two men!” She screamed out in even more disgust than before. I nearly had to chuckle to myself as I had forgotten that I hadn’t introduced Evan to her. But then again why would I ever want to introduce a child to such a witch, I had enough trouble bringing Christa and Alex around her at Brandon’s funeral and they were her own grandkids, never mind a child that wasn’t of the Windsor blood at all. “No mom!” I shouted out in defense. “Evan is the child Chris and I adopted.” “And where is this kid?” She questioned, not noticing that Evan whom was beside Chris seemingly already afraid of this mysterious woman whom he knew nothing about. “Hi.” Evan answered in a quiet for me, taking a step forward to present himself. Chris stood behind him ready to strike back verbally should my mom be ready to toss insults in his direction the way she tended to do with everyone. It was quite scary how we turned out to be quite opposites, yet I thanked god everyday that I never turned out to be like her. “Anyways.” she started up, paying little attention to Evan “Where is this new husband of yours?” As those words crawled into my ears I nearly fell backwards. I couldn’t believe that she knew about Gaz and me. I thought she was anti-wrestling in all way shape and form. Immediately my mind began to run rampant with what to say in response to her, without causing a big fight here in front of Evan and Chris seeing as though I was as sick of hearing about the accidental bonding of marriage between Gaz and me as they were. ...Oh my god, how did she find out!... ...Maybe some neighbors told her... …Or maybe she saw the actual wedding… … Either way, I’m screwed now!... …Think quick Tessa!... “He’s uhhh not here.” I said, tripping over my words as I tried to convince myself that as long as she didn’t ask to meet him I’d be okay, but what if she did ask? “You mean you have this clown here and not your very own husband?” She questioned, showing her disappointment in me though the tone in her voice. “Excuse me, but this clown happens to be my best friend who has been here for me a lot more than you ever have.” I said, careful to avoid telling her that Chris and I loved each other and would have gotten married had such an accident not occurred. “Well if that’s the way you see it than I won’t miss you a happy marriage!” She argued back in a huff, before stomping towards the door which she came in. Surely enough as I had predicated in my mind she opened the door and left without a goodbye which in a way was a sigh of relief for me considering I knew that if she had stayed around any longer her and I would have gotten into an argument, but surely enough there if she wasn’t too upset about my last comment there was someone else who was, and that someone was Chris. “Just a best friend now, am I?” Chris questioned with a glare that stated he quite obviously was not too happy with that statement. “Chris you know what…” “Don’t even start; I don’t want to hear it.” Chris said, before turning and walking away from without saying another word. Evan simply looked at me not sure of what to make of the situation. Being only four years old there wasn’t much he could suggest. I sighed and headed the opposite way up the stairs towards my room. Perhaps if I thought about the opportunities I had on both Tuesday and Friday to become number one contender for two separate titles things would things would seem less stressful. Much less stressful I hoped.
