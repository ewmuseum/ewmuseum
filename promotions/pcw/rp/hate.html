Date: RACIST? YOU CAN SAY THAT I hate them all… They make me see in shades of red. Infuriation beyond my own control drives me to fight them every day. Every day that is, until I can force my own sense of justice down their throats. Look at them. Look at you. Look at me. Compare… How can you look to one another with out falling ill? Ill with contempt for your pathetic peers, forcing you to the point of uncontrollable discharge of food yet digested. How can you look at yourself, and go on? You pathetic infidel. How can you stare me in the eyes, and think as an equal? The answer, assuming you were wrong in each case, is that you can’t. Not legitimately, not with a sense of universal truth to fend off bias. You are all sickening, but cope with one another. You are all disgusting in your own eyes, and you know it… But, your reflection keeps your secret when you lie. Between you and him/her. And they, ‘they’ being what I shall refer to you and them as, are fit only to be my slaves. My subordinates, forever under my iron fist. They deserve to be my slaves, just like they had belonged to someone so long ago… Picking cotton. I hate them, with their big mouths, and stupid white jokes. Everyone knows they are just trying to get out of the fields. I hate those big nosed cheapskates who snobbishly walk their paths with their big nose held high. They have those noses for a reason, as some say. The punching bags of God are the most efficient gas inhalers alive. I hate them. I hate how they wear towels on their head, babbling like a bunch of ignoramuses, wiping their asses with their left hand… Then they share snack crackers, and other big dish buffet items. Let them eat sand, and blow themselves to hell. Of course, they are no worse that those black skeletons who starve to the point of swelling. They like to shower in cattle piss. Those sand dwellers should give em some shit crackers, then they could unite. Easier to pick off that way. I truly despise the slit eyed fools who can’t even control their population. Killing off offspring… At this rate, they might as well kill themselves, too. Anyone would tell you that overpopulation is a good excuse to spread the domain out. Yet they fight to keep what they have. I can’t understand the mindset of these intruders, like ones who swim across the Rio Grande to solve their problems. Why not stay and solve your own? They come anyway. With them, there comes simpletons who deem it possible to make it across a body of water by tire. I hate them. Or, how about those fags from that faggot land Europe… If the Atlantic wasn’t so big, they’d be wetbacks, too. And let’s not forget to hate the fags that make faggot land more possible… Tea is to be laced with cyanide. There’s a solution. They used to know about dealing out the solution… Like when the German Nazi’s shoved the Jews into ovens… Or when that society rubbed off on the ‘Land of Opportunity’ with neo Nazi’s, who join the ranks of the white hooded knights that put all the niggers where they should be. In a tree, hanging. It’s unfortunate though, those stupid dogs aren’t smart enough to throw themselves into the fire as well. They deserve death equally. If it were my choice? And it will be… I’d choose the whites to slaughter them all, as they are the most efficient killers at the moment. The Niggers. The Jews. The Wetbacks. The Waps. The SandNiggers. The Chinks… All of them. And the Whites who did the killing? I’d take them out too. White’s aren’t above the rest. They are just another pack of dogs. But, in all reality, I’d rather use these self murdering fools… You. You. And You. …for work. Help them build my empire. In spite for them. I hate them all, and you all. Why must I spit in your face? Can you not see that what I hate is THE ENTIRE HUMAN RACE? I am a Goblin. A last of my kind. I was born to take over this Earth, and make it my own. Goblin’s were destroyed… a lost cause. I will be their last hope. I am a Goblin, and I am superior to you. I am a Goblin, and I will prove it one day. I am a Goblin, and I HATE THE HUMAN RACE. There is one place for you, and that is under my control! I am a Goblin, and I HATE EVERYONE OF YOU. I am a Goblin, AND I HAVE ALWAYS HATED EVERYONE OF YOU HUMANS. ALWAYS. I HAVE ALWAYS HATED YOU HUMANS …until her. Watching her for the first time not to long ago, I was deeply touched… Watching her, I was caught. Caught is something I couldn’t understand. But, she was in the arms of another man. Things… Complicated to say the least. Then she vanished. I knew she’d be back. There would be someone for me, as fate told me so. And she did come back. But, she fell into the arms of the same man. Watching her in the arms of another, I was in love with her. A human. Watching her in his arms, I was in love. She had to be mine. Watching her, forever out of my reach, I was in love with her. But, how? Watching her exist without me, I was in love… He was taken out of the picture. Watching her all alone… IWasInLoveWithWhat WasRightfully MineForTheTaking I broke the rules a little, but what rules are there anyway? I didn’t see them either. But, someone must have if they saw it fit to take her away from me. Pendragon, you will give her back. I will destroy you. Why? Because you are a human? That’s what you might think, after seeing the world from my eyes, knowing I would hate you even if you were my ‘Friend.’ And I do hate you. As I had when you first arrived. As I had when you first battled me. I hated you the day you were born, for you were born human. But, is it that hate that drives me to destroy you now? No. You are what stands between the human I love… The human I possess. Own. And keep. She is mine, and I must take her. I will destroy you on the basis of being the man stopping me from being with the one I LOVE. Love is what drives me. You cannot beat the power of love. You cannot beat the power of love.
