Hymns of The Fallen [2]: ...And Longevity For None...

3 October 2002

Date: 10-3-02 The nu winds were calling her, as the respirator began to pump inward and outward of her breathing state. Only had it been a few days ago that her very own life had been attempted to be cut off; taken away. The aquatic onslaught outside of her window had only begun, the deep darkness entrancing the moon's glow to be more and more unlikely to be seen. Lightning clapped in the darkness, opening a skew of light, before retracting in only a second. The slight movement of the door to the room slowly shifted her view from the window, as the figure by the door made their way methodically towards her bed. Their clothing of the dark, all black from head to toe, caused her eyes to widened. They were the souls, those locked and transfixed upon doing something to make their case in the world. All of those foes, of Cordozer Stevenson. The rain began to fall harder, smacking against the glass window like automatic bullets plunging through glass and objects. Maybe it was something brought up by the doctors to allow her to have some form of visitation, but during the nighttime was an exception, only close blood relatives could come in. Vying for position, the three of them, two men and one woman filled the sections around Botan, silently darting their eyes towards her. The men, wrapped up in a hugging embrace by their coats and leather black pants had their eyes shrouded by wrapping Oakley sunglasses. The woman's pierced tongue ran along her lips as her hair ran past her shoulders. Botan's voice trilled in the room, held back by white sheets tucked under the bed and the blood circulator imbedded deep within her left wrist. She shrieked, before the force of the woman kept her silent. 'Hush...child.' she muttered, before Ryoko nodded to her and kept her silence. The two men opened up their trenches, pulling out surgical equipment and lying them between her angles. She eyed their carefully, before shuddering in what the three of them might do with them. Baldie took one appliance by the edge, swinging the small item like a switchblade before eyeing where he would make his incision. The woman on the side of him stopped the movement, before removing Botan's gag. 'She atleast has the right to speak.' 'You've got that right, what do you want?' Botan's fierce voice in their opinion had been a slight reprieve from the shrieking sounds and pleading of their previous encounters. She slighty began to think, whether to plead for her life or not. She chose the latter, pushing her body weight upward and began to sit in a childhood fashion, fascinated by the figures in front of her. 'Inquisitive, aren't you?' the tremble before speaking to them had now vanished, as she began to understand some of their reasoning. 'Yes, I am.' the vixen replied softly. 'Well, myself am Hitomi, and these are Yitu and Iko.' the woman on her right replied. Yitu and Iko ceremoniously bowed with Botan raising an eye towards the both of them. She shook her head, before returning her attention to the one who wasn't mute.'Okay, that explains much.' she relpied to Hitomi, focusing mainly on her and her alone. The other two, she thought to herself, weren't the deciding factors in whether she'd be making it out of this hospital room in two days. 'Why do you want me anyway?' 'Actually, we don't want you, you would want us.' Hitomi told her sternly. Botan taken a bit back by her statement, rolls her eyes back, before placing her free arm under her chin. 'I'm not -' 'We know.' Iko slightly responded, as a clash of lightning suddenly shrieked against the poor and dismal clouds above. 'But we want you to know that even your brother cares, even enough if it means his life in doing so.' 'Cordozer...' Botan slowly muttered, looking down at herself, pitying that she got him into all of this, every single moment that she did something, his name was brought out as the troublesome one. 'Why are you here in the first place, have you other matters to attend towards? I mean, there are a lot of things that you can be doing other that trying to talk to me!' 'Hitomi -' 'Silence.' the fierceful woman anticipated the calling of her name by Iko, who slowly drew his head back in defeat. He couldn't stand up to this woman, no matter his brute strength and almost perfect capability of breaking her in two. 'We want your brother because of only one thing. We want him dead.' Botan's pupils widened for the first time, slighty disturbed that she would find amazement in someone else wanting to die at the very moment, but in all logic, it could have been handled differently. She could have been held hostage by her throat, and the small woman would stick a dagger some four inches thick and nine inches long into her chest cavity, killing her. A peculiar thought rose in her mind as she raised the headboard on her bed to a degree, why would they want Cordozer dead? She mused, before Hitomi grabbed her by the ankle, shaking her out of the mystic trance. 'If you're asking me why does we want your friend dead, ' Hitomi began. 'its simply because of this - the winding road to salvation has been broken, by the sinner, and rebuilded by the saint. If he doesn't contact you in the next forty-eight hours, then we will. ' She locked her nails deep into the ankle, causing blood to leak through the bed sheets. Botan's tiny gasp in pain were lurid, almost mouse like as she cringed back, holding the pillow to her face. Hitomi ran her fingers upward, before pulling away from her legs and brought a dagger from out of her breast pocket. Ryoko began to scream, before the sharp edge of the blade became close to meeting her face. She tried to blink, hoping that would rid some fustration from the blade being so close, but it didn't help matters as it brought her closer and closer to permanate scarring. Hitomi flipped the knife back into her breast pocket, a smirk around her black lips as the trio began to make their way out of the room, clutching the mind of Ryoko Yato into a tight and slow trance of what next. She removed the bed sheets to examine her ankle, minuscule holes showing where Hitomi got to her, making her almost cry at the wounds. 'Fuck!' she cursed loudly, holding back her tears from dropping down her caramel cheeks. Placing her hands on her face, she sobbed, wiping the small packets of sweat away. Looking down at her ankle once more, Botan only placed her hand upon it, feeling the missing pieces of skin. The slow caress only makes the pain run through her more and more, until she stops. Her eyes closed, as she hit the pillow to sleep, and dream that this was only a nightmare. To dream that none of it actually happened. She wished that were the truth, that those beings Hitomi, Iko, and Yitu didn't show up that night. But the truth made it all too worse, hard for her to even bare. Slowly whisked away to sleep tonight, sweet dreams of that night's fright. Only latches in her mind again and again, like a silent promise, drifted into the wind. [- = / = -] 'Morning.' I muttered to myself, as I continued to relish in the lifestyle of a almost certain god. Once again, I had done the deed, and now, it was time to seek some vaild help out of the situation.. '...shed so many tears...' Tupac playing out of the stereo as I continued to lay my head back, silently nodding my head and mouthing the words. Yet, its Thursday, why am I listening to Tupac? Maybe, for the slightest reason of the doubt, music is my gift from God. Wrestling is just an added necessity just to feed my ever growing ego. I could just have my ego as a pet and feed it wins and losses, shrinking it or making it ever more bigger. But, I'm not, I can not and will not. That's more the PCW way, anyhow. A man cannot be that way all of the time. Swinging my head upward, a few strands of my hair began to cover my face, the tipped red ends a blocking path of my sight. In the shallow light that filled the apartment, one thing stood out for certain, that I had the chance to make good on my promise. A diamond in the rough, so to speak. Maybe its the odd reality that we'll win, Team PURE over PCW, but I fairly doubt that'll be the exact truth. When the smokes clears, and the third hand has met the mat, I bet you that only one man, from one team will be standing high. The estranged thoughts caught up in my mind, those of what happened that fair night when I crashed their party, built it in and broke it down. And yet, it didn't satisfy me, not one damn bit did it hold my heart to a specific reigion where I was the king and they were my jesters, MY entertainment. I guess you can't have everything to your advantage, everything cannot be the very thing that helps you attain victory. I thought of myself as a Microphone Fiend, always craving that attention, like heroine. I needed a fix, that extreme fix. Its that same fix that'll I'll get Sunday night at Red October, in my homeland. That fix, in the styles of PCW is known as anything goes. No rules. My playground. Sunday night, we step into that playground exempting all of those who think they can do their damage, just the truth is brought out on that night. That easily can be said for every single member of the PCW tandem, the fifth man may not be someone I'll find as a challenge. But, the element of surprise is everfading, as PCW has done what its done as always, take a platform, and never use it. Fiend for the style, the exact fluid making you become bigger. I call them silly rabbits, following down that trail that leads to the noose around their necks. Its my lethal weapon, when I'm the fiend. The suspense of every single word built up from the lands of PCW is only making me anticipate it more...more and more. Until I stop, and then think how I'll knock them off the stage. How PURE will knock PCW off of their stage. [- = / = -] The days leading up towards the event have been press relations filled, because everyone wants a piece of the main draw in the 'land of the Rising Sun.' The fanfare that the fans give us are different, usually because we are the main attraction over here. Now with PCW here, we get more coverage, seeing how their performance affects the draw. Reporter:'Will it be a classic?' A reporter asked me as I ride the tour bus from Osaka to Yokohama. Its a promotion junket, from each large city to another, until the day of the 6th arrives. 'A classic? It might be, but was Hogan vs. Rock a classic? Not really, but the animosity from the people supporting those in the ring made it a classic. Their skills in the ring made it only a sub-par affair. Now, on the matter of Inphino Blitz and The End, is that a classic? Yes, by all means it is.' I told her that, the rain continued to pour down from the window sil as I continued reminiscing, of the past nights of glory, and all of those broken down into harsh memoirs. Those point breaking thoughts shivered my mind, as the cloudy grays hover about my head. I remember the first time I tasted victory, and how the local authorities happened to interject in the action. A quarrel somehow between myself on the streets of Toyko.'Fusuko!' I hollered his name out in vain, cursing at my wounds from the previous statements. I was bleeding from the mouth, an almost instant urge to take that and place it on Fusuko was my main objective. Down on this road, there were no stops, just rushing cars and people on bicycles, pumping their legs to get towards their destination. I chased after the boy, his spikey raven hair froliced in the wind, as sweat came down his face, so did mine. 'Come here scoundrel! Bleed my blood!' Rambling, he took the next corner, side-swiping away from my eyesight, causing me to second guess. Did he go this way, or the next way - I couldn't tell. His voice had been long gone, before I heard him squirming, screaming for help. His foot was caught in metal fencing, and his eyes as black as they were, were small and timid. In his appearence you wouldn't be able to tell me that he was able to throw a decent punch, but he surprised people. Looking at him, he begged for me to let him go. 'Stop, stop!' he cried. He released himself, pushing off the fence before starting to run once more, this time without abandon. Throwing my body to the wind, I leaped upward, taking hold of the fence and using upper body strength to climb over it. Silently, the small forces in my brain continued to plea that I beat Fusuko within an inch of his life. We ran down Ajawa Street, motoring down past some biker gang clad in a beat up Kahawasaki with silver trim and a black base color. The other two member didn't pay attention to the two of us, but their leader laced his eyes towards the both of us. Disrespect came at a hefty price then, you were beat and then tagged if you did so. He gunned his bike, with the two lackeys following suit. It took maybe a few seconds once they caught up to us. The main biker yelled, 'You've screwed up now kid!' I fought back a loss of breath, tossing the middle finger his way. 'Snot nosed punk!' he roared authoritatively. He revved up once more, before forcing himself onto the sidewalk, speeding ahead of Fusuko. 'What are you doing idiot?' I questioned the biker, before ducking billy club shots from the first lackey. He was scrawny, about 5'6', 140 pounds with golden locks and attractive face. Pretty boy joked, 'You know who youse dealin' wit?' 'Dumb@sses.' I concluded. He was taken back, before running towards me with a scream in his face. I sidestepped him, causing him to stumble ahead of my original place. He grunted, before turning his back and rushing forward. A swift kick to his sternum sent him back, before a fist to the face sent him back to the floor, covering his face. Pretty Boy was crying, Lug Head was only a few steps behind me, wanting to take the first swing. I ducked under his first punch, before kicking him in the back, bending him over. He turned back, swinging wildly, hoping he'd catch something. Nothing but air. 'Dammit!' he cursed, another punch was ducked by me, before I kicked him swiftly three times in the chest, before a roundhouse to the face knocked him on his butt. He was seeing stars. Fusuko came up behind me, and he also caught my attention, a strong backhand knocked him down, out for the count. That was my victory - at age ten, I was probably a nice kid from first glance, but it was only a ploy, just something to get people to notice me. Time for that strategy to be deployed once again.
