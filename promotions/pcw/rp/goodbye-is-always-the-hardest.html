Date: The Only Date That Matters It’s funny how things turn out, Matt silently thinks to himself, running his hands through his spiky blonde hair. Being back on the ground is a change for him after the past few weeks, but stepping off of the airship was the right thing to do and stepping out of the PCW is the only option left. It’s been one hell of a run… His mind wonders through his short stay in PCW, as he continues to walk through the large field, the grass crushing under his feet and the blazing ball of fire beating down on his face. He just smiles, letting the breeze make his trench coat flap like a banner, not paying any mention to the birds chirping around him and singing their hymns of chorus…preparing for the fallen angels that are going to be soon to come. He finds it funny that the taker of lives could have had his own life taken from him, but he dodged the bullet and now the only thing left was to say goodbye to those who stood by him. Each knowing who they are and each knowing they will have a special place in his heart no matter where he goes…those who trained him and helped him become the beast he is today, all without even selling his soul to the Deville to get it. His walk continues, his pace not quickening or slowing down, but keeping steady as he gets closer to the horizon. Remembering people like Snow, those who stuck their necks out to train him, but they are all going to become blood and ashes, just fail safe memories in the back of his mind to cling to when everything is over. These were the happy days…I’m sure going to miss them. He reaches the middle of the field and turns around looking behind him, leaving his past there. And Then There Were Nine
