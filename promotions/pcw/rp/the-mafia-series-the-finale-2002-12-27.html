The Mafia Series: The Finale.

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 27, 2002. “So, that’s why you’re Version One, eh?” The scene reopens to Jackson’s home in his beloved town of Weedville. Jackson and Al are still tied up, but have been relocated to the living room floor. Fat Tony – Voision One: Yeah…that Matt Groening, and the rest of the Simpson’s cast was unappreciative of my skills. Jackson Rust: My God…that was an amazing story. Al: *yawns* As Jackson sits on the floor, pretending to be interesting the Fat Tony (Voision One)’s story, Al slowly begins to daze off. Narrator: WAKE UP~! The glove slaps Al in the head, waking him up with a stir. Al: Ouch! Narrator: Hehehehe. As the Mafia files in and out of the house with different furniture and items, Jackson speaks up. Jackson Rust: Yeah…I know I can do jack shit from the position I’m in, but don’t you think carrying the furniture out is a bit suspicious to the public eye? Fat Tony thinks for a moment, before scratching his chin. Fat Tony – Voision One: You’re right. Okay, guys, new plan! We’ll cut the furniture into parts, and then we’ll glue them back together when we bring it back to the hideout. Al: But wouldn’t it be hard to put it back together? Narrator: What do you think Thai children were made for? Fat Tony – Voision One: Thanks. Narrator: I’m always saving his ass. Al: You are not! The glove appears, and threateningly swings back and forth. Al: Okay, you do.The glove disappears. Jimmy the Snoop: Hey…when is da other two coming? Jackson Rust: If they time it right, but I don’t think they will. Jackson chuckles to himself, as does Al and Fat Tony (Voision One). Jimmy the Snoop: Nah, nah…not that way. When are they getting back here? Jackson Rust: Oh. Err, I suppose in a few minutes. Fat Tony – Voision One: Excellent. He cackles, and rubs his fingers together, in a Mr. Burns-like fashion. Jackson Rust: Hey, Tony? Fat Tony – Voision One: Yes? Jackson Rust: What’s with that weirdo guy right there; the one picking his teeth with the knife? The scene pans over to see Johnny Tightlips picking his teeth with a knife. He looks around suspiciously, but doesn’t say a word, as Al, Fat Tony (Voision One), and Jackson look at him. Fat Tony – Voision One: That’s Johnny Tightlips. He don’t say much. Watch. Jackson Rust: Okay. Fat Tony – Voision One: Hey! Johnny, c’m’here! Johnny Tightlips: I ain’t moving nowhere. Al: He’s rather antisocial. Narrator: That’s an understatement. Fat Tony – Voision One: Meh. As the other Mafia members file in and out of the house with items of all the sort, Kimberly and Steve enter through the front door, tons of bags in their hands, proving that Kimberly used Steve like a pack-mule. Kimberly: We’re home. Jackson sarcastically attempts to clap, but his hands are tied up. Fat Tony – Voision One: Let me help you there. Fat Tony goes to Rust, and cuts the rope off of his wrists, and smiles, as Jackson sarcastically claps. Jackson Rust: Thanks, Tony. Better tie me back up, eh? Fat Tony – Voision One: Yeah. Jackson presents his arms outward, as Tony ties the remains of Jackson’s cut rope around his wrists, again. Jackson Rust: These are a little tight on the wrists, can I take them off? Fat Tony – Voision One: I suppose. Jackson nods, and simply shrugs off the stigma that is the ropes, pulling his arms back to his chest, and freeing himself. Al: My ropes won’t let me go! Al is seen struggling, trying to free himself of the hemp ropes. Kimberly: Jackson, you know that a line of crude Mafia members are taking your furniture, and cutting it up outside, and putting it in a rather unoriginal getaway car? Jackson Rust: Yeah, I know. Jackson stands up, and frees his legs of the ropes as well. Kimberly: Aren’t you going to do anything? Jackson Rust: No. Steve: W-wh-why n-not? Jackson Rust: They have walkie-talkies, man. Steve jumps into the air, before going to the floor, scattering the shopping bags around him in self-defense. Fat Tony – Voision One: We’ll only use them in drastic circumstances. Kimberly: You look like Fat Tony from the Simpson’s. Fat Tony – Voision One: Correction…I am Fat Tony – Voision One. And yes, I was fired. Steve: T-t-that s-s-sucks. Jimmy the Snoop: You think he don’t know, Sergeant Stupid? Steve: O-o-ouch. Narrator: Hey, Jimmy! Jimmy the Snoop: Yeah? The glove comes down! Jimmy the Snoop: *falls to the ground* Fat Tony – Voision One: I was meaning to do that to him for so long now. Thanks, you did my dirty work, and other clichés, and left me with a clean conscience. Narrator: Not a problem. Kimberly: So…how about you stop taking our stuff, eh? Fat Tony – Voision One: How bout I take something from you, doll face Al: Yeah right…her? A virgin Jackson Rust: Yeah, that’s a good one! Kimberly shoots Al and Jackson a dirty look, possibly contemplating slander charges; which promptly shuts them up, and gives me more of a chance to hit on her. Kimberly: No you won’t. Now, shun yourself. Narrator: *hangs head in shame* Kimberly: Very good. Frankie the Scuzz: Hey…we just took all da trinkets from the bathroom…we done here, or what? Al: MY BATHROOM TRINKETS! DIE~! Al lunges at Frankie, but Steve just so happens to be walking by, and is taken out by the airborne Al. Steve: O-o-ouch! Al: DIE~! Al stands up, trampling Steve all the while. Al finally gets up, and tries to charge, but Jackson sticks his foot out, tripping him. Jackson Rust: Hehehe…ass. As Frankie walks out of the door, with random toilet trinkets, Al swears under his breath. Jackson Rust: What was that Al? Al: *mumbles* Kimberly: You know, that two dollars worth of crap he just took out makes the bathroom look less crowded. Jackson steps next to Kimberly, looking to the bathroom. Jackson Rust: You’re right. Jackson stops and thinks for a moment. Jackson Rust: Al…how did you get out of the ropes? Al: *still mumbles* Jackson Rust: Al…how did you get out of the ropes? Al stops mumbling for a moment. Al: I, uh…wriggled my way out…with this knife conveniently placed on the carpet. Al goes back to mumbling, the whiny bastard. Al: Shut the hell up, man. Narrator: Make me. Al: I will. Narrator: Go for it. Al: Alright…umm… Al thinks for a moment. Al: Jackson…how do you kill a voice? Jackson Rust: Err…cover your ears? Al covers his ears. Narrator: NO! MY POWER! I’M MELTING! Jackson Rust: Gnarly. Jackson turns around to face Fat Tony (Voision One), but he’s nowhere in sight. Kimberly: Where’d the Mafia go? Jackson Rust: I don’t know. Jackson proceeds to look out of the nearby window. Jackson Rust: Oh, they’re getting away with Al’s ’86 Camero. Al looks up, shocked and angered in the process. Al: What? The Beast? They took The Beast? Steve: He-he-he…t-t-the B-b-be-beast. Al: NO! Narrator: YES! I REGAINED MY POWER! All of a sudden, super-strong tape tapes Al’s arms to his sides, not allowing him to cover his ears. Kimberly: Is Al the key to your Almighty narration power? Narrator: Yes he is, you dumb broad. Steve: C-c-cool. With those last words, a stolen car, and stolen bathroom ornaments, the scene thankfully, or regretfully fades out.
