Waiting for his arrival...

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-02 I stood beneath the street light allowing it’s glow to radiate above me, thinking that if anyone was walking down the street or even glancing out of their windows then I would be spotted. I had needed to find some place to hide and quickly before one or the other happened. For if I was spotted then my plans would be drastically changed before they began. I hurriedly crossed the street, making sure as not to make to much noise, to where an old abandoned house was sitting. It looked as though within the past few weeks someone had taken it upon themselves to renovate the old house. But at the moment it stood here empty as the construction workers had left for the night. I had entered the site through a gap in the fencing surrounding the old house. The surrounding area smelled of urine, mostly likely from workers who where to lazy to find a port-a-potty some where near by. Perhaps even a few homeless persons had stopped to relieve themselves as they passed by looking for a place to stay for the night. I found my way into the house through a window that hadn’t had the glass placed into it yet. Once inside I caught myself from sneezing from the dust that seemed to fill the inside of the room. This was a place that would be utter h*ll to someone with allergies. I found the stair case that lead up to the second story of the house. I had noticed a balcony from across the street and thought that it would allow me to hide and watch the house across the street with ease. Once upstairs I found the room that lead to the balcony rather easily. As I walked out onto the balcony I realized for the first time just how perfect this would work. This was the perfect spot for allowing me to watch the house across the street. The house at which he lived at all by his lonesome. As I looked across I found that he still hadn’t pulled into the drive. He would be soon enough though as I knew his schedule from following him the past few days getting to know where he went and when he got there. I could set my watch by this guy. He’s that predictable. It was near time for him to make his way home. He hadn’t ever noticed me except that once yesterday. Where I allowed him to know I was there after he walked out of Primetime Central’s main office building. That day he was afraid but nothing like he would be by the time I was finished with him. Nothing at all like what was coming… I could just imagine the look on his face once he got to his house. I carefully placed the card on his coffee table next to his television remote. A place I was sure that he would find it rather quickly. Once he found it his eyes were sure to grow wide with wonderment, his hands may even begin to shake with nervousness. He’d slit open the envelope with his finger, a letter opener, or even perhaps a pair of scissors or a kitchen knife, either way he choose he’d pull out the carefully hand picked card and look at it. The front of the card had an airplane on it zipping through the air. The plane was most likely headed to some unknown destination across the small globe that seemed to be floating just under the jetliner. At the bottom of the card in big bold letters where the words, Its Hard To Say Goodbye…, would be found. I was certain that the phrase would catch his attention right off. He might even drop into a dumb founded state before opening the card to the inside. Maybe even bend over to pick up the envelope one more time to check again for a return address, but there would not be one any where on the envelope to be found. I could just picture the moment he opened the card. His eyes scanning from the factory printed message to the carefully hand printed one on the left hand side of the card. He’d probably be like so many others and read the factory printed messgae first. [I]It’s hard to say goodbye to a friend who I’ve shared so many fond memories…[/I] A slight smile may come over his face and a hint of gleam of happiness may appear in his eyes. The mere thought of someone so thoughtful could warm a person’s heart. He would be glad to know someone had thought about him before his awaited trip. What a warm and caring gesture.He would then roll his eyes to the left hand side of the card where the hand written message could be found. At first he may notice that the hand writing didn’t seem familiar, that the person also didn’t have the best hand writing in the world. But no less the person’s caring thoughts were what mattered after all. He would then begin reading the enclosed message… [I]Dear Cal,You’re soon to depart from us, the ones you know and… Fear… but we will keep you in our minds while you are away…[/I] I can imagine him to stop reading at this point and ponder the question, what in the h*ll is this? A feeling of butterflies may enter his stomach but he will continue to read… [I]We shall not forget the moments we have spent together, as you too will think of them often. With fear in your heart and a look of despair in your eyes…[/I] At this point he will be asking himself hundreds of questions. Who wrote this? How did it get into my house? What kind of person has the nerve to send this to me? And the all powerful question, Why me? He may decide to have a seat and walk over to his plush leather chair that sits in one corner before finishing the rest of the card. It may be rather late so he probably will even turn on his lamp for more light to read by. He’ll hold out the card and realize he’s not even half finished with this strange message… a message of utmost nonsense. [I]We’ll lay awake at night waiting for the darkness to whisk us away, wondering when you shall return to us. When our eye lids become heavy and hard to hold open we’ll drift off into a land of dreams and… nightmares… some of which may come true once you return…[/I] Dreams… nightmares… coming true? At this moment he’ll think the sender is mad, crazy, and most likely that they should be locked away. Society shouldn’t have to suffer at the senders hands, he as well shouldn’t have to suffer at the senders hands and games… [I]Once the morning radiance breaks through the dark of night we’ll be awoken. The first thing we will think of this new day will be whether our lonely friend returned home sometime during the night, returned to face the… Fear…[/I] Now it’s getting strange and even sort of frightful. At first he may have thought it was a joke. A practical prank by someone he knew. But no this letter couldn’t be from a friend… nobody from his family would send this… a work associate wouldn’t either he’s pretty sure of that. It must… it must be… HIM… [I]We know you’re afraid and it’s understandable. We know that fear grips your very soul. We know that your plans to leave are to try and escape the ever reaching hands of fear. But fear isn’t something you can escape, it’s not something you can hide from, it’s not something you can shut out. Fear simply is…[/I] If it wasn’t strange before it defiantly is now. I can see him licking his lips and trying to get rid of the awful taste that seems to have arisen in the back of his mouth. He’ll set the card down and then walk to his fridge. Taking out a beer he’ll pop the top and slam back half the can in one overly long drink. He’ll then finish it off with a second gulp before heading back into the living room. He’d come back to his seat and look down at where the card sits, peering up at him like a curse from the depths of h*ll. He will stand there captivated by it’s over powering grip on him before he picks up the card one more time. He’ll come directly to his last spot where he had stopped reading. His eyes knowing picking up where they had left off just moments before. [I]In closing we’d like to make it known that we are not crazy, we are not in any way shape for form out of our minds. We don’t need to be locked up, carted away as the mentally ill, or given large quantities of purple and blue pills. We simply are psychotic freaks… Freaks who rather enjoy causing people like you to be afraid. In which you should be… you should be… Your Friend,The Freak[/I] Yes, indeed my card delivery would send chills up his spine. Cause him to sleep with one eye open that is if he could sleep at all. I can see each moment passing as if time almost seemed to stand still. After finishing the card he would know… All the signs would dictate that I was the one to send it… I was knocked back to the present time as a pair of headlights rounded the corner up the street. As the car got closer my heart began to beat faster and faster with each new turn of the cars wheels. When I finally realized that this car was that of the person I had been waiting for. A strange sensation jumped through my entire body as if telling me of the coming joy I would soon feel. I ducked as if trying to hide from the lights coming from the car as it turned into the drive across the street. After a few moments after pulling in the drive the engine was killed and the lights turnded off. Then the car door opened any my friend stepped out into a world that was soon to change for him. He slowly walked, as if burdened down by the world around him. He headed towards his front door, leading to what laid just inside for him to find. The excitement was almost too much to bare. I found it hard to squat behind the railing and stay silent as well as still as he twisted the knob of the door leading inside to his new life. As he closed the door to the outside world something happened I hadn’t planned on. A loss of having to miss the look on his face, the emotions that were sure to rise in his eyes, and the expressions that would come to his frightened face. I sat there and listened for what I wasn’t quite sure. But listening seemed like the logical thing to do. That is since the walls of his home were keeping the two of us apart. I noticed his living room light come on. But after that it seemed as though the world had stopped moving around me. The wind was silent, no cars passed along the street. I was in a normal neighborhood on a normal weekday night. Normal for all but myself and the man in the house directly across the street, that is if he found my card. What happened if he didn’t find it? The not knowing was beginning to eat at my very being. I had to know, I had to see if he found the card, and if he had found it what was his reaction. I looked around at the street one last time before heading back into the old dust filled house. On my way out I almost had to puke from the stench that rose from my surroundings and fill my lungs. Urine is not a pleasant smell. I climbed back through the gap in the fencing and then ducked behind a parked car. Looking around up and down both sides of the street making sure that nobody was out and about. When, I was pretty certain that the only stirring were me and the stars above. I made my way across the street in a rather quick fashion. Once across the street I ducked behind a bush when I thought I had heard something or somebody. But I realized that my senses where playing a prank on me. Always happens, you try to do something and those d*mn senses will try and tell you that your caught. I ducked low to the ground and made my wap up near the porch. Looking around I noticed that the only way to see inside was to get up onto the porch and to look into one of the windows. The others were to high off the ground to peer into without a ladder. Again I looked around making sure that nobody was around. Then I placed my foot on the first step slowly, anticipating a squeak that didn’t come. I then worked my way up the remaining steps with just as much ease as the first. Once on the porch level I made my way to a far window tucked away in the shadows of night. Once I was looking into the window I found that the drapes were parted just enough for me to see inside to the living room. What luck I had drawn with such a clear view into the house. Only one thing kept me from seeing what brought me to the porch in the first place. The angle to which I had to see. The coffee table was out of my view and from what I could see the room was empty. I went silently down to the next window to see if this one would allow a better angle of view. But to my disadvantage the drapes of this window weren’t parted. The drapes where white allowing a distorted view but a clear one was impossible at least from this window, but moving down one more would put me into the sight of someone coming from down the street. This window would have to do. As peered into the house I could slightly make out a figure sitting in a chair. Was it him? Was he the occupant of the chair? It had to be him, after all it was his house. Who else could it be? I moving my head from side to side trying to get a better look I thought I had seen something in his hands. He had it? He was reading the card? It was the card wasn’t it? I had a hard time trying to make out what it was. But what else could it have been? But from the view I had there was only one way to be certain. That was to knock on the door and ask him if he read his card. But then what? That would ruin the whole reasoning behind it. Wouldn’t it? I only had one clear thing I could do. I turned to walk down the steps. It was rather late and the only thing I could do was head towards my home and wait. He would find it sooner or later if he hadn’t already. And when he did I was certain to know. Somebody would spread the news after he had told somebody about it. Which he would do, I was certain of that. Only time could tell how long that would be. Only time was between me and his reaction…
