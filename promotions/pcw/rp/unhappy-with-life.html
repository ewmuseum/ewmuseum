Unhappy with life?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (The scene opens up with Chet King and Cindy Bi in Chet King’s black sports car driving down a road listening to music and enjoying the scenery, although all the scenery is really is a bunch of shops. Suddenly Chet King turns the music off and starts talking to Cindy Bi.) Chet King: I can’t take it any more, Cindy. (Cindy Bi looks confused as she reply’s to Chet King.) Cindy Bi: What can’t you take? What’s wrong? Did I do something? (Chet King takes his eyes of Cindy and back on to the road as he continues to drive down it. He takes a deep breath before continuing to talk to Cindy.) Chet King: You didn’t do anything wrong Cindy, it’s just that I can’t take it. (Cindy Bi still looks very confused.) Cindy Bi: What can’t you take? What’s the matter? Chet King: I can’t take anything, I can’t take life. (Cindy Bi’s facial expressions becomes very worried as she stares at Chet.) Cindy Bi: Life? What’s wrong with life? Chet King: Everything. Cindy Bi: What on earth do you mean? Chet King: I just mean that life is something I no longer enjoy. Cindy Bi: What?!? Two minutes ago you seemed fine. (Chet King lets out a long sigh before continuing.) Chet King: Two minutes ago I was as upset about life as I am right now, I just wasn’t letting on. (Cindy Bi seems even more worried now as her face tenses up putting wrinkles in her forehead and her eyes squint.) Cindy Bi: Since when haven’t you been happy with life? What could possibly be wrong? You’re now the longest reigning Continental Champion in PCW history….in fact you’re the longest reigning champion of any kind in PCW history! Chet King: I know, I know, that’s great…but still…. Cindy Bi: You’ve got not only your first ever World Title shot this Sunday at March Madness, but also your first ever pay per view main event. What could possibly be wrong with that? Chet King: There’s nothing wrong with that, it’s great, but still…. Cindy Bi: On Friday Fusion you have what you would have to consider to be one of the biggest matches in your career against Jeremy Howard, you’ve got to be happy with the opportunity to compete in that match. Chet King: I am, but still…. Cindy Bi: But still what? Chet King: There’s more to life then wrestling, much more to life! Cindy Bi: If wrestling isn’t the reason for your sudden problems with life, then what is it? Chet King: It’s everything. Cindy Bi: What do you mean by everything? Chet King: Everything. Cindy Bi: Chet I don’t know what you mean by everything, please try and explain it to me more. Chet King: I can’t explain it any further, by everything I mean everything. Cindy Bi: But you weren’t upset with wrestling, and wrestling is part of everything. Chet King: Meh. Cindy Bi: Perhaps you’re upset about your current love life. Chet King: Perhaps… Cindy Bi: Chet, unless there’s something you’re not telling me, you shouldn’t worry about your love life, I’m sure you can get any woman you want. Chet King: Maybe. (Cindy Bi scratches her head trying to work out what has caused Chet King’s sudden mood swing.) Cindy Bi: Look, Chet, I’m not just your manager but I’d like to consider that I’m a good friend as well. We’ve been through a lot together and I feel that I deserve to know what’s going on. Chet King: I’ve told you what’s going on. Cindy Bi: No you haven’t. Chet King: Yes I did, I told you that I’m unhappy with life. Cindy Bi: But you haven’t told me why! Four minutes ago you were your usual happy self full of bad jokes and puns with a positive, care free outlook on life. Chet King: I told you that I’ve been feeling unhappy for a while. Cindy Bi: I know you have, but I don’t believe it, I would know if you had. Chet King: Would you? Cindy Bi: I would. I want to know why you’ve had this sudden mood swing and change of attitude. Chet King: I’ve already told you. Cindy Bi: No you haven’t! Look you’ve got two big matches coming up. One against Jeremy Howard in which you will defend your Continental Title and the other a battle royal in which you have an opportunity to win the world title. Are you planning to go in to those matches with a negative outlook? Chet King: I don’t have a negative outlook on those matches, I know that if I give it my all I will be able to pull off a victory against Jeremy and if I give it my all then I will be able to win the world title. (Cindy Bi now seems more confused then ever.) Cindy Bi: Chet, I don’t know what to think. You seem to be upset, but yet you seem to still have a fighting spirit in that you believe you can win these matches you have. Chet King: There’s more to life then wrestling. Cindy Bi: You’ve already said that! But what do you mean? I still don’t understand why you’re upset, you haven’t explained anything to me! Chet King: Meh. Cindy Bi: I haven’t seen you in this emotional state since when we had our huge fight with each other, Mexi and Mona back in December. (Chet King doesn’t answer, but instead just looks straight ahead as he continues to drive down the road.) Cindy Bi: Chet, all I can say is that I’m utterly confused. Chet King: Life is confusing. Cindy Bi: Chet, I’m hoping that this sudden mood swing is just that, a mood swing. I hope that you’re not seriously unhappy with life for apparently no particular reason, as like I’ve been saying, seven minutes ago you were happy. Chet King: I wasn’t happy with life. Cindy Bi: So you say, but I don’t know what to think. I just hope this is some strange mood swing, and I’m guessing that’s all it is. So how about when we get back to the hotel you get some sleep and you’ll be back to your usual happy self in no time. Chet King: I doubt it. Cindy Bi: You will, and then maybe you can explain to me why you had this sudden strange mood swing. Chet King: I’ve already explained. Cindy Bi: You haven’t, and I’m not going to try and get an explanation any more. So just drive back to the hotel and then get some sleep and all will be good. Chet King: We’ll see. (Cindy Bi shakes her head pondering what just happened to Chet King in the last few minutes. We leave the scene as Chet King lets out a long sigh and continues to drive forward in his black sports car.)
