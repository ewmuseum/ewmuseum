Date: not entered I know how I was conceived… Akiyama and Glacia…they had sex, she got pregnant…and boom. Human and Celestial DNA combines and creates sweet little me. I should have known she was my mother anyway…I raped her mind…I saw what she did with Akiyama…I felt what she did with him. But I should have known earlier…when Fantasy mistook me for my mother. Fantasy…my mother’s ex…before she cheated on him with Akiyama… And I knew what she was doing now, and that’s why I wasn’t treating her so motherly. But…I want to know how I wound up in the basement of the Department of Divine Examination for most of my life, inside that containment capsule locked up from the outside world. Kept from the people…separated from mortals, and forced to have dreams and visions of things…bad things…dreams of things that I’m making come true now. So to get the answers…why not go to the source? To ask another question…to get a winner, why sift out the losers in elimination style? The Celestials are standing right before you…can’t see us? Check for the barcode. Official emblem of W I N N E R S “I wont bore you people with details. Your leader is dead.” A band of silver-clad CODE cultists murmured amongst themselves in that abandoned warehouse, just waking from their peaceful slumber induced by the fair goddess of gore…me. They had no idea what had happened…or that their only hope of salvation just ran off with his friends to lose some pathetic tournament to some pathetic Flat-Scan Wrestler. Shame for him…the god would have ripped the tournament in half, and not looked back. But tournaments were a waste…nothing but a time wasted for bragging rights that wasted more breath than Duke wastes whisky in a drunken stupor. This elimination free for all was a waste…but we had our reasons for participating. “Sato’s dead?” one of the cult members cried out, tears in their eyes. Geez…these people were supposed to be followers of the nVg, and they were mourning some asshole who was leading them blindly into death more than Celestial Salvation? These people had a lot to learn…just like the roster had a lot to learn about challenging the authority of the Neo Void God. Hey…the god was looking to destroy you all…and now he gets his opportunity in a big fed free for all…the smart thing would be to stay clear of the god, hope you get lost in the shuffle, and just in case he spots you…flee for your damn life. I sighed, glaring at the buzz cut peon who was whimpering in the masses. “Yes, Sato, as you have named him, is dead. What is your name?” He looked up, his face switching from sadness to terror. He had all right to…the goddess doesn’t ask your name to be friendly with you. There was a purpose to it all. Never put a name with the corpses… nonattachment means smoother destruction. “Me? I’m Sato’s brother, Ryo!” I pointed my finger at him, my thumb cocked back like a gun. “No, now you’re dead.” The nonchalant tone of the threat shot out like a bullet…but the correction was serious. Pulled the trigger…killed the mortal in cold psychic blood. His eyes rolled up in the back of his head as his legs folded up underneath him, crumpling in a heap before his brothers and sisters. They gasped, backing away from the body as I lowered my arm and looked back. Akiyama nodded as Anarchy smiled at the sight. Glacia stood off to the side, watching with her arms folded across her chest. Something was up with her…if she was going to be a part of this Celestial clan; she was going to have to get with the program. “First rule of CODE, children…there is no remorse for the dead.” Their eyes turned towards me, some of them looking shocked, others looking like they just saw a ghost. I stared back at them, my cold eyes sending drafts through the spacious storehouse. “If you mourn the dead, your tears will not bring them back. Death is a part of your lives, no matter how much you humans try to ignore it. You will all die…there’s no doubt about that. So why cry about something you can’t stop?” Murmurs ran through the people as I folded my arms against my chest. I spoke true words, no doubt about that. You most certainly didn’t see people crying about not being able to stop Akiyama and his rampage through the ranks. Well…actually Pharaoh sure did b!tch and moan about not being able to stop Akiyama after Red October… put on a huge depressed bit and everything. What a loser… “Will you lead us now, great Neo Void God?” a female called out, a Japanese woman with short brown hair. My eyes cut towards her, a threatening glare cutting into her own eyes into her mind…and once I’m in your mind, you’re as good as dead. She dropped to the ground, prompting everyone to move away from her crumpled corpse. “Second rule for belonging to the Celestial Order of Divine Existence…” I mandated with a strict tone. “You do not address the Neo Void God. He is above speaking to you mortals. I do the speaking for him. You have questions, you address them to me.” A bald man stood out next, throwing a question in my direction. “Will the Neo Void God lead us now? The people backed away from his body before I gave them another commandment. “Third Commandment of CODE,” might as well name them Commandments…write them down later and put them on the wall for people to read. “No stupid questions…period. I despise stupidity just as much as I despise the incompetent mortals that persistently challenge us in combat. They refuse to believe that they cannot win…and thus they must find themselves staring up at the ceiling in order to find out that they’re weak mortals.” Anarchy began clapping behind me, a sick smile on her face as she bounced on her heels. “This is fun!” “What are we going to do about the other CODE sects spread through Japan?” a younger man with black hair asked. “Are we going to combine and overthrow the government of Japan?” Another body hit the floor…a lot of them would be doing that in the mall on Friday. “Another thing…no asking questions without raising your hand.” I looked back at my sister and father, who were eating this up like candy. Glacia, on the other hand, continued to watch without a smile on her face. That didn’t settle with me well. A hand raised behind me as I looked towards Anarchy and Akiyama. Reaching back, I pointed at her, and the woman’s brain exploded from the inside just like the others. People were growing uneasy at the rapid killing of their associates…but what were they going to do? “Fifth Commandment of CODE…No questions PERIOD. If you have a question, then the automatic answer will be “Praise the Celestials.” Anything else…and it’s wrong…and being wrong equals your head going boom from the inside. GOT IT?” A murmuring of the people could be heard from amongst the horde. Dissection among the ranks? I don’t think so. They all clammed up when I threw my arms out towards them, silent in caution of being struck down dead. “I cannot HEAR you.” “Praise the Celestials.” They all said in a monotone unison. “Why does it sound like I need to strike the fear of the Neo Void God into you mortals??” “PRAISE THE CELESTIALS!!” That got some life out of them…it was either that or I get the life out of them the old fashioned way…slaughtering them all with your bare hands like the good old days…namely, a few weeks ago when we first got to Japan. I waved my hand around the room as I backed up to join Anarchy and Akiyama. “Now…pick up all the corpses and do something with them. Burn them, shred them, f*ck them like you would f*ck Avril Lavirnge’s dead carcass…I really don’t care at this point. Just get them out of our sight.” They started cleaning up the corpses as I turned around and smiled at the god. He ruffled me on the top of my head, as he has been known to do lately as Anarchy held my Cruiserweight belt across her shoulder and smiled. “Now…that was entertainment.” She said with a grin. “Much better than the spoof crap and the idiotic nonsense that most of the roster puts out that they call entertainment.” I nodded, agreeing with her. “Yes, my dead sister…only the truly Enlightened can appreciate our brand of twisted wicked pleasure…so basically we’re preaching to the converted.” I lowered my head, shaking it slowly. “Clyde with the World title…Rent A Hero with the Continental title…Semore Smoke with the Extreme title…It’s a sad day for intelligence across the globe.” “What about Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo? And Venneman Pendragon?” my sister responded inquisitively. I shrugged. “I have to understand what they say before I can call Playaz Inc intelligent or not. First I’m going to have to pay some attention to them. And Pendragon…there’s something about him being TV champ that just doesn’t bother me at this point. Main point is that no one’s going to stop the CODE from rampaging through PCW…not even the world champion or Darkstar.” Akiyama cracked his knuckles at the sound of the Goth. I smiled, patting him on his thick trunk of an arm. “So says the Divi--.” “Excuse me, where did you want these bodies to go?” A black haired woman wearing the CODE uniform of a silver tank top and silver jeans stood behind me, bowing respectably as to not invoke the god’s wrath. I turned around slowly and glared down at the back of her head. She looked up, her eyes fearing my gaze as she bowed again. “I mean…PRAISE THE CELESTIA--!!” She fell flat on her face…her mind nothing but mush now…thanks to bad TV programs, spoofs, and stupid fantasies of idiotic cretins. Oh…and that 390 pounds of gravimetric pressure per square inch that only my psychic mind can project. My mind alone was stronger than most of the flat-scans in PCW. What a waste of talent I say… “Don’t think I wont stand here and drop you all like flies! I have no problem with killing the whole lot of you! None of you are necessary…we can take the world apart brick by brick by ourselves if need be! Push your luck people…your brains will leak out of your ears if you continue to think we’re joking!” Everyone stared at me after that outburst. I glared at them all before motioning down towards the body at my feet, and exasperated look on my face. “You missed a spot!” Followers rushed to the scene, picking up the corpse and dragging her away…probably for some cult necrophilia or something else they do. Her brain wouldn’t be the only thing leaking out one of her orifices. I turned back and placed my hands on my hips, sighing as Glacia herself sighed as well, both of us blowing out a white wisp of vapor from our blue lips. It was strange…we both did that when we sighed…a vapor like when you can see your breath in the cold. “Anyway…let’s get out of here. We have some business to take care of down at the police station.” Glacia trailed behind us as father and sister walked by my sides towards the exit of the warehouse. I glared back at my mother…finding her matching looks with me. Some mother she was being…
