The 80's [5]

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19.01.03 'WE'RE METALLICA AND WE'RE HERE TO FUCKING ROCK YOUR ASSES!' The screams of James Hetfield are heard from the microphone, the screams from the crowd are all too known for anybody to deny. DUM....DUM DUM DUM.....DUM DUM DUM... The guitar rifts from one of Metallica's One rifts through the arena as tens upon thousands of fans halt their walking and begin screaming at the top of their lungs. Currently, the songs theme has been known to be used by Kid Rock for his American Badass song fourteen years later than right now, but the fans aren't going to care. Its rock at its finest. 'HEY! HEY!' As the fans begin to nod their head, Lars Urich begins jamming on the drums as Hetfiled with guitar in hand comes up to the micrphone, stringing chords together and waiting for the crowd to calm down from the frenzy of seeing the band. ARE YOU MOTHERFUCKERS READY TO ROCK?! YES! ARE YOU MOTHERFUCKERS READY TO ROCK?! WE HEARD YOU ALREADY! YEA! From the center of the crowd, Skylar Green stands out, his six foot eight inch frame towering over everyone else. The frenzy around him is hard not only for him to hear, but for his words to get out towards the crowd. Like much of the fans in attendance, a black Metallica shirt with blue jeans and sneakers are the tools of the trade, before Hetfield kicks in with the song. Skylar Green: 'There's a damn tape playing back there!' Fan #1: '....Shut up!' Skylar Green: 'What? Your mommy sucks nuts?' Fan #2: '....He said SHUT UP!' Skylar Green: 'You mommy sucks nuts too?' Fan #1: '...Hey Jase, lets BEAT THE BLIND DUDE!' Fan #2: '...but Harry, mom said we couldn't beat people at this concert.' Skylar cracks his neck, knuckles and toes with Harry and Jason argue on whether or not to beat the man for some odd reason. Meanwhile, from the back of the almost 13,000 people in attendance, one large voice breaks out without anyone trying to make it go unnoticed. 'THIS IS THE BIGGEST BUNCH OF BULLSHIT I'VE EVER SEEN IN MY LIFE!' The concert stops, a record is heard scratched to not even being heared as Dante Jackson stands in the center of attention, along side of him, Rent-A-Hero and Vanilla Skillz. Fan: 'Who the hell are you?' Dante clears his throat. Dante: 'Who am I? I'm the only man here in the entire room that knows just what the hell is going on! You hear this Renty?' Rent-A-Hero, clad in a red Addidas jumpsuit and a gold chain swinging from his neck nods in approval. Dante: 'Exactly! It seems here that this is a...' He stops before trying to sound out the word. Dante: 'Me - tal - ee - ca concert?' Jackson Rust: 'METALLICA DUMBASS!' Dante: 'Oh, yes, right Metallica! You five and your stupid Mexican bass player over there, step off the stage right now. You are in breaking of the law, BAD MUSIC!' Boos are heard from the crowd, as Skylar Green stands out as Harry and Jason sit back, watching the scene erupt behind them. He snickers to himself, placing his hand to grasp his chin. Skylar Green: 'Black people...' Dante: 'I HEARD THAT GREEN!' Skylar Green: 'Shit, he heard me.' Dante steps out of the way, as Vanilla Skillz lands back in the fray, faking some good decent Kung Fu action before the large Mani and Shin stand in front of the group as Chris Matthews and Masked Mullet stand on the other side, siding with the fans on this one. Vanilla Skillz: 'Once second, jus where da fuck are Marv n Matthews? Don't dey bitch asses hate each other?' Jason: 'Don't you hate not knowing how to speak proper English?' Vanilla Skillz: 'DAFUCKUP CRACKA!' Jason stands back, obviously scared of the screaming white man aligned with a black man, a super hero, two video game look alikes, and about a few more bad men inside of the arena whom are still unaccounted for that happen to deal with this type of group. James Hetfield: 'Oh come on, SECURITY!' Dante: 'FUCK SECURTIY! We'll leave only if you stop playing and give us Skylar Green, Chris Matthews, and The Masked Mullet!' Hetfield shakes his head. Lars Ulrich: 'Um, James?' The guitar player turns around, finding his drum player sitting down with hands on his hips. Lars Ulrich: 'How did these guys find out about the portal, they might be here to take us back!' James Hetfield: 'Shut up Lars! We only came back here because this is where we actually had an impact on music!' Skylar Green: 'Wait, YOU'RE TIME TRAVELERS?!' Knowing that they've been busted, Metallica breaks into one of their signature songs, 'Seek and Destroy'. The lights turn out and are replaced by strobes as Dante along with everybody else begin to fight their way out of the concert, dragging whomever they can as punches begin to fly, kicks are thrown in the air and black people are outnumbered 13,000 to 2. Vanilla Skillz: 'Homie, I think we best get our asses out this biatch. Dez cracka's playa hatin'.' Skillz shudders as the crowd submerges upon him and the rest of the crew as a huge pile of fights break out, Dante squirming out of the way befroe snagging Green down to the ground. Dante: 'Gotcha!' Skylar Green: 'The hell is this?' Mystery Voice: 'That is a good question...' As the voice rings into the ears of not only Dante Jackson, but Skylar Green as well, their eyes bring themselves upon the shady figure who came through the portal a few hours earlier. With dark rimmed glasses and a freshly loss set of hair, the doctor stands with ball shaped nose and cat like mustache. Skylar Green: 'You know him?' Dante: 'As many odd people I see on a daily basis, he isn't one of them.' Scientist: 'Many around here call me Dr. Steadily, but I'm here to bring you and your confidants back to the real world.' Dante shrugs his shoulders. Dante: 'What am I missing in the real world?' Scientist: 'How about Shania Twain getting a diary spot if you don't come back and finish yours?' Dante lets out a blood curdling scream as the scene fades to black.
