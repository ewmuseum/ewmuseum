Psychological Battlefield -Prelude-

Date: not entered Darkness… We couldn’t see anything… just darkness. It was peaceful…silent…harmonic… Is this death…? Some would think it’s hell…devoid of existence…cold and eerie…but for us…it’s splendid heaven. No more wasting time with worthless opponents…no more annoyances with pissy runts who think they can get over us…no more jackasses taking what belongs to us… no nothing… Silence…Void… Our paradise… =---=---=---= “You think you’ll be all right?” Sonata didn’t look too cool with the decision we made. Her twin sister waited for us, impatience plastered over her face like we wanted to plaster blood over PCW’s bitches. They were lucky we were only one entity…or we’d have swept over this retarded company by now. “Quit ya bitchin,” we muttered, pulling the black shirt off of our torso. “We gotta do this in order to get our head straight. Then everyone can die…or leave us alone…whichever comes first.” “Still, doing THIS? It’s like suicide!” she responded, putting her hands on her hips. “Like you know anything about the Void, sister.” Symphony muttered drolly. Sonata glared hard at her before looking back at the god with pleading eyes. “Are we gonna do this or not? There’s a Council Meeting coming up soon, and the council doesn’t appreciate when they KNOW you’re gonna be late.” “F-ckin psychics…” we hated the whole lot of em…Cyclonica didn’t give them all such a good rap. Now anytime I suspected anyone of reading our minds we were gonna rip them a crater in their chest. Bitch ass Tim thought he could get something against us…we’d have him screaming like the bitch he is after we’re done with him. “Let’s get this over with.” Darkcyde muttered from across the room, where he and the other Void Elements sat impatiently. “The longer he’s here the longer he’s an insult to our existence.” “So you say, f-cker.” We stepped towards the dark waters, right foot first into the black depths. We waded out until we were waste deep in the blackness and stopped. Before us was a massive waterfall of dark liquid, pouring, roaring, towering over us. An indoor waterfall…the waters ethereal. We looked back at Sonata one last time; her worried face etched into our mind one more time… before we stepped into the waterfall…into darkness… -=-=-=-=-=-=--=- The darkness wasn’t lasting…an image was breaking through. A spotlight… and a chair sat there waiting for us. As if we knew what to do…we sat down and held our head in our hands…thinking of the battles set to come…the adversities we were destined to face because of our name…because of who we are… “Hey…how’s it going?” That voice was familiar… we looked up around us, finding the darkness to have dissipated. Now we sat in the chair in a dim locker room. The sound of the roaring fans could be heard muffled past thick walls. But we were not alone in the room. A woman had her back turned to us, wearing tight black form fitting slacks with silver high heels and a shiny silver blouse that exposed some of her lean midsection. Her silver hair was tied back in a high ponytail, with some of it falling down the sides of her face. She turned around and gave a short nod, pushing the black spectacles up her nose to peer at us with orange eyes. “Omina?” we questioned, not completely recognizing the Celestial Oracle after such a long time. “Been a while huh?” she put the small book down in her hand and stretched her arms out. “Come on, give “us” a hug.” Before we could protest, she had thrown her arms around us in an embrace. After a pat on the back she stepped back and picked up her black book. “What are you doing here?” She looked up from her book with a knowing smile. “What are YOU doing here?” “We’re here to sort ourselves out. Get all these f-ckin voices out of our heads.” “Such language..” a disapproving look came on her face. “You’ve become vulgar since our last encounter.” “We’re old and bitter. Don’t f-ck with us.” She chuckled at our statement. “Yeah right. I’M OLD…you’re just a kid compared to my age.” This pretty woman looked like she was 20, and she said WE were a kid? “Yeah, right. Don’t argue numbers with us. Just tell us what’s going on.” Just then, a bell rang. Omina looked up, a look of satisfaction coming on her face. “Finally. Been waiting long enough.” She took our hand, pulling us to our feet. “Come along, we can’t make destiny wait.” “What the hell’s going on?” She winked at us secretively. “We’re about to clear your mind.” TO BE CONTINUED…
