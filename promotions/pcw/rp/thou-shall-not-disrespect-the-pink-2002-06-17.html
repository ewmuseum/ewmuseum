Thou Shall Not Disrespect The PINK!

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 17, 2002 The Heartbreakers have been a tag team in PCW for over two months now. In that two months they have accomplished the feat of becoming tag team champions, the owner of one of the greatest catchphrases in PCW history and they have defeated nearly every tag team in the federation. However, one tag team has not been defeated by Jack and Johnny, nor have they even gone toe to toe with this team. This team you ask? Well it’s none other than Cowabunga Connection. A team comprised of two “surfers.” Surfers? The only surfers that would be as cool as Cowabunga Connection thinks they are, would be surfers who dress in PINK! If they would just wear PINK! they would be a great tag team. They have all the tools that wrestlers in the tag team division need...arms, legs...if they would throw some PINK! on themselves, they would do just fine. However, since they are ignorant enough to ignore the PINK! and not follow its holy ways, they will find out, just like all of the other doubters of PINK! that Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart “have the force with them.” The scene opens to the sound of grunting. Loud grunting. Orgasmic grunting. It almost sounds like sex...but with more than one man’s voice. The echoing of the grunts gets more and more disgusting and it seems as if the cameraman is moving in even closer to the noise. As a door swings open, a gigantic scream is let out and a loud crash is heard. The cameraman moves around the corner to find Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart...moving furniture in their house. Oh c’mon, what’d you expect to see? Perverts. Jack Valentine: “Do you think that looks alright hun?” An awkward pause fills the room as Jack looks in the direction of Johnny Heart. Seconds later, Christina Livingston pops out from around the corner to answer the question. Christina Livingston: “Yeah babe, it looks great.” Jack and Johnny slap each other on their butts and commend each other on another decorating job well done. The new PINK! couch is a perfect addition to their living room. Johnny Heart: “Man I’m hot after that...that was more intense than a big, hard...bench press.” Jack Valentine: “Yeah, I could really go for a dip right now.” Johnny walks over to the window and looks out to see a perfectly sunny day, not a cloud in the sky. The water is glistening off of their pool as the PINK! flamingos that were recently put in surround the area. Johnny Heart: “The weather is perfect Jack...let’s strip down and head out there.” Jack Valentine: “Okay, you go ahead...I’ll meet you out there with a nice cold glass of PINK! Lemonade.” Almost like an advertisement, Jack grabs a bottle of PINK! lemonade mix and flashes it up at the camera. He gives a smile and a wink and continues in the ad like manner. Jack Valentine: “The official drink of The Heartbreakers, and the should be official drink of Primetime Championship Wrestling.” Inauspiciously, the ad like manner stops and Jack goes about making his PINK! lemonade. In the background, Johnny Heart is stripping down to nothing more than a PINK! thong, with the initials J.H. embroidered on the crotch. After making the lemonade, Jack Valentine does the same...of course having J.V. on his thong. The pair walks out side by side to...poolside...and they each plop themselves down on their comfy PINK! lawn chairs. Slowly, the pair sucks the PINK! lemonade down like a...well, like a glass of PINK! lemonade. Johnny Heart: “Ahh...chilled to perfection.” Jack Valentine: “Yes indeed.” Johnny Heart: “You know Jack, I am sick and fed up with teams like Pretty Cool Guys, Dark Messiahs, Cowabunga Connection...” Jack Valentine: (smiling) “And why is that my friend?” Johnny Heart: “They disrespect the PINK! And by disrespecting the PINK!, they disrespect us!” Jack Valentine: “You know, instead of trying to fight the evil do’ers, maybe we should try to join them...” Johnny Heart: “Join them?!? Are you crazy?” Jack Valentine: “What I mean is, we should try to become a part of their world and then let them know how much better it would be if they were like us. Supporting the colors proud.” Jack and Johnny both flip over on their stomachs at exactly the same time. They adjust their seats...and their thongs, and place their faces in a specially designed hole in their lawn chair. Johnny Heart: “How should we go about this plan Jack?” Jack Valentine whispers something into the ear of Johnny Heart. A large smile comes across the face of The Heartbreaker and the two begin laughing. Jack Valentine: “Huh? Don’t you think that is brilliant?” Johnny Heart: “Well, aside from spreading the word...don’t you think we should prepare in some way for our match against Cowabunga Connection?” Jack Valentine: “Ha...haha...hahaha.” Jack laughs nervously as he continues. Jack Valentine: “Totally Tubular Tommy and Heavy Metal Harry are not a threat to us...” He takes a big gulp. Johnny Heart: “Well, they are showing their anti-PINK! ways backstage. I heard them talking like we are no longer in the tag team title hunt, and that they will prove themselves as the number 1 contenders against us this Tuesday.” Jack Valentine: “They said that? I’ll show them a thing or two.” Johnny Heart: “Man, if I had them alone...I would...” Another awkward pause takes place in the scene. Johnny Heart: “Tear them to shreds!” Jack glances over at Johnny and shakes his head. Johnny Heart: “What? What’d I say?” The ambiguously gay pair smirks amongst themselves in a Steven Smith type fashion before Jack calls for them to flip again. As they flip, Johnny’s thong comes undone a little bit and the whole area “down there” becomes blurred out. Jack Valentine: “Johnny!” Johnny Heart: “What is it?” Jack reaches down and fixes the problem to the complete disgust of the cameraman. He nearly drops the camera in abhorrence. Jack and Johnny react as if it is not a big deal whatsoever. Johnny Heart: “Whoopsies...looks like little Heartbreaker wanted to come out and play.” Jack and Johnny share a laugh, but the cameraman finds the whole situation sickening. Vomiting sounds are heard off in the background to the delight of the viewing audience. Jack Valentine: “Cowabunga should realize that a package like us...no pun intended...will be the next tag team champions. They may think that they are the next big thing, but we are the real big things...again, no pun intended.” Johnny smiles at the witty remarks. Johnny Heart: “Yeah, the surfer dudes have it coming on Tuesday, and the beating we give them will be far out!” Jack Valentine: “It will be radical!” Johnny Heart: “It will be bodacious!” Jack Valentine: “It will be totally cool!” Johnny Heart: “But most importantly...” Jack Valentine: “It will be all about the PINK!” The scene fades as the Heartbreakers cross their legs in a very feminine way and continue to sunbathe.
