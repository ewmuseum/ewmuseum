Delivery!

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/02/2002 (Voice Over on black screen): 'You mean he's actually submitted something? D-Kay Calyx has actually sent in videotape to be aired on PCW TV? What in hell's name is the world coming to? Where is he anyway? We got something for the GTT, but this is the first PCW TV appearance in a while. You know, this is a landmark. PTC's greatest known jobber, besides HANK~! of course, finally gets himself some TV time. Well, we may as well make use of it. Here goes...' ***** The voice over ends as the black screen fades into a car park. Various cars are parked across the site that is situated next to a Truck Stop Cafe. At one end of the parking lot, we have what seems like hundreds of eighteen wheelers and big rig trucks. Whereas closer to the cafe, are a small number of vehicles from vans and side-loaders, to cars, to a few mobile news stands. The camera focuses on a blue BMW 850i pulling into the car park, as it swerves to miss a Ford Transit van at the entrance. The BMW follows the slip road around to the car park, the camera watching it as it moves. Finally reaching the cafe, the sleek German made automobile glides to a stop, and the engine purrs to a halt. The camera focuses in on the driver as he exits the car. The driver is a black man, around 6'2 in height, and 230 plus pounds in weight climbs out and locks the door. Dressed in a pair of black jeans, a Rammstein t-shirt, and a black leather jacket, he sets the alarm on the car and walks inside the cafe. The view shifts to inside the generic transport cafe. Waitresses all in their mid-thirties and upwards walk around in white blouses, and red and white checked aprons, delivering food and drinks to the large number of patrons. We follow our man as he enters, and walks past the first few tables, full of men dressed as stereotypical bikers, and towards the serving counter. As he stops, and grabs a tray, a woman approaches the counter, and flashes her best smile in a way to say 'How can I help?' Our man nods his head, and spends a few seconds looking at the menu before answering. 'I'll have a bacon and sausage sandwich, no butter. And can I have a mug of black coffee, no sugar as well please?' We watch as he draws a leather wallet from his right pocket, and opens it up. The woman behind the counter shakes a few strands of peroxide blonde hair out of her eyes, and keys the request onto the till. 'That will be $3.95 please.' Our man pays for the food and drink, and waits while the waitress hands him his change, and gets his coffee ready. Taking a white till receipt for his order, he places his mug of coffee on the tray, and walks away from the counter, sitting down at an empty booth near the window, just happening to overlook his BMW. A few minutes pass, with the coffee being drunk slowly until the waitress turns up with the sandwich. At the same time, another man enters the shot, and slides into the seat opposite. Dressed in a pair of blue jeans and a red and black flannel shirt, the 6'6, 265 frame slides in with ease into the booths made to fit truckers. His short, spiky, brown hair finalises the look. 'Hey D-Kay. What can I do for you?' 'Hey Mark. Nice look. A bit drastic don't you think?' The tale is complete. The black man is D-Kay Calyx, and the white trucker is Mark Estep. Both former PCW2 and NTICW wrestlers, now both wrestling in PCW. D-Kay finishes his sandwich as Mark shrugs his shoulders, not following the invitation that D-Kay laid out for his change in look. As D-Kay finishes, Mark's cup of coffee arrives, the same waitress as before delivering. 'How are you keeping yourself Mark? I've not seen you since NTICW I believe. Or was it that PCW versus NTICW sports thing? I can't remember.' D-Kay looks at Mark and smiles. Mark nods his head, as he continues to down his drink. 'I'm not too bad D-Kay. Been getting myself into the gym since I was signed to PCW again.' Mark places his mug onto D-Kay's tray, and smiles as he folds his arms on the table. 'So what have you been up to D?' D-Kay shrugs his shoulders as he finishes his coffee as well. 'Not much. I got to PCW, bombed as people said I would. Ended up giving Red Rock the Rising Star Title, before Juan Borro knocked me out of the title race. Now all I have to hope for is that Miyagawa retains until Friday, Walden finally gets the belt back, so I can have a title shot at March Madness.' Mark tries his hardest to hold back a laugh. 'Tim Walden beat Miyagawa for the TV Title? In your dreams my friend. And even if he does, then what? You've got Walden wanting to retain, and four other guys wanting the gold on Sunday. I respect you as a friend and wrestler, but you have no chance.' D-Kay shrugs his shoulders again. 'This is the thing Mark. It took me ages to get to PCW, and by the time I do, the two people who graduated before me from PWA have both held major titles. Including Snow being the champion for now. Whereas I've had two PCW2 titles to my name. I know I'm in The Deuces, and I know we're meant to make PCW2's name live on, but I don't think I'm able to. Hell...Boda's concentrating on Snow, and quite rightly. Walden is always doing his own thing. Red Rock's in mourning over his Rising Star Title, and Mexi has his vendetta against Tyler and Wonder to act out. Whereas I'm here, not in any major involvement in PCW, and quite frankly rethinking my role here.' Mark sits back in his chair slightly, scratching his bicep for a few seconds, before sitting forward. 'The thing is D-Kay. I saw what you were like in PCW2, and you had the makings of being good. You seem to have taken that for granted, and not continued working to improve. That's your problem. You get to one level and stay there.' D-Kay nods his head in defeat. 'I know, I know. But I just don't feel motivated enough. I could do with a right royal kick up the ass.' 'Watch it D-Kay. You say it, and I may just make it come true.' Both men laugh as the waitress returns to clear the table. As she's there, Estep and Calyx both order another pot of coffee, waiting until the waitress has left again before resuming the conversation. 'Anyway. Enough of my moaning. I have a job for you if you want it.' Mark looks on slightly puzzled. 'What is it?' D-Kay smiles, noticing the worried look on his comrade's face. 'Don't worry Mark. It's not that bad. It's just that I have an old friend who lives near St. Louis, and I have a gift for her. It won't fit in my car, so I wondered if you'd carry it on the way to March Madness?' Mark tilts his head slightly in query to Calyx, who shakes his head in return. 'Listen, all it is, is some studio equipment I borrowed from her husband when I was DJ'ing. It's a computer, a mixing desk, and an amplifier. He used to have it, stopped producing, and now I've given up production, he may as well have it back.' Mark nods his head, and takes a cell phone from his pocket. 'I'll have to check first. Make sure I have no other deliveries, and if I can take it, I will do.' 'There's no rush Mark. I just want to get it there by this weekend. If you can't do it, then I'll hire a side loader.' Mark nods his head again, and holds a finger up to D-Kay, before climbing out of the booth, and walking off with his phone to his ears. D-Kay enjoys his coffee for a few minutes, before Estep returns. 'I'll have to have a look at it. I have another job this weekend, so I'd have to see if it will fit in the truck. If it does, I'll take it up for you.' 'Thanks man. I'd be driving there as well, so I can take you to where it has to be. But, if you want a look at it, do you want to come back over to my house, I've got it all boxed up, and you can see how large it is?' Mark nods his head again, and both men stand up out of the booth. D-Kay waves Marks hand away as he tries to leave some money, D-Kay leaving the payment for the second drinks and a tip instead. The camera follows as both men leave the cafe, D-Kay returning to his BMW, before following Estep to the truck park. Fade to black. (Voice over): 'That was it? A conversation with Mark Estep? Oh my God. Will somebody please shoot this guy? Or at least fire him so he doesn't waste any more PCW time. God he sucks more than Bad Battio...'
