Football, Copenhagen, and a phone call

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-8-02 RP 1 Turmoil 10th For years the state of Tennessee had no professional football team. With the success of the University of Tennessee football team and their loyal fans, one would thing the Volunteer State would have an equally loyalty and successful NFL team. Not until the 1998 season. Coming from the state of Texas, the Oilers took their move to Tennessee. The non so popular team may have hopes of gaining more respect and success. Their first year in the new state wasn’t that good and by the next year, the new Tennessee Oilers were changed to the Tennessee Titans. Perhaps it was the name change that gave the team good luck. They even made it to the Super Bowl a time or two while the University of Tennessee was going to the SEC Championships. We now go inside of a plane. Our very own, Teresa Tomas and her tag along friend, Jack Slayter are sitting in a private section of the jet. Teresa sits next to the window. Her face is lit up like a Christmas tree. Jack is sitting next to her flipping through an issue of a hunting magazine. Instead of his usual boots and Stetson, Jack is wearing steel toe work boots with his Wranglers, a Winston tee shirt with the sleeves cut out and a camouflage cap reading ‘Real Men Hunt’. Teresa on the other hand is wearing a Tennessee Titans jersey shirt with Levi’s and Nike’s covering her feet. “How in God’s creation did you come up with Titans tickets while we were in Canada?” Jack looks to Teresa with a sly grin. Even though Teresa doesn’t share the same feeling for him as he does her, they share the same interests and he’ll use anything to get closer to her. “Cause I’m good. I know how much you wanted to be at their first game. Since the game is in Nashville, I’m sure you will want to see you mother.” Teresa gives Jack a smile and returns her baby blue eyes back to the window. The clouds and blue sky remind her of the ocean and its waves. She reaches to her right hip for her cell phone only to be stopped with Jack’s hand cupping over her. Teresa looks up with a questionable expression over her surprised face. “I know who you’re calling Teresa. Don’t. Calling him will only lead you to getting hurt. If I have to console you on his behalf, Heaven help me, I’ll.. “You’ll do nothing! If you don’t get your hand off mine, I’ll break your fingers. You’re full of horse shit and I’ll call who ever the hell I want to. Got it?” Jack nods slowly removing his hand from hers. Once she finds out he’s been lying to her about Scott, he’s dead. Since there is no smoking on the plane, Jack pulls out a can of Copenhagen from his back pocket. As Teresa dials Scott’s number, he stuffs a wad of the snuff under his lip and reaches for his empty bottle of Mt. Dew hoping Scott doesn’t answer. Scott stands in front of a large glass window in what appears to be a large shopping mall. He is dressed in a pair of black slacks and a silk button down black shirt, casual yet stylish, which is extremely odd for him. His hair is pulled back into a simple ponytail and a pair of silver framed glasses cover his eyes, he looks a little too classy to be the same Scott Taylor that is the professional wrestler. He looks down at his waist and he hears his cell phone go off. He reaches down and pulls it from the clip on his waist, pressing a button and placing it to his ear. 'Hello?' Teresa feels her heart stop inside her chest. Hearing his voice after so many months nearly overwhelms her. By the expression on her face, Jack knows he has answered and takes in a deep breath before spitting into his bottle. Teresa finally speaks. 'Scott? It's me, Teresa.” Scott nearly falls to the floor; Teresa was the last person he was expecting to hear from today. He turns and looks away from the display window before speaking back into the phone. 'Teresa...it's been awhile. How have you been?' Teresa sits back in her seat feeling more relaxed now while Jack listens nervously. He remembers what he told Scott when he called Teresa a few days back and his too relaxes somewhat. 'I've been doing ok. I'm back in the states now.' “That's good. I didn't get to watch Turmoil since I was doing some press stuff for OSW, but it's good to hear you made it back ok.' Scott walks over to a nearby bench and sits down, crossing his legs before speaking again. 'But, I'm sure Jack Slayter kept you company. So, you couldn't have missed me THAT much.' Scott says with bitterness in his voice.” Teresa hesitates before responding. She then begins to wonder what Jack told him the other day. She licks her lips choosing not to even look at Jack until she hangs up the phone. 'Considering you're getting married to some crewgirl, I don't see why you would even care if I missed you.' 'WHAT?!' Scott screams out drawing the attention of everyone around him. He looks at the people, who are staring at him and sneers, before he speaks back into the receiver. 'Engaged? Since when?! I hardly know any of the backstage hands anyway. The only female in OSW I've even talked to was Amy Campbell, and her and I don't get along. I don't know what the hell bird told you that.' It takes all Teresa has at this moment not to knock Jack right out of his seat and push him out of the exit door of the jet without a parachute. She balls her free fist tightly and Jack now knows he's gonna get it. As Teresa responds, a soft growl is heard in her voice. 'That little bird happens to be non other than that soon to be dead Jack. I'm not even going to ask what he told you.' Scott shakes his head, feeling a little better after finding out that his old 'friend' wasn't telling the whole truth. With a sigh of relief Scott speaks back into the phone, a little cautious though. 'I see......well, I'm going to go. I still have some things to do. Take care, Teresa?' 'Yeah, me too. I... I want to see you again sometime Scott.' Scott blinks and is taken back. He hesitates for a moment before he replies, 'Uhhh.... Sure. I guess that'd be all right. If you don't mind seeing me again.' 'Ok talk to you later then.' Teresa presses the 'End' button on her cell phone, disconnection the call. She casually places her phone back in its place at her hip and gives Jack a slow dangerous glare. Jack doesn’t hesitate to grab his spit bottle, step into the small bathroom behind them and lock the door. He can’t stay in that bathroom forever or can he? She grabs two of his luggage bags from an overhead compartment and places them in front of the closed door. Teresa then returns to her seat and pulls out a book from her own bag. The only way Jack is coming out of there is if the stewardess lets him out. “The Jack off should know by now not to mess with me.
