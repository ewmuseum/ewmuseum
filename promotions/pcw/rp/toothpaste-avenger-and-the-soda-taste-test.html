Toothpaste Avenger and the Soda Taste Test

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Where, Why, Who, and How? The scene opens on a small convenience store where as we saw last time; Rush Mason, Jason Core, and Jordan X Shabazz are all together, only the setting has changed. Jordan is carrying the shopping basket, while Rush heads to the back of the store, J-Core following behind, most likely confused by Rush’s anti-logical thinking. J-Core: Why do you want to taste the other drinks? Why don’t you just make a whole bunch of flavors and then no one can complain? Rush stops walking and turns around to correct his friend. Rush: Not true. Someone can ALWAYS complain. If I made every single flavor, some Black Jewish Argentine would complain that I didn’t have Parisian Espresso Mocha Latte Flake Bean, and so on with the bullshit. Therefore, I have to make sure none of that other crap tastes anywhere near the grandness that is CrunKola. Rush begins to walk again towards the back of the store where the soda cabinets are. J-Core: Speaking of which, will CrunKola be allowed if Crunk has been banned? Rush: Meh, Kannon doesn’t scare me. J-Core: Heh, you seemed pretty scared to me when he banned it. Rush: An unintelligent slob would think so, yes. Jordan is still keeping quiet, while walking with the shopping basket up the aisle. Finally they arrive at a huge cooler with hundreds of different drinks inside. Rush: Okay, we’re looking for jobber sodas. What are they again Jordan? Jordan quickly rattles off the names of all the other drinks being represented. Jordan: CrunKola, Krylorian Cola, Kahlua Iced Coffee, Darkstar’s Carbonated Goat’s Blood, Fanta, Pink Lemonade, Snapple~!, Tahitian Treat, Brisk, Lucozade, Perrier, and Poison Sobe. J-Core: I don’t care if I’ve already seen it; it’s incredible the way he keeps information. Not even looking up, Jordan quickly blurts again to J-Core. Jordan: Oh and Jason? Your left shoe is untied. Jason looks at him skeptically and then looks down at shoe to find that, THERE IS A LIVING PERSON IN HIS SHOE EATING HIM FROM THE FEET DOWN AGGGGGGGGH! Just kidding of course, he finds that his left shoe is indeed untied. Rush: Will you stop with the queer circus main event? We’ve got some soda to buy here and I don’t need to spend all day searching for some drink that’s only drunken by white people who spend their entire day in Starbucks and the Gap. J-Core: What’re you talking about? That didn’t even make any sense! Rush ignores Jason and looks around, quickly grabbing a Raspberry Snapple~! From the shelf. Rush: Jordan, why’d you get that basket? Jordan: Usually when you buy something, you put it in a basket or a cart. What’s this? DAMN THOSE TOOTHPASTE MAKERS! Jordan has now become enraged as he sees the toothpaste aisle and sees that every single box of toothpaste tubes is white. Rush watches on with what can only be described as a proud look while Jordan slides the toothpaste off the shelf, shoving as much as he can onto the floor, while J-Core just rolls his eyes and finally Jordan walks back, panting. J-Core: Can we get going, or am I interrupting your fun, you two? Rush: Au contraire, j’adore le singe rouge et mechant! Rush doesn’t realize that he just said he loves the mean, red monkey, but it’s probably all for the better. He pops the top off of the Snapple~! And takes a swig, which soon exits his mouth in a mist/spit hybrid which he spits out quickly. Rush: GOOD GOD! That’s the most god-awful thing I’ve ever tasted! Are you saying that white people routinely enjoy that crap? Jordan: I’ve seen them. Once the Snapple comes out, it’s like when they get the KY Jelly at OG’s place, everything gets anal. J-Core tightens up his face in a look of disgust, but Rush just puts the top back on the Snapple~! And puts it back on the shelf, as J-Core rolls his eyes. J-Core: You’re putting that back on the shelf? Rush: Of course, those white people only buy it to look cool anyway; they throw it away when no one’s looking. Rush then reaches around and lands his hand onto Kahlua Iced Coffee. He takes a swig, and then begins a hacking cough, sounding like he’s coughing up blood. Rush: I’ve tasted better laxatives than that stuff! No wonder Hitler killed himself if he had to be drinking that stuff. That’s probably why he talked all crazy too. Beishchenswag! J-Core: Rush, that’s German. Rush: No shit? Wow, I always thought they were speaking that gibberish shit that all teenage girls speak at those big meetings. J-Core sighs as Rush throws the Kahlua Iced Coffee into another aisle, where it presumably smashes open from the sound that can be heard after it lands. Jordan: We’re running on a tight schedule now. By the time that maintenance gets around to that, they’ll be kicking us out of the store. J-Core: How’d you know that? Rush: He’s also our getting kicked out of places expert. I don’t know where he gets this stuff. Rush has to search this time to find the right drink, but finally he finds the next drink on the shelf. He sees the Fanta and picks it up. He opens the white cap and takes a sip, after which he has a pondering look on his face. Rush: Well, it’s not CrunKola worthy; I suppose it’s not awful. Who’s fighting for this stuff? Jordan chimes in from the other side of aisle. Jordan: The End, former EW- Rush: S’ok, Jordan. I don’t need his entire bio. Well if End’s fighting for it, that puts it out of the running as well. You know, he could really stand to spice himself up, he’s so boring. J-Core: Actually- Rush: Just stop where you are. You’re going to say SOMETHING that has some sort of relevance but you’ll just be greeted by Mr. I Don’t Care, so don’t even bother. J-Core: Is Ignorance really bliss Rush? Rush is now the one whose ignoring and he quickly spots a bottle of Brisk. He gulps down the whole thing then turns to the camera. Rush: NOW THAT’S BRISK BABY! Wait, I mean…uggh…that tastes like a mixture between bad and awful. Man, I’ve had enough crappy drinks for one day. Jordan: Rush, you could still get some Pink Lemonade here, though I don’t know where you’ll find the rest. Rush: That’s ok, Queer Circus Cola won’t be winning anything. I’ll find the rest at a specialty store or something. The three walk towards the front of the store. J-Core: Rush, you realize that Poison Sobe will kill you if you drink it? Rush: Yeah, right, like PCW would let something that kills people be associated with it. Somewhere, Clyde smiles a big evil smile, as the scene fades to black…
