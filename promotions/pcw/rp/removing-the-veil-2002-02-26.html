Removing the Veil

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th Febuary 2002 He sits in front of a mirror. He sits there alone, probably unwashed and well... more alone. Sorry, crappy narrator. He stands up and turns, his hands reaching towards his head, touching the bandages lightly with his fingers. He brushes down lightly his white silk shirt and his white leather trousers... almost verging on Angel like. The room is almost completely white, with white tiles on the walls and white grouting also. The camera pans around and reveals the reason... it’s the bathroom in the Asylum gym. The camera pans around some more and shows the door to be open and standing in that open doorway is Natalia Miyagawa, herself dressed in a red halter top and red kilted skirt. She looks towards her husband with concern as he touches the bandages some more. Natalia: Bathroom looks good. Miyagawa: The workmen did a good job. Although they took my colour scheme a bit literarily considering all I said was “I dunno what colour it should be... just stick with white.” I kinda expected them to put something that wasn’t white in it. Natalia: The taps are stainless steel... and whose idea was the Jacuzzi? Miyagawa: That was mine. They wanted to install one of those French bum washing things, but I said it wasn’t worth it. I said to get them to install the Jacuzzi instead, although this one is only big enough for two. When they install the pool in the back garden in a couple weeks, I’ll get them to put an outdoor one that can seat ten people. Natalia: We’re getting a pool? Miyagawa: Oh yeah, good for training a pool is. Besides, this is summer and this is Anaheim. I expect that we’ll be spending a lot of time in the water. Hence why there’s going to be a Hawaiian style pool bar too and beach house. Natalia: How can we have a beach house without a beach? Miyagawa: You just make it out of driftwood and bamboo. Instant beach house. Steve’s taking cocktail lessons at the moment. Natalia: Ah, so that’s where the old geezer has got to. I was wondering. Miyagawa: He’s also doing a Law degree at night school. He’s trying out a new gimmick; he wants to be like I.R.S. but as a Lawler. Might work... I guess if he looses, he can find a way to bring a law suit against his opponent. Miyagawa strokes the bandages with his hands. Miyagawa: I think it’s time to come out of this towelling wrapped prison. My face needs to breathe, it needs air. Clyde has destroyed my face for long enough, and it’s time for it to come back into the light. Natalia: Well with the neon in here, it’s certainly the correct room for it anyway. Miyagawa removes the pin from the bandages and begins to unwrap the bandages from his face. Little by little his face is revealed to the world... minus the beard, and a few scars along the edges from the skin graphs. He throws the bandages to the ground, happy to be rid of them. Miyagawa: How is it? Natalia looks at Miyagawa worried for a second as he was unwrapping but the delight on her face as she sees her husband’s face has almost been completely restored to its former glory. Miyagawa: Nat? Natalia smiles at him with sheer happiness. Natalia: It’s good. There are some scars along the side, but they’ll go once the stitches are removed. There is one thing I’ve been waiting to do ever since Clyde did that to you. Miyagawa: Which is? Natalia walks forward, barefoot over the tiled floors towards her husband, Miyagawa. She leans upwards towards him, their lips meeting for the first time in a couple of weeks. She moves her hands up and cups his face; he suddenly makes a growl of pain, but continues with the kiss until it breaks naturally. Natalia: Sorry, I didn’t realise it would still be so tender. Miyagawa: It was worth it. Besides, at least it proves they connected the nerves up correctly. I guess that means no more no selling entire matches for me, although it was rather funny at the time. There goes that whole Miya-Streak idea. Natalia: Much to the relief of the populace, eh? Miyagawa: Yeah. All I’ve got to do now is to kill the stigmata of the Miyagawa-Porter connection... Boda is the natural person to inherit the Howard Porter tag; not me. It’s time to prove that. More people have jumped ship to OSW, and still PCW does nothing... it’s time that PCW had a strong leader who leads from the front, rather than from the back. When PCW calls, I answer. PCW called about the TV title division being poor, so I stepped in using Duke William’s offer, and saved the division. Now Snow isn’t showing up to much, and so I step in once again, to save the day. Natalia: Are you sure you’re not just Rent a Hero minus the mask? Miyagawa: Well I could just get the costume out for old time’s sake. Now that I’m pretty, oh so pretty, oh so pretty and pretty and glee... sorry... just felt like breaking out into song. Miyagawa turns back to the mirror and touches his chin lightly with his hand while looking deeply at his own reflection. Miyagawa: Well Tim Walden and Drake Maxwell had better be worried because let’s face it... the face is back! And you know why? Natalia: I do. Miyagawa: No, you’ve got to set me up for my catchphrase. Natalia: Oh right, sorry. Miyagawa: So are you going to set me up or not? Natalia: Why? Miyagawa: Because... I’m... Excellent. Well we got there in the end. And that’s a grand morale to a story for certain. Natalia: Da. The camera fades out as the husband and wife hug, happy to see that Miyagawa is once again well.
