Snow Joins the KKK!?!?!?!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered The scene opens on the street of Woodbury, which is located in one of the filthier trailer parks in all of West Virginia... Snow: “Well it IS the official Trailer Park state... The Killin’ Villain, Jason Snow, is sitting on the headrest inside his rented convertable. The red car gleams under the sweltering West Virginia sun. The driver of the car is none other than Mark Malloy, who seems to have finally forgiven Snow the disgrace of the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World. At the moment, that title is resting on Snow’s broad shoulder... Malloy drives the car nervously - cars like this usually get quite a bit of attention on spots where the most luxurious vehicle is a fifteen year old truck with chains on the tires. He glances over at Snow... at least that’s one thing he has going for him. He’s confident that Snow could take any man who dared try him... what he’s not confident in, however, is that Snow will defend HIM as well... Malloy: “I still don’t understand what we’re doing here, Snow.” The Killin’ Villain scowls... Snow: “How many times must I tell you, simpleton? Malloy rolls his eyes at Snow’s response - he should have known it was coming. He also knows that there are still more insults to come as Snow explains their reasons for being in this area... Snow: “I’m here to promote my match for Supremacy! I want the entire WORLD to see what I’m going to do to that oversized worm in a toga and that other oversized worm in a business suit!” Malloy: “You want to promote the match... here?” Snow: “Damn you Malloy! Have a heart! I don’t discriminate! Be they illiterate, jobless, swamp sows or filthy drunken trailer pigs - the Killin’ Villain will grace them all with his presence at Supremacy.” Malloy rolls his eyes and looks out the window at the trailers that they are passing. Broken shutters, yellow-tinged siding... he can’t imagine how any of them can afford to buy a pay per view event. Cautiously, he voices his opinion to the high-mounted Snow... Malloy: “You know, Snow - I really don’t know if these people have the money to order a pay per view...” Snow: “Nonsense! If they can’t afford to order a pay per view, then how in the hinges of hell can they afford all that beer!?” Malloy frowns - Snow has him there. Beer bottles are strewn across the many lawns. A few shirtless men are sitting in lawn furniture, staring at the convertable as it passes. ... Snow looks rather happy on this day - which is actually a rather odd sight. His long blond hair flows lightly with the breeze, and his cold blue eyes are covered by a pair of dark sunglasses... Malloy: “So... do you want me to stop the car?” Snow: “No need for that, peasant. Watch and learn...” Snow clumsily turns around and reaches into the tiny backseat of the rented car. He produces a rather large megaphone which makes Malloy’s jaw drop... Malloy: “W-what’s that for!?” Snow: “What the hell does it look like it’s for? I’m going to spread the message of my Greatness and Superiority! As soon as I get all the formalities out of the way, I’ll let them know how much joy I’m going to get out of planting Snake Skin boots into the asses of Big Alan Steele and Tyler Nelson.” Malloy cringes as he looks to his left. There’s a rather large man, wearing only an open bathrobe over his underwear, barbequing in his front yard. As Snow raises the megaphone, Malloy can’t help but hope that he doesn’t do anything too over the top... Snow: “Attention trailer trash, swamp swine, and scum of the Earth...” Malloy ducks in his seat. Not only was that over the top, it also may prove to be downright life-threatening. However, there’s nothing that Malloy can do. As he continues to cower behind the wheel, the PCW Champion continues... Snow: “...I, the Killin’ Villain and PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World, encourage you all to whore out your mothers, sisters, or children if you must - and buy PCW Supremecy, live, May 12, and only on Pay Per View.” Malloy, thinking that he has a plan, eases down on the accelerator and speeds the car up slightly - he’s hoping that if he can get into the next part of the trailer park, they may not have heard the cocky young PCW Champion’s statement. Snow, however, will have none of it. Without thinking, the Killin’ Villain aims the megaphone in the direction of Malloy’s ear and screams... Snow: “SLOW DOWN PEASANT!” Malloy nearly leaps out of the car and finds himself close to deafness. Not quite deaf, however, because he can hear Snow continue to shout through the megaphone... Snow: “Damn it, plebe! Do you not know that years of inbreeding have left the majority of these disgusting trailer dwellers without either the capacity to hear or comprehend me unless I speak slowly and repetitively?” Malloy turns to his left and spots an angry bald man stepping out of his trailer, wondering what all the commotion is about. Snow looks back out over his audience with a warm smile on his face and soon begins speaking to them again... Snow: “As you can well see, I am far Superior to all of you. But do not run in fear of your lives, for I come in peace.” Suddenly, a serious expression comes over the young World Champion’s face... Snow: “However, I simply MUST ask any females out there to divert their eyes from me every now and again because - yes - the mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!” A few of the rednecks that surround the care exchange glances. Snow doesn’t seem to notice that they’re becoming hostile. He raises his megaphone to his lips one more time... Snow: “On a more pleasant note - and not to take ANYTHING from the seriousness of my Orgasm-causing Greatness - PCW Supremacy will take place on May 12, 2002, just three days before I, the Superior Champion of the World and the most SUCCESSFUL man in PCW history, enter into the twenty-fourth year of my Superior life.” Malloy spots a man ahead departing his porch and holding a shotgun. Our PCW camera man, who has his camera positioned between Snow and Malloy, catches Malloy beginning to sweat. Snow, on the other hand, doesn’t notice that anything’s wrong. He continues his speech... Snow: “You see - through your good eye - I just so happen to be the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World. And on that fateful night at Supremacy, I will be defending against the scoundrels, Big Alan Steele and Tyler Nelson.” Malloy: “Snow, I think you’ve made your point... why don’t we...” Malloy is surprised to see most of the residents quickly make their way back into their trailers. Why? He soon sees the reason. Directly ahead, standing in the middle of the road, are two average looking men. Average or not, everyone in the trailer park seems afraid of them. When Snow finally notices them ahead, he smiles and tosses the megaphone behind him into the tiny backseat... Snow: “Ah! Stop the car, peasant! Those disgusting creatures ahead must be the head-swine around here!” Malloy: “Snow, I don’t think that’s such a good...” Snow interrupts... Snow: “Peasant!” When Malloy looks back up at the Killin’ Villain, he finds himself under the heavy fire of those cold blue eyes. It’s impossible to cross Snow without having to meet up with those. There may not be a more intimidating set of eyes in all the world... Snow: “I said... stop... the... CAR!” Obediently, the young PCW Analyst brings the car to a halt. The Champ hops over the passenger seat door, landing on the dirt road. He then tosses the PCW title back inside the car and approaches the men in the road - a broad smile on his face... Snow: “Hello there!” Snow stops dead in his tracks and scowls... Snow: “By Greatness, you smell like you’ve spent the day in a sewer and bathed it off in your own sweat!” One of the men is an older man with snow white hair parted to the side. He is wearing a set of crooked glasses as well as a plaid shirt and suspenders. With a warm smile, he extends his hand to the Champ. Snow doesn’t take this gesture too kindly... Snow: “Stay back! By Greatness, your odor is overpowering enough from here! Malloy! Shake the man’s hand!” Malloy, still afraid, steps up to shake the man’s hand. When he looks down, he says that the hand in question is covered in what appears to be tar and grease. Shaking the hand, however, is far better than any consequences that he thinks likely if he were to refuse... the two men shake hands... Unknown Man: “The name’s earl. This here’s Billy Bob.” Earl points a thumb over his shoulder at the second man - large, barely dressed, and thoroughly disgusting. In response, Billy Bob spits... Earl: “Now, I couldn’t help but overhear you as you was comin’ up... you’re in... with the brotherhood, am I right?” Snow: “The Brotherhood?” Snow narrows his eyes, unsure what the older man is talking about. Suddenly a notion hits him... Snow: “Oh! You must mean the Brotherhood of Great and Superior Individuals! Why yes, by Greatness, not only am I a member, I’m the Greatest of them all!” Earl seems genuinely impressed. Billy Bob, who one second appears to be as old as fourty, and the next could be mistaken to be a man in his early twenties, spits again. Malloy is pale white... Earl: “Never heard of them... I did here you mention...” Earl looks around cautiously... Billy Bob spits... Earl: “Supremacy.” Snow: “Ah yes! PCW Supremacy!” Earl: “PCW Supremacy?” Earl looks back at his saliva hurling companion and then back at Snow and Malloy. He puts his hands in his pockets and a warm look comes over his face. Suddenly, Malloy feels a little more at ease... Earl: “Can’t say I’ve ever heard of that branch... PCW... what’s that stand fer? Well, I know what the ‘W’ stands fer, what about the ‘PC’?” Malloy’s brow wrinkles. How can these men not know about PCW? If they are, indeed, wrestling fans? Well, they DID know that the ‘W’ obviously stood for wrestling... unless they thought it stood for something else... Suddenly Malloy turns as pale as an albino ghost... ‘PC’.... ‘W’.... Supremacy... ‘W’ Supremacy... he feels a pain in his throat and a sickness in his stomach... Snow: “Well, if I had MY say - and by Greatness, one day I will - the PC will one day be SG. SGW. Superiority and Greatness...” Earl interrupts... Earl: “Well hell, that IS a fine name for a branch now, ain’t it? Ain’t it Billy Bob?” Billy Bob spits. Malloy is having a hard time standing up. He knows what Snow is way too naive to realize - these people are WHITE SUPREMACISTS!! Earl: “You know though, son, a fine young boy like you could always join our branch - out here in West Virginia.” Snow’s eyes narrow... Snow: “Branch of what?” Suddenly, Earl realizes that he’s not dealing with an average person. He uses his hands to emphasise something that Snow should already have known... Earl: “The Brotherhood?” Snow smiles brightly and snaps his fingers... Snow: “OH! The Brotherhood of Superiority and Greatness! Well of COURSE I’ll join! How can I possibly turn down an offer to be recognized in yet ANOTHER society for Superior and Great Individuals quite like myself.” Earl: “Well, of course, we’d have to take a vote first, but I don’t see a problem with it.” Malloy feels as if he is about to become physically ill. He knows exactly what Earl and Billy Bob are involved in - Snow, on the other hand, has absolutely no idea. As far as he’s concerned, this is just another venture to feed his massive ego. As Earl scratches his chin and sizes up the young PCW Champ, the scene fades to black... Later... Black screen... Malloy: “Snow! You don’t understand! This isn’t what you think it is - these people AREN’T who you think they are!” The scene opens inside what appears to be a large warehouse in some unknown location. Snow and Malloy stand behind a curtain, though from our current view, we can see that just beyond that current there is a large gathering of all sorts of hillbilly and redneck... Snow: “What are you blabbering about now, plebe?” Malloy slaps his forehead with his hand. He realizes that he can’t approach the subject lightly with Snow for much longer. Quickly, he looks around at their surroundings. Just beyond the curtain, in full view of the gathered mass, there is a large wooden swastika. Malloy feels ill, but he has to point it out to Snow... Malloy: “Snow! Look at that! For the love of God, Snow! Don’t you know what that is!” Snow’s eyes narrow and he scratches his clean-shaven chin... Snow: “By Greatness...” Malloy breathes a sigh of relief, thinking he’s finally gotten through to the young wrestling superstar... Snow: “Why, that’s the most unusual coat rack I’ve ever seen! It’s a damn good thing it’s there though, I’m dying of heat in here.” Malloy begins to sweat. He grabs the Killin’ Villain by the arms and begins shaking him - he never would have thought he would attempt something so brave. Snow glares down at him, his teeth showing slightly... Snow: “Have you any mind for what you’re DOING, peasant!?” Malloy: “Snow! You’ve GOT to listen to me! DON’T go out there! These people are...” Snow interrupts by swatting the physically unimpressive PCW analyst aside... Snow: “Away with you, peasant! I’m about to be introduced!” Indeed, Snow is right. The camera peeks to our right to find Earl, Billy Bob, and another ten men - all clad in white robes - standing at the front of the stage with their congregation below. Earl raises his right hand and the congregation gives him a straight-arm salute... Everything grows eerily quiet... we, however, can hear Malloy begin to hyper-ventilate... Earl: “Gentlemen! On this evenin’, I would like to bring forth a man of Great power.” Though we can no longer see Snow, we hear his low-pitched voice repeat the compliment... Snow: “A man of GREAT power!” Earl continues... Earl: “And tonight, I would like to ask that we make him part of our branch. Let me introduce to you all... Jason Snow.” The congregation is silent as Snow makes his long walk across the stage - stopping only once to hang his jet black leather jacket on the swastika. The only sound in the room comes from his slow, heavy, footsteps. He raises his arm to the congregation, who nod in agreement, mistaking the gesture for a straight-arm salute. The congregation are all dressed in full Klu Klux Klan uniforms - headgear and all... Snow murmurs to himself... Snow: “Hmm... Odd attire for Superior individuals... unless... OH! The mere sight of THEM must be KNOWN to cause ORGASMS as well! That would explain the color... We can hear Malloy panting just behind the camera man. The Killin’ Villain stands beside Earl, who smiles and pats him on the shoulder. What’s that in the corner? Snow narrows his eyes as he notices a burning cross at the far end of the warehouse, in a corner behind the congregation... he mutters to Earl... Snow: “That’s an interesting choice of illumination.” Earl: “It shall illuminate everythin’, chil’ ...” Snow narrows his eyes at Earl... Snow: “Has West Virginia not yet been introduced to the light bulb?” Snow shrugs... Snow: “Wouldn’t surprise me.” Earl pays no attention to the comment. He turns back to his congregation and holds his hands high once more... Earl: “Are there any questions from the floor, before we take the vote?” There’s a loud rumbling from the audience while Snow smiles on the stage - he is, of course, one of the most naive people on the face of the planet. Snow believes that everything that goes on around him is there to simply feed his own ego. He was once quoted as saying the sun does not rise in the morning until he opens his eyes... One man, near the middle f the white-clad crowd, puts his hand up and asks in a loud, bellowing voice... KKK Member: “What do you believe in?” Snow thinks the question is an odd one indeed, but he knows a chance to brag about himself when he sees it. The Killin’ Villain puts his hand over his heart, looks up to the lights - and then realizing that they come from a burning cross in the corner, he turns his attention there... Snow: “I - the Killin’ Villain, Jason Snow... also known as the Leather Clad Devil, the Schemin’ Demon, and the Man Who Causeth Multiple Orgasms - believe that I am far Superior to all others...” There is a positive air about the crowd. Malloy must be having a heart attack by now... Snow: “And futrhermore, I believe that all others are far inferior to myself and my Greatness! Why, they should be THANKFUL that I ALLOW them to breathe the same air as I do! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it!” It happened. Snow has just said the one thing that can both be seen as extreme Egotism and extreme White Supremacy. The congregation breaks out into cheers for the young man, who they think is one of their own. In the background, Earl, Billy Bob, and the other ten men have huddled together. The huddle only lasts a moment, and Earl steps back out... Earl: “Men, may I present to you... our NEWEST member!” The congregation grows louder with their cheers and Earl pats Snow roughly on the shoulder. A moment later, Billy Bob approaches, holding a folded KKK uniform in his outstretched arms... Earl: Congratulations, son.” Snow looks down at the KKK Uniform... Snow: “Is this what I think it is?” He pulls the uniform from Billy Bob’s arms and holds it up to look at... Snow: “It IS!” He turns to Earl... Snow: “My very own Orgasm-Protection suit!” In the background, we hear Malloy slap his forehead again. Back on stage, Earl, who’s laughing, pats Snow on the pack and gestures to the suit... Earl: “Go on - try it on!” Smiling, Snow does just that. He puts the long white robe on, and then the long, pointed, white cap. He looks almost like a cartoon character wearing his KKK uniform. He holds an arm up to the congregation - they return a straight-arm salute... Snow: “That’s odd... We can sense Snow’s eyes narrowing as he watches the KKK congregation salute him. A moment later, Earl comes back and puts a red band around his right arm. Snow looks down at the band and sees the swastika that’s been sewn onto it... Snow: “Why would is the coat rack sewn onto this arm-ba... oh...” Snow’s head snaps up and we can see him physically begin to tremble. He turns his head toward the swastika, which he thinks is a coat rack. In fact, his own leather jacket is still hanging from the edge of it... Snow: “Oh...” Snow turns his attention to the burning cross in the far corner of the room and suddenly realizes that it’s NOT simply a source of light... Snow: “...Oh my...” Finally, Snow looks out onto the congregation, who are all wearing their KKK Uniforms - which he suddenly realizes are NOT Orgasm-Protection suits. They are all giving him the straight-arm salute... FINALLY Snow puts it all together... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS!!!” As quickly as he can, he pulls the KKK mask off, tosses it aside, and begins to struggle with the long white robe. The congregation doesn’t seem to like any of this. Snow doesn’t seem to care... He screams out at them... Snow: “By Greatness! YOU’RE not Superior at all! You’re nothing more than... than...” When he can think of no insult, he trips over the robe and falls onto his side... Snow: “OH DAMN YOU ALL!!! DAMN YOU ALL TO HEEEEELLLLL!!!!” Finally, Snow works his way out of the KKK Uniform and sprints off the stage - grabbing his jet black leather jacket in the process... In much the same fashion, he grabs Malloy and drags him like a ragdoll out of the warehouse and into the dirt-street. With a hundred angry White Supremacists on their tail, Snow tosses Malloy into the passenger seat of the car... Snow: “Damn you, peasant! Why didn’t you TELL me what kind of people these maggots are!”Malloy: “I...” Snow interrupts... Snow: “I mean, YOU’RE a peasant! You should know about the inferior affairs of these hillbilly swine! Damn you Malloy! This is all YOUR fault!” Malloy sighs - there’s nothing much more one can do in his shoes. Jason Snow is a man that’s impossible to argue with. The PCW Champion leaps over the driver’s side door and lands behind the wheel. He starts the car and the speed away from all of the angry white Supremacists... They did, however, forget one thing... the camera man... fade to black...
