Ellie~! Teaches Prep 101

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/02/02 Preps, you gotta love the. On every corridor of every High School in the world they lurk, ready to share their wisdom with other less fortunate students. If you have bad hair, they’ll mock you, tearing in like a pack of Hyenas while flicking their hair at you to exclamate their point. If your family are poor they’ll remind you of it, mocking the cheaper items you own before driving off in their expensive cars they were bought as a reward for getting their license. If you listen to introverted music and your entire wardrobe consists of Hoodies and baggy jeans, their insults will try and broaden your mind. Just like every school, every Fed needs a Prep, someone to give their valued opinions on the attitudes, clothes and tastes off the roster....Telling them how they should act to reach the next social scale, PCW has it’s own Prep, Xavier Kannon’s valet Eleanor Hall aka Ellie~! The Evil Super Prep is ever ready to speak her mind, having been raised the way every child should, having been in the in-crowd every kid wanted to be in, and now she’s with the wrestler everyone else wants to be...Wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon. So how fitting that Xavier’s next opponents are 2 of PCW’s stars most in need of help, social outcasts both by fate and choice, perfect fodder for Ellie~! to preach to the introverted. Because let’s face it, nothing makes a Prep feel better about themselves than to point out the inadequacies of others. We open up on Xavier Kannon, wrestling’s only Scientologist. He is sitting at a High School style desk, a Black plastic chair with a small wooden table attacked at one side looking forward at something, studying it attentively with his piercing Green eyes. His is wearing his trademark long brown leather coat which falls down around the sides on the chair, under it he has a Black silk shirt with Green and Purple fractal patterns. With these he has a pair of his exclusively made Black boot cut jeans with Purple and Green intricate embroidery running down the left leg and a pair of Black, Purple and Grey Skechers are on his feet. His long Auburn hair is neatly tied back and falls down his neck. We follow his line of site and see that he is looking at his valet, the spokesperson for Snapple~! and the Evil Super Prep herself, Eleanor Hall, or Ellie~! She is wearing a White, Purple and Black geometrically patterned sleeveless top and a pair of Blue sandblasted jeans, held up with a Rainbow coloured fabric belt. Her long, wavy Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights is loose, cascading perfectly in place down her back. Beside her stands a slightly shorter woman who appears to be a teacher, looking out over the class that Xavier’s seat flanks, containing a typical mix of High School kids, from the Preps to the Nerds, from the Goths to the Jocks, all forming their little cliques and showing disdain for the others. “Welcome to this special Prep 101 Class,” announces the teacher as Ellie~! stands proudly behind her, “Now taking this lesson is someone I’m sure you all know of, she puts the Prep in Preppy, I present to you miss Eleanor Hall.” The Preps in the front right corner give Ellie~! a round of applause and in return she gives them a little excited wave. The Jocks turn to each other and nod, checking out her rack. The goths look at her and wish they were as skilled with make-up as her and the nerds try not to shoot their wads at being so close to a real attractive woman. “Thank you Mrs Teacher woman,” Ellie~! starts with, “Now, the first thing you have to remember, and this will be your mantra....Preppyness is a Virtue. All of those who are not openly Preppy, don’t be ashamed, you should never be embarrassed that you were born to a better class of family, that your parents actually happen to have jobs that they don’t drive a pick-up truck, that the bath isn’t just there to wash the dogs in....We shall not be oppressed, we shall share our views and enforce them on others because quite frankly we are better than them!” The Preps again turn to each other and nod in agreement at her words as the Goths look at her in disgust. “Can I have a volunteer please?” she then asks, looking out into the room as all the Preps raise their arms right away, hoping to be picked by the queen of Preps herself. “No, not you lot, someone who has something wrong with them.” They put their hands down with broad grins on their face at the compliment. Ellie~!’s pale Blue eyes scan the room and then lock on one of the nerdy kids at the front. “How about......YOU!” she snaps, “So what’s your name? Fergus? Albert? Miles? Something nerdy?” Ellie~! then looks up to the class who are either fascinated or appalled, “Look at him...Yes, I know it’s hard but if you want to pass the class you have to. Look at his clothes, A horrible tank top over a cheap checked shirt, can you say EUGH?” The kid looks down at his desk through his thick rimmed glasses. “That is who you DON’T want to be....” Ellie~! states, “Do you want to go to Chess Club with him? Do you WANT to watch Voyager with him and watch him drool over that robot one? Do you want to drive with his Mom in their cheap car to the Mall so she can buy him discount clothes?” She looks out to the Preps who are shaking their heads and sneering at the child. “No....I don’t think you do!” she looks at the child who’s bottom lip is quivering as he feels the eyes of the rest of the class burning holes in his back. Remember, and you should listen to this Fergus, grades are nod everything. Remember you personality and your parent’s money will also help you get into a good college so you don’t need to cast aside your social attributes and become like Fergus here....You aren’t the ones who need to change...THEY do!” The Goths in the back left corner look at the bright and bubbly Ellie~! with disdain for her and all she stands for. “Oh, great....” she sighs, “The happy brigade are here...” the Prep sneers as she looks into the corner of Black. “What ARE you miserable about? Because if it’s about your clothes...I SO feel your angst, Black is SO not the new Black, in fact, Black hasn’t been Black for about 4 years now...KEEP UP!” “You know who you remind me of?” she asks them, actually looking insulted when gone of them raises an answer. “You all remind me of Drake Maxwell! You sit there looking unhappy, dressed like a homeless person, what is it you have a problem with? What is stopping you being normal?” She stands before them, shaking her head at their lack on interaction. “Work with me here?” she asks, “This is for YOU, not ME! Pretend this room here is SOCIETY! Start to interact, listen to music everyone can relate to, something sunny and happy you can bounce to...Not Mosh....Something you can listen to driving to the beach with the roof down, not your shouting and...Well, I don’t even know your stupid little words for it all.” “Well, if you don’t want to help yourselves then I am not going to help YOU...” Ellie~! says, turning her nose up at them, “But don’t come crawling to me afterwards and asking what’s the best waterproof mascara is...” she then looks over to the Jocks. “Now, onto you boys, you are so nearly there!” The Jocks are in the far right corner near Xavier, their proximity to the Preps indicating their closeness to the top of the social order. “Now, you need to cut back on the beer from cans....Keggers were created for a reason you know!” they watch attentively, mostly staring at Ellie’s rack than heeding her words. “Then, lose the sports clothes all of the time..PLEASE?!?!?! Wear tracksuit bottoms if you are actually going to run, if not, Denim didn’t make a comeback for nothing you know?” At the back of the room the teacher is looking on, wide eyed in horror at the words coming from Eleanor’s mouth and into the ears if impressionable teenagers. The Preps are about to burst into a round of applause as one of the metal kids finally speaks out. “Where do you get off telling us what to do lady?” the kid challenges, “We are our own people, we don’t want to follow the crowds and become just like everyone else!” Ellie~! shakes her head a the kid in disappointment. “Don’t you see you ARE following a crowd? It’s just a crowd of people like you that wear ill fitting clothes and listen to stupid music!” she states, “So it is a little case of the Miserable pot calling the happy kettle Black don’t you think?” the kid doesn’t reply and just shakes his head, “Well thank someone you have a few differences to Drake otherwise he would have rambled on for 5 years about how my attitude reminds him of a broken vase that he saw when he was 3 or something....His symbolism SO needs to be re-routed!” “You know what is the one thing those of you in here crawling up the bottom rungs of the social ladder have going for you?” she asks in closing as the teacher looks to be dreading what she might say, “That none of you are blind, at least you still have a grip on that ladder and not falling off it!” she adds as the teacher slaps her forehead. One of the children raises their hands. “Miss, I’m blind!” he says nervously. “Well, I’m sorry, but that is your problem!” she says uncaringly. “Keep to yourself, don’t be a burden on your friends that actually want to be someone other than another carriage on your freak train! At least you aren’t wearing a Gold mask or anything that makes you look like a cheap post-modernist horror movie spoof stabber sequel’s remake.....And my I say, that looks a quality cane....Gucci does a nice one.” The Preps than actually do a quick discussion of Gucci’s Winter collection of canes. “Well, I’m sorry....” Ellie~! says, “I’m afraid Prep 101 is over now....You, you, you, you , you and.....Um, you, the Jock with the bottle of Snapple~! You all pass, well done....The rest of you, well you failed, and seeing as you have to pass everything to graduate....I’ll look forward to seeing the Goths sell me the latest O-Town single, the Jocks pump my gas and the nerds sell me my next TV....Bye!” She gives them a quick wave and the Preps stand and give her an extremely fast paced round of applause, all perked up by Ellie’s speeches. “Come on...” she gestures to Xavier who pulls himself up and walks across the room, “We have to catch a flight to Calgary.....I hope you found this insightful!” she adds as she and Xavier leave the room, leaving the teacher to pick up the shreds as the shot fades out.
