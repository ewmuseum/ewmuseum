Business as usual...

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-30-03 The sun slowly begins to rise in the Western sky and breaking through the clouds it shines down upon one Jaslene Myst. She’s wearing a pair of plaid pajama pants with a white tank top. The goose bumps on her arms begin to fade as the sun’s rays reach her. She smiles as she picks her head up off of a deck chair. She brushes her blonde hair behind her ears. Jaslene reaches down and takes a letter from her lap. It’s addressed to Adam. The envelope is already open as she reaches in and pulls out a letter. She unfolds it and reads it as the sun shines through the letter. Jaslene lets out a sigh as she lets it fall to the ground. The letter lands face up, the header reads, ’Primetime Championship Wrestling’. Jaslene gets out of the deck chair and stands. She lifts her arms over her head and, arching her back, stretches out. She lets out a groan before placing her hands down on the wooden plank that forms the top of the barrier to the deck. The sun reflects off of the grass making i Ët come to life in a lush green. The smile on Jaslene’s face has slowly dissipated as she hears a door open behind her. She turns her head slightly to the right. “Jas?” a voice says in the middle of a yawn. Adam appears in the doorway leading out to the deck in a pair of black mesh shorts. “What are you doing up so early? It’s not even six fifteen yet.” She pays no attention to him. He steps out into the light and brings his right hand up to block the rising sun. He scratches himself and stretches before walking across the deck to stand behind Jaslene. “Is something wrong Jas?” he asks finally noticing her attitude. “Was it something I did?” Jaslene turns around quickly and points to the letter, “What’s that?” She points before placing her hands on her hips. Adam turns to see what she was pointing at and notices the letter. A look of concern comes to his face as he slowly backs away from her. “Now Jas,” he says holding his hands out in defense, “Before you say anything just hear me out.” She stomps her foot into the ground, “Damnit Adam! You can’t stay away can you?” Jaslene asks crossing the deck and heading back inside. Adam is quick to follow her back into the house. She walks into another room and slams the door shut behind her. Adam stands outside the door. “Come on Jas!” he says as he smacks the door a couple of times with the face of his palm. “You can’t be mad at me for long! I know you well enough to know you can’t!” A toilet flushes as running water can be heard through the door. After a couple of moments the door is pulled open as Jaslene pushes her way past Adam and heads into a walk in closet. She begins to rifle through some tops hanging in a row. “Adam, don’t push me!” she says not even bothering to look at him. “You knew how I felt about you leaving the ring. I fully supported you. But you also know how I feel about you going back.” “Jas,” Adam says reaching out grabbing her shoulders. “What is so wrong with me wanting to get back in the ring?” Jaslene pulls a mi •dnight blue top down. Holding it by the hanger, she looks it over. Taking it over to a mirror she holds it up to her shoulders and sticks her tongue out in disgust. She quickly places it back on the rack and beings to look for something different. “Um...Jas?” he says after a few moments of her ignoring his last question. “Are you going to answer my question?” “I don’t know Adam.” she says pulling a crimson red long sleeve shirt from the rack. “I don’t know if I can without saying somethings I might regret later.” She walks over to the mirror again and places the shirt over the front of her body. Jaslene turns her head to the left and then to the right before nodding. She takes the shirt off of the hanger and walks back into the bedroom. Adam is quick to follow. “What do you mean?” he says as she tosses the shirt down onto the bed. “Jas you know me better than that. There’s nothing you can say that would make me change the way I feel about you. So if you have something to sa y then just come on out with it.” Jaslene stops and slowly turns to face Adam. She takes in a deep breath. “Adam, I have nothing against you wanting to get back into the ring.” she starts, “I can respect that. But what I don’t appreciate is that you lied to me about it!” Adam takes a step back and leans against an oak dresser. He’s not quite sure what to say or do. Holding his hand up to his face, he strokes his chin and thinks for a moment. “Well I wasn’t sure what you would have said about my plans.” Adam states, “I know how much you like AJ and Amira but this was something I couldn’t let go.” “Adam! Come on!” Jas exclaims in a somewhat playful tone, “AJ has done so much more worse things than this. Just because he claims you dropped the ball, you’re ready to jump down his throat? You need to rethink your strategy there.” “I know what AJ has done to me inside and outside of the ring, thanks.” Adam says in a sarcastic tone. “I don’t know why but sometimes I get the feeling that he doesn’t respect my abilities in the ring.” “Ok look, the main thing is this…” Jaslene says pushing Adam out of the way, “You didn’t take me!” Adam walks over and takes a seat on the bed. He looks up to Jaslene as she pulls a pair of black pants from the dresser. “What?” Adam says opening his eyes wider, “You mean to tell me that you are pissed off because I didn’t take you with me?” Jaslene looks back and nods. She pulls a pair of socks out of one of the top drawers and walks back into the closet with the socks and the pants. “What are you doing?” Adam calls out as he lays back on the bed. “Getting dressed.” She calls back. “And you should start soon, I put together a nice suit for you. It should be hanging up behind the door.” Adam sits up quickly, “What? Why?” “Because we are going out for an all day date.” She calls back with a giggle. “Oh come on Jaslene!” Adam groans as he gets off of the bed. He walks up to the door and sure enough there is a suit hanging on the back of it. Adam pulls the suit from the hook, “Is this because I didn’t tell you about going back to PCW?” Jaslene reappears in the black pants. She’s got a black satin bra on. She picks up the red shirt and slips into it. Jaslene looks at Adam, smiles and nods. “You better believe it.” She says with a smile. “And you have no say in what we do or where we go. I’ve got our whole day planned out and we will not deviate from it. No matter how much you complain or beg. So don’t even try.” “Christ…” Adam says under his breath. “Why? I don’t want to go out tonight. Why can’t we just stay in?” “Because, you kept something from me and now you must pay.” Jaslene answers with a sadistic smile. “If you keep me out of your PCW career again, expect many more of these all day dates planned by me.” “Ohhhhh I get it now.” Adam says pulling the suit from the hook. “No longer content with the backstage? That’s ok, I can understand that. All right, I understand. I can take a hint. I’ll give you your time in the light too.” “That’s all I ask.” Jaslene says with a smile. “Now put your suit on. You still have to make up for not telling me that you were going back to begin with.” Adam drops his head. He walks into the closet as Jaslene sits down in front of a mirror and begins to brush her hair.
