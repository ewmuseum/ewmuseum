A Hallmark Moment

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 22, 2002 OOC Note: I haven't been my 'Roleplaying' self lately. As a lot of the old guys remember, I would post 4 or 5 RP's per week and that number has dropped drastically since my return. Also, my roleplays aren't of the quality that I would like them to be, but that is mainly due to school and time constraints. I am slowly getting back in the 'RP mode' once again and you all can expect to see me back in old form very, very soon. This RP is a short one, but it is just to get the new 'workrate' trend going again. As we fade into a hot pink screen, we hear the sounds of two grown men grunting…one in a very low pitched, manly voice and the other in a high pitched, almost orgasmic voice. By the sound of it, one would think that the two men were engaging in 'sexual activity…' The grunting continues until finally the grunting stops and a long, deep breath is heard. The pink screen disappears and the scene opens up with Jack and Johnny inside of a Hallmark store filled with heart-shaped balloons and chocolate all over the place. The camera gets closer and closer to Jack and Johnny who are in the corner eating a box of chocolates. 'Man, oh man is this candy good.' 'You're damn right it is…' The tag team continues eating their beloved chocolate in the corner and then tosses the box out undauntedly, without even paying for the chocolate. Jack and Johnny then take over two seats in the Hallmark and act like they are broadcasting their own show from right there in the store. 'Well, well, well…The Heartbreakers have advanced up in the tag team rankings yet again after destroying the poor, defenseless team of Misplaced.' The pair isn't even talking to one another; they are looking at the lady behind the counter as if they are performing some sort of a show. The lady behind the counter just glances over and rolls her eyes, ignoring the two humorous wrestlers. 'Rightly so my friend, rightly so. Those two hooligans didn't have a clue what hit them…I saw the fear in their eyes when we stepped foot into the ring…they didn't know what hit them.' Johnny is heard laughing next to Jack. Jack jerks his head over and questions Johnny. 'What's so funny?' Johnny keeps laughing, almost uncontrollably. 'What is it?' 'You think they were afraid…of you?!?' The laughing gets even louder. 'What's so funny about that? Look at these muscles…who wouldn't be afraid of them?' Jack tears off his shirt to the delight of the lady behind the Hallmark counter. This only causes Johnny to laugh even harder. After a few seconds of posing, Johnny decides to get up and rip off his shirt as well. The massive Heart makes Valentine look like a little girl, even though Valentine was a former bodybuilder. 'Not fair…you're taller. Plus, no one cares about the fact that your muscles are bigger than mine…I'm more defined, and that's why I get all the girls.' Johnny roars with laughter and falls over on the floor. The Hallmark lady is seen bending over the counter and looking at Johnny with a puzzled glare on her face. A frustrated Valentine stands off to the side with a look of disgust on his face. 'Jack, save yourself the embarrassment, I'm the one who won the match for us in the first place by getting that powerbomb on that no-namer...I am the stronghold of this tag team.' Jack responds in a very whiny tone. 'But I'm the one who got the pin…' 'Who let you get that pin? And who saved your ass every time you were in trouble in that match…which was most of the time.' Jack humorously responds in another whiny tone… 'I was in PCW first…' Johnny just looks over at Jack and gives him a sigh. 'Okay, okay…I'll say it. You're great, you're awesome, you're the best, you won the match…Yadda, yadda, yadda…happy?' 'Actually, now that you mention it, I do feel a little bit better about the whole situation now…' Jack sighs. 'But one thing I'm not happy about is the fact that the very same team that we beat on Turmoil of last week is getting a title shot against those miserable ingrates of Politically Incorrect on Fusion. That really breaks my heart.' 'Yeah, no kiddin' bud. I can't believe that PCW would book four miserable heartaches like that in the very same ring…I'm sure the great fans of would love to see The Heartbreakers take down the politically strongholds of Politically Incorrect instead of those other hooligans. Instead we have to face Pailthorpe and Crichton…The Forgotten Ones…' 'Forgotten Ones…the only reason that they are forgotten is because you and I showed up…every was forgotten then because we are the coolest, most popular dudes in PCW!' They both pause and the camera turns over to the Hallmark lady who has a straight face at the miserable line by Johnny Heart. 'And then on Tuesday we have to face those freakazoids, Fear Factory. What the hell is up with that?!?' 'Forgotten Ones, Fear Factory…what kind of gothfest is this? It's ashamed we have to face those ugly guys…it just makes them look even worse when they stand next to me…' 'Ahem…' 'And you too…' 'Why is that Johnny?' 'Cause it's all about the PINK!' Well, well, well…Jack and Johnny have finally arrived in PCW and proved themselves as a dominating force. Jack Valentine has tried his luck here in PCW for almost a year now as a single's competitor yet has prevailed to no avail. Johnny Heart seems to be the answer…a former PWA instructor brings a massive size to the team and some amazing maneuvers. Johnny has taught Jack almost everything he knows over the years, yet why is it that Jack made it to PCW and Johnny didn't? Injury, that's what. Johnny could've been a huge star, possibly even a World champion by now if injury hadn't have struck his career. Who knows, maybe if injury didn't strike Jack's career, he would be a World champion as well. But the fact of the matter is this: Two injury prone wrestlers seem to be at one hundred percent and are finally trying to make a name for themselves. After demolishing the Sensations of Annihilation and destroying Misplaced, two credible tag teams here in PCW, Jack and Johnny are moving on to bigger and better things. Do they have all of the tools to become champions? We'll just have to wait and see, but one thing is for sure, the pink is back in PCW and The Heartbreakers are here to stay! The scene fades to pink.
