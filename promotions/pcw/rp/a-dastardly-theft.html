A Dastardly Theft

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.16.02(#1 PPV) “My dear, dear friend. At last we meet. The sound of Rush Mason in what seems to be a coaxing, wooing voice, is heard in the blackness as our current promo starts in such a way. Sounding uncomfortably similar to a child molester choosing his prey, we continue to monitor Rush’s actions. I know you’ve seen me watching you. Okaaaaaaay…..and we go from strange to just downright crazy. However, we can get some insight as the scene fades in, showing Rush looking down at something in his lap, and grinning hugely. What the camera’s viewpoint shows looks somewhat like Rush is…well….ermm… Rush: Dear Bon Dieu, I never thought it would feel this good. However, as the camera switches the angle from which it shoots, we can see that in Rush’s lap is not his little Voodoo Chief, if you get our drift. What is in his lap is even more important than his own member… The PCW Heavyweight Title. Rush continues to look down at the gold, sitting on his lap. Although why a man who is not, to get technical, the PCW World Champion is holding the aforementioned title is rather puzzling. Rush: You beautiful little golden trinket. You feel so heavenly on my waist. Despite being held by both Mr. and Mrs. Chris Williams-Chase, you still look quite nifty. The Voodoo Villain’s happy demeanor is so much different from his usual outlook that it seems wrong in some sort of way. There is no seeing the normal whining, sarcastic, Rush Mason at this point. But why does he have that belt…? Rush: I know that Clyde was giving you fleas, and I’m sure you’re happy I rescued you from sleeping in an alley with the little whore and her retarded pet. With those kind, reassuring words, Rush gives the golden prize a short pat of affection on the front of its intricate design. He then lifts it up onto his shoulder, looking in the mirror and grinning like an idiot. Rush: Belty….at Alliances, I’m gonna carry my team of un-coordinated bumbling idiots to the biggest win of their miserable lives. And do you know why? Looking like a bad actor on a children’s afternoon show, Rush turns to the belt, still grinning like a buffoon. He looks at it for a few seconds, as if expecting the inanimate gold object to give him an answer, and then gives it another small pat. Rush: It’s ok, my friend….because I know why. It’s because I’m going to win it for those three worthless shits! Yay for us, Belty! As this promo gets even more strange, Rush picks the belt off his shoulder and gives it a giant bearhug, cuddling up to a belt, that has been touched by both Stewart Hix and Stryfe. However, this would be the moment where the fun, cheerful music comes to a screeching halt with the scratch of a record. “Yo dawg…uhh…this a bad time?” Rush jumps about two feet in the air, flinging the belt across the room, as he spins on a dime to see none other than his manager, Ray Ray, standing in the doorway, drinking a bottle of… Delicious, Sweet, Refreshing, Straight From The Cold Glaciers Of The Canadian Wilderness To Your Living Room: Clearly Canadian! Somewhere off in the distance, a cheesy sounding spokesperson mutters, “The clear choice when it comes to fizzling beverages!” However, Rush is not so enthused at Ray Ray’s entrance, as he glares at the huge man, tapping his foot irritably. Ray Ray: Well I mean….dawg, I saw you talkin’ to that belt, and I was wonderin’ if you was straight trippin’. Not phased by Ray Ray’s uncomfortably stereotypical words, Rush quickly retorts, sounding somewhat like an arguing 7th grade schoolgirl. Rush: Was NOT! I wouldn’t talk to a belt….no way….! Ray Ray just looks at Rush, both eyebrows raised, with that look that you’d get from your dad if you did something that wasn’t really bad, but it wasn’t really terrific either. We got that look a lot. Rush: I might’ve been….but it’s probably from all this training I’ve been doing for Alliances! I was probably hallucinating! Ray Ray continues to give Rush that same look, and just shakes his head at the Vooodoo Villain, who walks over to the door for a moment, looks both ways, and then turns back to his plus-sized manager. Rush: Okay, fine, I was talking to that damn belt. The huge man responds quite quickly with that sort of ‘I told ya so’ tones, as he uncrosses his arms and desists from giving Rush such an evil look. Ray Ray: Dawg…..told ya so.See, we told ya so. Rush gives a short shrug, and then takes a deep breath, trying to get himself out of a somewhat sticky situation, in which he might seem a little less than manly (and sane, for that matter). Rush: It’s because….this belt is all that’s been on my mind since I…stole it form Clyde! Dramatic music is somehow cued, and Rush looks around for a moment, then just dismisses it as Ray Ray’s eyes grow big, becoming interested in Rush’s story. However, there are somewhat noticeable gaps as Rush relays the story. Rush: Yeah…last week I saw Clyde at….a homeless shelter and then I just beat his ass and took the belt! Ray Ray gasps, looking down at the belt. He still seems shocked at the gallant acts of his managing partner. Ray Ray: Dawg…wasn’t Clyde that one you was terrified of? Rush spins quickly, thinking that he’ll see the Demon standing in the doorway, but there is no such thing, and he wipes his brow, breathing out in relief, as he sees there is only an uncomfortably stereotypical man in the room with him. Rush: Bah….Clyde? No way…. Ray Ray shakes his fingers, thinking back for a moment, and then continuing with his point for Rush’s fear of Clyde. Ray Ray: No way, dawg, he was the one that broke your back or something…and then when you hired me, you said that Clyde was never to get near you, cuz you don’t want fleas, but also you said because he scares the living shit out of you and- Rush quickly cuts his manager off, cursing under his breath to himself. He then motions to Ray Ray with a cutting motion that most people would recognize as a signal to stop. Rush: Ixnay on the earfay ofa ydeClay! Ray Ray is now thoroughly confused, and looks down at the belt, then once again back up at his employer, scratching his rotund bald head. Ray Ray: Dawg….I ain’t know you could speak Portugese. Damn, you multi-talented. Rush sighs and smacks his hand against the top of his recently shaven head. He turns away, leaving his belt on the chair he has been sitting on. Ray Ray then picks up the belt, apparently inspecting. Ray Ray: Hmm…nice title, dawg…fo’ sho. Rush nods to himself, smiling evilly while Ray Ray can’t see. He replies back in a condescending tone, acting as if he got the title in the true way of winning it. Rush: Of course it is. And by Bon Dieu, would you cut it out with those ebonics? It’s just so….uncomfortably stereotypical. Rush turns to Ray Ray, and both shrug towards the camera for a moment, then turn back to their respective positions. Ray Ray continues to run his fingers over the belt, as Rush calls over his shoulders once again. Rush: You know…Ray Ray, if you don’t fuck up my chances at Alliances or this week, I might let you polish the belt. Ray Ray nods, somewhat eagerly, but the is confused by something on the back of the belt and lifts it off the chair. Ray Ray: Yeah…that’d be cool…but dawg, why does this belt say on the back “This is a replica. This is a toy. Not to be used for any real wrestling purposes.” The camera shows Rush closing his eyes and frowning, as well as letting out a low groan as his plan is being uncovered. His reply to Ray Ray sounds more like a question than anything else. He finally makes eye contact with his large manager as he speaks, looking for some sort of belief from him. Rush: Uhh…Clyde had a fake belt? Ray Ray still hasn’t made the connection, shaking his head as he looks down at something on the chair, and before he can inspect it, remarks to Rush in a “tsk tsk” kind of voice. Ray Ray: Damn yo….you can’t be trustin’ those dark gimmicks. They sneaky. Rush suddenly lets out a huge grin, realizing that his low-intelligent associate doesn’t realize the truth of the matter. Rush just chuckles to himself as Ray Ray picks up a small paper, reading it off as the scene begins to fade out. “Dawg, what’s this receipt say ‘Replica PCW World Title Belt: $49.99’? “Uhh….WHAT?! Clyde had the nerve to go and buy a belt to fool me with! “Well…at least he left you a receipt so you could return it to him. Seems like a nice fella, that Clyde…”
