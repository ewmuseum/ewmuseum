A Couple In Denial.

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/05/02 At Supremacy, then-Commissioner and Continental Champion Xavier Kannon did what he said he was going to do. He was busted open by Duke, he was bruised and battered by Duke, he was beaten senseless by Duke.... But he beat Duke. As the Malice Crew battled the Ultimate Authority at ringside, Xavier, the roars of the crowd a mere dull murmur to him after his head was banged into the mat from a spinebuster. Xavier, blinded by his own blood, beat Duke. With Kannon down Duke called for another Whiskey Bent and Hellbound, Xavier’s ever present valet Eleanor snuck in and nailed him on the back of the head. As Duke turned around with surely evil intentions, Kannon wiped the blood from his eyes and sent Duke face-first into the title with a Drop-Out, and he beat Duke. Kannon beat Duke..... He may have been fired, he may have been re-hired, he may have to vacate his Continental Title and he may be stripped of his Commissionership.... But Kannon beat Duke. Just to recap..... Kannon beat Duke. “So, Xavier, how does it feel to finally be re-hired back to PCW?” “Xavier, is there any bad blood between you and CEO Nelson over your firing?” “Mr Kannon, what was your reaction to being stripped of the Continental Title and Commissionership?” As the frenzied sound of the wrestling media is heard we fade into our scene, one that depicts the front of Xavier Kannon’s home in the Jenna Elfman Court development in Hubbardville, Wisconsin... The City specially built for the nation’s Scientologists. “Xavier, how will you feel fighting beside Tyler Nelson on Friday after he fired you along with the rest of the PCW roster last Sunday?” From all directions questions are flying and the shot is drenched with bright flickers of light from the various camera flashes. The camera pans away from the gathering of reporters, up the steps to Xavier’s porch to where a seemingly bemused Xavier stands, looking down at them dumbfounded. “Mr Kannon, any comments?” Xavier furrows his brow, a look of curiosity showing on his face as he looks to each of the people who asked him a question. As always, by his side is Eleanor who is looking equally perplexed at the gathering of journalists gathered on their front lawn. “......Xavier?.....” Despite the frenzied activity he calmly turns to his valet, still not comprehending what is going on, as does she and the journalists start to quieten down expecting a comment or statement. Xavier : “I was fired?” His voice resonates total honesty, something that is reciprocated by his puzzled valet. Eleanor : “You.....Were.....Fired?” These perplexed comments send the assembled media into more of a frenzy than a spiteful, bitter and aggressive statement ever could have, and the collective sound of 20 or so mobile phone being reached for and speed dials to the office number are heard followed by an overlap of excited voices. “Harry....He didn’t know he was fired!” “It looks like Nelson didn’t tell him Mick, maybe he was trying to keep it a secret!” “We must have some major U.A tension here!” “Boss, things will explode at Fusion, make sure we have our best photographer there!” Again, looking down puzzled at the scene we see the 1st Couple of Scientology are shown, probably wondering where all these wrestling newspapers have suddenly sprung up from, and how they managed to get into Hubbardville, or found out what house he lived at.... But Kannon’s mind seems to be wondering where they heard he was fired. Xavier : “I think we had better go and watch the tape of Supremacy.... I may have missed something....” He calmly turns around and ushers Ellie~! back inside before following her, the closing of the door attracting the attention of the journalists for a split second before they return to their calls and we fade to Black. Tyler: As of this second, everyone on the PCW roster, including the newly crowned champion, are FIRED!! Dave and Duke: WHAT THE FUCK?! Jeff: Great move, boss!! Clean house!! We fade back in on Xavier’s living room where Kannon and Ellie~! are watching the end on Supremacy. Both actually seem surprised at the statement then suddenly Xavier comes to life, seemingly laughing it off and pausing the tape. Xavier : “Oh, they meant THAT?!?! Oh, that is just one of Tyler’s little jokes, he plays them all the time! He’s always firing people at the office, or in admin.... This time he managed to play it on the whole of PCW... What a Jester, how can they even think he would fire ME?” The shot again fades out on Kannon’s jovial face and as his voice is no longer heard, that of a deeper, more gruff man replaces it. “Xavier, the whole roster really WAS fired! All 50 or so had their contracts terminated and had to reapply... You were re-hired but PCW now has a new roster!” We fade back in on a Radio studio where Kannon and Ellie~! are shown sitting across a desk from a middle-aged host who seems rather frustrated at the Scientologist’s stance on the matter as he is still in denial at his firing. Eleanor : “A......New......Roster? Like, whatever!” Ellie~! sneers at the man’s aggressive statement towards her man as Kannon, still seemingly uninformed of the situation responds. Xavier : “Hah, yeah, everyone seems to have fallen for it....” The host slams his palm against his forehead in frustration as Kannon oblivious to the facts surrounding him and the events at the end of Supremacy. Xavier : “....I mean, why would he fire me? I own 20% of the company! I am the Commissioner, I beat Duke Williams.... I am the closeest thing there is to un-firable.... Wait...I AM UN-FIRABLE!” The host despairs and looks up to the ceiling’s synthetic light and holds his hands to his sides, palms facing upwards. Host : “Dear Lord...” Xavier : “Hubbard you mean...” Host : “*Sigh*” The man takes the stack of papers in front of him and taps them on the table, squaring up the edges before sliding the pile across the desk for the Scientologist to look at. Host : “There..... Look.... You were fired! It says so in that one..... And that one.... In ALL of them!” Kannon takes the stack and flicks through them, grinning broadly at a page before continuing through the various publications and chuckling to himself as he turns the pages. The host looks hopeful as Xavier slides the pile back. Xavier : “That Tyler, getting the editors in on it.... He really knows how to play a joke! I mean, firing me, Walden, Makonie and Politically Incorrect... Like he’d to that!” As he speaks, Ellie~! nods along, looking at the host with her usual distasteful sneer much to his annoyance. Eleanor : “He’d........Do.......That!” The Gold Patron Meritorious turns to his valet with angry eyes as if he is going to shout but one look at her naive face melts him and instead he calmly points out her error. Xavier : “No.... He wouldn’t.... Hence I was never fired! Look, Mr Radio Host Man, I forgive your error, you are no Scientologist thus I do not expect your brain to be quite as evolved as mine.... But anyone can see that this is a joke!” Eleanor : “Is........A.......Joke!” Host: “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!” As the host holds his head n his hands and wails in despair the shot fades out on the bemused faces of Xavier and Eleanor, not knowing what his outburst is for. “Hey Xav, glad to see you got your job back...” “Kannon, could to see you in PCW again, well done...” “Great to see you overcame the oppression of others once more to get back into PCW!” Our shot now opens up on the busy thoroughfare that is Kirstey Alley Boulevard, the busiest shopping street in Hubbardville located at the centre of the city. We see our beleaguered Scientologist looking all around him as people wander past and talk to him. This doesn’t stand well with Xavi who is still in denial at ever being fired, convinced in his heard that it was a big joke played by the CEO rather than Nelson losing his temper and firing everyone, even his friends. Xavier : “DAMMIT! YOU PEOPLE ARE MEANT TO BE SCIENTOLOGISTS! YOU ARE MEANT TO BE MORE ENLIGHTENED THAN THE PEONS THAT LIVE OUTSIDE THESE CITY LIMITS!!!” The Hubbardvillians stare at him, shocked, scared and fearing for their Gold Patron Meritorious’ sanity after his outburst. He takes a moment to get his breath back and looks at the faces around him before starting up again. Xavier : “I WAS NOT FIRED! IT WAS A JOKE..... A JOKE!!! DON’T YOU PEOPLE SEE THAT! I, XAVIER KANNON, GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS IN THE CHURCH OF SCIENTOLOGY CAN NEVER BE FIRED.... I OWN 20% OF THE DAMN COMPANY!!!” His eyes are crazed and a vein is seen throbbing on his forehead. Xavier : “I AM MAIN EVENTING FUSION! I WILL BE FIGHTING ALONGSIDE TYLER AGAINST THE SCOURGE OF THE MALICE CREW! DOES THAT SOUND LIKE SOMEONE WHO WAS FIRED? NO! I WAS NEVER FIRED.... IT WAS ALL A GREAT CONSPIRACY TO BREAK UP THE ULTIMATE AUTHORITY BUT IT FAILED!” The shoppers stare at Kannon wondering if he is drunk or high as the camera pans over to Ellie~! who is walking out of a boutique, her hands full with 3 bags in each hand. The prep just stares at him, the same as all the others. “SEE WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU TRY TO OPPRESS ME? YOU FAIL!!!” The prep shakes her head. Eleanor : “Wait....I remember.... We did, like, kinda get fired....” She looks over to her man who is still ranting and raving at anyone who will listen to him and sighs. Eleanor : “...I suppose I’d better tell him this....” The shot starts to fade on Ellie~!’s pale Blue eyes until we hit Black and only hear Ellie~!’s sarcastic tone. “Yay me....”
