Of Gods and Men

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/1/03 A bottle raises to a man's parched lips once more. The minute yet steady tremble of his hand ever so slightly causes the pretty poison within to shake and stir, like some terrible yet beautiful storm over the seas. He takes a drink, the bitterness within registering for only the briefest of instants. Allowing the liquor to sift inside of his aged mouth for a moment, he swallows. He remembers. He thinks back of years, years flown by in an instant....of how cruel time is. It ambushes you, and it always, always wins.... Inside this dark confessional booth, the only one the lone priest can confess to is himself. Has he been living a lie this entire time? A shame it would be. What does lie beyond this horrid existence? Is there anything at all? And even if there is, is it really worth losing a life over? Was it, rather. The bottle drifts towards his lips again, his hand now trembling. He has seen too many horrors....so, so many horrors. Now old and enfeebled. But it wasn't always like this. It wasn't like this a few short moments ago. A few short years ago. A few short decades ago. Drifting closer, the poison within beckons him. 'Escape,' it says. 'Escape the visions of your past, your wasted life. Imbibe, drown them out again and again.' And the old priest, eager for respite, heeds it's call. As he swigs, a terrible pain shoots through him. For one brief, absurd instant the old man thinks that his drink has gone down the wrong pipe....the pain then intensifies. 'Help....me....', he gasps silently, though no one is near. He lurches forward, struggling....then falls to the cold ground. The drink topples from his hand as his grip loosens, the murky substance spilling forth like blood beneath him. His true life essence after all....he would laugh, if laughter still existed in his dying form. 'Help....', again he sputters, crawling and scraping along the sticky floor. He reaches up desperately....and sees, on the wall above him, the cross. 'Help....me....' With the last of his effort, the old man reaches for that symbol that used to mean so much for him, pleading for assistance..... And the symbol does nothing. As the pain becomes too great to bear, a final thought wrenches his mutinous heart. It was just a symbol all along. His hand falls to the floor along with his spirit. No one is there for him as he dies. The light shines down upon him, though he is not blinded by it Rather, it soothes his nerves and comforts his long weary soul. For the longest moment memory escapes him, and he wonders what exactly has transpired. And then, piece by piece, it returns to him as the tide returning to a lonely beach. Memories of a life. A long life. Finally, the thought passes through his mind, and through his lips. 'Am I....dead?' 'That is up to you.' A voice, strong yet understanding, rings forth. It fills the air of this room, if such a place can truly be called a room, and then fades just as quickly. 'Unearthly,' whispers the old man. 'Simply unearthly.' 'Quite.' The elderly one looks up, and for a moment what he sees does not register in his brain. An angel appears before him, a glimmering being of pure light. And then in another instant, that wondrous being shifts to a demonic entity of pure terror. Feathery wings warp and twist into black and leather horrors, golden skin shifting to burning red. The old man blinks in spite of himself....and when his eyes open, this creature rests in a form somewhat in between the two. 'Welcome to my world,' the being states serenely. 'Your....world.' Nary an answer rises from the being's lips. Instead his only response is an extended hand....a token of assistance, which the old man gladly accepts. However as he does, a funny thing occurs. He realizes he needs no help at all. 'Eh?' For the first time, the priest takes a good look at himself. His body no longer twists and contorts in odd angles of agony....in fact, it as if his body were not there at all. Wondrously, the old man reaches out towards his own arm, now shimmering with a beautiful incandescence. He then lets out a small yelp of both surprise and glee as his hand passes straight through his limb, allowing him a glimpse of wiggling fingers on the other side. 'If you are finished with the joys of escaping your mortal shell....', the being intones somewhat impatiently. 'I think I shall never be finished, my friend,' says the priest with a smile upon his lips. The creature before him does not register the same impatience with this, however....he merely nods understandingly. 'Be that as it may,' he continues. 'I have brought you here for a reason.' 'Who are you?', stammers the elderly gentleman. 'Is this....Heaven?' These few simple words garner a cackle from the otherworldly visitor. He leans his head back, laughing hysterically....as he does, his form rapidly shifts to that of the angelic creature the old man had seen before, and the demonic nightmare he had seen just afterwards. 'Ohhoho,' chuckles the thing. And then, just as quickly, he reverts to his original state of body and mind. 'No my dear boy, this is not Heaven. And you may call me by my given name, Clyde.' 'Clyde,' repeats the priest quietly. 'What reason have you for snatching me up from the grace of God, Clyde?' At this, Clyde stares down at the man. No malice enters his eyes at all, however....he merely looks at him intently. 'Please. We are beyond the time for lies, friend.' 'What....what do you....', the holy man stutters, though he knows in his heart exactly what Clyde means. 'God is what I mean,' continues Clyde somewhat sadly. 'I watched your last moments, friend. I saw you reach up for the cross, and I saw it deny you. As so many have before.' The creature turns his back again, looking up. 'But that is why I have brought you here. After so many millennia, I wish to try a simple experiment of will.' 'So I am to be your pawn?', the priest states sarcastically. 'Yes,' Clyde replies shortly. 'But I ask you....given the choice between being the pawn of one who would deny you until the very last, until you found yourself dying in a puddle of cheap liquor, and being the pawn of one who would at the least give you an opportunity.....what would you decide?' Silence fills this strange, strange place. And then.... 'I....would choose the latter.' A smile crosses the creature's lips. An expression of both mild relief, and deep seated sadness....a look only one who knows the inevitable may have. 'Very well,' says Clyde as he turns. With shocking speed he then walks towards the old man, grabbing his wrist before he can even move. His burning, yet serious eyes lock on to him, regarding him. 'Come now....I have so much to show you.' With a start, an old man awakens in a cramped confessional booth. Looking about frantically, he pats his chest with both of his hands....and then breathes a deep sigh of relief as he finds their progress stopped by solid flesh and bone. 'A dream....a....dream....', he mutters while wiping his brow of a fresh sheen of sweat. Stopping to place a hand on his heart, the priest gasps in terror as he finds it beating quickly. Then however, it slows....resuming it's normal pace. A healthy, normal pace. Dumbfounded, the old man stands, and discovers the usual creaking agonies of such an act have left him entirely. Indeed, it seems a new vigor has crossed his body....an energy he thought he had forever lost. 'Hehe...', he laughs as he looks all around in glee. Then however, a strange detail passes his field of vision that causes him to stop short. Walking forward a bit, the priest cocks his head as if attempting to gain a closer look....but what he sees does not change. The cross that formerly rested upon his wall, is gone. In its place, resting in the same position as it was, now stands an odd, cross shaped burn mark. The elderly priest looks upon this odd sight....slowly, he extends a hand, gently feeling the imprint. And in his cornea, a most peculiar thing begins to happen, though he does not notice at all. A fire begins to burn. A fire eternal, dancing in the darkness. A flame to never die.....
