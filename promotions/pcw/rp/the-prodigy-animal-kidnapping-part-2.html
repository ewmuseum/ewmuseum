The Prodigy Animal Kidnapping (part 2)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/28-29/02 When we had left off, Kramer, Jason, and Grimer were on their way to KFC headquarters to shoot the new wild wings commercial. The scene opens up inside of a huge area where many people are wondering around taking care of last minute details for the new commercial. The camera pans around and spots Kramer. He actually doesn't look bad wearing tan colored pants which have brown tassels along the edges, no shirt, and a big Indian headdress that must way at least five pounds. To top it off, he is greased up with oil making him appear more masculine then ever before. He walks over towards the set where the commercial is going to be shot. As he is walking someone taps him on the shoulder which gets his attention. Kramer turns around and sees a taller, older man with a headset wrapped around his neck. Kramer notices him as Bobby Smart, the director for the commercial. Smart: Kramer I just wanted to say that it is an honor to get to work with you. Kramer: Why thank you Mr. Smart. Smart: But..... Kramer's mind starts to race at the dreaded 'but' word, which almost always means something bad. He waits a few seconds but Mr. Smart goes silent. Taking the iniative Kramer talks. Kramer: But what? Smart: Damn I forgot. Never mind. I guess it wasn't that important. Well be ready and on the set in five minutes. With that, Mr. Smart walks away leaving Kramer standing there alone. Kramer shrugs it off though and walks over to the food table where he snatches up a bottle of iced down water. Kramer opens it up and chugs it down like a thirsty camel sucking the bottle dry. He throws it away and proceeds to the set just in time. A woman walks up to him and powders his face as he takes a seat Indian style on the set which is covered with fake grass. Smart: Ok roll the cameras in five...four...three...two.. Before we hear him say one, the scene fades to black. The scene opens back up, but its four hours later. The whole crew involved in making the commercial are all standing around a screen waiting to see all of their hard work pay off. Kramer looks the happiest of all to finally have the finished product. That could be because there were at least twenty four takes, one small fire, and ten pounds of powder on his face due to all of the restarts. The camera guy presses play and the commercial starts. The sky is pure blue with no clouds in sight. The camera pans down and focuses on a big teepee. Then it turns to the left and focuses on Kramer who is holding Grimer and stroking him lightly on the back. Kramer pulls his head up slowly and watches as a chicken runs across the grass right in front of him. Kramer: It's dinner time! Kramer places Grimer in the grass and as soon as he does Grimer runs off and chases the chicken. We hear a struggle and see a bunch of feathers fly into the air. Kramer stands up and walks over picking up the dead chicken. He then picks up Grimer who gives him a high five. Kramer then walks over towards the teepee and steps inside. Kramer is now in a KFC restaurant. He hands the dead chicken to a black man behind the counter and in a matter of seconds Kramer is given a bucket of hot wild wings. Kramer takes the bucket and walks over to a vacant table and takes a seat. Kramer: He kills it. He points to Grimer who is sitting on the table. Kramer: They cook it. He points over to the direction of the KFC workers. Kramer: And I eat it. With that being said, Kramer pulls out a succulent wild wing and takes a bite out of it. He continues to eat as the scene fades to black for a mere second. It comes back on and we see an up close shot of Grimer's face. He looks towards the camera and starts to talk (thanks to computer technology of course). Grimer: Wild wings. Get em', eat em', love em'. A large eruption of clapping is heard as the commercial is over. Although it isn't the greatest commercial, everyone seems pleased with how it came out. Then in a matter of seconds everyone goes their own ways either to leave or start on another project. Kramer is one of the ones who leaves along with Jason. They shake a few hands and are outside. They walk to Kramer's black truck which is parked in the front lot located right in front of the building. Both climb in and the truck takes off. Kramer: Do you happen to know when the commercial starts to officially air? Jason: In just under a half hour. Kramer: That quickly? Jason: Oh and to top it off they were in talks with the PCW committee to air it during Gold and Glory. But nothing is confirmed yet. Kramer: If they pay them enough I know they will. Kramer reaches down and turns on the radio. No surprise there right? Anyway he turns it on and flips through the channels and stops on a news station. It's on a commercial at the moment so Kramer tries to find something to pass the time. Kramer: Are you psyched for Gold and Glory? I mean it's just a day away. Jason: Of course I am psyched. Hell everyone's psyched. Kramer: Yeah it is going to be a big night. Jason: Remember the other day I told you I put a grand on Lovecraft? Kramer: Yes. Kramer gives Jason a mean look at the memory of Jason betting against him. Jason: Well I thought about it and I put two hundred down on you. Kramer: Why did you put more money down on him? Jason: Two hundred was all I had left. The rest of my money is going to buying wigs for Cynthia. Kramer: Is that the bald chick you were talking about earlier today? Jason: Yes it is. While the two are talking the news comes back on and what the lady says catches both of their attention. Lady: Yet another animal kidnapping has plagued Tampa Bay Florida. This time it was Rocky the ChowDown cat food cat. Police are calling this the Animal Prodigy kidnappings. Yesterday it was Trident the show dog, and today Rocky, what will tomorrow bring? With the pattern police speculate the next victim might be a mouse. Kramer's eyes bulge out of his sockets as he quickly shuts it off. He couldn't imagine what he would do if Grimer was taken from him. But he knows the police do have a point with the whole pattern thing. Besides Grimer did just star in a commercial which makes him an animal prodigy so to speak. Jason: Kramer....hey are you alright? You look a bit pale. Kramer: I'm fine. Jason: You don't think they would come after Grimer do you? Kramer looks at Jason letting him know that yes he does think they will strike. Jason: How will they get him though? I mean he is so small that you could hide him anyway and they would never find them. Kramer: Hey Jason can you do me a quick favor? Can you check Grimer's food bowl and make sure he has some left. If he doesn't there is some pellets in the glove box. Jason looks to the side and doesn't see the cage. He then leans over and checks under the seat and still can't spot the cage. Jason: Kramer... Kramer: What? Jason: Where is the cage? Kramer: I gave it to you remember? Jason: No you didn't. Kramer: Jason now is not a time to mess with me. My stress level is at an ultimate high right now. Jason: Kramer I swear you never gave it to me. Kramer: Well then that means.... Kramer brings the car to an abrupt stop and makes a u-turn in the middle of traffic! He narrowly misses several cars as he goes fifty miles per hour back the way they came. The scene fades to the back of the KFC building. There a tall and short man are seen putting something in the back of a utility van. We can't see their faces as their backs are turned toward the camera. One of them begins to speak and we instantly recognize them as Tom and Marvin! Tom: I can't believe he just handed the cage to me. Marvin: He must have thought you were that fat guy that is always hanging around him. Tom: You mean Jason Alexander? Marvin: Hey wasn't he the guy from Seinfeld? Tom: Yes. Marvin: I always hated that show. I never really understood it. Tom: Well it was the show about nothing. They climb into their van and drive off as the scene slowly fades, and the plot thickens!
