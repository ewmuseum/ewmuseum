Another Goth Bites The Dust...

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/07/02 In siding with the Phantom Booker, and in doing so turning his Scientological back on Tyler Nelson, Xavier Kannon thought he was doing the wise thing.... The Chef and Alexis Parshin had left the Ultimate Authority so they were down to 4 and Kannon saw this as the perfect time to make his move... But nobody could have seen what Nelson would do in revenge.... When he aligned himself with the Dark Age. Rather than Fusion being a chance for Kannon, Frisco and TPB to solidify their position, it was hijacked by the CEO’s Dark Minions and culminated in one of the bloodiest beatdowns in PCW history as Clyde, Darkstar, Bethany, Talena, Syko the Goblin and Tyler Nelson left Kannon, Frisco, Duke, Ellie and the Booker in their own blood.... But Xavier must heal fast as 1 week on from that night he will face an enemy even more hated by him than the Dark Age.... His oldest enemy in wrestling and the man he has vowed to destroy in PCW... The Pharaoh as they fight in a “Chair on a Pole” match. With both these distractions the Scientologist must be more focused than ever.... Or, he could just kill a Goth then concentrate on the Pharaoh.. Yeah, that’ll work. “Like... How long do these things have to be in for? I SO don’t need to see the blood go IN or OUT of me!” Our shot opens up in a private wing of the L Ron Hubbard Memorial Hospital in Hubbardville, Wisconsin where wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon and his wife Eleanor are sitting bandaged up with portable blood infusers running into them to replace the rather large amounts they lost at Fusion. Xavier : “Not much longer my little Poon Puppy.... I think we are almost full again now...” The Scientologist has a bandage around his forehead and a large plaster over his right cheek, while luckily for her sanity, no exposed areas of Eleanor are visibly damaged.... Both of them are however dressed in hospital gowns... But as these are special gowns from a special Hubbardville hospital their asses won’t show when they stand up. Xavier : “I can’t believe my Scientological mind didn’t see Fusion coming.... I just assumed the narrow minded inbreds in Mississippi would have beaten Goths senseless with pool cues before they got to the arena!” As he ponders the bloody, brutal and rather one sided events of Fusion, Ellie~! sneers as she remembers what she was exposed too. Eleanor : “Like... SO... EUGH!!! I can’t believe that skanky Goth gutter slag TOUCHED me... I could smell all her rotten, mouldy infected piercings even trough the Mississippi air!!!” The Prep shudders as she relives the moment in her head.. The Goth girl who’s life she would have made a misery at school getting the better of her. Eleanor : “And like, WHAT is with “Gothic Sex Kitten”?!?! Kitten? More like a worm infested, flea ridden stray that eats out of a dumpster!” As his high and mighty valet rants on, Kannon reaches for his cell phone.. The same ne he used to have Hubbardville’ only Egyptian... Well, Hubbardville’s only guy who had BEEN too Egypt... Beaten and thrown into the river Hubbard. Xavier : “Don’t even waste your few Preppy thoughts on her my little Fuck Ferret... Goths may have won the battle on Friday but I have a feeling they will be in mourning come tomorrow... Which won’t exactly make a change for them...” Ellie~! breaks out of her sneering attack to look at her Husband, puzzled at what he means.... And for once with a little justification. Eleanor : “Scientologists can ban BLACK?!?!?!” The evil... Yet still not QUITE as evil as the Dark Age... Scientologist chuckles to himself. Xavier : “Better than that... We can kill GANGREL!!!!” With her absolute and utter disdain for anyone or anything who has a lifestyle out of “The Norm”... Xavier’s Preppy Wife’s pale Blue eyes glow menacingly with the thought of a Goth’s life being taken. Eleanor : “Wow... Us Preps, like, REALLY need our own killing squad...” She makes a mental note sure to be forgotten..... Wait...... Bit longer.... NOW. Eleanor : “So who else have you lot killed? Jesus?” Xavier : “Only his Second Coming...” Eleanor : “COOL!!!.... What about.... Um... You know? That.. Guy? With the...” She gestures to her hair... Eleanor : “..With the...” Then to her robe... Kannon quickly decides this isn’t going anywhere soon.. Correctly. Xavier : “We killed JFK.” She looks at him with wide eyes of disbelief. Eleanor : “Like..... So... NO... WAY!!!! The Scientologist cringes as he replies in his Wife’s Preppy language. Xavier : “Like... Way....” Despite the fact she has just heard the truth behind Kennedy’s assassination, the Prep doesn’t really seem THAT moved... Eleanor : “TOMMY LEE JONES WAS A SCIENTOLOGIST!?!?!?!?!” Kannon’s face contorts into an inquizical look until he realises what Ellie~! meant. Xavier : “You mean Lee Har-... Never mind.. Anyway, on with operation “ONE LESS GOTH IN THE WORLD“.“ He starts to punch the number of Gangrel’s soon to be assailant into his phone as Ellie~! looks on impatiently, now oblivious to the blood infuser running into her. Eleanor : “In..... The.... World!” “Excuse me Sir... You can’t use that in here....” Into their private room walks one of the Nurses from the Ward, and immediately Kannon’s wily Scientological senses pick up on the fact that the Nurse is no a fellow Hubbard follower like himself but rather a Nurse drafted in to help cope with the great Hubbardville Nurse shortage of July 2002... Ellie~! looks to her then to the cell phone. Eleanor : “That... In... Here?” The Scientologist looks slowly up from his phone and gestures to his chart to which the Nurse duly obliges and picks it up. Xavier : “What does the name say on that Chart?” Nurse : “Xavier Kannon-Hall” Xavier : “Now what is the name of this WING?!?!?!” Nurse : “The Xavier Kannon wing...” Xavier : “See... So do NOT tell me to turn MY phone off in MY Wing!!!” Nurse : “But it’s the Xavier Kannon Wing and you’re Xavier Kannon-Hall!” Xavier : “JUST GET OUT OF OUR ROOM OR I’LL HAVE YOU KILLED!!!!“ The Nurse looks quite taken aback by Kannon’s outburst and starts to back away to the door as Eleanor sneers at her. Eleanor : “Have... YOU..... Killed!” She flees from the room as the couple shake their heads and tut at her rudeness... Xavier then finally gets through to the desired person and a crackley voice is just heard above the machine failing and killing the patient in the next room due to the mobile signal... Attached to that machine none other than famous Scientologist Giovanni Ribisi. “Scientological Killings ‘R’ Us... How can I help?” Xavier : “Kill Gangrel...” “Sure thing... We have a 2 for 1 killing special on Goths at the moment Sir... Can I inter-” Kannon hangs up and lays back again, relaxing in sound mind again as he continues his blood transfusion.... Beside him Eleanor has a huge grin on her little face, enjoying the fact a man she doesn’t like is about to die. “Damn I wish I could get a booking soon.... Maybe I should lose the 4465484 stone the Doctor recommended.. Who would have thought fake blood had so many calories...” In his Gothic Cave somewhere Gothic we see none other than the Vampire Warrior himself, Gangrel... Or “That fat one out of the Brood...“.... Grabbing a bottle of every Goth’s favourite Darkstar’s Carbonated Goat’s Blood. Gangrel : “Ahhh.... Made with REAL Goat’s Blood for that authen-... HUH!?!?!?!?“ Suddenly all the windows in the house are seen breaking as the elite Scientological Killing Squad make there appearance armed to the teeth with state of the art weapons.... But it is too late as Gangrel is no longer there.... Bemused Assassins : “WHERE IS HE?!?!?!?!?” ... Because it’s daytime and he’s a Vampire so he fried as the curtains were pushed back when the Scientologists came crashing through... Kinda anticlimactic, but hey, they did give you JFK and nobody has worked that one out yet! The Assassins shake their heads and leave the pile of Ash formerly known as Gangrel on his Gothic carpet in his Gothic lair somewhere Gothic.... As the voices of the 1st couple of PCW are heard again to accompany the fading to Black. “Do you... Like, think he’s dead yet?” Black.
