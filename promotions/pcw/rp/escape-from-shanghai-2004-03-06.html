Escape from Shanghai

6 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/6/04 A slight drizzle of rain has picked up in the China homeland breaking the beautiful sunrise gracing their prescence. A sea of black umbrellas is seen from a bird's eye view, all rushing back and forth. The scene is familiar to PCW viewers as it appears to be the very same one where Brian Arson and Shaun Styles collided at the recent Pay Per View. Inside a bar sits the loser of the street fight, Shaun Styles. A few modifications have been made to his look as there are quite a few bandages covering the cuts and bruises on his face. His attire has not changed, as he sits on a wooden stool with his ripped denim jeans and a plain white t-shirt. A stereotyped overweight bartender comes over cleaning a mug with a white towel as he looks at the sad face of Shaun Styles. Bartender: Care for another one? Shaun Styles: ...No, I think I've had enough for one day. Shaun hiccups as he pulls his face away out of his hands and looks at the bartender. Bartender: For the love of shit, all you've been drinking is water, I'm sure you can take plenty more. The furious bartender lives up to his duties as he throws the towel to the side and pours a tall, frosty glass of...water and hands it to Shaun Styles who relucantly takes it. He engulfs himself in a big gulp throwing his head back before slamming the cup against the bar. Bartender: Having some problems? Shaun Styles: ...You could say that. Bartender: Why don't you tell me all about it? Shaun Styles: There's not much of a story to tell... Bartender: I got a couple of minutes to kill, business isn't going all that great right now. Shaun Styles looks around him finding nothing but empty tables with lonely seats next to them and a small television sitting on a shelf. Shaun Styles: If only people would try this water I'm sure business would pick up for you. Bartender: One can only hope...So how about telling me what's bothering you? Shaun Styles: As I said it's no story...But it all started on one fine evening when I lost my parents and became an underground boxer. Somehow I managed to mess up a rigged fight and ended up in debt to a big shot mobster in New York, head of the Laproni family, I believe it was. 4 Hours Later... Shaun Styles: I hated his rich, big shot attitude so much, he's been giving me shit ever since I got into PCW...And what's the deal with always wearing those shades, it's not even sunny! 3 Hours Later Shaun Styles: I mean I got nothing against sunglasses but the point of it is to protect your eyes from the sun, he wears them indoors for god's sake! 1 Hour Later Shaun Styles: And then I ended up losing our street fight... The bartender has long fallen asleep as Shaun Styles finishes his story. He looks around with a confused look on his face as he gets out of the stool and leaves the bar. Finding himself on the main street he looks up finding a giant clock on top of one of the buildings. Shaun Styles: Holy shit! I'm gonna be late for the boat outta here! With that said some quick running music, let's say 'Run Lola Run' begins to play as Shaun Styles sprints down the street pushing people out of his way to get to the dock. On his way there he sees a clearly wealthy man standing next to a loooooong black limo. Rich man: Free million dollars! Get your free million dollars right here! Shaun Styles: Dammit, why now!?! I gotta get to the boat or I'll be stuck here forever. Shaun Styles keeps on running as he looks at the wealthy man fade out of sight...
