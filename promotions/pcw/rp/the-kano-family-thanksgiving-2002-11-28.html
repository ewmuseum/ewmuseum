The Kano family Thanksgiving

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/2002 *The scene opens up on Thanksgiving night at the huge dining table inside of the Kano family villa located about one mile outside of the city of Las Vegas, Nevada. At the head of the table is Jon Kano's father, a man in his early fifties with slightly graying and balding hair and who closely resembles his son. At the other end of the table is Jon Kano's mother, a woman in her early fifties with blond hair and who still looks relatively good for being older. Around the table are several members of the Kano klan, but seated next to Kano's father is the 'REAL LEGEND' himself with his wife to his left. Jon Kano, dressed in a beautiful, jet-black Versace suit. Kano's wife, Kristin, is dressed in traditional materity clothes, as she is some seven months pregnant. Everyone is finished with their meal and all are having trouble breathing, ala traditional Thanksgiving pains.* Jon Kano: Damn, Mom, that was one hell of a meal. Kano's father (snickering): Like she cooked it. *Thw whole Kano klan share a laugh.* Kano's mother: You bastard. I did make the call and I did write the check to the caterer. Kano's father: Well, Christ, Jen. If we all got credit for making telephone calls, I should have a set of lips permanently attached to my ass. *Once again, the Kano clan share a hearty laugh.* Kano: Alright, Mom and Pops, lets just settle down a little bit. *The scene fades out and fades back in outside on the porch with Jon Kano and his father each lighting a cigar as they look out on the city that Jon Kano and his father have revolutionized, Las Vegas.* Kano's father: Well, son, you about finished with this little stage in your life? Kano: Come again? Kano's father: Well I am just concerned about your so-called wrestling career. Kano: Pops, don't start. Kano's father: All I am saying is that every week I see you on television and you are killing yourself. You fight for money, and you don't need it. You have child on the way, Jonathan. Kano: I know that. January 17 is the due date. Kano's father: Aren't you afraid of that child growing up with a father who is never there or whose father is in a wheelchair? Kano: I think about it every single day, but I love wrestling. I built PCW with my own two hands. Kano's father: Do you not remember Kano Enterprises? Kano: Pops, that is a stupid question. When I am on the road, I am doing work for Kano Enterprises on my laptop and sending it to wherever it needs to go. Kano's father: Yeah, but how many days out of the year are you in the offices? Kano: Well I am out on the road 275 days of the year, so roughly ninety. Then take out Thankgiving, New Year's Eve, and Christmas Eve and Day, which knocks it down to eighty-six a year or so. Kano's father: And you think that you are a responsible CEO? Kano: Pops, I got you in the office when I am not there. And I am doing what needs to be done on the road. Kano's father: I guess I should have seen this coming, even when you were a child, you always wanted to do everything. You weren't happy with just being the star baseball player, you had to be the all-city basketball center and the all-conference defensive end in football. You and your brother, God rest his soul, were carbon copies of each other, I swear to God. As great of an athlete as Ken was, you were better. Kano: Pops, lets not talk about Ken. Kano's father: Alright. Jonathan, I have given you so much shit about your wrestling career, I deserve to hear some of your rhetoric about your next match. Kano: Well Pops, tommorrow night I take on this punk bitch Aussie who is tougher than Mom's meatloaf and twice as stale. Kano's mother (from inside the house): I heard that you little son of a bitch. Kano's father: Jen, think about what you just said. Kano's mother: Shit, I just called myself a bitch. Marc, if you need me, I'll be in the liquor room. Kano: Anyway, Pops, this Aussie is named Flaymin Aymin. Kano's father: Wasn't that the guy who drove that tractor into the crowd a few months after you joined PCW? Kano: Yeah. Kano's father: And that is what led to Tyler Nelson becoming CEO of PCW? Kano: That is correct. I hold him personally responsible for the bastard known as Nelson coming to power and for the sudden decline in my career. But like the old saying, you can't keep this guy down. Tyler is gone, never to return again, and I am rising back to the top of the PCW mountain to reign supreme. But I am a forgiving person, Pops. Kano's father (sarcastically): Yeah right. Kano: Anyway, just when I was getting over Aymin's immensely stupid stunt, he took me out of Alliances with three other people. It took four people to take me out. I was raising hell, I was kicking ass, and not God nor mortal man was taking me out of Alliances. I was dominant in that match. I tasted newbie blood and I was on a tear to get the respect that a veteran of my status deserves and on Friday, it won't be a newbie, but it will be just as sweet when I prove to the world and Flaymin Aymin why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!!! Kano's father: Damn, Jon. You want to go back inside and eat your mother's homemade pumpkin pie? Kano: God, I remember the last time she made that, it made both of us violently ill. Kano's father: True. *The scene fades to black as the two Kano men put out their cigars and begin to walk inside of the house.*
