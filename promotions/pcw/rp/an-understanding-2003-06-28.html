An Understanding

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 28 - 03 The accommodations couldn’t please a homeless man, so it definitely cannot satisfy the growing tastes of one Dorn Tracer. Despite being the PCW Television Champion and one of the federation’s most formidable forces, yet the confusing contingent of higher-ups couldn’t reward his effort and dedication with a half-decent motel room. But that’s typical: Give the religious minority the shitty rooms and hand the ass-kissing majority all the lavish beds and bathrooms. The rather peeved Astronomer sits solemnly in the rickety wicker chair and slowly rocks himself back and forth, staring blankly at the cheap 25” dusty television set that sits upon the unpolished mahogany dresser. His clean-shaven, angered face stares right back at him, looking him deep in the eye as if prodding his soul and very being. The feeling unnerves Dorn somewhat and he gives a slight shiver, feeling a pang of cold course through his spinal column. “Yeesh… I’m kind of creepy looking.” The creaking of the cradled chair legs seems to get louder and louder as Dorn slowly rocks to and fro, piercing the thick motel silence. Neither of the adjacent rooms utter a peep, and it’s not exactly very settling to Dorn, strangely enough. “It’s too damned quiet,” The Astronomer mumbles to himself, casting his gaze to the floor. “I must be going nuts. Here I am, sitting all alone in this room and troubling myself over the silence…” Dorn’s voice trails off as his neck twists from left to right along with the rest of his head. He exhales with a bit of sorrowed force and rises to his feet, pushing himself up with his hands. As soon as the Astronomer is standing, an echoing knock comes from the door. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK! “Coming!” Dorn hollers, followed by a groan to himself. “Great… visitors. I guess it’ll get rid of this eerie silence, though…” KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK! The knock pierces through again, only louder this time. Dorn shoots an angry gaze at the door and bares his teeth in its general direction, as if trying to cease the knocking with a scary expression. “HOLD YOUR FUCKING HORSES!” The Astronomer spins on his heels and faces the door, thrusting his chest out and wearing the most annoyed look he can muster, hoping to scare away whoever has the notion to bother him. He strides towards the motel room entrance, stepping over strewn out clothes and various bits of trash and food that he has left from his week stay. As soon as he reaches the door, another series of knocks ring out. Unable to take the nagging knock noise anymore, Dorn grips the handle and rips the door right open, nearly bringing it off its rusted hinges. “WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU-“ “Calm yourself, Dorn.” The arrival scoffs with an exasperated look, rolling his dark brown eyes. The golden skirt of royalty and various pieces of precious gems clearly explains the visitor as Dorn’s stablemate: The Pharaoh. “I apologize for interrupting… whatever you were doing, but this is important.” The Astronomer gives a smirk and rolls his eyes as well, turning away from his partner and stepping back into the interior of the room. He holds the door open as Titu follows him in stride, ducking into the room and doing his best to avoid the trash strewn about on the floor. The poor excuse for a motel room draws a disgusted look from the Egyptian Madman as he gazes about at Dorn’s accommodations, somewhat understanding as to why his partner might have a sore attitude. Dorn sits himself back down in the wicker chair and folds his arms across his chest, giving an bemused smirk to his partner, “Have a seat.” His grin grows as he watches The Pharaoh look about the room for another seat, but to no avail. “Something on your mind, Titu?” “First off,” The Pharaoh grunts, a little angered by the lack of seating, “I can see why you’re so disgruntled. But in defense of those who have put you here, I wouldn’t give you much more, seeing as how you’re a hot-tempered infidel with a bad attitude…” Tracer damn near rises from his seat to assault his partner, a furious look dancing on his face like wild fire. The raised hand of the Egyptian Madman stops him, however. “But that’s not the point I came to make, so please, if you will?” “Dumb fucker…” Dorn growls under his breath, trying to suppress the ‘hot temper’ that Pharaoh so kindly described. The Astronomer sits himself back down and folds his arms back across his chest, only in a sulking manner this time. The right part of his lip curls into a nasty smirk and he squints at Xansiv, still seething over that dastardly and rather cheap insult. “Get on with it.” Pharaoh clears his throat and adjusts his weight, trying to find a comfortable standing position as he begins with his true point, “It is about Fusion, my friend, and our match against this team known as…” “Justice Inc.” Dorn cuts in, sensing Pharaoh’s struggling with recollecting their name. “Yes, of course,” Titu exclaims with lackluster enthusiasm. 'I have no concerns about THEM, mind you. It is about US…” The Astronomer strokes his chin and nods, believing himself to be thinking the same thing as his estranged infidel partner. Nevertheless, he remains silent as the Egyptian Madman continues with his ‘concerns.’ “We’ve only teamed once or twice, and though we were successful, we’ve also had our… problems…” Pharaoh’s voice trails off as he looks to Dorn, who merely nods in response. Titu clears his throat once again and continues, “With the group coming to true formation and our power being asserted, I need your pledge, so to speak.” Dorn scoffs and rolls his eyes, “Pledge? We’re a frat now?” Pharaoh grits his teeth a little and closes his eyes, thoroughly annoyed from that single sarcastic comment from Tracer. He exhales slightly and opens his eyes back up, staring at his partner with an irritated fire burning in his pupils, “Of course not. I just wish to know whether or not your loyalty lies with US, or with…” There’s dead air for a moment before Dorn pipes up, inquiring, “Yes?” “To whatever else BESIDES us…” Titu saves face with a smirk, trying not to sound too much like a fool and at the same time, not offend his partner into mutiny. “I believe it to be a fair proposition, and quite one-sided. All I want is your loyalty to us. That is all.” “Why so?” Dorn cracks with a grin on his face, doing his best to aggravate his stablemate. It sort of irritates Dorn how he’s been with the group since day one, and all of a sudden, Pharaoh is asking for his loyalty. The mere concept of it boggles the Astronomer, but nevertheless… “After all, I’m one of the founders.” Pharaoh nods in acknowledgement, “I’m aware, Dorn. But I feel as though there’s been dissension among the ranks, and frankly, it worries me. Trust is essential among us, and I cannot have our faiths, no matter how absurd they are…” He shoots Tracer a harsh look, who just returns it with a overly-sarcastic grin. “… getting in the way of our group’s goals. Right?” “Of course,” The Astronomer states with a false demeanor of cheerfulness in his voice. “Good, my friend! I am relieved… BUT…” As though playing the suspense game, Pharaoh turns his back to his puzzled partner and starts towards the door. He grasps the shiny brass knob and slowly turns his gaze back to Dorn, but never fully reveals his face. “… we shall see this Friday.” Dorn nods and scratches his chin once, shooting a grin at his partner, “We shall!” With that, the Triumvirate member pulls the motel door open, letting in a gust of warm air, and steps outside. The door closes abruptly behind him and Pharaoh leaves Dorn alone once again to ponder the situation. The Astronomer’s lips curl into a smirk and he nods to himself. “Oh yes, we shall see…” [End.]
