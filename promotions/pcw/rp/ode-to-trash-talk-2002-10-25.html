Ode To Trash Talk

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25-10-02 Skitzo is standing before the entrance of the Jumbotron on the set of Fusion. The Ramat-Gan National Stadium in Ramat-Gan, Israel is due to explode in a few hours with the dynamic action PCW promises to deliver. PCW always keeps its promises of course. As a part of this exciting Fusion card, Semore Smoke and the man himself Skitzo are booked for a No Holds Barred match. Rather than making the normal public appearances though with his master Syko, Skitzo finds himself standing alone before the Jumbotron with one other person: A cameraman. Clearing his throat, Skitzo looks out of the curtain at the already packed arena waiting for Fusion, then turns back to the camera as it fizzes to life with the Jumbotron, broadcasting Skitzo’s picture across the arena as well as all other monitors and TV apparatus within the confines of the National Stadium. Skitzo: Good evening Eastern consumers… Skitzo: Psst…copyright, jackass! Skitzo: So sue me… Skitzo: That’s the point you idiot! Skitzo: GOOD EVENING! I am Skitzo and today I have decided, fuck the promotional shit, fuck the big fancy adventures that have nothing to do with my match, fuck the crazy shit I do, tonight is a night of trash talking. No Holds Barred match, ourselves and Cocks-… Skitzo: -…Smoke… Skitzo: …Smoke! Skitzo grabs hold of the suspenders he’s not wearing and strolls around while the camera follows him, slicking his hair back as a long sliver of greasy black falls over his face. Skitzo: Now I’m not Lance Hawkins or Graphic Violence or some other dickhead who shoots off at the knowledge that someone dissed his sperm count, but dammit I know my shit. You see, for example, the only thing Old School about Old School Wrestling these days is the trash talking, so why not bring that aspect to PCW to further rape them in the ratings? Skitzo: I don’t see why not… Skitzo: So it’s time…it’s time…it’s verbal ass whooping’ time! The camera closes up to Skitzo’s visage, and he smiles cheekily back, before twitching and turning into a snorting, raving lunatic. The camera sits steady as those in the arena sit with no clue as to what to make of the schizophrenic. Skitzo: So here goes: Semore Smoke, you best be watching this because I’m not wasting an hour of my time before our match to tell you this just so you can scratch your prison-owned ass and turn away like a bitch. See, I’m new, but I’m also old. I was in PCW last year when you were barely a sperm swimming around in your mother’s throat. While you’ve been crawling your way up the ladder, I’ve been biding my time before I push you off of it. Syko’s lackey I may be, but a loser I am not… Skitzo: What about the Mall Brawl and tag match on Turmoil matches? Skitzo: Shut up. Anyway, Semore Smoke has his big 10-5-2 streak, well excuse me while I waste seven precious months of my life being raped over by the headmasters in PWA to get that kinda streak. Skitzo: I hope I don’t offend you with this by the way? Skitzo: I don’t give a shit; I’ll hit him where it hurts… Skitzo: His anal fissure? Skitzo: No…now, Semore Smoke has been booked against myself tonight, what implications does this hold? By gum I could lose tonight! What does that matter when in the process I have the opportunity to pull out a big old electrified, barbed wire, flaming 2x4 and send him crying home to his three-legged moustache-sporting, cock-munching mother………in law………twice removed………by the Postman. Semore Smoke is going to be HeadFucked and Mentally Broken Down into a shadow of his former petty self… Skitzo: Sure thing, DukeStar… Skitzo: At least my gimmick is decent… Skitzo: Gimmick? Skitzo: ……………… Skitzo: ……………… Skitzo: Anyway…so why am I wasting my time talking when I could be beating up some handpicked guy on the street to prove my physical prowess? Excuse me while I put on my Matthew Kelly outfit… Skitzo proceeds to dress himself in a really awful beard and wig, and attaches a plastic 6-year old to his groin. Grinning like John Leslie after a night with Swedish Weather Girl, Skitzo looks back into the camera. Skitzo: Tonight Semore Smoke…you’re going to be………………………a martyr to the victims of the upcoming 29/10 tragedy which will see three brave American men beaten worse than a housewife to a Texan wrestler. Semore Smoke is a good man, a brave man, a kind and decent man………well he will be until I smash his skull into little bitties before this sold out crowd tonight! Skitzo: The HIT, Rob Rollins, Bob Vurdo and Skitzo are going to be fucked worse than a redhead at an OSW PPV! Skitzo: Skitzo?!?!?!? Skitzo: Oh yeah…Semore Smoke… Skitzo: It’s a double whammy folks; one night is all it takes to end the life of a man. Heck 3 seconds is all it takes to rape him of his dignity. Have you noticed yet this recurring theme of sex? The sperm, the redheads, the fissures…IT ALL COMES DOWN TO THE FACT SEMORE COCKS IS FUCKED! Skitzo: He doesn’t stand a chance! Skitzo: Well he does, but he’s going to be beaten worse than a Pet Coon-wielding Government Mule! Skitzo: I may be talking a whole load of horseshit throughout the course of this broadcast, to the people I’m not fighting…to the people I am facing…they could lock themselves in a waterproof, fireproof, earthquake-proof, asteroid-proof, poof-proof fallout shelter 100,000 feet underground but that won’t stop ME! Skitzo rips off the beard and wig and hangs a sign around his neck reading “I R PWA Student” before clearing his throat again and pointing at the camera. Skitzo: FOR I AM THE MOST INDESTRUCTIBLE, INEVITABLE, INCOMPROMPTIBLE WRESTLER THE UNIVERSE HAS EVER DAMN SEEN!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Skitzo removes the sign. Skitzo: While you are sitting watching this in utter horror, I have only a few more hours of material left! Skitzo: Fortunately we’re in a different time zone so it’s only going to last a few more minutes! Skitzo: Semore Smoke…if you’re happy with your life so far, if you’re content with your career…don’t be. Fuck off. After tonight of course. Take your life and take it where no one gives a shit. After tonight of course. Go overdose on pills, dig your grave, and then bury yourself in it. After tonight of curse. Or…you could just let me end your life during our match! I host a range of possible endings for you, from humiliating to agonising to the heavily popular, quick and painless. Buy two get a Stalker free! Skitzo: Ohhhhhhh… Skitzo: Semore Smoke, HIT, Rob and Bob, I could go on for hours repeating myself and talking about how I’m going to end you all like a Nazi on the edge, or I could just get on with it-… Skitzo: I think I need a tomtit… Skitzo: What? Skitzo: An Eartha Kitt… Skitzo: What? Skitzo: You make this sound like a fed-ending promo…I’m gonna blow! Skitzo: Ok, hold up this won’t take a moment…SO IN CLOSING, I would like to form my ramblings in the style of a PMSing New Yorker called Steve who makes up multiple identities and thinks mannequins think he’s hot…*ahem* Skitzo: Freagin’ Semore Smoke, HIT, Bob and Rob…I’m gonna freagin’ rip you guys a new asshole and insert dynamite into it…light the freagin’ fuse and watch as your careers go up in freagin’ smoke. You made a goddamned freagin’ mistake somehow managing to get freagin’ booked against me, so it’s my freagin’ obligation to take you all to the brink of insanity and well… freagin’ push you over into the abyss. If you don’t like what I freagin’ said tonight go take a freagin’ fuck to yourselves because the bottom freagin’ line is Friday freagin’ Fusion will mark the freagin’ end of Semore Smoke, and the countdown to the freagin’ end of HIT, Bob and Rob, paving the way for the aforementioned pain applying to The End. None of you matter to me on my way to the middle-lower/mid-upper level of PCW, except HIT because he’s got Elephantitis or some shit. Basically what I’m saying is this………you’re all fucked so put your head between your legs and… Skitzo: Give yourselves a final moment of pleasure before I beat the living fuck out of you physically as I have done tonight verbally…ya’ll da weakest link mu-fuckaz………GOODBYE! Skitzo: Talk, much? Skitzo: I SAID GOODBYE! Suddenly the feed goes dead and the crowd listen as the screaming sounds of a schizophrenic touching cloth echo throughout the arena soon to symbolize the tombstone of Semore Smoke’s career. The End
