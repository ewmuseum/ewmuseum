To The Reservations: Arriving

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Something the something, somethingsomethingsomethingsomething Eddy looked back at the junky farm truck a farmer drove him in to an Indian reservation as he waved goodbye to the farmer, Bob. Bob waved back and drove off as Eddy began down into the pit of the reservation. The sky was blue with puffy white clouds scattered across. A pretty typical early Summer's day, and nothing new or spectacular. Eddy climbed down the rocky hill he was faced with, occasionally losing his footing here and there. At the bottom, he stopped and looked around. He didn't know exactly where he was, but he was going to find the chief of the reservation fast to talk to him about becoming a Native American. With no human in site, Eddy turned. 'Who may you wish to speak to?', said a tall, tan Indian man of a 20's age and slender. Eddy looked up at him, the Native American's long black hair tied into a twisted ponytail as it laid across his back and blew slowly in the wind. 'Chief Running Bull', Eddy said professionally. 'Chief Running Bull is busy at this moment searching for buffalo', said the Native American. 'Oh...' 'Would you like to visit the campfire?', said the Native American. 'Sure', said Eddy, in a friendly tone. 'My name is Sun Eagle. Pleased to meet you', Sun Eagle said extending an arm out to shake. Eddy shook it carefully, trying not to squeeze too hard. Eddy released slowly as Sun Eagle walked proudly across the stone as the surface. It was on a mountain which was pretty rugged and steep and not very good for your footing. Eddy tried to find little nooks to put his foot into so he wouldn't slip on his ass and get hurt. Sun Eagle walked like he wasn't aware he could fall. Eddy followed behind, stumbling as they made it to the bottom where it changed to a vast grass field. 'My name....is Young Tiger', said Eddy. He really only used Young Tiger as his in ring name, but it sounded like an Indian name so he decided to use it in this case. Sun Eagle nodded and continued on across the field. At the time, Eddy wore a regular man's clothes. A blue polo shirt was worn with a pair of black jeans. He didn't look very Indian, but he was planning on changing to animal hide once he was officially Native. Sun Eagle turned suddenly, stopping his movement and looking straight at Eddy. Eddy stopped as well, confused. He stared at Sun Eagle, wondering exactly what he was doing. 'Why do you come to visit chief Running Bull?' 'I am interested in joining the Native culture.' Sun Eagle raised a brow. 'Are you an Native already?' 'Not quite. I'm Hispanic', said Eddy in a quiet tone. 'I hope you realize your responsibilities being one of us.' 'I am aware of how you have a keen respect of nature and so forth.' 'I trust you, city boy. I hope you further continue our culture', said Sun Eagle encouragingly. Eddy nodded. 'I hope I'll enjoy my time in the culture.' Sun Eagle nodded as well and began for about one step, then stopped. He stuck his ear out as if he heard something distant. He leaned over toward the north to get a better hearing of the sound. 'Did you hear that?' 'No...What?', said Eddy. 'Shh...Listen.' Eddy listened carefully, hering very slight noises that he couldn't understand. It sounded like beating, or drumming, or maybe running of some sort but Eddy couldn't hear it well enough. It got louder by the second, and seemed like it was coming closer. Sun Eagle looked in the distance. 'I wonder where it could be coming from...' It got louder, and louder, and louder when finally the sound was not far away. Eddy turned and saw dozens of buffaloes heading toward his direction in a stampede. Eddy tapped Sun Eagle's shoulder and pointed. 'Stampede!' 'Not again...', said Sun Eagle annoyed as he run. 'Come on, follow.' Eddy followed Sun Eagle's trail behind him, running from the buffalo full speed. Suddenly, Eddy heard a buffalo cry, or whine, or die causing a huge beat and many dominos of tumbling buffalo. He turned and saw a buffalo, arrow through head, lying on the ground dead. Eddy looked as he looked farther up to see Chief Running Bull on a horse. He had 3 red feathers in his hair, black hair as well and animal skinned clothing. 'Chief!', shouted Eddy as Running Bull turned. 'Who is this?', said Running Bull, pointing at Eddy and directing the question to Sun Eagle. 'Young Tiger. He wishes to become one of us.' 'Ah...', said Running Bull. He jumped off the neighing horse and landed on his feet, walking over to Eddy. Eddy stood still as Running Bull looked at him. 'So...You wish to join our culture?', said Running Bull. 'Yes.' 'Then come with me.'
