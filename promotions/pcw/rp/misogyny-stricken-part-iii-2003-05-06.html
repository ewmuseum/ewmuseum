Misogyny Stricken (Part III)

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/03 The rain continuously pounded on the vacuous Bainbridge freeway, excluding the presence of Stone Burns' rented Jeep SUV, Stone himself, and Stephanie.. Stephanie was his wife that also had him embark his misogynist campaign by first murdering her but evidently not doing so by her mysteriously being there at that exact time, at that time of harsh weather, and at that exact location. Stone had now most likely yet unpremeditatedly 'killed' his ex-wife. Stone paced to and fro the asphalt of the freeway, luckily having no one in sight see the accident but still having him frighteningly planning for the worst case scenario seeing that it would be very difficult for him to get out of this muddle, especially when brought in to law. Stephanie laid there, arms and legs spread out, cuts on the lower and upper part of her face distorting the clear image of her face. She was rather dainty and pretty, as Stone was far from comely. Stone knelt down once again from before and fettered her unconscious body by sticking his blade viciously in to the road pavement crack, through her light brown colored hair for it to have a chance of standing. He struck her face with the front of his palm, in effort to revive and awake her motionless state. The misogynist continued until' a rough cough came from her mouth, along with a spurt of blood. Her eyes slowly opened as Stone took the blade out of the crevice and winded it back, but stopping him was a resplendent production of light in to his eye, indicating the Bainbridge Freeway Patrol.. Stone came off of her as her eyes crept open as he cunningly slid away, escaping from the identifying light. He dashed towards the driver's seat door, which at the time remained open, and leapt in, shutting the door. Stone looked forward, the wind whooshed in from the cracked semi-cracked windshield, he looked behind him but the patrol car was far from the area.. He pondered for the last time, and left the SUV. Stone ducked through the rain, as Stephanie stood there helpless and defenseless and attained her within the seizure of his hands. Stone brought her in to the back door and in to the rented SUV, he came over to the front and drove off just as the patrol car came meters away. As the rain blurred his onward vision, Stone flipped on his windshield wipers which caused an even bigger crack, he switched them off as he looked back towards Stephanie who tried to blurt out a few aimless words but the pain and lethargy overcame her. The blade proprietor Stone shook his head in disbelief to her presence but continued driving, faster and faster. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Stone awoke from his usual phantasm, on the air flight to the Yemeni located town of Sana. As usual his face totally perspired and his cheeks and the majority of his face crimson shaded. He looked through the plane window, viewing the serene image of motioning white clouds over the light blue sky. The sudden turbulence rattled Stone, and he jumped up over his seat and looked forward as the other fellow fliers gazed at him as he cowered back down on his seat, leaving his attention to the sound produced from his tape recorder to his ear phones which was a masculine voice repeating three vague words by the order of... 'WOMEN ARE EVIL!' And this somehow kept Stone motivated as a misogynist on his lengthy flight, thinking that he might lose his enmity because of the long period. Turmoil was emerging, and just hours away in Yemeni time. That tape in his player, and his daze somehow kept him well driven and trained for his upcoming match against Agent Matt Jackson. Lastly, next to him he cosseted his blade. More numinous was that it would actually go undetected through the airport detectors. Stone grinned widely and devilishly (as always) and continued to pet his weapon waiting for the arrival in Sana, Yemen and impatiently waiting to effort to annihilate the self-proclaimed 'unhurt able' Matt Jackson.
