God Damn are we Good.

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: God Damn are we Good. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 11/22/02 As a commentator the one thing you can say about Duke Williams is that he is both fair and balanced. Dukestar: Who do I think will win Alliances? Let me put it to you this way if I can. Unless you’re a member of the front office you’d have better luck playing pickup sticks with your ass checks then winning the main event. You dig hoss??? The Front Offices doesn’t even have to show up and they would ring the bell and declare us the winner cus we are fucking amazing. Duke Williams is a humble man who respects his fellow coworkers and employees. Dukestar: Is anybody else in PCW a two-time continental champion, Goth, god, president? No. That’s what makes me better then them. It pretty sad and down right pathetic when you look at the wastes of roster space we have floating around the company. A guy who thinks he’s a super hero? Some putz in prison. A couple colored guys who think they’re anything more then equal opportunity hirings. Come on hoss think about it. Its not easy being so much better then the closest competitor who just happens to be on the same side and same team as me. Some now we join the humble, fair, balanced, Dukestar The Goblin as we join his life already in progress. -The cameras pan in on the Malice Man who’s at home with his six year old son Andy.- Andy: Can you help me with my homework? Dukestar: Not now honey I’m watching CSI. Talk to mommy. Andy: She’s not home. Dukestar: Then it looks like your shit out of luck. -The little kid stares at his eccentric father with confusion.- Dukestar: Look I can help you in an hour when nothing is on TV. Andy: That would be past my bedtime. Dukestar: Well in life you need to learn to adjust to situations you can not control. Andy: ….. -The young child breaks down in tears. Duke back hands him- Dukestar: Shut up and wait for you mother. -Sad music starts to play as the camera pans in to Dukes face.- Dukestar: Hi friends and neighbors I’m Dukestar the Goblin, President of PCW. What you just saw was a scenario to show you child abuse. When you’re placed in a situation where your child needs you. The proper response is with love and understanding not violence. Now if your in the ring you need to respond with the opposite and kick them when they are down. But that is the ring and this is life. Director: CUT!!! -The camera pans back to show the home is nothing more then a set.- Director: Good take Duke but did you really have to back hand the kid? Dukestar: He ain’t mine and he was annoying. Director: OK thanks. Dukestar: Anytime. -Duke lights up an evil Goth cigar from the communist Cuba.- Director: IS that a Cuban? Dukestar: You bet your over paid ass it is. Director: You support a communistic economy? Dukestar: You may see it that way I see it as burning down their fields. Director: Well there is no smoking on the set. Dukestar: Right. -Duke blows smoke in the directors face.- Director: What do you think your doing? Dukestar: I’m an evil Gangsta Goth God and the 2002 winner of Alliances I can do what I want. Director: What ever. Dukestar: That’s right all you peeps at home heard me straight up out of Gothville word to my Dark hommies. G Goth Masta Goblin in Da house!!!! You see this weekend at Alliances will be the night of darkness. What two things are you guaranteed in life? -Duke scratches his Goth chin.- Dukestar: Death and Taxes. Two things the Front Office deals in everyday. Walden, Brandi, Kannon, and I can not be stopped. No force out there is strong enough to get the job done. How many foursomes have existed for as long as we have? -Duke allows you some time to ponder the question.- Dukestar: Nobody. We our the reigning champions of teamwork. Never a fight between us. Nothing but a common goal of victory. -Duke smiles.- Dukestar: You can’t stop us so give it up hoss. Kids that’s the honky tonk gangsta Goth front office truth. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
