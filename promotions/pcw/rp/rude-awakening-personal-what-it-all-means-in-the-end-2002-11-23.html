Rude Awakening – Personal – What it all Means In the End

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 23rd, 2002 Vilas walked out; looking down at the city below gasping at the figures he would soon be competing for it not only life but country battles. The porch hung high, casting a shadow below, and blocking the sun from all that lay near. The green foliage above and the sweet smelling air added extra comfort to an already depressing situation. Was he a coward… Did he just do what was right…? Was it the logical choice…? What ever it was, Vilas was feeling the effects high above the city landscape. He lends over the edge and glazed down to the streets, forcing his body into the roses that grew it elegant patterns. He took a deep breath, getting the smell of the delicate roses. He stood alone with himself knowing full well what his only option was now. I had everything figured out now, but it still wasn’t getting any easier for my friends or me. Safina wasn’t taking the news well and I don’t think Blassa will as well, even though he may have done the same thing if he was in my position. But I couldn’t worry about Blassa at the moment. I had to worry about Safina, because she was still feeling the effects that will be long lasting. “I finally had time to think about what you said,” said a voice, startling Vilas and causing him almost to lose his balance. He quickly turned around and looked at Safina, still depressed on the events the past two days have brought. She didn’t look back at him and kept her head lowered. “You ran on your duties, not only to me, but to your country. You reason for leaving is UN-acceptable.” All I could do was forgetting she even thought that, rather then say it aloud, were everyone else can hear. I knew it was something different and the meaning of me being in Thailand had changed, but it still made it hard to think and more difficult to come back and stand in front of friends and family and fight for them. One mistake and I am blocked for life. Justice in this world doesn’t come easy. “You think I had a choice,” Vilas shouted out, causing Safina to take a few steps back in caution, as Vilas anger was no starting to seep through the edges. “I came back to face what I had become. I don’t need someone reminding me of the mistakes I made, because in their eyes I am wrong. But to me I am one hundred percent right. To death should anyone thing I am a coward. I thought it and I was wrong. I survived. I used my own dreams to save my life and it’s just sad that some can’t see that.” Safina looked back at Vilas as the two connected with a ray of thought form each other. Vilas turned back around and looked out at all of Thailand again. Safina walked up behind him and looked out across the land. “This is why I came back. This is way I survived.” Safina smiled and nodded, turning around and heading back inside. Vilas stood outside and remembered his entire journey, one story at a time. He smiled as well and started to laugh. I gave up all this for a dream that was worth it. I shouldn’t be sworn at or even mocked, because I am this counties leader and momentum. They need me to become a force. Their political figures can’t help them and I don't think anyone can save them from them selves. They need a leader who thinks alone. They need me… «««««The Meaning»»»»» What does any of this really have to do with Vilas? Is he the hero everyone is waiting for, or is he just a figure of beliefs? Vilas is more then what he is taken credit for. He gave up his own life to free himself from death, starvation and reality. Vilas is more then a little figures…He is a trendsetter and more important he is the key to Thailand’s success in the future. They thrive on competition and they need someone to step up and fight for them. Vilas is higher then a god… Vilas is unqiue… Vilas is the future… The future may arrive today, or tomorrow, but either way the future always wins over the past. With a streak of fire and a slab of ice, two warriors will do battle with one coming out the victor. But the last question the remains in the minds off all the people in Thailand and all the known believers is… Can you put your countriess future, in the hands of a coward…? Time will tell. «««««Courage»»»»»
