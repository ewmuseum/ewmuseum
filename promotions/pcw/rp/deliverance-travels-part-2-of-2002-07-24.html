Deliverance...Travels, Part 2 of ?

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-24-2002 (We find ourselves looking onto an old dirt road. Judging by the weeds growing from the center of the road, a very seldom-used dirt road. Trees line either side of the road and the air is quite still. In the distance is a figure who appears to be on horseback. The camera zooms forward and we see Venneman Pendragon, looking a little bit concerned. Today he wears black chain mail armor that shines darkly in the sun. Around his waist is strapped the PCW Rising Star Title. In his right hand he grasps the hilt of famed Excalibur, and his eyes burn with their crazed light. He appears to be laughing and gibbering to himself...) Venneman: Hahahaha...WHooo...I am gonna have to get me a car; and a map. I have no idea where I am. One minute I was riding hard into the South Carolina countryside, the next I find myself here...in no man's land. God's country...The middle of nowhere... (Pendragon goes on talking to himself...Who else is there to listen? He rides on, oblivious to the beauty of his surroundings, his only worry on the fact that he is indeed lost. He rides on until he comes to a small wooden bridge that spans a bubbling brook. He warily pauses before leading his massive mount Xerxes out onto the bridge. Without warning, from below the bridge comes a voice...) Voice: Who goes there? Who be clop-clop-clopping across me bridge? (Venneman looks around wild eyed, unable to believe that someone would question him. He replies in his haughtiest tone...) Venneman: 'Tis I...Lord King Venneman Pendragon. Last of the line of Arthur, PCW Rising Star Champion. Who dares to question me? Voice: Well...Pendragon, Lord of the Jobbers. I dinna expect you to be by these parts. I will play along though...I am Tag, High Troll...This here is my bridge, ye gotta pay a toll! Unless ye want to be me dinner. (Pendragon shakes his head and begins to laugh. Soon he is bellowing his maniacal laughter and the 'troll' has a hard time getting a word in edge wise...) Tag: STOP LAUGHING!!! I MEAN IT! THE TOLL WILL BE YOUR TITLE! IF NOT THAT, YE GO INTO ME POT AND THEN TO ME BELLY! (Something is funny, cause Pendragon is STILL laughing at the disembodied voice. He finally calms enough to give a coherent reply...) Venneman: Why is it that everyone seems to be after MY gold these days? I feel like a damned Leprechaun. And what about these lost souls trying to take what they can never attain. Could I at least deal with men? I mean, we have you, Tag, who claims to be a troll. You yell at me like I am some kind of Billy-goat and stomp around down there when I laugh at your insolence. This Friday, I get to tune up for the upcoming PPV, and who do I get? I get a GOBLIN!!! Can you believe it? Tag: Well, err.... (Venneman starts laughing some more and spits out a few words between chuckles...) Venneman: HHAHAHAAHA!!! What next? HAHA!! Am...I...Gonna...Have...To...Wrestle...A FIRE BREATHING DRAGON???!!!! HELL NO!!! AND DO YOU KNOW WHY? BECAUSE I AM THE DRAGON!!!! (Venneman jumps from his horse and lands lightly on his feet; he is very agile for a big man.) Venneman: SO how do you propose to take my title, Troll? You gonna fight me for it? You gonna kill me and eat me? What? Let's get on with this cause I am tired of being lost and I grow impatient with this stupid charade! (A slight shuffling is heard from beneath the bridge...) Tag: I thought maybe we could flip for it...My bridge, my rules. Heads, its mine...Tails, you keep it...All right? All right. Venneman: Now wait one minute... (Before Pendragon can finish, a coin flies up from beneath the bridge. It flips over and over and as it falls, Pendragon swings his sword, quickly; efficiently. Like a warm knife through butter, the sword slices the coin, and the two halves fall into the brook below. Pendragon rests his sword point on the ground and leans on the hilt...) Venneman: How about a new game...My rules. You come up here. You try to take my title like a man, er, Troll, and if you can, it's yours! If not...You leave this place and molest not the travelers who happen by. (Venneman waits but gets no answer from below...) Venneman: Come on...A big bad monster should have no trouble taking something from a mere man! Come on...what say you, Tag? You up for MY game? (A shuffling sound is heard again, and this time, a couple of splashes accompany the sound. A small man climbs up the bridge. Though he is certainly ugly, and his clothes quite filthy, he is a man; nothing more. Pendragon puts his sword away and shakes his head sadly...) Venneman: And I had hoped to rid the world of some vermin...You are no monster, just a vagrant who has a death wish. Tag: My real name is Herman...Usually people just give me something to shut me up, mainly because it is man's way to be afraid of what they do not know. You've got me...Do what you will, Lord Knight... (The crazed light fades from Pendragon's eyes and his gaze softens a little...) Venneman: I am not going to hurt you. I tell you what; you get me into the nearest town, and we shall call it even. I have important business. (Herman's eyes perk up...) Herman: Can I ride the big horse? Venneman: You walk, I'll ride. (Pendragon remounts his horse and the two take off across the bridge, Xerxes at a slow canter so as to keep pace with Herman...) Herman: Next town is Booneville. Couple miles yonder. Not sure how the townsfolk will take to you, what with your outlandish outfit and all, but who knows...For the most part they are good people...Farmers and what not. Used to live there myself, till... 'My mind began to wander soon after Herman began to speak...Mindless drivel is not my thing. Especially when it is coming from one so low as Herman...How do I get into these situations? First New York, now this. I guess it is hard to stick to the old ways. But what do people expect from their Lord? I cannot be common. I must be the way that the creator intended...Noble, honorable. Were it not for me, nobility might cease in the world of today...Ahh, to have been born when there were others such as I...Men who bestrode the world like colossus'. Each action reverberating in a thousand lives, each decision affecting multitudes...But, of that old and noble race, I fear I am the last. The blood has gotten thinner, the hearts, weaker. Through no fault or want of my own, I am alone...' (The two climb over a rise and look down onto a group of several shanties's, a few busted out cars and a larger structure that stands surrounded by the trashy settlement. They ride down and into the mix of rundown buildings, cluttered by even more run down people. The camera pans around, maybe thinking to get a shot of Ned Beatty tied to a tree, cause this is a scene straight out of 'Deliverance'. Pendragon loosens Excalibur in its scabbard and climbs down from Xerxes. He strides over to the nearest person, a man in dirty overalls who wears a dog-chewed John Deere cap. As Pendragon approaches, the man spits a large wad of tobacco juice on the ground...) Venneman: Charming...You all probably know me already, but I am Lord Venneman Pendragon, and I need to use a phone. Man: Lord Venison what? Why, I don't think we ever saw nothin like you round these here parts before, on account of this bein the new millennium and all. Anyhow, I don't think I like the way you talk...I do like that fancy belt there though...you a ro-dee-oh rider or sumthin? Venneman: Rodeo? Hell no. I am a warrior, descended directly from King Arthur. All I need is a phone and I can be out of you fine people's way. What is your name, good sir? Man: People call me Zed. Zed Handers. I been mayor o' this here fine town for bout long as I can member. Venneman: Good...A man of authority...Now if you could just kindly show me a phone. Zed: Yeah, I gotta phone...But it'll cost ya. Venneman: Cost? You would not do this favor for your High Lord Pendragon? (Zed starts to laugh now, and he shows his mouthful of half rotted teeth in a hateful smile...) Zed: Well pal...I don't rightly know you as no lord, and I don't like the way you talk...What should we ask for, Zeke? (From behind a building comes a towering shirtless man who wears a chain leash. Covered with gnarled muscle, he looks like HE might try to eat Pendragon. Doesn't look to be playing with a full deck...) Zeke: Dah....Shiny belt...Shiny Belt!!! Zed: you heard him...Your belt for a phone call. (Venneman backs up a couple of paces and places one hand on his sword hilt, the other he covers part of his Championship belt. His eyes begin to burn defiantly with an insane light...) Venneman: I'll die before the likes of you touch my title. Zed: Not only are we gonna take that pretty piece o gold, but we are gonna have some REAL fun with you when we get you down...ZEKE!! (Pendragon glances at a tattoo on Zed's bare upper arm...'Booneville...Where men are men, and sheep are scared'...Fitting, he thinks. After the slight pause, he leaps into the saddle on the awaiting Xerxes. Zeke runs over and grabs the bridle. Xerxes rears up, but Zeke doesn't let go. Pendragon, holding the reins with one hand uses his other to rap the hilt of Excalibur onto the head of Zeke, who crumples like a paper doll. He then races past Zed who has pulled a gun. With a quick slice, he cuts the steel revolver in half with his sword and simultaneously delivers a boot to Zed's head. Zed falls down screaming and blood pours from his forehead. Pendragon instantly has his sword sheathed and rides low, pushing Xerxes for all he is worth...) 'Garbage...Were these grander times I would have slain the whole town...Modern law now looks down on those types of actions, though. I only hope this trip gets better as I get closer to my battle in Florida, AND my battle in the Sand Castle...And who would know a castle better than a King? I only hope that the rest of the people I meet don't want to make off with my gold. I have enough of that from the talentless fathoms of the Rising Star division in PCW. King's Gold will stay where it is; around the waist of a king...It will not be a 'waste' on the unworthy. I only hope the boat is big enough for the carnage I plan on bringing. There will be plenty of pain to go round...Damn, I HATE those rednecks!' >>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
