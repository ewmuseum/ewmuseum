Has Smoke gone insane preparing for this match?

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 18, 2002 OOC: Sorry for the sudden burst of RPs, planned on posting these last night, but didn't, so I guess Semore Smoke is the official sandbagger of Mall Brawl, :p{{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into a huge lobby-type place. The place is actually the front of the Grandberry Mall in Tokyo Japan where Semore Smoke and the other Primetime Championship Wrestling superstars will face each other for the President of Primetime Championship Wrestling's Continental title. Duke Williams now has to do what Rent-A-Hero would have had to do if he hadn't had beaten Rent-A-Hero at the Sapporo Dome in Sapporo Japan. Seems the Front Office plan to get rid of Rent-A-Hero's title reign succeeded in a way, but it backfired in the fact that it makes the President defend his title against the entire PCW roster in an elimination winner takes all brawl within the mall. In the middle of the mall lobby is an information desk, with several signs written in several different languages. A few ladies sit behind these desk to answer questions from any tourist who is there visiting the mall. Hanging the huge lobby already is a sign that announces that Primetime Championship Wrestling's Mall Brawl 2 will be taking place there later that night. Smoke has been scooping out the mall for the past day as he has never participated in the Mall Brawl, but did watch it on Television the year before as he was in Primetime Wrestling Academy, or Dream Wrestling Federation at the time, one of the two, he doesn't remember. The lobby is crowded by different nationalities of people, most of them are Japanese, but you can see several European and American looking people walking up and down the huge lobby, and going up the huge stairways that lead to the mall itself and are on both sides of the lobby. In the center is a single glass elevator, which Smoke is standing in front of, attempting to get on and go to the top floor of the mall. Smoke sits there for a few seconds after hitting the elevator button and finally the elevator door opens. He steps onto the elevator and hits the button for it to go up to the top floor, or floor two of the mall. The elevator slowly goes up, but Smoke enjoys the view out towards the mall as it goes up slowly. The elevator door opens and a woman is standing there right in front of him, this woman is not Japanese, but looks to be French, her question towards Smoke answers this question. The woman points to the elevator and says in French… Woman: Je cherche le rez de chaussee. {{{{Translated in English, the woman said 'I am looking for the lobby/1st floor.' Smoke points towards the elevator he just stepped off of and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Oui, C'est il. {{{{Literally means, 'Yes, this is it.' The woman nods her head and smiles at Smoke as he moves out of her way and she heads onto the elevator…}}}} Woman: Merci. {{{{Means 'Thank you.' Smoke looks at he woman and nods her way with a half-cocked grin on his face…}}}} Semore Smoke: Your welcome. {{{{Semore is pretty fluent in common French, but isn't extremely fluent with everything, he can say about three hundred words, and that's about it, which is one of the things that he picked up during his stint in high school and college. He took several French classes, but over the years after that, he's forgotten a lot of it. Smoke is obviously high though, many people can't tell though, but he is right now, he's swaying on his feet and hasn't dropped the crooked grin from his face yet. He's wearing the same clothes that he was wearing yesterday, the brown leather jacket, a now dirty white t-shirt, a pair of black pants, and a pair of the normal tan Timberland boots. He turns around and continues to walk around the mall, and like any man who is usually high, he starts to talk to himself as if he were a skitzo just like Skitzo…}}}} Semore Smoke: Scout this mall today. Only a few hours until the match, gotta scout the mall and find out where I'll fight. Downstairs right there is a McDonald's, that'd be a good place, but I don't think so… or do I want to… {{{{Smoke bumps into a man walking by him as Smoke is staring at the toy store, which is full of separate toys that the United States children don't get to play with. There is a row where several flashing lights catch Smoke's attention, he glares at them for a second and feels a little weird. He then glares away, but you can hear the sound of children screaming from that aisle. It seems that it's life-sized robots of the famed Japanese cartoon show called 'Seizure Robots' . It's a famed show that actually causes seizures when you watch it. Semore continues to look around the mall, but he then finally sees where he's going to take his match at with a few wrestlers, the escalators! It's the perfect place as it leaves a lot to the imagination when it comes to weapons, he can rip people apart by causing them to be sucked under the stairs as they go through the machine and such, and he can throw people off the second floor and onto the lobby floor below. It's perfect! Smoke walks towards the escalators and sits down right beside the entrance/exit onto the moving stairs. He then starts to rock back and forth as he's either going insane, or is just too high to realize anything that's going on. He begins to chant to himself as he sits there rocking back and forth…}}}} Semore Smoke: I will win, I will win, if I don't win, I'll kick you in the shin! I will win, I will win, if I don't win, I'll kick you in the shin! I will win, I will win, if I don't win, I'll kick you in the shin! {{{{He continues to chant the same thing over and over again, people start to throw him down several different coins and bills of currency, some domestic, some foreign. Smoke just sits there with the same crooked smile on his face as the hours count down to the start of the Mall Brawl match…}}}}
