Date: #1 Skitzo So there we stood all alone in the middle of the night on the dock. Just me, and the daughter of my sworn enemy, two beings who seek to regain the same thing… the person they cared about. Somehow, Akiyama had managed to kidnap Mina under my nose, no doubt while I was meditating. For what, are his own reasons, just as Skitzo’s reasons for winning the tournament are his and his alone… or theirs and theirs alone… or not alone… whatever. The point is he may wish to win for his own reasons, and I have my own as well. Most men who take part inside the ring of battle tend to have their own personal motives that drive them to the prize. Fame, wealth, whatever, Skitzo can pick any number of them for any number of his personalities, let him do as he pleases. All that matters at this point is getting Mina back. She’s more important than this tournament is, more important than any belt. It was sometime past midnight, and the night air was brisk, slightly damp from the sea air. Night seemed to be dead of sounds save the crashing of the tide underneath the dock where I stood with Cyclonica. She kept herself warm with her black shawl, I my black trench coat. Never would I dream of the day I stand side by side this woman on the same side. For as long as I’ve known she existed her goal in life has been the same as her father’s… to see personally that I retire from the game called life. But she had come to me for help, the sworn enemy of her father. How desperation brings the most prideful to their knees. I looked out into the sea, arms folded across my chest. The pier spotlight shone down on us, a lone pair awaiting the ferry. Strange… I had no idea the ferry even came at this hour. “Are you certain the ferry runs this late?” I asked her curtly. This could all be some elaborate ruse to kill me, and cut me out of the tournament. Akiyama was well aware of my positioning, and wouldn’t put it out of his mind to remove me from another world title pursuit as he’s done a few years back… back when I first had come to PCW. She glanced at me out of the corner of her eye, a cold look insulted that I would even ask her such a thing. “I said it would come, did I not? Do you distrust me that much?”“You tell me, oracle.” I leaned against the post of the spotlight, my head tilting down to close my eyes. I had been trying to keep my focus in tact for the tournament, meditating in any free time I could spare. I knew, however, that free time would be sparse, now that I had to search for Mina and save her from Akiyama. He did the same thing last time, removed my focus from the battle to come, and in the end caused me to lose while my mind was elsewhere. If he were to stop me this time… why use the same ploy knowing I’d recognize it?Everything was getting so complex… Cyclonica glanced over her shoulder, brushing away a few of her long locks to eye me. “Something wrong, monk?”I shook my head. “What makes you think so?”Her finger came up to her forehead, tapping it a couple times before smirking. “Seer,” she reminded me.“I never bought into your propaganda, woman.” I responded, leaning around to the other side of the post to turn my back to her. “I only call you “oracle” to mock you.”It’s one thing to mock a person’s job, but when you mock a person for who they are… or even for who they –believe- they are… that’s when you start stepping on toes. I watched as Cyclonica started to turn around slowly, her eyes piercing into me as she hunched over slightly. “Ohhhhh, so you doubt me… MONK? Maybe I should show you a thing or two about who you think YOU are!”“Please,” I scoffed, closing my eyes. “I’m in no mood for your games, woman.”Her arms dropped down to her sides as she grounded herself on those black heels of hers. “Good, because I’m through playing with you!”She lunged for me lashing out with a jab of her fingers, but I had ducked as she came into range. I glanced back to see her spinning around on her heels, leg raised high overhead to hammer into me from behind. It was simple enough to catch her leg on my shoulder and grab her head from behind to throw her over my shoulder. Her agility was up to par though, landing on her free leg and as soon as her other leg was released snapping a kick for my face. I only leaned back, watching her hop at me with her leg kicking at my face repeatedly. She was trying to back me towards the end of the pier, and she had succeeded to a point. There were only inches of dock between the water and me. The same leg that she’d been attacking with came high into the air, hoping to make contact with my forehead and send me into the waters behind me. To her disappointment, I slipped around her at the time she raised her leg so high. Her leg came cracking down into the pier, her heel chipping a few splinters off the edge of the pier. Before she could turn around, I gave her a shove with my finger in the back. The action halted right there as she started teetering on the edge, a gasp coming from her as she started to fall forward. Her arms swung for leverage and balance, but nevertheless she fell towards the water… and would’ve fallen if I hadn’t grabbed her… by her ponytail. Her eyes went wide with pain, a yell of pain echoing around us. “LET ME GO!!” “So you want to drown?” I shrugged. “Fine by me… last time I play the Good Samaritan.”“Do as I say monk!” she growled through gritted teeth. “It’s wise not to anger me!”My brow arched at her words. “It’s also wise not to let go once you have a tiger by its tail.” I jerked my arm forward to remind her who was about to fall into the water. “Then again, maybe I should do as you say…”“NO!” I jerked her back some, getting another cry of pain from the seemingly emotionless Divine Prophetess. It was almost amusing, standing here with a fistful of her hair holding her on the edge. But… it wouldn’t do me any good. It was merely a distraction, and I had lost track of the real goal… Mina. It was then that I noticed the warm, wet feeling on my cheek. I dabbed at it and looked down at my fingertips… blood…Cyclonica looked back, a narrow smile as she glared at me. “You’re out of focus, monk. Think you’ll win anything if you can’t pay attention to the fight?”I just stared at the blood on my fingers, entranced with it… or more entranced with the words this woman just said to me. She took her opportunity to level herself back on the pier, and I released her hair I turned my back on her and started walking back to the spotlight slowly, still looking down at my fingers. Skitzo was approaching soon… and if I couldn’t focus myself, I wouldn’t be able to advance in this tournament. Akiyama would have won again, and then nothing would change. But for me to achieve anything… I’ll have to change… I’ll have to adapt. I heard Cyclonica exhale, and I looked up to see her holding something out to me. She waved it at me, looking away. “Here.”I took it, finding it to be a pocket mirror and some tissue. This… gesture of kindness from a woman who just tried to knock me into the sea… she was most certainly an unusual being. She folded her arms and leaned against the post on the other side of me. “You sighed.”“What?” I said as I flipped the mirror open and inspected the wound. It was a gash done swiftly, narrowly almost like a razor had came across me. Blood was leaking slowly from it, welling up on the wound. “You sighed earlier, that’s how I knew something was wrong with you,” she explained. “Tell me, monk… do you know who you are?”“Inphino Blitz,” I responded immediately. “The Black Dragon Master.”“Great,” she said, scoffing. “Now give me an answer that isn’t memorized.”She was right… that answer came off automatically, like it was programmed into my subconscious. I closed the compact and started fumbling with the packet of tissues to tend to my cut. “What is in a name? It is nothing but a label, the real identity lying inside of one’s self. You walk around presenting yourself as this empty shell of humanity, brooding and sulking in the darkness with all your moody Goth “sorrow and despair” bullshit, when it’s nothing more than a mask you wear.”“As the Divine Prophetess mask you wear, oracle?” I looked over my shoulder at her, just in time to catch her icy glare. “Jab at me if you want, it doesn’t change anything,” she responded icily. “At least I believe in what I am. You don’t even believe in who –you- are… but… I can’t blame you. I’ve seen your face differently many times, even I would get confused if I didn’t pay attention.”“Hmph, nonsense.” I pressed a wad of tissue to my face and held it there to soak up. “Is it now? Tell me… who was the man who took away his own humanity, his own emotions so that he could repent for sins no one knows about, wallowing in his own self despair day in and day out spewing incessant melodrama to the point where it bores the hell out of people?”I opened her mirror and looked at the cut. It was bleeding still, and so I pressed the tissue back in. I looked at myself slowly as Cyclonica talked, looking at my black and blue locks, my depressed expression, the sadness in my eyes. I looked in the background of the mirror, noticing Cyclonica’s own black hair with familiar blue streaks. She was looking back at me, smiling wickedly… the only way she knew how. “Or do you remember the man that took Old School Fighting by force, that man who confused the hell out of everyone with psychobabble and warped philosophy while prettying himself up and making himself “ELEGANT.” Everyone’s still talking about that self-proclaimed savior… he was hated, not for his actions, but for who and what he was.”I sighed and looked back at the mirror… but I found a different face looking back at me. This one had jet-black hair, styled and flipped fashionably, a tuft coming across my left eye. My eyes were dark, eye shadow and mascara, jet-black contacts in and silver piercing in my eyebrow and black painted lip. That feminine look was familiar… seeing those eyes look back into me, almost smiling at me sinisterly. Cyclonica stepped close to me, looking into the mirror before at me. “I won’t even go into that man’s sex life… we ALL know.”I stared up at her slowly, watching her turn around and hold her arms up at her sides. “How about the renegade? Do you remember him at all? The badass in the trench coat smoking cigarettes and wielding a katana? “Fuck it, make ‘em all bleed”… that’s what he said. He was about the beatings, the bleeding, and the violence, everything there is to fighting… a man with a soul as dark as his 5 o’clock shadow.”She looked back and lowered her arms, gesturing towards the mirror. I pressed the tissue back to my face and looked at myself again. The face had changed again… looking at another familiar face. A shaggy mass of dark hair, with stubble from ear to ear. That man sneered back at me, cigarette dangling from his lips. I closed the mirror and flung it back to its owner. She caught it with a chuckle, slipping it back underneath her shawl. “What, didn’t like what you saw?”There was no answer for her as I folded the tissue from my face and slipped it into my pocket. The wound had stopped bleeding for now. I started to walk towards Cyclonica, watching her step in front of me and staring me eye to eye. There weren’t a lot of women who could do that… 6’3 Japanese women weren’t a dime a dozen.“If there is anything you believe in, monk, believe in yourself. But before you can even do that, you have to ask yourself “Who am I?” Until you answer that question, there’s no hope for you achieving anything.”I stepped past her, looking out to the sea. “Here’s our ride.”She turned around and found the ferry coming for us in the distance. I had to admit… she was right. If I was going to accomplish anything in PCW, or in my life, I had to figure out who I was inside. That Black Dragon Master answer isn’t going to cut it anymore. There are those out there who think me useless, a waste, a loser with no hopes of achieving anything anymore. But then there was the fans… and the hundreds upon thousands upon millions of fans out there believed that I was one thing…… A champion…Hmm… we’ll see if majority rules in this case…
