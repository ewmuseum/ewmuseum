My stupid, stupid car

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 10, 2002 The scene opens to a shot of a Honda Civic rolling down the San Francisco streets. The street is rain slicked, still wet from the morning and afternoon showers which hit the Bay Area earlier in the day. A few of the street lights are blinking on and off uniformly, in an obvious attempt to try and save electricity. Anyways, the Civic can be seen switching lanes wildly and it’s speeding down faster and faster, crossing red lights, not waiting for pedestrians, and honking relentlessly at cars going slower than it. What kind of a jerk would be driving a car like this? Tom Lexian: [humming to the tune of Three’s Company] Come and knock on my door….we’ll be waiting for you… A Toyota Echo flies into my lane, and proceeds to speed away at a cool 25 MPH in front of him. I slammed on the brakes and honk my horn incessantly. Tom Lexian: [angrily yelling out his window] What the fuck! Hurry up you old bag! I don’t have all day to wait! C’mon! Dammit, I hate it when someone interrupts me when I’m singing the themes to ‘Three’s Company.’ That really pisses me off, not that I’m not pissed already! The driver’s side window to the Echo opens, and the driver pops his her head. Britney Spears: [flips off Tom] Go blow yourself! Tom Lexian: [yelling back] Right back at you, whore! You drive me crazy! Britney Spears: [singing along to the tune of, “Crazy” by Britney Spears.] ……I’m not too deep! I’m so excited…. I hurriedly places his head back into the car and closes the window. Tom Lexian: God damn pop stars, always driving around in their fancy Echos and singing their stupid songs. If I had a nickel for every pop star that tries to serenade me with their songs, I’d be a millionaire. Suddenly, a car drives up to my driver side window. It’s an old Buick Oldsmobile, and the passenger opens his window up. Marvin Gaye: [singing “Sexual Healing” by Marvin Gaye.] Oooh, babbbbbbby…I’m hot just like an oveeeeeen….I need some looooooving, and, babbbbbbby….I can’t hold it much longer. It’s getting stronger and stronger… Tom Lexian: [singing along happily] ….and when I get that feelin’, I need….sexual healin’….sexual….healin’….oh baby now, makes me feel soooooo fine….. Marvin Gaye: Hey man, you got some soul for not being a black dude. What’s your name? Tom Lexian: Tom…. Marvin Gaye: [surprised] Tom? That ain’t no name for a brotha that’s got soul like you. You need a name that fits yourself, something that really pops. Something ghetto fabulous. [thinks for a moment] I got it! Your new name is…Earth, Wind and Fire. Tom Lexian: [shrugs] Wait a second, isn’t that a band? Marvin Gaye: We don’t worry about things like names. I mean, how many people are named Marvin in this world? Tom Lexian: Good point. Marvin Gaye: And you don’t see me suing every poor bastard with my name, do you? I’ll further evidence my point. I mean, how many people are named Gaye? Tom Lexians: Tons! There are tons of gay people. Marvin Gaye: Wait, did you say Gaye or Gay? Tom Lexian: Gay. Marvin Gaye: Oh ok, I thought you meant the other kind of gay, because I meant the Gaye as in the…oh well, never mind. Anyways, the point is, reach for your dreams son. And with a name like Earth, Wind and Fire, you’re sure to be a success. Tom Lexian: [honored] Why, thank you Marvin. I really appreciate that. Why, I remember as a young man… Suddenly, sirens and lights are storming into my rear view mirror. I check it, only to see that a police car is barreling down on them quickly. I looked over to Marvin, to tell him that everything’s going to be alright, but the Oldsmobile has already cut over the street barrier, and was headed into a local park. I had no choice now but to pull over. I hoped that the police car would ignore him, and go after Marvin’s car, but no such luck. The police car parked directly behind my Civic, and the police man walked up to my driver side window flashing a flash light directly into my eyes. Police Officer: [super high pitched voice.] Good evening, sir. Tom Lexian: [trying hard not to snicker] He…he…hell….hell…[busts up laughing] Police Officer: [places his hands on his hips angrily] Are you making fun of my voice? Just shut the hell up alright? It’s not my fault I was born with this vicious affliction, okay? Now just shut up… Tom Lexian: [still laughing] I’m so sorry, sir. Believe me, I would never make fun of a man of your stature. I mean, you are the voice of the law….[tears begin to fall as he cries] Police Officer: Stop laughing! Stop laughing right now! [draws his gun] I just have one question for you… I stare at the gun, and suddenly the situation is no longer funny. I raised my hands in the air, trying to show that I surrendered. That was a great time to get serious now, after I drove the cop to draw his gun. I am such a dumb ass. Tom Lexian: [scared] What’s that? Police Officer: [breaks down crying] Why doesn’t anyone love me? No, he was not crying. No, he was not crying. I could say that over and over again, but it was still true. God, he reminds me of Darkstar. The guy tried to take me out, but he couldn't, so he has to pretend that he's someone special. Well, good for him. Him and all of those books that he keeps writing from. Light betrayed light. Darkness will overcome all. How boring and drab. I bet the guy doesn't come out of his home to see that beautiful sun shining. I can't say the same for Clyde - he looks like he had just been roasted by the sun. Oh, and Syko the Goblin? Does anyone else need a shady looking sidekick? I already have two. The scene changes, and now we see the police officer lying in the drivers seat, as if it was the couch of a psychiatrist. He’s babbling on about his childhood, while I play with the officer’s nightstick as if it was a lightsaber. Police Officer: [reminiscing] ….and then when I was eight, there was the school play. The head of the play said I could play Peter Pan if my voice deepened a bit. I tried everything. I even paid my sister three dollars to punch me in the throat, hoping that would deepen my voice. But it didn’t work. Nothing did. Do you know how embarrassing it is to have all of your friends know that your sister punched you in the throat? Not that I had many friends…[stares up at Tom]…are you listening to me? Tom Lexian: [humming the “Duel of the Fates while playing with the nightstick] Come to me, Skywalker…I am your father….[suddenly remembers the officer]…Oh, what? Oh yeah, of course I’m listening. Yeah…uhm, Peter Pan. Yeah, great guy, nice tights… Police Officer: What are you talking about? Tom Lexian: What? Do I have to spell it all out for you or something? It’s exactly what it sounded like. I know Pete Pan, okay? Dammit, I hate it when I have to repeat myself. Police Officer: [incredulous] You know Pete Pan? Tom Lexian: [sarcastically listening for an echo] …….. Police Officer: [rolling his eyes] Oh for crying out loud… Tom Lexian: Hello? Hello? Echo? Did you hear that echo, Mr. Policeman? Why do you keep repeating what I say? I said I’ve met Peter Pan. You know, green clothes, lives in Never Never Land, fights off that one handed guy, Hook. Yeah, that Hook guy is pretty mean. Although he gave me decent scores during my dance off with Preston Pot, Pan’s evil mirror twin. Then there was Smee, who was as dumb as rocks. He reminded me of Tink, except without the killer legs. And then there was Hook, who gave me some kick ass scores during the judging. He yelled at me later for not paying him on time for the swimming lessons he gave me the week before. Police Officer: [confused] Son, are you sure you aren’t the one that’s supposed to be lying down? Tom Lexian: Ooooh! Now who’s the one with the attitude, Mr. I Have A High Pitches Voice And I Want To Cry About It? Police Officer: [shouting] I did not cry about it, you Star Wars obsessed, Peter Pan dillusional, Preston Pot wannabee! Tom Lexian: [shouting louder] I know what you are, but what am I? Police Officer: [yelling] A meaniehead! Tom Lexian: [shouting louder] I know what you are, but what am I? Police Officer: [turning red from yelling] A near sided gynecologist! Tom Lexian: [shouting louder] I know what you are, but what am I? The police officer smacked me. The police officer steps out of the driver’s seat, and throws me back into it. He slams the door shut, catching my leg in the process. I yelped out in pain, but the officer continues to shove the door closed, not seeing the leg impeding the door. Finally I get my leg in, but only after he has lost all feeling in his leg, and is now sweating profusely. Police Officer: [smirking] What’s the matter, son? Is the pressure of being caught by a Frisco copper finally getting to you? I knew you’d crack, sooner or later. Tom Lexian? [rubbing his leg] Crack? The only thing that’s cracked around here is my leg…that, or your head, you lunatic. Police Officer: [looking into the car suspiciously] Sure, sure. Just hurry up and show me your license, would you please? License and registration…. I took a heavy sigh and reached into my glove department. My eyes widened in disbelief as I realized that I didn’t have it. DIDN’T HAVE IT!? I looked back up at the officer who arched an eyebrow up. Police Officer: Well? Oh my god. I’m about as screwed as Darkstar on a date with Syko the Goblin, only, I’m better looking than both of them. Yeah, that sort of makes me feel better.
