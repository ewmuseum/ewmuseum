The State of Defense: Part Three - Packing Heat

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/26/02 The State of Defense… RP#3 for Turmoil and March Madness…#1.5 for Fusion Chapter Three: Packing Heat… An excerpt from “Cocky Canadians Weekly”, Week beginning 25th February: Page 10, Article: ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase – Who? **Advance Warning: In case you haven’t already, read the first two parts of this series…some elements carry on.** ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase – Who? : Cocky Canadians Weekly delves into the life of this Heated Young PCW Superstar… As written by: Jenkins McCoy… Chris Chase first came to light in the Middle period of the PCW2 tenure…as a youngster tipped for greatness at that very time, strutting his stuff on a minor stage, impressing all that surrounded him. Yet…until recently, he has left much to be desired as far as his form shows. From the depths of obscurity, he has scrapped, pulled and crawled his way to a new clime, a more impressive height, thanks to a little help from the New Breed. And now, he nestles at the top of a Cruiserweight Division, has defeated some of PCW’s best and brightest…and stands as a testament to aspiring young wrestlers everywhere. Accompanied by his New Breed cohorts, the sky’s the horizon for this 23 year old Canadian…but what makes Chris Chase such an enthralling and compelling to watch competitor in these days of Satanic Jobbers and Superiority complexes… The Arrogance Horizon It has been known for some time now, that Chris’ ego has been steadily inflating at a sharp rate, with every match he wins…it gets that little bit bigger. He is, without a doubt, on a tremendous hot streak…coming through the month of February without any singles defeats, and remaining the yet unchallenged PCW Cruiserweight Champion. If you face these facts, then it’s no wonder that ‘The Messiah’ has, recently…been committing himself to acts that he wouldn’t necessarily have been involved with three months ago. However, despite this, it’s understandable that we’ve definitely seen a more entertaining, a more extravagant Chris Chase…as his inflated ego causes him to think even more of himself. An important rule kids…every negative has its positive… Cutting to the Chase As an athlete, Chris’ talent and repertoire, although unproven, is close to being undoubtedly one of the more spectacular styles of technical wrestling that I have ever come to witness in the last couple of years…combining the grace of a Cruiserweight and Lucha Libre, with the Technician’s cunning and meticulous planning – Each part influences the whole lot greatly, and fuses together in a greatly cohesive manner. Breaking it down, it’s quite easy to find a multitude of trends within this fighting style of Chris Chase…in this simple, yet effective fusion between two underlying trends in Professional Wrestling these days. Innovation, rather than inventing new moves, is the whole philosophy of Chris Chase’s wrestling. It’s like how the saying goes: “If it ain’t broke, don’t fix it”, or “If the existing stuff isn’t useless don’t go about creating something completely new”. ‘The Final Analogy’ a finisher that is certainly reputed to be capable of much more than knocking out near Cruiserweights, is a direct example of this innovation…taking a standard Rocker-Dropper…and reversing it, changing the angle of impact…and completely befuddling the rest of the wrestling world. First used against fellow Canadian Tim Walden…it has proven to be the downfall of many an opponent after the last two or three months. And setting it up couldn’t be simpler…a spinning wristlock. So what of the rest of Chris’ maneuvers? Well, first of all, there are variants on the DDT, Bulldog and Neckbreaker – All Standard moves – that need to be explored. ‘The Epitomizer’ was made famous not only in Chris’ PCW2 tenure when Chris Sloboda found himself on the end of it one too many times, but also as an identical move to that of PCW Veteran Kid Wonder. However, there is one vital element that makes Chris’ unique…he doesn’t use the turnbuckle for momentum…instead using the second rope as a platform to execute the move. This too, can be applied to ‘Pure Lustification’ Chris’ Somersault version of the DDT, of which there are rumored to be a number of variants on the variant itself. Finally, there’s the Springboard Shooting Star Press…a tough maneuver to pull off by any means… Foes, Foes, Foes… …… Strangely enough…the rest of the page is torn off… Location: United Can-American Media Productions Offices, Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Time: 17:00 PM. Weather Conditions: Worse than usual… ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ The State of Defense, a term recently adopted to dub certain escapades of this type, of this caliber, of this overwhelmingly awesome stature. For the second time in a month, I find myself defending the PCW Cruiserweight Title…against Xavier Kannon of all people. Now, he’s had this misconception that I’ve been afraid of facing him…HA! So much for being a Scientologist, knowledge is quite elusive to Xavier, and with that…his knowledge on yours truly. I hardly backed down from driving Snapple out of my home country, keeping Canada free from the suckitude that’s usually associated with Xavier. For once, I felt empowered, like I had control over of how Kannon reacted to that, over what he and Eleanor quoted afterwards. That, and I managed to get a decent portion of airtime for a change, which is exactly how it should be… You know that you’ve made hefty progress when a magazine deliberately sets out to track your progress, rating you with a keen eye for detail. Cocky Canadians Weekly, a magazine that I’ve subscribed to ever since the birth of my career and basically my “other” Bible, published a critically appraising article on the subject of yours truly in the past few days, an article that stirs me to my very self-centered core. Peering out through the wintery fog of a late Tuesday Afternoon in Milwaukee, Wisconsin…Corey Williams and I trudge through the inches of snow, up a small flight of stairs leading up to the lobby of a Media Production Office, belonging to that of the United Can-American Media company. Pulling open one of the vast glass doors that marks the entrance to the lobby, I shiver in the coldness of the air in Milwaukee, a flu-inducing Zero degrees Celsius. I sigh approvingly as I step into the lobby, followed by my Controversial teammate, and greeted by none other than a jovial Dustin Downey… Obviously, this had a whole lot to do with that little idea of his that he managed to spark up a couple of days ago, funnily enough. He slaps the reception desk with a moronic grin upon his face as he sits upon it, waving the two of us over without pausing for any thought whatsoever. The lobby itself is an impressive affair, with sweeping archways passing over the mosaic floor. However, nothing can be quite as impressive as you know who….:: Dustin: Chris! Corey! Finally, you’ve managed to get here…and you’re nearly on time. Boy, for once, I’m early…and these guys are late…HAHA! ::. As I remove my overcoat, slinging it over my arm…I spot a petite figure behind that of Dustin. Sitting at her desk, her hair tied back into a small bun, is a rather pretty and young receptionist, who giggles at Dustin’s jibes at two thirds of New Breed…nibbling upon a ball-point pen as she goes about her work. Looking across myself, I spot Corey immediately licking his lips…and with an arrogant smirk, I hold the Controversial guy back in a metaphorical sense. Leaping off the reception desk, Dustin makes his way over to us, beckoning to someone in a far corner of the lobby….:: Receptionist: Hee…Dustin, you, you funny guy…so, these are the New Breed of grapplers that you were talking to me earlier about. Now, where’re those hamsters that you’ve promised me, Dustin? ::. Tapping my foot on the mosaic floor impatiently, I await the person that Dustin beckons from the far corner. Emerging from the shadows created by the arches, appears PCW World Heavyweight Champion, Jason Snow…who looks none too happy about being ordered about by Dustin Downey. Frowning at me, he slowly swaggers over, his eyes darting from side to side as he does so. I roll my eyes at Dustin’s lack of foresight as far as getting Snow involved in this whole thing….:: Snow: ~(Mutters)~ Insignificants fools, peons, cretins…OH MY GREATNESS!! It’s a THREE-HEADED MONKEY!! Dustin: Huh, what, where? Tell me, tell me! ::. Pointing in the region behind Dustin, who is subsequently sent searching high and low for such a mythical creature, Snow rolls his eyes and smirks conceitedly as he turns away from all of us heading for the door. Soon joining him is Corey Williams, who gives a wink to the curious receptionist at her desk. Giving me an acknowledging nod, he pulls on his fur overcoat, and races after Snow…who continues to cuss as he reaches the glass doors. After hearing a loud SLAM of the door, I cock my head at Dustin in a belittling way….:: Dustin: Chris, you didn’t see a Three-Headed Monkey run by near here did you? I swear that Mister Snow just said that he’d seen one… Chris: ~(Sighs)~ Dustin, trust me, there isn’t such THING as a Three-Headed Monkey, so get that foolish notion out of your head, and begin to pay attention to what’s really important at this point in time… ::. Dustin glares at me blankly, seemingly putting two together in his head before coming up with something totally irrelevant to the current topic in question, that article that had been written in the Cocky Canadians Weekly magazine that I’ve had my nose in ever since I purchased it on Monday morning….:: Dustin: Hounding Boda about the time when he lost to you, until the cows come home? ::. Irrelevant, yes, being a conception that I didn’t predominantly have in mind at this certain point in time, shrugging to myself as I slump down on a bench firmly fixed to the wall of the lobby. Chuckling to myself, I begin to ponder the possibilities of disdaining Boda-boy…until the time on the clock face above me hits home the real importance….:: Thoughts of Chris: Yikes…it’s Five o’clock already…jeez, it’s only another three hours before I’m due to be in attendance at Turmoil, defending my PCW Cruiserweight Championship against Xavier. Ah, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase – Crippler of Scientology…the Grand Conquistador de Spectacular…these aliases just roll off the tongue… Dustin: Surely you can’t miss a chance at mocking one of your greatest enemies of the past year, eh Chris? Chris: Meh…maybe later Dustin. Now, you told me that we were meeting that McCoy character, and I have just one thing to ask. Where in MY name is he? Do you realise that I’ve got to fight Xavier again tonight, Dustin? Seriously, you hyper-active cretin, this match is of the utmost importance if I’m going to go into March Madness with any credibility at all. Do you understand what that would mean to me? ::. Patting me on the shoulder, which scares me slightly, Dustin looks down up me, as I begin to rant about the importance of Turmoil’s win over Xavier, when it happens to come around in a number of hours. Looking around at the room, he pauses, before vaguely reassuring me that this McCoy guy would show up….:: Dustin: Patience my Messiah-rific friend, you’ll be fine and this McCoy guy will be here soon enough…I had him swear it to me on Scouts’ honour. Besides, the tips on fluent diction and vocabulary that he’ll give you will put you in good stead to mock Xavier for as much as you want later… Chris: True…I keep running out of words to say how much of a waste to society that Xavier and his little Preppie girlfriend are… Dustin: Atta boy! Now then, so much for a standard Dustin Downey pep talk…it might up being quite a wait don’t you know? ::. Oh do I ever. I’ve been brought here to be brought face to face with the man that wrote the article featured above, that frighteningly honest article on the honest-to-god awesome Chris Chase. Knowing journalists as I do, it’s easy to say that although they can meet deadlines without as much as breaking a sweat, when it comes to meeting up with subjects, the typical correspondent is usually later than Dustin to an idiot convention. I sigh to myself, knowing this, continuing to admire the sheer stature of the architecture above me. Down at the reception desk, the lady receptionist peers into her screen…before recoiling in shock and frantically reaching for her microphone….:: Receptionist: Would ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase and his manager, ‘Deadly’ Dustin Downey please report to floor 15 for a meeting with Jenkins McCoy…journalist extraordinaire – oh, no…forget that last part…he just added that for a little self-gratification… ::. Rising from my seat on the bench, I chuckle to myself, elbowing Dustin in the ribs in a sly, cocky kind of way….:: Chris: Heh, I haven’t even met this guy and I’m beginning to like his style already… ::. Wincing in minor pain, the Former PCW2 Cruiserweight Champion rubs his ribs…that seem still tender after all the crazy bumps that he’s taken during his entire career, I mean, who could possibly forget the 50 foot leap he took onto a fat-assed Italian Plumber. Of course, we WERE playing Super Smash Bros. at the time….:: Dustin: Owie…did ya really have to elbow me in the ribs, Chris? Besides, isn’t this a guy who wrote a promoting article on the likes of yourself Chris? ::. With a cocky grin, I nod…setting myself up for one of my famed rants, the likes of which are usually celebrated up and down the country, especially in the rose-tinted perspective that I look upon everything I do. Anyway, being self-critical for once aside, I rant…for the distinct lack of nothing to do in the final moments of this short skit, pulling out a torn piece of paper from my trouser pockets….:: Chris: Yes, why of course he is, Dustin. He wrote a brilliant article detailing on just how freakin’ good I just as well might be. And here, here is a sheet detailing exactly how much I’m going to severely outclass and destroy Xavier Kannon tonight. Why, it’s a metaphorical Cradle of Mockery, underlining the filth and despair that Xavier has brought upon the world by exposing Scientology to the masses. I tell you Dustin; I’m willing to bet that only one quarter of the population of America has the mental capabilities to look up the word in the dictionary… Dustin: Does that quarter include the population of Wisconsin? Chase: Why do you think that I say “Ah to Wisconsin with it”, Dustin? Wisconsin is the other, other place…a place so vile that scum sucking losers seem to come to it by the thousands. Note that I was dragged here, I didn’t actually want to come here…especially in the mutant Rat season… ::. Dustin looks at me with a frightened look…stammering….:: Dustin: M-M-Mutant Rats? Chris: Yes, Mutant Rats indeed Dustin…I tell you, it’s amazing how many socially rejected freaks that Scientology coughs up, take Xavier Kannon for example…now there, there is a true foul up if I ever saw one. Now then, about defending this Cruiserweight Title of mine… ::. And then, Dustin and I begin to head towards the nearest elevator…discussing the implications of tonight’s match, whilst being watched by a giant mutant rat that lurks above us, upon the impressive ceiling arches. The screen remains like that, before eventually fading away. And you can Justify…that!.::
