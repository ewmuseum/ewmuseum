Getting drunk on milk... The Conclusion!

Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin was knocked out unconscious as a result of sipping from the chalice of milk 6 years past its expiry date… With an official police alcohol test in mere minutes- Flaymin has to wake up and soon. But how? [The scene opens where we last left off. Flaymin’s new valet, Heather, grabs Flaymin’s limp body and begins to slap his face in an attempt to awaken him. However, Flaymin remains lifeless…..] Heather: What am I going to do!?! Bar Tender: If I were you, I would…. [The Bar Tender look’s at Heather’s curvaceous figure] Bar Tender: Well the first thing I’d do is check myself out naked in the mirror… [Heather slaps the Bar Tender across the face] Heather: I’m serious!I just got this gig and if Flaymin winds up in jail than that means I’m out of a job! [The Bar Tender reaches underneath the bar and pulls out the First Aid Kit.He opens it and pulls out some smelling salts. Heather looks at the smelling salts skeptically] Heather: Do you really think that’s going to work? Bar Tender: Huh? [The Bar Tender rips open the packet of smelling salts and begins to distribute it on the bar top in a way similar to that of a cocaine line- in thin strips. He then hands two women some alcohol and encourages them to lick the salt of the bar top] Heather: Hey, you’re supposed to be helping me!! Bar Tender: Listen lady, I’ve got a business to run.So your boyfriends out cold- who cares? Heather: But that’s only because he drank YOUR milk which was SIX FREAKIN’ YEARS OLD!!! [The Bar Tender ponders this thought and begins to feel sorry for Heather] Bar Tender: Look, I don’t know what more I can do for you.Your best bet would be to get him to a hospital. [Heather grabs Flaymin’s body and begins to drag him out of the nightclub only to be called back by the Bar Tender] Bar Tender: Hey, are you going to pay for your drink?!? [Heather turns around and glares at the Bar Tender. The Bar Tender nervously takes a step back] Bar Tender: Just joking… heh, heh, heh…. [Heather turns back around and begins to head for the exit. She makes her way out the front door and quickly indicates to the valet to bring Flaymin’s car around. A moment later, the car drives up to Heather and Flaymin. Heather opens the Passengers side door and shoves Flaymin’s carcass into the seat. Flaymin’s body simply slumps forward and leans against the dashboard. Heather frantically gets into the drivers’ seat and zooms off towards their hotel. Suddenly, Flaymin’s body begins to twitch and he starts to awaken…. Noticing this, Heather pulls the car to the side of the road.She lifts the handbrake and turns off the car] Heather: Flaymin!!You’re awake!! [Flaymin sits up in his seat and looks at Heather] Flaymin: Of course I’m awake. Heather: You were just passed out at the nightclub and so I had to drag you out of there. [Flaymin smiles sheepishly and shakes his head] Flaymin: I wasn’t passed out. Heather: What?? Flaymin: I just didn’t have enough money to pay for our drinks….. so I took a dive [Flaymin winks at Heather and grins while an obviously upset Heather grows hysterical] Heather: YOU MEAN YOU FAKED IT ALL JUST TO GET OUT OF PAYING FOR A $2 DRINK??? Flaymin: You think that’s good?You should see what I do to get out of paying Child Support! [Scene fades to black]
