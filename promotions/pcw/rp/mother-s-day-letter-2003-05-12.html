Mother's Day Letter..

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/12/03 Time: Yesterday, May 11, 2003 Prologue: Mother's Day is today.. I never took this day seriously as my mother would always tell me not to buy her anything and seeing that we were hung over poverty I wouldn't have the money to purchase anything from the market from a bouquet of flowers to a ring from the jeweler.. But I sensed she wanted something and every Mother's Day she would work her two jobs to feed us as my father wasn't there.. I knew she wanted to rest and smile to a present from Me.. but I could never make her happy. I couldn't do anything about it as I was only a little boy that meandered through the streets looking for trashed flowers that I could revive in to something modern looking... but finally, after a few years me with the help of my friends had mustered up enough money to buy my mother something. She was in work so it was the perfect time to surprise her.. I bought a bouquet of roses and a diminutive box of a choppy brand chocolate. I stayed home 'till late as she never came.. I was preoccupied by the thought of her being surprising not the thought of her never coming.. And she never came back.. and I've never had an explanation on why she never came home.. and I wasted my years on that.. Even if it wasn't my mother's fault I still despise her from that day because she left me to deal with orphanages and just didn't think about me... she didn't think about me. That's why this day is so sacred.. Where I effort to have my ever SO evil mother dissolve from my brain. To this day I still have the bouquet of roses and the tiny box of chocolate.. and I've actually framed it for recollection of what I did, which was caring about my mother even though I had nothing.. She had me thinking that she was sad for something but she didn't think of Me.. she was greedy ogre like every other woman. If I were to ever know my mother is still alive I would strangle her to death, knowing that I took 'care' of my past and the unnecessary people on the earth.. Mother’s Day is not to be joyous because it’s just an act for us, and me specifically to work for no reason, even with no monetary backing to get our maternal guardians. It's just a calendar day that was made by a greedy woman.. A woman exactly like my mother. And if she isn't dead.. I hope she will die soon and if I could have the chance to do it myself, that would be her Mother's Day gift.. from me... Stone Burns.
