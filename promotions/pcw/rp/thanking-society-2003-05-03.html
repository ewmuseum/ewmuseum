Thanking SOCIETY.

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 3, 2003 Begin Transmission. “You know, Mr. Chang, I’m getting quite tired of this.” (President Chang looked over at his massive charge quizzically, and responded.) “Tired of what?” “Of not having my own match to compete in.” (SOCIETY stands outside, in a dress shirt and slacks, untypical attire for the big man. He looks around, trying to put his feelings into words.) “Of not being able to step into the Primetime Championship Wrestling ring and introduce new superstars to the meaning of Conformity. The Ahutmoth found out, and he has not been seen since. Rich Cannon has been released after I cracked a steel chair into his head. And on Turmoil, Matt Jackson was Equalized. But…those last two were not in a match. Tell me, Chang, why did I go out and attack them?” (Michael looks up at SOCIETY, towering a foot over him..) “Because,” he said, “you need to get noticed. In professional wrestling, out of sight is out of mind. You must inspire awe in the announcers, the fans, and your fellow wrestlers. And, once you are noticed, your fame and glory will only rise.” “But why do I need fame,” SOCIETY asked. “I thought my goal was conformity..our goal to use this to spread the Order’s message! This is but a means to an end!” “Indeed, it is. But, you should have some fun while you do so. We can make ourselves a lot of money and notoriety, and both of those will help us as we establish our new Order.” (SOCIETY nods, as he begins to grasp what Chang has said.) “I see what you mean. So, if I continue to do well, I will get fame, glory, and money?” “Indeed you will, SOCIETY. Indeed you will. But the pathway to that continues through three men on Tuesday. And, I have some additional good news. You have been scheduled to compete in your first singles match on Friday. You will only face one opponent.” (The big man grins inwardly, the opportunity he has been awaiting at hand..) “Excellent!” SOCIETY exclaims, with no mirth or true joy in his voice. “I can now show the non-conformists of this world my skills in a fair combat. Although, for my opponent, it probably won’t be very fair…as I can only assume he is quaking in his boots at the prospects of having to face SOCIETY!”(Walking down the street, SOCIETY and Chang arrive at a jewelry store. Chang reaches out his hand, signaling for SOCIETY to halt.) “Alright, SOCIETY. We stop here.” SOCIETY was quizzical. “Why are we halting here?” “Well, my friend, I am here to get you a present, for a job well done thus far. A timepiece. I have had it prepared for you, and I want to give it to you know, in honor of your impending success.” (Chang throws the door of the establishment open, and nods to the owner.) “Good evening, Silvio. Is the watch ready?” “It is, sir.” (The owner nods respectfully, reaching behind his counter, and pulling out a beautiful watch.) “Here you are, Mr. Chang. Solid gold, studded with diamonds, as per your request.” (Chang reaches out, and places the glittering watch around SOCIETY’s waist.) “H-how much did this cost you, President?” “Cost is not important, SOCIETY. It is a gift to you from the Order.” (SOCIETY nods respectfully, admiring the object, before returning to his focus.) “My opponents are myriad..I face Krikorian, an astronomer, and a musician on Tuesday. Of these, I have only encountered Krikorian. The other two men are unknown to me, but they do not seem to pose much threat. Edwards, the starry eyed one, seems to be afraid of me…” “With good reason, wouldn’t you say?” (Silvio smiled after his comment, looking up at the huge man.) Chang was quick to respond. “Of course there is good reason! SOCIETY is mammoth! Immense! And the most capable member of the Order of Society and Logic!” (SOCIETY smiles..) “He does have reason to fear me, but on the opposite end of the spectrum, Shane Wethers does not seem to be concerned with anything but his pathetic music show. Of course, like everything else in his pathetic life, it shall fail!” Chang smiled evily at Silvio, asking for his leave. “Thank you for the watch, but it is time for us to leave now.. (Silvio nodded a goodbye, as SOCIETY and Chang head outside.) “Chang, I must be victorious Tuesday. And then, I have another match to win on Friday. As long as I do not lose, I shall continue to rise, and move on to Supremacy, and display just that! Rising Star Division, you must learn to CONFORM….TO SOCIETY!” End Transmission.
