Scream

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/09/02 'Neo Void God.' A sound like wet meat slipping along itself. 'Akiyama.' The noise of grinding teeth. 'The Divine Prophetess.' The dull thud of heavy footsteps. 'Cyclonica.' Nerezza stood alone in the parking lot of the Rose Garden Arena. His skin had gone from his pale colour to a fully darkened grey. His red sunglasses were gone, placed in the pocket of his coat. The purple hair that sunk into his shoulders and usually lined his back reached only as far as his neck. His arms grew closer and closer to the ground. The sound of stretching skin and bones clicking against each other silenced itself as deformed skeleton-like fingers gave birth to black claws. His jaw line extended far past the boundaries of a normal human, showing a small army of serrated and jagged ivory warriors. Each sang a song of hunger and blood. 'Anarchy...' The living nightmare lived up to his name. His eyes were a disturbing red shade and his human appearance had totally disappeared. In place was a creature from Hollywood. Sickeningly twisted flesh, devoid of the colour of life and any warmth. A face that bore no friendship, only the predatorial imagery of a fierce, carnivorous murderer. His entire frame hunched over. Now freakishly changed, the vampire's eyes twitched inside their sockets. With no other way to express himself, his claws curled up into his palms. The monsters brow lowered as he peered at the floor. His chest began to rise and fall as air necessary for talking, not breathing, gathered inside his cavernous ribcage. The beast's shoulders engulfed his neck and his head almost vanished into his body. The calm was over. The storm was about to begin. Words cannot describe a roar. Some pathetic efforts try ROAR! or RARRGH!. But you know when you hear a noise... a big loud noise that makes you stop immediately, like a huge bang outside your house? Or it's all quiet and then something behind you makes you freeze? Magnify it by a hundred. Now place it inside the echoing, empty space of the Rose Garden Arena's parking lot. Let the sound be amplified by the area. Now finally, think of everything everyone ever did wrong to you. Kids who bullied you. The time your parents just wouldn't get you that toy you wanted. The girl or boy who shattered your heart. Seeing or hearing about a loved one die. Come to tears if you must. Scream. You're still not even close to what ravaged the air that passed through the rows of teeth. The snaking tongue was shaken by it and even though his jaws ached from opening his lips that wide, Nascosto still had so much more to say. But nothing was left in his lungs. The futility of what he'd just done was apparent as his arms began to pull themselves back inside his sleeves. The sharpened nails regained their roundness and the black colour faded to pale fleshy pink. The very essence of rage wandered from his face as red became white once more and retreated inside the iris. The dark grey faded from it's deathly shade and became the normal skin colour. Finally, his jaw closed to an acceptable shape and only thirty-two of the ivory legion remained. The sunglasses once again sat on the tip of his nose. * * * So I looked for why I did it. I'd lost the match. I lost a stable mate. I even lost the privilege of causing misery to those rich snobs. It can't be that bad, surely? Listen to me, I'll only say this to a lesser being once. I have until the day that a giant wolf swallows the sun to live. Doomsday is when I leave. So how does something immortal kill time? I was in wrestling long before I became a vampire. So I'd become attached to it. After all, what else do I have? Killing for blood so I can sustain my other form? You can only butcher so many homeless drunks before someone makes a connection. So you make money to buy blood from some people without morals. You make money from doing what you're good at. In my case, I was trained to wrestle since I was a child. Fits, works, pays. But then you get too attached to it. Gaz and I have known each other since who knows when? Miyagawa, he doesn't talk to me much, but he leads us so I just take orders. But I was just getting to know Anarchy. She didn't mind that I'm a monster. She didn't mind when I said I'd probably drain her if I needed to. Then some... what in the hell was that thing? It was like me, above humans. I couldn't even touch it. Either way, it took Anarchy and smacked me so hard I didn't know what was going on. Took Gaz out too. Did it hurt Miyagawa too? I can't remember... ...but I remember that we didn't win the match either. More than that. It's not that we didn't win. We lost. We lost the #1 contendership too. To that un-groomed, alcoholic-smelling piece of excrement. Damn you, Duke Williams. Damn you too, Kannon. Damn the whole Front Office. Fines or suspensions for pranking? Have you all become so bitter that you have to make sure we have nothing left? * * * 'Having some quiet time, Squire?' came the only thing that had kept Nerezza from totally hating humanity. 'Hardly.' Nerezza replied. He was still facing away from his partner. 'Something wrong?' Gaz had more invited Nerezza to tell him, rather than asking. 'Miyagawa has no title. Miyagawa has no #1 spot. Asylum has no Anarchy. We have no fun' said the empty vampire. 'And I need...' 'I'll go for it. Just tell me who you get it from and I'll bring you back a bottle.' Gaz offered. Nerezza simply shook his head, swinging his ponytail back and forth. 'I need a walk anyway. I need to get away from this place anyway. The smell of it is offending me.' Still, he could sense that I had more to say. He wouldn't speak until I'd finished. 'Last week, we were riding high. We'd made fools of the Cowabunga Connection. We'd made fools of the entire roster. We'd even marketed a CD and publicly distributed those semi-nude photos of Ellie~! on the back. Now... we've gone from the crest of the wave to the trough.' 'It'll work out. C'mon. We have a shot at the tag team titles on the line, all we have to do is what we do best this Friday. We're the best thing to come of the Third Age! We were trained by Hessian, we made the final incarnation of NTICW worth watching, we rocked this place and we're Pranks With Attitude! Now we've done the pranks, so stop moping and show me some attitude!' With the pep talk in his mind, Nerezza lifted his sagging head up sharply. The gleam in his eyes shone once more and his fists tightened. The behemoth strode towards the door that led upwards to street level and planted a boot into it, taking it off the hinges and straight to the floor. A tick-tock of the boots faded slowly as the vampire went to collect his fuel. 'Damn...' Gaz coughed in awe. 'I'd better learn to watch my mouth'. He was right. We made waves with the new era of PCW. We were shown how to fight by bloody HESSIAN, of all people. The internet smarks called us 'The best thing in NTICW'. And as I marched towards that door to start my journey to my suppliers, I imagined, just for a moment, that the metal grille at the top was the armoured face of Akiyama...
