A World Of Wonder and A World Of Cheers

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/02 *Monologue* The Chef. The first PCW Grand Slam Champion, PCW Hall of Famer, and leader of International Incident. Chef’s had quite a PCW career and a lot of accomplishments. Chef was indeed the man of PCW. Was being the keyword unfortunately. Chef returned to the PCW as a former chef of the Taliban. Turning on his American fans and forming International Incident. The Chef’s merry men frustrated at America. So instead of joining their forces and fighting along with Chef’s Taliban comrades. They wrestle? Huh? Chef was a great wrestler. But I mean what has he done since his comeback? What has he really accomplished? Forming the International Incident providing American fans with bathroom breaks. What’s left of the floundering stable? Flaymin and Chef? At Turmoil Big Daveylicious gets to really make a name for himself as he fights Chef. Most critics would say Chef already has the match won with Chef’s history that’s understanding. But than again most critics are a$$holes anyway. Chef’s awfully confident about this match. He seems to think he gots a walk in the park. But how can you walk in the park when your legs are broken? (The scene fades to a man in a cheap suit known as Big Daveylicious’s agent, Tommy Stillinger next to him Big D’s lackey, Michael Graves. As we move back they are standing amidst the busy traffic of children, parents and foreign tourists that swarm the streets of Disney World. Tommy is quietly conversing with a guy in a Mickey Mouse suit. Graves, proudly wearing his recently purchased Mickey Mouse Ears Cap, stands by with a look of confusion and worry on his face.) Tommy Stillinger: (to the guy in the Mickey costume) So, you can't take off the mouse head no matter what? Mickey: Yeah. It really sucks, man. It gets hot. It stinks. Heck, you can't even take off the mouse head if you blow chunks. Tommy Stillinger: Blow Chunks? Are you a homosexual, macdaddy? Michael Graves: It’s nothing to be ashamed of. I accept your personal lifestyle. Mickey: No, I mean if you throw-up or vomit in the mouse head, you still can't take it off. Tommy Stillinger: Yeah, righhhhhhht. Well, talk to ya later, Mickey. (He looks over Graves shoulder puzzled.) Tommy Stillinger: Where’s Big D? Michael Graves: He’s at the “It’s A Small World” ride. Tommy Stillinger: What? Michael Graves: I tried to bring him with me but he got emotional saying “How beautiful it was.” The ride attendant tried to get him off but he powerbombed him. Tommy Stillinger: Good god! We must save him! (Graves and Tommy run off towards the entrance of “It’s A Small World” ride. They stop noticing a big line for the ride. Security guards are all urging a muscular bleached blonde headed man dressed in khaki shorts, white poly unbuttoned shirt, and the PCW Extreme title laying comfortably on the shoulder. ) Tommy Stillinger: It can’t be. (Unfortunately, for Stillinger the muscular figure is non other than Big Daveylicious with tears in his eyes. A security guard puts his hand on his shoulder.) Security Guard: Listen, buddy but it’s time to get off the ride and let somebody else have a turn. Big Daveylicious: No! I am the Extreme Champion! I want to ride it again! Security Guard: Sir, I’m asking you kindly to please get off the ride and let somebody else have a turn. Big Daveylicious: This is a world of wonder and a world of cheers. I mean it was peaceful they all got along. Security Guard: If you don’t get off this damn ride you’ll never get in Disney World again. Big Daveylicious: Can’t we just all get along? (As soon as he stands up the security guard grabs Big D by the arm and drags him out of the ride. Big D looks down to the floor all teary eyed with his head down bumping into Graves and Stillinger.) Tommy Stillinger: Big D, where’ve you been?! Big Daveylicious: *sniff* I was on the “Small World” ride. Tommy Stillinger: For 5 hours?! Big Daveylicious: It’s the most beautiful thing I’ve ever seen guys. All those little people getting along. You know it really touched my little tiny heart. You know it really is a world of wonder and a world of cheers. ...I love you guys! Michael Graves: I knew you crossed over! (Graves jumps onto Big D’s waist. Big D with a look of disgust pushes Graves off of him sending to the floor hitting the back of his head.) Big Daveylicious: I mean that as a male comfortable with his own heterosexuality. (Michael Graves holds his head in pain, dusts himself off, and climbs up to his feet.) Michael Graves: Damn tease. I’ll never get laid. Tommy Stillinger: Actually, Graves you almost scored with Minnie Mouse. Michael Graves: I know but when I was gonna kiss her but her head fell off! Big Daveylicious: Noooooooooo! You killed Minnie!(Big D hoists Graves up by his collar.) Big Daveylicious: Prepare to die, b*tch! Tommy Stillinger: You know Big D, Minnie isn’t real. Big Daveylicious: Well....he’s still gonna die! Mickey: Halt! I see that you wayfarers in search of jollity and relaxation have met with disagreement, disharmony and celastraceous turmoil of the first order! Stop your ceaseless bickering and follow me, my children! SPEAK TO ME, MOUSEKETEERS!!! (Big D pauses and drops Graves whom also pauses at Mickey.) Michael Graves: What’d Mickey say? Tommy Stillinger: I think he said “Hi” and “Oh, what a lovely tea party.” Big Daveylicious: Hey, Mickey! Mickey’s Da Mouse! Mickey: Actually, I’m not Mickey. Big Daveylicious: Huh? (Mickey takes off his head to reveal. Mad TV cast member, Will Sasso.) Michael Graves: Mickey beheaded himself. (Graves faints.) Big Daveylicious: Who are you? Will Sasso: I’m Will Sasso. Big Daveylicious: Can I have your autograph? Will Sasso: Sure, anything for a fan of the show. Big Daveylicious: Please sign it as Mickey. Will Sasso: Sure. (With that the scene fades to Mickeys head.)
