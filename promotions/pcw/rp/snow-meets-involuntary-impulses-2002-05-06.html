Snow meets Involuntary Impulses

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 6 Snow grins widely... this coming Tuesday, he along with the other members of New Breed will serve as guest referees in a match between Tyler Nelson and Big Alan Steele. No wonder Snow is grinning... he gets his major enemies all in the same place. Then again, we can bet that Ultimate Authority will be close at hand... Snow rests the morning newspaper down on the table and puts his face into his hands - he’s not used to being up this early. The small diner smells of breakfast, though we notice that there currently aren’t any customers. All the better for the PCW Champ - he’s never been fond of company... Waitress: “Are you ready to order?” No response... Waitress: “Sir?” She gets her response, not by words, but by a piercing glare from the young star’s cold blue eyes. Immediately, the waitress takes a step back... Waitress: “Um... are you ready to order yet?” Snow reluctantly decides that an insult will do nothing for him on this morning, and answers plainly... Snow: “Coffee.” The waitress walks away. She’s not a pretty girl, but would definitely be considered attractive by the majority of men. She’s of an average build, with large blue eyes and short black hair. As she walks off, she’s followed by Snow’s haunting, and accusing, eyes... Snow: “...wench...” We knew the Killin’ Villain couldn’t hold his tongue forever. He rolls his eyes slightly with bitter distaste and turns his head to a window. Snow looks tired - 9 am comes early for the Killin’ Villain. Other than that, Snow looks much the same as always. His long blond hair is hanging in his face somewhat and he’s dressed for cold weather, even though it is May and he’s in a well-heated diner... Waitress: “...Your coffee...” She sets the coffee, along with cream and sugar, in front of the Killin’ Villain, whose eyes are still drawn to something outside. Suddenly, the waitress takes us by surprise... Waitress: “GO(CENSORED)AMN ASS SH(CENSORED)T PISS F(CENSORED)K!!!!” Snow, in a jutting motion, leaps to his feet, knocking over his chair. His eyes are wide, but he has no idea what to say... Waitress: “I-I... I’m SO sorry! I’m so... I mean... it’s this thing I have. It’s my Tourettes. I’m so...” Snow: “W-what in the hell was that!?!?” The waitress, nearly in tears now, brings her hands to her face and continues begging for forgiveness... Waitress: “Mister, I’m so sorry. It’s my Tourettes, I can’t help it.” Snow gives her a look of accusation.... Waitress: “CU(CENSORED)T PU(CENSORED)SY F(CENSORED)K DAMN TITS!!” Tourettes slip again. Snow’s eyes grow even larger and he glares at the young, apologetic, waitress. She covers her mouth and speaks through her hands, her eyes welling with tears... Waitress: “Sir, I really...” Snow interrupts her harshly... Snow: “Damn it, wench! I have never in all my life heard someone so intentionally insult my Superiority and Greatness!” Waitress: “But sir! I can’t help it! It’s involuntary!” Snow scowls... Snow: “Involuntary... Spasms... chemical inbalance in the brain... I’ve heard all the excuses a million times!” The waitress can’t believe how insensitive Snow is being to her illness. She puts her face in her hands and runs off, leaving us with only the sound of her distant crying. Soon enough, an older man enters the scene. He’s obviously the owner of the establishment - owners always wear a look of confident on their face. He wears a white t-shirt that matches his hair along with faded blue jeans. He raises an eyebrow to Snow... Owner: “Hey... uh... is there a problem here, bub?” Snow: “You’re damn straight there’s a problem!” He points far off, where the waitress has retreated behind the diner’s front counter. In the background, we hear her faded words... Waitress: “SH(CENSORED)T F(CENSORED)K BITCH HIGH HEELS DAMN ASS!” Snow: “That’s the problem! Do you not know that I am a Great and SUPERIOR Individual!? Do you not know that I am the most SUCCESSFUL man in the history of PCW!? Can you not keep your wench from spewing obcenities!?” The owner shakes his head... Owner: “She can’t help it.” Snow: “Then FIRE her!” Waitress: BALL SACK BITCH TITS F(CENSORED)K ASS HELL!” The owner sighs and gives Snow an awkward look... Owner: “She has a disease... are you totally heartless?” Snow has to think about this question for a moment, as do the rest of us. Of course, there have been times that Snow has, indeed, seemed totally heartless. But on the other hand... well, on the other hand, there have been more times that Snow has been totally heartless. Before the Killin’ Villain can give an answer, however, the old owner leans in and whispers... Owner: “Besides, if I were to do that, she would sue me for wrongful dismissal.” Snow shakes his head in fury, not completely grasping the situation. He puts a finger in the face of the owner and looks at him threateningly... Snow: “I - you walking definition of pathetic - am the Killin’ Villain, Jason Snow. The PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World! And she is interrupting me while I contemplate the thousands of ways that I am going to humiliate and torture my opponents at Supremecy!? Do you know who my pathetic opponents are!?” In the background, we hear the Waitresses Tourettes acting up again... Waitress: “CO(CENSORED)KSUCKING FAGGOT BASTARDS!” There’s a long moment of silence while Snow stares through a pair of wide blue eyes at the cursing waitress. After the moment passes, he glances at the owner of the establishment and nods... Snow: “By Greatness... she’s exactly right!” An awkward moment passes as the waitress points a thumb to her chest. Snow’s eyes are wide and the owner looks frustrated with the insanity of the entire situation... Waitresses: “M-me? Right about... what?” Snow: “Right about Tyler Nelson and that flaming homosexual, Big Alan Steele.” With shifty eyes, the waitress approaches the Killin’ Villain again as he continues... Snow: “I mean, I had always known about the blatent homosexuality of Big Alan Steele... but to think...” Snow scratches his stubbly chin... Snow: “All this time, Tyler Nelson himself has been one of them! By Greatness, THAT explains all the PCW shows in San Fransisco!” Snow, scowling and shaking his head, grabs his jacket from the floor - it was dropped there when his chair tumbled. He then grabs his coffee, takes a sip, and tosses the rest on the floor, shattering the mug - the scowl sticking to his face the whole time. After he exits, the owner stares in his direction with awe spread all over his face. Calmly, he says to the waitress... Owner: “You know... maybe next time we get a famous celebrity in here, you should let me take his order.” The waitress rolls her eyes... Waitress: “Ah, f(CENSORED)k you!” The owner doesn’t even turn around to look at her... Owner: “The Tourettes, right?” A guilty look comes over the waitresses face before she kneels down to begin picking up the shards of the broken mug. As the owner’s brow furrows, our PCW Camera man no longer feels the need to be in the Diner, and the scene fades to black...
