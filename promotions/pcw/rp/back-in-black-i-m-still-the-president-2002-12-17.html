Back In Black: I'm still the President

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Back In Black: I'm still the President Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/17/02 The shot opens up on the construction site of the new PCW towers in the quaint, brainwashed, town of Hubbardville, Wisconsin. Walking onto the site with authority wearing a yellow hard hat and Carhart work cloths is Duke Williams. His demeanor and attitude are that of a superior being. Williams: What in the name of George McGovern is going on here? Worker: We’re building the new PCW towers. Williams: Like hell you are. Cease all operations immediately and pack up your bags. Worker: Who in Hubbard are you? Williams: I’m Duke Williams PRESIDENT of PCW and in charge of this build. Worker: The CEO Xavier Kannon is over seeing this operation. Williams: No, correction…he was. While him and his monkeys, and fat ass wife, were looking for a loophole to terminate my position as president. I found one that specifies my duties as President. You see as an un-elected staff member his duties are purely fiscal. He approved a massive budget to move OUR operations from New Jersey to this town. Now as an elected official I have the responsibility to carry out the spending and the planning of the new building based on the wants and needs of my constituents who are the locker room of PCW. So to put it in construction slang for you hoss….fuck you its my show. Worker: Let me get my boss. Williams: Bring the whole goddamn crew since I’m bringing in my own people. Hard working union workers from Chicago, Illinois guaranteed to get the job done right. The worker pouts away as the “Malice Man” looks over the assembled frame of the building. He points out the large room where his office should be. Along with the redneck floor of PCW dubbed the Southern Comfort Zone voted by the people of PCW 15-4 in a private caucus. Williams: I truly am a President of the common folk. Just call me Jimmy Carter. Now X you may have gotten me on Adrenaline but I’m still not impressed. No not by a long shot. You my friend are nothing but a turtle’s dick in my eyes. Have you ever seen a turtle’s dick? Neither have I but I bet they’re tiny and insignificant. Just like you Kannon. Sunday your going to get circumcised Malice Style. A man comes rushing towards Duke with contracts in his hand and arms flinging. It’s Mitchell Blake chief architect on the project. Blake: Who the hell do you think you are talking to my man like that? Duke just smirks as the man goes on. Blake: If you’re not out of here in fifteen minutes I’ll have the Hubbard police haul you off and they are not very pleasant to outsiders in these parts. Williams: Really?? How do they treat their own? Because you’re trespassing now the project is under my jurisdiction. I have a construction crew driving up from Palatine, Illinois right now. Blake: I have a contract. Williams: Congratulations I have a hard on. Since when have they allowed broads to do mans work? Duke winks over at a hot blonde construction chick as she passes by. The women of Hubbardville are so pure its sickening. The cold wind whips down from Canada onto the construction site as the men argue. Blake: This is not a laughing matter. This is business I have a contract. Williams: Yes with Xavier Kannon who has no power other then budget. Sounds like you may have sue Kannon. That’s a pity. Six big construction trucks pull into the site from Palatine. All the workers from Hubbardville gather behind Blake with wrenches and saws in their hands. Williams: Looks like we got our selves a stand off. Who’s got the quicker draw cowboy?? Blake: Get those men out of here this is not happening. Let me call Mr. Kannon. Williams: You can call him till his asshole whistles Dixie. You see this bandage on my head? He put it there. You think I’m really in the mood to deal with his shit, your shit, your mother’s shit, anybodies shit? No I’m not dealing with it very well. So by the letter of the law I’m in charge here. Now that may piss you off and you may be out of some money but guess what…I don’t give a shit. Merry HubbardMas and fuck you. Blake: My response is tough shit we aren’t moving. Williams: Then the project doesn’t get done. Blake: That’s fine by me we get paid per day. Williams: If the project don’t get done then the offices stay in New Jersey till then. Blake: Yea so. Williams: No I’m just thinking out loud. Yea that sounds good ok we have an official site shut down pending a courts decision. Blake: That could take years. Williams: Oh shit. Duke smiles an arrogant almost Kannon like smile as he walks away. Blake is not as happy as he explains to his workers what is happening. Duke walks over to the Hubbardville inn talking as he strolls. Williams: Inter office bullshit is never healthy for a business. As a matter of fact I have reversed all taxes placed on our staff and given everybody nice Christmas bonuses for a great year. You see Kannon you needed me in your control to make your Camelot and you had me. But like your forefather before you Mr. Tyler Nelson you too have let greed get the best of you. It has corrupted your instant to rule. Duke reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a Punch brand Churchill size cigar. He stops to turn his back to the wind as he lights it. Williams: Now your office building is on legal hold. How do you like them apples? I know you, your probably throwing a little bit of a fit right now. Very fitting really. You get so childish sometimes X. I mean look at the temper tantrum you threw on fusion. It reminded me of my kid holding his breath till he turned blue to make a point. Duke accents his point with a smoke ring produced from the fine cigar smoke. Williams: When are you going to learn X that you can’t out smart me, out play me, or out jive me. I’m the cool, kick ass, cowboy, with a short temper, and lots of power. You had me on your side and just like you did to Webster. You blew it. Duke laughs at his play on words like a painter admires his finished painting. Williams: This Sunday or sooner your going to be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound and kid that’s the Presidential, Man of the People, Honky Tonk Truth. Duke walks into the hotel as the camera fades. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
