Morbid Konquest

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/12/02 'Gaz....? GAAAZ..?' Nerezza screamed out, looking backstage for his partner. Figuring that the dressing room they had been assigned was not occupied, Nerezza had taken to the backstage area in search of the large hollow metal Portakabin that P.W.A had reserved as a spare habitat. The vampire would find his target in the parking lot, except with a modification to it - above the door hung a plaque with the Mortal Kombat dragon symbol on it. Figuring another madness-stricken idea, Nerezza knocked the door first, causing a giant, hollow clang to echo throughout the parking lot. After a few seconds, Nerezza was pulled inside by swift hands and the door was slammed behind him. Before the vampire could bring himself to his senses, he turned to see Gaz in a most peculiar outfit. Gaz's green tuxedo had been replaced by a simple white tunic that covered him from the neck to the waist with tightened, elastic cuffs. His legs were covered by the same material and white bandages encased his ankles and feet. Around his head was a tight balaclava with the face cut out entirely and for some reason, a wicker waste-paper bin. 'Rezz! No time to explain, hurry up and get changed!' The jester commanded, shoving a bundle of clothes into Nerezza's arms and stumbling outside to allow the vampire privacy to change. Examining the strange attire, Nerezza began to take off his ankle length vinyl duster and replaced it with what looked to be a black and yellow jumpsuit... * * * Emerging from the metal structure a few minutes later, Nerezza stepped out in a tight-fitting jumpsuit, all black with the exception of some shin guards, arm pads, a belt and shoulder pads that ran down the chest to connect with the belt. On his face was mask that covered from his nose to his chin, all coloured in yellow. 'Alright, Gaz. Explain what the heck these outfits are for?' demanded the Living Nightmare. 'Quite simple, Squire...' giggled the Lord Of Laughter, casting an amused glance towards his partner. 'This sunday on Adrenaline, we take on the notorious Black Dragons. I know nothing of this pair, so allow me to interpret. The Black Dragons are the criminal organisation in Mortal Kombat, right? Thus, I deduce this - The Black Dragon Gang, led by Kano, are here NOT to wrestle, but to steal the Front Office's treasure. We, as the warriors of the earth realm, must foil this plot!' 'I see...' scoffed Nerezza. 'And who might we be?' 'I AM RAYDEN, GOD OF THUNDER AND LIGHTNING!' exclaimed Gaz, holding up his hand buzzer and creating a shower of sparks. 'And you shall be the tormented soul, Scorpion!' 'What the hell? Kano is JOHN Kano, not the baldie with the red eye, you mook!' Nerezza cursed, shaking his fist violently. 'He's hiding it from us! He's trying to keep it all a secret, he even changed his finishing move from the Heart Rip to the Kano-Plex to avoid arousing suspicion! Do you see a cannonball roll anywhere in the archives of his matches? Or has he thrown a single knife? He's covering his tracks!' Gaz shrieked, gesturing with his hands to illustrate the cannonball roll. Nerezza stared at him blankly for a moment, wishing he could pull off his mask and breathe fire to incinerate Gaz. 'Listen, we need to think our strategy here... even if Kano was indeed the cyborg, then the Black Dragons outnumber us, three to two.' Nerezza frowned. 'Then let us... how you say... even things out?' Came a familiar, eastern voice. Gaz and Nerezza turned around to see a jaw dropping sight - Natalia Miyagawa, dressed from head to toe in deep blue velvet. On her head, a small headband kept her auburn hair out of her distinct eyes while a mask much like Nerezza's hid the majority of her identity. A high necked, sleeveless leotard clung tightly to her torso and gloves that came past the elbows covered her dainty arms. A little black leather belt hung around her hips, there for decoration only and her thigh-high boots left little to the imagination. In her hands were two folded-up fans that added a look of elegance to the sheer stunning appearance. Gaz's smile instantly rammed itself into the corners of his mouth and even Nerezza's eyebrow arched itself in amusement. 'I'd say we just had an addition to the perfect two...' Nerezza smirked, his fang gleaming. 'And what a perfect two...' Gaz whispered under his breath, adjusting the blue sash on his tunic. 'So, Gaz, why are we dressed like this?' questioned the soviet beauty. She flicked one of her fans out and began to cool herself. 'This suit is rather hot.' 'You're telling me...' The Jester replied with his typical cheeky grin. 'Well, I thought we'd begin by testing the moves we shall use this Sunday on the Black Dragons. First of all, Kitana's fatality is the kiss of death...' 'GUH...' Nerezza slapped a palm to his forehead and turned around as Natalia slid down her mask to advance on Gaz. 'I'll be off throwing spears if you need me...' The vampire began to walk away, then stopped dead in his tracks. 'And Gaz... watch out because that move might have you ending up looking like Anna Nicole!'
