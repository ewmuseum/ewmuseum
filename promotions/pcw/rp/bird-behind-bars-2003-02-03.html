Bird Behind Bars

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-3-03 Once again I found myself in the police station. This time however, it was for a completely different reason. I was arrested for crimes I didn't commit. I can remember being at home with Tessa. It was late into the morning but we was still sleeping. I don't like sleeping late, in fact I hate sleeping at all. That is when the horrible nightmares come. However, since Tessa and I have been sharing a room, all of that has disappeared. Finally I am able to get the sleep I long deserve. I was cudded up next to Tessa, her head laying on my chest as my arms wrapped around her. I could have stayed there forever but a knock at the door woke me from my slumber. At first I thought it was the children wanting our attention. 'Just give us a few more minutes please.' I whined, hating to be woken from such a wonderful sleep. I looked over at Tessa. She was still sound asleep. I nudged my nose against her cheek, starting to rub my face against her's. The knocking continued. I knew I wasn't going to get any sleep. When those children wanted something, they was more then likely going to get it. That or keep bothering us until we finally did break down and give it to them. I rolled out of bed and walked to the door. I was in just a pair of sleeping pants, flannel, and a white t-shirt. Tessa was dressed out in a cute pair of pink pajamas. I grabbed the doorknob and turned it, however when I stuck my head out to see where the kids where, no one was around. 'It must have just been a prank' I thought to myself. Then the knocking came again. It was coming from the front door. I rubbed the sleep out of my eyes as I walked up to the front door. As soon as I opened the door I was greeted by the two cops I had met only a few weeks ago about Evan. However, this time they was on a more serious business. Before I could react I was being pulled outside and handcuffed. Tessa walked out of the bed room to see me getting pushed up against the wall, face first, my hands being cuffed behind my back. She started to run to me but the other cop intercepted her. Tessa tried to fight past him, her face crying with concern and confusion. I could hear the metal clank as the cuffs was tighten around my wrist. The officer arresting me was reading me my rights. 'What did I do wrong? What's going on here?' I asked. My pleas fell on deaf ears as the cop continued to read me my rights. The other officer was bringing Tessa back into the house. The officer starts to pull me out of the house and into the police car. The last thing I saw from that moment was my head being forced into the police car and having to watch Tessa crying through the window. So now here I am, inside the police station once more. However this time it's a little more serious. I was sitting behind the desk of the cop that arrested me. He was the same one I filed the adoption paperwork for Evan with. The only thought going through my head at the moment now was something went wrong with the adoption process or something. My heart was inside my lung. I didn't need this type of stress right before a big match like the tag team rematch. I needed to keep my focus on P.W.A. and that trickster Gaz. Not sitting behind a officer's desk on unknown charges. The officer that arrested me walked toward his seat, a plain vanilla folder in his hand. He sits down and tosses the folder towards me. The folder opened up and pictures spread out in front of me. I looked down confused at what was going on. There was images of a park. Yet it was destoried. Tessa and my name was spray painted and craved into trees. I scratched my head and looked back at the officer. 'What is all of this?' I asked him. Was I arrested because of this? It didn't make any sense. I could only look toward the officer for help and information. He has a serious look to his face. That didn't make me feel any better. He put both his elbows on the table and held up his head with his palms. 'You know this won't help you with your trying to adopt Evan, correct?' he coldly said. That was the last thing I wanted to hear. What in the world is going on? I am arrested from my house, thrown into the back of a police car, and then told because some park is destoried that I might lose Evan. All this confusion was starting to get me angry. I hate not knowing what is going on almost as I hate sleep. I slammed my open palm onto his desk, causing him to jump slightly out of shock. 'Look what is going on here? Why are you showing me these pictures?' I finally blurted out. The cop regained his calm manner. 'We believe Tessa and yourself destoried the park.' he flat out told me. I nearly hit the floor. How could someone believe we would do something like that? I looked at one picture of a bench completely destoried. 'Ok look at that, how do you suppose we did that?' I slide the picture his way to let him get a closer look at it. He scratched his chin as he looked at it. He looked around for something then leaned in toward me. He motioned for me to lean forward too. He whispers to me, 'I know you two didn't do this. But the serg wants someone to blame for this. That park was completely destoried and he needs someone to cover the expenses.' I sat back in my chair shocked. So I was taking the fall for someone else because the state wanted money to pay to get the park fixed. Needless to say I was a little more then angry. Then an idea hit me. The officer looked at me confused as the smile crossed my face. I stood up and looked down at him. 'Mind if I make a phone call?' I asked nicely. The cop nodded and extended his hand to show his phone on his desk. I picked it up and started to dial a number. 'Hello Tessa, hey it's me. Look don't worry, everything is fine. I need you to do me a favor. Bring my check book down to the police station. Don't worry about why, just do it and make it quick. I love you, bye.' I hung up the phone. The officer looked at me confused as I sat back down. I leaned back in the chair and picked up the phone again. After dialing more numbers I answered, 'Hey Lars, look man do me a favor. Let the newspapers know I am going to donate a large sum of money to the state. For that park that was destoried. Yeah, that is the one. Look tell them to rename the park, 'The Tessa Windsor park' after I donate all the money. Think you can handle all of that? Good call me back at the house later, I'm down at the station right now. Thanks man.' I hung up the phone again. 'What are you planning to do Chris?' the officer asked. I smiled at him with my grin, tapping a finger against my bottom lip. 'If someone wants to try and mess with me, I'll show them that Chris Storm can and will stay on top. I'll simply donate the money needed to repair the park and in return all I ask if they rename the park for Tessa.' I told him with a smile. Yes, sure it was sucking up to Tessa. But I loved her and if I could name a park anything, it would be after her. The officer smiled at me and extended his hand to shake mine. 'Well that will help you alot with trying to adopt Evan. In fact I plan to put forth a good word in for you and speed along this process as much as I can.' he told me. I smiled even brighter. We shook hands and I was free to go. I prouded marched to the front of the police station. I was going to wait for Tessa to show up so I could tell her the good news. I laughed out loud, simply excited. P.W.A. is going to have one hell of a hard time trying to get the titles back from Tessa and I. I love her more now then ever. That bond will be the fuel to the fire to help us keep these titles. Friday can't come soon enough for me.
