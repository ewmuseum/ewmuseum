The Past, The Present, and Fidel (Part 3)

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/02 {The scene opens up with the camera zooming out from the sun and panning around a small city in Cuba. Suddenly it passes over two people who stand out in the crowd. One being a tall and muscular man wearing a baggy pair of blue jeans and a tight white shirt with a blue dragon on the right shoulder. The other is a small man in stature almost a soccer player of sorts. Wearing imported leather shoes, baggy leather pants a loose silk shirt with the Portuguese flag on it, wearing a pair of Oakley sunglasses, and over his shoulder is a fur coat.} {Zooming closer on the two it is noticeable that they are at a cigar stand purchasing the ever famous CUBAN CIGARS~. The huge man pulls out a fat wad and pays for a large box of cigars. After taking the box the two look at each other and stay on their way.} {As the men pass the camera everyone can now see, if they didn’t know before, that these people are none other than Golden Game. On a break from their Cuba tour for International Incident, the two go around seeing the sites in Cuba. Soon Juan begins to chuckle as Kevin looks at the unopened box of cigars then to Juan.} Kevin: Yo dude what’s so damn funny? Juan: I was just thinkin’ about our days after PWA. Kevin: Yeah what about’em? {Screen gets all fuzzy.} {BEGIN FLASHBACK SEQUENCE.} {The scene cuts to Kevin and Juan inside a lobby of sorts. Kevin just walking around in a pair of windbreakers and an EW T-shirt carrying his duffel bag looks for his new room. After getting inside he throws his bag down and takes a look outside a window near his bed. Seeing a few people walking from their cars to the entrance. Then a slight knocking on the door causes Kevin to turn around. Slowly turning around he notices, what his father would call, a wee little man.} Kevin: Hey bra, you must by my new roommate. {The man looks at Kevin and stares.} Kevin: Dude just don’t stand there ya know, take off yer shoes and c’mon in. This is just a big party, relax a little. {The man steps in and hands Kevin a piece of paper. Kevin looks at it and reads it aloud.} Kevin: Juan Borro from Lisbon, Portugal and Kevin Klaymore from Detroit, MI USA to bunk in room 28. P.S. Kevin, Juan speaks little English please help him out. {Kevin looks over at Juan and sighs.} Kevin: Great! I’m stuck with a foreign dude who can’t speak English. This is just peachy. Juan: Heya me knows some ting in inglis. I no stupid foreigner. {Kevin looks on with a bit of confused look on his face.} Juan: You mussa be man wit weely strong moosles. You is big man. Kevin: Well I’m a big man and I can kick some serious butt. Anyways I’m Kevin Klaymore… {He extends his hand out waiting for a handshake. Juan gives Kevin a firm and strong handshake.} Juan: I am Juan Borro. Kevin: Man that name sounds really familiar… Juan: I play football in home country. Was more better than all. Kevin: I was probably visitin my abuela in Spain and seen you play on T.V. {END FLASHBACK} {Scene returns to Kevin and Juan walking down the streets sharing in a laugh. As they continue to walk they pass a group of people outside of a governmental establishment that gives the people their share of food and money. Kevin looks at all of them standing in a line and a military like person jumps to the front of the line.} {BEGIN FLASHBACK SEQUENCE} {Scene changes to Kevin and Juan outside a night club waiting in line. As they wait a limo pulls up and a man with several ladies get out and walk in.} Juan: That isn’t fair Kevin. That man can’t cut everyone this not fair. {Juan jumps up and runs toward the man with Kevin following right behind. Juan grabs the shoulder of this man before he enters.} Juan: Who do you tink you are? Ricky Martin: I am the great Ricky Martin and I can do anything I want. Juan: I can beat you dancing. You are how you say… suck. Ricky: Really well then prove it then. {The three men enter the night club and a high tempo’d song begins to play. Ricky trying to be impressive goes all out first not wasting anytime. Shaking his bon bon and going all over the place. He stops and looks at Juan.} Ricky: Let’s see you beat that oh great and better dancer man. {Juan gets out there and SHOWS RICKY MARTIN OUT~. He puts Ricky to shame by mixing several dance styles into one that keeps the crowd in awe.} {As the music quits the crowd roots for Juan. Ricky getting jealous runs after Juan and is met with the Golden Goal from Juan. Two big bouncer men get up and go after Juan but are cut off by Kevin and a couple of roundhouse kicks. Kevin motions for Juan and they both leave the joint.} Juan: You know many fight styles Kevin… I’m supri-said. Kevin: Well I know lots of things and just remember those japanese use martial arts to win matches. Juan: But will not matter because… We are da chumpions Kevin: Yes we are the champions Kevin and Juan: We are the champions/chumpions of da world. {END FLASHBACK} {The scene returns to Kevin and Juan laughing even harder as Juan is almost in tears.} Juan: I can’t believe I used to talk like that. Even beating Ricky Martin in a Bon Bon shaking contest. {Kevin falls down laughing at that sound.} Kevin: Hahahahahahaha whoa dude cut it out man, that’s so totally hilarious. I still… hahahahaha can’t believe some of the stuff we did. Or in your case didn’t do. Juan: What? What didn’t I do. Kevin: Well back in PWA I don’t think you ever had a match before getting bunked with me at EW. {Juan gets a little smirk and answers.} Juan: That’s because they seen my greatness and knew I was too good to fight in the PWA. {Juan begins to smirk a bit more, then Kevin getting a little smirk of his own picks Juan up in a gorilla press like position.} Juan: Whoaaaaaaaaaa. Dude lemme down! Ahhhhhhh {Kevin, messing around, is about to let Juan down by throwing him but reaches back and holds on.} Juan: Not that waaaaaay. Kevin: Who’s the greatest now? {Kevin let’s Juan down gracefully. Juan dusts himself off and smacks Kevin in the back of his head.} Juan: I am… {Juan runs away as fast as he can with Kevin behind him.} Kevin: Oh when I catch you I’ll show you the greatest. Juan: Like I say you have to catch me first.
