Revenge Saga Part 6: Jaden a Hindrance?

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 6: A Hindrance? Crichton stood at the top of the hill, over looking the contents of a military base, just outside the borders of New York City. His knee-long black leather jacket blowing slightly in the wind, as he extracts a pair of binoculars from his coat pocket. He looks on for a second then places the binoculars up to his eyes. At closer look at the base, it was very active, trucks going here, soldiers jogging there, a lot more active than most military bases for this time of day. This must be the place! He surmised gleefully. Crichton had positioned himself on top of the hill earlier that morning and was expecting an ‘old friend’ to visit the base. The penultimate person that filled his revenge list, the rich industrialist named Miss Zemoff. He acquired information from a very reliable source that she was coming to this military base to test out one of her newest weapons designs. Hence the commotion within the military base. His source said that the show would start at 12pm, it was now 11.45am, so she was due any minute. Confident that Miss Zemoff was nowhere on the base, he places the binoculars back into his pocket and sits down on the grassy hill. Over the last couple of days Crichton’s feelings had been mixed about his whole mission of revenge. On one hand he still wanted to keep his vow that he made over the grave of his not long dead manager, to reap revenge on those who hunted the both of them across America and made their lives a living hell. On the other hand he wanted to leave all this life of pain and suffering behind him and settle down with his new found love Jaden. That name made his stomach churn with delight and the end of every one of his twenty digits tingle. The same effect Aeryn had on him when he first started seeing her, all those months ago. That was the thing that worried him the most, his feelings for Aeryn. It hadn’t been more than three months since Crichton buried his love Aeryn, beside the freshly destroyed military base in Connecticut, and now he was seeing someone else. He had no way of gauging whether this was too soon to be getting involved with someone else, the army saw to that. Was it too soon to start anything up with Jaden? that was one of the questions that had been rolling around in his head for the better part of twelve hours, when he and Jaden first got ‘close’. It all started a couple of days ago, when Politically Incorrect came to his and Andy Murray’s locker room and offered them a place in they’re new stable. A stable that claimed to be the future of the PCW and Crichton believed this to be true. So he and Murray heartily accepted to join the stable. For a very long time Crichton had been somewhat a loner, aside from his deceased manager, Aeryn Sun and even when placed in the Forgotten One’s stable, he felt alone. He was sure that his new stable would be different, he was quite confident in that matter. Then he met Jaden and the whole thing started. Crichton knew that something was going to happen when she rang him on his mobile and asked for his help. That night was very memorable indeed. And so a hindrance was formed. His feelings for Jaden hindered his vowed mission for revenge. He no longer felt the anger or hatred that drove him to kill those who wronged him in the past, his feelings for Jaden got in the way. His thoughts were no longer based solely upon hatred, but warm feelings for the woman currently lying in his double bed, in a motel near the city centre. Could he actually go through with these rampant cold-blooded murders, he justifies as ‘revenge’, without having the feelings of anger or hate with his conscious state? Did Crichton even want to try? What if he went down there, distracted with thoughts of Jaden and get himself killed. It would be exactly what happened with Aeryn and he so couldn’t put anyone else though the same mental agony as that. Crichton couldn’t bear the thought of it. Then Crichton’s concentration is broken, at the sight of the commotion currently taking place near the security barrier at the military base entranceway. Crichton swiftly extracted the binoculars from his knee-long leather jacket, once more and puts them up to his eyes. With magnification of the scene, he recognises the black, convertible, Mustang as it passes by the raised security barrier and heads deeper into the heart of the military base. Crichton lowers the binoculars from his eyes and strokes his chin a little. This wasn’t good, wasn’t good at all. The car belonged to Mr. Fleming, the man who shot Aeryn, the man Crichton assumed to be dead in the explosion he created at the Military base in Connecticut. If Crichton hadn’t seen it with his own eyes, he surely wouldn’t have believed it. Fleming was still alive. His target Ms. Zemoff was sat next to him in the Mustang, but Crichton hadn’t given her a second thought. To be honest he was half tempted to just leave Ms. Zemoff for the time being and go back to the Motel where he left the loveable Jaden, he was even contemplating giving up on his vowed mission totally. The sight of Mr. Fleming changed all of that instantly. Bottom line was that he still loved Aeryn and he simply couldn’t let her killer continue to walk the streets unpunished. He might have almost been able to forgive Ms. Zemoff, almost at least, but that was a different story now. He was now able to kill two birds with one stone, as the clichéd saying went. One thing that did intrigue him though, was the fact that she and Fleming were riding in the same car. Was Fleming now workign for Ms. Zemoff? he mused, placing the binoculars back up to his eyes. The magnification once again allows him to see the black Mustang in more detail, as Fleming parks the car at the far end of the Military compound, next to massive field. Must be where the advanced weapons testing were to take place. he surmised. Then he saw something else very interesting indeed. As Zemoff and Fleming get out of the car, they have a little passionate kiss before, opening they’re respective doors and heading towards the seats, which lay before the field. Very interesting indeed! It seemed that Ms. Zemoff and Fleming were now together! He really didn’t know what disgusted him the most, the thought of them two evil bastards having sex, or the realisation that if it weren’t for him those two would never have met! Regardless, this thought didn’t last more than five seconds, until another striking thought entered his brain. He had figured a much more…fitting way to punish Fleming. At least for the moment anyhow. Whatever distractions that were once rampant in his mind before seeing Fleming were now gone, realising that his feelings for Jaden were not a hindrance to his vowed mission after all, but a reminder of his love for Aeryn. He also realised that he hadn’t got over Aeryn yet, hadn’t even begun to mourn her passing and probably wouldn’t until his mission ended, until he no longer had his vowed mission to honour her memory. He wasn’t quite sure where this left himself and Jaden, but he was relieved to know that she was no longer a hindrance to any aspect of his life. Something that the two of them could build upon, once his mission of revenge was over and his mourning for Aeryn complete. Until that time, he couldn’t risk the objectives of his mission to obscure anymore, but wasn’t sure what to do in that aspect. Anyway, it isn’t the time to go through this now! He silently declares. Crichton once again places the binoculars back into his pocket, and fastens up the last couple of top buttons upon his knee-long leather jacket, finding it a little difficult with his black leather gloves on. He then begins to make his way down the side of the hill, towards the wire mesh perimeter fence that supposedly kept them in and everyone else out. The barb wire strategically placed at the top of the fifteen foot fence, added the icing to the cake. The sight of the fence could be conceived as ‘daunting’ by very many a person, but Crichton had scaled one fence to freedom all those months ago and now doubted he would have any trouble scaling the fence again. He mentally noted the irony in his actions, he was now trying to scale a military base perimeter fence to gain entry to the base, rather than gain escape from it. Crichton felt an odd tingling of excitement zip through his body, as he neared the fence. He stood not three meters away from the fence and eyed it up and down. A strange thought enters his mind, would my new found senses allow me to scale the fence more efficiently? he silently debated. He cocked his head to one side to size up the fence once more. There was nobody else here, nobody to chuckle if he failed and fell on his ass. Truth be told, Crichton was still having a hard time adjusting to his heightened powers and still didn’t know what the full range of those powers were. Crichton rocked back and forth, mentally preparing himself for the task at hand, and then simply let himself go. Whilst off the ground, he realise he had his eyes closed and was missing the experience of it all, but the wind rushing through the air, told him that there was some height to the jump. Slowly he forced his eyes open, to see the ground rushing towards him at an incredible force, or more accurately the roof of a barracks rushing towards him at an incredible speed. Crichton managed to turn back, to see gauge how far he had travelled, and found that he was not far from the fence, the wind had probably took him away. Crichton looked down again, and prepared himself for the inevitable collision, figured that if he landed on the roof, not making a scene at all, he might be able to get away with his stealth tactic. After all, his entrance into the compound had been less than stealthy to say the least. Crichton’s eyes forced closed as the impact with roof became ever more imminent. As soon as his feet impacted with the roof, his eyes were jarred open, just in time to witness the roof caving in under his feet and the majority of the roofing collapsing in upon itself. The chaos is short lived as the roof collapses in almost instantly, with the force of Crichton’s collision. The haze escaping from the broken plasterwork obscures the scene, momentarily at least. As the haze clears we can see the full extent of the impact caused by Crichton, the plaster work and the wood are in one big heap, drawing to the centre of the room, with beds and lockers barely recognisable amongst all of the carnage. Crichton had managed to take a building in one go, destructive to say the least, efficient? No. No movement at all can be seen from the wreckage that was once an army barracks…. To be continued….
