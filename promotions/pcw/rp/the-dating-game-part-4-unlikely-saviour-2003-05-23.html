The Dating Game Part 4 (unlikely saviour)

23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/5/03 There were many possible locations that James could have taken the beautiful brunette Michelle as part of his clause in the television show ‘the dating game’ picture if you will a quaint little French restaurant, perfect candle lit mood lighting, a string quartet playing delicately in the background, whilst waiters dressed immaculately poured the finest wine into your crystal glass. James however had wanted to impress Michelle as if the truth be told he was a bit smitten and it was during a moment of madness fuelled by pheromones that he had said the two worst words that he could have done in that situation. I’ll cook. Whilst it is a well known fact that the worlds best chefs are men this is a very small majority of the population and what is also a widely known fact is that for every famous chef who can whip up a soufflé in two seconds flat, there is at least a thousand men who would not know the difference between cabbage and cauliflower, James was one of these people. Standing in his kitchen staring blankly at the microwave James was wondering about just how wise his decision to cook was, he was earning money now and could easily have afforded to take a beautiful woman out for a meal, but no he said he would cook in a moment of madness and all he had in at the moment was a box of microchips, half a packet of wafer thin ham and some yoghurt, which at some point was milk but time makes fools of us all. James glanced at his watch and panicked it now read seven fifty five that left him five minutes for the world to swallow him whole or for some magic food to suddenly reappear. ‘Ding dong’ the doorbell rang which led to James’ heart leaping to his stomach she was here, Michelle was here and James had nothing absolutely nothing, sighing woefully James trudged towards the door and opened it slowly. There she was Michelle wearing the smallest red dress imaginable clinging to her body tightly showing off her curves, James’ mouth dropped slightly until he became aware he was nearly drooling. James: Hey Michelle you look, look wow. Michelle giggled coyly as she flung her hair back slightly over her shoulder, the hair glistening with shine and strength. Michelle: Well I had to try, for you. What have you got prepared for me then tonight are you going to show me your lunar module like you said? There is silence for a moment, not the comfortable silence, which can be experienced within a relationship, but a silence of total nervousness, before an even more nervous laugh. James: Well I was thinking that first we have a few glasses of wine whilst I put the finishing touches on dinner, if that’s ok with you that is? Secretly James was hoping that Michelle would want to just go out get something to eat of course at times like this, what you hope for never comes true. Michelle: Sure that sounds great I can’t wait to hear all about you what you do, I’ll admit when you first came round from the screen I was disappointed as I was told it was a celebrity version of the show but then I stepped back and took a look for a second and do you know something James? I like what I see. James smiled somewhat after coming to the realisation that Michelle maybe had a little thing for him, although that would probably be shattered the second that she found out that dinner was consisting of frozen chips and a yoghurt milk hybrid. Leading the way James led Michelle into the living room of his apartment, the room when dark all but for three lavender scented candles sat on a small oak table, the flame from the candles dancing forming shadows on the painted blue walls a bottle of wine was situated on the table placed delicately between two glasses. Whilst James didn’t have any food he knew how to put on a show. Michelle: This is beautiful James, is that your place or is it just rented? James: It’s mine it’s my one big purchase since I started my new job, I got tired of moving from hotel room to hotel room and I wanted a place to call home, it’s not much but it’s mine you know, I like having a place where I can do what I want when I want. Michelle: You haven’t actually told me what you do yet, Richard said something about you being rodeo rider. James sighed deeply again hanging his head forlornly, since the first time he had bundled Richard over he knew they had set off on the wrong foot, he was unsure whether or not this Rodeo rider was simply just a mistake or a vicious assault on his name. Just as James was going to open his mouth to set Michelle straight over his choice of careers the annoying beeping which was supposed to represent Intergalactic by the Beastie Boys began to feed through his mobile phone. Looking up apologetically James reaches into his trouser pocket and pulls out his mobile phone raising it hastily to his ear, simultaneously pushing the receive call button. James: Err hi look I’m pretty busy right now, is there any chance that you could call back later and you will have my complete undivided attention. Sharon: I know that you are busy sweetie but the question is how do you expect to get busy later if there is absolutely nothing in you fridge to feed your date, I saw the show by the way sweetie great work on getting the girl. James was taken aback not totally sure how Sharon knew that he had nothing left in his pack of cards, he’d played all the jokers and was left without a card. Sharon: Now if I’ve timed this right you should be hearing a knock on the door shortly after this phone call, you will receive a picnic basket filled with food and champagne, I want you to take the little brunette somewhere nice and give her a night to remember understand? A knock arrived at James’ front door with which Sharon hung up the phone leaving James looking extremely perplexed holding the phone his mouth ajar as he turned slowly to look at the door. Michelle: Aren’t you going to answer that? James nodded at Michelle whose smile would have made the devil create snow just so that he could spend a bit more time staring into her eyes, just spending time with her. As he opened the door a small robust man carrying a motorcycle helmet under one arm handed across a large basket, which creaked under the weight of goods inside it. Deliveryman: Have a good night sir The little fat man smiled and winked at James in a way that teenagers did when trying to keep a secret, before placing his helmet back on his head hopping on his moped, which puttered down the road. James turned back and re-entered his humble abode closing the door quietly behind him placing the basket lightly on the green carpet in the hall before entering the living room where Michelle was finishing off her glass of red wine. Michelle: So when is dinner ready I’m getting quite hungry. James: Well there has been a change of plan here you see I was going to cook but then I thought wouldn’t it be so much more romantic to have a nice secluded picnic, some champagne and we can talk and get to know each other a little bit better. The smile which came from Michelle’s face could illuminate the darkest of nights, her pure white teeth glistening as her lips curved upwards, she stood up and made her way over to James standing just in front of him before leaning up and kissing him on the cheek. Michelle: It sounds perfect. Taking his hand in hers Michelle led James out of the living room and leant down to pick up the basket that had been left on the green carpet, before leaving the apartment and into the warm In the living room the candles continued to flicker wildly they were never going to get totally out of control but there was always the risk as with so many things in life, risks had to be taken in order to get what you wanted and occasionally, those risks would pay off.
