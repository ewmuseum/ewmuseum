Whispers from white padded walls: Pt3: Conclusion

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/12/02 Winterbourne Asylum was everything you'd expect a structure that was inhabited by people who were mentally ill and unstable to appear as. Dark, gloomy, haunting, the startling realisation that it actually looked like it did the movies made it all the more frightening. Standing there. facing the outside of the caliginous structure stood The Shade himself, his dark appearance camouflaging him in with the rest of the scene. 'That old crazy bastard better be worth coming here for' * * * * * 'When can I see him? It's already been 2 hours?' shouted Martin Bauerman. His precise blonde come over sliding across his forehead as he banged the table of the reception. 'You will see Mr. Riley when he is good and ready to see you Mr. Bauerman. You seem to have forgotten that....' 'That Riley is just a crazy old bastard? NO ma'am! I have not forgotten! Either way, the man may be holding the one truthful piece of information about this Avatar Of Death as he has been called by his admirers, that could lead to his arrest!' 'Oh, yeah, I've heard about him a little.' 'What have you heard?!' 'I've heard that he's stopping the crime better than you folks are!' With that, the receptionist turned her back on Detective Martin Bauerman and thumbed through a set of files in a filing cabinet. Bauerman sighed, and returned to his set next to his partner Dave Brentford. 'Get the feeling this is going to be a long night Marty?' 'Yes Dave... and don't ever call me Marty again.' 'Hehe, lighten up. The crazy bastard will talk when he's ready.' 'Well this Avatar Of Death has been dropping in and out and become the peoples Paladin for the last few years. He's making a mockery of the department and if I don't get some hard evidence and information on him he'll make a mockery of me! * * * * * * * * Jacob Riley's room, or looney shack as they tended to be called by most locals, was obscured in darkness. The moonlight shining in being the only source of comforting light there. 'Got to speak, got to tell the truth now Jacob' he mumbled to himself as he sat crouched in a corner of the room. He usually did as night fell. He had a deep fear of the night, it reminded him of The Shade. 'Can't be shy, can't cry, tell them the truth and then say bye bye' He spoke the words in a light tone as you would say a nursery rhyme, adding emphasis on the rhyming words. 'Can't be shy, can't cry, tell them truth and then say.....' Suddenly a looming shadow fell across the light the moonlight had cast. 'Bye bye?' Jacob shot his head up before turning a pale shade of white. He clutched his legs closer to his stomach and tried to more further back into the wall. The Shade stood perched outside Jacob's barred window. A slight grin on his face. 'NN-N-N-N-NOOO! Your -y-your a bad man!' 'Is that any way to greet me now Jacob?' Slowly Jacob began sobbing slightly. The harsh realisation that his worst fear was standing right outside his window was too much for the already mentally unstable Jacob to cope with. 'Yourrrr not real' 'Oh but I am very real Jacob.' 'NO! I'm telling the police man on you! Their going to take you away!' Jacob spoke the words as a child would scare an another child by threatening to tell the head teacher, or their father on them. 'Take me away? What, like they took you away? Look where they put you old friend. In here.' 'I'm here because I'm special, they told me' Gideon couldn't resist chuckling. 'That's one way to put it. Either way Jacob, do you really think I wanted to become this? This...this.... this fucking anti-hero who finds solace in the shadows! NO! Just like you didn't want to be put in this asylum. Now I'm not going to watch as you spout your lies and stories about me to some prissy detective. I will not hide from them, they will meet death just like others have. Yet either way I don't want the constant hassle of them onto me. I'd rather die then be looked in a cell, I already live in my own prison everyday! I won't let you be the cause of me being locked in another one!' Jacob crouched up into an even tighter ball, Gideon's harsh words making him grow even more fearful of him. 'You always liked the taste of alcohol Jacob. Remember? It tasted so sweet. You liked the ladies as well, I can tell. After all these years i can still smell the scent of young innocent women on your body who you forced yourself upon. Yet.... wouldn't you like that back? If not that, just the freedom of this place?' Jacob slowly nodded cautiously. * * * * * * * * * 'Jesus, this is just one big fucking joke Dave' 'Relax Marty...' 'I-' 'Martin then, Jeez, just lighten up. Think of it this way, it beats doing paperwork.' 'Yeah.... but it doesn't beat getting home early and sipping on a nice cold beer.' With that Martin Bauerman rose up and started storming around the hallways of Winterbourne asylum. 'Wait, detective, you cant go there!' yelled the receptionist. 'Watch me. Hurry up Dave' * * * * * * The blade shone majestically as Gideon tossed it onto the floor of Jacob's room before it landed under the few remaining specks of moonlight being cast. 'There..... that's your ticket out of here Jacob' Jacob crawled forward slightly, eyes wide at the sight of the razor. 'Take it, it's okay' 'But..... it seems wrong' 'Let me put it this way old friend. You won't see me for a LOOOOONG time if you use this way out.' That comment was enough to cause Jacob to snatch the blade up into his hand. * * * * * * * * 'Room 241, where is it Dave?' 'Read the numbers on the room ya jackass' 'Since when did this become a frickin' hotel. Wait, here, this one just down here.' Dave and Martin jogged briskly down the hall, the receptionist jogging hopelessly behind them. 'Look through the window on the door first, see if he's sleeping.' 'I couldn't give a shit if he's taking a bubble bath Dave, that psycho is going to..... ' Bauerman's voice cut off sharply as he peeped his head against the small oval glass on the door. 'Oh my dear God!' Dave peeped through over Bauerman's shoulder, and placed his hand over his mouth to prevent any of his vomit from hurling out, it was a hopeless attempt, and it splattered over the lime green floor. 'Out of the way!' yelled a nurse as she drew a large set of keys from her pocket and unlocked the door. She simply froe to the ground, before turning eventually and sobbing slightly. Bauerman simply looked on with a mixed expression of disgust and anger. Inside, Jacob Riley's vocal chords lay dangling out of his throat as he lay in a pool of blood which was still gliding across the floor from his sliced throat. 'What kind of human being can make a man do that to himself?!' 'A pretty fucked up one I'd imagine' came a voice from the window. Bauerman turned sharply to greet The Avatar Of Death for the first time. Gideon simply stared at him with his deep blue eyes, hypnotising Bauerman in his gaze, much like he had with Jacob as he told him to cut through his vocal chords. 'Victims, detective..... aren't we all?' With that Gideon jumped from the window, vanishing into the night as Martin Bauerman stood there shocked, confused, and most certainly afraid. Whoever this anti-hero was, this Shade who was avenging many of the crimes his department couldn't clean up, one thing was for sure. It couldn't continue, but Bauerman had no idea whatsoever of how to stop him.
