Reliving a nightmare, part 4: Questioning the suspect.

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 Me and my partner had just come from the suspects home after finding he wasn’t there. Not surprising to us we had figured he wasn’t there in the first place. We did however wait around for awhile hoping that he would show up. But to for us that would have been to easy. So we decided to see if we could go out and find him. We were looking for him. For the simple reason that just a few hours before his ex had found a letter in her mailbox saying that she was being watched. Our man, the one she calls the freak is the only current suspect. Most of the people in the department feel he’s our man. Including myself and my partner. The captain, he isn’t quite as convinced as the rest of us. But the old man appears to me at least to have lost his edge. We pulled up across the street from a diner that we had heard he often frequents. From the outside appearance it looked as though it had been sitting there for years. The pain was peeling the sign was missing an e in the name, “The Nightlif.“ Glancing at my watch it was now 5:00pm. We got out of the unmarked patrol car and walked across the street. Once inside we found that the diner was just about empty. We found a seat near the back facing the door. Once we were seated I noticed that the dinner needed a major remodeling. The wallpaper had to be as least fifty years old. The seats had tears in them and the tables were marked up with graffiti from the past fifty years. A waitress dressed in a black and white uniform walked over and took our orders. I ordered a cup of coffee and the house special roast beef with a side of green beans and mashed potatoes. My partner ordered a tuna sandwich and some salty chips. Which I was going to have to pay for with his breath. As we sat there and eyed every perp that entered the joint we talked about what had happened earlier. We weren’t the ones assigned to his ex. But we are the ones assigned to bring in the suspect for questioning whether he liked it or not. I had a feeling that the guy wasn’t going to like it. That the guy was going to act like every other scumbag we ever had to pull in. But what ever it takes me and my partner always seem to get our questions one way or another. It didn’t really matter to us. In fact at time I rather enjoyed the guys that thought they were tough. We heard that our suspect was a very big guy, he apparently worked out a lot. A guy with as much muscle as he lacked brains. A guy that hated cops and hated detectives even more. We got the call for our rather interesting persuasive ways in how we got questions out of our suspects, at least that’s how it was explained to me once. Our captain said that he wasn’t sure that even we could bring this guy in. After all he made his living as a professional wrestler. Professional wrestler or not me and my partner were going to bring this guy in. Even if we had to bust some heads to get the job done. The captain said to get him in as quietly as possible. This guy has a big match coming up at the March Madness pay-per-view. A four corners match for some title or another. The captain says he put some money on this guy and didn’t want him roughed up before hand. That was unless things got out of hand. Which not many perps did with the two of us around. Finally our orders were brought over and we began to dig in when the bell connected to the diners front door began to ring. I looked up just as a monster of a man walked in. He was probably 6’6” to 6’7” and close to three hundred pounds. I wasn’t no slouch and neither was my partner but this guy out weight each of us by at least sixty pounds. And all of his weight seemed to be pure muscle. At first I began to have second thoughts, for the first time in my entire career on the force. But again he was just another perp with a over size body to match his undersized brain. But I wasn’t about to let this guys size intimidate me. He may use it to push around innocent women and weak men but not two cops. At least not me and my partner. I pushed my plate back and motioned to my partner that our guy just walked in. We both watched him as he sat in a booth in about the middle of the room. I stood first my partner followed close behind. We walked up to his table and looked down at the man as he looked up at us. A look on his face and burnt into his eyes said he didn’t want to be bothered, not now or ever. I didn’t care much if he did or not. Me and my partner were going to bother him if he liked it or not. I would make sure that he didn’t like it. “Mind if we join you?” I said in a tone that meant business. He stared up at us and snarled. “Yeah, I do mind. If you don’t mind. Could you get out of here.” He then went back to his menu. I had a feeling this was going to get rough. “Yeah, we mind also.” That’s when I motioned for my partner to take a seat. Then I slid in next to him and in front of this so called freak. He didn’t bother to look up again. He acted as though we weren’t even there. “I like I said I mind.” He said again as if we hadn’t heard him the first time. I took out my badge and showed it to him. He smirked and looked back at the menu. “Like that’s suppose to change my mind.” I snatched the menu out of his hands. “It better.” This had gotten his attention at least for the moment. “Fine, you got the badge. What do you want? And make it fast.” Now we just might be getting someplace. I flipped open a notebook and took out a pen. I made a few notes before asking my first question. “May I ask where you were today. Say around 11:30am - 12:45pm.” “I was at the gym.” I sighed. “Could you be more specific please?” He grumbled under his breath. “I was at the gym on 122nd and Choctaw.” I made a few marks in my notebook. “Could you please tell me what you were doing at this gym and can anyone verify that you were there?” He looked around the dinner at the waitress at the counter then up at the wall clock above where she was standing. “Is this going to take long? I am sort of hungry.” He acted as though his time was more important than my own. As if my investigation wasn‘t a big deal. Well pal it is a big deal and your our number one suspect. The only suspect in my book. “If you would corporate with my questions then no. If not then yes this may take awhile.” I snapped my fingers as if trying to get his attention. He glared at me in a cold menacing gaze. “I was lifting weights. And I couldn’t tell you if anyone would remember me there or not. I don’t regularly go down to that gym.” He’s trying to find an alibi I know it. He probably wasn‘t even at that gym. If there even was one at that address. Which I doubted very seriously. “And if you don’t regularly go down to this gym. Then can you please tell me why you were there today?” “I wanted to get a good workout in before this Sunday.” His pay-per-view match coming up. It could be likely he was there but also very unlikely. “Can somebody verify you were there today?” He rolled his eyes. “I already told you I don’t know.” I stared at him trying to read his expressions. Trying to see if he was lying. Trying to see if he was making up his story as he went along. Trying to see if this perp was playing me for a fool. “Is that because you weren’t there?” Let’s see if we can catch him in a lie. Most of these perps tell so many they can’t keep up with all of them. He then snapped back at me. “Listen fool, if your going to charge me with something then read me my rights. Otherwise get the f(censored)k out of my face. You get that a##hole?” I jumped out from behind the booth as if ready to stop a strike. “If you don’t like these questions or can’t answer them then perhaps you should get yourself a lawyer.” He stood up as my partner made his way out from behind the booth. “Maybe I will when I sue you’re a## for harassment.” I grinned as I grabbed his hand. “You do that pal. But right now your coming downtown with me.” Just as I finished this statement he pulled his hand away. “I’m not going anywhere except home. And that’s not until I had my meal.” “You can either come nicely or we can get rough. It’s up to you.” “Rough, just you try. I’ll rip your head off your shoulders and sh!t down ya throat.” At that instant I went to grab his arm again. He pulled it away and slammed both of his hands into my chest pushing me away. My partner went at him but was met with a fist to the face. I heard him screaming something about his nose being broke. I caught myself on a far table and came back at him. He met me half way and caught me with a punch to the ribs then a head butt to my head. I heard the explosion in my head as my own nose was shattered. The rest of the incident was a blur. I think the waitress called 911 as some from the back came out and tended to me and my partner. The freak stood over us and yelled obscenities as we both laid on the diners floor bleeding and in pain. He then made his exit before backup could arrive. ***Flash Forward*** Both me and my partner stood inside our captains office after lengthy visits to the hospital emergency room. He looked at both of us and laughed. He probably thought us getting our asses whipped by a professional wrestler was funny. It was as if he had expected such a thing from us and that monster of a man. He told us just as much. We told him our story and then what little details that the suspect had given us. I also told him I suspected the man to be lying about everything. He told me he didn’t care if the guy was lying. He only cared if we could prove it. He sent another squad car over to the gym to check his story. We then asked if he was going to send someone out to find him and bring him in. I was rather pissed when the captain said not unless his story didn’t stand. I asked about his putting hands on two of his departments best police officers. Then again he began to laugh. He said apparently the staff at the diner rather thought highly of the suspect. Said that he never caused any sort of problems before. Always left a rather good tip. That was until the two cops came in, that would be me and my partner. They had blamed the whole thing on us and said that the suspect was just defending himself. What a bunch of lowlife citizens this city is full of. The captain said that we should just drop it unless we wanted assault charges filed against us. So what more could me and my partner do. Other then sit and wait to see if the suspects story held or not…
