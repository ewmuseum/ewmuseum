Date: Biggest pain in the ass that I&#039;ve ever had to write. “…Goat? Brick? Sandwich? Tree? …Hello?” The large, colorfully tacky, African American woman holds the phone to her ear, a slightly confused look on her face. Her backdrop scenery: mystical candles, an ancient rug, and a gold throne, all a cheap ploy to impress the public eye with her “Superior” knowledge. What can she say? She’s addicted to souvenir shops. Large, colorfully dressed Black Woman: “It was goat, right? …Right?” She listens attentively to the dial tone for a little longer, before the scene takes to a new shot. “Call Miss Cleo, Now!” flashes repeatedly at the bottom of the screen, right below the big gal herself. Cleo: “Call me now, Child! Cleo is waiting! Cleo is watching you closely! Cleo…” “1-800-445-…” The numbers visibility is suddenly scattered into an unreadable blur, and Cleo is caught in between two men behind badges; one to her left, the other on her right. The next thing you know, she is in cuffs, struggling to get some angry last words through the lens of her cheap Sony home video camera. Cleo: “(Bellowing through clenched teeth.) Fo’! Cleo ain’t done, yet! Cleo da man, yo! Cleo gunna getchu! (Spits into the camera, disgusted.) Cleo knew dis was gunna happen! Cleo’s a psychic, Bi-atch! Cleo knew! Cleo knew! CLEO KNEW!” The shot dissolves to static. Moments later, a new TV ad begins, a black screen at first. “Do you have bad credit?” “Bankrupt?” “No education what so ever?” “Constantly running in with the police?” “Served long term prison time?” Fading in, we are greeted warmly by the maniacal, pint-sized Goblin, Syko. A cheap suit, and a cheap smile, with some slicked back hair thrown into the picture; Syko looks ready to compete with the “Majors.” Like Cleo, for example. Syko the Goblin: “If you have one of the problems just listed, then you’re probably broke, and waiting for a job. And folks, let’s face the facts… You want to work for cold hard cash on the spot. You know who you are. (Winks.)” Cue the sappy, solo piano riff, as the shot takes to a grassy field. A cuddly, soft, Golden Retriever pup frolics aimless through a pile of loose U.S. bills. And cutting through the crystal clear blue sky, an exuberant display of color, a rainbow. “If you have bad credit, we love you!” pans across the screen… “Well, now there’s hope. Hope for you… Goblin HQ! You see, nobody wants you, and you deserve to die, but Goblin HQ needs you, and you still deserve to die. But, somebody needs you, and that’s important!” …And then the shot returns to Syko, who still grins from ear to ear. Syko the Goblin: “What is Goblin HQ, and what do we do here? Well, that’s for the authorities to find out, (Chuckle.) but the fact is we need useless hooligans to do our dirty work. To throw yourself blindly into something you can’t escape, call…” There is some murmuring off screen, and Syko slants his view curiously away from the viewer to that direction. Syko the Goblin: “Huh… oh…” Nodding agreeably, Syko returns his smile to the viewer and wipes his brow gesturing a tone of relief. Syko the Goblin: “Phew! That was close… Well, don’t worry about looking for us, (A dark tone and overcast shadow the Goblin’s gleeful mood, suddenly, and he returns to a more fitting maniacal glare.) because we’re going to find you, anyway! MUWAHAHAHAH!” Once again, sudden static. We find our evil, scheming little fiend, Syko the Goblin, as he opens the door to his office, allowing entry to a large blue… Wait a minute, since when did Syko have an office? Well, that’s of no concern now, let’s just see how this plays out for us. Syko, clad in a suit of no value as seen in the commercial, leads a large hulking mass of blue muscle into his office, gesturing towards a seat for him to sit. Easily 5 times the weight and bulk of Syko, the blue fleshed man merely grunts, and plops down upon the tin folding chair, crushing it beneath his build. Across from the monster, Syko heaves heavily before falling into his own cushy throne, a glare from the corner of his eye falling upon the blue man. Shuffling through scattered papers across his desk, most of which just coloring book pages torn from their home, and polluted with a horrible taste for art; Syko acts as if he has something better to do. Syko the Goblin: “Well, Mr. … What was it?” Big, cut, blue man: “(SNARLS!)” Syko the Goblin: “Basher? (A perked eyebrow.)” Scooting around on his rump, slightly discomforted by the chair remains beneath it, Mr. Basher merely nods. Syko the Goblin: “Credentials?” Mr. Basher: “(SNAAAAAAARL!)” Mr. Basher, veins bulging from beneath his blue exterior, ferociously rips off his ragged shirt, revealing a blue, hairy, and bleeding barrel chest. Carved into his hide, along with several numbers, the words “BASHIR LYK BASH!” drip with blood, intriguing the Goblin. Syko the Goblin: “(Rubbing chin.) I like what I’m seeing…” Mr. Basher: “(Snarls, pounding chest.) …” The ground trembling beneath his wrath, Basher rises to hopping mad feet, and drives heavenward with a powerful jump… Moment’s later Syko finds himself trying to breathe, yet unsuccessfully as he is being suffocated by floating dust, plaster, and other debris. In between coughs, Syko tries to address the smoking crater that once was his desk. Syko the Goblin: “(*Caff!*) Congratulations… Mr… Basher… (*Caff!*) you’re hired! (More coughing.) What position were you applying for again?” Opening the door of his office, Syko stumbles out into the main vestibule of his facility, and cloud of pulverized foundation billowing behind him. A light, snowy coating of the afloat debris has showed him, leaving him with a pale and filthy appearance (More so than usual, that is.). Following him, bending at the knees and pulling his shoulders together just to fit through the door, Basher emerges as well, slamming the door shut with much force. A small hallway, 3 doors on the right, and 3 doors on the left… Syko the Goblin: “Okay… You’re officially the bodyguard for Goblin HQ, congratulations. Now, what I need you to do when we are here at the base is guard the front gate. And you mustn’t let anyone in who isn’t on ‘my side,’ however you must remember who our friends are, allowing them…” Wiping the tears from his eyes, while the fine powder settles at his feet, Syko finally can see Bashers blank, totally lost face. Syko begins to pace forward, extending the “walk and talk” gesture to Basher. Syko the Goblin: “Can you say, ‘Friend or Foe?’” Not really paying any mind to the Goblin, Basher snorts some snot back up his flaring nostrils, and scratches his rug haired head. Syko gives him a few seconds, before concluding he has no idea what the blazes is going on. Syko the Goblin: “Agreed, it’s too risky… How about you just beat everyone up, and that way if they aren’t against me, they’ll be weakened… But, make sure you don’t kill them! Just beat them up, so I can make sure myself that they aren’t…” Syko stops himself, stepping right in front of Basher to get his attention. Waving his hand back and forth in front of his face, snapping, and even flicking his nose, Syko becomes quite frustrated with Basher’s intelligence that is even out-shined by the his own. But, he gets nothing. Still, just a blank stare… Syko the Goblin: “(Sigh.) Just ventilate anyone that comes near…” Basher: “(SNAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARL!)” Tremors pulsate through the floor as the ripped specimen lumbers excitedly through the doorway at the other end of the hall, trampling over his newfound employer to get there of course. Forcing himself off the ground, groaning all the way to weak feet, Syko looks at the gapping hole in the wall/doorway that used to lead into the front lobby/waiting room. “I need you to sign my divorce papers, b*tch! Set me free!” Syko is attracted by the clatter of voices coming from his Secretary’s domain. A hop in his step, he trots to wide-open room, meanwhile brushing debris from his raunchy suit. “You dirty bumz! Zis iz no place uv your concern!” “If I wear bags on my head, people love me more!” “Vat daz zat have to do with anything?” Popping in, Syko scans the room undaunted with the chaos within it. Syko the Goblin: “Knock, knock? (Chuckles.) How can I enslav… help you?” Three ragged, hostile, dirty bums huddle tightly in the corner of the room, their long beards flared defensively. Filthy from head to toe, each emits a stench of a most foul strain forcing Syko to fan his face, and wipe away the salty residue welling up in his eyes. Broom in hands, a stout, bulky woman, easily twice Syko’s age a weight, keeps the bums stationary to the corner with missing swings. Beside her a frizzy haired, lab coat wearing, old coot frantically panics, pulling out full wads of gray hair by the fist. Syko the Goblin: “(Holding nose, thus voicing in a high-pitched tone.) L-A-B! Esther! What is the meaning of this?!” The old man turns to Syko, his eyes looking comically large behind his thick lenses that sit crooked upon the crest of his large nose. L-A-B: “Sir, zis filthy trash has been a nuisance all morning! Ze secretary and I have…” Syko the Goblin: “Slanderous swill! Incompetent fools! Can you not see these men are hungry for work?” Bum 1: “I’m Hungry! … FOR COMMUNISM!” Esther, as she is addressed, turns on her heel to face Syko. She has the exterior appearance of an old hag lunch lady, but her eyes speak of axe slaughtering. Her brow rises in a questionable manner, and her top lip scrunches in a snarl, revealing a small bit of her yellow teeth. Esther: “(Mumbles…)” Syko the Goblin: “Oh, come on, you can’t justify scaring away able bodied applicants!” L-A-B: “But… but… ze…” L-A-B stares in awe as Syko gestures for him to cease before approaching the bums with an extended hand. Brushing past the old scientist, Syko flares the fire in his eyes at L-A-B for a brief moment, maintaining a smile for the frightened dirt bags. Syko the Goblin: “How would you boys like a job? I have the perfect position for you…” Esther scoffs, tossing the broom defiantly over her shoulder… Esther: “(Mumbling.) …yeah, you have a good position for everyone… stupid… I have job… loser…”…And returns to her desk. Meanwhile the bums eye the Goblin’s generous hand, the bloodshot eyes bulging. Bum 1: “I… I WANT MY MONEY BACK!” Syko the Goblin: “Hah! Don’t we all!” The old Dr. Lucifer Abromowitz Borodinsky grabs the boss by the shoulder, protest still his main objective. L-A-B: “Lord Syko, please listen to reason! Zey all wandered in to get out uv ze cold!” Syko shakes his head in disgust, making the “Tut-tut” sound. Bum 2: “(Looking around suddenly, confused.) Didn’t I tell you hoodlums to get out of my house?” Syko the Goblin: “Yes, I think he read my thoughts exactly, L-A-B! Get back to your rat mazes, or have fun back in prison, serving your life sentence!” L-A-B: “I have a choice?” Syko turns with an indignant index finger cocked. Syko the Goblin: “… GET BACK TO WORK!” Bum 3: “NOW!” Bum 2: “PILLAGE THEIR VALUABLES!” Suddenly the hostile bums shove past Syko and L-A-B, laughing with an evil tone comparable to Syko’s most wicked forms of cackling. As they dash into the main lobby through the gapping whole Basher made, L-A-B tries to give chase, but Syko stops him with a clamped hand on the shoulder. Syko the Goblin: “WHAT INITIATIVE! They’re hired! Subordinates, meet my new henchmen, and the dirty workers of Goblin HQ! Esther, you’re in charge of bathing, feeding, and clothing them!!” Esther: “(Mumbling.) …feed them your… dumbass…”
