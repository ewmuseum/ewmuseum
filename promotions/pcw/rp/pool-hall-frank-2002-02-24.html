Pool Hall, FRANK~!!!

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 ::Segment Two:: --- Ganker and Hank arrive at the Pool Hall about fifteen minutes away from the arena, Janelle sits on a stool as Ganker prepares the table and Hank gets the Sobe beverages, Lightning, Energy, Orange Cream, and whatever the f**k else he feels like getting as long as it ain't the diet Orange Carrot or that Green Tea garbage. Ganker: So Janelle, yo sure must be special to bring out this aggression in Hank, he's not been this efficient in his matches before yo came into his life. Janelle: I've always been in his life according to him. He says he couldn't get me out of his mind for the longest time and everytime he tried to get me out, there would be something that would put me back in his head. Ganker: Yo two met one day when he popped out his old roller skates and went skating back in Plymouth right? (Janelle nods) Yeah, he was visitin' me, freak my parents out. --- Ganker and Janelle laugh as Hank returns with Sobe beverages and a basket of fries. Hank: (sips his Lightning) Damn this sh*t rules~! Ganker: (sips his Energy) Yeah, Sobe Lightning is much better than Sobe Energy, but I personally love Orange Cream~!!! That pink crap is good too I guess. Hank: I don't know all the flavors, but I definately stay away from the white kind. I don't know what it is, but it tastes funky. Ganker: I see dead people. (quivers) --- Hank laughs as he walks over and pats Ganker on the shoulder and grabs a pool stick. Hank looks to get to break, but turns around and repeatedly beats Ganker with the pool stick. Hank: NEVER SAY THAT AGAIN~! (angered) Ganker: AGHA~! THE PAIN~! THE AGONY~! MAKE IT STOP~! MAKE IT GO AWAY MOMMY~! --- Janelle and Hank laugh. Ganker: Yo both a couple of psychos and I hope yo both rot in hell. Hank: I won't rot, I'll rule over hell. Ganker: Not if I bet yo to it. Hank: Oi, I'd kick yo a** so damn fast it wouldn't even be funny. Ganker: Jus' break a**. --- Hank breaks and knocks 2 colored balls into the left side pocket. (no clue what the pool terms all) Ganker grabs another pool stick and aims the white ball right at this big bald guy. He beams him right in between the eyes and ducks under the table. He walks over and drops the ball on the table. Man: This your ball man? Hank: Erm, yeah. Man: Tell yo cowardice friend it's coo, jus' watch it next time and try to keep the ball on the board. Hank: It's either that or he scratches the damn thing. Man: (laughs) You're funny. (rubs gottee) You look familiar, where have I seen you before? (looks over at Extreme Title in Janelle's lap) That's right, you're Hank~! of PCW, right? Hank: That'd be me~! Man: You're famous here in Milwaukee, we love to watch your matches, but more importantly we think you have the best taste in women. Hank: Thanks, (smiles) I know. Man: Don't you have a match tonight? Hank: Yeah, triple threat, Karl LeBlanc and Jason Core, defending that there Extreme title my woman is currently holdin'. Man: So the prison raped angry black man vs the new jobber? Hank: Yo know yo wrestlin' terms and jail terms well I take it? Man: Wrestling comes second natures to me and jail, I've known friends who've been in and know the lingo. Hank: Cool shwingers~! Man: (confused) Huh? Hank: Word I made up. Man: Oh... Hank: What's yo name, didn't catch it? Man: Frank O' Shrine. --- Hank and Frank shake hands. Hank: Yo goin' to watch the match on that rinky dink television over there? Frank: Yup, drink some beers down and watch you kick a**, like usual. Hank: Damn straight~! Frank: What was up with your early PCW years? You jobbed to like everyone? Hank: Didn't care, I lost my heart, but then I returned to my roots the UWF and retrieved what heart I had lost. Frank: Coo. --- Ganker climbs out from under the pool table. Frank: (staring at Ganker) Boo~! --- Ganker jumps and slides under the pool table as Hank, Frank, and Janelle all laugh. Frank: You're friend's funny, I like him. Hank: He's annoyin' most of the time. Frank: Probably is, but good-luck Sabotage and the rest of the week you're booked. Hank: Thanks man, good to meet new people. Frank: It's good to meet a man who still lives on the streets he grew up on, no matter how much money he makes. Hank: Never lose track of yo roots, never forget the past or let it go, it's how I reunited wit' Janelle over there. I never forgot her and went back for her, because I knew deep down in my heart we were right for one another. Frank: Dude, you have to get me your number so we can keep in touch, and so I can torture that dude over the phone, (points to pool table) Hank: Sure, after the show. Frank: Where you want me to meet you? Hank: Backstage in my locker room. Frank: I need a pass to go get in there though. Hank: Or jus' stick beside me. Frank: Doesn't your match start soon? Hank: (looks up at clock then down at watch) About one hour from now. Frank: Let's head out. ---Frank, Hank, Janelle, and Ganker all head out of the bar, but Frank comes back and gets his wife and then leaves. Segment Two Ends
