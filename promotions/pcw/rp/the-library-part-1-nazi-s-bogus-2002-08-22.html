The LIbrary - Part 1 - Nazi's = Bogus

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 22 [Pink. Pop stars. Nazi's and Black People. What is the bogusness of PCW coming to?! Whatever happened to all the totally normal people with bland tights and mullets? It's all over. The glory, radical days of wrestling have been thrown out of the window to be forgotten. Now the Connection must do most hectic battle with a crew of oddballs. Pink. The Tubular One and the Heavy Metal One have torn shreds on those totally bogus dudes so many times that they lost count. Pop Singers. Once again these compadres have felt the Bodacious Bombs on many occasions. N'Sync can keep shredding their totally heinous tears. Then there is new gnarly turf. The most unusual pairing of two heinous dudes. Mark Makonie and Duzza. Makonie a Nazi dude who is hellbent on seeing black people suffer. Makonie's most heated rivalry with Jordan X Shabazz is something most will not forget. The Connection certainly doesn't forget and how easy of a Pay Per View match this is going to be. You might as well begin opening the wine, because the Cowabunga Connection is bringing home the tag team titles!] [The local Los Angeles library is a most heinous myth between many. It was once said that a total heinous librarian went insane from keeping track of that Index Catalogue and then she slaughtered two pigs. Then she commenced to eat them raw and then when they found her in the morning she kept saying that she was hungry. The only thing left of those totally gnarly pigs were the bones. Many of the locals have had their opinions about the place. That the ladies souls still lingers around looking to eat her next prey. Others say that they sometimes hear the oinking of pigs in certain places of the library. Either way the creepy atmosphere of the large library doesn't help much with this totally bogus myth] [The attractive brown haired librarian sits behind her tiny oak desk looking through the log of returned books. Her tiny spectacles hang down on her slender nose, looking as if they are about to slip off. Her hair is wrapped up in a tight bun, just as she was taught to do by her mother. She wears a white button down blouse with a navy blue dress that extends all the way to her ankles. She wears a tiny light blue sweater over her blouse. It may be a blistering heatwave outside but with the air conditioning going haywire it's like a freezer in the room. Sometimes it's hard to write because it gets so cold. She wishes that the maintenance man would get his act together and just fix the darn thing] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: No way, man!!! [The deep, scratchy voice of Harry almost frightens the poor librarian from her chair. She looks up at the two very different men. The man who frightened her looks so scrungy. He wears a black Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. His brown hair hangs down in his face and his eyes are so bloodshot. He looks like he had just rolled out of bed. Does her even know any kind of grooming techniques?! Now the man that accompanies him is so opposite. He looks like a Greek God in his tight PCW T-shirt. His bulging, cut tan muscles just stretch the fabric of the T-shirt, as if it's ready to burst off at any second. His khaki cargo shorts blend in so well with his smooth tan legs. His bright blonde hair is parted down the middle, bring out his blue eyes. He looks so dreamy with that million dollar smile. The librarian begins adjusting her glasses and making sure her sweater is on straight. She suddenly feels so nervous] Librarian: Is there anything that I can help you with, gentlemen? [Tommy smiles brightly to the oddly sexy librarian babe] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: We have come to this totally gnarly facility to do some research on Nazi Germany! [The librarian smiles and giggles like some sort of little school girl] Librarian: Well, we have a lot of information on the Nazi's. You know...Hitler really thought that what he was doing was a good thing for the human race. He thought that wiping out the Jews would make our society a much better place. It goes to show just how one man's opinion can change the face of history. [Tommy nods with a reassuring smile, sort of glad to hear that gnarly information. Harry can only stare off with a confused look] Harry: I don't get it, man. What's a Nazi? Who are the Jews? I need my blanket.... [The librarian looks at Harry with a bit of distain, not really sure how to handle him. She looks to Tommy] Librarian: Is he going to be all right? TTT: He's just totally afraid of that myth about the library. He thinks that some crazy babe is going to eat him. [The librarian now wears a warm, welcoming smile and flashes to Harry] Librarian: You don't have to worry about that now, sir. She's long gone and off to a better place. You can browse this library ghost free, and get your neat information about Nazi's. [Tommy nods, looking to Harry. The Heavy Metal One can just shrug his shoulders with a sense of being reassured] Harry: Ok, man. [Tommy then makes his way through the rows of books. His mission is to find the Non-Fiction section and to get to History. That's where all the totally bogus information about Nazi's and Germany is. Mark Makonie is a totally heinous Nazi dude who needs to be researched so that the Connection can fully understand what his motives are. You understand Mark Makonie and his totally bogus ways, you understand how to defeat the dude and take the Tag Titles] TTT: There certainly are a lot of books here, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy spies many books about Germany, Nazi's, and Hitler. He grabs as many that he can get his hands on, piling the large books into the arms of Harry. You never really notice how heavy a book is until you've held about twenty of them at once, straining to hold them up so that they don't fall onto the floor. Because they have to remain totally silent or else they could get kicked out and not get the totally gnarly info on Makonie's origins] Harry: There are a lot of dead trees in this place, man. [Tommy nods as the two make their way into a large area full of oak tables. There are dozens of tiny lamps on the tables to add a little light to the subject. In case some go into the night with their studies or their favorite book. Tommy begins browsing through the first book entitled 'Nazi's: Alive and Well' by Heath Higgins. Many of the words like 'slaughter', 'gas chamber', and 'Jews' tear at his totally confused soul. How could any bogus dudes ever do something like this to other compadres? It doesn't make any sense!] TTT: I don't get these totally bogus Nazi's, dude. [Harry has a folded up edition of Playboy in his hand as the centerfold is out. His bloodshot eyes are wide like two half dollars] Harry: I don't get how these chick's breasts got so big, man. They are all natrual too! [Tommy smacks the magazine from his hand] TTT: DUDE!! We're here to do some totally gnarly research. You'll have time for that later. Harry: Ok, man. [All through the books Tommy can only get one thing from these horrible Nazi's: HATE. It's totally bogus how narrow minded some of these dudes can be. How one event spawned a most heinous revolution that still plagues our society today. The bogusness must stop, it must come to a most abrupt halt] [The afternoon sun turned into the brightly lit moon as the library is totally empty except for three souls. Tommy, Harry, and the oddly cute librarian. Harry is passed out on a pile of books, dreaming about his big date with porn star Devon. Tommy just stares at one of the book covers with the Nazi symbol on it, feeling like something is missing from the world. The librarian takes a seat next to Tommy] Librarian: It's a horrible thing that happened all those years ago. It's ashame that so many people hate and dislike each other. [Tommy still stares at the cover, hearing the words, but not really having a response] Librarian: It may seem like there is a lot of anger and hate in the world, but it's nothing compared to what it once was in the past. The only reason you hear about it so much is because of the expanding media and the expanding population. [The librarian takes her glasses off and lets her brown hair from the bun. Her hair flows down past her shoulders, bringing out her beautiful eyes. Tommy looks up to her and sees a totally new person. Before he just saw a cute woman who read a lot of books. Now he sees a totally bodacious babe, with a totally bodacious mind to go along with it. Tommy smiles to her as she smiles back] TTT: I have to wrestle a Nazi this coming Sunday on Pay Per View. I'm feeling quite bogus about the whole thing. I know it seems like a longshot for a Nazi and a black dude to win the titles, but they really don't have much to lose. Neither do Harry and I, but I just suddenly feel bogus about the whole thing. We've had plenty of shots at these tag titles and yet each and everytime we come up short of that totally rad goal. [The librarian keeps the warm smile on her face, nodding. Tommy, for the first time in a long time, feels quite nervous in front of this babe. Someone like this it takes an intelligent mind to really bring out her bodacious beauty. Tommy knows he doesn't really have that, but he can try] TTT: So much is happening so fast. Harry and I learn that we are brothers and my entire world is turned upside down. What I believed in the past was a total mirage, and now everything has done a most weird 180. [The librarian places her hand into Tommy's, still with the warm smile upon her face] Librarian: I know who you are, and what you've accomplished. I never really thought of you as a deep person. Just another beautiful face among them all around here. Who would have thought that a surfer turned professional wrestler would have a beautiful mind. [Tommy can only smile, feeling most flattered] TTT: What's your name? Librarian: Valerie... [The two go closer and closer and their lips meet in a kiss. Soft, smooth...erotic. The feeling jolts through their bodies like electric current through a wire. Every part of their body going numb with pleasure. The kiss taking them out of the library and into their own little world] [Harry opens his eyes and stretches. He sees his half brother necking with the far out librarian chick. Good for them he thinks to himself with a smile. He notices that it's pretty dark in the library, only a few lamps on. It's sort of creepy...] Harry: Scary, man. [He looks toward the librarian's desk and sees a woman's figure standing there. His eyes pop out of his head when he sees her with a butchers knife, sharpening it on a block of metal. Her eyes become blood red, staring at Harry. He begins breathing heavily, letting his fear be known to the world] Scary librarian: I'm going to eat you, PIG!! Harry: NOT FAR OUT, MAN!!!! [The scene fades to black]
