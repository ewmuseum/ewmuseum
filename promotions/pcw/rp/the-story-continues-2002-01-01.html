Date: 1-1-02 RP 1 Fusion 1st Turmoil 5th Last we met, Teresa dreamed she was a Lady kidnapped by the evil Lord Timothy and rescued by the brave Sir Patrick. Tonight, the dream continues. Teresa wakens to yet another bed. This bed made of feathers and wrapped with white silken sheets. A light pink canopy hangs above and around the bed. She feels as though she knows the bed, as if she’s slept here many nights before, if it was her own. The sun sneaks over the mountain horizon just outside the window of Teresa’s room. The aroma of fresh baked biscuits and sausage fills the air. The last she remembers, she had fell asleep leaning on Sir Patrick’s back. Perhaps he had brought her to her so-called castle or whatever and now someone is making her breakfast. With a smile only brought by curiosity, Teresa crawls out of her bed. A violet colored silk robe is folded neatly at the foot of her bed. The room is just as she would imagine a bedroom in a castle would be, very elegant and antique-like. The floors are hardwood as is most everything else. The walls are decorated with candles and various portraits of people she hasn’t seen before. A fireplace with a warm fire makes the finishing touch to the décor. Before she can take the first step, her door opens and an older woman dressed in plainer clothes of a white and brown dress. She carries a tray of food. Good morning Lady Teresa. Your uncle, the King requested that your morning meal to be brought to you. He also says Sir Patrick will arrive shortly. The woman sets the tray on a dresser near her bed and exits the room. A cool breeze rushes through the bedroom window and sweep across Teresa’s shoulders. She stands and walks to the window. She sees nothing but a flower garden several feet below. The sun has risen a little further into the morning sky. Teresa, may your favorite uncle come in? King Roland stands at the bedroom door watching his sister’s only child gaze out the balcony window. Though now she is grown into a fair young woman, he can’t help but remember her sitting on the same bench gazing outside the same window. If only her mother could see her now. Teresa doesn’t move as she responds for her mind is still in a state of confusion. Uncle, why am I here? A deep sigh exits the old man’s chest. Teresa soon hears his footsteps approach and then feels his cold hands upon her shoulders as an attempt to comfort. You live here my dear. Please listen. I have something important to speak with you. Teresa looks up to the old man’s wrinkled face. She turns so that she faces him and he continues. I know right now, you don’t remember most of this, so I will tell you. Since you were a small child my maidservants and I have taken care of you as if you were my own. I have no heirs to my thrown since the last Great War took my only two sons. The queen, your aunt, took ill last winter and is no longer with us. My days are now numbered and I pray your memory returns before I depart because you are the one that must take your aunt’s place on the throne. Teresa’s baby blue eyes widen in disbelief. She finds herself short of words. Her uncle smiles and places a reassuring hand over her hand and gives it a gentle squeeze. I know this may be too much for you right now. The sooner you know the better. Now before I pass on and you take your rightful position on the throne, you must wed. The future King and your husband has been chosen and you will be wed within a month’s time to your rescuer and childhood friend, Sir Patrick. Sir Patrick? Marriage? Queen? Me? Teresa faints and her body tumbles to the floor.
