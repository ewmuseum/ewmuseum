The Wishmast --- The Last Hurrah, Part 4

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/28/02 {From the hands of Sandy Wong} Before I begin may I remind anyone and everyone who wishes to come to show up for The End's retirement party. I shall be the Mistress of Ceremonies... nah, don't like that name. I'll just call myself Hostess. And NOT like those little snack cakes. If anyone offers me their 'Twinkie' I'll have the Suburban Ninja Clan beat you up. The party... A lot of people have asked 'The End' why he's retiring. The more heartless of them ask, 'Why retire now? Why not a year ago? Why start wrestling?' And then there's the more polite... (okay, to be honest, only RAH, Pharaoh, and Tempest cared to put the question in these terms) 'Why are you retiring? You're doing a good job. You could be PCW Champion in a matter of months!' For that, I have come to provide you with a better explanation of why he has decided to stop. Most of this comes from his own words, my own questions, discussing his decision with him. So this is as good as it gets. It's been a long time, a year in PCW, a few months in EW and elsewhere. But the vast majority of his career was spent in PCW. The good times, the bad times, allies and enemies. From curtain jerking to managing a main event on more than one occasion. It's been quite a year, quite a rise for The End, a PWA student with wrestling talent who became a success story and an inspiration for aspiring wrestlers everywhere, that he could, though without sight, continue to wrestle, continue to work in the one industry where his heart truly lay. It was nearly a year ago that The End lost his vision to the business, to the shambles of a fed known as Eternal Wrestling. It was a career choice. Most of what he did was a career choice. The mask? His idea, The End merely wanted to be able to walk around in public without people going nuts. Sure he was tall, but nowadays who wasn't? The decision to go to Eternal Wrestling instead of staying in PWA for another week or so, thereby bypassing the option of surely being recruited to Blain Bishop's NTICW, regarded as the best version of the many-times defunct federation. That was another career decision. You see, there was talent by the boatload in that NTICW. Xavier Kannon. Hessian. The Pharaoh. Corg. Um.... Possibly Rush Mason and Anton McGonnel. ........................ The Josh. Zurick. Ken Kaze. D-Kay Calyx. Okay, maybe they didn't have that much talent, but at the time it seemed like it. And because of the aforementioned six wrestlers, they tended to bring the best out of their opponents. Before killing them. So going to EW was a career decision. It was small at its pathetic peak under Jax. No more than 15 people, no more than 5 of whom were actually NOT half-assing it at any given time. The workers, save for Young Tiger and Six-Finger Cisco, were lazy and cared only about themselves, not about the business to which they considered themselves God's personal gift. And there was Shane Wethers, who was too busy throwing coffee on people and clearing up work VISA issues to care one way or another. Yes, it was in the Cesspool of Suck known as Eternal Wrestling that wrestlers such as The End could go, prospective talents looking even better in their terrible environment. Those who may have just been another wrestler elsewhere would look like gods in Eternal Wrestling. That is why Six-Finger Cisco came to the federation. That is why Young Tiger would choose such a fed over another, more talent-filled one. That is why The End would opt out of PWA after 10 days in the academy. Smart business tactics. And what a year it has been. Winning the Cruiserweight Title on two occasions, becoming just the sixth person in PCW history to hold it more than once. Winning the Extreme Title, and thus helping to prop it up after Bernard Colt and Semore Smoke were its most recent champions. Placing well with commanding performances in Alliances 2K2 and Mall Brawl 3, thus proving himself to be a major threat to the top wrestlers in the federation. Being a quarter-finalist in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament didn't hurt either. PLC matches, Last Man Standing matches, the No Ladder Match... he's been in his own gimmick matches, and no matter what the outcome, win or lose he always managed to perform better than expectations. He always managed to elevate himself. But alas, it has come to an End now. The End is retiring. 18 months in the industry, 12 months in PCW. He had the epiphany: he's peaked. The End can do no better, The End can do no more. Everything he's given, that's as much as he can give. The rest would be a downward spiral, burning out to fading out. The End doesn't want that. He's taken notice of some people who don't relinquish the spotlight when their time in the business is waning. Experienced firsthand the annoyance of someone who's maintaining a high-card spot from past achievements, all the while more talented workers and are pounding on the shatterproof glass ceiling from below. He doesn't want that. Workers like El Romantico, Josh Starwind, Mani Shigeru, Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor. Even Jackson Rust, though he can be a bit of a twat sometimes. They are PCW's future. The Two Marks -- Amulet and Michigan if they continue on their current path. Bob Vurdo given a better workrate. Jamie Celt if he realizes that he works for PCW. Teresa Tomas, if she gets more overall confidence in herself she could be the most prestigious female wrestler PCW has ever seen. Duzza and Ali Khadaffi.... scratch that. Amulet and Michigan, Bob Vurdo, Tomas, and Celt if lightning strikes a long-lasting fire under his ass... they are PCW's future. And The End does not want to get in their way of achieving their ambitions. It's not his place to. The present is filled with talented wrestlers. Snow, Dante Jackson, The Pharaoh, Rush Mason, Rent-A-Hero, Totally Tubular Tommy, Chet King. El Diablo, Clyde, Darkstar, Duke Williams. They aren't PCW's future, they are PCW's current measuring stick for the top talent. They may not always be good people outside of the ring, but their talent in the ring can't be discounted. They are truly the best of the best. The End has wrestled with them, traveled with them, sometimes worked alternate projects with them; eaten with them, feuded with them, allied with them... Sometimes even being slight friends with them. And he's come up with them from the bottom to the top of PCW. But alas, it has come to an End. No more will The End wrestle in a PCW ring. The Carnival of Carnage, The End's heart was not in it. And truthfully he didn't want the Tag Team Titles, they would hold him in the federation longer than he would've liked. And he didn't feel right to vacate a title. Sunday Adrenaline would be his last match. His last hurrah. And he would go out in style, he would go out in a red-hot blaze of glory. He would go out on top, with a final 'W' in his records before hanging up his boots and saying goodbye to the federation that took him from prospective talent to household name. Saying goodbye to the people who have helped (willfully or not) to make his career. Saying goodbye to the industry that had been his best friend and worst enemy at the same time. The #1 Contendership to the Continental Title is what he would win given a victory. And The End would perform one last charitable act by choosing his successor, someone to replace him in the Continental Title picture. He could choose someone who was already ranked at that division. He could choose someone lower. The End could choose a wrestler from PCW or RUSH, it was his choice. The End could choose a hot dog vendor. The point being that he would do exactly what Alexander Isawa Miyagawa did for him after winning the PCW Cruiserweight Title a fourth time at Wrestle Vessel. He would step aside, give someone else a chance to shine brighter than anyone expected. It's as much as he could do. It's the last thing he could do. And it was what he was determined to do. Before... The End.... He would wrestle One Last Time.... And Never Again.....
