Date: kcuf Triads? Diablos? Westies? Hoodies? Can somebody say G-T-A? What the fuck do you think you’re doing son? Just what the hell is going through that rock hard skull of yours? You think you can create a plot that will topple me by ripping off some fucking video game? Who writes your script son, because I’d fire that sorry motherfucker. I guess the work reflects the artist, right? It reflects your shit attitude and uncreative attitude. I’ve come to notice that about the human race, our lack of desire and creativity. Our lack of passion for what we do. Our complacent outlook towards only allows a sick kind of rot to take hold within the bowels of our decrepit society. Yet this is not the greatest sin of all, my children. The greatest sin is that we turn a blind eye towards this complacency. We allow it to go on and on and on. We sit idly in the buses and malls, watching our fellow humans go about like chickens with their heads cut off. We sit and watch them shove one another over in a mad rush to gorge themselves on whatever product they’ve bought into. They see Brad Pitt or Randy Moss wearing it, sporting it, fucking it, and then they rush out to buy it. They feast on the propaganda that the advertisers set out before them. It’s a seven course meal, folks, so dig the fuck in. Throw yourself into the gym for hours doing high reps with low weights to fit yourself into those pair of size zero Abercrombie and Fitch jeans. Hit the dumbbells with heavy weight and low reps in the worn effort to make your clothes as tight as possible. Never mind the fact that the majority of Americans are genetically predestined to be fat or scrawny. Be sure to write all of the exercises down with yours reps inside of your littler goddamn journals, too! We gotta look good right folks? We have to because that’s what Ralph Lauren and the Gap are telling us to do right? I see you looking at that billboard, honey. Staring at her perfectly tanned skin, wishing you could have that honey gold complexion, never mind the fact that the cunt on that billboard is just as creamy white as your fat fucking ass; the only thing that separates you from her is about 50 pounds and a tanning booth. I see you sucking down that protein drink, son. It doesn’t matter that you’re always going to be a scrawny little fucker. It doesn’t matter that you’re always going to be a buck-fifty soaking wet, with your clothes on, you’re still guzzling that frothy brown liquid despite the fact that it looks like they poured right out of a toilet. You think that the girls are going to want you if you’ve got the mildly bulging biceps, the so-so chest, and the abdominals. I hate to break it to you, sonny-jim, but that bitch is going to be licking my shredded pectorals tonight. You know why? Because I didn’t buy into what Uncle Klein tried to sell me, and I busted my fucking ass on that weight pile. So while you look like the models in those jeans ads, trying to look mysterious yet approachable at the same time, I’ll be enjoying the oral pleasures of the women you want. Go beat off, junior, you can’t touch this shit. You don’t have the fucking genetics, the drive, the heart, or the mindset to achieve. All you do is lift your 35 pound dumbbells and wish you had this. Put in the time, motherfucker, and you still won’t get this. Back to the sermon… That’s just a little segment of our society. Just a tiny slice of the social stupidity that makes me sick to my stomach. It makes me wish that I was as unfeeling as a bug. Like a roach, just a motherfucking cockroach. Just scuttling along, putting in enough effort only to survive. Think about it. All you would need to do is get food and exist. Existence brought down to nothing but the very nexus of the definition of the word. Existing to only exist and nothing. Not bothered by any of the trivialities of an imperfect society that places their hopes in dreams in things that aren’t worth achieving in the first place. Think of how easy it would be. Scratch that. Even thinking is something that would not exist in the bliss of the Roach Theory. Thinking is an excess and our society is already bloated by excess. There is so much superfluous excess that it makes me nauseous. The excess that spews from the mouth of Jamie Celt, matches that which flows from mine. I WANT NOTHING TO DO WITH IT, MY CHILDREN! NOTHING AT ALL! CLEAR YOUR MINDS OF ALL THE THINGS THAT SOCIETY HAS THROWN UPON YOU. TAKE THE ARCANE SOCIETAL DISCOURSES AND IDEALS AND CHUCK THEM OUT THE WINDOW ALONG WITH THE NEW WAVE FASHION AND PHILOSOPHIES!!! SOMEBODY FLUSH THE MENTAL TOILET FOR THE BOWL OVERFLOWETH, SON! IT STINKS IN THIS JOINT! IT STINKS IN MY MIND! SSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO MMMMMMMMMMMUUUUUUUUUCCCCCCCCCHHHHHHHHHH SHIT!
