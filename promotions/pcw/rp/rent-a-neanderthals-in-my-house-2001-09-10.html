Rent-A-Neanderthals...IN MY HOUSE?!

10 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/10/01 The anniversary of September 11th is afoot, happening sooner than one would’ve thought, but the United States has come together as a country. One year couldn’t have changed Rush Mason. This time last year he was just entering an inter-fed of NTICW, PCW, and EW known as Prime Time Central, joining the PWA. There he matured enough to be called up to NTICW….where the story really began. Thus, that story has brought Rush into PCW, where he has now risen higher than many skeptics would’ve thought. Well….that’s not always good… “You….what are you doing to my first-rate home?!” Mmmhmm…that’s the man himself, Rush Mason. That’s his voice at least, and we can hear it although the scene is only beginning to fade in as is usual with Rush’s promos. The tone in Rush’s voice is a mix of surprise and anger as he yells at someone about something….which is basically what every single Rush promo is. “Wha…there are more of you sub-par plebeians inside my house?! Is it like some sort of parade for sub-parity?!” Yet again, we can only guess what Rush is referring to, but obviously he is expressing his love for fellow mankind with his fantastically original choice of words. “Oh my Bon Dieu…you have got to be kidding me. And finally the scene is fully faded in, and we see Rush standing inside a large lobby, looking around at a team of people who are all dressed in some sort of security uniform, and are either going through something or looking attentive and standing guard, saluting as Rush walks into the lobby. Rush: Can someone tell me why the Neanderthal Club is in my house?! At first no one moves, but finally one of the men walks towards him, and Rush nods, thinking he will get an answer, but the man begins to frisk Rush, and the Voodoo Villain steps back, looking at the man with a disgusted look. Rush: AHH!!! It’s The Neanderthal Ream Team!! You shan’t take this vessel without a fight!! The security guard tries to step back and look as non-threatening as possible and replies in a low grumble, trying not to alert Rush anymore than he already has. “Sir, we’re part of a security team that’s been sent to keep your home as safe as possible. Rush raises an eyebrow, not totally trusting the man, who quickly flashes a security badge and Rush shrugs, and asks again, although he taps his foot and tries to show his displeasure with his treatment so far.Rush: Okay…you nimrods have told me your ‘reason’ for being here….but who exactly sent guards…and second-rate ones at that….to guard me? The men look nervous with Rush insulting them, as though they’re young children afraid of doing something wrong in school. The same man from before replies again. “Well…the Front Office has sent security to all PCW superstars on the eve of this nation’s biggest tragedy. I suppose they felt you might need the most…especially considering how vulnerable you looked when Mr. Kannon fined you fifty-thousand dollars- Rush being Rush, he doesn’t let this fact hang in the air for two seconds, before he is shouting ferociously to the security guard, waving his arms and looking like he’s having some sort of seizure. Rush: I AM NOT VULNERABLE!! As Rush’s shout is heard, a few of the men jump in surprise, but no one notices, because all eyes are pointed to a fuming Rush, who has a large look of content upon his face, as the guard tries to sheepishly shrink, but unfortunately, he’s in the middle of the large lobby, with no where to go off to that he can escape the legendary Rush rant. Rush: I could’ve not paid that fine if I didn’t want to. In fact, I could get out of that tax if I so pleased…IN FACT THE UPRISING COULD BEAT THE ENTIRE FRONT OFFICE IF THEY LIKE! One of the security guards murmurs something about Black Attack and the word stablewhore can be heard, but Rush just shoots a glance, which quiets the whole room. The security man who has been talking to Rush the whole time takes a gulp and then speaks back in an even softer voice this time. “Erm…sir, you’ll have to go up to your bedroom, where we can fully utilize our protecting skills. Rush sort of half-mumbles and he turns away and starts to walk up the stair, clomping his feet in anger. Rush: Protecting skills my ass…I’d believe it if you weren’t a hairy ape-like creature with a sloping forehead… The security guards mumble amongst each other as Rush opens the door to his bedroom and just stands, his jaw open, while the security guards speak amongst each other as the scene begins to fade to black… “Man….these are so much better than our Rent-A-Cop uniforms.” “Joey…shh!” However, before we totally cut out, we are left with a black screen as we can hear Rush screaming. “AAAAAAARGH!!! MORE NEANDERTHALS!!!”
