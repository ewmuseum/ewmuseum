Being Watched - Chapter 3: Exploring The Unknown

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 25 - 03 Trying to hold his head up with his hands was like trying to lift a piano for Mr. Jones. Having lost the last three nights of sleep due to controversy and confusion among the company, he’s feeling a little worn and torn. His body feels as though a million lead weights are attached and his head is as heavy as a one-ton concrete block. As sits at the long steel table, surrounded by various strange tapestries and his own ominous reflection upon the polished walls, he can think of nothing but rest, “Gah… so tired…uhhhhnn…” Mr. Jones releases the cradling of his forehead and roughly rubs his eyes, trying to break the cement-like crust making his eyes droop. He doesn’t halt when footsteps begin to ring out from the far side of the room, coming closer every second. After about ten or so repetitions of clopping boots, the footsteps stop and a voice breaks through the air. “Evening, sir…” The thoroughly-exhausted Jones lifts his head up wearily and looks at his visitor: A young woman decked out in slick, steel armor with short black hair and a sneer of green lipstick. He nods, “Evening, Ms. Alicia…” “Please, Jones,” She strolls to his right side and takes a seat beside him, “You’ve known me for years. Just call me Alicia.” She suddenly flashes him a comforting smile, but only gets a grunt in response. “Company directive .55 states that any and all higher officials are to be called by their surnames…” The beautiful Alicia rolls her eyes and giggles, “You never change. Ever since I’ve known you, you’ve always blazed the beaten path. But not like any other man. You just go above and behind. And I think that’s why I fell in love with you.” “Spare me, Alicia,” Mr. Jones suddenly snaps groggily, obviously in no mood for this. He sits straight up in his seat and stretches out, relieving the bundled up tension wrapped around his entire skeletal structure. He then clasps his hands across his abdomen and sneers, “What do you want?” Her face suddenly contorts with mock hurt and her jaw drops, as if she’s ACTUALLY surprised that he would ask such a thing. “Jax… I’m HURT! I can’t just come and see you in a time of crisis? What happened to the beautiful thing we once shared?” “I said spare me. I really don’t have the patience for this soap opera right now. We’ve got a situation with the Brotherhood of the Machine and we think the Runners are involved, so all my attention is going to that. I APOLOGIZE that I cannot in this measly little game you call ‘cat and mouse’, but I really have no regrets about NOT being in it . Now, If you’ll excuse me, the councilors want to see me…” Mr. Jones begins to rise from his chair, but Alicia’s hands dart up and catch him around the arm. She yanks him down in the chair and flips her hair out of her face, giving him an annoyed frown. “Ajax, I heard about the situation earlier,” She begins, with a little impatience to her voice, “And I understand that you’re concerned about the company’s relationship with the Brotherhood. But you shouldn’t let it run your life. You’ve got advisors and councilors! You’re the damned Chancellor, for En’Flarim’s sake! You don’t have to do all of this!” Her attempt at convincing him to leave the situation at hand draws a sour look from Mr. Jones, “Hardly. It’s my duty and my honor to ensure our people are safe. That’s why I can’t share my love with you, because my love goes to everyone. And if the safety of my people are jeopardized because of some bastard’s PLOT to turn the company’s allies against it, then I’ll-“ “Plot??” “Uhh…” Having realized he said a little too much that not many know of, Mr. Jones quickly tries to cover up, “You’ve no doubt heard of the Steel Masked Man. That’s all I speak of. Nothing more.” He then turns away to pull his eyes away from her spying, interrogative look. However, she’s not one to give up. “Ajax,” She rises from her chair and turns him around forcefully, “You’re hiding something, and you only do that when it’s serious. I know of the situation too, and I can’t think of anyone that would want to frame the Brotherhood and have them turn against us alongside the Runners. Or to screw up our only ace in the hole in Dorn Tracer. There’s just no plausible motive. The High Templar Buyatis is dead and the company has jurisdiction over everything, so nothing makes sense… unless you know something I don’t.” Mr. Jones suddenly gets a dark, suspicious look on his face, “Why are you so anxious to know? You’re one of General Carns’ people, and he was the one who executed Councilman Damien on evidence HE brought up. Shouldn’t YOU know everything there is to know? Or maybe YOU know something I don’t?” He glares into her eyes and makes her backstep a little. She nibbles on her lip and turns her eyes away. “Well… I don’t really KNOW anything… but I’ve caught wind.” “Of what?” Jones inquires, that suspicious tone will deep in his voice. Alicia looks unsure at first, but she finally breathes a sigh and nods, “Alright, but you have to promise not to tell. I think it’s top secret.” When Mr. Jones nods, Alicia shifts her weight and begins, “Well, General Carns planted that symbol on the projection image of the Steel Masked Man to get back at the Brotherhood for their verbal assault on our division. Seems petty, but you know how Carns is. But his reasons seem to go beyond that. I think maybe someone ELSE is convincing him of something and making him take shots at the Brotherhood. He’s seemed rather vague and unsure lately, like he doesn’t know what to do next. I think someone is trying to manipulate bad blood between the company and the Brotherhood, but for what gain, I’m not sure. The High Templar is dead, so what else is there?” Mr. Jones shakes his head, “Nothing except the re-emergence of Astrology as a major religion. With Dorn’s recent success, we’re VERY close. People have begun to follow again and that means everything to us. But I don’t know who would want to sabotage it…” “I’m sorry, but that’s all I know, Ajax,” Alicia then gives a sigh and folds her arms across her chest, “I don’t know how much help that was. There isn’t evidence of a third party, but that’s what it sounds like. “ Mr. Jones rises from his seat and dusts off his suit before taking Alicia by the arm gently, “Thank you. You’ve been a great help. I’ll be sure to discuss this with the councilors, though I think they’ll be a little alarmed by it. I don’t really WANT to tell them, but I have to. So I’ll talk to you later.” Alicia nods, “Later, Ajax.” With that, Mr. Jones brushes past Alicia and heads for the far door. Alicia watches him leave closely and sneers when he’s gone. Suddenly, from behind a square steel pillar in the corner of the room emerges General Carns. He strides over to Alicia and folds his arms across his chest, “Think he bought it?” “Most certainly.” “Good,” Carns replies with a grin on his face, “Then the pawns are moving forward as planned. I relayed the message to the High Templar, and he has agreed. We should contact Dorn Tracer as soon as possible.” “We don’t have much time…” “You’re right, my friend…” [End.]
