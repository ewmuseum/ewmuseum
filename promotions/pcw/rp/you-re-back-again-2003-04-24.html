You're back again?

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th April. You’ve come back to me…again? Damn it….what’s your name? Daniel Hitchmen? And why are you here….again? You want to know even more about this new PCW Wrestler Sivasian Krikorian, right? Well sorry son…no can do, not for you, not for anyone….thank you, and wipe your feet on the way out… 100 bucks? Hmmm….that changes everything….quick then, I don’t have much time, my wife’s gone and tried to cut her piles off with scissors again…she’s in the bathroom screaming as we speak. Now listen…I’m gunna be straight with ya, I’ve had…shall we say, a couple drinks before you suddenly turned up, even though you’ve been here before. I mean didn’t you even get enough information last time son? No? How come, I gave you some background for your stupid magazine, go and friggin ask the man himself about….himself. Want me to show you an example? ‘Hello Mr.Krikorian, can we PLEASE ask you a few questions, you’re friend said we could…’ and there you are, go and speak to him son. Oh…yeah…$100….if I’m smiling, it’s got nothing to do with the fact I’m getting some hefty cash to speak for a couple minutes. What ya wanna know then, anything that I didn’t tell you last time I’d guess at. What’s that, you want to know how I knew so much, or rather knew and know so much about the mystery that is Sivasian, at least to you anyway. Alright, I’ll spill the beans. Firstly, my parents were born in a rough part of New York, both of them. They met each other, married…then my dad had problems, shall we say, you know, his gun was firing off too quickly….oh sorry. So anyways, they marry and I’m born, however, this is right after the second world war, and our family, my mother being an architect and my father being a builder get shipped of to help rebuild western Germany, you know, my dad always use to mutter ‘It’s easy money, what is it?’ and my mother would sarcastically reply ‘Easy Money’ and then rub her fingers together, mafia style. So yeah, we’re they’re, but we hate it. Once the contracts up and we’ve done our bit, I move out to the Ukrainian fields, we get A LOT for our money, acres and acres of land, shooting ground, a small swimming pool, and some cattle. So anyway, I meet this shyish kid, and we start to be friends. We go hunting together, have I mentioned just how damn good Siv was with the rifle. I should think if he was in a wrestling match, he’d use those sneaky features of his, his years and years of experience, you know he’s like 43 right now? Done everything in his life, I see these kids on TV and they’ve got nothing on Siv. Siv’s got the experience, ya know, shouldn’t be losing to an oaf and some stupid Pharaoh guy, really, let’s be honest. I should think Sivasian should be a great wrestler, he’s got…charm…well some at least, can speak pretty good English, but fans should be pleasantly surprised at his excellent calmness under pressure. I heard he had three men charge at him once, from about 500 metres with machine guns. Most people would panic, most, in fact, would friggin shit their pants, but not Siv, no, no not this calm man. He breathed a large sigh, took a deep breath, steadied his rifle and went along them, BANG! BANG! BANG! Took each man down with one head shot each. What was that question? Do I think Sivasian will be worried he’s facing two ends of the scale here? One huge man, weighing in at 350 pounds, and one man, quick as lightning? No, in fact I think he’ll cope well. He’s always been diverse and willing to adopt change. He’ll use his speed against the oaf, and the fast one shalt be beaten with his pure power. He has power you ask? Why yes, even though he’s 43 , he hasn’t let himself go like that Arnold Schwarzeneger….or however you say his name. His got muscles that are tighter than an asian prostitute’s pu… Oh, I didn’t realise you weren’t into cheap, skanky , sex jokes, sorry. And yes, I believe Sivasian will do well, will use his experience to plough threw people, to gain title after title and put Ukraine on the map. Yes, yes it could be seen as a crusade for Sivasian. Hey kid, fancy a drink? You’re treat? Thanks!
