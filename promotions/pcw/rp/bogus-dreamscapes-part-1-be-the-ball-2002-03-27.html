Bogus Dreamscapes - Part 1 - Be The Ball

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 27 [The scene opens on an upclose shot of Jessica Alba, star of the FOX television show Dark Angel. He lips full, red and supple, always ready for action. She is posed sideways so that her beautiful behind is accented in the spotlight that shines behind her. The scene begins zooming out showing that its just a promotional poster of Jessica, and it hangs on a very dull, light blue colored wall] [Two men stand in front of the poster, admiring the lovely creature that is the main attraction. The man on the left, with the long brown hair in a pony tail, wants nothing more than to give Jessica a taste of why they call him 'Heavy Metal' Harry. The metal one wears a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black pajama bottoms, and is bare foot. His bloodshot, glazed eyes are filled with uncontrollable lust. The man on the right, with the short, bright blonde hair, knows that Jessica wants to play a game of Yahtzee with him...all night long! 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, the other half of Cowabunga Connection, has the same lusting look that his long time friend and tag team partner has. Tommy wears a matching set of pajamas which are blue and yellow pinstripes, and on the back of the top it says in yellow letters, 'The Totally Tubular Pajama's'] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Jessica Alba is a bodacious babe. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [The two men are up in a hotel for the night, traveling through the United States to do promotional things for PCW. This isn't the most luxurious hotel that the Connection has stayed in so far, but it could be worse. There are two single beds with the same ugly light blue color as the wall paper and a large nightstand sits in between the two men which contains a lamp and a digital alarm clock. A large, oak entertainment center, which contains an average sized television set and a stereo, sits in front of them. The window, to the right of Harry's bed overlooks the poolside area and the gym] [Harry and Tommy then begin to slide underneath the sheets, to put an official end to another radical day as PCW superstars. The two men sit against the oak headboards, both reflecting on the upcoming gauntlet match] Harry: Do you think that we can win this match, man? TTT: Totally! The other teams are most bogus in all different areas. We've already beaten most of them anyway. [Harry smiles, nodding his head as he then looks up at the poster of Jessica Alba] Harry: Do you think that once we are PCW Tag Team Champions that we can party with Jessie, man? TTT: Most righteously, dude. Harry: Would I even have a chance with her, man? TTT: Of course you would, compadre! It would be sloppy seconds because I'd get her first. Harry: Oh yeah, man. [The two men then look up at the poster, kiss their hands, and then slap their spit onto the poster] Harry and Tommy: Goodnight, Jessica. TTT: Goodnight, compadre. Harry: Goodnight, man. [The two men quickly drift off into slumber, thinking about the upcoming tag team gauntlet match, becoming PCW tag team champions, and playing Yahtzee with Jessica Alba. Everything slowly starts combining into one and melding together. The darkness of slumber begins turning into light, entering the world of dreams] [The scene is of nothing but blinding white lights all around. The Tubular One, decked out in a white jump suit, floats through the air slowly. Its almost like he's died and is going to heaven] TTT: Gnarly. [His voice echoes and vibrates throughout this place he is in. He smiles and the bright lights reflect off of his shining teeth, which could make another person go absolutely blind] TTT: TUBULAR! Echo: TUBULAR...tubular... TTT: BODACIOUS! Echo: BODACIOUS...bodacious... TTT: RADICAL! Echo: RADICAL...radical... TTT: COWABUNGA CONNECTION RULES! Echo: GOLDEN GAME RULES...golden game rules... [Tommy seems absolutely confused by this situation. He swears that he didn't say Golden Game but the echo came back as Golden Game. Now why would the echo go and do something totally bogus like that? Must have been his imagination playing tricks on him, he'll try again] TTT: COWABUNGA CONNECTION RULES!! Echo: GOLDEN GAME RULES...golden game rules... [Tommy throws his hands on his hips, looking upward with a very angry look. Now that time he heard it! What kind of bogus crap is this? This echo is going to get a piece of his mind] TTT: Golden Game are totally bogus dudes, echo! Echo: They rule...they rule... TTT: No way! They are just two foreign dudes who got lucky! Echo: Shut up, dumbass...shut up, dumbass... TTT: Golden Game just lucked out because the champions before them were having bogus problems, and were attacked. That caused the total bogusness of Golden Game becoming the most heinous tag team champions of PCW, compadre. Echo: Your gay...your gay... TTT: I am not! Echo: Are to...are to... TTT: AM NOT!! Echo: Are to...are to... TTT: I'm going to kick your ass, dude! Echo: I'd like to see you try...i'd like to see you try... [Suddenly all of the lights go out and everything is drenched in darkness] TTT: Hey, who turned out the lights? [The lights begin to slowly dim up now. Tommy notices that his white wardrobe is gone and he's in something else. Too bad though, because that white jump suit looked gnarly on him. Then there is a faint sound of chanting and cheering in the distance, and it seems to be getting closer and closer. The lights still are slowly coming on above and they look like lights you'd see at stadiums or arenas. He can feel a slight breeze going past his legs, as if he is wearing shorts, but they aren't his swim trunks. They don't have the swim trunk feel to them, but a very smooth feeling] [Then the lights burst on and a large crowd roars in excitement. The stadium is huge. Tommy has never seen anything like this before. The see of people goes on forever as they are all dressed in Spain's colors, and waving their flag proudly. Tommy looks down at his clothing and its a blue and yellow soccer uniform. On his shirt, in yellow letters, it says 'Cowabunga Connection Sucks'] TTT: BOGUS! I didn't put this on. [Then a finger taps him on the shoulder and Tommy quickly turns around as if he's ready to defend himself. The man who did the tapping was Harry. He is in the same soccer uniform that Tommy is in and even with the most bogus slogan on the shirt] Harry: Soccer sucks, man. TTT: Totally, dude. Soccer is one of the most bogus sports! It in no way pales in comparison with the bodacious of them all...SURFING! Harry: Yeah, man. [The crowd is chanting and singing something in a foreign language. Harry and Tommy look all around noticing that they are in a gigantic soccer field. The nets on each side, the midfield, and the lines drawn out perfectly. Why in the hell are they on a soccer field anyway? They don't play soccer, they are wrestlers!] [From the other side of the field, a group of men come filing out from the corridor. Even in the distance you can tell that they are wearing green shirts and red shorts, the colors that one half of Golden Game, Juan Borro, wears. Tommy notices immediately] TTT: That's that bogus dude, Juan Borro! Harry: Yeah, man. [The men get closer and they all have long, dark hair which are tied in pony tails] Harry: They stole my hair cut, man. TTT: You don't have a hair cut dude! You have a hair style. [Harry shrugs and then points at the opposing forces] Harry: They stole my hair style, man! TTT: Much better, compadre. [The men aren't Juan Borro, but very cheap imitations of the man. Tommy notices that they are certainly more attractive than that most heinous dude. Harry stares at them with hate, wanting so desperately to cut their hair] Team of Imitation Juan's: GOLDEN GAME RULES!! GOLDEN GAME RULES!! Crowd: GOLDEN GAME RULES!! GOLDEN GAME RULES!! [A group of refs wearing the same colors as the team of Juan's strolls up dropping a soccer ball onto midfield. The team of imitation Juan's has about fifteen to twenty players and Cowabunga Connection just stands as two men] Harry: Is this fair, man? TTT: No way, dude. Even imitation Juan's have to cheat to beat the better team, compadre. Harry: Yeah, man. [The Juan's start off quick by just taking one swing at the ball and it bounces right into the net. The crowd goes absolutely crazy, as Harry and Tommy just stand there in confusion] TTT: We were supposed to stop that? Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy runs down the field and picks up the ball. One of the refs comes up and snatches the ball from Tommy's hands] Ref: No hands! TTT: I have two of them, dude. [The ref rolls his eyes and sighs] Ref: Your not allowed to use your hands in soccer unless your the goalie. TTT: Oh. What's a goalie? [Meanwhile the Juan's have dropped in another ball and are just running around Harry, passing the ball to one another. It's kicked toward the goal and it smacks Tommy right in the head] TTT: OUCH! Ref: Nice save. [Tommy then taps the ball with his foot and smiles like a little child that discovered something for the first time. He then kicks it again, but one of the imitation Juan's steps up and scores again. The crowd goes completely insane as the imitation Juan's celebrate like they are winning millions] TTT: That's cheating, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. Ref: They cleanly stole the ball from you. Then they cleanly kicked the ball around you and scored. You feel like half an ass right about now I bet. [Tommy smiles as if holding something over the ref] TTT: No, I am a whole ass! [The ref looks confused and just smiles] Ref: Yeah, whatever you say. [Tommy then kicks the ball a few times and looks down field. There are a lot of imitation Juan's down there, but he can get past them. He's The Tubualr One and he can do anything if he sets his mind to it. With the help of Harry it is an unstoppable force! That's why the Cowabunga Connection deserve to be the tag team champions, because they are an unstoppable force! Even a team of imitation Juans cannot stop the team of Cowabunganess!] TTT: Do you hear that, Har? Harry: Yeah, the crowd hates us, man. TTT: Not that. The other thing. Harry: The Juan's saying we suck, man? TTT: No, the other thing. Harry: The Quiet Riot, man? TTT: That's right...the Quiet Riot. You know what happens when they hear that, right? Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy and Harry begin going into super soccer mode. They pass the ball back and forth as if they have been playing soccer for years. They juke out a pair of Juan's making them fall on their faces. Harry begins bouncing the ball from knee to knee confusing more of the Juan's. He passes to Tommy who then kicks hard into one of the Juan's faces and knocks him out. The ball rebounds back to Tommy who then passes it to a wide open Harry. Its smooth sailing from here as Harry trots in for the easy goal] [The crowd turns absolutely silent. The imitation Juan's hold their heads low. Then, it all just disappears from view. It is now just an empty stadium, well not really empty with Tommy and Harry inside of it] TTT: We did it, dude! We beat the imitation Juan's! COWABUNGA! Harry: Yeah, man. [Suddenly the two men begin shrinking. Their bodies become more compact and round] TTT: I'm getting short and fat!! BOGUS! Harry: Morphing is far out, man. [The two men are shaped like two round balls, about the size of a soccer ball. An imitation Juan appears at midfield with a smile on his face] TTT: You look like a soccer ball, dude. Harry: So do you, man. TTT: Hey, what's that Juan going to do? Harry: Kick us, man. [The Juan runs forward and then boots the Tommy soccer ball as hard as he can. The Tommy ball flies out of the stadium] TTT: BOOOOOGGGUUUUSSSS!! [The scene quickly changes to Tommy sitting up in his bed. Sweat pours down all over his face and his chest. His gnarly PJ's are just completely soaked. Only seconds behind Harry sits up in his bed, also soaked in sweat. The two men look at each other with horror on their faces] TTT: Did you dream about the same thing I was dreaming? Harry: No, I was dreaming about Jessica Alba and not soccer. Not about soccer at all, man. TTT: Yeah, same here, dude. [The two men then turn their backs to each other, curled up with the sheets] TTT: Goodnight, dude. Harry: Goodnight, man. [The two men close their eyes and fall off into the slumber world once again. Then the light kicks in....] TO BE CONTINUED!!
