Final Thoughts

3 March 2002

Date: 3/03/02 Here go in the final words before the pay per view. The time for everyone to step into to the ring is rapidly approaching. It is a PCW pay per view, so expect the best entertainment around. Everyone has been talking a lot. Now it comes donw to everyone saying their final words, and it is almost time to prove it now. As the cliche goes, it is time to walk the walk. So now we join 'Amazing' Mike Storm for his final words before the March Madness PPV. So here we go to join him Scene: Camera opens to the locker room of 'Amazing' Mike Storm. He is stretching out in his wrestling attire. He finishes stretching and starts doing a few jumping jacks to get his blood going, to keep warm for his match up. He then stops, and sits down on the bench. He looks up to the camera. 'Amazing' Mike Storm: I am ready. I can feel it. I can go for hours on how I am feeling. I can not wait to get into that ring and beat Brock Newbludd like he needs to be beaten. I can not wait to gain the right to get match for the Television Championship. To get a chance to once again try and save the Television title. I am looking foward to having a Television Title match up, where it actually has a winner, instead of interference ending it prematurely. Mike Storm: I get sick of getting screwed out of the belt. I get sick of people saving the champion. I have had two shots at the Television title, and both times it ended with no winner. Both times I was basically screwed out of the belt. I will not allow that to happen this time around. You see Brock Newbludd honestly thinks that he will win the shot at the belt, but I will not allow it. I will not let him. I will injure myself to stop him. I will hurt myself for the sole purpose to hurt him even more. Scene: Storm gets up, he begins to jog a little bit in place. Mike Storm: Got to keep warm. Got to keep ready. I have said it before, I am full of energy. I have warned you about the Blitzkrieg coming your way Newbludd, and I hope you are ready to experience, because tonight I become the human blitzkrieg. I hope you know what you have gotten in to. Take some time to consider it before you go out to the ring, consider what you are up against. I mean to think hard about it, you will realize that you can not win, your attempts will be futile. You will try all you know, everything you can thorw at me, you will, but in the end I will stand alone. Mike Storm: I mean let us face it, that is what it will come down, the last amn standing. It is not going to be finished off with a regular wrestling move. It will not be won with a ddt, or a closeline, or anything basic like that. It will come down to an innovative move, or a painful variation of moves we normally do. Sure Brock calls himself the innovator, but what moves can you think of that he has made up? No one can think of any I bet. He had the Sky Twister Press, but that is just a rip off the most incredible high flying move PCW has ever seen, the Sky Twisting Moonsault, performed by me of course. Mike storm: Brock be ready, becasue tonight you are gonig to feel incredible pain, like you never know before. Like you did not think possible. You are going to see why I am truly, AMAZING! Scene: Camera fades out as stretches out some more.
