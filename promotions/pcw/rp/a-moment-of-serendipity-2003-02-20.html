A Moment of Serendipity

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/20/03 They say only fools fall in love. I guess that makes me a fool. A woman had entered my life and stole from me my heart. I knew I was in love with her from the moment I had first laid eyes upon her. Love at first sight I guess you could call it. However, how could I know I was in love with her? What could make me so sure of an emotion I can’t even describe? I guess you could call it a moment of serendipity. So without farther ado, here is how I almost cheated on Tessa Windsor. The day has been a long one indeed. It only seemed to be getting longer and longer as it continued to drag on. I was out and about in town by myself. Tessa had told me she needed to do some things herself so I dropped her off in front of the mall and went about my day, telling her I would pick her up later. I didn’t really do much. One of the reasons the day seemed to drag on so. I guess I just had so much on my mind that day. I mean wouldn’t you? I had actually kept a promise that I never thought I could. I had managed to defeat two men in one match. I had told Evan that I would do it for him and by Kami I did just that. I felt so great in that moment. Course life couldn’t stay as sweet for me as Tessa had to suffer a defeat at the hands of El Romantico, something that troubled me greatly. I wasn’t disappointed in Tessa as she had wrestled a great match. I just wanted to see her win the Cruiserweight title and more importantly, mop the floor with that arrogant bastard. Course the thought of those tag team titles still danced in my head like sugar plums. Friday, Tessa and I would get another chance to reclaim them. However it wasn’t P.W.A. that we had to defeat but rather the team of Clyde and DarkStar. Jackson Rust and I had defeated them before so I wasn’t too terribly worried about this match. But I knew not to get too sure of myself. A lesson I had to learn the hard way after countless attacks from the likes of El Romantico, Adam Stryker, and Erik Erratic. I still had the bumps and bruises from Turmoil to support that statement. So finally I decided that maybe it was time to go pick up Tessa at the mall. I figured we could share lunch together and talk about a game plan for Fusion. Arriving at the mall, I noticed finding her might be a bit of a challenge. Thankfully she wrote down a list of stores she would be at so if I needed her I could simply find her. I got out of the mini van and pulled the piece of paper out of my back pocket. I guess my luck wasn’t up to par however, as a strong gust of wind found to it that the piece of paper went flying from my grasp and across the large parking lot. “Shit! How am I going to find Tessa now?” I asked myself. I looked over at the large mall; countless stores with countless people inside of them. This wasn’t going to be easy. “Well…” I said with a sigh. “I guess all I can do is walk around until I find her.” It shouldn’t be so hard I thought. I raised my chin up and walked towards the entrance. The automatic doors opened for me as I walked near them. I was hit by a nice blast of warm air as I entered the mall, a welcomed change from the cold winds outside. Looking around there was people everywhere. Different stores hugged up against each other, lining the walls of the mall. Each store had several people inside and several more outside the store just looking around. I let out a groan, knowing this wasn’t going to be as easy as I had hoped it would be. I looked around for a good place to start my search for my lovely fiancée. The first place that took my attention was a nearby candle store. Tessa had candles all over the house. Maybe she was inside of there looking for more. “Gah not more of those candles. Any more and the house will burn down.” I complained. Needlessly I still marched toward the candle store. {30 minutes later} “Why would that make the mall so large?” I ask myself, still trying to find Tessa. After checking in multiple stores and still no sign of her, I was starting to get a little annoyed. I decided that after 30 minutes of searching I could use a break. I glanced around for a good place to take a break. There was the food count, a horrible excuse for fatting food. Chris didn’t even trust the health food. The food count was out of question. He spotted the coffee bar. A frown comes across his face. Drinking coffee with people who thought they were something they weren’t. College students trying to make it look like they where studying when they actually had no idea what they were even looking at. I don’t think I could stand being at the coffee bar, not even if I was hyped up on caffeine. Then I posted the place I could just hang out and rest my feet. An open book store was sitting across the coffee bar. I could just grab a copy of my favorite book, plop down on the chair, and enjoy myself. I made my way toward the book store, pleased with the nice atmosphere that greeted me. I looked around at the different sections that welcomed me. I made my way toward the news section. There was one paper left. “Chris! Oh my god it’s Chris Storm!” I hear someone say next to me. I turned to see some young kid standing there, tears nearly in his eyes. I gave him a smile and bent over to see what he wanted. He held out a pen and piece of paper. I gladly gave him an autograph and patted him on the head. I watched as the young kid ran off to his parents hugging them. He looks back over toward me and points. I wave to his parents and give them a smile. They wave back, I can tell the dad mouthed “Thank you” to me. They all walked off together like a family should. I turned back to look for the newspaper but much to my dismay it was now gone. My smile slipped off my face as I looked around for where my newspaper had gone too. I could see the top of it between the shelves of books. I made a course to the person with the newspaper, bent on getting it from them. I dodged past the fiction and through the sci-fi section. I followed the newspaper as it moved about the store. I finally was able to get a good view of the person, although it was only the back of them. I stopped dead in my tracks. My heart felt like it skipped a beat. It was a woman with the paper clearly. She had the paper open, reading it, so I couldn’t see her face but her body. My goodness what a body she had. My eyes couldn’t leave her curves. She went behind another shelf and my heart started to beat again. I had to grab onto the rack to keep myself from falling weak at the knees. “My Kami, she is beautiful. I don’t think I have ever seen a women more lovely then her. And I haven’t even seen her face yet!” I told myself. Then a thought hit me. What about Tessa, the woman I am suppose to marry? Just the vision of that angel had made me completely forget about her and all my problems. It was like an addictive drug. I felt bad about forgetting about Tessa, even if it was for a moment, but I needed to find this woman. I had to see her face. I just knew it would be enough to make me forget all my troubles. There was just something inside of me that told me it would. My attention was snapped as I heard the paper shuffle. I glanced up to see the woman was leaving the store. I quickly made my way out of the store to follow her. I tried to walk fast enough to where I could get just a glance at her face but she had a bit of a head start on me. I nearly fell over head over heels when I see where she stopped next. The women had walked up to a cookie store and grabbed a bag of cookies. Now if there was anything I loved more then anything else, it was cookies. My lips watered just thinking about them. I rushed up to the older woman behind the counter of the cookie store. “Hello, how may I help you?” she asked me politely as can be. I rubbed my chin and pointed toward the woman I have been following. “Can you tell me what that woman just ordered?” I asked her. The woman behind the counter gave me a smile. “She ordered half a dozen chocolate chips.” She said in a slow voice. I frowned a little. Chocolate chip was my favorite. “Anything else?” I asked her. There had to be more then just that. No one could just eat cookies plain. “Well…” the old woman said, tapping her chin. “She did get a small thing of milk to go with it. She did say something about not being able to eat them without milk.” I could feel my heart racing. I twirled around on my toes. Cookies and milk, what a match made in heaven. There was something about this woman that drove me nuts for her. I couldn’t figure it out but I didn’t care. This woman was something that I needed. She seemed to be able to make me forget all my troubles. I thanked the woman at the cookie store and hurried out of the store to find her. I spotted the top of her head, her magnificent hair floated in the air. I balled my fist as I just couldn’t seem to get enough of her drug. I was fending for another hit of her. Whoever this woman was, she was heading toward the athletic store. Whoa, is she for real? First she gets my paper, and then she buys cookies and milk, now she is going to an athletic store. I could feel my stomach twisting into knots as I started to get nervous. My palm starts to sweat. She was doing something to me, I couldn’t figure it out. What was this I am feeling? I feel like a school boy again. That was it; I needed to find out who this woman was. Out of impulse I grabbed a long stem rose from the florist and tossed him a twenty. I made my way to the athletic store. I can’t believe how nervous I am. I haven’t been this nervous since I first decided that I should tell Tessa how I felt about her. That was an internal battle for me just like now. “Oh man…” I caught myself saying. I can’t believe how I am acting over another woman. I thought I loved Tessa. Yet here this other woman is making me feel like a bird in flight. I felt ashamed of myself for thinking such thoughts and having such emotions for this woman I knew nothing about. I had no idea what her face even looked like, yet I felt like I could spend the rest of my life with this person. I felt so dirty. I know I should avoid this woman otherwise I might do something that would ruin my relationship with Tessa. “Ah the hell with is!” I shouted mentally. I needed to find the truth. Who was this woman and why was she making me feel like this? I just had to know. This was like some cruel joke. Here a man I have already promised my heart to another and now I find some other that makes my heart sing. Right now I was willing to risk everything to know the truth. I spotted the woman facing the wall of weights. There were different clothes and weights on the wall she was looking at. I still couldn’t see her face. I made my way up behind her, with each step my heart beat faster and faster. Soon I was right up behind her. I held the rose close to my heart. I wasn’t sure why I grabbed it though. It was just a spur of the moment type of thing. The woman still hadn’t noticed me yet, which was surprising because it felt like my heart was beating so loudly that everyone could hear it. I took the rose, went over her shoulder, and placed it under her nose. Before she could even react I moved in close to her and whispered into her ear. “You are without a doubt the most stunning woman I have ever met. Your beauty is unsurpassable. Not even the gentle touch of this rose is worth of you….” Before I can even finish what I want to say the woman turns around. My skin turns white and my blood runs cold. I can not believe what is going on. My mind is racing in a million directions now. The woman my heart has been beating for, the woman I have been addicted to like a deadly drug, and she’s….she’s….Tessa. Indeed it was Tessa. She turns around, tears building up in her eyes. She looked more beautiful in that moment then I could ever recall before. The entire mall seemed to have disappeared as for just a moment the world revolved around just the two of us. It was moments before either of us could speak. The words wanted to be said but never could find the strength to say it. My hand, trembling as is, reaches out and cups her face. I want to say so many things to her yet I don’t know where to begin. This woman I have been obsessing about was the same woman that captured my heart before. I can’t believe I just fell in love with her again. Is it even possible to love someone that much to where without even knowing it is them, you know you love them still? There was no doubt in my mind about the wedding. No fears of Clyde and DarkStar on Friday. There was only the love I have for Tessa. It was in that moment that I finally realized that I truly did love her more then anything else in the world. And so my hand shook as I held her face close to mine. I leaned in and gentle kissed her sweet lips. I brought my lips up to her neck, gentling placing kiss after kiss up her neck to behind her ear. My nose brushes a piece of hair away from her adorable little ear. I whisper into it with all the love I could muster up. “Tessa, I love you.” A single tear rolled down my face. A fool truly does fall in love on a moment of serendipity.
