Meeting Dirty Den

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st June 2002 Oh how the world mocks the unfortunate. Ok, so maybe this particular writer is still reeling from the old 2-1 at the hands of Brazil, but frankly, well he figured he would try to take his mind off it by writing another enthralling promo. So he wrote Xavier Kannon’s one, and now he’s thinking maybe writing a bad one might work just as well. Last time on the life and times of Alexander Miyagawa we saw what has to be called a very one sided conversation. Natalia made it completely clear through ignoring Alexander’s protests that Alexander should have a bodyguard. Oh and he should probably carry some mace too. Admittedly Miyagawa would have said that he would and then not had done it had it not been for a simple fact... Natalia was determined to hire the bodyguard for him. So much so that he got a knock on his hotel door at 3pm the following day, right here and now in Charlotte, North Carolina. He was lying on his back on top of the bed (fully dressed of course in a rather natty knitted black jumper) while watching some local news channel. He waits for a cry of “Room Service” or “Maid!” but nothing is forthcoming; eventually he responds to the knock. Miyagawa: Who is it? Nothing answers. So Miyagawa gets up from the bed slowly and wanders over to the door, admiring the always cream and wood coloured furniture on the way. He notes that his ice bucket is getting a bit low, and maybe after he gets this jerk to sod off, then he should go get a refill and a couple cans of Pepsi on the way back. He opens the door and is immediately pushed back by a masked figure dressed in what is best described as Ninja gear, just with a heavy black matrix jacket on as well, but over the top. He’s pushed back right to the best, struggling all the way. Masked Intruder: Looks like I came right in time. I could have been anyone. Miyagawa looks at the figure dressed in all, black, only the person’s eyes showing through the mask and he looks with fear at what the hell is going on in his hotel room. Miyagawa: You still could be anyone, what the hell is going on here?! The ninja person stands back up and releases the pressure holding Miyagawa down onto the bed. The person walks over to the open door and closes it quietly. Meanwhile, Miyagawa leaps from the bed and charges the ninja, but the ninja turns around graceful and executes a leg sweep, sending Miyagawa flying onto his behind. Masked Intruder: Have you quite finished tarting about, because I have to check the security of this room, otherwise I might have to get you moved. Miyagawa (looking really annoyed now): But I like my room. It’s opposite the ice machine and the Pepsi machine. What’s wrong with the room? The masked intruder walks over towards the window and checks what’s outside it and nods slowly. Masked Intruder: Good, a nice open field. No sniper positions. Miyagawa (sounding shocked): Sniper positions? Who would want to kill me? Masked Intruder: You’re in a contest of strength with a known Al-Queda affiliate and his affiliate. Not to mention that they are both consorting with a member of a known cult. Miyagawa: Huh? Oh, do you mean Chef, Parshin and Kannon? They’re nothing I can’t handle. Masked Intruder: Your wife didn’t seem to think so. That’s why I was sent. I spent four years in the Marine Corps and then I worked as a Minnie Mouse in Disneyland just to toughen me up. Miyagawa finally catches on to the fact that this must in fact be “The Bodyguard”. Miyagawa: I was expecting Kevin Costner. Masked Intruder: He was already on assignment, so you got me. Finally after all that, the intruder removes his black mask to reveal his face... a shaven ginger hair style with a rather pointy chin and rather long eyelashes for a man. Masked Intruder: And the Disneyland thing was a joke. You got that, right? Miyagawa shrugs and finally gets himself off the ground before sitting back on the end of the bed. Miyagawa: So what do we do now? Bodyguard: I protect you. Miyagawa: Which involves me doing what? Bodyguard: Acting normal. Miyagawa laughs are a few seconds at the joke. Bodyguard: What? Did I say something funny? Miyagawa: It’s that... well... I’m not exactly normal to start with. Have you never seen me on the television? Bodyguard: I examined a few tapes on the flight over and I feel that it may be in the best interests of my profession to protect you down to ringside as well. I have been paid extra to do so and I am sure you noticed that I am well trained in the martial arts. Miyagawa: Ah yes, but do you know Miya-jutsu? Bodyguard: Miya-jutsu is a martial art that you made up because you only managed to learn a single chokehold in Judo. So you mixed together some wrestling moves and called it a martial art. Suddenly there is a knock at the door, and like a flash of lightning, the bodyguard is standing to the side of the door with a small pistol raised. Miyagawa: Woah, put the gun away, it’s just room service. I ordered a pizza. Bodyguard: Has it got anchovies on it? Miyagawa: Of course not, they’re just all salty and... wait, what has that got to do with the gun issue? The bodyguard slips the gun back into the thigh holster and opens the door to the room service cart which is reeled in by a young man. The bodyguard tips the young man and removes the tray from the cart and places it on the bed. Bodyguard: Stand back. Miyagawa: Stand back? It’s a pizza for Christ’s sake. The bodyguard lifts up the chrome dome covering the pizza plate and sees that it is just a pizza under there. He hands the dome to the young man and virtually escorts him and the cart back out the door. Miyagawa goes over to grab a slice. Bodyguard: POISON! Miyagawa’s eyes dart about in a panic. Miyagawa: Where? Where? The Bodyguard closes the door and walks back over to the bed and points at the pizza. Bodyguard: On the pizza. Terrorists might have poisoned your pizza. Miyagawa: What terrorists? Bodyguard: Members of cults typically have followers. This L Ron Hubbard fellow has many followers and unlike the Jews in Germany during the second world war, they do not have to wear sashes on their arms with the Star of Hubbard or something. Therefore they could be anywhere. That bellboy could have been a follower of the cult of Hubbard and we would never have known. He could have poisoned this pizza. Miyagawa: Is there any way to tell? Bodyguard: There is a quick and simple way to tell. Miyagawa: Then do it. The bodyguard reaches down and picks up a slice of the meat feast pizza. He lifts it up and examines it closely. Then he starts to eat it. Miyagawa: Hey! That’s my pizza! Bodyguard: I could have sent it to the lab for tests **munches pizza** but it would have been cold and probably mouldy by the time we get it back. Miyagawa: Gah; can I have a slice of my own frickin’ pizza now? Bodyguard: Go right ahead, there isn’t anything wrong with this at all. Miyagawa shakes his head in disbelief as he picks up a slice of the meat feast pizza. Miyagawa: So what do I call you then? Bodyguard: The name is Den; some people call me Dirty Den. The couple shakes hands as they begin to share anecdotes... mostly about cults and terrorists.
