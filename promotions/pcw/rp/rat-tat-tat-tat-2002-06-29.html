Rat-tat-tat-tat!

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-29-02 Date: Friday, June 28th.Time: 8:28AM.Location: San Diego, California. (The scattered honking of horns brings us to the lovely scene of a traffic jam on a Southern California highway. We see a line of cars stretching for at least 3 miles back and a huge accident scene surrounded by emergency vehicles, which is obviously the source of the build-up. As we move back along the line approximately a quarter of a mile, we come across a convoy of black vehicles adorned with miniature sky blue and white flags, which are those of Argentina. In the convoy of at least 20 vehicles, we find one silver humvee that stands out. This particular humvee is situated in the center of the convoy and has a burly-looking man in fatigues at its gun turret. The gunner spins around constantly, looking for any troublemakers in the stopped traffic.) Gunner: 'Cómo?' (The gunner hears a voice emitting from the radio to his side and puts it up to his ear.) Gunner: 'Qué necesitas?' (The gunner glances to his left and sees an angry middle aged man honking profusely and yelling profanities at the mass of cars around him.) Gunner: 'Bueno.' (The gunner switches on a speaker and speaks to the driver in his thick South American accent.) Gunner: 'Meester! Plees cease jur honking and shat off jur car engeen!' (The driver looks at the gunner and flips him off. The gunner slides his sunglasses down and shakes his head.) Gunner: 'Dees is jur last warning, meester or I blow ju car!' (The driver flips him off once again and yells.) Driver: 'Hey, fuck you, buddy!' (The gunner flinches in offense.) Gunner: 'Eh!?' (The gunner then grins and speaks into his radio once last time.) Gunner: 'Estoy enganchendo!' (The gunner swings with his high calibred machine gun and points it at the man's car. The man simply lays on his horn and stares down the gunner, expecting a bluff. The gunner fires off a few hundred rounds of bullets into the tires and hood off the car. As the popping of bullets, metallic clangs of shells, and piercing pings of the bullets into the vehicle ring through the morning air, all the honking horns come to a halt. The horn of the honking car, however, is now stuck on an irritating, continuous honk) Gunner: 'Ay, mierda!' (The gunner briefly stops firing and the hostile driver retreats from his mangled, bullet-riddled car, screaming and running towards the emergency vehicles ahead. The gunner declines to fire upon the retreating driver and instead removes a grenade from his camouflage jacket and tosses it into the open window of the honking car.) Gunner: 'Ju blow now!' (The gunner yells something to the occupants of the hummer and slides back inside just as the vehicle explodes, sending pieces of glass and metal raining down on the area.) KA-BOOM~!!! (After the violent explosion, the gunner pops back up to his turret and smiles happily at the car in flames, the annoying driver yelling at him from afar, and dozens of other cars trying desperately to get out of the range of the crazed Argentinean militant. Even more people simply get out of their vehicles and run as fast as they can from the scene.) Gunner: 'Hey! Whar ju guys goeeng? Don' ju wanna blay wit me!?' (The gunner sighs and slides a cigarette in his mouth. Meanwhile, half a dozen commuters in the vicinity run past the humvee. The ) Commuter #1: 'You fucking maniac!' Commuter #2: 'I'm calling the cops!' Commuter #3: 'Don't shoot! Don't shoot!' (The gunner playfully points his gun at each of the passerbys as he cackles.) Gunner: 'Aw, come on! Don't be dat way! I only want to blow jur car...not hurt ju!' Commuter #4: 'Police! Police!' (The gunner rolls his eyes and spits at the running commuters.) Gunner: 'I yust tryeeng to do my yob! Jur hurting my feeleens!' (The gunner takes a drag from his cigarette and shakes his head.) Gunner: 'Pinche gringos. Todos son jotos!' (The fascist militia member shrugs helplessly and receives a fire extinguisher from down below. He sprays the flaming car for a few minutes until the fire is out, revealing the sizzling, blackened shell riddled with huge bullet holes. The gunner looks around one last time at the traffic resuming up ahead and bangs on the roof. The humvee passes the deserted vehicles up ahead and finally continues to head down the highway.) (We are now brought into the vehicle, where Frisco and two of his officials sift through documents in the backseats. Frisco wears his typical jet black FRISCACEMANI~! suit with a vodka martini in one hand and a cell phone in the other. He briefly speaks to his men before putting the cell phone up to his ear.) Frisco: 'Make sure our gunner gets promoted. He's been nothing but valuable to us during these last few turbulent months.' (A large burly man with glasses, known as Frisco's Secretary of Militia Advancement and Groupie Gynecological Screening, nods at the dictator.) Secretary of MAGGS: 'Presidente, he has cost us $32,000 in damages and fines. Excuse my retarded pun, but the man is a loose cannon.' (The gunner can be heard yelling something in Spanish and shooting from up top.) Frisco: 'Maybe so, but what are his stats?' Secretary of MAGGS: '29 confirmed kills and 94 enemy vehicles destroyed.' (Frisco sips his martini and smiles warmly.) Frisco: 'There you have it.' Secretary of MAGGS: 'Sir, may I remind you that 7 of the 29 kills were cats, 3 were birds, 14 were road raging motorists, and 5 were mimes?' Frisco: 'No, you may not.' (The secretary sighs and slaps his papers.) Secretary of MAGGS: 'This doesn't concern you at all!?' Frisco: 'Well...actually, yes. I would like to see the dead mime count a little higher than that.' (Frisco shrugs.) Frisco: 'But I think he's doing a great jo--....' (A few bursts of machine gun fire is heard, followed by a huge explosion. Frisco looks out of the window only to see a church van on fire and a group of nuns fleeing. Frisco smirks slightly.) Frisco: 'He's doing a great job!' (The secretary sees what the previous target was and his jaw drops to the floor.) Secretary of MAGGS: 'My God!' (Frisco raises his glass to his lips, but stops immediately after his secretary's cries.) Frisco: 'What!? Something happen?' Secretary of MAGGS: 'A van full of nuns!? What the hell is wrong with that man!?' (Frisco relaxes and resumes drinking.) Frisco: 'Will you settle down! He's just doing his--...' (The humvee rattles again as the gunner sprays bullets at a line of orange construction barrels.) Frisco: 'Job.' (The secretary slaps his head.) Secretary of MAGGS: 'Jefe, I seriously think you should reconsider promoting this man. He needs to be disciplined and shown how to properly identify threatening targets.' (Frisco smirks.) Frisco: You're one to talk, Enriquez. You need to work on properly identifying acceptable vagina for me.' Secretary of MAGGS: 'Wha?' Frisco: 'The last groupie you scanned had crabs.' Secretary of MAGGS: 'I assure you she did not have such defects when I examined her.' Frisco: 'She also had a penis.' (The secretary stumbles over his words.) Secretary of MAGGS: 'That was...uh...oh...um...definitely not there when I...erm..scanned her.' Frisco: 'When you can start identifying the differences between penises and vaginas, I will allow you to identify who is detrimental to our party. Work on that and you will get your other duty back. Militia Advancement will be placed under the command of Palando here. Are you okay with that, Palando.' (Rey Palando, Frisco's Secretary of Pencils, Brazilian Affairs, and Goatee Trimming looks up and nervously searches his suit.) Secretary of PBAGT: 'Oh..I..uhh..sir...do you need a pencil?' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'No, fucktard, I'm asking you if you'd like to take over Militia Advancement for retard Enriquez over here. If you didn't hear earl--...' (The humvee rattles as the gunner opens fire yet again. This time at a Taco Bell sign.) Frisco: 'If you didn't hear earlier, Enriquez is questioning my order to promote our gunner. I argue that he is not informed enough to make these kinds of judgments, as he cannot tell the difference between a penis and a vagina.' (The former Secretary of Militia Advancement hangs his head.) Secretary of PBAGT: 'Oh...' Frisco: 'Do you know what a penis is?' Secretary of PBAGT: 'Yes, sir.' Frisco: 'Do you know what a vagina is?' Secretary of PBAGT: 'Yes, sir.' Frisco: 'What do I have?' Secretary of PBAGT: 'A penis, Jefe.' (Frisco stares Enriquez until he gets the answer he wants.) Secretary of PBAGT: 'A very big penis.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Good. Now, what can I stick my penis in? Another penis...or a vagina?' Secretary of PBAGT: 'Umm....a vagina?' (Frisco claps.) Frisco: 'Very good! See that, Enriquez? Not hard at all!' Secretary of GGS: 'Sir, I told you she didn't have male genitalia before.' Frisco: 'I don't care. You should've been more thorough. If you looked hard enough, I'm sure you would've found some sort of penile seed that had the capability of growing a penis. Obviously, this is what happened and I blame you.' Secretary of PBAGT: 'Presidente, does this mean I get to have his Groupie Gynecological Screening job now?' (Frisco cocks his hand back and slaps the ingrate.) Frisco: 'No! You goddamned ingrate. I offer you a perfectly good job in Militia Advancement, and you want the luxury of looking at my naked groupies. How dare you make such requests. For that disrespect, you will be the Secretary of Being Frisco's Human Footstool for the next 5 weeks.' (Palando hangs his head, but it jerks back up in shock when the gunner opens fire on a truck full of grapefruits. Pieces of grapefruit fly all over the road and a few manage to fall through the porthole and onto Frisco's lap. Frisco picks a piece up and begins to snack on it. He offers it to his secretaries.) Frisco: 'Quieres algunos?' (His secretaries decline and shake their heads.) Frisco: 'Alright, suit yourselves. Just don't say I never offered you anything out of the kindness of my heart...and of our gunner.' (Frisco bites into the misplaced grapefruit as the gunner is clearly heard yelling at the truck full of citrus fruit.) Gunner: ¡ODIO El POMELO! (The truck is fired upon once again, causing all of the grapefruit to explode or fall onto the street. Horns honk from all sides of the vehicle. Frisco shrugs and takes another bite.) Frisco: 'Atta boy! Get that evil grapefruit!' (Frisco remembers he was supposed to make a call and frowns at his party members.) Frisco: 'You dickheads. You made me forget what I was about to do with your wretched insubordination.' (The secretaries apologize profusely as Frisco dials his phone. Seconds later, Frisco speaks into it.) Frisco: 'Yes, this is Francisco Valerius, I called yesterday about one of your patients. I am in the area and I was hoping that I could come by and talk to him.' (Frisco takes a bite out of the grapefruit and nods.) Frisco: 'Yes, I am aware that he is deeply unstable and thinks he is Asklepios. This is the main reason I would like to visit him.' (Frisco wipes his mouth and tosses the rest of the grapefruit out of the window. This is followed by the gunner trying to shoot at it as it hits the ground.) Frisco: 'I feel I am experienced in working with the mentally ill. I'm a PCW wrestler. Half my co-workers ain't quite right in the head. One particular co-worker of mine is eerily like the patient you have.....No, as tempting as it is, I would not like to have him committed. I have to severely beat him tomorrow.' (Frisco shakes his head at the response.) Frisco: 'No! No! No! You don't understand. Although he may be mentally handicapped, he's still freakishly strong and smart enough to avoid getting killed or injured by my men. We're almost on an even field here. I'm just hoping that this Asklepios fella will give me some insight to further help me do my job.' 'Thank you very much. I look forward to meeting him. Goodbye.' (Frisco shuts off his cell phone and smiles.) Frisco: 'Gentlemen, I think we have finally found someone who can help us effectively get to Steele.' (We move outside of the humvee to see the vehicle traveling down the streets of San Diego. The gunner on top maniacally swings around his turret, pointing his gun at anything he deems a threat. The scene fades out as he fires at an ominous stop sign.)
