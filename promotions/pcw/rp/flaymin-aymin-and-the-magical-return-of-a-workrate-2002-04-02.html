Flaymin Aymin.... and the magical return of a workrate???

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/4/02 OOC: I’ve been super busy as of late which would explain my lack of role plays as of late.But alas!!I’ve got two weeks of school holidays so for at least the time being I’m going to shift the tractor out of reverse and into dri…err… Neutral.From there the tractor can either go forward, backwards or just simply run out of gas and lay dormant :) Which will it be? I don’t know so stop asking so many f’n questions!! [Scene opens in the TV room of Flaymin’s hotel suite.Dozens of clothing pieces and empty food boxes lay scattered all around the room. The walls are covered with beer stains and smeared pizza slices, leaving behind a trail of tomato paste and mozzarella cheese. Two couches have been turned over and pushed together to form sort of shelter. It is underneath this makeshift shelter we find Flaymin Aymin and the recently ‘dognapped’ Talking Taco Bell Chihuahua. With a remote control in his hand, Flaymin switches between channels on the Giant TV in front of him. With his free hand, he pours some beer into the dog’s bowl.The drunken dog yelps in joy and then quickly laps up the beer.Moments later, the dog lets out a strange bark and then begins to shake from side to side before it eventually tips over to its side in an unconscious state Flaymin looks at the dog and pokes at it with his fingers.The dog however lies there motionless, heaving heavily] Flaymin: Damn…. I think I just killed the Taco Bell dog. [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders and then turns back to the TV] Flaymin: Eh…. I’ll just go steal another one. [Flaymin then begins to rifle through a whole bunch of empty food wrappers until he finally pulls out a large block of chocolate. He rips open the packet and shoves the chocolate in his mouth.For the next minute or so he chomps rather vigorously on the chocolate but doesn’t seem to swallow any of it.Bit by bit he inserts more chocolate into his mouth until there is physically no space left to stash the chocolate. With his mouth still full of mushed chocolate, he reaches over and picks up a bottle of out-dated milk.He looks at the expired use-by-date and once again shrugs his shoulders. Next he removes the bottle cap from the milk and starts chugging down the milk.Spurts of milk dribble down his face and onto his clothes and the carpet below. Flaymin continues to pour the milk into his mouth until his cheeks are finally the size of a Pamela Anderson boob. Flaymin then begins to violently shake his head from side to side as quickly as he can. It is at this point that his pornstar assistant- Jenna Jameson walks into the room and shakes her head in disgust. Flaymin looks at her but continues to shake his head like a maniac] Jenna: What the hell are you doing??? [Suddenly Flaymin’s mouth bursts open and chunks of chocolate and milk splatter all over the room. Drenched in milk and chocolate pieces, Jenna slaps Flaymin across the face] Flaymin: Dammit it woman!!There goes my chunky chocolate milkshake!! Jenna: Look at this place, it’s a mess!!Who’s going to clean up this joint? [Once again, Flaymin pokes the passed out Taco Bell Dog, which is still heaving heavily] Flaymin: Well I don’t the dog can eat anymore of this stuff. [Jenna looks at the dog and shrieks] Jenna : What did you do to the dog!?!?!?! Flaymin: I dunno.One minute he’s slurping beer and chocolate and then the next minute… KAPPUT~!He’s out cold. Jenna: How could you let this happen?!?! Flaymin: I told him to consume his alcohol in moderation, but did he listen??Nooooooooo! Jenna: And why did you turn over the couches for?? Flaymin: This….. this is a fortress….. a fortress of solitude.No one goes in….. no one gets out. [Jenna walks over to the couches and turns them back over into their up-right positions] Flaymin: Hey what are you doing? Jenna: The fortress is crumbling….. and so is your career. [Flaymin looks at the drunken dog and raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: Did you know I had a career? [The passed out dog lies still] Flaymin: Neither did I. Jenna: Don’t be stupid.You’re throwing away everything you have. Did you know that you’ve only wrestled twice in the past month? Flaymin: Twice?Damn… I need to cut back my efforts a little. Jenna: And El Diablo is kicking your ass on every show. Lets face it, your career has gone to the shitters. Flaymin: Who are the shitters? Jenna: You know what I mean. Flaymin: Is there a ‘Shitter’ family reunion that I haven’t been consulted about? Jenna: UGH!You’ve got a serious attitude problem, do you know that?? [Flaymin suddenly begins to yell in a high-pitched squeal] Flaymin: Oh my god!!A wrestler with an attitude problem!!Somebody alert the press!! Jenna : See what I mean? You’ve gone from being THE TV Champ to sitting in front of a TV spitting chocolate milk everywhere. Flaymin: Chunky Chocolate Milkshake.Get it right. Jenna: And what about your beer brewery?Huh? What happened to the beer company you were going to launch? Flaymin: Well the thing is…. I forget. Jenna: And what about me? You don’t even pleasure me anymore. [Flaymin picks up a nearby empty beer bottle and tosses it to Jenna] Flaymin: Here’s a dildo- go crazy babe. [Jenna looks at the beer bottle and then throws it back at Flaymin.Flaymin raises his hands to protect his face as the beer bottle smashes into the wall behind him] Flaymin: You’re going to have to clean that up.The dog doesn’t eat glass, you know. Jenna: You’ve dropped into the oblivion of jobber-land.And I don’t want to associate myself with losers. So I now present you with an ultimatum. [Flaymin stares at Jenna with a genuinely surprised look on his face] Flaymin: Oblivion?Associate?Ultimatum? Have you been reading the dictionary again? [Jenna ignores Flaymin and continues with her ultimatum] Jenna: Either you put your career back together….. Or….. We’re finished. Flaymin: You don’t mean that. [Jenna sadly nods her head. Having stopped acting like a drunken fool, Flaymin sits quietly and contemplates his future with one of the hottest pornstars ever to grace the XXX ‘movie’ genre] Jenna: So what’s it going to be? [Flaymin looks deep into Jenna’s eyes as………… the scene fades to black] Are PCW’s Premiere Porno couple breaking up?Can Flaymin resurrect his career? And most importantly, will the Taco Bell Dog make it through the night!?!?!?!?!?!?!?! Tune in next time to find out!!
