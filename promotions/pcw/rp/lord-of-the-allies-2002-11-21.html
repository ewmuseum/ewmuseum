Lord of the Allies

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 21, 2002 The air is calm and empty, but with an overwhelming sense of disturbance, like a good dream about to go bad. Trees rustle their silent disapproval at the unheeding forms dotting the beach like modern refuge, swaying back and forth in a hopeless dance of warding, and a curious monkey peers down from a small mountain of rock off to the left side. The island is definitely in disarray; free from direction and civilization, without lead. A gull cries overhead, screeching her scandal at the unwanted encroachment, but none of the water-logged trespassers twitch even an inch. Except one. Cool and refreshing consciousness consumes him, as if for one startling moment someone is pumping his veins full of ice water, and his left arm tries to rise feebly. Hair sharing its golden colour nestles into the sand, trying to gain the man some leverage with which to move. A sudden fit of coughing overcomes him, and salty seawater spews all over the beach, a little parting gift from the great and malevolent ocean at his back, now lapping serenely against the shore. His left hand suddenly leaps forward, clutching to the sand as if to keep from falling, then his whole body lunges and his right hand lands a little further. The man starts panting slightly from the undue strain this places on his ravaged body, but still he goes on, one heavy arm at a time, until he finally reaches rock. Able to get a better grip, he moves faster now, gaining confidence with his momentum. He crawls blindly up his personal Everest, wanting desperately to be the king of the mountain. &#039;Yes,&#039; he whispers madly, his long hair falling down in front of his face as he crawls. &#039;All mine... All m-&#039; the rant cuts off suddenly, mercifully being interrupted before it could really begin. He tries to raise his head for a look at the soft substance that his hand just encountered instead of chalky rock, but it won&#039;t cooperate. Intrigued, he begins probing around, squeezing and poking into this delightful softness for all he&#039;s worth. &#039;Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiieeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!&#039; The high-pitched screech shatters the morning&#039;s quaint silence, and a murder of crows take up a sympathetic call as they tear away from their roosts, startled and angry. Heads all over the beach begin to rise and look around in dazed confusion, then spew forth that little part of the sea trying to join them. Ears ringing like a malicious telephone, Xamine Herr Kanyon slowly lifts his head enough to stare down at the shapely crotch of Bendhero&#039;er Y&#039;all, cupped shakily in his hand. He chuckles at his wide-eyed wife and gives another squeeze, this time easy and reassuring. &#039;Nice to see you, too, my little fuck ferret,&#039; Kanyon snickers, then seems appalled at the raspiness of his voice. &#039;Like - hello?!&#039; Y&#039;all screeches indignantly, slapping at his hand. &#039;Can&#039;t you see that I&#039;m, like, &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; trying to get a tan so I&#039;m not &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; pasty like you? I mean, gawd... Look at you,&#039; She points at Kanyon&#039;s bare white chest, exposed since the sea stole his shirt, and snickers. She only has one word to sum up the situation of his skin, &#039;Like... Ewzzies.&#039; Rolling his eyes, Kanyon dismisses her with a, &#039;fuck off, Bellie~! You&#039;re starting to sound like Byron, for shit&#039;s sake.&#039; He turns around before he can see her shocked expression, so she sticks her tongue out at the back of his head. He is surprised to see most of the people that were lying on the beach are now up and gathered before the rock on which he stands, staring up at him expectantly. &#039;Tell Bellie~! to shut up, Kanyon!&#039; Tyger groans from the middle of the crowd, shaking his head in total weariness. &#039;It&#039;s just air. If she can remember to keep breathing, she&#039;ll find it&#039;s nothing to be scared of.&#039; A few chuckles spread throughout those gathered, all of which Bellie~! haughtily ignores as she tries to continue her tanning. &#039;Tell Bellie~! to put out!&#039; An anonymous voice calls, and more fun is had by all. &#039;Oh my gawd,&#039; Bellie~! huffs, rising to her feet. &#039;Like, listen up, peons,&#039; she calls loudly and obnoxiously, addressing everyone without looking at a single one. &#039;I would so sooner rape myself in my own poopshoot with a, like, porcupine, before putting anything on you.&#039; &#039;Yeah right,&#039; another voice calls, &#039;only thing you put out is STDs!&#039; Bellie~! seems on the verge of climbing down from the rocks to throttle every last one of them, but Kanyon restrains her with a few quiet words and a strong arm. She protests briefly, but eventually stops fighting and stands at his side. He turns to face the assembly. &#039;Obviously, we&#039;re going to have to establish some kind of order around here,&#039; Kanyon drones condescendingly, fixing his hands on his hips. &#039;We don&#039;t know where we are or how long it&#039;ll be until we&#039;re found... We don&#039;t even know if this is an island or the mainland. Do we even know how many survivors there are? Somebody find out!&#039; There is a moment of murmuring and shuffling, then a volunteer steps forward, a pair of shades somehow still on his face after last night. &#039;I just skimmed the crowd here, and it looks like we&#039;re about thirty.&#039; Everyone stops milling and looks up at Kanyon expectantly. &#039;What the hell do you mean you skimmed?&#039; Kanyon roars. &#039;You can&#039;t learn anything by skimming, Gayman! This is important - don&#039;t treat it like your career!&#039; Flaymin&#039; Gayman flips Kanyon the bird and turns his back, apparently intent on talking with the other crackas standing around him. The trend catches on and within seconds the whole group is abuzz with conversation, none of it counting. &#039;Someone count! We need to know how many we are! Damn you! Peons!&#039; Herr Kanyon seethes and seeks another means of berating them, but a redneck-looking individual jumps up onto the rock beside him, his mouth twisted in a smile that sets out a whole new definition for goofiness. &#039;Exactly thirty-two bodies, including Bellie~!,&#039; The Virgin Byron Calver reports proudly, then continues. &#039;We have a combined weight of roughly eight thousand pounds, and our demographics break up in the following patterns: of hair colour, one has purple, three hav-&#039; &#039;Would someone &lt;i&gt;please&lt;/i&gt; put him out of his misery?&#039; a masked mexican man demands in a thick accent, and there is a muffled gasp as Cruel Devil obliges him by whipping a still-dripping gun from his pocket. He points it at Byron and cocks the hammer, but the speaker quickly shouts in protest. &#039;I meant with a hooker! A hooker!&#039; Disappointed, Devil lowers the gun and shrugs. &#039;Can&#039;t help ya there... You&#039;ll have to talk to Kanyon... He&#039;s the only one who thought to bring one along.&#039; &#039;Unless, of course,&#039; Tyger pipes up at his side, his grin mirroring the mischief in his voice, &#039;you count Boris... He brought his purple-haired bimbo along, didn&#039;t he? Spittle?&#039; &#039;Spittle?&#039; Funktacular pipes in, quirking a little smile. &#039;Think she swallowles?&#039; Cruel and Tyger are taken aback for a moment, considering the source, then double over with laughter. A few others share in the amusement, but cut off suddenly when they hear the familiar rumble. The very ground shakes as the mighty OSW giant takes the first few steps of his violent response. The three jokers pretend not to notice. Timid Bologna takes a step away from his FSW teammates, shaking his head ruefully in the knowledge of where this must lead. Wrestlers scramble out of the way as Boris barrels forward, a humourless smile shining out through his beard and warning of catastrophe to come. At his side is Spittle, purple head held high and proud, her demeanour one of imminent destruction. &#039;Stop! Wait!&#039; Kanyon cries from his pedestal. &#039;If PCW has taught me anything, it&#039;s that we must have order if we&#039;re to survive!&#039; The crowd ignores him, more intent on the impending battle. &#039;We need to elect a leader! Someone to make the rules by which we all abide! Otherwise there will be more anarchy like this!&#039; He points an accusing finger at the melee about to erupt, and the people stop in surprise. &#039;Dah,&#039; Boris calls out and comes to a stop, placing a comforting hand on Spittle&#039;s shoulder as he shoots a threatening gaze at Team FSW. &#039;Perhaps you are right, Xaminer... But who would be leader? You?&#039; He pauses to let the absurdity of this sink in, then hammers it home with a mighty &#039;DYAHAHAHAHAHA!&#039; &#039;I don&#039;t see anyone better suited for the role,&#039; Her Kanyon rebukes, deadly serious. &#039;You may not agree with everything I do, but I have the good of the whole forever at the forefront of my mind.&#039; Bellie~! and Byron nod at his side, and there is murmuring among the crowd. &#039;You only see da good for yourself!&#039; Boris shouts, inciting those around him, then raises a quieting hand. &#039;If we are going to elect, elect me! I do not cheat and lie, like Herr Kanyon, and we will all work together for a piece of da pie!&#039; &#039;Get over yourself, Boris,&#039; Kanyon chuckles, shaking his head. &#039;These men are competitors... They&#039;re not going to go for your communist bullshit. Under my rule, we&#039;ll give you what you work for and deserve, and nothing more.&#039; &#039;Und we can become great friendly success like PCW, dah?&#039; Boris bellows a laugh to show what he thinks of that, then speaks to those around him in a calm and beseeching voice. &#039;We do not want your corruption, Xaminer!&#039; &#039;All of this is bullshit,&#039; Devil imposes himself into the battle, spinning around where he stands to look at all those around him. &#039;Elect me leader and the first thing we&#039;ll do is abolish all this talk of being governed... Do we really need the likes of Xavier and Boris lording over us? Fuck that... Every man for himself. Let power sort it out.&#039; &#039;Dah, right,&#039; Boris says with a confused look on his face. &#039;But who are you? Kanyon is PCW CEO... I am Russian Bear and OSW Champion... Everyone knows we are leaders... But you - you are...?&#039; &#039;I&#039;m &lt;i&gt;your&lt;/i&gt; Universal Champion, sasquatch,&#039; Devil replies, arrogantly arching his eyebrow at the big man. &#039;Universal what?&#039; Kanyon taunts with a laugh. &#039;Who&#039;d you win it from? Your title means nothing if anyone can take it away from you... And we could... So shut up.&#039; &#039;Yeah... Like, shut up, Universal Peon,&#039; Bellie~! chimes in, earning herself a smile from Xaminer. &#039;So come and take it, then, Kanyon,&#039; Devil challenges, taking a menacing step forward. &#039;I&#039;ll gladly show you how a champion deals with your type of bravado, and then I&#039;ll show your little cum receptacle there the same.&#039; Bellie~! opens her mouth to respond, but Kanyon cuts her off. &#039;Enough. Like I care if you want in on the voting... It won&#039;t matter in the end. Let&#039;s get on with it... The three candidates are m-&#039; &#039;Wait!&#039; A squeaky voice comes up through the crowd, interrupting Xaminer. A masked man sporting a 1090 hair-doo emerges from a gaggle of nobodies, arms crossed over his chest in defiance. &#039;Why must you always forget about RUSH? Our product and talent is much superior to yours, so stop pretending otherwise!&#039; Everyone seems taken aback by the rant, but the man continues nonetheless. &#039;I am The Marked Muppet, and I demand that you elect me as your lead-&#039; There is a sickening crack as Tyger Smells&#039;Em nonchalantly snaps the RUSH superstar&#039;s neck. The muppet crumples to the ground in a heap of dirty hair, and an uproarious applause breaks out for Tyger. He extends his arms out to the sides, knowingly, and shakes his head at the fallen man. &#039;Right,&#039; Xaminer says, clearing his throat, &#039;now that that little unpleasantness is over, let&#039;s get on with the voting...&#039; He shouts with great gusto, then adds as an aside, &#039;all you PCW guys remember who cuts your cheques.&#039; There is some disgruntled mumbling from the gathered, but nothing serious. &#039;Who votes for that, uh, Universal peon whose name I forget?&#039; Kanyon suddenly demands of the crowd, raising his arm high above his head to show them how to vote. Devil watches in humbled shock as only those from FSW raise their hands, and two of them grudgingly. Xaminer pauses for a moment, making sure there&#039;s no stragglers. &#039;Three votes,&#039; he smirks, lowering his hand and looking to The Virgin Byron Calver to make sure it&#039;s being recorded. &#039;Now how about Boris?&#039; Xaminer calls, and smiles in satisfaction as only the hands of OSW and remaining RUSH wrestlers reach for the sky. The smirk spreads into a rapacious grin. &#039;And five for Boris... Which means...&#039; &#039;Yay!&#039; Bellie~! gushes her approval. &#039;How many vote for me, Xamine Herr Kanyon?&#039; All of the PCW hands appear above all of the PCW heads, and Kanyon nods smugly, a greedy and power-abusing grin spreading over his face. A foul wind breathes in from the north, carrying with it the stench of depravity and disease, but no one pays it any mind. Somewhere in the woods an animal roars, in approval or disapproval remains a mystery... One thing is for certain, though. The island has a direction. The wrestlers have made their choice, and now they must try to live with the aftermath. A starving hyena cackles in the distance, enjoying some cryptic joke, and in all honesty, a more fitting sound wouldn&#039;t come along if you waited for eternity and a day.
