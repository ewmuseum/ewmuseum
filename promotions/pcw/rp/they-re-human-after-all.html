They're Human...after all...

undated

Date: not entered They’re already weaker… They’re human after all… That makes them flawed…no matter how much they think they have accomplished or will accomplish. They’re already weaker… It takes two of them to make up a team…it takes two of them to complete one task that one could perform if one applied oneself. It takes two of them…so that they can both be lazy and their two half-assed jobs can equal one decent job done…so they think. If their flesh weren’t weak…they would be able to push themselves and maybe…just maybe one day…they could hope to do what the Neo Void God is about to do come this Sabbath. Eighteen men…making nine teams apiece…and they are all fighting against one… One…GOD… What hope lies for them…lies still…stiff with death…frail, brittle, and not to be disturbed, lest all hope dies away in a cloud of dust. Eighteen mortals…against one god… Eighteen men looking towards three main things to conqueror… Three Gs… “Gold” …The PCW Tag Team Titles… “Glory” …Being Champions… “GOD” …Defeating the Neo Void God before he destroys their destiny of becoming champions…absolutely… Eighteen men that will find their heads cracked against the concrete as they plummet from the ring… Eighteen men of all different walks of society all banding together to put a stop to the one unstoppable force that has been pulverizing one entire division since his return a few weeks ago… “The Messiahs” Men who call themselves saviors…when they save nothing…not even their sorry hides. There’s no salvation from the one called Akiyama on this Sabbath. Can you save yourselves from him? Will you be able to ward off his attacks…will you be able to stop him from throwing you out of competition…out of his way as he moves ahead into his own destiny? Do you wish to save PCW from Akiyama? Do you wish to save the universe from the Neo Void God? Then your efforts are as futile as your efforts to win this match… No one is safe… Best thing you can do to keep the god from harming you…is to jump…commit match…suicide… “The Suicidal” They who consider themselves crazy…insane…risking their bodies and lives in order to gain what they want. There’s another name they call this brand of people… Dumbasses… They’re fools for risking everything in the face of the impossible…because it only gets themselves hurt or dead… Suicide is only good for one thing…and one thing only…and that is escaping the brutality that Akiyama will place upon you. Even with the taste of defeat being given to them so early before the true battle, they foolishly rush in to face the nVg again. Even monkeys know not to touch a hot stove after being burned…shows how far humanity’s set back on the scale of existence. But let them persist if they wish…if it’s death they wanted so bad…the god will be more than willing to grant their prayers…all they have to be is in the wrong place at the wrong time… …otherwise…they have to be… “The Misplaced” The lost souls…those without direction…in life and in this match. These mortals with no place to go…no direction as to what their purpose is. All they know is that they must win…or be doomed to be misplaced in this federation longer. Already their presence has been misplaced by the competition…has anyone even noticed them as a threat? No? It’s because they’re not a threat, if you’re not paying attention. Men without direction are said to be reckless…without cause or remorse… But still…they are men… It will be their own recklessness that leads to their downfall as Akiyama drops them over the top rope…there’s nowhere for them to run…nowhere for them to escape the wrath of one god upon an entire ranking of men. But…after this is all over…at least they will have one direction in their lives…it will keep them from being misplaced ever again… they will forever know when they see the nVg… …To run in the other direction and never look back… “The Pink” Pink…it’s the color of bruised skin… It’s the color of the flesh of the enemy as they feel the blood rush to their epidermis…internal bleeding’s a b!tch, isn’t it? Men who consider themselves the breakers of hearts find that their own hearts will shatter just as quickly in the face of adversity…then all their ambitions and dreams will leak out like jelly…and stain the floor with its pink tinge. It’s a color…nothing more… …Nothing more…like the ones who sing a color’s praises… …Nothing more… …That’s what will be left of them after this Sabbath… when they’re lying on the outside in pain, their flesh resonating a pink…hue…fuming with pain from their frail pink bodies. That…and nothing more… “The Cool” Are they really… “cool”? What makes them cool? Their style? Their attitude? Their lifestyle of attempting to be the desire of every pre-pubescent girl and gay teen across America…has pedophilia really sunk down to such a low as this? If it’s not for the fresh snatch…then what is the whole deal with their coolness? Money…popularity…fame…fortune… It’s their battle plan for success…why make it to the top on skill alone when you can flaunt around and dance and reach the big time just as quickly? Nonsense… Calling themselves cool…and trying to make music…it’s only a diversionary tactic…to drive you away from the fact that they are indeed without skill and talent within combat… But if you like to listen to their music…listen to their next big “flop”…Screams, Bells, and Defeat Screams as they are tossed out of competition… Bells ringing, rejoicing the victory of the god… Defeat…of everything that they and the others have stood for… The sales…just like their Tag careers…WILL plummet… “The Gnarly” Talk about your major wipeouts…even beach blanket bums are trying to halt the god’s destruction… They’re just another sector of the dissection of the division…nothing important about them…they’ll survive after the pain is over…after they’ve learned to deal with defeat. Surfers are losers anyway… “The Terrorists” They who strike fear into the hearts of America? What are they thinking…they think everything’s about nationalities and races…when the war is a lot larger than that. It’s not about country versus country…not about man versus man… It’s about men…versus god… …Humanity versus the superior being… And here they sit and fight amongst themselves…while the god merely sets out to destroy them all…it only makes HIS job easier. Fighting for religion…for politics… The real fight…is for your world… …It’s all about to end…come this Sunday… the entire Tag world collapsing at the feet of the nVg. And it’s is Apocalypse that strikes terror into the terrorists…because we shall see how strong they are willing to fight for their beliefs when it comes down to the line…when they stand face to face with the Neo Void God…utter their own flawed god’s name in awe…and pray to whatever god is listening for mercy and grace from the pain about to be delivered upon them. “The Politicians” It’s all about three things with these type of men… Money…power…respect… Raping their own species for monetary gain…calling them “taxes”… Taking power from their own kind, manipulating the masses to instill some sort of control over the civilization… acting as a leader to them…showing them the way they should live…when you’re only showing them the way to hell… Respect… In their own petty insignificant way…they’re trying to make themselves gods among men… It’s only a matter of time before those scandals get out…those votes begin to drop…and the people call you a government raping swine to your face… Then…your little haven of twisted laws and bills instilling “justice” will all fall…just as they will by the god’s hands… Because there is no stricter justice…then the Universal Reverb… …It strikes back a thousand fold more worse than the crimes against your own kind have been… No wonder humanity exists in corruption…it’s filled with villains like politicians and terrorists… But where there are villains…there are… “The Heroes” Yes…heroes…men named as such by whom? The people… Why is that? Because…they do what the people want them to do… Slaves are a better term for them… Puppets for the public… only doing what the people consider heroic… with the declining value of morals in this society, it wont be long that heroics will be classified as villainous acts…while villainy becomes as heroic and American as Duke Williams and Fried Chicken. Being liked…being popular…being in the sparkle of humanity’s eye…that’s not being a hero… Being a hero is doing what needs to be done no matter what happens or no matter who goes against it…sucking up to the masses is no means for heroism… But if these people want to be heroes so much…show up at Gold and Glory… Akiyama will see how far you can fly from the ring… =©= All men of shapes, sizes, and backgrounds…all attempting to topple god from his position over them… It will just not happen…not at all… No number of men can stop he who is above men… No matter how strong their will is…it will be broken… No matter how many troops you send against HIM…the numbers will diminish into nothing… Because… there’s no stopping the unstoppable… There’s only ONE God of PCW… And June 30th… HE brings Apocalypse upon the world of tag teams… They cannot prevent it…all they can do is be a part of the ruin…exist within the remains of the suffering left… They are after all… …human… =©=
