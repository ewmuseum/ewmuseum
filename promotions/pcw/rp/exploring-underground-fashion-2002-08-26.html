Exploring 'Underground' Fashion

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/08/02 “Wow... Like, cool shoes!” Our shot opens up on the wide pale Blue eyes of PCW’s Bambi-esque Commissioner Eleanor Hall as they stare longingly through a pristinely clean window at a pair of Purple strappy shoes. Eleanor : “Ellie~!.... Must.... Have!” Looking at the frontage of the store there is a staircase that goes down below street level and one that is tucked away on the corner... The latter obviously leading into the shop to all but the little prep. Eleanor : “Well... I’ve never heard of this place before so it must be one of those ‘Underground’ stores!” The chirpy little wife of PCW’s Champion looks from the “Holy Grail” of Purple strappy shoes to the Grey concrete steps and Black rail that leads down below street level. Eleanor : “So it must be, like, underground!” Before the lightbulb can appear above her head and fuse out in comedic fashion she makes her way down the un-even steps.. Descending them very carefully in her already substantial heels. Eleanor : “So ‘Underground’ isn’t just a clever name... Like Ellie~!” She gets to the bottom of the shady stairwell and waits for a few seconds, checking the time on the chunky watch that stands out against her small wrists that are adorned in all sorts of bands and bracelets. Eleanor : “Wow, they are like, SO slow opening the door for you off the main streets!” Longer and longer she waits by the big Black door before finally going to open it herself. Eleanor : “It must be one of those ‘Independent Women’s’ shops....” With a loud creak the door open and her vacuous eyes peer around the corner. *CREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAK!!!!!!* Eleanor : “... Although the clothes didn’t look like Lesbian Truckdriver fashion!” Barely being able to open the intimidating and heavy door much further than the width of her waif-like body, the prep slides through the gap and looks around... The entrance dark and shadowed. Eleanor : “I suppose no lights cuts down on, like, overheads.” Further and further she walks down the hall that is carpeted in deep Red before getting to another heavy Black door and stopping, putting her hands on her hips in a defeatist manner. Eleanor : “Are there.. Like.. NO door people here?” Again she pushes the heavy door open, only this time rapturous cheers are heard over the creaking of the door. “YEEEEEEEEEAAAHHHHH!!! YEAAAAAHHHHHH!!!!!!!” Again sliding through the narrow gap she looks out in awe at what is before her. Eleanor : “Like.... HOW cool?!?” Around a shallow concrete pit stand a small crowd of smartly dressed businessmen and a few more shabbily dressed men with large bundles of cash in their hands. They all cheer and wave their fists, directed down into the pit where we see 2 topless muscular men duking it our with bare fists. Eleanor : “Maybe this is, like, that OSW I’ve heard about!” In her usual oblivious yet infinitely curious way, the prep wanders further into the “Arena” so to speak, her choice of dress... A smart White Gucci skirt suit.. Helping her blend in at least a little. “COME ON!!! I GOT A THOUSAND BUCK ON YOUR ASS!!!” “STAY UP.. ..STAY UP!!!” “CHOKE HIM OUT... COME ON!!! CHOKE HIM OUT!!!” She tries to squirm through the small gaps in the crowd to get to the front, covering her ears against some of the louder shouts of encouragement. Eleanor : “Okay... You want him to win.. We GET IT!” As she looks through the gradually thinner group of bodies before her one of the men catches the other off balance and floors him with a right cross to the chin... The recipient dropping down below the wall and out of view.. Shortly followed by the deliverer. “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!” After a few seconds and a rousing cheer from the majority of the fight’s supporters the man who scored the punch rises up and holds his bloodied fist aloft while 2 men jump down into the pit and help out his opponent who’s right arm is hanging limp. Eleanor : “Injured limbs.. Like, SO rule!” “YOUR WINNER... AND STILL YOUR KING OF THE PIT... DAVE “WHIPLASH” SAVAGE!!!” The victor is pulled from the pit by the well dressed spectators who are pleased with his victory and even more so with the new, crisp bundle of notes in their hands. “IS THERE ANYONE HERE BRAVE ENOUGH TO TAKE THIS MAN ON IN OUR OWN CONCRETE JUNGLES?!?!” The gaze of the crowd flickers between the other fighting prospects who have attended the event, but all of whom are watching the so far unsuccessful attempts to put the losers arm back on it’s joint. “I think the tendon has gone.” Unsurprisingly none of them are very forthcoming after seeing the arm of the last opponent hanging limp by skill and weakened muscle alone. But there may yet be a challenger. Eleanor : “Hmmm... I DO have one of those mach things on Friday...” Yes. In the preppy little mind of Eleanor Hall the idea of getting into an unregulated brawl with a shoot fighter somehow appeals more than simply going to the gym.. Even if she can’t get the showers in the locker room to the perfect temperature. Eleanor : “Um... Like... ME!” She raises her hand and bounces up on the spot to get noticed.... The fight’s organisers and followers showing a look of total and utter bemusement at the little giggly woman wanting to shoot fight. Eleanor : “Hi!” The pause is then broken by a frenzy of activity. “I GOT 500-1 ON THE GIRL!!!” “I’LL GIVE YOU 750-1!!!” “I WILL DOUBLE THEIR ODDS!!!” The prep stands by the edge of the pit, looking down. Eleanor : “Are there, like, stairs?” One of the spectators drops down into the pit and lifts her down. While “Whiplash” jumps down into the pit and cracks his neck before punching the walls to warm up, Ellie~! takes out two hair ties and pulls her hair back into pig tails. “FIGHT!” As Dave licks the blood from his bleeding knuckles and adopts his shootfighting stance, Ellie~! slowly pulls her right hand up, palm open and ready to launch the Prep Slap O’Doom~! “C’MON MISSY!!!” She shakes her head at the labelling and approaches “Whiplash” who’s face looks confused as he whispers to her, his words drowned out by the cheering of the crowd.. Hoping that the times Chun-Li won in Sreetfighter II can be repeated here... Obviously not knowing Eleanor. Whiplash : “You serious girlie?” Suddenly starting to think about her method of training’s validity, Ellie~! looks less confident. Almost as if she has just been dropped into a pit with a man who can fuck up the arm of people 3 times her size in a no rules underground shootfighting bout. Eleanor : “Er....” *GULP* The mob around the pit shout louder and louder, intimidating the prep even more. Eleanor : “Um... I’ll give you $500,000 if you let me win!” He shrugs. Whiplash : “Fair enough!” He jolts forward at her, causing her to screams and cover her head, looking away as he whispers to her. Whiplash : “You’re meant to hit me!” Eleanor : “Oh... Okay!” With all the power of something not that powerful at all. In fact, not powerful enough to warrant a description of it’s power with the word “Powerful” in.. Ellie~! brings her right palm down and across the cheek of the “King of the Pit” that sends him dropping limp to the floor. Eleanor : “I WIN!!! YAY ME!!“ Despite the furious betting from them beforehand, the crowd don’t like it one bit an start to boo while one of the men who bet on her helps Ellie~! out of the pit.. A massive bundle of notes in his breast pocket. “YOU WON ME $3,500!!!” Spit is now flying down into the pit where Dave Savage is now getting up, enduring it all for the hefty amount he just won himself. “YOUR WINNER.... AND NEW KI-.. QUEEN OF HE PIT..... THAT WOMAN!!!” As some members of the spectators celebrate, one of the organisers hands Ellie~! what appears to be her winnings.. Not looking too pleased as he does so. “There’s your money.. Now you’d better get outa here quick Girlie.” The prep looks at the money, seemingly not that impressed with the reward. Eleanor : “Could I get those Purple strappy heels in the window instead?” Fade to Black on bemused face.
