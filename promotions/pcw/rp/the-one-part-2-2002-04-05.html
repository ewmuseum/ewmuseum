The One Part 2

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 5th, 2002 Pawn me off like your old black and white television. Just put me away behind the bowling ball in the cupboard. Hide me ever so carefully so you may never retrieve me again. Abandon me like everyone else, be a quitter just like the world. The world quit on me and now so have you. YOU! You have to have things your way. It always has to go the way of 'The One'. Ha. You make me laugh with your desperate needs. One thing that does not go your way sets you off. You act like the world is watching and you can not make mistakes. Was I mistake? WAS I A F'ING MISTAKE? You try to tend to me needs. It took you 24 years for you to notice. What a time it was for you to notice as well. You come back to me now. Now after 24 years. Now after I have a job which puts my infront of you every week. Do you think watching me on television is the same as visiting? DO YOU THINK IT ERASES THE PAIN? Shudder. You dare shudder? How can you shudder in my presence after what you did. You are responsible for your shuddering. Not I. I have had to grow up alone. I raised myself. Now you want to make up for you’re years of neglect? You want to just erase the past. You think you are a Chrono Ranger? YOU JUST THINK YOU CAN TURN BACK THE CLOCK? Again you shudder. Do you not like me screaming? After my years of silence now you shudder at my screams. Years of silence followed by screams. Why are you retreating? I thought you wanted to turn back the time. I THOUGHT YOU WANTED TO BE WITH ME! You know. You come looking for my forgiveness and now this is what you get. Do you think this is fair? No? Well guess what. 24 years of neglect is equal to several minutes of my screaming. HOW DO YOU LIKE IT? DAMN IT HOW DOES IT FEEL? I HAVE FELT THIS FOR 24 F'ING YEARS YOU DEADBEAT! So you think you deserve this? You think that this will make everything better? Now you just walk back into my life like nothing has happened. You think this makes us even? Well I have news for you. Nothing, NOTHING WILL EVER MAKE US EVEN! SHUDDER? YOU SHUDDER AGAIN? AGAIN IN MY PRESENCE? GET OUT! STOP TRYING TO MAKE US EVEN DAMN IT! WE WILL NEVER, EVER BE EVEN! I will watch you rot in hell for all I care before I ever hug you. Damn it man, 24 years without a hug. Now you want one? F'you.
