Don't Chat Back

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd March 2003 The night sky looms over an energy driven city like a curtain of black, whilst the bustling bodies of people below scurry about like ants. Illumination comes from the buildings that litter either side of the sidewalk and passing vehicles that follow suit. Even though the darkness has seeped in people go about their daily shenanigans and secret lives, for the night brings out the worse in people. Such a stereotype is to say that the night draws out the cretins of humanity like a halogen bulb to a moth but in most cases this is to be true. Drunks usually stagger about fighting gravity or themselves, muggers use the camouflage of the night to prey on the helpless and generally the awe of the city is that of unease. One building of interest, a television studio appears under siege by two distinctive individuals, “Really must we add breaking and entering to our list of accolades?” hissed the behemoth as he threw aside either side of his trench coat in annoyance. His speckle green associate saw to it that we didn’t need an identity parade to reveal these two illustrious names. Two names that were gripping the headlines and for all the wrong reasons, the most uncanny combination of misfits ever to grace a wrestling ring, be it Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A). “Where’s your sense of adventure!” jovially remarked the Emerald Menace. His free spirit however held absolutely no stroke over the aggravated goliath as his hands morphed from that of a human to something that would struggle at customs for now the vampire wielded a pair of razor sharp talons. With a twitch here and a hand movement there, the door mechanism clicked signaling their entry. “Open Sesame…” The glass-paneled doors swung ajar confirming entry and a polished path lay ahead like the infamous ‘Yellow Brick Road’. Stepping into the unknown the devious duo walked through the winding pathway of corridors whilst being the midst of an eerie awe, courtesy of the dimly lit conditions of the phrase – After Hours. Cautiously strolling around a blind corner both Gaz Green and Nerezza stare up at the over-hanging neon sign that radiates with the words ‘Studio 2’. The Jester emitted a sinister smile towards his larger comrade and pushed aside the doors of Studio 2 when suddenly a large arm blocked his route. The vampire was having second thoughts or was he to introduce more misery on the poor recipient of this next prank, either way Nascosto Ireul Nerezza wished to converse before the carpet bombing of pranks begin. “One day I plan to bring forth an army of the undead to steam train my enemy, humanity, but tonight that takes a backseat to your childish shenanigans. For once can we not demean ourselves to the cretin’s level of intelligence!” hissed the Angel Of Fear, his piercing red eyes burning a hole through his smaller accomplice who idly stands by nodding his head in an acknowledging fashion. “And with this army we shall overthrow the powers that be, for we shall be INVINCIBLE~! Please… As much as I love the Mortal Kombat Deadly Alliance angle I’m sure tonight’s charade won’t intrude too much – Now, all for one and sod the rest!” Like a General the Jester stormed into the studio or as he perceived it, his personal battle field. The audience seating arrangement litters the background while the Emerald Menace stands rooted to the laminate stage in the midst of props and recording equipment, entangled wiring causing potential deathtraps, a real obstacle course, one that would rival that used by Old School Entertainment. Dropping from his raised pedestal, Nerezza took aim behind the video lens of a camera as his associate rummaged about for what turns out to be a standard microphone. “I’m so glad you decided to operate the camera, you have a face fit for radio while…” murmured the Jester as the bloodsucker untimely interrupted with a bark, a growl enough to quench the comedy of the green tailored prankster and enough to get this charade back on track. With a few adjustments of the visual lens the vampires thumb rose into the air, an indication for them too start. Brushing down his forest green business suit, the one that has been super glued to his back since his foot pressed on a Wrestle Online canvassed ring, the Jester soaked in his last deep breath and lowered his jaw to begin, “Welcome…” “Welcome to Chat Back…” With a gleaming white smile etched along his angelic face the conceited trickster pointed out the bold lettering of the Chat Back logo, coincidentally authenticating that this indeed was the very same studio used by Chet King. “Why would someone go to all this trouble to break into a television studio, only to utilize the very same set by a man who has ridiculed and degraded the Front Office Entertainment Manager? Revenge? Revenge is such an overdone motive. Mr. King if I wanted to play a tug-o-war of words then I would’ve at-least did it on a grander scale, I mean, this spare no expense set just isn’t me. The Jester is a suit wearing, limousine driving son of a gun so I am a little more educated than to lower myself to either a verbal confrontation or your invitation of a Neanderthal brawl. I grow tired of this verbal game of tennis but you’re are the host of the phenomena that has swept America, no not the HIV virus but the epidemic they call Chat Back!” The Lord Of Laughter pauses and doing so creates a momentarily suspense and as you can imagine, the home audience will be on the edge of their seats in anticipation but only after this particular episode airs too the masses. “Pranks With Attitude being all evil and stuff would like to offer those who never got the chance to witness the blissful reunion of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor the opportunity to watch this tearful moment… I must admit this footage brought a tear to my eye… I was crying with laughter…” Courtesy of Nerezza’s cameraman skills he maneuvers the image over to a plasma screen television set where his fiendish business partner inserts a standard VHS tape into the player. The screen suddenly engulfs in static and the momentarily lapse soon quenches as the video begins to play footage taken from March Madness, we join the wedding scene midway where the Jester unmasks himself. The revelations of the Pay-Per-View continue as the joining of two persons is scampered by the tacticians of Pranks With Attitude who disrupt the ceremony in a way they only know how, CHAOS. As the wedding concludes the heinous attack loops over and over again… “And with the prankage invested in me, I now declare your marriage to be one BIG set-up, thus NULL AND VOID! You may head butt the bride!” And with those words both craniums of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor collided in a sickening thud, this small reel as stated on a loop, every time it plays both members of the Conniving Collective draw a smile that is a touch bigger almost to the point where they suffer the Jim Carey syndrome. Finally concluding the video pauses on the aftermath of the PCW Tag Team Champions and returns to its ever-ready presenter, be it Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green who smugly holds his catalogue pose, microphone at hand. “Well folks that’s all we have time for from this special edition of Chat Back, so goodnight and God bless…” Both P.W.A members upped and left but as Nerezza vanished through the entry door, the Jester as always at to have the LAST LAUGH and turned to face the camera one final time, the opportunist wanting to leave his calling card. “By the way… Chet… This is what I think of your show?” Again turning from the camera, the distinctive zip sound filtered and the unappreciated calling card was that of Gaz Green urinating over a set of props. The sound of liquid murmured over the silence, when without warning the vampire half of Pranks With Attitude ventured back into the studio in a moment of unknowing disruption. “GAZ IT’S THE LAW!!! Err… Arr… For heaven sake put it away!!!” “NOT NOW~!” With a fidgeting movement the Jester skipped about before disappearing alongside the Living Nightmare, ending another charade by the uncanny tag team of Pranks With Attitude.
