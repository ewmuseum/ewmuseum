The Definition of SOCIETY

9 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 9, 2003 Begin Transmission. (SOCIETY is alone. Once again, Michael Chang is not present. South Korea is now SOCIETY’s location, after another long plane ride. The big man is just getting off the airplane, as he stretches his long arms and legs out. SOCIETY looks around him at the mass of business travelers.) “The airport. The only other place you can find conformists. Of course, they conform to the wrong ideas, but they conform nonetheless. Business is the most obvious place to find conformists. So many employees, and so few employers. In essence, the business world is the struggle to rise from one who is forced to conform, to one who forces the conformity.” (SOCIETY nods to himself, pleased with his own analysis of the business world.) “In a manner of speaking, it is the same in Primetime Championship Wrestling. During their reigns, Media, The Ultimate Authority, and The Front Office all imposed their measure of conformity on PCW. It was up to the lower ranking roster members to conform. Many did not, especially those in a higher position. That is the equivalent of class struggle in PCW: The conformers versus those who do not wish to conform.” (Business travelers are hustling and bustling through the airport. Briefcases and attaches bounce off SOCIETY’s legs, as he shrugs off the accidental blows, making his way to a bench, where he sits to try and get rid of his jet lag.) “In a few hours, I face Shane Robertson, but that is not what is on my mind currently. I will be facing Matthew Jackson, an undefeated member of the Rising Star Division, and Brianna Washington. Ms. Washington may prove my most difficult opponent to date, as she has considerably more experience than myself, and has even captured a championship belt, the measuring sticks for greatness around here. Still, I doubt Brianna has faced a man of my scope. A human being of my size. A human being of my stature. And, most importantly, a human being of my cunning.” (A young man, of about twenty years, spots SOCIETY on the bench. The man looks at the giant cautiously.) “May I sit?” “Certainly.” (SOCIETY’s answer was brief, meant to diffuse any conversation, but this subtlety went unnoticed…) “So, sir, what do you do for a living?” (This was the type of small talk that angered SOCIETY most. He was a purposeful human being, one molded, yes, even created for that purpose. He prepared his next words carefully, hoping they would have the usual effect.) “I seek the total and utter conformity of humanity to my ideals. Those who follow my tenets are rewarded with popularity and prestige. Those who do not follow my tenets will be ostracized and shunned. I am what the different hate most, and the beautiful praise!” (The young man was horrified, only able to ask his next question in a hushed whisper.) “W-who are you?” (The answer was swift.) “I am SOCIETY.” (As quickly as he could, the young man got up from his spot next to SOCIETY, and vacated the premises. SOCIETY grinned, and stretched out on the bench.) “Works every time. Now, where was I? Oh, yes…” (SOCIETY’s thoughts snap back to his matchup on Turmoil.) “As I was getting to, this is my first main event. My first chance to prove what I am truly capable of. Jackson and I are two rookies, being given a chance to compete on the grandest stage of our career thus far. I have studied Jackson. He is more than capable, with four wins to no losses. He may be the only competitor in this division that will pose a legitimate threat to my aims. Despite this, I am not concerned. For, on my side, I have one of the most capable in all of Primetime Championship Wrestling, the Continental Champion, Violence Jack.” (A grin creeps its way onto SOCIETY’s face…) “Two men who appreciate violence will team up together to show a crowd of bloodthirsty non-conformists how violence is truly perpetuated. It’s almost poetic, in a manner of speaking. But for Washington and Jackson, the only poetry they shall hear will be the eulogies at their funerals!” (SOCIETY rises, walking off slowly in search of the exit, before snapping back around violently, wild-eyed…) “YOU WILL CONFORM……TO SOCIETY!” End Transmission.
