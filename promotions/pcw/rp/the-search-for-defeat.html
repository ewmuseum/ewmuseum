The Search For Defeat

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Present Time I will not fear defeat anymore. I am tired. Yes, I am. Erik Erratic, a boy brought by the limitless air into this place. Twenty years of age yet it seems like the world already gave me something more than I can handle. Born in an island in Asia called the Philippines, in there I witnessed the reality of poverty and so much more. Came to a country named United States, in there I experience life as a sovereign and so much more. And then came to a place called PCW, here I congregated and crushed head to head with a miscellany of people. Scientologists, worshipers of gods, rappers, schizophrenics, lovers, inventors, jokers, neo void gods, perfect storms, astrologers and the list go on and on and on. But amongst these diverse people, comes an intangible facet that lurks each and every one of them. The fear of defeat. With this dynamic, these people carry out whatever possible means of bringing victory. Just like I – when I was young and determined to get appreciation from the people. But today it all changes. Defeat won’t bring me down. Defeat won’t make me weak. Defeat won’t make me cry. Instead, defeat will make untouchable forever. Why else would these men and women wake up each day to tell us their out-of-this-world stories and adventures, tell us their thoughts, tell us their beliefs, tell us their history, tell us their life. Why else? Because that’s their diversion to their Achilles' heel, or their flaw. Because that is their mask that changes them to a whole different person and not just an ordinary human that lives an ordinary life. They have to be different to be successful. They have to be different to make an impact. But isn’t their differences also a similarity? Question my sanity. Question my understanding. But in the end, between us, who do you think will be absolute and complete. Not you, not until you grasp that trepidation and throw it into a bottomless pit of conquest. With my defeat came my triumph. With your defeat came a new chance. With our defeat came an ethereal force that will lead us to our ultimate immensity and prominence. Defeat is nothing more than a six-letter word. It will touch me no longer. ~ o ~ o ~ o ~ “Erik Erratic,” a man wearing an unsoiled suit, with a nametag that reads Mr. Jones, says in a peculiar manner. The businessman plays with The Assassin’s scripts and finally puts it down into his desk. He puts his feet up his desk and relaxes in his colossal leather, office chair. “First of all, I want to know why the hell your mom gave you that name.” “Do not flirt with my history,” The Erratic One roars sympathetically into the businessman, just as sensitive as an Astrologer regarding his beliefs. He sits idle on a wooden chair across the other man’s desk. “It is too much for you to grasp. Times-passed is nothing more unimportant than a false Greek god.” “Fair enough,” the middle-aged man replies, not wanting to dig in deeper into the background of the tolerant fiend he is facing. “So what did you want me to do again? Find you actors that aren’t scared of defeat? I don’t get you one bit. What difference does that make? I guess what I’m trying to say is: what are you trying to say, Mister, ah, Erratic?” “You are just like everyone else, dull and narrow-minded. You want to know the difference? I don’t have time to tell you the difference.” The Preeminent One continues slowly to make apprehending for the businessman easier, “So why don’t you just do your fucking job and find me actors that aren’t scared of defeat!” The businessman puts his feet down the floor in trepidation. He looks deep into Erik Erratic’s eyes and sees a man enveloped by a shield against conquer. The man picks up fiend’s scripts and puts it back in a brown sachet as he seeks for the right words to calm down his client. “Okay, Mr. Erratic, I’ll try to find those actors.” Erik Erratic stands up as well, impatience forming in his eyebrows. “You know what, don’t even bother. I doubt it if you could even find just one human being fitting for my next movie – an actor that is not afraid of defeat. Better yet, to be more undemanding, a man that is not afraid of defeat. This world is lacking.” And then the complicated and intricate man repeats, “This world is lacking.” Without saying another word, Erik Erratic grabs the brown envelope, containing the scripts for his next film. He heads out to the exit door of the office, opens the door, and gets ready to burst out of the room. But then takes a last look at the businessman shocked by the sudden turn of events. “We need more people that isn’t afraid, Mr. Jones. It is a necessity. We cannot get achievement and triumph without defeat.” And without anymore long-drawn story of his life and adventures, Erik Erratic, the very same man searching for that very same necessity, gets defeated in an instant in his search. But it doesn’t matter. Looks like he found one man any way. Then he steps out and closes the door.
