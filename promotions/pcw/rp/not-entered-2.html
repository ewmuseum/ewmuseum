Date: not entered 'Listen Tim. Miyagawa beat you. You've done the job to Leo Micelli and the Pimply Faced Intern Kid. What makes you think you can even eliminate someone tonight?' We open shot on the streets of St. Louis, Missouri as Tim Walden and D-Kay Calyx head towards the Savvis Center. Tim is dressed in his usual blue jeans and white t-shirt, whereas D-Kay is dressed in a pair of white jeans, white training shoes, and a white Fire Island jacket. The people around the two don't seem to recognise them as their attire allows them to blend in with the normal population of St. Louis. The only people likely to recognise the two Wild Deuces are loyal PCW fans. 'I know D-Kay. But this is Pay Per View. My invincibility didn't work on Friday, it will tonight.' 'Didn't work? You tapped out to Therapy! Damn man! Even Steve Jabowski can take that move and stay standing. You're getting worse man. Soon they'll ask you to drop one of your names, and count you as the next HANK~!' Tim stops in his steps as the word HANK~! is mentioned, causing a woman walking behind him to crash into him. Tim apologises to her as she mutters a few expletives edited from the aired version of this promo. 'How dare you mention his name? I am NOT that bad! I am Invincible Tim Walden!' 'Of course you are Tim.' Tim continues walking after D-Kay with an angry look on his face after detecting the sarcasm. Tim follows D-Kay past a row of shops and offices on the main street of St. Louis, before stopping at a bus stop outside a coffee shop. 'D-Kay. You do know that I'm meant to be cutting my own promo for March Madness? I mean, I am in this match as well.' 'Yes Tim, but you don't count. Impact, THE Multiple Man, Big Daveylicious and Afwa Kilimanjaro are involved. Granted, Big D, TMM and Afwa haven't made any efforts so far in training, and Impact lacks just that. But me and you, there's no contest.' Tim again looks hurt as D-Kay smiles. 'Come on Timmy. You've jobbed to Jeff Marx, Leo Micelli and more. Whereas I'm me!' Tim shakes his head while D-Kay bursts into laughter. Both men stand at the bus stop in silence for a few minutes until they are joined by another of PCW's recognisable duo's, Red Rock and Boolie. 'D-Kay! Timothy! Me and Boolie have been looking for you!' Red Rock walks into picture and shakes hands with D-Kay and Tim, as Boolie comes in and forces a hug out of the two men. 'Will you knock it off Boolie? I have to pin Walden tonight! I can't do that if you squeeze all of the wind out of me.' Boolie quickly releases D-Kay and steps backwards a few paces, nearly being hit by a Ford as he steps off of the kerb. Red Rock and D-Kay grab hold of Boolie's arms, and drag him back onto the pavement, and straight into a smack to the back of the head by Red Rock. 'Will you be careful Boolie? I'm fed up of putting myself on the line for you. One of these days you're going to get yourself killed.' Boolie, dressed in a pair of blue jeans and a red shirt, shrugs his shoulders at Red Rock. Red Rock, wearing his black jeans and Metallica t-shirt, sits down next to D-Kay and turns to Walden. 'Aren't you meant to be training to actually last past the first elimination Walden?' D-Kay starts laughing as Walden starts to reply, before thinking better of it. 'Hey Red. Who do you think would come out on top if me and Walden were to go one on one?' Red Rock looks puzzled, but sees an innocent question asked, and gives his reply. 'Probably you D-Kay. But you can never count Walden out. Trust in that tonight man.' D-Kay nods his head as Walden lets out a sly smile. 'Anyway guys. Me and Boolie must dash. We've got a little something to attend to before tonight.' With that, Red Rock and Boolie again shake hands with Walden and Calyx, and continue walking in the direction they were original heading. Walden looks at D-Kay, who shrugs his shoulders. 'Don't ask me Tim. He's a strange little bugger is Red Rock.' 'I thought you two were a tag team?' 'Yeah, but have you actually seen Metallyx listed on the roster or cards? We're not recognised, so it may be wiser concentrating on a solo career. Whatever I have left of it now my chance at gold has been thrown out of the window.' 'It's not my fault! Miyagawa spat at the referee and got his attention! If you had of done a better job at distraction, or even had Red Rock distract while you helped, then I might of won that match.' D-Kay shrugs his shoulders, and gives in to Walden's arguments as a bus pulls up at the stop, allowing both PCW stars on. D-Kay pays the fare and, amidst the looks received by other passengers, they sit down at the front of the bus. 'So where are we going then D-Kay?' 'Training Tim. You know, that thing where you actually make an appearance, and do some preparation for your match? Try it some time.' 'Yes D-Kay. Thanks for the lesson on my morals.' 'Or lack of Timmy. Anyway, listen, I've been meaning to ask you something.' 'If you propose, I'll kill you. Match or no match.' 'You couldn't kill me if you tried Timmy. No, what I meant was, if we work together tonight, and last until the end ourselves for a one on one match up, who do you think will pose the more of a threat?' 'Well, Impact poses very little, so he can go. Big D may pull something out, but will probably screw it up, THE Multiple Man and Afwa pose our biggest threat.' 'Then there's you. Right. Thanks for the insight.' 'Are you going to use this against me tonight?' 'No man. It's just a way of stable mates having a little conversation Tim. Nothing too drastic.' Tim shakes his head again as D-Kay smiles. The rest of the bus ride continues in silence until we pull up outside the car park for the Savvis Center. D-Kay and Tim are first off of the bus, entering the car park and walking towards the arena. Reaching the end of the car park, both men see the large crowd waiting for tickets early, and decide to try another way through after seeing the 'Staff' signs pointing to the rear of the arena. Tim and D-Kay wander around to the staff entrance, and seemingly arrive after a big name star of PCW as the multitude of media personnel wander about the back filling in reports and severing live TV links. Walden and Calyx manage to walk through the reporters, and into the arena, only to be stopped by PCW Television's main interviewer Cal Seaver. Cal, dressed in his usual black dress suit, manages to stop them both, and drag a cameraman in to help him. 'A few words before the Pay Per View gentlemen?' The two Wild Deuces stop, with Tim sighing as Calyx nods his head. 'What's wrong Tim. Fear of actually promoting yourself for matches?' 'In three, two and go. Ladies and Gentlemen, I am stood here with two members of PCW's Wild Deuces, D-Kay Calyx and Tim Walden. These two are both involved in the Six Pack Elimination match tonight against Big Daveylicious, THE Multiple Man, Impact and Afwa Kilimanjaro. What are your thoughts on having to face team mates?' D-Kay is the first to answer. 'Well, anything it takes to be honest Cal. I mean, Tim's a fellow Deuce, but to win a match, you have to pin everyone. Unless someone else pins Tim.' 'Tim? Your thoughts?' Tim looks at D-Kay and shrugs. 'Again. We have to pin everyone in the match to win. And I am not going to be covered for the three tonight.' D-Kay looks at Tim and smiles. 'Oh no?' Calyx grabs hold of Tim, and drops him with a powerslam on the floor, before throwing Cal on top of him. As he does, Leo Micelli walks out from behind the corner, and slaps down the three count. 'There you go Tim. You were covered for the three count.' At that point, PCW commentator Jeff Marx walks past the three of them in the corridor. 'Tim Walden jobbed to Cal Seaver~! CAL SEAVER I SAY~!' Jeff continues walking down the hall laughing as Tim shrugs off Cal, and storms off towards his locker room, with D-Kay following him down the corridor laughing, leaving Cal to clean up. 'Well there you have it. Neither D-Kay or Tim will give up on this match tonight, and apparently I am now in the long line of people holding a victory over Tim Walden, and this has upset him. But, rather than dwell on that, I'm told we actually have the first of our VIP guests turning up, Ethan Hawke!' The camera fades out on Cal Seaver and crew chasing to catch Ethan Hawke on film.
