Jaded Prayers...

Date: not entered Silence… …There's been a lot of that around here lately… …Any ideas why? Mortals just love to waste their time, do they not? It's not like they have much of it left in this world…all they do is talk…ramble on about nothing…assuming their words will be useful… But all they can do is speak…most certainly they cannot believe in their physical skills to deliver them out of harms way. Therefore they rely on verbal communications and assaults…maybe then they will be able to seem like a force instead of like a splotch of waste. Basically, the god has not been present for one reason and one reason alone... ...No one in PCW is worth his time. I just love to watch him toy around with you mortals...it’s so entertaining. It’s like watching a child play with his action figures. The rag tag bunch of mortals come this Tuesday can be called nothing less than action stars, however. Especially when their actions are in vain... They’re more like porcelain dolls...fragile...gentle...breakable... The god must handle with care... Unfortunately for all of you...Akiyama does not handle with kid gloves. He destroys...banishes... What is the NVG even thinking of when he’s fooling around with the likes of Jay Sylo? Sylo...he who thought of himself as better than Akiyama...he could never realize that he is nothing more than scum on the boot of the gods. What is he? He calls himself a servant of the one true god...there’s only one true GOD OF PCW...is he calling himself the servant of Akiyama? He’s better calling himself the B!tch of the NEO VOID GOD...that’s what Akiyama will make him out to be when their bout is ready to begin. What a fool this Jay is. You would think after being New Age and Ice Age that he would smarten up and move onto the next period in the timeline of humanity...the Middle Age... Why is that? Because he most certainly cannot make up his mind on what age he wishes to exist in. It would suit his fancies next to step into the Dark Age...I doubt however that his presence would be welcome there. I guess it is the duty of Akiyama to lead him into his next age... ...the Dead Age... Hopefully...Sylo will not be...Left Behind...in his own demise. Me personally...I think this whole thing on Tuesday is a waste of the god’s time. He has more important things to do than try to escape from an airport. Of course he could just teleport through the Void and be done with it...simple as that. But of course PCW doesn’t think about the advantages that exist within the Neo Void God. They are mortal...they cannot extend their minds to grasp those concepts. They know nothing of what Celestials are capable of. All they think of when they hear Celestial is Freaks...Ghouls...Monsters...Demons...Unholy Beings... Hmmm...maybe they do know a thing or two about Celestials. But they know not one thing about Akiyama. I would suggest further reading on the subject. Read what, you say? No...not the Book of Crunk...and not the Book of Sylo. If I wanted you to read smut, I would send you a pamphlet on Scientology. No...the Savage Tranquility is the supreme text for all your ethereal needs. And unlike the rest of them...it does not come with a played out tag line like “Austin 3:16,” “Crunk 1:87' or “Sylo 6:9'. Yes...you want literature to save your soul...read not the Koran or those other books...but read the Savage Tranquility...the Classic Novel of religious volumes. My mind’s wandering...it’s normal to wander sometimes...normal for me, anyway. When you’re looked upon to predict the actions of the universe, your mind tends to wander around when you’re not on duty. All in a day’s work for the Divine Prophetess... “Failures...they will copy you...mock you...challenge you...defy you...but all you can do against them to show your perfection...is destroy them.” ---Savage Tranquility, Page undetermined. Wandering does good for the soul, some would say...it helps you clear your mind and relax. Not in the case of the god, however. Wandering was a task...a daunting task that took him to numerous places all over the world. Sometimes these places were good...some were bad...either way, the lesson was passed along, and another mortal was enlightened. Where was the god this time? Akiyama stared up at the logo sitting atop the frame of the brick red building. In electric blue letters, the initials were clear... P...W...A Interesting...why was Akiyama standing before the PWA training facility? The one place, where the next generation of PTC stars were trained and sent out into the world? The god stood across the street staring up at the building as the pedestrians behind him passed on. Some of their glances wavered upon his massive frame, other avoided contact in order to spare themselves any trouble. They knew not that no one was spared when the wrath of the god was dished out. Akiyama took a step forward, but found no where to step. In the street before him was a horde of traffic, packed tighter than preserves in a jar. The eyebrow of the god raised as he analyzed the situation. He could either move the obstacle out of the way...or he could walk over the obstacle. The same options were present in the bout he was to participate in soon...so many obstacles in this match...and there were numerous ways to move around them towards a goal. He could either go through them, breaking each of their mortal frames like toothpicks...or he could go over them, and only demolishing those who find themselves foolish enough to attack him. It seemed like a common flaw within these people in PCW to provoke the god...and they never thought about the wrath that the GOD OF PCW would place upon them all. But alas...that is their human ignorance...it is encoded into their genes from creation...all you can do is feel sorry for them. So what do you think the NVG did? The first car slid across the street a few inches before slamming into the side of another auto. Horns honked and drivers swore, but their noises were like buzzing flies as Akiyama stepped across that river of traffic. He climbed atop of a Volkswagen Beetle to reach the middle of the street. The size of the god tilted the car forward, almost tipping it all the way over. Akiyama stepped off, and the car crashed back down on its tires, a dented hood exposing the footprint in the broken engine inside. A sedan’s roof was completely smashed in as Akiyama climbed onto it. Using it as a stepping stool, he climbed next onto the roof of a motor home. The mobile home shook as the weight of the god stood upon it. He glared down below at the last row of cars. A black limo sat parked in front of the building. Akiyama took no concern in leaping off the motorhome onto the roof of the limo. Just his luck, he crashes through the sunroof and finds himself standing waist high in the car. He puts his fists together and smashes the edge of the limo in a bit, allowing him to step up and out of the car. He had left the road in pandemonium after crossing that Red Sea of automobiles...but he finally stood before the PWA training building. Hope they don’t require appointments... {~Look at these children...busying themselves with training and preparation...they know not of the failure they will receive once they reach the outside world.~} The eyes of the god wavered to and fro at the words of Cyclonica, seeing the vibrant youths make their way from weight room to training room to gym and so on. They all were in workout gear, not wrestling attire as of yet. But they all had one goal in their minds... ...Hitting the big time... They were just like the children on Turmoil...with one goal in mind as well... ...Picking up the win... They both shared the same flaw though. Neither of them had what it took to obtain their goals. It took but a special person to actually make it to their dreams and aspirations. Akiyama was THE one to emerge out of Tuesday’s turmoil...who would be the special one in PWA to reach their dream? He felt his feet move forward...they guided him deeper inside the building, down corridors and past doors until he reached the gym. The wide, vast area, where a small indoor track circled around a mat-surrounded ring. Around and inside the ring, trainees were training vigorously to improve their skills. Their determination could be called admirable...but of course determination with the absence of skill or intelligence is stupidity. He noticed a reddish brown haired woman off to the side of the ring. Dressed in a pair of white sneakers, black spandex tights with white stripes down the sides, and a white sports bra, the woman sat with her legs folded underneath her as she watched the other wrestlers practice on earnestly. Akiyama could see the emotion in her eyes...her soul was raging from behind those dark, glassy orbs. There was something keeping her back...and it was eating her from the inside out. Stepping forward brought about the attention of the other young ones inside the gym. Those who noticed began to flock around the god, gibbering aimlessly with requests for pointers and tips on being the better wrestler. They must have actually thought he gave a care about any of them. They must have had him confused with the God that actually cares for them...Akiyama was not that same entitiy. All it took was a few bodies dropping to the tan gymnasium floor to send everyone backing away from Akiyama. Apologies to those poor kids...they were just victims of circumstance...just like all the others...just like the men on Turmoil. The god then made his way towards the ring. He stopped a few feet away from the woman, staring into her like she was his reflection. Behind her, three women approached her, all of them wearing short excersize shorts and sports bras of various colors. They were of the skank variety, something that the sitting woman was very far above. Akiyama heard their words...their harsh words...words of lies...insults...the falsehood dripped thick from their words like bile...Akiyama could sense their insulting lies. Humans all had the same quality...their emotions and senses were so easy to read. The reddish-brown haired woman stood up after hearing enough of their nonsense. When she turned around, one of them threw out a kick. She grabbed the leg and smacked the woman down with a stinging slap across her cheek. The second threw a punch at her from the side, but she grabbed the arm and threw her face first into the mat, making her scream out and tap in a reverse armbar. The third stood poised, ready to attack her, but a whistle from across the room blew. The trainer, dressed in all gray, shook his head at them all, a stern and silent warning. That would be the last warning. The women all picked themselves up and scurried away, planning more backtalk about the woman later. The woman turned around, her eyes piercing sharper than ice picks. Her eyes suddenly gazed upon the visage of the Neo Void God. She stared up at him slowly, the awe slowly setting in on his amazing appearance. Akiyama met her eyes, and the two of them stared at each other silently for at least three minutes. Then...the god spoke. “What a b!tch.” Her eyes cut into him quickly. “What the hell did you say??” “Those women...they were acting like b!tches...that is the correct word, is it not?” She cooled down a bit, her eyes still fiery, but a bit cooler. “Yeah, you would say that. They’re just jealous sluts who think this place is all about who has the bigger rack and the prettier face.” Akiyama cocked his head to the side. “What do you think this place is about then?” “Kicking @ss...showing how much of a b!tch you can be...the usual.” truer words have never been spoken. The woman’s eyes sparked with a fire after reciting that phrase. The god could read it in her...he felt the warmth inside her, the burning for the combat...the violence... “Why are you not training?” he asked her. His voice was resonating, like she could hear it inside her mind as well as in her ears. It made her a bit edgy. “What’s it to you, anyway? I don’t even know you.” harshness was her best trait, clearly. “It seems you know Jay Sylo, however...” what a low blow. “...very well, you will recall.” Her dark eyes flashed out anger and suspicion at the NVG. Seems like he was saying things that were intimate to her and her alone. There was a quiver in her voice as she replied. “Y-yeah...I know Sylo...sometimes I wish I never met the bastard.” “And why would that be?” “Because he’s the one that got me pregnant! He’s the big dumb@ss blue haired no-condom wearing b!tch that caused me to get pregnant and stop wrestling. He ruined my career before I could even hit the top!” Eyes sparkling with emotion...twinkling with tears moist in her eyes...she was too proud and too strong to let them fall to her flawless cheeks. It was at that moment that everything around them was null and void...nothing else mattered but him...her...and the situation at hand. Akiyama’s hand raised to her face slowly. She jerked back cautiously, but something pulled her into his hand. She felt his palm press against her cheek, and then her head nuzzling against his hand. It was a comfort that she had long forgotten existed...in her world of pain and suffering, those feelings did not exist sincerely. Everything has gone wrong up until this point...right now, everything was simply... Celestial... “Sylo calls himself a servant of God...did you know this?” She shook her head at the words of Akiyama, his hand still against her cheek. He curved his hand so that the side of his index finger grazed softly against her skin, trailing down to her neck. “Do you find it logical for a man to claim to be a servant of God, yet commit fornication with you?” Her moist lips mouthed “No” silently. The NVG’s finger ran across the trail of her jaw slowly. “He made you with child against your will...but to take the life of the child would indeed condemn you into damnation in the eyes of God...you would not want that.” Another shake of her head. The god’s finger trailed down the side of her arm; she could barely feel his touch. “Sylo took your dream away from you...why would a servant of God do such a thing?” His hand interlocked with her, their fingers tangling together in one mesh of digits. Holding hands...it was a common thing between a man and woman, but it was overlooked so much, no one knowing the truly blissful feeling of holding another person’s hand in yours. “Tell the Neo Void God what you desire...” “I...want Jay Sylo to pay for what he did to me...” it was barely a whisper, but the god heard her well enough. “It will be so...Sylo will pay for his crimes...along with anyone standing in the way of that justice.” he replied, his hand bringing hers up before her. He took her other hand and held it next to the raised hand, both of them balled up into fists. “There is one thing more...one more thing you desire to ask of your god...” “I wish that Jay Sylo never got me pregnant... I wish that bastard never touched me.” She suddenly felt a kiss upon her navel, warm and moist. Two solid hands were against her hips, holding her still. Everything felt like a dream... “You must pray...to be blessed...” she heard from Akiyama’s voice. It seemed to come from everywhere around her. “Pray to me...and we will bless you...Jaden...” “I pray that Sylo never got me pregnant and ruined my career!!” “Jaden...” “I pray that Sylo would pay for his crimes!!” “Jaden...” “I pray for your blessings!!” “Jaden!!” Opening her eyes, she found herself kneeling in the center of the wrestling ring in the gym. The lights were dimmed, and next to her was the trainer. His hand was on her shoulder as he tried to rouse her. Her hands were folded before her face, as if she were praying. “It’s closing time...” he told her. Jaden nodded slowly, standing to her feet and climbing out of the ring. They walked out of the gym silently, turning down the corridor towards the entrance. “Do you need a ride home or anything? I wouldn’t want anything to happen to you or your child.” Jaden reached for her stomach, shaking her head at his question. Her hands gently held her midsection as she walked. “No...I’ll be fine...” she looked down at her stomach, a serene look on her face. “We’ll...be fine...” She knew nothing of what happened to her, or if it happened at all...but all she knew right then and there...was that her stomach felt truly... ...Celestial...
