The Aftermath of Spring Fling

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-21-03 The scene opens to Adam Stryker’s bedroom. There are no lights on. The only light in the room is that trickling through the curtains of the sliding door. An alarm clock sits on a nightstand next to the bed. It reads 7:39. Something in the bed rustles. A hand, a rather large hand, comes down onto the clock. The hand gently lifts the device up moments later a groan can be heard. “Ohhh…” the groaning continues, “Jas? Where are you?” Outside on the deck, Jaslene is pacing back and forth with a cordless phone in her hand pressed to her ear. She’s dressed down in a pair of gray sweat pants and a white tube top. A smile comes to her face as she tries to hold back the laughter. “Haha…Gina stop!” Jaslene quietly exclaims, “I don’t want to wake up Adam. Yeah he did have an awesome match. I think it’ll help break this funk of his.” She walks to the end of the deck and looks out over the backyard. The setting sun reflects off of the deep blue pool. “Yeah Gina, I’ll be sure to let him know.” Jaslene says with a sigh, “I’m a big girl you don’t have to tell me twice. Ok Gins, love ya babe…come out here and visit soon. All right, talk to you later, love ya, bai-bai.” Jaslene slowly pulls the phone away from her ear, pressing the off button as the phone crosses her the plain of her hip. She sighs as the wind begins to pick up. Back inside, Adam is finally wide-awake. He’s lying in the bed with his arms out. He’s just staring at the ceiling. The door slides open, he moves his eyes just in time to see Jaslene step through the door. “Hey.” He says with a smile. “Hey, I was wondering when you were going to wake up.” Jaslene says closing the door behind her. “How long have I been out for?” he asks. “Well let me think…” she says tapping her cheek, “You slept to the airport, through the flight and the ride here. Without the limo driver I wouldn’t have been able to get you into the bed. I had to tip him an extra hundred for his help!” “When was this? A few hours ago?” Adam asks in a more curious tone. Jaslene just laughs at him as she sits on the bed. “Try about twelve to thirteen hours ago.” She says lying back and resting her head on his midsection. “You put on one hell of a show so I didn’t want to wake you. Speaking of which, how do you feel?” Adam brings his arm down and begins to stroke her hair. “Much better surprisingly,” he says with a smile, “Aside from a few aches and pains of course. But I’m feeling rested for a change.” “Well that’s good,” Jaslene cuts in. “Huh? What does that mean?” Adam asks looking lost. “Oh no reason, I had noticed you weren’t sleeping well in the nights leading up to Spring Fling is all. I’m surprised you were able to put on the show you did.” “Oh really?” Adam says sticking his tongue out at Jaslene, “Come on Jas, spill it. I know when you aren’t telling me the whole truth so out with it.” “You uh…have a match Wednesday…” Jaslene says quietly. Adam sits up in the bed. His bare chest is still read from blows he took during the match at Spring Fling. “Christ!” he exclaims, “This seems a bit sudden, who is my opponent?” “Well…uh…you see…” Jaslene hesitates for a moment, “Someone?” “Someone? What do you mean someone?” Adam says, “That’s like going into a restaurant and just telling the waiter you’d simply like to order food!” “I know, I know,” Jaslene replies, “But the card has it listed as No Disqualification/No Count outs Match Number One Contendership to PCW Extreme Title; Adam Stryker vs. Mr. Surprise.” “Ok hold on one second.” Adam says, “Number One Contender’s match? How does this happen? I’m something like two and ten in the PCW.” “Well maybe they are going to give you the push you deserve.” Jaslene answers, “You bust your ass for this company and maybe they are finally recognizing that and want to reward you!” “They are going to reward me by making me bust my ass ever harder? Assuming of course I even beat whoever the hell Mr. Surprise is and then win the Title.” Adam says shaking his head, “Besides Titles are a lot of work as it is to make it the Extreme Title on top of it doesn’t make it much of a reward.” “Just be grateful you didn’t have to waste time peddling around in the Rising Star, Shooting Star whatever the hell it is division.” Jaslene says raising her brow, “Things could be much, much worse.” “I suppose that is true.” Adam says with a sigh rubbing the back of his neck, “Do you have any idea who this ‘Mr. Surprise’ character might be?” “Pffffffffft…you’d have better luck just asking everyone in PCW. How the hell should I know?” Jaslene responds. “You shouldn’t even worry about that. You’ll be fine, remember how many matches you have lost like this?” Adam looks up to the ceiling and closes his eyes. “Hey!” Jaslene shouts at him, “How about none!?” Adam opens his eyes. “Good point.” Adam replies, “But that doesn’t mean I shouldn’t worry about the match. All of the other times there was no variable involved, this time it’s different.” “How so?” Jaslene asks. “Well what if come Wednesday my opponent turns out to be someone along the lines of Meanstreak? Or Frisco? Or any other PCW Hall of Famer?” Adam responds with questions of his own. “Adam be serious for a moment, do you honestly think that one of those people would come back just to fight you for a chance at the Extreme Title?” Jaslene says giving him a look, “Puh-lease, Adam you may be a good wrestler but I doubt PCW would go to such great lengths.” “Come on Jas, of course I wasn’t being serious.” Adam replies. “But seriously, I have no idea who is going to come through the curtain come Wednesday. It’ll be interesting that’s for sure.” “Yeah…” Jaslene says curling up next to him. “Anyway, enough of business,” Adam says looking down to Jaslene, “Who were you talking to on the phone?” “Gina. She called to congratulate you for your match at Spring Fling.” She answers. “Congratulate me? But I didn’t win.” Adam says. “You know that, I know that and Gina knows that.” Jaslene says, “She commented that your match was one of the better of the night. She loved it.” “That’s good, I’m glad someone got enjoyment out of it. When is she going to come out here and visit?” Adam says. “Whenever she can get a free moment is what she told me. Lord knows when that will be with her though. She seems to be extremely busy so it could be in a while.” She answers him. “Well hopefully it’ll be sometime soon.” He says. Jaslene just nods as she places her head into Adam’s chest.
