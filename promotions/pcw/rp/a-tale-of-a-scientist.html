A tale of a scientist...

Date: Just now... It was an ordinary day to him… His brilliantly devised alarm clock, years ahead of its own time technology wise, declared its all to the “Master,” stating clearly, “Sire, thy day of reckoning is at hand! Time to wake up, and take over the world, ect, ect.” And, as always he emerged from beneath his warm covers, stood on two wobbly feet, taking a daring peek at the light of day… Clamping his eyes tight, the searing burn of white too much to deal with on his own; he groggily just stands there, and doesn’t move an inch. “System activated,” announces the vixen like voice of the computer, and feeling a jolt beneath his unbalanced weight, the kooky appearing young man almost tips over. Luckily it’s nothing serious, but rather an effectively engineered way to start the day. Conveniently where he stands from the second he steps out of bed, a black conveyor belts comes to life, guiding the still sleeping on his feet fellow to a cleansing contraption. Behind the proper shielding, the machine will strip him bare, drag him into a shower to open his pours, and clean them out with hot jet streams of water. Then get to the brushing of the teeth to get the morning breath out of his mouth, and so on, and so on. The whole process ends with him being fully groomed without having to lift a finger and a nice, black cup of coffee to rev things up. Emerging from the rectal end of the human car wash, a small man, well trimmed in the proper attire to begin his day, and equipped with the mind God gave him, grins brightly… Peculiarly frizzy brown hair, even after being drenched, refuses to cease its heavenward reaching upon his head. A white lab coat, pocket protector readily placed, covers the major portion of his short body, and a pair of thick, black-framed glasses sits crooked at the tip of his nose. Adjusting the lenses accordingly, the young man brings the cup of coffee to his lips, takes an eager gulp of the robust, strong, black, boiling hot… Moments later three robotic maids, each with they’re own specific function, are busy sweeping the ceramics off the ground, and wiping up the still warm liquid from the wall. A stick figure on rubber wheels handles the broom, while a box like bot opens up its front end, awaiting the debris. Just a normal day… Or was it? No, there was something rather abnormal in fact. Exiting the technologically advanced bedroom, he emerges into the grand foyer of his considerably large mansion. Greeted immediately by a large, boxy robot, designed to appear as a butler of sorts, the young man is escorted from his over looking balcony top floor, to a staircase that curves to the marble below. Dropping one foot down upon the first step, almost timidly checking it as one would cold water before diving in. Activating, the staircase takes the role of an escalator… As he descends, another butler droid is there and the foot of the escalating stairs, waiting for him. Butler Robot: “Sire. The process is near complete. Shall I escort your royal ness to the laboratory?” Stepping off the conveying staircase, and onto the cold marble floor, he takes a smug look around the atrium that has hid the real purpose for his home so long. He worked “hard” to earn such a fabulous residence. But, there is no turning back now. “Master/Sire/???”: “Yes, I believe that would be the proper assumption, considering the occasion…” Responding, the Butler-bot’s single optic visor flickers green for a moment, and the action is followed by the echo of clanks from within his metallic hide. Suddenly, its mass partially disassembles, the exoskeleton surface opening up, revealing the mechanical mastery within; the gears rotating, the bare framework shifting to completely new locations, and the exterior folding into itself. No more a simple android servant, but now a form of household transportation, a hover-chair levitating in place for a moment, ready to be boarded. “Master”: “(Patiently…) To the laboratory.” The hovering chair, designed similar to a recliner like throne, makes the first move swinging around behind the master, and scooping him up. It zooms off, a high performance lounger, to where ever the good man should command. Arriving in his futuristic laboratory, the young genius felt a weakening tremor within, and he was incapable of repelling the surge of emotions flowing through him. Salty water emerging from his eyes, the fruits of his labor refreshing his reason to live, and it’s almost powerful enough to be compared to a mother pulling the kin from her womb. This is his greatest accomplishment… [I]Meet Dr. Lucifer A. Borodinsky (A for Abromowitz), renowned scientist and brilliant inventor of the twentieth century. Even now, in this day in age, he has made himself millions, in fact billions with future profits, all by the age of 26. Remarkable, and unnerving simultaneously: He is feared by those who know of his work, as he agreeably advanced the technology of the military, finding new ways for nuclear energy to be released further (As in MUCH further…), yet at the same time he is the favorite tool of the government, for his “generous contributions.” It all started when they discovered him, and made him their own. They made him the spokesman of technological advancement. But, although the best-known scientist of the time, TV was new, and many people didn’t know of him. He was revered by the science world, and to a few others, that had heard a little. His most amazing accomplishments saved away by “the authority,” for later use. They just asked him, he used his incomparable mind to do what they needed, and then was rewarded for his excellence. This mansion, secluded along the pacific coastline, where he resides in luxury was a gift. Some gifts are given with a purpose, or reason… The Government, upon offering terms with the youthfully, enthused, inventing guru, decided to get as much out of his noggin as possible. They gave him the funds; all that he needed to design the ultimate laboratory beneath his domain. Build them what they wanted, and remain with your fortune, but it all allowed him to see what the Gov. was really using him for. Every man-made creation he improved, they stored away, or covered up, lest the public’s dreams of flying cars and residing in space become reality. A member of the select few that truly knew the mechanics of the U.S. juggernaut. Point zero, zero, zero, zero, well one could go one for awhile… Anyway, it’s a fact that a small percentage of the Earth’s population is born with extraordinary intelligence. For them, it all starts will a simple idea, language for example. They mimic once, and pull the long haul the rest of the way. Lucifer was one discrete few, born in Germany, and immigrated to the U.S. before he had reached one full year of existence, yet he understood both German and English alike. In the states, he learned of the culture with haste, his curiosity uncontrollably wild. Whilst living amongst poor, poverty-stricken population in the west, slums he refuses to recollect, he started at the bottom of the education ladder like anyone else. But, they weren’t smart enough… Expelled from the classroom environment, the only one his parents could in fact afford, he was determined to excel his fathom of brilliance on his own. Sass mouthing, although it was a formal correction (Several, actually) of the teacher’s mistakes, was not permitted back then. He couldn’t have been ten the day he began spending all of his time within the Library. “Too anti-ignoramus” to continue his days of rotting along side his parents, he merely just left upon the first day of teenage life. That past, it was all unclear to him… He merely left to experience the span of the world, and build upon the foundation of his inquisitoring way of thought. Roaming from destination to destination, all the while doing what he did the best. Improving what others had already made, and absorbing the new found knowledge. Until that faithful eighteenth year day. It was a cool summer day, but he wasn’t outdoors to enjoy it. A busy body at heart, he only cared for Earth bound materialism, and improving it. It was actually within a Chicago radio station, KTER’s Headquarters, where he had first run in with the overlords of America. CIA and FCC barged into the radio station, legal papers of the stations immediate shutdown on hand, pursuing the broadcaster who was illegally playing frequencies from all over the globe. A young Dr. Borodinsky was taken into custody… However, to his surprise they meant not to punish him, but rather to make an offer. He agreed. Then came the mansion, the dream lab, and of course his own entourage of hand made robots. He had all the time to spare, just as always, contracting all things, and creating a new splice of mechanics, discovery, and development. Television? He created an upgraded projected image version, shortly after the release of the original… CD’s happened to be an unfinished blueprint of his… Rumor had it; he had gone to space over the course of a weekend… Expanding on the radio, he pulled a frequency attractor capable of drawing radio waves on any square inch of the planet out of his ass, which was the one they caught him with… Not one of which has ever been seen by the market. Only small things, only almost insignificant contributions to U.S. weaponry were seen. Everything else, capped. He made new weapons, destructive forces they commanded him to make, yet NOT ONE WAS USED! It was then the good doctor realized, that the world one will live in to see his creations exist… That was a world far beyond his years. And the world he lived in… Someone else’s creations long before his time…[/I]
