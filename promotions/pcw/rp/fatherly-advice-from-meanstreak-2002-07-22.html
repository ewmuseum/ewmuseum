Fatherly Advice from Meanstreak

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd July 2002 We arrive at our chosen destination. Actually it’s not so much chosen, more of forced upon us by an invisible foe. We’re in the car with Alexander Miyagawa, almost legendary wrestler of Primetime Championship Wrestling fame. Although it seems different... mainly because it’s not his old BMW Z3... it seems more like... and it is; a family saloon. He’s driving along, humming a tune mainly because the car radio looks rather old and probably quite broken. He pulls up to the side of the road and opens the door, stepping out and locking the car quickly. He stands up on the pavement, wearing a three quarters length black woollen coat and a pair of jeans... although presumably the same old black t-shirt under the coat... one of these day’s he might purchase a second outfit. He pulls a slip of paper out of the jacket pocket and reads it silently before looking at a house’s post box at the end of its front yard. In fact, as the camera pans out, you see that he’s in what appears to be a quiet neighbour hood with trees giving pleasant shade from the bright sun. The wooden slats on all the houses are painted pastel colours and each and ever lawn is wonderfully mowed to a perfect half inch height. In other words, the perfect neighbourhood for bringing up a child. Miyagawa: This place must be expensive. I can almost smell the... A rent-a-cop security car drives past quietly on this suburban street. Miyagawa:... rent-a-cops. Miyagawa shrugs and shoves the slip of paper back into his pocket before walking up to the white picket fence and opening it, shutting it gently behind him. He walks up the garden fence, the roses to the left of him loosing amazing and in full bloom while the grass to his right looks greener than a land lover on a cross Atlantic sailing trip in rough seas. He walks up to the front door and notices that it’s painted the exact same shade of pastel blue as the rest of the house. He stops for a second and takes the slip out of the pocket to re-confirm this is the right address. He nodes to himself and places it back in the pocket before gently knocking on the door three times. He waits for a few seconds and goes to knock again when suddenly the door swings open and there standing is none other than PCW Legend and OSW Superstar, Meanstreak. His bulging muscles... his baggy jeans... his ponytail tided back... the angered expression on his face... the barbed wire tattoos all across his torso... his beard... his flower patterned apron. HIS FLOWER PATTERNED APRON?! Miyagawa stands there almost gaping at the apron when Meanstreak suddenly smiles happily and strides forward, grabbing Miyagawa in a hug. Miyagawa: ARGGGGGHHHHH!!!! I DIDN’T DO IT! I DIDN’T DO IT! Meanstreak steps back sharply in surprise as Miyagawa curls up in a ball to protect himself. Meanstreak: Didn’t do what? Miyagawa: You were trying to kill me! Meanstreak looks remarkably surprised. Meanstreak: What? I’m happy to see you Alex, come on in. I’ve baked cookies! Meanstreak smiles excitedly at the idea of the cookies and Miyagawa gets himself back to his feet after thinking Meanstreak was going to crush him to death and follows Meanstreak into the house, closing the front door behind him. The hallway is nicely decorated with some flying ducks on the wall and a chest of desks just next to the stairway. Meanstreak shows Miyagawa through to the living room where a young toddler is playing with a cuddly toy whilst a pet monkey is sitting near it eating a banana. Meanwhile a television is blaring in the background with a program playing on it about how to cook a sponge cake in under ten minutes. Meanstreak: Brandi said you were coming... I’m so happy, Alex, I haven’t seen you in ages! Remember back when we were in PCW together? Miyagawa nods to Meanstreak’s question. Miyagawa: Yeah. You gave me a concussion. Twice. Meanstreak: That was fun... but I’m different now. I’ve had anger management classes and I feel much happier about it. Caleb is growing up and it’s great to be around looking after him while Brandi goes out and brings in the money. Miyagawa: So you’re happy being a house husband? Meanstreak: I couldn’t be happier. Miyagawa: So given the choice between hitting me with At Death’s Door or baking a cake, which would you choose. Meanstreak: The cake, of course. Why Alex, you’re my friend. You’ve always been my friend. Miyagawa takes a step backwards towards the door away from Meanstreak as he notices that the ink on Meanstreak’s tattoo’s are starting to run. He leans forward and runs his finger through one of the tattoos, and it just simply smudges. Miyagawa: Uh... Meanstreak looks down at the tattoos. Meanstreak: Oh don’t worry about that, I had them removed by laser. Brandi drew them back on before she left because she said it would scare you otherwise. Miyagawa: I came around here to get some advice on looking after a new born baby but well... you like baking cakes... you don’t want to hit me... you had your tattoos removed... and you know what I think? YOUR NOT MEANSTREAK YOU SICK FREAK!!!! Miyagawa suddenly turns about and opens the front door, running screaming to the car. He leaps over the picket fence and opens the car door quickly before jumping inside and driving off at speed. As he leaves, a second saloon car pulls up and out steps a ginger haired man with a ginger beard wearing a ruffled shirt. He closes the car door and walks up the path towards Meanstreak who stands in the doorway. Meanstreak: Heya Kremmen, how’s it going? Kremmen: Yeah, not bad... why has someone drawn your tattoos back on? Meanstreak: Brandi figured it would stop Miyagawa from being suspicious of me, but I think it just scared the hell out of him. Kremmen: He’s always been a bit of an idiot, that one. Kremmen sniffs the air gently. Kremmen: Is that your double choc and peanut cookies I can smell. Meanstreak: Sure is. And with that, we leave the two oldest school PCW’ers to their cookie feast, and leave Miyagawa to his paranoid delusions of fatherhood... till next time, viewers.
