The Evil Super Genius Prep Returns

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Evil Super Genius Prep Returns Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 03/10/02 “Still, like, SO doing nothing for me!” We hear the voice of PCW’s preppy Commissioner Eleanor Hall, but see nothing. “What? It’s been 30 minutes!” Then, the voice of her husband, PCW Champion Xavier Kannon is heard, frustration in his voice. Still, we see nothing. “Sorry, it just doesn’t, like, do it for me anymore.” Ellie~! sighs while we continue to see Black. “How about if I give you.... DOUBLE?” The sound of the prep gasping is then heard. “Now THAT, like, SO feels right......” Our shot fades in to see the vacuous, pale Blue eyes of the prep staring at 2 bottles of Snapple~!.... One Strawberry-Lime and one Peach Iced Tea... The Bambi-esque orbs not breaking away from the vessels of nature’s own goodness. Eleanor : “I’m, like, kinda feeling a little SUPER....” Behind her, hugging her around the waits with his chin rested on her shoulder is Xavier, trying to entice back the powers that his wife once was in possession of. Eleanor : “With, like, this hint of GENIUS...” Holding his wife tighter, Kannon’s bright Green eyes focus on the bottles in awe... Snapple~! ... The drink he held PCW’s Soda Endorsement contract for.... The contract passed down to him by Alan Kreigman before he left PCW. Presumed dead. Eleanor : “Then, there’s like, something else I feel.....” Kannon remembers the moment he passed the endorsement on to Ellie~!... The transformation the prep went through as the spirit of Alan Kreigman left in part in the endorsement’s legacy was incorporated into her. He looks down. Xavier : “Sorry, my little Poon Puppy. That’s me. The whole double penetration innuendo got me a little... Aroused.” Finally the little prep breaks off from the Snapple~! bottles and winks to her man before returning her gaze to the sweet elixir before them. Eleanor : “And a whole lot of, like, EVIL!!!“ A smug grin of satisfaction makes it’s way onto Xavier’s face as a whole new route opens up for him. Xavier : “Excellent... With both the powers of Hubbard and Evil on our side, Clyde will DIE.” Ellie~! turns away from the bottles and entwines herself with Xavier as the shot starts to fade out, focusing on the 2 innocuous bottles of Snapple~! that may just have cost Clyde his life. Eleanor : “Clyde...... Will...... Die!” As she purrs and kisses her husband, feeling the evil surge through her the shot starts to fade out... Just catching the prep pushing the 2 bottles off onto the floor with a crash as Xavier lays her down on the table and pulls her t-shirt off over her head. Then.... Black. “HELLO?!?!?!? I, like, SO do not see the Phase Transducing Regulators where I asked for them, like, SEVENTY HUNDREDMILLION YEARS AGO!!!” The Black is deserted once more to show the prep standing on a walk way HIGH above a Lab come Factory below her, where she looks down upon proceedings decked out in a White lab coat, Black-rimmed glasses and a slightly rosy tint to her cheeks. Eleanor : “And, like, WHO taught you to make Semi-Automated Redox Batteries? You haven’t even painted them Pink!” Under the lab-coat she has a Purple roll-neck jumper and her long, wavy Blonde hair is tied up smartly behind her head. The stylish Super Genius prep with the Evil side has returned. Eleanor : “Er, like.. YOU!” A worker stops, looks up at her and points to his chest. Eleanor : “No, not you.... HIM!“ The man beside him also stops and looks up at the domineering prep with a worried look on his face. In his arms is held some type of Organic weapon that burps. He as well points to his chest. The prep replies in her good old sarcastic tone. Eleanor : “Yes.. YOU.... because I REALLY want to talk to YOU!” The hapless worker doesn’t pick up on her sarcasm. Eleanor : “Like... SO not you!” Both men look up at her blankly and she stares back at them. Eleanor : “Well.... Like, go do, like... STUFF!“ The prep raises her eyebrows and the 2 men scurry away while she pulls a Black Discman sized contraption from her lab coat’s pocket and points it at the man she wanted the attention of. A whirring is then heard as we cut to the viewscreen of the device, each person in the room being shown as a set of statistics instead until it locks upon the person she wanted the attention of and his name, address, blood type, marital status and anything else you’d want to know about him flashes up. Useful, if Evil Super Genius Ellie~! didn’t have the Ellie~! part at the end. Eleanor : “Um..... YOU! The one with type B blood!” Fortunately for her the man she wanted turns up and looks at her. Eleanor : “Wow, that only took, like, forever!” All those around the man continue their work... Incorporating microchips, welding pieces of machinery and testing out new contractions all under the view of a huge banner on the far wall that reads.... OPERATION, LIKE, KILL THAT CLYDE GUY.. AND STUFF! Eleanor : “Like, go get me a Salad....” The man salutes. “Yes Ma’am.” Eleanor : “With Orange and Oregano dressing.” The worker turns back to his console and types in a number and letter sequence before pulling a lever and to a whirring noise the Salad of choice for the prep fades into existence in the microwave-esque contraption next to his console. Eleanor : “Um... HELLO?!?!? Food there.... Ellie~! here....” Taking the Salad out of the device he places it on a try and presses a button on the side... Then, on cue, the tray begins to hover up off his desk and up high towards the prep who doesn’t seem that impressed as her pale Blue eyes watch her food slowly defy gravity in reaching her. Eleanor : “This service is, like, SO slow!” Finally it reaches her and the prep takes the fork and tries a leaf.... Eleanor : “Like.... ELLIE~! TO RETARD!!! COME IN?!?!? I said “Dressing”, not, like “Smouthering”!” Her faces turns sour. Eleanor : “Give yourself, like, 30 lashes!” The worker hangs his head and pulls his boiler suit down then presses a button and behind him a holographic whip appears and starts to deal out his lashes. “Hmmmmmmmm, I’ll have to find a way to use that later.” Cutting back to the prep we see Xavier walk up behind her and grab her around the waist, making her jump before they both look down on the work being undertaken below them, Kannon pulling the collar of her roll-neck sweater down and nibbling her neck. Eleanor : “Mmmmm, he’s like.... TOTALLY going to be dead, and stuff!” Their eyes sparkle in evil glee as the shot cuts to the workers and their amazing and lethal contraptions.... Or being whipped by them... And the shot fades out to Black. “Yes he is, my little Shag Squirrel.... Yes he is.” “Like.... YAY US!“ ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
