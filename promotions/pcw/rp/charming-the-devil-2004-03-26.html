Charming the Devil

26 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/26/04 Charming the DevilI hooked you with a single stare,It was over before it began.Like fallen pray you were lying there,Holding death within your hand.You had a void and I touched you there,With the ease I rubbed my hand.There was a devil in your despair,A devil at my command.What I brought, you were unaware,That it was the best you had to give.But all you took from our affair,Are the fears you must relive.But don't forget in your prayers,That all I did was make you bare.And to place the blame for your scare,It's in the mirror you must stare.Because I can not bring to open air,What already isn't there… OPENINGA warm, humid breeze rolls across the empty, barren streets of Research Triangle Park. Long since having been silenced in our world, the echoes of David Thompson and Jim Valvano still resonate through the streets of Raleigh, North Carolina. A haunting silence has overtaken the capital city, bringing forth a warm, scrutinizing blanket that overlies the damned city. The statue lying on the battery lawn in front of the original Capital Building of Carolina’s most famous sons, their Presidents, lies in wait, watching out over the Radisson in a gloomy silence, as if they were waiting to move from their eternal molds and rule over all once more. The sun slowly sets on it’s well trodden horizon, as life slows to a crawl as the city prepares for it’s sleepless slumber, a contradiction to the uncaring world around it. If only the city knew what was lurking in it’s miniature alleys, paralyzing the city as they went, it might have done more to stop them. As it was, Hell’s only spawn were preparing to shed blood very, very soon. Closing time, boys and girls! Head on home, turn off the lights, and pray for forgiveness. Jesus may still love the little children, but the Dreams of Maniacs are a different tale… SCENEThe William Neal Reynolds Coliseum in Raleigh, North Carolina is brimming with anticipation over Saturday night’s Turmoil, emanating from the same locale. A long, midnight-shaded stretch limousine lurches to a stop by a tethered red carpet, flanked on either side by long, velvet ropes bounded by golden capillaries. A host of photographers and journalists from various, sundry magazines like Entertainment Weekly, Rolling Stone, and Esquire line the carpet, flashing top of the line digital cameras while being smashed by a raucous mob of gothic heiresses and malefactors, screaming in vein to catch a glimpse, cop a feel, and relish a taste of their sole reasons for living on a wretched world that cares nothing of them. A broad shouldered, husky man steps into view from the blind side of the stretch, taking great pains to straighten his all black chauffeur get-up as he walks around the opposite side towards the rear door, positioned conveniently to parallel the studded walkway lying before it. The chauffeur makes a move for the door, hardly noticing the long line of equally-attired mountains running to add a layer of protection from the unruly mob accumulating around the area. The brash guard opens the door, revealing nothing but the leather interior of the stretch. With little in the way of climatic suspense, a sultry, fiery red-headed woman steps into an onslaught of camera flashes. Literal shreds of intricately woven cloth barely reveals her feminine exquisiteness, as her European splendor outshines everything else in the capital city. Another vixen, this one cropped with silky blonde hair steps out, dressed similarly to the first, barring the extra material needed to case her sparkling diamond veil. Her radiance nearly drops every hot-blooded male in the crowd, as she moves to take up arms with her subversive counterpart. A heartbeat later, a tall, almost critically imposing figure steps out, smoothing his overdrawn sports coat shortly before adjusting his Italian styled shades. A lengthy, scarcely visible scar penetrates his left eye, casting a red, streaking glare underneath the shades as he literally lets a primordial smirk stretch across his stone cold features. The crowd erupts into a frenzied roar as England’s darkest psyche, Christian 'The Devil’s Advocate' Smith waltzes out onto the carpet, taking the two heavenly creatures in stride as he makes his way towards the awaiting phalanx of corridors inside the coliseum. The two women lead the way in front as Smith turns towards the crowd gathered to watch his every move. He removes his sunglasses, letting the crowd admire the craftsmanship of the Japanese shoot fighters he’d subdued years before. With the words rising to his lips like an elixir of life, Smith felt compelled to embrace his 'creatures of the night' while they were still around to feel his presence among them. It was a truly uplifting experience, for all the wrong reasons. He should have been ashamed at the feelings of excitement, but the thought of leading on the pathetic bastards that had waited all day to catch a glimpse of him was too good a prospect to pass up. Advocate: 'Then the Jews demanded of him, ‘What miraculous sign can you show us to prove your authority to do all this?’ Jesus answered them, ‘Destroy this temple, and I will raise it again in three days.’ The Jews replied, ‘It has taken forty-six years to build this temple, and you are going to raise it in three days?’ But the temple he had spoken of was his body. After he was raised from the dead, his disciples recalled what he had said.' Ladies and gentlemen, as I promised so many years ago, I have returned… The crowd surrounding the journalists and photographers erupt into a chorus of constant screams. Screams of approval and willful desire to here more from their archetypal, the prototypical Divinity of their deepest desires. The media personnel are less than enthused, and slowly let a touch of angst mix with their complex, hard to read expressions. Advocate continues in his diatribe, unfazed. Advocate: I have risen from the ashes of veterans long since sacrificed on your bloody altar, and I am back to wreak havoc on this pathetic, futile sport! It’s time to make those sorry sons of bitches in there bleed, and you’re going to have one Hell of a time watching me do it! I think everyone has forgotten just how graciously evil we truly are! It’s time to remind them of our teachings, and to make them pay dearly for mistaking my presence here in Primetime Championship Wrestling! Let them pray, because it’s the last thing they will ever do! Advocate quickly turns towards the coliseum, leaving the masses to a wash of cheers and ranting screams of Latin praises. The camera flashes light up the deepening night sky, as Advocate quickly loses his edge, intentionally. As the two women make for a lonely wooden door not a hundred feet ahead, he loses the rage in his mind and becomes more intensely focused on the matters at hand. If he meant to prepare his two prodigies, Dom DeMerius and Ethan Richards, he had to be urgent, to the point, and precise with his words. A careless, reckless abandonment of reasonable logic was the last thing he needed to provide in the face of two viable, yet dangerous pawns in his personal game of chess with the wrestling world. He would have to trek carefully into the Lion’s Den. Advocate: Yvonne, Selena. Head to my private dressing room, I’ll meet you there. I have some unfinished business to tend to before we get down to the matter at hand. Some unfinished business, indeed. The two women literally sigh to themselves, their disappointment clearly visible and not the least bit hidden. They casually stroll away from Smith, who puts his game face on as he braces for an unexpected showdown with his children. Despite his own attempts to craft them in his image, they’d become more like sniveling little weasels, succumbing to his every wish… when they chose to. Make no mistake, Richards and DeMerius were as free-willed and independent as two delightfully twisted sadists could be. But he found a certain necessity in their personalities to yield to their creator that he found, disturbing. It wasn’t the word he was looking for, but it would have to do. He was already growing frustrated with his philosophical wonderings, and quite frankly, he couldn’t give a shit even if he had the time to rationalize such trivial matters. They were his slaves, bounded by a fear that thousands of others had grown to accept over his career as a living, breathing embodiment of the Devil incarnate, and that was all that mattered. Frustrated beyond belief at the inopportune meanderings his mind was taking, Smith pushed all random beliefs out of his immediate plans and threw the balsa wooden door open, not surprised to find his two protégés startled by his sudden entrance. DeMerius, as always, had a Cuban cigar plugged into his dark, unreadable expression, while his compatriot Richards was seemingly oblivious to his surroundings. Whether that particular trait was due to him being so focused on the match coming up for his team, or whether it had to do with being coked up as usual, he supposed it didn’t really matter. Their presence marked the beginning of a very interesting game, one that he was very much looking forward to playing. Advocate: Gentlemen, glad you’re not busy, because we have quite a few matters to discuss. I assume that you’ve heard the rumors about my return to wrestling, here in Primetime Championship Wrestling no less. It’s no concern of yours, though. It’s a matter for another day. Tell me, are you ready to wrestle tomorrow night? DeMerius: Is Paris Hilton fucking hot? Don’t worry ‘bout a thing, boss. We’ve got it covered tomorrow night, ain’t that right, Ethan? Richards: Yeah, uh… sure, whatever you say, Dom. Advocate: I’ve told you before, Ethan. Mix your personal pleasure on your own time. Now’s not the appropriate occasion to start playing with Mary Jane or snort a mountain of cocaine. You have to give the people something new, exciting, and fresh tomorrow night in order to maintain your reputations. As much as I hate to admit it, wrestling is as much flash and style nowadays as it is blood, guts and gore. You’ll have to cripple them after you get those pathetic sons of bitches off their feet. Win the crowd, and you rise to the top. Alienate the crowd, and there wont be a crevice deep enough in all of Hell to protect you two when they roast you both alive. Richards: That’s… That’s, uh… not true. DeMerius: The little stoner has a point, Smith. I could give a shit if I get endorsements from NIKE or Reebok. I could care even less about becoming some idol to a gothic underground contingent like you are. See, I lost the desire to find hope in being an actual wrestler long before you ever found me in Canarsie. And I’ll learn to feel compassion for 'regular' people long after you’ve found your niche in Hell! Advocate: My, my, aren’t you quite the firecracker tonight. Tell me, who lit a fire underneath your callous ass? Or was it that bitch of a whore you made love to in Nashville while you had your strange premonition about your father? Let’s have it, Dom, because I’ve got plenty of time to put you in your place long before Olf Magnus Svensson and Zhou have the chance to! DeMerius: You know something, Chris, you taught me not to fear a single, solitary person. And as much as you would like to convince people to the contrary, you’re still a man, just like those pathetic fools you associate yourself with. You see how far having 'friends' got me, didn’t you! Well let me enlighten you for a moment, Devil’s Advocate! I’m not your slave, as you so succinctly put it once. I’m a fighter, I’m a warrior, and I’m a death dealer of the first order. That movie magic shit is as worthless as your attempt to work your evil over myself. Richards, the stupid fucker that can’t lay off the crack? Intimidate him to your heart’s content, but there is nothing, and I mean absolutely nothing that I fear anymore! Maybe ten years ago when I had the blood of my parents on my hand, but not anymore! Advocate briefly considers crippling Dom where he stands, but thinks better of it. DeMerius wasn’t as incompetent as he looked, and having someone on his side with that kind of ferociousness would be a definite asset when he made his move to enter the Face to Face tournament. Richards was expendable, but Dom was a commodity that he would dearly pay without. A smile stretches across his emotionless, indifferent features, lulling DeMerius out of his false sense of bravado. Advocate: Good man, Dom, and a very good deal. I’m proud of you, in spite of your shortcomings here and there. You’re a fearless bastard of my own design, and you’re holding your end of the team up flawlessly. Too bad I can’t say the same about your partner. You, though, have talent. What a balls up that show of bravado was, my faithful friend. DeMerius: I thought you might like that. Advocate: Ah, but time is always against us. I’ll be here through the night, enjoying some of North Carolina’s more worthwhile contributions to this wretched sanctuary for immorality. Never forget who you are, Dominic, that’s all I can teach you anymore. You possess the skills needed to become a winner, all you need is a temperament on that anger you have kindling inside you. That rage is going to kill you one of these days, and it’s certainly eating you up inside. DeMerius: It keeps me warm. Advocate: The fires of Hell will give you plenty of warmth when your dead. You’ve maimed enough innocents to earn a one-way trip there. Whether you live to fight another day or succumb to Zhou and Svensson tomorrow night is up to you. Be ready to fight, be prepared to bleed, be primed to win. I’ll catch you in a while. DeMerius: I’ll be here, waiting. With a smirk only a serial killer could appreciate, Advocate saunters away from an ice cold DeMerius, who is still seething with a rage he can’t contain much longer. With a forceful tug at the handle, the door swings open gingerly as Advocate steps out into the cool, dank corridor, now devoid of the life that was brimming over the edge of reality back outside. A quiet, malignant hum was originating from somewhere deep inside the arena, but the noise wasn’t bothersome. Soothing, in fact, so much so that he was actually lulled into his old day dreams again as he absently ran his fingers across the concrete wall of the hallway, wondering not for the first time why he couldn’t feel the cobble, earthen stones that made up his own personal hallway. A lot had to be done before he could rest, but watching his two accomplices cripple a Shaolin Monk and his Magnum Opus would be quite the sight. Content to remain in the city long enough to make his intentions well known to the world, Advocate surrenders to the thoughts of bloodlust and greed, wishing desperately for an absolution to his nightmare that would never come… *** For the longest time, all that Dominic could do was try not to scream. It took him a cool, calm and collected moment’s worth of rationalization to realize that the pitched, whining noise was actually coming from his clenched jaw, and that he’d managed to crack two teeth in the process. For whatever reason, DeMerius suddenly has an urge to inflict true, physical pain on Zhou and his bastard friend, Magnus right then and there. Richards never bats an eye, but he manages to stop shaking long enough to stand up, the effects he’d suffered through moments before nothing more than a charade to avoid meeting Advocate face to face again. Although he would never admit it out loud, he personally despised the man, and actively feared him in and out of wrestling. He wasn’t personally ashamed to admit that fact in public, either, though he would never back down from the chance to fight him. DeMerius could care less; he was ready to brutalize Svensson’s complex features in a rash of hideous torture. DeMerius: It’s that son of a bitch’s fault, all his. If it wasn’t for that living, breathing pestilence Svensson and his bitch friend, we wouldn’t have to put up with that man. It was a terrible move, letting him prance us about the world like his prize possessions. But we can make him sorry for doubting us in the first place. Richards: Doubting us? You heard him, acting like I was the worst acquisition since Pamela Anderson got Hepatitis. I, unlike him, have more constructive methods of dealing with bitches that fuck with my emotions. I have certain… ways to elicit glorious screams of pain from the lonesome losers that I get to play with. DeMerius: We’ll get our chance with them, Ethan. We’ll get our chance to show them who their masters are! How sweet the taste of blood is in the night air, wouldn’t you agree? Richards: That it is. Say, I wonder if we can make Zhou actually say something tomorrow. Isn’t he supposed to be the strong, silent type? DeMerius: Well, we’ll see how long he goes without begging for mercy tomorrow night. Say, should we skin him alive first, or break a couple of bones to get warmed up. You know, I’ve lost a bit of my touch since the last time I had to cripple some pathetic bastard. DeMerius: I’ve made people do very strange things over my career, my friend. It doesn’t take much effort to get people to scream, does it? I’m sure Zhou will be more than happy to sit down over a cold brew when he has to pick his arm out of his ass and rearrange his facial features!Richards: Ah, just like the old days.DeMerius: It’s been a long ten years, Ethan, but it’s been the best ten years of my life. I don’t know if I’m ready to give that up yet. Richards: Why would you give it up? I mean, if you‘re afraid- DeMerius: There isn’t anything on this planet that I fear, nor anything that will keep me from going down to that ring to whip a little ass for those weak-ass fans tomorrow night! I’ve never backed down from a challenge, and I sure as Hell ain’t gonna let some two bit queers like Zhou and Svensson no less, show me up in front of the largest crowd in wrestling history. This is my dream shot, a once in a lifetime opportunity. Sure I was taught by a multiple time World Champion, and I’m to be honored with awards out the ass… but I haven’t worn gold yet, and that’s something that will eat at my soul for the rest of my days if I don’t take this opportunity now and run with it!Richards: What opportunity? Wrestling two has been losers in some incestual paradise? If you’re going to make history, at least do it some place worthwhile like Los Angeles or Boston. This place his Hogshit, U.S.A.DeMerius: Well, make no mistake. The Carolinas aren’t exactly the classiest places to be, but they’ve got a lot of history in wrestling. And if you don’t make it to that interview with Meltzer in twenty minutes, YOU might be history when Advocate finds out!Richards: Yeah, alright. I'll try and get finished as quickly as I can at this press conference. We should still have a few minutes to party with the ladies tonight at that club down the street... DeMerius: Don't keep me waiting too long; you know how pissed off I get without the bitches crawling all over me. I might just have to beat the Hell out of you before I ever get a chance to maim Zhou! Richards: You know, if I weren't wrestling those two dipshit rejects tomorrow night, I'd just say to Hell with all this ritzy shit and spend the night with Cal Roberts’ old chick. Always want to piss him off, heh heh. I wont be long at all... DeMerius slaps him a rare high five, and heads towards the conveniently placed PlayStation 2. Instead of doing the sensible thing, Richards straightens his suit out and wrestles with his tie as he heads towards he opposite way, pushing the door open and instantly focusing his attention towards the Radisson Conference Hall nearly five blocks down, where wrestling journalist Dave Meltzer would be waiting for him. As he rounds the corner, he walks straight into a vibrant party of sorts. Gallant decor lines the makeshift gala, as hoards of photographers and media journalists line the outer walls of the conclave. On the main floor of the great hall sits numerous dignitaries from all over the state of North Carolina, including Senator John Edwards and Governor Mike Easley. EuroSport President Elijah Weaver and a pair of Maxim Magazine editors are wrestling with themselves to keep from strangling each other at the upscale function, while wrestling legends like “Superstar” Billy Graham and “Nature Boy” Ric Flair, of course with his escort are busy sipping wine near the front of the conference. He scans the crowd for Olf Svensson and Zhou, the so-called “Reluctant Heroes”, but he comes up empty. He does; however, from the corner of his eye, spot a rather serious looking Devil’s Advocate walking towards him, holding a bottle of Smirnoff Ice in each hand. Richards: What the Hell is this shit? I’m in the locker room for ten minutes, and they set up some huge ass Turmoil party? Granted, it’s Turmoil, and it’s PCW, but what’s the big occasion?Advocate: You know Richards… while I'm not a huge advocate of disavowing the finer tastes of wine, champagne and alcohol in general, I believe that I'll draw the line at wearing this goddamn suit and drink a fucking martini at this taco-munching whorehouse! Richards: Christ, what crawled up your ass and died?Advocate: Nothing, my blonde-haired friend. Nothing at all, especially when you meet the two chicks I picked up along the way. Glad you’re here, though, since you’re due up to speak in about… sixty seconds. Richards: Sixty seconds? What the Hell? I guess your friend and mine, the chauffeur forgot how to get to this wonderful establishment early enough to let me know that I had a fucking press conference to attend. Good God, man! Why the Hell are we staying at this place instead of the Howard Johnson like the rest of the guys? I feel out of place with all these snobs with rods stuck up their ass! Advocate: It’s called the high life, my friend. Dom is a great friend, but he doesn’t fit well into this social lifestyle. You do; you’ve got the talent, the charisma, and most importantly, the desire to make it really fucking big in life. Hey, you’re sipping wine with the finest this hick state has to offer! Have to be happy about that. Richards: The only thing I'm happy about is being reunited with the feeling of Zhou’s nose breaking under my fist! And by my watch, I’ve got twenty four hours left to cripple him, and fifteen minutes left to get to my radio interview! Advocate: Don't worry, Ethan. You'll get your chance to make conversation with Meltzer and whoever the Hell else you‘ll be talking with... you'll get your chance. It won't be long, but you do have some business to tend to right now. Richards: Fuck this shit, I’m out-Advocate: Listen to me, you ungrateful little prick. I don't care what demons you have are holding you back, and I don’t know what some jerk off did that screwed up your big return, and I don't know where you've gotten this attitude about having no respect from your peers, but if you never take another step inside a ring for the rest of your life, you will remain now and always one of the greatest pure wrestlers of your time. No matter how hard Zhou or Svensson will try tomorrow night to prove you different, you’ll be one Hell of a superstar here in PCW. Now get your head out of your ass and have some fun. I’ll take care of this pathetic little gathering.Ethan Richards nods his head and turns to make his way towards the exit, not for the first time wondering why he felt no guilt when he stood up a bunch of losers. As he walked, he noticed that the noise was dying down further as he left. He cocked his head to the side, glancing at the now empty hall. With his mind set, he firmly wishes to just beat up on the Reluctant Heroes and get the Hell out of Raleigh as quickly as possible. Ethan pushes through the glass swinging doors, noticing a more ghastly chill in the air than there had been earlier on. No one around, no one to talk to… just like home. He let a smile spread across his face as he made his way down the street, thinking about the two hundred bones that he would break the next night, and in which order he would break them.Exeunt All.
