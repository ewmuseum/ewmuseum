Tales of the Golden Toe: Tale of El Romantico (Part 2)

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/02 Tales of the Golden Toe: The Golden Heart as passed and the Golden Toe has come back again. The Golden Toe is the old Juan Borro, the fun loving foreign soccer player who did his all to help others. Through the tales of the Golden Toe Juan gives us insight into present experiences with his life and duties and encounters with people. The Golden Toe has seen his calling, and that calling is to do good, be good, and be a power for good. No force will stop Juan’s GOAL~! Time: 8: 14 P.M Location: Barcelona, SpainScene: Underground Fighting Circuit “The story behind the man, is sometimes just as important as the man himself, history means everything, there is no world without history.” Part 2 Things in life are unpredictable. Like the way a person can snap at you at any second, or the way you go into something knowing exactly what’s going to happen, but when you finally do it everything changes. Juan thinks he knows about this El Romantico character, but does he really. Even when he gathers all of the facts and elements of his life, he still not a hundred percent sure of the outcome. With this said the scene opens. Juan Borro is seen making his way back to the Underground Fighting Circuit early in the morning after the night before. He’s there to try and gather some information about El Romantico and where he can find him. When Juan arrives back in the stadium he goes to the front office where he sees the president of the promotion working the phones. “ No, all endorsements with the romantic are off. He doesn’t do any endorsements, not ever for me, so find someone else to bug.” The President shouts on the phone. He then hangs up the phone and notices Juan in the doorway. He signals for Juan to come in and take a seat as Juan goes over and gets comfortable. “Yea, you probably don’t remember me, but I was here yesterday and had a little bit of a problem?” Juan asks hoping the president remembers who he was. “Well there are a lot of people at my promotions, did you lose something or leave something behind, if so we have a desk for that.” The president replies while he sorts through the papers on his desk. “No I was the one who entered the ring area toward the end of the show to challenge El Romantico, but he disappeared before I could get an answer to the challenge.” Juan states, trying to remind the President. “Ahh yes, you were the one who was foolish enough to try and stand up to El Romantico. Not a smart move, not at all.” The President answers as he gets up and grabs a blazer to put on. Juan follows him across the move and begins speaking. “Why do you say foolish? Everyone keeps telling me how great this guy is. If he’s so great how come I’ve never heard of him?” Juan asks. “It’s because not much is known about El Romantico. I’m sure you’re not the only one to know about that, but he’s very mysterious. Not even I who handle all of his bookings and managing know enough about him to draw a valid opinion. “That’s odd.” Juan responds. “It sure is. I’ve always wondered things about him, but when I wanted to ask it to him, he’d be gone and just leave a white rose.” The president responds not knowing the reason for El Romantico’s actions. “That’s weird, I just thought the white rose and all of the other things about him was a big act or gimmick.” Juan responds now confused. “No, that’s what makes this guy so mysterious to the outside world. We don’t know why this guy does these things. I hear the ladies he comes in contact with feel some of his emotion, but he’s never around in the morning to talk things over with them.” The President says with a smirk. “Sounds like a hell of a player to me.” Juan adds as him and the president share a small laugh. The tow men go silent for a moment before the president takes a deep breath and decides to give Juan the little insight he knows. “Well there is one thing I know.” The president says lightly and confidently what. “What?” Juan responds willing to listen. “If I tell you, you have to promise not to tell anyone. He said if I ever told anyone he’d haunt me forever.” The president says. “Why would he tell you and why would you think he could possibly haunt you.” Juan asks thinking it’s all silly. “He doesn’t tell anyone anything, but I made him tell me for the job opportunity. I’m a little scared to say anything, because sometimes I believe El Romantico isn’t even human and can really haunt me.” The president says being completely serious. “That’s hard to believe, but I’ll keep your trust and secret of the story. So what is it about anyway?” Juan curiously asks. “Well it’s said that when he was a young boy his family was murdered in his own house while he was sleeping.” The President says while shaking his head. “Woah, I can see how that can lead to trauma.” Juan says surprised about what he had just heard. “That isn’t the whole story either. He told me something went through his body that could never be let out to the public. This thing killed the men who killed his parents, and is the same thing that gives him the power he has now.” The president says with his hands out not knowing the meaning to it all. “That’s something I’m not sure if I can believe, but I’ll try to so I can get a better understanding for this man.” Juan says wanting to know more. “Well that’s all I really know. If you could find anything else I’d love to hear it, but I doubt anyone else knows anything about this mysterious man.” The President says as he walks back to his desk. “Well thanks for the help, I’ll have to get going.” Juan says as he leaves the office. When Juan exits the building making his way back to his car he hears some noise coming from up above him on the building. Juan looks up at the top of the building and sees none other than El Romantico dressed in all white. “What are you doing here?” Juan shouts over to El Romantico. “I should be asking you the same question. Don’t bother going around and asking. I’m right here, when you want me you’ll know where to find me.” El Romantcio responds. “Then how come it took me this long just to see you again.” Juan responds not understanding El Romantico’s statement. “You’re just looking in the wrong places.” Ell Romantico replies. Suddenly, El Romantico disappears before Juan can speak again. Juan wonders what he meant and how he could find him again. The Scene fades to black with a white rose falling from the sky.
