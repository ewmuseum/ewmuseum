Reunited Part 1

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/30/03 The sun beat down on the small town ice cream store conveniently located beside a small pond as the temperature rose to seventy degrees. Down south in Louisiana where I would find myself this Tuesday when I teamed with both Skylar and Gaz against The Pharaoh, Dorn Tracer, and Dante Jackson, they might consider a seventy degree day cool, but to us northerners a seventy degree day was a sure sign that summer was coming and was quite an exciting event. As I climbed out of my car my adopted son Evan copied my actions and did the same as he climbed out from the backseat and took his place by my side. I led him by the hand towards the counter where we could buy ice cream which was conveniently placed so that you could purchase ice cream outside as well as inside. Of course with such a rare gorgeous day like this I decided it would be best for us to buy ice cream outside and eat it by the pond, and it seemed that Evan had no problem with that as he simply smiled as we walked up to the counter without having a line to wait in. “Can I help you?” Asked the lady at the counter as we strolled up to it, Evan standing on the tip of his toes so that he could look in and see what was going on inside the store. “Can I get a small vanilla ice cream in a dish and what do you want Evan?” I questioned the four year old beside me who was fascinated by the happenings inside the ice cream store although there was nothing going on except work and others eating their food inside rather than outside where we chose to eat. “I want a chocolate chip ice cream with sprinkles!” The kid shouted with much excitement inside his voice which caused the lady at the counter to smile before she went off to get us our orders. While waiting Evan did his best to jump up on the counter and I decided that I might as well be nice and help him up onto the counter where he could fully see what was going on instead of just standing there on the tip of his toes curious as to what is going on but unable to find out just like I felt when it was announced that if Gaz, Skylar, and I defeated The Messiahs of the Dark Prophecy how was I supposed to go on and defeat Dorn Tracer right afterwards. Sure it offered the chance of a lifetime to defeat Dorn Tracer and walk away the television champion but at the same time I was just about screwed over from the start. How did they expect me to have enough energy to go on and defeat a man who had already kicked my ass in the past? It seemed impossible unless somehow I could muster up the strength to weaken up Dorn and capitalize. It seemed doubtful but I had to at least try although right about now I probably should be focusing more on Krikorian considering that I face him on Fusion which occurred before Turmoil. “Here you go!” The woman said in a cheerful high pitched voiced used to talk to a child as she handed Evan a cone filled with chocolate chip ice cream and covered in those rainbow sprinkles and then handed me my order as Evan jumped off the counter as his attention shifted from what was going on inside to his ice cream. “Thanks.” I said to the woman before I dashed off towards Evan who was already on hi way towards the pond where a group of people were gathered around to feed the ducks or just enjoy the scenery while they ate their ice cream. “Can we feed the ducks?” Evan questioned as he ran back towards me, tugging at my arm. I simply nodded a yes as he ran back towards the lake and took a seat at one of the empty picnic tables around the lake. Looking around I noticed a great variety of people from different backgrounds but I saw a face which really caught my eye. I took a minute to stare at it and I could have sworn it was Chris it looked exactly like him. I took a seat beside Evan and was careful not to look away from the man who was practically a replica of Chris; it had to be him it just had to be. “Evan stay right here, I’ll be right back.” I said as I stood up from my place at the picnic table and began to move towards the man I was studying carefully as I listened to him call out to someone. “Christa, Alex, it’s time to go!” He called out to two children as I let those name fall into my ears before realizing that I knew those names very well. Those were Brandon’s kids and where they were Allison was sure to follow. I couldn’t pass up this opportunity and just ignore them, I had to let them know I was still around and that I still cared for them no matter what thoughts Allison might have brainwashed them but then a sudden question hit my mind. Was that Chris with Allison? Why was he with her? The question stung in my heart the way mace stings at your eyes but I had to make my presence known no matter what the circumstance of them being together was so I ran towards the man who just had to be Chris and let my emotions overcome me before I could even think. “Chris!” I said as I shouted towards the man and ran over embracing him with a hug. The man stood there in silence for a moment before realizing the opportunity placed before him and then wrapped his arms around me as well as he pulled me closer to him. “Oh Tessa Bear I’m so glad to see you.” He said as he planted a kiss on my forehead as I could not help but to weep tears of joy for the reunion I never thought would take place. “I’ve missed you so much that I think we should make up for lost time right now.” He said as he took my hand in his before turning to the woman I despised Allison and giving her a command. “Allison can you watch Evan and the kids while Tessa Bear and I take a walk?” “Fine.” Allison snorted in disgust as he and I began to walk towards Allison’s car and climbed into it as it came to life as all three of the kids waved to us before we backed out of the parking lot and began our journey to pave over the bumps and reestablish our relationship or so I hoped.
