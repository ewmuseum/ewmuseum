Date: not entered RP 1 for Turmoil 12, 1 for Fusion 15 We have all heard the rumors and stories of Paris being the city of love and all that mushy romance that makes preteen boys vomit and adolescent girl’s dream of Prince Charming. What a joke. At least that’s what Teresa thinks. All the stories are made to fill our heads with hopeless dreams and dead end roads to that little lie they call romance. Today, Teresa strolls down the sidewalks of the great city of Paris, France. With a camera hung around her neck, she is the typical sight seeing tourist taking picture and stopping to stare at a large building or two. The rest of the Roster is scattered throughout the city. She hasn’t formed any strong friendships in PCW thus far, so she thinks little of where everyone else is. Suddenly someone bumps into her from behind as she takes a picture of a large water fountain. Teresa is nearly forced into the water but her hand grabs the edge of the fountain preventing her fall. She turns around ready to slug someone but instead steps back as if she seen a ghost. “Watch were you’re going Nerezza. You almost made me fall in” The pale faced tag team champion offers Teresa a hand but she had already stood up. He nervously slides his hands in his pockets. “I’m sorry Teresa. In my search to understand human romance, I choose you to join me in an experimental date” Teresa can’t help but laugh a little. She has never been asked out in an experimental date before. She combs her long fingers through her blonde hair. Heck, she’s bored and company is always nice. “Umm ok just don’t bite me or anything. Where we going?” Nerezza looks to Teresa confused. He hadn’t thought of that part. He looks around and sees a fancy restaurant. He smiles pointing to the expensive restaurant. Meet me at the front door of the restaurant within the next hour. Dress appropriately. An hour and a half pass and Teresa still stands at the door of the said restaurant. She hates dresses and those uncomfortable dress shoes as well. Despite this fact, she adorns herself in a long dark green dress with a slit up the thigh, matching heels and her hair is worn in a French braid. Nereza finally arrives wearing a black tuxedo he found in a store window a block away. The two speak as they are being seated in a booth seat. “So Nerezza, you’re telling me you have never been on a date?” “That’s correct.” “And you asked me to come here so you can get a taste of what a real date would be like?” “Correct again. So Teresa, now what?” Teresa looks up to Nerezza from her menu with a sly smirk “First we order our food. After dinner, we take a walk around the city then rent a hotel and have wild monkey sex all night.” Nerezza gives Teresa a blank stare for a moment, then moves his dark eyes to his own menu. Teresa’s fingers take the top of his menu and tug down so she can see his face. “I’m just kidding. If you seriously want to know, just do what you feel. Be yourself and if you’re with the right person, the romance will fall into place like a puzzle.” Teresa sits back in her seat. Whatever she got herself into this time, she is positive this date will never be forgotten.
