***That Night*** 12:00 midnight | [Everyone had gone home except for Darnel and I. The smoke stained on the walls making the wallpaper peel and turn a nasty almond brown shade. The place was dim with only the glowing glare from the T.V. and the yellow kitchen light giving life to the place. Snow fell pass an old leafless tree which scratched against the window with its branches every sway it took caused by the wind. I was just about to stiff up cocaine when Darnel came out the bedroom wearing a loose button up shirt covering a wifebeater underneath. Of course Darnel was acting strange today but it still didn't hit me to the full affect where I questioned it. Darnel, who has a odd resemblance to Deebo off of 'Friday', sits down on the black leather couch and watches T.V. for a bit while looking at the small clock which stands up on the big screened T.V.. I took a snort of the line of cocaine I drew across the table before walking off the tile covered floor of the kitchen, to the black carpet floor of the living room only to sit on a couch across from Darnel.] | 'Wutz crackel-lackin' Deebo?' Clowning around, Lil' Thug greets Darnel. 'Look, diz shit has been on my fuckin' mind all day. So I'ma come right out wit da shit.' 'Wut da hell you talkin' bout D?' Completely slipping his mind, lil' Thug remembers his strange behavior as of late, 'Yo, datz riight. Yo ass has been actin' a lil' funny all of a fuckin' sudden. Wutz poppin'?' 'Aight, diz iz how it goes. I'm short on money and today iz pay day. You kno' dat corner sto' up da street?' 'Yea.' 'Well they gon' be a lil' flat broke afta I roll up and out dat bitch tonyte.' 'You bout to rob da sto'? Hell nigga, why didn't you tell my ass?! Why we waitin'? Shit, I'm boutz to get me some chedda(money) too!' | [I got my shit together.] | 12:40am | [We arrived to the corner store by foot, which come to think about it, may have been a bad idea... Anyways, as soon as we approached the poster advertisement filled thick glass double doors the Asian couple at the front desk immediately begun eyeballing us like an eagle does prey. Of course we popped our collar in their direction and kept on walking to the back where a huge freezer stretched along the whole back of the store. Darnel and I looked up and down all of the liquor knowing we were just going to steal the shit anyways before the Asian bitch got all in my business... mistake #1.] | 'Get what you want and leave!' Said wit arms akimbo, she looked in an act of provocation which pissed me off. 'Look bitch, as soon as I find wut da fuck I want, I'll roll up outa diz piece but until I do, I will take my motha fuckin' sweet tyme. You got a problem ho? Well solve it on ya own cuz I'm threw wit yo chicken head ass!' Pissed I turned back around. She must not have known I'm a very irascible person because her old ass came back with something that made me slap her ass. 'Your momma! | [I know Darnel was thinking, 'No she didn't...' before looking at me because I slapped the fuck out of her stupid ass. Quickly her husband took action by grabbing a shotgun and doing an unreal flip over the counter.] | 'I'll kill you bitches!' Said with a slur and slight spitting on 'bitches', the Asian man who towers at only 5'6ft stands there looking like he is going to do a Bruce Lee number on us. Of course I backed back and gave him 50 feet, metaphorically. 'Now get the fock out! Now!' 'Fock?' I clowned on his accent of fuck, which probably wasn't such a good thing at the moment seeming as how he had a gun. 'Man, shut yo bitch ass up!' Darnel pulls out a gun and blasts a hole in the Asian man's head. | [The Asian lady on the other hand was skilled too, as she did some brake dance number on the floor before springing to her feet and kicking the shit out of Darnel. It soon ended as I sent one to her peanut head. She dropped as if someone took her soul and left the outer shell.] | 'Stupid bitch. Darnel grab da money, I'ma get us sum 40z.' | [About five minutes into the theft and murder, cops swarmed around the place like bees on honey. Darnel grabbed the money as I grabbed the 40z and we made a 100yard dash out the back door and into the woods that lied to the back of the store. At first I was behind Darnel as a result of him being first out the door and I could hear people in close pursuit behind us but it got darker and darker with each step. But the one thing that was on my mind, the one thing that was so stupid I totally forgot about the cops, was Darnel... He was wearing light up shoes... I wish I was close enough to bitch slap his ass but I ditched to the left and let the cops follow his path. Soon they caught his ass, and the money.] | | [Police sirens lashed out into the snowing night like jagged knifes as police officers lay Darnel over the hood of a white and blue police car. The dull voice of a chubby officer is drowned out by the hectic stress and sirens causing the scene. Wind ripples Darnel's button up over shirt covering a white wifebeater as he shakes his head in shame while getting pulled up with handcuffs making him none ambidextrous. Walked over to the back seat, an officer makes sure he doesn't hit his head before shoving him in and shutting the door behind him sealing his freedom. Channel 4 News arrives to the act played out in this delusion that seems only dream real. Darnel, with tear blurry vision, takes one slight glance outside unwillingly letting the Channel 4 News camera get a shot of him before putting his head back down.] | | [While I sat there catching my breath, I got a hard on watching two people fuck in the woods. He must of heard me drop my lighter cause when I looked up a flash light was pointing in my direction. So I figured, why not add another body to my gun.] | 'Damn, I can't believe dat nigga wore light up shoes and fucked up my nyte! ...'
