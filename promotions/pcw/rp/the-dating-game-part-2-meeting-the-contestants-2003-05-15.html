The Dating Game Part 2 (Meeting the contestants)

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/5/03 Despite it being early morning, the television studio was alive with life, runners frantically tearing up and down corridors trying to get everything in order for the producers and directors before they were fired and their dreams of one day working more actively in television shot completely out of the window. Stood quietly leaning up against a wall is James Edwards he had been told to make himself look nice in the message he had received from Sharon but she had not specified exactly what defined as nice. He was standing there with his arms crossed against his chest the cotton from his plain white t-shirt which was tucked neatly into his dark blue denim jeans, although he thought that he looked reasonably dapper his demeanour would indicate that although he was a celebrity he would not dress like one and try to make himself over the top. A small man came bounding round the corner holding a series of note cards reading them frantically not looking where he was going exactly and smashing into James sending himself flailing like a rabbit in the headlights to the floor cursing frantically. Man: Hey your fired watch where you are going buddy. James looked down at the man, people always seemed to fall to the floor whenever they hit James people had a habit of just bouncing off of him, he extended his hand as he had down so many times and helped up the little man. Man: Yeah cheers but you’re still fired off you go The man who was now back on his feet began to walk off before James called out in his general direction in a hope to get his attention or something. James: Hey chief I’m here for the show I’m supposed to be one of the celebrity contestants or something here for the dating game. The man stopped dead in his tracks and underwent a transformation of sorts his whole attitude had changed in a moment, the small short, somewhat annoying man had somehow made himself a being of stature and a smarmy looking man with eyes which looked as though they had been involved with the devil at some point, probably making a bet with him or some sort of deal. Man: Ahhh Mr Edwards I’m glad you could make it at such short notice, I hope you understand that before was a simple mistake, you know how it is early in the morning too hyped up on sugar and caffeine wanting to get the show on the road. The man displayed a nervous laugh as James simply looked at the man who was fumbling his cards, well it was more of a deathly glare than a look as James simply burned a hole through the man’s forehead with his eyes, not literally of course you have to understand. Man: Anyway Mr Edwards I don’t have to tell you how great it is to have you here I know that you put your body on the line every week and it’s a great credit to your sport. ‘It’s a start’ thought James to himself nodding silently allowing a small smile to form on his face breaking the taking in the mans words of respect, he knew what James did and for that he had to be thankful, maybe being a celebrity was not such a bad thing after all. Man: Hell if I were in the rodeo I’d be shit scared the horse was going to land on me or something like that, has a horse ever landed on you? ‘Swing and a miss’ thought James as he sighed deeply, a sigh which made him re-think what exactly he was doing on this god forsaken show at seven o’ clock in the morning. Man: Anyway I just realised I haven’t introduced myself, I’m the shows host Richard Nobleman. James: That’s not a very German name. Richard: I know but this ships out to America and the networks decided that the name Luther Generalous was not exactly in line with what the chiefs had in mind so I’m Richard ok anyway I have to get ready for the show we begin filming in an hour so go do whatever you rodeo guys do to warm up, you’ll hear a bell ring in the green room when we are about to start filming. With that Richard or Luther as he had previously been known walked away from James and around the corridor leaving James propped up against the wall still somewhat bitter about the rodeo comment, it half crossed his mind to go out there in full wrestling attire and snap the mans neck…or at least put him in an STF for a few minutes until he worked out what he actually did for a living. James pushed himself up to a more vertical position using his shoulder as a lever and ambled nonchalantly to the so-called green room, which was more of a sea blue with a hazel wood finish. James entered through the door, which was slightly ajar and threw himself down carelessly on one of the purple beanbag style chairs, which engulfed James as he sat down, leading to a nasty sensation of being eaten alive on James’ part. He sat quietly for a moment mumbling to himself about rodeos and horses until the door flung open and two figures were standing at the door, turning his head to inspect who James was up against his jaw dropped slightly as he tried to take in who was standing before him. On the left was a man who was past his prime, but clearly trying to regain his past days of fame, his hair slicked back and wearing a black leather jacket with more wrinkles in than his face, the man looked at James who was still struggling to come to terms with the situation, raised his two thumbs and turned them outwards slightly before uttering a single word. Man 1: eyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy James turned his head slightly and looked at the other man who was somewhat taller than the first man his hair slicked back again but not the same effect as the first one it also had a slightly greasy look about it. Man 2: heeeeeeeeyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy The second mans tone a lot more whiny than the first, the sort of voice that you want to slap regardless of the owner. James swallowed deeply as the two men entered the room closely followed by Richard who was ushering them to chairs. Richard: Ah James you found your way here I just thought I would show the other two competitors here so you can have a little chit chat get to know each other build up a friendly little rivalry you know. The room fell silent, in the distant a cricket could be heard chirping. Richard: err anyway. James I want you to meet Henry Winkler. Henry: eyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy Richard: and David Schwimmer David: Heeeeeeyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy James stared blankly for a moment he looked first at Henry then at David before finally saying the only thing he could think of, the only thing, which would make sense in a situation like this. James: Hey.
