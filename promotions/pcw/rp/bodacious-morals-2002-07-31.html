Bodacious Morals

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 31 [What is the big big in getting drunk? You sit there and pound back alcoholic beverages, that aren't even good for you, just to get a feeling of release. I'm sure if someone smacked you in the head hard enough you could emulate that same feeling, but apparently that's not nearly as fun. To be considered cool, to be with the in crowd, or to have a party you need to have alcohol present. So, that's what the world seems to tell the general public with movies and television shows. People just need a reason to indulge themselves into a stupor and forget about their crappy sober lives. It's pretty sickening if you think about it long and hard] [The blue mustang, with yellow trim, pulls into the parking lot of Beer World. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy wearing his blue and yellow PCW shirt that reads, 'I'm Tubluar, And Your Not', sits behind the steering wheel looking a bit apprehensive about being in the parking lot of Beer World. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, Tommy's half-brother and tag team partner, eagerly counts his money and his bloodshot eyes look so excited and happy. Harry gets out of the car, wearing his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots, and turns back to Tommy] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: You want anything special, man? [Tommy just shakes his head as Harry practically runs into the beer surplus store] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Totally bogus... [Tommy just doesn't get why people need alcohol to have a good time. When you've got the Beach Boys playing, bodacious babes dancing, and an old guy who thinks he's still a teenager you've got yourself quite an entertaining party. You don't need alcohol to make the fun any better. The only thing alcohol will cause is the loss of morals, hangovers, and totally bogus breath. It adds too many negatives to increase fun factor] [Tommy remembers back to a most gnarly bash that was going on in Santa Monica when they graduated high school. The Beach Boys played on, bodacious babes danced, and there was a middle aged dude in a leisure suit who thought he could still be gnarly with the babes. It was turning into a most happening event. Then Brock Johnson, the high school quarterback and Tommy's nemesis, brought a bunch of kegs. The dancing bodacious babes got totally trashed and couldn't really dance all that well. The football team started trying to fight jellyfish and Harry threw up in the punch bowl which Holly Smith drank out of, which made her spew onto the stereo system, which caused a short circuit, and it electrocuted the dude in the leisure suit launching him onto a crowd of bodacious babes. It was a most bogus accident which was caused by the appearance of alcohol] Girl's voice: Can you buy us beer? [Tommy was about to give a most needed tongue lashing but the appearance of two BODACIOUS babes stopped his rant. The girl leaning on his car nearly took his breath away. She was very tall and had a very nice set of legs. Very tan. She wore very low cut grey nylon shorts that stopped at the top of her thighs. If this bodacious brunette were to bend over there would surely be some asscheek hanging out. Her face was very slender, just like her body. High cheekbones, not too much makeup, and her lips were pouty and red. Tommy was amazed by this brown haired vixen. The blonde girl was very beautiful but it was hard to compare her to the brown haired vixen. The blonde girl was a lot shorter, but had a nice voluptous back side, a nice rack, and she had a combination of blue eye shadow that faded into purple. She was most bodacious in the end] TTT: We'll have to see about that, bodacious babe. [Harry trots back to the car carrying three cases of Rolling Rock. His mind is racing so happily about the beer and the celebration that he doesn't notice two very hot babes standing only feet away from him. He looks up and he drops the beer on his right foot, but it has absolutely no affect on him] TTT: DUDE!! You dropped the beer on your foot! Harry: ...... Brown haired girl: Is he going to be all right? TTT: Yeah, he'll be fine. Why don't you babes hop in the most tubular ride in LA and we'll go back to my pad. [The two girls look at each other and smile. Tommy celebrates inside his head because that's always a good sign] Brown haired girl: SURE! [The two girls hop in the backseat of the car, ready to get going. Harry just stands there staring at the two girls with the three cases of beer crushing his right foot] TTT: Dude, would you snap out of it?! We've got a most heinous match against Pretty Cool Guys for the number one contendership. You don't need to get hurt, compadre. Harry: ...... [Tommy gets out of the car and puts the beer in the trunk. Harry still stands frozen outside of the car so Tommy picks him up and sits him down. Harry faces the windshield but quickly turns his head back to the very hot babes in the backseat. Tommy slams the car in gear and they head out of the parking lot toward Tommy's most gnarly pad] TTT: Babes, I have to just say that your looking most excellent. Brown hair and blonde haired girl: Thank you. TTT: There are a ton of most gnarly chicks around LA but it's not everyday that you are in the Beer World parking lot and two of the most rad women I've ever seen in my life approach my car. I have to say that I was a bit taken back by your beauty. [The two girls giggle in the back seat, looking at each other with devilish smiles. Tommy notices in his rearview mirror that the blonde haired girl is making tonguing signals to him. It looks like this may be most tubular after all] Brown haired girl: So, you guys are wrestlers? TTT: Yeah, we're the Cowabunga Connection from PCW and we are TOTALLY bodacious in a far out way when it comes to kicking ass. After Friday we're going to get our number one contendership for the tag team titles and then coast that gnarly wave to win the titles that are rightfully ours. Right, Harry? Harry: ...... Blonde Haired girl: Is he mute? TTT: No, he's just extremely stoned. So where are you ladies from? Brown haired girl: We actually just live up the hill from the Beer World. TTT: Gnarly. So, I'm guessing that you couldn't afford the beer. We can hook you up, babes. Blonde haired girl: We can afford it. TTT: Oh. Well, how old are you babes then? 19, 20 years old? Brown haired girl: 15. [The mustang skids off of the freeway and onto the shoulder. Tommy quickly recovers and jumps back on the freeway] TTT: 15?!?!? TOTALLY BOGUS!! [The blonde haired girl begins massaging Tommy's shoulders. Tommy has a look of fear on his face. Normally this would be a most bodacious thing but right now it's starting to feel a bit uncomfortable. He's 24 and a grown man. He's accomplished a lifetime of achievements and he's got a young teenage girl feeling up his musclar shoulders. This is wrong, this is so wrong. This is so horribly bogus that words can't even describe it, even though they already have] TTT: Listen, babes. I think you need to know a little something about the law and about morals. I'm 24 and I don't think it's going to be right for 15 year old dudettes like yourselves to be around someone that is 24. [The brown haired girl begins masssaging Harry's shoulders. Harry looks like he is about to explode by the gigantic smile across his face. He is in ecstasy but he shouldn't. He should be feeling the same thing Tommy is] Brown haired girl: Tommy, there is nothing wrong with indulging in something younger. We may be 15 but it's just an age, honey. It's not like this whole thing is going to be broadcast on television. [Tommy looks into the camera with fright on his face] Blonde haired girl: Seriously. You need to loosen up and just let us take care of you two. Trust us. If we get our hands all over you then you'll be locked to get that number one contendership for the tag team titles. You should pull over at a motel and we can get IT ON!! [Tommy now has sweat pouring down from his brow. They have brews in the trunk and two very young teenage girls in the backseat. They are getting back massages from these young teenage girls and they WANT TO GET IT ON!] TTT: This is just BOGUS!! You babes are going to have to go back home. You need to find dudes closer to your age. This is just against my morals. I can't be involved with a 15 year old dudette and be able to live with myself. Do I look like one of those totally bogus pedophiles!! NO WAY!! [The blonde girl stops massaging and leans into the backseat, obviously she is let down. The brown haired girl keeps massaging Harry as his face is filled with absolute pleasure. He is surely floating on cloud nine at this point] [Tommy smacks him in the shoulder to bring him to his senses] TTT: DUDE!! That is a most bogus thing to do. We don't need to give these babes the wrong idea, and we sure as hell don't need to corrupt them. [Harry looks hurt and apalled that Tommy would stop such a thing as this] Brown haired girl: We thought that you were all about getting down to business, Tommy. TTT: Babe, I am about getting down to business with bodacious babes that are around my most gnarly age range, not young dudettes who just got off of their training wheels. Maybe in 5 years, babes. [The Tubular One drops the young babes off at the Beer World parking lot so that they can journey back up the hill to their home. Tommy wishes them well in their future endeavors and not to get mixed up with older compadres because it just spells trouble. They seemed to not really care] [On the ride home Harry has his arms crossed and a very visible scowl across his face] TTT: Dude, now all we have to worry about is getting that number one contender spot on Fusion, compadre. We were left out at Wrestle Vessel and we watched on monitors in the backstage, but now we're getting back in the ring to show the world that we are two most bodacious dudes and the greatest tag team in the history of WRESTLING! [AT this point Harry would add, 'Yeah, man' to the mix but he just stares ahead with the scowl tatooed on his face] TTT: Are you pissed because I took those little girls home? Harry: YEAH, man!! TTT: They were 15, dude. That's totally bogus. You need to have some sort of morals, dude. When you were 9 they were just being born, dude. When I was with my first bodacious babe at the age of 14 they were only 3 years old. They were still in diapers when I was around their age. That's BOGUS!! Harry: That's when you use your imagination, man. Who cares how old they are. It's just a number, man. They were hot!! TTT: It's over with, dude. They are back home and we're going to get ready for Fusion and be totally gnarly like we always are. Harry: I hate you.... [The scene fades to black]
