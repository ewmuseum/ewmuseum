The Death Of Rent-A-Hero-Part 2-Losing The One You Love

27 June 2002

Title: The Death Of Rent-A-Hero-Part 2-Losing The One You Love Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 6-27-2002 OOC:I don't know what you people will think of this...but thiswas one of the most hardest things I ever had to write. I hope this gets the proper amount of reading it deserves. Thank you... It took a series of three events to effectively murder PCW's only superhero....now as I mentioned in the eulogy of our fallen hero, I would not stand and watch his death go unavenged. You people are responsible for his fall... His descent from the heavens.... The eternal slumber he now lives with EVERYDAY... And for that, the only true torture that I could imagine would avenge him properly, would be to subject you all to his final dying moments. All of you soulless monsters can sit and watch...watch as an innocent man dies right before you...They always say, 'Never look in the eyes of the ones you kill....because the stare they return to you....a stare of inevitable death, will haunt you FOREVER.' If that is the case, I hope every last one of you watching this will never be able to have a sound sleep again....I hope everytime you even close your eyes to blink, his sea blue eyes stare right back into yours and scare you to death. I don't want to do this....but in order for his vengence to be carried out... It must....It all started at 'Crystal Beach', the beauteous golden sand beach on the outskirts of the city...there would be where the slow chemical that was destroying Rent-A-Hero, would begin it's conquest...See you in hell... Day 1-Crystal Beach... ..::/::..Our story begins under a blanker of twinkling stars, all shining down upon the city and its inhabitants as the night sky is now in total control, the moon taking over the shift for the now resting sun. Our focus however...is not in the city, but at the beach....Crystal Beach. The waves thunder in, smashing against the sandy coasts as golden grains of rough sand morph into a soggy mashes of sand, the crashing of the waves making a wonderful and relaxing sound, a harmonic massage to the ears. And in the middle of all this, laying comfortably in each others arms under a blue superman blanket with red and yellow 'S-Shield' imprints scattered about it, are none other then our very own Rent-A-Hero and his sacred love, Amy 'Aimz' Campbell. Aimz turns so she is looking right into our hero's eyes, still enwrapped about her bare waist by the strong spandex covered arms of Rent-A-Hero, donning his black and white Superman costume with a matching mask. As usual, our hero is estatically happy when with his love, his face bright and full of joy like a newborn as he sighs comfortably at the feel of Aimz's soft fingers invading and caressing his blonde locks. Aimz on the other hand, has a strange look in her dark eyes....a look as if she could cry at any moment....a look which is strange for even thoguh she is within the arms of the man she starts and ends the days with the words 'I love you'...it looks as if she is being murdered on the inside by something unknown. Aimz brushes back a loose strand of her wild red hair and kiss our hero softly on his full lips, breaking it and looking. to him, quietly inquiring as she continues to caress his hair..::/::..Aimz:'Are you okay Rentie?' ..::/::..Even leaning on his side, our hero's smile looks just as joyful and exuberant as if he were standing tall with his chest poked out, answering in a low romantic tone while still gazing into the eyes of Aimz, lost in all that is her..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'How can I not be my love...I'm prepared and ready to battle the Crusaders Of Light, I'm syked and at my highest Energon level to take the tag team titles with Snow at Gold & Glory, and I'm here with you...life couldn't get any better. Everything is in full circle Amy. On Fusion, I battle the Crusaders of Light alongside Snow. They rpeach and speak of saving the PCW and saving truth within it's walls....first of, THAT'S MY JOB, and they need to stop trying to steal it before they recieve a BIZNITCH SLAP O' DOOM FOR THEIR TROUBLES!! Second of all, WHERE ARE THEIR COSTUMES, WHERE ARE THEIR SUPERPOWERS, AND DO THEY EVEN HAVE A CATCHPHRASE?! They're no real heroes...just super-zeroes and I wiill put and end to their super-trickery and falsehoods alongside Snow, for the SUPERIOR HEROES FOR RENT are not a force to be messed with. The Crusaders need to worry about who is going to save THEM after they recieve this Kryptonian BEATING on Fusion. As for Gold & Glory, those tag team titles are AWAITING credibilty and rightful owners in this company. Personally, I don't think a tandem of surfers, filthy politicians, or a pair of 'really close' guys obssessed with hearts are going to do tose belts proper justice. I fear not the men who are in that ring and one by one, they shall be subjcted to Snow and Rent-A-Hero's COMBINED SUPER SAIEN KAMEHAMEHA POWERS OF TAG TEAM-NESS!!! We don't care what team it is, even if it is the army of one, Akiyama...they shall recieve justice in equal amounts and when the tag war is over, Rent-A-Hero and Snow shall stand tall with those tag team titles. And as for you....you've always made me happy Aimz...incredibly happy...' ..::/::..Aimz's face contorts into a guilty smile, her eyes beginning to water and her voice slightly chokign as they lay together, Aimz placing a finger over his lips and sniffing lightly before answering..::/::..Aimz:'Stop...please stop...' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero takes no heed to her request, kissing her finger and moving it gently from his lips, preferring to lock hands with her instead as he sits up just a little. Amy follows suit, wondering as to what's going on as Rent-A-Hero takes grasp of her hand with a loving tightness, looking right into her eyes....the world seems to go silent just for this one moment and the stars shine just the right amount of spotlight on our hero as he keeps his stare locked....clearing his throat as he fights to say the right things..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Amy...you are the greatest thing that has ever happened to me. From the moment I met you in Wal-Mart, fending off security guards with your fighting talents, to the time we shared intimate pleasure together...to right now, you have ALWAYS made me happy. You've told me so much about men who have hurt you, men who have treated you with such unimportance...and I ponder with every last Rent-A-Sense why so....do they not see what I see? I see a beautiful, smart, caring young woman who has given a hero more reasons to want to be a better person and has given him the capacity to share his feeling with another. Amy...you have changed my life so profoundly and a day without you...is a lifetime in the land of Bizarro World. Last night, when I laid next to you and felt your warm body against mine...I knew I couldn't let you go...I knew I wanted to eb there for you...I knew I wanted to protect you.'Amy:'Rentie...what's going on?' *trembling slightly* Rent-A-Hero:'What's going on Amy...is....I can't stand you being just a 'Lois Lane' anymore. It's not enough...I need you, forever. I want to settle down. I want to have kids. I want to own a home with a white picket fence and a Superman electronic gate. But most of all...I want someone to share all of that with. I have been told,'If you have lived life without love...you never lived it.'.....::/::..Our hero pauses slightly, Aimz's hands trembling within his. Like a good man, our hero squeezes just a little tigher, rubbing the top of her hand gently wiht his thumb to calm her as Aimz grows ever so much more nervous. At that moment...the world slows. Our hero gets on both of his knees, unearthing a small black box and opening it slowly before her..::/::.. ..::/::..Two 12K platinum rings set within this soft black jewelry box...one thicker than the othe rbut both equally precious and so bright the stars above have to put on shades to look at them. Within each, are pleathora's of tiny diamonds surrounding one large diamond in each, our hero licking his lips and looking deep within Aimz's eyes....he knew and she knew this one moment would changes EVERYTHING..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Amy Campbell....my heartbeat...my love....my friend...my everything....will you marry me?' ..::/::..Silence was the mood, our hero's breaths getting heavier each time for every second she was silent. Aimz stares right back into the sea blue eyes she has grown to love so much, her silence brooding as her eyes grow watery....a single tear running down her eye and making a downward spiral down her cheek until it plummets off tot he sand below. She could only imagine how many times she said to herself,'No man would ever want to marry me,' and now right before...her destiny awaits. The silence is soon broken...with the words..::/::.. Aimz:'We need to go home...'Rent-A-Hero:'Is that the new way of saing yes on this planet!?' *smiles* Aimz:'Rentie...we need to go home.' Rent-A-Hero: *reluctantly* 'Right away then...' ..::/::..With no argument to Aimz' request, our hero stands up along with Amy, closing the black ring box and lifting the beach towel from up off the sand. Aimz has her arms folded and her head down as she heads back up qith quickened steps, something just not right within her as Rent-A-Hero shortly follows, his eyes filled with worry and fear of rejection...the perfect moment, delayed..::/::.. Back At His Apartment.... ..::/::..Back at our hero's humble abode, the door swings open wide, Aimz stepping through with arms still folded and Aimz rushing into the bedroom, still not a word said to Rent-A-Hero as if he was not worthy of acknowledgement. With a sigh, he places the beach towel down on the couch, still holding the ring box in hand as he just stares at it, pondering what he could have done wrong. As he heads into his bedroom, light barely seeping in through the blinds of his windows, he asks..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Amy...what did I d...' ..::/::..Upon stepping into his bedroom...he is interuppted...not by a clever Aimz-ism....but by her herself...standing before him in just a lacy black bra with a matching thong with her eyes reddened from tears, her body a scuplture of perfection and just not the type you could say 'no' to as the two garments mentioned hug her sensuous curves. She takes slight steps towards Rent-A-Hero, our hero still confused as to all that has happened. He dosen't know what to think of not having his proposal answered, only to be confronted by the wanting body of Amy. He wants to speak, but is cut off with a passionate kiss with Aimz holding his head, tears starting to flow from her closed eyes as Rent-A-Hero gives into the deep kiss. Fighting is among the last things on his mind, his hands instinctively around her waist as the kiss deepens, Aimz looking to Rent-A-Hero with a confusing look...one of complete and total happiness yet incredible sadness...as if she was going to see him for the very last time...finally, the words escape her mouth as she makes her mellow request..::/::.. Aimz:'Make love to me...' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero says it himself often,'Whatever shes wants...I will give to her...and if I don't have it, I will see she does.' And so...with just a quiet nod, he kisses her yet again, taking her into his arms and kicking the door shut behind him as Aimz holds him tightly, returning our hero's physical display of love with every ounce of her being....the two of them drifting into what could be described as...an ominous night of total ecstacy.... The Next Day... ..::/::..The sun has risen....light showers the room of Rent-A-Hero as he lays comfortably, his head sunk into his Kirby's Dreamland pillow as the sheets are strewn all over from last night. Shortly...a stray beam of light hits the eye of our hero...opening them slowly as he blinks. Groggily, he shakes his head, his vision clearing until the world is not a blur, but the wonderland he awakes to everyday. With a smile, he looks over to his right, looking to awake his princess with a kiss....but.... She's not there. Our hero's eyes widen with quiet shock as he exits the bed, looking about at the floor...no trace of her clothing, no garments of Aimz from the night before laying loosely. Desperately...he rushes to the closet, opening it wide...only to find her clothes....from the baggy cargo pants to the glittery triangle tops....gone. He rushes to the dresser, opening EVERY last drawer in desperation of something that is Aimz related...yet he finds absolutely nothing. Our hero steps back, sits on his bed with a straight face, and looks out to the sun....deep in his heart, he knew the nigth before meant something. It meant the future would change for the both of them... It just turned out... Their future was not together... And with that, our hero laid on his bed, not letting a tear slip, not breaking his silence....yet shortly, it is broken...with only a few simple words..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'You could have at least said goodbye princess...' ..::/::..With that, our hero...broken and without his love, just lays himself down in silence. Not the normal exuberant man we know him as...but a broken shell of a man who knew love...and then lost it. He knew deep inside, that the downward spiral would begin..and he realized that no pain could ever begin to match what this feels like. With that he knew as he gazed into the sun....that his death would come soon. You know what else he knew...he knew... The he was drawing very close to the very last Rent-A-Time...
