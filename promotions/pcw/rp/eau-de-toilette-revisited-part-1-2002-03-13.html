Bertha: You said we were f*cking going out to eat tonight, and I sure as f*ck am not going to spend the mother f*cking whore @$$ night lying on the ground, b*tch! As the Chef's Militant Islamic garb scrapes against the concrete sidewalk, he looks up into the sky to see a familiar number. The Chef: Praise Allah! It's 69 69th Street! My restaurant!!! Bertha: Restaurant!?!? Bertha lets go of the Chef and stares at the building across the street. The Chef: At last, I have returned to Eau De Toilette! The Chef looks down from the sky and gazes at the building... The Chef: Great Muhammed's ghost! Bertha: What's the f*cking problem!?!? Is it f*cking delapidated f*cko? The Chef: Worse...It's a DENNY'S!!! AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! The Chef prostrates himself on the concrete again, only this time slamming his fists upon the cold, unforgiving surface. The Chef: Why Allah!?!? Why!?!? The clone reaches over to comfort the Chef. Bertha: Don't worry honey, we'll get the restaurant back the way you wanted it to be... The Chef: By constantly praying to Allah until something happens? Bertha pauses for a moment. Bertha: No!!! We do things my f*cking way! Bertha pulls a rolling pin out of her pants. The Chef, seeing the culinary weapon of destruction, smiles, but then grimaces with disgust. The Chef: Where were you hiding that rolling pin? As Bertha begins to answer, the Chef interrupts. The Chef: Nevermind, I don't want to know... Bertha: Well, I don't f*cking stick it up my pleasure hole if that's what you are f*cking asking f*cko! That spot's reserved for you and the f*cking occasional roll of Bounty! The Chef: What? Bertha: Well, it's Playtex's f*cking fault, they don't make big enough Tampons! Bertha: There, don't you f*cking feel better now? Bertha sets the Chef back down. The Chef: Well that my lunch is secure, let's retake my restaurant! The Chef raises his left arm in a display of militancy, Bertha follows by raising of the rolling pin. The duo charges towards the restaurant, ignoring all street signs along the way as the camera mercifully fades to black... Narrator: Will the Chef and Bertha be able to conquer Denny's?!?!?
