Emptiness (The Story of Jacksione Rusty and Serik Erracles) Part II

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1899 Isolation is his only choice. Serik Erracles have been alone for twenty years and he must be alone now. Away from the celebration on the flat land below. Here in mountain of Olympus, Erracles is free. Just like the Greek gods that once lived in this place in splendor and ease, he would feel the same way. Regardless of the arctic winds of the mountain, he will feel warmth. Regardless of the insignificance inside him by being alone in a dark, harsh mountain, he will be the quintessence of importance. With Jacksione Rusty’s lose, came Serik Erracles triumph! The Rising Star is now Erracles’ possession! Serik Erracles is not afraid anymore. He just became the absolute being of the world. Even bigger than King Creon! No struggle is there he cannot face. “The vanquisher is I!” Serik shouts amidst the harsh blizzard of the remorseless mountain, lifting the Rising Star up in the heavens. Serik suddenly skids, but the power of the Rising Star made Serik stand at will after his fall. “Is that all you got?” he taunts the gods. “All of you are not even worth my time!” This man is possessed! How dare Serik mock the gods? “Who are you?” a man suddenly screeched beyond the horizon. “Why have you stolen my possession?” Serik Erracles’ eyes move upward to the summit of the mountain. A man standing strong amidst the violent blizzard is staring at him. The darkness coalesced with the whiteout was enough to make identifying difficult. How could he possibly know who this man is, waiting for him on top of the mountain. It was impossible. But Serik knows. Somehow Serik knows that it was Jacksione Rusty waiting for him. The Rising Star told him so. “My name is Serik Erracles! And I did not steal your Rising Star, I took it from you like it was nothing!” “How dare you use the Rising Star for your own good. You cannot possibly last in this forbidden mountain if it weren’t for my possession. And your blind mind cannot possibly grasp the meaning and significance of that gold. Give it back, Serik, before the gods punish you for your sins.” An inferno of wisdom was coming out of Jacksione’s mouth as the blizzard seems to not affect him at any rate. He sure is the chosen one. “The gods are just like you Jacksione, weak and ignorant. Sure, you knew I would come here. Sure, you knew I pilfered your most precious talisman. But what makes you think you would survive in this place with me on your way. You are foolish, Jacksione. Now you must die.” But instead of Serik Erracles’ making a move, it is himself that got hurt. Jacksione is still more powerful than him. Serik falls into the ashen snow and breaks his wrist. A shout of pain is release from his mouth. Erik stands up holding the Rising Star, hopeless and marred. Looks like it is Serik that is about to die. “Noooooo! By the clout of this magical gold in my hand, I covet and demand to be healed!” And then he is healed. “Stop it, Serik. Just give it up!” Jacksione starts to gait away from the summit and reach out for Serik down below. “Do not use the Rising Star for blasphemy no longer.” “Tell that to the deities.” And then Serik throws the magical item into the storm below. The gold caught by the unforgiving winds down to the destiny of the unknown. “What have you done?” Jacksione rushes toward the very same direction but then stumbles down the mountain along with the golden traitor. What a way to breathe his last. And then it is finally over. ~ o ~ o ~ o ~ Despite all the laughters he created, all the jokes he crafted, all the people he healed, all the mountains he moved, all the stars he raised, there was this indication of infertility and barrenness inside Jacksione that only one true God can elaborate on. Everything the prophecy said came to be. A man surrounded by greatness came to this town, Weesville, just like predicted. Everything he was presumed to do, he has done – and has done with purpose. And then a villain came, suddenly struck by Zeus’ malevolent thunder and took the life of the supreme being that served his principle for five years. But one part of the prophecy was a lie. It said after his demise, he will not be empty, eternally. Boy, was the prophecy flawed and erroneous. Looks like Jacksione Rusty was empty all along.
