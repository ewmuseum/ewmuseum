Ragamuffin's Revenge - Part 2 - Visiting Shai

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 20, 2002 [Shailyn Matthews now knows what it's like to be used. As she arrives back to her tiny apartment, that sits next to train tracks and is above a young couple who enjoys shooting their own porno movies, she begins wondering what life would be like if she didn't stick by her religion, her faith. Would she be stuck working at some lawyers office, with all those ugly lawyers gawking at her, trying to get her to do things with them? Would she be out of the sardine can they is called an apartment and in a beautiful home, that's cozy and comfortable? A place where she doesn't have to worry about whether or not she's going to get raped or robbed, and so she doesn't have to worry about some stupid train barreling by at two o'clock in the morning!! She takes off her beige trenchcoat and throws it on the old green, floral patterned chair. She wears a white blouse and a black skirt that goes down right to the tops of her ankles. Her slender body and tight curves make it look as if she is some kind of big time movie star or model, but she is far from it. Her very pretty face, with very little make up, attracts light to her face and makes her seem so angelic. How ironic, huh? This woman who does right in the name of God, for her religion. She's never had sex before, in fact, she's never touched another man in her life. The good book says that you should wait until marriage, and that's exactly when she'll do it. That's her dilemma now, because it's beginning to ruin her life. All the goals that she is setting out to do are becoming harder to reach because of her strong belief in God. She doesn't want to defy him, but she does not want to fail in life. She was, and still is, in love with a man, a very special man. His name...'Totally Tubular' Tommy, PCW superstar and surfing legend. He was a perfect gentleman and he was one of the funniest men she has ever met. Such a handsome man, actually the most handsome man she has ever dated. She was so happy with him and saw a bright future with him. Even though she wasn't happy with Tommy's profession, she could get by that because of her love for him. Right when she thought things were going to come to a bright point, a man came along and destroyed everything. That man was Randy Raggagu, AKA Ragamuffin. He got her drunk and tried to seduce her at a party, a party that Tommy threw himself. The Tubular One walked in on the horrible situation and knew that Shailyn was just a liar, another babe trying to cash in on him. Her head was spinning too much to figure out what was going on. Luckily, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, Tommy's younger brother, accidentally came to the rescue by nailing Ragamuffin with his cowboy boot. Shai got away but her relationship with Tommy was completely destroyed. He never returned her calls and he never would talk to her again. She was supposed to be office manager of a major medical practice and she declined to job to wallow in her misery. She has blamed Ragamuffin every single day for every single hardship that she has faced since that night, and if she ever sees him again...she will make sure that the devil has his way with him] Voice: Shailyn. [The woman jumps in fright as she stumbles into the corner of the living room. A dark figure is diagonal from her, hiding in the darkness. The figure is quite tall and quite skinny, but nothing else can be made out. Shailyn gasps for air, so shocked that there is some type of intruder in her house. She knows that this is it, she is going to die now. This man is going to rape her and kill her. She'll be all over the news and her parents will see that they were right about her moving from North Dakota to California. She begins crying] Voice: Shailyn, I am sorry for scaring you. There is no need to worry about anything. [The man flicks the light switch on the off-white wall, which leads into the three foot wide kitchen. The light reveals a face that Shailyn would never dared to have seen. The extremely large Independent T-shirt, the extremely baggy cargo pants, and the crappy Independent baseball cap. IT'S RAGAMUFFIN!!] Shailyn: YOU!!! [The tears still stream down her face, but it's no longer in fear...it's of anger!] Shailyn: YOU!! I hate you! [She begins making her way over toward him. It seems the tables have no turned, as Ragamuffin is the one who is feeling the fear. The demented look in her beautiful brown eyes only shows that she should be the one locked up in the looney bin, and not Ragamuffin. Her hangs begin to clench into a fist, ready to take some sort of action. Ragamuffin figures that this might have been a really bad idea] Ragamuffin: Shai, I know that you love Tommy and all, but there are things that you need to know about him! He's a horrible person! He was only going to use you, take your most sacred gift away from you and toss you to the side just like he's done with so many others. You were just going to be a very prestigious trophy in his case, and you know full well how much of a trophy collector the man is!! That night I was only trying to protect you, to save you from his evil clutches. I guess I was too late... [Ragamuffin only holds his head down is failure. Even through his madness he still had a soft spot for certain people, actually all people except Tommy and his follower brother. Those two morons who do nothing but bring misery to others lives. Shailyn slowly lets her fists unclench, knowing full well that she wasn't going to do a thing anyway. She doesn't like violence, and even though she wants to see him suffer, that's not the way God taught his children. Ragamuffin needs someone to help him, but she thought that he WAS getting help?] Shai: I thought that you were in a mental institution? [Randy looks back up with a blank stare in his eyes] Ragamuffin: I was, but I broke out. There are two people who need my help from Tommy and his stupid brother. I need to make sure that the wrongs are right in their case. I can't let anymore people get hurt by that HORRIBLE man! [For some reason Shailyn gets this feeling that she believes this man, trusts the words that flow from his mouth. She always thought Ragamuffin to be quite odd and so against Tommy, but maybe Randy is right...maybe Tommy is really just a horrible person] Shailyn: But why are you here? Ragamuffin: I thought you could tell me where Tommy is at, so I can stop him. Shailyn: Why not go to his agent? Ragamuffin: Your his girlfriend, I would figure you would know his whereabouts. Shailyn: I'm not his girlfriend anymore...that ended the night you were 'apparently' with me. [Ragamuffin looks surprised by the words. He never knew that Tommy and Shai were broken up. He knew that he caused some intense friction and gave Tommy some hurt for a while, but he never knew that they were broken up. His heart begins to feel that familiar feeling, that feeling it hasn't felt for such a long time...that feeling of love. Before he gets too caught up in it he pushes it away and stays on his task] Ragamuffin: I'm sorry to hear that, Shai. I didn't mean to hurt you in anyway...just Tommy. [Shai forces a smile] Shai: I know...I guess certain things happen for a reason. But...I do know where Tommy is at. [Ragamuffin sighs glad to have the whereabouts of that scumbucket, Tommy. To know finally take care of him to save two lives from becoming totally destroyed. Saving two careers that will have such a bright future, as long as some dumb surfer doesn't cross through their path. Ragamuffin begins to sidetrack as he notices the beauty of Shai. Her pink lips parting slowly into a half smile, and the way that it begins to even light up the most horrible of places. With her beautiful smile he begins to feel security, a sense of accomplishment, and knowing full well that he is going to be able to stop The Tubular One] Ragamuffin: Where has he gone to? Shai: He's going to be in Toronto for a big autograph signing, and then once that is over he begins his way over to North York, Ontario for Friday Fusion. Then, of course, back to Toronto for the Pay Per View.... [Randy begins going toward the door, knowing that time is short] Shai: Be careful, Randy!! Ragamuffin: Shai, it doesn't matter. I'm an escaped mental patient...if I get hurt or killed it is not going to matter. I'm a man with nothing to lose...I just want to make sure good people don't get hurt anymore.... Shai: Who? Ragamuffin: Sandy Wong and The End... [The scene fades and comes back in to Andy's Beer Store Emporium, eh. There is a very large line out in the cold for one man named 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. PCW fans are pumped to have the Pay Per View in their city and meeting one of the wrestlers is far out in a major way! Inside, its freezing because of a broken heater and Tommy is forced to wear three coats and two pairs of gloves just to keep from freezing to death. Harry just sits in his short sleeved Twisted Sister T-shirt and black leather pants like nothing is wrong. He shows no signs of freezing, but his bloodshot eyes show sign of marijuana use. Tommy signs his picture and tosses the pictures back at the people, hurrying the line along. People try to snap photos but Tommy hides his face. Heinous Canadians!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: This is going to be most bogus, Har. I'm trying to concentrate on destroying The End and getting you Sandy Wong! Now Ragamuffin's out of that totally heinous institution and now we've got police protection all over us! [Standing behind the former Cowabunga Connection is a mountie, who has not moved in well over half an hour. Tommy tosses his signed photos at his fans, not even saying hello to them. Lets face it, they are Canadian. Who really wants to greet a dirty, smelly Canadian. Not Tommy. He probably would greet dudes and dudettes in the States, but who knows what he'd do...matters if he was with a bodacious babe the night before or not] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: I can't believe that dude still has a most bogus vendetta against us. He only kidnapped you and like anally raped you...TALK ABOUT HEINOUS! Harry: He didn't rape me, man. TTT: Dude...how come you were having pain in your butthole? Harry: Roids, man. [Tommy nods, and then shrugs as he just scribbles jibberish on his photo and hands it to an eight year old child] Boy: I love you, Tommy! [Tommy shrugs] TTT: Still...that dude is most disturbed and I hope they catch him soon. It is MOST DIFFICULT to land a bodacious Canadian babe with these bogus mounties always around us. It's almost been 24 hours since I had a gnarly session of Yahtzee. I've got a lot of extra stress building up and I need release. Working out and training can't fully satisfy it, I need YAHTZEE!! I NEED IT, DUDE!! Harry: Calm down, man. [Tommy begins writing Harry's name on his picture and handing it to people. He doesn't even make eye contact with them, just looking over at his brother. Every once in a while he'll look back at the large mountie who just scans the room for a possible Ragamuffin sighting] Harry: Do you think that Sandy Wong likes to be on top or the bottom, man? TTT: That's a most bodacious question, Har. I'm glad that you asked it. Most babes are aprehensive at first when it comes to being with a new compadre. Harry then looks over at Tommy with a blank stare on his face] Harry: What were you saying, man? I was checking out that cardboard cut out chick.... [Tommy sighs and shakes his head] Meanwhile....
