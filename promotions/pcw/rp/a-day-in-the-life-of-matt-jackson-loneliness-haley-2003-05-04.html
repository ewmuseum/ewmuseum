A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Loneliness: Haley

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/03 Loneness can do weird things to people…make them take revenge for a person causing it. Stone Burns is a lonely man and thus he has sick fantasies…fantasies of killing me. They were nothing more then mere fantasies because when we stepped into the PCW ring; he just didn’t have it. He found out challenging Christian MacManus wasn’t something that you should do lightly. Daring to dig deep into the archives of my life…claiming he can kill the same way I do, but it’s just in a different fashion. Stone Burns is nothing more then a ploy…filler in my life. He can’t stand the fact that he is failing in the PCW so soon and I am having the great successes of life. Wondering how exactly a man who is nothing more then a trained killer can move up so quickly…simple because there is more to the killer then meets the eye. Christian MacManus is more then a man running around with a knife slicing women. I killed to protect national security, to protect the people of this country…people like Stone Burns. To protect a man that hates women and kills them for no other reason than he likes it…a murder? I think so. Christian MacManus like Stone Burns? If killing to protect the country makes me a murder then I think so. I fear Stone Burns? I fear no one…not even Lady Death. Countless people have tried to kill Matt Jackson…Christian MacManus. Not one has gotten the job done, but Stone Burns believes he can be different…a stand out in this faceless society. The only problem is that Stone is doing this all on false pretenses and misinformation. Believing that I fear him one-on-one…which isn’t even close to being true. I don’t fear a man I saw nothing of until last Fusion…barely getting a win over Dan. I don’t fear a rusty old knife…when I have a brand new automatic pistol and I don’t fear a man who murders women…when I have pulled the trigger on them myself. -Christian Michael MacManus ** Matt Jackson’s apartment is a small cozy one…the walls a soft off-white colour and are decorated by pictures of Matt and his family, each in it’s own metal frame that fits so snuggly on the wall. A large leather couch is against the back wall, with a average sized window above it; the pillows of the couch are out of place and tossed around…the blanket at the end of it, is twisted and hanging down. The rest of the room is in the same disarray. The oak table in front of the couch is covered in empty glasses and dirty plates and the two leather recliners on the other side of the table are covered in dirty clothes and papers. Lying on the couch is Matt’s princess…Haley Hilton. Her blonde hair is scattered around the pillow behind her head and her petite arm hangs down to the wooden floor. Her silk pajamas are wrinkled and the black marks under her eyes show her lack of sleep over the past few days. She rolls over and reaches out for the bottle of vodka that sits on the table. Pulling it closer to her she notices that it’s almost empty and then the pounding headache sets in. “How much did I have to drink last night?” she questions aloud…her voice cracking the entire time, letting her really taste the dryness of her mouth. Sitting up Haley covers her slender face with her hands and begins to sob. Everything in her world is slowing falling a part and the only shoulder she leans on is gone…she is alone. For the first time ever in her life she is alone. Her family is trying to keep her away from the man she loves and the man she loves is being held hostage somewhere…because of her father. Why can’t anything work for me…first I lost my job at the firm, because of my father. Then when I finally get a job on my own, he tries to keep me away from it…I chose to step into that ring and face the consequences. It was my doing and not Matt’s, but my dad just doesn’t get it. She continues to let the tears fall down her cheeks, like an angel crying for a lost soul. Her eyelids get heavy as she lays back down on the couch sobbing. She doesn’t want to go to Turmoil this week…it means that she will have to stay in the hands of Mark Makonie. As per one of her father’s stipulations, she must me at all PCW cards and in the eyesight of Makonie or he would kill Matt. A part of her wants to go though, at least that way maybe she can see Matt…even if it’s just for a second. I want to make sure he is all right, not just from the men and Makonie, but also because of Stone Burns. The guy is a psycho and worse yet he is a murder. Then again Matt isn’t a saint himself, but I have never seen him bring a weapon to a PCW event like Mr. Burns did. Looking around the room with her pale blue eyes, she is searching for another bottle…another momentary solution to her problems. Nothing comes of it though, all the bottles are empty, finished off by her…her alone. Taking the empty vodka bottle she throws it at the wall in front of her, watching it shatter into thousands of pieces like her life. I am alone…
