I'm Not A Jerk - Part 1 - Making A Child Cry

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 1, 2003 [Being Continental Champion is not an easy thing. Being adored and admired by millions upon millions of people is certainly hard to live up to. Making sure that you set the right example for the little dudes and dudettes of the world is certainly tricky. But somehow 'Totally Tubular' Tommy is stepping through adversity, one again, and making sure that he makes himself a resepectable champion, someome to really look up to, and someone that every single wrestler wants to be in the future. He's going to have to represent PCW as their second highest champion in the federation and that's going to take a lot of hard work, and certainly a lot of determination. Inside and outside of the ring, Tommy is going to have to become an ambassador of the title that he holds. He didn't win by some fluke or stupid mistake, he EARNED exactly what he got. He earned everything that he's ever gotten, because he always makes sure he puts the hardest work into everything that he does. The ambassador of the Continental Title is going to have a long, hard road ahead of him whether he likes it or not. Tommy is ready, born ready, for the challenges that face him ahead. Even if the bodacious God himself has to come down for a challenge, Tommy will face it like the REAL man that he is] [Gold's Gym in Los Angeles houses some of the most famous body builders and athletes on the west coast. It was said that Arnold Schwarzennegger came in for a most excellent workout that left most of the members just watching in awe. Of course, that's when Arnold was on the juice, but a lot of things have changed since then. Men and women are working harder, pushing their bodies to the limits and then beyond. As vein filled men bench press 500 pounds and barely break a sweat, and the women ALSO press 500 pounds without breaking a sweat, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy can only stand in front of the far right wall, which is just a gigantic mirror, and pose. His blue swim trunks seem to be his workout gear of choice, as he flexes his pecs in the mirror. He flexes them to the rhythm of his entrance theme 'Surfin' USA' and then lets out a small giggle because of the sheer ridiculousness of it. The other members in the background don't even bother to notice this man, this man who stopped the Tubular Tykes foundation. They don't want to bother with a jerkface who is just into things to make money, to become more rich than he already is. The people in the gym pump harder, push themselves harder knowing that 'Totally Tubular' Tommy is just some arrogant, scared little boy. That once he finds out what reality is really like, he's going to be in for one of the biggest rude awakenings of his life. And when that comes, he'll surely lose everything that he has] Little Boy: Tommy? [The boy looks to be about eight or nine years old, and wears a PCW T-shirt with a pair of blue jean shorts. The boy looks to be the son of another member, or maybe the bodacious babe owner. Like it matters to Tommy, as he is flexing his biceps in the mirror. His million dollar smile flashes to himself through the mirror. The other members can only take a glimpse to see the tanned surfer smiling with a shit eating grin, flexing his muscles like he's some sort of God or something. But isn't that what Tommy's goal is? To be a God? To be someone that everybody worships and prays to at night? Is that what this flexing, professional wrestler wants? The boy looks up at his idol, and then looks at him in the mirror, and then back up to the flexing, former surfer] Little Boy: Tommy? Can you sign my autograph book? [Tommy is too wrapped up in himself to even hear this little child. This poor kid who just wants Tommy's John Doe in a little notebook with Bugs Bunny on the cover. This child who is just trying to meet his idol, maybe ask him a few things while Tommy signs the notebook. Ask him about how hard it was to become the Continental Champion. Ask him how to become a professional wrestler. Ask him to maybe take his mommy out on a date because she dates too many bad men. Tommy now is straightening his right arm, watching his tricep muscle bulge out. He purposely strains himself and growls into the mirror like some sort of bogusly possessed demon. Then he giggles to himself once again just to watch all of his muscles strain together and bulge out. The other members in the background, still working hard, glance over just to confirm that Tommy is truly a jackass. That, yes, he is the biggest asshole in Los Angeles. Wait, the biggest in California. No, it could be safe to say that 'Totally Tubular' Tommy is the biggest asshole on in the United States. That could be pushing it, but these other gym members are taking into the account that you never know what this jerk will do next] Little Boy: TOMMY!! [With a raised voice the boy is able to get Tommy's attetntion. The former surfing champion glances down at the boy, who smiles brightly...so innocently. Tommy then looks back at himself in the mirror with a sort of blank stare. He then glances back down to the child] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: I'm busy here, little dude. Little Boy: You seem like it, but I was just wondering if you could sign my notebook real quick. PLEEEASSSE?! [Tommy once again looks into the mirror with a blank stare, flexes his right pec once, and then glances back down at the child] TTT: Give me the notebook, compadre. [The boy quickly hands over the tattered notebook and Tommy looks through it at the names. Bruce Willis, Arnold Schwarzenneger, Spider-Man...wait...Spider-Man?! That's totally bogus to have Spider-Man autograph something, he's not even real. He's just some totally heinous creation of some totally bogus old dude. That compadre can't autograph something if he doesn't even exist. People these days, dudes. Trying to make some poor defenseless little dude believe that Spider-Man exists. Why don't they just come down here and tell him that Santa Claus is just a totally heinous fraud? That the Easter Bunny is just some bogus dude in a bunny suit. That the tooth fairy is bogus in a most heinous kind of way, because she DOESN'T exist. It's just made up stuff to make little dudes and dudettes feel better. So that these little compadres don't have to face reality at such a young age. Talk about BOGUS!!] TTT: You do know that Spider-Man is just a comic book character, right? [The boy looks up at his idol with a bit of confusion in his eyes. He doesn't quite understand what The Tubular One is getting at] TTT: Like, Santa Claus is just made up so that we can get bodacious presents, Spider-Man is made up so that we feel like he's saving the world for us. It's quite bogus, I know, but I'll be your superhero. [The boy's face begins to well up as if he's about to cry. He didn't know that Santa Claus wasn't real. Did his mom just lie to him ever since he was a REALLY little kid? Is she the one who went down the chimney and put the presents underneath the tree? This can't be! And Spider-Man, he HAS to be real. He saw the movie in May and he was swinging all around the city as he would anywhere. Spider-Man is a true superhero!! He HAS to be real!] Little Boy: They are real... TTT: Little dude, your going to have to face some totally heinous realities one day. I remember I was in your position before, it's tough, but you have to accept that these dudes AREN'T real. They are bogus made up characters so we believe that things are going to be all right, instead of heinous...which is what reality is. [The boy holds his head down as a few tears run down his face. They are all fake. Made up. His mommy told him that they were all real, and lived in a far away land to protect their identities. Except Spider-Man, he swung around and saved everybody. He is cool, but HE IS FAKE! The other members of the gym look over at the little boy, the son of Carla Zimmer, the owner. Some of them stop their workout as the boy breaks into tears and that jerk Tommy just goes back to posing in the mirror, tossing the boys notebook onto the floor. He doesn't care what he did, he feels that he had just done the little dude a most excellent favor. The boy falls to the floor in sadness, holding onto his notebook with a tight grip. He can't get a grip of everything being fake, a fraud. IT'S ALL A FRAUD! The boy tosses the book across the gym and runs away, and Tommy, flexing his pecs again, doesn't even notice a thing] Gym member: YOU JERK!! You just crushed that kids hopes! [Tommy just flexes his pecs, tuning out the totally bogus people here. He's learned to do that quite well in the past few weeks, and it's really coming in handy now] Gym member: Do you even hear me?! You totally ruined that child! YOUR SICK! [As Tommy begins to go into another set of bicep flexing, a fairly attractive woman in a business suit approaches him. Her brown hair is pulled up into a bun and her extremely long skirt with a bright floral pattern is REALLY screaming to be pulled off, not in the gym, but in a secret place. Her dark brown eyes are screaming for someone to just breakdown and hold her because raising a nine year old alone is not an easy thing to do. Meeting guys who are loaded up on steroids and just want to get into her panties are beginning to really annoy her. She needs some kind of change from it all, but she doesn't know how. Yeah, that's exactly what those dark brown eyes are saying. Tommy notices her skin tone, which is quite pale for a babe living in Los Angeles. Most babes are tanned to perfection, but this babe is pale like Wednesday Adamms. It's...kind of bodacious in a way] Woman: Tommy? [Tommy smiles brightly to her as the gym members begin to go back to what they were doing. This situation is going to be under control, because no one ever disobey's the owner of the gym. Nobody!] TTT: Hey, bodacious business babe. What can I do you for? [Tommy arches his eyebrows and the woman just keeps a very stern look fixed on the surfer boy] Woman: I am the owner of this gym, Carla Zimmer. I hope you have noticed me before on the picture, which you see when you enter the gym everyday. [Tommy looks to the front entrance and, wouldn't you know it, there goes a most bodacious picture of the babe] TTT: Gnarly picture. Carla: Thank you, but you should know from coming here for so long that I do have a son. TTT: EXCELLENT! I'd like to meet the little dude sometime. Carla: You already have. TTT: I have? That's weird. Carla: Yes, in fact, you met him only a minute ago. TTT: Oh. Well, if I would have known that the little dude... Carla: That's far enough, Tommy. I've been reading a lot of horrible things about you in the paper lately and, to be honest, I wanted to believe that these papers were lying. That a happy go lucky man like yourself was just getting a bad wrap and that you were always doing the right thing. Now, after seeing my son crying his eyes out about how everything is fake, I've come to a conclusion that you really are the jerk that the papers are making you out to be. That YOU are making yourself the jerk, and for once the paper is reporting the facts. [The entire time Tommy wasn't even listening, just flexing. His pecs are now doing a dance to the rhythm of 'Powe oF Love' by Huey Lewis and The News. Tommy giggles once again and then turns back to Carla, the owner of the gym] TTT: Listen, babe, I'm most sorry about your son. The dude is nine years old and he needs to know certain things to grow up. Your a most bogus parent if you let him think that Spider-Man is real!! I mean, come on, even I KNEW that Spider-Man wasn't real when I was a most bodacious little compadre. Now, Carla, your a bodacious babe and it seems that your in need of a gnarly night out on the town. So, to make it up to you I'm going to take you out on a date tonight. Nothing fancy, but nothing too casual. I have the perfect place for us to go, it's a most excellent establishment. Leave the little dude with a babysitter, let him tough out this new discovery and we'll have ourselves a good time. [Carla should say no. In fact, she should slap this surfer across the face and kick him out of the gym. But, for some reason she can't resist him. She wants to go out with him for a night out, she was to eat dinner with him. She wants to end up back at his beach house and then make MAD passionate love to him. Let it out of her system, let it out of his system. She's going to give this man the night of his life. But why does she feels this way? After what this man did to her son? Well, maybe it wasn't such a bad thing. In fact, it was a favor. That's a way to look at it. A favor. She can't really place how he is doing this. She can see through a man's B.S. a mile away, and she knows that he's laying it on thick...but...there is something. Well, it could be because he's standing there only in a pair of extremely skimpy swim trunks...and nothing else] Carla: Well...all right... [Everyone in the gym is shocked] TTT: TOTALLY GNARLY!! [Fade to black]
