Date: 9-30-2002 There are few things, I know for certain, in the short time; I’ve been alive. One, you can’t trust anyone completely. Two, the moment you do, you get your ass kicked. Three, never, EVER, under estimate Akiko Hiroshima. Three was the only thing on my mind at the moment, actually, as I quickly raised my hand to parry a kick lined for my temple. She rarely held anything back. To be honest, this was more of a practice in reflex, then skill, she rarely left any openings to exploit, and her small size made a point spar incredibly hard. Not to mention the fact, that I really didn’t want to hurt her. Her aggression ran wild, though, much to my surprise, as she scissors swept my legs right from under me, and I crashed down to the ground, managing to keep my chin to my chest, so I wouldn’t bounce my skull off the stones. “SHIMATA!” I damn near shrieked. I was definitely caught off guard by that sweep. My eyes widened, in a bit of shock and horror, as she tried to follow up with a stomp to my solar plexus, thankfully, I managed to grab her foot, and pull her off balance. “Halt! Stop!” I let out a breath, as she pulled herself up, looking a bit peeved that she had been taken down. “What?” She asked, well, it was more of a demand, to be perfectly honest. “I’m done for today.” I said, sitting up slowly, and rubbing the back of my head, pulling out the tie in my hair and shaking the amounts of leaves and dirt from my hair, that had gathered when I fell. Sometimes long hair was a pain in the ass. “Already?” She pouted. I guess she had lots of energy today. Mental note: Less caffeine intake for Akiko. “Fine, you want to quit, we quit. I have surprise for you, anyway.” She said, with a strange tone to her voice, I couldn’t quite pick it up. It definitely made me curious. I sighed, and picked myself up off the ground, dusting off the worn and torn Gi, I was wearing. It had been a long time since I brought it out of storage, but it definitely looked less funny, then wearing tights. Especially when training at Buddhist Temples, and in tree groves, how few they were in the more popular parts of Japan. I grabbed my sports back, loaded with clothes, as Akiko grabbed hers, and we just started walking back, paying no mind to the fact that we were both hot, and sweaty, and dressed oddly. I took her arm in mine, and she gave me a look, the slowly smiled, as we pushed our ways through the seemingly endless sea of people. In the apartment complex, we approached the small room we shared. Living in sin could be so much fun. Secretly, I hoped for another work out once we got inside, but as I opened the door, my heart somewhat sank. The hopes for my “other work out were gone,” and Akiko rushed by me, and greeted her long time friends, Savanda “Lady Cat” Carlson, and Kelly Bowe, with a big hug. “You?” Was all my flustered mine could make my mouth say. “Yes, us.” La Cat, smiled, as she grabbed my arm, and pulled me into the room, with me acting like the slug I was, just kind of plodded in like some sort of bovine. “Still haven’t gotten him to loosen up, eh, Kiko?” La Cat smirked, as she pinched my cheek. “Do you need me to show you how?” “Savvy!” Kelly giggled, and smacked La Cat on the arm, playfully, and Akiko rolled her eyes. I stumbled, trying to come up with some conversation, as they all stared at me so intently, “Err, so Kelly, how’s McAlli..UMP!” I was abruptly cut off, but a hard elbow to the gut. Akiko glared at me with those brown eyes, before grabbing my ear and hissing into it. “Kelly lost the baby, and Jason left! Do not bring it up!” Well, this was news to me. “What?” I flat out asked, very confused by this, the last time I saw Kelly and Jason, they were happy, and all lovey dovey. Could so much change in so little time? Of course, it was kind of ironic for me to say such, knowing what I have said, and done. Of course, I was only replied with another elbow to the stomach, to let me know I would get my answer later. And, that I probably wouldn’t be getting that “extra training”, that I had been hoping for tonight. Once more, my heart sank. I just stood there, trying to regain somewhat of a shred of rationality, as the “Trippy Trio” bantered on. I just gave a sigh, and started to walk off to toss my bag into the closet, and change into something a bit more comfortable, and less smelly. “Where do you think you’re going C-boy?” La Cat asked, with a smirk on her face. “Cianan, c’mere I got something for you, that Akiko said you might want for your adventure in PCW.” This now, rose my curiosity, what did I leave in America that would help me now? How did Savanda get into my apartment in California? There were plenty of other questions, but she waggled her finger, beckoning me over, with a sly grin on her face. I approached slowly, I never knew what these three plan in their deranged little minds, especially when they were all in once place, to be honest it’s rather scary. Well, during all of my thinking, Lady Cat got impatient, “Are you coming, or not?” “Fine! I’m coming,” I sighed, and slowly walked over, noticing a small black metal briefcase at her high heel cloven feet. She gave me a little bit of a wink, before bending down and picking up the briefcase, and with a brush of her arm, she cleared my desk, piling everything against the wall. “Hey now, be careful with that!” I shouted, but she ignored me, a devilish smile on her red painted lips. “Oh hush up, C-boy and take a gander at this.” She grinned, and flipped open some latches, and threw open the top of the briefcase. The contents made me gasp; I was shocked, and somewhat horrified. What I was looking at, was the same thing, I was addicted to. The chemical, that enhances body, and destroys mind, that has had some strong people under its grasp. How did Savanda find this, of all things? “Why.. Did you bring these here?” My voice was cold, and my eyes were now narrowed, as I glared at Savanda. She pouted, and stood up, crossing her arms over her stomach, resting her weight on one side, “You don’t appreciate what I’ve done? Akiko said you were feeling a bit skittish about your match in PCW. I know that the last time you were this worried about something, this is what you used.” She patted the contents, before scooping up a syringe, and held it out to me. I just stared, at her hand. Then at Savanda, then, my gaze shifted to Akiko, my eyes questioned. For, I had no idea what I should do. My mind was a whirl, of thoughts, and past circumstances. This was a shock, to say the absolute least, for Savanda to be fueling an old habit. “So, what do you think, Cianan?” Kelly Bowe asked with a big grin, as all three girls watched for my reaction. “Take it away.” Was all I could force out of my throat, the words sounded raspy. The old urges started anew, and I could feel the aches in my body, and the throbbing in the back of my mind. I turned, and slowly walked into my room, slamming the door behind me. This was definitely too much for the moment. “Well, that didn’t go as planned.” Savanda sighed, and pouted slightly. Kelly Bowe just nodded, and gave a soft sigh. Akiko stood slowly, and walked to the door, knocking on it lightly. I didn’t answer, I didn’t move. My head hurt too bad, just seeing the Noir, it made me hurt so badly. I couldn’t believe Kelly and Savanda brought that there. I couldn’t believe Akiko. I couldn’t believe myself, for wanting it. I was weak. “So..” “Damn..” “Weak..” I breathed out, slowly, as my eyes shut. To be continued…
