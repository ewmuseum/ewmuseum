A work out with Bob Charlie.

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: A work out with Bob Charlie. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 9/26/02 -The scene opens up in the gym of local celerity Bob Charlie. In the ring two amateur boxers spar like mad. On the back mat is two sweaty men doing push-ups. The smaller solid one is doing double the speed of the tall built one. They are referee and judge Bob Charlie and Duke Williams.- Bob: Come on you slow nitwit get your ass in the air like your fucking a 400 pound gorilla and get your stomach on the ground like your crawling away from here 600 pound husband you sick bastard. LETS GO. Williams: I….I….I…I…am Bob: I’ve seen retards at the Special Olympics do better push up’s son. Your weak….guys with one arm, three ears, and half a brain doing dubble time. Williams: They….they….they……. -Bob rolls out of a push up and into a sit up position where he starts working his stomach with fast as lighting pushups.- Bob: You see that pretty boy I’ve already done my hundred pushups and now I’m on my sit ups for fun. What’s a matter with you kid? Williams: I….I…..I…..I -Duke struggles to do the last few push ups.- Bob: Finish up those last five and we’ll go run a mile. There is no way I’m losing to that peanut Jeremy Johnson. You beat him in your first PCW match did you know that kid? You destroyed him and I’m going to do the same in my first match. I haven’t had this much fun since I left my job as a drill instructor for our United States of America armed forces unit. You hear me Malice Boy? -Duke rolls over on his back and tries to catch his breath.- Bob: I’ve never heard Duke Williams speechless before. You need this training don’t you boy? HUH? You know why the south lost the war? Too many biscuits and not enough sit ups. Trust me I’ve read all the history books. Weak people lose wars. -Duke gasps for air.- Bob: You soft Duke Williams you need exercise. Come with me for a jog through Gettysberg. Lets go Williams….Mr. President….come on. -Duke sits up and wipes the sweat off his brow.- Williams: Listen here Richard Simmons I’m tired, old, and over worked. I agreed to come over for a light work out not a concentration camp work day. Bob: This is a light work out….come on lets get less jabber and more jogging. Williams: Bobbie I’m done. Bob: When’s the last time you were in a gym big dog? Williams: Everyday for two hours…I hit the weights hard. Bobs: When’s the last time you did any cardio training? -Duke thinks on that one as he looks in his bag for a cigar.- Williams: Maybe a few weeks. Bob: When’s the last time you jumped the boxes? Williams: Never. Bob: Jumped rope? Williams: A few years. Bob: No wonder your so soft you need to work you heart. Williams: I need a cigar and a glass of water. Bob: Come on Duke I want to win this match. Williams: I can beat JJ in the blink of an eye. You can beat JJ in two blinks of an eye he’s not an issue. What is an issue is that weasel Miyagawa. This guy has sold his soul to the devil so many times its not even worth a damn anymore. Does he impress me? No. Will I be happy to hand him his ass? Your damn right. This kid needs to be taught a lesson in respect. He will be taught that lesson by the professor of Malice when I bring the Front Office work ethic to the ring and wipe him out. That bobby is the Honky Tonk Truth or I’m not Duke Williams. Bob: We’ll send them Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound with the 1…..2……3!! Williams: Ding ding baby ding ding. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
