Title: Pain? Suffering? Confusion? Featuring: White Mexican Date: March 12/2002 “What is happening to me?” The scene fades in to reveal everyone’s favorite Mexican Mexi is sitting balled up in the corner of his hotel room shivering and holding himself. “Make it stop!” He screams as starts to pull at his hair in agony. Prologue Now by now you are wondering… What is wrong with Mexi? Well let me explain. As invincible to pain as Mexi may seem, it doesn’t mean his body won’t react to the punishment. Since May 10th he has been a proud member of the Primetime family and has never backed down from a challenge. As we sit here breaking the twelve day of March it totals just over ten months of punishment. Even more so recently Mexi has been worn down like never before. Shanghaied 2002 and he would feel pain for the first time in years tearing his shoulder. Maybe he rushed back to soon, maybe is shoulder never fully healed but the non-stop beatings week in and week out seem to be catching up to him. Attacked on Turmoil, twice more on Fusion and he still made it to Sabotage. But now he is paying the price for it, unaware that what he is feeling is pain, extreme pain that he is not used to feeling. It has him scared, it has him worried and it has him completely unfocused. End of Prologue Mexi starts to scream in agony as he twists and turns clutching his stomach. “AHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!! WHAT IS HAPPENING TO ME?!?!?!?!” Epilogue And so we shall realize that the Mexican we know and love is going through a metamorphosis of sorts. The TEQUILA~! drinking, no pain feeling, loveable Mexican is changing… But is it for the better or for the worst? Only time will tell… End of Epilogue “IT HURTS!!! IT HURTS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” He screams as the screen fades out. ---- View White Mexican's Biography
