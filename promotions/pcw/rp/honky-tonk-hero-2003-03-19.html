Honky Tonk Hero

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Honky Tonk Hero Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-19-2003 ..::/::..Our story begins as the blue sky gives way to an orange dusk, the sun slowly starting to take it’s leave so the moon make take it’s place. We find ourselves on the outskirts of the city, obvious by the lack of buildings and life around for each set of miles. However, the quiet outskirts are only disturbed by one presence…a wild bar standing erect within the orange dust known as the “Piledriver”. Loud rock and roll can be heard emitting from within, bikers revving up their engines loudly on the outside as they partake of beer and crude jokes. In the midst of all this, a candy red 67’ Chevy pulls within the parking lot of the bar, parking in one of the fronts spots as the engine cuts off. Shortly, the passenger and driver’s side doors swing open as Teresa Tomas and Rent-A-Hero make their exits from the truck. Teresa wipes some dirt from her hip-hugger jeans as a few of the bikers near the entrance whistle at her, following with various cat-calls and such as she merely shrugs them off. Our hero rounds the truck, still adamant about NOT taking off his PCW Crusierweight title in public as he makes it a wardrobe addition to his Venom costume replica. The bikers continue to whistle as Teresa begins to make her way inside the bar, placing her hand against the rotting wood door as our hero shoots his eyes over to the biker, saying to them in a scolding tone..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”I don’t think the lady appreciate your various hoots and hollers you leather-clad ruffians! Please refrain from your disrespectful antics shot towards my sidekick!” Biker 1:”Whatcha gonna do boy…use your heat vision on me?!”..::/::..The bikers laugh together, slapping hands at the joke as our hero stands with his hands firmly on his hips, saying to the group in a rebuttal..:/::..Rent-A-Hero:”NO….but I do have SUPER SAIYAN STRIKES O’ DOOM….so battle if you dare!” ..::/::..Teresa shakes her head in shame, grabbing our hero by the strap of his title as she drags him into the bar, our hero steadily throwing slaps to the air as the biker’s laugh heartily. Finally inside the club, our hero and Teresa’s ears are invaded by the hard sounds of “Cat Scratch Fever”, the bar patronized by a seedy lot of bikers, loose women, drunks, and other characters. The bar is filled with hard drinks and they are being consumed like water by the customers, the sounds of laughing and pool balls being struck into empty pockets filling the air as well. Our hero can’t help but say one thing..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”THIS IS HELL-O!!” Teresa: -arms outstretched in glee- “I’M HOME!! BRING ON THE JACK!!”..::/::..Teresa makes a beeline straight for the bar, hastily speaking to the stout bartender as he gets in the process of preparing her a hard drink, The Tease rubbing her hands in delight in expectation. Our hero makes his way to the bar as well, the patrons eyeing our hero with disdain as he attempts not to stir up any trouble, sitting on the stool next to Teresa as a glass filled with to the brim with an alcoholic mixture is sat right in front of her, the country beauty consuming it without prompt as our hero flags down the bartender. He stops, sending an unfriendly sneer our hero’s way as he asks politely..::/::.Rent-A-Hero:”Excuse me my good man…I was hoping I could get a Kool-Aid, straight up…maybe a Gummi Bear or two for an extra kick?!” -winks-..::/::..The bartender is frozen in his tracks…breaking that freeze with a hearty laugh that emits throughout the whole bar. Trying to keep himself from laughing long enough to tell everyone clearly, the bartender holds his large stomach in a cocktail of delight in pain as he beckons throughout the bar..::/::.. Bartender:”YA HEAR THAT BOY’S?! THE SPANDEX MAN WANT’S A KOOL-AID….STRAIGHT UP!!..::/::..The entire bar stops in their tracks and let out a unison laugh, overcoming the music in the bar as everyone is swept up in the asinine drink order of Rent-A-Hero, albeit our hero and Teresa. Clenching his teeth, our hero looks ready to fire back with one of his trademark “Rent-isms” but Teresa grabs our hero by the arm, saying..::/::..Teresa:”Rent, jus’ take it and move on lil’ buddy. No good’ll come of you tryin’ to cause a scene ya hear?”Rent-A-Hero: -reluctantly- ”If you say so Teresa….but I shall not be disrespected again.” ..::/::..Looking to the bartender yet again, the bar just starting to come down from the laughter, our hero asks yet again with uncanny politeness to him..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”I’m sorry my good man…how silly of me. Perhaps, I could have a Snapple instead?”Bartender: -loud enough so the whole bar can hear- “I suppose you’ll be askin’ for a Hi-C next huh?! This is a BAR…if your not drinkin’ or fightin’….YOU DON’T NEED TO BE HERE CITY BOY!! Now since you ain’t doin’ either one of em’, I think it’s high time ya left…CONDOM HEAD!!”..::/::..The bar laughs yet again, our hero clenching his teeth as they make a slight gritting sound, our hero opening his hand so the palm shows as Teresa finishes up her drink quickly, mumbling..::/::..Teresa:”Oh brother…”..::/::..Our hero raises his hand up high, anime graphics overtaking it as it finally raises to it’s peak, our hero swinging it over and SMASHING the palm of his hand into the bartender’s face as he goes plopping down to the ground, out cold as our hero exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”BIZNITCH SLAP O’ DOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!! Now, make me a Kool-Aid, DRINK BOY!!”Teresa:”Uh…Rent…”..::/::..Our hero turns his head behind him, the entire bar on their feet and with their fists or weapon of choice at the ready as the dozens and dozens of patrons look to take out our hero for his attack on the bartender, Rent-A-Hero gulping as Teresa stands by his side, looking to the screen before saying..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”CAN’T I GO ANYWHERE WITHOUT HAVING TO FIGHT A CRAZED MOB?!”..::/::..The scene fades, our hero and Teresa backed up to the bar counter with the bar patrons ready to lay the beatdown upon the two, the odds looking HEAVILY unfavorable..::/::..WILL RENT-A-HERO AND TERESA SURVIVE THE BAR ONSLAUGHT?! CAN RENT-A-HERO USE HIS RENBT-A-POWERS TO SAVE THE DAY?! WILL OUR HERO EVER ORDER A DRINK THAT WON’T GET HIM LAUGHED OUT OF A BAR?! FIND OUT…SAME RENT-A-TIME…SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
