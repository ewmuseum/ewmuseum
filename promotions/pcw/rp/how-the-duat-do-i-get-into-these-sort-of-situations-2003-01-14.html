How the Duat do I get into these sort of situations?

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/01/03 ‘How the Duat do I get into these sort of situations?’ Pharaoh’s voice was a plea. He looked up at the face in front of him and considered the thought again. It was useless really - he just couldn’t come up with a reason. The face, as it happened, wasn’t so much a face. It was more a mug, and not so much a mug as in the face of someone ugly, but as in the mug of something ugly. Isiy turned to him, shaking her head, ‘Well, you will put people through car windscreens, Titu.’ ‘I’ve already explained all that, my dear,’ said Pharaoh, narrowly dodging the beast’s talon-ridden fists again. ‘I was in a situation where I would do anything to put across my point. And besides I’m over that now.’ He wasn’t of course. He still hated DarkStar as much as he did that fateful Friday night. ‘If you say so.’ Persisted Isiy, her eyes hopelessly lost in the scene a few feet before her. ‘Look, stop being like this and go get the net!’ bellowed Pharaoh, once again his body defying gravity to an extent as he back flipped his way out of the reach of the monster’s unholy smelling maw. ‘From the tennis courts? What the hell is that going to do?’ she protested. ‘Something more than you’re doing right now, that’s bloody what!’ How it had got there, he didn’t know, but the scaly creature - red in colour and as angry as the fires of hell was lividly prowling The Palace courtyard - currently engaged in trying to decapitate and quite possible eat Titu Xansiv. It had happened around ten minutes ago. The Pharaoh had been sat outside, nursing his wounds from his past few wrestling shows, when out of nowhere, and he meant out of nowhere, a monster, around seven feet tale, as bulky as a cow, as resilient as a camel and as pissed off as Statuz Quo in an English lesson had appeared with puff of smoke. A puff of smoke for the love of Ra! The first thing it had seen was the sunbathing Pharaoh, a glass of lemonade in his hand and a magazine in the other, and since then Titu had played the victim. The whole incident was completely unreal. ‘Where the Duat did you come from?!” Pharaoh demanded to the world in general. ‘One minute I’m relaxing, next I’m being attacked by a demon! Can’t I get a rest anywhere I go? I feel like I’m still at bleeding work!’ The creature let out a thunderous roar. ‘There’s no need to get like that, I was just asking!’ said Pharaoh, the monster staring at him angry-eyed. ‘Come on, Isiy! He’s noticed me!’ ‘Noticed you? Titu, he noticed you ten minutes ago, you daft idiot! He’s been circling you ever since!’ Pharaoh jumped out of the way again, hitting the gravel with a crunch and rolling out to his right to avoid a mammoth foot full of razor sharp claws from crushing him to death. ‘Bah!’ he managed tiresomely. The monster was slimmer towards its waist, and the colouring began to fade around the stomach. It was a ghastly thing, though. Its back covered in scales and its face full of teeth and saliva. Pharaoh glanced at it with a shudder as it approached once again. Ok, he though, Isiy’s typically taking too long to get the blasted net, so I’m going to have to –he shuddered- try and knock out… Leaping to his feet, and narrowly missing a massive forearm to the head, Pharaoh ran at its side and took out the monster’s weakest part with his shoulder. The fiendish creature plummeted to the floor with a heavy thud, kicking up a tidal wave of pebbles. ‘I think I got it!’ he shouted in his wife’s direction. ‘No you haven’t,’ she replied from across the yard. ‘Look behind you!’ Titu turned. He knew before he came face to face with the snarling thing that it would in fact be standing behind him, spit building up in the corners of its mouth, and an expression saying ‘ You bastard’ spread over its horrific mug. With a splash of gravel, Pharaoh hit the floor hard. He’d been back-body-dropped time and time again, but they seemed nothing in comparison to the throw the monstrosity just tossed him into the air with. What was worse it, it had only used one muscular arm to do it! The soft patter of Isiy’s footsteps littered the air as he groggily opened his eyes. She had the tennis netting in her hands and was trying, single-handed, to restrain the beast with it. ‘Hang on, my love; let me help!’ shouted Pharaoh heroically. Isiy paid him an expression of uncertainty as he ran up behind the beast and tackled it in the lower back. The monster no sold the move and turned to face the Egyptian, Pharaoh’s face growing pale. ‘Dammit! It worked last time!’ He said, scolding himself. ‘Well, I mean, look at its back, Titu, it’s solid as a rock, covered in those scales, you should have known really.’ Pharaoh winced, ‘Bah!’ The creature’s huge fists began to cave in the patio floor as Xansiv dodged countless thunderous punches, the beast growing ever angrier as his target failed to stay still for even a moment. Eventually the huge crimson monster slowed its attack, gradually becoming unmotivated and sluggish. ‘Now’s our chance!’ shouted Pharaoh, Isiy crouching a few feet away picking up the net. ‘Our chance?!’ she laughed, ‘what do you mean our chance? You’re the one fighting it!’ Pharaoh sighed impatiently, ‘Just get the net over here now, will you!’ Rolling her eyes, Isiy jogged across the drive to where Titu was intensely avoiding being pounded into the ground. The creature was obviously strong, but it lacked brains whatsoever. Titu had figured out a pattern in its movements, and after some time of using it to his advantage he’d began to wonder if the fiendish thing had caught on to what he was doing. After a few minutes he’d decided perhaps it hadn’t. He leapt toward his right and rolled out of the way of another clattering blow to the tiled squares lining the patio. Titu dived forward again, knowing the huge behemoth would most likely try to hit him again after he landed on the gravel and dashed toward Isiy. ‘Right I’ll take one side - you take the other. Run around him, and hopefully we’ll trap his arms to his sides.’ He yelled over a gurgling roar of anger. ‘This’ll never work, you know. It’s too big.’ Said Isiy, clearly sceptical. Xansiv gritted his teeth, ‘Have you got any better ideas?!’ Isiy blushed, taken back by Pharaoh’s frustration, ‘Well, err, no…’ ‘So stop contradicting me! Now—… MOVE!’ The crimson behemoth plunged its mountain-sized fist into the gravel next to Isiy. She yelped as Titu dragged her out of the way and said: ‘Cummon!’ Isiy ran to her left and Pharaoh to his right. They circled the monster twice, wrapping it swiftly in the tennis netting. Soon they had the beast completely bound, Pharaoh struggling to tie some sort of knot in the material to keep it from escaping and wreaking even more havoc on The Palace grounds. ‘Ok, go get the Palace Guard.’ He said, breathing heavily. Isiy nodded, returning five minutes later with two men adorned in ancient armour, halberds in their hands. ‘‘Sup, chief?’ one asked, not yet far enough out of the building to notice the huge red creature struggling vigorously in the make-shift binds. ‘Oh,’ he stammered, finally seeing it. ‘Jesus Christ, boss. What’s this all ‘bout then?’ said his partner. Pharaoh shook his head wearily, ‘I think it’s a present from some... friends.’ ‘Pretty big bloody present if you ask me!’ exclaimed the second guardsman, oblivious to Titu’s sarcasm. Sighing, Pharaoh waved a hand in the monster’s direction, ‘Get rid of it, will you?’ ‘Get rid of it, sir?!’ said the first guard, utterly shocked. ‘How the blooming heck do you propose we get 'rid' of summat like that, milord?!’ Pharaoh shrugged, still weary from his confrontation, ‘How the Duat should I know? This is what I employ you for, guys. Just use your heads, and you’ll find a way.’ ‘This wasn’t in the contract.’ Mumbled the second guard to the first. Pharaoh turned his attention to Isiy, ‘Told ya’ it’d work, didn’t I?’ ‘Heh.’Isiy prodded a shard of plaster with the tip of his sandal, ‘Yeah, it sure worked, Titu. Looks just like we won a battle out here today.’ Pharaoh waved a hand dismissively, ‘Well at least we stopped it from causing any more damage to The Palace; that’s all that counts really.’ ‘True.’ Said Isiy with a smile. ‘Cummon, let’s go inside for sex!’ She grinning wildly as Pharaoh embraced her around the waist, the guards, pondering how in the hell they could possibly ‘get rid’ of the thing in front of them, standing expressionless in the backdrop.
