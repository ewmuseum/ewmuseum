Making the Commercial- before recording

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th April After weeks of war with Flaymin Aymin, El Diablo can now relax and do his commercial for Taco Bell. El Diablo is of course the Taco Bell spokesman along with the Chihuahua, and a lucky change of pace it is, as no longer will he have to wear that mask, he has his new Mexican flag face paint which should suit the whole Taco Bell image. As memory tells us, El Diablo was offered the spokesman job after rescuing the kidnapped Chihuahua from the sadistic Flaymin Aymin. And the rescue was a momentous day for the entire population of Mexico that they even had a festival to celebrate his return. Of course though, El Diablo has had many changes since then, most notably the mask being gone, but also mentally and it is really unknown how he will react to this commercial. The scene is inside a small studio in Mexico City, Mexico, only a handful of people are in there. The director, the cameraman, a couple of stagehands and of course El Diablo and the loveable Chihuahua. Diablo has his hair loose and is wearing his Mexican face paint, as well as a white vest and red pants with black boots. He is basically in his wrestling attire. The room is laid out strangely, to the left are several metal tables and in the center of the room is where the commercial will be filmed and on the right are several props, like large plastic taco's and burrito's. On the left of the room on the tables are the scripts for the commercial, and several pens and pencils. Also on the left are the many wires running from the camera and lights that are behind the camera and the several high microphones. In other words the entire room is very full and there is no room for error on anyone’s part. El Diablo walks over to the tables on the left and picks up his script and starts to read through it slowly. 'Ah, Mr Diablo,' says the director, while offering his hand out for a handshake. El Diablo quits looking at the script and places it back on one of the tables, he looks curiously at the directors hand and starts to study it by walking around the mans entire body. After he finishes studying it he shakes it reluctantly. 'What do you want?' snaps Diablo to the director while quickly removing his hand from the directors. The director is a strange looking man, very short at about 5'5' but also very wide, and is quite overweight. He has thinning black hair and a big black beard that covers most of the mans face. He is obviously Mexican and wears a loose white shirt with black pants and black shoes. 'I'm the Director of this commercial, I just thought I'd introduce myself to you,' replies the director whilst looking at El Diablo strangely. The Mexican Devil notices this and looks worried. 'What?' quips Diablo, 'Why are you looking at me like that? What? WHAT?' shouts Diablo in panic. He looks like he is on the defensive. 'No reason,' responds the director, 'We'll be ready to film soon,' he finishes. He quickly walks away from Diablo and shakes his head in disbelief as he does. What is wrong with El Diablo? He seems very weary of everyone. What changes did he mean when he mentioned change at his brothers grave? Is this the change? Instead of picking up the script again, he takes a look around the studio, and studies what everyone is doing and looks out for anything abnormal. 'They're all out to get me,' he mutters to himself, 'I should do this commercial and leave,' he finishes whilst shifting his eyes from side to side. Suddenly something scratches at his legs. Diablo jumps back a little and looks down. 'Holy crap,' he yelps, 'Why are you attacking me?' he gasps at the thing scratching at him. Down near his legs, the thing that was pawing at him was none other than the Chihuahua. The other people in the studio didn't seem to notice El Diablo's outburst. He looks down at the Chihuahua in some sort of fear. 'What do you want from me?' says Diablo in a fearful voice while shifting his eyes, to look all around him. The dog doesn't say a word, obviously. El Diablo then sighs at his stupidity and kneels down to pick the dog up. He picks him up and stands back up to his feet. 'Dogs can't talk,' he says to himself calmly as he pets the scrawny Chihuahua. 'Can't we?' says the dog in a typical Mexican accent. El Diablo looks down at the dog wearily, and in shock. 'What?' asks Diablo in a barely audible tone. 'I said we can talk, dammit!' the dog speaks again. Diablo looks around the room and everyone else is going on about their business finely, like it's only El Diablo that can hear the Chihuahua speaking. 'What do you want?' asks El Diablo curiously. The dog looks up at him. 'Stay out of the commercial, it's MINE,' yells the dog at Diablo. Diablo then drops the Chihuahua onto the floor, it lands on its back then rolls over. 'MINE!!!!!!' it shouts again to Diablo's dismay. 'Hey shut that dog up will you,' shouts one of the stagehands at the other side of the room. Diablo smirks. 'You can hear it too?' he shouts back. 'Who can't? It's been barking for minutes,' he replies. The Metaphor of Magic looks in shock then thinks to himself while leaning against the table before speaking to himself. 'If all they heard is barks, then I can understand dogs, I'm like Dr Dolittle or something,' he says to himself in glee. He looks down at the Chihuahua and attempts talking to it. 'What do you want?' he asks it in an insinuating way to get it to talk. 'BARK!!!' it replies back. El Diablo sighs. 'Damn I lost it,' he says angered, 'I lost the power,' he finishes. He bangs his fist on the table as stagehands finish setting up the stage. He thinks some more and then has a look of shock on his face. 'Or maybe he never spoke at all. Maybe he did, I don't know, I'm so unsure,' Diablo says as he looks around the room. The set is almost complete, meaning almost time to start filming. El Diablo looks upwards. 'Why have I been forsaken?' he yells loudly as the room looks at him curiously. Diablo notices this. 'What? Dammit what?' he shouts at them. Everyone ignores him and continue doing their work. Diablo looks relieved, but still very weary of everyone. 'That was a close one,' he whispers to himself. He picks up the script once more in a final hope of reading it, but as he does he notices the video camera, and some kind of desire comes over him. 'I can do that,' he says with glee. El Diablo drops the script and walks towards the video camera slowly, like he is sneaking towards it. He reaches it and looks through the lens for a few moments before being halted. 'Hey! Stay away from there,' shouts the cameraman at El Diablo. Diablo steps back and walks away from it with a look of anger on his face. 'I'll get to film something, damn you,' whispers Diablo under his breath. He walks back towards the tables on the left of the room when the director speaks through a megaphone. 'OK, we're almost ready to start filming, take you're places people,' the Director says. El Diablo then picks up the dog and starts to walk to the center of the room where the main set is. 'MY COMMERCIAL!!!!!' the Chihuahua shouts at him. 'Aaaaaaaaghhhhhhh,' shouts El Diablo surprised at hearing the dog talk again. He then launches the dog high in the air and steps back a bit. 'Uh, I'll just watch the first scene,' says Diablo nervously and shifting his eyes rapidly to make sure everything is normal. Luckily the dog lands on a large burrito prop. Will El Diablo ever appear in this commercial? And just what is wrong with him?
