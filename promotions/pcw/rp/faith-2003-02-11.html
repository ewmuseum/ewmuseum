Faith!

11 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-11-03 Everyone has to believe in something. There has to be something that drives people otherwise they would never do anything. There must be reasoning behind the thought; otherwise there is only chaos. The world cannot survive in chaos and so people cling to what beliefs they have. In their moments of need they find themselves turning to their belief for strength and inspiration. Something Chris Storm has now done. Chris is busily digging through his closet looking for something. His head was buried in the closet and his butt was high in the air, sticking out. Tessa walks into the room and sees Chris half in the closet. She giggles to herself as she sees his backside high in the air. Tessa looks around to make sure no one is around and sneaks behind Chris. Lifting her hand high in the air, she brings it down on his butt. The slap echoes throughout the house. Chris jumps out of the closet and falls flat on his face. Tessa can’t help but fall onto the bed laughing. Chris gets up, rubbing his tailbone. He turns and sees her on the bed laughing. Chris tries to jump on top of her but Tessa rolls off the bed. She gets to the doorway. “Too slow old man. But uh…what are you looking for?” She asked him. She walks over to the closet to see what he was digging for. Chris jumps off the bed and rushes toward the closet. Tessa steps back surprised. Chris noticed her jumping back and scratches the back of his head nervously. “It’s uh, nothing really. Just some stuff I had packed away that I was trying to find. Nothing to concern yourself over.” Tessa stands back looking at Chris, who goes back to searching through the closet. Tessa starts to leave the room when suddenly she stops at the door again. She looks back at Chris. Biting her bottom lip she ask him a question. “Chris, you aren’t hiding more, you know what from me?” she hesitates, not wanting to say the word. “Porn?” Chris asks. Tessa lowers her head. That is what she was going to say. Chris knew it too. He cast her a smile and turns back to the closet. “Here it is!” he shouts with joy as he pulls out a white box. Chris puts the box on the bed. Tessa walks back into the room to see what it is. Chris opens the box and places the lid next to it. Tessa leans over and looks at it. “Sheets?” she asked, confused. Chris looks at her with a weird look. He looks back down at the box and grabs the white item inside. He pulls it out and holds it in front of him. “This isn’t a sheet. It’s my Shinto robe.” He tells her. Tessa mouths an “Oh” She still looks confused. Chris starts to put the robe on. He looks in the box and pulls out the Shinto Priest hat. He walks over the stand up mirror and admires himself in it. He notices Tessa standing in the background, looking out of place. He turns to her and says, “Tessa, what’s the matter? Something on your mind?” She just looks around, not wanting to say anything. Chris walks up to her and gives her a hug. Chris rubs his arms up and down her back. She looks at him and looks into his eyes. “Chris, I didn’t know you was Shinto. Where did all of this come from?” she finally asked him. “I’ve been a Shinto. I’ve just let myself lose touch with my faith. But thanks to you I have found it again. I hope my being Shinto isn’t a problem for you.” He tells her. Tessa looks up at him and smiles. She kisses him soft on the lips. “Oh course it’s not. I will love you no matter what.” She kisses him again and walks out of the room. Chris starts to take off the robe and hat. He gently places them back into their box. Once he is finished he sits back down on the bed and looks out the window. He stares out at the small front yard in front of the road. There are several other houses nearby. A small tree grows along the walkway. He lets out a sigh. “The wedding is getting closer. Soon Tessa and I will be man and wife. I love her, I really do. But do I love her enough to give up my single life, to give up all of my freedoms and cares just for her? Not only will I be taking a wife, but also I will be a father to three children. It’s just so much for me to take at once. Maybe it’s just cold feet. After all, all guys get nervous before their marriage.” He tells himself. Chris lifts his head up and looks out the window again. Alex and Evan are now playing in the front yard. They just seem to be chasing each other around. “Kami, I need your help with this one. Guide me to my right decision. I need to be sure Tessa is the one I was truly meant to be with. I know I love her in my heart, but I just want to be sure she is the one. Marriage is a big step. I mean it was only a few months ago I threw her face first into a giant cake and was Powerbombing her through a table. Now I’m sleeping in the same bed as her and telling her all of my secrets.” Chris says out loud, hoping for some answer. He falls back onto the bed and stares up at the ceiling. He knows he must find the truth for himself. No one is going to show him the path he must walk, that is something for him to discover for himself. Chris rolls over and looks at the alarm clock on the nightstand. He lets out another sigh as he notices it’s getting late. He sits up on the bed and gets back to his feet. Chris looks around the room and grabs his gym back. “Back on the road again. I must push these doubts aside for the moment and focus on what is important right now. Victory. Victory over Dorn Tracer and victory over the team of Erik, Adam, and El Romantico.” Chris growls at the mention of their name. They were without a doubt three of his least favorite people. Sure P.W.A. was bad, but what they all have done was much worst. Erik Erratic and Chris Storm go way back. Back to when Chris had returned to the WIW at their PPV. Erik was just rising to the top and Chris had come back and was quickly taking the spot from him. Erik introduced the WIW to Tessa Windsor and quickly showed his affection for her by becoming her protector. Yet the tides turned and Chris and Tessa soon became involved with each other. Something that enraged Erik very badly. Erik took his rage out on Tessa and the kids. Cheap attacks and brutal chair shots was his key weapon. Chris has still yet to forgive Erik for throwing Tessa off the WIW stage down below through the tables and wires for the electrical equipment. Adam was another one Chris could not stand. Yet his hate for him only begun two weeks ago. Tessa found her locker room defiled with obscene words spray painted all over the walls. This act of disrespect had struck a note deep inside Tessa, as she was very sensitive about the way people pensive her. She was a sweet and innocent girl. Then the very next week Chris had discovered his own locker room in ruins with comments on the wall. Marks of “Imperfect” and “fake” covered the walls. Those reached Chris as he prided himself on his self-perfection. Adam then made himself felt as he attacked Chris from behind. The blood lost from that fight is something Chris will not soon forget. El Romantico is different from the rest. He has not actually done anything to Chris, yet Chris still hated him more then all the rest. Why, because El Romantico is targeting Tessa. He is trying to ruin her image and emotionally scar her. Chris can take the beatings and the blood lost. He will take the name-calling, the abuse, and the scorns from others. But the one thing he cannot stand is someone taking shots at his sweet Tessa Bear. To think that El Romantico is trying to put the moves on Tessa, that is over the edge. Chris had already gone overboard with Jackson Rust. Now with El Romantico, his last nerve was being pushed. Chris lets out a sigh however as he tried to push the thoughts of them away. He still had to focus on the match ahead. Dorn Tracer would not be easy as he has already defeated Erik Erratic, a man that defeated Chris three times before. Chris was determined to win however and was getting ready to go to the gym to train. He looks back at the white box with his Shinto robe and hat and smiles. He had finally rediscovered his faith.
