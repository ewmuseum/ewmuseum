Revenge Saga Part 7: A confrontation with an old enemy...

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 7: A confrontation with an old enemy… Darkness. Nothing but darkness. For an instant Crichton couldn’t remember where he was or even who he was. He tried to recall his name, but his concentration was broken, by the realisation of a huge plank of wood pressing hard across his chest as more rubble fell on top of the huge pile that already existed. Of course he had no way of knowing this, he only felt the resultant of this chain of events. The huge plank of wood pressed hard upon his chest, making it considerably harder for him to breathe, even without his memory his instincts told him that he was in real danger. Slowly but surely his memory was coming back to him, but in distorted pieces that made no sense to him whatsoever. The first memory was a feeling, a feeling of love. However, with this feeling not one but two names entered his mind, which he thought was odd. The first was the name Aeryn, as soon as he contemplated the name her face came to him accompanied by strange feelings of hate and anger. Those last few feelings he knew were not directed at her, rather they existed because of her. Then it all fell into place, this woman Aeryn was murdered, hence the feelings of anger and hatred, and hence the second woman. The second woman’s name was Jaden. His feelings for her were very much similar to the feelings he felt for Aeryn, but they were fresher and were in the early stages of development. As he thought more about the feelings of hatred and anger connected with the woman named Aeryn, a memory of a man’s face appears in his mind. The demented look in the man’s eyes, made it difficult for him to believe that it took so long for him to remember it. Soon after remembering the man’s face his name, Fleming came to him, followed by the almost slow motion replay of the gun that he fired, impacting upon his love Aeryn. Crichton. Crichton was his name, for it was the last word top come from her lips, as she slowly slipped away. He could now see how the anger and hatred were connected to the twisted man and the familiar feeling of anger entered his minds and instantly the super soldier serum, that generates adrenaline at an uncontrollable pace kicks in and more than three times the normal amount of adrenaline enters his bloodstream and begins to flow through his body. His efforts were doubled as all of his memories came flooding back to him faster, another by-product of the abnormal amount of adrenaline pumping through his body. All of his memories were now with him once again. His vowed mission of revenge, where he was and what he had done to get into this position and more importantly that the twisted man, named Fleming was on the site. The adrenaline flowed faster and faster through his body as he began to explode with rage. He would not die this day. ************ Two Months Ago… (At the same time Aeryn Sun died and Crichton was injected with the super serum) As the Military base explodes into flames all around him, Mr. Fleming stumbles half-conscious, through the debris rubble, to the door of the barracks that leads to the outside. He manages to get through the door, but as he takes his next step he trips and falls to the floor. He could feel the darkness of unconsciousness looming towards him, as his eyes are diverted to the building in which presumed the initial explosion took place. It was only a matter of time before the fire spreads to other combustible sections of the base. It seemed that the other soldiers on the base knew this too, as he notices them heading towards the exit/entrance way of the compound. The super soldier serum had proved to be a success, but was it too much of a success? That was the question that Fleming continued to ask himself. Crichton was too strong, he broke away from his bonds with the greatest of ease and threw him half way across the room, without even trying. What made the situation worse was the death of his love Aeryn Sun, this uncontrollable rage plus the super serum made him almost unstoppable. Almost. One thing was certain, if he managed to get out of this thing alive, Fleming would be the one to test Crichton’s vulnerability. Such bold vows for someone on the brink of death. Slipping into unconsciousness and the military base crumbling around him. He very much doubted that he would get out of this one. And they were his last thoughts as the warmth and darkness took over and he slipped into unconsciousness. The very next memory he had was the wind flowing through his air, forcing him out of the warmth of unconsciousness and back into the land of the living. He was flying! He was a little confused at first, his mind not able to process the data he found himself confronted with. He only really understands when he notices hands under his armpits, lifting him up. Fleming manages to turn his head slightly and confirms his suspicions. The Thunderbolt known as Mach III had saved him from certain death at the compound. As the thought crosses his mind, a deafening explosion takes place at the military base, levelling the majority of the compound. Fleming felt his stomach jump into his throat, as he accurately guessed that he and his saviour, Mach III, were heading towards land. The landing was surprising clean, as Fleming stumbles to the ground, then being is propped up by the Thunderbolt known as Atlas. Mach III lands comfortably close to him, making it look as simple as walking. After the clean landing, Mach III slowly moves closer to him. “Fleming, we have a little business to discuss!” Mach III states clearly. A smile appears upon Fleming’s face, as he nods in agreement. ************ As John Crichton seemingly flew over the mesh wire security fence, three fully armed soldiers assemble, having seen the bizarre incident and watch as the he crashes soundly through the barracks roof. Slightly dismayed at the site, the three guards hesitate a little before one of them snaps out of the trance. “Jerry, Miguel, we’d better check this out!” The soldier declares, heading towards the barrack door. “That guy just crashed through the roof! Terry, we should call for back up!” Jerry replies still dismayed by the scene that took place mere seconds before. “We should at least alert security!” Miguel follows up. Terry shakes his head. “There’s no time! We gotta get in there now!” Terry declares, confidently opening the barrack door and entering the establishment. Jerry and Miguel simply look at each other, shrug, and then follow their commanding officer into the building. The scene before them could be described as nothing less than chaos, but oddly calming, at least to Jerry and Miguel. The roof had caved in on itself and nothing but rubble confronted them. All three of them instantly surmised that whatever the hell flew over the fence and crashed through was most definitely under all that rubble. This was a good thing because the soldiers sure as hell didn’t want to fight the person…the thing that caused it. All three soldiers eased themselves, letting their guns point to the side rather than directly at the pile of rubble. “Are we gonna call security now?” Jerry questions. Terry simply shakes his head. “There’s a big demonstration going on out there! We don’t want visiting soldiers to think we can’t handle a simply thing like…” Terry’s voice trails off as he notices sections of the rubble, beginning to move. The three soldiers are no longer at ease and aim their guns directly at the sources of movement. “What the hell is under there?” Miguel questions, rhetorically. “Bugger me if I know! But I don’t think it will be friendly!” Jerry replies. “We’re not gonna give the thing chance to decide what side its on, when it comes outta there just fire upon the damned thing!” Terry orders, sweat trickling down the side of his head. The other two nod in agreement. The movement in the rubble becomes more and more frequent and more volatile, as a couple of planks of wood fall off the pile that currently buries Crichton. With each and every second the movement becomes more volatile. Then some kind of primal scream is heard, causing the three soldiers to edge more and more towards the door. “FLEMING….YA BASTARD….” Crichton bellows, adrenaline flowing, as he simply wades out of the rubble at full speed, pieces of stone, wood and plaster are sent in all directions, one piece catches Terry at the side of the head knocking him to the ground. The other two however are seemingly paralysed by fear, but in reality they don’t have much time to respond, as Crichton charges through them, taking them out with a series a punches and kicks, far too fast for the naked eye. Without even breaking his stride, Crichton bursts through the barracks door and makes his way towards the area where he saw Fleming last, this time not bothering to use any stealth tactics whatsoever. Crichton rounds the corner of the barracks and there he is confronted by a gigantic robot. Crichton takes a step back in amazement, especially when he notices the same familiar twisted face, at the control box, at the very top of the robot. The robot was at least twelve-foot tall, and Crichton decided that it was more of a battle suit than a robot. Fleming was actually inside the robot at the very heart of the machine, protected by a steel alloy shell. You don’t have to be a genius to figure out that this whole thing was an ambush. Crichton cared very little about being ambushed, his anger saw to that. He surely wasn’t going to back down that was for certain, but he did have his doubts. Had he underestimated his enemy? It didn’t matter now anyway, the battle was going to happen and he decided that he would face it like a man. A nice change from some of his recent actions, he realised, but some things were out of necessity rather than choice. To be continued….
