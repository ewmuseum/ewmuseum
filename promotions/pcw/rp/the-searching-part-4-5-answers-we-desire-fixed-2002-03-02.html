BOBBY STORMBRINGER The Searching? - Part 4.5? - Answers We Desire --2-28-02, Thursday afternoon, 2:21 pm EST, still one day before Fusion-- The next scene fades up, not too many moments farther ahead than the last promo Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer has cut. What we see first is a side view of Mr. Prime Time's average-sized living room from right in front of the front door, on the inside. Further on in the background is the combonation dining room/computer room. A doorway leads to the narrow kitchen, and a hallway leads off to the right from in between the combo room and the living area. The computer side of the dining room is fashioned with a desk that is built into the wall for the ancient computer and accessories like headphones and a microphone to sit upon. Just above that are three shelves with compartments to the left and the right, which makes six total. Cluttered junk like old phone books, scissors, novels, cigar boxes, and pewter dragons litter those. Right next to the desk, against the wall on the kitchen side is a CD rack with all sorts of selections. Games and music alike. Quake three, Baldur's Gate two, Linkin Park, Californication from the Chili Peppers, The Beastie Boys Anthology, a couple of Eminem cassette tapes for both of his most recent albums, Starcraft, both Diablo one and two, and the list goes on and on and on for the Florida packrat. A packrat is someone who never throws anything away, just in case you did not know. A portable telephone also sits on top of the tall CD stands that so closely resemble the same stands that hold his videotapes in the basement. On the left side of the room is the smallish, pale tan dining table that has been pressed against the wall, three stiff chairs pressed against it on the other three sides. The table is peculiarly clear, thanks to personal assistant Carl Everstrong. Just above the table hangs a very lame joke. Carl worked up the nerve to buy The Showstopper a WWF 2002 calender for Christmas, instead of the PCW one that B.S. really wanted. After letting Carl hear about it, B.S. just bought himself the PCW calender. Ironically enough, the 'ol' mentor' himself is pictured above the table in green for the month of March. Triple H. See what happens when you have lots and lots of money when you are young? The right people provide you with the services and training that you require in the formative years of your career. Mexi's training with Ultimo Dragon is a perfect example. While Lance Storm and Triple H's combined efforts gave Bobby Stormbringer the tools, it was up to 'The Bringer Of The Storms' to apply those tools to his most beneficial advantages. The foundation is sound, yet those brittle knees were anything but that. Now that the knees seem to be in the clear after all the surgeries, a bit of quickness and a burst of speed when desperately needed can be achieved in any given match. That's exactly how he beat Brock Newbludd, exactly how he got by in the number one contender's match for the Rising Star title, over Rush, Anton, and Leblanc. Yes, Karl Leblanc, current jobb- err, Extreme Champion. Yeah. The belt that has managed to be less well off than Rising Star. Unless he is World Champion, Mr. Prime Time plans on all of that changing, and very soon. The narrator wishes to use this time to remind you that he has already beaten FOUR current or former PCW Champions, and one while he still was a champion. Namely Hank~! HANK I SAY~! Who should be number one or at least the number two contender for the Extreme title after Madness if he is still titless?? Take three guesses, people. Hint: He's defeated the last two Extreme Champions, and it's only not three because Brandi high-tailed it out of here. The camera shot spins around to the other main room in the area, the living room. New, rare paintings have been framed and entrapped in glass for their own protection. All paid for by cold, hard, Eternal Wrestling money. These irrational contracts, trying to buy the loyalty of others... part of the reason they went bankrupt? Does anyone really care!? Didn't think so. The old, rickity entertainment center and modest TV inside of it? Gone. The seventeen-inch television set now also resides in Bobby's bedroom. Replacing it is a gigantic wide screen TV that is almost as high as the ceiling, and half as wide as the entire wall! A Playstation 2, which B.S. finally bought to celebrte his PCW contract, sits right in front of the massive viewer. A stack of PS2 games are stacked twenty-five high on the left side of the TV. Yet another VCR, this one only for 'family' viewing, sits on top of the TV. The AOL-Time Warner cable is run right through it, so no cable box is needed. Damn you AOL. Damn you straight to the bottom of hel- um. Sorry. Got a little carried away there, but those corporate bastids need to be dealt with! Almost as bad as GlobeTech, I'm tellin' ya. The new coffee table is much nicer, a combonation of a glass top and black marble throughout the rest of it. 'Adorable friend' Kira liked the evergreen-colored fake-leather couches, and B.S. was in no hurry to get rid of them, so the love seat and main couch have stuck around. As average-sized and 'un-mansionlike' this rent-to-own house is, at least the insides of it are now close to respectful, in The Showstopper's eyes. We zoom in on PCW's Bobby Stormbringer and Kira with her pink tank top, gold necklace, and dark yellow shorts, both lounging in eachother's arms and watching a DVD of Monty Python and The Holy Grail. We catch a sneak peek of one of the best scenes, 'The Black Knight'. After a while of snickering and flat out laughing at those strange Brits, Bobby and Kira start holding a semi-quiet conversation. KIRA: Hehehe! This movie is too much. STORMBRINGER: I can't believe you've never seen it before. This is one of the few things that Carl actually got right over the holidays, buying me this. With my own money that I paid him, but oh well. Take whatcha can get. KIRA: Must of been a nice surprise, then. Kira beams a smile at her guy, while taking occassional glances at the silliness on screen. B.S. just smirks back to the younger woman who'll turn twenty years old in July. Not for what she said, but rather for what he's about to say. STORMBRINGER: Actually? It wasn't one. Kira snaps her head around and frowns at Bobby, while B.S. manages to not laugh at the expression somehow. KIRA: Wha..? Why not, babe?? STORMBRINGER: Because I peeked while Carl was at the store one day, just like right now. Then I replaced the tape. Heh heh, he never noticed! Kira punches Bobby in the shoulder again. She was just being playful, or so she thinks. Yet.. Bobby silently opens his mouth to 'scream' in pain and he rubs the spot that will certainly be bruised by the morning. So how does such a tough broad of a tomboy ever look this good!? KIRA: That was meeeaann. He went to all that trouble to find that for you, too. STORMBRINGER: Oh c'mon, hon. The surprise doesn't matter, it's the fact that I do appreciate it. That is what matters. KIRA: Mm-hmm. Sure. Whatevah. STORMBRINGER: No really. I mean it. Hey! KIRA: What?? STORMBRINGER: Ha, don't you realize what you just said? You said.. oh that's right. Your family's from New York, so you have the accent too. Bobby squeezes Kira around the waist and he chuckles at her for the northeastern accent slip up. Kira does her best to ignore it, alhtough her teasing smile is a plain indicator that she can't do that, at least not fully. KIRA: Where up north where you born again..? STORMBRINGER: Bridgeport, Connecticut. Same as Tyler Davidson. Well, the same state, not the same city. I don't think so, anyways. You know after I moved here to Orlando I found out something. KIRA: Mmmmkay... What did you find out and who's this Tyler guy? STORMBRINGER: Oh, uh. I found out that entire city of Bridgeport went bankrupt. Heh, I'm not kidding! KIRA: Wow! Is that even possible?? STORMBRINGER: Apparently. I feel bad for the people there now, but I thought it was pretty freggin' funny at the time. Oh yeah, Tyler Davidson is someone that I may face off against soon. KIRA: What, that tournament thingy? STORMBRINGER: Heh heh. Yes, the Global Title Tournament. Winner faces Nosferatu for a shiny gold belt. B.S. didn't want to say that his first name was Vampir, because he knew that Kira would start to freak out, if only for a few seconds. She's just a bit on the nutty side like that, ya know? STORMBRINGER: I have to beat Brian Gaines.. Brain Games.. whatevah. In my opinion, if I can beat Ritchie Syxx, then I should be able to beat this guy. KIRA: Didn't you already beat Syxx last week, though..? STORMBRINGER: Exactly my point. Then Davidson has to get past Ben J. Palithorpe, a former World Champion. KIRA: Ouchies. STORMBRINGER: Tell me about it. That one should be a war and come down to the wire. Either guy would be tough as all hell to beat, I think. The third round is where my gut told me I would end up going. No one expected me to get by Ritchie, so all this is now gravy for my rep. KIRA: Maybe those disrespectin' f*ckers'll start figuring out who the real top guys of pro wrestling are, right? You gotta love chicks who curse up a storm. 'Curse up a storm'..? Ack. Stupid puns. STORMBRINGER: Right. Maybe, that's up to their own visions of the future and actually trying to have some common sense towards things. KIRA: Enough about tha tourney. Tell me about PCW.. gimme a spoiler. Whatcha gonna do? STORMBRINGER: When MiyaHogan runs wild on you? What? Kira cutely pokes Bobby in the tummy. A sensitive area, and it makes Bobby's eyes bug out, makes him laugh through gritted teeth. STORMBRINGER: Hehe, quit it! KIRA: Don't start that 'What?' sh!t again, please! You drive me nuts with that Austin thingy. STORMBRINGER: Heh, alright. Here's the short version. I want the World Heavyweight title, since I'm in the match for it. Might as well go for the whole thing right now. If I don't get it, I want Darkstar, Miyagawa, or Xavier to get it. I sure as hell don't want Tyler Nelson or K-Dub to get it. KIRA: K-Dub..? Oh yeah, Kid Wonder. STORMBRINGER: Right... that Jimbo. The Chef wold be almost equally sickening. No, at least K-Dub is just 'uber cocky'. Chef would definitely be more of a travesty. KIRA: Why is that, hon? STORMBRINGER: Don't ask, darlin', it would only piss you right off. KIRA: Um. Okay? Keep goin', what else you want to do? STORMBRINGER: Make sure that Green.. sorry, The End.. doesn't get involved in Jeremy Howard's business with Green's buddy Drake Maxwell. That same guy, Maxwell. What do I want? I want to break him in two and let Xavier literally pick his bones, since he deserves it. Xavier's been dealing with this fool since NTICW, and I couldn't imagine having to listen to those opaque ramblings for months on end. I ramble too, but at least I speak the truth. KIRA: That's why I lov- uuhhh. STORMBRINGER: Huh? KIRA: Forget it, keep going! Kira blushes, her cheeks becoming a bright red.. but Bobby isn't looking, threfore he doesn't notice it. STORMBRINGER: After all this is over with Maxwell and his blind lil' buddy, then I'm going straight after LeBlanc. That joker better not think that I forgotthat 'Black Magic' in my eyes and all that other crap he did to me a few weeks ago. Plus he's Extreme Champion? If I don't work out with the World title in the battle royal, I'm going straight for that division. I won't try to step on Jason Core's toes cause he's cool wif me. But still... no one tries to punk me out without some sort of a response, at least eventually. KIRA: What about these guys.. these three guys who tried to attack you last month..? Bobby looks at Kira and she seems to be getting scared for The Showstopper, as it is read on the expression on her face. Bobby sighs and pulls the new light in his life close to him, lightly resting her head on his chest. Bobby runs his fingers through her soft red hair as a shot in the dark at comfort. When Bobby talks, he speaks much more quietly. STORMBRINGER: Kira... don't worry about it. I can take care of myself. My wrestling career is hotter than ever, and life with you around is great. Carl isn't even getting on my nerves as often. Kira lets loose a weak laugh, which is what Bobby was shooting for. STORMBRINGER: Babe. I'm not gonna let three jealous jerkys take me down. They an make this is much of a mystery as they want, I don't care. All of us will get what we deserve. Good, bad, or indifferent. I believe that, and I don't know I could go on if I didn't. KIRA: Bobby..? STORMBRINGER: Yeah? KIRA: Can we go out and see a movie? B.S. laughs at how timidly the question was asked, and he nods cheerfully to the fellow Northeasterner. STORMBRINGER: Righteous idea. I want to forget about it all for now.. everything except for you. Besides, this is my day off. B.S. kisses Kira on the forehead while she smiles. The Showstopper looks down at her while she raises an eyebrow, looking back up at him. KIRA: Time to argue about what movie we're gonna see, I bet. STORMBRINGER: And baby.. that's no b.s.! Kira giggles at the sudden insertion of the catch phrase that is sweeping the nation and she reaches up to caress Bobby's face while he smiles that Tom Cruise-quality grin of his. The camera pans over to get a good look of the movie and it shows the Black Knight without arms or legs, King Arthur and his squire *ahem* 'riding' off in search of more knights for the round table. KNIGHT: Ahh! Running away, aye!? Come back 'ere! I'll bite yo' legs off!! With that classic moment, we cut to black. One day until Fusion, where B.S. plans to do nothing except cheer for Tyler Nelson. We'll explain that potentially world-ending event later. Two days until his GTT match in the second round. Four days until the first real chance for a dream to come true for Robert Maxwell Stormbringer II. Six days until Drake Maxwell, one on one. Hopefully. Ten days until Magic Kingdom Massacre. Ten days until some real answers for B.S. and the people close to him. Answers for everyone. However... Will they be the answers we desire? THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002.
