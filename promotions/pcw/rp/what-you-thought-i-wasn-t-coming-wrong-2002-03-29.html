What? You thought I wasn't coming? Wrong!

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-02 In nine days, a battle of epic proportions will be waged. Thing is, it never had to be like this. But, it is. For weeks now, Xavier Kannon and Jeremy Howard have been at each other's throat nearly every time they are in the same building. And, with the mixed tag match scheduled for tonight on Fusion, how far will these men go? It's no secret, they're both out for blood. At what cost, though? It's a safe bet that Friday Fusion is only a taste of what's to come at Spring Fling between these two. So, how does a lunatic get ready for such a big match? Well, as the scene begins to spiral in from black, the Krazed Lunatic is seen tossing his shirt back on in the backseat of Jessica's red Ferrari 355 F1 Spider she was just presented with in Jeremy Howard's last promo. Jessica sits behind Jeremy, a black silk comforter covering her luscious body. Jeremy wraps his arm around his half naked girlfriend, relaxing in the backseat of her dream machine in the garage of his Elkhart, Indiana home. Jeremy: I feel better already about Fusion. Jessica leans over, planting a kiss on his cheek. Jessica: I knew my love would help ease you. Jeremy: Just having you in my life eases me. Wrestling is just a second life for me. Jessica: You still going to wait until later to tell me that plan of yours? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jeremy: Just remember this, you won't be in harms way unless you choose to be. Jessica is seen reaching underneath the blanket, reattaching what is presumably her bra back together. Since she was wearing a light blue leather bodysuit before this fornication of love, it's going to be a bit harder to hide what these two lovers were doing to the cameras. Jessica: We have five hours until Fusion. What are we going to do? In the background, outside of the garage, noises of splashing prevail in the wind. Justin, Jeremy's now freed brother is playing with Troy in the massive sized pool that dominates Krayzie's backyard just as one were to step out of the backdoor of his house. Of course, he also has a basketball court, tennis courts, a full fledged dancing club and other various pleasures in his backyard. But, the pool is where his brother and son choose to have fun right now. Jeremy: Well, those two appear to be very happy right now. Jessica: Yeah, but Troy's been in the pool for almost an hour. He's got to get out soon.... And, I'd like to take him with us this time. At least to Sacramento, if not the arena. Jeremy helps Jessica zip up her bodysuit from the rear, then puts her on his lap. Jeremy: Why don't we take Troy to the arena before Fusion, show him some of the Kings memorabilia, then let him watch the show with Justin? Jessica: You want Troy at ringside? Jeremy: Not ringside. Maybe a few rows back. Jessica sighs, shaking her head back and forth. Jessica: What is Troy going to think of me when he sees me beating Ellie's face until its bloody? Jeremy smiles. Jeremy: Hopefully he's watching the bright lights on the ceiling during that part. Jessica: Good, I won't have to let up on her battered face then. Jeremy: Alright Mike Tyson. Let's grab the boys so we can head out to Cali. Jessica reaches underneath the blanket, grabbing something on the anatomy of the Krazed Lunatic. Jessica: Whatever you say boss. Jeremy gets up from the backseat of the Ferrari, stepping out of it. Then, like a true boyfriend would treat his lady, Jeremy helps the lovely Jessica out. Jessica glances back at the red vehicle. A little over three years ago, Jeremy presented her with that car for her eighteenth birthday. It took every single dollar he had earned from winning the match that cost him his brother. But, to the Krayzie One, it was well worth it. Jessica: Thank you, honey. Jeremy reaches out for her hand, grasping it with the love of a thousand hearts wrapped into one. Jeremy: It was my pleasure. Jeremy, still attached at the hand to Jessica, leads her out of the garage and back through the house until they reach the back door that resides in the den. Jeremy turns the knob on the door, opening it up. Once there, the two parents catch sight of their two year old son splashing about in the kiddie section of the pool while his uncle Justin keeps a watchful eye over him. Jeremy: Justin, ever been to Sacramento? Justin turns his eyes away from Troy, looking directly at his brother. Justin: Only if you count the dreams I had while locked up. Jeremy: Pack a suitcase. You're going with us. Justin's eyes light up. Justin: You mean, I get to go to the home of the Kings? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jessica smiles, doubling the 'yes'. Justin: Cool! Jessica reaches down and grabs the baby out of the kiddie pool, while Justin rushes, a big no-no around a pool, toward the house to pack his things. Jessica: He sure seems excited. Jeremy: I know. A silence falls over the air, until a thought comes to Jeremy's mind. Jeremy: You know, if Chef does start to ramble on endlessly about Islam during our match and I fall asleep in the ring. Jessica: Yeah? Jeremy: That'll leave me wide open to get squashed by that disgusting Bertha Clone. My twenty second birthday is the day after Turmoil! I can't spend it squashed! Jessica just shakes her head, sighing. Jessica: Just throw a pork chop away from the ring and you won't have to worry about Bertha. Jeremy: Then I can just stuff my boot down the Chef's throat to silence his polluted views on the world. Jeremy kneels down, helping Jessica dry Troy off with a towel. After they're done, Jeremy looks up at Jessica, into her eyes. Jeremy: By God, I think we have a plan. And, that's a scary thought. This man is widely known for doing things out of the ordinary and somewhat outrageous at times. Whatever this man has in mind for Fusion and/or Turmoil, can't be good for the opponent standing across the ring. Rather, it's definately bad. The question is, is it psychotic? As the scene begins to fade out, the Krayzie One begins his trek toward Fusion and Xavier Kannon.... to black!
