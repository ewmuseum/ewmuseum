It's Tough To Say Goodbye

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/03 Payback, they say it’s a bitch. If all that’s true then it should be interesting what happens what happens on Thursday with a four corners match that places the members of P.W.A. and Chris and I all against each other. There were so many motives that could cause anyone one of us four to destroy any of the three others we were facing. For example perhaps Gaz would seek revenge at Nerezza for seemingly not caring that Natalia left him. Or perhaps Chris could lash out on me for not doing more to try and prohibit the marriage of Gaz and I. Then again it could always just turn into an all out tag team brawl as would be suspected with P.W.A. giving all attempts to make Chris and my lives a further living hell. Too bad payback didn’t apply in all cases or I would have gotten revenge on the woman named Allison long ago for tearing apart a happy family. Today was the day it was set to happen. Any minute now Allison would come parading through that door looking for the children that were rightfully hers and our family would no longer consist of five, it would be lowered to three. I knew that, Chris knew that, and the kids knew that. The question was who was it affecting the most? I could tell that the kids wanted nothing to do with this woman who was a stranger of them for the majority of their life, but at the same time maybe things would change for them for the better. At least they wouldn’t have to be around the constant tension that had been existing as of late inside the house because of the mixed feelings shared between Chris and I. But at the same time Chris and I were like parents to them and I can only imagine how much it would break their little hearts to have yet another person they loved torn away from them like that. Then there was Chris. Could you only imagine the things running through his head right now? Perhaps he was feeling the slight tinge of guilt I was feeling. Maybe if we weren’t too busy dealing with our own personal problems as a result of P.W.A.’s prank gone wrong then perhaps we could have stopped Allison from ever getting custody. I’m sure that he fells like just as much failure just as I have. I’m sure that now he’ll only become much more silent and withdrawn than he’s already been all week. Of course there was still Evan, the youngest out of all of us to worry about. First he has his own mother taken from him after a violent fit of rage from his father, then he loses his father to jail time, and now he’s having the two other children he considered siblings ripped away from him because a woman suddenly changes her mind and decides she is ready to take care of kids. The poor child must feel as helpless as I did the day I ended up being accidentally married to Gaz. As for me, I didn’t know what to think. Perhaps Allison had changed. Maybe now she could actually be a good mother and not walk out on her children as she had before. I seriously doubted it because of the smirk she had displayed every time she came over to remind me that the children belonged to her but what could I do? Absolutely nothing considering the only thing I shared with the children was a last name that I no longer had. The only thing I could do is wait with the children and do my best to assure them that everything was okay, even if I couldn’t assure that myself. “Tessa?” The soft voice of Christa fell on my ears as she tugged at my shirt. “Will you still come visit us?” “Of course.” I replied hoping that perhaps that would comfort her somewhat, though I had no clue what would happen from here and if I would ever see her and Alex again once Allison took them. With that question answered the house once again filled with that silent tension as we all waited for the arrival of Allison and cherished what could perhaps be our last moment together. Knock Knock Knock Just as expected Allison had arrived right on time. I walked over to the door and slowly opened it as the children reached over to their suitcases looking like they might burst into a chorus of tears at any moment. I didn’t blame them for looking so disgruntled, I wouldn’t want to leave my true home to live with such a woman either. “It’s so nice to see you.” Allison proclaimed in a phony cheer, flashing me a smile and then looking over at the kids and embracing each one of them in a hug as if she had been there for them their whole lives. “Well hello there!” Called out a voice from behind Allison that I was not expecting. I could tell it belonged to a male because it was much deeper than a female voice could ever get. I looked up and noticed someone I had hoped just like Allison I would never see again, it was Ein. What was he going here? Could he and Allison be…nah! That could never happen, or so I hoped for the children’s sake. “What is he doing here?” I mouthed over to Chris without actually saying a word. Chris simply shrugged; he must have been as clueless as I was. “Honey these are my children, Alex and Christa.” Allison said to Ein as she pointed to each child at the mention of their name. “HONEY?” I questioned aloud in shock. This couldn’t be happening, but it was and now the children I considered my own were going to a Chris twin and a woman I couldn’t stand. “You didn’t know Tessa?” She questioned before stating “Ein and I are engaged!” “ENAGAGED?” I sputtered out hardly able to believe the words going into my ears. Instead of coming back with a witty response as usual both Ein and Allison just stood there and smirked knowing that they wouldn’t have to say anything because they had already on my nerves without even trying and pretty much defeated me with that simple sentence. “Let’s get on our way now kids.” Allison said while taking Christa by the hand as Ein gripped onto Alex’s and lead them towards the front door. Without saying a word both the kids waved goodbye to Chris, Evan, and I with sorrowful faces. There was nothing I could do now, they were gone out the front door and not coming back. The only thing I could do now is hope that somehow I’d be able to see them again but until then I had a match for both Tuesday and Friday to worry about, so I headed upstairs towards the bedroom to get my thoughts off what had just happened before my mind and get my mind focused on Tuesday and Friday where I had a chance to turn all this bad luck around.
