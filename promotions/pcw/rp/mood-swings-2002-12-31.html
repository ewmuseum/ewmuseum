Mood Swings

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Mood Swings Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 31/12/02 “Morning, my little Poon Puppy.” Our shot opens up as the low New Year’s Eve morning sun creeps into the bedroom of Eleanor Kannon-Hall’s Greenwich Village apartment where we see the PCW CEO Xavier Kannon in the unfamiliar clothing of a Black robe, carrying a tray of Toast, Coffee and Fresh Orange Juice. His Red hair is tied back in a pony tail and he has yet to shave, or shake off that ‘just got up’ look. “Like.... Not night anymore and stuff?....” From under the thick covers comes the sleepy voice of PCW’s Commissioner, Eleanor, accompanied by rustling as she slowly pops her head out into the daylight, rubbing her eyes as Xavier puts the tray on the beside table and kisses the top of her head. Xavier : “It’s morning indeed, and we have a flight in 2 hours.” Ignoring the bit about catching a flight, Ellie~!’s eyes start to well up about it being morning and not night anymore, her faces screwing up. Eleanor : “But Ellie~! likes night.... And now it isn’t night anymorrrrrrrrrre....” Talking through the tears she tries to sniff back, the little prep can’t contain herself anymore and bursts into tears in mourning for night. She scurries under the covers as her sobbing is heard from under the covers as Xavier rolls his eyes back and mutters to himself. Xavier : “That time of month again...” As she sighs, he realises he might have spoken a bit too loud, a suspicion that was accurate as Ellie~! is heard yelling from under the bedclothes. Eleanor : “WELL I’M.... LIKE.... SO SORRY FOR BEING A WOMAN AND STUFF!!!” Xavier cringes as her shrieking voice pierces the fabrics and gets into his ears. As more sobbing is heard from under the covers, Xavier pours himself a glass of Orange Juice and starts to prepare for a day of emotion and mood swings. Eleanor : “I DON’T HEAR YOU COMPLAINING ABOUT EVERYTHING ELSE YOU GET FROM ME, LIKE, WAY BEING A WOMAN!!” After taking a small sip and rubbing his eyes, Xavier slowly sits down on the side of the King Size bed and rubs the sheets where Ellie~! is huddled up in a ball, still sobbing lightly. Xavier : “I’m....” He pauses, realising that sympathy is not a usual trait of his, but shrugging it off. Xavier : “.... I’m sorry my little Shag Squirrel... Do you want to stay here today instead of flying?” There is a respite in the crying from underneath the covers and he smiles, hoping that’ll be the end of it before her shrill voice again blares out to startle him. Eleanor : “HELLO?!? SO, LIKE, NOW YOU DON’T EVEN WANT ME AROUND YOU?!?!” The Scientologist grits his teeth and shoots his head back in annoyance at walking right into that one, not knowing what to say to ease the feeling of a very temperamental prep. Eleanor : “I thought you loved Ellie~!...” Her voice softens as she coughs back more tears, Kannon leaning over and hugging the mass of covers the little prep has pulled around her, kissing the top of the pile and stroking where her head is to ease her. Xavier : “I do... I do... Now come out of there and have some breakfast...” Slowly, we hear her answer softly and timidly. Eleanor : “Like... Ok...” After much scurrying about under the covers, Ellie slowly peeks her tired little head out, her eyes Red and puffy and her hair frizzy at the ends. Kannon lifts his feet up and swings them around to lay on the bed, Ellie~! dragging her top half covered with a Lilac vest out from under the covers and resting it on his chest. Eleanor : “Ellie~! want toast.” Her Bambi-esque eyes look at the breakfast tray before she hugs him around his torso and closes her eyes, smiling as she tries to find a comfortable spot to bury her head. Xavier : “You can have whatever you want my little Fuck Ferret, I didn’t think you’d be wanting a big breakfast if we have to fly in a-” With these words, Ellie~!’s pale Blue eyes shoot open and her head springs off his chest. The look of happiness and contentment gone from her face and replaced with anger as Kannon looks at her peculiarly, plate with toast on in his hand. Eleanor : “And, like, why’s that? Are you SO saying I’m fat and stuff?!?” Muttering, Kannon doesn’t know how to respond and instead just passes her the toast which she knocks out of his hand and tumbling down onto the floor. Xavier : “All I was saying is that we have a flig-” Before he can even finish his sentence, the water-works start up again in Ellie~!’s vacuous eyes and her lip starts to quiver. Eleanor : “Now you, like, think Ellie~! is WAY fat and ugly!” Bawling her eyes out again, the little prep pulls the covers back over her head and starts to mutter through the tears about being no better than Teresa Tomas and she’s from the South. Xavier : “Maybe I should just say nothing...” Kannon shakes his head again as he sighs, Ellie~! now muttering about being too fat, ugly, horrible and like rank and stuff to talk to. Xavier : ...Or say something...” With more sobbing coming from under the covers, Kannon pulls them back but Ellie~! tries to pull them to her again, trying to hide but Kannon calms her down, running his fingers through her hair then kissing her ear, whispering something softly into it that cheers the little prep up and softens her resistance. Eleanor : “Like.... Okay.” Pulling the covers over him, Kannon rolls behind Ellie~! and spoons her, pulling the hair from her eyes and kissing the side of her neck before reaching across to the other bedside table to pull a tissue from the box and dry her eyes with. Xavier : “See, you’re not ugly and fat... You’re Ellie~!” As she smiles in his arms and rests her head on the fluffy pillow, she mutters softly. Eleanor : “And that’s good, right?” Behind her, his chin resting on the top of her head and nestled in her long mane of Blonde hair he thinks it over for a second, his eyes giving away that he wants to make a sarcastic answer but he can’t. Xavier : “It’s the best.” Replying warmly, Xavier holds her tighter and the content little Commissioner purrs, stretching her body out before retracting it again and snuggling back up to her man. A minute or so passes, and Ellie~!’s closed eyes suddenly open again as the sound of Xavier snoring is heard behind her and we see that Kannon’s head had rolled sidewards and his mouth dropped open, the CEO now deep in slumber and his preppy little wife lets out a giggle before rolling around her rest her tired head on his sleeping body and pulling the covers back up over her shoulder as the shot fades to Black. “He... Like... SO falls for it every month and stuff!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
