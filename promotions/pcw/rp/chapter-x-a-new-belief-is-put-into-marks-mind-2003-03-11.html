Chapter X: A New Belief Is Put Into Mark’s Mind…

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-3-03 ::Introduction:: Heading into the week Mark is thinking about the match at March Madness. He has only this chance in his career to win the Heavyweight title and he could do it. But it is very unlikely because there are wrestlers like Skylar Green, Akiyama, Xavier Kannon, and Snow not to mention a few. How can Mark win? He doesn’t have a chance since they have already been in the high cards and Mark is in the mid cards. Mark doesn’t know how to perform in a main even battle royal. All eyes will be on him and the other competitors, but this is going to show who is ready to hold the title outright. We continue to look into the past of Mark Makonie from the last time he was in PCW and his changing of beliefs. ::End Introduction:: Chapter X: A New Belief Is Put Into Mark’s Mind… As Mark goes into the little airport in the little Mexico city he notices that a lot of the writing on the building is in Spanish. Mark is not going to be able to handle this long enough to stay and put the beliefs into the Mexicans. Maybe this isn’t the right place to get the Mexicans to believe what he does, but he is going to try. “What in the hell does that read?” Mark asks a man standing right next to him as he looks up on the outside of the airport. “El portón 1.” Says the Mexican wearing the dark black glasses so you can’t see his eyes. “You need to speak some English here.” Mark says to the guy as he is talking to him like he is still in the US. “Yo no sé ingles.” The Mexican says as Mark is starting to get a little mad at the guy. “Fuck Sie!” Mark says in German to the guy as Mark heads into the little airport. Mark goes into the building that has el portón 1 on it and he sees that there is a secretary standing there at the desk. Mark starts to go towards her as he swings the bag over his shoulder so that he doesn’t have to carry it in his arm any longer. The airport is made up of one baggage claim and one gate and that is it. The walls are a blue color with a high ceiling being an off white color. The wallpaper is made up of some green cactuses. The lady is sitting there typing on her computer as Mark walks up to her. She is yet another spic lady and hopefully she can understand him. “Excuse me where can I get a taxi around here?” Mark asks as he sets his bag down on the patterned blue carpet. “Apenas correcto afuera.” The lady says as she doesn’t look up and Mark gives her an evil look. “Does anyone around here know how to speak a fucking word in English?” Mark asks as he is getting red in the face. A security guard dressed in a blue coat comes walking in as Mark screams at the top of his lungs. He starts to casually walk over to where Mark is standing as he needs to talk to him. Mark is looking over at him as he sees that the guy has a blonde mullet haircut. Mark is standing there trying not to laugh as he comes over and you can also tell that he is part of the spic nation. “Do you mean a translator?” The guy asks as he leans his arm on the desk and takes his glasses off. “Well someone who can tell the difference between a Spanish alphabet and an English alphabet.” Mark says as he takes a breath and then another so that they can understand what he is talking about. “Well there is a taxi outside if you would like to take that to the nearest city.” The spic security guard says as he is putting his glasses back on and is heading to go outside to continue his job. “Fuck Sie!” Mark says to the lady as he picks up his bag and the lady looks up from her screen and Mark just smiles at her. Mark swings his bag over his shoulder and he starts to head outside. The lady just looks back down at her computer as she starts to type some more and she is getting back to work. The gym shoes against the carpet make it a cushion for Mark to walk on and he just starts to head out of the sliding doors. A gush of warm air comes into the building as Mark exists and is going to start his new beliefs in this new town that he is going to be taken to. “This is Pedro and he is going to be your private taxi driver for your stay in Mexico.” The security guard says as Mark is heading to the trunk of the car. “Agradable reunirlo Marca.” Pedro says as he extends his hand towards Mark at the back of the yellow car. “What in the hell did he just say?” Mark says as he directs his attention towards the security guard in wanting an answer. “He said that its nice meeting you Mark.” The security guard says as the sun is blinding him in the eyes even with his sunglasses on. “Alright.” Mark says as he looks at Pedro and leaves him hanging. “Who paid for this taxi for me?” “Umm..a guy by the name of Jax sent some money down here.” The security guard says as he starts to walk over to the back seat door. “He said that his number is on this card if you want to get a hold of him. He heard about your firing and he wants to get together with you sometime while you are down here.” Mark grabs the card from the security guard as the security guard opens up the door for him to get in. Mark puts his head down so he doesn’t hit it on the top of the door. Pedro then shuts the trunk door as he goes around to front of the car. He nods at the security guard and then he gets into his car door. The security looks at Mark and signals for him to put his window down. Mark puts his window down as he keeps on looking at the card. “So I don’t get any thanks or a tip for helping you out down here?” The guard asks as he puts his head near the window. “The spics shouldn’t get any respect around here.” Mark says as he puts up his window and he tells Pedro to drive off.
