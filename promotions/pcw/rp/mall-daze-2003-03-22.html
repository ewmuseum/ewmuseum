Mall Daze

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/22/03 The snow of winter was melting away and giving room to the rays of the sun, a sign spring was on its way. The temperature was reaching in the fifties and people all over were breaking out their spring time clothes, which meant only one thing for the couple of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. It was time to take the kids shopping for some spring clothes to wear. The mall was busy as normal as people walked around looking at the different stores. A few people commented to each other and stared as the local celebrities passed by. Chris, Tessa, and the kids was use to the staring on by now. Being famous in a town like Chatham, people tend to look at you as if you were really something special, even if underneath it all you really were just like them. The gang made their way to a clothes store to browse around. Tessa was admiring all the beautiful spring colors while Chris kept more to himself and quiet. Something was bothering him very badly but he wasn’t saying what it was. Alex, Christa, and Evan walked around the store looking at the different clothes. Evan made sure to keep close to Tessa while Alex and Christa wondered around the store more. “Chris what do you think of this?” came the angelic voice of Tessa Windsor. Chris looked over to where the voice rang out from. Tessa was holding up a cute little white tank top with a little tiger kitten on the front and “Grrrr” on the front in black letters. Although the shirt was very cute and would look wonder on Tessa, Chris just wasn’t himself right now. “Uh . . . it looks alright I guess. Just buy it if you life it.” Chris replies, looking out of the window of the store. Tessa frowns and folds the shirt over her arm. Tessa notices little Evan beside her. She looks down and kneels beside him. “And how about you little mister, what do you think of the shirt?” she asks him. Little Evan takes the shirt and looks at it. He holds it up against Tessa to see what it looks like on her. While nodding his head, “It looks very pretty on you mommy.” Tessa lets out an “Aww” and kisses Evan on the forehead. “Thank you sweetie” she tells him. Tessa rises back up to look for the other kids. Suddenly Chris’ voice rings out through the store, shocking Tessa. “Tessa come see this! Quick!” he shouts out to her. Rushing to his side, Tessa joins Chris at the window. Chris points out toward another store. Tessa let’s out a frightening gasp. There walking among the people was a man that caused both Chris and Tessa much pain and suffering, more so to Chris. Yet something seemed different about him. He wasn’t as threatening as he normally is though. Still, his mere presence is enough to send cold shivers down the spine of Chris. The cold words roll from his lips as he says his name. “Ein….” Those cold blue eyes turn and look straight at Tessa and Chris. The weight of his stare makes Tessa grab Chris’ hand for support. A snarl etches on Chris’ face as Tessa squeezes his hand tighter. If Ein was to make one false move, Chris would be ready to attack him without a second thought. Instead Ein simply looks at them with a smirk on his face. He turns his head back in the direction of where it came from. “Oh my goodness!” escapes Tessa’s lips in shock and surprise. Chris’ eyes grow wide with surprise. A woman walks up to Ein and wraps her arm around his. She turns and looks around and when her head is turned they can make out who it is. “Allison….what is she doing here?” the words came from Chris. He just stared on at the scene before him. He couldn’t be sure but something inside of him was telling him that Ein threw they were waiting. But still, what was Ein doing with Allison? Chris just waited on as Ein turned and wrapped his arms around Allison. He turned his head for a second and looked at Chris and Tessa with a sadistic smile on his face. He looks back at Allison and kissed her straight on the lips! Chris turned to Tessa. “We need to get out of here.” He tells her. Tessa just looks at Chris stunned. “But I want to know what is going on” Chris just grabs her wrist instead. “Tessa I have a very bad feeling. Now let’s get out of here right now. Please.” He pleads with her. Chris looks at Tessa with sad eyes. She frowns at him too and stands up. “Alex, Christa, Evan it’s time to go.” She calls out. Chris breathes a sigh of relief upon hearing her words. He stands up fully also. The three children gather around the two. Chris and Tessa take them by the hand and calmly led them out of the store so not to arouse any attention from the people nearby. Chris keeps his eyes on Ein the entire time. Ein however keeps his eyes locked on Chris as well. Just as Chris and the others make their way toward the doors leaving the mall Ein wave’s goodbye to them laughing to him. Allison, mother to the children and ex-wife to Tessa’s brother. Ein, the twin image of Chris Storm and deranged madman bent on making life miserable for Tessa and Chris. What started out as a simple day at the mall has turned out to be a weird twist in the already weird enough life of the Schmoopies. Why were Ein and Allison together? Why did they kiss? For right now those questions remain a mystery.
