Date: Recently... Fuck. Fuck! FUCK! FUCK! These Godsdamned battle royal things are getting annoying! I’m frickin’ sick of them! All I seem to be booked in lately are pointless bouts featuring an array of talent-less ‘cruiserweights’! When I tossed away that meaningless title at Spring Fling, I wasn’t trying to tell PCW that I’d like to be given ANOTHER chance to take it back! Still, I played to the crowd and acted as a dominant force throughout the match itself…obviously. Then again, if all they continue to do is book me against jobbers, why the Duat am I even BOTHERING staying here? Well, I’ll tell you why! Because I have a place to fill in PCW! I mean, looking about the roster at the moment it seems that El Diablo, Flaymin Aymin, Skylar Green, Clyde [if you can count him] and I, are the longest serving members of the PCW collective! Aside from us select few, the rest are newbies, rookies, low life mongrels who can’t get a job anywhere else. With the exceptions of my friends Violence Jack and Dorn Tracer, I truly believe the federation has contracted a cancer with these new signees! Still, when questioning their sanity, they give you some shit about ‘the fed having to move on’. I’ll give THEM on. I swear. The fact of the matter is, with Ra and Horus behind me, I’ll bring about the age of The Egyptian King to Primetime Championship Wrestling, and then what shall they do? NOTHING! They don’t hold the POWER over me to be able to! You see, with the Gods at my side, nothing can overcome me! Perhaps I was eliminated by some ragtard in an over-the-top elimination match, but this coming Fusion I’ll prove that Titu Xansiv hasn’t lost a beat! TITU XANSIV HASN’T LOST ANYTHING! ARGHHH! I’m STILL the man who beat Frisco last year! I’m STILL the man who overthrew that PATHETIC fool, Xavier Kannon! I’m still that same old Egyptian who recently beat the likes of current PCW Champion Totally Tubular Tommy, Clyde AND Violence Jack! I can do what the Duat I WANT around here, and NOBODY is going to stop me! No matter if you’re Skylar Green or if you’re the new Cruiserweight Champ, Miss Moonlight! Nothing can stop me now, and by this time next month, ‘the fans’ will see EXACTLY who and what I am! …A force to be reckoned with!
