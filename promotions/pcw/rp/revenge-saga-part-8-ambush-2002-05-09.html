Revenge Saga Part 8: Ambush...

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/02 Revenge Saga Part 8: Ambush… Last Time… Crichton rounds the corner of the barracks and there he is confronted by a gigantic robot. Crichton takes a step back in amazement, especially when he notices the same familiar twisted face, at the control box, at the very top of the robot. The robot was at least twelve-foot tall, and Crichton decided that it was more of a battle suit than a robot. Fleming was actually inside the robot at the very heart of the machine, protected by a steel alloy shell. You don’t have to be a genius to figure out that this whole thing was an ambush. Crichton cared very little about being ambushed, his anger saw to that. He surely wasn’t going to back down that was for certain, but he did have his doubts. Had he underestimated his enemy? It didn’t matter now anyway, the battle was going to happen and he decided that he would face it like a man. A nice change from some of his recent actions, he realised, but some things were out of necessity rather than choice. ************ Fleming reported back to the new army General now in charge of the newly dubbed “Project: Crichton” just after General Tranter was murdered. It had taken him that long to get back on the case, because he had to heal from the last encounter he had with Crichton, he had to develop a new strategy outside normal military channels and he had to maintain a low profile while Crichton was in charge. Ever since Mach III had saved him from the explosion that fateful day in Cincinnati, he had been developing the plan to ambush Crichton, he had to make sure Crichton would be there at the right time. He also had to make sure that the bait had to be substantial enough for him to walk into the ambush blindly and not be more cautious. Him alone being there could have accomplished that, but Ms Zemoff was also in attendance to ensure his anger and hatred would boil. Actually, Ms Zemoff had demanded to come along, for she and Fleming were now an item and she didn’t want him to face the savage animal that was John Crichton alone. Especially after hearing about the murder of General Tranter and Crichton’s latest scuffle with the Avengers, which seemed to end in a stalemate. Fleming made sure that he hadn’t underestimated Crichton this time, like he had done so many times before, even when Crichton hadn’t been injected with the super serum. Another reason Ms. Zemoff was in attendance was to see the efficiency of her robotic battle suit close up and watch the piece of advanced machinery in action. Ms. Zemoff had assured him that the battle suit was powerful enough to destroy Crichton ten times over, but the question Fleming kept on asking himself was; How can Crichton’s might be gauged? Crichton had fought the Avengers not so long ago and only did he survive, but he battle them into a stalemate. The Avengers were incredibly embarrassed by the whole affair. Fleming figured that the public’s dismay with The Avengers was only right, since they were the ones who helped Crichton escape in the first place. Fleming was not risking much by battling Crichton at all though, because even if he managed to survive the might of the battle suit, the Thunderbolts were waiting on standby, in a secret location not too far away. The demonstration of his ‘courage’ would gain him a high reputation within the military ranks and he would get more and more business thrown his way. Also if the robotic battle suit faired well during the confrontation it would gain the woman he loved a weapons developing contract with the United States army, which could earn her company billions. Ultimately the combination of these two after effects would make Fleming one of the most powerful men in the world. Getting the factors of the ambush within the compound itself was easy. The new General had appointed Fleming in charge of the operation as soon as he had come out of hiding and because ‘Project: Crichton’ was top priority Fleming was given the authority to do more or less whatever he wanted. He was appointed the head of the project because of his experience in dealing with Crichton previously, he knew almost everything about the man. The main problem he had in setting up the ambush was manipulating Crichton to actually show up on this day. It was no easy feat, Fleming could vouch for that. Fleming had trouble tracking Crichton down and the complications with some new woman he was seeing stood in the way of his objectives. In the end he lured Crichton to the compound by sending one of his agents out, posing as some kind of double agent and told Crichton that Ms. Zemoff would be attending a weapons testing program at the base today. That was at least enough to lure the man to the base. If he’d had ordered the agent to simply tell Crichton that he was going to be at the base too, that would have tipped the scales into the unpredictable. Crichton was unpredictable enough as it was, but Fleming figured that Crichton wouldn’t wait till the military base, he would have attempted hunted Fleming down straight away. This would have left Fleming’s preparations for Crichton short and probably would have lost to Crichton in battle that day. And now here he was, safely encased within the robotic battle suit, about to do battle with his arch-foe, the one man stopping him from being uncontrollably powerful. ************ Crichton stood face to face with the twelve foot robotic suit, undaunted and unafraid by the sight before him. Frankly, he didn’t have time to be any of those things, he only had time for anger, hatred and his lust for revenge. Each of them giving him courage and bravado matched by none. Even though his emotions were total focused upon the task at had, the thirst for revenge had presented him with an ironic and cruel way to adequately punish the murder. “So murderer, I guess your feeling quite, smug now that you’ve drawn me into this ambush?” Crichton bellowed at the twelve-foot monster. Crichton could see Fleming’s face turn from simple smugness, to a smug smile, through the clear plexi-glass upon the robotic battle suit. “Yes Crichton, I would say that it was quite an accomplishment, luring someone with as much experience as yourself into an ambush like this, I’m really disappointed in you John.” Fleming taunts, the smug smile etched upon his face not fading for an instant. “Then why did you bother to set this ambush up if you thought I’d spot it a mile away? What if the ambushed backfired?” Crichton questions. The smug smile upon Fleming’s face turns into a laugh. “You see Crichton I know you too well. I knew that your thirst for revenge would cloud your senses, alter your perception, and let me manipulate you in ways I never could before the whole revenge issue kicked in. Basically, your far too trusting now John, not nearly paranoid enough for the kind of game you’ve got yourself into.” Fleming explains, cockily. A smile develops upon Crichton’s face. “What if I did see the ambush coming and I did know this entire thing was going to happen, but I was relying upon the only unknown factor in this whole equation. The super soldier serum and its unpredictability for example. Crichton replies, equally cocky. The statement doesn’t knock the ever so smug smile from Fleming’s face. “That.” He begins “is why I have plan B!” he states mysteriously. Crichton shakes his head in disbelief. “You can’t plan against the unpredictable Fleming, you should know that!” Crichton declares. “Yes, but the backup plan I have is a very powerful one indeed!” Fleming declares. “So why isn’t this all powerful plan B, plan A?” Crichton questions mockingly. The smile upon Fleming’s face transforms from a smug one to a vindictive one. “Because I would prefer to handle scum like you myself!” Fleming declares, manoeuvring his robotic suit into a fighting stance. Crichton nods smirking slightly. “I’m so glad you just did that; I was tired of talking anyhow!” Crichton states taking a step back, developing a fighting stance of his own. Crichton didn’t know how he could defeat Fleming, while he was wearing that suit, he was next to impossible to beat. There was only one thing that could save him now, his new found powers and his ability to out think Fleming in battle situations. Crichton was taught how to use seemingly ineffective things as very effective weapons and since Fleming was never in the army he wouldn’t have that training. The only problem was, these tactics were taught to him specifically for use on humans, not humans wearing indestructible robotic battle suits. He just had to improvise, but that wasn’t going to be as easy as it sounded. Crichton’s thought process is broken, as Fleming brings a huge robotic fist aimed directly at Crichton’s head. Crichton doesn’t have much time to move, but he does, successfully rolling out of the way before the impact of the robotic fist. However, the sheer impact of the collision between the robotic fist and the concrete floor knocks Crichton off his feet, allowing Fleming to gain a better strategical position over Crichton. As Crichton regains his bearings Fleming moves into position, as the twelve foot robotic frame’s shadow engulfs Crichton. Not for the first time since the battle started Crichton decided that this wasn’t going to be easy. It wasn’t going to be easy at all.
