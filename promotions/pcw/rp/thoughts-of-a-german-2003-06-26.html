Thoughts of a German

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th June The scene opens up in a quiet and calm setting aboard the airship, upon the sun deck and in particular, the juicebar. As the camera pans around, there are a few people scattered around going about their business…some bar workers are cleaning up after the previous night’s events, some guests are relaxing near the pool and a few VIP members enjoy a quiet chat and their morning coffee. In the corner to our left, there are two figures sat on two high stools at a chest height table. One is veteran PCW journalist Cal Seaver and the other, judging by his immense size and build is PCW’s newest addition, Matthias Kuntz-Hunt. Cal sits with one leg crossing over the other and back up straight, as he glances at the clipboard, which rests on the edge of the round table. Hunt is sat back on his stool, although it looks as if the stool itself is going to shatter into a thousands pieces, due to Hunt’s size. As Cal talks, Hunt looks relaxed enough and tilts his head back with a nonchalant expression evident. The interviewer himself seems a little agitated, remembering his last meeting with this man ended with Hunt clamping his hand around his throat. However, he is a professional and duely gets on with the job at hand… Seaver: I know that things have changed since you last appeared on PCW television, such as your name and how you like to be referred to. So for the folks that aren’t aware of your new alias, how about you tell the world right now? Hunt pauses for a few moments as he clears his throat and sniffs to clear the nasal cobwebs. He then talks in a near-perfect English accent, although there is a slight tint of German in his deep-toned speech. Hunt: I’m sure that my name change occurred when I was part of this RUSH-PCW merger. In fact, I made it quite public. There’s an awkward silence as an embarrassed Seaver glances down to his clipboard as Hunt sighs and shakes his head. Seaver: Oh? Erm…well, what I meant to say is, there are some new faces in PCW since you were around for that short period. Hunt: Geht so…I see. I just wish that you pointed that out in the first place, to avoid the confusion we’ve come across. Seaver: Yes well… Hunt: Respekt. To show my respect to my family back in Germany, I decided to use my full name when competing as an active competitor. Matthias is my birth name, Kuntz is my mother’s maiden name and Hunt is my father’s family name. Sehr einfach, it’s quite simple if you think about it. Seaver: Just one tiny request before we continue; is there any chance you can pause talking in German for the duration of this interview? A frown slowly appears on Hunt’s face as he adjusts to get comfortable. Seaver: It’s just that…well…we don’t understand you when… Hunt is staring a hole through Seaver with his trademark glare, which forces the reporter to halt talking in mid-sentence. Hunt: I am German, Herr Seaver and if I do happen to mutter the odd German phrase or word, then it’s because it’s in my nature to do so. I’ve seen plenty Mexicans who do the same and likewise with the French-Canadians. Besides, whatever I do say in German is immediately translated into English; but I know that you fools are too ignorant to learn a new language. I hear that the United States of America represents the free world? Well why don’t you all adapt yourselves to the ever-increasing free world and enhance your intelligence while you’re at it. Cal nods his head as he clears his throat and Hunt motions for the interview to get off the ground. Seavers: I apologise. Okay then. Now you’ve been working “undercover”, so to speak, in the last few weeks by targeting Abner and subjecting him to many mind games and numerous attacks. Hunt: Er ist rührend…simply pathetic. I pride myself on being a ring technician, a mat specialist; learning everything there has to know about the art of wrestling…with some power and strength added into the mixture. Then you have ein arschloch like Abner stroll in with alcohol drinking and completely destroying tradition. Alcohol doesn’t belong in wrestling…it never has and it never will. I don’t care what has happened elsewhere, but as far as I’m concerned, if you want to drink yourself stupid, then Gehen Sie zum Bar….go to the bar. Seaver: But similar to where speaking the odd bit of German is natural to you, Herr Hunt; drinking beer is very natural to a man like Abner. Hunt: It’s unnatural to mix wrestling and alcohol. Seaver: Well, I know of a certain man who has ch… Hunt (Interupting): Nein. Don’t bother…that “certain man” took a chance and it’s paid off. Seaver: True. But we’ll move on. So your first act towards Abner was to sabotage his favoured alcohol collection for the entire airship tour. Hunt: Genau. He’s weak and I was going to make him more vulnerable by taking away his precious beverage and selling it to some under-aged kinder in Colombia. Cal jerks his head up in shock, glance at Hunt with a surprised expression. Seaver: Excuse me? Hunt: As long as the alcohol is as far away from a wrestling ring than possible, Ich sorge mich nicht, wo es geht…I don’t care where it ends up. The reporter shakes his head in disgust Seaver: So you’d rather see a bottle of beer being drunk by a 12 year old, instead of… Hunt (interrupting): Hey, is this interview about Abner und Ich or is it about some disobedient children in South America? Cal quickly returns to the topic at hand. Seaver: Okay then. So you believe that Abner is soiling the traditional wrestling with his alcohol? Hunt: Genau…exactly. Plus, you just have to look at him to realise that his wrestling technique is a little rough. I mean, people like him – and I’m referring to all rednecks in this sport – should be locked up in one place so they can all drink beer and curse as much as they want and not damage the reputation of this company and this sport as a whole. Seaver: I just have to bring up the events of Turmoil last night, where you eventually unmasked and Abner pinned you in an impromptu match! Hunt: Nummer Eins…impromptu is the key word right there, I had no time to prepare myself. Nummer Zwei…that wasn’t a wrestling match, Abner wouldn’t know a wrestling match or wrestling ability if it unzipped him and sucked on his inner self und Nummer Drei…he got lucky, using all those tools and having some assistance from his hick inbreeding buddies. There’s a pause for a few moments and just as it seems that Seaver is going to ask another question, Hunt continues to talk. Hunt: Men like Abner don’t deserve to be in this sport. He’s a clown, ein idiot, and a moron who takes up valuable airtime on PCW television by rambling on in his incoherent tone. Ich bin ein Besucher in der USA…I’m a foreigner in this country and even I can speak better English, now what does that tell you about him? Seaver: Er…that he’s… Hunt (cutting Seaver off): In the last few weeks, I have driven Abner so far from the point of his usual comedy routine that he’s unbearable to listen to and watch…more worse than usual. I saw him react to my goose chase last week and his “pissed off redneck” routine was a joke. Either he’s a disgusting lovable comedian or he’s an unconvincing badass. Es ist ein Witz, alle davon. It’s this type of rubbish that makes my blood boil and it’s rubbish that is my inspiration to take Abner and hurl him off his airship altogether. Seaver: So what about… Hunt (cutting him off, again): Yes, I am another foreigner taking a swipe at the good ol’ United States but the difference between me and some vixer von Kanada oder ein penner von England, is that I’m not here to warn you all that the end is nigh. I mean, what kind of idiot would go out and say that he or she is going to destroy a cesspool this big? Seaver tries to get a word in, but Hunt doesn’t give him the opportunity. Hunt: I’m here because I choose to be here and seeing as this country prides itself on the freedom of speech and the freedom of expression, I’ll say whatever I want, when I want to and nobody can do nothing about it but listen. I work here and I get paid in American Dollars, but that does not mean that I have to fall in line and kiss the Stars and Stripes every morning, does it? A silence appears and Seavers glances around, motioning to Hunt if he’s finished talking….doesn’t seem so. Hunt: Abner muß behalten, über seiner Schulter anzuschauen…he needs to watch his back. His ability in this business nor his manhood has been questioned thus far…I will attempt to change that. Seaver: Well, I guess I’ve heard everything I needed to and I’m sure the viewers watching have been very interested in what you’ve had to say. Is there anything else you’d like to add to close this up? A slight pause as Hunt raises an eyebrow before responding to Seaver’s call Hunt: Sure. Lassen Sie, daß die Spiele beginnen…let the games begin. With that, the camera slowly zooms back and moments later, Seaver extends an hand out to the German, seemingly to thanks him for his time. Hunt himself ignores the gesture as he stands up, he glances down to the hand and a brief smirk appears on his face before he strolls away from the bemused PCW journalist.
