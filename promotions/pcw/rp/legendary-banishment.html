Legendary Banishment...

Date: before Sabotage Time holds no bounds to any individual…lest he permits it to confine him… ~~~Savage Tranquility, page undetermined He stood in silence, neither movement or breath unveiling his position. The room matched his perimeters. Everyone was gone, everything was turned off, save a few dim light fixtures. No cameras there in his face…no directors or producers telling him how to act or what to say. All it had been was slavery… Slavery to society… That’s all the media industry had become at this point in humanity. Nothing but a task in pleasing someone else…being what they want, conforming to his or her ideals, being a role model towards the people… …Being a legend…to humanity… Legends…they were many descriptions for being a legend. Fable. Myth. Fairy tale. That would definitely peg Legend Inc in his place recently. The man is gallivanting in a dream world that does not exist, having adventures with figments of his subconscious. Never…Never…Land…it would fit the outcome of his current situation in perfect succession. For neither he nor his ally Frisco will… Never… Never… Land…a victory over the Neo Void God. While prancing around having adventures with young boys in green tights and pirates may serve to be sufficient training in defeating Drake Maxwell, it shall prove ineffective against the NVG. Akiyama himself sat down in the darkened studio in a small director’s chair standing low at the ground. By the bending of his legs, it looked more like he was squatting in the seat rather than sitting. But he was, his back slouched against the back of the chair and his eyes glazed and staring down at the floor hollowly. He was dressed in his fighting attire of black zippered vinyl pants, mesh transparent shirt, and black vinyl tape up his arms. His usually neatly groomed black and brown hair was tossed askew all over his face, shadowed and masked by the dual-colored strands. But through those strands, those almond orbs that you would call eyes…they were empty. His eyes have been nothing more than portals to the soul of the god for quite some time now. When men gaze into his eyes when standing before the threshold of battle, all they see is danger…all they see is the will to defeat inside of the NVG. But otherwise…when combat is not an option…those eyes of his were as empty as all the threats that Frisco and Legend could spit out in totality. And why were they empty? Because…he knew that the thing he fought for the most within this federation… championships …were empty accolades. What was inside winning these hunks of gold and leather? Was it supposed to inject some sense of pride and self-worth into winning them? Did it make your skills and techniques improve and expand by winning titles? No… They were only material possessions…nothing more… He looked down to his hands. They were each clutching onto something. They were championship belts…the Tag Team belt…and the Television belt. Akiyama held them up slightly to his shaded vision, letting what little light there was inside the room glimmer against the yellowish metal. Then he lowered his arms back down to his sides. The god’s fingers loosened, and with a clank of metal both straps hit the floor. “Useless…” he uttered. Behind him was the set of the TV show “Frasier”. The Neo Void God had previously filmed a guest spot where he played a schizophrenia patient with corresponding multiple personalities. Once again…a plot from the public relations diva Seraphina Jameson that had proved to be successful in making him a televised icon. In one week Akiyama had appeared more than 39 times on new shows all over television, from public access to Comedy Central to CNN to Telemundo and Lifetime. And from putting time and effort into becoming a television legend…he had become the PCW Television champ. Was it worth it all? Akiyama dropped his strict code of bowing away from the limelight of society’s vision. Though they were the exact ones who he was supposed to enlighten…there also was a line he drew when associating with society…and alienating those who are not upon his level. Being this television leader broke that code…so if he were to break his code, what made him any better than all the rest of them that drops their morals at the glance of fame and status? It was pretty worthless… Fame…status…they were all nothing more than affection from strangers you’d never know…people you’d never meet. Legend also has another meaning… Star. Celebrity. Prodigy. Marvel. These names were acquired through fame…status…being well known and liked by the people. And the people…where what made legends who they are… But their opinions are flawed. If Akiyama had endured the agony and humiliation of staying aligned with that politically ignorant International Incident…they would have not even grown to know what the NVG was capable of doing once provoked strong enough. They would have never gone to love the Void Banishment and the joys that exist from the event. Yet he chose to do the right thing…and disband from those global goons. Turns out…the right thing is what the people wanted…and Akiyama was thrust into their minds and hearts. Akiyama rested the undersides of his forearms against his knees, a deep sigh escaping from his lips. “Celebrity…we are not.” Those words hissed out of him softly like the air from a bike tire. “We are no superstar…we are no pop idol. What we are is just…Celestial.” “Legendary status comes and goes…just as the storms are here and there, it never lasts, it never rests for long. It’s all a matter of what you were able to accomplish…if you were able to do something someone else could not…then the status is yours. But there will come a time where that legend…that act that has made you so grand and so much of a prodigy…it will be toppled.” He stood up from his chair, looking down at the titles at his feet. Then kneeling on one knee, he placed his palm upon the Tag Team Title. His finger traced the lettering on the gold slowly. “Tag Team…we have called it the Divine Titles on more than one occasion. We have gained legendary status by holding onto these belts for the longest of any possessor. Our names are down along with the rest of the legend makers…Akiyama and Pharaoh…the Divinity…Longest Tag Team Title reign. But to think about it…do we really want our names on that list?” He stopped for a second, not budging from his position. It was almost as if he was listening… “If you are never remembered…then you never existed. These social beings pride themselves upon making sure that their existence is not forgotten. They have some weird phobia of not being significant enough in this realm. This phobia builds up other social defects, such as overbearing egos and complex disorders. They tend to think too highly of themselves and less of others. And two of the biggest specimens are Frisco and Legend Inc. Yes…they do hold rather highly exaggerated opinions about themselves…and that is only from the blind admiration of the people that has elevated their egoism. Both of them are out of their time…and out of their league. Two returning elders…hoping to assume leadership of the tribe once again, but come to pass that new leaders run things. The Divine titles are not for them…they will never be…for despite what they have accomplished in PCW or anywhere else individually or collectively…they have yet to come against the Neo Void God. And that…is where the legend dies…” The NVG placed his left hand back on his kneecap, and slowly moved his right for the Television title. His black nails ran up and down the large red V in the center of it. Then he paused suddenly…once again as if he was listening to someone… “Are we a legend for dethroning Miyagawa as Television champion? Are we a legend for defeating the World champion? No…because there have been so many others that have done so without as much effort as it took Friday. As a matter of fact…we’ve disposed of him before…so doing it again for the second time doesn’t give us any kind of accomplishment…and neither does taking another belt away from his clutches. What we did on Fusion was nothing… but how we went about preparing for Fusion is an issue.” “How degrading for us…complying towards humanity’s means and norms, when we are the one who defies and destroys all rules and aspects of human society. The road to becoming this “Akivision” champion…it was littered with temptation…and we gave into it blindingly by the goading of that Seraphina Jameson. Maybe my sin was meant to be…just another digit upon the HAND OF FATE. Maybe we were supposed to defile our beliefs so that we could change society through their most prized technology. And it is because of this that we will go on to do legendary things through the aid of television. And from our actions and words…we will become a lege~~” Akiyama halted in mid syllable. He stared down at the title his hand touched, a look of clarity seeming to form upon his face. Then he scooped up both belts and dripped them upon both of his shoulders, gripping the sides of the belts tightly. “No…why become something that is doomed to be forgotten, fade away, passed over, and dead…when you can be a god…and exist forever in the Celestial Heavens. Being a legend is worthless…when your legendary legacy is bound to be destroyed.” He felt something down at his leg…a light…repeated pushing. Glancing down through his locks, he saw the canine from “Frasier”…Eddie…wrapped around his leg. The god kicked his left leg straight up in a horizontal angle, reached forward, and peeled the dog off his leg. Then he held the dog before his face, gazing into his eyes. “Eddie…naughty puppy…” The dog pants softly for a second, before the sound of splattering water is heard against the floor. His lips went from their narrow pressed state to a frown of disapproval. He dropped the mutt back down in his own filth and raised his foot upon the back of the animal. Eddie whimpered…squirmed…whined… but never escaped from underneath the sole of the Neo Void God. Audible cracks of bone were faintly heard, but Akiyama ceased his torture for a moment. “So many people who choose to disrespect and ignore our dominance…they shall be ground into a sticky paste just as all the others. They shall become legends in their own context…by being beaten by the Neo Void God will their legendary status rise…and they shall simply…be banished.” Darkness swallowed the dog from underneath. And just as quickly as it opened, it closed just as Akiyama brought down his foot. Scraping his sole against the ground a few times, he sighed before walking off towards the exit of the studio. Banishment… Banishment itself was simply… Legendary…
