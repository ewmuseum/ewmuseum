Press Conference

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 Feb &#039;03 The scene opens at a press conference with members of the media sitting on chairs on a blue carpeted floor. On the wooden stage sits, behind a desk, Chet King and his manager Cindy Bi. Cindy Bi stands up and starts to talk to kick off the press conference. Cindy Bi: I’d like to thank each and every one of you for coming here today to discuss my client, Chet King’s, future. Before we get started, I’d like to remind you that todays conference is to do with Chet’s wrestling career, not his career as a talk show host, so please keep your questions related to wrestling matters. A member of the media stands up to ask a question when Cindy Bi cuts him off. Cindy Bi: Excuse me, but I don’t believe I’ve allowed questions to be asked yet. I’ve got a few statements I’d like to make on behalf of my client firstly. Cindy Bi continues, but not before tapping the security guards on the shoulder while pointing to the man who asked the question early. This causes the security guards to get up and go over to the man, but he’s sees what’s about to happen and flees out of the building as fast as he can. Cindy Bi: There, that’s better. Cindy picks up a piece of paper off the desk and begins reading off of it. Cindy Bi: Over the past couple weeks there have been some negative developments regarding my client and his position in the wrestling world. After losing two matches, both after cheap and vile tactics used by the opposition, my client has apparently been dropped to the TV Title division according to the rankings on the PCW homepage. Let me be the first to say that there is nothing wrong with being in the TV division, infact it’s an honor and a very high achievement that most competitors would love to receive, but the truth of the matter is that there is no doubt that my client deserves to be ranked higher. Chet has not worked his ass, excuse my French, off for over two years in PCW now just to go after the TV title. Oh no, Chet deserves to be going after bigger game. Chet has not fought with anyone and everyone who’s challenged him to be going after the TV title. Chet has not climbed his way through the ranks of PCW, from the Extreme division to the Cruiser division to the Tag division to the Continental division, beating everyone in the process just to go after TV title. Chet isn’t going to go after the TV title, at least not at this stage in his career, perhaps he will later when he’s going for the grand slam of titles, but at this stage he’s got his eyes set on a bigger prize. And if you haven’t guessed yet, the prize which he’s going for is the World Championship. And Chet will win this prize in one month time at March Madness. It will be a classic fairytale story. Chet who has paid his dues, and climbed up the PCW ladder for over two years now, will win the World title at March Madness. What the point here is, is that two cheap losses to my client does not mean he deserves to be demoted, and he will show the world that he is still at the top of his game when he takes the World title at March Madness. Now, if there are any questions Chet will be happy to answer them. A woman in a blue dress stands up. Woman: Carolyn Anniston here from we_are_smarks_so we_know_everything about_the_wrestling_biz and_our_opinions_are_the_ only_ones_that_matter.com Cindy Bi: Hi Carolyn, ask away. Carolyn Anniston: Chet, Cindy has been talking about how you’re going to win the world title at March Madness, but you’ve got to get through Gaz Green firstly before you can even think about March Madness. What’s your opinion on Gaz? Chet King: I’ve got no problem with Gaz, he’s a talented wrestler and I respect him…wait a minute, what am I saying? I don’t respect Gaz, I mean I used to, but ever since he joined the Front Office my respect for him has just disappeared. Not only that but ever since he joined the Front Office he proved that he really is a funny man, he’s proved that he’s the joke of PCW. Carolyn Anniston: So you don’t think you’ll have too many problems defeating Gaz? Chet King: Truthfully Gaz has shown he can go with the best of them, so I do expect it will be a tough match, but after coming off two cheap losses I can guarantee you that I’m not going to lose another. Cindy Bi: Next question please. A man with big wire framed glasses stands up. Man: Hi, Richie Bill here from marks_are_stupid_and_not near_as_smart_as us_smart_marks infact_we_are_so_smart that_we_can_combine_the words_smart_and_mark to_make_the_word_smark.org Cindy Bi: Hi Richie, feel free to ask your question/ Richie Bill: I’d just like to ask you about your low promo rate at present time. Most of us remember a time when you were pumping out twenty promos in a week, but at the moment your rate of promo production is tediously slow, why is that? Chet King: I thought that it’d be sensible to take my promo rate slowly, and concentrate most of my time on improving my wrestling. But it’s become clear to me that my low promo rate isn’t pleasing many people, and thus starting from this moment my work rate will once again reach a high rate. Richie Bill: Will it reach the phenomenally high rate of twenty promos a week once again? Chet King: I don’t plan to go overboard, but it will be strong. I’m not only doing this to please all the people out there, but I believe that it will help me gain momentum for my quest to finally win the World Championship. Cindy Bi: We have time for just one more question. This causes the members of the media to grown as they were obviously all expecting to get to ask questions. A lady with a yellow t-shirt stands up. Lady: Sylvia Forman here from not_another_smark_site.net Cindy Bi: Please ask your question Sylvia Sylvia Forman: How come you’re only allowing one more question? There are at least fifty of us here, and we all want to ask questions. Cindy Bi: Because that’s all I’m allowing. Sylvia Forman: Well that’s ridiculous, but none the less I’ll ask my question. Cindy Bi: You’ve already asked it. Sylvia Forman: What do you mean? Cindy Bi: You asked why only one more question was allowed, thus you asked your question. Sylvia looks flabbergasted at this. Sylvia Forman: You really are a bitch Cindy. Cindy Bi: That’s it, no one calls me a bitch! Cindy Bi hops off the stage onto the floor, and goes over to Sylvia where the two start to have a bitch fight as the scene fades out. [The reason for this cheesy ending is to allow all those fans of Cindy Bi to get to see her in action :)]
