Adventures with Pete [3]

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 18, 2002 8==0 0==8 0 || || 8 Dum…dum…dum! The search for the amulet of Orion continues, as our two fine heroes, myself and Psuedo Hall of Famer, Pete Rose are making their way towards Reno to finally meet with the mysterious, R.L. Stine. Now, as you all saw before in our last episode, Tom and Pete were traveling in a train. Pete had gone a bit crazy, and began destroying property with that damn bat of his. Of course, I had told the truth, as I always do, and blamed the entire thing on Pete, pointing the proverbial finger at him. No, not that finger…I meant it figuratively. Anyways, that went and opened a can of worms, because suddenly the entire train wanted to grab Pete and take him to the local Nut House. Well, we escaped, and now we’re on our way to see the Wizard, the Wonderful Wizard of Oz….because, because, because, because….BECAUSE!…Because of the wonderful things he does…. Wait, ugh! Stop! That’s the wrong movie! Anyways, if this R.L. Stine fellow is as smart as I think he is, or as Lara had told us, he should be able to point us in the right direction to find this amulet and have ourselves a time machine. Yeehaw! And every that passes brings us closer and closer to Tuesday Turmoil…man, I’ve been thinking a lot about this match. To defeat two highly thought of wrestlers on Tuesday would be incredible for a career. And did I mention it is on a Tuesday? That deserves an obscenity! 8==0! [The scene opens up to a car moving down the street in the middle of Reno. The dazzling lights of the “Biggest Little City” are shining brightly onto the pedestrians and motorists moving about in the city. You can occasionally spot a police car coming down the road, making sure everything is in order and that there isn’t any funny business going on. The car that is immediately visible happens to be a Volkswagon bug, which is a sky blue color. As the camera pans in closer, we can see that Pete Rose is driving with Tom Lexian sitting in the passenger seat.] Tom Lexian: [checks the speedometer] Jesus Christ, Pete, how fast do you have this thing moving? Pete Rose: Pipe down, Tom. You sound too much like my wife now. Tom Lexian: Whoa, hold up there a moment. You’re married? Pete, you never told me that you were tied down, bud. Pete Rose: Tied down? Who’s tied down? Not me, that’s for sure. Tom Lexian: But you just told me that you were married. You can’t be married and not tied down, it’s impossible. The old ball and chain probably has you doing all of her bidding, doesn’t she? Pete Rose: Don’t call her that. Tom Lexian: Call her what? Pete Rose: A ball and chain. She’s much better than that. If I were to describe her, she would be like the anchor keeping the sun from floating away. God, she’s so beautiful. I’d give anything just to see her pretty face right now. Tom Lexian: [impressed] Wow Pete…I didn’t know she meant so much to you. I apologize for calling her the old ball and chain. But let me ask you something…if you love her so much, why did you volunteer to go on this mission? I mean, you must have known how dangerous it is. We’re up against a fucking dragon, for god’s sake. Pete Rose: [quietly] I know, I know. But I had to take the chance… Tom Lexian: Because you want to make up for those past mistakes… Pete Rose: [nods his head] You don’t know what it’s like living in my shoes, Tom. To be hounded everyday of your life, ridiculed at every mistake you’ve made…hell, they won’t even let me into Cooperstown. Tom Lexian: Well, they did say that you would never be inducted into the Hall of Fame, Pete. You were a bit of an embarrassment for them. Pete Rose: Oh sure, I understand why they won’t induct me into the Hall of Fame, but I don’t understand why they won’t even let me visit the Hall of Fame. I mean, if I pay admission like everyone else, I should be able to get in. Tom Lexian: That’s true. Still, you were banned from everything that was baseball related weren’t you? I suppose that would mean that you wouldn’t be allowed in the Hall of Fame either. Pete Rose: Pssh! That’s a load of frog shit! Hell, you know that my statue belongs with the rest of them in there. Hell, do you know how many hits I had during the course of my career? Tom Lexian: [shrugs] A lot? Pete Rose: Damn right! Hell, I even have a card shop right next to the Hall of Fame. The point is that I should be a baseball icon. Instead, I’m nothing more than the butt of every baseball joke there is. Even passing fans like yourself make fun of me. That’s what kind of a joke I am. If I can find the amulet, I can change everything. Tom Lexian: How does your wife feel about all of this? Did you even tell her? Pete Rose: I told her I was going away on a business trip. It was close enough to the truth that I don’t feel guilty about it. She does love me, though. I don’t know why, but she does. All I know is that she deserves better than me. Hopefully I can’t make that possible. [Suddenly, a car cuts off the Volkswagon, and Pete goes crazy, honking the horn and shouting outside of the window.] Pete Rose: [head stuck out of the window] Hey man! Screw you! Where’d you learn how to drive? Mongolia?! [Tom’s trying desperately to pull Pete back into the car.] Tom Lexian: Jesus, get back in the car Pete. You’re going to get us killed! Pete Rose: Me? That guy up there is the one who’s going to get us killed. Didn’t you see how he swerved in front of us like that? Shit man, it’s people like that who make me lose sleep at night. Dammit, where the hell is my bat?… Tom Lexian: [looks at Pete oddly] Why? Pete Rose: [reaching into the backseat] Well, I need something to throw at that car, don’t I? Tom Lexian: [shocked] Oh, for the love of God. You are not going to throw a bat at their car, Pete. You can’t! Pete Rose: Why not? Give me one good reason… Tom Lexian: What happens when we get pulled over for throwing foreign objects at other cars? We’re going to get booked and then we’re going to get interrogated. Pete Rose: Bah. How do you know we’re going to be interrogated? Tom Lexian: Duh. We have an asian guy sitting here in a Volkswagon bug with Pete Rose, the all time hitting champion of Major League Baseball. Do you see anything wrong with that picture? And I’m sure they’ll somehow get out of us that we’re on this holy mission to retrieve the amulet of Orion so we can travel through time. They’ll lock us up faster than you hitting a ball straight up the middle. By that time, I’m sure they’ll hear about your antics on the train, and how mentally insane you are. Pete Rose: [shouting] I am not insane! Tom Lexian: [crossing his arms] Sure you’re not…you psycho! [Pete Rose slaps Tom Lexian. Tom bitch slaps Pete, and Pete responds by stopping the car and trying to choke out Tom.] Tom Lexian: Get off of me!? Pete Rose: [still choking Tom] Not until you tell me that I’m not crazy! Tom Lexian: ….you’re….ugh…not…ugh…*cough*…crazy! [Pete stops choking Tom, and tries to catch his breath. A chorus of horns can be heard from behind, as cars are honking at both Tom and Pete for stopping in the middle of the road. Pete starts up the car quickly and gets going.] Pete Rose: This is one fine vehicle. I’d hate to see it marred by another fight, Tom. Tom Lexian: I would agree, if I didn’t think that this car wasn’t a piece of crap. Pete Rose: A piece of crap? What the fuck are you talking about? This car is a beauty. Listen to it purr. Tom Lexian: [puts his ear to the engine] All I hear is the tank about to die, Pete. Get over it man…Bugs are out. Beetles are all the rage nowadays. Pete Rose: Beetles suck. What the hell is up with that new fangled design? I like the smell of rusted metal and ugly colors. Sure, the second we get in an accident, we’ll probably get crushed. I’ve seen a lot of Bugs get totaled by Corollas, man. It’s not pretty. Tom Lexian: [sarcastically] Gee thanks, Pete. You continue to inspire me with confidence about this car. Pete Rose: The truth is, this car is the best thing that America ever built. Good god, I love this car. Anyways, you keep talking about my life and this car…but you haven’t spoken much about yourself. [Tom is looking out of the car window, admiring all of the lights.] Tom Lexian: I don’t have much to say. I only have a few things on my mind. Pete Rose: Like that match you have coming up…look, I know that the match is important to you. But it’s not going to do you any good if you let it rest in your heart and you don’t express yourself. The feelings are just going to eat you up, Tom. [Tom stares at Pete in amazement.] Tom Lexian: Pete, that was beautiful man. I didn’t think you had anything like that in you. Pete Rose: What do you mean? Tom Lexian: Well, you know…feelings and stuff. Pete Rose: Tom…just because you considered me moral-less just because I bet on my profession doesn’t mean I don’t have emotions. Hell, I nearly cried when they told me I was banned. Now, let’s stop all of this nonsense. Tell me what you’re thinking. Tom Lexian: Gosh Pete…if I didn’t know any better, I’d say you were a psychologist. Pete Rose: [nodding] Bachelors in Psych., University of Maryland. Tom Lexian: Really? Pete Rose: [shrugs] No, but it makes me feel better. Kind of feeds my ego. Now stop stalling – talk to me. Tom Lexian: Well, Kannon and Snow are the kind of guys that you would want to show your daughter. I mean, if I had a daughter, I’d want her to show me one of those two bastards. Good lucking, successful, and I heard they both own Dairy Queen franchises in Iowa. Pete Rose: Good lucking? You mean good looking? Tom Lexian: [shakes his head] Do I look gay to you. [Pete begins to open his mouth] Tom Lexian: Don’t start with me, Pete. You know I’m still upset with you over the train incident. Pete Rose: Fine. But at least tell me what’s so great about this title you want a shot at. Is it like winning a World series? Tom Lexian: Well, no, not quite. That would be winning the World title. But this is only a step away from that World series that you’re talking about. Here I am, making my return, and I have no titles? This is ridiculous. They sure as hell didn’t make it easy on me, but pssh, who said life was easy? Pete Rose: Amen, brother. Tom Lexian: And did you hear the big news? Pete Rose: What big news? Tom Lexian: Well, geez man, haven’t you seen him? You can’t miss him…terrible hair, saggy ass, and those huge, dented nose which just cries out to you, “Please…oh god…give me plastic surgery.” Oh, it’s very sad, Pete. I feel sorry for the guy. I might even feel more sympathetic if I didn’t want to run his skull into the ground. Pete Rose: Don’t talk about Kid Wonder like that. Tom Lexian: Pete, Pete…try to stick with me here, okay? I’m speaking symbolically. And Kid Wonder has nothing to do with this. If I’m at a N’Sync concert and I have a ticket to give away, I’ll call up KW. But I’m talking about that nasty Tyler Nelson. My god, he’s just so evil! Pete Rose: Well, don’t you feel better now? Tom Lexian: A little bit, but I haven’t gotten to talk about Tyler Nelson yet. Pete Rose: What the fuck… Tom Lexian: [puts his finger to his head, tapping it, trying to remember] Well, let’s see if I can recollect correctly…I believe he sort of runs the show here in PCW. In fact, he used to be the world champion. And then he used his fame and money to make sure the only thing that happens in PCW is written in his script. Pete Rose: Not exactly fair, was it? Tom Lexian: Well, life isn’t fair. Pete Rose: You said that already. Tom Lexian: Pete, don’t interrupt me again. You asked me to vent. Now, I don’t even have that much of a problem with Snow or Kannon, but if I can win this match, eventually Nelson will have to deal with me. It’s all in the plans, my baseball betting friend. Pete Rose: Hey, don’t talk like that. I didn’t bet on baseball. Tom Lexian: Liar, liar, pants on fire. [Suddenly, another car swerves in front of them and this time, it crashes into the car. Pete and Tom, thankfully, are wearing seat belts, and are saved from any bodily harm.] Pete Rose: Son of a bitch…look what he did to our car! Tom Lexian: God, that was a Camry that just rammed us….and we’re in a fucking bug. Shit, we could have been totaled. Pete Rose: Calm down, we weren’t going that fast. Tom Lexian: [opening his door to get out] It doesn’t matter how fast you’re going. All that matters is the fact that we were in a VW Bug. That pretty much sealed our fate, don’t you think? [Both Tom and Pete get out of the car. There is smoke rising from the hood of the car, and a huge dent could be seen from a mile away. Tom just shook his head in disgust. There goes the deposit. Yup, they had rented this car. Smart move, huh?] Pete Rose: Can you believe this? Dammit…where the hell is the other driver? Tom Lexian: [peering over at the other car] Watch it…someone’s coming out! [The door to the Camry swings open, and a dark figure emerges. Tom watches carefully, expecting some large, burly, black man to come out and beat the living hell out of the both of them. Tom wasn’t sure if he could defend himself properly, and he sure as hell knew that Pete would get the crap beat out of him. After all, he did get beat down often…besides, he left his bat in the car. I guess memory is the first thing to go with old age.] Pete Rose: He’s coming out…where’s my bat? Tom Lexian: [preparing to fight] You left it in the car, smarty pants. Pete Rose: Damn… [Suddenly, a man dressed up in a bird’s suit emerges from the car. He has black feathers with a yellow beak, and is flapping around wildly. He jumps on the car and rolls off, falling on his head and flapping his wings some more.] Pete Rose: Uhhh…what? Tom Lexian: I know who it is… Pete Rose: Who? Tom Lexian: Can’t you recognize him? It’s Big Bird! Pete Rose: [looks closely] Are you sure? I mean…I thought he was just a character. Tom Lexian: [shrugs] What can I say? The guy was a sick puppy, okay? Let me try to communicate with him… [Tom moves up closer to the birdman, and throws him some seeds. The birdman jumps on top of them.] Tom Lexian: Can you hear me, big bird? [The bird man looks up at Tom.] The Puzzled Bird Guy: Quack! [Tom pedals backwards, confused.] Tom Lexian: Oh my god…he’s no longer Big Bird… Pete Rose: What do you mean? Who is he then? Tom Lexian: He’s…he’s…he’s….The Puzzled Bird Guy! Ahhh! The Puzzled Bird Guy: Quack! Pete Rose: [pushes Tom forward] Well, don’t just sit there. Talk to the man… Tom Lexian: Uhh…how are the seeds, The Puzzled Bird Guy? The Puzzled Bird Guy: Quack! Tom Lexian: You like seeds, don’t you? I bet you like them as much as you’d like to have me win the Continentel title. The Puzzled Bird Guy: [starts slurping up some seeds] Quack! Tom Lexian: Well, you can’t have them! Now, go play in traffic or something…. [Tom throws some seeds into the flow of traffic. The Puzzled Bird Guy runs after them.] The Puzzled Bird Guy: [running] Quack! Quack! Quack! Pete Rose: That was pretty mean, Tom… Tom Lexian: [shrugs, heading back for the car] Well, puzzled birdmen aren’t supposed to be driving. That’s how accidents to occur. [Tom grabs his bags and Pete’s bat.] Tom Lexian: C’mon, we have to meet R.L. Stine. We’ll walk it from here. [The scene fades out as we watch Tom and Pete heading for the palace of R.L Stine and we watch as The Puzzled Bird Guy flaps off into the distance. Stupid birds.]
