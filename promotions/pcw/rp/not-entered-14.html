Date: not entered The sun was beating down despite it being winter, the clear blue sky forming a huge dome in the sky, a dome that would soon be broken. A gentle breeze was blowing James’ hair the blond tints almost golden in the sunlight. His eyes were focused on the object in front of him watching it intently scanning up and down as he had done so many times before. A tear fell from James’ eyes hitting the dusty pavement being absorbed in an instant. The space shuttle Atlantis stood proudly on the launch pad. The shuttle, which would soon be taking his sister to the stars. Another tear fell to the earth once more being consumed by it. He was not crying for any reason in particular. It was a whole mass of reasons. He was proud, so very proud of his younger sister Emily who idolised him, proud of what she had accomplished in a few short years. Yet there was an overwhelming feeling of jealousy and resentment. The shuttle in front of him was meant to be his ticket to the stars, for as long as he could remember he wanted to see the stars, to fly so high that he could almost reach out and touch them. James could not help but feel that someone he loved had snatched the dream from him. James shook his head before letting it drop so that his chin was almost touching his chest. A small hand touched James on the shoulder and gave him a little squeeze. A smile formed on James’ face as he turned around and gave Emily a large hug squeezing her tightly. “Hi spaceman” said James releasing the grip on his sister as he looked down at her, her face beaming back up at him. “That’s spacewoman thank you very much” grinned Emily as she flung her arms around James once more. “James” she said pulling away sufficiently enough so that she could make eye contact with him. “I’m scared” “Of course you are” explained James in the typical big brother tone, which he adopted whenever talking to Emily. “You know that you are the best man…sorry woman for the job, you must be if you beat me to it” Emily’s head dropped as James said this prompting him to cuddle her reassuringly. “You aren’t going to hate me are you” asked Emily tears beginning to well up in her deep hazel eyes. James took her hand in his left hand and ran his free hand down her cheek lapping up the one single tear with his thumb as he spoke to her. “Nobody understands my dream more than you Emily because you share my exact same dream. You have always lived your dreams through me. Now the roles are reversed. How many people will have the opportunity that you have ahead of you’ James spoke with Emily taking in every word. James indicated to the shuttle at the launch pad still standing tall and proud. “Some people may be able to say that they have seen a shuttle on television, we can say that we’ve been in one but most important of all, I can say that my sister went into space and I can tell them just how proud I am of the person that I love most in the world.” Spoke James his voice a calm and delicate tone. He slid his arms around her waist and pulled her tightly against himself with her resting her head against his chest. For that moment it seemed that the world itself had stopped, until Emily’s pager beeping frantically broke the moment of serenity. Glancing down at it Emily looked up at James but did not need to say anything, they both knew that the time had come for Emily to go. They looked at each other for one last time, there was no speech just a lingering look before Emily turned away from James and began the long arduous walk to the shuttle. James did not move from the spot he was on until he heard the mighty roar from the engines and rockets from the shuttle thrusting it into the sky and out of the earths atmosphere towards the stars, finally her dream had come true. James felt a hand on his shoulder once more. “That was a very noble thing that you did. Sacrificing your dream like that” spoke the owner of the hand, a male voice that seemed to assert authority even though none was intended. “Have you ever wanted anything so much that you just knew that you would get it sometime. It’s just a matter of time. My time had run out though. They’ve cut my training. That’s why I got in contact with you. I need your help.” Said James without even turning around to dignify the person with an answer to their face before continuing. “Dreams are something we all have but very few of us are fortunate enough to fulfil them. I’m nearly thirty. I can’t be dreaming about going into space for the rest of my life, I need to find a new dream, one that I can fulfil. My dream has to change to something more acceptable. I know I have the physical and mental endurance to do this. I will do this!” In that moment James Edwards let go of his childhood and embraced the future. The man stood behind James lightly beckoned for James to come with him. As James turned around he saw a pure white limousine parked in front of him. “Welcome to your dream.” Said the unknown man through smiling teeth as he entered the limousine closely followed by James, a moment’s doubt hit James as he took one last look back at the launch pad before entering the vehicle slamming the door and the past behind him. The engine was revved into life as the wheel struggled to find traction shooting a lot of dust and debris into the air as the car shot forward towards the horizon and a new beginning.
