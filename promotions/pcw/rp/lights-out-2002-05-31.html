Lights Out

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-02 I had been keeping the basement a secret for all two months of the training, but now it was an imminent need. For without the looming dark basement, my WITs would not be able to advance to the next stage of their training. And this was the part most important to all… When I first showed them the secret door in the closet, the secret door that could only be unlocked with a key I had, the WITs had gasped in surprise. Surprise turned to disgust as we descended down and they saw what awaited them. In the basement, there were no lights, no candles, and no cracks in the ceiling. It was pitch black. We all stumbled around for a few minutes, and WIT 2 almost had an accident on the stairs, but in the end our eyes adjusted and I began to explain to them what this game would be. I figured that in attempting this right now with my WITs, I was killing two birds with one stone. The first reason was that they needed to do this sooner or later… there are lessons in this world that not all want to learn, but that does not mean they should go untaught. The second was more of a personal relief… one that was completely in charge of my logic at the moment, and I could not shake unless violence came about. The WITs were about to see the effect of the anger they had just provoked in me… and it was not going to be a pretty sight. 'In a way, this is like Hide & Go Seek… but toned up a little. Basically, with me closing my eyes, you three run and hide in this basement. When we are all ready to begin, this shall be a battle of survival. You three work as a team, trying to attack me without drawing attention. I, the hunter, must search high and low for you.' 'What happens if you catch us?' asked WIT 1. I simply smirked, and I believe he got the point. I told them all to hide from me, as I buried my head in my hands and began a silent count to fifty. When I reached twenty-five, I stopped, paused a moment, and then called out, 'Ready or not, here I come!' The basement was not entirely that large, yet it's infinite boxes and closets made it perfect for concealing. The truth was that I, like the WITs, had never been down here before. It was only when I discovered the secret room that I knew we had to conduct training here. 'What's that,' I asked myself. I had suddenly heard a scuffling somewhere in the darkness, and knew I had my first target. I remained completely silent, and waited without breathing for the right moment. The urge came to me all at once, as I turned around just in time to duck a fist from WIT 2. I was in perfect position, and concentrating all my fury into my right hand, I pounded it into his upper back like a sledgehammer. Enveloping was the darkness, but I was sure I saw red liquid spit out of his mouth as I tossed his limp body into a pile of boxes. WIT 2 was finished. The next attack took me by surprise. The second I released WIT 2, a large forearm closed around my neck, and I began gasping for breath. I struggled to escape, but the grip was too strong, and therefore I knew I was facing off against WIT 3. Using my elbow as a boulder, I hit him in the stomach over and over until he stumbled backwards. When the large WIT recovered from the blows, he charged speedily at me, but now all I had to do was leap out of the way and he was smashing into the wall, face first. 'Two down… one to go,' I thought as I examined the area for signs of WIT 1. I heard the slightest sound, and turned my gaze upwards, where I found a body pressed against the ceiling. Creative, yes, but I had my target. Now all I had to do was wait for him to… Drop! He came down on me like a spider, but I stuck up a fist, nailing the attacker in the face. I took his neck with both of my hands, and ran his head into the wall hard, then followed it up with a powerful clothesline to the back. Believe it or not, the WIT had the nerve to keep going, and so I grabbed him from above, lifted him up in pedigree fashion, and dropped him on the brain with the Battle Cry, my PCW finisher. There he lay, face down in the center of the basement, agonizing even to breathe. 'My work here is done,' I said aloud, looking around at the carnage that befell my eyes. It was then that I heard a noise coming from the pile of boxes, and WIT 2's frantic voice beckoning me over. I approached him cautiously, expecting a surprise attack, when I realized he was bending over another body. On further examination, I could tell that it was WIT 1, bleeding profusely from a cut in the temple. Soon WIT 3 had come to join us, as we tried to wake up the unconscious trainee. Suddenly, a thought popped into my head. If WIT 1 was here, hurt badly, then whom had I most recently beaten up? Without a word, I jumped to my feet and practically ran back to the center of the basement. The body was gone. My throat tightened up, and sweat began to poor down my face as questions surfaced in my mind. '…Who hurt WIT 1? …Who was the person that leapt from the ceiling? …Where did that person go? …Will WIT 1 be okay? I paused for a second, realizing how crazy the recent events in my life had been. I didn't know how much longer I could stand it. 'What's going on?' I asked myself. Truly though, deep down inside, I knew the answers to all the questions previously mentioned. I knew what was going on, and I wouldn't let myself accept it…
