Canterbury Tales....Part 1: The Prophecy

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-10-03 And after two millennia and three following the coming of the one savior, there shall emerge a champion of the light. A man of the past, born into the present, shall wreak havoc upon the wicked, doling out swift justice to those most deserving of the punishment….. -From a Tattered Scroll unearthed in Babylonian Excavations…… The shot opens to a view of an immense cathedral, and an immense man standing before the open doors. Clad in a white cloak, the man’s blonde hair blows lazily back from his head. The shot pans near and we see Venneman Pendragon, eyes glued to the mighty church… “Canterbury Church. The cleansing of one’s soul, but at what cost? What must I lay bare in order to free mine self from mine own demons? The archbishop has always been able to help, but I go now to an unfamiliar father. Rowan Williams has been enthroned as the new Archbishop, and I know him not. A man of wisdom, certainly, lest he not be the archbishop, and yet, unfamiliar. I need this, though, so I go on…..” Pendragon bows his head and crosses himself as he crosses the threshold into the immense place of worship. Once in, he passes quickly the pews that line either side of the sanctuary and makes his way to the small confessional chambers in the rear. He pushes through the curtain and takes a seat…. Archbishop: I knew that you would come, young King. What is it that you are troubled by? Pendragon: I am haunted by mine own dreams. I bear the burden of history upon mine own shoulders, and sometimes I can feel the iron grip I hold on reality slipping, be it even ever so slightly. I know not what is my true destiny, yet the dreams have become more vivid with each passing night… Archbishop: Do you know the prophecy? Pendragon: I know of many….Which dost thou speakest of? Archbishop: At the beginning of the third millennia, a warrior, cut from the mold of times past, is to appear and save the world from the darkness. In his fist he carries the light, and in his words the truth. He is to be an enormous golden man, with an ancient lineage, directly traceable. Take from that what you will…. Pendragon: What dost thou mean? Take from it what I will? I need answers!!! I DEMAND them! He rattles the cubicle but receives no answer. Frustrated he gets up and storms from the church. He passes a few startled priests along the way, and he hears the voice once more…. YOU SHOLD HAVE NEVER RETURNED!! He jerks to a stop and grabs the nearest Anglican priest by his shirt and hoists him up to eye level… Pendragon: What dost thou preach to me, half man???? Priest: Umm…Ahh…I said nothing, your greatness…. Pendragon: Then who spoke the words that smote mine heart? The priest just shivers with fear until Pendragon slowly lowers him back to his feet. Instantly, the man runs behind a pew, and Pendragon stands for a moment with a quizzical look etched upon his face, the strain evident in his deep blue eyes. Finally, he turns and slowly walks from the cathedral into the open air. Once out, he stops and takes a few deep breaths before walking to where he has left his warhorse, Xerxes, tethered to a fence row. He grabs the reins and begins to lead Xerxes from the church. The horse nuzzles his back and Pendragon pats the horses muzzle. He shakes his head, and his eyes are elsewhere… “Twas not the first time word of the prophecy of the golden man had passed mine ears, and it will certainly be not the last. I wonder if the prophesized is indeed I. Am I the stuff of Legends, like the lies of mine forefathers? I guess it matters little, when I have a hard time wrestling with mine own sanity…” Suddenly, a hazy manlike shape rises up in front of Pendragon. Startled, Xerxes rears and pulls the reins from Pendragon’s hands. Pendragon stares at the apparition as if he knows what it is and from where it came. He draws his sword and drops smoothly into a ready position. THOU SHOLD NOT HAVE COME HERE! Pendragon: And who art thou, to tell the Lord Pendragon where he should and should not go….Art thou mine mother? OUR MOTHER IS LONG DEAD, WHELP!! Pendragon: It cannot be…Thou hauntest my dreams and now thee would enter mine waking hours? This is impossible! THOU IS IMPOSSIBLE. TO THICK TO REALIZE YO CANNOT WIN….GIVE IT UP, BROTHER! The apparition floats closer and makes contact with the physical form of Pendragon. Lightning shoots out from his body at the point of contact, and he falls to the ground, stricken unconscious. Xerxes has found his way back and now nervously nuzzles his fallen master… “What has happened? Why must I wrestle with mine own mind, my very being? I AM The prophesized, and I will rid the world of the darkness. For months, I wandered in the dark before finding my way to PCW once more, and now once more I wrestle with the darkness that threatens to overtake my very soul. And in PCW, I must reprove my very worth. Why it is this way, I know not. But Friday I have the chance to bring punishment to the epitome of that which is wrong in the world today….And in so doing, perhaps I may bring peace to mine own self, and to the multitudes who long for when times were simpler….Happier….” YOU SHOULD HAVE NEVER COME BACK. THE DARKNESS WILL OVERTAKE THEE. DANTE JACKSON WILL MAKE SHORT WORK OF YOU. WASHED UP!!! FINISHED!!! NEVER WAS!!! KNAVISH FOOL!!! DOOM AWAITS THEE!!! Pendragon sits straight up and his eyes fly open as the scream escapes his throat…. Pendragon: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The scream subsides but the insanity still resides in his eyes. He uses his hands to steady himself as he rouses himself ever so slowly, and he shakily finds his feet, Xerxes looking nervous as ever. He pushes his hair back from his eyes and wearily climbs into the saddle. He slumps forward as Xerxes takes him home. He knows the way….. That is one good horse! To Be Continued......****FINE****
