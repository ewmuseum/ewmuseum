CRUMPETS~! are not enough[part 1]

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/11/2002 JENKINS INDUSTRY CENTRECUBA27/11/2002 Cuba, the place of communism, hated generally by other democratic countries of the world, and so, by harbouring a terrorist, they’re putting themselves at risk, but then, when this terrorist has such a great plan amazed in his brain. It may well be worth it. And so, who is he? Dr.Jenkins, an evil man, born and raised in the communist country from an American family. His general dislike of America, Britain, Canada and many other of the worlds super-powers has come from his Cuban education. ‘You shall hate all the other democratic countries in the world’ he had thumped and forced inside his young, innocent mind. But, which country to take down? ‘I could simply ask Bush to hand me over the reigns to the States, he’s dumb enough’ thought the man as he sat, his legs propped up against his wooden desk. ‘But then, who wants a country full of over-weight people?’ he carried on his thoughts. ‘Canada’s the one, damn no military, but then it’s so cold…hmm…’ he pondered, pouring himself some iced tea. ‘I wonder, I just wonder…..’ he muttered as he flipped over the page to reveal a large 5 foot by 3 foot world wide map. Snapping his fingers he pointed towards Britain. ‘Britain eh?’ asked his dumb looking henchman, ‘ Alwight mate? How’s the tea and saucers over there?’ he replied in a thick stereotypical voice. ‘No, not Britain fool!’ cried out the man, stroking a cat which just happens to suddenly appear on his legs. ‘Oh really? What then boss?’ replied the man known as GOD, crossing his arms and trying to look mean. ‘SHIT!’ cried out the evil scientist, ‘the cat just took a dump on my leg!’ Throwing the cat off and flipping pieces of faeces from leg, he turned back to his henchman, ‘I’m talking about England, damn English, awful teeth, and cant play any good sports to save the world…’ he replied, giving out an evil laugh, before coughing. ‘So, how do you plan to invade Britain sir?’ asks GOD, who now happens to be donned in full military specialist black uniform, with semi-automatic machine gun in hand. ‘Well, what do they live off?’ asks the man. ‘Tea sir?’ asks the heavy next to him. ‘No dumbass, CRUMPETS~!’ he replies, smirking. ‘EXCELLENT! So, we’ll poison they’re CRUMPETS~!’ replies GOD, trying to be useful. ‘No, we’ll just stop them importing and exporting, dumbass’ says Dr.Jenkins. ‘Right you are sir.’ Comes the reply. THE ‘RED’ BUILDINGCUBA28/11/2002 The building is lined with military people, all straddling long, heavy guns. Inside, many of the top businessmen in Cuab, having been brought up hating Democratic countries, sit, all with some form of facial problem, whether it be burns, cuts, scars or just major acne problems. ‘Right gentlemen,’ comes the reply from Dr.Jenkins, wearing a surfing suit, and with a surf board at his side. ‘Umm…sir…’ asks GOD, staring over the meeting, ‘shouldn’t you be in, you know, a business suit with a cat?’ Pondering for a second, we suddenly see an obvious change and break in filming, as Dr.Jenkins now has a leather chair, a cat on his lep, which he strokes, and a black business suit. ‘Better GOD?’ asks Jenkins? ‘Perfect sir.’ ‘Now gentlemen, as you will see from the chart next to me,’ says Jenkins, as he flips the top sheet over, to see a bar-chart, a huge percentage of which is a red line to show the word ‘ CRUMPET~!’ on. ‘This shows that England’s major export, in fact 98% of their income from exportation is from CRUMPETS~!’ he says, smirking evily. ‘I plan to destroy all CRUMPET~! Making in the world, and tell England people no longer want CRUMPETS~! As scones shall replace them.' ‘Brilliant, sir’ says GOD, standing cross armed. ‘Yes GOD.’ Comes the sarcastic responce. ‘Now, this will bring England to it’s knees, all it’s military forces shall be handed over, and used to take over the world. Failing this, the same shall be implemented with fish in Spain, Chocolate in Switzerland, Croissants in France and Smoked Sausages in Germany.’ The Evil Doctor replies. ‘What do we have to do?’ asks one man, smoking a cigar. ‘Give me your armies, access to your planes, and I’ll give you a percentage of the world…now, are you in?’ ‘I call dibs on Disneyworld!’ cries GOD out loud.’ ‘ANYWAY,’ continues our favourite good, yet in this movie, bad doctor, ‘ are you with me?’ Smirking between them, we see the men around the long table nod and shake each other’s hands. ‘Excellent.’ Says the Doctor. Suddenly a walky-talky appears in his hand, ‘Go ahead as planned’ he says down it, to someone on the other end. DEBBIE’S TEA ROOMBLACKPOOL, ENGLAND28/11/2002 Sipping his tea and reading his morning papers, Mr.Uk. sits snug. The wind battering the windows from outside sounds appalling, and as a trickle of cold wind peers in through the cracks in the windows, he pulls his fleece up further onto his neck. Suddenly, a mobile[cellular] phone ring-tone is heard, and everyone in the moderately busy tourism style café checks their pockets/bags/handbags. Finally, taking his time, Mr.Uk. raises his light blond hair and ice blue eyes from the paper and flips out the latest, and I mean it’s just come from a prototype line, phone from his pocket and flips it out. ‘Hello,’ he cooly replies, placing down his cup of tea as it is said. ‘You what!’ is heard screaming down the phone, ‘never, ever, ever!’ Suddenly a toilet flush is heard, and in walks the famed PCW wrestler Andy Murray, adorned in his usual kilt and bag-pipes. Suddenly, a burst of wind flutters his kilt up and everyone shrieks. Mr.Uk.’s eyes cringe as he sees his partner in crime’s genetalia. ‘Aww wee lass, you’re nothing but a wee pussy me tells ya.’ Says the Scotsman, sitting down. ‘Terrible news,’ musters Mr.Uk., tasking another slice of fruit cake frm the silver dining plate in front of him. ‘What? Someone’s planning to stop the exportation of CRUMPETS~! From England and therefore bringing your wee country to it’s knees?’ asks the Scotsman, readjusting his testes through his kilt. ‘No, Nikki’s just spent another 4 grand on my credit card!’ snaps back Mr.Uk. ‘Oh, right…’ replies the Scotsman, drinking a pint of ale, even though it reads only 8:13 am on the digital clock on the wall. ‘So anyw….’ The Englishman is cut out by the phone ringing again, sighing and picking it up, his face turns serious. ‘OH MY GOD ANDY!’ cries the Englishman aloud. ‘What? Nikki’s just spend another large amount of your wee money on your credit card?’ asks the Scot. ‘No, don’t be dumb, someone’s planning to stop the exportation of CRUMPETS~! From England, and therefore bring the country to it’s knees!’ shouts UK back. ‘Oh well….’ Says Andy. ‘OH well! Come on!’ shouts Mr.Uk. and hauls the Scotmans toward the door. Mr.Uk. stands with his back to the thick lead wall, his black clothing tries to hide him in the darkened warehouse, and he pulls a small metal handgun from a strap on his thigh. Sighing deeply, and with heavy breathing, sweat pours down his fore-head and drops like a water-fall onto the floor. He snaps the gun back, and loads in another cartridge of rounds. ‘I’m coming for ya, you dumb Scotsman,’ he calls out into the air, towards Andy Murray, standing in the opposite direction. Swiftly spinning around the wall edge, he ducks down low, brushing his finger-less gloved hand through his hair. As he crouches and walks forward a glimmer of light flickers from a window above. He turns and squeezes the firm trigger. A slight recoil forces it back into his palm, and a man flops down on the floor. ‘Come out come out wherever you are!’ he says as he chuckles, still crouching low. Suddenly from behind a man jumps, he attempts with gleaming knifes to kill Mr.Uk., who swivels and shoulder throws the man over. Readjusting his 100% pure silk tie, he continues to walk. Suddenly Andy Murray spins and swivels out from behind a wall and unleashes several shots aimed in the general direction of Mr.Uk. He dives out the way and smoothes back his hair, before standing, breathing heavily now. ‘That the best you got old man?’ inquires Mr.Uk. smiling to himself. ‘Oh wee, you’ll get it stuck to you now!’ Screams the mad Scotsman in reply. Mr.Uk. laughs to himself, and simply backs against the wall again. Suddenly, as loud thumping footsteps are heard, Mr.Uk. pears his foot around the corner. A heavy thud comes after as the dazed Scotsman hits the floor. Standing above him and with the handgun in both his hands, pointing straight at Murray’s forehead is Mr.Uk. ‘Well, I win again, fancy some tea?’ asks Mr.Uk. as both men remove their digital head-sets.
