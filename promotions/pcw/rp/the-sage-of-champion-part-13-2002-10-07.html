The Sage Of Champion: Part 13

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-7-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 13” Chapter 5: Mission Of Violent Ray UnfoldsDay 6: The Insane One, Mir, Begins with BladeD :: The scene is picked up where BladeD, Mir, and General Powers were supposed to be making the plans out for the mission of “Violent Ray,” but BladeD and Mir started going at it again and we were left in the middle of a lot of heat, so we will pick up in the middle of this heat :: BladeD: The day you are better than me in this is field will be when… when… It is so far away I don’t even know when it is going to be. Athens Mir: I will tell you. I will officially be better than you when I do a better job accomplishing this mission than you. General Powers: :: tries not to laugh, but tries to quietly laugh. Mir hears the laughing:: Mir: Fine then. I see that you two are in this together against me. I know what the mission is and I know how to get there, so I am leaving right now! :: Mir begins rushing out at the door and general stops him :: General Powers: Now you wait a minute! You can’t leave! We haven’t gotten the plans out yet! Mir: Well, I can see that you, general, and BladeD are both against me on my experience, so I am going to get in this alone for the most part… You just send BladeD out when you are ready. I am going now and you can’t stop me! :: Mir ,without anymore words, he storms right out of the office and it seems like he is now on the mission of “Violent Ray” on his own for right now, so there is no telling the kind of trouble he is going to be getting into to. :: BladeD: Forget him! We don’t need him anyways. General Powers: I am glad he left. BladeD: Why is that? General Powers: I don’t know. I just had a sickening feeling in my stomach the whole time he was here. I just don’t trust that guy. BladeD: Yea, I understand where you are coming from… I mean how could he think he could do a better job than me? He is probably going to end up dieing. General Powers: I would be shocked if he lived through this. BladeD: I know I will because there is no way he is ready to take on Violent Ray… I am the only person in the world ready for something like that because I am the only person in this world that had the experience of Metal Armor. General Powers: Yes, I agree. That is why I want you to take this job on and forget about BattlePowers. BladeD: C’mon now! You just had to bring BattlePowers back up again didn’t you? General Powers: Well, you have to either worry about BattlePowers now… :: BladeD stops Powers in the middle of his sentence. :: BladeD: I am going to get into this mission now and get my stuff with BattlePowers solved later. That is all there is to it. General Powers: You can’t do that because when you… :: BladeD stops him again :: BladeD: I will worry about after Violent Ray… Enough said. General Powers: ::sighs:: All right then. If that is what you are going to stick with, then that is what you are going to stick with and we are just going to move on because we need to discuss the plans for Violent Ray. BladeD: All right then, go on. General Powers: Your first location is going to Chaos City, Russia. BladeD: Chaos City? Are you sure you got the name right? General Powers: Yes, see. It isn’t really a city at all. It is just a big base… Not like an air force base or anything. Just a… well you could call it a battle base. You are going there first. BladeD: And why am I going there first? General Powers: Because you are going to have to find Sergeant Mills. Now remember BladeD that once you get into Russia you can’t be caught. No one must know that you are in Russia from the time you get there to the time you get out or you will be captured and God knows what else? BladeD: Yes, I understand. Now is Sergeant Mills on our side or their side? General Powers: Well, he is actually a spy. It is kind of hard to explain because he is their Sergeant and all, but when you see him at first he is going to act like he is for Russia, but once he finds out who you are you will have no worries because he is going to give you helpful information. BladeD: Well, what kind of helpful information? General Powers: He is going to tell you a little bit about Violent Ray, he is going to tell you secret doors and things of that sort for the places you are going to have to be entering to retrieve stuff. You know… Just that usually kind of information that is going to help you out on this journey you are going to embark on. BladeD: All right. I understand it now… But aren’t I going to have a secret identity so I can talk to people and things of that sort? General Powers: Yes you will. In this suitcase right here you have all that you will need. Fake birth certificate, fake social security number, fake passport, ect. :: BladeD opens up the suitcase. :: BladeD: Derek Smith…. ::pauses:: Common name. General Powers: It’s a common name to us, but the people in Russia don’t know the common names of United States citizens, so it really doesn’t matter. While you are in Russia you are known as Derek Smith… Once you meet Sergeant Mills tell him you are Derek Smith, then tell him you are BladeD. After that he will know who you are. BladeD: That sounds simple enough. General Powers: Also, this probably won’t be a surprise, but you have to remember that whenever possible you must act like a regular civilian. You can’t start looking suspicious because if you do get put in jail or anything of that sort you are going to fail this mission. This isn’t like Metal Armor where we dumped you one place that was all you had to do. BladeD: Yea I understand general. General Powers: You just have to remember that you must interact with people and you will be traveling a couple of places. You have many sub-missions to accomplish to get the “big prize.” BladeD: What about Athens Mir?General Powers: Well, I had no clue that he was going to be here in the first place, so I didn’t have any of that fake id stuff for him anyway, plus I have no idea what he is going to do. The only thing in Athens Mir head is that he needs to get RMA Headquarters and retrieve the information on Violent Ray. The only problem is that there are a few steps… He is going to get caught that is for sure. He can’t just try to find a way in there and get the information. BladeD: Why is that? General Powers: Because they have cameras for everywhere you step while you are inside and outside of the building. BladeD: ::laughs out loud:: ::pauses:: Wait a second! Then how am I going to get to the information if I have camera on me the whole time? General Powers: Well. It isn’t that hard see. When you go to talk to Sergeant Mills he is going to have all the information you need to get away from the cameras. You are just going to have to talk to him and he will explain everything to the fullest. BladeD: Oh… :: The scene cuts from there. It looks like Athens Mir is now on his own and from what it sounds like Mir doesn’t stand a chance in retrieving the “Book of Violent Ray,” but Athens Mir is an insane fellow, so I am guessing that is going to find a way to at least get about half-way through the mission before he is into any kind of trouble, but I could be wrong. You just never know with a guy like Athens Mir. The scene cuts from there. ::
