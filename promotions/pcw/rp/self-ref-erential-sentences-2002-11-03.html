Self-Ref-erential Sentences

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Self-Ref-erential Sentences Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 3, 2002 The scene opens to find Tim Walden sitting on a bench in a Swiss park wearing a smart casual brown jacket, polishing off the remains of his lunch as passersby wander along the sidewalk..The camera pulls back slowly to show the remainder of the bench, where PCW referee Bob Charlie, dressed in some snappy casual street clothes, is growing impatient...'Could you possibly eat a little faster, Tim? I'm getting bored from waiting...' Tim looks up from his last couple of bites, the realization of Bob's bothered state finally sinking in. 'All right, just hang on one more second, I'm almost done here.' 'Okay,' Bob half-sighs, 'So let's get this straight again...You've got some referee experience, but you're looking to improve on your skills for tonight's match.' 'Yeah, so I figure it's like this..We'll go over the rules and such right now, discuss the basics, you know that stuff pretty well, so I'll just need to know all the pointers of it...' Tim dusts the crumbs off his lap as he stands, before sitting back down contentedly..'So what's the first thing you've got to tell me?' Bob turns, distracted by the Alps in the background briefly. 'Well, er, basically, there are two parts to this lesson. Firstly, we'll go through a verbal discussion of what is necessary to be a proper referee, and then we'll put this knowledge into practice. Now, what do you remember of your previous training?' Tim puts his hand to his chin, deep in thought..'Well, there was the obvious 3 counts for a pin and ask if they give up if there's a submission..What else do I have to remember here? I mean, what's normal speed for a pin count in this case?' Bob leans over slightly, holding his hand over the bench with a mildly pleasant expression still on his face, though a little sterner. 'In most cases for pinfalls, it should be counted as such...' Charlie slams his hand down onto the bench. '1....' Again he slaps the wood. '2.....' He whirls his wrist up briefly, and for the third time his hand comes down. '..3! As you can see, that's the general rule, but it can be flexible in exceptional circumstances.' The referee looks back up at Tim with a wink, which Walden responds to with a barely discernible nod. 'And a similar speed for raising the arm for submission via knockout?' Tim's inquisition is rather odd, as he usually spoke very knowledgeably, but with this he has to stay on the cautious side of uncertainty...Bob nods, and Tim continues. 'So, what fouls should I be concerned about that I might not be aware of, and how can I be more certain to spot them?' Bob started to speak slowly and deliberately..'Now, generally there aren't many things you should worry about in this perspective that you haven't already had to deal with in the ring yourself, just simple rules: If a chokehold is used, or a hold is in the ropes, there's a 5-count, and if that 5-count is completed and the hold's still on, then you disqualify. If any sort of foreign object is used, you call for the DQ, if anyone runs in then you call a no contest or DQ whoever benefited...You got all that so far?' Walden smiled slightly. 'Yeah, I got that...Anything else?' Bob answers matter-of-factly, 'Well, generally there's a bunch of things that aren't legal in the match, you'll want to give warnings usually at first, but use your own discretion. These include rakes, gouges, some bites, closed-fist punches, and the dreaded low blow...Of course, you probably won't have to call much of that tonight, but it's nice to know.' Tim nods..'Yeah, should be a fun little match to officiate, easy Front Office win in any case, bit of a warmup for this Tuesday for Miss Wi--Br--er, just Miss Wilson for now....Unless that damn Trapezoid shows up again. I swear, the guy has it out for me more than the rest of you lately, it's always cheat Walden, attack Walden, brawl with Walden... He runs in constantly, he cheats like all heck breaking loose, and for whatever reason the crowds cheer him...They must have one hell of a screwed-up moral system out here..Well, whatever happens, it's all right, I know we can win on Tuesday, since I'm obviously better than the Trapezoid..He obviously attacked me in the World Title shot match because he was jealous that while he was jobbing to BOB FREAKIN' VURDO over an Extreme Title shot, I was holding my own against the best in PCW, and he couldn't handle it! The whole point of his existence at this point is plowing through us from behind, and he loses the element of surprise when we fight him 2-on-2..He only knows how to beat us by cheating, and with you calling the match, we know that won't work this week..Then there's that tease Teresa Tomas...' Tim's words are interrupted by a chuckle from Charlie. 'What's so funny?' 'Oh..I guess I just found all the T sounds a little ridiculous at once.' Bob shrugs, as Tim looks on non-plussed..'So anyway, what about Tomas?' Tim's face remains rather stern, but cracks into a little grin as he resumes speaking. 'Tomas has been a great thorn in my side from the beginning of my run as Financial Reclamation Executive, but the run of tax evasion comes to an end Tuesday thanks to her own mistake of putting too much on the line...I realize the repercussions of defeat, but I know we can win this one, we can beat the forces of evasion to bring the money to where it belongs, and I'll have myself a nice little title shot and an assistant to boot...And when it happens, she's going to be wishing that she'd never tried to avoid paying up in the first place. It just goes to show that to resist is not futile, but resistance will eventually be crushed nonetheless...So when's the second part of my training?' Bob gets up from the bench, and begins to walk away. 'Follow me, I've got an idea.' Tim proceeds to do so, and Bob leads him down the street along the park, as the scene fades out, before fading back in on them in a mall, semi-crowded by the shoppers as they proceed past, Tim looking mildly confused as Bob has a mild gleam in his eye..'Take off your jacket.' Tim does so, revealing a black-and-white-striped PCW referee's shirt and putting it in his duffel bag, but as he turns back...'Bob? Where are you?' He looks around the crowd baffled, before turning around abruptly, finally spotting Bob and walking toward him, when suddenly the face disappears from sight, and a mild scream is heard..'Bob?!' Tim hurries over, only to find that Bob has just schoolboyed a customer! '...Oh!' Walden immediately drops to the floor and begins counting..'1...2...NO!' He holds up his hand to signify that for whatever reason, the customer has kicked out...The PCW referee gets up quickly, slipping through the crowd as Tim tries to keep up, rushing forward but losing him briefly, only for another yelp to aid his direction, as he runs over to where Charlie has managed to catch another patron in a rear chinlock. Walden slips around in front of the besieged shopper..'Would you like to give up?' 'YES!' screams the shocked consumer, and Tim immediately pries Bob off of her..As Bob suddenly swivels around to face the next middle-aged male, rearing back his foot, which Tim sees and dives to grab, stopping him mid-swing! Before Bob can take off again, Tim grabs him firmly by the shoulder. 'Er, Bob, the mall security is going to come here pretty darn soon, and if we don't get out of here, you're going to be PCW's referee to be wearing a different set of stripes.' Bob thinks, for about a split-second before turning to Tim: 'All right, that's decent enough work for a singles match.' Charlie and Walden quickly break into a swift walk away from the scene, whistling innocently as they step in near-unison....A little too conspicuously. 'Here they are! These are the guys who did it!' A rush of people crowd around in a mild panic, and Walden slides his jacket back on as the crowd gets denser..Possibly in the intellectual sense as well, since as they leave, they reveal Walden and Charlie, both wearing sunglasses and sitting down on a bench in the mall before getting up to walk away..Tim suddenly stops, and Bob steps forward a little further before turning back.. 'So, think we should grab a coffee?' Walden shrugs, as Bob nods and both men walk away...And the scene fades to black. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
