In Search Of Self: Psyche Out

6 February 2003

Date: 06-02-03 Turning away from the closet, Skitzo hadn’t much on his mind. The past had been long and dire for our hero, enduring weeks of pain at the hands of Statuz Quo. No not a beating, not a curbing, rather the lack of wages due to complete sucktitude. Skitzo had been looking forward to his return to PCW, taking a year out due to the fact he was declared crazy hadn’t helped much, but being back in his home, cursing like a sailor and enjoying every match, venue, signing, Skitzo felt somewhat like a new man, or men? Thus far, his time in PCW had taught him but few things: - 1. P.W.A. make for good comrades, however their Christmas cards leave something to be desired. 2. Statuz Quo, while a seemingly great target for any white man, should not be messed with, as ratings will drop lower than Quo’s “rep” when it’s revealed to the world he owns a ranch called Kiddie Land and lures poor black children into his gummy bedroom for treats and sweets. Michael Jackson doesn’t look the way he does because of negligence to health, he was one of the first kids to visit Quo’s land of joy and happiness…for Quo anyway…muthafucka… 3. The Stro, while the damnedest jobber thar ever be, is a shitty manager who for $5 will not take you to the great heights of superstar and legend, but rather stand by idly and be randomly killed by skeletons and fucked by giant pandas. He also delayed production of Blitzo 2, being that the reel is embedded in his soft, pulpy brain…*sigh* And so he sat down, his head in his hands and his legs crossed, sighing solemnly and wondering what was next. A call from work notified him of the Turmoil booking, but even the prospect of a place in the March Madness rumble hadn’t quite registered in his mind yet. Gatorade! sales were on the increase, and the spokesman deal was going well, other than that, life in PCW was… Skitzo: Fucking boring… It felt strange, but it was true. The Statuz Quo episode had been fun, but it went nowhere. Calling someone a n*gger and mocking the ghetto way of life can only be entertaining for so long, even freestyling gets old after a while. After that, there was nothing. His travel bag sat as solemn as he next to the 14” colour TV, the main compartment unzipped to reveal some clothes, a comb, and a towel. Nothing out of the ordinary, in fact very bland considering the kitbag would usually contain a 20” black rubber dildo, some KY Jelly, and fake ID cards. If any Ring Rats were stupid enough to want to fuck any of the PCW wrestlers so they could claim child benefits 9 months later when they give birth to a child who isn’t property of PCW, never mind want to fuck Skitzo, they’d be obliged to buckle up and open wide. Skitzo: Menmenmenmenmen… He was very tired. Travelling on the road and not being booked was tiring, because the boredom setting in meant the only thing left to do was sleep. Skitzo didn’t often travel with other PCW wrestlers, simply because he didn’t identify with most of them, and the ones he did were off cutting promos for venues, while he lagged behind trying to decide what to do next. Kicking homeless people and stealing guide dogs from the blind and using them as speed bumps was fun and all, good PR when he wasn’t at shows, but it didn’t serve as a good hobby. For two hours previously he had entered his hotel room and set about doing something with himself. Sure he was bored at the moment, but this is William Williams, Skitzo, Mr. Psycho. He may be bored but he isn’t one of those E-fedders he hears about that watch things like PCW then make up their very own federation and write stories as fictional wrestlers online. Christ knows he’d rather wank off a badger than be something like an E-fedder. He’s already a wrestler; why pretend he’s one? So two hours ago, the receptionist at the hotel looked rather stunned as Skitzo entered the hotel, with something long and thin in one hand, and something bulky and wide in a binbag over his other shoulder. Receptionist: Good afternoon… Skitzo: Afternoon Ma’am! Could you have someone bring these up to my room? Mr. Williams up in 27C? Receptionist: Certainly sir… Skitzo: They are rather ‘fragile’ items, could I ask they are treated with care? Receptionist: Certainly sir, I’ll have someone deliver them immediately. 27C, correct? Skitzo: Yes, thank you! Skitzo left the items by the receptionist desk and skipped over to the elevator. Today had been a sunny day and he woke up with a fresh breath of optimism breathing down his neck. He had dressed as smartly as he could to make a good impression. Booking into this hotel one week before Turmoil meant maintaining a dignified appearance, attitude…he had to behave himself. Yet five minutes after booking in, he found himself bringing in some vulgar materials for a dastardly project. Some men like to cuss down their opponents with a promo that berates them to the ends of the earth, some like to go on wild adventures and use symbolism to mock their foes…Skitzo had something else planned. Taking the elevator to the third floor, a smile stretched across his face, for he knew that while he’d probably be exiled from Oregon for it, he’d get the message across. Totally Tubular Tommy, a Continental Champion, a man who took the division to a new place, a pioneer for surfing wrestlers everywhere…and he’s wrestling a paranoid schizophrenic with a soda contract for a place in the March Madness main event for the World Title…either way Skitzo is fucked, but when you’re mental in the head such as he is, fucked is good. Fucked is very good. So the key card was swiped and the drab mint green door slid open, the stale smell of a generic hotel room enveloped Skitzo, but he didn’t recoil. Hotel rooms generally smell somewhat inviting, no matter what or where, unless they are in very poor conditions. The feeling of being in some place new excites everyone, and generally drives them to do things they normally wouldn’t at home…being a wrestler travelling on the road, doing wild things happens quite a lot, especially when you suffer from the type of schizophrenia that suits wrestling to a T. A few minutes later, a knock came to the door and Skitzo nearly caught his penis in the zip of his pants. Damn the porno channel for being so cheap and available at that time of day. He shot up and answered the door, a pretty young thing was standing there flashing its curves and holding Skitzo’s things. Unfortunately this was one of those ‘things’ that had those ‘things’ tucked away to become one of those curvy ‘things’ that men like Skitzo would put their ‘things’ in and enjoy…suffice to say Skitzo gave the very effeminate bellboy a tip… Skitzo: The world has enough problems without some confused little cretin changing what s/he was meant to be just so they can feel they belong when they’re part of a group of, pardon my obscenities…FUCKING OUTCASTS!!! Now take your $3 and leave! With a look of shame on its face, the bellboy takes his/her tip and leaves, while Skitzo takes his things into the room and slams the door shut. A dog barks from an alley outside, which prompts Mr. Psycho to open the large window. Beyond the double-glazing, people buzzed, cars purred, birds sang and music pumped through the streets as life carried on regardless. Taking a deep breath of fresh air into his lungs, Skitzo turned back to the two bags and smiled. The first one, long and slender, was ablaze with colour, printed with a design akin to a narcotics-based hallucination. Taking the contraption out of its plastic sheath, Skitzo stood it up against the wall next to the window. From the black binbag lying at the foot of the bed, something woolly was poking out. Skitzo’s eyes lit up as he ripped the binbag open and pulled out something woolly…but it wasn’t a sweater, it wasn’t a carpet, it wasn’t even a jacket…no, from the binbag, Skitzo removed 107lbs of limp, cold, dead sheep! Skitzo: I love the smell of dead rotting Suffolk Ewe in the afternoon! At this point in time it’d be wise to flashback to the phone call to the local crazy farmer who keeps sheep when they’re not the most reasonable choice of livestock, but time is of the essence. So pulling the sheep out of the bag, taking care not to rip its neck off, being that Skitzo himself took a bread knife and cut the throat of the sheep, laughing as its pathetic baaing turned into gurgled screeching, until finally it succumbed to the eternal slumber of death. Where could a man do this where no one would find it suspicious? A couple of blocks to the East is a giant billboard advertising Maxi-Pax tampons…below that billboard is a severed lamppost oozing with blood. Sick? Advertising Perfection? Due to the fresh kill having been made 10 minutes or so previously, nerves in the corpse were still causing the sheep to twitch, which caused Skitzo to laugh again. Thinking to himself he had wondered what the best way was to intimidate an opponent was…and so twenty minutes later found himself in the presence of a dead sheep, complete with broken surfboard fragments jutting out of the grotesque cut throat, the anus, belly and spine. Sounds delicious, looks even better. With that, Skitzo threw his work of art into the closet of the hotel room, splashing the blood over it and turning on a small electric heater, to allow for maximum stinkage. At this point, he turned away, nothing on his mind. The fun was over, now he had to wait for it to ‘mature’. At this time the boredom set in, it was entirely possible ADD could have been setting in, but then having reminisced the day’s events, time had passed in the real world, and Skitzo realised a tremendous stench of rotting flesh was coming from the closet. Approaching it, Skitzo opened the door wide, pulled out the electric heater, and grabbed hold of the corpse. Taking his creation, Skitzo opened the hotel room window even wider, and peered out into the streets below. Already gathered was a small crowd reading the banner Skitzo had prepared, as the sheep grew smellier. Tying a noose from his kitbag around the throat of the corpse, all supported by the fragments of surfboard, Skitzo nailed the rope to the wall, tossed the corpse out, and watched as the people below screamed. Bile, blood, urine and faeces dripped down the wall of the hotel, over the banner, which read “Skitzo def. TTT @ Turmoil – March Madness Qualifier!” and onto the streets. Laughing maniacally, Skitzo immediately moved back into his room, closing the curtains and lying down on his bed as the screams continued, the corpse rotted as the sun set on a beautiful horizon, and the first day passed slowly. Until Turmoil, Skitzo would ready himself without actually preparing to wrestle, after all, when you can psyche out someone with a dead sheep and a surfboard as effectively as Skitzo, pinning a PCW Continental Champion for 3 seconds wouldn’t be that difficult a feat…would it? The End?
