Winner

28 May 2003

Date: 28/05/03 .... Sometimes, I have to shake my head in disbelief.... .... I mean, what pleasure do the men at the top take in making me defend my belt so soon after winning it....? .... What perverse thrill does it give them to see myself and Abner tearing one another apart....? .... Obviously it must do something pretty big for them, since this is Abners first match since his return.... .... In my opinion, there’s plenty more on the agenda for Manuel Labor to accomplish with the Extreme Title.... .... But you know what....? .... Let him try.... .... Let him try to take the belt away from me and add himself to the list of enemies with Stryker and Law.... .... The fact of the matter is, it won’t work.... .... No matter how hard they try, they won’t cause me to lose this belt..... .... On the contrary, I can guarantee that when it’s all said and done at Turmoil, The legend of the the Lucha will be stronger than ever.... .... No matter who walks out as the Extreme Champion, the most important thing of all is that Manuel Labor will wlak out.... .... And there isn’t anything that can be done to prevent that from happening.... .... ANYTHING.... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The camera's flicker on and we see Labor mopping away at the floor, his eyes gleaming as he glides like a dancer across the dry areas of floor. Splosh, splosh, wipe wipe, the rythmn stays the same and the floor gets cleaner. Obviously the young Mexican has returned from his journey home but unlike the jet set lifestyle of champions such as Skylar Green and Dorn Tracer, Manuel has to keep working on his days off to supplement his meagre income. As the mopping continues, Manuel begins to hum a song and it seems to help him work faster. He continues cleaning and he seems to have moved into one of the many office spaces in the back of the arena. All of a sudden his mop hits carpet and he stops quickly. Moving like a member of the Suburban Ninja Clan the mop is quickly dried and set against the wall as Labor twirls grabbing the lead of a vacum cleaner,drawing to his hand, flicking the switch and beginning to hoover in one fluid motion without losing one beat of the song. The spot lights gleam off the rapidly cleaning floors as Manuel dodges between chairs and tables, the hum of the computers barely discernible in the background and the noise of the crew setting up for Turmoil hardly noticable. As suddenly as it started the hoover is switched off and Manuel flips over one of the desks and begins to clean the windows looking down on to the ring area. As he swipes at a persistant stain he begins to talk to himself. Labor: You are just like what so many people think of me. To Adam Stryker and Kael Law I am a nobody, a nothing. I am still just a Mexican Janitor but I am not. Manuel punctuates these last three words by rubbing extra hard on the window until the stain vanishes. Placing his forehead against the window Manuel looks down at the workers constructing the ring and fixing the pyros. Labor: These people look like ants to me. But they are not they are people just like me, employees of PCW and Human beings. What these people think of themselves is just as important as what anybody else thinks, in fact more so. If I had listened to people running me down I would be in a toilet somewhere in downton San Franciscio having given up on life totally. Instead I had the heart, I believed and look where it got me. I am the PCW Extreme Champion. I am a winner. Pusshing himself off the glass with both his hands he sighs deeply before moving on to the next office as the camera fades to black. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- .... So it’s come down to this.... .... Champion Vs. Challenger.... .... Mexican Vs. Redneck.... .... Labor Vs. Abner.... .... Not exactly the ideal match to have straight off the back of a PPV, but hey....? .... What can you do....? .... All we need to remember is that this is the biggest match of the year for Manuel Labor. His first defence.... .... The biggest stage on which to showcase his talent.... .... Each and every wrestler on the PCW’s roster will be looking to make their mark this month, and myself and Abner are no different.... .... It’s just somewhat unfortunate that our opportunity to shine is against one another.... .... Still, we’re never one to shy away from the spotlight or from opportunity.... .... We’ve both had the pleasure of taking on some of the company’s top guys, and even though we may not have always come out on top, we’ve proved ourselves time and time again.... .... Regardless of who wins and who loses, one thing is for sure.... .... This Sunday, Manuel Labor is going to do everything in his power to entertain you Lucha Fans.... .... And when it’s all said and done, he will be walking out of the Astrodome with his heads held high, and a message sent out to each and every other person within the company.... .... Manuel Labor just keeps on getting better and better....
