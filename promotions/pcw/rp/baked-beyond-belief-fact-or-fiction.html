Baked Beyond Belief: Fact or Fiction?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/13/02 (#1 for TT and FF should be 13th as well) Open in a dark studio, lights in the background glow a white greenish-blue and circle around and around on some pivotal center that we cannot see. The background itself seems not to be a wall, but an endlessly expansive vacuum of bluish-black. Some strange, creepy Outer Limits music plays over the feed. Jonathan Frakes walks into view, wearing his red and black Starfleet uniform. His hair is neatly gelled straight. He has a confident air about him. Standing in front of the lights, Frakes addresses the viewer. Frakes:'There are many stories to tell, some seem stranger than others, some may be so bizarre that one may wonder if it is actually truth. Indeed, the lines separating fact or fiction are at best blurred. What I shall present to you are a series of such stories, carefully selected to test the viewer, that being you, in how well you can determine if something is truly reality, or if it's all just... smoke and mirrors... Smokemore Weed here. I'm just your average guy, living in your average community of average people living average lifestyles and working average jobs for average pay. But I have a secret. A deep, dark secret that no one can know about. You know the Alien? You know, like the movie? Large head, acid spit, mouth within a mouth? Yeah, that one. You know the Predator? You know, like the movie? Wears a mask that gives it sight in different spectrums of light and heat? Has badass weapons and is part of a whole planet that conducts intergalactic hunts for sport? I have them. Both of them. As pets. Little miniature ones, neither can be more than six inches tall. Sometimes they're cool. The Alien especially. Give it weed and it just goes into the cutest little stupor you've ever seen. But it's a hassle, cause Predator gets really focused on hunting when it's high. Like I'll just be tappin' that ass with a hot chica, right, and then I'll feel a prick on my ankle and there'll be a little toothpick (Predator Spear) sticking out of it... Oh, did I mention that I have Alien and Predator as pets? It's cool to put them in a cage after I just gave them like a whole bag to smoke, and they'll be all cool and shit, and then I unwrap a Hershey's bar and put in in the cage with them and then they'll, like, right away just up and start trying to kill each other to get at it, before I get so hungry myself that I'll take it out and start eating. But I'll never close the door so I'll be eatin' my Hersheys and there'll be an Alien and a Predator hanging off my arm kicking and scratching at me. Then I'd just put some grapes in a bowl of water to get them off me, then put it in the microwave. Hehe. Wait, what was I talking about? Oh, right, Karl Marx. Man, Marx was a punk. He was all like, 'Human history goes through stages and shit.' So? He thought he was doing anything special. If he thought that the working class was gonna revolt, he'd be like, 'I'm on your side. Redistribute their property! *Points to other rich people*' But he wasn' t even rich. He was mooching off of Friedrich Engels. He'd come home and be like, 'I discovered more stuff at the museum today for my research!' And Engels would be like, 'Why don't you discover a job?' Marx was cool in one part. He did say that the downfall of capitalism was inevitable, that capitalist employs its own gravediggers. But he also said that, 'The only reason I tell you this is because whenever someone says that something's inevitable that you people try to prove it wrong. Hint hint, wink wink, rich people. Put down the revolution, look at this sweet deal we've got! We're not going to give it up without a fight!' Where'd he say that? Somewhere in the back. Back of what? What was it... something Manifesto... That's it! Ostend Manifesto! Huh? No, it's not about the US wanting to buy Cuba or go to war with Spain for it in 1852... that's the Zimmerman Note, dumbass! So, anyway, I says to Mabel, I says... Though the music hasn't stopped in the studio, the lights come up in the foreground a little more. We can see better. That's not Jonathan Frakes, it's Misfit Richard dressed like Commander Riker. That explains it. He sits leaned back in a stool, his torso away from the camera, his head cocked away from us off to the side. Richard:'I had more stories to tell you, but I can't remember them. So let's just deal with this story. What do you think? Fact or fiction, the tale of how Semore Smoke successfully defended his PCW Extreme championship against The End?' A pause, he seems to think anyone's taking the time to pay attention to him and consider his answer. The room is dark. Barely a hint of an object can be made. It seems as though an extremely dim light source emanates from behind some obstruction. A camera within this vacuum zooms out to bring into focus a human form, sitting in front of a wax candle that's down to its last inch of wick and light. The image is but a silhouette against the dim glow of the candle that gives the wall behind it somewhat of a grayish-orange hue, the crate on which it sits looking to sway as the flickering candle light comes closer to its last bit of spark. 'Anonymity...' The voice is a low whisper, slightly scratchy by nature. There is a sense of knowledgeable calmness coming from this figure in the one word it has spoken thus far. A world of wisdom held within an indistinguishable recepticle, crouched in a seat upon the hard concrete floor, its arms folded over its legs bent up for a stable resting position. 'Such is a wonderful situation in which to exist...' Something of a snicker, the feeling of a smirk coming from this thing which has somehow commandeered an PCW camera. 'The buzzword on everyone's lips, the one thing everyone has running through their minds: Black Friday. Certain people think of it in certain ways, but the most important and relevant to the PCW is its next Friday Fusion event by that name... Black Friday Mall Brawl...' The silhouette looses a sigh, followed by a deep breathe as it continues its hushed monologue. 'And look who will be participating in this event...' 'Some of them have been around the wrestling circuit quite a bit... Others are relative newcomers to the sport. But everyone wants the PCW Continental Title...' 'Everyone except me...' Could this be right? Someone disinterested with the PCW Continental Title? The figure does give a pause, but continues. 'This may be an oddity in perspective, but what does the Continental Title really gain a person? Money? Power? Fame? Prestige?' 'Call me a freak if you must, but wrestling is not an lifetime goal for me, I am not driven by delusions of grandeur, I have no aspirations for avarice...' 'It is not even a matter of proving myself the best...' A shrug of the person's shoulders... 'I notice that a lot, hearing everyone say 'I am the best of the best. And winning the (Insert Belt Here) Title will just prove what you should already know to be true.'' 'Funny, to think that these persons, babies all of them, not really possessing any knowledge of a deep universal TRUTH, speaking in absolutes...' The candle's light grows more and more dim with each passing second, the spot on the wall shrinking into itself while the figure uses more oxygen in the room to kill the single struggling flame behind him. 'To flaunt victory, to prove greatness, to achieve or sustain wealth... such are the motivations of championship contenders. I consider myself a rarity, someone who wrestles not to become a God among men, but merely for the sport of fair and honorable competition...' 'But this may be a flawed view. After all, I have nothing to my name. No titles won, in fact, Justice will be my first wrestling match EVER. I am a nobody, I will be the first to admit it...' 'But sweet, sweet anonymity... Therein lies my greatest advantage...' Advantage? This admitted rookie, with no wrestling experience, is talking about advantages? Ha! 'I have no reputation for success. No preconceived notions of greatness. No established expectations to live up to...' 'And THAT is my best weapon in this sport... because I HAVE EVERYTHING TO GAIN AND NOTHING TO LOSE... The figure comes up to a stance, facing the camera though no details of the figure can be made. Seems tall, about 6'8' by estimation. 'And perhaps the one and only TRUTH in the universe is simply this:' The candle's light finally whisks away, the room becoming darker and darker until there is but a faint afterglow around the body of the man. 'A man who has nothing to lose can indeed be the most dangerous....' And with that, the room goes pitch black and the camera shuts off. *Two hours later* Richard:'Yeah, that's right. Semore Smoke defending his championship? Oh, you know that that's pure bullshit. Of course, I couldn't care if it's true or not. Why? Because I'm higher than a muh-fuh right now...' A puff of marijuana smoke comes from Richard's mouth. He stares at the camera with glazen eyes. Richard:'This has been Baked Beyond Belief: Fact or Fiction. Go to bed, mommy.'
