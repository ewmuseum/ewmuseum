The rookie pumped enough into him to kill a T Rex.. and this aint no damn jurassic park... He is dead...

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/02/03 Alone in this darkness I fight to stay alive... Swirling hate reverberates as Signs of life and light fade. This world that once might have been mine... Is soiled in the bowl of filth called humanity... Thoughts Swirling, Pain Growing... Hate filling, Regret dying... Mani was right... Maybe life does have a meaning... Maybe life's not as fucked as it may seem... The scene opens in a tight gray room full of heavily clad militants. Man: Shin Yagyuu.. You know why were here... Shin's red eyes flare open in the darkest corner of the room as his voice spills from his lips bleak and empty.. Mr. Aldante doesn't like his pet project anymore? So he sends a military Task force after me? What weren't Shigeru's destructive fits enough to tell you that we can't be stopped? Man: Lost Angel can't be stopped correct... But you can Dark Angel.... The men suddenly pull back as the leader tosses a grenade into the room.. The explosion sounds outside the room as the men all put their backs to the far wall.. Subordinate: Sir do you think that killed Yagyuu? Captain: Highly doubt he could survive... The subordinate nods and runs over to kick the door open.. Seconds before his foot impacts, a black and red gloved fist rips through the door splintering it to shreds. Captain: No! They told me he didn't have a Nanosuit! The fist suddenly opens and grabs the young lieutenants leg. A quick jerk pulls the leg inside the room and a second later the mans screams of agony fill the air as his body falls over backwards. His leg now a stump bleeding profusely.. Captain: Get back! An instant later the door rips clean from it hinges as Shin Yagyuu steps out through the opening... His black pants and mask replaced with a red and black skin tight Nanosuit similar to Mani Shigeru's... What was that you were saying about me not surviving... One of the soldiers stands up from a sitting position on the concrete. The young soldier brings his assault rifle to his hip as he unloads round after round into Shin's body.. The room goes silent as the clip empties and Shin falls limp to the concrete... -=- Who am I? I don't know... Who do you think you are? Shinosuke Yagyuu? That is a name not you.... I......... You? I don't know who or what I am.... You are a murderer.... Murder? The act of Taking an innocent's life..... But... If I kill the guilty Im still a killer? Killers are not Murderers... Mani is a Killer... You are a Murderer... Why... Why do I care who dies by my hand.... Because Shinosuke... Because why? Because you were once a father.... What? Yes... Of a young boy... A young boy with red eyes... Why do you think the raZed chose red for your eye color? But ... Why can't I remember... Because... Life locks away painful memories.. How could a son be a painful memory? A Murderer... Can make anything a painful memory... -=- Shin lays motionless in the back of the military vehicle.. His arms and legs bound to keep him from moving.. The back of the vehicle locked tight... A smile crosses Shin's face as he lays down flat.. Mani got a blue wire made of energy... Shin's eyes close as a bright red flare shoots from him forearm.. The bindings fall off his arm as the blade of light breaks through them.. I get a blade... In the dark van the blade cuts off the remaining bindings.. Shin sits down in the darkness awaiting the van's destination point... Maybe... just maybe, Mani is right... Many of the death's I've caused... Were not deserved... My thoughts wander.. That damn voice can convince me of anything... Maybe it's my conscience... The vehicle suddenly lurches to a stop.. Shin jumps to his feet and prepares for the door to open... Voices can be heard from outside the vehicle... Voice: For he's a jolly good fellow 2nd Voice: Think he is still dead in there? Captain: The rookie pumped enough into him to kill a T Rex.. and this aint no damn jurassic park... He is dead... The door swings open as white light pours into the holding area of the advanced military vehicle.. Rookie: Cap.... Where is he? In the back of the holding area two red orbs light up followed closely by a large red energy blade. The men's faces turn pale as Shin lunges out of the holding area into the warehouse sized clearing.. A swift motion takes the captains head from his shoulders. The bodies of Shin and the captain fall to the concrete floor for an instant before Shin leaps to his feet.. The soldiers all back away as the masked face stares a burning red hole through them... Well.... Who's next..... The rookie again lifts his rifle loading Shin full of bullets... The group of soldiers cheer as Shin falls limp to the dirty concrete floor... Rookie: Guy's... Are we sure he is dead this time? The rookie walks over and turns Shin over onto his back.. Soldier: Well Rook... Is he dead? Rookie: gak... The rookie slumps forward as his eyes lock with Yagyuu's Rookie: I'm................ sorry... As his compatriots watch on the rookie falls onto the body of Shin Yagyuu.. The red energy blade piercing out of his back and severing his spinal chord... Soldier: You.... YOU BASTARD! I'm a bastard? Shin slowly stands up... You willingly help a company that destroys families.. Kills men.. Uses people... and breaks souls... and I AM A BASTARD! The unholy metal scrape of a raZed scream fills the air as Shin crosses the gap between the men and himself.......... Fade
