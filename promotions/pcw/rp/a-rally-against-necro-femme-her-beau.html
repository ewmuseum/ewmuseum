A Rally Against Necro-Femme & Her Beau

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.28.02 (#3 FF) “Folks, get these before they hit the shelves and be the first person on your block to fight for a good cause!” Uh-oh, that sounds surprisingly like one of those cheesy infomercials that has a guy the size of Syko talking about how he got to be the size of Akiyama in three weeks, only by taking this new protein shake….along with several thousand steroids every hour! “Take a stand against the disgusting filth that is infecting our…well your, since I’m not having a little mongrel child screw up my nice ass house, your children’s television!” Now that sounds quite a bit more like Rush Mason. The scene finally begins to fade in on Rush, who seems to be carrying a sign that we can’t get a glimpse of just yet above his head, and seems to be standing outside a building with old gray Ionic columns, giving it the look of some sort of important building. Rush: I’m just trying to help you nimrods, here. Normally, I wouldn’t waste my time on your kind, but even you people deserve to know about these two criminal mastermi….well, criminals. Several people walk by Rush, rolling their eyes, although Rush doesn’t spy their response, or he might well spaz entirely out. Despite being quite a large well-built black man standing in the middle of a busy day on a city street in front of a building, Rush also seems to be carrying a sign that carries a message that doesn’t exactly thrill the onlookers. “Get a job, crackhead.” As one of the onlookers walks by, he remarks this to Rush, who turns on a dime and spots the man in a suit and briefcase, walking further away from Rush. However, the Voodoo Villain takes the opportunity to shout after the man. Rush: Hey buddy, it’s not your fault that you just made that worthless comment! I know you might be a nimrod at heart, but you’d feel a lot better if these two criminals were behind bars. As Rush turns back to the street, we can finally see what is on the front of the sign, and the response from people shouldn’t be surprising, as the signs have two pictures, on one side is DarkStar, while on the other is Talena. Pointing towards Darkstar is a large black arrow, along with words labeling him, as “Dead & Fucked.” Talena’s is no better though, with an arrow pointing towards her picture and a label that reads “Necro-Femme.” Rush: People, we need to put people like this into jails, not into the wrestling ring with superior individuals such as myself. However, it seems as though many passer-by’s do not think so, as they either avert their eyes, or give a sneer in Rush’s direction. However, luckily Rush has flyers that are just smaller models of his posters, but strangely, people don’t flock to them. Rush: C’mon people, if you put those kiddie porno people in jail, you have to at least give me something for this. But of course, there is nothing, because no one wants to even think about necrophilia, and to be perfectly honest, no one’s really that fond of Rush anyway. Thankfully, no one except PCW wrestlers have the courage to say so to a 6’4 260 pound black male, or there’d be spatula marks on faces across the country. Rush spies a man carrying his young daughter and walks in front of him, trying to be as serious as possible. Rush: Sir, I would just like to enlist your help in the fight against people who are content in making this world a worse place for you beautiful little…oh my Bon Dieu, is that really a child? That’s the ugliest thing I’ve ever….Shit, I screwed that up, didn’t I? The man flicks Rush off, and Rush smacks his forehead, cursing under his breath about how he needs to remember who his audience is here. Luckily, he sees a young looking college student or so, and everyone knows that college students love to get involved in political rallys and all of that good stuff, so taking another chance, Rush jogs over. Rush: Say, young man, could I interest you in a fantastic way to take a stand against the disgusting perversion that the youth of today face? The young man looks rather confused, but can’t really say no, with a rather large black man staring him down in the face, so from underneath his huge hoodie sweatshirt, he extends a hand, and Rush piles literally dozens of his anti-DarkStar flyers. “Uhh…sure, what the hell is this for?” Thinking quickly, Rush realizes that college students are always talking about certain subjects, so in a momentary flash of what he believes to be ingenuity, Rush combines several of those elements. Rush: Well…that’s a guy who makes his slave women and children cut down the rainforest, killing thousands of whales. The college student looks rather confused, but just takes the flyers and gives a mumbled ‘thanks’ to Rush, who smiles proudly at his ability to sell his ‘product.’ This time, Rush spies a young child, who is alone, and figures that it could be another great sell. He sprints over, starting to make the child run away, but then Rush catches up, and starts to woo the child with a soft voice. Rush: Hey there buddy, where’re your parents? The child spies Rush’s sign, and gulps loudly, while Rush looks around, hoping this isn’t the son of Cletus Jones, but alas, there seem to be no obnoxious trailer park trash around, so Rush asks again. Rush: I’m not gonna hurt ya, little fella. I just wanna make sure your parents aren’t members of the KKK who’re gonna try to fry my ass like a bad piece of KFC, ya know? The child doesn’t really understand, but nods anyway, and Rush notices that he seems to be wearing a black rope and white face paint, as well as a black choker. Rush: Hmm…wearing that, you almost look like the son of… As if on cue, Dark Gimmick, and a woman who appears to be his love interest, or maybe his hate interest, not really sure how that works, step right in back of Rush, as he closes his eyes and turns around, face to face with Dark Gimmick, trying to smile. Rush: Oh….DG…I….uhhh……didn’t even see you ‘til now. Dark Gimmick doesn’t look amused, though,. He looks down at his son, who runs over to his father’s feet, and DG clears his throat, Rush asks another question. Rush: Wait, I thought Clyde was your- Dark Gimmick: Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die. *ahem* Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die Die. DG turns, and his small child turns around, throwing a middle finger Rush’s way and proving ‘like father, like son’ in a squeaky voice. “Die.” Rush stands in the middle of the square, and just throws his poster down, sick and tired of rallying. He turns and starts to head back to his baby blue 4.6 Range Rover, muttering to himself as the scene fades out… “Bah…dark jobbers, they’ve just got no manners these days…”
