The REAL Islam: A Memo to The Chef from The Prophet

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/15/02 The scene opens in complete darkness. El Diablo:“Are you sure that this is part of the movie?” End:“Trust me, you wrote this in. You just don’t remember.” El Diablo:“... If you say so. Just bring up the lights and I’ll read the cuecards.” With no music, the screen fades into show The End, in ring attire, sitting on a folding chair on the foreground from a black background, with a spolight on him. He leans forward in the chair, addressing the viewer directly with the blank tone of a public service announcer. The End:'And now, a message to The Chef from Muhammad ibn Abdallah, otherwise known as the Prophet of Islam.' The camera moves past The End, into the black background. After a few seconds, the lights come up in the back. El Diablo is dressed like an Arab man in white tunic and headdress. He has a long beard glued onto his face to make him look more the part, and he's applied a cream that makes him look darker. The backdrop is a simple desert template wall, with sand dunes, bright sun, and blue skies. There is but one plant painted on the wall, shaped like a palm tree. El Diablo, with his natural and trademark cockiness and arrogance, plays the part of Muhammad as he addresses The Chef. El Diablo:'Chef, you've been angering me. Not that I'm Allah or anything, but I'm up in Paradise watching what's going on, and I can tell you that you're f*cking up my message! Who did you learn your Islam from, Denzel Washington? El Diablo, The End and I have gotten together, and we're putting this little informative commercial to set things straight, and show you the true Islam, not the doctored version that those dumbass Shii fundamentalists preach. So pay attention while we go over the history of Islam, and the way YOUR PROPHET set it up, and how the people that converted you f*cked it up!' With The End giving narration, El Diablo begins doing his acting monologue. End:'610 A.D. The Prophet Muhammad goes to a cave on the summit of Mount Hira just outside Mecca.' El Diablo:'Man, this is trippy. I come here every year to pray, and fast, and give alms to those lazy poor people who keep asking for handouts. But that's not even the problem. My tribe, the Quraysh, has become rich, and so are ignoring the noblesse oblige; even though it sucks, we should still do it. And my people don't have an organized religion to believe in. We seem to have no prophet and no scripture.' El Diablo acts like a man wallowing in self-pity. He cups his hands around his and breathes a heavy sigh; he won't cry, even acting. End:'On the 17th night of Ramadan, however, Islam as we know it came to be.' El Diablo stands erect, with his arms at his side and eyes wide open. He acts like something is holding him. in this position. El Diablo:'Something... holding on.... A force of immense power... Can feel it....' El Diablo strains his head, as though he has to force it to turn from side to side. El Diablo:'A voice? From where? What? Yes, I understand.' El Diablo stands still and silent. The End breaks in. End:'That night, Allah, through Muhammad, spoke the first words of the Quran. Muhammad kept this revelation, along with followup revelations, a secret for two years, only speaking of them with his wife Khadija, and her Christian cousin Waraqa ibn Nawfal.' El Diablo is joined onstage by Catherine Zeta-Jones (his wife) and Rae Dawn Chong (cousin). Of course, they are covered from head-to-toe in blue cloths, so you'd only know from their voices. El Diablo:'Wait a second, I've got Catherine Zeta-Jones onstage with me, and she's covered up! Take that shawl off!' Tugging at her clothes, El Diablo rips off the shawl that covers. Catherine Zeta-Jones. Unfortunately for her, she thought it safe not to wear anything under it besides her panties, so the topless Zeta-Jones gives the camera and excellent view of her NIPPAGE~! El Diablo:'Hehe.' Zeta-Jones grabs the clothes from El Diablo and runs offstage. He shrugs and grins, looking after her. End:'Sorry about that, we'll come back after this commercial.' Are you fat? Lazy? Are you thinking that you'll never hook up with a woman, and you're so hopelessly not like El Diablo and The End that you indeed never will? Then convert to Islam, where it is the woman's duty to not only marry you, but give you as much sex as you demand. And if she acts up or gives you lip about anything, there's all the other wives and concubines that you can go to without shame, after you have your complaining wife beaten and stoned by your relatives. Islam, live like a king! After the last commercial, we find the re-clothed Catherine Zeta-Jones standing beside El Diablo on stage. Rae Dawn Chong is slightly out of view to the right, only that half of her body showing. While Zeta-Jones is ready for the scene, El Diablo is small-talking about gibberish, trying to smooth things over from before. El Diablo:'Sorry about before. Didn't mean to strip ya naked. What are you doing here, anyway? Oh, crap, we're on.' El Diablo quickly gets back into character, which is little more than El Diablo with lines to recite. The End goes back to his narration. End:“They both believed that Muhammad had indeed heard the words of God, but only after two years did Muhammad feel confident to preach the holy words Allah had delivered from Muhammad's own lips.' El Diablo moves forward in front of Zeta-Jones and Chong. El Diablo:“It’s been two years, I think I’ll go preach now.” El Diablo walks offstage, as do Zeta-Jones and Chong. End:“But within ten years, Muhammad had earned many enemies in his community, most of the richer men not finding Muhammad’s message too good for their business. So, under pressure and persecution from the leaders of the Meccan community, Muhammad and about 70 Muslim families made the hijrah, or pilgrimage, from Mecca to Medina.” El Diablo and hordes of extras run across the stage. El Diablo:“Quickly! They won’t catch us if we get to the Red Sea!” Extra:“That was Moses and the Hebrews.” El Diablo:“Oh yeah...” After everyone finishes running across the small stage, El Diablo walks back on and delivers and emotionally charged monologue complete with accompanying hand gestures to match his speech. El Diablo:“I had to leave Mecca because the selfish leaders of that community would’ve killed me if I stayed. They would’ve killed me because I was showing the people a better way. I was preaching the Quran. I was teaching Al-Lah’s word of Islam.” El Diablo starts to his left, pacing back and forth in front on the stage. El Diablo:“Islam means surrender. Surrender to the will of God. You believe to do that, Chef, but you are sadly mistaken. You see, you follow the ‘Kill every mother-f*cking thing that moves if it’s not doing what we [being fat, old Islamic fundamentalists who got rich off of construction while doing nothing personally to contribute to Jihad].’ What I preached is different.” El Diablo is joined on stage by Mel Brooks and Billy Graham. El Diablo:“You see, I preached that all peoples of the world, all organized religions that had received the written word of God, were Al-Lah’s children. Al-Lah is THE God, you understand that. But what you, and your cowardly associate Bin Laden, fail to understand is that I preached that Christians and Jews had received their words, their edicts, from Al-Lah as well. He had just come to them in a different form, and given them His words in their own language.” Everyone onstage joins hands. El Diablo:“Indeed, even when our Quraysh, the first and most dedicated Muslims, and our allies and converts took over Christian and Jewish settlements (which was merely for survival and business), I never required those conquered peoples to convert to our way, because they were already living as Al-Lah had intended. If they wished to live our way, I would let them, but who was I to force a different will upon those peoples than what Al-Lah set for them?” The stage is cleared, except for El Diablo, who gets a close-up as the lights dim on the back wall. El Diablo:“The kind of ‘Islam’ that you follow, Chef, is the kind that is preached by Usama Bin Laden. A man born of a father who got rich in the contruction industry. A man who hides in caves and mountains while sending OTHER men to their deaths. A man who has to re-shoot his videos fifteen times because he keeps forgetting not to do his video speeches in front of the sign that lists the name and coordinates of his current base camp. A man who must edit out of his videos all mentions from his followers as to why he will NOT be joining them when they go suicide bombing for Jihad today.” El Diablo points directly at the camera in an accusing way. El Diablo:“YOU, Chef, are a coward, and you make me sick. If you die and DARE to show up at the gates of Paradise, I shall personally kick your ass, castrate you, and have mongooses to eat your face as I use your eyes as stress balls. “For you, and everyone else who is f*cking up the religion the way I set it up perfectly well, here’s a little wake-up call: GET WITH THE F*CKING PROGRAM!” El Diablo backs away from the camera as the lights fade to darkness. The cameras shut off, the show is over. El Diablo approaches The End as the blind one attempts to walk out of the studio. El Diablo:“Where are you going? We still have to film your fight scene with Wee-Man.” Wee-Man runs in wearing a white karate gi with a black belt. He does a karate stance and chops at The End. Wee-Man:“Hi-ya!” The End kicks Wee-Man in the face and walks out. El Diabloo speaks to the cameraman while he helps up Wee-Man. El Diablo:“Did you get that? Excellent! Print it!” Cal Seaver, who watched the whole thing, approaches El Diablo. Seaver:'I'm guessing that this was a promo for your tag match with The End on Turmoil?' El Diablo:'I thought it was for the movie I'm doing, but the notion had crossed my mind.' Seaver:'Well, it was. It's obvious. But if you went through all that trouble to yell at Chef, wouldn't you want to give at least a passing mention to Alexis Parshin?' El Diablo:'Who?'
