Running into an old friend...... Part one.

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/2002 **Our scene opens in its typical promo situation. A wrestler, on the road, away from home and family. Five days away from the biggest match of his life. Five days to covet his beloved world title. Five days to.... AWE FUCK IT. This is Boda we are talking about. Nothing within PCW can take away from Boda that which he loves.... His World Title. Not some wind bagged little, over exaggerating prick. Nor some former goggle wearing, wife laid up in the hospital, former shell of his past, has-been. This is what being the world champ is all about. The respect, the admiration, the hordes of others wanting, and saying anything to get at the goat of Chris Sloboda. From the disrespect of Chase, to the badmouthing, hateful words spewed by Miya, this is what its all about.** Time: 9:35 pmDate: 4/1/2002Place: Phoenix, ArizonaWhy: Because he’s the World Champ, and he can. **NOW, our scene opens, after that last tangent, inside an Outback Steakhouse in downtown Phoenix. Sitting in the far corner, away from all the normal people, sits one PCW World Champion. There, in the corner, alone, desolate, almost full. After all, in front of him sits the remains of a full rack of ribs, tiny giblets of corn that fell from the fork onto the plate. We watch as Boda lifts to his lips, a cool refreshing glass of sweet tea. With an audible ‘AHH’, Boda puts the glass back down, before letting out a rather indiscrete burp. Boda looks around, noticing that no one seen him, and calls for his tab. The rather portly, unshaven waiter walks over, with the typical little black check booklet. He lays it in front of Boda, and takes a few of the empty plates away. Boda opens the booklet, and stares at the check in front of him** Boda: [talking to himself] “Damn that’s a lot of food I ate. Oh well, it was good, and SHIT, look at this....it’s free too. Wonder why?” **As Boda looks back up, there is a man, a rather tall man, standing in front of the table. Boda reads his nametag. BOB SMITHERS. Boda shuns the thought that he knows the man standing there. When suddenly Bob speaks to the World Champion.** Bob: “So, a man with all your talent, had to blow it with a short attitude.” **Again, Boda just sits there, looking at the tall one.** Bob: “Oh, I see. You go on to be an MVP, and then become one of the most successful wrestlers in the world, but you can’t talk to your old fraternity brother.” **Boda lets out his usual chuckle, as Bob continues to speak his mind** Bob: “You know, for someone who was clumsy in college, you seem to be doing well for yourself.” **Boda is now really letting the laughter roar.** Bob: “Shit, I bet you fall flat on your face this Sunday, and you let that little man, Chris Chase pin you.” **Of course, this breaks the straw on the camel’s back. Boda stands up, looking this man straight in his tan face. Some snorting is heard between the two.** Boda: “The way I remember it, it was YOUR clumsiness that broke YOUR leg in the March Madness tourney that year. And, as for Chris Chase... [laughing] .... actually pinning me, there is a better chance of GOD himself pinning me in the center of the ring on Sunday.” **Boda steps around the table, and the two men share a quick, brotherly embrace.** Boda: “Holy shit Bob.... how the hell have you been?” Bob: “Well, apparently not as well as you have been. Man, let me tell you.... I never expected this side of you. I mean, you loved basketball, you dreamt basketball, hell, you slept with the rock next to you every night.” **Boda nods in agreement. Boda: “You’re right. I did love the game, more than anything. And I still do. But, Bob... I found something better. More fulfilling WRESTLING. I mean, coming out and jamming in front of 20 thousand fans was fun, but coming out, and getting booed, like you are the ultimate bad guy, badder than Gehngis Khan, Napoleon, hell, more hated than Hitler. Now, that, my friend is pure adrenalin rush.” Bob: “Chris... you always got booed. Especially in other gyms, when you would kill the boards, and the opposition. What makes now any better?” **Boda thinks for a moment** Boda: “Well, for starters..... I can beat the hell out of, say someone like Chris Chase, who is loved , for unknown reasons, by the fans. This is a man who is heralded as ‘The Messiah’, so the people love him. Well, I get booed for beating down the ‘Messiah’. I get screamed at for slapping my big hands around his neck, lifting him high into the air, and bringing him crashing down to the mat with a Bounced Czech. Or, better yet...... [Boda gets that sadistic, evil look in his eyes].... the sound of Natalie Miyagawa crashing to the concrete, and the escalading effect of the fans all jeering me, was, almost.....ORGASMIC” **The last few remaining patrons look in the direction of Boda, surprised at his sudden choice of words to outburst too. Boda just shoots a evil smile at the customers, who in response, turn away.** Boda: “Sorry, Bob. It’s just I tend to get excited about wrestling. Hell, I get excited at the thought of taking Miyagawa and Chris Chase, standing them face to face. Then, grabbing each one, by the back of their greasy little heads, and with a THUD.... smashing their pathetic faces together. Then, being it is a PPV match.... I take it one step farther......I’ll do it again, and again, till from their noggins, drains the blood Miya wants from me.” Bob: “Damn Chris.... I must say... you are a lot different than in college. Oh well, pal, nice seeing you again. Keep in touch when in Phoenix.” **Boda shakes Bob’s hand. The two nod, and pat each other on the back. Boda signs an autograph for Bob’s son, and heads off, to the hotel he is staying at, which is conveniently, at least for this promo, right across from the Outback Steakhouse. So, there we have the monster of PCW, a gentle man, as far as friends and family is concerned, but, stick him in a match against Chris Chase.... and this gentle giant becomes uncontrollable. Well, looks like Boda found the front door, and has gone in to the front desk. Boda checks for messages, and hits the elevators.** BING BING **The elevator doors open, to the 4th floor. Boda steps out, and look towards his room. 420. Boda heads for his room, when suddenly, he grabs his stomach, and runs to his room. Swinging it open, Boda rushes into the bathroom, and slams the door shut.** SLAM **For the next 10-15 minutes, we are previeded to a slew of shitty Chris Chase promos. Which, would in reality take more than the allotted next 10-15 minutes.** FLUSH **Boda opens the door, and looks rather ill. So, the big badass pushes the camera away, sending it tumbling to the floor in the hallway. We see Boda climb onto the bed as the scene fades out.** TOO BE CONTINUED...........
