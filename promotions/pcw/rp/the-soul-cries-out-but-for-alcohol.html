The Soul Cries Out, But For Alcohol?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.12.02 (#1 TT) And so, due to an unfortunate set of circumstances, and to an uncanny turn if events, an irreparable damage has now been done, eternally committed to Abner’s pride: he’s lost his debut match. Yes, he’s lost his first match in PCW. And what a match it was. Coming in ill-prepared, unfocused and only mildly confident (though totally cocky), Abner was chokeslamed……yes, chokeslamed by Kai, and then because of this, inevitably lost the match….. But a chokeslam? To be picked up and the air and slammed into the mat by a much smaller opponent, well…..well it’s quite embarrassing! You see, maybe Abner would have expected a technical, foot-flinging, arm-dragging victory by his worthy opponent….. but to be overpowered!? No! Certainly not. And such tragedy has never struck him before….. And he doesn’t intend to let it happen again. Though he faired well in the beginning of the match, and certainly, throughout this event, showed his resiliency, Abner showed no motivation. Jabbing to the crowd here and there, a couple of low blows, and physically taunting his opponent, Abner showed no real charisma……and as a matter of fact, Abner had no character at all! But now, all that must change. You see now, now he will work on the mentality, on the physiological and character building aspect of his much-needed training. No longer will he focus on petty technical details, for as far as wrestling goes (that is when he actually got some offense in…..), Abner didn’t look too bad…..certainly better than what he expected of himself. As a matter of fact, it was hard to tell that he was a rookie. And throughout the match, as defensive maneuvers also showed, he certainly had a satisfactory ring presence. So why did he lose this match? This one that he thought he’d prepared so well for? Who knows. Maybe that’s the way it was supposed to be. A wakeup call? You see, Abner never knew he could fight…..no, wrestle that well, and he never knew that he was, well…..that lame either. Oddly enough, where he should have failed, he excelled at (wrestling), and where he should have excelled, he failed (mentality). So how, when and where to fix it? With a little help, now, and here….. ---------------------------------------- “You need to express yourself Abner, you need to live…..” What the ‘ell? “You need to rise with the waters of the ocean, you need to breath with the colors of the wind.” Jesus Christ! “Uh, lady….pardon me ma’am, but I don’t think this is ‘elpin me much.” “Why, whatever do you mean?” Abner shakes his head in about thirty percent confusion, and sixty percent disgust (and as you know, he can’t do math, so as for the other ten percent, who knows?). The young hick comes to his feet. Staring across the room at his “spiritual guidance counselor”, Abner certainly doesn’t know what to make of it all. Goddamnit, now why’d I even come here again? Was it ‘cause a somethin’ I ate? Naw….. Scratching his head, it comes back to him. Ah yeah, ‘cause I lost that match with Kai! Yep that’s it, uh-huh …. Chuckling. That monkity-monkey-monk sure did whoop me good….. More chuckles. And then, catching himself….. Hey…..that’s not funny! Not very funny a’tall. See, if it weren’t for losin’ to that son-va-gun, I wouldn’t be here in this goddamn hippie’s office! And indeed, he’s right: he wouldn’t be. Staring around the room, Abner doesn’t quite feel “at home”. He’s entrapped in a ten-by-twenty prison of “flower power” and “John Lennon hour”. “Damn!”, he thinks to himself. “If I’d a only known….” Yes, but what good does that do now? Abner’s standing on an environmentally safe carpet, red, and made of some sort of unknown, liberal-induced material. The walls are painted a dullish white, with a faint hint of pink. And all around him, surrounding the inmate on both sides, are various posters of cultural leaders…..Lennon, Garcia, Zinn, and Scooby Doo…..(don’t ask). So, turning to face a modern-art poster that reads “We are the protectors of the Earth”, you can imagine how uncomfortable this hick felt. Turning to face a statue of a nude Roman with the message inscribed reading “The God’s Love You”, Abner’s stomach turns. And I thought D-29 was bad! Horseshit…... Interrupting his thoughts, “Good…..good, very good”, the women interjects. Abner shakes himself out of it. “I can tell, you’re starting to open up your soul now…..good, good, yes, laughter the potion of happiness……please, continue.” “Uh….no thanks, ma’am”, Abner scratches his head, “I do believe that uh…..uh…..well you see, my soul is beggin’ for a beer! So praise the lord! Or Mother Earth…..or somethin’ like that…..anyway, gotta follow yer heart ya know.” Abby roles her eyes as the redneck gallops across the room. “No, no silly…..that’s not your soul speaking…..it’s your ‘darkside’, my friend…..fight it Abner, fight the darkside, fight it.” This time, Abner rolls his eyes. “Yeah, whatever.” Reaching down, fishing out a can of Bud from his cooler, he pops it open and begins to start his own technique of “soul healing”. One that has more quicker, and desirable physical effects too. “Is not! It’s my goddamn ‘darkside’, hippie…..so leave me the hell alone!” Momentarily, the poor women goes into shock. She lifts back her head of long blond hair, rubs her hands across her cheeks and takes in a deep, deep breath. Her chest slowing rising (to Abner’s delight), she looks as if she’s going to explode. But then, suddenly, she stops. Facing Abner, a twinkle comes into her light blue eyes as her face in engulfed in an overwhelming smile of unknown joy. Oh, Jesus….. Folding the creases in her long black skirt, she nods to Abner. “Oh….how could I have been so lame…..of course…..of course…..hmm…..” Intently, she rubs her chin. Abner, staring to get worried, “What!? What!?” “Oh you poor man, that’s not you crying, it’s your soul crying out for help….” “But…” Interrupting him, “And wait….” stopping to cuff her hand over her ear, “Do you hear it?” Abner shrugs. “Here what?” “It’s your liver silly! It’s saying, it’s saying….hold on a second, the voice is becoming clearer…..” “Yeah, you hold on too.” Abner flips his head back and downs the rest of the beer. Almost rolling her eyes, Abby thinks better of it, ignores him, and continues on. “It’s saying ‘help me, help me! All of this alcohol is killing me. Help me, help…..please, please stop!” Horseshit! And Abner’s had enough. Quickly turning to face the doorway, he picks up his cooler and makes for an escape. “Noooo! Don’t go!” The cry of the women stops him dead in his tracks. Abner turns to confront her. “Well Jesus Christ lady, I came here to work on my character…my, my wrestling personality, and all you give me is this hippie horseshit ‘bout my soul and liver….I mean, c’mon, what the hell is this, Oprah!” Hmm…..Oprah….. The women seems disheartened. “Abner, Abner, Abner…..poor, poor Abner. You see, all you need, and all I was trying to give you”, almost in tears by now, “was some spiritual guidance.” She pauses for a moment to wipe a single teardrop from her eye.”And now, I can see that that is not the case….all, all you want…..” gently weeping now, “is to, is to get some mental courage to beat your opponent…..and boost your dreadful ego.” Now in full fledged tears, she rushes forward and lunges for Abner’s arms. Holy hippies n’ horseshit….. “Hold me now Abner, hold me….” “Bbb……bbb…..but I…..” Abner, not expecting such a thing, shrugs and slowly opens his arms to her. Embracing her (mainly due to the loftiness of her breasts protruding through the tightness of her tee-shirt), Abner begins to calm himself down. But enough is enough. “Alright now ma’am, don’t cry no more…..see, I’ll no longer be part of your troubles…..” I hope…… Abby slowly backs away. Sniffing, she reaches up and wipes a hand across her face. “Oh Abner, what a troubled individual you are…..” Damn right! Lost my first goddamn match…..and the, come this Tuesday, I’m facing not one…..not one and a half, but two, two of the best cruiserweights in PCW! And win, lose or draw, the way my back is now, I’m sure to come outta there quite a wreck….. “Your soul cries out for….(now, changing to Abner’s interpretation) blah blah blah! Blah blah blah blah blah blah blah.” See, come Turmoil, live in ol’ Mexico City, I’m gonna need all the tequila I can get my paws on if I wish to come out that match feelin’ good! And that’s exactly what I intend to do….” “Mother Earth…..blah blah blah…..I cannot blah blah blah (switching back to reality), and so, you see, if it wasn’t for such a nasty childhood…..” Abner cuts her off. “Err…..right….but I, uh, gotta get goin’ now…..” “………..” “Bye….” Abner spins on his heels, and before the liberal lady can protest, marches out the door. But he’ll come back. Shit! Suddenly, stopping in his tracks, Abner turns and rushes back into the room. The lady peers at him with a question of optimism and concern in her eye. Abner sighs. “Oh…..whew…..almost made a big mistake…..” The women lets out a sigh of relief as well. “Well Abner, it’s good that…..” He puts up a hand, and cuts her off again. “I know…..it’s good that I didn’t for forgit mah beer.” Close one! The women blinks an eye as Abner bends over and scoops up the cooler into his arms, cuddling it closely as if it’s an only child. Turning back to address her for a last time, “How is it that you hippies say g’bye….ah yeah!.....cheers!” Abner tips an invisible hat to Abby. I think that’s it, cheers, right?.......(mental shrug) “What?” Ignoring her, he turns and speeds out the door, lugging his prized package behind in a possessive grip. “But…..”, she cries after him. “See y’all later!” And Abner exits the vicinity. So…..this Tuesday, Turmoil, Mexico City……its yours truly verses El Romantitastical…..or somethin’ like that…..verses Amber Tolken…. Well Romantico, no reason to fear him, see after the ol’ Mexican (you know the one) at D-29, I know how to deal with his type…..just don’t bend over, and you’ll be fine….. And as for the “forbidden fruit”, yeah, the other one might want a taste of ‘em, but no, not me…..I think I’ll just kick his ass an’, uh, leave it at that….call it day. Nods his head in approval. Now, onto more important issues…..forbidden fruit, eh?......yeah, well where’s the “forbidden” drink Goddamnit! ‘Cause now that I be goin’ down to ol’ Mexico, Lord knows I got to get some of that shit! And indeed, he would. ---------------------------------------- And so our favorite ex-con/redneck/cult leader, a PCWer with a 0% win rate, is determined to change these stats…..but how? Is he focused……yes…..but on the right thing? Probably not. And is he determined? Yes…..but only to get wasted viva la Mexican style….. And if he does in fact get wasted in the sense of inebriation, will he also be wasted by his hungry opponents at Turmoil? He hopes not….. But we’ll see…..we’ll see…..
