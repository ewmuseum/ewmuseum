NO Sell This........

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/30/04 The trip from Raleigh to Chapel Hill isn’t a long one. It’s a mere 30 minutes, 40 minutes if you stop for a drink, along mostly Route 40. But it’s long enough to kick back, think about what’s going on in ones life. Be it the troubles of a longtime friendship, turned bitter hatred, or the addition of a great group of men to protect the fans of PCW from the boring dribble of The Primetime Player, or the current challenge of The Face to Face Tournament. But this trip, the main concern is not some piss ant, Johnny Come Lately, but rather the ceremony, being held at Turmoil. So here we have it, in a H2 Hummer, traveling up Route 40, Ryan Knox, in control of the steering wheel, and doing a damn good job at it too. Sitting next to him, the Hall of Famer Chris ‘Boda’ Sloboda. Lounged out in the back, half asleep is Luke Chadbourne. But, missing is Mike Dangerously. He had to fly home, to be with his family in the face of a family emergency. It’s unclear whether he’ll be at Turmoil this week or not. Boda told him to take his time, and settle up the family first. Chris reaches over, and turns down the stereo, bringing Luke to blurt out. ‘I like that song’. Boda blows it off with a mere wave of his hand. The big man has a smile on his face that could only be chiseled off. After all, the past two weeks have gone his way. First, defeating Jon Kano and garnering a spot in the Face to Face tournament. And just a few days ago, Boda and Knox defeated Kano and Kanton. But we all know the real reason he’s in a great mood. ‘Man, I’m happy for you. That’s a great honor an athlete can receive. Hell, now no one will ever get to wear #13 while playing for The Tarheels again.’ Ryan shares in Boda’s happiness. He knows Boda’s gonna put him in his speech, so why not. After all, both these two have a history in PCW, and that’s why they’ve come to work together in The Haters. ‘You really don’t know how elated I am Ryan. Things are great. Hell, the only thing that can make this a better year... is winning this tournament, and becoming a 3 time PCW World Champion.’ The former World Champion shifts around in his seat. ‘You know I hate not driving. Why’d I ever let you talk me into you driving?’ ‘Because I won the match, I pinned Kanton. That’s why. And besides, you need your rest. You’ve got a tough challenge ahead of you this week.’ Ryan laughs, almost not finishing his statement. Luke laughs too, and then dozes off again. ‘What? Hey man, I’m not taking this challenge lightly. After all, he’s a self proclaimed Giant Killer. He’s The Thriller. Dude... I’ve got to get prepared for Nathaniel.’ Boda is serious in his demeanor. So serious, that Ryan is taken aback. But that all changes in an instance. ‘For real dawg... I’m getting challenged by a free agent, that’s like what? 150 soaking wet? I saw him backstage, I figured he was some new crew member. But let’s give him credit... he picked the biggest and the baddest to make a fool out of him.’ That brings a chuckle to the three. ‘And what about his name, Chris. Nathaniel Oscar Seller? N.O Seller? What’s he suppose to be? Some Meanstreak wannabe?’ Ryan brings up a blast from the past, the most notorious no-seller in PCW history. Luke asks who, as both Chris and Ryan shake their head. ‘You need to learn you PCW history’ ‘Yeah, I thought about that. After all, at least it’s a gimmick. Weak at best, but still. But Ryan, I shouldn’t talk about another man’s gimmick. After all, it’s been well noted that I have no gimmick. I shouldn’t make fun of some kid, claiming he’s a no-seller. I mean, come on, he might not sell cars. He probably doesn’t sell drugs. Shit, he probably isn’t a chair salesman. But come Saturday, that little cruiserweight fly will know what it’s like to sell a Boda Bomb, a bounced Czech and hell, maybe even a Boda Drop.’ Boda reaches over and turns the A/C up a click, as Ryan pulls off Route 40 and into a Hardee’s. The scene fades with the Hummer stopping in the drive thru.
