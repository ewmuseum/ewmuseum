Title: Karma always comes back to get you Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Oct.6, 2002 Looking back, it was really not that much fun to be there.. Being in Japan was all right, a new experience and new field to train my technical skills toward..I came there expecting to be given a true test for my ability to wear down an opponent, to gain even greater prowess as I worked out at the gym and experimented with new holds, and to find just where I was at on an international level as I entered into the prestigious Ultimate Jr. tournament the upstart PURE federation was holding, which had drawn in a great number of big names both in Japan and abroad...I felt interested, I honestly was considering leaving FSW for PURE at that point because of the promise it showed and the reputation of its owner, Sean Williams, noted Puroresu epicure....And that's where things went wrong. I signed up for PURE: Pure Ultimate Ring Entertainment. That meant, to me that it was going to be traditional Japanese puroresu, may the best person win through pure application of their grappling skills to a traditional setting, no ridiculous gimmicks, no ownership struggles for power taking up the wrestlers' hard-earned TV time, no pointless matches made up for no reason but to make non-wrestlers look good in the ring...I mean, I was in NTICW for a couple of appearances, if I wanted crap like that I'd've stayed there! So I signed up for the Ultimate Jr. tournament, and I traveled over to Japan to see how my presence would go over..And you know what? I enjoyed it, I was boosting my repertoire to prepare, I found a base of fans I didn't even know I had, and I was all ready to finally have a real challenge as opposed to those ridiculous fake-ups FSW had to make their guys look good...Sure, there was a new minority controller to the fed's interests in Jamar Wilson, but I figured he'd realize it was best to step aside and let the federation's driving forces push its entertainment... That's why I only figure with numbers now, they don't decide to act purely for their own self-interest, a 3 will always be 3, it won't change itself to a 9 simply because it wants to make a bigger number..Jamar on the other hand didn't appreciate true puro, and it showed in his attitude...An attitude I hope to crush with my I drew Gina Muniz in my first-round match, and to be honest, I didn't know if I would win. I was facing talent that I didn't have experience with, only had heard of the career and accomplishments. I thought I could do it, though my past experiences with female wrestlers threw me off...And I trained my ass off. I worked hard for that battle, I actually looked forward to seeing how I fared, before one event that changed my outlook on the federation... I was in the ring, ready to fight my heart out, and that little gutless puke Wilson showed up, carting along a big fat wheel, like the sort you could see at some low-rent state fair booth in one of the crappy states, like Rhode Island or Wyoming, or New Mexico...Come to think of it, who the hell would want to be considered a new version of Mexico? But that's besides the point. The fact of the matter was not on the the wheel's existence itself, but on what had been placed on the wheel.....Gimmicks. They ranged all around the sort of gimmicks one would expect from a federation like PCW, OSW or FSW, and this was what had aggravated me whole-heartedly. PCW would put you into gimmick matches often, but at least they had the decency to never have claimed to be anything but an entertainment-based gimmicky federation, you knew when you came in that you were going to have to be a brilliant wrestler in various disciplies AND tough as nails, with a little ingenuity on occasion...PURE, on the other hand, was pulling the ol' bait-and-switch on me. They got me to come over with the promise of a pure wrestling experience, and to be handed this gimmick wheel, complete with ridiculous name, was a direct insult to my integrity..I held firm though, I wanted to at least compete and show my skill, make my abilities the forefront...And what does Jamar do? He gives the wheel a spin... Bra and Panties?! What in the bloody hell is that? I came to Japan to wrestle, not to strip some girl! This was the ultimate insult, that this little prick was going to waltz in and tell me not to try to beat my opponent with my technical skill, but merely to quickly grab some clothing and tear it off for a quick bit of T&A for his enjoyment...This was where I knew PURE was going down the drain, for though Sean managed to change the match back to a normal wrestling match, which I proceeded to press the advantage throughout only to get caught with a comeback, climaxing in my defeat. Sure, I probably could've won it with my advantage throughout the match had Jamar's gimmick stuck, but this was not what I was about. I wanted a fair fight, I wanted to earn my victory, and should I lose, so be it. I didn't want some farce that went against all my efforts.. So I lost the match, and that should've been the end, but I was in luck. The match'd been such an impressive duel that when Kyle Lamen did what comes naturally to him and didn't show up, I was invited to replace him in the tournament against Zander McCain in what had been declared a no-disqualification bout...This meant that my repertoire of technical wrestling would have to expand a little to adapt to the cheapshot and weaponry options, the not-that-different but still startlingly new fashion in which the battle would take place..And battle we did. I was impressed by the newcomer, he showed great spirit, and we fought tooth-and-nail for the better part of 10-15 minutes..Then, we charged each other, weakened by the battle, and each struck the other full-force to the skull with a steel chair...knocking both of us out for a double-pin situation. Now, as much as our toughness had carried us through that much of the battle and won over the crowd, I knew it was tainted to move on to the second round with failure to attain victory in at least one match... I still was thirsting for a battle which would not be tainted by hesitation, interference of those dueling owners, or non-decisive finishes...It never came. Jamar and Sean didn't even have the guts to continue the tournament, cutting it off suddenly because they hadn't even managed to get their travel arrangements together and get the wrestlers to their matches every week, then delaying cards constantly further and further back...What respect was I to have for a federation that couldn't keep its own business straight? PURE could not hold a candle to PCW in that right, they just weren't able to maintain good business in a competitive market, surviving merely as a leech to the TokyoPop corporation, of which Jamar Wilson was a pathetic representative, trying to hire big name stars and immediately alienating them with his egotistical behaviour..Was it any wonder that Lamen didn't show, DarkStar's second-round tournament match never came about, and some of the federation's former stars deserted it, Miyagawa was nowhere to be seen now in this new version, Kensuke Suzahara refused to come back himself, The HIT was slated to be part but it became evident he wasn't coming either, the ego of Jamar Wilson would not let him say that 'We're going to change the matches over because the talent wouldn't come there', but just decided to throw away the viewers' time and ours as wrestlers completely...And the team he leads to Red October reflects that ego... Cianan, Enmity, Khadafi, generally wrestlers who had little respect for puro's tradition themselves..They would all try to avoid the technical aspects, cheaters to the last man...But this time things would be different.... There could be no evasion of puroresu for them in front of their hometown fans against the evil 'gaijin' as the Japanese considered us... ....But were we all that bad? Violence Jack, tough as nails enough to defeat Flaymin Aymin in utterly brutal Extreme Title matches...Jung Loc, PURE's own Jr. Champion who had proven his greatness in PCW with some astonishing skill in his Cruiserweight Title reigns and while he was PURE through-and-through, I felt he just might understand that they were destructive to his own wrestling..And if he couldn't understand that, the Front Office would have to insure that he knew what he was doing..The 5th person...What can I think about this 5th person? I cannot truly think that much, for in spite of my efforts to learn, Brandi has indicated that it's in the contract that I cannot know yet... It brings us to Brandi Wilson herself, who probably knows better than anyone how it feels to have one's talent overlooked by a federation, and PCW's history takes a darker turn where she's concerned. From the moment she debuted, she was just treated as if she was a glorified manager of the Storm Brothers, constantly put in matches against the Loserweights of the federation and Bethany, then stalked for months on end when she finally started to try to make a name for herself, before getting harassed by Big Daveylicious of all people...I sympathize deeply for that, to be able to beat Corey Williams on her own merit but while he made friends backstage and got into the best groups for it, right down a cheap run with the World Title because his friends decided to play hot potato, she was stuck losing in Jello matches with Kya Brady because of those nosy Satanic jobbers, and she finally managed to take her rightful place as Extreme Champion through sheer superiority... But then, just like me, she got fired because Tyler Nelson felt like throwing his power around, she was a superior technical wrestler up until the day she got thrown onto the street, probably because Mona was angry at not being able to beat her with all the cheating she could muster and Nelson wanted to please his little whore...This sort of injustice, like being typecast as a hillbilly or discriminated against for Scientology's low societal perception, was not something that we could just sit through and like, but now it was our turn on top...The underdogs had slipped their way up to the power, and for those who had the power before, like Nelson, Jamar, and the pathetic shells of true wrestlers that were on Jamar's side, this could only spell trouble, for while there was always authority to get them out of a tough situation before.... Now, there could be no escape from the revolt of those who had been struck against.. Now, there would be no evasion. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
