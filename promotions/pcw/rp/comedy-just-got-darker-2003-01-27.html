Comedy just got darker...

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/01/03 “Twenty six, zero one, zero three. I couldn’t be any more insulted if I tried. Surprisingly, my anger isn’t aimed at Gaz. It’s aimed at around five or six individuals who aren’t deserving of their lives. Let me see now... Gunn. Bane. C.B.Judgment.New Blood. The vampire-slaying collective also known as Bloodbath. I don’t know whether Gaz is paying them to do this, or whether it’s part of their own foolish crusade. Either way, it’s time for me to thin the herd. Starting with their muscle, Bane....” “Another.” came a displeased voice. The sound of a newspaper smacking down onto the pool table caused the heads of all in the room to perk up. The headline, like something out of a joke, reads “Dracula in Oceana?” in bold print. Carl Black, the resident intelligence and communications specialist of the team turned around and sighed, breathing in an unwanted cloud of Gunn’s smoke. Clearing his throat, he looked the leader of the team in the face, expecting a response. Gunn smiled and continued to puff on his cigar. “Don’t you think it’s a little co-incidental that Oceana is the next place PCW’s going to hold a show?” Black coughed. “He’s been forced to bleed. He’s going to need to refuel. Why aren’t we there?” “CB’s right.” Came the enthusiastic chorus from Judgment. Flicking back some of his white dreadlocked hair, the clean-up section of the team made a fist. “We know he’s been bleeding and he’ll be hunting soon. Let’s nail him while we have the chance!” Gunn pushed himself up from the leather chair and set the lamp besides him swinging with a tap. Kicking a pool cue into his hand, he pulled a few strands of blonde hair from his eyes and folded the sheets open, scanning the pages with his baby blues. “Hey, Bane...” Gunn grinned. “You’re going to like this one. Apparently, he did the same thing as last night and wigged out. Went into his true form again, except now he’s doing it all the time. Think he’s trying to send a message?” “Hmph.” The mountain of a man snorted and raised an eyebrow. “Contest of size? Fine.” Standing up, Bane began to run his left index finger along his arm. It stopped on a clear patch of skin. Surrounding it on all sides was an artistic yet disturbing gallery of vampire faces. Each one seemed to be in it’s final moments of death. Some didn’t even have their teeth left in. Bane snapped his fingers, making a loud clicking sound.“Judgment!” he called, giving a glance over towards the youngest. Black shades met black contact lenses as Judgment looked back. “Yeah?” he answered with a small quiver in his voice, as though he’d done something wrong. “Get a picture of him for when I come back.” Bane grunted. Standing up, the giant cracked his knuckles and made his way towards the door of the room. Everyone braced themselves as the CRASH! of the slamming door shook the entire room. “Okay. Someone tell me WHY he does that?” asked Black. “He’s a bitter guy. Just likes smashing things, I guess.” chirped New Blood. “Not the door... I mean the skin thing. I’ve seen him do it twice now.” Black said, looking out of the taped-up window that offered a small peek into the street below. The engine in Bane’s jeep roared into life and the vehicle sped off, leaving a trail of exhaust fumes that hung in the air like Gunn’s cigar smoke. “Oh, that.” New Blood gulped. “Well, everytime he kills a vampire, he gets Judgment to give him a new tattoo. He just selects a place to put it and then we look for a picture of the soon-to-be recently re-deceased. Then when he comes back, he gets a new victim added. “Shouldn’t we all go, then?” Black continued. New Blood shook his head and scratched his lighting-shaped sideburns. “Bane takes things personally. The first time he ran into a vampire, it challenged him in a test of strength. Of course, the vampire was only just re-born, so it was still more or less human. Bane went mad and pushed his arms so hard that the skin split above the vamp’s elbows and he literallty turned the arms inside out. The vamp was walking around for three minutes or so with his hands all flappy after his fingers fell out of his... well... fingers.” “And what New Blood don’t like to emphasise is that the first vampire he killed was his ex-girlfriend. That’s what made him so quiet. Use to be a loudmouth li’l bastard. Now he’s the strong, silent type, literally.” Gunn explained. “Nerezza seems to want to make this personal with us. Apparently, he only used to turn into a vampire when he needed to, but the paper here makes it sound like he’s doing it all the time now. If he wants a personal challenge, Bane’s the one to give it to him. In the meantime, Judgment can get ready with the needles.” * * *A few days later... * * * Another day at the office. Well, at the bar. Blood Bath’s headquarters seemed empty without the massive Bane, but it was a thing that the members of the vampir ekilling team had got used to, especially with the lone wolf attitude that their largest member always projected. It was around 10:30am. New Blood was behind the bar, tinkering with some sort of device that looked like a Game Boy with an antenna on top. Judgment lay sprawled across the sofa where he always was, sketching on a notepad. The pencil had put out a decent likeness to Nascosto Nerezza so far, ready to be added to Bane’s arm like another trophy. CB was challenging Gunn to a game of pool. So far, only the blue stripe and the black 8-Ball remained, with Gunn lining up the cue ball to sink the 8 and end the game. Chalking the tip, he blew and scattered a little dust into air. “Time’s up... and with one to go...” Gunn cockily grinned. As he pulled back ready to pot, a knock at the door shredded his concentration, causing the cue to go sideways and leave a trail of blue chalk streaking across the table. “DAMMIT!” he exclaimed. CB lined up to take his shot, but Gunn’s gloved hand clamped firmly onto his shoulder. “Hey, what’s the problem?” CB squirmed and pulled away. “Everyone, the door just went. Get on guard, for Christ’s sake.” spat Gunn. Taking one last puff of his cigar, he pressed the end into one of the ash trays on the bar and pulled one of the pistols from his pocket, removing the safety and tucking it into the back of his stonewash jeans. Adjusting his bandana and vest, Gunn looked backwards once at his crew, who all remained poised and masked by the door. Gunn nodded, then slowly turned the door knob... “Special Delivery, no name on this, sorry. I’m going to have to get a signature here.” came the request. Gunn opened the door fully to see a mailman with the typical hat and bag. Sweat dripped from his head in the sun and he looked tired from carrying the huge package in his hands. Gunn eyed it suspiciously. It was oblong and tied in a red ribbon. “Fine. Where do I sign?” the impatient leader of Blood Bath grunted. “Just down here, sir” replied the mailman, holding up a clipboard. Gunn sighed and took the ballpoint from the mailmain, scribbling down something that vaguely resembled a name and scraped the clipboard with his studded spiked wristbands. The mailman withdrew the clipboard and handed over the parcel with an intimidated look in his eye before scurrying out of view. The door closed behind him with a loud thud and Gunn quickly carried it over to the pool table. “New Blood, metal and explosives detector, STAT!” Gunn ordered. New Blood sprang from his place behind the bar and vaulted the counter. Turning and pulling on a stool that seemed nailed into place, the front panel of the counter slide across to reveal an array of strange looking gadgets. Like a scene from Men In Black, New Blood scanned the contents and picked out something black, shiny and no bigger than a regular pen. Holding it horizontally, New Blood turned and began to point to towards the mysterious and suspicious package. “No beeping... it’s clean.” he sighed with relief. The entire room gave an accompanying “Phew!” alongside him as the verdict was delivered. Gunn wasted no time in tearing the ribbon off. As he lifted the lid off, a disgusting stink invaded the nostrils of all present as they gathered around the pool table. “Man, that is SICK!” Judgment declared, turning his head around from the stench. “Give it a rest, it’s probably some trophy that Bane couldn’t get through customs. Let’s have a look... there’s a card.” Gunn said, pinching his nose and fishing a small card from the wrapping paper. “I couldn’t decide which picture to use. Pick one and get the needles out.” Gunn read. “Chalk up another one for the big guy then. I’ll get my kit.” Judgment said. “That won’t be needed, Judgment...” Gunn grimly gasped. “Why, boss?” the asian asked curiously, tilting his head to one side and pulling a wave of dark hair out of his face. “Bane won’t be coming back, bro...” New Blood gulped in a hollow manner. Gunn unwrapped the package to reveal two huge, muscular arms covered in tattooes of disfigured vampires. Scattered among the bloodstained remains are two photos. One is of Bane laying on the ground and backing away with bloody marks and slashes all over his face, the second of Bane’s corpse, without arms or a head and leaking blood all over the concrete...
