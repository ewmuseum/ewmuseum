The Spider’s Web.Prologue.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Some time in January Alright, this is what really happened. Not that I expect anyone to believe my story. Behind me sit twenty inmates of St. Mary’s Hospital for the Mentally Ill. Through the glass partition comes the thunder of their dentures clapping together. “Really, could you please like, NOT do that?” See, where we’re sitting now is at the edge of humanity. The friends of St. Mary’s bus freewheels down the dunes, see with a vehicle like this you need to milk the gas for all it’s worth. Anyway, if you were planning on actually reading the beginning of a shitty story, don’t. To tell the truth I haven’t planned the story out. I don’t know what’s going to happen. If all goes to plan I’ll be dead before we hit the casinos. Or what could happen is the sirens catch me and somehow make a miraculous rescue. I’ll still die. But at least we’ll know that the inmates are OK. The flickering yellows come from the casinos in the distance. Yellows with backdrops of red, greens and blues. Closer and closer, ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the Nevada desert. The meter just hit a hundred. This isn’t a story where I turn into Lucifer, no I don’t turn into Jesus either. See: Steve Douglas Also: Angel Dalton Steve Douglas, the big Jesus-ophobic.... Instead what you’re reading shouldn’t look any better than rotting matter, what you’re reading should make you sick. What you’re reading should piss you off. Because what I’m really, really trying to do here is get on the nerves of all those Abner’s. All those Steve Douglas’. Those Angel Dalton’s. I hijack their bus; they ask what time is mass going to be. These are people who remember me. These are people who think I raped their cat sixty years ago. Tore up their Sunday dress, the nice pink one she used to wear all those seventy years ago. What do these people want? Like, hello? I’m SORRY. Everybody, everybody, your attention here.... Ah fuck it.... Well, just before the final minutes, I’ll say it again, I’m not a murderer. I’m a saviour. So if you’re still reading now. Stop. If all goes to plan I die at the end. They die at the end. Everyone’s dead. Story’s ruined. The Spiders Web.?. Ladies and gentlemen, it’s now 8.30 p.m and death lies around the corner.
