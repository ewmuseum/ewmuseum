The Final Countdown - 'The Searching' Interlude

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-03-02 .Song listening guide: 'The Final Countdown' by Extreme? To The Extreme? Something like that. It's from the 80's and you can still hear it over the PA at the end of tight basketball games. Plus I think they have a VH1 special. :P BOBBY STORMBRINGER The Final Countdown - 'The Searching' Interlude --3-04-02, Monday, 6:32 pm local time, one and a half hours before Madness-- Our quick scene that is only meant to keep things updated fades in on the personal dressing room of Bobby Stormbringer in the Savvis Center, in St. Louis, Missouri. Before moving into the general Asylum locker room for last minute reminders on secret strategies, B.S. is taking the time out to mingle with what might as well be his only true family, in the best sense of the word. His personal assisstant and almost serigate father, Carl Everstrong, as well as the only female on Earth that he 'marks out' for more than Aimz, and that would be the semi-normal, yet adorably nutty Kira. They have grown so close so quickly that they might as well be boyfriend/girlfriend, or even husband and wife. At every chance that the two get, they are utterly unseparable. The only thing that is lacking is to make it 'official', for one of the two to step up to the plate, stop taking it slow for the sake of taking it slow, take an actual risk. Maybe even ask the other to be their 'spouse', (What?) be their 'attached', (What??) to 'go steady', (What!?) to 'see one another', (What!?!) to 'date', (WHAT!!) and to basically admit that they are just head over heels in love with each other. But the thing that is lacking is saying with their words, not just their longing eyes. Hell, they haven't even kissed yet! Well, not on the lips, anyway. Just everywhere else above the collarbone. There is a television monitor near a corner of the rectangular-shaped room, as well as only two lockers side by side. Neither of them are padlocked. The TV monitor's volume is low and unhearable, but the Mark McGuire-lovin' crowd is just beginning to file in to the arena. Cal Seaver and Sandy Wong are also just moments away from their Pay-Per-View pre-show, complete with one jobber match and thirty-nine minutes of hype. You also have to allow for the cable commercials which take up sixteen minutes of an hour long program. Bet you didn't know that, eh? Well, it's eight of thirty. So I, the narrator, is quite sure that it is only doubled. Wait, that involvs common sense in the entertainment industry. Maybe I'm not so certain after all... Guys like Tyler Nelson and 'Vinnie Mac', all these promotors... all they do is throw this show up on cable somewhere to try to get people to buy at the last minute. Kids? This you very last chance to beg your parents to shell out thirty bucks that you didn't do anything to deserve! Soon, it'll be thirty-five dollars. Considering how much Boxing charges for their.. *ahem*.. 'events'.. then wrestling will catch up in about five years to the fifty buck scamming range, since at least you'll know you have at least two and a half hours of something to watch. Does anybody watch that boxing undercard? Didn't think so. B.S., Carl, and Kira all sit on a long and unusually comfy bench in front of the lockers. Carl wears his usual slacks and white t-shirt, looking like a poor man's Anthony Hopkins, like usual. Kira wears a stunning red dress that is still simple enough to not be mistaken at someone who should of been at a Senior prom somewhere.. since she looks so much younger than she really is and all. Meanwhile, B.S. is already decked out in his wrestling gear. Luckily for all of PCW minus Asylum, the sledgehammer seems absent. KIRA: Wowww.. this is all so exciting! All these reporters, all these fans. I don't know why that teenager wanted my autograph though. Isn't that wierd?? STORMBRINGER: Nah. You're in my promos, so people see you. You're freggin' hot, especially tonight, so lil' teeny-boppers want something to stare at between the matches. Even if it's just your signature. Sick little heathens.. KIRA: I'm hot..? STORMBRINGER: Um, yeah?? Always. KIRA: T-thank you, babe... Kira's voice trails off after she stammers. The redheaded cutie looks away and bites her bottom lip, smiling at B.S.'s casual, but still kind and caring words. CARL: So sir. What tasks do you want me to take care of tonight? STORMBRINGER: Namely just make sure no one comes in here and steals our sh!t. CARL: Oh, eh... alright, sir. STORMBRINGER: For right now.. could you go get us some sodas? Yourself somethin' too, of course. You got the change? CARL: Yessir. STORMBRINGER: Good. I'll pay for it later. Off ya go. Carl leaves, almost never being offended for being dismissed so hastily. Being relieved of one duty for another so qucikly like that is just part of the job description. Carl shuts the door behind him and Kira shimmies herself just a bit closer. B.S. doesn't hesitate to put an arm around his 'wonderful friend's' shoulders. KIRA: You really aren't thirsty, are you? Hehe. STORMBRINGER: Nnnope. Heh. Just wanted some alone time before I gotta go see the rest of the stable guys, get in 'Game Face' mode and all that. KIRA: I understand. That Fusion card come out yet? STORMBRINGER: Yeah, finally. I get to team with Darkstar for the first time. You know, the Goth guy? KIRA: Oh yeah! Well that's cool, you always talk about how much you respect him. STORMBRINGER: Yep. Good as he is, he's underrated. We face Politically Incorrect.. including that guy who hit me with a key. KIRA: What guy- STORMBRINGER: Oh!! I uhh.. must not of told you about that match. Look, I gotta get going.. another thing I was wondering.. KIRA: ..Yes? STORMBRINGER: How about, um. How about a kiss good luck..? KIRA: I thought you'd never ask, m' love. Even though Bobby was expecting just a peck, Kira turns sideways to lean against his chest, and she kisses him passionattely. Of course, Bobby returns the affection! Duh! So as they wrap their arms around each other they finally smooch. About damn time. So er, in ase this gets 'out of hand' let's just get on out of here. Deadline in four minuts or something ridiculous like that anyway. Good luck to everybody at March Madness! Good night! Ha, I feel like Johnny Carson or something... THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION.
