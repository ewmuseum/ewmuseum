MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS-Lose Yourself In The Madness....RAH DISS 29 OTHER MEN...LYRICALLY!

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS-Lose Yourself In The Madness....RAH DISS 29 OTHER MEN...LYRICALLY! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-16-2003 ..::/::..The sound of a record slowly beginning to play overtakes the scene, piano slowly filling in as the light starts to come up to full, where we find none other then Rent-A-Hero, bouncing up and down like a boxer preparing for a big fight. He is within an empty arena, inside a dormant PCW ring as the piano plays ever harder….a rap beat and rough strums of a guitar slowly fading in as the camera focuses in on our hero’s face, adorned in a Green Lantern mask with a linen skully cap on. He looks up from the mat, into the camera as “Lose Yourself…In The RP by Rent-A-Hero” appears in white letters at the bottom of the screen..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Look…..if you had…one shot….one oppourtunity….to BIZNITCH SLAP O’DOOM 29 other wrestlers for the World title…in one moment…would you capture it…or let it slip?” ..::/::..The beat kicks in hard, a drove of faceless wrestlers racing down the steel ramp leading to the ring our hero is in as they start surrounding it, our hero cautiously holding his ground as he looks about at the opposition. He then starts rapping to the beat, constantly looking about at his would-be assailants..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”His palms are sweaty, Knees weak arms, are heavy There’s vomit on his spandex already,Arms are spaghettiAnd at the pay-per-view…he’s ready to smack villains, Serving justice live in front of millions, He’s got 29 fiends to fight….from Kannon to Tracer, Ready to rip em’ all like a sheet of paper, One by one, over the rope they gotta go, He’s only got one shot to be the champ…and he’s ready to go…Chorus:”YOU BETTER LOSE YOURSELF…. IN THE ROLEPLAY, THE SPANDEX, THE MADNESS, YOU BETTER NOT GO OVER THE ROPE,YOU ONLY GET ONE SHOT, DO NOT MISS YOUR CHANCE TO GO, THIS OPPOURTUNITY COMES ONCE IN A LIFETIME…WANNA WIN, YA BETTER HOPE!”..::/::..All the men rush the ring, our hero trying to fight them off with furious punches and kicks, but getting beaten in the process by the faceless mob. They beat him mercilessly, backing him to the ropes as they tie him between the top and middle rope. His face is being pummeled by punches and kicks as he continues rapping with total emotion..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Gotta toss the Schoompies, their way too mushy for my mind, Then I end it for Skyla Green, he can’t see himself go over cause he’s blind, Gonna beatdown on Darkstar and make him feel the pain, Then I’m tossing Dorn Tracer…YOU SUCK FOR BREAKING MY RISING STAR TITLE REIGN, Gonna beat on Walden till he starts to whine and complain, Send him flying over the top like a supersonic airplane,Then I BIZNITCH SLAP O’ DOOM Duke, show him who’s the boss,Once he goes out, he’s like “WHY YA DO THAT, HOSS?!, Taking out that skinhead Makonie, you can put money on that bet,I’ll have him screaming like Ivan when he goes over ”NYET NYET NYET!!!” Chorus:”YOU BETTER LOSE YOURSELF…. IN THE ROLEPLAY, THE SPANDEX, THE MADNESS, YOU BETTER NOT GO OVER THE ROPE,YOU ONLY GET ONE SHOT, DO NOT MISS YOUR CHANCE TO GO, THIS OPPOURTUNITY COMES ONCE IN A LIFETIME…WANNA WIN, YA BETTER HOPE!” ..::/::..Our hero frees his arms, striking back with vicious kicks and punches as he amps up his offense on the faceless competitors. He turns one about quickly, DDT’ing him with authority to the mat as he pops back up and delivers a picture perfect hurracanranna to another would be attacker. Defensively, he starts to back up to a corner, his attackers slowly stalking him with intent to destroy..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”There’s no escaping, Skitzo won’t get the chance to try any dildo raping, I’ll send him over 11 times…one for him, and 10 more for the other persona’s he’s harboring,Clyde’s a bad mofo, they call him the “Burned Man”, When he goes over the rope, they’ll be calling him the “Tossed Man”, Pendragon is a knight, a noble hero amongst the 30 I’m battlin’, But I’ll toss him, Lancelot, Percivel, and that old guy Merlin, Kannon’s a weasel, a real challenge to put out for me, But when he gets tossed, I sure hope he lands on ELLIE~!”Chorus:”YOU BETTER LOSE YOURSELF…. IN THE ROLEPLAY, THE SPANDEX, THE MADNESS, YOU BETTER NOT GO OVER THE ROPE,YOU ONLY GET ONE SHOT, DO NOT MISS YOUR CHANCE TO GO, THIS OPPOURTUNITY COMES ONCE IN A LIFETIME…WANNA WIN, YA BETTER HOPE!” ..::/::..Our hero’s attackers dog-pile on him in the corner, stomping and beating him without any sign of mercy. Suddenly…our hero pulls from some kind of hidden strength, the beat growing harder as he tosses all the men off of him in one fell swoop. A newfound energy has overcome him as he pummels any man he can get his hands on, tossing them over the top rope of the ring with all his might as his rhymes grow ever more intense..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Nerezza, Gaz Green, Pharoah, and Dante Jackson, Are receiving an all-expense paid trip OVER THE TOPE, better start packin’,I’ve got a title to win…I can’t chat with Chet King, But we can talk later after I send ya out the ring,El Romantico, once I beat you down, change your name to El Romanti-HO, You might be a poet, but you can’t keep up with THIS Rent-A-Flow, Totally Tubular Tommy’s flying like an explosive rocket projectile, After I toss you, you’ll be like “Déjà vu…didn’t you just beat me for the Continental Title?”Chorus:”YOU BETTER LOSE YOURSELF…. IN THE ROLEPLAY, THE SPANDEX, THE MADNESS, YOU BETTER NOT GO OVER THE ROPE,YOU ONLY GET ONE SHOT, DO NOT MISS YOUR CHANCE TO GO, THIS OPPOURTUNITY COMES ONCE IN A LIFETIME…WANNA WIN, YA BETTER HOPE!” ..::/::..The ring thins faster and faster, man after man being tossed over the top as our hero grows ever more intense with each elimination. His opponents fly over the top with authority, slamming against the ground as the camera shakes to reflect the impact. Finally…all his opponents are gone, Rent-A-Hero on his knees as the PCW World Heavyweight title appears in his hands, a tear falling from his eye as the beat slows….his emotion pouring through with the last few lines..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”I’m tired of choking, tired of the waiting,Tired of the biznitches with their Rent-A-Hero hating, Now is my time, time to prove I’m the best in the game, When people talk about Stanislav, Nelson, Howard, Kriegman, Steele, Dragon…I want people to say MY name,March 18TH…..the Madness comes my way, And when it’s said and done, THE HERO will be victorious that day, Bring your best PCW….give me all you got…I beg you, I’M DEMANDING! Thought you could break me….erase me….SCREW you….the hero is still standing… Bring your best for the pay-per-view if you want to defeat me, Now how about you guys guess what was in character and what was OOC? Chorus:”YOU BETTER LOSE YOURSELF…. IN THE ROLEPLAY, THE SPANDEX, THE MADNESS, YOU BETTER NOT GO OVER THE ROPE,YOU ONLY GET ONE SHOT, DO NOT MISS YOUR CHANCE TO GO, THIS OPPOURTUNITY COMES ONCE IN A LIFETIME…WANNA WIN, YA BETTER HOPE…..
