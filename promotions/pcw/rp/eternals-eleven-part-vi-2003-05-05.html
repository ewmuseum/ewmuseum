::~Eternals Eleven~:: (Part VI)

5 May 2003

Date: 05/05/03 Prelude: Time is one of the most precious things known to man, but it always seems to be taken for granted, that is until you have a deadline on your hands. Shane Wethers was locked up in the county jail on Friday after being caught in the mafia hide-out and after being released the following day he has lost out on valuable recruitment time. Shane now has the mission of finding six more members to fill the gaps of his security team. It started of when they signed ‘The Screamer” Burrows to take a place in the team and most recently they got the services of Shane’s cousin Huey Lucio and his three associates to help out. With the addition of Shane’s agent ‘Mikey Fishmore’, it brings the numbers up to five. But still there is a long way to go before they reach the required number of eleven guards to watch Shane’s back at the E-Fest Concert. Each week new members are revealed and things are starting to get very interesting. Now Shane looks to make the latest additions to his team, but who’d have thought he’d choose this person after the passed problems between them. I’ll say no more… lets find out! The scene opens on a cold autumn afternoon in the suburbs of New Jersey, the home of Bon Jovi as some people refer to it as. It’s a quiet part of town, down a long winding road surrounded by small canyon-type mountains that provide a nice view in front of the sunset and the derelict landscape that surrounds the half empty part of town. An eagle can be seen soaring through the skies and gliding in a spectacular way. In the Area is a small county jail that is surrounded by large Fencing and barbed wire. There’s a lot of high-tech equipment monitoring the security of the area such as CCTV, sensors, audio’s and various devices. There are two cards stood in there black uniform at the gates of the jail, giving gate access to incoming and out coming police vehicles full of prisoners. The police open the gates once more for someone who’s faint shadow can be seen. As the camera comes into focus you can tell that its Silus Wethers, Shane’s devious, yet ridiculously stupid brother. Silus is dressed in a Navy Blue ‘Daniel Hetcher’ suit, a white shirt, a yellow tie and black ‘Clarks’ Shoes. His light brown hair is slickly brushed into a manageable style and he has had a clean cut shave, unusually smart judging from Silus’ previous appearance. He stands at the gates as the officers open them, he reaches into the inside left pocket of his suit jacket with a smug look on his face, he pulls out a cigar and drops it as he takes it out. His hands go like jelly as he tries to stop the cigar from falling on the floor, whilst the Guards watch on at his comical mistakes. He bends down to pick up the cigar, he stands up straight as if nothing happened and battles the embarrassment. He puts hi thumb up at the guards as he turns and backs out of the gates, getting the hooks of his briefcase caught in the gate rail, he tries to pull it loose but his briefcase opens and papers fly out all over the area. The guards continue to watch on in disbelief at this new age ‘Charlie Chaplin’. Comedy isn’t a common thing around a jail so when a clown like Silus arrives, you know things are gonna turn all ‘Comedy Central’. Silus loses his balance and falls forward, creating a huge ripping sound. The sound comes from a rip created in his suit trousers. He walks away with a highly embarrassed look on his face as the guards close the gates in a fit of laughter. He reaches into his left inside pocket once again with his right hand, he pulls out a black cellular phone. He opens the flap on the phone and dials a number and waits for the ring tone. It rings and someone answers “Hi…yeah its me, I’m ready to talk, come and meet me”.. I’m outside the jail. How far off are you?…” “two minutes?” Silus confirms, “Alright. Hurry up cos I’m feeling a bit…er…breezy. Ok..see ya!” he shuts the flap on his phone and sighs. He stands, looking at the empty length of road that lays before him, his first taste of freedom taste sweet. He puts the cigar in his mouth and pulls a silver lighter out of his left trouser pocket. He flips open the cap and the Mikey runs high, almost singeing his eyebrow. He drops the cigar and lighter, holding on to his face as the lit cigar falls into his outside left jacket pocket. He caresses his face, making sure there are no burn wounds on his face. He sighs in relief and laughs lightly to himself as he breaths in, in a relief. As he does so he frowns and begins to sniff the air as if there was some strange scent coming from somewhere near. He looks down at his jacket to find it’s on fire from the pocket. He realises and he begins to run around in circles like a lost puppy… he squeals and dives on the stony ground, rolling about trying to put out the Mikey. He finally succeeds as he looks down at his pocket to find nothing but a burnt hole. He stands up and brushes himself down as before, he stares down at the pocket again, he sighs and takes of his jacket, throwing it on the floor in a fit of rage. He begins to kick at his jacked, as he does so, a black limo with blacked-out windows comes around the corner and stops on the side of the road next to where Silus is stood. He leans forward to the window of the drivers seat as he winds down. Sat in the front seat is the limo driver. He opens the door and it the frame of it, hits Silus in the nose. The Chauffer steps out and looks apologetic. The Chauffer is also wearing a black suit, a white shirt and a black Chauffeur’s “Oh I am so sorry sir” the chaffeur apologises in a not-so sympathetic tone “can I get you some ice for that wound?” The Chauffer walks toward Silus handing him a handkerchief, but Silus pushes it away “Get away from me you insane little nerd.” Silus detests, “I don’t need your help… now where is he?” The Chauffer points to the back of the car and Silus snatches the hankercheif from him, as he walks toward the passenger side of the car at the back of the limousine. He gives the driver a spiteful look as he continues to walk towards the car. He opens the side door at the back of the limo, to see Shane Wethers sLucio sat wearing a white suit, a blue shirt and a yellow tie. Huey smokes on a huge Cuban cigar, as his associates watch on. One of them is big and looks well built with a crew cut hid below his hat, the other is thin but broad and looks like a typical Dean Martin. They are wearing black and white suits and a hat. Silus gets in the car and closes the door. “Huey, your looking great..and…” Silus begins, Silus looks at Huey’s associates “ …and you guys have reminded me that there’s a dominos night on at the Albion tonight.” He adds, receiving at not so pleased expression from the group. The thug-like associate sits forward ready to hit Silus but Huey raises his hand at him and orders him to simmer down as he turns to face Silus. Huey faces comes into view, he has a typical unshaven stubble-look and a typical relaxed expression on his face “Silus,” Huey sighs “you called me out here cos we need you to help out with Shane’s concert, so either you get to it or I will blow your brains all over this nice interior I have, and I don’t wanna do that but if you push me I won’t hesitate to do so, whether you’re my cousin or not.” He states in an impatient tone of voice. Silus swallows hard and tries to hide his intimidation “Huey…” Silus says in a trembled voice, “do you honestly think I’d mess you around? Without releasing any verbal reply, Huey exposes the inside of his jacket and points to his gun holster, he nods at Silus “Ok damnit!” Silus yells “I thought you people represented family respect! Seems Shane doesn’t know anything about respect, after all he put me in the slammer!” Huey sighs and sits back “Don’t start bitchin’ again Silus.” Huey says in a calm tone, “Your brother may have beaten you into an oblivion but us Dons have a new technique pain, it’s called ‘castration’.” Silus frowns and moves further away from Huey “But think about it!” Silus states in an angry manner, “He always took the lime light when we were young, I was nothing to our father. So I aimed to help this family by getting rid of that virus known as Shane” Huey laughs to himself as he takes of his hat revealing his brushed back dark brown hair. He plays with the rim of his hat “Is that so?” Huey asks sarcastically. Silus nods his head “Listen you dumb schmuck,” Huey says in a deep growling voice “you better have not got me all the way here just to tell me that you won’t help!” Silus stands his ground “It depends what’s in it for me!” he shouts. Huey’s grin fades as he looks at Silus, dead in the eye “Yo’ pretty brave for a washed-up old loser,” Huey states. “so why don’t you save me from breaking yo’ bones and tell us. Think about it, it’ll save you the pain and me the effort.” Silus shakes his head “Like I said…” he replies “It depends what’s in it for me!” Silus and his two associates whip their guns out of the holster and point it at Silus, with bitter looks upon their faces. Huey begins to raise his voice “Don’t mess with me you little sh#t!” Huey yells with an ongoing look of frustration and impatience. “I don’t have time to be messin’ wit’ pathetic little pukes like yourself. Now tell me before I blow your brains into the middle of next week!” Silus continues to stand his ground “Go ahead! Go on! Shoot me!” Silus dares him “Then you’ll never have me helping out! So go ahead!” The guys hold there positions as the intensity fades (To be continued in Part VII)
