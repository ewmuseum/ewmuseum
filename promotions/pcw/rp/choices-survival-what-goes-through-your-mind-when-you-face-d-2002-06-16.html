Choices & Survival.....What Goes Through Your Mind When You Face Darkstar...

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-16-2002 It seems like there is ALWAYS a choice in life with you... You can't be both or make a compromise no matter what you do, it's always... One or the other...Rent-A-Hero or Rent-A-Villain.... Being normal or being superhuman... Loving someone intensely or driving yourself insane alone... Never a compromise for you and you can expect no different tonight.... You lace up your boots, the roar of the crowd faintly falling upon your ears as you realize that only an hour from now.... You will be staring down the man that will make or break your career... And just like EVERYTHING else in your life.... This match happens to have no compromise.....just choices.....choices that could effect you forever... 'Forever' is a pretty strong word eh? Well in this case, it is the most 'delicate' word to describe the effects of your choice after the match.... So what are your choices might you ask? Well......you could run...That seems like the smart idea. Run from the monster. Hide from death. Avoid destiny. Even the hardest of men have made attempts to avoid having to face Darkstar. What makes Darkstar so dangerous actually is that he LACKS something.... In wrestling, in life even, we often look for what people have to offer and gauge them by that...not by what they lack.... Darkstar lacks remorse for his actions...In the correct soul, that is the most dangerous quality. It's amazing to stare down a man who will grind your bones into a fine powder, sprinkle it on his Corn Flakes, and call it a day. It's amazing to know you will face a man who destroyed a rising star....Jay Sylo. Sylo had what it took to become something in this company and on a simple whim..... Darkstar and his band of misfits snatched his future with no second look... For Sylo, that was the most painful experience of his life and it would take time to recover from the mental and physical torture that was inflicted upon him.... For Darkstar... It was just another Tuesday. Lacing your boots, you figure running is a BAD idea....it's bad enough that Darkstar thinks he's the agreesor even when you stand up to him.... He just likes it more when you run. So you figure, stand up to him.... Get in his face... Stare down into the eyes of the lost soul and let him know that you don't fear him...that you want him in the WORST way possible. Let him know that YOU won't hold back.... And then you figure, that won't work either. As you just mentioned, Darkstar is a complicated monster.... He's not the 'textbook monster' you stake in the heart, or douse in holy water... He's complex because he enjoys toying with human emotions.... If you run, he just chases until you piss yourself to sleep.If you stand up to him, all he does is feed off the fact that you had the guts to stand up to him.... Of course that's BEFORE he rips your guts out. I mean, why would he want you to keep them? He's been successful because he knows how to break an opponent in the ring....without even raising a fist to his prey. So you figure you have ONE last choice....This choice is the one you NEVER wished to resort to, but if you want any chance of coming back to your locker room 10 pounds heavier and with the accolade that you slayed the demon....you have to take it. There may be no return from what your preparing to do....But to make an impact, you have GOT to go to extremes. So what's your last choice? Give him what he wants... Give into the slight hints he's been dropping... Fight like him...Think like him... Feed off your hatred.... Feed off of getting ditched by that redheaded slut, Amy... Feed off of Snow's torment.... Feed off of everyone saying you don't have what it takes to break to the top of this game... Feed off of all the kids ins chool who called you 'freak'... FEED OFF OF THIS BIG BALL OF HATE YOU'VE BEEN HOLDING FOR SO LONG.... You think your EVER gonna beat Darkstar in a technical masterpiece? Think again... You better use every trick in the 'Hardcore Fighting For Dummies' handbook and a few he dosen't know if you EVER expect to do the one thing nobody expects you to do... Survive. Surival and choices have been your life....if your not making choices that never seem fufilling... Your always trying to survive until the NEXT big roadblock that you either have to climb or break down. Darkstar is the equivalent of the Berlin Wall... But even that wall fell down...So your gonna low blow him, make him bleed, kick him while he's down, choke him with the ropes, rub your forearms across his face, spit on him, hit him when he's not looking.... And that's just the first five minutes of the match.... Your coming into this match scared, there's no sugar coating this fact..... The butterflies have been rumbling EVER SINCE you heard from word of mouth you'd be taking the goth monster.... But when the bells rings and those fans are breathing life into your veins.... He better be the one that's scared....You've made a reputation on beatings odds and you've pissed off a lot of betting men who have chosen to take the 'safe bet'. The bets get no safer than Darkstar.... But you have a feeling when it's all said and done... There will be three absolute certanties... One, the match will NEVER be a wrestling masterpiece the likes of Angle/Benoit...Two, You and Darkstar will be changed forever by this destined bout, good or bad remains to be seen... Three, there will be a group of men at the local sports bar PISSED that 'that masked a**hole' beat the odds again....Wow...I sound like someone I used to know Rent-A-Hero I believe his name was?Eh, forget names....your not about to go one on one witht he Super Sadist without having a Cherry Coke first... No sense being on the brink of death and standing right there at Heaven's Gate, staring down his Lordship in the eye and asking... 'Is there a water fountain around here?'
