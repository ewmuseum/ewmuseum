Ransom details for the Taco Bell dog released~!

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/3/02 When we last left off, Flaymin was in the midst of kidnapping El Diablo’s soul mate- The Taco Bell Dog. Did Flaymin succeed in the dognapping operation?How on earth would he have crossed the Mexican border with the dog still in his possession?Where is Flaymin now?What, if any, are Flaymin’s ransom demands for the world-renowned dog? [Scene opens in a small dark room the size of a toolshed. Not much is in the room.A table, two chairs and a floodlight is about it.No windows, no ceiling chandelier….. no pinball machine. Suddenly footsteps can be heard in the room. Moments later a powerful floodlight brightens up the entire room.Standing in the centre of the room, squinting and covering his face with his hands is none other then Flaymin Aymin. The floodlight showers Flaymin with its bright penetrating light] Flaymin: Dammit Jenna, the floodlight is too bright.Turn it down! [Jenna fumbles around with the floodlight and eventually manages to turn down the light to a manageable state. With the floodlight still focusing firmly on the centre of the room, Flaymin signals Jenna to keep the light exactly as it is. He then reaches over and picks up a cage.He opens the cage door and pulls out the infamous and not to mention, stolen Taco Bell Chihuahua] Flaymin: Jenna, is the camera ready? Jenna: Hold on a sec. [Jenna presses a few buttons on the video camera and waits for a moment as a buzzing sound is heard from the camera] Flaymin: What are you doing? Jenna: Rewinding the tape.I’m going to have to tape over our homemade porno. Flaymin: What?No, don’t do that! Jenna: Don’t worry, we’ll just film another one later. [Flaymin shakes his head in disappointment, saddened by the fact that his homemade porno is being erased. A minute later Jenna raises her hand and extends her thumb in a vertical motion. Flaymin nods his head.He then raises the Taco Bell dog and nestles it in his hands, in front of his chest] Jenna: Alright, it’s ready to go. Flaymin: Gotcha. [Flaymin suddenly puts on a mean, deep accent- sounding like a cross between Darth Vader and The Crocodile Hunter….. go figure.] Flaymin: Hey how’s it going?Uh…. I mean, Greetings fellow Taco Bell fanatics. Right here before me, in my arms, nestled in front of my chest, in my very hands… Jenna: Alright, we get the point!! Flaymin: Yeah, um… anyways this feels kinda strange because I’ve never kidnapped a dog before and asked for ransom.But hey there’s a first for everything, right? Jenna: Save the life story for Oprah and just get to the point. Flaymin: Right. Here is the famous Taco Bell dog.Kidnapped yesterday afternoon from the Taco Bell Head Office in Acapulco, Mexico-Which may I say is a fine Tourists Destination. Why did I kidnap the dog?Simple really. El Diablo sacrificed my hair, so I kidnap his closest companion- this prissy, stuck up little mutt. It looks like a toy and God knows I’ve kicked it around like a football for the last 24 hours. Diablo, I know how much this Taco Bell dog means to you.It’s your source of pride.Your source of power.Your source of self-gratification.And not to mention your source of free Taco’s…. What does this mean for you? Well firstly you’re going to have to pay for your tacos and burritos.And with your cheapskate rating already phenomenally high, I know that in itself will be a burden on your cheap, jobberiffic ass. But being the kind and gentle natured man that I am, I’ll throw you a lifeline.A ‘Get out of Jail Free’ card so to speak. [Jenna searches through her pockets for a moment] Jenna: Uh Aymin, I didn’t bring any ‘Get out of Jail Free’ cards. [Flaymin slaps his forehead in frustration] Flaymin: Woman, It’s a figure of speech.Get with the program! Jenna: We have a program? [Flaymin sighs.He then runs his right hand over his mouth in a ‘Zip your lips’ motion] Flaymin: Diablo, if you ever want to see this dog again, you must comply with a few ransom demands I have set. [Flaymin pulls out a piece of paper from his pocket. The paper flips open and continues to flutter all the way down to the ground.Eventually, what was initially a small piece of paper turns out to be a metre-long ransom document that stretches down to the floor] Flaymin: This is ‘The List’. Ransom demand number one reads:’45 minute phone call to Foxy Roxy and Super-loose Suzanna: $325.00’ Oh shit…. Wrong bill. Forgive me for the mistake, these days it’s getting harder to tell apart Phone-sex bills from a Ransom note. [Flaymin tosses the phone-sex bill on the floor and reaches back into his pocket and pulls out an even longer document- this one twice as long as the original paper] Flaymin: Here we go. No wait…. This is just the receipt for a shopping spree at ‘Dildos R Us’. Jenna, do you have the ransom note? [Jenna shrugs her shoulders and shakes her head] Flaymin: Well apparently I have misplaced my ransom note. So as a change of plans I’ll have to unreal my ransom demands off the top of my head. And remember El Diablo, you must comply with our every demand if you want to see this stupid dog liberated. Demand Number One:Um…. You are to sacrifice your girlfriend, Angel…. To the Porno-gods. [Jenna joyfully jumps up and down with the camera still in her hand] Flaymin: Jenna and I require the services of your woman for ‘Project Threesome’.Once we have ravaged her body, you will be free to re-collect her…. Not that she’d want to go back to your two-inch tumour you call a donger. Demand Number Two:We demand $10 million dollars to be placed in the phone booth at 6pm tomorrow. Jenna: What’s the point of that?? Flaymin: I don’t know.Isn’t that what all the super-cool villains do nowadays? Jenna: I don’t care, I just wanna get freaky with Angel! Flaymin: Alright, scrap demand number two. The New and Improved Demand Number Two:We command that you to tear up your PCW contract and be banished from wrestling forever. Jenna: Don’t do that!El Diablo matches are the only time any of the fans get a chance to sleep at wrestling events.Why don’t you try again? Flaymin: I don’t know what to say.I just want his woman. Jenna: Well then lets keep it at that. Flaymin: Ok, then it’s settled.El Diablo, you are to forfeit your girlfriend in exchange for the Taco Bell dog. A bitch for a bitch! And if you ever get horny, I’m sure the dog will open wide and let you rifle up its backside…. All you have to do is ask nicely. [The dog looks at Flaymin and yelps in fright] Flaymin: El Diablo, the ball is now in your court.If you ever want to see The Taco Bell dog ever again you must comply with our every order, which shouldn’t be so hard since there only is one. Ok Jenna, stop recording.We need to send this ransom video to Diablo as soon as possible. [Jenna looks at the camera and then looks back up at Flaymin with a confused look on her face] Jenna: Stop recording? Flaymin: Yeah, you know the button with a red little circle on it and the initials ‘REC’ printed above it. Jenna: That’s strange.I pressed the button with a little triangle on it with the initials ‘PLAY’. Flaymin: Why did you do that????You f*cked up the whole ransom tape!!I mean, do you know hard it is to get into character for a scene like that? Jenna: I pressed ‘Play’ because I thought the camera would then ‘play’ what I was filming. Flaymin: But you didn’t film anything! Jenna: You never asked me to record anything so don’t chuck a hissy-fit. And besides, don’t you think that surveillance camera would have recorded everything. [Flaymin looks at the surveillance camera in the top corner of the room and then nods his head] Flaymin: True.I guess that not all hope is lost. [Suddenly there is a knock on the door. The door swings open and in walk two policemen] Policeman: Have you idiots finished using the interrogation room yet?We have other aspiring criminals who wish to record ransom videos. [Jenna puts down the camera as the two policemen handcuff Flaymin and Jenna before leading them out of the Police Interrogation room and back to their jail cells] The End~!
