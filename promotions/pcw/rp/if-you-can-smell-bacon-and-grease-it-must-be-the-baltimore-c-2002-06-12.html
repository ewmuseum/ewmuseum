If You Can Smell Bacon and Grease, It Must Be the Baltimore County Police.

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th June 2002 It’s an amazing view. Ok, so it’s not “Grand Canyon Amazing”, but it’s nice. Not exactly “Green Fields of Ireland Stunning” either... but it beats the “Concrete Jungle Blues” even if it is in fact a concrete jungle itself... it’s amazing how nice a harbour looks when there’s skyscrapers all around it. You stick a harbour in a village and it’s just a harbour with dirty fishermen surrounding it. But you throw some skyscrapers behind it and have a nice hot day, and all of a sudden it’s transformed into one of the most pleasing sights to see. Mainly because if it wasn’t for the cool breeze off the harbour, it would probably be a good ten degrees hotter wherever you’re standing. Miyagawa is enjoying the bright sun the day before Turmoil at Baltimore harbour. He’s standing at one of those Tourist booths next to the harbour with a large map of the town, and his sunglasses adorning his face. He’s shunned his regular long black jacket because of the sun and is instead wearing just a pair of jeans and a loose fitting USA soccer shirt from four years ago (the old Stars and Stripes design). He leans over at the counter towards the young student intern who looks rather like the PFIK’s twin... so it must be the Pimply Faced Intern Guide. Miyagawa: I’ve got the day off, so what would you recommend that I go and see in Baltimore... I’ve never really had any spare time in this city even though I’ve been here a few times... on business. The PFIG looks nervous as if it was his first day on the job... but you know these interns, they just don’t hold up under the pressure of such grilling questions as “Which way to the zoo?” or “Do I need my passport to get pass the security gates at the museum?”. You get the picture. PFIG: Erm...well... there’s the B and O Railroad Museum on W. Pratt Street. That’s meant to be quite good. Miyagawa: BO? It’s the Bo Raider Railroad Museum? Is that what you’re saying? PFIG: Erm... no... not really... what’s a Bo Raider? I know a Tomb Raider... in fact I have a picture in my wallet of Lara Croft completely na... Miyagawa: Anything else? Something non-museumy... you know, something interesting, preferably out in the fresh air? PFIG: You could take a boat trip, I sell tickets. Miyagawa: What do I get to see on the boat trip? The PFIG looks confused at the constant questions. It’s like Cal Seaver, but on a good day. PFIG: Erm... um... err... other boats? Miyagawa raises an eyebrow in surprise at his answer. Miyagawa: Ah... well... I suppose they can’t build a statue of liberty in every harbour, it would just get old. PFIG: Do you like religion? Miyagawa shrugs non-chaletly. Miyagawa: Depends on the religion I guess. PFIG: Well we have a Jewish Museum here in Baltimore. Miyagawa: Never been one for any of Chris Chase’s dodgy arts. If it’s his love for Corey William’s backside, or not being able to work on the weekends, well, I’ve never been one for it. The PFIG once again looks completely confused and bewildered. No change there then. PFIG: Who? What... er... where? When? Come again? Sorry? Miyagawa: Never mind, it wasn’t important really. Miyagawa: What about somewhere to eat? Can you recommend me somewhere for that, at least? The PFIG with enthusiasm... what can I say? At least students know the cheap restaurants. PFIG: What type of food would you like to try? Miyagawa: What do I get to choose from? PFIG: Well... we have Afghan, American, Asian Fusion... Miyagawa: Stop right there? Afghan? PFIG: We have an Afghan restaurant in Baltimore, yes. It’s been voted top restaurant in Baltimore just recently by a couple of the local papers. Miyagawa: Ok... but I don’t want my taste buds to be exploding. **Silence** Miyagawa shrugs with disappointment. Miyagawa: It was a joke, lighten up a little. What have you got for me in Asian Fusion? That sounds rather like my sort of style. The PFIG points over towards a hotel. PFIG: The place is only just over there in that hotel, it’s called the “Eurasian Harbour”. I’ve eaten there a few times, it’s quite nice. Miyagawa: Thank you kindly... just one more thing. Don’t suppose you know the Korea versus the USA score from the World Cup do you? The PFIG once again looks rather confused. PFIG: What’s the world cup? Miyagawa shakes his head in annoyance and partially a lack of surprise. Miyagawa: Never mind. Miyagawa sniffs the air for a second. Miyagawa: What’s that smell? PFIG: What’s it smell like? Miyagawa sniffs the air again, a little more carefully this time. Miyagawa: Bacon... grease... yeah, it smells like bacon and grease. PFIG: Must be the Baltimore County Police.
