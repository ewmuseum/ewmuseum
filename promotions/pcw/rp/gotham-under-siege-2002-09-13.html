Gotham Under-Siege

13 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13 - 09 - 02 A grim night hung over the infamous city of Gotham, home of the cape crusader 'Batman' and those who threatened its tranquil setting. Behind its deceptive facade of a peaceful backdrop lurked a mass of super villains all plotting the City's takeover, hidden in the shadows until a day loomed where they may finally hatch a full-proof plan. We pick-up this tutorial of Gotham outside, what appears to be an abandoned warehouse. Dirt ridden walls, decaying and shattered glass stretched across what was once a car park, most likely the windows had been vandalised by bored youths with little else better to do. This part of town lay home to the industrial age with hoards of factories churning out smoke into the dead of night at least showing that the area was still active but the bowels of this complex is where the real truth lay buried. Slowly creeping down a flight of winding stairs, murmurs filtered over the dense silence, yet to be recognised the sound grows in volume with every step taken. Almost at the heart of the source the words begin to take form and a conversation can be made out between a party of people. 'There has been an Uprising....' roared one individual, his voice almost distinctive. 'YES! A plague of Ja Rule following fans that call themselves The Uprising!!!' hissed another. 'And they aren't our only concern, Anar... I mean Cat Woman is now at the mercy of another villain?' yelled a third and final person with a tone of authority. The camera finally fixates on three of Gotham's most renowned super villains with a unique difference. Standing in a jovial manner was Gaz Green adorning a suit with a wayward colour scheme and most notably face paint he obviously masquerades as Batman's archenemy The Joker. His comrade and fellow Pranks With Attitude member wears a heavy metallic suit, head enclosed beneath a dome glass structure, he portrays the brains of the operation Mr. Freeze whilst Alexander Miyagawa paces back and forth in a lime green business tuxedo with question mark insignia's randomly placed, the Asylum leader opts to play The Riddler. 'Another villain? For crying out loud the man is a God....' bellowed the Lord Of Laughter grinding his teeth. 'And a Neo Void God at that. I mean we nailed him with everything we had and didn't once faze him.... Chair shots he simply brushed off, Therapy had no effect, how do you suppose we combat something indestructible?!?' said Miyagawa, a riddle he had no immediate answers to. Nerezza.. I mean Mr. Freeze stood motionless as though his suit had ceased working but that wasn't the issue, the genius lay immersed in thought, trying to piece together every part of the puzzle. 'A little participation would be nice my little snowflake?' joked the Jester as he curled the corners of his mouth into a smile. The sinister professor ignores the pleads of input from his associates and continues to dwell in the far reaches of his mind. 'I knew we should've hired Danny Devito for a cameo role as the Penguin!' cackled the Joker substitute Gaz Green trying to spurn his colleagues who feel quite contempt to simply switch off. 'THAT'S IT!!!' cried the cold-blooded assassin. “.... Decided to come out of Hibernation then?' questioned the ever-inquisitive Riddler adjusting his bowler-hat. 'What? We should've hired Danny Devito, I was merely joking....' retorted the emerald menace before an untimely interruption on the part of Mr. Freeze. 'Let's look over the facts - We can't reverse the situation and kidnap Cyclonica because she plays the pawn to Akiyama and in all honesty I can't see him dwelling on the situation. We also can't humble him since he wears that indestructible armour that makes him a foe to be reckoned with but on Earth he still has the disadvantages us mortals contend with....' proclaimed Mr. Freeze rallying the troops. 'Yes we know he can be temporarily stopped but that took a car and his regenerative power was unbelievable!' said Miyagawa still in full portrayal of the Riddler. 'The key word is 'STOPPED' all we have to do is Incapacitate him and I should be able to muster something up capable of such a task~!' injected the frozen felon. 'Imagine if we could go one further and harness his power between the three of us, Gotham would crumble under our very own tyranny!' yelled the Green Lantern look-a-like. The evil Asylum members agreed by nodding that Akiyama's downfall would require technological wizardry only Mr. Freeze could piece together but the fact still remained that 'The Uprising' was another agenda that needed to be addressed. 'Now we have the resources to bring the Neo Void God to his knee's how do you suppose we tackle our next agenda involving two members of 'The Uprising' known as Playaz Inc?' asked the devious professor through his dry, cracked lips. 'Hmm....' sighed all three individuals in unison. The camera watched on with interest as Gotham's super villains again immersed into their own thoughts. Like de ja vu Mr. Freeze returned to his motionless state of thinking, the Joker pranced about with a yo-yo whilst the Riddler strode back and forth, head tilted at the ground. 'THAT'S IT!!!' roared the conniving comic, drawing attention to himself, his voice echoing off the dense concrete. 'What? What....' replied both Mr. Freeze and the Riddler, looking towards their business associate for answers. 'Hey, I'm kidding....' whispered Batman's nemesis, holding his hands in the air. Again the scene drew back into silence, awaiting for their prayers to be answered by a greater power (hopefully not a Neo Void God but beggar’s can't be choosers) but nobody could draw this episode to a conclusion until Gaz Green once again injected with an out-burst. 'THAT'S IT!!!' yelled the Prince Of Pranks, this time gaining no recognition from his co-workers. 'No guys I genuinely have an idea....' continued the PCW comedian with an evil glint in his eye, as both Nerezza and Miyagawa glare with keen interest. 'Where's Fusion this week?' 'Err... Isn't it in... Hubbardville!!!' groaned the master of confusion The Riddler. 'Exactly~! And the place will be packed to the rafters in Scientologists, which will work to our devious advantage, we shall kill two birds with one stone as they say' screamed the Joker with a sinister smile. The Joker Gaz Green then proceeded to whisper his plans in the ears of his two active listeners as they haggled together like a bunch of football players before the big kick-off. His words were taken in well as his fellow Asylum members rejoiced in smile's at what would taken place during Friday Night Fusion, the PCW's flag ship show where Pranks With Attitude would face Playaz Inc in a number one contenders match for the tag team throne. 'Very good my jovial friend....' grinned the behemoth Mr. Freeze as he towered above the rest of the three man party. 'HERE'S TOO OPERATION HUMILIATION~!' bellowed the super villains again in unison. Suddenly the cameraman ushered forward kicking an unnoticed wrench to the ground that ricochets off the surrounding metal, heard below by the Asylum trio who waste no time in checking the disturbance. 'WHAT THE F....' hissed the Riddler, gazing up to the rafters. 'We had better check this out?!?' ordered the Joker in a commanding voice. The cameraman took the idol threat as a remark that he had indeed over stayed his welcome and set hotfooted from the premises pursued by the evil of Gotham. -To Be Continued-
