The System

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/02 What’s a matter you look upset? What you didn’t honestly think that Metllyx was gonna win did you? You didn’t honestly think a tag team that beat Jobbers Inc. was gonna win? Damn, your ignorant! Of course with the likes of Big Daveylicious and Mike Frampton together as the Sensation of Annihilation will always serve up a free trip to the Emergency room. Unfortunately, for the likes of John Crichton & Andy Murray after Fusion they’ll be spending some time there too. If they think they’re not. They’re damn ignorant! But they’re not the only wittle ones with that problem. The PCW is blinded by ignorance. You don’t see it? You got Jason Core being ignorant thinking that he is actually a good wrestler. That he is actually thinks he is a true champion. That he is so ignorant he thinks he’s gonna go home after this Sunday with the gold. When truth beholds this is the same man that jobbed to Karl LeBlanc! Who’d he beat for the Extreme title? Corey Williams? Rent-A-Hero? Tom Walzack? No, instead of fighting somebody actually good he fought LeBlanc. But the difference this Sunday is that Jason Core is fighting somebody actually good in his life. Instead of fighting the poor putrid jobber of the month. (The scene fades to a muscular man standing at a doorway stunned. Behind him is an orderly. The man is sporting khaki pants, plaid shirt, and a ratty robe. We take recognize the man by his Bleached blonde hair, blue eyes, and black goatee. It’s Big Daveylicious. We’ve barely can recognize him without his Oakley sunglasses. No, reaction comes from Big D. Finally; we zoom back and notice what he’s stunned about. He’s in a mental hospitals day room. Light pours out from the heavily grilled windows to the room. Card tables, desks, fold up chairs are all set along the room. We hear cartoons noises from a TV they have set up. The patients are playing cards, games, and watching the TV or just minding their own business. All dressed in ratty robs or K-Mart street clothes. Suddenly, the orderly gives Big D shove.) Big Daveylicious: Get your petty hands off of me! Orderly: Move it, son. (Suddenly, a mentally ill patient in his late 40’s walks up to Big D and the orderly.) Orderly: Ah, Angus. This is Big Daveylicious. Why don’t you show him around? Tell him the TV rules, show him the games an' stuff, okay? Angus: How much you gonna pay me? Huh? I'd be doing your job. Orderly: I’ll get you pixy dust. Big Daveylicious: Pixy dust? Isn’t that from the Disney movie Peter Pan? Angus: Pixy dust it is my man! (The orderly walks away chuckling; Angus turns to Big D.) Big Daveylicious: Why do you want pixy dust? You know it’s just from a cartoon movie, right? Angus: That’s what the system wants you to believe. The system wants you to believe there is no Peter Pan. Big Daveylicious: There is a Peter Pan. It’s a second rate peanut butter company. Angus: No that’s what the system as also led you to believe. Were just the systems little puppets. Big Daveylicious: Who you callin’ little? You should keep that to yourself, Tinkerbell. Angus: Very well. I’m gonna give you the grand deluxe tour of the Mental Hospital. It’s no Neverland or Captain Hooks ship or even the Mermaids lagoon. But hey it’s home. (Big D rolls his eyes. Angus leads him to the card table where each patient is playing cards, checkers, chess, or playing a jigsaw puzzle. They stop over at where two old men play chess.) Angus: These are the games. Chess, checkers, cards, puzzles. We got’em all. It’s not an adventure with Peter Pan fighting off pirates and hanging out with the lost boys in those long lonely nights. Big Daveylicious: Where’s Monopoly or LIFE or Candy Land? I mean this is a mental hospital there is bound to be a kid’s game! Angus: You see the system doesn’t want that inner child of you to fly. I used to fly but the system put me in here. Big Daveylicious: Yeah, whatever, Tink. Angus: I have you know Tinkerbell is a very beautiful fairy. So if your implying I am I’m damn proud. Big Daveylicious: She’s a Disney cartoon character who opens up the shows with the Disney logo. Angus: But that’s what the system makes you believe. You see those old men playing chess. (They both take a look at the old men playing chess. One old man is grabs his Rook and pauses about the move.) Big Daveylicious: Yeah, what about them? Angus: What do you see? Big Daveylicious: I see two old men who are older than the Pharaoh and look like they need they haven’t been excited since opening a bottle of Viagara. Angus: Focus more into it. Big Daveylicious: They’re playing chess. Angus: No that’s what the system had made it look to you. When it really is two men controlling a battle of medieval kingdoms. The good fight the bad. When these kingdoms actually like each other but fight to they’re bloody deaths because of these two old men. But the system doesn’t want you to know what in fear of losing control of… Big Daveylicious: ZZZZZZZZZZ (Big D is standing in place with his eyes closed and his head tilted to his shoulder sleeping. Angus looks worried at the sleeping wrestler.) Angus: Wake up before the boogey man gets you! (As Angus yells Big D jumps, startled he falls on his butt. The old men jump in fear one of their hands upon jumping knocks the board on the ground with the chess pieces falling. One of the old men is taking out his frustrations on Angus.) Old Man: Dang nab it! I wasn’t gonna get him! I was just playing chess. That’s all but what happens? I retire from being the boogey man and everybody still thinks I’m out to get them. (The “boogey man” leaves walks away. Stomping his feet on the ground.) Big Daveylicious: Where am I? (He wipes his eyes as Angus stares at him.) Angus: In a mental hospital. Big Daveylicious: This is a place for crazy people. I’m not crazy! Angus: Very few of us here are actually mentally ill. I'm not saying you're not mentally ill, for all I know you're crazy as a loon. But that's not why you're here. Why you're here is because of the system. Big Daveylicious: I’m here because of my damn partner having multiple personalities. Angus: Ah, reminds me about me and the lost boys. Do you want another partner, you big sexy stud? Big Daveylicious: Not that kinda partner. I’m a singles and tag team wrestler, Tinkerbell. Angus: Damn. Ha! You’re no wrestler. Big Daveylicious: The hell I’m not! Angus: That’s what the system makes you think that. Big Daveylicious: Ugh. Angus: I’m not even talking to you. I don’t even exist. Neither do you. The only difference is. I’m a character that’ll last a day or two. You got the best role out of anybody controlled by him. Big Daveylicious: Him? Angus: A 17-year-old controls you! Big Daveylicious: No. I control myself. (Angus looks hysterical.) Angus: You’re the main character controlled by a 17-year-old! A 17 YEAR OLD! HE’LL USE YOU AND ABUSE YOU! JUST ASK PETER DAMN PAN! (Big D laughs this off Angus takes this as an insult.) Angus: WHAT’S FUNNY?! Big Daveylicious: You are one crazy b@stard. Angus: WHAT THE BLOODY HELL IS A MATTER WITH YOU?! A 17-YEAR-OLD CONTROLS YOU! YOU IDIOT! (Big D obviously getting tired of this babbling idiot. Angus pushes him and Angus has just added gasoline to the fire that is burning into Big Daveylicious. Big D rears his right arm and punches Angus in the mouth. Angus falls to the floor. But Big D continues to beat up the poor ol‘ mental patient as he stomps away on him. The orderlies swarm over to Big D grabbing him and hauling him away from Angus. One of the orderlies take out a needle. Big D notices it in all this excitement. ) Big Daveylicious: I DON’T NEED DRUGS! I’M A MENTAL PATIENT DAMN IT! (The orderly pierces the needle into the skin of our mentor. His eyes start to close as the scene fades to black.)
