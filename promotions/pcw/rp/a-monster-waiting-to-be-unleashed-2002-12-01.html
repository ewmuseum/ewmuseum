A Monster Waiting To Be Unleashed

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-12-02 Walking down the street, he had no remorse whatsoever for the child he pushed into the busy traffic. While the mother followed him angrily, screaming obscenities and hitting him pitifully across the face rather than help her child, he looked straight ahead, ignoring this mere wench. Suddenly the woman was on the ground, her clothes torn off and her curves being shown off to the public. He threw them down at her. Suddenly he began singing and whistling, the next second he was back in an aggressive mood. He was Skitzo…and this is his tale. Skitzo: I win a Gatorade contract then lose to that fucking muppet of a Maths teacher?! WHAT’S WRONG WITH THIS WORLD! Skitzo: After a hard week of ‘rasslin’ I personally couldn’t be assed doing any PR work. Anyway, those African kids woulda enjoyed the fifty crates I sent… Skitzo: The crates were empty… Skitzo: WHAT?! Skitzo: Yeah, remember we mixed the drink together with vodka in a massive punchbowl and drank it all, then said fuck those Twiglets if they want a drink they can do a rain dance? Skitzo: Oh yeah… The Mental One makes his way down a lone street towards the suburbs towards the outskirts of a town not to be named. All around him were signs of fear; something in the area was keeping people at a comfortable level of paranoia and fear. Something in the area was keeping the people at bay. Something was striking fear into their hearts. Skitzo: Something’s really got these people spooked, their fucking lifeless! Skitzo proclaimed such as he pushed over an old woman, and carried on, looking past the now-empty roads at a large woodland area ahead, parted only by a small dusty road leading to somewhere nobody wants to go. Voice: You don’t want to be going in there sonny, bad things live in them woods! An old man chimed in, drawing Skitzo’s attention. The Mental One couldn’t help but acknowledge the feeble human. Skitzo: I know…and it’s going to help me defeat a Vampire and a Jester! Skitzo: I can’t believe they’re fighting you, him, me and I and this guy! Skitzo: You could say…P.W.A. at…At Death’s Door! Skitzo: HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA-…no…no you couldn’t…else it’d take away the mystery aspect of the opponent… Old Man: You’re one of them!!! GETOUTTAMAWAY!!! Skitzo is pushed aside by the old man, and watches as he hobbles away into the distance. Scratching his head, Mr. Psycho simply shrugs his shoulders and proceeds analysing the situation ahead. Skitzo: So P.W.A. want to fuck with a schizophrenic…they want to pull their pranks and play their games…well they’re going to fucking crash and burn like a middle-eastern aeroplane pilot! Skitzo: That was low man… Skitzo: Yeah 50,000 to 10,000ft in five minutes generally is… The woodland seems to call out to Skitzo as he moves out of the last street and waits before the mouth of an ominous black hole surrounded by aging trees and swamp-life. A musty atmosphere gathers about Skitzo, somewhat calming The Mental One in this nervous environment. Skitzo: There’s something in there that’s going to help me vanquish those two bastards…and by god I don’t care if I get mauled in the process, it’s going to take me to the top! Skitzo: This place reminds me a lot of back home… Skitzo: I love Louisiana… Skitzo: Ok lads…let us go forth and conquer! With that, Skitzo took the first step forward, his spine chilling as the foot made contact with the cold dirt of the road. He set about on his way through the brush, then the undergrowth beneath the great canopies above, sticking to the middle of the road as the sun slowly but surely faded out behind the thick tops of the trees. He felt vulnerable for once, for this area of the world was home to one of the most dangerous creatures on the planet, a beast powerful enough to crush a man, from his body to his soul, the people of the town filled with fear and indeed around the world knew this Monster’s name. Skitzo: This place is fucking creepy… Skitzo: Rather here than a graveyard at midnight… Skitzo: Pffft! Hey Skitzo aren’t you a necrophiliac? Skitzo: Do I LOOK like I took it up the ass from Vin-… Skitzo: AH-AH-AH-AH-AH-AH-AH-AHEM!!!!!!! Skitzo: We’ve all heard the contract stories, and half the time the contract isn’t even given out! Something began croaking in the distance. Wasn’t the knowledge of swamp life obvious to Skitzo, he would have believed something not of this world was stalking him. Pushing on still, the trees began to thin out and the road becomes sturdier, with fewer potholes and actual signs of paving and tar further along the road. In the far distance Skitzo can make out the silhouette of a large building, surrounded by grasping razor wire, mesh fences, and guards. As he walks the last few feet to the building, Skitzo begins conversing again. Skitzo: -…so the guy says, “I’d rather have a bottle in front of me than a frontal lobotomy!” Skitzo: Heard it back in ‘Nam… Skitzo: I was never in Vietnam… Skitzo: I saw a video on it so go fuck yourself… Skitzo: Ok we know what we’re looking for here; this place doesn’t look like it likes our type… Skitzo: Yeah we best stick to the ‘sane’ approach-… Skitzo: Quiet, here’s a guard! Skitzo stops as a man carrying a pistol approaches him. Dressed in a fine black uniform and looking sharper than a Scotsman’s wit, the guard nods in the direction of Skitzo, keeping his hand firmly clasped around the gun in its holster. Guard: How can I be helping a young man like you on a fine day like this? Skitzo: Good day sir. My name is Mr. Williams; I called in advance about picking up one of yours for a promotional appearance at Primetime Championship Wrestling? Guard: Ah, again? My, my, he has been as busy boy with that ol’ PCW this past while I do say…what manner of business is he partaking in this time? Skitzo: Well I don’t know if you follow-… Guard: I do, and last time I did check you had quite a showing at the Pay-Per-View. Tell me Mr. Skitzo, how does that Gatorade taste knowing everyone of your peers, your followers and your enemies are drinking it with pride for their promotion? The sharp southern drawl spieled by the guard took Skitzo aback, but he stood his ground in this verbal confrontation. Skitzo: Feels good…but I was wondering if I could attain my “mystery” tag team partner now so I may prepare for the match on Friday… Guard: I like a man who comes well…prepared… Skitzo: I like a man who doesn’t waste time… Guard; Ah I see how it goes, very well then follow me to the front lobby and I will see about your…partner… With that, the guard took off swiftly and marched off towards the building. Passing through gates and security checks, Skitzo followed the guard that reminded him ever so slightly of Anthony Hopkins’s portrayal of Hannibal Lecter…sophisticated yet deadly. Taking the last few steps up the front of the building, Skitzo could just make out the name of the building partially hidden by bright, cold sunlight. Skitzo: Shady Acres Asylum for the Mentally Disabled… Skitzo: The only place to find a mystery partner, eh? Guard: Excuse me? Skitzo & Skitzo: Nothing! Guard: Ok wait here; I will get your accomplice for you… Little did Skitzo know what he was in for as within a few moments the guard returned, hauling behind him a large cage, a very large cage, dotted with decent sized air holes as opposed to the conventional bars, transported on a buggy of some kind. The guard dismounts from the buggy and approaches Skitzo. Guard: Built into this structure is a microchip used for a number of purposes ranging from tracking to transportation permits. You should reach your venue without much trouble, now this structure is super-strong to prevent occupants…”escaping”, so you’ll need this key card to open it and that should be about it. Optionally there is transportation that can take you from here to the venue under strict supervision of Shady Acres security… Skitzo: Sounds good…how far can that take me? Guard: To the nearest airport, where you will fly DIRECTLY to your PCW venue and stray not a mile further, do I make myself clear? People like Xavier Kannon may own a promotion as prestigious as that but he does not own property like this…be on your guard Mr. Williams and do not let this creature out of sight, it’s extremely dangerous, a Monster, a Lunatic… Skitzo: Ok, can I go now? Guard: Yes of course. The helicopter is waiting around back. If you’ll follow me… Skitzo obliges and follows the guard on the buggy, while inside the cage the beast that is his alleged tag partner growls and roars. In the back of his mind The Mental One goes over Fusion. A Vampire and a Jester taking on a Schizophrenic and a returning Monster…such an event is somewhat of an insult for a creature of his magnitude to return to, but alas Skitzo has him in his control…if it ever could be controlled. Boarding the large helicopter, Skitzo glances at the cage, and notices a furious glare boring a hole through him, and with a great gulp Skitzo takes his seat in the helicopter, on his way to prepare for Friday Fusion and indeed the next week of PCW action, complete with The Monster They Call………………
