Truly Misplaced -- Part 2 of 2

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/11/02 The last time you visited in on the life of Misplaced, it was a rainy Wednesday morning at around midnight. The very strange team were having an argument, and there sidekick wasn’t there to help solve it. So it’s now Thursday, and this twisted team is in a big twist. They have a big match tomorrow that can help prove them talented and push off their recent job of doing the job. Who knows what might happen between these three men, and can Jackson solve anything? Stephen Cairns was last seen midnight of Wednesday morning at a European Bar in downtown New Orleans. It was a crummy joint, but his choices were limited to standing in the dark rain or sitting in a hellish bar. It’s Thursday afternoon and he still hasn’t returned to his hotel to check in on his partner. Carey J. Nowski did the usual Wednesday morning and ran back to his hotel. He had started this argument by bringing up the subject of suing the X-Files. Stephen found this stupid, and then they split. Of course, Carey got the better end of it by getting the air conditioning hotel, room service, and a computer. He hasn’t put much effort into finding Stephen apparently since he is still in that same hotel room. God only knows what he could’ve been working on in that room. Finally, you have Jackson “Hoe” Pallish. He is the sidekick of Misplaced, and he resides from New Orleans. His mama’ and him own a farm in the suburbs of New Orleans. Yes, a farm in the suburbs. He couldn’t tag along with his team due to his mama’ making him stay and help watch the children. Nobody is really sure whether he has a wife, or if his mama’ is his wife. That’s something that should be left a secret. Jackson should be returning to the team soon only to find that there is no team. Now it’s time to visit in on Carey. He hasn’t really moved out of his room besides eating in the hotel restaurant. Carey has learned by now that Stephen can get tired of people and run off for a while. It’s getting a little serious now though. They have a match tomorrow, and they’re nowhere near each other. Carey decides that there isn’t much he can do but wait for Stephen to come to the room. As he’s sitting next to his laptop writing something, the hotel door knocks. Carey rushes to the door hoping to see Stephen, but it’s not. It’s Jackson. He gives a slight sigh, but immediately proceeds to open the door. Jackson makes his way in and runs around the corner to see no Stephen. Jackson: “Yo’ man, what’s up?” Carey pauses with a response, and he starts to pace around. Carey: “Oh just the usual. We have a match in less than twenty four hours and my tag team partner is nowhere to be found.” Jackson: “What you talkin’ bout’? Where da’ hell he be?” Carey: “I have no clue on that one. He ran off Wednesday morning and he hasn’t been back yet. He got upset with me, and that’s all there is to it. He’s a big boy so I can’t control him. He just better damn well show up before tomorrow night!” Jackson shows a depressed look to Carey. They both sit on a bed and give depressed looks. Jackson: “What we be doin’ jus’ sittin’ here on our asses? Lets get up and find dis’ New Zealand mother!” Carey: “What are we going to do? There’s whole city of New Orleans and then some were he could be hiding. I doubt we’ll find him just like that.” Jackson: “You wanna’ win tomorrow night?” Carey just simply nods his head. Jackson: “Then we best get up and find Stephen!” Carey: “Lets do it.” Carey just gives in, and then these two men are off to find one very stubborn guy. They have a whole city to look through, and it’s unlikely they’ll have any luck. Carey grabs a piece of paper and writes a note to stick on the door incase Stephen returns. It reads, “Stephen, we’re off searching for you. Just wait in the lobby if you happen to come back. Oh by the way, Jackson said he is going to beat you down if you don’t show up. Of course, you’ll never see this if you don’t show. Anyways, just wait downstairs. Carey.”. Now what would normally happen with Carey’s luck in this situation? I’d say that Stephen would come marching back to the room. That’s unlikely. So here we have two strange men searching down a men among millions. Now where would an angry New Zealand cricket freak go? I doubt there are many choices in New Orleans. That’s why it’ll be so hard to find him. Well they’re off. Carey J. Nowski and Jackson “Hoe” Pallish are going to attempt to find a man in a city with absolutely no clue where he is. Of course, this could be called an emergency. They have a big match tomorrow, and I don’t think they want to lose. Carey and Jackson enter the silver Chrysler Sebring rental car. They’re pulling out of the parking lot, and then the biggest surprise happens. It’s Stephen. Here comes this tired, dirty, and rugged looking man walking towards the hotel that they stay in. Carey quickly notices Stephen and puts the car at a halt. Jackson wastes no time jumping out of the car to get all up on Stephen. Jackson: “Man, what the hell you thinkin’? Why you gotta’ just run off like dis’? You more immature than dem’ koon’s that mama’ stays up to catch. You need to teach yourself…” Stephen ignores every word that Jackson says, and he just continues towards the hotel. He enters the lobby and heads straight for the elevator. Carey obviously follows him. He runs to catch up to the closing elevator, and he just makes it. Stephen gives an upset looks and just turns away. Carey: “What’s wrong with you? What have I done to you? You just ran off from your teammate a day before a match. You’re an adult, now act like one!” Stephen: “It’s over. I can’t team with you and I can’t stay around that clown Jackson. This was all a mistake from the very beginning. Please just stay away from me.” Carey: “Ok. I respect that. I just want to know what’s gotten into you. I don’t want this team to split. You’re a good friend and partner. I didn’t think I’ve done anything to ruin that. I want to win tomorrow, but in order to do that we both have to act like men.” The elevator comes to a halt and Stephen rushes out. Carey is rushing right behind him. Carey runs ahead, opens the door, and quickly snatches the note that was on the door. Stephen ignores his every action and goes into the room. His shadow is behind him. Stephen sits on a bed and just strokes his hair. Carey takes a seat next to him. Carey: “We can do this. We’re a good enough team to beat all these other guys. We just need to be a team. No more crap. We just need to do this. Focus!” Stephen: “I’m going through tough times. I’m away from my homeland and we’re not exactly tearing up the wrestling standings. I came over here to win, and I haven’t been. I gave up cricket for wrestling. That was a decision that’ll change my life forever, and I want it to be for the better.” Carey: “It will be. It will. We just need to win tomorrow, and who knows what that could spark. We just need some confidence, and then we can actually do good. In order to do that we have to get along. You have to realize that I’m not exactly normal. I have my little sci-fi games, and you’re an athlete. We’re truly Misplaced, but that doesn’t mean we can’t be successful. I say we go out tomorrow and kick those other guys down to the floor!” Before they can continue there conversation, there odd sidekick comes bursting through the doors. Jackson: “Was’ up? We all coo’?” Stephen gets up and puts on an angry look. He walks over to Jackson and stands in front of him. They’re in a stare down. Finally, after moments of pressure Stephen smiles. Stephen: “We coo’.” They all break a chuckle and make there was into the room. They all have a seat on the beds and turn on the television. It seems that this Misplaced trio has worked it out in time.
