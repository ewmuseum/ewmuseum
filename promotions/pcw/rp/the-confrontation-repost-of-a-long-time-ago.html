The Confrontation (Repost of a long time ago)

Date: To get everyone caught up on what happened. Last time with SyN and his group, they were continuing through Nick Ramsey’s office to reach Nick himself and hopefully save New York city. The team entered floor three to a bunch of men who jumped them afer noticing SyN. After a very interesting confrontation between all of the men on the floor, men escorted Michael O’Connor and Joey Maxwell, SyN’s team out of the building, leaving SyN there himself. With guards on SyN’s shoulder, the men are taking SyN to Nick Ramsey, which is bound to be very interesting. There was a pain on both of my shoulders that were weighing me down. There were two men pushing down my shoulders to make sure I cooperated and didn’t cause any trouble. My hands were held behind my back who held them there, making it impossible for me to grab a gun and fight back. On top of that there was another man holding a gun to my back, who kept threatening to pull the trigger if I made any moves. BOY, these guys showed no mercy! About five feet ahead was the door where Nick Ramsey’s office was. I expected his office to be a small room with controls all around with buttons and other things on them and then in the centre, a red button that would de-activate the bombs. I would find out soon enough. We reached the door and one of the men punched in a code, making the door open where we walked in. The office was a lot bigger than I thought! The office was about 20 by 20 feet. On the far wall was a high-tech computer. On the monitor were cameras of all six bombs in the New York buildings, watching them to make sure nobody de-activated them. In the centre of the screen were three more pictures of the New York buildings that had bombs in them. On the right hand corner of the screen was a timer ticking down: 8:28 8:27 8:26 Just over twenty minutes till New York would be down and out. Beside the computer, facing the opposite direction was Nick Ramsey. He was wearing a black suit and tie, something that a professional would wear. He finally turned around to look at me. He wasn’t that mean looking! He had brown hair that was combed back and parted to two sides. He had blue eyes and a normal nose and mouth. He didn’t look that mean at all but calm and determined which added that evil look into him. He looked about my age, maybe a little bit older, about twenty-nine or thirty, something in there. Nick looked at me and grinned at me. He shifted to the side a bit and I saw it! It was a small, red button that was on the wall, closer to the ceiling, beside the computer. It was the only thing that would save the world! I also noticed that on the roof was a small door, probably an escape route or something like that. The guards on my shoulders and back backed off. I expected one or two of them to stay to make sure I didn’t try anything but they all left the room, leaving a one on one battle with Nick Ramsey! As the guards left, one of them spoke. “Boss, here is SyN. Choose what you will with him. We’ll be outside the door, waiting.” That was all the guard said before he left the room like the rest. I shifted free and looked at Nick. I didn’t know if I were to speak to or not but it didn’t matter because he spoke. “Steffen Youri Neirlinger, we meet again.” Wait a minute! How did he know my name? We never knew each other. He had a strong German accent, but his name? It wasn’t German! “Blah, Nick Ramsey, vat a fake name vor me. It iz zo fake and yet, nobody haz zaid anyzing at all!” So now I knew that Nick Ramsey wasn’t his real name, but what was it? “You were zust like you were az a kid Steffen. Alwayz leving me behind and out of ze group. You even left me back at Germany, with mom on your zide. Perfect little Steffen, what a little coward you are.” Mom? MOM! That means that Nick Ramsey........is........is.........my brother! Thoughts filled my head: I was brought back to the day we left for America, me and mom. I see my mom hugging another boy like me, my brother. She looked at me, she was crying. Together we got onto the boat to America, the little boy being left behind. As we left dock, I said the boys name: “Sven”. I hadn’t realized that I had said Sven’s name out loud but Nick......Sven was nodding at me. “I knew you would remember Steffen. You left me behind! You left me to die on the streets on Germany! Mom thought I would never survive! I got so sad and then I met Joey, or Frankie as he told me. We became friends but I was using him and now I don’t need him for there is only twenty seconds left!” I suddenly toned out of what Sven was saying. I looked at the timer ticking down at the screen: 0:16 0:15 0:14 I looked at the red button and then back at the timer, and Sven, who was rambling on. 0:13 0:12 “So sad...” 0:11 0:10 “...I will have revenge...” 0:09 0:08 0:07 I looked at the red button and knew that this was my ONLY chance! 0:06 0:05 0:04 0:03 I lunged into the air, aiming at the button. 0:02 I saw Sven see what I was doing and pull out a gun, aiming it at me but I was at the button. 0:01 I hit the button as the trigger of the gun went off. I felt some pain in my side and realized I was bleeding. Everything was a blur. I heard the trap-door opening and a helicopter above and the silent beeping of the bombs being shut down and aborted. Then everything went black... (New RP will be up in a bit, following off from this.)
