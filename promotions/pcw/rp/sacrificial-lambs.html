Sacrificial Lambs...

Date: not entered Watch the flames flicker and die… Hell hath been quenched by the had of the god… The Devil hath been put in his place as what he truly is…a mortal threat, and not an immortal threat. His power is limited to the human, and could never really touch the god’s might. It was one of the mortals themselves that put an end to the Devil…an unnecessary assist to a job the god had already completed. The Devil does nothing but to steal, kill, and destroy from mankind, but it seems like mankind had stolen, killed, and destroyed that of the Devil’s…how ironic. Third Dawning…was indeed just that. The destruction of El Diablo did nothing but to signal the end for all that defied the Neo Void God…this was the dawning of a new era. Just as Kannon assumed power, so shall the god…and PCW will never be the same again. Turmoil was a skip away…but the god had plans for Fusion instead. 7 man/1 god tag team match. Akiyama was to team with mortals Snow and Frisco, along with Xavier Kannon, the champion and the owner of PCW. Across from him…weaklings, fools, more mortals… El Diablo, Duke Williams, Rent A Villian, and Darkstar. These were supposed to be the men PCW cheered? A foolhardy old letch, a schizophrenic villainous hero, a pathetic waste of a Satanist, and a Goth…these were whom the people cheered for? Mankind really is flawed then… But cheers are not our reward…hate is our reward. Because anyone can cheer…it takes no effort at all to snatch the people into your palm. Stealing away the energy and effort of humanity by making them despise you and loathe your very being takes more strength, more power, more intelligence…overall, more. Any jackass can get cheers…just do what you’re told and you’re loved by the people. Mindless peons… We who hold our own individualities are hated because we go against the flow. We who make our own rules, we who follow no one but ourselves. We defy authority in a world of obedience. We are wolves amidst sheep watching them being led to slaughter by their precious protectors. And we have four sheep ripe for the slaughter right before our eyes this Friday… Sheep are the best sacrifices for the god…their purity will be stained with their own blood after being ripped to shreds. Speaking of sheep… “BAAAAAAAA!!!” “Shhh! Keep Bo Peep quiet! We can’t risk ruining the ceremony before it’s started!!” “I’m trying, dude! But Lamb Chop here is SOOO not trying to cooperate!!” “Tie it up and let’s hurry up and finish before the fire goes out!” The scene opens up to a small wooded area out in the suburbs of the unknown. A small altar stood in the center of the clearing with a fire burning atop of it. From out of the shadows around the altar stepped two darkly cloaked individuals. Their hoods covered their faces from view as they stood in front of the altar. “O, dark prince, we praise you this night.” A dark voice said underneath the hood. “We praise you this night.” The other cloaked man said. Another voice seemed to echo through the air from nearby, as if there was one more or more surrounding them. “Bless us with your darkness so that we may show others the light.” “Dude, you sure that’s the passage?” A sigh came out from underneath the hood as the speaker threw back his hood. Standing there was a teenaged boy with gelled black hair and white makeup and black eye shadow and lipstick. He glared over at his partner with dark eyes. “Yeah, I’m, like, SO sure that’s the passage, man!” The other cloaked man pulled his hood back, revealing a pudgy-faced teen also in pasty makeup and black lipstick and eye shadow. He ran his fingers through his spiky black hair and shrugged his shoulders. “Doesn’t sound like it to me, dude.” The other “dude” punched him in his arm before glaring at him “Well then, KEN…the day you learn Latin is the day you can recite the incantation! You got it?” Ken flashed a grin of metal, the fire reflecting off his braces. “My name’s Xanadu! You don’t have to get your Joe Boxers in a twist, Gill! Go ahead!” “Like, thank you!” Gill shook his head as he threw his arms out towards the flames. “Baal, we pray to you tonight for the soul of your child, your son, El Diablo. Your sweet Mexican Devil has been taken from us by the evil Neo Void God at the Third Dawning, his body broken for you, and his spirit shattered in your name.” “In your name.” Ken and the other voice chimed in. Gill gave them a side-glance before moving on. “Your golden child goes up yet again against the heathens, the one called Akiyama and the ones that have disrespected your children, Snow, Frisco, and Kannon. With dark warriors, Diablo will lead a crusade against the unclean ones, and ultimately regain your lost honor from the Third Dawning.” “OH GREAT BEELZEBUB!!” Ken surged up out of nowhere, interrupting Gill’s speech. Gill looked on with annoyance as his arms lowered. “We pray that you’ll somehow return the favor to the beating that Diablo took in your name! We somehow hope that you’ll return the favor to Darkstar, who suffered defeat in your name as well!! We hope you return the favor to Duke Williams, who isn’t really your servant, but is just as crude and lewd as you are! We hope you---!” “KEN!!” Gill snapped, growing impatient with Ken’s rambling. “MY NAME IS XANADU!!” He wailed at his smaller friend. Gill stared him down, slowly drawing the bigger man to lower his hands as well as his head. “Sorry, Gill.” He muttered ashamed. “As I was saying before I was RUDELY interrupted…” he gave a glance towards the silent Ken before raising his arms again. “Tonight, we offer you sacrifice for the well being of your servants in battle at the end of this week. A lamb…symbolizing the white cleanliness of our warriors as they go into battle…their wounds cleansed and the blood of their enemies staining their skin! Ambrosia!! Bring out the offering!!” Another black robed person came walking towards the altar, struggling with the squirming lamb tied in their hands. The person sat the lamb before the Gill and stepped back. Gill took a knife and held it above the bound lamb. “Satan, this is for you! Drink of this feast and bless our warriors!!” Just then, the ropes came undone from the lamb’s hooves. It pleated loudly in the night sky before kicking Gill straight in his crotch area and running off into the woods. Ken and the other cloaked person stepped forward to stop it, but it was long gone by then. Gill looked up in pain as well as annoyance at the cloaked person. “I thought you tied up the sacrifice, STEPHANIE!!” The cloaked person pulled off her hood, revealing a bleached blonde with pale makeup. A bruise was on her cheek, with a sour look on her face. “I SO tried to keep that wooly mammoth still, but it wouldn’t behave! I, like, did the best I could, GILL!!” “My name is Phoenix, STEPHANIE!” Stephanie glared back at him. “My name is Ambrosia, GILLIAM!!” “My name is XANADU, KENNETH!!” Ken said out of the blue, trying to join into the argument. Gill and Steph looked at him with dark eyes before they both punched him in his arms. He let out a yelp of pain as he rubbed his arms slowly. “Sorry…I just wanted to belong…” “Well then, I’ll help you belong, Xanadu.” Gill smiled at his companion. “Go get the spare lamb I told you to bring before we came out here.” Xanadu shifted his eyes back and forth, looking up and down at his shoes. Gill glared up at him, standing back to his feet and looking him in his eyes. “You DID steal the spare lamb from the petting zoo stable, RIGHT?” Gill asked with more authority. Ken looked up at Gill’s eyes and smiled a weak, tinny smile. “Well…I had my headphones on at the time, and I didn’t hear you entirely over NIN.” He admitted slowly. “So when you said get the spare lamb, I thought you said get the spare…lamp.” He held up a small red lantern for Stephanie and Gill to look upon. Gill looked at Stephanie with a disgusted look before slapping the lantern right out of his hand. “I hate you.” Gill bluntly remarked before turning to the fire. Ken looked down at his shoes as Gill put his hands on his hips. “Well, Lucifer…looks like my BONEHEAD buddy here forgot to, like, bring the extra sacrifice, so there’s nothing for you to take! We’re SO sorry, ya know? But don’t worry…I’m sure your warriors can take care of Akiyama and the others without your blessing!” Just then, there was a rustling in the bushes nearby. Stephanie looked over and smiled. “Hey! The lamb’s back!” But instead of a lamb, a tall black-cloaked figure came stepping out of the brush. The three posers looked on as a white wisp came trailing out from the person’s hood. “You lost your sacrifice, children?” a familiar cold voice echoed out at them in the darkness. Pulling back her hood, completely dark eyes bore the icy grip of death into them. “Your own lives should prove to be sufficient offering to the nVg!” Scatter, mice…save yourselves from your undoing…depart from evil, that you may do good another day… That was sound advice for those fools come this Friday…running away would be the best option…unless they actually like the suffering personally prophesized by the Divine Prophetess. Minutes later, Gill, Stephanie, and Ken all huddled at the edge of a ravine. They were out of breath, and their makeup looked to be running…. from sweat or tears, one or the other. “Did…we lose…her?” Ken huffed as he leaned on Gill’s shoulder. “I don’t…know…” he replied, looking back at the ravine behind them. “But how are we going to escape her with this gap in the path?” “Only human…” the voice of the Divine Prophetess echoed through the woods as she glared at them from mere paces before them. Stephanie gripped Gill’s arm tightly as the three drew closer together, as if there were safety in numbers and closeness. Akiyama has been known to drop numerous men at times…that theory was null and void. “What are you going to do us us??” Stephanie squeaked out at Cyclonica. Cyclonica tilted her head to the side and shrugged. “Nothing.” Was all she said. The trio looked up confused. That was all the time needed… Behind them the Neo Void God leaped out of the ravine, landing in a crouched position with his hand hammering into the ground. He let out an inhuman howl behind the youth, scaring the piss literally out of them. Screams filled the air that night…followed by silence… Then later on, smoke floated up afterwards towards the heavens… Sacrifices…they were nothing more than just that… They would find themselves burned for just that…charred from the flames of pain ignited by the god of PCW… Akiyama. So says the Divine Prophetess…
