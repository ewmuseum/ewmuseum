'When we're alone' - the words tripped off his tongueAs though to be alone were nothing strange.'When we was young,' he said; 'when we was young....' we thought of age, and loneliness, and change.We thought of how strange we grow when we're alone,and how unlike the selves that meet and talk,and blow the candles out, and say good night.Alone.... The word is life endured and known.It is the stillness where our spirits walkand all but inmost faith is overthrown. 'Alone' -By Siegfried Sassoon. Dear Yuu, When we were practicing today, we foolishly dropped our sword on our foot. In the process, we managed to bruise our bare toes. This incident proves to us that we need to train more. How ironic that it happens now, in the isolation of the mountains. We say this because we would have loved to show the people who live on the earth that we were not really superhuman, that we were not perfect. But even then, the people would not understand. They would have laughed at how a man such as myself was able to achieve the birthright of godhood given to us. The narrow-mindedness of the world is so prominent that it sickens us. We mediate in order to help expand our mind. We work hard in training to perfect our skills. While we were practicing an overhead strike, we hit our arm on a nearby rock and the weapon slipped from our hands and fell to the ground. The hilt struck our exposed foot. We should have watched for that rock. But we are thankful that it happened here in training. If it had happened in the real world, we would not be here right now. The mercy in the real world is minimal. From experience, we know. We dislike those who are jealous of our ability. First of all, we are not perfect. We make our mistakes and stumble on our words. We say little because we fear what people would think if we stated our mind. Strong as the stories say we are, we are still incompetent in many ways. The rock, the boulder on our training ground stopped us as we thought we had the ground memorized. Perhaps that is not a lack of skill. Perhaps that is a birth of arrogance. We should not be proud of our position as the Neo Void God. The position does not prevent us from going into battle. We have to enter the wars, we have to slash our sword, and we have to draw the blood of human beings. Ourimage may instill fear, but no longer do people surrender their weapons to us. Instead, they fight us. In battle, the blood splashes over our arms; the warmth seeps in through our gauntlets, across our shoulders, over our face. The salty taste flecks our lips. We fight through a sea of death. In war, we are treading in blood up to our ankles. The warriors who die in battle die not from the sharp end of our blade, but from drowning in the blood of men, their comrades, their opponents, and their own. Despite this, people envy us, and want to take our place. They want to fight the painful wars, to endure the hostile environments, to sit through the impatient speeches. They want to manage the armies, deal with the incompetent fools at the top of PCW, and wade through the blood of friend and foe alike. They think it will bring them glory, and strength, and honor. They think it will earn them beautiful women, riches, and attention. They think that friends will flock to them like flies to feces. But we wish we could tell them that it is not easy. We look at ourselves. Are we enjoying women? Do we have friends flocking to us left and right? Do we enjoy the riches and power that they think our status provides us with? We are still alone. But every time we think of this, we hear the voice speaking to us, reassuring us, that she is there. We shrug it off, for we know the truth, and the truth is, we are not with anyone else, and are therefore alone. Why do we train? We train so we will no longer be afraid. We do not have to be alone. The world awaits us, but so much of the world will ignore what we truly are, and instead, they will strike at the reputation that they dreamed up for us. PCW and Nelson created the reputation, the man who tried to be the god of so many men. He told us what to do, where to go, and how to act. Our mistake, back then, was letting him pull the strings, letting him move our mouth like a dummy. Nelson will see what he has created, and he will finally see the truth, the truth he had denied us for so many months. But we cannot face the world. Ourstriking arm is not perfect. We drop our practice sword. We must concentrate on our abilities before heading to the world. It is not the strength of our body that we need, though. It is patience we need, so we can tolerate human beings. ** Akiyama **
