Title: Heartbreakers, They Got The Best of Me... Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: PPV day PCW Supremacy. The year 2002. After a record-breaking title reign, Politically Incorrect came up against the Heartbreakers. In a match with no interference, cheating, or other assorted BS, The Heartbreakers succeeded in executing their Heart Attack finishing move, scoring the one-two-three, and taking the tag team titles from Politically Incorrect. That makes this thing personal. Because, you see, Politically Incorrect never got a return match. Hell, the Heartbreakers never got to defend the title. PCW shut down temporarily, causing the firing of the roster. While this firing indicated a betrayal of the politicians on the part of Tyler Nelson, just imagine what it did to the Heartbreakers. Two men who had ascended to the tip-top of their profession: tag team wrestling, to hold the gold for only an hour, and to lose them thanks to corporate suits, not in -ring competition. Worse, neither team got a shot at the titles when PCW came around. Instead, an ill-fated tournament began, then a giant battle royale, and then, the politicians went off to fight Akiyama, with no title shots, and the Heartbreakers had a nice battle with the Pretty Cool Guys, but still, neither group was satisified. They wanted the tag titles. Bad. But, even more importantly, Politically Incorrect wants another go at the Heartbreakers. They are man enough to say, to quote Mariah Carey, 'Heartbreakers, you got the best of me.' Once. And, if the politicians have any say in the matter, never, EVER, again. ::The deck of the mighty WrestleVessel is being prepared for a night of professional wrestling unlike any other. Tonight's pay-per-view looks to change many things in the company, as titles and pride are at stake. The tag titles, however, are what concern three teams and one God on this evening, as Akiyama, the Pretty Cool Guys, Heartbreakers, and Politically Incorrect clash, with the PCW Tag Team Titles on the line...:: William S. Powell: I have to admit, this is quite impressive... ::Powell is looking out over the boat, at the massive steel cell being erected in the bay. His eyes turn to the boat, and the PCW ring being put together on board the deck.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Indeed, William..I've never had the opportunity to wrestle in such a...a......unique setting. William S. Powell: I have. I wrestled in the Playboy Mansion. That was fun.....mmmmmm......all the women......and our buddy Tim Walden jobbed to Bobby Stormbringer who was holding a picture of Amy Campbell from OSW. ::Powell chuckles, as his partner merely rolls his eyes.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Must you ALWAYS bring that up? Tim is such a nice fellow, he does not deserve this constant mocking! William S. Powell: Lighten up gramps! Learn to have a little fun! Mocking Walden should be an Olympic sport, damnit! ::Lovecraft decides to do what he always does: ignore his partner's stupidity. It is an art he has grown to excel in. Anyway, The Governor pauses to bask in the glow of the warm Jamaican sun, and poses a question to his partner.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: What happened to Akiyama, anyway? William S. Powell: I don;t know...I quite expected him, or his Divine Prophetess at least, to be talking about how us mortal men don't have a chance of defeating him. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Who the hell are you kidding? You could care less about Akiyama! You just want to watch Cyclonica prance around the beaches of Jamaica in a thong bikini, and you know it! William S. Powell: I DO NOT! I HAVE NO DESIRE TO WATCH CYCLONICA PRANCE AROUND THE BEACHES OF JAMAICA IN A THONG BIKINI! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Thank God, maybe the beatings got something to register in that little brain of yours. William S. Powell: I WANT TO WATCH CYCLONICA PRANCE AROUND THE BEACHES OF JAMAICA WEARING NOTHING AT ALL! GO ME! :Powell dances around in a circle, celebrating his one-upping of his partner. Lovecraft silently seethes..:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Damnit man...this is our chance! If we win this match, we become two-time PCW World Tag Team champions! We join the ELITE of tag team wrestling. Guys like Golden Game, who we beat, and those Westsidaz punks, who couldn't beat us at the IPPV, and will soon be beaten by us anyway. This night will show the world that Politically Incorrect is the most dominant tag team in this company. Ever. Period. End.....of.....SENTENCE! William S. Powell: Don't......ripoff......Ellie ever again or I'm going to be forced to beat you severely about the head and shoulders. Now, onto the Heartbreakers...the guys, that besides us, got screwed over most by the restart. We haven't had the chance to meet eyeball to eyeball since then. You beat us once....but that will not happen again. We were overconfident from the time period that we had the titles for eleven years, and we got beat. But now.....we want those titles, AND WE WANT A PIECE OF YOUR ASS!! Reginald V. Lovecraft: You might want to rephrase that with those two... William S. Powell: Yeah, uh, WE'RE GOING TO KICK YOUR ASS! AND BEAT IT DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Thank you, Mr. Trapezoid..anyway. It's Election Day today. We're victorious. That's all, it's early, I need my nap.. ::Fade to black..:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
