Date: not entered Open in Edgar R. Boxleitner Prison, a.k.a. 'The Box' to its inmates. A maximum-security prison situated in Death Valley, CA. The closest comparison to life in The Box, as the inmates would say, is Scared Straight meets Boot Camp meets 'Apocalypse Now,' raised to the tenth power. The hardest work imaginable, labor like one couldn't imagine to be done in 120 degree temperatures, or freezing temperatures in the harsh winters. Randomly given extra work assignments, scalding hot shower water, dangerous mining work that is hazardous to a man's health. Why would anyone want to be a part of this 'by-prisoner-request-only' torture? Simple, really. Prisoners sign up to go to this hellhole in the middle of nowhere because it means freedom. Plain and simple, the worst offenders get themselves sent here to have their sentences reduced up to 9/10th of the length they were originally sentenced to serve in prison. Lifers surviving The Box can hope to see the outside world in ten years. And lesser offenders get out even sooner. It's purposely hard. It's a quick fix, an easy way out; at the same time, it's excruciatingly difficult. Skylar is wearing jeans and a yellow shirt. He's not wearing his mask; this isn't a public appearance as the PCW wrestler, so his mask is not required. Though he's still got on his white denim coat over his clothes, that's just something of a sentimental attachment piece of clothing. He received it as a gift from Sandy Wong. Skylar's got a neatly trimmed goatee; his face is plain, with no distinguishing marks other than his facial hair. His skin is tanned, light coffee-colored. His thin black hair is tied back around his head. The black-uniformed guard, an Asian man in his late thirties, stocky and tanned with a full moustache and unkempt hair, escorts Skylar into the meeting room where he has arranged to see an old friend. A face to face chat is rather rare; most often they merely exchange phone correspondence. Sitting down at the chair across from the prisoner, the two separated by bulletproof glass, Skylar is immediately greeted by a Hispanic male in his mid-fifties. He's wearing an orange jumpsuit that nearly matches his natural hair color. His face is scratched up, a scar running down his left cheek from the ear to the mouth. A knife slash, one that cut deep. It is not, however, from a prison fight. That scar has been there for years on end. His dark complexion is a testament to the amount of time he's spent in the sun, and his hands are callous from hard work. Skylar: How's it goin', gramps? Gallagher: I feel like shit. Skylar: Better than usual, huh? The two share a laugh between each other. Gallagher: So, how's it been? Skylar: Alright. The old man shakes his head with a chuckle. Gallagher: You're in the main event. Think it's better than alright. Skylar: Meh, it's just matches. Nothing life-altering. The prisoner behind the glass waves his right index finger back and forth. Gallagher: Don't talk like that. I trained you since that joke of a backyard fed, Hardcore Wrestling Federation, to get to where you are now. You're a main eventer, a World Title contender in a federation that's on a global scale. You're at least helping to sell out stadiums around the world. Nice you could fly back in time to catch this little chat of ours. Skylar: Although it is odd that you choose the date you retired from professional wrestling as the date for us to have these meetings. Gallagher presses his face against the glass. The guard keeps a close eye on him, making sure that he's not about to try anything. Gallagher: I'm old. I haven't ever felt more alive since when I was in that ring. The fans, the adrenaline... nothing could ever match that. If anything, I hope that I've impressed upon you what a cursedly addictive blessing it is to be in the sport that you are in. That's why I was so worried when you faked your own retirement at the end of last year. Skylar: I retired my ring name. I never once lied. Gallagher: Yeah, you're fuckin' terrible at it. The two just sit there for a moment, both of them thinking, 'Just like old times.' Skylar: So... you're out in another 8 months. Gallagher: And I'm counting every day. It's my hope that I could call you 'champ' next time I see you. A nod from the Blind One. Skylar: Likewise. Gallagher: You got too caught up with that Violence Jack. You ascended him, you always could. You let the business get to you. Tilting his head to the right, Skylar shrugs his shoulders. Skylar: Maybe it's just me, but I would stand to look like a total freaking wuss if I was to let Shanahan get away with burning down my house and killing my fiance without beating him within an inch of his life and putting the fear of God in him. Gallagher: Guess you have a point. The business is just that, a business. That alone shows how much it's changed from the old days when it was a sport first. I never changed with the times, and look at me. Had to leave the business bitter, a shell of my former self. No money saved up, nothing left to do, in prison for nearly killing a man myself. Skylar: You need to do it in a ring so it's nice and legal. The old man smirks. Gallagher: Heh, believe me if I could go back... knowing the kind of impunity wrestlers get for drubbing each other to death I'd have had a body count higher than Ted Bundy by the time I retired. Deliberately clearing his throat, the guard points to his watch as Gallagher turns his head toward him. Gallagher: I've gotta go now. Too bad these visits never last too long. Skylar: Hell, get out of here in one piece and I'll treat you to a lobster dinner. Gallagher: You're on. Warnin' ya, though, I'm gonna gorge myself. Might be a good idea to take that champion's paycheck. Grinning, Skylar crosses his arms over his chest. Skylar: I just might. Gallagher rises from his chair. Skylar: Oh, one thing. Gallagher: Yeah? Skylar: What're you doing for work after you get out? Gallagher: Hadn't thought about it. Skylar: Fine then. I'll get you a job. PCW or PWA? The old man furrows his brow. Gallagher: You can do that? Skylar: Remember, wrestling's a business now. And businesses require networking. If there's one thing I've gained, it's contacts. Tell me what you want to do and I can get you hired. Gallagher: I'd want to wrestle. Skylar laughs. Skylar: In that case you'll be PCW's new Women's Champion. Another hearty laugh shared between the two. Gallagher: You always knew how to ruin the moment. Skylar: And I always knew that the moment was probably becoming too much of a Hallmark card than either of us could stand. Gallagher: Yeah... You take care of yourself. And finish this business with Pharaoh and win that damned PCW Title. Skylar: Will do. Gallagher turns to leave. Skylar waits until he and his accompanying officer have left their half of the room before getting up and walking out to catch his flight to South Korea for PCW Turmoil. Another 'Skylar/Pharaoh Challenge', this time the Tornado Tag Match. So far Skylar's ahead 2-1. Will he extend his lead? Will The Pharaoh even things up? How much of a factor will Miss Moonlight and Chet King play? The match isn't far off now. Only time can tell...
