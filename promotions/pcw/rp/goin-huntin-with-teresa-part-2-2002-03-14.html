Goin' Huntin' with Teresa Part 2

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-14-02 RP 2 MM Somewhere out there in the middle of no where, Teresa sits comfortably high in a tree stand while Scott, her hunting partner-in-training sits in a green fold up chair. The young OSW star sighs deeply. The overpowering boredom falls upon him like a thick blanket on a hot summer day, just as hot and as suffocating. Two and half-hours the couple has been sitting, waiting and watching for the slightest movement. Two and a half-hours and nothing. Teresa peers down to Scott and whispers. “You still awake down there?” A deep sight exits Scott’s lungs and he looks up. His brown eyes pleading. “Yeah, I’m still awake. We’ve been sitting here for hours and haven’t seen even a bird fly by. Let’s just go” By now, Teresa has climbed out of the tree stand and down the trunk of the tree. Her boots land on the ground beside Scott. She leans her shotgun against the tree. Both hands grab each side of his unbuttoned camouflage shirt his wears over his green tee shirt. With a slight tug, she pulls him closer to her. Scott’s hands hold onto her waist and their foreheads meet. “Hungry?” Teresa asks walking him backwards till his back meets the tree. “Thought we’d never eat. What you bring?” Suddenly, a rustling is heard a few yards away. Teresa quietly, but quickly releases Scott and turns around. She reaches for her gun and hands it to Scott. He holds the firearm with awkwardness. Teresa has showed him only once how to use the gun and though he’s not afraid of shooting it, he’s just scared of making an idiot of himself in front of her. Teresa steps to the side of him and points ahead where a turkey struts along as if he owned these woods. “See that?” she whispers “That’s a gobbler, but for you city folk, a turkey. Remember what I showed you and relax, aim and fire.” Teresa steps back watching as Scott brings the shotgun up to eye level. He waits a moment aiming, takes a deep breath and pulls the trigger. The bird takes flight a few feet into the air. They are large birds but they can fly to some extent. The impact of the gun kicks Scott back and he stumbles. His hand reaches for the tree stopping his fall. Teresa approaches him and carefully removes the shotgun from his shaky hands. “I think my shoulder is dislocated now. Did I shoot it?” Scott rubs his extremely sore shoulder walking toward the orange Honda Rancher. Teresa bought the four wheeler brand new a year or so back and it still runs as good as the day she brought it home. She shakes her head following him. “No, you missed. Good for a first time though. I remember my first hunting trip. It was deer season and my dad showed me this beautiful young buck. He said it would be perfect for me since I was just a little girl then. After I shot his gun, I feel flat on my ass and the deer disappeared in the woods. I had a large bruise on the side of my face for two weeks.” The story gave Scott little comfort. His shoulder still hurt like hell and he definitely will pay Teresa back for putting him through this. He opened the cooler resting on the back of the ATV and pulls out a sandwich and a can of Dr. Pepper. “Next time we come out here, we are not hunting.” Teresa sits on the seat of the four wheeler and pulls a can drink out. She smiles. He will have a bruise on his shoulder for a week or so, he just hasn’t realized it yet. “Real men hunt ya know..” She takes a sip of the Dr Pepper and pulls out a sandwich from the cooler. Scott responds in a cool-like manner, not affected by statement and with slight sarcasm. “In that case, I must not be a real man then.” “That’s why Mom don’t like you.” Teresa grins teasingly. “I hope Walden kicks your ass Sunday.” “No you don’t.” “Yes, I do. Either him or me.” “You know I like my men tough.” Scott climbs onto the ATV sharing the seat with Teresa. He sits in front of her and leans her back near the handlebars with his hands to her shoulders. “ And we all know how much a wimp Tim Walden is.” Scott finally says before leaning closer to Teresa..
