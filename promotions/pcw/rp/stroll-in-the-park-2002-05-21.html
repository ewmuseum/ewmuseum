Stroll in the park

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st May 2002 Primetime Championship Wrestling Debut Roleplay Title: A stroll in the park A pitch-black screen appears which begins to slightly flicker. After a couple of seconds, a thin but long white blur appears at the center position of the black screen. The small blur begins to slowly increase size as it glides toward the possible viewer. After a couple more seconds with the blur still coming toward us, we are able to make it out as a string of text. Given a moment, we are finally able to figure out what it is reading. The text, now in clear view reads A Price Brothers Production. After just one more second, the string of text flies at toward the screen in a tremendously fast paced motion; then disappears from site. The black screen quickly changes to a full-blown white screen. After just a mere couple of seconds, the sound of a couple of pigeons is heard. The screen begins to change from the bright white to a regular screen. As we fade in, we can see a lot of green and blue in the blur. When the camera is finally set to normal, the green and blue colors that were once a blur now reveal a beautiful outdoor park on a brightly lit sunny morning. There is a gray pathway right in the center of the screen winding off into the distance. The gray pathway consists of pebbles ranging in several shapes and sizes. Over to the left hand side of the screen are two brown and wooden park benches which have yet to be seated. Neatly mowed green lawn is surrounding the underskirts of the benches reaching of into an unnoticeable distance. To the right hand side of the screen is a large sky-blue lake in an oddly shaped circle form. Walking up the pathway toward the screen are two men. The shorter of the two looks to be about six foot. He has spiked blonde hair and a silver bar piercing in his left eyebrow. He is wearing a black T-shirt, a pair of black jeans (consisting of several silver chains) and pair of black shoes. To the left of him is a taller man looking about six foot one or two. This man a neatly combed short brown hair and is wearing a white polo T-shirts, a pair of smart-looking black pants and a shined up pair of black suede shoes. The two men, after closer examination are revealed as one of Primetime Championship Wrestling newest tag teams, The Price Brother. Chet is the man with the spiked blonde hair and gothic looking clothes, and Michael is the guy with the short brown hair. Chet has both of his hands in each pant pocket as Michael has both his arms crossed over in a folding position. The camera begins to stroll backward as the brotherly team draws nearer. With the camera backing at the same pace as the brothers, the distance between them stays around ten feet or so the whole way. Chet: “ Michael, I’m totally psyched about the match this Friday. Our debut tag team match, and you’re debut match all together!” Michael: “I know what you mean. I’m totally exited just about being in Primetime! I made it in without even wrestling professional yet!” Chet: “You’re, but only because you’re with me.” Michael: “What makes you say that?” Chet: “Oh…I must be mistaken. You see I was under the assumption that PCW only took on guys who have actually wrestled before! Oh stupid me!” The sarcasm from Chet is produced heatedly. Michael: “Alright Chet, I get your point. There’s no need for sarcasm like that.” Chet: “Lighten up Michael. Anyway, back to our match. We’re fighting a team called Politically Incorrect. The two guys are into politics and stuff; each one seems like a geek. You can relate.” Michael: ”Yeah…wait! I can relate?” Chet seems reluctant to answer and quickly changes the subject. Chet: ”So anyway, the match. One of the members from Politically Incorrect are called William S. Powell. He’s also known as ‘The Master Debator’. He’s an extremely talented high flyer like you. He’s got a series of moves that only we can dream about. His finisher is a 450 Splash. Remember you tried to learn that when we were kids and broke your leg?” Michael: ”I try not to.” Chet: ”I’ve watched this guy on video tape, you’d better be careful with him.” Michael: ”What about the other guy? Who’s he?” Chet: ”His name is ‘The Governor’ Reginald P. Lovecraft. He’s a huge guy.” Michael: ”What moves of his should I be aware of?” Chet: ”To make a long story short…everything. He’s six foot seven and just on three hundred pounds. He can pick you up and toss you anywhere he feels.” Michael: ”You know you never could give a straight answer, you always had to add something irrelevant. What moves should I specifically look out for?” Chet: ”Oh God, I could give you a mile long list but you’d forgot about half of them. My advice Michael is too steer clear of any move he tries. If he poses or anything else that gets the crowd going, don’t be a hero. Get your butt out of there and give me a few moments to calm down. Our main plan will be this. You listening?” Michael: ”Yeah, I am.” Chet: ”You take a Powell and I’ll work on Lovecraft. Take Powell down and keep him there. Knock a few moves on him then hit your second rope moonsault to keep him from getting up for a while. As soon as you do, come over and assist me with Lovecraft. We’ll team up on him then hit our double team move. After a hit my crucifix piledriver, you hit your second rope moonsault and either one of us get the pin. Can you handle that?” Michael: ”Sure thing.” The brother’s walk passed a small hotdog stand. The red and white striped cart looks disgustingly dirty. Chet: ”Hold up Michael, I’m gonna get a hotdog. You want one?” Michael: ”From a filthy stand like this? Heck no! It looks like it hasn’t been cleaned in a year!” The hotdog salesman pokes his head under the squared roof of the cart. Salesman: ”It hasn’t.” Chet laughs, as does the salesman. Michael doesn’t seem to be at all amused. Salesman: ”What can I get ya sonny?” Chet pulls out a brown leather wallet from the back of his black jeans. Chet: ”Just a hotdog buddy.” Salesman: ”Comin’ right up.” The hotdog seller begins to fiddle about at the underneath of his cart. The camera concentrates on both Chet and Michael. Chet: ”You know, if we win this match to go through we’ve still got to face some incredible opponents.” Michael: ”Such as?” Chet: ”The Heartbreakers, Pretty Cool Guys and our old buddies from the academy; The Dark Messiahs. That’s just naming a few.” Michael: ”Erm…” Chet: ”You nervous?” Michael: ”…yeah.” The scene comes to an end.
