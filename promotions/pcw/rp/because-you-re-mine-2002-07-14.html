Because you're mine

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/14/02 The doorbell rings on a quiet little house out in the middle of nowhere. At the sudden noise a sleeping man jerks......tumbles, and plummets off the couch crashing to the floor. Clyde: Rotten son of a......! The demon known only as Clyde shakes his head, clearing the cobwebs.......he then stands, glaring at the door which rings again. Clyde: Who the hell? On the outside a finger pushes the bell........again, again, until a crash of wood interrupts. The interloper whips his head back, staring wide eyed at the massive blade sticking out through the door itself. Man: Whoa, man. Clyde yanks his machete back out through the door, then peers through the hole he has just created in it with a scowl. Clyde: .......well, can't say I expected to see YOU here. What do you want? We zoom out a bit, to gaze upon the figure outside......dressed all in black, a cowboy hat atop his head. His face is weathered, showing the travels of many a mile........he is none other than Johnny Cash. Cash: Let me in, boy. Begrudgingly, the demon accepts.......opens the door, which ends up falling off the hinges anyway. Johnny Cash steps over it, looking around as he enters the house. Clyde: Pardon the mess. The Man in Black looks upon the carnage around him........bodies strewn in all directions, hanging from the ceiling. Bones crunch beneath his boots as he walks........Johnny raises an eyebrow to Clyde. Cash: Interesting place. Clyde: So what brings you here, Johnny? Cash: (Dusting himself off) Well, I'm supposed to be interviewing you for MTV. I don't really know why, I do what I can for money these days. Clyde: Oh I get it, this is one of those spoof deals. We're spoofing Duke's highly crappy interview with Gilbert Godfrey. I gotcha. The singer looks on quizzically.......then shakes his head. Cash: I'm not here to break kayfabe, I'm just here to interview. Clyde: So interview. And sit down, you're making me nervous. Johnny does as asked, brushing a skull off the worn and tattered couch. He writes in a small journal, then speaks. Cash: Mr. Clyde.......uh........what is your last name anyway? Clyde: ..........I don't know. Cash: ........okay. Mr. Clyde, you work for Primetime Championship Wrestling. I don't follow it truthfully, tell me what you do there. Clyde: I kill people. Cash: .........you kill people. Clyde: Yes. Clyde and Johnny Cash stare blankly at each other........after a long moment the crooner shakes his head again. Cash: You kill people in a wrestling promotion. That's just dandy. Clyde: Isn't it? Cash: Eh.......moving right along. Uh.......do you enjoy killing people? Clyde: Well certain people irritate me. Like the guy I'm currently feuding with, Duke Williams.......he fired me and my friends, then sat around and laughed about it. When we got our revenge on him he punished us all by making us watch an interview with Gilbert Godfrey. Cash: .........he sounds like a mean bugger. Clyde: That was cruel and unusual. Cash: Uh, let's see.......not gonna ask your favorite color, I know it's blood red or whatever you youngsters like these days. Clyde: Actually it's light mauve, but close enough. Cash looks on........writes something else on his pad, then continues. Cash: If you could be an animal what would it be? Clyde: A bat. That way I'd be blind and wouldn't have to watch Duke's crappy interviews and crappier commentating. Cash: Mm.......ok. And I'm to hype a match you have coming up. Clyde: Eh, it bores me. We have Tom Lexian the current champ, who's suddenly decided he's a member of the holy Roman church and is going to liberate Palestine from the Muslim infidels. I'm shocked he hasn't drawn the wrath of Chef yet. Cash: .........odd. Clyde: Chris Chase, I don't even know what he thinks he's doing.........he used to call himself a messiah, but we crucified him and ended that. So now he calls himself a saint. Cash: Yeah. Clyde: And of course Duke, who I understand is a fan of yours. He's a big fat drunken cowboy, suffering from gout I'd imagine, gets his jollies by assaulting women in showers and beating up little girls.........and has interviews with Gilbert Godfrey. Yet he's cheered. Johnny Cash shakes his head once again, grinning slightly. Cash: Sounds like my kind of guy. The burned man stares a hole through the singer........his eyes piercing. Cash: .........see something green? Clyde: No, but I can see something red all over this room if you say anything positive about Duke in my interview again. The Man in Black glares at the demon. Cash: Sounded like a threat. Clyde: Maybe it was. Cash: Bring it on. Suddenly the old man kips to his feet, striking a kung fu pose. He assumes the position of the crane, the bat, the rat and several other animals as Clyde nods knowingly. Clyde: I see. You have trained well..........but it matters not! Have a taste of my superior technique! The demon leaps forward, throwing several lightning quick strikes accompanied by snapping sound effects seemingly out of nowhere. Johnny Cash however moves just as fast, blocking all the punches. Cash: You're pretty good old son. Clyde: If you start singing Devil Went Down to Georgia I'll kill you where you stand. Cash: Like to see you try. The elderly singer in black jumps into the air.......defying gravity, he flips several times then lands behind Clyde, throwing out a kick and nailing him in the back of the head. Clyde: Oof! Clyde stumbles forward, flying through a set of glass bottles and vanishing. Brushing glass off his jacket, Cash marches forward. Cash: Your fighting skills are weak, ol' boy. Just as Johnny reaches the table it flips up, catching him in the teeth. Cash: Agh! Cash stumbles away, as once more Clyde leaps out hitting a kick to the side of his head. He then rebounds all the way across the room, ending up on the far wall which he runs straight up. Clyde: (Making Curly Howard noises) Woo woo woo! Johnny Cash looks up unbelievingly as Clyde runs up to the roof, then across it, right over his head and drops upon him. Clyde: DIE! The burned man produces a knife, attempts to jab it in Johnny Cash's eye. However the singer rolls over top of him, punching him several times in the face. Cash: (Punching) Punk.....ass.......bitch! Cash attempts another punch, but Clyde bucks him off. The two men turn to each other.......nod, and charge. They each fly through the air, then as they reach each other stop dead in mid flight. Clyde: Ha! Cash: Ha! Clyde and Johnny begin striking each other, their fists becoming blurs. Neither man gains an advantage, for the better part of 30 seconds they trade blocks and punches. Finally Johnny throws a left which slips through Clyde's guard and hits him in the nose.......a second punch follows. Dazed, the burned man shakes his head as blood drips from him........then points up. Clyde: See that? Cash: (Looking up) What? At the distraction Clyde swings his foot and kicks Johnny Cash straight between the legs. He falls from his odd state of suspended gravity, hitting the ground hard........Clyde looks all around, then down before shrugging and falling as well. Clyde: Argh. The demon stomps angrily up to the old man, putting him in a headlock. Cash: (Panting) Gonna kill me now? Clyde: (Panting) It'd be too easy. I'm gonna keep you around, torture you. You're gonna be my plaything for a while, Johnny.......my little reminder to Duke. Cash: Ugh. Johnny Cash attempts to struggle, but finally passes out from exertion. Clyde grimaces into the camera, squeezing him tighter. Clyde: MERCIFULLY FADE TO BLACK ALREADY!!!!!!!! ..........and we mercifully fade to black............
