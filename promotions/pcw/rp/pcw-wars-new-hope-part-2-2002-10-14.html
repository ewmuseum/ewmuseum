PCW Wars: New Hope Part 2

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14 - 10 - 02 A desert known as 'No Man's Land' serves as our opening backdrop. A dune sea stretches as far as the eye can see, miles upon miles of sand. In the distance our two heroes’, the droid duo of Rush-3PO and R2 D2 scurry along the wasteland before them, leaving behind the wreckage of their transportation. Rush-3PO: 'R2 that's another fine mess you have gotten us into!' The R2 unit replying with a series of beeping sounds. Rush-3PO: 'Damn speak English dude - Anyways I need to rest otherwise my funky joints are going to freeze up!' As the taller android sits, basking in the surrounding landscape, his companion shot off like a locomotive, making a sharp right he turns in the direction of the rocky desert mesas. Rush-3PO: 'Where's the fire?' A series of electronic beeps pour from the R2 unit, who simply ignores the half-hearted, pleads of his metallic friend. Rush-3PO: 'What settlement? Watch your tongue bitch... I mean go that way, you'll be nothing more than a trashcan within a day, you nearsighted scrap pile!' said the robot with a hiss. R2 retorted rudely and makes haste in his chosen path whilst Rush-3PO pursues the opposite direction. The rolling droid trudges towards the towering mesas and the bronze Rush-3PO decides to tackle a series of endless dunes, peering back he snarls at his disobedient partner. Rush-3PO: 'I knew I shouldn't have listened to a food-blender - That little twerp will find my cast iron boot up his motherboard if we ever cross paths again' said the droid in a huff of anger and frustration. Rush-3PO glared at a reflective glint of light in the distance, which revealed a large object moving towards him. Rush-3PO: 'Wait... What's that... Transport... In the name of Hubbard I'm saved!!!' The bronze coloured translator droid waved frantically, yelling at the transport he tries to draw its attention. The scene switches back to gigantic rock formations that barricade in the R2 D2 droid, the place is shroud in mist and eerie sounds of creatures stirring but the small unit continues his journey convinced he is alone. Waddling further on, his progress is momentarily cut short by a pebble that hurtled from the tip of the canyon. Brushing off the near escape, the R2 unit notices a flicker of light followed by a pair of glowing eyes lurking within the shadows and tries an evasive maneuver, only to be struck by a magnetic ray with paralyzing effects. R2 D2 falls to the ground with a heavy thud before being swamped by cautious creatures in hooded gowns known to the natives of this planet as Jawas. A select group of these Jawas heave the robot onto their shoulders and head towards a behemoth of a vehicle that stands four-storey's high, placing the droid under a hoover-like tube, the mass of metal suddenly springs off the ground. The interior of this craft resembles a scrap-yard as the floor is littered with parts of droids and old circuitry. R2 D2 flickered back into life and activated a light on his forehead, which penetrates the darkness, allowing him to freely roam. Rush-3PO: 'R2 you old dog, it's you, it's you!' The robots embrace; well Rush-3PO actually wrapped his arms around the cylinder frame of his comrade. Changing back to an exterior view, we witness the vehicle lumbering towards the planets two glowing suns, which have began to set behind a mountains ridge. Returning back to the wreckage of the life-pod, we can clearly make out a team of Stormbringers searching the aftermath. One of these Stormbringers with the nametag 'Bobby' yells to another officer who stands feet away. Bobby: 'Someone was here, checkout the tracks!' A second trooper followed the tracks, finding metal fragments imbedded into the sand. Trooper: 'Look sir.... You know what this means?' Bobby: 'Something to trade with those Jawas!' Trooper: 'Hmm-... Err-... Not quite sir, more like droids!' Heading back to the shenanigans of the droids we watch on as the vehicle known as the Sand Crawler crashes to a halt outside the farming residence of the Skywalkers. A door opens with a blinding light, followed closely by a descending ramp way as a hoard of droids are brought out into the Tatooine atmosphere and lined up by their captors, this collection of robots include the unlikely combination of Rush-3PO and R2 D2. The Jawas talk gibberish between one another as their potential customers inspect them individually. The alcoholic uncle of Alexander Skywalker, Owen limped up to the droids, drooling over the casing and blinking violently as the sun dehydrates him, he tries to judge those droids up for sale. Aunt Beru: 'Owen we need a translator and be sure to get one that speaks Boca~!' A. Skywalker: 'Doesn't look much choice....' Owen: 'We-... Err-... Need one of those-... One that can make a mean cocktail and speak some-... Speak Boca~!' As the Jawa leader squeaks in his own unintelligent language, Rush-3PO interrupts with an introduction that captures the attention of the staggering Uncle Owen. Rush-3PO: 'Yo white man, lay some skin on me - I could do this job standing on my head and I make a sweet 'Black Russian' cocktail if you smell what I'm cooking!' Uncle Owen's eyes light up at the very mention of alcohol when suddenly his interfering wife bellows on the transaction. Aunt Beru: 'OWEN HE NEEDS TO SPEAK BOCA REMEMBER?!?' Uncle Owen turns back to the droid who tilts his head and whispers into his ear, unbeknownst to the surrounding party of people. Rush-3PO: 'Typical wife, huh?!? Well give me a week and I'll eradicate your problem brother, can we say sale then?' The dozy Owen smiles and negotiated a deal over the translator droid with its present owner, in this case the Jawa leader. Uncle Owen then proceeded to purchase a red astro-droid similar in appearance to that of R2 D2 as his blue counter-part bounced about trying to be seen by the Skywalker household. Uncle Owen: 'Alex, take these into the garage and give them a clean! Those damn Jawas could at the very least wash their hands when dealing with my goods' A. Skywalker: 'Alcoholic to racist to damn slave driver, sure you don't want me become a Jedi Knight in my spare time too?' Uncle Owen: 'You can play Jedi's with your friends once your chores are done. Now get on with it lad...' Alexander Skywalker waltzes off towards the compound followed by his new apprentices Rush-3PO and the red astro-droid as R2 D2 bellowed a series of high-pitched noises. Restrained by a slimy Jawa, R2 watches on as his age old friend heads for his new life. Owen continued to negotiate the purchase when without warning a metallic plate shot off the head of the red astro-droid as it sparked wildly. A. Skywalker: 'WHAT THE F....' Uncle Owen: 'Well I'll be damned - You guys have just tried to screw me, what's this, the new Front Office?' A. Skywalker: 'Uncle Owen, its a bad motivator....' Uncle Owen: 'Don't laugh you ugly mother f.... Why if I were two foot shorter I'd kick you in the nads but since I'm an honest man I'll settle for a replacement' The Jawa leader ushers the blue R2 D2 towards his customer reluctantly, trading both units in a straight swap. The small trashcan looking droid shuffled forward under a torrent of whistles, his chest plate alight with flashing L.E.D's. A. Skywalker: 'Uncle Owen take that one!' Rush-3PO: 'Uh, he's a fine bit of kit sir. Worked with this geezer before and I can safely say he's a hard worker, first class condition, he be a sweet bargain!' Alexander Skywalker takes the bickering droids into the garage, which is a dark, musty place full to the brim with battered appliances and other discarded items. The two droids enjoy an oil bath as the young Skywalker dwells on his misfortune of living on a heap of rock with no real future prospects. A. Skywalker: 'Farming like totally sucks, I wonder if the Sand People will let me become a rebel like them?' murmured the miffed teenager. The droids were dried off and Alex took a wrench in which he plans to fine- tune them. Clawing the wrench around the cylinder frame of R2 D2, Alexander Skywalker chips a small metal fragment causing the droid to play projected footage of Princess Anarchy, the three dimensional view flickers in the light as the Rebel Senator addresses the listener. Anarchy: 'Help me, Charlie Loc Kenobi. You're like my only hope!' A. Skywalker: 'What's this... Tell me it isn't pornography?' R2 D2 sheepishly spins around answering with beeps only Rush-3PO can translate. Rush-3PO: 'Listen you DOS running piece of crap - He asked a question... What be that shit?' The footage replays over again courtesy of the little droid. Anarchy: 'Help me, Charlie Loc Kenobi. You're my only hope. Help me Charlie Loc Kenobi you're my only hope!' Rush-3PO: 'She be some bird we pimped ages ago. R2 says its a malfunction, old data. Pay it no mind' The young Skywalker stares fixated at the beautiful Princess Anarchy Organa. A. Skywalker: 'Who is she?' Rush-3PO: 'Some babe on our last voyage dude - Well I think, however if my hard drive serves me right, she was of some importance....' A. Skywalker: 'Is there anymore to this recording?' Reaching out to touch the R2 unit, it retaliates by scrabbling away frantically. Screaming with a hoard of beeps, its partner in crime Rush-3PO steps in. Rush-3PO: 'Behave! Your gonna get us into trouble and what’s this about being the damn property of Charlie Loc Kenobi, who be this white dude?' A. Skywalker: 'I wonder if he means that Kenobi guy who runs around like Jesus Christ, wearing a itchy brown gown held together with a piece of string!' Rush-3PO: 'Well If you take off these restraining bolts, he may be able to bring up the full message?!?' Removing the bolts, R2 D2 begins to play the exact same footage. Anarchy: 'Help me, Charlie Loc-....' Suddenly the little droid does a full three-sixty and wheel spins away, cutting the recording short and high-tailing it from the scene whilst screaming thunderous beeps. It's owner Alexander Skywalker and Rush-3PO watch on in amazement as the unit vanishes into the darkness of the desolate desert, still roaring its monotone voice box. A. Skywalker: 'You must be kidding me - What did that runaway tool box say?' Rush-3PO: 'Hmm-... FFFFFFRRRRRREEEEEEDDDDDDOOOOOOMMMMMM!!!!!!' A. Skywalker: 'Well we had better go after him!' - To be continued -
