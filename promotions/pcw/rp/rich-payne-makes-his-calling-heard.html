Rich Payne... makes his calling heard..

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th June? Community General Hospital...The very place where Dr Mark Sloan works... a man who is usually for fighting the criminals of this world and placing its murderers behind bars! Many had assumed this would be the case in all scenarios that take place upon his watchful eye... but alas, there are some moments in the movement of time... when the Good Guys aren't everything that they are made out to be. There are moments in the mans life... where he wants to take a ride on that Wild Side... and even at the age of two hundred and twenty three years of age... Mark Sloan will always want to be able to experience that ride on the wild side, on at least one occassion... maybe even more if he could get away with it. Today, the world was in shock... the world was in nothing but complete awe... and nobody could understand the true motive that had come with the sudden decision to kidnap Ryan Moore from the waiting room as Rich Sherwin made his uncanny escape from the tiny confinements of that very room. However.. we had heard Sherwin say that he was going to attempt to grab his Tag Team partner back from the very jaws that contain him! We heard Rich Sherwin state... that he was going to go all out... and bring Ryan Moore back to the business with him...We heard Rich Sherwin state... that he was going to do the very things that superheroic people like 'Rent-A-Hero' could only dream of..We heard Rich Sherwin state... everything listed above once all of the cameras had turned off and all the microphones had been packed away in their little black boxes! Now, we have heard the apparent movement of Rich Sherwin... we have heard of his apparent movement with regards on what to do with Ryan Moore, but the very question will still remain... at this second in time, the second after that second in time... and by God, its going to remain, the second after the second of the second in time! Phew... there really has got to be an easier way to convey all of this information through without having to worry about all the... tongue twisting words that will generally make themselves known at this very cunning point in terminalistic time. Does the word I just used even exist? Did I steal that one from out of my new edition of 'The Really,Really Big Book of superlong words to make you appear smart!'? The answer to that question... is something that you're going to have to look up as you browse through all your copies of the book to make sure that it really was taken from page 32, section 5:A. As we look ahead at the episode of the show that will be on hand... one can only begin to wonder about the things that are going to happen with Rich Sherwin and his ingenius plan of coming to rescue Ryan Moore! Stay tuned... to find out what kinda cracka shit is going down next... on PRETTY COOL G!!! Bonjour!Now, I'm sure that every single one of you will understand that word as being 'Hello'... or 'Welcome'... or something to do with a welcoming, I'm not too sure these days since Ryan Moore ruined the last French Dictionary that I had bothered to purchase from one of the many bookstores that litter this tiny little planet Earth. As the narrator kindly explained in the opening to this very drama filled episode, Ryan Moore had indeed been kidnapped by the very Dr Mark Sloan and his son, Steve. I don't know what things that these evil people intend to do to my friend down below... I have no personal thoughts about what they could be doing at this very moment in time... however, I did know that these freaks needed to be stopped... at any and all costs... for today, I was going to be known... as Rich Payne... the man... with nothing to lose. I will reopen the story in the confinements of a small room located on the ground floor of the building. It had been my place of hiding since escaping the claws of Dr Mark Sloan and his son on the first day... and I was sure that the time here wasn't going to get any easier. It was a Cold day in hell... the only problem with the situation now being that I was a few thousand feet short of the firey base known as the Demon Lords home. I looked around at my current surroundings... there wasn't a whole lot to see, and nothing that I could really get to use as a sufficient weapon against the outside forces of the hospital... however, I decided to get accustomed to the small confinements of the Supply Room and thought about bringing somebodys 20yr old sister here when everything was all done and dusted... of course, don't let the peverse thoughts of Rich Payne move you away from this very emotional piece. With that in mind, I grabbed the first things on the first shelf inside the room... to my amazement, I had managed to grab three unused syringes... and not one of them had been able to pierce my skin and infect me with some kind of disease... yes, it sounds very stupid... and yes, I had already stated that none of this equipment had been used.. but you never know with these freak infested fictional hospitals... anything... and I mean... ANYTHING could be hiding around the corner... could be hiding under your bed... or even in your DAMNED AFRO... ARGH, ARGH, ARGH... so just you remember, the next time you think about needles, you look forth to the Rich Payne saga and you think about the monkey lice-spiders that WILL come to your hair! Picturing the rather primitive needles as being the throwing knives that I'd seen as circus folk travel around the country, I assumed that this would be my big shot... that this would be my only chance at being able to rescue my friend from the abyss that is known as the demon demonage of demons. True, this case had just turned into something that proved I should join I friends Michelle Steel and Danielle Stone at being unable to tell the difference between a hospital and a drug raid... but the difference was never there, for I had been duped by the front of a hospital... they had been duped by the word 'Hospital'. I carefully began to open the door to the supply closet a little... keeping all noise down to a bare minimal as my head stuck out from behind the wooden device. Looking from side to side of the small corridor.. from the wall to the fire exit about five feet away on my left, I made a dash out of the supply room, jumping over to the other side of the corridor and getting my breath back. It had been said that the first movement was always going to be the worst in this sequence of events... and the first movement had indeed been the worst, the first injury of the mission had been sustained... I looked down to that bastard of a wound and I said... 'Hey Mr Papercut... don't hurt me anymore please... or I'm going to plaster you up.' Shaking my hand around to make the pass subside, I began to slink up against the wall in true 'Solid Snake' fashion... gee, 'Rent-A-Hero' would be proud of my heroics... and the chances of him being ever happier that somebody could walk in his footsteps after I come to save Ryan Moore... would be an even bigger feather in my cap. As I continued to slink up the wall, sliding to the end of it with every passing step, I began to realize the truth behind everything. I peered around the corner and saw the ugly nurse seated at her station... some twenty feet away from me or what-not... there was a couple of goons in white suits standing a few feet ahead of her. I glanced down at all of the ammo that was left in my hands... 'Just the three shots...', I mumbled to myself as the intensity surrounding this situation began to intensity that little more. I looked at the clock at the other end of the corridor... hearing the sudden... 'tick'... 'tick'... 'tick'... of the second hand. The moment for the first strike was now... and if I was even going to think about taking any more time with this... well, the chances of me being able to save Ryan Moore may as well be totally forgotten about. Soon after debating everything, I stepped out from behind the corner and ran at the two guards switching to bullet time, jumping in the air and moving with all the graces that Max Payne had in his video game. I was moving through the air at a consistant rate as my aggressors struggled to turn around to face me. It was the moment of truth that was going to shape the history of the world as we knew it.. I threw the first dart at one of the guards... it totally missed...I threw the second dart at the other guard... it totally missed...I threw the third dart at the totally ugly woman behind the desk... her psycho-ugliness forced the dart to veer off to the right of the screen and connect with the bulletin board. I hit the floor and looked up in shock, trying to taking in the fact that I had been unable to hit any of the guards with the darts that I had thrown through in uncanny fashion. The two men reached for what should have been their hip holsters... and my eyes began to close.. when we realized that nobody within this hosptial was even allowed to have a hip holster with them. The first guard ran over to my body as I quickly trying to run through 'Bullet Time' again... using the super effective 'Kung Fu' mod, I was able to force the first enemy to the ground with the educated use of my feet before doing one of those really cool things... where you are on the floor and like, jump back up to your feet... I think its called a 'Kip Up' or something, but regardless, that was one move that both my mom and pa were against in the early years of my teenage life. The second guard began to run toward me, I came out of bullet time and stepped to the right as he came through with a flurry of fists. As he went sliding past, I knew that it was time for me to hit... the MOST DEVESTATING MOVE IN THE WORLD... my right arm was thrust out infront of me, I aimed it at the second guard, palm facing of course... and took a few steps back, the distance between myself and that ugly bitch being a little non-existant. This was not the moment of Max Payne that would take my interest in the video game to an all new high... instead, this was back to the very movements that Goku had taught me on his visit to Planet Earth. I continued to look over to the guard and smirked a little. 'Now... you're going to find out why I'm going to win the Battle Royal for 'Pretty Cool Guys' this Sunday Night at the 'Gold and Glory' Pay Per View...' I made the remark in the coldest of circumstances, laughing a little more as I tried to focus my 'Ki' on this amazing foe. I spread my legs so that they fell shoulder width apart and yelled out, immitating the very same movements that one would normally make when firing a general Ki Blast. The sound effect for the explosion played, but the guard was still standing tall, looking as though nothing even came close to touching him. I looked over to the character in question and stomped my foot on the floor for a few moments. The bad guy turned around and I looked him in the face with a stern eye. 'Is this part where I'm supposed to fall over to pretend to be dead?', he asked, the very essence of newbie-ness following his every movement around the immediate location. I nodded my head in the sheer movement of disgust as snickerd to myself as he dropped down to the floor in dramatic fashion. I turned my head over to the only other character in the room, and tried to shield my eyes from the glare of ugliness that was staring at my face, reaching over the table, I grabbed the women by her top and looked her in the eye, forcing myself to do so... with the most dominant of fears over-casting my whole persona. 'Now... you're going to tell me where I can find Ryan Moore... since you took him to wherever...', I growled, sounding as nasty as a three year old boy. Regardless of this tame element of fear I was creating, it was enough to make her scared and pass out under the pressure. I looked around in the Main Corridor of the building, dropping down to my knees and looking up to the ceiling... the end of the segment was coming.... I let out a shrill cry of 'RYYYYYYYYAAAAAAANNNNNNNN' as the scene slowly... but surely, faded off into the darkness that is black.
