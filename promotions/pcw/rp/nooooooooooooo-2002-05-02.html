NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/02 Never give a manipulative, arrogant, repugnant Scientologists power. Something known now that the PCW fans wished that had known before Spring Fling, and if they had they probably would have chipped in together and bought the company themselves. Ever since Spring Fling, the Gold Patron Meritorious has been un-controllable. With the Continental Title around his waist he had ego enough, but throw in a 20% ownership of Primetime Championship Wrestling and the Commissioner’s post that went with it, Kannon’s head has swelled to enormous proportions. With him he has the CEO of the Federation, Tyler Nelson. Add to that the Personnel Director Tim Walden to couple with Kannon’s match making abilities, and the Ultimate Authority have been able to sculpt PCW in their image....One that certainly doesn’t match up to the fan’s view of how PCW should be. So far he has booked himself in very few Title defences, always making sure the odds are in his favour each time. He has booked a 4 Corners Match involving 3 teams from the Ultimate Authority against Duke Williams and Jordan X Shabazz.....But now he is taking it even further, by making himself the Special Referee for Duke William’s #1 Contenders match for Xavier’s Continental Title....Where not many are holding out hope of him officiating fairly. “So how many of them have signed up for it?” Although we are yet to see any visuals, we hear the confident, obnoxious voice of the aforementioned Commissioner talking. His inquiry is followed by a rant back from what sounds like Tim Walden, the tinnyness of the voice hinting that the Scientologist is engaged in a phone conversation with him. “All *BEEP*’ing 5 of those mutha*BEEP*’ers have put their *BEEP*’ing signature on their *BEEP*’ing *BEEP*ey contracts so we are now *BEEP*’ing free to do what the *BEEP* we want with the bunch of *BEEP*ers!!!” As the shot starts to fade in we are shown the Hubbardville branch of Xavier’s Commissioner’s Office located in John Travolta Towers just off Dianetic’s Drive in the Financial District of Hubbardville. Xavier : “Good......This should show them all......Duke, the fans.....The whole WORLD that I am a man of no fear and that Hubbard’s Continental Champion will go down in history as the hardest fighting PCW wrestler ever...” The Scientologist sits at his Mahogany desk, hand-hand crafted by Jenning’s of Hubbardville in their craft shop just off Wehatenicolekidman Avenue in the lower East side of the city. On the desk in front of him in a large pile of paper work with FOR FUSION stamped on the top and he is observing the top sheet during his conversation. “Well Whoooo *BEEP*’iing Hooooo!!! *BEEP*’ing good for you, I get to do all the *BEEP*’ing work to make you look good.... *BEEP*er!” Xavier : “Bye Tim.” The Gold Patron Meritorious reaches forward and presses the button next to line one on his phone and just as Walden goes to retort, with a click, his line is severed and Kannon can go back to surveying the paperwork. Xavier : “Now....In what order shall this plan of mine happen. Do I open up with the big one to really shock them....Or do I bring them slowly, saving the best for last? Maybe throw everything in at once....” He sits at the desk sorting through the literature in front of him, obviously planning something for Friday that by default won’t sit well with either President Duke Williams or the fans. Kannon is dressed a lot smarter than we usually see him out of his Corporate life. He is dressed in a well cut Black jacket and trousers and under it he wears a $3000 Kashmir rollneck sweater from Page’s of Lisa Marie Presley Boulevard. His long, auburn hair is neatly tied back, without a stray strand in sight which only helps showcase his bright Green eyes all the more. Xavier : “Ah *BEEP* it....I’m the Commissioner and I can do what the hell I like, anyone has a problem with it I’ll call up some no-Gimmick “I just want to be myself” guy from the bush league to go over them in a Dark Match...” At his own mention of the power he has he smirks to himself, sliding the pile of papers out of his way and rising up from his huge chair and stretching, lifting his arms high above his head and pushing his head back as far as it will go. After he finishes extending his 6’2 frame to it’s limits he walks across his office to a Mahogany wardrobe, with similar carving as his desk. Xavier : “The paper work......The work.....Bores me, now....THIS is going to be worth all the planning though...” He opens the wardrobe to show an entire rack of suits hung up, a lot still having the price tickets hanging from them which the camera zooms in on and follows along the length of the rack. $6,000 $7,500 $2,000 $4,750 $5,625 Xavier : “Hmmmm, next time I let Eleanor take me shopping I’d better bring my own Credit Card, I think I may have maxed out the company’s....” As the camera pans along the Blues, the Blacks and the Greys one item seems to stand out and Kannon’s hand heard to it, it’s flashes of White acting as a beacon for the Scientologist. Xavier : “Ahhh.....All the suits, the shirts, the sweaters, the $500 ties....I’ll probably wear them once and forget to pick them up from the Dry Cleaners....Now THIS, oh, this garment will hold many fond memories for me.” His hand grabs to top of a hanger and he pulls out a White and Black striped referee’s shirt but with the usual PCW logo that adorns the left breast pocket it has the Ultimate Authority’s U.A logo emblazoned upon it. Holding the shirt up for him to observe with relish he walks back to his desk and removes the hanger, casually tossing it to his hat stand as he passes, the hook catching on one of the pegs perfectly. As the Scientologist falls back into his chair which looks the epitome of comfort, he lays the shirt on the desk in front of him. Xavier : “I do believe that this will be the first ever PCW match I have had the honour of refereeing....Better make sure the old reflexes are up to scratch...” As quickly as he can he taps his open palm onto the shirt, doing as fast a count as possible, almost seeming shocked himself at how quickly he was able to carry out the task. Xavier : “Wow......That will come in handy. Now, for a Duke count...” He nods his head 3 times, as it to help pace himself for the count, working out the rhythm in his head before counting another 3 on the desk but this time with intervals pushing 2 seconds. As he finishes he looks pleased with the count, trying to keep the pacing in his head. Xavier : “There.....Okay, THE plan is now all finalised.....I have my referee’s shirt and my counts worked out....What else is there left to do?” Suddenly, right on cue a crackle is head and over the intercom speaks Xavier’s Secretary at his Hubbardville office, Hannah Alley, sister of famous Scientologist Kirstey Alley. She immediately starts to speak in an alarmed voice, not even checking to whether Xavier is busy or not. Hannah : “Mr Kannon Sir....I have ju-” Even through her urgency Kannon cuts her off, not looking to happy that his quiet moment of contemplation was interrupted so quickly. Xavier : “Hannah....Please, I know you are Kirstey’s sister...But really, you are the disappointment of the Alley family, which is why you are working here. Now what have I told you about always asking me if I am busy?” Her panicked voice replies back, trying to get her information through as quickly as possible. Hannah : “Sorry Mr Kannon, it won’t happen again...But I have been checking the list of new subscribers an-” A rather annoyed Scientologists again cuts Kirstey’s sister off, letting out a sigh at her lack of protocol and rolling his eyes as if to say “Why can’t she be more like her sister...” Xavier : “Aren’t you forgetting something?” As the Gold Patron Meritorious leans forward so his head is almost over the intercom there is a pause of a few seconds before his secretary replies with the desired words. Hannah : “Am I disturbing you?” With this a look of contentment falls back down the Gold Patron Meritorious’ face and he leans back in his chair and casually replies. Xavier : “I got time...” Hannah : “As I was saying...I have been going through the list of new subscribers and I found something you might want to see....It’s very troubling in the least....” With this the sound of a mechanism kicking in is heard and a monitor lowers it’s self from the ceiling and stops a foot or so off the desk to Xavier’s desk, the technology innovated by Grey’s Electronics of Hubbardville. On the screen is a long list of names which Xavier scrolls down.... Trent, G.K - Lawyer : Harvard Graduate. Harmon, B.E - Judge : Been favourable in Scientology’s court cases. Then as Kannon’s eyes get to the one highlighted name on the screen his eyes widen and his jaw dropped. He looks like he wants to speak but something is blocking the sound and what looks like a tear is forming in the corner of his left eye. Williams, D - Wrestler and Broadcaster : Pioneer of Malice Style. As Xavier looks blankly at the screen the concerned voice of Kirstey Alley’s sister speaks over the intercom again. Hannah : “Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon? Are you okay? What do you want me to do?” Still staring at the highlighted row on the screen, the hated words “Malice Style”, a tear starts to roll down his cheek. Xavier : “No..........” He starts to shake his head, trying to convince himself that it is a joke, that the PCW President and his arch nemesis hasn’t infected his beloved religion. Xavier : “No..............” Again the concerned voice of his secretary crackles through. Hannah : “Mr Kannon....Sir? Who do you want me to call? Are you there? Kannon’s face contorts, disgusted and upset as a tear makes a track down each of his screwed up cheeks as the camera zooms in on... Williams, D - Wrestler and Broadcaster : Pioneer of Malice Style. .....As the shot fades out. Xavier: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! With this anguished scream we hit Black and as the wail dies out we only here the concerned voice of Xavier’s secretary, sounding as if she expected this reaction. “Er........Mr Kannon? I’ll......Er.....I’ll make some calls....”
