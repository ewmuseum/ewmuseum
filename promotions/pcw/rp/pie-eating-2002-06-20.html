Pie Eating

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 20 [The scene opens on the Pittsburgh Steelers Logo. The red, blue, and gold stars mesh together in a semi-circle which make absolutely no sense as to why they are named The Steelers. In fact, they got the name because of the vast amounts of steel factories that were in the city, but now there are none. So, why still call them the Pittsburgh Steelers? Maybe because of tradition or maybe it's because the city is too damn lazy to go and change the name. Either way what the Steelers and 'Heavy Metal' Harry have in common is that he constantly wears a replica helmet, named Jerome Bongus, which is a new model of his once destroyed bong. The scene zooms out to show one half of the Cowabunga Connection, Harry, standing with a bunch of teenagers, whom all giggle with their eyes bloodshot. One of the teenagers, wearing a Linkin Park T-shirt, a pair of cargo shorts, and Vans tennis shoes, begins to laugh] Teenager in Linkin Park Shirt: There is no way that Yoda can kick my ass, man. [Harry, wearing his usual Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots begins shaking his head in total disagreement] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yoda would make you his barnyard bitch, man. Teenager in Linkin Park Shirt: No way, man! He's got skill with the light sabre and he's got great power of the force but I'll kick him in his gonads, man. Harry: Gonads? Teenager in Linkin Park Shirt: Yeah, man. Gonads. Harry: You wouldn't get near his stomach anyway, man. [The group of teens bust out in laughter. Across from the Linkin Park kid a pimple faced kid with a Yankees cap backwards takes control] Teen In Yankees Cap: Dude, your gonads aren't in your stomach. Gonads are your balls. Harry: Gonads are balls, man? Teen In Yankees Cap: Yeah. Harry: Holy S**t, man! So that's what all that Gonad talk was about in Biology class. If I would have known that I would have passed Biology and not have been stuck in my sixth year of high school, man. [The group of teens bust into a large amount of laughter once again. As they laugh a shadowed figure comes over, showing that the young men with their older stoner friend have hidden themselves under bleachers to smoke some pot] Teen In Linkin Park Shirt: Busted, man!! [A light surfer chuckle fills underneath the bleachers as Harry sighs with relief, thinking that they were all busted and he'd end up in jail. That's not a good thing for a high profiled professional wrestler like himself. The shadowed figure walks under the bleachers revealing himself to be the other half of The Cowabunga Connection, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. He wears a PCW T-shirt, a pair of blue and yellow shorts, and black sandals. He has his Oakley sunglasses atop his bright blonde hair, showing off his baby blue eyes that always catch the ladies off guard] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDES!! [The group of teens all smile and give props to Tommy as he comes underneath the bleachers. That's one thing about Tommy, he is just a magnet for people no matter what he does. He could be wearing a scuba outfit with garlic hanging from his crotch and he could still score girls numbers and have every guy in the room want to be like him] TTT: This is a most gnarly get together. I'd love to stay and chat but we need to get Harry to the stage. He's about to do the most bodacious thing ever! [Harry looks absolutely confused by Tommy's statement] Harry: I thought that the most bodacious thing we ever did was beat The Heartbreakers on Tuesday, man. [Tommy smiles and gives Harry a thumbs up] TTT: Yeah, that was bodacious but this is a different kind of bodacious, dude. Harry: Ok, man. [Harry still looks absolutely confused] Harry: What is that, man? [Tommy points out from the bleachers to a large banner hanging over a stage, which reads 'PIE EATING CONTEST'] TTT: Pie eating contest, dude! [Harry looks at the hundreds of pies that are being readied for the large competition. He sees gatherings of very fat men, very skinny men, and men who don't even look like men...wait they are women so that would explain why they don't look like men at all. Harry smiles, happy that he was able to figure that one out. The smile soon fades as he realizes that pie isn't his thing, but ice cream sundaes are far out] Harry: I can't do that, man. TTT: Yes you can, compadre! Your the pie eating king. [Harry shakes his head] Harry: That's a different kind of pie, man. TTT: Different kind of pie? Teen in Yankees Cap: You know, a nice juicy, wet, pus--- [The teen is interrupted by the loud sound of the announcer getting the pie eating contest ready] Announcer: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN!!! We have the biggest turn out for a pie eating contest ever!! We've got big ones, little ones, and in betweeners all ready to take down some of the tasty pie this side of the US. I'd like to thank Primetime Championship Wrestling for letting 'Heavy Metal' Harry participate in this competition. WHAT A SPORT FOLKS!! [The large crowd claps and boos giving a mixed reaction for PCW's resident stoner. The teens push Harry from under the bleachers as he begins making his way over to the stage, very cautiously. Tommy looks to the teens with a very assuring smile on his face] TTT: Did you guys totally see us destroy The Heartbreakers on Tuesday? Teen in Yankees Cap: That was pretty cool, man, but I'd prefer to have seen Politically Incorrect hand you your asses, dude. [Tommy seems to be a bit insecure at the bringing up of Politically Incorrect] TTT: We'd wipe the ring with Politically Incorrect, dudes. We already did once but, of course, there was outside interference and we were screwed out of our tag team titles. But with the victory over The Heartbreakers, who bogusly were the tag champs before the reopening. We gave them a lesson in surfing the waves and they lost, which makes The Cowabunga Connection the PCW UNCROWNED TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!! [The scene then cuts over to Harry, who takes a seat in front of a juicy apple pie. He looks it over remembering how much he hates pie. Well, he hates the food product but not that 'OTHER' kind of pie which he's known for eating. Unfortunately, after he eats that it usually goes away and he doesn't get to do anything else with it, feeling used and abused. With that thought his stomach begins rumbling loudly. He looks down and realizes that he hasn't eaten a thing at all today. With the smoking of two bowls, five bong hits from Bongus, and two joints.....] Harry: I've got the munchies, man... [A buzzer sounds and the participants begin slamming their faces into the pies. Harry is the only one who hasn't dove into his pie as he observes what is going on around him. The people cheer and scream as if they were at a baseball game, this begins to frighten Harry as he takes a look at the pie in front of him. He doesn't like it...but DAMN he's got the freakin' Munchies!] Harry: Right on, man. [The scene then cuts back to Tommy who is still under the bleachers with the group of teens, watching Harry dive into his first pie like a pro] TTT: This is a most gnarly sight, dudes. Harry will win this contest, pick up that totally rad prize money and then we can go to Fusion and totally show Pretty Cool Guys why we are the UnCrowned PCW Tag Team Champions, compadres. Teen In Linkin Park Shirt: The Pretty Cool Guys piss me off though. I mean they are a couple of boy band rejects who can't wrestle. TTT: You hit the nail on the head, dude. Those guys are totally bogus in a most heinous way, without a doubt. I don't even know how they got into PCW. I mean...come on...they were in NTICW that is just grounds for being rejected for PCW. [The group of teens, though extremely stoned, look at Tommy with confusion] Teen In Yankees Cap: Didn't you and Harry move up from NTICW? [Tommy thinks about that for a second, and then his eyes show fright. He quickly schemes a cover up] TTT: Yeah, but we came from a much different, totally bodacious NTICW. [The scene then cuts back to Harry, who is digging into pie after pie after pie. He's taken down apple, cranberry, blueberry, raspberry, pecan...if it's a pie he's probably eaten it by now. His goatee is covered with whipped cream, and a mixure of assorted fruits. His glazed, stoned eyes show a look of fullness. His stomach has taken too much and it feels as if he's about to explode] Harry: This is not far out, not far out at all, man. [Harry then unbuckles his black, leather belt and his stomach shoots outward, giving it more space. He now feels more room open up and he throws his face back into the pies. The people around him have slowed their pace down, as many have already dropped out just trying it out for fun and the free pie] [The scene cuts back to The Tubular One who is completely full of himself around these teenagers. It's easy to be full of yourself when they are ugly, pimple faced, geeks who just sit around talking about RolePlaying games. Who in the hell plays those stupid games anyway? Talk about a bunch of bogus dudes!] TTT: Pretty Cool Guys do have a most bodacious offense and they have some heat going with The Heartbreakers, but in no way can they ever over take the bodaciousness that is The Cowabunga Connection. Teen In Linkin Park Shirt: Granted The Pretty Cool Guys are, well, Pretty Freakin' Gay but they have lit up the tag team scene in PCW. I think they have cemented themselves as strong contenders. Considering that your the 'Uncrowned tag team champions' your going to have to defend that against a pretty decent team, unlike Fear Factory and Suburban Uprising which were just jobber teams thrown to the slaughter. [Tommy once again seems really insecure with the kid] TTT: DUDE!! Your talking out of your most bogus ass. Fear and Uprising were most competitive teams that we just happen to run out of PCW because of our bodaciousness. The same will happen to The Heartbreakers and especially The Pretty Cool Guys. I can about guarentee that, dudes. Teen In Linkin Park Shirt: Whatever, dude. [Tommy screams loudly, obviously upset with this conversation and storms toward the stage to go cheer his friend on in the pie eating contest] [The scene then switches over to Harry, with his stomach sticking out as if he were Homer Simpson. He looks hypnotized with large chunks of pie hanging from his lips. He looks to his left and sees one of the skinnest men he has ever seen still taking down pies like they are tiny fragments of Skittles. The guy has no shirt on, showing off his bony chest and gangly arms as if he's rubbing it Harry's face that he's extremely skinny and kicking his ass in this pie eating contest. Harry looks out to the crowd and sees Tommy give him a thumbs up] Harry: No way, man. [Harry slams his face back into the pie like a true champion would. The fight goes on for a few more minutes as the now hundreds of pies have dwindled down to about three pies left. Both of the men look winded and ready to let all those pies back up] Harry: I'm going to burst, man. [The skinny guys falls out of his chair, passing out. The crowd cheers loudly as Harry has done the impossible, and that's won the 2002 Great Western Pie Eating Contest and he doesn't even like pie, the food product kind that is. Tommy runs on stage with a large smile on his face, knowing that this is a sign that they will finally be able to gain their rightful passage to being the CROWNED tag team champions] TTT: You did it, compadre. How do you feel? Harry: Like Shamu, man. TTT: GNARLY!!! [The scene then fades to black]
