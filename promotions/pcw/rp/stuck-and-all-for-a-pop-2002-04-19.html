Stuck And All For A Pop

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/19/02 The scene opens up outside of a supermarket, Kroger's to be exact. The automatic doors slide open and instantly we see Kramer walking out with a plastic grocery bag in hand. He is wearing a new summer outfit that he had picked up a day or two ago. It consists of a white pair of jean shorts, an official Duke Williams t-shirt, and a black visor, and white Reebok's. He looks over longingly to the vending machines and hustles over as his throat is growing dry. Kramer: YES THEY HAVE IT!! Woohoo!!! Move over Mountain Dew, Moon Mist is whats cool. Of course if you don't know Moon Mist is just like Mountain Dew, except cheaper! Kramer digs into his pants (I should say he digs in his pocket, because some of you will get the wrong idea). He pulls out a wrinkled dollar and walks closer to the machine ready to insert it. The dollar slides partly in but quickly back out. Kramer pulls the dollar out and presses it up against the vending machine trying to smooth it out. After a minute of doing that he slides it back in and luckily it goes in this time around. Kramer slams the Moon Mist button and waits. The dollar, twenty ounce bottle makes a smack type noise as it falls to the bottom. Kramer bends down to pull it out but it won't budge! Kramer: What the? He pushes his arm further into the machine trying to wiggle the pop out. Finally he clasps his fingers onto the lid and is about to turn it sideways. He tries to pull back but his arm won't budge. Its stuck!! Kramer: No...no....this cannot be happening! His mouth is getting dryer and dryer every passing second, and his struggling is only causing tricklets of sweat to flow down his face. Starting to feel uncomfortable he does a little moving around until he finally manages to sit on the concrete floor making less of a strain on his arm. He takes a moment to think up a way to get out of this highly embarassing situation. Kramer: Wait I know! All I gotta do is use some butter and my arm will slide right out. He peers over to his bag which is a good two to three feet from arm reach. He tries to use his legs to pick the bag up but all attempts fail. A little boy around ten or so walks up to the vending maching next to Kramer. Kramer: Hey kid! Yeah you...look can you hand me that bag right next to your feet? The kid hands the bag to Kramer. He then watches intently as Kramer pulls out a bucket of Bonnet butter and opens the lid. Then he grabs a big glop of it with his free hand and shoves that into the vending machine as well hoping to grease his hand up to slide it out. He yanks but neither hand comes out. He bangs his head against the vending maching getting very irritated. He looks back over towards the young boy who is laughing at him. Kramer: Haha....laugh it up you little punk ((he murmurs under his breath)) Can you do me a favor? Can you go get a grownup out here for me? Boy: Sure. A few minutes pass by as the little boy comes back with a woman who is his mother. Woman: What seems to be the problem? Kramer looks at her with a raised eyebrow. Kramer: My arms are stuck, can't you tell? Woman: Well what in the world do you want me to do? Kramer: Call someone or something!!! Before she can pull out her cell phone, her little boy stops her. Boy: Wait I can help him. Mom do you have a bobby pin? She plucks one from her frizzed brown hair and hands it to him. The little boy runs over and stretches it out. He then shoves it into the key whole to open the vending machine and fiddles around, and finally manages to unlock it. The front of the machine opens up and we can see Kramer's arms hanging down. The boy then starts kicking feverishly at Kramer's hands. Kramer: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING? THAT FRICKEN HURTS!! Boy: I'm trying to kick your hands out. The boy tries to kick again but with all his force, Kramer pulls his own arms out. He then gets up with melted butter dripping from both hands and grabs his grocery bag. Before he walks away he thanks the kid and grabs his pop. The scene slowly fades as the anticipation for Fusion starts to build within Kramer as he is to face John Crichton, a man who is sure to be a tough opponent.
