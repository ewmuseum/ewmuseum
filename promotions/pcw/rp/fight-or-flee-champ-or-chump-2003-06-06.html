Fight or flee? Champ or chump?

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/06/03 To say the word champion bestows many an image in a persons mind. They may drift towards their favourite baseball, basketball or soccer team and think of a time that their team were champions they would be remembered for years to come always a part of the history books and their loyal supporters would always be able to remember where they were when their team became champions. However wrestling is a different game all together, people merely watch for entertainment for the joy and the thrill of watching two or more people tear into each other for all it’s worth, the adrenaline and aggression that courses through the combatants very veins is what pulls people to watch wrestling and champions are soon forgotten. People will remember the first champion and should the ultimate unthinkable happen and the federation close down they will always remember the last champion however what becomes of those stuck in the middle of the mix, those who had their time to shine but used up their fifteen minutes of fame and in the process. The dulcet sounds of footsteps can be heard in the distance the sound waves echoing and rebounding off the walls showing just how large and empty the room was. Glancing around the room there is nothing of interest; there are simply four walls a single door and no furniture to clutter the room with the exception of a small mirror on the far wall, cracks and dust showing it’s age. The footsteps stop causing the room to descend once more into an eerie silence, which captivated and consumed everything except the lone figure that was barely visible in the darkness of the room, his silhouette providing contrast in the room. A quiet intake a breath can only just be heard as the silhouette once more begins to trudge forward his feet sound heavier with every step as they slam down on the cold hard wooden floorboards, the footsteps themselves giving an indication of the pain and suffering that the figure had been through, each step was filled with raw pain and emotion. Eventually the silhouette reaches the far end of the room and gazes like a being possessed in front of it, it becomes apparent that the silhouette is being caused by the choice of costume on the being totally black with only a small hint of green emanating from under the hood of the mans jacket. Gazing deeply into a small mirror hanging at head height on the wall before lowering the hood on the rain soaked jacket allowing him to see his face more clearly, the face of James Edwards. James did not move for a good few minutes just looking at himself in the mirror occasionally raising his hand to his face and touching it gently as though checking that he was still there, that this was all real. His eyes were blood shot, which could have been a result of the cold harsh rain that had decided to tackle him, however this was not the case. The eyes were bloodshot due to the tears that James had shed over the loss of Michelle. He now only had one hope to see her again but that would result in him having to face reality and face facts. “Who am I?” James spoke, his voice dry and somewhat husky as the words slowly formed in his mouth. His own deep green eyes glaring back at his trying to penetrate his own soul looking for answers. “I’m not a champion that’s for sure.” James opened up his coat to reveal the rising star title wrapped firmly around his waist, after a brief moment of fiddling with the fastening system James removed the belt and raised it in front of his eyes. “I fought for you, I bled for you, I cracked bones for you and ultimately I lost it all for you…so why don’t I feel like a champion, is it because I know that I have such a tenuous hold on you, knowing that this at Fusion you may be taken away from me after such a short time. I know that it can happen as the same happened between Matt Jackson and myself. A man who had pushed himself so hard for so long to achieve gold for me to ultimately steal his thunder just three days later.” Said James as he continued to stare at the rising star belt, which had been held by, many other champions…others who had disappeared with time. “I look at you and I ask myself is it worth it? Is it really worth the hassle of having to look over my shoulder every minute SyN is the one vying to take you from me on Friday but who then? Who will be the next to stake their claim at you, will I even be the one to defend you? I can’t believe I’ve reached the point where I put my dream above personal happiness a happiness I could have shared with Michelle.” James’ words becoming more anger fuelled as he throws down the belt bringing up a small cloud of dust, which engulfs James momentarily before settling back on the floor. “You don’t know just how much I want to call it a day do you, but Tuesday will be a new beginning for me. Spending the next month stuck on that airship, the only reason I’m going on it is in the vein hope that at some point it will stop in Australia and I can find Michelle…or she can find me that would probably be easier, but of course everything comes with a price doesn’t it? It seems they have decided to step me up a gear and put me up against the last world champion. World champion, not rising star but world champion. James began to laugh to himself letting out a small chuckle of disbelief as he did so shaking his head frantically from side to side. “No you see they weren’t content with watching me fail time after time against Matt Jackson but now they want me to go against Totally Tubular Tommy. How am I supposed to do that? How am I even supposed to match his ability? TELL ME HOW!” James screams at the mirror a few particles of spittle land on it and the image of James Edwards simply stares back at him not able to answer for not having it’s own point of view, it’s own heart or soul. “Do I just throw it all in now? Do I go to Perth and find Michelle and settle down and just say fuck it? Fuck the dream, Fuck the belt, Fuck PCW” James’ breathing is now highly erratic as his anger and frustration at himself is beginning to show more and more, something which James who had always remained so carefree and focused previously, known as the man with the large goofy smile had not allowed himself to do. Then in a moment of clarity almost James turned and began to walk away, turning his back on it all. That was until his foot made contact with the rising star title which had been dropped in the copious mounds of dust, James dropped to one knee and scooped up the title in one hand and looked down at it whilst clasping it tightly in his left hand, the name James Edwards staring right back at him as the name on the plaque, although slightly dusty. James rose from his single knee to a standing position, not taking his eyes away from the belt. A small grin appeared on James’ face as he slowly and tentatively proceeded to put the belt back around his waist and doing up his coat covering the belt before turning around and staring at the mirror one last time. “No…I’m not going to throw it all away, I’m going to fight not flee. I’d rather be forgotten than be known as the man who gave up, who threw it all away without knowing what he was truly capable of. I’m going to be there on Friday and Tuesday and I’m going to win because ultimately I’m not a chump…I’m a champion.” With the words barely out of his mouth James turned away from the old cracked mirror and begins the walk down the room again, his footsteps no longer baring the resemblance of a man in pain but of a man who has made a decision, a decision that he will follow threw until the end whenever that maybe. James reaches the end of the room and opens up the door, the heavy patter of rain can be heard in the distance as James simply raises his hood covering his blond spiky hair and steps out into the night.
