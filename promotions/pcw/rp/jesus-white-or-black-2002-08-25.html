Jesus: White or Black?

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 ::Introduction:: How many chances do you get to win gold? Well right now Mark and Duzza are getting a chance in a lifetime to win the tag belts at Third Dawning. They hate each other though, but they are going to be putting that aside for now. Since they both have never ever tasted gold before they are going to let their differences go at Third Dawning. Plus this bonding between the two may change they way they feel about each other after they win gold. But Mark would never go to liking African Americans, would he? Only time is going to tell right now as the two are trying to come up with a plan to win tonight and make it a memorable night for the both of them. Will they actually get the Tag Team titles? ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to a big city. There is all types of people walking around in the streets. There are African Americans walking around in their Fubu, Sean John, and Phat Farm gear. Then there are the whites that are walking about with Abercrombie and gothic types of gear on. There are oriental people also walking around in the streets of the big city. Mark and Duzza are on a street corner waiting for the light to let them walk across the street. Duzza is wearing the usual, baggy blue jeans with a shirt and he has some headphones resting on his neck. Mark is dressed in the black jeans with black leather boots, he has on a white shirt with a Confederate Flag on the front and a saying on the back. “What are we doin here now Mark?” Duzza asks as he is standing there looking at Mark. “Since it is Sunday morning and we have a match tonight, I thought that we would go to someone to help us out.” Mark says as he takes a step towards the curb of the sidewalk, looking at the light. “Oh great I remember where you took me to that club the other night.” Duzza starts to explain as the light turns green. “We ran into the biggest bunch of Nazi’s, so what makes me think that is not going to happen again?” “No trust me, this guy is not a Nazi so you have nothing to lose.” Mark says as they walk across the cross walk to the other side of the street. “Well this is a time I will trust you again, but I don’t know why I am trusting a Neo Nazi.” Duzza says as he remembers back in the locker room on Fusion. “Well since you said you had my back on Fusion I guess I will try to give you some trust.” “Well I have to have your back if I am going to win some gold.” Mark says as he starts to laugh. “Because if you do something stupid like you did at the club then I would have to drag you back out. But if I didn’t have your back then you would have just got killed in there and I would have no chance at any gold.” “So that is the only reason you have my back is to win gold?” Duzza asks as he is looking at Mark as they walk down the sidewalk. “Basically that is correct.” Mark says as he is still laughing a bit, but it is only under his breath. “Since you know I hate your type, so why would I like you any other way than just a tag partner?” “Well I don’t know.” Duzza starts to reply as he has his head down looking at his kicks. “But I guess we both have each others back, but only for wrestling purposes and that we hate each others types.” The two continue walking down the street of the big city that Third Dawning is taking place at. Duzza starts to look at the stores that are on the side of the street and he doesn’t really see anywhere like he was in “ghetto.” There are not his style of clothing shops, and all they have is a Virgin music store to his likings. “Why are all of these stores around here for the white people?” Duzza asks as they get to another intersection where they have to wait for the light. “What are you talking about?” Mark says as he is looking at Duzza who is looking around at the people they are standing by. “I see a lot of African Americans going in and out of these stores.” “Well see the clothing stores around here are for the white boys, not the black kids.” Duzza says as he sees a good looking lady right next to him. “All this city has is a Virgin music store to my liking, but they probably don’t have R. Kelly music there since this is a white town.” “They probably don’t carry a child molester in their store here.” Mark says as he starts to laugh a little bit more at his own joke. Duzza shakes his head as they go across the cross walk to the other side of the street. Then Mark goes down the side street and Duzza follows right by him as he is wondering where they are going. Mark keeps on going and then a church is showing up in the picture as Mark looks at the church and then at Duzza. “Oh great a white peoples church.” Duzza says as he is looking at the church and the people going in and out of it. “Just in time for second service.” Mark says as he looks at the big clock right below the bell on the church. The two of them head across the street over to the white sided church. The sign in the grass is: St. Marks Lutheran Church. With a saying at the bottom of it. Duzza is shaking his head as Mark is leading the way of the two to the stairs. All sorts of type of people are going in and out of the church from the first service and going to the second one. Mark and Duzza head up the stairs as they both go through the double wide doors next to each other. “Man this place is already makin me scurred.” Duzza says as they are in the hallway of the church. “No more with that ganster talk, we are in church.” Mark says as he is looking at the ushers giving away programs for the church session. “This is a place where we can pray to the Gods above for tonight. See he can get us through the match tonight, and plus he is white.” “How do you know that God is white?” Duzza asks as they head next to the ushers giving away the programs. “Look at Jesus, he is white and that makes God white.” Mark says as he takes a program from one of the ushers. “But how do you know that Jesus is white?” Duzza asks as he takes a program from one of the ushers. “You never saw him in person.” “Yea, but look at all the pictures of him.” Mark says as he looks up at a stained glass of Jesus on the cross. “You have never seen a picture of Jesus as an African American.” “True, you have a point, but he still can be black.” Duzza says as he is thinking of a way to come back.“See there had to be blacks around back then since we wouldn’t be here if there weren’t. So I think that Jesus was black and that these people have it all turned around with the white Jesus.” “You are too confusing to fight with Duzza.” Mark says trying to avoid the conversation. “See you know I am right so you are going to avoid the conversation.” Duzza says as they both sit down in a pew. “We will just have to find out tonight how good you are in the ring.” Mark says as the bells for the service is about to start. The priest stands up at the front and Mark and Duzza just sit in the pew thinking about Third Dawning and how they could be tag champs. Now these two have their differences and it would be the weirdest tag champs in the history of PCW. But the real question is, is that can they pull it off? The scene fades as we see Mark and Duzza praying to God for their match tonight. Then we see the two arguing about the Jesus race again as people are staring at them as they argue even more.
