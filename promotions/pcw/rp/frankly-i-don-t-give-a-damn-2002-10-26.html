Frankly, I don't give a damn...

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/26/02 Aliens... What they looked like. Where they were from. Their purpose for being together. That's all I did. I freaking eavesdropped on aliens. I couldn't even hear them, except for Kenshiro, he's still not adept at psionic speaking. Who was there? Or rather the proper question would be what was there? How about a giant balloon? 60 x 100, how do those dimensions work for you? Five tentacles, four eyes around its body for 360 vision. Positively charged body to repel the lightning in the upper atmosphere of Jupiter. What was there? How about a snake? Or, something like a snake, not a proper ophidian. It has two legs, too stubby to walk on, but just enough to stand on. Odd, seeing as how the thing lives in liquid. The Uranus delegate had 6 arms lined its sides, perfectly aligned with its feet, the claws designed to grasp and tear at prey, evenly spaced with overlapping range of motion so objects may be shuffled from one pair of arms to the next. Fire! A wheel of fire, not hot, rotating counter-clockwise. The life of Mercury can't sustain a physical form, too hot. So what to do instead? Well, just take on a form of sentient fire. That works! Man... I didn't really want the Extreme Title, I was aiming higher. Ultimately the Extreme Title does nothing for the winner. Only eight of the hundreds of people who have been in PCW have won anything past it. The Extreme Title shortens lifespans. The Extreme Title predestines the vast majority of its titleholders to mediocrity. But then again I might want to keep this title to give it prestige the likes of which it has never had before since Akiyama. After all, who better than someone more than human to wear such a belt? Who indeed... What big names come to mind when you think of the Extreme Title? Let's list some of the few. Flaymin Aymin... He's the man who holds the record for holding the Extreme Title the most times. Four title reigns to his name All of them lackluster, at best. He beat everyone for the title when they were at their worst, either racked with injuries or because his opponents just sucked harder than the Ream Team when Steve Smith gets wood. Dan Hollywood... he holds the longest reign at 42 days. Amazing that he would actually allow his career to stagnate by allowing himself to be saddled with this heat-sucking belt. He at least won it at the second Carnival of Carnage and outclassed 26 other people, but once you do you drop the belt, Hollywood! That set your career back 42 days to be holding onto that belt! At the very least, I've already kept from getting to one record in the Extreme Division, and managed to last more than 15 minutes wearing the belt. Xavier Kannon's title reign shouldn't even count. First Miyagawa wins it, then gives it to Ellie, who then gives it to Xavier. Lovecraft might as well just picked the thing up at the front door, especially with all the interference in the match in the first place. So for that I can be somewhat thankful. At least when Miyagawa gave me the Cruiserweight Title I could keep it for another month. What other names come to mind? Ed. He's dead now, no one really misses him. What was he last in PCW, working aat McDonald's? That's what the Extreme Title does to the normal person, reduces them to finding work outside PCW to pay their medical bills since it's so much. Ed managed to get to the Hall of Fame off of doing nothing above the Extreme Title level, not aspiring to anything more. At this rate, I should be inducted. Frisco and Trashcan Man are also in the Hall of Fame partly by outclassing generally lousy talent. Well, I already defeated Frisco. Poor talent, I can't imagine anyone with actual skill would lose to him. So that adds to my list of conquests. Trashcan Man? He's stinking up OSW, but then again that place is the plague anyway. Doug Troy? He likes to come into the headquarters every now and then and yell something about tildes and WWE wrestling, but other than that the threat of newer talent has successfully driven him away. The same thing happened in OSW. Came back only when 'the good ol' boys' were leading things. When Switchblade started pestering him, he ran away faster than the French ran away from the Germans. LORD CROM GRIFFIN DAGOTH HELD THIS TITLE!!! Why would I even want to touch this thing after that? Have there been any recent credible champions? Rush Mason? I beat him. Many times. All without help. He beat Kramer WildWing, a guy I think I'm like 2-0 against or something. Whatever. He's useless now, he always was useless. I'd be holding him back from the Television Title if he wasn't busy getting his ass handed to him by Venneman Pendragon. Of course, Venny isn't all that impressive. Bernard Colt? It wasn't even called the Extreme Title when he wore the belt, so that point is moot. He sucked, his title reign sucked. AND HE WAS BEAT BY SEMORE SMOKE! Semore Smoke is a jobber, if for nothing else than feuding with Kano and Corey Williams. That will last forever more. Smoke will have no more heat or fanfare, he'll just be another guy because of associating himself with those people. Jason Core? He jobbed to Karl Leblac! TWICE! So as respectable as I'd actually like to find him, he's off the list. Violence Jack? He made Makonie drink Duzza's blood, but he's also a poor wrestler. He got lucky facing talentless opponents in the GTT until Kimbusa just fell on him and pinned him in 12 seconds. I would have been in the Semi-Finals if I faced him. Man, this is getting really depressing, and I've turned this promo into a rant about losers. What was I saying? Oh, yes. I even TRIED not to win this title and Smoke still couldn't beat me. I even TRIED not to defend this title and I'm still the champion. And now there's a triple threat match, myself against Venneman Pendragon against Skitzo. Winning this match will be easy, and not only that, I get to cement myself in TWO title divisions. Oh, this is too easy. But first things first. I'm not ready to deal with Skitzo and Pendragon yet. No... I'm still stuck on my previous opponents. So what shall I do? Simple. I'LL CUT PROMOS AGAINST ALL MY PREVIOUS OPPONENTS FIRST! Hopefully Pendragon and Skitzo won't be too upset that I don't find them a priority... Then again, hopefully they will. I'm hoping for a damn challenge. A pinfall, a submission, anything. I actually WANT to be defeated cleanly outside of a clusterf*ck. Hasn't happened in a while, I'm starting to forget what it's like. Please. Someone... Refresh my memory.
