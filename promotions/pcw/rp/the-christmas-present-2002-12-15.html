The Christmas Present

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-15-02 All the lights and colors danced before my eyes. The candy canes, gum drops, and more was in a parade of color as I passed by the different stores. Ideas of what to get them kept playing in my head. I could also see their reaction to each gift. Although none stood out like I had hoped. I touched the bandage on my head. I had first made a quick stop to patch up my head although I didn't mention the part about hitting the car with my head. I passed by a store that had a giant teddy bear in the window holding a box. My face lit up when I saw it. I knew what to get Tessa know. I went inside the store and pointed out the bear to the clerk. He picked it up and carried it over to the counter. I was started to get excited again. I finally decided what to get my Tessa for this Christmas. This was my first Christmas and it was going to be my best ever. I quickly grabbed the box the clerk put the bear in and hurried off. I looked around the pakced mall for the next store to go to. I already knew what I was getting Tessa now. She would be so surprised. I was so excited I literally ran to the next store. It was a travel shop. I looked at the different posters of the different cites. A sales clerk walked up behind me, watching me study the different posters. 'Planning on going somewheres this holiday season?' she asked me. I turned on my heels and faced her. She let out a gasp. 'You're him! I mean you're Chris Storm!' she shouted with glee. Before I knew it she jumped up and hugged me tight. I looked around worried. I looked down at her and pulled her off me. She stepped back and looked at me with her eyes shining. I just scratched the back of my head nervously. 'Uh yeah. . hi. I'm sort of trying where to go though. I can't make up my mind.' I told her. I turned away from her and looked at the blown up pictures of the different cities. She skipped behind me and looked at the pictures with me. I could feel her press up against my back. I quickly took a step forward and turned toward her. When I stepped away from her she lost her balance and fell forward. She hits my chest as I caught her. I stood her upright. She looked at me blushing like a little school girl. Then she noticed the bandage on my head. 'Oh my goodness! What happened to you?' she tried to touch the bandage. I pulled back from her and hurried over to the counter. I opened the travel book on the counter and looked at the pictures. 'Uh it's nothing really. Just a little cut. Look can you help with something?' I told her, trying to get her attention off me and more onto finding a place to go for the holidays. She walks up next to me and stood by my side. She looked at the book then up at me. 'Sure, anything for you.' she said with a hint of seduction in her voice. 'Just my luck, I found another stalker like the nurse' I told myself under my breath. I pointed to the book to get her eyes off of me. 'Which city would be nice to visit this time of the year?' I asked her as I looked through the different pages. I flipped to the last page and saw a picture of a city with bright lights. There was a large tower in the background lit up like a Christmas tree and a table with a couple enjoy each other's company. I looked at the caption to see it was Paris. 'How about here? Is Paris good?' I asked. Her eyes lit up. She hugged herself as she said, 'Paris. Oh how romantic! The city of love.' She did a spin on her toes and looked at me. I gave a fake smile as I seriously wanted to just get my tickets and leave this place. I looked at the picture again and envisioned Tessa and I as the couple in the picture. A smile crawled onto my face. 'I'll take it. Give me two tickets to Paris.' I told her. She danced behind the counter and punched the information into the computer. I gave her the rest of the needed information and within seconds two tickets printed out from the machine. I payed for the tickets and started to leave. The sound of her voice cut me off however. 'Oh Chris! Can I please have your autograph!' she howled at me. I turned around and looked at her. 'Beep. . . beep. . . beep. . .' thankfully my cell phone went off. I held up a finger to her letting her know to give me a moment while I answer the call. I grabbed my phone and walked out of the store. 'Hello.' I answered. The other end was silent. I started to wonder what was going on. At first I thought someone was pulling a prank on me so I was about to hang up when I could hear someone crying. I started to get worried now. 'Hello!' I said a little louder. Still no reply. But the crying got louder. I could tell now it was Tessa on the other end. Something must have happened. Millions of ideas ran through my head as I tried to think of why she might be calling me. 'Tessa what is the matter? Why are you crying' I asked her. I tried to remain calm and speak to her softly. She stopped crying for a second and replyed, 'Chris, it's Christa. . .' My heart sank into my stomach. I had to lean against the wall as I prepared myself for what she was going to say next. But already thoughts was going through my head like a freight train in the night. Then it hit me. When I left the house I only saw one person sitting next to Tessa. She had looked like there was something bothering her all day but I just pushed it off that is was just her match getting to her. How could I be so stupid? My thoughts was interupted by Tessa has she finally found the strenght to finish the sentence. '. . .she's gone! I looked everywheres for her but she's gone!' My mind went blank. I was still trying to take this all in at once. Christa gone? How? When? Why? Who? Millions of questions ran through my head. It's impossible. I had just played with Christa yesterday. We was wrestling on the bed and having fun. There was nothing wrong. So how could she be missing now? As all the questions ran wild through my head one thing kept standing out. Josh Starwind. It had to be him. He is trying to get at me before the match. It had to be. 'Chris!. . .' Tessa screamed at me on the other end of the line. I snapped out of my thought. I quickly started trying to comfort her but it wasn't doing much good. 'Angel Society took her! What are we going to do?' she told me. I was blown away by that. Angel Society? Why would that kidnap Christa? It didn't make sense. Then I remembed. 'Oh shit! Tessa aren't you facing one of the members of Angel Society today?' I asked her. She started to cry again but she was able to reply, 'Yes, and we face both of them at the PPV.' I can't believe it. They had to kidnap a child just because we are their opponent? Wasn't it bad enough that they attacked us at our debut? Now they are trying to ruin our lives? 'Don't worry Tessa, I'm on my way home right now.' I told her. I quickly grabbed my stuff and rushed toward the nearest exit. Why did all this have to happen now? Just when I was finally pumped about a match something like this had to happen. All my thoughts left the PCW and my debut match. No longer was I thinking about suplexs and submission holds. I just wanted Christa back. No matter what it took. Angel Society was going to pay for this crime. I can promise this. I have been in this business a long time and never before have I seen such an under handed trick. This was beyond the wrestling ring. This was personal. The child is only eight years old for crying out loud! A little girl that had no part in this. There won't be any need for the PPV because at Adrenaline when I get my hands on Angel Society there won't be enough left of them to have a match. That is my promise. Or my name isn't the Perfect Storm.
