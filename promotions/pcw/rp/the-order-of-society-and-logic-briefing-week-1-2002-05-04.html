The Order of Society and Logic Briefing: Week 1

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 4, 2002 Begin Transmission. (The scene opens to a scrambled feed, which slowly begins to correct itself. As the fuzz disappears, the capital letters “OSL” are readily visible. A voice begins to speak.) “Welcome to the Order of Society and Logic Briefing. We are broadcasting live from a secret location in the United States of America. I am Charles Lin, production agent for the OSL.” (The image now turns to one of SOCIETY, holding The Ahtumoth upside down in Conformity.) “Every week, esteemed members, we will report back to you with a progress report on how Mission PCW is proceeding. And so, without further ado, Michael Chang, and SOCIETY.” (The room that can now be seen is virtually devoid of anything. It looks like a basement, with concrete from wall to wall. The only two objects in the room are two simple, wooden chairs, in which SOCIETY and Michael Chang sit. Chang opens the briefing.) “Good day, fellow acolytes of the Order of Society and Logic. We are here today to update you on our progress. Thus far, we have not been able to…silence any of our political targets, but the in-ring aspect of our plans have gone well thus far. SOCIETY has been victorious in the only match he has competed in, but I will allow him to discuss this later. Rather, some aspects I was not aware of in professional wrestling have come to my attention.” (Chang reaches into his pocket, and pulls out a note card.) “Merchandising opportunities in professional wrestling are prevalent. Fans purchase t-shirts, foam fingers, and various other souvenirs supporting their favorite wrestler. While SOCIETY is technically what is considered a “heel”, or bad guy, it is possible that he will gain supporters due to his destructive style. Have Research and Development look into creating a SOCIETY t-shirt. Be sure to include his “catchphrase”, “Conform….TO SOCIETY!”. It is what he is best known for, along with his ability.” (The President continues scanning down his note card.) “What else did I need to tell you? Ah, yes. It is entirely possible that the Order of Society and Logic will be…..expanding over the next few weeks, to include Primetime Championship Wrestling superstars. I have sent out feelers to a few individuals, and they seem willing to step onto the right path and work for our ideals.” (Deeming everything he needs to say to be finished, Chang places his note card back in his pocket.)“And now, SOCIETY shall explain to you what he has done over the past weeks.” (SOCIETY looks out at the expected viewing audience.) “As President Chang alluded to earlier, I was victorious in my first matchup. This was two Fridays ago, as I battled a Ukranian sniper, and a man only known as the Ahtumoth. I was victorious over Ahtumoth using my Conformity submission hold. Since then, I have appeared on every show in some capacity, attacking Richard Cannon with a steel chair last Tuesday, and Matthew Jackson last Friday. I have two important matches coming up this week, however.” (SOCIETY pauses to catch his breath, and continues on, in his surprisingly calm, eloquent tone.) “On Tuesday, I am set to face four men. One is the same Ukranian sniper I faced in my initial match. He is uncompromising, but I am larger than him, and seemingly more skillful. I also battle a would-be musician named Shane Wethers, who seems too busy worrying about his concert to worry about SOCIETY. Making him conform should be no issue at all. Finally is a would-be astronomer named James “Lunar” Edwards. He is likely to pose the biggest threat, if only because I have not encountered him in combat yet. Still, all three men do not seem to pose a threat, as I advance onwards in the “Rising Star” division.” (Chang looks over at his charge, as if to remind him of something. SOCIETY remembers quickly, and continues.) “I almost forgot…my match on Friday is against Shane Robertson, I believe the man’s name is. He seems to be a follower of some religious creed. For this, he shall be eliminated, do not fear.” (SOCIETY looks over at Chang, who nods.) “Trust me, fellow Conformists. SOCIETY will continue to do well in the ring. Eventually, out of it, he will encounter our targets, and they will be eliminated as promised. Until next Sunday, this has been the Order of Society and Logic briefing. Take care, my friends, and remember, Censorship..Equality..” (Both SOCIETY and Chang finish the motto together.) “CONFORMITY!” (With that, the scene once more fades to fuzz, as Charles speaks.) “This has been a production of the Order of Society and Logic.” End Transmission.
