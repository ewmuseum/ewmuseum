Title: Renty-4-09:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 1-3-2003 RENTY-4-9:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.Written: 1-3-2002 The Following Is Taking Place Between The Hours Of 9:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. ....This Is.... Renty-4 ..::/::..Our story begins in a blaze of speed, as we find none other than PCW's own Rent-A-Hero racing down the super-duper-extra-secret-with-sugar on top-passage-tube hidden underneath the sandbox of his newly opened Rent-A-Dad's orphanage. The wind streaks through his short blonde hair as he races faster down the tube, his spandex replica of the Green Lantern's costume making crinkly sounds through the strong wind as the speed grows ever faster and faster. Our hero looks to the screen, seemingly unphased by the blinding speed at which he is spiraling downward as he addresses the audience in a calm manner..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Oh hello, you might be a little daunted by the sudden blaze of speed at which I'm racing down thsi tunnel. Well, FEAR NOT my Earthling audience of Rent-Heads, in just a few mere seconds...I shall land safely on a safety mat specially designed for these kind of spe....' !!~SPLAT~!! ..::/::..Our hero is cut-off mid sentence by a sudden smack into the ground, which has finally made it's first appearance in this story...and not in a friendly way. Peeling his body from the cold hard floor, our hero dusts himself off, walking about wobbly in the darkness as he struggles to collect himself..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Mental note.....never let the kids use the safety speed mat for a trampoline.' *Shrugs off he diziness* 'Rent-Lights on.' ..::/::..Suddenly, the darkness is pushed away by the overflowing light, the dark room giving way to a blue and red cave with a mettallic glow. The cave however, is filled with state of the art computer equipment and technology segmented on various levels by stairs, the very front wall brandishing what looks to be a steel door with an 'R-A-H' insginia written upon it in bold yellow letters..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'500,214,299,832,938,092 payments and it's FINALLY mine....the RENT-CAVE!!' *He smiles as he admires his new base of operations* 'Wonder Woman V2, activate.' ..::/::..Our hero traverses up the first set of steps leading to the steel door mentioned earlier when suddenly, a shimmering light beams from the middle of the Rent-Cave, beginning to rotate with incredible speed until finally...a blue hologram of the one and only WONDER WOMAN is standing right before our hero..::/::..Wonder Woman V2:'Hello Rent-A-Hero, what is the situation?' ..::/::..Our hero seems to have forgotten...seeing as how he's staring aimlessly at the picture perfect copy of Wonder Women, the translucent drool flowing like a gooey waterfall from his mouth as he oggles her, the hologram just tapping her foot and crossing her arms..::/::.. Wonder Woman V2:'I'm JUST a hologram Rent...' Rent-A-Hero:'And what a bootylicious hologram you are....' *snaps out of his dazed trance* 'My apologies, I was dazzeled by your beauty. The sirens went off upstairs, and the red ones only sound off when their is certain danger about to happen, where did this alert stem from?' Wonder Woman V2:'Searching now...'..::/::..The steel door with the R-A-H isignia tattooed upon it slides open smoothly, revealing a projection computer screen as green lines of incomprehensible data run across the screen, Rent-A-Hero's holographic helper standing still in a trance as she tries to search for the data..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'What are those green lines of data? They must be super secret data so hard to decipher that it would make even the Puzzlemaster stand....puzzled.' *Looks to the screen* 'Okay sue me, I can't use a HUGE word for EVERYTHING.'Wonder Woman V2:'Oh....I just set the data to be read that way cause it reads like the computer data from the Matrix...that movie was so cool.' Rent-A-Hero:'Can I marry you?' Wonder Woman V2:'Only if you've got a fiber optic cable, 3 GHz of memory, and a hell of a CD-RW big boy.' *Snaps out of her trance* 'THERE, I got it...the data is coming to the screen now. The alert seems to have stemmed from....PCW''s WEBSITE!'..::/::..Our hero looks bedazzaled by this discovery, eyeing the screen as the PCW roster page 'N-Z' loads up on screen, displaying the ID's of various PCW performers from Duke Williams to Teresa Tomas. His eyes stay locked upon the screen as Wonder Woman continues to read the data, the screen zooming in closer as Rent-A-Hero's ID begins to load..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Now there's a face any Kryptonian would love. Hey...I blinked and they STILL used that picture, they're worse then the DMV.'Wonder Woman V2:'Very funny..but do you notice something missing? Something...blue?' ..::/::..Her hints send Rent-A-Hero's eyes right toards the 'face/heel' status bar...the popularity of that wrestler measured in how many dots they have. Our hero stumbles back in shock, his muscles tensing and his voice filled with vengenance as he exclaims..::/::.. ''WHERE IS MY BLUE DOT?!WHERE IS MY EIGTH BLUE DOT!!!' ..::/::..Wonder Woman pans about Rent-A-Hero, steady reading more data as she rationally explains all she knows to him, our hero's fists clenching at this news..::/::.. Wonder Woman V2:'As you know, those red and blue dots determine heel and face power within your federation...with your eigth dot missing, you are not in total balance anymore Rent-A-Hero. Your body will begin to lose more and more power until your finally drained up dry. Without that eigth dot, your body will never stabalize this condition. The dot was recently transfered from your I.D. to the e-mail of one 'Jonathan Gigabyte' last night at 8:30.'Rent-A-Hero:'Trace that e-mail address my holographic hotty of HTML and print me out directions to whatever you find, I MUST have my blue dot back. How long do I have before my body...destabalizes?'Wonder Woman V2:'I'd estimate 24 hours....which just started 20 minutes ago. You don't have much time...' Mysterious Voice:'She's correct Rent-A-Hero...' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero and his holographic helper turn with great alarm, both of their eyes widening at whatever it is they see, masked by the camera angle at the moment. Our hero is especially dazed, his fingers tensing and a bead of sweat running down his forehead as he exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'GREAT EXCLAMATION POINTS OF PANTERA...IT CAN'T BE YOU!!' Meanwhile..... 9:45 ..::/::..Upstairs, in the video room of Rent-A-Dad's oprhanage, the pleathora of orphaned children are fast asleep on the large quilted rug within the room. The glow of the TV radiates over them all as Bugs Bunny leaps high in the air for a slam dunk in one of his definitive parts in Space Jam. All the while, the kids aren't the only oneswho have fallen into an innocent slumber....the lovely blonde babysitter, Rosaria Jones, leans back in the wooden rocking chair positioned behind the group of kids. Her limp body rocks back and forth in the chair as she slumbers peacefully, the room quiet and serene.... Until her sleep is interrupted by a loud 'click' from the other room, Rosaria's eyes slowly opening as she can notice light seeping through the cracks of the door leading out of the video room. Rosaria shakes off her tired state, alert and keen as she raises up from the rocking chair and slowly creeps towards the door. As she grows closer and closer, she hears whispering...which grows ever more audible as she continues until finally...she hears..::/::.. Voice:'Find the kids and the Rosaria woman, we want them alive, remember what the boss said.' Other Voices:'We got it...no prob...hey...what's behind this door right here?' ..::/::..Rosaria gasps quietly, her soft brown eyes noticing the crackks of light growing darker as someone approaches the door. She quickly turns the knob lock to the right, locking the door from her side as she backs away from it, knowing that will not buy her enough time to get the children safe and to also worry about her own well being..::/::.. Rosaria:'God...if you hear me, I need you now.'Voice:'The door is locked, I'll get on it right now.' ..::/::..Frozen inplace, Rosaria is startled by the sounds of picking, the knob jiggling rhytmically as it begins to loosen....the lock growing ever weaker as she loses more and more time to draw a plan..::/::.. Rosaria:'Renty...where are you?' 5... 4...3... 2...1... 10:00..... ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
