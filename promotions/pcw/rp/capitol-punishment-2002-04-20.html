Capitol Punishment

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-20-02 OOC Note: Although I forgot to mark it in my last RP, this should be counted as my second towards Sabotage. Thanks. *A loud movement of human and verbal cacophony filled the halls. Growls, snarls, shouts and screams came from behind the door. What lied inside that possessed a man to emit such torturous screams? He was inside the torture room, strapped up against the wall. Various machinery poked and gouged at the flesh, ripping and tearing away at him. Why was he here and who or what led him to such a horrific place? It was quite a force to be reckoned with to manipulate Stryfe into committing such acts on himself. That force was none other than Stryfe. But why would he do such things to himself so willingly? Had he lost his mind again? Not necessarily. That left him years ago when it was beaten and raped away by his old foster parents that corroded his outer shell of a once innocent child, exposing the demons within to the outside world. Shreds of the of shell, of that child still hung off of him though and he was convinced they knew what was best for him. For arriving to late to save Sierra as well as not watching his back from the one known as “Syko”, the boy had done a bad thing and he needed to be punished. That’s what led him here. It was the only way he could teach himself these important lessons he never learned or forgotten in a manner to make sure he would never forget. He celebrated walking again and returning to PCW with a huge win not with sociality and joy, but with isolation and despair; With pins and needles poking into his chest and his ever so precious blood spilling out onto the floor. A party was taking place and no one was invited.* *He finished tutoring himself and soon after and crawled out of the chamber. He couldn’t even walk for Christ sakes. He had to slither on his stomach into bed to rest. It was a rather morbid sight to see him lying in a pool of his own blood, sweat and piss, but it was the high price he had to pay for his ever-valuable education. “They must pay…. All of them… Palithorpe…. Krazie…. And most of all… Syko. ” He stammered, breathing heavily. *It was only ten o clock in the morning and already he was getting delusional again. All he could do was blame his opposition for all his troubles and focus his consistent rage at them. It only continued to build more and more with each passing second that he waited for his time in the ring to come. He was always near a breaking point in one way or another and this time he could very well erupt prematurely. He just couldn’t sleep though. The excruciating pain kept him awake to keep mind focused, or perhaps the opposite depending on which way one wants to perceive it. It seemed that nearly instantaneously he began gaining some of his strength back so he could fulfill his new desire; To take a walk to clear his mind of the plentiful issues that encircled him. Of course, he would have looked rather strange just wearing his boxer shorts. He began to get dressed. For the most part it was the same old street attire of his ripped jeans, but instead of his common fishnets, he put on a heavier plain black shirt to cover up the scars. He slipped on his shoes and walked out the door into the arms of the streets. For the most part it was another stroll in the park, that is depending on whose perspective you would follow. Many people were wandering around as well, attending to their everyday business, whatever it may be. Why would they care about another simple human doing the same? To him, the answer was, “All the reasons possible.” In the world around him of people socializing, running errands, talking on their cellular phones and such, and saw grotesque faces, twisted and distorted, laughing and pointing at him. Jeremy tried as hard as he could not to show his fear. Deep down inside he knew it was only him eroded mind playing its little games with him. It was a damn shame he couldn’t convince himself that though.* Dave’s TV *Those were the words on a sign he passed during his walk. The shop was brand new and intrigued Stryfe quite a bit. He noticed several televisions through the window and caught a glimpse of what was playing. Apparently a young man by the name of “Krazie” had been filming a shameless self-promotion for the world to see* “Quite pathetic.” He thought to himself at the sight of the atrocity after hearing the references made to Connors. *A man walked up behind him. He was one of those prisoners of conformity; well dressed and groomed with a briefcase in hand. Stryfe couldn’t stand people such as him but elected not to say anything about it since the man probably liked it no better.* He tapped the junkie on the shoulder and asked him, “That Krazie guy. He’s pretty good, don’t you think?” He confusedly responded, “I have to fight Jeremy Krayzie Howard on Sunday?” The conformo shared a laugh at the expense of his confusion and continued, “No, the name’s Kid Krazie.” “Some kind of a ripoff I suppose?” “Basically.” The man told him. He then bid him farewell, saying in closing, “Well, I gotta run. Work sucks y’know, but whatcha gonna do? Later.” “Get away from me! I’m gonna do it! I swear to god I will!” *A voice echoed throughout the streets from afar. Another man had been pushed over the edge apparently as he stood on a ledge. He was a young man at about twenty-one years of age and already he had nothing left to live for. His story wasn’t much different than that of Jeremy’s own. He had suffered the loss of his wife and child both within the past two weeks and just could not cope. Already hundreds of people had gathered around to try and coax the man out of his grave decision lying ahead of him.* He again shouted below, “I TOLD you! STAY BACK!” *Though everyone had crowded around the front entrance where he was standing, surprisingly no one had been guarding the rear end of the building, which Stryfe walked over and snuck around to. On closer inspection, there were people back there as well. It was only two security guards who apparently had no idea what was going on behind them. How was Stryfe to get past them though? Surely he was capable of fighting them off, but if it could be avoided, there was no point in causing so much commotion. Somehow with his great speed, he snuck into the back door. But this is where the questions arose. What was he doing here anyway? Would his appearance affect the outcome of the suicidal man’s decision to live or die? In other words, would Jeremy be a friend or foe? He crept onto the elevator and rode up to the twenty-fifth floor: The top. It only made sense that he would stand on the highest ledge of the building. He listened for the man’s shouts and screams to grace his ears.* “Fuck off! I’m gonna jump, goddamnit!” *That was him alright. He ran over to the scene to make sure he wouldn’t miss his chance to work another one of his miracles.* “I don’t know who you are, but stay away!” he shouted to the junkie as he noticed him creeping up from behind. “Relax young one. I am not here to cause any harm. I can help you get what you want from this.” He calmly told the man under the pressure. He could only then ask Jeremy, “What’s that supposed to mean?” With that said, Stryfe replied, “It’s quite simple actually. You claim that you want to end your life, correct? However I have personally noticed a plethora of hypocrisy in that statement. If you want to die so much, why are you hesitating to jump?” “I understand you have lost ones very close to you from the cries of the people below and inside of this structure. Looking at you, I know you must be a believer in the Christian way: A blind follower as I like to say myself. And where does one like you believe your family goes to after their earthly passing? Heaven of course. Kill yourself now and you shall rejoin them. No longer will you be apart.” *There was nothing left for the man to say. The “street goth” had made his point. With one deep breath and a sweep of his feet, the man left the ground and plummeted to the ground far below into a bloody mess for all the world to see thanks to one man, one mind. The manipulator had struck again. What would possess a man to do such a thing though? Surely he really has lost his mind to go so low as to assist a fellow man into committing suicide. He didn’t see it that way though. Not by far. Among the many things he has resented in his life, living in pain was among the top of his list. Why should anyone have to suffer? He felt by resolving the man’s conflicts with life, Stryfe had done his part in making the world a better place so those with a better outlook on life wouldn’t have to be slagged down by the depressed. His match with Kid Krazie and a continuation of his confrontation with Syko now were the only things that lied ahead. It was now time to focus those power of manipulation towards these two individuals… …But for the time being, it was back to the chambers for him.*
