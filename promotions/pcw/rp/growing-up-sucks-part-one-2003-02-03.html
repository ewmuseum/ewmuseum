Growing Up Sucks; Part One.

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 3rd, 2003. For as long as he, or anyone else, for that matter, could remember…Jackson Rust had always been the prankster that never grew up. The type of person who never got sick of giving wedgies, power noogies and the whole primary school works. But, everyone has to grow up eventually. It’s just part of like. Maybe Jackson was a late bloomer in developing…mentally. After all, was it normal for a twenty-three, almost twenty-four year old man to behave in a manner such as Jackson has been doing for the entirety of his life? No. But, there was still time to revamp…to change his personality and behavior. Give him a chance…who knows? Maybe he could alter his persona…or maybe not. With hard work and determination, anything could be achieved. Jackson Rust is no pushover, and will definitely force himself to do better to a certain extent. And only he can decide his breaking point; if there is on--- Narrator: Jesus fucking Christ…whoever decided to write that pussy voiceover deserves to be castrated by a bloody Canadian. Jackson Rust: Tell me about it; and the point of this series of promos is to teach ME how to grow up? Pah-lease. Al: *gulps* Steve: A-Al? Al: W-what? Narrator: Now, c’mon. Obviously Al wrote and promoted the fucking voiceover. Jackson Rust: Al…how could you, man? Al sighs. Al: Well, that’s actually a really funny stor— Jackson Rust: Skip the ‘that’s a funny story’ gig and get to the point, bitch. Al: Fine, but by doing this, you know the promo will suck, and will have to eventually restart it? Kimberly: No he won’t…he’ll be conservative, and just order you to “Shut the fuck up, bitch, and restart the scene”…right Jack? Jackson Rust: Precisely. After all, my new poser looks like Satan, and I will damn you if I want to. Al: Sorry milord. Jackson Rust: Quite okay, underling. Narrator: *snaps fingers magically* With the snap of the Narrator’s fingers, Rust now holds a black demonic pitchfork, and fake horns. Jackson Rust: What the hell? Get it off of me! Rust reaches for the novelty horns, trying to get them off, but is cut short. Narrator: No! Leave them on! They add to the scenario of you seeming like Satan. Jackson Rust: *shrugs* Fine. Al: You look so dumb in that. Jackson Rust: Shut the fuck up; bitch! Kimberly: See? I told you. Jackson gleams angrily at Al, before somehow transforming him into Rosie O’ Donnell. Al: Holy shit! Change me back! Change me back! Jackson Rust: No, you’re damned as Rosie O’ Donnell until the end of the promo. Al: Feck… Al thinks quickly, and runs off shot, well…waddling, anyway, as he is Rosie O’ Donnell, and barrels his way into the production crew, desperately crying to them. Al: End the God damned promo…end it! Editor #4: We can’t, Mither Al…it’th theduled for about theven Microthoft Word pageth. Al: C’mon…can’t you like…turn the camera off, and right back on; thus starting a new promo? Editor #4: No. Al grabs the nerdy editor, and holds him up to the wall, as the editor looks down upon the Rosie O’ Donnell fraud, almost scared to the point of urinating himself. Al: Do it now, you lisp-baring sonuvabitch! Editor #4: *urinates himself* Al: Eww… He quickly drops the editor to the floor, disgusted, of course…albeit, you can’t hold it against the poor editor…anyone who has a good sexual preference would do the same if held up by Rosie O’ Donnell. Editor #4: Thorry Mith O’ Donnell. I couldn’t help by pith mythelf as I had to witneth the girth you call breathth. Al: I’m Al, you dumbass. Editor #4: You look a lot like Rothie O’ Donnell. Al: I know, that jackass, Jackson Rust, transformed me into her because I laughed at his Satan attire. Editor #4: Oh. Al: Now that I have efficiently got you feeling sorry for me, do you mind restarting the promo? Editor #4: I get paid by Jackthon Rutht…not you, athhole. Al: …die. Al angrily waddles back to the setting in Rosie O’ Donnell form, and curtly retakes his position in the scene. Jackson Rust: Look at the necrophilliac…he looks like Rosie O’ Donnell! Kimberly: You made him like that. Steve: P-poi-point be-be-being? Jackson Rust: Touché, Steve. Steve: Th-thanks. Al: Now, before we get into anything that may cause tension…….DIE DIE DIE! I think we should cut a promo of some sort DIE DIE DIE! Jackson quizzically looks off at the pissed off Rosie O’—Al… Jackson Rust: Yeah…before tension builds…PSYCHO PSYCHO PSYCHO! Narrator: Hmm…how about we don’t do these THREE EMPHASISED WORDS THREE EMPHASISED WORDS THREE EMPHASISED WORDS thing anymore? Jackson Rust: That was nine. Narrator: You get my point. Jackson Rust: I do— Kimberly: See…he gets it, now let’s get on with the promo. Al: Good idea…where do we start? Jackson Rust: How about pointing out the fact that every El Romantico promo is about the same? Narrator: What do you mean? Rust sighs. Jackson Rust: Is it just me, or has no one else noticed every one of those damned things has him chasing some girl…basically being rejected and so on…I only caught glimpses of them through skimming, but that’s the basic gist of them. Hell, even in his semi-novel form, not one quotation mark… Kimberly: I probably wouldn’t worry about the guy’s spelling and writing…maybe you should bother worrying about your match against him for the March Madness qualifying spot. Jackson Rust: Maybe I should… Narrator: Are you? Jackson Rust: Most likely not…but you never know what my next promo holds in its dark, dark depths. Steve: I d-do. Steve taunts the demonic-looking Jackson Rust with his next promos potential script. Jackson snarls, and snatches it out of his hand, before tearing it to shreds. Al: Didn’t you need that? Jackson Rust: Nah, we got copies. Kimberly: That was the prototype…as in, the only one written up. Rust flushes with anger before throwing the tiny shreds of paper to the floor. Jackson Rust: Fuck! Jesus H. Christ! Al: Loser. Jackson Rust: *ignores Al* Why didn’t you tell me, Steve! Steve shrugs. Steve: I d-du-dunn-dunno. Jackson Rust: What I wouldn’t give to rid of these novelty horns, this demonic pitch fork, the gothic worshippers, and replace them with some sort of comedic promo situation. Narrator: *snaps his fingers* WHOOSH! Jackson Rust: Why did you say “Whoosh”? A mystical light blows through the scene, ridding Jackson of his Satanic features and random other stuff, as well as reverts Al back to…well, Al…rather than Rosie O’ Donnell. Al: Thank God. The scene reverts up to heaven where a man that strikingly resembles George Carlin winks. God: No problem, ya fucker! The scene reverts back down. Jackson looks at Al in scorn. Jackson Rust: Bastard! I turned you to Rosie for a reason! Burn in Hell! The scene once again reverts, but this time down to Hell, where a woman….strikingly resembling Courtney Love sadistically grins. Satan: I’m waiting…Al. The scene reverts back up to Earth, where Al stands still, unmoving and frozen in fright. Al: N-no… Narrator: Stop stealing Steve’s gimmick. Jackson Rust: Yeah, God knows I had to think hard enough to work the stutter in into my promos…right God? The scene once AGAIN pans up to heaven, where angels on strings float around. God: Yeah right… You do the math of what will happen next in the scene…I’m on a fucking coffee break. Jackson Rust: See what I mean? God knows what I’m talking about! Narrator: I’m ending this promo before it gets anywhere else…you’re supposed to be ‘growing up’, remember? Jackson Rust: Was that the point of this promo? Kimberly: You didn’t notice the producer waving the big signs that said “GROW UP!”? Jackson Rust: No…I must have missed those. The producer angrily throws down the poster board signs that read the aforementioned quoted text from Kimberly’s last line of talking. Narrator: End. The scene ends…rather curtly and abruptly if you ask me…I think it should have stayed going, but that’s only the writer’s opinion and— Narrator: FUCK OFF! Fine. The scene ends.
