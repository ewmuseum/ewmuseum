Competition

18 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-18-02 -=[We open the scene at Thug roC’s house where the family is all sitting for breakfast. Thug roC eats his food but can’t help notice how sad his oldest son, Michael is.]=- Thug roC: What’s wrong with you boy? Michael: Katie won’t call me back. Why wouldn’t she be interested in all of this sweet goodness? Thug roC: You gots to be kidding me. With statements like that, I don’t even want to call you anymore. -=[The wife, Shannon, walks in with a plate full of food and goes to the youngest daughter, Lisa]=- Shannon: Aight Lisa, we have to load up on food. You have a big soccer practice today. Lisa: Mmm, french toast. I love soccer! Shannon: I’m glad you like it hun, because the way you play soccer is the way you should live you life. Thug roC: Yea, just look at Michael Jordan. He never quits, even when he quits, he still never quits. Lisa: I just wanna score once this year. Michael: Join the club. Shannon: Lisa, don’t worry about scoring. You are going to score many goals with mommy as your coach cause mommy won’t let you fail. Michael: You’re talking about competition, that’s when I leave. -=[Michael leaves the kitchen carrying his breakfast plate]=- Shannon: Terrel, I’m not too competitive am I? Thug roC: Of course not. Race you to the door! Shannon: GO! -=[Shannon runs off the screen as Thug roC just sits there watching her run as we go to commercials]=- -=[We return to roC-TV where Thug roC is making him a sandwhich in the kitchen with nobody around]=- Thug roC: Finally, the all mighty roC-sandwhich is complete. This indeed is the best sandwhich in the food business today! -=[The side door opens and in comes a screaming Lisa who runs right past Thug roC. Right behind her is a storming Shannon who is wearing a shirt with the word ‘Robins’ on the front in pink. She has a whistle around her neck and snatches the sandwhich off of Thug roC’s plate]=- Shannon: Thank you hunnie. -=[Thug roC is confused as Shannon takes a bite out of the sandwhich]=- Shannon: Wait a minute; I’m on a diet. -=[Shannon throws the sandwhich away and continues her rage]=- Shannon: Terrel, I set Lisa up like 50 times and she couldn’t make not one goal! Thug roC: Maybe you had a good goalie. Shannon: There was no goalie! -=[Michael enters the house from the side door carrying a bag of soccer balls, a goalie net, and some flags. He staggers into the house very tired]=- Michael: Where can I put these mom? Shannon: Did you tighten the net? Did you count the shin guards? Did you even wash your balls? Michael: I haven’t even brushed my teeth yet! Shannon: Michael! If you want to be equipment manager, then you have to work harder then that! Michael: I don’t want to be equipment manager! Shannon: I try to let you into my soccer dynasty and you want to throw it all away! For what? For nothing! Michael: Dad help! Thug roC: Just go hose down you balls son. -=[Michael takes the equipment out to the back]=- Shannon: I’m sorry Terrel. Maybe, maybe I am taking this too far. Maybe I shouldn’t be so serious about this. Maybe my dream about having an all-star little girl soccer team is…isn’t all that important. Thug roC: There you go… Shannon: I WAS BEING SARCASTIC! -=[Shannon storms out of the room. The scene switches to later on in the day where Thug roC is sitting in the living room reading the paper. Lisa comes down the stairs, but very slowly and quietly. She does a commando roll across the floor and pops up right beside Thug roC]=- Lisa: Where is she? Thug roC: Who? Your mom? Lisa: Yes. Thug roC: She went out. Lisa: Good, we need to talk. -=[Lisa walks over and takes a seat next to Thug roC and Michael walks into the room]=- Thug roC: What’s the matter, playing soccer isn’t fun with your mommy coaching? Lisa: Dad it’s horrible! She tells us to play, blows the whistle, and then yells at us! Ask Michael! Thug roC: Is this true boy? Michael: Yea, she’s yelling at 5-year-old little girls! She’s telling them to drop and give her twenty, those kids can’t count pass 3! Then she yelled at this one kid so bad, he pissed himself. Thug roC: I thought it was an all girl team… Michael: …I was scared -=[Michael runs up the stairs in disgrace]=- Thug roC: [To Lisa]. Ok, I’ll talk to your mother for you Lisa: She’ll yell at you… Thug roC: I’m not afraid of her. Lisa: You should be… -=[The scene changes back to later that night. We see the master bedroom where the bed is obvious occupied, but the cover is over both Thug roC and Shannon so you can’t really see anything. Suddenly, Shannon pops up out of the covers]=- Shannon: I won! Thug roC: [Breathing hard]. OK, lets go best 3 out of 5 Shannon: OK lets go Thug roC: Wait! I need a break! Not even Michael Jordan plays the whole 48 minutes! Shannon: Good, that’ll give me some time to work on the playbook. -=[Shannon grabs a binder and starts writing in it]=- Thug roC: Don’t you think you’re taking this coaching thing a little too seriously? I mean, it’s suppose to be fun for these kids. Shannon: Terrel, I’m trying to teach these kids teamwork, discipline and respect. Thug roC: But that’s not fun when you’re 5. Hell. It’s not fun when you’re 25. Shannon: [Laughs]. Are you trying to tell me how to coach now? That’s hilarious Terrel; you don’t know how to do what I do. Thug roC: Maybe not, but they’ll have a lot more fun if I coach them. Shannon: Then fine, why don’t you take the team Terrel? Thug roC: I don’t want to team Shannon: What you can talk the talk, but can’t walk the walk? Thug roC: It’s nothing like that… Shannon: Are you chicken? Thug roC: Fine, I’ll take the team! Shannon: Good. Thug roC: Matter of fact, its starts now. Race you to the back door. Shannon: GO! -=[Shannon sprints off the camera and down the stairs]=- Shannon: [Yelling]. I won again! Thug roC: OK, while you’re down there can you make me a sandwhich? -=[We fast forward to a day later where the girls soccer team is sitting in the living room while Thug roC stands up talking to them. He wears a shirt with ‘Robins’ across it along with a whistle and a hat]=- Thug roC: Aight, lets learn the basic rules of soccer. And rule number one; always get a firm grip on the ball. -=[A little girl raises her hand]=- Thug roC: Yes Melissa? Melissa: In soccer, you’re not suppose to touch the ball with your hands! Thug roC: Whatever, lets check the soccer rulebook and see if you’re smart-ass is right. [Picks up rulebook]. [Reading]. Rule #1, don’t touch the ball with your hands. [Puts down the book]. And I knew that, duh! That was just a test to all of you others. In soccer, you’re not suppose to touch the ball with your hands! But lets forget about all of that. Lets go play some soccer! -=[The team cheers as they all rush out the house. We find them later at the soccer field stretching out. Then we see them all in a straight line and Thug roC comes by with the ball and hits them all on the forehead with it. Finally, they all stand in line and practice kicking the ball in the goal. The thing is, the net was covered with a life-sized picture of Shannon and the kids were hitting it right on the head. We fast-forward again to after practice when Thug roC arrives home with his daughter Lisa who seems overjoyed. Thug roC goes into the kitchen for some water where Shannon is standing]=- Shannon: How was practice? Thug roC: Great! Shannon: Just out of curiosity, what plays did you go over? Thug roC: No plays, we just went out there and fooled around. Shannon: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING WITH MY TEAM!? Thug roC: Huh? Shannon: They were running around like chickens with their heads cut off. Thug roC: You were spying on us. I thought that tree had a nice ass. Shannon: That was me, that was me. And you were pathetic Terrel. What were you doing out there? And don’t think I didn’t see my picture in the net! Thug roC: Hey, it’s way to early for you to criticize my coaching method. Shannon: What coaching method? You didn’t have a coaching method. You didn’t do any key stretches, you didn’t go over any plays, you didn’t even use your whistle! How are you going to get them to shut up if you don’t use- -=[Thug roC blows his whistle]=- Thug roC: They are only little girls. This is about fun, not always about winning. Shannon: Fine. When you lose your first game, don’t come running to me. -=[Shannon leaves the room]=- Thug roC: We won’t lose our first game. I hope. I think we won’t lose. I pray… -=[The next day, we see the Robins all walk into the house with their heads down and frowns on their faces. Thug roC follows them trying to cheer them up]=- Thug roC: Come on guys, we didn’t lose that bad. Melissa: I don’t know. 18-0 is pretty bad. Thug roC: But keep in mind that we scored 12 of those points for them Lisa: [Sadly]. I just hope I get to score one goal… -=[Shannon walks into the room full of sad kids]=- Shannon: Did you all have fun? Was it fun losing 18 to zip? Thug roC: Are you here to gloat? Shannon: Yes I am. And I will be sure to teach that to the kids on my new team, The Blue Jays. -=[The whole room gasps]=- Melissa: Because the Blue Jays are big and strong and mean. Lisa: And one of them looks like they shave -=[The scene fades to commercial]=- -=[We come back to roC-TV where a soccer game is about to take place. The Robins have arrived to the field in a mob time fashion. All walking in a big group with determined looks on their faces. On the other side of the field we see the Blue Jays arrive in military fashion. They have formed two straight lines, and they all are walking in step with each other. In the front of the team stands a player with a uniform, but looks like a guy. Shannon has a coach shirt on and approaches Thug roC]=- Shannon: The Blue Jays would like to tell you good luck. Thug roC: I thought this was an all-girl game. Shannon: It is. Thug roC: What about him? Shannon: Oh that’s Ida. She’s from East Germany. She was raised under a power plant Thug roC: That still doesn’t explain why she’s wearing a cup. -=[A whistle is blown and the two teams take the field. The game starts and it’s total domination from the start as the Blue Jays score off of the kickoff with a booming kick from Ida. We see clips of the goalie missing ..1…2…3…4…5…6..7..balls go right by her. We continue to watch a girl from the Robins run into Ida and fall to the ground as Ida acts like a brick wall. We then see the referee looking at his stop watch as time winds down. Thug roC’s daughter, Lisa, gets the ball and makes a wild kick that actually goes in as time expires. Thug roC rushes the field and Lisa is happy as anything. She takes her shirt off revealing her small sports bra and starts waving her shirt in the air as the team celebrates with her. [USA women’s soccer team parody]. She runs wildly around as the Robins lose the game 17-1.]=-
