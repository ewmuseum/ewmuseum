El Romantico, El Diablo, and Eminem in the Same Sentence, Somethings Not Right!

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20/02 Girl your my angel, you’re my darling angelCloser then my peeps you are to me, babyGirl you’re my angel you’re my… “Good god, what is this garbage on the radio.” Diablo shouts as he flips from station to station. “It’s NEW YORK music.” El Romantico replies while laughing at El Diablo pull his hair out over the situations. Yo, I can’t sing. I feel like singing.I wanna fuckin sing.I got my baby back. “Sing to your self you retarded degenerate.” Diablo shouts as he tries to switch the station but accidentally breaks off the knob. “Umm…. Romantico we have a problem.” Diablo says as he holds up the broken radio switch. “NOOOO!!!!!!!!” Romantico screams as he realizes there stuck listening to Eminem, the fakest human being to ever live. Someday I sit staring at the windowWatchin the world pass me by “He must really have no life.” Diablo says as he pays close attention to the lyrics. “Yes, we have words for these type of people in Espania. You sure as hell know what the word is: GRINGO!.” Romantico and El Diablo shout out at the same time. Sometimes I feel like the worlds on our shouldersEveryone’s leaning on meCos sometimes it feels like the world’s almost overBut then she comes back to me “Is that proper English?” Diablo asks Romantico. “No, I don’t think so, but I think it’s something called ebonics.” Romantico says with a silly smirk on his face. “Oh you mean like Playaz Inc.” El Diablo then sags his pants down to his knees, and throws a hat to Romantico. Romantico puts it on sideways and then applies the hydraulics to his Cadillac. They pull over to the first group of urban looking woman they find and Romantico sticks his head out the window. “Yo baby you wanna bizounce back to my place and make sum babies. I like a hunnie wit sum funk in da trunk fo sho.” The woman replies with a quick slap to the face of Romantico, even though he thought he did on hell of a good job. “You idiot, you didn’t get her because you forgot the most important part.” Diablo says as he shakes his head to show how ashamed he is of Romantico. “Oh yea….. REPPIN ESP! REPPIN ESP!” Romantico screams out the window to the fine woman who is fine GONE! “What can I say, I’m no Eminem, and she obviously didn’t come back to me.” The two men share a laugh after Romantico’s statement until. I act like shit, don’t faze meInside it drives me crazyMy insecurities could eat me alive “Diablo, do you have insecurities?” Romantico asks as he throws his hat to the back of the car. “I’ll tell you who definitely have insecurities, Team Bastardos.” El Diablo says as Romantico takes over. “Oh, you’re telling me. I’ll bet money all of the members of that team started off as little rich kids, who mommy and daddy bought everything for. Then one day they needed to waste some money, so they bought this cool looking instrument that looked like a see through plastic clarinet, accept you had to light it up before you play it. And from then on it went all down hill.” Romantico says as he holds his hands in the air to state the obvious. “Not to mention that Vilas guy is like three feet tall.” Diablo responds casually. It all makes sense when I look into her eyes “Eww, that’s just nasty. He should be arrested for doing such slanderous business with one of his kin.” Romantico shouts with his reaction to the tormenting lyrics. “It’s becoming legal now, look at the Celestials. You try and tell me there not gay. I mean it’s the total, big and dark powerful type thing. But there all gay, I guarantee it.” Diablo says with satisfaction stamped on his face. “I believe it, why else would they have Boca on there team?” Romantico replies. So here’s what I’m facin: 3 felonies, 6 years of probation. “Woah, he sure is violent.” Romantico exclaims. “I heard he Bladed some guy across the neck?” Diablo says with no pun intended. “Yea, that reminds me of Team CTMB.” Romantico shouts out as he hits a bump on the road. “You mean the team who’s so unoriginal they just took the first letters of their names and put them together.” Diablo asks. “Yea, them.” Romantico quickly responds. “Now is it me, or do they sound like names you’d use for armory. I mean Bladed, like sling blade, Trigger, like the Trigger of a gun, Celt, like a Celtic Armlet, and Mandrake, well that sounds like some other type of absurd sword.” Romantico points out. “You see your not making any sense, because you see the name is BladeD not Bladed.” Diablo comments. “Bladed?” “No, BladeD.” “Bladit?” “No, BladeD.” “BladeD?” “Yea, that’s it.” If you ever need anything, daddy will be right there?Well guess what daddy’s hereAnd I ain’t goin nowhere babyI love you “What’s he going to do when he’s on tour?” Diablo wonders. “Who knows, who cares, songs over.” Romantico swiftly responds “O yea, and remind me when we get back, to smash that radio of yours into little pieces.” Diablo says as he lays back for a nice rest of the trip. AGREED. And now for our Back-to-Back Eminem weekend, this is “Lose Yourself”. NO!!!!!!!!!!!!
