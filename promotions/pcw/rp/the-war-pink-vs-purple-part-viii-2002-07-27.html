The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part VIII]

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 27, 2002 Mystery Man: “Come on Valentine, let’s get back to the nightspots.” The bike roared down the empty street. They fought their way through the flow of people leaving the nightclub, Valentine handed the mystery man his wallet saying that it must be his round. The mystery man started chatting up the prettiest of the waitresses to try and get a drinks order out of her. Eventually after the exchange of phone numbers the order of drinks was provided, and the mystery man handed over a PURPLE~! punch and the wallet to Valentine. The club was usually in virtual darkness but as it was after closing time the lights were brighter. A lot of silver sculpture filled the available vantage points and crannies. Whatever the sculpture was supposed to be, it permanently looked as if it was being viewed from behind. It seemed more impressive from the metalwork point of view than the artistic point of view. The mystery man ignored the sculpture and dragged Valentine over into the corner where a pair of girls sat at a table strewn with empty glasses and two crumpled cigarette packets. The mystery man was sitting down and chatting up the blonde girl in an instant. Valentine pulled up a stool and started chatting to the other girl who was by now being neglected. Valentine with a girl you say? Could this be the end all to the rumors that he is in fact a homosexual?!? Well, he was just being nice. In spite of the impression given by the table, she was still reasonably sober, and evidently used to being neglected when with her friend. Her friend was absolutely stunning. Tall blond and very striking; she could easily have been a model, it was obvious why she had caught the mystery man’s eye immediately. The other girl was called Jenny and was on a brief tour of the Caribbean islands on another cruise to fill in the time after University and had picked up Sue, another model on the trip over. She had a job to go back to, and this was effectively her last night in the islands. Valentine guessed at them having about ten minutes before being forcibly ejected and drank through his PURPLE~! punch steadily, when they did get ejected he drank the last mouth full and handed over the glass to the bored bouncer. The mystery man had been so busy chatting up Sue that he had not touched the PURPLE~! punch. He stood up, realized that he was about to commit the ultimate sacrilege and leave a drink unfinished. He picked up the still full glass, put one arm around Sue, and while walking out, downed the PURPLE~! punch in a single gulp, placing the empty glass on the bar as he left. Jenny and Sue stayed not far from the nightclub and Valentine and Jenny followed the others dutifully. Jenny put her arm around Valentine’s waist and pulled him towards herself. He turned and pushed her away. She tried again, but again Valentine pushed the young girl away. Valentine turned and ran down the street, away from the girl, frightened it seemed. He sprinted in a girl-like fashion back to his hotel room by the Wrestle Vessel loading dock to find Johnny waiting in the room. Jack glanced over at the TV to find two men kissing on the screen, Johnny quickly shuffled around and changed the channel. Jack Valentine: “What the hell are you watching?” Johnny Heart: “Nothing...nothing at all!” Jack smiled. Jack Valentine: “Let’s go out...” Johnny Heart: “Where to?” Jack Valentine: “Anywhere...we’ve been so pent up with this war between the PINK! and the Purple that we haven’t even discussed our match this Sunday against the Neo Void God, Pretty Cool Guys and Politically Incorrect.” Johnny Heart: “Alright, grab your coat...I found a great spot the other day.” The pair headed out the door and when we pick back up with them they are standing outside an Asian pub with a large Asian man standing guard as the bouncer. The name of the place...Lo Nyg Johnson’s. Johnny Heart: “You’ll love this place, great pub.” Heart led the way through the double swing doors. The pub was in a back street, and seemed to be hunched under a gray tenement. Inside the place was dark and cold. A long bar was along the right wall and a vast variety of optics and pumps stretched for countless yards. Johnny Heart: “What are you having then Valentine...” Jack Valentine: “A PINK! Lemonade please.” Back to PINK! lemonade, eh? Could this be a sign that Valentine’s meeting with PURPLE~! mystery man didn’t go so well? Valentine cast his eye along the length of the bar, the wall was lined with optics and paneled with dark wood and interspersed with mirrors advertising various traditional beers. If the pub had not been the favored haunt of the living dead, a pasty faced bunch of assorted reprobates and alcoholics, he would have quite liked it...you see, The Heartbreakers were ones that like that flamboyant, outgoing type of men...erm...people. Heart had ordered their drinks, and was now launching into a lengthy monologue on the merits of the tag team championships, decidedly peculiar or something like that. The monologue made copious reference to the specific gravities of the relevant beers, Heart had apparently memorized the specific gravity for the majority of European beers and was now giving Valentine the benefit of his learning, with all the relish of a football fan recounting the statistical minutiae of his favored team. A drunken couple at the far end of the bar were having a rather noisy argument, and his friends were taking his side. With a loud blast of obscenities the woman left the group and came to the other end of the bar where her work mates were gathered. The work mates then started to shout abuse to the other end of the bar where further obscenities were exchanged. The noise quickly reached the sort of level where it became impossible to really get any louder. The bar staff were pulling down shutters to protect their precious optics. A single half filled pint glass was flung from one end of the bar to the other. The glass flashed through the air, beer spraying through the air in a variety of arcs. Heart stopped explaining the merits of real Russian vodka and cast an eye up and down the bar, then resumed his monologue. Valentine nodded sagely at the relevant points. A steady flow of bottles and glasses was now flying up and down the length of the bar, ... Johnny Heart: “Do you want to know something Jack? The teams that we are facing this Sunday are rather good, but not great, anyway tequila is not nearly as good as people say but of course it has to have the worm in it to really be authentic” The air was filling with broken glass as it exploded across the room, and Heart continued his ramblings about the various liquors in the pub, comparing them to tag teams. Johnny Heart: “Look there Jack...there you have a Grizzly Mountain Stout style beer...a black liquor. Very similar to Akiyama. It is a very good liquor, but really, people only drink it once in their lives just to try it. Just like Akiyama‘s tag team title victory...it will only happen once, and he will not have a chance to capture it in defense.” Jack smiled. Johnny Heart: “Next down the line you’ve got good old fashioned Miller Lite. The beer choice of many Americans. Just like Pretty Cool Guys, Miller Lite will make people sing and have a jolly good time but at the end of the day, they’ll be passed out with no memory of the night before. Except the Pretty Cool Guys memory loss will be courtesy of a PINK! ass pounding...erm...kicking!” Jack laughs again. Johnny Heart: “And finally you’ve got Guinness...the most sophisticated beer on the shelf. Only the smart ones drink Guinness, but deep down under the beer-tasting flavor is nothing but a dirty, no good ploy to get you drunk...just like Politically Incorrect. They are nothing but dirty, no good politicians who will stop at nothing to win, but in fact it will be them throwing up at the end of the night....throwing up and away their chances of becoming tag team champions once again.” With his hand over his pint Heart led the way and they sheltered in the toilet doorway watching the glasses flying. The ammunition was now almost exhausted and the obscenities were starting to flow again. Johnny Heart: “I suppose another drink is out of the question at the moment.” Heart had drained his glass. A huge man appeared from the snug and started fighting with the group of men, some of them tried to hold him, but he shrugged them off like a child shaking off a coat. The police were now piling in through the doorway. Swatting aside the other customers the huge man turned his attention to the police, he dropped his head down and charged at the nearest policeman. He tried unsuccessfully to back out of the door, but another policeman was behind him. The blow caught him heavily in the chest and he started to slump. The huge man backed off now suddenly bald the gasping policeman was clutching at a dank brylcreemed wig. Heart was at the bar gesturing through the grill to the bar man to try and get another PINK! lemonade. The bar man was not going to open up the grill, so Heart was pointing at what seemed to be the narrowest bottle beer available. The police had now regrouped and were on the point of pinning down the huge bald man. A truncheon lashed across his head, and it twisted back. He shrugged off two officers quickly and his open hand caught the face of a third and pushed him bodily to one side. The bar man was unable to get even the thinnest of the bottle beers through the grill and Heart was not about to compromise with damaging his freshly made-over face. Johnny Heart: “Come on Valentine, no drinks, no music what kind of pub is this?!?” The bald man had disposed of all the police in the pub to his satisfaction and a fresh van load were coming through the door way now, and were being knocked down as quickly as the first van load. Heart tapped the man on the shoulder, he turned round, blood was dripping thinly down his face from a gash in his head, his eyes looked dead to the world, his lips pulled back in a primal growl, Heart’s fist flashed forward quicker than the man could see. A single blow landed him on the chin. His head whipped back, his face seemed suddenly frozen and immobile as his body fell back lifelessly. A police officer was suddenly at Valentine’s elbow. Police Man: “Who the hell are you?” He said incredulously. Johnny Heart: “The next PCW tag team champions...that’s who.” Heart led Valentine out of the bar... It had appeared that Valentine and Heart had made up their minds, they were going to stay with the PINK!, and the evil PURPLE~!, who tried to get them to sleep with a girl was out of the picture. But something in the back of all of our minds tells us that the meetings between The Heartbreakers and the PURPLE~! are not over yet...not by a long shot. Stay tuned for part IX to find out what happens next!
