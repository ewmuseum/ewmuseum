We practice with our bokken every day, now. We feel almost at tune with the sword. In the cave, lies our blade, the Saishukessen Ougii. Every day, we will clean it with oil, but we never carry it with us, nor do we ever draw it completely out of its sheath. A sword is drawn out of its sheath for one purpose only. We do not ever intend to execute that one purpose again. We feel the night sweep over the cave. We see the sunlight, blocked from view. It is beginning to get cold, despite the fact that it is midsummer. Today, when we went hunting, we were using our wooden bokken, as usual. We thought we had a good shot at a deer, but when we let the sword loose, it arched in the air and missed its target, as if guided by an invisible force. It hit the deer in the hip, wounding the creature. We raised our fists to strike, but something told us to run. It wasn't just instinct or habit. It was a voice. Run, Akiyama, it said. The voice was hauntingly beautiful. But the scariest of all was that the voice was familiar. Run after the deer… chase it down. It did not take more than ten minutes for us to catch up with the animal, because it was wounded. The trail of blood made the chase easier, and our body was attuned to physical exercise. We found the animal soon after, trying to limp away weakly. And then, we don't know what got into us. As we think about it, our hands are trembling, and our eyes blink uncontrollably. We feel fear, nothing but fear, when we think of what we did today. If we were to finish off an animal, we would use our fist and crush its throat. But when we went to the deer today, we gripped its neck in our two hands and twisted. We... The mind pains to think of such a memory. It wasn't the right thing to do. We saw the blood on our hands. The spots wouldn't go away, even when we tried to wash them off later. Even now, our hands are tarnished red from the blood of the animal. We have no idea what has gotten into our head. Something is trying to grip us, take us away. But the most fearful thought is that, when we killed the animal, at that moment, it felt like we've done things like that our entire life. As the neck of the.... no, we won't describe such things! Let the vile thoughts, blood, violence, let them get out of our head... we don't want to see it ever again... blood.... We want to wash our hands again. We've never spilled blood on our hands before, until today. We detested the spilling of blood on our own hands. The way we hunted was as merciful as possible, but we never used our own hands to destroy a life. Never before did it happen. We are trying to change! What has gotten into us?? The night is falling. We're starting to hear a voice again. It's begging us to leave. It's telling us that it is no good staying in the cave, like a coward, a man of no action. Who are you? What are you? We know you. But we don't know where. It's trying to suck us in. As we walk, we see images, of Nelson, Chef, Legend Inc, Darkstar, Boda… talking with us, giving us orders. Chef treated us as a friend. But we will take a step, and then we will hear the Chef yelling at us. Who is putting these thoughts into our head? We tried so hard to be a good man, someone who is not feared. We met Grace, and she believed us to be one of the nicer people she has met. We like ourselves! We are WE! Truly WE! We have no wish to be cold-blooded! Our name is Akiyama, and we are a good person! We know our friends, and we know that they like us the way we are. We are a man who honors life, respects others, and... ... no! We are we! We are we! Get out of our head! We are Akiyama! Yes, the darkness is falling. We will not go! Do not make us go! We want to be a good man! We will not hunt for revenge! We will not destroy for our own pleasure! We are WHO we MAKE OURSELVES TO BE! Leave us be!! Why do you want us to kill? We will not pick up the sword. we have left the Saishukessen Ougii untouched. It has not drawn blood in almost five years. We are good.
