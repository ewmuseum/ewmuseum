Tales of the Golden Toe: Tale of El Romantico

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/02 Tales of the Golden Toe: The Golden Heart as passed and the Golden Toe has come back again. The Golden Toe is the old Juan Borro, the fun loving foreign soccer player who did his all to help others. Through the tales of the Golden Toe Juan gives us insight into present experiences with his life and duties and encounters with people. The Golden Toe has seen his calling, and that calling is to do good, be good, and be a power for good. No force will stop Juan’s GOAL~! Time: 8: 14 P.M Location: Barcelona, SpainScene: Bus “ You think you know someone, but right when your most sure, something happens that pulls you miles away. It’s called the mystery of life, a mystery still not yet known to man kind.” We meet up with our national hero in a bus going to Barcelona. Juan is taking a trip to Spain to broaden his horizons of the outside world. Juan has always looked down on Spain, because other Portuguese people and politics brain wash people like himself to have a hatred for Spaniards. Juan, wanting to experience new things has taken the short trip to Spain. Juan wants to see the fighting spirit and culture of the Spanish so he arranges plans at an Underground Spanish fighting circuit. The circuit itself is a cheap place. A place for drunks and low lives to shed there miserable lives and be free for one night. What some don’t know is that same place beholds one of Spain’s fighting icons. The same fighting icon Juan is ready to seek. The scene opens with Juan on a bus, reading the award-winning novel The Giver. This is a book about a mysterious environment and civilization where all knowledge is withheld in the hands of one man. Juan wonders what it would be like to be the only person in the world who could feel pain, see colors, and be the beholder of painful memories. Is it more a burden to know to much, or is it an honor? Who knows? While on the bus Juan looks around and out the window to see all the different people out there. So much he never knew about and always thought he knew about. He also thinks about evil and how it enters your body like some type of bug. Why are people evil?What drives people into hurting others?Who puts thought in people’s heads to begin with, brainwashing them to do and say certain things?Does anybody really have a mind of his or her own? These are a lot of questions that Juan experiences within himself. He wonders how they can be answered and how he would feel if he were to know the truth. All Juan really cares about now is the path he is directed in. Juan knows this path can spell success for him. And he just hopes it’s all that God intends. When Juan arrives at the Underground fighting circuit he takes a look around. He notices the business people all the way to the lower class citizens. Either way the building spells sin. Sin Juan doesn’t want to be present in, but curious to what lies within those brick walls. Juan walks straight into the building, before a man in a trench coat stops him. Juan turns around and acknowledges his existence in a polite manner. “Did you pay for your ticket?” The man says staring at Juan. “Yes, I had them preordered before I came so I wouldn’t have to go through the huge hassle to get them here. “Good. I noticed you look a little new around here and I just want to offer you little bit of advice.” The man says as he looks around. “Sure, what would the advice be?” Juan asks the man in return. “I’ve seen you on television. You’re in that wrestling promotion Primetime championship Wrestling. You guys work hard.” The man says as Juan interrupts him. “Yes, we do.” “Yea, well you may work hard, but don’t even think about coming near the ring when he comes along.” The man says cautiously. “When who comes along?” Juan replies. The man then leans closer over to Juan and whispers lightly in his hear, with his fingers trembling in fright. “EL ROMANTICO!” “Who is this man? I hear so much about him, but I never really got to learn who he really was?” Juan curiously asks. “As do so many others my friend, but I don’t have that answer. I don’t think anybody has that answer. El Romantico is unknown. His location is unknown. His real name is unknown. Even his family is unknown.” The man responds. “ Is it just a big myth of a character or is he really that mysterious.” Juan asks wanting to know more of the facts. “No, he is no big myth. He is one hundred percent real. The man is a genius lover and a genius fighter. In both things he is said to be better than any other man on earth.” The man enviously says. “That’s impressive, who could possibly want more than that.” Juan replies’ thinking this guy is too good to be true. “You better go inside, the event is about to begin.” The man adds as he runs off. Juan goes to his seat, which is on the top of the first floor. There are thousands of fans cheering and holding up signs. He sees El Romantico posters and signs, women screaming they love him and men saying they want to be him. Juan wonders how these people can love someone so much without knowing anything about that person. The event starts and throughout it all the fans go wild. Juan is slightly entertained, but finds nothing too special about it. It seems like another everyday underground fighting league. Juan is not impressed to say the least, but stays in his seat in anticipation for El Romantico. The matches go on and the hour’s pass, until the final match finally comes. Juan is on the tip of his seat to see the supposed great action. Suddenly, all of the lights go out and the fans cheer. Juan looks around as white sprinkles begin to drop and a bright white sunlight sparkles through the stadium. The sight is blinding but the other fans are used to it. When Juan finishes squeezing his eyes he sees a man dressed in all white and with a rose in a rose in his hand in the middle of the ring. The man is none other then El Romantico. He is not a huge man, maybe six feet, around two hundred and twenty some odd pounds. He doesn’t seem special, but has glistening black long hair and a smile where he teeth twinkle in the sunlight. A woman runs up to the ring desperately anticipating El Romantico’s arrival. She grabs him and looks in his eyes. He then gives her a short kiss as she passes out right there on the floor almost looking like she died. They sweep the woman away as the loads of others charging him are held away. An announcer than grabs a microphone and screams out to the crowd. “Who my friends would dare take on El Romantico?” The crowd goes silent, as Juan is about to stand up and accept but remembers what the man in the trench coat had told him before. “Don’t even think about coming near the ring when he comes along.” Juan decides to let it go as some other huge barbaric looking man in the crowd offers his assistance. The man poses for cheers, but gets none as the people are all devoted to El Romantico. The ring announcer wishes the contestant luck, and even gives him some brass knuckles to use. Juan looks on and sees there being no way a man who is a foot bigger and a hundred pounds stronger with a weapon could possibly lose. The bell then rings, and the bigger man take the first move. El Romantico doesn’t even move as he lets the fighter come to him. Once he is close enough El Romantico kicks the big man straight in the jaw stunning him then right in the stomach sending him flying through the cage. Juan can’t believe what he just say as El Romantico just decimated his larger opponent in seconds. Juan couldn’t believe it at all and figured it must be rigged or staged. Wanting to know the truth Juan goes running down at the ring and calls out a challenge against El Romantico. “Hey El Romantico, I want you in the ring now!” Juan commands. The peaceful man doesn’t respond for a few moments before he lightly speaks in his echoed voice. “Maybe another day.” The lights then spark for about three seconds leaving Juan blind. When Juan turns back around at El Romantico he is gone. Left in his path is just one white rose. The scene fades to black with Juan picking up the white rose and looking up. TO BE CONTINUED……………..
