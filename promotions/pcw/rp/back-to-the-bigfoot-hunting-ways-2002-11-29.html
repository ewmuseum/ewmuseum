Back To The Bigfoot Hunting Ways

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29.11.02 “Your doing what?” hollered Duzza’s wife, Brittany Duzza:“ Searchin’ for bigfoot a’ight” Brittany:“ Boy you crazy” Duzza:“ Yo I jus’ got to get away from everything for a while, yo more than welcome to come babe” Brittany:“ I ain’t going to the woods in California, have you forgotten that we have a one year old son Malek?” Duzza:“ Babe, I’ll be back soon ok?” This conversation is taking place on the driveway of Duzza’s Hampton mansion. Brittany is dressed in a white crop top, and white shorts, a beautiful African-American woman of just 25. She is holding hers and Duzza’s baby son Jimmy, named after a friend of Duzza’s who was killed in a drive-by shooting. Duzza, and three other men. All African-Americans are putting camping and hunting equipment into Duzza’s black 4x4. Duzza is dressed in baggy camouflage pants, a camouflage sleeveless body warmer and a dark green bandanna. For once Duzza has little jewellery on. The only jewellery he has on him is a pair of earrings and a watch. Duzza kisses his wife and son good bye before getting into the drivers seat and pulling away down the drive. Duzza and his friends caught a plane from New York to L.A and from there proceed to drive down into the think wilderness of California, the place which has more bigfoot sightings than any other state in America. After getting lost many times, it is night time before they are even within a hours reach of the campsite where they are meeting up with the rest of the people looking for bigfoot. Duzza is still driving, in the front with him is Jay, in the back is TJ and Kyle. TJ:“ Yo man, why you want to find a bigfoot anywhere dawg? You know we probably the first black guys to look for it. I Don’t like the affect white people havin’ on yo dawg” Duzza:“ Player please, I ain’t out here to look for bigfoot, I’m out here so I can get away from it all, y’know. What’s better than bein’ out in the wild wit’ three of my boys, hey maybe we’ll catch a bigfoot and then my worth will go up even mo’. I’ll be $50 million instead of $40” Jay:“ That would be fly dawg. Yo man these woods go on for ever, anything could be hidin’ up in here dawg, we been drivin’ along this road, surrounded by trees and we seen what? One car? You sho’ this even the right way?” Duzza:“ Well this is the only road we done seen for an hour ain’t it? This has to be the right one” The night gets darker and eventually they arrive at a well-lit camp site. There are about four other cars there. All big jeeps. Three people come out to see them. Two of them white, one Native American Guy:“ Hey, glad you could make it” Duzza:“ Well, we did get lost back there, but we here now” All of the gear is unpacked and taken into a large tent. All together there are about 16 people at the camp, including Duzza and friends. They all sit round a camp fire, one of the heads of the search explains everything that will be happening. What to do when in trouble etc etc. At about 12.00 everybody goes back to their tents to get some rest. Duzza and his friends all sleep in a big 6 man tent. At about 1.30 Duzza is awaken by a small sound, he looks over to see Kyle holding a pocketsize TV. Duzza:“ Nigga, what tha fuck yo doin’?” Kyle looks around with a shocked face, realising that he’s been busted, he drops the TV and does a terrible job of hiding it Kyle:“ Err I don’t know what you talkin’ ‘bout Duzz” Duzza:“ Fool I saw yo wit’ that TV? How dumb you think I am dawg? Do I look like Dexter Thomas? Damn Kyle, go to sleep” At 6.00AM they are woken up by the camp leader, to much complaining from Duzza. Some one who is not really a morning person. At 7.00AM the group of 16 trek of into the surrounding woodland All armed with hunting rifles. And all the men carrying camping equipment on there backs, they trek through the dense woodland of California. Two men are on horses and three hunting dogs are with them. They walk through the woods all morning with out seeing a single animal, no deer, nothing. One of the leaders of the party, Earl talks to Duzza Earl:“ Duzz, I saw you getting beaten up the other day, you looked pretty beaten up” Duzza:“ Shut the fuck up man. I came here to get away from all that dawg, not to be reminded of it by a Native American, gees” Earl:“ Sorry Duzza, have you noticed how the woods seem practically empty? No bears or elk, nothing” Duzza:“ Yeah ‘sup wit’ that dawg?” Earl:“ I think it’s a good sign” Duzza:“ Your tha expert” After some more walking they come to Bluff Creek. The very place that the most famous piece of bigfoot footage ever captured was filmed. Nothing has changed in this place from 1967. Still the same fallen tree’s across the ground, it is untouched by time. Kyle:“ So this is tha place where they saw tha other bigfoot, hmm I don’t see anything” Suddenly Earls’ horse starts acting crazy, jumping around snorting, Earl just manages to stay on and three other men have to hold onto the horse, after a few seconds the horse calms down. Earl has a wide smile on his face Duzza:“ What you smilin’ ‘bout dawg? You almost got thrown to Miami by that horse” Earl:“ Oh Duzz, it’s close, it’s very close” Jay:“ Oh please say your talkin’ ‘bout something other than bigfoot” Earl:“ No my friends I am talking about sasquatch” Jay:“ Aww man, we gonna die, it’s gonna kill us all, I’m too pretty to die” Duzza:“ Shut up n*gga, you actin’ like a white boy. Jus’ keep it real, ain’t nobody gon’ be killed by bigfoot” Jay:“ Ok ok Duzz, I belive ya, man now I need to use tha bathroom, aww man hope we come to like a bathroom or motel or somethin’ soon” Earl:“ No chance, were about a 40 minute walk from the neareast town” Duzza:“ Jay, your in the woods, fool. You don’t need a bathroom, jus’ use tha woods dawg” Jay:“ You want me to go off the trail, of the safe trail and into the wood bit? You crazy?!” Duzza:“ What? What you been smokin’ dawg, every one goes in the woods dawg” Jay:“ There’s a bigfoot in there! Fool. I seen all these horror movies dawg, and you know what I found out?” Duzza:“ No, but I guess ya gon’ tell me” Jay:“ There’s either no brothers or tha brothers always die first. I ain’t gon’ die in the middle of the woods” Duzza:“ Then ya jus’ gonna have to hold it a’ight” Earl:“ You two be quiet I think we about to see something” Slowly all of the party go around a massive redwood, suddenly all of them and stunned into silence. Right infront of them, with it’s back to them is a real-life sasquatch. Sitting on a fallen tree trunk, just resting. Slowly all the men raise their rifles, then suddenly ‘beep beep bee beep’ a cell phone goes of. Suddenly the massive, 8 foot tall, 400 lbs bigfoot jumps up turns around and stares at the group then lets out a massive, bone chilling roar before turning and running into the woods at remarkable speed, rifles are fired but none hit the bigfoot. Earl:“ NOOOOO. I Don’t believe it, we was so close, whose fucking cell phone rang? Whose was it?” Everybody turns and looks at a nervous looking Duzza, who is holding a cell phone shaking his head Duzza:“ Err sorry” Earl:“ Sorry, sorry? Your just blew, the most perfect chance I have ever had of catching or taking a photo of a sasquatch. Do you know how rich I could have been? Do you know?” Duzza:“ Err very rich?” Earl:“ Yeah I would be very, very rich. What the fuck you got a cell phone for anyway?” Duzza:“ Well I didn’t think I would receive any signal in the middle of nowhere” The cell rings again and this time Duzza picks it up Duzza:“ Err who is this?” Voice:“ This is Statuz dawg! How you doin dawg?” Duzza:“ Whyyyyyyyy!”
