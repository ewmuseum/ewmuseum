Back to Basics Pt V: End

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/04/2003 “Fuck!” Jade grunted as she span on her side. There was a large black shot gun pointed in her face. It did not move as Jade attempted to swat the gun out of her eyes. Jade sat up against the wall and saw the outline of a shadowy man stood in the doorway, the end of his gun pressed harshly into her face. “I suppose this is the part where you shoot me and my brains go splat against the wall and then I wake up again after Mani killing my friends Hmm?” Jade rushed out her sentence in one breath as she pressed her palms down onto her thighs, rising to a standing position. She lets out a grunt as a strong hand grips around her entire 14 inch muscled bicep. “No shooting? This isn’t right!” Fear stung the back of her throat as she was yanked out of the room; fear or bile. As the man drags Jade onto the area between all of the room’s the light illuminates his muscled ebon body as he puts his shotgun into a tan leather holster attached to his chest. “No killing Jade! There will be just a slight torturing, and lots and lots of blood, sharp objects, blunt objects and bribery. You know the kind! You taught me well!” Jades opened in pure shock and agony as his fingernails dig deeper into her skin. Her eyes close again filled with detestation. Jade grabs the mans wrist and yanks it into the air the wrong way. His body twists slightly so as not to snap the wrist he has seized. Jade jumps onto the railings, grabbing a piece of his matted hair and kicks the man in the chest. “Not well enough apparently…Damien.” It was the man she had given a job after she had last taken back her club. How she had employed him for him to no doubt leak information to Mani. He was a rat and the filthiest kind. Jade was obviously in a high state of adrenaline rush and in a most angered disposition. “You disappoint me so. How you were not prepared for that to happen to you? I don’t know. Silly and stupid! I am not suprised I ditched you for Carys. She has so much more talent than you it is shocking.” Jade says as she confidently struts down the stairs. Her long black finger nails scratch into the railing, cracking the paint work as she does so. “Now tell me how many more of these imbecilic idiots are left or did Mani just…” Jade giggles, unable to finish her sentence. “…leave you in charge?” As Jade reaches the bottom stair she instantly realises that she spoke far too soon as Damien grips her ankle. His hazel eyes surge open as he flings her by the leg into the wall. Jade bounces face first from the wall back into the stair. Her grip on reality becomes a little blurred as she lifts her hand up to her head. As she looks at her rubs the red liquid, blood between her fingertips, it is now tainting her brunette/white hair all the way down to the tips of her hair. Her eyes begin to go a little cross eyed as she lifts her head off of the step. “Real…subtle…Jade.” Jade mumbles as blood drips down the side of her face. She manages to stay awake all apart from the fist which follows into her gut. “Bet ya think that hurts huh?” Jades eyes roll into the back of her head. -[Fade]- Jade had been ‘out’ for about four hours. It had given her time to think, to evaluate her life. There were a hell of a lot of people playing in her brain and it wasn’t something that could be ignored, no longer. Part of her thinks she was out for so long because of her need to think things over. She barely even slept these days and that –was- her thinking time. Well when she got down to it the root of her problems was trying to manage to lead her double life. On one hand she had the PCW life; full of money, beatings, friends and enemies. The friends of course falling under the Lacuna and Chris Storm category. Then there was this life; the odd beating here and there, More often than not there were vicious enemies, torturing…soon to come no doubt, drugs, hookers and weapons of death. There is a small tub of smelling salts wafted beneath the nose of Jade. Her eyes flutter open as she groans, the throbbing pain flooding through her brain. “Son of a …” Jade screamed with absolute agony. Never before had she even reached a pitch close to this as a needle was placed into her arm. Not a knitting needle, not a sewing needle but a drugs needle, it rested inside her bluest vein as the top was slowly pushed in, emptying its contents into Jades veins. Her eyes fluttered as the back of Damien’s hand was plastered against her cheek. “Filth.” Damien whacks a fist against her face and her head slings backwards. “Scum.” Jade fires another dizzied word at him. “What is this shit?” “It is a present from you dear cousin.” Jade tries to look at him but she can not see straight. “Coke! In case you were wondering whore. I suggested to Mani that you may do well working with all the other cows we have, making jolly with punters for high amounts of cash. Alas, he had bigger and need I say better plans for you.” Jade seems to fall back unconscious. Damien put his hand under Jades chin presumably to check her pulse. He obviously feels the wrong place as he leans in his head and tries to listen for breathing. Jade hogs back and sends a flying ball of phlegm and blood into the face of the torturer. Damien clicks his fingers and a large skinny man steps into this picture of fun and happiness. He is wearing a full black suit and white shirt as he pulls out a small black zip up bag. There are an assortment of knives and needles, and general torture toys. Jades eyes light up “What…now you don’t even…” She gags under the pressure that has been placed onto her stomach. “Now you don’t even torture people yourself? You really are a disappointment, scraped the bottom of the barrel when I found you didn’t I? You pathetic little cocksucker!” Damien hits Jade around the face. “Are you getting weaker?” Jade snorts as blood pours from a dried scab beneath her nose. “Do not hit the subject anymore! You’re taking my fun away you idiot!” The man in the suit shouts. “How am I supposed to torture somebody who is half dead?” The man attempts to push the ox, Damien, out of his way so he can carry on where he left off. Jade’s eyes groggily light up. She looks at the heat between the two men and realises that an angry Damien is as hard to control as probably anything on this planet. “You’re gonna take that shit from this skinny little fuck up of a man?” And so the mind games began. “Look at him Damien? He should be doing what you say! You’re the big dog here right? Well sick him.” Jade growls as some form of dementia sets in on her. The coke begins to take full effect as it swirls around with her blood. Her head nods up and down dizzily. “Are you his bitch or something Damien? I mean look, I am coked to the eye balls and this bitch is bossing you around like he runs the joint. I mean I can’t even feel a thing right now and you hired him to torture me why.” The man sticks a spike into Jades leg and Jade in all her coke headed glory Jade giggles. “See?” Damien picks the 200 pound man up above his head and tosses him against the bar. Glass shards ricochet with drops of blood everywhere. “Now lets get down to the real business.” -[Fade]- “No! You don’t do it like that!” Jade mumbles, sweat caressing her forehead as she falls in and out of consciousness. “You need to twist a little more to get the total pain out of it you here?” Jade was annoying the absolute ass off of him and he hated every minute of it. “Oh give it here!” Jade grabs the knife and pulled it out. “Now watch!” Damien wasn’t the brightest of creatures and this just proved it. Jade jammed the knife into his leg and gave a loud crunch when it twisted. “Did I like, break something?” Jade chuckled to herself. “Enough of this bullshit! We are both here for the same reason! Ransom money baby!” Damien was delighted at the sound of money in his pocket. “That plan is a little feeble if I do say so myself. Who the hell is going to pay money for a tramp like me to be saved?” Jade was a little down to say the least. Too much crap was being thrown at her. A tear chased down the rosiness of her cheek. “You should have kidnapped Lacuna, and then at least somebody would have given a damn!!” Her emotions were becoming vocal. Damien hits Jade again. “Shut up, the bitch will save you! You have a bond that can not be denied! Jade ridicules Damien’s claims until Damien throws a phone at her. “Ring this rich bitch!” Jade taps in the numbers blearily as Damien holds her head up. “She aint gonna pay!” Jade had no confidence in anything at the moment. Goodness knows how that will affect her when it comes down to the match at Tuesday Turmoil but now was what mattered. -[Fade]- Jade grinned, a few minutes had passed since she had rang Lacuna and Jades own words rang in her head. “You don’t have it?” Jesus that was harsh. “Could Steve bring it perhaps?” That was the positive reaction Jade had gotten from Lacuna and it all resulted in this… Jade was being carried out of the building over Lacuna’s shoulder. Blood pretty much covered the whole room. Jade was unconscious with blood all over her; not just her own. Lacuna hols her much loved Steve in her hands as what was Damien lies in a bloody heap with half of his face blown off. How? Or is the real question. Who?
