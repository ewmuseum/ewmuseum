Continental Champ? With This Secret Weapon, Yep

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.5.02(#2 FF, #2 AD) “Welcome to a very special day, ladies and gentlemen.” The voice that greets us to open the promo, of course, belongs to the snide Continental Champion contender, Rush Mason. “You, the most devoted followers of Bon Dieu’s teachings are here to see another of my masterpieces.” One might question what other masterpieces Rush has thought up, but the attention is now diverted to the scene, as it is totally faded in. We can see Rush Mason standing in front off some sort of square-shaped object. The object, however, is blocked by a large blue tarp over. Rush: Now, I warn you, this must remain a secret. He seems to be addressing the crowd of ten or so people that are standing around him, quite a diverse crowd, but apparently fellow Voodoo subscribers like Rush. Ray Ray, the always-eloquent manager, is standing to the left of Rush, looking down at a large switchboard of flashing buttons and switches. Rush: If this weapon were to fall into the hands of Tim Walden…well, the circumstances would be catastrophic. Walden might….actually win a match…without the assistance of his pet whore! A gasp is elicited from the crowd, shocked at the ramifications that losing this secret weapon could bring. Tim Walden winning a match without his cheating fiends of a stable at his side could very well bring the apocalypse and end life as we know it. Rush: Now, I will release to all of you my secret for winning the Continental Title on Fusion. Ray Ray…hit the switch! Were Ray Ray a competent and understanding companion, the dramatic effect would be followed by a swift unveiling of the box-shaped mystery “thing,” but of course Ray Ray is not any of those things, he’s simply Ray Ray. Rush: Ray Ray…please tell me that you remembered what you’re doing. Obviously, the giant hasn’t remembered his duties, as he looks utterly baffled by the switchboard in front of him. He scratches his head as he replies, partly to Rush, and partly just to himself. Ray Ray: It’s…this….one? As the switch is hit, Ray Ray watches on as a ring of fire comes up around Rush’s visitors, as the Voodoo Villain smacks his forehead. He walks over to the switchboard and hits the corresponding button for releasing the tarp, as the ring of fire dies down around his visiting fellow Voodoo’ers, who may be a bit singed, but relatively un-harmed. Rush: It’s my community service. I hired a retard off the streets to keep him safe. The crowd, many of them brushing themselves off or pinching themselves to make sure this isn’t a dream, looks up at Rush, mostly looking rather upset at the little fire shenanigan. Rush: Any way….Behold my creation! The crowd does avert their gaze to the cube, which is transparent and inside is the true surprise, which is pointed out, due to the amazing observation of one of the crowd, who makes his statement in a rather disappointed tone. “That’s just a guy….in a box.” However, Rush is quick to contradict another one of Bon Dieu’s children, giving him the scoop on what, indeed, seems to be just a man in a box. He sits at a small desk, apparently scribbling something down as Rush looks in and grins. Rush: To the untrained eye, perhaps. Ray Ray, show them exactly what this little fella does. This time, Ray Ray is able to pull off his task with little trouble. He speaks into the microphone on the switchboard, calling in to the man in the glass cube. Ray Ray: Dawg…add forty-two to sixty seven fifths…then multiply it by three and…ya know…square root it. Despite not knowing even what addition means, Ray Ray gives the orders with remarkable poise and confidence, trying to regain the adoration of Rush’s visitors. Rush looks back to his small crowd of followers and points into the cube, dictating the purpose of this secret as his caged weapon begins to work. Rush: You see, as all of you know, I will be facing the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid in the attempt to capture the belt that I so deserve. A customary fan of PCW, or wrestling in general, would not be encouraging of such a cowardly fool holding a Primetime Title, but these pilgrims of the Voodoo faith are totally behind Rush and what he stands for, so they nod, though the connection between this man and the HIT is uncertain as of now. Rush: So…what is the one thing that the HIT loves to gloat about, over others? Other than the fact that he’s pinned Walden? But when you think about it, who hasn’t pinned the Lackmeister? To be truthful, the Voodoo Villain himself hasn’t pinned the Financial Reclamations Officer, despite numerous encounters in cluster matches. However, the visitors don’t wish to point this out, as they continue to wait for the reasoning as to how Rush’s secret weapon will work. Rush: The answer, of course…is his extraordinary math skills. Trapezoid just loves to point out the square root of the homo sapien species’ median fertility rate…all of that nonsense. So, what better way to combat the genius than with an equal? And still, the crowd fails to see the connection, but the man in the box has been standing for a while now, and now as Rush walks over, the cubed one passes the paper under the glass. Rush: And we should have your proof right here… Grinning widely at his project’s success, Rush holds up a paper, which brings a few stifled giggles from his crowd, as one of the guests points out a small flaw. “He’s a phallic artist?” Rush, somewhat red-faced, looks down at the piece of paper, the side of which he’s looking at it features a rather non-flattering picture of male genitalia. He throws a glare up at Ray Ray, who responds with his ordinary shrug, and then flips the paper over, which features the number “12.891857895586.” Rush: Check it with a calculator…but that there is as good as any one you can find. His name…? The crowd is now listening intently, more interested in this secret weapon, of sorts, now that the real point of it has been seen. Rush:…the Calculating Lowly Isosceles Trapezoid. The C.L.I.T for short. The seemingly mature crowd has to work quite hard to keep from bursting out in laughter. With that, Rush begins to end his presentation as the scene begins to fade out, but not before Ray Ray poses a question to Rush. “Why wouldn’t the HIT just attack that dude and then continue with the match?” “I mean, c’mon what else does the guy have other than his math nerdmanship? He’s gotta defend the geekiness of his kind…”
