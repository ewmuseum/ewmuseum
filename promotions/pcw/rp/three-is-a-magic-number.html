Akiyama held two belts, and will gain another soon…Yes, he will…He will have three belts won by the NVG…That's a magic number. Akiyama’s drawn the third spot in the Madness battle royal...how fitting for him anyway...since he is the Neo Void God and three times any man within the battle. When looking at him deeper, the god harbors not just one...but THREE conscious beings within him...resulting in the plural dialogue... Three...is a magic number... Akira Akiyama knew of the power of mathematics…possibly more than that Hardcore Isosceles Triangle chap. But this time there was a problem dealing with a certain number that he and only he could figure out. The number...3... Three has always haunted him since the day he was born. Born on March third (which is ironically today), the third day of the third month...in the year 1974...Nineteen Seventy-four. Add both of those numbers up together and what do you get? 93...9...3... The number nine’s always managed to sneak into his life as well. Add all of the numbers up in 1974 and you get 21. Add 2 and 1 up...and we’re back to three... 3... It really doesn’t matter about the number that Akiyama is coming in on. It will only prove to multiply his already intense fighting skills. It’s just another training routine to him...and this time he gets to come in early and test both his endurance and stamina against twenty other men. He’s toppled more men in longer amounts of time...and this time there will be no difference. Now…let’s get on with the life of Akira Akiyama… “Don’t peek!” “Quit Squawking. We’re a god. Blindfolding us wont matter. We’re all seeing and all knowing.” Tumble…tumble…tumble…WHAM!! “ARRRGH!!!” “Watch out for those stairs…mister all seeing and all knowing.” “We’ll have you filing paperwork in hell if you do something like that again.” “No you wont…you know you can’t live without me.” “We can frickin try…” “What was that, Aki?” “Nothing, Yuu…let’s just get this over with.” We find the NVG standing in a parking lot somewhere in St Louis, Missouri. The day is clear, perfect for a battle… …Or more importantly…a birthday… Yuu stood next to him in a pair of tight white vinyl Capri pants, white-heeled sandals with calf straps, and a white spandex bikini top with white glitter all over it. Her light brown hair was tied back in a ponytail like the god’s, and on her face was a pair of white sunglasses. Akiyama was dressed in his battle gear…having been training for the match tonight previous to the interruption. Covering his eyes was a black bandana. “This had better be good.” Griped the god. “To interrupt us in the middle of our strenuous training results in Void Banishment…but you’re an exception to the works.” “You’re going to love this!” she said with a shriek of excitement in her voice. “No…what we’re going to love is doing battle with all of those others inside that ring tonight. What we’re going to love is winning that title. What we’re going to love is~~” “CHUCK-E CHEESE!!” Yuu ripped off the blindfold and held her hands up to unveil the building before them. The hideous cartoon rodent sat on the banner grinning down at him, and Akiyama knew of his current location… “Hell…” The camera shifts from its current location to inside of the building. The first image caught is that of a leg. The camera slowly moves up but just then everyone is caught off guard with the loud 'buzz' of a noisemaker. The camera moves back a small bit to show a startled Akiyama.) “YUU CUT IT OUT!” he glared over to his right, where Yuu stood next to him in the dining area. Up on stage the animatronic vermin dressed in clown suits danced around as their mouths open and closed to the voices on the speakers. “ But Aki...this is your birthday party!” Yuu blows the noisemaker again as Akiyama lets out a low growl. He grabs the noisemaker and throws it in the trash can near by. Behind Akiyama stands The Pharaoh who has his arms crossed. The camera pans once more and shows Jay Sylo who has a 'not to happy' look on his face. Yuu rushes over to him and puts a birthday hat on Sylo's head. “Must I be ridiculed like this Akiyama?” he asked feeling degraded. “Just ignore her...she will go away sooner or later…or be banished into a mysterious realm of nothingness…whatever comes first. Sylo sighed. “Please...sooner.” The Pharaoh, Akiyama, and Sylo all look a bit stunned as Yuu begins to clap and skips greets the newest guest. Standing in the entrance is 'Invincible' Tim Walden. “ I'm here.....” he muttered confused. “Don’t know why I was invited…but I’m not gonna complain when I could get banished for not showing.” “Yippy!” Sylo turns his attention back down to Akiyama. “I really don't have time for this, I have a rising star title shot coming up and I need to be focused. “How do you think we feel? We have a world title shot and yet Yuu is pulling this shit at the last minute. Lets just go with it, what's the worst that can happen?” Sylo snarls a small bit as the Pharaoh begins to fall asleep. Sylo jabs him in the ribs and quickly he leaps back to attention. “HUH WHAT?!” “At least try to stay awake....if we have to suffer consciously, then you do too.” A few minutes later there is another guest. Dkay Calyx comes strolling in with a golden DJ Box with the words Neo Void DJ written on the bottom. Inside is a CD of the Gods of techno LP. He strolls over to Akiyama with it. “Happy birthday man!” he exclaimed with a smile. The god shrugged, “Thanks...we think.” “ So Akiyama...how about making me the god of DJ's?” Akiyama raised his eyebrow at him, wondering if he was serious or what. “We will… uhm… think about it...” Dkay walks away and begins to bug The Pharaoh. Just then a few other people enter the room. Pretty soon the room is full as Akiyama reaches over pulling Yuu close to him. “How many people did you invite?” Yuu beamed out with her smile. “All of PCW!” Yuu said this with total enthusiasm. Akiyama's jaw drops as she skips off toward the refreshments. Then Darkstar walks into the room and slaps Akiyama on his back. “Happy birthday, man. Isn’t it good to see another day alive?” “At this exact moment…no.” the NVG replied. Then from behind a swarm of screams are heard. Akiyama looked out to the arcade area to find Clyde and Bethany terrorizing the kids. “Hey! Come try one of uncle Clyde’s candied apples!” Bethany handed one to a small girl, who sniffed it and dropped it to the ground. “That doesn’t smell like candy! It smells like bad meat!!” “Well, it is candied with the blood of your parents.” Bethany remarked in a matter of fact tone. “You think they would leave you here alone?” With that said, Beth grabbed the girl by her hair and shoved her face down into the apple rolling along the floor. Akiyama shook his head with a sigh as DA MAN stepped over to greet DA GOD. “Hey…I hope you die on the same day you were born…so that I can laugh at the irony of it.” He handed the NVG a box and walked over to the refreshments. “We got you a bunny rabbit.” Akiyama shook the box around; there was no movement, but a solid item within. “Did you forget to put holes in the box?” Clyde gave him a look that screamed, “Get real.” “Nope.” And with that, he moved on to plunder the food. Akiyama shook his head as the Violator came in. Aki looked down at him, as the professional jobber waved in a friendly fashion. “Hiya!” the Violator said clear as day. “Happy Birthday!” The entire room froze, all eyes locked dead on the Violator. Akiyama glared at him, keeping his voice calm. “Did you bring a gift?” “Um…er…” he looked around nervously. “I am the Violator?” Akiyama roared out and grabbed him by his face. And with one mighty swing, he threw the PCW jobber across the room into the animatronics. The Violator looked up as Akiyama threw out his hand at him, uttering those magic words of dismissal. A huge black portal opened up and swallowed him whole. The entire room exploded in applause for the NVG, all except for Clyde and Bethany. “D@mn it!” Bethany growled. “Violator drove us over here! Now who’s driving us back to the arena?!” Clyde shrugged. “Um…you?” Bethany mumbled as she walked towards the door. “Yeah, see how long you live when I get behind the wheel.” Akiyama turned back to find a loser in a huge cow suit prancing around the room. He shook his head as the cow approached the area where Sylo was sitting. Sylo was trying to ignore it with all his might, but the cow had the audacity to continue to annoy him. Then the cow touched him on his shoulder. “That’s it.” Sylo stood up and kicked the cow in its gut. Then he lifted him up and powerbombed him through the refreshment table. He pointed down at the fallen sow while looking at Akiyama. “Akiyama…banish this heifer.” The Neo Void God shook his head in shame. “We can’t take this. We’re leaving.” Yuu ran up to him with a disappointed look on her face. “What? You can’t leave! It’s your birthday!” “Of course we can! We don’t have to sit here and take this crap from you or anybody else! We have a title match that we’re very busy training for in the wee hours before! You’re wasting our time with all of this nonsense!” Yuu lowered her head in sadness at the outburst of the NVG. Akiyama looked over her shoulder, where her hands were behind her back. He pointed to whatever was behind her back slowly. “What is that?” She brought it around to show Aki. In her hands was a small white red velvet cake with the words, “Happy Birthday, and Good Luck Aki!” written on top in red icing. Everyone watched the two of them stand there in the middle of the room, the tension as high as the ceiling. Akiyama took the cake and looked at the candles atop of it. Three… He blew them out and smiled. Yuu’s eyes lit back up at the sight of Akiyama’s appreciation. He gave her a hug with one arm tightly. He did have more important things to take care of…a world title match tonight…a TV title match on Fusion…but this time they would have to wait… Three… It’s a magic number…
