The Life of a Future Dictator Isn't All Peaches and Cream

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13/02 (The up-tempo beat of the Gipsy King's 'Bamboleo'--made famous by the Burger King commercials which feature it, plays as the skyline of beautiful Houston, Texas is shown set against a clear blue sky on this, a warm near-Spring day in March. The music gets louder as the view comes closer to the city's center and farther from the bluish-green waters of the Gulf of Mexico. The surroundings pass by quickly until the view finally stops at a towering green-glass paneled skyscraper. A silver stretch limo parked out in front of the building looks suspiciously familiar as 6 Hispanic men in dark blue suits and brown trenchcoats stand guard around it with their hands in their coats and their sunglass-covered eyes constantly darting around. Seeing through the empty spaces between the guards, we see mini Argentinean flags perched atop the ends of the car's hood and trunk. The flags fly gently and proudly with the slight coastal breeze. Upon further examination of the important car, two square cut-outs in the Plexiglas-looking windows display some kind of black metal barrels protruding slightly from the interior. The presence of the snooping camera evokes a frown from a guard and a subsequent call into his radio.) Guard: 'Tigre dos cinco, tigre dos cinco. Está usted allí? Cambio.' (An unintelligible reply emits from the radio.) Guard: 'Tenemos una cifran siete dos uno. Cambio.' (Another unintelligible reply emits from the radio.) Guard: 'Si, punta be-ese-quatro. Una alerta, por favor. Cambio.' (A brief pop of static is heard as the guard looks up to a building across the street and nods. The camera begins to turn around just as a piercing shot from a rifle can be heard and the air only centimeters away from the camera is cut through with a sharp whoosh!) Cameraman: 'Holy fucking mother of Christ! Someone's shooting at me!' (The picture spins around rapidly and a bouncing shot of the street and lower parts of buildings comes in and out of view as the cameraman runs rapidly out of the area. Boisterous laughs from the guards can be heard along with the poor cameraman's heavy breathing.) --SCENE CHANGE-- (We are now seemingly in the building that was previously shown, as we can see the limo, guards, and running cameraman--which all look like tiny ants through the window of the very high vantage point. The music of the Gipsy Kings nears its end as the view backs of from the window to absorb the rest of the room--which is a classy office filled with paintings and marble busts of various leaders and conquerors such as Alexander the Great, Julius Caesar, Augustus, Caligula, Vespasian, Aurelian, Constantine the Great, Gengis Khan, Napoleon, and even Adolf Hitler. Among these images of saints and monsters, we come across a huge painting of someone who dwarfs them all--former PCW World, 2-time Continental, Television, Cruiserweight, Rising Star, and Hardcore Champion Hall of Famer, Frisco. The painting features a vast ring with decomposing bodies of men Frisco has defeated stacked up sloppily on top of each other, and a God-like rendition of Frisco standing on top of the stack with a belt held to the lightened-break in a dark, stormy sky. The scene immediately cuts from this morbid painting to the man himself--who looks like just as much of a mortal as anyone else, with bags under his dark eyes and a pale look on his weathered face. Frisco takes his gaze off a stack of papers on his desk to look up at the camera and looks back down again before immediately snapping back up to speak.) Frisco: 'Ummm, how did you get in here?' Cameraman #2: 'I...uhh..we..uh...' (Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'Let me rephrase the question...why are you here?' Cameraman #2: 'I'm..uhh...from PCW..you have a promo to cut...remember?' (Frisco lets out a sigh.) Frisco: 'Oh right, I have to cut promos. It's all part of the deal right? If I'm on TV to hurt people and win titles, I have to talk about hurting people and winning titles too. Is that it?' Cameraman #2: 'Yeah...umm..sure.' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'No, I'm correct.' Cameraman #2: 'Yes, yes..of course, sir.' Frisco: 'Why can't they just leave me be and allow me to have a match whenever I please without all this talking nonsense? Don't they know I have more important things to attend to?' (A black phone on Frisco's large oak desk rings loudly. Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'See what I mean?' (Frisco presses a button and speaks.) Frisco: 'Yes?' (A soft female voice emits from the speaker.) Secretary: 'Mr. Valerius, your agent is on line two.' (Frisco rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'Thank you, Maria. Go ahead and transfer the call.' Secretary: 'Yes, sir.' Frisco: *Under his breath* 'Motherfucking bloodsucking douchebag.' (A click is heard, followed by a man's voice.) Agent: 'How's my favorite client!?' (Frisco perks himself up from his frowning state with a fake smile.) Frisco: 'Rob, my good man! Nice to hear from you again.' Agent: 'I can't express how happy I am that you decided to continue your wrestling career. After your injury in December, I was truly scared that you would finally call it quits.' (Frisco smirks.) Frisco: 'Like I'm really going to retire before completing my title sweep. Come on, Robert, you know me better than that--or have you been concentrating on socializing with the money you take from me more than your beloved client?' Agent: *Nervously* 'Ohh, come on, champ! You're a true pal! I don't know what I would do without your guidance and advice. You are more of an asset to me than the money you pay me.' Frisco: 'Now you're overrating me.' Agent: 'Good one, champ. Anyway, I just wanted to let you know that management is trying to get a hold of you. They really need you to cut some promos for the upcoming match. They're a little disappointed that you didn't cut any for the last one.' (Frisco looks up at the camera and back down at the speaker phone.) Frisco: 'Yeah, I know, I'm on it. They'll suck, but I have little time to deal with entertaining the masses. I'm much too busy trying to secure my spot to be the voice of the masses.' Agent: 'Huh? What's that supposed to mean?' (Frisco rolls his eyes and dips down to the speaker.) Frisco: 'Hello, fucktard!? My presidential race?' (A cough and laugh emit from the speaker.) Agent: 'Wh--wha-what are you talking about? You mean....you were serious?' Frisco: 'When am I not serious? You didn't think my speech in front of hundreds and thousands of Argentineans was a pure publicity stunt, now did you?' Agent: 'Well, uhh...yeah, I did...I thought it was a pretty good one at that.' Frisco: 'Yes, dear Robert, I am running for president of Argentina. My country is in a perilous, uncertain time in its history, and it needs a leader. Have you seen the brainless puppets they have in charge of my land? There's a new one every week, for fuck's sake. The people of Argentina need someone who will be constant and strong. They need a man who will organize every aspect of the economy and polish every bit of the Argentinean lifestyle. Wrestling is a petty sport--a vastly unimportant endeavor for a man of my stature.' Agent: 'Please don't say that.' Frisco: 'It's only a matter of time before I'm out of it, but I just need one more run to complete my legacy and show the people of my homeland that their future leader is someone who will never leave something unfinished.' Agent: 'Does that mean you're going to get a sex change and win the Women's Championship too?' (Frisco ponders the statement for a moment.) Frisco: 'No. Please don't mock me or I'll give your mother a filthy Sanchez with a red-hot poker.' Agent: 'Heeyyyyy! I'm just messing with you champ!' (Frisco twitches and reaches under his desk to take out a bottle of STOLICHNAYA VODKA~!) Frisco: 'Is there anything else you wish to tell me?' (Frisco pours the delicious vodka into a martini glass and drops a small red chile pepper into the clear liquid.) Agent: 'Yes! We have another endorsement offer!' (Frisco smiles as he swishes around his martini and takes a sip.) Frisco: 'Fucking marvelous. From whom?' Agent: 'Goya...they make food products such as beans, dried chilies and spices...' Frisco: 'I am aware of Goya's fine products.' Agent: 'Great, great! So, what do you think?' Frisco: 'I approve of their endorsement..however, instead of making the check out to me, I would appreciate the donation to go to my campaign funds. Tell them to make the check out to El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina. I'll send a representitive to sign the contract for me.' Agent: 'Umm..well...' Frisco: 'Is that a problem?' Agent: 'No...not, not at all. I'll call them up right away.' (Frisco nods and takes a sip of his martini.) Frisco: 'Beautiful. Goodbye.' Agent: 'Wai...' (Frisco presses a button on his phone that cuts off the voice.) Frisco: 'Now, where was I?' Camerman #2: 'Huh?' (Frisco rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'I was talking to you about something, you inconsiderate goat-felcher. Do you remember what that was?' Cameraman #2: 'Well..uhh...' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'I swear to fuck, nobody is helping me one bit. Do you know how hard it is to run for president? I have to make appearances after appearances and spend dollar after dollar just to have a chance. This campaign has already sucked nearly 3/4ths of my income from me. If I don't start winning crap, I'm going to retire to a goddamn oolitic limestone cave in Cyprus and spend all day getting fat off bitter, rancid grapefruits.' (The cameraman blinks.) Frisco: 'I'm kidding!' (Frisco cracks a slight smile.) Cameraman #2: 'Oh..haha.' (Frisco starts laughing louder and the cameraman joins in before Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'Shut up!' (The cameraman jerks up and nods.) Frisco: 'Anyway, me and Legend Inc. right? Taking on some other guys, right? For the Tag Titles?' Cameraman #2: 'Yes.' Frisco: 'Oh, alright then. That's all we need to say.' Cameraman #2: 'Ummm...' (Frisco looks down at his stack of papers and back up impatiently.) Frisco: 'Good day.' Cameraman #2: 'Yes, sir.' (Frisco gives a dismissal gesture as he sifts through a stack of documents. The scene zooms past Frisco's shoulder and through the window into the busy Houston metropolis until the feed goes off.)
