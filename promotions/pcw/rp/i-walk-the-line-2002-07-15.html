I Walk The Line.

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/15/02 -Duke Williams is dressed as always in Black from head to toe. His hat is arched slightly to the left and the only color on him is a PCW logo on his shirt. He walks along the side of a road in what looks like an urban US city. A Pcw camera crew follows him around.- Williams: Ahhh buckaroo’s its scamp Williams time again. -Duke starts to applaud.- Williams: So you kidnapped Mr. Johnny Cash. A 70 year old man who’s dying. John is in touch with his God. Death does not scare him nor does he being your captive frighten me. John is a man who always walked the line of darkness and light with out ever stumbling to one side. He sang songs of prison, oppression, love, murder, jealousy, and loath. -Duke waves to a fan who yells his name.- Williams: Yet on the same albums he sang of salvation, redemption, God, and blind faith. You can pick up a digital bible where Johnny Cash reads versus to you. So you taking him in as a prisoner of war is one I’m willing to accept. To quote a Johnny Cash song “I shot a man in Reno just to watch him die.”. Lets play. HUH? -Duke rubs his nose with his thumb.- Williams: So I fired you. You seem to have a real hard on about that. It seems to rub your balls the wrong way. -Duke spits on the sidewalk- Williams: But really could you blame me? Look at you. You’re pathetic; Clyde. You have the work rate of PWA amateur. You even work out hoss? I can stab, jab, and burn, and get just as evil as you. However when it comes to getting in the ring I can wrestle, fight, and move. You my friend are a stiff. -Duke shakes his head agree with his statement.- Williams: You’re a f*cking loser Clyde. Where are your fans man. Where are the guys who pay money to see you. There aren’t any. The ones that do like you can’t afford to come see you. Your just not cut out to sell T-shirts. If you can’t do that then you just don’t belong. -Duke pulls a cigar out of his pocket. His hand is still bandaged up from Darkstars crucifixion of Malice. He sticks the cigar in his mouth.- Williams: A goddamn hermit. A leper. Nobody wants to be associated with you humps….whoops I take that back. I almost forgot about the aids infected Nelson. The Charles Foster Kane of PCW only nobody ever loved him. Citizen Nelson. He had it all and just like that….it was gone. -Duke snaps his finger.- Williams: Rosebud motherfucker!! Just like the Phoenix the prick rises from the ashes. Only this time he brings with him the spawns of hell. More or less the losers I fired once. I’ll fire again…and I’ll fire’um till they’re gone. Cylde I don’t care if you have Hank Williams backing your ass you still ain’t gonna jive in PCW as long as I’m around. -Duke fumbles through his pockets for a lighter.- Williams: The only thing you said that held any water was the fact the Gilbert Godfrey promo blew. Yes sir. I’m man enough to admit when I create garbage. That was garbage. On the same not it was still more enjoyable then looking at your face. -Duke finds his lighter and light up his cigar.- Williams: Then there is DarkStar. The only piece of talent this little team of tyrants has. Look at you kid. Look at your life. You have metal objects protruding from your face. -Duke takes a big puff of smoke.- Williams: Cylde, his face is all burnt and f*ck up. That’s not his fault. You on the other hand did that shit by your own free will. How do you get thought the airport hoss? -Duke mumbles to himself- Williams: Why would that hot piece of ass hang out with you? Umm I can still taste her skin. -Duke sticks out his tongue and flickers it.- Williams: I’d love to eat the shorts right off her. Anywho it still staggers my mind figuring you little rodents out. What’s the deal hoss? You can crucifix my body but you can’t hang my will. Now its my turn isn’t it. Ya see now I need to think sick. The nail gun was a nice touch. I did that. Darkstar on da boards. It’s almost halftime I need to score don’t I. -Duke rubs his beard.- Williams: Why do you whine so much DarkStar? Its not very becoming of a young star. As a matter of fact its down right annoying. Boo-hoo Duke fired me. Boo-hoo he ruined PCW. Shut the fuck up you whiny little brat. -Duke ashes into a garbage can.- Williams: You know what I have that you don’t. -Duke reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out two brass balls on a string.- Williams: Brass balls. You need to get you a set. You need to accept the fact that you were fired cus you sucked. You didn’t go to OSW cus they didn’t want you. Now you’re back against my will. So cut out the tears, get a hair cut, take those things out of your face, get some better ring attire, and lets make your career work. -Duke smiles- Williams: I can see a three sixty turn around hoss. Wait a minute. -Duke thinks that over.- Williams: Math isn’t my forte. That would be a one eighty. I can change you for the better. For me to change you one eighty I need a sixty nine with Talena. -Duke shrugs his shoulders.- I think it would be a fair trade off huh? We can make you a star, drop the dark jobber image, and I can get blown while eating out Telena. What a plan where do I sign up. We can change your theme music to some Tim McGraw. I think “Angry All The Time” would work. -Duke ponders the potential.- Williams: We can make you a legend….whoops don’t want to step on any toes. -Duke grimaces.- Williams: We can’t forget Tommy who’s keeping my title warm. If I called you a legend that would be gimmick infringement. Kano’s all ready pissed. Tommy’s not making a lot of friends as it is. So again instead of finding ways to pry that gold off of Tom’s waist I have to team with him. Now they add some other hump to the mix. Joy. I’m thrilled. I fight my battles by my self. -Duke adjusts his shades.- Williams: I don’t like help, I don’t need help, and I sure as shit didn’t ask for help. But I’m stuck with it. -Duke takes another drag on his Punch cigar.- Williams: So I’ll deal with it. How will I deal with it? That’s for me to know and you to find out. Turmoil a few fuckers are going to be sent home Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. -Duke tosses his cigar at the camaera.- Williams: Cus your mine I walk the line. =w=
