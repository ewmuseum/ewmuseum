Dare Devil...sorta. Bravery with a piercing

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-23-03 RP 2 Turmoil 25th “I need a change, something different, something crazy, something gutsy.” Teresa paces the floor of the Louisiana home she has been staying at for the past few weeks. Momma Tomas is shopping for material for a new dress while Scott has left to watch his little sister’s softball game. The only sound in the two-story house is the refrigerator motor kicking on. Teresa stops at the fireplace. Her baby blues passes over a picture of her and Scott, which rest on the mantle. The picture was taken on Valentine’s day, just a week ago. Someone from the local newspaper had snuck a photo of the couple in Baton Rouge. Scott was dressed casual, but with fashion, black slacks, a short sleeved button up shirt, and black penny loafers. His shoulder-length black hair was tied back in a slick ponytail. Scott’s sharp dressed look wasn’t the only thing that made the picture so special. This was a once in a lifetime sighting. Teresa Tomas was wearing a dress. Scott had somehow talked her into wearing it. The dress was dark purple in color and just-above-the-knee in length. Thin spaghetti straps over her shoulders held her dress up for a perfect fit. The one thing she remembers most about that night was how hard it was to walk in those heels. They had cut the picture out of the newspaper and framed it. “You’re a genius Teresa Jane Tomas. Scott is so gonna kill you.” Teresa speaks out loud and rushes out the door. Minutes later her Chevy step-side is parked in front of a tattoo parlor. The building is surprisingly well kept up and appears sanitary compared to the usual in-the-back-downstairs places. The roof is flat and blue. The rest of the building is white with samples of various tattoos painted all over, front and back. Inside, Teresa takes a seat on a small gray couch. The room was made as a living room one would have in their home, two couches, two chairs, a coffee table, and even a ceiling fan. A young boy was in a leather chair like the one you would find in a beauty salon. He definitely was a teenager and by his wild attire, it was obvious he was a metal head. He was getting an eyebrow piercing and looked awful familiar. The boy winces for a second as the small hoop is pierced through his eyebrow. A look of surprise covers his face once he glances to her direction. “Teresa? What are you getting?” “Well, hello Adam. I’m feeling a little daring today” Adam is a kid she met at a local club one night. He is a lead guitarist in his band. She periodically sees him around town and goes to his shows when she can. She sticks her tongue out after answering his question revealing she is looking to have a small piece of metal pushed through her tongue. Adam sticks his tongue out showing her his tongue ring. “It hurts like hell but only for a second. If I can do it, I know you can.” The young boy smiles at his favorite wrestler and slides out of the chair. Teresa takes his place. Adam decided to stay until it was over. The whole process didn’t take long. First she chose the size of the stud. Size 10 was chosen being that it was the smallest and most likely least painful. Teresa felt daring today but she didn’t want to push it. She can take a few falls inside and out of the ring but a sensitive body part such as her tongue is a different story. Second, she was given a mouthwash to gargle. It kills all germs in the mouth. Teresa had cheap beer that tasted better. Adam laughed at the face she made because of the foul taste. Then, she was given another mouthwash-type-substance to gargle. It numbed her tongue slightly. This was followed by her nearly numb tongue being pulled by what looked like a small set of tongs. The ring is pushed through the bottom of her tongue and a small ball is screwed on top to hold it in. “Well, what you think?” Adam asks handing Teresa a small mirror. She observes the small metal ball on her tongue and grins. “Ouch, you wasn’t kidding. That hurt like a son’bitch. I like it.”
