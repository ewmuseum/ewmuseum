Date: 29.12.02 You thought The End was a flower? I'M THE KING OF RAP AROUND HERE! Yo. Yo. They call this cat Dante Jacksonal, no hype no bars but I'm a bit original, with my punches and my sharpened #2 pencil, i've been lyrical since you hopped out of your crib at your first pinnacle, and heard a Rakim verse that you thought was criminal/1 Now that was step one, born on a written pad starring straight at the sun, look at a crime fighter shape shifter hero like Jet Li in 'The One', call me Ulaw, I am nobody's bitch, I take the microphone and make you slit yourself all at the wrist/2 Ask me what poppin in my CD changer, well there's Nas Illmatic and a few dopplegangers, but there's one verse that sticks in my head like a poking hanger, around Jon Benet's neck as she screams, 'No Daddy, don't take away my check!'/3 [Notorious BIG]I got seven Mack 11's, about eight 38'sNine 9's, ten mack tens, the shits never endsYou can't touch my richesEven if you had MC Hammer and them 357 bitches Uh, now these little boys beckon, who wants to go after the crown I reckon? There's this cat named Chet from the Georgia section, got his own talk show and uses my name as a weapon. He's doesn't realize than when I kick in the door, the man-i-ac steps in/4 What, you thought I was serious when I said step in? I really mean it so when the blood flows, police don't drag you in for questionin', The dude thought he had my blessinin', act like he was one of dem special men, now he's down six thanks to some smith and wessonin' oh/5 Play that slow music down low, so I end up sippin on some Cris or some iced down Moet thou, pick up the phone and I hear my man Quo's at the spot so, I put on my gear and clip the mic on the waist, it would fit at a better place, but these MC's have no room to wait/6 Hop in the three fifty z, 'It Ain't To Tell' bumpin' on easy, lookin at my watch it reads 8:46, which means i've got 8 bars to spit on some track mix, four words to speak to the easy competition, before its six seconds after he wants this battle done again, he wishin'/7 I can't be big and then turn my face and hide, that's for foolish men with false pride, I could look up at the ceilin', and imagin how many people want to be there when I've got my eyes closed and they've sealed them'/8 Before then, I've got to wait and keep it straight, on some 2nd Comin tape, which I hear in the streets carries mad weight, so to the Mista Jacko who keeps me on the lyrical beat, keep it easy in the streets, and to taste a virgin is sweet, hell the stuff keeps me on my feet/9 Uh, my head his the pillow and the lights dim down, played to a venue of clowns, masterminded them in-between towns, i speak with verbs, adjectives and various nouns, haters wanna beef my vocals shall annoint them plaow plaow/10
