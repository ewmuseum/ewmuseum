Coping with Villainy - Part 4 - Henchmen are BODACIOUS!

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 25, 2003 [Julio's Fresh fruit warehouse used to be one of the most booming places in the warehouse district back in the ninties. People of all ages and creed would makes their way to the warehouse just to get a wonderful taste of Julio's freshly grown fruit. Some say that it was the best in the United States and that he had to have used some kind of magical drug to make his fruit taste so good. Well, the people were correct. It seems that Julio was implanted a growth drug into his plants, an addictive and harmful chemical. Julio honestly didn't know that he was doing any wrong, just that his crops were growing at such a rapid rate and he was making loads of cash. Well, the authorities got involvved and Julio now rots away in a jail cell for just trying to provide some fruit. After the story enter the press the people of SOuthern California paniced and felt that they would soon die of some kind of exotic disease, but that's not the case. The only harm it ever really did was just make them poop a lot, and that would soon wear off with time. Julio is gone. His fruit empire is forever buried. And he left behind and TOTALLY BODACIOUS warehouse...] [A group of men stand outside of the old Julio's warehouse. The painted on name of Julio is faded and some bricks are missing. Julio would be upset to see the horrible shape of his warehouse. All the other surrounding warehouses aren't in much better shape as the world forgot that this part of town existed...except the group of men standing outside. Some of the men are large, strong looking, while others are very tiny and scrawny looking. They are quite intimitdated by the large men that stand beside them, but a few of them know that a swift kick in the crotch and the biting of an ear will even things out smoothly. These are some of the dastardly, toughest men in Southern California. They have seen things in their lives that would surely strike terror into the hearts of men, and they have experienced such violence that it's the only thing that they know. The warehouse door opens into the morning sun as the group of men try not to look excited, but they are to find out who called them all here. At the foot of the door stands Gregory Gastion, super agent and businessman. He wears his ARmani suit and tie perfectly, with nothing out of place. Remember, he has to impress the ladies if he's ever going to have his seed carried on. Some of the men notice who Gregory is and their excitement is downgraded to absolute confusion. Why would some really rich super agent be down in a bad part of town, in an empty warehouse, and recruiting some of the baddest guys in Southern California? Well, they are about to find out as Gregory waves them inside the darkened warehouse] Gregory: Gentlemen, I've been instructed to have you line up, shoulder to shoulder, so that my employer can look you all over. To be honest with you, my employer has absolutely lost his mind and I wouldn't say that he's mentally competent. So, if he does something stupid, which there is about a one hundred percent chance of happening, don't take any offense to it. [The men walk into the darkened warehouse, standing underneath the only light. They are shoulder to shoulder still trying to figure out what this is all about. A big bank job? Maybe a casino needs to get robbed like in that Ocean's Eleven movie. This is really starting to become frustrating for some of the men as their patience begins to wear thin. Gregory disappears into the darkness and his outline can be seen climbing up a small step ladder to some sort of stage. The men can't really make it out, but it's got to be a platform of some kind. Then the voice overpowers them...] Voice: DUDES! Welcome! [The deep voice carries through the warehouse with a pwerful echo. The men look around a bit startled by the insanity of the echo. There is a few moments of silence as the group of men are a bit confused as to whats taking this tall man a while to instruct them of whats going on] Voice: ECHO!! ECHO: ECHO!! Voice: Harry, this echo is bodacious. Harry: YEAH, MAN!! Echo: YEAH, MAN!! 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: BALLS N BOOBS!!! Echo: BALLS N BOOBS!!! Gregory: Guys... TTT: I AM THE ULTIMATE, BODACIOUS WARLORD OF PCW!! BOW DOWN BEFORE ME, DUDES!!! Echo: BEFORE ME, DUDES!!! [THe group of men look from side to side at each other and some of them actually get down and bow to Tommy. Others just stand their ground, feeling that they should bow to no one, especially some dumb sounding guy who's obsessed with his god damn echo] Gregory: Tommy, may I suggest that you tell these VERY BIG, VERY DANGEROUS guys why their here before they start to kick our asses. TTT: Greg, I'm a professional wrestler...none of these compadres can even come to the gnarlyness that I possess. Plus, I'm a super villain! Gregory: Your not a super villain. TTT: Shhh, that'll be enough of the bogusness. NOW! GENTLEMEN! Echo: GENTLEMEN!! TTT: I'm sure that your wondering why your in this totally heinous warehouse. I bet your standing there next to the foul smelling compadre who you'd like to beat up, take his money, and then leave him to rot in a heinous grave. I BET YOUR ALSO WONDERING... Echo: WONDERING!! TTT: ...exactly who I am. [One of the larger henchmen decides to speak up] Large henchman: Your 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. No other schmuck around this world talks like that anymore. [There are a few moments of silence] TTT: SILENCE!! I AM PURE EVIL AND I WON'T ALLOW THIS BOGUSNESS!!! [Tommy's shadow points to the other side of the warehouse and a man operating a large bulldozer comes roaring into the scene. The large henchman doesn't look too frightened, but he should be because the bulldozer scoops him up and then crashes through the side wall. Then decides to keep going, obviously toward the dock area to dump the large man into the water. Teach him not to disrespect a radical super villain] TTT: Now, how about them apples, dudes? [Everyone seems to be silent and obeying now. A lot more light pours in through the side of the building because of the bulldozer incident. The group of men now can see Tommy pretty clear as he is in his blue and yellow Armani suit and his brother 'Heavy Metal' Harry is decked out in his Twisted Sister gear, as usual. Some of the men are pretty excited now that a cool professional wrestler like Tommy would invite them here for a chance at some cash. What a guy he is!] TTT: If you get out of line like that bogus dude, then that's the heinous fate that you'll receive. Gregory: Tommy, why don't you just use the bulldozer guy as one of your henchmen. He could be another super villain that you control. You could call him Dozer or something... TTT: Greg, did I ask for your bogus opinion? Gregory: No. TTT: Good, then shut your heinous cake hole. Harry: Mmmm, cake, man. TTT: Now, dudes, I've called you down here for a most EXCELLENT chance at redemption. I know that most of you compadres are down on your luck and you just need one small oppurtunity to bring yourselves up from the bogus. I'm that chance. I'm going to be the bodacious dude that helps you climb from the scum of the earth to the FEARED MEN that you deserve to be. Because once we're finished with this city, and once we bring down that TOTALLY HEINOUS RENT-A-JERKFACE, they will fear us all! WE WILL HAVE THE POWER!! WE WILL BE GNARLY!!! Echo: BE GNARLY!!! [The group of men nod to each other, seeming to like this chance that they are receiving. Tommy smiles to himself, knowing that his speech has totally gone over well. He only tried to rewrite it twenty times, so it better have been totally bodacious!! Harry decides to light up a bowl] TTT: You see because I am the ultimate super villain... Greg: Tommy, you are NOT a super villain. Your a professional wrestler with some SERIOUS head problems. TTT: Greg, remember that bogus opinion thing earlier? Greg: Yes, I remember it. But I'm not going to stand back and watch you turn into a freakin'--- TTT: SILENCE, GREGORY! Your heinous comments are no longer needed at this meeting. Harry, my evil twin brother, dispose of this totally bogus dude!! [Harry takes a couple puffs out of his bowl, holding up his hand as if telling Tommy to hold on a second. Gregory takes a few steps back as if he were in a bit of fear. Then he realizes that Tommy has sent Harry after him, the extremely high and stupid brother. There really isn't much to fear] Gregory: LISTEN! He's not your twin brother! You are BROTHERS, but he doesn't even look anything like you! Are you stupid?! TTT: YOU BOGUS FOOL! We are not identical, any heinous compadre can see that, but we are TWINS and that's all that matters. Now, Harry, take out my agent...THE HARD WAY!! [Harry looks at his brother completely stoned out of his mind] Harry: What's the hard way, man? [Tommy shrugs] Henchman: KICK HIS ASS! [Harry then looks at Tommy, who then nods. Harry walks over and grabs Gregory by the back of his Armani suit and then commences to throw him off of the small platform. Greg goes down with a large thud and then complete silence. The henchmen all grin and are pleased with the power that was displayed. Tommy just smiles stupidly, and knows that his super villain ways are totally bodcious in a far out way] TTT: Now, dudes, you have seen the awesomeness of my power. You have been chosen by The Tubular One to seek out taking this city to be ours. Starting on Sunday night when I single handedly take out that bogus Rent-A-Hero and rid PCW of it's only superhero! Then we can follow through with our most excellent plan! [The henchmen cheer and charge the platform to celebrate with their new employer. Together they will take Southern California by storm as part of the biggest crime wave in history. Once Rent-A-Hero is out of the way it can all go down most smoothly. Because at Shanghaied the superhero will fall from grace, and he will be destroyed, ONCE AND FOR ALL!!] Gregory: My hips...oh god...my hips... [Fade to black] To Be Continued...
