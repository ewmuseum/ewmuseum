Adventures with Pete [8]: Why the Black Suits?

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 24, 2002 No, anyone but them. Not the two cocky black suits that tried to drag me into an intergalactic struggle that threatened both my life and the entire planet. Why did they have to keep running into me? Couldn’t they bother some unsuspecting alien that needed the attention? But I just stood there, glaring at Sourpuss and Chopsticks. They stood there as well, staring at me as if I were looking into a mirror. I wondered what they were thinking. Could they be happy to see me? And why not, shit, I am the one that stopped Sporty, the living, breathing butterfly that could have demolished this world. Shouldn’t they show some gratitude? Sourpuss: So are we going to sit here all night staring at one another or are we going to talk? Chopsticks: I think our dear friend has caught frostbite on his tongue. Tom Lexian: [sneers] That’s pretty funny, Chopsticks. I see your personality has gone an upgrade since the last time we’ve seen each other. Chopsticks: And I see you haven’t lost your touch for getting into trouble. Tom Lexian: [shrugs] Never have, never will. Trouble finds me sometimes. Sourpuss took out a small notebook from the inside of his jacket pocket and began scribbling down notes. Meanwhile, Chopsticks continued to eye me. We had no love loss, and on a deeper level, I think we hated one another. That really was too bad. I bet he would have been a great forward on my pick up basketball team. Chopsticks: You really did yourself in this time, Lexian. Tom Lexian: Yeah I know. French fries can clog up the heart. I’m looking at triple by-pass surgery in at least three years. Sourpuss: Shut up, both of you. Lexian, you have no idea what you’re in for. It’s not the simple story that Lara Croft gave you either. I felt a bit defensive. What did he know about Lara? While I felt a bit embarrassed falling for a lady that just sort of looked like a video game character, it was every boy’s fantasy to be with a woman that was larger than life. That’s why women like Britney Spears, Christina Aguilera and Shakira are so sought after. That’s why Madonna has been around for so long. I mean, has anyone else seen her lately? She’s like forty years old. That’s damn old. Tom Lexian: Leave Lara out of this. She’s none of your concern. Chopsticks: [laughs] What, you think you and Ms. Tomb Raider actually some sort of connection? Because you’re living in a dream world… Tom Lexian: Why so cranky, Choppy? The inflatable doll go flat on you again? Chopsticks rushed towards me, but Sourpuss held him back. He was a lot stronger than he looked. Then again, how strong does a three hundred year old man look, anyways? Chopsticks resented Sourpuss for stopping him, but he was his supervising officer. No matter how bad you thought you were, there was always someone a little higher on the ladder that can waggle a finger at you and tell you to stop. That’s why I’m a wrestler. Sourpuss: That wasn’t Lara Croft that you thought you saw. It was a shape shifter. Tom Lexian: [puzzled look] Shape shifter? As in someone that can change shape? Chopsticks: [begins clapping] Bravo. I mean, I didn’t he’d get it on the first try. That’s really impressive. Tom Lexian: Stick it up your ass, Choppy. Sourpuss: The shape shifter wants the amulet for herself. Something about wanting to go back into time and altering the time line so that shape shifters rule Earth. Tom Lexian: When did they ever have a chance? Sourpuss: Do you remember furby dolls? Tom Lexian: [nods] Sure, who doesn’t? Sourpuss: That was them. They were actually fairly popular for a time, but of course, they didn’t catch on for very long. They just looked too weird to have any lasting value. Chopsticks: But they have a new plan now. Turn the furby’s into Britney Spears dolls, and then you get control of Earth. It all made perfect sense. Of course, Britney Spears controlled half of this world anyways, at least the male controlled regions, so that would be a good chunk of the pie already. Could these shape shifters actually take control of us? And what would they do with us? Slaves? Or worse? Lawyers! But shape shifters only existed in sci-fi movies and really bad Star Trek episodes. But who was I kidding? I was living in UN-REALITY and weird things always happen. I didn’t have a chance to just scoff and chalk it up to lunatics who read way too many Kevin J. Anderson novels. Call me crazy, but I believed them. Tom Lexian: So how do you intend on stopping her? Sourpuss: Get to the dragon before she does. You see, she has hired Mr. Vader to do her dirty work, but in a way, you’re on the same trail. She tricked you into looking for her, but now that she knows that you know about her, she’ll have Vader looking for it directly. Tom Lexian: But then why did he attack me? He didn’t know I knew about you guys yet. Chopsticks: He knew we were close though. He probably figured on taking you out now, and not leaving too many clues for us to find the dragon before he did. Tom Lexian: Well, that’s okay, because we have no clues either. We have no clue where to start either. Chopsticks: Well, that’s just perfect. We thought you were good at this sort of thing. Tom Lexian: Maybe someone told you wrong then, Choppy. The only semi-clue I have is that Melvin the dragon likes to hang around the Canadian border. That’s all R.L. Stine told us. Chopsticks was a little more than peeved off. He didn’t like the answer I gave him, and when he didn’t like something, he let you know it. That asian nose wrinkled up and the lines on his forehead looked more like waves crashing against a beach head. But I had seen worse. After all, I have seen Night Shard without a shirt on before. Chopsticks: What do you mean? That’s incredibly important and useful information. Why are you out here in the middle of nowhere? You should be on the Canadian border! Pete Rose made his way over to me, checking his neck for any scars. He took a nasty choke hold from Vader who probably could have flattened his wind pipe in about two or three seconds. Why he didn’t do so was beyond me, but Sleazer cut those ideas short. Still, Pete must have been worried this might affect his singing career. I wondered if he could still talk, and if he could, would he be singing soprano from now on? Pete Rose: I think he a point, Tom. [rubs his head] Besides, I’d love to see Canada. It’s a beautiful country, you know? Why, they even have two great baseball clubs up there. Sourpuss: The Expos and the Blue Jays? You call them two great baseball clubs? Pete Rose: Look, you maybe the so called expert on aliens, Darth Vader’s and other weird phenomenon’s, but I am the hall of fame baseball player. I know good baseball when I see it. Tom Lexian: [whispering in Pete’s ear] You do remember that the Expos were almost contracted from the league last year, right? Pete Rose: [bringing me aside] That was just a joke. Selig would never contract a baseball team. There is just too much to gain from that market. Tom Lexian: [surprised] Montreal, you mean? I’m sorry, but I think all they care about is hockey up there. As Pete and I argued the point some more, the black suits were continuing to get more upset. I could hear Sourpuss telling Chopsticks how fed up he was with these “idiotic” people that didn’t get the big picture and wouldn’t take care of business. Of course, Chopsticks answered simply that it was because of me that Lara was going to get a hold of the famed amulet and somehow affect the time line in an evil way. Well, thanks for pinning all the blame on me, guys. Finally, one of them left, leaving Chopsticks behind to clean up the mess. Chopsticks: Listen, if you two girls are finished, I’d like to wrap this up. Now, we need you guys to get to that amulet before anyone else does, especially Vader. If it falls into the wrong hands… …okay, so you’ve heard this part before. Don’t be embarrassed, because I know I’ve heard the line too. There will be hell to pay if myself and my merry men didn’t get to the amulet quick, and something tells me they weren’t trying to scare me. So tell me, how do you convince a 400 foot dragon to let you take his amulet? What does a dragon do with an amulet anyways? Tom Lexian: We’ll get the amulet back, Choppy. You don’t have to worry about that. Chopsticks: Make sure that I don’t. We’ll be closely monitoring you guys. Tom Lexian: [saluting] Thanks Mr. Choppy. Your satisfaction is the reason why we work so hard. Chopsticks: [leaving] Asshole. Tom Lexian: [looking around suddenly] Oh my god! Sleazer! Where’s Sleazer? Pete Rose: [shrugs] I thought he was with you. Tom Lexian: [smacks Pete on the side of the head] Don’t be ridiculous, okay? You know we’ve been standing here together for the past few minutes. I heard a groan coming from one of the turned over tables. I rushed over there and Pete helped me lift the table off of Sleazer. He had his eyes closed but he was moving – I guess that meant he was still alive. I felt around his neck to make sure I felt a pulse… …THUNK THUNK…THUNK THUNK… A nice metronome beat to that sleazy heart of his. I breathed a sigh of relief, thanking God that he wasn’t dead. I helped him up to his feet as he rubbed the top of his head. Sleazer: Oh my gosh…[dazed]…where am I? Tom Lexian: Do you remember who you are? Sleazer: [rubs his head] How could I forget? I’m a loser. Pete Rose: I guess there’s nothing wrong with his memory. I hit Pete on the head for his remark. You can’t mess with a man after he just got his ass beat. I know when I get my ass beat, you get away from me. I don’t want to talk to you, I don’t want to see you – just let me dwell in my own embarrassment. It’s the American way. Tom Lexian: That was a brave thing you did out there, Sleazer. I guess you’re not the two faced coward I always accuse you of being. You showed some real heart out there. Sleazer: [smiles] Thanks Tom. I guess I had it in me the whole time. Pete Rose: Well, he would have thunk it. Sleazer is a hero? I couldn’t believe it even if I saw it again. So, my victorious crew marches on. We cleaned up the joint a bit before allowed Chopsticks a chance to change the memories of all of the people that were in the café at the time of the blast. While we walked outside, I noticed a streaking star in the dark night. I immediately thought of us and our intrepid mission to find the amulet. It may have been foolish and down right stupid, but no one would change our destiny but ourselves. It was our mission to screw up. Around this time, Pete began chasing Sleazer with his bat. Something about Sleazer looking at his ass or something. I just chuckled and watched the show. Some things never change I guess. Oh goodness…
