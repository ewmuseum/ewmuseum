Date: 6 - 5 - 03 “Why won’t this blasted thing remove itself from thy neck!” We rejoin our bad ass and superior hero, Dante Jackson as he rests bed ridden inside of Stately State Manor, searching for a mechanism to release the shock collar system Gaz “The Jester” Green entrusted upon his neck at the end of May 31st edition of Fusion. We do need to inform you, the reader, that this was that exact night that everything in the struggle between good green and bad green came to a sudden apex. This was the same night Jackson was almost blown to bits by a robot resembling his trusted manager and wife Tracy Jackson, that he knocked the utter life out of Violence Jack; Bruce Shannahan and then, was screwed once more for the PCW Continental championship, currently held by the King Midas of Tag. Inside of the Manor, a pastry ceiling with charcoal clouds inscribed in between the give the room a much more polished and realistic look, have been pounded on repeatedly by the swollen knuckles of our protagonist as he groans and howls in anger, instead of pain. He swallows hard, releasing his neck backwards in a send off of stress before moving back towards the ceiling, suspended from the heavens as he continues to pounce upon the walls. Dante: (English accent) “Bloody bastard!” The Messiah roars as his knuckles sandwich themselves in between a section of grooves carved into the ceiling. Dante: “GAR!” Stepping back inside the room, a silver tray lined with essential eateries ranging from a slice of apple pie, a juicy Porterhouse steak and a glass of orange juice, the trusted maid of the family dusts particles off her uniform, before setting the tray down upon a wooden desk, stationed along with a lamp upon the countertop. Elise: (shocked) “Oh, oh my heavens!!” She holds her head upwards, aghast at our hero as he continues to wail upon the rooftop of his home before moving out of the room, muttering towards herself with a calm distinction. Elise: “Oh dear, he’s lost his mind!” Tracy: (reassuring) “No, he’s rather lost his temper.” The beauty of brains and brawn sidesteps the woman with a waited grasp upon the tray. Looking back at her, Tracy smirks a preppy type of smirk, much like Eleanor Kannon-Hall during the late portion of two thousand and one and the majority of the two thousand two year. Tracy: “Good, Elise, you can go now.” Instead, Elise doesn’t even budge; her heels clack upon the floor ever so slowly, making Tracy raise her eyebrow up in disdain. The clumpy hired minion steadily holds her very own ground as the heroine of the Jackson family approaches her, using one of the more evil traditional scowls made present by female managers today. Tracy: (sternly) “I will kindly ask you…” The madam of malevolence tells in hushed tones. Tracy: (scowling) “To leave this room the way it is; wench.” The ‘w’ word curled off her lips slowly, peeling off towards the senses of the trusted manager’s secondary henchwoman. Elise made herself scare, scattering out of the room as Tracy held her arms upon her hips, pleased with herself. Then, from above the mighty voice of our hero bellows out as he repels downward, letting his feet guide his way to the red-carpeted floor. Dante: “Maybe you shouldn’t have been so unruly towards Elise, she was only helping!” The Messiah caresses the bare back of his wife and trusted manager, comfort move as it were. Dante: “I mean, you can’t be bad to everybody who tries to help me out, then we’d have no friends.” Tracy scowls. Tracy: “Neither enemies.” The Messiah trudges along the floor, walking through the manor filled with henchwoman and trusted advisors as the sparkling element and gaze of the shock collar so tied and fitted around his neck, allows him not to even have a solid moment of peace and continuity. He begins to take a few steps towards open country, releasing the front door from its knobbed hinges, twisting the opening and pulling backwards as rain clouds block the sun shining ever so brightly, leaving a pattern of light grays and tones of sadness left over the Houston afternoon. Looking back with a controlled whimper, our hero turns forward, watching the groundskeeper work and slave his way towards a paycheck, hedging the flower garden as a fountain stands overshadowing him from the sight of our hero. Dante: (quizzical) “Hey Gus, does the sun ever shine brighter than tis was morning?” Gus The Groundskeeper: “No sir, no siree.” The hired help nods as he continues snapping the hedges, leaving small trails of leaf to décor the front lawn. Dante: (sourly) “Oh.” He sighs heavily, whole-heartedly moving on through the garden before the distinct sounds of protest fill the area of parking and other residences surrounding Stately State Manor. Zooming quickly towards the outside, the knocking of a man at the gates continue to tremble the brass that happens to bar anyone who is not welcome from the premises as there is only a singular man standing on the outside of the gate. As a camera pans downward upon the specimen, he quickly gyrates his hips and hurls a brick into the screen, leveling the camera to debris and pieces of glass to scatter along the cemented sidewalk. Intercom Guard: (annoyed) “Excuse me, excuse me!” The intercom buzzes from the lower side where the camera shards lie. A feminine voice lashes out from the small air pocketed speaker as the man looks down at the intercom, pressing the LED button. Intercom Guard: (annoyed) “Um, why are you stepping on the premises?” The man shrouded in a white GQ dress shirt with a skinny or less than average muscular frame speaks in a weak and more geeks tone. He stumbles in his speech a bit, before stopping and collecting his breath. Man: (stuttering) “I-I-I’m here for The Messiah.” Intercom Guard: (outraged) “Do you have an appointment? I can’t see you since you KNOCKED OUT MY CAMERA!” The guard monitoring the grounds burns into the skull of the man as he trembles in obvious fear of the woman somewhat as he fixes his spectacles, adjusting his tie from the neck before speaking again. Man: (excited) “Why yes, um - yes I do!” The guard levels into the chest of the man, as another camera peers into the breast pocket of the small pencil pusher continues to writhe along the nerves as she reads out the name along the gold bridled faceplate. Intercom Guard: (reading) “E-L-L-I-O-T S-A-N-D-E-R-S.” Elliot nods. Intercom Guard: (dryly) “Wait.” She stops herself. Intercom Guard: (shocked) “THE Elliot Sanders?” Elliot nods, adjusting his belt as he switches one buckle towards the other as the intercom buzzes once more, switching Elliot’s attention. Intercom Guard: (gossipy) “Not to be a bit bossy, but can you please stop fidgeting? You’re asking to speak to Dante Jackson, probably the most overworked, overrated, and barely even responsible man on the face of the earth!” From inside, the visage of Tracy Jackson standing over the intercom speaker runs into frame as the women snickers under her breath, before Tracy’s white fingernails run along the side of the crème colored desk. Tracy: “Stop telling the truth.” Elliot Sanders: (whiny) “But madam…” Intercom Guard: (sourly) “BUT NOTHING!” The wife of the Messiah roars as she scowls with intention to verbally harm. The woman cowers in fear as she taps a small circular red button, retracting the brass gates from their respective hinges as Elliot begins to make his way inward through the hedge maze of the Manor, sending his eyes to wander. Meanwhile… Dante: “GOD!” The Prodigal Son yells from ground level as he stands with his hands outreached towards the sky, hoping that the Lord will hear him with a plea and cry. Soon, a bright light trounces upon the ground creating a small crater in the surface as Elliot looks on in awe. Elliot Sanders: (amazed) “Whoa…” Storm clouds begin to rumble and circle all around as Dante begins to float, or levitate as some might say upwards toward the clouds! Gazing out from an upstairs window, Tracy shakes her head at the Messiah, before looking downward and spotting reporter Sanders still trying to make his way past the hedge maze. Tracy: “Just a bit to your right!” Elliot: (shouting) “WHAT?” Tracy: (shouting) “TURN RIGHT FOR THE EXIT!” As the message relays back to the dimwitted reporter, Dante is inches away from the light hovering above Stately State Manor as the clouds begin to form inward, sucking along particles of matter along with as the Messiah vanishes! Elliot: (shouting) “BURN BRIGHT WHORE HE SHIT?” Sanders turns his body right as he catches a glimpse of the exit as the sparkling fountains brush upon along his right shoulder blade. The horrible sound of buzzing approaches the reporter, before he swings around and finds a weed eater firmly placed in his face. Elliot: (scared) “AHH!”Gus The Groundskeeper: “Aww, don’t be scared. Its just a utensil to use for the canary’s and the chickens!” Elliot: “You didn’t have to hold it near my face!” Gus The Groundskeeper: “Quiet! Just go on inside them doors there so the lady can have her time with ya.” Gus chuckles. Gus The Groundskeeper: (under his breath) “And probably shrink you back to side that crazy…” Elliot: “What was that?” Gus The Groundskeeper: (quick) “NOTHING!” Elliot shrugs his shoulders in disbelief of the groundskeeper, but steadily places his feet closer and closer towards the front doors of the manor as Tracy begins to step down the stairs in anticipation for the dashing, yet odd reporter. Tracy: “He’ll be here any second!” She rushes down to center floor, looking around and watching her workers act like bees with a purpose, not trying to get in the way as a prop figure of a hooded man with a loaded pistol jars the young heroine right in the face. She bounces backwards for a quick second, before back handing the wooden plank away, sending the mechanism retracting back to a dormant position. Tracy: (sighing) “This’ll be the day I finally win Best House!” Pulling the front door’s golden knob, the quirky Sanders stumbles into the house, as his camera firmly attached to his backpack slips out and crashes onto the floor, yet it doesn’t shatter. In a quick moment, Elliot begins to hold his chin. Elliot: (thankful) “God Bless You Acme Cameras.” “HEY! WE’VE GOT ANOTHER BLESSING COMING!” We re-rejoin our big and badass hero to protect all good in PCW, Dante Jackson as he begins to walk the clouds, shortly making quick strides as he continues to make his way towards the house of God. Looking around him, he can see mountains outstretched far from the earth and landing upon slick, greasy peaks here. Rivers of clear, cleansed water sink as far as the eye can see as the spirits of fishers and fishermen alike forge to bathe upon this river, hoping to catch something. Looking ahead, Dante notices a small, yet peculiar maniacal man running away from the gates. Dante: (with glee) “HERMES!” Wait, that’s not right. As the camera turns towards an entrance sign, the look can be etched in old style Greek print. Dante: (boldly) “Welcome to MOUNT OLYMPUS!”
