That's My Room

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 26, 2003 There is nothing like the smell of freshly brewed coffee to start your day off, especially when you are starting the morning off with the person you care for the most in the world. For me that person was Chris Storm and although we had many tasks that needed to be completed in a small period of time we brushed those worries off for a moment to simply enjoy being together without any tension brewing for the first time in what seemed like awhile. “So Tessa, what are the plans for today?” He asked while sipping coffee that he had made himself out of the mug I had bought him for Christmas. “Nothing really exciting. I figure we can finish unpacking the boxes, settle everyone down in their own room, and then once all that is said and done we can plan out some sort of strategy for Tuesday.” I said, as I walked over towards him sitting down in the seat across from him at the kitchen table. “Sounds like a plan to me.” He said as he finished off the last sip of coffee standing up and tossing his dirty mug into the sink obviously in a rush to get all the work that stood before us done with. “Slow down boy. There is no reason to rush, we have all day.” I shouted as he headed towards the boxes in the hallway that were neatly lined up against the stairway. “I would not be in such a hurry if we didn’t have three kids arguing over who gets what room.” His echo rang through the empty hallway into the kitchen. “Huh?” I said totally clueless to the situation, forgetting that the kids were with it not because I was trying to block them out, but because quite obviously fatigue had eaten away at them leaving them practically speechless until now. “Just listen.” Chris said before going silent to let he hear the war that was breaking out upstairs between Christa Alex and Evan and surely enough the silence was broken through childish insults that roared throughout the house putting an end to what seemed would be a peaceful morning, so much for that. “So much for a nice start to the morning.” I sighed, placing my coffee cup on the plain white placemat that stood before me, and then heading off in direction of the stairway that would lead me to all the commotion. “Want me to come with you into the war zone?” Chris teased with a smirk. “You better believe you’re coming along partner.” I teased back before grabbing him by the arm and leading him to the top of the stairs where Christa, Alex, and Evan stood with pouts on their faces and their arms crosses for an extra dramatic effect to make the fight look that much worse than what it really was. “Tessssaaaa” All three of them chimed in a whining chorus. “One at a time, damn it. Now someone please tell me what in the world is going on up here.” I said looking each of them square in the eyes to let them know that anymore shouting would not be tolerated no matter what it was about and who it came from and no matter what measures I had to take to make sure that this morning would be a peaceful once, instead of one consisting of constant whining. “Alex took the bedroom I wanted.” Evan pouted, his little blue eyes filling up with tears. “You’re a little kid. Little kids don’t need the big bedrooms.” Alex argued back in a sharp disciplining tone. “And why are you crying?” I asked as I turned to Christa who was twirling her long brown hair as the boys continued to exchange angry glares with one another. “I don’t have a bedroom.” She pouted before continuing on with her story. “Where am I going to sleep?” “Christa you don’t need to cry, you know Chris and I would never let you go without a place to sleep.” I answered softly, as I kissed her cheek to calm her down and then turned to the boys who looked like they were going to start world war three at any moment.“Tell him I get the bigger room!” Alex demanded, before looking over at Evan and making a teasing face that pretty much laughed at Evan for being the younger and smaller of the two. “I’ll tell you how it goes. Seeing as though Christa isn’t the only one of you three who isn’t fighting for the big room, she is the one who gets it.” I smirked looking over at Christa who was now filled with joy. “No way!” Alex began to shout. “I’m not sharing a room with him!” “Well this is only a four bedroom house so someone’s going to have to share.” I said looking over at Alex and Evan and then looking over at Chris who seemed to be in his own world at the moment. Perhaps he was thinking about the match we would have with P.W.A. and the chance we had the claim the tag team championships. I couldn’t blame him for thinking about that if he was. I’d be thinking about the same thing if I didn’t have these pitiful arguments to deal with first. “Why don’t you and Chris share a room? You’re always together anyways.” Alex shouted in his typical witty way as if to laugh at us for not coming up with this same idea ahead of time. Hearing his name Chris snaps out of his thoughts and looks over at me. I look back at him and see if he has anything to say to Alex. Being as clueless as me about what to say Chris just shrugs his shoulders staying as silent as before as Alex smirk becomes wider, knowing that we can’t argue back about why we shouldn’t be sharing a room considering the amount of loved we had for each other. “Well why can’t you share a room with Evan?” I argued back trying to avoid the topic of Chris and me sharing a room. “I asked you a question first, answer it!” He retorted with an attitude, wanting to make statement. “Alex! Gah, we will talk about it later. You just unpack your stuff and stop giving me an attitude. Come on Chris.” I said, leading him by the arm once again only this time down the stairs to get away from the topic of Chris and me sharing a bed. I’m sure Chris was having the same thoughts as me at the moment, but what was the solution and when would it come to me about what to do? I couldn’t wait much longer to find out. Bedtime would arrive quickly and by then I needed a solution by at the moment I just needed to get away from all the childish arguments so I began to unpack the boxes that sat at the bottom of the stairs hoping that maybe Chris would have more guts that I and give us the solution that we all wanted to hear.
