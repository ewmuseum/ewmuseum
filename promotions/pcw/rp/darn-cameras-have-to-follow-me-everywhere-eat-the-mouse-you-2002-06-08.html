Darn cameras have to follow me everywhere. Eat the mouse, you stupid lizard!

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/02 'What are you doing?' asks Lamar the Leper, his hairy chest and beer-belly bulging out from his open red bathrobe. Luckily, he wore his boxers today instead of briefs. They used to be white, but are now stained yellow and brown all over. The End, dressed in his black ring attire as always (at least he washes the outfit every day and has at least ten of them) turns his attention away from what he is doing to answer Lamar. 'I'm trying to have Juju develop a taste for rodents,' End answers. Juju the Albino Iguana is rejecting the mouse held in front of him, shaking his head with closed eyes and saying, 'Wonk.' Lamar scratches his knotted hair and razor stubble, and wipes some crust from his beady eyes. Lamar asks, 'Why?' like a child, blowing his Roman nose with his bathrobe. Juju continues to turn down the mouse. 'You see,' says The End, having to get up from his kneeling position and chase the retreating Juju around the empty garage, 'Kramer WildWing is looking for a someone to watch his pet rodent while he's away at Turmoil.' The creaky wooden door opens up and through it steps Richard, his scruffy beard entering a full .5 seconds before the rest of his body. He is dressed in a blue flannel shirt and jeans, with Kirkland brown work boots. Richard sees The End chasing Juju around the garage. He walks over to Richard and inquires about this early morning sight. 'What is End doing?' Richard leans over and whispers to Lamar. Lamar points at The End and says in a loud voice, 'He's teaching Juju to eat mice!' Richard jabs Lamar with his elbow. The End keeps his attention on Juju, but elaborates. 'My plan,' The End explains, 'is to get Juju to go into Kramer's place, ingest the mouse, and then KWW will be so heartbroken that he won't be able to compete.' Slade says something, standing at the door leaning against frame. 'Sloek jisyu nu ansh duehrf,' says Slade, who's impeccable gray Armani three-piece suit, clean-shaven face, and slicked hair cannot change the fact that barely anyone in the world can understand what he says. His friends can, though, and respond to him. 'Richard agrees with Slade,' the scruffy-bearded one says, nodding his head in affirmation while watching the chase. 'Kramer WildWing is not that much of a threat. You could beat the man at his best.' The End gives up chase and tosses the mouse out the garage door into the wheat outside. There is a wooded area outside, but no road, or any other buildings. Actually, it just seems to be a garage attached to an only slightly larger house/shack. 'Well,' says End, dusting off his hands and patting the now upright and stationary Juju on the head, 'what do you guys want to do with the free time I have until my match?' Richard answers, 'Aren't you supposed to be cutting promos for your match?' The End shrugs and moves out of the garage past Slade. 'I'm doing a promo right now. It's really weird, but PCW seems to send cameras to follow all of us, since anything we say or do is broadcast on the promo channel. Sometimes, even people's thoughts and dreams end up in the editing room. It's really weird.' Lamar, with curiosity, prompts more discussion on this topic. 'Is it true?' he asks, wide-eyed and anxious to know if he's really on camera at this very moment. The End nods his head. 'Sure,' he says. 'How do you think I knew about KWW needing a sitter for his rodent?' Slade the terminal sleepwalker (never wakes up from sleep, subconscious mind operating in the conscious world) gives a for-the-heck-of-it answer. 'ESP?' he says. The End walks back out into the main room. It's as empty as the garage. The whole house is. The only thing in the room is a fur-loincloth clad Drek Redaxe, kneeling out in the middle of the floor. He turns his head up to The End. 'Permit me to be askin' a question,' Drek begins, 'but whose house is this?' The End answers Drek without paying much attention to him. He opens up a window on the east wall by the door, the white-washed walls about to fall apart from the stress of this mere action. 'El Diablo said he rented it for a scene from his new movie,' The End says, 'and he wanted me to check it out to make sure it's safe to use.' Slade, with inquiry and confusion inflected in his voice, says, 'Asioj moundguh ijrfui idsnn epi vrjf uhdm?' The End shrugs, replying, 'I have no idea why he didn't gauge this place's safety before he rented it. He doesn't always think thing's through, but he's harmless.' The view of the room shakes, and the camera pans up to the ceiling, before rolling back to Lamar's fat, ugly face. His nose hairs protrude 3 millimeters out of his nose, enough to be noticable. The camera is looking up into his booger-filled nose, which makes it worse. 'I found the camera!' Lamar shouts with excitement, waving it around like a working-class single parent with a winning lottery ticket. The End rushes over to Lamar to snatch the camera away. 'Put that thing down!' he orders Lamar. 'You know how much those things cost if you break it?' Lamar drops the camera; the device breaks into many pieces. Luckily, the cameraman that Lamar stole the camera from has a spare. When the picture returns, Lamar is standing in the southwest corner of the room, facing into the wall with a blue conical hat upon his head. We can guess what it says. The End and the remaining Misfits clean up the pieces of the broken camera. Far away, in the accounting office of the PCW building, $5,000 from The End's next paycheck. The End, sweeping up the smallest bits of the broken camera with a makeshift broom of wheat chaff and some long, sturdy tree branches, speaks aloud while cleaning up. The Misfits all stop what they are doing to listen to him. 'It's funny, actually,' says The End, leaning over against his broom, that a wrestler as talented as me, is not only going into a #1 contender's match for the PCW title, but instead of training for it, I'm scouting locations for a movie and cleaning up after my groupies (Syko was right, I do have some). This is a completely awkward situation I'm in.' Drek Redaxe gives his two cents in the break in The End's monologue. 'It's not that bad,' Drek reassures his taller comrade. 'You're good enough that you don't need to train. You're slim, trim, and fit, and you're one 'ell of an athlete. You've got nothin' to worry about, your match is against a man whose career as a spokesperson for chicken has been more successful than his wrestling career, a man whose manager is a shopping cart and whose highest achievement in PCW is beating Duzza, and someone I only know by his name, Hiro Saotome.' The End gets off his broom and returns to sweeping. 'I suppose you're right. I'm ready. I've been ready. No one can beat me, and when I win, then I'll face either Charlie Loc (who I owe for attacking me in PURE), or Juan Borro (a guy I just don't like on principle). It's time for me to step up to what is mine, instead of waiting for it to come to me. Turmoil is the time when it begins. The beginning of the end.' Richard breaks in. 'That was corny,' he says. The End nods his head. 'Yeah,' End replies, 'but at least I don't refer to myself in the third-person.' The other Misfits laugh at Richard's expense and go about their cleaning (that camera broke into MANY pieces). Lamar sweeps over by the rear wall. The window in the back is propped open by an orange extension cord that reaches down into the wall where there is a single outlet. Lamar bends down and grabs the cord. 'I wonder what this is for?' he says aloud to himself. A hand shoots out from the camera's POV and there is a voice that shouts, 'No!' Lamar tugs hard at the cord, and the feed goes black.View The End's Biography
