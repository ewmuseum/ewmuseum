Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 10)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/9/02 In an isolated chamber somewhere on an offshore cleanup facility, a man with very bizzare hair stands with his arms wrapped around the president of the United States. Raiden: BREATHE!!!!! Bush: Ack! Over and over our hero applies the Heimlich....then at last a half chewed bit of pretzel flies from the president's mouth, scultching and sticking on the far wall. Bush: (Coughing) That was scaritic. Raiden: Huh? President Bush straightens his collar....then abruptly turns and grabs Raiden by the crotch. Raiden: (Pulling away) Wha? Get off! Agh! Bush: Oh...I'm sorry. My daddy always said the best way to get to know someone was to reach out and give them a firm shake. Raiden: I don't think he meant their groin, sir! Bush: Oh. The president wanders away with a blank expression, sitting by a small keyboard with a red button at the side marked 'do not touch'. Raiden: What is that? Bush: That's a keyboard, with a red button at the side marked 'do not touch'. Raiden: ....yes, but what does it do....? Bush: Oh. It's the nuclear button. My daddy always said he had the power to hit that button whenever he wanted, and that one day I would too. The chief executive looks on wistfully, his face disturbingly dull. Bush: And that day has come. With that Bush reaches over and plucks the red button. A tiny shockwave seems to pulse through the room. Raiden: What was that? Bush: I just nuked Canada. Isn't it cool? Raiden: What do you mean you just nuked Canada? Bush: (Grinning) I mean I just nuked Canada. Dropped a few bombs on it, made it a parking lot. Raiden: You're kidding. Bush: Nope. Watch this. President Bush reaches over his head with his left arm and hits the switch stylistically. Another shudder goes through the room. Bush: There goes Zimbabwe. Raiden: (Aghast) Stop that! Why are you doing this!? Another push of the button, a chuckle rising from the leader of America. Bush: Bye Mexico. Raiden: Mr. President, stop nuking the world! Are you working with the terrorists? Bush: Well, if you asked me two hours ago I'd have said yes. My power is insigmirant. But I want it to be superfluous. Raiden: Come again? Bush: (Shaking his head) I'm just a figurehead, really. The real power lies in the hands of the mods. Raiden: The mods? Bush: Yes. I'm surprised you haven't heard of them.....they're the real ones behind this country. The ones who're controlling the script. Raiden: I don't follow. Bush fiddles with the nuclear button absent mindedly. Bush: They choose who becomes president. They chose my daddy....he was a good man. He told me I could become president one day. That was right after I broke the mailbox hitting it with a stick. I think he was being sarcastic. Raiden: ......... Bush: The policies, such as the space program I set up to see if Invader Zim really existed, and the tax refund I gave to everyone so they could go buy Snickers bars. They were actually set up by the mods. Raiden: Knew there had to be a reason congress didn't laugh you out of the hall. Bush: I do like Snickers bars. Anyway, where was I. Raiden: The mods. Bush: Oh, right. They rule this country. They chose my daddy to be president. He was a good man. Once he told me I could be president. Raiden: You said that already. Bush: Oh. Where was I? Raiden: (Slapping his forehead) The mods. Bush: Oh. Raiden: So who are they? Bush: Even I don't know who they really are. Even my instructions were sent to me by little circles in my daily jumble. I do like the daily jumble. Raiden: We all do. Go on. Bush: All I know is the decisions are made by a bunch of folks called the Wisemen's Committee. My daddy gave them his loyalty, so they made me president. Sometimes they asked him if they could take it back. They were funny fellows. Raiden: ....... Bush: But even if a checker gets to the wrong end of the board, he's still just a checker. Raiden: Huh? Bush: I wanted to be kinged, so I could double jump. I do like checkers. Raiden: ....if you get to the 'wrong end of the board' you ARE kinged. Bush: Maybe that's why I never really won. Anywho, I wanted to use Metal Gear as a bargaining chip so I could get into the mods. Then I could push the red button and blow up as many countries as I wanted. Bush hits the button again, giggling. Bush: Bye bye Cuba! Raiden: I don't believe what I'm seeing. Bush: But Kriegidus isn't content with that. He wants to challenge the mods and take over the world himself. Raiden: Wow. Bush: (Twiddling his thumbs) He doesn't know. What would we do without the mods? We'd be people without scripts.....we'd all have to take care of ourselves. I was never good at taking care of myself. Raiden: So you sided with them to prevent anyone from having to think for themselves. Bush: But when I told Kriegidus about this he said 'pawns can never become players'. Then he said 'god you even look stupid'. He's the stupid one, pawns aren't even used in checkers. Raiden: ......who is this Kriegidus guy? Bush: He was my predecessor in the office, Alan Kriegman. At least that's what the people knew him by....I knew him by his code name, Kriegidus. He was the third son of Dai Lo, the brother of Liquid Clyde and Solid Snake. Raiden: Hm. Bush: But he had a falling out with the mods when he started some kind of incident on Shadow Moses island. That wasn't very nice of him. He should have a reason to attack people Again George Bush presses the red button. Bush: Bye bye Maryland. Raiden: Hey, I have friends in Maryland! Bush: Turns out he was trying to get test data from Metal Gear. But he kind of slipped up, sort of like I do sometimes while I'm eating pretzels, and the public found out. Bush pops a pretzel into his mouth, and immediately begins choking. Jack rushes up behind him and goes to the Heimlich. Raiden: BREATHE!!!!! Bush: Hackack! The food treat flies from the leader's esophagus, hitting the other side of the room. Bush: Thank you. So where was I. Raiden: Kriegidus. Bush: Oh. So the mods took him out of office, and he was supposed to have an accident. That was the first time I got to push the red button....they let me blow up the town he was supposed to be in, Pleasantville New Hampshire. But he got away. George hangs his head sadly. Raiden: How? Bush: Well I never learned to read maps very well....I kinda missed and nuked Shanghai China. Raiden: That was one helluva miss. Bush: That's what the mods said. They punished me by taking away my Family Circus comic strips. I do like Family Circus. Anyway, after he escaped he went underground with Hosselot and took over this here army you see. Now he wants to take the mods biggest weapon. Raiden: Metal Gear? Bush: Wow, you're smart. But it's not the normal Metal Gear. You're standing in the next Metal Gear. Raiden: Huh!? Bush: This whole facility thingy was built to camulanaflague the new model. Metal Gear Ron. Raiden: Dear god. Bush: Yup. It's basically meant to control the whole country, and has a whole bunch of nukes. See? The president again mashes the button, producing a shudder. Bush: Sayonara London. Raiden: I really wish you'd stop dropping nukes. Bush: But the real reason for it is that they can control the digital flow of information with it. I don't really know what that means. Maybe they mean to take the people's Family Circus comics. I do like Family Circus. Raiden: ...... Bush: Kriegidus wants to detonate a nuke up in the atomosisaphere. Raiden: The what? Bush: The sky. Raiden: Oh. Bush: He thinks it'll knock out Wall Street, screwing up the mod's power base. We don't have much time left, but maybe you can stop them from launching Ron. Raiden: How? Bush: There's a scientist holed up down below. Go down there and talk to em. President Bush stands, looking at Raiden for a second. He then picks up his Rolds Golds bag, pulls out a pretzel and offers one to the commando. Bush: Snack? Raiden: No thanks. With a shrug, Bush throws the pretzel in the air and leans his head back. Just as it enters his mouth though a loud 'BANG' rings out. Bush: (Choking) ACK! Jack looks around in shock to see Revolver Hosselot standing with his smoking gun pointed to the air. Hosselot: Whoops, finger slipped. Raiden turns again and tries to aid the president, but Hosselot darts forward and holds his arms. Raiden: Let me go! Bush waves his arms frantically, his face turning purple. He then slinks to the ground, Hosselot finally lets Jack go who rushes to his side. Hosselot: .....god what an idiot. The old man turns to leave, as Raiden holds the dying president close. Bush: Listen....uh.... The man looks on, his eyes vacant. Bush: .....I had a speech here, but I just can't remember....uuuuugh. George’s head falls, but he pulls it up again. Bush: Oh yeah. I..... Again he pauses. Bush: Wait it’s gone again. Raiden: (Slaps forehead) Bush: Um.... Raiden: Uh, something I should be doing? Bush: I think so.... Raiden: Something to do with my mission? Bush: Hmmm..... Raiden: ..... Bush: Oh. Stop the mods....without free will, there’s no difference between submission and rebellion. Bush smiles somewhat goofily. Bush: I made that up myself.....uuuuuuuugh. The leader of the U.S. slumps, and dies in Jack's arms. He looks on, his eyes somewhat wearily. Raiden: What the hell......
