A new direction

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/02 'How... how did he do it?' questioned Nerezza. The vampire was alone. Usually he would take to the highest steeples of whatever church or preferably cathedral happened to be close, sitting up there with either birds or bats or even stone gargoyles for company. He would stay there until the skies lit up with the dawn's smile, where he'd retreat to his 'normal' life. But today, the vampire was in a different surrounding, almost alien to him. He sat slumped over a table with a bottle of vodka in one hand.His expression usually bore anger or indifference, even occasionally a smile would surface from his gloomy lips. Instead, a look of sadness clung to his visage as he punctuated his loneliness with gulps from the alcohol. 'Hey, are you going to use that?' Came a voice from the side. Expecting Gaz Green to be the owner, he spun around with a faint gleam of hope in his eye, even so masked by his signature red sunglasses. To his dismay, it was just a fat, balding bartender. 'I'm not done with it yet...' he croaked solemnly. 'Just remember to use it properly!' the overweight staff spat, pointing to a sign above the bar reading 'If you're not here to fight or pick up women, don't come back!'. 'I knew I shouldn't have come to a bar specially constructed for PWA students...' he thought to himself, blotting his mind even more with copious swigs of the vodka. 'Then again, seeing he and I are the only ones here, I may as well strike up some kind of conversation.' Nerezza gulped down the last of the liquid. It stimulated his throat but he showed no outward sign of it as it even tried to provoke a reaction from his dead neck. He staggered over to the bar and parked his huge frame onto a stool, leaning over and pretending to browse over the array of bottles. After a moment of false deciding, he turned to the podgey man, who looked up while wiping a glass clean with a towel. 'Rezz Bull and Cider, please.' Nerezza muttered. 'Got ID?' The barman replied, arching an eyebrow up. 'You just sold me a bottle of vodka...' came the patient answer. 'I'm a barman at a PWA Student bar, it's my job to be consumer-unfriendly, never to count the money I'm given and to encourage the fighting and immoral meetings in the rest rooms. Besides, you never know when a talent scout may be watching ready to give people contracts!' the tender cockily said, brandishing his towel. '...explains a lot.' The living nightmare grunted under his breath as a tall glass, a can of Rezz Bull and a small cider bottle were placed before him. Nerezza tested the theory and slid a single coin towards the barman underneath a black fingernail. 'Keep the change.' Complying to his program, the barman took it and deposited the coin into the cash register. 'Thanks.' he said, then went back to polishing his glass. 'Well I'll be Boris Nakobi's second cousin, twice removed...' 'So why the long face?' the barman enquired, again as if he were a robot programmed to do so. 'Just thinking about how bad things are looking right now...' Nerezza tapped the side of the glass with his index finger rapidly. 'You must be one of the wrestlers - had a big loss or something recently?' he asked, attempting to hide his paunch as he leaned closer. 'Yeah... something like that.' 'When did this start?' Suddenly, Nerezza's thoughts began to wander. He and Gaz were rushing at the silver-haired japanese guy, ready to clobber him with two chairs. The next thing they knew, it was like they had hit metal, or even something harder. Then their faces were caved in after a single punch from him. Next thing they knew, they were watching him kidnap Anarchy. The sound of another freshly sparkling glass clicking next to another woke Nerezza back up. He suddenly dropped from the stool and made his way towards the toilets, locking the cubicle door behind him. 'Akira Akiyama... the Neo Void God. So literally, I'm not the only inhuman around. But that power... he's linked to all the collective negative energy in the world, maybe even the universe! That sort of power would sustain me forever... and if I could just get hold of some of his blood - ' Nerezza's saliva dripped to the floor. His eyes became larger than his sunglasses as the possibilities of what may happen swam through his mind like bullets from a firing squad. The thing that kept Nerezza alive since he turned on his vampire teachers back in PWA was negativity, rather than the blood he only used to transform. If he were somehow connected to the void, he'd have an unlimited supply of energy. For that, he would have to somehow take Akiyama's attributes. The only way possible was for him to take Akiyama's blood into himself. That, added to his vampire powers would certainly tip the scales. The vampire unlocked the door and stepped out, looking in the huge mirror before him. Standing in front of it was a man of 6 feet tall, weighing a little over 260lbs and slightly overweight. Reflecting back was a monster, 6'7 and 350lbs in his clothing, except with arms that scraped his ankles and wings that sprouted from his back, arching across the mirror and exceeding it's surface's reflective limits. For a moment, Nerezza imagined himself with the power of the void... he closed his eyes and thought back to the armoured monstrosity that was Akiyama, tapping into the supernatural. When he opened his eyes again, he saw his own deformed body again, this time clad in the same clothing but with the skin covered in a deep purple kind of bone, or plates of armour like a dinosaur. His eyes peered from behind the mirror with an eerie feeling that made even Nerezza step back and do a double take. The noise of a glass smashing in the main room snapped him back to reality. In the mirror again was just his plain human form. Walking up to the sink, he ran some water and splashed the ice-cold droplets over his face. Wiping it in his T-Shirt, he opened the door once more to see two men brawling on top of a pool table while the jukebox played a rap tune. Both men were dressed as though they had just stepped out of an Eminem video while the bartender clapped and applauded the fighters. Sidestepping the fray, Nerezza made his way towards the exit as one of the fighters planted an uppercut beneath the other's jaw, sending him off the table and crashing to the floor. Almost simultaneously, a man in a grey business suit sprinted past Nerezza like an olympian. The vampire stayed for a second, interested in whatever was about to unfold. The smart-clad man pulled a piece of paper from his pocket and raised it to the victor. 'Hi, I'm Rock Cold Kurt Hardy of the XHWCEF, would you like a contract?' The vampire rubbed his eyes and shook his head on the way out. 'Can't sleep, humans will eat me.... can't sleep, humans will eat me.... can't sleep, humans will....'
