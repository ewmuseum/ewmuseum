Lance....MISSING!

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/26/02 {::The first win, ever of Lance Bell's career, and what happens? He becomes PCW's Rising Star Champion. Now everyone will know who Lance Bell is and what he stands for. Lance Bell is the most cockiest person you'll ever meet, but he can back it up with nothing but straight facts. It was a tough battle, but Lance Bell of course came out on top, and took the title away from Red Rock like he said he would, now, Lance Bell has to go up against six guys in the course of two weeks.... on Tuesday, Lance Bell goes up against foe, Jon Kano, Newbludd & Mike Storm, and in his first ever PPV appearance and his first time defending his Rising Star Title, he goes up against Ripper Ryckman, Steve Johnson & Jay Sylo, but today, Lance is going to have some R and R with his crew and some others as he celebrates his win over Red Rock for his first professional title.::} {::The scene opens to a room full of balloons, a cake with Lance's face on it, and couple of beverages and some food next to it. Then a big screen T.V. with a paused caption of Lance holding the title in the air. Then the camera shows JuJu walking past the T.V. sitting down looking disturbed. Then Lance walks to the living room where JuJu is and has the Rising Star Title on his shoulder, he also has a camera and he will be asking his friends what they think of him winning his first match and winning the Rising Star Title at the same time, Lance smiles and begins to speak....::} Lance:Why do you look so mad, you should be happy for me, I'm the new Rising Star Champion. Isn't it a wonderful thing? JuJu:Man, wonderful for you! I'm still mad over Friday Morning! Lance:Wait, your still mad over that day? JuJu:Hell Yeah man, I went in there thinking they was going to leave me alone and you guys were going to get cracked on, but what happened? I got played out my own damn self. Lance:Well, if it helps you a little bit, James The Third got cracked on a little bit? JuJu:Well, that doesn't help (JuJu gets up from the chair and walks away...) Lance:Aww, JUJU! Jon:Hey Lance... Lance:Whassup, what you been up to? Jon:Ya know, nothing major but setting up for your big party.... Lance:Kool, smart thinking, so Jon what do you think about me winning my first match and becoming PCW's Rising Star Champion? Jon:Well, I knew you were going to beat up on Red Rock from the beginning and....... Lance:Um, Jon? You there? Jon...................................very cool.... Lance:Jon... WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH YOU? ARE YOU SUFFERING FROM SOME TYPE OF A DISEASE? Jon:(chuckles), no, what I was saying that I knew you were going to beat Red Rock from the beginning, I mean he had no opportunity of beating you, and I'm telling you now, everyone saw that you would win. Lance:Well thanx Jon, I knew you always believed in me, so where you bout to go now? Jon:Well, I'm going to make some phone calls to some important people that have contributed to- Lance:Uh, Jon? Jon:........................... Lance:Uh, you there? Jon:................. Lance:Oh boy, never mind. {::Lance walks away from Jon as he stands there with his moth open, unable to say anything. He heads to his kitchen which is down stairs. He looks around as his crew sets up for his big party bash. In the corner he sees C.A.S staring everyone down as he has his arms folded up, refusing to participate in anything. Lance walks over to him as Lance starts to speak...::} Lance:Yo, C.A.S, Why the long face? C.A.S:Well, why should I be happy? Lance:Well you fool, I'm the new Rising Star Champion...... C.A.S:So what? And Canada won on Sunday, which shows how much you Americans suck! Lance:Yo, C.A.S, shut the hell up and tell me what you think about my win before I bust your ass. C.A.S.:(squints eyes towards Lance)No... COMMENT! {::Lance just rolls his eyes and moves away from C.A.S. He looks for someone who has some freetime and he sees Flava. Flava is sitting down talking to someone on his two way machine. He has a cheesy grin on his face as Lance walks over to sit next to him. Lance holds up his camera and begins to talk to Flava....::} Lance:Hey, Flava, whatsup? Flava:Nothin' God.... Lance:Wait, I'm not God, I'm Lance 'The Sensation' Bell, but I don't blame you for the mix up (a smile comes across his face) Flava:Fool, you know what I mean, it's slang my friend.... Lance:Yeah, right, anyway, what do ya think about me winning my first match, and on that match winning my first title in PCW and ever in my career? Flava:It was all good son, you represented kna' mean? I was proud for you man, I was like you on top of the world now dawg, now all you gotta do is still be bustin ass like usual! It'll be all good.... Lance:Hey man, can you cut down on the ebonics for a little bit? Flava:(eyes widen)ASKING ME TO NOT SPEAK EBONICS IS LIKE ASKING ME NOT TO BREATHE! Lance:So, your saying you cant do it? Flava:OF COURSE NOT, JEEZ LOU WEEZ (gets up from seat) GOSH, I'M OUT OF HERE BUDDY! {::As Flava leaves, Lance shakes his head, he has two more to get in film. Kyle, and James the Third. He sees Kyle leaving the kitchen while drinking some sprite. Lance walks up to him and stops him in his tracks. Lance puts back his film and begins to interview Kyle as he looks confused....::} Lance:Hey, Kyle, I need some words from you about my win... Kyle:Sure dude, totally.... Lance:Right, anyways, I just wanted to get some of your feelings about me winning on Sunday and getting the Rising Star Title? Kyle:Dude, you should have saw me, it was totally rad how you won and hit the frog splash from the top, it was like, SO TOTALLY AWESOME! Lance:Ok, how long do you think I'll be able to keep this title? Kyle:Well, anything could happen, but I wish for you to hold the title for a very long time, that would just be some hot shit right there, totally narly and everything! You just need to be focused dude. Lance:I feel you, well, that's all Kyle, call over James The Third.... I'mma interview him too and that'll be it.... Kyle:Sure dude..... (yelling)JAMES, GET OVER HERE DUDE LANCE WOULD LIKE TO SPEAK WITH YOU.... catch you on the flip side Lance-a-nator Lance:Heh, right.... {::Kyle gets up and walks away. Then, from a distance Lance can spot out James The Third walking. For some reason, James is walking incredibly slow. Like he has something in-between his ass. Lance stares at him and shakes his head. Then Lance looks even more impatient as James makes no attempt to at least walk faster...::} Lance:(yelling)GOD DAMN MAN HURRY YOUR ASS! James:(with a British accent)Please, have some respect for yourself and calm down... Lance:Man, whatever, I just wanted to get a few comments from you about me winning Sunday night and becoming PCW's Rising Star Champion? James:I think it's very good. I mean we need more idiots holding titles in PCW... Lance:WHAT? James:I mean, smart people holding titles in PCW, congrats on your win man... Lance:Yeah, whatever (hears a beeping sound from the camera)....GOD DAMNIT, no more film, I'll be right back James... James:Allright.... {::Lance gets up and runs up the stairs, the crew is almost done setting up for the little party he is having. A few minutes later after hanging up all of the decorations and finishing up the cake, the party is ready to get started. about 12 people have arrived in under three minutes. About 15 minutes into the party, Lance has not come down to see his guest. Everyone starts to wonder what's is going on, JuJu brings up the situation....::} JuJu:Yo, where the hell is Bell man? James:Well, he went to get some film up in his room.... Kyle:Guys look over here (takes a note from the kitchen refrigerator).... It's a note by a mysterious man! JuJu:Ooooh, READ IT! (everyone gathers around Kyle). Kyle:Well like here it goes.... 'I have taken Lance 'The Sensation' Bell hostage, why have I taken him hostage?simply because of money! I'm asking for 50,000 dollars, if you give me 50,000 dollars, you will get back Lance Bell, if you give me 75,000 dollars, you will get back Lance Bell & his precious little title, my number is at the bottom of the page..... call me if you decide that you want to see your friend again....' Dude, this is like totally not cool! Mike:We gotta get Lance back, he has a match coming up on Tuesday, and at the PPV, it'll mess up his plans! {::The scene fades to black as Kyle picks up a phone and starts to dial the number at the bottom of the page...::}
