Inner Demons or Outer Demons? Judgment....Part 3

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-16-03 “What is a man to do when his mind betrays him? When all that he had once believed to be reality turns ‘round and becomes scattered through the catacombs of his own persona? What is the Knightly King to do, when facing the odds of both innumerable enemies, and the fanciful wanderings of his own mind? I guess I’ll do what I always do….Ante up, and kick in…..After all; what else is there?” During the speech, the shot has slowly been coming into focus, and we find ourselves in a hospital room. Venneman Pendragon lies on a bed, unconscious. Devoid of his armor, he looks vibrant in the white light, despite the wires attached to him and the monitors that bleep bleep bleep his vital signs. Slowly, the massive man’s eyes open and he peers around at his surroundings, a look of livid anger rising deep in his blue eyes. With a sudden motion, he rips the monitoring wires from his body and rolls from the bed. He is a bit wobbly on his feet, but otherwise, he seems to be fine. He pads to the closet, looks in and lets out a sigh of relief… Pendragon: Aye….at least the knaves have left my clothes and my beloved Excalibur in an accessible place. Now…’Tis high time I got out of here. A hospital is no place for a king… Pendragon pulls his pants on under the small hospital robe, and then removes that in favor of a linen shirt over which he straps on his golden chain mail armor. He belts Excalibur to his side and moves over to the bed to pull on his boots. As he is doing this, a small man in a white coat enters the room and gives Pendragon an admonishing look. Pendragon gives him a glare that could freeze a campfire. The doctor just shakes his head…. Doctor: And where are we off to, Mr. Pendragon? Pendragon sighs as he begins to lace up his other boot, and just shakes his head… Pendragon: When wilt thou foolish commoners get it right? ‘Tis Lord Pendragon, and I do what I please, go whither I want. Doctor: You need rest, and I doubt you will get it after you leave these halls. Pendragon: Thou art probably correct, but who art thou to tell me what it is that I need? I have problems thou art too small to even comprehend. And I MUST go, so out of my way…. Pendragon rises and strides confidently towards the door. The doc obliges and moves… Doctor: That’s right…I am to small; after all, I am only a doctor. Pendragon stops and stares the smallish man in the eye… Pendragon: Thou have bravery to stand up to me as thou art doing, but thy job is to merely fix what gets broken. I am not broken. At least, not in any way thy healing may help. Quickly, Pendragon turns and heads out the door. He walks down the drab hospital corridors, passing doctors and nurses clad in white. He passes a door that is guarded by two uniformed police officers. He slows and takes a peek into the open door. In the bed lies a man who appears to have been savagely beaten. He pauses a moment and the man looks up. Fear emanates from his entire form and he begins to shudder. Beaten man: The King of the Night….The Night King….King of Darkness….King of Light…Good Christ protect me! Pendragon’s jaw drops. He turns to one of the cops, who just shakes his head in response to the unasked question… Cop: Greg Stevens in there. Probably read about him…We got him courtesy of a Nighttime Redeemer as the paper put it. Don’t mind anything he says. I’d say his mind is pretty well mush after what he went through. And rightly so. If I had the chance, I’d shake the feller’s hand who roughed him up like that… Pendragon nods and begins again on his way. He quickens his pace as he makes it to the main doors and bursts out into the sunlight…. “Damned sawbones…The fool believes himself able to preach to me. The man has no idea. I care not that the knave merely tried to do his job, he has no idea of what I…and how in the hell did the defiler of women and children recognize me? There have got to be some answersssssssssssss..” His last words transform into a scream as the shot suddenly becomes wavy. Pendragon drops to one knee, holding his head with both hands…. YOU KNOW YOU WILL GIVE IN….SOONER OR LATER….’TIS THY DESTINY! ONE HIDES NOT FROM FATE, LORD PENDRAGON!! AND DID THEE SEE? GREG STEVENS KNEW THEE? BUT HOW CAN THAT BE, WHEN I AM THE KING OF NIGHT? Pendragon: Who….What are you? Why dost thou vex me so? I am the LIGHT! I am the way…Not the darkness! Passersby stare quizzically at the large man, on one knee, hand on his head, speaking into the air. Speaking to no one they can see. They move quickly to distance themselves from the ranting, armor clad giant, lest he lash out at them… I AM INSIDE! I AM THEE, THOU ART I!!! DIDST THOU NOT REMEMBER THY PROPHECY? Pendragon: A warrior…A warrior of the light. But thou speakest dark words in mine head…WHY DOST THOU HAUNT ME SO!!!??? Pendragon rises and stumbles down the street, but the voice does not cease… THOU KNEWEST THE CONSEQUENCES OF A RETURN. THE PROPHECY IS DESTINED TO BE FULFILLED! THE WARRIOR WILL EVOLVE…LIKE IT OR NOT, WE ARE THE SAME THOU AND I… Pendragon draws his sword now as he shambles along, unaware of where he is, unaware of where he goes. The people he shares the street with scatter at the sight of the deadly blade, and the madman who wields it… Pendragon: Never…Thou LIES! Tis not good that thee represents…Thou art evil, and I shall fight thee until I can fight no more! I WOULD WORRY LESS OF ME, AND MORE OF THE MEN WHO WISH TO TEAR THEE APART. IT WOULD SEEM THAT THE FOOLS OF THY…FEDERATION…ARE LINING UP TO GET A SHOT AT THEE! WHEN WAS THE LAST TIME THAT MEN FELT THEY COULD DO WHAT THE MULTITUDES DID TO THEE THIS PAST TUESDAY? WHEN WAS THE LAST TIME THOU WOKE UP IN A HOSPITAL? Pendragon drops to his knees and holds the hilt of Excalibur with both hands in front of his eyes. He closes them and begins to chant softly to himself; anything to make the voice stop…. Pendragon: King of Light. In me find redemption…Banish the darkness, protect mine soul… HAHAHAHAHA! THY MEDITATION WILL ONLY SERVE TO MAKE THEE BELIEVE THY EMPTY WORDS… The voice grows softer as it drones on, and Pendragon stays motionless… THY SHALL HEAR FROM ME AGAIN…THE NIGHTTIME REDEEMER…FITTING, I BELIEVE. THOU SHALT HEAR OF HIM AS WELL!!!!! The voice finally quiets and Pendragon opens his eyes. Sweat pours down from his forehead with the exertion it took to exorcise his demon. He looks around and rises wobbly to his feet. He stumbles to the street and hails….GASP!!! He hails a taxi. That never happens. At last, one stops and Pendragon climbs wearily into the back. The cab zooms away as the scene abruptly changes once more…. ***************************************************************************** A large figure stands atop a building. In one hand he holds a sword. A black cloak blows back from his large frame and his hair shimmers in the wind. He stares down at a convenience store across a poorly lit street. Without warning, two men in ski masked slip into the store and suddenly shots ring out. Beneath flowing hair, eyes that glow red take in the entire scene. With a sigh, the figure leaps from the building and lands deftly in the street. He strides over to the 7-11 and waits patiently outside the door. Inside, the men have shot the attendant and jumped the counter to retrieve the cash from the register…Their take? 50 bucks. An innocent lies dead because these men needed drug money. The lurker stands outside in the shadows, waiting for the murderers. Finally, they take their leave of the place and run from the 7-11, right into the lurker. They stop and stare at the man in surprise…. Lurker: Didst the two of thee believe thyselves safe? Did thee expect no punishment? No retribution? The two men look at each other and nod. One of them has a shotgun which he levels at the Lurker… Murderer: Yo…Why dontcha suck my dick? Accessory: Or better yet, why dontcha just blast him, Jimmy? Murderer: Sounds good…. BLAM!!!!!! The shot rings out, but where the Lurker stood, there is nothing but air…. Murderer: What the hell? The two men turn and find themselves face to face with he of the red eyes. With a quick motion, he swings his sword up and slices the barrel of the shotgun in two as if it were not really made of steel. The two men stagger back in fear, but the Lurker follows. He grabs the accessory by the throat and hurls him against the wall of the building. He drops like a stone, and crimson blood stains the stone wall like some crazy graffiti. The murderer drops the shotgun and falls in his knees… Lurker: Dost thou wish to beg for mercy? Murderer: YEAH!! Mercy! Wonderful word! I’ll take it! Give me mercy! The red eyed glare falls full onto the kneeling murderer, and the Lurker’s tone becomes even more harsh… Lurker: Didst thou show yonder shopkeeper mercy? Does he not lie dead in a pool of blood? How much was thy take? Murderer: What? Lurker: How much did thy steal? Murderer: Fifty bucks…You can have it! Just don’t kill me! The man reaches into his pocket and pulls out a couple tens, a couple fives and some ones and holds them out to the Lurker…. Lurker: I need not thy ill gotten money. I am here to pass judgment, and to carry out punishment. Thee is a murderer, many times over. This time, you shalt pay for thy transgressions…I am the King of the Night…King of the Darkness….Lord of the Light….And my word is final…. With that, the sword flashes up and the hand that holds the money is soon separated from the arm. The murderer clutches his severed limb and sobs incoherent ramblings…. Lurker: Thy punishment is death, and hell awaits thee…. The blade flashes out again, and this time a red slice appears down the front of the sinner. Murderer:AAAARRRRGGGGG!!! Lurker: Now….Meet thy destiny! The blade flashes again and the murderers head flies from his shoulders. Blood flows from the neck, and the Lurker gives the head a strong kick that sends it tumbling over to the man’s accessory. The Lurker follows and stares at the unconscious man… Lurker: Thee may yet live, to serve the earthly punishment for thy crimes. Hell can wait, but believe me…It is thy end…. With that, the lurker bends down and wipes his blade with the hair of the fallen man. He rises, sheaths the sword, and strides into the darkness….. ***************************************************************** Pendragon sits upright in bed, and wipes the sweat from his forehead. He quickly leaps to the floor and flies from the room. The scene switches to show him opening his front door… “I am afraid to look….afraid of what I might see….” He strides into his yard and picks up his morning paper. He unfolds it and the camera zooms in on the front page headline….. NIGHTIME REDEEMER Pendragon: Good Christ….. The scene fades as Pendragon’s knees give out as he reads the front page article….
