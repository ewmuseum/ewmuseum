Manfred Mania - Part 2 - Curing Tommy

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27 [As we last left Tommy and Harry, The Cowabunga Connection, they were arguing about whether or not you could remember being born or not. Tommy was obviously winning the battle but then a strange man wearing a tuxedo with a large forehead and a wiggling mustache happened to make an appearance. Harry introduced him as Manfred, and he was going to help Tommy recover from his illness] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy, still feeling quite ill, looks at this Manfred person in astonishment as he sits up on the leather couch he has resided in for over a week. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, still in his 'Twisted Sister rules, Tool Drools' apron, puts his arm around the waist of the large Manfred. The Lurch-like Manfred looks down at Harry with a scowl and Harry quickly takes his arm from around Manfred] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: What do you think, man? [Tommy still stares on in astonishment at the large figure who now has a slight smirk on his face] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: He's one big compadre, Harry. Harry: Yeah, just like my coc--- TTT: NO!! Just stop right there with it, dude! How's this Manfred dude going to help me get better? [The man known as Manfred walks over to Tommy. His large, black dress shoes make thumping noises with each step he takes, really showing how big this man is. Manfred leans over Tommy on the couch and begins looking into his eyes. Tommy returns the look and then looks over at Harry with a bit of fear in his eyes] TTT: Is this dude taking a trip on the otherside of the sexuality spectrum, compadre? [Harry shrugs] TTT: Um, Manfred, I just want you to know that I'm strictly a bodacious babe magnet. Your a large and in charge dude, but I don't think a babe magnet and a large homosexual dude like yourself would mesh well together. [Manfred stands up straight with a large scowl on his face, taking some offense to that accusation. Manfred then walks behind the couch and pulls up a poster of a half naked Pamela Anderson. He then points at her crotch and smiles brightly] TTT: Oh, so your into the babes too? [Manfred nods with a sly smile on his face. Harry, as usual, seems to be confused as he thinks about the woman known as Pamela Anderson] Harry: Doesn't she have some kind of disease, man? [Manfred and Tommy seem confused. Tommy then slowly begins to nod his head as if remembering hearing something about it] TTT: I think he's right, Manfred. That bodacious babe has Hepatitus, which is a totally bogus disease. I wouldn't touch her cooter with a thirty foot pole if I were you, compadre. [Manfred's sly smile fades into complete sadness. He takes one last look at the poster and then rips it up. He drops the pieces to the floor and then walks back to the front of the couch, continuing where he had left off. Manfred presses his hands into Tommy's chest and then into his stomach. Tommy seems a bit apprehensive about this but then begins to relax as he suddenly feels his body fighting the disease] TTT: This is totally bodacious, Harry, I can feel my gnarly white blood cells fighting the bogus disease cells!! Where did you find this dude? Harry: Wal-Mart, man. TTT: Wal-Mart?! Harry: That store that has falling back prices and has a bunch of old people working there, man. TTT: I know the store, but what was a dude like this doing at Wal-Mart? Harry: Working, man. [Manfred gives Tommy a thumbs up. Tommy then looks back over to Harry] TTT: How'd you know he could help me? Harry: He told me that he could, man. TTT: So, he can talk? Harry: No, man. TTT: He's mute?! Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: How'd he tell you that he could heal me? Harry: He wrote it on a piece of paper, man. TTT: Oh. [Tommy looks back over to Manfred, who is still busy doing some voodoo like things to Tommy's upper body] TTT: How much are we paying him? Harry: He's living here now, man. TTT: WHAT??! Harry: He's living here now, man. TTT: I heard you the first time, dude. Harry: Then why's you say 'WHAT??!' TTT: Because we don't need some stranger who looks really weird staying in our house. Not that he's bogus or anything, but we don't really know him that well. He could be like that heinous scary lady from that Stephen King movie, Misery. 'I'm your number one fan.' Then she starts bogusly hitting that author dude. Harry: I know him, man. TTT: You met him at Wal-Mart, dude. It doesn't mean that you know his personal history or anything. Harry: I asked him if he killed anyone, man. TTT: Then what did he say? Harry: He just shook his head, man. TTT: Do you think a homocidal maniac would tell you that he kills people?! Your being totally bogus lately, Harry!! First you want to sandwich Jessica Alba, which isn't too bad, but I don't want to see you naked! Then you were trying to massage my gnarly nuts after you poured soup all over them. NOW your trying to bring in some Lurch dude into our house, that we don't even know!! [Manfred stands up, obviously finished with what he was doing to Tommy. The Tubular One looks up at Manfred with a bit of confusion as to why he stopped. Tommy then stands up from the couch starting to feel different, in fact, he feels one hundred percent. Wait...he feels better than one hundred percent. HE FEELS TWO HUNDRED PERCENT!! HA! How about them apples, dudes and dudettes!! Two HUNDRED percent! How many people can say that they feel TWO HUNDRED PERCENT?! Well, anybody can say that, but Tommy feels it] TTT: I feel totally BODACIOUS, dudes. Like he just used his hands to take out that heinous illness that plagued my radical body. My nose isn't running, my cough is gone, and I can swallow my saliva without that bogus pain. Manfred, your a most gnarly DUDE!! [Manfred nods with a smile. Tommy then looks over to Harry who has a cheesy grin on his face] TTT: I think you should make a bedroom up for our good friend Manfred here, because he's staying in the most TUBULAR household of them all!! Harry: Far out, man. [Tommy now begins thinking about his upcoming matches. A big matchup against Dustin Downey and a member of the Ultimate Authority, Karl LeBlanc. UA has totally been bogus in a most heinous way and it's time to make them pay for them costing the Cowabunga Connection the PCW Tag Team Titles. They are the number one contenders for the tag titles and nobody can take that away, not ever the Ultimate Authority] TTT: Harry, we're now going to rise to the most bodacious heights that we deserve to be. After we teach Karl LeBlanc a lesson and show Ultimate Auhtority that we aren't just some surfer and a stoner, that we are the most lethal tag team force in PCW history. Then we can catch that big wave, surf it with our most gnarly skill and take down Politically Incorrect! Take the tag titles away from them and reign supreme just like we were meant to, dude. Because we both know that being free and having fun is the most gnarly thing in the world. Not being led under some dictatorship like the Ultimate Authority! Harry: Yeah, man. [Manfred nods] TTT: Sunday Sabatoge begins our second rise in PCW, dudes. We are going to be bodacious in a far out way! Harry: Yeah, man. [Manfred nods] [Tommy goes to walk to his bedroom to finally truly recover from his bogus sickness, and that's to clean himself up. That's when Manfred stops Tommy] TTT: What is it, dude? [Manfred holds up four fingers with a sly smile on his face. Tommy just looks at his fingers with confusion. Tommy then turns to Harry] TTT: What's that mean, dude? Harry: I don't know, man. [Manfred just keeps his four fingers extended with his smile growing more and more] TTT: The Four Horsemen? [Manfred shakes his head] TTT: The Four Amigos? Harry: That was the Three Amigos, man. TTT: Oh. Um, Foursome? [Monfred nods wildly] TTT: NO WAY, DUDE!! That is totally bogus. Just because Pam Anderson has Hepatitus, don't go trying to bag my bodacious babe. [Manfred seems sad and Tommy begins walking toward the bathroom. He looks back at Manfred and shakes his finger at him] TTT: And I mean it, dude! [Tommy disappears out of view. Harry walks over to the sad Manfred and puts his arm around him] Harry: He's really uptight when it comes to Jessica, man. [Manfred nods. The scene then fades to black]
