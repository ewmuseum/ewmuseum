'Ellie~! The Nerd Slayer' - Part Two : The Takeover.

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/06/02 Xavier Kannon is known as one of wrestling’s most temperamental personalities.... He is known for liking constant compliments, attention and rewards... So if any PCW Superstar was to go on strike from his own promos it was always going to be wrestling’s only Scientologist himself. Already enraged by the Executioner’s Chair delivered to Eleanor Hall, he was further angered by Legend Inc, one of the men he will face on Tuesday, refusing to even acknowledge him... Or the match... Or PCW... And was finally pushed over the edge. The Gold Patron Meritorious sent a recovered Ellie~! out to fill his promo time and told her to find something entertaining to do... ... But it seems that entertaining thing has found her, as it turns out she is the next in the blood line of the Nerd Slayers.... One to each Generation born... Who’s sole duty it is to repel the forces of Geekyness. Normally this being thrust upon what is still a young woman would be quite traumatic... Unless they did that everyday anyhow... “Like.... NO WAY!!!” “Like... WAY!!!” “No..... Way!” “Like.... So Way!” Our shot opens up at the Prep’s 2nd residence after the Corridor.... “The Mall” and we see a pair of Preps, dressed as not to clash but compliment, an act that was probably arranged for 20 minutes on the phone before they left. The pair of them are discussing something unimportant, shrieking to each other in a mixture of excited and annoying voices... Prep #1 : “So I was all, like, no way... Then she was all... Way!” Prep #2 : “Like... No way!” Prep #1 : “Like... WAY!” As they talk, if you can call their lack of any real conversation talking, they, and everyone around them freezes as a voice cuts in on the Public Announce system... “You think you are so TRENDY don’t you?...” A squeaky voice with a slight lisp bellows out from every speaker in every shop in the Mall and the shoppers all look amongst each other, wondering what the voice is going on about... As it speaks again, disdain has crept into the otherwise unthreatening voice... “Look at you 2 little Preppies with your “Likes” and your “Ways”... Thinking you are better than the rest of us...” The 2 confused girls turn to each other, their faces blank and expressionless. Prep #1 : “There’s..... Like, no way he’s talking to us right?” Prep #2 : “Er.... Like... WAY!” “YES I AM TALKING TO YOU!!!“ As the squeaky voice yells the sound breaks up, coinciding with the spit flying from his mouth, probably containing braces, and into the microphone... “For too long your... KIND.... Has had the free roam of this place.... Your GAP’s.... Your Virgin Megastores and your little accessories shops.... NO MORE!!!” Again the ditsy pair look to each other, even with the voice being aimed that them, still not knowing what is going on... Prep #2 : “Are they like.... Closing early today or something?” Again the frustrated, lisping voice squeaks out around the Mall. “Oh, we are closing all right.... This Mall is now MINE! No more GAP.... No more “PREPPIE” shops.... From now on things will be different....” Prep #1 : “Think they’ll build a Smoothie Bar? Or a shop that just sells Yoga videos?” The malevolent... Yet totally unthreatening voice cackles out... Doing it’s best to sound deeper... “Oh... There will be none of those... There will be a Games Workshop every 3rd store.... Discount flannel shirt emporiums... Shops that only sell thick rimmed glasses... An arcade that only has Spectrum games.... “ Preps #1 & #2 : “Like.... NO WAY!” The rest of the people in the Mall just stand around confused.... But the 2 Prep’s faces are masked in fear and abject terror.... “That’s right you two little pre-programmed trend followers.... This Mall is about to become a GEEKS PARADISE.... WE OWN IT NOW!!! HAHAHAHA!!!” The voice stops and wheezes for a second before the sound of an inhaler being used is heard and it continues... “HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” From all entrances, exits and fire escapes walk Nerds... A whole Army of them... Seemingly in the most geekiest dress they can find as a uniform... Each of them has a walkie talkie and within 5 seconds all of them start to use the communicators, working out little codes to use... “This Mall is now a PREP...... FREE...... ZONE!” The 2 Preppy girls look to each other and shudder, watching as the Nerds close in to surround the customers, sneering at their fashionable purchases as our shot fades to Black... Preps #1 & #2 : “Eeeek!” To each generation a Slayer is born,She alone will stand against the forces of Geekyness....She is... The Nerd Slayer... “Excuse me, who are you again? President of LOSERLAND?!?!?!?” As the annoying, whiny tone of Eleanor Hall is heard our shot fades in on the confines of a GAP store in an undisclosed location... Seemingly not being used but all the racks are full anyway... “Good... Good... You are growing stronger by the hour...” From behind a rail of patterned tops walks the man we saw before in the Black car, his English accent easily recognisable... He is dressed in a stylish Black suit and his hair looks as if he has just come from the stylist.. The fiancé of Xavier Kannon seems pleased with the praise she is getting, doing a quick bounce on the spot and applauding herself... Eleanor : “I know, who would have thought Ancient Callings could be so fun?... YAY ME! In a way it’s kinda good that Xavier went on strike, I SO do better promos than him.... Maybe I should get the match against Legend Inc. and Snow.... That’d like TOTALLY rule!” As her excitement begins to decline her cell phone rings and she reaches into her Red Prada bag to retrieve it, checking the caller ID and turning to her mentor... Eleanor : “I have to take this...” The Englishman, sent from the Watcher’s Council to find Ellie~! nods, gesturing to her to take the call before going to check on something in the GAP store come Slayer training lair. Watcher : “Go ahead.... The radiation from cell phones will help make you stronger.... As will ingestion of Evian water...” She holds the phone up to her ear.. Eleanor : “Hello?...... Oh, hey Xavi..... Oh, I’m good, long story, some ancient blood line, me being next, humanities last line against geeks, yada yada yada..... Yes, I know you are still on strike.... Don’t worry, it’s going well so far, they aren’t missing you at all...... I don’t know if that’s a good thing or not.... Okay, I’ll be home in time for Popular..... Byzzzzzzies!” As soon as she hangs up a Nerdy kid leaps from behind the Denim jacket rail, the Watcher not batting an eyelid as Ellie~! jumps backwards startled... The boy’s greasy hair is tightly slicked back and his checked shirt and unlabelled jeans look dirty and un-ironed.... Evil, yet wonky eyes stare at her through thick lenses and dribble runs down his chin from his metal filled mouth... Nerd : “Agh....Urgh..... Farscape.....Eugh....Star Trek...” As she recovers from the shock, Eleanor strikes a pose, holding out a palm while her other hand rests on the hip of her Silver star embroidered flared jeans.... Her long, wavy Brown hair flowing down her back bouncing with natural volume as she adopts her stance... Eleanor : “Like... Where did you come from? The Greaseball convention at the Loser Civic Centre?” With the bitchy insult the Nerd combusts, fuelled by the natural oils in his hair and on his skin before all that is left of him is a pile of ashes with the glasses sitting on top... Slowly sinking into the mound due to the weight of the thick lenses... The Watcher claps, pleased with what he has seen... Watcher : “Good... You coped with the Nerd’s “Bush Lurking” ambush well...” She again soaks up the praise, struggling to understand what he is saying... Eleanor : “I, like SO did!Your voice is all funny.... Are you from Norwayland?” She receives a blank look until their heads both snap around to the main doors as a man comes running through them.... Exhausted and bleeding from the head... Watcher : “Brad.... What happened?” The man collapses on the floor, breathless... Blood pouring from a head wound as the Nerd Slayer and her Watcher appear at his side.. Brad : “The Mall....... Nerds, taking over...... Have the place barricaded...... Geeks everywhere...... Fashion Sense down to 3%..... I tried to escape but took a Blood Angel Chaplain to the head.... Those things are heavy and quite sharp....” The Watcher’s face turns solemn.... As if he has just remembered something... Watcher : “How could I have been so stupid.... Tonight is the Feast of Cyron Balver.... A Nerd worshipped by a small but fanatical base of Geeks... I had forgotten all about it...” As the Watcher looks to Brad’s injuries, seeing what his mistake has cost, Ellie~! gets to her feet looking quite chirpy. Eleanor : “Fear not Norwaylandman or wherever you come from... For I, Ellie~! am the Nerd Slayer.... And I’m not going to, like, let ANY miniature based games take place in my Mall..... It’s like... SO time!” As Ellie~! packs her Slayer’s Weapon, “The Preppy Book of Put-Downs” our shot starts to fade to Black, showing her head through the doors and off to meet her destiny against the Cult of Cyron Balver as we finally hit Black. “Wait, do you, like, think I should change this top?” To be continued.....
