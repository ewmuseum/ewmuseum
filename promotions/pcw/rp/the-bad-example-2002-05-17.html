The Bad Example

17 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-17-02 [Fade In] [The shot reveals to the viewer that we’re located in a restaurant, no wait, a coffee shop. It’s a little diner/coffee shop featuring a few tables and chairs lining the windows of the shop. There’s bar with about twelve stools surrounding it, of which only a few are occupied. Two police officers sit next to each other snacking on donuts and coffee on the far side. Near the middle is a man wearing a long overcoat eating a large breakfast of bacon, eggs, toasts, hash browns, orange juice, and a cup of coffee.] [On the other side of the bar across from the cops is a ‘Scandalous’ Ryan ‘Streak’ Stephens. He’s wearing khaki shorts, a Calvin Klein T-shirt, a baseball cap, and brown leather sandals. He’s drinking a soda water, and writing in a small notepad. Waitresses are bustling about behind the counter to pour coffee, and hand out breakfast plates. Stephens seems unaware of his surroundings, focussed merely on whatever he may be scribbling in the notepad.] [Perhaps he’s writing another one of his bogus rumor mill reports to release on his site, which is growing increasingly popular to PCW fans. This time one about Mark Makonie, his Friday Fusion opponent, perhaps. Or maybe he’s planning out his agenda, his ascension to the top of PCW. Nah, probably not. It takes tons of hard work and time to become one of the best in PCW, and Stephens is a smart man, he realizes this. Maybe he’s planning out a creative and breathtaking in-ring display like the one he did calling out Nelson and the Ultimate Authority.] [Speaking of which, what was he thinking? The Phantom Phive is merely a bunch of new guys in PCW who haven’t so much as learned the ropes yet, and Stephens is out there calling out Tyler Nelson. Sigh. The Phantom Phive seems to be in a little trouble lately. They’ve been quiet, only Stephens has been out in the limelight. It was just last Friday he made the challenge to Mark Makonie, thanked the fans for backing him, and told them of his new job. What’s in store for Stephens? If only we could read what he’s writing in that little notebook, perhaps we could find out.] [Our guessing stops as a waitress approaches Stephens. She’s an older lady, probably in her early forties, and yet she’s trying to make up for it with make up, and a push up bra (picture Susan Sarandon in Bull Durham).] Waitress: Would you like anything to eat with your coke sir? Stephens: [barely looking up] No thank you ma’am. [The waitress is curious now, of what’s holding his attention so.] Waitress: What are you writing about? You a health inspector or something? We keep this little place pretty clean! [Stephens doesn’t look up, and yet he doesn’t seem real annoyed either. Perhaps he likes the chance to talk, it will give him a break from all the writing.] Stephens: I’m not a health inspector, I’m jotting down some ideas for my web site, so I can update when I get back to the hotel. My wife went shopping an it’s boring in the hotel alone, so I figured I’d get something to drink and get out of the room for a bit. [So our first suspicion was right! He’s probably creating a rumor to post about Makonie. The waitress now seems interested.] Waitress: A web site? Computers fascinate me, but I’ve never really had time to learn how to make web sites and stuff like that. What does your web site entail? [Stephens closes the notepad and stick the pen through the silver spirals at the top.] Stephens: It’s basically a site where I post rumors and news regarding PCW wrestlers. I also write my opinions regarding things such as shows, the PCW talent totem poll, and much more. Waitress: Sounds kind of cool I guess. I don’t know much about wrestling, I just like the hard bodies. [pointing at the notepad] So can I read what you were writing? Stephens: [smiles] Sure, on my web site when I go back to the hotel and post it. Waitress: Ah, come on you know I’ll never go look, just let me have a little sneak peak. These workdays are long, it will give me a little perk, come on. [Stephens sighs and opens the notepad to the page he was just writing on, and slides it across the table to the waitress.] Rumor has it that Edward Norton may sue PCW wrestler Mark Makonie for copyright infringement of his character in American History X. Norton stated, “That guy is trying to be just like my character in American History X, and the reasons we copyrighted it was because we didn’t want anyone trying to be racist and full of bigotry like the character in the movie. Now this Makonie guy has gone and become just that. This guy is a disgrace to the television industry, and CEO Tyler Nelson should get him off the air. He’s setting an awful example to kids all over the world. I hope Stephens puts him in his place!” Further information brought to our attention was that some middle school kids drew swastikas on their arms and tried to fight a group of black kids from the same school, claiming they were Mark Makonie’s handymen. This man must be removed from television shows or it will cost PCW severely. [The waitress seems shocked after reading the rumor report. She looks at Stephens somewhat concerned.] Waitress: There is really a man on nationally televised shows who runs around being a Nazi, where kids can actually watch him, and imitate him. That’s absurd. I hope somebody can get rid of him. Stephens: Well I intend to try ma’am, now if you’ll excuse me I have to go train for my match. [Stephens gives the woman a polite nod, and slides two dollars across the table, then snags his notebook and inserts it into his side pocket. As he turns to leave the woman realizes not only was he a web page owner, but Stephens, the wrestler. She tries to call out to him, but he’s already out the door.] [As Stephens exits he thinks to himself, the waitresses’ reactions was perfect. He wants everyone who reads about Makonie, to have the same reaction. He’s setting an awful example for kids and he has to get off the damn television, even if he has to do it himself because Nelson won’t. Stephens begins walking down the moderately crowded sidewalk back towards his hotel pondering various thoughts.] [What is to become of him in the upcoming six man tag match if SuMa and Thunder don’t bring their A games? He knows he can defeat any of the men he’s fighting in a one on one battle, but not three on one. And what about his stable the Phantom Phive? He’s the only one who has been making a point to be at shows, regardless of whether he needs to be for his new job or not. Just as Kano is the glue that holds the Malice Crew together, Stephens may be the glue that holds the Phantom Phive together, or maybe, just maybe, he’s the rock they break themselves against. He’ll withstand, he always does, but what about those around him? Stephens seems mildly worried as we fade to black.]
