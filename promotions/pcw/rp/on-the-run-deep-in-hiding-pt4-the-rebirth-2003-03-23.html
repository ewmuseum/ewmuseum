On The Run, Deep In Hiding pt4- The Rebirth?

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd March 'I haven't stood here in a long, long time,' Diablo's familiar voice says. He is of course looking at a church in his home city of Puebla. The church is quite small, as it is only meant for the small village that surrounds the church. The church is wooden, but is also very sturdy, it has stained glass windows and a spire atop it. El Diablo is home. The Mexican Devil takes a long deep breath and slowly walks in through the main doors of the church- a place where many thought El Diablo would never be, including himself. Diablo scans the insides of the church, which sites several pews, a baptising bowl, many, many paintings of biblical characters and of course at the front, where the sermons are given. Diablo looks around further and finds what he is looking for, the confession box. 'I'm taking your advice Pedro, it's time to be cleansed,' El Diablo looks up as he speaks to his dead brother Pedro. The Mexican Devil walks slowly towards the confession box, looking around as he does. He stops at the bowl wear children are baptised. 'Well, I am a bit hot,' he comments aloud to himself. He dips his left hand in and suddenly pulls it right out in lots of pain as steam stems solemnly from his hand. 'AH CHRIST IT BURNS!' Raul yells out very loudly, before covering his mouth and looking around again trying to be inconspicuous. 'Fuck this really hurts,' he whispers softly to himself. The water in the baptismal was cold, so perhaps El Diablo was burnt by it due to his choice of name. The Mexican Devil finally reaches the confession box after his slow walk and then stops just outside it to smarten himself up. He combs his dyed red hair back with his right hand and tucks his white vest into his blue denim shorts; he again takes a deep breath and proceeds to step into the confession box. He takes a seat and opens up the mini door so the Priest can hear him. 'Forgive me father for I have sinned. It has been many years since my last confession,' Raul tries to say calmly but comes across as very nervous. 'THESE HAPPY DAYS ARE YOURS AND MINE......' A loud shuffling is heard and what sounds like a television being turned off is also audible. 'Oh, um, carry on my son,' the Priest states. Diablo looks very baffled, but just rolls his eyes and carries on. 'Well father, in my life time I have done some very bad things...' Diablo says before getting interrupted by the Priest. 'Were they sexual things?' the Priest asks. Again, The Mexican Devil looks very baffled. 'NO!' he shouts. 'Oh, carry on then,' the Priest, says very uninterested. El Diablo seems to be getting a little impatient by the attitude of the Priest but carries on nonetheless. 'Well, it's my way of life really. I'm a wrestler and wrestle under the name of El Diablo, and for a while I was a true Satan worshipper,' he says whilst rubbing his right hand across his brow. The Priest coughs a moment. 'Oh, is that all? I forgive you,' the Priest says surprisingly calmly. El Diablo really is baffled now, and steps out of the confession box. 'WHAT THE HELL?' he shouts loudly and swiftly opens up the booth where the Priest sits in. To his amazement, upon opening the door, he sees several empty bags of potato chips on the floor and a small television in the corner, but the most surprising thing he sees in there is a sweaty man who must weigh about 250lbs. 'Carlos?' Diablo exclaims. The man looks up at him and smiles. 'RAUL!' he shouts in his typical Mexican accent. Carlos springs up to his feet and embraces El Diablo with a hug. 'How have you been man?' Carlos asks him while brushing his long brown hair back. Diablo shrugs a moment. 'I just scattered Pedro's ashes, so I'm a little happy/sad from that. Otherwise life seems to have been ok lately. And you Carlos?' he responds and asks as he raises his eyebrows. Diablo hasn't seen Carlos in five years, the time he left to go to the United States so to see him in church is quite a surprise to him. Carlos was a long time childhood friend, one of the few friends Diablo did have, so it is a happy moment to see such a long time friend that he happened to lose touch with. 'Oh I'm fine, apart from the fact that I'm in the middle of robbing the church,' he replies while looking around suspiciously. Diablo looks around too and sighs loudly. 'Sheesh Carlos, if you needed money you could have asked me,' El D replies whilst patting Carlos on the back. Carlos steps away and shakes his head, 'I don't need your charity, man. I can make a living,' Raul thinks for a second then scratches his head. 'You call this making a living?' he asks. Carlos scratches his chin. 'Well sure it ain't ideal but it beats working some nine to five job you know,' Carlos replies sternly. Diablo runs his hands through his smooth red hair while looking straight into Carlos' dark brown eyes. 'Just get a job, this isn't right.... in theory,' The Mexican Devil states as he smiles devilishly. Carlos smiles back. 'Look man, the cops will be here soon, we should really head out and catch up,' he says sincerely. Diablo smirks, 'OK, but next time steal from a bank or something,' On that note, Police sirens are heard for the first time and are quickly closing in. 'Oh one last thing Carlos, where is the Priest?' Raul asks his old friend. Carlos laughs. 'Look up man,' he points his left index finger upwards as he speaks. Diablo looks up and smirks, as tied, gagged and blindfolded on a cross on the wall is the Priest. 'That's just like you Carlos,' Diablo states while shaking his head. Carlos quickly goes back into the confession box and grabs the television and a large wad of cash, which he has stolen from the church. 'OK, lets go,' Carlos, whispers quietly to El Diablo. Diablo nods and both go to exit the church from the back door, as the sirens get nearer. Carlos busts through the back door and starts to run, Diablo however stops for a second. 'I tried Pedro, I really tried. But this is a part of me I want to keep,' Diablo says aloud to his dead brother. He quickly departs the church and runs for cover.
