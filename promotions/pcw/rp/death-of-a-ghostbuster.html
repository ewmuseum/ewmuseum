Death of a Ghostbuster

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday, December 13, 2002 (If this RP sucks, or if I spelled Dan Akroyd's name wrong... I apologize) *cue the theme to Ghostbusters*When there's something strangeIn the neighborhoodWho you gonna call?GHOSTBUSTERS!! We arrive at an old, abondoned house in the small town of Munsons Corners, New York. There is an old rumor saying that the house... an old, crickity, beat up piece of wood... is haunted. A few people in the past have gone up there, but none have ever returned. To see what all the fuss is about, we enter the supposed haunted house. 'I've almost got ya!' Upon entering, we see a giant ghost. We also see actor Dan Akroyd. He has a pack on his back similar to the one used in the Ghostbuster movies he took part in. Dan: Try to ruin my holiday, will you!?! Dan, dressed in a brown suit similar to the suits the Ghostbusters fought in, has a giant hose-type fixture in his hands. H appears to be trying to suck the ghost in... or whatever it is that they do. Dan: Be a nice little ghost and come here! Akroyd is fighting to bring the ghost in. The ghost, having probably seen this sort of thing in the movies or something, is fighting for everything its worth. Dan: Dammit! Come here! Akroyd really starts blasting now. The ghost, who is a plae white color, decides to give up the fight. Akroyd blasts the ghost and sucks it in. Once he does, Dan falls backwards onto the wood floor. Dan: Finally. Akroyd breathes a sigh of relief. While on the ground, Akroyd takes the heavy pack off of his back. Dan: Wait until I get back to the big city. I'm gonna tell everyone how I vanquished the ghost and ended the stupid myth once and for all. Dan can't help but to laugh a little. Dan: I'll be a hero! 'Bah!' Dan: Who said that? Dan slowly rises to his feet. He starts to look around him. 'That killing... was pathetic.' Akroyd looks around a little more. Then, the actor turns around. The second he does, he gets floored with a right hand. The Dark Gimmick, the evil PCW ref, suddenly pops up into the picture. Gimmick, decked out in his traditional cloak, looks down at the floored actor. Gimmick: You... are pathetic. Dark Gimmick bends over. He holds his arm down. Then, with his skeletal hand, The Dark Gimmick punches through the back of Dan Akroyd. After a couple seconds of fighting, Gimmick rips the spine out of the actor showing it in clear sight. Gimmick: You... shall die. The Dark Gimmick casually carries the now dead actor by his exposed spine. He carries it over to a hook. The hook is the type of hook you'd place a coat or something on. Dark Gimmick hooks Dan Akroyd onto the hook by his spine. Dark Gimmick, with a blank look on his face, looks at Dan. Gimmick: Never mess... with a supernatural force or else you... will suffer... the consequences. With his bony hands, Dark Gimmick smacks the dangling actor across the face. Gimmick: Now... you become what you fear... what you kill. The Dark Gimmick walks over and grabs the pack that Dan was wearing on his back to fight the ghost. He looks at it for a few brief moments, then chucks it... hitting the dangling actor's body. Gimmick takes one last look at Akroyd's body before slowly walking away. Fade to black
