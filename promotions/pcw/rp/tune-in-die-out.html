Date: not entered You know how tiring it gets to have to deal with this old relic every couple of weeks? I swear the boy wont stay down…he’s got heart I tell you what. PCW’s answer to the bionic redneck…Duke Williams… But geez…it’s not enough we have to deal with that mental midget again…we have to take on Darkstar, Rent a Viro, and El Dildo, Snow’s new jailhouse toy. It’s just another slow night for the god…how disgusting… All three of these men…they have something against the other. Snow against Diablo… Frisco against Rent A Hellian… Xavier Kannon against Darkstar… I’ll be damned if I allow the god to fall into a slow ass rut with the Malice Man. The god is the #1 contender…the top of the game, the only man around this wretched place that has defeated Xavier Kannon in fair and open combat. Hey, it was a while ago, but that’s besides the point. XK got his face crushed by the god before…and it will happen again the moment the two of them collided. But for now…that mortal’s our partner in crime… Heh…the god, a CEO, a criminal, and a drug lord…against a Goth, a redneck, a Mexican, and heroic villain. Only in PCW, people… SKKKKKKKKKKKKT!! “It’s been days since the search party has been on the hunt for the three missing teens. But local authorities say that luck has not been on their side.” A close-cropped blonde woman in a black jacket and red blouse looks on at the camera as she shuffles papers. “Police say that they still have hope, but so far they’ve found nothing. The parents of these three are devastated at the disappearance of their offspring. Here’s Jack Duncan with a word with the children’s’ parents. The scene cuts to show a black bald man in a brown suit he stands next to five adults, one man in a t-shirt and jeans with two couples behind him; a preppie man and woman, and an executive looking couple. They all held worried look on their faces. “I’m Jack Duncan on location in the Harrington area with the parents of the missing children. Sir, what are your feelings on your child out there in the middle of nowhere?” The lone man gives him a wary look before speaking. “What kind of dumbass question is that? Um, I can say dumbass on TV right?” “On this channel, you can sir.” Duncan responded with a nod. “That’s a dumbass question then! Our dumbass kids are out there kidnapped by some dumbass kidnapper or rapist doing God knows what to them dumbass kids!” The preppy black haired man stepped forward and spoke into the mike. “All I know is that I want my son home safe and sound.” His wife jumped in out of nowhere. “WHAT ABOUT THE CHILDREN!?” The couple in business suits stepped forward, both of them on their cell phones. The husband held his aside and looked into the camera. “I’m paying ten grand to anyone that finds my boy. No further comment.” Mrs. Prep jumped in again. “PLEASE! WILL SOMEONE THINK OF THE CHILDREN!!” Duncan stepped back aside and looked into the camera. “You have it there, folks…distraught people looking for answers…I’m Jack Duncan---.” “I’MA KILL THOSE DUMBASSES!!” a man yelled off camera. Duncan sighed deeply. “Back to you in the studio.” The woman looked blankly at the camera before looking down at her papers. “If you’ve seen any sight of Gilliam Woods, Stephanie King, or Kenneth Dunn, please contact the authorities of their whereabouts.” SKKKKKKKKKKT!! “No, I don’t think we’ll be doing that…” A silent TV stood atop a wooden crate, the screen blank like a window of darkness. Stepping to its side was the Divine Prophetess, Cyclonica. Still wrapped in that black cloak reaching her ankles, she nodded her head into the direction the TV was facing. “See there, children? Your parents as well as the police are hot on our tracks. Surely your lives will be saved at that point.” She raised a finger to her chin, thinking for a moment. Then she shook her head, dismissing the thought. “No…wait…you WONT be saved. I forgot…sorry.” In the reflection of the TV set, three cloaked bodies could be seen hanging from meat hooks. Turning around, the three little Satanists from before were hanging by bound wrists above the ground, terrified looks on their faces. “You wont get away with this!!” Ken yelled out at Cyclonica. The Divine Prophetess whipped her head towards him, paying him some attention. She stepped towards him and grabbed him by his chin. “Why do you good-natured people always say that when you’re about to die?” he let out a squeak as she gripped his face tighter, her nails digging into his skin. “It…really…bothers me!” “What do you want with us anyway, lady?” Gill shouted out at her. Cyclonica looked at him, shrugging her shoulders. “If I remember why I’m doing this…remind me to tell you. Until then…SHUT UP!” Cyclonica walked away from them, drawing closer to a dark corner of the dank and drafty wharehouse. She leaned against the wall and folded her arms underneath her cloak. “What now, dear god?” she asked. Peering closer into the darkness, the crouched appearance of the god could be seen. His arm came out from the darkness, his fist balled up with his thumb sticking out. Slowly, it went south. Cyclonica nodded. “As you wish.” She said with a flat grin. The she walked over towards the dark trio, untying the strings at her cloak. Gill looked over at the hand of the god signing their fate and shook his head. “No, he doesn’t mean that!” he said quickly. Ken nodded quickly in desperation. “Yeah, he’s just, like, playing with you! Thumbs DOWN means to let us go!” She whipped off her cloak and draped it underneath the teens’ feet. Then she walked over to Stephanie and looked into her eyes…they were holding a lot of fear there…a lot of despair and defeat. That’s the look Akiyama saw a lot from these mortals…as they said their defiant words…their eyes still held that look. He’d see it on Fusion…and after that as well… “Come on! We didn’t do anything wrong!” Gill yelled in panic. “Well…not yet at least.” Ken muttered. “We were trying to make a sacrifice to Satan, after all.” Gill swung his foot towards him and kicked him in his groin. Ken squealed in pain, but the cursing and the crying turned silent as Cyclonica backhanded both of the boys. “Silence!” she hissed. “Why cant you be more like your friend! She’s accepted her fate, now you two do the same!” she turned back to Stephanie and rubbed her finger against her cheek. “Why can’t all of you do that…you foolish mortals…accept your fate and die…” Steph’s eyes flickered up, but shy away from meeting the eyes of the Celestial. Cyclonica cocked her eyebrow up as she pressed her two fingers against her soft lips. She then stepped away from her, turning her back on the children. Yeah, there were screams…but they didn’t last long… Yeah, there would be screams tonight…but they wouldn’t last long either… Cyclonica did not listen to their cries of pain…she tuned them out…as she tuned in… SKKKKKKKKKKT!! “BE SURE TO TUNE IN TONIGHT FOR PCW FUSION! IT’LL BE AMAZING ACTION WHEN 8 OF THE COMPANY’S FINEST SUPERSTARS GO HEAD TO HEAD IN AN 8 MAN TAG MATCH!! IT’LL BE DARKSTAR, EL DIABLO, PRESIDENT DUKE WILLIAMS, AND THE PCW CONTINENTAL CHAMPION RENT A VILLAIN AGAINST THE TEAM OF SNOW, FRISCO, THE PCW WORLD #1 CONTENDER, THE NEO VOID GOD AKIYAMA, AND THE PCW WORLD CHAMPION AND CEO XAVIER KANNON!! DON’T MISS IT!!”
