The Race Part 2

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/04/03 (The scene begins with the camera panning down a high street. You see The Overweighter walking down the road. He walks into a paper shop. The camera cuts to inside the shop, you can see The Overweighter walk in. He walks over to the cooked food part. He sees sausage rolls and pasties. Overweighter grabs the goods and walks over to the counter.) The Overweighter: Hi, do you have a microwave? Shopkeeper: Yeah, over there. Do you want anything else? The Overweighter: No thanks. (The Overweighter pays for the food and walks over to the microwave. He puts loads in at once and turns on the microwave for 40 seconds. While Overweighter is waiting he looks around. He looks outside the store and sees his brother Candy Cane. His brother notices Overweighter and walks in. Overweighter tries to hide behind an isle of sweets but Candy Cane still knows where he is.) Candy Cane: Overweighter! I know your hiding behind there come out. (The Overweighter walks out from behind the sweets and tries to get rid of his brother.) Candy Cane: What are you doing? The Overweighter: Well you told me to run so I did. Candy Cane: Yeah but you were running away from me! You also came in the closest shop there was. The Overweighter: That's because I came to get a drink, I didn't want to dehydrate myself. (The microwave stops and makes a pinging noise. Candy Cane turns around to look at the microwave when Overweighter uses his head for the first time in his life!) Candy Cane: Overweighter what's this? The Overweighter: err, err…LOOK IT'S FRED!!! (Candy Cane looks around while Overweighter quickly opens the microwave and runs around the isle. Candy Cane turns around to see an empty microwave with the door still open. Overweighter is now running out of the store while Candy Cane stands there like a lemon. Overweighter goes to take a bite out of a sausage roll when all over a sudden a seagull swoops down and snatches it straight from his hands.) The Overweighter: Hey give that back! (Candy Cane hears Overweighter outside and goes running out.) Candy Cane: Hey! I told you no snacks! The Overweighter: But I'm hungry! Candy Cane: The only time you can eat is at breakfast, lunch and dinner. The Overweighter: You missed out brunch and supper. Candy Cane: No! (Candy Cane takes the rest of the food from Overweighter before the birds do.) Candy Cane: come on you need to get in shape for this race. The Overweighter: Can't I just give them the money that I said? Candy Cane: Yeah you can but you would have to sell the house! The Overweighter: Oh! Candy Cane: Come on get jogging! (The Overweighter starts to jog down the road while Candy Cane jogs behind him. What Candy Cane doesn't know is that Overweighter slipped a sausage roll in his pocket before they got taken away. He takes it out and eats it at the same time as jogging. The Overweighter has got a blocked up nose and can't breathe through it so the only place he can breathe is through his mouth. He is eating his sausage roll. He stuffs the whole thing it and can't chew it. He is slowly running out of breathe while jogging down the road. He decides to try and swallow it in one. While he swallows it his body tries to breathe at the same him. It gets clogged up in his throat. Overweighter still can breathe but only a little bit. He doesn't say anything to Candy Cane about it stuck in his throat but He wants to cough it out really bad. Now when he breathes it makes a funny squeaking noise. I loud squeak when he breathes in and a quieter one when he breathes out. He isn't getting enough air in his body to get to his muscles because of the sausage roll.) The Overweighter: Squeak!!! Squeak! Squeak!!! Squeak! Candy Cane: What's the noise? (Overweighter can't talk because of the sausage roll. Overweighter doesn't want to tell Candy Cane because he will get angry with him for eating something. Candy Cane decides it's time Overweighter has a rest and they stop.) Candy Cane: Overweighter you can have a rest now. (The Overweighter stops and tries to get rid of this sausage roll in his throat. He wants to do it without his brother seeing him.) Candy Cane: What are you doing? (The Overweighter has is back faced to Candy Cane.) The Overweighter: … OUCH! (The Overweighter squeezed it down his throat and you could hear him scream like a schoolgirl.) Candy Cane: What's wrong? The Overweighter: I had something in my throat! Candy Cane: Well it's out now so let's get going again. The Overweighter: What's going on now? (People are now marching down the street with Rainbow coloured flags, the men are all wearing tight Italian looking jeans. There are other women that are dress and look like men. A man walks up to Overweighter.) Man: excuse me are you in the parade? The Overweighter: …err… Yeah??? Man: Oh sshuper! The Overweighter: … Man: Who are you with at the moment? The Overweighter: I'm here with my brother. Candy Cane: Hi. Man: Oh I get it, were all brothers! The Overweighter: No, he really is my brother. Man: Oh my god you sick freaks! (The man runs off he is running like a girl and with his wrists bent.) The Overweighter: … I'm confused, it's sick to have a brother now? Candy Cane: You're an idiot he asked you who you're with, he meant dating! The Overweighter: Why would he ask me that? (Candy Cane leans over to him and whispers the answer in his ear.) The Overweighter: No! Candy Cane: Yeah. The Overweighter: No! Candy Cane: Yeah he is. The Overweighter: Okay he does look it a bit. Candy Cane: So I take it you don't know what that parade is? The Overweighter: A normal stupid parade that we have every year because of something someone did ages ago that was really good? Candy Cane: Nope, I'll tell you. (Candy Cane leans over to him and whispers it again.) The Overweighter: A GAY PARADE! (Everyone around him stops what they are doing and looks at him. Everything is totally silent, people outside have stop to look at Overweighter. Overweighter takes a big gulp and slowly steps backwards towards a back ally. Candy Cane follows him, they both have stopped breathing and soon as Overweighter is closer to the ally he begins to run down it. Candy Cane does the same. Once they have gone everyone acts normal and carries on with what they were doing before.) Candy Cane: You had to shout it out loud didn't you!?!! The Overweighter: I'm sorry it became a shock when I found out that the rainbow flag is the gay flag! Candy Cane: I thought you knew that? The Overweighter: LOOK AT ME, I'M STUPID!!! Candy Cane: True… Come on we need to train for this race. The Overweighter: Hey there is only a week left to train. Candy Cane: Okay Overweighter if you don't eat a thing other than proper meals I will let you eat as much as you want after the race. Is that okay? The Overweighter: I'll do it if and IF!!! You pay for everything I eat on that day. Candy Cane: Okay. The Overweighter: You got yourself a deal! (The Overweighter shakes Candy Canes hand as the camera fades to a different scene. You can see The Overweighter in a jogging suit and his holding a skipping rope in his hand. He is on top of a building with a flat roof. Eye of the tiger begins to play. He starts to skip slowly and gets faster and faster. The camera pans down through the ceiling while he still skips. You can see people trying to read the paper and drink a cup of coffee. Every time Overweighter jumps it causes a small Earthquake and the person spills his coffee everywhere. You can also see the light hanging from the ceiling swinging side to side. The camera cuts to a barn with a close up on a supported piece of wood going through the whole barn. Suddenly you see Overweighter attempting to do a chin up. He does one chin up and you don't see him again until a few minutes later and he is struggling even more to do the second chin up. Once he does it the supporting piece of wood breaks and Overweighter crashes to the ground. The camera cuts to Him and Candy Cane at a beach. They begin to race each other down the beach. Overweighter is running as fast as he can. He is winning until Candy Cane Suddenly sprints even fast and wins. They keep on racing and Candy Cane always wins.) Candy Cane: Come on Overweighter you can do it! Run faster!!! (The camera cuts to inside a butcher's freezer there is a bit part of a cow hanging from the ceiling. Candy Cane is standing behind the cow. He is holding it so Overweighter can bunch it. Overweighter goes to punch it and every time he does he takes a bite out of it, without Candy Cane seeing it. The camera cuts to Overweighter on his own in a street jogging down the road. Some kids playing Basket ball see Overweighter running and they decide to run with him. He runs past a bar and all the drunks see him and also decide to follow him. Suddenly loads more people start to follow him. The camera cut to in front of him, he is running towards it. He runs past and suddenly all the people follow him. The camera changes again and it's a long shot from the air down onto Overweighter and the crowd following him. Suddenly Overweighter begins to sprint down the road and the crowd can't catch up. Overweighter gets to loads of steps and he begins to run up them as fast as he can. Once he gets to the top he turns around and puts his arms in the air.) The Overweighter: YEAH, I DID IT! (The background slowly fades away with the image of The Overweighter raising his arms fading away even slower.) Find out if The Overweighter can win the race and get to keep his money? And find out if Overweighter gets to beat his brother Candy Cane at a race. Find out in The Race Part 3!
