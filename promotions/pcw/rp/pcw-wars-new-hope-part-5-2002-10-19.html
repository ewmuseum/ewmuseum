PCW Wars: New Hope Part 5

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 - 10 - 02 Our opening scene for this chapter is within the confines of the Death Stars control room where the Empires top brass have congregated, namely Darth Pharaoh and Grand Moff Tarkannon, both individuals feeling increasingly bitter towards the deceptive Princess Anarchy. Darth Pharaoh: 'Her resistance to the mind probe is considerable. May I suggest that we cut our losses and destroy those neighbouring planets, it'll be a case of eventually hitting the right one!' An Imperial Officer interrupts the conversation mid-way. Imperial Officer: 'The final checks are complete and all systems operational. If this is target practise can we at-least blow up some insignificant planet like Earth first, please, please I'll be your best friend....' Tarkannon: 'SILENCE PEON~! I think it is time to demonstrate the full power of this station, set a course for Princess Anarchy's home planet of Alderaan' Imperial Officer: 'Which ones that?' Tarkannon: 'Uh... Arr... Don't we have satellite navigation aboard?' Returning to our hero's we find Alexander Skywalker concluding the sale of his landspeeder with a grotesque insect like creature that hands the young Jedi apprentice a number of coins. A. Skywalker: 'He says it’s the best he can do. He even used the XP-38 as an excuse, personally I find him a tight fisted bast-....' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Profanity leads to the dark side Alex, remember that. That money will be enough!' Charlie Loc and his faithful side kick Alex Skywalker leave the speeder lot and walk down a dusty alleyway passing a bunch of strange creature, followed closely by the R2 unit and his sobering droid colleague Rush-3PO. Alex peers back with a forlorn look aimed at his former asset, the landspeeder and vanishes around a murky corner, a corner inhabited by someone watching from the shadows. C. Loc Kenobi: 'If this ships as fast as he's boasting, well we can wave good-bye to our secondary plan!' A. Skywalker: 'Secondary plan? Oh, you mean that Captain Kirk and his Enterprise ship....' The scene suddenly enters that of docking bay 94. A vast slug-like creature known as Pizza The Hut shows-up with a hoard of purple-faced henchmen who swamp the docking bay on the orders of their slavering hulk of a boss. Pizza's face is scarred and grim, a true testimonial of his prowess as a vicious killer. Pizza: 'Stop playing Hands Solo!' A voice behind them startles the pirates who turn to their assailants, in this case Gaz Solo and the giant Wookie Cherezza, no weapons they look defeated. Gaz Solo: 'Its Gaz now... Stop with the masturbating jokes already. I'm a smuggler, I have women on tap, why would I need to masturbate, huh?!?' Pizza: 'Women on tap. So why is that every time we meet you’re almost joined at the hip to this Wookie?' Gaz Solo: 'Uh... Err-...Arr-... Well-... Never mind, this is about the money I owe you?' Pizza: 'Gaz, my boy, there are times when you disappoint me... So why haven't you paid me? And why did you fry poor Greedo, if you were going to escape then why didn't you just vendor him unconscious with that hand-buzzer of yours... After all the things we've been through together!' Gaz Solo: 'You sent Greedo to blast me!' Pizza: 'Hands... I mean Gaz; you're the best smuggler in the business. You’re to valuable to fry, boil or toast and would be difficult to eat without a glass of red wine at the tableside, I would need something to wash the bad taste from my mouth. As for Greedo he was merely relaying my concern at your delay, he wouldn't dare blast you silly!' Gaz Solo: 'I think he was, Pizza in future don't go sending any of your twerps comprendes?' Pizza: 'Gaz, Gaz! If you only hadn't dumped that shipment of spice... You understand I can't go making exceptions. Where would I be if every pilot who smuggles for me dumped their cargo at the first sign of an Imperial starship? It's not good business' Gaz Solo: 'You know, even I get boarded sometimes. I had no choice but I have a charter now and can pay you back in full, plus a little extra, how does some T.L.C from the Wookie sound?' Cherezza backs off as Pizza The Hut glares with puppy-dog eyes, almost love struck. Pizza: 'Put your blasters away. Gaz my boy, I'm only doing this because you’re the best and I need you. So for an extra twenty percent I'll forget the Wookie offer and keep the deal strictly cash, allowing you some more time to compensate me... Remember though, disappoint me again and I shall place a large bounty over your head, a sum so big that it will ensure you don't step foot on another star system again!' Gaz Solo: 'Pizza I'll pay you because it'll be my pleasure... You slug!' Pizza: 'What did you say infidel?' Gaz Solo: 'Err-... It'll be my pleasure, how about a hug!!!' The human Gaz and the grotesque being Pizza embrace. Gaz however finding it difficult to get his short arms around the mass of flesh of Pizza The Hut, his face subtly smiling but deep down this moment will have cemented the worst day in his miniscule life. The party finally leaves as Gaz and his Wookie counterpart continue the prep work of the voyage on the craft known as the Millennium Eagle aka Roland. The ship itself is a large, round, beat-up, pieced together hunk of junk that could loosely be called a starship. The scene reverted back to the human and droid collective as they near dock 94, passing an ominous creature who speaks into a transmitter with a hiss, his voice disguised by another language, those eyes however seem awfully familiar, possibly belonging to that of the hidden creature that occupied the passing corner minutes earlier. A. Skywalker: 'WHAT A PIECE OF JUNK~!' The tall figure of Gaz Solo comes down from the boarding ramp. Gaz Solo: 'She'll go point five passed the speed of light, a little faster than that landspeeder huh, kid? She may not look like much but she has it where it counts and I've added a few special modifications of my own...' Alex scratches his head. Its obvious that he is unsure about this escapade but it's too late as the rest board under Cherezza's supervision. Gaz Solo: 'We're a little rushed, so if you'll hurry up and board we can get this show on the road!' The team board, watched closely by a grinning Gaz Solo who basks in the knowledge that his passengers are suckers for accepting his extortionate price, which inadvertently settles the debt between the smuggler and its collector Pizza The Hut. Cherezza entered the cockpit, strapping himself in he fires up the roaring engines pursued by the ships cargo of Charlie Loc Kenobi, Alexander Skywalker and the two bumbling droids. Rush-3PO: 'Get your furry ass in gear dude - I'm almost sober and would really like to find the next water hole, biatch!' The scene changes to a shot outside as eight Stormbringers race to the docking bay numbered ninety-four. The creature with the transmitter points directions as the Imperial Troopers pass-by and head through the gaping entrance. Trooper: 'Stop, stop, that ship is not ready yet....' Gaz looks up and sees the Stormbringers rushing into the dock, armed with blasters, some of which open fire causing the captain to fire some wayward shots of his own. Trooper: 'I mean where's this guys license and why's there a Wookie sitting in the pilots seat... Blast 'em!' Gaz Solo: 'Chewy get us out of here!' The ship rises from his motionless position, still under-fire from the Imperial foot soldiers below, but with a thunderous roar the craft vanishes into the morning sky leaving behind the slum dwelling it once occupied. Returning to the interior of the vessel Gaz straps himself in next to Cherezza who points to something on the radarscope. Lurking in the wings is two behemoth Corellian ships that almost bathe Tatooine in shadow causing Gaz to frantically type information into the ships computer, a series of events unbeknownst to the Rebel force sitting outback. Gaz Solo: 'HOLY SHIT! Looks like an Imperial cruiser. Our guests must be hotter than I first thought. Try and hold them off, angling the deflector shields whilst I prepare for the jump to light speed' The Millennium Eagle pirate ship races away from the yellow planet of Tatooine, followed by the Imperial stardestroyers as the scene is forced back into the cockpit of their prey, namely the ship branded Roland. The curious Alex and his mentor Charlie Loc peer over the shoulders of the crafts navigation team as the events unfold. Gaz Solo: 'Stay sharp! There are another two trying to cut us off....' A. Skywalker: 'Outrun them? I thought this ship was fast' Gaz Solo: 'Watch your mouth kid, or you'll find yourself floating home. Trust me we'll lose them once we make the jump into hyperspace, besides being a pro I know a few maneuvers of my own, one I call the Darkerside Of Comedy!' The ship shudders as a flash explodes outside the main window. Gaz Solo: 'Here's where the fuc-... I mean this is the fun part!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'How long before we can make the jump into hyperspace cause at the moment we're looking death in the face?' Gaz Solo: 'Huh - What are you some Jedi Knight? Give the navigation computer some time to pull out the coordinates, sheesh, anyone would swear you could see the future!' The ship jerks violently once more as another laser hits. Rush-3PO: 'R2 kiss your metallic blue ass good-bye....' A. Skywalker: 'Are you kidding. At this rate we'll be space debris in no time....' Gaz Solo: 'Traveling through hyperspace isn't like shagging sheep, sorry, I mean it isn't like dusting crops, boy! Without the right calculations we could fly straight into a star or bounce off a super nova that'd end the trip real quick, wouldn't it?' The ship is now constantly under laser fire, taking a real battering, which inadvertently sets a red warning light off. A. Skywalker: 'What does that mean wrist jerker?!?' Gaz looks up with evil intent. Grinding his teeth at yet another masturbating joke Gaz opens his mouth to respond but Alexander cuts him short with a half-hearted apology. A. Skywalker: 'Ok... We're even now!' Gaz Solo: 'We're losing deflector shield so be a good boy and strap your self in. I'm going to make the jump to light speed, that'll cheer you up!' Suddenly the Millennium Eagle zooms into infinity, taking literally a second. On the crafts disappearance we are taken back to the Death Star battle station that looms over the planet Alderaan. Entering the control room is Mr. Tatooine who we last seen having a small disagreement with Darth Pharaoh over his obsession with regards to the force. The Admiral bows before the higher-ranking officer of Tarkannon. Mr. Tatooine: 'We've entered the Alderaan system!' Pharaoh and two Stormbringers enter, accompanied by the reluctant Princess, her hands still bound together. Anarchy: 'Tarkannon I should've known it be you holding Pharaoh's leash. I recognised your egotistical, self-centred, manipulative awe when I came aboard' Tarkannon: 'Charming to the last. You wouldn't know how hard I found it signing the order to terminate....' Anarchy: 'MY LIFE?!?' Tarkannon: 'No nothing as heinous as that. We're going to demonstrate the full power of this battle station on a planet so insignificant, so miniscule, so pointless, so unnoticed that the people who reside there would rejoice if only they'd survive!' Anarchy: 'Well get on with it - I'm not going to lose sleep over the destruction of Earth!' Tarkannon: 'Earth? Not quite but Alderaan would make a suitable replacement....' Anarchy: 'NO! Alderaan is peaceful. We have no weapons. You can't possibly....' Tarkannon: 'You would prefer another target? A military target? Then name the system or better yet name the planet hiding your Rebel base!' Tarkannon waves a menacing clenched fist towards the face of Princess Anarchy. Princess Anarchy can only reply softly as she grudgingly hands over the information, trying desperately to save countless lives. Anarchy: 'Dantooine. They're on Dantooine!' Tarkannon: 'There. Lord Pharaoh, she can be reasonable, even if she is a Celestial bitch. The Peon has pissed me off for long enough continue with the operation and fire when ready... Anarchy: 'WHAT?' Tarkannon: 'Sucker! Dantooine is such a peon of a planet, so small and worthless, I may as well destroy Alderaan first and then head for Dantooine, crushing the Rebel Alliance in one foul swoop!!!' Darth Pharaoh: 'Commence primary ignition....' As the party watch the final moments of Alderaans existence Anarchy sheds a tear. Suddenly a huge beam emanates from within a cone-like structure and converges into a single laser beam that hurtles towards Alderaan, wiping out the green planet instantly. The scene switches back to the cockpit of the Millennium Eagle then stretches outback as Alexander wields his lightsabre against that of a small hovering droid who acts as a sparring partner before closing again, ending yet another chapter in this on-going saga.
