Mythical Horror

undated

Date: not entered Asag, Gallu and Namtar, the three demonic beings that now share a part of El Diablo's soul. But, they are only a small part of the Babylonian history and mythology that El Diablo so openly believes in, but what else is there for him to discover and follow in this mythology? He once mentioned his beliefs were in the seven clay tablets. But what are these tablets and why would El Diablo live his lifestyle to conform to these tablets? The seven clay tablets of ancient Babylonia are precious, so precious that El Diablo does not even possess them as a belonging of his. So, all El Diablo has to go by is the translated version in books, which could, in fact be translated incorrectly. So why would The Mexican Devil put all of his trust into something that is not 100% genuine. What these tablets are, is highly unknown and what is told on them is also unknown, but El Diablo seems destined to discover all he can about them to live the life they describe on them. Some historians say that the tablets describe many of the mythical tales about ancient Babylonia. Others claim that it describes a way of life to live, whichever it is, El Diablo has a burning interest within him. With only days to go before the epic March Madness battle royal, El Diablo will want to discover this Babylonian truth and use it to advantage in the match up. He already has somewhat of a slight advantage anyway with entering at number eleven, but that is still a long way to go. Again, El Diablo fights for the PCW Title. Again, El Diablo will no doubt lose, but this time he has a much larger force behind him and within him, that can surely give this Mexican Devil a good showing at his first PPV for three months. With the powers and demonic feelings of Asag, Gallu and Namtar within El Diablo it will be like trying to stop King Kong destroying cities. Sure, El Diablo is a lot smaller, but the sadism is still there, deep within the power hungry El Diablo. Still, the Mexican Devil remains reading up on Babylonian history from his huge book on it all. Yet again he remains in that same leather chair rather than a change of scenery for any of this, and Angel is getting anxious to leave. Angel begins to pace up and down the room wearing her short skirt and tight top. But instead of noticing the obvious, El Diablo remains enthralled in his book. It almost appears, as El Diablo loves that book and Babylonian history and mythology more than his own wife. Sure, it's possible, but why would it come to that? 'I want to get out,' nags Angel. El Diablo peers over his book, as his eyes flash a nasty red color like the demons within him are in control, a look of evil consumes his face before he looks back down at the book in front of him. He doesn't even need to speak for Angel to feel some sort of fear in his presence. If anyone was watching them now, they might be thinking that Angel is a prisoner and Diablo is the prison guard. Of course it's not that, but it is an eerily accurate comparison. 'I have to get out,' she pleads with her husband. He just peers over the book again, only no evil red eyes show. The look could just be part of Angel's imagination. 'Can't you wait?' he asks in a tone of annoyance. Angel looks very irritated and looks at the gold watch on her left hand, in frustration. 'Fine!' she yells at him, as she goes and sits back down on the sofa. She acts almost like a child, as she sulks in the chair and pouts her lip. 'Stop that,' responds El Diablo to her child-like ways. For once he appears to be taking notice of what is going on around him, which kind of stuns Angel a little. 'You haven't let me leave the house in near two days,' she exclaims with passion. El Diablo lowers his book for a second. 'I am in deep preparation for my match on Sunday,' he roars at her, 'I need to be at a good mental state going into the match and this is what I do to prepare now,' he finishes, almost yelling at his beautiful wife. She stares across at him with a look of anger in his eyes. 'So, why must I be here?' she asks inquisitively. The question seems to spark an anger in El Diablo, which has never been seen before. 'Because you are my wife and you should and WILL stand by me,' he shouts at her. She locks eyes with him and has a look of both fear and anger on her face, but chooses not to speak. 'I have discovered the first tablet,' he states with a lot of pride, 'It is like a long, long poem, that I may have to share one day,' he finishes with a sinister smile on his face. Angel stands up once more. 'Can we go now?' she asks with impatience written all over her. He keeps his eyes locked on the book and the description of the first tablet, purposely ignoring his wife. 'When in the height heaven was not named, and the earth beneath did not yet bear a name,' he quotes from the tablet with power and passion. The tablets seem to tell the tale of all Babylonian existence and that is just what El Diablo wants. El Diablo wants all the power from their existence within him, to appease the demons within him, and these tablets that he reads seem to be giving him all the positive power he wants and requires. 'Soon, I will be prepared and these heathens of my soul will carry me to all success, I am the true darkness,' he comments to his wife. His eyes light up that evil red color as Angel stands watching in fear. El Diablo doesn't move, however and just seems to stand in some sort of solitude. Angel is bewildered. El Diablo lets out a large roar and some sort of aura spawns from his mouth as he tilts his head back and his eyes remain that blood red color. Suddenly he drops back down into the chair, passed out with his eyes wide open and with that same blood red color. Angel just watches on in horror.
