Michael 2.0 (Going for Poser based Irony)

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd Febuary 2002 The darkened room once again as we return to. We return to. We return to. Don’t worry, I haven’t got repetitive typing injury, just I was going for a more experimental cinema type of intro there. Like Run Lola Run where they keep repeating stuff over and over and over. The darkened room once again as we return to. We return to. We return to. Don’t worry, I haven’t got repetitive typing injury, just I was going for a more experimental cinema type of intro there. Like Run Lola Run where they keep repeating stuff over and over and over. However that was closer to annoying than stylish, wasn’t it? Well where was we... we return to the darkened room where now sits Miya wearing his all black (and non New Zealand) clothing with the black bandages wrapped around his head. Sitting on the black table in front of his body sitting on the black chair is a black telephone. It rings... blackly. Miyagawa snaps forward and grabs the handset and holds it to his ear, it being one of those older ones that still actually have a cord. You hear a voice on the other end of the line but it’s not too clear. Miyagawa: So it’s on then... **waits a few seconds before talking again** Ok, cool, I’ll be there in ten. The camera cuts now to a bright sunny day outside a picturesque white washed house with a little white picket fence, which does bring back memories of a previous promo where it was suggested that Miyagawa had sex with a seventy year old... **writer cringes** Anyway, we continue beyond that. A BMW Z3 in British Racing Green Metallic pulls up in the small road beyond the picket fence and stops. Miyagawa opens the door and steps out; the only thing having being changed is that his bandages are now white. He looks at the house with interest as he slams the door shut. He walks up the driveway, the gravel crunching underfoot as he does so. He hops up onto the front porch, the white washed wood underneath taking his weight like it has so done so for so many others in its lifetime. He raps his knuckles on the white washed door three times... before noticing the bell and then pushing the button for that previously mentioned doorbell. It rings (just in case logic hadn’t kicked in and made you realise that previously). He waits a few seconds there; in fact it takes it up by attempting to scratch his head, through all the bandages and all. Finally sounds of movement are heard inside and a presence is on the other side of the door. The door opens and the camera pans up over the jeans, the checked workman’s shirt, the white feather wings and up onto the face... the face... of John Travolta. Miyagawa: How much do I owe Xavier for this then? Travolta: He said if you lie down and let him pass in the GTT, you guys would be square. Miyagawa: He can go to hell then. I’ll get him a part in one of JJ’s movies or something... or I’ll get JJ to film the Adventures of Alice in Scientology or something like that. It’ll get the kids involved. Travolta: Didn’t you know that according to scientology, Angels don’t exist. Miyagawa: Actually they do, but only if you can get paid several million dollars to play a fallen one in a movie. It’s nice that they let you keep the wings anyway. Travolta: Where are my manners, come on in... You are Miyagawa; right... he said you’d be wearing bandages on your face, so I presume that it’s you. Miyagawa: Don’t worry, this is Miyagawa under all these wrappings... and for those smarks out there, these are pre-recorded prior to when I go back under the knife to get my face reconstructed. Just don’t tell Clyde that before Turmoil, or else the game will truly be afoot. Michael... erm... John Travolta I mean shuffles aside, seeing as with those massive wing props on his back, shuffling is about as fast as he can move properly. No pun there intended... props... properly... bah, crappy audience. Miyagawa wanders into the still rather picturesque little house, with sowings on the walls in frames and stuff like that. A brown upholstered couch lies on top of a multi coloured rug opposite a small television in the main room... which incidentally is where Miyagawa is ushered to by John Travolta. Miyagawa sits down on the couch, and John Travolta, large prop wings and all sits himself down next to him. Travolta reaches forward and grabs the remote control and turns the television on... to the Olympics of course. Travolta grabs a can of beer from beside the couch and passes it over to Miyagawa, who promptly opens it, before Travolta does the same with a second can. John sits back, as best he can with those damned wings strapped to his back. Miyagawa: So how many of these little skits have you done for Xavier on Drake Maxwell before? Because I’m sure he’s realises the whole Michael-Fallen Angel-Scientology connection going on there. I did, and I only thought of the idea six hours before coming here. Travolta: Will telling you the truth affect how many cans of beer you pay me in? Miyagawa: No, that number is already fixed at forty one. Travolta: Forty One? Miyagawa: Symbolic. It’s the number of days I need to get to with my Television Title reign in order to break the current record... although personally, between us, I’m actually aiming for fifty... it’s a much more rounded number, and would be a record for any belt as far as I am aware... although I’m going to check that later. Travolta: Well in that case, sure, Xavier has film numerous skits involving me insulting Maxwell in various different forms. The time he got me to fall off the roof was quite painful however, and I completely and utterly refuse to do that again. Miyagawa: Ok, one question then, and it’s a pretty damned important one too. Travolta: Shoot. Miyagawa: Can you get me a part in the Pulp Fiction Sequel? Travolta: Come again? Miyagawa: I just came all this way to find out if I could be in the Pulp Fiction Sequel. I already know that Maxwell is scientifically a dog turd by his own admission, and that he could bore the hind legs off a donkey if he needed to. I know a hundred percent that Drake is a type of Duck and Maxwell is a type of coffee so in reality he’s actually a turd from a dog that just ate dinner and coffee at a Chinese restaurant. I’m good with detective work like that. Besides which, the last time we faced each other mono et mono, I absolutly gave him a whooping. The least that could happen is that I get booked against someone actually in the Television Title rankings, no oh no, too simple. Travolta: So there was no reason for me to wear these damned wings? Miyagawa: Nope... I just wanted to see how much you looked like Zurick’s tag team partner in them. Sorry to waste your time. So do you reckon I could get a part...? I’ll even play someone that gets killed, in fact that might be better. Travolta: Erm... I don’t think so. With those bandages, I would say that you have a face for radio, but on the other hand if they do a really good job with a scalpel, then by all means come and see me again. Miyagawa: Darn... I guess I’ll just have to stick with my starring roll as one of the Gondor soldiers in the Two Towers then. Did you know that they’re thinking of renaming that because of the World Trader centre thing? Michael... err... John Travolta leaps to his feet as he hears the mention of the Two Towers. Travolta: How did you get a part in the Two Towers...? I stalked Peter Jackson for ten months and he kept pushing me away until he finally got a restraining order on me. Miyagawa: Restraining orders are just another way of saying I love you... or so my good friend Steve Jabowski once said. Travolta: I hate you, get out. No one who gets a part I want will ever be welcome here again! Miyagawa: Get over it, you don’t even live here, this is a set at Walt Disney studio’s tour on one of its sound stages. In fact, if you go outside now you can hear the cries of extremely bored tour guides slitting their own risks as they have to act surprised at that Jaws attack for the tenth time today. Travolta: Bah. Miyagawa: Humbug. And with that, the camera ironically fades to green. What? You never seen a camera fade to green before? It just must be Smallville “Oh my god, land full of mutated bastards” o-vision. Grow and prosper with it. Oh and to avoid complaints I would like to add this soundbyte from a promo on a PCW card from several weeks ago. Miyagawa: Because... I'm... Excellent. Sorry about that but the national board of wrestling promotions were getting complaints because that wasn't said in the last Miyagawa promo. We will endevour to make sure it doesn't happen again.
