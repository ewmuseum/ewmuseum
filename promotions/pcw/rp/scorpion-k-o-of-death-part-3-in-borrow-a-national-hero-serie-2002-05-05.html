Scorpion K.O. Of Death (part 3 in BorroW-A-National Hero series)

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/5/02 Tales of the Golden Toe: The Golden Heart as passed and the Golden Toe has come back again. The Golden Toe is the old Juan Borro, the fun loving foreign soccer player who did his all to help others. Through the tales of the Golden Toe Juan gives us insight into present experiences with his life and duties and encounters with people. The Golden Toe has seen his calling, and that calling is to do good be good, and be a power for good. No force will stop Juan’s GOAL~! Time: 1: 20 P.M.Location: Lisbon, PortugalScene: SKO Stadium “Football will NEVER be the same” Part 3 Last time we saw our national hero BorroW-A-National Hero, he fought the evil of Zinedine Zidane and the Smelly Frenchies. Now him and the rest of team Portugal are ready to defeat them once again, but this time IN THE CAGE! The scene opens with Cantona driving Zinedine Zidane along with Juan Borro, Pinto, and Figo who make up part of Team Portugal. Cantona promised the men a new and exotic form of soccer, one that neither man could possibly be ready for. Both are skeptical, but they know that in the end there will be one winner. When they arrive at the Scorpion K.O. Arena, it looks like it’s just one large abandoned ship. To Juan it looks nothing like what he saw yesterday. Juan Borro: What is this? I saw something completely different yesterday. What exactly are you doing? Cantona: Don’t be so eager young Juan Borro, just wait. Once we get inside you’ll see everything. Juan Borro: I better. All of the men make their way into the ship. Zidane looks around and strokes his finger against the door of the boat’s cabin. He attracts a large amount of dirt and dust on his finger, almost as if no one has stepped foot in this place for years. Zidane: This place can use a paint job. Cantona quickly opens the door, and barrages of insects come flying out the door. Juan ducks along with the other men. They all give each other this weird look, but Cantona urges them to step in. As the men explore the inside of the warehouse, Figo pulls Juan over to a corner for a second. Figo: Something fishy is going on here Juan. I don’t know what it is, but it doesn’t seem good. Why would Cantona want us to play here? I mean what kind of satisfaction does he get out of it. Juan: I don’t know, but I just want to play some soccer. There is nothing to be afraid of Figo, Cantona is weak and retired I doubt he’s going to turn out evil and try and kill all of us. Figo: You never know. Juan and Figo join the pack again. Cantona looks over his shoulder to make sure everyone is there. He then smiles and escorts them to this small room. When in the room, the men have little space and it’s hard to breath in there. Pinto: What the hells are we doing here I can barely breathe? Zidane: All I see is a bunch of cobwebs and dust. Let’s get out of here. Zidane tries to exit, but Cantona stops him. When Zidane turns around Cantona is pointing at another small door. A door that Zidane would a sworn wasn’t there a few minutes ago. Zidane: How did that get here? Juan: I don’t care I just want to get out of this little room. This place is like a friggin maze that doesn’t end. When Cantona opens the door there seems to be a long slide connected to it. Cantona then lets out his hand inviting Juan to slide down. Juan: I’m not sliding down that thing. I don’t know what could be on the other side. It could be an endless hole all I know. Cantona paces around laughing. He then puts his hand on Zidane’s shoulder and makes a sleigh comment about Juan. Cantona: No wonder why Portugal didn’t what him anymore. Juan then approaches Cantona furiously and grabs him by the neck, shaking him back in forth. He doesn’t stop until Pinto comes over to hold him back. Juan: I left the national team on my own accord you ass hole. What would you know? I should have known this whole thing was a scam when you had something to do with it. Cantona: Don’ take offense Juan, I’m just stating how you see to be AFRAID to slide down a little baby slide. Juan: If it’s such an innocent slide then why don’t you be a man and go down first. I mean if it’s as safe as you say you’d slide down. Cantona: Fine. Cantona waives goodbye as he sits down and slides down the long slide. Juan goes over and checks if he can see him, but all he sees is pitch black. Juan thinks to himself for a few moments, but then takes the plunge himself down the slide. Once the other guys we Juan slide down, they go down themselves. On the way down the long slide becomes quicker and quicker. They can all see absolutely nothing and all they hear is the loud laugh of Cantona. A whole minute later Juan notices a bright light. When he reaches that bright light, he falls a good twenty feet straight to the hard ground. About ten minutes later Juan Borro, Figo, Pinto, and Zidane all awake from the hard fall. The scene is blurry but when it gets straightened out they see all scorpions around them. Hundreds of scorpions surround them looking like their ready to eat. Juan: What the hell is this? Cantona then appears outside the cage. We smiles and twiddles his fingers at the expense of the four other men. Cantona: That my friends is Scorpion K.O. Juan: Get us out of here. We’ll die in here. The scorpions start attacking, jumping on all of the men as they begin screaming. Within seconds the scorpions are covering Juan’s entire body. Juan: HELP!!!! Juan can only see out of one eye now, as he’s ready to take his last gasp of a greatly lived life. Juan takes a deep breath, but when he exhales all of the scorpions are gone. Him along with the other men in the cage look around in wonder about what just happened. Figo: What was that? Cantona: That my friends was all your own imaginations. Juan: That’s a loud of shit. All of that was real. There is no way that all those scorpions were in our heads. I want out of this cage. And I want out now! Cantona: None of you will be let out until you compete. Zidane: No, we refuse to compete. Cantona: Oh I doubt you want to do that. You see the losing man has to suffer extreme consequences. Juan: Like what? Suddenly, Cantona stirs up a large Scorpion, bigger than the whole arena. The scorpion has fire in his eyes, and a loud roar within him. Pinto: Holy Shit! Cantona: My friend over there is hungry. And the loser will be his meal. On the count of three a ball will fall from the top of the cage and the match will begin. First goal wins. Oh and as for your buddies Figo and Pinto, Juan. They won’t be helping you. Cantona snaps his fingers and both men suddenly cant move their feet almost as if they’re stuck. Cantona. 1……………………………………2………………………………………………… Juan and Zidane take one last look at each other as the word three comes out of Cantona’s mouth and the ball falls from the top of the cage. Before the ball hits the ground both men make a make rush for it. Juan knocks it down with his shoulder and then cradles it on his left foot pushing back with it. Zidane makes a swift swipe at the ball as he pops It up in the air. Zidane leaps to gain possession then tries to out do Juan with fancy footwork. Zidane finally makes his move to the right and starts a dead sprint, but before he can get anywhere Juan steps on the ball enabling Zidane to score. Next, Juan sees an open goal. He winds up and takes the blast but before he connects with the ball Zidane comes sliding under his legs intercepting the ball straight from his path. Zidane and Juan are now on opposite sides and are staring each other down. Zidane licks his finger and puts it in the air, as he steps back and lets out a blast. The ball is traveling a good 100 mph right at Juan. The ball looks like it’s going to go between Juan’s leg, but Juan closes the in the nick of time stopping the ball right there. Zidane becomes angry and charges for the ball. Juan sees how quick he’s moving and hops up jumping over Zidane’s slide with the ball still on his feet. Juan charges down the cage with a clear goal. He shoots dead on and……………..NO! Something happened that didn’t let Juan score. Juan looks over at Cantona who is laughing. The out of nowhere Zidane grabs the ball and takes a rocket of a shot scoring on the opposing goal. Cantona: GOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLL~!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Zidane wins. Juan: That wasn’t fair. Cantona: Too late. Zidane disappears and the large scrpion comes out ready to eat Juan. Juan tries to run away but he can’t leave the cage. Pinto and Figo are still motionless as well. Right when everything looks like it’s going wrong, El Romantico appears. El Romantico appears out side of the cage and kicks Cantona through the cage on the inside. The then quick jumps in pulling Juan, Pinto, and Figo all out in the nick of time. They then wait and look as Cantona is left alone in the cage with the scorpion. With one quick motion the scorpion entirely eats Cantona. When the men are finished watching, they turn around and El Romantico is no longer there. Juan looks around but all he sees is the same white rose. SCENE FADES TO BLACK. The Golden Heart Tales are not to be written or sold by anyone but Golden Heart Tales Production. Copyright 2002.
