The Painful Past (Chapter 1)

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/4/02 Lust. Passion. Emotion. Wishing I had them, wishing I could feel them. But I can’t, and there is nothing I can do about that. You see I’m not a stupid man. I know the facts of life, I know that sometimes everything isn’t what it seems to be, but I didn’t want to believe that. I was so young…and I was so stupid. I remember it like it was yesterday. The past is sometimes a shade of gray; Jesus Christ I don’t even know my mother’s maiden name. But there is one thing I know. I know how hard it was for me. If I could have done it all again…no I would have made the same mistakes. He’s such a freak. My mother says that he’s a bastard and that we should stay away from him. Who would go near him anyway, he smells like a dead rat. *The girls giggle together in the corner and go back to jump roping* Oh man, my mom gave me this nasty sandwich, I don’t want to eat it. Then go throw it at the freak show he eats anything. Freak boy come out come out wherever you are. I peeked out and God I ran after that half eaten anchovies sandwich like it was the last meal I’d ever eat, but then again I never knew what would be my last meal those days. You see back in those days Barcelona was a shadow of what it is now. I would tell you the year, but I don’t even remember what it was. For me school was shelter. There were 5,000 children in the elementary school I attended and to them I was just a name. I went by the name Manuel and I barely ever attended classes due to the ridicule of classmates. I lived in the cellar. The janitor felt bad for me and gave me a spare set of keys in and out of the room. For me it was the cold nights that would stick out in my head. Miguel…. Miguel are you here? I’m in the back. Miguel I received my check from the school today and to celebrate I bought some salmon for us to share. Thank You. Migeul, tell me about your past. Why have you no home? I sometimes wonder if you’ll be able to make it in this world. As do I, as do I. At that time I was still at a young age, I don’t remember the exact age but it was young. All I remember was that three years later I was a reformation came across the school systems in Barcelona. The well to do children that were put in high class institutes, the smart in high learning schools, and the rest in public districts. Then there were the rest of us, the poor. Miguel De Saviour the minister of Barcelona set up a gracious housing system and school for us, or at least HE thought is was gracious. We called it the “El Infierno en la Tierra” because for us, hell was the only word that could describe such a place. SIT DOWN! Don’t make me say it again. I’m sorry, Signorina Morales. Sorry is not good enough, get up here. I’m sorry I will not do it again. GET UP HERE! Now get on your knees and extend your hands. Signorina Morales was what she made us call her, but we all thought she was a witch. Her punishments were beyond human. We’d stay out in the snow bare naked with no food or anything. Sometimes we’d be whipped, she’s throw us in burning hot showers. Anything you think of as incredibly inhumane she did to us. We tried to fight back, but it never worked. Even though this place was a hell, we needed it, because we had nowhere better to go. Then it happened, one day when I must have been around eleven or twelve years of age, my life changed. It’s almost like I woke up one morning and I was a completely new person. I looked different felt different and I held so much power. Only three months later I had completed my studies at El Infierno en la Tierra and I was back on my own. It was hard in the beginning to go back to that unsheltered world. But then I was given a signal. I frequently attended a poets café, I would make a few rhymes and it would pay my way day through day. I was the youngest one there, but they treated me like gold. I was a regular there and everyone knew me. It was one day when I was getting ready to leave that it all changed. Great Job Miguel your writing this week really touched me, I’ll see you same time next week. I’ll be here. I was walking home that cold winter night when I saw her outside. My little Angel! Huh? I turned around and saw a woman in rags with tears rolling from her eyes. Do you need any help? Don’t you know who I am? No I’m sorry but I’ve never seen you before. But you have, I’m you mother. What? And then it came to me, her eyes. They stuck out in my mind like a gunshot. For the first time I remembered the past. I’d have these long dreams of emerald green eyes with an orange tint and then I saw them in her. This was really my mother, and I didn’t know a thing to say. I had enough money now and I was renting this place from a woman with six children. It was small, but it was something. I brought my mother there and I tried to speak o her about my past but it came to no prevail. It was like something was wrong with her, she stuttered her words and didn’t remember anything. I finally had the chance to find out about my past and now it was just slipping away. I knew she was on some type of drugs, but I just wished she could have told me something. All she kept doing was sing this song to me. She repeated the words “My blue eyed boy, so strong, so grown.” I treated her well the next two days and even though she was hard to live with It meant a lot to me to know what she signified. I wish I could have had more feelings toward her, but to me she was just a woman. I did realize a mother was a big deal, but my heart just didn’t let it sink in. That morning I woke up to screams in the front of my small room. I ran out and just as I got there I saw a man pull a knife on my naked and abused mother. He slashed her right across the throat and did it with this sick smile on his face. The closest thing I ever felt to emotions came to me and I used it without thinking at all. I ran at the man and speared him to the ground. He tried to stab me, but I dodged and elbowed him in the chest. I then shoved my foot in his groin and punched him countless time until my fists were filled with blood. His friend then came charging from a car and hit me blindsided. He kicked me a few time before I threw him down and bent his knee back breaking it like a twig I then broke the other and had him on the ground screaming for mercy. I just wasn’t thinking as I planted both of their heads on the curb making them bite down on it. I slammed down on both of their heads busting their jaws so badly it hit their own brains. I felt satisfaction, a satisfaction I wanted to feel again. TO BE CONTINUED…………………
