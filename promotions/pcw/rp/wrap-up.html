Date: not entered FWOOM! The body of Vic Kevlar, longtime sparring partner for Big Alan Steele, plummets to the Earth on the down side of a huge, arching back body drop. The impact shakes the ring to its very foundations, while forcing all the air from Kevlar’s lungs with a rush. He stares unseeing and gape-mouthed at the ceiling, while a familiar voice chuckles over him. “Come now, Lord Kevlar! Surely you do not expect such a paltry defense scheme to foil one as mighty as I, Big Alan Steele!! Hi-Keeba!!” We now rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he chuckles at his friend, desperately trying to suck wind. Beneath the luxurious exterior and plush confines of Stately Steele Manor lies a place known only as the Cellar, where only the crudest implements of aerobic exercise and strength training are allowed, a veritable inferno where Steele comes to be forged by the burning-intensity provided by trusted manager, Sue Saksnussum, off to the side, a cup of Starbucks in her hand and brandishing a well-worn whip. A few of the Graces mill about, ready to attend to some of the lesser duties involved in the training sessions, such as towels, water bottles, and mops for Kevlar when necessary. Sue scowls. “Al, you’ve already wasted 8 seconds!! Get moving before your heart rate drops below your target!” Editors Note: “Wait a minute?! What’s going on here?!” Big Al salutes his dark overlord before scooping up the noodle-like form of Vic Kevlar. “Nothing personal, my good friend… but I must prepare thoroughly!! Though New Breed expects to send me forth unto new worlds of pain and retribution, I shall make their victory quite costly indeed…” “Understood,” groans Kevlar. Big Alan Steele grunts slightly as he hoists Kevlar into the air… Editor’s Note: “STOP! HOLD UP EVERYTHING!!” Cut to a conference room, where two writers poke around on a desktop computer, while the editor paces up and down the room. The place is an absolute mess, with pizza boxes strewn about, crumpled up papers tossed about haphazardly, seemingly trying to avoid the dustbins, and a veritable forest of pencils stuck in the ceiling tile blocks. The entire place also has the funk of humans who haven’t washed in quite some time. The first writer, a wiry man with huge coke-bottled glasses, tucks at his stained polo shirt collar. “Check out the gazonga’s on that one!! Whoo hoo!!” The second writer… all skip it. They all look the same. “Aw man! I’d love to tie her up in a Gordian knot!!” The two geeks hoot and wiggle their fingers together, in a shameful display. The editor, though his dorkiness shines through, at least he doesn’t look like he combs his hair with a pork chop, turns a bright red at seeing their display. “Will you guys PUH-lease stop looking at online porno sites and focus on the task at hand?! I mean, dear God! What kind of drivel are you trying to pull here?” The first writer pushes up his glasses on his nose. “What? You wanted something plot specific about New Breed and you got it. What more do you want?” “Well, first off, we have a reputation to maintain here! No second class roleplays! Training sessions showing our man beating up a sparring partner in the ring? Cheese-O-Meter is off the charts! Hello! Hello? HELLO!!!” The two writers, once again immersed in porn, leap up from their seats, startled. The editor gets in close, his fetid breath steaming on their necks. “NOT TO MENTION that we had already STARTED a series! And it was never finished! How do you losers explain that, hmmm? The International House of PAINcakes – does that ring a bell?” The second one stammers out a feeble explanation. “Well, you see, it turned out that it would have been longer than previously anticipated, so we had hoped…” “You hoped the paying public wouldn’t notice as you sent out a pathetic, cheap, and tawdry RP in it’s place, preying on our target demographic’s notoriously short attention spans,” the editor finishes. “Bah! Pathetic!” “Well, when you say it like that, it sounds bad…” “IT IS BAD!!” The first guy holds up his hand I defense. “Alright, alright. We’ll just… add a few bits to the roleplay is all…” We re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he body slams Vic Kevlar to the mat, the concussive force echoing against the damp cellar walls, Steele straightens himself… then gets a quizzical look over his face. “Pardon me, but Ms. Saksnussum…” WHUP-CRACK!!! “Less break time, more break Kevlar! Hai!” Steele flinches from the sting of the whip. “Tis of utmost importance that I ask of you this question!” WHUP-CRACK!!! “Ask away… so long as you don’t mind your flesh!! HAI!!” Big Al roars as he grabs the snapping whip end, keeping it’s metal tip from again slicing into his tender man-flesh. “By the gods woman! Enough motivational techniques and answer my queries!!” Click! Sue pulls out a revolver, her voice cold and emotionless. “I knew the day would come when you would turn on me… or can you talk while training?” Steele quickly grabs Kevlar’s head and bodily lifts him into the air, quickly performing a set of German suplexes. Sue grins savagely, before uncocking the .44 and lowering the barrel. Big Al huffs and puffs as he strains to get out his question. “How did thou… escape from the clutches… of the vile terroristic vermin… infesting the International Cake of Pan Haus?” Sue puts a finger to her pursed lips. “It was pretty easy, Big guy…” Editor’s Note: “Flashbacks? What is this, a very special episode of ‘Family Ties’ during the strike year?” Inside the revolving IHOP, located far above San Jose in a fantastic space needle-ish building, terrorists have taken the entire opulent pancake house hostage! The leader, Marcis, stands at the table where Sue, the Masochist, and the Graces are seated. “Well, your little friend Big Alan Steele has survived my men after all. But it shall NOT be long before he lies dead at my feet, and your Ace up your sleeve shall prove as ineffectual as A U.N. peacekeeping force!” Sue smiles smugly and casually raises a coffee mug to her lips. “Then why is there fear in your eyes?” Marcis snarls and fires a pistol into the air, frightening the less-hearty into panic. The Graces cower and the Masochist turns white as the villain waves the pistol at the group. “Well, I doubt then that you shall find me the ever-so gracious host you have come to know me as!” Marcis barks at the table, showing Sue with spittle, that she calmly wipes away. Making a grand gesture, turning to the restaurant proper, he shouts loudly enough so everyone can hear. “Should any of you try and escape, that’s anyone in the entire restaurant, including your Big Alan Steele, then I shall personally kill each one of you and your friends! What do you think of THAT, Ms. Saksnussum?” Silence. The leader silently chuckles, his back turned to the table. “I thought that might keep you quiet for a while…” He looks over his shoulder, quite sure of himself. Sue is gone, her empty seat mocking him in contempt. Marcis sighs. “Crap.” We return to the cellar, everyone looking at Sue with accusing eyes. She scowls at them, her eyes burning with a dangerous fury. “WHAT?! Oh, come on! He was BLUFFING!! You’re all standing here, right? Listen, a kidnapper is useless without his hostages… you were safer there than you probably are here!” Suzi steps forward, uncharacteristically shouting Sue down. “Oh yeah? Then what about the poor Masochist, huh?” “Oh… well, he’s out of critical condition, anyways…” says Sue, refusing to make eye contact. Suzi whirls around to Big Al. “They shot him over and over and over again!!” “By the gods!!” “Yeah! It’s a good thing that guy started with the Masochist, because his supreme powers of pain tolerance kept us all alive… by making them get tired of using their ammo. They just got fed up and stopped. The they tossed him out the window.” Sue dismisses the entire story with a head jerk. “Bah! 22 stories is like a walk in the park for him. They say he’ll be out of traction any day now. Besides… how did YOU blondes get out, huh?” Back to the IHOP, where evil minions are tossing a blood-stained Masochist out a broken window. Cindi raises a hand to gain the attention of Marcis, who is clutching at his sore trigger-finger. “Yes, what is it, you bubble-headed buffoon?” Cindi raises her chin defiantly. “I would suggest you start taking us to the bathroom in groups, unless your cleaning staff is REALLY good at what they do.” Marcis takes only a second to ponder the problem. “Point taken. We’ll take you out in one big group, guarded, off course, by my men.” The Graces are all forced to stand, then are led off by a veritable squadron of guards. Marcis turns his attention to other details… “Sir!” One of the guards runs up, his face flushed from a run. Marcis groans. “What is it.” “The blondes! They’re all… gone, sir!” “What?!” “Yeah! I swear, it was like herding cats! No threats worked… they would just kind of wander off aimlessly! We’d stop one, then two would go off. We’d get them, then three would be missing! Finally, we were down to just the one, so we locked her in the bathroom, with no chance of escape, and she…” Marcis finishes in heavy tones, “Escaped.” “Yeah… I guess.” “Remind me to kill you later.” “Uh… sure.” Back to the cellar, where a shrugging Cindi finishes up her story. “… and we all just ended up at the bottom of the building and went home, cuz we were tired.” Everyone who heard the story shakes their head, trying to make sense of the blonde’s ramblings, before Sue stands up and points an accusing finger at Big Al. “Oh yeah? Then how did you get out, Big guy?!” Big Al folds his arms across his chest. “Twas on all the news shows…” The sprinkler system rains water down on the restaurant, drenching the destroyed décor. Steele is near one of the picture windows, pulling hastily at the fire hose at a safety checkpoint. His toga has been stripped down in tatters, revealing his muffiny pectorals, as well as a host of flesh wounds and bloody gouges. Looking back desperately, he watches the terrorist leader Marcis crawl towards a detonator. Marcis grabs it and laughs maniacally. “Now I take you with me! See you in Hell, Steele! BWAHAHAHAHA!!!” Big Al punches at the glass, and it spiderwebs before falling away like a millions diamonds. He lunges forward, leaping into the air… Marcis stabs at the button… A fiery blast rips through the IHOP, exploding out of the circular structure in a 360 degree radius. The concussive force blows Steele out of the window, propelling him further like he was shot out of a cannon. “Yippee Hi-Keeba Mamma Jamma!!” Big Al freefalls until the hose runs out of length, snapping him to a halt harshly. The revolving restaurant begins to spin faster and faster, whipping the dangling Steele around and around. It keeps picking up speed, the brakes on the motor blown out in the blast. The city of San Jose speeds by, followed by the ocean, then San Jose, etc, etc. With the G-forces beginning to make our hero black out, he deftly unties the hose, and hangs on for dear life, only his mighty, vise-like grip keeping him from certain doom. The restaurant begins to lift out of its perch, the centrifugal force making it rise up the building, the metal groaning and straining as it does so. The city of San Jose flashes by again, and Big Al lets go, slung away from the rising structure, twisting and spinning out of control… Back to the cellar, as Big Al finalizes his tale. “By the hand of Zeus, I was able to time my flight of Icarus proportions, thusly allowing myself to land safely in the Pillow Factory at the San Jose Furniture Development Park.” “After saving the people and dispensing justice on the bad-guys, we assume,” chimes in Sue. “Naturally.” “And what about when the building rose up and spun off, crashing into the Orphan’s home? Was that your doing?” “But of course not!” an indignant Big Al replies. “I would never allow such a dark deed to occur, would I have been able to prevent it. Nevertheless…” “Nevertheless the little urchins are running their filthy paws through my stuff right now, no doubt. I really wish you didn’t take the ALL in.” “But I must!” booms the grinning Steele. “Though I was not able to prevent the tragedy, we surely can lend assistance in their hours of need!” Sue sighs. “Oh well… looks like everything wrapped up in a neat, little package.” WHUP-CRACK!! “Now back to work! HAI! New Breed awaits!!” Editor’s note: “Yes. That’ll do. Back to your porn…” Writer's note: 'Hi-Keeba!'
