Hank/Lucifer, same person? SS#3

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-02 Segment One: Hank and Josh are sitting down in strip club, with a couple bottles of Sobe in front of them. Hank takes a swig of his as Josh just has his hand set around his bottle. A fine dark skinned female walks over to Hank and starts to rub him the rightway. Hank grins as Josh starts to mouth Janelle to him, Hank then gets wide eyed and smiles up at the woman and pushes her away and looks down, very distraught. Hank takes another swing of his Sobe and finishes it off. Hank sets it back down on the table and picks it up by the top and starts to smack it to his hand. Josh wipes his brow as he does not remove his eyes from the Sobe bottle in Hank's hands.Ganker then enters the strip joint and walks over to them and takes a seat as Hank puts the bottle back down and Josh quickly snatches it for his own sake and safety. Hank: 'So Ganker, what brings yo here?' Ganker: 'Came to get yo, Janelle knows yo here Hank, but Tanya does not know Josh is here.' Hank: 'Cause I'm a good boy, I tell my woman everything.' Ganker: 'She'd kill yo if yo didn't boyo.' Hank: 'Also true.' --- They all share a laugh. Josh: 'How's Tanya?' Ganker: 'In bed, she's fantastic...' --- Josh gets angry, but like usual, he does nothing, but shakes his fist at Ganker before flipping him the byrd. Ganker: 'It's fun to watch this boy get angry huh Hank?' Hank: 'I'd like to plead the fifth.' Ganker: 'Since when yo all law happy?' Hank: 'I ain't, I jus' wanted to say that right then boyo, yo gots a prob wit' that?' Ganker: 'I don't.' Josh: 'I do~!' --- Josh looks at Hank ready to be smacked, and then looks at Ganker, who's trying to be sneaky. Then from behind a floating hand comes up from behind Josh and begins to attack him, mainly trying to strangle him. Hank and Ganker fall out of their seats laughing their a**es off. They get back up and help Josh get rid of the floating hand. The hand then attacks Ganker and clenges to his face. Ganker falls trying to get it off and Hank yanks it off and throws it in the trash and locks the lid down on it. They retake their seats and once again begin to talk. Hank: 'I have to admit, Jason Core seems to have a nice respect that I could get used to.' Ganker: 'People respect yo?' Josh: 'Of course, who wouldn't respect Hank? Not many people can job as much as he can and still remain tall and with as much pride as he has.' --- Josh laughs with Ganker. Hank: 'I won't do sh*t cause that's true. Win, lose, or even draw, I always come out the winner because I know I'm doin' what I want. Jason Core, Karl LeBlanc, they don't have the passion I have for this sport. I mean I can remember turning on the old television while still unemployeed, waitin' for that call from PCW, sayin' they wanted me back. I can remember sitting by the phone and watching the television at any PCW event that was currently playing. Watching Meanstreak, 'No-Selling' and Doug Troy tryin' to be what he once was. I am Hank~! I have a passion for this wrestling business that is not in most wrestling fans, or wrestling stars even. I want, eat, bleed, sweat, all of it, for this business. I'd sell my soul to keep this bus...' --- Right as he said the part about selling his soul, a figure that looks like Hank appears, only he is wearing all red, has horns and a pointy tail. Lucifer: 'I am the Mourning Star~! I heard you said you'd sell you're soul, so I'm here to make good on that.' --- Josh and Ganker looks at Lucifer and then back at Hank. They can't even break out one word, out of pure shock. Hank: 'Damn freak~! I told yo once boyo, don't get the plastic surgery to look like me~!' (angered) 'How's Hitler? Burnin' well I hope.' Lucifer: 'No in fact, he's burning really bad. I felt like torturing him.' --- Both Hank and Lucifer share a laugh. Hank: 'Yo have two open slots in hell?' Lucifer: 'Why do you ask?' Hank: 'This Sunday on Sabotage I have to take on a couple of PCW jobbers, Karl LeBlanc and Jason Core, I figure I might as well do them the big favor and end their pathetic and worthless lives now.' Lucifer: 'No, not yet.' (laughs deviously) 'But I'm sure I can work something out, only because we have such a high-respect for you down in hell.' Hank: (conceited/cocky) 'What can I say, I'm the pure meaning of EVIL...' Lucifer: 'You personify everything hell stands for. Hell, sometimes us underlings down below can't even stand to watch your matches because they're so horrible. I mean you stabbed a wrestler and you beat yourself up.' Hank: 'Yes, yes I do.' (grins deviously) Lucifer: 'But I'll work on opening those slots for...' (scratches head) 'Damnit, I forgot the jobber's names.' Hank: 'Erm, me too.' --- They both share an evil laugh as Josh and Ganker are still in awe, trying to still explain this action. Hank: 'Ah, I remember now~!' Lucifer: 'Quickly, who are they?' Hank: 'Damn, I forgot.' (laughs) Lucifer: 'Great, well I'll just remember there are two coming down this Sunday after Sabotage airs live.' Hank: 'Ah yes, Karl LeBlanc and Jason Core.' Lucifer: 'Well if you don't mind, I actually have important business to attend to, other than sticking around in this hell hole of a planet, talking to the likes of you.' Hank: 'Later...' --- Lucifer is right on the verge of vanishing as Hank stops him. Hank: 'Yo will have the doctors turn yo back right?' (Lucifer nods) 'Man, I'm serious, I ain't no dumb sh*t like Joseph Stalin, but the way, how's he burnin'?' Lucifer: (annoyed) 'Badly like Hitler, may I leave now?' Hank: 'Erm, jus' agree to get the plastic surgery done so yo don't look like me. I really don't like a wuss like yo lookin' like me.' Lucifer: (grins) 'Yup, I will... HEY~!' --- Hank poofs Lucifer out of his apartment and back to the depths of Hell where he belongs and bangs Ganker's and Josh's heads together and walks into the kitchen and makes himself a turkey sandwich. Josh and Ganker get up rubbing their heads in unison. They walk into the kitchen and sit down at the nice new glass table and glare at Hank. Ganker: 'Yo Lucifer or Hank?' Hank: (sighs) 'I'm Hank numbsack.' Ganker: 'Good... Two people who look like yo is too scary for words.' Josh: 'I thought I was going to be double smacked.' Hank: 'Wouldn't give yo the pleasure Josh. Hell, that's my personal fun, I wouldn't let that damn wuss in on it.' Josh: (smiling) 'Thank you... HEY~!' --- Hank finishes making his turkey sandwich as he walks over to the chair by Josh's and places his plate down. Hank walks over to his new sound system and places the Metallica Black Album on and turns it up to full blast. Josh swipes a bite of Hank's sandwich and turns the other way right as Hank takes a seat. Hank: 'Which of the two of yo too a bite outta my sandwich?' --- Josh points to Ganker and Ganker points to Josh. A bread crumb falls from Josh's mouth. Hank grins then lets out a sly and very devious laugh and the... WHACK~! SMACK~! WHACK~! SMACK~! WHACK~! SMACK~! WHACK~! SMACK~! WHACK~! --- Josh begins to whimper like a little bytch. Hank: 'There's nothin' worse than watchin' a fat man weep.' Josh gets up and runs like a girl over to the couch and continues to whimper there. Hank: 'FLY FAT A** FLY~!' Ganker: 'I WANT YO EXTREME CHAMPIONSHIP~!' --- A candlestick gets chucked at Ganker's face as he falls backwards out of his seat. Hank: 'Fool~! Yo never speak down to me or my title~!' --- Ganker pulls himself to and sits back down in his seat. Ganker: 'I wasn't, I said I wanted yo title.' Hank: 'I can find another candlestick and if not, there is a television over there that I can replace.' Ganker: 'Psychopathic freak~!' Hank: 'Yo damn right~!' Ganker: 'Cause,' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God~!' Hank: 'Yeah, I jus' bet.' Ganker: 'I carried yo a** in NZW, the only reason yo were so much of a success cause I was around yo and everyone loved me and thought yo were so cool cause yo got to hangout wit' Ganker Ransolver.' Hank: 'Damn, and I thought it was cause I was well... The better athlete, the better looker, and well jus' around all better than yo and everybody else? Damn oh well, guess yo jus' suck.' Ganker: 'Is it my fault yo are a loser who will never amount to anything more than the worthless PCW Extreme Championshp? And no I don't suck yo do.' Hank: (snilfs the air) 'What's that I smell, bull something... That's right,' (grins) 'Ganker's Bullsh*t.' Ganker: 'Nope, I can't smell nothin' over that sh*t yo spewin' from yo hole.' Hank: 'Oi... Jealous Jackass.' Ganker: 'I win, I wore yo down to worthless and pathetic name calling~!' Hank: 'Fine, yo can win monkey, but I'll tell yo who won't win this Sunday on Sabotage.' Ganker: 'And who's that?' Hank: 'Karl LeBlanc and Jason Core, the two biggest freaks to enter PCW and think they stand a chance to capture my PCW Extreme Championship that I've had to work years to capture, winnin' this title was hard, but takin' it off of me will be god damn near impossible. They'd have to kill me to take the title off from around my waist.' Ganker: 'Well jus' stay healthy for when Team Nirvana gets placed in a tag team title match for the PCW Tag Titles. Those tag titles are as good as ours.' Hank: 'Cause I'm on the team and I never fail when it comes to aquiring gold.' (grins a cocky and conceited grin) Ganker: 'No, it's cause Team Nirvana is the team to be remembered and respected. There wasn't no team in the NZW who could even come close to takin' us down. We were the top dogs in the yard.' Hank: 'My NZW World Title is still in tact in the bedroom, not one man in NZW could ever take that title from me. Now don't get me wrong, I had some great challengers, Misfit for instance, a great competitor, but jus' wasn't good enough for my level of godliness.' Ganker: 'I'm beginning to think all Canadians are bullsh*tters.' (laughs) Hank: 'I'm beginnin' to think my Canadian boot is goin' to be shoved down yo American throat.' Ganker: 'At least it's not a Canadian Throat.' Hank: 'No comment...' Ganker: 'I think The Crow was a good competitor to challenge at yo NZW World Championship.' Hank: 'Yeah, he was good, but he wouldn't have come out wit' the victory over Hank~! He wasn't good enough, nor ready to take on such a god, such as myself.' Ganker: (sarcastically) 'Yeah, I jus' bet.' Hank: 'No, dude, really. Take this this Sunday on Sabotage for example, both LeBlanc and Core for example, both great athletes and competitors, but neither of them have what it takes, neither of them are ready to step into the ring wit' such a god such as myself. Yo know, myself and Triple H have something in common, we both have a good work ethic. We are willin' to take the fall to a jobber to put over a match or that superstar. I mean why do yo think Rock even pinned him at all? Damn jobber.' Ganker: 'Rocky's my idol...' Hank: 'Kid Wonder is my God~! Mike Kreller is my Role Model...' (((real life quote from the real Hank Gunyon here))) Ganker: 'Yo a sick man to have such a dumb sh*t as yo role model and an even bigger jackass for yo god.' Hank: 'What can I say, too many chairshots to the head.' Ganker: 'That must be the case.' --- Josh returns to the table, wiping the tears from his face first off. Hank: (glaring over in Josh's direction) Sup crybaby? Have a good cry?' Ganker: 'A**clown as returned~!' --- Josh runs back to his couch and begins to whimper on Ganker's pillow. Ganker and Hank both begin to laugh. Ganker: 'Who's pillow is that? I feel sorry for the poor sap who has to sleep on that wet sh*t. Hank: 'That's yo pillow dawg.' Ganker: 'WHAT~!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!' --- Ganker jumps up and starts to attack Josh. Ganker grabs his pillow and begins to beat Josh repeatedly with it. The scene now fades out to absolute/complete/utter nothingness.
