The Sandy Wong Show -- Take That Chet King!

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/5/02 {Transcript format} Sandy:'Welcome to the Sandy Wong Show! I am the incomparable Sandy Wong, and joining me is my partner in crime, Skylar Green. Hello, Skylar.' Skylar:'Hello, Sandy.' Sandy:'How are you?' Skylar:I’m fine. I just got back from the doctor.' Sandy:'How’d it go?' Skylar:'Terrible! First, they stuck this thermometer into my backside, and then they forced me to swallow an awful-tasting pill! The bath afterwards wasn’t so bad, but they sprayed this weird, smell stuff on me. When it was all over, the nurse gave me a bone-shaped cracker and this hospital collar.' Sandy:'Skylar, you crazy fool, you went to the vet!' Skylar:'No wonder the doctor tried to neuter me!' Sandy:'Ewwwzzies. Anyway, let’s bring out our first guest: The host of Survivor!' Survivor:'Hello, Sandy. Thank you for me having me.' Skylar:'So, let’s get right down to this. I’ve had trouble surmising the supposed brilliance behind the premise of your show.' Survivor:'Excuse me?' Skylar:'There's no element of death!' Survivor:'There's not supposed to be.' Skylar:'What's the point of calling it Survivor if there's no Lord of the Flies or fights to the death going on?' Survivor:'It's just a name.' Skylar:'Shut up and get outta here!' Sandy:'... I-- Skylar, just let me handle the guests, please?' Skylar:'Oh, sure thing. Sandy:'We’ll be back with our next guest, J.D. Salinger; but first, let’s go to Rene St. LeRoux with the fashion forecast for this week.' Rene:'Welcome one and all to the wonderful world of Fashion Weekly! From Monday to Wednesday, I expect flannel to warm our hearts and dazzle us with its excitingly bland color palette. Tuesday will ring in the use of spandex as an all-purpose material, and my prediction is that flares will come back from Thursday to the following Tuesday. Hot pants and halter-tops are definitely “in” for the unisex gathering, but make sure you meet the physical requirements before wearing these outfits. Bell-bottoms shall make an ephemeral comeback at about 11am on Friday and will come back to the grave on 9pm that very Friday. And lastly, let me give you one piece of advice: do not wear anything that was living when attending a PETA convention. For Fashion Weekly, I’m Rene St. LeRoux.' Sandy:'Thank you, Rene. Now, please welcome the one and only, J.D. Salinger.' Salinger:Hello.' Sandy:'Why did you come out of seclusion?' Salinger:'I needed some more toilet paper and some fish for dinner.' Sandy:'Interesting... So, why did you decide to come on our show?' Salinger:I didn’t want to. This man shoved me into his car when I came to answer the knock at my cottage door.' Skylar:'Hehehe, he’s just joking, everybody.' Salinger:'No, I’m not. Don’t leave me alone with this man. Help me, please!' Sandy:'Skylar, you told me that he called us asking to come on the show. Skylar:'He called us, I kidnapped him...what’s the difference?' Sandy:'The latter is a federal offense.' Skylar:'True, but that’s never stopped me.' Sandy:'Anyway, now, we have George Richards with the Weekend Outlook.' George:'I’m George Richards with the Weekend Outlook. Look for the weekend to encompass the days of Saturday and Sunday. There is a continuing heated debate as to whether or not the weekend actually begins at 5pm on Fridays, but I won’t get into that. Expect every club to be packed, whether it’s the old folks’ club or any of the fine clubs in the area. The tourists will continue to flood our markets and consent to be gouged by our vendors. God bless America. For the person eating out, go with what you know. Choose a place that specializes in your favorite foods. If you’re looking for just anything, I’d personally recommend Olive Garden. If you’re going out with friends, hit the road early to avouid late night traffic. If you have absolutely no taste or shame, then take the bus. This has been the Weekend Outlook.' Sandy:'Before I begin, I’d just like to say that Skylar will not be joining me anymore. During our last break, the FBI took our friend away, so from here on out, I’ll be running solo. Enough about that, though; our next guest is a professional wrestler. And no, we’re not shoving DDP, Goldberg, or The Rock down your collective throat again. Here tonight, we have the ECW’s own Amish Warrior, Roadkill. Here’s Roadkill, ladies and gentlemen. Roadkill:'Chickens!' Sandy:'That’s nice. What’s your real name?' Roadkill:'Chickens.' Sandy:'Okay....' Roadkill:'Chickens.' Sandy:'Is there something wrong with this guy? Gary, what’s up?' Roadkill:'Chickens.' Sandy:'All right, Gary, my stage manager, tells me that Roadkill is contractually obligated to stay in character 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. And his character is allowed only to say the word, “chickens.” I don’t know how I’m going to have an interview with this guy. Tell you what, Gary, go to the weather, I’ll think of something.' Hail:'This is Hail Storm with the weather: Look out your window. That was the weather. I’m Hail Storm.' Sandy:'Thank you for that brief weather forecast, Hail. Well, Roadkill spotted our buffet table during the break and he’s been stuffing his face since. I don’t recall him stopping to breathe or even chewing. Anyway, now it’s time for sports. Sandy:'I’m Sandy Wong and I’ll be doing sports. Don King is advertising his own celebrity deathmatch to be held in the Dominican Republic and broadcast all over the world. He’s billed this one as a 6 man elimination tag match with Jerry Seinfeld, Jerry Springer, and Jerry Lewis against, Jim Carrey, Mariah Carey, and Drew Carey. This is a mud-wrestling match, though God only knows who booked that stipulation. Carl Lewis is scheduled to race against Secretariat as an opening attraction to the Olympics. The WWE’s Kurt Angle is rumored to be carrying the Olympic torch through the final leg of the route. Reports indicate that Kurt Angle started these rumors.' Sandy:'Anyway, that’s it for the Sandy Wong Show. For my former sidekick, Skylar Green, I’m Sandy Wong. Goodnight!' Sandy:'Hey, Sky?' The End:'Yeah?' Sandy:'What's a transcript talk show with nothing but script and talking have to do with your Continental Title Match or your teaming with Dante Jackson this Sunday? I see it designated in the location field, but this doesn't have anything to do with it.' The End:'Transcript and lack of ambiance aside, this is relevant because you asked about it. Thanks.' Sandy:'Oh, Sky...'
