Splitting a way to Separate

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Date: 22nd February 2002Time: 12:45pmLocation: Boise, Idaho The Journal entry of El Diablo A Mexican Restaurant in the United States sure sounds appealing to me, but I wonder what these pathetic Americans would think of the idea. Maybe Angel was right, perhaps I am crazy to take on this project, it is very off putting, it is a huge distraction, but then again I know the money it should guarantee if a success, and that is a lot for me, hell it would be a lot to Bill Gates, another puny American. I know this business venture will set me back slightly, but the rewards it should reap make it, just make it a risk that I must take, a risk that I have to take, a risk that I would be stupid not to take. My only worries now are if the builders are reliable and if they can build this complex on time. My scheduled date for opening is April 3rd, and that is the date I want to keep, I will not be happy if I have to change it and my builders will know and feel it if I am unhappy. I stand corrected, they aren't my only worries about this project, they are just the immediate worries, as in the long run I need to select a menu and then the look of the inside of my restaurant. Of course all of this worries me, but I will just have to let it, as Angel was right about prioritising, and this will have to become my third priority every so often, especially when I have to wrestle in PCW. One concern of mine, however, is that voice I heard. I still have no idea of who or what it was and that is quite disturbing, but I'm sure it has some relevance to this restaurant and when I see what it meant, I think I may finally be satisfied with all of this. And I'm sure that Angel will feel the same, then she will see, then she will see that I am not crazy and that perhaps a voice did exist. I suppose I'll get back to writing this later as Angel is calling me, I really need to write these things down, it really does keep my sense of mind and sanity. El Diablo stands up from writing his piece and closes shut his journal of entries. 'What is it Angel?' asks Diablo. Angel walks into the room that El Diablo is currently in. 'About this restaurant,' she says to him. El Diablo looks across at her from the seat in which he is sat in. 'What about it?' asks the confused Mexican to his wife. Angel sits down next to her husband. 'How much will it be costing?' she asks wearily. El Diablo looks at her again ands miles. 'Not as much as you think,' he tells her. He seems rather cautious in actually telling her the figure, and Angel notices this. 'Honey, that is not an answer,' Angel states sharply. El Diablo and Angel are in Boise, Idaho, the location of this weeks Friday Fusion, where El Diablo must beat Anton The Kid to reach the March Madness main event and in that match he will receive another PCW Title shot. The two of them are obviously in a hotel on the road ready for Fusion. The hotel room is basic with just a bedroom and restroom, the bedroom has a bed, two chairs and a television as the main necessities for them, and also a closet in which to place their clothes in should they desire to it. The Mexican Devil is seated on one of those two chairs and is wearing his black leather mask, along with a black top and black pants that he usually wears. And Angel is just wearing a bathrobe and her hair is very wet, indicating that she has just got out from the shower. The robe grips her curvaceous body well. Angel goes to sit down on the bed as El Diablo hides the journal in which he writes in, on the white-carpeted floor and slides it under his chair. El Diablo eventually responds to Angel, 'Well, it's all you need to know right now,' Angel starts to rub her wet hair a little in an attempt to dry it. 'I need to know and you best tell me soon,' she leaves him an ultimatum, 'When I finish in the bathroom, I expect an answer,' she finishes. She heads back into the bathroom and closes the door behind her as El Diablo picks up his journal and opens it, to where he was at. Women, I don't see why she needs to know, but I guess I'll just have to tell her. Well, tonight I face Anton The Kid to determine my fate and honestly I think I can beat this little twerp. Well I have to, I need to, I must and I'm positive I can, I hope. Thankfully Vampir had himself disqualified, as against him I will have stood no chance, but now that I face Anton I have at least a 50% better chance at winning and with Angel supporting me I believe I can win. I'll be the first to admit I've been lucky to get this far, Alan Steele wasn't ready to fight me and I understand that with myself pinning him in that mall brawl. And with Vampir losing I get an easier attempt at reaching my second attempt for the PCW Title. I remember the first, losing to Ivan, that now OSW traitor, and I must say a match I deserved to win, but I will get my second attempt by being victorious tonight. Sabotage, yet another day on the road, when I head to Canada, at least it's out of the United States, I really hate this country. I go against judas, Akiyama and Pharaoh, Golden Game while I team with Flaymin Aymin, currently I really cannot stand any of them. Akiyama will definitely receive his payback from last Sabotage, as I'm sure Aymin and I shall be victorious over the non-title defending supposed Gods, I'm so sick of them. And Turmoil, Anton again, I laugh at that, as we meet yet again, but I'm sure I capture victory again. Angel will be out soon, so I'll end this entry here. Indeed the bathroom door does open and Angel's hair is now dry and she is wearing just a black thong and black bra. El Diablo again hides his private journal from his wife. 'So, how much?' she asks him once again a little angered. El Diablo sighs and stands up. 'Don't be too shocked,' he says as him and Angel go and sit down on the end of the bed. 'Just tell me,' says Angel. El Diablo stares at Angel. 'Nearly $100,000' he states. Angel has a look of anger on her face and steps up from the bed. 'You can sleep on the floor, and you definitely aren't getting lucky tonight,' she yells at him as she pushes him onto the floor and gets onto the bed all by herself. She leaves her husband lying on the floor as she starts to go to sleep.
