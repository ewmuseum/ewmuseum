Over the ocean and through the sky...

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/04 We arrived there around 5 PM in the afternoon. The sun was still out, and would probably set in another two hours. The warmth wasn't enough to counter the fact that the temperature was already about 70 when we stepped off of the plane. The ride over wasn't too bad, although I wish that they had some better choices for movies than 'Bend It Like Beckham' and 'She's All That.' I swear, that's all that they have in the way of in-flight movies. At least I don't have to worry about watching them; but the flight had the kind of system in which everyone heard the movie whether they wanted to or not. I sat in first class along with the other PCW veterans, though I've always considered myself to be less than a veteran. Boda and Kano sat next to each other. You'd never know it just by looking at them, but they both have Game Boy Advances and apparently their favorite game to play on the flight over was... Pokemon. I kid you not. Rent-A-Hero kept trying to give Kano pointers (he lost about seven times in a row to Boda), but he was reminded of the Ultimate Rule of Video-Gaming: Never EVER offer assistance. It also works in checkers, chess, and just about every other game. If someone wants help, they'll ask. But no one ever asks for help. There's too much pride on the line. Especially if it's a guy. Darkstar sat too close to the door for our comfort. We wouldn't put it past him to throw open the door at 35,000 feet and kill us all. We had Bobby Bob sit on the isle. Our road agent, Bobby Bob legitimately weighs 437 pounds for being about 5'10'. Even in a first-class seat, Darkstar couldn't have slipped by him without a struggle, giving the rest of us time to get there and tie him down. But he didn't do anything. He just slept the whole way through. Talena listened to some CDs. Though 'She's All That' played a bit loudly, and I think I heard her grating her teeth throughout the whole thing. Rent-A-Hero sat next to Rafaela and Tanya. I brought the girls along. They've never been to China, so why not? It would turn out to be a smarter move than I realized at the time. Skitzo was apparently seated next to Talena in a window seat (we separated Darkstar and Talena across the aisle so that they couldn't plan our demise on the plane), but also behind Kano and in front of me next to Afwa. That has to be a problem with multiple personalities; he couldn't decide on wear to sit, so he played a huge tournament of Rock, Paper, Scissors with himself. By the second round, we all pretty much shouted, 'SIT NEXT TO TALENA, CRAP!' Cal Roberts complained about not being able to sit in first class since he was the World Champion. Boda just told him, 'Sit in the back, kid,' and left it at that. He appealed, saying that if Jay Phoenix could be seated in the front, then so could he. I pointed out that Phoenix briefly worked for PCW in the past, and Kano told Roberts to shut up and take his seat. Afwa has to ride first class everytime he goes somewhere. The seats in coach aren't made to accomodate someone of his girth. He felt right at home, and even knew the flight attendant (Sheryl) by first name before anyone said anything. Rent-A-Hero explained to the girls how we were actually flying forward in time by crossing the Pacific to reach China. Or at least he tried. He got caught up in making sure that they understood that the International Dateline was not a 900 number, and that he didn't find that out 'the hard way'. In one of the few moments of solidarity on the plane ride, pretty much everyone laughed at that. Propaganda sat in the back, and we were that much happier for it. He started preaching in coach. Imagine having to put up with that being right next to you on the plane? The coach section, which had a number of regular people in addition to the wrestlers, all stood firm in their demand that he 'Shut the hell up!' That only made him work harder, though. He even had the coach section flight attendants pass out the communion wine and wafers. One guy said, 'What the hell is this, I asked for a 7-up...' Another one said, 'Are you sure that this is the blood of Christ?' To which Eric Sagar answered in the positive. The guy then said, 'It tastes like grape juice.' Propaganda said, 'The Lord can taste like grape juice if he wants to.' The guy answered with, 'Does the Lord come in any other flavors?' Sagar shot back, 'No! Now drink!' The guy smacked his lips in the bad way. 'I hate grape juice.' 'Just drink it!' Propaganda commanded. Another person, probably not a Catholic, said, 'You let people beat, stab, crucify, and kill your Lord and savior, and then you eat his flesh and drink his blood, and I'M the bad guy here?' Priceless. Oh, I could tell you more, but there's no need. That's a good idea of what was going on up there in the sky. Plus, everyone else will either deny everything that I've said, or give their own stories. Really, every perspective is but an interpretation of events as they happen around them. No one person is completely correct in their description of occurences, so take my little narrative as you will; I won't call it an accurate account of what happened, just one account of what happened.
