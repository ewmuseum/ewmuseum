A Man Of All Traits Part 3

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 31st, 2002 (The scene opens to an empty auditorium. Mike Frampton is wearing a costume that resembles the Victorian Era. He is on stage with several other people. Michael Graves can be seen sitting in the audience with a script.) Mike Frampton: If I only knew thigh courage then thigh could... Line Michael Graves: Save thee. Mike Frampton: Then thigh could save thee fair maiden from the grasps of the... Line Michael Graves: Evil tyrant. Mike Frampton: The grasps of the evil tyrant Johnathon Crichton. Director: Sorry the script does not say Johnathon Crichton. Mike Frampton: Well what does it say? Director: Lord Huggleberry. Mike Frampton: Crichton sounds better. Director: Try again. Mike Frampton: The grasps of the evil tyrant Lord Huggleberry and his minion William Mumphry. (Graves stands up.) Michael Graves: Come on Mike we need some passion! Can't you show some passion. Mike Frampton: You want passion, I'll give you passion. (Mike takes centre stage.) Mike Frampton: Blue Spanish eyes, I... Director: Listen, this is a Victorian Era play not a Simpsons rip off. Mike Frampton: Maybe it should be. Director: Yes or maybe you're fired. Mike Frampton: Hmm, I see and who will you have replace me? Director: That guy. (The director points at Graves.) Director: Have you had any acting experience? Michael Graves: I was in a winter play in grade two. Director: Good enough take his clothes. (The scene fades to white. A moment passes and the scene fades into the show that night. Graves is on stage and he is acting out Mike's part.)Michael Graves: Romeo, Romeo, where art though Romeo? Oops, wrong play. If I only knew thigh courage then thigh could kill all the baddies. Err, line. (The crowd begins to boo.) Michael Graves: Oh boo yourself. (Mike steps out onto the stage and taps Graves on his shoulder. Graves spins around and is slapped in the head.) Mike Frampton: Get off the stage and let me do this right. (The crowd cheers.) Mike Frampton: If I only knew thigh courage then thigh could save thee fair maiden from the grasps of the evil tyrant Lord Huggleberry and his minion William Mumphry. Then the power of John Crichton would cease to exist and only thigh would rule this land. (Mike then is eyed by the director for changing the script.) Mike Frampton: Then with my last ounce of life I will destroy the kings of the Primetime world and I will be the last man standing. (The director points to the script.) Mike Frampton: For they will all feel the Sensation of Annihilation. No man can withstand thigh power and I will Command and Conquer all men who stand in thigh way. (The director passes out and hits the floor hard.) Mike Frampton: For though art the one and only. For though art thigh protector. For though art thigh fiend. For though art thigh master and ruler. For art though the Punisher and for though art thigh supreme digital warlord! (The crowd falls silent waiting for his next words.) Mike Frampton: Lord Huggleberry, John Crichton, you will be annihilate and thigh will reign supreme and thigh will be the one who takes your kingdom and makes it thigh own. Michael Graves: WOO! (The crowd turns to Graves.) Michael Graves: Umm, proceed. Mike Frampton: I am the only champion ready to take the helm of our lives and take on all challengers. The land of Baradua will be safe and thigh will be the ruler. (Mike Frampton then walks and trips. He flies off of the stage to a chorus of boos. The scene fades out. A moment later the scene fades back in to Frampton and Graves sitting a table.) Michael Graves: Maybe you could... SLAP (Fade to black.)
