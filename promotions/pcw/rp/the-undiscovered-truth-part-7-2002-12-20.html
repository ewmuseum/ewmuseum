The Undiscovered Truth (Part 7)

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/20/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 6 I’ll admit it, I never saw it coming and I never expected anything from anyone. I went through like trusting myself and only myself. I didn’t have friends; friends were just people who wanted to find out information about you so that a few years down the road they could use it against you. Friends are only your friends until they find ones that are better. It’s a sad truth but it’s true. I didn’t experience a lot of things in my life, but I sure did witness it. The number one thing I witnessed was lying. Yesterday morning I shared part of my soul with a man. I usually don’t do that type of thing, but when I’m nervous and my future is on the line, it just happens. Of course I didn’t expect any sympathy from him. I never thought he would try and help me out. I mean why would he? What would I possibly do for him other than higher the chances of him getting fired? But he pulled through for men, and I’ve never had anyone pull through for me like that before. All I’ve had in my mind all day was him and his wife, struggling to get his daughter the proper education and life she needs. But I’m going to make this all change. I won’t let that girl’s life be meaningless. I won’t let that mans good gesture go unrecognized. I’ll repay in the way that would bring a smile to his face and think that it’s worth it to save some lost causes. In a profession where he has to secure people against their will everyday, maybe he’ll realize sometimes it’s more beneficial to go beyond the rules. I remembered something he told me the day before and then I knew exactly what to do for this man. “ I can’t afford the house I have on the corner of East Ave. even when I’m living across the street from a dump. How will I ever survive?” Later that day I paid the man who I didn’t even know the name of a visit. I thought about how I’d present myself, but then decided to just keep the whole thing quiet and leave a letter. So I arrived at the small and old house with nothing but the best thoughts in my mind. I felt good about myself because I know I was about to make someone’s season. Right as I went up to that door snowflakes began to falls from the sky. A flake fell onto the slope of the cheek and I wiped it away. I slipped the letter through the crack of the door and never turned back again. Even though it may have not been the greatest spiritual present, it was sure something he needed. *In the house* The man’s wife is seen sweeping around the house when she spots the letter just lying there on the dusty floor. What’s this? She grabs the letter and sees the initials E.R on the front. Under it is small letter is says “For the man of which heroes are born”. Hector! Do you know what this letter is doing here? Hector comes down the steps and then sees him wife holding a white envelope. At first he doesn’t know what’s going on. He sees the letter E.R in the front but doesn’t put two and two together. Where did you find this? It was just here on the floor near the door. Hector opens the envelope and sees a small typed letter. The letter reads: Dear Mr. Mystery, You saved me from a faith that could lead to hardships and tribulations. I told you part of my past that most have never learned. You took that information and used it wisely. You helped me even when I was just thinking about myself. You realized the problem I was going through and helped me get around it. I could only pray that God have more men like you on this earth. May you live well, and when you one-day die and rise to heaven, I hope we embrace again so I can formally greet you. For now this will have to do. PS: Treat her like a princess Signed El Romantico Hector is deeply touched by the letter as he now realizes what this is all about and feels so grateful for being recognized for this. Hector, what is this? Why are your eyes tearing? Hector then opens the envelope and see’s a check for five hundred thousand dollars. His eyes can’t believe what their seeing as he stands their in awe. It’s a gift from God baby. It’s definitely a gift from God. Hector shows the check to his wife, who practically passes out from the amount she sees written on the front. AH DIOS MIO! *Outside in the snow* Romantico walks through the snowy and well-lit streets as he feels a strong wind of air pass on through him. He looks behind himself, but doesn’t see anyone their. The scene fades away as an imprint of a lightly colored body is shown flying up to the sky as Romantico walks off into the snow.
