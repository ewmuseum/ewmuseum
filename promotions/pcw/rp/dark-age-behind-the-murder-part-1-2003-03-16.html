Dark Age: Behind the Murder (Part 1)

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/03 Cheesy music begins as the TV flickers, and the nearly LSD trip like intro for VH1's Behind the Music begins with 'Dark Age' listed on the right side. Voiceover: Today we follow the story of wrestling's most animated stable. Footage of the multiple members of the Dark Age, from the originals to the most recent version, with four mannequins and a box of cheese wearing a cape. Voiceover: From the dizzying highs... DarkStar and Clyde are shown sitting around with a bottle of vodka resting on the World Title between them. DarkStar: Wooo... Clyde: Yep. Voiceover: To the dizzying lows... DarkStar and Clyde are seen wearing OSW T-shirts and waving little OSW flags in the office of the Phantom Booker, circa 2002. DarkStar: So, when do we start? TPB: You guys are in PCW? Voiceover: All next, on VH1's Behind the Murder. We fade in on a familiar figure wearing a tuxedo. Dick Clark: Hi, I'm Dick Clark. They began as a group of ragtag jobbers. The early careers of the Dark Age were tumultous to say the least.....in fact, the tapes of this period have been mysteriously lost to the sands of time. We cut to Darkstar shoving a cassette labeled 'WES OUTLETT AND ONWARD' into a woodchipper. DarkStar: Hey! Get that camera out of here! Dick Clark: But suffice it to say that Darkstar and Clyde were somewhat less than successful. Each floundering in singles careers that were going nowhere fast.....until fate intervened. TPB: You guys are in PCW? Clyde: So yes, now that it was determined that we were in fact in PCW after around two years of being there, we were thrown together into a stable. We cut to a shot of newcomer Vampir Nosferatu wowing onlookers by displaying a copy of the Catcher in the Rye, with the author's name scribbled out and replaced with his own. Vampir: Symbolism! Dick Clark: And so it came to pass that this group of misfits came together, and on the Gold and Glory pay per view spectacular, the unthinkable happened. We cut to a shot of what appears to be Clyde wearing an Alan Kriegman Halloween mask, with Darkstar wearing a Stryfe costume. 'Alan' begs for mercy, as Stryfe closes in and hits him with the dreaded WHINY-DEPRESSED-SUICIDAL-DRUG-ADDICTED-KICK-OF-FEAR and pins him for the totally clean victory. Clyde: (Nods) Darkstar: And then we crucified him. We cut now to 'Alan' being lifted up into the rafters nailed to a giant picture of Caged Explosion. Clyde: Good times, good times. Dick Clark: Time went on though, and trouble began to form in the group. We cut to a shot of Vampir photocopying the entirety of War and Peace, signing his name on it and holding it up in front of Darkstar and Clyde with a goofy grin on his face, as they leave in disgust. Badge: (Standing in line at a soup kitchen) I never liked that Vamp guy. He was a cancer from the beginning, and HEY bitch, that's MY FUCKING SLOP!!!!!! Dick Clark: Though Vampir Nosferatu's complete lack of regard for originality was glaring, the band of misfits continued to ride the wave of success regardless. Darkstar: Well shit, we couldn't very well come out and say 'look everyone, one third of our stable is the bastard child of Drake Maxwell and Count Chocula, let's call the whole thing off' could we? I mean what were we doing before we got the band together? Trying to recruit the Preacher? Darkstar glares at Clyde, who merely looks on. Dick Clark: Originally the plans were for Stryfe to be the sacrificial lamb, and for his world title to be divided three ways amongst the true members of the Dark Age. Various shots of Stryfe jobbing to countless men, women and small animals slide by on the screen. Dick Clark: However this plan hit a snag when Stryfe jobbed out to one Jeremy 'Krayzie' Howard, losing not only his own, but the Dark Ages grip on the title in record time. Darkstar: Now really, we all knew Stryfe spent more time on his back in a fight than Aimz did on a date, that shouldn't have been a shocker. Clyde: Bah, we were supposed to do a run-in.. Isn't that what all evil stables do, run-ins to prevent a title switch? Dick Clark: However due to the machinations of certain higher ups in the company, that was not to be. We cut to what appears to be a giant green booger, wearing a Raven's jersey and an 'IR SNOTBALL' beauty pagent ribbon. He sits in a beanbag chair, pulling various levers, singing 'lalala, I'm in charge, lalala' while several large weights fall on cartoon images of the Dark Age members and wacky music plays. Dick Clark: In fact due to the sheer force in which Stryfe's career hit the ground, the entire company fell apart beneath it. And when the smoke cleared, only one thing was certain. Darkstar: We were back to jobbing hell. Clyde: (Sighing) Indeed. Dick Clark: (Over a baby building a castle out of Legos) PCW's leader however would not let his company sink so easily. The baby's foot stomps out of the top of the screen, smashing it flat while screaming 'I WANT MY BIG MAN CHAMP!' Darkstar: So then everything was rebooted, with the sole purpose in mind of giving either Big Man A or Big Man B the title. Either way we all were the loser. Clyde: (Grinning) Aha, but I had another trick up my sleeve. The scene shifts to an image of Clyde and Ed losing to Meanstreak and Ivan Stanislav in the second round of the world title tournament. Clyde: ......not that you twit. The scene shifts to Clyde jobbing to Crom Dagoth's chokeslam. Clyde: ......not that either. The scene shifts to Clyde reading his PCW rejection letter from Ryan Knox. Clyde: DIE!!!!!! The burned man leaps and tackles the cameraman, as we view the final match at Red October between Meanstreak and Ivan. Darkstar: So yeah, this pointless little feud was coming to an end. Conventional logic would have had Streak going over here clean, while none of us evil types were ever heard from again. Dick Clark: But that was not to be either. Again we cut to the Lego castle, which the mysterious giant baby smashes flat while screaming 'I WANT MY OLD FART WRESTLER COMEBACK!' Dick Clark: And so it came to be, Clyde gained his opportunity to once again bring his merry band of killers into the spotlight. A string was attached however.....for doing so, he would have to add an additional member to the Dark Age. We cut to Seraph, dragging a puppy dog along by the ears and muttering to himself. Darkstar: .......besides him. We then cut to a gravestone in a cemetary, while crickets chirp and the wind blows. Darkstar: Yeah, there he is. Judas Steele! A dog wanders by, lifting his leg and pissing on the grave. Dick Clark: And so Clyde, Darkstar and Vampir Nosferatu were granted the chance to assault the monster known as Meanstreak at his moment of triumph. But actually hurting the man proved somewhat tricky. The scene shifts to Clyde drawing various stick figures on a chalkboard while Darkstar voices over. Darkstar: Now here you have a guy who basically was the Incredible Hulk. He threw cars at people, he was hit by buses, for two years he REFUSED TO SELL. Clyde draws a dollar sign on the board, then puts a big 'X' through it and folds his arms with a scowl. Darkstar: That and we needed a way for Judas to blabber on in dramatic fashion about whatever mess he had planned while we figured out a way to do this, since he was sprung on us at literally the last minute. Again we cut to the dog by the grave, taking a massive crap on it. Darkstar: So, we had to come up with a plan. The original Kriegafixion was still a good idea, but it needed something extra. The scene cuts to Meanstreak nailed to an upside down cross with a knife sticking out of his eye, falling off the roof of the Kremlin like Wile E Coyote. Smaller, smaller he grows, until vanishing in a comical poof of dust. Clyde: (Shrugs) I still wasn't sure if he'd sell that, but Vampir was too busy signing his name on the Bible and calling it an original work of his to hijack the tank and run over Streak's corpse like he was supposed to. Dick Clark: The plan worked to a degree, with the Dark Age once again being back in the main event scene. However certain people were not amused. Meanstreak: (Laying on a hammock with his foot in a cast and sipping a Mai Tai in Hawaii) Aaah gra gra fuckin cunt fuckin cunt gra rah rah gra cunt kill rah. Bad Godzilla Voiceover: Those darn persons caused me great discomfort. My character was sexually violated. Never will I return, such is my shame. Unless I am given every title on Earth. OH NO!!!!!! WE MUST FLEE!!!!!! Dick Clark: With the monster departing, new blood was needed to fill the gap. Someone original, someone young, someone fresh in the people's minds..... We cut to a wrestling ring where Meanstreak lies on the mat in a coma. Into the ring slides Mr. Burns, twiddling his fingers. Mr. Burns: I'm greedy. Burns puts a foot on Streak as the ref counts to three. Mr. Burns: (Taking his title) I'm greedy. Crowd: (Boos) Mr. Burns: I'm greedy. Dick Clark: (Over an image of a speeding train) With the title lost, the Dark Age had come to a screeching halt. The train stops abruptly, causing a screaming engineer who somewhat resembles Zurick to fly out of the front and splatter horribly on the tracks. Darkstar: Well, I think even old man Ty.....Burns knew nothing good could come out of that reign. So he did the best thing for us all. The scene shifts to Clyde, Darkstar, Syko the Goblin and Stryfe standing next to Badge in the soup kitchen, while Vampir hunches nearby moving a homeless man's hand along a book and forging his signature. Dick Clark: When Behind the Murder returns, we'll take a look at the reboot that shook PCW to its core.....and just why in the name of hell itself a Goblin was included in their ranks. Darkstar and Clyde: BAH!!!!!!
