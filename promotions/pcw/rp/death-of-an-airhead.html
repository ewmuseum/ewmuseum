Death of an airhead

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday, December 21, 2002 We find ourselves in an open field in Pretty Prairie, Kansas. Out there, a young man and a young woman, both of whom are teenagers, walk through the flowers hand in hand. The guy, wearing a black top, jeans, and sporting a small bulge in his pants, continually looks over at the girl and smirks. The girl, wearing tight jeans and a belly-bearing top, looks completely spaced out. Her blonde hair flows back in the gentle breeze. The guy lets go of the girl's hand and instead puts his arm around her waist. Then he slowly slides it down to her ass. Just as he does, the girl stops and turns to face him. Girl: Uhh... Scotty, like... where are we? The guy hangs his head and sighs. Scott: I don't know. We're alone. I know that much. Girl: Are we lost or something? Scott: No, Em, we're not lost. Emily: Oh. Okie Dokey! The girl, whose name is Emily, smiles and spins around. She starts walking again. However, the girl walks about 10 feet before Scott places his hand on her ass. Emily jumps a little and turns around. Emily: Scotty! Scott has a big grin on his face. Scott: What? Emily: Someone, like, grabbed my butt! Scott: I know. Emily: Well, like, go find the guy who did it and, like, beat him up. Defend my honor... or something. Scott just sighs at the airhead girl. He looks down at her chest and gets a perverted smile on his face. Scott: Let's do it. Emily: What? Beat him up, like, together? Scott: No! Let's fuck! Let's fuck right out here my little fuck ferret! Emily: Fuck ferret? Like... what's a fuck ferret? Scott: It's a sexy nickname I made for you. Emily: Wow! I gots a nickname! YAY ME!! Emily jumps up and down in delight... clapping her hands for some unknown reason. Scott, wearing a giant grin on his face, stares at her breastes as they bounce up and down with her. Scott: Let's do it right out here. Emily stops bouncing. Scott: Nobody is watch... Before Scotty can finish his sentence, a giant skeletal hand reaches out and grabs his throat. We zoom out some to see that the hand belongs to The Dark Gimmick. As Scott struggles to breathe, DG stares with a blank stare. Gimmick tightens his grip until we hear a snap. Once the snap is heard, the guy falls to the ground... motionless. About three feet away, Emily looks on with a blank, unintellegent stare. She looks at the guy on the ground... then over to The Dark Gimmick. Emily: Uhh... like... uhh... who are you? Gimmick: Follow me. Gimmick starts to walk. Emily, however, doesn't budge. She just shakes her head. Emily: No way freaky looking dude. When I was a little girl, my mommy told me to, like, never follow strangers. The Dark Gimmick turns around looks at Emily. He shows no sort of emotion on his face. Gimmick: I've... got credit. Emily: Credit? YAY CREDIT!! Emily smiles and bounces with joy. Emily: Let's go! Emily grabs The Dark Gimmick's hand and starts pulling him along. For once, DG shows some emotion. From the look on Gimmick's face, he doesn't appear to be enjoying what the girl is doing. Gimmick: Stop! Emily stops pulling The Dark Gimmick. The two stop walking about ten feet from a giant pole that just 'happens' to be there. Next to the pole is a giant ladder. Gimmick: You... want credit? Emily: I want a cute little doggie to play with and cuddle with and love for and care for and... The Dark Gimmick sighs. Gimmick: Stop... your babbling. Climb... that ladder. Emily: Okies! The bubbly teenage girl bounces towards the ladder. The Dark Gimmick just looks down and shakes his head in disgust and aggitation. Gimmick: This bitch... better hurry up. Emily climbs up what appear to be a 20-25 foot tall ladder. She stands at the top rung of the ladder. Emily: Lookit! I'm, like, at the top! Emily looks down at Dark Gimmick and waves. Gimmick just sighs. Gimmick: See... the rope? Emily looks around up there. She then grabs a rope that's attached to the pole. Emily: Here it is! Emily holds up the rope for Gimmick to see. Gimmick: See the giant hole? Put... the hole... around your neck. Do it... then get credit. Emily: Okies! Emily puts the rope around her neck. The second she does, the hole closes... choking her. The ladder then tips over. The sky starts to darken as Emily struggles to get the rope from around her neck. The Dark Gimmick looks up at the scene and shows a small, sinister grin. After 30-45 seconds, the struggling stops. Emily just hangs there as day turns to night. Fade to black
