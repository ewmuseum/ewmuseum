The Search Is On- Part 1

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-02 ::It’s a Tuesday night in Atlanta, Georgia. Just about 48 hours since Shawn Mosely shocked almost everyone by winning the Rising Star Title. Now in his first match since the unbelievable match, he faces Andy Murray this Sunday for the very belt he just won. Now Mosely has also found a new challenge, and that’s to find Stacy, the love of his life so to say. The scene takes place inside of James Creon’s office of his huge gym, in which he owns. There sitting is Shawn, glaring at the top of the ceiling as he sits on the black leather chair and his head rolled back looking up. He’s wearing black sweat pants with a white beeder on. He sits there just glaring at the ceiling as he begins to day dream of memories past:: ----------------------Flashback-------------------- Its about 1 and a half years ago where the flashback takes place. Shawn is of course still living in his huge Palm Beach Mansion along with his ex-manager, Joe Armstrong, and his ex-girlfriend Stacy. It’s late as he is talking to Stacy in his living room. “I just don’t get it Stace. You’ve been mad at me for a week now because I won’t visit your parents now?” Shawn says with confusion. Stacy looks up at him with tears in her eyes. “Shawn. That’s not it.” Stacy replies. Shawn’s face becomes more confused. “What is it then? Just tell me.” Shawn says as he runs his hands threw his long dirty blonde hair. Stacy doesn’t say anything for a minute or two, but then she finally begins to talk. “I just. . .I just. . .I just love you, Shawn.” Stacy replies as she wipes her tears from her cheeks. Shawn just gives a great big sigh as he gets up and goes and sits by her. He puts his right arm around her and begins to talk. “Stacy. One more time now. What is the problem?” Shawn says comly. Once again Stacy takes a few seconds to reply. “I have to tell you a secret.” Stacy replies. Shawn sighs once again in anticipation for what she will say. “I. . I. . I’m pregrnant Shawn.” Stacy stumbles to reply. “Uh. . what?” Shawn says. “I don’t know. Its been more then a month now, and I just go it checked out. I’m so scared Shawn. . . “ Stacy replies as her tears start to flow out and she leans into both of his arms. Shawn just smiles, and hugs Stacy with all he has. --------------------End of Flashback-------------------- We now see Shawn sitting back on the black leather chair with his head glaring at the ceiling and a bright smile on his face. He was just brought back in a moment of time that he once shared with Stacy. A moment in time where only they know what happened. A moment where Shawn actually thought that life couldn’t get any better. As he sits there, the door suddenly swings up as James Creon enters the room. “Hey! Shawn. What’s going on” James says loudly. Shawn jumps up quickly in shock. “ You ready for that match?” James asks. Shawn regains his thoughts and replies this time. “Yeah. Just that my mind isn’t totally on that right now, James.” Shawn replies. James turns around and looks at Shawn as he was looking for something in a draw. “Why. .what’s on your mind?” James asks as he knows exactly what Shawn is going to say. “I need Stacy. Or at least know where she is.” Shawn replies with a determined voice. James just sighs and replies. “Shawn. Please, don’t worry about that. Give it time and I will do everything.” James says. Shawn just shakes his head. “No” Shawn replies. “What?” James asks. “That isn’t good enough for me. I can handle both lives James. I need to find her.” Shawn replies. ::Before James even has enough time to reply. Shawn gets up and runs out of the room. James runs to the door and tries to stop him, but he knows he just has to let him go. Where will Shawn go next? That’s a question that will hopefully be found out. As the search for Stacy, his old girlfriend, is on. Only time will tell what’s going on with the Golden One::
