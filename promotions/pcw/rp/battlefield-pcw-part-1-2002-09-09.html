Battlefield PCW....Part 1

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-9-2002 Daybreak. The sun is just starting to peek over the horizon, and the shot encompasses the beautiful sight. The shot pulls back to show mountains in the distance and a green valley. Zooming in on the green valley, it appears that a large camp is set up. Large tents of different colors dot the landscape, and figures can be seen on the move between them. On down the shot goes and we are met with a strange sight; the men who occupy the camp are all clad in some kind of armor. The shot zooms up a path between tent rows and comes to rest on a group of four men who stand around a table. One man dominates the scene, Venneman Pendragon. He is clad in a gleaming set of chain mail armor. Atop his head rests a headpiece that is also forged of the golden mail. His fierce blue eyes burn with intensity as he studies what appears to be a map on the table. The three men who stand with him appear to be soldiers. To his left, a hawk nosed fellow with the hard stare of a seasoned soldier. This is his second in command, Richard Smith. The fellow to his left is clad in a boiled leather set of armor, and he has a scar that runs from his forehead to his chin by way of his left cheek. He wears a patch over this eye and stares coldly at the proceedings. This is the Infantry commander, Jonathan James. Across the table, clad in light chain mail armor, is a delicate looking fellow in appearance, but his eyes belie the sternness of one accustomed to the heat of battle. Pony tailed blonde hair hangs halfway down his back almost concealing the quiver of arrows strapped thereto. William Davis, commander of the Archery units. Pendragon points ecstatically at the map as he begins talking.... Venneman: We are encamped here, in the valley of Summerset. I am sure our enemy knows that we are here, as any good commander would have scouts roaming the countryside. The Dark Lord Jason is not one to be trifled with....I only wish that Skyler Welson were still here....He would know better than I the lay of the land and the mindset of mine enemy... 'It had been some time since I last found myself in the land of Gauze. Many things had changed. My friend Skyler Welson had disappeared, bested in some battle by Cravier Dannon. None knew his whereabouts, and few wished to give me any leads with which I might use to lead my force better. I only knew that Dannon did not want the Lord King Venneman Pendragon back in the land. I must say, I did not wish to be here either, but suddenly I was, with my entire army as well. All I had to go on was a tear of Parchment that read 'Keep the land safe from the uprising.....' Now, what that was to mean, I knew not. I only knew that there was a wily commander out there who would like nothing better than to best the greatest champion of good that the land has ever known. The Dark Lord Crush Jason. So called because of his skin of ebony. Supposedly his army boasts the most highly trained contingent of braggarts the world has ever seen. I could only hope they wished more than to merely hurl insults....' Pendragon stares into space for a moment, his eyes clouding over with the deep-rooted insanity that is entwined with his very mind. The three commanders look at each other uneasily until he comes back from his revelry.... Venneman: Richard. I put you in charge of the scouts. Have your men been successful in garnering information on mine enemy? Do they know me? Few in the land know me by site, merely reputation. Richard: ..... Venneman: Good Christ man! Speak up, lest demons hold thy tongue! Richard: My liege...Indeed they know thee for what thou art...The greatest living commander. The Dark Lord has enlisted the help of an old nemesis....Lyko the Joblin.... Venneman starts to chuckle a bit. Soon, the chuckle grows until he is roaring with wild laughter. Again, the men look at one another uneasily, but when their eyes fall upon Pendragon only the reflection of reverence is beheld. Finally, he calms and instantly goes on as if nothing happened.... Venneman: So. Mine enemy has come back for round two. That is not exactly what I had wished to hear. Lyko has observed me in battle...The vermin may be able to discern some of my stratagems. Hmm...No matter. None shall stand between my goal and I...The Land shall be protected...Now. Scouts report that the enemy has encamped a fairly large force here..... Pendragon points to the map and the others nod.... Richard: 'Tis good land our foe has chosen. Front scouts report that it is the high ground. He has chosen to face us on his own terms. Venneman: He has, now? Does he expect me to merely move my force from this well protected valley and attempt to march my men straight to him? That shall not happen. Richard: Then what do you propose? Venneman: I need to see the enemy.... Venneman turns from the table and hurries down the path and into a tent that bears his sigil, Golden Dragon on Blue. In a few moments he emerges wearing a black cloak with hood. Venneman: Don one as well, good Richard. The black shall blend with the stone of Ebony that makes up the mountain that Jason has selected as his headquarters.... The scene cuts to show the two cloaked men looking over a ridge. Before them stands an immense black peak. The two men peer at the immense camp of the enemy. Richard whistles as Pendragon merely stares.... Venneman: Have you ever seen an army to match it? Richard: In size, no....What chances have we? Venneman: Size is not everything, Good Richard....An army is more than size. An army is heart...Determination. Our soldiers are all volunteers. There is nothing stronger than the heart of a volunteer. How many in yonder force do ye believe to be conscripts? Forced to fight for an unjust cause for an unjust man in an unjust world? I would wager a fair amount of them are....Of course, ye have the bloodsuckers such as Lyko...Joblins always go where they think they might get something extra. It is all about the looting and pillaging for that sad group...... Pendragon stares on as a large black man clad in black armor emerges from a tent, an ensemble of gleaming black knights in tow.... Venneman: Jason....It must be. Look at the entourage! Suddenly, hoof beats can be heard. Pendragon and Richard both slide down the ridge and come face to face with a fresh faced young man in the clothes of a courier.... Young man: Lord King Pendragon, an important message your way comes..... He hands Pendragon a scroll. Pendragon unrolls the paper and frowns at what he sees..... Venneman: Hmmm....A match with Rush Mason, to be refereed by Bertha...Syko the Goblin as a special enforcer....BAH! Why must the whelp always be thrust into my spotlight? Can I have nothing as mine alone? No matter. It shall give him a chance to gaze upon he who shall have his title back. Richard: Rush Mason sir? That name sounds oddly familiar.... Venneman: Indeed...One more carcass Kannon has thrown at me....Funny how art imitates life, is it not? Richard stares quizzically at Venneman as the scene fades............ ***TO BE CONTINUED******
