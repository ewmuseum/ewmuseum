Date: (#2 for FF) “Going Over The Info” I sat on the plane that was taking me to the site of my first PCW match, in a date that was to be announced. I was against Ali Khadaffi, a tough black guy who hated everything about the world. His finisher was simply called “Fuck Rag”, shows his intelligent level, huh? The plan I was on was pretty plain. I was the only one beside the personal pilot that the king had ordered. There were four seats, for extra people that weren’t occupied. Other than that, there were windows beside each seat to look out into the sky and the ground below and up at the front was a curtain, leading to the pilot’s area. Basically, the trip was boring. I sat perched on the seat was occupying. The seat was leaned back a bit so I could rest easily. There wasn’t very much for me to do. Behind me was all my storage that I would need. I basically had every pair of clothes I owned, that was it. Beside the clothes was all of my equipment. A specially-made wooden bow with a small case for arrows, a sword that lay inside a special, safety pouch, and my fencing equipment. If there were someone on the plane with me, I could practise any of the things behind me with them but lucky me, I was stuck to reading some newspapers. What were the newspaper’s about you ask? Well, before I left the castle where I had been occupied in for the past four years, the king had gave me various newspaper articles and jot notes of the amulet I had to find. All the newspaper’s there had the same information, just in different words. I scoured through the articles once more, grabbing one that I had only read twice and red it again. It was from a newspaper, don’t know from when but it said: Amulet Stolen Yesterday, newspaper’s report that one of the seven precious amulets were stolen from King Mathers. Mathers, newly appointed king checked his collection of special amulets that were made for his father and passed down to him when he noticed that one amulet was missing from the collection. Where the amulet is, we don’t know but as so as information comes, it will be posted here. I put the article back into the folder that held all of the information and took out the article behind it, from the same newspaper, an update on the amulets. Amulet Important One An update to the amulet incident where one of the seven amulets were stolen from King Mathers collection. The amulet that was stolen a few nights back is now said to be the one amulet that has ten times the power of all six other amulets. What this means, not many people know. All we do know is that now, the amulet that was stolen, is much more important than recently stated. How will the King react to the missing amulet? More news as it comes out way. I sighed, putting the article back into the folder with the others. All these newspapers and none of them can get a story out that is different. One thing I noticed was that none of the papers but one said anything about the whereabouts of the amulet. One lone paper out of all of them had information about the location. The story was the front page of the newspaper. The name of the newspaper was unreadable, a Chinese or Japanese name, I couldn’t tell. Anyways, the top story of the newspaper read: King’s Amulet Stolen; South Korea Sea When I had first read the caption, I had gotten excited because it might be around Korea and the PCW events were being held all around Korea! Then, I read on, Authorities today around China and Japan have been buzzing at the loss of the one amulet that King Mathers, an English King held with him. The amulet, which was stolen last week from the King’s compound, was nowhere to be found but now, reports are telling various sources of this newspaper that Japanese and Chinese government leaders are cheering about the stolen amulet, bringing to the belief that Chung Won Faut, the great mastermind has taken the amulet. That part didn’t seem like very much information and I’ll be honest, it definitely wasn’t. The thing that got my attention though, was the picture that was in the centre of the page. The picture had a man. The man was Chinese or Japanese, I could never really tell the difference between them. The man had wrinkles on his face, like he was in his late forties. The man was wearing a black business suit and it looked that he carried a briefcase. In the background behind him, it looked like there were two men that were protecting him with big guns. I guessed that the picture was of Chung Won Faut. Underneath the picture was an unknown caption. For some reason, this writing was the only writing that was in Japanese or Chinese, and since I didn’t know either language, I was puzzled. That was all the whole thing said. I sighed, putting it back into the folder. I looked out the window and saw that the private airplane was landing in a smaller airport. I stood up, ready to leave the plain. As I got up, I thought of Ali Khadaffi and what pain he would take next Friday. I promised that he would pay and I don’t break promises. Another promise I will make is this: I promise that I will track down the amulet, AND BRING IT HOME !
