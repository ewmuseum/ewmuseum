Chapter VIII: How To Take Over A Town With A Famous Speech...

21 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-2-03 ::Introduction:: Mark’s first chance at a title was blown last Friday when he didn’t win the Extreme title but he looked extreme enough. He gets another chance this Friday against Skitzo and Bob Vurdo to try to take that title away from Skitzo before March Madness. Mark knew that Duzza left this company because he couldn’t take the heat that Mark was dishing out to him. The pain was too great to stay in the business and Mark now needs to find someone else to work with in the fed. ::End Introduction:: Chapter VIII: How To Take Over A Town With A Famous Speech… Mark walks more into the city as the sun breaks out from beneath the dirty gray clouds. People are starting to follow Mark into town across their lawns and it just looks as if the whole community is acting like Jesus is back in town. “I am going to change this city around.” Mark says as he looks back at who is following him. “I am going to make people believe that I am right for this town!” “We love you Mark!” The crowd says in as a unison. Mark is walking with his head high and a smile on his face as he enters the main part of the city boundaries. The people are still following behind him as he leads them and he sees the town square ahead. People are looking out of their apartment windows and out of their business windows at the commotion that Mark is causing. Dogs are getting routed up from all of the people being in the streets. “Come follow me to the promise land of the whites.” Mark says as he is proud to stand and see the flags waving. “We follow.” The crowd chants that include men and women. Mark goes around the town square and he goes up to the memorial in the middle. He sees that it is of some African American people and at the top is a famous political speaker for the black community. Mark looks up at him and he gives the statue the middle finger as he is looking at a real person. The crowd behind Mark puts up their middle finger like Mark did. Mark then goes away from the statue and he holds his hand up to signal to the crowd that he doesn’t need them to follow him to where he is going. A podium is set up as this thing looked like it was planned. Mark heads up to the podium and he steps on the wooden steps so that he is over the podium. He fixes the microphone as it makes a screeching sound of the speakers. “We are gathered here today to celebrate a very special date in the history of this town.” Mark starts to speak as he sees the attention of the crowd is very good. “This day is going to change your lives somehow and someway. I am the new leader in this town and all of you will do what I say.” The crowd of people start to walk in closer and closer to the podium as people have their heads hanging out of their opened windows. The mayor is sitting down on the other side of the town square with a big fat cigar hanging out his mouth. He takes a puff as the end of the cigar gets red hot and then he takes the cigar out of his mouth. He leans his head back and he blows a cloud of smoke in the air. He looks over in Mark’s direction to listen to more of his bull shit as the mayor thinks. “Who here believes that the white race is dominant above all the other races?” Mark asks as he speaks into the microphone. “Who here believes that the black race is shit?” “We do!” The crowd says as the sound of one with their voices combined. Mark is still talking as a police car comes rolling up from behind Mark on that street to the town square. It looks as if it is running out of gas and finally comes to a halt as it backfires out through the muffler. No one hears it as Mark is talking loudly over all the outside noises. Out come two colored cops as on the side of their car reads Raleigh Police Department. They have evil grins on their face as their uniforms look like there are worn. “There is that racist motherfucker dawg.” Says one of the cops to the other as they are both together walking towards Mark. “Lets get him and finally take care of him for fuckin life.” The other cop says as he lost his hat in Raleigh from chasing Mark already a few days earlier. They head up to meet Mark from behind and he is not paying attention to what is behind him. Only to what is in front of him and the good of his crowd. The two cops are coming near and are on the outside of the town square. They are taking one step at a time to try and not to startle anyone around them. “So as I said before there are going to be some changes.” Mark says as he is not knowing what is going on behind him. “Like how it was before the day Rosa Parks didn’t get up. Like those days where there were separate bathrooms for the colored and white people.” Mark stops briefly as he can feel people behind him. He looks down into the silver metallic mic and he sees two black figures creeping up behind him. Mark looks up with his eyes and gives an evil look at the crowd. He nods his head back and the crowd nods their heads forward as they are speaking a sign language. “Now!” A cop says to the other as they reach for Mark. As they both reach for him, Mark hops over the podium and he starts to run through the crowd of people as they run right behind him. The cops radio in more help and within minutes there are cop cars pulling within distance of the crowd. Mark is leading the crowd as he is holding up his middle fingers as he runs down a hill out of the town. Mark looks up and he sees a small little airport ahead with its orange flag blowing in the mild wind. “There is my escape from this evil world right now.” Mark says to himself as he looks at the airport like its his dream. Mark reaches the airport as the crowd behind him has split up into different parts of the town from the police cars running through the crowd. There are about two cars chasing Mark as their bumper is scraping against the skin of Mark. He is getting worn out from his right leg hurting as he turns into the airport entrance. “Hey sir what would you like?” A secretary at the front desk asks as Mark enters. “Just get me on a damn flight right now.” Mark says in-between taking breaths of air. “I don’t give a fuck which one just one out of this country.” “Alright sir but its going to cost you some cash.” The lady says as she is typing in her computer to find the next flight. Mark leans up to get more air and he reaches deep into his front jean pocket. He feels for the wad of cash that he had earlier in the day so that he can find out how much money he really does have. He grabs it and he pulls that out of his front pocket. He slams that on the desk as he leans his elbows on it as well. “Where will that get me?” Mark asks as he still has a little bit of money stored in his back pocket. “Anywhere you would like.” The lady says. “Fuck it.” Mark says as he goes towards the only gate they have there. “Sir you can’t leave yet.” The lady says as she is getting Mark’s flight booked. Mark doesn’t care and he is just speed walking as the cops run into the entrance to the airport. The lady looks up and she notices the cops and she hides the wad of money Mark slammed on the desk. The cops look very strict and the lady backs away from her computer and holds her hands up. “No mam you are not in trouble.” One of the colored cops says as he is trying to look at sweet and cool. “You may put your hands down.” “All we wanna know if you have seen a fuckin white head racist in here.” The other cop asks as they are both coming up to the desk. The lady doesn’t say anything all she does is points towards the gate as the door is closing. The cops can see Mark through the glass as they are running towards the gate, but as they reach outside all they see is Mark getting loaded in the plane. They start to sprint towards the plane that is just sitting there. Mark closes the door as the cops come up to the door and pound on it. “Where you wanna go?” The pilot asks. “I don’t care just get me out of here.” Mark says as the plane starts to move forward and eventually off the ground.
