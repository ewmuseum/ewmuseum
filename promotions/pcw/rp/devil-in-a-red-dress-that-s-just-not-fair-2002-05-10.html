Devil in a red dress? That's just not fair!

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/10/02 Things have gone to hell in a hand basket since this past Sunday Sabotage. A couple of complete and utter imbeciles went on a rampage that can only be described as premeditated retardation. Flaymin Aymin and El Diablo ransacked, ravaged, restructured, ran over, and otherwise reduced the Chrysler Center in Ann Arbor, Michigan to near rubble. And all because of a stupid mask? Or was it over a whore? Maybe hair? Who knows, and does the CEO care? The simple answer to that dumb question is a resounding NO! All Tyler knew was that in one week he would be getting HIS PCW World Championship back from Snow. This minor issue could not distract him…..not now. The repercussions have been felt by the CEO of PCW almost since the show ended. The proprietors of the arena were screaming in one ear, backstage managers were screaming in his other ear, medical technicians were behind the arena people screaming, and not to be outdone the wrestlers were screaming in his ear as well. Mere minutes after the total devastation that took place, the CEO was being bombarded by phone calls from lawyers and ambulance chasers seeking large sums of money for clients that they didn’t even have yet. Fucking bloodsuckers. Good thing for Tyler that he has Dr. Rupert Healey as his assistant. The good doctor brings a rather large group of bloodsuckers to the bargaining table as well, and they do an unusually good job of getting what they want. The CEO deferred the petty injury issues and other distracting matters to Healey to handle. He has bigger and dumber things to take care of….namely Snow and Big Alan Steele. But the problems just won’t go away…….and a new problem arises that the CEO must deal with. “The King of the wrestling world should NOT have to deal with this BULLSHIT!!” greets us as the scene fades into view. The CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson stands behind the large oak desk in his massive executive office in PCW Headquarters, screaming into the receiver of a phone. He is frazzled to say the least, face red and pulsing with veins on the verge of exploding. “I have a World Title match this Sunday and they want to file multi-million dollar lawsuits against me?! WHAT THE FUCK?!?!” The CEO quickly slams that phone receiver down on the desk and raises another one to his other ear. He is no more happy with whoever is on the other end of this line than the other. “I don’t care about little Johnny with the broken leg and permanent brain damage. Offer them some money to shut them up.” His face contorts into a vile looking scowl as he listens. “They don’t want money?” asks Tyler, nearly in shock. “What kind of worthless waste of skin doesn’t want money? Are they some sort of religious fanatics or something?” A short pause as the CEO listens some more. “They want WHAT?!” Tyler moves the phone’s receiver away from his ear and holds it out in front of him, then screams into it. “THERE IS NO WAY IN HELL THAT I AM GOING TO APOLOGIZE TO THAT LITTLE SNOT NOSED BRAT AND HIS FAMILY FOR A LITTLE BROKEN BONE AND A SLIGHT CONCUSSION!!” SLAM goes that phone receiver down on the desk. “Maybe if he was dead I might send them a letter with five bucks in it,” mutters Tyler to himself as he raises the first receiver to his ear once again. “Listen here, Healey,” barks the CEO authoritatively. “I need you to mop all of this stuff up and fast. I can’t let all this shit get in the way of me kicking Snow and Steele’s asses this Sunday. Stall, reschedule, pigeonhole, postpone, do whatever you have to do to brush this all under the rug until after the pay per view. After I win MY title back then I can focus on this piddly stuff.” We can’t hear what is being said on the other line, but Tyler gets a puzzled look on his face. “You’re sending WHO over?” No sooner does the sentence roll off the frustrated tongue of the CEO than there is a knock on his office door. Tyler looks at the door in amazement, looks at the phone, then looks at the door again. “I think he’s here. I’ll call you back.” SLAM goes that phone down on the base, followed by Tyler trying to make himself look presentable. He straightens tie and pops his cuffs, then heads toward the door. On the wall beside the CEO are several pictures of him with the PCW World Title as well as some pictures of Media. Strangely the faces of Chet King, Dan Hollywood, and Mr. Showtime are all cut out and replaced by the image of the CEO himself, seemingly inferring that he WAS Media. Tyler reaches the door and pulls it open, revealing an extremely sexy women in a red dress. Her brown hair dangles from her head in curls, ending down around the middle of her back. The fire red lipstick accentuates her supple lips, which have her tongue running across them at the present time. The red mini dress hugs every inch of her perfect body, ending just below her ass. The pair of finely toned legs run sexily down to a set of red stiletto heels. The CEO clears his throat to speak to the unexpected surprise. “Hmm-mm…..how may I help you, hot stuff?” As Tyler looks the woman up and down with elevator eyes, she looks longingly up at him. “Doctor Healey sent for me,” she replies in a soft and sensuous voice. “He said you could use my assistance.” “You bet I can,” coos the CEO, a broad smile forming across his face. “Come on in!” Tyler moves aside and guides the young damsel into the office with his hand, watching her ass as she passes him. A mischievous smirk sneaks across the woman’s lips as she strides confidently into the office. The CEO looks around outside the office before closing the door and walking to the front of his desk. “Please, sit down,” offers Tyler as he points to one of the comfortable black leather chairs that are positioned in front of his large desk. The woman complies and sits, crossing her legs seductively as she does so. The CEO takes a seat on the front edge of his desk, never taking his eyes off of the immaculate prize before him. “Now, what is it exactly that you are going to do for me?” The woman looks up at Tyler with a flicker in her eyes. “I’m going to take care of all your problems, Mr. Nelson.” The giddy CEO can barely contain himself. “I like the sound of that! Where do you want to do it, the desk?” An overzealous CEO stands and spins around, beginning to move various objects from the desktop. He is halted just after he gets started. “No, no silly,” says the mysterious woman softly. “Your OTHER problems, like that little episode on Sunday.” A disappointed CEO turns back around and retakes his seat on the front edge of the desk. “Oh that,” pouts Tyler. “I can handle that, but I can think of a few things that you CAN take care of.” The CEO offers a flirty wink, which causes a sheepish grin from the woman. “I don’t think you understand, Mr. Nelson,” offers the woman. “Doc Healey called me fix everything for you.” Tyler looks down at the woman with a skeptical eye. “And just how do you plan to do that? Unless you are going to come up with a whole shitload of cash that I don’t have to pay back then I don’t know how you can help me.” “Details, details….,” dismisses the woman. “We’ll go over that later. First things first, we need to talk about my fee.” “Well,” says Tyler as he stands up and moves around behind his desk. “I don’t usually pay more than a grand, but since you are one grade-A piece of ass you can name your price.” “Anything?” inquires the woman. “That’s what I said, isn’t it?” quips the CEO. “I was hoping you would say that,” purrs the woman as she crosses her legs to the other side. “On your desk is a contract. Look it over and sign it on the last page.” Tyler chuckles and begins to look down on his desk. “But there isn’t any contract on my des….k.” Tyler is dumbfounded by the contract that has suddenly appeared on his desk. He looks up at the woman who only offers an innocent smile, followed by a nod toward the packet of legal papers. “How did…,” wonders Tyler aloud. There is no response from the woman. Tyler starts to read the first page and is startled at what he sees. “What the hell is this?” asks Tyler sternly. “Just read it,” says the woman somewhat forcefully. “Everything you need to know is in there.” The CEO of PCW looks the contract over carefully, flipping from page to page with growing anger. A few snide looks up at the devilish woman accompany the scowl on Tyler’s face. The woman simply sits in the chair, staring at the CEO and seemingly enjoying his elevating rage. “This is blackmail!” shouts the CEO as he flings the contract in the woman’s face. She is unphased and doesn’t flinch. “Is there a problem?” Tyler stares a hole through the woman, his eyes piercing her. “Hell yeah there’s a problem! That whole contract is a sham!” The woman sighs as she plucks the contract off of the floor. She carefully places the papers back on Tyler’s desk and looks him in the eyes. The CEO is taken aback as her eyes seem to turn a hue of red, much like the color of her dress. Tyler quickly collects himself. “Don’t try that shit with me again,” warns Tyler confidently. “It may have worked the first time but certainly not now. I don’t have time for these games.” For the first time in the visit it seems that the CEO knows exactly who he is dealing with. We haven’t heard any mention of the woman’s name, nor any familiarity between the two up until now. “Hey, I had to try right?” asks the woman playfully. “Now, what’s the problem with that contract?” “You are asking for too much this time,” complains Tyler in what can almost be deemed as a whine. “I thought I gave up a lot the last time, and now you want all of this? It’s highway robbery!” “Don’t like being beaten at your own game, eh?” The woman smiles and leans forward in her chair. “As always there is some room for negotiation….” Tyler smiles slightly. “....but not much.” Her smile disappears. Tyler’s does, too. The CEO reaches forward and grabs the contract from it’s resting place on the desktop. He rotates it and begins to look it over once again. “Lemme get this straight….you Australia, Jamaica, and Southern Montana in exchange for eliminating all of these lawsuits and anything else associated with that debacle on Sunday?” The woman simply nods with a devious smile on her sensuous lips. Her eyes squint evilly as the smile turns into a grin. Tyler raises a thinking finger to his chin and rubs while looking down at the contract. “You know I don’t have the kind of money I used to since that fucking Mexican and that Goddamn hillbilly screwed me out of everything,” says Tyler remorsefully. “I just don’t know if I can come up with everything you are asking for.” “That’s too bad, Ty,” says the woman feigning sadness. “I guess we won’t be able to do business this time around.” “But I gave you those great sweat shops in Brazil, Guatemala, and El Salvador last time,” grovels the CEO. “Doesn’t that count for something?” “Those have been great….the pain and suffering I have inflicted on those people has brought hours of satisfaction,” replies the woman. “But I need something new…something different. I need a new challenge, because to tell you the truth, third world sweat shops just don’t do it for me anymore.” “Hmmmm…….” Tyler returns to his thought process. We can almost hear the wheels turning inside of his greedy head. The woman taps her fingers impatiently on the arm of her chair, looking rather disinterested. Suddenly Tyler snaps his fingers loudly. “I’ve got it!” exclaims the CEO. The woman looks at Tyler with a puzzled expression. “I thought Ed had ‘IT’?” “Fuck that son of a bitch! I’m talking about something that should pacify even your most evil of impulses,” says Tyler cryptically. “I’m listening…,” the woman says cautiously. “I thought of a deal for the ages,” says the CEO like a child on Christmas day. “Did you just say Christmas?” snarls the woman. Sorry. “How would you like to get your hands on the land of the Gods?” offers Tyler gleefully. “You’ve peaked my interest,” concedes the woman. “What are we talking here?” Tyler almost floats around to the front of the desk, standing in front of the woman. He places his arms out in front of him as if he is making some sort of great offering. “This Sunday I face Big Alan Steele and Jason Snow for MY PCW World Heavyweight championship,” starts Tyler. The woman interrupts the CEO. “I know. There’s some heavy action on the toga wearing freak in Vegas. Odds are 2:1 in his favor.” Tyler brushes the comment off and continues. “Anyway, the toga wearing freak is the supposed son of Zeus or something like that. He thinks he’s descendant of the Greek God’s,” explains Tyler intently. “I think he’s full of shit myself, but what if it were true? What if he is from Mount Olympus and is the son of the King of God’s? How would you like to get your hands on THAT piece of the pie?” Tyler looks on with anticipation as it is now the woman’s turn to contemplate. “This could be good,” says the woman. “What’s the deal?” The CEO rubs his hands together deviously. “Okay, you give me the win this Sunday and guarantee that I get MY title back, and in return I give you Olympus and I’ll throw in Canada for free.” The woman shoots Tyler a foul look. “What in the HELL would I want with Canada? Only thing that comes from there are jobbers. Snow’s from Canada, right?” She turns her nose up in disgust. “Yeah, I figured since I was going to kick his ass, too, that I would throw that in as a bonus prize,” the CEO says. “I know it’s not much, but it’s better than nothing.” “I wouldn’t be too sure about that. You might have to give me something extra to take Canada off of your hands,” demands the woman. “You sure drive a hard bargain. Okay, I’ll throw in a steak and lobster dinner from Chez Revou Foo-Foo,” concedes Tyler. “That’s the best I can do.” The woman thinks for a minute. “I thought you wanted me to take care of all these lawsuits and protect your money? This isn’t like you, Tyler.” The woman leers at the CEO skeptically. “I need a guarantee that I will get MY title back,” says Tyler. “I can’t let anyone else have it. IT’S MINE DAMNIT!” “Okay, okay…” says the woman soothingly. “I’ll take that deal. I give you one World Title match victory over Big Alan Steele and Jason Snow, you give me Mount Olympus and Canada in return.” “Don’t forget about the deal maker!” Tyler reminds the woman. “Ahh yes, how could I forget? The steak and lobster dinner. Certainly a delectable addition,” the woman says. With a snap of her fingers another contract appears on the CEO’s desk. “That should be to your liking now, Tyler.” The woman points at the contract on the desk. “Just sign it and I’ll be on my way.” The CEO spins around quickly and snatches a pen from it’s holder on the desk. He skims through the pages until he reaches the last page where he eagerly signs his name on the bottom line. The woman peers over his shoulder and gets that devilish smile on her face again. Tyler finishes signing the contract and turns around, handing it to the lovely woman. “There you go,” says Tyler as he hands the papers over. “Verrry good,” purrs the woman. “It was nice doing business with you again. Unfortunately I have to go. Saddam Hussein has been calling me all morning. Something about he and Osama having a big party for me.” She starts to head for the door when Tyler gently places a hand on her shoulder. “Are you sure you have to leave just now?” The CEO slowly turns the woman around, looking at her with lust in his eyes. She returns the gaze and her shoulders loosen. “I guess I could make them wait a little longer,” sighs the woman seductively. “It’s not like they’re going to have the party without me. I’d have the American’s find them performing oral sex on each other.” Tyler pulls the devilishly beautiful woman in the red dress closer and they embrace as the scene fades to black.
