Blind Man's Bluff

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.26.02(#2 TT, FF) “Yeah, I’m over here.” A monotonous sounding tone can be heard in Rush Mason’s voice as it comes across in the total black. A fade in begins, as more of the not-so-excited Rush is heard once again. “Oh wow. You’re almost there. This is so fun.” Speaking in such a boring tone, Rush makes the activity he’s partaking in seem boring, but as the scene fades in, we can see it’s much more than that. It’s something that seems to be a large group of people walking around inside. Rush: This fuckin’ reeks. I can’t believe the Front Office now requires community service hours or taxation. I thought that was called torture. Giving a ‘Humph’ as he watches on from his chair, Rush turns to his side to see Ray Ray walking towards him. Sitting down next to the Voodoo Villain, Ray Ray listens in as Rush remarks to no one in particular in his annoying, whiny tone. Rush: Ya know, these people could just as easily be living normal lives with stupid seeing-eye dogs. Of course, they’re wasting valuable time by being lazy. At this, Ray Ray looks up to the large group of people, and then makes a noise indicating new understanding. His voice expresses some surprise over what he has just learned, although it’s not exactly a Euclidean discovery. Ray Ray: They blind? Damn…I thought they all just found some stylish glasses and was pimpin’ together. This, of course, brings a sigh from Rush, who doesn’t even to bother to whine about the curse of a dumb manager. He simply continues his bitching and moaning about having to do “community service.” Rush: After I got through with The End, I was hoping that the only time I’d interact with those freaks was tripping them in busy streets! The large manager just nods in agreement as the two men continue to look on, watching the many blind folks prodding with their walking sticks, as well as some who are just sitting, taking in the situation around them through other senses. Ray Ray: Dawg, I’m hungry. You think them blind people would notice if I just took me a little sumpin’ sumpin’? Mason shakes his head at the use of Ebonics, obligatory for any man of stereotypical stature such as Ray Ray. Rush dismisses his employee with the whisk of a hand and Ray Ray walks over to a blind man sitting on the bench and he begins to nibble on the man’s sandwich, which is near by. Rush: Ugh…my head hurts again. I think those people who used to be my janitors discovering the use of modern tools wasn’t such a good thing. Returning with a large sandwich in his right hand and a Gatorade in his left, Ray Ray chomps loudly, sitting back down once again. Rush looks over, quite surprised that his manager was able to steal food from even a blind person. Rush: You just stole from the seeing-impaired? Stopping in mid-bite, Ray Ray thinks for a moment, not totally comprehending what seeing-impaired is, but then gives a nod, and as Rush continues to stare, his signature shrug. Ray Ray: What could I say? The sandwich is good, dawg. Rush just turns his head, looking back on the large mass of blind people. Suddenly, Rush bolts up out of his chair, an idea striking him as his face begins to glow with an evil grin. He makes his way to the middle of the floor of the open room where Rush:Okay, everyone, time to play a game. Having to follow their “instructor’s” words, the people congregate, listening to Rush as he announces the rules of the “game.” Rush: It’s called Blind Man’s Bluff, I’m sure you know the rules. ….GO! With that, Rush begins to walk around, tripping random people and cackling evilly, as Ray Ray just watches, frozen in part shock, part boredom. A lethal combination. Rush: Yeehaw! I feel like a hick with seventeen sisters! I’m in the arms of Bon Dieu, Ray Ray, sonny! After a few more minutes of pushing the many people around and now watching as a mass of people writhe on the floor of the building, Rush flashes a large grin, while calling back to Ray Ray. Rush: Let’s go, buddy, our work here is done. Ray Ray, still partly in shock, looks down at the blind people, then back up at his sandwich as he follows behind, asking a question of Rush as the scene fades out to black… “Dawg…how they gonna get up?” “I dunno. Don’t they have seeing-eye legs?”
