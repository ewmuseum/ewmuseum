Fighting Through Sickness

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 14, 2002 “Is Jack worse today?” Johnny Heart’s voice echoed from behind the door. The opening and closing of the front door brought in a swirl of dust from the porch outside. There came, too, a breath of sharp, humid June air to tired little Christina Livingston where she paused, foot on a stair, the tray steadied in her hand, looking back at her boyfriend’s partner. 'No. He’s feeling a little better, but I don’t know if he’ll be ready in time for Fusion,' the voluptuous blonde replied. “Oh, well, I don’t think that will be a problem...I mean, either one of us could take that Flaymin Aymin fellow alone, but we’re in a handicapped match with the aussie,” Heart said. You see, Jack Valentine had encountered the flu, summer flu if you will. Normally the Heartbreaker can pull through many obstacles if it means his wrestling reputation is on the line, but this time it was different...this time it was more serious. That’s why we haven’t heard from the Heartbreakers in a little over a week. Luckily, they haven’t been booked in any matches up until now, in a match ordered by The Phantom Booker for some strange reason, to fight Flaymin Aymin. Did the Phantom Booker have some hatred towards Flaymin Aymin? Did the Phantom Booker think that The Heartbreakers couldn’t take Flaymin Aymin in a one on one match? Is the Phantom Booker looking for new members to his uprising group, The Phantom Phive? Whatever the reason, The Heartbreakers were not looking forward to the event on Fusion. Christina carried the tray up the stairs, slowly, as she had been worn out from the past week of slaving for Jack Valentine. On the tray was a bowl of soup, and nice big glass of water, and about 12 different colored pills...probably to make him feel better...at least for the night. As she turned the corner to the long hallway, she paced herself step by step as she walked down the hall. Somewhat of a loud, screaming voice could be heard coming from down the hall. Christina put the tray down and sprinted down to Jack’s room to see what was the matter. When she got there, Jack had a patch on his eye, a PINK! bandana tied around his head, and he was standing, like a pirate, on his PINK! satin sheets. “What in the hell are you doing Jack?” Christina screamed. Jack didn’t answer for what seemed to be an eternity. He then looked to the sky and began rambling on again. “Father, surely this day's march is done. It is time to rest, and eat, and sleep. If we press onward now, we cannot see our steps; and will not that be against the word of the PINK!, who bids us not to put confidence in the legs of a man?” Jack stated, in a very odd manner. “What are you talking about Jack? Pressing onward? Word of the PINK!? Have you lost your mind,” Christina said as she began walking closer, and closer to Jack Valentine. Jack stopped, and as if he could only hear Christina, he said: “Halt, who goes there?” Christina stopped stiff in her path and replied: “What kind of bullsh*t are you pulling Jack? Get your ass off of the bed and let’s go...we’ve got a match for you to win tonight.” After flailing his arms around for a bit, Jack froze in silence...and in motion. He just stood there like a statue. “Johnny! Johnny! Get up here...something’s wrong with Jack,” Christina screamed at the top of her lungs. Johnny didn’t treat this as a big surprise. He paced himself up the stairs and swaggered down the hallway. “I know something’s wrong with him woman...he’s sick!” Johnny sarcastically stated. “No Johnny, look at him...he’s lost his mind,” Christina said, as she pointed to Jack. Johnny paused, and then turned around and laughed. “It’s probably all of those pills your giving him...you’ve got him more drugged up than Cowabunga Connection,” Johnny laughed. Suddenly, Jack awoke from his frozen state and began rambling on once again. “Courage, brothers, and forward yet a little! The PINK! will light us presently, and the path is plain. Well know I that the journey is weary; and my own heart wearies also for the home in the PINK!, where those I love are keeping feast this Friday night. But we have work to do before we feast tonight on victory. For this is the time to destroy, and the heathen people of the ring are gathered at base of the ring to worship, PINK! Strange things will be seen there, and deeds which make the soul PINK! But we are sent to lighten their darkness; and we will teach our kinsmen to keep the PINK! holy,” Jack said, in an ever so long and pointless speech. “What the hell is he talking about Johnny?” Christina nervously asked. “He’s perfectly fine,” Johnny smiled, “Just don’t give him any more of those pills...you know...on second thought, it may be a good idea for him to get some of those pills...he seems a little more dedicated to the cause.” “The cause?” Christina asked. “He going to go into Fusion and fight for his colors...” Johnny began. “When did you become so patriotic. Should I wear a red, white and blue bikini to the ring tonight?” Christina interrupted. “Bikini?!? Red, White & Blue?!? How tacky. How can you be so ignorant...it’s all about the PINK!” Johnny proudly stated. When Johnny said the word PINK!, Jack shifted his head towards him. “Fine young soldier you are my lad...a fine young soldier. You shall do the PINK! proud along my side tonight as we destroy anyone who supports the colors of the enemy,” Jack nobly said. Johnny smiled. “You need to get some rest before tonight Jack, you need some rest,” Johnny tried to calm him down, while still laughing at his antics. Christina didn’t find it amusing whatsoever. Instantaneously, Jack fell into a deep slumber, mumbling out every now and then....PINK!....PINK!....PINK!
