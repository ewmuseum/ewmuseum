Tim's first heel promo

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Tim's first heel promo Featuring: Tim Walden Date: March 12 (The scene opens on a bedpost, the remainder of the room outside the frame but for the post and a bit of the bed below it..The camera slides smoothly to the right of the bed, where the numbers on the black digital clock begin to come into view..a 7, then the all-familiar colon, followed up by 5 and 9...They hold steady, for what seems like an eternity, but in reality is only about 45 seconds of wasted television time, before the time turns over to 8:00...And nothing happens..A hand reaches forward and quickly presses a couple of buttons, bringing up a new time of 9:45, immediately rolled back like some cosmic time machine, first hopping back an hour to 8:45, then sliding forward in the minutes, from 45 through 00, before stopping at 8:03...Then, another button is pressed, and suddenly the alarm goes off..An arm quickly slams into it, and the camera pulls over to see Tim Walden as the bearer of that arm under cover of sheets with a case of bedhead, and what appears to be another form to his left...He immediately looks at the cameraman angrily.) Tim: DAMN YOU, YOU MO*BLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP*ING SON OF A B*BLEEP*!!! I NEED MY GO*BLEEP*MN REST FOR THAT BA*BLEEP*D MEXI TONIGHT!!! NOW LEAVE ME ALONE BEFORE I KICK YOUR *BLEEP* ASS!!! (The camera turns a 180 and vamooses, and the scene fades to black, then fades back in on a different locale...This just happening to be a city street, as per Waldenpromo rule: One street-starting promo per week, so Tim can wander into some building and make his minor interactions with people before he goes into his typical ranting and raving trash-talk...The outfit du jour is an odd clash, with Tim wearing a harsh red T-shirt in addition to his blue jeans, somewhat baggier than usual..He keeps walking, the camera moving to his side..He turns to speak...And immediately walks into a lamp-post. He no-sells, unfazed.) Tim: STUPID DAMN LIGHTPOST! GET IN MY *BLEEP*IN' WAY, WILL YOU?!?!? (And with that, Tim kicks the post, and nothing happens...Until he kicks it again, and grabs his foot in mild pain.) Tim: Damn inanimate objects, think they own the freakin' sidewalk! (Tim keeps walking forward, as a park replaces the buildings on the left...He quickly notes the bench that sits just beyond the bus stop just to the right of the corner, though it's atypical of bus stops to exist to that side of the bus stop, since after a turn the bus would already be slowed..Blame the city planner!) Tim: Hey, let's go over to that bus stop, I've got something to say right now... Cameraman (sighing): But this camera's heavy, and we just passed a bench a couple of seconds ago! Tim: Don't whine at me, you don't have to fight some worthless *bleep* nutcase who bounces around the ring for minutes on end while the crowd does all his damned talking tonight! I swear, the guy's not much more than a crazed jumping bean! Now look at this, we've got to run just to make the freakin' damn walk light! (Tim rushes forward across the street, as the orange 'DON'T WALK' light blinks on and off, the camera bobbing frantically behind him, further and further behind him...Quickly, he dashes over to take a seat at the bus stop bench, while the cameraman finally reaches the sidewalk and the camera is brought to a rest...The cameraman, a 30-ish brunette man in light brown suit, rushes into the picture to take a seat a couple of feet from Walden..) Cameraman (panting heavily): I'm here today with Tim Wal-- Tim: INVINCIBLE Tim Walden, thank you, PCW's current archetype of the infallible wrestler, and the man who is going to obliterate the White Mexican, Mexicano Momar, the former non-feeler of pain in the PCW ring tonight....Not too much, of course, since afterward he'll have to take his rightful position in the wrestler-lackey relationship as the inferior of the two of us...My only real worry about it? That little Presidential piece of s*BLEEP* Duke Williams...I mean, that guy is single-handedly driving PCW into the ground as interim CEO, signing all the crap he can get his hands on from the NTICW, losers like Totally Tubular Tommy and Carey J. Nowski, who'd be better served setting up rings than actually using them! And another thing.. (At this point, a pedestrian walks by Tim...And is tripped for no good reason, his middle-aged balding yelp of pain emerging upon hitting the camera, which falls to the side and immediately turns the signal to snow....And the scene fades from snow to black.) ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
