The CWS Opens It's Doors...(Part One)

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-9-02 **OOC NOTE: Trying novel-style. Forgive suckiness.** **Theme Music: 'Pour Some Sugar On Me' by Def Leppard** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, It's time. The school's reopening. I've got two students so far, and there're some auditions for people coming in soon. I'm excited...but I don't know if I can afford to be spending my time on this, when I need to practice my in-ring in preparation for Rush and LeBlanc...but that's the way the cracker breaks, y'know. I miss you, as always. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- On Pensacola street, right off of the Florida State University main campus, there's a warehouse. And in that warehouse, there are years of history. In 1934, it was built, and use to house coal. The smell has never totally left the rafters. In 1961, it's ownership turned over, and the warehouse lay apandoned until the University bought it up in 1965. They used it for storage of old sports gear, and the like...up until the interior renovation of Doak Campbell Stadium in 1998. And since then...it has lain dormant. Until now... The large front doors of the warehouse swing open, in the early morning light, and the figures of three men are shadowed in the entrance. 'This is...just...AWESOME.' says the taller figure to the left. 'God, Jase...this place smells kinda like sulfur.' is the voice to the right. Mark Farmer and Joe Stephenson, two fraternity members of Phi Kappa Psi's Florida State Chapter, and the first two entrants into the 'new' Core Wrestling School, follow their new mentor, Jason Core, into the warehouse. On the inside, very very simple furnishings. A table. Several folding chairs. One hanging punching bag...and a BRAND NEW PCW ring. 'Whoa, Jason...man, is that a REAL ring?' Mark's eyes bug out in sheer delight at the sight of the majestic ring, completely roped and matted. 'Totally real, boys. Totally real.' Their instructor, a relatively pleased, but serious Jason Core, rests himself on one of the aforementioned folding chairs by the table. 'We've got a big day today. The auditions for Core Wrestling School. You two are in, but I'm gonna need at least eight people, not including you, to make upkeep. Joe, Mark...let's get down to it. Let's get that stuff outta my truck.' With these oh-so-inspiring words, Jason and the boys head out to the car to setup. And thanks to the magic of time-lapse imaging, it's now 9:00 AM, and the Core Wrestling School tryouts are ready to begin! (At least, that's what the banner out front says.) We see back inside, where Jason, Mark, and Joe all sit expectantly at the table. 'Dude, this sucks...can we go now?' Joe says. Mark slaps him upside the head. 'No, man. Are you gonna take this seriously at all?' is his more responsible fraternity brother's response. Mark takes a quick look at Jason, who's gaze is firmly fixed on the unmoving doors. His dark eyes are emotionless. 'They'll come, boys.' Jason turns to Joe, and pulls his keys out of his pocket. 'Do me a favor, Joe...take my car down to Krispy Kreme, and grab two dozen donuts and a gallon of OJ.' Jason tosses the keys negligently to his student. 'Yeah, no prob, boss.' Joe takes the keys, and dashes out of the warehouse, almost running over a sharply-dressed young man in his early twenties. 'HEY! Is this the Core Wrestling School!?' the young man asks. He's clad in what appears to be wrestling gear, with high black wrestling boots, leather pants that look designed for in-ring work, and a buttoned up polyester shirt that sparkles blue, black and silver. His eyes are covered by trendy dark sunglasses, and his dye-job-blonde short hair is spiked like a pop star's. 'Yeah, I'm Jason Core...and this is my school. It's under renovations.' Jason stands up at the desk, and holds out his hand to shake the youth's hand...but is blown off as the kid just stands looking at the surroundings with his hands on his hips. 'Whatever, man. I can't believe that the real Jason Core would have a shithole like this be his wrestling school,' was the smart-alecky comment from the young man. Mark jumped to his feet, and tried to defend his friend and mentor. 'Hey, asshole. You try running a wrestling school, okay?' 'No thanks, man. I'm here to learn, not to teach...although I probably COULD run a better operation than this...' Jason made a concerted effort to step between the feuding young men. 'Hold up, hold up...what's your name, kid?' 'I'm Nick...Nick North. From Pennsylvania.' Jason's eyes get kind of wide at the mention of his home state. 'Pennsylvania?' 'Yeah, State College. I just graduated from Penn State, and I was told to come down here and find the best. I didn't believe that a wash-up like you could actually BE the best...but then I see you win that pretty PCW gold, and I think it might not be such a bad idea to come take a look at what you have to offer. Doesn't look like much, but I was told you were the best...are you?' Needless to say, Jason is more than a little thrown by the powerful comments by the headstrong and obnoxious youth. 'Yeah, I think I'm the best...and I'm not the only one that thinks that.' Nick seems a bit amused by this remark, judging from the almost-paternal smirk on his cocky face. 'Cool. I'm in.' Mark jumps in between the two men, hands high and open. 'Whoa, whoa whoa WHOA! Don't we have to screen these guys first, Jase? See if they've got what it takes?' Mark says, but Nick is still nonplussed. 'C'mon J-Core...who is this dude, your kid? Check me out...' Nick tosses his glasses into his shirt pocket, and removes his shirt. His body, which looks about 240-250 lbs, is VERY well-built. He flexes his arms, but Jason shows no emotion. 'How about that, Core...lackey? Is that good enough for you two? Or how about this...I already can bump, bleed, slam, work, ref, and do a hundred other things. I've had indy training before, but these guys try to keep ME down, to keep their egos high, and I'm sick of it. I want a place where I can be a bright shining star, and get recognized for it, alright? And if this is gonna be that place, awesome. If it's not, I couldn't care less. Am I in, or out?' Jason looks at Nick one more time, and then turns to Mark for his reaction. Mark slightly shakes his head from side to side to say 'No frickin' way' and Jason nods in response, affirmatively. 'North, you're in.' Mark looks shocked, while Nick looks non-plussed. 'F*cking right I am...good choice, J-Man. I'm gonna make you famous...' Nick goes ahead and puts on his shirt again, and throws the sunglasses atop his head. 'Do you kids have anything to eat, I'm starving here?' Mark opens his mouth to speak...but stutters, and marches off, his mouth muttering words to himself. Things about not being able to believe an asshole like North could be admitted to the school, and how when Joe gets back, he better get the pulp-free OJ. Meanwhile, Nick pulls up two chairs, and sits in one, while resting his feet on the other over by the sign-in table. Jason tosses the kid a handful of paperwork to fill out, from a pile on the table. As the kid looks at it, Jason kicks the chair out from under his legs, startling Nick. 'Look, you might have raw talent, but I do NOT want you causing problems here, alright kid? You WILL get along with the fellow students, because that is what you do here. I do NOT care if you have arguments between other students. Look at me and The Rush. We have to use weapons of destruction to main and kill each other on Tuesday, but no matter who wins, we are STILL teammates. You and your fellow students will be the same way, or I will force you into a FAR less superior school And you WILL NOT receive preferential treatment due to prior knowledge. I WILL make you relearn everything you know, until you know how to do it right, okay?' Jason's face is dead serious as he delivers the brief speech. Nick's nothing, if not confident. He playfully slaps Jason's shoulder, which elicts a slight wince. 'Yeah, whatever, Pops. So who else have you seen today?' Jason spreads his arms to indicate the vast nothingness that exists in the room. 'Awesome, J-Core...I love private attention.' is the sardonic reply of the obnoxious Nick North. Well, it's one down, and seven to go...until the CWS is whole once more...but hey...it's only 9:15... [To Be Continued.]
