Date: not entered Is Snow finally getting helped? We know that he cracked a long (LONG) time ago, but we now see him sitting across a desk from a licensed psychiatrist; She’s looking at him with heavy eyes - he’s not her first client of the day. She’s an older woman, with short curly hair and a thin-lipped mouth. She leans her elbows on the heavy desk and sighs... Psychiatrist: “Mr. Snow... exactly what do YOU hope to gain from our sessions?” Snow leans back in his own chair and stares at the older lady. We can’t help but wonder what would finally convince him that he DOES have some sort of mental problem. Finally, the young wrestling star gives his answer... Snow: “I don’t know! How in the hinges of hell SHOULD I know!? It wasn’t MY idea to come here in the first place! The Psychiatrist looks confused. She, like the Killin’ Villain, sits back in her chair and narrows her eyes at the young, blond-haired, champion... Psychiatrist: “Well, if it’s not your idea, then who’s idea was it?” Snow scowls, but says nothing... Psychiatrist: “Who?” Snow turns his head to the side - he’s obviously holding something back. The Psychiatrist knows it... Psychiatrist: “You’re going to have to tell me, you know...” Snow’s eyes meet hers once again, but only for a brief moment. He looks much the same as he always does - only his leg is nervously shaking and his hair is wet. It’s slicked straight back and stuck down to his head with the moisture making his hair look a bit darker than usual. Other than that, he’s just the same as always... Snow: “Ok! Ok, fine, damn it! FINE! My...” The Champ hesitates... Psychiatrist: “Your...?” Snow: “My...” He hesitates again... Psychiatrist: “Your... what?”Snow, who we can’t help but think looks a bit embarrassed, glances at the camera and then back at the Psychiatrist. The Killin’ Villain leans in close to the Psychiatrist and whispers something too quietly for us to hear. When he’s finished, he scowls and leans back in his chair. There’s a moment of silence that’s finally broken when the Psychiatrist speaks... Psychiatrist: “So your wife made you come here.” Snow’s face twists with rage... Snow: “Damn it! Tell the whole inferior WORLD why don’t you!? How do you think I feel, being a Superior Individual who’s forced into doing things by his... his... his... WIFE!?!?” The Psychiatrist opens a drawer and pulls out a notepad, seeing an opening in Snow’s thick skin... Psychiatrist: “Well why don’t we talk about it? How does that make you feel?” Suddenly, Snow leaps up and backs away from the desk, shaking a finger at the Psychiatrist with an insane smile on his young face... Snow: “Oh no! Oh no! You’re not tricking ME that easily! I’ll not sit here and talk about my damn feelings and how my parents ruined my life! Not THIS Killin’ Villain, wench! Not me!” The Psychiatrist stays calm in the face of Snow’s wild behaviour. She makes a little note in her notebook, calling for Snow’s attention once more... Snow: “Don’t do that!” Psychiatrist: “Don’t do what?” Snow: “Don’t write your damn notes down! How DARE you write about a Superior Individual? Thinking you can dissect and understand him! Don’t you know how far Superior I am to you? I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it!” The Psychiatrist sighs and sets her notebook down on the desk, folding her hands above it and staring silently at Snow... Psychiatrist: “Well, Mr. Snow, if you don’t want to be here... I guess all you can really do is... leave.” Snow: “Yes! Damn it, that’s exactly what I thought.” Snow turns to make his exit, passing the many plaques and certificates that line the walls. As he reaches for the door knob, the Psychiatrist clears her throat and sighs... Psychiatrist: “Of course... if you do leave, I’ll be forced to call your wife to let her know about your progress. I mean, since she is your spouse, and IS the one who wanted you to be here...” Snow stops at the door - his entire body frozen. We can hear his teeth grinding from across the room. After a long moment of silence, the Killin’ Villain whirls around and bellows... Snow: “BLACK MAIL!” The Psychiatrist shrugs as the scene fades to black... Later... Snow sits on the opposite side of the desk from the Psychiatrist once more, staring at the older lady with hatred burning in his blue eyes. It seems that the threat of her calling his wife has convinced him to stay a while longer. The Psychiatrist pulls up a piece of white cardboard with a rather large inkblot in the center of it... Psychiatrist: “Tell me, Mr. Snow... what do you see in this picture?” Snow: “Picture?” Psychiatrist: “On the card...” Snow stares at the card for a long moment. To most of us, the ink blot is obviously in the shape of a butterfly. But things don’t always appear to Snow as they appear to the general population... Snow: “Well what the hell do YOU see on the card?” Psychiatrist: “That’s not important. What’s important is what you see.” Snow looks again at the card.... Snow: “I see an ink stain at the center of a white card.” The Psychiatrist rolls her eyes... Psychiatrist: “The picture!” Snow: “I don’t see any...” Suddenly, Snow’s eyes grow wide... Snow: “Ohhhh.... the picture...” Snow leans in close and stares at the ink blot for quite some time with wide eyes and an expression of awe on his face. A piece of hair has fallen out of the stock and is now hanging in his face... Snow: “I see...” He hesitates, brushes the lock of hair from his face, and then continues... Snow: “I see myself, in all of my Superiority and Greatness, stomping the INFERIORITY out of Big Alan Steele! What a sight! Oh, what a sight, indeed! Steele is lying in the center of the ring - we’re at Supremacy - and I, the Killin’ Villain, am struggling to pull my Snake Skin boot out of his ass!” There’s a long moment of awkward silence. The Psychiatrist turns the picture around and views it herself, wondering how Snow could get such an idea from it. A moment later, she sighs and holds up a second picture. To us, it’s obviously a boat... Psychiatrist: “And this one?” Snow: “I see... I see myself at Supremacy again... but this time... this time... I’m holding Tyler Nelson’s decapitated head up for all of the audience to see! Oh! Look at the way they envy me! I am, the Killin’ Villain, after all - and far Superior to each of them...” The Psychiatrist sighs - she thinks that Snow is mocking the experiment. Little does she know, Snow is being serious about what he sees in the pictures... She decides to try it one last time and pulls up a picture that appears to each of us to be a rabbit... Once more, she sighs... Psychiatrist: “And this one?” Snow stares at the picture for longer than either of the other two, scratching his stubbly chin... Snow: “Oh...” He leans back in his chair... Snow: “Oh my...” Suddenly, Snow leaps up from his chair and points at the card, his eyes full of rage... He exclaims... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS!” Psychiatrist: “What!?” Snow: “How DARE you present a picture like that before my Superior eyes!? How DARE you, wench!?” Psychatrist: “W-what!? What do you see!?” Snow’s cold blue eyes narrow as he glares at the aging Psychiatrist, his teeth are clenched tighter than his fists... Snow: “Ohhhh! Don’t play dumb with ME, wench! It’s obviously a picture of Tyler Nelson...” Snow scowls... Snow: “Defeating me... Me! ME! I’m not just another station-wagon driving simpleton! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! The mere THOUGHT that Tyler Nelson could EVER defeat a man so Superior to him should be OUTLAWED!” Snow stomps down his Snake Skin boot and turns toward the exit... Snow: “I refuse to go through any more of this, wench! I suggest that YOU learn to tell the difference between a Superior Individual - like myself - and a mere PEASANT!” With that, the fiery young PCW Champion makes his exit, sure to slam the door behind him. The Psychiatrist, who’s overwhelmed by surprise, can do nothing more than stare with wide eyes and a dropped jaw. A moment later, she looks back down to the final card... suddenly, her eyes grow wide... Psychiatrist: “Hmmmm...” Does she see it too? Fade to black...
