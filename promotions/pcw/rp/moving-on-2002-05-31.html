Moving On

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/05/02 Yeah, did you see those guys run?......... Pussies.......... They couldn't get out of there quick enough. The Supreme Machine sits gazing blindly out the window of his private limousine as he continues his conversation. Who?  The Malice Crew?......... Will if they want some all they have to do is ask........... You know me, I'm an equal opportunity ass kicker........ Yeah, I know. He laughs loudly as the phone conversation continues. I don't give a damn about The Malice Crew, Ricky........... Don't worry I won't do anything until the boss says it's OK, but I won't take any crap from them........... I don't care.  If any members of The Malice Crew are stupid enough to get in my way I'll treat them just like I do everyone else. A stunning blonde driving a small red hatchback drives up beside the black limousine.  The Supreme Machine watches her intently through the heavily tinted windows as a wry smile appears on his face.  He nods his head silently as he listens to Ricky Thunder on the other end of the phone. I'm going to be there at Fusion, I don't care what you say........... Hey!..... Hey!...... I never said I was going to interfere in your School's Out match Rick, I just said I was going to be at Fusion, that's all. The red hatchback with the blond behind the wheel slowly overtakes the limo and disappears into the early morning traffic.  The smile vanishes from The Supreme Machine's face as his concentration returns to the telephone conversation.  He takes a long sip of Perrier as he listens. OK, OK.  I won't interfere in your match........... Happy now? The Supreme Machine smiles broadly as Ricky Thunder continues to chastise him from the other end of the line. You're just like your damned brother, you know that?.......... Yeah, you little bastard........... Anyway Rick, I wanted to ask you about what you were telling me about your uncle.......... I didn't want to say anything in front of the other guys.......tell me what happened. He turns his gaze back to the tinted window beside him as Ricky Thunder recounts the events of the past few days.  In the distance he can see the familiar skyline of New York City with the rising sun reflecting brightly off the thousands of windows.  A look of surprise comes across his face in reaction to what Ricky is telling him. That old dog!........ Your brother and I stayed with him a couple of times when we were passing through........ No, I never picked old Tony as a mobster............ Shit!........... That's fucked up! He takes a long drink from his bottle of Perrier as Ricky continues. A what?......... a Cobra GT?....... How much?....... $25000........ Fuck!......... Just hang on there a minute mate, let's get one thing straight here......... You are keeping it all right?  Like you're not going to do anything stupid like give it away are you?............. Good!......... I didn't want you to succumb to guilt or something stupid like that. The Supreme Machine laughs as he watches a school bus drive by the limousine.  The children's faces are pushed hard up the the bus's windows as the try in vain to catch a glimpse of the limo's passenger through the heavily tinted glass. So how's it drive?......... What?  No, that's alright.  I'll stick to the limousine thanks.......... Where am I now? He looks out at the approaching skyline. We're just getting in to New York.  I move into the new place today.......... No, there was nothing wrong with the old one, just felt like a change after all these years.  You know how it is, new job and everything.......... You'll have to come over and have a look around............. And the offer still stands if you ever want to come over and use the training facilities. The limousine catches back up to the school bus.  The children are still jammed up hard against it's windows. Have you seen the Tuesday Turmoil card?  I've got my first match........... The Multiple Man......... No me either but I'm getting David to send me some videos and such............ You know David........... David my agent!........ Yeah that's him............. They should be waiting for me when I get in........... What?  Of course I'm ready.  Never felt better.............. Don't worry.  By next Tuesday I'll know all there is to know about him.  David is very thorough.  He has another long drink of mineral water as the limousine draws away from the school bus.  He attention is taken diverted from the window as the nearby fax machine beeps into life. Relax, I'll be fine.  By the time I get to the new compound everything will be set up and ready to go.......... Yes, I know......... Look, the ring was set up yesterday.  The gym is ready and waiting.  I'll be in there testing it out this afternoon. He reaches out and picks up the first of the pages that are coming out of the fax machine and scans quickly over it. Here....... I'm just getting a fax from David now......... He says everything is ready for me, I've just got to stop by his office and pick up the keys.......... And he's got me a whole lot of stuff about this Multiple Man guy for me to look through.......... So do you see?  I'm going to be more than prepared come Tuesday night. The fax machine beeps one final time as the last of three pages emerges into the tray.  The Supreme Machine picks the two pages out of the tray and quickly scans them before putting them with the first page and laying them beside him on the back seat.   As he turns back to the window, the school bus pulls back along side.  He chuckles softly to himself as he looks at the children. In case you've forgotten, young Thunder, I'm yet to lose a singles match since I returned to wrestling three months ago and I can assure you I don't plan on allowing my PCW debut to be the first.  Tuesday night is going to be business as usual, trust me.......... Anyway, I'm going to let you go now, we're almost there.......... You too....... I'll see on Friday unless you drop around before hand........ OK........Bye. The Supreme Machine hangs up the phone and picks up the papers that had come through the fax machine.  As he scans over them, he becomes aware of the school bus that is still keeping moving through the traffic beside his limousine.  The children are still hard up against it's windows trying to see into the limo.  He puts down the papers and presses a button that lowers the tinted window.  The children's faces light up as they see the world famous wrestler sitting in the back seat and they wave and cheer madly.  The Supreme Machine waves back at them before closing the window back up.  He sighs as he reaches down and pushes the intercom that connects him to his chauffer. Yes Sir? The Supreme Machine looks back towards the still cheering children on the school bus. Can we get ahead of this damned bus please.  These children are starting to piss me off. Certainly Sir. Thanks He sits back and watches the school bus disappear behind them as the limousine roars off towards New York City.
