Weird Format? DEAL WITH IT!

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/18/02 I have a history with the Cruiserweight Title(1) and with Rent-A-Hero.(2) And come Fusion, I will capture my second PTC title(3), my first PCW title.(4) Many things have gotten in my way(5), but nothing will stop me from fulfilling my claims.(6) This may be a little hard to understand, but rest assured that this has no point.(7) Take this for what you will, but I need to feel appreciated.(8) I’m going through some hard times right now here in Outworld(9), but I’ll be okay in time for my match. Goodnight.(10) 1 -- First, during the period when I was between jobs after I quit EW when it was expelled from the PTC, and the time when I was floating around in the PTC with no definite job secured (though there were offers from GWF), I ran upon a conversation in the PTC complex lobby that then PCW Cruiserweight Champion Juan Borro had initiated. He claimed that he could defeat any cruiserweight in the PTC. I took him up on his challenge, citing my win over him in the first PTC Global Title Tournament, but he seemed to back away from the challenge, saying that he didn’t have to face me since I wasn’t signed with PCW. In essence, Borro was saying that he’d face anyone in the PTC as long as they wrestled under a PCW contract. I would finally get signed to PCW shortly after the New Year, and immediately made my presence felt as the mysterious figure claiming to be “Justin Howard,” though not really making a formal introduction to the PCW crowd until Juan Borro’s Cruiserweight defense against challenger Xavier Kannon. During the match, I came out to the top of the entrance ramp and just stood there, just to show Borro that I had signed with PCW, and if he won the match I’d soon be challenging his title, like he tried to say I would never do in that conversation… maybe two weeks before that. Kevin Klaymore attacked me even though I had no intentions of going down to the ring. Seems Borro put him on alert. Heh. But Juan Borro is floundering in the mid-card now, and Klaymore is… is he still alive? No one knows? Meh. Anyway, Kannon won the Cruiserweight Title, because Borro spent like five minutes yelling at me when he should’ve been concentrating on Kannon. A quick X-Bomb, and the match was over and we’d have Xavier Kannon for Cruiserweight Champion until Chris Chase won the title at Shanghai’ed later in the month. Borro was also in that match. Why? Because we had a #1 contender’s match. I was doing fine, he might’ve gotten in a move or two, I don’t remember. Anyway, as I was pinning him after hitting him with a death move of sorts, Kevin Klaymore and El Diablo run out to save their International Dependent. This was obviously way before Klaymore got fired and Diablo quit I2. So, with a 3-on-1 situation (it was a no-DQ match), Borro won and got to go onto Shanghai’ed to face Xavier Kannon and Chris Chase for the Cruiserweight Title. I, though remaining on the Cruiserweight Title contendership list never really got another chance to move up in the rankings, and stayed at #5 for months, until I recently moved up to #3 or something. 2 -- Having won my debut match with ease (then again, Tim Walden’s only put up a decent fight maybe two times in his career), I was getting ready to face Darkstar. This would actually be something of a challenge. While preparing for my match while they introduced Darkstar, Borro and Klaymore attacked me. They weren’t really stopping until Rent-A-Hero, who was already sent back down to PCW by that time, ran out and scared away Golden Game. The damage had already been done, though. I wasn’t at 100% by the time I made it down to the ring, and even though I tried, Darkstar won. And to think that Rent-A-Hero helped me, the man whom I had called an utter moron a few weeks before (and with good reason to, he suggesting a bra & panties ultimate submission match… he’s not quite right in the head). 3 -- The first PTC title I ever won was in EW during the time it was considered one of the “Big Three.” In a match with Xavier Holmes (not to be confused with Xavier Kannon), I won the title, though the result was not favorable as Holmes’s enemy, Jacob Radder, screwed him to help me win the title. I was never really booked to defend it except for one time against Duzza in a match that I won. After that, EW got itself ejected from the PTC and I quickly quit after I found out what happened. 4 -- Though having title shots, including the #1 contender’s match for the Cruiserweight Title with Juan Borro, the Rising Star Title match at Shanghai’ed, or the World Title March Madness main event, nothing’s really worked out for me. The Rising Star Title match I just didn’t want to win. The Cruiserweight #1 contender’s match ended in a screwjob. The World Title match… I’ll admit I just didn’t do as well as I should have. So, I’ve been getting title shots in PCW, I just haven’t had anything come through for me yet. I’m doing everything in my power to make sure that such is not the case on Fusion. I will not leave without the Cruiserweight Title. 5 -- First, the Cruiserweight Title #1 contender’s match, which we’ve already talked about. Next, the Rising Star title match which I was thrown into even though I didn’t really want that title. After that, I really just floated around, wrestling matches, with my main “thing” being just concentrating on matches and doing the “Justin Howard” thing on and off. I worked with the recently fired Drake Maxwell on that. Jeremy Howard never really got back at me, but I guess that’s because things always came up. After March Madness, which I’ll admit was my own fault for losing, though I did swerve Drake Maxwell because I was just messing with him, Bobby Stormbringer starts bothering me, and complaining about… something, I don’t know, I never really paid much attention to him. Anyway, he kept getting in my way, trying to pester me while I’m trying to get title shots. How’s a man supposed to carry on his career when a bitter co-worker won’t get over the fact that I absolutely destroyed him in his stablemate’s match? Around this time, I joined Akiyama in the Celestials. They were going through some in fighting, but I didn’t think that would affect me. It really hasn’t except for that they’re all split up now. At least Akiyama and I split on mutually respectful terms, instead of a huge falling out. It’s not that hard to do so, but not many people know how to really talk to him. He’s not that bad when spoken to the right way. At Spring Fling, Stormbringer and I had a match. I was getting ready to win, but then Clyde and his “Dark Ages” get involved. I didn’t need their help. They needed mine. Why else would I join with a group of men who’s closest taste of gold in quite a while will be when I win the Cruiserweight Championship without their help? Rest assured, it will be a victory for me, NOT for the group. This Syndicate thing seems to be falling apart as quickly as it started. And Charlie Loc wants a piece of me now. We’re set for a Best of Five, which shall be booked into the cards as soon as possible. It will be so sweet to win the Cruiserweight Title, and then constantly defend it against Loc, proving once and for all that I am better than him. Of course, he already knows that, otherwise he wouldn’t have started anything with me. 6 -- My claims of being the tallest cruiserweight in PCW history will be compounded by being the tallest cruiserweight champion in PCW history. Making things even more spectacular is that I shall be the first blind champion in PCW history. Most likely in PTC history. Ticker-tape parades shall be thrown in my honor. Wild parties in the best clubs in the world… and Stormbringer and Loc will be shot if they come anywhere near me. 7 -- The handler is dealing with post-modernism at school, so he’s trying his hand at writing a post-modern RP. Oh, by the way, did I mention that I’m a talking trout? 8 -- Especially after my wife threw me out of the house. I wasn’t doing anything with that woman, we just went to a hotel and played around. And why was that b*tch following me? She’s got some trust issues. Now she wants a divorce, and I’m saying, “Where you gonna go, ‘cause you ain’t got no job skills, and no one else wants you, ugly as you are. Only reason I married you is because I’m a blind talking trout!” She didn’t seem to like that, but she can put these nuts in her mouth if she’s got beef wit dat. She took me to Divorce Court and Judge Maybeline be talking out her ass ‘bout things she ain’t got no business talking ‘bout, like how I go out with my boys. Now, a man’s got to do things outside of the house, I can’t be sittin’ up in the house with her stank ass all the time. Gotta get out. But the judge is like, “Maybe you need to spend more time with your wife.” Easy to say for a woman who ain’t been married. She don’t even know what it’s like being wit’ someone, and she’s supposed to tell me how I run my thang? Hell no! 9 -- The Shokan and Centaurs are fighting again. Goro and Montaro are challenging each other in the Pit of Despair. No one has ever come out, so this is going to be their final battle. Since Shao Kahn was desposed by Shinnok, things just haven’t been the same. Everyone is fighting amongst themselves. The chosen warriors of the earth realm don’t even have to do anything, all the Outworld forces are fighting each other. Baraka and Reptile lead their people, while Ermac is in charge of Shao Kahn’s former guard. Goro and Montaro lead their people, but after they both die in the Pit of Despair, I think both races will just go on a killing spree that won’t stop until nothing is left alive in Outworld. It’s a terrible state of events. And did I mention that I’m a talking trout? 10 -- Not for me, it’s not. Then again, good is a subjective term. But, we must remember that the road to Draconia winds through Misty Fields. And the Oobles are none to happy about munknacks getting too close to the dimblepanks.
