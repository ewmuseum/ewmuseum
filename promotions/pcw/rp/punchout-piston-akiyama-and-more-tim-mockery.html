PunchOut: Piston Akiyama and More Tim Mockery!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: PunchOut: Piston Akiyama and More Tim Mockery! Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered In PunchOut, The HIT has made great progress. Okay, so he beat Von Kaiser. But why didn't he tell us? Because, no one in PCW compares to Von Kaiser. But now, Trapezoid and Little Mac have an opponent that decidedly does. The Master of the Banzai Attack. Feared foe in the World Circuit. But first, the World Video Boxing Association Minor Circuit Champion... He is the deadly Piston Honda! And now, Trapezoid must avoid the deadly hands of Honda..... As in real life, he must avoid the Hand of Fate... Just call him Piston Akiyama. The battle is on. LET THE CARNAGE COMMENCE!! ::Trapezoid sits intently, as the screen for 'THE TITLE FIGHT' appears. He stares at the screen, looking at Piston Honda's visage appear on the screen. Trapezoid puts the controller down, and instead looks out to ponder the coming wars...:: The HIT: The Celestials....to many, odds on favorites to win at Alliances. Led by Akiyama.....the Neo Void Gangster, God, Revolutionary, whatever he is now. Before the godhood came Yakuza Akiyama. I swear, he's like a Barbie doll! 'Come get your new Neo Void God Akiyama, with Void Banishing action!' And then, if you get sick of THAT, there's always 'Human Cyclonica with Goth Chick Action!' It's ridiculous....Akiyama's been everything but what he wants to be. PCW World Champion. The title I wear around my waist was once the 'Akivision' title....the title I vy for with Teresa the symbol of the 'God of Tag'. The whole Divine thing's becoming a holy pain in the ass to listen to.... :Trapezoid hits the button to begin the fight, and is INSTANTLY whacked by a right from Honda. Piston opens up, forcing Trapezoid immediately to the defensive.:: The HIT: He hits like a hammer.....an anvil. Each punch is devastating. Say what you will about Akira's identity crises, but when he strikes you, it damn well feels like you're being hit by a god. But then.....you start to figure him out. As strong and fast as he is, he has weaknesses... ::Mac dodges a quick right hook from Piston, and then throws a jab to counter. His eyebrows flash, as HIT recognizes this as the sign for a jab. Making Mac duck again, he stuns Piston with a blow to the gut, earning a star.:: The HIT: No matter how godly he seems, he has a weakness........watching the God, there are patterns. And the key to victory over a man with his size and strength is to exploit those patterns. ::Honda drops back at 1:30 in the round, preparing for his Banzai Attack...:: The HIT: Here's one. Honda's about to come at me with his punch rush. I let him get in, I have to block all his shots, and I'll probably take damage. But, if I time this right.... :As Honda comes in, Little Mac lets fly with a perfectly timed Star Uppercut! Honda staggers backwards, falling to the mat, as the ten count is near academic.: The HIT: YES! YOUR WINNER, AND NEWWWWWWWWWW W.V.B.A. MINOR CIRCUIT CHAMPION.....LITTLE MAC!! ::More terrible dancing follows from the Trapezoid.:: The HIT: Akiyama's strength is undeniable. If he strikes me with a Hand of Fate, it's likely over. If he hits the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse, it's over..Hell, if my student The End hits One Last Time/Never Again, as I found out first hand in PURE, it's over. The key is, to avoid those occurances BEFORE they happen! To dodge them.....avoid them, prevent them from ever STARTING! That can only be done through study....training, and, oh yes, a hell of a lot of in ring time. ::Trapezoid smiles....eyes staring at the screen, as Little Mac begins running in the game, training for the next circuit.:: The HIT: That right there......that's Alliances. Four matches in one night for Tim Walden and myself. A marathon.....in which we sprint for 15-20 minutes, job in between, and then sprint again.....each sprint getting more and more strenous. From a tag match.....to a Ladder Match........to an Alamo Match....and lastly, the big one, Alliances......our endurance is going to be pushed to the LIMIT! I've gone eight matches in a row, in Trick or Treat III. But Tim, can you? Can you wrestle four matches in one evening? Do you have the fortitude, the pride, and most of all, THE GODDAMNED WILL TO HANG WITH ME THROUGH EVERY DAMN ONE OF THEM? Can you, or will your body break under the stress? I know the damned answer already.... ::Trapezoid grins, turning to leave the hotel room, NES still on..: The HIT: You're going to CRACK, Timmy. You're going to shatter under the pressure of the biggest night of your career! Just like all the losses to Aymin....the losses to me...to EVERYONE! WHEN THE PRESSURE'S ON, YOU CHOKE! This is no different! Four matches, four losses! It's all elementary mathematics! You have an equal chance of losing all of them, thus your probability of losing the last match is still one in two! AND EVERY DAMNED TIME, THAT LOSS WILL COME AT THE HANDS OF ME, OF ME! THE H! I! MOTHER-FUCKING T! ::A fired up Trapezoid exits, running off to lord knows where.:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
