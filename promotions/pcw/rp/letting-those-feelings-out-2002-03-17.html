Letting those Feelings Out

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/17/02 OOC: Sorry this is short....[detail withheld]. Expect better rps very soon :) The scene opens up in Kramer's bedroom. He is sitting up on the bed with no shirt on and beads of sweat pouring down his face due to the hard work he did outside. He had pulled weeds, planted some things in his hopeful garden, and even got around to mowing the grass. And normally that is unusual for Kramer because he is sort of lazy and barely ever gets up except for getting the remote. And even then its not much work. So as Kramer is sitting there he starts to crack his knuckles as he looks towards the camera. Kramer: Everyone knows that my stints in PCW haven't been at all grand. I lose more matches than I win and yet I keep a positive attitude. Now I am not exactly sure about how I manage to do this but I do, and I have become accustomed to it. And so everyone knows that when they see me wrestle they shouldn't expect much except my enthusiasm and sense of humor that I like to bring to the ring with me. Even my opponents shouldn't expect much, but still they should always come prepared, ready to have some what of a challenge. So if you add all of this up does this make me a jobber? He pauses for a brief second as he puts a white wifebeater on that was sitting next to him on the bed. Kramer: When you think of a jobber you think of people who never win everything, and are push overs right? Whatever you do never ever put my name with jobber before or after. Sure I have had many losses but I am not the kind of person you can throw down once and get the win right away. Instead you have to work hard and earn it. And if you must associate me as a jobber I won't hold it against you because you are of course entitled to your oppinion however wrong they may be. He flashes a smile to the camera. Kramer: I just felt like getting that out because I have heard a lot of fans and press talking about me like that. But thats alright because one of these days I am going to make an awesome come back and I am going to go up to these people and say 'I don't suck as much as you thought now do I?'. Boy I can't wait for that day to come. Its going to be one glorious day. Realizing he is starting to go off track he gets back on topic. Kramer: Next in line to talk about is my match against Reeco Suave on Sabotage. He is newer to PCW but not to the sport of wrestling. And from what I have witnessed in the past in his JEW days, he is a great wrestler with a good head on his shoulders. Being as I don't know him extremely well I can't say anything negative, at least not yet. Normally I wait for my opponents to crack on me before I crack on them, and unfortunately I don't think he has said much of anything about our up and coming match or me. For me this match is important because if I lose I have noticed that I don't get matches for like a weeks time. And that gets really depressing when I am sitting at home waiting for the bookers to put me in a match. So I doubt that Reeco will mind much if he loses because he has a whole week of matches lined up for him. I can understand why I don't get more show time but I feel that if I had more to go for the better I would get. But I also no that they are waiting as well as just about everyone else, for me to prove that I have what it takes to survive in PCW. So as always I am pumped up for my match and hope that this will be the night that I bring home a victory. The scene slowly fades.
