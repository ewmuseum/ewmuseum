A Lesson in Practicality.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as the preferred transport of the Greek demi-god, the Winnebago, creaks back to Stately Steele Manor. Huge scorch marks and deep indentations adorn it’s once well-waxed finish, as the bent axels cause the monstrosity of a vehicle to limp to the front gates. With a final death rattle, the great titan shudders at the entrance before the axles give way with a metallic PING! The belly of the beast crashes to the ground, a thick, acrid smoke beginning to plume from the engine’s hood. “NOOOOOOOOO!!!” Big Alan Steele bursts from the driver’s side door, leaping to the ground and rushing to the front of the vehicle. He tries to wrap his arms around the front section, in an awkward attempt at a consoling hug. Big Alan Steele: (wailing) “Oh, what manner of sins must a man commit in a single lifetime to bear witness to such a terrible tragedy!! You hath given me your all, dear old friend… and thusly sacrificing your own life to help protect the unworthy lives of the Lord of Pain and I, through the maelstrom of fire and brimstone… oh, the pain of it all! THE PAIN!!” There is a clatter on the other side of the Bago, as the Masochist emerges from the cab, the door falling off to the ground and the handle still in his hands. Steele glowers menacingly at the Masochist, who casually strolls around to the front of the huge vehicle. He shakes his head sadly, before forming a gun with his fingers, shooting an imaginary bullet into the near-dead crankcase. Masochist: “Well, that’s all she wrote on the ole girl… gurk!” The final sound wrenched from the Masochist’s throat as Steele grabs him by the collar of his t-shirt, angrily lifting him off his feet. BAS: “Blasphemy! How dare you defile the corpse of this great warrior, now gone into the breach of the Elysian Fields?! How could one be so cavalier in the presence of a now slain compatriot, who hath just protected our skins from a gauntlet of fire rained down from above by nefarious and demonic villainy?! Infidel!!” Masochist: (ack!) “I’m… sorry… need… air… please…” Steele drops the Masochist unceremoniously to the ground, and returns to his tender ministrations to the car that thought it was a boat. The Masochist rubs his throat and tries to get a few deep breaths in, eyeing Big Al in fear. BAS: (rubbing hood) “Tis okay… you shall soon sleep among the heavens, in the comfort of the immortals… safe from the slings and arrows of an uncaring Earth…” Masochist: “Ooooookay. You are officially creeping me out here, Big dude. It’s just a Winnebago…” Steele turns on the Masochist with wild eyes, forcing the shrinking Masochist to step back uneasily. BAS: “JUST A WINNEBAGO?! Twas a chariot sent from the heavens, spiriting us to yon Hades-spawned troglodytes, seeking out decay and devastation wherever we went! Through the swamps of the Keys! Through the raging tempestuous forces of nature on the great American plains! Through the constant gonorheic rainfall of Washington state! Through…” Masochist: (chiming in) “Through the downtown ambush firefight at State and Main in San Jose.” BAS: (snarling, shaking fist in anger) “Yes… we shall NEVER forget this day…” Masochist: “I mean, like DAMN! All we wanted was a few frosties at the Dairy Queen and BAM! Fire and brimstone!!” BAS: “The awesome fiery forces of nature personified in the guise of the vile demons, the KREE FORCE! With their damnable powers of volcanic proportions, their well-timed attack left us faltering for quarter! And though they have since paid DEARLY for their evil deeds, I could not save her… caught in the whirlwind of flames and magma… SHE PAID THE ULTIMATE PRICE FOR JUSTICE!!” Big Al sobs and collapses on the hood, the cover springing open and streams of sizzling liquids squirt out. The Masochist puts a tentative consoling hand on Steele’s shoulder. Masochist: (mock heavy tones) “She will be missed, Al. She will be missed. But she is in a far better place now… tended to by angelic gas station attendants who cater to her every need. Never again will she need an oil change or a gas up. The wax will always shine in the sun and the trips will all be long stretches of highway.” Big Al looks up, and despite his teary eyes, a smile creeps onto his features. BAS: (wiping nose with his toga) “You really think so?” Masochist: (with confidence) “I know so. Now come on. Let’s get ourselves cleaned up. That battle left us filthy!” Steele looks down as his soot-stained toga and the likewise burnt appearance of the Masochist. Rising up to his full height, he slaps the Masochist on the back, knocking the wind from his lungs. BAS: “Ha ha ha ha! But of course! Come, Lord of Pain, and let us dispense with these tattered rags not fit for a plebian, and gain us access to more tasteful finery! Not to mention Sue is going to cease my life functions upon my arrival…” Masochist: “Oh man! We’ve been gone for hours! And we said we’d be back in like 15 minutes! Dude, I don’t envy you.” BAS: (pondering) “Shall an uncharacteristic training session cessation for dairy confectionaries turned full scale molten gladiatorial combat ultimately gain the nigh invincible Big Alan Steele? Perhaps she will understand our quandary.” Masochist: “Dude, she NEVER understands. I Mean, she’ll just say, ‘Well, you should have taken care of them quicker,’ like the last time. The taco run turned aerial dogfight on the 747. You are a dead man.” The two make their way up the front walkway, until Steele begins to slow down. The Masochist matches his pace, looking concerned, as Steele’s brow furrows in consternation. Halfway up the walk, they stop, Steele’s eyes boring holes into the front of the house. The Masochist tries to follow the gaze, but can only see the door. Masochist: “What’s up?” Steele sniffs the air and holds up a hand for silence. BAS: (slowly) “The game is afoot…” Masochist: “Wha… ?” BAS: “The stench of evil abounds! Can you not smell it?” Masochist: “I think that’s the Bago. It’s starting to burn pretty badly…” BAS: “SADOMASODOKEN!!” Instantly, the stunned Masochist finds himself airborne, hurled javelin style by a mighty throw from Big Alan Steele. The Masochist has barely time to scream before crashing into the front door, splintering it to pieces. The Masochist then slams into the far wall, taking out a antique china cabinet, housing most of Sue’s little collectables and knick-knacks. Big Al leaps through the door, poised to take on anything, but finding only the Masochist, near unconscious with rivulets of blood trickling down his face. Steele reaches down and hauls his bloodied partner to his wobbly feet. Steele’s face a mask of grim determination, he presses his finger to his lips for quiet. BAS: “Come! Evil is all about!” Big Al looks this way and that, before slowly moving towards the hallway to the cellar steps. Dragging the Masochist behind him, he stops at the stairway leading down, and slowly opens the creaking door. The air is filled with an electric tension. Suspense hangs in the air around Steele as he places a sandaled foot on downward leading step. Then, grabbing the syncoptic Masochist by the shirt, he slings his companion down the stairs with a crunch, leaping into the darkness behind him. Steele rises up, face to face with a glowing figure, tiny sparks of energy flying from the grinning intruder, a slender man in a bluish costume with lightning bolts along his sides, and a pencil thin villain-esque mustaches above his lip. His mouth is curled into a sneer as he places his two hands near each other, a growing ball of energy forming in his cupped hands. BAS: “So! You wish to challenge the man-god, Big Alan Steele, within his own domain?! Then you shall pay the ultimate price!! Hi-Keeba!!” Steele crouches and prepares to leap, but a familiar female voice stops him cold: the voice of trusted manager Sue Saksnussum. Sue: “Al! Knock it off! Stop scaring my guest!” BAS: (choking on the word) “GUEST?!?!” Sue: “Yeah. Hey, I’m really sorry about that. It’s cool, right?” Glowing Blue Man: “Forget it. It’s nothing. Happens all the time with these hero types.” The lights down stairs flicker on. Sue: (looking around) “Ah, good! Candi finally fixed the fuse box…” Glowing Blue Man: “Dreadfully sorry about that. Sometimes I don’t know my own wattage…” Big Al straightens up, scratching at his lush, yet manageable hair. BAS: “By the gods! Have I missed something?” Sue: (exasperated) “Yes, Al! As usual, you missed EVERYTHING! Where have you been? You went for ice cream THREE hours ago!” BAS: “Ambushed! By the KREE FORCE NATION!” Sue: “So? Well, you should have taken care of them quicker.” Masochist: (in the corner, broken) “I told you, man…” Sue: “I go to ALL this trouble to line up a training session, and you wander off, fighting bad guys! We’ve got major work to do before the PPV, and I don’t have time for you to get all sidetracked! Now, I’ve invited here Mr…” BAS: (growling) “I know of him. Tis the ELECTRODE, an inhuman conduit for electrical energies, stored up in his person and released with terrible force upon the unsuspecting populace!! Tis a true villain of Hades!” Electrode: (smiling and bowing) “Guilty, as charged.” Sue: “Good, then introductions aren’t necessary. Well, today he’s your instructor.” Steele blows his lid. BAS: (howling) “WHAT?!?! Tis an outrage! Tis true madness!! You have descended into the realms of insanity, Ms. Saksnussum!!” Sue: (calmly) “Listen up. Today, we’re going to do a little aversion therapy, to avoid the little mishap that went on Tuesday night.” BAS: “Aversion therapy?” Sue: “Yes. You see, you’re an outright idiot Big Guy. I love you and all, but an idiot nonetheless. When a guy like Snow, of ALL people, offers you his hand, you DON’T shake it!!” Electrode: “You shook SNOW’s hand?! You poor dope… whatever horrors were visited upon you were most certainly earned.” BAS: (raising a fist to strike) “Silence, defiler of all that is good!!” Sue: “STOP IT! He’s right, Al. On a scale of 1 to 10, ten being the dumbest thing ever done on Earth, that rated a 72. But that’s what today’s training is all about. I’ll make sure you never pull such a bonehead maneuver ever again, with Electrode’s help.” Electrode: “I live to serve, ma’am. Especially at the rates you are paying.” Sue: “Anyways, ole Electrode here is going to continually offer you his hand in friendship. Each time you take it, you’ll get a mild, yet memorable shock. So, let me preface everything that happens with this blanket statement… IF YOU GRAB HIS HAND, HE WILL HURT YOU. Badly. Starting now.” BAS: “But of course! Tis a villain!!” Electrode: “No I’m not! I’m only here to be friends! Come on, let’s shake on it.” The blue man extends his hand to shake, Steele looking at it warily. Suddenly, he breaks into a wide grin. BAS: “Ha ha ha ha! Tis good to see that the tide of good hath overcome your formerly evil ways! Put her there, newfound friend!” BBUUUZZZZZZZZZZTTTTTTTTT!!!!!!!!! The hair stands up on Big Al as his body is juiced with unchecked electrical current. Screeching in pain, the man-god struggles to let go, but his hand holds tight of its own accord, the muscles all tensed up from the electricity. Finally, after what seems like hours, the flow stops, and a smoking Big Al collapses in a heap on the floor. Sue tsks tsks the whole scene. Sue: “Well, can’t say I didn’t warn you.” BAS: (groaning) “Senses… tingling…” Electrode: “Gee, I’m sorry. I didn’t mean that. Here… let me help you up.” BAS: (looking up) “Tis okay, as I’m sure you did not mean it…” GGGGGRRRRRRZZZZZZZZZZTTTTT!!!!!!!!! Sue: “Oh Al. When will you ever learn?” BAS: (sizzling) “Tis an offense to refuse the hand of another in friendship… how can one resist the very laws set forth since the beginning of time?” Electrode: “You know, guys, he’s right. This is wrong. Listen Big Guy, I’m sorry. Can we be friends?” BAS: “I do not see why not…” CCCCRRRRRZZZZZZLLLLLAAAAKKK!!!!!!! Steele crumbles into a fetal position, shivering as leftover currents course through his body. Muscle spasms tense him up from time to time, making odd twitches in his movements. Sue: “He WILL hurt you, Big Al.” BAS: “I can taste colors…” Electrode: “Um, excuse me, Sue? But how long, exactly, should this continue?” Sue: “Oh, I have you penciled in for the night, if you are willing.” Electrode: (grinning) “Oh, I’m willing. I just wonder if Big Al is able.” BAS: (still fetal) “Y-Y-you shall succumb to my kindness…” Electrode: “He’s right, Sue. I just can’t harm a guy that all he wants to do is be my friend. Come one… let’s get you up…” BAS: “Indeed…” SSSSSHHHHRRRRLLLLLLLAAAAKKKK!!!!!!!! Steele crashes to the floor again, his skin literally bubbling up from the intense energy passed along him like a conductor. Sue wrinkles her nose in distaste at the grisly scene. Sue: “C’mon, Masochist. Let’s get out of here. I can’t stand the smell of burning flesh.” Electrode: (to Steele) “I’m sorry. All is forgiven?” BAS: “B-b-b-b-ut of c-c-c-ourse. Would be dishonorable to not forgive and…” KKKKKRRRRRZZZZZAAAAAKKKKK!!!!! Sue gingerly closes the door to the cellar steps as the lights flicker around the house… ~HOURS LATER~ Sue crack open the door, the smell of cinders burning her nostrils. Sue: (cautiously) “Uh, hello? Are you guys resting or what? I haven’t seen a power surge in the lights in a little while now…” Sue creeps down the steps, the lights almost completely out. A strange glow from the cellar is the only source of light. Finally, Sue reaches the bottom of the steps… Sue: “Holy crap!” Big Alan Steele is curled into a tight ball, emitting a soft glow of light. Next to him is the dried up husk of a body that used to be Electrode, now wasted away. Steele has sharp, episodic seizures, a puddle of drool underneath his mouth, his eyes staring, unseeing , straight ahead. Sue walks up and sternly wags a finger at Steele. Sue: “Dammit Al! What happened? He was supposed to teach you a lesson!” BAS: “He… has…” Sue reaches down to poke her charge… ZAP! Sue: (shaking her hand in pain) “Damn! You’re nothing but a big ball of static electricity!” BAS: “I… overcame his evil… through the use of my friendship…” Sue: “Bullshit!” BAS: “By my continually absorbing his energy, he gradually weakened, thus creating the desiccated form you now witness. His villainous ways shall no longer plague San Jose…” Sue: (screaming) “BUT YOU WERE SUPPOSED TO LEARN TO NEVE ACCEPT A HANDSHAKE FROM SUCH PEOPLE EVER AGAIN!! DIDN’T IT HURT?!” BAS: (teeth chattering) “Indeed, but now, our bonds of friendship shall last for all our lives… though admittedly that was not such a long period of time. But twas the handshake that won the day! Hi-Keeba!!” Sue: (seething) “Great… well at least I won’t have to pay him the cash I promised him. Now maybe we can afford that new Winnebago Dreadnaught model…” BAS: (still fetal) “A new chariot? Hi-Keeba!” BUZZ!
