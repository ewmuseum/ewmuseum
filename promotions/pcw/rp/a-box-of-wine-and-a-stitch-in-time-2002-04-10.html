A Box Of Wine And A Stitch In Time

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-02 **Theme Music: 'Jump Right In' by The Urge** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Since I'm away so much, I've hired a co-headmaster for the CWS. He can't really wrestle at all right now, but he can help with workout and knowledge things better than most people. And Violator SHOULD be some help, especially now that we have someone who can talk to him. Wait a second. I left Kyle Lamen and The Violator in charge? Oh, man...those kids are in trouble. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [The scene comes up on the now-familiar insides of the CWS warehouse. Kyle Lamen is standing in the ring, while the combined CWS (for recap, Mark, Joe, Nick, Sandra, Eddie, Jamie, Pegleg, Jacques, Chris, Violator, Hombre) stands outside the ring. All of them are dressed in their workout gear, and sweating pretty good from the workouts Kyle's been running.] Kyle: Alright, kids. Right now, we're gonna talk about our gimmicks. Now, Jason and I have each talked with you about gimmick ideas, and now, you're going to decide on yours. So one by one, you guys come into the ring, and let us know who you are...with the two exceptions we talked about earlier. [Most of the CWS students faces are a mix between excitement and fear.] Jacques: Sacre! I will go first. It is ze 'onorable thing to do! [Jacques of Arc climbs into the ring, and as he stands, he begins stroking his handlebar moustache.] Jacques: I am...Jacques of Arc, the 'onorable defender of ze people of France...the greatest people in ze world! You silly Americans do not know true talent when you see it! [He strikes a very...French pose.] Jacques: I am ze Frenchest person who 'as ever lived, zerefore, I am ze greatest person who 'as ever lived. [He leans over to Kyle, barely refraining from breaking the all-important kayfabe.] Jacques: Where is my wine bottle? I must now brandish it menacingly! [Kyle gets kind of a pained expression on his face, and he rubs his hand through his fair carefully.] Kyle: Uh...we kind of couldn't afford a wine bottle, Jason's the only one with money...and he's at Turmoil tonight. He's not back yet. [Jacques throws his hands in the air, and says in a very not-French way...] Jacques: Amateurs! [He starts to walk away, but Kyle grabs his arm.] Kyle: Hold on! We got you something, it's not exactly the same... [Mark and Joe (the fraternity brothers on the outside) nod and Mark picks up a large brown shopping bag with a large object inside. He hands it up to Jacques, who takes out the object to reveal...] Jacques: A BOX OF WINE?! [Kyle nods, and sure enough, Jacques is holding a rather large box of Publix white wine. The ultimate in low-quality alcohol. Joe pipes up from the outside.] Joe: Yeah! We had it leftover from our last date function. Chicks love the cheap wine. We told them it was vintage. [In the ring, Jacques just stares, unbelieving.] Jacques: A box...of wine? [He rears back, and throws the box at Joe, who tries to catch it, but the box rends and soaks him in the cheap alcohol.] Jacques: You imbeciles can't even find ze right gimmick for me? [Joe is drenched in alcohol, and a couple of the students around him chuckle at his predicament.] Joe: Imbecile, huh? [Joe slides into the ring quickly...] Kyle: Oh crap... [As Kyle hightails it out of there, not wanting to be put in a position to aggravate his neck injury.] Joe: I'll show you who's an imbecile! [Joe (approximately two hundred forty pounds) picks up Jacques (approximately two hundred ten pounds) and tosses him over the top rope to ringside, and sadly, right on top of Nick North, who half-catches/is half-knocked down by the plummeting Frenchman.] Joe: How do you like that? [Little does Joe know, that Jacques' only friend, 'Pegleg' Paula Hampton has slid into the ring behind him. She ducks her head under his arm and delivers...] Pegleg: ARRR~! [...a back suplex on Joe, onto the mat. Nick jumps into the ring, to hit Joe, but his buddy Mark jumps in to intercept Nick. Sandra comes in, in order to break up the fighting, and soon, every member of the CWS is in the ring fighting. 'Emo' Eddie Russo is knocked down from behind by a double somersault plancha by El Hombre Pequeno Del Fantastico, Violator lifts the enormous Chris Andrews into a Violating Death Drop, all the while, Kyle Lamen, unable to do anything, prays vehemently on the outside.] Kyle: Please don't let them hurt each other...frickin'-A! Jase is gonna kill me. [The door to the CWS slams open, but no one hears it. Jason sprints to the ring, no longer favoring his right leg, and slides in. He takes Eddie Russo and El Hombre each in one big fist, and gives them the preverbial 'look of death.' They both roll out of the ring in a hurry.] Kyle: Our hero! [The three women jump out of the ring, and Chris rolls out, still feeling the effects of the VDD. Mark and Joe lift Jacques into what appears to be the old Six-String Samurai finisher, the Third Impact (Olympic Slam into inverted cutter). Jacques clutches his head in pain, but it's obvious that the two guys let him down easy. Nick, Joe and Mark all leave the ring quickly as Jacques rolls out...leaving only one person left, eyes alight in battle rage.] Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!! [The Violator has one meaty hand snake out from his chest and clutches Jason around the neck. The monstrous dark gimmick lifts Jason into the air almost effortlessly.] Jamie: Violator, NO! [The monstrous dark gimmick pays no heed to the teen whore. Jason's face turns bright red with a lack of oxygen.] Violator: PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!! [Violator lifts Jason even higher, but Jason wrenches his body out of the Violating grasp. He falls to the ground, but manages to kick Violator in his shin, which elicits a howl of pain.] Jason: *huff* [Jason gets to his feet, and hooks the Violator into HIS OWN VIOLATING DEATH DROP!! As the Violator crashes to the mat, his eyes go from the red of rage to a kind of puppy-dog brown. Jason stands up, and the Violator struggles to his feet.] Jason: I go away for a whole day, Kyle... [Violator stands up, and looks at Jason a bit fearfully.] Violator: I...am the Violator. Jason: Jamie, what did he say? [Jamie climbs into the ring, and looks at Violator.] Jamie: I'm not sure. I think it was something about going out of control whenever there's a clusterf*ck. [The Violator nods emphatically, adding...] Violator: Prepare for total violation. Jamie: Oh, and he says he's really sorry. [Jason nods a little, then pats the big Violator on the back in a friendly way.] Jason: It's okay, big guy. You ALL should be sorry. What on earth went on here? [Kyle carefully climbs into the ring, careful not to jar his injured neck.] Kyle: It's my fault. There was a box of wine, and fraternity boys...I'm not so good at handling people, I guess. [Jason shakes his head.] Jason: At least no one got really hurt, am I right? [There's an emphatic 'Yeah' from the assembled students.] Jason: Well, I guess this means we're ready to start doing interpersonal stuff. We can get some of these moves out of our system, huh? [Most of the CWS students seem very happy that they can start working on moves, especially Nick North and Mark Farmer.] Jason: ...But don't think that getting into fights like this is alright? I see this sort of thing again, and you're out, capiche? [The assembled CWS'ers nod, as Jason wipes a little of the sweat from his brow. VDD'ing the Violator himself IS hard work, y'know...] Jason: Alright then...everyone go home and get some sleep. Tomorrow, we start in-ring drills. I'm exhausted. [And the collective CWS picks up their belongings and leaves...save two men, Core and the Violator. The Violator walks over to his corner of the warehouse, and lays atop his blanket. Jason looks at the grimy, dirty Satanic Jobber, and lets out a dramatic sigh.] Jason: Hey, Violator? [The Violator's head pops up.] Jason: Why don't you stay on my couch, rather than sleep here? [The Violator stands up, warily.] Violator: I am the...Violator? [Jason nods.] Jason: Yeah, it's okay. Come on, I even have digital cable. [The Violator's eyes light up happily.] Violator: Prepare for Total Violation! [Jason lets out another, slightly happier sigh.] Jason: At least, out of all this mess, I got a little bit of training... [The monstrous Violator looks at Jason happily, and then gives him a big, Violating hug. Jason forces a smile, as his ribs are nearly crushed...and our scene fades to black.]
