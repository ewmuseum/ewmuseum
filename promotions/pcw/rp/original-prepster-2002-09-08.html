Original Prepster

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/09/02 “Stupid Jester thinks he can.. Jes-.. Um.. Jeste-.. Er.. Play a joke on Ellie~!” Our shot opens up on the devious grin that belongs to PCW’s Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Eleanor : “Well, Ellie~!s can, like, SO play jokes too!!!” As we zoom out we see the Prep in her best “Clandestine” outfit of Black mini-dress and a Black bobble hat.. Looking more of a cross between Breakfast at Tiffany’s, a Hooker and a Mountain Climber that someone undercover. Eleanor : “Xavier will be home soon so I’ll test out a few of my pranks...” She’s standing in the hall way of their Hubbardville home, located in the Kelly Preston development in the upper East side of the city. From her Prada backpack she pulls out some Plastic Explosives and pulls a chair to her so that she ca nervously climb up onto it. Eleanor : “We’ll start with a little bang above the door... Make a change for the usual bang he gives me on the stairs when he gets back...” Ellie~! blushes as she realises what she just said then reaches up and attaches the plastic explosive to the wall above the door then running the fuse down to the door frame where she rigs it up to blow once the door is opened. Eleanor : “It’s amazing the things you learn being surrounded by Scientologists.. Eeeep, when an Ellie~! goes up she must go down... Ellie~! doesn’t like going down...” The prep balances on the chair and drops a leg off it.. The tall pair of Black heels she wears not making her descent from the piece of furniture very easy but after 30 seconds or so... And grabbing onto the chair’s back for dear life, her foot touches the floor and she can climb off to look up at her contraption with pride. Eleanor : “Xavier will, like, SO be pleased with me...” Her trademark clapping and bouncing on the spot commences as she glows with pride. Eleanor : “YAY ME!!! Now... For the spike pit...” The giggly little prep walks away from the door to the lavish Oriental rug that is the focal point of the hallway and yanks at it, revealing it is covering a 6 foot by 3 foot pit containing rows of sharp metal spikes. Eleanor : “We should SO get “House and Gardens” here or something.. This house, like, totally rules now... Pit needs something else though...” She stands pondering for a seconds, hand resting on chin before a finger shoots up into the air. Eleanor : “SNAKES!!!” She scurries around the pit in her high heels to the cupboard under the stairs where she opens the door and rummages through... Before emerging with a Brown sack. Eleanor : “Here we go..... EEEEEEEEEEK!!!” One of the snakes protrudes it’s forked tongue out of the bag and Ellie~! yelps and throws the bag up into the air allowing the snakes to fly out before falling into the pit.... One being unfortunate to land on one of the spikes... She peers over and looks at the impaled reptile. Eleanor : “Cool.. It, like, works and everything! She then skips happily down the hallway for a few more feet then stops. Eleanor : “This is the best prank since we told Fiona Borer at High School that Plaid was fashionable, like, a YEAR before it was and she SO wore it!” From one of the drawers in the hall table she takes out a length of wire and goes about securing it tightly to each skirting board then looking at the carpet in front of it. Eleanor : “Hmmmm, some C4 would, like, SO go well there... Now, where did I leave it...” The prep rummages through the cupboard and drawers but draws a blank before it finally comes to her and she scurries off into the kitchen. *CRASH!* *BANG!* After a minute of so the preppy little Commissioner scurries back with a C4 boards that she casually drops onto the floor. Eleanor : “I wonder what C4, like, IS anyway?” She places the C4 board 5 feet away from the trip wire and looks around for a few seconds before grabbing another rug and pulling it over the explosives. Eleanor : “Okay... Explosives above the door, spike pit with Snakes, trip wire and C4 boards..... Something else needed.... Something that’ll, like, SO get to his ears!” Ellie~! ponders this for a second before it finally comes to her. Eleanor: “Like, NELLY! How could Ellie~! not think of Nelly!” The excited prep darts into the living room, the sound of her high heels giving away the franticity of her action before she emerges with a MIDI System, a copy of “Hot in Herre”, a Laser Pen and some electrical wire. Eleanor : “So the CD’s connected to the.... Um... Bit where the CD’s go...” She ejects the CD tray and takes the CD out of it’s case... Her fingers starting to smoke due to the violent reaction between pure evil and human skin. Eleanor : “Then the Stereo’s connected to.. Like, some wire...” Almost effortlessly she fiddles around at the back of the stereo before emerging a lone wire... Eleanor : “.. And the wire is connected to the Laser Pen...” She fiddles about with them for a couple of seconds before putting the pen on top of the stereo and rigging it up for a continuos beam. Eleanor : “Motion sensing, like, SO rules!” She skips off around the corner as the voice of her husband, PCW CEO Xavier Kannon is heard. “MY LITTLE FUCK FERRET... I’M HOOOOOOOME!” The sound of Kannon opening the front door is heard as Ellie~! screws her face up and covers her ears. *KKKKKKKKKABOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMM!!!!!!!* Xavier : “IN HUBBARD’S NAME!!! THE HARI KRISHNAS ARE TRYING TO BOMB ME!!!” The startled Scientologist’s bright Green eyes shoot all over the place and he’s in shock from the explosion above his head that has blown the door off it’s hinges and taken out a large section to the wall. He staggers further down the hall... Onto the rug. Xavier : “ELLI-..... HUH?!?!?” As he puts his foot on the rug it caves in and the Scientologist teeters on the edge of the pit, looking down at the spikes before falling in. Xavier : “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!“ The nimble PCW Champion just about manages to contort his body so he falls in between the sharp spikes and breathes a sigh of relief... .... Until a Snake’s tongue touches his face. Xavier : ”SWEET FUCKING XENU!!!!!!” Around the corner we quickly cut to Ellie~! who is giggling, pleased her pranks are working. Eleanor : “Sweet.... Fucking... Xenu!” Quickly switching back to Kannon we see him frantically swatting the Snakes away from him as the CEO scrambles out of the pit, then staggers further down the hall, not noticing the trip wire. Xavier : “Umph...” *BA-BA-BA-BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!!!* He goes flying over the wire and lands on the rug where 4 explosive charges go off, causing him to grab his face. Xavier : “MY EYES!!!!!” Swaying and staggering around blindly, his distressed form then cuts the laser beam. “IT’S GETTING HOT IN HERRE.... SO TAKE OFF ALL YOUR CLOTHES...” The Scientologist collapses on the floor in the foetal position, covering up his face and ears with his arms as he convulses on the floor... Pure annoyance in musical creeping into his brain. Xavier : “HUBBARD TAKE ME FROM THIS TORTURE!!!” “I AM GETTING SO HOT.... I WANNA TAKE MY CLOTHES OFFFFF....” Ellie~! takes a quick look around the corner to see the site of her husband rolling around in a ball on the floor, yelling to try and block out the sound of the music.... Much to the pleasure of the Commissioner as the shot starts to fade out. Eleanor : “Ellie~!s like, SO make great Jesters... Or PREPsters even!” As she starts to bounce on the spot and applaud we fade out to Black, only the sound of Nelly and Kannon wailing being heard.
