Golden Heart Chronicles: Lucy In the Sky with Diamonds Chapter 2 Part 2

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/02 Golden Heart Chronicles: The Golden Heart chronicles are an insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we get to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The Golden Heart Chronicles are split into different chapters where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. Read and Enjoy, hopefully this will fulfill you’re thoughts on how Juan Borro really is. Time: 9: 43 P.M Location: MexicoScene: Spring Break “Lucy In the Sky with Diamonds.” Part 2 The scene opens with Juan Borro sitting down holding his knees up to his chest, right by the shore of the beach. It’s a warm night, with a twilight sky and Juan is just sitting there comfortable and peaceful. Most of the kids here for Spring Break are either in clubs, or other hot spot, but as Juan grows older he’s never one for the huge scene with a bunch of people. (Juliet comes out to the beach in a small silk robe. Juan hears her footsteps on the warm golden sand, but just turns his head in the other direction. She taps his shoulder and slowly sits down right by him. Juan doesn’t say anything, but continues to look up at the sparkling sky.) Juliet: Juan, I just don’t understand you and why you always see the need to be alone and separate me from your life. (Juan looks over at her and sees the dad look on her face. She goes to grab Juan’s hand, but unlike previous times Juan holds it back, and ignores Juliet being right aside him, zoning himself out.) Juliet: What have I ever done to you to deserve this, Juan? I love you more than life itself. I follow you from country to country, wanting to be with you. Wanting to be by your side when we go to sleep, but it could never be that way with you. You’re always either out, or trying to remember your past. (Juan stares over at her and looks at her emotional eyes, but still doesn’t respond. Throughout all of this Juan’s expression hasn’t changed one bit.) Juliet: You are such a selfish and stubborn man. I don’t care what happened in you past, but whatever it is, it sure turned you into a piece of sh*t. (Juan finally breaks the silence with just a few words, but a few words with a lot of meaning behind them.) Juan: I can’t do it anymore. Juliet: You can’t do what? Juan: I can’t go on. I though I made everything correct but I really didn’t. Just take a look around Juliet nothing has changed. This all started back when I went to prison. I thought I’d be a good guy wanting to help the Chinese, look where that got me. Then I get out and think a can resurge my business career by going into politics. (Juliet looks on very seriously, as Juan continues his small rant on the last few months of his life.) Juan: Yea, the political me sure got far. I was a joke if anything. Juliet: You aren’t a joke! Juan: Those people sure thought I was a joke. Just goes to show when you think you can do well, it’s really nothing. The same goes with International Incident. I don’t know if these guys realize it, but I wake up every day and don’t feel like I’ve affected any person’s lives. I try to lie to myself and say I do, but I really don’t. All I want to be able to do is influence someone. Juliet: you have honey, you have. (Juliet goes over and lies down on Juan, but Juan has none of it as he gets up and kicks sand to the side, replying angrily and loudly.) Juan: I haven’t done anything. I go around and make fun of the American public and I notice that these people hate me. Juliet: Why would that bother you? Juan: It doesn’t, but when my own people start to feel the same way, my heart hit hits the floor. I put all I can put into it and this is what I get, along with all of the other mistakes in my life. Juliet: Are you saying that what we had was all a mistake? Juan: No, I’m just saying it didn’t amount to anything. I never felt any differently. I thought it would bring me some light, but I received nothing. It’s just too much is going on for me to worry about myself. Juliet: How can you say that. (A tear rolls down the eyes on Juliet and Juan just turns away.) Juliet: Don’t expect me to come back. (Juan doesn’t respond and Juliet leaves very distraught and upset. A small tear is seen rolling down one of Juan’s eyes, but he quickly wipes it off trying to show no real emotion.) (Juan really does love Juliet, but now is a time where they can’t be together.) What has it been, that Juan is doing wrong? Why are all of these feeling coming to him now? Is Juan’s message through wrestling mean nothing to nobody? Does his people still look up to him as their hero? And what does his past have to do with all of these new thought and emotions? (With that said, Juan goes into a flashback.) The scene begins with Juan getting off a plane and arriving in Mexico for Spring Break. Juan has the package the men gave him, and managed to keep everyone from it just like they told him. Now all Juan had to do was bring it to the man. Juan takes out the map that shows where the man who he has to deliver the package to lives, and quickly goes on his way. While on the bus, Juan sees a young couple right near him. (Scene goes to couple) Man: I love you and I hope you know that I do. You know I’ll always be here for you through thick and thin. Meanwhile, Juan is slightly laughing at the corny type vows the young man is making to the woman with him, which is actually all a ploy to probably get laid. Woman: Oh I love you too. You are the sweetest and most heart-filled man I’ll ever meet. All I want to do is be yours. Man: Then fall in my arms, and we can make love tonight. That way no matter what we’ll be together forever. (The woman then falls in the arms of the man, as the two begin to make out, and supposedly sow their love for on another.) As Juan listens in he just realizes how much of a crop of sh*t phony love really is. It shouldn’t be about depending on a person for a good time, but it should be about how much that person affects you and makes you feel. At that point Juan promised he would never be a part of a phony love, or end it if it became phony love, not to say his love with Juliet was phony, but Juan may have had a feeling like it was. Back in the bus, Juan notices how he has arrived at his destination. He pulls the tight ropes above him, to force the driver to stop. Juan gets off of the bus and look ahead at the house where the man lives. Right before Juan goes in he hesitates a it, and decides to see what is actually in the package he is handling. Just so nobody notices him, he goes over into a corner, where nobody can see him, and he is away from public view. He slightly opens the package a little at a time. When he finally makes an opening big enough to slick his finger in he sees this small little eye drop on a little piece of paper. There are a few so Juan decides to take one, wondering what a small thing like that could do. When picking it up, Juan drops it and it falls on his arm being absorbed into his skin. Juan shrugs like nothing happens and is about to go up to the door, when he feel a bit awkward. Juan shakes a it as his head goes into an alternate euphoric universe. He sees all different types of colors, where everyone resembles different types of fruits. In real life Juan walks right into a wall, but is in such a state of euphoria that he can feel absolutely nothing. It gets worse as Juan starts to sweat and feel sick. He then sees leeches all over his body, and starts to scratch him self to get it off. He keeps doing this and his skin begins to wear down, but he doesn’t know how any of it I going on, he then begins to scream in horror as more and more leeches jump on him, until he is broken out of it, by a pedestrian in the street who is holding his frantically. Man: Are you all right? Juan snaps out of it and sees his bloody skin. He becomes nervous and just panics around trying to stop the blood flow. Man: Excuse me can I help you? Juan: No, no, I’m fine, excuse me I’ll be on my way. The man leaves and Juan approaches the door and knocks, while a little out of it. A man slightly opens the door and Juan hands him the bag and speaks. Juan: This is the Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds. The man behind the door nods and takes the bag he then slams the door on Juan. Juan walks back down the steps, but tumbles and falls down the steps completely out of it. With this the scene fades to Black. TO BE CONTINUED…………………….
