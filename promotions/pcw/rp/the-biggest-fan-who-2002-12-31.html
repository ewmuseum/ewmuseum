The Biggest Fan. Who?!

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-31-02 RP 1 Fusion Jan 3rd A huge shopping mall rest in the center of a newly paved parking lot. Two levels can be seen from the entrance and a third level is hidden underground. The roof is black and the signs of the various stores hang from the building. The windows have a mirror effect to them and the afternoon sun reflects brightly off them. Two young boys are dropped off at the front entrance by a green SUV, obviously their mother. The boys notice a large sign taped to the mall window which reads ‘TODAY ONLY. PCW TV CHAMP TERESA TOMAS AUTOGRAPH SIGNING’. In small print lists the time and whereabouts Teresa’s booth will be. The two young boys rush in. Sitting at a table in front of a pizza parlor, Teresa has been signing pictures, tee shirts and a few ball caps all morning. She is dressed in her comfortable Levi’s and a blue Tennessee Titans jersy. The two young boys purchase a photo of Teresa wearing her famous camouflage leather pants and black halter-top. Over her shoulder is her 12-gauge shotgun. Her sandy blonde hair is worn down and she has a fiery look in her eyes. The two boys, one looking to be 16, the other obviously a little brother is around 12 nervously place the photos on the table in front of Teresa. The older one speaks trying to hide his shaky voice. “Could you please sign these photos for us?” Teresa uses her black marker and signs her name over the top left corner. She then slides the pictures back across the table. The two boys grab their autographs and hurry out of line. The older boy sneaks a kiss on Teresa’s cheek before darting off behind his smaller brother. Before Teresa can look up to the next person in line, a deep voice enters her ears. “Hello beautiful.” “You?!” Teresa’s surprise not only shows in her face but her voice also. She stands up facing the man eye to eye. It’s Cassius Deamont in all his silver haired glory. He is wearing baggy blue jeans and a Teresa Tomas tee-shirt. Looking around, Teresa doesn’t see any other Front Office members lurking around. “What do you want Cass? Can’t you see I’m busy with my fans at the moment?” Cass steps back placing his left hand over his chest. “Teresa Baby, I’m hurt. I am your fan as well. Let’s say after you’re done here, you can get busy with me somewhere more private?” A huge black security guard standing behind Teresa begins to step forward. Teresa stops him with her hand keeping a suspicious eye on Cassius. “Did Walden put you up to this? Or was it Brandi? You just tell them that…” Cassius stops Teresa when he grabs her shoulders. He’s not giving up so easy. “None of them knows I’m here. I was hoping since I’m your biggest fan, maybe I can get some special treatment if you know what I mean.” Cassius’s hands move down her arms and toward her chest area. If he were paying attention, he would’ve seen that famous temper sparking in her eyes. Without warning she throws a punch to his gut. When Cass hunches over, Teresa takes the back of his head and slams it into the table several times. Now when she speaks, she’s yelling getting everyone within hearing distance attention. A small crowd forms. “FOR ONE, NO ONE CALLS ME BABY. TWO, NO ONE TOUCHES ME WITHOUT MY SAY SO!” When Teresa pulls his head back for one last bash to the table, Cass gives Teresa puppy dog eyes as in asking why she just attacked him. Teresa pauses for a moment seeming as though she will give pity to the guy. The crowd is chanting “DO IT! DO IT”. A sinister grin forms on the temper ranged redneck. She leans forward, kisses his forehead and slams it into the table one last time. “I think I’m done for the day. Thank y’all for your support.” Security escorts the Top Rope Temptress out the doors and into her Chevy pick up while Cassius Deamont is left lying face first on the autographing table surrounded by Teresa Tomas picture.
