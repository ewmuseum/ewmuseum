Revenge Saga Part 22: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine...pt2

21 July 2002

Date: 21/7/02 Revenge Saga Part 22: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine…Pt2 Fleming shuffled uncomfortably, adjusting his tie a little. He hadn‘t been this nervous since his first day working in the federal agency. There were two main things that bothered him at the moment…The first was his meeting with the madman who was in charge of the Thunderbolts…and the second…the second was getting to the meeting. Fleming had suggested that he go by car, but the Thunderbolts…or to be more precise the man behind the Thunderbolts decided that it was better for him to fly. Not any ordinary flight though, the Thunderbolt known as Mach III was coming to pick him up. Fleming didn’t trust any of the Thunderbolts…never did, so he was more than wary about the arrangement. Thinking about his situation…or more accurately situations, he blamed one person and one person only for the miss dealings, the tragedy and the general unrest in his life. That one person was inevitably the man known as John Crichton. Ever since Fleming was put on his case all those months ago, his life had been one long downward spiral. He had been in more near death experiences than he could count on one hand, he had been forced to betray his country to firstly the Russians and very likely the Thunderbolts and he had lost the woman he loved. He had made enough sacrifices in the pursuit of John Crichton, it was time he was rewarded with something. His mind was brought back to reality by the dramatic swoop of Mach III as circled to land. Within seconds he had landed, and was already making his way up the path to him. Fleming readjusted his tie again and walked forward to meet his ‘chauffeur’. Fleming: Ah…Mach III how are you doing? Mach III: Enough of the damn pleasantries…we’ve got a fairly long flight ahead of us…so I suggest we get on with it! Fleming: Whatever. With that Mach III rises in the air a little, the jet engines latched onto his back allowing him to defy gravity, he holds his hands out in Fleming’s direction. Fleming shrugs a little before grabbing on to Mach III’s wrists and allowing himself to be drawn up, into the air. ‘This isn’t so bad!” He silently surmised, being slowly raised further and further into the air. Mach III: Hold on to your hat! With that some secondary engines upon the wings of Mach III’s battle-suit begin to purr provocatively and then the both of them were flung forward at monumental speed. ************ Logan aka Wolverine reached the ‘Heart o’ the City Motel’ in less than fifteen minutes. He was almost pulled over at least three times for speeding, but managed to ‘resist’ arrest all three times. Wolverine’s hyper senses picked up two scents upon The HandBag Warrior, so he assumed that one of the scents was John Crichton, since he had been in close contact with him. Once he reached the motel, he found out that he was right, both scents were here and these scents would direct him to the exact room Crichton was staying at. Wolverine jumped out of his open-top jeep and pulled his mask over his head. He always did this when going in to battle. There was no bones about it, this would be a battle…a tough one at that…but Wolverine knew that he would be the one coming out on top…after all he had faced The Incredible Hulk before…and survived, barely. He could take this guy, no problem. He stubbed out the last remnants of his cigar upon the paint work of a flash, BMW his jeep was parked next to, before heading in the direction of Crichton’s scent. Warily he continues to follow his nose, the scent takes him to the door of one of the motel rooms. He warily looked around, to both see if he was being watched or to spot something that might suggest that he was walking into a trap. He spotted nothing. Wolverine reached out a gripped the door handle, testing to see if the door had been left open. It hadn’t. He expected this John Crichton to be easy prey…but he didn’t think he was going to be stupid. Wolverine casually stepped backwards, away from the door, and looked round one last time. Lightening quick his foot impacted with the wooden door, breaking the wood away from the lock and almost taking the entire thing off its hinges. The room was pitch black, with curtains drawn to restrict the flow of light to the room. Next came the scream. Wolverine sniffed the air coming from the room. Crichton hadn’t been there for more than an hour…and there were two other scents now emanating from the room…two scents he had never smelt before. Wolverine cautiously peered into the room, to see a couple sat up in bed, looking bemused at the sight before them. Crichton had gone. That much was obvious…the two new scents…plus the surprised couple in the king sized bed suggested as much to him. Wolverine simply turned from the sight before him…and moodily walks back to his jeep. He opens the door and gets in. This would be far more interesting than he first realised…now he would have to hunt Crichton down…quite a feet in a city this big…but he loved to hunt…he was almost enjoying this business again! ************ Crichton opened the door of his car and stepped out onto the concrete pavement. He warily looks around…he had paranoid for a while now…ever since he escaped from the military base in fact…but he had to be…he had a number of people after him now…all of them bad news…all of them very dangerous men. He casually strolls to the back of his car and opens the boot. He extracts his suitcase from the cold grasp of the boot and headed up the path towards the door of Jaden’s house. She had told him that he could come and stay with her whenever he wanted…but he never could bring himself to stay with her…he was trouble or more accurately trouble was attracted to him like flies around shit…a bad analogy he knew, but it was the only one he had. His reasons for taking the chance now were purely pragmatic…they were necessary to his survival…the last thing he wanted to do was bring trouble upon the woman he loved…upon Jaden. He knew that heat from the law…from the superheroes that governed the fair city…and from the villains that attempted to destroy this fair city. In essence he was on his own…he WAS the minority. So many people thought that he was a monster…that he was some kind of ruthless killer…he was not, but that didn’t change opinions. So the question he now asked of him was ‘Should he become this villain the people expected him to be?’…his heart told him no, but he knew that he had a ruthless streak within him…it had even reared its ugly head on occasion. He hadn’t drawn attention to it in a while, but the inner battle still raged on, ravaging his conscience…and his goodwill. He feared that one more battle would set the ‘other’ side of him free. He had to avoid that at all costs. So he brought himself to her home. She kept ‘that’ side of him at bay…well the best anyone could at least…and nobody knew of her home…allowing him to live in peace…for a while at least. His wrestling career was the other thing that kept his mind at peace. It gave him the opportunity to vent some of his anger…it paid well…and it gave him at least the chance to lead a normal life. Only one thing stood before him and this ‘mysterious’ normal life of his…and that was revenge. It had been many months since the death of Aeryn…normally a man would had been past grievance…but he couldn’t…not until her death was reattributed in full. Before her death he was simply wanted by the army…and after her death he was wanted by everybody, including villains. Once Fleming was dead he would have his normal life…or what he conceived to be normal at least. But would his ‘dark side’ emerge before that time? Bits and pieces had already managed to leak through his consciousness, causing him to do something’s that he wasn’t proud of. Like his actions the other week against Heaven Rayne. Yes, she was hurting the woman he loved…but was it really necessary to cause her so much damage? Again he had been subjugated to one of his greatest fears…inflicting harm upon a woman…it was no mere slap either, it was a merciless attack that led to injury…the doctors said that she would be on the injured list for months…that is, if she ever was able to return to wrestling at all. Part of him regretted his actions…the other part was ‘pleased’ by them. He really didn’t know what sickened him the most…injuring the woman, or part of him liking it. Yes, his mind was a very fragile place at the moment…he didn’t specifically know what would set him off, but he did know that it wouldn’t take much…not much at all. ************ It had taken almost an hour to get to their destination…by the end of it Fleming’s arms hurt, but he suspected that Mach III didn’t much care about that. The landing hadn’t been too spectacular either, but he coped with it. After all, the meeting with the ‘big boss’ would be far more painful than what he felt now. He didn’t know the exactly where he had been taken…but he suspected that was the point of it. After dropping Fleming to the ground, Mach III circled around and within seconds landed beside him, with the greatest of ease…making it look like second nature to him. Fleming looked at the great wooden doors that stood before him, as they slowly lowered…covering the harsh looking moat that stood in their path. The castle itself was huge…and it served the purpose it was meant for…perplexing all visitors with the image of grandeur…Fleming knew however, that it was not a king sitting behind the stone walls, it was a villain…the illusion of grandeur did not work for him. Fleming let off a nervous sigh before following Mach III into the castle. This was going to be a long day indeed.
