RENT-A-NEW-HERO

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 27, 2002 Night Shard, Robert Yarney, threw his hands in disgust as he toiled endlessly on the Crusader Mobile. It stopped suddenly the other day as they were pursuing the criminal, 'Jackal,' who was a known hacker in the Bay Area. Now, most hackers were loveable enough just looking for a little thrill. Not Jackal, though. The guy had hacked into the Starbucks coffee house mainframe and ordered one hundred pounds of lima beans instead of coffee beans. Needless to say, this put a lot of San Francisco business men and women in a precarious position. FLASHBACK! Angry Woman: I WANT MY COFFEE! I CAN'T LIVE WITHOUT IT! Night Shard holds his ears as the woman continues to bellow at him. Although he has a soft spot for all women, he can't stand nagging. If he wanted nagging, he would have married Roseanne. Night Shard: Please, please...try to calm down miss. We're doing all we can... Angry Woman: [grabs Night Shard by his shirt] Can you bring us coffee beans? Are you going to do what Jesus did and break some bread and make it turn into coffee beans? Night Shard: [thinks for a moment] Actually, I think he turned water into wine, but... Angry Woman: I WANT MY COFFEE! I HAVE TWO CRAZY KIDS AT HOME THAT NEVER LEAVE ME ALONE AND MY HUSBAND IS HAVING AN AFFAIR WITH OUR MAID! Night Shard: You have a maid? Damn lady, you must be loaded. Why are you so upset again? Crimson puts a vulcan death grip on the woman who falls to the ground unconscious. He coughs lightly and admires his work. The Crimson: Few people know the deadly asian arts of the nerves. It can get one out of many predicaments. Night Shard: Yeah, whatever. That lady nearly blew out my ear drums. I'm guessing this was the Jackal again? The Crimson: I'm afraid so. He's quite the hacker. Not many people can re-route a million dollars worth of coffee beans into produce. Night Shard: [shakes his head] This could be bad. He watches several business men fight over a a can of Folgers instant coffee. Night Shard: Real bad. END FLASHBACK! So there they were with a coffee disaster and the Jackal was nowhere to be found. Luckily, they got an ID on the guy from his old girlfriend who complained that he was spending too much time on his computer. They chased him all the way to the Bay Bridge when inexplicably... The Crimson: Is this car running? Night Shard: [slams his fist against the dash board] God dammit! Yes, the car stopped on the middle of the bridge, and they watched as the Jackal got away in his Miata. And a pink Miata at that. How embarrassing. So Night Shard was left trying to repair the Crusader Mobile which had left them high and dry. There was only one problem: WHAT THE HELL WAS THE PROBLEM? The Crimson: [drinking the juices of an apple using his fangs] Have you had any luck finding the problem in the car? Night Shard: Does it look like I have had any luck? This is the heaviest paper weight in the Bay Area, bar none. The Crimson: [leans his head under the hood] Maybe it's a problem with the fuel injection... Night Shard: Look, you're the vampire and I'm the mechanic. I think I should handle the car related matters. Any word on the Jackal? Crimson continues to suck the juices out of the apple as he strolls around the garage. The Crimson: No, not quite, but his girlfriend hasn't had much contact with him. Since the chase, he's kept a low profile. Night Shard: It doesn't surprise me. The guy's face is plastered all over the place. I wish Tom was here. He's always good with tracking people down. The Crimson: He's still doing that Sportscenter scam. Last I heard he got that loan from the bank. Night Shard: [shocked] No shit? I was sure this wouldn't work. Especially after all that talk with the Joker. I mean, is this a Batman comic or what? The Crimson: Well, we could get a lot of publicity if this Sportscenter thing works out. We aren't exactly welcomed by the public. Night Shard sits up and wipes the oil from his hands. Night Shard: You know, I don't get it. We spend half our day trying to fight crime, and this pathetic city doesn't appreciate a damn thing. I guess charity doesn't get rewarded anymore. The Crimson: No, it doesn't. But we don't expect a reward, do we? Night Shard: No, I guess we don't. [slumps over the hood] Would you mind handing me the wrench? The Crimson: [smiles, walking away] I'm just the blood sucking vampire, remember? You're the mechanic. Laughter resounds down the hall as he walks away. Night Shard shakes his head. Asshole. * * * Robert Yarney stands in front of his mirror wearing only his boxers and his Night Shard mask. His belly hangs out like a sack of potatoes and he has very prominent man breasts. Still, he begins to flex and pose. Night Shard: So, RENT-A-HERO, you want to question us? The Crusaders of Light? You don't know anything about us! You don't know our powers, our mission, or what we're trying to accomplish. Yet instead of helping us, you are being a nuisance and questioning the people that we're trying to help. What's all this stuff about being reborn and that the hero is dead? You aren't superman! You can't just die and come back! Robert grabs an orange and throws it in the air. Before it falls to the ground, Robert uses his telekinetic powers to keep it floating in the air. He smiles as it hovers above his head. Night Shard: You see that? That's true hero power. What can you do? All this negativity makes me sick! I can't stand your attitude, or your partner for that matter. Tell him to get his personal life together, or for the love of god, a counselor! When we meet, you will both learn the power of... He throws both arms in the air, showing huge slabs of cellulite flailing in the air. Night Shard: THE NIGHT SHARD! The door swings open, causing Night Shard to fall to the ground in embarrassment and the orange slamming into his skull. He rubs his head in pain. The Crimson: [holding the door open] Robert...what the hell are you doing? Night Shard: [looking around] Uhm, nothing? The Crimson: Well put your nothing away and get dressed. They spotted Jackal downtown. It seems our hacker friend went into a local pawn shop and tried to sell his car. Night Shard: Alright, well, warm up the car. I think I got it running. The Crimson: Fine. [leaves] And stay away from that mirror. Rob stands up, wiping the orange juice from his face. As he stares into the mirror once more, a heroic smile envelops his face. Night Shard: See you later, RENT-A-NEW-HERO! Same Crusader time, Same Crusader channel! And with that, Night Shard runs out of the room with amazing grace. We all wonder how long it will be before he realizes he's still wearing only his boxers and mask. Okay, we pray he finds them soon.
