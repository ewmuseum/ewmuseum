Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part VII]

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 28, 2002 Gold and Glory is exactly that. The Heartbreakers have waited 2 months to get a shot at the gold that was taken from them, and the glory that will come from winning them back will be orgasmic. There was never a better prize, nor a handsomer one. No man in the tag team division could be happy without holding on to that beautiful prize. They would be unhappy with themselves, unhappy with the business and pretty much unhappy in their personal lives as well. The belts had begun their life on October 22 in the year 2000, born into the hands of Alan Kriegman and Dillan Meyers...collectively known as Goovil. They have taken their toll traveling throughout PCW history, dealing with greedy, self-centered, madmen...but also capturing the greats, the heroes and the legends. Those belts are like the holy grail, because anyone who gets ahold of them have ever lasting eternity in PCW...in the record books and in the minds of the fans. The Heartbreakers had their share of the grail on May 12, 2002, when they defeated one of the very teams that will be in the ring with them this Sunday, in Politically Incorrect. Just like the many other men who have had their hand on the title, The Heartbreakers reigned supreme that one night, targeted by many, but cheered by most. Unfortunately the title reign lasted merely an hour, making it the shortest reign in PCW history. However, this upcoming Sunday, only 4 other men besides the Heartbreakers have even come close to touching the titles... Akiyama...the god...the almighty...the favorite to win this match because of his massive size and power. Nothing more than a giant goof if you ask us. I mean, come on...the man doesn’t even understand the power of the PINK! He prances around with his little Cyclonica, claiming that he’ll banish us. The only thing that will be banished is him, from the match, at the hands of Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart...and they can’t wait to get their “hands” on him. The Chef...a hall of famer. We all know Jack Valentine’s history with hall of famers...Trashcan Man, Ed...they both competed head to head with Valentine, and nearly got their asses handed to by the then rookie. Third time’s a charm Chef, and this Sunday, you and your little partner Alexis...isn’t Alexis a girl’s name? Alexis is a girl’s name! Anyways, you and sexy lexy will be ours for the chopping, and we’ll give you a salad to toss like you’ve never seen before Chef. And finally, Politically Incorrect. The Political Machine...ooh, we’re scared. It’s not like we didn’t beat you already, and become the first ever tag team to have an UNDEFEATED reign! In all seriousness Lovecraft and Powell, you two men found out what happened when you disrespected the PINK! and soon enough the others will find out and you will all do the “job” that we will love forever...or at least for a few minutes. The Heartbreakers never cared a cent for anything but the PCW tag team titles, and of course the PINK!...and well, they did care about joining Pretty Cool Guys...oh yeah, they also care about their fans...ahem...anyways, they mainly care about the tag team straps. With the first color in the east, a PINK! one, Valentine and Heart awoke and sat up. For awhile they gazed at the scroll of the morning bank and the spars and hanging canvas of the brig, like a man who wakes in a strange bed, with a child's simplicity of wonder. They wondered above all what ailed him, what they had lost, what disfavor had been done to them, which they knew they should resent, yet had forgotten. And then, like a river bursting through a dam, the truth rolled on them its instantaneous volume: their memory teemed with speech and pictures that the yshould never again forget; and they both sprang to their feet, glanced at the other, and both began to walk violently to and fro by the bed. Jack Valentine: “God...God...God!” Johnny Heart: “Akiyama?” Jack Valentine: “No...we’re taking this way too lightly...we need to toughen up, prepare...bask in the glory of this PINK! morning and recollect all of the harm that has been done to us.” Johnny Heart: “What harm?” Jack Valentine: “Don’t you get it...those are our titles that we are fighting for on Sunday, yet the higher ups seem to want to claim them as vacant.” Johnny Heart: “It’s just our chance to become 2-Time champions Jack...a feat that only few have done.” Jack Valentine: “I don’t care...I’m sick of everyone disrespecting the PINK! The fans respect it...why don’t the other wrestlers catch on.” Johnny Heart: “They aren’t as pretty as us?” Jack Valentine: “Good answer.” An hour went by, while the day came brighter, and the sun rose and drank up the clouds: an hour of silence in the house, an hour of agony beyond narration for the Heartbreakers. They prayed long and hard to the gods of the PINK! sunrise and thought about what it would be like to have those belts back. They neither acquitted nor condemned himself of winning the match anymore: they no longer thought that they suffered. Pictures of them handing the title belts over to Tyler Nelson ran through each of their minds over and over. And time passed, and the sun swam higher, and his torment of those pictures were not abated. Jack Valentine: “Akiyama is obviously our biggest competition Johnny...” Johnny Heart: “What about Pretty Cool Guys?” Jack Valentine: “I lost all respect for them when they turned their backs on us...and...and...and...” Jack bursts out in tears. Johnny walks over to Jack and consoles him by rubbing his back. Jack Valentine: “I’m ok...I’m ok. Those bastards kicked us to the curb and ruined my dreams of becoming a PoP sensation. They will pay...they will pay!” Johnny Heart: “Um...what about Akiyama?” Jack Valentine: “Oh yeah...the biggest disrespector of PINK! that I have ever laid my beautiful brown, with a tint of green eyes on. He says we have to chain together to win this match, but we can’t because of our own selfish desires? Hell, we link up all the time, don’t we Johnny?” Johnny Heart: (Smirking) “Of course we do.” Jack Valentine: “You want a monster Akiyama...I’ve got a monster for you right here in my pants...” Both of The Heartbreakers pause and laugh. Jack Valentine: (Continuing) “It’s called the Valentine’s Day Massacre...hell, me and Johnny both have monsters in our pants...” Johnny Heart: “That’s the double spinning wheel kick...right?” Jack Valentine: “Right.” Johnny Heart: “Why’s that move named after you?” Jack Valentine: “Oh shut up, most of our moves are named after you anyways.” Johnny Heart: (Adjusting himself) “Yeah, I guess you’re right.” Jack Valentine: “Anyhoo...” Johnny Heart: “Why are we so concerned with Akiyama? Shouldn’t we care about the other 8 teams that are going to be trying to knock us around...” Laughing from the cake boys again. Jack Valentine: “Those other 8 teams are not a god. Besides, Akiyama is the only one that poses a challenge to us. He is the one who laid us out.” Johnny Heart: (Laughing) “You said laid.” Jack Valentine: “I did, didn’t I?” The pair high fives, and as they do the sky blackens and a lightning bolt crashes to the ground. Jack Valentine: “You don’t think that was him...do you?” There was no more said by one or other, and they shook in fear. Akiyama was the one man that they feared. The man they feared they could not escape. The man they wanted no part of in the ring on Sunday. Johnny Heart: “You know, I just thought of something Jack...” Jack Valentine: “What’s that hot stuff?” Johnny Heart: “We don’t have to pin Akiyama...all we have to do is get him over the top rope.” Jack Valentine: (Like an idea just flashed into his head) “Oh yeah...as much as I would love to mount him....for the 1, 2, 3 of course...we can trick him over the top rope. With the power of us both, hell, we could even knock him over the top rope.” Johnny Heart: “A hard knock.” Jack Valentine: “Very hard.” As the two converse about how they are going to eliminate Akiyama, they snicker and laugh at the ambiguously gay comments as the scene fades out to PINK!
