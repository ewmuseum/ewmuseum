Alliances Promo #3

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/21/02 Scandalous Skylar Green sits in an empty press room with a folding chair and folding table in the center with only a little light shining down on the furniture. SSG, wearing a black Armani suit and loafers for no reason, faces the camera as we open to the promo. SSG is tacking away at an old typewriter From somewhere off camera, a CD players blasts 'Better Than You' by Metallica. SSG:'And so it comes to this. Alliances. Next to Carnival of Carnage and March Madness it'll be the biggest clusterf*ck of the year. I'd hate to say it but someone might actually might actually beat me.' A moment of silence. SSG:'BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!' And with a sigh, Skylar Green continues his promo. SSG:'No, seriously, there's a chance that lightning will strike me or something and I won't be able to kick out of anyone's pitiful offense or that I'll tap out to some laughable submission. But I won't let that happen. It's not just for me, no the fate of the entire world rests upon me.' As he talks, Skylar brings out a piece of newspaper from his pocket. SSG:'How do I know? Well, one time when I opened the minibar in the backstage area, I came upon a portal to another dimension. A dimension of pure unadulterated suck. And a paper shot out from the portal. It was a newspaper from the other dimension, dated November 24th. Here's what it said.' It was a short-lived celebration for the wrestler who eliminated The End from the Alliances Main Event. The End already failed to win the Alamo Match and now a wrestler who no one was even expecting to land a punch eliminated a favorite to carry his team to victory. What happened? The arena itself vanished without a trace, but that wasn't the worst of it. Later that day, traffic came to a standstill as four equestrian archangels representing War, Famine, Pestilence, and Death, respectively, made their way down the interstate. Veritable chaos erupted as cars slammed into each other, swarms of locusts devoured pedestrians and others in the open air. The seas and rivers ran red with blood; the resident vampire population is VERY happy to say the least. The dead rose with malicious intent to destroy us all. Luckily their advanced decomposition meant that a good hard kick to the head would stop them. Denise Cooper, 24, described the events. 'I was driving my new red Dodge Neon off the dealer lot when these four guys on horses came right at my car. Each of them had to be about 10 ft. tall,' said the scarred, thinning Cooper who appeared to be afflicted by polio, scurvy, and rickets. 33-year-old Donald Lewis mirrored Cooper's sentiments. 'I was just changing a tire off Exit 49 at the Mobile station when I saw this huge guy with a scythe.' Your intrepid news reporter did some checking and Nacosto Nerezza has an airtight alibi for not being the culprit. Apparently he was seeing a doctor about a long-standing problem erectile dysfunction (and NOBODY would make that up). He rode by and sliced some guy's Ford Tundra in half. He didn't dive out of the way in time and got half his head cut off. Hadn't seen anything like that since that one Monty Python movie.' Hospitals around the state instantly filled with scores of people suffering from malnutrition for no apparent reason. Several hundred thousands of people complained of injuries ranging from simple lacerations to mortal wounds. All of those treated, though there were many who could not be due to overcrowding, (hospitals across the nation declared code green) seemed to have every illness known to medical science. In fact, pathologists have discovered 43 new diseases thatnks to the rapid influx of victims. Of the diseased persons, none worse than the one man who came in with Five-Star Fever, only afflicting those working in Five-Star Wrestling. Workers from OSW also came down with the bubonic plague, which doctors have redubbed 'Old School Plague;' their NSW counterparts suffer from Herpes Zoster covering their entire body, but that's because of Jane Reagan, not the Horsemen. RUSH workers suffer from mediocrity and anonymity, which is worse than being diseased. Setting aside their licensing differences, the heroes and anti-heroes of various comic book universes, led by Rent-a-Heroin (not a misspelling, this is his new nickname as the masked wrestler/superhero is actually addicted to the drug; which would explain his erratic and delusional behavior) joined forces to defeat these menacing foes. Slightly Injured in battle were Batman, the worthless Black Knight who used to get his ass kicked with the Avengers (I think he's wrestling as Mark Amulet) Green Arrow, Spiderman, Captain America, Black Panther (or as he calls himself these days, Rush Mason) and Havoc. Also sustaining more serious injuries were Cable, Gambit (Mark Michigan to the uninitiated), Hulk, (he's such a pussy nowadays, moonlighting under the name Jon Kano) Prototype, half the roster of the New Gods, Scarlet Witch, Spiderwoman, Bishop, the Fantastic Four, Martian Manhunter (calling himself Bob Vurdo), Cloak and Dagger, Domino, Puck, and Hawkeye. 'The battle finally left the country as the Horsemen hid in a Kid Rock concert long enough to draw a pentagram on the ground that transported them to Egypt' Ozzy Osbourne reported while biting into the head of the remains of the Lindbergh Baby. There, to this very day and hour, Superman, Iron Man, War Machine, Flash, Cyclops, Green Lantern, (Kyle Raynor, not the black one) Spawn, and Lobo fight to subdue the evil foursome while their evil minions attempt to animate the Sphynx. The heroes seem to be losing and if they fail all is lost. Oh... The sun eclipsed itself. There was no moon in the sky, it was pulled out of its orbit and should collide with Earth in about a week. We are so royally screwed. SSG:'So as you can see, I'm not just fighting for myself. The good of the world rests upon my shoulders. I can't let them down. After all, Rent-A-Hero has in two dimensions already.'
