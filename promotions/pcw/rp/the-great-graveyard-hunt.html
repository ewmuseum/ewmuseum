The Great Graveyard Hunt!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [The scene opens at the ‘Weepy Willow Graveyard’ on a bright, sunny day. A black car drives along a gravel road, past a number of graves.It then comes to a stop at the end of the intersection. The driver-side door opens and out steps Flaymin wearing a bright red suit. He then walks over the passenger side door and opens it.A moment later, Heather steps out and shakes her head] Heather: I can’t believe you wore that damn red suit. Flaymin: The black one is in the wash! Heather: You look like you’re here to raise the dead. Flaymin: Why would I want to do that? Heather: I don’t know. [Flaymin shakes his head in confusion.He then pulls out a map from his pocket] Flaymin: Says here that Angela’s grave is right over there. Heather: Near that shady tree? Flaymin: Yep. [Flaymin and Heather walk over to the grave near the large tree.Along the way they pass a bunch of other graves as Flaymin reads the labels on the tombstones] Flaymin: ‘In loving memory’….. ‘You will be missed’….Can’t these people come up with anything original? Heather: Like what? Flaymin: I don’t know…. How about ‘Blam! Blam! You got shot in the head and now your dead- thanks for the memories’. Heather: Ugh!Thank God you’re not reading the eulogy. [Having arrived at the shady tree, Flaymin looks at the tombstone directly underneath it] Flaymin: ‘Erica Michaels’. Heather: This isn’t Angela’s grave. [Once again, Flaymin pulls out the map and begins to read it] Flaymin: Look at what it says here. [Heather looks at the map] Heather: I think you’re reading it the wrong way. Flaymin: You mean its in Morse Code? Heather: No idiot, I mean you’re holding it upside down. Flaymin: Oh. [Flaymin turns the map so that its facing the right way.He looks at it and scratches his head in bewilderment] Heather: Where did you get this map from? Flaymin: The guy at the front gate. Heather: You mean that guy in shabby clothes…. Flaymin: Yep. Heather: Who was unshaven and with his hair all over the place… Flaymin: Uh huh. Heather: And who smelt like a pile of shit….. Flaymin: That’s the one. Heather: He’s a fucking hobo!! [Flaymin pauses for a moment] Flaymin: Really?He didn’t look gay. Angela: For fuck’s sake you got ripped off. Flaymin: That asshole! [Flaymin suddenly storms off towards the car] Heather: Where are you going? Flaymin: To find the guy and get my money back! Heather: What about finding Angela’s grave. Flaymin: She’s dead and buried- its not like she’s going anywhere in a hurry or anything. Heather: Forget about the hobo- lets just try and find her grave. [Flaymin reluctantly nods his head] Flaymin: Fine. Heather: There has to be an administration office or a reception area here somewhere.I think I saw one near the Crematory. Flaymin: Cool, I have a hankering for some whipped cream! [Heather raises an eyebrow at Flaymin. Flaymin just smiles in response as they both walk through the graveyard towards the reception area] Heather: Doesn’t seeing all these graves make you wonder? Flaymin: Yep.I could be making one hell of a living selling tombstones…. Heather: No I meant, doesn’t it make you wonder about life and how fragile it is. Flaymin: Not really. Heather: Well it makes me wonder. Flaymin: All I know is that the Cream place we’re going to is making me hungry. [A few minutes pass before Flaymin and Angela finally arrive at the reception besides the Crematorium. They walk over to the friendly-looking elderly receptionist] Receptionist: How can I help you? Flaymin: Double choc-chip with whipped cream, thanks. [Heather pushes Flaymin aside] Heather: We’re looking for an Angela who was buried here last week. Receptionist: What is her surname? Flaymin: I don’t know.I think it starts with an A….Or was that an F…..No wait, I think it was a K…. Heck, I think you’re better checking it all off. [The receptionist pauses in bewilderment and gives Flaymin a blank stare] Flaymin: Hey! Hey!Wake up Grandma! [Heather slaps Flaymin across the back of the head] Heather: I’m sorry but is there a way to look her up without a surname? Receptionist: Sure.When did she pass away? Heather: About two weeks ago. Receptionist: And did she die in this city? Flaymin: No she died on the other side of the country. Receptionist: And they flew her body here? Flaymin: Not that I recall. [Heather looks at the receptionist who in turn looks up at Flaymin] Receptionist: Are you sure you’ve come to the right cemetery? Flaymin: I don’t know, I was hoping you’d be able to tell me that. Heather: If she didn’t die here and she wasn’t buried here- what the hell are we doing here?!?! Flaymin: Settle down…. You’ll wake up the dead. Receptionist: Sir, I think you may have your details crossed. Flaymin: Maybe so.Anyways, thanks for your help! [Flaymin turns around and walks away as Angela rubs her head in frustration] ……Camera fades out….
