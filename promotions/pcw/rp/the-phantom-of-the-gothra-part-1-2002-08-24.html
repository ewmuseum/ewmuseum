The Phantom Of The Gothra-Part 1

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-24-2002 Voice Of Rent-A-Villain:'Sometimes in life....we do things we know we'd never do under any other circumstances.....things that we'd NEVER fathom having to do.....but because of events in our lives, we have no other choice but to make an impact by going to extremes...so we enter alternate realities and worlds we would never dare set foot in....and we realize that we must overcome fear to overcome the adversary that stands in our way...it is for that reason that I...' Voice of Brandi:'What's with the one man speech? And why are all the lights off?!' ..::/::..The pitch black scene with only the voice of Rent-A-Villain breaking the eerie silence is shortly murdered, the ominous effect completely destroyed as we hear the flicking of a switch, the lights coming up full blast as we find Rent-A-Villain, decked out in his unusual vice-versa red and black mask, see through pants, elaborate body paint, and see-through pants as he angrily pulls at his honey blonde and pitch black streaked hair as he angrily stomps about in place like a little school girl in the midst of a temper tantrum. Across form him stands the Queen of PCW and dating interest, Brandi, who folds her arms and shakes her head towards our vile villain as the two of them stand in front of what appears to be a random store in the mall, the various mall patrons walking by unconcerned with what is going on our vile villain exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'BRANDIIIIIII.....YOU KILLED MY EFFECT!! How many more RP's do you think I'll get the chance to use that Twilight Zone intro again!?' ..::/::..Rent-A-Villain pouts, the mood for his intro killed as Brandi just giggles at how cute he can be when he's angry. Brandi pinches Rent-A-Villain's tight abs, making him squirm like a newborn which is followed by a tiny giggle by the friendly fiend, Brandi breaking his look of anger as she replies with her soft, angelic voice..::/::..Brandi:'Oh don't be silly...there was NO SENSE in making everything dark like that. Besides, people were staring. Anyways, we're here now....are you ready to go in or not?' ..::/::..Our vile villain slightly quivers, turning to his right and looking up as he looks upon the banner of this particular mall store....this store not just any ordinary venue for our friendly fiend. Looking upon the jagged red letter title, it reads, 'Warm Subject....No We're Not Hot Topic', just reading that title making our villain quiver with fear as his eyes then wander over to the glistening display windows at the store. His body almost breaks into shock as he looks upon bondage pants, spiked dog collars, and wide array's of punk band shirts....all staples of clothing that even in villain form, he has come to despise. With a nervously strong gulp, he the turns back to Brandi and replies..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Brandi....I have faced wrestling soccer players.....bested 10 men in one match....have been thrown off buildings....I've even been handcuffed to prison bars and beaten to an inch of my life....I'm EVEN going into the most hardcore and life-shortening match in PCW history, a 3 way No DQ match between Darkstar, myself, and Snow.....a match that will take years off our lives and probably go down in the annals of PCW history as the most horrific match ever to be shown on pay-per-view. This match will make a Vamprella comic look like an issue of Reader's Digest.....BUT I AM NOT GOING IN THAT STORE!! I refuse to try to enter the 'Goth Side' so I may truly understand the mysteries of Goth-hood and have an edge on beating my two opponents! This was a terrible idea...' ..::/::..Brandi bats away a loose strand of her auburn brown hair away from her eyes, obviously not wanting to watch Rent-A-Villain submit to his fears as she takes him by the hand and encouragingly replies..::/::.. Brandi:'Don't chicken out Renty....it's mind over matter. You HAVE to see this side of things for you to truly understand what it is your facing, besides, it'd be nice to see you in some bondage pants.' *She giggles slightly as Rent-A-Villain continues to look quite peeved* 'Come on....if you do this for me....I'll give you something you REEEEALLY want...'Rent-A-Villain:'You mean....'Brandi:'Yes...'Rent-A-Villain:'With the....'Brandi:'Mmmhmmm... Rent-A-Villain:'So if I do this....I can get some hot, steaming......QUAKER OATMEAL!!?!?!?!! Brandi:'Yup...with honey too! And then me and you can watch Cowboy Bebop on DVD!' ..::/::..Rent-A-Villain looks to the camera, a smile overcoming his face as he says to the audience..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'Now that's what I call a woman....beautiful....smart....can wrestle....knows how to make oatmeal....AND she's got joystick thumbs. They don't make em' like her no more good viewers!' *Looking back to Brandi* 'ALRIGHT ALRIGHT!! BY THE MIGHT OF MEPHISTO, I'LL ENTER THE STRANGE GOTHIC GARDEN OF WARM SUBJECT AND SLAY MY FEARS OF GOTH-HOOD....BUT YOU BETTER HAVE A LOT OF HONEY IN THAT OATMEAL WHEN THIS IS OVER!! ONWARD BRANDI!!' ..::/::..Our vile villain, turns to his right again, walking with his chest upright and his head held high....only to run SMACKDAB into the glass display window of Warm Subject as he slides off it slowly, leaving fingerprint streaks upon it before hitting the floor. Shortly, he bounces back up and exclaims excitedly..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'THIS APPEARS TO BE SOME SORT OF FORCEFIELD TO WITHHOLD ME FROM ENTERING THIS VENUE OF VILLAINY!! I SHALL HAVE TO USE MY SUPERSONICULTRASMASHMOUTH...'Brandi:'Ummmm Renty....that's the display window, the entrance is to the right.' ..::/::..Our villain looks to his right, seeing the gapping area which is the entrance to the store. With a nervous blush and complimentary smile, he looks to Brandi and says..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Guess I need to get that Rent-A-Vision adjusted...I wonder if they have prescriptions for Kryptonians. ONWARD!!' ..::/::..Brandi giggles, following behind our friendly fiend as he enters the punk/goth novelty store known as Warm Subject, the two greeted by 'Tear Away' from Drowning Pool as it blares over the speakers, the purple and red lighting setting the unusual mood for the store as the two bare witness to the unusual items littering the store....from glow in the dark thongs to full replica Vampire Lestat style outfits. As Rent-A-Villain eyes the right wall littered with various band shirts from the Misfits to Jimmy Eat World, Brandi taps our vile villain on the shoulder as he turns around, Brandi brandishing a white belly shirt to him with 'Bootylicious' written in blue bubble letters on it as she asks..::/::.. Brandi:'You think this'll look cute on me?'Rent-A-Villain:'I wouldn't doubt that, but what is this flavor of 'bootylicious' and where may I taste it?' ..::/::..Brandi sighs, turning from Rent-A-Villain as she begins to browse some of the other shirts on the horizontal rack, our vile villain's eye catching a black t-shirt reading,'If I close my eyes, will you disappear like all my other nightmares?' Appalled, by the message, our villain replies..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'BY THE ATROCIOUSNESS OF DOCTOR OCTOPUS, THAT SHIRT JUST INSULTED ME?! This must be some kind of new sentient being who uses mental suggestion to get you to read the shirt so it can insult you...well I'm not reading THAT shirt aga....D'OH!! I'm not reading it aga...D'OH!! I AM NOT GOING TO.....D'OH!!!' ..::/::..In the middle of his battle of wits with a t-shirt, which the shirt is obviously winning, Rent-A-Villain is suddenly approached by a young woman with heavily died purple hair and green eyes. Her dress sense is about as unique as Rent-A-Villain's as she is donned within a leather corset top, baggy black bondage pants, and flaming red and yellow boots, her face littered with piercings from her eyebrow to her lip as she looks to Rent-A-Villain and asks..::/::.. Gothic Girl:'May I help you sir?' Rent-A-Villain:'GYAH...IT'S MISS METALICO!! I THOUGHT YOU DIED IN ISSUE 26 OF THUNDERBOTS OF FATE!! PREPARE TO BE VILLAIN-NATED BY ME...BOW TO MY...' *deep deeep breath by Rent-A-Villain as the woman watches on dumbfounded* 'EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEVIL!!! I'm getting better at this.'..::/::..Undaunted by the silly attack line of Rent-A-Villain coupled with his raised arms and 'evil' wide-eyed look, she simply rolls her eyes and says..::/::.. Gothic Girl:'I'm a sales clerk here sir...you know, I help you with stuff.'Rent-A-Villain:'Oh...well excuse me metallic saleswoman of gothic wear and accessories, I had no idea the dress code for sales clerks got so relaxed. I came in to....well....this feels dirty....try some of your...' *gulp* 'Gothic wear.'Sales Clerk:'Well we've got a wide selection of punk and goth styles to choose from, I'm sure we'll find a look that suits you. Just feel free to look around and if you need anything, let me know.' Brandi:'RENTY!!!'..::/::..The sales clerk and Rent-A-Villain turn to their right, where they find Brandi with an angel costume in hand, complete with soft fluffed wings and a silver halo included. Apparently liking what she has in her grasp, Brandi says..::/::.. Brandi:'Renty, I'm going to try this on...I'll be RIGHT back. Miss, changing rooms?' Sales Clerk:'Right there next to 'whips and whipped cream'. Rent-A-Villain:'Why would anyone want to buy whips and whipped cream together? Humans and their silly customs...' ..::/::..Shortly, Brandi has located and entered one of the changing rooms, our vile villain still browsing the shirts as a chorus of 'D'OH'S!!' rings through the shop....the chain breaking when suddenly, Brandi's usually pillowy soft voice exclaims..::/::.. Brandi:'WHAT THE.....HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEELP!!!!'..::/::..Our vile villain's head shoots over to the dressing rooms area, shortly followed by Rent-A-Villain dashing towards the changing area with extreme haste. The screaming has stopped, alarming our villain as he kicks in the door where he finds Brandi's white cut off shirt hanging off of. His eyes immediately shoot to a trap door that has revealed itself in the dressing room, Brandi's silver necklace with the cursive 'B' charm strewn on the floor as friendly fiend takes it within hand, knowing she would NEVER take it off unless to wrestle or if it was snatched from around her neck. Gripping it in his hand, our vile villain looks at the trap door and exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Well....it wouldn't be a Rent-A-Villain RP without SOMETHING dumb happening. Noticing the 'scary movie' overtones of this situation, I might as well expel the obligatory 'common white guy' line in this situation....' *ahem* 'I GUESS I BETTER GO CHECK IT OUT!!! AWAY I GO!!'..::/::..With that, Rent-A-Villain leaps high into the air, falling down the dark reaches of the trap door as he can be heard yelling on the way down..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'THIS IS A LOT FARTHER THEN I THOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUGHTTTTTTT........::/::..With that, the scene fades, these mystery of the trap door and Brandi's disappearance yet to be explained. WHAT WILL HAPPEN TO BRANDI AND RENT-A-VILLAIN?! WHO ACTIVATED THAT TRAP DOOR?! DID BRANDI PAY FOR THAT COSTUME FIRST?! FIND OUT NEXT TIME AS RENT-A-VILLAIN DOES WHAT HE DOES EVERY RP.....TRY TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD...ONE DUMB SCHEME AT A TIME!!
