The Calling (Playaz Inc)

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 December 'Is dat a mothafuckin word?' A dank, grim setting isn't the ideal opening for a scene but nonetheless this next installment known as 'The Calling' opens in such a backdrop. Bland dull walls, poor quality furnishings clutter the living quarters as the camera fights against a mist of smoke, giving the whole room an artificial feel of the outside with its array of clouds. A few spontaneous coughs and the sound of people in conversation indicate activity when suddenly strolling through a doorway steps a young white male who appears pale in complexion, rivaled only by that of Pranks With Attitude's Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. The youngsters hair a dull blonde, scruffy and bundled over his scalp my hay whilst his clothes appeared dirty, sagging over his scrawny body, way to big for his physical build. 'Oh, biatch, where da fuck is dat home grow?' said a distinctive voice with a harsh tone. The young boys eyes widened with a touch of fear as he fed his hand into his pocket producing a small cube of a dark substance contained in a small polythene bag. Taking a large gulp the teenage lad stepped forward, handing the merchandise to none other than Playaz Inc's Ebonics ranting Statuz Quo. 'Yo Statuz stop being so hard on the boy-....' injected fellow Playaz Inc member Dante Jackson as he rattled a small green bag, removing a number of plastic pieces and aligning them along a holder. 'Anyway it's your turn!' continued the African American as he stared cold heartedly towards his tag team partner who sat quietly rolling two pieces of wafer thin papers and crushing the substance handed minutes earlier, creating a 'joint'. Peering down it appeared as though the former Tag Team Champions were passing the time away with a competitive game of Scrabble while indulging in a few relaxant narcotics. The game is still in its infancy as the board holds merely three words, one of which is 'ship' and stepping up to the plate is Statuz Quo scratching his, looking all confused. 'Check out dis mothafuckin bad boy of a word, Dante!' roared a jovial Statuz Quo as he took another drag of his joint. Panting and wheezing he handed over the relaxant to Dante Jackson who wasted no time in taking the item down to its roach, expressing his satisfaction with a wide gaping smile. 'Wha-....' Quo then proceeded to place a 'K' next to the existing 'S' that belonged to the word ship, following up with a blank playing piece that represents the players choice of letter. The already 'S' 'K' and blank piece were then greeted by a 'T' and 'R' which ran onto a triple letter score, leaving a smug looking Statuz Quo and more importantly a very confused Dante Jackson. 'SK8TR - HOLLA BACK YOUNGIN!!!' cried the Ebonics ranting Negro has he held a triumphant pose aloft. Flickering his eyes and trying to retain his laughter, even to the point of patting his forehead in disbelief. Holding a dumbfounded facial expression, the N.W.A fan Dante Jackson finally replied to his colleague. 'You can't have that word brother, there's no damn numbers in Scrabble and there's no rule concerning Ebonics, nice try but this blackman ain't no fool!?!' With a look of pent-up frustration Statuz Quo grudgingly took back his set of letters and re-arranged them foaming the word 'STAR' courtesy of the blank playing piece. Making a mere a four point word Statuz Quo began to pout with a few well chosen words before using his rage in a more physical manner as he picked up the board and threw it towards the young white male who stood motionless overlooking the charade. - SMASH - The board turned to debris as it narrowly missed the Playaz Inc guest and crashed against the dank walls of the dwelling. Portraying a spoilt child Statuz Quo slouched into his dusty sofa picking up a crimpled newspaper and immersing himself in the flowing black print, his head swaying from one sentence to another. 'Well I guess that's it-.... Sour loser-....' The room during this time has been subjected to a dense silence with Statuz Quo reading the already mentioned newspaper and Dante Jackson lighting up another 'spliff' and soaking its content heavily. One puff after another and Dante Jackson hogged the joint to himself gazing glassy eyed at his tag team comrade in a trance like state. 'This be some sweet shit brother!' 'I see the stock market is in crisis again-...' said Statuz Quo from Dante's trance like portrayal. Shaking his head and looking rather star struck Dante speaks up once more. 'Did you say something?' Shaking his head in a 'No' fashion, Dante peered down at the joint, lowering it into an ashtray that sat on a neighbouring coffee table, one with a transparent glasswork surface. Lifting a hand full of envelopes, the former Champion siphoned through them one by one, disregarding the more important, namely bills of sort. 'GAS BILL!!!' 'WATER BILL!!!' 'RENT!!!' Literally throwing the latter Dante finally stopped at one unmarked envelope and tore it open via his nimble fingers. Fingering through the print, the former RUSH superstar looked almost in fright, his mouth ajar, possibly stuck for words, a perfect time for Statuz Quo to make an impact. 'What iz dat shit? It betta not be some mothafuckin eviction notice, with Kannon taxing my ass he'd be lucky to get a fuckin French franc, let alone any Nigga American money biatch!' A few timely stutters and Dante proceeded to read aloud. 'Dear Playaz Inc, Pranks With Attitude would like to offer a select few the opportunity to get back into the Tag Team title scene by becoming the number one contenders via a meeting on the address on the back of this letter. No ulterior motives, no sinister master plan, just a competitive contest between those we feel worthy of our Championships, in this case both Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo, collectively known as Playaz Inc. All the details have been branded on the back of this letter, enclosed is the address and directions. Hope to see you there, Thanks Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A)' The Ambassador of Ebonics Statuz Quo gazed star struck and confused. His face cringing Statuz Quo replied breaking up the moment of silence, talking in his harsh tone of voice. 'Speak fuckin Negro 'n' not some white mothafuckin language like English Dante, huh?!?' 'Dear fuckin homeys Playaz Inc, Pussies With Attitude wanna give you bruv'ors a chance to win back da PCW Tag Team mothafuckin Championship by offering you guyz the number one contender ship by showing up at a little bruv'erly get together comprendes. P.W.A have nothing up des sleeves, no fuckin tricks, no backstabbing antics, just a simple contest for me main men Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo. All da details are on the back of dis letter, enclosed is da address and directions. Hope you mothafuckin black boyz don't pussy out and come, Holla Back Youngin Crackaz With da Attitude (P.W.A)' With a huge sigh Statuz nodded in a gesture to say he understood. Both Playaz Inc members soaked in the information and finally it was Quo to express his reaction towards this P.W.A invite. 'I'm gonna hit dat Jester with me newest move bruv'er, da LOLOLOLSMACKAMOTHAFUCKINJESTERHOLLABACKYOUNGIIIIN!!! So I say Playaz Inc get des asses in gear and fuckin show deez white crackaz-.... Dante to the mothafuckin Harlem Negroid Mobile!!!' roared Statuz Quo quite jovial, pointing to the entrance of the apartment and looking to have hit a copyright infringement with his subtle Bubba Ray Dudley impersonation. With the duo racing out, almost knocking of their poor white slave, the scene momentarily closed. With a flicker the scene closed and re-opened outside in the sunshine of Harlem New York capturing both members of Playaz Inc getting into their choice of transport. With a roar of the engine that protruded from the bonnet and a few jerks from the tinted windowed Cadillac, the hydraulics kicked in and eventually the vehicle shot off, bouncing down the street like a child’s space hopper. 'WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!' Watching the vehicle vanish into the distance the scene finally drew too a close leaving nothing but a blank image, no sound, simply nothingness, closing a caper that has involved one time PCW Tag Team Champions Playaz Inc. - To be continued -
