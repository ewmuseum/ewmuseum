The Training - Chapter 3 - The funding

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th August 2002 ***OOC Note: The following RPs are from a series I started while I was back in PWA. I shall be carrying on the series here in PCW so I figured it might help to provide you with the beginning segments of the series. Thanks :-)*** Chapter 3 – The funding RECAP: Since Charlotte Lawrence has been introduced to on screen viewers, she has caused a big splash. First she revealed to boyfriend Joe Nero that she was illiterate, and in an emotional scene he told her that he didn’t care. One hot summers day, she also revealed to Joe whilst cooking the dinner that she wanted to start a career. In wrestling. Now her heart is set on this path of roller coaster rides, she has flung herself at it, watching videos of classic matches from years gone by. (The scene fades into a large space, which on first impressions appears to be a boardroom. The centre of the room is a gigantic oak table, with a vase of blooming flowers resting on a frilly piece of cloth in the middle. The walls are covered with a warm red patterned wallpaper, and have various pieces of artistic magic hanging from the wall, but the main one is in the centre. Two metres high by one metre wide it is also the biggest. The mahogany frame engulfs a full colour picture of Snow on his knees in the middle of a wrestling ring hugging the PCW World Championship Title, blood dripping gorily from his forehead. The light glistens off his sweat soaked body, as well as shining off the golden plates permanently stuck to the pure leather strap. A small caption in white tahoma font in the bottom right hand corner reads: PCW Shanghaied 2, January 27th, 2002.) (Back into the present day, and three people in suits sit around the table, the lights from the ceiling focusing down on them. The constant patter of rain on the windows can be heard through the silence, which is only broken by one of the men speaking up.) Michael Davenport: Have any of the trainees sustained new injuries during this week of training? (Michael looks up as he removes his silver rimmed glasses and places them carefully on the desk in front of him, while also letting go of a single piece of paper that has a list of names. Young, in his early twenties, with spiked up ginger hair and freckles around his cheeks, he certainly seems like your typical ‘Jack the lad’. This image is not complimented though by the smart black suit and blue tie he is wearing. His high squeaky voice attracts attention from two colleagues, one of whom stops chewing on his biro pen momentarily. One would assume he is a coach of some sort, due to the tight fitting tracksuit he has on.) Lee Harper: None were recorded in the log book, except one of the young lads in Hessian’s class who picked up a thigh strain. I was quite impressed by him actually, he’s working through the pain. Either way, nothing this meeting needs to know about. (As Michael leans back there is clearly some tension between the three co-workers. Resting in his executive leather chair, he knows all to well the relationship between the boardroom and it’s employees. The boardroom has the money, and needs to keep the money to keep the company going. All the employees want to do is spend the money to ‘improve facilities’. The third person, Lisa Robinson, sits quietly in her blouse and knee length skirt. She’s the secretary responsible for typing up any decisions made in the meeting. That is if you can call it that of course. All the two men are ever interested in doing is getting one up on each other. Male chauvinists.) Lisa Robinson: We haven’t got much time left gentleman. Mr.Sushi is arriving in an hour to check up on his class and their progress. I suggest we move on to the recruitment videos? (Lisa points to the stack of videos on top of the television set. At least 10 high, the brands of the videos are all different, indicating that they’ve been sent in from different places. They also all contain a sticky yellow post-it note, detailing the name and address of the creator.) Lee: Lisa, may I remind you that you are my secretary, merely here to take down notes. I make the decisions, understood? (Lisa nods, all the while wishing she could slap the taste out of his conceited mouth. Still, having spoken those words, Lee rises out of his seat and removes a video from it’s case. Bending down gingerly, he slides the video into the video player, and presses the jumbo sized sponge ‘Play’ button. Backing up into his seat, he tosses the case loosely onto the table. The post-it reads: Charlotte Lawrence23 Flower Crescent, Prairie Town, Oregon On the glass television screen, the video starts playing. Half asleep, the executives laze back in their seats, hoping for some much needed sleep. It doesn’t matter if they miss out on a potential student. They have enough as it is...) (The quality of the video is very poor, as it’s obviously recorded with a home camera. Standing in the centre is Charlotte Lawrence, who is looking her naturally pretty self. Her blonde hair is tied back in a bunch, with just a few strands left hanging over her face. A smile is spread welcomingly, and the blue eyes look as deep as the pacific ocean. She’s also wearing a white t-shirt, and of course her trademark blue jeans, accompanied by a black buckle belt. The background is just her garden, with the tall chestnut tree reigning over all it shadows, and the green grass is finely cut, the loose cuttings scattered over the concrete slab patio. Visibly taking a deep breath, a slight glance of nervousness to the cameraman, probably Joe, shows a distinct lack of confidence.) Charlotte: Hi! My name is, um... (Stuttering, Charlotte stops. A small tear can be seen in her eye, but she wipes it clear, sucking in oxygen, trying to stay composed. Obviously Joe doesn’t know how to use the ‘rewind’ feature on the camcorder. Slightly more determined, Charlotte starts again.) Charlotte: My name is Charlotte Lawrence, and I would like to be a wrestler. Well, obviously, otherwise I wouldn’t be contacting you guys. Oh, hi to you folks watching this! (Confidence starting to flow freely once more, Charlotte waves into the camera, endearing herself to the viewers, whoever they may be.) Charlotte: Unfortunately, I do not have any previous wrestling experience. Yes, I know what you’re thinking, ‘little blonde girl wants to join wrestling promotion because she has tits and ass, but has no idea of how physically strenuous the job is’. Good news folks, I’m not one of those people! No, I want to join your industry because of the mountainous challenges it provides. I have no intention of showing my bits on television, you’ll be glad, or not glad, to know. I also know how demanding wrestling is. Since my interest in wrestling has peaked, I’ve acquired some Japanese videos, and some of the wrestling stunts they pull are amazing. None of the hardcore stuff, that’s disgusting. Anyway, I’ve got to go and cook the dinner, my boyfriend has me well trained. I hope you pick me, and if not, best of luck! Byeeeeeeeee! (Charlotte waves frantically into the camera, before it switches off.) (Back in the boardroom, the television screen is now filled with black and white fuzz. The men, for their part, are sat on the edge of their seats, dumbfounded and probably exhausted from the energetic and original display they have just seen. For obvious reasons, Lisa isn’t affected in quite the same way, as she makes the final notes with her snazzy metallic parker pen.) Lisa: Makes a refreshing change from all the losers smashing lightbulbs and glass over their heads. Doesn’t have any wrestling experience though, it would be a gamble taking her on. (The men are still gazing at the blank screen, having not moved their eyes once. Lisa clicks her fingers in front of their faces, snapping them out of their never ending trance. Shaking his head, Michael seems lost for words.) Michael: She had a, um, well, nice, you know, personality. Lee: Very, err, enthusiastic. Powerful voice, and I suppose she had some, ehem, good looks... (Lee’s voice trails off and his incoherent mumbles become quieter and quieter. Lisa just twiddles her thumbs, rolling her eyes in her head. At least this young woman has got her partners agreeing on something for once.) Lisa: OK, lets sign her up then. I’ll send Charlotte Lawrence a letter, notifying that we would like her to attend one of our upcoming training camps. (The scene fades out as the trio move on to the rest of the recruitment videos, and we finish on one sign. Hanging on the back of the big oak door are three initials. P. W. A.)
