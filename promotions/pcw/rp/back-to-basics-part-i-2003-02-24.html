Back to Basics Part I

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/02/2003 KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! “Hello! KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! What?! Now you’ve locked the door on me?” Jade mumbled, creating the sound of the door being banged with her lips. Jade kicked the bottom of the door with her bare feet and moaned as she bent down on her knee’s plucking a hair pin from her head. “I can not believe that I have to do this for the second time today.” Jade jams the hair pin in the door and twists around for a moment. There is a small click and Jade smiles. She pulls the handle down and pushes her weight against the door. There is a catch on the inside and as it is activated the door stops. Jade growls and pulls out a nail file pressing against the chain begins to dissolve at the pressure. After the chain snaps Jade tip toes into the bed room. Jade had already pestered Lacuna at three AM this morning. Rather angrily Lacuna had shaken her right out of the room. Jade could not understand just why Lacuna had to sleep so bloody much but it kind of upset her. Well now she had no chance! It was of decent time, 6 AM, and Jade had a huge headache. Those creepy workers of Lacuna seemed to always be around but did not understand simple orders such as 5 AM wake up alert! Stupid. Jade rests at the bottom of Lacuna’s bed and places an arm on the silk sheets to steady herself on the bed. Lacuna stirs about the bed but doesn’t even look up. Jade whistles loudly and looks at her. Not even a wrinkling of the face as the light creeps in through the expensive cloth drapes. Jade rests her hand on Lacuna’s side waiting for the heat in her hand to wake her up in Lacuna’s incomfort. Still not even a murmur from her unconscious friend. “Wake up girl! I haven’t got all day.” Jade ordered in a clear tone. She shakes Lacuna’s side and -still- there is not even a moan or groan of tiredness. Lacuna is sleeping like a rock. Jade Sits next to her on the bed and pushes her feet against her back. She makes a final check she is not awake before pushing hard against Lacuna’s back. Lacuna falls straight out of her bed and Lacuna crashes to the ground with a loud thump. Lacuna’s head bobs up over the mattress as Lacuna’s hazel eyes glare at Jade. “Ouch you bloody moron!” Lacuna’s British tone was loud in the air as Lacuna stumbled back onto her bed. Lacuna pulls on a dressing gown over her red silky nighty as she hums to herself. There is a servant in her en suite bathroom turning on her shower and Jade looks up from the bed to the door of the bathroom and shakes her head in disbelief, Lacuna had not been up for two minutes and already there were on of her slaves ringing out the soap cloths and preparing towels. “Hell, I bet you already have someone in there ready to scrub your back don’t you?” Lacuna looked up from her black silk robe and leant back against the bed. She didn’t even react as she was too easy rubbing the sleep from her black eyes. Her toes bend over and crack as she stretches the whole of her body out. “You know you have a serious personality problem sometimes?!” Lacuna still does not speak to her. “Are you still mad because we managed to get our head’s banged together by dumb and dumber on Friday?” Lacuna pouts as Jade pokes fun at their identical black eyes marking their faces. “Are you still here?” Lacuna rolls her eyes as she sits up on the side of her bed. She gingerly pats her finger on the dark purple bruising. Lacuna darts into the bathroom to escape a moaning Jade as she is left quietly babbling on with herself, excuse after excuse pouring from her mouth. Jade feels a sick feeling in her throat as she goes back to just what happened on Friday. With the beating of the title holders and their rather annoying enemies. Jade stamps her feet on the bed as she realises that Lacuna has left the room. Jade takes a second out again, this being the fourth time today that she had mulled over the son of a bitch sequences. Jade never knew that things would go that badly. Lacuna had her own agenda going into that match which involved beating the holy, seven shades of crap out of Tessa Windsor. Jade wanted absolutely nothing to do with fighting Chris Storm; sure she knew it was going to happen eventually because how else would they take the tag team titles from them on Tuesday? That foot to the balls of Chris Storm was an unfortunate accident. When the four for them got their heads banged together it made them look like amateurs and this more than anything pissed Jade off, that is, pretty much is what was on Lacuna’s tits as she would have said in her little English accent. -[Fade]- The slightly overweight Brook slouches back in a plush purple velvet recliner. There are chips and sauce nestled in his lap as he recovers from a full night watching Steven Segal on TBS. A mixture of saliva and chips dribbles from his mouth and down his chin. His eyes are about to fold to the back of his head as he hears the noisy beating of feet against wood as Lacuna and Jade hurry down the large steps leading to the hall. Brook spat the food from his mouth into a bin and wiped his chin. As he rushed out of the room he busily brushed his comb over into shape. His dickey bow gets pulled tightly around his neck and He makes a grab for the door which connects his bed room to the hall. Jade is running rings around a still tired Lacuna. Jade was bouncing up and down as her energy was finally coming to light as Lacuna slouches into herself. “Lacuna honey! I have another surprise for you to enjoy! Out of your own bank account of course, you will enjoy it though!” Lacuna put her chin into her chest to hide her excitement. Jade speeds into the kitchen, Jade shouts out to Lacuna. “Lacuuunna! Come heerre!” Jade bellows as her voice carries into the hall. “Your surprise is here sweety now come and get it!” Jade’s voice turns into more of a bark as Lacuna rolls her eyes, her bare feet dragging along the carpet. “Get your ass in here.” “Will you bloody wait? My arse is going to be right with you when it wakes up with the rest of my body!” Lacuna mumbles her face going slightly red with her anger. “Jesus Christ Lacuna! I’ll wait here all day!” Jade was bouncing around the kitchen. “Oh I don’t go into the kitchen it is strictly for the workers!” Lacuna needed to relax some more maybe get some sleep, more definitely some breakfast. “Gerad!” Lacuna called, he was one of her chefs. He came scooting into the hall. “Full English! Make it snappy, this is the most important meal of the day!” Jade zoomed into the hall. “Lacuna are you eating again?” Lacuna nodded and Jade shook her head. “Gerad could you be a dear and take the day off?” Gerad looked at Jade weirdly. “Tell all of the cooking staff to take the day off. Lacuna is going to be real busy and besides we can always order a pizza eh?” Jade looked at Lacuna who was snarling being deprived of her food. “Now follow me into that country park sized back garden you have and let the fun begin!” -[Fade]- Jade runs about in the great estate whit her arms by her side. She is making aeroplane noises and spinning around like a maniac. There is practically a whole theme park in the sight of Lacuna. “It is suprising what people can do for you if you pay them the right amount of money Lacuna.” Jade dance all the way back to Lacuna and stopped bouncing on the spot. “I figured it would be a while before we went back to the Carnival.” A smile breaks across the face of Lacuna as Jade snorts, a small look of disdain enters the features of Lacuna’s face before being replaced with a large grin again. Jade drags Lacuna by the hand and leads her to this giant fan. Jade uses all of her strength to lift her into a gorilla press and throws her onto the fan, it lifts her in the air where she is left to scream like a little girl. “Jade!” Lacuna yells. “Get me off of here you little bitch!” Jade giggles as she turns the machine back up. Jade takes the quiet time at what is now 7AM in the morning after all of the waiting around for Lacuna to get a small drink. -[Fade]- “Brook sweetie!” Jade shouts as she goes back into the ‘house’ through the back door. Brook storms into the room, no doubt angrily waiting for his next task to be delivered. “Just get me a drink; I think Lacuna wants you too.” She giggles to herself as she slings her smallish body onto the silver plated work top. Brook gasps as he slings a bottle of water at Jade. Jade blows a kiss at him as he runs through the back door to his flustered boss. Just as luck, or a severe lack thereof, would have it Jade spits her water everywhere after hearing a crash through the front door. Jade storms into the front hall and is mortified to see a house brick there. There is a note attached which Jade reads worriedly. ‘Dearest Whore, I’ve taken the liberty of claiming back the club. Security has been stepped up to say 300% more than it was. Ha Ha stupid girl. All attempts to claim it back will be utterly futile dear. Mani’ Jade drops the brick on the ground as she heads out of the door. “That prick!”
