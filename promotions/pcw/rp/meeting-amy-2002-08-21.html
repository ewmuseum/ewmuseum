Meeting Amy

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-21-02 The room is very comfortable looking. The walls are painted with a friendly looking mural of children running, laughing, and playing underneath the warm summer sun. The carpet is a shade of emerald, and the furniture looks very inviting. The woman at the front desk promptly smiled and invited Brandi to take a seat on one of the chairs, which she had done. Now, despite the atmosphere of the room, Brandi feels quite nervous. She looks around uneasily, not quite sure what to expect. Had she been this nervous before stepping into the ring that she would later leave from with the Extreme title in hand? No, she hadn’t. Her debut in PCW? Her Cruiserweight title shot? Would she feel this nervous on Turmoil when she gets another shot at the same belt? No, no, and chances are, no. Butterflies do flips in her stomach as she contemplates the possible results of this meeting. One…she loves me, we get along great, and we have a good time. Two…she hates me, we argue, and I never see her again. There isn’t really an in between in this case. It’s all or nothing, and it’s making her nauseous. Normally she wouldn’t be this nervous, but she had been asked to do this by a good friend of hers and she didn’t want to let her down. So there she sits, teddy bear in hand, waiting and hoping. Her eyes drift back and forth between the closed door in front of her, and the bear in her arms. She hugs it close to her chest, sighs, and tries to relax. The woman that had invited her to take a seat must have sensed her nervousness, because she walked over to Brandi and had a seat in the chair next to her. She smiles once more, and looks towards the door. Woman- Don’t be so nervous. You’ll do fine. Trust me. Brandi forces a laugh, but it sounds so fake. Brandi- I wish I were as confident as you. The woman looks at Brandi sympathetically. She notices the present Brandi has brought for the girl, and touches it gently. Woman- She’ll love it. These kids aren’t used to these kind of luxuries. Brandi nods and tries to stop her eyes from watering. She hopes that the woman doesn’t continue. She doesn’t need to go into the office teary eyed. She doesn’t need to know about what the poor girl had to endure, growing up the way she did. Having a father who was hardly ever home, and a mother would couldn’t afford her. Constantly hearing the fighting about what to do with her. Brandi can’t stop the tears from coming as she realizes that she already knows how the little girl feels. She had gone through something very similar. Brandi quickly wipes the tears from her cheek, hoping that the woman hadn’t noticed. If she had, she didn’t show any sign of it. Ms. Davis, the woman in charge, is Lindsey’s good friend. They talked every night, almost religiously. One night, Lindsey had told the woman that Brandi would help if she could, and the two of them found a little girl with a case similar to Brandi’s. Actually, most of the children here were alike. Their parents are drug addicts. Alcoholics. Prostitutes. They’ve been in and out of jail. They don’t want their children, or are simply incapable of raising them, whether it be emotionally, physically, or financially. Brandi realizes that her cheeks are wet again, and she slowly wipes them dry. The woman next to her looks like she is about to say something, but Brandi is glad that the large wooden door in front of her opens before she has a chance to. Instead, the woman gets up and grabs Brandi’s arm, giving it a gentle squeeze. She smiles, and walks away. Brandi gets up and peaks into the office. She is surprised to find that its only occupant is Ms. Davis. She’s sitting behind her large oak desk, scribbling something down on a piece of paper in front of her. She looks up and motions for Brandi to come inside and to close the door behind her. Brandi does, and has a seat in one of the two chairs in front of the desk. She immediately regrets this when the woman stands up and extends her hand. Brandi, embarrassed, quickly jumps back up and shakes the woman’s hand. She doesn’t sit back down until Ms. Davis does. So much for first impressions, huh? Brandi feels slightly out of place now. Ms. Davis looks very professional. She wears her hair up, and has on a blue skirt-suit. Brandi is dressed casual in her blue jeans and white tank top. She hopes that the woman hasn’t made a lasting decision on her just yet. Ms. Davis- Hello, Brandi. It’s good to see you made it here alright. Brandi smiles politely and nods. Ms. Davis leans forward a bit and puts one hand on top of the other on her desk. Ms. Davis- Ok, let’s get right to the point. Amy is waiting in the next room to meet you. She’s been looking forward to it, and honestly, I think she’s going to like you. Before you meet her, I have a few ground rules to lay down first. There will be no swearing, drinking, or taking drugs around this child. Is that understood? Brandi nods, and Ms. Davis looks satisfied. Ms. Davis- Alright. I didn’t think that would be a problem with you. Lindsey speaks quite highly of you, and it’s good to see that a few celebrities are taking interest in becoming mentors for these children. The only thing that you need to do is show her love. Make sure that she knows she has someone to come to and talk to, and most importantly, have fun. Now, I just need you to sign something real fast… Ms. Davis slides the paper she had been writing on earlier across the table to Brandi. Brandi reads over it quickly, grabs a nearby pen, and signs the bottom of the paper before sliding it back over to the woman. She looks over it, smiles, and stands up. Ms. Davis- Well, now that all the boring legal stuff is out of the way, you can meet Amy. There’s the butterflies again. Brandi forces herself to smile as she follows Ms. Davis into the next room. Once she looks at the little girl sitting against the back of a chair, and swinging her feet carelessly, the butterflies disappear and a genuine grin replaces the nervous smile. She kneels down next to the little girl, and is greeted by the smile of youth. Brandi- Hello there Amy. Amy- Brandi! Brandi is caught off guard when the little girl reaches up and throws her arms around her neck, embracing her in a tight hug. Soon, she regains her composure, and returns the hug. When they let go, Amy jumps off the chair and begins to ramble on like most children her age. Amy- I’ve been waiting for you to come all day. Everyone kept telling me that I’ve got to be patient and that you’ll be here soon. They said that once you get here we could start having fun and I’m ready to have fun. I couldn’t sleep last night because I knew that you’d be coming and now here you are and now I’m so happy. Ms. Davis and Brandi look at each other before bursting into laughter. Brandi- Is that so? The little girl smiles and nods. Brandi- Well, since you’ve been waiting all day for me to come, would you like a present? Amy- Yes! Ms. Davis- Now, what do you say Amy? Amy- Sorry, Ms. Davis. Yes please. Brandi pulls the teddy bear out from behind her back and holds it out to the girl. She stares at it for a few seconds, not quite knowing what to think of it. Just as Brandi worries that Amy doesn’t like it, she runs up and wraps the bear up in a hug. Brandi stands up and Ms. Davis smiles. Amy- Thanks! I love it. Brandi- I’m glad. What are you going to name it? Amy- Hmm…I’ll have to think about it. Brandi laughs and Amy runs off to show the other children her new toy. Ms. Davis- Well, it looks like the two of you hit it off great. She loves her new bear, and she feels close to you already. Brandi- I hope so. All these kids seem like all they want is a friend. Ms. Davis- That is want most of them want. We do the best we can for them, but often it isn’t enough. More people need to get involved and see that these children get the companionship they seek and deserve. Most people don’t seem to care though. It doesn’t effect them if they pretend to ignore all these kids. However, if they just open their hearts up to them, they will find love that they’ve never known before. Brandi finds her eyes tearing up again. She manages to keep them under control though, because Amy is running towards her. Amy- Brandi! Are you gonna take me somewhere today? Brandi looks down at the small girl and shakes her head. Amy looks disappointed, but she hugs her new teddy close and nods as though she understands. Brandi- I’m sorry Amy. I won’t be able to today. I’ll be busy all day. Amy- Maybe another day? Brandi- Of course. It’ll be really, really soon. I promise. Ok? Amy- Ok. If I want to talk to you will I be able to? Brandi- You bet. Just let Ms. Davis here know and she’ll give me a call. Amy- Ok! Bye, Brandi. Amy hugs Brandi’s legs, and runs off once again. Ms. Davis leads Brandi back through her office and out to the front room. Ms. Davis- I really do hope that you hold true to your word, Brandi. Amy is a wonderful little girl. She just needs someone willing to give her a chance to show it. Brandi- I know, Ms. Davis. I will be away at shows often, but I do believe you have my cell phone number. If not, Lindsey does. Feel free to call me on it anytime, for any reason. Brandi and Ms. Davis exchange their farewells. As Brandi walks past the cheery woman at the front desk, she thinks about the young girl and her eagerness to find someone to love. She thinks about her own past, and even now, she has no idea how much this small child will change her life.
