House Call...

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17.09.02 The scene opens up in a suburban area. The same suburban area that Makonie lives in. It is late in the evening, about 9 o’clock at night. The street lights are on and the street is pretty much empty apart from the occasional car driving along the street. The camera moves into Mark Makonie’s living room. It is a large room that leads directly to the kitchen. On the wall there is a big swastika flag above the fireplace. On the mantle piece there are pictures of Makonie’s family. On the other walls there are pictures of his fellow soldiers from the white supremacy organization he is part of. There are two white leather couches in the living room facing a big television. There is also a small glass table in the living room, with different magazines on it. There are a couple “White Supremacy Monthly’s” a few “White Pride” newsletters and a “White Is Right” magazine. In the living room, there is on white guy, is head shaven, tattoo’s up his arm, he looks like he was an extra on American History X. An obvious neo-nazi. He is sitting on the couch, looking at some magazine. Makonie then walks in, carrying two bottles of beer. He hands one to his friend who is called Mike before sitting down. Mike: “You know, the brotherhood isn’t happy with you right now Mark” Makonie: “Yeah I know Mike. But what can I do?” Mike: “Stop teaming with that coon for one” Makonie: “I’m sick of him. He had me in the studio rapping the other day. He had all these pre-written lyrics for me to rap. They went like “fight the power, kill the white folks” Mike: “What??” Makonie: “Yeah. He seemed to think it was funny” Mike: “I don’t see how you got yourself into this mess” Makonie: “What mess? I’m not in a mess” Mike: “Mark, you’re a neo-nazi, a once respected member of the brotherhood, on television every single week standing and working with a nigger” Makonie: “And?” Mike: “And? Mark, what happened to you? Did that monkey brainwash you?” Makonie: “Please. Can’t you see what I’m doing? Before I won the tag belts, how many times a week did you see a skinhead on television?” Mike: “Well, if you count all the white guys on that tv show ‘Oz’ I don’t know. 10, 11?” Makonie: “Real skineads. There was none. I am the first true nazi on television. I am spreading the word of the brotherhood world wide. Me being on television every week is much better for exposing us to the world than them stupid websites you have” Mike: “That might be correct, but why can’t you have a white tag partner?” Makonie: “I guess I could…” Mike: “Then I suggest you find one before it’s too late” Makonie: “But I’m a tag champ, I just can’t give that up” Mike: “You know exactly what you have to do with that coon. You have to take him out” Just then there is a knock at the door Makonie: “Who could that be?” Makonie walks up to the door and opens it. Standing in his doorway is none other than his tag partner Duzza. Duzza is dressed in a baggy white t-shirt, big black jeans, Timberland boots and he has a black beanie covering his braids. Makonie: “Duzza? What are you doing here” Duzza: “Dawg, you ain’t gon’ belive this shit dawg, but man I was rollin’ through the neighbourhood and ma ride broke down! You belive that shit? I only just got it back from the garage as well” Makonie: “And what do you want me to do about it?” Duzza: “Err, let me in so I can ring someone to come pick me up?” Makonie: “Don’t you have a cell? Couldn’t you have gone downtown and pick-pocketed someone for their cell like usual?” Duzza: “Aww c’mon dawg, that ain’t ma style. And I left the cell at home, c’mon dawg, lemme in, we gots to talk” Makonie looks back at his friend Mike who is sitting on the couch wondering who is at the door. Makonie: “Duzz, this isn’t a good time. Seriously?” Duzza: “Wha? What’s wrong? You got a girl up in here? Ha, c’mon dawg, let me in” Makonie: “Listen I said no, now your for a fit guy. Just run home, blacks are good at running” Duzza: “Was that a racial slur?” Makonie: “No. Now leave. Before I call the police. You know the police would shoot your black ass. Actually, maybe I should ring them, don’t go anywhere” Duzza: “Dawg, that ain’t even funny, you have any idea how many times the police have shot at me?!” Makonie: “None?” Duzza: “Lucky guess… Now c’mon dawg, lemme in. We gots to talk ‘bout our match anyway, we’re facing Redemption Song? I think that’s their name, I ain’t got a clue what Redemption means though” Makonie: “You don’t have a clue what half the words in the English language mean” Duzza: “Yeah well… yo I got a question, as a nazi, what do you feel about native Americans? Or Indians? ‘Cos they ain’t white, but then they’re not really that dark skinned either” Makonie: “Ah, the Indians. They are one of the white races greatest accomplishments. We managed to nearly wipe out an entire race of non-whites. It makes me proud to be white” Duzza: “There is something wrong with your head dawg. Seriously. It’s actually quite worrying” Makonie: “Ah fuck off” Duzza: “Naw dawg, I ain’t goin’ no where till you let me phone one of ma homies and I get a lift back home” Makonie: “Listen, I don’t know if it’s because your black and as a black man your about 50% dumber than everyone else. But you are not using my fucking phone. Now get the fuck off my property” Duzza: “A’ite dawg, ain’t no need to get all racist about it. Ya coulda just said no, geez” Makonie: “I did say no! About 10 minutes ago” Duzza: “Aww c’mon please dawg! What you got against me?” Makonie: “Have you failed to notice that I am a neo-nazi?” Duzza: “Ooh yeah…” Makonie: “Now get out of here… I’ll see you at Turmoil.. that’s if you make it out of the suburbs alive!” Duzza: “Y’know cracker. Even for a nazi, you’re a mean mother fucker… peace easy” Makonie then slams the door as Duzza walks back to his car, wondering what to do next. Mike: “Who was that?” Makonie: “Err… Jehovah’s Witness…”
