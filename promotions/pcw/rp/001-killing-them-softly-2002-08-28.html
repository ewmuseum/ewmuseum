001 - Killing Them Softly

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-28-02 The American Dream. How does it differ from the promiscuity of today's youth? For one, the dream is fabled and not even more of a dream as most would known to expect. Because dreams have happy endings, and for the common, that happy feeling tends to lead towards selling out. Take PCW Third Dawning for example, Statuz Quo, who is by far the only person in PCW withstanding his Playaz Inc. brethren hasn't sold out one bit. Sure he's taken the image and tweaked it, unlike Dante Jackson whom has taken the damn 'status' and made it a joke. Wonder about it for a second, the point of Dante Jackson changing whom he happened to be, just for a taste of gold. Never happened when he was one of the quiet people, not saying anything to get somewhere. Selling out to gain something in white America? Bad. Selling out to gain something for the black man? Good. Yes, Jackson came to a realization that maybe he gave up too much towards everybody and that well, his talents went so far as to go into the ground. As Dante will tell you right now, the old is gone. And the new- presents itself on your television screens today. The plane roared with common distance in between and the lousy service food made him almost throw it all up down onto people in their homes. It was only worse when he got off the plane, the commotion from the flashlight bulbs popping and his eyes blood red made it even unbearable to be this way. He was the embodiment of what he wanted to be since an adolesence. A Rap Superstar, he could tell by the limo waiting outside of Bush Intercontinental Airport waiting to pick him up. 'Well, aren't you going to open the door?' said the young man with a chip on his shoulder, the limo driver stood there still. 'I'll open it, keep your mind on that little contract you have in your hand.' a sarcastic tone from the driver caused Dante to turn his head in adolescent wandering of what the man did to him to cause such a reaction. He figured out his contract out in the car, Def Jam's highest signed rookie to come in with a six figure income, six million with three million taken away from taxes. The rest averages out in one million take home pay, easy money for his talents. He wondered how the in house production would make the melodies for him to take his lyrical hymms on. It switched away from the music to wrestling and how the hell would he take a transition from the small leagues to the big leagues. He remembered the phone call from Jeremy Howard, just in the exact fashion like The Pcw had with him before. The limo driver with a wraparound ear piece microphone in his ear called out to Dante, shaking him out of the concentration. 'So, you getting adjusted to this fast lifestyle yet?' He had just been an offical star two days, hadn't even stepped into a recording booth yet. The accent from the driver reminded him of a few Martin Scorsese films like Goodfellas and Bringing Out The Dead. 'You Italian or something?' Dante asked, sipping on a mixture of Coca Cola, Sprite and Hawaiian Punch on rocks. This drink would be his equivalent to Purple Stuff, Bacardi mix, the usual hip hop drink you'd find in the clubs. The driver took a drag from his cigarette, before tipping the bud on the ashtray. 'Italian, its like me asking you are you black and from the ghetto.' Stereotypes, Houston had its share of things to joke about these days including Italian men here instead of New York. He pulled closer, edging towards Westimer and Bissonett. 'Its the quirks.' 'Quirks? The hell is a quirk?' Dante scoffed. 'Quirks, like...'He didn't know, he tried wondered some of the lingo that common people passed daily. 'Problems. Quirks are small cracks in your basic foundation and right now, the music world has its share of them. With your scandals, and celebrity marriages going down right now, who can you trust.' The driver told him as the speed rose to forty-five. Dante's eyes rose as he left his eyes to the stars amongst the clouds. It was atleast 9:15 by his watch, enough time to atleast talk to Tracy. He needed something to hear before he even stepped inside of a PCW ring and this was the time. Before long, the silence was cut deep by a sharp ringing from the cell phone located in Dante's pocket. He looked at the face, the green screen lighting up with the letters blankly starring at him for responding neurons to click. Pushing the send button, a voice crackled onto the phone. 'Hello?' 'Who dis?' 'This Dante, who's this to come ringing on my phone right now?' 'Quo, and we have some business to take care of.' 'Business?' Dante nodded, 'Well, doing something spectacular for the first time you step inside of the ring happens to be a major damn thing that needs to be taken care of!' I couldn't lie to him on that one, he had a point. PCW is the thing, something every single worker in the business shoots for and only few attain it. Its odd however, that I was special enough for this, but he guess that's how fate works. I take this very seriously, no matter how my intentions for it might be. I'm the new poster boy for music everywhere, not some fall off like Thug rOc. He sat in silence then, before the limo continued down the streets, onto the destination of his final. It was now that he entered territory for him to make more than what he already had in before. It was PCW, and he felt it. * * * The sun rises on the small suburbia of Houston, Texas and a nice June day. Obviously, if you're used to the 'Civil Disobedience' style of suburbia, then you're not even close to the 'actual' truth. There are gunshots that ring on an uncommon basis, there are hoes running around the place and they're usually are about thirty or so gangbangers around the place known to stir things up. Its early in the morning, and nobody is ready to work or anything like that, but we catch upon two old women walking towards one house, barred by gate door, before you could enter the front door. In blue and polka dotted church dress attire, they walk up to the door and wait for someone to open it. The woman on the left, the one whom doesn't wear glasses and seem old and batty rings the door bell this time. Cutting into the scene of the house, a man gets out of his bed, slipping into some house shoes and walking towards the front door. Pulling the door open, we see the man - Dante Jackson looking about as high as an airplane in flight starring at the two women. 'Hello, are you prepared for Jehovah's return? Cause if you aren't we have a pam-' the old woman tells him, before he slams the door in her face. A curled up look grows on her face. Her eyes lower, 'Well fuck you! Dumb-ass motherfucker.' She turns away from the house as she motions to the half-dead woman on her right. 'Come on, these motherfuckers don't care about us.' Dante walks back into the kitchen, before looking into his refrigerator searching for something to eat. Pulling out a box of Rasin Bran Crunch cereal, he pours into a large orange bowl and pours milk on top of the cereal. It didn't once mind to him that Jehovah's Witnesses would knock upon his door, they were pest and it was their nature to do so. It was a joke for the people to be treated seriously, although it was how it happened to be thought in Dante Jackson's mind. Check the clothes - Red Houston Astros baseball jersey, black FUBU jeans, long platinum chain with microphone pendent drenched in diamonds and platinum. Red and black Air Jordan XII shoes laced neatly on his feet. A horn blasts out from the outside, causing him to stop enjoying his cereal as a emerald green '64 bounces around the street on hydraulics. A hispanic man is seated in the driver seat, for the people that don't know around the neighborhood, 'Gonzo' is the main drug dealer, and a major pain in Dante's ass. 'What's up homes?' Gonzo snickers to Dante as a chuckle. Dante isn't the one playing around, although this would be the usual mood for him. Dante raises his right eyebrow at Gonzo, 'Ain't a damn thing! Stop blasting your motherfuckin' music, I just might catch Ebola from your dead Mexican ass!' Gonzo threw up a middle finger to Dante, only for Dante to shrug it off and walk back into the house, chuckling to himself before plopping down on a white leather sofa. He picks up the remote to the 56' television set albeit a few feet away from him and turns it on. First channel that pops up - MTV. 'Britney Spears on my televison twenty four days, seven hours a week.' He flipped the channel, this time to ESPN, an review in highlights of the United States Open, the biggest major in the profession of golf. Dante changes that awfully quick, knowing full well that Tiger's won it anyway. 'Ain't nothing on...' A knock on the door changes that, Dante gets up from his couch and walks towards the door, looking into the peep hole. He chuckles, before letting the man in. 'Ike!' he cries out, before slapping hands with him. Ike was a smart brother, having his G.E.D and all, but the main thing he'd like to do was smoke weed. Lots of weed! 'My man Jack, n***a there's been major heat on you on the streets.' Ike told him, going into the kitchen. He looks in it, barely finding a thing. Dante tilts his head, 'From who?' 'People! Wait a minute, ya'll ain't got no Kool-Aid?' Ike asked. 'No, we don't and besides I told you I don't drink Kool-Aid!' Ike shakes his head, 'What person you know in this neighborhood doesn't drink Kool-Aid!? You got a bunch of sugar, but no Kool-Aid. A bunch of meat, and no patties. For fuck sakes, you have condoms but no pussy!' 'So?' Dante notes the sarcasm of his comment and slaps Ike against the back of his head. Ike rubs his head, as Dante plops back down into the chair, clutching his chin. 'Tracy told you that she was pregnant didn't you?' 'Erm, no?' Ike responded. 'She did! Remember at Kevin's party? Of course you don't remember because you were doing your best impression of Elvis and high on so much Buddha, you could have moonwalked.' Dante chuckled, before resting his head along the back portion of the sofa. Like Ike would happen to care, the main thing he happened to be worrying about was a patch in the middle of his face and dredlocks streaming all the way down to his back. Dante leads Ike out of the house, stepping onto Dante's front porch. Dante sniffs the air, before sighing with a smile on his face. 'Where are you going?' Ike asked Dante as he steps inside of his 1999 Cadillac Escalade, cutting on the ignition. Dante slammed the driver side door in front of Ike, who happened to climb into the passenger side. He pushes his foot on the gas and takes himself out of the driveway and into the streets of Houston. 'I told you, I'm going straight to the studio. I got to give it to the Source for their cover story on me.' Dante kept his eyes on the wheel while Ike continued to shrug his shoulders in doubt. 'Like I care with your career.' Ike lowered his head. 'You should, because I'm paying your ass into college, believe that!' Dante chuckles to himself. Ike switches the radio from 97.1 to 97.9, which causes Dante to slap his hand in protest. Ike looks at Dante with a glare, towards his style of choice. 'You rolling with me, either you listen to the right station or you listen to nothing. Understand that, or you get out.' Dante told him ferociously, before popping in a tape. Ike questioned, 'Speaking of which, the hell is on that tape?' Dante grunted, showing that it happened to be of his own, nothing couldn't be more pure than Dante himself on a mix tape, and if you didn't understand that before while watching Making The Star on MTV, then you know it now. 'Still, you did that PCW thing didn't you?' Ike started up once again. 'Oh yeah, last night I wrecked Semore smoke, eddie mandrake, and spiderman wannabe and gave them a CHECK!' Dante chuckled at his accomplishment which can be seen on the replay to Third Dawning on most In Demand PPV channels. 'I seen that.' 'Shut up Ike.' Dante whispers to himself, before continuing on to Platinum Beats on Beachnut. The studio housed many talents, some of which who happened to be in major contracts with the major labels in rap right now. Pulling into the studio parking lot, Dante kills the ignition to the car and pulls open the driver side door. He gets out, walking towards the entrance into the building, while Ike struggles to get out of the car. Soon, pulling up to the studio happens to be a man with a jerri curl wrapped in rollers. Being the only person Dante knows to wear a pink shirt, he chuckles as he hops out of a 61' Pink Pontiac Firebird. 'What's up Kelly?' Dante shouts out to the man. He throws up a middle finger, 'Its Worm!' 'Whatever.' Dante tells him, before muttering under his breath 'Florida Evans lookin' motherfucker.' Worm walks into the studio, Dante followed right behind him as a light smile tickles his funny bone. Checking in with the cabinet he smiles before looking into the studio filled with marijuana smoke floating around the room. Dante wafts his hand in the air, trying to get it into his mind that he came here to focus on finishing up a few tracks and editing the lackluster before the June 25th release. He quietly went inside of the recording booth, alreadt preping up the equipment and began his work, finishing what he had started nine months before. It was finally finished, 23 years of work bled into one simple thing. He sighed heavily almost cutting through the mood like a devilish laugh through the gates of heaven. It was almsot perfect, the way he began the journey it was almost fairy tale in essence, but now it was completed in metamorphosis it evolved him into a man. Dante struggled with the concept of being something that he wasn't. Not the fact that he had a few things on his mind since a few days earlier, but it ate at him from within. That passion of breaking inward a new light. He was complete as the old mold of man with the same fans cheering him on inside ballrooms and indoor living conditions that seated more than 4,000 people. That was the old, now it was in with the new. He slugged his body into the Cadillac truck with Ike still in the front and drove away from the studio, finished with one journey. Still however it dawned upon him that with new beginnings he had to end past things. That finality struck a single chord within him. He had a match with his new found friend Statuz Quo against two nobodies whom happened to be fresh off losing to Cowabunga Connection. The only ties between them other than Dante's greatness in the match, is that he and Totally Tubular Tommy could eventually met in the Bracket 5 Championship in the GTT. They weren't people to him, just statistics to add to his repertoire of victorious moments. 'Would you please stop the monologue!? You're starting to become one of them philosophical white boys!' A voice interrupts the procession, that of Dante Jackson whom happens to be sitting down at home watching televison - as usual. 'How the fuck am I supposed to think with you trying to be people who I don't know? Like Chris Matthews!' You mean that guy from Hardball that shouts when he speaks regularly? 'NO! I mean that white boy karate kid who won the title that should have been around my waist! Didn't you cover that already?' I'm sorry, but I did not. 'Man, either you start describing shit or I'm firing your ass! You aren't Rent A Villian's narrator! You don't make insights on what the hell I do!' Fine, oh and Jason Cunningham, one of Dante's friends is there, still tripping out from dreams and the usual ouch comments. He's been like a common vegetable ever since, and Dante's been trying to keep him in check. 'Dante,' Jason starts up. 'You really shouldn't take everything to so much context.' Dante eyes Jason with a quizzical look, the one he happens to give towards people who are out of their race, by overstepping into the culture of the white devil. He chuckles to himself, before flipping the television channel. 'I'm the man Jason, and when you reach that pinnacle, then you can talk to me about taking shit into context.' Jason sighs to himself, before Dante steps out of the room with a glass of Sprite in hand and a deep wonder into his future. Jason walks out of the room, before Dante comes back with a joint, light and ready for puffing. Jordan leaps out of the window in disgust for the joint, causing Dante to look oddly at the action. 'It's just a little weed...' Like the Cowabunga Connection ever tasted the fine herbs, besides Jordan would more than likely be able to save someone after Chrondoman ran amuck in Houston after the Weed Wars. Like that white ATF dude said when we're fading out of this shit... 'Nobody FUCKS THE GIANT JOINT!'
