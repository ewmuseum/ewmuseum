At an Australian's beech house...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Moments ago... Our scene opens up on what appears to be a titan-sized red tractor. As we pan out we find that this monstrous vehicle is in fact parked in the well-kept driveway of what appears to be a whitewashed beech house. The little gem mine of a home overlooks the beautiful rolling waters of the ocean and as we pan out a little more we find The Pharaoh, along with Isiy and his new advisor Wimlog Mogre standing just outside the driveway. The Pharaoh: Who’d have thought he’d live here? He always gave the impression that he was a man who couldn’t keep a place as big as this tidy… Isiy: Yeah… Wimlog: Well apparently, milord, he has enslaved a group of men and women from Finland to work as his chefs, cleaners and well his basic abuse-takers… Pharaoh strokes his chin thoughtfully… The Pharaoh: I see…come then, we haven’t much time to cut this… Pharaoh begins to trek up the beach house’s drive and slides skilfully past the bright red tractor with minimal effort. Wimlog follows and Isiy does too, both having little to no trouble passing the tractor by. Soon Pharaoh, along with his two-person entourage find themselves standing upon the house’s doorstep, a selection of wind chimes blow gently in the wind ringing out a wondrous rhyme of music in the salty air of the ocean. Without further stalling PCW’s resident Arrogant Egyptian nocks loudly upon the whitewashed door… The Pharaoh: Now, when he answers I want you two to welcome him as though he was a normal person… Isiy: He is a normal person, isn’t he? The Pharaoh: Technically...yes…but he’s one of those sick sadistic porn-star types…you know the breed, the kind that purposely piss people off to get on their nerves… Isiy: Ah…I get your point. After a few moments of waiting without a sound coming from behind the door of the gorgeous beach house a terrible bang sounds out from within the sanctuary. Pharaoh looks on in wonder as suddenly the door flies open and out comes a small man, banged up and bruised…his origin is most likely the land of Finland, or so it is according to Wimlog. Wimlog: I told you he kept people as sslaves! People from Finland! The Pharaoh: Hush, my ugly little friend! Mogre folds his arms over his chest in anger before frowning moodily, all this in response to his master’s inconsiderate comment. Finland Native: Yes? Can I help you? The Pharaoh: Yes, my good man, you can indeed! We’re looking for-… Pharaoh’s words are cut short by the sound of a deeply Australian accented voice… Voice: Who the hell is it? The man at the door, obviously one of the Finland slaves that Wimlog spoke of earlier turns his head to looks back into the beautiful house. He gulps before he speaks… Finland Native: Erm…I don’t know who they are, sir, but they appear it be here for something… Voice: Like what? The man turns back to Pharaoh and his two-person entourage and whispers… Finland Native: What is it that you want? The Pharaoh: I request to see Flaymin Aymin at once! I’m here on very important matters! The voice, obviously that belonging to one of PCW’s most famous superstars, Flaymin Aymin, seems to hear this and suddenly the sound of footsteps echoes out through the hallways of the beech house. Before long the’ Flaymin One’ himself stands in front The Egyptian trio. Flaymin: Titu Xansiv? Is that you, sport? I haven’t seen you in months! Quite surprising when you consider the fact that we work for the same company, eh? Aymin laughs and then turns to the Finland slave stood next to him…he looks as though he is about to give the man his pleasantries to leave the four, but instead he simply kicks the small man out of the doorway causing him to go flying down a corridor not so far from the beech house’s entrance. Flaymin: So, what brings you here, mate? The Pharaoh: It’s about our match on Turmoil… Flaymin: We have a match on Tuesday Turmoil? Blimey! The Pharaoh: Yes, yes, we do…but not against one another…you see we have an eight-man tag team match coming up… Aymin nods his head in response, seemingly taking in Titu’s words… The Pharaoh: You’ll be pleased to know that one of our opponents is El Diablo! A wide smile grows on the Flaymin One’s face as he is informed of the news but his smile is abruptly wiped off when suddenly another voice, that of a young woman, echoes out through the halls of the Aymin residence… Female Voice: Aymin! What have I told you about kicking the Slaves around? You can’t keep doing-… The charming voice is quickly matched up to Jenna Jameson, porno superstar and Flaymin’s current valet and girlfriend. Jenna’s eyes grow in shock as she sees the trio standing outside on the doorstep. Jenna: Aymin! You never told me that we had company! And why haven’t you invited these people in? Aymin: Sorry, babes…Pharaoh do you and your pals wanna come in then? Pharaoh chuckles. The Pharaoh: If you wouldn’t mind. As Pharaoh, Isiy and Wimlog enter into the ocean-side home of the former TV champion Flaymin seems to notice the beautiful Isiy standing, smiling and looking around the house. Flaymin: And who is this lovely little creature, Pharaoh? I don’t think you’ve introduced me… The Pharaoh: Aymin…that’s my wife, Isiy…you two have already met… Flaymin: We have? The Pharaoh: Yeah, remember when we were in International Incident together? Backstage you two met at one of the shows… Flaymin ponders the thought… Flaymin: I don’t seem to remember, but what a beauty she is anyway! Isiy blushes a little. Isiy: Well thanks, Aymin… Jenna frowns, obviously a tad jealous of the way her boyfriend greeted Isiy. Flaymin: So, Pharaoh, tell me a bit more about this ‘eight-man tag team match’! The Pharaoh: Well the match is in fact the team of Me, You, Boda and Juan Borro, facing off against a team composed of Miyagawa, El Diablo… Aymin growls… The Pharaoh: (Continuing) Jason Core and Rent A Hero… Aymin strokes his chin, contemplating the match… Aymin: Well it should be a great match… The Pharaoh: And WE’RE going to win it… Aymin: Well winning is everything; all I really wanna do is kick El Diablo’s short Mexican ass! The Pharaoh sighs. The Pharaoh: If we loose, Flaymin, then we have to kiss our opponents asses! Now I’m damn SURE you want to win now…even if you beat up El Diablo and his team win, he’ll get the last laugh… Aymin frowns. Flaymin: I ain’t never gonna be kissing that goddamn Mexican’s ass! Never I tell you! Wimlog: Then you, along with your team need to defeat these guys… Before these words from the Pharaoh’s new advisor Flaymin had not noticed the presence of the short, hunchbacked freak…and now that he has noticed him his reaction is something that can only be called comical… Flaymin: What the fuck is that?! Wimlog: My NAME is Wimlog Mogre! The little cretin hisses angrily. Aymin looks up from the ugly face of Wimlog to stare blankly at the Pharaoh. Flaymin: Jesus Christ, Pharaoh, is this the level that you have stooped to? Hanging around with gargoyles? Wimlog Is about to snap when Pharaoh calms him and begins to speak in his defence. The Pharaoh: He is NO Gargoyle, Aymin! Could is be true? Is Pharaoh actually sticking up for Mogre? The Pharaoh: (Continuing)…he’s simply very, very ugly… Perhaps we spoke too soon. Wimlog hangs his head in shame as Pharaoh and Aymin share a humoured chuckle. Aymin: Good one, mate! The Pharaoh: Thank you. But now, my Australian friend, back to business…the match is for Tuesday so don’t forget about it… Aymin: Me? Forget? Nah, sport! That’ll never happen…an Aymin never forgets! Suddenly one of the small Finland slaves jogs up to Flaymin, a worried expression about his face… Finland slave: Sir, sir! Flaymin: What is it? Finland slave: This just came through the post! The slave hands Flaymin a sheet of folded paper. With a confused expression Aymin unfolds it and reads through, all the while a very dazed and puzzled expression fills his now-troubled face… The Pharaoh: What is it, Flaymin? Aymin looks up from the paper. Flaymin: It says here that I owe rent money to a guy for renting out a beer brewery…I don’t remember doing that? Jenna: You did, Aymin, a long time ago, remember, it was called the BONZA BEER BREWERY. Flaymin: Hmm…I seriously can’t remember anything about it… A moment of silence passes. Jenna: So what are you going to do? Flaymin: I guess I’ll have to pay the rent…damn It, I really can’t be arsed! Pharaoh smiles arrogantly. The Pharaoh: Aymin, perhaps I can help out… Aymin: What do you mean, Pharaoh? The Pharaoh: Why don’t you give me the beer brewery? I’ll pay for the rent, and then buy out the place; perhaps we can even be partners in ‘crime’, as it would perhaps be… Isiy: What?! Jenna: What?! Aymin smiles. Aymin: Then it’s done, mate! Pharaoh smirks broodingly. He obviously has a plan up his sleeve… The Pharaoh: Excellent! Now if you’ll excuse us, we must be off…my time to cut this promo is almost over… Flaymin: Damn the PCW time limits to cut our promotional vignettes! To hell with the time limit! Won’t you stay for a brew? The Pharaoh: Well I am quite accustomed to an ol’ beer every now and again, Aymin… Flaymin: Great! Then you can stay for a couple! Isiy frowns at the suggestion, but Pharaoh wants to bond with the Flaymin One so that there isn’t any chance of him turning his back on our Egyptian hero in their match on Turmoil…so reluctantly, but not showing it at all through words, Pharaoh agrees to stay… The Pharaoh: Fair enough, Aymin! I’ll stay for a few jars, but after that I’m off! I have a busy schedule, my Australian friend! Flaymin: Ok. I’ll just go and get the brews! You guys take a seat! Jenna show them to the lounge, babes! Aymin jogs out of the hallway down a corridor as Jenna escorts Pharaoh, Isiy and Wimlog into the beech house’s lounge. The scene fades to black…
