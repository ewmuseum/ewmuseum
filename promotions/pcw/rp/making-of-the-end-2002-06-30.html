Making of The End

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/02 Sandy Wong; Hello, Mr. Shabazz. Jordan X Shabazz: Hold up! I specifically asked for a black interviewer, what’s going on here? Where’s El Diablo? Sandy Wong ignores Jordan’s typical ignorance and continues on. Sandy Wong: Let's start. How did you get involved with this movie? Did you approach El Diablo, or did he come to you? Jordan X Shabazz: Well, El Diablo and I go back to the PWA when he promoted me to the NTICW. I appreciated that a lot, because it got me to the PCW, and when I heard he was making a movie, I said 'I’ll be damned if this man doesn’t put me in the movie' so I stood outside his house with my shotgun everyday for a few weeks, and then he finally allowed me into the movie. Sandy Wong: Who is your character and what does he have to do with the story? Jordan X Shabazz: My character is Diddy Kong, since he IS the cutest male role, El Diablo chose me to play him. He’s a cool man who just makes sure that everything goes as planned. I don’t want to ruin the movie for all the Malice Crew fans out there, so I won’t go into that any further. Wong: What was it like working on the set with The End, El Diablo, Timo Bolamba, and others? Jordan X Shabazz: Ha, I love working with the End. He’s so very funny behind the scenes I don’t know what to say. Sometimes El Diablo, Timo, and myself would act like jackasses and play tricks on the poor blind man, like the day we glued all the pages of his script together. He usually reads with his fingers, but he couldn’t open his script. We were laughing our asses off. He’s a good sport, except one time he stole my towel… I guess that was his black side coming out. The End:'Jordan X Shabazz is an idiot. I never liked him, really. He kept bothering me. Playing practical jokes like gluing the pages of my script together. Not that it mattered. El Diablo was so stupid he never put any words on the pages. 'I took Jordan's towel once and shoved him in a room with Jaden, since I knew she'd kill him for it. Never happened, though. Sandy Wong: Hello, Duzza. Duzza: Yo... Sandy Wong: Let's start. How did you get involved with this movie? Did you approach El Diablo, or did he come to you? Duzza: Well to tell you the truth I don't actually know how I got involved with the movie man. One minute i was chillin backstage with my man Jordan, the next thing you know I'm talking to that Mexican El D and before you know it I've got a part... Sandy Wong: Who is your character and what does he have to do with the story? Duzza: I am Diddy Kong's owner dude. Y'know I was supposed to get the main part yeah, but what with the music industry calling me I'm just too busy, so I was like, aight it's cool, i'll take a small part. I'm just one generous brother... Sandy Wong: What was it like working on the set with The End and El Diablo? Duzza: Well...it was cool, I guess. I mean, there were a few brothers around and El D is Mexican so you know, I didn't have to see too many white people, ya know. Not that i got anything against white folk, it's just... they annoy me! The End:'I know Duzza back from our days in EW. Beat him on two occasions, and sadly I believe those were two of the four matches he ever wrestled. He sucked so much that the bookers really didn't want to put him in matches. Not like now where he wrestles with Mike Frampton on PCW Fusion. I was guaging the crowd reaction while that was going on. The vast majority.. actually, everyone except for Duzza's wife, son, and groupies, were saying-- Let's all go to the bathroom, this match sucks! 'To Duzza's credit, he's an idiot, so it's not his fault. And in the making of the movie, at least he stayed away from me. However, I have no idea why El Diablo would hire someone with so little talent in ANY field. Diablo's just stupid like that.' Sandy Wong: Why would you get involved with this movie? Minion the Imp: Well Sandy, the reason I got into this movie, as I'm sure Hessian did... was for the snatch. You see these movie guys running around with fur coats and diamond-encrusted scrotums and all that lovely puss hanging off their arms... I'm sure I'll get plenty of that, but other than that I thi-... Hessian: Actually... the character of Drax-528 and I are similar, in the movie Drax is preparing for the war between man and machine, whereas I was preparing for the war at OSW's next Pay-Per-View, Ascension. The prospect of such an enticing part intrigues me, and so I took pleasure in playing the part of Drax-528-... Minion the Imp: ...and he wants the Snatch... Hessian: Shut up, Minion. Sandy Wong: Mm-hmm. How did you get involved with this movie? Hessian: I would expect a wench of your stature to ask a question like that... I am the perfect man to play the part of Drax, the similarities between the part and me meant I was suitable, so I signed on with the full intention of bringing the character to life, and doing it in style. Minion the Imp: I'm just here as Drax-600 for the pussy... Sandy Wong: Ugh... Who exactly are your characters? Hessian: Drax-528, as you should already know, is a cyborg, a ruthless machine who knows that the scum of humanity and the cyborg/machine population are heading towards a great war, and of course being a cyborg it wants to crush the fleshy, weak creatures... like I said the character and I have a lot in common... Minion the Imp: Drax-600 just wants some snatch... Hessian: Minion... Minion winks at Sandy. Sandy Wong: What was the atmosphere like on the set? Hessian: Working on such a prestige film as this, the atmosphere was tense. A lot of work has been put into making this film a success, from makeup to special effects to the story as a whole. All the actors, actresses and crew have been working hard, and the atmosphere reinforces it. Alot of hard work has gone into this, and being in the business of wrestling, that kind of atmosphere in performing for the crowds and making sure an opponent is snapped in two while being made to look good is similar to the atmosphere in working on a large scale movie such as this. Minion the Imp: Yeah...snatch is tense sometimes...but ya gotta do what ya gotta do... Sandy Wong: And what was it like working with El Diablo and The End. Hessian: Not a day went by that I didn't consider killing El Diablo. He was THAT annoying. The End felt the same way. Out of everyone on the set, I didn't mind him as much, because he's perhaps one of the few people not like me who understands me well enough. The End:'I find Hessian bearable enough. Maybe it's because we hate the same people. We'd actually plot ways to make El Diablo suffer in between takes. I personally had to hold Hessian back a few times, not because I cared for El Diablo's well-being, but because I'd probably be held accountable for El Diablo's death in some way. 'I'd want Diablo to die, but by my hand.' Sandy Wong: How did you get involved with the movie? Ricky: Well I was sitting here backstage one night, and I heard rumours about some kind of low budget flick being made. I figured, hey, I got nothing better to do. Right as I was going to go find El D and ask him for a bit more information, there was the knock at my door. There stood old El D himself. Said he had come by to offer me a part in the movie. Sandy Wong: El Diablo, didn't seem to even recognize you on Fusion* Ricky: Man, he is just trying to be funny. He KNOWS, I am the best thing for this movie since, Monica Lewinsky for the white house. Sandy Wong: Who is your character in the movie? Ricky: Nathan DeFalco. He controls the largest corporation in the world, responsible for the creation of the first cyborgs. The patent is still good, so in a way, the entire cyborg population owes its existence to Nathan DeFalco. So esentially without me, there is no storyline. Like I said, he knows who I am. I am the neccessity for this movie. Sandy Wong: Is your leg injury getting in the way of anything? Ricky: No, we already shot my scenes. I'm good. Sandy Wong: What was it like working with El Diablo, The End, Timo Bolamba, and others? Ricky: You should be asking them how it feels to be working with the sharpest rookie in the history of the game. I know a lot of these new guys would be intimidated by such people, not me. I don't owe these guys anything. I mean what they have done for their career is amazing, but it won't touch what I have planned for mine. Sandy Wong: Will we be seeing you in anymore movies in the near future? Ricky: What do I look like? A wrestler or an actor? I mean come on, I know I am damn good at pretty much everything I do, but I mean it can only go so far. If I decide to pursue an acting career then it will be after I call it quits in wrestling. I'm not trying to be The Rock and violate every aspect of my contract just so I can record a flick or two. We will see when the time comes. The End:'Ricky Thunder, for having accomplished so little in PCW, had his head up his ass the entire time telling us it smelled like roses. El Diablo just can't remember because he's got mental issues, but I would legitimately ask who he was, because he had too much of an ego for having nothing behind his name. 'El Diablo never could remember what role he had for Thunder. Not that it would matter, though. I was getting to my last nerve with El Diablo, and this close to killing him and stopping production.' As Sandy Wong begins to make her way away from the filming area, a figure comes up from behind her and taps her on the shoulder. She turns around slowly and we see 'Bad Attitude' Jesse Ramey, still in his on-set wardrobe, standing and smiling. Sandy: (Speaking in confusion) Hello, Duzza. Jesse: (Looking at Sandy a little confused) I believe you have me mistaken, Ms. Wong, I'm 'Bad Attitude' Jesse Ramey from Five Star Wrestling babe. (Rubbing his hand through his hair) I was told you wanted to see me for an interview? (Asking a little confused) Sandy: (Shaking her head in amazement) Oh, Let's start. How did you get involved with this movie? Did you approach El Diablo, or did he come to you? Jesse: (Laughing) As much as I'd like to say that he came to me, I can't. I was currently out of work, just coming off of my Old School Wrestling job and just so happened to see that the movie casting soon and I found the first flight out. Luckily, they knew who I was, (Looking at Sandy laughing) and they knew my history in the movie business. I was actually very lucky. Sandy: Oh, well let me ask you this. Who is your character and what does he have to do with the story? Jesse: (Laughing) Well, I play a narrow minded general who wants nothing more than to go to direct war. He and the Vice President of the US, played by Kwik FX, are more or less on the same page. My characters name is General Abernathy and will most likely have a good part in the movie. Sandy: Would you say that you and your character have anything in common? Jesse: Somewhat, maybe and then again when I really think about, we probably have nothing in common what so ever. He really is a hot head and wishes to jump to conclusions about things, instead of analyzing the situation. Sandy: Well, I’ve only got one last question for you. What was it like working on the set with The End, El Diablo, Timo Bolamba, and others? Jesse: To be quite honest, nothing more or less of what I expected. Most of the time you wouldn’t expect to see any of the people you are about to work with as the irrational types. In all honesty though, I couldn’t have worked with any better people. Always professional and always willing to joke around, in my opinion, this movie has one of the best atmospheres to it that I’ve ever worked on. Sandy: Well, thank you for your time. Jesse: You’re welcome, Sandy. The End:'I've never been in the same room with this man. Nothing to say about him. 'But I would like to take this time to reiterate my disgust for El Diablo.' The special ends with alternating interviews between El Diablo and The End talking about each other. El Diablo: The End has been a pleasure to work with. End: There's not a day that goes by that just seeing him or hearing his voice doesn't make me want to break his neck. El Diablo: He's funny, and always had jokes to crack us all up. End: I constantly insulted him, but he was too stupid to realize it. The rest of the crew understood, but I don't think anyone wanted to burst his bubble. El Diablo: A really nice guy, class act. End: I didn't care about the movie. Showed up late, gave him a hard time just because. The others did the same as well, pretty much because El Diablo would take anything up the ass and still ask for more. El Diablo: I hope to further work with him in the future. End: I hope to kick his ass in the future.
