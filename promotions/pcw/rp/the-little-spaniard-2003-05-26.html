The Little Spaniard.

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26-05-03 We are in the midst of an old house on the Spanish horizon, sitting facing a very old television set. The age is clearly determined but the brand let alone model is uncertain. The television has a dark brown outer casing with no knobs or buttons just a black opaque screen. Suddenly, the silence and disposition of the scene changes but to not much more after the television seems to have turned itself on seeing as there are no people or figures in this segment as far as the viewer can observe. The television now on only displays a loud, ominous sound in correspondence with the lack of reception on the television. All that can be seen is the flashing white and black void some call ‘snow’. But as the maddening sound of the lost television reception lingers words and phrases begin to push their way into the tv snow. Only at first they are obscured but then they take perceivable form. The words and phrases such as “Egotistical Icon”, “The Little Spaniard”, “Spanish Bull”, and “The Bullfighter” begin to mesh against the obscurity of the television snow. Then the audio seems to be changing a bit as a buried voice seems to be coming through a bit… [Tv Crackling] “Lost Souls without direction. Men who aren’t emphatic on their goals. Their identity in question. Their desire undetermined. PCW seems to breed these ciphers….. Their ideology fatuous. Their incoherent replications lack of conformity. But soon their blind tranquility will be no more. Their leisure will be nonexistent. Once they obtain a glimpse of what awaits them on the other side they will be referred to “The Soul Asylum”. They’re overriding fear will control their pitiable mortality. Every move dependent of our calling. As we murmur their names they will respond with violent contradictions and gradually grow insane by the hour. Their sense of what is rational in this world will be trampled upon by our inclination. Their sense of what is virtue and sin forever irrevocable in the gloom they wonder. The murkiness swallowing their sobriety and bearing dementia deep inside them. As this grows within we shall watch his life be turned upside-down. Acquaintance by acquaintance departing. As they stare from the outside of your painful walls, we will surround you with, they seek renounced tolerance. Tolerance that will not be located. Eventual solitude will consume you and your life will be manifested into our own little game. Your life ruined for our amusement. Your being turned into a raillery which we laugh upon. Then you will be sent away, on your own. Your own to trek boundless void. Just be sure to have your luggage prepared because your Odyssey is about to begin….Odyssey that will send you over the sands of time…..eternal time… let the expedition of the Lost Souls commence….A small Spanish man, about five foot two in height and around one hundred and fifty pounds mumurs out from his lips. [Cease Crackling] 'The smoke of my afterthoughts in all their sheer malevolent influence. Now the PCW has returned… great… now I can go back and see out my destiny. The PCW has built a fan base and fortune on my sweat and blood. But I am pleased to see the PCW return as one of the dominant forces in wrestling today. Pleased to see it return. Even though it may be a shadow of what it once was I’m sure the next generation of superstars will be ready to take this federation into another period of prosperity. But regardless, I see some familiar faces and some that chose to sit this one out. Now I’m not one to be critical of others.' A small Spanish man, about five foot two in height and around one hundred and fifty pounds mumurs out from his lips. 'I don’t care to disparage others, because the truth is I could care less, but it is quite clear the mouth of some is bigger than their will. There have been some high talkers previously in the PCW. Some that claimed so much and held themselves so high. On the highest platform. The comical thing is… where are they? Where are those who claimed to be The Baddest Boy on the Planet? Where are those who claimed no one would be Unforgiven? Where are those who maintained they were the Showstopper? The Bad Dream? And those who claimed they were The Figure of Fear? Where are those who would boast and swank about their potential and ability? I’ll tell you what happened to them. They’re out hiding.' Sergio conveniently added. 'Hiding from the epoch. The new epoch of Aftermath and Consequence. You see everything is fair gain. I don’t give a damn about your excuses or your pleas. Save it for someone who cares. You want to know why I still continue and persevere on? Because I am the Immortal Warrior. I always and forever will have the heart of the warrior. I will continue to look for a fight. I will always look for a fight. I am here at my own will to inflict my resolve upon all of you once again. I’m sure we can debate whether those who chose not to revisit their roots is the direct result of fear but one thing that is not contentious is who is ready to clash and who isn’t. You see when I heard the PCW was back I didn’t give it a second thought. I know my time here is far from over. You see I have PCW baby blue running through my body. This is not an infatuation but an instinct. But the love hate relationship with myself and PCW isn’t a pretty one but nothing is wholly pretty. As a PCW superstar there is much more to prove let alone do except cast my legacy in stone. And there’s no better way to do this than to continue to persist and kick people’s asses.' Said the little Spaniard as he wondered around the room, looking to find his true meaning. 'Do I consider myself immortal in the sense of my continuing warrior traits? Yes, but not just there. Now there’s no question in the gist of fame. But further implication. Some consider an immortal a God. Now I ask you what is a God? A God could be someone’s idol. A God could be someone who is worshipped. Looked up to during time of tribulation. Well in this significance of the term I am a God. I am worshipped. I am an idol. role model. Fill in the blank, I am a God! Its not a matter of opinion it’s a matter of fact. Now! PCW tell me do you remember who I was? Do you remember what I was all about? What I brought to the table? What my goals, desires, and abilities were? PCW do you Remember Me?!' Sergio stutters as he faces in the direction of the sun. -----------------Remember me? ('Several executions')Remember me? ('I have no remorse')Remember me? ('I'm high-powered')Remember me? ('I drop bombs like Hiroshima')----------------- [Tv Crackling] 'We depict our opponents as fragile contemptuous fools. This decree is more than substantial. Supremacy over our position is without a doubt infeasible. So is their really any foe out there that can guide himself or themselves over their predestined failure? Of course not. No one will be able to break the bonds, disarming the impending disaster that will soon nourish itself on the cadavers of the ignorant.' As Sergio began to laugh. 'Proximate adversity is what we speak of….but then again evil doesn’t have to be sincere. Could we just be trying to alarm those who listen? Perchance…but are you willing to bet your life on it?….One by one their souls will be taken….the world will witness ineffable strength that will illuminate on their television screens on the day 2003. This will be the illustration of the tales we speak of. Sergio Bullent of the PCW has yet been observed. Consider this time nearing. As soon as the gold is held high by the new king this claim will be confirmed and made official…..step forth and take judgement…' The little Spaniard puts out his thing, bony right hand and waves. The time to carry out the unholy deeds is now. The final window into the next conflict has been closed. Our denunciation will be carried out on our latest victim with blasphemous intentions. Once this task is satisfied another will be sacrificed. And this cycle will continue on forever lasting never ceasing. The Soul Collector will be making rounds soon. Don’t be surprised to find him on your doorstep for demand is great In this business time is inapposite……Soon the odyssey will begin for the forsaken ones. Take in account your soul is worth more than anything to our kingdom of torture so no plethora will be able to be placed instead…the excursion will soon begin for the Lost Souls…' Sergio Bullent removes his white Spanish shirt revealing the eleven inch scar he recieved whilst being thrown into the air by a raging bull. [Cease Crackling] -----------------Remember me? ('I just don't give a f*ck!')Remember me? ('Yeah, f*ck you too!')Remember me? ('I'm low down and I'm shifty!')Remember me? ('I'm shady!')----------------- [Tv Crackling] 'Soon our victims will bathe in The Styx. Soon their unaware conscious will defeat them…..their incomparable is not recognized because their egotistical aspirations has taken them down into oblivion. They wonder the ends of nothingness to speak to anyone who will listen. Anyone who will listen to past success that is irrelevant in the new diversion they play. This unawareness has manipulated itself into a contagious disease that most if not all has seized.' A laugh comes from the direction of the small Spanish wrestler. 'This “Epidemic” will defeat them long before they are physically dominated. Fate will beckon their collapse after each man’s time runs out. One by one their souls will decorate our walls. Their blood our water, their pain our comfort, their fall our trophy…revelations noted in the final days the Moon will turn blood red ….consider the rise of our warrior and crowning of him another such revelation…..a new king will soon rise from “The Styx”….' Sergio takes down one of his red robes he used to bull fight with and tied it around his waist. The Unsuspecting will be easily defeated. As they are no challenge to the wrath they are unaware of that is approaching them like an inferno. They indulge themselves on meaningless lapsed performances and tired accomplishments that are more than impertinent. As the tempest builds with the tension in the air our martyr’s rise and fall. With every clap of thunder they feel the offense we dish out. Following is the lightning which is their imminent K.O.' The Little Spaniard conveys as he tightens his shoe laces. 'Let it be warned that opposition will be burned alive. There will not be opposition but scant “warriors” given the unfortunate task of defending their pathetic existence. Fighting for their right to live…….. Let it be known that every time that grandfather clock explodes with those ominous chimes it is but the screams of the demented. The out cry of the psychotic. Even though they are ignored by man they are sheltered by our walls. Despite they are ankle deep in their own nauseating urine they know they are comforted in our walls in which he, the ordained(Sergio Bullent), will fight to add and conceal them…..in our Soul Asylum….' Serio Bullent picks up a bag that was already pre-packed and sets it over his shoulder. [Cease Crackling] -----------------Don't you remember me?Remember me?Remember me?!Remember me?!You will Remember me!----------------- Abruptly the television switches off and the scene freezes finally showing Sergio Bullent dissapearing out the door of his home in Madrid with his bags all over his shoulders and a plane ticket in his hand to Kuwait. The Little Spaniard is showen at the horizon as the scene fades to dusk.
