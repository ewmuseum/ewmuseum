Semore Smoke in rehab!!!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Previously Recorded on August 5, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene fades into a dark and stormy day in downtown Wilson, North Carolina. The heat is still around as you can see people praising the rain as they walk down the road, but still feeling the unbearable heat. People are still walking the busy streets of Wilson in wake of the storm. Cars are busily passing each other on the now busy street, which was not so busy the day before. Semore Smoke stands in the wake of many people as they try to pass him without bumping into him. Semore is wearing a black leather jacket, with a white t-shirt underneath it, and is also wearing a pair of black baggy jeans. Standing beside, wearing a business type suit is Mick Palmer, who is Semore’s witness for the rehab sessions that he will be taking today. The session the day before went well, with several “breakthroughs” as the doctor in charge called it. But, as Semore had thought the day before, he was not “cured” of his obsession, but it actually fueled his obsession. Semore stood up to a great applause that day from everyone as he told his life story of dealing and drugging up, but he stated the he never tried the hard stuff, such as cocaine or crack, stuff that could literally lead to death in one sniff or smoke. But his only problem was weed, but a problem to him it wasn’t, considering it helped him more then hurt him, but he didn’t say that in front of them, he made it sound as if he were struggling with his obsession and trying to stop, when in fact he wasn’t. Anything to look good in front of a judge and Semore would do it. Now they’re back for the second session that will start in the next ten minutes on this Monday morning. Semore looks both ways before crossing the street in front of a wave of three or four cars. Mick follows suit and makes it across unharmed. They both walk up the same familiar steps of the rehab center, which is still in between the bank on one side and the convenient store on the other. A robber could literally go and rob a bank, then a convenient store and then seek rehab for his crimes right in between both places in the same day. Smoke and Palmer enter the familiar lobby and Smoke walks into the waiting room as he had already signed up for today’s session the day before. Smoke sits down in the same seat he sat in the other day, and so does Palmer who has been following suit ever since crossing the road. Smoke notices that there is a small crowd in the room, which means that most everyone in the rehab has daily jobs, so choosing the morning service was a smart idea by Semore. Palmer turns to Smoke and says in low tone that resembles that of a whisper… Mick Palmer: We’re lucky that there is a small crowd today that means this will go by fast and smooth. Are you going to pull the same act that you did yesterday with the whole helpless thing? {{{{Semore nods his head and starts to get into his helpless mode as he starts to rock back and forth like a mad man who needs a fix, even though he had just smoked a joint in an ally-way fifteen minutes ago…}}}} Semore Smoke: I just hope they buy it. I can’t be thrown in jail for another drug possession, or even a damned smuggling charge, that I didn’t even commit. I need the doctor to buy it, as well as the people, and I need to make it sound like I’m cured after my last session on Wednesday. That way on Thursday I’m in the clear. {{{{Palmer shakes his head as he whispers back…}}}} Mick Palmer: What if the judge or the court decides to pull a fast one and bring the doctor in instead of me and use him as a witness against you? That could really hurt your case if he says that you only came to three sessions and then quit the day of the court case. Semore Smoke: I didn’t take that into account, but I don’t think it’ll happen, I’ve been to the place, and you can have a different doctor every time you come back, so there is no way that they can pull that on me. I know this place too much, they don’t help anyone, just make it worse so they come back and pay more money luckily I have a tab here. {{{{Mick smiles as he just shrugs off Smoke’s comments of having a tab at the center. A few minutes have passed and the session is scheduled to begin in just one more minute, but as if by some twist of fate, as soon as Mick looks at his watch, the doctor for today’s session opens the door and several people file out of the room, when the word “several” is used, it is actually only five people, but “several” can me more then two or three. The people in the waiting room, which comes shy to that exiting the room by one person stands up as the doctor signals that she is ready. Smoke smiles as he has had this person before, and she bought everything last time, and this time he thinks that he can probably land a date with her as well by doing some puppy dog acting. After the last session members leave after giving each other several hugs, she beckons for us to all come in. Semore and Mick stick to the back of the line, but when he gets up to her, she smiles at him…}}}} Doctor: I’m glad to see you back here Semore, I’ve heard nothing but good things about you when it comes to you and these sessions. {{{{Semore looks away as if he’s ashamed of his past and then he fakes some tears as he says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Anything to get past my evils in the past. I hate how many people I hurt in the past, and I wish I hadn’t hurt them all. {{{{The doctor buys it! She looks at Smoke with a sympathetic look in her face, then she looks behind him and asks…}}}} Doctor: Who’s this? {{{{Semore looks back and steps to the side to let Mick shake the doctor’s hand, Semore then says to introduce him…}}}} Semore Smoke: This is Mick Palmer, he’ll be my witness to these sessions, seems I got in trouble for something I didn’t do at all. But I can’t get out of it unless I come back to these sessions. Doctor: Pleased to meet you Mr. Palmer. Yes I heard about your small problem and I believe you, when you were here last time with me I felt that you had taken great strides to getting your evils past you. Semore Smoke: Yes Doctor, I did too. Can we please get this meeting started, I must get ready for a show tomorrow. Doctor: Sure! {{{{The doctor smiles and steps aside so Mick and Semore can enter the room and sit down at the end of the big circle of several chairs. The doctor walks into the middle of the circle and sits in the middle of the circle. She then looks around and says…}}}} Doctor: This isn’t bad for a morning turnout. Hello everyone, my name is Dr. Smith and I’ll be your advisor today as you all share your stories of horror with the rest of the group. Now I’d like to start off with a formal patient here who worked with me, Semore, if you wouldn’t mind, I’d like you to start this off with your most current story. {{{{Smoke stands up in front of the small group and does the same ritual as always…}}}} Semore Smoke: Hello everyone, my name is Semore Smoke and I’ve been without a joint for six months. But I’m back here because of a crime I didn’t commit, someone switched bags with me at customs when I was coming back into the country after an international Pay Per View with PCW. I was pulled aside and the bag that was there was checked, inside was five kilos of Marijuana. It wasn’t mine, so I denied the whole thing, I saw a guy walking away who seemed to be smiling as he walked away, but I don’t know if that was him or not. I didn’t try smuggling it back in, but I was arrested and sent to jail for a day, then I got bailed out by a friend. I’ve got a court date set for this Thursday, which I will uphold of course. But that’s pretty much it. {{{{Everyone claps as Semore sits down and Mick starts to scribble on some paper, which he did the day before when he took notes on the other session, which he plans to use in court on Thursday. Smoke smiles and does the whole act again as the people clap, and then the person closest to Smoke’s right stands up and tells his story…}}}} Man: My name is John Owens, and I’ve been with out cocaine for one week now. It’s been hard, but I seem to have managed, and this past week I’ve had the most energy I’ve ever had in my life. But last week I was found passed out in the bathtub of my hotel room and was almost dead thank god I was revived and given a new life so to speak. I’m going to turn over a new leaf and quit the obsession, but since last week, I’ve been seeking rehab, and I think that you guys can help me. {{{{Everyone claps, including Smoke then the person next to him stands up…}}}} Woman: Hi, my name is Shannon Bass, and I’ve been without Heroine for two days now. Sure, it’s not a long time, but for a Heroine addict it’s extremely long. I stopped my obsession when my arm started to swell the size of a huge baseball bat and I couldn’t move it. I felt my heart seem to almost explode and I sought out medical help. They told me that it was the Heroine and with that I went to jail for a few days, got a court date next week for my usage of an illegal substance. But, I had never felt the pain that I felt that day, and I’ll stop for sure, there is no way I’ll go back to lying on my floor and writhing in pain from the multiple injections into my arm. {{{{Everyone claps, she sits down and then the person next to her stands up…}}}} Another Man: Hello, my name is Steven Turner, and like Semore over there, I had an obsession with Marijuana, and like him I’ve been without it for months. But, last night I was tempted with a joint from a friend of mine, and I almost took it, but then I remembered my twelve steps and I denied the smoke from a friend. That’s why I’m here today, I realized I wasn’t completely over my obsession so I wanted to try and get over it. That’s pretty much my whole story there. {{{{Everyone claps as usual, but Semore seems to clap out of sympathy for the man, he is clapping louder then the others. He sits down and the last of the people in the room stands up, this woman looks to be a total wreck…}}}} Another Woman: Hey, my name is Jessica Hollowell and I haven’t quit anything that I’ve done yet. I’m here to get help from my obsessions, I do coke and heroine at night while I walk the streets for a living. I’ve done every drug imaginable and before I came here I was on heroine… I’m looking to please get help, so please help me… {{{{She sits down in her seat, as she breaks apart into a weeping fit. Some people are crying for her, and Smoke just seems to be in shock at what she’s been through in just the past few days. Smoke has never done the hard stuff, so he doesn’t know what that actually does to you from a first hand experience. But he’s heard enough horror stories to stay away from the hard stuff. Dr. Smith stands up and looks around at the group as some cry and others just sit back in shock. Smoke is one of the shocked people, and it’s legit shock, not faked. Mick is trying to hold back tears, but a tear goes down his cheek as he holds the crying back. Semore just pats on Mick’s back and this encourages him to let it go and the tears start to stream down his face. For the President of a federation that has a reputation for being hardcore, he seems to be very sensitive when it comes to stuff like this…}}}} Dr. Smith: Well, we will make sure that we comfort you in all your endeavors, Jessica, and we’ll always be here to help you. But you need to stay with someone who will stop you from doing these drugs, these monsters that will end your life. That will be the first step of your help, then we’ll get to the other steps tomorrow in our session. I hope everyone feels better with sharing their feelings with everyone else, Semore, I wish you the best of luck Thursday in your trial, same goes for the rest of you who might have court cases, whether you did it or not. You have to be proud to be seeking help, not many people do that, but they try to bury it, and that leads to more drugs. We’ll meet here tomorrow at the same time, so just stop by if you want to join us. I wish you all good luck. {{{{She walks over to the door as everyone stands up and start hugging everyone, most everyone wants to hug Jessica who has the biggest problem of them all in there, although there were others who did heroine and cocaine in the room, Smoke never did them. He also gets into the hugs, as does Mick. They’re the first people through the door and surprisingly waiting there is another group of five or so who are there seeking help. Smoke and Mick just walk out through the lobby and entrance and through the double doors, Smoke breaks his kayfabe of the acting, and then just heads down the street, splitting paths with Mick who goes the other way, they’ll talk later, but Smoke needs to get ready to fly for his match the following night on Tuesday Turmoil…}}}}
