Skylar Green relatives are cab drivers?

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 14, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up onto a bright and sunny day. There is almost no cloud in the sky as the scene fades back to show a huge building, an airport at that. It then zooms in on a single person dressed in American street clothes, a white T-shirt, with a dark leather jacket over it, a pair of brown slacks, and a pair of tan Timberland boots. The person looks up at the sky to see the nice weather, and as he looks up he shades his eyes away from the bright sun. But you can see who the man is now it's none other then Semore Smoke. Beside him sits two bags, one for luggage, and the other as a carryon duffle bag. Smoke picks up the two bags and walks forward to call for a taxi. The people walking by him on the sidewalk though outside the airport are of different races and origins, most look to be of Asian decent though. Looking behind Semore, you can see a sign that is written in big bold red letters, but the language isn't English at all, but looks to be Japanese. Semore Smoke must have just arrived in Sapporo, Japan, where he is set to defend the Primetime Championship Wrestling Extreme title against the man who used to be his friend, in fact they were partners in the Cannonball Run race just a couple weeks prior to the booking, none other then Skylar Green AKA The End. Semore stands at the side of the street after waiting already for about a minute or so for a ride. He calls for another taxi as it begins to roll by and this one pulls over and stops for him. Smoke smiles at the foreign cab driver, and the cab driver smiles back at him. Smoke opens up the back door of the foreign car and throws his two bags over into the far seat behind the driver. He then climbs into the somewhat small car, smaller then the American taxis at least, and tries to make himself comfortable in the smaller seat. After getting himself situated, he hooks the seat belt and looks up at the driver and asks him the simple question that he asks all foreign cab drivers… Semore Smoke: Do you understand English? {{{{The cab driver looks up into his rear view mirror and says in a British sounding type voice…}}}} Cab Driver: Yes sir, I am fluent in several languages. Where will I be taking you? Semore Smoke: Good, I need to go to the Sapporo Arena. How far away is that from here? Cab Driver: Not too far sir. You wouldn't happen to be one of those wrestlers who are performing there later on Tuesday night would you? Semore Smoke: Yes sir, the name's Semore Smoke. {{{{A stutter comes over the mans voice as he tries to catch his breath after a sudden burst of excitement. Smoke just smiles as he's had the same reaction before to his names, just not usually from middle aged Japanese men…}}}} Cab Driver: Re… re… really? Wow… You may know my half brother then, Skylar Green. {{{{Now Semore Smoke is shocked…}}}} Semore Smoke: Your brother is Skylar Green? Cab Driver: Yes sir. Hold up, lets pull out of this parking lot before the guards get on us for sitting here too long. Ever since the American hijackings, all airports around here have been strict on waiting outside the airport too long. Semore Smoke: Same in America. So tell me… what was Skylar like growing up? {{{{The cab driver pulls out of the parking spot beside the airport and merges into the traffic heading out of the parking lot and onto the highway. The cab driver veers to the right with the traffic and exits onto the road and heads straight, as soon as they get onto the highway, they pass a sign that reads '2 miles to Sapporo Arena' in several different languages, including English. The cab driver then answers Smoke's question…}}}} Cab Driver: Well, he was your normal Japanese kid. After being blinded at an early age he just wasn't there… he kind of just lost touch with the people here. Semore Smoke: Ah… trauma I guess. Well, you are aware that I'm facing your half brother this Tuesday in a match right? Cab Driver: Really? You and Skylar are going to go at it? The last I heard, you two were partners in some cross-country race in America. Semore Smoke: Yeah, the Cannonball Run, we won the race just barely, but ole Skylar set the prize money on fire! I don't care if he is blind, you shouldn't be that stupid! Cab Driver: Easy my friend, Skylar is just clumsy at times. Let me tell you of the time when he was about five or so. He was up on the stairs and he just fell down the stairs and hit his head hard… something that we here in Japan find very funny. Semore Smoke: Ha! I wish I could have seen that. That might be why Skylar has a little lost in his brain. He fell and hit his head at an early age and just never regained it back. Cab Driver: Could be… So… any special match you guys are in? Semore Smoke: One mile eh? {{{{Smoke read the sign that the cab driver passed as it said only a mile away from the arena. Traffic isn't any worse then that of someone just leaving an American airport, so Semore expects to still make good time. Over the horizon, you can now see the top of the dome of the arena. Smoke then answers the question after looking forward for a few seconds…}}}} Semore Smoke: Sorry, I was just admiring the scenery. Yes, we're in a hardcore match, not sure if there is any extra stipulations. I'm defending my Primetime Championship Wrestling Extreme Championship. Cab Driver: Really? So you're the PCW Extreme Champion, that's pretty good in wrestling I hear. That's the equal of begin a New or All Japan World Champion, you should be proud. Have you defend the title much? Semore Smoke: Oh… I'm the only champion coming out of Red October this past Sunday to defend the title I won on Wednesday Turmoil, then on Friday Fusion, and now I'm defending against The End, or your half brother, Skylar. {{{{The cab driver is about a quarter mile away from the arena now as he starts to slow down to make the exit off the highway to enter the parking lot of the arena…}}}} Cab Driver: Pretty good. Who have you defended against, I know a few wrestlers in PCW, or at least the ones my brother speaks about. Semore Smoke: Well, first was Jamie Celt, he's a former NTICW World Champion. But, hell, I'm a former NTICW World Champion, no big whoop there, and Celt made my reign look like Xavier Kannon's! {{{{The cab driver chuckles as Semore continues on to talk about Rush Mason on Friday…}}}} Semore Smoke: But my first challenge came from Rush Mason this past Friday. He brought to me his best, and I brought him my best, and I guess the guy couldn't beat me in a hardcore style match up. Cab Driver: Heh. Good. You sound to be a tough champion, I wish you the best of luck against Skylar and I will wish him the similar type of luck whenever I talk to him before the match. I hope you two put on a great match for the fans, you do know that Japanese fans are the most bloodthirsty fans you can find? Semore Smoke: Yes I do. Remember, I won this title in Japan, I've had nothing but loud arenas around me for the past week, and I hope to have that same reaction for weeks to come. {{{{The cab driver pulls around to the back of the arena up to the backstage entrance, which is marked off by security tape, mainly to keep fans out of going through the back door. The cab driver puts the car in park and looks down at the gage and then back up into the rearview mirror…}}}} Cab Driver: That'll be about… carry the one… three American dollars. Semore Smoke: Thank you. {{{{Smoke grabs three dollars out of his pocket and throws it to the front seat towards the driver. The driver picks the money up and puts it in his money tray for the money he has made for the day. Smoke then opens the door and climbs out of the small cab, cracking his back as he pulls himself out back into shape. He grabs back into the car and grabs his bags. He pulls them out of the car and closes the door behind himself. He looks one last time at the half brother of Skylar Green and smiles. He then mutters under his breath as he walks towards the arena back entrance…}}}} Semore Smoke: Nice man. Just wish his brother could return the favor. Skylar, don't think I haven't forgotten about the money, you will pay for that million dollars you cost me, plus for my fifty thousand dollar Dodge Viper that you wrecked. I will get my money out of your ass Tuesday, if it's the last thing I do. {{{{Smoke walks up to the back door and opens it. Right in front of him is a huge Japanese security guard. Smoke pulls out his ID from his pocket and shows it to the security guard who lets him pass after he shows it to him. He then walks down the corridor, to set his stuff up in the locker room for the event that will take place the very next night…}}}}
