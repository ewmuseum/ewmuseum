Title: Frustrations and Preparation Featuring: Tim Walden Date: September 27, 2002 It's rather a paradox of life, one which has plagued my career, for while it shouldn't happen like this, my opposition exists in many forms, only one of which has truly dominated me over the past year, which leads me to a singular conclusion... The only thing tougher than facing a brand new enemy, an X-factor per se, without any prior knowledge to give one an edge, no hope to rely on anything there may be to help decide the match, nothing to build on strategically... Is facing an old adversary with experience out the wazoo, and yet STILL no true knowledge....Just that almost every time you've met, he has come out on top, and for the life of you, you just can't understand why....What makes him so special, beyond his size and his ability to suddenly finish an opponent? A sigh..You'd think that for taking it so many times, I'd actually know what the Violating Death Drop IS...Honestly, I get the feeling that it changes with every fight so I won't be able to get the chance to counter it....But I must, I must prepare, if not to counter the maneuver, then to delay his offense effectively enough to allow me to bring the match to my court, the realm I excel in, that of technical wrestling, something that that hulking deficient would be unable to cope with..If I could bring him to the mat, I would no longer have to rely on trying to outpower him or countering some move out of nowhere, because I could at that point feel truly secure in that my technical superiority would give me the advantage I need.. ...But what about distraction? What would it cost me for the unexpected to strike, whether it be Scourge making the save for his team, Clyde and/or Bethany showing up and turning the match into a total stabfest, or Asylum running in just because they don't like us for whatever reason they'll make up to hide their jealousy...If I let my guard down, I'm right where I was before, on the receiving end of a loss... Can't let it happen. No, if someone runs out I don't let it get to me..If they come out, it's not directly affecting me yet. If they run into the ring, the referee will stop any shenanigans. If the referee is unable to order them away from ringside, I won't mind taking an attack from an outside source as long as he can call the DQ and give me the win...If he can't, then I guess it's up to my trust in Xavier and the Front Office to win the day for our team..Unlike most of my former allegiances, they don't mess things up when I'm in need... There's nothing to worry about..I just have to put the mat into this match, confine him to the canvas..I look forward to it, just as I look forward to the nameplate over my desk each day...I feel comfortable in the office, the financial statements a gleaming show of black ink to look over, to take pride in the job I do well, both inside and out of the ring..I have more to show for my efforts, my first-year economics courses before I left to wrestle full-time add on to my skill in the ring, for I can decisive strike my opponent with attacks which are like little surtaxes on physical health, building up into the grand full deduction that is a loss on my opponent's record...I cannot be defeated tonight..I must show the world...And it starts with what held me back for so long... Tonight...Violator.....You will file your final return to the ring.... PREPARE FOR TOTAL TAXATION. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
