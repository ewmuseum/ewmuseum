Date: blah | Darkness oozes at an untanted earie around a half oval dimmed light. Slightly swaying side to side, barely shedding light on what lerks. Vacant spider webs clenching onto the ends of the light by old threads, never to let go for a earthquake. Thunder storms eat away at the dark clouds above giving rumble to the cellar at times. Rust ate metal bars standing before me almost crumbling by it's worst enemy; time. But there I sat, in the darkness, in my own thoughts. Remembering being behind this bars so many times before, but not grasping the reality of their meaning. Being trapped in. Knowing you might never live to see the sun again. Never being able to have a thought to yourself with 'watch your back' being close in mind. As the light swaying said to side with each rumble, words appeared on the ground in a worn chalk texture. 'This is not me', ran over in my mind as I watched the words appear like a quija board. This is what it has to feel like when anyone steps against me... | S *One word, 4 letters.
