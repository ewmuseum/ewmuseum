Date: 5-26-02 Introduction: Waking up in his hospital bed, Trigger puts his thoughts down into words, as he begins a triumphant account of the life of a warrior. May 26, 200211:00 PMWarrior Chronicles #1Written by Infinite Warrior Trigger The following aphorisms are truths, to be accepted as truths and believed as truths, so help me if not that I would throw my remissful life into the endless abyss… I am a warrior. I have said it many times; in times of glory and in times of need. In times of hope, and in times when the odds are against me. I am a warrior. Why do I say these words? Why do they give me strength when I have none? Why do they give me pleasure when I have pain? Simple. I am a warrior. A fact that will never be altered and will never change, because I am a warrior. What is a warrior? Ah, a deep and delicate question. One that will not be answered in a few short words, and will not be understood within a few short minutes. Never will you fully understand what in means to be a warrior; it is a secret that only the warriors will know. Nevertheless, I will attempt to explain it in words that come deep from within my soul. In this world, there are problems. Problems that corrupt our every day life; that make things terribly hard, and attempt to destroy our souls. There are different types of problems we face, some with an easy way out, others that simply cannot be defeated, but all problems nonetheless. Sometimes people think they can solve problems, but really all they are doing is pushing the difficulties away for someone else to handle. These problems get passed around, all over the world, until they finally destroy someone. There is no ending the hardship. What if these problems could be solved, however? How would our world change… how much better of a place would it be? No emotional stress or burden, carefree intentions, and relaxed attitudes would be the result. A better world indeed. So who is brave enough to take problems from the world and make it their own problem? To struggle with dilemmas that could just as easily be thrown upon the next unsuspecting victim? Who would do this for the sake of the world and mankind as we know it? Then comes the warrior. A normal human being, seeking redemption for misfortunes of the past, decides that they are too unfortunate to repair. Nothing can save them. They realize that the only way to free themselves is to accept the world's predicaments with open arms, and pray that in doing so they have made life easier for one measly soul. Does this hurt the warrior? How could it not? We warriors are humans, after all, and a prick to my skin would draw just as much blood as to yours. Being a warrior, taking in humanity's issues, is not a simple task, and in the end it leads to a helpless destruction. Am I willing to give into that destruction? Is any warrior? No. Because what makes warriors different from average people is our honest disposition to accept great responsibility, and not crumble beneath it. For some, this task is not doable. Many the warriors have been demolished by this very fact. Even my mentor, the great Dan Archer, could not bare the stress. He was just as strong on the inside and on the out as I, if not stronger, yet so much hurt was burden that he fell from his mighty stance, and has never regained himself. Sad, it is, thought it will never happen to me. I am an Infinite Warrior. The Infinite Warrior. I have passed all the tests, I have studied all the teachings, and finally, I have found my inner soul. I have proven myself to be a man worthy of becoming more. And that is why I am a warrior. But in this modern world, where problems of enormous proportions run amuck, one man can surely not bare so much responsibility. To attack the issues of today, a warrior is forced to use brute force in attempting to give relief to the world. The inner freedom of mankind has turned into a battle! Instead of silent acceptance, warriors' actions have turned to violent attacks and hateful resistance to conform into society. Can you blame us? Us warriors, we were never looking for peace. We had one simple motto. Where there is a battle to be fought, you can find a warrior. Where there is a warrior to be taught you can find a battle. Where a battle is too much, and a warrior is not found… there is no hope. Warriors know how to do one thing and one thing only. We know how to fight, and that being the prominent source of our gratification, we do it-- over and over again! It is a pity that the world has come to this… when society's own silent protector must resort to becoming a violent menace. This is a fearsome life, though, and we are only its products. Will you fully understand what it is to be a warrior? No. Never. Do I fully understand what it is to be a warrior? Absolutely not. There are secrets in this world that even wise experts cannot uncover. Every day I learn more, and if there was not more to learn I would not live another day. If this world was not so chaotic, warriors would not exist. If warriors did not exist, the world would remain chaotic. What can we do? What path do we take? What plan do we read? Who do we listen to? Sit back, and listen carefully. In the distance, you can hear my battle cry.
