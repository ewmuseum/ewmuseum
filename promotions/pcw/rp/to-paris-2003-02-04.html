To Paris

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-4-03 Why did I do it? I’ve been getting that question a lot lately. I guess people are still reeling from the news that Tessa Windsor and I are more then just tag team partners. We are more then just dating. We are now engaged. I had finally asked her the four most important words that ever came out of my mouth. “Will you marry me?” Now everyone wanted to know why I did it. Why did I fly her to Paris and then get down on my knees and ask her to be my wife? I simply smile as I recall the facts just a day before we left for Paris. I was so nervous. I was nearly ashamed of myself. Image that, me a WIW:NG Hall of Fame member and current PCW Tag Team champion, nervous of asking a women one mere question. Course this was no average women. This was my Tessa Bear, the women that caged my heart. Tessa was still upset about the cruel prank pulled on her by the P.W.A. Our tag team match was leering at the end of the week. In the frame of mine Tessa was in, there was no way she would be up for defending our tag team titles. I hated seeing her in such a mood. I felt like there was nothing I could do to cheer her up. This was the woman who saved me from the life of loneliness and depression. I was falling into a spiral of black emotions, yet she had pulled me out. With a warm smile she could melt my heart. Yet here she was, crying over this sick and twisted joke pulled by our shadily opponents. I had managed to get her into our room so the children wouldn’t see her. The package delivered to us containing the pathetic joke was burned, as it should be. I put my arm around her, letting her cry onto my shoulder. This prank had hit her personally. I was at a lost for words. I looked around the room for something, anything, to make her feel better. Then I spotted it, like a beacon of hope shining through the darkness. The belated Christmas gift that we had discovered, a set of plane tickets to Paris. I grabbed Tessa’s shoulder and held her out. She looked at me with that sad look on her face, her eyes red from crying. I merely gave her a smile and held a finger up, letting her know to hold on for a second. I got up off the bed and walked over to the stuffed bear holding the box containing the tickets. I removed them from their box and held them up for Tessa to see. I walked back over to her and sat down next to her. “Tessa, let’s go to Paris now. Come on, we can leave tomorrow!” I told her, giving her a reassuring smile. Tessa smiled slightly as her face lifted. I could tell she had her doubts though. She was biting the bottom lip, something she normally does when she is unsure about something. I laid my hand on her shoulder and looked into her eyes. “You deserve this vacation. We both do. We can take a jet to Paris, stay a night or two, and then fly back in time for our match. Come on Tessa, you know you want to go.” I said. My eyes never left hers. She brightened up a little more. My words must have been reaching her. At least I hoped they were. Yet I saw all my hopes of her cheering up fade away as her lips fell to a frown. She looked at me with those cute sad eyes and asked, “But what about the kids? We can’t just leave them here.” I turned my head from her. I had forgotten about them. She was right; we couldn’t leave Alex, Christa, and Evan all alone for a few days while we ran off to Paris. Maybe I would just have to take them with us. But the trip was meant to be something to lift Tessa’s sprits. I couldn’t think of a way to work this all out. Then suddenly Tessa jumped to her feet. She became quickly excited. She pulled me up and hugged me tightly. I tried to figure out what was going on to cause this sudden mood swing. “We can just have Jackson Rust baby sit them! He was supposed to do it when we were going out to it but we had to cancel. I’m sure he would love to watch the kids for us.” She shouted with glee. My little angel had found another way to fix our problem. We were off to Paris now with no worries. Jackson would be able to watch the kids. We both knew and trusted him. But there was another slight problem; I still hadn’t built up the courage to ask her the big question. I will admit I actually bought the ring for her shortly after we entered the PCW together. I was just too afraid to ask her the question though. Each night I told myself I would the next day. Yet each day I backed off. That was one of the reasons I was getting to jealous of Jackson Rust. I was afraid that my lack of commitment to Tessa might cause me to lose her. However I knew Paris would be the perfect place to ask her. It was the city of love. Under the night stars high atop the Eiffel Tower, the mood would be perfect for me to declare my love for her. My only worry was what she would say. I was foolish though to ever doubt her love. Oh course she said yes. Soon we would not only be the PCW Tag team champions, but we will also be man and wife. When Friday Fusion came around, P.W.A. will learn they cannot change that.
