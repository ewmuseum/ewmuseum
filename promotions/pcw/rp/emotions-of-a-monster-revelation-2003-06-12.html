Emotions of a Monster- Revelation

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 12, 2003 (OOC: I apologize for the length of this, but I am having difficulty getting online. I don't want to no-show again, and, with any luck, will be able to put up a few more this weekend- Michael.) Begin Transmission (SOCIETY sat alone in his room on board Cloud Nine. He was certainly anywhere but that proverbial state of mind, however. $100,000 of his money were gone, and to top THAT off, he had a match with the whole of the LOVE group that had been tormenting the Order and himself to worry about.) “This is incredible! Yet AGAIN, I am being fined and suppressed by the conformists of this company! I would have thought that by now they would have learned…I conform to no one, or no ideals, save my own of course.” (On SOCIETY’s dresser in the room, a photograph sits. The photograph is covered with a black piece of crepe paper, as SOCIETY moves over to sit on his bed.) “Of course, the fine is not sufficient for those who conform not. They also wish to punish me physically. Punish me mentally, punish me emotionally. And to do THAT, they throw me into the ring with three women. Three women I despise. With every shred of my being.” (SOCIETY reclines, his nearly seven foot frame barely fitting on the sleeping surface.) “Blast it! I WILL have to call Primetime Championship Wrestling about this. Those in charge of booking these rooms have made a grevious error. Miss Moonlight could not fit in this bed, let alone SOCIETY!” (Grumbling mightily, SOCIETY takes a bottle of wine from among his bags, and a crystal wine goblet, and pours himself a glass of White Zinfindel. He savors the aromatic wine for several seconds, before taking a small sip.) “Ahh…even in these squalid surroundings, it is good to know that there is still SOME semblance of the finer life! A life Miss Moonlight knows—“ (SOCIETY frowned. That was his second reference to his hated rival in as many sentences. And not in the context of destroying her, either. Something was wrong.) “Something is wrong.” (No shit, Sherlock.) “This is odd. This is very, very odd...” (No longer able to take the room’s confines anymore, SOCIETY to rose, the door flying open, and closing with a resounding CRASH! The crash caused the black crepe paper to fly off the photograph, revealing the horrible truth...) A picture…of Miss Moonlight. End Transmission.
