Its my birthday and I'll do what I want!

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-3-02 Neither man can control his actions anymore. A piece of furniture that most people use to sit in, became a weapon of destruction between bitter enemies, Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon. Now, with only days until these two men at Spring Fling, can they control their violent urges until then? Or, is Fusion going to completely explode on this fued into a knock down, drag out brawl that sends both men to the hospital? If either man have their way, it just might. For, in Kannon's match, it was the Krayzie One that rushed the ring just as the Gold Patron Meritorious was about to send Brock Newbludd to a neurosurgeon for the next year of his life. Kannon might have won his match via a disqualification. But, was that attempt at crippling Newbludd worth a Krayzie Death Slam directly onto a chair? Obviously, it was worth enough to bring out Kannon during Howard's Continental title match with the Chef. Because, as Kannon hit the ring and began his vile assault on Jeremy Howard with a chair, it was very evident that he was out to injure. Last Sunday, Kannon send Howard to the hospital after putting him through a Snapple machine. Was Xavier Kannon successful again in his plan to put Krayzie out of business? No. Today, April the third, is Jeremy Howard's twenty second birthday. And, as learned in previous Krayzie promos, this man can become inhuman at times. It'll take more than repeated chair shots to the body to put this man down. With that said, the scene fades into the Krayzie One's luxurious home in Elkhart, Indiana to find Jeremy and his family celebrating the day the Lord sent down this man to fight the evil of this world. Jessica, Justin and Troy: Happy birthday!!!!! Jeremy, choosing to go shirtless with blue jean shorts and black Nikes, resides on his black couch. While Jessica stands on one side, Justin on the other and two year old Troy standing across from Jeremy, on the other side of the marble coffee table. Sitting on the marble table, glistening from the sun, is an ice cream cake adorned with a salutation to the day. Jeremy: Now this is a cake. Jessica seems excited at Jeremy's reaction. On the cake is a picture of the PCW World, Continental, Television, Tag Team and Rising Star title belts. A picture for every belt Jeremy's won in Primetime Championship Wrestling. A glowing smile crosses Jeremy's face as his girlfriend stands to her feet. Jessica: I can't just let this cake melt. It's time to cut it! Justin: I hear that. Troy slaps the table a few times with his hands out of excitement like a typical kid. Jeremy: I think somebody else wants a piece of cake. Jessica, wearing red leather shorts and a matching naval bearing top, bends over right in front of the Krazed Lunatic. Her rear end features leave little to the imagination as her shapely curves are very clear for the eyes of the Continental Champion to see. Jeremy: He can have that cake. I think I have the perfect cake right here. Justin: It's no wonder you've got two kids at twenty two. Jessica slices a piece of cake, pushing it right in front of Troy's wide eyes. Jeremy: Here baby boy. Eat up. Jeremy hands his young son a plastic fork to eat with before retreating back to his place on the couch... in front of Jessica's sexy bent over rear end. Jessica cuts three more pieces of the large cake for Jeremy, Justin and herself. Then, she takes her seat right next to Xavier Kannon's worst nightmare. Jessica: I'm sorry I couldn't throw you a party. Those plans got muffed up when you got sent to the hospital last Sunday. Jeremy: Its cool. Only egotistical whiners who need people to kiss their ass throw partys for themselves. Justin: Speaking of which, isn't Kid Wonder's tomorrow? A really wide grin takes shape on Jeremy's face. That grin only lasts a few seconds as Jeremy's face turns to a manaical look. Jeremy: Yeah. And, I have a gift for him. Jessica: You do? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jeremy: It'll be better than the gift I'm going to give Kannon on Fusion. Jessica: You didn't tell me anything about all of these gifts. Jeremy: I know. They're all surprises. Jeremy takes a few bites of his cake, then stands to his feet. Across from where he sat on the black couch is Jeremy's black entertainment center full of Sony electronics. Jeremy makes his way over toward the thirty five inch television that sits right in the middle, pushing the 'on' button. Jessica: Oh God. Here we go again. Much to everyone's dismay in the Howard household, an Xavier Kannon promo is half way done on the PCW promo channel. Jeremy takes a few steps back, watching with great interest. Jeremy: Great, just the garbage I need to ruin a good day. Jessica: Tell me that bitch didn't just say I'd kill myself. Jeremy sighs, holding his head from last night's vicious chair attack from Xavier Kannon. On his left side, a shiny bruise is viewable. Justin: She did say that. And, she said she'd take five hundred men at once in a marathon of sex better known as the Eleanor five hundred. Jeremy turns toward his brother, a big question mark on his face. Justin shrugs. Justin: Prison isn't exactly nursery school man. Jessica: You're probably right Justin. The three go back to viewing Xavier Kannon's filth of a promo. That is, until they get to the part where Eleanor Hall, slut of Xavier Kannon, threatens to rip Jessica's bleach blonde hair out. Jessica: I just know that bitch didn't! I'll rip her f^cking face off! Justin reaches down and covers Troy's young ears. Jeremy: You're staying home on Sunday and that's final. Jessica: But.... Jeremy: No! I already have a plan. And, I'm going to execute it on Fusion. Jessica slouches down on the couch, saddened at not getting the opportunity to get her hands on Xavier Kannon's valet and known whore, Eleanor Hall. Jeremy: Besides, whose that bitch calling brain damaged? I'm not the one dating a loser. Justin: No, Kannon and Hall are. Jeremy laughs, as does Jessica. At least one of the Howard brothers has a funny side to him. Jeremy, more known for being Krayzie, doesn't have time for comedy. He's only four days away from a vicious battle that could scar both competitors for the rest of their lives. Jessica: I just wish I could get my hands on Ellie one more time..... One more time. Jeremy: You're staying here with Troy and Justin. Jessica: You can't do that! Jeremy grins. Jeremy: It's my birthday. I can do what I want. Jeremy turns off the television, choosing to forego watching the rest of Kannon's crap he calls a promo. Instead, the Krayzie One ducks out of the room and into the kitchen for something, leaving Jessica to still ponder Eleanor Hall's words. Jessica: He might think I'm not going to Fusion.... But I think differently. Jessica gets up from her seat, making her way toward the kitchen and Jeremy. Two year old Troy continues to slop away at his ice cream cake while Justin just shakes his head. If this fued can go to another level of hate, then the Howard side of this is going to usher it there. The scene fades away to another shot of Jessica's tight red leather shorts hugging fit to her rear end..... to black!
