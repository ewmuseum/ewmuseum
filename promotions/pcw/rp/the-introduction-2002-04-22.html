The introduction...

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/02 OOC: The ending of this RP is going to lead to a series of Rps that I will do later on. Probably next Monday is when they'll start. Please try and enjoy. Oh thank God. The wait is over. Four months ago, Jordan X Shabazz entered the PWA under the name of Jordan Crazy. He didn't turn heads; nobody gave a damn about him. He made a lot of big friends in the PTC, and he made sure that people liked him. Outside of the ring. Then, one day, NTICW President Timo Bolamba was down on men. The NTICW had just let go of a mass exodus of talent. Jason Jameson, The Cowabunga Connection, Misplaced, most of the NTICW's rising stars were taken. Timo was talking to a friend of his in the OSW, and he threw Jordan's name out there to Timo. Timo checked out a few of Jordan's matches, and he decided that Jordan would make a good addition to the NTICW roster. Jordan was signed. At that time, Jordan didn't really have a set character or gimmick; he was more or less a decent wrestler without a persona. Then one sunny Florida day, Jordan was speeding in a residential neighborhood when he was stopped by a police officer and he was given a ticket for $400. Jordan thought the stop was racially motivated... Jordan X Shabazz was born. Jordan became a Civil Rights Extremist who held Press Conferences to spread his propaganda monthly. He always claimed that he wasn't doing the Civil Rights thing to get himself over; he claimed he was doing it to help all black people around the world. It was always Jordan's goal to make it to the PCW, even when he was NTICW Extreme Champion he didn't really want to be there. That may outrage some people, but it's the truth. Jordan wrestled his ass off in the NTICW, only losing one match due to outside interference to Lance Perfection. But, Jordan got revenge on Lance, and his stable The Perfect Circle, when Jordan defeated Mark Makonie at the NTICW's Pay Per View, Rock On. The day after the Pay Per View, Jordan begged, and pleaded with current PCW Continental Champion, Xavier Kannon, for a spot in the PCW. Xavier Kannon, who had been flattered to the extreme, decided that it would be okay for Jordan to start out as his Lackey~! A job that Jordan enjoys doing. After being his lackey for a few days, Xavier Kannon decided that he would give Jordan a crack in the ring. On Friday Fusion, Jordan stepped into the ring with accomplished ring veteran and current PCW announcer, Duke Williams. Jordan's inexperience in the ring with veterans was apparent, as he didn't even get in a single offensive move. But, to quote Jordan 'It's all gravy.' Because the contract that he hungered for, was given to him on the following Sunday Sabotage. Jordan was supposed to kiss the ass of a wrestler that the Ultimate Authority approved of. Jordan was willing to do it as well, that's how bad he wanted a PCW contract, and he was willing to lick, tongue deep inside the ass, of a person for a contract. Then, 'Ich Will' By Rammenstein hit, and Jordan immediately knew what this meant for him. After feuding with Mark Makonie in the NTICW, it was evident that the music playing was his. Jordan looked immediately distraught and depressed. The pressure mounted on his shoulders. He didn't know what to do, in other words, Jordan was at a crossroads. To lick, or not to lick, that was the question. The hatred, the hate for not all white people, but for ignorant white people, was visible in Jordan's eyes. He looked at the young black children in the crowd [All three of them]. And he realized that he was a role model, they looked up to him, in the NTICW, and he got the crowd to hate him by making remarks about their ethnic backgrounds. He was black, and damn proud of it. Mr. Batee said to Jordan 'YOU JUST SET THE BLACK RACE BACK ABOUT A HUNDRED YEARS HAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHHA!' on the PTC Malice show, he wasn't laughing about Jordan setting the black man back, he was laughing because he's a jackass like that. Either way, those words sunk in on Jordan. He thought about them, day after day after day. PCW, was it worth licking ass for? Yes. Being Xavier Kannon's lackey and setting the black man back a hundred years, was it worth the PCW? Yes. Why didn't he do it then? Shoot, I don't know, perhaps teaming with Rush Mason was something he had wanted to do for quite a while...Or maybe The Malice Crew offered him something that The Ultimate Authority wouldn't have...True friendship. The scene opens up on the road. It's on the freeway of Greensboro, North Carolina. It's Rush Mason's hood. Jordan had gotten the PCW contract he hungered for, and as a reward, Rush decided to take Jordan out on a shopping spree that he wouldn't soon forget. Black men love to shop. So what? It sounds gay? Think I care? Rush and Jordan had already bought in total, eight pairs of jeans, twelve pairs of running shoes, and twenty-five different t-shirts. They also are rocking duel badass PCW visors that Duke Williams gave them. Both men were happy; Jordan was grinning like an idiot and singing like one too. They continued to speed in the rented Jeep on the freeway. What Rush, or anybody else for that matter, didn’t know, was that Jordan’s shoulders were taped up after falling victim to Xavier Kannon’s deadly X Bomb finishing maneuver. Jordan didn’t show that he was injured as he and Rush sang in Jordan’s rented Jeep. Jordan X Shabazz: “TOO MUCH MONEY, TOO MUCH CASH, ALL Y'ALL HATERS CAN KISS THIS ASS!” The words rang out from the Jeep as Jordan drove at least 40 over the speed limit endangering his life and those around him. Rush: “TOO MUCH M-O *Pause* N-E-Y, AND ANYTHING I S-E-E I B-U-Y…” Those were the only words to the song that Rush knew, him and Jordan hadn't gotten to be closer friends then that. Jordan lowered his speed, and the music, so he and Rush could talk for a while. Rush: “Say, Jordan, trust me when I say this, the PCW is nothing like you've ever been involved in before. Three cards a week, you're on the road damn near 250 days a year, and you have to deal with annoying white people every where you wrestle.” Jordan screams in horror, because in his mind, white people are devils. Not all of them, but a good portion of them are devils. Rush laughs as Jordan pulls off the highway and begins driving in a residential neighborhood that is filled with gangs and covered with graffiti. This is a ghetto obviously. Rush is known around these parts as a big star, and somebody who's stayed true to the streets. *** The scene reopens as Jordan walks into a three story tall building in Greensboro. Rush already took everything they had bought and stored it in his church for when Jordan inevitably visit’s the church and takes his clothes with him. Jordan is surprised at the plush amenities that furnish the lavish first floor of the Talk 950 Greensboro offices. Beautiful Green carpets, numerous leather chairs and couches, brand new computers, and a busy office as well. Three secretaries are working the front desk; all of them seem to be engrossed in some sort of work. One of them who are on the phone motions for Jordan to sit down. She hangs up the phone after talking for quite a while. She looks at Jordan and speaks. Secretary: “The boss will be down in just a few minutes.” The secretary chews her gum with passion. Jordan nods as he relaxes back into the chair. *** 30 MINUTES LATER. “Mr. Shabazz, wake up.” The voice startles Jordan. Jordan looks at the man up and down and he almost faints from disbelief. It’s…It’s…It’s…David Duke. David Duke, the openly racist politician owns the radio station where Jordan X Shabazz will be working once a week. Jordan X Shabazz: “Uh… hi.” David Duke wears a big grin on his face. Jordan realizes why he had to wait so long; the racist David Duke didn’t want to see Jordan when Jordan wanted to see him. He wanted to make him wait; he wanted Jordan to feel inferior to him. David Duke: “Yes, Mr. Shabazz, you will start two weeks from now Monday mornings from 6 Am to 10 AM. That is all.” David Duke promptly turns around and he walks away from Jordan, as Jordan stands alone with a stare of disbelief on his face. Jordan X Shabazz: “How the fuck am I supposed to deal with this racist cracka?” Jordan looks around the once bustling with radio shop and notices he’s being looked at by most of the people on the floor. Some of them have stopped what they are doing. Jordan realizes he’s the only black person on the floor, and since the owner of the entire station is David Duke, Jordan puts two and two together as he realizes that he isn’t welcome until next week when he hosts his radio show. Jordan X Shabazz: “I’m out.” In red, some words flash across the screen: How will Jordan be received by PCW fans? Will he be cheered for standing up to the Ultimate Authority and fighting against Xavier Kannon and his group? Or will he be just another PCW wrestler that most everybody forgets about… We’ll have to wait and see. Fade…To…gray.
