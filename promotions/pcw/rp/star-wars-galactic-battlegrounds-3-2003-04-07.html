Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds 3

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 7 - 03 OOC: I can't seem to decide on a style... or subject, for that matter. Oh well, on with the show! ---------------------------------------- ‘Yes sir… roger.’ ‘Start production on the Walkers immediately. Mechanical wheezing noise, ‘Yes sir…’ Hanging high within the sky like a great beacon of hope and light, a mighty sun burns forth across the red arid land, casting a red glow accompanied by a dark shadow across the horizon. The cracked, rolling hills of the land spring forth from the dust like dulled teeth, reaching towards the heavens and creating a rather awe-inspiring geological sight to all who care to behold it. But right now, those in attendance could really care less. A couple of tall figures are conversing near what appears to be a large military base camp, judging by the sheer number of soldier-like figures present and the ammo depot tucked away in the back of the base. Both figures are very similar in appearance due to the likeness of their suits: White, slick, tough material with matching helmets and rifle blasters tucked in their gloved hands. The only things differentiating the two are their voices, which come forth from within the confines of the helmet muffled and mechanically transmitted, and the fact that the taller figure has a strange insignia on the right bicep of his suit. Its golden color signifies possible importance, but other than that, no difference between the two. The taller one slowly gazes about the area, ignoring the clanking and sputtering sounds of the base behind him, then looks at the smaller figure, ‘It’s too quiet. The reports stated heavy Rebel activity in this quadrant.’ ‘What do you make of it?’ The smaller one inquires, with a heavy wheezing emitting from the depths of the helmet. ‘Suspicious. The rebel scum are probably hiding beneath the sands. The Walkers will weed them out,’ The taller one scoffs, a hint of disdain in his mechanically altered voice. ‘Besides, the brunt of our forces are stationed on this planet. They don’t stand a chance.’ The smaller figure shifts nervously, looking down at his white boots for a moment before slowly bringing the gaze of the slick black eyeholes of his helmet back to those of the more important man, ‘The Emperor arrives here today. He’ll be unhappy to find out that progress has been slow thus far. We’ve only managed to take 30% of the planet, and he ordered 60%.’ The taller man nods and lets out a slow, monotone wheezing noise. He stares off into the dust-encrusted horizon for a moment, as if these rebels loom just over it. He can only hope, for his smaller comrade is correct. The Emperor will not be happy… ‘I know. Maybe we’ll be able to weed out the rest of the desert rats before his arrival. If we cannot take all 60%, we’ll take what we can. Did you call in the production of the Walkers? We’re going to need 200 to take Az-Kazim.’ The smaller man nods, ‘Yes, sir. They should be done in 12 hours…’ As if on cue, a LOUD creaking noise sounds forth from within in the base and the two turn around, looking deep into the base at the War Factory. The huge metal door has already begun to slowly withdraw into the building, emitting a sharp grinding noise that cause all that hear it to cringe. But the feeling of irritation subsides into pure awe as a gigantic, four-legged machine steps forth from within factory, followed by three more. ‘Wonderful…’ The taller figures whispers, the reflection of the machines manifesting themselves on the eyeballs of his helmet. The ‘Walkers’, as they’re called, stride forth from the factory and start off towards the back of the base, probably to be equipment. Their composite armor glints in the sun and the barrels of their cannons cast an eerie glow across the faces of the war machines. The huge metal door of the factory closes behind them and they soon disappear behind the Communication Center, a huge domed building with a multi-branched satellite on the top it. Men in protective suits and goggles rush forth from the factory and chase after the machines, eager to get to work. Back at the couple of troopers, the small one turns to the taller one and nods, ‘Perfect.’ ‘Only 94 more to g-‘ Suddenly, a sharp whistling sings forth from the horizon, followed by a low rumbling noise. The troopers whip around and stare at the edge of the land, wondering if that could be… ‘The Emperor…’ The smaller one gasps, seeing the huge Battle Carrier looming over the hill, surrounded by a contingent of Hover Bikes. The Imperial battalion rolls forth like a great tank, unhindered by neither rock nor hill. ‘Hell’s Bells’ by AC/DC mysteriously begins to play in the background as the huge, behemoth Carrier hovers towards the base, completely overshadowing even the largest of buildings. The two guards stand in awe for a moment, remaining unmoved even while Hover Bikes zoom past them and carry a huge whoosh of wind with them. The Battle Carrier stops about 30 feet away from the two and nearly knocked them over with its harsh landing. When the gargantuan machine is finally landed, the troops scurry over to the left side and stare up the metallic girth, awaiting the arrival of their master. A metal door about 50 feet up the side of the carrier slides open and a stepped metal ramp protrudes from it, hooking itself into the cracked desert below. From within the confines of the giant Battle Carrier steps a single-file group of strangely dressed figures. They’re wearing all red, with long red capes and pointed red helmets. They carry long red staves and strides slowly down the metal ramp towards the awaiting guards. ‘Hell’s Bells’ finally cuts and the front man for the red group nods at the taller white guard. ‘Greetings,’ He states, his voice low and without metallic distortion. ‘Have the preparations been made?’ ‘Yes sir…’ The taller guard states, bowing his head slightly. The red figure nods, ‘Good. Now, make way please…’ Suddenly, the red figures come bounding down the ramp and stand at attention on either side, evening their numbers out. The taller guard stands at the right side and the smaller darts to the left, creating a wall of men for the approaching Emperor. They don’t have to wait long, for the man himself finally steps forth from within the gargantuan desert carrier… Standing tall and wearing all black, the Emperor strides slowly down the ramp, his arms tucked beneath his flowing black cape. A large, menacing helmet sits on his head and a robotic breathing noise emits from within it, almost like the helmet itself has asthma or something. The entire contingent of men is silent as the Emperor finally reaches the bottom of the ramp, walks through the pseudo-corridor of men, and turns around to meet them. ‘I… am Dorn Vader…’ That strange breathing noise emits forth from the helmet again, ‘I am your master now. I really don’t have anything else to say except the damned rebels better be eradicated by now, or you’ll all face the wrath of the PRIDE!’ The red guards shift uneasily, while the white guards just give each other inquiring looks. Before the taller one can stop him, the smaller guard speaks up, ‘Emperor, the rebel forces are still out there, but we’re already starting the hunt for them.’ ‘Good’, Dorn Vader states, giving that breathing noise once again, ‘See to it that you do not fail me. If you fail to destroy the rebels, or if they even TOUCH this pl-‘ WHHHEEEEENNN!!!!! A Desert Striker craft rushes by, knocking the Emperor down and sending his guards into a frenzy, firing their lasers into the air. As the one cycles around, three more take its place and buzz the heads of the guards. One of the Strikers even DECAPITATES one of the red guards!! The four Strikers buzz around the Carrier and come right back around, heading right for the Emperor! ‘STOP THEM!!!’ The taller guard cries, firing his laser rifle at the attacking crafts. One of his wild shots hits home and the craft goes crashing into the other three, sending them all sprawling into the ground. They crash about 20 feet away from the Imperials and the drivers of the vehicles lie motionless in the dirt, some in their own blood. The Emperor climbs to his feet and dusts himself off. ‘Bring those fools here!’ He demands, pointing an angry gloved finger at the rebel assailants. ‘If they have a leader, bring him to me!’ The white guards rush over to the downed rebel crafts and salvage through the wreckage. Three out of four of the rebels are mangled and dead, while another lies motionless, trapped beneath a crashed striker. The couple of guards, each take an arm and yank the rebel out from beneath his predicament only to drag him into a worse one. They drag him through the dirt and drop him in front of the Emperor, who merely scoffs. ‘Rebel dogs,’ He points at the downed soldier. ‘Nothing but trouble. Pick him up.’ The white guards lift the rebel to his feet and smack him around for a moment, rattling his brain and getting a stir out of him, They pull off his helmet and as soon as the Emperor lays eyes on his would-be attacker, he gasps dramatically! ‘El Lucas Muffdiver!! The evil rebel leader! We have finally found you!’ Dorn Vader cries, his metallic voice ringing with rejoice. The white guards let go of Lucas, who is able to stand on his feet, but he doesn’t know what the hell is going on. ‘You have challenged me, but now you shall feel my wrath! Your resistance will fall! PREPARE TO FEEL THE PRIDE!’ Dorn Vader whips his cape to the side and a thick, black leather belt is revealed. On the front of it is a huge, decorative gold plating with a star in the middle of it. The plate of the belt suddenly sprouts a long, crème-colored laser that stops when extended at about 4 feet. Dorn Vader grips it and begins to wave it around, contorting his body in the respective direction. El Lucas Muffdiver takes one look at the laser and his eyes widen in surprise. Before he can react, Dorn Vader swings the thing and lops the head off of the rebel leader, watching it roll across the ground like a steaming tapeball. ‘HAHA! The rebel leader has fallen! Now, we will crush his PRIDE and the Resistance will be done!’ The evil Emperor reaches his hand out and points towards El Lucas Muffdiver’s nuts. Vader concentrates for a moment and suddenly, Muffdiver’s nuts explode within his pants!! The Emperor nods in a satisfactory manner and turns to one of his guards, ‘That’ll show him… and on Fusion, Romantico should expect the same!’ [End.]
