Leaving the past behind

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/02 *Ricky steps out of the cab at the airport and pays the driver. He arrived with nothing more than the clothes on his back this afternoon and now he leaves the same way. Everything was as if it were a dream. A horrific nightmare of sorts. Ricky leaving Fusion, feeling good about the turn his career is taking, coming into Camden to visit his uncle, and now, with everything that has happened, it just makes his ordeal about his brother seem so pointless. Ricky enters the airport, and heads over to the ticket counter.* Ricky: How far is it to Teaneck? Teller: Roughly 100 miles. A bit over. Ricky: What's the closet flight to get me in? Teller: New Brunswick. That will still leave you about 40 miles outside of Teaneck though. Ricky: How much is a ticket? Teller: One way or round trip? Ricky: One way. Teller: Ok, let's see here, we have one adult, one ticket, one way. I can get you on a flight that leaves tonight at midnight, price being, $276.00. Ricky: WHAT? Teller: Yes sir. That is the going rate. Ricky: Well, damn that! Where can I rent a car around here? Teller: Outside the main doors, there is a shuttle that runs between the terminal and the car rentals. Ricky: Thanks. *Ricky makes his way back out front and sits on the small wooden bench, waiting for the shuttlebus. As he is sitting there a guy in a long black trenchcoat comes up and sits down beside him. Ricky glances over at the guy who has a cigarette hanging loosely from his mouth. He is wearing dark sunglasses, and his hair is slicked back. He has his coat clenched around him and he then turns and looks at Ricky* Stranger: The booses want you to know, we are sorry for what happened to your uncle. Ricky: Excuse me? Stranger: Your Ricky right? Ricky: Yeah, but how did you know that? Stranger: We know a lot more than people think we do. Ricky: We? Stranger: Come on Ricky, you're a smart kid. Tony always preached about how good of a kid you were. He was always worried about you though. After that injury you had.... Ricky: Whoa! Wait! Who the hell are you? Stranger: Names, Tino Ciccoli. I used to work with your uncle so to speak. You waiting on the shuttle? Ricky: Yeah, I gotta get outta here. Business to take care of. Tino: Friday's show eh? Yeah, I don't blame you there is a lot of talent there waiting to rip you apart. Ricky: You know what, I don't even know why I am haveing this conversation. If it wasn't for the likes of you my uncle would still be alive! Tino: Whatch your mouth kid! Tony chose us, we didn't choose him! He was weak! He didn't have the heart needed to survive in this world. but he did have one thing. Compassion for his family. Whether it be 'our family' of his blood family, Tony had nothing buyt love and respect for all of us. So you don't go running off at the mouth until you know the facts! Ricky: Look, all this is new to me ok? I don't know exactly how to grasp this! I mean all my life I thought my uncle was a good guy and now I am finding out this? What the hell! How do you expect me to respond!? Tino: I don't care how you respond. And for your information, your uncle WAS a good guy. Ricky: Well the way I see it is this. How can you be a good guy AND a murderer? Tino: Your ignorance amazes me kid. Your uncle was so intelligent with everything he knew and did. All you can manage to do is come back with the half assed replies? Ricky: F^ck this. I'm out of here. *Ricky gets to his feet and heads over to the curb as the shuttle comes pulling into the pickup lane. Rick takes one final look back at the bench but the guy is gone. There is an envelope lying there, Ricky asks the driver to hold on for one second and he heads back over and cautiously picks up the envelope. He looks around but Tino is nowhere in sight. Ricky jogs back over to the shuttle and boards it. He grabs a seat right up front and stares at the envelope. Finally he slowly begins to peel the top part of it off, and notices the letter inside. he pulls the letter out, and as he unfolds it a check falls to the floor. He picks it up not looking at it, but goes back to the letter, which reads:Dear Ricky, Our deepest apologies. What happened to Tony wasn't supposed to. He was a good guy. The bastards who were responsible for his shooting, have been captured and dealt with accordingly. Please accept these two gifts as our condolences. Again, we are sorry.The Family Ricky flips the check over and sees it is made out for $25,000, and inside the envelope there is a key. He asks the driver to stop and let him off. Ricky then makes his way back up the sidewalk and across into the parking garage. On the key is a remote starter. He pushes the button and he can hear the car start up. He follows the sound up to the second level where the rumbling gets louder and louder. He finally spots the car responsible for the noise, and he slowly approaches it looking it over.* Ricky: 1969 Shelby Cobra GT. Steel gray, blue racing stripes. Damn this sumbitch will fly. *Ricky glances down at the license plate that reads RickyT and smiles a bit. He hops into the drivers side and a small smile comes across his face. He sees stuck in the dashboard in a picture of his uncle, and him when, Ricky was only 3. He takes the picture and sticks it in his shirt pocket. He drops gears, and the tires scream as he pulls the cobra from the garage. Ricky pulls out onto the highway entrance ramp exiting the airport and lowers the top on his new car. He goes screaming down the highway, hellbent on finding Teaneck, New Jersey.*
