A bad night of sleep

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/22/04 The first night in a foreign country always means a hard nights sleep for me. I’ve been traveling abroad for almost 8 years. It all started with traveling in the summer to play in leagues in Europe and Spain. Then of course, with my transition to the world of wrestling, I’ve done a lot of shows and a few Pay Per Views in other countries. A lot of times, the sleep deprivation is due to the unusual surroundings. You know the feeling you got as a little kid, sleeping at a friend’s house. That uneasy feeling that something didn’t feel right, because you weren’t in your own bed, in your own room. That’s the feeling I usually get when I travel period. Be it in the States, or across the great blue oceans. Sometimes I have trouble sleeping that first night because of noises not familiar to me. And then there’s always the ‘We had a shitty flight and almost died’ reason behind a bad nights sleep. But this time was different. The flight was rather smooth. Even with Buzz complaining the whole time that he had to sit with midcarders and curtain jerkers. The food was rather mundane, the chicken tasted gamey, but that still wasn’t the reason for my lack of beauty sleep. Nope, there was a deeper lying reason. Paperwork. Yep, paperwork screwed up my sleep last night. We fly half way around the world, to entertain fans, and someone, for some reason, got the paperwork screwed up. Yep, paperwork caused me to toss and turn all night. I couldn’t help it. After all, it’s 7 am in the morning. 12 hours from now, we all were suppose to meet the fans, and then at 8 pm, put on the biggest Pay Per View of the year. Sure it’s only February. But PCW history has always been dictated around this PPV. Hell, this is the home of Tyler Nelson’s historic take over of PCW with Media. I won my fist tag team title here in the first Shanghaied 3 years ago. And now, because some one didn’t dot the Is and cross the Ts, we’ll probably not know what this years Shanghaied would of provided. Oh hell with it. I’m sure even those bumbling lawyers can fix this goof up. After all, the world wants to see me spread the blood of Christ. But for now…. Forgetaboutit. There’s a whole city out there, waiting to introduce its Asian culture to The Big Bad Boda Daddy. Propaganda’s ass whooping can wait. Now, let’s see. I wonder if Jon is awake yet. Well, his ass better be, because I’m hungry, and I don’t want to eat alone. I’ll just call the front desk and find out about some good restaurants to eat at. ‘Hello, this is Chris Sloboda in room 69. I’m looking for a great place to eat breakfast. Okay, thank you, and hold all my messages.’ I always wanted to say that to a concierge. Now to wake Jon’s ass up. ‘Jon…. Get up. I’m hungry. Meet me downstairs in five minutes.’ Well, since the show’s on hold for now, I guess it can’t hurt to explore Shanghai. I’ve never had the chance in the past. Of course, we’ve never had this problem coming to China before. But I’m sure all will be straightened out soon. Time to head downstairs and get some grub.
