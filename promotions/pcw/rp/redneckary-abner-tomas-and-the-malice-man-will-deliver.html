Redneckary....Abner, Tomas and the Malice Man Will Deliver

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15.12.02 (#1 Decimation) And so he returns….. That’s right, after a week of sorting through legal business, Duke and Abner hammered out a deal. In a tough decision, Abner officially resigned from the PCW roster…..under Kannon’s command that is. You see, Abner, PCW’s favorite ex-con-redneck, has a new occupation at PCW’s headquarters. Director of Redneck Affairs. Yes, that’s right, from now, the hicks will be under his protection. Abner, Duke, Tomas…..and any other hickish roster member, shall fear the Front Office no more. A new day has begun, and as the sun rises, so does PCW’s average alcohol per-capita intake. Xavier Kannon and Front Office as whole shall melt under the heat of this sun….under the heat of Abner. As unequipped as he is, and probably ill-prepared, Abner is determined to fare well at his knew position. A hard worker, up-front redneck, and dedicated drinker, Abner has no fear. He doesn’t exactly know what he has to do, but By-God, he intends to do it! Yep….I whupped their asses…..whupped ‘em all, an’ whupped ‘em good. Vurdo, Michigan, Mandrake, Kano.....they all fell to under the weight of Redneckary! And they certainly did. Sunday Adrenaline, Inglewood California, is where it all begun..... Tomas gave Abner a favor, and Abner paid her back. All of the opposition fell, and all of their future opposition will fall. But it's more than just the rednecks, no, it's more than that. Xavier Kannon, dictator of PCW, is an enemy to all. While the Director of Redneck Affairs & company may be enemies of the state, Kannon is an enemy of the people. For under his reign, no one is safe. Feared by all, but respected by few, he rules his company with an iron fist. But iron isn't very ductile. Iron does not bend.....it breaks The harder the Front Office squeezes, the more sand slips through their greedy fingers.....and the Malice Man catches it.And slowly, day by day, grain by grain, the Rednecks are building an army, forging opposition. 100% CEO, 100% owner, and a 100% worthless pile of Horseshit! I don't bend over backwards for no one.....or frontwards for that matter! If ya got a problem with me, come deal with it.....and tell me to my face.....like a man! All these weeks I've been here, and never once has Kannon had the balls to face me face-to-face! Naw, Duke has a pair, but Kannon doesn't. Xavi fired me from behind mah back, and Duke hired me to mah face. So Kannon want's my respect? Well he's gonna hafta earn it, here? So Decimation will be quite a test.....a test for the rednecks, and a test for the Front Office.December 22, live from the Toronto Skydome, Xavier Kannon faces Duke Williams one-on-one in a cage..... And who knows what's to come? As Director of Redneck Affairs, Abner is to 'watch out' for all of those assigned to him.....including Duke. So come Decimation, who's going to get decimated? Will the Front Office be placed on the Back Burner? Will the cowboys take the reign? Yes by Jesus Christ, yes! And it remains to be seen whether or not Abner will compete in the event or not. As an unofficial member of the Wrestler Roster, he places his career in the hands of the Malice Man. Will Duke deliver? He certainly has the political power to get the young redneck booked, but the will? You can bet on it. Still..... So the dice is loaded against the rednecks, but where's the barrel of the gun face? I may drown in a bottle Kannon, but someday, someday you'll be swimmin' in this redneck's piss! So at Decimation you try to play Toss the Hoss? Then guess we'll play Flog the Boss! ---------------------------------------- And so, the Redneck Saga of PCW has begun.
