Trying to be a Dude...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered The Cowabunga Connection… a team that has the reputation of being the ‘Nearly Team’ here within the walls of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Like ‘The Pretty Cool Guys’ and myself, we’ve both had the smell of Tag Team Gold… we’ve both been able to get a taste of Gold… getting close to the very Championship belts that we desire… we’ve been able to get our shots in at each other… and the PCW Tag Team Champions… but, to common knowledge, neither of us have been able to place those Titles over our shoulders and be happy with a good nights victory. Well, they’re in the Tag Team contest on Sunday… ironically, much like us… Tag Team Titles on the line! “Now”, I hear you wonder… “Exactly, how do you expect to beat a team as talented as The Cowabunga Connection? They have the skills to kick your ass… but, will you be able to prevent the said ass kicking from ever taking place?” Well, the answer to your proposed question… PCG will not only be able to prevent the kicking of the anus from these boys… but we will also be able to make sure they are the ones… on the end of such a beat down. Therefore, this next instalment of the story… will be something that a lot of people have been unable to come to terms with for a little while. You see, PCG are not only all about taking their opponents to the ‘Wood Shed’… but we are about finding the strengths and weaknesses of our opponents. You see, the whole basis of this… is to spend some time… once again, Undercover as a Trio of ‘Metal Loving’… ‘Surf Happennin’ dudes… who like to chill by the beach and look at the babes. Now, instead of just talking about the small… pointless things of life, I think its about time we took a look at the scenario as it unfolded, on the confinements of a small beach shack, somewhere… across the United States. We open the scene in that very shack… Stimpo sitting behind a counter, looking blankly at the cash register infront of him, Ryan seated to his left, looking at the entrance to the shack… and me, sitting down in a chair on the otherside of the store… looking cool, very cool… well, too cool for these two people anyway. “Hmm… how long do you think we’re going to have to sit in this place… doing nothing but pretend to be a couple of Cool Surfer life dudes?” I asked both Ryan and Stimpo, forgetting that this whole entire plan was my idea in the first place. They glanced over to me, not looking too impressed with the way that I’d asked such a question. Instantly, I knew that I’d said something wrong… but I couldn’t quite get my finger on it at the time… especially since there was literally a catalogue of things that they could both be mad about. Maybe they thought the job on that Purple Palace was… something of eccentric… even thought it WAS Stimpo that set all that material alight. I tried to smile my sheepishly nervous smile at both of them, it came out after a while… but the two guys across the room were refusing to buy anything I was throwing at them. “Gah… will you seriously stop trying to work that smile on us?! It’s the dumbest thing that I’ve ever had to experience… and the fact that it doesn’t even work, hell… that really takes the mickey out of everything that’s ever come to happen here… besides, YOU’RE the one who said we gotta stay here for ages!” Stimpo yelled, sounding pretty damn annoyed with both me… and the way the things were sucking ass at the moment. He got up from his seat behind the Cash Register and looked over toward me with… something of a menacing glare. I tried to look away from my fellow-bandmate… but I was unable to do so, especially when he was looking the way that he did. “Stimpo… calm down and feel the awesome vibes man… I see the radicalness of awesomeness entering your mind… and preventing those mucho cool tunes to blow your mind away… hey,hey,hey… that was damn cool yeah? I’m one of those mean Cowabunga Connection people now aren’t I?” Ryan shouted out proudly, trying to detract the attention that I was drawing to myself with that pathetic little question I asked at the beginning of it all. Ryan Moore… was ironically the only guy that would be able to diffuse any kind of situation with some kind of funny… or sometimes dopey comment, and this little take on him being like one of the “Cowabunga Connection” was fun… fun to hear, something for me to actually laugh at for once. “You know what guys… I think we should just stay here for a few more hours or something… you know, see if somebody comes along so that we can test our awesome knowledge of all sorts against them… because lets face, there isn’t going to be anything as testing as this… is there?” I asked, wondering if there was going to be a harder test in the existence of the universe. Both Stimpo and Moore decided this was the time to turn the other cheek and they pretty much ignored what I had to say with regards to the situation that we were presently in. “Hey… Hey… Hey…”, I started up again, looking at the disappointment that littered their faces and killed the mood of the room, “… Don’t worry about the fact that nobody has come along to check us out… because lets face it, we know who totally rules at doing this kind of thing… and its not those pansy-wannabes called the “Cowabunga Connection”… is it?” In what seemed to be the period of ‘Zero to Hero’, my fellow singers started to become interested in what I had just said… the reasons for this sudden rise in interest were totally unknown… even to somebody like myself. For a second, I thought I was seeing PCG as their best of being one total unit… and then, it suddenly came to me… after all of these months in PCW, I finally figured out why PCG were noway near the level that we had been able to achieve while working in NTICW… like, when we won their Tag Team Championship Belts within four matches! The Pretty Cool Guys had been lacking the unity that came along with three people… and now, I was starting to look ahead to that amazingly huge match on Sunday Night at “Third Dawning”… hmmm… Hmmm indeed. I waited for another couple of seconds to pass before saying anything else to the other two members of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’, “… Fellas… you know, this moment here… it’s just like the days where Ryan and myself were employed by NTICW… alongside our former friend… whose name eludes me at this current moment in time. You know… I think this is going to be the very night… that the Pretty Cool Guys will be able to leave a PCW Event with their first Gold… their first chance to shine… and show the World exactly what will happen in the following weeks. The era of PoP is going to begin my friends… oh yes… it will!!!” I screamed the last line at the top of my voice, expecting my comrades to laugh and cheer along with me… but to a shock, I receive no such applause… not even an ovation. Instead, the dire… disgusting silence that had existed for seconds before… filled the room. I tried to laugh… just to see if this was all a huge joke… but then, I felt somebody’s breath coming down upon my neck. Interested to see who this character could be… I turned around… shocked by the view of an interesting looking “Dude” character. He looked at me with an interesting glare… and then, I moved out of his way and slinked back to the seat that had contained me for the majority of this piece. There was a small period of silence within the room… and then he spoke, “… What in the hell are you saying dude? What in the hell is this PoP that you speak of? That kind of music… is so unradical… dude, I can’t believe I just walked into your shop and heard you ranting on about PoP and some Wrestling match… we don’t wrestle, we don’t listen to PoP either… we surf the waves on our boards.” “Dude… he would so NOT rant about PoP unless he was totally dissing it or something… I mean, that’s like totally going against the book of coolness… its like totally turning your back on all your brothers and swimming out to sea… instead of seriously surfing and listening to HEAVAY Metal!” Stimpo cried, in a voice that really made things sound like he was taking the complete and utter piss out of the ‘Surfer-like Guy’ standing a little distance away from myself. Either way, something happened that made everything change… because this guy, he just stood there and like… turned around before walking out of the complex. I tried to think about why this character had decided to do such a thing… hell, I spent most of the day trying to figure out why he had walked away… maybe Stimpo had said something offensive? Maybe he had said something that really meant something else… and seriously killed our cover. “Stimpo… what the hell did you say to that guy? He seemed to walk out of this place… in like something of a seriously nasty mood.” Commented Ryan upon looking at the man as he walked out of the building. I couldn’t help but start to fear for everything at this point… namely down to the fact that something was just said that hurt the feelings of a guy like him. We continued to sit in the shack… waiting for somebody else to come in and do something… however, that just wasn’t going to be the case… infact, the chances of anyone coming into the shack now… were about equal to Elton John walking through the Mens Bathroom without looking at everyone else. Stimpo shrugged in answer to Ryan’s question about what had just been said and everything continued to be quiet for the better part of ten minutes. Suddenly… there was a loud knock at the door of the shack. I stopped, trying to figure out who it could be… but after a few knocks, the shack door was looking horrible… almost deceased like. Quickly… I motioned to the back of the place… Ryan and Stimpo getting from their seat as the door finally busts open, wooden shards going everywhere and PCG running off into the background. “I should have assumed you were going to say something stupid!!” I cried out as I ran away into the darkness of the picture, Stimpo and Ryan Moore close behind. Well, after this instalment… we see that PCG have come to another failure in their constant attempts to understand and become the enemy. Will this plan kill their hopes of winning the PCW Tag Team Championship Belts come Sunday? Or will something more sinister happen? Tune in NEXT TIME… to find out the answer to that question!
