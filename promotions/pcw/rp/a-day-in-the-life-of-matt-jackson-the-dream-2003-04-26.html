A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, The Dream

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/03 Death is a strange thing…it can make people react in different ways, let them feel different emotions and worst of all it can make you fear things. Death is the one thing that can make a man break…even the most trained men, still fear death. Everyone fears death, the unknowingness of when it is going to come..when it is going to finally strike. I had a dream while I was unconscious… Haley’s bedroom walls wear a soft shade of off-white paint. Pictures of her and Matt are hung up, in different types of metal frames, along with a seemingly out of place picture…one of her and Matt when they first came into the PCW, it is larger then all the others. A queen-sized bed stands in the center of a room, the silk sheets tossed all over the place. Next to the bed, is a moderately sized metal nightstand, on top sits a digital alarm clock. Off to the right, is a dresser, it’s glass top is covered in make-up bottles and hairbrushes. Lying atop the bed is the beautiful blonde…Haley. Her hair is spread out over the pillow and she is wearing a new pair of silk pajamas, her slender face is split by a wide smile…that welcomes her boyfriend into her room. She notices that he is wearing casual clothes for a change, a blue tee shirt and khaki shorts. He gives her a smile…walking across the hard wood floor to her bed. Sitting on the bed…Matt takes her hand and holds it tightly. Haley stares up at him with her pale blue eyes and smiles…gripping his hand tighter. Matt looks at Haley and sighs, as he runs his hands through his short brown hair…letting the spikes play on his hand like thousands of needles pricking him. He doesn’t feel the slight bits of pain. Haley’s hand trembles. “Matt I am scared for you this week.” Her eyes get bigger and her face reads like a book…telling him the fear she is feeling. “You are going up against three people and I am not going to be there with you this time.” “You know everything is going to be all right Haley,” Matt says, letting a reassuring smile cross his face, and his voice lacks on concern. “Matt you can’t just pass this one off…I know you have more PCW in ring experience then these guys, but they could be a surprise. I mean have you looked at Stone Burns, the guy is a Misogynist…he hates women Matt and he carries a knife.” Stroking her petite hand, Matt nods. “He carries a knife and I carry a gun. He touches my girl and I will kill him. You have nothing to worry about…I promise.” The look still doesn’t leave Haley’s face, as she stares up at him with puppy dog eyes…trying to let him know her strong dislike for the situation. “What about Sivasian Krikorian? Are you just going to pass him off too?” She questions, her voice ever persistent and probing. “What about him Haley? He hates American’s and is a famed sniper. I would die to protect this country and I hold no other feelings in this match…Haley I think you know how determined I am to do the job I was brought in here for…and no one is going to stop me.” Pushing his hand away…Haley sits up and stares at him in amazement. Her eyes slowly probe him…probing into Matt’s soul. He shifts uncomfortably and stands up off the bed…pacing around on the wooden floor, listening to the floor creek under him, Matt rubs his hands together and then turns back to his princess. “H-H-Haley….I l-l-love-“ Suddenly it ended and I was forced back to my horrible reality…forced back to my hell on earth. With that dream ending my impending death was near. I wouldn’t be able to go on for much longer. I wouldn’t get to make it to Turmoil…ending the hopes and dreams of three other men. I don’t want to leave Haley, but she is going to be alone and I am going to…die alone.
