Elvis Lives!

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 March 2002 We arrive at our destination... a seedy nightclub in Los Angeles, California. The place is so seedy that the workers from the local theme parks come here to chat up some women and let off some steam. The camera pans over to the bar where Minnie Mouse is dressed as a hooker wearing a tight leather catsuit and heels and is getting chatted up by Goofy with slicked back hair and a silk shirt. What? You thought this was going to be a serious promo? Get real. Suddenly a man wearing a perfectly horrible Hawaiian shirt stumbles into the bar, followed by a man with a rather bad “Angel” complex. No it’s not Drake Maxwell or any of the other fools who call themselves various types of fallen angels who fail their angles or anything like that. It’s not even someone who looks anything like David Boranaz... although the person did once meet someone called Buffy... although it was Buffy Vegas. That’s right, it’s Miyagawa. He’s having a night out while his poor wife is in the hospital. The evil bastard. And of course that means the person in the decidingly dodgy Hawaiian shirt is none other than... Miyagawa: JJ, will you please stop acting like a tit, I am just completely not in the mood. JJ: You’re never in the mood you boring fart, now let’s get to the bar and have some alcoholic mineral water! Miyagawa: I hate living so close to Hollywood. It means an abundance of stupid fads that last for ten minutes whilst cool, and three years in the mind of Jeremy Johnson. JJ: See, you’re in such a bad mood that even your cutting insults aren’t all that cutting anymore! Miyagawa just grumbles and walks his way over to the bar along side the ever annoying and ever skipping JJ. The duo get to the bar in the seedy nightclub and stop to wait for the barman to come over. Miyagawa: Two beers please. JJ: Make mine an alcoholic mineral water. The barman looks at JJ suspiciously and turns to Miyagawa. Barman: You really shouldn’t allow your son to drink at that young an age. Miyagawa just looks back at the barman plainly. Miyagawa: And a packet of cheese and onion crisps if you would. JJ: But I only like Salt and Vinegar! Miyagawa: And you think I’m buying Cheese and Onion why? Buy your own sodding crisps. The barman grumbles a bit more and fetches the two beers, the packet of cheese and onion crisps and a bottle of Hollywood style Alcoholic mineral water and places them on the counter. Miyagawa hands over some notes and gets some coins back. Miya shoves the packet of crisps into a pocket and grabs the beers with his two hands. JJ grabs his bottle of clear white crud and wanders over to a table near a nicely lit stage and sits down. Miyagawa looks over at him and only then realises the stage element is there. Miyagawa: The little bastard has taken me to a karaoke bar. I’m going to kill him. In fact, I’ll give him a PURE contract and then when I kill him I won’t be sent to prison like Boda should have. Yes, that is what I shall do. I shall have vengeance... on Boda too. Miyagawa sits down at the table where JJ chose and starts drinking one of the beers. Miyagawa: This is premeditated murder, you do realise? JJ: Murder? Miyagawa: Yeah, because if either you or I get up there and sing, then everyone here is going to die laughing at us. JJ: Take a moment to admire the clientele for a second will you? Miyagawa looks around the bar... a couple of hookers... horns... black denim jackets.... jeans.... a couple of suits... wait a second, horns? HORNS?! Miyagawa’s eyes go wide as he notices something else, this time with rotting flesh and a rather large claw... then a guy with two heads... Miyagawa: Circus folk? JJ: Nah they’re demons. Like me. Miyagawa: You’re a demon? JJ: Totally. I’m a class seven “Scriptah” demon. Miyagawa: And I presume you annoy people to death then? JJ: No, I’m a nice demon. So are all of these good people. You see that guy with the horns and the snappy purple suit. Miyagawa looks over and spots the fellow while drinking the rest of the first beer. He nods at JJ in response. JJ: His name is Lorn. I’m not entirely sure what type of demon he is, but basically when you sing Karaoke, he can see your future. That’s why I brought you here. Ah look, he’s coming over. Lorn indeed, from the Angel television series walks over towards the table. Lorn: Angel! What are you... wait, who the hell are you? JJ turns to Lorn and grins and waves. Lorn: Oh god, it’s him. Miyagawa: Finally someone who likes you as little as I do. Lorn: Amen to that brother. Oh look I just went all Hulk Hogan... that’ll never wash off. **looks at Miyagawa** Wow your emotions are just flooding out, I don’t think you’ll need to sing at all. Lorn sits himself down in a chair next to Miyagawa. Miyagawa does his best not to look at him for a few seconds and then looks over... and keeps staring at his horns on his forehead. Lorn: First demon, eh? JJ: You betcha... listen, can I go sing though? Lorn: Go knock yourself out. JJ: Yay! JJ scarpers off up to the stage and picks his song from the list quickly and it starts playing.... “All Shock Up” by Elvis himself... JJ: A well I bless my soul.... What's wrong with me?.... I'm itching like a man on a fuzzy tree.... My friends say I'm actin' wild as a bug.... I'm in love.... I'm all shook up.... Mm mm oh, oh, yeah, yeah! Lorn and Miya look at each other for a second. Miyagawa: Well if Elvis ain’t already dead then JJ just killed him. Lorn: And if he is, then he’s rolling in the grave. Listen, I can feel your pain. Miyagawa: So you’re a demon therapist now then? The pair look back at JJ murdering the “king” and then away again. Lorn: But seriously. You’ve got a lot on your plate at the moment, and it’s going to be the most turbulent time of your life. But there is something you have to realise... if you fail, then Natalia will forgive you. Miyagawa: I know her well enough to agree. But there is something else that you have to realise... Lorn: I hear you. But you have to learn something else too. You have to learn to forgive yourself. Taking it out on other people inside a ring won’t help you. Miyagawa: Yes it will. If I want to face Boda and get revenge then I have to defeat Chris Chase and Corey Williams on Friday. And if I want to defeat them, I have to take Williams OUT tomorrow at turmoil. And when I say out, I mean completely out as in no return possible. Out, dead, buried, injured, killed. That is what I have to do, and I intend to do it. Lorn: Well, if you can wrestle as well as JJ can sing badly, then I’m sure you will. Miyagawa: I do have to ask you something though... Lorn: You’re correct, when I told JJ to go knock himself out, I meant he should knock himself out, not that he should go and sing. I don’t want to punish anyone by listening to his singing voice. I do sense that you’re going to give him a job though. Miyagawa: Yeah, PURE Interviewer... they sit behind the camera so the fans are safe.
