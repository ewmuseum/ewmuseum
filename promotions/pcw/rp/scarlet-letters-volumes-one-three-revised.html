Scarlet Letters...Volumes One-Three (Revised)

Date: Past The Tree of Life Location: The unknown wilds of Canada… In the dark womb where we began My mother's life made us a man, Through all the months of human birth Her beauty fed our common earth. We cannot see, nor breathe, nor stir, But through the death of some of her. -From 'C.L.M.', by John Masefield. Dear Yuu, We are here yet again, we never thought we would be able to stay here, but the more we think, the more we are afraid to leave our abode. It is peaceful here, where we do not have to deal with the other people, and the truth of our existence. As long as we stay, the truth does not have to face us. Here, the truth can be hidden, can be squashed without a sound. But even as we lie, asleep, we can hear a voice pierce our mind, a soft, gentle voice, prodding us to wake, prodding us to move forward, away from the shelter of darkness, away from the walls that we build up around ourselves. There is nothing else we can say, except for 'no'. We cannot bear to face anyone outside. All we can do is think and recite the truths we have received in the Void. There is everything in the world to reflect on. Our father, he had respect for life, though he was a maniac who tried and succeeded in raising a monster of battle as us. Our mother. She was just as mad as he was…both of them insane for the combat only…now their ambitions dead now. There is nothing we can do. These facts sound like lies to us. It is the truth that we are not human. In our rage in Tokyo, in our obsessive search for acceptance and the truth, we tore the city apart and deemed ourselves worthy of being a Celestial, as our ethereal parents, Commission and Offering, were before us. How ironic it is that we discover that both of them committed a immortal sin in the creation of us. We discover we are a virus, a banality of society as well as existence. We are not human. We knew it then, but we did not accept the fact. We are not human, but new hybrid of Celestial entity... more than an basic god… We cannot bear to face the world. It makes us wonder why our purpose is. We are intelligent, powerful, and worthy of living in the world, except for one reason: gods tried to play God with the other gods. As with any dictatorship, where one man, or a god, tries to control their gods, the gods eventually rise up against their masters. Man on this Earth has rejected gods. Gods has served a purpose in the minds of men. They are rising up to rebel. It is no different for us. The men of PCW, the CEO Nelson, Wonder, their champion Snow… they will suffer, just like the men suffer for believing that gods will follow like string puppets, bowing to serve them. Man made one mistake, just as Nelson made his mistake. We were intelligent. We cease, at a point, to be a doll, controlled, manipulated. We think for ourselves. And at that point, we deem the evil of mankind unworthy of his existence. They resent us for demanding savory battle when none has been given to us, none, except for a miniscule percentage, and the act of fighting us in itself is not an act willed upon one's self. If these evil men controlling PCW wanted the battle rigged, wanted the battle in their favor, he should have left his Neo Void God out of the picture and left his mindless animals on the earth. Nelson has made the same mistake. He decided to play God, decided to push away the HAND OF FATE directed towards him. We should punish him as well as the others for their subordination. But we will not. Again, it's left to the gods who look over them to gain their own power…we have our own agenda… We will no longer be a doll. We will not be manipulated by anyone, or anything, except for ourselves. For we are the solidarity Neo Void God, and no one else has control over us. But we are afraid to face the world. We remember the past... no, we remember the truth. The truth makes us angry. Not the truth itself, but the fact that the gods that Nelson, Chef and Wonder tried to be hid the truth from us. They thought their puppet was not intelligent enough to find out. But we were. We should not be afraid of the world. We can see a slight point of light from the darkness of the cave where we reside. We shut ourselves out, yet we speak of our quest. We are only as good as Nelson is by doing that; claiming one thing while doing another; denying themselves the truth and denying others the truth. At least we know we are not afraid. Our quest will not be vengeance. It will be the building of courage, the honing of our skills. That will be our quest. It may be days, or years. But we will let one quest lead to another. We are still afraid. But the difference is, we am willing to challenge the fear, willing to control it, instead of letting it control us. The weakness of man lies in their inability to take control of their own lives. By letting their lives be run by someone or something else, they are free of the harsh truths of the world. Working for someone, with no question of their goals, is only denial. We will be like that no longer. We stare at the world. We may be afraid, but we will not let it take over us. ** Akiyama ** Fortune and Men's Eyes When in disgrace with fortune and men's eyeswe all alone beweep our outcast state,And trouble deaf heaven with our bootless cries,And look upon myself, and curse our fate;Wishing us like to one more rich in hope,Featured like him, like him with friends possest,Desiring this man's art, and that man's scope,With what we most enjoy contented least; Yet in these thoughts myself almost depressing,Haply we think on thee and then our state,Like to the lark at break of day arisingFrom sullen earth, sings hymms at heaven's gate;For thy sweet love remembered, such wealth bringsThat then we scorn to change our state with kings. 'Fortune and Men's Eyes' -by William Shakespeare. Dear Yuu, The days narrow in on the event called Madness. Here we sit, working and toiling away, trying to train our mind and body to be stronger, more disciplined, and better than theirs. And yet, no one will recognize our greatness. Today, we went hunting. We found a large deer in the woods, and managed to kill it with a fist straight to the chest. In the legends and stories about us, they say that we are inseparable with our skills, which it is our only friend and only weapon, but they are so wrong. Although the technique adapts to our opponents, we still utilize the same lethalness that it was created to exhibit. There is nothing more absurd than trying to hit squirrels with the Danse Macabre. We made a composite Kendo stick with some wood and intestine in our excursions in the woods. It is not a very powerful weapon, but it certainly can assist to kill large game, but rarely on the first shot. We are good with our hands to finish off a suffering animal, however, and they are always dead within ten seconds after we hit them. The stories about us being a cold being, with no emotion. We are not cold. We are only prudent. The humans of the city are worse than we are. They set out traps; try to kill beavers and wolves for their fur, with no regard for the life they are slaying. We slay to survive, just as the animals slay each other to survive. Hunting is a pleasure that we can admit to. The other one is training. We sit outside our cave, meditating, letting thoughts come to us, concentrating our Celestial energy and balancing both the Void and the Aura within us so our actions will be more powerful. As always, it is in preparation for our quest, the quest for us to not be afraid of the world. The more we see them, the more the humans of the city disgust us. Yet, watching them leaves us with some sort of longing, emptiness inside us that hunting and training cannot fill. The voice inside us tells us that she can fill that emptiness, but we don't believe her. There is no one else around here. When we saw the other hunters, they are talking with their brethren peacefully, sitting for a well-earned snack. One of them had a girl at his side, and although she appeared to be a frail creature at the side of the man, she was very capable of defending herself and making her own decisions. They all talked as if they were family. We believe that, in fact, they were family, two families camping together. The family we had never let us experience things like this. Many people envy us. They want to feel the experiences of war, of heroism, of battle, of being one of the most powerful swordsmen and martial artists on the earth. However, as glorious as those things may be, they will never fill the emptiness inside us. We worked hard for our reputation, our title of godhood, but in the end, it gave us nothing. We wish we had a friend. However, the humans that we know, they are all shallow and weak. They think of nothing except for themselves. They think not of the animals they kill, the environment they decay, the people they hurt. They only think of themselves. Us, we think of the animals, and give thanks to this global realm when we manage to bring a deer down. When we gather berries and leaves, we thank the realm for providing us with such a rich bounty. When we train, the sun shines on us, on our bareback. The wind tosses our hair around gently, playing with it as if we were a friend. As we hunt, though, we realize one thing. The forest and the planets and the animals, all of them are getting weaker, more tired, and less muscular. The plant wilt, the animals are slow and cumbersome. We look around, and realize that the ground is starting to get brown and hard, dry and cracked. The sunlight in the sky, even, is starting to dim in the sky around us. The energy of the earth is being absorbed; the truth of the existence is being taken away. It is all thanks to men like Tyler Nelson. We must remember the quest that we train hard for every day. We do not wish vengeance on him. We wish to be disciplined enough to face the world, and then strong enough to make a change. Humans like the CEO are not fit to live. The selfish purposes, the people who harbor them, and the truth they concoct do not deserve to live. We are not the only one who has been betrayed by him. Our hand tires, but our left arm itches to practice with the sword. The Saishukessen Ougii is sheathed, placed at our side. We do not draw it unless we intend to draw blood. We reach forward, past the Saishukessen Ougii, to grip the wooden practice sword we use when we work on our technique. We end our writing here. We will train now. The purpose, we will remember. We want to be strong so we will not be afraid. ** Akiyama ** The Spring of the Water of Life 'When we're alone' - the words tripped off his tongueAs though to be alone were nothing strange.'When we was young,' he said; 'when we was young....' we thought of age, and loneliness, and change.We thought of how strange we grow when we're alone,and how unlike the selves that meet and talk,and blow the candles out, and say good night.Alone.... The word is life endured and known.It is the stillness where our spirits walkand all but inmost faith is overthrown. 'Alone' -By Siegfried Sassoon. Dear Yuu, When we were practicing today, we foolishly dropped our sword on our foot. In the process, we managed to bruise our bare toes. This incident proves to us that we need to train more. How ironic that it happens now, in the isolation of the mountains. We say this because we would have loved to show the people who live on the earth that we were not really superhuman, that we were not perfect. But even then, the people would not understand. They would have laughed at how a man such as us was able to achieve the birthright of godhood given to me. The narrow-mindedness of the world is so prominent that it sickens us. We mediate in order to help expand our mind. We work hard in training to perfect our skills. While we were practicing an overhead strike, we hit our arm on a nearby rock and the weapon slipped from our hands and fell to the ground. The hilt struck our exposed foot. We should have watched for that rock. But we are thankful that it happened here in training. If it had happened in the real world, we would not be here right now. The mercy in the real world is minimal. From experience, we know. We dislike those who are jealous of our ability. First of all, we are not perfect. We make our mistakes and stumble on our words. We say little because we fear what people would think if we stated our mind. Strong as the stories say we are, we are still incompetent in many ways. The rock, the boulder on our training ground stopped us as we thought we had the ground memorized. Perhaps that is not a lack of skill. Perhaps that is a birth of arrogance. We should not be proud of our position as the Neo Void God. The position does not prevent us from going into battle. We have to enter the wars, we have to slash our sword, and we have to draw the blood of human beings. Our image may instill fear, but no longer do people surrender their weapons to us. Instead, they fight us. In battle, the blood splashes over our arms; the warmth seeps in through our gauntlets, across our shoulders, over our face. The salty taste flecks our lips. We fight through a sea of death. In war, we are treading in blood up to our ankles. The warriors who die in battle die not from the sharp end of our blade, but from drowning in the blood of men, their comrades, their opponents, and their own. Despite this, people envy us, and want to take our place. They want to fight the painful wars, to endure the hostile environments, to sit through the impatient speeches. They want to manage the armies, deal with the incompetent fools at the top of PCW, and wade through the blood of friend and foe alike. They think it will bring them glory, and strength, and honor. They think it will earn them beautiful women, riches, and attention. They think that friends will flock to them like flies to feces. But we wish we could tell them that it is not easy. We look at ourselves. Are we enjoying women? Do we have friends flocking to us left and right? Do we enjoy the riches and power that they think our status provides us with? We are still alone. But every time we think of this, we hear the voice speaking to us, reassuring us, that she is there. We shrug it off, for we know the truth, and the truth is, we are not with anyone else, and are therefore alone. Why do we train? We train so we will no longer be afraid. We do not have to be alone. The world awaits us, but so much of the world will ignore what we truly are, and instead, they will strike at the reputation that they dreamed up for us. PCW and Nelson created the reputation, the man who tried to be the god of so many men. He told us what to do, where to go, and how to act. Our mistake, back then, was letting him pull the strings, letting him move our mouth like a dummy. Nelson will see what he has created, and he will finally see the truth, the truth he had denied us for so many months. But we cannot face the world. Our striking arm is not perfect. We drop our practice sword. We must concentrate on our abilities before heading to the world. It is not the strength of our body that we need, though. It is patience we need, so we can tolerate human beings. ** Akiyama **
