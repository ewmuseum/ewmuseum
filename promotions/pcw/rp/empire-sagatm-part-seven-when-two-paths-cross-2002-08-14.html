Empire Saga™ Part Seven: When two paths cross…

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/8/02 Empire Saga™ Part Seven: When two paths cross… Prologue… His name: Davis Fury. He had but one thing in common with the Crime Lord known as John Crichton and that was the fact that they’d both lost someone close to them. Once, Crichton had another thing in common with Davis Fury. Revenge. Fury wanted revenge for the death of his wife and Crichton once wanted revenge for the death of his girlfriend. Now Crichton’s intentions were clouded to say the least. Fury had managed to keep up the exploits of John Crichton via various sources he still had within the police force. According to all reports Crichton’s intentions had taken a complete 360degree turn. Nobody knew why, but since his intentions were already suspect, nobody thought to ask why he suddenly decided to become one of the most notorious Crime Lords in the U.S.A. Fury had no time to question John Crichton’s intentions in the past, but now it seemed their paths were beginning to cross. Whilst searching for the Underworld Criminal known as Tolkien Anderson, Fury’s attention was brought to questionable actions of John Crichton…and how that affected him. Apparently because of John Crichton’s new found power, Tolkien Anderson had been forced to go underground, making in next to impossible to find him. He had one choice and one choice only… And so their paths were destined to cross…that he knew, but he still had reservations about the whole thing... What could he do against a man with super human abilities and a whole tyrannical empire backing him? Chapter One: When Two Paths Cross…[John Crichton] ‘No you fool! This will not do!’ Crichton bellowed, as he lent further back in his black, leather chair. The man stood, cowering upon the other side of desk, fumbled with his blue hat nervously, uncertain of John Crichton’s intentions with him. ‘I’m…sorry Mr. Crichton, but I could do nothing…they shot Jeff dead in the street I had to give myself up, just to keep myself alive!’ He stated hesitantly. ‘I see. So you don’t think I’M WORTH DYING FOR! Barry…it’s real simple, each and every time you get caught doing random jobs…that weren’t specifically authorised by me…you endanger this entire organisation!’ Crichton pauses for a second ‘Especially when you are caught!’ ‘But I didn’t tell them anything sir! By the way thanks for breaking me outta that place, they were treating me like a… Barry trails off as Crichton rises from the desk. ‘…a common criminal?’ Crichton suggests, walking around and sitting on the edge of his desk, slightly to the right of Barry. ‘Yes…’ Barry replies warily eyeing Crichton up. ‘Well Barry, I am very glad that you’ve not ratted me out to the police…the problem is however, they will still be looking for you!’ Crichton begins. ‘Indeed, the majority of the police force are bumbling fools, but one day I have no doubt they will eventually find you and given the choice of ratting me out or going to prison, which one would you prefer?’ Crichton questions looking at Barry menacingly. ‘Well…I…uh…haven’t …uh…thought about it much…I…’ Barry replies nervously. Crichton simply nods in acknowledgement and rises from the table and begins to circle Barry like a vulture. Barry’s beady, little eyes track Crichton’s movements suspiciously. ‘Exactly!’ Crichton begins, stopping right in front of Barry. ‘That is why appropriate…precautions must be enforced!’ Crichton declared. ‘Wha…what do you mean?’ Barry questions, the stench of ‘nervousness’ filling the room. ‘Well Barry, I mean this…’ Crichton trails off as his arm extends lightening quick and his hand grips the man’s throat. Then he simply lifts Barry into the air, with the greatest of ease. ‘What…arghhh…are…you…arghhh…doing?’ Barry questions nearing unconsciousness. ‘Tying up a few loose ends!’ Crichton replies a sick grin developing upon his face. ‘George! Reginald! Get your asses in here!’ Crichton bellows. Within an instant the two bumbling Henchmen enter the room, wearing their obligatory black, three piece suits. Very normal for these two…however the cuts and bruises upon their faces are not…another by-product of interfering in a wrestling match. Crichton smiled slightly at the injuries. Crichton simply tosses, the now unconscious, Barry across the room, whom the bumbling idiots catch without fuss or trouble. ‘What do you want us to do with him, boss?’ Reginld questions, draping the small man over his shoulder. ‘He’s a loose end that needs to be taken care of…leave no fingerprints and make sure the police find the body…we need them to stop searching for him…they’re too close to us.’ Crichton explains, sitting back down in his black, leather chair. ‘Righto boss, we’ll take care of it!’ George replies, opening the door for Reginald to walk through. ‘Excellent!’ Davis Fury... Davis had been searching area of Chicago, Illinois that the locals deemed ‘bad news’ and still he found no leads on either John Crichton or Tolkien Anderson. His time was running short because his career within the GW demanded that he return to work by the end of the week. He felt like he had discovered nothing and his time in the city was fruitless, but he knew this to be false. He had learned many things about his original foe, Tolkien Anderson and some bonus information on this John Crichton fellow. It had become clear to him that as long as this John Crichton remained in power, Tolkien Anderson would remain under ground, hiding from potential tormentors. ‘Should he try to topple John Crichton’s organisation to persuade Tolkien to come out of hiding? Could he topple John Crichton’s organisation? Was it worth the risk to do so?’ After all Crichton’s organisation was growing in both size and numbers each day…almost one twentieth of criminal territory was coming under Crichton’s jurisdiction every week. The local police could not cope with the increase in crime and that was with only a fraction of Crichton’s men operating within the city. He had spread his forces all across North America…it was only a matter of time before his empire expanded out of anyone’s control, never mind the police. Davis Fury walked into this thing with one single objective, find Tolkien Anderson and punish him for the murder of his wife, but it wasn’t that easy. He found a whole heap of shit down here that needed to be cleaned up and it appeared that fate wanted him to handle the poop-a-scoop. Was it his destiny? Or had he read the situation wrong? The questions came to him by the bucket load, and before he even began to think about taking Crichton on they needed to be answered…which would take a lot of soul searching, but like he said…he was in town until the end of the week and anything can happen in a week…anything To be continued….
