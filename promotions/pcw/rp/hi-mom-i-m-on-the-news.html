Hi Mom! I'm On The News!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: By A Man Who&#039;s Gon&#039; Get Him Some Extreme Gold Our scene opens up with the man that is frequently called Rush Mason, possibly because that’s his name, sitting at a news desk, looking surprisingly spiffy. He’s wearing what would be considered normal garb for a newscaster, but very fancy clothes for the PCW superstar. Rush: Yes, that certainly was a beautiful and uplifting story. Rush smiles and nods his head as a voice comes from off-stage. Voice: That was the story about the riots and mass rape in Argentina! Rush’s nodding stops and there is total dead air for a few seconds, but Rush just puts his smile right back on. Rush: Oh…right I was talking about how happy those rapists were going to be tonight. Anyways, our next bit of news comes from, wait it comes from me. The PCW’er looks around for a few seconds, forgetting what he’s supposed to be doing as sighs can be heard from behind him and all over the set. Rush: This segment of the show is the consumer report, and I guess since the last guy who did this was actually killed during the testing, I’ll do the report, or at least I’ll make something up so that it looks like I know what I’m talking about. Rush thinks for a second, wondering why his cue cards would say that. Rush: Oh wait, that part I’m supposed to wait until commercial to say. The new member of the Uprising looks up with a blank look on his face, and then quickly a visual comes up in the upper left corner of the viewer’s screen. The first visual is just a regular looking slice of pizza. Rush: This week’s test was fast food and to discover what kind of fast food is preferred by the American people. Surprisingly in last place was pizza. Suddenly, out of nowhere, the screen begins to move rapidly forward, looking as though someone is fast-forwarding it. It stops and Rush now has a visual of KFC up on corner of the screen. Rush: Kentucky Fried Chicken won out against all others as the best fast food, as chosen by the American public, but it is terribly unfortunate that such a suck-ass promoter represents this food. Of course, Rush is talking about Kramer Wild Wing, the current title holder and KFC spokesperson, and there are more sighs from behind the Voodoo Villain at his use of profanity, but he just continues with the newscast. Rush: Yes, Kramer Wild Wing- Just as Rush speaks, a visual of the man comes up onto the corner of the screen. Rush: The man who is now on your television screen is Kramer Wild Wing, extreme champion of Primetime Championship Wrestling. We cut to behind the set, where two people are talking. Producer: What the hell is this? And where are my regular news anchors? Secretary: Well, we couldn’t find them and this guy said he had a past in radio. I’m not sure what this segment is all about though. Producer: What can we do? Secretary: We can’t take the news off the air now, and I suppose we just let him finish it, then kick him out. Now the shot goes back to Rush, talking yet again about KWW. Rush: Kramer Wild Wing is a terrible person. In fact, here I have proof that he is a child molester- A visual of KWW’s face superimposed onto a priest’s body is shown. Rush: A thief- A visual of KWW’s face superimposed onto Kenneth Lay (CEO of Enron)’s body Rush: And a killer A visual of KWW’s face superimposed onto OJ Simpson’s body. Rush: Yes, this man is the epitome of terrible, and in fact should be arrested and placed in jail immediately. The shot again switches to behind the set, where the same two people are talking. Producer: Okay, how can we stop this moron from continuing to rant? Secretary: I guess I have one idea… Now our shot switches back, yet again, to Rush at the news desk. Rush: In fact, KFC sucks. I hate to be so blatantly truthful, but KFC sucks major donkey wa- Just as Rush speaks, a bucket of KFC is slid out onto the news desk, where Rush looks at the bucket and then gulps. Rush: I…uhhh…I hate KFC. It’s my least favorite….food. Rush continues to look towards the bucket and finally mutters under his breath. Rush: Must…resist…urge….to…devour…delicious…chicken. The Voodoo Villain is now sweating profusely and trying to speak to the camera. Rush: KFC…is just so….disgusting and…mouth-wateringly refreshing. Its tender succulent breasts are ripe for the eating. He finally gives in and picks up the bucket of chicken, holding it gently to his chest and walks off camera holding it as the news goes to commercial. We see the producer and secretary once again. Producer: Who was that guy? Secretary: Some Voodoo follower. Let’s call it an act of terrorism, it’ll boost rating. The two walk off as the sound of ravenous devouring of chicken can be heard, and black fades in circularly, Looney Tunes-style, and opens up a small circle for a moment as the head of Jordan X Shabazz pokes out. Jordan: Y’all come back now, ya heard? Wow….let’s fade to black already…
