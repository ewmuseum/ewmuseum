A visit to Jeff Marx

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/01/02 Damn it! The first things we see, as the scene come into focus is a chair fly across the room we are in. The room is huge and has very expensive furniture all around it. There are leather sofas, chairs, and the leather sectional. They are all black, and all of them have either a coffee table or end table made out of marble next to them or in front of them. Off to the right of the room is a full bar. In the center of the room is against the back wall is a huge entertainment system. TV, DVD, and stereo are all there and are some of the biggest and nicest items out on the market. Then another item that is unidentified comes flying across the scene. The camera panes over in the direction of where the items have just came flying from. Standing there is Steve Johnson in a pair of black sweats and no shirt, with white socks. His face is red with absolute anger, and his body is trembling with the same type of anger. He stands there looking around the room for something else to throw, when a woman we have never seen before walks in to the room. She is a very hot woman with long brown hair, a perfect little as, and long tan legs. Here bright blue eyes are shinning in the middle of her shocked face. She stands there looking around the room. Women: Steve what is your problem? Have you completely lost it? Steve (his face turning a little less red): I am just pissed off about the match last night, and the other recent events this week Dawn. Can you believe that stupid jackass ref DQ me in my debut match up? I swear if they hadn’t fired him after the show last night, I would go down there right now and teach him a little respect. Dawn: Don’t spend a lot of time worrying about that now unemployed ref. For his ignorance he is now standing in the unemployment line. You have much more important things to think about this week. Remember you can make up for the crap from last night, this Sunday when you compete for and win the PCW Rising Star Championship. Steve: True I have a very important night coming up this weekend. But that is not the only thing I have on my mind that needs to be taken care this week before that fateful evening. Dawn there is no doubt in my mind that on Sunday I will get retribution by beating not only one man, but three men in one evening and grabbing my very first PCW gold. But I also have to pay a couple of visits to some places this week as well. Dawn: What places are so important that you need to waste your precious time visiting? Steve (Smirk across his face now): Well the first person I need to visit is my new friend Jeff Marx. Jeff and I need to have a little heart to heart chat. You want to go alone and talk to him too? Dawn (Smiling): Of course I do. You know I am always ready to support my man. She walks over to Steve and gives him a kiss. They smile at one another and stand close to one another, looking into each other’s eyes. Dawn: But first I will need to go change into something a little more presentable. Ok. Steve: Well that is perfect. I need to change also, so since we will be in the bedroom any way… They both give each other a look with hungry eyes. They walk toward the bedroom together as the scene fades away. : Scene Ends: The scene reopens to the inside of the PCW main offices. We are in the main lobby area, where there is a huge receptionist desk in the center of the room. There are some offices along each side of the lobby and a set of elevators and stairs to the higher floors. After a few moments Steve Johnson and the very hot Dawn walk through the front doors. Steve is wearing a pair of black Adidas pants, a plain white T-Shirt, and some white Adidas shoes. Dawn is wearing a pair of nice dress slacks and a white blouse. Her long brown hair is up in a ponytail. He blue eyes seem to dance with excitement. The two walk up to the receptionist desk, where the young woman is filing her nails. After a moment she looks up and her eyes light up. Women: Oh my gosh it’s Steve Johnson! Right here in front of me, at my workstation. You are my favorite wrestler. I have been following you for your entire career. You have no idea how glad I was to hear you signed with PCW. I have wanted to meet you because I have a huge crush on you! Steve just rolls his eyes at the young woman who seems to be annoying him. Dawn however has a very angry look on her face. She looks right at the woman and slams her hand down on the front desk. Dawn: Look you little sl#t! Steve is my man and if you continue to make those little remarks and stupid teenager faces I will beat your head right into this desk here. You follow what I am saying? Woman (with a look of fear and shock): Um… yes…. I am…. Really very… Steve: Enough of your stupid stuttering. We could care less how sorry you are. We can see that by looking at you. Just don’t let it ever happen again, and answer my questions. Women (Tears starting to swell up in her eyes): Yes Steve. What can I do for you today? Steve: That is more like it. I want to know if Jeff Marx is in today. And if he is, then I want to know where his office is. Can you handle that? Woman: Yes Steve, I can. Let me just look here on the computer. (She types something in on her keyboard) Yes it shows here that he did clock in today. His office is on the third floor. Now did you have an appointment with him? As the woman pulls out a book from under the front desk, Steve and Dawn look at each other. They both begin to get an evil grin. Dawn: Do you really think we made or need an appointment you little twit? We can wherever whenever we like. There is no need for us to have an appointment. Now just sit there, continue filing your ugly little nails, and shut your pathetic trap! Steve: See, I have all the appointment I need right here. Steve points at Dawn and the two smile. Then they begin walking toward the elevators. They get on the first one to come down and push the third floor button. They stand in silence as they ride the elevator to the third floor. After a few moments the elevator comes to a stop and the doors slide open. They step out onto the third floor, where there are lots of people hurrying around the floor with various projects. There are offices all along both sides of the floor. Steve and Dawn stand there a moment looking at one another, then turning and looking around the room and into the glass windows of the offices to find Jeff Marx. Finally they spot him in an office by himself about the third one down on the right. They begin walking toward the office, but about half way there they have a short pudgy little man try and talk to them. Pudgy Man: May I help…. Steve just shoves the little fat man aside causing him to fall down to the ground. A smirk comes across Steve’s face. Steve: No you can’t. The two continue on until they reach the office Jeff is in. They walk in and close the door behind them as Jeff looks up bewildered. Steve just walks over to where he is and grabs him by the throat forcing Jeff out of his chair and up against the wall. There is a great amount of fear in Jeff’s eyes. Steve: Hello Jeff, you remember me right? The guy you kept calling a… what was it… Ah yes, a jobber on Turmoil. Now Jeff you do not even have any idea how bad you hurt my feelings calling me such awful names. Why would you want to hurt my feelings? Jeff (Steve with an evil grin is right in Marx face): I…uh…am… so sorry. I really had no idea who you were at the time. I would never try and piss a great wrestler off. Especially not a big one, like your self. Please, do not hurt me! Steve (shaking his head): You misunderstand Jeff. I am not here to hurt you, only hello you. I have come here to give you a little advice on your less than quality announcing, so that you will be better informed and much more intelligent. Now first off… Steve drops Jeff who falls to the floor holding his throat. Steve walks back around to the other side of the desk and sits in the chair and props his feet up in Jeff’s desk. As Jeff finally stands up, Dawn walks over and stands next to Steve. Steve: Like I was about to say the first thing you need to learn is who to and who not to call a jobber. There is a very easy way to tell the difference Jeff. I am not a jobber. Never have been never will be. The fact that Ripper even got a win is because he got lucky enough to have a very stupid ref calling our match. As you could see afterwards, he quickly helped me kick the hell out of that ref. And you might wonder why he did. The answer is clear; he did not want me to kick the hell out of him afterwards for the travesty of me getting cheated in my debut. So the first lesson in Jeff…. I am not a jobber. Do you understand that? Jeff (shaking his head in understandment): Yes Steve I do. Again I am sorry. Steve: Now the second lesson is how to tell who is a jobber. The first part of that lesson is easy. Any one with the unlucky fact of having to face Steve Johnson is a jobber. So on Tuesday you were really half-correct. Ripper is a jobber and always will be in his PCW career. So come Sunday Mr. Marx, when you sit there at your announce position and call my Rising star Title match you should be able to tell the differance between me and the real “jobbers” in the match up right? Jeff(Still showing a great deal of fear): Yes I will. I will do my job much better on Sunday night. In fact I will be the first to praise the fact that you are the new Rising Star Champion. It will be a unforgettable night. Dawn: You are right about the fact that it will be one unforgettable night. Not only because Steve will make his Primetime Championship Wrestling Pay-Per-View debut, but because he will walk away with the first of what will be many titles. You are looking at the next Rising Star Champion and that is a absolute fact. Steve: Well now that we have this little misunderstanding taken care of, we will let you get back to your busy schedule. Steve gets up and him and Dawn walk toward the door. Marx sits down in his chair breathing a sigh of relief. As they are walking out the door Steve stops and turns back toward Marx. Steve: Oh and one more thing Jeff. Jeff: Yes Steve. Steve: I really hope I do not have to make another visit into your office after a match. The next time you will not find me nearly as nice and generous as you have this time. Are we understood? Jeff(clearly a limp in his throat): Crystal clear. I have learned my lesson and you have made me a changed man. Steve(With a smirk on his face again):Good. I would really hate to have to give you an advanced lesson in your job. It would not be nearly as easy on you; and would be much more time consuming. Have a great afternoon. Steve then walks out of the office and shuts the door behind him. Dawn and Steve begin to walk back toward the elevators and again the little fat man tries to talk to them. Man: I am sorry... Steve(As he pushes the man away again and down onto his fat ass): I am not interested in how sorry you are. Steve and Dawn continue to the elevators. They push the down button and when the elevator arrives they get on. As the doors begin to close we see them both with that grin on their faces. Just as the doors close completely the scene fades out to black.
