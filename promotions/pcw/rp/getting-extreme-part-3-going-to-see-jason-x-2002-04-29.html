Getting Extreme - Part 3 - Going to see JASON X

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 29 [The scene opens on the large structure of a local movie megaplex. It's Saturday night and the lines to purchase tickets are immense. It's date night, new movies have opened, and people are in need of some entertainment. This megaplex, in Los Angeles, is one of the largest in the state of California. So, getting a chance to see a movie here is an absolute priveledge. Many big movie stars have been seen at this movie plex to catch flicks that they aren't in, isn't that so modest of them? Though on this night many men look to take their dates to the newly opening Jason X. That's right, Jason X. The hockey mask slasher is back for the tenth installment of killing horny teenagers because he can, and there is nothing they can do about it. Many young men waiting in line hope to frighten their girlfriends into their arms, then hopefully get a little something something after the movie is over] [Inside the lobby, it looks as if there is someone extremely famous there with the crowd of people all gathered around. It's almost like a rock concert is about to take place. Three men standout in this large crowd and those men are: 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, and their newly acquired healing friend, Manfred. Tommy is dressed in his usual Hawaiian button down dress shirt, blue and yellow striped shorts, and a pair of black sandals. Harry is also decked out in his usual attire, which is a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. Manfred stands with perfect posture in his perfectly ironed tuxedo. His black hair slicked back, it looks like he put shoe polish on the top of his head, and his black, bushy mustache twitches from side to side every now and then. Manfred's face, unfortunately, looks very fearful] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Come on, dude! It's a Jason movie. These movies are totally bodacious in a far out way! [Manfred begins shaking his head, obviously not a big fan of horror movies. He is giggling to himself, finding humor in Manfred's fear of Jason] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I can't believe he's afraid of Jason, man. TTT: Shut up, dude. He can be afraid of what he wants to be. Remember he did climb that most gnarly canyon yesterday, and you didn't. Harry: Neither did you, man. TTT: I did after you took Manfred out for ice cream. Harry: No way, man. We were only gone for five minutes. TTT: Fine, don't believe me. Harry: Ok, man. [Manfred is still shaking his head, really not wanting to see this slasher movie that has a very large cult following. Tommy tries his best to get Manfred to go inside as Harry walks away from them, maybe to go to the bathroom] TTT: Manfred, these movies aren't all that scary. I know it may seem bogus that people are being hacked, but that's what makes the movie totally gnarly. Jason is sort of like a most righteous anti-hero by slashing teenagers who do most heinous activities at summer camp. It teaches many lessons in life and tells teenagers not to have premarital sex, not to do drugs, and to not let the little weird kid drown in the lake. [Manfred looks at Tommy with confusion and then takes his index finger and begins sliding it in and out of a little O he made with his other hand. A smile is across Manfred's face showing that he enjoys sex] TTT: I agree, dude. The premarital sex thing is a most bogus lesson, but we're not teenagers anymore! [Manfred nods, seemingly settled down now. Hopefully he'll go in to see this tenth gnarly Jason movie. Tommy has waited so long for this next installment to be released, and finally it's here. It's like it was some sort of dream, but it's all reality and his favorite bodacious hockey masked compadre is back to kick futuristic butt!] TTT: Just go in there, give it a try, dude. If you get too scared then you can just leave at anytime. [Manfred nods very sheepishly, but it's a nod, and that's a good sign. Tommy then starts drawing his mind toward that Extreme title match in the Cincinatti Airport. Talk about a total slashfest that is going to be, just like the Jason movies are] TTT: Manfred, do you think that me and Harry have an honest shot in this Extreme title match on Tuesday? [Manfred shrugs a little bit, unsure whether or not they have a shot] TTT: Yeah, I know what you mean, dude. I'm usually a most confident compadre when it comes to my matches, but this one seems so unsure. There are a lot of very hardcore dudes that can make this match completely bogus for Harry and I. The Rush, Jay Sylo, The Chef...all these dudes are really tough and are most proven hombres in the Extreme title division. [Manfred nods. He then taps Tommy on the chest and makes like he is making big muscles] TTT: Are you saying that I'm totally ripped? [Manfred shakes his head. He then repeats the motion and then points to his stomach] TTT: Are you saying that I have a strong stomach? Sometimes the Jason flicks get a little gory, but nothing I can't handle. [Manfred once again does the motion, pointing at his stomach, and acting like he's throwing it on the ground] TTT: Um, are you going to kill me, dude? [Manfred shakes his head once again, getting very frustrated. He repeats everything a few more times trying to get through to Tommy, but maybe he's just beating a dead horse with this. At least Manfred knows what he means and that's what really counts] TTT: OH! Your saying I have strong insides. [Manfred nods violently, happy that Tommy finally got it] TTT: That's very gnarly of you to say, dude. Now if you sit through this Jason X movie, you will show just how extreme you are! You've gone sky diving, climbed a most gnarly canyon, and watching a Jason movie will top the sundae. Just like me and Harry taking out all those bogus dudes in the Cincinatti Airport, and then one of us becoming PCW Extreme champion. That will prove once and for all how extreme the Cowabunga Connection truly is. [Suddenly a man wearing a hockey mask jumps out of nowhere right in front of Manfred. The Lurch-like mute, Manfred, drops to the floor and curls up in a ball. His face is contorted in utter fear and fright. Tommy pulls the mask off and reveals the culprit to be Harry] TTT: That was totally bogus, dude! Harry: That was far out, man. Did you see how fast he hit the floor? TTT: I had him talked into going in with us, and now you just ruined it, dude! Harry: Sorry, man. [Manfred slowly rises to his feet, with a sheepish look on his face as he doesn't take his eyes off of Harry. Manfred now hides behind Tommy, just like a little child hiding behind it's mother for protection from the bad man. Harry now wears a God-like smile, knowing the power he holds over the much larger Manfred. Harry throws the mask back over his face, Manfred then ducks behind Tommy with is fear increasing. Harry pulls the mask up and Manfred slowly comes out from hiding, then Harry pulls it back down and Manfred hides in fear] Harry: This is far out, man!! TTT: Your being BOGUS dude! Ever since I've been sick your being bogus. Manfred healed me and your trying to scare the crap out of him! What's wrong with you, compadre? Harry: I'm just having fun with him, man. It's not like he's going to die because I'm wearing a hockey mask. TTT: Need I remind you of when we were little bodacious dudes and you used to pee your pants everytime you saw Jason? [Harry's face drops from the powerful smile to a look of fear and anger] Harry: No way, man. I never peed myself. TTT: When the fourth movie came out, it was the first Jason movie you saw, and you peed like you had bladder problems. It was a most radical sight. I never saw so much pee. Harry: I did have bladder problems, man. TTT: Everytime Jason came on the screen? Harry: It was that Jason Bladder Infection, man. TTT: I never heard of that, dude. Harry: It's rare, man. TTT: Yeah, only in bogus dudes who listen to Twisted Sister. [Harry's face lights up] Harry: How'd you know that?! TTT: A guess. [Manfred seems a bit more brave, now knowing that Harry used to pee himself. Manfred has a smile on his face feeling that he can conquer this fear that he has. After all, he is a grown man now and should be able to handle something like this] Harry: My Jason Bladder Infection isn't funny, man...fred. [Manfred nods with a large smile on his face. Harry then pulls the mask down and Manfred jumps behind Tommy] Harry: Loser, man. [Tommy rolls his eyes] TTT: Will you two stop this bogus nonsense. Lets go in and watch the movie. Editorial: Due to The Tubular One not giving away the spoilers to Jason X, I will not tell the fine readers of this RP the plot line, though it's not really any different from the other movies. Hotter chicks, better effects, and it's IN THE FUTURE!!! Wait...I just did give it away...BAH~! [As the movie goes on many people are seen leaving the theater that plays Jason X. The movie had only started half an hour ago and about half the theater has already left. The movie couldn't be that bad, could it? After an hour only a fourth of the people remain to catch the climax. Unfortunately, the only three people that remain our the main characters of this promo: TTT, Harry, and Manfred. They all come piling out with smiles on their faces, except Harry] TTT: That movie was totally worth the wait, dudes. It was the best in the series. Many bodacious babes getting slaughtered and that new upgrade he got was BODACIOUS! [Manfred nods wildly, overcoming the fear of Jason, and finding a new love of slasher films] TTT: How about you Har-- [Tommy looks down and sees a large spot of liquid in Harry's crotch. Harry looks none too pleased as a small puddle of pee begins forming underneath him. He looks almost like a small child who overflowed his diaper] TTT: I guess that Jason Bladder Infection is still with you, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. It comes back every once in a while. [Manfred pulls the hockey mask over his face and walks toward Harry like some sort of zombie. The large spot suddenly begins to grow in size. Tommy laughs wildly as Harry holds his head low in complete shame] Harry: Jason isn't far out, not far out at all, man. [The scene then fades to black]
