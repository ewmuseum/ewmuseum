Growing Up Sucks; Part Six. Trying is the First Step to Failure.

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 9th, 2003. Screw the little morality booster that I have been putting here for the past five promos. You can do without it for now…and if you can’t, then you need a fucking clinic, my friend. “God damn it!” The scene opens as the voice of Jackson Rust rings throughout the hospital cafeteria, as that is where the scene is opening to…otherwise I wouldn’t have put the ringing effect. The entourage sits on either side of the table as Rust looks up in anger. Jackson Rust: He was almost dead… Al: How did that scalpel to the eye not kill him? Jackson Rust: I don’t know! It must have been solidified in some previous surgery. Steve: S-so…c-clo-close. Jackson Rust: I know! I’m going to have to kill him one way or another. Kimberly: Why are you killing him again? Jackson Rust: He stole my name… Rust snarls. Kimberly: He’s like forty-four…he’s had it longer than you. Jackson Rust: Not Jacko, not the Jacko name! Narrator: Yes, I told him that. He believes me now. Jackson Rust: Plus I just hate him. Al: Who doesn’t? Narrator: Judging by your bedroom…you. Jackson backs away slowly, falling from the table’s bench, and to the ground. Jackson Rust: Dude…you have MJ merchandise or something? Al: Yes…err…Michael Jordan, yep. Jackson Rust: C’mon, we already know white men can’t jump, so you have nothing of Jordan’s… Narrator: He has a Michael Jackson plush toy. Steve: *vomits* Narrator: And he sleeps with it. Kimberly: *vomits* Narrator: And dresses it up. Jackson Rust: *vomits* Narrator: And plays kissy games with it…*vomits* Al: I do not! And now we can’t leave on the count of vomit is everywhere! Jackson Rust: Your fault, you God damned psycho. Kimberly: *recovers* What the hell were you thinking…or lack thereof? Al: I…I don’t know! Leave me alone! Narrator: Pussy. Al turns around on the bench, and runs forward, not before slipping on vomit and landing square on his back…in pain, of course…in pain. Al: Ouch…man… Jackson Rust: I tried doing something like that to kill off Michael Jackson. Narrator: Shall I show the reader? Jackson Rust: Yes. *REVERT-TO-POTENTIAL-DEATH* Michael Jackson walks along the hallway, his face bandaged up from the stabbing, and surgery that Jackson Rust had given him. Jackson (Rust) walks toward the recovering pop singer. Michael Jackson: Oh, hey Doctor. Rust walks by and pretends to not see him, before ‘accidentally’ flailing his arm out, and pushing Michael down the ever-convenient stair well. Michael Jackson: Ah! He tumbles head over heels down the stairs, and lands with a thud on the bottom. Rust smirks, thinking of a job well done. Michael Jackson: I’m okay! A little banged up, but okay! Jackson Rust: FUCK! *REVERT-TO-NEXT* Michael Jackson is standing in front of a soda machine, dropping coins in to eventually reach a dollar to get a drink. Al and Jackson stand across the room, Rust handing Al a crowbar, before pointing and whispering something to him. Jackson Rust: Attack. Al: Okay. Michael is still stood as Al silently tears across the room, wielding the crowbar. He swings it, but Michael leans over to get his drink, causing Al to run headlong into the machine, the crowbar landing on his chest. Michael Jackson: Thanks Mister, but I got the drink…thanks for the help, though. Al: No…problem… *REVERT-TO-NEXT* Jackson Rust: Let’s not… Narrator: Okay. *DOESN’T-REVERT-TO-NEXT* Jackson Rust: Good. Kimberly: So when are you gonna kill him? Jackson Rust: Next promo I wager. Narrator: Classic three parter? Jackson Rust: Yep. Narrator: Same series? Jackson Rust: Hell yeah, I gotta make this bitch last through March Madness. Narrator: Wow. Jackson Rust: This is a sad promo…it’s gonna be the shortest of the series… Kimberly: Yeah? Jackson Rust: Yeah, handler of me has to go once more. [name withheld]: Sorry… Jackson Rust: No biggie…just post some more before you go to New York. [name withheld]: Will do. Narrator: Yes! The scene ends, but only because I have to go…I have not much time left, and I have to get ready for school, and a trip to New York, and yaddi, yaddi, yaddi…you get the point. The scene ends, just to let you know.
