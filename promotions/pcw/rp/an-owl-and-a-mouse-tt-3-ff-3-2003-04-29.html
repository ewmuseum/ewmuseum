An Owl and a Mouse... TT#3 FF#3

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/4/03 “I’m glad you’re here, Mr. Rage.” The nurse said, attempting to match the pace set by the mammoth man, as they walked along the corridor of the hospital. Blind Rage glanced at her, obviously, scaring the young blonde nurse a little. “Why is there something wrong with him? I thought you said that he could leave today?” He replied. “No.” She simply stated, opening the ward door. “He’s simply getting on our tits!” Blind Rage glanced into the ward room. For some reason they’d put him in the old person’s ward, Blind Rage couldn’t understand why, nor did he care. He hated hospitals, reminded him of death, despite being next to invulnerable. It was the smell, he told himself, it was the smell. As soon as he entered the room, he understood what the nurse meant; a group of nurses surrounded the bed, attempting to gain a blood sample without getting their asses squeezed. Blind Rage could understand why though, all of the female nurses looked like a group of playboy playmates. Blind Rage let out a chuckled, causing the nurse to glare at him with an angry expression. They both entered the ward and made their way past all of the old people, finally reaching the end of the ward. Blind Rage tried to ignore the old man in the opposite bed from Warrior. He was sat in a brown, leather chair with absolutely no pyjama bottoms on, with a cheeky grin upon his face. “Oh and he refused to take his mask off.” the nurse added, as they reached his bed. “Sounds about right.” Blind Rage replied. “Claimed that he wanted to keep his secret identity…” said the nurse. “Not that any of us cared.” “Still…” Blind Rage began, barging past the line of nurses. “…better that you don’t know.” “Er…my pants have fell off again.” The old man without any trousers declared. All of the nurses sighed in unison and Warrior developed a smile. “Smooth move Mr. Tucker!” “Warrior!” Blind Rage bellowed. “DO NOT encourage public nudity…especially with the elderly!” *** “I guess you had the same idea as me, huh?” Warrior asked, as they finally breached the perimeter of the hospital. Of course Warrior landed Blind Rage with the hospital bill, he said that it was his ‘pittance for not saving him sooner…and accusing him of bestiality.’ “Yeah.” He said. “I thought you’d want to be involved in this so we waited.” Warrior smiled. “Thank you! I have a bone to pick with whoever is behind this.” The van journey was short and sweet. Badger Man and the Handbag Warrior briefly took the piss about finding him with the dog and Warrior informed them all of what he got up to with the nurses, but when they arrived at the fire station, it was back to business. Getting to talk with the Chief proved to be harder than any of them had ever conceived. He refused to talk anybody who were wearing tights. Blind Rage forced the issue and soon the Chief reluctantly agreed to talk with them. “Look,” The Chief roared angrily. “I don’t have time to sit around here playing silly buggers with guys in dressed in Lycra.” Blind Rage stood up from the remarkably comfortable seat and leant upon the table in front of him, both knuckles resting on the pine exterior. His muscles speaking more words than he ever could. “People’s lives are at stake here…chief!” He said. “When you were called away on that bogus fire at the harbour, we were the ones who saved the family from a fire in their home. I think we’ve EARNED your time.” Obviously a little intimidated by the massive exterior before him. “Wha…wha…what hoax fire? We went down to the harbour and…and…wait a minute…” “We need to know what you remember at the harbour!” Warrior exclaimed. “I don’t remember what happened, but it never occurred to me that we were that long…I’m confused.” The Chief mumbled. “Can you tell us anything…even the most unlikely detail…” Blind Rage pleaded. “Yeah!” he declared. “I do remember something. There was this guy, kinda plump, dressed in an owl uniform. And there was this midget with a cigar…wearing a mouse costume. They…they…I don’t remember anymore.” “Don’t worry, you’ve been very helpful. We’ll get these bastards!” Blind Rage declared
