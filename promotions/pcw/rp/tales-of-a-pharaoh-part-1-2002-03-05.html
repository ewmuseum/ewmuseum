Tales of a Pharaoh: [Part 1]

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/3/02 Tales of a Pharaoh: [Part 1] Intro: After a disappointing night at PCW’s March Pay Per View entitled ‘March Madness’ for the three Divinity Members in Jay Sylo, The Pharaoh and Akiyama the three Godly beings find themselves in a new predicament in the form of a new and terrible foe in the shape of up-and-coming PCW superstar Legend inc. and his new tag team Partner…Frisco! Legend inc. and his newfound ally, PCW hall of fame member, Frisco are now set to face off against The Pharaoh and Akiyama with the tag team titles on the line this coming Sunday in Dallas, Texas…Will The divinity keep hold of their titles or will they fall at the feet of this new and greatly feared tag team? Only time will truly tell us, but for now we join our hero’s somewhere in Texas at a small, discrete and fairly unhygienic Café… Location: Unknown locality, TexasTime: 3:35 PM “This is a terrible piece of news indeed!” I said as I looked into the deep dark eyes of Akira Akiyama. The people eating at the Café of which we had come to feast at all looked up from their meals as I bellowed out my frustrated words. Akiyama looked on with a stern face. His eyes glimmered in the bright sunshine from outside but a sense of displease was about him. “That it is… Sunday Sabotage will result in being our biggest test yet…” he sighed. I placed my face in my hands and breathed a deep sigh of distress. Could it be possible that the reign of the current PCW tag team champions was to come to an end this Sunday on PCW Sunday Sabotage? With opponents of the calibre that we had been given it would seem so, but still there was a certain amount of hope left, not much, as it would seem, but some nonetheless. I looked up at Akiyama’s stern face and, with a look of dismay, uttered the words “What do you propose we do?” A silly question I know but one that needed to be asked. I already knew Akira’s answer, and without further a due he murmured the words that I believed he would… “Win…” he paused… “What else can we do?” I sighed again in disbelief. How could this be happening…March Madness…it had gone down as being possibly the worst night in the Immortal lives of the members of the Divinity… First Jay Sylo looses his chance to become the PCW rising star Champion by getting himself disqualified from his match and then Akiyama is abruptly eliminated from the world title battle royal. Things got worse… As Legend inc. eliminated Akira with his own momentum a face from the distant past showed up to ruin our night. PCW hall of fame member Frisco revealed himself to be the new tag team partner of Legend inc. and without wasting time at all made his mark on myself by taking a beating to me. I couldn’t believe it when I saw his stern minded face grinning at mine. He was a man possessed and possessed with only one single thing…to take the only title that he had yet to capture in PCW…the tag team…and who at the moment held one half of the two prestigious belts? That would be Titu Xansiv…The Pharaoh…ME! Akiyama looked down at the golden brown bun filled with a single slab of pork-type meat named a burger on his plate in front of him and once again sighed a long drifting breath…“Tidings like these put me off my food…” I watched as he picked up the burger and unwillingly took a bite… “But I need my energy to be of a high level if we are to successfully defend these titles!” Akiyama smiled and pointed to the shining tag team titles sat next to him on the red leather seat of which he sat upon. I looked down at my burger. It wasn’t exactly what I’d call a pretty sight but Akira was right. If we were to defend our titles then our energy was required to be high. I, like my friend and fellow God, took a displeasing bite of the Mortal snack and cringed. “I must say; this isn’t the nicest thing I’ve eaten all day, in fact this has to be the worst!” Akira laughed as I threw the bun-wrapped burger down onto my plate with such force that the three parts of the snack separated, small shards of dry bread darted about the man-made Marble deigned table. “Where’s Jay?” I asked. “He’s ordering our drinks.” Replied Akiyama. “I see”…a dull silence fell over our part of the fairly spacious café…finally I broke the metaphorical ice… “You know there is still hope…” Akiyama looked up from his burger… “There is?” he asked sarcastically. “Of course!” I started “Think about it! We are Gods and Frisco and Legend Inc. simply aren’t…in all reality they are nothing compared to our Godly prowess!” Akiyama grinned, “That I suppose is true…but on the other hand they are two finely perfected wrestler,” I cut him off in mid sentence… “But they are no where near as good as The Divinity!”… Arrogantly I smiled, pleased with my comeback. The confidence that had seemed to leak from me after March Madness was coming back; I could feel it! Some PCW has-been and a PCW jobber weren’t going to get this God down! As we chatted Jay Sylo came back across the unhygienic floor towards our table. His ice blue hair glimmered in the sun and at one point actually reflected the sun into one of the customer’s eyes! “AH!” yelled the man in his mid thirties, wearing all leather and a pair of black bikers boots. “What in gods name was that?!” Abruptly I stood up, fierce and bright, and in all my might and anger I yelled… “How dare you take the name of one God in vain! There are many countless Gods that you obviously know nothing of…if your going to foul mouth a god then at least get it right; there are many, not just one!” The man, seeing me, leapt up from his seat… “And who the hell are you?!” Behind me I could here Akiyama and Jay sigh…they knew what was coming next. “Here we go again,” whispered Akiyama to Sylo who smiled in response. They obviously didn’t know I’d heard them but I did, being a god must do something to ones hearing! Jay sat himself down next to the neo void god and the two watched as I argued with the leather-clad man. He was a fair sized mortal but not as tall as myself and by far not as well toned…although he was very widely and strongly built… “I, good sir, am ‘The Pharaoh’ Titu Xansiv, YOUR PCW world tag team champion, former ruler of Ancient Egypt and the bearer of the two books; the book of the dead and the book of life…I think the question now is, who in the Duat are you?!” The man was clearly taken back. Or so I thought. He looked me up and down, as though he’d never seen a tall, unusually royal-looking man wearing a set of Golden ceremonial bangles, a regal blue and gold Egyptian crown and a long white silk loincloth before. Behind him was a small party of men, dressed in the same fashion as him, sat around the table of which he had just been sat at. He turned to them and laughed. “Looks like we’ve got a good ol’ fashioned nutcase right here boys!” I was clearly outraged, why I wasn’t of the sort and I had more important problems at hand than to be insulted by such a minor Mortal and let him get away with it. With a look of fury I gazed upon the man and after giving him a look of the same qualities he gave me I gave my opinion of him to my “Boys”. And so it was that I turned to Akira and our newfound friend and fellow PCW wrestler Jay Sylo. “Hey “Boys” it looks like we found ourselves an idiotic mortal man who believes he can insult me with feeble words!” I bellowed in a mocking tone. I turned back to the man just in time to see him lunge at me; I ducked and pushed him out of the way using his own momentum to carry him forward. He stopped himself from moving any further by putting his hands up and pushing of the wall that he was headed for. To which I laughed cockily. “Come now, surly you didn’t think that you could take me out that easily?” I arrogantly shouted to him. I now turned to his friends. “And you…do you dare back up your friend? Come now, I welcome all of you to this confrontation!” Without a moment too soon the men sat around the table, obviously bikers of some sorts, pushed their chairs away from under them and leapt forward. One met a swift end as I kicked him in the gut and followed up with a thunderous boot to his chin. He fell swiftly and heavily to the black and white tiled floor of the Café. At this moment the others backed off as if afraid to fight me. “What’s the matter?” I asked sarcastically. I wanted them to fight me, all of them! It would be good training for my match this coming Sunday on Sabotage. Every one of these Mortals were built like tanks, if I could take the five of them that were left then nothing could stop me! Nothing! Legend inc. and Frisco were good but surly, surly they couldn’t even take on these five bikers as a team…and I was doing fine so far on my lonesome. All at once the five of the leather-clad men jumped me. All five men, at least 250 pounds collided with my Godly body hitting rapid-fire shots to my kidneys and ribs. At that moment I suddenly felt the weight of the men drop, it would seem that at least three of them had given up but that seemed odd. However it was not that at all. I looked up from my place on the ground where the five had taken advantage of me to see Akiyama holding one by the throat high above the ground and another two being carried away to an unmarked destination by Jay Sylo! The two that were left with me continued to kick me whilst I was down on the cold ground until I managed to tumble them both to the floor with kicks to the backs of their knees. I swiftly leapt up and dragged one of them with me. He was disposed of with a destructive punch to the jaw and a hasty kick to the same area. He fell and tumbled to his knees and then I watched in delight as he simply collapsed on the floor, a trickle of blood flowed from his nose. Now I had one left to dispose of. It was the very same man that I had started off with. “You again!” I yelled. “The very same, boy!” he replied before adding “And its you who’s going to be left in a mess this time…” I smiled haughtily. “I doubt it.” Without a moment too soon I lunged at him, I was ready for this guy, he was nothing compared to me, but to my astonishment he scooped me up and ran me into the sharp corner of a wall. My bare-back cracked in pain, as did my vocal cords. I was quickly dropped to the ground with a fair amount of force. Once again my back felt pain and my vocal cords channelled it into a yelp that drained slowly from my mouth. “Look who’s laughing now!” he grunted. I writhed in pain and placed my Godly hand on my back. A lump had already begun to take form. I struggled to my feet and he met me with a forearm to the back, the very same spot that he had smashed on the wall. “AH!” I yelled in utter pain. He laughed sadistically in response but his laughter wouldn’t last long, I was sure of it… Outside the café I heard fighting taking place…Akiyama and Jay had obviously taken their fight outside and were making good work of the other bikers…I hoped. Inside the confines of the small restaurant a different story was taking place, but not for long; he may have been in control now but I had a plan up my figurative sleeve. The man went to pick me up but I sliced his leg from under him causing him to plummet to the floor, he yelled out in distress as he landed on his vast back. I dragged him back up to his feet and sarcastically dusted him off. “Now is the time that you meet your end, my friend!” I said before roundhouse kicking him in the chin…a trail of blood flailed in the air as his mouth and nose burst open allowing the red, iron filled substance to pour like a tap. I smiled as he heavily hit the black and white tiled floor with a ‘thud’. I grinned in delight of my work and for a moment looked about the place; now wrecked from the destructive forces gathered in the small sanctuary. Tables were toppled and drinks were spilled; I knew I hadn’t done any of this; it was clearly the work of Akiyama and Jay. The customers that had chosen not to take part in the fight had hidden under their tables cowering from the brawl. “I must find Akiyama and Sylo!” I said before turning towards the door that led outside…but I was met instead with a shining metal double barrel shotgun… “You ain’t goin’ nowhere!” said the voice behind the trigger, one that recognized as that of a husky woman. I gulped and took a step back “Come now, can we not talk about this, fair lady?” The woman behind the trigger, who I now made out to be the owner of the cafe was however far from fair, her chubby body and ugly face watched me with slanted eyes and she grinned yet frowned at the same time, a very unusual expression I know but one that I feared. She had character that was for sure! “I don’t think so, buddy” she replied grinning… To Be Continued...
