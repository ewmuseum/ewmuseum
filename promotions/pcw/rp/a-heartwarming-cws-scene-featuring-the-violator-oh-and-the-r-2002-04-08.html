A Heartwarming CWS Scene, Featuring The Violator. Oh, And The Return Of A PCW Champion!

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-8-02 **Theme Music: 'Television' by Bad Religion and Rancid** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, For a tough guy like me, I'm not supposed to be scared. But Sunday night, I was scared twice. The first time was for myself. It was when I felt something give in my knee, and I felt sure I couldn't wrestle again. My knee had blown out again. The second time was when I saw that asshole Nelson and his 'Ultimate Authority' run roughshod over PCW at the end of the night, with that Judas Healey. I was scared for PCW fans, and PCW employees. What makes a real man, Jen? A man who can be scared...but still do something about it. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [The scene comes up on the inside of Jason's CWS warehouse on Monday afternoon. Everyone sits around, looking about nervously. From the usually chipper Sandra, whho nibbles absently at a lock of blonde hair, to the cynical Nick North, who just looks generally warm and uncomfortable. As usual, 'Emo' Eddie Russo is on the verge of tears. And in one corner of the ring, the giant Violator sits, strangely enough, with Jamie Hunter, the youngest CWS student.] Jamie: Hey, Violator...I'm worried about Jason. [Jamie frowns a little, and so does the Violator.] Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!! [His yell turns everyone's head in his direction, as he looks around, confused. The CWS students go back to whatever else they were doing, no longer paying attention to the dark gimmicked behemoth.] Jamie: Could you keep it down just a little, Violator? [Violator puffs up his chest to bellow something else, but stops. He stares at Jamie, with a look of a kind of wonder shining through his big, dumb eyes.] Violator: I am the Violator. [Jamie smiles.] Jamie: Much better. [Violator also smiles, and Jamie pats the big man on the knee.] Jamie: Are you worried about Jason too? [Violator looks confused.] Jamie: Jason? You know, the guy who runs this school? Wrestler? Extreme Title? [Violator's eyes light up, finally understanding. He nods sadly, and affirmatively.] Violator: Prepare for total violation... [Jamie nods in agreement. She looks around for a moment.] Jamie: So...where have you been staying all this time? It's been a couple of weeks since you've come here. [Violator points to a corner of the warehouse, where a coal-marked blanket resides.] Violator: I am the Violator. [Jamie looks at the blanket with a mix of shock and dismay.] Jamie: You're kidding! That absolutely will NOT do! I'll talk to Jason. [Violator gives a grimy, dumb look of gratitude to the sixteen year-old Aimz wannabe.] Violator: Prepare for total violation! [Jamie gets to her feet, and mutters something to herself.] Jamie: Jeez, usually I LIKE it when guys say that. With him, it's just kinda creepy. [The wooden door of the CWS bangs open, and slightly limping, enters Jason Core. All of the CWS students hop out of the ring and rush to their teacher's side. Jason's wearing a large brace on his right knee, and looks a bit beat up, but wears his track pants and t-shirt proudly, and betrays no disappointment.] Mark: Are you alright- Sandra: We were so worried- Eddie: *sniff* Pegleg: Arr~! Jacques: Sacre- Violator: I am the Violator? [Jason waves them off, and they clear him a little breathing space.] Jason: I'm fine, I'm fine. [He flexes the knee a little bit, as if to prove it.] Jason: It's not a ligament tear, it's just some minor tendonitis. It'll be fine. [The CWS students breathe a collective sigh of relief.] Jason: That's part of the good news, and I have some more. I'm late because I met a friend of mine down at Dr. Harris's clinic. He's doing some rehab from a serious injury he sustained recently. [Jason moves out of the doorway, and a not altogether unfamiliar face to PCW steps through the door.] Kyle: Frickin'-A, Jase...is this all you can afford? [Kyle Lamen, last seen in PCW wrestling as 'Kyle Velocity' steps through the door, wearing his traditional t-shirt/jeans street clothes, his spiky hair partially dyed blonde, and wearing a conspicuous neck brace. The collective CWS gasps at the sight of a REAL wrestler and celebrity walking in. Eddie and Chris freak out a little more than anyone else, as Eddie bursts into tears of joy, and Chris starts to spazz out.] Chris: Oh my god, you're like...totally my frickin' hero, man! [Kyle's a little embarassed.] Kyle: Frickin'-A~? Chris: Frickin'-A~! [Kyle nods.] Kyle: I like this guy, Jase. [Time for Emo to run up and talk to Kyle.] Eddie: *sniff* Oh my God, Kyle...we're *sniff* so lucky to have *sniff* you here... [Jamie pulls Eddie out of the way violently, and puts a hand on her hip, and a seductive look on her face.] Jamie: Ever f*ck a sixteen year-old virgin? It's legal here. [Kyle would jerk his neck back in surprise, except for the brace. Instead, he just almost topples over in shock.] Kyle: Is she for real? [Jason shakes his head, and interposes himself between Jamie and Kyle.] Jason: Whoa there, little lady. I hate to tell you this, but you're out. [Her eyes get big.] Jamie: OUT!? [Jason nods.] Jason: Yep, I can't afford to train someone as young as you. I finally found a replacement, to keep the school going, so you're out. I'm sorry. [A highish pitched Spanish accent cuts through the air.] Voice: Ay de mi! Am I late, muchachos? [A masked Mexican midget enters through the door, flamboyantly dressed in lucha wrestling gear.] Jason: Hombre, these are the students. Hombre: Hola! Yo soy El Hombre Pequeno Del Fantastico! [He bows with a flourish.] Jamie: You are f*cking kidding me. TELL ME you are f*cking kidding me. [Jason looks a little sad, but resolute.] Jason: I'm sorry, Jamie. [A dark mood crosses the faces of all of the CWS students, the looks and people's faces are uniformly sad...] Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!! [The hulking dark gimmicked brute pushes his way up to Jason.] Violator: PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!!! [The monstrous form of the Violator goes toe to toe with the Headmaster of the CWS, with neither man backing down.] Mark: I wish I knew what he's saying... [Jamie pipes up.] Jamie: He's saying that he doesn't want me to leave. He likes me. [Violator nods affirmatively.] Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!! [Jason is shocked.] Jason: Wait a minute, you can actually understand what he's saying? [Both Violator and Jamie nod.] Jamie: And don't you have a match with Tim Walden coming up? If ANYONE knows how to beat Tim Walden, it's the Violator. [Violator nods ferociously, as Nick pipes in from the back.] Nick: And he's just like Jay Sylo...a big dumb, animal! [Violator looks hurt. Jamie walks over to the 250+ lb. Nick, and simply hiptosses him to the floor in rage.] Mark: Whoa!! [Jamie walks back over to Violator, Jason, and Kyle.] Jamie: I'm an asset here, and I THINK you want to keep me around. [Again, the Violator nods, as Jason thinks, and chews at a fingernail.] Jason: Alright, you're in. [A cheer comes up from the CWS, as Jamie runs up and hugs Jason. The CWS now is thirteen strong, and it seems a lucky number...despite what happened at Spring Fling...]
