Date: Today, but fuck, this is a repost because I was dumb enough to leave off anything of importance. The scene opens up with the (God knows why) familiar face of Australian superstar and advocate of all things crocodilian, Steve Irwin. Steve Irwin: G'day, mates! Today on Crocodile 'Untah, we have a real su'prise for you. In me 'hand, I have a very rare, an' dangerous, snake. You see this? This is the Wangschlongcok snake, a native to Th' Outback here, with a venomous bite. One bite from this sucker can fell a chargin' rhino in its track and...CRICKEY!!! The bugger bit me! Uhhhhhhhhhhhhh............ Steve Irwin falls flat on his face in the dirt, and the camera backs out to show Jackson and Steve sat on the couch in Jackson Rust’s housing. Jackson Rust: You know, I love these out-take shows. How about you? Steve: Th-th-they r-r-r-r-r-rock! Jackson Rust: Oh, what next? The unseen footage of Cathy Lee Gifford's facelift un-lifting on TV? I hope Regis had a bucket to catch that in... Kimberly: There you are! A reporter for http://pcw.ewmuseum.com has been searching the entire arena for you, trying to get all the relevant sound bites. Jackson Rust: And he didn't think to check my house? Where's he been looking, Wisconsin? Kimberly: No, he's been in all the relevant places; Starbucks, Taco Bell, an AT&T phone box... Jackson Rust: Damn, you could’ve told me we're having to be almost subtle when it comes to corporate shilling. I guess we'd better tell Al to get that Bed; Bathroom & Beyond robe off, then. Al: But this isn't a robe... J ackson Rust: What, did you borrow a shirt from Big Fat Bubba from the road crew? Al: Well, uh, yes... Jackson Rust: Well that clears that one up. My house isn't situated next to a rendering plant; we're just suffering the emissions from Big Fat Bubba's perma-stained armpits. Currently sagging around Al's midriff. Al: Oh my GOD! The robe/shirt flies into shot at the far left. Jackson Rust: Did someone forget to give Gentle Ben his electrolysis this morning? I mean, what do you think, Kim? Kimberly: I've never seen that much hair on a person before. Shouldn't you moult during the summer? Al: Leave my manly bush alone! Jackson Rust: Al, you just said that, live, on national TV. The worst part is it sounded like a quote from hermaphrodite porn or something. Steve: I nee-nee-nee-needed th-th-that mm-m-m-men-men-ment-mental im-im-mimage... Jackson Rust: But then again, it could be worse. Our dear, beloved President seems to be suffering something along the lines of the menopause right now... Kimberly: Oh come on, you know as much as I do that men don't suffer from the Menopause in any way... Jackson Rust: I don't know, the impact of Steve's foot on his nut-sack might well have given him some impromptu ovaries. I could expand on this, but it'd get real weird, real quick... Kimberly: Which reminds me. That reporter I told you about? He's standing right behind you, notebook in hand, scribbling away. Jackson Rust: Uh oh...hey. Reporter: Can anyone tell me how you spell 'Menopause', by the way? Jackson Rust: T-I-double P-E-R G-O-R-E comma T-H-A-T A-N-N-O-Y-I-N-G B-I-T... Kimberly: You can use a spell check later on, as long as you got the gist of it. Reporter: Thank you. But what are your thoughts on the upcoming Pay Per View? Jackson Rust: As long as Jiffy Lube does not sponsor it again, they stiffed us for advertising costs on the last one, and still expected a 'Women Wrestling in a Puddle of Oil' match up. I'd rather see a 'Women Wrestling Puddle of Mudd' matchup, to stop that whining little moron polluting the airwaves. How much did Blurry suck, again? Kimberly: Not as much as Nickleback, whose song How You Remind Me is, quote, 'A pile of over-played, me-generation, stupidly bearded, whining, crappily lyriced, 'Look at me, I'm Eddie Vedder' irritation posing as a pile of horseshit in the centre of South Dakota while Linkin Park played the Family Values fest,' unquote. Jackson Rust: Oh yeah. And below the ballpark that is Deadsy. At this point we should be thankful that there hasn't been a record label venture as yet. Or, worse still, sponsorship of some touring festival or other. Can you imagine how galling that idea would be? Which reminds me, do you still have that copy of the Neurotica album, or did it go Frisbee? Al: eBayed it, and managed to afford a cup of coffee. Bad coffee from a run-down cafe that made Sbarro look like Haute Cuisine... Jackson Rust: And how strange the words 'President' and 'Neurotic' can...basically, get in the same sentence. I mean, deciding to throw in Adam Stryker to the four way pay per view match for no other reason but, well, who else wants to look bad with that no-talent hack in the ring with them? Plan 'B', have him stink up the match, but he won't win. Reality sucks, doesn't it, jackass? Kimberly: I think you encroached on trademarked territory there... Jackson Rust: Ah...we're live, aren't we Al? Al: Yes. Jackson Rust: Life is Peachy, track number eight. That covers most of it. But what the Hell? Suddenly recalling 'Oh, wait, I meant to tell you, these two guys are also in the ring, so you have to knock three people out to get the win. Sorry, slipped my mind.' Now, if Steve kicked his nuts into orbit beforehand, it could be explained slightly better, as one of his testicles became lodged in his throat, and the lack of oxygen was affecting his concentration... Reporter: You do know this is a family-orientated website, don't you? Jackson Rust: Steve, tell your lesbian joke, give 'em an education... Al: NO! Jackson Rust: The hairs have it, I guess... But what other surprises does the card have in store? Firing the ring announcer because her assets are not much of an asset? Hiring Aimz because her ass is much of an asset? Deciding to hire some obscure obscurity as special guest ref, only to realise he was lied to by the dude's website; a little backyard experience is all an 8 year old from South Carolina gets? Special edition PCW toilet paper? Reporter: Err, we have that, and it currently retails at $5.99, plus shipping and so forth. Kimberly: Please tell me there isn't a 'used' variety in a presentation pack. Reporter: Okay then, I won't. Kimberly: Euch... Jackson Rust: Just because you run this Federation, own our contracts, pay our wages, and can fire whoever you want because they looked at you funny, or forget to put them on TV for months on end...oh forget it, I won't finish that sentence, it would’ve sounded dumber than Dumbo at a Dumb and Dumber convention in Arkansas. Reporter: Oh, go on, it'll make great copy. Jackson Rust: No. Reporter: Please? Jackson Rust: Only if you assassinate every person responsible for all the Spam my e-mail gets, which never seems to get blocked no matter how many times I do so. It seems almost as if they have some deal worked out with Bill Gates to keep us annoyed, and pay up for a better filter or, better yet, extra storage space. Oh, and the cast of Will & Grace, just on principle. Reporter: Anything else? Jackson Rust: Yes, round me up fourteen Dave Gorman’s, so we can get a Dave Gorman Division going. I just want to see the Undisputed Dave of Dave Gorman’s belt... Kimberly: Can you please stop talking about Dave Gorman? We're starting to get negative feedback, mainly from Dave Gorman. Jackson Rust; Gorman? Gorman, Gorman, Gorman!! Gorman... Kimberly: And don't try to re-enact that scene from Being John Malkovich; it doesn't work. Reporter: What will I need? Jackson Rust: A machete, high-powered rifle and an AK-47 for the former, a phone directory and a very large net for the latter, plus cages. Reporter: Okay then, I'll get to it. The reporter walks out, asking directions to Bullets, Bloodbath and Beyond in the background. Jackson Rust: Okay, I think we'd better leave it here. It's getting weird...
