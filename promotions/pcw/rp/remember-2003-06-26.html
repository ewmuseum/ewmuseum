Remember?

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/03 Friends and Enemies. They are the people that can make or break you. With a simple snap of your finger your best friend can become your worst enemy, or vice versa. So why is it for so long I trusted so many people who just turned their back and walked away on me leaving me alone to fend for myself when times got rough? I loved Chris Storm with my heart, and with the snap or a finger or should I say the snap of a hand that sent his head into a brick wall he suddenly decided it was time for us to part ways and left me, without any word of where he was going or why he was leaving. It was not that big of a deal to me first, I still had the partnership of my then best friend Jackson Rust and although my heart was broken from the departure of Chris, I had Rust’s shoulder to cry on. But when what do you do when your best friend repeats the same action as Storm and leaves you with no one to cry to? I obviously had to make new friends and learn to break my shyness, which is where Lacuna Debris and Miss Moonlight stepped in. I knew I could trust Lacuna because she had been put in a similar position as I when Jade walked out of PCW and left Lacuna partner less. Miss Moonlight though was someone who I hoped for once would not be one to leave me when times get rough and so far she had proven that she would do exactly that by helping me out numerous times against both SOCIETY and Sivasian Krikorian, and in return I had done the same for her when she found herself needing help. With this recent luck of finding myself new friends it made me wonder if I should try to test my new found good luck and see if it would continue to work with the waitress named Heather who had recently acquainted herself with me when I had become discouraged about all the bad luck that had come my way in the form of Gaz and Sivasian whom I would have to face this Tuesday on Turmoil. Then again it’s not often I get a stroke of good luck, so I might as well take a hold of the opportunity and chance my luck with befriending the girl named Heather who looked to be around my age and see how it goes from there. Besides that there was just something about that girl that just brought me back to my days of youth. I didn’t know what it was, but there was something about her that brought back both memories that were good and bad. “Well if it isn’t Tessa-a-roo!” Proclaimed the red headed Heather as I reached the top of the first deck stairs and looked over to notice that she was just getting off her shift at the juice bar. “Hey.” I said rather shyly, afraid to greet her as if I had known her forever yet reluctant to say nothing. As I looked into her eyes in a non passionate manner, and more in the way of just trying to study her past and find out why every time I was around her the past came back to me I was caught off guard by another line of hers. “Is something wrong? You look very confused. Did you forget me already?” She questioned, waving her hand in front of my eyes in a joking manner, causing me to slightly smile and lift a bit of the tension that stood between us, yet still I could not stop thinking about where I might have known her from. “Forget? Huh...Oh no how could I forget? You’re the waitress Heather I met yesterday.” I replied, still confused as to why she would think I would forget her name already when I just met her yesterday and her name was still fresh in my head. “No Tessa, I mean do you forget me?” She asked once more, looking me in the eye as if I were giving the wrong answer or something. “What do you mean did I forget you? I just told you no.” I repeated once more to make it clear to her that I remembered her face and that I knew exactly who she was and why she was here aboard this ship. “I don’t think you do because if you did you’d remember something else.” She said, toying with my mind in a similar way that a puppeteer manipulates his puppet into doing, saying and believing what he wants it to. “What else is there to remember?” I questioned not fully understanding what on earth she was talking about. Remember? We had only met once and discussed some of the awkward and unusual things I had done this past Fusion that made people question why on earth was wrong with me and why I was doing these things. What is there in that conversation with her which was so specific that I needed to remember? “The past.” She said with two simple words, looking at me as if the fate of the world depended on me remembering this past that she was talking about. “The past with Chris?” I questioned, almost positive that it was what she was talking about, but quickly she shook her head no and looked at me rather painfully as if my not being able to remember was eating away at her inside and would gradually eat away until there was nothing left of her. It was kind of like the way I got when people mentioned my marriage with Gaz, or the fact that Chris was no longer at my side. “No, back when you were still a kid.” She said in an encouraging voice surely thinking that this would automatically trigger the memory that she wanted me to remember to come back, but still it did little good considering I had many memories from when I was younger than I was now. “But technically in most peoples eyes I still am a……oh my god!” Suddenly the memory which she was talking about hit me and I felt dumber than a high school dropout. How could I forget? Right before me stood a friend I had grown up with practically all my life, and lost contact with after graduating from high school simply because I took off faster than a thief in the night and didn’t bother to keep in touch with any of my friends simply because I didn’t want my mother or father to know where I was because I know once they found out I wanted a career in professional wrestling, they would turn their noses up at me like I was the scum of the earth. “You remember now don’t you?” And with that I simply nodded my head and remembered back to all the times we spent together, and now with us reunited I could only imagine the many times more we could add to the collection should she not turn her back on the way many others had chosen to do in the past. The question is would this friendship last or would it dissolve into thin air like it had done in the past because of jealousy problems. Heather was always the apple of my parent’s eyes and I was always told that I should be more like her in order to become a success. But looking into the future and at the position we were both in now, who was the success now? It was me. As tempted as I was I wanted to rub that in her face for kissing up to my parents all the times she did and making herself out to be the better child, but somehow I couldn’t. Not because I didn’t have it in me, but because I hated to turn my back on someone the way I had always had other turn their back on me, and rub their success in my face.
