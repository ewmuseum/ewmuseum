The Saves the Day Saga: Hot Time in Delaware

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-10-02 DarkStar stumbled tiredly into his beautiful house in Philadelphia, before he could barely break the threshold he was rushed and nearly toppled by his lover, holding her favorite doll in her right hand. She jumped into his arms like a child and spun about, it procured a smile from DarkStars weathered face, but not much more. Talena: Welcome home lover. DarkStar just nodded, very unenthusiaticly. Talena noticed, her lower lip protruding past her top lip in a pouting motion, he pinched it between his thumb and index finger, she squeaked and he hugged her. DarkStar: I'm sorry, you just wouldn't understand the extent of the horridness I had toda-... She silenced him by holding up her slender finger. He raised an eyebrow as she kissed him, pulling away slowly she nodded. Talena: Fate lost a game of chess to your little friend Death which caused a temporal time rip to open up and swallow all deity's in existence in their respective afterworlds, which normally would cause all life to go straight to nothingness or whatever is beyond and end in a horrible, blood curdling scream that only the doves can hear.... DarkStar stood, his mouth gaping wide open. Talena: Did you bring home milk? We needed milk. DarkStar slowly nodded and placed the milk on the dining room table. Not breaking eye contact with Talena. DarkStar: So telling you about the fa-... Talena: -ct that you now have to take up the rolls of every major deity and filter their jobs through your own body until you can figure out a way to bring them all back from where they are would be pointless. Yes, yes it would be. DarkStar ... ... Stop that. Talena shrugged and laughed. Talena: Tis a gift. DarkStar just shook his head, a bead of sweat dripped off the blue of his hair, he wiped it off with his left hand and sighed. DarkStar: Thats why I love you, although it would be impossible to lie to you. She smiled, and her beautiful blue eyes changed back from their white forms. Talena: It is, but only when I'm in my moods, other then that you can lie to me and I would be as oblivious as a school girl in a sex shop lover. DarkStar: ...beautiful analogy. I dont know what I could possibly do in this situation however, I have to figure out where the rest of the deitys are... until then I have to stumble through a blsated regime as the ruler of all that is supernatural. I haven't even been to my realm since I got back. Damn it. Talena jumped into his arms and bit his neck, her doll falling onto the coffee table in the living room. Talena: Well... wanna have sex? You can tie me up if you will. The King of the Other Worlds looked down at the small woman in his arms and nodded rather quickly. DarkStar: In the light of things, having sex would seem kind of useless, the Other Worlds are collapsing by the second... But.. But... ... your not wearing any underwear are you. She smiled ear to ear and nodded rapidly. DarkStar: Ok, come along then! Talena: Yay! Fade to black, well... that was silly wasnt it. Pssst... Your still writing Whoops. To Be Continued
