The Trial...(Part II)

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-20-02 ::Introduction:: Ever since Mark murdered that man they haven't been able to find out who did it. But now the detectives have the evidence so that is why it is being trialed right now. Then you have Stephens running his mouth in his promos. Mark called him a low carder because that is the truth, he has not earned a spot in the high cards yet. Mark has seen one or two main events when he came into PCW and now Stephens hasn't seen one. But Mark is not saying that is a bad thing and knows that Stephens is not that bad of a wrestler. Right now Mark is worried about the trial and how he is going to make it to Fusion if he is guilty. ::End Introduction:: Mark is still sitting in the chair that we left him dressed in his orange jail suit with the black sneakers that he has to wear by the jail. Mark's attorney goes up in front of the stand after he gets all of his papers organized and sorted out. He stands there palms sweating into his hands as he is dressed in a black suit with shiny new black dress shoes. He coughs real quick and then tries to speak. Nothing comes out and then he goes back over to the table and he gets a glass of ice water. 'I would like to call up Anita Slumlocker to the witness stand.' says Mark's attorney as he is leaning on the judges stand as he looks at the jury. A blonde headed lady stands up from her little section and she makes her way past the people who are sitting by her and then she goes in the aisle. Her pink dress touches the red carpet that leads to the swinging doors in which lead to all of the stands. She goes over to the witness stand and a guard comes up to her. 'Do you swear to tell the whole truth and nothing but the truth so help you God?' asks the guard as he places Anita's hand on the bible and makes her raise her other arm. 'I do.' Anita says in a sweet voice that sounds as if she is always innocent. Anita goes over to the bench that is sitting behind the stand and she sits down putting her dress in front so it doesn't get all tangled up. Mark's attorney goes over to the witness stand and he stands there just staring at Anita for a little bit. 'Where were you in the afternoon of December 12, 2001?' Mark's attorney, Dave, asks Anita staring right in her hazel eyes. 'Well I was across the street from Harry's bar in the strip club.' says Anita as she looks into the jury as she is telling her story. 'I was done with my shift and I was going to go over to Harry's bar to just get myself out of stress you know. I saw Mark Makonie and Denzel fighting in the bar and then I saw Mark throw him out of the bar. But the funniest thing I saw was Denzel fighting back and Denzel beating Mark down to the ground with a led pipe. I went inside to call the police and then I heard the police sirens downtown.' 'Well this sounds as if the story has turned around in her situation!' says Dave as he looks right at the detective on the other side. 'Then I just saw Denzel leave and then I saw Mark just lie there on the street hurt.' says Anita as she almost starts to cry. 'I felt so bad for him.' 'I have no further questions your honor.' says Dave as he heads back to his seat. Dave gets back to his seat and he pours another glass of the ice cold water. Anita walks by him and then she taps him on the shoulder for some apparant reason. Mark is looking at the judge as he goes through his folders of records on Mark probably and the situation that he is in right now. 'Is there any evidence that any of the parties would like to present to the jury?' asks the judge as he gets all of his papers sorted. The detective from the other party opens up a brown paper bag that is sitting down next to him and his partner. He stands up and presents the judge with the brown paper bag. The judge opens up the bag as the detective is heading back to his seat to sit down. 'What the detective has just presented me is some of the photos that were takin by the police at the scene of the murder.' says the judge as he shows the jury all of the photos that were in the bag. Mark and Dave look at the photos and see they are the real photos of the murder that actually happened. Mark's head goes down in shame and realizes that this is the end of the lieing road for him and his witnesses. 'These pictures have police numbers on the bottom and they are pictures of Denzel Warrington on the afternoon of December 21st, 2001.' says the judge as he looks at the pictures somemore. 'These pictures show the proff of the scene of crime. There are fingerprints here that match Mark Makonie's and they are on the coat of Denzel.' The judge gets down from the stand and he passes the pictures around to the jury. The jury passes all of them around and they all get a good look at them. The judge gets the pictures back as he goes back up to his stand to call more of the action. 'Do you have any evidence to present to the jury?' asks the judge as he stares right at Dave. 'No I am sorry judge we have nothing.' says Dave as he is looking up at the judge pleading his case. 'Jury please get your results in because it seems as this case is over.' says the judge looking over at the section where all the jury sit and talk. The jury is writing their results on a little paper and handing them down their row. Mark is sitting by Dave knowing that this is the end and knows that he might have to job to Stephens at Fusion because he can't get out of jail. The head person for the jury stands up and he brings the paper over to the judge's stand. 'I hearby find that Mark Makonie after reviewing all the results of the jury and the facts, GUILTY!' says the judge loudly as he looks over at Mark and then he sits back in his chair. The guards in the brown uniforms come over to the table where Mark and Dave are sitting and they have Mark get up off his seat. They take the silver handcuffs and put them on Mark's hands behind his back. Mark has his head down as the media is in the court room now taking shots of Mark as he is being helped out of the court room. He goes past the jury, but doesn't bother looking at them because he knows he is wrong. 'We finally caught the guy after a whole year.' says one of the guards to a camera as they are heading out of the court room. They take Mark out of the court room and they take him back they way they came. They make him leave the rest of his clothes in a basket and tell him that he will get them back when he gets out of this place. Mark's head is still down and everyone is trying to grab Mark's hands like they tried to before. They all have probably been turned gay in jail just like everyone else. The guards take the keys and they unlock the cell of Mark's. They can do it manually or with the electronics. Mark gets thrown in his cell by the two guards as they laugh together when they see Mark sit on a stool. The guards go away and Mark looks out of the cell to look at his enviroment. 'Now this is what I am going to be looking at for the next couple of years is this gray wall and the other cells.' says Mark as he is talking to himself and looking out of his cell down the hall. Mark goes back to his stool and he just looks over at the wall that he can barely see in the low light. Mark starts to think about his match with Stephens and how he can get out of his cell and to the event. He then looks around to see if anyone is listening across the hall. 'Very soon I will get out of here and hopefully it is before Fusion so I can prove Stephens that he is wrong.' says Mark as he looks out the tiny window that is not that big and guarded by rails. (Scene Fades) Stay Tuned for Part III
