Date: Meh Finally, after over a year of being in complete shadow, Young Tiger, also Eddy, has been found by The End, also Skylar Green had found him somehow. Eddy was wearing a blue shirt with black jeans, trying to dress decent for his friend to come over. He sat on a really tattered porch. Broken, beaten, looks like it would collapse on Eddy any second. But it didn’t, and stood above him, just like the sun would. He perched a seat on a stair and sat. Sky said he’d be there by 5:00 PM. It was now 4:45. He couldn’t wait to arrive at the PCW HQ. He was not only excited for that, but to meet his old friend too. A beaten up brown car pulled in from of Eddy’s house. By the expression on Eddy’s face, this was not good. It wasn’t Skylar’s car, but instead, a fat, crooked toothed hick stepped out with a bald held and dirty clothes. The hick slammed to door, and walked up to Eddy. Eddy froze still as the hick screamed. 'Yer a bastard! You still ain’t givin’ me my money for rent. I ‘oughta whip ya’!' 'Now, sir…' 'Don’t give me that sir talk boy! Git off muh’ land righ’ this second before I kick yo’ ass.' Eddy stood still for second as the hick walked closer to Eddy, shaking a bulging fist. He knew the hick wouldn’t even try hitting him, because he threatened more then actually did, but Eddy tried to be civil with him. 'I have the rent. I just don’t have it with me now-' Then, before he could finish, the hick did what Eddy doubted all these times. He pushed him. 'I said get off muh’ land!' Before this could worsen, a blue SUV pulled in front. Inside was Skylar with dark sunglasses, and he saw an Asian woman in the passengers seat. It was his girlfriend he was told about, Sandy Wong. Skylar stepped out first, wearing casual clothes like a polo blue shirt, blue jeans at his sunglasses and the Sandy didn’t move at all. Skylar walked up to the hick and Eddy, he had saw Eddy being pushed by him. 'Let me guess…You’re the landlord?', Skylar remarked. 'Yaw! Wut about it?!' 'Well, I’m paying for Eddy. He getting out of this place and coming with me.' 'Show me da’ money then bastard!' Skylar put his hands into his jeans and pulled out money. He handed the stack to the hick landlord. The hick glance at the money in surprise. It was more then the rent was. 'Keep the change. Eddy, c’mon…' Eddy took one more glance at his home and the hick counting the money as Eddy walked up to the truck. The house still looked like sh*t. While in the car… 'So, how are you doing?', Sandy said. 'Good, I guess.' 'So, tell me about you. Sky mentioned things briefly.' Eddy paused for a moment to think of what to say, and then started up. 'You see, I quit PCW to pursue other things. I gave up a lot of my money to charity thinking I’d have a decent job. However, I didn’t get one, and spent a little to much cash, so, I was in a nice house here, but was evicted. So I moved into this, writing silly short stories to a magazine around here, which is a bunch of papers stapled together basically…' 'Sky told me you couldn’t find your parents. Couldn’t go to a local library and find out?' 'Well, the deal is, the next public library is probably 5 hours away or something. I don’t even know where it is. Nobody is even friendly enough around here to do anything like that and drive me to it. So, without a car, I just had hope that I could find a way to reach my parents and siblings.' Up on a billboard, the city’s name was stated, with. '300 friendly residents' written below. Eddy chuckled. 'I haven’t even met anyone friendly around here.' Sandy sighed. 'People just aren’t to nice these days…' Eddy looked outside, staring at the boring fields and flat land. It felt like when he was a kid. He always stared outside on car trips, bored at the fields. He never liked cities like this. However, his nicer house was suited in a greener area, so then it wasn’t a problem. But now, it was boring for him. His mind drifted in other place besides what lie before him. He thought about his parents, his friends, hell, even Mr. T came into his mind. Mr. T always showed up in one of Eddy’s promos. He remembered his little brother Hunter who always was playful and always looked up to Eddy, and his parents, who supported and cared for him all his life. He smiled and little, leaned back into the leather of the SUV and drifted away into a daydream. Eddy had his wrestling attire on, his boots, and his smile, ready to go for the action in the ring. 'I want to see you win tonight Eddy! C’mon, you know you’re the best ever!'his dad said. 'That’s a little overconfident dad, but I’ll try my best.' 'I wish you the best of luck, we’re going off to Hawaii on a vacation but we’ll be sure to watch', his mom added. 'OK.' 'Bye.' 'Bye' 'See ya…' Then, Eddy trekked off into the PCW building, ready for his match. He couldn’t of been more excited. He popped his knuckles, and walked straight towards the locker room. He walked toward his equipment, taking a brief glance at it. But not for long. He walked and heard 'Eye Of The Tiger' on the PA system. It was his turn to fight. He walked up towards the arena area, glancing around the room for a second as he walked towards the curtain. 'Hailing from Dallas, Texas, at 5’11’’, 185 pounds….YOOOUNG TIIIGER. He smiled and walked into the curtain. The cheers overloaded his ears. The fans stood on their feet in happiness. Fans held up signs with random things about him on them. He walked down the ramp, proud and happy. He walked straight towards the ring. He was ready. Hours later 'Let’s stop somewhere for food.' 'Yeah, I’m starving. 'Me too.' There was a truck stop. It had, in big bold red letters: 'The Best Damn Hamburgers in Wyoming.' Skylar pulled in and opened his door, as everyone else followed. Sandy brushed some of the dust that gone on her clothing as the car stopped where it was. Eddy walked out of the truck and coughed. The dust gathered on his shirt. His brushed himself clean, and covered his mouth and nose He followed behind Sandy. She had already made it in, but Eddy still needed in. Skylar had the door open for him. He walked over into the door, brushing himself off one more time before proceeding through the other door to enter the restaraunt. They all walked in, with the smell of hamburgers in the air. Eddy was drooling at the smell, he had any decent food for 5 days. He turned and looked around. Sky and Sandy followed behind him. 'God, I’m so hungry', said Eddy while entering. 'Mmmm, the hamburger’s smell good', said Sandy as she walked into the restaurant. All three entered as it opened up. It was half bar, half restaurant. It had a bar, but it also had other seats around, not really any professional waiter to guide you to any certain table, and not smoking/non-smoking sections. Of course, so many people smoked you really couldn’t bother giving a sh*t. The End spotted a table toward the back, sitting 4. He didn’t see any 3 seated ones, at least not without anyone at them. He gestured them to come over as they walked at sat down. 'Where’s the menu?', The End asked. 'I don’t know.' 'Those other ones got menu’s. Why not us?' 'Not sure…' 'Maybe this one was reserved or something?' 'Oh c’mon. We’re in a truck stop in Wyoming. They can’t be THAT popular. 'Hey, here someone comes.' A skinny man in a white shirt, blue jeans walked up with a receipt book in his hand. His has was short, and a dark shade of brown. 'Hello. Here’s your menu’s', in a thick country accent almost too hard to understand. He hands out folded green pieces of paper too each of them. 'Anythin’ in particular you want to drink?' The three scanned the drinks section. The End was the first to say something. 'Water' 'You sir?' 'Uh…I guess water too.' 'Eh, I’ll get water as well.' The waiter nodded and walked off 'I’m going to the restroom. Be back.' Sandy stood up and walked away towards the back. The End then leaned forward a little to Eddy. 'Eddy, how long has it been since you actually talked to your family?' 'Uh….7 months. I think.' 'What you guys talk about?' 'I have no idea…Have you ever talked to them?' 'Yeah…' 'And…' 'We’ve talked numerous times.' 'OK…How about most recently?' The End stopped. '5 months.' 'What?! Well, then, were they worried?' 'I don’t know. I talked to your sister.' 'Ah! Melissa…She all right?' 'Well, too be honest, not really.' 'Wha?' Young Tiger noticed Sandy was coming back. Sandy took at seat at the table. 'Hi.' 'Hey.' 'The drinks still aren’t here?' 'Yeah, busy day.' 'I can tell. Hey Sky, come with me real quick.' The End stood up. 'Hey. Can I come?' 'Nah, just wait here for the drinks, K?' 'OK…' They followed each other down the hall and outside to the car. Sandy leaned against the wall as Sky stepped out into the cool air. 'Well…Did you tell him?' 'I’m trying to avoid it. Unless he brings something up about them, I’m going to just avoid it. He’s coming back, Sandy. I’ll tell him if he really brings it up. He just said something of it to. 'I feel sorry for the dude. You never know, he could turn evil…' 'I don’t know. But he has to find out eventually, it’s just going to be hard for me to tell him.' 'Yeah, true. Why don’t I tell him? I could probably do it in a less strict fashion.' 'The truth’s coming out. He asked me when was the last time I talked to his family, and I told him is sister, and then he asked how she was doing, and I said not too well, right before you came back from the restroom.' 'Well…We can do nothing more then be honest.' Sky nodded as he opened to door back in. He walked down the hall, and saw Eddy twiddling his thumbs. The drinks were now here, and he gradually sipped some of it now and then. Sky walked back to the seat, and sat. Sandy followed shortly after. 'When do we order food?' 'He asked me, and I said I didn’t know what you guys wanted, so I told him to wait a little until you guys were back.' 'Oh', said Sandy, lifting the menu to her face. 'Mmmm…The Bacon Cheeseburger sounds good.' 'They’re pricey.' 'Oh well. Maybe the quality will fix that.' 'True…' Eddy turned in his seat. 'Sky..What did you mean my sister wasn’t doing well?' Sky and Sandy looked at each other. The truth was to be told…
