The Faceless Man

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 13 - 03 Trudging quietly through a never-ending black void, Dorn Tracer knows little of where he's at. His eyes dart from corner to corner, scouting his surroundings and looking for possible signs of life. But he sees none... hears none... 'Where am I...?' He wonders, his own voice echoing within the depths of his head. His boots touch nothing, yet he walks upon solid ground. There's no wind, no land, no sea... nothing. Just an endless shadow of blackness, unmoving and surrounding his being. A certain uneasiness begins to build within his body as he continues on. He can't hear, he can't feel, he can't smell... his eyes are the only things giving him any bearings. What is this desolate black of shadows? As he walks along, the thought runs through the crevices of his brain over and over, like a broken record player. 'What is this place? Why does it never end?' Answerless questions flow through him, but bring up nothing. All Dorn can do is continue walking, never becoming tired and never feeling his muscles tighten. How long has he been walking? He doesn't know. When will it ever end? He can't say. Suddenly, like a great eye, a silver ball appears high in the sky some distance away from him. It starts as a thick slit, then slowly opens. Dorn stops dead in his tracks and gazes at the strange object, which strangely resembles the moon. The Astronomer stares at it from behind his dark shades, the face of the giant star reflecting itself in the lens of his shades. 'How beautiful...' The Astronomer thinks, being reminded of the great lunar star that hangs in the night sky. He starts to walk towards it, feeling it beckon to him. The great moon... calling for Dorn... what a wonderful feeling. Tracer walks closer to it... wanting to revel in its glory... But he suddenly stops when the star casts down a column of light, blinding Dorn and causing the Astronomer to stagger back. The light even pierces through the lens of his shades, causing him to shield his eyes in a panic. The aura dies down after a moment and Dorn gazes upon the aftermath... A lone figure stands within the center of the column, wearing nothing but black pants, black boots, and what appears to be a black riot mask. The Astronomer takes one look at the man before turning an eerie pale color. 'Er... what the...???' The surprise echoes from his voice and takes on a rather... panicked tone. Who is this man? And why is Dorn so shocked to see him? But like he felt with the moon, the figure seems to beckon towards him. Tracer can't help but feel himself step forward... Dorn reaches him after about 10 or so steps and he stares into his own reflection, manifesting itself upon the plastic face guard of the riot mask. He slowly reaches out to touch his own face, but the man suddenly snatches Dorn by the wrist. Before the Astronomer can react, the man bends Dorn's wrist back and forces him to his knees, causing Dorn to groan and struggle in agony. He looks up at the masked man and gasps when the figure reaches behind his back and brings out a nightstick. 'What the?? What are you doing with that? LET ME GO!!' He cries, but it's all for naught, as the figure raises the nightstick, which meets the moon perfectly. Dorn can only watch as the man brings the nightstick down with amazing force... right onto Dorns' face... ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! 'NOO!!!' Dorn Tracer rises from his bed like a blast of lightning, his eyes as wide as beach balls and sweat pouring down his face. His entire body is soaked in a slimy layer of sweat that merges with the blanket and sheets, causing Dorn to get out of the bed and whip the sweat off his arms and hands. The look on his face is one of disgust and exhaustion, as if he just had sex with some nasty fat chick. 'Just a dream... ' He mumbles through his heavy breaths of exhaustion. 'God that was strange... but why did it seem so familiar...' The puzzling event runs through his mind over and over, playing again and again. Some parts he can't quite remember, but only because that is typical with dreams. He strips out of his white wifebeater shirt and walks into the adjacent bathroom, shutting the door behind him. There's silence for a moment, followed by the squeaking of metal and the sound of running water. *** 5 Minutes Later *** The door to the bathroom flings open and Dorn comes striding out, draped in a fuzzy blue towel. His body is a dark shade of red from the hot water and his hair hangs all over his face like a mask. A steady stream of vapor pours forth from the white room, dissipating into the bedroom but casting a nice wave of heat. The Astronomer sighs in relief of a hot shower and sits down on his still slightly damp bed. 'Dreams don't happen for no reason... and this one seemed too familiar,' He still seems to be contemplating his dream, which bothers him like a bad mosquito bite. The Astronomer rises from the bed with a puzzled look on his face and walks over to the set of mahogany dressers where he keeps various knick-knacks. Sliding the top drawer open, he finishes through the various odds and ends, looking for something in particular, 'Let's see... where is it... I swear that guy reminds me of something...' Dorn mumbles to himself, still dripping wet with a towel on. He moves some more things aside and brings out what appears to be a black binder, covered in a layer of dust. A beaming grin crosses Dorn's face and he nods, 'Here it is! Just what I was looking for!' He takes the small binder over to his bed and sits down once more, in the same damp spot. Flipping the binder open, he thumbs through plastic sheets that hold a variety of photographs, signifying that this must be Dorn's photo album. He stops upon coming to a page full of pictures showing he and his family, huddling in a little group. There's himself, his mother, his father, and his brother... they look really happy. Dorn scoffs, 'Looks can be deceiving. Fuckin' bastard...' The Astronomer pounds his fist in the general direction of his father, feeling the rage building within his body. He forcefully flips the page and continues on, looking the various photos with a puzzled eye. He doesn't know WHY he's looking through his photo album. He just... seems to be... But the answer reveals itself when Dorn comes upon a single picture. He almost passes it, but it catches his eye and he stops, frozen in motion. A feeling of terror creeps up on him... his hand begins to tremble... It's a picture of a man... in black pants, black boots, and a black riot helmet... Standing under a street lamp, with the moon in the background... And under the figure, in black marker, it reads... DAD [End.]
