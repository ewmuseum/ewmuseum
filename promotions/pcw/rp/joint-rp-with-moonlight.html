Date: Sky: #3 for Fusion Enter into the gym (which is really Miss Moonlight's room, which she donated for exercise equipment to be placed in the Cloud Nine airship). Moonlight is pressing some weights over on the bench. Skylar walks in wearing some thin shirt and shorts for working out. Skylar steps onto a treadmill and starts it at a brisk 10 mph jog. Hearing a female voice counting in Japanese off to his left side... Sky: Hey. Miss Moonlight looks off to the side, seeing the PCW World Champion. Moonlight: Yo. Moonlight: Big match tonight... eh? Skylar shrugs his shoulders in the jog, biting his bottom lip. Sky: Not really. The Order is a non factor. Dorn and Titu can't get along too well. Moonlight: True... The Order is inept. Still a lot for one man to take though. She presses the weight harder, her eyes straining nearly shut. Moonlight: Still... things would be easier... with backup, eh? Sky: Offering? Finally she racks the weights, a loud thud of bar against metal. Moonlight: Me? I'm sure you'd find better. After all, I'm already booked tonight. My dear friend Debris and I will show people what it means to be a woman and talented. A smirk from the World Champion. Sky: Yes... people always have other things to do. But they always say, 'It would be easier with help.' Not faulting you, just stating things as they stand. Moonlight sits up, wiping the sweat away. Skylar closes his blank eyes. Sky: That's why I don't have a partner. No one wanted it. Moonlight: The very reason I said it exactly like that. No one has the courage to take charge... no one wants to be a hero Sky: It's a scary prospect; taking responsibility outside of oneself... Moonlight stands and points at Skylar. Moonlight: How does that fit with what you did on Turmoil? Skylar tilts his head to the side. Moonlight: Instead of fighting, you just gave up. Sky: I did what Pharaoh told me to do. His plan didn't work out. Moonlight: But you didn't teach him a lesson in the ring. Skylar snickers. Sky: That's what Fusion is for. Miss Moonlight walks away, shaking her head. Moonlight: If you say so. Sky: Thanks for the vote of confidence.
