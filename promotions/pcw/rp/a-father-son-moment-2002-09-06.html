A Father/Son Moment

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/6/02 Soft music twangs through the loudspeakers of a quaint restaurant. Quaint as in it's rather sickening to look at....rats scurry across the floor, dining on the leftover scraps of overweight patrons. An old ceiling fan spins, wobbling and threatening to fall, beheading whoever happened to be underneath it at the time. We won't even venture into the kitchen of this diner. In other words, it feels right at home for the family who eats there today. Clyde: Bah. The demon named Clyde twirls his fork in a pile of steaming eggs, mumbling to himself. Across the table, a skeletal figure in a black cloak watches and grins his eternal bony grin. Dark Gimmick: You aren't going to eat, son? Clyde: Feh. The physical incarnation of evil shakes his skull, then waves his finger. A plume of black smoke rises from the omelet, and when it dissipates a pile of writhing maggots crawl on Clyde's food. Dark Gimmick: Need flavor? Clyde: That's not it, dad. Dark Gimmick: Then what.... A fly springs forth from the plate of food, buzzing around DG's head. He takes a moment to open his mouth, flicking a lizard-like tongue out and snaring it. Dark Gimmick: (Munching) ....is it? Clyde: Bah. I'm rather bored. Bored with life, bored in general. Gimmick watches his son, chewing the meaty fly. He then shakes a bony finger at the demon. Dark Gimmick: What you need is a purpose in life, boy. Clyde: What do you mean? Dark Gimmick: I mean....WAITRESS!!!!! Abruptly a middle aged woman in a horribly gaudy skirt wanders up, pulling a pen from her mass of beehived hair. Waitress: What can I get yo.... The lady stops short, staring at the skeleton sitting at her table. Dark Gimmick: What? Waitress: .....you could use some moisturizer. Dark Gimmick: (Scoffing) Mortals these days. Clyde boy, what would you like? Clyde: (Mumbling) A quick death. Dark Gimmick: Okay. At that the Gimmick whips his hand out, catching the waitress by the arm. He pulls her in and bites her wrist, opening a huge gash.....as her life's blood leaves her, she wobbles, moans softly and falls limply Clyde: That's not what I meant, dad. Dark Gimmick: Oh. Shaking his head, DG pulls the waitress's hand up and holds her severed wrist over his empty glass. He squeezes a bit, filling it to the brim with the remainder of her blood then drops her rudely to the floor. Dark Gimmick: (Sipping) So yes. You need a point to keep existing....I mean sure, murder in and of itself is fun, but even it can get old. You need to think about your future! Clyde: Leave me alone. The burned man lays his head on the table, closing his eyes. Like any father though, Dark Gimmick refuses to relent (ie: shut up already). Dark Gimmick: No son, you need to plan ahead. Have you ever considered genocide? Think of the long term, dammit! Clyde: I'm doing fine, dad. Really. Dark Gimmick: BAH~! DG takes a large drink of blood, then plants his glass and stares across the table at Clyde. Dark Gimmick: Invest. Buy some chunks of land in the middle east, they're always having some kind of war or another. Blood! Death! You'll literally never run dry. Think boy! Clyde: Ugh. The depressed demon twiddles his fork in the pile of maggots, as a small boy wanders by with a balloon. Boy: You have bony face. Dark Gimmick: So do you. Boy: Do not! Dark Gimmick: Do too. Boy: Do not! Do not! Dark Gimmick: Do too! Do too! Look. Gimmick reaches into his cloak, producing a mirror....he holds it in front of the child's eyes. Boy: See I don't have a bony..... His eyes grow wide as he stares into his reflection, and sees that his face now appears to be missing. The poor boy glances to his left, where DG smirks and holds his freshly ripped off face in his bony fingers. Dark Gimmick: Do too. Boy: WAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!!!!!! DG slams his fist off the table, laughing hysterically. Dark Gimmick: Bwahahaha....now that was funny. Clyde: Bah. Dark Gimmick: So yes. As long as you continue your petty killings you'll never get anywhere. I raised you better than that, boy. When will you settle down? Clyde: Leave me ALONE. Dark Gimmick: You've been dating the same eight year old for what seems to be the past eighty years. When are you going to tie the knot? I want grandchildren to torment and torture, you know. Clyde: Feh. Clyde shakes his head, as DG steals a bit of his maggot omelette and scarfs it down. Dark Gimmick: (Chewing) So after we dispose of this Mandrake kid tonight, let's you and me look at some quality death camps. You're never too young to start thinking about mass extermination of the human race you know. Clyde: Bah! The demon stands abruptly, sliding off of the bench and making his way out the door. Alone at the table, Dark Gimmick shakes his head. Dark Gimmick: And he leaves me to provide the tip. I taught him well in the ways of evil. Gimmick reaches casually underneath the table, pulling up the dead waitress again. He holds her close, whispers. Dark Gimmick: Tip....some blush will help with that deathly pale complexion of yours. Once more DG laughs, bellowing loudly at his own joke. Dark Gimmick: Bwahaha....deathly....I kill me! Hell I kill all of you! Ha ha! Finally after a long moment he ceases his mad laughter....looks around to find the remaining members of the restaurant staring horrifically at him. Dark Gimmick: ......ah you all suck. Giving the corpse a playful swat on the ass, Dark Gimmick rises and begins to follow his son out the door, as we mercifully fade to black.......
