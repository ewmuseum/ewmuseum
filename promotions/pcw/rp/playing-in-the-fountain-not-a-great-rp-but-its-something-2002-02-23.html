Playing In The Fountain (Not a great Rp, but its something)

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 23 2002 It was no suprise to see Timo get the win over Kramer to advance to the next round for a chance at the greatest title in PCW. Kramer went in knowing there was a small chance to win, yet he tried to keep together and not let Timo, and the doubt from just about everyone intimidate him. And considering he was sort of rusty from being out of the ring for so long, he did pretty decent. Due to this big tournament, Kramer hasn't had many matches in the past two or so weeks. Well actually he hasn't had any matches, which has really discouraged him. But the bookers finally heard his cry for any match. So thats exactly what he got. The very first match of the upcoming Sunday Sabotage, Kramer gets to go against Bob. Bob seems like a tough competitor. Kramer doesn't doubt Bob's abilities, but he doesn't want Bob to doubt his abilities either. All in all it should be an interesting bout. Who knows Kramer may have shaken off more rust by then. The scene opens at night. Only a fountain can be seen due to the light that is shining down on it. The fountain is spewing out fresh water from tiny holes inside of its gray mold. We hear a sudden splash as a plastic dolphin toy is slammed down disrupting the whole beauty of it. As you look at the dolphin you see the hand attached to it, then you go up and see the face of Kramer who looks like he hasn't had a nights sleep for quite some time. The blue tank top he is wearing has a few holes all around. And his jean shorts look like they were home made as we can see the seams coming out from the bottom. He starts to sing. Kramer: They call him Flipper, Flipper, faster than lightnening, no one can see, he's smarter than me hahaha!! From his pocket he pulls out a little lego person and places them on the brim of the fountain which the water doesn't touch. Then flicks the lego person into the water and watches as it sinks to the bottom. Kramer: Oh no! Little Monte fell in the water! He can't swim! We need someone who can save him! Wait....its Flipper! Kramer moves his hand and causes the toy dolphin to go under the water with the lego person. Then he pulls the lego person out and lays it flat. Kramer: That Flipper is a hero! He may not have hands or feet but he is a major hero who saved Monte's life. If he wasn't there then Monte would have drowned, how sad. Oh but wait Flipper is in his own amount of trouble. Here comes GI Sam with a harpoon in hand. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a GI Joe doll which has a fake spear in hand. Then he pushes it onto the dolphin and lets the dolphin float away. Kramer: GI Sam just killed flipper! I repeat he just killed Flipper! How cruel and mean that was. I can see the headlines now, 'Flipper Killed By GI Joe Reject'. He grabs all of the toys and puts them back in his pocket which causes his pocket to dampen a little due to the water. Then he stands up and flips off his boots and sits and rests his feet in the fountain. Kramer: How soothing and cool. A ringing disrupts the silence. Kramer reaches into another of his pocket and pulls out his cell phone which has an American flag on the face. He lets it ring one more time before answering. Kramer: This is the Wild one, talk to me. Jason: Kramer where in the world are you? Kramer: Umm...oh I am in a fountain. Jason: What!? Oh never mind don't answer that. I just called to remind you about your match on Sunday against Bob. I hope you haven't forgotten. Kramer: Don't worry I didn't. Its still fresh in my mind. I even remember that it is the very first match too. Jason: Whatever, just come prepared this time unlike last week. That was a sad way to start your way back in PCW don't you know? Kramer: Of course I do. I got my behind kicked but like I said before I don't really care. I just wrestle because to me its fun. So when I face Bob, it may be partially to win but its mostly going to be for me just to do what I love and have fun doing it. Jason: You having fun does not keep the sponsors supporting you. You winning is what will do that, and honestly right now our funding is starting to die down due to your lack of work rate, lack of wins, and the time you were too busy being lazy to resign to PCW. Kramer: What funds? And who are my sponsors? See you never tell me any of that crap. Remember I am just the wrestler, and you do everything else? You should remember considering you have given me that dag on speech several thousand times. If it means that much to my success then I will try a little bit harder but I am still going to have fun whether you like it or not. Now I have some important things to do so I am going to let you go. Jason: Oh god your playing with those toys again aren't you? Before he can say any more Kramer hangs up and shoves it back in his pocket. Kramer: What can I do now? I feel poetic....There once was a man named Kramer who sucked at everything. His only claim to fame was his wild chicken wing. When he wrestled he almost always lost. He began to bite as much as Jack Frost. He was probably going to lose again to the wrestler known as Bob. Maybe he should look into getting a new job? He continues rambling on and splashing in the fountain as the scene fades. Judging from his actions Kramer seems to have gotten a tad bit weirder, but what has sparked this? Sometimes he seems sane and other times he seems like a two year old. If there no end to the madness? Plain in simple, Kramer is one weird fellow.
