The End provides an abridged script for Blade 2 and talks about his opponents for Turmoil

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/02 Before I begin, I must say that I saw Blade 2 last Friday, and it was a very good movie. I suggest everyone see it if they haven't already. Then again, I know that wrestling may get in the way of free time for leisure pursuits such as movie-going, so I shall provide you with an abridged script for Blade 2. After that, I might say something about my opponents... we'll see what mood I'm in later on. Scene 1: The movie opens in a blood bank in the Cezech Republic. The dialogue makes us believe that in a country full of Czechs, with minorities of Slovaks, Poles, Germans, gypsies, and Hungarians, that English would be the language that they would all choose to speak (odd, since English is nowhere near being the national language). A guy that looks like the European answer to Steve Buschemi walks into the blood bank. He sits down next to a man (named Nomac) who is pale and sickly looking, yet very well built. This will obviously come into play later. A very unattractive woman calls for Nomac. He follows her down through the halls of this blood bank that looks like the torture champers of Vlad Dracula (who the f*ck is their interior decorator?), they then move into a room with metal walls and a computerized lock. Thinking that we're finally out of the drab dungeon-like setting, we move past the doors into ANOTHER BRICK-WALLED TORTURE CHAMBER! Woman: Nomac... we need your blood.... Nomac: (Pretending to be afraid) No, I don't want to!) Woman: Tough.... Nomac is strapped to the table. A man puts on a special metal claw designed to extract Nomac's blood. 85% of the audience feels sorry that he won't be able to use it. Nomac: You shouldn't strap me to this table. That makes me angry. And you won't liek me when I'm angry.... Nomac hulks up, breaks his bonds, and proceeds to eat the three people in the room, all of whom turn out to be vampires. The credits roll, and Blade talks. Boring. Scene 2: Ridiculous looking vampires, ranging from euro trash to an old man in a pink feather boa, are running from Blade. This scene will reiterate just how badass Blade is, in case we've forgotten since hsi last movie. Vampire: Run! Blade is badass! Blade: (Running behind) I am badass. Badass Blade kills two vampires after jumping up to the rafters above them and dropping down. The vampires burn to a crisp after a few chops from his sword. Blade: My sword is badass, as well. The vampires run away from Blade. They come to the edge of the rooftop. They don't want to hurt themselves, so they slide down the ladder to the ground. Blade on the other hand jumps down off the roof and lands unscathed. All of this is to prove just how badass Blade can be. The vampires get on their motorcycles and rush Blade. Blade disposes of the vampires and their motorcycles with the greatest of ease. Remember, Blade is badass. Blade captures one vampire. Blade: Take me to Whistler. Pervert Vampire: You look quite attractive in black leather. And the trenchcoat is so you. However I cannot tell you where Whistler is. Audience: Didn't he die in the last movie? Blade: (to audience)That's what we wanted you to think. (to vampire)Take me to Whistler or I shall grind your head off. The Pervert Vampire takes Blade to where they are holding Whistler. Blade moves in, takes out all the bad guys, except for the Pervert Vampire that's just so old and gay that he can't possibly put up a challenge for Blade. Blade is badass. Blade: I am badass. Audience: He is badass. Blade finds Whistler and returns to his hideout. We meet the new guy that helps Blade with his weapons. The audience thinks of a mature-looking Edward Furlong to draw comparisons. Edward Furlong (not really): You got Whistler! Blade: Quiet. Edward Furlong: Shutting up. Blade gives Whistler the vampire cure. IN the morning, Whistler is no longer a vampire (because we learn that he couldn't kill himself in the last movie and is made a vampire, dragged around the world, and tortured by the vampires). The next morning, Whistler learns he was gone for two years. There is a touching scene with he and Blade. Female Audience: Blade is sensitive. Male Audience: Yes, but Blade is still badass. Blade: Indeed I am badass. There's another scene of badassness coming up in about five minutes, so if you still need snacks, go now. In the meantime, Whistler and Edward Furlong will argue. Edward Furlong and Whistler argue about who's the better guy for Blade. We are quite perplexed by this, seeing as how they should be banding together for the common good. Blade is standing in the background, counting down the seconds to the next scene in which he may prove his badassness. Blade: 3... 2... 1... The security system goes nuts. Two vampires are in Blade's hideout. Whistler: Two vampires are here. They got past your security system. Edward Furlong: You'd think that wouldn't you. For you see, I let them in! Whistler: That line doesn't come for another hour. Edward Furlong: Oh yeah. Blade quickly goes to fight the vampires and make us forget this. Blade fights off the two vampires. Blade: I am badass. Vampires: You are badass. Audience: He is badass. The vampires stop fighting, and they tell Blade to come to the headquarters of the vampire nation. Whistler: What? Edward Furlong: What? Audience: What? Me: Vampire Nation? I never saw that on the map in World Geography.... Blade: Okay, let's go. I'm much more trusting of vampires now than I was in the last movie. But remember, that's just because I'm very very badass. Scene 3: Nyssa, the female vampire is disappointed. Nyssa: You came too easily. Blade once again proves how badass he is. Blade: I've got enough explosives on me to take out an entire city in case this doesn't go down right. Once again, I am badass. Audience: He is badass. Blade goes to the Vampire Nation. We learn that it is run by a lawyer and some vampire that's too old and weak to stand upright. Blade: You're kidding me. Lawyer: No, unfortunately.... Old Vampire: We need you to hunt Nomac. Blade: Uh... why? Old Vampire: BECAUSE! It'll take up and hour and a half and let you prove once again just how badass you are! Blade: I'm sold. Lawyer: But you'll have help in the form of the Blood Pack. Audience: The name is so corny, we can tell that none of them are going to survive. Blade: Blood Pack? Nyssa: We were training to hunt you, but sh*t happens. Scene 4: Back at the hideout, Blade discusses the Blood Pack with Whistler and Edward Furlong. Whistler: So? Blade: Sounds interesting. Whistler: And? Blade: They can't be trusted. Edward Furlong: I want to shift the focus away from myself by asking you to watch Whistler. This will be dealt with again later on. Blade meets the Blood Pack. None of them have very intimidating names. Add to the fact that the only one we recognize is Ron Pearlman, and we know that these vampires are going to die. Ron Pearlman: I am a badass, too. My role as the main villain in the Adventures of Captain Zoom have no doubt prepared me for such a badass role. Blade proceeds to make a fool of Ron Pearlman and place an explosive inside of his head. Blade: I am badass. Audience: He is badass. Blade and the Blood Pack get weapons from Whistler and Skud (Edward Furlong) and go to hunt Nomac at the only place worthy of being filmed... A VAMPIRE NIGHTCLUB! Scene 5: At the nightclub, Blade and the Blood Pack patrol with weapons out in plain sight. They won't look conspicuous at all, will they? Audience: It doesn't matter, because Blade is badass. Blade: That's right, I'm badass. Good to see you've been paying attention. Nomac and some of his converts (he turned them into the new breed of vampire that Nomac is) infiltrate the club. The new breed and the Blood Pack fight. An Irish vampire named Preacher is eaten by the new breed. The New Breed is f*cked up because it opens it's entire face to eat you. New Breed: We are f*cked up. Audience: They are f*cked up. Blade: I'm still a badass. Audience: Oh yeah. The New Breed is immune to silver and garlic. Blood Pack: Crap. A little Asian vampire with a sword beats up one of the new breed, but the thing escapes. The Asian vampire doesn't say a word, which is an attempt to make him as badass as Blade, but not nearly good enough. Blade: What he said. A big guy with a wicked face tattoo is beaten up by a new breed thing about half his size. His girlfriend comes to the rescue. Girlfriend: Are you okay? Boyfriend: Yes. I shall preserve my strong man image, even though I'll turn into a new breed vampire within thirty minutes. Girlfriend: Okay. Ron Pearlman and another vampire shoot a new breed vampire with about 500 rounds of ammunition AND THE BITCH CAN STILL RUN AWAY! Ron Pearlman: This is fun. Other Vampire: This is fun. Audience: Lord forbid they run out of ammo. Ron Pearlman: We won't. As the only recognizable Blood Pack member, I must survive for later in the movie, where I shall pester Blade some more. Audience: Okay. Outside, Whisper is nowhere to be found, and Edward Furlong's van is being attacked by the New Breed. Edward Furlong turns on the UV lights, which are the only thing that still kill the new breed. Edward Furlong: Use your UV lights! Ron Pearlman: Okay. Ron Pearlman shines his UV light on the new breed vampire that is alive, but not able to move anymore. It explodes is a bright blue ball of light. Audience: Whoa. They splatter better and cleaner than the normal vampires. Blade and Nyssa run into Nomac. Blade shoots at Nomac, but does no damage. With fighting skill, Blade chases Nomac away. Underground, Blade and Nomac fight. They are evenly matched, and neither can defeat the other with standard methods (Blade with silver, garlic, and the vampirism cure; Nomac with brute force) Blade chases nomac away with sunlight. Nomac: Damn sunlight! Blade: This will not be our last fight. I need to prove to Nomac how badass I am, but that has to be saved for later because right now, there's an actual plot we must get to. Audience: Plot? Oh yeah... this movie has one. Very well.... Blade goes back upstairs, and talks with Nyssa. Blade: Why didn't you tell me they were immune to silver and garlic? Nyssa: We didn't know. Blade: If you did, would you have told me? Audience: Wow. Interesting. I have a feeling this same thing will come into play later. Blade finishes off Preacher who is changing into a new breed, and they head back to Blade's hideout... but first, Whistler has a present for them, a dying new breed vampire. They take the corpse with them. Scene 6: Nyssa performs an autopsy on the new breed. Its metabolism burns out too fast, and its heart is encased in bone only vulnerable on the sides. Blade: This will no doubt come into play later. Scene 7: A scene with Blade and Nyssa. Nothing interesting happens, moving on. Scene 8: UV bombs are produced as a joint effort by Whistler and Edward Furlong. The Blood Pack is given new tight reflective leather outfits that make them look better, but not as badass as Blade. Blade: I'm still the most badass. Audience: Yes, we know. Blade almost cares about Nyssa's safety, but hey, she's still a vampire, right? Scene 9: With Edward Furlong safely back at HQ, Whistler accompanies Blade and the Blood Pack down into the sewers that the new breed hide in. Audience: This doesn't make sense. Edward Furlong: It will.... They all split into groups. Blade, Nyssa, and another black vampire that won't survive go down one route. The vampire couple and the Asian vampire go down another path. Whistler goes away with Ron Pearlman and Other Vampire. The audience is already suspicious, because the vampires HATE Whistler. Ron Pearlman: Other Vampire, beat Whistler to sh*t while I go on ahead. Other Vampire: Okay. Whistler: F*ck. Whistler releases pheramones for the new breed, and then gets beaten up by the Other Vampire. The boyfriend vampire becomes a new breed, eats the Asian, and then chases his girlfried to eat her. The girlfriend sacrifices herself and kills her boyfriend by opening the sewers to allow sunlight to blow them both up. Audience: Touching... I think. Ron Pearlman runs into a group of new breed vampires and sets up the UB time bomb that will flood the sewers with sunlight. The handle is stuck. Ron Pearlman: Damn! Blade, Nyssa, and the black vampire go into the asswater. The other vampire is eaten. Nyssa runs away while Blade detonates one UV flashbang and kills the five new breed with a quick swipe of his sword. Blade: Remember, I am badass. Audience: We get the point! Other Vampire is still beating up on Whistler. Then, the new breed comes and eats Other Vampire, ignoring Whistler. Whistler backs away, not wishing to press his luck. Everyone must regroup. The regroup consists of Blade, Nyssa, and Ron Pearlman. They meet at the main junction of sewer pipes, and shoot away the new breed, even though the silver bullets can only somewhat hurt them. Blade: We're going to need that UV time bomb. Ron Pearlman: I left it down that tube. Feel free to go and get it. Blade: Very well, get as far away from here as you can, I'll set it off. Blade has the majority of new breed chase him to the UV bomb. Ron Pearlman and Nyssa still have problems of their own. Blade gets to the bomb and has trouble with arming it. Ron Pearlman: By the way, Blade, did I tell you that the lever's stuck? Audience: This makes it clear that Ron Pearlman is not a badass, just a prick. Blade fights off the New Breed as best he can whiile trying to arm the bomb. Meanwhile, Nyssa and Ron Pearlman are cornered and fighting off the new breed. After a while of shooting, Ron Pearlman decides that he is badass again and tries to fight the legions of new breed with just two knives. They overtake him. Meanwhile, Blade kicks the bomb and it arms. In ten seconds, the UV blast fills the tunnel. All are killed. Nyssa is mortally wounded. Whistler is accosted by Nomac, the only new breed vampire yet to die in this movie. Nomac: I'll spare your life, and you'll tell Blade my story and give him this ring. Blade: Okay, time for the sensitive part. Blade allows Nyssa to drink his blood to heal. Just then, familiars (humans controlled by the vampires) led by the EVIL LAWYER flood the sewers and shock Blade to unconsciousness. Whistler escapes the sewers only to meet guns to his face. Whistler: Uh... hello.... Scene 10: Back at Vampire Nation HQ, Blade wakes up with Whistler and Edward Furlong. Ron Pearlman stands there with a shotgun in hand. He managed to survive the new breed attacks and UV radiation. Also there is the Old Vampire. Old Vampire: Hello, Blade. I am going to explain the plot to you, as is a cliche of villains who will utterly fail by the end of their movies. You see, I want to create the perfect vampire immune to silver, garlic, and sunlight. Nomac's a step in the ring direction, but he just has a problem with trying to kill us. Whistler throws the ring at the Old Vampire. Whistler: Nomac gave me this ring. What's it mean? In the only time the Old Vampire breaks character, he says; Old Vampire: (OOC)What the f*ck? You haven't figured it out yet? He's my son, geez. (IC)Okay, I'm done being evil, I have to go now. Old Vampire leaves, and Ron Pearlman stays, smiling. Ron Pearlman: Okay, Blade, here's where I'm going to reiterate that I'm a prick and not a badass. Ron Pearlman shoots Blade's legs out from under him with the shotgun. Blade takes out the detonator and presses the button to blow the explosive inside Ron Pearlman's head. The bomb doesn't go off. Blade: What the f*ck? Edward Furlong steps forward as Ron Pearlman takes the bomb out of his head. Edward Furlong: Oh, this is the time where I tell you that I'm a familiar working for them. The bomb's a dud, and I've been in league with the vampires all the time. Ron Pearlman tosses the dud explosive to Edward Furlong. Edward Furlong: Vampires will rule the world, something I need to go into excruciatingly boring detail to talk about, and I must explain how I want to be a pet instead of cattle. So, what do you have to say? Blade: Okay, well I knew about you, and the bomb's not a dud. Edward Furlong looks down at the explosive in his hand. Edward Furlong: F*ck.... With a press of the detonator, Edward Furlong blows up into millions of little pieces. Ron Pearlman then calls familiars to shock Blade. Audience: You know, if those shock sticks shorted out, they'd just be poking him. And why can't Ron Pearlman just kick Blade around himself. Ron Pearlman: BECAUSE! Scene 11: Whistler is being beaten up by Ron Pearlman. Whistler escapes and takes down Ron Pearlman with a kick to the groin and a punch to the face. No one in the audience believes this. Audience: I don't believe this. Blade is taken to a room where the familiars, led by the lawyer, strap Blade down and start extracting his blood to create the master race of vampires. Blade: Didn't we already do this in the first movie? For the same purpose? Lawyer: Yes, but you won't be strapped down for nearly as long. Blade: Okay. By the way, Nomac's coming after you guys. Lawyer: Don't be silly. The alarms sound on cue, with Nomac single-handedly killing and eating an entire battalion of familiars. Nomac: I'm badass, too. Audience: He's badass, too. Blade: I'm still badass. Audience: You're bleeding to death on a table. Whistler: Not for long. Whistler kills the lawyer and takes Blade to the blood bath room they started in. Ron Pearlman shoots at them. Blade manages to fall haphazardly into the bloodbath. Ron Pearlman thinks he's won, but Blade, who's lost ungodly amounts of blood, as well as the feeling in his legs, steps out of the blood bath in ten seconds good as new, dripping with tasty, tasty blood. Familiars flood the room with shock sticks. A fight ensues. Blade beats the hell out of the familiars, kills quite a few of them, and even gets off a good vertical suplex. Blade then kills Ron Pearlman by slicing him up the middle with his badass sword. Blade: I am badass. Audience: I nearly forgot. Nice job. Whistler tosses Blade his shades. Yes, this is badass indeed. Scene 12: Nomac gets to the Old Vampire and Nyssa, but they escape. Nomac eats more familiars, who should know by now that their bullets only make Nomac angry. Nomac: Morons.... Nomac gorges himself this day. Scene 13: Nomac eats Old Vampire and just bites Nyssa. Blade comes in and they fight. It's even at first, then Nomac takes over and beats the sh*t out of Blade. Then Blade comes back, but Nomac comes back again with a series of vicious elbow drops. Nomac breaks Blade's sword. More fighting, with Blade fighting defensively. Finally, Blade stabs Nomac through his heart, and Nomac dies. Then, a touching scene with Blade taking Nyssa to die by sunlight, as per her request. Scene 14: Blade kills Pervert Vampire in London's version of the Red Light District. Blade: You didn't think I forgot about you? Never forget that I am badass. Audience: He is badass.... Okay, now about my opponents. 1) The Rush is a man I've dealt with before, though never in a wrestling match. Well, there was that one match on March 10th, but Stormbringer interfered and I was never able to make it to the ring. Anyway, I helped Akiyama test The Rush to become the God of Crunk. If Akiyama hadn't been called away on business, the test would have been much more... hazardous. Earlier on, though, we were opponents in a different way. Rush was working for NTICW, myself for EW. We had spoken many times in PTC forums, defending the honor of our respctive feds. We had even done so most eloquently, and most professionally. Given this, we were signed to a match at the tentative PTC interfed pay-per-view, something that never happened. I'm looking forward to facing The Rush in the ring. 2) Clyde I've only faced him once, earning a victory over the Burned One, or whatever he's got for a nickname. He fought hard as well, but his attention just wasn't on it, I don't think. He'll have to pay attention with three other men in the ring. But nevertheless, my highest profile win to date has been winning cleanly against Clyde. Oh, and Clyde, if I choose not to address you in any other RPs I do, that's my choice. I DON'T HAVE TO TALK TO YOU EVERY WAKING MOMENT UNTIL THE MATCH! YOU'RE NOT A VERY GOOD CONVERSATIONALIST, ANYWAY! But that's going past what I originally planned to say, so I'll drop that. 3) Chet King I've never faced him, haven't talked to him... I know he's done more than me, and it will be great to get in the ring with him and fight him. Maybe even put him down for the submission or pinfall. 4) The End Whatever will happen shall happen, but I don't expect to lose this match on Turmoil. I'm the one that's got everything to prove and nothing to lose. I'm the one that will walk into Turmoil the fourth challenger, and the one who shall walk out of Turmoil the victor. Promo over P.S. Don't worry, my usual line of RPs is ready and I'll be posting the next Father/Son RP soon enough.
