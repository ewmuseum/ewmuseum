Anarchy Returns, Dudes - Part 2 - KerPLUNK!

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 12, 2002 Adam The Anarchist: You know, the world is tilted sideways and spins on an axis. Sort of like a human soul does. We all spin around trying to find where we should stop spinning, only to pick the wrong spot. We end up useless really. Everything is predetermined within ourselves, like we're some kind of living, breathing computers. Our creator, the bastard that he is, has programmed us to serve his feeble little goals. Well, I don't fall into that category, my friend. I rebel against my programming of having to do certain things and having some inner voice tell me not to do something just because it's 'wrong'. You know what I have to say about that?! [The man named Adam pulls a tiny red stick from a plastic cylinder, holding it up with a wicked smile upon his face. Suddenly a cluster of marbles fall from the red sticks holding them up and they all pile together at the bottom of the cylinder. Adam, with his long black hair flowing behind him, turns to a small boy who is picking his nose] Adam: Pure Anarchy, my friend. [Adam then drops the red stick holding a sick looking smile on his face and then he walks away from the child. The boy seems confused by all of this, not really taking a turn at KerPlunk. He removes the finger from his nose and stares at the pile of marbles at the bottom and then smiles brightly] Boy: I WIN! [He then commences to eat the booger at the end of his index finger] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: This is TOTALLY bogus, dude. [The surfing legend and one half of Cowabunga Connection walks into Herbie's Flashbacks with a very frustrated look on his tan face. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, the other half of Cowabunga Connection and resident stoner, walks with a purpose into the store. Harry's smile is so chipper, so bright, and not his usual style. Usually the smile on his face is put there from days upon days of taking hits from his bong. This smile is put there on his own free will, that's because he's going out with The Olsen Twins!] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I've got to buy them a present, man. [Herbies Flashbacks is filled with many toys, games, and really anything you can think of from the 70's and 80's. He-Man figures, Transformers, Go-Bots, GI Joe's, Voltron, and so much more. Harry begins browsing over the old-time Barbie collection that Herbie has, apparently his wife was really into them, or so HE says] TTT: Dude, you don't need to spend all of this green on these babes, dude. They are just toys. [Harry seems sensitive about the subject, giving Tommy a very stern look] Harry: They just aren't toys, man. They are collectors items and they gave me this far out list. [Harry pulls out a crumbled up piece of paper with cheese stains on the back of it. He hands it over to his brother and tag team partner. Tommy notices that there are three things on the list: Malibu Barbie, Fun Time Barbie, and Barbie's Super Playhouse. Tommy looks at the Barbie shelf spying the three items on the list. He notices the prices on the wall next to them and does the fast math inside his most bodacious mind] TTT: 1,000 Dollars?!?! Totally heinous, dude! [Harry takes the list back, not really paying attention to Tommy freaking out about a little bit of money] Harry: It's an investment, man. TTT: Harry, you do know that I have a lot of things on my mind these days. With all this bogus stuff going on from my past, the GTT, and those totally bogus Pranksters With Attitude, I'm feeling most frustrated with our positions in PCW. We do have that match against newcomers, Total Violation. They may be new to PCW, but they aren't new to a wrestling ring, compadre. We're really going to have to put our gnarly game faces on for this one to keep our momentum toward the tag team titles. [Harry just stares at the Barbie collection letting his mind suck him into the far out rewards he will receieve once the chicks see their presents. The long, sweaty night that will commence will be unbelevable. Both of them wanting a piece of the 'Heavy Metal' and that is exactly what they will get. All he has to do is buy the twins their far out Barbie stuff and it will come to be. Far Out] TTT: Dude...are you even listening to me? [Harry still stares forward thinking about his far out reward] TTT: DUDE! [Harry then jumps and looks over angrily] Harry: Don't do that, man. [As Tommy is about to lay it all on the line about Harry's relationship with the Olsen Twins, Adam The Anarchist approaches the two men. Tommy backs off of Harry and nods to Adam, a long time friend of the Cowabunga's. Adam nods to the two men with his Tool T-shirt being worn proudly] Adam: I noticed the insanity that was about to be unleashed over here. I had to make my aura known to my friends. I need to tell you some important details about life, about the way we live as human beings. [Tommy seems confused about something] TTT: Dude, did you get rid of your constipation?? [Adam stares at Tommy with much hate in his eyes. Yes, Adam was constipated for quite some time, but thanks to a surgical procedure that kept him sidelined for many months from performing his Anarchy, he is quite bitter about the whole thing] Adam: Yes. TTT: Congratulations, compadre. Adam: Thanks. I just wanted you both to know that God does not exist. He is just an imaginary character created by the bigots and politicians to make many of us be controlled. They use mind control to take our money away from us, use it to lie to us, and they make us work endlessly for no true rewards. It's all God's fault. He's the one to blame for all of this. So, if you believe in God I'm telling you to stop immediately..... Believe In Nothing. [Tommy and Harry both look to each other for a few moments. They then look back to Adam, and then once again to each other] TTT: What in the name of religion are you talking about, dude?! Adam: Did you ever watch a sea otter do the funky chicken? [Now both of the Cowabunga's look extremely confused] TTT: No, can't say that we have, dude. Adam: Well, once you do then you'll know what I'm talking about. Until then...you'll have to live just like the rest. You'll be programmed and controlled. I have to go now. My mind is telling me that I should hang out with you guys, but I know thats what was programmed in my head so I must leave this place. Rebel against my feelings to break free of the spell. [Harry thinks about Adam's last comment] Harry: But what if you were programmed to rebel against your first thought, and to actually LEAVE the store, man? [Adam is silent, just staring directly at Harry with a very blank stare] Adam: Spiral Out, Keeping Going. [Adam then walks out of the store, the bell ringing as he opens the door. Tommy and Harry watch him leave knowing there is a large screw loose somewhere in that compadres head] TTT: He's really gone off the deep end, dude. Ever since he crapped himself in front of those supermodels at my one party he's never quite been the same. Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry then grabs the Barbie items that he needs to get for the Olsen Twins. Tommy just lets out a long sigh, not really agreeing with this] TTT: Are you really sure this is what you want to do? [Harry smiles brightly] Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry then takes the stuff up to the counter as a middle aged man with thick glasses begins ringing Harry up. The man looks so happy, probably because he's selling 1,000 dollars worth of Barbie junk to some poor sucker who doesn't really know any better. Tommy notices the brightness radiating from his brother. He's never seen Harry so happy before in his entire life. Harry is no grumpy person, but for the past 5 days Harry has been floating on top of the world since meeting up with the Olsen Twins. Tommy knows that he should feel bodacious that his brother is feeling gnarly, but there is something that Tommy really doesn't trust about the whole situation] Herbie: Thanks for shopping here, guys. [As Herbie finishes his sentence Adam The Anarchist leaps through the front window of Herbie's store. Adam's skinny body slams onto the floor like a ton of bricks] Herbie: MY WINDOW!!! [Adam immediately stands up, throwing up the devil horns] Adam: Pure anarchy! [He then runs out of the store] TTT: Totally bogus, dudes! [Fade to black]
