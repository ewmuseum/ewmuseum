Title: Powell taping the show? Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 28th Friendship. It can be a rather double-edged sword. Friendship can help you, and it can hinder you. Especially in the field of romance. Ask Byron Calver about that sometime. Regardless, male friends tend to look at romance in one way, with one question. 'So, did ya nail her yet?' William S. Powell is a 20 something young man, so he is no different. After observing things about Reginald V. Lovecraft he never knew before, he now looks to gain a one-up on his friend. With several cameras in his hand, and a spare key given to him by Reginald for emergencies, William S. Powell is off to the Emperor's Suite of Caesar's Palace. Video evidence of the deflowering of Reginald Lovecraft would make big news in the political journals. Heck, he could even make C-SPAN, his ultimate dream! But, Powell must work fast! Even as he enters Caesar's Palace, Reginald V. Lovecraft and Katherine are on their way back from Great Adventure. Can Powell rig the room in time? We shall see.. ::The Master Debator enters Caesar's Palace, a foolishly evil grin on his face. Ever since his failure at getting footage of Reginald's aborted attempt to ask out Katherine mere days after joining PCW, he has lusted after finding compromising footage of the two. Why? As proved by Bill Clinton, evidence of politicians engaged in sexual activity has a way of drawing attention and viewership. As Lovecraft's friend, Powell stands to get lots of time on TV....and this means...:: William S. Powell: My God, the publicity! I'll get national attention! The National Republican Convention could see me, and I could get nominated for a national position! It's typical William S. Powell...using others for his own gain. He's learned the political game quite well. William S. Powell: I can't help but feel a little bad for the poor guy...I mean, he's a virgin at the age of 47! And now, that he's finally going to get some...it's going to be plastered all over the news! ::Powell chuckles:: William S. Powell: Still, he's a good tag team partner...and an outstanding warrior. To all of those in that Schoolhouse deathmatch or whateverthefuck it is, I warn you, DO NOT overlook the Governor of PCW.....Lovecraft's a dangerous mofo. Which is why I'm kind of worried. ::A nervous glance from Powell.:: William S. Powell: If Lovecraft manages to figure out what the hell I'm doing, he will tear me a new rear end. He is not a wrestler, per se. He has his own fighting art. As the Neo Void God, Akira Akiyama has Koro-Dragon-Waza, or whatever the hell it is, Mr. Lovecraft uses the deadly art of 'I'mgoingtokicktheabsolutelivingfuckoutofy ouusinganymeansnecesaryandthenpissonyourgrave'-fu. Basically, this means he runs around and uses the one type of offense he knows, punching, and weapons, combined with the few wrestling moves I taught him. He has quite the temper.. ::A worried Powell climbs atop the plush sofa in the suite in order to affix a camera into a small corner. The camera seems to be top-of-the-line spy equipment. How Powell got a hold of it shall of course, remain classified information.:: William S. Powell: In essence, my tag team partner is preparing himself for war.... ::Suddenly, Powell hears a loud noise.:: William S. Powell: FUCK! HE'S HERE!! WHAT THE HELL CAN I DO?! ::Powell quickly runs into the room's spacious closet. Reginald V. Lovecraft and Katherine Willis enter, tired after their long drive.:: Katherine Willis: Are you okay? You look exhausted! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I am, a bit....too many coasters. Katherine Willis: Poor baby! You;ll be much more exhausted shortly.... Reginald V. Lovecraft: What do you-- ::Katherine has heard enough speaking for the day, as she presses her mouth to Reginald's passionately. William begins whispering to himself.:: William S. Powell: Wow, that's a quite interesting way of preparing for an extreme match... ::He takes out a small notepad.:: William S. Powell: Note to self.. GET MYSELF AN INTERN SOON! Damnit, it's been way too long since I had sex.. ::Powell sighs to himself, as he hopes his one camera is capturing everything accurately:: William S. Powell: PCW....Reginald V. Lovecraft is a dangerous man. Even more so if he finds out I'm taping him. Prepare to meet him in battle, for he is your next Extreme Champion. Until then, Katherine...please.....make it kinky! MY FUTURE LIES ON THIS TAPE! ::Fade to...:: Paid for by Politically Incorrect. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
