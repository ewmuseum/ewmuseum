Empire Saga™ Part Six: A demonstration for the troops…

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/8/02 Empire Saga™ Part Six: A demonstration for the troops… ‘‘The Chief Anarchist’ John Crichton…the Television Champion. That was his moniker before his match with Pendragon on last Tuesday’s of Turmoil…he met the rookie head on in the ring and ‘the rookie’ made him tap out. Thought by some, as the greatest PCW Television Champion of all time, he lost the title that night, but on the other hand so did Pendragon. Syko the Goblin stole the belt from him soon after the victory…an action worthy of an unworthy champion. The loss to a rookie almost destroyed Crichton’s confidence in his wrestling abilities…how he beat other, more experienced opponents, if he couldn’t defeat a rookie. The answer unquestionably baffled him…but he would get his answer on this weeks Tuesday Turmoil. What if he lost the match? Would the loss confirm his suspicions? These questions needed answers…but any such answers could only be developed after Turmoil…gearing up for his Hardcore match against the much respected Violence Jack…after all Violence Jack was the current PCW Extreme Champion…perhaps if he proved himself a worthy competitor…he might receive a shot at the title…he doubted it, but he very much depended upon hope.’ [{The camera opens up within the very familiar surroundings of the main meeting room within the front of John Crichton’s organisation, Pastelli’s warehouse. The room is full of Crichton’s ‘employee’s’, who are talking to one and other wonder why they had been called to yet another meeting. Crichton stood behind his formal wooden stand once more, as the last of the people enter the building by the door at the back, closely guarded by Henchmen George and Reginald. The last few seats are filled and Crichton’s takes this a cue to begin.{] Crichton: Hello everybody, and thank you for coming to another one of my meetings…I imagine that they are becoming tedious to you all… [{His audience begin to nod in confirmation, which is closely followed by a few murmured remarks that even Crichton’s sensitive hearing could not pick up.{] Crichton: Well this meeting, I assure you, is a very important one… [{A few more murmurs, which suggest some of the crowd, disagree with his last statement.{] Crichton: I hear that some of you doubt the importance of this meeting, well let me begin with then first of two statements. We have a spy in our midst! [{The crowd are in uproar at such a statement…some shouting abuse…some simply shaking their heads in disbelief.{] Crichton: The second statement is…I’ve been in heated business talks with one of our rivals…a Don Rico Darnelli… [{The crowd gasp in shock{] Crichton: And I’ve ‘persuaded’ him to let me be the ‘owner’ of his territory from now on… [{More gasps from the crowd{] Crichton: During the business talks I realised he knew some information that could of only come from within these four walls…hence my suspicions of a spy. [{More murmuring in disbelief{] Crichton: After the conversation he confirmed as much when I ‘pressed the issue’ with him. He even gave me the name of the person as JOEL SAMUELS [{Pointing at him in the front row{] [{Joel’s face is covered with shock, as the crowd looks at him suspiciously{] Crichton: Joel Samuel’s has been giving out information about this organisation to the Darnelli family and God knows who else…I’ve made sure that Darnelli won’t tell another soul, but I don’t know who else this maggot has told, so… [{Crichton nods at his Two henchmen, who have already taken positions near the traitor, and they grab Joel roughly by the armpits and drag him onto the stage. He kicks and screams, but to no avail…the crowd are silenced, waiting for the next turn of events to take place. Crichton steps out from behind his formal wooden stand and menacingly walks over to Joel. Joel is still lay upon the floor in both confusion and shock at what is happening to him and does not notice Crichton drawing ever nearer. In fact, he only fully realises when Crichton’s left boot stops a millimetre from his head. Joel slowly looks up as Crichton’s massive hand attaches itself to his neck. Crichton then simply lifts him, high up into the air and looks back at the crowd{] Crichton: Because of this…this traitor our business will have to re-invent itself for a while, change ‘trading’ routes and essentially shake up each and all business ventures. We have to spread out and get different contacts…abroad if necessary…why? Because this little butt plug has jeopardised this whole operation by blabbing to the wrong people at the wrong time… [{The majority of the crowd nod at this proposal, with few clapping and cheering the proposal, as Crichton walks to the centre of the stage…Joel still raised high above his head.{] Crichton: I cannot and will not tolerate any kind of betrayal within this organisation and if any one of you can’t handle that I suggest you leave now…because this butt plug has forced me to perform a demonstration of what happens to traitors… [{Crichton then hurls Joel towards George and Reginald. Joel smashes against the wall before the henchmen grab hold of him.{] Crichton: George…Reginald…take care of him, would you? Henchman George: Yes Boss. [{The two henchmen drag Joel away through the double doors and into the ‘warehouse’{] Crichton: When you betray this organisation, I’ll take it as a personal insult…understood? [{A variety of nods immediately follow this last statement.{] Crichton: Dismissed. As soon as the traitor was revealed Dan Lyons breathed a sigh of relief. He was beginning to think that his guise upon infiltrating this business organisation had been discovered…in truth he had done well to last this long, but he hadn’t expected it to take this long to turn his ‘troops’ against him. He had a hold over them by using fear tactics…this wouldn’t be as easy as he initially thought…he had managed to convince a few of them that Crichton wasn’t the man to lead them, but he doubted that they would have the same stand point after this little demonstration…that brought him back to square one…how could he take this Empire down? He really didn’t have an answer for this question, but he sure as hell would continue to look for one.
