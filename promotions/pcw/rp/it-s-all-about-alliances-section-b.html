Date: (Promo #5 if totaling with The End) Recapping the story thus far… Akiyama’s parents, Yoshihiro and Yukina Akiyama, and his cousin, Japanese pop princess Ayumi Hamasaki, have graced Jahana Castle with their presence, and everyone is immediately being struck down in their path. Meanwhile, the Maxis family have come calling to the Blitz dojo, and between AJ Maxis’ scheming, Ryoko Godai’s lethargic demeanor, and Mina’s sarcastic attitude, this will be a visit to remember for a long time. And on top of that, PCW wrestlers are placing more effort in shilling cans of soda than they are trying to win Alliances. Shows where the priorities lie with some people… Anyway… Title: Family Time The room was silent. The three guests sat on one side of the low table, Yoshihiro, Ayumi, and Yukina side by side on the silver sofa. On the other side, Akiyama sat in between Cyclonica and Anarchy on the adjacent silver sofa, Jenna sitting on the arm of the sofa next to Anarchy, while Glacia leaned her back against the back of the sofa peering outside of the room. The walls were black, and the carpet was a vibrant gray. In between the two sofas was a steel coffee table, which currently held a crystal dish of lifesavers. The silence… Akiyama motioned his hand towards the candy, and Ayu exhaled like she was holding her breath. “About time!” she reached down and plucked an orange lifesaver, throwing it into her mouth with a smile. Yuki looked at her son, the ice being broken somewhat. “So these are your girls, Akira?” she asked, though the answer was obvious. Akiyama nodded, his back leaned against the sofa with his arms folded across his chest. He had retrieved his mesh shirt in the meantime and put it on, refusing to go topless the entire time. Anarchy and Cyclonica were slouched back in similar positions as their father, both of them staring carelessly around the room. Then Yuki asked the question of the day. “So who’s their mother?” Cyclonica sighed, the white vapor trail floating out of her lips. Anarchy slowly looked away as Akiyama thumbed back at Glacia. Glacia looked back slightly, noticing that everyone was looking at her. “What?” she asked curtly. “You are these girls’ mother?” Yoshihiro questioned her. Glacia replied with an ice blue nail pointing down at Cyclonica. “Just her.” then she thumbed over her shoulder towards Anarchy. “I’m not responsible for that.” “Hey!” Anarchy glared up at Glacia, but her icy glare soon pushed Ana’s look away. Yuki shook her head slowly. “So then who is her mother, Akira?” He shrugged. No shame in admitting when you don’t know. If some of the men going into battle soon would only do that. They did now know what they were getting into…they did not know if they would win or lose… They didn’t even know if they’d make it out of the god’s clutches in one piece. Unsure notions remain floating through their minds, like the stale aftertaste of a bad nightmare. “You don’t know who her mother is?” Yoshihiro was both taken back and ashamed. “I thought I taught you better than that, boy!” then his attention turned from Akiyama to Anarchy. “Miss, who is your mother? Refresh my son’s memory for him.” “We DO have names, you know.” Cyclonica muttered, tensing her fingers together in a bored state. “Excuse me?” Yuki’s attention was set on her now…and that wasn’t a good thing. Cyclonica looked up from her slouched position, staring at her grandmother with an attitude. “I said, that we do have names, you know.” Lot of men did nothing but mutter and groan when other people weren’t paying attention to them, for whatever reason. Maybe someone didn’t pay attention to him or her because what he or she had to say was insignificant…or what he or she had to say was nothing more than utter nonsense. Duke Williams was always on the receiving end of lack of attention being placed on him. Lord knows that whenever he gets on the microphone, the portion of the audience whose necks aren’t red know that it’s time to do one of three things: 1- Go take a piss, just like how Duke pisses on the English language when he opens his mouth. 2- Go buy some food or souvenirs…anything to take their minds off the drivel he’s muttering. 3- Go get a blowjob from Brandi in the janitor’s closet next to the Nachos stand. If something’s going to get sucked besides the ratings, it might as well be your shaft. You might want to write that down…you know…Just in case. Yuki gave Cyclonica a haughty look. “Is that right?” Cyclonica sat up, resting her forearms against her thighs as she leaned forward. “So says the Divine Prophetess.” “Who?” Ayu asked, her mouth busy sucking on the candy in her mouth, much like the HIT sucks on the breast of quality television, thus sucking all the entertainment out and saving none for the people. It’s a shame…such a math genius and he cannot figure out that: TI2 (HIT + Clyde + Chef – Darkstar(subtract the dead weight))+ Alliances = Defeat Cyclonica glared at Ayu quickly. “Me…that’s me…I’m the Divine Prophetess.” “What is that your cult title?” Yuki snapped back at Cyclonica. Akiyama sighed as Cyclonica stared in shock at her. Cyclonica shook her head. “NO----well yes, it IS my title in CODE, but that has nothing to do with why I am called that! I am the Divine Prophetess because I dream the future to come!” Yuki’s response was nothing more than a blank stare. “…….is your daughter on drugs, Akira?” That was the last straw according to Cyclonica. “Look here…”Nana”…” Cyclonica leaned forward more, pointing her finger at Yuki as she spoke. “I don’t know who you think you are to come waltzing in her and taking control of things, but you’ve got another thing coming. We are the Celestials…superior frickin beings! We don’t answer to anyone but ourselves! Do you understand me?” Cyclonica had sharp eyes…but even she didn’t see the fist coming at her face. ……….But Akiyama did……… Cyclonica sat still, her eyes wide in stun as her father’s hand stood in between her and a KO from Mrs. Akiyama. Yuki’s glaring eyes turned from Cyclonica to Akiyama. “You’ve spoiled them, boy!” Yoshihiro accused in a scolding voice. “That one is nothing but a smart mouthed bossy one,” he pointed at Cyclonica. “And the other one is a weakling!” Anarchy stuck her tongue out at him as he pointed at her. Yuki pulled her fist back, prompting Akiyama to take his hand back. She stood up slowly, smoothing out the wrinkles in her skirt. “You dare repel your mother’s fist?” she asked calmly, though there was nothing but spite filling her voice. “Your mother who trained you from birth into the man you are today? Do you think you’re too big to still get SPANKED?” Akiyama was next to stand, looming over his mother. He glared down at her, and with a slow sadistic grin on his face; he nodded slowly. Yoshihiro jumped up next, holding a fist up at his son. “FOOLISH BOY!! YOUR ARROGANCE IS BEYOND BELIEF!” Ayu was shadowed under the standing Akiyama family. She stood up calmly and placed her hands on both Yukina and Yoshihiro’s shoulders. “Now, now…we’re all family here. There’s no need to get angry. Why don’t we all sit down at have a nice fruity lifesaver?” “SIT DOWN!” By order of the Akiyamas, Ayu plopped back down and reached for the candy bowl. If it was only that easy to shut Ellie up… No…wait…put a phallic shaped object in her face, and she’ll clamp down on it like Maggie Simpson on a pacifier. That’s one problem solved. Yoshihiro turned back to his son after that little detour. “Nobody likes a martial artist with a bad attitude! You need some spiritual training!” Yuki nodded. “But first our stomachs are in need of some pancakes.” Her finger went straight to Jenna. “You…go fetch us some of the pancakes from the kitchen.” “I’m not the maid!” Jenna fumed. Cyclonica begged to differ. “You sure? You clean out Anarchy’s p---“ “SHUT UP, Cyc!!!” Anarchy glared at her sister. Cyclonica returned the look, along with her tongue waggling from her mouth. “You shut up, ANA!” “I’m gonna rip that tongue out of your mouth!” Anarchy motioned just how she would do it. Cyclonica shook her head. “I’m sure you’d put it to good use.” Jenna sighed and walked out of the room, heading into the kitchen. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Hana Maxis stood still, her arms raised at in front of her, her palms turned outward, and her legs bent and grounded. She stood looking ahead in the backyard of the Blitz estate, between the training hall on the left and the garden on the right. In between those two mentioned articles, was a shaded path leading down into a quaint forest trail. Inphino walked around her, his arms folded behind him as he analyzed the young Maxis woman. He raised her arm slightly before reaching down to her thigh and extending her right leg out further. With a nod, he faced her and matched her stance automatically. His face was blank as he instructed her. “Kick.” One word was all she needed to react. Her leg shot out, arcing perfectly and making contact with Inphino’s open palm. A hard impact was made against his hand, but his arm didn’t seem to show that…not moving an inch. Hana returned her leg to the ground and remained in her stance. Over next to the house, Mina de Margo stood against the wall with red sunglasses on her face and her arms folded. She yawned as she turned her head towards the steel table, shaded by the white umbrella atop of it. Maxis and Godai sat under the shade with uninterested expressions, the black sunglasses on their faces shading their eyes from reading. “Is this why she brought us here?” Godai moaned. Maxis shrugged his shoulders as Hana shot out at Inphino’s palm with a punch, the smack audible from his position at the table. “Probably. You know how she is with him. Him and Akiyama…” “But Akiyama I can understand,” his wife responded as she looked down into her palm pilot. “Akiyama was the one protecting her for most of her life. She grew up with him, and now that she’s on the verge of blossoming into a beautiful young specimen---.” “Specimen?” Maxis eyed her questioningly. “--- She’s bonding out with our number one enemy. Do you know how hard it will be to kill him when she’s clinging to him like a love struck stalker?” Maxis nodded slowly. “Pretty hard…I imagine. But if we get Akiyama to kill Inphino…we’re not to blame. He wants to kill him anyway…I’m sure she’d understand if he did it out of “protection” of her.” Godai raised an eyebrow at him. “Explain.” “Well…say Inphino had…immoral plots towards Hana, and Akiyama found out about them. It would be in no fault of his nor ours if Akiyama took out Inphino if he had motivation to harm Hana.” Ryoko Godai turned to her husband, removing her sunglasses and giving him an affectionate look. “That’s why I love you.” AJ patted her knee and smiled. “I know you do.” “Mom! Dad! Watch this!” Ryoko and Alexander turned back towards the yard, watching as Hana spun around and popped a roundhouse kick into Inphino’s waiting palm. She jumped up and down, a smile on her face. “Isn’t that precious.” AJ Maxis and Ryoko Godai jumped as Mina stood behind them, a droll look on her face. “How long have you been standing here?” Maxis asked nervously. Was his plot exposed? “Long enough.” Mina said, raising her nose up at them. Godai stared at her husband as she backed away from them into the kitchen. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Jenna walked back into the sitting room, where the Akiyama couple was sitting with their arms folded impatiently. Akiyama was on the other side of the room, pacing back and forth with his fists clenched. Cyclonica and Anarchy were still sitting on the sofa looking bored, now with Glacia taking Akiyama’s seat in the center. Ayu…of course…was still chewing away on lifesavers. The look Jenna received from Akiyama’s parents wasn’t too good. “Where’s breakfast?” Yuki demanded. Jenna lowered her head, straying away from their stern gazes. “Um…it’s all gone.” Yoshihiro looked to Akiyama and Cyclonica next. “WHAT?? You didn’t save some??” Yuki stood up and sighed, her hand going to her temple. “Damn. Nothing upset me more than this. I was starving! You know how long the drive is to get up here is?” She shot her finger out at Akiyama, who stopped pacing and faced his mother. “You…outside…now. Get ready for your asskicking…” She stormed out silently as Yoshihiro followed next, stopping in the doorway and glaring back at his son…and then walking out. Cyclonica stood up and threw her own glowering look into Akiyama. “THAT’S where you got that stupid catchphrase from? Your PARENTS?? That’s lame! “Get ready for your asskicking…” ugh…I can’t believe I’m related to them.” Akiyama growled as he stepped around her and headed for the courtyard…to fight his family… Yep…in his goal to remove everyone from his path towards success… …even his own parents were targets against the system.
