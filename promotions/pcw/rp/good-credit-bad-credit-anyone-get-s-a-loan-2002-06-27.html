Good credit, bad credit, anyone get's a loan

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 27, 2002 Tom Lexian's Apartment - Bedroom 8: 34 am Tom is front of a mirror in his bedroom fiddling with the tie on his shirt. Whatever he did he just couldn't get it straight. Wrap it around once, go over and down, through the hole to make a loop, pull down and... Crooked again. Dammit. 'Nat!' Natalie Dischner, the -oh so- awesome girlfriend of Tom Lexian. So sweet, so kind, so good with ties...she was currently in the kitchen cooking up breakfast. Tuesday was usually Tom's day to cook, but today the schedule took a left turn. Tom had other errands to run today, like getting SF National Bank to loan him two million dollars... * * * Natalie Dischner: [comes into the room] Tom! Legend Inc: Nat, the tie...it's not straight... Natalie Dischner: [comes over and begins messing around with the tie] I see that, Tom. Here, just hold still...[adjusts the tie]...wow, it's been awhile since I've seen you all dressed up like this. Legend Inc: Yeah, I hate doing this kind of stuff. [fiddles with the tie] Natalie Dischner: [slaps Tom's hand away and smiles] Hey, you keep your hands away so I can fix it. Legend Inc: I don't know, Nat, this whole plan with The Joker is really tricky. If I don't get that loan today the plan is basically dead. Natalie Dischner: Well he said he had everything set up right? I mean, all you have to do is go down there and sign the papers. Legend Inc: Yeah, but what if they start asking me questions? That could screw everything up. Plus, this I.D. that I have that says my name is, 'Tee Howard,' doesn't look like me very much. Natalie Dischner: Didn't he use a real picture of you? Legend Inc: Yeah, but it's way back in my early days. Back in the day when I was 'AfterShock' and I had my hair up and all of that makeup. I hardly look anything like I do now. Natalie Dischner: Well it should be good enough. Is Robert going with you? Legend Inc: Oh, you mean that dead beat friend of mine? No...he had to take his kid out to Great America today. It's some discount day or something. Natalie Dischner: It's so nice to see Brenda actually letting Robert take Brad out once in awhile now. I mean, after the divorce she wouldn't even let him in the door. Legend Inc: Tell me about it. I can't recall all the times he tried to get me to help him break into the house. Natalie Dischner: It's good for him to spend time with Brad. Anyways, I'm sure you'll do just fine by yourself at the bank. Legend Inc: [crosses his fingers] Yeah, well, here's to hoping. Natalie Dischner: [finishes the tie] There you go my handsome man, your tie is fini! Legend Inc: [inspects the tie] Nice craftsmanship, Nat. You're a regular tailor. Makes me wonder why you bother working at CBS to begin with. Natalie Dischner: Tailors make pennies. CBS is a billion dollar company. Legend Inc: So you're making billions now? Natalie Dischner: [pauses] No, but close to it. Maybe a few hundred million. Legend Inc: [laughs] Well honey, wish me luck. When I come back I hope you've woken up from your dream. Natalie Dischner: [hits Tom in the arm] When you come back I expect to be two million dollars richer. Legend Inc: [leaves] That's what I'm hoping...expectations are a different matter. * * * The Legend Mobile was purring like a kitten while it drove down Embarcadero street...well, not quite. The 98' Honda Acura had a few bumps and scratches and the engine stalled sometimes. Damn piece of junk. Plus, it looked like an old clunker compared to the glamorous cars that rode next to him headed for downtown Frisco. As he got stuck in mid-morning gridlock, Tom started to formulate the conversation with the loan officer which he was going to have. Hopefully the guy was a sports fanatic and a big baseball or football fan. That would be great - than they could talk about the SF Giants or the SF 49ers and stray away from the topic for what the loan was going to be used for. It was brilliant! The plan was fool proof and nothing would derail the plan now. Tom turned the car into the SF National Bank's parking lot which was already filling up this early in the morning. He got out and headed for the main entrance which was guarded by 3 security personnel. Tom wasn't all that surprised. SF National was the city's biggest bank and attempts to rob it have been numerous all though not that successful. As Tom entered the building he marvelled at the sheer size. Marbled floors and a towering ceiling, and already a bustling line. Tom glanced over at the loans department and saw an empty desk to sit at. He sat down and put his feet up and waited... * * * Alexandria Chantel: What the hell do you think you're doing? That's my desk! Legend Inc: [falls out of the chair and gets up quickly] Uh, oh, I'm so sorry. I was just..uhm...here, let me get that chair for you... Alexandria Chantel: [picks up the chair shaking her head] Sir, the desks are only for loan officers, so unless you are giving a loan today I suggest you sit in the customer seats. Legend Inc: [feeling like an idiot] Oh, of course. [sits down] Alexandria Chantel: [sits at the loan desk] Alright, why don't we start from the beginning. My name's Alexandria Chantel. You are? Legend Inc: My name's Tom...uh, excuse me, Tee Howard. I'm here to finalize a loan I have. [pulls out the documents] Alexandria Chantel: [accepts the documents with a puzzled look on her face] Tee Howard? That name doesn't sound familiar. Who was the loan officer who wrote up this contract? 'Oh, fuck.' Legend Inc: [stalling] Uhm...[cough]...excuse me? Alexandria Chantel: Who was the person that issued the loan contract? I need to know so I can pull out your file under her registration PIN number. 'Can't stall anymore...dammit Joker, you never told me the loan officer's name...better think quick....' Legend Inc: [thinking] I believe it was Betty....yeah...uhm, Betty...Ford? Alexandria Chantel: [looks up at Tom] Betty Ford? I don't know a Betty Ford that works here. 'Oooo, bad guess Tom...make an excuse fast...!' Legend Inc: [stuttering] Well, are you...uhm, sure? How long have you worked at this bank? Alexandria Chantel: [looks down] Well, actually, only a week. But I still don't know a Betty. 'Attack! She's vulnerable!' Legend Inc: [laughs] Only a week, huh? Well, that probably explains it then. Why don't you just call your manager and ask him or her to call down Betty so we can wrap up this loan already. I'm a very busy man, you understand? Alexandria Chantel: Sir, I'm quite capable of handling this transaction myself. We don't have to call Betty down just because.... Legend Inc: [interrupting]...Excellent! Then we can finish this deal and I can head down to the ballpark for that Giants game...do you watch baseball? Alexandria Chantel: Actually no, I hate sports...[checks Tee Howard's name in the computer].... Legend Inc: Oh....that's a shame.... Alexandria Chantel: ...I have your file here, Mr. Howard. So, you're CEO of Howard Petroleum Inc.? 'CEO of a damn oil company? What the fuck was Joker thinking...' Legend Inc Well....yeah. Yeah, it's a bull market out there and I'm the bull. [laughs] Alexandria Chantel: I'd say so. With the sky rocketing gas prices I'd expect you to be doing well...[looks at the loan contract]...two million dollars? Shouldn't you have that kind of money in your back pocket? Legend Inc: I do. Alexandria Chantel: So why are you pulling out a loan? 'So why do you keep asking dumb ass questions, lady....' Legend Inc: It's actually for a short term investment. I have a sweet tip on a nice little Nasdaq stock today and I want to use this money for it. I hate using my own money...uhm, makes me feel like I'm gambling.... Alexandria Chantel Oh, I see. Well what stock is it? I also invest a bit. 'Figures...' Legend Inc: Oh yeah...uhm, Trojan. Trojan Industries. Alexandria Chantel [confused] Trojan? As in the condom company? Legend Inc: [proudly] That's right. They make fine products, don't they? Alexandria Chantel: But Trojan isn't a technology stock. 'Oops.' Legend Inc: [shifts around in his seat] Well...not yet. Today they're going to be branching out...to the internet. Alexandria Chantel: In what fashion? Legend Inc: Well, you know how porno ridden the internet can be... Alexandria Chantel: Yeah, it's disgusting. I can believe how many perverts we have in this country. Legend Inc: Yeah, well...Trojan is going to fix that by...starting an internet condom service. Alexandria Chantel: Excuse me? Legend Inc: You know what - it's way too complicated to explain. I'm sure when it gets big then you're going to want to know more, but for now just know that it's coming out today. Alexandria Chantel: I guess so...anyways, the contract looks ready. All that needs to be done is for you to sign it. 'Yes! Uh-huh, back that ass up!' Legend Inc: [eagerly grabs a pen] Just show me where to sign.... Alexandria Chantel: [pulls the contract back and stares at the I.D.] Wait a second...this is you? Legend Inc: [feeling nervous] Yes...yes, of course that's me. Why do you ask? Alexandria Chantel: [inspects the I.D. closer] Is that barbed wire in the background? Legend Inc: [looks at the picture and recognizes the background of the hardcore cage he had just fought in] Why, yes that is? You're awfully perceptive - you should be an optometrist. Alexandria Chantel: No, I hate staring into people's eyes....but I'm curious...where exactly did you take that photo? Legend Inc: [makes a grab for the contract] That's a really wild story that I'd love to tell you..after I sign my loan.... Alexandria Chantel: Oh, of course. [gives Tom the contract to sign] Legend Inc: [signs the contract and breaths a sigh of relief] Whew...glad that's over with... Alexandria Chantel: Now back to this picture.... Ring! Ring! Ring! Legend Inc: [answers his cell phone] Excuse me a second, I have someone on my phone...Hello? The Joker: Get out of there now before she asks you any questions. Legend Inc: What? Wait, who is this? The Joker: Your fairy god mother....who the hell do you think it is, moron? Legend Inc: [realizes who it is] Oh, it's you. The Joker: Yeah, it's me. Get out of there now. I have already set up a meeting with you and Jimmy the Fish in an hour. Legend Inc: Alright, well, how do you suggest I get out of a particular predicament? The Joker: I don't know - tell her you have some illness or something. Legend Inc: Ok, I'll see you later. [hangs up the phone and stands up] I'm so sorry, we're going to have to finish this conversation later. I'm not feeling very well. Alexandria Chantel: [hands Tom the loan contract] Oh, what's wrong? Legend Inc: [takes the documents] Uhm, I'm very constipated right now...I'm sorry. It's really bad. [walks out of the bank in a hurry] * * * Tom walked out of the banked and sighed. He had made it. Perhaps the toughest part of the mission was over...now all he had to do was meet Jimmy the Fish at....wait, where was he supposed to meet him? Oh, that bastard...son of a bitch didn't even tell him where to meet Jimmy the Fish. How was he supposed to place the bet now? Tom shook his head in disgust. The contract was worthless if there was nothing to bet it on. Tom headed to his car and got in quickly slamming the door shut. He started up the car and turned on the stereo which was oddly switched to 'Tape' mode. As he turned the key a tape began to play. The Jokers Voice:: 'Alright Lexian, I know what you were probably thinking when you left the bank. You realized that I hadn't given you the address where you were supposed to meet Jimmy the Fish, right? Don't lie, I know what an idiot you are. You didn't even realize that I hadn't given you any directions when I was on the phone with you. Anyways, you have to meet Jimmy the Fish at the McDonalds at King and 5th avenue. Go there now - chances are he's early. Give him the loan contract and tell him you want to beat it all on Germany to win it. That's it. Once Germany wins tomorrow just collect your money and I'll give you further instructions. Oh, and Lexian, don't screw this up.' The tape cut off leaving Tom with an odd smirk on his face. What the hell was this, Mission Impossible? The bugged phones and the taped instructions really weren't too creative on The Joker's part. Anyways, it was time to go. Tom was feeling a bit hungry anyways. He brought the car back into traffic and headed for the Mickey D that The Joker had instructed him to go to. Why McDonalds, Tom wondered. Didn't the guy have a club or some casino like other cool bookies? No, he had some fast food joint - and a really bad one at that. Tom pulled the car into the McDonalds parking lot and got out of the car. The parking lot was hardly full so obviously there wasn't much of a breakfast crowd. Tom walked into the store immediately being hit by the fresh smells of grease and five dollar bills. He looked around and immediately spotted a largely obese man eating an Egg McMuffin in a corner booth. He had a black shirt on and brown leather pants. He could be a bookie. Or a slob. Or both. * * * Legend Inc: [walks up to the man] Jimmy the Fish? Jimmy the Fish: [looks up] Yeah, that's me...who are you? Legend Inc: [sits down] My name's Tee Howard. I believe our common associate has arranged this meeting. Jimmy the Fish: [wipes his mouth] Ah yes. So you're the agent for...what's his name...Legend Inc.? Legend Inc: That's right. Jimmy the Fish: Ok, and if I am correct, I believe that he wants to bet two million dollars on the Germany vs USA World cup game. Legend Inc: He most certainly does. Jimmy the Fish: [pulls out a piece of paper] What team exactly does he predict will win? Legend Inc: He wants to beat two million on....[thinks]....Germany. Jimmy the Fish: [looks up] So two mill on the Germans? Legend Inc: [pauses] Yes. Jimmy the Fish: [writes down the information] And you do have the first payment, correct? Legend Inc: [hands Jimmy the loan contract] There it is, all two million of it. Jimmy the Fish: [accepts the papers] Now, let's discuss the payment procedure. I give 2:1 odds that Germany will win, thus if they win, I'll give you four million smackeroos. If Germany loses, you will lose your two million dollars as well as another two million over a six month period. Legend Inc: I see. Jimmy the Fish: If you don't pay....well, that's for you to find out for yourself. But a word of advice: just pay. Legend Inc I understand. Now, how will I collect the money if I win? Jimmy the Fish: IF you win, then I will arrange a meeting with you to collect your money. Legend Inc: [stands up] Ok, I believe everything is set then. Jimmy the Fish: That's what it looks like. It's a pleasure doing business with you Mr. Howard. I hope we have a long prosperous relationship in the future. Legend Inc: Yes, one can only hope.
