WELCOME BACK RAH FINALE AND A HALF(Reasons inside)

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-2002 OOC:'I want to kill my mom right now...seriously....I love her with all my heart but 30 more minutes was all it would have took to have made this memorable. Now it's just gonna be shined crap and I hate it...I really hate that feeling, cause no matter how much you polisha turd...it's still a turd. I will bring this party series to a close in a later RP and I'm pissed tonight isn't the night. Juan, if you win, all power to ya man, you earned it, I'm jsut glad Iput up some kind of a fight. Enjoy the turd everyone...' ..::/::..When we last left our hero in the second installment of his welcome back party, we found our hero on none other than the very stage of the club, mic in hand along with the three members of NTICW's only boy-band, Pretty Cool Guys. Our hero's guests are all captivated in utter awe at what they're seeing, everyone from OSW's Corg to NTICW's Syn fixated upon stage with their jaws dropped and in pure awe as the lights begin to focus in on the quartet now domineering the stage. All four men are dressed in matching tangerine orange jumpsuits, each with 'PCG' written in neon green letters in the front and their respective names written on the back which read off individually as Rich, Ryan, Sean, and the honoray member of the group for the night, Rent-A-Hero, who took the liberty of complimenting the jumpsuit with a matching orange Puck mask. From afar, even with the club lights at a low tint, Rent-A-Hero's 'Lois Lane', his one and only, his PS2 of passion...(Who gives me this material??) ...Amy Campbell watches on from a far off club booth, a slight smile overcoming her face as she brushes back a strand of her fiery red hair away from her eyes and focuses in on our her, Timo happening to catch a glance at Amy's 'glossy schoolgirl' look and commenting..::/::.. Timo:'I don't think Jason would be too happy about that look Missy..' *snickers*Amy:'Jason who....' Timo:'Yup...she wants him more than Gary Coleman wants to grow.' ..::/::..Timo continues to laugh under his breath at Amy, who is usually never the type to get swept up in a river of emotion as she keeps her eyes fixated on our hero as he taps the mic a few times, a loud feedback signal letting him know it's on. With a Herculean clearing of his lungs, our hero looks out to the hot PTC crowd and beckons on the mic, the crowd either utterly stupified, or utterly fascinated to hear what it is he has to say..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'GREETINGS GOOD PARTY-GOERS!! First off, I'd like to thank you all for coming to this Herculean shin-dig planned by none other than the Krayzie One himself and it truly is an honor to be graced by the presence of you all. It warms a special place in my heart of hearts...' Crowd:'WOOOO!! WOOO!! YEEEAAAA!!' Rent-A-Hero:'But speaking of hearts....and NO I don't mean Juan 'Golden Heart' Borro, the same guy who's gonna recieve a KAMEHAMEHA BEATDOWN RAY by yours truly on Sabatoge, for after he is smackered and beaten into a pulp for daring to go toe to toe with the hero...he will be soon dubbed Juan 'Broken Heart' Borro, because I'm gonna remove that gold plated exterior, grind up that soft insides, and send you on your way with that heart in two piece knowing good and well that you were bested and defeated by the PCW's one and only champion...belt or no belt....RENT-A-HERO!! Anyways, the heart I'm referring to is my own fellow party-goers...and for quite a while, my heart has been misplaced and has been aching for comfort...solace even. It took TODAY to realize something that I coulda figured out a long time ago...that I'm in love.' *Locks eyes with Amy even while being so far back, Amy reciprocating with a soft and loving look as well* 'I'm in love with the same woman who I once saw in my eyes once as just a mere sidekick...as just a friend...but I've come to realize that she's so much more...more than that...she is my love....she is my heart. She is the one that gives me reason for going on and tonight, what better way to prove that love for her....' Talena:'SHUT UP AND LET PCG SING ALREADY, GOD THEY ARE SO HOT!! AHHHH!!!' ..::/::..The three core members of PCG wave modestly at the goth princess, Talena, Darkstar looking to his girlfriend with a sneer as she modestly tries not to notice, realizing she let her love for the boy band slip unexpectedly..::/::.. Darkstar:'Somebodies been feeding you some of that N'Sync crap...well your due for a brainwashing when we get home...and you know what that means...NOFX, System, need I say more...' Talena:'Yes hun...but if I'm gonna get washed anyways, might as well enjoy this da** song! GO AHEAD PCG....SIIIIING!!'Rent-A-Hero:'Well, I guess we don't wanna keep the crowd waiting so I've asked Pretty Cool Guys to back me up on a song we co-wrote and it's called....'Amy, Be My Lois Lane'. HIT IT GUYS!!'..::/::..The lights begin to flicker in a pattern of blue, red, green, and purple as a techno/pop hybrid beat kicks in, filling the speakers of the club as they flow all throughout the structure. The crowd looks to be moving to the beat in delight, Amy blushing at the mere mention of a song named after her as she watches on with a warm feeling in her heart, the songs beat kicking up as PCG along with Rent-A-Hero begin to step rhythmically to the beat in a parrallel line, Ryan starting off the song.... AMY, BE MY LOIS LANE written by PCG and Rent-A-HeroRyan-Girl you know you look so good, Girl you look just like you should, Don't you know that your so fine, Red-headed lady won't you be MIIIIIIIINE!! Sean:'Her kisses taste like clear Gummi Bears, I melt like hot butter with just one of her stares, It drives me crazy she's not in my RI-OTCH(REACH said in the sense you'd say 'biotch'), IT HURTS SO MUCH, I WANNA CRY LIKE A BIOOOOOTCH!!'Sean And RAH-What am I supposed to do, How many more lonely nights can I make it through, YOU CAN MAKE IT STOP, YOU CAN TAKE AWAY THE PAAAAAIN, AMY, SWEET SWEET AMY, WON'T YOU BE MY LOIS LAAAANE!!'*Beat stops for a minute* Ryan And Rich-'You betta save him gurl...' *Beat picks back up* ..::/::..The vast majority of the crowd can't help but fall in love with the spirited song as they begin cheering out of their mind for the quartet as they break into another dance number in the middle of the stage. Amy has pratically fallen in love all over again with our spandex enwrapped, singing and dancing hero as a single tear droplet runs down her right eye, wiping it away quickly so no one will take any notice as she watch on in continues awe, love, and flattery at the act of her hero. At that moment, a random clubbing female a in a tight blue mini-skirt giggles lightly, looking towards the stage and saying quietly to herself..::/::.. Clubbing Female:'That Hero guys looks absolutely cute...I wonder if a shine a Bat-Signal into my apartment..will he come. Hmmm....'Amy:*Turns head slowly to the side with homicidal tendacies showing* 'WHAT WAS THAT BI**H?!' ..::/::..In that instant, Amy tackles the female, a slew of punches and kicks following as only the sound of her screams and Amy's truck driver vocabulary fill the air at their table...but unbeknownst to everyone...an evil lurks in the rafters, that evil being none other than.....THE HIT!!! Yes folks, the same HIT who has tormented our hero through various other RP's...and it looks to not to have stopped here as he walks the rafters carefully, looking downward with evil intentions towards our hero. WHAT SHALL TAKE PLACE?! FIND OUT SAME RENT-A-TIME, SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!! (Mom...you will pay. Bet on it.)
