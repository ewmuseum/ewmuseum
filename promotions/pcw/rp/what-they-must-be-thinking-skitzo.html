See? You're all just doing your own thing, you don't care about getting anywhere. No common advancement. I've sat through too much of it. I remember the No Ladders match last year where you fought over each other over who would grab the belts, frustratiting even SYKO THE GOBLIN to the point that he just shoved you away. Or what about Alliances? Remember getting eliminated 12 times in a single match? That wasn't us, that was 12 other guys. Besides, that was against 4 good wrestlers. Vilas Lavagna jobbed to David Arquette. I'd expect that from a a midget luchadore from Thailand who makes Gloria Estefan look massive, but there was no excuse from you all. We're Skitzo! We can see everywhere, hear everything, know everything, if we work together. But that won't happen. You can't work together. You're incapable of working toward the advancement of Skitzo. That's why you need me. You need a dominant personality, an alpha female to take over control. We still have a voice! Yes, but I can block you out. No you can't. We''l rise against you! We will? Geez, you don't have to yell, guys. This police officer is staring at us. I told you we shouldn't be talking to each other in public. Go away, nothing to see here! Det starter med Totally Tubular Tommy. Han tvilt Skitzo, at Skitzo var et trussel, og han falt til kraften av prosthetic phallus. Vill slik også all av Skitzos motstandere føler hans hennes vrede. Skitzo er først personen da Frisco holde Extreme og World Titles samtidig. Å, ja.. Frisco. Hva et lyserødt. Skitzo, Skitzo... selv Skitzo. Hvilken som helst av dem, eller resten av oss, sparker ham omkring lik en fotballball. Han godkjenner sannsynlig av analogien, fotball er en av de eneste tingene til side fra olje som berettiger landet som ikke være ødelagt og gjenoppbygd for bruk som en ferieflekk for middelklasse Americans. Han slår ikke Skitzo på hans best dag, men han er på dødrekke eller noe nå, likevel. Og resten av denne føderasjonen er full av vitser og washouts. PCW tilhører Skitzo. What the hell? Seriously, how do we have Skitzos that speak other languages fluently when they're languages none of us have ever heard? I don't know, don't care. You all are just noise. I am going to succeed, despite being associated with you all. I'll hold the Extreme Title forever, and use it to pay for the operation to get a vagina. WHAT?! WHAT?! WHAT?! WHAT?! WHAT?! I don't need to discuss such things with you all. I'm the dominant Skitzo, remember? Remember what? Ugh... I'm hungry. Shut up. You shut up. Don't make me come over there. Bring it, bitch! I'll punch the shit out of you. Try it! Ow! Why'd you hit me, you were aiming for him! I hate you all... We love you, too. Sorry, I was in the bathroom. What's going on? SHUT UP!
