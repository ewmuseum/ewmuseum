Warrior Chronicles 3

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-24-02 The celestial. He feels he is alone in the world. But surrounded by many, his loneness is extinct. His power is gone. The celestial. Strength in one. Strength in none. Power to fight… no will to win. The celestial. He thinks he is brave. He knows he is a coward. He will fight. He’ll try to triumph. He’ll put everything he has into the battle. He might win, he might loose. But in the end, he’ll come away with… nothing. Their powers… so exotic. So incredible. At least so it seems. And so the people believe. And so the people are afraid. And in the end they bow down before these magnificent powers and throw themselves at the mercy of the oppressor. Fools. All of them. But lucky fools. For all the while, while the celestial strikes fear into the hearts of all… there is an even greater power looking out from above. This power has no rights, takes no rights, and gives no rights. He is there not by choice, but bound by a commitment never spoken, nor written or read. A commitment so powerful, it could only take place in the soul. A commitment like no other. The commitment of a warrior. The warrior may seem but a mortal from the first glance, but if a mortal is a coward then a mortal he is not. If a mortal does not have the strength to succeed, and the will to go on… then the idea of a mortal means nothing to the warrior. He is a mortal, however. The warrior has the pride, the mind, and the honor. The fight is on and the fight has been won. The victor doesn’t matter. There is a true victor nonetheless. The celestial. Glory ever fleeting. The day of destruction is approaching. The day when the veil is lifted to reveal beneath the extraordinary beauty of an angel the hideous face of a monster. The warrior can see through that veil. He sees what lies beneath, and he protects the mortals. Death before defeat. The celestial. So respected. So feared. But they know the time is coming… They know the warrior sees them… They know they must stop him. Shin Yagyuu. Cyclonia. Anarchy. The End. Akiyama. We know their names. We’ve seen their faces. And they want us to believe… to believe in the ‘inevitable’ fate of the mortals. The celestial. Run! Hide! Fear! Run from the terror. Hide from the madness. Fear not the being, but the war it has brought forth. The war was coming. The war was always coming. And finally it hit… So many victims it held. So many casualties inflicted. So much PAIN! ANGUISH! SUFFERING! Hell… fury… In the end, there was only one true victor. The warrior prevailed. And so it ended, there and then, and once again the warrior left to pursue the quiet life that only a warrior could seek. And there was nothing left of the villain. Nothing but the nightmares and memories… of The celestial.
