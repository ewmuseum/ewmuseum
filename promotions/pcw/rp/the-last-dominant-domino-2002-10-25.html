The Last Dominant Domino

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.25.02 O’Hare Airport, Northwest Chicago… -The constant thrum of multi-million dollar engines fill the air out on the runway, workmen and lackeys in gray jumpsuits running left and right as they load luggage and refuel the massive machines that grace the cloudless Chicago sky. A loading gate locks on to the fuselage of one of the planes, ushering a flood of tourists and businessmen into the body proper. The entire scene creates an image of organized chaos, so much action happening, yet coming together with the practiced flow of clockwork.- -Inside is a much different story.- -With his black wrestling duffel bag sitting in the seat beside him, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins lounges back in a cushioned chair, waiting in Gate 17 with only a handful of other people. His red bandanna tied snugly over his straight dirty-blonde hair, Rollins folds his hands in his lap and grins, locking gazes with the camera dead-on.- Renegade: Less than twelve hours until go time, when the first step up the PCW ladder is set before two hungry individuals. Who’s got the bigger appitite, thought… that’s the real question. -His grin widens as he relaxes further, an aura of supreme confidence pouring from his every movement and nuance, his eyes glinting with a deep assurance. The hustle and bustle of the world’s largest airport mills about behind him, but the crowd doesn’t seem to phase him in the slightest as he continues, almost jovial.- Renegade: I’m glad you’re out havin’ your fun while you can, Mark. That is, while you can still walk. The dusty atmosphere of the Middle East isn’t exactly my climate of choice, but I suppose it works for some people. I’ve visualized this night since day one, Mr. Michigan, and I hope you brought your flying shoes, ‘cause the trip across the Atlantic ain’t the only air time you’re gonna get. -His smile fading, a quizzical look fills his eyes as he leans forward, his hands gripping the arm rests by his sides.- Renegade: But enough about Fusion tonight. (his tone darkens) Let me take the PCW faithful back a few weeks, to the final rounds of the illustrious Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. I busted my ass to carve out a name for myself in that tournament… and guess what? (he motions around the limited waiting area) It paid off in the end. In “the end,” how poetic. -A bitter laughs escapes from his lips, his mouth twisting with disgust.- Renegade: I didn’t just beat my opponents, oh no. I slaughtered them. Mike Meyhu, Bradley, Jerms, and even PCW’s own Jack Valentine had their egos crushed like a tinfoil can when they stepped in the ring with yours truly. But then… he showed up. Skylar Green, everybody’s beloved Extreme Champion The End. -Relaxing once more, The Renegade leans back as the view begins to pan out, encompassing the gray plastic coffee table in front of him. And the dozens of dominoes that sit atop its smooth surface.- -Lined up in one huge triangle, the dominoes dominate the table like a black plague, covering nearly every inch with their small rectangular bodies. Surveying the intricate construction, Rich Rollins puts his face closer, his expression an odd mixture of reluctance, suspicion, cockiness, and confidence. He runs his hand slowly above them, gently gliding along the perfect triangular shape without touching a one.- Renegade: Imagine that these little pieces of polished wood represents the entire PCW locker room. Here (he indicates a random domino), we have Xavier Kannon, there’s Rush Mason, and maybe even Akiyama over in the corner. Bundled together in a massive pattern, these dominoes, when provoked, knock one another down until only one is left. The last dominant domino, if you will; it’s the nature of competition, the friction between men to become the king of the mountain we call Sports Entertainment culminating into a clusterfuck of chaos. It doesn’t take much… -Tipping a single domino, Rollins watches as a path is cut straight through the middle of the pattern, dominoes falling in perfect sync until reaching the pinnacle of the pyramid, knocking down the single point of the entire construction.- Renegade: … to start a landslide. One after another, rolling through the order like clockwork, the inertia alone is enough to take any one of these dominoes to the end of the road. But dominoes aren’t the same as people, oh no. People are much, much different. -Slowly looking upward, The Renegade once again stares straight into the camera, those dual blue orbs glowing with an inner light.- Renegade: People have ambition. People have pride. People have determination, a sense of self-preservation that refuses to let them just walk away and give it up. Dominoes only follow the laws of gravity, knocking one another down in turn until nothing is left but a mess. A wooden body can’t stand up to the passion of someone with everything to prove, everything to gain… and everything to lose. It’s a twisted, dangerous balancing act we play around here and I’ve kept myself balanced for years, because nobody has been able to knock me off my perch, save Skylar Green. You think you’re the guy to match The End’s tenacity, Mark? You think runnin’ your mouth can match the intensity of my bare fist? Or my strength of will? Sometimes it’s tough to figure out which one is the stronger of the two. But in a few hours, we’ll all find out. -Grabbing his duffel, Rich Rollins slings the bag over his shoulder as he lays a forearm down on one end of the table. In one smooth motion, he sweeps across the table like an avalanche, sending the dozens of dominoes flying off the table to the bright red carpet below. Straightening himself, he looks to the camera one last time as the grin returns to the corners of his mouth, a self-satisfied smile.- “We’ll all just have to find out.” ~Fin~
