The Story of 'Krazie'

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9 ********Story of “Krazie”******** (The scene opens in the San Jose, California Holiday Inn with Krazie standing on the balcony in the early hours of morning. He has a towel wrapped around his waist and his straw cowboy hat on his head. In the room we see the sheets are ruffled and strewn all over the room. The suitcases are opened and lying on the bed, the television is on and the bathroom door is closed.) Krazie: “All in two days!? I sign a contract, get booked for Friday, then get booked for Sunday!” (Krazie slumps down with his forearms perched on the balcony railing. He just looks on at the sky thinking.) Krazie: “Multiple Man, Syko the Goblin… two people that are far from being considered PCW Rookies. I guess it’s time I earn my place here in PCW.” (Krazie smiles as he thinks about what a win over The Multiple Man and Syko the Goblin could bring him.) Krazie: “What a way to start a career! A victory over The Multiple Man followed by a victory over Syko the Goblin!” (His smile is washed away as he thinks of the opposite. He thinks what a loss to both men could bring to the table.) Krazie: “What a way to start a career! Or should I say what a way to end a career! A loss to the Multiple Man followed by the job to Syko the Goblin!” (The bathroom door swings open as Tiffany walks out holding a small washcloth over her lower private area. She has a smirk on her face and a slight twinkle in her eyes. She walks to the balcony door and stick her head out clearing her throat getting Krazie’s attention.) Tiffany: “What’d you do with all the towel?” (Krazie looks back at her holding that tiny wash cloth and can’t stop from laughing. He stands there and shrugs as Tiffany just shakes her head playfully.) Krazie: “What there aren’t any towels in the bathroom?” (Tiffany shakes her head again as Krazie crosses his arm across his chest and props his other arm on the crossed arm. He puts his head in his hand and looks like he is deep in thought.) Krazie: “I’d offer mine but it’s already wet and it’s sort of being used right now.” (Krazie winks at her as she steps foot out onto the balcony. Krazie stands there smiling but in the blink of an eye Tiffany is holding his towel and he is standing there hanging freely.) Krazie: “What the….” (Tiffany doesn’t stop there as she runs back inside and slides the balcony door closed and locked. She stands there with the towel in one hand and the washcloth in the other. Krazie just standing there trying to open the glass door.) Tiffany: “What? I can’t hear you? Did you want in? No? Ok well when you feel like coming in you can just let yourself in.” (Tiffany turns around laughing as Krazie just stands there watching her make her way to the bathroom. The scene fades as Krazie just stands there buck naked and free.) ******Intermission****** (The scene re-opens in the Shark Tank arena where PCW Ring Crew members set up for Friday Fusion. They unpack the crates, build the ring and set up the electrical wiring all the while Kid Krazie is unseen sitting in the nose bleed section all by himself. He is sitting back watching the workers work and thinking to himself.) Krazie: “With all the thinking I do, I really need to start talking to people! I have never witnessed anyone that thinks to themselves as much as I do. Maybe that’s the real reason why they called me “Krazie” back in high-school!?” (Krazie thinks back as he remembers how he got the nickname Krazie.) ******FLASH-BACK****** (The flashback begins in a two-story high school Natatorium (pool area). There on the first floor of the massive room standing around chanting are about sixty kids pumping their fists in the air.) Kids: “JUMP! JUMP! JUMP!” (There sitting on the rail of the second floor balcony over looking the clear blue water of the pool is none other than Kid Krazie. He is a freshman in high school attempting to be the center of attention. He is hanging on to the rail for deal life as the kids begin to chant louder. Krazie can’t tear his eyes away from the pool, as he gets ready.) Kids: “JUMP! JUMP! JUMP!” (Krazie stands on the rail with unremarkable balance. With one last look to the pool he falls forward falling through the air. His arms are spread as the crowd of kids go silent! After a few seconds of silence there is a loud splash and the kids all grimace after witnessing the impact!) Kids: “OOOOHHHHH!!!!” (Krazie surfaces and makes his way to the side ladder. As he pulls himself up the crowd of kids grimace again as they see the bright ruby red stomach and chest of Kid Krazie. The crowd of kids are silent as they stare at Krazie making his way to the bleachers clutching his stomach in unbearable pain. Then as Krazie grabs a towel and puts it over his head the crowd of kids erupt.) Kids: “CRAZY! CRAZY! CRAZY!” (Out of nowhere the doors to the Natatorium bursts open and the principal and assistant principal come running in with some P.E. Coaches. Kids scatter left and right avoiding the “law” leaving Krazie to fend for himself. As kids run away one stops and shakes Krazie’s hand.) Kid: “Man, you are one crazy kid!” Krazie: “And don’t you forget it!” (The flashback shifts from the Natatorium to the office of Principal Gordon. Gordon is sitting behind his desk not looking too happy as Krazie is standing there dripping wet still with a ruby red stomach and chest area.) Principal Gordon: “Why would someone do a stunt like that?” Krazie: “They said I wouldn’t do it…” Principal Gordon: “So what? Just because they said you wouldn’t, you did it anyway?” Krazie: “Yeah! I had to prove them wrong…” Principal Gordon: “What am I going to tell your father? ‘Yes Mr. Thompson your son has just been suspended from school due to the belly flop he did in the Natatorium?’ I mean come on son, what am I supposed to do?” Krazie: “You got to do what you got to do…” (The Principal just shakes his head and sighs as he looks at Krazie standing there in pain.) Principal Gordon: “Alright, go ahead and wait out in the hall…” (As Krazie turns and makes his way to the door the Principal stops him. Krazie turns back around with a look of pride on his face.) Principal Gordon: “Was it worth it?” (Krazie doesn’t waste any time for thinking he straightens his back and clenches his jaw as he answers.) Krazie: “Yes sir!” Principal Gordon: “My last question is did you get anything out of this stunt?” Krazie: “Besides pain sir?” (The Principal smiles and nods.) Krazie: “I got respect… and more importantly I got a name!” Principal Gordon: “And what name would that be?” Krazie: “Kid Krazie, sir!” (Krazie leaves the room as the Principal just sits back shaking his head.)
