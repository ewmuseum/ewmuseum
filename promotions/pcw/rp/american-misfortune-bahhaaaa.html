American Misfortune...BAHHAAAA!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #1 PPV OOC: Kinda short, but there's more to come, don't worry. All the capitalists seem to have forgotten about Alexis Parshin, the Uzbek Annihilator. However, do not think that he’s gone. In fact, the game has yet to even begin… “BAAAAAHAAAAAAAAAAAA!!!” The booming laugh of Alexis Parshin comes loud and clear as our scene opens up. We see Alexis and his trusted friend Raghab Karszat sitting in a movie theater, where a few other spectators stare at the huge screen, while Alexis and Raghab both laugh loudly. Alexis is wearing a leather Uzbek military jacket over a shirt with Arabic characters on it and featuring the Twin Towers. Raghab, or what can be seen of the chubby man, is wearing a sweater that makes the associate of Parshin look somewhat like a demented sheep. “My my, that was certainly a wonderful scene, was it not Raghab?” Alexis speaks loudly, even in the theater, which causes some of the other spectators to look at the large man, but upon seeing Parshin’s size, no one wants to say anything to the massive man. Raghab nods back to him, noting that he is not large enough to warrant the same kind of respect from the movie’s audience. Our shot circles around to show what the movie screen is. The movie happens to be the film adaptation of Tom Clancy’s The Sum of All Fears and the audience has just seen the city of Baltimore be leveled by a right-wing Neo-Fascist’s low-grade nuclear bomb. “That man, while somewhat confused with this Nazism, is a true hero.” Alexis speaks of the bomb’s owner as a normal human being would speak of Martin Luther King Jr. or Ghandi. “Indeed he is, Konstantin. We can sympathize with him. The Uzbek people have suffered long with no help or respect from America or their Russian friends.” Alexis nods and looks up at the screen to see a hospital where blast victims are being treated. “Ha, look at those pathetic Americans Raghab! They are no more then pathetic insects now, I wonder how they appreciate it!” Raghab looks around at the increasingly annoyed crowd, and whispers to the large man, “Konstantin, perhaps you should be a bit quieter. These people do not exactly enjoy the volume at which you are speaking.” Alexis booms back in a tremendously loud voice, “They do not like the volume of my voice?! Well then let them tell me!” The man stands up and Raghab gulps in fear as he addresses the entire audience, “If any of you pathetic Americans have a problem with me, then by Allah’s grace tell me now!” The fear in the other audience members is visible, and there is obviously no response as Alexis sits back down, chuckling to himself. Just as the man sits down, the entire screen becomes blurred and then goes entirely black. “Arrgggh! Your damn capitalist movie theaters are pathetic!” Alexis makes his displeasure known as he and the rest of the audience sit in the dark theater. “Konstantin, perhaps we should go meet with Kareem to discuss strategy for Gold and Glory instead of…” Alexis’ voice comes booming again back at Raghab. “Do you think I have not already thought of that, Raghab?!” Raghab shakes his head quickly and Alexis stands up. “Where are we going Konstantin?” Alexis doesn’t answer, but Raghab just waddles along behind as Alexis exits the movie theater and stands on the street outside. “Allah has given us our task Raghab. On Sunday, that task will be fulfilled partly as I destroy all of the infidels that try to stop me.” Raghab scratches his head, confused, wondering how Alexis is going to do all this by himself in a tag match. “Yes, Sunday will indeed be a fantastic day. On Sunday, the PCW tag team jewels are returned into the most deserving of hands-Allah’s…”
