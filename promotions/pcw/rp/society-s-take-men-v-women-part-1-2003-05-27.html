SOCIETY's Take: Men v. Women (Part 1)

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27, 2003 Begin Transmission. (SOCIETY withdrew himself from the Rising Star Title Match at Supremacy for a reason. Now, his reasoning will be put to the test. In the space of a week, he will face virtually the entire female roster of the PCW, consisting of Tessa Windsor-Green, Miss Moonlight, Lacuna Debris, and Brianna Washington. An interesting test for a man who, on the Falklands, didn’t so much as SEE a woman for most of his years..) “SOCIETY, this is an important week for you. Your star is rising, and you must grasp it by the throat! Your rise to the top BEGINS NOW!” (The massive SOCIETY looks across at Michael Chang, in between flipping through the pages of a Cosmopolitan magazine that he has resting in his lap.) “Yes, Mr. Chang, it will.” “What the hell is that you have in your lap?” (SOCIETY holds up the magazine, so that Chang may see the cover.) “A women’s magazine, sir.” “Why the hell are you reading Cosmopolitan? You have a match to train for!” “This IS my training! Don’t you get it, President Chang?” (Michael looked at the immense SOCIETY. He looked so sure of himself, that Chang realized a rebuke would only serve to annoy his large protégé. He decided quickly to let the Conformist explain himself.) “Explain. Now.” “Well, sir, my research has shown me that this is the typical type of magazine that women enjoy reading. By reading it, I hope to uncover any weaknesses that I may be able to exploit. Remember, there ARE no female members of the Order of Society and Logic, so this is quite foreign territory for me.” (Chang sighed aloud.) “I knew it was a mistake never letting women join.” “Why, sir?” (SOCIETY’s curiosity had been peaked. As knowledgable as he was of most matters, the female species was one matter he knew precious little about.) “Because, you need to know about women in order to defeat them.” “How so? All I have to do is turn them upside down, lock in Conformity, and they will surrender like anyone else!” (Chang had to give him that one..) “True, SOCIETY. However, there are some slight, physical differences between men and women.” “Oh. I seem to have found one.” (SOCIETY has opened to an article in Cosmopolitan about this summer’s best swimsuits. A swimsuit model is on the page, wearing a somewhat revealing bikini. SOCIETY points at the women’s chest, quizzically.) “What are those?” (Chang squirmed uncomfortably in his seat. This was NOT the line of questioning he had wanted to answer, and one he had hoped he could avoid.) “Those are breasts, SOCIETY. Or, as they are called in other parlances, boobs, jugs, and/or mammaries.” “Oh!” “Your pectoral muscles are hard and strong. A woman’s pectorals lie below and in those breasts. Understand?” “Yes, I do, Mr. Chang. Wait a minute…They’re different from us! WE MUST MAKE THEM CONFORM!” “And how do you propose to do that, SOCIETY?” (Chang braced himself, for what he expected to be a somewhat off-base answer.) “We must remove these breasts. They serve as a major divisive factor between men and women. And you know what the Tenets of the Order say!” “Differences destroy. I know.” “Well then, this shall be simple! With Sivasian Krikorian and Stone Burns alongside me, I shall ride into battle against Moonlight, Windsor-Green, and Debris, and remove their breasts by force in the name of Conformity! And then, Washington will face the same fate when I meet her in single combat!” (Chang shook his head, realizing the impossibility of SOCIETY’s plan, yet still amused by it, and glad of SOCIETY’s readiness for combat.) “There are some other differences I must show you. Come, SOCIETY, we are leaving this hotel room.” (SOCIETY rises, and allows himself to be led by Chang out of the room, as we fade to black.)
