Street Fighter 4... at last?

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/02 'That's my exact point!!' roared the voracious vampire as he stomped up and down the dressing room. It seemed out of place for him to be so loud and vocal, normally allowing Gaz to take the helm of the team. 'We've been flattened, gassed, beaten up, Hallmarked... in Vorador's name, I'm having my doubts!' Gaz looked up at the chaos being caused by his partner with concern. He placed the screwdriver and hand buzzer down onto the table next to him and rose up with a the look of the enforcer in his face. 'Easy, Tiger! Remember, we've also beaten Cowabunga Connection dressed as Ninja Turtles, knocked some stupid Romanian gymnast out, got Ellie~! to do some self-bondage acts only to be discovered by Byron Calver, sold the pictures of that over the internet on Ebay, released a musical album, won the tag team titles and all that without even so much as breaking kayfabe!' The Jester kept his distance from the livid creature, scared that he might transform into his destructive vampire shape and start wreaking a little too much havoc. Nerezza paused briefly , grinding his teeth together like a hyperactive millstone before loosening his fists. 'We still lost the tag team championships... sometimes I think it's all a conspiracy.' The vampire paused again, then his eyes lit up behind his red lenses and a look of joy tore across his lips. 'That's it!' 'What's it?' Gaz jumped back as Nerezza slammed his fist into the palm of his hand in triumph. 'Think about it - what other classic scenario does this resemble?' He said, reaching forward and shaking Gaz by the shoulders. 'GAH! errrr, I give up, let go, let go!' he exclaimed. The Angel of fear gave up his grip and made dramatic gestures with his hands. 'It's so simple a child could see it - we're in a videogame!' Gaz looked at his partner in a compound of confusion and concern. The apparently insane vampire continued his rant, gazing at the peach coloured walls blankly. 'Look this over - PCW has always had a shady history. ALL fighting tournaments are backed by evil conglomerates and corporations - in Killer Instinct, it was Ultratech. In Tekken, the Mishima Zaibatsu. Streetfighter had Shadolu. And the former owner of PCW was The Phantom Booker - isn't that a shady name?' Gaz looked on with part intrigue and part willingness to order several amounts of relaxing drugs for both himself and the mad bloodsucker in front of him. 'That means that all PCW owners must have killed thousands of wrestlers' fathers over the course of the last two years! Have you ever seen Clyde's father?' 'Of course we have, he's Dark Gimmick, that dead guy who referees every now and - ' The Emerald Menace was interrupted as a wildly wagging index finger was almost shoved up his nose and the insane eyes of the monster pressed almost into his. 'PRECISELY! HE'S DEAD! Why else would Xavier Kannon aned Clyde be fighting so fiercely? And only a few weeks ago, some poor innocent bystander by the name of Bobby Stonecutter was killed LIVE on air! You know that in a few years, a little StoneCutter child will come to this tournamen - err, wrestling organization to take revenge for his father!!!' 'Wait, wait, wait.... Rezz, you've lost the plot.' Gaz covered his eyes and waved a hand in front of his face. 'Bobby? INNOCENT? I think you're losing your grip on reality. Carry on with the story though, this is actually making sense!' Nerezza went back to his oddly concocted tale, stopping only to take out a can of Rezz Bull from his pocket and wolf it down in one greedy gulp. 'So the reason Kannon is taxing everyone is to fund his terrorist organization, the wrestling is merely a front for his true motives! Anarchy, Cyclonica and Akiyama seem to be getting into the mad family revealing part, we must be in a videogame! AND I KNOW HOW TO PROVE IT!' Nerezza took a step back, then crouched down for a moment, kicked as hard as he could at thin air and jumped up with a hard punch. As he landed, he performed a quick jab followed by a short kick, then took a breath and waited. '....What was all that about?' Gaz squawked, baffled. ' Down, heavy kick, up, heavy punch, light punch, light kick!' Nerezza cried in victory. 'One of the bosses is now available!' At that moment, a knock came at the door. Both members of P.W.A looked at each other. Nerezza, sporting a giant smirk. Gaz, looking like someone who had just tried to work out The Matrix's plot. Slowly, the lord of laughter inched towards the door, not knowing what to expect. He opened the door sharply, yanking it back and ready to respond with a superkick. Standing there was Tim Walden, carrying a briefcase. 'Oh no, the F.R.E.A.K is here.' Gaz sighed, lowering his leg. 'That's F.R.E, actually!' Walden responded, opening his briefcase full of PCW talents' tax money. 'No, F.R.E.A.K is right. Financial Reposession Editor Ass-licking Kannon!' Gaz replied with his trademark grin, sweeping his green spiked hair back. 'Look here, just give - ' he was cut off as Nerezza pounced through the doorway, gripping Tim by the collars. 'YOU! You're not Tim Walden, you're actually his genetic clone, you have all of his moves and the exact same fighting style, now what has The Front Office done with the REAL snivelling loser that normally comes to take our money? Answer me, clone!!' The madman shook the tax collector rapidly until he managed to blurt out something. 'I-I-I-I-I AM-M-M-M-M HIM-M-M-M-MM!!! 'LIAR!' Nerezza scowled. 'The real Tim Walden smells worse than this, looks twice as ugly and the only thing you have in common is his terrible fashion sense!' Walden barely loosened Nerezza's furious grip and squirmed out of the way, crawling on his hands and knees until he was out of the reach of the mind-robbed murderer. 'Rezz, he's telling the truth, look at him, not even a clone of Walden could be so wimpy! Look at how he crawls, it's the real thing!' Gaz screamed, yaking the vampire's arms away and hoping he wouldn't upset the beast too much. Before Nerezza could think things over, The Jester ran back inside and quickly re-emerged carrying a large paper bag. 'Mr Walden, please accept our apologies. We've counted out our taxes for the month and here they are. If you'd care to open the briefcase up, please?' 'Uhh... uhh...' Walden was still shaken up over his encounter with the mad vampire and simply did as he was told. Gaz turned the bag over and emptied the contents of it into the case. A shrill sound of metal made Tim jump as he looked down to see The Jester emptying thousands upon thousands of coins into the case. His jaw gaped wide open as penny after nickel flowed like a river of copper and silver into the case, piling up one after the other. 'What the hell is this!?!' he barked in disbelief. 'Well, those are british pennies and I think we have a few nickels in there. Don't worry, we counted it all out and it's accurate. Of course, we don't think you'd believe us, so you can count it all yourself. Have a nice day, Freak.' Gaz said, pointing out all the different varieties of coin. Tim turned around and with two arms, dragged the case along the ground. Nerezza poked his head around the door frame to see the heavily laden collector inching his way along the corridor. 'You know, I'll bet that money is all going to be spent on creating a super-soldier that's going to turn against it's creators. It'll probably come down to one of us having to defeat him, only after we've both been pitted against each other.' The behemoth mused. 'Rezz...' Gaz said, looking weary of the videogame references. 'Yeah?' replied the living nightmare. 'I AM RAYDEN, GOD OF THUNDER AND LIGHTNING!' he bellowed, holding his hand buzzer into the air and causing it to spark wildly. Within seconds, his partner had ran off in the opposite direction, screaming for his unlife. '...I think it's time I confiscated his Playstation.'
