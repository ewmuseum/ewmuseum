The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude(Part 9)

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-28-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 9” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 3: The Mission Begins :: BladeD is in his room, laying on his bed, thinking. He is very unsure of what is going to do and there is a lot on his mind as to what is going to go down as he gets ready for the mission of his life so far (at age 14), but the question still remains to what was in the unfinished part of that letter. I am not sure about you guys, but that really bugs me that we haven’t gotten the chance to hear the rest of the letter. At least we finally got some information about Metal Armor, but there is still more to this letter. This letter is almost a book in itself because there are so many pages and all of it contains very important information about Metal Armor. Anyway, back to BladeD. :: BladeD: Man, life can be complaining sometimes. All I want to do is go out and save the world. I mean I know I am risking my life and all, but I don’t care. Why can’t my mom understand that? Is it because she loves me so much? I don’t know, but I wish I did. BladeD’s Mother: Bladed! Bladed son! Are you in here? BladeD: Mother. I am in here. BladeD’s Mother: Can we talk? BladeD: Well, can you give me a few minutes please? I am trying to do some thinking right now…. BladeD’s Mother: Yes, I guess I can. BladeD: Thanks. BladeD’s Mother. No problem son. BladeD: I really don’t know what to think of all of this. Commander told me not to worry about it too much because if I worry about it too much, then it could hurt me too much emotionally to do a good job while I am out there. Which in the end could cost me my life, but I mean… What can I do? It is the only thing that is on my mind. Plus it isn’t the easiest thing in the world to get off a person’s mind. Saber: Can we talk? BladeD: Only if you don’t let mom know you are in here because I just asked her to leave. I am trying to some thinking, but you aren’t worrying badly over me like mom is, so I guess it is ok that you are in here. Saber: Well, you have to understand that mom loves you more than anything in this world and she is just looking out for you. BladeD: Yea, yea. Saber: At the same time, I wish I could be in your shoes. I wish I could be someone so awesome as to be able to be the one that is going to be saving the world at only the age of 14. BladeD: It is pretty awesome, but it is something that is taken seriously. It isn’t something to laugh and joke about, because if I do screw this up at all it could and most likely will be the end of the United States. Saber: What if you just have a little minor mistake? BladeD: If something minor could be the end. I am the only thing the U.S. Basically; it is either I, or nothing. Either I get this job done and done right, or boom. We are all gone. Saber: Metal Armor is that powerful? BladeD: Saber. You don’t even know the beginning of it and I wish I could tell you, but I can’t. Saber: What about that Athens guy or whoever? BladeD: He decided to get in this alone and has already headed out. Saber: But that means he is in it too!!! So it is he and you!!! Not just you!!! You lie!!! BladeD: Well, you have to understand that Athens Mir is most likely going to get caught and when he does it won’t matter because he doesn’t really have any information other than me being around. If he does tell them, I don’t think it is going to be a problem. Plus the chances of him completely this mission are slim to none. He is almost not even a factor in this. HE is there and I will probably not even see him, because I have been told that he is going to be caught very soon. That is what is predicated at least. He may not even be around I get there, so it isn’t that big of a deal. Saber: Oh. That is kind of funny, but what if he… (BladeD stops him.) BladeD: It isn’t possible so don’t even say it. Saber: Dang you. BladeD: Ha, ha, ha. Saber: Well, I still think that you should keep him in mind while you are out there. BladeD: That is why you aren’t in the position I am in. See, he is going to be in my mind, but he is going to be in the back of my mind because there are way too many other things that I have to worry about other than him. If he were any kind of a factor in this, then I would worry, but the fact of the matter is that the only thing he can do is help the situation. He can’t harm it, so I don’t need to worry about it. If he is there when I show up maybe we can work as a team again and if he isn’t there when I show up, then big deal. He is just jealous because I am better at this type of thing, then he is and he is trying to prove that he can do this kind of a job. Saber: And that isn’t possible right? BladeD: That is what I have been trying to say silly goose. Saber: Grrrrr. I hate when you call me a silly goose!!! BladeD: Well if you didn’t act like one, then I wouldn’t call you one. Saber: I hate you sometimes!!! BladeD: Ha, ha. Oh well. That isn’t my problem, so you just live with it! Anyway, you know I am going to be gone for a long, long time right? Saber: Yea, I know, but do you have any idea of how long other than a long, long time? BladeD: No I don’t. I wish I could say only a couple of months. I mean it isn’t like I am going to be gone for a year, but there is a lot more to this. First I will get there and start the mission, which is going to take a while. The mission itself will probably take a week… Maybe longer, then after that there is still more to do. I mean I have paper work down there and all kinds of crazy stuff to do. It isn’t like am going to be in there and out. Saber: I know. BladeD: Anyway, we can talk about this later. I need to do some more thinking.Saber: Ok. :: And we end there. Nothing more to say than that, so stay tuned in for the next part of this interlude, but when are we ever going to get back to the real story LOL? ::
