A Bad Day (Saga pt.16)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th December Deuce 00 Deuce § It was well after sunset when the trio had finally came back to the hotel from their shopping trip. Ben was more than relieved when a few bellhops came to take Eliza’s bags to her room for him. He generously tipped them for the trouble. 'Why don’t we all go out tonight for dinner. Sort of a double date?' Eliza said walking with Ben to the elevator. 'I’m sure you can convince Kai to come'. 'Sure, I’ll ask him, but I would think it better if Cami liu did the asking. He can’t say no to her'. Ben flashed a smile towards Cami liu. They rode the elevator to the suite, and proceed to the room. Once inside, Ben walked over to the door to Kai’s room. 'Hey Kai! You here pal?' Ben said knocking on his door. 'We’re all going out for some dinner tonight. You have to come with us? NO EXCUSES!!!' he yelled towards the door. Ben paused at the door, but got no answer. 'Hmmm, I wonder where he is?' Ben thought aloud. 'There is a note here on the counter.' Eliza pointed out. 'What’s it say?' He replied heading toward Eliza. 'I have no clue, it looks like it is written in Chinese.' She said. 'Dammit! He knows I am not to good at reading Chinese.' Ben muttered. Eliza handed him the folded note. Ben opened it and he started to read it to himself. After reading it for a minute, he looked up to the pair of women staring at him waiting for an answer. 'What the hell...' he said looking shocked. 'What does it say?' Cami liu was the first to ask. Ben just shook his head in disbelief. He reread the letter once again to make sure he did not make a mistake translating it… 'He left.' Ben said still shocked. 'Well, I can tell that much.' Eliza replied. 'Gone to where? Does it say what time he will be back?' 'That’s just it! He's GONE! I don’t think he is ever coming back.' Ben said. Cami liu’s heart sank. 'This is what it says'? Ben translates. MY FRIENDS, I AM SORRY I WILL NOT BE THERE TO SAY GOODBYE TO YOU ALL. I MUST TAKE MY LEAVE NOW. THANK YOU FOR YOU KINDNESS BEN, I WISH YOU THE BEST OF LUCK FOR ELIZA AND YOURSELF. LOOK AFTER THEM ALL. 'Something is just not right here. Kai is one not much for words, but he has never left without saying goodbye face to face.' Ben said trying to think. 'Ben? There is a crumpled up parchment here on the table with a dagger.' Cami liu pointed out. She felt as if a piece of her heart was taken away from her. 'I never got the chance to tell him,' she thought to herself Ben picked up the crumpled parchment and straightened it out so he could read it. 'Damn! More Chinese writing, but this is not Kai’s.' he said looking intently at the parchment. 'Well? What does that one say?' Eliza said impatiently. '…' Ben was speechless as he looked up from the paper. His face turned a deathly pale. 'WHAT??!!!' Eliza and Cami liu asked in unison. 'I know why he left now.' Ben paused. ' He has gone after the Demon alone.' 'Who is the hell is Demon?' Eliza asked confused. Cami liu felt her blood run cold. She remembered back to the first time that she had encountered Shoki. If not for Kai, she would have died that day. Kai had barely survived himself, and now he was heading off to face him alone. 'Remember when I told you about our Master being murdered by his own brother?' Ben said to Eliza. She nodded her head. 'Well, that is what we call him. Seems he followed us here. Now he has issued a challenge to Kai'. Ben replied with a note of worry in his voice. 'Oh my… and Kai is going off to face him alone? He killed your Master, is Kai strong enough to beat him?' She asked. 'I…I don’t know?' Ben said shaking his head. 'Why would Kai go off alone? I know it is a matter of honour, but it was your Master too.' Eliza asked. 'Shoki said that if Kai did not accept his challenge, that he would destroy all that he held dear to him. Starting with his friends…' Ben’s voice trailed off as he looked at Cami liu. Cami liu felt tears start to rise up deep within her. She fought hard to keep them suppressed. 'BEN!! You must stop Kai from throwing his life away! If this man killed you Master, he will surely kill Kai!' Eliza said with tears in her eyes. 'It does not say were he was to meet him at. It just says you know the place.' Ben replied. “Maybe I can catch up to him before he disappears completely'. 'You two stay here. There’s no telling if the Demon is still around. I’ll go out and look around for Kai.' Ben said running to the door. 'Ben?' Cami liu said starting to follow him. 'I know what he means to you, but the Demon has it in for you as well. Please just stay here with Eliza. I’ll find him.' Ben said trying to smile reassuringly. 'Be careful Ben!' Eliza said with tears in her eyes. 'Always.' He replied as he closed the door. Ben ran down the stairs, taking three at a time. His thoughts started to wander as he sped down each flight. Damn you Kai! Why did you go off with out me?? Finally reaching the bottom floor, Ben ran through the lobby, dodging people as he made his way to the front entrance. As he ran out, his thoughts had distracted him so much that he slammed into a large man. Both toppled to the ground. Ben quickly recovered to his feet. 'So sorry.' He said hastily as he turned to continue his mad dash. 'You have insulted me sir! I seek retribution for your lack of manners!' The large man said climbing to his feet. 'Look, I said I was sorry.' Ben replied turning to face the man. Ben caught a rather hard * slap * against his face from a leather glove. Ben recoiled slightly from the blow. 'WHY THE HELL DID YOU DO THAT!' Ben yelled, holding his hand to his cheek. 'You have knocked down a gentleman in your clumsy flight oaf! I challenge you to a duel of fisticuffs. For I, Sir Harrison, will know retribution. The man spoke holding his head high and proud. The man was English by his accent, and was rather well muscled in his short sleeve white shirt. Ben struggled to control his rising anger. 'Look, I said I was sorry. I am in a major rush to find someone right now, so if you will excuse me, I really have no time for this. Ben gave a short bow and turned once again to head down the street. When he suddenly felt a sharp pain to the back of his head as the man lashed out with a punch. Ben fell to the ground and rolled to stop. 'You have FOUND someone swine!' Harrison spat at him assuming a fighting stance. Ben looked up at the man who had * clocked * him upside the head and wiped a trickle of blood from the corner of his mouth. Ben’s anger finally flooded through him. He was trying to save his best friend, and this guy was holding him up. 'Alright! If you want a piece of me, THEN COME GET SOME!!!' Ben yelled as he ripped off his shirt and hopped to his feet, assuming his fighting stance. Ben’s vision was going red with rage. ? You picked the wrong man to challenge today! Ben thought to himself feeling his berserker rage fuelling him. §
