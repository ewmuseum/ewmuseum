| Kicking Your Ass |

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.24.02 | Humid hair felt heavy on the skin interludes sweet to my body. Scorching hot sun rays glistening off our skins as we sat out in ghetto smoking a blunt whole coming up with new ways to exert it from our bodies. My boy Dante sat to the side of me drinking back a forty, chilling for ironic, with me. Tall red brick buildings built around us in no certain fashion made up the projects of which I knew all to well. Buuday and Sheeca were out as usual since the sun first made its presence over the builds. Before long, I used my other lifeline of escaping the heat by taking off my wife beater and tossing it to the side. | 'It's hotter than a bitch out herre! Damn, Buuday and Sheeca betta get they asses back soon!' Squinted in eye, I looked as far as I could both ways for Sheeca and Buuday, then sitting back down and smoking my blunt again. 'Where da fuck Duzza go?' 'He prolly took his black ass home! You know it's to hot out here for us black folks! Shit, as soon as we get inside we gone had changed five darker shades already! We gone be the crispy ass motha fuckas around here pretty soon!' Stating the obvious, Dante continued, 'And if you didn't lock us out the house we wouldn't be going through this! I swear, black folks...' Mild tone irate, Dante was through. 'It's not my fault! All I kno' is dat the house wuz all foggy 'n shit from da weed clouds and then when I went to bounce out the doo'... Hell, wut da fuck did happen?' Lost myself as to past reminicing. 'Whatever, when the fuck Buuday and Sheeca coming back?! They got spare keys.' 'They prolly on they way right now.' 'I can't believe you locked the keys in the damn car Buuday! I outa kick yo ass right now!' Furious reigned the present expression on Sheeca's face as she stared down Buuday with eyes of threat. 'I'ma choke you, stab you, then KICK YOUR ASS!' 'CALM DOWN! Damn, it wuz a motha fuckin' accident! We got a damn cell phone call lil' Thug to come pick our asses da fuck up.' | Sheeca brushes her silky brown hair out of her face while getting her cell phone, on the other hand, Buuday pulls weave from her purse and begins fixing her hair. All Sheeca can think while dailing the number is, 'I'ma whoop her ghetto ass!' Other, and other, and other again. | *ringing* *ringing* *ringing* *ringing* ............ *dail tone* 'That motha fucka ain't picking up the phone! BUUDAY! I'MA KICK YOUR ASS!' | White people walking by begin to stare and some even calling for the police on the sly. | 'Oh lord, now look wut ya did! Now you KNO' them police go be rollin' up any second now dat those white people callin' da police over YO loud ass!' Thinking of an idea, 'You kno' wut, they myte jus' been wut we needed Sheeca, nice lookin' out girrl.' In the motion of leaning against the car and flicker her horse hair out of her face. 'Yup, they comin' any fuckin' tyme now.' Still trying to convince myself they were coming... The sad part is I knew they weren't coming. 'You kno' wut be sum fuck'd up shit?' 'You mean more fucked up then locking us out the damn house, melting in the sun, and being concerned about Crips coming and shooting us the fuck up? What ever could that be?' Lipped with a tone of sarcastic remarks. 'Funny *fake laugh: ha-ha-ha-ha :straight face* No.' Continuing, 'Wut I wuz gonna say is dat it'll be fucked up if they locked they damn keys 'n they car.' Followed with small laughter. 'Don't even play like that, you know good and well they shopping, well Sheeca is... Buuday is a ho.' Thinking, 'Hey, didn't you give Duzza or Ali a key to your apartment?' 'Hell motha fuckin' naw! They my doggs 'n everythin', but fuck around and come home to a house straight jacked!' I eye balled Dante giving an assumetion. 'HhmmmUh, I kno' you stole my damn chain nigga!' 'You still on that shit?! I told you, this ain't yo chain! It may say Statuz Quo on it but still, it doesn't mean it's yours!' Not realizing how off that came out. Coming out of my ugly, perplexed look given to Dante, 'Anyway. It's hot as FUCK out herre and we have to put PWA 'n check AGAIN.' 'PWA?' Goes harder in thought, 'OH, you mean CWA; Crackaz With Attitude, OH ok... We have to fight them AGAIN?! Damn...' Trying to keep a serious face, we gave in and bursted out laughing. 'Ma'am, what the hell are you doing?' An officer stood stern before Buuday and Sheeca who might as well be play writers after this scene they put on. 'Your head is great but your prices are to damn high...' Said with sly on the low, 'I mean, your head is sweating from the heat coming from the sk-yy' 'Yeah, nice one. Played off real smooth.' Licking her teeth and giving the cop a sarcastic sigh. 'Buuday, this ain't a porno movie where they like 'Hey it's a beautiful day, *sex, sex, sex, sex*' Or, 'Hey we locked our keys in the car, *sex, sex, sex, sex* 'Oh, you tryin' to be funny? Didn't work. Moving on, don't you see I'm tryin' to get us a ride home?!' 'No.' 'Well I am!' 'THEN DO IT! ...Wait, I take that back! I mean... GET US A DAMN RIDE!' 'I kno' you ain't gettin' crunk wit me!' 'That's it, I'm walking!' Giving up on Buuday. I bursted out in a uneasy laugh, 'Guess wut playa.' 'What?' 'I had the keys in my pocket the whole fuckin' time, ain't dat sum funny shit?' Afterward, getting up, unlocking the door and walking inside after hours of induring heat. 'I'MA KICK YOUR ASS!' *One mile of walking* | Buuday stolls up in the Mercades Bens to catch Sheeca walking exhausted like she just walked across a dessert. | 'Hey girrl! Guess what, you gon' laugh when you here this!' *Laughing uneasy: 'This is sooo funny!' 'Yeah, well I found the keys, they got lost when I was putting weave in my hair. Ain't dat funny girrl?' 'I'MA-KICK-YOUR-ASS!'
