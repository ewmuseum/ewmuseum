The Material Saga: Robert Smith of the Cure v2.0

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 8, 2002 The light bulbs began to flash, the glare sliding across the camera lens as if it was sliding out of bed to go to work in the morning. This is a very busy place, the photographer motions for the two people standing against the backdrop to move closer. The two people being DarkStar and Talena. Banners around the room declare 'Hot Topic; DSTA clothing line available soon!' Obviously referring to the deal with satan that DarkStar and Talena have made... Wait. Did I say Satan? I meant Hot Topic. The two strike a pose, looking almost obnoxiously well dressed for a pair of street goths. DarkStar straightened up the color of his black and red polo shirt ($16.99, only at Hot Topic) and looked past the camera for a second. He waved the man in the distance over... 'Who is it darling?' Talena looked up to DarkStar and then away to see who was walking towards them, the light from the camera made it hard to tell. 'You dont remember Robert?' DarkStar laughed, 'I guess the last time we saw him was a long time ago.' 'Robert who?' she thought for a second, 'Is it Stormbringer?!?!? I'll kill him!' DarkStar held her back, 'No hunny dumpling, its Robert Smith... of the Cure.' Almost as if it were a catchphrase, about a dozen people said 'of the cure' at the same time DarkStar did, he just looked around weirdly as Robert Smith walked up and extended his hand. 'I havent seen you in ages Gavyn!' DarkStar took his hand and shook it, he noticed they were both wearing the same shade of nail polish, 'I know Robert Smith of the Cure! What have you been up to?' Robert Smith of the Cure ran his hand through his frizzy black hair, 'Same old same old, nothing to exciting, whats new in your world.' DarkStar opened his mouth to speak, but then thought for a second, 'Which world?' There was a awkward silence. 'Oh yeah,' exlaimed Robert Smith of the Cure, 'You are the Dream King and all.' DarkStar nodded. 'Yep.' Awkward silence. 'Well I mean other then that.' 'Oh!' DarkStar thanked the gods that the awkward Robert Smith of the Cure-like silence ended, 'Well I just signed on here to put out my own line of clothing, real good money and all.' He shrugged, 'I can't help but feel like a complete tool though.' Robert Smith of the Cure motioned that he knew exactly what he was talking about, 'I know what you mean, friend, but its just something that you have to deal with.' 'I don't quite understand, Robert Smith of the Cure.' Robert Smith of the Cure put his arm around DarkStar and led him to a couch away from the lights, Talena sat down next to them while they talked. 'Well. It happens every now and then, people like us become hot comodities in the spot light, I mean, look at Marilyn Manson, Trent Reznor, Fairuza Balk, all these dark little people for brief periods become the most important thing around.' DarkStar was beginning to understand. 'Why, Gavyn, Even I was once like that.' DarkStar gasped and put his hands to his mouth in shock, 'You? You, Robert Smith of the Cure, once went through this, much like I?' Robert Smith of the Cure hesitantly nodded his head. 'Yes, young Gavyn, even I.' He went into Robert Smith of the Cure Story mode, 'You see, for a very long time, nobody liked goths at all, they were just new wavers who wore all black and put make up on. Then one day, it became cool to like goths, Siouxe and the Banshees, Bauhaus, these bands made everything pretty and romantisized it... and in the middle of it all...' 'Was I... Robert Smith of the Cure.' DarkStar sat, mouth agape, 'Wow Robert Smith of the Cure, how did you deal with it?' 'Well, Gavyn Drazail of PCW, I dealt with it the only way I knew how...' He paused to emphasize the drama. 'I made damn well sure that everyone in the world knew I was better then them.' The pair laughed, DarkStar thought to himself that he already knew he was above the worthless organ transporters that human beings were. 'Its not your fault you are this good, and its not your fault that people have started to notice how much better then them you are, this is what they want, you didn't sell out...' Robert Smith of the Cure stood up and put his hand on DarkStars shoulder, 'You bought in, now live, Gavyn, Live!!!!' DarkStar stood up, feeling much better about himself and his new cocky attitude (291.99, available only at Hot To-... wait...) Talking to Robert Smith of the Cure was one of the best things hes done in a while, he wanted to ask another question but another man came running into the area... Robert Smith looked at him and spoke. 'Simon Gallup, bassist of the Cure! What is so urgent!' Simon Gallup, bassist of the Cure, pointed towards the large skylight on the top of the building, 'Look!' DarkStar made a mental note that he didnt know why a photography agency had a large skylight on its building, he did see a light shining into the sky with what looked like Mascara and a lipstick container crossed into a X. 'Holy Crying Goth Boys Robert Smith of the Cure! Its the Cure Signal!' 'Quick! To the Cure-mobile!' Robert Smith of the Cure promptly left, to go battle evil doers in the name of the Cure. DarkStar smiled broadly and walked with Talena back to the photoshoot. 'Well Gavyn, that definately made my day.. or at least my list for oddest fucking thing ever.' DarkStar agreed, but stayed silent. You would be silent to if you got life advice from Robert Smith of the Cure.
