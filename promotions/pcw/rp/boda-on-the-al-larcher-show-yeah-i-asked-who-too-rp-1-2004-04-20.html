Boda on The Al Larcher Show. Yeah, I asked 'Who' Too. RP#1

20 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/20/04 ‘Five minutes Mr. Sloboda’ Sure, five minutes ain’t nothing. I’ve already been waiting damn near an hour. But hey, it’s a chance to talk to the fans out there, listening on the radio. I love my agent when he sets up talk show interviews, hell, television interviews are even better. But I never turn down an interview. Well, I have turned down Rush and Larry, but they always want to talk about how wrestling teaches children violence. They also think wrestling gives children bad role models. I figure they’ve probably only watched matches featuring Shaun Styles, because everyone knows I’m a positive role model with the young fans. ‘Two minutes Mr. Sloboda. Big Al will open with some theme music, and then you’ll be brought in, given a headset and the interview will begin. And again, thanks for coming to 98.5 FM today.’ Hey, no problem lady. After all, I was only supposed to be on The Mancow Show this morning, in Chicago. But thanks to some bad luck, I’m in…… Wait, what’s the name of this town again? ‘Paris, sir’ That’s right. I’m in Paris, Illinois. Now for those of you playing along at home, that’s merely 3 hours south of The Windy City. This morning, I should have been on air with The Legend of Chicago Talk Radio, Mancow. But I’m not. Somewhere between leaving North Carolina, and jumping on my plane to Chicago, I ended up here in….. in….. ‘Paris, Illinois’ Thanks. Paris, Illinois. To put it in prospective…. If Chicago was the Boda of Illinois…. And I’m talking about, TOP OF THE MOUNTAIN. The MAIN reason people come to Illinois, then Paris is the Shaun Styles of Illinois. But I’m not going to go on about my bad trip experience right now, after all….. ‘Time to go on the air Mr. Sloboda’ See, it’s time for me to be me. Time to please the fans listening in……in….. ‘PARIS’ Damn, she’s about as angry as Styles was when he found out his World Title shot is about to come to a screeching halt this week. After all, the poor kid had a shot at this World Title tournament, had he been seeded against the likes of Arson, or Buzz or Skitzo, but not me. I’m running on something none of the other seven have in this tournament. Experience. Pure driven experience. None of these seven have been PCW World Champion in the era of Streak, Stanislav, Snow, Howard, Kannon, Chef, Boda. None of them. Only two of them have been PCW World Champion in this, the ‘NEW’ era of PCW. The ‘Kano-ran’ era of PCW. And of course, those two title reigns combined lasted…. What? 7 days and 5 minutes? None of these seven have ever beaten out 20 other men in a battle royal to be crowned The World Champion. ‘Uhmm, Mr. Sloboda…. We need to go’ What? Come on, I was busy talking about why I am the odds on favorite to win this Tournament. ‘I am sure Big Al will talk about the tournament. He is a huge wrestling fan.’ Good. Well, lead the way. Let’s catch up real quickly, while Boda is taken from the Green room of WACF “Your Country Alternative” Radio Station, and into the studio. Seems the #1 seed in the Face to Face tournament has succumbed to some sort of travel dilemma, when his trip was somehow routed not to Chicago, but 3 hours south of there to….. to….. ‘GOD DAMMIT, HOW HARD IS IT TO REMEMBER PARIS, ILLINOIS’ Damn, chill out. Okay, he’s in Paris, Illinois, and not Chicago. And due to this, he’s gotten his agent to get him on The Big Al show of this station, and has been forced to miss his slot on The Mancow show. So here he is, on of all things, a country music station, about to meet a DJ he’s never heard of before. At least he’s supposed to be some sort of wrestling fan. Maybe he’ll be okay. I hope so. Boda is taken into the studio, and given a headset. The music playing is some country song, not his Theme as promised. Already, this is looking bad. Across from the desk, sitting behind a microphone, sits a twenty something, portly, grizzly faced DJ. He’s wearing a black cowboy hat, and a …. DUKE WILLIAMS t-shirt? Boda sits, with a quirky smile on his face. The chorus of the song closes out the music. Where does it go, the good Lord only knows, (scratch 'it') seems like it was just the other day, I was down at Green Gables, hawking them tables, And generally blowing all my hard earned pay, Piano rolled blues, danced holes in my shoes, There weren't another other way to be, For loveable losers, no ACCOUNT boozers, And honky tonk heroes like me Wait, that’s Duke Williams’s theme song, not Boda’s. What in the hell is this Big Al doing to him? Big Al: Welcome back to The Big Al Show, only heard here, on WACF 98.5 or online at www.985wacf.com, with live… wait, we still don’t have live streaming, so scratch that. Any ways, please welcome into the studio, a legend in his business, and the man that helped make Duke Williams a household name in wrestling…. By losing to him a lot…. PCW’s Hall of Famer…. Boda. Boda: Thank you Big Al, and it’s nice to meet you. But, you must have been misinformed. My theme song is ‘Soulfire’ by 12 Stones… The official band of Chris ‘Boda’ Sloboda. You played Duke Williams theme song. Boda chuckles a bit at the mistake, but that chuckle soon diminishes. Big Al: That’s no mistake my friend. This is a country music station. We don’t play none of that hippity, hoppity rock and roll junk. And besides, there is only one wrestler in PCW history that deserves to have his music played on my show. Boda: Let me guess, Duke Williams. Big Al: Ding, Ding. You win a prize. After all, Duke used Honky Tonk Heroes, by Waylon Jennings. That’s my favorite song of all time. And he’s also the son of the man I met a few weeks ago in Memphis. Hank Williams Jr. See. Al Larcher shows Boda a picture of him, in a green John Deere Hat, standing with Hank Williams Jr. Big Al: Nothing better in life than to have been drinking whiskey and smoking a stogie with that man right there. But hey, I’m entitled to my opinion. You’re here to promote PCW, the show in Chicago this weekend, and the World Title Tournament being held and that will what? Culminate in three weeks in Washington DC on PPV? Boda: That’s right Al. It’s the Face to Face tournament. 6 weeks ago, right after Jon Kano managed to get his grubby hands on the General Manager position, he instituted an open challenge format tournament. He named 8 men, and they had to face any challenger in the next 5 weeks, with hopes of holding their spot in that tournament. I wasn’t one of the original 8, but I beat out Kano, and went on to beat 2 more challengers, to become the #1 seed, and the odds on favorite to win the whole thing. Big Al: Yeah, that Kano sure is an assbag, using Duke’s untimely death to his advantage. When I look back at Duke’s career, I think about a great man, that had a great career, including beating you for the Continental title on September 25th, 2001. Boda: Wooh…. He also beat Vampir Nosferatu in that match. With all due respect to Duke, can we talk about my match this week, my career or the PCW as a whole…. Please? Big Al: All right big guy. So, what is going on for Boda as of late in PCW? Right there, that should be a clue that Mr. Larcher is no more than a closet Duke fan. He doesn’t seem interested in anything that doesn’t revolve around the late Mr. Williams. At least he’s not a Shaun Styles fan. Boda: Well, I’m the #1 seed in the tournament. I finished with a 3-0 record, which means that this Saturday night, in Chicago, I get the pleasure of beating Shaun Styles, to go 4-0, and advance to meet one of the other three winners from this week’s show. Big Al: Who’s Shaun Styles? Was he one of the open challengers? Well, Al Larcher isn’t all that bad. He doesn’t even know who Styles is. Now, if I was a heel I’d shit all over Styles’ career and lack of accomplishments to make myself look that much better. But I won’t. Boda: No. He’s the PCW Extreme Champion. And well….He’s the Extreme Champion. But hey, it’s not his fault he has to face me in the first round of the finals. If he’d done a little better, beaten a few more challengers…. He’d be facing some one less intimidating as me. But, I’ll give Shaun credit. At least he’s no Jon Kano, and won’t resort to some low down, cheap ‘I need help from my friends’ tactics and beat me. That I’m sure of. Styles doesn’t have any friends. Okay, so I threw in a few cheap, low down, dirty tactics. I’m the top face in this fed, but when it comes to matters of the heart… like being the PCW World Champion again, the cocky heel inside of me sometimes slips out. Oh well. Big Al: He’s the Extreme Champion? And he’s facing a man of your statue, you’re legendary status? Poor kid. Well, he does have one thing you don’t. Boda: What’s that? Big Al: He’s never lost to Duke Williams like you. Boda: Damn you. Are you like the Duke Williams Fan Club President? Big Al: Why? You want to join? Boda: No thank you. Listen, Al, let’s talk about current PCW events, not the past. Big Al: Ok. So, what else is happening with you? Boda: Well, I challenged Kano to a match at the PPV. And he’s agreed to put that G.M position up for grabs too. Big Al: So, if you make it past this Styles kid, then you face Kano for the GM title AND a match for the World Title? Boda: Well……. Big Al: You’ll have to answer that one after this commercial break from our sponsors. We’ll be right back, after this. 98.5 FM, Your Country Alternative is brought to you by 2004 Flame Worthy Awards. We put Dolly on, so you don’t forget about her. When we all know you’d rather see Shania or Carolyn Dawn Johnson. But no, Dolly begged for us to use her, so she can live in her past glory. So hell, why not. We’ll be nice. So, the show goes on for a few more commercials. Boda sits there, pondering what the hell he missed on The Mancow show. And he’s still trying to figure out how he got here. He booked a flight from Memphis, to Chicago, and ends up in Indianapolis, with a limo waiting to take him to Chicago. But somehow, that damn limo ends up breaking down in….. in….. ‘MOTHER FUCKER, THIS IS PARIS, ILLINOIS. YOU FORGET AGAIN, AND I’M GOING TO BITCH SLAP THE STUPID OFF YOUR FACE’ Okay…. Listen, she’s too high strung. Let’s continue this little adventure next roleplay. Someone needs some Midol and sleep. See ya soon
