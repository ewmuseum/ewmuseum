Because I Died

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/02 The scene opens up on a rainy morning. Buses and assorted vagabond cars roar along the roads as stragglers, people of all ilk stalk the concrete boulevards. The buildings pierce the heavens, rising upon high.....and in the middle of the bustle, a tall man walks with a purpose. Clyde: Bah. His eyes remain focused.....the killer seems to know his way around this city. He takes a turn into an alleyway, past trash bins and the refuse of humanity. Muttering obscenities to himself, he finally reaches his destination....a large portopotty. Clyde: Bah! The burned man enters the toilet, closes the door behind him. With a mumble he clambers on top of the bowl, placing his feet carefully within it. He then looks over the stall to find a homeless man staring at him with wide eyes. Clyde: What, never seen a grown man climbing into a toilet before? The gentleman continues to stare....until a brick sails towards him, causing him to scramble drunkenly away. Clyde: Beat it! Sputtering and cursing, the monster shifts both of his feet into the loo....he then reaches over and pushes a knob located nearby. Suddenly a whoosh of water fills the air, the burned man spins rapidly. Clyde: Aggggggggggh! Faster and faster Clyde rotates, slowly lowering. Finally he vanishes into the tiny hole....the bum peers over the lid, blinking. Bum: Crazy drunk drivers. Down, down through an endless abyss plummets the demon, through the eternal void until finally he comes to an unceremonious halt, crashing hard to the cold ground. Shakily he stands, wringing himself off. Clyde: Bah. Shaking his head, Clyde continues on...down a set of old, stone stairs until he reaches oddly enough a wooden door. He walks to it and knocks, a small slit opens in the panel. Voice: Password? Clyde: DIE!!!!! Voice: Come in. The panel closes again, several locks snapping and releasing. Finally the door creaks open....Clyde steps through to see a completely empty room. Clyde: The hell? Voice: What. The burned man looks down to see a hooded and cloaked figure, his face shielded from view. Clyde: Shouldn't there be someone in this room? What kind of cockamamie spoof is this? Hooded Figure: You haven't used the key yet. Clyde: What key? What are you babbling about? Hooded Figure: Close your eyes and stick out your tongue. With another curse, Clyde does as asked....then opens them again as the little fellow places something on his outstretched tongue. Clyde: Ack! The burned man chokes a bit, then sputters.....the tiny room seems to fill with assorted colors and shapes. Clyde: Hey that wasn't the key! Hooded Figure: It's the key....to the UNIVERSE. The room shifts and spins, spiraling madly out of control as the acid takes effect. Clyde drops to one knee to steady himself, closes his eyes.....then opens them again to see a man in grungy clothes standing before him. Man: (Pointing) I know you....but like.....I don't. Clyde: Stewart Hix!? Hix: I think so, but I'm not sure. The stoned former PCW world champion scratches his grungy hair, as Clyde stands again. Clyde: What the hell are you doing here? Hix: I don't know man.....I don't know much of anything. Stewart stares at his shoes, then breaks into chuckles for no real reason. Clyde: Ugh. Hix: Well man, lemme introduce you to the gang. Very reluctantly, Clyde follows the worst champion in history through the chamber. The smell of vomit permeates the room as he wanders through endless corners, until finally emerging in what appears to be a pool hall. In one corner of it sits a bald man with a scowl, playing cards with a fellow in a black cloak. Hix: Clyde, let me....uh.... Stewart Hix stares blankly into space. Hix: I forgot. Clyde: You want to....introduce me!? Hix: Oh yeah! Clyde: (Slaps his forehead) Hix: Clyde, meet Vegita and Death. Vegita merely waves a hand behind himself, not looking up from his game. Vegita: I draw three mana. Death: Ah he ba death car. Clyde's eyes widen, he grins insanely. Clyde: My leech!!!! At these words Vegita pauses....turns, white as a ghost. Vegita: ......yuh....YOU! Abruptly Vegita stands, knocking the game of Magic over. He backpeddles rapidly, reaching around and finally grabbing the first weapon he can find....which happens to be a furry and clawed arm. Jason Wulf: Hey! Wulf weakly tries to pull his arm back from Vegita's grasp, but only succeeds in tripping over his own two feet. The cannibal waves the severed appendage back and forth like a switchblade. Vegita: Stay back man, stay back! Hix: Whoa whoa whoa, easy man.....he's not here to fight. Clyde: I'm not? Hix: No dude. You're here cause you're facing Seymore Smoke soon and you need advice. Clyde: Why the hell would I need advice to kill that moron? Hix: .....I don't know. Once more Stewart scratches his head, as behind the group a voice warbles in a monotone. I've been.....everywhere man..... Clyde: (Turning) Dear god, no. The monster looks on in horror as a large albino sways back and forth on stage, clutching a microphone. I've been everywhere, man'Cross the deserts bare, manI've breathed the mountain air, manOf travel, I've had my share, manI've been everywhere Clyde: That's it I'm outta here. Clyde attempts to leave, but a hand clutches his shoulder. The demon turns to find a drunken irishman breathing fetid fumes into his face. Adam Riggs: Ah kin nah say wha you do.....bu..... Clyde: Your breath smells like Amy went diving crotchfirst into a brewery. Begone. Once more the monster tries to escape, but the drunken irishman holds him fast. Adam Riggs: Ah no, you neea more HIIIIIIGH! The jobber grabs Clyde by the back of the head and shoves him forcefully to the ground. There a line of white powder rests....the demon tries to resist and hold his breath, but eventually he must breathe in. Riggs: HIIIIIIIGH!!!!! A wave of color breaks through Clyde's vision, washing over him. The Preacher's singing fills his head, echoing through it quite endlessly. Preacher: I've been.....everywhere man..... Hix: See man, it's the secret of life.....old jobbers never vanish, they just die off. Then they come here. Yeah. Clyde looks to his right, to see a man in a large green suit breakdancing next to the crooning satanist. Picasso: Heroes in a halfshell, turtle power! As the Last Ninja Turtle spinaroonies, the bum the demon had seen previously wanders up to the monster. Ned: Tootsweet! Ned shoves a pipe into Clyde's mouth, then lights it up. As the demon inhales the crack smoke his skin grows numb....he stands, as the room spins. Clyde: Ladadadadada. The Preacher's droning slowly shifts tune....the burned man looks around in awe as Vegita, Jason Wulf, Ned and Stewart Hix stand arm in arm, swaying back and forth. Vegita: I was gonna eat some little kids, but then I died. Wulf: I was gonna get a fleabath too, but then I died. Hix: Now I'm....uh.....something.....and I know why..... Ned: Come on Clyde, join us! The burned man stands next to Hix on the end of the line, swaying along with them and singing drunkenly. Clyde: Because I died, because I died, because I died. Picasso breakdances past the line....Clyde watches him, then trips and falls into Hix, causing him to stumble into Ned who dominos into Wulf and Vegita. All five men lie on the floor unmoving..... The colors fade. The room slows. The Preacher's singing still echoes in the burned man's mind as he lays still in the dark and empty room...... because I died because I died because I diiiiiied Footsteps slowly ring out....the small hooded figure stands over the demon. It pulls back its hood, shakes its head disgustedly. Bethany: Last time I feed you pixy sticks. And as the little girl kicks her down and out lover, we mercifully fade to black.......
