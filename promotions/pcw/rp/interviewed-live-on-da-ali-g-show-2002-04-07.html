Interviewed Live on Da Ali G Show!

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/4/02 OOC:For those of you that don’t know, Da Ali G show is a an English show in which the host of the show (Ali G) acts like a total moron and ridicules most his guests by saying really stupid stuff. What happens when one idiot (Ali G) meets another (Flaymin Aymin)? The birth of the International Idiots Association is just around the corner! [Scene opens on the set of ‘Da Ali G Show’. The audience in attendance roar loudly as Ali G cracks yet another joke about 101 different was to conceal drugs. Dressed in a shiny yellow pair of baggy pants with a matching jacket, Ali G bends over to demonstrate how to shove cocaine up your ass while simultaneously play some music on a turntable] Ali G: A’ight, so that iz how it iz done! But I iz not going to accept any reponsobilitlity if youse get caught by da coppers.I iz saw nuffing. [The crowd laughs and clap their hands in applause] Ali G: Da next guest on da show is a wrestler.When he’z not rubbing baby oil or sticking hiz head up uver wrestlers asses, he’s buzy makin’ pornos. [The horny crowd cheer wildly at the mere mention of ‘porn’] Ali G: A’ight…. Respect for da brother….. respect. And ‘ere he iz, Flaymin Aymin…. Or sumfin like dat!! [The crowd begin to holler and hoot as Flaymin makes his way out onto the stage. Flaymin waves at the crowd and then takes a seat on the couch in the centre of the stage. Sitting on the other couch, lazing back is Ali G. Ali G looks at Flaymin and nods his head approvingly] Ali G: Now you iz a wrestler. Flaymin: I sure am. Ali G: And a pornstar. Flaymin: Yeah I do some of that too. Ali G: Duz dat mean you go around f*cking uver wrestlers? [Flaymin laughs and sternly shakes his head] Flaymin: Nah mate, I don’t swing that way. Ali G: But I thought dat iz what you would do.You know, like doing two fings at once: Wrestling and f*cking. Uze already grabbing each uver’s balls and rubbing each uver’s bodys- so why not pull down their pants and finish da job? [The crowd laugh in approval once again] Flaymin: Is that what you’d like to see?Man-sex? [Ali G raises his hands and shakes his head] Ali G: You iz got it wrong, I be f*cking my Julie…not some horny poofter named Julian. Flaymin: Julie.Julian. It sounds the same to me. [Ali G stops smiling and begins to frown] Ali G: Dat iz not funny.I’z call my West End Crew and they’d whoop your ass. Flaymin: Yeah and I’d bet they’d enjoy every second of it too. [A loud ‘Oooooooh’ sound echoes from the audience. Ali G raises his index finger and puts it over his lips] Ali G: Listen youse kangaroo f*cker, I iz be aksing da questions- you just answer dem. Flaymin: Sorry boss. [Ali G looks at the crowd and smiles proudly] Ali G: He iz scared because I iz showed ‘im my 12 inch python before da show! [The crowd cheers once again] Ali G: Now Flaymin I iz heard that you used to have an afro.Is that because you liked bush? Flaymin: Gotta love the bush. Ali G: And speaking of bush, iz it true dat you be f*cking da pornstar- Jenna Jameson. Flaymin: Right you are. Ali G: But everyone be f*cking her. My dog ‘az even had a go! What do you do when uver people are f*cking Jenna? Flaymin: I’m usually busy boning your mother. [Once again the crowd lets out a loud ‘Oooooooooooooooh’. Ali G looks at Flaymin and points his index finger in Flaymin’s direction] Ali G: Don’t you iz be talking ‘bout my muther…. her four boyfriends will come down and smash you like a rat. Flaymin: Tell her I said hello. Ali G: You really know my muther? Flaymin: Actually I was talking about your sister. [Flaymin winks at Ali G.Ali G looks at Flaymin and smiles in confusion] Ali G: A’ight, a’aight….. I don’t know what the f*ck you talking about but it sounds good. [Flaymin grins and then strokes his chin] Flaymin: Yeah she was pretty good…. [Ali G continues to smile and then shrugs his shoulders in bewilderment] Ali G: Me is finking that you been popping too many of dem pills. Flaymin: Hey, I’m all natural- from top to bottom. Ali G: The bottom of what? Flaymin: I don’t know… the bottom of the ocean? Ali G: Now you iz know heaps of wrestling moves, hey? Flaymin: Yeah quite a few. Ali G: You iz know how to do da Karate Kid Kick?You know how he iz put his arms out like a plane and him lifts one leg up. Flaymin: I don’t do bullshit moves. Ali G: But do you yuze your moves on da street? Flaymin: Like what? Ali G: A’ight dis is da scenarario.You iz walking down da street and coppers come up and tell you to ‘spread em’.Do you iz pull out a wrestling chair from underneath the ring or sumfing and then smash it on his head? Flaymin: Where did the chair come from?I thought I was walking down a street. Ali G: A’ight, forget da chair. Pretend dat youse is still fighting da copper, iz one of your buddies gonna run out of nowhere and smash the copper wif a baseball bat? [Flaymin rolls his eyes] Ali G: You’ve been paying a little too much attention to English Soccer fans. Ali G: We got no baseball bats- cricket bats are iz way better.Fick wood and it cracks ya head like eggs. [Flaymin laughs and claps his hands] Ali G: Now my next question.You iz a big wrestler star.You iz making lots of money. Flaymin: Yep. Ali G: So why iz it you drive a f*cking tractor? Flaymin: Well they’re big, mean, loud and tough. Ali G: Big, mean, loud, tough?I fink youse been f*cking one too many wrestlers- youse iz confusing tractors wif wrestlers. Flaymin: You really are hooked on all the homosexual actions in wrestling, aren’t you? [Ali G looks at the camera and pauses.His eyes move to the left… and then to the right.He continues to remain silent…] Ali G: Dis interview iz over.I iz not going to be answering pooftah questions from a wrestler who wears lycra shorts. Flaymin: I don’t wear lycra. Ali G: A’ight. I guess yuouse is prefer da fishnet shorts eh? [Having been the brunt of one too many gay jokes, Flaymin gets up and approaches Ali G. Ali G puts his hands over his face and cowers back into the corner of the couch] Ali G: Please don’t f*ck me, I iz not a wrestler!I got no baby oil!I iz got not ‘roids!! [Flaymin picks up Ali G and hoists him above his head. The crowd rises to their feet and begin to chant. From atop of Flaymin’s hands, Ali G looks down at Flaymin and yells] Ali G: Hey, hey, youse iz watch where you put your hands you filthy bastard! [Flaymin flips Ali G off the top of his shoulders and on to the couch below.Ali G bounces off the couch and hits the floor below.The audience in attendance start jumping up and down and yelling while Flaymin poses for the camera. Dazed and confused, Ali G gets up and jumps onto Flaymin’s back as the scene fades to black]
