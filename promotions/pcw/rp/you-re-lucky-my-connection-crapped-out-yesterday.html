You're lucky my connection crapped out yesterday.

Date: not entered (We return to the hot and spicy, sweet and sour confines of Unca Chen’s Crazy Kung-Fu Cookin’ Kitchen a mere five minutes after the last installment. The table groans under the weight of a massive pile of scavenged food. The Entourage, as is their wont, eat like starving wolves.) “What kind of fraud joint are you running here?!” (All that is, excepting for THE Multiple Man, who is having a word or two with the management.) Tmm: “I came here looking for a friendly dining atmosphere with good friends and good times ALONG with continuous attacks by talented ninja cooks! And you ask me why I’m not satisfied?!” (Steve grabs a hold of yet another piece of beef onna stick.) Steve: “Y’know, if I knew it was gonna be THAT easy, I would’ve gone way back when…” Keira: “Don’t flatter yourself, those weren’t real ninja. Old ladies at the mall are more dangerous.” (Further off, Tmm gesticulates wildly like his arms came straight off that Lost in Space Robot.) Tmm: “I KNOW ninjas and those were definitely not licensed by the International Ninja Commission! What kind of fraud are you running here?!” (Val sifts through the big pile o’ food looking for a little more of that chicken and broccoli.) Val: “What’s with all these won-tons?” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Val: “That doesn’t make any sense.” (Tmm rips up the front of his shirt in agitation.) Tmm: “See this scar? I got this from General Tso hurled at over ninety mile per hour at the hands of a REAL ninja! It clung to me like acid and dammit it was piping hot! Your little wanna-be’s barely cracked seventy and don’t think I didn’t check!” (Shrimp disappear at a frightening rate into the gaping maw of HV.) Steve: ”Do you breathe through your ears or something?” (Tmm comes back, still red with anger.) Tmm: “That charlatan gave us coupons. As if I’d EVER come back to this house of lies! I’ll bet they only have a twenty foot squid in the pit at Tempura night, those slackers!” Val: “Well, you know how things get watered down for public consumption, remember the Hard Rock Café’?” Keira: ”That was never cool.” Val: “It wasn’t? Then what am I thinking of?” Steve: ”Sahib’s Tiger Pit?” Keira: “Ugh… that one went downhill real fast. I hear the maiming rate went down over forty two percent for last year alone.” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: ”There’s just not enough EXTREME eateries anymore. Remember the Bakery of Doom with it’s Dark Muffins?” Steve: “The Razorblade Eatery? Not a curved surface in the place.” Keira: “I was always partial to the Kill Grill, in order to BE the man, you’ve got to EAT the man!” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Val: “Pound of Flesh Steakhouse, where the meal only gives you the FIRST bite free.” Tmm: “B.Y.O.M., we don’t ask WHAT it is, just how you like it cooked.” Steve: ”Slobberknockers.” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]?” Steve: “It’s this specialty place up the way. You see, they serve these great burgers, but the thing is… they serve it on this HUGE honkin’…” Keira: “We’re QUITE familiar with your tastes, thank you very much.” Steve: “It’s not like that at all!” Keira: “Suuuure it isn’t.” Tmm: “Hmm… you realize that we haven’t brought up my opponent in over three consecutive promos?” Val: “Why ruin a perfectly good streak by starting now?” Tmm: ”Mmm… quite. Still these Won-tons are pretty good.” (Camera fades out.)
