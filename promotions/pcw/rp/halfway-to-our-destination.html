Halfway to our destination!

Date: not entered Some more time has passed since Rich Sherwin had been seen last on Television sets around the world. It had been… too long for all those adoring females to have gone without seeing the face of Rich Sherwin… but now, they have the chance to rejoice… because he’s back… back again, to appear in the second instalment of the greatest… erm, story to have ever existed in the World. In the last piece, we had been able to see Rich and Ryan sit down in a Café… exchange a few words before Moore stormed out of the location in something of a huff. We’d seen Sherwin handed a leaflet… talking about something that was going down in Canada. Why it’d been handed to Sherwin… when he was so far away from the location? It’s a question… that’ll beg an answer… but probably never ever see one. As we watch the next instalment of the story unfold infront of our very eyes… Sherwin, has made some strides in his attempt to get to Canada! Will this only cause… problems?… for the idol of PoP?! Stay Tuned… Well… you’re back, for the second part of the most interesting journey in my life. I call it this… because it was the most interesting thing that I’d ever been a part of… in the history of my minor existence. Regardless, upon leaving the little Café… I decided that my first act of business would be to go home, book a ticket or what-not before getting ready to leave the home… and begin my journey to Canada. I’ll have to skip the gory details of what went on at home… because I don’t think that anyone around the world will find me… packing suitcases of clothes to be even significantly interesting. Anyway… after doing all that, I left the house… and hollered or a cab, in the same way that Will Smith did on his way to Belair… We rejoin the picture outside the home of Rich Sherwin, he looks to be dressed in his very normal attire of black pants, white vest and open shirt, one that depicts multiple images of various Warriors form hit animated Show, Dragonball Z. Like stated before this, he hollers for a cab in the same style that “Big Willy” did at the beginning of every “Fresh Prince of Bel-Air” episode. A plush yellow cab pulled up to the sidewalk, just a few feet away from the body that was being idolised and positioned correctly on the walls of Teenages Girls rooms across the United States of America. The window of the cab was lowered… and the driver looked out with a surprised look on his face. “Oh My God!,” he said, starting to scream and exclaim at the very top of his voice. The phrase was repeated a few more times in a tone of voice that would make Jim Ross blush. Sherwin looked rather coyly at the driver… looking at the back to inspect for ‘Dead Bodies’ before returning his look to the driver, who was now attempting to calm down. He continued with, “… I can’t believe that its really you man… I can’t believe, that I’m sitting here in this Yellow Cab… and you’re there… you are the ICON… you are… the LEGEND… you are, the most awesome character in the business today!” Sherwin looked a little embarrassed about all of the praise that was being heaped upon him by this unknown driver, but, instead of just getting into the cab and getting on with the journey, Sherwin had to go out of his way… and do something that would make his ego seem very noticeable. Time passed… well, a few seconds passed… before Sherwin happened to strike a pose on the sidewalk… looking rather toned, rather buff… and relatively ready to come back into this game and take “No Prisioners.”… you do know, that’s an exaggeration right? The Cab Driver nodded his head in a “Cool” manner to indicate that he recognised exactly what Sherwin was showing off on the outside of the car. Seconds after this, our hero pulled open the back-door of the cab… slung all of his luggage into the back… and set off with his newly found acquaintance… to the Airport which would allow Sherwin to already be upon the half-way point of this amazing journey. The Cab Ride… I assumed it was going to be full of fun, one of those things that you remember for the rest of your life… because it was good. Well, in my case… this cab ride was the of the most pathetic journeys I’d ever been on… in my entire history of riding cabs. Its like everything he’d said before this was… nothing but a fake… a hoax, an attraction to get me to ride the bloody thing… meh, I felt like telling the guy a few things about “Insults” when I got out… and then bail without even paying a dime… but, since this cab had central locking… I was in a no-win situation. The Cabbie continued to drive down the streets like any normal member of the public, looking in his mirror from time to time to see if his customer was behaving. Rich continued to look out of the random mirrors with his head held low… his face only just beginning to express the kind of emotion that a child would show… if he were crying out for something decent to be done. “What seems to be the problem?”, the Cabbie asked, sounding a little concerned for the ‘Mental Health’ of his pick-up. Sherwin took his head away from the window and Driver a ‘Stone Glare’… continuing to show a face of “No-Emotion”. There was a awkward silence between both driver and customer… until, the driver decided to speak up again, “… Look man, I know you didn’t like the way I was working my pitch when I came across you… but really, at least you’re actually on your way to your location… its not as if you had to wait another thirty minutes or so for somebody else to come along.. ya?”, he asked, sounding kinda upbeat about his statement… but secretly hoping that he wasn’t one of those “Drivers on Request”. “What seems to be the PROBLEM?”, Sherwin began to answer… in a somewhat, tone that mocks that of the driver, “… you have the absolute decency to ask… ME… what the problem seems to be? Well, how about the fact that I’m probably the only guy… who was stupid to come on a crap-infested trip like this. Hell… I could have called my boy, Jordan and got a faster ride than this… and he wouldn’t have even asked for a real deal of money… well, Gas money may have been the only thing he’d want… but other than that, I know I’d be assured of a quality ride.” “Well… what seems to be the problem with this ride? Is there something that you’re… not exactly happy about?”, the Driver asked, continuing to sound concerned… and not overly made at the comments that were just made about him… and his decisions within the business of Cabbies. Sherwin looked out of the window for a few more moments… trying to think of the right words to say when posed with a question like the one he was facing. “Hmmm…”, he began, sounding very… uncertain about his future past whatever chunk of dialogue he was about to spit out to both the driver… and himself. “Well… you see, the most boring thing about this ride… is the fact that you have SO failed to put any kind of music on as SOON as I stepped in your cab ya? You just sat your ass down… and started acting in a worse manner than Fezz from “That 70’s Show”… hell, are you two even related or something?” “EXCUSE ME?”, was everything that the driver could say as he stopped the car in the middle of the road and looked behind in the back seat at Sherwin. With his eyes seemingly bulging from his head, the driver continued to keep his glare locked on Sherwin… locked on Sherwin as if to say, ‘You say ANYTHING.. and God, you say anything else about myself… or my family, and your ass can damn as hell walk the… SHIT, ten seconds to the Airports Main Entrance!’ I didn’t quite understand the reason why the Driver kept looking at me that day… I personally thought my comparison was quite fair… although I could have gone one step further and called him ‘Jug Head’ or something… that probably woulda pissed him off a lot more or something. Anyway… I didn’t quite get his problem… and with the Airport being just a few seconds away, I couldn’t be bothered to bring anything up… well, anything that would keep me with the freak for any damn longer than necessary. The next scene we saw… the Cab had pulled up in one of the ‘Parking Bays’ just infront of the Main Doors. Sherwin got out of the door nearest the pavement and pushed his luggage down upon the floor… something which made the Luggage Carriers move like thunder… just to get close to them. Sherwin stepped out of the car after this, looking around and inhaling a large amount of… toxic air. He coughed after doing so… and walked into the complex, without paying the driver… and without his luggage, although… that was all being dealt with by those “Money Searching” like folk. He slowed his walk down a little bit… so that the “Luggage Boy” behind him was able to catch up. “Erm… erm… erm… excuse me, but are you Rich Sherwin from that federation in this Country, Primetime Championship Wrestling?”, he asked… sounding a little happy, but a little reserved in case he’d made another one of those false identification things. Sherwin looked happy about the fact that somebody had been able to recognise him without the help of something like a name-badge… or a blatantly huge poster about five feet away depicting the face of the “Chosen Celebrity” “Well… I’m the one that they call ‘Rich Sherwin’ around the walls of PCW,” he started, sounding impressed with himself and looking upon the fan with something of a large smile. The Luggage Boy… took the smile a little too “Literal” and began to run toward the “Luggage Platform” with all of Sherwin’s bags… maybe he was just a little shy about meeting somebody with the same amount of greatness as Sherwin? Its not something that anyone is sure to pick up on without help… but it happened. That kid was weird… its like he had developed some kind of crush on me, the kind that… only those cute girls have… but hey, sometimes there has to be the odd one out… and I think I found the ‘Oddball’ of the pocket on that day… in that very location. So, to quickly continue on what was being stated… that idiot ran to his destination with all of my belongings in tow… the service was fast, so I was still debating the reason for his reaction to my smile… although, that wasn’t the thing I was giving thought to at the moment. Instead, I was looking over toward the terminal that I was supposed to be… going through… “Excuse me… Excuse me Sir!”, cried a voice from behind Richie. He turned around quickly to see what the commotion was all about… but failed to see anything of a significant view. Rich scratched his head a couple of times before turning around and continuing with his walk toward the terminal. As he got his ticket checked out and the like… Sherwin walked through into the gangway type thing, a large lump being sported on his back. What would this be? What could it spell?… and could it and that voice heard in the last few moments of it all… have any resemblance to the fate instore for our amazing hero? Don’t know the answers to all of the questions posed forth!? Tune… in…. NEXT TIME… hopefully ya?
