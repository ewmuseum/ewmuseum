Getting Back Into The Habit

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th Sept 02 The midnight-blue Nissan 300ZX skids to a halt, kicking up asphalt as it does so. The engine cuts off, the drivers door opens, and a pair of black boots step out. The boots belong to PCW's latest re-signing D-Kay Calyx. D-Kay, dressed in a pair of blue jeans, and a black 'True Playaz' T-shirt, turns and slams the door shut, locking it and setting the alarm with the button on his key ring. Walking away from the car, D-Kay has to jump back as a white Mercedes speeds past him. Cursing under his breath, D-Kay shrugs his head, and leaves the small side turning and onto the main street. Looking up and down the street, he slowly walks across, smiling as he causes a juggernaut to slow down and sounds its air horn furiously. Getting back on the sidewalk, Calyx heads straight for the building on the corner, and enters through the front door. 'Hey guys. Long time no see!' D-Kay walks slowly up the steps into the shop, a broad smile on his face as the counter staff turn and look at him. Greetings are exchanged backwards and forwards as D-Kay leans on the counter and shakes the hands of the three men. The shop is otherwise deserted. D-Kay, and the three assistants are the only people in sight. Taking a quick look around, it establishes itself as an independent record store. 12' vinyl's cover three of the four walls, the other being a huge shop-front window. The counter is just above waist height, and has a chunk cut out of the middle, allowing whoever is behind the counter to use the turntables and the mixer. D-Kay looks around, and lets out a low whistle. 'Jesus. You got a hell of a stock in.' D-Kay smiles as one of the assistants walks up to him. The assistant must be in his early twenties, has ginger hair, is wearing a blue t-shirt with 'The Disc' printed on the front, and a pair of black combat slacks, and must be no more than 5'6' tall. 'You want a drink D-Kay?' 'Sure thing, munchkin.' The other two men behind the counter laugh at D-Kay's wise crack. D-Kay lets a sly grin wash over his face, but the ginger kid just frowns, and walks off, grabbing a few coffee mugs from the back of the counter. A second assistant, in his mid thirties, bleached blonde hair receding from his forehead, and dressed again in blue t-shirt and black combats, walks over to D-Kay and nods. 'What brings you back around here man?' 'Nothing much, Clive. I'm getting back into things. Back in PCW, they already fired me once, so I figure I better start DJ'ing again so I have something to fall back on.' Both men chuckle, as Clive leans forward. 'Listen man, I'm sorry to hear about Allison. Richard and Leigh know, but they also don't want to mention it to you. If you ever need to talk...' Clive is cut off as D-Kay waves his hand. 'Thanks man. I know you mean well, but I'll handle it my way. The thought is appreciated though. Now, how about you sort me out with some tunes?' Clive smiles, and nods politely. The chimes on the door sounds, and another person enters. The third shop assistant smiles, and walks to the back of the shop, the other man walking through a glass door just to the left of the top of the steps. Grabbing a few tunes off of the wall, Clive turns back to D-Kay. 'How long has it been since you last called in?' 'Jesus. Nearly five months. I got some catching up to do. Oh, and it's strictly drum and bass now.' Clive nods, and starts to frantically pull various vinyl's down from the racks. Placing an ever-growing pile on the counter, Clive suddenly spins around, and reaches under the counter, coming back with a carrier bag. Printed on the bag is The Disc logo, and D-Kay's name in biro. 'I knew you hadn't moved, so I knew you'd be back in some day. I kept some aside.' Clive places the bag on the already tall pile, and turns back to the wall. D-Kay laughs. 'Damn. Looks like you've been saving for the past few years! How much are you trying to get me to part with?' A few seconds later, and two piles rest on the counter, both reaching from just above D-Kay's waist to just below his chin. D-Kay lets out another bout of laughter, and shakes his head, ducking through the door cut into the counter to stand behind the decks. Leigh, the ginger haired kid, walks back into the room, his smile still not showing back on his face, and leaves two mugs of coffee on the counter in front of D-Kay. Clive thanks him for the drink, and stands beside D-Kay. 'You're back in PCW?' 'Yeah man.' 'How are you coping with that tax that Kannon and Williams have imposed?' 'Two options really. One, he announced it before I signed that all roster members had to sign. Now, that must have meant 'at that time.' In my contract, there isn't a clause saying I have to pay it.' 'Come on D-Kay. You know Xavier Kannon. He's tax his own mother if it improved his own lifestyle.' 'Second option. I still have my original severance pay from PCW when Tyler Nelson fired me. If push comes to shove, I'll use some of that.' D-Kay smiles, and pulls the fist 12' out of its sleeve, and places it on the turntable. 'New decks?' 'Yeah man. Technics SL1210 MKIII's. Gemini Pro-626 MKII mixer. New Kenwood amp and graphic equalizer. And then Kenwood bass bins, sub woofers and speakers. Full new sound system in here.' 'How'd the shop afford that?' 'Some bastard stole the original system we had. Insurance paid for this.' D-Kay laughs, and hits the 'start' button, skipping the needle to various points on the vinyl, leaving it nearly ten seconds on each section. Shaking his head, he removes it and replaces it into its sleeve. He turns to Clive as he continues to play through the piles. 'So, Clive. What's it been like without me in? Business dropped while you've got no one to fob all your shit off to?' Clive laughs. 'Nah man. It's been quite busy recently. We've just had Dieselboy, AK1200, Dazee and Craze in the other day. Got our website address and paid us a visit. The house room next door is going well. Joey Negro was in a few weeks back, spent $900.' 'So you've now got me here to try and beat his sales, huh?' --- Small conversation passes until D-Kay has finished the second pile. The tunes he has listened to now fit two piles in front of him, one distinctively smaller than the other. Handing the smaller pile back to Clive, he shakes his head. 'For those, thanks but no thanks. The rest.' D-Kay smiles and sighs at the same time. Looking at his watch, and realising two and a half hours have passed, he shrugs and turns to Clive. 'What's the damage?' Clive continues counting the number of vinyl's D-Kay has actually agreed to buy, moving them through the pile as he does so. After a minute, and a consultation with a calculator, Clive shakes his head. 'To you? $380.' 'JESUS!' D-Kay shakes his head, and looks slightly surprised. Flicking through the selection again, he shakes his head, and, reluctantly, hands over his Visa Card. 'You kill me man!' Clive smiles as he runs it through the till, handing D-Kay the slip to sign. Giving D-Kay his receipts back, Clive grabs hold of a plastic bag, and, finding he can only get half of them in, mutters a few expletives. Jumping up onto the counter, Clive reaches for a record box on a shelf above one of the record racks. Fetching it down, he just manages to get all of the tracks in there, and closes the lid. 'Three choices. $85 now, borrow and return, or on debt list?' 'Debt list. I haven't got enough limit left. I just had to buy a new car.' 'Why?' 'Some little bastard burnt my BMW out. And they've stopped producing them now. Insurance won't pay out because I left it in a public place, and unlocked. So I know have a midnight-blue Nissan 300ZX. Better than nothing.' Clive sighs, and shakes his head. 'What are we going to do with you?' 'I don't know man. I don't know. Listen, I'm going to hold a barbecue next Saturday at my house, you're invited.' 'Thanks D-Kay. Oh! Almost forgot to ask. I saw you on Fusion, when do you re-debut?' D-Kay smiles, and walks back around to the front of the counter. Lifting the record box off of the blue top, he places it by his feet. 'Friday night. L. Ron Hubbard Arena in Hubbardville. Kannon's on an ego trip.' 'Sounds pleasant. Who've you got?' 'I asked for Chase, and got him.' Clive flashes a puzzled look towards D-Kay, who simply smiles. 'Why Chris Chase?' 'Why not? Watch Fusion. You'll see!' D-Kay smiles, and exchanges more pleasantries as he leaves, thanking Clive and saying goodbye to everyone. Leaning slightly to one side due to the weight of the record box, D-Kay drops it and sprints forward when he gets back to his car. 'GET AWAY FROM THAT CAR YOU LITTLE SHIT!' D-Kay gives chase to a youth dressed in all black, but gives up, and walks back to his record box. Picking it up, he unlocks the trunk, and drops the box in. Walking down the drivers side to his door, he sees what the youth had done. A broken window is the first thing noticed, but below that, scratched into the paintwork, presumably by a key, is the inscription 'D-Kay Sucks!' D-Kay punches the car roof in annoyance, and climbs into the car, starting his engine and heading off to find a garage.
