Mothers Day Flight But No Mother

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12/02 Savoir to executioner. Funny isn’t it? How things change? Mike Frampton seems to describe Big Daveylicious as a great guy who saved his floundering career by tag teaming with him and getting him a job. But now an evil b@stard who is hell bent to destroy Mike and save his spot in the spotlight. Which is true! Mike seems so bent up in frustrations from all of Big D’s attacks that he is now his former self Punisher 2000...big deal! Mike can be a different person if he wants. Mike has multiple personalities for God sakes! It’s terrible that he’ll be remembered as a jobber or maybe that guy who was Big D’s tag partner. Frampton realizes this and doesn’t like it. If you have no name in this business than your not going anywhere. So he wants Big Daveylicious out of the spotlight and let lil’ Mikey to take his spot. After 7 months Big D got himself into the spotlight by winning the Extreme title. Does anybody honestly think he wants to say “Okay, Mike here ya go. Here’s your spotlight.“,? Of course not! He’s clawed his way to get where he is. Already established a name for himself as an Extreme Champion by defeating the likes of Chef and others. He’s on the top of his career and isn’t willing to let anybody hold him back. Mike’s right about one thing though. The bell does toll for him. Not for his turn in the spotlight. He hasn’t earned that. Not for his time to go to bed. Not for his time to eat dinner but for his time to bury himself. Because that’s what he done crossing the likes of Big Daveylicious. (The scene fades in on an airplane cabin, in a dark night on May 11th. The airplane and crowd noise drowns out much of the conversation between the passengers getting on the plane and the blonde, clipboard-carrying Stewardess who checks their boarding passes. A uniformed policeman, possibly a sheriff, enters and the stewardess sees that he's wearing a gun on his hip, so he shows her his badge as well as his boarding pass.) Stewardess: All right. Fine. Enjoy your flight. (The policeman heads for his seat. Big Daveylicious enters. Big Daveylicious is wearing Armani suit, Gucci shoes, and Oakley sunglasses with the Extreme title on his shoulder. He is holding onto a suitcase. Big D gives the plane an apprehensive look while showing his pass to the stewardess. She clears him to go through. There's no assigned seating so Big D walks over to the nearest seat. He is about to put his suitcase in the in the overhead bin. He opens the bin and he sees Michael Graves all curled up in the overhead bin munching on a burrito.) Big Daveylicious:No, I had to buy another damn plane ticket for today. I can't see my mother for Mothers Day because I'm fightinga jobber. To top it off my lackey got on the plane. Michael Graves: Hey, Big D. Is this messed up or what? I told them I wanted the cheapest seat in this plane. I didn’t mean I wanted this. Big Daveylicious: This sucks. Michael Graves: Hey, can you help me out here? I am kind of cramped up in here. I would really appreciate it. (Big D shuts the overhead bin with a smile on his face. He sits down on his seat that is on the aisle side. We can hear Graves banging against the overhead bin. Some of the people are making their way to their seats.) Michael Graves:(Banging and pounding against the overhead bin.) You son of a moneys Uncle let me out! I can’t breath! Somebody save me! Big Daveylicious: Graves, don’t you think if you stop shouting you could possible save some breath. Michael Graves: Oh, yeah. I forgot. Thanks Big D you’re a genius. Big Davceylicious: That I am. (Suddenly, a little boy walks over to Big D in the aisle with his mother behind him. The little kid has a Big Daveylicious action figure in his hand.) Big Daveylicious: Hey, my little Daveylicious-atic. Tis I, your Mentor! Can you say Mentor? Little Kid: Men… Men…Men… Big Daveylicious: Stop right their! Your beginning to sound like Mike Frampton in Florida in Disney World during Gay Day! Little Kid: My name is Timmy. You’re my favorite wrestler. You rule. I have all your posters, shirts, action figures and…and every thing! Big Daveylicious: Everything? You better not have not stole my Gucci shoes. I’ll kill ya! Michael Graves: (Banging and pounding against the overhead bin.) Damn, it let me out! The burrito is making me have gas! Timmy: What was that? Michael Graves: Tis I, the man of the darkness. The thing that lays under your bed. The cold that will make you shiver! The stain on your shirt! The piss that you pissed out!The evilness of your bean bag burrito! I am Michael Graves! Big Daveylicious: Michael you’re the evilness of a bean bag burrito?Michael Graves:(Pound against the overhead bin.) May The Powers Of Michael ….. (Suddenly, the overhead bin bursts open and out falls Michael Graves right onto Timmy. Graves is on top of Timmy. Timmy’s mother has a pissed look on her face. She starts hitting Graves with her purse.) Michael Graves: My bean bag burrito! Big Daveylicious: Now, that is sick. Timmy’s Mother: Don’t molest Timmy! You sick gothic freak! (Michael stands ups and gets off of Timmy. Timmy stands up as he is crying.) Timmy: The bad man hurt me Mommy. Timmy’s Mother: Is that so? (Suddenly Timmy’s mother grabs Timmy’s Big Daveylicious action figure and walks away from our view down the aisle. Michael Graves jumps over Big D onto the window seat.) Big Daveylicious: She kicked your a$$! I thought the evilness of the bean bag burrito could of stopped her? Michael Graves: Just because I wear black and have several pictures of Michael Jackson doesn’t make me a child molester. It makes me a... Big Daveylicious: A big gay supporting fan of Michael Jackson? Michael Graves: Uhhh....Yeah. (Graves sees that he's seated by the auxiliary exit and tenses up.) Big Daveylicious: What does the man from the third sector of hell afraid of the emergency window? Michael Graves: Just because I come from Tennessee doesn’t mean I’m not fearful. Big Daveylicious: Your a pansy, Graves. Michael Graves: No, I’m an Evil Sourcer from Hell... (The Flight Engineer gets ready to shut the cabin door.) Michael Graves: I am the Evilness Of A Bean Bag Burrito! May the powers of… (The loud thump of the cabin door slamming shut startles Graves.) Michael Graves: Oh my God! Where all gonna die! Big Daveylicious: This is gonna be a long flight... (Suddenly we hear a voice through the P.A) Captains Voice {voice over):Good morning. This is your captain speaking. Welcome aboard flight 151 non-stop to Canada. We ask that you turn your attention to the front of the cabin for pre-flight safety instructions. (A female flight attendant steps nearby Big D to demonstrate the seat belt.) Female Attendant: To fasten your seat belt, insert the free end into the coupling. Michael Graves: Tehehe! She said insert. What she doing.... Female Attendant: Hi, I’m Sarah. I can I help you with that? (Sarah hands reach down smiling reach down and pull one strap from between his legs.He looks down at his lap as Sarah leans over him. A loud CLICKING can be heard. He stares blankly at her.) Sarah: There you go. You're all set. Big Daveylicious: I...love you. Michael Graves: You don’t love her. You want to do her. (She smiles and walks off. Suddenly, the plane engine begins to hum.) Michael Graves: What's going on?! Big Daveylicious: We’re taking off. Michael Graves: I’m not taking off my clothes! (Big D bangs away at his seat belt as Graves looks out the window and realizes they're in the air.) Michael Graves: Ahhhhhhhhhhhhh! WE’RE ALL GONNA DIE! Man: Ya hear that? Something must be wrong. Woman: OH MY GOD! Big Daveylicious: Enough of this crap. I’m talking to Sarah. (The plane arcs upward as Big D finally gets the belt off as the plane is in full thrust. He rises and goes tumbling backward down the aisle. Big D grabs the door to the hang able luggage. It all comes tearing out. People are screaming as flies up, hitting several overhead luggage racks, which open and spill their contents. He lands in the galley, causing food to go flying and coffee to pour freely.) Big Daveylicious: I’m not hungry! (The plane starts to level out as people stop their screaming. Underneath the ruble Big D’s head pokes out. He's directly across from the flight attendant station where Sarah is strapped in her seat.) Big Daveylicious: Hey, baby. (He stands up proudly brushing himself off.) Big Daveylicious: Wanna do it? (The plane starts to lose turbulence causing him to fall and roll across the hallway once again knocking him into his chair as he catches it. He looks to us.) Big Daveylicious: What? I only wanted her to put my seat belt on. Okay, I wanted SEX! (We fade outside of the plane as the plane once again levels out. With that the scene fades to black.)
