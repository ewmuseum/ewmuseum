Something Retro(Part 1)

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 15, 2002 [A middle aged man is in the middle of an arena floor; holding a microphone, wearing a tweed sport jacket, dress pants, and a white undershirt with a red, white, and blue tie. He has black hair, and brown eyes.] Man: Hello everyone, and Welcome to this…the American Gladiators Special…Today, someone has accepted the challenge of three, count ‘em, THREE former competitors here tonight. We have also encountered most of the former Gladiators…Allow me to introduce them now! [The lights in the arena grow strong as the American Gladiators walk down the ramp of an elaborate set that has the letters AG made out of metal pipes welded together vertically. Underneath it, out walk seven muscle bound men all weighing in at over 250 pounds. All of them are wearing, pretty much the same outfit, with their names emblazoned on their chest of pant legs. The crowd begins to cheer very loudly.] Man: LASER! [The muscle head who looks like a brown-haired Guile strikes a power pose for the crowd.] Man: HAWK! [A tall muscle head with his head shaved like Hawk from the Legion of Doom yells out to the crowd.] Man: SABRE! [One of two Black gladiators, also known as the bald one does some shadow boxing.] Man: TURBO! [A rather plain looking muscle head simply folds his arms, showing off his pythons.] Man: GEMINI! [The other black Gladiator, he’s the haired one, as he gets himself pumped up.] Man: NITRO! [The most vocal of the bunch simply talks trash as he gets the crowd going] Man: AND THUNDER! [The largest of the group simply flexes one arm showing his massiveness.] Man: THESE ARE YOUR AMERICAN GLADIATORS…I’M MIKE ADAMLE, LET THE GAMES BEGIN! [With the crowd going absolutely crazy, The American Gladiators theme begins to play as the screen comes to life with previous footage of the Gladiators in action is shown in little windows that float about the screen as the theme plays. Eventually the theme reaches it’s end with the American Gladiators Logo flashing across the screen one last time before we return to “Gladiator Arena” and our host who is now sitting at his broadcast position with his co-host, Larry Csonka, a rather tall and old man.] Mike: Welcome everyone to American Gladiators, as I stated at the top of the show, one man has challenged three former Gladiator contestants to face him in this hallowed arena. This man is Eddie Mandrake, a professional wrestler from the Primetime Championship Wrestling Organization. In a recently televised interview he called all of them out, and we managed to get them all here to Gladiator Arena…and Larry, they all mean business. Larry: Mike, I talked to all of them before we came onto the air, and they are all pumped and ready to begin. Wesley “Two Scoops” Berry especially…because I am sure he would love another chance to run circles around the Gladiators again. Mike: Indeed, Larry, the prize structure is as follows. Fourth place here gets $25,000, third place gets $50,000, second place gets $100,000 and the man who comes in first gets $250,000! Our four competitors will compete in seven unique physical contents. After every two events, one of the competitors will be eliminated leaving only two. After the second competitor is gone, the last two will go at it in within the Eliminator, the extreme obstacle course. The winner of that will receive the top prize money. With that let us head into our first event, and that is Assault! Larry: To refresh you all of the rules. The competitors have sixty seconds to get from station to station, to fire off weapons to try to hit the target above the gladiator’s shooting platform, all the while, trying to avoid getting tagged by the tennis balls that the gladiator will be shooting at the competitors. The competitors will get one point for every weapon fired, and ten points for hitting the target above the gladiator. Mike: And the gladiator that will be shooting the competitors will be…Laser! [Laser doesn’t even motion at the crowd and simply stares down the first combatant. A short black man wearing a red tank top and pants.] Mike: Coz Worthington, only at 5’6 and 175 pounds, one of the smallest male contenders, and was one of the most memorable. Larry: One of the first to prove that contenders can defeat the gladiators in Powerball and Breakthrough and Conquer. Mike: Indeed, let’s head to our referee, Larry Thompson. Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: And we are off and running here…Coz moving fast here to station number 1. Laser manages to get off three balls before Coz got into cover. He picks up the crossbow gets set and fires… Larry: Ooh! It just sails wide of the target. Mike: Indeed that was a close call with the crossbow. Coz now into the open…Laser firing off his shots… (Whistle Blows) Larry: Coz got tagged in the leg on his way to station number two. Laser is celebrating his victory. Mike: With the firing of the crossbow at station number one, Coz gets one point. Let’s move onto our next competitor, Rico Costantino. Six feet tall and 228 pounds, he has bulked up since the last time we saw him at Gladiator Arena. [While Mike was talking, Rico makes his way to the starting point. He’s wearing the same thing as Coz, except its blue.] Larry: Last time we saw him here, he broke the blocking bag during the Eliminator! And made Gemini look like a fool! Mike: I remember that Larry. Either way, he’s ready to go here in assault. Larry #2: Contender Ready! …Gladiator Ready! (Whistle Blows) Mike: And Rico is off and running…Laser shooting, and missing by a country mile. Larry: Too bad he doesn’t have “Laser” sight! HAHAHA! Mike: That was absolutely horrible there Larry. Rico at station number one with the crossbow, with Laser still trying to pick him off. Rico fires… Larry: Just a bit outside! Mike: MORE LIKE INTO THE CROWD DUMBASS! Larry: Oh, so it is! Mike: Rico off and running to station number two. Laser not looking too good on that cannon right now.. Larry: DUH! He keeps missing! Mike: Rico at station number two. He sets himself up with the rocket launcher. He fires… Larry: And hits the clock! Mike: Rico now psyching Laser out… Larry: Sure enough, it’s working. Laser can’t hit the broadside of a barn today. Mike: Rico, thanks to the psyche-out, makes it to station number three without any trouble whatsoever. Now aiming the dodge-ball cannon… Larry: Whoa! Rico almost got nailed there! Mike: Indeed, that cannon is a death trap for most of the contenders…Rico fires… Larry: HEY RICO, WE’RE NOT PLAYING DODGE-BALL HERE! HIT THE TARGET! NOT THE GLADIATOR! Mike: That wasn’t nice. Rico now…en-route to station number four…Laser now getting his aim back. Larry: Without a doubt, he’s making Rico juke and jive his way across the floor. He’s there now. Let’s see if he’ll actually get close to the target. Mike: Rico with the pistol now…He fires… Larry: THERE! THAT’S BETTER RICO! Mike: Good encouragement there Larry. Larry: I was kidding. Mike: Bastard. Rico’s on his way to the final station. Laser really making Rico shake that thing! Larry: I thought you were married Mike. Mike: I am. Rico at station number five…chucking those softballs. Larry: Hehehehe. Mike: What’s so funny? Larry: Mentioning Rico and Soft Balls in the same sentence. HAHA! Mike: Shut up moron. Rico misses all three fifteen seconds lift on the cloct can he make it to the finish line… POMP! (Whistle Blows) Larry: Guess not when he got that tennis ball in the ass. Mike: With the five weapons fired, Rico gets five points. He’s down on the floor with Larry. [We see Larry Csonka, and Rico on the floor.] Larry: Rico is it just me or have you gotten better at this event? Rico: Well Larry, my career in the WWE has improved my speed quite a bit, unfortunately, it’s made me bigger physically, so Laser just had a big target. Larry: Well, either way, you got five points, congratulations! [Rico salutes the crowd and Laser begins to pump them up again.] Mike: Well, Larry, here comes the man who taught the Gladiators a lesson in humility…His name…Wesley “Two Scoops” Berry. He was the first Gladiators World Champion, and we’ll see if he still can kick it up to 110 percent. [Out comes another black man who is a little bigger Coz Worthington, and he’s wearing the same thing as the first two contenders except Green.] Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: And we are… POMP! (Whistle!) Laser: HEAD SHOT! YEAH! Larry: You were saying Mike? Mike: I can’t believe it! Laser with incredible accuracy, delivering Two Scoops NO POINTS!! Larry: All I can say is wow. Mike: Well, absolutely disappointing for Wesley Berry in this event. But now moving onto our final challenger, Eddie Mandrake, a professional wrestler representing Primetime Championship Wrestling. He, in a televised interview, called for the challenge, and he got it. [Out walks Eddie, wearing the same thing as the other three competitors, except a very deep purple.] Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: Laser looking for the head shot again, but this time he misses, and Eddie gets to station number one. He grabs the crossbow…takes aim…and fires… BONG! [Sires and smoke begin to wail everywhere.] Larry: BULLSEYE! TEN POINTS FOR EDDIE! [Eddie releases no emotion as he stares down Laser who is getting a rather large smoke treatment.] Mike: Incredible shot by Eddie Mandrake, he hit the little white spot on target, and he gets ten points for it. Therefore at the end of one event, Eddie Mandrake leads with ten points, Rico Costantino in second with five points, Coz Worthington in third with one point, and Wesley Berry in last with no points as of yet. Larry: Stay Tuned folks! Coming up next, Breakthrough and Conquer! [Fade to commercial]
