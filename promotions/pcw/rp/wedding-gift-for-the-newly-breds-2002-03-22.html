Wedding Gift for the NEWly BREDS.

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/22/2002 *Time off. For the normal, everyday wrestler is a much needed break from the every day bump and grind. For the World Champ, a day off is few and far between. It’s always a welcomed change from facing hateful fans, and from having to beat down another jackass that thinks he is worthy of a title shot. So, what does a World Class Champion, such as Chris Sloboda do when faced with a few days off? Well, he doesn’t sit in a room, and punch an inanimate object in hopes that his wife will miraculously be saved from death. I don’t whine about getting shafted out of a title shot at the PPV, only to be stuck wrestling a curtain jerker match against some closet homosexual. He also doesn’t sit on his laurels, like his opponent, his thorn in his side. Sure, if his ego was bigger than his actual wrestling talent, then he would sit back, and do nothing, make no noise. But, Boda is far from egotistical. He is far above whining. Boda goes where he goes when ever he is able to catch a break. HOME.* *After pissing off Miya again, after watching Chase become the #1 World Title contender, Boda has returned home. But, unlike previous Boda promos, this one will not feature the wife, and kids. No. Because, first, Chris is to smart for that. Miya is getting desperate, so why give him the opportunity to get at The Champs family. No, instead, Boda’s family is safe, some where else. Of course, this doesn’t make Boda scared for his wife and kids. It makes him smart. See, Miya gets a hold of Stacey. or Carleigh or Mackenzie, and he holds them, making Boda give up his blood to save Natalie. So, being the smart Champion.... Chris has them very well secured. And, speaking of Chris..... Chris Chase is and has suddenly become much more than an annoying pest that you can not get rid of. Now, amidst the chaos of Boda/Miya, some how, Chris Chase, the other closet homosexual, has gotten thrust into the spot light. Oh well, wrestling Chase is like paying taxes. It’s something one has to do. So, since I’ve gotten off on a tangent, I’ll get back to the point of this paragraph. Boda is home, in Orlando. No, not visiting the wife and kids. But, actually, taking care of some business. See not only is Boda the World Champ, and a damn good one at that [here comes the typical champion ego],but Chris Sloboda is a business man of sorts. Overlook for a moment all the Czech background. Look past the Red Army days, and remember what Chris Chase seems to touch on promo after promo. Boda is a former NBA star. And, though it was only 5 short years that he played for the Orlando Magic, he was smart. Boda saved the millions and millions a year he made. He invested, said millions, and soon, those invested millions had baby millions, and thus, Chris Sloboda has and lives a wealthy lifestyle. TANGENTS, I keep going off on tangents. Instead of getting to the point that no matter when, no matter where, it doesn’t take strolling minstrel bands, or crying over a dying wife to make Boda the MAN, the PCW World Champ, hell, it doesn’t take all that to make BODA PCW. Actually, the real point is that, while enjoying some time off, Boda is downtown, in Orlando, getting set to attend a business meeting.* SCENE: Downtown Orlando. In the proximity of Orange Blossom Trail, and Sand Lake Rd. Site: The Florida Mall. Time: Oh, it’s about noon time. *So, here we go. Boda is in the Florida Mall. Dressed in normal, everyday Orlando weather type clothes. [shorts and a tee-shirt]. He’s seemingly lost in something other than being the man that will deny Chase that which he seeks. Boda walks in and out of virtually every store. Finally, coming to Letchers, he stands for a minute, before going in. We are grateful enough to be allowed to watch as Boda walks up and down each and every isle. Finally, after what seems like forever, Boda stops, grabs a can opener and heads for the front counter* Boda: “This will be perfect for those two.” *The hulking monster of a World Champ slowly strolls to the counter, to pay for the can opener. When he does get to the register, he is bombarded by a friendly, maybe over friendly, cashier* Sally: “Hello, did you find everything you need sir?” *Boda tries to be polite and smiles. He places the can opener on the counter* Sally: “Oh, the Nipendenso 1000. Nice choice. But, if I may make a suggestion. I’d go with the Spinkter5000. It’s a much better can opener for the money. It’s got more features, and is quieter.” Boda: “Jeez, really? Man, decisions, decisions. Tell you what Sally. I’m buying this as gift for two guys at work. They are really, I mean really close [winks]. These two are as tight as a married couple. And, I heard rumors that they are indeed going to tie the knot soon. But, that’s only after I get done beating the living shit out of the one. The ‘man’ of the said relationship. His name is Chris, and he’s a jackass. He thinks his shit doesn’t stink, and to tell you the truth, I enjoy kicking peoples asses, but this time, with Chris..... I’m going to take GREAT PLEASURE in beating him and his ego down.” *The look Sally has on her face now, after listening to Boda spew off about beating Chris Chase straight back to jobberville, is unexplainable. She looks rather scared too. Boda finally catches on, and explains to her.* Boda: “Hey, don’t fret.....no injury will come to me as a result of the decimation of Chris Chase. In fact, I wouldn’t mind snapping the neck of Corey Williams, Chase’s ‘special’ friend, if Williams sticks his nose in. I.....” Sally: “Sir...... you’re scaring me. And the others.” *Chris looks around. The others Sally is referring to is the customers shopping in Letchers.* Boda: “Sorry. I sometimes get carried away when it comes to work. I mean, after all, I’m the best at it.” *Sally gives Chris a look of ‘what the fuck’* Sally: “Work? Sounds more like wrestling. And I hate wrestling.” *Chris looks at Sally as if his puppy just died* Boda: “Hate wrestling? Hate wrestling? Jesus, hating wrestling is so damn un-American. Shit, Sally hating wrestling is like hating apple pie, or sweet tea. It’s not right. Now, on the other hand, you can hate a wrestler. Like, say for instance..... Chris Chase. Hating him is what one should do. If there is one wrestler to hate, his initials would be Chris Chase. Now, if you actually liked Chris Chase.... Sally, that would make you one of two things. First, it’d make you a sorry excuse for a wrestling fan. Even though the fans are sorry to begin with, that would make you the sorriest. Secondly, and this is important, pay attention.....Liking Chris Chase makes you a complete, utter, imbecile. Liking Chris Chase is like liking a flaming 10 inch needle shoved into your spine, and turned 360 degrees. And even a tough son of a bitch like me wouldn’t like that. I.......” *At this point, Sally has lost it, and is seen screaming as she runs from the cash register. Boda stops, and stares, shaking his head. He looks at a woman in line behind him, and smiles* Boda: “She asked about my work.” *The store manager, Mike, walks to the register, to replace Sally. He recognizes Chris as Orlando’s own BAD ASS.* Mike: “Wow, Boda. I thought you weren’t shopping here ever again after the last time.” Boda: “Mike, hi..... hey, ixnay on the hoppingsay.” *Boda points to the cameraman.* Mike: “Oh, you’re shooting a promo. Sorry. So, will this do it for you sir?” Boda: “Yes.” Mike: “So, how goes the world of PCW, Champ?” Boda: “Very well. Well, considering I have to waste my time defending my title against a man with as much talent as a snail, it’s doing all right. I’ve got the title, I’ve got the fame, and finally, I am getting the damn respect I deserve. So, Mike, if you don’t mind, I’ll be paying cash for this, and heading out. I got a meeting to attend.” Mike: “Sure... that’ll be $35.55.” *Boda pays for the can opener, and leaves. He walks through the mall, and out into the parking lot. He walks to his new 2002 Lincoln Blackwood, unlocking it and starting it with his remote keyless entry. As he opens the driver’s door, he turns to face the camera, and he shoots his usual demeanor look.* Boda: “Chase.... I know you are watching this. I know you’re sitting there, in the comfort of your home. You probably got Dustin rubbing your feet, and Corey sucking your..... well, this is a PG promo, so I’ll leave that part to your imagination. You see what I have done to Miya lately? I know you’ve witnessed not only the physical beatings, but also the mental games. Well, Chasey boy..... keep fucking with me, keep trying to come after me and MY title, and I’ll make what I’ve done to Miya,..... look like a kindergarten scuffle compared to an all out RIOT. Now, about Sunday......this Sunday, you, my little friend, get the first taste of a Boda sized ass whipping. It starts on Sunday, and ends at Spring Fling. All the insults, they mean nothing to me. Keeping this title.... MEANS THE WORLD TO ME, and I’ll do anything, and I mean anything to keep the World Title. If that means beating your mom down... then so be it. If it means destroying your other half, Williams, then the pleasure would be all mine. Shooting to the point, because that’s what I do.... Chase.... Chris.... just because you beat me once, last year... doesn’t mean your facing the same Boda this time. In case you’ve seemed to have forgotten.... I hold the PCW World Title. Last time we faced, I was equivalent to merely a low carder at best. So, this isn’t the same low card dance partner this time around. See you on Sunday, boy. I’ll bring your new can opener too.” *As Chris gets into his Blackwood, his cell phone rings. Boda answers it as the scene fades out* Boda: “Ok, I’m on my way there. Yes sir.”
