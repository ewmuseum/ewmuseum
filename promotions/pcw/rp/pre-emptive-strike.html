Pre-Emptive Strike.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered “Gentlemen! Behold my latest creation benefiting mankind!!” Lightning punctuates the statement, the close proximity of eh bolt illuminating the windows and shaking the walls with a mighty thunderclap. The damp stone walls of the castle crackle with energy, as a heaving scientist in a lab coat throws back his head, cackling in unabashed glee. “BWAHAHAHAHA!!” A lone assistant looks around, confused. “Uh, there’s only me here, sir…” “SILENCE!! For years I have slaved, toiled, poured my very ESSENCE into my project, which now is complete! Here!” The mad scientist roars in victory as he pulls on a suspended chord, releasing the sheet covering a huge, demonic-looking device, towering high into the ceiling. Great stationary orbs glow with an eerie light, encircling the base as a cannon tube sits atop, able to rotate and focus in any direction. Another lightning shot strikes outside in the tempest, adding to the evil aura of the machine. The assistant looks at the machine quizzically, checking his clipboard. “I thought we were looking for a cure for cancer…” “And what do you think this is?!” Thunderclap as the assistant pushes up his thick glasses, running his hand over his chin. “It looks to be a Focusing Disintegrator Device.” “EXCELLENT!! Apparently I chose well at the San Dimas High Career Day!! Here’s a cookie!” “But sir,” the assistant stammers, accepting the chocolate chip cookie, “How is a Focusing Disintegrator Device a cure for cancer?” A dark glower falls over the scientist’s face as he turns to a window, another bolt silhouetting him from the darkness. “The academy… they all said I was a fool! Mad with ambition, they all cried out in unison! Well, I showed them that being in the top 60% really DOES mean something!! And today, I have proven it!! This device…” the scientist whirls, gesturing grandly to his creation, “Is the end of all known cancer treatments! This laser is so effective in destroying targeted tissue, so precise as to the area affected, and so deliberate in it’s dosage as to render all other forms of treatment obsolete!!” “By doing…” “Egad man!! Have you NOT been involved in my creation at all? It completely OBLITERATES human tissue, scattering it to the four winds!! And once it is locked on target to the cancerous lesion, it does the same, wiping the tumor clean from the host, the beam only focused on the targeted cancer cells!! WHO DA MAN?!” “But,” presses the assistant, chomping on his hard-earned cookie, “Why is the cannon so BIG?” “Alas, the technology of the era forces my hand. I can’t miniaturize the cannon without sacrificing the process. Ah! But perhaps a demonstration is in order!!” “No, its okay. I’ve seen it work before…” “Quick!” shouts the scientist, “Get me a 18 lbs. frozen turkey!!” The assistant sighs and goes to a nearby freezer, producing a magnificent bird, ripe for roasting. He then quickly gets out of the way to the scientist’s side, who is already busily slapping at buttons and switches. Both drop a pair of black goggles over their eyes as the machine moans, powering up the laser cels. The cannon tube begins to slowly light up, concentric circles glowing brighter and brighter. “Now, watch in awe as I eliminate a pocket of flesh behind the breasts, a perfect hole the size of a quarter!!” The laser whines and the tip of the cannon grows red. Suddenly, the beam erupts from the end, only in a wide spray instead of a pencil-thin beam. All that remains of the turkey is the bones, nary a speck of meat left behind. The scientist is stunned as he shuts the beam off. He walks out to the bones and picks them up gently, almost tenderly. “I… I don’t get it…” “We go over this EVERY night!” yells the assistant, throwing his clipboard to the floor in disgust, “The cannon beam is too wide! That’s as thin as we can get it with the end we have attached. In fact, it can go much, much wider…” “But, my calculations…” “Are wrong, old man! They were for a cannon with a much smaller energy plant, but in order to eliminate the cancer completely, without missing a single atom, we had to increase the power plant size. Every, friggin’ night!! When are you gonna get it?!” BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! CRASH!! Both the scientists look up in surprise, speaking in unison. “Big Alan Steele!!” “What am I?” pouts Big Al’s sidekick, the Masochist, asa he steps over the fallen, iron door, “Chopped liver?” Steele places a gentle hand on the Masochist’s shoulder, an almost fatherly smile on his face. “Indeed you are not, old friend! Come now! We have business afoot!” Our two heroes, whom we now rejoin, one of which Big and Bold, Big Alan Steele, set about their ‘business’ as it were. They move with purpose about the laser disintegrator. “Excuse me? Excuse me?!” queries the assistant, moving to block our demi-god hero, “”EXCUSE ME?! Just what in the blue Hell do you think you are doing?” “By the gods!!” exclaims Steele, “But where are our manners, young Lord of Pain?! My deepest apologies, youthful assistant to great blooming evil, I am Big Alan Steele and this is my compatriot, the Masochist.” “Yo,” says the Masochist, waving as he places a small, rectangular block to the base of the laser. “I know who you are,” sniffs the assistant, “But what I want to know… wait. ‘Blossoming evil.’ What are you talking about?” “This device,” starts Big Al, “Your master here is a brilliant man…” “Though a little short on cards in the deck,” chimes in the Masochist, as he sticks a timer to the block. “Indeed!! Your master has great intentions and an intellect to possibly allow those intentions to come to fruition! However, what he has in intent, he lacks in sanity. Though HE is not technically evil, yon machination of great power conceals a dark power, capable of being used in terrible ways!! Why, its possible uses could frighten you to the quick!” The assistant’s face grows red with anger, “It’s a MACHINE!! It has neither good nor evil intentions! It is as you use it, a mere tool! And this tool is a weapon against cancer!” “Nay! Though on the surface it may seem as such, but it hath neither the scope nor focus to be a useful tool in medical science.” “So then now what?” a shaking assistant, asks, “Now what happens?!” The head of the masochist pops out over the final power cel. “All the C4 is set, Big guy.” “You’re going to BLOW IT UP?!?!” Big Al urns, speaking gravely, “I see no other option. This beam could singe the flesh off of entire armies, should be set so!! The world is not in need of such a device!” The scientist shrugs, “If it’s for the common good…” “FOR THE COMMON GOOD?!” screeches the assistant, grabbing the scientist by the collar, “We have spent years of research and years of our LIVES on this thing… and it’s a cure for cancer!! That has to be right, right?!” “Meh… not necessarily…” “Please gentlemen,” A soothing Big Al pressures, “Please move away so we can destroy this abomination, this perversion of science – the smoking remains climbing to the heavens, an ashen offering to the gods of Olympia!! Hi-Keeba!!” “Can I push the button this time?” Begs the Masochist. “NO ONE,” bellows the assistant, his voice becoming stronger, “is pushing ANY button!! I have spent half my life in the service of that old fool, developing this disintegration device, and I shall NOT allow you to ruin a lifetime of work!!” The assistant runs over to the machine, holding his arms out wide, in an attempt to delay the detonation. “Easy there, Randy,” soothes the scientist, “I can see their point. Should some evil force gain a hold of our invention in its current state, untold wanton destruction would be soon to follow.” “You don’t get it, you old goat,” snarls the assistant, his voice taking on a sinister, feminine tone, “They want to destroy that which we have wrought! I won’t allow it!” Steele nods knowingly to the Masochist, who slowly clears the area. Big Al pulls a small, handheld device from within the folds of his toga. Slowly, he moves the scientist back from the blast zone. “What are you doing?!” cries the scientist, “You can’t blow it with my assistant still there!!” “Oh, can I not?” smarms Big Al. “Fools! Wretches!! You are making tragic mistakes with dire consequences!!” wails the assistant, his voice now full octaves higher, a hollow, lilting quality to it, “You toy with force you cannot comprehend!! This device will allow me to rule the… er… cure cancer!” “Wait a minute!” barks the scientist, a dawning realization on his features, “It wasn’t ME who made the focusing calculations!! That was YOU, Randy!!” “Clear,” states Big Al, calmly. FWA-BOOOOOOOOM!!! The C4 detonates, blowing the base and collapsing the structure in on itself. ‘Randy’ manages to run and dive, but failed to escape the concussive blast, tossing him end over end and crashing into a nearby table of scientific equipment, groaning in pain. The scientist, Big Al, and the Masochist all rise up from the safe positions, noting the destruction. “Well done, Lord of Pain!” salutes Big Al with a thumbs-up, “Again, your knowledge of demolitions astounds me!! To contain such a ferocious blast in such a small quarter…” “Bah,” shyly acknowledges the Masochist, “Twern’t nuthin’.” “My machine…” quietly asserts the scientist. But soon, he freezes in horror at the sight upon the table. “My assistant!!” ‘Randy’ has assumed a ghastly shape of a long, ugly purple female with pseudopodia, almost like a giant centipede. It’s limbs thrash about in pain, as green eyes lock onto Big Alan Steele with unadulterated hatred. “You shall pay for this, Big Alan Steele!!” the shrieking, high pitched voice assails. “What is the meaning of this?!” “unfortunately, good man of science, you have been coerced and seduced by one of the most diabolical evils imaginable!! The vile and treacherous doppelganger, able to assume the form and voice of any being on Earth!! You are lucky to have survived in its presence this long!! Thank the gods, noble scientist… thank the gods!!” “Yeah,” adds the Masochist, “She probably only kept you alive so that she could gain the weapon.” “A weapon? But I had only intended it…” “Intended it only for the purpose of good? HA!!” chuckles Big Al, “If I had a dinari for each time I had heard that!” “Don’t blame yourself, sir,” says the Masochist, “You aren’t the first to fall for it.” Big Al wags a finger in front of the scientist, “Indeed, the road to villainy is paved with the good intentions of fine, upstanding citizens such as yourself, man of science!” ‘There are others?” “Of course!! Why, you are merely our third stop of this dark evening!!” “Yeah, we’ve got at least 4 more stops to go, too,” reiterates the Masochist. “Where shall we go now, Lord of Pain?” “Let’s hit Dr. Horaotio’s next, and his machine that creates giant tsunamis.” “Giant tsunamis?” ponders the scientist. Big Al smiles… “All in the service of good, of course! A dastardly attempt at controlling the ocean’s tides to better aid sea travel… but middling within Poseidon’s domain?! Hi-Keeba!! These mortals know not what which they meddle!!” “So that’s it, huh? You just show up and destroy things before they become problems.” “And no better plan shall you find!! Tis all relative, as like my opponent come Tuesday, the Pharaoh! He truly believes himself good, but his false gods shall fail him, and the error of his ways shall become apparent, once they are shown in the illuminating light of the gods of Olympia… my gods!! Hi-Keeba!” The Masochist frowns. “That may have been your lamest tie-in yet, Big guy.” “Shut up,” growls Steele, as he stuffs the doppelganger into a burlap sack, “It had to be done! Now come… the night is young! We must relegate this villainous scum to the proper authorities and continue our night of cleansing…” “Hi-Keeba!”
