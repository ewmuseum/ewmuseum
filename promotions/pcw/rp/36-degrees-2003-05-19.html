36 Degrees

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/05/03 My black hair glistened in the sunlight as I drove along the road. A half brunt cigarette hung from my mouth and every now and then the ashes would fall off and burn into my arm before falling to the floor and dissolving. In the state I was in now, I was hoping perhaps the ashes would set the car ablaze and let me die. It's better to burn out than fade away after all. For an hour, I felt alive and human and perfect and complete and happy and full of emotion. And then, after that hour past and after the effects of my dream had faded, I felt dead again. I felt my temperature dropping back down. The average body temperature is 37.5 degrees Celsius I dropped to 37.4.37.337.237.137.0Down. Down. Down. Lower. Faster. Falling. 36 degrees Celsius. At 36 degrees Celsius, you're below average. When you're below average, you're weak. When you're weak, you're vunerable. When you're vunerable, you're worthless. When you're worthless you amount to just what God will tell you that you are. Nothing. .... Some say that Life's a bitch.... .... Personally, I think that's a little harsh on the old girl.... .... Sure, she likes to throw you the occasional swerve ball.... .... Yeah, she doesn't always play nice, or hell, even fairly a lot of the time.... .... But a bitch....? .... That's just cruel.... .... Still, there's one thing you've got to give her credit for.... .... There's never a dull moment with her around.... .... She always likes to keep you on your toes, or at least, she does me anyway.... .... Don't get me wrong though.... .... I'm not defending the things she does.... .... It's obvious that she dishes out her fair share of pain and suffering, as well as the few moments of joy that we all wait patiently for.... .... It doesn't matter who you are, or how much money you've got in the bank, or have many diseases you've found the cures for, or how many banks you've robbed.... .... She treats everyone in the same way.... .... Equality for all.... .... Kinda poetic, ain't it....? .... Nah, I don't think Life is a bitch.... .... I think she's just a little misunderstood, s'all.... .... Others say that Life is like a box of chocolates.... .... You never know what you're gonna get.... .... Me, I'm not big on surprises.... .... When I unwrap a sweet expecting to find a strawberry flavoured chocolaty treat, there'd better be one.... .... However, that isn't always the case.... .... Life never goes exactly to plan, EVER.... .... Sometimes what we think is strawberry turns out to be coconut.... .... And boy do I hate coconuts.... .... My opinion on Life is somewhat different however.... .... I see it as big ass, cheap-looking seventies game show.... .... You roll your dice, you make your moves, and you can even play your trump card if you get a nice triple score full of luck.... .... The only difference between this and something like Family Feuds though is that there are no rules or instructions.... .... There is only us.... .... WE make the rules.... .... WE run the show.... .... WE have the choice to do whatever we want with it.... .... What I want is to climb towards the top of the PCW mountain.... .... I've been so close before.... .... But I think I'm ready to make the push now.... .... And that push begins next Friday in the Tables Match for the Extreme Title.... .... It's time to spin the wheel fans, and there's only two things it can fall on.... .... Will it be Win or will it be Lose....? ...Either way it will be Hard Work!...
