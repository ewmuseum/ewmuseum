Inner Demons or Outer Demons? Part 6...Montana...

20 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-20-2002 Dawn breaks over the city of St. Helena, Montana’s fair capital. The citizens wake from their cozy slumbers, and all seems right with the world. The shot rushes through the city, and finds a large Knight’s Inn. Through the doors and up the stairs, to the very top floor. This floor is one room. The shot pans around and lights on our hero, Venneman Pendragon. Shirtless, he stands at the window, peering out onto the city. The prominent muscles of his back tense and loosen, tense and loosen, as if he is wrestling with himself. Now from outside the window, we get a look at Pendragon’s face. His eyes are filled with worry, and his mouth is set in a stone frown. He shakes his head as he stares into the calm city…. “I tried. Truly I did. I tried to stay awake, but ‘twas impossible. Asleep in yonder chair, I know not what the night held for me. Did I sleep the night away, as any normal person would? Or did I creep from the shadows of mine room into the darkness of the city at night? I fear to read the newspaper headlines…I fear to leave the room, lest someone recognize me as the perpetrator of some heinous act. Only one way to find out for certain…” Pendragon strides to the bed and pulls on his lined shirt of armor, straps on his sword and dons a black cloak. He sighs as he leaves the room and heads or the elevator. He steps through the open door, and it begins to shut… “I only hope what I find does not serve to haunt me….” ********************************************************************** The scene cuts to show the front of the hotel, from which a heavy hearted Venneman Pendragon emerges into a bright day. Snow covers the ground, but the sun is shining, and everyone else looks happy. He walks down the street and the first thing that catches his interest is a small newspaper stand. He slowly walks up to the old man working the stand, and asks for a paper…. Pendragon: Morning paper…Please… The old man turns and his face lights up. He grabs a paper and quickly hands it to the waiting Pendragon… Old Man: Here it is! On the house! Whatever you want! You deserve it, young fella! Pendragon: What is it of which thy speak? Thou knowest me not… Old Man: And so modest…I like that… Pendragon shakes his head and turns to walks away. Suddenly he stops, and puts a hand to his head. The screen wavers and suddenly changes….. *************FLASH************** Same place, only now night shrouds the city. The same old man gets his newspaper stand ready for his morning business. Out of the darkness, comes a young white guy with a shaved head. He is a large fellow and just looks mean. He snarls at the man and pulls a knife… Shaved Head: All right, Old man….Gimme all your money….. Old Man:…… Shaved Head: NOW OLD MAN!!! Voice: I think thee would be wise to find other pastures in which to graze, whelp…. From the darkness, steps…Venneman Pendragon! His eyes glow red with fury, and finally we see that it is truly him who has been taking the nighttime jaunts, as if we did not know already….But, as they say, seeing is believing. The Shaved fella punches the old man in the face and turns to face the man stickin his nose in where it doesn’t belong… Shaved Head: What’s it to you, freak? Dressed up like some sissy ass faggot! Why dontcha just move along, sissy boy…. The red eyed Pendragon shakes his head and frowns at the man hurling the childish insults. Shaved Head waves his knife in the direction of the large blonde man… Pendragon: Art thee going to wave that steel? Or find a place to place that pig sticker….. The Shaved man lunges in and Pendragon deflects the knife and delivers a quick punch to his face. The man staggers back and wipes the blood from his lip… Pendragon: Is that all that thee has? Shaved Head: Alright, faggot...Now I’m pissed off! The man rushes in but Pendragon catches his hand and twists it around….Hard. There is an audible snap and the knife drops to the ground. The shaved man follows soon after, his forearm now hanging at an extremely terrible angle…. Shaved Head: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH!! The man holds his arm as he drops to his knees, and Pendragon frowns down at him, red eyes glowing in the night… Pendragon: Thou knowest that thy actions were indeed questionable. Thee have been punished for thy transgression. Let me not see thee again. I shan’t be so merciful next time… Pendragon kicks the man in the face before sinking back into the shadows, and the old man simply gapes with wonder at the cloaked hero… ************FLASH************ Back to the present, Pendragon shakes his head with the memory and quickly walks away. People clear out of the way when they see the look of confusion on the giant man’s face, but he notices them not. He just strides headlong into the crowd, not knowing where it is he goes…. “Good Christ! I did it once more…What I do not understand though, is why I am doing virtuous things while under the control of mine dark side brought on by the prophecy. It is a frightful thing to know that thy body does things that thee has no control over. I need answers…” WHAT ANSWERS DO THEE SEEK, BROTHER? Pendragon: We are not brothers, thee and I… THAT IS CORRECT…WE ARE THE SAME, BUT I CALL THEE BROTHER FOR SANITY’S SAKE… Pendragon: thee are one to speak of sanity, whilst thee drives me from mine… THOU KNOWEST WHAT THOU MUST DO, DOES THY NOT? Pendragon: Not listening… ‘TIS STONEHENGE THAT HOLDS THY ANSWERS. ‘TIS THERE THAT THE TWO OF US MUST BECOME ONE. ONLY THEN WILL THE PROPHECY BE COMPLETED… Pendragon: Will thou not cease to vex me, demon??? NAY…NO DEMON. A PIECE OF THEE THOU NEVER KNEW WAS THERE. I AM THE DARK SIDE, BUT BY EMBRACING ME, THEE CAN FINALLY BECOME WHOLE. WE ARE THE LIGHT, THEE AND I. LET NOT THY MIND BE FILLED BY THOSE WHO CONDEMN DARKNESS. WITHOUT DARK, THERE CAN BE NO LIGHT. THE UNIVERSE IS ALL ABOUT A BALANCE. AS SUCH, BEING CREATURES OF THE UNIVERSE, HUMANS SEEK A BALANCE AS WELL. THEE HAS BEEN LACKING THAT BALANCE FOR SOME TIME NOW. DOST THOU NOT SEE? UNLESS THEE COMPLETES THE TASK THAT REUNINTES OUR PERSONA, DARKNESS WILL TRIUMPH, FOR THEE ARE ILL EQUIPPED TO DO BATTLE WITH IT. HOW CAN ONE DEFEAT DARKNESS WHEN ONE HAS NONE OF HIS OWN? TO KILL IT, THOU HAST TO LOVE IT A LITTLE…. Pendragon gets a strange look in his eyes as the voice drones on, and he runs right into a hot dog vendor…. Vendor: Hey buddy….You ok? Pendragon shakes his head and his eyes clear a bit… Pendragon: Aye….I am fine…Thanks… The man’s eyes widen…. Vendor: HEY!!! You’re Venom Pendeon!! Pendragon slaps his hand over his eyes and shakes his head…. Pendragon: ‘Tis “VENNEMAN PENDRAGON!” Thy Lord King and Knightly savior. AYE!! THEE TELL HIM!!! Pendragon: Shut up… Vendor: Ok… Pendragon: Not thee…..Never mind… Vendor: Sir…Would it be asking a lot to get an autograph? Pendragon: Autograph? Umm…Yeah, no problem… The man pulls out a napkin and hands it and a pen to Pendragon. Pendragon looks down at the napkin that reads “Dick’s Wieners”. He smiles at the name and scratches his signature down. The man takes the napkin and smiles broadly. Pendragon: May I? Vendor: Sure! The vendor dips out a hotdog and hands it to Pendragon who eats it in two bites… HUNGRY? Pendragon: Missed me breakfast this morn… Vendor: I understand…That’ll be two bucks! Pendragon looks at him incredulously…After all, he did give the man an autograph. Sighing, he reaches into his cloak and pulls out a five…. Pendragon: Keep it, buddy…You need it… Pendragon strolls off though the crowd now, clearer headed for having eaten something, yet the words of his “dark half” weigh upon his mind… “Continental gold on the line here soon, and I cant stop worrying about mine other half…What is the words I speak to mineself are the truth? But if ‘tis really me, why would I drive myself crazy?” I TRY TO GET THEE TO SEE…. “And why would I interrupt mine own self?” HUMPH… “To defeat the darkness, one must love it just a little…It does make sense. Or does it? I know there are forces who would try to unseat the lord Pendragon as the bastion of light, the savior of truth. Blast this damned prophecy! I have an important matchup Friday…” AYE…I DO HAVE A BIG MATCH…I WILL CLAIM THE CONTINENTAL TITLE AS MINE OWN… “Tommy lacks focus…He is an idolater. He of the naked statue. Blast it! How can he be mine opponent? A worshipper of an idol and an herb? All natural yes….But good Christ! When I left, the man toiled in the tag division…I return and now I must strip him of mine Continental Title?” AYE. ‘TIS OUR TITLE, VEN. “And why would I call myself Venn? I am the Lord King….I even think myself as that. Thee has much to learn…” AYE…THOU HAST KEPT ME UNDER WRAPS FOR QUITE SOME TIME. I AM JUST NOW COMING INTO OUR OWN. WE NEED ONE ANOTHER, LORD KING….THEE AND I. WE WILL CONQUER ALL…THE DARKNESS, THE LIGHT AND ALL THAT STANDS IN OUR WAY. I AND THEE. IT WILL BE SO. “Maybe that is what scares me so. That perhaps I will conquer all….After that, what will there be to accomplish?” TO BE CONTINUED
