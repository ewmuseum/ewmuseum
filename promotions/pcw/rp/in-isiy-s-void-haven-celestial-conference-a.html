In Isiy's Void Haven...{-Celestial Conference 'A'-}

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered 'What is more important towards the victory of battle? The physical aspects of the win...or the mental aspects?' ~Savage Tranquility, page undetermined 'What do you feel when we run our fingers through your hair? Is it hate?' Black nailed fingers running through strands of equal darkness...falling down in a cascade of black. 'We know it is hate...but hate for whom? Us...or...him?' Those nails again...tracing the lining of a petite jaw line... 'Why would you hate us? We have done all we could to suit your needs as you stay here. We have given you pleasures that only a Celestial could make you feel...not like what he could do to you.' Fingers curving around the small collarbone...lining a small cut above her breastplate. 'Do you think he is any closer towards gaining you back, dear one? Just because he has managed to take something from us?' One finger...pressed lightly against her moist black lips... 'Shhh...there is no need for words. We are not the one to be hated here. It is he...who should be hated. After all...it is he who is slowly losing his mind...to the Void.' And our vision suddenly expands...so that everything is seen in the area. Gray skies lofted overhead...not gray clouds, but actual gray skies. Below...black blades of grass fluttered in the light breeze, matching the pattern of the black waxy leaves sprouting out of the black barked tree...standing solemnly amidst the field of black grass. Off to the side, a river of darkness flowed with a gentle trickle, the black liquid looking to be thin and light, yet no one dared to test how deep it really ran. Say hello...to the Void... ...Isiy's haven, to be more specific... Akiyama stood there...as usual...in his vinyl ensemble. Over his shoulder, instead of the Akivision title, was his silver ponytail. He had been defeated by Pharaoh Fusion...his TV belt gone...but you think something as petty as that is going to phase him so greatly? No frickin' way. Across from him...stood Isiy. Her lithe frame stood against the wide frame of the tree, and her hands were bound. Not by rope, or chain, or any other type of mortal restraint. Her hands were lodged into the bark of the tree, as if they had grown around her wrists to trap her. Her feet were lodged into the black earth beneath her, leaving her sprawled open in an X formation dressed in only her black satin bra and panties. She glared up at the god with her dark eyes...there was hate there... 'You should hate your husband.' the NVG said nonchalantly. 'He's the one strategizing on defeating us ...he speaks of us more than he does of you these days. He's letting himself become consumed with jealousy and anger and hate and revenge...succumbing to the darkness that exists within him.' He held up a glass of pure crystal filled with a clear, yet rich red liquid. He held it under his nose and inhaled the aroma. Then he took a sip with his pinky propped up in the air. 'You know he was out celebrating...basking in his temporary glory just because he won a silly little mortal title from us. That title has been tainted for so long by tons of mortals before us...and just when you thought there was hopes for making the thing truly divine...someone like Pharaoh comes along and wins possession of it...dropping its value lower than before. Insects don't even want that belt anymore...and neither do we.' He leans in closer to her, his nose grazing the tip of hers. 'He's out celebrating...drinking...partying with Horus...while you're still here captive.' Akiyama stepped back and took another sip of the drink, the ice clinking against the sides of the crystal. 'Of course if our most cherished love one was taken hostage by some... 'evil'...' he quoted his fingers at that word. 'We would be out there searching aimlessly for her, no matter the circumstances. But not our good friend Titu...he's out drinking that mortal swill called whiskey with his mentor...flaunting the belt off to mortals who are now even above spitting on such a lowly championship. He might as well start getting used to drinking mortal drinks and eating mortal food. But you must give your husband a bit of credit...he's so excited about winning that belt. A belt that we elevated just by winning...a belt that we barely carried around with us in public, yet still it basked in glory because we owned it. It's that optimism that makes you feel sorry for him, isn't it, Isiy?' The god turned back towards her, only to find her still glaring into him. He chuckled a bit, his free hand scratching the back of his head. 'You pity someone who would think so positively upon himself...yet has so little to work with. You know how pathetic he really is...you've been married to him for thousands of years. Why did you marry that bug-eyed chimp anyway? Could you not find any other ways to grab hold of godhood other than to wed that mangy mongrel? Heh...maybe you didn't have much to work with in Egypt.' Another sip of the Divine Blood. 'In the beginning...we held all the cards...and he had nothing. But now he has the TV belt...that's right, once Pharaoh won that belt, it lost all value and right to be called the Akivision title. He has the TV belt...so now there's an equal advantage playing field between us. He wants you...dear Isiy...and we...we want the TV belt back.' He leaned in close, his lips breathing cool breaths into her ear. 'Or do we?' Isiy looked on confused as Akiyama smiled at her...that dark grin we have all grown to fear and hate. 'What kind of a god would we be if we were so intent on taking away a piece of leather and metal from him? What kind of deity would we be if all we wanted was gold? By the Void...we created an entire realm!' His hand waved across the entire area for the Egyptian goddess to see. 'If we have the power to create realms...how much pleasure are we going to get by taking a small strap away from Pharaoh? Answer...none. You see...this fight has nothing to do with belts and who can gain back wives or any other sh!t like that. This battle is about...who will lose their godhood and who will rise above as the god of PCW. If Pharaoh wants to be shallow and pride himself upon holding materialistic gold...let him be a shallow god. It will make it all the more sweeter to beat the godhood out of him come this Sabbath.' Akiyama took another sip of his drink, and brought it back from his lips with a sigh. 'You see...your husband is the one who's been disadvantaged the entire time. With each passing day, he grows more and more confident that he's ready to fight us at Spring Fling. By the Void...he's even stopped training because he believes just because he was able to capture the TV belt that he's more than ready to take care of us in combat. He needs to realize something though...he's lost all possible advantage. When this battle was made...it was made with one point of goal in our minds...taking what made Pharaoh everything. That means...taking his godhood. That's all we ever cared about...not that belt...not you, Isiy...only making him MORTAL!!' He exhaled a deep breath and calmed down. Raising the glass to his mouth again, he relaxed again, that smile returning to his lips. 'Have you heard? Have you heard the news?' he stood straight in her face, asking that question over and over again. Then he stepped back with a realizing look on his face. 'No...of course you would not have heard. You've been here all this time. Well here's a news flash for you, sweet one...your dear husband Titu Xansiv...has been FIRED from PCW!' Her hatred faded, and only concern and worry showed in those dark orbs of vision. The god only looked back with his own...always of darkness. 'Yep...came through this Sunday. He's been fired...turns out PCW needed to trim the fat of some slackers, and Pharaoh was the biggest slacker PCW had to offer! Now he's all wallowing in his self despair like that bastard Inphino Blitz! We tell you...it's just getting better and better for the Neo Void God! First Pharaoh stops training, then he gets fired...and next he's talking to a cat and calling it Carmoon!' The ice twirled around in his glass and he took another sip. 'Pharaoh thinks that Carmoon has been reincarnated into a cat. How ignorant is this man? We guess it only proves that no matter how stupid one man may be, his beliefs are just as foolish. But no...wait...Pharaoh will most likely try to use that quote against us in some mundane little pun. How predictable will be be? As for his little friend...it's just more proof that he's no way near capable of fighting us on a mental nor a physical level. Carmoon's not reincarnated...the b!tch-slave isn't even dead! Isn't that right, Carmoon?' To their left, the mammoth slave Carmoon hung from a branch with his hands and legs bound by black portals encircling his wrists and ankles...little mini void portals keeping him bound and helpless. Around his mouth was a black bandana keeping him gagged. His eyes blazed anger towards the god, but the NVG could care less if some slave was angry with him. 'You hear that Carmen? Pharaoh's found replacements for the both of you. He's found himself a new pet in place of you...' he poked a finger into Carmoon's chest before turning to Isiy. 'And you...he's found himself a new p^ssy.' he ran his hand across her cheek. She tried to turn away from his touch, but she turned right into the other awaiting hand of the god. He chuckled as he pressed both palms against her face. 'You see...he's not even thinking about you anymore. You know what his response was when he was fired? He said that he was outraged that he was removed from the company, and that he could not extract his revenge on us...Akira Akiyama...No mention of Isiy...no mention of retaining his godhood...he's only worried about that piece O sh!t title and getting revenge on us. Now you know just how deep we have engrained ourselves into his mind.' She shook her head at him, refusing to believe his words. Akiyama stepped back, a look of hurt shock on his face. 'Oh...you don't believe us? Would you like to hear for yourself?' he snapped his fingers, and all around them the voice of Pharaoh echoed: 'A champion AND a god fired from PCW: This is preposterous. I will be signed back and for more than the salary that I was getting before. Doctor Rupert Healey will not stay in my way of destroying Akira Akiyama.' Isiy's eyes filled with tears...either she believed Akiyama...or she had really missed that rapscallion Pharaoh and his horrible accent. Carmoon was mumbling something in a rage from his bound status next to the god. Akiyama took another sip of his drink and grinned that darkness into him. 'Oh...you have something to say? You wish to speak to us? You wanna converse with your Neo Void God???' He took one long gulp, finishing off his Divine Blood. Then he pulled back and shattered the glass against the jaw of Carmoon. A muffled cry of pain came from him as Akiyama began to crack his knuckles. He began punching into Carmoon's body like he was a boxer and the Egyptian slave was a side of beef. With each punch, Akiyama accented his words with force. 'DON'T...YOU...TALK...TO...US...YOU...SORRY...SACK...O...SH!T! YOU'RE...NOT...WORTHY...OF...BEING...PISSED...ON...BY...US!! IT'S...ALL...ABOUT...BEING...TOP...GOD!!! YOU'RE...NOTHING...SHE'S...NOTHING...PHARAOH'S...NOTHING!!!' 'Hmph...you sound like Charlie Loc over there.' The sweat was beading on the god's forehead as he pummeled Carmoon brutally, but the voice caught him in mid punch. He stood frozen as he noticed a presence behind him. Slowly, he turned to face the new person behind him...he who dared to step into his Void. He recognized him immediately. The blonde, handsome man in all black...and the silver sword in his grasp...plus that smile on his face defined him from all the other Void dwellers. 'Shinnosuke...' the god's growl was ominous. But if you laid eyes on a man who was connected to the single most hated man in your existence...you wouldn't be too pleased with a visit from him either. Yes, it was Shinnosuke, the Void Assassin...messenger and bodyguard of the Daughter of Darkness, Hinageshi. Usually a visit from any of the Void Elements meant crap for the god. Let's see if they were due to remain in their rut of predictability like Pharaoh. 'Long time, no see there, Aki.' that smile never left his face. He was one of the rare Void dwellers who was known for smiling. Akiyama had grown to hate that smile during his tour with the Void Elements back last year. 'My, how you have grown.' 'Assassin...you interrupt the party we're hosting for our guests...invade our realm...and then you play coy with us like we all of a sudden give a rat's @ss about liking you.' how blunt...gotta love that. 'State your business before we suddenly remember the specifics about why we don't like you.' A chuckle from Shinnosuke. 'Same old Akiyama. You're a slave to consistency, aren't you, NVG?' One spark was enough. The god surged on him, his paw locking around his neck and hoisting his body into the air. Shinnosuke was no small man by any means necessary...but with the full rage of the NVG pumping through Akiyama, he was being shaken about like a rag doll. 'You're beginning to bore us...just like Pharaoh...and anyone who has anything to do with Pharaoh is due for a Celestial @sskicking with style from the Neo Void God these days!!' Shin grinned as he peered down into the animosity that swirled in the god's eyes. Then a swift action happened. Shinnosuke has swung his blade around to meet Akiyama's arm. The blade sparked against his skin, but the god still dropped him down to the ground and retracted his arm. He glared down at the mark where Shinnosuke's blade had struck him. There was no cut...but there was a faint red welt appearing. The Void Assassin grinned at the sight. 'Oooh...someone's been working out.' his laugh rang out like sandpaper against wood...grating... Akiyama glowered at him, a small smirk of his own building on his face. 'We're above you and your Void Elements, Shinnosuke...we are Celestial...and your little blade there wont cut it against us alone.' 'Bravo, Neo Void God! Speaking of which...that's exactly why I'm here to see you.' he pulled his blade back and folded his arms. 'Since you are now proclaiming yourself to be Celestial, that puts you under their jurisdiction. So now you have to answer to them. And lately, they haven't been too pleased with all this God Wars you've been brewing up against Pharaoh.' The god's eyes widened in anger. 'Why the f^ck not??' 'Well, for starters, this!' he motioned his hand towards Isiy and Carmoon. 'They aren't too cool with you holding these two hostage. They are part of the Egyptian godhood, after all...and it's causing problems with the Egyptian/Celestial relations.' Akiyama looked back at his captives, and then looked back at Shin with a blank stare. 'Son of a frickin' b!tch.' 'My thoughts exactly. So saddle up, Celestial One...we've got a meeting to attend.' The NVG looked back at Isiy and Carmoon. He sighed before nodding. 'We'll come back for you two later.' And with that said...the Neo Void God and the Void Assassin disappeared.
