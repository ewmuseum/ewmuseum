Lucky #13

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/2002 *The scene opens backstage at the Savvis Center in St. Louis. It’s PPV time, and of course that means it’s time for Cal Seaver’s Pre Pay Per View special. And that means it’s time for Boda to grace his show with his picks for the PPV.* Cal: “Any one seen Boda?” *Cal shouts out in the room that is occupied by the PCW stars enjoying a catered pre-show meal. Red Rock walks over to him* Red Rock: “Yeah, he’s locked himself in his locker room, and won’t let even me in.” Cal: “Oh? Wonder what’s up. Well, he’ll never pass up doing my show. It’s a tradition.” Red Rock: “Whatever. I’m going for more food.” *Cal leaves as Red Rock goes to tend to his food. Cal walks through the halls of the Savvis Center looking for the locker room of Boda.* Cal: “Oh, there it is.” *Sure enough, Cal reaches Boda’s room, and knocks on his door. But, Boda doesn’t answer right away, and Cal knocks again. And again. Suddenly Boda yanks the door open, and looks angered by Cal’s visit.* Boda: “WHAT?” Cal: “Well, I just came to get you for the show. Am I bothering you?” Boda: “Yes Cal. You are. I’ve got my wife and kids here, and I’m spending some time with them, before I go out there tonight, and do what I must do. Win the title.” Cal: “So, you’re not going to do the show?” Boda: “Not this time Cal. I’m to focused on the match, and I need time with the family. But, because I like you Cal, I’ll give you a prediction on one match, and you can quote me on your show.” Cal: “Okay. Thanks.” *Chris steps out into the hallway. He’s wearing his wrestling gear, plus a ‘Best Daddy In The World’ shirt. He closes the door behind him, as his little girls can be heard playing. Boda leans against the wall in the hall, and tells Cal to pay attention. Cal motions for one of the cameramen nearby to come over. As soon as the young cameraman comes over, Cal tells him to start shooting. As the red light turns on, so does Boda.* Boda: “Tonight Cal, is going to be a night like none other. First time in PCW history that twenty men battle it out in one match, for the World Title. And in that battle, some friends will get hurt, some hearts will get broken, and in the end, one man will stand triumphant with that which is rightfully his..... The World Title. And. we all know who that man is going to be.” Cal: “Boda?” Boda: “Damn right. You know why? Because this is my type of match. I’m the biggest, baddest son of a bitch in this match, and because of superstition.” Cal: “Superstition?” Boda: “Yeah. What number did I draw Cal?” *Cal pulls out his list of the matches, and counts the spot Boda drew* Cal: “Number 13. You’re the 13th wrestler in the rumble. But, 13 is associated with bad luck.” Boda: “Indeed it is. But not tonight Cal. No way, no how. After all the heartaches of me not winning the title time after time, tonight, I am, and you should, HELL, everyone of the fans should bet on #13. Tonight, #13 will be my source, my inspiration for victory.” Cal: “Okay. I’d take that bet, especially based on previous performances against Snow. So, any other predications?” Boda: “No. Only because tonight, no other matches matter to me. Now Cal, let me get back to my wife and kids.” *Cal tells the cameraman to cut, and thanks Boda for his input. Chris shakes his hand, and tells him to make sure to use that on the show, and he also invites Cal to the post PPV party to celebrate Chris’ victory. Before Cal leaves, he asks Chris on last question* Cal: “Off the record..... What’s going on with Mexi? Is he getting out?” Boda: “Don’t worry about it, was what he told me, and I believe him. Besides, he’s a Deuce, and we always work out the hard shit. And, even if he isn’t here, he’ll be backing me up. Now, I’ve got some FAMILY business to attend to.” *Cal leaves, as does the cameraman. Boda opens the door to his locker room, and walks in, and picks up both girls who come running to him. He shuts the door. Fortunately, we are able to see inside the locker room, thanks to the ever-present PCW cameras. Chris walks over to Stacey, his beautiful wife of 5 years, and kisses her, then drops his daughters on the couch next to mommy. Chris pulls up a chair, and sits in it, and watches his two kids play, as he reaches over and takes his wife’s hand.* Stacey: “So, you ready?” Boda: “Honey, remember the first time we made the playoff? I scored 45 against Philly to get us in. Remember how excited I was to be in the post season?” Stacey: “Yeah, you didn’t sleep for days. Then you scored another 44 points the first game of the playoffs.” Boda: “I’m more excited, more ready than that time. Stacey, tonight, it’s got to be my night. Tonight, I must become the champion.” *Both girls walk over to daddy, and each hug on his massive legs.* Girls: “Daddy, you are a champion to us.” *Chris looks at his two little ladies, and smiles. They release their hugs, and go back to chasing each other around the room.* Boda: “Damn, those two are so sweet. I’m going to win it for them. I know they are proud of me, but in a few hours, I’ll make them prouder. Stacey, tonight, I’ll make the fans proud.” Stacey: “Honey, you know I’m always proud of you. But, I got something for you.” *Stacey reaches over, and kisses Boda on his ear. She then whispers something into his ear, that causes him to jump from his seat, and leave the room. He tells Stacey to stay put, he’ll be right back. Chris walks out into the hallway, and looks up it and down it, then heads off for the entrance to the ring. Along the way, he stops to ask Dave Kern a question. But Kern being Kern is more interested in the main event outcome tonight.* Boda: “Hey, Dave, you see any of the Deuces?” Dave: “Why, you planning your strategy for tonight?” Boda: “No, got that already worked out. I need one of my guys to come with me. Where are they?” Dave: “Probably still eating. So, do you see yourself as the odds on favorite to win tonight?” Boda: “Kerns, I’m in a hurry. But, if I must answer a question to get an answer from you then, YES, I see myself as the odds on favorite. I’m bigger, better and have been hotter than anyone of them in that very ring. Now, where are the Deuces?” Dave: “I’ll tell you in a sec, so, come Turmoil this week, you face Miyagawa for the first time one on one. And, the World Title could be on the line.” Boda: “Now, Now you have you want to interview me? I’ve got my wife waiting for me, if you know what I mean, and I need some one to watch the kids. Get my drift? But, to let you in on a secret.... it will be for the title, as I will be defending it against Miya on Turmoil. And, that’s the closest he’ll get to the title, by wrestling me one on one, because he won’t win it tonight, and he won’t be walking out of Turmoil either with the title. Now, where can I find the Deuces?” *Kerns tells Boda where to go. So Boda runs off down the hall, and finally stops at the catering truck, where D-Kay and Red Rock are still eating. Boda tosses their food out, and tells them to follow him, he has a mission for them. The two follow Chris back to the locker room, and he tells them to wait outside. Chris goes in, and returns with his two little girls.* Boda: “You two are in charge of these girls. If anything happens to them, I’ll personally kill you. Now, get them ice cream or something, and give me 30 minutes or so. Here’s twenty bucks.” *The scene fades out as Boda hands the duo a twenty spot, and shuts the door as Stacey walks up behind him. Now, that’s what I call a pre match warm-up*
