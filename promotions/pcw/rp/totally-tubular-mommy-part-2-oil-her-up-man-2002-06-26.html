Totally Tubular Mommy - Part 2 - Oil Her Up, man

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26 [The scene opens on Tommy's privately owned beach. It's about an a couple acres of excellent beach that members can enjoy for a 50 dollars yearly fee. That includes a gnarly locker, free towels, and a couple free passes for Tommy's owned coffee house, Tubular Tiki. The beach has been largely populated as of late due to the blistering hot weather throughout the entire United States. It's good to stay indoors with heat like this but you can't miss out on gnarly rays like this. So, people have been grabbing their boogie boards and rafts to hit the beach. The sky is clear, the sun is at it's high point, and the water no longer looks like water but just a giant sea of people] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy and his tag team partner, 'Heavy Metal' Harry sit underneath the blue and yellow umbrella, shading their bruised bodies from the sun on this day. Tommy is in his blue and yellow swim suit, showing off his totally ripped, tan chest to the bodacious babes that happen to pass on by. Harry, even though it's about 90 degrees out, is wearing his black Twisted Sister T-Shirt, black shorts, and he has let loose and gone bare foot today. The other day the headbanging, stoner was out there in his cowboy boots] [Tommy seems to be a bit preoccupied. Usually he'd be strolling his land and talking to the regular babes that totally dig him, but he can only sit and look out to the beach. It could be that his mother's visit has plagued his mind too much. She just showed up out of nowhere to spend time with her son, but it doesn't make any sense as to why she would come like this. Once Tommy's father, and Harry's, died in the ring she shunned him and let Tommy live his life. He stayed out of her way and he stayed out of hers. She went through men like alcoholics go through alcohol. Like drug addicts go through lines of cocaine. Like caffiene addicts go through coffee. Like...I think we get the point. Tommy didn't pay attention to the men because he was so taken up with surfing that he never had time to even notice them anyway. Harry would have noticed but he was either too stoned or busy trying to get the neighbors dog to smoke a bowl with him. Now his mother wants to step up and be mother of the year...it's just not cutting it] [Tommy turns to his mother to seek his answers, and he nearly leaps out of his lounge chair to see two muscle bound idiots on each side of his mother, oiling her body up. She wears a pink two piece bathing suit, showing off her large breasts to all the passing public...which would explain why guys kept looking in this general direction] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Mom, what in the name of bogusness are you doing?! [Tommy's mom looks up with a very satisfied smile on her face] Tommy's mom: What's it look like, honey? [Harry looks over and his eyes bug out of his head seeing Tommy's mom decked out in her small and tight two piece. It's as if he'd seen it for the first time] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: It looks far out, man. Can I help? [Tommy's mom's smile turns into a quick scowl] Tommy's mom: NO! [She then smiles brightly again as the two muscular slaves rub the oil into his back] TTT: Don't you think it's a little bogus having two guys rubbing you while your son sits only a few feet away from you? [Tommy's mom shakes her head, the situation not phasing her a bit] Tommy's mom: It's not 'bogus', as you say, at all. I'm letting loose, having a good time. You should go find a couple nice girls out there and have them rub some oil on you. [She holds up the bottle of massaging lotion for Tommy to have] Tommy's mom: You can use it if you want. Harry: You should get me some girls too, man. TTT: I don't need any bodacious babes right now. I've got to concentrate. [Tommy's mom shakes her head, disappointed in her son] Tommy's mom: Are you this uptight about a couple wrestling matches? You really are your fathers son if that's the case, which it obviously is. TTT: It's stressing, mom. Yes we have been performing most bodaciously the past few weeks. We've ripped up the tag division excellently but it's still a long road to gain the titles which have Unofficially been ours since March Madness. I've got a four way match to be involved in on Friday which is just TOTALLY heinous by the PCW booking committee because I need good health going into that battle royal at Gold and Glory. [The two men begin going lower on Tommy's mom and Tommy quickly interjects] TTT: DUDES!! You go any lower and I'll give you a Wipeout as fast as you can say 'GNARLY'! [The two men freeze and then move their hands toward her shoulders] Tommy's mom: I have never seen you so stressed before!! In fact, I've never seen you stressed at all! Even when you were traveling the world as a professional surfer you'd be so happy go lucky, just having some good old fashioned fun and you'd win every tournament that you went to. You really need to loosen up. I really think you need a woman...and she needs to either suck your co-- Harry: I need that too, man. Tommy's mom: ...or she just needs to get on top of you and fuc--- Harry: ...and that too, man. Definitely that, man. TTT: MOM!! That's totally bogus coming from you! [She shrugs and that makes the two men lower their hands to the middle of her back] Tommy's mom: Your all grown up now, Tommy. You can stand to hear your mother talk about those sexual things. Actually...it's good to be back around this neck of the woods because I was known as Queen of the COC--- TTT: THAT WAS YOU?!?!?! TOTALLY BOGUS!!!! AHHHHH!!! [Tommy holds his head in his hands and tries to wipe on of the most disturbing images from his head. The two men rubbing down Tommy's mom take this change to go lower, and lower toward his 'pubic' region. Tommy then grabs on of them by the neck] TTT: I warned you, dudes!!! [The two guys look really frightened as Tommy has absolute fire in his eyes, something rarely seen in his baby blues. The two men then get up and run away. Tommy's mom has a mixed reaction on her face. She is upset with her son for chasing away her beefcakes and she is slightly frightened by her son's outburst] [Tommy then turns sighs, trying to let himself calm down...looking down at the sand that is digging into his toes] TTT: I didn't think that PCW would be this hard, mom. I feel so bogus these days because I don't think that I'm putting my best effort forward to help this team succeed as the tag team champions. Yes, we've taken out some of the most heinous, yet most tough, teams in PCW history but these days the tag team division has grown ultimately gruesome. I've been so fixed on winning those titles that I'm letting my personal life and my friendship start to slip away. I broke Harry's bong a few weeks back, mom!! What kind of friend would break another friend's bong!! Maybe I'm working too hard...your right...I do need to loosen up. I need to roam this bodacious beach, find a bodacious babe and then go play some YAHTZEE!! [Tommy looks over to his mom who is now being rubbed down by two very, very sexy blonde women in even skimpier bathing attire than his mom. They smile up at Tommy as he sees his mom with her eyes closed and a smile on her face. Tommy gets up and storms away in frustration] TTT: ARGH!! [Harry then leans toward the three beautiful women with a look of pure excitement on his face] Harry: Can I join in, man? Woman # 1: NO!! [She slaps Harry across the face] Woman # 2: NO!! [She slaps Harry across the face] Tommy's mom: Harry, I'm ashamed of you!! [Tommy's mom then slaps Harry across the face. He sort of has a shocked look on his face and then his stoned smile comes back] Harry: So, you like it rough, man. [Tommy's mom then punches Harry in the face, knocking him over] Harry: SLEEPY TIME!! [The scene then fades to black]
