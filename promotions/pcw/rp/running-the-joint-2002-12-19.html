Running The Joint

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Running The Joint Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 19/12/02 “So, what’s this, like, plan and stuff?” The less than enthused voice of PCW Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall is heard as we fade into the CEO’s office in PCW’s New Jersey headquarters where the Front Office continue to co-habit until their Hubbardville offices in the Travolta Plaza, Hubbardville are finished. A project being severely hampered by the actions of PCW’s President, Duke Williams. “We taking Duke and putting him on those, like, acid baths so he WAY dissolves and stuff?” As Ellie~! continues to speculate, we see that she is with her husband Xavier, and PCW’s Personnel Director Brandi Wilson, still on leave with a rib injury suffered at the hands of Jon Kano, so throwing herself with relish into her backstage tasks. Xavier : “Well, my little Poon Puppy, it’s time we start to make Duke’s power around here a little more ‘Inert’.” As the CEO’s eyes light up in that way only one of his schemes causes, Ellie~! turns to Brandi and covers her mouth, whispering. Eleanor : “Um... Like, what’s inert mean?” Brandi’s eyes roll as Ellie raises her eyebrows, prompting an answer that doesn’t come before Xavier continues on, pacing around his office as if to mark it his territory. Xavier : “With Duke’s ‘meddling’ in Hubbardville, it looks like we’ll have to hang around this cesspool a bit longer, so we need connections, we need the people who control this place on OUR side.” With Xavier pausing for a minute, Brandi looks confused down in her chair, gesturing to the 3 as she questions him. Brandi : “Wasn’t that meant to be the point of all this, that WE run the show?” Nodding, Kannon collapses beside his wife on the leather sofa, Ellie~! leaning towards him and resting her head on his shoulder while he strokes her hair, answering the Personnel Director with a slight bitterness in his tone. Xavier : “Yes, in theory that’s how it should be... But we’re the Queen Bee of this Hive, and we need he drones and workers on our ‘private’ payroll.” Ellie~! closes her vacuous light Blue eyes and purrs as Xavier runs the tips of his fingers through her long mane of Blonde hair. Across the office, Brandi gives a conniving smile and nods along with Xavier, now understanding him. Brandi : “A binned memo here, a stray fax there...” Seeing that they’re both on the same page, the evil Front Office pair lock eyes, smiling and slowly nodding their heads. Xavier & Brandi : “... Means Duke is nullified.” Speaking in a soft whisper from the comfort of her husband’s arms, Ellie~! smiles too. Eleanor : “Nul... Li.... Fied!” Now they’re on the same page, Miss Wilson seems intrigued. Brandi : “So, who’s our first target? Duke’s secretary? If we get her on side we can book him on the wrong flights, change his meeting times... We’d own his schedule.” Although seeing the possibility in the plan, Xavier shakes his head. Xavier : “Have you seen her? She looks like a Truck Driver. He’s got his Rednecks on his own personal staff, it smells like a farmyard on his floor.” Brandi doesn’t look too pleased at imagining that, screwing her face up. Brandi : “So who’re we going for?” Before Xavier can even smile before speaking the answer, there is a knock at the door which breaks Ellie~! from her rest and makes her sit up straight, wondering who it is too. Xavier : “Enter...” The handle turns and all eyes are on the door as it slowly opens, the creak adding to the suspense. Xavier : “We’re going for the one man who controls everything around here, who knows the location of every memo, where every e-mail was sent...” With the door now fully open, a shadow is cast all the way to the foot of the Mahogany desk, Xavier’s eyes lighting up while Ellie~! looks repulsed and Brandi seems rather taken aback. Brandi : “Him? This has to be a joke, right? A joke revolving around that, that, sub-human?” Her eyes look hopefully at Xavier, while Ellie~! keeps looking him up and down, muttering ‘Ewwwwwzies’ under her breath. Brandi : “Xavier?” The camera pans away from the Personnel Director’s concerned face to show the figure standing in the doorway, the hub of PCW’s operative wheel... The Pimply Faced Intern Kid. Xavier : “Pimply, welcome... Take a seat.” With Ellie~! and Brandi’s eyes burning holes in him, the nervous PFIK makes his way over to one of the chairs and slowly sits down, beads of sweat forming on his greasy and spotted complexion. PFIK: “Er... He-” Before he even gets a word out, Brandi cuts him off. Brandi : “Does he have to speak? That voice makes me want to go back in time and abort myself.” Pondering her crude delivery, Xavier turn to Brandi and gives her a reassuring look before focusing his attention back on the Pimply Faced Intern Kid, Ellie~! deciding she’s head enough of looking at him and rests her head on Xavier’s chest, his left arm moving around to hold her. Xavier : “How long have you been a measly Intern here? 2 years now? Where’s the job progression? Where’s the promotions and pay rises?” Pimply Faced Intern Kid looks deep in thought about Xavier’s words, nodding in agreement as Brandi sighs and fidgets, not liking the plan so far. Brandi : “Maybe there’s a reason, like that he’s a moron?” Kannon turns an annoyed eye to the bitchy Personnel Director who just rolls hers back and reclines in her seat, gesturing for Kannon to carry on. Xavier : “Well, the Front Office recognise your work, and we want to commend you for it. After having to serve Nelson and Duke, we feel it’s finally time you reaped your reward.” Excitedly, the PFIK leans forward. PFIK : “You mean I’m not an intern anymore?” Xavier : “No....” He goes to let out a cheer before Xavier finishes. Xavier : “...You’ll still be an intern, just working for US, and us alone.” Despite the huge opportunity, the PFIK looks hesitant. PFIK : “But what about Mr. Williams?” Almost disappointedly, Xavier and Brandi shake their heads, Xavier leaning forward and looking right at the intern, Ellie~! moaning at having her head-rest move. Xavier : “What has Duke ever done for you? Other than that Cigar scandal that he managed to keep hushed... I’m willing to offer you a glamorous new role, I’ll even convert the broom cupboard into your office, put up a picture of a window....” These small concessions seal the deal, the Pimply Faced Intern Kid leaps at the chance. PFIK : “I’M IN!” Brandi sneers. Brandi : “Shame it’s not Quicksand you’re in.” With Xavier arching his eyebrows at her once more, Brandi again holds her hands up to signal that it’s the last time. Kannon looks back at the excited intern with the sparkle back in his eye as he leans back, Eleanor dropping her head back to his chest. Eleanor : “Sand...... You’re.......In!” As the Pimply Faced Intern Kid looks set to seal the deal, the shot fades to black on the eager PFIK, the smug CEO, the sleepy Ellie~! and the annoyed Brandi. “Now, a few memos will start going astray....” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
