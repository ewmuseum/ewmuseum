Gargano The Wanderer

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 13 - 03 John Gargano and Tyler Preston are both secure on the luxurious Cloud Nine Airship. Tyler Preston has turned his room into a swingers bash. While on the other hand John is all by himself. Gargano always spends his time with his tag team partner these days. Learning about him and such, that is what a good team does. What can John do with his free time? Could he go eat lunch or maybe go chill by the pool? How about he does it all, John decided that he is going to be a wanderer. John made his decision that he is going to travel around the whole airship. He is not going to let this moment pass him by and he is going to visit every single place on the airship. But first he has to find out where he is. The scene opens up with John Gargano surprisingly without his Tag Team partner Tyler Preston by his side. John is fidgeting thru his suitcase which looks somewhat as big as the wall which contains it. Gargano: (Out Loud to Himself) I can not believe they gave me this size of room. I mean look at this place my bag is as wide as the walls! I know they gave Tyler the same size room but come on……The Tag Champs deserve better than this! John rips clothes from the bag left and right hitting his elbows on the walls repeatedly. It looks like he is looking for something but it can not be certain yet. He finally pulls out a piece of folded up paper and throws his bag on the floor in an excited rage. Gargano: Eureka! Finally I found it. This ship is so big and they give me this little room. Luckily I have a map to find my way around. I feel sorry for the poor sap that doesn’t have one of these bad boys. John unfolds the piece of paper to reveal a different array of greens and blues. He suddenly gets a confused look upon his slender face as he places his finger along the ripples of the map. Gargano: (Confused to himself) What the hell is this? I can’t find a thing on this freaking map! He runs out of his room then gets stopped suddenly as his shoulders do not fit thru the small doorway. John then turns to his side then slides out of the room slyly making sure no one noticed. He walks down the hallway of the ship finally running into one of the stewardesses that is on her way to another room. Gargano: Hey! You there! Do you know how to read this map I bought from the guy in the bathroom of the airport? Stewardess: (Politely) Oh Yes Sir! What is this map of? Gargano: It’s a map of the Airship Duh! The stewardess stops suddenly then looks up at John with a confused look upon her pretty little face. She then shows John the map to try to enlighten him but to no avail. Stewardess: Sir this is not a Map of our present Airship. Gargano: (Confused) It isn’t? Stewardess: No sir….This here is a map of Georgia! John Gargano looks down as the stewardess points out that the different array of green and blue simply symbolizes that of earth and water. John plainly embarrassed begins to slowly slip away from the stewardess disappearing without a trace. He slowly slips back into his room and closes the door behind him. He runs and hops on his small cot like bed and puts his hands on his head in a sigh. Gargano: (To himself) I can not believe this….I bought that map for 15.00 bucks from that kid! And it’s useless because I am never going to go to that hick state Georgia. What does PCW management expect us to do…..Know our way around this ship? I do not even know what floor I’m on! John glances at his wrist watch as his face reflects off it gently. He then winces a little bit and then rubs his eyes softly signaling his lack of energy. He looks up at the dangling metal chain hanging from his light and slowly tugs it turning off the lights. Gargano: (While Yawning) Tomorrow will be a different day. Tomorrow I will travel the whole ship….I will go everywhere…..Yeah everywhere you’ll see…I’ll be a wanderer. Yeah that’s it a wanderer. Laying his head softly down upon his fluffy white pillow he begins to talk in a “out of it tone” rambling on and on about what will happen tomorrow. While he doses off he begins to sing himself to sleep Gargano: I’ll be the wanderer…..The wanderer…I’ll wander around and around and around……… With that he falls out of this world and into the dream world into a deep sleep….
