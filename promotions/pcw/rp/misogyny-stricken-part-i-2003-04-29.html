Misogyny Stricken (Part I)

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/29/03 Date: 04/28/03 The Bainbridge urban city edifices stood tall, not nearly as tall and elaborate like the New York and Chicago locations, but still seemingly large. The late evening was rather dormant, the usual buzz and bustle in the street walkways and roads faded as night slowly arrived. The audible cricking of the neck, and cracking of the fingers took over the audio breaking the silence. Below the building structures, and beneath the sky was the misogynist himself, Stone Burns with the devilish grin over his face as he advanced onward, where a fine dressed woman in her thirties, stomping through the street walkways with her heels and holding her black trench-coat over her substantially revealing tube-top. Stone cunningly walked slightly beside her in his machination to catch his prey. Not even a policeman was in sight to keep the streets out of harm and harassment, and it was just Stone and the woman. Being negligent and oblivious to her surroundings she bent down over for an unapparent reason and clutched her heel but her head was so far down, she could see through her opened legs and there stood Stone trying to avoid to be seen and 'blow his cover' but she got up and whirled around after Stone slyly peeked through the crack of her legs. She looked terrified and tried not to say anything but she had already came around for a reason and that was to address him, although she has turned around for that simple reason she whirled back around and began walking, this time faster paced. Stone seized the moment, dashed toward the now semi-running woman and usurped both of her hands as she swung her head to the side momentarily blinding Stone with her resplendent blond hair. She cried out.. 'HELP ME! HELP ME!' The repetition of words only further infuriated Stone as he unheraldedlyJabbed her in the jaw, bringing out a line of tears as Stone covered her mouth with his hand only for her to quickly nibble vigorously at with blood spilling from her mouth. Stone removed it, shaking his hand in pain with the usual.. 'AHH!'.. She ran off afraid but too lethargic and scared to get far as Stone went after her, soonly tackling her to the floor. You would think a man wouldn't treat a woman, especially of her dainty characteristics like this. But Stone just viciously and carelessly coiled her arms around to her back and hoisted her up, somehow pulling the blade out of his now three-year old black suit that he was evidently clad in. She ceased her screaming because it came to no avail and Stone had now placed the blade near her waist and she obviously felt the prick coming from the blood-caked weapon. He slowly walked forward with her, not saying a word along with her following in his arms. They came to the corner of the street where he stopped, and turned over to her as she sluiced his hand in tears and blood. Finally, he blurted a few words in to her ear, as she listened on.. 'You're going to die you know?' After those five crisply spoken words she slowly nodded cooperating and with much effort to stop his assault from going any further and maybe having mercy upon her. But as he had killed two other women, he came with his blade to the side of her stomach, seeing that he was an ambidextrous misogynist he held her mouth with his right hand and stabbed at her stomach with the left as she tried to wriggle free. Stone brought the blade back and sharply stabbed her once more in to the stomach now going in to further depth and pain. She shouted so loud, it passed his hand, and bounced off the building glass like a basketball but still no one around to hear. Stone shoved it in further as she began fading, and falling from his possession, dying slowly yet definitely. Her faced became pale as a white foam came from out from her mouth, covering the bottom of his face with the natural substance. The beeping and humming produced from vehicles approaching their spot made Stone scramble, grabbing the blade out of her stomach and leaving her their to decay until' someone had spotted her. This was the first time in a month he had murdered a woman in particular as it was very unusual for a misogynist like himself to have a drought of that kind but he had pulled one off, ending the streak wrought across his mind. And this was just pre-Turmoil, where he would once again challenge three other oppositions to a bout but sickeningly yet, killing was his drive and training session for an event like this.
