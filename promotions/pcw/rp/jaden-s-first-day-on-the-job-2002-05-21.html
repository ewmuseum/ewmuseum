Jaden's first day on the job!

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 21, 2002 Love and being loved is all Jaden wanted to feel. She has heard rumors that Sylo has left PCW. She was shocked; appalled you can say that he left. Sylo was someone who experienced her good and bad times but now he is leaving her side. She thought that she would never see this day. In her heart, she believed that they would conquer all in PCW, but he heart was wrong. After The Phantom Booker announced the new roster for PCW Jaden was glad to see her name and Sylo’s on the list. She was thrilled because that meant she would be taking classes with him, Orientation class. Welcoming the new comers into PCW; however, that seat next to hers will be empty because Sylo will not be there to fill that seat. The first day of orientation classes, Jaden just sat in the back of the classes lonely, crying her heart out because Sylo was not there to fulfill her needs. After a couple of days, she noticed he was not coming back. She had to start her life all over again making new friends and finding her own path in PCW. Jaden walks into the classroom late again but this time she was late because she is making herself up. She was putting her makeup on and grooming herself so that every man would want her and every woman would hate her and that’s just what happened. “Ms. Jaden you are late again,” the teacher said tilting his glasses with his finger, giving her a stern look. “Find a seat and please do not fall asleep again.” You began to hear whispers among the students in the class talking about Jaden. Even her teacher was getting hot. His palms began to get sweaty just looking at Jaden. He had to keep all his emotions under control so he could continue teaching the class. As the teacher continues teaching, the group of new PCWers sees this little head bobbing up and down peering into his classroom window. “What the fuck,” the teacher says under his breath as he walks towards the door to see what the commotion is outside of his room. The teacher opens the door to find Brandon Selox. “Why are you outside of my door? Shouldn’t you be on the XWA wing,” he says with his arms folded. While the teacher is outside dealing with Brandon, Jaden is in the back of the room with her legs crossed at the knee popping chewing gum. “Excuse me class,” says the teacher walking back to his desk. “Jaden you have a visitor and make sure your little fan club stays away from my door and please hurry back so we can continue our lesson.” “You got it teach,” she says standing up and fixing her skirt that is so short, it makes Jesus scream GOT DAMN. “Hi, who are you?” “My name is Brandon Selox. I am a big fan of yours, Jaden! Will you sign me an autograph?” Brandon says in a blissful voice. “Sure why not,” as she gives Brandon an autograph picture of herself. Jaden and Brandon stare at each other not knowing where to go from there with their conversation. Then all of a sudden there was a knock at the door. It was Jaden’s teacher. “Jaden we would really like it if you would join the class,” he says in sarcastic manner. Before Jaden goes back to class, she places a kiss on Brandon cheek. “Bye,” she says but when she turns around to look back at Brandon, he is on the floor. Jaden begins to think to himself damn I am good! As Jaden walks to her seat the bell rings. “Oops I guess that’s the end of the class isn’t teach?” “Jaden just leave,” he says shaking his head in aggravation. Jaden begins to get her belongs to leave to go to her next class, gym. She has to many books in her hands and she begins to fumble with them and they begin to fall out of her hands. “Shit!” “Let me help you with that pretty lady,” the voice behind her says. “Thank you!” “I am John Crichton,” he says gazing into her eyes and giving her a Colgate smile, showing her he had pretty teeth. “I am Jaden.” “I know who you are. I always know and notice the beautiful woman of the fed. Come on I will walk you to your next class.” “Oh, you cant I promised Duzza that we would walk to class together…sorry, maybe next time,” Jaden says. “Ha, you got to be kidding. You would rather walk with Duzza then me. You cant be a real woman,” he says laughing in Jaden’s face. Jaden bitch slaps John, kicks him in his golden area, and walks away stepping over his bruised body saying BITCH. Jaden meets Duzza in the hallway. “What’s up Jaden, what’s the matter boo?” “I was fighting with John Crichton! That guy is a jerk!” “I forgot to tell you about him. He is dangerous and a borderline psychotic, or I should say a plain old BITCH.” “Well thank you for telling me now Duzza,” she says. “Better late then never. Just tell him your gay or something so he can get off your hinges,” he says laughing, saying it as a joke. “OK,” she replies smiling at Duzza Everything was peaceful until John walked into the hallway where he found Jaden and Duzza. “So this is Duzza,” he says staring at him up and down. “Jaden, come on baby I know you want me…don’t you.” “I am not your baby and I am GAY, so just leave me alone,” Jaden says screaming gay at the top of her lungs, running into the women’s restroom. Everything just stops. Her words flowed out like sword cutting all the guys hearts that wanted to be with Jaden. “DAMN, Duzza that isn’t my type a girl unless I am having a threesome but that’s another story. Bye.” John just walks away and just replays what Jaden said repeatedly in his head. Lurking around was her little fan Brandon. “Umm…excuse me Duzza did Jaden say what I think she said?” “Move yo! Go try to holla at another girl in this piece, aiight,” he says still caught up in the moment. Brandon begins to walk away and he sees Anna Keyson at the other end of the hallway. “Anna…Anna, wait I am a big fan of yours!” “What am I going to do with you Jaden? You just got here and you are causing all this commotion. Damn girl,” Duzza says to himself walking into the women’s bathroom to check on Jaden hoping that he sees girls…girls…girls in the bathroom, too!
