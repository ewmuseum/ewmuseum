Bodacious Bows, Gnarly Arrows - Part 2 - Dead Eye Harry

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13 [Archery is totally bodacious in a far out way. You have that bow in your hand, the arrow is cocked back, and it's ready for most gnarly deployment. You get this suddenly, most excellent tingling feeling through your body. It's like your wisked away to another time, another place. Into a time where a gun would have been thought to be some kind of evil wizardry. Your in the forest, bodaciously looking at your target with an eagle eye. Your a sniper, going for a radical kill to lead your army to a most BODACIOUS victory. The arrow is released and with a rush of speed it cracks right into the target] [Many gnarly archery dudes come to mind, actually only two do. Robin Hood and that totally bodacious elf dude from Lord of the Rings. Those dudes really knew what they were doing with a bow and arrow. In speaking of Robin Hood, probably the most bodacious of them all, Men In Tights is a most underrated Mel Brooks flick. It took a most boring story, that was killed by that bogus Kevin Costner and Mel Brooks turned it into a gnarly LAUGH FEST!! You had to have laughed when Blinkin', the blind servant, was sitting on the toilet reading his braile Playboy! HAHAHA! Now that is totally bodacious in a far out way] [In 'Heavy Metal' Harry's mind, the most bodacious archer is rock singer Ted Nugent. After seeing one of Ted's live shows, Harry became inspired to become a great archer himself. Ted would go to the otherside of the stage and fire to a tiny target on the otherside and fireworks would fly out of the stage as Ted then went to finish a most bodacious song. Harry looked like a little kid seeing his hero perform something great and magical. That brought the Connection to Bobby's Bow and Arrow Range for a day of gnarly shooting. Both men need to let out some most bogus aggression after losing to The Heartbreakers this past Friday. They once again dropped the ball with the tag team titles, something that either man were not wanting. Now they have a second lease on the titles with the triple threat Tag Team Tornado match this coming Friday. Those totally bogus politicians and those totally heinously dressed Pink dudes!] [In the forest it seems like it is pitch black, but through open spots in the trees you know that the afternoon sun shines high above. Fallen branches, thorn bushes, and strategically placed shrubs line the entire forest, seeming like some kind of medieval war zone. The long brown hair man in the Twisted Sister T-shirt jumps and dodges his way behind trees like some kind of expertly trained sniper. He searches all around with his bloodshot, stoned eyes. He knows that his target is somewhere around these parts, he can smell it....wait...that's just himself reeking of weed. He curses himself for lighting up that fatty earlier. It doesn't matter, man. That was the past. This is the future, and there are a lot of cardboard deer to kill!] Voice: DUDE!!! [Harry turns, with his bow drawn to the target, and arrow cocked back. A blonde haired man in a PCW T-shirt is behind a shrub as a cardboard deer shoots up. The blonde haired man slaps a picture of Reginald P. Lovecraft from Politically Incorrect on the deer and then he dives down to take cover. Harry lets loose and nails the photo perfectly between the dirty politicians eyes. The blonde haired man jumps up and sees the arrow sticking out of Lovecrafts forehead. He smiles brightly, his perfect white teeth gleaming even in the shade] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: TOTALLY BODACIOUS!!! [Tommy then runs over to another shrub and ducks down behind it. Harry then draws another arrow, getting it ready in his bow. He then tucks his body into a quick roll behind a thorn bush as the cardboard deer pops up, and so does the Tubular One] TTT: COMPADRE!! [Tommy slaps a sticker of William S. Powell on the deers head and then takes cover behind the bush. Harry does a diving leap from behind the bush and fires the arrow for YET ANOTHER perfectly placed shot into Powell's forehead. Tommy leaps up with a large look of shock on his face, giving his tag team partner and brother a thumbs up] TTT: RADICAL!!! [Tommy, without any hesitation after his exclamation, runs over to the next shrub. Harry then begins rolling on the ground like some kind of headbanger tumbleweed to a tree stump, getting an arrow ready with record time. Tommy flips the cardboard deer up...] TTT: DUDE!!!! [Tommy slaps a block of Pink onto the deer, and then dives behind the shrub just like the past two times. Harry does a front flip followed by two very far out backflips and then does a giant leap in the air like he's a martial arts film star. While in mid-air he takes his shot, nailing the pink directly in the center. Dead Eye Harry strikes again! Tommy stands up and laughs wildly, loving that Harry is being most excellent with these arrows] TTT: BITCHIN'!!! [Tommy then runs over to the final shrub in their area. Tommy is holding back his laughter for some reason, obviously having something up his most bodacious sleeve. Harry walks slowly to the next target as if he's Arnold Schwarzenegger, playing the Terminator. His stoned face is showing pure focus and determination. He is ready for the next target and whoever it is he will take out. Far out, man] [Tommy flips the deer up and jumps up with it, sticker in hand. Who will it be?! Tommy slaps it on much faster this time and slams into the ground faster than he did the last three times. He's hiding his face as it is turning a large shade of red from the laughter. Harry pulls out three arrow at once, lining them down the bow. Harry has a trick in mind from the Rambo movies. He's going to nail all three of those arrows into this freakin' target. Harry pulls back and he...STOPS?!?] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: NOOOO!!!.....man. [A picture of Ted Nugent with Dee Snider, from Twisted Sister, is on the deers head. Harry breathes wildly, glad that he didn't shoot while he had the chance. The sweat pours from his brow, not from the rigorous workout session of shooting the cardboard deer, but the totally not far out scare he got from almost shooting Nugent and Dee. Not far out at all, man] Harry: That was close, man. [Tommy stands up laughing like crazy and pointing at Harry] TTT: I almost had you, compadre! [Harry nods his head with his spaced out smile on his face, bow and arrow still in hand] Harry: That was a dirty trick, man. [Tommy shrugs as he begins walking over to Harry] TTT: No harm done, dude. Nugent and Dee are still bodacious on the deer and you TOTALLY nailed all those other bogus things. I have to say, your a regular Elven dude from Lord of the Rings! [Harry nods his head with a reassuring smile] Harry: Elves are far out, man. Especially that chick elf... [Tommy smiles] TTT: Liv Tyler is a bodacious babe. [Suddenly an arrow wizzes past and nails Dee Snider right in between the eyes. Harry has a look of pure hurt and sadness in his eyes. A tear begins rolling down immediately] Harry: DEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!....man. [The Safari Owner jumps out from behind a bush, laughing with his high pitched voice. He sounds like a wicked witch...rather a most gay wicked witch] Safari Owner: GOT HIM!! [His lisp is very strong now, obviously this dude is excited to have nailed a heavy metal legend right in between the eyes] Harry: He...killed...Dee.........man..... [Tommy has a look of pure hatred on his face. It is something most rare with him, but when you hurt his brothers feelings like that only hatred will follow. Harry means a lot to Tommy and when you hurt his bro, you hurt him. This Safari Owner is going to pay the most bogus price for pulling that kind of stunt] TTT: Give me the bow and arrow, dude. I'm taking this compadre down!! [Harry shrugs his shoulders in a very shocked and saddened state] Harry: I'm out of arrows, man. [Tommy looks at the empty arrow holder with shock. Where can he get extra arrows from?] TTT: This is BOGUS! [The Safari Owner approaches the two men with a very arrogant smile on his pale face. His brown hair parted on the side like some kind of bogus super dork. He looks like a super gay version of the Crocodile Hunter in his tight khaki shorts. This dude needs to incorporate some gnarly blue and yellow into it so that way he doesn't look so freakin' gay] Safari Owner: Well, boys, looks like I'm one up on ya. HAHA! You think that I'm some kind of stupid, ninny! TTT: Ninny? [The Safari Owner seems confused, not really sure what he said either] Safari Owner: Shut up!! Raggamuffin told me that you two would be here, and I set this all up. We never let amateUUURRSS in this place! [Tommy shrugs and smiles, pointing to all the gnarly bullseyes that Harry nailed] TTT: Doesn't look like an amateur to me, dude. [The Sarfari Owner just shakes his head, keeping that gay arrogant smile on his face] Safari Owner: Lets face it, boys. Your never going to amount to much in PCW. You've been there for all these months and haven't amounted to SQUAT!! You just bend over and take it up the.... [Harry breaks in] Harry: Isn't that what you do, man? [The Safari Owner puts his hands on his hips and gives Harry a scowl] Safari Owner: NA UH!!! [Harry and Tommy look at each other with a bit of fear] TTT: Dude.... Harry: HAHA...man. Your Raggamuffin's friend, man. Safari Owner: That's right...WE'RE TIGHT!! [The Connection laughs like crazy] TTT: YOUR TIGHT...HAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Harry: YEAH, MAN!! HAHAHA!!! Tight, man. [The Safari Owner looks absolutely mad now. He raises his bow and tosses an arrow in, aiming right at Harry] Safari Owner: Well, lets see how you like it with an arrow in your head, BOY!!! [As The Safari Owner pulls back to go for the kill an arrow comes out of nowhere and nails the Safari Owner right in his ass. He screams a high pitched squeal as if Michael Jackson had just taken the stage to start up a rendition of Billy Jean] Safari Owner: RIGHT IN MY ASS!!! [Harry giggles] Harry: I bet he's used to saying that, man. [The Safari Owner trots away in pain] Voice: CAT SCRATCH FEVER!!!! WOOOOOO!!! Harry: TED NUGENT, MAN!!!!!! [The hard rock legend himself, Ted Nugent, comes down the hill with his bad ass specially made crossbow in hand. He smiles and pats Harry on the shoulder. Harry looks like he has just had a few orgasms. Tommy smiles and nails Nugent with a high five] TTT: Totally bodacious shot, dude! Ted Nugent: F**K YEAH, MAN!! Harry, always a pleasure to help a fellow fan out. Harry: ......., man. Ted Nugent: I heard that you guys were heading out here. My agent gave me word that Raggamuffin set this f**ked up thing to take one of you guys out of competition so that you can't compete on Friday. That cat has got some serious problems, man. Harry: ......., man. TTT: Totally, dude. He stole my bodacious girlfriend and has been trying to make our lives a living hell for months now. Something needs to be done about that dude. Ted Nugent: Well, guys. How about we go hunt us down a freakin' fairy!! [Tommy nods] TTT: Send Raggamuffin a little message. Ted Nugent: HELL YEAH!! Nobody messes with the most bodacious tag team in PCW. You guys are winnin' those titles!!! WOOOOOO!!!! [Nugent heads through the woods like a rock singer possessed. Tommy nods and follows after him. Harry just shakes his head, now with tears of joy in his eyes] Harry: ........, man. [The scene fades to black]
