No Means NO!

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/13/03 The flicker of a candle flame is the only source of light in the area. The wind blows at the small flame, making it seem almost to dance. The air was nice and cool with a scent of rose petals intertwined with it. All the lights in the Windsor home were turned off as light music plays throughout it. The soft tunes carry their way into the dining room where the table is set with a flower, candle, and two white plates set. It was all the makings for a romantic diner. From the kitchen walks Chris Storm with a silver tray in his hand. He sets it down on one of the plates and makes sure everything is in its place. Once he is satisfied with how everything looks, he walks toward the living room and straight to the door that leads into Tessa's bedroom. He grabs the handle and lets out a deep breath. Saying a silent Shinto prayer he opened the dark room to see the form of Tessa sleeping in the bed. Chris crept silently towards the bed to make what he had planned even more of a greater surprise, hopefully Tessa wouldn’t think it was someone else resulting in the surprise being more of a problem than a help. Crawling onto the bed beside Tessa he reached out slowly to her shoulders and shook her softly to wake her up. “Chris?” She questioned in a half awake state, her blue eyes only opened slightly. “What’s going on?” 'Shhh' he told her as he put his finger to her lips. Chris leaned forward and kissed her forehead as she started to sit up in the bed. Tessa covered herself with the blankets as Chris stepped back into the light shining from the window. The moon bathed enough light on Chris so that Tessa could see he was wearing a nice silk shirt with the outlines of a tank top on under it. Fine white pants cover his legs, rolling down them to leather black shoes. Chris was dressed to kill and it looked like Tessa was his prey. He leans over to her and curls his finger, letting her know he wants her to follow him. 'I have something special planned for tonight.' Then he exits out of the room leaving Tessa lying in the bed, trying to cover herself. “Men” Tessa signed to herself as she crawled out of the bed and followed Chris out of the room dressed in her pink silk pajamas. She ran down the hall to catch up with Chris, being sure to be careful not to make too much noise so that she would not wake up Evan who was peacefully sleeping in his room. “Chris what exactly are you trying to show me at eleven o clock on a Sunday night?” She questioned curious as to what his motives were for waking her up in the middle of the night for something that obviously wasn’t planned. Chris merely smiled and laughed to himself. 'She'll flip over this. I hope…' he thought to himself, a slight ping of doubt in his mind. Chris quickly looked over his shoulder to make sure she was following him. She didn't seem to happy about getting woken up at this time of the night but Chris was banking everything on her being surprised and pleased by his romantic gesture. Opening the door leading into the dining room, Chris let Tessa enter first. Tessa let out a gasp as she looked around at what stood before her. She could hardly believe her eyes. Before her stood something that had turned her doubts of her and Chris ever getting along again upside down. “Oh my god Chris, it’s beautiful.” She cried out as she turned to him and embraced him tightly to show her gratitude to his romantic side. Chris was smiling ear to ear with joy. He looked up and thanked the Kami for letting his plans work. He just wraps his arms around Tessa as she hugged him tight. As much as he was enjoying this moment, silently wishing it would never end, Chris had to break away from Tessa. Pulling out her chair, Chris smiled warmly. 'Have a seat Tessa dear. I spent hours making sure everything was just perfect for the woman I cherish with all my heart.' Tessa takes a seat as Chris pushes her in. He walks into the kitchen for a second leaving her alone in the dining room. The sweet smell of the roses and burning candle, scented strawberry, tickles her nose. Tessa feels something under her foot. She looks under the table to see Chris has laid out a bed of rose leaves under the table to create the smell in the area. She looks back up at the silver tray before her. “Chris this is wonderful and sweet, but shouldn’t we be resting. We have a big match coming up on Tuesday against P.W.A. and El Diablo with Flaymin whatever his name is on our side and we don’t know if he can keep his side of the team.” Tessa said, feeling rather guilty that she had to bring this up, but also knowing that if they spent too much time doing things like this it would take away their focus from the match making them both somewhat more vulnerable which always led to their downfall. The words fell on deaf ears however as Chris came back into the room with two wine glasses and a bucket full of ice and a bottle of wine. Laying the wine, glasses, and bucket on the table, Chris looked at Tessa and smiled. Thought she didn't seem to want to, she smiled back at him. Chris' smile disappeared as he kneeled down next to her. 'Tessa, I know you think those tag team titles are important to me and winning this match this week is important also. I will admit that it is important to me. Those tag team titles mean so much to me.' He started. Chris took his hand and cupped her face, staring directly into her eyes as if he was trying to look directly into her soul. 'But they only mean that much to me because I am holding them with you. I'm not a tag team wrestler. I will openly admit that. Three years writing and this is only my second tag team title I have ever had. But this is something I am sharing with you and I don't want to lose it to anyone, especially not to P.W.A.' He had to take a seat now, it was starting to get to him as the words came flowing from his heart straight to his mouth. 'Tessa, we can not get married and our rings are void but what we have that is real is these titles around our waists. That is our bond together. That is the one thing that truly keeps up together in my mind. Tessa, I love you and I want to be with you. Right now that means keeping these titles around our waist and I would do anything to keep that happening right now, no matter how much of a sacrifice I have to make.' “I understand Chris, which is why I think we should be getting some rest right now.” She tried to explain to him in a sense that he would understand and hopefully not disappoint him. “Why don’t we go up to bed and tomorrow afternoon we can have this dinner instead. It’s late Chris and we need our sleep.” She continued to explain as the hope that he would understand lingered deep inside her mind. Lowering his head, Chris started to think about it. He just smiled and looked at Tessa. 'I love you' rolled off his lips as he stood up. Tessa smiled as he started to pick up the table. Chris came back out after everything was picked up and took Tessa by the hand. He helped her out of her seat and toward the living room where they could separate to go to their individual rooms. Chris however stops Tessa. 'You look so cute in your little pink pajamas. I…' he started to walk toward her. '…just want to…' he was right next to her know. '…kiss you all over…' his arms managed to find their way around her waist, pulling her close to him. His lips were right by her earlobe. '…your body.' With that said he started to kiss her behind her ear and take her earlobe into her mouth gently suckling on it. “Chris…I love you, but we need to get some sleep.” She said, as she rested her arms up against his chest slowly pulling away from him to show her lack of interest. Managing to successfully turn away she began to walk towards the stairs. Not wanting to give up so easily, Chris moved toward the stairs also. He had that gleeful smile on his face as he cut Tessa off, posing on the stairs as sexy as he could. He winked at Tessa, who just slightly laughed it off. 'Oh come on Tessa, the match isn't tomorrow. It's Tuesday. We can sleep in late tomorrow.' Chris said as he moved down the stairs and embraced Tessa once more. He moved a lock of her sleepy hair out of her face. 'Sweetie, I love you and I want to show you that love tonight.' He rubbed his face against hers. 'Just give it, I promise I'll make it the most wonderful night of your life.' “No Chris, now good night! I’ll see you in the morning.” She said as she gave him a peck on the cheek hoping that would be enough sign for him to leave her alone and let her go get the sleep she desired for a good performance on Tuesday. Little did she know that she was going to find out that Chris was probably the most stubborn person she knew. Convinced she just needed to be shown how much pleasure she could be shown if she only gave in, Chris let her walk toward her room before coming up behind her and pressing his body up against the back of her as he embraced her. 'See Tessa, doesn't this feel so right. I won't hurt you I promise. We can take it nice and slow.'“Chris, NO!” She shouted becoming obviously upset with his attempts now, and seemingly not understanding what she meant when she said no the first few times. “Now go get some sleep.” Chris just frowned, trying to think of something, anything. 'Tessa you are turning me away and you don't even know if you will like it or not. I'm not just trying to do this for me. Tessa we love each other so why is it so wrong? I mean it's so lonely in my room without you. I need your warm embrace on my side. I just need you so much right now.' He moved in closer to her, trying to hold her in her arms. 'I am yours Tessa; you can do anything you want with me. Just let me show you my love. I mean what if something happens in the ring Tuesday to one of us? Then we would never have gotten the chance to make our love physical instead of just words. You never know what will happen in that ring Tessa.' “NO!” Was the last thing heard from Tessa before the door of the bedroom slammed shut in Chris’ face, leaving his attempts a failure. Staring at the door stunned, Chris could not move. His entire body was frozen. All he found himself about to do was lean against the door and ball up, ready to cry. To him it seemed like the woman he loved was rejecting him. His head just fell into his arms as he let the weight of tiredness take him over. He pulled himself up from the door, his back facing it. 'Just so you know Tessa, I still love you, even if you don't love me back.' With that said he walked off to his room where he would send another night alone and cold, silently cursing the ill-fated turn of events that took his should have been wife and turned her into Mrs. Green. Tuesday was coming and he would have to try and team with her and Flaymin to defeat P.W.A. and El Diablo. With their relationship still on the rocks could the tag team champions actually rise to face the challenge or will they fall victim to another prank?
