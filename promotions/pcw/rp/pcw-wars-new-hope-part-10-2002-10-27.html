PCW Wars: New Hope Part 10

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 - 10 - 02 We last saw the Rebel force make an amazing escape from the Empires ultimate weapon known as the Death Star battle station. However the jovial attitude was short lived as the magnificent seven sustained their first loss, as Charlie Loc Kenobi sacrificed his own life in a bid to win survival for the remainder of the team. Struck down by the ruthless hand of Darth Pharaoh, the behemoth watches on as his victim’s allies make their getaway via the Millennium Eagle. The scene re-opened within the confines of the Millennium Eagle as the saddened Alexander Skywalker dwells on the loss of Charlie Loc Kenobi, Anarchy offering comfort in the form of a blanket, which she draped along his shoulders. A. Skywalker: 'I can't believe he's gone-...' Anarchy interrupting with a dose of support. Anarchy: 'It wasn't your fault-...' A. Skywalker: 'No literally, I can't believe he's gone! His body vanished, disappeared, call it what you will but that was a case of the David Blaine-...' Suddenly Gaz Solo emerges from the vessels cockpit in haste and explains that their escape hasn't been sealed as yet. The Empire has thwarted the Rebel's best efforts by bombarding the darkness of space with sentry ships. Gaz Solo: 'Come-on squire... We're not out of this yet!' The smuggler/ captain Gaz Solo led the young Jedi apprentice to a gun turret and both individuals strap into the bucket seats ready to do battle. Adjusting his head set Gaz Solo blares down a microphone, as the ship jerks about in an evasive manner. Gaz Solo: 'You in, kid? Okay, stay sharp!' Cherezza and Anarchy search the heavens for attacking TIE Fighters. The Wookie pulled back on the speed controls and the ship bounces slightly as Alexander harshly grips the laser cannon's control button. As the enemy crafts draw nearer Cherezza maneuvers the Millennium Eagle into an attack position and the night sky became bathed in gunfire, a lighting spectacle. A number of wayward shots go adrift, then suddenly a TIE Fighter takes a hit to its engine and spirals from sight, courtesy of the trigger-happy Gaz Solo. Gaz Solo: 'BANG~! Rebel's one, Empire nil-...' Another two TIE Fighters flock to the pirate ship, swaying between Alexander's cover shots, the youngsters frustration seeped through as he speaks via the microphone of his headset. A. Skywalker: 'DAMN~! They are too fast' Cherezza continued to manipulate the controls and Alex finds himself stalking a lonesome ship by swiveling in his gun mount, biding his time the Jedi wannabe squeezed the trigger dusting the Imperial threat instantly. A. Skywalker: 'We be showing you bad ass mothafucka's, how we kick da shiznit Rebel style!' As Alex basks in his triumph, the monotone voice of Rush-3PO filters over. Rush-3PO: 'You'd be taking da piss boy! Remember me and you are gonna dance once this shit is all over and I'm going to be taking my cast iron boot and sticking it down your damn throat, believe!!!' A. Skywalker: 'Uh-....' As the two converses Gaz takes out yet another TIE Fighter, meantime though Cherezza navigates the Millennium Eagle 'Roland' inputting the necessary information for them to make the jump into hyperspace. Awaiting the on-board computers response Gaz Solo and Alexander Skywalker continue to rid the surrounding area of the Emperors minions. Anarchy: 'Well mop head can we like, hurry up-... I mean piece of junk versus the entire Empire armada, hello, we are like going to be space debris!' As Cherezza howls his disapprovement at being called a 'Mop Head' he fiddled about with the dashboard and the vehicle jumped into hyperspace as the cockpit window reflected the lighting of the maneuver. Emerging from hyperspace, the victorious Millennium Eagle moved majestically through space. Leaving our hero's halfway across the Galaxy we return to the control room of the Death Star as Darth Pharaoh glares at a sea of stars. Grand Moff Tarkannon enters the scene and the two warlords discuss the events that transpired minutes earlier. Tarkannon: 'Those Peons have gotten away... BLAST!' Darth Pharaoh: 'They have made the jump into hyperspace' Tarkannon: 'You're sure the homing beacon is secure aboard their ship? In the name of Hubbard, Pharaoh, you had better be right!' Back in the cockpit of the Millennium Eagle, Gaz removed his gloves, gloating about his skills as a captain. Cherezza heads into the back too check on the reported damage and the team converse once more. Gaz Solo: 'Not a bad piece of rescuing, huh?!? You know, I even amaze myself...' Anarchy: 'That doesn't sound to hard! Are you thick, is that your trouble-... They let us escape, they're probably tracking us as we speak?' Gaz Solo: 'Not this ship, sister' A. Skywalker: 'Sister? Whoa-... This is like a family reunion!' Gaz Solo: 'WHAT? The mushroom is off again - The guy should be kept in the dark and fed bullshit. Anyway what's the deal with the R2 unit, I mean it don't talk, it don't serve and as far as I'm aware it isn't a stylist-...' Anarchy: 'Installed in its memory is the technical readout of that battle station. I only hope that when the data is analysed, a weakness can be found. This fight is far from over!' Changing too that of an exterior view we witness the Millennium Eagle descending upon a green moon that orbits the planet Yavin. The pirate ship soars over a dense jungle and towards an ancient temple shrouded in an eerie mist and the fascinating cries of hidden creatures. Keeping at a low altitude the vessel eases up to the point where it hovers lightly over a landing pad and crashes to the ground. The pressure powered loading ramp lowers and the Rebel collective exit to a welcoming committee led by Commander Willard who isn't able to contain his excitement at seeing the remaining royal of Alderaan. Willard: 'YOU'RE SAFE!' Anarchy: 'Look I know you mean well, but that big bad battle station is like on our trail so if you could get this R2 unit's information extracted then we maybe able to muster a counter-attack!' The Commander exchanges a look of sympathy with the lowly smuggler Gaz Solo and takes the droid away for examination. As the Death Star prepares to orbit the far side of Yavin, a prep talk is assembled to combat the threat. Standing before an electronic wall-display and in front of his peers, Dodonna informs the surrounding parties of their course of action, in this case a suicide mission so to speak, but desperate times call for desperate measures and desperation was certainly the awe of this discussion. Dodonna: 'The battle station is heavily shielded and carries fire power far greater than half the star fleet. It defenses are centred around a direct large-scale assault but call me nuts, I think a one-man fighter should be able to penetrate the outer defense!' A pilot stands erect, the pilot in question being Gold Leader who questions the strategy. Gold Leader: 'Your Nuts! The odds are against us! The Rebellion will perish! It's suicide-... Sounds like my kinder mission. Suit up then men, we're going to war' The pilots and navigators stand to attention and flee the scene, muffling Dodonna's explanation of this mission, however the alert Alexander Skywalker listens with intrigue. Dodonna: 'Listen fools - Only a precise hit will set off the necessary chain reaction. It'll be like hitting a pin head but that's the deal, the Empire have constructed a technological masterpiece and in all honesty I'd be kissing my own ass good-bye!' Gripping a suitcase from behind a projector Dodonna ventured off. Vanishing in the congested crowd, Dodonna disappears leaving Alex to make his own exit, most likely heading to the hangar. Returning to the battle station Darth Pharaoh and Grand Moff Tarkannon watch a computer-generated screen with interest, as a row of lights indicate the rebel base's location. Inter-Com: 'Orbiting the planet at maximum velocity. The moon with the rebel base will be in range in about thirty minutes!' Darth Pharaoh: 'Oh, this is going to be a day to remember. First I showed old Kenobi who was the better man and now I shall crush the 'friggin Rebellion-... I'M KING OF THE WORLD!!!' As the Syth Lord coughed and winced, the scene reverts to the hangar where Alex has suited up and sits patiently in the cockpit of his X-Wing, watching a flashing dot on his scanner he witnesses the departing Cherezza and Gaz Solo. As the lonesome star pilot sits-by awaiting clearance, Princess Anarchy wishes her valiant knight the best of luck as he embarks on a mission, on behalf of humanity. Anarchy: 'What's wrong?' A. Skywalker: 'I only wish Charlie Loc could've been here-...' Anarchy: 'Err-... Get over him already! Granted you were one-time lovers but he couldn't get the job done, now its your turn' A. Skywalker: 'Lovers? Arr-... Ewwww'zzies, I thought it was Gaz who had to deal with all the gay jokes!' Anarchy: 'Oh! I'm sorry, it's just he said you two had a special bond and I like thought HOMOSEXUAL here, please-... Adam and Eve, not Adam and Steve, well Smith anyhow!' The conversation is cut off at his prime as R2 D2 is loaded into the X-Wing Fighter and it appears the battle will certainly commence within literally minutes. Starting up his engines via a number of dials aligning the dashboard, Alex is taken back by the voice of his old mentor, Uncle Owe-... No, Charlie Loc Kenobi. Kenobi's Voice: 'Alex, the force will be with you!' Startled the young Jedi apprentice responds, surprisingly enough the work going on around him ceases and listens, unbeknownst to the pilot that he looks quite the fruit cake. A. Skywalker: 'Charlie Loc! The force-... It's all right you saying that but it didn't exactly help you against Darth Pharaoh now did it?' As the former farmers boy silences, the listening audience break into a mass of hysterics aimed at the rookie pilot who blushes, rolls his eyes and grinds his teeth with a series of facial expressions. Mumurring under his breath he concludes the segment. A. Skywalker: 'Thanks A lot, now look what you made me do-...' - To be continued -
