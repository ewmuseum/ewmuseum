Turning the Gears

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/13/03 Skylar awakes in his bed after the loss he took at Tuesday Turmoil. The Pharaoh attempted to blackmail the PCW Title out of Skylar, but he failed to attain it. Skylar lost, but not the way that he was supposed to. The Pharaoh had to pin Skylar to take the PCW Title from him. Instead, Skylar submitted to a Side Headlock before The Pharaoh could take it into a Headlock Takedown, which Skylar was obliged not to escape or kick out of. What bargaining chip could The Pharaoh have possibly had? Two, actually. Two with names: Rafaela and Ines. Small children, little girls... Skylar was to become their legal guardian, per the request of their fathers. That was interrupted the first time by Violence Jack, and now once more by The Pharaoh. Titu Xansiv had adopted the smaller children in the stead of Skylar Green, who was too busy trying to return them to the State of Florida since they had run away. The deal was supposed to be Titu's crowning achievement in villainy. He had brought the children into the struggle between he and Skylar. Jack had burnt down the house, but wasn't aware of the children sleeping inside. The Pharaoh, on the other hand, went out of his way to use them against Skylar. Sky had to lose to The Pharaoh or the children would remain separate for all of their pre-adult lives. The kids didn't deserve that, but The Pharaoh didn't deserve to win the PCW World Title without lifting so much as a finger. It was a tag match. The Pharaoh convinced Dorn Tracer that his plan would work, thus sparing Brianna Washington from having to deal with him that night. Brianna was present when Titu came to get Ines and Rafaela, and upon hearing what The Pharaoh planned to do with them, offered her help in strategizing a way to keep him from getting what he wanted. And so, Skylar submitted when he should have been pinned. There was still the issue of trying to get the children back from The Pharaoh. He would certainly try to use them again. Skylar knew it, Brianna had known it... anyone could see it. Skylar had to get those children out of the custody of a man who would place their futures and their family ties in jeopardy for his own ends. He couldn't do much about it, though, being stuck on the airship. He had to get someone to deal with it for him in the United States. But who could he call to deal with it? Tania stands over him wearing her black Miami Heat jersey. It went down to her knees. She'd locked herself in the bathroom all night, but now she is out and about. 'Get up,' she commands more than requests. 'What time is it?' Skylar asks. Tania looks at the small white Powerpuff Girls watch over her right wrist. '9 AM.' Skylar nods his head and sits up. He has green shorts on, no shirt. His hair is still silver, he hasn't been able to wash it out. Then again, he has been considering just leaving it in for the duration of the Cloud Nine tour. Skylar points to the table where most of his personal affects are rested. 'Hand me my phone,' Skylar says. Tania is slow to comply, she still blames him for Rafaela and Ines being gone. She tosses the phone to Skylar with a 'Hmph' and goes back into the bathroom. Skylar picks up the phone from its spot underneath his left arm where it landed. 'He should be up now,' Skylar considers the scheduling of something, or rather someone. Opening the Nokia 3800, Skylar hits eleven digits and presses the large green 'send' button. A ringtone. A second. The voice on the other side answers with heavy panting. An exercise regimen. 'What do you want?' the deep voice demands. 'I already gave ya some damn motivation.' Skylar shakes his head. 'Listen first, Rush, then talk.' Rush Mason, Skylar's former rival, isn't exactly a friend. They're not really enemies anymore, though (Rush's retirement cut that prospect short). 'Fine, what?' The bathroom door comes open and Tania comes out wearing some jeans and black sandals. Without so much as a word, she leaves the room. Skylar's head follows her steps out of the room. '... Anyway, I take it you saw Turmoil last night?' 'Yeah, not any arena I've seen.' 'We're on a touring airship now,' Skylar shrugs. 'Glorified blimp-plane/cruise ship.' Rush doesn't really find that too interesting. 'Yeah. Point, please?' 'I just wanted to hear your delightful voice.' A sigh from the PCW World Champion. 'Seriously, though; you saw what The Pharaoh was up to?' Though he's talking to a blind man over the phone, Rush nods his head. 'Yeah.' 'Well, I need your help.' Mason takes in a deep breath. 'You need my help? That's the first time you've ever said that! Hold on for a second, I gotta go mark my calendar.' 'Seriously, Rush,' Sky says. 'Here's what I need you to do. Get a tape of the show, take it to the Department of Children and Families in the State of Florida. Show them what Titu's doing with those kids. They need to be out of his custody as quickly as possible.' Rush sighs himself. 'I could do that. But what's in it for me?' 'Forever knowing the fact that there's something that I can't do that I need you to do for me. What, do you want me to pay you?' A smirk from Mason. 'I guess that's good enough for me. It could take a few weeks, I'll have to get a new VISA.' 'Just do what you can to help us.' 'Alright. Lata, playa.' 'You know you've never sounded quite right in ebonics.' 'Yeah, I know. Sue me.' Skylar clicks 'end' on the phone, finished with the conversation. Things have been set into motion. Skylar job is to keep The Pharaoh away from winning the PCW World Title in a crooked fashion. Rush's job is to get the children away from The Pharaoh. Duties have been split. A simple plan made. Now all that must be done is to pull it off.
