Reconstructing Harry - Part 1 - Airplanes and Cellphones

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 23, 2002 [After March Madness Tommy turned inward on himself. He just sat in his beach house watching the tape over and over again. How he showed his true strength as a champion by sustaining the vile submission from Rent-A-Hero, only to be pinned by some lucky schmuck who happened to just sort of tumble on top of him. Rewind, play, and falling deeper and deeper into depression. He stayed strong in the Battle Royal too, only to be eliminated cheaply. Once again the forces in PCW have held him down, pushed him back to the times when he and Harry were the Cowabunga Connection. Only then...they were the good guys. The good guys were supposed to win and capture the championships to give the people what they deserved to see. They loved the Cowabunga Connection and they would certainly have loved to see gold around their wastes. But as Tommy watches the pin by Dante Jackson one more time he comes to a shocking conclusion that it doesn't matter if your good or bad, it just matters whos ass you kiss. It only matters if a persons nose is brown in the locker room and they get the big shots, they get the chances to shine, and most of all they get to wear the bodacious gold. See, Tommy proved them all wrong by going out there and controlling his own destiny, working harder than ever before. He showed all those big wigs that you don't need to kiss up to win the titles. Well, it was only a matter of time before they finally came to their senses and did something about it, which they did. And just who are they? Who are these mysterious forces that lurk in the shadows, praying on those who do not kiss up? Tommy doesn't know who they are, and if he did he'd totally go over there and beat the crap out of them. He pauses the tape on Dante rolling over in exhaustion and grabbing the belt. It was a heinous travesty none the less, and there will be a gnarly day for redemption] [The flight to New York City was most difficult for The Tubular One. The second he walks into Los Angeles Airport security is all over him like one of those heinously cheap suits. They search him, go through his bags, and even rough him up a bit. When Tommy sits at the gate he asks a nice little old lady if SHE got roughed up when she walked into the airport. The old lady just giggles at Tommy and shakes her head. Tommy then realizes that the conspiracy continues and that the only way to break this spell is to complete a task he set out to do over a week ago, Reconstruct Harry. As Tommy hands the ticket to the attendant at the front desk he begins devising a bodacious plan that will get his brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, back into his groove. Ever since he quit smoking weed a month ago he's been extremely...like...smart. He's taken this whole new way of thinking and that he needs to move away so that he can start a new future. When Harry said that he was leaving it left Tommy feeling very down, more than he let Harry know. AFter March Madness, Harry left the arena and went straight to the airport. It was most bogus as Tommy hadn't really sucked in the fact that he lost his Continental Title and his brother was leaving him. He returned to the beach house feeling most bogus as he was alone. And he stood in the living room for hours thinking about all of the radical memories he and Harry shared together. With a few quick calls he immediately got some bodacious babes to come over, but they didn't fill the void] Stewardess: Would you like something to drink, sir? [As Tommy looks up from the emergency instructions he notices the gnarly looking stewardess with a bright white smile across her face. As tough as it may seem Tommy forced a smile across his face showing off his pearly whites as well] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: No thanks, bodacious stewardess babe. [She sort of giggles and then pushes the drink cart away as Tommy looks out of the plane window. The clouds look most puffy from the extremely high altitude as he notices one of them looks sort of like Harry taking a most righteous bong hit. Jeez, now he's seeing clouds that look like Harry. Before it was people, then it was a dog, and now he's seeing clouds that look like his brother. Either he's losing it or maybe he's ALREADY lost it] Stewardess: Hi. [She takes the aisle seat with that bright smile across her face again. She has a very tanned complexion, sparkling blue eyes, and strawberry blonde hair. Tommy watches her hair, catching a slight scent of her shampoo which drives him wild] Stewardess: I noticed that your 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. Is that true? [Tommy holds his head down looking at his tray] Tommy: At this bogus moment I wish that it weren't true, but I am The Tubular One. [The Stewardess gets very happy and claps her hands together in celebration. She then holds out a napkin and a pen] Stewardess: Could you give me your autograph, please? [Tommy shrugs not even thinking anything about it. If he were still holding the Continental Title and had his brother by his side this autograph that he was about to sign would cost this babe an easy fifty dollars. But for some reason that little fact has slipped Tommy's mind. It could be the depression or it could just be that this babe is so bodacious that he doesn't even remember. He scribbles his John Doe on the napkin as it just looks like a bunch of squiggly lines, but that's the way he was taught to do it] Tommy: What's your name? Stewardess: Melissa. [Tommy scribbles a small thought to Melissa underneath his name and then hands the napkins and pen back to her with a smile. She looks like she's just died and gone to heaven with the smile across her face. She slowly climbs to her feet not even taking her eyes off of Tommy for an instant] Stewardess: Thank you, Tommy. Thank you so much! It was a shame what happened to you on Sunday. I hate that black man!! [A couple black people in the nearby rows look up at her with angered looks. She sort of seems embarrassed by the moment but she looks back to Tommy's tanned face] Tommy: Well, I guess that makes two of us. [She giggles and then starts making her way back toward the front of the plane. Tommy waves at her as if signaling a short farewell. That's when his cell phone begins ringing. He sort of jumps back against his seat in shock. He pulls out the phone and looks at the name on the phone. He looks confused at first but then a moment of disgust comes across his face] Tommy: Dude, what do you want? Man's voice: Tommy, you need to get back to LA right away. Tommy: If something went wrong with Tubular Tiki then you have someone else handle it. I'm on a most excellent trip to meet my brother and I am a BUSY compadre! If it IS about the chain of waterparks and the construtction costs then just spend what you have to dude. I'm loaded and money is no problem, you know that. [Tommy's agent, Jerry Higgins, sighs on the other end of the phone always hating to deal with Tommy] Jerry: Tom, this isn't about any of your business ventures. This is about a call I got from PCW front offices. I was kind of startled that they would be calling me but they informed me that you weren't answering your phone. Tommy: I have caller ID. Jerry: I know. Tommy: Then why did you wonder about it!! Jerry: Listen, the front office just wanted me to tell you that you have a big match coming up this Tuesday. [Tommy rolls his eyes] Tommy: Oh yeah? A big match against some totally bogus compadre. I probably won't even get a most righteous rematch for my Continental Title! Because if it's a rematch then I'll show up, but if it's not then you can call them up and tell them to shove their match. Jerry: Tommy, your in a 10 way dance for the vacant World Championship. Tommy: See, that's what I'm talking about! You give them a most bodacious title reign and then they... [Tommy pauses for a moment to really let Jerry's words sink in on him. If Tommy's ears aren't deciving him he could only swore that he heard he was getting a shot at the World Title] Tommy: ...dude, are your lying to me? Jerry: Why would I lie to you about that? It's a 10 Way Dance and the winner gets the vacant PCW World Title. Duke Williams forfeited it on this past Wednesday Turmoil and he retired, going out on top. Tommy: Dude...this is BODACIOUS!! I'm going to be the World Champion!! Jerry: Tommy, they aren't just going to hand the belt over to you. You have to get through some really rough competition. Tommy: Pft, Jer, your being totally heinous! They SHOULD hand me the belt. It will only make things easier on everyone involved in the match. Jerry: It's a No DQ thing too. Anything can go in this match and with 10 of the best in one PCW ring it's going to be VERY crazy. Tommy: Crazy I can do, dude. I'm a SUPER VILLAIn...remember? Jerry: Of course, how could I forget? Tommy: Your sarcasm is most bogus at this moment. Thanks for the call, dude. I'll send you a fruitcake or something. Jerry: Joy. Tommy: SARCASM!! Jerry: Sorry. Tommy: Later, dude. [Tommy hangs up the phone as a smile slowly starts creeping on his face. He may have lost the Continental Title at March Madness but it seems that the wrestling Gods have smiled down on his once again. Now he gets a shot at the World Title, a chance to trully stand at the top of PCW and show all those heinous compadres just who the best in the world is. Nine other men or not he's going to get through them without a problem because that belt is just as good as his. As he looks out of the window he realizes that he needs Harry more than ever now] Fade to black
