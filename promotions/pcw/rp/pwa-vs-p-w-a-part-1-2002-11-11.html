PWA vs. P.W.A (part 1)

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 - 11 - 02 'Where the hell is he?' hissed the distinctive voice of Natalia Miyagawa. The scene opened on the rage fuelled eyes of the Pranks With Attitude valet, as she stared cold-heartedly at her watch. Looking up at the door, Natalia peers once again at her watch and then again at the door as though her head were fixed in a pendulum motion. Capturing the slender physique of the Russian beauty, she paced sternly back and forth, obviously waiting for the arrival of her PCW asset Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'Oh! Where could he have got too?' murmured the recently acquired manager. With a solid hour passing by since we last saw Gaz Green tackling the daunting staircase, Natalia settled on the idea of her man going M.I.A (Missing In Action) and decides to call a truce on the bickering. An exchange of squabbling that has escalated out of proportion, with her tail between her legs, Natalia ventured towards the door. Standing before the door, the separated wife of Primetime veteran Alexander Miyagawa bent over in a Stacey Keibler moment, retrieving the mounting mail that littered the welcome mat. 'Surely it can't be fan mail - Maybe-....' Natalia returning to her vertical base was unable to finish the latter part of her sentence due to a disturbing interruption filtering from behind the two inches of wooden door. Curiosity may have killed the cat but this feline was willing to risk life and limb as she pressed her ear against the door. Again the silence was broken by that of heavy panting and groaning, a familiar sound however to those who have endeavoured the two hundred plus segments featuring Hubbardville's first couple Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Kannon-Hall. 'Oh!' blurted the PCW diva, taken back with shock. Again Natalia pressed her ear against the door and again the sounds continued openly. Granted these unexplained sounds were nothing close to orgasmic but nobody said Xavier Kannon was well endowed, well at-least in body parts aside from his head thought Natalia Miyagawa. 'Arrr-.... Errr-.... Arrr-.... Errr-.... Arrr-....' The level of sound fizzled out, when suddenly one last bellowing groan exploded. With that final 'Argh' Natalia stormed through the apartment door like a locomotive, head tilted and eyes shielded she ranted and cursed, unbeknownst to her that the moans of passion were indeed those of struggle. 'Excuse me there's a time and a place! And believe me this is neither-....' 'WHAT THE F-....' roared the Emerald Menace, Gaz Green as he ascended the final step in this uphill struggle. Obviously the heavy panting, groans and moans belonged to the luggage-lumbering Jester who dropped his cargo to the ground. Removing her hand, Natalia looked at her colleague with embarrassment, eyebrows raised and jaw lowered she stood with a dumbfounded expression. Our Conniving Comic however could care less as he wiped the sweat from his brow, looking every bit the shell of his former self. 'THAT'S IT!!!!' cried the Jester as he exceeded the end of his tether. 'What's it?!?' questioned Ms. Miyagawa. 'The Front Office can book me in any kind of match for any show, cause there's no way I'm going to tackle them stairs again. I feel two stone lighter, no wonder I qualify for this Cruiserweight Tournament, another couple of flights and I may have been the first anorexic gimmick to emerge in Wrestle Tattler!' proclaimed the Lord Of Laughter, a nametag not appropriate for this particular moment in time. The devious duo Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa made a joint effort of carrying the bags into the confines of the Jester's pad, laying them down on the laminate flooring. Slouching into a leather reclined chair, a manner of relaxation, Gaz grasped the remote of his home entertainment centre before bashing away at the keypad. Suddenly the speakers kicked in with the annoying sound of feedback followed by Linkin Parks - One Step Closer, yet the blank screen of the television set remains. Scratching his head in confusion, a re-enactment of Stanley Laurel, the regarded Prince Of Pranks looked for answers from the snickering Natalia. 'Hmm-... So where's MTV?' asked the PCW Tag Champion. 'Gaz, I know your not exactly quick on the up take but isn't it kinder obvious what's the problem?!?' retorted the petite Russian rolling her eyes, trying desperately to contain her growing hysterics, the giggles almost seeping through the corners of her cherry red lips. 'Oh Yeah - The speakers are working but the tube has blown!' replied Gaz in triumph. 'IT'S THE BLOODY STEREO-.... You dumbass!' screamed Natalia in frustration, lowering her tone as she came to the end of her comment, unbeknownst to Gaz that he has been brandished a 'Dumbass' and for good reason. Swapping over the remote Gaz pressed a series of buttons and footage of Friday Night Fusion's Main Event began to play. Notably it was the latter part of the bout, drawing closer to its conclusion, a Pranks With Attitude victory. Drawing a smile was the verbal exchange between Gaz and his boss, CEO Xavier Kannon before its penultimate climax of a finisher delivery. 'LAST LAUGH~!' 'LAST LAUGH~!' 'LAST LAUGH~!' continued the Velvet Trickster favouring the RAW style of commentary. His gloating and growing ego were soon thwarted as Natalia intruded upon the proceedings both physically and verbally as she showcased her vocal prowess whilst handing over the bundle of letters. 'OK ENOUGH-.... I understand that scoring the 'Last Laugh' over Kannon was a pinnacle moment in your career as was the greatest successful title defence in PCW history but must you keep on about it? It was bad enough sitting through your rants on the plane, then the limousine ride back here but now you've gone and hit the hat-trick!' injected the stern Russian with authority. 'Err-... Arr-... Well-... Hmm-...' stuttered Gaz Green in response. 'Lose the Gareth Gates portrayal and read the mail. You may find your P45!!!' snarled the beautiful valet, a complete u-turn to her somewhat calm and relaxed state we've been accustomed to. 'P45?!?' gulped the former NTICW competitor before ravaging through the envelopes like a wild animal, sending fragments of paper fluttering into the air before unearthing one document of interest. Eyes swaying side-to-side as he followed the small print Gaz looked concerned, which spurned a similar look from his travelling comrade, a look followed by the warranting of an explanation. 'What is it? What does it say-....' Soaking in a deep breath Gaz dictated the document out loud. 'The first couple of lines are legal gibberish but then-... It basically goes on to say 'The Headmaster of Primetime Wrestling Academy has consulted legal aid over the use of the initials P.W.A and would like to take action if their demands aren't met'. It continues 'An ultimatum has been issued. Therefore the name Pranks With Attitude must be abolished and you have a week from the date stated above to comply otherwise the matter will be settled in a court of law'. Whoa this is serious and according to this both myself and Nerezza have been dealt copies!' 'Who's the company representing the Academy?' said the inquisitive diva, sympathising with Gaz who tries to fathom the events that unfolded merely minutes ago. 'Duey, Screwyu and Bigtime Lawyers firm!' answered the once jovial Jester. Dwelling on the situation, one half of the dynamic duo and his associate, in this case Natalia Miyagawa weigh up the available options. Seconds filtering into minutes and still nothing when suddenly Natalia had a brainstorm, a swerve that may work in their favour. 'I GOT IT!!! We'll counter sue the Academy-...' 'For what?' interrupted Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'We'll counter sue on the grounds that P.W.A have been used as cheap publicity by Primetime Wrestling Academy in their bid to gain both recognition and potential talent. All we'll have to do is prove to the Judge that there has been an influx of students since Pranks With Attitude graduated and that your departure spurned an instant growth in applicants to the Academy!' Taking a huge gulp and the information drawing a cheeky grin, Gaz Green leapt for the telephone. 'Who you phoning?' asked Natalia. 'I know just the man to represent us on such a case, JOHNNY COCKRAN!!!' responded the King Of Komedy enthusiastically, a return of the Jester's glowing attitude, which over the course of an hour has taken a real battering. 'THE GUY WITH THE CHEWBACCA DEFENCE?!?' With Gaz hammering at the keypad of the telephone and Natalia offering encouraging smiles, spurning on her gladiator the scene eventually closed, only to re-open on the next instalment of the on-going saga of Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A).
