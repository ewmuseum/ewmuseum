The Transition: Monday Morning

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (FBI Branch Office, New Jersey; Monday, 11:39 pm EST) Skylar was sleeping in a cell 10x10 cell. Not the circumstances he'd have liked for getting some peace and quiet, but at the least it was away from Violence Jack. Violence Jack.... He tried not to think about him, tried not to let his anger build up. Violence Jack got him arrested on bogus charges, burnt down his home... the girls. Were they safe with the police? Did Jack have them? .... Forbid it, were they in the fire? Jack would die a slow death if so, just slow enough that he could be given the names of those innocent children he killed. Skylar put that out of his mind. Sandy was somewhere, he didn't know. Why would they arrest him during his match anyway? He was one second away from winning... Violence Jack must have reported that Skylar was a flight risk, to leave the town as soon as the bell rung. Good for him, the destruction of his home and the false arrest... Jack no doubt felt that he is in control, that he had Skylar where he wanted him. Good for him, that he felt as though he were in any way, shape, or form in control of the situation. What had he accomplished? Skylar's house came furnished at no extra cost, the owners moved while there was filming for Bad Boys 2. The problem with the IRS would easily be resolved by his receipts, which he kept in a secure account in Wachovia Bank, something that only he and Sandy knew about. His mind went back to Sandy. She would know to stay with people. Skylar didn't know who, he would call her once he was released. A simple house-arrest bracelet and he'd be sent on his way. If Snow can get out to wrestle, Skylar could, as well. Though this case is about tax fraud, and if you're not a big energy corporation giving money to the federal government, they will most definitely strike down upon thee with the wrath of God (separation of Church and State my ass). Violence Jack... Skylar knew what he was trying to do. Destroy his life, bit by bit. Personal, with the loss of his home. Big deal, the dwelling is replaceable. Sandy and the kids... they are what made it a home, not the walls or the furniture within. If Skylar had them, home could be a cave. Sandy was his friend and companion, his love interest. She was the key to his strength, her support gave him the ability to do almost anything. The children... Skylar knew their former guardians, both of them. Through drug overdoses and gang-violence, they had lost two families before. Skylar wanted to spare them more hardship and give them a stable place to call their home. And after only a month, he already broke his promise to them. A cheap trick that Violence Jack played, foregoing his match to find Skylar's house and bring it down. Skylar remembered the home invasion of Crimson Flame in Eternal Wrestling. Crimson Flame was a goof, but Skylar and Young Tiger were there and they saw the kind of stress it was putting on him as a family man, the way the attack was affecting his children. Last, but not least, this arrest would lead to a severe audit for Skylar, and he'd be watched like a hawk even if proven innocent (which was assured once Sandy could go get his endorsement check receipts and the house note from Wachovia). Professional, by holding back his career. Jack should be at the Extreme level, but he came in gunning for Skylar, and Xavier Kannon (no fan of Sky) would have no problem putting him in the Continental area just to keep Skylar from attaining it. Violence Jack was unnecessarily occupying Skylar's time, and getting booked to do so. Skylar was disappointed, has been for a while now, that the Golden Hummer is broken. Back when it was operational, the riff-raff was dispatched before anyone had to deal with the fruitcakes and wackjobs of PCW. Hell, Dark Ages could even be fired to go back to clean-up duty for the Saotomes and SuMa's of the federation. All that was happening was that Jack would keep Skylar from continuing on with his career in a month. Violence Jack had won these first few skirmishes. Nothing major, he had just bought himself some time to come up with some other sophomoric game to play. Actually, to call them games is wrong. Games have declared rules and referees. Jack's games were just random attacks on anything related to Skylar. Jack took this to be a war. There are rules to war, as well, but in modern times those rules are ignored and replaced with one, the most convenient: anything goes. Violence Jack wanted a war? He would not be obliged. Violence Jack was a shell of himself. The little boy Bruce Shanahan, the kid who witnessed his friends die and not he wants to share his pain. The man who couldn't succeed on his own, so took to worshipping obscure gods with hard-to-pronounce names in order to gain confidence. The wrestler who would pick out all the broken Cheerios at his seat at a table in the buffet area because he believed that eating them made him any less perfect than he already was. Violence Jack, Bruce Shanahan... he was just a boy in a man's body, desperately lashing out, wanting attention. Wanting to be comforted. He wanted to have loved ones, he wanted to be liked and befriended, and to be invited out for a drink. He wanted to be involved, but his actions kept pushing people away. So he'd respond by doing something even more violent, only to push people further away. It was a never-ending downward spiral that Jack forced himself into. He'd need to be physically taken out of his misery. And that would be Skylar's time. March 16th, the pay-per-view. March Madness. He would take Violence Jack one-on-one in the ring and make him realize the error of his ways. If not, Jack would just have to be assisted in breathing for the rest of his life. Skylar could wait, and Jack was no doubt running out of ideas. Skylar could wait, he had other things on his mind anyway. The Continental Title, which he would get another shot at sometime soon for Totally Tubular Tommy's holding onto the ropes to win. The referee's decision wouldn't be reversed, but another match would come his way. The World Title, which he could get a shot at just by beating Adam Stryker on Tuesday Turmoil. If he could make it to the arena in time, he knew he could beat Stryker. It was a very good possibility. Skylar would never guarantee victory, but his lack of a clean loss for months on end made him feel very confident about his abilities. Bar Stryker cheating in any way, Skylar had every reason to believe that he could and would win, going on to the Battle Royale. A little conflicted, Skylar felt, as to which should mean more to him. The opportunity to win the World Title? Or the opportunity to wring Jack's neck? He would decide later, even so, the match with Violence Jack would come first, before the Battle Royale should Skylar be booked to both matches. But one thing was for certain... 'You've gotten my attention, Bruce.....'
