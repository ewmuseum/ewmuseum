Cleansing (part 2)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01 - 01 - 03 (New Years Day) Prologue: Our last chapter of 'Cleansing' saw the Conniving Collective of Gaz 'The Corporate Jester' Green and Natalia Miyagawa well away from the adrenaline fuelled City life-style of Las Vegas Nevada and into the dank backdrop of its outskirts where they had decided to P-A-R-T-Y. From the usual pencil pushing office work to the social life of a Front Office Jester we captured our host Gaz Green mingling with the natives at a grim nightclub known as the '6th Gate'. Even with celebrity status the PCW performer and his valet weren't permitted yet with a few well-chosen words from his silver tongue, the dynamic duo finally graced the dance floor where the night would turn a little sour. SOUR? Well sour for those attending as the establishment turned to that of a senseless blood bath as the guests were unveiled to be vampires. Disposing one of these nightly creatures with a little timely aid from Natalia, the rejoicing was short lived as a group of weapon wielding thugs homed in on our heroes and that's where our next episode begins-.... 'OH CRAP-....' gulped the Emerald Menace as he peered over to the mounting group of thugs who continue to toy with their arsenal of weaponry, which seem to range from baseball bats to lead pipes. 'So tell me, do you have another bright idea?!?' questioned the inquisitive valet who cowered behind her client who's retort was to peer over to the rage-fuelled group of thugs with a half hearted attempt at a smile. Being that of a dead end and in the company of a group of merciless vigilantes, it looked as though there was no escape for the Conniving Collective as they stared death in the face. Even for a mastermind prankster like Gaz Green the options appeared to favour everyone except himself. 'So you see yourself as a slayer, huh!?!' roared one of the thugs as he stepped from the density of his flock. The man in question was African-American with short bristled hair, tattoos ridden his body with tribal bands wrapped on his upper arms and a tight fitting tank top giving the unknown individual a gym enthusiast look. With a dose of sarcasm Gaz Green merely looked behind himself as though the man were speaking to another. The man continued to press forward yet Gaz and the faithful Natalia held their ground waiting for the inevitable when suddenly the leader of this vigilante group stalled only a few feet away. 'I asked you whether you saw yourself as a bit of a slayer?' said the ring leader once more, only to be interrupted upon by the untimely comment of the Emerald Menace who again shadows his own fear with his own deceptive facade. 'Slayer? Gaz The Vampire Slayer has a nice ring to it but I don't think I could ever make the transition from wrestling to television-....' 'Comedian, huh?' hissed the gang member as he grinded his teeth. 'Well I try-....' retorted the Jester with another sarcastic remark as he continued to shield Natalia from the advancing forces of this ruthless mob who's leader grins on with evil intent. Waiting for yet another vampire moment, the music blaring from within the nest of the '6th Gate' Gaz waited for the group to finally change and avenge the death of one of their own who had been killed only minutes earlier. Suddenly however the ringleader stretched out his arm as though to gesture with a handshake, which the Jester obliged to with a dumbfounded expression written over his once jovial face. 'Arr-... Err-... To take a word from your arsenal squire, HUH?!?' said the Front Office member with intrigue as he shook hands with this stranger before him, his band of men still brandishing their choice of weapons aloft. Peering back to his fellowship the man gave a subtle nod and one by one the weapons lowered, easing the tension between the two parties but still leaving so many unanswered questions that litter the corner of the Jesters mind. Still plastered with his dumbfounded facial expression those answers would materialise as the man informed Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa the reasons of their presence. 'We're a vigilante group but not just any we have sworn to make the streets safe to walk at night. No longer will people be scared to walk out on the streets in darkness and no longer will people fear the vampire threat-....' 'They call me Gunn and this group are 'Blood Bath' and for obvious reasons. No reason to tell who you guys are because we already know Gaz Green, we knew you might stumble on this hornets nest and we thought this be an ideal time to see whether you really have severed the ties you once had with the vampire they call Nascosto Ireul Nerezza!' concluded the leader, now known as Gunn who's hand has been wrapped in a thick steel chain giving it a whole knuckle duster effect. 'So how did I do squire?' asked the King Of Komedy standing smug and his inquiry seemingly involving an initiation test. 'Not bad for a rookie!' retorted the man known as Gunn as he rubbed his bristle-ridden face before Gaz injected himself into the conversation once more enlightening those around of the nature of this encounter. 'So you guys are the exterminators - Well I must admit you look the part but if you want to see pieces of paper with the presidents head courtesy of The Front Office then you must fulfil a task that's right up your street-.... I want you to eradicate the thorn in my side-....' injected the Corporate Jester with a serious tone about his voice and his face looking far from jovial, a stern gaze of disgust filtered as he reflected over both recent events and their encounter at Decimation. 'YOU WANT THEM TO SLAY NEREZZA?!?!?' interrupted the Soviet Scarlet unaware that this whole episode was indeed a charade laid on by the Blood Bath to see whether the contract was genuine or a ploy to set them up for slaughtering. The team of the Blood Bath and the PCW contingent of Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa re-entered via the fire exit and back into the nightclub '6th Gate' which now resides in a desolate silence with the only bellowing coming from the speaker system. Even though the music continues to blare, the occupants have since vanished, to be replaced by piles of ash that litter the dance floor, first inspection would indicate that the Blood Bath have already made their presence known. Suddenly from the darkness of this dank dwelling emerged another bloodthirsty vampire who had hid undetected from the mass slaughtering. Fighting for its survival the male vampire lunged from the black emptiness, his attack like a silhouette through the lighting spectacle of the disco. - THUD - Even before the vampire could make an impact, the being had been eradicated in mid-air by a projectile fired by Gunn who stands over the heap of burnt ash quite triumphantly. The silver harpoon lying on the heap, waiting to be retrieved, glistening as the lights danced over. 'ANOTHER ONE BITES THE DUST-.... SO TO SPEAK!' screamed the former Pranks With Attitude member quite sarcastically as the Blood Bath conquer another vampire infested dive, their sole purpose in their pitiful lives. From within his velvet business jacket Gaz 'The Jester' Green removed a folded piece of paper to the onward glares of those surrounding. Handing over the document to Gunn, the Blood Bath mastermind gazed over the small print with his head swaying in a pendulum motion, observing the pros and cons of working for the Front Office Entertainment Manager. Seemingly satisfied Gunn reached over the wooden bar but to no avail as he lost out in his quest for a pen, only for Gaz to issue one before his eyes and Gunn finalised the contract of 'Nerezza' by signing away his soul to the devil. Looking somewhat official it appears that the contract on Nerezza's head has been snapped up by bands of vampire slayers who call themselves ‘Blood Bath’ who have been drafted in after the constant failure of Gaz Green. 'Is this a ploy by the Jester to simply intimidate his vampire associate Nerezza?' 'Has the pressure got to Gaz Green and his only option left is to eradicate Nerezza totally?' 'Or has this contract been issued from the powers above, is Xavier Kannon testing his Front Office Jester's loyalty?' Whatever the reason, everything will surely come to light as Nerezza joins those who perished in the '6th Gate' by fighting for his survival against the ruthless fellowship of the Blood Bath. Slowly fading out the image closed on the satisfied face of PCW's Corporate Jester whose eyes peer down at the fine print and most notably on the signature of the contracted Gunn. - To be continued -
