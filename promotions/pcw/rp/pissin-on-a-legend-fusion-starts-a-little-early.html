Pissin' On A Legend: Fusion Starts a little early.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered ::Our scene opens up at the PCW Pavilion in Atlantic City, just a few hours before Friday Fusion begins and the new PCW is started. Not even the dark matches (where we get to see the likes of Lady Knox~! Vs Boca Del Inferno and the Ultimate Warlord in a Ménage Je Trois handicap match) have begun yet, but the arena is already half-full, and you can't be surprised. Atlantic City is the home of the most hardcore PCW fans in the world that harbored the very birth of PCW. Those that were there in the beginning have returned once again and anxiously await the arrival of a new PCW, full of new life and a freshness that will hopefully bring it back to the top of professional wrestling, regaining its spot as the World's premier federation. But with the arena less than half full, and no action to be seen for at least an hour or so, everyone is sitting stretched out and relaxed, talking amongst themselves and eating snacks, not really paying any attention to the ring sitting in the middle of the arena. Suddenly 'Bulls on Parade' by Rage Against the Machine hits over the PA, the crowd turn to look where the music is coming from with curiosity. Corey Williams struts out from behind the curtain with no pyros just his own flamboyancy and charisma. He walks out and holds out his arms with a huge grin on his face, expecting the fans to cheer him. Even when they begin to boo him he takes no notice, just flashing even more tooth. He climbs into the ring and does a 360 twirl whilst holding his arms out, his long, flowing blonde hair and bear fur coat follow his body, doing the same twirl just a second or so after. Corey walks over to the side of the ring furthest from the entrance and places one foot on the bottom rope and another foot on the second rope and taunts to the fans, who respond with boos accordingly. He leans over the top rope and one of the PCW ring crew hand him a mic. He returns to the middle of the ring once more and flashes that Hollywood-style grin before putting the mic to his lips.:: Corey: Now I know that some of you, very few of you, were hoping that the new era of PCW would begin with The Phantom Booker, Big Alan Steele of even Duke Williams and the Malice Crew coming out and talking about how the new era 'couldn't have been achieved without you, the fans', but in case you hadn't noticed, this ain't no 'fantasy fed', it's reality. ::Did you get that last gag?:: Corey: And in reality, we base life on facts. And the fact is that you fans who sat on your asses and watched everyone else do all the work, you fans who went to OSW 'because it has 'Streak', you contributed ABSOLUTELY NOTHING to the coming of the new PCW era! ::The fans boo but Corey grins, the boos just egging him on.:: Corey: There is one man whose ambition, vision and skill made this new era possible, one man who stood up against all that the old PCW stood for, one man that will continue to fight for all of you to get rid of the old school trash this place has gathered, one man who deserves the thanks and gratitude of EACH AND EVERY ONE of you fans.... and that man happens to have the same name as I do. ::Corey laughs at his own little joke, too damn amused with himself to notice a 'Corey Sucks!' chant coming from somewhere in the back of the arena.:: Corey: And being the man who single-handedly PIONEERED the new era of PCW, I feel that it is my duty, nay, MY RIGHT, to be the very first man you see on new-era PCW television. Besides, I couldn't deny all my fans in Atlantic City.... ::The crowd cheers.:: Corey: ...Who have botched up their life so bad that they need to live life through me.... ::The crowd boos, Corey finally responds.:: Corey: Hey, I'm not trying to be controversial or anything, it's just the damn truth! ::The crowd boos even louder, but Corey has gone back ignoring them and finishes off the previous sentence.:: Corey: ...The right to get their daily dose of controversy. And as the most controversial billionaire on this planet, being 'what the people wanna see' and the man who PIONEERED the new-era of PCW, I feel I have the right to say that, although Nelson and the Booker can squabble all they want about ownership of PCW, but when it comes to the new-era, I MADE PCW! ::The outrageous claim from Corey causes the fans to boo loudly, as they always do..... Stupid fans, why can't you have more variable set of responses!? Corey begins to pace back and forth within the confines of the ring.:: Corey: But before I declare that the new era of PCW has begun, unofficially of course but there was never anything controversial in officially anyway, I've got a little bone to pick. There's something that's been bugging me as I look around the locker rooms of PCW and see guys like Miyagawa, Chet King, Duke Williams and Jon Kano. And it finally struck me when the Phantom Booker decided to hold Fusion here in Atlanta City; sure, PCW's new era has begun, but until all the resembled and symbolized the old era of PCW has been destroyed, PCW cannot move forward. Until guys like Miyagwa, Chet King, Duke Williams and Jon Kano take their rightful place in a retirement home, until The Phantom Booker and Nelson finally give up and give the people what they wanna see by making me the official owner of PCW, the until the kid from 'Jerry Maguire' finally reaches puberty and until Clyde retires to become a priest, PCW cannot truly move into the new era and defeat OSW. ::Corey stops pacing and returns to the middle of the ring.:: Corey: And although I can't fix ALL that tonight, I've gotta start somewhere, and there is one thing I can fix....... This building has been a part of the old PCW for longer than anything, it saw the birth of PCW and is the home of the old PCW's most dedicated fans, it harbors the very essence of the PCW legend....... that is why it must be destroyed. That is why it must serve as the ultimate example to those so-called legends out there that their days are numbered. This building must be disrespected, vandalled and raped, just like all the other PCW 'legends', starting with Kano on Turmoil, will be. So.... ::Corey unzips his pants.:: Corey: Let's get to it then shall we? ::Corey drops the mic and then his black leather pants, followed by his leopard skin underpants, exposing to the crowd what he has often referred to as 'what the people REALLY wanna see'. Corey then proceeds to soil the PCW Pavilion ring, creating a dark yellow stain on the blue mat.:: Corey: That's what I think of your legend! ::Corey continues to piss, oblivious to the many fans booing what has to be one of the vilest acts in the history of PCW, even viler than Doug Troy jobbing to Hank. In fact, Corey is so oblivious that he begins to cheerfully whistle a tune. Finally four security guards run down to the ring and approach Corey. Corey zips up his pants and picks up the mic, holding out a hand to tell the security guards to slow down.:: Corey: Whoah whoah whoah! Calm down guys, I'll go peacefully. ::The guards continue to approach Corey.:: Corey: Jesus! I said calm down, OK? Now, just hold onto these while I finish this promo and then I'll come along quietly. ::Corey reaches into his pocket and gives each security guard a thousand dollars cash to 'hold onto' and then turns to the audience.:: Corey: Ladies and Gentleman, may I now (unofficially) declare the NEW-PCW... brought to you by CCW himself.... OPEN! ::'Bulls On Parade' by Rage Against The Machine hits over the PA system and Corey tosses the mic onto the pee-stained mat. He climbs out of the ring and walks off looking pleased with himself, 'escorted' by the four security guards, who now seem a little more interested in stopping the fans from throwing stuff at Corey than from bringing him to justice. The scene fades to plaid!:: ~The End~
