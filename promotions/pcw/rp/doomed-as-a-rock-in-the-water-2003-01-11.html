Doomed as A Rock in The Water

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 11th, 2003 A walk through central park would normally be considered a pleasurable moment. Especially in the winter time when the park’s beauty is brought out through the snow that had collected on the ground and in the tree’s. But it was hard to focus on its beauty when I had much more important matters on my mind. Would this quarrel between Chris and I ever come to an end? Or would it continue on and make matters much worse than they already are. We barely spoke anymore and if there was any existing love left it had yet to be shown. People normally say that things have to get worse before they can get better, but how much worse can they possibly get? Hopefully this would be the end of the road and things would take a turn for the better, but I could only dream as I walked through this winter wonderland. Walking across the Bow Bridge I walked over to the edge looking down at the frozen lake below me. I thought of the fish underneath that layer of ice and it reminded me of how I too was feeling at the moment. I felt trapped by emotions just like those fish below the ice that were trapped in a frozen hell. There was no way out for them or myself. I did not wish to lose Jackson as a friend, but at the same time if I continued my friendship with Jackson Chris would take it the long way and no longer want a part in my life. The choice was mine but the choice I had wanted was not an option. Picking up a somewhat large rock I tossed it forward and watched as gravity overtook it and pulled it down towards the frozen lake. [b]Crash[/b] The rock impacted with the ice, causing it to split. It was a good thing I had decided not to bring Christa and Alex skating today or they would have had the same fate as the rock tumbling below the icy surface. Thinking of them I could hear the laughter of children humming a tune inside my ears. Noticing it was not the laughter of either Christa or Alex I looked up to see where it was coming from. Scanning the area with my deep blue eyes I came across a small group of three children playing along the shore of the ice. I smiled as the children served a reminder of the angels that awaited me back at Chris’ apartment. I looked at my watch and decided it was time to head back home before they became too attached to Chris but before I could reach the end of the bridge the happy tune of laughter that played in my ears became a tune of horror as the laughter sunk into cries of help. Shooting my eyes back in that direction I had noticed that one of the kids had fallen through just as the rock and was failing in being able to keep his head above the shoreline. At first I was frozen in my spot with anticipation of hope that the kid could do it just like the little engine that could, but as seconds went by and still no sign of the child I couldn’t stand by any longer. My child or not I had to save him and save his parents from the same grief I almost had to go through nearly a month ago. I ran down the path leading to the lake as the cold winds of January bit at my already rosy red cheeks. Sure it was barely above the zero degree mark and sure the firefighters had already gotten plenty of calls of help for these boys, but I couldn’t stand here and wait for them to arrive, for I had already heard plenty of stories of how once the firefighters got there it was already too late. Reaching the shore I tossed my jacket aside and immediately the winter air started to attack, but I refused to let it get the best of me. If I could handle all the emotional stress that had piled up upon me the past few days not to mention being double teamed by Xavier Kannon and Darkstar this past Sunday then I could sure as hell handle the numbness of a January breeze. “Help.” The chorus of two children roared as they watched on helplessly as their friend slipped under the ice once more. I ventured out onto the ice, not knowing what lied ahead of me. I couldn’t help but wonder what would happen if the ice collapsed under my weight. Would I be able to use all my strength to keep myself above water, or would I be consumed by the same fate as the rock I tossed over the bridge. I blocked that from my mind as I kneeled down on the edge of the ice and stretched out my arm as into the cold icy waters it went. I managed to grab a hold on some part of the child’s body. Whether it was his arm or leg I wasn’t sure, all I knew is that I had a grasp on him and I wasn’t going to let go even if it meant risking my own life. I could hear the ice creaking in the background. Any minute now it was due to give. Hopefully it would wait until I along with the child was safe. I used all my strength to pull the child from the water. I succeeded but not before the ice gave way pulling us both in. Suddenly my body was consumed by the same numbness that had overcome my cheeks and ears, but I refused to let go of the child, even if I had to go with him I would make sure he was safe, after all I would want someone to do the same for Alex or Christa, even if it was Teresa Thomas my opponent for this Tuesday’s turmoil. Thrashing my legs wildly I did my best to keep above water, but to no avail I was pulled under the icy hell. I closed my mouth and prayed that if I was destined for hell whomever it was that was in charge of this earth whether it be God or Kami, that they would take back every wrong thing I had ever done and let me be happy up in heaven. Just as it seemed as though I was doomed I could hear the sirens of fire engines penetrating through the barrier of water. Within a matter of seconds I would be saved. Before I could think another thought I found myself being pulled up by strong arms out of the icy hell with the child in my arms. I did it. I had saved the child just as the firemen had saved me. I felt a sigh of relief come over me as the cold air started to eat away at me once more. I could feel my body temperature dropping with the cold but once again I refused to let it win. Instead being carried away in the arms of a firemen and then being placed on the ground I found the strength to refuse any further help and head back home to the ones that really mattered to me more than my life. The ones I had realized could be taken away from me in a matter of seconds, Alex, Christa, and Chris. But before I could be pulled away I felt a sudden tugging at my pant leg. Thinking it was either Alex or Christa, I pulled the child close and embraced him only to find out it was the friend of the child had saved. Thinking of Christa and Alex I couldn’t help but smile and look down at the child wondering what he wanted to tell me. “Thank you for saving my friend.” He said, looking happy but still rather shaken up by things. “Not a problem.” Was the answer I gave, before walking off back in the direction of Chris Storm’s apartment. But before I could get far I noticed that the young child was following me. “Miss, can I come with you and call my parents from your house?” The child asked. “It’s not my house, it’s my boyfriends, but sure you can come along.” I said figuring it would do no harm. Christa and Alex would not mind having a playmate even if he looked to be a few years younger than them, not to mention I’d hate to say no to the child after all he had already been through this morning. So I took him by the arm leading him back towards Chris’ apartment while thinking that things could not get worse from here. They had to get better. It was like one of those mandatory things just like me beating Teresa Thomas this Tuesday. If I didn’t beat her surely Chris would think I was just as helpless as the child who had fallen through the thin layer of ice and that would be the end of it. I had to win this. It was one of those things required to me in my mind, just as saving the child on the ice and if I could do that then I sure as hell could beat Teresa Thomas and that was what I’d do. I’d beat Teresa Thomas and prove to Chris that he did not need to worry along with proving that I was worthy of winning a title as prestigious as the PCW Television title. I would do it if it was last thing on earth I did, but first I had to get home and warm up. After all who wants to spend their morning in the bitter cold? Certainly not me.
