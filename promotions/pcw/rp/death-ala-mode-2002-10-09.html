Death Ala Mode

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th October 2002 “May I take your order sir?” A small, smartly dresses Japanese waiter bowed beside me holding a pen and a small fancy looking pad in his hand. This place fuckin’ scary… I was actually overwhelmed that someone actually asking me what I wanted to eat, usually I had to ask the waiter to come to my table but the service here was great, so great it was beginning to scare me. “I’m ready waiter….” Fat Man raised his hand from across the table and smiled behind his glasses as the waiter moved over to take his order. As the talked in Japanese I screwed up my face, I not belong here…but they giving me free dinner so I guess I not care! It must’ve shown because the man sitting next to me began to chuckle before nudging me in the ribs as I glared at him. The man continued to chuckle before speaking, “You should really learn Japanese Jung, it’d do you a world of good and it would impress the company!” Pffft, I thought to myself. I already done enough for the company! Only reason I out here now is because I getting paid tonight! I smirked at the thought of getting paid. “I see you’re considering the idea Jung…very good!” The man smiled and nodded his head, Why do all Japanese have to fuckin’ bow?! It was o.k for them to bow when they were sorry or when they thanked someone, but after every sentence it got a bit annoying. “Can you do me favor….” I let my voice trailed off as I motioned for the businessman sitting next to me to come in close, I may be rude but I didn’t want to make a scene in the fancy restaurant so as he leant over I whispered in his ear, “Bow again….I going to snap your fuckin’ neck!” I patted him on the back before smiling and leaning back in my chair, the businessman leant back in shock gulping as I winked at him, I love it when they scared! Fat Man finished ordering his food as the waiter moved over to the businessman sitting next to me as both of them began to converse over ordering as I sat and looked at the menu confused as a White Boy…. Udai must’ve seen this because he leant over the table, “If you need help ordering Master Loc, I can just order you the same thing I’m having….” “And what would that be…I not want a 4 course meal Fat Man!” I snapped back at him trying to convey the point that he was getting on my nerves, Udai chuckled to himself as his belly began to bounce up and down underneath his black suit before he snapped his fingers and the waiter returned to him. “Master Loc would like what I’m having, and a nice glass of….” Udai glanced over to me as I smiled and leant back in my seat taking time to think of the most expensive glass of alcohol I could. I smirked once more before just stating matter-of-factly, “The most expensive bottle of Champagne you got…and put it on ice White Boy!” I finished the sentence off with a wink as Udai smiled a huge white grin at me before bowing to the waiter who then moved off as I glanced over my shoulder at the businessman who was paying for it all as he began to fan himself getting a little hot under the collar. I nudged him in the ribs, making sure that my elbow dug in hard as he tried not to wince I chuckled to myself. Now let see who going to want to speak Japanese…. “So were all the good pussy?” I blurted out bluntly at normal conversation volume, Udai chuckled and covered his mouth looking down and trying not to laugh as the TokyoPop businessman sitting next to me blushed and wiped a drop of sweat from his brow as people behind us began to turn around and stare. Heh, keep looking! Make him feel the heat…. I sniggered in my head as I continued to get dirty looks from the surrounding posh people sitting at the tables around us. “What do you mean by that?” The businessman pretended he had no idea what “pussy” was and took a sip from a glass of water sitting in front of him. I put on a shocked expression before placing my hand on my chest, “You not know what pussy is?!” I pretended to be taken back by what he said as people began to turn around in their seats at the mere mention of the word, Now it going to be Jung time to embarrass you! I turned around to the people behind me and put my hand over one side of my mouth before shaking my head and stating, “Can you believe?! He not know what pussy is!!” An old lady dressed with a blue tiara, shiny dark blue dress, glasses and wrinkly old skin and gray hair gasped as her husband shot a glare from across his table at me as I explained again. “I know! It shocking!!” I shook my head at the old Japanese man as I heard Udai muffle his laughter from across the table as I turned my attention back to our table. The businessman gritted his teeth and shook his head in frustration, clearly embarrassed, “If the Company wasn’t paying you….I’d make an example out of you!” Heh, we see about that! I took a look over at the live band playing oriental music at the stage at the front of the restaurant before I nodded towards Udai. Fat Man shook his head as I lurched over with one of my hands and grabbed the businessman around the throat as the band stopped playing and everyone in the restaurant turned their attention to our table shocked as I was near assaulting a high up TokyoPop representative. “Why you not make an example out of me now?! HUH?! Or you want me to make one of you?!” My eyes grew wide as he began to sweat and shake, “I-i-i-i was joking Jung…i-i…” I clenched my fist before raising to his face. Cutting him off I spoke, “It Master Loc to you! And you ever threaten me again, I goin’ to break your fuckin’ face! You hear me?” The businessman begin to nod furiously as I tightened my hold around his collar before he managed weak smiles to the audience we had attained. They looked on in shock as I released him and the band then continued to play as though nothing had ever happened. Moments after the whole ordeal the waiter brought out a bucket of ice with a bottle of Champagne sitting in the center of it which looked like it cost a million dollars. Now that what I talking about… I smiled and just as the waiter turned to leave I snapped my fingers as he turned to look at me. ”How much this cost?” I asked him with a smile, As all Japanese tend to do he bowed before smiling and answering in a snooty voice, “The bottle of Champagne was 500 dollars.” I wonder what going to happen if I say this… I widened my eyes pretending to be impressed by the price as I looked back over to the businessman before glancing at Udai and then asking to the patient waiter. “I think I want another one…this guy paying for it all!” I reached over and tapped the businessman on the shoulder pretending to be best of friends with him. Once again Udai began to chuckle as he leaned over and whispered in my direction, “You’re lucky he’s not taking this out of your pay Master Loc! But keep going, this is amusing!” I nodded my head as Fat Man began to nod his before leaning back in his seat and preparing a white napkin around his neck as three waiters each brought out silver platters in front of us. Udai looked like something out of comic strip as he grabbed his knife and fork in each hand showing no class and awaiting the waiter to remove the top to his dish, beside me the businessman looked white as a ghost knowing this meal would cost him over a grand. Thank god we not saying grace! I licked my lips expecting something like a big succulent lobster and my eyes grew wide with anticipation. “Enjoy your meals” The head waiter said as the other three waiters lifted the tops off of our dishes as my eyes slowly shrunk back to their normal slanted selves. A huge grin crossed Udai’s face as he began to tuck into the small serving of squid that was stuck in front of his surrounded by a little rice. What the fuck…. The businessman next to me took a sip of champagne as the waiter poured me and Udai one as the businessman then began to tuck into a tiny bit of chicken with some tofu surrounding it. This not right!! I looked down and saw a small serving of squid surrounded by a little white rice as I looked up at Udai who had finished his meal and was wiping his mouth before taking a sip of his champagne. “What the fuck up with this?!” I stared at my food before looking up at Udai, Udai smiled at me and looked at my platter licking his lips, “It’s the finest squid in Tokyo…if not all of Japan Master Loc! Don’t you like squid?” I looked down at my meal, “No I just used to getting more on my plate than a prawn!” Udai chuckled to himself again as he put his knife and fork in the center of his platter before pushing it away from him and continuing to sip on his glass of champagne. Fuckin’ call this an expensive meal! I pay two dollars and get bigger French fries from Macdonalds! I shook my head and glanced over at the businessman who was still taking small bites out of his chicken. “So when I going to get paid?” I got straight to the point considering we’d all finished our meals, The businessman took another small bite out of his chicken before wiping his mouth with the napkin around his neck, “The money has been deposited in your bank account.” “How much money?” It occurred to me they’d never told me exactly how much I’d be earning if I pulled of the win at Red October. The businessman wiped his mouth and grumbled, “Exactly 1 point 5 million….” Fuck that a lot of money…. I smirked and leant back in my chair knowing I had just become a millionaire as Udai clasped his hands together and bowed before pouring himself another glass of the expensive champagne. “So I not need to stay here and pretend to be upper class anymore….” I trailed off, The businessman looked kind of took back as he had nothing to combat the question, “No….” “Good. Then I getting my expensive bottle of champagne and going back to my hotel room to get drunk in peace while listening to decent music!” I emphasized the word decent as I new the snooty people would be listening in. How you like that? HUH?! I patted him on the back before getting up out of my seat as Udai held up his glass motioning he’d meet me once he’d finished dinner. I was just about to get up out of my seat when the waiter appeared by my side holding a platter with a black cellular phone sitting atop it, What now?! I glared upwards at him, “What you want?” “This phone call, it’s for you Master Jung Loc…” he bent the platter with the phone on it down to my level as I picked it up and breathed into the phone. “Who this?” I asked getting straight to the point, There was a chuckle before a familiar voice cut in, “Don’t you remember me Jung? I’m the happiest man in Tokyo!” “Oh yeah….the fucker who pull the gun on me and told me to win!” I snapped back at him with a sarcastic tone, “That’s right Jung! I’m the man who threatened you with your life and made you win me 5 million dollars!! But lets put that behind us….I’ve phoned to congratulate you on your achievement!” The man chimed, I shook my head knowing he wanted something else, “Really….” “Well actually Jung, I phoned to tell you I have another 2 million riding on you for your last event in Tokyo! I figured I’d skip the formalities…” The man trailed off, I heard a chuckle as he began to speak again, “You know the drill, you lose and you’ll be leaving Japan in a bodybag! Good luck Jung and thanks in advance for the now 7 million! I am greatful!” “Pfft, whatever White Boy….” The man hung up as the last words escaped my mouth, the Yakuza betting money on me once more. I slammed the cellular phone down on the platter before grabbing the expensive bottle of Champagne out of the waiters other hand and picking myself up and storming out of the restaurant. -=***=- I not believe this…. I like a fuckin’ animal! They bet money on me and give me nothing in return and expect me to win it for them or else I lose my life?! Whatever they think they pulling, I going to make sure that it come back on them! They not scare me….they just lucky that I not going to lose! I not like losing and I not going to lose any time soon! The Vietnamese Viper is going to leave teeth marks in Japan by the time he leave! Mark my words….
