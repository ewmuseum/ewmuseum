Andy is in town part 1

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22-1-03 15-1-03 6.30pm The Casino. Bob Vurdo sits at the table in the room at the back of the casino puffing at a cigar. Sitting on his right is Tony who has a rather concerned look on his face. Sitting on the left side of Bob, Mat and Lukey nervously look at the face of Tony. 'What do you mean she was already dead?' Bob can't figure out what the two men are talking about when they say the receptionist was already dead when they arrived at her home yesterday. 'When we got there she was dead.' Mat wriggles around uncomfortably in the chair that is too small for a man of his size as he tells Bob the story. 'Tell me everything from the start.' Mat nods at Bob who is trying to think of who could have killed her. 'Well me and Lukey here drove over to the house you told me to go to just after you rang when we noticed a few police cars out the front. I decided to get out of the car and ask one of the officers what happened. When I did the officer said that she was murdered with a meat cleaver to the forehead and that was it.' Bob gets up from his seat and takes a few paces around the bare concrete floor of the room. 'Ear less fucking Andy.... That sneaky bastard.' Tony looks over at Bob who immediately stops pacing around the room and turns back to face Tony. 'Who's Ear less Andy?' Asks Luke. 'Ear less Andy is a mean motherfucker who doesn't have any ears. He has just shown up in Amber Hill and already is starting trouble. He would have killed the receptionist and taken the tape.' 'What happened to his ears?' Mat elbows Luke in the arm for his ignorance as Bob prepares to answer the question. 'No one really knows but there are lots of rumors about it. Some say he cut off his ears and sent them in a box to an enemy to make the enemy think he was dead. Some say he cut off his ears and ate them so he didn't starve when he was in jail. I really don't give a flying fuck but that man is trouble.' 'What will we do about it?' Tony is obviously very concerned with the situation. 'Want me and Lukey to go around and kill him?' 'We can't kill him because he most likely has the tape that has the evidence that I'm in the Mafia on it.' 'Ahhhh.' 'Fat' Mat nods at Bob. 'We will organize a meeting with him and see what he wants for the tape.' Bob can already tell that Tony is formulating a plan in his head. 'All right well I have a job for you Mat and Luke.' Both Mat and Luke look up at Bob who sits down in the seat he was in before. 'I want you to find Ear less Andy and tell him we will meet him next Thursday night at 7.00pm.' 'Sure boss.' Mat smiles at Bob who nods his head in a way that suggests Mat and Luke leave. Mat lifts himself up off the seat and grabs Lukey by the shirt to tell him they're leaving. The two then walk out of the room. *** 17-1-03 A bar in Amber Hill. Luke sits on the stool at the bar of 'Lockleys Pub' in a fairly crowded room. There are people all around the place sitting in booths talking, sitting at the high tables in groups of four, at the bar or playing pool. Sitting in one of the booths is Mat who is too big to sit at the bar stool due to the risk of breaking it and the unconfortability. 'What can I get for you.' The middle aged bartender seems fairly cheery as he smiles at Luke. 'I am actually looking for someone.' 'Who?' The bartender leans in closer to Luke. 'A man known as Ear less Andy. I was told he has been coming in here lately.' The bartender pretends to think for a second. 'I might know something.' The bartender holds out his hand asking for a bribe. Luke obliges and pulls his wallet out of the back pocket of his blue jeans. He hands the bartender a fifty dollar bill. 'See that guy over there playing pool.' The bartender points to a large, rough looking guy who is playing pool with another rough looking guy. Luke gets off the stool and walks over to the pool table and begins watching the game. 'Earless' Andy takes a shot and manages to sink the 6 ball into the far corner pocket. Andy's pool partner then notices Luke looking on at the game. 'Who are you?' Andy's friend looks up and down Luke. 'I am Luke and I'm looking for 'Earless' Andy.' Andy immediately turns around and faces Luke. 'I am Ear less Andy what can I do for you.' Andy sounds like a rough man with the tough guy accent. As Andy smiles at Luke revealing his yellow teeth with two missing on the upper row. 'I need to have a word with you.' Andy smiles once again and raises an eyebrow. 'Concerning what exactly?' 'Vurdo.' Andy smiles and begins laughing to himself as he hears Bob's name. 'What does that fucker want now?' Andy moves in closer to Luke which makes him nervous. Luke is a very good fighter but he knows when he would be outstrengthed and outfought by a man and if there ever was a man it was Andy. 'He wants to speak with you.' Andy pauses to think for a second. 'Sure. I will speak with him on one condition.' Andy smiles at Luke. 'What's that?' 'I want you to do a job for me.'
