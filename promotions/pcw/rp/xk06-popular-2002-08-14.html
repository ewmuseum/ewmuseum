XK06 : “Popular.”

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/08/02 XK06 : “Popular.” Location : Where It Matters.Date : 13th August 2002, 10:30pm. Nobody ever grows up wanting people to hate them. At school it’s all about popularity... Those that have it use it and those who don’t crave it. People change to be popular. “YOU FUCKING SACK OF SHIT!” Look at our society and it’s rulers. The government that sets the Tax level... Legislates law that tells you what you can and cannot do... Decides when a nation’s youth will be sent off to die in war... All there because they got the most “X”s on ballot papers. They were the most popular. “I HOPE YOU BURN IN HELL MOTHERFUCKER!!!” Governors.... The men and women who run cities. Those who control the Police that protect you as you go about your daily business... Who budget the Fire Department that will pull you from your burning building while you are passed out from Smoke Inhalation... Who hold the purse strings on the hospital that will resuscitate you... ...They will be which man was more popular on voting day. “DISRESPECTFUL COCKSUCKER!” Homecoming Queen... The crowning achievement on a girl’s high school tenure... Goes to whoever is the most popular.. Whoever has the most admirers. “WANNA TAKE ME ON.. COME ON YOU FUCKING YELLOW BELLIED BASTARD!” If there was a survey of the whole planet that asked the simple question “Do you want people to like you?” it’s fair to say that the YES vote would far outweigh the NO ballots. “WANKER!” Basic human society was founded on popularity.... The “Alpha” male is only a fancy way of saying he is the most popular. Be it the stronger, younger or fitter member of the pack... He is the one who’s gene line is carried down.. He is the most popular. “KISS MY ASS YOU LITTLE QUEER!” Take the workplace.... Bosses want to be liked. They don’t want to be the “Suit” with the big office... They want to be “One of the boys”.. They want to be on their worker’s level. They are in a higher position with a superior wage and better benefits yet they want to bond with those lower than him... He wants to be popular. “YOUR WIFE’S A FUCKING WHORE!” In wrestling, especially on a small scale, popularity equals money. Both in a personal and technical sense. To be popular with the fans.. Having them like you will sell your merchandise. It will have them paying the $19.99 for your t-shirt or buying one of your tapes. Them liking you, cheering you, will pay that bill you left on your kitchen table. “CUNT!” To be “Over” with the fans leads to more than just merchandise revenue. When a crowd cheers your name during your matches... Pops for your every move and rises from their seats when your music hits you can get that push you wanted. More TV time. Higher up in the card. Better wage package... All from being popular. “GINGER TWAT!” Then there are those who hate society as a whole. There are people like Xavier Kannon who see society as one big oppressive mass that want to see him held down. Even if he did make them that way. “FUCKING SHIT EATER!!!” Before, Xavier was one of these people who played up to the fans. He gave into his most simplistic of human instincts... To be liked. He loved walking through the curtain to a huge cheer, the sound of 1000’s drowning out all but the bass of his music. “COCK SUCKING FAG!” But that quickly changed. In a blink of an eye the cheers morphed to jeers.The slightest change in inflection altering the noise from a sound of love to a sound of loathing. “BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!” Since then he has told them what he thinks is wrong with them. He has berated them for not accepting his beliefs. Insulted them for cheering those he dislikes... Each insult drawing more jeers for him to scream oppression at. “ARSE BANDIT!!!!!!” Then at Turmoil he took the next step... While the crowds had almost become immune to his insults.. Numb to his spiteful words... They were not expecting what he said about something they care for the most... Primetime Championship Wrestling. “YOU SCREW GOATS!!!” He found the Achilles’ heel... The one thing that even a PCW crowd hardened to the Scientologist’s actions could still get passionate about.... Their absent legends. He dragged their names through the mud when they were not there to defend it. Kannon saw his opportunity and he went for it... A nerve was struck. “FUCKING PANSY ASS FAGGOT!!!” He told them how he felt like he had never done before.. The first PCW Champion and the last were mentioned... He took the names they cherish and did his best to not only bash them.... But take the shine off them. He wanted the fans to hate him and they did... Because he made their heroes a little less untouchable. “WORTHLESS PIECE OF CRAP!” It worked. They tore the building apart to try and get to him. No bigger compliment can be given to a man like Xavier Kannon. He knew they would not reach him but the anger in their eyes was all too clear. “BURN!” He defaced PCW. He disrespected it’s past. He mocked it’s heroes. He trashed everything it stood for. “PRICK!” Right before he agreed to he match where he plans to win it all. Popular? No. Powerful? Yes. “SHIT EATER!” Guess which one he cares about.
