The Clone Wars Part 4: Finale

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/02 The camera fades in… We see a still image of the previous Chef promo with the Chef and Emeril quarreling on the kitchen floor of Emeril’s Kitchen of Doom!!! Narrator: Welcome back to another hilarious and god-awful Chef promo! When we last left the Chef he had defeated Emeril, well somewhat, and he was springing into action to save Bertha Clone #1, who was being threatened by Bertha Clone #2… Let’s roll some footage from the last promo just to take up some of your valuable time… The still image starts to play. The Chef: Now where was I? Emeril: Um, I believe I was on top of you smacking you in the face… The Chef: Oh, ok…wait a second! …. Emeril: What? The Chef: (Stupidly) Weren’t you holding that rolling pin over there too? Emeril: Yes, yes I was… CRACK!!! The Chef slams the rolling pin into the face of Emeril, causing the wooden kitchen utensil to break in half! The Chef: No soldier of Allah is THAT DUMB!!! The Chef turns around and is startled to see #2 on #1 holding a chef’s knife over her head, ready to drive it into her blubber filled body!!! The Chef: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The screen pauses fades to black, and then the camera fades in once again to another image. Narrator: Now let us continue with the finale to the Clone Wars series!!! The Chef sprints towards the Berthas, trying his hardest to break up a potentially life-threatening scuffle! Bertha Clone #1: OWWW! F*cking watch that b*tch @$$ f*cking knife, it’s ho @$$ d*ck f*cking pointy!!! Bertha Clone #2: F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! The Chef jumps up behind Bertha just as she thrusts the knife down into his Bertha’s stomach. He grabs her flabby arm and restrains the forward progress of the knife. The Chef: Fight back now Bertha! Bertha Clone #1: F*cking ok b*tch!!! Bertha kicks with all her might, nailing Emeril’s Clone in the stomach and sending her and the Chef flying back INTO THE WALL!!! The Bertha Clone #2 slams into the wall, with the Chef flattened in between her and the wall, and slowly falls face first to the floor with the Chef on top. THUMP!!! Bertha Clone #1: F*cko, you saved me!!! Bertha runs over, grabs the Chef, hoists him up, and gives him a BERTHA BEARHUG…OF LOVE!!! The Chef: Bertha…you’re…squeezing…the…air…out…of….myyyahhhhhhhh… The Chef slumps over the shoulder of Bertha, passed out from the loss of oxygen. Bertha: F*ckface!?!? I’m gonna get you f*cking out of here! F*cking KEEE-YAAAHHH!!! Bertha jump kicks the window in the kitchen and is about to jump out of the kitchen WHEN SHE IS ATTACKED BY EMERIL!!! Emeril: BAM!!! BAM!!! BAM!!! Emeril keeps on kicking Bertha in the shins, trying to weaken her balance. It doesn’t work though, as Bertha maintains a tight hold on the Chef. Bertha: Stop with those god for-f*cking-saken sound effect, motha f*cka!!! Bertha leaves the Chef draped across her shoulder. She grabs Emeril by the throat, lifts him up into the air, and SWALLOWS HIM BY HIS HEAD!!! Bertha: MMHHHHGGHHHF*CKINMMMMMHHHHGGGHHH!!! The voice of Emeril can be vaguely heard. Emeril: Oh god, I’m in heaven, I’m finally united body to body with my true love, BAM!!! AHHHHHHHHH!!!! Suddenly Bertha begins to chew on the head she has stuffed down her mouth. She continues to hold Emeril up off the ground, and violently knees him in the groin time after time to create one of the most hilarious combinations of torture ever! Emeril: AAHHHHHH!!! Stop chewing…and hitting me in the BAMBINOOOOOOOS!!!!! Bertha finally pulls Emeril out of her mouth and stares him right in the eye. Bertha: F*cking don’t both me next time, because I f*cking LOVE TO F*CKING SWALLOW!!! Bertha winds up and SOCKS Emeril right across the kisser, sending him flying over the table onto the floor on the opposite side of the kitchen. She then bids a farewell to the still KOed Bertha Clone #2 and leaps out of the broken window, carrying the Chef with her!!! Bertha Clone #2 begins to stir from her slumber, and rubs her noggin, wondering where the heck she is… Bertha: What the f*cking f*ck!?!? “Bertha, you’re ok!!!” Emeril runs over and tries to hug the powerfully portly woman, but Bertha quickly clinches her fists, causing Emeril to put on the breaks and hold up. Emeril: Bertha, don’t you remember me? BAM!!! I’m the guy that helped make you… I’m the guy that taught you how to live, laugh, and most important…get freaky, BAM!!! Bertha: What the f*ck are you talking about c*cksucker!?!? Emeril is surprised by Bertha’s response, but then notices the big bump by her forehead. Emeril: BAM!!! That’s a nasty looking bump you’ve got there, do you remember getting it? Bertha: I don’t remember a c*ck @$$ed thing, ho skank!!! Emeril: Oh my goodness…YOU’VE GOT…AMNESIA!!! Bertha: I f*cking do!?!? Emeril: Yes!!! Let me help you…BAM!!! Emeril walks towards Bertha with his arms outstretched, but Bertha, questioning every thought in her mind, does not appreciate his kindness. She quickly lifts up the amiable Emeril, spins him around in a gorilla press type fashion and launches him at the refrigerator, SLAMMING HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE KITCHEN APPLIANCE!!! Bertha: I f*cking gotta get outta here!!! Bertha Clone #2 leaps out of the same broken window and leaves the kitchen and her former lover Emeril all alone… The camera then shows an image of Bertha walking away from Emeril’s Mansion of Doom, still holding the Chef over her shoulder. Bertha: We better f*cking hurry up f*cko…we’ve gotta make it to the @$$ c*cked plane for mother f*cking Fusion!!! The Chef finally stirs from his slumber and examines the surroundings. The Chef: What…what’s going on Bertha? Bertha: We just f*cking saved each other's lives, that’s f*cking all! The Chef: Great…well what in Allah’s name happened to the other Bertha and Emeril? Bertha: F*cking beats the sh*t out of me…ho @$$ b*tch! The Chef: Oh, well I bet we will probably see them again…Allah willing… Bertha: And when we f*cking do, it still won’t be the f*cking last time… The Chef: Well you know my captivating cow; every hero needs an antihero. Captain America has the Red Skull, Sherlock Holmes has Dr. Moriarty, Van Helsing has Dracula, Bin Laden has everyone else…it’s all a rich tapestry! Praise Allah for bad guys to beat around!!! Bertha: Yeah…f*cking praise that c*ck sucker…b*tch… “THE END” flashes on the screen. The camera fades to black…
