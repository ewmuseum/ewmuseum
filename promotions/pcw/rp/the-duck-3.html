The Duck 3

Date: Just now A tear of joy rolled down the side of his face as he beheld the most precious item he’d ever obtained in his miserable excuse for a life… “My… my precious…” We join the dastardly deliverer of deceit, Syko the Goblin, as he lovingly gazes upon the PCW Television Title in all of its shimmering glory. Lurking within his own impenetrable vault, the Goblin seems to be taking a personal moment to bask in the visage of the belt. Dare he touch the majestic belt of gold upon its pedestal, or should he leave it to the tempting pose that allures him so? Either way seems like a stairway to heaven, a forever ascending journey to greatness, a… “Quack?” Syko the Goblin: “ILOVEITANDITLOVESME!!!” …Blurbs the suddenly erratic Syko, turned away from the belt, his body taking the form of a human barricade. The vault door, still hanging ajar, has allowed easy access for an unwanted guest to enter. STG: “(Tone of malice.) Yoooou!” Scowling, Syko points at the intruder accusingly, and takes a step back. STG: “You BLOODTHIRSTY mongrel with wings! What do you think you are doing here?!” The Duck, as it pretty much is known as, has come in search of its fondest friend, Syko. Curiously, the Duck tilts its head to the side a bit to try and look around Syko to see what is perched on the pedestal behind him. Duck: “Quack?” The Duck sees the title, then hears a click. Returning its focus to Syko, it finds itself staring straight into the barrel of a .45 caliber. STG: “YOU’RE HERE TO STEAL IT, AREN’T YOU?! YOU TRAITOROUS BEAST! I KNEW IT ALL ALONG!” Mouth agape in astonishment of the accusation, the mallard pleadingly shakes its head “No.” The Goblin scoffs. STG: “Ha! You are just like that blithering fool, Pendragon. You’d do anything to get your filthy mitts on this belt (Pointing a thumb back to the magnificent item.), wouldn’t you? WOULDN’T YOU?!” Silence. The Duck is pretty much blank headed on the subject… But, Syko maintains his question, waiting rather impatiently for an answer. The Duck looks at the belt. Then returns to Syko. Belt. Syko. Belt. Syko… STG: “Bah! You just earned yourself a few extra minutes…” Shoving the pistol back into a holster (Pocket) Syko waves the Duck off, dismissing it. STG: “Bide you time well, fowl, for you leave me to my work, now! I have plans for Pendragon, and don’t have a second of time for you! Get lost!” The Duck looks at Syko for a moment, reluctant. STG: “I SAID LEAVE ME, BEAST!!!” Syko points to the cracked safe door, and grudgingly, the Duck waddles towards it, its head hanging low. About to step out, the Duck looks back, but Syko is still pointing to the exit. Finally, it exits, and takes slow steps once outside… There is silence for a moment. The Duck continues to slowly trudge away. But, suddenly, the Duck hears something! From within the open vault! It perks up at the tone, and its tongue falls from its bill, joyously hanging from the side. From within the vault: “I’m sorry, my very precious friend…” Just about flipping head over heels, the Duck races back to the vault, poking its head in first, just about to enter… When it stops. STG: “…Did that mean ol’ ducky-wucky scare you?” The Ducks eyes widening in horror, it watches as Syko tenderly holds the belt in his arms, rocking it back and fourth gently. STG: “Don’t worry…” The gaze Syko adores the finely crafted belt with… STG: “…It won’t get you. You’re safe with me… mine forever and ever! (Giggles!) And if it, or any other meanies try, I’ll just have to killy-willy them brutally, because I love you soooooo much!” Rubbing his cheek against the belt’s plate, as if romancing it, Syko begins to scrape up his flesh upon its jagged details. Finally, Syko places the blood stained precious down upon the pedestal again, and leaves the safe with haste, looking back one last time before shutting the safe door. A loud bang signifies it has locked into place. Taking a moment to sigh, Syko leans against the safe door, grinning from ear to ear, before walking off. The Duck was gone before he ever got out there… --- We open up with a dazzling shot of the PCW Television Title… As a large, stainless steel kitchen knife pierces through it. The Duck glares at the picture on the wall. Its foe. Its nemesis. Its archenemy… The TV title. The fire in the mallards eyes could set it ablaze at any second. As it hurls another blade through the TV title picture on the wall, the Duck contemplates one thing. How to get rid of it. To be continued…
