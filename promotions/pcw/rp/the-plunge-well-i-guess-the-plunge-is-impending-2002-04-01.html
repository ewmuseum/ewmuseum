The plunge, well I guess the plunge is impending

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/1/2002 ~Love. Has there ever been such a strong emotion? Hell no. Love has made many men do things that they wouldn't normally do. It has been recorded that a man has lifted a two thousand pound car off of a loved one because of the raw emotion of love flowing through his body. Love is such a unique emotion that in its purest form is the single greatest thing on this earth, but it can so quickly turn into hate. Jon Kano has loved so few people in his life, but he himself is loved by so many. The one person Kano has loved more than his deceased brother has been his longtime, on-again, off again girlfriend, Kristin. These two have been through a lot. They met when they were fifteen years old and freshmen in high school. They became unseparable instantly. They went through Kano's brief army career, and grieved together went Kano's brother died. Kristin has put up with Jon Kano's constant absences from Las Vegas, where she shares Kano's mansion with him, and she has been the most forgiving woman Kano has ever known. You see, when Jon Kano first became a major star in the wrestling industry, he lived the life. He drank (which he still does, as you all know), he smoked (which he still does, as you all know), and he womanized. When Kano arrived in Vegas after a road trip, Kristin confronted him. At first he denied it, but then he admitted it. Kristin left, and Kano continued to womanize. At that time, Kano was still a very nieve person. He was only twenty-three years old and figured he could have any woman he wanted, which may have been true. He soon realized the error of his ways. On another stay in Vegas, Kano and Kristin got back together and have been together ever since. Recently, Kano has been thinking about marrying Kristin. He loves her, without question and without reserve. She loves him. The reason Kano and Kristin haven't married already, is the problem of timing. Now may very well be the time for this step in Kano's life.~ *The scene opens up inside of a deli located in Mesa, Arizona, a lovely suburb of Phoenix, Arizona. At a little round table in the corner is PCW's very own one-man papparrazi, Cal Seaver, and seated across from him is PCW's very own living legend, Jon Kano. Cal Seaver is wearing his trademark K-mart clearance sale polyester suit. Jon Kano is dressed in brown Doc Martin boots, baggy blue jeans, a black 'Spring Fling' t-shirt, and a black hat with PCW's logo across the front. Both men are half-done with their sandwiches and are talking amongst theirselves.* Cal: So Jon, why exactly did you ask me to meet you here? I know it wasn't just to talk about your golf handicap and the NCAA Final Four. Kano: Off-the-record? *Cal looks very cautious.* Kano: Cal, promise me this is off the record. Cal: Alright, it's off. *Kano reaches into his pocket and pulls out a small, black velvet box.* Kano: I want you to look in the box and tell me what you think. *Cal opens the box and the camera pans to show the huge diamond-encrusted ring.* Cal: Damn, I didn't know you felt this way about me. *Kano reaches across the table and pimp-smacks Cal.* Cal: OUCH!!!!!! Kano: That is for Kristin. Cal: I figured that. So you are finally taking the plunge, huh? Kano: Yeah, you wouldn't understand seeing as you are a flaming homosexual and everything. Cal: I AM NOT!!! WHY DOES EVERYONE SAY THAT!!!! Kano: Maybe it is because you haven't had physical contact with a female since 1986. But anyway, what do you think? Cal: I think Kristin will love the ring. Kano: I hope so. It is quite odd. Cal: What is? Kano: I have been successful in everything that I have done. I have become one of the biggest icons in PCW history. I founded and I run a Fortune 500 company. I won Kristin's heart, lost it, and won it back. Yet everytime I think about asking her, my knees begin to tremble. I don't know why. I am not an easily shake-able person. I am the same guy who kidnapped PHD and tortured him for two and a half months in the Nevada desert. Yet everytime I am in Vegas and as I see her on the couch taking a nap or laying there watching the big screen and I think to myself, 'I am so lucky. I have the most amazing woman in the world and I'll do anything to keep her.' I sincerely believe that and I thank God everyday for my career, for my life, and most of all, for her. She has been the one person that has been with me through thick and thin. I can call her at four in the morning and talk to her about anything. She knows me better than I know me. I seriously cannot imagine my life without her playing a major role in it. Cal: Jesus, Jon, are you going soft? Kano: No. I am still the biggest prick in PCW today, and a REAL LEGEND, but this is truly how I feel about Kristin. Cal: Why are you going to marry her now? Kano: Well, I am going to ask her to marry me. I have been thinking about it since about Thanksgiving. I look at my life and career and I think I can take on this challenge of marriage now. I am ready. I just hope to Christ she is. Cal: Well, Jon, not to get off subject, but shouldn't you be concentrated on El Diablo? Kano: What the hell is with everybody? Just because I spend five minutes talking about my PERSONAL life, doesn't mean that I am not thinking about my match with El Diablo. He is a quick little f*cker. I'll give him all the credit in the world, but he has never faced a mna quite like Jon Kano. He got his ass handed to him by Ivan Stanislav, but so did everyone else, and Ivan is no Kano. I am the one who revolutionized PCW. I am the one who defined the lifestyle of what a true superstar is supposed to live. I am the one who took the old school, then I took the new school, and then I combined those two and made them MY SCHOOL. I am the one who beat Alan Kriegman twice, I am the one who beat Tyler Nelson twice, and I have beaten Boda. Those three men may just be the three biggest superstars in PCW history and I beat them. El Diablo is a good wrestler and is absolutely great at teaching the developing talent the ways of this industry, but he has never really beaten the caliber of opponents that I have beaten. I am the New Age Icon and a REAL LEGEND because of who I have defeated and what I have done to make this industry just a little bit better. El Diablo may one day be a legend, but right now the legend in this match is me. I like Diablo's style, but I have a style the likes of which have never been seen before. I combine flair, charisma, and raw talent into this style and the style is called excellence. El Diablo uses many unique maneuvers and tactics to win his matches, but when all is said and done, and when all the dust has settled, it will be myself and not El Diablo who will still be standing in victory because all of EL Diablo's unique moves will not be able to equal the deadly duo of the Kanospear and the Kanoplex. And all of that, the style, the moves, the contrast, and the destiny of myself is why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Now, Cal, lets go across the street and get royally sh*tfaced. Cal: Well you can, I'll just have a couple Shirley Temples. Kano (mumbling): F*ckin' p*ssy. *The scene fades to black as Kano and Cal Seaver make their way to the bar across the street.* ~Well call the Weather Channel, hell has just frozen over. Kano may just have made the best decision of his life, or the worst.~
