Golden Heart Chronicles: Lucy In the Sky with Diamonds Chapter 2 Part 1

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/02 Golden Heart Chronicles: The Golden Heart chronicles are an insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we get to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The Golden Heart Chronicles are split into different chapters where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. Read and Enjoy, hopefully this will fulfill you’re thoughts on how Juan Borro really is. Time: 1: 16 P.M Location: MexicoScene: Spring Break “Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds” Part 1 The scene opens with Juan Borro, Juliet Svetna, Chef, Bertha, Kevin Klaymore, and Mandy. They are all seen carrying bags to their hotel where they will be staying for their Spring Break Vacation. They all decided that the women wanted to get away and relax for once, so this is what the majority decided on. (Juan was a little hesitant to come, but know one really knows why. Everyone thought the wild and action packed Juan would love to be in a place with such thrill and life.) Chef: Let’s check in, I made reservation at the most elegant and enchanting hotel. In the picture it looked something like a palace. We want nothing to little for such royal class people as our selves. Mandy: I don’t care, as long as I can get a nice tan, and feel the cool beach breeze, that and some good food of course. Juan: I still don’t see why we had to come over to this child infested hell whole, just to enjoy a beautiful beach and atmosphere. Why couldn’t we just enjoy ourselves at one of my beach houses? (Chef goes over to Juan and pats him on the back, letting out a roar of a laugh from the culinary conquistador.) Chef: Oh Juan, you sound like Scrooge. Take in the moment and the atmosphere. What do you have against having some fun. (Chef then nudges Juan on the shoulder.) Chef: There are some fine ladies around here. (Bertha comes over and smacks Chef in the head, knocking him off his feet and onto the golden sand.) Bertha: You f*ckin do that and I’ll f*cking b*tch slap your quere a$$ across the whole f*ckin poor a$$ sh*t hole they call Mexico. Chef: Of course dear. (Chef wipes the sand off his clothing as he gets back up. He smiles at Bertha and when she turns around he closes his fist and murmurs something under his breath. Meanwhile Juliet is looking around for the hotel and sees it over at a distance.) Juliet: I think I see the place we’re staying at. Chef: Great, are you sure it says “Kings Palace”, which is the name of the elegant hotel branch. Juliet: Yea, it sure does, but maybe you’d like to take a look at it yourself. (Chef rushes over in excitement, but when he sees the hotel he sees nothing but a small looking lodge with young adults making out all over the place. The place is far smaller and cheaper looking than Chef had seen in the brochure. Chef’s face becomes bright red in anger, as he goes running up to the door.) Chef: This must be some type of joke. Bertha: What kind of ranch sh*t piece of cow lard is this mother f*cker. You told me it’d have a f*ckin big enough bed to fit my a$$. Chef: Shut up, you’re not even real. (Meanwhile, why Chef is about to complain, Juan and Juliet stay behind, as Juliet notices the displeasure in Juan’s eyes.) Juliet: Juan what’s wrong, you seem so mad and angry. If you really didn’t want ot come here you should have just told me. Juan: No, I want you to have a good time. You’ve done so much for me, so I might as well let you have a few days of fun. Juliet: Thanks Juan, but I’d really like to know what’s bothering you, maybe I can try and fix it or something. Juan: No, thanks for asking, but it’s just me. Nothing you should have to worry about. I’ll try and have some fun around here. (While this conversation is going on Chef is charging through group of people and couples throwing them out of his way in pure anger. Kevin is just eating some pizza behind Chef watching all of the young people go flying in amusement.) Kevin: Where is the camcorder when you need it! (Chef’s anger fills up more and more as he gets closer to the front desk. One couple he throws aside gets fed up and speaks.) Guy in couple: What the hell do you think you’re doing. I can call the police on you for just throwing us out of you’re way like this. (Chef is about to go after the young man, but Bertha holds him back and takes over the issue, resolving it in her own special way.) Bertha: You f*ckin know what? (Before the guy can respond, Bertha breathes in his face, with her bad breath causing him to pass out and fall to the ground. The girl with him goes over and fans him off, to try and awaken him.) (Chef goes straight up to the front desk cutting the whole line. He slams his fists on the desk, and starts screaming. A young kid with pimples and crackling voice who works in the hotel comes out to talk to Chef.) Pimply Employee: Excuse me sir, but can…can you go to the back of the line. Chef: No! I demand to know why this place looks so run down and terrible. I paid good money for this place. Pimply Employee: Well sir, his hotel is a Spring Break hotspot and we try and keep it well maintained. Plus, nothing comes too cheap around here. Chef: What are you kidding me! I paid money for a place that looks like a palace, not a shack of teenage dirt bags. I even had a brochure that had a picture of a palace under this name. Pimply Employee: Oh yea that was a mistake. Chef: A MISTAKE! Pimply Employee: Well sir, what did you expect and actual palace on a beach during Spring Break? (The kid chuckles making snorting pig noises with his nose.) Chef: Are you trying to say that I’m stupid? (Chef’s face turns red as the kid starts to becomes frightened.) Pimply Employee: No sir, not at all. (Chef becomes enraged and jumps over the counter, grabbing the kid and tossing and twirling him everywhere.) Bertha: Kick his f*ckin a$$. (Once again, Juan ignores what’s going on. He just jumps over to the back desk and grabs the keys for his room, as Chef continues to beat up the employee. Juan makes his way to his room upstairs as Kevin, Mandy, and Juliet follow.) (Once they arrive in the room, Juan goes over to the bed and lays down in comfort. The sounds of loud music and kids screaming are all around them, but Juan tries to zone out of it.) Juan: Finally, all I wanted was a place to lie down in. (Juliet goes by Juan’s side, as Juan puts his arm around her. Juan thinks about the feeling of the two of them just being together.) (Kevin and Mandy are already ready to go out and do something. Mandy goes over to Juliet and speaks.) Mandy: Let’s go do some shopping, before it starts getting dark out? (Juliet turns to Juan.) Juliet: Is that all right with you. Juan: It’s fine. (Juliet goes off with Mandy, as Juan goes over and leans near the window. He takes in some air, until Kevin approaches him.) Kevin: I’m going to go out go out for surf, do you want to come. Juan: I’ll meet you out there a little later. Kevin: Cool. (Kevin exits, and Juan draws his attention back to the window. He looks outside and sees all the people enjoying them selves. Suddenly, Juan’s mind flashes, and he remembers something that had happened in his past.) (Flashback begins) Juan is seen at the age of probably sixteen. He walks into a dark garage with two men in it, who are leaning on their cars. Juan approaches then and speaks. Juan: I heard you guys were looking for someone to work for you? Man 1: Yea kid. You see there is a cool hotspot down at Spring Break, and all the kids are having loads of fun. Juan: Yea, I heard about it, it looks cool. Man 2: Well we have a great job for you, where you’ll be able to go there and have loads of fun. Juan: Really? Man 1: Yea, and all you have to do is bring something of ours to someone over there. It’s kind of like your or messenger. Juan: What’s the message? Man 2: Well it’s more like we need to give him something, and we’ll pay you five hundred American dollars for doing it. Juan: Great what do I bring? Man 1: Just this stuff, that makes people feel really cool and happy. It’s the new big thing, but what you have to do is make sure no one else finds this new big thing, because people become greedy and want it for themselves a lot. Juan: Okay. Man 2: Make sure no one, and I mean no one sees it. Juan: Where does the guy live? Man 1 hands Juan a map. Man 2: Once you get off the plain just follow that, and it will lead you to the guy we’re talking about. Man 1: Yea, and once you’re their, tell the guy you have some “Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds” for him. Juan: Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds? Man1: Don’t ask kid, just get that to the guy. You’re plane leaves tommorow morning don’t be late. Once you get it to the guy, enjoy the rest of your trip, and do whatever you please. Juan: Great. (Flashback ends) (Juan thinks back to that time and nods his head. He then leaves his apartment to go meet back up with Kevin.)
