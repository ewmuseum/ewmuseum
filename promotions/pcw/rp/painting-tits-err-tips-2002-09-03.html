Painting Tits... Err... Tips

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd September 2002 We arrive at our chosen destination, a room filled with light. Mainly so because it’s not filled with anything else. Except for masking tape all around the floor and several dust sheets over what appears to some furniture. In amongst all that carnage, is none other that Alexander Miyagawa, PCW Legend… and he’s busy painting the wall. Now what seems rather pointless is that he’s painting it the exact same shade as what it is already. At this point Natalia Miyagawa, his darling wife, walks into the scene to observe the “master” at work. Natalia: You know… Miyagawa doesn’t even turn about to speak to her, he continues to paint with his roller in hand, rolling the plain white paint over the top of the plain white paint on the walls. Miyagawa: I know exactly what you’re going to say. Natalia: No you don’t. Miyagawa grins, although Natalia can’t actually see it as he continues rolling (but not in an illegal drug abuse type of way). Miyagawa: Then you say what you’re going to say and then I’ll tell you if I knew it. Natalia gains a bewildered look on her face. Natalia: And what will that accomplish? You say what you think I’m going to say. Miyagawa sighs for a second. Miyagawa: You will say… You missed a bit. Natalia looks at him for a second before finally speaking at last and pointing towards the large window at the other side of the room. Natalia: Yeah but… you did. Look, there’s a huge patch under the window there. Miyagawa shakes his head in a negative response. Miyagawa: That’s just dried, I haven’t missed a bit, I assure you. Everything is covered, and I’ll be done in ten minutes or so. Then later I can go around and touch it all up at the top with a brush, ok? Natalia: You know lord of the rings? Miyagawa raises his eyebrows in surprise at the line of questioning. Miyagawa; Yeah… but I’m not doing an Elven themed room next, ok? Natalia: As if I would ask you to do that… ok… well… maybe I would. But anyway, the evil guys… the Uruk-Hai… Miyagawa nods. Miyagawa: The fighting Uruk-Hai, what about them? Miyagawa turns about just in time for Natalia to stick her right hand in the pot of white emulsion and give Miyagawa a hand print on his face, which at the moment in sans-dust mask and/or goggles. Miyagawa stands there with a frown on his face and drops the roller beside the pot. Natalia (acting all innocent): What? What are you looking at me like that for? Miyagawa leans forward and kisses her powerfully but only for a few seconds before releasing her… wiping as much paint onto her face as possible in the process. Natalia steps backwards in surprise before realising why he had done it. Natalia: Ok, this means war! Miyagawa grins at her as she picks up a paint brush, dips it in the pot of emulsion and flicks it at him, splattering all over his Duke Williams t-shirt. Miyagawa: Dammit, I paid five bucks for this shirt after Fusion, now look what you’ve done to it. Natalia stands there grinning. Miyagawa suddenly looks over her shoulder. Miyagawa: I think Christopher is crying. Natalia turns about to rush off to check on the baby, giving Miyagawa enough time to plunge both hands into the tub of emulsion and run up behind Natalia putting both hands around the front of her and grabbing hold of both tits, before releasing and backing away laughing. Natalia turns around and shows off the two huge hand prints on her formerly black t-shirt. She looks piss. Natalia (in a joking manner): Ok buster, you’re in for it now! I’m going to give you an ass kicking you aren’t ever going to forget! Miyagawa jokes back with her. Miyagawa: Oh shit, Mohammed Ali had better look out, because the wife is on the warpath! And we leave the decorating couple in the midst of chaos as Natalia chases Miyagawa around the room… presumably until he lets her catch him, but just for fun.
