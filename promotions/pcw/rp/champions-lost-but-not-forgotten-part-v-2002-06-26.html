Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part V]

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26, 2002 It befell in the final days counting down towards Gold & Glory, and The Heartbreakers are still the number one contenders and kings of the tag team division, and so reigned, that there was a mighty mass of others in the division that held war against them for their spot at the top. And the teams were called the best that PCW has ever seen in the division. And so by means of the PINK!, The Heartbreakers sent for these teams, charging them to bring everything that they have to the pay-per-view, for they were fighting for the fairest prize of them all, the tag team championship. So when the teams were to come into the ring with The Heartbreakers, by the means of great powers they were to immense in possibly the greatest battle in PCW’s tag team history. Jack and Johnny loved the tag team division and they made sure that they were the most prepared for this match as it was the biggest match of their careers. Other teams were not so prepared, and a small few were even more prepared, however, preparation will have little effect on the PINK!. Jack Valentine: “Your ass looks great today Johnny.” Johnny Heart: “Great scott Jack, yours does as well.” And as nonchalantly as ever, the Heartbreakers carried about their days in the same manner as if nothing was to happen this coming Sunday. Maybe it was for the better. After all, the other teams were fretting over their placement in this match, and striving to win with all their might, but the Heartbreakers need not worry so much. They did have the power of the PINK! behind them, and that power is far greater than any power some God or PoP star could bring to the ring. The God’s must worry about their power, the void, and the PoP stars must worry about their music and their record deals, but with the power of the PINK!, there is nothing to worry about. Akiyama is right, the PINK! is a color and nothing more...however it is a color that brings pride, dignity and gold stars into the eyes of The Heartbreakers. The Heartbreakers were advised to come to the ring, at least appearing that they were more focused than the rest of the bunch; and it’s not like the general public is looking at The Heartbreakers as a major threat in this match, however, as usual, the hypocrites want a right and a a wrong at the same time. Jack and Johnny just were. No, that’s not a typo, they just were. That’s all they were. Maybe it’s a little to much Heidegger, but The Heartbreakers were nothing more than that. Were they champions? Sure. Were they talented? Why not? But when all is said and done, forgetting about the lost and losing the forgotten...the Heartbreakers were just...The Heartbreakers. A couple of boys who wear PINK! to the ring, like to show off, and love to dress with the latest style. Nothing more. Don’t look for their past because they’ve always been this way. Don’t look too much into it, because you’ll find nothing. They really aren’t lost, or forgotten for that matter. They are just The Heartbreakers. Jack Valentine: “What’s on the agenda for today Johnny boy?” Johnny Heart: “I don’t know stud...how about kicking back and watching some movies?” Jack Valentine: “Sounds like a plan, buddy.” As The Heartbreakers conversed in their...ahem...pardon the language...queer...manner, they each got up and went to their movie collection, skimming row by row trying to find the right movie. Johnny Heart: “Let’s see...we’ve got, Birdcage, In & Out, Boys Don’t Cry, Get Real...” Jack Valentine: “Over here I see Philidelphia, It’s my Party, Threesome and...what’s this?” Johnny Heart: “What is it Jack?” Jack Valentine: “It looks like a pornography...it’s not mine, what is the meaning of this Johnny.” Johnny Heart: (Nervously) “Umm...” Jack Valentine: “I don’t want to see that filth in our house again, understood?” Johnny Heart: “Yeah Jack...whatever.” The camera zooms in on the black case with the lettering “Hard and Long” on the cover as Jack walks to the garbage can to throw it away. Jack Valentine: (Talking to himself) “Hard and Long? Must be a long movie or something...and hard to watch.” Not knowing that it was a gay movie, Jack carries about his business unaware. Jack Valentine: “Now...back to the movies. Do you notice any specific theme to these movies Johnny?” Johnny Heart: “No, what’s that Jack?” Jack Valentine: “I don’t know...they just seem a little similar to me, but what the hell, as long as they’re good, right?” Jack and Johnny both pause and awkwardly look at the camera before continuing. Johnny Heart: “Here’s one that’s interesting, it’s called ‘Two Girls in Love.’ What do you think about that?” Jack Valentine: “Sounds like a sweet story...I’m up for a romantic movie, how about you?” Johnny Heart: “Well, that’s why I picked it. Maybe later we can listen to some Barbara Streisand.” Jack Valentine: “Oh, I would just die if she came to town in concert.” Johnny grabs the videotape and they pair walks over to their big screen TV. Johnny puts the video in and Jack plops himself down on the PINK! leather sofa. The scene shifts forward a couple of hours and the end credits are shown playing and sad music is heard. Jack Valentine: (Crying) “That was so sad!” Johnny Heart: (Crying even harder) “I can’t believe she did that.” As the pair wipes the tears from their eyes, the scene fades out and yet another quote on quote “gay” evidence is cited in the books. Still though, nothing is confirmed on the Heartbreakers sexuality, even though Jack does have Christina as a girlfriend. But aside from the sexuality questions, the match was only days away and both The Heartbreakers didn’t seem to have a worry or gander about their upcoming match. And so after the movie, their lives went on, with the gold in the back...and not in the front of their heads where it belongs. All the fans said they would pray for and desire The Heartbreakers. So The Heartbreakers will do their best for their fans to capture their belts. They shall fight in this battle and win the war for the tag team titles, without breaking a sweat off of their perfectly PINK! bodies. No God, nor PoP star, nor Surfer, nor Messiah, nor Suicidal maniac, nor Hero, nor Misplaced soul, nor Terrorist, nay Politician will stand in the way of the all mighty PINK!
