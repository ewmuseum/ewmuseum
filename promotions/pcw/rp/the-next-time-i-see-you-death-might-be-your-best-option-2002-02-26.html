The next time I see you, death might be your best option!

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-02 It doesn't matter what kind of roadblock you put in the way of Jeremy Howard. He will overcome it. This man, the former two time World Champion of Primetime Championship Wrestling, is the epitome of heart and determination. Its those two key elements, of his twenty one year old life, that the Krayzie One hopes will lead him to finding his two year old son Troy, win the Continental title and beat Drake Maxwell for the freedom of his brother. This week, the heart and determination of Jeremy is going to be tested. The question is, will it succeed...... or fail? While each and every fan of the Krazed Lunatic wonders about his possibility of failure, Jeremy Howard is out doing everything he can to make them believe. It's that reason that the scene begins to fade into the now wrecked looking black BMW X5, that the Krayzie One is normally seen driving, parked in between a group of trees, only fifty or so yards from an old, run down church . There, Jeremy and Jessica can be seen moving around the vehicle, trying to move a number of branches onto the BMW, making it harder to spot the sport utility vehicle. Jessica: I've got this really weird feeling in my stomach. Jessica lays a few branches on the hood. Jeremy shoves the last branch onto the windshield of the BMW. Jeremy: Just stay close to me. You know I won't let anything happen to you. Jessica: I'm not worried about me. I'm worried about you. Jeremy puts his hand on her face, gently stroking it in a loving manner. Jeremy: Yeah, you and everyone else..... Just don't forget who I am. Jeremy flashes her that maniacal smile, that somehow is supposed to reassure this worried woman. Then, Jeremy grabs her by the hand, leading her through the wooded area that surrounds the church. It's dark now, making visibility really hard. Off in the distance, beyond where the church sits, a cloud of smoke can be seen. Jeremy and Jessica head straight for the cloud of smoke. Jessica: What could they possibly be doing? Jeremy, like a man in the military, keeps his eyes watching the entire wooded area. The last thing the Krazed Lunatic is looking for, is any sort of surprise coming there way. It's already bad enough that the man that was driving the black Trans Am of earlier knows about Jeremy, from the car chase. Jeremy: I have no idea, but I'm about to find out. Jessica: I'm scared. Jeremy squeezes her hand, letting her know without speaking that he's thinking about her. If it's one thing that the lovely Jessica should know, its that there is no way in h#ll the Krayzie One is going to let a thing happen to her. As pertaining to Jessica, he's like her bulletproof vest. Jeremy: Just hang in there, honey. We're so close to getting our son back. Jeremy and Jessica creep along the woods, getting closer and closer to the church and the cloud of smoke. Jessica: I know. I can feel our son close. After a few more feet of walking, the loving couple come to the edge of the woods, gaining a perfect look at the old, run down church. Cobwebs line every corner of the church, resembling a scene out of a cheap horror movie. Jessica's watery blue full bodysuit that she wears has no arms to it, so the chills in her body are very evident. Jeremy: That church looks empty. Jessica pulls back a bit. Jessica: You're not thinking of going in there, are you? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jessica's head drops, the fear rushing through her body. But, there's no time for fear. Whether its scary or not, its something that she's just going to have to face down. Lucky for her though, she's taking one of the craziest son of a b!tches to ever grace a PCW ring before. She's taking the Krazed Lunatic, Jeremy Howard, with her. Jeremy: I'm sorry, but we're just going to have to do it..... For Troy's sake. Jessica sighs. Jessica: Then I guess I'm going to have to do it. Jessica tightens her grip around Jeremy's right arm. Jeremy takes a deep breathe, then starts to proceed forward, slowly. The two lovers creep across the front lawn of the church, getting to the front door without a problem. Still, not a soul can be seen. Jeremy peers into the side of a window on the church. Nothing. Jeremy: Odd. Everyone must be in the back, where that cloud of smoke is coming from. Jessica: This is an occult. They could be sacri...... Jeremy puts his right hand index finger to Jessica's lips, silencing her of any negativity. Now's not the time for that. Now's the time for action. Two year old Troy is within his grasp and he's not about to lose that. Jeremy: Just follow me. Jeremy muscles his way through the front door, gaining entry. It's even darker inside of the church than outside. If the lovely Jessica's hold on Jeremy could get any tighter, then it would be now. Jeremy makes his way across the small sized church, getting all the way to the other side. Jeremy: Oh my f^cking God. Jessica: No. This can't be true. The two lovers take one step back, trying to regain there senses after what they just saw. A large number of people, dressed in black robes, stand in a complete circle around a massive fire, chanting something not of the English dialect. In the middle of the fire, burns a cross. That alone bothers the Krazed Lunatic, being he's a Baptist Christian by birth. But, where is Troy? Jessica: I don't see Troy. Jeremy quickly grabs Jessica and pulls her to the side of the window, as a single man, dressed in a white robe stands upon a platform just outside of the circle. He appears to be the leader, since the circle of members are facing him. Jeremy: Where's this Lipowitz? Jessica: Is he that guy in white? Jeremy: I don't know. It has to be though, he looks like the leader. Suddenly, Jeremy and Jessica turn to each other, staring into each other's eyes. Jeremy: He is Keith Lipowitz. Jessica: He has to be. I remember finding out from someone that he was the leader of this occult. Jeremy knods his head, agreeing. Jeremy: He's got to be the only one wearing white for a reason. Jeremy turns his head toward the fire, its circle of members and the man that is presumably Keith Lipowitz, watching the events of the occult transpire. Its hardly audible, but the Krayzie One can pick up parts of what this leader is saying. Most prominently, the words 'Thursday', 'Sacrifice' and 'Enemy'. Its unknown which one, but one of these three words strikes a nerve in Jeremy's body. Jeremy: Something's going down Thursday. Jessica: What? Jeremy glares at the occult. Jeremy: I don't know, but I'm not going to miss it. Jeremy glances down at his watch, noticing that the time is eight o'clock. Jeremy: Shit!.... We gotta get to Turmoil, so I can take care of what we talked about earlier...... But don't worry, I will be back..... I will be back for our son. Jeremy grabs Jessica's hand, leading the beautiful woman out of the old, run down church and back toward where the black BMW X5 is hidden. If this occult is still unaware, they'd better start preparing themselves. A psychotic lunatic has found you. If not for the fact that he has a prior commitment, h#ll might've come early for you. But, it'll have to wait a day or two. But that's all. Just a day or two, until two year old Troy Howard is returned to his rightful mother and father. Knowing that, the scene fades away to Jeremy and Jessica racing to the BMW to get back to Turmoil and Jeremy's plan for that.... to black!
