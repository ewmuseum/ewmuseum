Taking over KFC

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-11-02 Bob walks into the room in the back of the casino looking fairly beaten up. He has a black right eye, a bruised left cheek and is slightly limping. He makes his way toward the table which is the only furnishing in the room where Tony and another large Italian man are sitting. Bob pulls a chair out from under the table and sits down before looking over at the two men. 'We can't find Joey or Pietro anywhere.' Tony doesn't comment on Bob's appearance he moves straight onto business. 'Why did it run anyway?' 'Turns out they didn't do their job that great and must have got scarred of the repercussions.' 'What repercussions?' Bob seems confused by all this no one really knows what happened that night to Joey or Pietro. 'Dunno.' 'Well we'll just have to find ourselves some more replacements to fill up the ranks.' Tony looks over Bob for a second before shaking his head and telling him 'There are very few people left in Amber Hill to recruit.' 'It's a big city there's bound to be some young men out of jobs.' Tony shakes his head. 'We have other things we have to do right now as well.... The works pilling up Bobby.' 'All right.... All right.... I want all our men to hit the mattresses down the factory coz those black men will be looking for justice soon and we don't know what happened to Joey and Pietro.... I don't want anymore men dead.' Bob pauses before adding 'I want three or four guys to move in next door and watch over my house.... I don't want anyone getting hurt.' Tony nods his head in agreement with Bob. 'How'd ya get those bruises?' 'My match on Turmoil got a bit rough this time round....' Bob opens up his mouth revealing the lost tooth. 'I'm ganna have to go get a gold one now thanks to that little shit.' 'What good is this wrestling stuff doing anyway?' Tony just doesn't understand why Bob cares about wrestling so much. 'It's a good way to make money and earn respect...' 'Yeah.... Yeah.... Heard it all before.... I think we should give you a few men to go down with you and make sure you win those matches.' 'No this is something I want to do on my own right now.' 'Alright.' Tony decides to drop the subject. 'How we ganna finish off those black men anyway?' Bob has already thought ahead as he smiles menacingly. 'It's pretty easy from here.... I just gotto buy out that KFC in Rinning Street and we'll move from there.' Tony smiles at Bob as he can see where the plan is going. 'What ever happened to that little guy that we used to get inside the blacks?' 'He never came home after that night when he went to the party.' 'So that's the end of him?' Bob nods his head 'For now anyway.' 'Well.... Should we go and have a little talk to the manager of KFC?' Bob thinks Tony's proposal over for a few seconds. 'All right lets organize it now.' Bob gets up from his seat and slowly walks out the door with Tony and the thug following. Bob takes his cell phone out of his pocket and dials up the directory number. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob Vurdo, Tony and the thug walk out of the elevator and into the waiting room of the third floor. They are scheduled to meet with Jeremy Simpson in just five minutes. Jeremy knows Bob is coming in to make him an offer on one of his many branches of KFC but is planning to refuse the offer as they bring in a lot of money for him. Little does he know they are about to make him an offer he can't refuse. The three men sit down on three of the four chairs set up in a line along the back wall of the small waiting room. Jeremy's receptionist looks over at the men from behind her thick glasses. She looks down at the papers she has in front of her before looking back at the men. 'Who are you?' Bob Vurdo turns to face the middle aged woman and smiles at her. 'Bob Vurdo.... I have a meeting scheduled with Jeremy at 5.25.' The lady looks down at her watch which reads 5.24. She then has a quick look through the page with Jeremy's schedule on it. 'Ah yes.... Bob Vurdo he will be with you in a moment.' The lady picks up a phone and without dialing a number says 'Mr. Vurdo is here to see you sir.' The lady pauses for a second before ending the conversation to the person on the other end with 'Ok.... I'll send him in.' Bob gets up from the seat without being told and walks over to the door which has Jeremy Simpson written on it. Bob turns the door handle and enters the room, Tony and the thug both follow and enter the room before the thug closes the door. Jeremy looks to be in his mid 30's as he sits behind his desk looking up at the three large men who entered his room. Bob can tell he is intimidated by him this will make things a lot easier for Bob. The man extends his arm signaling for Bob to sit in one of the two chairs positioned on the other side of the desk. Bob and Tony both sit down and look accross at Jeremy. 'Ah I will get you another chair.' Jeremy reaches for his phone but Bob places his hand over Jeremy's. 'Don't worry about it.... He will stand.' Bob smiles at Jeremy who is looking rather frightened after making contact with Bob Vurdo's rough hand. The thug moves over to the corner of the desk on the way to the exit. Even a simple move like this has Jeremy even more nervous, he begins wondering why the man is standing on his path to the exit of the room. 'Lets get down to business.' Jeremy's attention snaps back to Bob Vurdo's cold face on the other side of the desk. He notices the bruises on his cheek and under his eye obviously from a fight. 'Ok,' Jeremy says nervously looking around at the three men who have their eyes fixed on him. 'I want to take over your KFC in Amber Hill.' Bob's doesn't take his eyes of Jeremy for a second as he knows it is intimidating him even more. 'I'm sorry but the Amber Hill KFC is beginning to bring in a lot of profit for me.' Bob shakes his head and looks at the man for a second. 'You haven't heard my offer yet.' The man gets even more nervous as Bob says this. He breaks eye contact with Bob and begins looking down at his papers nervously. 'I know as a millionaire you run into a lot of problems with people trying to get the better of you.... I am prepared to offer you protection from any threat to any of your business's and you yourself.' 'I don't need protection.' 'Yes you do.... Believe me you need protection.' Jeremy knows exactly what Bob is talking about now. 'How much money is there on offer as well?' He is going to have to give it up but it may as well be for as much money as he can get. Jeremy Simpson doesn't want to do especially not now. 'Fifteen thousand dollars.' 'Alright.... I'll take it.' Bob smiles at the man before putting his hand on his shoulder. 'Where do I sign?'
