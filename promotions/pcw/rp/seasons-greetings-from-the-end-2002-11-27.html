Seasons Greetings From The End

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.11.02 A mild pop comes up from the crowd, but for the most part indifference greets The End and Sandy Wong as they walk out to his Powerman 5000 entrace theme, 'Bombshell.' Dave: The End lasted to, appropriately enough, the end of the Alliances Main Event, outlasting 56 other men to get a #3 ranking in the match, shared with Rush Mason. Jeff: And they were eliminated by the combined might of the Front Office and Chris Matthews and Caleb McGlocklan. Rush Mason jobbed to Xavier Kannon and the members of his own former federation. Rush jobbed to RUSH! Duke: RUSH is crap and we never shoulda picked it up. Homegrown PCW talent beats out RUSH by leaps and bounds. Dave: Duke, that's the first time in a long time you've shilled our product and our wrestlers in the same thought! Duke: *Shrugs* Meh. The End comes out with his sword over his shoulder strapped to his back with a red sheath. Sandy Wong is carrying a mailbag. The End gets a microphone from a tech and starts talking. The End: Season's greetings! Or rather... He starts over. The End: Good evening, Western consumers! Some pops for Nerezza's new catchphrase. The End: You all probably wanted to hear that from somebody, and ol' Nacosto back there isn't exactly in the best of moods right now; I didn't want you to get disappointed. Anyway, it's getting to the holiday season, as of tonight we're just one day away! Cheers for the holiday season. The End: You know, the family and togetherness, the spirit of giving and whatnot... More cheers for the holiday season. The End: And I thought, PCW is exactly like a family. Love/hate relationships abound, we're all trying to show up one another. So with that in mind, what better to do for family getting this close to Christmas than give them some presents, right? To the wrestlers, the crew... the fans. I already gave Boca his freedom and safety from the Celestials killing him, and I released a flock of turkeys into the wild with devices to protect themselves. *Cut to the uptown area of the city, where turkeys with Raven .25-caliber semi-automatic pistols and AK-47 rifles are running amok in Bloomingdale's.* Fans pop for PRESENTS! Sandy Wong is nodding her head with The End's speech. The End: So let's get the ball rolling to what I like to call -- The End's Christmas Wish Show! Sandy, if you would please read out some letters for everybody. Sandy Wong gives a word of affirmation and reaches into the bag, taking out one note and reading it. Sandy: '1. A 12-foot hoagie. 2. A bag of Doritos. 3. Another bag of doritos and hoagie. Signed, The LEGEND of PETE!' The fans POP for PETE! Dave: Pete's name got a rise out of them! Jeff: Losers. Duke: Even for a scared pacifist he'd kick your ass. Jeff: Falling on me doesn't count. Sandy Wong takes out another letter and reads it aloud. Sandy: '1. Wear G.O.D. merchandise for an entire show. This includes shirts, hats, gloves, bandanas, pants, and anything else available. 2. Let G.O.D. play with your sword! 3. Massage... This one has no signature but I think it's from G.O.D.' Lukewarm response. Duke: RUSH = SHIT Jeff: You're supposed to hype it because Xavier Kannon bought it. HYPE RUSH, FUCKER! *SMACKAFUCKER!* Jeff: *Cries* Sandy: 'Abner, PCW's favorite ex-con/redneck/cult leader, would love to indulge his corroding liver in a six-pack of Bud!!!!!' The End: Beer and food. Not much for wishes but if that's what they want, fine. What next? Wong pulls out a letter but doesn't read past the sender. Sandy: It's from Bobby Stormbringer and his handler. '1. To be allowed to post on the ew-247 boards again without being harrassed by 'Greedy Game Players' as either A??? D?????? and/or R.M.S. [[fan wish]] (one isn't made, FYI.) 2. To have an Active PCW Roster Spot as an IC-WRESTLER and retain FULL CREATIVE CONTROL while under an OOC WT banner of operations. [[character wish]] (No evil wishes by others against me. heheh. Other than VP-C, of course...) 3. To be managed by 'Controversial' Corey Williams until the day I die. [[handler wish, prays to L. *RON* Hubbard.]]' Shaking his head, The End passes the letter off to someone backstage. The End: Um... Bobby, simple stuff. The first one I had no idea what you were talking about. The second wish; well, you're talking to the wrong guy anyway. Brandi is the Personnel Director, not me. Corey Williams? You want to be managed by him? Laughs erupt from the crowd. The End: I haven't been able to locate him. Sorry, that's one letter I can't help. Sandy, what's next? Sandy takes out one more letter. Sandy: 'I am a GREAT LUCHADOR WARRIOR, but my mask is very plain. A simple blue mask, it's like a hood. I want something more flashy, more dynamic, more fitting of a GREAT LUCHADOR WARRIOR. Something that will give me....' Sandy stops. Sandy: Then it goes on and on like that for another ten pages. It's signed Stimpo. The End: Well, that's enough for today. So, let's bring everyone out here. Come on, guys. Bobby Stormbringer, G.O.D., Abner, Stimpo, and PETE! come out onto the stage. Stormbringer, for having been killed by so may people so many times on PCW television, is a rotting corpse. G.O.D. walked out in a towel just in case he was going to get that massage. Abner is just wearing his jeans and red flannel. Stimpo wears his ring tights and his blue mask. PETE! walks out acting like a scared animal. He has on tightly fitting jeans and his barbeque sauce-covered shirt wrap around his flab and manboobs for all to see. Sandy Wong: The stuff is backstage. The End tells everybody to wait for a moment. He goes to the back and brings out a box of goodies. First up is Bobby Stormbringer. The End: Bobby, here's your present. Reaching into the box, The End pulls out a Bible and cross and hands them to Stormbringer. Stormbringer puts his hands around the gifts but drops them to the floor. Stormbringer: IT BURNS! The End: *Shrugs* Well, start praying, because I'm sorry but I can't help you. Stormbringer leaves the stage. Next up is Stimpo. The End: Stimpo, you said your mask was too plain, basically. Well, I had Sandy talk to the tailors and whip up something for you. Hope ya like it. The End rummages around in the box and pulls out a mask. It is dark blue with white lightning streaks going down the face. Devil horns crown the top, and there is real black hair attached to it that would reach down to Stimpo's shoulders. The mouth is open but covers the chin, and a mouthful of 'demon fangs' are set on the outside. The eyes are covered with a see-through white fabric and more lightning designs shoot from the sides of the eyes. The mask comes with a full matching chest armor, complete with shoulder pads that are crowned with real stainless steel spikes, matching spiked gauntlets, and a matching cape. Stimpo puts on the present and spins around, the cape flowing behind him. He jumps up and down in excitement. The End: I take it you like your present? Stimpo nods and runs off down the apron slapping high fives with the ringside fans, circling the ring before coming back up the ramp and going to the back. The End: Who's next? Abner? Abner walks up, expecting the six-pack. The End goes into the box and pulls out a case of Budweiser. There's a note attached to it. The End: You asked for a six-pack, I got you a case. That should hold you for the moment. And look at the paper. That's a voucher good for a LIFETIME'S SUPPLY OF FREE BEER. Abner's eyes and mouth go wide open like he won the lottery. He takes the case and runs off, ecstatic. The End: So... PETE! and G.O.D. are left, huh? G.O.D. nods and PETE! is slowly shrinking away and getting closer to the backstage area, but Sandy pulls him back. The End: You'll get your presents backstage, but I won't be escorting you to them. They will. Hit the music! The Fanta music starts up. Sandy: Fanta's not the official sports drink of PCW, Gatorade is. The End: Yeah, so I changed things around a little. 'Want Gatorade? Don'tcha want some? Want Gatorade? Don'tcha want some?' The chorus confuses people at first but they ignore it once the newly hired Gatorade Girls walk out. Sandy: That's what you did yesterday... The End: *Shrugs* Wasn't much to rent them to Gatorade to use this one night. Sandy: Does Skitzo know? The End: I sent him a check for my being involved in Gatorade this one segment. Backstage, Skitzo shakes his fist. Skitzo: I didn't see one cent of that money! Skitzo chimes in, whacking Skitzo's fist down with his pimp cane. Skitzo: You didn't earn it in the Alamo Match, biatch! Skitzo: Who you callin' a biatch? GIMME MY MONEY! Skitzo interjects, fixing his fur coat and adjusting his purple leather hat with white cheetah line over the top and a yellow feather in the cap. Skitzo: Whoa, whoa, whoa. Chill ya'll. Let's just sit back and enjoy the Gatorade Girls. All the Skitzos look at the room full of naked Gatorade Girls in front of them and nod and shut up. G.O.D. looks at the Gatorade Girls. G.O.D.: They're wearing... my merchandise! The End: Yep. A two-for-one special. I'd let ya use my sword, but it kinda electrocutes anyone other than me who tries to wield it. Now, follow the Gatorade Girls to the back and they'll massage any part of you that you ask. G.O.D.: *Uncharacteristic exclamation* Sweet! PETE! is a little afraid of the women, but The End coaches him through it. The End: PETE!, just follow the Gatorade Girls and they'll take you to an all-you-can-eat buffet catered by every restaurant in a 50-mile radius. PETE! cheers up and goes with the girls, licking his lips. The End and Sandy Wong are left alone on the stage now. Sandy has a mic. Sandy Wong: It was nice granting Christmas Wishes, we should do this more often. The End: We will, every show, in fact. But we're still not done. Sandy Wong: We're not? The End: No. What about the rest of the Gatorade Girls? The music kicks up again and 100 Gatorade Girls walk down and surround the ring and line the stage. Jimmy Kimmell & Adam Corolla: JUGGIES! Jeff: No, NIPPAGE~! The End: Girls, you know what to do! The Gatorade Girls flock backstage. 4 of them walk up to Dave and Jeff and say they'll be waiting backstage. Duke just gives the women by him a blank stare, but there seemed to be some message conveyed in it that they just walked away. The End: Every PCW wrestler backstage is getting Gatorade Girls. Except for the women, they already have Gatorade Guys waiting backstage for them. Sandy: Wow, you thought of everything. The End: Nah, because I don't know what they really want, that's just an earlier promise I made that I wanted to keep. But if you've got a reasonable Christmas Wish, send it to [detail withheld], or if I'm online with Sandy on one of the PCW webcasts then send it to AIM screen name [detail withheld]. You can send me a wish if you're a current PCW member, a past PCW member (but please make sure you left on good enough terms that your being here won't get you shot and myself fired), or any of the new guys from RUSH. Sandy: We're running short, they wanna get to the next match. The End: Well, that's my time, I'm out! Bye! The End's 'Bombshell' music hits the speakers and the crowd gives him a decent pop, a little more warmed up to him now.
