Back Where We Belong

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-6-03 Finally we made it back to the US and back home. Paris was great and boy was it ever life changing. I would have never thought I would have did something like that ever before. But, I summed up all my courage and asked. 'Tessa, will you marry me?' The reaction was more then anything I could have ever hoped for. Yet all good things must come to an end and our time in Paris had to come to a close. There was only a few days left before the big rematch against P.W.A. Tessa and I had just finished unpacking our stuff but we still needed to pick up the kids from Jackson Rust. However Tessa and I was in no big rush. Jackson could survive with the kids a little longer. After all, Tessa and I put up with them everyday of the week. They maybe a handful but you can't help but love them. I just fell down on the couch and let out a sigh in relief. I let the back of my head hit the sofa and simply closed my eyes. I suddenly felt a weight on my chest. I opened my eyes to see one half of the PCW Tag team titles resting on my chest. I looked up to see Tessa standing behind me with her title in her hands. She was smiling down at me. 'Don't forget we have to defend these against P.W.A. I doubt they will be so easy this time.' she told me. He grabbed my title and held it out to look at. The gold was nice and shined, I could see my reflection in it. I turned around on the sofa to where I could look at her better. With a confident smile, 'No worries Tessa. We beat them once before. P.W.A. can not fully work as a team. They know they can't beat us.' Tessa didn't seem so sure though as she held her title closer to her. 'How can you be so sure? They have to be upset that we won.' 'P.W.A. has been pulling pranks on us because they are trying to split us up. To get us to fight each other. If they have to use such under handed tactics then they must know they can't beat us. After all, if they was sure they could mop the floor with us then they wouldn't be so worried. We have their back against the wall this time. We are the champions, not them.' I said. Tessa smiled at me and kissed my forehead. I watched as she walked off toward the bath room. I slipped back down onto the sofa and turned toward the television. With nothing else to do I turned the TV on. There wasn't much on. However something caught my eye. Nothing I really should be interested now, but it was a commerical for a new strip club that was openning up in town. Now I wasn't interested in the strip club itself. Rather what the commerical said. 'Men, are you looking for a good time? The Gentleman's Club is what you are looking for. We're new to town and we are openning up with a huge event. So if you are having a party, or a Bachelor's party, or you just want to come and hang out, then come to the Gentleman's Club this Saturday for our big openning day bash. All the most beautiful women in the area will be performing live for your enjoyment. Fountain drinks are only $2 and mixed drinks are only $5. This is a one time only party. Be there!' I had completely forgotten that I was going to have a Bachelor's party! I need to get everything set up for it. Who am I going to invite? Where am I going to have it? What....a thought hit me. I don't have that many friends I can really invite. Not to mention I gave up that wild life long ago. Hmm, this could be a problem. I need to make some contacts and get stuff roleplaying. I got up from the sofa and walked towards Tessa and I's room. I went straight to my dresser and pulled out my address book. Although I threw out my little black book of numbers, I still had a book for friends and other such people. Not to mention a special list of people who owed me a favor. I spotted the name I was looking for. If there was one person that could have a slamming party it was him. No one knew parties like this man. After all, with a nick name like the Disco King of 1979 and Groozy Toozy Tom, what else can you expect from the man called Laguna? Laguna has been a friend of mine for a long time. We go way back. Although back then I woudn't admit he was my friend. But he's been there through thick and thin and I know he would be the master to handle my Bachelor party. I picked up the phone and gave him a rang. Sadly enough I had forgotten that he was in the military and was still searching for a wanted bandit across the US. Something about a 'Deathsycthe Outlaw' or something. I guess I would just have to wait then. Damn just my luck huh? Oh well, how hard can be setting up one little part be after all? I'll just call Rust and get everything cleared away with him. But I will deal with that in due time. After all, I still need to worry about two big matches. P.W.A. and Dorn Tracer. Defeating P.W.A. ensures I keep gold around my waist. Defeating Dorn Tracer gets me a spot in the Rumble for March Madness. I believe there was mention of the World title with that match. A smile came to my face. 'World title huh? I think it's about time I added a little singles gold to my waist. I wonder if anyone has ever held both Tag Team and World title here. I might just have to be the first man to ever do it.' I laughed to myself. Visions of glory danced in my head. I could defeat P.W.A. and Dorn. I knew it, there was just a matter of actually doing it. Well I'm finally back home. I'm where I belong. Now the hard part comes. Planning the wedding and the parties. Trying to manage all this while keeping a successful wrestling career and being a father to three children. It won't be easy but then again, I never back down from a challenge. And this is my biggest challenge ever.
