Making babies.... or breaking up marriages?

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 When we last left off…. Flaymin and his ‘assistant’ Jenna were in the midst of a counselling session with infamous Australian ‘psychologist’- Robbo the shiela-trouble solver. The ‘heart-stopping’ promo reached a thrilling ‘climax’ when Robbo suggested that Flaymin and Jenna have a baby in order to bring the couple closer together. Will Flaymin and Jenna go through with the ‘One-minute wham-bam-thankyou ma’am jungle monkey love’ session? [Scene opens outside ‘Robbo’s Shiela-Trouble solving joint’. Flaymin and Jenna walk out of the rotting hut and begin walking back to the main road to find a passing truck that will take them back to the city. Flaymin walks along the street quietly, keeping to himself. Upset with Flaymin’s silence Jenna begins to speak] Jenna: Well? Flaymin: Well what? Jenna: About what Robbo said…. You know about having a baby. Flaymin: I’m not sure.Is that what you want? Jenna: Yeah why not? Flaymin: Think of all the pain when you’re in labor. Jenna: Pfff.I’m as loose as The Grand Canyon.That baby will slip out like a marble through a basketball hoop. And don’t get me started on the whole breast-feeding thing. [Jenna pushes together her plump breasts to demonstrate for Flaymin their sheer size and grandeur. Flaymin looks at the enormous pair of breasts and smiles] Flaymin: And think of all the time you’d have to spend raising it…. and feeding it…. and pooping it…. and playing with it…. And what about when you’re on set shooting pornos? [Jenna looks at Flaymin and scowls] Jenna: You don’t want this baby do you? Flaymin: Uh… [Jenna launches a full-scale hissy fit and begins to yell and wave her arms around in anger] Jenna: Dammit, Robbo said this would bring us together!!Robbo said a baby was the key to our future!!Robbo said…. Flaymin: Robbo wipes his ass with empty beer bottles.Are you going to do that too? No wait…. You’d probably end up sticking the bottle up your ass instead.The pleasure never stops for Jenna!! Jenna: And being an asshole never ends for Flaymin!! Flaymin: I guess we have something in common after all! Jenna: We’re skating on thin ice here….. reeeeeaaaal thin ice. [Flaymin looks around at the remote, dusty desert that surrounds them both] Flaymin: How on earth did you come up with ice? All I see are dead cows, drunken 10 yearolds and a never-ending dustbowl. Jenna: We came here for couples counselling.I guess it didn’t work. [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: We gave it a shot. Jenna: Well maybe we should give a baby a shot. [Flaymin gives Jenna a strange stare and raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: You wanna kill a baby? Jenna: What? Flaymin: You know…. Blam!-Blam!Aaaarrrggghhhh!!!! [Flaymin dives onto the floor and attempts to mocks a dead person…. But ends up looking like a hobo humping the ground] Jenna: Why do you have to be so unsupportive? [Flaymin gets off the ground and dusts off all the sand] Flaymin: Me?Unsupportive? You’re the one that was f*cking El Diablo’s wife backstage at Fusion. The least you could have done is invited me to join! Jenna: Angel understands me! Flaymin: And El Diablo is a jackass.Out of all the people in PCW, you had to get freaky with the wife of my arch nemesis. [Flaymin suddenly pauses and begins to stroke his chin in an evil manner] Flaymin: Hold on a sec…. That’s probably a good thing. Maybe they’ll get divorced!That’ll tear his little heart apart.It’ll make him vulnerable.It’ll make him a loser. I’ll be able to rip his midget Mexican ass to shreds! Jenna: You want me to break El Diablo and Angel up? Flaymin: Um…. Yeah, yeah, that sounds like an interesting proposition. That’s what I want…. that’s exactly what I want.Just go out there and do your thing.Don’t stop until their divorced! Beating El Diablo in the ring is one thing- destroying his personal life is on a whole other level! Jenna: Isn’t this… wrong? Flaymin: You love Angel with a passion.I hate El Diablo with a passion. Together we can achieve our ultimate fantasies: Love…. And utter humiliation. [Jenna looks deep into Flaymin’s eyes. They look at each other in silence for almost a minute.Analyzing the situation.Analyzing Flaymin’s dastardly proposal.Analyzing their future… together….. or with Angel…… [Scene fades to black] What happens next? Tune in for future episodes of the Wonderful and Whacky Adventures of Flaymin!
