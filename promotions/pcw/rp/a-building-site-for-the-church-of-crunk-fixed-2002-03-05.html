A Building Site for the Church of Crunk(FIXED)

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/5/02 March Madness was promoted as the biggest PPV of the year in PCW, perhaps one of the biggest in PCW history. A weaker than usual under card was made up for by the two fabulous main event matches. First, The End SWERVE~! ed Drake Maxwell which allowed Jeremy Howard to not only retain his Continental Title, but also to be re-united, after three years, with his brother Justin Howard. Then, it was time for the big kahuna. Finally, the time had come to see the Brawl For It All. The brawl was action-packed, certainly the best PCW match this year so far. The final five men in the match, not a very unexpected list: Snow, Chris Chase, Miya, Mexi, and the new PCW Champion, Boda. However, a very unexpected name lay at number six on that list. One that might’ve been on an obituary list a few hours earlier: The Rush, Rush Mason. The Crunk as Hell God made his triumphant return to PCW TV after being M.I.A for almost a week, after a kidnapping and a very strange following sequence of events. This means that a new chapter may have just opened in the PTC wrestling career of the Rush, with what looks like a new place in PCW with a match against Xavier Kannon and The End, two of PCW’s top new wrestlers, on Sunday Sabotage. We, will, however see if he can live up to the hype. The scene opens up with a shot of an old, run-down building. In a good neighborhood, this building stands out like a sensible thought in the rants of The End, or a true sentence from the mouth of Xavier Kannon. The windows and front door are boarded up, with paint falling off on every side of the old building. Strangely enough, however, there is a small group of fancily dressed people standing in front of the building, seemingly inspected it. The camera pans in and one of the figures that can be identified is The Rush. The 6th finisher in the March Madness main event is standing amongst a whole bunch of white men in suits and ties. Hopefully, they haven’t kidnapped him like those other crazy white guys. Rush is wearing a dark blue Mecca t-shirt with baby blue writing that says Mecca on the front, a very interesting pair of baggy baby blue leather pants, a white North Carolina baseball hat with a baby blue do rag underneath, and a pair of black and red Nike Shox. Rush: So, Larry, what do you think of their asking price? Do you think they’re trying to rip me off because I’m black and we should bust caps in their asses? Three white men gulp, and try to fake smiles at Rush, thinking that they must be very careful not to be killed. Rush begins to laugh out loud, and the three men followed with very nervous laughs, though they have no idea what they’re laughing at. Rush: I’m kidding. Jesus, guys take a joke. Well, I’m not expecting a barrel of fun from three guys named Trent, Victor, and Lawrence, but you could show a little enthusiasm. Get excited like I am! I placed 6th in the main event at March Madness! Larry: Well Rush, as for the offer, I’d say it’s a little high on their part. This property really isn’t worth their asking price. However, with what we’re going to do, we can still make a large profit if we sell the land after fixing it up! Rush: Larry, Larry, Larry. I told you we’re not selling the land, this is the sight of the first Church of Crunk Larry: Rush, I already did explain that it’s not a very financially sound move, starting this Church of yours. I mean, we’re talking about losing tens of thousands of dollars if you fail. Rush: Don’t worry about it. There’s no way that Crunk can fail as a religion. Just look at the Mormon religion, it was started by a revolutionary like myself. Where is it today? Larry: It’s in Utah, where they don’t allow music, and fun itself is outlawed pretty much. Rush: Bad example, anyway, the Church of Crunk will not fail, because it has the full support of PCW behind it. Larry: Who exactly is backing your Church? Because I would sure like to see their signature on this check instead of yours. Rush: You’ll see in due time. For now, just help me out with this transaction so that we can get started faster. Larry, shaking his head, walks around with Rush, around to the side of the house. Rush stands, inspecting, and turns to Larry, who is doing some calculating on a small legal pad. Rush: Stop all that science shit. You remind me of Xavier Kannon when you do that stuff. Larry: Sir, may I interject a thought, with the assurance that you will not become mad at me? Rush: Go ahead, I’m in such a good mood ever since Sunday. Larry: I was also your financial manager when yourself and Mr. Kannon were the tag team champions of NTICW, right before Mr. Yoder was forced to shut down and your largest paychecks for NTICW and the most endorsements were coming during that period. Rush: That’s uhhh….because I helped Kannon get over as a heel. Besides, it’s not that I don’t like the guy; Kannon is crunk in my book. Ellie, however, is another issue entirely. That crazy little white girl thinks that she pinned me?! Have you ever heard a more blasphemous thing in your life Larry? Larry: Sir, I don’t know if you remember the fact that you were involved in NTICW’s Playboy Mansion Match. Ms. Hall was also a member, and she pinned you on a roll-up. Rush: Hmm… I don’t seem to remember any thing of the sort. Larry, you should really get some sleep, you seem to be hallucinating. Larry shakes his head, not surprised at Rush’s denial of the fact that RUSH JOBBED TO ELLIE~! ELLIE~! I SAY! However, Larry also remembers the fact that KANNON JOBBED TO CARMOONZ~! CARMOONZ~! I SAY! However, he says nothing, with Rush being in a good mood being such a rarity, with all of the “blasphemy” he has to deal with. Rush: Larry, could you do me a favor? Call a press conference, telling people of the new sight of the Church of Crunk. Also you can just sign the sheets to buy this building, I’ll sign later. Larry is going to remind Rush that he is a financial manager, and not an agent, but the thought would be wasted on the cocky Crunk as Hell God, so he just gets onto his cell phone with Rush’s agent, walking away towards the front of the house and the three sellers. Rush turns back towards the house and begins to talk to himself. Rush: Well, it looks as though the Church of Crunk can continue its pursuit of worldwide recognition. I can’t wait to do some preachin’ like I did to the Amish with my three singin’ ladies. I feel like breaking out in a Negro spiritual! Out of nowhere, a guy in a white shirt pops into being right next to Rush. Guy: I’m from the PTC, and we would like if you referred to our African friends as African-ancestral-American citizens of color. The guy then goes back into the void from wherever he came from as Rush has a confused look on his face. Rush: Let’s just go to that press conference. XxXxXx 1 Hour Later… XxXxXx The scene opens up once again after a short break, and Rush is at a podium, much like his preaching to the Amish, however this time instead of lots of white people who have no clue what they’re doing and think Rush is just a crazy black guy is well… lots of white people who have no clue what they’re doing and think Rush is just a crazy black guy. You can’t win all the time, I guess. Rush stands at the podium, and he steps up the media quiets down and a few cameras begin to flash on the scene. Rush: We will hunt down the evildoers, and we’ll smoke them out of their rabbit holes, then we’ll smoke ‘em back in it. No that’s not what I meant. This, ladies and gentleman, is the sight of the first Church of Crunk, right here in Greenville, North Carolina, the place where I was born and raised, and the place where I still live. Well, now that’s out of the way, are there any questions? A male reporter and a female reporter both raise their hands and Rush calls on the woman first, and she stands up, revealing a very nice set of breasts and she’s fantasstic, if you know what we mean. Female Reporter: Do you really believe that a Church revolving around you and only you will be successful? Rush: Well, I think this titty…err city has needed some religious guidance for many years. I think that I’m the breast…umm…best man for this job, and this church is the most impressive chest I’ve ever seen…I mean, this church is the most important thing to come to this city since I was born. Rush smiles, trying to ignore the fact that almost everyone noticed his three instances of referring to the reporter’s chest cavity. The man reporter stands up, strangely glum for a day that should be celebrated, at least a little bit. Male Reporter: Sir, I know that you must be, as well as I am, extremely saddened by the death of the Rush in a tragic car accident. I was personally close with Rush and I have just been devastated since the terrible day. Rush has no real way to respond to this question, but he looks closely at the face, and it seems somehow familiar. Rush: Someone grab that man and pull off all of his fake features on his face. I wanna see who that doofus is that dares say that the Crunk as Hell God has died, especially from a mortal wound in a simple car accident. The man is now involved in one of those little circles of dust you see in cartoons, and arms and legs are all over. When it’s over, a tired looking Phoenix Rose Jr. can be seen sitting on the ground. Rush: It’s you?! I thought you were stuck in Malaysia for that little terrorist incident? Or did you get out on an insanity plea? Phoenix: No, but I was the first male to ever go to an all female jail, and I escaped by making friends with the butch lesbian guard. Rush: Fantastic, now get your punk ass out of MY press conference. There’s no douche bags allowed. He is thrown down a manhole, by a strategically placed large black man, who then jumps down after him, yelling something about tossing a salad, anyway back to the press conference. There is one last question from the crowd and Rush calls on the tall reporter, who actually has a real question. Male Reporter: It will probably cost a lot of money to full re-furnish this building and do everything to fix it up? Are you willing to make that sacrifice for your church? Rush thinks about this question for a minute, then replies into the microphone, as pens scrawl on small pads. Rush: Who said anything about fixing this building? Rush then grins like a moron across his entire face, and all of the white people look at him with that look of “Okay, well the black guy just made his first dumb joke, now can we go back to calling them coloreds?” However, Rush continues to grin like an idiot, nothing can spoil his manner for now, but we’ll have to see if that can continue until his match on Sunday, so the scene, escaping from stupid jokes, fades to black…
