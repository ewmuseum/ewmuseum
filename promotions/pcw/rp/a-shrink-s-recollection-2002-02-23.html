A shrink's recollection...

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-2 His one hour session was up but yet I was wanting for him to stay. This monster of a man intrigued me. But he had other things to do, so he said. He lifted his massive bulk of muscles off the leather couch and walked towards the hard wood door. Just before walking out he looked over his shoulder. “Next week Doc?” Knowing what was coming, same thing week after week, I replied. “Next week, Rip.” He then opened the door and walked out closing it behind him. Another session in the books, but yet I felt we could have went even deeper. Dug more into his mind trying to find what plagued him so, to cause him to act and treat people the way he did. Looking down at my notes, most of which were replicated from the week before. I think back to the first time we met. Six months ago almost to the day, but still I hadn’t gotten to the root of his problems. If anything I haven’t gotten anything out of him other than stories from the previous week. That first day he was just as he was today, calm and always trying to walk around my questions. Many people may think that he wasn’t smart. But he’s a lot more intelligent then anybody gives him credit for, a lot more. I was told I was an idiot for even taking the man as a patient. A few of my current patient’s even requested to see a different doctor. But I couldn’t just turn the young man trapped in the body of a beast away. He intrigued me and I wanted to get to what was inside his head. He didn’t intend to tell me anything at all. His intentions where nothing more than to just sit there and listen to be babble on about what ever I wished to talk about. For the first month the only thing I knew about him I heard from his probation officer. He court ordered him into therapy and put a restraining order against him, as that he couldn’t go anywhere near a certain young lady. A young woman he was obsessed with. After the first few weeks after his first initial visit he began to open up. And would answer some of the simplest questions, but for allowing me to dig any deeper, he just wouldn’t allow it. We had made a pact after the first three weeks that he would talk about what was going on in his life now. But not about anything from his childhood or even memories of anyone in his childhood including his parents. The young woman he was obsessed with and ordered to stay away from was named Emily. He told me they met at a local strip club. Him a loyal customer and her a waitress. He said what attracted her to him was the way she carried herself. As if she were royalty in a room full of vagrants. He should have known how she was by that, he had said. But they soon began to talk and he came back night after night to just see her. He laughed when he told me he was probably the only guy in the strip club that came to see a clothed woman. This was the only time I could recall a smile coming over his face.Everything was going great they even began seeing each other away from the club. They meet for dinner or a cup of coffee sometimes. Then one night she invited him back to her place. He said he was reluctant at first. He hadn’t ever trusted anyone before… he wouldn’t go any deeper into that subject when I pushed he seemed to get upset. So I left it at that. He then said that things began to tumble downward when he seen her at the bar one night flirting with another customer. He didn’t go into detail about what had happened. Just that after that night he was banned from the club. So he began waiting in the parking lot for her to get off work. But she ignored him as if he never existed. He tried calling her at home but she had her number changed. Then one night he went followed her home from work. That’s the night he says all h*ll broke out. He said that the whole time at the house was a blur and he couldn’t remember exactly what had happened. But by the time he knew anything he was sitting in the back of a police car handcuffed. And she was lying on a stretcher being wheeled into the back of an ambulance. He was booked and thrown into the slammer. Three days later he was charged with aggravated assault and given ninety days and a hefty fine. As well as the restraining order and therapy with me. I finally had gotten out of him why he was here with me every week, and it had only been two months since he started seeing me. I could tell then that this man was going to be one of my biggest patients, not only physically but as a patient as well. All of that was four months ago. Since then we’ve talked about his hopes and dreams for the future. What he felt he needed to do to accomplish those goals. Any new people he had met or came into contact with. And what he felt about his continued visits with me. That pretty much covers the next four months of his visits. But today all of that had changed. And I’m certain that this change isn’t a step forward… He arrived on time as usual. For a man that didn’t own a watch he kept excellent time, at least when he was scheduled to see me. Perhaps it was the fact I got paid by the hour. He walked in with a sly smirk on his face, an expression of emotion. Something he hadn’t shown before when he had first arrived. He always walked in slowly as if he regretted that he had to be here again. But today he stood tall and sure of himself. Something else I wasn’t sure I had ever seen in him before. He looked as though he had been shopping. He wore a new pair of Nike sneakers and loose fitting denim jeans. His shirt was one of the same kind he’d wore every week since he met him, a white wife-beater shirt that showed the detail in his massive chest, and allowed his massive arms to hang free. He walked right in and then proceeded right to the leather couch and laid down as he spoke. “How’s it going Doc?” I was shuffling papers getting ready for our session so I had replied without looking up. “Fine and you?” “Great Doc.” At this I dropped what I was doing and looked up at him. “Great.” “Great.” He replied. I was caught off guard he’d never sounded so chipper before. I knew something had happened. But what? I rose from behind my desk taking a notepad and pen along with me. I walked around and sat in the chair that laid just a few feet from where he was lying. From the very first day he always seemed comfortable laying on his back on the couch. Most of my patients didn’t like it, it seemed to make them feel uncomfortable. Not this man, I have the impression he could relax anywhere. “What’s going on?” I asked. “The usual.” Was his reply. “Nothing new?” I knew that something had happened. Just from the way he was acting this day. Acting differently then he had since he’s been seeing me. “Maybe a few things.” He always did this. It was if he wanted me to dig and have to work at getting straight answers. It was like a game we played. And he was rather good. “So you want to tell me a few of these things that are new?” “Perhaps.” He said this with a smirk on his face. By now most ordinary people would blow him off or get pissed and say something they shouldn’t. But being in my position I had to find away around his childish games. “How about you tell me about something new then? What ever just pops into your mind.” He sat there in silence for a few moments deciding which of his few things he should or should not tell me. “I got work.” This please me… at first. “What sort of work?” “I’m a professional wrestler.” I was rocked by his statement. A man such as him should not be in any sort of situation where violence could erupt. I as any one else couldn’t predict how he would react. “What do you think about this?” He began to open up after this question. Even telling me something I did not know. “Nothing really… I get to step into the ring and hurt people… I won’t get into trouble with the law for doing it… That’s about it.” Great he’s going to be enjoying this line of work. “Aren’t you afraid of getting hurt?” A smirk came over his face. “Afraid… you think I’m a p*ssy Doc?” A tone of anger rose in his voice. “No.” I responded quickly. “Not at all.” “Then what are you getting at?” He shifted as to get a better look at me. This was a moment I should back up and regroup on. “It just occurred to me that I don’t like pain. I thought maybe you felt the same.” Let him think I was thinking of myself and not him. “Pain… Doc pain don’t matter much to me.” His or someone else’s I thought. “Anything else new?” I thought I should change the subject for awhile. At least until I could think of a better line of questioning. “Now that you ask, yeah one other thing.” He leaned back on the couch and placed his hands behind his head. “What’s that?” I asked. Not knowing what sort of answer I would receive. At this point a look of utmost enjoyment came over his face. A grin as evil as any I’ve ever seen. I knew now I was seeing the man they called Ripper… “I found another…” he seemed to growl as he spoke. Another? Another what? Does he already have one? I was confused at this point. “Would you like to indulge further?” He turned his head and looked at me. “Indulge… yes I’d like to indulge…” He turned his head back away and towards the ceiling. I sat and waited for him to go on with his another story. I felt a chill go up my spine and the dryness in my throat as he began his strange tale. A tale that chilled me to the bone, it would be a while before I got another word in. I just sat there and listened. “I knew who he was before… before I had made the phone call. When I called the first time nobody answered. But I knew he was home the light was on in the living room. I knew where he lived cause I had followed him from work earlier in the night. A second time I called and then he picked up… I told him I wanted to meet him. At first he was reluctant but something changed his mind. I’m still unsure of what that something was. But he agreed to meet me. I told him in an hour at a park across town. This thought seemed to put fear into his voice. The fear was what I was looking for. Once he left his house I waited for ten minutes to be sure as that I thought he might return for something. But he didn’t. I made my way over to his yard and walked up onto his porch. The screen door was partway open. As I pulled it back and twisted the knob to his door I found he had forgotten to lock it on his hasty exit. Once inside I looked around to get a feeling for the room. After which I walked to the kitchen and opened his fridge. I took out a bottle of beer and went back to the living room. There I waited for him to return…” At this point I interrupted him and asked if he needed anything. He said no but I got up and poured myself a glass of water anyway. I needed it. This wasn’t something I had planned for. I thought that this meeting would be like so many before. But it wasn’t… I returned to my seat and asked him to go on. He was reluctant at first as if he was wondering if he should continue. But he did… continue… “Once he returned home I had a buzz going. I had drunk at least eighteen beers by then. I wasn’t finished but he had interrupted me. We had a small chat and then I had left. It was as simple as that. But that one chat had put the fear into him. He knew I sounded familiar but he didn’t exactly know where he had met me before. I could tell that much. The next day I followed him again…” I stopped him and asked. “Again?” “I had followed him the two days before. Getting use to his schedule you know.” He then continued with his tale. “He ended up at the main offices of PCW… that’s the place we both work.” I scribbled a few notes as to not forget he mentioned that he worked with the guy. “While he was inside I bought a paper to read, I normally don’t read the paper. It’s full of crazy people you know.” This coming from a guy that refers to himself as the psychotic freak, a name the girl he’s not allowed to go near called him in court, ever since he’s referred to himself as the same or as just THE FREAK. He continued. “Once he reappeared outside he was nervous and agitated. As he walked down the steps and onto the sidewalk I placed my hand on his shoulder. He seemed to know exactly who it was. His fears were realized as he turned to face me. Again we had a small chat, this time he told me he was going away for awhile. Vacation he called it. At this point I left knowing I had to do something for him before he left. I went and bought a going away card and a black pen to write with. Inside the card I wrote him a message to let him know I’d be thinking of him while he was away. I then took the card to his house. This time he door wasn’t unlocked. But when I walked around the house I found it rather ease to push the back door open with my shoulder and putting my weight into it. I then took the card to the living room and placed it by his remote. He was sure to find it there. I then left it was getting late. I’m sure he’d want to rest when he got home. I walked across the street and waiting in an old abandoned house. When he finally came home I waited but the urge to know if he found he card was to great. I had to find out. I walked across the street carefully as to not be heard or seen. I made my way up to his porch to peer into a window. But I couldn’t see. I only had one thing I could do. I would have to wait until our next meeting. So I turned and left.” He then looked back over at me as if waiting for a response. I looked him in the eyes and was unsure of what to say or do. “You know that you could get into trouble for what you did?” I wasn’t sure why I asked this. He set up and looked at me with a sneer. For the first time I was afraid of this man. “Doc… your not going to tell on me… are YOU?” He raised the level of his tone. Which sent a chill through out my body, almost sending me out of my chair. “No, of course not.” He set back down and seemed to be under control again. I found out for the first time what could set this man off, betrayal. I realized for the first time that I was probably the only person in this entire world he had any sort of trust in. Possibly ever, other than that poor girl, who he had thought crossed him. And look at what he had done to her. It didn’t really matter much if I wanted to warn somebody or not. I couldn’t he had patient doctor privileges. Which in turn meant I had to keep my mouth closed whether I liked it or not. Unless I wanted to lose my license that was. “I’m happy to hear that, Doc.” He said with a seriousness in his voice that I hadn’t heard often before. With that he stood and at first I jumped back a bit. I knew that he knew he put his so called fear into me at that moment. But only he knew if I had any reason in being afraid. He turned as if heading towards the door. I stopped him. “Where you going?” He turned and pointed at the clock. “Hours up.” “Let’s just talk a few more minutes.” I was trying to get him to stay. I needed to be sure that he hadn’t done anything he wasn’t telling me about. “Sorry Doc… but I gots things I gots to do.” He said before he turned for the door again. I had rerun the entire hour with him back in my head. It had been forty minutes since he walked out the door. I was wondering if I would see him again next week. Or if he’d be in a slammer by then, I had a strange feeling he was stalking this poor guy for some reason. Just what that reasoning was I was unsure. If there’s one thing I’ve learned about Ripper since he’s been coming to my office. He doesn’t do anything without good reasoning. It may not be good reasoning to anybody else, but to him it’s good reasoning…
