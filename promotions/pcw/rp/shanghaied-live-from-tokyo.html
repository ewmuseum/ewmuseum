Date: not entered “Oi… It’ll be fine. We’re almost there.” Ukyo Matsuura sat in the window seat in the cabin of the airplane, looking nonchalantly out the window with a bored expression. He was in a black tank top with a white jacket over and black slacks with stripes down the sides, white gloves tapping against his chin as he propped his head on his elbow. “Just a hour or so and we’re in Shanghaied. Don’t sweat it, baby girl.” Sitting next to him was Naomi Kawaii, also known to the public as Miss Moonlight. She sat rigidly in her seat in a rainbow tube top and yellow overalls, her hands gripping the arms of the seat tightly. Her eyes were focused straight-ahead, teeth clenched. “Yeah…” she said through her clenched teeth stiffly. “It’s… all… good…” Suddenly she grabbed the airsick bag in her lap and heaved into it. Ukyo glanced at her, sighing as he adjusted the two tone black and white framed specs on the bridge of his nose. “Man… why can’t you be like ‘Sho?” Next to Naomi, Basho Amuro sat wearing a white sweatshirt and blue jeans. Why should Naomi be like Basho? Basho was knocked out cold. “S-summan…” Naomi apologized in Japanese as she wiped her mouth and leaned back. “I don’t do well with planes.” “Yeah I noticed.” Ukyo said as his brow wrinkled with irritation. “Who’d think the most high flying chick in PCW gets airsick. You ought’a be ashamed of yourself.” Naomi apologized again before leaning over and puking again. Sighing again, Ukyo rubbed his temple with his eyes closed. Suddenly the captain came over the speakers. “Attention passengers, we’re dealing with a bit of complications, so we’re going to have to make an unscheduled stop for a few hours while we check things out.” “Complications??” Naomi said, her eyes getting wider. “What kinda’ complications?” “Don’t worry ‘bout it.” Ukyo muttered. “Just be glad you’re getting off the plane for an hour.” “We’ll be landing in Tokyo, Japan in about 30 minutes.” Ukyo shifted in his seat, folding his arms across his chest and trying to ignore Naomi’s whimpering. Suddenly, his blue eyes flashed open, a surprised look on his face. “AWWW SHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIT~!!!!” Title: ”Shanghaied in Japan” Subtitle: ”Tokyo Turmoil” “Sir, you’re going to have to keep it down. Others are trying to sleep.” Basho grumbled as he scooted onto his side and continued sleeping. Ukyo, on the other hand, was by far no way as peaceful. “We CAN’T stop in Tokyo! That’s just NO GOOD!!” he protested loudly in the cabin. The flight attendant tried to calm him down, but she had a hard time doing so. “We don’t have a choice. There’s technical difficulties that we have to check out before continuing the flight.” “TECHNICAL DIFFICULTIES?” Naomi gulped as she clutched her airsick back tightly. “Man, screw THAT! Do you have any idea what’ll happen to us if we step off this plane?” Ukyo said as he pointed down to the plane figuratively. “The three of us are DEAD if we step off this plane in Tokyo! Do you have any idea what they’ll do to us when word gets out we’re back in Japan??” “No, not really.” The stewardess said scratching her blonde head. “Are you like, famous or something?” Saying something like that to a guy like Ukyo is committing ego homocide. He dropped his head and exhaled deeply, looking back up at her with weak eyes. “Something like that…” She clasped her hands together as she smiled at him. “Wow! Really? Can I have your autograph?” she pulled out the airsick back in front of Basho’s seat and handed it to him along with a pen. That ego homicide was thwarted as Ukyo grinned and signed the bag. Hey… a few kind words and you had yourself a friend with ‘Kyo. “There ya go, cutie.” He flashed her a suave smile as he passed it back to her. “U…kyo… Matsu… ura…” she said aloud. Then she looked back up at him, cocking her head to the side. “Uhm… so what band are you in?” “Band?” he responded questioningly. “I’m no rock star. I’ma wrestler!” “Oh…” she shoved the bag back in his hands and walked away. “I don’t care much for wrestling…” Ukyo’s jaw dropped as he watched her leave. Naomi suddenly snatched the bag from his hands and threw up into it. Ukyo’s eyebrow twitched as she sighed and handed the bag back to him, leaning back in her seat weakly. “Man…” he muttered, holding a bag of puke in his hands. “This is some ol’ BULLSHIT!” “SIR! Please be QUIET!!” -=-=-=- Naomi stood with her black backpack on rubbing her stomach lightly as the sounds of yelling and struggle came closer from a distance. She didn’t look up as airport security came walking out of the terminal with Ukyo in their arms. They dumped him on the floor and walked back down the terminal, locking the door just as Ukyo ran back after them. “Shit!!” he growled, turning around rubbing his rear. “Damn goons. Oi, baby girl…” he said as he walked over to Naomi. “Where’s ‘Sho?” “I don’t know,” she responded, still rubbing her stomach. “I think he got off before us. I didn’t see him.” “That’s just great. You feelin’ any better?” She shook her head. “I’m still nauseous.” He sighed, looking around the airport. So far, no one had paid much attention to them past the whole security fiasco, but they only took him as a disgruntled passenger or something. “C’mon,” he said as he took her by the wrist. “Let’s get you something to sic on that sickness.” Ukyo and Naomi walked out onto the city streets and headed for the nearest shop they could find. Ukyo was having a hard time trying to be inconspicuous, his piercings and unique look almost a dead giveaway to those familiar with him. A number of people stared at him, pointed and whispered as he shoved through the crowds. Naomi covered her mouth as they pushed through the people and ducked into an alley. “Damn it…” Ukyo said huffing. “I’ma get spotted if I spend more than another minute out there! How come you ain’t gettin’ no heat?” “’Cause… no one’s seen me before without my Miss Moonlight mask on.” She said matter-of-factly. “I’m safe to walk around here… I guess.” That statement alone was enough for Ukyo to flip her off with a finger to the corner of his lips. “You suck.” He sat his knapsack on the ground and ruffled through. “What’re you doing?” she said, kneeling down in front of him slowly. Her face was flushed as she tried to keep her sickness under control. She watched him pull out a bandana and a pair of sunglasses, pulling out his piercings and shoving them into the bag. “If I’ma get outta this unscathed…” he tied the white scarf around his head and slid on the black sunglasses. “I gotta go incognito…” -=-=-=- “Hi, can I help you?” Ukyo and Naomi stood close to each other, trying not to look inconspicuous. Well… Naomi was just looking sick in that convenience store; Ukyo was the one looking around suspiciously. “Uh… yeah…” he said warily. “Me pal here’s got a bit of the sickness, and she needs something to blow out that nausea she’s got. Hook me up, kind sir.” And with the biggest grin he could paste on his head, he officially creeped the shopkeeper out. “Uh… sure kid…” he eyed him before walking over behind the counter. He came back with a small blue box and passed it to them. Naomi took the box and started to open it as the man rang the price up. “That’ll be 850 yen.” Ukyo smacked his forehead. No yen, dumbass. He pulled out his wallet and handed him a credit card. The shopkeeper looked at his card, and then looked up at him with an eye cocked on his wrinkled face. “Uh… can I see an ID please?” Ukyo’s eyes rolled slightly behind his sunglasses as he passed him his ID. The old man scratched his bald head before looking back up at Ukyo. The photo on the ID showed Ukyo sticking his tongue out and flipping the camera off at the corner of his mouth. He looked back up at this person, watching him shift nervously as his female friend popped two pills in her mouth. “Uh… you didn’t fake these did you?” “Huh?” he asked taken off guard. “Well it says here you’re Ukyo Matsuura,” saying the name caused a few customers to look in his direction. “But you don’t look like him… much…” That raised a few eyebrows from both the shopkeeper and the customers, starting to move in closer. Being the Ukyo “ICE COLD” Matsuura he’s always been, he played it cool… “WHAT?? UKYO MATSUURA?? THAT BACKSTABBING SON-BITCH CHARLATAN? HOW DARE YOU SAY I LOOK LIKE THAT DASHING ROGUE?! HAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” “Well…” a lady customer came up, pointing to the ID and looking closely. “You do favor him. You both have those pouting lips and sharp eyebrows.” A younger girl came up and started being nosy as well. “And you’re kinda cute… like him…” “You DO kinda resemble that punk some.” The shopkeeper said gruffly. “PUNK?!” Ukyo growled, holding his fist up. “Who you callin’ a punk gramps?! I’M THE HYPER-MAX SUPERSTAR YA SHITHEAD!!” Naomi shook her head as Ukyo realized his slip, his angered face changing to surprise as he looked around at the staring faces. He slowly looked down at Naomi… and after snatching back his cards they broke off running out of the store. -=-=-=- Ukyo and Naomi piled into the backseat of a taxi and slammed the door, the driver taking off into the streets. “Where to?” he asked glancing back in the mirror. “Uh, the airport,” Naomi said a bit relaxed. Those pills must’ve been working about now. Ukyo sat his bag in his lap and ripped off his sunglasses. “Man, that was HELLA close!” he took off his bandana and shook his hair to get his spiky wild hair back in place again. “How’d they recognize me?” Naomi was about to respond, but she decided not to and slumped down in her seat. The driver looked back, watching the youngster hunched over in his seat with his hands to his face. After a couple minutes, Ukyo leaned back up with his eyebrow piercing, his lip piercing, and all his ear studs back in. “Glad we’re on our way back. Then we can get on the plane and—“ The tires screeched to a halt in the middle of the road, sending Ukyo and Naomi jerking forward. “OI!! WHAT’S THE PROBLEM, IDIOT?!” The driver turned around glaring at Ukyo. “Get out of my cab, you punk!! I know who you are, Matsuura!” “Aw shiiiiiit!” begrudgingly, he climbed out and slammed the door behind him. “You go on ahead, I’ll—“ the driver sped off, leaving Ukyo in a cloud of exhaust. “How DARE he get in MY cab?” the driver said angrily. “You ought’a watch the company you keep miss,” he said glancing in the window back at her. “You never know what kinda parasite’ll attach themselves to you.” Naomi stared ahead, and after a minute nodded. “Uh… yes sir.” -=-=-=- Back at the airport, Naomi waited at the gates for Basho and Ukyo. The call for all passengers had came, but she’d seen neither of the Mega Soaring Arrows. By her face, you could tell she was starting to get worried. But you should always put your faith in the MSA to come out on top. Just wait ‘till Shanghaied… “YOU BASTAARRRRD!!!” All of a suddenly Ukyo came speeding around the corner dashing for the gate. Behind him a huge Japanese man slid across the ground trying to give chase, along with a small girl in a black dress trying to keep up with the both of them. Ukyo turned around and snickered at the huge muscular man. “Aw man, why can’t you give me a break?! It’s been a rough day… just ask your girl how rough it was!” “DAMN YOU!!” the man picked himself and started chasing after him again, sending Ukyo fleeing yet again. Naomi sighed as Ukyo vaulted over a set of chairs that the man chasing him stumbled over. He jogged up to her puffing and grinned at her. “Nice to see you’ve kept out of trouble,” Naomi said tired and sarcastically. Ukyo shrugged his shoulders. “There’s two things ladies love that describe me, and that’s bad boys and big dic---“ “MATSUURA!!!” the muscular man stood back up and pointed at him. “YOU’RE DEAD!!” “Screw THAT!!” Ukyo handed his ticket to the attendant and ran through the terminal with Naomi. The man tried to go after him, but the attendant and security held him back because he was sans-ticket. All the man could do was watch Ukyo turn back and flip him off cackling in that raspy annoying voice of his. -=-=-=- “Attention ladies and gentlemen, we’ll be arriving in Shanghai in about fifteen minutes. Please prepare your things, and we hope you had a nice flight. Naomi and Ukyo sat alone on the plane… some reason Basho didn’t make it. That really set off the two on a glum note, Naomi’s frown sunken onto her face. “What happened to ‘Sho?” Naomi whimpered almost childlike. Ukyo shook his head and smiled weakly. “Don’t sweat it, he’s tough. He’ll find a way to get to Shanghai.” He got up and passed by her to go to the aisle. “Watch, he’ll turn up when you least expect it. Ukyo walked to the back of the cabin and knocked on one of the bathroom doors. “Oi, buddy! You’ve been in there for a while! I gotta use!” he said annoyed. “You know if you’re having trouble, the handle says FLUSH genius!” The toilet flushed, and Ukyo gave a sarcastic clap. “Boy, aren’t YOU intellectual?” The door opened, and Ukyo stepped back as Basho stepped out of the stall. He yawned, stretching his arms out wide with a drowsy look on his face. “…’Sho?” Basho looked down at him, and smacked him down on his forehead. Then he walked past him to his seat, where Naomi was surprised as well, a high pitched gasp coming from her. Ukyo just stared at him, his pierced eyebrow twitching in his direction. “Man… that’s just the biggest load of BULLSHIT I’ve EVER SEEN!!” “SIR Please be QUIET!!”
