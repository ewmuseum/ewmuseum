The Horror..... The Horror...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/07/02 (RP#2 for FF) Xavier Kannon’s revenge know no bounds.... Last time we saw the Scientologist and his Preppy young wife Ellie~! they were setting off for Ancient Egypt in order to convert Rammesses III to Scientology to spite the Pharaoh in revenge for being pinned at the hands of Zen Dula... An event that if completed would radically alter the World today as we know it... ... Will the duo succeed with their dastardly and possibly World ending mission? Or will the fact he’s taking the whiny and easily bored Eleanor along with him come back to haunt him.... Something he may not have to worry about if a certain chain of occurrences happen at the impressionable stage in time.... “I know that Science wasn’t my best subject... Or Maths, of French, or History.... But isn’t Time Travel a bit dangerous?” We rejoin the 1st couple of PCW as they trek across the sands of Ancient Egypt towards the great Pyramids in their quest to convert Rammesses III to Scientology thus send existence crashing down all around them.... A rather large price for getting revenge on the Pharaoh. Eleanor : “And these SO weren’t the right clothes to wear for this...” She looks down at her attire of White lab-coat, Pink roll-neck jumper and knee length Brown suede skirt before she closes her eyes and looks like she is concentrating hard... Screwing her eyes up as in a blink of an eye her clothes change to head dress, White silk shirt and Khaki cargo pants. Eleanor : “That’s better...” The Scientologist looks at his broadly grinning valet with a glare of disbelief. Xavier : “..........” The prep picks up on this and explains, saying it very casually. Eleanor : “Well, by thinking that I wanted to change my clothes here millions and billions of years ago then my present self back in Hubbardville will remember it and change into something better suited before she jumped into the time hole to become me.... Or something like that..” As she details her reasoning, Xavier’s jaw slowly drops at the thought of Ellie~!, admittedly hardly the brightest star in the sky working something like that out. Xavier : “How do you DO that?!?!?” The Preppy wife of the Scientologist just shrugs and continues on her trek to the Pyramids. Eleanor : “It just comes to me!” Kannon takes a moment and shakes the disbelief out of his head before catching up to his wife, the Pyramids of Giza looming large on the horizon in front of them. Eleanor : “So.... Is it dangerous?” Kannon : “Well.. Hard to know until we go back and see if you changing clothes started a cataclysmic chain of events that send a cascade of time and space distortion into our dimension and tear the fabric of our world apart.... I think Hubbard may have written a book about it...” In typical Eleanor Hall fashion, her Preppy mind goes from understanding all about time and space kinetics to pure bewilderment again. Eleanor : “Was that a Yes..... Or a No.... And is that a Yes it’s dangerous, or Yes it isn’t dangerous?” Xavier : “I think as long as we stay here and don’t do anything it’s safe.... But as you have shown our thoughts here are capable of altering the world around us...” Ellie~! again looks confused but decides to just do what she usually does.... Nod and look pretty. Eleanor : “Ahhhhhhhh... So a Yes then....” The 1st couple of Scientology continue walking through the desert... All around them dunes of White send stretch out to where they meet the bright Blue horizon. Still, they just walk.... Nothing to attract their eye apart from the giant structures before them... Until eventually it happens.... Ellie~! gets bored... Eleanor : “So.........” She looks around, trying to look for something to be the topic of her conversation before the words in her mouth escape the small barrier put up by her brain and slip out. Eleanor : “... That Bodyguard really kicked you ass huh?” Beside her Kannon suddenly stops walking, his bright Green eyes growing wide as in his head he can see only one thing.... His concentration so strong that in his most Scientological of brains the thoughts arrange themselves in such a manner that time and space bend and distort and in one blinding flash the couple disappear. All that is left are there foot prints in the sand..... Which suddenly stop where they were whisked away through time. As we zoom away from the prints a Camel comes walking over the Dunes and looks down at the trail with no end with his big Black eyes.. Camel : “Odd.” As the Ship of the Desert walks away we cut to the secret underground Research Laboratory back in Hubbardville where alarms are going off and Scientists are running around almost aimlessly, well, more to give a sense of panic. “THEY’RE NOT THERE ANYMORE!!!!” “WHAT DO YOU MEAN THEY AREN’T THERE ANYMORE?!?!!?” “ONE MINUTE THEY WERE ON COURSE FOR THE PYRAMIDS THEN THEY JUST WENT OFF THE SCOPE!!!!” “ISN’T THAT THEM THERE?” “NO... THAT’S A CAMEL!” “FIND THEM!” Fading out of the Research Lab 500 feet below the surface of Hubbardville, Wisconsin where the Scientists funded by Scientology carry out there experiments into time and space we see pure White... Blindingly bright light. “Wow... I think we ended up in the Sun.. It’s colder than I thought it would be...” “Nice one my little Poon Puppy...” “Hey, I SO didn’t, like, get us here!” “You made me think of...... Oh Hubbard.... We’re here....” Zooming out from the bright lights we see Xavier and Ellie~! on the main lighting rig of the Vaught Hemmingway Stadium in Oxford, Mississippi where below then the arena is packed to capacity.... Xavier : Oh no.... This is now technically an ARENA RP.... Hubbard forgive me!” Eleanor : “Hey... Isn’t that you?” In the ring below we see none other than the Scientologist himself.... Being hoisted up into the air by a 7 foot Egyptian giant... Eleanor : “Whoa... You were SO thinking about jobbing to Zen and now we are here.... That, like, sucks!” The noise in the ring is drowned out by the dull roaring of the crowd who are cheering wildly as Kannon is slammed into the mat and Zen drops down to cover. Xavier : “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!” Eleanor : “Wow... He’s like SO pinned you twice now!” Xavier : “SHUT UP MY LITTLE FUCK FERRET!!!” Eleanor : “Well TOUCHEYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!” Xavier : “Gha-ARGHHHHHHHH!!!!!!” As he bangs his hands onto the lighting rig he slips and falls, plummeting towards the ring to the disbelief of the crowd. Eleanor : “If Zen, like, pins him after he lands that’ll SO be 3 times!” As the Scientologist plummets to the ring in Owen Hart fashion we hear the voices of the Scientists... “I FOUND THEM....” “WHERE?” “MISSISSIPPI!” “WHERE WAS THAT IN 132 BC?” “NO.. THEY ARE THERE LAST TUESDAY!” “THEN GET THEM THE HUBBARD OUT OF THERE!” “DOING SO!!!” Cutting back to Kannon falling to the ring his plummeting form is suddenly engulfed in raw energy and his completely vanishes. Eleanor : “Hey, like... WHAT ABOUT ME!?!?!” Right on cue the Prep becomes engulfed in raw Black and Purple energy that wraps around her and flickers. Eleanor : “Like, they SO need to get hat in Pink and Silv-” Before she can finish whining she vanished in a flash of light and we cut back to the Research Lab where the Scientists are crowded around the Black and Purple hole in the floor... “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!” They all hop back as Kannon is ejected from the hole and flies 10 feet up in the air before he crashes down on the floor. “Like... SO AYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!!” Just as he lands, Ellie~! is expelled from the time and space vortex and flies up higher than Kannon before dropping down onto him as the shot starts to fade out and the Scientists flock around them. “Are you all right?” “Gold Patron Meritorious... Where did you go?” “What did you see?” Kannon looks at them, his wide Green eyes full of fear... His body shaking and his bottom lip quivering as we hit Black.. “The Horror..... The Horror.....”
