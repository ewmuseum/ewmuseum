A Reflection

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 - 23 - 03 So Jonathan Drachen has emerged from within the ashes of a recently reborn RUSH Wrestling federation. Like the great Phoenix bird, Jon Drachen has been reborn from a wasted pile of human filth, now leading a new life with a new purpose in mind. That purpose, however… Is to bug the living fuck out of me. I look at it like this: Jonathan Drachen is a failure to the 10th degree. He failed to live up to any sort of feasible achievement in RUSH, his pizza business failed because of his maniacal demeanor, and he’s going to fail to defeat me in PCW. Not to be arrogant or cocky, it’s just what my feelings tell me. The bright lights above my head fuel my very emotions, and they don’t steer me wrong. But… why would they tell me Drachen is going to lose? It’s all too simple. You just have to look at the bare facts in front of your face. Let’s recap: Jonathan Drachen entered RUSH with quite a resume. He was an XWA Tag Champion, and an XWA TV Champion. And he was still relatively new. That’s something to be admired… except for the fact that XWA was a rundown shit house that nobodies competed at. He probably had to beat some 100 pound kid that was just looking to live some cocaine-induced dream. Wow, what a victory. But yet, it got him into RUSH. That’s when the real fun started. Almost immediately, he started to butt heads with RUSH’s premier superstar in Cassius Deamont. The egos of the two morons began to form some rather heated friction, and soon, blows were exchanged. It looked as though it would be a 1 on 1 bout, until the likes of Chris Matthews got involved. Chris Matthews was hated by the crowd just as Cassius Deamont was. So one has to believe they would team up to take out the fan favorite in Jon Drachen. Ol’ Pizza Queen didn’t even see it coming. That shows how stupid he is. He figured it’d be a triangle feud with no teaming up, just a straight 1 on 1 on 1 affair. Oh yeah, I’d love to see the diploma for his college education. It’s all too evident that Matthews and Deamont would team up to rid RUSH of the likes of Drachen. They even went as far as to create a stable with Tim Walden, called the Solution. The Solution proceeded to cause Drachen mortal pain and agony over the next couple of shows, even ripping away his World Title shots when they came to him through pity. He was never able to capture the title, get a noticed win over Deamont, or really accomplish anything at all. His credentials got him absolutely no where and he ended up in the nut house because of the mental blitzkrieg that Deamont and Matthews pulled on him. So now, he’s legally insane and in PCW. If he can get into PCW, then just about anyone can. He’s a no talent ass kisser who lost his marbles, both physically and mentally, all those weeks ago. The effects are still profound and he still seems to be having these episodes when his RUSH tenure is brought about. So what better to way to exterminate a cockroach than a roach motel? Just take the very thing that he fears most, and crush him beneath my boot. I’ve already brought about the initial event that led to the whole debacle, now it’s time to bring about the rest of the nightmare. But it surely won’t get rid of him. I will have to find something else to get rid of Drachen… something about his past… something that left him so scarred that he would never think of doing it again if it was up to him… But what?………....Of course. Yes, I just thought of something. Something that would KILL Drachen, both inside and out. A beautiful idea. Thank the stars. The time passes quickly, and there is much to be done. Jonathan Drachen, prepare to relive your misbegotten RUSH days. To the death. [End.]
