Quiet Contemplation 2: Not so quiet

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/03 “Surely this is a sign” Lacuna grumbled to herself as her slender feet caressed the soft grass of the lawn leading up to her overly large house in America. “I may be opening Spring Fling, but I’m moving up the card.” She was quite elated at her chance of facing one of the bigger names on the Primetime Championship Wrestling roster, and the deep patch of though had passed by a little. Now it was time for her to be joyous and celebrate a little. Maybe she would even have a drink or two. Usually it was Jade who brought on these little fits of happiness, but recently Jade and Lacuna had separated a little. The reason was unbeknown to Lacuna, but they were two very separate people and sometimes it was better that way. She nodded to the groundskeeper, Hamish, who was sitting on the porch of his equipment shed, basking in the glorious sunshine the day had presented them with. He was a tall man and well built with it, clearly of Scottish origin as his name suggests. He had a very square face and well cropped ginger hair. Lacuna liked Hamish, and he was one of the very small minority of her workforce that were not actually English. “Good day ta yer, Ma’am” he announced in broad Scottish tones as she came within earshot of his booming voice. Lacuna stopped and beamed back a smile at the huge man “Good day, Hamish. I trust things are well?” “Aye, that they be. And how might you be on this wonderful day?” he inquired, surprising Lacuna somewhat as she was hardly expecting a conversation from the man. Her workforce barely even acknowledge her presence through fear. This was one of the reasons she liked Hamish so. “I am somewhat elated, to be honest Hamish, things seem to be looking up in the profession of professional wrestling. I am being placed against people of a higher calibre than Jackson and Hilton, and when they are decimated after the PPV then finally Significant Others will gain the respect they deserve. Maybe even the tag titles alongside it. This is my launch pad, Hamish. Its quite exciting really.” Throughout Lacuna’s somewhat extensive ranting, Hamish simply nods and smiles back encouragingly. There was little point in ever telling Lacuna to keep her feet on the ground because her moods changed too quickly for the statement to ever be taken in the correct context. Lacuna was beaming a brilliant smile now; she was purely ecstatic about the possibility of moving up the card a little, even if she was reading far too deeply into it. After all, it was a character trait of hers. “Now all ya’s gots ta do is go out and win it, lass” there was no hint of insult in his voice, just the simple tone of reassurance. Hamish enjoyed seeing Lacuna in such a mood, as it was a brilliant distraction from her every day avoidance of humanity or general stupidity, which she seems to alternate between daily. “You just leave that up to me and enjoy your sunshine. Take a day off, you deserve it” The shock on Hamish’s face was enough to inform that it was a very rare occurrence when lacuna gave anyone a day of, let alone insinuated that they ever deserved anything over than a kick in the balls. “Thank you Ma’am, have yerself a good day on me, eh?” “I will, goodbye Hamish” Lacuna smiled as she continued on up the grassy slope up towards her goliath of a house on the hill. Now she felt good. She felt damn good. In fact, she felt bloody brilliant. She wanted to do something reckless and blow a ton of money on something she would never use like a waffle making shop or an electricity powered hammer company or something. Maybe she would buy a speedboat and put it in the duck pond. That would be a right royal lark, wouldn’t it? As she approached the house, she saw the present jade had brought her to try and get here to lighten up a little, and now seemed like the perfect chance to destroy it. The silver Mercedes convertible lay parked in the stone driveway, the key left carelessly in the ignition. There had never been any need for it to be removed because nobody ever got into her grounds. There was nearly no chance of doing so while escaping with your ass anyway. “Now this will be fun…” she muttered to herself as she vaulted over the door of the sleek vehicle, landing softly on her ass in the driving seat. Her right arm instantly shot out and began examining the CD collection stored in the car for something to get the adrenaline pumping. “Nevermore… Jade’s” she tosses the CD onto the gravel where it lands with a scratching shatter “Disturbed… Jade’s. System of a Down? Urgh, Jade’s…” A huge grin spread across her face as she saw the title of the CD she held in her hand. It was a compilation; one of the early Kerrang! albums she purchased in ‘94 back in England. Disc two was one of the greatest discs ever made and on it was Lacuna’s favourite song of all time… Steppenwolf’s ‘Born to be Wild’. She tugged the CD from its broken case and thrust it eagerly into the CD player, skipping through the first 13 tracks. Damn she loved this song! It epitomised everything she ever wanted to be! The memorable opening riffs sparked up as the engine roared under the extreme revs Lacuna was pumping in as she floored the accelerator, belting off down the gravel driveway at ferocious speed, spraying a large amount of the small stones around as the tires tore it up, looking for any small amount of grip they could find. As the car roared around a turn, the gates opened slowly, just fast enough however to let Lacuna through without ruining the side of the attractive convertible and she burst out onto the thin country roads with a roaring engine and blasting speakers. “Yeah God I’m gonna make it happen, take the world in a love embrace, fire all of your guns at once and explode into space!” she roared along with the music, banging her head to the infectious riffs as the car sped along the largely straight country lane, the hedgerows on either side of the car blurring into one constant green smudge as the car continued to belt down the road. Then a thought occurred to her; a ranger dangerous thought that not only excited her, but also fuelled the adrenaline already pumping into her stomach. She knew all the farms in the area were owned by the same farmer, and were linked together by eroded tracks in the mud. “Now -this- will be fun” A sly grin sped across her face, and the car turned a sharp right, straight up a ludicrously bumpy trodden mud pathway… ------ Mr. Baker was a farmer. He had recently purchased the land from his cousin and had moved up from Cornwall to run the much larger estate. He was a peaceful man, and he had no quarrels with any one. Today, he was happily ploughing the land, dragging the plough on the back of his high-powered tractor around one of his smaller fields. Today was one of the reasons Mr. Baker wanted to be a farmer; the sun shone down, baking the land and ravishing the crops in the heat they needed to grow successfully. As he drove, he whistled merrily. All was well in the life of a farmer. Then the roaring began. Mr. Baker raised his head, adjusting the glasses that were slipping down the end of his slightly hooked nose and pushing up the flat cap adorning his head. It was odd to see such a cloud of dust forming too, like some sort of storm was forming. The roaring was getting louder, and he edged the tractor towards the road. What was approaching was a silver convertible, and it was slowing to meat him. “Well I’ll be darned…” muttered the farmer as the car approached and stopped to reveal its driver, a tanned woman, thin of features and pitch black of hair who smiled as the car pulled to a stop. “Well what’ll ye be wantin’ then m’lady?” he was being cautious of the woman, who did not even turn her head to meet him, rather to raise her right hand from the wheel, slowly extending her middle finger. “Well I’ll be damned…” and he pressed down on the accelerator. ----- Lacuna wondered what was coming over her. Just because Jade had brought her this car didn’t mean she had to become her did it? She saw the tractor approaching and pressed down on the accelerator hard, but all that happened was that the wheels spun wildly on the loose dust coating the road, creating a large cloud of dust, yet no forwards movement. The tractor was pulling alongside her as she suddenly realised that the only way to get the car to move would be to start it slowly and work upwards, and so she did, edging away slowly, being outpaced by the high-powered tractor. “So you want a race do you?” she called out as she grabbed a pair of purple lensed sunglasses from the dashboard and began to pick up speed down the linking roads. The roads were badly rutted from the heavy wheels of the tractor, which gripped on tightly to surface and made steady progress compared to Lacuna, who was bumping around the road like she was on a bouncy castle; the shiny sports tires not even trying to hold the road as the back end of the car swung around ludicrously down the road. She then realised quite how pathetic the situation was. She was in a sports car, and a tractor was gaining on her. So she floored it. The back end spun out, the wheels skidded and she jarred the wheel hard right, spinning the car round one hundred and eighty degrees, but just wide enough to avoid the encroaching tractor and allowing her room to go home. That was enough excitement and/or law breaking for one day, now its time for some sleep. And she went home to bed, albeit very quickly.
