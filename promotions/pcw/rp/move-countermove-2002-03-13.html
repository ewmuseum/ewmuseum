Move/Countermove.

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.13.02 “1… 2… 3… Dodge… 5… 6… Step back… 8… 9… Attack!” A tall but thin man in a sterling white suit (complete with white derby) and the blackest of sunglasses, lunges with malicious glee, swinging a knotted cane with precision accuracy! We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as the cane smashes into the side of his head. Big Alan Steele: (rubbing temple) “Ouch! You shall pay marginally for such a weak and ineffective attack upon my person!” The grinning man in white steps back and straightens his bow tie, his smile fading into consternation. Though one can’t see it, the man’s beady eyes are looking around, drinking in the environment of downtown San Jose. The steady ring of the alarm system of the bank he robbed has alerted the police, who have begun circling in on the two duelists. The street has become barren, but many civilians peek out of doorways and windows watching Big Alan Steele fight this new, nimble menace. Man: “I, Chronos, master of ALL TIME, have to admit, though I have calculated your fighting techniques down to the very mannerisms with which you attack, I have underestimated your strength and toughness in fending off my direct hits!” BAS: (growling) “Once I get my hands upon you…” Chronos: “But you can’t! I have studied your movements completely and know your weaknesses! I have timed this whole escape down to the very SECOND! No one can stop Chronos, master of ALL TIME!” BAS: (standing straight, cracking knuckles) “Fool! You cannot escape the meaty grip of justice as it closes in around your neck! Look, as even now the street ways are closed off to you by the SJPD! And it has been proven that you will not be able to harm me, Big Alan Steele, son of Zeus! Tis only a matter of time before you succumb.” Chronos: (laughing maniacally) “Only a matter of time?! Sorry, Big Alan STOOGE, but time is something that shall remain on MY SIDE!! BWAHAHAHAHA!” Chronos reaches into a pocket and pulls out a transmitting device. Still laughing, he holds it aloft and stabs at the button… A cop car racing to the scene freezes. A bird in flight hangs in midair. Faces are frozen in expression. A police officer can be seen, donut raised to his lips, but not eating! Clearly, all time has stopped!! The man in white squeals in delight and begins dancing around in a victory dance. BAS: (wry) “An impressive toy. But it shall help you for naught!” The man stops dancing, a look of complete shock on his face. His jaw drops low and stammers as he tries to make sense of Big Al not being frozen in time. Chronos: “B-b-but how did you? Why aren’t you? How can you… ?!” Steele steps forwards, hands on his hips in a dramatic pose, laughing heartily amongst the frozen scene. BAS: (grinning) “Though you have stolen the technology from Dr. Willow that allows you to separate the various layers of the space-time continuum with the positronic nexus capacitor, thereby allowing you to isolate moments in time, I too have paid a visit to Dr. Willow, who hath supplied me with this noble device which allows me to be tapped into your own time current from your own time nexus!! Hi-Keeba!” The wiry man gives a snarling smile, sarcastic claps in his white-gloved hands. Chronos: “Very impressive, Big ASS Steele. BUT, in doing so, you have fallen into a trap! Though I admit to stealing the nexus, I am as great a genius as he, and your tagging along on my time wormhole ride has resulted in the summoning of caveman assassins from the FUTURE, which I have made a bargain with that should any disturbance in my continuum be felt in the FUTURE, they shall dispatch immediately, reigning all manner of futuristic caveman death capacities upon you! (checking his un-ticking watch for effect) In fact, I believe they’ll be here right about… NOW!!” A ripple appears over the smug head of Big Alan Steele, and a small and oh-so precious kitten materializes, dropping into his outstretched arms. The kitty purrs, as Big Al strokes it gently. BAS: “No so fast, Master of LIES. Your dealings with future races duly noted, we had anticipated such a state of affairs and set up a jamming device on yon Lord of Pain…” Big Al gestures to a prone, broken, and bloody Sado-Masochist, frozen in a half smile as he soaks in his endorphin kick. BAS: “… that would change the coordinates of your cave hit men and sending them to the Victorian era, whereby they would be mocked roundly for their poor etiquette and ill manner of dress!” Chronos: “And their panic button that would bring forth an army of dinosaurs from the Jurassic period to wreck havoc upon your house and home?” BAS: “Indeed! Twas figured out in the nick of time, and relegated to sending this small feline from the Clinton White House, Socks, to be sent here in their stead…” Steele tosses the cat high into the air, and it explodes with great force, the concussive blast whipping around still air and smoke! Chronos: (grumbling) “And I see you knew that I would booby trap that as well.” BAS: (thumbs-up) “Hi-Keeba!” Chronos: “Well, Big Alan DOPE, I must say you have proven quite the nuisance! But I have other tricks up my sleeve that you shall find most…” BAS: “You mean such as the positronic bi-location device stored on your belt?” Chronos: (crimson with fury) “Yeah?! What of it?!?!” BAS: (suppressing a giggle) “Oh, nothing. You should try it. May work, you know…” Chronos: “You dare mock CHRONOS, Master of all TIME?!” BAS: “Not at all.” Chronos: “Don’t patronize me!! Take this! BWAHAHAHAHA!!” The man in white slaps at the button on his belt, and a small parachute accompanied by a small firework unfurls, a message dangling to the ground: ‘Hi-Keeba.’ The belt starts playing a corny and lifeless version of the Olympic Theme Song. Steele puts a hand over his mouth to stifle a laugh as the fuming Chronos sputters in rage… (Hours later, though time has stopped, so really, no time later, but in THEIR reality, hours pass, you know?) The two combatants sit at a small café table on a nearby side street, waiters frozen in time, ducking for cover. Steele drinks amiably at a soda while Chronos pours over a sheaf of paper, busily writing with a pencil while intermittently taking sips of hot tea. Chronos: “Okay, did you find out about the concept time drag machine in which the device creates a extremely dense portion of antimatter, thus bending the time coefficients and allowing the wearer to travel along a slower path, thusly greatly increasing his speed in relation to the normal time line?” BAS: “Yeah, twas located in the bedroom closet, correct? Well, the polarity twas changed, thus creating a dense matter, which would suck the space-time continuum for the wearer backwards, creating a SLOWING effect. You would be in my grasp within seconds. More tea?” Chronos: “Yeah. The service is terrible here – I feel like I’ve been waiting for service forever!” The two engage in light laughter, Big Al standing and pointing towards Chronos with a ‘good one’ gesture. He heads to the café for more tea as Chronos sighs and scratches off one more on his list. Steele comes back and pours, as Chronos scratches his head. BAS: (sitting down, soda refreshed) “Any more diabolical devices you wish to use?” Chronos: (thinking hard) “How about the vortex creating subspace vacuum which shall surely suck you up into another dimension of an alternate reality?” BAS: “Broken.” Chronos: “The cross-gate trap that captures you in a sort of ‘still frame’ of time that becomes lost in the swirls of the space-time continuum?” BAS: “S&M removed the distributor cap.” Chronos: (snarling) “I KNEW I shouldn’t have used such simple technology on that one! The subspace energy flux capacitor that stores energy from the surrounding areas of the temporal planes and releases in into a steady stream of deadly and incinerating energy upon your hapless body?” BAS: “By the gods! You KNOW that does not work! Do not bring such deception into our game of human chess!” Chronos: (shrugging) “You can’t blame a guy for trying a bluff. Well, it’s crude, but I guess I’ll have to go with my DISINTIGRATING RAY!!” The man in the hat pushes away from the table and whips out a futuristic-looking pistol, pointed at Big Al’s amused face. Chronos: “DIE DIE DIE DIE!!! Muah!” He pulls the trigger… and the gun turns to dust. Chronos: (heartbroken) “What do you know… it disintegrated.” BAS: “Foolish one, your eagerness has belied your training! Everyone knows that such a weapon designated for normal space-time continuum activity cannot be fired in a mobius loop state! The released energy turned in upon itself!” Chronos: (slumping back into chair) “Dang. This leaves our battle to remain in the physical realm, and though my timing is impeccable, you shall surely wear me down with your oaf-like endurance and strength until I tire and make a mistake!” BAS: (arms crossed in hero-like fashion) “Looks like your future is not so bright, eh Master of Time? Hi-Keeba!” Chronos: (back up and yelling with frustration) “Oh yeah?!?! Well at least I didn’t lose a wrestling match to a former SOCCER PLAYER and his CULINARY SPECIALIST FRIEND!!” Big Al’s teeth grind together and fire burns in his eyes… Back to real time, as paramedics load up a moaning Chronos, limbs at odd angles, into the back of an ambulance. Steele stands casually to the side, dabbing soda water at his toga in an attempt to pre-treat the blood stains left by Chronos on his attire. The ambulance sirens kick up and the vehicle makes its departure as a worried cop steps forward, rubbing his forehead beneath his hat. Big Al doesn’t even acknowledge him as he starts to speak. Cop: “Hey Al. Yeah, uh, me and the guys were wondering…” BAS: (mumbling, in bad mood) “Hmmm?” Cop: “Uh, we were curious… what happened to you?!” BAS: “Your meaning is unclear, Praetorian. Speak!” Cop: (gulp!) “Well, normally with these lesser villains, you just kinda tie them up and pin a note to their backs, leaving them in front of our station. What got into you today?” Steele gets a faraway look, fists clenched in anger… BAS: (growling angrily) “He struck a nerve…”
