The Thing About Coffee...FF#1

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/03 “RARRRRRRRRRAAAAARRRR” The wall effortlessly crumbled around Blind Rage’s ears as the rampant superhero blasted into the streets of New York City. Another hole in the already decrepit Justice Inc building. The passers by on street almost freeze in shock as the animal like Blind Rage stomps through the street, with white roam streaming from his mouth. As he slowly plodded across the road, a car unable to break in time slams into the side of him, instantly writing off the car and barely leaving a dent in the leg of Blind Rage. In fact the impact wasn’t even worthy of his attention. Blind Rage continues upon his mindless rampage, with nobody daring to get in his way. Warrior hesitantly poked his head around the hole in the Justice Inc wall. Slowly, he steps outside onto the streets, the carnage caused by his partner. A couple of steps behind him, The Handbag Warrior and Badger Man also emerge from the building. Each of them appear to have already had a decidedly rough time of it, their costumes torn here and there and Warrior wore a nasty bruise along the side of his face. “Well that’s just fabulous!” said Warrior rubbing his face in a soothing manner. “Now, just how the hell are we supposed to stop him?” Badger Man and Handbag Warrior simply look at each other in despair, then attempt to scarper. Warrior however, catches them, in time and drags them both back. “It’s your fault we’re in this mess Badger Man and Handbag Warrior, you’re the only one who stands a real chance of taking him down…” Warrior explained. “It wasn’t my fault…I didn’t give him the coffee!” Badger Man replied, face in hands, weeping. “Then who did?” Warrior asked. “Just why does he react this way with coffee, anyhow, sugah?” The Handbag Warrior asked. “He’s caffeine sensitive…in a normal human that wouldn’t do much damage, but due to Blind Rage’s abnormal mutation that gave him his powers, coffee as a surreal effect on him.” Warrior informed, checking that his laser axes were indeed attached to the side of his leg. “As this ever happened before?” Badger Man asked. “Yes. Just the once.” Warrior said, grimly. “And what happened, sugah?” Handbag Warrior questioned. “Well…he destroyed brothels because they wouldn’t give him a special superhero discount…he destroyed a kebab establishment because the owners couldn’t tell him what the kebab meat was…despite the dog collar attached to the base of it…he…no wait…that was the time he got drunk.” Warrior said. “And what happened with the coffee…” Badger Man prompted, getting a little angry. “Oh I remember…he left a trail of disaster and the authorities found him the morning after, sleeping next to an incredible perky hen.” Warrior said, with a slight grin. “Where’s the nearest farm?” Badger Man asked. ~ Blind Rage arrived on the farm not long after they arrived, with some of Spider-Man’s webbing attacked to his arm, a barbed wire fence wrapped around his left leg and some kind of strange old man clinging on to his back, poking the rather large superhero with a stick. The rest of the team had been preparing for his arrival and had devised a few methods that they thought could possible stop the mammoth. First thing they had to do was get the old man off him. Warrior burst out of some bushes, both laser axes drew, but without the laser part turned on, after all, they didn’t want to seriously damage their friend in any way. Warrior didn’t pull his punches, or indeed wait for Blind Rage to strike first; he simply hit Blind Rage with a flurry of attacks using the steel laser axe handles. The attack bore no fruit at all. In fact, the attack had bent the axe handles almost in a ninety-degree angle. However, whilst Warrior had begun the attack, the Handbag Warrior had managed to swoop down and take the old man away from the danger. In the air, however, the old man had begun to poke the Handbag Warrior with the stick almost immediately. “What the hell are you doin’, sugah?” The Handbag Warrior asked concerned. “Staby, staby stab…staby, staby stab….” the old man simply repeated. “Aw…screw this, sugah!” The Handbag Warrior said, as he simply let go of the old man from fifteen feet in the air. The old man fell through the roof of the farm’s barn. Meanwhile, Blind Rage had shrugged Warrior aside, sending him slamming into a wall. Badger Man had found a tractor and consequently, began to charge at Blind Rage with it. The impact was nothing less than chaotic. Metal parts scattered the entire field and Badger Man was propelled trough the wire mesh and into the chicken coup. White feathers were everywhere. It was now the Handbag Warrior’s turn. After dropping off the old man into the barn and witnessing Badger Man’s obvious failure he began to charge, whilst simultaneously swinging his handbag, gaining momentum all of the time. The impact was that of epic proportions. The sound was deafening. The ground shook. The aftermath was equally shocking. The Handbag Warrior lay at the bottom of a fifteen foot deep pit and Blind Rage was now at the bottom of what was once the barn, all of the walls had caved in upon him, and now over a tonne of bricks and wood were atop of him. There is a brief shuffle under the rubble, before one of the top layers of wood, which once had been part of the barn’s roof, was pushed aside and the large superhero emerged without a single scratch. He seemed a little confused at the situation and glanced around. Warrior and Badger Man surrounded him in a fighting stance. “I’ll attack…then whilst he’s occupied with me…you jump him!” Warrior said, aimed at Badger Man, who simply nodded in reply. “Jump me? Why?” Blind Rage said, looking incredibly confused. “Rage? That you?” Warrior asked, hesitantly taking a step closer. “Yeah. What happened?” he replied. “Er…you may have…er…somebody…er…you had coffee again.” Blind Rage rolled his eyes to the back of his head. “Who the hell give me coffee?” ~ “Mwhahahahahahaahhahahahahahaha” the Outrageous Owl-Man laughed…in an evil manner of course. “What is it, ass?” the gruff voice of Little Willie asked. “What did you just say?” He beamed. “Nothin’. So what is it?” “The plan worked perfectly…Blind Rage’s reaction to coffee nearly destroyed half of town!” “Good job I obtained that blood sample, huh?” “Indeed, Little Willie. The next target shall be that camp superhero, The Handbag Warrior. I’m afraid you’ll have to deal with this guy I’m homophobic. “Homophobic?” “Yes, I’m allergic to gay people.” Little Willie nods in understanding then scarpers off.
