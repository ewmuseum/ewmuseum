Date: 27/5/03 Beat me once shame on you. Beat me twice shame on me. Beat me three times getting kind of silly Beat me four times…that’s not going to happen Standing alone underneath the night sky is James ‘lunar’ Edwards standing quite still, the light whisping of the wind is all that is audible, not even James’ breath can be heard as he stands staring obliviously in front of him, what is he staring at? Nothing for there is nothing to see except an endless sky that is encompassing him. Solitude that’s what he was experiencing right now, he had foolishly allowed his mind to stray from the mission that was always first and foremost in his mind, the dream that he HAD to accomplish at all costs but first there was an obstacle that stood before him, something or someone the like of which he had not been subjected to before. In his life he had not been pushed to the limit like this man had done to him. Matt Jackson. From day one Matt Jackson had been the thorn in James’ side costing him his very first match, but James had come to deal with that, he could accept that at the time James was very much the underdog in the four way bout with Jackson being the more than favourite having a few matches under his belt already and working hard at what he did. The second time though was something quite different, he had done something that no other person had been able to do in singles competition and that was put away SOCIETY he then had to face Shane Wethers before getting to Jackson, the third match he lost. The wind picked up slightly as James’ back arched up the way it does when someone inhales deeply, his t-shirt clinging to his body rippling slightly in the breeze, his knees locked as he continues to stare out into the night. The third time he had faced Jackson he lost again but not even through his own fault, another man had cost James the title by allowing himself to get pinned. [i]no[/i] That was not true. If James was meant to win he would have won through his own ability, his own merit and he would not have to resort to blaming others, the question was though would he ever be able to topple someone like Jackson a man who in all essence he fought side by side with at Supremacy. He had the chance to strike him down, that one point within the match that could have proved to be so pivotal at the end of the day. When he looked at Jackson and Jackson looked at him…and they did not strike each other they simply went back to attacking the other two involved in the match. [i]Had that been a rookie mistake? Should I have waited until he had turned his back and hit him from behind…could I really do that to another human, there are unwritten rules that men should abide by when fighting each other, should I really throw those out of the window in the quest for personal gain? No. If I can’t win through fair means I don’t want to win at all. I will be pushed as far as my body will take me, I will suffer all the mental anguish that I have to but I will never change who I am…I couldn’t do it[/i] The silence was broken by the sound of a milk float travelling along a street preparing for it’s morning rounds, James also became aware of birds awakening and beginning their song which they would sing without want for nothing, apart from the occasional worm to provide nourishment for them. James glanced over his shoulder and noticed that the sky was becoming lighter and the darkness was being taken from him once more, the world was taking away his view to a dream much as it always did, forcing him to live under the suns fiery gaze. James: A new day a new beginning. Continuing to stare at the remaining sky which could be deemed as night-time his mind wonders towards the sister that was currently up there, if the truth be told the dream was fading, everyday his love for the business that was called wrestling was growing more and more, the link was Emily. The one person left in James’ life that he would allow to grow close to him without fear of disgust or being distraught whenever they saw him in pain. The truth of it all was that he loved Emily and she loved him the sibling bond that would never be broken not even in death, he knew that she would always be with him watching over him. Turning around James left the remaining night sky behind him which was slowly being engulfed by the dulcet tones of the day for a few moments James watched the sun makes it’s way above the horizon, climbing into the early morning sky, forming dew on the grass beneath James’ feet. Wednesday would more than likely be James’ last chance at the Rising Star Title in a long while he had to make it count. This time there would be no excuses. No excuse with it being his debut match No excuse with him having to fight two others before getting to Jackson No excuse with it not being him that got pinned. At Turmoil it was simple James Edwards vs. Matt Jackson for the rising star title and James was not about to let that opportunity go to waste not again. Not by a long shot
