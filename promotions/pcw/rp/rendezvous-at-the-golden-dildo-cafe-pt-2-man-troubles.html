Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin and Enigma had stumbled across a Gay restaurant named the ‘Golden Dildo Café’.Whilst Enigma quickly picked up the aroma of ‘Elton John’ in the air, Flaymin was none the wiser and took the advances of the gay staff in his stride. How will Enigma break the news?How will they escape? Do they even want to leave!?!? [Scene opens in the Golden Dildo Café. Flaymin and Enigma carry their food trays and walk over to a table to sit down.Flaymin looks at the table which is shaped like giant ass cheeks and smiles] Flaymin: I need to get myself one of these tables. Enigma: Why? Flaymin: That crack in the middle would stop any of the food rolling off the table. Enigma: You do realise that crack is…. An ass crack. [Flaymin looks at Enigma in confusion] Flaymin: What? Enigma: This whole place, doesn’t it seem odd to you? Flaymin: Its very… friendly. Enigma: *sigh*When we first walked into here a few minutes ago, did you bother to read the giant, flashing sign on the roof? Flaymin: Sure, it said ‘Bookings Necessary’. Enigma: Underneath that.What did it say underneath that! Flaymin: ‘Maximum Seating Capacity- 140’? [An irritated Enigma bangs his fists on the table and begins to yell at the top of his voice having lost his patience] Enigma: NO YOU IDIOT!! GOLDEN DILDO CAFÉ!!!THAT’S WHAT THE SIGN SAID! [The other gay men dining at the restaurant suddenly stare at Enigma and begin to gossip to one another. The gay waiter in blue hotpants walks over to the table and speaks to Flaymin and Enigma] Waiter: Is everything alright here, gentlemen? [Flaymin simply shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: I don’t know what’s going on….. Enigma: Yes waiter, everything is fine. Waiter: Are you sure?Maybe you’d like to make the most of our pleasure room? [The waiter points at the ‘Rumpy Pumpy Room’ in the corner of the restaurant, where gay couples can be seen boning each other behind a silhouette curtain] Flaymin: Cool!Puppet show!! Enigma: They’re not puppets… Flaymin: Do you want to go and take a peak? [The waiter looks at Enigma and winks at him, encouraging Enigma to get up and ‘pleasure’ Flaymin in the ‘Rumpy Pumpy Room’] Enigma: I don’t think we need to go there to sort out our issues.We can thrash it out right here. Flaymin: Dude, that sounds sooooo gay!What kind of place do you think this is!?! [The disturbed waiter leans over and whispers into the ear of Enigma] Waiter: I’m sorry sir but we can’t allow you to…. ‘thrash it out’ here in front of all these eating customers. [Enigma shakes his head in frustration] Enigma: You don’t get it. [Flaymin looks at Enigma with a disappointed look in his face] Flaymin: I bring you to my favourite restaurant and this is what you do to me?I can’t take this anymore! [The waiter nods his head in approval and begins to comfort Flaymin by rubbing his back] Waiter: He’s right you know.He deserves better than this. Flaymin: Thank you!At least someone understands.You know I try and I try- but he doesn’t pay any attention to my needs.What about me, huh? WHAT ABOUT ME!?!? [Enigma sits in silence for a moment gazing at Flaymin and the waiter trying to comfort him.He then looks around the restaurant and notices that everyone is also staring at him] Enigma: Thank God I wear a mask…. [Having heard Enigma’s latest comment, the horrified waiter stares at Enigma and begins to snap his fingers in the air] Waiter: Oh no!Oh no you didn’t!!Giiirrrrrrl, you ain’t nothing but a bitch! Flaymin: Yeah! Waiter: How dare you bring that kind of attitude in here!! Flaymin: Damn straight! Waiter: This prestigious homosexual restaurant doesn’t need customers like you! Flaymin: Yeah!……….What? Waiter: Huh? Flaymin: This is a gay restaurant? [Enigma reaches over and slaps Flaymin across the head] Enigma: What do you think I’ve been trying to tell you for the last half hour!! [Flaymin slowly gets up and makes his way to the door as Enigma follows him] Flaymin: What kind of freak are you? Enigma: ME???? Flaymin: Yeah you!You walked into here, ordered your food, sat down to eat AND THEN decided to spring this information on me!! Enigma: YOU BROUGHT ME HERE! Flaymin: Oh no!Oh no, you didn’t just raise your voice! Enigma: Dear God… you’re starting to sound like one of them! [Flaymin’s face grows pale as he realises what he just said] Flaymin: Get the car….. We’re going to need to hold an emergency session at the local strip club! [Scene fades to black]
