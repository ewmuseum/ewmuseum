The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part XVI]

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 24, 2002 Elton John was bored, they had eaten Chinese that night and he had insisted on speaking in a Liverpool accent all night, he sat on the bed loading and unloading thoughts on what had just happened. First he did it with his right hand, then his left...put a finger to his head that is. After five of each he did it with his eyes closed, five times each way too. Seeing as Valentine was there he stopped short of the really bizarre and gross meditation techniques such as using his teeth or with his hands cuffed together. He fished out the spring hand strengthener and started clenching it in his hand as he spoke. Elton John: “Come on Valentine, I’m bored. Lets go out, see if you can meet any women.” Jack Valentine: “I happened want to think about mine and Johnny's title defense tomorrow night. Can't you keep your dumb little glasses on straight for one minute?” Elton John: “Come off it, get real Valentine, you win a title once, and you want to spend the rest of your life trying to keep it. You had some fun that was all, fact of life, men of the world, all that garbage. Lets get out there and see what more fun we can have, I’m in the mood for a really short girl...or boy, you know slim, attractive, and short. I don’t know though, nurses are nice too, and military sorts. A military uniform really suits a man. Come on Valentine, I’ll soon get you fixed up.” Jack Valentine: “You go out, I’m worn out. All this traveling doesn’t go well with me. I’m in need of a quiet night in. A night of preparation.” Elton John: “Okay you be a boring old th ... thing if you want. I feel lucky,” and off he went, hand strengthener in one hand, and the words of Dirty Harry on his lips, “do you feel lucky punk...” Valentine switched on the power at the socket in the wall. Then he switched on the modem, two lights flickered then just the one. Then finally he switched on the laptop. It made a sort of orchestral chime as it started up. The laptop slowly booted up and eventually the computer desktop appeared. He double clicked on the folder for the internet. On he went to research the history of Duzza and Mark Makonie. The Heartbreakers had come to know the Pretty Cool Guys and Cowabunga Connection, but Duzza and Makonie was something brand new to them. The modem sounded out the dialing of numbers and then a couple of rings and the connection was made. Screens filled with information on Mark Makonie and Duzza. Makonie, the Nazi and Duzza his black opposite. Valentine stared for hours at the screen, studying everything he could possibly imagine. Shifting to Elton John's neck of the woods Elton John had not found it difficult to pick up another girl. He had wandered round the city nightspots, well those that did not charge for admission, and had a drink in most of them. An attractive girl with black hair and a strange pillbox hat had caught his eye. She had been dancing with a group of what looked like colleagues from work. She was gorgeous and fit too. They had danced away most of the evening. She must have had thighs like a footballer, only hers were definitely more attractive. They headed out before the nightclub closed, which was virtually a first for Elton John, and his Yellow Brick Road ways. She said that if they were quick they could catch the last bus. They stood shivering in the bus shelter. She wore a short skirt a dark collarless blouse and a collarless jacket in white linen. Somewhere along the course of the evening the little pillbox hat had got lost. She really did have an attractive laugh. She cuddled up to Elton John, and he opened up his jacket, she put her arms around him and held him close. She could smell his sweat, and feel the hairs on his chest. He let out a smirk. Woman: “Gosh you feel strong, are you into bodybuilding or something?” Elton John: “Yep, that’s me Mary, don’t let the size of me fool you” Woman: “Really, you’re not pulling my leg are you. I do like strong fit men.” She laughed, yet Elton remained that he was still strong. Elton John: “If you want strong and fit, I’ll show you...” Elton John grabbed hold of the edge of the bus shelter roof. He could do this with his feet still on the ground. He tensed himself. Elton John: “I’m solid muscle, not one ounce of fat.' He pulled himself up sharply. As he pulled the roof dipped towards him and all the stagnant water and empty crisp packets that usually lived up there took the opportunity to make a break for it and headed in his direction. A smelly torrent of water flooded over him, and he let go, spluttering bad temperedly. Woman: I don’t know about that ounce of fat, what about between your ears.” Mary laughed for most of the journey home, after she had sorted out a taxi. The bus had refused to take Elton John on board, despite of having a bus load of vomiting students there already. The late bus had to draw the line somewhere. Even a lovely laugh could get to be tiresome after a while. She ran Elton John a hot bath, she was beginning to think he would never stop shivering. Elton John lay in the hot bath. He was facing the window, it was made of stained glass. All different shades of blue. Somewhere outside there was a street lamp, it was caught in the glass, repeated, magnified and diminished, countless times. Maybe there was more than one street lamp out there, maybe it was a reflection. He started to try and work out the physics of it, and gave up. The bath was hot and welcoming, after the cold shower. Mary had filled it with blue bubble bath. She was sitting on the toilet seat, a pile of letters in her hand. She flicked through the envelopes. Mentally noting where they came from, before selecting one to open. Elton John: “What do you do for a living then Mary, when you’re not rescuing cold wet men?” Woman: “Apart from that, well I do have a bit of time left over, you’ll never guess.” Elton John: “Oh, I don’t know, university lecturer, teacher, you tell me.” Woman: “Well actually I’m a spy, top secret, all very hush, hush you know.” Elton John: “Really Mary, a spy, and you’re telling me about this.” Woman: “Well us top secret spies know who to trust, and I’ve got you down as one of the good guys.” She ran her hand across his wet chest, catching a finger in the matted chest hair. Elton John: “It is good to know that the defense of the realm is in your safe hands, I will sleep soundly in my bed after saying my prayers now.” A wet bubbly hand fixed itself around her neck, and a very nice neck it was too, strong and smooth. Woman: “Well it is a tough job, and a dirty job, but someone has to do it you know.” Her hand slipped down, a nail traced a path across his back. Woman: “You certainly look like a tough cookie, I couldn’t do anything like that you know, I’ll leave it to you tough cookies.” His hand fixed itself, gripped in her hair, the good, straight, dark hair, he pulled her closer. Woman: “That’s why we are here, you would not believe the things we have to do, in defense of the realm, for Queen and country, but I enjoy it really.” Her finger teased around his nipple, their lips met. Gently she held his lower lip between her teeth. He raised his eyebrows, a hand teased out the buttons of whatever she was wearing. It really did not matter, buttons come undone, that is their function in life. Her skin was smooth and warm, like a humanized alabaster, there is a certain electricity about the touch of another’s skin. Skin that yielded. The scene shifted to reveal Jack studying the tag team match again, and then quickly shifted back to Elton She was struggling to balance, although she wanted to bend forward, she had to lean backwards to retain her balance. A steadying hand touched Elton John’s side. He wasn't too strong, the live tautness of muscle quivered beneath his skin. Like a boxer’s hand that so easily formed a fist, his body now relaxed was in readiness. A scar traced itself across his back. And having traced that one, another crossed it, and another. An accident probably, metal and glass do so much damage. Again, shifting to Jack and Johnny this time, and back to Elton, even quicker. He had said so little about himself, and he was good company, a presence, her eyes had been drawn to him as soon as he had come into the club. He had a sort of magnetism and confidence that you don’t learn. Whatever he did he would excel at. He was that kind of person. And none of it really mattered, none of it, she could feel the wet steamy air against her back, and she let herself fall gently forward, tensing and releasing she came to rest on that strong dark chest, an inquisitive tongue worked its way from her nose to her eyebrow. A soft kiss was planted on her forehead. She could feel the wiry stubble on her face. But he was so gentle, she could feel the stubble, but it caressed her as gently as a hand might. All your life is a series of mistakes, we tumble from one to another, she tumbled and fell. She swung around to the backside of Elton and smacked him in the back of the head, knocking him out unconscious. Woman: 'Now we'll see what happens when the PINK! tries to mess with PURPLE~! again!!' She let out an evil laugh and proceeded to tie him up as the scene shifted back to Jack, unknowing that his partner in crime was in deep trouble just 2 days before Dawning. Would Jack Valentine be able to focus on the match this Sunday when he finds out that Elton John is nowhere to be seen? Or will the trauma of this leave The Heartbreakers and the PINK! out of luck forever? Stay tuned for the next episode.
