Growing Up Sucks; Part Five. You Walked Right into That One.

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 9th, 2003. Patience. That’s today’s lesson, but basically only because most people aren’t very…patient. They sort of need stuff down right here, right now, in a way. They need to learn how to chill out; man…you aren’t going to die if you don’t get your latte within ten seconds, God forbid. I wonder if some people have ever heard the line, “Take a chill-pill,” it would help them lots, if they were real anyway. But anyway, Jackson Rust babysitting. That took effort…that took patience. That took restrictions not to kill any of the children (wouldn’t that be a crying shame?). Not really, but meh. One step at a time until he’s grown up mentally…yep. The scene opens up. Yes, that bland opening has just opened the scene. Actually, where the scene is, well, it’s (surprise, surprise), in Rust’s house once more. Jackson Rust: What? Oh…you were expecting novel style again, weren’t you? Ha! Fuck off ya bastard! That was the only time you’ll see something of quality coming out of my fucking promos! Jesus Christ it’s cool to swear again…those kids were gonna break me down if I had ‘em for another minute. Al: You just reverted to that to make this promo of some depth didn’t you? Jackson Rust: Yes, yes I did. Kimberly: At least you managed three days with the little bastards. I was surprised the novel opening of your last promo wasn’t the end of the aforementioned promo…what a time that would be. Narrator: Wanna do a non-lackluster promo now? Jackson Rust: Yes. Let’s. Narrator: Yer choice: pose as a plastic surgeon, or as a Vegas stripper. Rust scratches his chin, deep in thought. Jackson Rust: Well, on the bright side of a stripper…I’d be able to play with my own jugs…but I don’t look good in spandex. Surgeon it is. Narrator: *shrugs* Okay… *KA-FLASH-INTO-NEXT-SCENE* The scene reopens into a surgical lab. Steve, Al, and Kimberly all look in from a window on the outside (gotta love those viewing windows), as Jackson Rust, decked out in full surgeon regalia, the little mask, and coat…goggles for some strange reason, walks over to a table with several female nurses. Jackson Rust: G’evening, ladies. Nurse #1: Good morning doctor. Jackson Rust: Morning? What the fuck am I doing up? Nurse #2: Swearing… Jackson Rust: Yare, yare…so…who’s the lucky patient? Nurse #1: He’s been with us, several times. You may have heard of him…Michael Jackson? Jackson’s eyes widen. Jackson Rust: Did you say...”Michael Jackson”? Nurse #2: Yes, she did. Jackson Rust: Not now…did you? Nurse #1: What she said…yes, I did. Rust slowly turns his head to glare at the window, where the Narrator’s glove dangles in the air, and shoots it dirty looks, and swearing under his breath. Jackson Rust: He stole my name…and he will die. Nurse #1: What was that? Jackson Rust: Nothing…nothing at all. The emergency room door barges open as two unknown men push in a stretcher supporting Michael Jackson on it. Michael props himself on his elbow, as he queerly waves to the nurses and Rust. Michael Jackson: Hi everyone! I brought flowers for you! He pulls out a basket of carnations and hands them to Nurse #1. Michael Jackson: And you! Again, he pulls out a basket of daisies, and hands them to Nurse #2. Michael Jackson: And you! He pulls out a basket of white roses, and tries to hand them to Rust, but Rust ‘accidentally’ sneezes, and swats the basket to the floor. Jackson Rust: Umm…oops. Michael Jackson: No worries! I had a spare! Michael pulls out another basket, this time red roses, and hands them to Rust. Jackson cautiously takes them, but quickly points into a general direction. Jackson Rust: Look! A diversion! Michael Jackson: Huh? The nurses and Michael turn into the aforementioned general direction, as the men that wheeled Michael in leave, and Rust throws the basket out of the door before it closes. Michael and the nurses shrug, before turning back. Michael Jackson: Where’d your flowers go? Jackson Rust: They’re on a coffee break…*mumbles* you he-she plastic fucker. Michael Jackson: What was that? Jackson Rust: Nothing. Nurse #1: Okay…so what are we lifting, moving, shifting, adding on, or taking off this time? Jackson Rust: By the looks of it, I’d wager the face. Michael Jackson: Yep! Jackson Rust: Who did that shock? The nurses shrug, before backing Michael’s stretcher into the section of the room where all the needles are at…yes, I’m not very good with medical terms. Nurse #2: Okay, we’re going to put this sleeping mask on you…don’t worry, you’ve done this literally millions of times before. As she places the gasmask on Michael, Rust leans over, and makes “slitting throat” motions with his thumb, glaring angrily at Michael, and mouthing the word “die” over and over again. Jackson Rust: Die! The nurses turn around. Nurse #1: What? Jackson Rust: Err…diabetes! Nurse #1: Okay. Rust glares at Jackson again, before leaning over to his prosthetic ear (hey, it has to be fake, too), and whispers. Jackson Rust: *whispers* Don’t plan on waking up, fucker. Michael Jackson: Huh? Rust scores with a hard shot to the face, almost shattering it (literally). Michael’s out, and Rust frantically slaps the King of Pop’s jawbone back in place. Nurse #1 comes back with several utensils. Nurse #1: Wow, he went out fast, eh doctor? Jackson Rust: Yes…yes he did. Steve looks to Al. Steve: H-he’s g-gon-gonna d-die. Al: Who? Steve: Mi-mike-Michael. Kimberly: It’s likely… Narrator: Better write out for another lawsuit? Kimberly: I’ll get to it later. Narrator: ‘Atta girl…procrastination owns. Kimberly: Indeed it does. Al: So, as Jackson tries to accidentally kill Michael Jackson, wanna grab a bite to eat so we can resume this promo for a later time? Kimberly: We got nothing better to do. Steve: Y-yeah! F-food r-rul-rules! Narrator: Yes, yes it does. In fact, it rules so much, that I’m willing to end this promo for some. Al: Wouldn’t you end this promo for any reason, anyway? Narrator: No one has to know that. Al: Oh, okay…this was to get the promo a bit longer, right? Kimberly: Seems it. Steve: A-aye. The scene mercifully ends…for now! *insert evil laugh*
