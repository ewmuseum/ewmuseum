Moving Days Part 1

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-23-03 The papers where signed. Now begun the hardest part. The waiting. To make it even worse Chris didn't have much to do as it was. His match with Jackson Rust was this weekend. The tornado tag team match has passed already. A quick upset for them. They defeated the World champ and Darkstar. Quite the nice feather in his cap though. Chris was still sickened at the thought of having helping Jackson Rust gain a victory though. But it is all in the past for right now. As for now Chris must just wait. And he needed to talk to Tessa. There was going to need to be some changes made and he needed to tell her about them. Chris slowly opens the door to the waiting room. Tessa and the kids had finally found a program they call could enjoy together. Chris smiled as he watched them sit close together watching the small television. He sneaks up behind her and slips his arms around her waist and hugs her tight. Tessa is surprised but calms down when she notices it's merely Chris. She turns around in his arms and faces him. 'So what did the officer have to say.' she asked him. Her voice was fulled with concern. She bit her bottom lip, waiting for good news. Chris merely smiled at her and rubbed his nose against her. Tessa lets out a giggle. 'He said we can have temporary custody of Evan. That is until they can find a home for him. Which I might add, I filled out the paper work requesting us to be his legal guardians.' Tessa's eyes lit up with joy. She gave Chris a big hug. 'Oh thank you. You are the best.' Chris just smiles. But his happiness quickly dies down. Tessa gets a worried look on her face. 'What's the matter Chris?' 'Well you see there will need to be some changes.' he told her. Tessa simples stared out, trying to figure out what was meant by that. Chris grabs her shoulders. 'Tessa we need to move out of the apartment. They won't let us keep Evan if we stay in that small apartment.' Tessa let out a sigh of relief. 'Is that all? We can just move into my house I had before I moved into the apartment. It's two stories and it should be larger enough for all of us.' she told him. Chris nods in agreement. He touches foreheads with her and whispers into her ear. Tessa blushes at the sound of his voice. Chris runs his hand through her hair and kisses her. Their romantic adventure is cut short however as Alex and Christa start to giggle. Chris hugs Tessa and looks over her shoulder to the kids. He gives her a quick kiss to the lips and the two part. Chris stands in front of the kids and places his hands on his hips. He looks over both of them. 'Well it looks like we are moving kids. You'll be returning to your home in Massachuetts.' Both of the kids jump for joy. Then run up and huge Chris. He looks at them surprised. 'Ah come on now, I thought you both liked it at my apartment.' Tessa laughs at Chris and walks up behind him. She places one hand on his shoulder. 'Two words Chris. Seperate rooms.' Chris just lets out ah 'oh' and looks at her. She laughs again and the four of them leave the station, off to the apartment to start their packing. A new adventure is about to dawn apon the lives of these people. It will not be easy but they are willing to try. However one question remains. Why did Chris not tell Tessa about the relationship needing to be more serious? What is he planning? With his clear distain for the events at Turmoil, no one can tell what is on his mind at the moment.
