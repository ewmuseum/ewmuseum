Matt Land, Part 1- The Grab

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/30/03 The blue Monte Carlo stands still on the cold streets of Washington, the slender man in the drivers seat is Matt Jackson, more commonly referred to as the ghost. The headlights of passing cars highlight his short brown hair; his face has a look of total relaxation. He is in his element, being a person that no one knows and no one wants to know. Jumping from identity to identity, making sure never to be noticed and certainly not dwelled upon for more then a few seconds, that was his way and it would continue to be his way. He has been following Sarah Clarkson all day, getting to know her patterns and to double check the information he had been given. Everything had matched the file exactly and now he was just waiting for his chance to strike. There hadn’t been an ample opportunity for him to make his move yet, so he was just biding his time. The British Intelligent officer’s daughter would soon be in his possession and he would get everything out of her that the DCI wanted. His brown eyes cautiously scan the streets, as he waits for her to come out of her friend’s apartment. She has been up there for quite a while and he is growing tired of sitting and staring at the dashboard in front of him. It was all part of the job though and at least it was giving him sometime to think about his upcoming match on Turmoil. That was the only thing keeping his mind off of Haley, he hated the fact that he had to cancel their date just a few hours earlier, but this was important and he wanted to achieve his objective. Haley will be all right on her own, he thinks, trying to hide his own disappointment from himself. I can’t always be there for her and she is going to have to get by on her own every once in a while. His own words shocked him. After just a few short weeks he was starting to fall madly in love with her and it was affecting him in every which way. He has been trying to hide it, not wanting it to interfere with his work. She was only in the PCW because of him. Haley Hilton needed Matt Jackson and it was a hard fact for him to get used too. Running his hand over his chin, he feels the stubble that he has been growing. It was a good way to make him less memorable and that had been his secondary objective. He has done it for the most part, but he couldn’t be certain until he had Sarah in his grasp. If she even remembered him for a minute, then she could run back to her father and tell him everything and the rich brat would be more trouble then she was worth. He wouldn’t let it happen though; he wouldn’t let some spoiled brat bring him down. I don’t care how much money she has, or what kind of friends. I am still the best in this business, he tells himself, hoping that perhaps he can convince himself of this. He knew how dangerous this was going to be, but he wanted to get it over and done with as quickly as possible. He had more pressing issues to deal with and that was the total demise of the PCW. He was growing to hate it and that was probably for the best. It will make it a lot easier once he serves everyone with a pink slip from Uncle Sam. He can see the look on every one of the employees’ faces as he sinks deeper into the padded seat of his car. A smile forms on his lips, knowing that he is going to be the one who brings down the great beast. Even his boss has been unable to get rid of the man that is Jeremy Howard, but he would be the one to do it. None of that matters to him at this moment, the only thing he cares about is the objective and bringing her down. There is nothing he can do to further reach his goal of making the PCW disappear and he knows that. It would all come in time, but for right now, he had to be on his guard. Sarah would be leaving soon, if the intelligence was right and then he would make his move. Looking down at his watch, the digital numbers blink blankly at him. She has almost been up there for three hours and his patience is starting to go thin. He wanted to make his move soon and keep good on his promise of having the information for the DCI after Turmoil. Sarah was looking like she didn’t want him to keep good on his promise and that was something that he doesn’t like. When she finally did come out, he was ready. Seven different disguises and countless names he had used over the day had made it a lot easier to get by. He had dressed just right to make the grab and get right into the interrogation. A freshly pressed white shirt and wrinkle free black pants gave him the look he felt would strike fear into her.His attention was snapped away from the dashboard, as the main door of the apartment swings open. Matt stays unmoving in the car just a few feet away. A smaller woman walks out, with shoulder length black hair and is wearing a heavy sweater and khakis. She walks slowly, rubbing her hands together, trying to keep them warm on the cold DC night. Her car is only ten feet away and Matt knows he is going to have to make his move before she reaches it. Grabbing the handle of the door, he applies a little pressure and it pops open. He steps out as stealthy as possible and sprints like a cheetah after its prey. Within a spilt second, he has reaches his objective and she is out cold in his arms. He struck her in the back of the head with all his might, not enough to do anything serious, but just enough to render her unmovable. He quickly scans the area to make sure no one has seen him. Spotting no one he throws her up on his shoulder and makes his way toward the Monte Carlo. Matt places her in the passenger seat and smiles at his own handy work. He believes that there is no one even close to him in this business, and he is probably correct. The ghost claims another victim and he is proud that he was probably the only one who would have been able to handle this mission so easily. *** Matt leans against the massive brick wall behind him, his arms are crosses over his chest and a smile is painted across his face. He has been standing here for a few hours, awaiting Sarah to awaken from her unconscious state. He was hoping that it wouldn’t take her too much longer to wake up because he wants to get started right away. The punishment was only just starting for her. The days and quite possibly weeks a head were going to be hard on her. Sarah is going to be forced to stay awake for days at a time and live off of very little food and drink. Being locked up in the small cell at the warehouse in DC the CIA owned wouldn’t add much help. Matt knew all the techniques that he was going to have to use on her to break her spirits. He knew them all too well because he had been on both the receiving end and the giving end when he was a part of the Rangers. She lets out a small moan and starts to stir a bit. Matt pushes himself off the wall at her movements and walks over to the opposite side of the cold steel table that her head has been placed on. She pulls her head up and glances around the room with hazy eyes, trying to figure out where exactly she is. Nothing is familiar to her; the white brick walls and the small metal door on the right are the only things that stand out, probably because they are the only things in the room. “Where am I?” She screams out, her voice is laced with fear. Her big black eyes lock onto the ghost in front of her. His form fitting white dress shirt, with the sleeves rolled up to reveal his massive forearms does nothing, but add fear to her. Just the affect he was looking for. Matt stands unmoving and silent, not caring to answer her question. His blank expression and his cold eyes remain on her. He is trying to intimidate her even more then she already is and it seems like it is working. Sarah has always had the easy life and nothing to this extent has ever happened to her before, but Matt doesn’t care, it just makes his life easier. “I asked you a question and you better answer it!” she yells at him once again, trying to show him she isn’t afraid, but it is to late. Jackson knows that he has the upper hand and all he has to do is keep up the moves, in this human chess game. Just a few short days and he was hoping that he would have the young lady in checkmate. “You are here to answer my questions,” Matt tells her, with smugness behind his voice, to show her that he is going to get what he wants. “Now we can do this one of two ways, my way- the easy way. Or we can do this the hard way and you can just slowly let yourself starve to death.” “It will never happen, my father will notice I am missing and within hours he will come save me.” “Ms. Clarkson I wouldn’t bet on it. Your father is going to notice you are missing, but that isn’t the hard part. No one knows about this place and they aren’t going to. It is like finding a needle in a haystack. All your money, power, and friends are useless here. You are going to be taken out of your fantasy world and brought into the real one you snobby bitch.” She knows that there is a truth in his statement. No one would probably find her here, even if her father sent some of his best British agents to find her it would do no good. She was a prisoner at this mans hands and there was nothing she could do about it. Matt walks behind the metal chair that his objective is so tightly locked into and places his hands on the back of it. Sarah tries to whip her head around to face him, but it is useless, she can’t see his face and it doesn’t matter if she does anyways. “We are going to talk about you father though and you are going to tell me everything I want to know,” Matt hisses into her ear, his tongue like a snakes touching her earlobe every now and then, sending chills down her spine. She begins to shake, as she is starting to understand what this is all about. This man was a professional and there was nothing she could do to beat him. She knew he was going to get all the information out of her, but she was going to try to hold out as long as possible. She didn’t want to give up anything about her father that might damage him. “ I don’t know anything and if I did you actually think I would tell a creep like you?” she says, trying to force her face into a look of calmness. Matt only smiles back to her and make his way towards the steel door on the right of the wall. He jerks it open and turns around to face Sarah once more. “You do know something and you are going to tell it to me. For now I am going to leave you to ponder how you want to handle this. I gave you the two paths and now you have to pick one you little bitch.” With those words Matt shuts off the lights and slams the door behind him. He wants to leave her alone and scared in the dark room. He would be back in a few hours to jeer her out of any sort of sleep she has fallen into her and question her a bit more. Until then though he needs to focus some of his attention on Turmoil and his upcoming match with The Significant Others. Haley was going to be no help to him, so everything was on his shoulders. They will not present a problem to me, I am Matt Jackson and this is mine, No one can take it from me!
