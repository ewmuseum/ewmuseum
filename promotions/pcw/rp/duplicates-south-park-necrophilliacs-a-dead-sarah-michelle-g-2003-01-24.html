Duplicates, South Park, Necrophilliacs, a Dead Sarah Michelle Gellar, Triple H-Zilla, Signs from Above, and a Poke-battle You Don't Want to Miss.

24 January 2003

Date: January 24th, 2003. The scene reopens to a foggy, desolate motel room, location of Al. He lies in the bed with the heavy blankets pulled up to his neck, as he pouts a bit, complaining to himself. Al: I’ll never be loved or accepted…damn! He pouts a bit more. Al: I’m not a necrophilliac; I’m not in love with Editor #4, either! Damn the narrator and the entourage! Damn them! All of a sudden, a bright white light shines down upon Al’s motel room, as the heavenly figure of Jesus Christ floats down, wobbling at the strings that are tied to his wardrobe. He tries grabbing at it, but to no avail. Jesus Christ: *muttering* Damn strings…I told them they were to fucking weak. Al looks up to the dangling Christ, in awe, of course. Al: J-Jesus? Is that you? Jesus Christ: OH SHIT! With that said, the strings supporting the levitating Savior snap, and cause him to plummet the four feet to the ground, landing with a thud. Al sits up and looks over the bed, to see if Jesus is all right, but Christ jumps to his feet and dusts his robe off before shaking his fist upward. Al: Jesus, you okay? Jesus Christ: Damn, that hurt. I mean…yeah, yeah…thanks for asking, kind sire. Al: No problem. Al looks at Christ quizzically. Al: What in your name are you doing here? Jesus shrugs. Jesus Christ: I…don’t know. Just sort of sent here, I guess. Al: Oh. Jesus reaches into his side pocket of the robe, and grabs several index cards, flipping through them swiftly, before stopping at one, and glancing at it. Jesus Christ: I understand you have a problem…Avon. Al: It’s Al. Jesus Christ: That’s what I said. Al: Oh. Al blinks blankly at Christ, as Jesus makes death threat gestures, but crossing his throat with his thumb. Jesus Christ: What’s your problem, my son? Al sighs a bit. Al: Well, Jesus…I don’t think I’ll ever be fully accepted as ‘just one of the guys’, y’know? Jesus nods understandingly. Jesus Christ: Well, Alonzo, I have this theory of how to be accepted. Al: It’s Al…and what is your masterful theory? Jesus Christ: It’s quite simple actually. It goes something like: accept me or go to Hell. Al nods in agreement. Al: Well, you see Jesus…my friends just make fun of me, and abuse me a lot. Jesus Christ: Oh, I see. Well, my dad, God, gives me shit sometimes…I just tell him to SHUT HIS HOLE OR I’LL KICK EM IN THE NUTS! Al slides up his bed a bit, backing away from the semi-enraged Christ. Jesus Christ: And if that fails, just banish them to Hell. Al: Well, you see Jesus— Jesus Christ: Please, call me Jeez. Al: Okay…”Jeez”, well, I don’t exactly have the powers to banish people to Hell. Jesus slaps his forehead. Jesus Christ: Jesus H. Christ! Well, I better piss off then. Only supreme beings are supposed to know I REALLY exist. How can I be so fucking stupid to think you, “Al”, could be a supreme being. Al: Maybe it was my masculine, intimidating appearance? Jesus laughs his; excuse me, his ass off right n’yah. Jesus Christ: Anyway, I have to pretend to care about the Earth again, adios amigo. Al: Are you Mexican? Jesus looks around quickly, realizing no one is around, before placing his finger on his lips. Jesus Christ: Shhhhh! Christ then disappears with a flash! Al: Wow. Al proceeds to pull out a small notepad labeled: “Sure Signs of the Apocalypse; Volume One”, and scripts in: “Jesus is Mexican.” Al: I imagine we have about however long it takes till the tequila runs out before we are all doomed to a fiery, gruesome death. Al nods, and white a punishing white blare, the scene fades out, but quickly reopens to the JacksonCO studios, again, as Jackson Rust, Steve, Kimberly, Editors #1-4, Generic Producer, and a model of Triple H-Zilla all look over him. Jackson Rust: Dude, what were you dreaming about that made you do more crappy pelvic thrusts? Al: Jesus… They entire entourage, bar Al, leaps back a step in disgust. Editor #1: I know you’re a necrophilliac, like mythelf, but even I wouldn’t thcrew the Lord! You thick bathard! Al: No! No! No! Kimberly: You just admitted it! Jackson Rust: Yeah, dude…you are SO going to Hell. Steve: P-per-pervert. Editor #4: Didn’t he have enough punctures and wounds as it was? Al: No! Editor #4: No? He had five! He doesn’t need more wounds! Al gleams at everyone with burning hatred for a moment, silencing them all. Al: No, I mean I saw Jesus in what I suppose was a dream. Generic Producer: Oh, no you didn’t. Al: Huh? Al furrows his brow at the producer. Generic Producer: Actually, you were having a bit of a case of sleepwalking, so we took advantage. Jackson Rust: Yeah, ass. Generic Producer: So, we strapped you down to a wall, and put a fake beard, and goatee on Jackson, made him talk in a Mexican accent, and tied him onto some fishing wire, before decorating the scene as a motel room! Triple H-Zilla: RAR~! I R GOD~! Jackson Rust: Ahem! No, I AM! Triple H-Zilla: ROAR~! I AM THAT DAMN GOOD-UH! Triple H-Zilla tries to act intimidating as he spits water over a seven-mile radius, glaring at Rust with increasing “job the opponent to me” speed. Jackson Rust: Oh yeah? You’re nothing to the Gerbil F’N King. Triple H-Zilla: Que? Rust reaches into his pocket and pulls out the 2D animated gerbil: LEMMIWINKS~! Jackson Rust: Aha! Rust is quickly reverted into super-Pokemon form, as he is dressed into a red and white vest with a dorky hat on. Jackson Rust: I CHOOSE YOU LEMMIWINKS! Jackson throws a gerbil ball, and breaks the door of it, allowing Lemmiwinks to free himself from the clear plastic hell. Lemmiwinks: *squeaks* As Triple H-Zilla looks down upon Lemmiwinks, cheesy Pokemon battle music comes into play, as some sort of time warp prepares us for battle. Lemmiwinks cranes his neck upwards to the much bigger Triple H-Zilla, as the monster digests “performance enhancing medicine”, also known as steroids. He growls as he prepares to duel Lemmiwinks. Jackson Rust: Lemmiwinks – SQUEAK ATTACK~! Lemmiwinks nods and walks to Triple H-Zilla, before letting out a deafening squeak! Triple H-Zilla sighs, before raising his gargantuan foot, and slamming it upon Lemmiwinks! A referee that looks remarkably similar to Earl Hebner comes onto the scene. Earl Hebner: Fifty HP left! Jackson sighs as Triple H-Zilla grows an extra seventy feet in height thanks to the steroids, laughing at Lemmiwinks as he retreats back to his base. Jackson Rust: Lemmiwinks – SUPER STEROID INDUCTION POWER! Lemmiwinks nods, before crawling into his rolling ball, and ingesting a single steroid pill, before crawling out. Triple H-Zilla looks down at the Gerbil F’N King, as Lemmiwinks begins to shake. Jackson pumps his fists in encouragement, before suddenly Lemmiwinks grows to about triple the height and mass of Triple H-Zilla, and bites his lizard-like head off, and goes back to gerbil form. Jackson Rust: WOOHOO! Earl Hebner blinks in shock, as this is the first time Triple H has ever lost at his own game. Triple H-Zilla collapses to the ground as rats immediately begin to gnaw at his festering lizard body. Hebner walks to Lemmiwinks, and bends over, raising the gerbil’s paw in victory! Lemmiwinks: *squeaks* Jackson goes back to “Rust form”, as he ditches the crappy hat and vest, and picks Lemmiwinks from the ground, and holds him in the palm of his hand. Al: Wow…way to go Lemmiwinks~! Kimberly: Wait…did…did, Triple H-Zilla job? The scene cuts to Triple H-Zilla being devoured by rats and flies, and whatever animal lives in the JacksonCO studios. Jackson Rust: No…Lemmiwinks just kicked his arse. Narrator: Like you’ll do to Storm, right? Al: Dammit, not him. Narrator: Oh yeah. Hey everyone. Jackson Rust: Dude! You missed dissing Al! Narrator: Shit! I was too busy laughing my ass off on the PTC Forums of the posting of your pictures gallery. Kimberly: Oh. Jackson Rust: Well, both were pretty funny. Narrator: Yeah…what did you do to Al? Steve: No-nothing… Steve whistles innocently. Kimberly: Steve raped Jesus Christ in his dreams, albeit, that was a lie, and he was sleepwalking, so we made Jackson dress up like Christ and insult him. Narrator: I told you the plan would work. Editor #1: Yeah, and it turned out to be a great promo after all. Narrator: Even if I was fashionably late. Al: Yeah, whatever. Narrator: Don’t whatever me, son…or I’LL KICK YOU IN THE NUTS! *SMACK* Al: *no sell*© Jackson Rust: No! That’s my move. Kimberly! Kimberly slaps Al. Kimberly: You owe Jackson three hundred dollars for the use of *no sell*©, and owe me double that because I made it copyrighted. Al: Shit. *SMACK* *SMACK* *SMACK* Al holds his face in pain, as he is abused again. Narrator: This kicks ass! *SMACK* The scene abruptly ends.
