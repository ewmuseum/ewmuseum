The Krayzie One No More

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/09/02 “You could never let me have my moment in the spotlight, could you?” Our shot opens on the angry, snarling eyes of PCW Champion Xavier Kannon... The bright Green irises burning with rage against their bloodshot background. Xavier : “It always had to be you before me, didn’t it?” The camera zooms out to show his nose and forehead, the latter contorted with wrinkles of anger as he snarls. Xavier : “While you were here, I was always second fiddle, wasn’t I?” Further out we pull, to show the sneer on the face of the Scientologist, his head almost shaking in rage as his entire body becomes tense. Xavier : “Even after I drove you out you came back, didn’t you?” His face is an uncharacteristic bright Red that only helps to highlight the bruising he has around the side of his left eye. Xavier : “Even in the Third Age the washed-up and past-it have to steal the show, don’t they?” The Red turns even deeper as the tensed up head of the Gold Patron Meritorious limits the blood flow to his head... Eyes still angry... Mouth still snarling.... Hair wild and untamed..... Face bruised. Xavier : “You had to ruin MY MOMENT.... DIDN’T YOU?!?!?“ Finally he snaps and lunges forward to the sound of glass shattering. Xavier : “Well, it will be the LAST time, yes, it will...” The camera pulls out to show Kannon holding his bleeding right hand up to his face, small shards of glass protruding from his knuckles, the crimson liquor of life flowing from them. Xavier : “Well, you’ve had your swan song.... You reminded the PCW fans who you are just so they can forget again... You may have even sold a few more t-shirts... But I’m here, and you’re NOT.” As his fuming Green eyes glance past his slashed fist, they lock onto the object that did the damage. Xavier : “I’ll be reaping the benefits of PCW while you’ll be watching it back home in Elkhart, if they even have electricity there.” The Scientologist is in the PCW Headquarters.... Not in his office, in fact, far removed from the “Corridor of Power” where the Front Office spend their time. Xavier : “I’ll be seen by millions every week.... You’ll be talked about by fewer every day.” No, he is in the “Corridor of Respect” where on the wall before him are Black and White photos of the PCW Hall of Fame, faces of PCW’s legends looking back at him... But he only cares about one. Xavier : “While I’m flying around the world with my wife living every day as a honeymoon, you’re stuck at home with Jessica and that bastard child.” The photo shows Jeremy after his match at Decimation, a Black and White section of time frozen in immortality. The PCW World Title that Kannon now carries in the hands of the Krayzie One. Xavier : “I am the present, and I am the future.... While you, you have to share the past with the rest of these over-rated peons.” It was not Kannon who put the photo on the wall, it may not have even been Duke... But somewhere in PCW, even during the Kannon regime, is someone that respects the past. Respect Kannon doesn’t have. The past he doesn’t care for. Xavier : “I hope you enjoyed landing that punch to my face Jeremy, I hope you savoured the moment your knuckles bruised me... Because you’ll never feel that again. Sitting on your couch watching Jerry Springer to see if your neighbours at on it is the only thrill you’ll feel now.” The frame of the picture is dotted with Kannon’s blood and the image distorted by the broken glass that houses it.... Scientologist’s blood dribbling slowly down the sharp shards. Xavier : “And that Krayzie Death Slam, how did it feel knowing that you’ll NEVER do that move again? All around the world, peons probably rose from their seats... But to see it again, they’ll have to line MY pockets by buying the DVD.” The cocky Green eyes of Kannon look right into the victorious pair of Howard, just after he had finished doing battle with Ivan Stanislav and Frisco. Xavier : “When you left Fusion, it was probably with a sense of pride... Knowing that you had struck a blow and made a point.” The smirk now turns into a conceited smile. Xavier : “Well thank you Jeremy, you made more than one point.... Hopefully when the ratings come in, you’ll have given us at least an extra 1.5 for that segment. Nice going.” Finally the eyes of the Champion are drawn away from those of the legend as the sound of heels approaching is heard and Eleanor walks towards him, a curious look on her face. Xavier : “Come here and look at this, my little Poon Puppy.” The little prep walks to his side, pulling the long, light Brown hair from her face as she looks at the photo. Xavier : “Never again shall we have to worry about him... That photo will be the last time my blood is spilt near him.” Blood still flowing freely from the glass studded wounds in his fist, the Gold Patron Meritorious licks the Red fluid from his knuckle. Xavier : “My blood....” With is un-injured left hand, he takes Ellie~!’s hand and squeezes it tight, reminding himself of what he has. Xavier : “My Love.... “ Then with a smug grin replacing his sneer they guide the camera back to the photo of Howard’s jubilant face. Xavier : “The Krayzie One....” He continues to stare at it, looking past it’s captured moment of jubilation to the fact that Howard will never again set foot back in PCW.... The one thing Kannon and Howard agree on.... Just then, as Xavier nods confidently to himself, the frame drops off the wall and is head crashing on the floor below. Xavier : “NO MORE.“
