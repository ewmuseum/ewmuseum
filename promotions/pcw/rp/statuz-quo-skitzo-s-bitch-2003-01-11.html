It was a sunny day in a shady town, everybody wearin’ nothin’ but a frown. Of all dem niggaz and all dem hos, sittin’ ‘round watchin’ the plants grow…ain’t no muthafuckin’ Hyacinths up in dis bitch, they be growin’ the real shit, AIIIIIIGHT! We turn our attention to a cracka bitch foo’, walkin’ down the street like his paycheck grew, this cracka got a plan up his sleeve, it’s a shame ignorant niggaz be his pet peeve. Skitzo: Down in the Ghetto where nobody goes… Skitzo: There’s a pimp motherfucker, pimpin’ his hos… Skitzo: With a pimp-pimp here and a pimp-pimp there! Skitzo: Pimpin’ hos without a goddamn care! On and on the muthafucka struts, he gotta have a sackful of guts, ‘cos he ‘bout to boot the unbootable, fuck wit’ the unfuckable…he gonna go toe to toe…and fuck up Statuz Quo! Skitzo: Nigga pleeeez! He ain’t got none! Gonna make him my bitch, son! Skitzo: I’ll bust his ho’s cherry, break his ass in half with a dictionary! Skitzo: When he wants sex the sumbitch gotta pay-ah! Skitzo: LOL LOL LOL TRU DAT PLAYA!!! He don’t belong in the hood, the one man brood, he wants to whip Quo real good. He already fucked his hos, smacked a bottle off his nose, a war gonna start up, Quo’s momma be a black-faced Tup! Skitzo: BAA! BAA! BLACK SHEEP MUTHAFUCKA!!! The cracka done stopped, his ears just popped, either brain gone dry or he’s up really high. The next thing white boy knows, he’s standin’ at a door, the halls be littered with skanky whores. Skitzo: Damn son, our boy must be gaggin’ for some STDs… Skitzo: Instead of condoms he uses Anti-Freeze! Skitzo: SUCKAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!! From daylight to lamplight, Skitzo’s in the projects, he inspects, checks, lookin’ for a clue, tryin’ to find Quo’s apartment, wouldn’t you too? Skitzo: No… Knockin’ on the front, he’s ready to take the brunt, gonna get ready to call out dis cunt! He ain’t all dat, just a fuckin’ black prat, he ain’t got shit, his sister gotta harder hit, get on his knees and hiss my grit-… Skitzo: SSSSSSSSSSSSSSS-So this is it, time to rumble… Skitzo: You boys get ready to toss and tumble! Skitzo: He gotta limp wrist, talks a lotta pish… Skitzo: Tho’ he’s fuckin’ petrified of the Queen’s English! Footsteps on the landing, our boy stays standing, comin’ up the stairs be the-… Skitzo: Spanish Inquisition? Skitzo: Check ya position, them boys be pimps! Skitzo: They hung like shrimps! Skitzo: I don’t see Quo in amongst dem chimps! The cracka cocks his head, these hos be cornfed, country pimps are bling-blingin’, sing-singin’, they REP’N Nebraska TWENTY-FO’ SEVEN!!! The little one approaches, taps Skitzo with his cane, takes our boy to the window lookin’ at a storm drain… Little Pimp: Peep dis cracka, it’s a long way to the ground; you’ll be in little pieces when yer bodies found. Whatchoo doin’ up here, no fear, your kind ain’t welcome! Skitzo: Yo, what rhymes with ‘welcome’? Skitzo: I dunno dude, just start another muthafuckin’ verse! So the pimp backs off, Skitzo’s pissed off, shakin’ his head until it goes red…the muthafucka splits, 12 perfect fits, the pimps got the shits, runnin’ off, like a gang of ‘fraid bitches, hidin’ in the ditches, Skitzo’s finally done and recombines back into one! Skitzo: Muthafucka where’s Quo at? Skitzo: The little fucking rat! Skitzo: He probably knew I’d show, bringin’ my whiteboy flow-… Skitzo looks up at the screen, with a smile so pristine… Skitzo: Remember kids, tampons are NOT projectiles! Skitzo: Let’s start the show, this is worse than fuckin’ X Files, rappin’ like a ho, there still ain’t no sign of Quo! The bitch ain’t showin’, and I’m still flowin’, someone send out an S.O.S.! Then from around the corner, Skitzo hears a funny sound… Statuz Quo: LOL LOL LOL LOL!!! Skitzo: THE BITCH IS IN THE HOUSE!!! Running to the door, spittin’ on the floor, Skitzo kicks the door down and lays his eyes on Statuz Quo! Statuz Quo: Wut da muthafuck?! Skitzo: Yo bitch you jus’ ran out o’ luck! From the sofa stand up ten fuckin’ thugs… Skitzo: Yo yo yo yo yo yoooo!!! I jus’ brought ya’ll some drugs! Statuz Quo: Yo dawg you frontin’??? We were ‘bout to watch fuckin’ Good Will Huntin’! Skitzo: Hold up man, get it through ya head, I fuckin’ hate you man I wish that chu were dead! You ain’t got shit on me, I’ll pin you for the 1-2-3…oh, and then I’ll fuckin’ teach you bout dem A-B-Cs, BIATCH! Statuz Quo: Ho, gimme one good reason, fo’ I open on yo’ ass like it’s huntin’ season! Skitzo: Yo, listen up prick, you can get shot up like a hick or you can suck ma dick! Statuz Quo: Man, fuck you, yo’ rhymes getting’ greyer-… BANG! BANG! BANG! Skitzo: LOL! LOL! LOL!!! FALSE DAT PLAYA! He shot up Quo’s ass, left the carcass, turned the gun on Statuz’ thugs, shot them up and took their drugs, then he fucked Quo’s hos, with his dick hung like a horse-… Skitzo: This is better than playin’ fuckin’ PimpWars! Skitzo pulls out, wipes off and tucks up, turns his ass around and sees a sight that makes him jump! A room full of Quos, pointin’ Aks at his ass, the bullets come flyin’…and mark the end of this freestylin’-… -------------------------- Waking up in his bed, Skitzo checks his head, the dumbass dreamt it all, from the pimps to his big fall, even though he wishes so the dream was never real, ho! Skitzo: It don’t matter if it was real or not, I own Statuz Quo like his moms owns dry rot! He’s a pretender, ain’t a contender, hell I bet the little cunt’s a fuckin’ cross-dresser! Skitzo: Fuck I’m tired, that shit was real deep…time to get sum sleep! Skitzo: Ah well shit, wasn’t that just sumptin’? LOL! LOL! LOL! HOLLA BACK YOUNGIN’!!! The End, MUTHAFUCKA X/!
