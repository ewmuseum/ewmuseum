Defining The Alliances

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-21-02 There are fifteen teams in this match and each one is significantly different than the rest. Except there’s one trait that they all have in common: everyone wants to win. Each team and its members will do their best to beat all fifty-six of their opponents. They’re all going after a win here and there isn’t much in their way… just fourteen other teams, five rings, five cages, lots of ladders, and tons and tons and tons of grudges. Tons of grudges. Grudges that stem back years. Back to the dawn of time… of Primetime as the case would be. Grudges between The H.I.T. and Ivan Stanislav, grudges between Xavier Kannon and Darkstar, grudges between Rush Mason and Playaz Inc., they’re all dangerous grudges. They’ll be threats to everyone in the match. They all know it and viewers should as well. Big moments are happening and we’ll all just have to see how it turns out in the end. What will happen? Surely when sixty, count them all, sixty men are in the ring together with some much equipment and so much ferocious anger building in them surprises will happen. Grudges will be put to rest. Others will form. New beginnings and old endings are all upon us as the Alliances hits the airwaves. But what exactly does each team stand for? What makes each team unique? Well, in G.O.D.’s mind it all depends on who is in them and what they will do. In his mind, the only mind that matters, Team RUSH is invincible… Playaz Inc.: Statuz Quo, Dante Jackson, Duzza, and Ali Khadaffi. Basically three guys who wanted to leave their stomping grounds for, “The Big Time,” and one… well, black guy. What is Duzza really? He isn’t much of anything really. He’s a good wrestler, but he doesn’t have that extra ‘umph.’ He just doesn’t have the drive… in G.O.D.’s mind. Playaz Inc. are of no threat to Team RUSH. RUSH will just have to show Quo and Jackson what they’ve missed out on. See what the say after Alliances, won’t we? The Celestials: Ooooh, scary! Magic and powers and voids, oh my! A Neo Void God against a real G.O.D. Let’s just see what the outcome of this power match will be. JJ & His Three Manly Men: M! M COMES AFTER L IDIOT! Don’t Annoy The Front Office: Ha! This is just putting one guy who should be in the Pay-Per-View anyway! But David Arquette won’t be able to carry this team to victory alone. Kano & Three Others: If they win I’ll give everyone in the match $10,000. You can quote me on that. I don’t spend money. Hell, I hope they win so I can get rid of my paycheck! 2 x 2 = 4: Really? I didn’t know that. Team CTMB: What fed are they representing? I’ve never heard of a Wrestling Tattler fed named Chronic Tuberculosis and Molesting Baby Wrestling. Mismatch: Please don’t let them win. Please don’t let them win. Please don’t let them win. Please don’t let them win. Please don’t let them win. Please don’t let them win. Please don’t let them win. Please? Rush & The Extreme Crackas: The name says it all. Team Bastardos: Kano & Three Others… same deal here. Team International 2: They won’t win, they’ve got the Front Office after them. If they win they’ll all be fired or something and their egos are way too big for that. The Front Office: They won’t win, they’ve got the Team International 2 after them. If they win they’ll all be fried by them or something and their egos are way too big for that. Team FSW: Boda? Way past the term ‘has-been.’ Tyler Nelson? Meh… him I could see, but with the team he was given, what can he really do? “Five Star” Mike Marvelous? Those teeth won’t get him that far in this match. Timo Bolamba? SILENCE SAMOAN! Team OSW: Many say this team is the most promising. Big Ivan Stanislav. Amazing White Mexican. Agile Tempest. Young Kid Wonder. What do they have that no one else has? Size? Greatness? High flying technique? … High flying technique? Well, when the match is over I’ll go up to each of them and ask if they felt the RUSH! Team RUSH: One word: Winners. G.O.D. goes into this match with a mindset like no other. He goes in knowing that he has Tyler Valentine, Livewire, and Matt Elia right beside him. He goes in knowing that he will ultimately be bigger, better, and stronger than everyone in this match. He goes in knowing… Everyone at Alliances will feel the wrath of G.O.D.
