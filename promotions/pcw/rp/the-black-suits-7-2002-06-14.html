The Black Suits [7]

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 14, 2002 The Agency HQ Time Unknown The lights shimmered as I walked underneath them through the mysterious corridors of The Agency's HQ. They each seemed to tell me to turn back - that I didn't belong here. Oddly enough, I agreed with the lights. It felt weird agreeing with an inanimate object, but then again, I didn't exactly consider myself normal. Or my life, for that matter. After all, I was roaming around some unknown facility, tracking down a rogue butterfly alien. Thats right, I was tracking the alien, not vice versa. I had to stop for a moment, and consider in actuallity what I was doing. Let's see: a) The alien was stronger than me b) The alien was probably smarter than me c) The alien could kill me and d) I'm just a wrestler for god's sake. Oh man, I was way in over my head. Thankfully, my sense of judgment was never that good, so I continued on. The sirens blared every two minutes, alerting all of the members of The Agency to the emergency. Strangely enough, I still hadn't seen any agents yet on my walk thus far. I would think, considering that the security of the facility was at stake, that someone would be good enough as to be patrolling. I wasn't sure what direction I was going in, whether it be towards the middle of the facility or towards the exit. In the back of my head, I was hoping the exit, because nothing would thrill me more than to be rid of this nightmare I called my life. It had been one hell of a week, that's all I knew. Even though I wanted to leave, something told me that I had to stay. Was it my conscience? No, my conscience gives me morals, not a death wish. Was it my sense of duty? Duty to who? The agents that strapped me to a stretcher and wanted to erase my memories? Was it my manhood? Bingo. Okay, maybe it was a bit too macho to say that my manhood was driving me to fight this thing. But hell, the truth sometimes hurt. Looking back at Sporty and I's history, I think we were destined to butt heads and finally collide in one epic battle. The first time I had ever even see Sporty, he had busted in on my lunch, tore up the joint, and then proceeded to completely beat my ass. It was fairly embarrassing. I tried to shoOt the guy, but the damned gun turned out to be a water pistol which some snot nosed kid dropped. There's nothing more idiotic than firing a Super Soaker 2000 at a two hundred pound alien bug. If you don't understand that, I wouldn't blame you. It's one of those things you have to experience, although I must admit, it was fairly comical to see the guy writhing around being soaked. Of course, he....err, it didn't think it was very funny, especially when he crashed into the tables underneath him. Eh, you'll see no remorse on my face. Then we met again, this time in the kitchen of the restaurant. I had appeared threatening at first, or at least I hoped so, wielding a butcher knife and a death wish. Unfortunately, I suppose I hadn't considered the man's...err, it's super human strength and agility. I knew about it's strength, but I suppose that it is always coupled with some other extraodinary ability, huh? Bully for me. So suffice it to say, he kicked my rear all around the kitchen until all I could defend myself with was a head of lettuce. Now, that is down right pathetic. Even the agents saw me holding the head of lettuce, which only made me look worse. Hell, I was a wrestler, not a superhero, but I still had a bit of pride. Now the last confrontation we had was only a few moments ago, only it wasn't quite a fair fight. I was strapped down to a stretcher, and he was running around playing Butterfly man from the Planet X. In all of the struggle, he knocked over my stretcher. Sure, he didn't assault me directly, but the symbolism was still there, don't you think? Well, that was going to change right now. I was tired of being beaten and battered, and every man had his boiling point. I was so hot, you could probably stick me in a pool and boil eggs. I looked forward, not really seeing anything else around me except more winding corridors. I began to get worried. At this pace, I wouldn't be able to even find the flying menace until it was too late. Now, let's get something straight here - I was doing this for The Agency. Sure, I wanted to make sure that butterflies didn't rule the world, but I also wanted to be the one to make the ninth hour save. Even if it meant dying in the process? My, I am becoming your ultimate fan favorite, aren't I? Another concern that was beginning to get a rise out of me was the fact that I had no idea where I was going. Uumph...I already mentioned that a few seconds ago, but it's still a concern. I remember being stuck in that room when I first awoke, and how it's circular nature made me sick to my stomach. Well, these corridors seemed to curve and bend as well...the only problem was, I didn't whether it was curving in or curving out. I was in trouble, for sure. But I did have a gun - that was my saving grace. I felt like I had a fighting chance now, all because of a semiautomatic weapon in tow. Now, that says something about your ego. Smoke began to arise from the corridor ahead, and I pointed my gun forward to catch whatever was coming out of the smoke. I wouldn't take any chances now...not when some huge butterfly man could have caused all of the damage I was seeing in front of me. I approached the cloud of smoke slowly, trying to open all of my senses to decipher what was in front of me. That was when a figure jumped out at me, nearly causing me to fire the weapon. It was a good thing I hesitated when I did, because it turned out that I wasn't in danger after all. A man dressed in a black suit fell out of the cloud, coughing and falling to the ground. His hair was a mess, but then again, with these guys, what else was new? He had fresh bruises on his face and a trickle of blood flowed freely from his lip. He struggled to breathe, and it appeared as though he was getting ready to give up. I put my gun down, trying to get the guy to wake up. I slapped him around a bit, and he began to sob. I suddenly realized that he probably thought I was Sporty, just trying to torture him. I calmed him down, telling him that I was here to help and to tell me what had happened. When he finally stopped crying, he explained the entire situation to me. Sporty had come by this way, using the elevator to make it to the underground chambers. That, apparently, is where the crystal was being stored until it could transported off of Earth. Sporty fought his way through the security barricade, and forced open the elevator doors. The guard had tried to stop Sporty, but what can you do against a pissed off butterfly? Not very much, I knew that from experience. At the same time, he said the last thing he saw was Agent Chopsticks and Agent Sourpuss flying down the elevator shaft after Sporty. I asked him dubiously, 'Flying?' He replied that he was using that as a descriptive metaphor. Oh, silly me. I looked up, seeing a few other bodies lying on the ground. I wasn't sure if they were dead or not, and I didn't plan on finding out. I may be a wrestler, but I wasn't tough enough when it came to dealing with dead bodies. I was a bit squeamish to say the least. I asked the man where the other agents were, and he told me that they were ordered out of the building. Apparently, only Agents Sourpuss and Chopsticks were responsible for taking out Sporty, dead or alive. If they were unsuccessful, the Agency HQ would be going up with a bang. That included Sourpuss, Chopsticks, Sporty, and myself. That didn't sound like a gun. I suddenly had another reason to help take out Sporty, because I was certain I wouldn't be able to find my way out of here before a winner was decided. I suppose I would be the factor to tip the scales. I finally asked the man where I could find some weapons, and he handed me a keycard. He told me there was a weapons locker on my right, and I could find an assortment of weapons to use against the creature. I thanked the man, letting him go. His head slipped back, and he suddenly went unconscious. I was pretty sure he wasn't dead, but he would be, if we didn't stop Sporty. The weapons locker turned out to be closer than I thought. It was right in front of the elevator shaft, and I made my way inside to arm myself. I did have a pistol, but I was rather certain that it wouldn't be enough to take out Sporty. I was actually willing to bet good money on that. The locker held an assortment of guns, from high powered rifles, to elegant elephant guns which could probably cut me in half. It was a tough choice, but I decided on a semiautomatic rifle, a uzi, and a can of raid. The semiautomatic rifle was for long distance attacking, which I realized would be rather useless considering how fast it moved. But still, I needed some sort of long range attack, and the rifle would do. The uzi was a bit more elaborate, but if things got desperate, I would at least be able to start spraying bullets, no matter how many he could take. And of course, I had always wanted to use an uzi. Then there was the can of Raid. Now, you're probably thinking...why the can of Raid? Well, it was pretty simple - the guy's a butterfly, but even a butterfly is a bug. When all else fails, I was going to spray the son of a gun. It bordered embarrassing, much like spraying it with the Super Soaker, but hopefully I wouldn't have to resort to using it. I left the weapons locker, breathing deeply, and realizing that I was coming closer and closer to an inevitable end. Either I would come away from this stronger than ever, or I would die here and now. I didn't like the choices, but it wasn't like I had a choice anyways. I had the rifle slung over my shoulder, with the pistol and uzi in my belt strap, and the can of raid hanging inside my pocket. I felt like an ill-equipped solider, but I also felt dangerous. I had never held a rifle in my hand - I felt like I had an odd power all of a sudden. I reached the elevator shaft, to see the doors ripped off and fallen on the ground. I stepped on it, making way to the shaft's side. This was obviously Sporty's work - hell, it even smelled like him. I looked down the shaft, seeing a three or four story drop. The buttons to call the elevator back up and been ripped up and destroyed....how had Sourpuss and Chopsticks gotten down there? That's when my eyes noticed the cord which was used to hoist the elevator in the air. That's what they had used to get down...even the old geezer. Oh jesus christ...I didn't have time for this. That's when I realized that I really didn't have time for it, and that I needed to get down there. I closed my eyes, taking a deep breath, and grabbed the cord with both hands, sliding down the cord. It felt cold and metallic on my skin, and while it burned a bit going down, I was just praying that it didn't snap. Thankfully it didn't, I would reach the top of the elevator car with little problem. The escape hatch on the elevator was already propped open, and I slid down it quickly. The thud I made as my feet hit the ground made me clinch the gun tighter to my chest. I realized that I had made too much noise, and too much noise attracts too much attention - especially from dangerous butterfly bugs. I was slowly running out of cute pet names for Sporty - he would have to die soon.
