Reality Check

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today Williams: Doesn’t anybody make normal promo’s anymore? Alf: Shhhh I’m trying to watch the credits. -The lights turn on and we see “The Malice Man” Duke Williams and Alf sitting on chairs watching the recent PCW promos on a flat screen TV that hangs on the wall. Duke just shakes his head as Alf gobbles down popcorn dropping it everywhere. The room is not very large with a few recliner chairs scattered around it. The TV is on the center wall and photos of Duke Williams in the ring hang freely along the rest. The temperature is seventy degrees and some other useless details include the peeling wall paper, a picture of Merle Haggard, and a Tivo.- Williams: What ever happened to reality man?? -Duke opens up a bottle of Jim Beam and downs about half of it with out any ill effects.- Williams: I mean in my day we would strive for reality!! What happened to that element? Alf: Face it Duke you a dinosaur!! Say, do you have anymore of those processed cheese snacks? -Duke hands Alf a bag of Cheesy Poofs he begins to immediately dig in.- Williams: You won’t find anything unreal in my promo’s……I relate with the people I don’t try to con them. ALF: Ahem! -Duke looks at Alf.- Williams: Yea but I found you on the street that doesn’t count. I don’t get messages to meet you at the donut shop. ALF: If your buying, that isn’t a bad idea. Williams: How can you think of food at a time like this. Alf: I just do its not really hard. Williams: I’m a damn traditionalist I don’t need this crap. Alf: Lighten up! Maybe you could get some powers and fly in your next promo. Maybe you could smoke a doobie on the roof of the White House like ole Willie did? Williams: Only Willie Nelson could do that. Don’t people look at these phony promo’s and say damn that’s gay. Boy is that fake! I mean who the hell really believes that Legend Inc is having a meeting with the Joker, or that Miya has a Cave in his Mansion, or that the world really would let Kannon and Ellie get married and run the risk of bringing little Kannon’s or Cunt Rags into the world? How about a little reality man!! Lets get back to the real world hoss!! -Alf grabs the remote and starts to watch a rerun of Mr. Belvader- Alf: Yea….right on…..realism!! Williams: You dig what I’m saying don’t you? Alf: HA HA HA that Wesley is such a trouble maker HA HA HA!!! -Duke walks over and turns the TV off.- Alf: Hey I was watching that. Williams: Tough! Grab my shades we’re going for a ride. -The scene changes to Duke in his broken down pick-up truck doing eighty in a forty five. The scenery flies by as he cruises down the road. Amazingly after a half a bottle of booze he’s not drunk. Duke is doing thirty five over the limit yet he will not be pulled over.- Williams: Before you came along I’ve never fibbed in one of these promos and now I’m on a mission to bring the realism back to wrestling. Alf: Are we going to stop at Denneys? Williams: They don’t allow your kind in there. Alf: What are you talking about they have gray steaks and kids with nose rings hang out there. Williams: They don’t let fur balls in. ALF: Willie I think your going off the deep end. Say what about fish? Can we stop at Long John Silver? Williams: Jesus you talk a lot. ALF: I wouldn’t talk so much if I had food in my mouth. -Duke turns the wheels and slams the breaks and just manages to miss three cars while sliding into the McDonalds drive through. With in three minutes they are back on the road and Alf is downing some McNuggets.- Williams: Did you see that Alf? Realism!! We went got food the way normal people do. Very real stuff if you ask me. Alf: Yea…yea…yea….real….you got your food in three minutes….the order was right….your driving with a puppet. Go on please. Williams: Are you saying this isn’t very real? Alf: You’re the one doing all the talking. I’m trying to eat. -Alf is no working on the fri’s- Alf: You know what’s not real? Williams: I’m afraid to ask. Alf: They call these things French fri’s but they don’t come from France? That’s f*cked up. HA Williams: Will you please be serious!! This is a major problem. I need a drink. -Duke is cruising down the road when he reaches into the glove box and pulls out the Jim Beam bottle and drinks down the other half.- Williams: Maybe the PCW could call a meeting and we could stress realism and creativity. You know you could be creative without being unreal. Alf: Do you have any ketchup in here? Williams: Yea in the glove box. -Alf opens up the glove box and the shoot changes to inside a refrigerator filled with booze and boxed food. We see a furry hand reach in and grab a beer and some ketchup. We then see Alf pulling the stuff out of the glove box.- Williams: We can form the council of realism. I could become president of that! Alf: Yea, cus we all know the bang up job you’ve done as PCW president. -A hint of sarcasm is in Alf’s voice.- Williams: Yea right on! Alf: You hungry? Williams: I could go for a pork chop. -Alf reaches into the glove box and pulls out a pork chop and a left over box of pizza.- Willimas: Thank you. Back to forming my speech on reality. Alf: Oh here we go. Williams: You know what screw this lets go to Red Lobster. I’m sick of ranting. Alf: There is a God!! Williams: You know the reality of things is this Sunday I will be crowed the PCW Continental Champion!! Alf: You guys wear crowns?? That’s gay. Williams: Its really a belt. Alf: Then why are you talking about crowns? Williams: Never mind! Alf: Whatever HA! Williams: This Sunday the reality will kick in when you kiddies step into the ring with a trained veteran. A veteran who will find no fear in a double cage. I veteran who will walk out the PCW Continental Champion. Just as sure as the day is long and OJ is guilty. I will dominate and win this match. I will do it in the name of realism. No fear, no regrets, just pure, unadulterated Malice. This Sunday it’s Malice Time. The rest of you are going Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound! Alf: Right one Hoss!!! -Duke pulls into the Red Lobster parking lot as the camera fade.-
