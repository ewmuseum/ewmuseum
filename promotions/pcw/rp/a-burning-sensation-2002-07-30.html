A Burning Sensation

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th July The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#1 for Turmoil (31st July 2002)… Chapter Two: A Burning Sensation… From PCW Wrestle Vessel – 28th July: Dave: We’ve got an inferno blazing, and Chase is about to feel what Hell is like. Jeff: He’s going to Utah? Clyde picks up Chase’s body, and brings the man up to the top turnbuckle. He places Chase onto his shoulders, but somehow Chase starts to come back into consciousness, and he begins to punch at Clyde!!! Dave: CHASE IS SOMEHOW STILL IN IT!! Jeff: Yeah until right about now. However, Clyde doesn’t even pay attention the punches BUT HE TAKES OFF FROM THE TURNBUCKLE IN POSITION FOR THE SHATTERING BUT SOMEHOW CHASE SLIPS OUT AND TURNS THE MOVE INTO THE FINAL ANALOGY THROUGH THE FLAMING TABLE AND THE GLASS PANE!!!!!!!!!!! Dave: HOLY MOTHER OF GOD!! Jeff: HOLY HUBBARD ON A STICK!! Duke: JESUS CHRIST MARY AND JOSEPH!!! JJ: Whoa…like…HOLY SHIT! Somehow, Clyde landed on top of Chase and JJ counts, trying to see amongst the blaze. 1…2…3!!!!!!! Winner: CLYDE - - - - - - - - - Romans 11: 30-32 Just as you who were at one time disobedient to God have now received mercy as a result of their disobedience, so they too have now become disobedient in order that they too may now receive mercy as a result of God’s Mercy to you. For God has bound all men over to disobedience so that he may have mercy on them all… - - - - - - - - - This is the story of a man who sinned, repented, sinned, repented again. An Epic tale, spanning the course of at least a year, starting from this very moment. Sure, we are over baking this, but it’s for YOUR own good. Your own good, you can’t decide what’s good for you or not – you’re not mature enough to do that…the god’s of your television tell you what to do – how to react. You’re nothing more than repeats of the same mould, clones of each other on the thought level. You like the same things, you do the same things, and you wear the same clothes. God left you a long time ago – he left you for a prettier, younger race with larger bosoms and a tighter stomach. His legacy lives in the mould-breaker – the sinner-repenter man, unbeknownst to the rest of society. Living in the land of the Canuck, this man possesses all the otherworldly traits capable of raising him from the dead time and time again. He’s a man who tends to be easily led, he needs a leash. Freedom’s too much of a cliché for him, as is the continual pursuit for higher living. Madness is never far away; it dances and gyrates temptingly in front of this individual – the immaculate temptation for the Savior-Fiend. His conscience lives in the land of the confused, constantly spanked by a midget with a tuning fork – an obsession that even the scruffily dressed kids on the door won’t admit to. Sure, a confusing analogy is better than no analogies at all – it’s better to be confused than to be as plain as day after all. This plethora of confusing diction and spiraling analogies can only lead to one conclusion of them all. Someone’s got something on his mind. Like a Privateer on the Spanish Main, the very effect of this someone is staggering – like the roller-coaster ride of the shanty sea tunes of old. It’s perplexing that this man stands at a crossroads with seven or eight paths…when it would usually be something for a simpleton to decipher. It’s become a question akin to that of the infernal meaning of life – and why we don’t live on Uranus. Maybe God decided against homoerotic overtones or toilet jokes – maybe that’s telling those that commit such unnatural acts – particularly the toilet jokes. Words were meant to be misunderstood. That’s why dictionaries aren’t any fun. The diction on show here could be accompanied by explanatory footnotes – but then again – that wouldn’t be any fun at all. Someone’s got something on his mind. Saving the world never did seem the cheap option. Billions of pounds were spent in the making of movies to illustrate such points. To save the world, you’d need a good 8-10 overpaid actors, CGI special effects and a plethora of costumes – along with a clichéd script complete with the odd cheesy one-liner. “Do they ever give up?” says one of the actors, “Give up what?” said his slow-witted sidekick. Following a burning frown, the first actor would reply with “With trying to save the world with their CGI Aliens?”, and his accomplice would reply with “Do you want fries with that?” It’s funny when you think about it. Saving the World was always one of those things that seemed to be locked in continual battle with the realms of fiction. Every once in a while, the Bravado kid questions this. The self-proclaimed World saver – maybe it is fiction after all. How can the events of a wrestling federation be tied in with the fate of the world? Well…someone’s got something on his mind. To pay to view is no sin of yours; you have the freedom to do so. Not that you people appreciate your freedom, you continually abuse your “right” to free speech. Yes, you can talk…but really, is it really appropriate for you to blabber on like that? Will you stop whining? Stop that damn yelling! - - You’ve never been better nominees for piranha chow; you’ve never been duped as badly as this in your lifetimes. You’d believe anything you were told. By this moment, you’re already believing that we’re correct…that we’re revealing the faults of the human race. So that’s why we sent the Bravado Kid, you may already know of our plot to save the world from the world saving movie genre and the trait that plagues the rest of the human race – the fact that you’re easily duped. Has the Bravado Kid, otherwise known as the Sinner-Repenter or Savior-Fiend, really been sent to save you all…or only for the progression of our existence in the eternal scheme of things. The emaciated tapestry of fate’s a mess, the equivalent of a jumper sewn by your grandmother for the Holiday season. Happy Holidays. The Bravado Kid took a hammering. A beating with Bravado is no shame. In fact, it’s something that we’re somewhat proud of, of our candidate for global acknowledgement of progression. Progression after all, does come with setbacks. Do you think god would be so boring as to come up with the infallible progression of mere mortals? Sheesh, and I thought the apes were tough to educate. The gibbons made light work of understanding this philosophy, the Tigers managed to gorge themselves on the very meat of the topic. And the Dolphins got through the course swimmingly. The diversity of the human species means that you’ll all fail to realize the true potential of this very concept. Progression with failure was always the main goal. It was always brought up in company meetings. Just ask the Dinosaurs and their mocking bird descendants. Our man sacrificed himself for failure. You can learn from failure. Learning from victory is impossible. It makes it easier for you to lose if you carry on blindly. The Bravado Kid’s learnt that not everything makes it the right way up. Sometimes the toast lands on the wrong side. And sometimes the real winner is the man who loses at first… The Bravado Kid dawns on this for the first time: “NO! NO! FUCKING NO! What did I tell those idiots about what to do with junk mail? Well, to tell you the earnest truth, I told them to fashion clothes for the homeless with the seemingly indestructible paper from which they’re fashioned. Double-Glazing? Reader’s Digest Competition info? Losing weight on a low budget?” “Ooh! The Book’s Direct Complementary catalogue – the very bible of the book-collecting enthusiast! Do you realize how truly blessed we are to be given a copy of this glorious thing? Only another several million people are ever given the right to order books through the Internet, mail or phone. “Exorcizing Demons for Idiots” seems like a worthy purchase to me…” “Seems a little worthless now that I managed to exorcize the bastard thing. Puzzling that, how I was able to do that without any previous knowledge or experience of ridding demons…” “Indeed it is…Chaserino! Now then, excuse me whilst I go drool after the lady on the front cover of the catalogue. I’ll only be a few minutes…” “Go right ahead you excitable idiot…how’s that idiocy coming by the way?” ::. Chris Chase looks a little more impressive this way. He’s been the extravagant sort for a while now, refreshingly different from those also-rans that could be compared to himself. The Wrestle Vessel’s vanished into thin air for the time being – set sail for the far flung regions of this world, where we’re safe from the annoying screaming of some crucified Scientologist. We find our awesome butcher boy at a seedy motel somewhere in the vicinity of Miami, Florida. Plant life’s all too common around here, not that it’s all that respectable. These plants spent too much time tinkering with the Vice girls of the district – a delectable hobby for those concerned with the form of stem and leaf. Those damn seductive Lilies. Chase never received all that much religious education – so much for Canadian Curriculum. At least that explains for his intolerable defamation to Christianity and Judaism of the last six to eight months. Riding on the name of ‘The Messiah’ meant that Chris rode the religious bus into superstardom, only to blow it at the very beginning of his Global reign… That brought about a gradual but distinct change in the way that he was perceived post returning. Being fairly unlucky recently is testimony to this distinct change, and until the transformation is complete, the ever-impressive youngster’s going to have to not rely on pure luck alone any longer. It’s 1996 in his head again. Six years ago, his formative days were just beginning. He was the new kid in an area renowned for a quality, yet all too predictable output. Then he came along and burnt the rulebook. Sure, he was intoxicated at the time, but he meant well all the same. Six years on, and he’s getting ready to do the same. His unfortunate position after delivering a desperation move on recent foe Clyde meant that he had no chance of ever recovering to win the match on Sunday. Not that he cares now. Something’s on his mind again. #1 Contender’s matches were always his forte…Xavier Kannon’s the World Champion. And you know just what Chris Chase thinks about Xavier Kannon, don’t you? This is why he’s not narrating today – he’d blabber on about that for half an hour – caring not for descriptive detail… It’s yet again, a sweltering day in the world around Chris Chase – not that he lives in an air-conditioned void, devoid of any stroke rendering heat. Our hero receives an abundance of junk mail; perhaps you could call it a plethora. It’s enough to bury a man under, with the vast amounts of useless snippets that he receives. Even at places where he doesn’t even live. Not that this is a major factor in the whole situation. That’s just to throw you off. Chris sighs, glancing around the poorly decorated and sorry state of his room. He left his wallet on the Wrestle Vessel, and as some glorified monkey helps himself to all manner of gyrating women and alcoholic beverages, Chris is left with nothing except an under repaid credit card and sixty-two Canadian Dollars. Strictly speaking, that isn’t exactly much. A ceiling tile begins to peel itself off of its anchored position in the corner of the ceiling. Chris himself bears a few scars from his battle with Clyde on Sunday. A scratch here, a couple of bruises there. He’s a quick healer, and he’ll have to be. With his hands placed firmly on his hips, he shakes his head whilst glancing down at the pile of junk mail strewn all over the floor….:: Chris: Sheesh, they could have supplied a complementary dustpan if manual labor was going to be involved. These seedy motel managers have no respect for the powers of God… Dustin: ~(In the Distance)~ Still blabbering on about God? Well, hitting your finishing maneuver through a glass pane and a flaming table wasn’t exactly the best idea you’ve ever had; in fact, it possibly rivals the time I asked you to throw baseballs for me… Chris: ~(Sighs)~ Meh…the cretins that clean up this joint can deal with this… ::. Turning his back to the pile of junk mail, seemingly ignoring its presence in his own, Chris Chase pulls on the trailing ends of his shirt collar without so much as flinching from his stare. Blinking, his eyes begin to flitter from to and fro, idly observing his surroundings with a cynical eye. Snorting with disapproval, he runs his hand through his hair as Dustin Downey – the ever-moronic PCW Referee, enters the room again, replete with a band of misguided fool Satanists. Chris was never a fan of Satanists…heck, they weren’t too fond of him either – especially those Mexican Satanists who ran around summoning Demon Chickens that Dustin knows only too well about… If I can remember rightly, it was the incident where the dynamic duo known as High Impact at that time visited Mexico City in search of higher inspiration – if only for Dustin to be tarred and feathered and chased around the city. Of course, the quick thinking Chase punked out Dustin, and dragged off the unconscious body of the half-wit. It’s raining outside. It’s been raining for five minutes right now. It’s also raining where “The Handler” resides, but he’s too pissed off to comment. I think he’s crazy. (Yawn) The spontaneity of it all astounds the likes of a perfectly normal narrator. Or, in total context to the scene at hand, the lack of it. It’s a gentle pace, a contradiction to the man of the story. Refreshingly, the air conditioning seems to be working, much like the effect of Chris Chase on the general public. Exposure was always great in the right context, and form. Slumping into a slightly uncomfortable chair, Chris reaches across to a pendant, flicking it into his own hand before passing it over his own head…ignoring Dustin and the trio of Mexican Satanists….:: Dustin: Hey! Look who I found hanging out in the corridor setting fire to some old lady’s pair of briefs…it’s those crazy midget guys that punked me out in High Impact episode #27. Don’t you remember that at all, Chris? ::. Chris pokes a glance upwards at his former Tag Partner and the diminutive Satanists, and in a nonchalant way, simply sighs….:: Chris: Why should I even bother? They’re Satanic, Mexican, and Midgets. Do you seriously think that I could exist in the same room as these blasphemers or even possibly tolerate it? Dustin: Awwwww, Come on! Look at them, they look so damned sad…it’s almost a fake attempt in order to infiltrate the realm of the Saint, from within they can control the very things we do and even put an end to your connections with the man upstairs… ::.~(Musing of Chris Chase)~ To think that I needed reminding of my imminent match-up with those random bunch known as El Diablo, Akiyama and Rent-A-Villain – Great, that’s all I need Post-PPV Hell. Pretence aside, I’m positive that the swift return to that squared circle place would mean instant success. But that doesn’t stop the scaremongers from gathering like a pack of ravenous seagulls, feeding off the very life of my World Title dreams. After battling my way back into this narrator position once more, I’ve got an opportunity to laugh, mock and thoroughly berate Xavier Kannon’s World Title win. No…don’t sigh, don’t even begin to pass it off as some random threat towards that Scientological bozo. People find me hard to approach these days…I hate that. A Saint for better or for worse, that’s what I am now. These past few days I’ve been inconsolable after that loss to Clyde. Going out in the way I did was enough to satisfy my quest for eternal progress. What, surprised? Did I become that unpredictable bastard again? Well…yeah. Your baby’s coming home! To a lesser degree, I’ve been waiting for a reason. A reason to mellow out into this slightly less volatile guy, this brilliant person who actually tolerates the strange and wonderful. It’s reasonable…but my profession, my mission disallows it. In fact, I’ve got to embrace this concept as the Bravado Kid. I was great once. I was brilliant before. Nothing changed there. Once in a while, whilst I was on “Vacation”, I imagined myself and the PCW World Title alone. Alcohol didn’t have anything to do with what was going on at the time – it was just a vacation from the “Vacation”… ~(Narration)~ Two Hours Later…in a backwater suburb, there’s a commotion waiting to happen. Two children throw stones at a car, the car’s that of an ousted person of the Void persuasion – nothing too surprising there. The place reeks of Akiyama here; it’s the NVG armor…undoubtedly. Funny, NVG was always considered to refer to Night Vision Goggles. Maybe Akiyama’s revealing something about his own Night Opus, the kind of suspicious dealing that he seems to partake post-event. There’s a howling from behind a closed garage door…maybe it’s a dog. Our camera’s pan, and finds the self-proclaimed Celestial Cowboy and his moronic chum standing on the sidewalk…grins firmly engraved on their faces. The rain’s stopped now, as a single-engine aircraft drifts a good two thousand feet above the all too familiar duo. What are they grinning about? Well…I don’t want to spoil the surprise….:: Child No.1: NEO VOID FREAK! Child No.2: NO ONE WANTED YOUR SORT AROUND HERE! FUCK OFF BACK TO JAPAN! ::. Chris chuckles to himself….:: Dustin: What’s so funny this time? Did Jesus crack the one about the midget and the deranged Parking Warden, or did God manage to destroy Iraq by blowing his nose again? Chris: Of course not, my moronic and highly embarrassing chum. It’s the thought of these kids, the language they pick up these days and the fast approaching platoon of Panzer Tanks. Seriously though…it’s the thought of the Void. I always considered myself to be forever a part of that place…then, Akiyama shows up, decrees Blasphemy on me, denounces the Void as his realm, and goes Pseudo-Japanese on us all… ::.A puzzled look is directed at Chase by his comical comrade, who holds up a figure of this dread locked mofo dressed up in an Astronaut’s suit, grinning in a boyish and immature manner….:: Dustin: Surely there’s nothing wrong with that…I mean, Pseudo-Japanese merchandise rocks the known Universe. Lookee at this Ghetto Astronaut figurine complete with miniature blowtorch and space helmet. His backpack’s even reminiscent of a Ghetto Blaster. GO GHETTO MAN GO! Chris: I don’t think you quite get the idea. Pseudo-Japanese must be stopped at all costs – you seriously don’t want a dozen Miyagawas and Akiyamas running around do you? Heck, if I don’t do something, Pseudo-Japanism will take over the world, we’ll enter a world packed with neon lights and two-foot long cubicles…and I’ll have to refer to you as Dustin-san. It’s damned wrong. Two-foot long cubicles are TOO DAMN restricting for humanity…and besides, when since is a Ghetto Astronaut Pseudo-Japanese? Dustin: Since it was made in Japan, silly…what’s with the Vitriolic overtones today, Chasio? TO BE CONTINUED!!
