The Talk. The Kidnapping Part 7 [Finale, thank God]

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-03-03 (Ha) Eddy, quite pleasantly surprised when he walked outside in his powder blue bathroom and lifted up the morning paper had known, officially that his sister was now safe that Bob Vurdo had died. Eddy was not happy that Bob Vurdo died, for Eddy never liked it when anybody dies, unless it was Hitler, but his sister was safe from pain and Eddy had no worry of searching anymore. It was merely 6 AM at the time, Eddy frying eggs over the stove he had cracked after he looked at the paper, a had began making some bacon earlier which was almost done now. Sneaking a strip to eat, Eddy lifted the greasy strip and ate it, not wanting any bacon any more because of how the grease could effect your heart. The eggs were still cooking, and Eddy opened the fridge for a glass of refreshing orange juice to start the day. However, the carton was not even full and could support about 1/5 a glass. Eddy sighed, lifted the carton to his lips and drank the remainder as he tossed it into the recycling bin. Milk had caught his eye and he pulled it out and opened a counter and pulled a glass down, tipping the milk over and filling the glass up before putting the milk back in. Eddy, about to sit down, noticed his sister, dressed unlike Eddy who was in his robe, and she was still very beautiful as always. She had a blue blouse and black pants. She had eye liner, some red lip stick and maybe something else on her face that Eddy didn’t really know because he didn’t know any other make up really. 'Morning, sleepy head', said Eddy smiling. 'Hey', said his sister, Melissa who pulled a chair out and sat down, smiling of course as she did it. 'Wow, it’s glad to be back.' 'Yeah, isn’t it? I’ve missed you', said Eddy sitting down with milk in front of him. 'So, tell me, what happened?' 'It’s a long story…', said Melissa. 'I’m ready.' 'Well, this is how it began… I remember seeing you come in one night, and hearing your voice scream my name a few times. I was upstairs doing laundry, when suddenly some man in black swooped under me, grabbed me and started downstairs. I screamed and panicked, hoping you could stop him, but before I knew we were out the window. He ran outside, threw me into his car and threatened me with a gun of some sort, I can’t remember honestly, my ears were so blurry in tears the gun wasn’t answerable. But of course, I nodded and sat still while I remember duct tape going around my eyes and mouth. I started to shake my hands around to be free but the man who had kidnapped me said 'Don’t try to take them off bitch or your head will be off faster then you can say death'. Naturally I was scared, but nodded and he roped my hands around my back. The car was moving by somebody else driving, he talked a few times but I didn’t really know what it was. So, I sat there and waited, eyes covered, mouth covered, arms tied scared to death as I heard random people talking.' 'Hold on, let me check on the eggs…', said Eddy, getting up and heading toward the kitchen. He looked, they were still cooking and weren’t done. Eddy then came back to the table ready to hear the rest of his sister’s story. 'OK, continue.' 'So anyway, I fell asleep and woke up in some room, God, the only thing I remember is the smell, HORRIBLE. Like a gym locker room with some strange rotten egg smell, not a very great smell. Anyway, the took tape off my eyes and mouth, and I was in some small room with moldy walls, I can’t even remember, and across from me was a man named Bob Vurdo' 'Vurdo?! Go on' 'He offered me a joint, but I refused, and said I was going to call the police on him once I was out, and he chuckled and paced around, then I screamed your name and said you would save me, then he laughed and said you were a pussy…' 'Sigh, go on…' 'Eventually he left, I was alone in the room with my hands tied and on a chair. So I sat there pretty much all day, I was let go to pee and they gave me food, mostly shoe leather bland tasting steak and a half eaten carrot. 'OK, how were you found?' 'Simply put, when Bob Vurdo’s head exploded on Fusion, one of his Mafia members was found, and fessed up where I was.' 'Pretty simple.' 'Yeah. I’m hungry. Where’s breakfast?' 'I have the bacon ready. Eggs, maybe. Let me check.' Eddy got up and ambled into the kitchen. The bacon was still warm, the eggs were finished, and everything was okay by Eddy’s standards as he lifted the pan of eggs and forked the eggs over onto a large plate. He forked the bacon onto a different, slightly smaller plate and began to head into dining room. He sat everything down in front of him, and began to eat an egg before he looked down at his wrist below the table, symbolizing he was looking at his watch that was viewable by the camera. Then his eyes shot and he stood up. 'Eddy? Where are you going?' 'I have to go, I planned something about this time.' 'At 6:30 AM?' 'Actually at 7, but it’s a 30 minute drive.' 'What? What is it?' 'I can’t explain ALL the details, but I was suppose to go out and help children, discipline and psychological wise. Anyway, I’ll see you later.' 'Well, bye, I guess. Won’t you eat?' 'It’s OK, I’m not too hungry. Besides, I can always run by Mickey D’s a pick up a breakfast meal. Anyway, see you.' 'Bye.' Eddy, coughing and straightening his blue shirt, made his way out the door, slamming it behind him softly and walking away.
