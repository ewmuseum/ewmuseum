Um… yeah :) [Scene opens in a hospital waiting room.A few people casually mill around the room, while others slump down in the stiff chairs- ergonomically designed…. In 1841. Flaymin and his exotic new valet, Hirraani, fall into this category.Hirraani sits in her chair, looking up at the ceiling in boredom, while Flaymin is busy watching an episode of Bold and the Beautiful on the TV situated in the centre of the room] Hirraani: You know, this place kinda smells. Flaymin: Eh Hirraani: And the seats are awful. Flaymin: Eh Hirraani: Why are you mumbling? Flaymin: Dammit woman, can’t you see I’m watching TV!Ridge is about to get back with Taylor for only the sixteenth time this year- this sort of heart rendering television doesn’t happen everyday, you know. Hirraani: All I see is a middle-aged man in dire need of a haircut and a slutty looking brunette. Flaymin: That foxy looking brunette has hard her heart broken plenty of times this season alone, would it hurt to show a little respect? [Before the thrilling conversation can continue any further, the receptionist calls out to Flaymin] Receptionist: Sir, Doctor Anderson is ready to see you. Flaymin: Tell him to wait until the next commercial break. Receptionist: I’m sorry, but he also has several other patients to see today. Flaymin: Then tell them to wait. [The receptionist shakes her head in dismay.She then reaches under her table and whips out the television remote control.She aims it at the TV and with a single click- the TV turns off. Flaymin angrily gets up and heads into the doctors office, with Hirraani in tow. They walk into the doctor’s office and take a seat.Dr Anderson sips on some coffee and then places the mug on his table] Dr Anderson: Welcome Flaymin, nice to see you could make it today. [Flaymin grunts and then nods his head] Hirraani: So doctor, what’s the prognosis on Flaymin’s testicle? Dr Anderson: Right, right… the testicle. [Doctor Anderson gets up and walks over to his filing cabinet.He rifles through it momentarily before finally pulling out a folder labelled ‘Freaky Ball Popping Accident’.He opens up the folder and takes out a few X Ray slides and hands them to Flaymin] Dr Anderson: This first slide shows exactly what your um… ‘nutsack’ looked like right after the accident. [Flaymin and Hirraani look at the X Ray which shows a gigantic hole in Flaymin’s nutsack] Hirraani: Ouch!That must have been awfully painful. Flaymin: *Sarcastically* Gee, what gives you that idea? Dr Anderson: Now this next X Ray shows what your nutsack looks like right now. [Flaymin looks at the X Ray and raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: There is no difference between the two photos except for the fact that the hole is sealed? Dr Anderson: Exactly.And that’s where the problem begins. Flaymin: Problem?? What happened to the replacement testicle you guys were going to insert?You told me I was going to have Balls of Steel!! Dr Anderson: That was the vision.But the donor pulled out at the last second. It seems Mr Red Rock was unwilling to donate his balls of steel to your cause. Flaymin: DAMN THAT SON OF A BITCH!!! [Flaymin bangs his fists on the table in apparent anger] Dr Anderson: Mr Red Rock said that he required his balls of steel to perform as the main event in a travelling freak show he was working in. Flaymin: Wasn’t our deal legally binding? Dr Anderson: You mean that slurred signature on the back of that empty beer carton? Flaymin: That’s the one!It took me about 20 minutes to forge his signature. [Doctor Anderson rolls his eyes and shakes his head] Dr Anderson: That um ‘contract signing’ isn’t official.I’m afraid that you are now left with three apparent options: 1. Find Red Rock and convince him to donate his balls of steel.2. We can insert a prosthetic testicle.3. You can just live with one ball instead of two. Flaymin: That’s blasphemy!! Hirraani: Do you even know what ‘Blasphemy’ means? Flaymin: Not really, but it sounds good. Dr Anderson: Have you considered the prosthetic testicle? Flaymin: I ain’t having some artificial crap integrating around with the family jewels.It wouldn’t be a good mix….Two distant worlds would collide…A battle would rage for eterni- Dr Anderson: I get the point. I guess that only leaves one option- Find Red Rock and convince him to donate his balls of steel. Flaymin: If that’s what I have to do, then that’s exactly what I’ll do. [Scene fades to black as the Doctor and Hirraani give Flaymin a cautious stare] How will Flaymin find Red Rock’s Freak Show Troupe?More importantly, how will he convince the man to donate his balls of steel? Only time will tell….
