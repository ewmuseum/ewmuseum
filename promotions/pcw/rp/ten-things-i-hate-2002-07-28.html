Ten things I hate

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-28-02 Ten things I hate. The issues talked about in this piece are based on the opinion of Scorpion and not those of the PCW. #10: Reality television. Reality television is number ten on my list because it’s one of the most dumbest concepts for a show out there today. It all started with “The Real World” and “Road Rules” but it took off with “Survivor.” On “Survivor” twelve people where stuck on an island and had to survive or an amount of time. Sounds good right? Wrong. There were doctors on the island incase anything happened. The show should have been called “People stuck on a island doing nothing for thirty days.” The show sucked plain and simple. After the chronicles of Survivor flourished on the television scene then CBS, NBC, FOX, ABC, MTV and E! had to get in the action. CBS creates Big Brother. The concept of the game is to see how long one person can last in a house for three months without any contact in the world and you live with twelve other people. A person gets voted out every week. How exciting are watching people in a house these days? Is that how bad television is today? They should have a warning before the show where they state that “what you are about to witness sucks and shouldn’t be on television but we have no other choice because it’s the summer season and every other show is a re-run.” Once CBS gets in the game NBC has a great idea…why don’t we copy everyone else and make reality television! Brilliant idea! It’s like high school all over again where everyone is smoking marijuana and drinking a twelve pack. NBC gets the concept for “Fear Factor” a television show where every episode someone eats a former body part of a animal or jump off of a building, time and time again. Fear Factor should state before each show that it’s not airing a re-run; they are just doing the same concept over and over again in the same show. So CBS and NBC have reality television going so ABC decides to get in the game and creates “The Mole” a game where people get backstabbed and have to find out who is “The Mole” the person who is backstabbing. The Mole is the only game that comes close to real life because everyone in life backstabs someone in life and this is the only show that resembles that. CBS, NBC and ABC have reality television shows so MTV decides to create a different concept for a reality television show. Shore they already have “The Real World” but let’s create a different real world concept and just use the family of a rock star. Who do they pick, none other then the one the only, Ozzy Osbourne. The same guy that bit off a bat’s head. I’m a fan of Ozzy and it was a great choice by MTV. I thought the show was funny but thanks to “The Osbournes” it opened up the door to E! Television’s new show where the follow former Playboy playmate, Anna Nicole Smith. They follow Anna everywhere from her plastic surgeon to her liposuction physician. How exciting is that! “The Osbournes” was good, I personally don’t need to watch a former hot female who became a overweight pig twenty four seven. CBS, NBC, ABC, MTV and E! all have reality television shows so ABC comes up with the idea of taking a popular British television show where people sing and try to get a contract deal. The concept is called “American Idol.” To bad this concept already happened ten years ago. It was called “Star Search.” Yeah call Ed McMahon he knows what I’m talking about. Reality television sucks plain and simple unless it’s “The Osbournes” and my personal favorite, “Jackass.” People laugh at stupidity and those are the two best shows if you have a interest in doing that. #9: Telemarketers This has to be the worst job in the world. I feel for these people but they ask for it. They call you at all hours of the day and they want to sell you something your absolutely not interested in. They flap their gums for five minutes straight and when they are finally done what do you say, “I’m sorry I’m not interested.” Why what a waste of using your vocal cords. My favorite ones are the chimney cleaners who ask if you want your chimney clean. I just say to them “sorry I don’t have a chimney, gosh I can’t even afford my home.” I then begin to sound like I’m crying, what do they do? They hang up and I’m on the ground hysterically laughing. Telemarketers just piss me off and that’s why they are number nine. #8: Twenty-four hour news channels Twenty-four hour news channels like MSNBC, CNN and FOX News Channel are the dumbest excuses for channels at my home. They should just have one and not three different ones that report the same news time and time again. My favorite Twenty-four hour news channel moment was when the FBI found a “Dirty Bomb.” Well each channel had to tell everyone in the world what the “Dirty Bomb” consists of just incase we wanted to make one of our own and how much damage they can cause if they are detonated. It’s like these channels want the terrorists to attack us so they could have something new to talk about. I hate them and it’s a waste of a channel. #7: Rap artists in movies I have absolutely nothing against the rap genre at all. I think there talent is an unbelievable. I do have a problem with going to a movie theater or renting a movie from the local blockbuster and I get a movie with a rapper in it. They are some of the worst actors/actresses in the world. That movie with Method Man and Redman, “How High” was horrible. It was a stupid concept how many people would believe that two thirty year old men would end up at Harvard University? I surely didn’t. I love their music but this movie was just horrible as are other rap artists in movies. Will Smith is the only that can consider himself an actor because he did a great performance as “The Greatest” Muhammad Ali. If you can’t act don’t bother taking the part cause there are other people in the world that could use the work and have worked their asses off to become a actor. #6: People capitalizing on tragedies This is one thing that really bothers me especially which happened almost a year ago with the attack on America on September 11th, 2001. People all over the place began to raise the prices on American Flags because people were becoming very patriotic and wanted to show their support for their country. People then began to take the FDNY, NYPD and PAPD logos illegally and putting them on hats, shirts or anything that could make money. The vendors knew that people were proud of these heroes’ acts and wanted to show their way of saying “thank you.” So vendors to these logos and put them on anything they could and sold them as high they could get for them so they could take as much money as they could from our pockets and put more money in their pockets. Money is the root of all evil and some people’s greed infested their bodies when this tragedy took place and all I can say on this is “screw you and you will get what’s coming to you.” #5: Over priced athletes This is what are putting leagues in deep water and is taking the public eye off of their sport. These players from all different walks of sports are getting paid in millions and higher to play a child’s game. These paychecks that these players get only feeds there ego which leads to players believing that they are bigger then the sport and so they spit on every fan or every other person that worked there ass off to get the sport where it is today when they hold out on contracts for more money or threaten a strike. These players need to remember that the fans put their hardworking money in their pockets when they go out to pay for a ticket of a curtain sport. Alex Rodriguez gets paid two hundred and fifty million for ten years to play for the Texas Rangers. Now I can think of ten or more baseball players that are better then him and deserve that money more then him. A thing that pisses me off is those players today is that they don’t go all out and more. They half ass everything and they don’t hustle. They could cause there team the whole season when they don’t go all out and most of the times there are curtain franchise players that screw it up for everyone, the franchise that they work for and all there fans across the globe that pay to see there team go all out to win and these players don’t give a rats ass if they don’t win or not anymore because they know that at the end of the day that they will be getting paid a huge sum of money. It’s basically a slap in the face to all their fans and that really ticks me off which leads to my next topic. #4: Major League Baseball The moment that made baseball fans here in America go over the edge was this July’s “Mid-summer classic” the MLB All-Star game. Eleventh inning, New York Yankees skipper Joe Torre runs out of pitchers. They consult with Baseball commissioner Bud Selig about what they should do. What great idea does Bud Selig come up with? Let’s call the game at a tie and send the fans home pissed off after they paid there hard earned money to watch the game live. First off I’m not a big fan of Bud Selig for what he has done for the second greatest hitter of all-time (Ted Williams is first) Pete Rose. Bud Selig has kept Pete Rose out of the Baseball Hall of Fame for twelve years now because Pete Rose bet on Baseball. Shore I don’t agree with what he did but the man has spent a long time out of baseball and they re-instate the man. There are players in the Hall of Fame that have done worse things then Pete Rose and they are in the hall of fame. There are people that have betted on baseball and been caught and they have spent there time. I think Pete Rose has already as well. So you can tell I’m not a big Bud Selig fan anyway. Major League Baseball has pissed me off a lot now with the talk of a strike again. What is it this time are they not getting paid enough or do they just want to make the fans pissed off again and steal another season away from the fans of the game. It pisses me off a lot and that’s why I ranked it number four on my list. #3: Corporate America and how they screw there employees over The perfect example of this happened in the last couple of months was with Enron. Enron screwed all there employees, stealing there money, the companies money and running with the money and letting Enron to go out of business screwing everyone in there paths. I can’t stand people in the corporate area of the world thinking that they are higher then everyone and they think they can get away with everything. Hopefully are American judicial system puts them in there place. #2: People that sue other people at a drop of a hat This is one of the dumbest things that has every happened in the Judicial system across the world. I feel bad for McDonalds because they have had two of the most ridiculous cases in the world. The first one, which happened a couple years ago, involved an elderly woman that spilt coffee on her lap. What did she do? She sues McDonalds for a million dollars and wins. She won the case because the top wasn’t on to tight or for some stupid crap like that. This was so stupid and the judicial system let her get away with it. But I have a new favorite one that not only involves McDonalds but it involves the fast food giants in America. McDonalds, Burger King, Wendy’s and many more fast food restaurants are being sued by a couple that blames the fast food giants for getting them to be fat. I hope that these people don’t get away with this because if they do maybe they should so there parents to for feeding them when they grew up. What’s next? Someone sues Subway because they lost too much weight? This is ridiculous. #1: Stock Market analysts Give me a break, could you not see it coming or did you just have to give us the look like “we didn’t know” and just shrug your shoulders? I could have told you that are stock market system were in the toilet. It doesn’t take a rocket scientist to see that. Now thanks to them I have to listen to it every night on the news. They better start working their asses off to get our economy back where it is. Some of them made the stock market terrible right now and hopefully they know how to get us out of the mud. That’s the top thing that currently pisses me off right now. That’s my top ten list for things I hate currently. It was a bit humorous and serious but it all made a point. There are something’s in the world that pisses me off sometimes and this is most of them. I now feel like that guy on Fox Sports Net, Jim Rome.
