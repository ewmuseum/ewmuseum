One Of Those RP's That Doesn't Mention My Opponent (Part One).

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/06/02 “The history of organisations in Dianetics and Scientology is a history of people. It is the history of a number of people finding their feet, finding their initiative and finding their way of life.”– L. Ron Hubbard. Over the course of his life L. Ron Hubbard wrote many works... From Sci-Fi epics to applications of modern metal practices to the books that founded the faith of Scientology, and although he has a huge amount of works published, the air of secrecy around Scientology always makes one wonder what is being held back. Do those within the faith have a chance to read unpublished work? Or is it held in libraries high within the complex structure that is the religion of Scientology? One such man high within this order is Xavier Kannon, who on Friday finally got a taste of what he and the Ultimate Authority have been dishing out... He gained a Title, he lost a Title, he got attacked post match and he left the arena bruised and battered... But are his bruised ribs and splitting headache foremost in his mind? Or are the Gold Patron Meritorious’ thoughts on more Scientological matters as his Cage match with Miyagawa approaches? “They’re coming.... We have to leave this place.... “ Our shot opens up in a dusty, hazy, poorly lit room. The floor is simple, unvarnished boards and a layer of dust seems to cover everything and filters out most of the light coming from the partly boarded up window, itself covered in a layer of dirt. The door, again made from simple wooden planks flies open, sending a small waft of dust off the floor and spreading throughout the lower reaches of the room.. Man : “Somehow they knew we were coming for it.... Someone must have tipped them off....” Through the now open door comes charging a distressed man, wearing a shabby looking suit with un-done jacket and scruffy shirt... His hear is long, wavy and Brown and on the bridge of his nose sits a pair of round, Black framed glasses. Over his shoulder is slung a Green canvas bag that appears to be empty and is flung onto the bed as another man, dressed almost identically but a little less dishevelled throws some papers into it just as it lands and they pack up anything of their’s in the room. Man : “But don’t worry, I’ve tracked it down... The only copy in existence..” Man#2 : “Excellent.... I’m starting to wish we’d never been roped into this though... We’ll get out of here and call the Gold Patron meritorious to report our findings...” They both head for the door but one doubles back and heads back to the sideboard by the open and partly boarded window to pick up his carton of Cigarettes and match book when he suddenly clasps his throat... The 2nd man turns back, looking concerned... Man #2 : “Hurry up alread-” The first man spins around, before he has a chance to pick up his cigarettes to reveal a lavishly feathered dart sticking out of the side of his neck... He tries to force some words out, but they are whispery and pained as his pupils dilate, a sweat forming on his brow due to the constriction of the throat from what is most likely a toxic dart... Man : “Bl-.... Black..... Bo...Book-.... Black Books....” He collapses down onto the dusty floor, gagging for air and releasing the Green canvas bag that his colleague swipes, making sure to stay low and out of view of the window before sprinting through the door and onto a similarly, sparsely decorated hallway, chunks of plaster missing from the walls Man#2 : “They must have tracked him... We should have known the enemies of Scientology would want to intercept us...” He leaps the first flight of stairs, landing with bent knees at the first right angle bend, sending a cloud of dust up and causing a worrying creak from the ageing wooden steps... “This is their room, ransack it!!!” As the 2nd man... Well, he’s the only one left alive now so lets just call him “Man” descends the creaky wooden stars as quickly as possible as more voices are heard coming from the opposite end of the hallway... “Check the dead one... We need to find where the book is.... And find me the other one... Tear this place apart and cleanse it...” The suited man continues to leap down the stairs, looking up the stairwell every few seconds to see if he is being followed, the Green canvas military looking bag trailing him, attached via one loose shoulder strap... “I SAID FIND HIM!!!” He finally reaches the bottom of the stairwell and looks around in every direction before looking up to see a flash of Orange robe that brings terror to his eyes... Man : “It’s them,... It’s the HARI KRISHNAS!!!” From his comments earlier about contacting a Gold Patron Meritorious it can be assumed he is either a Scientologist or at least employed by Scientology.... And if there is one enemy of Scientology who wants to see them crumble even more than the US Government it is the Hari Krishnas... ....Before the 2 faiths lived in harmony, both against violence from their core beliefs... But then something happened... That person was Xavier Kannon. After many, many, rather too many comments about their “Stupid Bald Heads” or “Fruity Orange Robes” at Religious conventions the Hari Krishnas could hold back no more, a splinter group, forsaking the ancient code of non-violent conduct made it their goal to thwart the followers of Dianetics at every turn... ...And it would assume that they are in the midst of doing this now, trying to intercept the information at this particular link in the chain. “I SEE HIM.... AT THE BOTTOM OF THE EAST STAIRWELL!!!” Upon hearing this cry the man ceases to look around and sprints out the door, flinging it open.. “AYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!” Fortunately for the man a Hari Krishna had been stationed at the bottom of the stairwell outside the exit but had chosen to stand the wrong side off the door and a flash of Orange is seen tumbling over a short, crumbling Brick wall and down into the gardens below, landing with a thud. Man : “Thanks Xenu, I owe you one.... Now to get out of here...” His panicked eyes dart around as he looks up to the window to see smoke now bellowing out of it, then his rapidly darting Hazel eyes lock onto something... Man : “That will have to do...” He sprints again, this time along the elevated path that overlooks the sloped gardens connecting the old, relic of a house with the road running past it, surrounded by forest and other ruined buildings... As we zoom out from his emotion drenched face we see a moped parked up at the end of the drive... A hissing of another dart is then heard followed by the dull thud of it embedding into something as the panicked man turns around he sees another lavish dart sticking out of the Green strap of the canvas bag... Man : “They’d better pay me Calder’s share as well after this... I’m a Book Dealer not .... Well, a guy who nearly GETS KILLED!!!” He hops onto the Moped, who’s keys are still in the ignition and tries to start it to no avail, causing a sound that alerts the crack squad of Hari Krishnas who are ransacking the burning building... Man : “Hubbard.... Good ol’ L Ron... JUST START THE DAMN MOPED!!!” He tries again, this time resulting in the satisfying turning over of the engine so he can hop his feet up off the floor and pull away up the hill snaking the trees as a crowd of 5 Hari Krishnas, all dressed in their Orange robes come running out to watch him go... Hari Krishna : “Again a man who worships beside Kannon has smited us... But one day... One day we shall bring about the downfall of Scientology and the one they call Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon...” Our shot fades out on the angered face of one of the bald little men dressed in Orange and fades in on something a little more familiar, the living room of Xavier Kannon’s Hubbardville residence in the exclusive Kelly Preston Development on the upper East side of the city.. ...He is lying back on his couch, resting up after the beating he took at Fusion... Being tossed around like a rag doll by Reginald Lovecraft, taking an Executioner’s Chair from Politically Incorrect to lose the Extreme Title, being the victim of a 5 Elements Frog Splash by Miyagawa then being picked up and thrown out of the ring by the Supreme Machine. *Ring Ring* *Ring Ring* *Ring Ring* The phone in the hall rings and Xavier sits up to go and get it before grabbing his side and wincing in pain then lying back down flat. Xavier : “My Scientological pain racks my Scientological body.... Damn those Peons and their oppression of my right to not be in agony...” He decides to wait until the machine picks it up... “This is Xavier Kannon, I am away on my Scientological business so leave your non-Scientological message and I will give you my most Scientological reply...” *BEEP* As the tone sounds we cut back to the panicked face of the man running from the Hari Krishnas and as we pull back he is shown in a phonebox with the Moped pulled up outside... His eyes shoot in all directions, looking out for the merest flash of Orange and hurriedly waiting for the message to end... *BEEP* Man : “Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon, I’ve found it... I’ve found the book that prophesises the rise of the “Son of Xenu”.... As written by Hubbard at the birth of Scientology... The only copy on the face of the earth...” As he speaks he continues to look around him, peering out through the glass of the phonebox ... Man : “We have found it in a shop called “Black Books” in London, the Hari Krishnas are onto us... Calder has been killed... But they don’t know.. They don’t kno-” He is cut off as a flash of Orange is seen reflecting in the glass of the booth followed by the hissing sound of a dart flying and he clutches his neck, dropping the receiver and slumping down onto the floor holding the side of his neck and his eyes roll back into his head. As the man exhales his final breath as we cut back to Kannon’s house, the Scientologist seemingly unconcerned by the man suddenly ceasing to speak and his last breathless words being spoken... His dying statement being lost on Kannon’s answering machine and the one man who can hear it not caring at all for the loss of life... Rather than mourn the loss of an employee his eyes are glowing, the buzz seemingly relieving some of his pain as he begins to talk to himself... Xavier : “The “Legacy of Xenu”.... The work that claims here will be another great Scientologist... One who will stand above any Thetan... A man who could claim to have been conceived by the Universe itself.... And that person will be ME!!!” Our shot zooms in on Kannon’s bright Emerald Green eyes as the shot starts to fade to Black.. Xavier : “England... And years of perpetual worship, here I come...” We hit Black, the sound of Kannon cackling fading out just after it, leaving us with silent darkness.
