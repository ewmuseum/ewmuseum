Reality used to be a friend of mine

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/18/02 'BAH!' Yet again the sounds of a man angered ring forth, this time in a small hotel somewhere in Texas. We pan over to see none other than Clyde hunched over a computer, snarling. Clyde: So he wants to break kayfabe, does he? The burned man jams on his down arrow, scrolling through line after line. He reads rapidly, his eyes narrow slits as he scans. Clyde: Damned Dark Gimmick..........I knew he was hard up for work but to sell the footage of our group counciling session to the END of all people.......... From out of the small bathroom which happens to be the only other room in this abode stomps Bethany, leaping into Clyde's lap who grunts in pain. Clyde: .............little to the right............ The little girl looks up at her lover...........sneers and grinds herself into his groin before finally moving to the side. Clyde: (Grimacing) Thanks hunny. Bethany: What are you doing? Clyde: Reading the End's latest promo. He got some footage of that group counciling session me and Dark Gimmick went to that one time. I need to type something up in response but I'm out of ideas. Bethany raises an eyebrow.........glances at the monster quizzically. Bethany: Type? Clyde: Yeah. The burned man hits the keys in rapid succession.............then slows. Clyde: ..................why AM I typing? What am I typing? Clyde looks at his screen............glances at what he has just written. Clyde: .........from out of the small bathroom which happens to be the only other room in this abode stomps Bethany..........dear god who wrote this dribble? Bethany: Uh...........didn't you just write it? Clyde: Why would I write something describing what you just did three seconds ago? What sense does that make? Idiot. The burned man goes back to his keyboard............brings up a small window. Clyde: Who the hell is Danielle and why the hell is she in this chat? Bethany: What chat? Speak english! Clyde: Doesn't matter............can you go hide under the bed or something then come out again? Walden's annoying me in this chat as we speak about how little demonic girls would have no reason to be coming out of a bathroom. Bethany: Why are you chatting with Walden? What in the name of God are you talking about? Clyde: .............I don't know. The burned man continues to type............looks up a few paragraphs. Clyde: Bethany: What chat? Speak english! Bethany: Huh!? Clyde: Ugh...........I need a drink. Clyde opens the refrigerator located directly behind him.............pulls out a full bottle of rum. He twists off the top, sniffs it quickly. Clyde: I'm thoroughly confused. Only the power of alcohol may save me. A coughing rings out..........Clyde glances over the monitor to the hotel door propped open by one of his shoes. Clyde: What the hell was that? Bethany: I don't know...........I don't know what the hell any of this is about..........I'm so confused! Clyde: Well, downing this bottle may not help your confusion, but it'll make me feel a lot better about End breaking kayfabe yet again. With that the monster begins chugging............chugging...........chugging. Finally with a loud belch he slams the bottle down, grins. Clyde: Heh......... Clyde's eyes then roll back in his head, he falls facefirst onto his keyboard. jhjnhhju Bethany: ...........what in the name of God was that? Suddenly Clyde rises..........his head begins shaking violently back and forth. Drool flies from his mouth, splattering on the walls as he convulses. Bethany: (Standing quickly) Ugh! Slowly an acrid smoke begins seeping from the burned man's nose. It drifts, more and more, taking a shape...........as Bethany looks on it begins to grow solid. Bethany: Uh............ The smoke gradually changes color, taking on the appearance of slightly pasty flesh. Before her very eyes it becomes human...........soon a tall man sporting shoulder length stringy black hair and a Crowbar-esqe goatee stands. Clyde: (Looking up) I know you. Josh: DIE!!!!!!!! The man known only as Josh leaps, grabbing Clyde's head and slamming it into the wall. He bashes his skull off the keyboard once............twice..............thrice. 787uy jh7juh ynm Bethany: Huh!? Josh looks up from his thrashing of the demon...........glances at the little girl. Josh: ..............I know you, you're that Claudia reject I came up with two years agoAGGGGH!!!!!!!! The distraction proves to be enough for Clyde to fight back, sucker punching the former Verizon monkey in the jaw. Josh tumbles head over heels, smacking his head off the wall and causing an empty coffee pot to fall onto his head, shattering into a million pieces. Clyde: (Standing and dusting himself off) I don't know who the hell you are, but you're interfering in my wallowing in self pity. Josh: Self pity is MY gimmick you Wishmaster/Freddy Krueger/Randall Flagg/I can't come up with an original idea to save my life asshole! Full of rage, the mysterious person charges at Clyde again............only for him to fly through the air as Bethany extends a foot and trips him. Bethany: Call ME a Claudia reject.............whoever you are. Josh's head again careens off the wall.............Clyde towers over him. With a shake of his head he grabs the now empty rum bottle, breaking it over his handler's skull. Clyde: You're annoying. The lights fade out for the ill fated depressant............... ............finally he is awakened by rain falling upon his face. Josh looks up to find himself tied to the roof of the hotel...........at the edge stands Clyde with his back turned. Clyde: So here it shall end. Josh: You can't end me moron, you'd be ending yourself. Clyde: True, but realistically you haven't done anything with my character since Trashcan Man retired. I'd be doing myself and, hell, the world a favor by killing you and ending your pointless drivel now. Josh: ............ The burned man turns around............marches. Looking down upon his creator he smiles.........but it is a sinister smile. Clyde: Now we shall fight to the death. Here on the roof of the Sun Suites hotel in Lewisville Texas, one of us will perish.......... Clyde kneels............with a swipe of his long, sharp and dirty fingernails he slices the ropes holding Josh Pollard. Josh: You couldn't think of a better location than the roof of this dump? Clyde: Bah. Josh: You stole my catchphase too, assmonkey. Clyde: DIE!!!!!!! With that Clyde clutches Josh by the throat, lifting him overhead. Josh responds by kicking Clyde between the legs........however the demon merely grins. Clyde: You wrote me into losing my damn balls. Clyde looks on............slowly realization dawns on him. Clyde: YOU WROTE ME INTO LOSING MY DAMN BALLS!!!!!!!! With a shout of rage the burned man throws Josh across the roof..............he skids, nearly falling off the edge but hanging on. Clyde stomps after him, his fists clenching and unclenching. Clyde: No one writes me into losing my damn balls and lives. Josh: Ugh.......... Far below in the hotel room, Bethany hears the commotion. Bethany: I've got to help Clyde! The little girl rummages through the varied trash littered throughout the room............tossing aside paper and empty soda cans, she finally comes across what she is looking for. Bethany: (Grinning) There we go. Bethany grabs hold of a large bottle marked with a skull and crossbones............with an evil laugh takes a large swig of moonshine. After the first she sways...........then puts the bottle back to her lips again. She chugs...........and chugs.........finally tossing it over her shoulder. Bethany: (Crosseyed) Whoooooooo! Suddenly a flash of pain rips through the child's abdomen............she doubles over in agony. Bethany: Nuh..........no...........no! A puff of smoke begins to emerge from the little girl's nose...........more and more, filtering out. Finally it takes shape........solidifies into what appears to be a dead ringer for former WCW star Daffney. Lena: Hi honey! Bethany: No! Not you! Lena: Come on baby, let's go join the party! Josh’s girlfriend grabs Bethany by the hair and begins dragging her up towards the roof..............meanwhile up above the psychopath sits atop Clyde slapping him upside the head. Josh: This is for making me waste so much time on your worthless jobber self! Clyde: BAH! Josh raises a fist for the killing blow..............when suddenly he is distracted by Lena hauling Bethany onto the roof. Lena: Look what the cat dragged in! Josh: Hi Lena! I'm just writing Clyde out of my life permanently, it'll just be a second. Lena: Cool. Lena looks on as Josh pounds the life out of Clyde............not noticing behind her Bethany reaching into her dress. She pulls out a piece of paper..........crumples it up into a ball and chucks it straight over Lena's head with all her might. Lena: Hey! With a nonchalant gesture Lena bitchslaps Bethany, knocking her flat............however the ball still sails through the air. Clyde reaches up and catches...........pokes Josh in the eye, and while he is distracted uncurls it. Clyde: Huh. Josh: (Clutching his eye) What is it? I can't see. Clyde: Look. The burned man shoves the paper into Josh Pollard's face...............he takes his hand away and stares. Josh: Denton County...........lawsuit............default judgement...........five thous........sand............dollars..........? Josh looks on............suddenly his features begin to twist. Josh: No. The tall man stands............clutches his head. Josh: NO!!!!!!!! Lena: What's wrong Josh, is Katie IMing you again? Lena jogs over to her lover..........looks at the paper. Lena: Ah crap............ With a loud rapport that shakes the heavens Josh Pollard explodes. A massive conflagration erupts............flames shooting for the sky. His body dissolves along with that of Lena..............disincorporating into a smoke which flies into Clyde's nostril, as the Lena smoke enters Bethany's. Both of them fall flat from the impact...........after a moment the clouds part, the rain abating and the sun shining down. Clyde: Ugh........... Bethany: Ugh............ The demonic pain slowly rise to sitting positions........look to each other. Bethany: Clyde............what the hell just happened? Clyde: Don't ask, kiddo...........please, don't ask. And as the demon looks into the sun, pondering future battles and the true evil lurking inside him eternally looking for escape, we mercifully fade to black..............
