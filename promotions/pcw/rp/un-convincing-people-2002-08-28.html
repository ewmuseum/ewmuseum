Un-Convincing People

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th August We cut to a crowded street where a stream of permanent shoppers are doing what they do best… shopping. Walking down this aforementioned street are none other than Miyagawa and Jeremy Johnson. Miyagawa is wearing a rather large sign with “I’M ALEXANDER MIYAGAWA, STOP ME FOR AN AUTOGRAPH” in rather bold white letters on the black t-shirt. JJ is carrying one of those “Golf Sale” signs that you see about, except the arrow has been tilted down to point at Miyagawa as he walks and it now has “FORMER PCW WORLD CHAMPION” written on the sign. There is a cluster of activity as an old granny approaches the duo. Miyagawa: Yes my dear, what can I do for you? The Granny peers at him through a pair of rather thick glasses. Miyagawa ducks down to go the same eye level with her so that she feels more at ease. Then Miyagawa gets a mild whiff of urine and straightens back up. The Granny adjusts her glasses carefully. Glasses: Have you got a pen? Miyagawa: Have I got a pen? Of course I have a pen! Miyagawa fishes a pen out of a plastic bag that he carries. The Granny doesn’t read the logo on the sign of the pen, but the camera fades in and you can see that it’s a black plastic ballpoint pen with “I’M ALEXANDER MIYAGAWA” written along the side. Miyagawa: There you go my dear. Granny: Thanks. And with that the Granny walks off. Miyagawa pauses for a second with a perturbed look on his face as he realises that she never wanted an autograph in the first place. Miyagawa: Hey! Hey! Dammit! I’m not a pen distribution facility! Don’t they have pens at bingo or something?! JJ looks pretty darn uneasy with the number of people now looking at the shouting man with the sign pointing at him. Miyagawa strides over to where a woman is pushing a pushchair with a newborn baby in it. Miyagawa: Would you mind if I kissed your baby, it’s for publicity purposes. The woman takes one look at him and suddenly blurts out. Woman: You sick paedophiliac freak, stay away from me and my baby! And promptly rushes off. Miyagawa looks at her running off in absolute shock before glancing over to JJ who simply shrugs when meet each other’s gaze. Miyagawa: What do I have to do? Pay people to let me sign them an autograph? How far have I fallen for morality? I call people out JJ, I tell them the truth about Jeremy Howard and this is the payment for being honest. Howard used Asylum to further his own career, he used me. He RAPED my career. Where did I go when Howard was in Asylum? JJ just shrugs back at him, deliberately not trying to draw this conversation out any longer than possible. So basically JJ is much, much smarter than he looks, and we finally have proof of that at last. Miyagawa: I’ll tell what happened… he left me to be pinned by Chet at Alliances… I never won a single belt because I was choking under his shadow… and when finally, at March Madness when I came third because of dastardly tactics by Mexi and I appeared like the man I really am… HE BROKE UP THE DAMNED STABLE BECAUSE HE DIDN’T WANT TO SHARE THE SPOTLIGHT!!! And once again, people stare. They all stare. They don’t want to, but they do. A man in a suit walks up towards them. Man: Now listen; I don’t know who you are… Miyagawa gives him an evil stare and points at the shirt he’s wearing. Miyagawa: Read the shirt, suit boy. The man frowns back at the PCW wrestler. Man: Ok, I don’t care who you are, but keep the noise down. A heavy truck drives past making far more noise than Miyagawa was a few moments prior to it. JJ stands next to the man and points down the side road. JJ: Golf sale is this way sir. Man: What? JJ: The arrow sir, it’s a golf sale, and it’s down that road there. Man: Golf you say, eh? I like golf. JJ: It’s such a macho sport sir, it suits you sir… just down that road there… no wait… DAMMIT… no…. no… NO! NO! NO! NONONONONONONONO!!! The man steps backwards as JJ drops the arrow and looks up into the sky as his eyes glaze over, his muscles bulge, his skin starts turning red and all the veins in his body bulge outwards of the skin. JJ: I’M NOT GAY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Somewhere in deepest darkest Africa a white man in typical attire is being hunted by Zulus trying to get him to hand over his farm. A young white girl walks up to him and holds him closely, afraid. Girl: What was that daddy? Man: That was those damned Zulus… and apparently one of them isn’t gay.
