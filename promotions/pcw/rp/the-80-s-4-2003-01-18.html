The 80's [4]

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18.01.03 One brisk wind hits the face of Dante Jackson, the cold and yet equally talented rhyme slayer rises up from the little bench where he had a brief, but yet undeniable covnersation with Statuz Quo. The eyes of the man are not weary, just caught in the pool of what to do? Afterall, he's defeated many a man, including Chet King, Nerezza, and every other person that hasn't been wise enough to know what to do when facing the Lyrical Messiah. Of course, for every outstanding Blueprint for success, there is always the beta of it, sending you back to square one in search of another one. For now, as everybody else around him has a good time, he's focused upon his oppoennts, which for a certain second disc is all he happens to talk about, ranging from everybody low, to the high echelon of talent in Primetime. As that first disc, seventeen tracks laden with every flow sharp and every topic about life and trying to break the ever growing sophmore jinx present in many rap artist first two times out, its a push and shove incident that can grow into many a thing bigger. In the mind of Dante Jackson, anything is just about possible, whether or not he'd like to accept it for what it really is. From the left eye, he can see Skylar Green walking off, headed towards the Hyman Stadium after seeing the sign for a Metallica concert placed in the middle of the Alantic City Boardwalk. Reading his watch, its about 6:30, show starting at a clear 7:00 and Skylar off to go see the kings of metal at the moment, all because of their somewhat 'subliminal' messaging. The right eye tells much the same, Jackson Rust basically in the buff along with the Masked Mullet standing over talking to some beachheads and condom hat wearing men and women who hold their surfboards and bikni tops in-between their arm pits. Soon, Rust says something needed for no words as he is mauled by a woman, citing that even in the nude, Jackson Rust has a power over women. Mullet comes over sulking with his head down until he checks ou tthe Metallica concert sign plastered in the middle of the street. A large smile grows on his morphed face, rushing off to go see what all the hoopla is all about. The others, Chris Matthews whom has just stuck to whereever he doesn't have to be around anybody that doesn't support him, or his crew leans against a brick wall, while Vanilla Skillz, Mani Shigeru, Shin Yagyuu, and the ever funky freshed dressed Rent A Hero sport their styles of hip hop trends by participating in a rap contest, the crowd screams as its Mani's turn to talk on the mic as Rent hands it to him, previously trashing the others with his comic books and superhero rhyme style. Sonic marbles along base nots and heavy guitar rifts accompany Mani as he somewhat talks into the microphone, the small fracas around him nodding their heads in amazement as Mani tosses the mic down with ease, the crowd going nuts over him. Shin folds his arms back along his broad chest smirking at his partner as the ever funny Vanilla Skillz steps up to the plate, doing what he does best, being good and comical at the same time. Beats from various men Jam Master Jay, Kool Herc, Kool Moe D, Cut Creator, those names that helped pioneer hip hop into the future forefront that Dante leads by example. In the eyes of every man whoever wanted a spot in the game, you have to accept the roots in front of you. Dante knows his roots from the ground up. Sure, he may not pride himself on rapping about them, but to ladies, that's another story. He doesn't need to live the lifestyle and have ladies flcok him everywhere he goes, he has a wife and a child, so that they wouldn't be placed in the crossfire, they're away from the scene, except for Tracy whom happens to manage her husband. Certain songs have been recorded towards women and yes, away from the mysogonist treatment that has earned rap somewhat of a bad name. Dante is passionate, and wanting of female fans, not just to uncrease recod sales, but to not look at him is if it were some Greek God put out of their reach. Picking his wiry frame off of the bench, he slaps hands with various crew members, before opting not to take a spin on the microphone, hurling everyone together and telling them to head towards the Metallica concert. They listen of course, except for the hip hop heads, who wouldn't be caught inside of a Metallica concert if they're lives depended on it. You're stuck in a distant past, it depended on life and death. As the group moves away towards the scene, a rift in the middle of the boardwalk occurs, the looming phase of a giant spiral shaped whirl encased in blue hue and thunder crashing all around it, a burly man with gray hairs and poindexter glasses falls onto the pavement, with a note attatched to his lab coat pocket. 'Round up the others and take them back to present day.'
