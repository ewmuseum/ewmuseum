The thin watery line between a man and his tugboat

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin had just been denied access to the Wrestle Vessel by the ship’s captain.With Flaymin posing such a dangerous threat of sinking the boat whether out of idiocy or…. Idiocy, he was refused permission to get anywhere near the Wrestle Vessel. So how is a poor (Aussie) brother supposed to get to Jamaica?Simple.By himself…. In a rusty old tugboat on the verge of falling apart. The Journey to Jamaica begins now!!! [Scene picks up on the wharf where we last left off.Flaymin looks at the rusted, old piece of crap that floats before him. He slowly reaches out to try and touch the tugboat (don’t ask why)] Flaymin: Hmmm…. Rusty. [Wrapping his leg around one of the wooden stumps stapled onto the pier, Flaymin reaches his hands out and tries to get control of the rope tying the tugboat to the pier. He quickly lunges forward and in the process, misses horribly and almost lands in the water.Flaymin quickly retreats backwards to avoid landing in the ocean. He gets back up and scratches his head] Flaymin: How on earth am I supposed to get to Jamaica if I can’t even get onto the damn boat?? [Flaymin strokes his chin as he tries to conjure up another plan that will get him from the wharf and into the boat without getting wet… or hurt… or dead… or… well you get the point. Flaymin suddenly clicks his fingers together] Flaymin: Ah ha!I could just grab the rope and use it to pull the ship towards me! [So Flaymin does exactly that….He crouches down onto his knees, grabs a hold of the thick, white, platted rope and begins to tug on it] Flaymin: That’s strange, I don’t ever recall tugging being so difficult to do.With all my donger tugging expertise, this should be a cakewalk. [Flaymin continues to pull at the rope…. But the tugboat doesn’t budge. Flaymin pulls….Pulls some more….Keeps on pulling…. Until….. SNAP!!! [The rope suddenly snaps in two sending Flaymin hurling backwards and smacking his head on the hard wooden floor of the pier. Dazed and confused, Flaymin stays on the floor and grabs his aching cranium and rubs it] Flaymin: This sucks. Maybe I could swim to Jamaica? [In the corner of his eye, Flaymin notices two little kids watching on in amusement as Flaymin makes an ass of himself. He gets up off the floor and frowns at the two little boys] Flaymin: You think this is funny? Kid #1: Sure do, Mister!You’re clumsy! Flaymin: Hey punk, do you wanna come here and pull this rope?? [Under his breath Flaymin begins to mumble to himself]Flaymin: Hey, that’d make a great pick-up line… Kid #2: What did you say? Flaymin: I said go jump in the water and get my damn rope. [The two kids shake their heads and continue to laugh at Flaymin] Flaymin: Look, I need to get to Jamaica quick fast.How about doing me a favour? [The little boy with an obvious case of head lice steps forward and grins at Flaymin] Kid #2: I accept cash, cheques, MasterCard. Visa and American Express aaaaand on the odd occasion- Diners Club. Flaymin: Say what? Kid #1: We’ll get you the rope, but you have to pay us. Flaymin: Why you no-good, cheating little bastards!I ain’t paying for nothing! [The head-scratching kid cups his hand to his ear (like an unnamed 50+ yearold geriatric WWE wrestler), the kid then closes his eyes as if focussing his hearing on something in the distance] Kid: Do you hear that? [Flaymin looks around in confusion] Flaymin: Hear what? Kid: Its Jamaica!They’re even laughing at you over there! But to get there, you’ll have to shell out the moolah! [Flaymin places his hands on his forehead like sun visors and looks out over the vast blue sea] Flaymin: I don’t see Jamaica. [The kid sighs and whispers to his buddy] Kid #1: This’ll be easier than I thought. Kid #2: Forget the money, let’s try and get his car keys! [Growing increasingly irritated, Flaymin turns back to the kids and growls] Flaymin: Listen here you midget mother schmuckers, if you don’t fetch me that damn rope I’m gonna throw you both into the water. Kid #1: Now, now Mister there is no need to chuck a hissy-fit.Just hand over the money and we’ll get you on that crappy boat. [Flaymin considers his other options…. Then he remembers he has no other options…. So he thinks harder…. Wait!!… No, no… still no other options. Having exhausted his intellectual abilities, he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a $20 note and hands it to the kid. The kid looks at the $20 with an insulted look on his face] Kid #2: Are you kidding me??That won’t even cover 5 packets of Pokemon Cards!! Kid #1: Heck, we couldn’t even buy a prostitute with that money! Flaymin: You’re obviously looking in the wrong spots- The Rush’s mother works for free. Kid #2: We’re still not taking the money!!We want $50 and not a cent less! [Once again, Flaymin hesitantly reaches into his pocket and pulls out another 30 dollars and hands it to the kids] Flaymin: Now get your asses into the damn water and fetch me that freakin’ snapped rope. [The little kid looks at all the money in his hands and a smile from ear to ear quickly develops. The two kids then turns around and begin to walk away much to the surprise of Flaymin] Flaymin: Hey!Hey!Where the hell are you going?The water is thataway! [The kids walk over to a nearby plank of wood that has been dumped on the pier.They each hold up one end of the wood and walk back over to the end of the wharf where the boat is. They place one end of the plank on the pier and the other end on the tip of the tugboat. Both boys then step back and look at Flaymin] Kid #1: There you go, now you can get in the boat! [Flaymin hangs his head in shame, shaking it from side to side] Flaymin: Why the hell didn’t I think of that? Kid #2: Lets face it Mister, you’re a dumbass. Flaymin: Eh, I still get a free trip to Jamaica! [Flaymin walks up the rickety plank and safely makes his way across it and into the tugboat.He walks around the boat, inspecting its… well, crapiness. Next, Flaymin walks into the control room and quickly turns on the motor of the tugboat. The motor begins to rumble until it finally turns on and runs smoothly. Flaymin then looks at the navigation wheel in confusion] Flaymin: So which way to Jamaica?? [Scene fades to black] Which way to Jamaica, indeed….. Just wait and see ;)
