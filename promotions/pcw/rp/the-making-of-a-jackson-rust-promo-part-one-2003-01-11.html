The Making Of: A Jackson Rust Promo; Part One.

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 11, 2003. And welcome to MTV’s “The Making Of…A JACKSON RUST PROMO!” As the dumbass voiceover ends, the scene opens up with droning MTV-like music. The view is of a white trailer, with “Generic Stars” written in black marker; in crude, maniacal writing, of course. Would you expect anything else out of a Jackson Rust promo? Anyway, the door of the trailer suddenly bursts open, as Jackson Rust walks out, a pair of dark sunglasses adorning his face, as he walks down the few steps that lead up to it. 10:57 AM. Jackson walks a bit wobbly, as the result of the time being before two in the afternoon. He looks around for a bit, before walking down the paved walkway to wherever the scene may be. Jackson Rust: Here we are, on location for a Jackson Rust promo…brought to you by that dumbass station, that has collected millions of mindless teenagers…this is: M-T-V! Narrator: Do you know how much this promo idea sucks? Jackson Rust: Yeah…I know. But Steve wanted to be known as the next pop princess. Narrator: Oh Jesus! *shudders* Jackson Rust: *nods* As Jackson approaches the scene, Al, Kimberly, and Steve are seen talking to some producers of the sort, as the producers themselves look at Steve quizzically, as he is dressed up in a replica Britney Spears schoolgirl outfit from her first video. Steve: H-hit m-me ba-baby one m-mo-more t-time! Producer #1: Stop. Producer #2: Please. Al: Yuck, man…don’t you have common decency? Steve furrows his pop-princess-like brow. Steve: H-huh? Al: You’re hangin’ out, man! Steve looks down, before adjusting the what-would-be-cute-on-a-girl shirt, and tucking his chest back in. Kimberly: Right…now, go piss off. Jackson walks onto the scene, looks at the producers for a moment, and then Steve, who is trying to keep himself covered before the Paparazzi comes and snaps photos of him, and takes them on the Internet. Jackson Rust: I thought he was kidding. Kimberly: No…he’s Steveney Spears. Narrator: Oh shit. As Steve now begins to work on his dance moves, the glove from the previous promos appears behind him, and taps him on the shoulder gently. Steve: H-huh? Steve turns around, as the glove lashes him across the head, sending him flying back several…yards. Producer #1: Now, what say we work on the promo? Jackson Rust: *shrugs* Producer #2: Rightio. Jackson Rust: Dumbass. 11:16 AM. The location is now in front of a green screen, which is used to place any background for the promo. Jackson stands at the side of it, running his fingers the length of whatever until he gets bored as the camera crews begin to set up. The producer known as Producer #1 walks up to Rust. Producer #1: Now, we didn’t know if you wanted to do the promo yourself, or with look-alike actors, so we got the look-alikes anyhow. Jackson Rust: *furrows brow* Producer #1: *snaps fingers* At the snap of his fingers (literally), four people walk from behind the screen, attempting to look like the characters they are paid to act like. Jackson walks up to the man who is supposedly going to play ‘Jackson Rust’ in the promo. Jackson Rust: Well…he isn’t as good-looking as me… Kimberly: Oh my God! No! Kimberly points, and screams, and stumbles back as she looks at the only partially looking female in the lineup. Standing at about five feet, four inches, and weighing in at about four hundred pounds…she tips the scales – oh hell, she crushes the scales. Kimberly: That’s not my double, is it? Kimberly Look-Alike: You know it, Toots. Kimberly: *faints* As an audible thud is heard from Kimberly’s fainting, Al walks up to the person who resembles him… Al: Hmm…he looks like me…but I’m not that fat…and what? He’s doing the exact movements of me! He’s awesome! Jackson Rust: That’s a mirror, Al. Al: …oh. I uh…knew that. Jackson Rust: No you didn’t. As Steve stumbles to his feet from a few yards back, recovering from the glove slap, his look-alike begins to chat up the unconscious Kimberly. Steve Look-Alike: Being knocked out is as good as saying, “Yes”, right? Jackson Rust: You’re fired. Steve Look-Alike: Why ever would you do that? Jackson Rust: You have the dumbest fake British accent…Steve isn’t from England. You don’t have the legendary stutter…Steve does. You stole that line from one of the PCW weekly events, as quoted by Jeff Marx. Steve Look-Alike: ELLIE~! WILL AVENGE MY FIRING! Al: Go fuck yourself. Steve Look-Alike: Okay! As the Steve look alike goes running behind the screen, the sound of unzipping of pants is heard, before an audible groan of pleasure is heard. Narrator: Eww… Al: Okay…my fault -- hit me. Jackson kicks Al in the nuts. Jackson Rust: Bastard! Al limply falls to the hard floor as Jackson stands over him in anger. Al: My nuts man…they’re…dangling…from…my…ass. Jackson Rust: I play soccer. Al: Ouch… Jackson Rust: *shrugs and walks off* 12:30 PM. The scene is again relocated into the trailer, which actually started this promo. Jackson Rust: This sucks. Al places a bag of ice on his crotch area. Al: It hurts. Kimberly: It’s emotionally injuring me. Steve: I’m a pop princess! Jackson stands up, and kicks Steve in the nuts. Jackson Rust: Imposter! Steve: Ouch! The trailer door opens, and in walks Steve. Steve: H-hey. Steve Look-Alike: Bastard. Al: Go fuck your—never mind. Jackson leans over, and picks the imposter Steve from the floor, and runs him headfirst through the wall, that cheap wall. His head dangles outside as Jackson grabs a conveniently placed set of darts. Jackson Rust: Anyone for a game of darts? Steve Look-Alike: NO! Rust pretends to sneeze, and manages to launch a dart right into the sphincter of the imposter Steve. Steve Look-Alike: OUCH! JESUS CHRIST! TALK ABOUT PENETRATION~! DO IT AGAIN! Jackson drops the darts on the floor, and calmly sits down upon a sofa, before turning to Kimberly. Jackson Rust: Y’know…that just…freaked me out. Kimberly: Indeed. Al: Let’s do this tomorrow or something…I need a God damned attitude adjustment. Steve: Y-yeah! Jackson Rust: *nods* The scene abruptly closes. Man this promo sucked. Stay tuned for the next episode of ‘The Making Of: A Jackson Rust Promo!’
