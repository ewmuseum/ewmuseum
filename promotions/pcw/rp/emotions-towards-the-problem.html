Emotions towards the problem...

Date: Recently... “I’m just hoping he doesn’t attack me again like he did a few shows ago.” “I doubt he will, dear.” “You never can tell with Dorn, Isiy! He’s full of frustration; frustration he seems to be taking out on me!” [[Upon these words, our scene begins to open up inside the Afghanistan locker room of Titu Xansiv, better known to the fans of Primetime Championship Wrestling as The Pharaoh. As has already been reported, security is tight tonight, and the slightest bit of suspicion drawn from wrestlers, fans and workmen alike will be dealt with on the spot. By the Egyptian’s side stands his gorgeous wife, Isiy, adorned in a denim skirt and a clingy yellow PCW logo T-shirt. Pharaoh, as always wears his ancient Egyptian garb and looks immensely aggravated, tired, and battle-worn.]] The Pharaoh: “Tonight I must trust him anyway, whether he attacked me or not.” Isiy: “Indeed. Just try to remember that he’s supposed to be your ally, whether he DID attack you or not. You know what Dorn’s like anyhow, he loves to win; he won’t let your differences get between him and a victory.” [[Pharaoh nods, looking down at the floor in a daydream sort of state.]] The Pharaoh: “I suppose you’re right. As always.” [[Isiy cracks a toothy smile.]] Isiy: “Yep.” [[The Egyptian beauty takes a seat next to her husband on the rickety bench he sits upon. She puts a loving arm around his broad shoulders and cuddles up to him.]] Isiy: “What’s wrong, Titu?” [[Defiantly as always, Titu shrugs his partner’s arm from him and stands up, pacing back and forth in front of her as he speaks.]] The Pharaoh: “Recent events, Isiy. That’s what’s annoying me. I mean, look at YOU for example! You’ve got a match on Turmoil and you’re not even bothered! I can’t stand to see you get hurt out there, my love! How do you think this is affecting me?” [[Isiy shrugs.]] Isiy: “I’m up for this battle. After how Tommy and Harry pushed me around last Fusion, I’m more than ready to kick some ass. And besides, I DID German-suplex Harry from the ring-apron last week!” [[She giggles, quite pleased with herself. Titu stops in his tracks and stares, mortified]] The Pharaoh: “Sometimes you amaze me, woman! Why, by Horus’s name, you could get destroyed out there!” Isiy: “Yeah, but you taught me a few moves a while back! I’ll—” The Pharaoh: “Speak no more! You’re ignorant words are VENOM to my ears!” [[Titu stalks across the room and stares into the mirror situated upon one of the stained ‘whitewashed’ walls.]] The Pharaoh: “Look at ME, Isiy. Bruises everywhere. The doctors tell me I’m practically deaf in this ear now! Thanks to Totally Tubular Tommy and Skylar Green, I’m a walking mess!” [[He pauses, sighing heftily.]] The Pharaoh: “How do you think you’ll fare out there on Tuesday if they’ve done THIS to me?!” [[Isiy shrugs once more.]] Isiy: “Better than you’ve done? I may win the match for us.” [[Pharaoh’s face grows red with fury.]] The Pharaoh: “I honestly don’t think you GET this! This ISN’T a GAME!” Isiy: “Oh, pipe down, Titu; do you really think Tommy and Harry’ll be worrying about the match this much?” The Pharaoh: “NO! Mainly because they used to be a T-A-G T-E-A-M. A tag team TOGETHER! AS A TEAM! They have the Godsdamned advantage, woman! Can’t you see we’re the underdogs going into this bloody match?!” [[Isiy sighs, unimpressed with her husband’s attitude TOWARDS THEIR PAIRING IN THE TAG MATCH BETWEEN Tommy and Harry.]] Isiy: “Will you just CALM DOWN?! For the love of God, Titu, you’ve already beaten Tommy…and Harry’s in no fit state to compete.” [[Titu sighs, frustrated.]] Isiy, continuing: “So what are you worrying about? A victo— [[Titu turns, interrupting Isiy as he does.]] The Pharaoh: “You.” [[Isiy grows silent.]] The Pharaoh: “It’s you. I don’t want to see you get hurt out there. You mean EVERYTHING to me, woman! By the Gods! You aren’t going out there that night; I shan’t allow it. I’ll find another partner.” Isiy: “Yeah, maybe Dorn or Violence will team with you.” [[Isiy’s voice is mocking, even after the touching words of her husband.]] The Pharaoh: “This is hopeless! Can’t you see I just don’t want you to get hurt or harassed by those two idiotic dope-loving fools?!” Isiy: “Can’t YOU see I just want to experience the rush of getting in the ring?” [[Titu throws up his hands in defeat.]] The Pharaoh: “A few months back you’d have NEVER considered getting in the ring. What’s the Duat has changed now?” Isiy: “I don’t know. I just feel like a change. All I do these days is accompany you to the ring for the fans to gawp at. It gets annoying, you know? I’d rather get in there and be apart of the action.” The Pharaoh, pleading: “Yes, but you’re HARDLY the right shape to, my little jewel of the Nile.” [[Isiy frowns.]] Isiy: “What the hell was that supposed to mean?” [[Titu’s eyes grow wide.]] The Pharaoh: “Nothing.” Isiy: “No, Titu! Are you saying I’m…fat?” [[Pharaoh cocks an eyebrow.]] The Pharaoh: “By the Gods, no!” Isiy, scowling: “Oh, yeah! What then?! What’re you TRYING to imply” The Pharaoh: “NOTHING! I just don’t think you’re—” Isiy: “Don’t give ME you’re excuses! I’m outta here. I need a coffee!” [[And with that, the Egyptian enchantress storms from the locker room, slamming the door harshly behind her.]] The Pharaoh: “Bah! You’re still not going out there Tuesday. Mark my words, Isiy!” [[He pauses, his eyes falling sad.]] The Pharaoh: “My dear love.” [[Scene fade.]]
