A romantic little experiment Part 2

13 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/11/02 Recap: In an effort to understand human beings and their emotions, Nerezza has set up an experimental date with Teresa Tomas. In a hasty move, Nerezza chose a random restaurant - one that was going to cost him dearly. His pen inks the recollected memories of the day in his diary. All in the name of this science that people call love... We sat in that restaurant with our roles reversed. 27 years of generally comfortable life was preying upon ages of instinctual hunting. She had me by the throat despite us not touching. I was weak in mind and my body was on the verge of beginning to rot away if I didn't drink soon. Although it's the negativity in the minds of man that enforces our existence, it's their blood that keeps our bodies from looking like the corpses we truly are. Luckily, I could make out a little of the french on the menu. Out of the delicacies such as Escargot and Frog's Legs that france had become famous for, I was forced that night to order rare meat that still had the blood present. And her... how she tempted me... Not Teresa, of course. Our waitress. She was obviously a girl dependent on others, forced to work. Probably a little rich girl who just had her credit cards cut up and needed to find work to stay alive. She smiled at me every time she walked past... no doubt, the amount of money I'd spent was what drew her to me originally. I couldn't start flirting with her now, that would ruin the experiment. But I enticed her a little. Seeing Teresa's notion of 'Wild Monkey sex' was just a joke, she wouldn't be with me after the date was over. That left me plenty of time to turn that pretty little girl into a steak in silk stockings. Admittedly, the uniform distracted me slightly... but I suppose the money you spend in THIS place isn't all on the food. The uniform that she wore was the classic one that men fantasize about... white, frilly, lacy parts and a moderately short dress, black stockings and a little garter, complete with high heels. Pity these sort of things don't exist in Japan. The restaurant itself was just as decorated - I noticed Teresa glance around but the snob in me loved it and drank in every moment of it's beauty. Glorious carmine red curtains swooped across the windows and the chandeliers swaying gently glistened like diamonds. Behind Teresa at a point just out of the corner of her eye was a marvelous picture that looked like it was depicting the Crusades. Men in heavy yet artistic armour with graceful lances meeting for battle. A little bloodshed was dabbed on... the fallen body of one such soldier, trodden on like the dirt he lay among and bleeding from an arrow wound. Gazing at it only made me all the more hungry and cast my thoughts to the waitress. Nevertheless, that banquet would have to wait. I browsed up and down the menu, trying to make out the other dishes in French when I heard a plate being placed on the table. The waitress may as well have slapped Teresa's face. In front of her was the french equivalent of a quarter pound burger without the bread. Just a piece of meat decorated with various and presumably inedible vegetables. I tried to hide my face behind the menu again as Teresa's well-known temper was beginning to surface. 'This is it?!' I can only assume that the waitress imagined her to be my ungrateful wife from the thoughts in her head. Bearing in mind that I speak little french, she certainly thought it loud enough for me to recognize what she was thinking. She walked off with her nose held high and uttering something that human ears would miss. I hope nobody else was watching us when I saw Teresa blatantly raise her middle finger behind the waitress' back. After a few more moment's silence, I heard a plate slid in front of me and the sound of her heels marching away. My steak, oozing off-colour blood. I had to eat or risk looking less than human. Choosing the lesser of two evils, I speared it with my fork in an attempt to release as much of that blood as possible. Mmmm.... I had to act as polite and well mannered as possible but every chew tempted me to savagely tear open the meat and let the blood drip into my mouth. But, for Teresa's sake, I thought I'd stay with a knife and fork. I had to say something at this point and with no worry of the food going the wrong way and choking me, my speech was distorted a little as it came out past some steak going the opposite way. 'Does most women gesture the waitress like that?' Who knows, perhaps even though she was usually a tough woman who looked as though she lacked class, perhaps she was the same as me. Although I wore the clothes of a mad metal musician, deep inside me, the vampire in my craved taste, art, the finer things in life. If she asked, I could simply say I mistook it for another culture's custom of gratitude. 'I'm not what you consider most women.' She replied. Hmm.. touché, Teresa. Uh-oh... the waitress... sorry, THAT waitress had come back, now holding a bottle of champagne. And she was looking directly at me. She asked something that I couldn't understand, but offered the bottle. I simply replied with 'Oui, Merci.' She giggled and began to fill my glass, completely ignoring Teresa. Again, for the sake of my date, I had to make it look as though I was displeased with the way she acted but putting up with it for her. I noticed the waitress give a nasty scowl to Teresa and left the bottle behind for me, refusing to pour a glass for her. This time, she saw the full hand sign that Teresa gave. Now I was under pressure. Customers looked from all directions at us as Teresa refused to retract her hand. I had no choice but to take her hand back to the fork and go back to my own meal. She gave me a look that had 'Spoil-Sport' written all over it. I sighed heavily. 'MUST you do that?' 'Do what?' she answered innocently. 'You know what' I wearily said. 'The bird?' 'Yes. The Bird.' 'Yep!' I looked at her disgracefully. Clearly, if this... 'romance' thing.... is a lucky reaction between two matching people, then it certainly wasn't the right combination at this table. I remained silent, concentrating on the bloody meat. I was ready to write off this experiment as a failure. And if love is so hard to acquire, like she said, then I would have to go through so many more experiences like this. A drain on my money, which was in short supply anyway thanks to that bastard, Kannon. A bigger drain on my patience, which I have only myself to blame for the lack of. It wasn't for a while that I noticed a constant trickle that I noticed Teresa had really taken to the champagne. Her face was becoming extremely red and it was showing from the look on her face that it was starting to take effect. I knew she was dying to ask me something... what it was, I couldn't tell. The alcohol was slowing her brain down and if it can be called 'reading' a mind, then I was looking at The Old Testament written by a pre-schooler. 'Are you really a vampire?' I should have seen it coming. Only one week ago, my patience left me and in front of Darkstar and Scourge, the body mass that I normally keep hidden in my stomach to keep my wings flattened flowed into my arms and legs. I diverted the blood in me to help give these new muscles life and in a moment, the overweight, pale skinned Ozzy Osbourne look-alike had turned into a monster with whipping arms and inhuman strength. Either she was extremely busy at the time, or thinks that several thousand fans are fooled by good makeup and prosthetic artists. I gave her a stare... nobody could be THAT ignorant. She felt it, alright. She'd pushed THE button. After taking another glass of champagne down, her courage came back and she carried on. 'Vampires have fangs right? So, let me see or do I have to give you a taste of Southern Comfort and find out myself? And yes, I do mean my wrestling move so wipe that fake confused look off your face.' If this is how humans show affection for one another, then I think I may have given the wrong message to several wrestlers on many occasions. I plunged the last piece of meat into my mouth before looking for something to wash it down with. To my dismay - and shock - she'd taken more than three quarters of the entire bottle! I made do with it... now was hardly the time. 'If you're finished, I'll show you. But not here...' I purred at her. She was up like a shot and probably would have dragged me away, literally, if the waitress hadn't approached me for the bill. Well, with only a few hundred french francs left, I could tell I'd have to lay off the pranks for a while. This date has been more of a bloodsucker than I was ever capable of. Nerezza puts his pen down for a moment as Gaz and Natalia are heard coming through the door. The both of them are chit-chatting loudly and probably don't notice he is even in. 'Gaz called himself a ladies man once... how in Vorador's name does he do it?' The vampire sighs to himself, then clicks the top of the ball-point and starts scribbling once more. I led her along the street we met in, past the fountain where it began. The sun was getting a little easier on my eyes and a gorgeous effect happened to that fountain. It looked like liquid fire, flowing yet burning. I can understand why this city is so well renowned. But also heavily populated. Obviously, we were not the only ones about. Couples, singles, workers... even one of those pathetic street mimes. I noticed one of those couples staggering out of an alleyway, with a 'guess what we've been up to?' look smeared across their faces like the girl's rose lipstick. It looked like in Paris, one respects another's wish for privacy. It also looked like I had found my little place... my hiding hole. With a gentle tug on her hand, I beckoned with one finger and walked backwards, step by step into that alley. 'Follow me, Teresa...' As soon as I saw her shadow blend in with that of the buildings, I stepped closer. She hesitated, leaning against a wall in a defiant posture. 'This better not be a joke or I swear, you WILL be hurt.' My, my... she was stalling. But then again, she couldn't be blamed. Swimming in her tipsy curiosity, she'd followed a man that, if rumour was correct, bites people until they bleed, then sucks the very blood from their bodies under cover of seclusion. She was Daniel, standing only one step away from The Lion's Den. I inched backwards, further into the alley. Her courage was vanishing and mine was restored. 'What's the matter?' I taunted. 'Scared of the dark?' She almost became one of those lions... snarling because not only was it somewhat of an insult to a girl of such unusual character, but also because it was partly true. Darkness never scares us... it's what lurks IN the dark that we fear. But she wanted to prove that fear was not around her. 'No.' she said, proud and brave. Now the fly was a mere inch from my parlour... 'Come closer then...' I purred. She relaxed her pose and the click, click, click, click of her heels said that the spider was ready to pounce... but not today. I could have had her... she was hypnotized by me. Well... if we had the power to hypnotize, I'm sure she would be, but that's just a metaphor. But seeing we'd been spotted together, it wouldn't bode well for her bloodless body to turn up one night after being seen with a man who 'plays' a vampire. I repressed the thoughts anyway - I had the attention of a young waitress. Though her personality was bitter, the sheer elitist, snooty attitude she had would be sweeter than all the candy in Paris. Teresa looked at my face with a child's curiosity. I slowly gave a smile, showing that indeed, my teeth not only had an irregular sharpness to them but the canine teeth, upon my wish, would extend to just above my lower gums. And just like a child, she forgot the rule about poison ivy. Look, but don't touch. She reached out with those delicate hands but the temptation to bite her was becoming greater and greater. The steak from the restaurant was an appetizer... I wanted more but HAD to control myself. I closed my lips and nodded slightly, then decided to make my way back out of that alley. The situation was pushing my instincts towards my predatory nature and I was unable to control them. Nor was she able to control what the champagne was telling her, as I found out soon enough. 'Wait, what about your wings?' I stopped as she took my hand in both of hers. Vampires of my species, the Ireulim, are born with wings. Well, re-born in most cases. These wings are like the breasts of a woman, not shown to anyone you didn't totally trust and certainly not something to take lightly. I had learned from my mentor, Rabbarius, that when a vampire is looking for a companion of the opposite gender, the male of the Ireulim would spread his wings and the female would usually seek the one with the largest wings as a sign of physical superiority. I guess no matter which species you are, human or vampire, one thing is true to them all: Size matters. I couldn't compare to others, but I felt somewhat... less than well-endowed with my wings. I guess insecurities like this are always present in males. But whichever member of the front office had gotten a look at my physical exam had now made it a widely-spread rumour that I had wings. I thought I had those photographs shredded, but after the No Ladder match and Skitzo's insane ramblings, it was now the second biggest rumour about me. And here I was, in a sheltered alley with a human girl asking to see both my biggest pride and most closely guarded secret. I had to ignore the fact that while I was familiar with human etiquette and manners, she knew nothing of my kind. 'You know, vampires have wings. Can I see yours, please?' Well, at least in my shock, I'd gotten over my urge to bite her. She really wanted to see them though. Her face was like that of a child once more and her icy blue eyes looked like she was at her 5th birthday, expecting a magician to perform a trick. 'Promise you won't laugh?' I said. The shakiness in my voice was easily detectable as she picked on one of my most sensitive issues. 'I promise. If I do, you can kick my ass on Fusion this Friday.' she cooed with confidence and honesty. I could tell that she meant it. Well, here goes nothing. I removed my dinner jacket and began shifting some of my body mass to support the lifeless flaps of skin on my back. They were already beginning to 'inflate'. I unbuttoned my ruffled shirt and handed them both to her. The evening air was cold and the sound of the water running from the fountain did nothing to make me feel any warmer. It felt as though I was in the arctic and close to the sub zero waters. It only made my wings stiff and rigid, unwilling to stretch out. I turned my mind to the bath I was in when I left the hotel to pursue this date, imagining that the sound of the fountain was the water flowing from the tap. I soon felt warm again as I tricked myself into believing I was soaking in that relaxing bath... and my wings gently and gracefully unfolded. She stood in their beauty... it's not the first time I've shocked someone. The very reason I named my finishing move was because it would, in my opinion, leave my opponent in 'Awe Of He'. She was no different. Her chin could have touched her toes. I knew someone was not too far away and wanted to end the demonstration as fast as I could. His... or her... breathing was quiet, but I knew about it. They were wary about stepping around the corner, probably waiting their turn in this alley. What Teresa said next didn't help things at all... 'OH MY GOSH! HOW HUGE! I have to touch them...' she gasped. I recoiled. Not even if the combined harem of all the women in WT... be it Anarchy, Cyclonica, Aimz, Brandi, Ellie, Teresa, Aimz, Tempest or Jane Reagan were tempting me with sexual teases would I let anything that wasn't a vampire touch them. 'No please, this is the first time I’ve showed them.' I whimpered. I backed up, retracting them a little but still giving her what she asked for. A view, nothing more. But it was futile, the champagne was telling her that she wanted to touch them. 'Please? I promise. I won’t hurt you. I just want to touch.' Like a trigger on a gun, I jumped as soon as I felt those wings she desired so badly touch the wall behind me. I was cornered and there was no way possible that I could climb the walls to escape. Flying was completely out of the question. I pulled them back against my spine and snatched my clothes from her, putting them on as fast as I could while I bolted past her, knocking her aside. Doing the only thing I could think of, I screamed out with a word that won't be written in this diary, save to say that it gave enough of a distraction for me to slip out of sight and scramble up a building before anyone noticed me. Only Teresa and a few french people will know about that... With the alarm raised, I had to slowly make my way back to the restaurant. A little after closing time, I hung around the back entrance where the girl was still in her uniform and cleaning up. Bags of what appeared to be the remains of what wasn't eaten were being placed into the gigantic bin around the back. I knew exactly what to do with the little waitress. She jumped when she saw me, but looked at me as though she was expecting me anyway. I'd learned enough about charming on this one day, taking her hand and kissing the top. 'Enchanté...' I said. She said something I couldn't understand, too much french. I merely responded with '.....Anglais? English?' She giggled at me. 'You speak only a little, monsieur? You are quite the old-fashioned type.' She gave me a little smile and I playfully yanked her towards me, taking her off her feet for a moment and trapping her next to me with her own arm. She was facing away from me now and I pulled away the dress on her shoulder. She gasped as my lips kissed her from there, then slowly up towards her neck... and I finally 'NEREZZA!' The vampire jumps as the voice of Gaz breaks the concentration of his diary entry. 'Gaz... you startled me.' Nerezza panted. 'Why's there a french maid's outfit on your bed?' The Jester asked. 'Oh...' Nerezza smirked. 'Novelty gift for Natalia. Although I'm sure you'll appreciate it more than she will. I would have left it on yours, but I didn't want her to see it and I figured that you'd walk into my room and she wouldn't. It'd be better if you gave it to her, you know?' 'Hm, good thinking, Squire. Let's get a move on though, I want to try out the gym this place has before we leave.' he motions with his thumb and turns to go. 'Okay... just let me finish up here.' Nerezza grunts as he stands. The vampire adds one more line to his diary, then clicks his pen to retract the nib and follows Gaz towards the elevator. The final line says: ' In conclusion, experiment failed... but not a total loss.'
