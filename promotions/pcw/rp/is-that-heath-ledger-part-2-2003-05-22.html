Is That Heath Ledger? (Part 2)

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 22, 2003 The last that we saw of the young, aspiring prankster and his less than compliant counter part was on the set of an upcoming wrestling movie entitled “To the Ring and Back”. Tyler Preston, a very serious protégé of the Pranksters with Attitudes, took it upon himself to pull a prank on John Gargano’s over controlling agent, Mick Varelli. John pleaded with Tyler not to go through with it, but everyone that knows Tyler Preston knows that those pleas were unsuccessful. Decked out as acting superstar Heath Ledger, Tyler goes off on his own to fool the unsuspecting agent for all the trouble he has caused over the past month. Tyler circles around the ring that is being set up in the center of the scenery. He bumps in to one of the tank-topped workers. The worker shoots him an angry glance. “Watch where you are going buddy. I don’t come in to your place of work and try to keep you from getting things done,” the gruff voice growls as his bearded face turns back to the ring where he is currently screwing in some bolts. Super star or no super star, this man was giving Tyler no respect, and continues trying to get things done. Tyler brushes off the hostile words from the overweight man and continues on his way. His bright blue eyes scan the entire premises. Finally, Tyler’s face lights up as he spots Mick Varelli standing in the corner gabbing away on his cellular phone as usual. He looks more than professional in his black, three-piece suit. He moves his hands as he speaks as if the person on the other line will actually see him. Tyler figures that it’s either now or never, so we walks over to the corner, not wanting it to look obvious that he is targeting Mick. “What was he doing there in the first place? I said that he is either in or out, and he told me that he wasn’t going to go through with it,” Mick barks in to the phone, nodding his head up and down. He adjusts his red power tie and looks around as the person on the other end fills him in on the requested information. Mick then focuses in on Tyler, who is walking about minding his own business, conveniently looking just like the famous acting star Heath Ledger. Mick’s eyes grow to the size of bowling balls as he almost drops his small, cellular phone. “Donny, I’m going to have to call you back. Some huge business just came up, but if things go the way I think they will, you better get the champagne ready,” Mick says with confidence as he hangs up the phone before listening to a reply. He shoves the phone in his front pocket and jogs over to Tyler who is looking off in to the distance. “Excuse me, sir? You look awfully familiar, do I know you from somewhere?” Asks Mick, trying to make it seem like he is the one in power and that the Heath look-alike doesn’t own any respect from this man. Tyler just chuckles and prepares a witty response that only Heath himself would pull off. “That depends, have you ever gotten out to the movie theatre?” Tyler responds with a question of his own. He smiles at Mick, throwing the ball in to his court. Tyler stands back, interested at what Mick’s response may be. “Wait a minute, I think I do know. Keithe Pledger, am I correct?” Mick continues his oblivious act, but it isn’t fooling anyone. If this were really Heath Ledger, it may prove to be a tad offensive. Tyler doesn’t mind though, glad that Mick is playing right in to his hand. “Actually, that’s Heath Ledger, but you were pretty close,” Tyler says. He tries to create the illusion in Mick’s head that he is actually getting somewhere with the super star. “Oh that is right. Heath Ledger, how are you doing man. I’ve actually caught one of your flicks the other night and I must say it isn’t the worse thing that I have seen,” Mick says, hinting at a compliment but at the same time, not giving to much respect to the assumed super star. “Yea, well I guess it’s just a blessing. I’m glad to be doing this whole acting thing. Unfortunately my agent just ups and quit on me the other day. I’m struggling to find something, but I can’t complain yet,” says Tyler, knowing that this piece of information will get Mick into a frenzy. A sudden jolt of excitement exits Mick’s body but he sustains it with much control. “Really?! Some agents can be more than heartless. Not me though. I picked up this young actor named John Gargano, you just ask him, I’m like a second father to him,” Mick brags as he tugs on the front of his jacket. Tyler smiles gladly as things are working and that Mick is clueless. “Well you just tell that Gargano guy that he is one lucky person. I would kill for an agent like you. You seem like a very nice guy,” Tyler compliments. Mick’s cheeks get a rosy red from embarrassment. A man of this status before has never complimented him. “I’ll tell you what Heath, I think that I could work that out. I can’t make any promises, but you seem like a nice guy that I wouldn’t mind allowing to jump on my ship,” states Mick. Mick begins to think incredibly high of him. After all, he is manipulating a man that has been in a wide array of movies. “No, I couldn’t cause that much trouble for you. The only things that I would do his hold your company down. I just came around this set to talk to the director. Maybe he has a small job for me,” Tyler says as an act of pity, wanting Mick to jump at it and plead that he join his company. “It’s really not that much of a problem. John is set with this movie, so I have a little down time that I can donate to someone else. That someone could be you,” says Mick as if his time is so precious. He puts on an act that he is willing to be a great humanitarian and help out this already huge actor. “That’s awfully kind of you Mick, but I can’t put you through that,” Tyler says as he attempts to walk away. Mick jogs up next to him to make sure that he doesn’t get to far. Mick smiles, but doesn’t want to seem desperate. Mick puts his suited arm on Tyler’s shoulder and begins to walk with him. “Heather, trust me man, there is plenty of room for you in the family. I actually think that you career is pretty established, and won’t be hard to keep going,” says Mick. He finally musters out a compliment, but doesn’t want the star to feel to much at power. Tyler laughs glad that Mick finally broke down. “Wow, you are truly willing to go out on a limb like that?” Asks Tyler one more time to see if Mick is really sincere. “I wouldn’t be the guy that I am if I turned you away,” Mick offers. Everything seems to be going good for Mick Varelli. Tyler scratches his chin to give Mick’s offers a thought. He finally comes to a consensus and informs Mick. “You are a man above all men Mick. I would be glad to join your company. Show me where to sign and I’ll do it right this minute,” Tyler says enthusiastically, wanting to sell this prank. Mick jumps up in the air and gives a fist pump. When he lands, he looks around to make sure that no one saw. He pulls a piece of paper out of his pocket and unfolds it. “Here is a rough copy of one, we’ll make another copy later. Just sign this dotted line. It’s just the basic requirements,” Mick coaxes and he shoves a bronze plated ink pen in Tyler’s face. Tyler has no choice but to grasp the pen. Tyler looks over the white piece of paper and quickly skims it. He chuckles as he presses the button on the ink pen and prepares to right. Mick looks away to give “Heath” his privacy as he makes the big transition. Inside Mick, he is more excited than he ever has before. Tyler prepares to stun Mick. He lowers the pen to the paper and scribbles the signature of Tyler Preston. He folds the paper up and hands it to Mick. “There you go Mick Varelli, it was truly a pleasure doing business to you…I mean business with you,” Tyler stammers. Mick smiles and offers a handshake to the newest employee. Tyler receives it with a firm grip of his own. “I’ll be in contact with you Heath,” Mick finishes the deal. He begins to walk away from Tyler when Tyler grabs him by the shoulder and keeps him in place. “Oh wait, there is one thing I forgot to mention. I don’t work with scum!” Tyler exclaims as he shoves the face of Mick Varelli away from him. Tyler walks away laughing. Mick quickly grabs for his pocket to argue that he already signed the contract. He unfolds it to find that Tyler’s name is written on the dotted line. Mick rips up the paper in rage and throws it on the ground. He cusses a few times and gets on his cellular phone quickly, ready to call someone that can get Tyler in legal trouble even though Tyler had done nothing wrong. John watches from the other corner of the set, nervous as Mick’s reaction, hoping that it doesn’t have any negative repercussion on his career. Tyler passes John as he laughs. He shoots John a quick win.
