Senses and senselessness

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/11/02 'You didn't even hear me, did you? You didn't realize I was in front of you when I stood there and you didn't realize I was behind you until you felt me. I saw you. I saw you as a meal on legs. I saw you as a worthless victim, nothing more. I'll see many more victims soon. All I need is a little fuel... you're perfect. What do you see? You probably see a brick wall. You probably see stars up above... how poetic and touching. You probably see red too, a little slipping past your eyes. Doesn't matter. Your sight will be taken in a moment. I see the back of your filthy, unwashed head. I see little ticks and lice in there. I see your neck, pulsing like a raging river. And I see my claws going right through you. What do you feel? You feel something you've never felt before. You can't even begin to describe it. Not once in your life have you had this sensation. That's because you only feel it once... we all do. It's called death. The one thing everyone fears. I've been there and done that. But like everything, once you've done it, you'll probably be indifferent to it. The majority of people seem to do it once and then stay dead, so it must be what every last one of you humans need. What do I feel? I feel something warm trickling into my palm and collecting like a pool underneath a waterfall. I feel your sagging, limp body struggling to convince itself that it's still going. I feel the bones inside you. It must be odd, feeling something moving around inside you and you can't scratch it or do anything about it. You're in the seat and buckled in... helpless. I feel the same little emotion that I get when I throw someone on the ground, or hear that bell ring after a count of three and it's me pressing the shoulders down. What do you smell? Well, that doesn't matter, seeing your nose is clogged with some rather nasty fluids right now. I'm sure that if you could inhale, you'd smell what I smell. I smell that lovely, coppery, metallic little scent like a coin. I smell fear. And we can... I smell it all over you. I can understand something, very quiet but clear. Through a few sobs, it's sooo clear... I....na... e... I...ana... ie... I..n..na...die.... I.dn...wna... die... I don....wan....die... I don't wanna die... I don't wanna die... But die, you shall. Ignorant humans like you consider yourselves the top of the food chain. It's inconceivable that you, almighty man, could be so vulnerable. Even with all your technology and advancements, all your recorded knowledge, you still couldn't stop this happening. And I smell your disgusting sweat, just like I do every week from you ungroomed humans. Now I have to put up with that stench, multiplied by Vorador knows how many times as little groups of four pile around me like a mass burial. What can you hear? You can hear me, whispering in your ear, lecturing you about what a weak and pathetic species you are. You can hear drip after drip of something splattering on the ground. You can hear the rustle of your own clothing as you squirm like a maggot on the end of a fishing hook. You can hear your head pounding madly like some crazed drummer. You can hear music from a car. I can hear something else. I hear your heartbeat slowing down. I hear your lungs expanding and falling and trying to deal with the fluid leaking into them. I hear gurgling from all that blood gushing around in places it's not meant to. I hear you SUFFOCATE. I hear something in the future. It's the toll of a bell when they find you, cut you up, put you in a box and either immolate you or cast you into the ground. I'm used to bells signifying the end of something. Every single week, a bell tolls for someone else. You might get used to it. What do you taste? We both taste the same thing here... but it's different to us. You taste copper. I taste sugar. You can't stand it, can you? If you bite the inside of your both, you spit as much as you can to rid yourself of the sensation. But your neck is like candy to me... make a hole in the wrapper and let the saccharin flow. And I have such a sweet tooth. You taste death, fear, defeat, sorrow and despair. I taste blood, renewed life, euphoria, satisfaction and victory. And now what? You see nothing. You can't smell. Your hearing tells you you're dreaming. Your taste is numbed. Your body can't even feel these claws in you... you're, in simple terms, expired...' Nerezza flung his arm to the side and slung the corpse from his massive nails. It impacted the wall and sunk to the floor in a crumpled-up heap like a marionette with severed strings. The vampire raised his dripping, moist, warm claw up to his lips and slurped up the last of the remaining blood from his tentacle-like digits. As though some form of narcotic had just entered his body, he sighed with happiness and staggered backwards against the opposite wall. The sound of his own giggling accompanied him and his eyes fluttered closed. His tongue darted in and out of his lips, devouring any remaining blood and painting his teeth hideous scarlet. 'I've got to stop transforming so much...' the Living Nightmare whispered to himself. 'The refueling cost is killing me.'
