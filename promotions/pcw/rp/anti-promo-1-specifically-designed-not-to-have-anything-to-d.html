Anti-Promo #1: Specifically Designed not to have anything to do with my matches!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Gak! So? Ya want relevance? YOU WANT PURPOSE? I'll write what I want, when I want, and you're going to like this tripe! All I can warn you is that this promo is a spoof on the President's State of the Union Address tonight, and reads something like a match written by Clyde. *Skylar Green, dressed as President Bush, walks up to the podium in front of the PCW staff and crew.* Green/Bush:'Thank you all for coming to the State of the Union Address. I think we're all in agreement that if I just stuck to doing this, which is apparently the only thing that the Constitution requires of me, things would probably be a lot better off now. But, lemme just tell ya how things are going.' He releases a sigh and gets started. Green/Bush:'First of all, for those of you who just got released from prision, just illegally immigrated to this country, or if you just woke up from a coma, let me introduce myself. I'm President George Walker Bush and I'm President and you're not! NEENER, NEENER, NEEEENER!' *A 54-year-old man stands up with a microphone and points at Green/Bush in an accusing manner.* 'Just who the hell do you think you are?' he shouts in a question. *Green/Bush is confused at the question. Then again, paper confuses him.* Green/Bush:'I... *pauses* I just said.' *The man in the audience keeps going.* 'Well, let me tell you who I am. I'm *pause and RVD thumb pose* Vincent.... *RVD thumb pose* Kennedy.... *RVD thumb pose* McMahon!' *Following this pointless self-shilling outburst, he sits back down.* Green/Bush:'Uh... right. Anyway, I've got a lot of stuff to talk about, so let's get started. First, there is some worry as to if democracy will be preserved with a Republican dominated Congress. Well, don't worry, we've totally gotten rid of that. All Democratic candidates and office-holders ANYWHERE in the country have been liquidated and replaced with super-efficient cyborgs programmed to follow every Republican command. We'll soon do the same thing to the Liberatarians and Green Parties. FEAR ME!' *There are some murmurs about people in the crowd who are considering switching party affiliations. Green/Bush continues.* Green/Bush:'Next, there is growing concern that our prisons are filling up too much. Believe me, I feel your concern. That's why by tomorrow morning 10 AM EST, everyone currently incarcerated anywhere in the US prison system, whether federal, state, municipality, township, locked in the gallows, or just stuck in a corner with a dunce-cap on, will be executed WITHOUT FAIL.' *A pink suit stands up and leaves, Diane Sawyer running out to get one last interview with Robert Blake before he dies.* Green/Bush:'Third, some people have asked me about the sagging economy. They are dead now.' *Everyone's expecting a joke and some laugh, but Green/Bush is serious and just goes on to the next point.* Green/Bush:'Fourth, we're invading Iraq because it sucks and has nuclear weapons. And stuff.' *Saddam Hussein stands up from the crowd.* Saddam:'Bush, you wanna come on TV and run your mouth! Talking about how you're gonna defend American values! How you're gonna protect democracy! Well, why don't you see what's gonna happen when The Dam puts on the oppressed people's shoes... Walks down the oppressed people's ramp... Gets into the oppressed people's ring... And proceeds to whip your candy... no wait, I outlawed candy... um.......WHIP YOUR GOAT ASS! So don't sing it, JUST BRING IT!' *Saddam stretches his hand out to Green/Bush.* Saddam:'So what do you think about that?' *Green/Bush takes a gulp after being shown up and initiates a response.* Green/Bush:'Well--' Saddam:'IT DOESN'T MATTER WHAT YOU THINK!' *Saddam takes the mic and holds his head all the way back as he yells into the microphone.* Saddam: 'IF YA SMELELELELELELELELELELELELELLLLLL-OW! WHAT I-RAQ..... IS..... COOKIN'!' *Jay Leno stands up at the opposite end of the room.* Jay Leno:'Hey, that was my bit on my show!' Handler:'But you didn't do it with enough energy, so kindly shut your ass up and SIT THE FUCK DOWN!' Jay Leno:'..... Got me there.' *Eric Bischoff runs out and hits a Diamond Cutter on Jay Leno.* Bischoff:'Bitch!' *Bischoff and his terrible, terrible hair-dye job walks out, wearing the same black jeans and black leather jacket that he wears every Monday night because he doesn't realize that the James Dean look was only popular for James Dean. Green/Bush starts his speech again.* Green/Bush:'To generate more money for the economy, I'm going to allow for oil and mineral drilling in all national parks!' *SWAT teams escort out certain members of the audience.* Green/Bush:'Some people think that that's a bad idea. Well, as you can see, the Thought Police are rounding them up for extermination up as I speak. Uh, fifth... Good news! We no longer have a population problem in the country.' *Green/Bush gives a thumbs up to Vernon, the Racist Wrestling Goblin.* Vernon:'Yes... that solves part of the problem. But the minorities are still around.' Green/Bush:'What do you suggest?' Vernon:'I say that you send all minorities, including women and children, to Iraq to use as soldiers.' Green/Bush:'We don't have that many weapons.' Vernon:'Who said anything about weapons? As for myself, I request 100000 tons of napalm and a Howitzer with a never-ending supply to conquer the entire Pacific rim.' Green/Bush:'Not much of a conquest, but done!' *Vernon the Racist Wrestling Goblin walks out, making sure to lift up his white KKK robe so he doesn't trip over it. Green/Bush's servants bring out a globe.* Green/Bush:'After Iraq, we'll need to invade and blow up another country after Iraq. Really, that'll be like a 5-day war at most. Iran's not important enough, and North Korea's just in the axis of evil because we needed an REALLY Asian country in there. So I have a globe here. Tell you what I'm gonna do. I'm going to spin this globe and throw a dart. Wherever it lands, we'll invade that country and destroy every living thing within its boundaries!' *Spins globe and throws dart.* Green/Bush:'There, after Iraq, we'll invade the... United States of America! Sounds like a cesspool of communism and Estonians! Hear me, America, your days are numbered!' *Everyone runs out panicking and screaming. Green/Bush is left in an empty room with Sandy/Laura at his side.* Green/Bush:'Lastly, I make the following decrees -- goths, rappers, and surfers are banned from the country! That means that Skylar Green wins Tuesday Turmoil and Friday Fusion by default!' *Laura/Sandy pats him on the back.* Laura/Sandy:'Very nice, dear.' Green/Bush:'You mean it?' Laura/Sandy: *Sighs*'... No, not really. But the pay is good.' *Nodding, Green/Bush leaves the podium.* Green/Bush:'Damn, right.' *End*
