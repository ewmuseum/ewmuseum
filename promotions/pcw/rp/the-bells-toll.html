The Bells Toll...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: (Turmoil #1) The bells toll for thee… I can hear them…they ring constantly in my head…like an alarm. It is quite alarming, hearing their chimes as a signal of the impending end. But I am used to them…the bells are the music to which I dance. Dance…the dance of prophecy… It was dark that night…the moon shimmered along the ocean waters like a row of diamonds. Many mortals considered this sight to be a beautiful thing…a pretty event of the planet. Me…it was just atmosphere…environment. It would all pass away in due time…the moon shattering away like the big dinner plate in the sky it is, the waters evaporating into nothing as the sun reaches its highest heat…one last flare before its extinction, ushering in an icy death for this planet. That was the order of events…for the planet anyway. The people…however…would be long gone by the time that happened. If the god and I have anything to do with it, that is. A lot of people were walking around dressed in formal attire, their suits and their ties, women in their cocktail dresses and their nylons. There were various shows scattered all over this vessel, all of which had some form of PCW star involved. Pretty Cool Guys were holding a show over in the dinner hall, Heartbreakers were doing some Broadway routine at the theater…whatever…El Diablo was starring in a one man skit he directed about the life of the Mexican Devil. Just about everyone had something to do that night to promote the pay per view the next night. We do not…promote well. What kind of act could the god put on anyway? Banishments? That was no parlor trick, as the politicians put it so lightly a while ago…there was matter behind that act…one they shall never understand. That’s why they call Akiyama a magician…because that is the only logical explanation their feeble minds can produce. But they…they’re worthless at this point… All of the tag teams are worthless… When the Neo Void God made his return, he had one goal set out, and that was to become the God of Tag. By doing so, he sought to prove the ineffectiveness of the collective humanity, as they cannot defeat him in both singularity and plural standards. He has achieved his goal…he is now and forever more the God of Tag, having possessed the Tag Team titles alone, and rendering each tag team in his path to be nothing more than worthless. Now that the mission is achieved…it’s time for the next step… World domination… Silent night it was that night…aside from the inside activities and the random roaming chattering band of humans, the boat was silent. Everyone was in anticipation for the next day’s event. Wrestle Vessel… battle on the boat. It really didn’t matter where the god fought…as long as he made waste of the opposition. PCW provided us with a suite on the vessel, with a sitting room, two bedrooms, and a balcony with a view of the ocean behind the boat. It was pretty much useless, considering we don’t have use for beds or bathrooms much. Most of the day Akiyama sat in front of the window and glared at the sun, like he was upset with it for shining down on him. I wandered the boat half the time; with the other half of the time I sat with Akiyama and thought on strategies and future. Room service kept bugging us about dining…no matter how many times we told them we didn’t need their food. Finally they knocked one time too many, and the god crushed their knuckles. That proved to be an interesting event for the day, as everyone in the area peered out of their rooms at the weeping cabin boy with the crippled hands curled up on the ground. I didn’t hear his scream…over the bells anyway… They were ringing hard at that point…hammering my head with their melody. What melody, you ask? It was the melody of our opponents on Sunday. Which melody? That’s none of your business, you nosy flat-scan…you’ll just have to wait until it happens. And there we stood…the nVg and me…the constant loiterers, as we have no specific place we belong in. We stood on the balcony facing the back of the boat, watching the ripples in the water as the boat surged forward towards its destination. He leaned against the wall, with his huge arms folded over his broad chest, his head lowered, his eyes closed, and his ponytail draped over his shoulder. I leaned against the wall as well, one leg over the other and my head gazing out at the stars. I held the tag team titles in my arms, cradling them like a precious child in the hands of a loving mother. But I cared nothing for them…neither did the god. Not anymore… The job was done…and they were pretty much worthless. Much like the competition around here was worthless, and ineffective. There was a bigger prize on the line now, that being the Wold title. Turmoil was the night that would send the Neo Void God into the top of this federation. He faced three lightweights for a chance to face the champion for the belt. Biggest bout to date…and we would not squander this opportunity to further prove our superiority amongst the people. I rubbed my fingertips along the hard surface of that gold. It scratched at my touch…and that brought a feeling of emptiness within me. Being the champ at this point held no meaning, and retaining them would be a waste of the god’s precious time. He could be doing better things, dishing out more damage, and terrorizing more people instead of simply bashing the same teams over and over night after night. Even deities get frustrated with repetition. Now we had some new blood to splatter on the ground. Christopher Chase…Rent A Villain…El Diablo… The saint…the villain…the devil…and the god…seemed like a fair grouping for a battle. More people walked by us, their eyes stopping upon us and staring in awe at our demanding presence. It’s understandable…not everyone has seen a god up close and personal before. I feel that by existing out here amongst the people I’ve grown a bigger understanding of them, more than I learned in the containment field I was captive in at the Dept. of Divine Examination in Japan. I spent a lifetime in there…I was due for a vacation anyway. I understand them better…that still doesn’t mean I don’t want to bring Universal Harmony upon them. Spill blood…awaken the Savage Tranquility…and plunge the universe into Universal Harmony…it was a simple task, but only the god is worthy of doing it. Just as he is worthy of earning that title shot…just as he is worthy of winning the title himself. Just imagine the blood of humans he can spill as champion…challengers will come left and right…and they’ll find themselves flung away like waste. Why not start wasting the blood now? Three vessels are ready, willing, and able to give their blood for a chance to fight the champion. I am hoping to have the ring stained in red by the time the bell rings for the victory of the god. Yes…the bells… Maddening they were… I never thought a sound would be so sickening… My hand trailed from the belts to my head, clutching my skull tightly as the bells rang out harder in my mind. An exasperated gasp came out from me as my back arched off the wall. I stepped forward, my eyes angered with the heavens. “I hear them! I hear them you bastards! You think you’re getting the message across??” my fist struck the air in a rage with the sprites that both gifted and cursed my psyche. 'Time is as abundant as the air these mortals breathe! Days and weeks are but seconds and minutes to the Neo Void God! I will not be rushed into anything! Everything is on course!” My eyes flickered from the skies to the belts, and then back to the skies again. “You want these?? This garbage?? Are you willing to give it all up for these? THEN HERE…TAKE YOUR GARBAGE AND LEAVE US BE!!” I took the sickening belts I held and pulled back to hurl them into the waters, hoping to satisfy the unknown forces. They were nothing to me now…what was done is done, and now all I wanted to do was get rid of them so that I could ease the bells pounding in my skull…or at least change the tune they were ringing to. Launching these pieces of matter might be enough to ease the bells…and silence them for the time being. Those other 6 men might be upset with that, but I cared nothing for mortals and their desires. I’d gladly bathe the planet in acid if it would only give my mind a break from the constant ringing of the bells. My arm shot forward…with enough power to possibly throw them back to shore… But I didn’t…and do you know why? The god stopped me. I looked back at the hand locked around my wrist, the hand wrapped in black tape. My eyes trailed up to his, which were stern and demanding. He shook his head slowly, almost a warning in appearance, as if he would had punished me for doing so. There was a motive behind his eyes…and I knew what he wanted. My arm lowered…almost in defeat…but this was no defeat…there will be no defeat come Sunday…there will be no defeat come Turmoil… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The next morning was Sunday…the day of the show called Wrestle Vessel. The bells tolled…still… But the bells will toll until someone answers them… They will be answered this day…as they will be answered on Turmoil. I found the god standing on the balcony at sunrise, his face twisted in thought. He was getting ready for combat…targeting his entire essence physically and mentally into becoming the god of battle…the monster god. It would be in this format that he would execute the plan we discussed last night for our bout with the three tag teams. It would only prove to be a warm up match of sorts for the more important battle afterwards. I could still hear the rhythm of the bells within me… … “Clubbed to Death” has such a nice ring to it…
