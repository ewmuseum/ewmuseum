SOCIETY Training

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 12, 2003 Begin Transmission. (In a small gym in an unknown location, SOCIETY sits, sweating profusely. Drops of sweat drip off of his forehead to the ground, as he awaits Mr Chang's next order.) “It’s time again, SOCIETY.” “Time for what?” the Conformist asked, his voice indicating weariness. “Time for your studies,” the voice shot back. “You must continue to study the tenets of the Order of Society and Logic.” “But Mr. Chang,” SOCIETY protested, looking and sounding something like a senior in high school who has no desire to do one more homework assignment, “I KNOW what the Order of Society and Logic is all about already! Why do I have to go over it again?” “Because, we all need refreshing in the tenets, even I, the president of the organization! Now go!” (An annoyed looking SOCIETY rises from his seat, taking his book, and walking to the other side of the gym. Michael Chang sits by himself, watching the hard bodies working out around him, as he pulls out a small book, with blank pages, and begins to write in it.) “Today is May 11, 2003. I must admit, Operation PCW has gone much more smoothly than I ever could have planned. I had to get SOCIETY away from me so I could write by telling him to study the tenets, tenets he probably knows better than anyone else in the order. But still, I have to be very, very careful around SOCIETY. His absolute obedience to me is the only thing that keeps me in a position of power.” (Chang looks up from writing, to spot SOCIETY on a leg press machine, reading the Tenets of the Order of Society and Logic, while effortlessly pressing out six-hundred pounds with his legs, in sets of fourteen repetition.) “His power is truly remarkable. We knew what we were doing when we took that test tube from Britain. More frightening than his power, however, is his mind. He is intelligent…almost too intelligent. For him to be of worth to the Order of Society and Logic as a soldier, he must not question me. If he ever does start to question me…I am in severe jeopardy. He outweighs me and is taller than me by great measures. If he ever wished to cause me bodily harm, I doubt I could do anything about it. He could return to the Falklands, and crush the entire Order in a matter of minutes. Our mission would lie in ruins, and we would be just another joke of history. That is why I am the only person who could accompany SOCIETY. Anyone else would not be able to keep renewing his indoctrination. I can.” (SOCIETY has moved over to the bench press, lifting up five-hundred and fifty pounds without any aid from the other gym patron who is spotting him. This is when SOCIETY is truly at his best: proving his might, strength, and intelligence to others.) “SOCIETY was engineered to be the perfect assassin, and that he is. But, he cannot be taken out on missions. The entire world would immediately turn to eliminate him, as it does all threats. He would be focused on, and eliminated immediately. Operation PCW has ceased to become a military operation carried out against VIPs. It is now an operation of education, to spread our message, and hope it takes hold. Rather than the gun and the knife, our weapons are now education and publicity. And, indirectly, the massive arms, legs, and chest of SOCIETY…” (Two dumbbells in hand, SOCIETY begins pressing the metallic weights skyward overhead. Finally, the monster begins showing signs of fatigue, his presses slowing, but a look of marked determination still all over him.) “His tests grow more laborious, as his notoriety rises. Two top stars on Turmoil, and two men he must battle solo on Friday Fusion. Agent Jackson, SyN, Makonie, and Brianna Washington are all SOCIETY cares about. And, as Sivasian Krikorian can tell you, once a truly good sniper gets his targets in his sights, it may as well be end game.” (The weights hit the padded gym mat with a resounding thud. SOCIETY walks back over to Chang, his “Conform” shirt off, revealing a massively muscled chest, and the somewhat haunting tattoo of ‘CONFORM’ across his chest.) “I am finished studying, President.” “Studying? It looked more to me as if you were working out.” “But Mr. Chang, that IS my studying! I must be prepared for the four individuals that I will face this week! Is that not more important than mere book learning?” (Chang picks up his journal, and hides it behind his back, nodding to SOCIETY.) “Yes it is, SOCIETY.” “Excellent! I cannot wait to enter the ring and crush more non-conformists!” (SOCIETY strides purposefully to the door, opening it, and headed for their car, leaving Chang alone.) “He’s perfect, all right. Way TOO perfect.” (Chang follows SOCIETY out of the gym, shaking his head.) End Transmission.
