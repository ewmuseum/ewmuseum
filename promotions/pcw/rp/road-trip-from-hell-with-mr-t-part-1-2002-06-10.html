Road Trip From Hell With Mr. T Part 1

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 10th *Monologue* On the road again, turn the page. *End Of Monologue* 'Are we there yet?' asked Graves 'No.' replied Frampton 'Where are we going again?' asked Graves 'Texas.' answered Frampton 'Why?' asked Graves 'To fix my life.' replied Frampton 'Why?' asked Graves 'I need to.' answered Frampton 'How?' asked Graves 'Two ways.' replied Frampton 'Which are?' asked Graves 'Either, a) make up with my parents or b) kill them.' answered Frampton 'Hmmm, tough choice.' replied Graves 'Not really.' answerd Mike 'What do you mean?' asked Graves 'I first try and make up. Then if that fails, I knife them.' replied Frampton 'Man you are screwed up.' said Graves 'You just learned this today?' asked Frampton 'Get some help, you scare me. I pity the fool as crazy as you.' said Graves 'Wrong, I PITY THE FOOL! Mr. T pity's the fool named Michael Graves, FOO!' said Mr. T/Frampton 'Ah Mike?' said Graves 'Whose Mike FOOL? I know no Mike!' said Mr. T/Frampton 'Mr. T?' asked Graves 'The one and only.' said Mr. T/Frampton 'WOO! I LOVE YOU!' said Graves 'Ah man, this is sad. Get a woman.' said Mr. T/Frampton 'I'm not gay.' said Graves 'Sure your not.' said Mr. T/Frampton 'LOOOK OUT!' screamed Graves **CRASH** (1)To Be Continued(1)
