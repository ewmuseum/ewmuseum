A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Norticfest

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/03 The skinheads make me sick, everything they believe and hold to be so true is bullshit. Basing beliefs off of the fact that one race is better then the others…how is one race any better then another? White people cause just as many problems, as minorities, but they just pass the blame off on someone else…another race. See, that’s what bothers me so much about them. A true white powerest would step up and take the rings and do all the things they preach. None of them do it though, they just say they hate minorities…none of them with enough of a pair to act on it. When was the last time you saw a hate crime in the paper? For me it’s been a while, in fact the most common hate crimes are against homosexuals…they aren’t even a minority. Sure the skinheads think they are sick, but I am sure half the people behind those hoods, have engaged in a little homosexual activity. Making their beliefs pointless, showing that they can’t even back up what they believe in. The whole organization is just corrupt, a bunch of scared kids reaching out to each other for support. Each coming from a broken home of sorts, being brainwashed by these people, telling them that they belong…letting them believe that they actually do. The whole process makes me sick…wishing that I could just forget about them and move on with my life, but alas I can’t. Mark Makonie isn’t going to let me; he forced me to join the skinheads…to go back to being the Chameleon. This is the only way I can hunt him down and get Haley back. Entering his world, meeting his people and pretending to hate minorities. I took the proper steps to get Haley back on Turmoil, holding that gun to Makonie’s head, ready to pull the trigger and become a murderer if he didn’t give me what I wanted. Now, on Turmoil I face the threat that has hounded me since the inception of my PCW career. The man that has caused me the most pain out of anyone here…taking the measures to remove Matt Jackson from PCW, for nothing more then mere pocket change. All based on beliefs in an organization that is more off base then communism, the only problem is…that it hasn’t failed yet, just caused worthless suffering for countless lives like the beliefs of Marx. *** Taking a deep breath the Chameleon pulls open the medium sized dressing room door and walks into the tiny room. Staring him in the face is his nazi recruiter Tom Manger, who is wearing a pair of black jeans and a white tee shirt, without sleeves…sporting his swastika tattoo proudly. Next to him is the main attraction of Norticfest, Shane Weathers. The young nazi rock star is wearing similar dress to Manger, except he is shirtless, letting the black swastika on his chest be shown to all. The men smile as Matt takes a few steps in and shuts the door behind him. The room is cramped with the entering Jackson, but it was even before he entered. Right behind Shane and Manger is a white wooden vanity counter, with a mirror attached to it on the back and lights all around the mirror. On top of the small make-up counter are countless articles of make-up; each glass bottle sets a top it, like birds perched on a tree limb. Right in front of the counter is a small chair, it has a cloth seat and a cloth back to it…with a swastika embroidered onto the back of it. Matt stops himself from letting his mouth almost fall to the floor and quietly just smiles letting it split his angular face in half, seeing himself in the mirror. The flesh coloured swim cap still firmly in place on his head and his long black trench coat hanging perfectly over his white dress shirt and black dress pants. The agent calmly skims the dressing room with his dark brown eyes and continues to watch the two skinheads in front of him. Manger takes a step forward and reaches out his bear like hands to greet his newest addition to the skinheads. “I am glad you could make it Simon, this is going to be huge man…we are going to get the message across big here. News people are here, looking for some sort of riot to come from so group of niggers that aren’t pleased.” The two men laugh, as the Chameleon just nods his head and forces his smile to grow wider, showing his white teeth. “Yeah, I was checking out the grounds before I came back here…looks like you boys went all out and I am glad that I can be a part of it. Anything that helps the movement and gets our beliefs more widely known…I will help do,” Jackson quickly lies…not letting the smile fall away from his lips. Simon’s fellow nazi’s seem to be excited at the gesture, as Weathers, turns around and grabs his black guitar from a corner of the room. Looking over Manger, notices that he forgot to introduce the two young skinheads. “Simon, this is Shane Weather, my padawan so to say. He is a great guy when it comes to setting up an event like this. Shane did all the work into setting up the two-day concert. The only problem was he wanted to call it Weathers-fest, but we had to shoot down that idea.” All three of the men laugh at the last comment, as Matt brushes his hand over his trench coat, making sure that his Beretta 9MM is there. “We could call it, Fusion for all I care…just as long as the message gets across and people know what we stand for,” Jackson says…hitting his hand on his barrel of a chest to get his point across. “It’s great to have you aboard Simon, always good to see another young man with the same beliefs as us and one who seems to be, as into this as you is a great addition. Manger told me about how you flew him to South Korea, just to get a meeting down, very impressive,” Shane bellows out from the couch on the left wall…he is sitting casually tuning his instrument of racism. Matt begins to slowly pace around a small area of the room, rubbing his petite hands against his tanned face…feeling a little out of place, in this over white powered room. The fake smile is still ruff on his face, as he feels the poweryness of the make-up that he applied on his face…to cover up the bruises and beating he has taken over the past few weeks to establish himself as the hottest young prospect of the Raising Star division. “Manger told me you were some kind of wrestler is that true Simon?” Shane asks, letting his question cut through the silence of the room, like a knife through butter. The Chameleon stops for a minute, trying to let an answer roll off his cracked lips. Well, the gamble paid off once when I told Manger about my career in the PCW…why not roll the dice again and see if I can land on something a little better then snake eyes. Nodding, the operative turns toward the young skinhead who is laid out on the couch now with his Gibson, strapped across his chest. His opal orbs lock onto the agent and await an answer. Jackson only stares into them, seeing the hatred deep inside of them…feeling like they are almost burning a hole through his 5’8” frame. “Yes, I wrestle for the PCW…the was part of the reason I contacted Mr. Manger. There isn’t a day that goes by, that the niggers and spics try to hold me down and I was sick of it…sick of people not respecting my beliefs and shunning me for them,” Jackson lies, trying to peer into Weather’s soul to see if his gamble paid off. Letting a huge smile cross over his thin lips the skinhead shakes his head up and down at Matt, showing him his ugly yellow teeth. Matt gags at the sight, but doesn’t let it show…quickly regaining his composer, as he turns back to Manger. The middle-age skinhead gives him the same reaction, revealing almost similar teeth. What is it with skinheads and bad teeth…you bastards can’t find a toothbrush. I guess looking better then the people you hate isn’t high on their list. Then again, I guess all that work about talking about how great the white race is, doesn’t leave a lot of room for hygiene. “Yeah, we have that same problem in everyday life…you will soon learn ways to get past it. I mean look at my career choice. Music is a great form of art, but instead of people enjoying it…they listen to that rap shit the niggers put out and slap us hard working whites in the face. It makes me sick,” Weathers boasts, plucking a string on his Gibson. People like you make me sick…there is probably a reason no one listens to your music, it sucks…you are just like Shane Wethers, trying to get a cheap push from nothing more then a mediocre talent. The only difference is…Wethers can wrestle…well kind of. “I know totally what you mean,” Manger pipes up. “Back at my old job the niggers just invaded. Forced me out of the construction business. That’s when I joined the movement I didn’t want that bullshit Affirmative Action holding me down. I say, if they can’t do it themselves we don’t need the government to step up and say they can do it.” Funny you guys want anarchy and yet you do nothing about it. You are just trying to make a statement like Stone Burn, getting people to fear you because of shock value. It’s nothing more then that and it’s almost humourous how like mindless drones you follow suit. “Yeah, in PCW they just let them run a rampage. In fact, I think they hate me so much over there, that they stuck me in a massive gauntlet match. Letting eight other no ones get a shot at me…forcing me to have to show them white power,” the Chameleon lies again. Starting to go back into his old groove, becoming Simon when he has to. Lying to these bastards is getting easier now…in the business of filth I guess I just come out a head. Except for Dan Osbourn who has penetrated it in more then one-way. A porn star that actually believes he is cut out for fighting…trying fighting in the desert of Iraq, we will see how funny you are then. Penetrating a lady is a little easier then walking across the dark desert, with sand blowing in your face…just to kill another operative in the deadly game of cat and mouse. “That’s bullshit, but I know you will come out a head. With our beliefs fueling you, there is no one who can stop you. Those men are going to be in for a rude awakening, but I think we should let Shane get ready for his performance. So lets go out and meet some of my right hand men. I think you will enjoy this one guy Simon. He is like stone, killing countless minority bitches without a second thought and best off, the sick bastard uses a knife.” A knife? Stone Burns? Impossible, I guess there are just more murderous people out there like him…it doesn’t matter because he is soon going to pay the price. Falling at my feet on Fusion and watching me take the gauntlet match like I so deserve.
