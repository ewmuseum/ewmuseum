Getting the answer to the BIG question

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/2002 Place: The Anaheim Memorial HospitalTime: Just after noon The scene does indeed open in the lobby of the Anaheim Memorial Hospital. Yes, the same hospital that Natalie Miyagawa has been laid up in since her brutal attack by Boda. Alot has happened since that fateful night. Boda won the World Title back, then lost it 3 weeks later at Spring Fling. But, more important than that, Boda found out that not only does he have the same rare blood type as Natalie, but in fact.... they are brother and sister. Or so Miyagawa has said. But, where is the proof? Not once, has Boda seen the concrete evidence. Then, why did he donate the blood? All that is about to be answered. Boda is going to visit his ‘sister’ and find out once and for all, for Miya said she holds the evidence. So, here we see Boda, all 6’11 of him walking through the lobby. He still sports a bandage from his right arm, the spot where he gave of his own blood, to save his ‘blood’. Boda heads into the elevator, and pushes the button with 4 on it. The silver doors of the elevator shut, and the car moves up, towards his destination. Once the car comes to a stop, the shiny doors open, and out steps The Ameri-Czech monster. Boda turns to walk towards the room Natalie is recovering in. As he walks down the hall, he ponders the weeks to come. *Is it truly possible that Natalie is my sister? Miya says she has solid evidence. That don’t matter. I mean, does it? Do I have a sister, and why have I never been told of this before? Wait, think about your match on Tuesday. #1 Contender for The World Title....again. But, this time.... I’ve got some talent to face. The Legend..... Frisco.......Tyler Nelson. Like I said.... some talent to face. Some low life, amateur, NO talent to face. Give me a break..... Stop thinking about whether of not this Russian chick is indeed your sister. Think about how easy getting a title shot against New Breed is going to be. Whether it’s Corey, Jason, Chris or Downey holding the title, making a farce of it, it won’t matter. Beating either Nelson, Legend or Frisco will be nothing more than a warm up. Granted.... granted for one minute that Frisco has the only ounce of talent as great as mine. So, Frisco might give me the hardest time in that match. That much I give him.So, it will at least come down to me and Frisco, beating one another, till as usual, I get the upper hand, and Bounce him hard. Then it’s on to winning another World Title. Then.... oh yeah... then on to finally face Miyaj...... my supposed ‘brother in law’.* Nurse: Excuse me sir, where are you going? Boda stops, and looks down at the station nurse. He tells her where he is heading to. Boda: Room 420. Natalie Miyagawa. The nurse looks on the sheet of paper on her clipboard. Nurse: I see. Well, it says here that she is to have only family visiting her. Sorry. Boda: Well, actually.... she is my sister. Nurse Hatchetface [god, she is ugly], argues that Boda can not be her brother. Boda shows his bandage, and the paperwork he had to fill yesterday. The nurse stops arguing, and buzzes Boda into the ICU area. Boda nods as he heads towards room 420. So, I will soon meet my ‘sister’. Do you know what it’s like to grow up, knowing only your mother and brother, then finding out that not only do you have a sister, but you almost killed her? It’s enough to make one a little crazy, a little mad. Still, unless the proof is rock solid, I still have doubts. Not that they will cloud my judgement. No, I’m focused on one goal this week. To re-establish my self where I belong.... ON TOP!! Legend Inc..... bah.... he’s more like a Self Proclaimed Legend. I’ve seen nothing impressive from him. To me, he’s another waste of space on the roster. Tyler Nelson.... what money can’t buy, his thugs take for him. But, that’s not going to include a victory this week. No, I’m stronger then them. I don’t take orders, and I don’t listen to, or suck up to AUTHORITY. Frisco.... as much of a legend as he is, he’s not strong enough to beat me. Besides.... Frisco and Legend seem to have a little quaral between them, so this will make Turmoil’s victory even easier. All the cards are on the table, and it looks like I’ve got the Royal Flush. Boda comes to a stop. He looks at the door in front of him. Boda: 420. Time to find out the truth. Boda opens the door to Natalie’s hospital recovery room. The sounds of the medical equipment attached to her is the only sounds we hear. It is dark in the room, the shades drawn shut, and lights off. Boda turns on the light, and reveals Natalie, lying in her bed. The room is full of flowers, and bouquets.Boda steps into the room, the smell of the hospital piercing his nostrils. The flowers are from various family members of Natalie’s. Her father, Miya, Miya’s family. So much love for the woman that lays in that bed. Boda walks over, next to the bed, and looks down at the sleeping woman. She lays there, in a state of rest fullness. Boda pulls a chair over, and sits down. So, we are family. Me and you. But how? I know all my relatives. What makes us family? Natalie starts to stir, she turns over, her face staring directly at Boda, yet her eyes are still shut Look at you. There in this bed, because of your husband. It’s not my fault, it’s Alex’s fault. He made me do this to you. So, be angry at him, not me. As for my anger.... yeah, I have alot of anger for your husband. But soon, it will all be said and down. Soon, your husband will be out of PCW, leaving only me, as the true icon of PCW. The only true old schooler left in PCW. It’s not about wins or loses, or titles, it’s about respect. And come Supremecy, Natalie, I will have all the respect I need. It starts on Turmoil, when I once again, show the world why I am the PCW Monster, when I take out Tyler, Frisco and Legend. Then, onto a shot at anyone of the New Breed jackasses that are currently disgracing the World Title. That title belongs to me, and I’m coming to get it. Natalie stirs again, this time her eyes open, and she jumps, obviously scared. She calms down quickly, realising who is in her room Natalie: What are you doing here? Boda: I saved your life. I’ve come to check on you. And to see if you are indeed...... my sister. Natalie sits up, and stares at Boda. She sits on the end of the bed, and reaches out, and touches Boda’s face Natalie: Yes..... it is true. You are my brother. Boda: How? Why? Natalie: It all started about 24 years ago. A man came to town........ TO BE CONTINUED
