We should help in the community.

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th May 2003 [ ] – Sivasian speaking directly to the reader, his thoughts. :: :: - Action, and reflection on other stuff from a narrator Krikorian: ‘’ – Someone speaking [ It’s really getting to me, ya know. All this Order of Society and Logic stuff. Whilst SOCIETY and Chang seem to be working on the partnership to help rip the heart of PCW out, I’m stuck all alone. No wife, no children, I can’t see them. But of course, I did this. I signed the deal to hand over my possessions and agree not to fully communicate with someone who puts the order down, and my wife just happened to do that. The kids are too young, they wouldn’t understand. I truly hope my wife can help me trough when this is all said and done. Sure, Chang may be correct in saying we’re just playing off each other, but hell, my wife knew that the deal with my parents will forever hang over me until I find their killers, and I’ll try all the way up to the day I’m lying on my death bed. It was due to happen, and some day I will find them, I’m sure. Nothing can stop me now; PCW may just be a way of me selling out to whatever to truly get the job done. Unlike people like Matt Jackson, who’s sole job in life it would seem is to accomplish his dreams within the ring, I have other stuff to work towards, the ring stuff is merely a second way for me to make money, my parents deaths, the weirdness surrounding their deaths, and ultimately finding their killers is, at this very moment, far more important than me winning some cheap gold inside PCW’s ring. Sure, the commercial and money side would be great, but still, it’s on the back burner, shall we say. ] :: Chang had indirectly asked him to do it. He was sick of hearing Krikorian pester him via the telephone, and so he’d sent an E-mail, promising the world to him. He said someone would be round, someone to escort him and take him to where he needed to be. Unsure as to what Chang meant, and only being told to be around the house Sunday lunchtime, Krikorian had, of course, done this. Hungry, but not wanting to eat in case this supposedly ‘urgent’ job to do for the Order was physical, Krikorian sat and held his stomach. The sounds where whirling and swirling around, and making him lurch in pain every time a rippling sound came from it. Krikorian didn’t want to move though, didn’t want to up set Chang, the man who, whether he’d lied or not, said he could help him through this tough time of trying to find his parents killers. :: :: Most people that know Chang, at least from an outside perspective know he’s more than likely a cheating, slimey bitch. Most cult leaders lie, mainly for the money, to be honest. Of course, those within the cult will tell you that Chang is a loving man, who adores his creation SOCIETY and off course couldn’t focus without love and honesty. Yet Sivasian seems determined to believe him, to actually accept what he says like the pope himself has said it. :: :: Suddenly a knock at the door, and the time to maybe make or break Sivasian. As he opens the door, a ginger haired man peers his head round the corner. The kid can only be of around 20, with freckles, huge bubbling spots of hormonal juices, and deep brown eyes. :: Krikorian: ‘Come in son, you are from the Society correct?’ :: The young kid blushes, maybe wondering what it’s like to be in the presence of someone like Krikorian, someone who’s by now at least, moderately famous in the world. :: Kid: ‘Sure am sir. I brought my car, of course donated to me by the Society to help us promote ourselves throughout the world today, tomorrow and in the future.’ :: Krikorian smirks a little. Even the kid appears weird and, well, slightly freakish, he admires his effort. Even though the kid appears to be put in a trance by the cult, Sivasian himself feels someone connected to them. :: Krikorian: ‘That’s great, now, aren’t you here to tell me how I’m suppose to help the Society?’ Kid: ‘Indeed sir, and you will be helping the community. Chang is, shall we say, slightly worried about secrecy and worried that our overall worldwide reputation might be falling, and so, if we assist in the community, we can help.’ :: The kid has rushed the sentence without breathing, and wheezes slightly. Krikorian hands him a glass of water and nods. :: Krikorian: ‘Firstly, slow down.’ :: The kid, still nameless to Sivasian, nods back and smiles. :: Krikorian: ‘Secondly, you’re not American are you?’ :: Sivasian’s eyes are extremely, unwelcoming as he says this, and the kid seems slightly taken aback by the sheer aggression of it. :: Kid: ‘No sir, I’m from the mountains of Italy, my English is good, no?’ Krikorian: ‘Excellent, you might say. Nowadays though, everyone speaks English. ’ Kid: ‘Of course.: Krikorian: ‘Thirdly, where are we going today?’ :: The kid pulls a small brochure from his back pocket of his blue jeans, and hands it to Krikorian. It’s wonderfully bright and well presented. Bright yellow letters set out the heading of, ‘St.Michael’s young and depression centre.’ Sivasian shrugs, slightly unsure of what is meant. :: Krikorian: ‘A slight, explanation, maybe?’ Kid: ‘Why, we’re heading down to the youth centre to play sports, serve drinks, and maybe for you to tell stories?’ :: Sivasian smirked, of all the things in the world. Serving drinks to children whilst faking a big smile and telling them over elaborate stories that their retarded brains could be seen as Chang going soft. :: Krikorian: ‘Sure, can we pick up some food on the way, I’m starving?’ Kid: ‘I guess, there’s a non corporate café which serves food down town.’ Krikorian: ‘Great! We don’t want them fat cats getting any…fatter, do we?’ Kid: ‘Certainly not.’ **** :: The manager of the youth centre, right in the middle of Wolfsburg, couldn’t believe a top wrestler would be joining them today. Most of his members where retards, he knew this, but their parents and donations coming in meant he was on hefty pay to keep the place running. He’d had the centre specifically painted for the occasion today, and so the bright blue walls were shining in the beautiful days sun. As the small car pulled up, and the large, muscular and tall Ukrainian stepped out, he applauded. :: Manager: ‘Hey! Welcome to my youth centre, please, come in and have a great time.’ :: Krikorian was suspicious, something wasn’t quite right with this man, from his single ear piercing to his cowboy hat, he couldn’t quite pick it up. Never the less, he’d do anything for Chang at that moment, and stepped on into the Youth Centre. Standing at the entrance was some 80 kids, most dribbling and wheelchair bound. As Krikorian gagged and was about to be sick, he quickly led himself through a door to a small kitchen. A portly old woman stands, serving out orange squash paper cut portions, and filling them with water. :: Portly Old Lady: ‘Welcome…Mr…Krikorian?’ Krikorian: ‘Listen lady, it’s plain and simple, I’ll do my bit, but with NO RETARDS! That ginger loser I came with can handle them, ok?’ Portly Old Lady: ‘Sir, I’m insulted! But since we barely ever get visitors, go and join the red group.’ :: Krikorian nodded as the woman handed him a tray of orange drinks. He stepped out where his ginger co Society of Order and Logic helper was attending to handing out drinks. He headed over to the corner which read ‘ red group ‘ and handed out the drinks. Most of the children seemed genuinely delighted to see him, but the feeling wasn’t mutual. Sivasian hated retarded children, and believed that those who had run away from home and ended up here deserved nothing less than his brilliance being here. :: Krikorian: ‘Right, who wants to hear a story then, kids?’ :: A small, retarded kid steps up, as drool starts to droop down the side of his mouth. Sivasian shudders and stands up. He quickly calls his younger assailant over, and speaks to him in a hushed whisper, out of earshot of the kids. :: Krikorian: ‘OK, we need to promote ourselves, but this is way too much man, can we leave please?’ :: The ginger kid nods, he doesn’t want to anger Sivasian after all. :: Krikorian: ‘Sorry kids, I’ve gotta go, err….Syn from PCW will come and read you a story!’ :: Even the retarded children with no legs celebrate and jump up and down, as Sivasian grabs his coat and leaves. ::
