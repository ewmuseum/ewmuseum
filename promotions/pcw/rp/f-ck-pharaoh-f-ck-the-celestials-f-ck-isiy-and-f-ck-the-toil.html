Date: not entered 'We bet they said something because that wrinkly old f^ck Horus said something about the Deist's League not going along with us harming Isiy or something like that.' Akiyama walked along the 'uber' long corridor leading towards the Deist's League meeting room. Earlier, the god was in a heated conversation with Isiy and Carmoon, Pharaoh’s family…but then he was interrupted by the Void Assassin, Shinnosuke. He brought news about the Celestials wanting to speak with him on dire matters…? Behind him followed Shinnosuke on his left, the teenage-looking Daughter of Darkness, Hinageshi, on his right, and the massive Darkcyde` behind him. Behind them were the rest of the Void Elements, Kiyone, Chronic, Mystic, Symphony, and X. All of them were clad in black, and they all looked as menacing as the Void. 'You really f^cked things up here, kid.' Darkcyde` growled deeply. The NVG rolled his eyes at the leader of the Void Elements. 'You always think we've f^cked something up. If there's something that's f^cked up, it's the NVG's fault! Oh...Inphino Blitz left the Void Elements...Akiyama f^cked it up!! Celestials are mad at the Void Elements because he's screwing around with Egypt...Akiyama f^cked it up!! PCW’s fired everyone because they’ve spent too much money on pyros or some sh!t...Akiyama f^cked it up!! Well screw that!! F^ck you! F^ck Pharaoh! F^ck Egypt! F^ck the Celestials! F^ck Spring Fling! F^ck the toilet!!' Hinageshi looked up at the god, her solid black eyes peering into him like a mirror. Akiyama shrugged and turned away from her gaze. 'Geez...sorry, Hina. But 'Cyde really pisses me off sometimes. Isn't that like your Void Art or something?' 'No.' he snapped sharply. Shinnosuke grinned. ' Yeah it is! Just like Kiyone here hates everyone and everything...isn't that right Kiyone?' 'Shut up!' the smaller brunette snipped at the Void Assassin, her gaze burrowing into him. 'I hate you!' The god shook his head as their footsteps echoed through the hallway. 'We hate all of you, so quit squawking. Could anything else happen that could piss us off today?' 'Ahem.' They all stopped in their tracks and looked behind them. Standing there in all her silvery glory was the Celestial Oracle…Omina. Dressed in her silver blouse and black slacks with silver heels, she stood there with her arms folded across her chest. Her silver eyes stared into Akiyama as she brushed away a few stray strands of her silver locks. 'Did you think you could just drop in and not see me, Akiyama? The Celestial Oracle knows all…' The NVG stared at her for a good few seconds. Then he simply turned on his heel and continued on down the hall, the Void Elements following silently behind him. Omina glared at them with wide eyes. 'Hey! Don't you ignore me!!' she stomped her foot and walked briskly to catch up with Akiyama. She shoved past Shinnosuke and stepped before Akiyama, walking backwards to look at him face to face. 'Piss off, Omina.' He muttered drolly. 'We don't have time for you now. We really don't need you at all as a matter of fact. Cyclonica's been just as helpful to us as you have been.' 'Cyclonica? That frosty b!tch??' she snapped angrily. 'She couldn't read palms let alone top me in the psychic business!' {~Oh really?~} Omina looked around as the voice echoed through Akiyama's head. She stuck her tongue out at the walls and nodded. 'You're darn tootin', Cyclone!' {~At least I'm not the one who couldn't keep Inphino Blitz under control, and he's the most passive-aggressive guy on the planet when not provoked!~} 'Shut up! If it weren't for these Void Elements, he would have never grown bitter at all of our work! Don't you blame me for other factors!' Darkcyde` groaned aggravated. 'Now these two are bickering like a bunch of b!tches with their heads cut off. Geez…you f^cked up again, kid!' 'D@mn it, Darkcyde`!! You blame one more thing on us and we swear we're banishing you to somewhere where even you cannot return!' Chronic chuckled a bit. 'Where's that?' Akiyama stopped for a bit, forcing everyone else behind him to stop abruptly as well with an irritated groan. He looked down at his boots and thought for a moment. Then he turned to Darkcyde`! with his finger pointed dead in his face. 'We'll banish you to the Aura…how bout that, 'Cyde?' Symphony gasped, her hands covering her gaping mouth. 'You wouldn't dare!' The god turned to her next. 'You're lucky we don't banish you to the Aura after that entire Yuu stunt you pulled on us…so quit squawking!' 'You don't even have the power to banish to the Aura, Neo Void God!' Darkcyde` stepped up toe to toe with the slightly smaller NVG. Behind him, the Void beasts Mystic and X had grown feral and hostile…their backs arching as they balled up their fists for a fight. Akiyama was not budging from the monstrous presence of the Darkcyde`! 'You can't frighten us with your bluffs.' Akiyama balled up his fists and grinned darkness… 'We've done some training since we've last met, 'Cyde…you don't know what the freak we're capable of…do you?' A clap suddenly broke the tension between the two goliaths. They both looked down to their side, where Hinageshi stood with a stern look on her face. The Daughter of Darkness glared up at Darkcyde` before turning to Akiyama with the same glare. Both men took one last look at each other and stepped back. Behind them, Mystic and X pulled back, both of them growing back to their full near six and a half foot height. Akiyama hissed at the blonde Amazon and the hooded man known as Mystic and X. 'You know what…we're going to put all this crap aside for now. We're getting grilled by the Celestials…and we still have a battle to plan for this Sabbath, so 'Cyde…no more of this Pharaoh-Akiyama feud type sh!t…got it?' With a shrug, the Void Elements leader nodded reluctantly. 'Whatever…let's just make sure we come out of this meeting without getting banished somewhere we wouldn't like.' The groups of Void dwellers turn back towards Omina and start down the corridor again. Omina still walks backwards so she can stay face to face with Akiyama. 'You know…I'm going to have to help you out here in this meeting.' 'Why is that, Omina?' he god asked without interest. 'Because…there's going to be a conference between the Celestials and the Egyptians about this thing you're pulling with Pharaoh. They have appointed yours truly as the speaker on your behalf.' She said with a nod and a smile. 'You're going to be our lawyer in this thing?' he shook his head, a desperate sigh leaking from his lungs. 'We're as good as dead, people.' She glared at him with a hurt look. 'Hey! I did a good job of dealing with you! I think I can deal with this matter just as good as your invisible friend Cyclonica could!' 'Why'd you have to bring her up again?' Akiyama asked. He knew mentioning her would bring her about. {~I helped Akiyama become Tag Team and Television champion. What have you done…Extreme? Ooh…big accomplishment!~} Omina sulked as she walked backwards, her lips quivering into a pout. 'You know what…screw you and your Divine Prophetess, Akiyama. I don't have to do a thing for any of you. You'll just have to go without my Celestial insight this time!' Once again the god stopped in his tracks. The Void Elements behind him all stumbled to come to a halt, their swearing and grumbles echoing through the hallway. 'D@mn it!!' Kiyone growled. 'I hate you so much right now!' 'Arrrrgh!' Chronic exclaimed. Mystic and X turned towards him with a questionable look. He shrugged sheepishly. 'What?' Akiyama thought for a minute again, this time with his eyes closed. Then he looked back up to the Celestial Oracle with a defeated look on his own face. 'You're just as frustrating as Pharaoh sometimes. Fine…you can help us out. But if you screw this up…you're getting banished!' Omina smiled, raising her hands in victory. 'Yes! I win! Now, let's go talk to those sandy-@ssed Egyptians!' She turned around…and smacked right into the silver double doors. Her body fell to the marble floor with a soft thud, her hand reaching up to rub her sharp nose. 'Ouch…' looking back at the Void Elements and the NVG, she laughed weakly. 'I'm okay…just a bruise.' Akiyama looked down at her, bending at the waist to look her eye to eye. 'Like every word that oozes from Pharaoh's lips…no one gives a CELESTIAL f^ck.' They all stepped over Omina as the NVG pushed the doors open. She glared at their backs as she stood back up and rushed to catch up with them. A huge skylight in the ceiling lighted the entire room…actually, the entire ceiling was transparent. Above them they could peer out in space, the light from the sun and moon illuminating the room brightly with an ethereal glow. Around the room stood black marble pillars that matched the marble tile to perfection. In the center of the room sat a long black polished table with plush leather chairs surrounding it. Sitting at the far end of the table on the left were the Celestial Gods…Hinata, Kirin, Commission, and Offering. The Celestial parents of the NVG smiled at his presence. They attempted to stand, but the Celestial leader Kirin motioned for them to remain sitting. Akiyama took a long look at the Celestials down the table and sat down at the end closest to him. Omina sat on his right, while Hinageshi sat on the left with Shinnosuke flanking her side. The Void Elements stood behind Akiyama like sentinels. The NVG folded his arms and leaned back, his feet propping on the table lackadaisically. 'So, Kirin…what can we do for you?' he asked with a grin. The Universal Celestial didn't smile back. He simply widened his eyes slightly, and Akiyama fell backwards in his chair. The god jumped back up and glared down at him. 'Don't put your feet on the table. It's rude.' He said with a demanding tone Akiyama picked his chair back up and sat down again. Kirin stood up from the table and straightened out his silver jacket. 'As you know, Akiyama, we're here to deal with an issue that you've recently decided to make your business. I believe you know what it is.' 'Finally! We've been waiting for you Deist's League guys to say something for a long…long time. We think it should stop right now!' A surprised look came over Hinata's face. 'Oh…then we're totally agreeing on this?' The god nodded. 'D@mn frickin' straight. It's about time we did something against this injustice!' She smiled at ease. 'Then you're going to put a stop to it, right?' 'Us?' He looked up innocently. 'Don't you think you guys should do something about it?' Kirin glared at Akiyama. 'Why should we? We didn't start this mess.' Aki leaned back in his seat, folding his arms. 'Well neither did we.' Hinata sighed, lowering her head before looking back up at the Neo Void God exasperated. 'What are you talking about?' Akiyama raised his eyebrow. 'No…what are YOU talking about?' 'We're talking about this fiasco you've struck up with Pharaoh and his people!' Kirin explained with a trace of anger. 'What are YOU talking about?' Aki looked down at the table awkwardly. 'Oh…we were talking about the bias that PCW was keeping against us by forcing us to work with such lowly jobbers like Pharaoh and keeping us in the lower card ranking despite our enormous skill!' Kirin and Hinata both looked away frustrated. Offering looked down at Akiyama with her dark eyes looking rather caring. 'Akiyama…do you understand the severity of this situation here?' He nodded at the Void Goddess. 'Yes, mother. The question here is…does Pharaoh understand the severity of the @sskicking we're planning on giving his fired, soon to be mortal @ss come this Sabbath!!' Offering tried smiling at him, but the smile faded into a depressed frown. The Aura God patted Offering on her shoulder for comfort. 'There, there, dear.' She smacked him hard across his face. 'Don't touch me. What did I tell you about touching me?' 'Sorry.' He scooted away from his wife slowly before looking down at Akiyama, mouthing the word “Period” to the Void Elements and the NVG across the room. In unison, Akiyama and his entourage took a large scoot back from the table. Commission nodded. 'Son…how you doing?' 'Celestial, pops.' He replied with a smile. 'How YOU doing?' 'Oh, I'm feeling heavenly.' He flashed a bright smile that beamed all the way across the room. The Void Elements hissed at the light from his smile. Kiyone shielded her eyes away from the glare. 'It burns!!' the Void Element of Hatred hissed serpent like. 'I hate you and your Mike Marvelous smile!' Akiyama stared straight into his father's smile without any effect. Commission stopped smiling brightly and folded his hands. 'But you know who's not doing well?' 'Pharaoh?' the god asked. Commission shook his head as the rest of the room sighed. 'No…not Pharaoh.' He replied. Akiyama shook his head in reply. 'Yeah, it's Pharaoh. You see…we have his wife and his man-servant…which on another topic, we wonder what kind of god has a “man-servant” anyway…if you know what we're saying…' Omina held her wrist out rigidly before dropping it limply to signify his meaning. He gave her a thumbs up before continuing. 'We have his people banished to the Void, and he's holding some pathetic piece of matter as barter for their lives? Are we the only one that sees the complete stupidity about the matter?' 'Akiyama…I'm not talking about Pharaoh.' Commission replied, a bit more flustered than before. Once again, Akiyama shook his head. 'Yeah you are! No one else is as screwed as Pharaoh Punk@ss… we have his only family, and we threaten his godhood this Sunday…and all can do is grab onto his cat Carmoon, and the hope that we're going to risk everything for that worthless belt and slip up like some mortal! He's the one that's going to end up mortal after all this is over!' 'Akiyama. This has nothing to do with Pharaoh!' the sudden outburst from the normally passive Aura God drew stares from everyone in the room. He hunched his shoulder with an embarrassed grin. 'No…wait…this is about Pharaoh…sorry.' Kirin was ready to spit. He paced back and forth across the strip of floor on his side of the table. 'Akiyama, you need to realize that you're a HUGE liable towards us Celestials. We're already looked down upon because we almost run the universe while those other gods merely pose as deities who look like they can do something. Then ever since you came around, we've began losing the support of the other gods. The first thing was that Big Alan Steele situation, which left us without the support of Mt Olympus!' The scene cut to show Zeus and Hera sitting around on their golden thrones amongst the cloudy-wispy Mt Olympus. Zeus is twirling a slender bolt of lighting in his fingers, while Hera sits with a mirror in hand looking over her hairstyle. 'Do you like my hair, Zeus?' she asked. The old god yawned and nodded robotically. 'Yes, dear.' 'Do you think I'm the prettiest goddess alive?' Another stale nod. 'Yes, dear.' 'Then what are all of THESE??' Hera twirled around and tossed a stack of magazines at Zeus's feet. He looked down to see the title 'PLAYGOD' written in golden letters… a shot of Egyptian goddess Isis on the cover. He smiled faintly as he tried scooping them up out of the sight of prying eyes. 'Um…National Geographic?' The scalded wife stood there with her hands on her hips, a sour look on her face. 'Well…what do you have to say for yourself?' Zeus rubbed the back of his head apprehensively, his eyes shifting back and forth struggling to find an answer. Then a revelation flashes in his mind. 'Akiyama f^cked it up.' Hera opened her arms wide to accept her husband. The two hug, with Zeus winking into the camera giving a thumbs up sign. 'SCREW YOU, ZEUS!!' Akiyama shook his fist at the heavens above. Then he raised his hands to show off his black nails. 'YOU SEE THESE??? THESE NAILS ARE YOUR FAULT!!! YOOOOUUUUUUUURRRRR FAAAAAUUUUULLLLLLLTTTT!!!!!!' The Celestials on the other end of the table shook their heads in disapproval. Kirin leaned against the table on one arm and tried talking to Akiyama again. 'Akiyama, you listen here and you listen good. After you totally knocked us out of good standards with the Greeks, we had something built up around the Egyptians, with you and Pharaoh working as ambassadors. But once you two parted ways, the alliance weakened. Then you pull some insane stunt as to kidnap his family? What were you thinking?' 'We knew what we were thinking!' the NVG replied a bit upset at the scolding. 'We were thinking to banish his family and totally scorn the bastard for betraying us…then later kick his fired Imoteph looking @ss all over PCW!! What's so difficult about understanding that?' “What you can’t understand is that if we lose support of the Egyptians, that’s like two of the top god sects against us! That’s not going to sit well with everyone else!” Kirin tried to explain again. Akiyama shrugged his shoulders. “What about the Norse gods?” Hinata scoffed at his suggestion. “Those uncivilized peasants?” The scene shifted to show two blonde long haired men in Viking garb sitting across from each other at a oak polished table in a garden paradise. They both held a saucer and a cup of tea, while they conversed under the serenade of the opera singer Sarah Brightman. “Did you see that wonderful piece from Madam Butterfly last night on PBS, Odin?” the first blonde asked the other. The one with the handlebar mustache nodded curtly. “I most certainly did, Thor. It was quite enthralling to sit there and really take in all of the viola during the solo riffs. It was almost as delightful as that Beethoven 8th Sonata we heard the other night at the symphony.” The cleanly shaved Thor nodded. “Oh, yes, those were good times, old bean.” The two of them took sips from their teacups, their pinkeys raised daintily. Just then, another blonde muscular man in Viking attire came rushing in like something was important. You’ll never believe what happened just now.” he said excitedly. “Someone finally put out a decent reprisal of Chopin?” Odin asked with a questioning glance. Thor chuckled as the man shook his head. “No, you know that nightclub down by Mitgard?” “Yes?” “They turned it into a French bistro! They’re importing the Chateau De Numonte and the Brie from Paris as we speak!” They all stared at each other for a minute before bursting out into laughter. Thor wiped a tear from his eye. “Oh, Loki...you and your impish tricks!” “They’re vile and dirty!” Hinata finished. Across the table, Akiyama stared up into a corner of the room with a confused look on her face. Then he turned to Hinageshi while pointing a finger upwards. “Did you just see that? Tell us we’re not the only one who just saw that segment.” The Daughter of Darkness smacked the god across his face, sending his focus back towards the end of the table...and eventually the head Celestials. He cleared his throat and nodded. “Yes...anyway, there’s nothing we can do to help you now. The battle’s begun, and we’re not the type to back down from anything, no matter who asks it of us! Pharaoh’s getting his face booted in by the Divine Sword, he’s getting his chest caved in by the HAND OF FATE, and his neck’s being ripped apart so that he has no choice but to scream in The Darkness!” “I’d think again if I were you!” Kirin glared down to the Void Elements and Akiyama sternly. Then…from the far side of the conference room, there is a knock. Hinata looked up with a smug smile. “And here are our guests.” She walked…no, glided…towards the set of golden doors at the back and reached for the knob…
