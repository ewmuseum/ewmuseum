On the air....Part I

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-1-02 ::Introduction:: Having lost his match to Duzza on Tuesday Turmoil, Mark felt as if he didn't have the momentum to carry on to Friday Fusion to pick up the Rising Star title. That is true because he didn't pick up the belt that he wanted. What belt does Mark want real bad? Well he doesn't want a belt. All he wants is to have some respect shown for him and to have him recognized all over the e-wrestling world. No matter what Mark does he always just gets shut down or closed off by the wrestling world. Even by his own company he isn't easily recognized, all that hey recognize is the name and not what is behind the Mark Makonie name. Well Mark has a golden oppertunity coming up on Tuesday Turmoil to prove himself to all of the e-wrestling world once again by standing alone in a four corners match with three of the greatest names in PCW that are recognized today. ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to a radio station in the city of Las Angelas, California where there is suppose to be Dennis Miller hosting the show. Mark is schedule to make an appearance at one of Las Angelas's best radio stations, across the whole country. Mark pulls up in his black Pontiac Bonneville that looks like a mafia car. It looks as if a mafia guy is going to come out of this car, but instead it is Mark coming out dressed in his blue denim jeans and his black boots. Also he has on a black shirt with the Confederate flag on the front and on the back it says: The real flag of America. He locks up his car and he goes up to the front door that has a clear window and some silver trim to it. He goes in with the bell sound and he goes up to the front desk. 'How may I help you?' asks the lady at the desk who is typing on her computer not looking up at Mark. 'Yes I am here to make a special apperance on the radio show with Dennis Miller as the host.' says Mark as he is staring at the lady who looks as if she is in her mid thirties. 'Well I will page Dennis real quick.' says the lady still typing on her computer. The lady goes over to her phone and she picks it up. She looks at the names on the paging list. She finds the one that goes out to the radio hosting. She talks in the phone for a little while with someone and then she hangs up the phone. She then just goes back to typing on her computer keyboard as fast as she can. Mark continues to look at the lady then he starts to get a little unpatience. 'Hey what did he say?' asks Mark still trying to keep his cool with the lady typing. 'He will be out in a little bit.' says the lady as she is not even paying attention to what Mark is saying or what not. Mark just keeps on waiting at the desk looking around the building at what he is seeing. He sees some of the rock bands that have come to the station and a big plaque award for something that Mark does not care about. Then Dennis comes out of the booth in his red Hawaiian button down shirt with khaki shorts. He looks the same with his beard and his little long hair. 'Mark Makonie I take it.' says Dennis as he looks at Mark and his goatee that looks as if it scares him. 'Well that would be me.' says Mark as he looks at his beard and then his Hawaiian shirt kind of laughing of what he is wearing. 'Well you go on in a little bit so let me show you around the building and show you where we will be talking at.' says Dennis as he is turing away and waving for Mark to follow him. Mark goes away from the desk as he keeps on looking at the lady who is still typing on her computer and looking staright at the screen haven't blinked once as Mark has been there. Mark then follows Dennin around the station starting with the bathrooms, showing Mark where they are. Then he goes to the other booths around the station showing him where other people host shows from. 'In that very booth there, Jason Kidd was a special guest on the show.' says Dennis pointing in a booth that was occupied by a religous show at the moment. 'So once Jason Kidd was a special guest, one of the premier basketball players that there are today.' says Mark as he is looking at Dennis and then back to the booth. 'He is a white guy who can actually play basketball and people make fun of him because of his color. They probably think that he got a skin change to white instead of staying black. Is that what people want to think because he is white and good at basketball that there is a fluke in the gene system or in Jason Kidd?' 'Well I didn't want to get into too much detail there Mark, I just was showing you around.' says Dennis trying not to make Mark upset before he goes onto the show. 'Well it just makes me mad when we are taken advantage of by all races around.' Mark adds to his same topic as he is looking through the glass of the booth to the black preacher on the radio. 'They always think that they have to be the best, that no other race may join them in what they think they are good at. Well even some of the white race discriminate the white race by listening to the music of hip-hop is what they call it. I just call those people who listen to that sh**, whiggers. Yes there is such a thing as a white ni**a.' Dennis is trying to calm Mark down as he takes him away from the booth that he was showing him and to have him go into the lounge area. They both go into the lounge room and they sit down at a round table with a couple of blue roller chairs around it. Dennis takes one chair and he rolls is out and he takes a seat in the chair. Mark rolls one out, opposite of Dennis, and he takes a seat on the blue padding on the back and on the bottom of where Mark is sitting. 'I would just like to go over the schedule of events we have today.' Dennis says as he is fiddling with his thumbs, back and forth. 'We will go on the air in about say fifteen minutes from now. That should get us enough time to prepare for what you are going to say and to get you all wired up.' Mark just nods as Dennis is trying to explain to Mark what they are going to do to him, like how they are going to wire him and all of that junk. Anyways, Mark is thinking about what kind of questions Dennis will end up asking him. Will they be personal or just business related? Mark has no idea for what he is into right now as he is probably going to be asked to give his life story. He doesn't need to be giving his life story with such a big event coming up on Tuesday. He is not even thinking about the Friday match in advance because then that would mean Mark is looking beyond the Tuesday match which he shouldn't do because of the huge wrestlers who are in it. 'Let's get going if we want to be ready in time.' says Dennis as he is getting up from his chair. Mark does not say anything once again as he is still looking across from his chair to where Dennis was sitting. Dennis goes over to Mark and he snaps his fingers one time, nothing happens. Mark snaps his fingers again yet in the other ear, nothing happens. 'Mark, we need to get going!' shouts Dennis into Mark's right ear and Mark hops in his seat a bit. 'Oh alright, you should've said or done something to tell me we needed to get going.' says Mark as he is getting up from his chair and following Dennis out of the lounge area and into the hallway. They then reach the booth in what is going to be their booth to theirselves. The janitor is just cleaning up the trash that was left over from the show before them. The guy looks up as the two of them enter the room and he looks exacatly like Joe Dirt. 'It is Joe Dirt!' says Mark as he goes over to the janitor who is Joe Dirt. 'Well if it isn't another one of my fans.' says Joe as he is sweeping up the floor somemore. 'I loved your movie and Brandy oh was she a fox and she was white, that is what I liked best about her.' says Mark as he is watching Joe clean up the trash. 'Well I will let you two be one with the booth because I am rockin n rollin outta here so I will see you tomorrow Dennis.' says Joe as he winks at Dennis, heading towards the door. Mark and Dennis head over to their microphones and there is really no wiring up they have to do. Mark was wondering what the wiring part was because he was unsure of that. But anyways, Dennis checks both of the microphones to make sure that they work and they do of course. But they are both seated and it is about a minute before they go on the air to the primetime air wave of California. 'Welcome to California's best host, and I am Dennis Miller alongside here we have a man that not many of you people know.' says Dennis as he is talking into the microphone with his headphones on. 'You know that we had Joe Dirt in here before, well we have a follow up to him, but I cannot guarantee you that it will be as good. Enough for me to stop talking and I will introduce our guest that we have in the studio today. He is all the way from PCW wrestling who is not recognized by the media or fans, he is Mark Makonie. Mark say hi to all of the listeners.' 'Hey all of you out there.' says Mark as he speaks into the microphone with his headphones on. 'Now Mark not many of us know you, but a lot of us know your past. Let me ask you some questions about you and wrestling.' Dennis asks Mark as he is looking over at him and Mark nods as he is looking at Dennis. 'Now I hear that you lost your shot at the Rising Star Title just a night ago. Now what happened?' 'Well Dennis I just was not prepared in general for the match.' Mark says as he is acting natural into the mic. 'It all started with that African American man Duzza and those damn white guys that interfered in that match. I had Duzza right where I wanted him, but then here comes Goeren and Trigger to interrupt my match. I was like what the hell is this! That really made me mad about losing that match because then I really wasn't up for the challenge for the four corners match. Trigger won the belt fair in square, but there was just too many people in the match. I thought it was suppose to be elimination in the first place, but it ended up being just a regular match.' 'I see Mark, it really hurts when you lose something like that because you have been ready for that chance for a while.' Dennis says as he is just going along smootly though his questions. 'Yeah it is a shame that I had to even be treated like that out in front of New Jersey like that, but I guess it just wasn't my time to be a champion.' Mark says as he is imagining how he felt after his match. 'Now what you have on Tuesday is a pretty big match and you have only head from one man his opinions, Jon Kano, tell me more about that match.' says Dennis as he is getting into the mode of questions. 'Well Jon Kano, I don't know the man too well, I don't even know the name that well. But for what he is saying about how Shabazz's pain is his pain or something like that is a buch of bull**it. Those two people are two different races, now how in the hell can they feel each others pain? He goes on and says that he has defeated this man and that man, well the world does not want to head about your winnings because I know that you don't have many of those. Nobody cares about the past, I head that all Jon Kano is, is that he is all talk. That he can't back anything he says up.' Mark is saying as he is going through Jon Kano first of his oppoenents. 'What about The Chef?' Dennis adds as he cuts off Mark before he can speak. 'The Chef, he is one of the top notch people around in this game. He goes balls to the wall everytime that he goes out into that ring. I may very add that he is probably on the verge of getting one of the top spots in PCW. Wait, he already has a top spot, he is recognized everywhere he goes and I am not. That is besides the point because I am the underdog in this match and it seems as The Chef is the favorite to win.' Mar says as he knows nothing about The Chef, but will find out soon. 'Now we have a guy that is in New Breed, a stable in PCW, Corey Williams.' Dennis says as he is not knowing what he is talking about. 'What are your thoughts on him in your matchup?' 'New Breed I should say are a bunch of scammers to the wrestling world. They work for nothing, they don't deserve to be around in this business becaues they are some of the worst son of a b*tches I have seen around here in a long time. They are not good guys, like me, but show some respect for the business and I don't care what you say. Now Corey Williams, he has held the top of the line title before, he is pretty well known around the world of wrestling, but I don't know anything about him to talk bad. All I can say is that he is part of a stable that no one likes and I am part of a stable that no one will end up liking because I am racist against the colored people around here. All I can say about this match coming up is it will be an exciting one before the main event.' Mark ends as he stands up from his seat and exits the booth. 'Well it looks as if Mr. Makonie has left the booth, so we will continue this tomorrow.' Dennis says as he tells the producer guy to cut everything off. Mark was not in the mood to talk about his match because he needs to get away from everyone right now. He feels as if no one is liking him around the wresting world and that he has no friends. Just like it was back in high school, no friends. Mark needs to do something about that and fast. Scene fades to white
