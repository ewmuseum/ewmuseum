Date: 10.01.03 refuse to accept the view that mankind is so tragically bound to the starless midnight of racism and war that the bright daybreak of peace and brotherhood can never become reality. I believe that unarmed truth and unconditional love will have the final word - martin luther king 'We must live together as brothers or die apart like fools' – Martin Luther King --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The scene opens in a dark room. Lit only by the small amount of light seeping through the window, covered by pulled curtains. Duzza is close to the camera. Only his face is visible. His eyes are bloodshot. His hair is cornrowed back and his face looks tired. Very tired. Duzza: “ I’m sick of this bullshit man… for real… black hate white, white hate black. It’s jus’ the same ol’ shit man, just a different day. When we gonna stop hating each other dawg? We fight over some petty arguments… I dunno why people gotta hate each other, but it annoys the fuck outta me. Shit, mah wife, half Jamaican half Chinese. If that ain’t a wicked mix, nah’mean? It’s 2003 people, all that racism shit, thought it was s’pose to die out back in the 60’s? Shit, ya average American can’t remember what he had for breakfast, how he gonna ‘member the civil rights battles of the 60’s? I ain’t gon’ lie, I used to hate white people. Shit, I still ain’t no cracka lover! Heh… but I can see a white person and not want to kill them now. Unlike when I was younger. But then there’s this thing, it’s called “growing up.” Most people go through it, some sooner than later. I hate how as black men, we are assumed to be either gangsta’s, drug dealers, pimps, thieves or uneducated. Fuck is that all about? That’s nothing but ignorant stereotypes evil white men have designed to keep us down. Well fuck that. It’s about time me and mah people, AKA Playaz Inc, stood up for they rights. See, me and mah man Ali, got a match with these two cracka’s. Shit, I done forgot they name… Chris Storm? Yeah, and his woman. Please, they putting two of the hardest brotha’s in PCW against a white boy and a woman? Say what? Please… I guarantee even if something happens and me and my dawg lose, we still gonna give them fools a beat down. Whether the bell has rung or not. ‘Cos this is far beyond wrestling now man. See, this is life. It’s like hip hop. It’s hip hop for life. I love hip hop. No one can ever take that away from me. Other than mah wife and mah song, hip hop is my love. Hip Hop ain’t never done me wrong! Not like half the mother fucker’s in my life. Hip hop isn’t music ya see, hip hop is a culture. It’s my culture. No don’t get it twisted, this ain’t a black thing. Hip hop has no color. It’s a culture. Blacks like your average emcee, whites like Eminem, Bubba and HayStak, Latino’s like Fat Joe, Angie Martinez and the dude from Cypress Hill, even Asians like Jin and the sole Chinese chick in every rap video have a place in hip hop. But, ya see, hip hop is just like Playaz Inc. We hated on by people outside who can’t understand us, we not considered real by the critics, but we got love and just like rap, Playaz Inc will always be on top of the world… Please believe that” With that, the camera fades to black
