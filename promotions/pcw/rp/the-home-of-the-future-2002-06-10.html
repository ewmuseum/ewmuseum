The Home of the Future

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/10/02 The camera fades in… The Chef walks through the door of his newly bought apartment, and sighs a deep sigh, examining the surroundings in this lovely home. The Chef: I love working with Mr. Nelson… I definitely like living it up! Praise be to Allah for living it up! The Chef jumps down on the ground and prostrates towards the direction he thinks leads towards Mecca, which is actually directed towards Cincinnati. The Chef hops back to his feet and utters a stern command, but to whom? The Chef: Perrier me!!! Suddenly, a slot in the wall opens up. A falcon rushes out of the open slot, and flies over to the refrigerator. The falcon uses its razor sharp talons to grip the refrigerator door and open up the kitchen appliance. Bertha: Do I hear a f*cking fridge door f*cking opening!?!? The Chef: Umm…no, that’s just the wind! Yeah, that’ll do… Suddenly a slot in the kitchen floor opens and a chimpanzee hops out from the apartment below… The Chef: Hey, isn’t that Janet Reno’s helper monkey? The monkey walks over to the fridge, grabs a Perrier, and walks over to the Chef, handing him his flavored water beverage, and then backing away, prostrating on the floor. The Chef: Thanks Mr. Giggles! The Chef then sniffs the Perrier bottle; he draws back from the bottle, emotionally devastated from the rank odor coming from the Perrier bottle. The Chef: Hey Reno! Wash your monkey more often! Bertha: What the f*cking did you say about mother f*cking monkeys!?!? The Chef: Go back to reading your copy of “Stuff” Bertha! Bertha: O-f*cking-K b*tch @$$ mofo d*ck cunt!!! The Chef: Yuck, this Perrier smells like a monkey ass… The Chef holds up his bottle and a contraption near the bottom of the wall opens a cabinet door. Out comes a goat, clad in soup cans with a bell strapped around its neck. The Chef tosses the bottle at the goat. The creature swallows the bottle whole, and slowly walks on back through the cabinet door, not without doing some business on the Chef’s perfectly cleaned floor. The Chef pulls a lever near the front door. The ceiling opens up and Martha Stewart falls to the floor. She is wearing a dominatrix outfit, and is cuffed by the feet and the hands. The Chef: Mop my floor, now! Martha uses the towel stuck in her mouth to wipe the goat crap off the floor. Her face contorts, obviously appalled by the smell coming from the goat crap. The Chef: Hey Martha, weren’t you banished to the Void? Martha mumbles something, but it can’t be heard because of the towel used to gag her. The Chef, in an act of courtesy that would make Allah smile, removes towel from her mouth, and places it on her head, rubbing the goat poop in her hair. Martha grits her teeth with anger, but realizes she is helpless against the Chef because he’s in cahoots with Tyler Nelson. Martha: Meh, the Void is actually a British Petroleum station in Tucson… The Chef nods and pulls the lever again. A crane comes down from the ceiling and picks Martha up, slowly carrying her back through the ceiling’s opening. Bertha: Was that my f*cking sister mother f*cking flirting with you, f*cko!?!? The Chef: Yes, but we weren’t flirting. I rubbed goat poop in her hair! Bertha: F*cking sweet!!! The Chef pushes a button on the wall, which causes the floor to move forward like a conveyor belt. The conveyor belt stops right before the Chef enters the faboulously carpeted living room with Bertha laying on the couch with a copy of Stuff covering her face. Bertha: ZZZZZZZ…c*csucker….ZZZZZZZZZ…f*cking @$$ humper…ZZZZZZZ…queer as f*ck b*tch @$$ ho…. The Chef: Awwww, isn’t that the cutest thing you ever saw…? The Chef grabs a blanket off the top of the couch and goes to drape it over Big B, but the blanket, as large as it is, can’t quite cover her entire body from the head down. The Chef: Hmm, I need another blanket… The problem is, there weren’t enough blankets in the house to cover up Bertha, counting the bed sheets!!! The Chef: My Allah, you’ve packed on the pounds B… I better stop taking you to the Olive Garden. Those six Chicken Parmesans a night is probably causing a lot of the sixteenth roll of blubber… The Chef sits in his camouflage-colored leather recliner, pulling out his leather-bound, golden trim version of the Holy Koran. The Chef: Allah this, Allah that, I’m tired of reading this thing, isn’t there a Book on Tape version of the Koran!?!? Suddenly a slot in the ceiling opens an the exact item that the Chef requested falls from the ceiling onto his lap, well actually more towards his groin… The Chef: YOWZA!!! Allah’s twins will be out of action for awhile… The Chef notices the text on the plastic cassette holder. The Chef: Hmmm, the Noble Koran on Tape…as told by Bob Newhart!?!?! PRAISE ALLAH!!! The Chef sits back, relaxes, and listens to the words of Mohammed as told to him by Allah as the camera slowly and mercifully fades to black…
