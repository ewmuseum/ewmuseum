Transition: History( PCW, Part 1)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered *Five weeks ago, when Skylar was in FBI detainment.* PCW offered me a spot because they were bolstering their ranks. NTICW was closing just like EW. (Cassidy had Dexter Thomas and Duzza kidnap NTICW's president Blain Bishop. I just tagged along, but didn't do much of anything. Vampir Nosferatu made an appearance, and Zurick turned on the fed, as well.) So PCW was taking in all of NTICW's talented people (and even some people they knew would just be jobbers. PCW also took myself, Six-Finger Cisco, and Young Tiger from EW right away. We all started out in the Rising Star Division, them about two weeks ahead of me because I took a little break. After all, PCW had taken in a large influx of talent. I'd be facing people better than I could have faced in EW. I took some of the extra time to train, the rest to relax. Cisco was quick to jump up to the Television Title division. He considered himself a freak, although he only had an extra thumb on his right hand, something that could be easily and painlessly removed with an hour-long surgical procedure (and besides, there are far worse physical deformities that occur just as often). I never believed his story about getting kicked out of Texas, or beating up George W. Bush, but he had talent. Doesn't seem to be doing as much with himself over in OSW now. Maybe he hit his peak last year. Sad, if true. Young Tiger would stay in the Rising Star division for a little while longer, but he would leave before he had the chance to go higher. He used to run a PWA class himself before joining EW and then later PCW. Only 18, but had (still does) a good head on his shoulders. Doesn't let his skill make him get an ego. Modest, he is. He made sure to teach that in his class (though they didn't pick it up too well). Since then, he has returned, and is considered a prime candidate for Cruiserweight Champion. I came to PCW, started in the lower card, but occasionally had matches against PCW mainstays like Darkstar. I was the tallest cruiserweight this company had ever seen, and given my blindness, they thought I might be a draw in the Cruiserweight Division. So they put me in a match with Juan Borro. He was my first PCW feud. I had defeated him a few weeks before during the Global Title Tournament. He said that he had other things on his mind, but still, it got him upset to lose to 'some masked jobber from EW.' When I came to PCW, the bookers capitalized on that and threw us into a No DQ match for the #1 contendership to the Cruiserweight Title. I fought well enough to win, until International Incident came in. I hadn't any experience with fighting that many people at once, especially with weapons involved, so they got the better of me. It was allowable in the match, nothing I could do about it. Juan Borro avenged his loss to me. He's dead now, but I could just dig up the body and pin it. Clyde's done it before... After Juan Borro, I just wrestled some matches as something of a dark heel connected to Drake Maxwell as he was wrestling against the Asylum to get to Jeremy Howard. It was a time in my career where I was really getting some good experience wrestling blind. You see, Drake Maxwell was the NTICW World Champion right before the federation closed, and coming to PCW he considered that reason enough for him to be challenging Jeremy Howard for the Continental Title. Howard obliged, but only because Maxwell came into possession of his brother, Justin. I volunteered to pretend to be Justin Howard. Apparently Justin is only like 6'3' or something, so it shouldn't have worked. I shouldn't have been able to play Justin, and Jeremy Howard not have known something was up. And that's because he did know that something was up. Hell, it was Jeremy Howard's idea. He needed an inside guy to help him get at Maxwell, and keep up with what he was doing. I remember all that stuff I did clearer than anyone else. The most I did to Howard before the Cage Match at March Madness was a few clotheslines. A short chokeslam, nothing major. Once I unmasked myself, the plan was to just stick with Maxwell anyway, make him think still that he was in control. During the match Maxwell and Howard had at March Madness 2K1, I did 'safe' versions of my moves. They weren't intended to harm him, just to give him a breather. And when it came time to finish it, he just whispered 'Do it,' which only I with my extra-sensitive hearing could detect. Grabbed Maxwell by the hair, tossed him off the cage, and a loud thud later, he's out of the match. Jeremy Howard wins, and Drake Maxwell has the worst back problems he's had his entire career. Oh, yeah, Bobby Stormbringer wasn't exactly let in on the 'plan' (the fewer knew, the better, and Stormy had a big mouth) so when he came out I had to beat him down for real. Even had to carry him backstage and lock him in a closet so he wouldn't have interfered further. So after March Madness, I'm in a three-way feud with Stormbringer and Maxwell. Maxwell wanted revenge on me for costing him the Continental Title. Stormbringer was just mad that I showed him up so badly (didn't touch me in that MM match). And they both had issues with each other because they wanted to be the first to get at me. So they were fighting over which one would get to me. Maxwell still had problems with his back, so he couldn't do too much. Maxwell got himself fired for strange backstage behavior. Given that PCW has been home to terrorists, murders, and Karl LeBlanc in the past, it must have been REALLY bad for Maxwell to get fired. I think the last thing involving him and his PCW contract was the piece of paper being thrown into the Void with him after he was permanently banished by Akiyama. So it was left to just Bobby Stormbringer and I. Stormbringer was left on his own after Xavier Kannon broke up the Asylum. He was eager to prove he could handle himself, and trying to beat me, the man who completely showed him up at the March Madness. He lobbied for a match against me at Spring Fling, but there was this thing where he... Well, he got himself fired. Seems some guy named Doc Healey thought it was a good idea to fire him, Big Alan Steele, and a few others too insignificant to my own history to remember. But I had nothing to do, if I didn't beat the crap out of Stormbringer at Spring Fling, I wasn't getting booked. So, I took matters into my own hands, and got a special unsanctioned match to take place before the pay-per-view itself. In that match, I just played around with Stormbringer and let him get in some token offense. Then the Dark Ages made their presence and beat him to shit. So, after letting him save some face by allowing him a double-countout. I just hung around. There was a problem with management, won't go much into it. Won some matches. At the Supremacy pay-per-view, I entered into the Soda Contract match because I had nothing else to do. Chef won it. I might've done better with it had I had sight to know what paper I was looking for. After that, I just wanted to be left alone. I was in the Dark Ages to that point... just because. No reason. For my career... my adult life... I've been something of a journeyman. I've never had a clear direction to go, a destination in mind. I just go. But I've always been the kind of guy who wants to be left alone when I want to be left alone. El Diablo didn't respect that, after Supremacy with the mass firing and rehiring, he had plans for a movie and he wanted me to be a part of it. I had no interest in it, but he was crazy or something back then (not to say he's of sound mind now) and wanted me for the role. I said no a number of times, he never took the answer. There were auditions and rehearsals, he held them at the shows. There was no script, no hint of a movie set or tapings, and yet there were 'Making of' specials and commercials for it. Diablo once nearly missed a match of his because he was too busy trying to put this movie together. Not that it ever got off the ground. He needed my cooperation for it, but I didn't want to do it. I mean, he was even trying to make us a tag team. That and helping me in matches that I didn't need his help with. He ran out in a match with Kramer Wild Wing, Hiro Saotome, and Kid Krayzie once. All three of whom were easily defeated by myself, yet Diablo thought he was doing me a favor getting involved anyway. Whatever to get me to do his movie. It was named after me, and involved cyborgs, but that's as far in the planning as he ever got. Oh, and then we wrestled Chef and Alexis Parshin. Those two were ultimately worthless against us. I remember that match; I started off, showed them up. But I had no intentions of winning the match, because that would have meant much more dealings with El Diablo and I didn't want that, I just wanted to be left alone. So I tagged him in, hit the One Last Time on him, and left him to be pinned. It's not that I hated him, but I wanted my own personal space, and he insisted on violating that. There is an issue of just how personal your life can be in this business, but to that point, and even now, I've sort of prided myself on keeping everything apart. I didn't want to wrestle because it would lead to other things, and I didn't want to be the center of attention. I just wrestled because I wanted to wrestle. That is a bit of a foreign thing, I'll admit, but hey, what can you do? So, at the risk of people not liking me because Diablo was a fan favorite at the time, I beat the crap out of him. Show after show, for three shows. He came there, and I took it to him. I even had him roughed up by the Dark Ages because they had nothing else to do. They weren't exactly employed by that point, but they wouldn't stop showing up for work. To Diablo's credit, he won a pay-per-view match between us at Gold & Glory, but much of that came from the fact that my leg gave out. And we'd been fighting for an hour prior to our match even happening. At this point, I'd like to make a detour and talk a little bit about PURE, which I wrestled for simultaneously with PCW. *Next Session: PURE*
