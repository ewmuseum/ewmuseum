The Three States of Mind: Drugged Up- Hallucinations

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th July Stage two of the three stages of mind of El Diablo is his hallucinatory state. Would the Mexican Devil take drugs? Would PCW allow it if he does? Afterall all they do is cause hallucinations and don't improve his in ring performance, but lets see stage two of El Diablo's confusing mind. The scene is that of El Diablo's bedroom in his home in Chicago, Illinois. The room consists of a large double bed with blue duvets, a bedside cabinet that has an alarm clock and a telephone on it and a mirror against the wall. The carpeting is a dark blue and the walls are painted blue and white. Opposite the bed is a television on a brown wooden table. Apart from that the room is quite baron. Lying in the bed and under the duvets is El Diablo, again with the face paint on and his black hair tied back. He is wearing a white vest over the top of his body, and what he wears on his lower body is unknown as the blue duvet covers it up. In El Diablo right hand is a box of 'Mescaline' tablets, and right now the look in El Diablo's eyes is not a normal look, but a look of confusion. 'Wow!' shouts El Diablo, 'Nothing like Mexican made dr...um headache tablets,' says El Diablo to convince himself he's doing nothing wrong. But what would PCW do if they found him taking these Mescaline tablets or carrying them? El Diablo rolls over onto his side in the bed and drops the tablets on the floor while a funny smile comes across his face. 'Whoa, all the pretty colors right in my bedroom,' comments the Mexican Devil. Of course there are no colors, but El Diablo can see all the colors of the rainbow right by his bedroom door. 'I miss home,' he says, 'These tablets just take me back home,' Diablo says while having a strange smile across his face. Is this really the right way for El Diablo to prepare for such an important match in his career? By taking god knows how many Mescaline tablets. Whatever the circumstances are, this really is wrong of El Diablo to do, especially at such a critical stage in his career. The stage where his actions could make or break him, and a win over Duke Williams would really catapult him to new heights, however a loss would leave him where he was before- nowhere. And the one problem wrong with this is that El Diablo doesn't seem to see that there is any problem. Suddenly the telephone rings, and this alerts El Diablo by making him jump up a little bit in his bed and then landing back down. 'Now I know why bungee jumping is so fun,' Diablo says obviously out of his mind- well when is he ever in his right mind? The phone continues to ring. 'Ahhh! That's one large bee making that noise,' El Diablo says while swatting away at the air around his bed, to obviously no avail. He begins looking around the room with a glazed look over his eyes, Diablo then looks at the bedside cabinet. 'Hey you!' says a voice that isn't El Diablo's! The phone continues ringing as El Diablo looks startled and looks at the phone in worry. He then points at himself with his right index finger. 'Me?' asks Diablo to the phone. 'Yes, answer me dumbass!' is the voice El Diablo hears. El Diablo looks scared, so he leans over and picks up the handset of the phone to cease it's ringing sound. 'He-llo?' Diablo asks nervously. 'Ah, Mr Diaz,' says the responding voice. Diablo looks around the room cautiously. 'How do you do that?' questions the Mexican Devil. 'Excuse me?' the male voice replies. 'How do you, a telephone talk?' asks Diablo. A long silence occurs. 'Uh, did I offend you Mr Telephone?' asks Diablo to reassure himself. 'Look, Mr Diaz, this is your lawyer Tom Clancy,' responds the voice. Diablo blinks and tries to focus properly. 'I hired a telephone as my lawyer?' El Diablo asks nervously. Again there is a silence and then an angered tone on the other end of the phone. 'No, are you an idiot? I am calling you to inform you that your wife has filed divorce proceedings,' says Clancy. El Diablo thinks for a moment. 'I'm married?' he questions. Instead of a response a rattling is heard then a dial tone. 'Oh no, I killed the phone,' states the Mexican Devil in fear. He suddenly drops the handset on the bed, then gets both his hands and starts pushing up and down on the handle of the handset. 'One...two...three...four...five,' he counts with the pushes he does. He then puts his mouth to the earpiece and breathes into it, like you would when giving CPR. He then places his ear to it and still hears the dialling tone. 'Shit, he can't die,' yells Diablo loudly. He repeats the CPR process several times but to no avail. Eventually he hangs up the phone, lowers his head and does the sign of the cross over it. 'Goodbye Mr Telephone, you lived a long and prosperous life,' he comments sadly. Diablo then lies back down on the bed and rests his head against a large pillow. 'And now for Fusion,' he mutters as his eyes start to drift away. Slowly and suddenly El Diablo falls asleep after his recent hallucinations. Good job he won't be taking Mescaline during the match against Duke Williams on Fusion, otherwise he really could be in trouble. But is El Diablo this crazy every time he takes a trip back home with the Mescaline? And just what will be on El Diablo's mind once conscious? He should hope that Duke Williams is.
