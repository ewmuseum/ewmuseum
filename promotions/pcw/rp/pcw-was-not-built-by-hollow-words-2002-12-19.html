PCW Was Not Built by Hollow Words

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/2002 *The scene opens up inside of Jon Kano's plush hotel room somewhere in Canada. The scene seems focused on a large vanity mirror just outside of the bathroom, which the door to is shut. Then suddenly, the door opens and guess who steps out? The one, the only, the legendary Jon Kano with only a towel wrapped around his waist and his Television Championship Belt over his right shoulder. Kano has beads of water all over his body, giving his large muscles even more definition and his hair is soaking wet. Kano isn't his jovial self, but seems to have a focused look, a very intense look as he stares at himself in the mirror.* Kano: Teresa Tomas. Teresa f*cking Tomas. I am not like anyone you have ever wrestled before. You ask why I attacked you? Oh I did not attack you. And don't think for a second that you helped me win this title. You see, Teresa, when I step away from this game that you and I play. When I finally say I have had enough, my legacy will say among other things that I was one of the most resilient wrestlers to ever step foot in the squared circle that is my personal playground. I can take a beating and keep on coming. Hell, girl, Ivan Stanislav broke my f*cking neck. Two cracked vertabra in my neck from his vicious attack. Somehow, someway it was fixed without surgery and I was back in the ring within three weeks. A year ago, come Decimation, I took on AFL-CIO and Big Bubba McCoy by myself and took a beating, but beat those three. So don't think I owe you a God damn thing. *Kano takes his Television Championship belt and places it on the counter.* Kano: I have been to hell and back, Teresa. You cannot break me. I stood toe-to-toe with Ivan, Meanstreak, Doug Troy, Tyler Nelson, Boda, The Chef, and countless other household names. Do you think you have as much talent as any of them? Teresa, you may have some talent, but you are not going to be able to hang with this 6' 10', 304 pound monster of a man. I was one of the ones who built PCW, and you are a woman. The truth hurts, but Teresa, WOMEN DID NOT BUILD PCW, IT WAS BUILT BY MEN. PCW WAS NOT BUILT BY HOLLOW WORDS, IT WAS BUILT WITH BLOOD AND IRON. I was here for all of the major events since PCW opened, I am the man who made PCW. Teresa, you are too young, too green, and too inexperienced to take this title from me. Do you know that I never lost my Continental or Television titles cleanly? I was screwed out of both of them. I have been continued to be screwed over since I lost the Television Title. *Kano pauses for a second and takes a deep breath.* Kano: Teresa, some people say its your time. Teresa, some people say that I ain't got it anymore. Well, Teresa, I still got it. I have proven that I still got it. I am approaching my twenty-seventh birthday and right now I am at the top of my game. Teresa, when I am at the top of my game, not you, nor Duke, nor anyone else in PCW can defeat me. Teresa, I'll leave you with this thought, do you got what it takes when the chips are down on Sunday or are you, heh, going to choke once again, because one mistake is all it takes for me to take you out with the KANOSPEAR OR KANOPLEX BECAUSE I AM WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano snatches his PCW Television Championship belt from the counter and walks away from the mirror.*
