Dream a Little Dream

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/05/03 …. I had the weirdest dream last night…. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Why am I on this mountain? How did I get here? What am I climbing towards? These are all questions that I have, but I don’t know the answers. All I know is that I’ve got to keep climbing.... When I look down, it’s endless. I can’t see anything except for more mountain. There is no base; no-where I could climb down to. Nothing. But when I look up, I can see something that gives me hope. I’m not quite sure what, but it’s the only thing I have to go on. That peak, at the very top of this mountain, where the light shines so brightly.... That’s where I’ve got to go if I’m ever to get off this hunk of rock. And so I climb. I struggle so hard that my hands are being worn out. Blood is beginning to seep out from behind my fingernails, mixed with dirt and rubble. It’s painful all right.... But it’s got to be worth the pain.... It’s GOT to. Oh yeah, I forget to mention.... I’m climbing with a talking mountain goat too. “You know, you’d get to the top much quicker if you didn’t stop for all these breathers” he says to me. “Fuck off” is all the reply I can muster, and who can blame me? For as long as I remember being stuck on this mountain, I remember having this goat by my side, talking to me as I had none him for years. This is starting to freak me out.... Yet, I continue to climb. If not to reach the top, then to lose this stupid goat at the least. His smell is starting to get unbearable. “Do you know why you’re here Manuel?” asked the goat when I rest and take a look at the eternal sunset in the background. “Because this is one weird dream?” I asked back, but no. The goat shook his head as he explained his question further. “I’m not talking about the mountain Manuel boy. I’m talking about PCW. The wrestling world. The choices that you have made that led you to this point?” I thought for a moment. Why am I here? “Because I’m a damn good wrestler, and it’d be a shame to have wasted my talents in an office.” I say, smiling widely. The goat bites my ankle. “Wrong answer.” As I rub the bite mark, contemplating whether or not to hit him back, I think some more. Then I say, “Because I want to make something of my life and be seen to a winner?” This time, the bastard bites my right arm. I go to hit him instinctively, but my lift fist goes right through it’s head. Literally. “Try again Manuel.” And so I lie down, trying to forget about the painful bites and trying to ignore the uncomfortable mountain surface. Why did I choose this as my livelihood? Why did I become a wrestler.... I guess originally, it all boiled down to honoring my father. Hell, I even quit Mexico cause I really couldn't cope with the memories.Wrestling was the perfect escape.... But then.... It hits me. I know what the goat is talking about now. “Because I wanted to win back the love of my parents by proving that becoming a wrestler didn’t mean I was throwing my life away. That it could mean that I was just as successful as a lawyer, or schoolteacher, or whatever, only my kind of success was different. It may not have been reliable, but it would be something that I could build my life around. Is that the answer you’re looking for?” The goat smiles. Strangely, it’s almost the mirror image of my smile. He then flicked his head to the left, as if indicating to me. I turned and looked, and to my astonishment, we were at the top of the mountain! Only, when I looked back, it wasn’t we anymore. The goat had disappeared. I pulled myself off the ground, just as confused, if not more so, than I was when this mess first started. I scratched my head, just as a bright golden light started to grow from behind me.... I span around hazily, and there, in the middle of the light, was what looked to be the Cruiserweight Title, laying down on a large plate, as if some sacrificial offering. “What the....?” I couldn’t even finish the sentence, as I walked over towards the belt, touching it with the tips of my fingers and then recoiling in pain. MAN! That things hot! “I wouldn’t touch it if I were you.” I turn around, and there stands Skitzo, smiling. At least, I think it’s Skitzo. He looks the same, but all the colours are wrong, and his voice doesn’t sound right. But hey? What do I care? “Holy crap.... Coach! I mean, sorry.... Skitzo! Is it good to see you!” I exclaim, as I move towards him looking to shake hands. “Shut up.” I retract my hand nervously, as his smile fades and he walks past me without a care, picking up the Cruiserweight Title and then hoisting it onto his shoulder. “Look man, I don’t want any trouble.... I just want us to carry on the way we are.” He looks down at the belt, smiling, before turning back towards me and saying “SHUT UP.” I don’t know what else to do. Skitzo walks up to me, coming so close that we are literally toe-to-toe. I can almost smell his breath. Smells like kippers. “Manuel, you have lost sight. You should be thinking about this,” he says, tapping the Title, “.... Not ME.” I’m in shock. “But, you’re my friend…. My amigo ! Together, we were set to kick ass and make PCW a better place!” “No.... I am but a distraction. The reality of your existence is exactly what you told the goat earlier on. You’re not a wrestler because of Skitzo. You are a wrestler because of your parents. You won’t to make it to the top because you want to win them back.” I can’t find words to reply.... What is he trying to say? What is he trying to do to me? “And so here’s the deal. You can’t even touch this Title, because you’ve lost track of what it means. But there is one thing that you can do that’ll change that.... And all that is is to forget about being me.” It smiles. I say it, because it can’t really be him and I realise that now. “Stop being an idiot Manuel and do what’s right. Do what you have to do for a change, and stop worrying about Skitzo because if you don’t, then I can guarantee that you will never win this strap and become nothing. And you don’t want that, do you Manuel?” Reluctantly, I nod my head. Skitzo smiles, as he then takes the belt off his shoulder, telling me “Good. Now that you understand, you can be saved.” He then moves around behind me. I go to turn and take a look, but find I’m paralysed. Not with fear, that’s for sure. But I cannot move nonetheless. My eyeballs look down as Skitzo's arms come into view and he begins to wrap the Cruiserweight Title around my waist. It doesn’t burn now. In fact, it feels good, as if something new has flooded into my body. I can feel him fasten it at the back, before suddenly being able to move again. “Now you are free.” I turn around but Skitzo is no longer there. It is my father. He smiles at me grimly, as I fall to my knees, tears in my eyes. He then puts a hand on my shoulder, comforting but at the same time, painful. He grips digs in, feeling as if it is ripping my muscles apart. I look up and his skin seems to be melting. His clothes turn ragged, as if aging as well whilst I try to get away but can’t. Within seconds, it is just a skeleton standing in front of me, holding my shoulder so tight that blood is beginning to escape. I can’t break free of its grip. I can’t escape the pain.... I’m trapped. But then the belt unfastens and falls to the ground, and the skeleton disappears. I collapse on my back, crying, as the blood seems to envelope me entire being. Without warning, the goat sticks it’s head in front of mine. “Don’t worry about it kiddo. You’ll be fine.” It said, before then moving. I can hear it trotting off, as I lift my head to try and spot it, desperately attempting to force words out of my mouth, but I just couldn’t find any.... As I do this, I then notice the background. I watch as the sun that has been hanging forever just over the horizon finally slithers down behind the clouds and out of sight. Blackness. ........ ...... .... .. . -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- …. At first, I didn’t have a clue what to make of it…. …. I was tempted to try and call Pharoh and get his psychiatrist’s number, thinking I’d gone completely off the deep end…. …. But then, I sat down and started thinking…. …. Maybe it wasn’t so weird after all…. …. Maybe it’s just a reminder as to why I’m here, doing what I love…. …. Of what’s really important…. …. There’s one thing that I definitely have learnt though…. …. Don’t trust a talking goat….
