A Voyage Into the Abyss

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 9 (OOC: For anyone looking for my last promo, look at #981 which is also listed to count towards the PPV. No-show my ass!) (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) I’ve spent these past days studying manuals and manuscripts issued by my Blackhelm affiliates in preparation of whatever deeds lay ahead during my tenure in their organization. In these past days I’ve become acutely studied in a vast quantity of hideous abominations that cause me to shudder at the very mention of their names. Beings such as The Creator Of All Things and The Thing That Should Not Be, the blind idiot god Azathoth; Ithaqua, wind dweller of the far North American/Arctic wastes; the enigmatic rovers of time and space called the Hounds of Tindalos; that which dwells beneath the sands of Africa, Shudde M’ell; plus a variety of others I’ve received comprehensive dossiers of from those of the Coalition and my spiritual contacts, Father Craven and Bishop McCarver. This undertaking has become a tedious one; I didn’t know I had to become a damned occultist to join their little agency but I have seen things that have caused even an unyielding skeptic to question reality. As if it all wasn’t arduous enough, I was recently notified of my match-up with ‘Punisher’ Mike Frampton this week at PCW’s Tuesday Turmoil. In light of recent events in my life I would normally feel somewhat irritated at such belated notification. Of course, this was an exception. An exception in that it was indeed Mike Frampton. As such, I found myself reminiscing over last week’s Extreme title match time and again. I’ve simply become weary of the games that have come to plague this business, the innumerable ploys used to help the struggling, second-rate talent ‘get over’ at the expense of the deserving. Because of my employer’s obstinate ambition to help young Frampton and ‘Extreme disgrace’ Big Daveylicious find acceptance among the fickle wrestling fanbase, a plethora of superior talent has suffered. I still remember that moment an hour before the contest started in the corridors of Cincinnati International, when an ensemble of Rolex-toting stuffed-suit executives filed into my locker room (or should I say, the locker room I shared with all the other sacrificial lambs being led to the slaughter that night) and demanded we all hold back the extent of our talents, for sake of ‘helping our brothers out’ as they put it. I distinctly recall that marketing weasel Kerry Byrne nod in my direction as we made eye contact; apparently my reputation for defying orders precedes me. To further drive the point home, one of those brainless lackeys lurking in the back of that corporate cluster made sure we noticed the wavering booklet of pink-colored slips in his right hand. I still recall the appalled expression on the faces of the collective Cowabunga Connection, and the clandestine rage portrayed through the body language of Jon Kano and the Chef. It was a crime, and every soul in that locker room knew it. Something I like to refer to as ‘corporate rape’. For this reason my demeanor altered from a scowl to a grin as my opponent’s identity was revealed. The realization I was being handed the opportunity to claim vengeance made my skin crawl, but it did so out of pure ecstasy rather than trepidation. In truth I hardly feel the need to soil my hands with the blood of Frampton; but desire is a far cry from necessity, they are vastly separate entities. The PCW audience has been conditioned to believe Frampton is something special, not disparate of that farce of an Extreme champion, Big Daveylicious. I would prove to them that the synthetic talent called the Punisher was truly nothing more than a red herring. Alas, my joy proved to be fleeting. As I wandered through the airport yesterday only moments following the crack of dawn, my vision still clouded in a tired haze no less, I received that telltale chime from my cell phone (personally I despise the wretched things, but that yuppie Tyler Nelson forced me into carrying it for ‘business purposes’). It turned out to be the booking committee vice-chairman, his voice randomly iterating an irksome crunching I assumed to be his breakfast. In that high-pitched and profoundly aggravating voice the bastard has, he so calmly informed me that Turmoil had been cancelled for the week and thus my battle with Mikey was postponed to a later date. Obviously anticipating my impending fury, he promptly stammered out that I would be compensated for my trouble accordingly with a late addition Pay Per View match-up on Sunday. The committee had spoken and as a result I had drawn fellow recent recruits Anna Keyson and Ryan ‘Streak’ Stephens in a bout touted as one ‘showcasing the new talent’. Still annoyed by their petty peace offering for canceling my chance at redemption (not to mention my correlated lack of sleep last night), I returned to my ‘humble’ little abode in the covert warzone that is inner Boston to obtain a few more hours of sleep, which I now plan to do. If I can’t garner a measure of retribution by eradicating Frampton’s hollow dime-a-dozen carcass, I may as well do the next best thing by doing so with a pair of yuppies. * * * I found myself standing on the outskirts of a meadow, surrounded on all sides by wavering willows and lush grassy vegetation with a thick evergreen forest of cylindrical pines and cone-shaped spruces forming the perimeter. Thunder clashed in the distance, a jagged streak of lightning penetrating the heavens with a brilliant flash that illuminated my surroundings beyond their murky twilight tinge into a momentary hue of pseudo-daylight. A pallid waning moon was visible overhead in the budding darkness of early evening, casting inadequately visible light onto the moor upon which I stood. Beneath my feet was a dirt path giving the appearance of heavy travel from my point of location, gradually thinning out as it stretched further toward the edge of the woods. Compelled forth by whatever unseen force, I pivoted on my heel and began to walk down the trail, the grass wilting onto the path and gently caressing at my calves as a light breeze begins to wisp about the field, leaves fluttering in the gentle wind. Wallowing through the dense sea of foliage I traversed, mystically drawn through the brush under some hypnotic trance of supernatural origin. Moonlight peeked lightly through the thick trees to reflect off shimmering leaves coated in dew, and revealing my my path which was no longer marked by any visible trail as I forged ahead through gangly underbrush and the various flora and fauna of this unfamiliar woodland. All was quiet, starkly contrasted to my preliminary anticipations of owls hooting from their nests in the trees and baying wolves on far-off ridges. The brush was beginning to dissipate somewhat, and as such I hoped it would lead to a clearing or even a backwoods residence. I’ve never been an outdoors-type of guy and hiking is an experience as foreign to me as can be; regardless, I felt an uncanny sense of calm and serenity as I made that pilgrimage through the dense brush to whatever arcane magnet propelled me forward. My suspicions were confirmed after a remarkably brief trek of nearly a mile in distance, the wooded region abruptly merging into a dark clearing beneath an expanse of open sky. It was an extensive graveyard, still and seemingly abandoned in a state of disrepair and dilapidation. The sprawling necropolis was animated under the pale light of that ashen sphere overhead, casting drawn-out shadows that appeared to dance and sway in slow and quirky movements as if doing so to the tune of some sinister ballad of blasphemous origin. The slabs marking the multitude of ancient graves sat crumbling in a decrepit condition, tumbling from their base to rest on the brown and neglected grass with the names and lifespans illegible through the countless years of neglect. In the center of the array of degenerating tombstones towered a gloomy crypt, formed seemingly of granite in a predominantly Gothic design yet tinged with perfect curves and acute angles defying normal human building method. Set along the roof of the crypt itself at random intervals sat a variety of gargoyles, carved of onyx and basalt and all shining with a fine polish in the pale light emanating from overhead. As I made this observation I glanced above and noted the livid sky appearing discolored, a reddish tint splashed over the normally midnight ceiling and giving a light scarlet effect. Even the stars themselves somehow appeared as a virtual panorama of color, Polaris taking on an aqua shade and the visible planets burning with a disconcertingly fiery hue as if forged in some searing furnace and placed jointly in the night sky to accompany the bizarre gallery of heavenly ornaments. Those engraved beasts seemed to watch me with their eyes of shimmering jade, possibly implanted by some mad craftsman decades ago before this cemetery found itself derelict and cast off to its current existence of decay. It was undoubtedly odd that such a brilliant structure could be erected in the core of the wilderness, and left to fester without a shred of upkeep no doubt. A legion of bats swept from the resting spot within a crevice of the sharp overhang, flooding the silence of the night with a shrill melody of squeals while creating a staunch cacophony with the deathly calm I had become accustomed to by this point. Amidst the heavenly phenomena and the uncharacteristic quiet plaguing the wild this night, normally even a jaded bastard of my fortitude would frankly piss his pants. Yet for some peculiar reason tranquility gripped my emotions, carrying me nonchalantly to the door of the tomb as it slid silently open as if by some sensory mechanism. I emerged into a gulf of darkness, an ironic relief from the morbid surroundings of the upper world. Initially all was dark, but moments later I found the stairwell abruptly illuminated by a drab light emanating from an indistinguishable source. I cast my eyes about the granite walls, finding neither torches nor any alternative sources of the primitive and rudimentary luminance yet detecting a cluster of obscene glyphs scrawled about the walls. These drawings were elaborate and complete, unlike their primordial predecessors of aeons past, and were more along the lines of monochrome murals sketched in a substance not dissimilar from coal. Satyrs and impish demons (among other nameless things) pranced and cavorted about in scenes of apocalyptic nature, scenes repulsive yet simultaneously alluring in some macabre fashion. I glimpsed farther left, seeing more of the drawings similar to those already described. Satisfied, I pried my eyes away from the strangely fascinating sketches and descended down the dimly lit flight of stairs toward completion of whatever profound and clandestine quest had led me here. I trudged down the winding staircase into the labyrinth below and began making my way through the intricate catacombs, a pungent odor of centuries-old decay wafting through my nostrils. I found my way through the tunnels of that grand subterranean maze, through the muck and grime built up over forgotten time in those corridors, reaching a great cavernous seal over ten feet on height and nearly eight in width. The door was marked with yet more of the befuddling glyphs, the most prominent being a mock portrait of monstrous composition, a horrid beast with humanoid qualities in its posture yet adorned with appendages in the resemblance of wings and a mass of rudimentary tendrils or tentacles. I had suspicions of what specter the image represented, yet before I had the opportunity study the imprint more studiously the door opened wide to reveal a vast chamber ahead. To call this hall majestic is absurd, yet deceptively fitting at the same time. In the center of the room towered a dais glistening with a brass tint, with a quartet of obelisks soaring above in each corner of the room, each constructed of that shimmering black metal I had the misfortune of seeing time and again. Around the room, illuminating all before me, existed a throng of suspended spheres of incandescent light. Innumerable effigies of a number of beings decorated this broad chamber, placed on pillars of black. Those small flares of light danced about the room, coming to rest at the foot of a substantial monolith as I leveled my eyes forward to what authority they answered. To describe with proper justice the terrible monstrosity before me I do not have the vocabulary, and I do not believe words exist in the English language to wholly describe justifiably what loomed before me in the bowels of that isolated crypt in what I now believe to be the dreaded and mythical land of Leng. It was simply an amorphous abomination, one I dare say held kinship with those nameless beasts caged in New Miskatonic’s underground facility in that it seemed to possess no true and defined boundary in its existence on either the physical or ethereal plane. Its form fluctuated fleetingly, contours merging with bordering shadows and morphing continuously into new and grotesque contours. In all fact, shadow seemed to be the lone element comprising its flesh (if one could call it that) in that which appeared to be shadow in the dim chamber shifted and swayed in contrast to those incandescent orbs of light floating erratically about the room. Spawned of some hellish netherworld, all that remained constant of the demon was its unchanging eyes that pulsated bronze and flashed arbitrarily. “Shanahan....” The lone word reverberated in my head with a reaffirming echo, transferred through telepathic means I would wager as it was procured of no audible foundation. “You have become a thorn in the side of our kind, Bruce. Following that pitifully disgraceful rout in your little GTT tournament our plans initially revolved around simply leaving you empty and bitter as always, for you to wallow eternally in your apathy and ire on the road to the brutal and vehement demise awaiting you as foretold in the stars. You were but a fleeting plaything, Bruce, a momentary object of our divine disparagement to squander away the countless millennia of our collective, tedious imprisonment.” The fiend shifted, turning its back to me as I watched it morph as one with the shadows again. The looming fiend and devourer of souls again altered form into one compact body of wispy shadow, brimming with thick muscle and adorned in a ceremonial headdress or turban akin to that of ancient Egyptian pharaohs. In fact, as that heinous deity turned back to me again I swore he had taken on the guise of ex-PCW champion Pharaoh! “But no .... you choose to pursue your dreams, to shadow those foreboding revelations haunting your slumber and even your daily life, ones We instilled in your petty mortal mind. The Purple One and Stephens shall rend your flesh this Sunday, but your soul already resides in Our clutches. Our physical forms are detained by the Elders, yet Our divine messages will forever wrack the minds of the weak and compel the pervasive to wreak further unspeakable acts. You find solace in your spiritual guidance and your Blackhelm compatriots, and for now you remain safe only through mystical antiquities and accursed relics crafted by the Elders to preserve our perpetual doom. All sources of artificial comfort in light of the wrath We shall instill upon your race. Soon the Cleansing comes, and by Our edict the heavens themselves shall quake and the Earth shall split, and from fiery chasms of brimstone and flame We shall come. There shall be no escape, and there shall be no salvation. Worry as you wish over what happens to your mortal flesh against the Purple One and Stephens. There is a far greater Gehenna in your future, Shanahan. And there is no reprieve in the stars. I sank to my knees on the floor of that crypt, those words burning in my mind as the realization sunk in. 'Nyarlathotep. No…..it can’t be….' The loathsome monstrosity gave no audible answer as the echoing responses in my head dispersed, save a garbled chant comprised of distorted and inhuman monosyllables. Its tone was partially sardonic, yet at the same time had an almost melancholy indifference though its exact meaning was lost on me. I had no knowledge of alien tongues, especially not that of whatever cosmic abyss this fiend had arisen from. I thought back to my readings, the myths and theories surrounding the benevolent deities of primordial ancestry marked as the CCD: “To nightmare is to be one with Nyarlathotep. Nyarlathotep, the crawling chaos; Nyarlathotep, the Messenger.” One who had been said to pass into mortal form under the guise of an Egyptian pharaoh in many a tale (a fitting description for why He also resembled the PCW superstar in question). Where Nyarlathotep went, rest vanished; for the small hours were ripe with the screams of nightmares. Nightmares which indeed are Nyarlathotep.” * * * If ever I have needed a prayer answered, this is that night. I do not know what is imminent, of what forces have me in their crosshairs. Butif there is a God, I pray he helps me now. I've come to fear my dreams and the repercussoins they may hold in this bitter reality. I truly have no refuge left, and as such I no longer have anything to lose. Take that for what it is worth......
