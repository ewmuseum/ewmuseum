Preparations

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-7-03 Friday, March 7th 2003Primetime Championship Wrestling Presents:Friday Fusion [ It’s only Friday morning and already the Target Center has tonight’s event in bright lights. On the outside of the large building, the setting is that of a tranquil one. A far cry from the scene we’ll all witness in by the time late afternoon rolls around. Just like the hours leading up to every PCW event, the scene outside would be that of a chaotic one. Vehicles filling the lots as far as the eye could see. The lines of people going the same distance. ] [ Inside, the preparations of tonight’s said event appears to be ninety-nine percent done. The promotional banners and flyers are everywhere upon first entering. Off to the right of the entrance, we catch a glimpse of one Eve Desecourt. She appears to be standing beside the merchandising stand, holding a t-shirt up in front of her. ] “What do you mean??” Eve asks, appalled. “This is Eric ‘Dominion’ Wolfson’s new t-shirt! How can you not want to sell this tonight?” [ The man standing behind the stand looks at the shirt for a moment. ] “Listen lady.” He replies, sounding like it’s around the fiftieth time he’s said it. “I repeat. This article is not on my list. And if it’s not on my list, it doesn’t sell. Understand?” [ Eve huffs a little, tossing the shirt over her shoulder. Reaching down into the bag she’s brought with her, she digs around a little before pulling out a baseball cap. ] “Look.” Eve demands, looking down at the hat in her hand. “Eric Wolfson: Rising Star Champion. See, it says it right there. Now you tell me. After Dominion wins that Championship tonight, do you have any idea how his merchandise is going to sell? Do you have any friggin’ idea?? It’s going to sell like a cheap prostitute in a trailer park! And by the looks of you, Goober, you can relate to that analogy.” [ Shaking his head, the man merely lifts his walkie talkie and requests the presence of the arena security. ] “Oh, it’s like that?” Eve asks, shocked. “You can’t make me go anywhere. I’m a business associate of Eric Wolfson, who happens to be under contract with the promotion selling out your seats tonight. You just work for the arena. You can’t make me leave! Now, let’s talk teddy bears. I’ve got…” [ Suddenly, Eve is grabbed by her arms by two security personnel. They begin to drag her off. ] “You can’t do this to me!” Eve screams, kicking her legs. “I’m trying to make you people more money, can’t you see that??” [ Eve drops the teddy bear out of her hand and kicks it toward the merchandiser. The security is quick to drag her off. Meanwhile, Eric Wolfson pushes the curtain aside and peers out into the empty arena. Taking a deep breath, memories of his past begin to fill his mind. It’s been quite some time since he’s performed in front of a crowd as large as this one would be tonight. A big differential in terms of the underground fighting he’d done in recent weeks. ] “One… Two…” Wolfson begins, throwing the curtain o the side. “Three.” [ Wolfson steps out onto the stage, taking a moment to, for the first time today, get a good look at the vast complexity of the arena. Until you actually step out in the lights, it’s impossible to really get a grip on how many people can actually fill this large room. ] “Good work up there guys.” Wolfson hollers, looking up at the men on the scaffolding putting the final touches on the large screen above the ramp. “Remember, it’s ‘The way you like it’ by Adema for me tonight, dig?” [ The men shrug, as it’s not their job to handle anything of the sort. None the less, Wolfson slides down the ramp toward the ring, where three men appear to be congregating. ] “What’s up, boys?” Wolfson asks, hopping up onto the apron. “You boys ready for a little training?” [ The three men nod their heads, finding their respective corners. Judging by the academy shirts they’re all wearing, it can be assumed that they are from the PCW Academy, most likely preparing for dark matches later tonight. The truth is, Wolfson had found these guys in the back and offered to spar with them. Of course, the match would be a four corners. ] “Ok, boys. Remember what I told ya.” Wolfson spoke aloud, making his way to the last free corner. “Tonight, not only am I getting a warm up, but you guys are getting the experience of being in the ring with a guy who actually MADE the PCW roster, dig?” [ The bell is rung and all three of the young guys rush Wolfson. In true Dominion fashion, however, Wolfson slides out of the ring and drops down to the floor. The three men inside, eager for the win, begin to turn their attentions to each other. ] “Christ.” Wolfson says under his breath. “Why do I gotta be the guy with *everyone* jumping on?” [ As all three men begin battling it out inside the ring, Wolfson sulks around outside in observation. Finally, he slides back into the ring after grabbing a chair. In three swift movements, all three men are laid out by way of the steel chair Wolfson wielded. ] “And this is why you’re in the Academy, boys.” Wolfson challenges, tossing the chair to the outside. “Not ready for the big boys. First rule in battle, fellas. You gotta be ready to do *whatever* it takes to get the win. But, since you’ve all failed in that task, allow me to demonstrate what a real warrior is like.” [ Reaching down, Wolfson lifts the firs man off the mat. He’s barely conscious, hardly able to stand on his own legs. Wolfson bends his victim over or applies a standing head scissors and grabs the victim around the waist. He lifts the victim up so he is over the Wolfson's shoulder. From there, Wolfson places both his arms under the arms of the victim and extends his arms out as far as they can. Wolfson pulls down on the victim's arms/upper body, flipping the victim's body over so he is falling face first towards the mat. On the way down, Wolfson hooks the victim's head in a front face lock and falls to the mat, driving the victim face/head first into the mat. ] “Rule of Dominion.” Wolfson informs, climbing back to his feet. “See, now you’re out. You’re hurting. You’re not getting back up for a good while. I pin you. See how easy that was?” [ Wolfson simply slides out of the ring and heads back up the ramp. Truth be told, he didn’t give a shit about those young guys or helping them out. All he really wanted to do was give his finisher a workout. ] [ Meanwhile, Eve kicks her foot across the ground, angrily sending several pebbles scattering outside. She’d been thrown out of the building, and she was quite upset. ] “And they just threw me out!” Eve shouts into her phone. “Are we leaving, or what? I’ve got to talk to my lawyers.” [ Eve pauses, and finally hangs the phone up. Within seconds, Wolfson pops the door open and holds it open for her. ] “Not yet.” Wolfson replies as Eve steps back into the building. “I’ve got to meet Larry here shortly. Apparently, he’s got some news for me. Some news that he says will ‘guarantee me the Rising Star Championship’ tonight.” [ Eve raises her eyebrows, looking on. Suddenly, the two are bombarded by security. Again, the two men grab Eve. ] “Eric!” She shouts, again kicking. “Do something… Not again!” [ Wolfson looks on, laughing to himself a bit. ] “Ok guys.” Wolfson begins. “Let her go, she’s with me.” [ As the two men continue to drag Eve away, Wolfson looks to the third man of the security team. ] “Are they deaf?” Wolfson asks. “I said she’s with me.” [ The man just shrugs his shoulders. ] “I can’t do that, sir.” The man starts, looking up the hallway toward Eve. “She was being quite disruptive earlier, and as he was being removed the first time, she broke away from our security and punched a merchandiser in the face.” [ Wolfson’s eyes widened, as he began laughing. ] “So what?” Wolfson asked, still laughing. “He probably tried hitting on her. Who knows.” [ Suddenly, Eve broke loose from the security and rushed down the hall, with both men in pursuit. The third man, still standing beside Wolfson, started to join the chase. ] “Yo, hold on.” Wolfson said, grabbing the man’s arm. “Let your boys handle that. Come, take a walk with me. I’ve got some information for you that I’m betting you’ll find beneficial tonight.” [ The man nodded his head. He wasn’t in that great of shape, therefore he wasn’t too excited about running around the arena anyway. Both men began walking down the hallway. ] “Listen, I’ve got a tip on something going down tonight.” Wolfson started. “A woman by the name of Cozette du Pres is employed by PCW. Word has it that she has ulterior motives for her visit here tonight. I’m not exactly sure what those motives are, but you can bet they’re not good.” [ The man nodded his head. ] “So here’s what I suggest.” Wolfson continued. “Word has it that this plan of hers is to take place immediately before tonight’s Rising Star contest. So instead of grabbing her as soon as she arrives at the arena, I suggest you grab her as soon as her music hits. That would be the best time. You gotta catch her in the act, y’know?” [ Both men continued to walk down the hallway. Wolfson hid a smirk he was fighting, while the security guard took the information in. ] “And you have no idea what this act entails?” The man asked, raising an eyebrow. “Because, I mean, I can’t just grab one of the PCW competitors without some sort of probable cause.” [ Wolfson nodded. ] “I can understand that.” He replied. “I’m just giving you a heads up. It’s not that I’m trying to eliminate one of my opponents tonight, either. It’s just that, see, I’m a believer in the judicial system. I don’t like crime on our streets and certainly not in my federation. I just wanna nip it in the bud as soon as possible, dig?” [ Wolfson held his hands out to his sides, in an attempt to show he was sincere. ] “I mean, who knows.” He continued. “She could be selling drugs. She could have a bomb strapped to her, hell if I know. I just got a tip that she’s up to no good this evening. That’s all. I’m just doing my part as a concerned citizen.” [ The man nodded in agreement. ] “I see where you’re coming from.” He replied. “I’ll see what I can do tonight. Just before the Rising Star match, you say?” [ Wolfson nodded, patting the man on the shoulder. ] “That’s right.” Wolfson answered. “You’ll have to grab her at that exact time. So what if she misses her match, y’know? We’ve got to take extra measures in times like these.” [ The man nodded his head. Apparently, he was buying Wolfson’s story. And if that was the case, this four corners match would degenerate to that of a triple threat. Something Wolfson was aiming for in reality. ] “So you do your job tonight.” Wolfson commanded. “And I’ll do mine. Listen, if you do this, I’ll buy you a drink this evening. Hell, I’m going to be celebrating after the show anyway. What with doing away with this whole Shooting Star thing and becoming the PCW Rising Star Champion and all.” [ The man agreed just as his walkie talkie went off. Apparently, they needed his help with a certain woman they were unable to capture. The man rushed off, leaving Wolfson standing alone in the empty hallway. ] “That’s right, little sheep.” Wolfson whispered. “Your strings are being pulled.” [ Wolfsont turned away and headed back toward the room that would serve as his dressing room this evening. There, he’d meet with his agent and manager Larry Jones. Apparently, he had big news for his client that would make this evening’s Shooting Star bout almost automatic, in the favor of Wolfson. ] [ Tonight, the Champion would either solidify his reign or fall to the hands of the new comer. Tonight, the new comer would either solidify his dominance or be knocked back a step. Only time would tell. Fusion hung in the balance. ]
