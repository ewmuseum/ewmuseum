No Gold, no Glory: ONLY GUTS!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now NOTE: THE TOWN NAMES HAVE NOT BEEN DONE! On January 30th, we will see both the World Cup Final and PCW's Gold and Glory Pay Per View, in both events both gold and glory will be on the line. And much like the World Cup, professional wrestling is a sport of beauty, passion and emotion. But this Sunday two men, Jon Kano and Corey Williams, will walk into a ring and attempt to beat the crap out of each other until one man falls and cannot get up. This match will not contain the general displays of beauty, passion and emotion. For in this match there is NO gold on the line and NO glory on the line. This is a match for respect. So in this match, what we will see is nothing but pure guts and determination; brute strength, tough SOBs and a never say die attitude. Or at least we hope we will. For one of these two men has often been accused of being a 'showman', even on Fusion he was accused of being 'all talk.' It is not Jon Kano, for Kano is better than anyone else at PCW when it comes to getting it the ring and getting the job done, he has been doing that since the early days of PCW. No, Kano, although an entertainer, can kick ass any day. But Corey Williams, on the other hand, happens to be a big fan of both gold and glory. Sometimes he's made some of the most seemingly illogical decisions just to get a hint of gold or a glimpse of the spotlight. His constant controversial comments and actions have made him despised by both the fans and some of the fan-friendly wrestlers in the locker room. Hell, he is even hated by some of the 'heels' for the shit that he says. We've seen him join both the New Breed and Ultimate Authority for a bit of glory in PCW, to make a name for himself. As far as gold is concerned, one need only look at his history, which in some controversial cases need not involve any research. He won the PCW Rising Star Title thanks to interference from Big Daveylicious whilst trying to convince the PCW fans that he was their 'true hero.' Later on whilst trying to build himself up as a serious threat to PCW's big names, Corey accepted the PCW World Title from his friend Jason Snow, before giving it back to him, acting as a pawn in Snow's game just so he could claim something so many others couldn't; that he had won the PCW World Title. Dating all the way back to his Indy days we can see evidence of Corey sacrificing respect in his quest for gold and glory when he sacrificed the friendship and respect of his long time manager and the man who saved him from a life of crime, Don, in order to gain a victory over a seasoned Indy veteran and to win his fed's major belt. Corey's dedication to this business, or lack thereof, has often seemingly been considered a weakness by some critics as he can found partying more often than he can be found training. We have also seen him in many other ventures other than PCW, including his many connections with controversial celebrities outside of PCW as well as his many family problems and also the billionaire's business ventures which he seems to take out less for his love of money and more for his love to deprive people of their possessions. Of course, his party-hard 'I don't really give a damn about anyone or anything' attitude managed to get him pretty far during his New Breed days, but as Corey would say 'that was then and this is now'. Back then his goals were gold and glory, right now his goal is respect. ::Our scene opens up at an exclusive club in New York at 6:00 AM on June the 30th, the day of both Gold and Glory and the World Cup final. As our camera scrolls across the room we see various celebrities as well as the general public gathered all together to watch the match at the early time, none of which are Corey Williams. At the back of the room sits a huge TV at which the game will be displayed upon shortly. However, right now a famous advertisement that has been shown throughout the World Cup is being aired. On the huge screen we see a picture of a dark skinned man with the words 'Pele- Ambassador of Football':: Pele(on large screen TV): ...there was one thing we never talked about: Erection problems.... ::Our camera swings around to see Pele, IN THE FLESH, watching the ad with a Budweiser in one hand, an open metal briefcase full of money on the table and a big smile on the grin.:: 'Ambassador of Football my ass!' ::Pele turns around only to see Brian McBride, popular US International soccer player back from Korea/Japan and his team's impressive run there.:: Pele: What is your problem? McBride: You're my problem Pele, what have you ever done for football apart from use the name you got from it to make yourself money? Pele: I'm Pele, the greatest player to ever live and the Ambassador of Football! McBride: You WERE the greatest player that ever lived, but now you're nothing but a washed-up has-been that does nothing but cash-in on his name. You sold Football out! ::Pele looks at the angered face McBride with concern, until suddenly he breaks out into a grin.:: Pele: Well Mr..... I'm sorry, I'm too important to remember you're name, I'd love to stay here and debate with you but I've got a plane to catch. ::McBride looks at Pele with a mixture of disgust and disbelief.:: McBride: What!? You're not even gonna watch the damn game!? Pele: I'm afraid I don't have time, I'm NEEDED in Yokohama, Japan in just 90 minutes, luckily the US government are going to lend me a stealth jet to get there. After all, you can't have a World Cup final presentation without the Pele, and there will only ever be one Pele. McBride: There won't even be that many if I can help it. ::Suddenly Brian McBride charges at Pele and lunges on top of him, knocking him down. The two begin to fight and shortly after, a full-scale bar fight ensues. Again, Corey Williams is not among them. Scene fades out.:: ::Our next scene opens up at a stylish hotel room in Lexington, Kentucky, the site for PCW's Gold and Glory Pay Per View. It is empty.... Scene fades out.:: ::Our scene opens up at a not-so stylish gym in the outskirts of Lexington, Kentucky. There, in the wee hours of the morning as dawn approaches and a small fraction of the nation are watching the World Cup whilst the rest of the nation are enjoying a Sunday morning sleep in, we see Corey Williams, looking as little controversial as someone possibly can, in a furious workout routine..... Scene fades out.:: On Friday Fusion we saw both Jon Kano and Corey Williams have their dedication to this match questioned, in both cases each man stepped up to the challenge; that will be the case. Jon Kano has definitely proven his refusal to ever say die over the years, and now we see that Corey Williams too is ready to sacrifice gold and glory for respect. You may not agree with his motives, that being to end the career/reputation of one of PCW's long-time greats for personal gain, but no longer can you question that he will go into his match against Kano and give 100%. Both of these men will walk into the ring at Gold and Glory as equals, but only one will walk out as the victor, with a little respect under his belt. The other will be lucky to walk out at all. Yes, this most certainly will be a match for respect. But NEITHER of these two would have it any other way, for with respect on the line this is the only way to truly find out is the better man, who deserves the respect, acclaim and accolades. And even without Gold, even without Glory, perhaps there can be some beauty, passion and emotion found in this match. Most definitely not your general displays of beauty, passion and emotion, but if Clyde can find beauty, passion and emotion in amputation then most certainly we can find some here. Two men will walk into a ring and beat the shit of each other, it's enough to bring a tear to your eye. Makes you wonder why they call SOCCER the beautiful game. !The End!
