Monkey Spanking

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 20th [The scene opens on an upclose shot of a small monkey. The tiny thing holds its ape like fingers on the little cage that it's captured in. His brown, almost reddish, hair stands up in all directions with its dark, curious eyes. It's little tail whips around the cage area, liking something that he sees. The scene begins to zoom out showing many small cafes surrounding the one the monkey is in. It appears to be a very exotic, high priced pet store. Dogs are barking, cats are meowing, and the little fish are glubbing through the tropical fish tanks. At the entrance a very skinny, bald, and dorky looking guy stands behind the cashiers counter. The man wears a flannel like button down shirt and he wears very thick spectacles. The little monkey in the cage begins screeching out, obviously a bit hungry] Dorky guy behind counter: Shut up, monkey. I'll get the spatula out and spank you. [The screeching stops and the man picks up a newspaper from the countertop and begins going over the article he was reading earlier. The front door opens and two men walk through, and they don't seem to be a couple of guys who would come into an exotic pet store like this. The long, brown haired man with the bushy brown goatee, wears a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. He looks very stocky, not fat, but just very thick. The dorky pet shop owner thinks that this guy needs to learn that the eighties are over. The man following behind with a pearly white smile seems like he'll be a real winner. He has bright blonde hair and wears a PCW T-shirt with a pair of blue swim trunks that have yellow lining on the waist. This guy just seems like another dumb surfer who wants to buy a dog, which will starve to death in a matter of weeks. The dorky pet shop owner puts down his newspaper, rolls his eyes, and decides to help these gentlemen] Dorky pet shop owner: Can I help you gentlemen? 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Maybe in a little bit, dude. We are here strictly on business. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [PCW's newest tag team, The Cowabunga Connection, begins splitting off through the store to browse the merchandise. Harry goes over toward the insect section as Tommy begins browsing the larger cages with the cats and dogs. The dorky pet shop owner then leaves the counter area to keep an eye on these men] Dorky pet shop owner: What kind of business are you on gentlemen? [Tommy turns from tapping the cage of a collie] TTT: We have to do some research to get inside our opponents heads, compadre. These dudes are most complicated and we need to figure them out. [Harry turns from the preying mantis bin] Harry: Yeah, man. Dorky pet shop owner: I get this. I can tell by your PCW T-shirt that you guys are wrestlers. I've caught that show before, just didn't catch my attention enough. You guys are just looking for a place to do some promo to make yourselves more marketable in PCW. I get it now. [Harry and Tommy both turn to the dorky pet shop owner again and give him a confused look, honestly not knowing what the man is talking about. The pet shop owner looks at the facial expressions of both men and then comes to a realization] Dorky pet shop owner: OHHH!! You guys are 'in character'. Ok, I'll just do my best to go along with it. So who are these opponents? TTT: Samboro, Jameson and...uhh... Harry: Jarvis, man. TTT: Jameson and Jarvisman. Dorky pet shop owner: Jarvisman? What kind of wrestlers name is that? TTT: I don't know, dude. Take that up with Samboro. I think it's pretty bogus too. [Tommy comes across a bulldog lying down with a very depressed look on its face, just like most bulldogs have. The dog is white and around it's jaws it has a grayish tint. Tommy taps against the glass trying to get the gnarly dogs attention] Harry: I'm trying to say Jarvis, man. Dorky pet shop owner: Yeah, we get it. Jarvisman. Harry: No, man. Jarvis....MAN! Dorky pet shop owner: I get the picture, guy. Jameson's partner is named Jarvisman. Is he some kind of superhero? [Harry sighs loudly to himself and then walks further into the insect section of the pet shop. The dorky pet shop owner sees the tiny brown haired monkey trying to escape from his cage. He smiles wickedly to himself and pulls out the spatula from his pocket. He stalks slowly toward the cage, ready to catch the monkey off guard. He pulls back slowly, smiling wider...] Harry: OH MY GOD, MAN! [Harry's screech interrupts everything going on in the pet shop. The dorky pet shop owner lowers the spatula, puts it in his back pocket and turns to see what is the matter. Tommy looks annoyed as he was having fun playing with the dog. The two men walk over and see Harry standing perfectly still with his arm extended. His face is completely drained of blood and his usual bloodshot eyes are drained white] TTT: Stop being heinous, dude. [The dorky pet shop owner notices that the top of the cage has been taken off where Harry stands. The owner's face now begins to drain white as he goes to pull at his hair but finds nothing at the top of his head except skin] Dorky pet shop owner: That's the Tarantula tank, fool!! [Tommy quickly leaps on a chair next to him, looking all around] TTT: Tarantula's are bogus, dude! [Harry slowly turns to show a large, black and yellow taratula crawling up his arm] Harry: Get it off, man. [The dorky pet shop owner begins to take out his spatula, but it has gone amiss! He goes into his slacks pockets frantically but the spatula cannot be found. The owner has no choice but to go back to the days when he started at the pet shop, to just handle the creepiest of all creatures with his bare hands] Dorky pet shop owner: Just stay still. Harry: Ok, man. [The dorky owner, with lightning quick speed, yanks the large arachnid off of Harry's arm and throws it back in the tank. He then slams the lid on tight, making sure that nothing can open it. Harry falls over against the wall and breathes heavily as the blood begins returning to his face. Tommy still stands atop the chair and laughs at Harry] TTT: Your such a wuss, compadre. You were afraid of that most bogus spider. Harry: You were too, man. [Tommy steps down from the chair] TTT: Was not, dude. [The dorky owner lets out a sigh and the smiles] Dorky pet shop owner: Another good deed done, guys. [Tommy then goes about wandering through the pet shop, spotting something very odd. As Tommy looks on in confusion the dorky pet shop owner begins his questioning] Dorky pet shop owner: I had a spatula in my back pocket, do any of you know where it went? Harry: No, man. [Tommy just stares on at something, trying to clear his vision to make sure he's seeing things correctly. Yeah, he's seeing things just fine...It's a little monkey holding a spatula. Tommy then turns to the dorky guy] TTT: Dude, why is that most gnarly monkey holding a spatula? [The pet shop owner runs over to the little cage holding the monkey, trying to reach in and grab the spatula. The monkey won't have any of it as he smacks the hands of the owner, making him retreat his hands back to the outside of the cage] Dorky pet shop owner: Give me back the spatula, monkey! [Harry has seemingly forgot about his encounter with the tarantula and stands next to Tommy, wondering exactly what is going on] Harry: That monkey is pissed, man. TTT: Totally, dude. Isn't it bodacious that we won our most excellent debut? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Rudolph von Dookieman and Mason Crapper stood no chance with The Tubular One and the Heavy Dude. That is just one righteous victory closer to our most triumphant goal, The PCW Tag Team Championships. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: This Friday we have the most animalistic dudes of Samboro and then on Sunday we have those most dorky dudes Misplaced. [Harry scratches the side of his head as the dorky owner still tries to get his spatula] Harry: What did we misplace, man? TTT: Nothing. We are wrestling them on Sunday night. It will be a most bodacious affair, one which we'll win, of course. Harry: But how did we misplace a wrestling match? TTT: The match isn't misplaced, but the team we're facing is. Harry: So, the tag team is misplaced, man? TTT: Yeah, exactly. Harry: So, that means an automatic victory, man. TTT: Totally! [The two men then look over to the owner, who's face is beat red with anger and frustration. He points his finger at the monkey, who then smacks it with the spatula. The dorky man then jumps up and down in a little hissy fit] Dorky pet shop owner: That's it! Your going down, monkey. You've made a fool out of me for the last time! [The owner trudges throw the corridor back toward his counter near the front entrance. He goes out of sight, obviously to get a weapon much more vicious than the spatula. Maybe this owner is going to kill the monkey, that would be mighty bogus to do. This monkey just wanted to play with a spatula and this X-Files dork had to get all bent out of shape. Talk about being a heinous dude!] TTT: Are you thinking the same thing that I'm thinking, compadre? [Harry's eyes light up in complete happiness] Harry: Go over Jessica Alba's house, spark a bowl, and play Yahtzee, man? TTT: No, dude...but we'll do that after we do what I'm thinking. [Tommy walks over to the little cage of the monkey] Monkey: Ooo ah? TTT: It's all right bodacious monkey dude, I'm here to free you. [Harry begins laughing as Tommy begins to unlock the cage] Harry: Far out, man. [Tommy finishes unlatching the cage and the monkey dives to the floor, spatula in hand, and goes toward the counter. The dorky owner emerges with a frying pan in his hand now. The man's eyes say it all. He's enraged and tired of this little monkey always driving customers crazy. In fact, he's going to go out of business because of this cursed monkey. Now this little swine is finally going to go down] Dorky pet shop owner: Ok, monkey...YOUR GOING TO DIE!!! [The pet shop owner confronts the cage and sees that it's opened. He immediately looks over to the only other humans in the room, Harry and Tommy] Dorky pet shop owner: Who let him out? [The two men shrug as if not knowing how a little monkey unlocked its own cage] Dorky pet shop owner: That was a very wrong thing to do, boys. Your going down for this! [Just as he finishes the sentence a spatula cracks the man right into his crotch. The dorky pet shop owner drops to the floor in immense pain. The monkey celebrates as if it had just won the PCW Heavyweight Title] Monkey: OOOOOO AHHH!!! OOOO AHHH!!! EEEEK!! [The monkey then begins slamming the spatula down into the man's crotch area repeatedly, hitting harder and faster with each lash] TTT: TOTALLY TUBULAR, DUDE!! HE'S SPANKING HIS MONKEY!!! Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!! [Tommy then loses the smile on his face and shows a look of pain] TTT: Should we stop this, dude? I mean it's not right to leave a man being assaulted by a monkey, especially in his most gnarly area of the body. Well, gnarly to him. Harry: No, man. [Tommy then smiles and keeps watching. The monkey seems to be a little worn out from all the beating. It drops the spatula and seems to have let out all those years of aggression in just a small amount of seconds. The pet shop owner lies on the floor crying like a little baby] TTT: It looks like that righteous monkey is done, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [The monkey then opens its mouth wide and bites right into the pet shop owners...] TTT: TOTALLY BOGUS!!! HE BIT RIGHT INTO HIS... Harry: Sausage, man. [Harry isn't paying attention and notices the sandwich on the desk. He ends up interrupting The Tubular One. Tommy then grabs Harry by the arm and drags him toward the front door] TTT: Lets get out of here, dude. Harry: Ok, man. [The two men exit the store. They go out onto the sun filled evening sky in Los Angeles. They jump into Tommy's red Mustang convertible and take off down the empty street. Tommy looks to Harry with a smile] TTT: We are going to bodaciously take out Samboro on Friday, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. Jameson will go down...and especially Jarvis, man. [Tommy looks at Harry confused] TTT: Jarvisman? I thought his name was Nervil, dude? Harry: No, man. Jarvis, man. TTT: Is Jarvisman some kind of gnarly super hero? He just seems African to me. Harry: NO, MAN!! JARVIS........MAN!! [Tommy rolls his sparkling blue eyes, showing the obvious] TTT: DUH! I know its Jarvisman, but I don't get why he's called that. Harry: ARGH, man! [The scene then fades to black]
