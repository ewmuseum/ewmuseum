Lost Chapter #2

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28-4-03 Mark wakes up in a bed that he has sank down into. His vision is blurred when he wakes up as the bright light is coming in through the partly opened curtains that are over the window to the bedroom. The white sheets are reflecting the sun to Mark’s eyes, but are not helping him to see any better. Mark rubs his eyes as he tries to get the sediments out of his eyes from the night of sleeping. Mark is trying to think but all he can think about is the pain that his head is going through right now as he is all bandaged up. “Where in the hell am I at?” Mark screams in a moderately tone voice. “If I am in a coon’s house get me the fuck out of here right now!” Mark moves around but realizes that his leg is in severe pain. He tries to lift his head but realizes that hurts even when he lifts it up as he touches his forehead and finds that that is all bandaged up with white tape. His jaw has a newly fresh piece of tape around it to hold his jaw from the surgery. Mark rolls over in his bed as he is trying to reach the edge so that he can finally get his legs up to walk. “Hello sunshine.” A woman’s soft voice sounds as she walks in the door of the room. Mark turns his head as his whole body is on the edge of the bed. His bright blue eyes get big as he sees who this lady is. He is not concentrating on his body as he falls off the edge of the bed with a loud thud as he lands on the hard wooden floor. The lady puts down her silver platter tray on the dresser as she goes to attend to Mark. But as Mark can see this lady is old and isn’t going to be able to help him out much as he is trying to get up in on his own. The lady gets over there as Mark sets himself up against the bed with his legs stretched out to the wall. “Markus what happened there?” The old lady says as she sits on the bed right next to Mark’s head.“Grandma what in the hell am I doing here?” He asks as he looks out the widow, but only sees blue sky outside. “The police reported that you were left on the street so they brought you back here for the night.” Grandma says as she is looking down on Mark and sees the scar across his eye. “I was fuckin knocked out by some coons Grandma, I couldn’t help that I was left out to dry on the streets.” Mark says as he starts to put his legs under himself to get himself up. “Well its good to see you again boy.” Grandma says as she gets up off of the bed and goes over to the dresser. “I brought you up some lunch so that you could eat if you are hungry, but now I will leave you alone since you look like you need your rest.” Mark puts his feet under him and gets all the way standing up, but finds out that his right leg is in no shape of doing any physical work. He looks at his clothing and his wife beater was ripped off as he found a piece of cloth in his belt of his black pants. Mark starts to walk over to the wall where a mirror is hanging up. He goes over slowly like a turtle in a desert as his leg is really bothering him. Mark finally gets over to the mirror, but he has to put his hands on the edge of the lower dresser to hold himself up as he gets close up in the mirror. He notices that he has a little scrape over his right eye and feels like he just got in a car wreck. “What in the hell got in those coons last night that attacked me?” Mark asks to himself as he found he did nothing wrong. “This proves to how dumb they are. All they can do is hit and run from people. Just the same way they do to their own people with the drive bys and shit. But now I can feel that payback is in the picture when I heal.” Mark steps away from the mirror and he goes over by the wooden door. As he steps on the wooden floor it creaks in a lot of spots from the old wood in the house. The white paint on the walls is coming off and you can see some gray splotches in some spots of the walls. Mark picks up his black leather boots that go everywhere with him to fit the Neo Nazi look. Mark sits down on the bed with the white sheets still messed up. He puts on his boots but doesn’t bother lacing them up as usual. He looks at the fried chicken on the silver platter that his grandma fixed. Along with that there is some mashed potatoes with some brown seasoned gravy over the top of the white fluffed mountains. “If you didn’t know my grandma you would’ve thought she was a coon herself.” Mark says as he eyes the food and the he raises his fist and smashes that through the potatoes. “I swear the whole USA is turning into a big black community.” Mark opens up the white wooden door and as he opens it, it creaks as he steps foot outside of the room. Mark heads down the lonely hallway with only pictures of his mom on the wall and that is all. Mark looks at his mom’s pictures as he continues down the hall to the stairs. “My things have changed since those pictures were last taken.” Mark says aloud, but no one can hear him as he passes by her high school pictures, wedding pictures, and then pictures with him. “Those were the happy times in both of our lives, but some shit in her life has fucked things up for the both of us and I am happy she chose that life with that coon so I don’t have to live with her shit anymore.” Mark is not caught up in heat at all as he makes it to the stairs safe without punching out a picture like he always does when he comes to grandmas. Mark grabs onto the spiral wooden railing going down the stairs with some wooden decorative on it. He steps down on the green carpeted as the stairs under him creak with old age in them as he goes down one by one to the bottom of the stairs. “Well look who decided to wake up.” Grandpa says as he is sitting down next to the stairs on the black leather recliner that he got for last Christmas from his wife. “I guess, but for now I am going out of here.” Mark says as he goes by his grandpa and heads to the wooden front door that was stained just a couple of days ago so it doesn’t show its age like the rest of the house is. “Markus don’t do anything that you will regret.” Grandpa says as Mark passes him and as he looks up from his paper to see how bad Mark looks. “Violence won’t get you anywhere in life Markus!” Mark doesn’t bother listening to his grandpa’s words as he opens up the stained door and he exits the aged house of his grandparents. Mark heads down the gray cement stairs, but not as fast as he would like to go since his leg is keeping him from doing that. Mark gets down the stairs as thoughts of last night is going through his head about the coons that attacked him. As he looks up from looking down on the cement Mark can see blacks fill up the streets. Mark’s face turns to a scold as he looks at every black he can to get them to feel the effect that he is there. “Where did Markus go?” Grandma asks as she comes out of the kitchen after she heard the door close. “He went off to the real world and its time that he finds out how rough it can be from the lifestyle he has chose.” Grandpa says as he is reading the paper still and is not looking too happy. Grandma in her apron, goes up the stairs and she finds that nothing has been broke in the hallway and that everything was as perfect as she had left it before Mark left. Grandma looks at the white wooden door and is afraid to go in to see what she might find. Grandma goes up to the door and she holds onto the black door knob that was put in about the time that the house was built. She turns the door handle as the wind sucks the door in without her having to push on it. Then she notices the mess that Mark left with the food and she screams as grandpa gets a scared look on his face with the shock of grandma’s scream as Mark is still out on the streets looking for the coons that attackted him.
