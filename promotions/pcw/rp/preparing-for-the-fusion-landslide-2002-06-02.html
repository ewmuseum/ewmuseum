Preparing for the Fusion Landslide!

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Preparing for the Fusion Landslide! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: June 2 Partial success. On Friday Fusion, Politically Incorrect achieved the epitome of this definition. They successfully invaded the PCW Rising Star Title Match, destroying Heaven Rayne and putting the world on notice that The Political Machine had arrived. Success seemed to be emminent for Reginald Lovecraft as well. After fighting through a school of opponents, Lovecraft planted Xavier Kannon with Furman v. Georgia, eliminating the cocky Scientologist. But alas, despite the best efforts of Mr. Lovecraft, it was a resurgent Xavier Kannon and his infamous X-Bomb that took Reginald out of the match, giving the win to PCW legend Miyagawa. Needless to say, Reginald was not pleased. And, a few hours of choice words later to Duke Williams, PCW President, netted The Governor the opportunity he wanted. Lovecraft. Miyagawa. Extreme Title. NO INTERFERENCE. Lovecraft is confident. Miyagawa may be a legend, but Lovecraft has shown that he is worthy of hanging with PCW’s best. William S. Powell gets the chance to prove that shortly enough. In Fusion’s main event, Powell takes on Tyler Nelson, The Phantom Booker, and Duke Williams. All three men are nigh-legendary in PCW lore. Think that William Powell cares? Of course not. The brash young politician is ready to battle (and defeat) the longtime standard bearers. It will be a momentous occasion indeed for Politically Incorrect. :The Politically Incorrect limousine pulls out of Teaneck, New Jersey with a combination of elation and depression. The Political Machine has been constituted, successfully, but Lovecraft is still rather steamed. Powell, however, is the picture of elation, as he is ready for his main event contest.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: THAT SON OF A BITCH SCIENTOLOGIST KANNON! I AM GOING TO MURDER THAT BASTARD!! ::Campaign Manager Schultz steps in, to try and calm down the massive politician.:: Roger Schultz: :Calm down, Mr. Lovecraft! Screaming will do you no good! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah, well, all I know is that he and his soon to be wife are going to be in the same ring at the same time Tuesday, facing Jaden. If Kannon tries to fuck Jaden over, I swear, Eleanor Hall will be marrying a parapalegic! Roger Schultz: At the very least, you got your Extreme Title match with Miyagawa next week…no interference. Reginald V, Lovecraft: Absolutely right, Mr. Schultz! It took some doing, but I convinced the Hoss of the Malice Crew, Duke Williams, to grant me that contest. I do not hold Miyagawa responsible for what happened. He’s a competitor. He took advantage of what had happened…if he was the one being X-Bombed on the limo, I would have done the exact same thing that he did. ::Lovecraft has an intent look in his eyes….his competitive juices jumping at the bit for next week.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Miyagawa…it is a matter of pride, now. I do not hate you, for I have no reason to. It is only with desires for power and glory that I come after you on Friday Fusion. Two days ago…the gold was mine..only to be taken from me by a vengeful man with a limousine, and a Death Valley Bomb onto the cold hood of a limousine. But, on Fusion….no Therapy can save you, Alexander. You must go toe to toe with the largest member of The Political Machine. You must look back into the eyes of the man who you know in your heart had that title in his posession before Xavier Kannon entered the picture. We both hate Xavier Kannon..but for now, that hatred shall be placed aside, so that we may duel as do true Extreme competitors. ::William S. Powell chooses now to speak up. Glancing at Louis Brown and Katherine Willis, The Master Debator grins, a confident smile.:: William S. Powell: And, as my partner defeats Miyagawa for the PCW Extreme Championship, I shall be preparing for my first main event without my partner at my side. It is a fatal fourway matchup, featuring the leaders of each stable in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Tyler Nelson of the Ultimate Authority. Duke Williams of the Malice Crew. The Phantom Booker of the Phantom Phive. And, most importantly, William S. Powell of The Political Machine! ::At this, Reginald’s ears perk up, leading to him speaking.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I thought we were co-leaders! William S. Powell: We are! But, you’re taking on Miyajobber in the Extreme Title match earlier, so ONE of us has to be in it, so it’s me by default. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ahhh…okay, okay.. William S. Powell: Anyway….its quite the interesting situation I have here. I face Duke Williams, probably one of my least favorite people in the world, if not for his giving of the deserved rematch to Lovecraft. Country music playing, tobacco chewing rednecks are not usually people that I associate with, or even share opinions with. But for once, on this rare occasion, I agree with “The Malice Man”. Tyler Nelson is a complete and utter piece of shit. ::Powell continues, offering the above statement in a bland, matter of fact tone.:: William S. Powell: However, this one similarity between the two of us does not mean that I believe Duke Williams to be a good person. I hate Duke Williams, and his pathetic Malice Crew, consisting of Jon Kano, Rush Mason, and, and..I don’t even know who the hell is in the goddamn thing any more since Legend Inc. left. Duke Williams would be the black sheep in this stable war, if not for the fact that he were the President of PCW. But, rest assured, that your office will not save you. Going Whiskey Bent and Hellbound will not save you. The Master Debator will demonstrate why, unlike my partner, some superstars lessen with age. ::The Debator laughs blandly, a dry, caringless laugh.:: William S. Powell: And then, we have The Phantom Booker..a man who is not exactly at home in a Primetime Championship Wrestling ring. If the Booker wishes to test his skill against actual wrestlers, he does so at his own peril. Hell, I bet even Duke Williams could beat the Phantom Phive’s leader at this point in time. Booker’s somewhat known for his finishing move, what he calls the TPB-DDT. Geez, with that, the DVD-RDVD, and other such moves, maybe I should rename Election Day the TPM-ED….nah. ::Lovecraft chuckles despite himself, as he snuggles up with Katherine Willis. Powell continues.:: William S. Powell: And then, once the Booker is humbled and humiliated, and Duke Williams has choked on his tabaccy spit, is Tyler Nelson. The greediest son of a bitch I have ever seen in my life. I’m almost glad Tim Walden sold his shares to the Phantom Booker…because, it means that you do not have them. Tyler, you can pick up The Chef, Corey Williams, Goeren, and some guy from Uzbekistan….but they are nowhere near Mark Makonie, Politically Incorrect, and Tim Walden, despite what Dave Kern said.Nelson’s time nears its end…as when he steps in the ring with The Master Debator..he will discover that it is indeed Election Day! And on Election Day, William S. Powell always triumphs! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And on Fusion, The Political Machine will claim the first of its many victories…IN A LANDSLIDE!! ::Fade to:: Paid for by the Friends of The Political Machine“We own you all.” ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
