Welcome to GIMMICKS r US! How may I help you?

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/11/2002 *The scene opens on a sunny, bright day. Besides the brightness of the sun, we are graced with visions of a busy street, as cars and pedestrians alike zoom by our camera. On the sidewalk, stands one monster of a man. A former World Champion, who plans on reuniting himself with that 40 pounds of gold that was stolen from him by a sly Miyagawa. Boda stands amidst the crowd of pedestrians, hurriedly trying to get to their appointed places at the right time. Boda starts to pace, and every so often stops to look up, then shakes his head before pacing again. Boda looks up again, and shakes his head no, before walking away.* Boda: “No, I’m not doing it. I won’t go in there, and I won’t buy into the propaganda.” *As the man that will make Miya regret the rematch coming up walks off, our camera man puts the camera down, and runs up to the Big Bad Boda Daddy. He stops Chris Sloboda from walking off.* Cameraman: “Come on man..... we need to do this. I thought you were the Ameri-Czech Bad Ass. Why would you try to run away?” *Boda turns to face the cameraman.* Boda: “Mike.....” Cameraman: “The name is Steve.” Boda: “Interrupt me again, and it becomes Dead Punk Camera Man.” Cameraman: “Point taken.” Boda: “So like I was saying earlier......Mike.....I am the Ameri-Czech bad ass. I am the Big Bad Boda Daddy. I’m not running away. I just don’t see the need to go on with this. I mean, look at me. I got to the top of PCW just fine. So what if some backstage banter is being spread about me. This is a bad idea, and I could think of five other things I’d rather be doing. Like getting ready to go one on one against Miya for a rematch for my title he happens to be wearing. Or, I know, instead of this, I’d rather be chasing Chris Chase down, and beating him till he finally realizes that I am that much better. Or, I could be sitting on a plane back to Orlando. But no..... not this time around. I’ve got to go torture myself I’ve got to go in there, and do the one thing I never thought I’d have to do.” Steve [the cameraman]: “Come on Boda. Besides.... it’s for a good cause.” Boda: “A good cause? What the hell kind of good could possibly come from this?” Steve [the cameraman]: “I don’t know, maybe like Corey Williams will stop ragging you on the boards, and in his promos.” *Boda looks at Steve, and rolls his eyes* Boda: “Mike.... look at me. I’m 6’11, 350 pounds of muscle, talent, and lately, a lot of pent up anger. Do you think I give one god damn iota about what Corey Williams thinks? No. I could care less what that New inBreed mutha thinks. Hell, if we all listened to and believed in what Corey thought, then Snow would be the Supreme Ruler of The World and Chris Chase wouldn't suck ass. So, give me one more reason to do this.” *Steve the cameraman ponders the reasons, then as if a light bulb went off, he smiles at Boda, who in turn steps back, wondering if Steve is come ci, come ca.* Steve [the cameraman]: “Well, no one else has ever done this, to my knowledge.” Boda: “Really? You mean I’d be the first.” Steve [the cameraman]: “Yeah, and then The WWF could rip you off.” *Boda looks at his watch* Boda: “Sure, beside, it might be fun. And I got nothing else to do, short of winning back the World Title later. Let’s go.” *Steve smiles, and walks to his camera, and lifts it up, slinging it over his right shoulder. Chris walks to a door, and opens it. The two head inside. The inside is a store, with various people shopping and browsing* Boda: “This isn’t too bad. I’m a take a look around.” *Boda goes off, walking around and looking at the merchandise on the shelves inside the store. It’s your average run of the mill store, with aisles, and up front , cash registers. Cheesy store music plays over head. Boda picks up an item, and lays it back down. Meanwhile, a flash passes the camera, quick as lighting. Boda moves to another aisle, and stares at all the merchandise.* Boda: “Man, I don’t see anything here that would benefit me. Look at all this old shit.” *Boda picks up a box, and suddenly gets this eerie feeling like he is being watched. The flash passes the camera again.* Boda: “Mike... there’s some one behind me right?” *Both Boda and the cameraman turn and sure as Boda’s going to regain the world title at Turmoil, there is indeed some one watching. And staring, and smiling. The white of his teeth is almost blinding under the fluorescent lighting. Boda shakes his head and rolls his eyes.* Boda: “I hate salesmen.” *As Boda puts the box down, and tries to walk away, the man behind him suddenly, in a flash, appears in front of the man that will kick Chris Chase’s ever loving ass next time he decides to hit Boda with a steel chair. Boda sighs heavily, as he looks down at the salesman, who is thrusting his hand out for a handshake. Chris reads the name tag on the man’s left breast pocket. Bill Schilling, Store Manager.* Bill Schilling: “Hello, and Welcome to GIMMICKS r US.” Boda: “Hi.” Bill Schilling: “You know.....we’ve been expecting you for some time now.” Boda: “Really? And why is that?” Bill Schilling: “Well, because you my friend need a gimmick. It’s the talk of the wrestling world. Boda.... the man with out a gimmick. That makes for a bad career.” *Boda is taken aback by the shrewdness of the store manager.* Boda: “Bad career? Have you been talking to Corey Williams? Listen, I’m a former World Champion, two time tag champion, former Continental Champion. Come on.... what more could a gimmick actually do for me? How would a gimmick help me, or benefit me in winning back the title at Turmoil against Miya? What should I do, become a lumber jack, and thus force the wrestlers on the outside to not attack me? Come on Bill..... I’ll buy a gimmick if you convince me of first..... one that would work for me. Two.... what benefit a gimmick will give me. I mean, come on..... I have gotten by on my talent and my strength. I don’t think I need to be a truck driver, or a male nurse.” *Bill picks up a box, and holds it in his hand. He pulls Boda aside* Bill Schilling: “Listen, I’m not arguing the point that you are not indeed a great wrestler. But, what you lack is heat. You lack that little something that separates you from everyone else. Like take this gimmick here.” *Bill hands the box to Boda.* Bill Schilling: “Now, this is a gimmick that has never been played very well.” *Boda reads the box. It reads HOMOSEXUAL SPANIARD. Upon reading the box, Boda chucks it to the side, and it hits Boca Del Inferno in the head. Boca looks down, and squeals with delight. Boca picks up the package, and heads for the cashiers. Chris looks at Bill, and shakes his head* Boda: “Do I look like I need to play a homosexual? Come on Bill. I’m a monster. Get me something to work with. No stupid shit. I’ve got a world title to win back.” Bill Schilling: “Okay, well, let me see.” *Bill pulls a catalog off a shelf. He skims it, reading off a few possible gimmicks for Boda* Bill Schilling: “Okay..... let’s see here.... Pizza Delivery Boy....” Boda: “Jonathan Drachen” Bill Schilling: “.....Scientologist......” Boda: “Xavier Kannon” Bill Schilling: “.......Gothic Warrior.....” Boda: “God, where do I start? Darkstar, Hessian.... hell, any of the devil worshipping freaks in the world, are Gothic Warriors. Keep going.” *Bill reads from the catalog. Every possible gimmick, is met with a wrestler’s name by Boda. Bill starts to get frustrated, as if he is not doing his job well. Finally Bill reads one last possible gimmick from the catalog.* Bill Schilling: “.... how bout becoming an over cocky, wrestler, who really sucks. Other than that, I’m stuck.” Boda: “Well, Bill.... I don’t know if it’s their real gimmicks.... but that sounds like Corey Williams and Chris Chase. God knows we don’t need more of them. Come on.... this store is full of gimmicks, and you can’t find an unique one for me?” Bill Schilling: “I’m stuck as to what I can outfit you with. First time I’ve had this problem.” *Boda nods at Bill, and comforts him.* Boda: “Tell you what, I’m going to go win that World Title on Tuesday. I’m going to do it with out a gimmick. Because, when you’re as big as me. When you’re as talented as me. You don’t need a false front to get the fans to either boo or cheer for ya. It’s worked for me for 18 months, and you know what they say Bill. If it ain’t broke.... don’t fix it.” *Bill nods, with a look of rejection on his face.* Bill Schilling: “Sorry. I guess you really don’t need a gimmick after all. You do seem to have all the tools to do what you do best. But, before you leave.... would you mind going on our mailing list? Our new 2003 models will be coming out soon. Maybe there will be something new for you to use then.” *Boda shakes Bill’s hand* Boda: “Sure Bill. And thanks. You’ve been a lot of help. But, I’m a stick with what I know best. Playing myself.” *And with that, Boda leaves GIMMICKS r US, and ends his little excursion to try to find a gimmick. The moral of this story..... No gimmicks necessary, when you’re as good as Boda. The scene fades out as Boda and Steve the cameraman stroll down the street*
