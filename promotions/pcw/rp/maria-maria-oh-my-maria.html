'Maria, Maria.....Oh, My Maria!'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.11.02 (# 3 TT, #1 SA) The year is 2024. A grizzled and tired old Abner flops down in his chair. It is made of leather: black, comfy and has been around…..just like him. Abner’s hair is gray, and his bones are chilled. Due to nearly twenty years of stiff PCW competition, “The Inebriator” was finally forced into calling it quits. Retiring completely from all PCW positions early last fall, Abner had now had over a year to relax. But no rest for the wicked. For you see, his mind, still young and adventurous, dreams of the “Golden Age”. Longing for the days of old, the old man is taken back….. Ah! The good ol’ days. Abner lets out a sigh. The former PCW champion, battered but unbowed, wishes that he could still take it. But he can. He can certainly still manages to dish it out, but in order to take the relentless onslaught of the new generation of PCWers, “The Inebriatror” would simply have to remain drunk all the time! And here you me, it’s not as if the thought hasn’t crossed his mind once or twice. But it’s not so. It is simply not meant to be. Those days are over….. So slipping down into the chair that his granddaughter bought for him last Christmas, he closes his eyes and begins to drift away…… Slowly, Abner embraced the thoughts, songs, and the spirit of yesteryear….. And then, suddenly, his fragile thoughts are interrupted. Shaken but not stirred, reluctantly, Abner opens his eyes. It’s the phone. How rude?. As a soft buzz echoes throughout the retired wrestler’s chamber, he’s forced to confront the beast. Reaching for a red button in front of him, he presses it and a small, rectangular-shaped, object comes into view. Rising up vertically from the desk in front of him, this futuristic piece of technology still amazes old Abner, still “stuck in 90’s” you know. And from this translucent panel, a message emerges: LONG-RANGE COMM. PRIORITY: ONE. “Goddamn Martian telemarketers!” Abner grumbles under his breath. But with nothing better to do…..well, except for maybe play a little virtual golf, Abner reluctantly decides to take the call. And letting out a sigh, he reaches up and roughly taps the screen. “Humor me” he proclaims as the image comes into view. “Hello?” Abner asks. And what he sees causes the old man to gasp. Forcing himself to breath again, Abner can’t help but notice the throbbing pain of his intensely beating heart. “Hello, Abner?” a soft, warm voice with a gentle Latino accent replies. Abner is at loss for words. And the, the memories of that night….. That night so long ago….. Monday, December second, 2002?..... The night before Turmoil. ---------------------------------------- …..And yet, the night it still young. So holding his lover in his tight grip, Abner gracefully drags the intoxicated prostitute across the parking lot. In gentlemen like fashion, he throws open the backdoor and stuffs the package into the limo. Hopping in back, and lying comforting hand across his romantic acquaintance, he calls out to the driver. “Go!?” And as the first of the drunken bandits…...and the pimp’s men….reach the car, the driver puts the PCW-owned machine into gear, and there off into then night. Abner lets out a sigh of relief. “Are you okay?” Abner looks over at his lover with a remarkable sense of concern. “Uh…..I…...I, uh, I…...think so?” She replies, her voice tainted with a soft Hispanic accent. “ I hope so” Abner says to himself, his emotion slipping through. And so the hick, as gently as he can, takes his lover’s hand as she drifts back out of consciousness. And then the horrors of the incident flashes through his mind. [Memory] You see, earlier on tonight, Abner had gotten himself into a bit of trouble. Maria, an extremely attractive young Mexiacan women, somehow found her way into his redneck arms. And holding tight, Abner wasn’t planning on letting go of her. This beautiful prostitute, for time being ,was his life. And she had come to him the night before: battered, bruised and afraid. And he simply could not resist her. Begging for help, Abner had “no choice” but to invite her into bed with him….. And now this mistake would catch up with him. You see, after a wonderful day of love, sex, booze and……well that’s about it, Abner decided to take the lady out for a drink. And being the “gentlemen” that he was, Abner decided to take his love out to the classiest place in town: the nearest bar in sight. And that’s where the trouble began. Her luscious long, dark hair….deep, brown eyes…...bulging breasts…..MINE! This was Abner’s feelings, and for a short time, it was a safe thought…..but Abner didn’t have her all to himself….. The catch was, Maria was not alone. The bar was filled with other factions you see….. Finally, after weeks of searching, they had found her. But who? You see, no, not only was Abner there, but so was her old boyfriend…..and so was her pimp. Santiago, her evil Mexican pimp, was chasing her throughout the city in order to receive a payment for an old debt. But unfortunately for him, Dagario, Maria’s ex, had other plans of his own….. And so did Maria herself. And these, in combination, led to disaster. Maria, afraid of the pimp and disgusted with her boyfriend, sought the bar as a refuge. But there was no rest for the wicked. For not long after she had gotten there, Santiago, followed by his horde of pimpish imps, and Dagario, followed by his gang of “bitch stealing” thieves (in Santiago’s words), made there presence known as well. And with both groups moving in on her, Maria had nowhere to turn…..but to Abner. So comforting herself in the lap of this drunken pro wrestler, she was safe. But not for long. You see, Abner himself was not one to keep quiet. Drinking and partying since the early hours of the evening, this wasted PCWer was in no position to keep a secret. Just after finding out about his tag match at Adrenaline, with too much already on his mind (i.e. a three way on Turmoil verses Token and Romantico), Abner…...well, he just needed to relieve his pressured soul. And so he turned to his bottle, and consequently, all hell broke lose…… “Over here, over here!” The entire bars gets quiet, turning to face Abner, Now in control of the crowd, be begins to make his proclamation. “Lookie ‘ere peoples, I got mahself a good ol’ Mexican ‘ore! And she’s a good un too, uh?! Yep, pretty near perfect if I do say so mahself…..” “Shh, Be quiet!.....” the prostitute warns him. But it’s already too late. “Maria!” cries out a tall Spaniard. Dressed in pure black leather, jumping from side to side of the bar, this man seems surprisingly……motivated. “Dagario!?......No!” and quickly turning to Abner, “Help me senior Abner, help me!” SoAbner obediently gets to his feet. The intoxicated hick is in no position to fight……or is it that he’s at his best? Well, as the night went on, the latter would be proven false. So tipping over his tool, vomit running down the front of his plad hunting shirt, the big Vermont redneck sternely questions his potential foe. “And who might ye be? ‘Cause if you be lookin’ to take my lady Maria…..well I reckon that you’d be better off?.....And he gets cut short. A swift blow from the Spaniards fists sends him reeling. “Out of my way, stupid, drunken American!” “No, no, no!” Maria shrieks in half-concern and half-self pity as the Spaniard roughly grabs at her. Getting a hold of one of the strips of white on her gown, Dagario nastily rips at this poor women’s clothing. “You best let go of me!” And taking a step forward, catching him unaware, Maria slaps the stunned Spaniard across the face. “Bitch!” And before he can retaliate, quickly, two of his goons seize the struggling women. And regaining his control of his temper, Dagario begins to speak. “Ah yes, Maria….and who have we…..” losing his voice “....here?.....” “Stop!” In a tainted Spanish accent “Son-of-a-whore!” Dagario cries out in light of a new development. For standing in front of him, mere feet away, stands the pimp and his gang. Abner, recovering from the blow, quickly gets to his feet. Uh-oh….. “Ah, senior Santiago…..” Degario politely replies. “Senior Degario…..” The pimp nods his head. The Spaniard, backing up against the bar and grabbing an empty bottle, “So…..uh…..is there anything I can do for you?” He tightens his grip around the potential weapon. Staring into Dagario’s eyes, “Certainly senior Dagario, certainly……” He takes a confident step forward, backed by a handful of tough looking guards. But Santiago’s men, though outnumbered, are also very tough in their own rite. And so taking their positions, they prepare for the worse. Santiago, grinning up at his opponent, decisively asks ‘And just what would that be?” And then all hell broke lose. “Mexican bar fight!” a random man cries out. But Abner, already to his feet, sweeps up Maria in his arms, and somehow, by the grace of god, through all the confusion, vomit and bloodshed, miraculously manages to make an escape. And so they’re off into the night…… [Present] And so we’re brought back to the harsh reality of the present situation. And the circumstances are indeed bad for our hick. Being chased through the streets of Mexico by two warring parties of Mexican hoodlums, the PCW newcomer is not quite where he wants to be. With a match against two tough opponents the following night, and an eight man tag match less than a week away, Abner should be training hard…..not DRINKING hard. But anyway, that’s the way it is with him……(nods) “Step on it!” Abner commands the PCW driver, and drives him into action. And so the long, black company limousine, swerving around a corner, barely manages to remain undamaged. Taking off chunks of the nearby sidewalk, sparks fly in every which way, and they’re lucky not to crash. But it’s for a good cause. For lack of speed would only allow the quickly advancing bikes to overtake them. And the drunken, and partially enraged redneck, simply cannot allow them to take his “love” away from him. No, that would be just terrible..... No! They’ll never get mah Maria, nevah! But unfortunately, that’s not the case here. Running out of gas, with the hotel nowhere in sight, the driver is forced to pull over...... And soon, it’s over….. And the last thing Abner remembers, being torn out of the car and beaten, was the look on her poor, beautiful face crying “Help me Abner, help me!” And then she was taken away, gone from sight, and stolen from his heart….. “Shut up, bitch!” And then there was a solid smack, followed by screams of pain. Was it Dagario, or was it Santiago? Abner doesn’t really remember. But what he does remember was coming momentarily to his feet, and attempting to rescue his lady in distress. And he made some progress…..notably, a small pile of unconscious gang members strewn out along the dirty street, but the odds were just to great. And moments later, closing his eyes, lying face-up on the cold, hard surface of the roadside, Abner remembers looking up into the beautifully lit night sky. The moon, the stars, the heavens…..and her face, all sparkling in the wonderous Mexican sky. And then, in an almost heavenly trance, he thought to himself….. If only I had more beer….. And even though it was completely irrelevant to the situation, it was still a beautiful thought….. And then the world turned black. ---------------------------------------- And yes, those were the days….. So staring back up at the screen, gazing into the eyes of an almost forgotten angel, Abner reads the dark lines on her delicately aging face. Sighing, he mentally runs his hands through the dark folds of her thick, graying hair, and peers into her eyes. Deep, brown, and surprisingly youthful, the sparkle of yesteryear is still trapped somewhere in her youthful mind, he can see it. And with his mind made, Abner smiles up at his friend and utters the words that he’s been holding in for over twenty years…. You see, after two decades of sleepless nights, lonely Christmas’s, and a cold bed…..all Abner had to say was this: “Call back later ya stupid whore, I’m tryin’ to drink mah beer!” And with that, he reaches forward, lifts the can off his desk, and downs the Bud in one swig. The screen goes black.
