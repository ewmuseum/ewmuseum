Confused and Bemused...

2 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd May 2001 For the first time in a couple of weeks of promo filming, we are not in the “Celebrity Allotment” so for all those non-garden enthusiastic people out there that must come as a great relief. Mainly because on this channel you don’t get to see Charlie Dimock’s bouncing boobies. Nor do you get to see the Boobie Boy lust after them. Sometimes she must just think “I know, I’ll wear a bra today” and then forget about it. Ah, just to think the number of Americans who will be looking at this here paragraph and trying their hardest to work out whom the heck Charlie Dimock is and why a man other than Jon Hull would want to wear a bra. Just to think out there that there is always a Blackadder and always a Baldrick... and that most Americans still won’t know who they are either... so therefore we must travel through the ages to see Syko and Miyagawa as they always were... back to... Syko: I wish this narrator would stop talking such enormous bollocks. He always is spouting bollocks as large as mine. Miyagawa: Stop talking about the size of your bollocks, it just isn’t polite. It’s rather gross actually to be completely blunt. Look you two, this is my part of the promo and I can talk about whatever I want. For instance the incompetence of the service of British Telecom. Those very same people who have managed to delay the PURE results by three days currently and is still increasing because of the fact that they cut off MY DAMNED PHONELINE. Miyagawa: Why did they do that then? Dunno, I had paid the bill and everything, I tend to think of it as gross incompetence and stupidity. I phoned them earlier and they couldn’t even confirm what time the second engineer was going to be coming around today... oh did I mention, the Engineer came yesterday, did a few diagnostic checks and said it was something wrong at the service center. So the service center did a few diagnostic checks this morning and said that it was a fault at my end, so now after THREE frickin’ days, the second engineer is coming out. Syko: You should do what I did when my ADSL line went down. Kidnap the engineer and hold him to ransom until they fix the line. Admittedly they did decide they didn’t want him back and I kept him in a dungeon for six months... actually... I think he might still be there. Miyagawa: You don’t have a dungeon. Syko: I do... although everyone else knows it as the Pennsylvania Hotel, New York City... opposite Madison Square Garden. Miyagawa nods enthusiastically at that motion about the Pennsylvania. Miyagawa: I’ve stayed there. It is a dungeon. The bathroom doors don’t even shut properly. The place is complete waste of space; they should knock it all down and rebuild the entire thing. Miyagawa and Syko look at each other confused for a second. Miyagawa: Erm, Mr. Narrator... you do realise that this is the second page now, and you still havn’t done the actual introduction, instead we’ve just done a rant about British Telecom and a crappy overpriced hotel that the handler just happened to stay in back in October. Oh damn, you’re right... now where are you... Syko: Aren’t you supposed to tell us that? It wasn’t a question... it was rhetorical. And I know Frisco is going to come down on me like a tonne of goddamn bricks for managing to get that word in at the wrong place... but at least I spelled it right. Well, at least I spelled it right on the British version of Word XP’s Spell-check... I’m sure the American version is going to find a lot of spelling errors in this, although the one thing that the Americans should do is to stop claiming that they speak English and admit they speak American, which is a completely different language. Miyagawa: The intro would be a pleasant experience right now. Is that a hint? Miyagawa & Syko: YES!!! Whoa, chill boys. Miyagawa & Syko: GET ON WITH IT ALREADY!!!!
