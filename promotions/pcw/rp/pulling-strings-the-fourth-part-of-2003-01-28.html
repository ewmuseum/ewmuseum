Pulling Strings, The Fourth Part of

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.28.03 “Get up boyo, ya haven’t done nothing yet!” cried the voice of the old man sauntering inside of the ring with Dante at the moment. As the lights continue to dim lower and lower into the moonlight crescent. Sweat colored their bodies as the stick substance covered them from their heads to their midsections as Dante huffs and puffs his way through the regime. “Show me!” the Lyrical Messiah calls out. They lock up, each man trying to pin the others down in a Greco Roman Wrestling affair. Their legs are contorted, joined at the hips as the vertical bases of each man tends to bend lower and lower, until Dante pushes off, catching the old man with a basic leg takedown. His chin scrapes along the mat, red welts and bruises are perfectly seen on his hairy, yet pale colored chest as Dante wenches the knee forward, taking momentum out of the sails from the old man. “Ya got me sonny boy?” Dante chuckles, “Maybe, or you just might catch me in one of those holds I might not get out of.” “You’re right!” Kipping his knee into the sternum of the African American wrestler, the old man pushes himself back up while Dante doubles over, holding his abdomen in obvious distinction of pain. “Ah,” he slowly moans out as if he were a cow in mating season. The old man sweeps him up by the feet, before the body’s weight comes crashing down back to the mat. “Alright, all right,” the old man repeats with great ease. “Enough for today.” Dante shakes his head, “C’mon! If you could do this to thirty different guys in a single drop of sweat, you can’t handle a man like me?” Trying to handle Dante was out of the question as the man stiffened his shirt, placing it back on while letting his callous and bruised hands place the buttons back inward through the tiny oval shaped holes. At an age like this, its hard to understand just why men would still be going through the training and persevering of their bodies in order to compete in such a sport, the blood becomes hard, arteries clogged with an excess amount of it, leading to the brain not even getting air support and boom, a seizure occurs. “Kid,” the old man tends to breathe heavily as he sat down in a folding chair. “If you had as much brains as you have talent, you would have been World Champion a long time ago.” Dante sighs to him, “I know that, you know that and most of the world knows that, but your point is?” The man quickens to stop Dante in mid-sentence. “I just showed your ass what my point is, now c’mon, I’ve got some new fresh kids coming in – get out!” Location:Jackson Mansion; Houston, TexasDate: January 28, 2003Time: 9:41 AMTending to her stares in front of a mirror, Tracy sat back in the almost empty Jackson mansion located on the outside skirts of grass and land in suburban Houston - The Woodlands. Her eyes seem to tell the tale as the couple’s nine month old daughter Simmone cries out for her mom, “Mom-e, mom-e!” “Yes?” “Dada?” the young girl cries out, in a tone of question as Tracy nods her head. “I know, I know. He’s out in Japan, doing something for the damn job he works for, like his father to his mother…” The young woman only shifts her body towards the phone, picking up the handle and dialing the numbers as she holds the phone to be connecting in-between her head and her shoulder. “Hello?” “Yeah, Mr. Jackson?” “Tracy, its nice to speak to you, but why are you calling me, shouldn’t my son, your husband be there with you?” Tracy laughs, “Stop reading my mind, Eric. But Dante’s not here, he’s in Japan, busy coming off a win over Chet King. The man tried to run him over with a Golf Kart!” Eric Jackson, the older matriarch of the family was almost sixty now, just about close to living a wrestlers retirement as you could get. Thinning gray hairs appear as he makes various coughs and cries upon the phone, before his wife Pamela taps him on the back. “Are those coughs catching up again dear?” He nods, “Ye-“ he coughs once more. “Yeah, but I’ll be fine.” Tracy snickers over the phone, “So, old man can’t hold his throat?” “Shush child,” he hushes her with a finger over his parched and chapped lips. “Now, where is Dante?” “Off in Europe,” she answered with a despondent tone in her voice. “Wrestling against Skylar Green, and Totally Tubular Tommy for some title.” “That wouldn’t be the Continental Championship now would it?” Tracy scoffs, “I don’t keep up with the current stats on him, and it’s not my job at all in the first place, so calm down with that. If he were truthfully caring about me –“ “He’d be doing what he is now.” “What?” Tracy shrieks in horrid gasps as her eyes begin to widen with a softened tone in her voice. “You’re kidding, you are actually taking his side!” “I was a wrestler once, do you actually think I’d side with the wife on this one? Ask Pamela how she dealt with it.” He responds coldly before coughing once more, yoking up more phlegm before spitting it into a trashcan. Pamela’s voice can be overheard the phone, “Ew, you nasty old man!” “Honey, I’m on the phone, what else do you expect from me?” he resonates his voice towards his wife, sending her to drop back down into the kitchen, not paying any mind from Eric as he leans back down into his chair. “So, are you okay with this? Dante is supporting you and my granddaughter, don’t be mad at him for it.” Sadly, Tracy approves of this, sitting herself down upon the bed with the phone still stuck in-between her arm and ear as she hangs up the phone, quietly murmuring to herself before laying her head down on her pillow, to sleep and rest.
