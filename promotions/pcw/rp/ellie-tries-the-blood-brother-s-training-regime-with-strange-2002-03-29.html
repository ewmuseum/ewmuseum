Ellie~! Tries the 'Blood Brother's Training Regime' With Strange Results...

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/03/02 Our shot opens up in the dark living room of Xavier Kannon’s residence outside of Miami. Bang Bang BOOM!!! Click Click CRASH O’DOOM~! As we move through the door there seems to me another light source other than that of the high moon filtering through the closed curtains of the floor to ceiling windows and between the lour noises the clicking of buttons can be heard. Click Click CliCliClick CRASH! The camera moves in to see the large flat screen TV that is attached to the East wall is alive in a frenzy of violence and explosions, the sounds of which resonate out of the Surround Sound speakers. “COOL!!!! I JUST TOTALLY KILLED THAT HOMELESS PERSON, HIS DOG, THAT POOR LOOKING WOMAN AND HER SCRUFFY KIDS AND THAT FAT GUY ALL WITH ONE MISSILE....YAY ME!!!” As the camera moves over the Black leather sofa we see Xavier Kannon’s valet Ellie~! sitting cross legged on the rug hammering away at a Playstation 2 controller, relishing the violence she is unleashing on the huge screen a few feet in front of her. She is wearing a White spaghetti strap top and a pair of baggy White and Blue striped pyjama bottoms topped off with a pair of big, Grey fluffy slippers. Her long Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights is un-tied and falls down her back, just touching the rug. Eleanor : “Wow...Killing poor people is like Thai food...No matter how much, you always want more! Now, where is the Welfare Office in this down....” She pauses the game and reaches to her left where she grabs a bottle of Bud Light and takes a sip to finish it, tossing it to her other side where it lands with a smack against another 10 or so bottles in a pile. Eleanor : “Beer, Playstation and a lack of sleep....Wow, a wrestler should use this as a gimmick!” She un-pauses the game and gets back to destroying a City as we see the character she controls wandering around, blowing up anything that moves, doesn’t move, or hasn’t made up it’s mind as to whether it will move or not. Eleanor : “I should be in my own game...PREP WARS where I, the valiant female hero must point out the wrongs in all who walk past her in the corridor....I’d be all Hey, Jessica, your roots are showing! Oh, and hey, I heard you dropped your price down from $2 to $1.50...Economic slowdown seems to be effecting you!!!....That game’d SO rule!” She gets back to her work laying he computerised City to waste, spurred on by her thoughts of her own Video Game, going about the havoc with added gusto. “Ellie~!.....My little Poon Puppy.....What are you doing?” Standing at the door to the living room is wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon wearing one of his own Black t-shirts with an image f his face on the front and “If Hubbard Had A Son....” under it. With this he wears a pair of Black jogging bottoms. His long Auburn hair is both un-tied and un-brushed so it falls wildly down his face. Xavier : “It’s 3am.....And what is with the noise?” He strolls in, rubbing his eyes who in their tiredness seem to lose some of there Emerald Green sparkle. He looks at the screen and the carnage unfolding, looking surprised as he drops back into the sofa, looking exhausted. Xavier : “I thought you vowed never to play things after you found out you didn’t ACTUALLY get a Blue Hedgehog and a 2 Tailed Fox with the game?” She doesn’t turn to face him and in the slight lull between perpetrated violence she grabs another already opened Bud Light and takes a large sip before returning to button bashing. Eleanor : “Yeah, but in the real world I ca only be SO bitchy to people before I get in trouble, in this world I can do whatever I want...Just how reality should be....Anyway, you should be doing this to research Brock Newbludd, I think he did this before even I did!” The Gold Patron Meritorious does not look too happy with Ellie~!’s new choice of pastime, looking un-nerved by the violence unfolding on his TV before his very eyes. Xavier : “My little Shag Squirrel, averaging 100 beers, 13 hours of Playstation and 4 hours of sleep is NOT a good thing! The Blood Brothers were a disgrace to wrestling, the drank to much, didn’t work out enough, set a wrong example to the nations youth.....Why do you want to be like them?” Ellie~! tosses the now empty bottle of Bud Light into the pile.... *CLINK!* ....The looks to Xavier with her pale Blue eyes looking bloodshot and then to the patch of carpet where a pile of full Beer Bottles once sat. Eleanor : “Ellie~!.......Want........BEER!!!!” The Scientologist sighs and look at what his valet has been reduced to through the mind numbing tedium of obsessive video gaming. Xavier : “No more Beer for Ellie~!.....” he then gets up and walks past his Zombie-fied valet and turns the console and TV off as she sits there for a few seconds staring at the blank screen with a smile o her face before she shakes her head vigorously, seemingly snapping out of her trance. Xavier : “...And that is enough gaming for one day....” He looks to the curtains and sees the Moon filtering through against the night sky. Xavier : “......Or tonight. You are no longer merely a valet, as of Friday you are a wrestler, it’s me and you against Jeremy and that Girlfriend of his, Team Living In Sin or whatever they are called. So enough of the drinking....” He picks up all the bottles and carries them to the bin where he drops them in, the smashing being heard where Ellie~! is now standing. Xavier : “....No late night gaming....” The Gold Patron meritorious then pulls the plug for the console out of the wall socket. Xavier : “....And plenty of sleep, exercise and Water to flush the alcohol out of your system, then you’ll be my lean, overly mean, little Fuck Ferret, fighting machine ready for our big Fusion match where you finally get your hands on Jessica!” She seemingly appears to be coming around to his point of view as she crashes onto the sofa, looking up wide eyed at Kannon as he mentors her ready for her PCW debut. Xavier : “Because lets face it....The Brock Newbludd pre-match work-out isn’t really doing that well for Brock himself so the chances of it working for you my little Poon Puppy aren’t that good. I mean talk about falling from Grace, if Grace were just a little bit above the gutter you have an accurate appraisal of the situation!” He perches on the arm of the sofa as Ellie~! tries to digest his words and make sense of them, instead just settling for a blank nod with doe eyes. Xavier : “So.....You ready for Friday?” His valet excitedly sits up straight, now eager to talk about the match after she has been pulled from her gaming induced apathy. Eleanor : “I WANNA BE MEANSTREAK!!!” The Scientologist looks puzzled by his valet and tag team partner’s outburst. Xavier : “But Ellie~!....Those tattoos really wouldn’t suit you!” She looks back at him with a curious glance, as if what he said had been preposterous. Eleanor : “No silly......For the match on Friday, I have chosen who I want to be...I wanna be Meanstreak...He is like, BIG and nobody can hurt him so we win....” Even for someone well schooled in dealing with PCW’s most famous Prep, Xavier seems lost for words. She looks at his blank face for a few seconds before continuing. Eleanor : “How do you think I, like, SO became a Video-Gaming Goddess? I choose the best characters, so I choose him...As long as you choose a good guy too...We’ll SO win! Hey, maybe you could choose Meanstreak too? They’d have to have different coloured ones so we know who is who but we would totally kill Howard and slutty slutty bitch bitch!!!” Kannon looks at her curiously, wondering if she has taken a knock to the head or something as he tip toes around her on his way back to the stairs. Xavier : “Um...Okay Ellie~!, maybe you should sleep on that idea and when you wake up maybe you’ll be less delusio-...I mean, less tired...” He looks at her out of the corner of his eye as he orbits her, she replies with a look of her own, wondering what is wrong with Xavier. Xavier : “So you put your head down my little Shag Squirrel and stay away from sharp objects until your pretty little brain works properly again...Okay...” As he gingerly steps backwards and out of the door she watches him with narrowing, suspicious eyes, wondering what on earth has got into the Scientologist. Eleanor : “Er......OKAY!...Like What-EVER!“ He slowly closes the door and leaves Ellie~! in the darkness of the living room as the shot starts to fade out. Eleanor : “Maybe Meanstreak is like his character....I could go with Ivan?” As Ellie~! ponders which character to fight as at Fusion the shot hits Black. “I wonder if I’m in the game?”
