Sleep (after Jade's next rp)

Date: now It was quite strange how things panned out when living with the person you most hate and most like all rolled into one. Jade was a mischievous woman and indeed caused just as many problems as she solved, but they were fun problems and Lacuna usually enjoyed solving them afterwards. Jade was the sum of all the opposites of Lacuna’s morals, and the contrast pleased her more than she could really let on however, without upsetting Brook, who she knew was the closest thing she had ever had to a friend. Her latest escapades had brought one hell of a frown to Lacuna’s tanned forehead as she attempted to work out just what had happened. What she had gathered was that Jade had set up a blind date for her by posing as Lacuna in a dating agency without telling her. This had led to beating the bowels out of the guy (damn yanks) in an alley behind the restaurant, which was odd really. It was very safe to say that she did not like the guy; he was short, smelly, fat, hideously ugly and above all else, American to the teeth. She despised his quiet patriotism, his love for the country and all its obvious faults. Being near such a narrow minded fool was offensive to every ounce of her being, and the whole way through she cursed Jade for ever letting her near such an idiot. Then came the retribution. It was sweet and possibly too enjoyable as they pounded on the creep. What they did was clearly illegal and could get Lacuna deported back to England, thusly losing her a job. Jade had assured her that no guy would admit to being beaten up by a pair of women in a dark alley with no witnesses. Yet still, she was worries that the police would appear up her driveway at any minute asking for her to ‘accompany them to the station’ or the American equivalent, which would obviously be less courteous. She sat in her room and stared into the deep purple darkness of the far wall, opposite the four posted bed in which she sat and thought deeply. The purple of the walls created a strange sensation in her. They had always said colours were linked to emotions, and Lacuna had always believed that purple led to a more regal feel, but what she felt was contemplation as deep as a quarry pit, turned into a lake by the falling of the rain. She was becoming silent again, as if unable to move from her self-emancipating prison. Her servants were avoiding her and Jade was asleep, so she was alone again. The feeling of loneliness was overlapping her senses. “Maybe she’s right, maybe I do need someone” She shrugged softly and closed her eyes to the light, her hand clasped tightly across her flat stomach as her legs kicked gently at the springy mattress. She was now too deep in thought for her own good; she could feel herself being carted away into the land of contemplation. “I’ve always been alone… It’s the only way forwards…” She stood now, somewhat unsteadily at first as she struggled to maintain balance. Her feet were aching sharply as she was unused to the heels she had adorned for her ‘date’ and she practically tip-toed across the room towards the mirror where she sat at a small wooden stool and admired herself in the gilt-framed mirror that she had bought so many years ago, one of her first possessions to indicate the wealth she was accumulating at the time. In front of the mirror was an oak dressing table, coated in all manner of beauty products and a range of different hairbrushes and combs. God knows why she had so many when she could have got by amply with just the one, but she felt it as a necessity. She was possessive and she knew it… oh god, here comes the thinking again… “I could if I wanted, couldn’t I?” It was true that she could get a man if she wanted; she was rich, enjoyed sports and violence and was incredibly attractive when she wanted to be. It was just not part of her mentality to go out and get a bloke. The self-doubt was one of her major problems; it came about whenever something new that she had not contemplated before arose to pester her head with its incessant nagging. The mirror before her reflected the smallest glint of water forming in her tear duct, and she flung her hand at it angrily, her mouth twisting into an angry snarl, her eyes flashing with anger. She always made herself like this; she was the one who got herself down. “People only drag you down, it can be done on your own, it can be done oh so easily. I don’t need anyone else, I’m singular. Me against the world.” every word she spoke seemed to bring with it an air of confidence as if they were used to reassure her beliefs that a strong one is better than a weak two. She stood again and passed across the large bedroom, scraping her feet on the thick black carpet that hides the floor. She was heading across the pair of leather armchairs across the room in front of the wall mounted television and leant on the back of one before falling over it and landing hard onto her back, staring up at the high ceiling. Her head was buzzing with thoughts as she closed her eyes tightly. She really wasn’t in the mood for this; thinking can only get her down. Then she smiled; it broke across her face like a virus. There was only one way to get rid of a feeling like this and that was large amounts of pizza. She jumped to her feet, ignoring the pain, and practically sprinted across the room to where the phone was placed on a large oak dining table. She glanced at it, and the glanced at the small lift chute linking the bedroom with the kitchen below, and she shrugged. “I never liked phones anyway” she turned to the chute and clearing her throat loudly “ORDER ME A PIZZA NOW, AND DON’T EVEN ATTEMPT TO COOK ONE. SAME AS ALWAYS!” she screamed down the chute, her powerful voice echoing loudly as it passed down the metal shaft. There was no reply, there never was from the kitchen staff. They knew that arguing would be more than their jobs were worth, and it was true that none of them could cook a convincing pizza to save their lives. She sat down on a wooden chair by the huge oak dining table and picked up the phone twiddling it idly in her thin hands. Now she had to try and avoid thinking while she waited for her order of food to arrive and so she could stuff herself with far too much food for someone of her build to ever be able to eat without gaining weight. There was little point as she would just shed it during her daily exercises off anyway. She smiled as she pressed a quick dial button on the sleek black handset of the phone, which linked directly to the room Jade was sleeping in. Lacuna pressed the handset hard to her ear, sweeping the hair out of the way prior with the back of her hand. Finally the dial tone sounded up as the phone began to ring loudly in Jade’s room. ----- Jade stirred in her sleep as the phone on the bedside cupboard began to ring loudly, the handset vibrating slightly with the effort which added to the overall noise. Suddenly her head shot up, her eyes closed tightly, and she ran a hand over her head as if to wake herself up. “Urgh!” she grunted as she finally acknowledged the ringing that had woken her up. She ran a hand through her frizzed up hair which had scattered in all directions during what had appeared to have been a rough night. Her makeup had ran and was smudged into a clown like apparel beneath her odd coloured eyes which had finally opened under the constant pressure to find out what the hell the ringing noise was. She fell off of the bed with a loud bang, and slowly as she lifted her head back above the level of the bed, she saw the minor vibrations of the phone and flung herself across the bed, catching the phone and, with obvious difficulty, finally jabbed her slim fingers into the correct button. “Yerh?” she answered. Not even awake enough to form her words correctly, much to the delight of Lacuna, who was creasing herself with laughter on the other end of the phone. “Haaaaa! Haahaaahaaaaa!” her laugh was admittedly odd, but it was easily enough to wind Jade up, even though she was still trying to wake up and speak properly. “Wilshutit ‘for I kickyas?” Jade’s response just set Lacuna’s laughing fit off further, as now she was coughing through lack of breath. “Have a drink of water and wake up, woman. This is just my punishment for last night! You deserve this big time, bloody bitch” Lacuna was enjoying this, as Jade was recovering and slowly began the ascent back towards coherent speech. “Well I suppose I aught to let you get back to sleep, see you in a couple of hours. Ha!” And she hung up the phone, for her pizza had arrived, and she was very, very hungry.
