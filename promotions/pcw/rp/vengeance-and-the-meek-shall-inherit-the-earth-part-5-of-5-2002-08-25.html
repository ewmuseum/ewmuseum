Vengeance: And the Meek Shall Inherit the Earth...Part 5 of 5

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-2002 The shot opens to show Venneman Pendragon, dangling by a rope that hangs down from a sheer cliff face. He appears cool, calm and collected, even though it would seem he is in mortal danger. I guess it is not the way of the Knight to get nervous... 'Damn I feel nervous. I hate always having to be on these outlandish adventures. All I really want it my TV Title back, and yet I have to come all the way out here, to the jungles of Africa to steal some jewel that I do not even want. I could say that I was misled by prophecy, but that would be merely an excuse. I had to get back to the states, and I had to get there now. Time runs short... Pendragon looks up and hollers out... Venneman: XERXES!! MY FRIEND!! COME HERE!! The shot pans up the cliff side and we see that Pendragon's horse, Xerxes, has indeed come to the edge of the cliff. Pendragon points to the rope and Xerxes leans down and takes it into his mouth. He then begins to back obediently away, rope in mouth. Ever so slowly, Venneman begins to inch his way up the side of the cliff, back to safety. 'Even after having dispatched more than I can count, I guess I can say that goblins are not so bad. In their own way, I guess they are people too...Inasmuch as they have a personality. Bah! Goblins have no place in the world anymore...What am I saying?' Finally at the top of the cliff, Pendragon pulls himself into the grass. He stands and gives a Knight's salute, left fist to chest, to the Goblins who have gathered on the other side of the drop. He then turns away as the Goblins participate in what can only be called a group moon... 'Always taunting, always vulgar. The goblin is indeed a rare and dying breed. Whereas the Knight, in its own way, is a fading emblem of nobility and honor...Two things that are hard to find in the current world. I guess that Sunday will be a battle not only for my Title, but also on who can claim the stronger force in the world...The noble Knight, or the treacherous Goblin?' Pendragon shakes his head and walks over to his horse. He pats his friend and climbs into the saddle before taking off into the jungle... >>>FLASH
