Book of DarkStar: 45 - (End of the New Saga)

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-10-02 The Book of DarkStar: 44 - 77 44 For the seven days they did war. 45 The Dream King, the Knight of Fire, the Cowboy, and the False Messiah. 46 The battle began and ended with the same amount of confusion that it was started with. 47 As it was written the light was confused and the dark was in order. 48 Betrayal was the word on the lips of the Cowboy as he walked away from the False Messiah with narry a prayer on his mind. 49 Light had betrayed itself. 50 It was proven that the only trueness is the Darkness. 51 There is no light as it was spoken of only days before this. 52 The Legendary one took the beating of his life 53 and on top did come the darkness. 54 Until light betrayed itself again. 55 The original False Messiah joined forces with the Legendary one. 56 He said unto them that they shall not be a force to be reckoned with any longer. 57 Oh was he wrong. 58 For the Dark Age have risen to the occasion like they have any other before this. 59 The blood is on the Dream Kings lips. 60 In the realm the taste seeps into his mouth unlike ever has he tasted before, for the gold is glittering in the eyes of the unknown. 61 Looking towards them all he spoke. 62 'Woe onto the ones that despise our presence, 63 Woe to the ones that expect us to dissapear at the return of the 'messiah', 64 woe to the ones that chose not to follow us, 65 and woe be unto you, the ones that chose not to believe in us, and instead chose to dip your hand into the undeniably disgusting pot of light.' 66 The lights were turned off on this chapter. 67 Yes, the winner of this battle was fortold long before it actually took place. 68 It is Darkness. 69 Darkness breeds everywhere, in hearts, in the dreams, in your nightmares, its complacent and quiet and sits in the back of your mind waiting to be shown. 70 The 'bad guys' embrace that, the Dark Age has embraced it, 71 and that is what makes them the better. 72 If you walk in the shadow of death, you don't need to worry about it seeing you in its peripheral vision. For you are always directly behind it, waiting for it to make a mistake. Then capitalizing. 73 The good does not see that, 74 for they see those of us that choose to walk on the side of 'evil' as those that do not see the light. 75 and thus he said to them again, the words that Legend Inc. should have heeded in the beginning of this epic battle. 76 'Light and Dark in both extremes, 77 is equally impossible to see through.' DarkStar put his pen down and looked up from the study, the window was open and the darkness shined through, for this is the realm of dreams... and this is where all is true and nothing is real. He muttered under his breath and stood up, closing the window and looking out into the nothingness that is the outer realms. You will never know... Duke Williams, you will never understand. Christopher Chase, you will never believe. Legend Inc.,... You will never be the same.
