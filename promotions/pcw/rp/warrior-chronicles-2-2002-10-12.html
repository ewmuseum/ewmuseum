Warrior Chronicles 2

Date: 10-12-02 Warrior Chronicles 2 'I Am Dead I am hardly alive. I am not even living. I am dead… Dead and never coming back. But look at this: a shadow in the moonlight. A shifting, changing, thrusting force. Out of the way. It comes now. Out of the way! It’s coming! It’s coming! …It’s here. It’s here. Now watch as it lays waste to villages, countries, civilizations. Ruined. Dead. But this force… it continues… the slayer is gone, all hope is lost, and yet the dragon remains. So clear. Ever horrifying. Ever killing. I am dead. The Beast… There was a time, however, when I was living. Everything was blooming bright; the world shined in many ways. So many ways. But all the while, a dark cloud was hanging over the sunshine. It moved throughout the world, but nobody bothered to look up at the sky. They knew it was there, and that was all that mattered. Soon, rain began to come down from the cloud. Slowly at first, but then more, and more, rapidly brewing into a great storm. The people didn’t notice though. They put out their umbrellas, and as long as they were dry, all was fine. Then came the thunder. Noisy… strong… violent. The winds would howl, and the sky would blacken. The people refused to listen. They refused to look. They simply covered their ears, and continued with their lives. To them, the world was still shining. The next plague brought lightning, but the obliviousness of the people had no bounds. They sat inside their dwellings. They curled up into bed. They wrapped sheets and blankets over their heads, drowning out the fierce-some night. And finally, when the people could avoid it no more, they got out of their beds, out of their houses, and stood in the open. They tilted their heads up to the sky… and they gasped a silent gasp of disbelief... of remorse… of sorrow. For before them was not a storm, but a much worse hazard. Before them, was the beast. But it had been before them for much longer… for the beast was just a disguise. The Warrior… The people, oh so foolish, they tried to escape. But they could never really have escaped. This beast was a beast of much power… and no man could stop it. No man would be brave enough. But there was one who could… One who had the potential to become savior to the poor people, to free them from this awful shame called the truth. This one… The warrior. He hides in the shadows, for it is here that the people shunned him. They didn’t want him. They didn’t respect him. They didn’t even know what he was. But now he was their only hope. What a shame. The warrior shouldn’t help them. The warrior shouldn’t have to. But the warrior did help them. The warrior did have to. It was his very being that bonded him to the restrictions of his kind. Like so many before him, he was a warrior, and had to do what any warrior would. The warrior had known of this day. He had prepared for it. Even if he didn’t truly believe in it, there was a part of him that knew it would come. And while the people were hiding from the beast, the warrior was training for it. He knew this day would come. He was prepared. And so the legendary battle begins. On one side a monster of inevitable proportions. On the other, a spirit pure enough to defend his beliefs. But as the battle continued, the line between the two became thinner and thinner. The Beast Within The Warrior… They fought and they fought, never stopping to refresh their minds, bodies, and souls. The people watched in horror, knowing that their fate lay completely on the shoulders of the warrior. But the warrior did not fight for the people. He fought for a much greater cause. The warrior did not fight for the people, but in the end, he won for the people. He destroyed the beast… at least as far as anyone knew. As far as they cared. The people went back to their lives, knowing now that the sky was surely clear, and the sun most definitely shining bright. Knowing that the warrior would be looking over them. This warrior that they neglected once more. But now, the warrior senses something new. Up in the sky, behind the sunshine, a shadow emerges once more. The beast is back, weak in its current state, but growing much rapidly than before. The warrior is the only hope of stopping this terror before it consumes the people once and for all. And the warrior tries. He does everything in his power to stop the beast. But when it comes down to the last moment… When all that is needed is one final blow… The warrior cannot stop it. He tries so hard… but in the end he is helpless. Because in the end, the warrior and the beast… they are one in the same. And it ends… And so you see, that I am dead. But I am not truly dead, only hidden behind a bright sunshine. Look up, people. Look up into the sky! The beast is coming… but this time around, I cannot stop myself. For I am dead. I am dead. …Yet now, I am returning.
