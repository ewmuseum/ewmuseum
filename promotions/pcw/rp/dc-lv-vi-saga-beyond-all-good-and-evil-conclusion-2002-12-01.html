DC/LV/VI Saga: Beyond All Good and Evil (Conclusion)

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/12/02 The vibrant ecstatic constant flashing of the neon blue and red from the police car siren ricocheting off the shadows and night sky made the outside of the abandoned warehouse seem like some one man deserted rave. The one man inhabitant of it being The Sinister Paladin himself Gideon Poe who stood outside, merely sizing up the warehouse, which was much larger and modern than he thought. There were at least 3 stories to the place, with numerous grand windows on the outside with metal bars guarding them on the outside, and the building was a hideous grey. He felt uncomfortable to say the least as he stood there with the snow pounding down around him. '....Roland' he called silently. As if by cue Roland the eagle came soaring out of the darkness and perched himself comfortably on Gideon's right shoulder. 'I hope your more prepared than I am' he spoke to the eagle, who replied amusingly turning his head to look at Gideon before flapping his wings slightly. 'So we're both as prepared as each other, that's reassuring.' Gideon then slowly sighed and walked to the police car and lifted up the bonnet. Inside there enough weapons to start a full scale riot in any state. Whatever was going on inside, Clarkson had been informed to bring numerous amounts of fire power for protection. Gideon quickly reached his hand in and retrieved two small Uzi's and a sawn off shotgun, followed by the most unusual weapon in there. A large curved Samurai sword, which Gideon sheathed down the back of his long black leather trench coat. 'You'll need more than that to stop them' croaked a familiar voice. Gideon turned sharply, seemingly unconvinced the voice belonged to the man he thought it did, but he was unpleasantly mistaken. 'What are you doing here old man?' Out from the snot came the small 4't tall figure of Jeremiah O'Barr, staff in hand. 'As my student Gideon, I feel as though I should only be here to at least aid you in someway' 'You poisoned me three years ago, you can aid me by giving me the antidote.' Jeremiah sighed slowly before drawing closer. 'I know more of these men than you do. I know their past plans and their future endeavours after this. I know....' Before he could finish he was cut off by Gideon slamming the bonnet of the police vehicle. 'Then shut up and get moving'. Gideon then tossed the old man a small magnum which he barely caught and looked somewhat confused at it. 'Very well. Yet it seems to me that you have forgotten that by doing this you could very easily die' 'Half of me is hoping for that anyway. Now let's go' * * * * * The inside reeked of filth and blood. It was a scent that Gideon had become all to familiar with. The blood stains were clear on the large dark green walls of the first story, yet there were no bodies. Whatever the first story had once been was anyone's guess, but the large bandstand at the bottom of it allowed Gideon to assume it had once been a bar of some sort. 'What the hell happened here?' It was almost hard to tell whether the wall was supposed to be painted in dark green or the crimson blood red. 'You really don't want to know what they've done here' 'I'll take your word for it' The dark duo then walked ahead further, Gideon leading the way with Jeremiah following slowly behind. His staff making a repeating rhythmic tap into the cold concrete floor as he used it whilst striding. Ahead was a moulded murky shaded door. Gideon looked at it tensely before turning the stiff door knob and pushing it forward and opening it. She was young, about the same age Shelly had been, just before she had been ordered to execution by her father. She was tied and gagged and lay on the small wooden table. Her panties half way down her legs with her mascara running smoothly down her face from the tears. Opposite her stood a tall burly man, ginger stubble taking over his face. He stood there doing zipping himself before responding without looking up. 'Hey Randy, she's all yours if you want her, I know ya don't usually like sloppy seconds but this young one has....' His expression at the sight of Gideon was an image of a thousand fears and regrets. He saw not a man, but an Avatar Of Death. 'Wh-what the.......' His words were drowned by the sound of a bullet from the Uzi tearing through the flesh of his leg. He fell down and grasped the wound immediately, while the girl looked on in horror. 'Go on ahead' Gideon told Jeremiah. Surprisingly The Elderly Evil simply nodded with a disturbed look on his face, and made his way slowly up the wooden stairs at the end of the room. 'Who th....the fuck are you?!' Gideon walked dominantly over to the fallen worthless rapist and looked down menacingly at him. 'I go by many names.....' Gideon then crouched down slightly, and ripped away the mans hand from his wound. 'Some call me a Shade....' Gideon then proceeded to ram his middle and index finger into the gaping wound of the man, who responded with a high pitched scream deep from his lungs. 'Some call me an Avatar Of Death, a Psychotic Clown, a Deep Dark Demented Rebel, an Anti-Hero in today's twisted society, and a Sinister Paladin to the new age...' 'Fuuuuuck!!! NO!!' 'Either way, to you I am nothing more than mercenary. An agent of death. I am your ever lasting pain and your eternal suffering..... I am the exclamation mark at the end of the sentence that represents your life.' Jeremiah stood at the top of the first flight of stairs. He watched not what his protégé was doing. There were limits to what he could teach Gideon and watch Gideon do, and there were the limits at which Gideon exceeded and pushed past. These were the things Jeremiah grew tense and scared of. Now was one of those times where Gideon was exceeding them, now was one of the times where he had to turn away. The sound of the man's mouth being gagged.. The sound of his screams being muffled... The sound of bare fingers tearing at an open seeping messy wound... The sound wrists being bent and snapped... There was more, but Jeremiah scuffled with his mind to force those sounds and images out, and just barely managed it. The next sound he heard was the sound of the girl breathing a sigh of relief as she was untied, and the tense horrific moment of her seeing Gideon's grotesque statement laying flat lined on the floor, eventually followed by her vomiting, which was predictable, and lastly followed by her running from the scene and out the door. 'Ready?' Jeremiah turned and looked at Gideon, calm as usual, and nodded. As they ventured further up the stairs, music greeted their ears, faint and muted, but clearly there. Followed by a few murmuring voices, about a dozen. Soon it was accompanied by another voice, a voice Gideon recognised as Shelly's fathers voice. This time it reeked of unholy ness and pure unadulterated evil. 'Gentleman.....the time is upon us.......!' Gideon continued up the flight of stairs until he reached the near top. Ahead was a cold steel gate which led to the room. On a large circular table sat 10 men. 5 in their late 70's and early 80's, obviously the veterans. The other 5 were relatively young, obviously the new brainwashed recruits. Then on a small podium stood a tall man, his hair bleached red and tied back. He wore nothing but a white shirt and black shoes and trousers. He was the owner of the unholy voice, he was Shelly's father. 'Our time of reckoning and inevitable judgement has reached us!' 'Here' whispered Jeremiah, and placed a small circular pouch in Gideon's palm. 'What's this?' whispered The Shade. 'A little home made recipe. I think it will come in handy' 'Gentleman, I have dipped my head into the rotting bowels of hell and inhaled it's glorious scent. I have reached up into heaven and snatched down God's chosen ones. I have bathed in the blood of pure fallen angel's!' Shelly's father cackled hysterically as he spoke. It made the hair's on Gideon's neck stand at attention. 'He goes by the name of Malocheg' spoke Jeremiah softly. Gideon simply nodded. 'Now gentleman! it is time for us, together, to feat upon the warm flesh, of another fallen angel of MY bloodline!' Slowly Malocheg went somewhere behind the small assembled stage, and bent down. 'His bloodline?' spoke Gideon to himself. However that question was answered when Malocheg lifted up his piece of meat for them to feed on. A small child, a girl, no more than 2. 'Goooooood God! That must be Shelly's daughter! His bloodline is his granddaughter!' The 10 men applauded triumphantly and the sight of the terrified and confused child while Malocheg looked up at her as he held her grinning menacingly and psychotically. 'Her flesh will taste as ripe as fruit picked from the garden of God!' 'Yes!' yelled the men! 'She will be our sacrifice, and she will bring OUR good Lord from his abode!' 'YES!' 'Then when he has risen, he will put right every wrong that has been brought upon this world by THEIR God!' 'YES!' 'Then we....WE......Will take this world for ourselves!' 'The sacrifice of a soul of the bloodline of one of the founding members of DC/LX/VI.......will free us all' 'YE.....' The sight of the small pouch flying through air caught the attention of all the 10 men sitting at the table as it landed dead centre on it. Each one looked at it with confusion and puzzlement. 'HUH.....' BOOM! A bright engulfing light incorporated by an almighty ear shattering bang sent all 10 men at the table soaring backwards. Three of them were already laying motionless several yards from the table covered in flames. The rest were treated to the entrance of The Avatar Of Death. 'WHAT THE HELL!' yelled Malocheg as he placed the child down quickly. The next sounds were that of bullets soaring out of the twin Uzi's Gideon Poe was carrying. His long black leather trench coat swaying majestically as he turned to face each member of the committee known as DC/LX/VI. Their blood stained the walls with grace and sadistic beauty. Their screams were shrill and mournful. 'Come on!' yelled one of the younger members to Gideon. 'Take me, I'm willing to die for Lucifer, I'm willing to....' Gideon aimed the Uzi at his head. 'Tell him Gideon Poe sent you...... and tell him I'll probably be seeing him sooner than later myself' BANG! The man dropped effortlessly and Gideon continued, emotionless and uncaringly. 'You can not stop us!' 'BANG!' 'HE WILL RISE NO MATTER WHAT!' BANG! '................SHIT!' 'BANG!' They all stood up for their beliefs, they all died unmercifully for their beliefs. Apart from one. 'So...............your him' Gideon turned to greet the owner of the voice. Malocheg strutted slowly down to meet Gideon, his endless faint smirk still clear on his face. 'The slayer of slayers. I must say he's not what I expected Jeremiah.' Jeremiah slowly came trotting out of the secluded area at the entrance of the room, to greet the remaining member and leader of the sinister cult Gideon had just decimated. 'I was expecting something much more toned and precise' 'He's good enough for me' 'My father wouldn't have thought so' 'Your great grandfather would have' 'Ohhhh yes of course, you were under his leadership. Of course you lost your nerve old man, you lost the will to continue in all that was good and right. You refused....' 'I refused to live under your demands and your twisted ideas!' 'But these ideas are now almost a reality Jeremiah! These ideas are now coming to head, and if I have to be the last man to bring them to fruition....' Malocheg then jogged back and reached down behind the podium, drawing the small child from behind it and holding her aloft high above him. 'THEN SO BE IT!' he bellowed through gritted teeth and a sly grin. Gideon dropped his remaining weapons instantly, and gestured to Malocheg. 'Come on, put the child down' Gideon walked slowly towards Malocheg. 'Back dark man! Back paladin!' Malocheg drew a blade from his back pocket swiftly and drew it across the child's neck. 'Her death will not go unnoticed' Slowly but surely the child began to cry in fear. 'Her death will bring back the true God of our kingdom......she will bring about the rebirth of the earth. Her death will....' Malocheg's speech was cut off by seeing Gideon dart towards him out of the corner of his eye. His reaction was timid and slow, and he stalled long enough for Gideon to tackle him to the crowd, with Malocheg holding onto the child as he fell, but letting go to fight back at The Avatar Of Death. The two rolled around scuffling and brawling for what seemed like hours, neither man gaining an advantage. Malocheg was older, much older than Gideon, but he was still broad and strong, while Gideon was younger and more athletic. His senses were more developed and his reflexes were sharper. Gideon knew he had to press those attributes to his advantage. 'Die you scum!' came Jeremiah's voice, Gideon simply rolled his eyes as Malocheg, who was on top of Gideon, turned to face Jeremiah, who was bashing Malocheg with his staff to no effect. Malocheg simply chuckled as he stood tall, driving his foot right into the chest of Gideon before doing so, driving the air right out of him. 'Come on old man, do you seriously think you can hurt a disciple of Lucifer!'Jeremiah simply looked at Malocheg, black and expressionless. His expression when he soared through the air and into the wall from Malocheg hoisting him up and throwing his 4ft tall 90lbs body however was full of pain and suffering. Malocheg then turned his attention back to Gideon.His face glowing and seeping with sick foul intentions. 'Where's your God now?' 'ROLAND!!!!' 'Ha, cry for your mere mortals to come and help you.' Malocheg drilled his boot sickeningly into Gideon's face, sending him from his position on all fours right back to the ground. The sound of a an old grandfather clock from the far corner of the room then chimed once. 'A HA! Midnight is upon us! The day of salvation is nearing!' The clock chimed again signalling Two. Malocheg grabbed the small child from up off the floor carelessly. The clock chimed again....Three! 'The time of reckoning gentleman is approaching!' Four! 'It's time to ask yourself, who you place your faith in' Malocheg walked to the centre of the room, and lifted the small child up to face a large skylight at the top of the ceiling. Five! Neither Gideon, or the semi-conscience Jeremiah responded. 'Very well gentleman......' Six! 'Roland!' Gideon called again. Above through the skylight a cluster of dark clouds began forming quickly, incorporated by thunder and lightning. Seven! Gideon checked the sawn off double barrel shotgun he had underneath his jacket. One shell left. Then his prayers were answered. Out from the shadows came soaring his one positive and anted companion, Roland the eagle. 'GENTLEMAN......' Eight! 'BOW DOWN.......!' Nine! 'FOR.........Arrrrrrgh!' Roland, with stealth and majesty swooped down on the face of Malocheg, and began pecking and gnawing away at his unguarded face. Gideon aimed the shotgun, but had no clear hit, especially with him stumbling with the child still in the air who was now crying to vast high pitched wails. Ten! Gideon got to his feet and stumbled forwards before breaking into a darting run. he could see Malocheg losing his grip on the child. Eleven! Gideon aimed and shot the gun without looking into the air, sending the skylight smashing piercing smash. Malocheg dropped the child at the same time as Gideon tossed the weapon away, and she fell compactly into his outstretched arms. Gideon then rolled out of the way as Roland took the flight. Twelve! On the twelfth stroke signalling midnight, Malocheg looked up to see the various shards of large sharp glass soaring down towards him. This time he did react, with a high pitched yell as several large pieces impaled him at once. Blood came spewing instantly out of his mouth before he fell to the ground. Gideon watched on as Malocheg lay quivering and twitchingon the floor, blood seeping out of several places on his fallen body. Gideon lay the child down gently, and walked over to the fallen Malocheg. He looked up to Gideon, with the same demonic look in his eye. 'This is for your daughter.' Gideon then made sure of his demise by stamping down viciously down upon Malocheg's throat. The sound of his last gasp of breath signalled the end of his life, signalled the end of this apocalypse, and signalled the end of DC/LV/VI. The repeative wail of police sirens filled his ears next, as he turned sharply to gather Jeremiah and Roland. The child sat quietly on the floor staring up at Gideon meaningfully, searching for answers. He looked down at her, and felt an endless amount of remorse. He crouched down gently and looked at her. 'Your mother, extremely young and naive as she was, may just have died for this world to remain how it is.' The baby looked at Gideon, and she smiled. Was it a smile thought? It was hard to tell with babies. But he softly smiled back anyway. * * * * * * * * * '1.....2.....3!'The officer yelled as he booted down the door. He paused as he aimed the weapon down and seen the vast amount of dead bodies scattered across the floor, and the small baby sitting down amongst them, looking up at them with innocence, not a care in the world. 'Goooooooood God!' Good God indeed.
