The final score (Part 1)

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 11, 2002 [“Bright light city gonna set my soulGonna set my soul on fireGot a whole lot of money that's ready to burn,So get those stakes up higherThere's a thousand pretty women waitin' out thereAnd they're all livin' devil may careAnd I'm just the devil with love to spareViva Las Vegas, Viva Las Vegas…” This is the thought running through Eddie Mandrake’s mind as he drives on I-515 heading into the City of Sin. Eddie decided to take Quaid’s advice and go for his final score. A final $10,000,000 score! Eddie, driving a blue Subaru WRX, exits the highway near the Vegas Strip. A few minutes later, and he arrives at Caesar’s Palace. He steps out of the car, wearing a “got blood?” no sleeve t-shirt, and faded jean shorts. He pulls his bag out of the trunk, and throws the keys to the valet. He walks into the hotel, carrying his stuff, and disregarding the bellhops’ feeble attempts to carry the bag. Eddie is trying really hard to restrain himself from using one of the bellhop’s arms as a paintbrush, and painting the walls blood red. He reaches the concierge desk. The goofy looking clerk has one of those “I’m going to swindle you hard” looks. Eddie drops his bag by his side, but a crafty bellhop picks it up. Eddie, through the corner of his eye sees him taking the bag…and clobbers him with a roundhouse right. The bellhop drops to the floor leaving a small blood streak on the white tile. He turns back around to the desk with a smile on his face.] Eddie: Reservation for Mandrake? Concierge: Ah, yes. We have you in Room 1701 in the Forum Tower. [The Concierge hands the keys over to Eddie. Another Bellhop tries to pick up the bag, and ends up getting a kick square in the jaw while he’s bent over. Eddie picks up his bag, and runs for the elevator hoping to avoid more bellhops. The bellhops are in hot pursuit, as Eddie is literally sprinting across the ceramic tile floor. He is coming up fast to the elevator, and just stops running. He slides across the floor like an ice skater gliding across the ice. The door is coming up fast, and it is still closed. For what seemed eternity, the door finally begins to open. Eddie flies into the elevator, and hits the “17” button. The bellhops continue to sprint to the elevator. The door closes…all that can be heard are massive thuds as the bodies pile up at the base of the Lobby Elevator door.] Eddie: (Breathing Heavily) Whew. Those bellhops have sticky fingers. I hope I don’t meet any more of them. [The elevator reaches the seventeenth floor, and sure enough. There is a group of three other bellhops, waiting at the door. Now, they’ve decided to arm themselves with crowbars. As soon as the door opens, all three decided to strike. Mandrake, knowing that they’ll try some team effort instinctively, jumps over the team, grabs a fire extinguisher, and whips it at the bellhops. They all catch it, and the elevator door closes right behind them. Eddie now, after that workout, makes his way to his room, opens the door. A very posh room when the door opens up. Something that you would receive when you order “The Emperor’s Package”, except Eddie just used his savings from bounty hunting to pay for the room. Eddie heads for the bedroom, and places his bag onto the dresser. He looks over briefly, and sees a note. He opens it. It reads… Edward… Meet me in the casino, at the poker room in one hour after you get this. If you look in the night table, you’ll see a little something that will keep you busy while you wait for me (If you would like to wait in the casino that is.) Your future employer. Eddie, after reading it, looks in the night table. He reaches in and pulls out a twenty dollar bill. Eddie thinks one thing, other than gambling.] Eddie: (Thinking) ROOM SERVICE! [Upon greater reflection, he decides to wait down stairs in the casino. He opens the door to his room, and this time, to his surprise, NO BELLHOPS! He casually closes the door, and locks it and heads down to the casino. A Few Minutes Later [We see Eddie heading for the door of the casino, when a security guard stops him at the door.] Security: Can I see your I.D.? Eddie: Sure. [Eddie hands him the I.D. The guard looks it over and looks at Eddie. He hands it back.] Security: In you go. [Eddie enters the casino, and sees a mosaic of games that bring Caesar’s Palace to life. With a rather large bank of slot machines to his east, card tables to his west, craps and roulette to his north, Eddie is a little overwhelmed by everything. He heads for a vacant one-dollar slot machine. It’s the Double Diamond Progressive Machine. He studies the machine rather intently. He studied the handle, the money slot, the winnings hopper, everything short from dissecting the machine itself. He then puts the twenty-dollar bill into the money intake slot. The “Credit” display shows that he has twenty credits now. He now hits the “Bet Max” button, and the reels spin. They spin, and spin, and spin some more. The first stops, Double Diamond. The second stops, Double Diamond. The third stops…DOUBLE DIAMOND! At that point the machine makes a lot of noise, and flashes of light.] Eddie: WAAAGH! WHOA! [Eddie flies off the stool and ends up back first on the carpeted floor. It looks like a standard judo break fall. He’s looking at the lights at this very moment. He looks to his left, and to his right…out of the corner of his eye, he notices a pair of spiffy looking loafers. Eddie adjusts his sight to see a rather large man with wearing all black with a very small gold pin on his lapel.] Large guy: Congratulations, you just won a good…eh, twenty-five thousand dollars. Eddie: Really? Help my ass up and let me look. [The large man helps Eddie to his feet. He looks at the digital display, and sees the credits still racking up.] Eddie: Wow. I got Twenty-five grand after twenty bucks. Large guy: You my friend have what we have “A horseshoe up your ass.” Eddie: I’m lucky. It wouldn’t be the first time. Large guy: Perhaps. Eddie: You my contact? Large guy: Yup. Follow me, and I’ll take you to the boss. Eddie: Can I get my money first? Large guy: If you get your money, I’ll give you the escort to the bank myself. Anyway, I’ll get security to hold the machine, and your cash until my employer finishes with you. Eddie: Well…Since you put it that way… [Eddie, now with a 7’ 3” escort is taken from the floor headed to his next destination.]
