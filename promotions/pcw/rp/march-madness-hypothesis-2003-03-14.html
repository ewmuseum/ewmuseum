March Madness - - Hypothesis

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14 March 2003 Origin, the beginning, call it what you will but the genesis of Gaz “The Jester” Green is simply another tale of somebody’s relentlessness to get into the wrestling profession. In truth I can’t really understand why anybody would wish to pursue such a venture, first and foremost there is the physical abuse from taking countless bumps and then there’s the emotional warfare between two parties. The wrestling business soars either side of the emotion spectrum, from the highs of being a figurehead and champion to the heartache of loss and deceit. Fatigue is a huge issue and the use of painkillers is essential so why do we, the superstars of Primetime Championship Wrestling continue, ‘The lavish life-style?’ ‘The opportunity to be in the public eye?’ ‘The prayer that one day you can be deemed World Heavyweight Champion?’ There’s no denying the fact that all of the above play a substantial part in ones reasons for persevering in this game. Even the lowest of the low are fairly well treated and take home a salary that would place them above those regarded as Middle Classed thus offering their families a fruitful life. For some, the wrestling industry is the only way they can break into the spotlight and appear in the public eye like an icon. Many of the rosters are far from literal geniuses and can hardly grasp the English language so without brain comes brawn and to be a key figure in this profession that latter maybe the only trump card within their arsenal. An example of such would be Duke Williams or to the masses, ‘The Malice Man’. He may not be the most articulate person to ever grace a microphone, however he does his talking within the confines of the squared circle and as a seasoned veteran he can put up his hand in honour and proclaim that he has done it all. As a former World Champion he has been right across the spectrum of emotion from the pressure of being on top to the solitude of being in the gutter right at the bottom. Duke Williams may not be the next Steven Hawking and heaven forbid, but he has worked his way to the top by clawing forward and paying his dues, As mentioned earlier, without brains you can always rely on brawn but a bigger tool than those two combined is heart and with this, anything is possible. Nothing is impossible, only improbable but with determination and heart anyone, no matter stature or size can accomplish their goal. So by adding these pieces of information to the equation don’t be shocked by the aftermath of March Madness, even if Jason Snow is overthrown by somebody like Gaz “The Jester” Green. To hear Clay Clayborne announcing your name as the next World Heavyweight Champion is the glimmer of hope all those involved in the Main Event will be holding out for. A thirty man battle royal where there is no favourites, there is no under dogs and everyman for themselves. It is the magnitude of this event that will determine ones loyalties as relationships evaporate and the durability of friendships are tested as each combatant tries to etch their name in stone. It is opportunities like these that make the suffering worth it. Within the thirty names there are established individuals who have already graced the World scene and there are those who may never will but in an event like this, anything can happen. And with so many people boiling over with bad blood then March Madness may see the feuds erupt, which to most can be a disruption and it is with these types of disruptions that back doors emerge. Such interruptions can benefit a sole entity, begging the question of, ‘Who?’ Who is the million-dollar question? This entire event oozes talent so the verdict is open house and many would be rooting for the Neo Void God Akiyama whilst others may opt for Duke Williams as he makes his last stand in open country. However a force not to be overlooked is that of The Front Office, which will be well represented by four times Tag Team Champions Pranks With Attitude and more importantly the devil incarnate Xavier Kannon. ‘With so much on the line can this alliance survive?’ In a perfect world the triple threat would tactically eliminate the competition and leave the outcome to the survival of the fittest but come Sunday you can be rest assured that the twenty-seven other combatants will have something to say about this. March Madness will be a case of swings and round-a-bouts for The Front Office, Pranks With Attitude Already well documented is the inner conflict of Pranks With Attitude, so with the World Heavyweight Championship at their mercy will their squabbling resurface. Both Gaz Green and Nascosto Ireul Nerezza already have battled one another through two barbaric Pay-Per-Views, stipulated by a Special Guest Referee and First Blood. Headlining under the ‘P.W.A’ banner the devious duo may continue to combine their efforts thus leaving Xavier Kannon out in the cold, which may cause a rift within the Front Office faction. During the teams Asylum tenure they attempted to thwart the tyranny of Xavier Kannon so there is definitely past heat but present priorities must be to relieve themselves of their current thorn, Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. Xavier Kannon There are those who do the walking and there are those being walked upon, Xavier Kannon is certainly the latter. In truth the Scientologist wouldn’t think twice of eliminating his current crop of stable mates in Pranks With Attitude but entering this thirty man mix he already has the attention of Akiyama who has relentlessly tormented the former Primetime Championship Wrestling owner. As he carries the weight of being stalked by the Neo Void God, this may inadvertently open the doors for Pranks With Attitude to stab their business partner in the back, or for a better analogy, throw his battered and bruised carcase over the top rope. Nascosto Ireul Nerezza The vampire. The silent brooding type. Not the most sociable person his alliance alongside Gaz Green may play a factor in one of the members of Pranks With Attitude becoming a World Champion but he will have to do so by going through The Schmoopies Chris Storm. Even the silent types have their enemies but what makes the behemoth a plausible winner is his ability to morph into a goliath, a transformation that shocked the wrestling world as he battled through the odds to retain in PCW’s first ever ‘No Ladder Match’. Gaz Green Let’s not beat around the bush, the Jester has trod on many a superstar’s toes and will most likely be a marked man the moment his feet make contact with the canvas. His verbal diarrhoea possibly could see him tangle with Chet King, his arsenal of tricks have almost certainly won him an enemy in Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor, and there’s the countless other victims who have fallen foul. However maybe through this bombardment of assassins he may fluke a result, stranger things have happened, ‘Syko The Goblin becoming PCW owner, anybody?’ Really the moral to this story is that; come March Madness, don’t be surprised to see the victor as somebody you ignorantly overlooked…
