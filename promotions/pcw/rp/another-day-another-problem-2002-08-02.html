Another Day Another Problem

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.08.02 At Turmoil Duzza competed in what was probably the easiest match of his career. A simple beating of the now fired Refugees made a easy for win for Duzza. What happened after match was the problem. Mark Makonie, evil neo-nazi scum and tag team partner of Duzza, reached his hand out to Duzza, making Duzza believe he had seen the light, was no longer evil nazi scum. But Duzza was tricked by the nazi and hit Duzza with a brutal clothesline then kicked him while he was down. The main problem though, is that Duzza has to team up with Makonie again on Fusion, against Duzza’s former homie Rush Mason and new boy Blayze. It is with thoughts of how Makonie and him will work together that plague Duzza’s simple mind as he walks through the Dallas Airport, worn out from his grueling 6 hour flight from Haiti. He signs a autograph for one single fan, before walking to the rental car desk at the airport. Duzza is dressed in a pair of very baggy white slacks, a black plain basketball jersey that is very baggy and tanned Timberland boots. His hair is set back in long cornrows and looking quite worn out, Duzza is carrying one big bag over his shoulder that has all his clothes in and is carrying a smaller back in his hand that contains the most important things to him, like a basketball and RnB CDs. Duzza walks to the car rental desks and while pulling out his wallet, he is about to talk to the woman at reception when another man, rudely interrupts Duzza just before he can speak Guy: “I would like a rental car please, any car, just now” Duzza: “Hold up sunshine, I was here befo’ you” It is then Duzza turns to the man, who is a muscular white guy, who is about the same height as Duzza. He is dressed in a white t-shirt, blue jeans and has a white baseball cap on his head. The two men instantly recognize each other. Duzza: “MAKONIE!” Makonie: “DUZZA!” The woman at the desk then decides to speak. Lady: “I’m glad you two are friends, because there is only one car left for rental, so it appears you will have to share” Duzza: “Friends? We ain’t friends” Makonie: “I ain’t sharing the car with this fool” Duzza: “Is this a joke? This is a freakin’ set up ain’t it? OK, jokes over, the hidden cameras can stop filming now, I know what’s going on” Lady: “I’m sorry, I don’t understand” Makonie: “I don’t belive this. First I have to team with you, then I have to sit next to you on a plane to Haiti and now I have to share a car with your black ass” Duzza: “Cracker, fuck you, you ain’t sharin’ no car with me, I got here first, the car is mine, all mine, so get to walkin’ white boy” Makonie: “Don’t white boy me, black boy. I spoke to this woman before you did, I asked before the car first, that means you get the car” Duzza: “Fuck you, this car is mine. Your ugly racist ass can hitchhike to the arena for all I care, now get outta my face fool” Makonie: “What’s wrong Duzz? Don’t want to share a car with the white boy who kicked your ass?” Duzza: “Peckerwood, you ain’t no thing but a chicken wing. You got lucky that’s all, caught my ass off guard, next time you try something like that I’ma be prepared and kick bitch slap you back to Europe” Makonie: “You sure do talk a good game Duzz, shame you can’t back up all them words” Duzza: “I’ma do more than back my words up, cracka, I’ma back you ass into a blender you fuck with me again” Makonie: “Ahh I’m sick of your shit, you ain’t gonna ever do anything Duzza” Duzza: “Oh is that right? You best watch your ass on Friday then dawg, don’t forget you in the ring with three nigga’s” Makonie: “And don’t you forget that one of them blacks participated in that three on one beating you got the other week and the other black is his lackey” Duzza: “Ahh like I give a fuck! Blayze will be gone faster than Thug roC was and Rush don’t bother me. Rush fears me, I could break his black ass faster than a klansman in the hood” The lady chooses this time to interrupt Lady: “Umm excuse me, but do you want this car, because if you don’t make your minds up soon, I’ll give it to the next person” Duzza: “Shut up bitch” Makonie: “Yeah, bitch, shut it” A look of shock and disgust comes across the ladies face. She quickly signals for two security guards two come over. The lady tells them to escort Makonie as Duzza, who are two busy arguing to notice the security guards. The guards are massive mountain of men, they effortlessly pick up both Duzza and Makonie and walk them towards the exit Duzza: “Fuck you doin’? Put me down mother fucker” Makonie: “Oi, do you know who we are?” Duzza: “Look what you got us into this time cracker” Makonie: “Me? You called her a bitch” The two security guards dump Makonie and Duzza outside of the airport just as a bus is pulling up Makonie: “I don’t fucking belive this you got me kicked out of a airport” Duzza: “Yeah well…you get used to it after the fifth or sixth time it happens. See you at the arena” Duzza then gets up, picks up his bags and jumps onto the bus Makonie: “WHAT? You can’t leave me here” Duzza: “Then get on the bus fool” Makonie: “Duzz… I hate you” Duzza: “I know… I hate you too” With that, Makonie gets on the bus with Duzza as it pulls away, where it is heading nobody actually knows, but most probably it’s not going where Duzza and Makonie want it to go…
