Visiting The DeRidder Museum

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th of July, 2002 We are greeted with the smiling face of Denis McGee; one of the curators at the DeRidder Museum located, of course, in DeRidder, Louisiana. Mr McGee is surrounded, obviously since this is a museum, by many highly treasured relics from ancient pasts. Just from gazing around for a while we can pick up the timelines of certain exhibits. In a glass case towards the very far side of the room, for example, it is plain to see a huge yellowing bone, posed in an apparently ‘scary’ manner…it is of course a lizard-like skeletal arm, from the Jurassic period. Mr McGee walked next to someone whose shape was currently silhouetted by the sun shining through the entrance of the museum, an entry entirely made up of glass and silvery metal. Mr McGee: “Well, sir, its certainly an honour to have you with us…and indeed a pleasure for all of the historical society and fans of yours alike to get this once in a lifetime chance to see you opening the new Egyptian wing of the DeRidder museum!” The two men moves past the entrance and the mystery man was quickly revealed to us as ‘The Pharaoh’ Titu Xansiv, an Egyptian who has recently had troubles in the wrestling industry with a Scientologist … Xavier Kannon: a name that Pharaoh knows oh too well, and with unfortunate results. Concussed at the hands of the vicious Kannon, Pharaoh was thereafter rushed to the nearest hospital where he was treated with a slight concussion and had to have six stitches to close up the slash on his forehead. The Pharaoh: “It is indeed an honour for me, too, Mr McGee. Now, if you don’t mind, could I possibly take a look at some of the recently brought in artefacts for the new wing?” Mr McGee smiled thoughtfully and then waved a hand in the direction of a dreary cream cloth hanging down heavily over what appeard to be a doorway. Mr McGee: “Of course, of course! Right this way, Mr Xansiv.” Mr McGee appeared to be a man who didn’t get out much. He was growing older each day and though he wasn’t old enough to have developed it yet, he had gained a shuffle in his walk. A pair of thick-rimmed spectacles were constantly at the brink of teetering off the bridge of his nose, yet somehow they never did, and to add to the look, his hair was going grey at the tips and a bald spot had already begun to evolve on the back of his head. Mr McGee: “So will anyone else be joining you tomorrow at the opening?” Pharaoh considered this for a short time, and then at length said: The Pharaoh: “Only my wife, I’d expect.” Dennis smiled, as though reflecting on a time, long past. Mr McGee: “Get along, do you, you and the wife?” Titu immediately noticed the dismissal of the word ‘your’. Instead Mr McGee had substituted it with ‘the’. This was a sure sign of the past, Dennis McGee’s past. The Pharaoh: “Yeah…we get along very well…it’s been almost-” Pharaoh paused; his face became a portrait of a very eccentric mix of expressions. Sadness and anger flowed through his visage like water over a waterfall. The Pharaoh: “-Six…years since we first…met.” He said it as though he didn’t wish to, and Dennis noticed this. Mr McGee: “Something wrong, Mr Xansiv?” Pharaoh fell silent for a moment and then abruptly snapped out of it. The Pharaoh: Oh, no, no… Mr McGee nodded, not entirely pleased with Pharaoh’s response, and then continued his walk towards the grubby cream cloth hanging over the entrance of the new wing. He flung it aside as he got within arms length of the material and revealed the new wing. Pharaoh peered into the darkly lit area and strained to see the new artefacts. Mr McGee: “Sorry about the lighting, Mr Xansiv…” McGee walked towards one of the far walls of the new wing, as though searching for something. He was obviously a man who loved the place dearly; he knew it like the back of his aged hand… He flicked a switch and a few distorted lights flickered on. The Pharaoh: “Excellent! That’s much better…” Pharaoh looked around. Much of the area was finely crafted and upon every flat surface a glass cabinet was found, airtight and concealing a relic of ancient Egypt’s past. Pharaoh noticed a mask of Tutankhamun; he smiled remembering his great heritage. Mr McGee: “Beautiful isn’t it? The mask of Tutankhamun!” The Pharaoh: “Yes…beautiful it truly is, Mr McGee…” Pharaoh’s concentration on the mask was broken abruptly as Mr McGee shuffles loudly towards a huge sarcophagus at the far end of the Wing. Pharaoh grinned, revealing his pearly white teeth, before following the curator to the huge coffin-like structure, a look of amazement protruding his face. The Pharaoh: “Is this the--…” McGee smiled in a cocky manner. Mr McGee: “Yes, it is, indeed! The Sarcophagus of Amun-Ra!” Pharaoh slowly extended a hand to touch the brilliant thing. Many gold’s and royal blue’s washed each other in a bath of priceless gold that is the sarcophagus, the museums prized possession. Pharaoh suddenly recoiled his arm and turned to Mr McGee. The Pharaoh: “The people who come to see this will be amazed, Mr McGee! Even I myself, a great archaeologist, am amazed…” Mr McGee: “Yes, when it was brought in I knew it’d be the star attraction - the big things always are…” Pharaoh walked further into the new wing on the DeRidder Museum. His thoughts were overworked with the image of Kannon at present… Xavier Kannon - the man who beat up Isiy last Tuesday on Turmoil, the man who unfairly beat Titu that very day too – would soon learn his lesson… Pharaoh hated him…he despised him…he wanted Kannon dead for what he did to Isiy! But at least tomorrow he’d get a chance to relax for a day or two before Friday when he’d have to actually fight Xavier in a Chair on a Pole match. Mr McGee: “I’m going to go and brew some tea, Mr Xansiv, will you be alright here for a moment?” Pharaoh looked up from the mummified corpse he was studying. The Pharaoh: “Yeah…I’ll be fine.” Mr McGee: “And shall I brew a cup for you, too?” The Pharaoh: (Turning back to the mummy) “No, I’m fine for now.” Pharaoh looked back down at the mummies around him. He sighed. Why had Kannon come back to haunt Titu after such a long time? Kannon said it was because he’d never pinned Pharaoh before but that didn’t make any sense! What Xavier had done to him on Turmoil was wrong, and as Pharaoh had stated on Fusion Xavier would pay for what he’d done! Around him were the things of his great country’s past. On this Friday’s Fusion he’d defeat Kannon in Egypt’s name…and in the name of his loyal fans! Voice: “And who might you be?” Pharaoh looked up to see the strict face of another Curator standing with his head held high and a snobby expression splashed upon his long, horse-like face. Curator: “Don’t you know that this area is out of bounds to the public?” Pharaoh stood at his full height. He was at least half a foot taller than the man in front of him. The Pharaoh: “I’m Titu Xansiv, the man who’ll be opening this wing to the public tomorrow…now, I believe, the question is ‘who are you?’” Pharaoh already didn’t like this guy. It is said that first impressions count, and in this case the curators first impression hadn’t given Pharaoh a very good notion of him at all. Curator: “I’m Howard Franklin, head administrator of the Egyptian ward. It’s a pleasure to meet you Mr Xansiv…at last.” Franklin held out a hand, expecting a handshake from Titu. Pharaoh didn’t return the gesture; he simply smiled a lopsided grin. As though it never happened, Howard pulled his arm back and pushed his hands into his pockets. The Pharaoh: “It should be an excellent day tomorrow, Mr Franklin.” Howard Franklin: “Yes…that’s very true. The new artefacts are splendorous finds.” Pharaoh nodded, looking at a glass jar shaped as a cat. He knew that inside the pot would be an organ, shrivelled and decayed. Judging from the hieroglyphics carved into the small thing it would appear to be the heart. Mr McGee walked back into the room, a tiny cup filled with steaming hot tea was found in his hand. He took a sip. Mr McGee: “Oh, Howard! I see you’ve met Mr Xansiv, then?” Howard turned and looked Mr McGee up and down in a disgusted manor. Howard Franklin: “Yes…I have…” Pharaoh noticed the way Franklin reacted to McGee. It was quite strange; he’d try not to get within a three-foot diameter of Dennis, in a way that’d suggest he thought McGee was carrying a deadly virus. The Pharaoh: “Dennis, you were just explaining to me that the sarcophagus would be tomorrow’s main attraction. Yes?” Mr McGee: “Yes, that’s-“ Howard Franklin: Yes that’s right…it’s the sarcophagus of- Pharaoh had had enough of this guy. The Pharaoh: Yeah, I know…the sarcophagus of Amun-Ra… Franklin seemed a little taken back. Howard Franklin: Yes that’s right…I didn’t know wrestlers were so…educated. The Pharaoh grinded his teeth, anger and annoyance flowing through him. The Pharaoh: “Actually I’m a very well educated Archaeologist, and I was born in Egypt into a highly wealthy family, Mr Franklin. You’d be surprised by what you don’t know!” Once again Franklin seemed a little taken back. Howard Franklin: “Oh I see…well then I’m sure you’ll know what THIS is then, do you? Franklin suddenly swung his thin arm down towards large sword…it shimmered brightly in the dull light. The Pharaoh: “It’s a sword decorated in prayers to Seth, god of war.” Howard Franklin raised an arm to Pharaoh’s face, made a slight girlish grunt and walked out of the new wing in a very theatrical way. The Pharaoh: “How can you put up with people like HIM?!” Mr McGee smiled. Mr McGee: “That’s a simple one!” Dennis paused. Mr McGee: “I just ignore him!” Pharaoh smirked realizing that Mr McGee wasn’t so pushed around as he’d first imagined. The scene faded to black…
