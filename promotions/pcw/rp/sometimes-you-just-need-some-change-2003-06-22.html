Sometimes You Just Need Some Change

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/03 Sometimes you just need to get out and get away from your problems. It was too bad for me though that I couldn’t get off this airship and just solve my problem. There was still three weeks left of the airship tour which meant I still had three weeks left of having Gaz right there in the same room harassing me in any way possible. However three weeks for me seemed like eternity, especially when I had two children I had to care for on top of that and be sure to keep away from the influence of Gaz. The last thing I wanted was to have two little pranksters running around the house and putting me through the same hell Gaz had been putting me though. There seemed to be so many problems piling up for me and so few ways to escape for them. Tonight however I would take some time out for myself and take a page out of my opponent for Fusion’s book. If Abner could go out and drink away his worries day in and day out, why not try the same? Sure it wouldn’t solve much and just leaving me feeling like crap in the morning especially considering that I was not the drinking type so the effects would probably only be even worse, but then again I’d wake up feeling like crap anyways just looking up and noticing that the Prince of Pranks was still in the same room as me, so for me it was off to the first deck. “Can I help you?” Asked the woman standing behind the counter flashing me friendly smile that every waitress or anyone who worked with food or drinks seemed to bare with them. It was that type of smile that hid their real problems. Even if they were having a rough day and each and everyone of their customers shouted at them and gave them a small or no tip their job was still to smile and show customer appreciation, sort of like how my job was to go out there and give it my all defending my Television title against Abner no matter what was bothering me. “Yes get me a Margarita or two or even three.” I answered sounding not nearly as the cheerful as the woman that stood before me. “I’m sorry mam but we don’t solve alcoholic drinks here, this is a juice bar and we don’t have the permits to serve any type of alcoholic beverage.” She answered as sincerely as possible “Then how does Abner always manage to have a drink with him?” I questioned not suspiciously or angrily, just curiously and seemingly as confused as to where all of Abner’s drinks came from as the woman. “Perhaps he brought them on board himself.” The woman suggested with seemingly the most logical explanation as to how Abner always seemed to have a drink along with him. “But why do you care so much about getting yourself a drink, you really don’t seem like the drinking type.” “I’m not…but every once in awhile it’s good for a change.” I answered, feeling like I was going against my moral and a well set law, yet I was twenty one and there was nothing illegal about me drinking but then again why should I change my morals now just because of the frustrations of both Gaz and Sivasian, besides isn’t that what they would want me to do anyways? “Perhaps that’s the reason you threw that guy over the stage on that stretcher on Fusion.” She said to make conversation and obviously proving that she followed PCW, whether she was just a small or loyal PCW fan was not apparent yet though. “Well…yeah…not to mention he deserved it.” I answered a bit ashamed of my actions on Fusion considering I hated to sink as low as having to use a weapon to take down my opponent, but then again at the moment I felt I had no other choice if I wanted to walk out of that arena still alive and breathing. “And I suppose change is why you are trying to put Chris behind you.” The woman continued on as my face dropped at the sound of his name. Had I not been gotten used to him being gone it would have made a tears well up inside my eyes and trickle down my cheeks, but I had convinced myself that I had to be strong and realize that if I continued to let his name weaken me like this it would eventually lead to my downfall. “I don’t need to put him behind me; he was the one who left which means he put me behind his mind for whatever reasons.” I answered trying to hold back any sort of memory that would turn around and sink its claws into till I cried out and admitted that I wanted him back here with me. “You know there is no reason you need to deny that you’re still heartbroken over the departure of Chris, I know I’d be heartbroken too if I ever lost someone I cared for as much you seemed to care for him.” The woman tried to relate, but knowing she could never understand how I truly felt inside about all that happened I simply brushed off her words and walked away, not wanting to hear another word about him so that I could avoid a river of tears. “Wait, don’t go yet!” The woman said as she ran out from behind the counter and ran to catch up to me before I could reach the stairs that would lead back down to the third deck where my room was located. Instead of saying anything knowing that I would instead just choke and breakdown in front of a total stranger I simply stopped where I was going and turned around to look at her and listen to what she had to say. “I’m sorry if what I said upset you, but if you ever need someone to talk to I’m always here for you. The names Heather.” She said as she extended her hand in friendship. In approval I simply nodded my head to show her that I understood what she was saying and followed through with the same action as her extending my hand an shaking it, signifying a start of a new friendship. I didn’t know much about this girl but she seemed friendly enough and if she could be the one to help me deal with the loss of Chris, then it was all for the better especially since I knew I’d never be able to get over him on my own, but then again it seemed I would never be able to get over him especially now when I just needed a shoulder to cry on with the frustrations of Gaz and Sivasian only seemingly to grow larger as each day went by.
