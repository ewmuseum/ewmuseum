Nature Called, and We Put It On Hold #2

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/10/02 The camera fades in… A still scene from the previous Chef promo is shown with Manuel and the Chef chatting about the disappearance of Bertha. Narrator: Welcome back to another ridiculously stupefying Chef promo! Last time we saw the Chef, him and his adopted son Manuel Hung were decided what they were going to do to find Bertha, who had plunged hundreds of feet down a waterfall into the wilderness. The still scene begins to play… Manuel: We’ve got to find mommy, Chef… The Chef: I know little guy, it looks like we are about to venture into the French wilderness. Manuel: Yay, a camping trip! The Chef: Not exactly Manuel. Manuel begins to frown. The Chef: We will have to use our animal instincts to venture into the savage zone of nature, and rescue the probably hysterical Bertha before she gives up on humans and begins mating with bears! A tear falls from Manuel’s left eye. The Chef: Aw heck, let’s roast some marshmallows!!! Manuel: Sweet! I love you Chef!!! The Chef: And Allah loves you Manuel, but he’d love you better if you converted…hmmmm? Manuel: Sorry Chef, I’m an Episcopalian ‘till I die… The Chef sighs, but thinks to himself. The Chef: HeHe, when he sees that it is I that controls the graham cracker and chocolate bar supply, he will sacrifice his faith for S’MORES!!! MUHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! The scene pauses again for the Narrator. Narrator: Let’s continue with the second part of this hilarious Chef promo! Gus? Gus the Video Guy: Got the tape ready, friend. Narrator: Well then do you thing… Gus the Video Guy: Here goes, friend. The scene begins to play. The scene is early morning in the wilderness. The sun is just peaking above the horizon. The birds are out singing, and the bees are flying after them, trying to have sex with them. We see a bush wedged between two gigantic trees. The air in the French Wilderness flies by and brushes the bush ever so slightly… “AAAHHHHH-CHHOOOOOOOO!!!’’ Out from the bush crawls the Chef, with a red runny nose and watery eyes. The Chef: I hate nature…AAAAHHHHH-CHHHOOOOOOO!!! Manuel creeps up behind him. The little chap sniffs the air and breaths it in, letting it invigorate his body. Manuel: I wuv nature… The Chef and Manuel both sport the latest in Islamic Militant camouflage gear. They are equipped with the usual suit, boots, hat, make-up, and canteen; but also have pepper spray, cattle prods, Bowie knifes, Perrier for thirst quenching, “How to be a Suicide Bomber” by Ahmed Ahmed Rasheed Omar, and the much needed comb for the develop Islamic beard. The Chef: Ready to find mommy Manuel? Manuel: I sure am Chef! The Chef: Good, why don’t you go first? The Chef pushes the tyke in front of him and they crept through the woods slowly. One of the bushes rustles and the Chef jumps back and rolls up into a ball, quivering like a frightened Chihuahua. The Chef: What is it!?!? Manuel: It’s a… The Chef: A bear!?!? I hate bears!!! Manuel: No Cheffy, it’s a… The Chef: An elephant!?!? Dear Allah no! The last thing I need is another elephant run in…(The Chef thinks back to Spring Fling and his match against BAS). Manuel: Would you be quiet Chef!?!? The Chef: Oh, ok…what’s in there sport? The creature emerges from the rustling bushes… Bertha: About f*cking time!!! Still dressed in her Perrier Can, the Rhinoceros of Roanoke grabs the Chef by the collar and hoists him up into the air. Bertha: You f*cking have some explaining to do, f*ckface mother f*cker!!! The Chef begins to sweat with anxiousness. The Chef: Ummm, it was an accident…? Bertha’s angry frown quickly turns to smile, and she drops the Chef on the ground. Bertha: Sounds f*cking good to me! When do us c*ck suckers f*cking eat!?!? The Chef: Um well dear, we came to save you, so all we’ve got is… Bertha: F*cking what ho @$$!?!? The Chef: Trail mix and Perrier, that’s everything!!! Bertha’s smile turns to a look of distrust. Bertha: Are you f*cking telling the queer as f*ck truth home f*cking slice!?!? The Chef: Yea…I mean…nah…um…OK! We have jerky too! Bertha’s eyes light up. Bertha: F*cking turkey jerky!?!? The Chef nods. Bertha: F*cking gimme!!! Bertha rips the Chef’s backpack practically right off his arms, pulls out the plastic tub of Slim Jims and downs all of the beer heart sticks, including their plastic sealed packages. Bertha: F*cking satisfying, what’s for f*cking dinner f*cko!?!? The Chef just sighs. Manuel runs up to Bertha and attempts to give her a hug. She just holds him back with her hand. Bertha: You’ve got f*cking jerky too pansy @$$!?!? Manuel: Yeppers! Bertha: F*cking AWWW… Bertha gives the child a bear hug, but while hugging the lovable young lab, she rips open the top of his backpack, and unearths another tub of Slim Jims. She downs the second tub, plastic and all. She then pushes Manuel aside… Bertha: I’m f*cking hungry, what’s for f*cking din… A loud roar interrupts Bertha’s gluttonous antics. The Chef: What in Mohammed’s moustache was that!?!? A BEAR jumps out of the brush and frightens the entire group. A piece of a shirt hangs from a tooth in the bear’s mouth. The Chef attempts to read it… GRRRRRRR!!!! The Chef: Hmmm, “Perrier Camera Crew”, He ate our camera crew! YIKES!!! Bertha: To the f*cking Bertha Mobile f*ckos!!! The entire party screams and takes off into the wilderness. The leap over semi steep ditches, chop through thick brush, swim across small ponds, and sprain their ankles on large unseen rocks. The finally rest near what seems to be an open field. The Chef: (Catching his breath) I…I…I think we lost him… GRRRRRRR!!! The group screams simultaneously once more and takes off through the field. Manuel: What do they grow here daddy? The Chef: Does it look like I’m a farmer son!?!? No! I just prepare the food, I don’t grow it! The Chef, Bertha and Manuel all scamper off into another forested area, surrounded by evergreen, clear running water, and cute little furry creatures. Bertha: AWWW, a f*cking bunny! Manuel: Look, a gopher! Bertha: F*cking beaver at b*tch @$$ twelve o’clock f*ckface!!! The Chef: Look, a big furry thing with claws! Bertha: That’s a mother f*cking bear… The Chef: Oh yeah…run!!! GRRRRRRRR!!! The trio scampers back through the forest and back to the open field. The Chef: Ok, let’s put our heads together, we can get away from this bear, maybe even sauté his @$$ in a rich Madeira gravy, but we have to do this thing together, are you with me!?!? Bertha puts her hand up in the air. Bertha: F*cking with ya! Manuel does the same. Manuel: I’m dis many years old! The trio’s little powwow is a success, and all three high five each other. The Chef: Let’s go take down that bear!!! CLOCK!!!! Suddenly the Chef rolls his eyes up and falls face first onto the ground. Bertha: What the f*cking c*ck sucking @$$ humping f*ck was that!?!?! “FORE!!!” A man approaches driving a familiar looking cart. He drives up to Bertha and Manuel and steps out. Man: Hi, I’m a professional golfer who would not like to disclose my name for inter sport copyright purposes. Bertha: What the f*ck are you doing in the French Wilderness f*cko!?!? Manuel: Yeah! Golfer: Um, last time I checked, this was a golf course… Bertha: F*cking WHA!?!? The Chef wakes up from his mysterious slumber. The Chef: Good gracious Allah, there’s a lump on my head! The golfer looks to the ground and picks up his lost golf ball; problem solved. Golfer: Sorry about that, I got carried away with my 3 Wood, hit that a little too far to the left if you know what I mean… Say, to make it up to ya, let me buy you a drink at the clubhouse… The Chef: DEAL!!! Bertha holds the Chef back… Bertha: What the f*cking is going on!?!?! I though we were trying to f*cking get away from a b*tch nuggeting bear in the sh*tty as f*ck forest, motha f*cka!!!! The Chef: That’s all changed now; I’ve got free booze!!! Bertha sighs. GRRRRRR!!! Out from one of the forested area emerges the bear that was stalking the couple. Golfer: Quick, to my cart! The trio all sprints to the cart.The Chef, Manuel, and Bertha all slide in, there’s no room for the golfer. Golfer: But this is my cart! You can’t… The Chef: But I can, call this Allah’s will… SCRREEEETTTCCCHHHHH!!!! The Chef steps on the gas, kicks the poor man in the face and speeds off with Bertha and Manuel hanging on for dear life… Golfer: COOOOMMMEEEEE BAAACCCCKKKKKK!!! The Chef: Yeah, and get eaten, sure…I’m coming back! Bertha: We f*cking did a bad f*cking bad f*cking bad thing back there… The Chef: Yeah, I know, we are responsible for the death of a golfer… Bertha: No, we f*cking turned down a free drink! The Chef: Oh, well Bertha…the guy left his wallet here on the cart… Bertha’s eyes light up. Bertha: F*cking b*tch @$$ sweet! The cart speeds away into the horizon towards the clubhouse as the camera mercifully fades to black.
