Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto (my best and longest rp ever, worth a read)

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 OOC: Before anyone says 'this does not relate to you match' let me explain. Baal – He represents Steve JohnsonMephisto – He represents Rip RyckmanDiablo – He represents the rising star champion Lance Bell.And Arcane Sylo – Well that's self explanatory. Also please really read this one, I believe it's one of my best ever. WARNING: NOT FOR THE WEAK AT HEART! Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto – The Three Evil Powers: During the mid-twelfth century, after the Church of Zakarum had gained prominence in the East, the church decreed that the visions of Akarat would be spread throughout the known world in order to redeem the masses. Thus, the church selected a group of its most charismatic and devoted priests and sent them on a mission to proselytize the people of the west. Unfortunately the church had not prepared these men for the rigors of travel or the hazards of the world. Those priests who survived their missions recounted tales of harsh weather, inadequate supplies, attacks from bandits and even encounters with horrible monsters. To ensure the success of future missionaries. These 'Protectors of the word' proved to be more successful at converting the native peoples than the priests they were assigned to defend. Impressing the locals with daring deeds, powerful weapons, and martial prowess was far more convincing than the condemnations of a soft-spoken monk. However, once the word had been spread to every major city of the west, the 'Protectors of the Word' faded from public view. Some decades later, Paladins were again called into service. During the height of the Time of Troubles, the church commenced a second campaign of conversion. This time, however, the unconvincible were deemed to be evil. The Zakarum Inquisition spread through the lands like a tempest, laying waste to all suspected of demonic possession or corruption. Leading this crusade was a new generation of Paladins, known as the 'Hand of Zakarum'. These cavalries of righteousness swept through the lands, expunging the taint of demonic contamination wherever they found it. In the midst of this bloody crusade, a rebellion arose within the ranks of the Paladins of Zakarum. The rebels condemned the methods of the Inquisition, proclaiming that their new order of Paladins should protect the innocent, and that the evil corruption they fought was merely evidence of their forebear's failure. They resolved to fight the true source of corruption, the Three Prime Evils – Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto. And so, these rebellious Paladins left their Zakarum brethren and ventured west. (A hoard of men traveled west each wearing a godly plate of armor. Their swords were at a halt on their sides and large shields laid resting on their backs. One stood out of the crowed, due to the fact he road ahead and was taller, bigger, and had a menacing look to him. His hair was dark black, as black as a thousand midnights. On his chin he had a patch of hair which matched the coal black color of his hair. His body was covered with armor which glimmered in the fading sunlight. To his side a huge claymore ready to see action rested. The hoard seemed to follow him as if he was the leader of this band. No man spoke a word, not even a sound caressed their lips. The current surroundings were rocky with sands carpeting the ground. Dead trees and various bones which had been sucked down by the sand laid as a reminder of all the pain which had once ventured through. Their travels were long, they had traveled for days now only resting at night, A mission is what they were on. One of the men on the journey rode up next to the one who appeared to be the leader, he then began to speak in a whisper afraid that anything louder would upset the gods.) 'Arcane Sylo, Do you really think the Three powers are behind this?' The rider said in such a low voice that if you weren't listening you wouldn't hear. 'Flail, we have journeyed a great deal together and I have never felt evil this strong. I do believe it is the three which have captured the Rising stars and are holding them for their own greed. If we do not stop them soon then all will be lost,' Arcane Sylo said looking to Flail. (He only nodded and fell back into rank. The one known as Arcane Sylo continued to ride forward. He was a determined man who had lost his family a long while back. He turned to the Paladins for refuge and the monks soon taught him the ways of the church of Zakarum. They knew even as a young boy he was special, that's why they did everything in their power to protect him. He rose through the ranks quickly soon becoming the head Paladin. He was the one responsible for destroying the first rise of Diablo. It was quit a battle but in the end Arcane Sylo banished Diablo back into the depths of hell. Diablo would have remained there but his two evil brothers spilled the blood of the Necromancers protecting the shell and unleashed their evil brother once more.---- The band continued to ride finally making it to the edge of the west mountains. Each man looked up not sure of what awaited them on the other side. The mountains were cold and gray, spots of blood covered the sides as a reminder of the pain others had went through. They were headed for the Necromancer's old camp where the shell of Diablo once lived. They knew that if they were to find him he would be near the shell because that's where his power dwelled.) 'Stay alert men, never let your guard down because the minions of Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto roam the mountain side keeping intruders out. Stay with me and you will live, fall behind and you will parish,' Arcane Sylo said as he began to ride up the mountain. (Each man followed behind and soon they found themselves in the thick of the mountains. Darkness lurked around like a predator but each man stayed calm. Light never touched the black soil here and Arcane Sylo knew there was danger here. Quickly he came to a dead halt and held his hand up signaling for the rest of his troops to hold still and not say a word. Slowly Arcane Sylo demounted his horse and withdrew his massive sword, the sword of the gods.) 'Come out fowl beast, I can smell your evil and I can taste your blood now reveal yourself!' Arcane Sylo yelled into the darkness. (Just then from the east roared out a few beastly figures. Their skin was a dim gray, each held either an axe or a sword with blood splattered on the blade. Arcane Sylo snarled a small bit as the demons snarled back showing their razor sharp teeth as their eyes glowed a hell red.) 'Intruder you will dieeeeee!' Snarled what appeared to be the head demon. (More demons emerged, a good twenty of them at least. Quickly the band of men dismounted their horses and withdrew their weapons. Arcane Sylo pointed his sword forward commanding the men to charge. Arcane Sylo ran toward the demons and quickly threw the god like sword through the gray skin of the demons. Blood flew all around as he dug the sword deep, cutting the bone as demons yelled out in dying pain. The other men began to attack, some doing the killing and others being killed. Arcane Sylo was now in war mode as he began kicking and slicing through Demons like a man possessed. He let out a bloody roar as he charged toward the head demon. The demon's blood red eyes expanded as it watched the sword come flying down splitting it's head in half. Arcane Sylo snarled and kicked the dead, limp body of the head demon aside, the remaining demons, noticing their leader was dead, scattered back into the blackness. Arcane Sylo paused and looked, he had lost three troops out of thirty. He nodded to the rest of the troops who all had the adrenaline pumping throughout their bodies.) 'Burry them, they died a warriors death and they deserve a warriors burial,' Arcane Sylo announced to the rest of the troops. (Soon they were buried. Each of the remaining warriors surrounded the graves as Arcane Sylo said an ancient Zakarum prayer for the fallen. He drove each mans sword down into the head of the dirt leaving it as a headstone. Not another word was passed between the men. The Paladins had come to know death as a right not a curse. Death was the ultimate goal but you would only have the rewards if you lived your life to the fullest and lived your life right. Only the top Paladin soldiers who learned the art of Valirium magic could move on and have the right to move possibly into the throne of the gods right hand man. Arcane Sylo had learned the art of Valirium magic and he knew it would come in use against the battle ahead. After a night of rest the men saddled back up and began to ride through the forest once more. The only sounds were that of the horses foot steps banging into the earth below. Soon though, they stopped as Arcane Sylo dismounted his horse once more. Laying on a near by rock was a Necromancer who was badly injured. Arcane Sylo moved over to him to check the wounds. He was close to death and needed the power of Valirium magic. Arcane Sylo placed his hand over the fatal wound and began to chant an ancient Valirium hymn as a blue aura moved around his hand. Soon the wound closed and the Necromancer was back to normal.) 'Thank you kind sir,' The Necromancer said looking up. (Arcane Sylo only nodded.) 'Your a Paladin aren't you?' The Necromancer asked. 'Yes, I am Arcane Sylo the leader of this band. We are headed west to finish off Diablo, Ball, and Mephisto. This time I will end it,' Arcane Sylo replied. 'Wait, your the man who banished Diablo the first time correct?' The Necromancer was curious. 'Yes, I banished Diablo back to hell the first time. I should have destroyed him but no I decided to banish him. This time he will not get off that easy but tell me Necromancer...' Arcane Sylo was cut off. 'Chrome,' The Necromancer announced. 'Excuse me?' 'Chrome is my name, I was at the shell when Ball and Mephisto unleashed Diablo. We all banded together trying to unleash our magic to stop them but something was different, they were more powerful than we ever imagined. Soon all the Necromancers were dead except for me, I managed to escape before they killed me. They almost succeeded until you showed up and healed me....thank you Arcane Sylo,' Chrome said still grateful. (Chrome was a medium sized man with a medium build. He had silver hair and wore armor made of bones. In his left holster he had a dagger which was common of the Necromancers. His hair was pulled back in a pony tail and his eyes were a dim gray. On his back he had a dark blue cape which was soaked in blood.) 'I help the good and the Necromancers did help with the downfall of Diablo the first time. Tell me is Diablo and his brothers still located near the shell?' Arcane Sylo asked. 'Yes, last time I was there he had resurrected his palace from hell. There are many demons surrounding it he and his brothers each rule a level of the palace, Diablo is at the top,' Chrome replied. 'I can take you there if you like, I mean I do owe you my life,' (Arcane Sylo nodded as one of the troops brought a spare horse. Chrome mounted and the men began to ride off. Not a word was spoken as they traveled but soon they were at the very top of the mountain looking down. What they saw was a horrible sight. At the bottom of the mountain laid the dead bodies of thousands and thousands of Necromancers. Fires burned and the land had been turned into a barren waste land. Though a good distance away, the palace of Diablo and his brothers could be seen. It was dark and cold, not even in your worst nightmare could you imagine it. Blood soaked the walls and dead bodies laid on spikes, almost like ornaments on a Christmas tree. The Palace seemed to scrape the dark, blood red sky as rain poured down onto the land turning the blood of the Necromancers into a river of death. Chrome began to shake, enraged at the sight. Arcane Sylo looked over to Chrome and placed a hand on his shoulder.) 'Do not worry, they will pay.....Diablo and his brothers will pay for their sins,' Arcane held a look of promise in his eyes. 'Men, the battle ahead will be long and hard. I do not want to drag anyone in this that is afraid of dying. Now is the chance to turn back if you want,' (None of the men budged, they were loyal through and through. Arcane Sylo nodded to them and turned his attention to Chrome nodding to him as well.) 'Then let us ride!' Arcane Sylo yelled out. (The men let out cries of battle as they rushed down the mountain at great speeds. Demons could be seen feasting on the dead bodies of Necromancers as the band charged. Arcane Sylo drew his sword and charged letting out yells of battle. The demons looked up only to be decapitated by the on coming sword of Arcane Sylo. More demons popped up. Arcane Sylo leaped from his horse onto one shoving the sword through the black heart of one of the demons. Chrome jumped down and began throwing what appeared to be the bones of the dead from his finger tips, using them as spears. He started to chant a spell as he pulled out what appeared to be a dagger. Skeletons raised from the ground, each holding a weapon as they charged at the demons fighting. The rest of the Paladins fought some getting killed in the process. One demons knocked away the sword of Arcane Sylo. He looked as the Demon swung an axe but quickly he moved and picked up the dagger of a dead Necromancer, impaling the beast. He held him high in the air and threw him toward other demons knocking them down. Chrome continued to shoot the bones of the dead at the demons as some of the skeletons he rose were destroyed but others continued to kill. More demons charged from the north as Arcane Sylo picked up his sword and drove through them like a steam roller. Slicing the blade through each of them turning them into nothing more than piles of rotting flesh. The battle waged on for a good thirty minutes and once over more blood stained the ground, some of the warriors but most was the blood of the demons. Arcane Sylo's sword was now covered with the dark red blood of the demons. He snarled as he wiped the blade off and put the sword up. He looked....he had lost all of his men but about ten. He let out a deep grunt and looked to Chrome who continued to stab dead demons.) 'YOU SORRY BASTARDS!' he yelled. 'YOU KILLED MY FAMILY, YOU KILLED MY FRIENDS!' (Arcane Sylo approached and pulled his new found friend off of the dead body of the demon. He looked him straight in the eyes as warm tears ran down the face of Chrome.) 'I told you they will pay my friend and that they shall. I do not need you to lose it you must be strong because I will need your help,' Arcane Sylo continued to look into Chrome's eyes as he patted him on the shoulder. (Chrome nodded his head up and down as his silver pony tail, now soaked with blood, swayed in the back. The men mounted their horses once more and began to ride toward the palace. The knew danger awaited them at every turn. A thick of forest surrounded the Palace. Arcane Sylo knew this would be a good cover for them to attack. Arcane Sylo had a strange feeling inside, the danger was growing yet he never felt scared. Diablo was a powerful foe and with his brothers he was even more powerful but this still didn't seem to scare the warrior they called Arcane Sylo. Faster and faster they road, almost like some force field was pulling them toward the palace. Soon they found themselves in the thick of the forest, staring out toward the palace. At the palace gate large demons with razor sharp axes and thick, ungodly plates of armor stood. Arcane Sylo looked back to his men concerned for their safety then back to Chrome.) 'Chrome, how far in the art of the Necromancer magic are you?' Arcane Sylo asked. 'Very far my friend,' Chrome replied. 'Good...I need you to surround us with a superior bone shield. Can you do that?' 'I believe so,' Chrome nodded as he said that. (He held his hands in the air and began to chant a small bit. The bones of dead Necromancers began to rise up and make a shield around the Paladin's and himself. Blood dripped off as Chrome turned and signaled to Arcane Sylo that the bone shield was ready.) 'Ok men, we charge at these beast and give them everything we have. Once inside the rest of you will go with Chrome to take out the minions of Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto..' Arcane Sylo was interrupted. 'But what about you Arcane Sylo?' One of the troops asked. 'Do not worry about me, I will destroy Baal, Mephisto, and then I will destroy Diablo. Men, stay alive so that we may feast and drink the blood of these foul creatures! Now let us go!' Arcane Sylo yelled as he began riding off followed by Chrome and the rest of the troops. (Soon they were at the gate attacking the large beast who didn't know what hit them. Arcane Sylo drove his massive sword through the chin of one of the monsters killing him instantly. He picked up the axe of the dead gate keeper and sliced through the chest of the other guard. Soon the troops jumped on and began driving their swords through the heart of the large gate keeper who plunged into the lava below the bridge. The men pushed the doors open and was met by a band of demons. Arcane Sylo chanted a spell which shot ice out freezing the demons long enough for the troops to attack. Quickly, slicing a few demons on his way, Arcane Sylo made his way to a large set of stairs. Demons dropped down but Arcane Sylo quickly cut through them kicking open a large door. There on a throne sat Baal. Baal was a green demon with ice blue eyes. He was large at least ten feet tall and very muscular. On his body he wore the armor of bones, the armor was made out of the toughest warriors he had killed. Baal snarled and stood up quickly.) 'ARCANE SYLO!' He boomed in a deep dark voice. 'Baal, you sorry sack of evil...are you ready to die?' Arcane Sylo said withdrawing his sword. 'No, how about I just destroy you now and feed on your dead corpse. Your bones will surly make fine weapons for me!' Baal said laughing. (Arcane Sylo laughed back which enraged Baal who charged at him stomping the whole way. The ground shook but Arcane Sylo stood still. Baal withdrew a large sword and swung down at Arcane Sylo who rolled out of the way.) 'Is that the best you have beast?!' Arcane Sylo yelled provoking Baal. (Baal let out a loud yell and swung down again. Quickly Arcane started to chant and ice flew from him freezing the arm of Baal. Arcane Sylo leaped slicing it off and showing his sword through the black heart of Baal. Arcane Sylo jumped back and began to chant once more. A large beam of light began to shoot up and finally shot though the skull of Baal sending him to his knees. Baal's blood which was a green color flowed out making a puddle. Arcane Sylo pulled his sword out and snarled.) 'This is for the Necromancers!' (Arcane Sylo quickly slashed the head of Baal off as it rolled off to the side. He picked up a near by spear and stuck the head on top propping it up for all to see. Arcane Sylo snarled once more and dashed to the next set up steps. He kicked the door open and feasting on the dead was Mephisto. Mephisto was a large beast, larger than Baal. He was blue with spikes coming from his body. His eyes glowed yellow and his teeth were at least three feet long and razor sharp. His armor was his own body, the spikes on it were as hard as bone. He was at least twelve feet tall and nothing but muscle.) 'Who dares interrupt me while I feast....Arcane Sylo?! Baal come quick!' Mephisto yelled in a darker voice. 'No use Mephisto, Baal is dead. I killed him and now I will kill you!' Arcane Sylo yelled as he charged at Mephisto. (Mephisto roared as Arcane Sylo continued to charge. Mephisto pulled a large axe and swung it down but Arcane Sylo moved in time. The ground began to shake from the blow but Arcane Sylo held his balance and jumped slicing off the hand of Mephisto. Mephisto let out a roar as he swung his good arm nailing The shield of Arcane Sylo. Arcane Sylo flew into the wall, a bit dazed from the blow. He quickly got to his feet though and charged back. Mephisto Swung his axe but Arcane Sylo leaped over and drove his sword down into the heart of Mephisto. The beast roared and knocked Arcane Sylo off. Arcane Sylo flew back into the wall as the beast known as Mephisto walked forward with the sword sticking from his chest. Arcane Sylo began to let out a chant as Mephisto raised his axe high into the air ready to kill Arcane Sylo but just then small beams of light began to rain from the sky piercing through the skin of Mephisto and killing him. His blood ran down the stairs as Arcane Sylo pulled his Sword from Mephisto's chest. Next was Diablo. Finally after all this time Arcane Sylo would finish off Diablo. He let out a chant which healed all wounds he may have had and his energy was refreshed. ) 'Finally, we will meet,' Arcane Sylo snarled. (He trudged up the stairs leading to the chambers of Diablo. Quickly he kicked the final door open. Standing looking out at the destruction was Diablo himself. He turned and smirked at Arcane Sylo. Diablo wasn't as tall as his brothers nor as big. He wore a black cloak and his face was like a skull. His eyes were sunk back in his head and were the color of fire. The tips of his fingers were sharp and pointed like daggers.) 'So, you managed to kill my brothers? Very good Arcane Sylo I was hoping we would meet again and I would have been oh so sad if my brothers had killed you first. You see I still haven't forgiven you for stopping my reign of terror so long ago but now, now I will end you!' (Diablo shot fire from his finger tips. Arcane Sylo quickly through his sword up dodging them.) 'Ah, ready are we? THEN LETS GO!' Diablo yelled out. (He flew forward nailing Arcane Sylo back into the wall. Slowly he began to choke him but Arcane Sylo nailed him with a foot to the midsection. Diablo moved back a small bit as Arcane Sylo slashed down into the skin of Diablo. He let out a roar and pushed Arcane Sylo back into the wall driving a spiky nail into his shoulder. He clawed his face and rendered Arcane Sylo helpless.) 'So how's if feel to finally be on the receiving end?' Diablo asked. (He shoved a spiky type nail through the other shoulder. He then pulled his finger back and licked the blood) 'So pure, I will love drinking your blood!' Diablo snarled as he raised his hand high. (He was going to deliver the final blow but Arcane Sylo moved his arm driving his sword into the sternum of Diablo. Diablo looked shocked as black blood ran out of his mouth.) 'NOW YOU DIE!' Arcane Sylo yelled. (He kicked Diablo back as Diablo let out a yell which would turn your blood cold. Arcane Sylo sliced down as Diablo's head rolled off holding the look of shock on it. Arcane Sylo shoved his sword in the bottom of his head and looked at the head of Diablo. He snarled as the palace began to shake. Quickly he rushed down the stares, over the dead Mephisto, down more stares darting over the body of the dead Baal. The Palace was sinking back down into hell as Arcane Sylo dove away just in time. He coughed out some dust and looked up, standing before him was Chrome and his warriors. Arcane Sylo stood up and held the sword with Diablo's head on it high. The men all let out cheers as Chrome nodded with a smile on his face.) 'Thank you Arcane Sylo, now the souls of the Necromancers can live in peace,' Chrome said to Arcane Sylo. 'It was my pleasure, and now the rising stars are free,' He said pointing to the sky where the stars now sat dancing their dance of freedom. (The two shook hands as Arcane Sylo threw Diablo's head out for the masses to kick around. The stars seemed to shine down on Arcane Sylo, they proclaimed him their champion.) (Years passed and Diablo's reign of terror never surfaced again. Arcane Sylo passed on as an old man but it is said that now late at night when you look into the stars you can see him holding the head of Diablo as he watches over the sleeping earth below. Always watching...)
