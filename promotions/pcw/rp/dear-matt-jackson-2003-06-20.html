Dear Matt Jackson

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.20.03 The following one and a half page letter will be delivered to Matt Jackson upon next touchdown of the PCW’s Cloud Nine Airship. A nice little secretary with a Rolodex gave me his home address, where the little will be sent, after I shelled out a couple hundred or so dollari. Oh well. The letter will be enclosed in a plain white envelope with no return address. A small sticker will be holding down the back flap, a golden sticker with the initials “SR” on it. Can you guess what they stand for? If you can’t, stop reading now. Dear Mr. Jackson, I can honestly proclaim that there was not a single soul in the PCW Airship more saddened than I to hear of your potential separation from this company. While I may have been disappointed, I was definitely not surprised. I was disappointed upon receiving news of your departure solely because I wasn’t finished with you, Mr. Jackson. While I had successfully made an example out of you for all members of the PCW to see just how Silvio Rochelli works, I feel that I didn’t get to fully demonstrate my capabilities. But, oddly enough, I want to thank you. Thank you for your infinite stupidity. You muddle in muddy waters, something you didn’t know was in there just might bite you. And that’s exactly what happened. You decided to mess with affairs that in no way involved you but you decided to make my business, your business. For whatever reason, you decided to interrupt and attempted to deter me from my rightful spot on Primetime Championship Wrestling television. Perhaps you have a chemical imbalance, or short-term memory loss, but when I first entered the PCW, I guaranteed that anyone who chose to get in my way would be taken down in the most efficient way possible, whether or not it’s ‘ruthless’ or ‘overly aggressive.’ Case in point: Haley Hilton. Once again, thanks to your stupidity and your open friendship, or perhaps relationship with Haley Hilton, I was able to see where I could do the most damage in the shortest amount of time. Efficiency, Matt Jackson, is the key. You’d really expect a so-called ‘CIA Agent’ to conceal his relationships or his identity a little better. Allowing your relationship to be seen on television for all to see was just waiting for someone to come along and do exactly as I did. But that was of no concern to you, was it Mr. Jackson? No, because you are THE Matt Jackson and you can take care of any problem. Or you could until I, Silvio Rochelli, came along. Isn’t that so, Mr. Jackson? I believe it is. From day one, I’ve been one step ahead of you. From day one, you’ve always been playing perfectly as I thought you would. You were a perfect little puppet to a rehearsed puppet master. You performed well doing just what the government trained you to do: to be a pawn. Thank you for my contract with Primetime Championship Wrestling. A move I’m sure you regret by now. You’d never expect that someone new, some ‘just out of PCW rich prick’ would tear down the “mighty empire” of ‘The Agent’ Matt Jackson. I only speak the truth, Matt Jackson. I never once spoke a lie when saying I would win this war, if not the first battle. I never once lied in saying you would be brought down or that it simply ‘wasn’t over’ yet. But you, doing just as your government trained you to do, told a tissue of lies that continued throughout my short career. How you would win, how I was nothing but a rich prick, how I was just out of the PWA and too wet-behind-the-ears to be able to defeat you. Not only did I ultimately defeat you but I’m also the one responsible for eliminating your PCW career. I did what James Edwards couldn’t in five matches, in one. Because I was efficient and decisive in my approach and thanks to your ignorance, you didn’t see my inner-workings as they occurred right under your nose. So, Matt Jackson, I’d also like to thank you for your extreme ignorance. You are shallow. You are the idiot of idiots. Allowing public relationships, missing out on the entire scheme of things, placing yourself on a pedestal and looking down on others without realizing that you are clearly losing the war, and being ignorant enough to miss it all, led to your downfall. By putting Haley Hilton, your supposed love, in a potential situation to get hurt, you were forced to step off the airship in order to protect her. And I’ve eliminated you as a threat. This game is chess Matt Jackson, and you’ve been playing checkers all along. And I also have to wonder… Throughout my short career with the PCW, I’ve heard that you are one of the biggest up-and-coming stars within this wrestling promotion. Is this the best that Primetime Championship Wrestling has to offer? Matt Jackson, are you the best that PCW has to offer? If that is so, consider yourself replaced. So thank you for realizing when you are truly defeated and stepping down and for solidifying my position as one of ‘PCW’s biggest up-and-coming stars,’ having defeated the former. As much as I have to thank you for, Matt Jackson, I can only laugh in your face. For you will be watching me create the empire you only wish you had from your living room and not from the backstage area. You will watch me dominate a ring you used to occupy from a television set in your living room. And it will be rubbed in your face night after night, day after day, as you realize that you went up against Silvio Rochelli and, like I guaranteed, it ruined you. I do no make shallow guarantees Matt Jackson. Thank you for being the example. Sincerely, The man responsible for ending your PCW career,Silvio Rochelli Silvio Rochelli OOC: It's scientific fact the teenage brain doesn't work before noon. It's 8:30.
