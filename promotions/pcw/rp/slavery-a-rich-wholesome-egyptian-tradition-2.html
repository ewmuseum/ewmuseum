Slavery…a rich wholesome Egyptian tradition…

Date: Recently... [Part 2 of 2] ‘Don’t you think they’ll notice?’ said Isiy, her eyes filled with uncertainty. Pharaoh shrugged, looking out into the darkness ahead with a grin. He couldn’t actually see what was going on below, but he hoped for the best. Surely the men and women gathered in the yard wouldn’t have noticed that they were in fact building a pyramid in praise of Xansiv, right? ‘Notice? Ha! They won’t realize a thing, Isiy. Have no fear, my fair beauty for the Gods are with me on this project!’ his voice was more than confident, a little too confident for Isiy’s liking. Deep down, she could feel that something was going to go terribly, terribly wrong. Technically Titu was breaking the law. He’d kept the hopeful wrestling fans in The Palace grounds since lunchtime and now it was clearly verging on nightfall. This was slavery, to an extent, but he’d told them plain and simple that they didn’t have to stay if they didn’t want to. She sighed. ‘What if one of them calls the cops? This is technically against the law…you’re forcing them—’ ‘Forcing them?’ Pharaoh cut in sharply, ‘I’ll hear none of it, my love! Why, they can leave when they want to, I’ve already addressed that!’ he finished with a dismissive wave. Isiy lay back on the bed, huffing. The room had always been beautifully decorated, even before they’d added their own personal touch to it. But ever since Titu’s belief in the ancient Gods had begun to take over his life things had quickly changed. More statues lined the walls; more silk curtains had been thrown here and there to add a more ‘eastern’ stroke to the room; the balcony had been reshaped with hieroglyphics; and finally a vast shrine to the God Ra had been built in the corner of the bedroom. It was beginning to shroud his thoughts, she realized. All he ever thought of these days were his precious career and his ever-precious Gods. Isiy herself had thrown away these beliefs some time ago; no longer did she rely on what the ‘Gods’ thought to guide her life. She understood now that she was in total control of her own destiny… Titu thought this over a little differently however. ‘Are you coming to bed yet?’ she said, sliding under the silken cloths, ‘It’s getting late.’ Pharaoh smiled, ‘Just a minute, will you? The slaves are up to something!’ Isiy stared into the back of his head, blank-faced. ‘You can’t even see down there, so stop pretending and get in to bed, Titu!’ Xansiv pulled a face and turned to his wife, almost expecting to walk straight into an argument. Instead he found, to his surprise, something much more enjoyable on the bed. Isiy was almost fully unclothed and looked extremely vulnerable. She paid him a sexy look and said: ‘Cummon, I’ll make it worth your while.’ It didn’t take him too long to get in beside his queen. *** The pyramid was almost complete now. The guardsmen looked it over again and pondered whether or not the slaves actually knew what exactly they were building. Pharaoh had shiftily slivered the idea for the pyramid into the plans for the new patio because, as he explained - ‘I may as well, since they’re already building it.’ Pablo, the only Mexican amongst the group turned to his colleges and frowned. ‘You think they know, Esse?’ he said, stubbing out a cigarette. Pablo had never really liked his fellow workers; the way in which he addressed them as ‘Esse’ was proof enough of this. It was, he considered, a good job none of the others spoke a word of Spanish or else, he’d decided, he could be in great trouble. But he loved the way he got away with his little secret; it frilled him. Harold, the eldest of the guard, and the man who earlier that day had been given the order to throw the ignorant jock out of The Palace grounds, smiled, downing a mouthful of coffee and said: ‘Well, I doubt it…but they’re prolly not in…telli…gent enough to realize, see?’ Pablo shrugged, ‘Still think it’s a lil tight, Esse’. His strong Mexican accent shone in his voice. It had never been something he’d considered a character defect; he grasped his rich Mexican heritage by the throat and clung onto it with all his heart, after all. He loved his people and their ways whether or not anyone else did. ‘True,’ exclaimed Bernard Johnson, the stereotypical Brit amid the guard. ‘Bloody wrong, if you ask me, chaps…bloody wrong.’ A few of the others concurred, a smaller group however sighed in disgust. Some of the guardsmen couldn’t stand Bernard, which was accepted heartily by the Egyptian-battle-clad warriors since everyone can’t get along with everyone. But Bernard couldn’t come to terms with the reasons behind his disliking, which he’d learnt didn’t actually exist. ‘Yeah, well no one did ask you, Bernard, so shut the hell up,’ Grunted Ford, the biggest and strongest, but still inevitably the dumbest of the guard. Bernard scowled, ‘How dare you tell me to shut up, you big oaf! Why, you don’t have the bloody brains to demand my silence, old chap!’ Bernard was a brave one, everyone agreed on that, but you just didn’t say something along those lines to Ford ‘the mountain’. It wasn’t at all hard to see where from this nickname arose. Ford lunged at his comrade but froze when Harold stepped in his way. ‘Now, now, Ford; there’s no need to get like that, is there?’ ‘No, sir.’ Said Ford, looking down. Harold had served the longest duration of time at The Palace, even if it was only a year, and was looked upon as the leader of the others…even if his hearing wasn’t what it once was. ‘Sir!’ bellowed Steve, another guardsman from further down the yard, ‘I think something’s happening with the slaves!’ Harold repositioned his spectacles and peered forth into the murky darkness ahead. The air was still warm from the previous afternoon. ‘Where, lad?’ He really needed to clean these darned things. Steve pointed down into the construction yard. The men and women were approaching at a steady pace. Harold gulped - he could see their faces now and the expressions weren’t the most hopeful. He gulped again. ‘Maybe they just want a cup of coffee?’ said Ford, beaming in the face of death. *** ‘Cummon, we’re almost there!’ shouted Henry to the world in general. ‘Smash some skulls, alright!’ bellowed a random jock. ‘Like, yay!’ shrieked a random blonde. ‘SMASH SOME SKULLS, ALRIGHT!’ raved the same jock, a little too enthusiastically this time. ‘Like, yay!’ rattled the voice of the preppy teen again. ‘Fazer some—’ and the nerd was dropped with a clean right hook. Henry turned around as soon as he heard the crack of skin upon skin, and caught the sight of the nerd collapsing to the ground. He sighed. ‘Ok, cummon now; who did it this time? It was you again wasn’t it, Jack?’ Jack’s face looked a little guilty but he denied it nevertheless. Henry sighed and turned back in the direction of The Palace guard, ‘Let’s just keep on moving shall we?’ He marched on, the others trailing behind him. ‘SMASH SOME SKULLS, ALRIGHT!’ ‘Like, yay—’ ‘Oh shut up will you?!’ *** Pharaoh got back out of bed and walked into the bathroom, beads of sweat clinging to his dark skin. He splashed some cool crisp water up onto his face, rubbed his eyes and looked into the mirror grinning. Titu you old Set, you, he thought. Isiy lay asleep on the bed now, the silk sheets sticking closely to her curvy figure as she dreamt of things which, Titu concluded, probably involved shopping, jewellery, sandy beaches and of course overly expensive shoes. His knowledge on women was fairly narrow, although he did know exactly how to please them. He looked back at his reflection and smiled again. It wasn’t long before his attention was drawn to the sound of clattering outside. Frowning, he walked puzzled into the bedroom and out onto the balcony overlooking the yard. ‘Damn, I can’t see a bloody thing in this light!’ he said to himself and sprinted back into the bedroom. He could clearly hear the sound of a battle going on outside now, it was more than clear when the yells of pain and distress began to litter the previously quiet night air. Leaping two steps at a time, Titu finally managed to come to the Palace’s front door. He opened it and looked out. All the action stopped at once ‘What in the unholy name of Osiris is going on out here?’ he bellowed, everyone dropping their weapons (which included Coffee mugs and gardening tools) with a clatter. ‘Err, sir—’ ‘Oh, shut up, Bernard! Will someone just tell me what’s going on?! For the love of Ra can’t you tell that I’m trying to mentally prepare for my match with DarkStar at Shang’hied III?! It’ll crown the new number one contender to the world title, for the Gods’ sakes’ ‘Err, sir, maybe—’ ‘Did I not just tell you to shut up, Johnson?’ rallied Titu, his eyes stern and unmerciful. ‘But, sir,’ persisted the Englishman, a trickle of blood flowing from the corner of his mouth, ‘You’re…naked.’ Titu looked down, surprised. ‘Oh…’ he began, ‘Right, well everyone wait right here, ‘k? I’ll be back just as soon as I’m…err…not naked.’ He left sharply, leaving the men, women and guards looking on in shock. At last a dizzy blonde said: ‘Did you see the size of that?!’ Everyone sighed. Twenty minutes later… ‘So you all actually thought that I was keeping you here beyond your own wills?’ The now fully clothed Pharaoh looked genuinely astonished, even if deep down inside he wasn’t, and cared little for the people now they wished to leave. ‘Why, I’m awfully sorry about all this mix up, my friends. By the Gods, don’t be so silly, go on…get yourselves off back home, where you belong. Once again I stress my deepest apologizes.’ Damn it, damn it, damn it, damn it! ‘Please, men, show them out…’ his words trailed off as he finished with a ‘pfft’ and walked back towards the house. Another plan failed. But he’d get this all fixed. There’ll come a day when another group of wrestle-crazy fans wander down the wrong part of California and are brought back to the Palace to finish off what has already begun, and then my plan will be complete! Either that or some experts would have to be called in. Damn experts and their damned constant demand for my damned money. Pharaoh sighed, punching the air, as in the backdrop the bruised guards led the bruised men and women out into the dark street beyond. Henry’s plan had failed…well, the ‘Overthrowing of The Palace’ part of the plan at least, but at any rate they were finally on the outside of the Palace walls…where they belonged. *** Beyond reality, in what many would consider the Egyptian equivalent to purgatory, Thoth, Osiris and Anubis watched as the scenario unfolded and came to an abrupt close. ‘SILLY BAN ON SLAVERY. VERY FOOLISH.’ squawked Thoth, knowingly. ‘T’wasn’t like this when Pepy was Pharaoh.’ Said Anubis, frowning. ‘Then again, Shrugged Osiris. Pepy was useless as Pharaoh, which therefore led to the peasants revolting and his subsequent downfall.’ The Gods nodded in agreement. ‘ALWAYS HIGHLY KNOWLEDGEABLE, ORISIS - BUT NOT AS MUCH AS I, EH?’ Thoth chuckled. ‘Yeah, shut up, loser.’ Retorted Osiris drawing silence to the expanse they stood upon. *** Back in the Palace gardens… The nerd opened his eyes. ‘Where—where am I?’ he said to himself, squinting into he darkness. Then, as he looked around at the construction site, he realized the inevitable. ‘Noooooooooo!’ No on ever said the inevitable was pretty. The End.
