Date: 06/23/03 [The following footage was captured and recorded following the events of Friday’s Fusion, and was aired sometime after the show. The version you are about to view is an illegal, pirated version. However, the word “pirated” contains the word “pirate”, and since pirates are so cool, this footage will be aired anyway. The source is unknown. Enjoy.] ~A young boy sits alone on a couch. In front of him, strewn out across the table, are a splattered, intricate framework of potato chip collages, of which only a boy of such could create. He reaches out and grabs a few of them, and shoves them into his mouth. An empty bag with the label “Wavy Lays: Classic” falls to the floor as he begins to crunch down on the chips, the dry, salty taste watering his mouth. Sitting beside him on the couch is a remote. He picks it up and points the small black switch at the television. One click later, and he is viewing pirated footage, compliments of his father, PCW fanatic, who was there, at Fusion. The screen goes hazy. After fooling with the tracking, this smart young boy draws a cleaner, clearer picture into view. Satisfied, he sets down the remote and begins to watch the tape. A moment later, he reaches for more chips. (START) 'For the Night ' Dave: Alright folks, this is it! Jeff: “It”?!? Oh no, not “it”! ...Jesus, Dave...what the hell is 'it', anyway? Sarcastically, he flops down and hides beneath the announce desk, pretending to be scared. Dave (rolls his eyes): Well, we just got word from the back that the final showdown between Abner and the Masked German is about to take place, and I for one, want to see it! Jeff: And I, for one, sure as hell don’t care!!! ...Well, on second thought, maybe I’d like to see that German dude pound the piss otta that STUPID Redneck!!! ...But just a little... Dave: Regardless, this is starting to shape into one hell of a feud, and who knows, maybe tonight, live on Fusion, the German will reveal his true identity! Jeff: Heh.. I wouldn’t count on it, chum. Dave: ...”Chum”? Jeff: ... The feed chanegs to that of the backstage area, where you'd think there’s a riot in session. A full-blown, fully manned team of security guards stand in line, blocking Abner’s entrance into the cargo area. Abner: Outta mah way, pricks! Six or seven of them stand, facing Abner, wearing full black riot gear, carrying nightsticks and all. One of the larger ones, sturdy and almost Abner’s size, steps forward and raises the tip if his tinted, dark visor. Security Chief: I’m sorry, sir, but just can’t let you in. Abner: And why NOT!?! Security Chief: VP-W's orders...don’t want ya to make a scene. Abner looks down at the floor and shakes his head in disbelief. Abner: Make a scene! Make a scene! Did you see the SCENE that that damn German made back in MY interview, camerman, interviewer, and all??? Security Chief: I’m sorry sir, but there’s nothing we can do to help. Abner: But- Voice: Let him through. A black leatery gloev takes a cold, hard grip on Abner’s shoulder. Abner turns back and finds himself standing face to face with- (PAUSE) The boy reaches out and snatches another handful of chips. (PLAY) Security Chief: Boys! Get- But he gets cut short, and the lights go off. Yet again, there’s the sound of struggle and a hot, heavy air fills the arena as the fans wait in shock. A muffled grunt here, a clang there, and then finally, a snap... Voice: EINGANG! The voice echoes throughout the arena, as the lights flicker, finally settling back on, and we’re left again with another gruesome scene. Fans: *GASP* But, to their dismay, there is nothing there! The security guards gone...all gone. Every single one of them. And Abner; nowhere in sight. The hallway is just as it was when we left it, and as the dust settles, and the smoke clears, there's not a trace of struggle or misdoings. The audience begins to get nervous, a chill goes up their collective spine, and the camera fades out once more. The clean hallway now fades into the view of a gloomy, box-filled room, in which the lights slowly turn on. There are crates evrywhere, piles of random materials strewn left and right, and this is no doubt...the Cargo Hold. Abner: Why you little- Voice: STILLE! SILENCE!!! The eerie voice is muffled by the filled, cramped room of shipping materials, and reverberates off from some of the larger metal crates. Voice (continuing): Now you’ll DO as you’re TOLD! Abner (spits at the figure): Bite me, dough- *Zap.* Abner: Ahh!!! Abner reels back in pain, holding his injured hand. Still, the camera faces away from the unknown figure, who is still hidden behind it. But sure enough, a leather gloves comes out with a small, black stun-stic... Not to our surprise, it is, of course...the Masked German. And starting again, we’re treated to the entity of a buzzing, robotic voice. Masked German: Are you ever going to learn? You can't outsmart me and you never will... He reaches out and smacks Abner across the face. A substantial boo is the overwhelming response of the audience. Masked German: You represent the ignorance of your fellow countrymen, and now, I will punish you for that ignorance. You people are tearing the free world apart with your egomaniacal ways, and on behalf of the rest of the world, I am here to show that the United States of America is not the be all and end of all this universe. (PAUSE) Taking his time, the kid reaches over into the cup-holder next to him and pulls out a can of soda. He pops open a can of Coke, and takes a sip. After wiping his mouth with the sleeve of his shirt, he sets the can down and begins to watch the movie again. (PLAY) He reaches out and slaps at Abner again, but this time, the redneck catches his hand. Abner pulls himself to his feet, eyes bulging and reels back his fist, tightening it into a ball. There’s a massive pop from the fans. But then, just as it looks as if the redneck’s about to strike... *ZAP*, and he falls back to the floor, crumpled up in a fetal position. This time, an audible “YOU SUCK, YOU SUCK, YOU SUCK!!!” chant breaks forth from the audience, and Abner, though hurt very, very badly, forces a smile...the first time he’s really got this amount of people behind him since he’s his stay in PCW. Masked German: Any attempts to resist...and you will suffer. He bends over, slaps the redneck , and then stands back...BIG BOOT to the face. Abner rolls over, nursing his wounds. Masked German: The time for hiding behind a mask is over. The time for disguising my voice is over. The time of your country's dominance, in this sport, is over. He points a finger directly at the camera, meaning at the fans, who boo back in response. Masked German: You see...not everything IS as they appear... There’s a sound of ripping material, and a couple snaps. And then, the camera pans back, but just enough ot catch the back of...of the Masked German’s hair! His maksed is off, but due to the odd angle of the camera, we can’t see his (or her) face. Masked German: See what I mean? Abner: Well, well, well...if it isn’t- *ZAP* He falls back, motionless and collapses to the floor in a heap. Masked German: ....See what I mean? There’s the sound of evil, tainted laughter as the camera pans out, and the scene fades to black. (END) Tired, the kid yawns and decides to get ready for bed. With one click of the remote, the tape pops out, and the screen goes black.~ Lying his hotel room bed now, Abner begins to contemplate this series of events. It seems to him, that for once, not only has the attacker got the best of him, but the better best of him, too. It seems to him that, for once, in the end, he didn’t pull through. And it seems to him, that, unlike the countless other times in his life, he may not make it through this feud unharmed. That us unfortunate. But that is the way it is. Life is not what you make of it, and, you are not what life makes of it; rather, you make yourself. And Abner must realize this. As the Masked German has so successfully been able to do, Abner, too, must capitalize on his own faults. Use them to his own advantage. He must see what is his level of contemplation on such a high level, at par with this obviously brilliant German character, and indeed, match with it. Things aren’t as they were before. Life isn o longer just fun and games for our old-school “hick”, and he must evolve as a wrestler, as a person. He must reach new heights, and, in fighting back against such a dirty foe, lower himself to new plains, divide, and conquer. The time for the beer drinking and redneck partying is over. Abner must get down to business. “Who is this damn German fella, and what does he want?” Abner thinks to himself. He rolls over, curling up, and tucks in his feet between his legs Indian-legged style, except lying down. He pulls the cover sup past his chin, and peers up at the darkness. “With my eyes, if I wait, wait just long enough…I can pierce through the darkness, but this German guy…this…this bastard, can, with all that I have, cut him in two? Well, ‘guess Tuesday I’ll find out…” As far as Turmoil is concerned, you see, Abner has a plan. It’s not exactly fail-proof, but it’s bail-proof, in that, if he has to drop or bend it at the last second in light of some new, unknown, unplanned opportunity, he can do it with ease. And so the time has come for Abner, as with all people, another growing stage in life. Yeah, he’s still a redneck. Yeah, there’s still Cuzin Ed. And yeah, …HELL YEAH…he still drinks. But now, there’s a new part of him. A demon awoken by the grimace of such a fiends as the soul of the Masked German, and one, in all its glory, that will strike out against the injustices of this foreign foe, this fiasco caused by this fascist, Nazi-ist creep, and Abner will rip him apart, tear him to shreds. Or so he thinks. But only time will tell, and in the meantime, of course, Abner has a few beers to drink. …Hey…you can’t expect me to end on such a serious note, can you?
