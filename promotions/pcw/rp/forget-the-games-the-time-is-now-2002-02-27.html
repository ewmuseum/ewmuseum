Forget the games. The time is now!

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-27-02 Has the picture been painted clear enough for you? On Turmoil, it should have been. At any point, Jeremy Howard can and will nail the Krayzie Death Slam. That's the picture Drake Maxwell and Chet King had better get painted before they step into the ring with the Krazed Lunatic. This man isn't coming to play games. No, this man is coming a possessed man. He's possessed on winning the Continental title and gaining the freedom of his younger brother. Is the picture clear enough now? If not, then think back to all the things that the Krayzie One has done to try and gain the freedom of his brother, or get his son back. Keep thinking. Think of all the things he did to Stan Hinkle and Richard O'Bannon to strike that dagger of fear into them. Insane car chases, jumping through two story high windows and leaping off the roof of buildings. When Jeremy Howard wants something, especially his family, there is no end to the things he'll do to get them back! Just how far will he go, though? To find out, the scene fades into the twelve foot by twelve foot living room of the Krazed Lunatic. There, a camera catches up to Jeremy and Jessica standing in the center of the room in a loving embrace. Tears aplenty run down the cheeks of the lovely Jessica, whose wearing a pair of blue-ish plaid night pants and an almost revealing white top. Jeremy, on the other hand, isn't crying. Rather, the Krayzie One is holding a serious look on his face. Jessica: I.... I don't see why..... Jeremy pulls back from his girlfriend a bit, staring into her beautiful blue-ish green eyes. Jeremy: When I get down there, it's going to be very dangerous this time. I can't have you in harms way while I'm trying to get Troy back. Jessica: I'm in harms way being in Elkhart all by myself with my father lurking around. Jeremy knods his head, knowing how much Jessica fears her sickeningly perverse father. Jeremy: Your father won't make it to the front door if he shows up. I asked Bobby and Xavier to watch oer you while I'm out. You, nor anyone else can see them, but they can see everything. The fearful feeling that did control Jessica, begins to slip away at the mention of Jeremy's Asylum stablemates watching over his house while he's away. But, that still doesn't do much to calm her in the way of her worries about Jeremy's safety. Although, worrying about the Krazed Lunatic's safety is almost a lost cause. This man is going back to the old, run down church to get his son back. Even if it means taking on well over fifty men that circle a monstrous fire. Jessica: What about you? It's just too dangerous going there all alone. Jeremy releases his loving hold on her, still staring into her eyes. Jeremy: Whatever happens, happens. Just remember, I love you and I'm doing this for you. Jessica goes to speak again, but Jeremy puts his right hand index finger to her lips, silencing her. By the look in Jeremy's face, its no use trying to talk him out of this insane idea. All Jessica can do is pray for her boyfriend, the former two-time World Champion. She watches, as Jeremy picks up his bag of things and heads out of the living room and through the foyer. Apart of her just wants to break down and cry out of fear. But, she knows deep down inside her, she's going to have to be strong and hope Jeremy is able to pull off this seemingly impossible task..... Something he's known for doing. Jessica: Please, just bring my baby back. The front door shuts. Jessica rushes over to the window and watches Jeremy put his bag in the back of his sport utility vehicle. She sees him give one last look toward his house, then he steps into the driver's seat. It might be wrecked all to pieces, but that's not going to stop the Krayzie One from going to get back his son. With Jeremy Howard on his way back to Terre Haute, Indiana for the final battle to get his son, the scene fades..... to black! I put my trust in you, Pushed as far as I can goAnd for all this, There's only one thing you should know An excerpt from 'In the End' by Linkin Park Moments later, seven hours by real time, the scene fades back into the black BMW X5 of Jeremy Howard's crossing into the city limits of Terre Haute, Indiana. The sun is beginning to set, creating a darkness over the sky. Still, the Krayzie One continues on, not holding back for a second from his mission. This occult that is holding Troy from his parents is about to get a rude awakening in the form of a hostile and psychotic Krazed Lunatic. Jeremy: Alright. All I need to do is find that alleyway, then follow it into that public park. Jeremy slows up a bit, choosing to take a side road into downtown Terre Haute. Cautiously, the Krayzie One proceeds down the side streets, keeping an eye out for anything that looks familiar from before. The car chase happened so quickly, it'll be a wonder if he's able to remember how to get to the old church. It takes Jeremy a few turns down roads he hasn't seen before, but he finally finds an alleyway from before. Jeremy: Phew. Found it. Jeremy turns into the alley, beginning to make his way down toward the end of it. All of the sudden, before Jeremy can reach the public park from before, a white van slams into the side of Jeremy's BMW. The force of the hit sends the BMW slamming into the side of a brick wall that lines the alleyway. Jeremy hits his head hard on the steering wheel, knocking him unconscious. Just how is Jeremy Howard going to overcome the impossible odds now, when he's not even conscious. Can he overcome the odds? Suddenly, Jeremy's mind slips away to other times in which he supposedly couldn't overcome the odds: Ivan has Frisco reeling against the ropes. He swings a big right hand, but Frisco ducks it. Then, from seemingly out of nowhere, Jeremy Howard runs and hits Ivan with a huge running clothesline sending him over the top rope and to the arena floor. Dave: HOWARD JUST TOOK OUT STANISLAV!!! ALEXI RUSLAN IS IN THERE ALL ALONE NOW!!! Alexi starts to get to his feet. Frisco grabs Ruslan. He lifts Alexi up and carries him to the ropes. FRISCO THEN HITS RUSLAN WITH THE F-BOMB OVER THE TOP ROPE AND ONTO IVAN STANISLAV OUTSIDE THE RING!!! Dave: F-BOMB!! F-BOMB!! FRISCO WITH THE F-BOMB!! Duke: What’s Jeremy doing? Jeremy stands behind Frisco. Frisco slowly backs up and talks some trash to the Russians outside the ring. Jeremy grabs Frisco and hits him with the KRAYZIE DEATH SLAM!!! Dave: KRAYZIE DEATH SLAM ONTO FRISCO!! WE COULD HAVE A NEW CHAMP!! Jeremy makes the cover. One! Two! Ivan tries breaking it up, but is too late! THREE! Winner and NEW PCW Heavyweight champion: Jeremy 'Krayzie Howard Jeremy quickly rolls out of the ring and is handed his title as Ivan stands inside the ring, pissed off as ever. Dave: HE'S DONE IT!! JEREMY HOWARD IS THE NEW CHAMP!! Jeff: And now we're gonna have to shut down! Noooooo! Dave: HE HAS OVERCOME ALL THE ODDS AND WON THE BIG ONE!! HE'S BEATEN IVAN STANISLAV!! For many months, Ivan Stanislav was considered the monster of Primetime Championship Wrestling. He went through men like they were nothing, putting a certain few in the hospital. No one man could beat Ivan Stanislav. And, when he won the World title, no one thought he could be beat for it. That is, until the Krazed Lunatic proved all of his critics wrong and overcame all the odds in beating Ivan for the PCW World title at Decimation. He won! But,more importantly, Jeremy Howard overcame the odds! Snow wastes no time smashing the shovel into Howard's body. Howard curls up into the fetal position to protect himself. Snow does not stop hitting him. Dave: This has gone to far. Jeff: But it was ok a minute a go when Snows head was on the line. Duke: Well, yea so? Jessica jumps on the apron and pleas with Snow to stop. Snow then exits the ring and chases Jessica around with a shovel in his hand. Dave: This man is a nightmare. Jeff: No that's Snow, Nightmare is probably in some other federation. Duke: You don't hit a ho hoss! Dave: Very PC thanks Duke. Duke: Anytime Hoss!! Snow gets sick of playing cat and mouse with Jessica turns his attention to the ring where Howard is laying in a pool of his own blood. Dave: Go for the title just end this match!!! Jeff: Look's like Howard is swimming. Duke: My God we may need a medic! Snow bypasses Howard and starts to head up the ladder with a slow cocky ascend. Dave: I think we have a new champion!! Jeff: It's about time! Duke: GET UP HOWARD!!! All of a sudden the crowd pops as a bloody hand rises up like from the night of the living dead. Howard slowly gets up to his feet. He is covered in blood! Snow is about two links away from the top. Howard walks towards the ladder but stumbles and almost falls. Dave: He's alive!! He's alive!! I don't think he's going to make it. Jeff: Not a chance. Duke: COME ON HOWARD!! Jeff: Who's the mark now hoss? Snow looks down and sees Howard, he gets to the top link and starts reaching for the gold. Howard grabs the ladder and starts shaking it violently. Snow hangs on for dear life. Dave: Timber!! Jeff: This sucks. Duke: Who-HA! Snow falls out of the ring and hits the ground hard after a long fall. Dave: The tide has changed yet again!! Jeff: I hate Tides! Duke: I dig it! Howard quickly with little energy he has props the ladder back up and makes his way to the top. Dave: He's going for the gold!!! Jeff: Ya think? Duke: OH my God how can Snow still be on his feet? Snow gets up slowly and slides into the ring. Howard is almost to the top. Howard sees Snow and jumps for the title instead of hanging on. Snow knocks the ladder down. Howard comes down with the ladder and the belt. Howard doesn't get to ride the ladder down he just falls straight to the bottom. Jason Snow thought he'd use another means to beat the Krayzie One. The former best friend of the Krazed Lunatic thought he'd just beat him down with a shovel until he couldn't move anymore. But, like a phoenix rising from the flames and bleeding profusely, the Killin Villain was unable to keep the Krayzie One down for the count. He might be twenty one years old, but he can't be kept down. Weapon or not, it's nearly impossible to keep this man down! He'll overcome the odds! Vampir Nosferatu gets Jeremy to his feet. Jeremy tries to strike Vampir, but Vampir kicks him in the stomach. Jeremy tries to fight it, but Vampir forces Jeremy into the powerbomb position. Howard just dangles. Vampir lifts Howard into the air, then powerbombs the Krayzie One through the second table. Howard's body shatters the table. Table pieces lay everywhere in the ring. Vampir goes over to Maxwell. Duke: Oh hell no! Dave: Duke, what are you doing. Before Dave can stop him, the Malice Man Duke Williams leaves his announcing position, sliding into the ring. Vampir has his back to Duke. Duke smashes Vampir in the back with a forearm. Vampir drops Maxwell, falling into the turnbuckle. Duke grabs Vampir. The two men begin to brawl. Vampir goes for the clothesline to Duke, sending him over the top rope. Vampir leaves the ring to chase him. Maxwell somehow gets back to his feet. Maxwell grabs Howard. Jeff: This is it! Break that jobber champions face! Dave: Howard had better do something here! Jeff: No! Just job like a bitch! Maxwell brings Jeremy to his feet. Suddenly, out from the back runs the girlfriend of Jeremy Howard, Jessica. Jessica races down to ringside and over to where Jeremy's World title belt lays. Drake catches sight of her. This small opening is enough for Jeremy to regain his senses. Drake takes his eye off Jeremy. Jeremy blasts Drake with a superkick. Drake falls back into the ropes, then bounces forward. Jeremy locks Drake in. Jessica shoves the World title into the ring. Jeremy lifts Drake up, then hits an implant DDT onto the World title belt. Drake's face smashes into it! Dave: DRAKE MAXWELL JUST MET A PSYCHOTIC ENDING!!!!!!!!!!!! Jeff: Damn that whore! Jeremy leans his arm over, making the pin on Drake Maxwell. 1....... Duke tries to take Vampir Whiskey Bent and Hellbound on the outside. 2....... Vampir counters, driving Duke's head into the ring post. Vampir turns his attention to the ring. 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Vampir slides into the ring. Too late! Dave: HE'S DONE IT! JEREMY HOWARD HAS RETAINED HIS WORLD TITLE. BARELY! It doesn't matter how great the odds are, Jeremy Howard will find a way to overcome them. And now, in his unconscious state, one has to wonder just how exactly the Krazed Lunatic plans to overcome the odds now? If he plans to get his son back, the Krayzie One had better figure it out real soon. Or, two year old Troy Howard just might not get that family reunion his mother wishes for him. Also, just who are these men working for? The scene fades away to six men dressed in all black carrying the Krazed Lunatic away from his wrecked black BMW toward the white van...... to black!
