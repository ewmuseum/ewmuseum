Title: Conspiracy Theory Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: St. Patrick's Day! Conspiracy. There are many who believe government influence in our lives reaches farther than any of us could imagine. In his masterpiece 1984, author George Orwell pictured a future under communist rule, with humanity constantly under the surveilance of 'Big Brother', an Orwellian psuedonym for the government. But this was a totalitarian government. It seems impossible to think that in a democratic society, in America, that our government is nothing more than a setup designed to shadow our every move. Yet there are those that believe that this is so. There are those that feel we as a society are simply victims of a giant government conspiracy. Coincidentally, these men, the tandem of Misplaced, must face exactly that which they fear the most. The government. And, the members of the government they face, well, they aren't above using conspiracy to achieve their aims. After all, they ARE......Politically Incorrect. ::The office of Reginald V. Lovecraft sits in an office complex in Washington D.C. We know, despite what he calls himself, Lovecraft is not a governor of any state. However, he IS a governor, in a manner of speaking. He is the governor-general of a rather influential, yet little known special interest group. The Comission Of National Democratic Old Men, or C.O.N.D.O.M. for short, has been in existance for over sixty years. Many of its members are old southerners, champion of states rights, and well, not the most racially tolerant people in the world. Lovecraft, however, has guided C.O.N.D.O.M away from its initial purpose of opposing civil rights, and on to fundraising....the question of course becomes, fundraising....for what?:: Secretary: Yes..you'll donate $100? Excellent! With your donation, you will recieve your free C.O.N.D.O.M. flashlight in the mail! Just stay on the phone so I can get your address.... ::Yes, this all seems highly odd. Lovecraft sits mere feet from his secretary's desk, wearing his trademark red suit. A scientific calculator is in his hand, as he begins crunching numbers.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Man......this is some serious money we've raised here. Secretary: How much do we have? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Our total right now is about $5 million. Not bad at all, especially considering our origins. Secretary: Don't you have something coming up later on? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah...I've got my second PCW match coming up tonight against this NTICW tag team called Misplaced. In fact, I'm headed to confer with my colleague about that as we speak... ::Throwing his calculator into his briefcase, Lovecraft marches out of his office building in downtown D.C. Waiting at the door is his faithful sidekick and partner, 'The Master Debator' William S. Powell.:: William S. Powell: SO, how goes C.O.N.D.O.M.? ::Powell snickers, as Lovecraft shoots him a withering look. Powell quiets, before Reginald nods to him.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Quite well actually, we've got $5 million in our coiffers as we speak. William S. Powell: What are we going to do with all that money? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Oh, you know exactly what we're going to do... ::They grin, evily, more evily than usual.:: William S. Powell: Man, I bet Misplaced is pissing their pants right now. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes....they must see a government conspiracy in this right now.. William S. Powell: Yeah, those two guys are paranoid.........paranoid, but entirely correct. The government does tend to have its hand in many things, I mean, let's look back at history.. ::Powell smiles. As a former political science/history major, Powell knows well what he is about to speak of.:: William S. Powell: Back in the '80's...the Iran-Contra scandal. President Ronald Reagan wanted desperately to help the Nicaraguan contras...but he didn't have money. And so, he dealt with the Iranians...behind the country's back. He did what HE wanted. And the government covered it up. You're afraid of Conspiracy Carey? It's everywhere....there is no escaping it. ::Both men nod slowly, in sync with each other.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Whether or not you want to know this Carey...government's job is to uphold the will of the people... William S. Powell: What the hell does that mean? Reginald V, Lovecraft: Exactly, William..'will of the people' has been open to interpretation since the days of our founding fathers. Alexander Hamilton argued that it was the job of the government to temper the will of the people...that people were stupid and ignorant, incapable of making proper decisions. Conversely, Thomas Jefferson argued that the people were fully capable of making their own decisions, and should have direct voting rights. The end result is our government today..and to be honest, our government is somewhere in between these two extremes.. William S. Powell: Indeed...why do you think the Electoral College still exists? Whether we will ever admit it or not, government, and politicians, are afraid of the direct power of the people. That's why we dread initiative, recall, and reform so much...I like my job. ::Powell smiles, straightening out his Armani suit, indicating what his job has allowed him to attain.:: William S. Powell: In short......what Misplaced faces tonight is two politicians. Two men.......who may be part of a conspiracy, or they may not. No one quite knows. Except us.....and we're not telling. FEAR US!!! Farewell, gentlemen.... ::We fade, to....:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically Incorrect! ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
