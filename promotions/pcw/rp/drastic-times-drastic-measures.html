Drastic times. Drastic measures.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: I skipped school to RP! I HOPE YER ALL HAPPY!!! …Enter the Creative Team. “Okay, okay! Props to whoever came up with that static gag!” “Count it!” Rustling about in busy discussion, the writers for “Syko the Goblin” take their spots at the long table, ready to work the magic that has made that little Goblin such a success. Writer 1: “Okay… (Clasps hands, giving his co-writers a serious glare.) you just wrote the best RP of all time. How do you top it?” The hustle-bustle stops… They all look quietly to one another. Writer 3: “I got it…” Swiveling their seats, all eyes become focused on the young, upstart member of the team. Writer 3: “Alright… Try and visualize this.” He grins, building up anticipation. Writer 3: “O-S-W 4: Payback. Return from hell.” BAM! Suddenly, the door is kicked from its threshold, falling splintered to the floor! A smoking canister of tear gas is hurled through its absence and onto the table… It detonates, filling the room quickly with an eruption of eye stinging gas! Writers: “AHHHHHHHHH!!!!” The writers frantically scatter, stumbling about blindly. Several silhouettes scramble through the door! RATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTATTAT! The gas soon diffuses, revealing the writer’s corpses all scattered about on the floor… Their blood paints the walls. Standing over the fallen ‘Geniuses,’ four armor clad ‘troopers’ scan the room, their sights aligned with their automatic rifles. A first relays into a walkie-talkie. ???: “We’re in. Over.” The walkie-talkie crackles, responding… “Initiate the new story.” “Noo…uhgg…” One of the ‘Troopers’ looks down to see one of the writers tugging on his leg. Writer: “O-S-W… series… a good idea…” BLAM! ???2: “The Deadline is 6:00 EST.” …? Fin? TBC? …Derrr…
