Blood...Stained...Hands

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/02 Within the past eight days, the world of PCW has been changed. There has been the forming of the Political Machine and Eleanor Hall’s gaining of the Extreme Title, but definitely the most influential event has been the re-formation of one of PCW’s most powerful stables ever, the Ultimate Authority. With Tyler Nelson still at the helm and Xavier Kannon still on board, things looked good, but with the additions of Corey Williams, Chef, Goeren, and Alexis Parshin things look pretty damn good. “Well, Raghab, Allah is certainly watching over us as we speak.” Our scene opens up on Alexis Parshin and Raghab Karszat standing in a long line of some sort. Alexis is wearing his brown leather army jacket over a shirt with the Uzbek flag in the upper right corner. Raghab, looking lively as always, is wearing a black Ultimate Authority T-shirt with large camouflage pants, only complimenting his rotund stomach. The two men seem to be towards the end of the line. “Raghab, you say that the Uzbek sympathizer Tyler Nelson has assigned me a body bag with a name on it?” Alexis smiles evilly as the words roll off of his tongue, while Raghab scratches his wide forehead, thinking back to what the news he was delivered by one of Mr. Nelson’s lower associates. “I believe that both of Allah’s own sons will take on a blasphemous American, not that it really matters who it is, but I believe he calls himself Blood Angel.” Alexis’ smile continues to spread on his face, stretching his arms in anxiety for Blood Angel. “He is…one of the Phantom Booker’s little followers. Myself and Chef will destroy him for the honor of Allah and the pride of Tyler Nelson.” The line moves up, Alexis sliding up while continuing to speak to Raghab. “Raghab, I’ve been blessed with a mentor and powerful allies. We will not fail Uzbekistan or Allah.” Raghab is already staring at the food up at the front of the line, apparently some sort of small café line. “Raghab, turn your petty interests away from food for one moment, do you realize the chance that I’ve been given?” Raghab just nods, half paying attention and half trying to finish the conversation as soon as possible. “First, it will be Blood Angel. An angel is nothing…just a speck in the sea of Allah’s grandness. He is no more than one posing as important, feeding off of the Phantom Booker’s almost invisible power.” Raghab is now clueing in a small amount, but still is only nodding, afraid that talking might elicit a bad reaction from his boss. Once again now, the line moves forward, and Alexis’ words get a small reaction from a few people nearby, but most dismiss it as nonsense. “The Ultimate Authority… I have allied myself with power once again.” Raghab chimes in at this point, looking for some suck-up point, “Always a smart one, Konstantin.” Alexis turns to Raghab and gives him a blank, cold look as Raghab takes a deep breath and fades back off into his own little world. “I have had power that would make a normal man sick from its taint, but now… power intoxicates me again.” Raghab is now definitely paying attention once again but is very confused by the cryptic language of his boss. “Intoxication allows my dominance, which will continue over all who stand in the path of me and of the Ultimate Authority.” Raghab listen in even more closely, and is drawn back by Alexis’ next words. “RAGHAB!” His associate quickly stands up from his slumping position and stands at his full attention, facing Alexis. “Now that I think of it, do you realize what happened on this Tuesday Turmoil?” Raghab scratches his chin, giving the impression that he is trying to remember, but having no actual idea of what his boss is talking about. “I made the biggest debut in history of the PCW.” “I don’t doubt your talent and dominance, Konstantin, but..” Alexis silences Raghab as his booming voice overlaps that of the smaller man. “Of course I did! Raghab, you could never understand such absolute power, and never will!” Raghab realizes he’s beaten and doesn’t bother to argue with Alexis, as is rather smart when he becomes angry and frustrated. “I gave their pathetic champion a run for his money. I gave him all the skill he could handle, and it took all of his skill to throw me out of the ring.” Raghab just nods, slightly embarrassed by the events of before. “And with all of the powerful figures of the Ultimate Authority, the growth of the dominant force will only be accelerated exponentially. We will take Big Alan Steele, Duke Williams, and anyone that stands in our way down to the size that they are: insects.” As Alexis finishes, the line moves up one last time until Alexis is in front of the counter, and a young pale girl steps up, asking in a perky voice for his order. “So, what would ya like sir?” Alexis scratches his chin for a while, feeling at the stubble, and looks at the area behind the counter and the menu. “Let me see, I will have two Snapples.” The young girl squeaks with enthusiasm at this, “Like, awesome choice. Snapple Rocks!” Raghab mutters to himself, fulfilling a strange urge, “Snapple…Like…Rocks!” Alexis hears part of this and just looks at Raghab strangely, dismissing the strange behavior. The girl brings his two girls out and Alexis looks down at the two bottles. “Do you have a larger drink?” Alexis’ low voice doesn’t seem to have the same intimidating factor on the young girl as it does on most people. She shakes her head, her auburn pigtails flying wildly. Alexis voice begins to rise as he replies to the girl. “How am I supposed to be refreshed? This is your moronic capitalist ideas for you!” Alexis takes both bottles and smashes them in his palms, their contents spilling all over the desk as the girl looks on, then replies in her high voice, “What’s your, like, problem?” Alexis doesn’t reply, he just starts to walk away, as several people in line look at him with contempt as he walks away. “Raghab, this capitalist foolishness has gone too far. It begins with Blood Angel, and then we continue to take over from there.” Raghab’s face now grows into a smile, as it often does when Alexis thinks up something particularly devious. “Yes, Konstantin, the infiltration has begun, and now we will step into power with speed.” Alexis makes no physical sign except for a small nod and continues to speak. “Big Alan Steele will be further down the line, I will have that god…no, that mortal’s blood to stain my hands. The blood will wash from my hands, but never from his conscience, if he lies in a hospital bed, alone and dying. That will be when the war is over…”
