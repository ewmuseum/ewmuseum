Sorry about my appearance, I wasn’t expecting you so quick The young man hands Teresa her large pepperoni pizza with extra cheese. She hands him what she owes leaving a generous tip. What my work don’t know won’t hurt them. Teresa takes a beer out of the icebox and sits beside him on the bed. Jeff turns his head to look at her as if he wanted to ask her something. Instead, he quickly looks back to the TV and takes a drink of his beer. A commercial interrupts the movie. Something on your mind Jeff? Teresa asks before setting her bottle on the nightstand. Unfortunately on her part, Jeff hasn’t done his homework and doesn’t realize this. He sets his bottle on the floor and crawls on the bed next to her. Just remembering she had ordered a pizza to be delivered at 9:30, Teresa crawls out of bed dressed in her jeans and black short sleeve shirt. She opens the door to a semi tanned young man wearing a red and white uniform with the name ‘Jeff’ on his nametag. We end this pitiful promo with a slamming of a door and a pizza delivery boy wearing a large pepperoni with extra cheese.
