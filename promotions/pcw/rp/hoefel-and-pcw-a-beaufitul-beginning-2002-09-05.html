Hoefel and PCW....a Beaufitul Beginning!

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-5-02 ~ The scene opens inside the home of PCW's newly acquired superstar, Hardcore Hoefel. It's about 12:30 pm, and the sun is beaming in through the windows of the apartment. Just from the looks of things, you'd think last night was a rough one for Hoefel. There's empty bottles of beer all over the place, some lying on their side, dirty clothes scattered throughout the room, and half-eaten plates of food sitting on the table. Footsteps are heard coming towards us from one of the other rooms. As the camera rotates its attention to the doorway, we see Hardcore Hoefel slowly trudging his way into the room. He's wearing a pair of baggy black sweatpants, and has a black bandana wrapped around his skull. He appears to be a wreck. Hoefel looks around the room, shakes his head in disbelief, and laughs. He lifts his head up, and notices our presence. ~ Hoefel - Damn parties.... ~ He takes a deep breath, and then makes his way into the kitchen to fix himself some breakfast. Hoefel opens one of the cabinets, and pulls out a box of Corn Pops and a bowl. He opens the box on the table, fills the bowl with Corn Pops, and then goes into the fridge for some milk. He hangs his head in disgust when he sees that the only thing left in the fridge is a 16 oz. bottle of Budweiser. Hoefel reaches for the bottles, cracks it open, and takes a swig. He then walks back over to the bowl, and pours some of the Budweiser into it so the cereal isn't dry. ~ Hoefel - I swear to God, I'm gettin' a lock for my fridge next time I throw a party. Assholes don't even have the common decency to leave me some milk for breakfast in the mornin'. Speaking of assholes, I'm sure by now you've have heard I've signed with Primetime Championship Wrestling. And when it comes to asshole, this place has an abundance of them. ~ Hoefel fills his mouth with a spoonful of Corn Pops and Budweiser before continuing. ~ Hoefel - You've got one of the most ignorant people on the face of this earth under contract to PCW in the form of the racist, slur slangin' Statuz Quo. The only word this illiterate bastard seems to be able to get out of his fake-gold plated mouth that anyway can comprehend is cracka'. Quo I'll keep it brief.....watch ya' lip around this 'cracka'. I ain't like the rest of the white boys PCW has. I didn't have that great suburban, middle-class white family upbringing I'm sure most of them did. I grew up on the streets, done more time behind bars then you, your dead-beat father, and your crackwhore of a mother did combined. So be careful whose toes you step on, cuz the next time they might be the wrong ones. Just a friendly warning.....Holla' Back Youngin'. ~ He takes a few more spoonfulls of Corn Pops and Budweiser while deciding who his next target will be. He then puts his spoon down and continues. ~ Hoefel - As of my signing, Primetime Championship Wrestling is in shambles....in case you haven't been able to realize it on your own. We've got the staff trying to take dictatorship over the wrestlers, we've got the wrestlers struggling to hold onto their rights, racists, nazis, aliens....I mean, PCW's got more drama than your regular weekday soap opera. It's really sickening. It's all going to change tho', starting right now. As a matter of fact, starting at this past Turmoil....the second my signing was announced. I don't know if you can feel it, but the tempo has already begun to shift....just from my very name being added to the PCW roster. Shit's about to pick up around here, and a lot of people just ain't gonna be able to keep up. The weak'll die, and the strong'll survive....but only the true LEGENDS will flourish. ~ Hoefel takes the last few spoons of Corn Pops and finishes them. He then lifts the bowl to his mouth to drink up the excess beer left in the bowl. He places the bowl back in front of him and continues. ~ Now as far as my debut goes, what is the front office thinkin'? There going to square Hardcore Hoefel off against....a Sergeant Slaughter rip-off? You can't be serious, watchin' this guy is like watchin' re-runs of G.I. Joe. Futuristic warfare? Does he have any clue how completely and utterly ridiculous any of the shit he claims actually is? Does he even know the first thing about war? About the military? I doubt he does. I was in the military....served nine months in the Army before getting booted out on a misconduct discharge. I got through bootcamp though....and all his high-tech, warfare simulation bullshit he's been talking about sounds like is the night infiltration course we had to complete. It was no big deal...to be honest it was actually a lot of fun. What I want to know though is does he really think of that military crap really qualify him to step into the ring with greatness? With someone of the caliber of Hardcore Hoefel? With a true LEGEND of this sport? I don't think it does, if I'm wrong correct me. BladeD, do us all a favor....stick to playin' with G.I. Joe's and leave wrestling to the big boys. If you do that you just might not get hurt. ~ Hoefel's interupted by a knock on his door. He slowly pulls himself up and out of the chair, and walks over to the door. When he opens it we can see it's only the mailman. Hoefel takes the mail, closes the door, and begins flippin through it as he walks back to the kitchen. ~ Hoefel - BILL.... ~ Hoefel tosses one piece of mail up in the air as he continues to walk. ~ Hoefel - BILL... ~ He tosses another piece of mail backwards. ~ Hoefel - ANOTHER GOD DAMN BILL!... ~ Hoefel flings this piece of mail like a frisbe off the wall. He goes to throw the final piece of mail but stops short.... ~ Hoefel - PCW....what the hell are they sending me mail for?!? ~ Hoefel sits down, opens the envelope, and begins to read through the contents. ~ Hoefel - EZform XK?!? 40% taxes on all wrestler's earnings?!? Fill out and return immediately?!? ~ He sits there for a minute, seemingly in deep thought over what to do about this piece of mail concerning PCW taxes that he's just received. ~ Hoefel - OH! I got it! I know what I'm supposed to do with this! There's some great guys up in the PCW front office....no bullshit, always lookin' out for their wrestlers in their time of need. ~ Hoefel reaches into his pocket and pulls out a small bag. After closer inspection we can see that it's a dime bag of weed. He empties the contents out on a cd case that was lying on the table near him, and begins to break the weed up with the greatest of precision. ~ Hoefel - Only the finest hydro would be fittin' for a situation such as this.... ~ Once he's done breaking it up, Hoefel picks up his EZform XK and begins tearing at it. After a minute or two, Hoefel has tweaked with the EZform enough to the point where he's satisfied. He takes the broken up weed, puts it inside the EZform XK, and begins to roll it! He licks the edges and makes sure it's rolled nice and tight. Hoefel puts the make-shift blunt in his mouth, and removes a lighter from his pocket. He flicks the lighter, and an orangish red flame shoots out the top of the light. He brings the flame to the tip of the blunt and holds it there, allowing it to catch. He begins to inhale until he feels the blunt is lit well. Hoefel then removes the flame and places the lighter back in his pocket. After another pull he removes the blunt from his mouth. With a big kool-aid smile on his face, Hoefel delivers his final comments. ~ Hoefel - I knew I signed with Primetime Championship Wrestling for a reason. I run out of papers, and what do they send me?!? Mutha fuckin' papers! That's what I call lookin' out for your employees! I can see it already, this is going to be a BEAUFTIFUL relationship between the PCW front office and Hardcore Hoefel! ~ The scene fades to black as Hoefel continues to smoke. ~
