Revenge Saga Part 9: The Battle Ensues...

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 9: The Battle Ensues… From Last Time… A smile develops upon Crichton’s face. “What if I did see the ambush coming and I did know this entire thing was going to happen, but I was relying upon the only unknown factor in this whole equation. The super soldier serum and its unpredictability for example. Crichton replies, equally cocky. The statement doesn’t knock the ever so smug smile from Fleming’s face. “That.” He begins “is why I have plan B!” he states mysteriously. Crichton shakes his head in disbelief. “You can’t plan against the unpredictable Fleming, you should know that!” Crichton declares. “Yes, but the backup plan I have is a very powerful one indeed!” Fleming declares. “So why isn’t this all powerful plan B, plan A?” Crichton questions mockingly. The smile upon Fleming’s face transforms from a smug one to a vindictive one. “Because I would prefer to handle scum like you myself!” Fleming declares, manoeuvring his robotic suit into a fighting stance. Crichton nods smirking slightly. “I’m so glad you just did that; I was tired of talking anyhow!” Crichton states taking a step back, developing a fighting stance of his own. Crichton didn’t know how he could defeat Fleming, while he was wearing that suit, he was next to impossible to beat. There was only one thing that could save him now, his new found powers and his ability to out think Fleming in battle situations. Crichton was taught how to use seemingly ineffective things as very effective weapons and since Fleming was never in the army he wouldn’t have that training. The only problem was, these tactics were taught to him specifically for use on humans, not humans wearing indestructible robotic battle suits. He just had to improvise, but that wasn’t going to be as easy as it sounded. Crichton’s thought process is broken, as Fleming brings a huge robotic fist aimed directly at Crichton’s head. Crichton doesn’t have much time to move, but he does, successfully rolling out of the way before the impact of the robotic fist. However, the sheer impact of the collision between the robotic fist and the concrete floor knocks Crichton off his feet, allowing Fleming to gain a better strategical position over Crichton. As Crichton regains his bearings Fleming moves into position, as the twelve foot robotic frame’s shadow engulfs Crichton. Not for the first time since the battle started Crichton decided that this wasn’t going to be easy. It wasn’t going to be easy at all. ************ Crichton’s eyes widened as Fleming’s twelve-foot robotic frame looms over him, engulfing him with shadow. Fleming raises his arms above his head, and Crichton notices the sadistic grin etched upon the seemingly demented mans face. It all happened in a second. The realisation of his situation propelled Crichton’s adrenaline output to the maximum of the already abnormal levels, coupled with the fact that the man who murdered his love Aeryn Sun was moments away of ending it all that caused him to erupt. Aeryn’s murder would not be avenged if Crichton didn’t act and act now. A primal scream erupted from Crichton’s mouth, causing Fleming to pause in astonishment, as Crichton jumped to his feet and charged at the robotic plated man. Fleming found this quite amusing and compared it to a fly attempting to charge a car’s windscreen, as Crichton got ever closer to impact. Fleming lowered his arms. He would show that he could not beat him, and if he stood his ground he would be crushed like the fly against the car’s windscreen. The impact when they collide is almost deafening to the audience of high ranking military officials and weapons designers alike, as Crichton tackles the metal monstrosity around the waist and to everyone’s astonishment (including his own), Fleming is lifted slightly off the ground and dumped to the floor like a very heavy sack of potatoes. The move Crichton executed is widely known in the wrestling community as a spear, not one of Crichton’s preferred moves within the wrestling ring itself, but after the effect it had on Fleming he was damn well going to add it to his repertoire. The Impact of the twelve-foot robotic suit controlled by Fleming hitting the floor rocked the entire military base. Many soldiers on sentry duty by the Military Base entry thought it was an earthquake, even though New York does not sit upon unstable tectonic plates. Those nearer to the impact with the ground were literally thrown off they’re feet onto the cold concrete floor. Many are in shock at the exemplorary show of strength on Crichton’s part, and realise that if he could take the twelve-foot monster off his feet, he might actually have a chance at defeating the metal monstrosity. Most of the high ranking army officials and some of the weapons designer civilians flee the immediate area each heading for their respective modes of transport. Ms. Zemoff however stands her ground. She had invested interests in the battle, the first was to see how well her robotic suit faired through the battle and the second was that the man she loved was encased within the robotic suit and was liable to lose the battle with Crichton. Fleming, still encased in the robotic suit, slowly gets back to his feet. Zemoff noted that this design flaw needed to be fixed, before the new model of the robotic battle suit came off the assembly line. Crichton once again adopts a fighting stance as Fleming draws ever near. Crichton noted that the left arm of the robot didn’t seem to move, and a few wires were loose at the shoulder joint. I must of hit it harder than I thought! Crichton muses silently, his confidence building by the second. Fleming visibly tries to move his left arm, clearly to no avail, Crichton takes the opening and jumps onto the front of the twelve-foot monstrosity, whacking the plexi-glass panelling, trying furiously to get his hands on Aeryn Suns murderer. Flemings eyes widen as a slight crack appears in the plexi-glass plating. Panicking, Fleming presses a series of buttons without any sequence to speak of, rotating the top half of the battle suit rapidly, causing Crichton to lose his grip on the front panel and sending him five meters back, scraping his shoulder as he skids across the concrete floor. Fleming releases the plexi-glass plating, as the battle suit, begins to tumble again, this time totally out of control. Fleming evacuates the suit before it hits the ground and catches fire. Fleming stumbles into the arms of Ms. Zemoff. It’s time for plan B!” Obviously out of breath after his ordeal. “Not shit sherlock! How the hell did Crichton do that?” Ms. Zemoff questions bewildered at the turn of events. “It’s the super soldier serum! It’s working better than the US military could ever have expected!” Fleming replies. “Well then, shouldn’t we get the hell outta here before he comes back?” Zemoff questions. Fleming stands tall and grabs Zemoff by the shoulders. “My sole purpose is to bring that man in for further tests, and I intend to do that, by any means necessary!” Fleming states, confidently. Zemoff nods in understanding. “I understand your reasoning, but I fail to see the logic in it! I’ll send out the distress call to the Thunderbolts immediately!” Zemoff replies, hastily rushing off to the car to send the distress signal. Fleming holds himself up against a wall, holding his chest in agony, a resultant of his gruesome battle with Crichton. “Fleming! Did you really think you could get rid of me that easily?” Crichton states, walking up slowly to Fleming. Fleming turns painfully, to face Crichton and a desperate smile fills his face. “I know Crichton! I guess I underestimated the unpredictability of the super soldier serum! But I did notice that the strength of yours only developed when you were angry!” Fleming states, releasing his grip on the wall and standing up straight. A sly grin appears upon Crichton’s face as he draws ever closer to his prey. “Yeah, but you seem to forget that your no longer in that robotic suit of yours and one on one without any tricks or super strength I could kick your ass anyway!” Crichton declares confidently. Fleming nods in agreement with a smug grin etched upon his face. “Uncontested! But I never said anything about this fight being fair! Remember plan B?” Fleming replies. Crichton stops in hi advance and a puzzled look appears upon his face. “What exactly is Plan B?” Crichton questions very wary of the situation presented before him. A wider grin appears upon Fleming’s face. “Ah well! I expect that they’ll be getting here any minute!” Fleming declares cockily. “Who will be getting here shortly?” Crichton asks a concerned look beginning to form upon his face. “The only people who managed to bring you in last time…The Thunderbolts!” Fleming states with relish in his voice. With that the skies seemingly open as all hell breaks loose upon the compound. Bolts of electricity thrown by the Thunderbolt known as Jolt, impact upon the military base’s concrete floor. Crichton takes a step backwards. He sure as hell couldn’t take on all of the Thunderbolts all at once on his own, even though he had a debt to pay with them for getting him into all of this, he couldn’t risk injuring himself before getting even with Fleming. The bombardment continues as Mach III launches a variety of missiles that managed to ravage the majority of the military base. Crichton had already begun to escape the ensuing destruction, dodging missiles this way and bolts of electricity that way making his way to the security fence. Then he comes across the body. Or more accurately what was left of the body. It appeared that the victim had caught both a missile attack and part of an electricity blast. Crichton had only stopped at the corpse because there was something about the body that seemed familiar in way. Crichton managed to turn the corpse over with his foot. As soon as Crichton saw the face of the victim, he recognised her instantly and soon after felt an overwhelming sense of warmth and justice. At last he had a little revenge that he could take away with him, because Fleming would have to live with the fact that through his actions the woman he loved, Ms. Zemoff, had been caught up in the bombardment. He simply walked away from the scene, a smile developing on his face.
