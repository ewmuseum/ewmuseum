Introducing Wimlog Mogre

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Somewhere in the mass of Time... :: The scene opens up on a beautiful sunny day in Cairo, Egypt. As per usual the men and woman out on the streets rush about gathering bits and bats of food, clothing and water for the day, or more than likely the week, ahead. The scene of course opens up outside the home of PCW’s Television champion, The Pharaoh, and the former god can be seen clearly through one of the double glazed windows of the mighty mansion just on the banking of the River Nile. We begin to close in on Titu as he stares, with a smile of deep thought upon his handsome visage, looking outside of his home until finally we find ourselves inside the Palaces interior. As described The Pharaoh stands upright gazing out onto the vast cliffs of rock and the harsh desert far, far away in the distance. Suddenly Pharaoh moves away from the window and heads towards a colossal whitewashed door behind him. The room that we are currently within is well decorated in a light orange and yellow colour scheme. A border of deep yellow featuring many tiny golden hieroglyphics floods the rooms lining and three huge beige sofas find themselves situated in the room’s center. To finish the basic description of the room we have a silver wide-screen Television pushed up towards the far wall, positioned so that the three sofas are all in viewing sight of it. Pharaoh heads for the rooms exit and opens the door with a squeak. We Follow after the TV champion and find that we are now in a long and lengthy corridor, much to the same design as the palace’s lounge. Titu heads towards the end of the hallway until he finds another whitewash door and as before he opens it wide. Behind it we find the palaces dining room. A huge spacious area, it too is furbished in a classical Egyptian design, but in here things seem a little more down to earth. Not many statues of the Gods can be found, unlike in many of the other rooms, and a vast table, obviously for eating at, is found featuring a long white silk cloth over it to help keep scratches off of the table’s surface. Pharaoh walks to the other side of the room where we find a set of two glass doors leading outside onto a patio made from faint grey rock and limestone. Pharaoh opens the doors and heads outside onto the terrace. There, just outside the patio, is Isiy Xansiv, soaking up the sun in the green flourishing garden of the Palace whilst reading a book. A huge grin overcomes the TV champ’s face as he sees her laying on her stomach upon a huge red towel, wearing a light yellow bikini, quite a revealing one at that, and wearing a pair of fashionable Oakley shades upon the bridge of her nose. Titu heads towards his wife and then sits down next to her on an additional towel by her side. :: The Pharaoh: Has the mail arrived yet, my love? :: Isiy looks up toward Pharaoh, his face reflects in the lens’ of the Oakley shades. :: Isiy: Nope…what are you waiting for anyway? :: The Pharaoh sighs doubtfully. :: The Pharaoh: The card for the next show, Friday Fusion…I think I might have to defend the TV title against Jason Core Isiy: (Turning back to her book) Well we do live in Egypt; you have to expect the postal service to take a while to get here from back over in America…and even if you do have to fight Core I’m sure that you’ll be able to defeat him! The Pharaoh: (smiling) You have a good point! Jason Core wouldn’t stand a chance in the ring against me! I’m at the goddamn peak of my career! Isiy: Exactly… :: A silence falls over the pair.:: The Pharaoh: I was thinking… :: Isiy takes her eyes back off the pages of her book and looks up at Titu again. :: Isiy: Thinking what? The Pharaoh: Well ever since Horus left I seem to have lost the competitive edge over my opponents… You see when Horus was by my side he’d advise me on how best to overcome an opponent … Isiy: What are you getting at? The Pharaoh: Well I was thinking of hiring an advisor! :: Isiy frowns in confusion, but her expression can’t be made out because of the sunglasses shielding her gorgeous brown eyes from the sun. :: Isiy: An Advisor? But what for? :: Pharaoh sighs in disbelief. :: The Pharaoh: Don’t you listen to anything I say, my love? For advising, Isiy! To give me advise on things, ya’ know like an advisor would… Isiy: Sorry, Titu, but I don’t see any point in doing so… :: Pharaoh frowns. :: The Pharaoh: You see, Isiy, I hope to get quite far in Primetime Championship Wrestling, and without an advisor to guide me then the possibility of that happening seems fairly low. :: Isiy turns away from her husband and continues to read her book. :: Isiy: If you say so, Titu. Go out and find someone to ‘advise’ you then… :: Pharaoh grins, obviously not picking up on his wife’s sarcasm, and stands up proudly. :: The Pharaoh: (Arrogantly) Then that is what I shall do! I shall find myself an advisor! :: Pharaoh begins to walk away from Isiy when she suddenly shouts something to him causing him to turn around to hear her out. :: Isiy: Don’t forget to pick up a bodyguard on the way… :: She smiles sarcastically, but she’s quite amused by her own irony. :: The Pharaoh: (Smugly) Excellent idea…since Carmoon has been gone I have been missing the protection of a Bodyguard…this time perhaps I will hire two, or three! :: Hearing this Isiy sighs again. The scene begins to fade out but quickly return to a view of The Pharaoh walking aimlessly in the cobblestone streets of Cairo. The sun looms down harshly upon him as he walks eyeing many people up and down, but none seem to be what he’s looking for. :: The Pharaoh: Who would have ever thought that out of all the population of Cairo nobody would fit up to my expectations.. :: Pharaoh sighs and shakes his head to and fro. :: The Pharaoh: Damn this expedition, it makes no sense at all! Isiy was right! Why am I looking for an advisor anyway! :: Not a moment too soon has he said these words that a spindly hand full of five long sharp fingers pat Titu on the shoulder. :: Voice: Looking for an advisor, are we, sir? :: Pharaoh spins around in fright. :: The Pharaoh: What in the name of Horus?! :: Right in front of Pharaoh is a tiny man, or creature as would be a more fitting name for this spindly fingered devil, of a grotesque visage and stature, his back is arched and his head is covered by a hood that is attached to the back of a long thin black robe. The man looks to be somewhere in his mid forties and his fingers are sharp as razors, deadly nails arch out of his fingers. :: Man: I thought I heard you say that you couldn’t find an advisor…well, my friend… The Pharaoh: Just shut up for a second… :: Pharaoh eyes the ugly cretin up and down and nods his head with a smile. :: The Pharaoh: Perfect! You look the part anyway; the whole creepy, sneaky, ugly bits are all in the right places… :: The short man frowns in response to Pharaoh’s ‘ugly’ comment. :: Man: Hey! I’m nowhere near ugly! (With a smug grin) I have ravishingly good looks! :: We close in on the cretins face. His wrinkled skin features many open pours, but they aren’t filled with puss or dirt, his eyes are small and squinted and an evil brooding look fills them. One of his mysterious eyes is blue and his other is a rather dirty brown in hue. His lips are huge and bulbous and his nose is long, thin and pointed. His ears are quite pointy too, and he has a remarkable resemblance to a goblin. :: The Pharaoh: (Doubtfully) No…of course not. :: Pharaoh studies the ‘thing’ in front of him with confused gaze. :: The Pharaoh: Now onto more pressing matters. Let me ask you this… what is your name, fiendish creature? :: The man is quick to respond, but the tone of his voice shows a deep anger about him, obviously triggered by Xansiv’s ‘Fiendish creature’ comment. He is obviously very wary about his appearance. :: Man: My name is Wimlog Mogre, sir! :: The Pharaoh seems intrigued and as Wimlog speaks Pharaoh notices his sharp serpentine tongue, lashing from side to side as he speaks. :: The Pharaoh: (Stroking chin thoughtfully) Hmm, Wimlog Mogre, eh? Well Mr Mogre have you any experience in advising greats such as myself? :: Wimlog smiles evilly. :: Wimlog: Yess, yess I have… :: Mogre seems to speak with a slight lisp, which come out as a faint hiss. :: Wimlog: I have much, Mr-… :: He trails off purposely, seemingly looking for Pharaoh to fill him in on his name too. :: The Pharaoh: Xansiv, the name’s Xansiv… :: Pharaoh pauses for a second with doubtful expression disturbing his striking mug. :: The Pharaoh: Are you of an Egyptian heritage? :: Wimlog is quick to reply. :: Wimlog: Of coursse I am, masster… :: Pharaoh grins arrogantly looking the hunch-backed man up and down with keen eyes of great interest. :: The Pharaoh: Then follow me, Mr Mogre! You just found yourself a place as my new advisor… :: Wimlog snickers wickedly. :: Wimlog: Excellent, Ssire :: Pharaoh begins to back off in the direction he has just walked, presumably the way back to the Palace. Wimlog Follows eagerly. :: Wimlog: So, what will I be advising you on, my Masster? :: Pharaoh smiles superciliously. :: The Pharaoh: Well you’ll find that I am actually a wrestler working for a company named PCW, it stands for Primetime Championship Wrestling, now I want to get to the top of the stock pile, as it would be, and to do so I have decided that an advisor would be perfect on giving me…well advise on situations in the business… Wimlog: Ah! I see! Well to be honest I haven’t really got the slightest idea about the wrestling industry, but I do have the knowledge that you must face an opponent or perhaps many in matches. So it would seem appropriate if I asked whom your next opponent, or opponents for that matter, may be? :: Pharaoh sighs. :: The Pharaoh: The thing is I really haven’t got a clue…the card for Friday Fusion, the next show that I should be involved in, has yet to arrive in the post from America, but I have the certainty that it could very well turn out to be against a man by the name of Jason Core… :: Mogre strokes his chin thoughtfully. :: Wimlog: Well then…why don’t you tell me a little bit about this ‘Jason Core’? :: Pharaoh frowns as he begins to tell of the former Extreme champion’s life, wrestling career and business as a trainer in the business of professional wrestling. Mogre smiles wickedly as he is filled in on Jason’s facts. Our screen begins to fade to black :: “I have the perfect plan, Masster…” :: Exclaims Mogre as the scene finally fades to black. ::
