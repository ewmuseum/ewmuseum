adding gas to the fire (Vurdo territory #3)

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28-10-02 Bob Vurdo has been beside himself after his one true love Rosanna was taken by Ronald Jackson. To add to that one of his greatest friends and possibly one of the greatest allies to the Vurdo family Sammy 'the slugger' Sacila. Sammy killed more men for the Vurdo family than any other hired gun in the history of the industry. Bob Vurdo has never been in love before and he had all that taken away from him by one man Ron Jackson. Once he noticed that Rosanna was gone he chased after the men but he was to late, they had already driven off with his girlfriend. Since then Bob hasn't been able to do a thing. He has searched every corner of his head for an option and a way to get Rosanna back. Bob sits in his chair in his bedroom looking out through the large window onto the street he lives on and his front yard. He is still trying to find a solution to getting Rosanna back but he can't come up with a thing. He notices a mail truck pull up outside the house and press the buzzer at the gate. Bob watches the fence open and the truck drive up the driveway to deliver the package yet he doesn't move. He doesn't really care for anything at the moment and he won't be able to care for anything until he has Rosanna back. Bob notices his mother collect the package from the mailman as he looks down at the front door. His mother signs for the package before closing the door. The man walks back through Bob's path and hops in his delivery van before driving back down the driveway. 'Robert!!!' Her yell can only just be heard from downstairs. His mother has been doing her best to give Bob space since the incident. 'I'm coming!' Bob gets up from his seat and walks downstairs. He opens up the door and walks down a small hallway before appearing on a small verandah that leads to the stairs. He walks quietly down the stairs in an effortless fashion. His mother stands in the lobby holding a package in her arms as she looks up at her son. 'A mailman just delivered this package and its addressed to you.' Bob walks up to her mother as she smiles at him and collects the parcell. He gives her a kiss on the forehead before walking away and saying 'thanks.' He walks into the lounge room where he takes a seat on a lounge where he opens up the package. He sees a handwritten note on top of a video tape. Bob looks over it in a curious fashion before picking up the note and reading it. Watch the video and see my proposal. Bob curls his lip up and takes a deep breath in to prepare himself for the video. He gets up from his seat and puts the video in the VCR before turning the television on and sitting back down in his seat. Rosanna is sitting in a dark room with no light sauce at all other than the one that's shining above the camera. She is on a chair tied down with rope half naked with her top and bra off. There are big red marks across her chest above her breasts where it looks like someone has been beating her. She looks up at the camera revealing her bruised face and tears streaming out of her eyes. Her mouth is gagged tightly by a piece of cloth. The black man Ron Jackson then walks into the shot with a sick smile on his face. He moves around behind Rosanna and leans over her before kissing her on the cheek. He then starts giving her a massage which makes her scream into the gag and wiggle around on the chair. Ron looks up at the camera with a sick look in his eyes like he is the devil himself. 'I've been riding this bitch non stop for the last twenty four hours Bob.' Ron has a certain coldness in his voice. 'However I have a proposition for you.... If you and your friends leave town by the end of this week then I will let her go with you. Amber Hill will be my town Bob Vurdo and unless you want to see your bitch get killed there's nothing you can do about it. If you haven't moved in one week I will let every one of my friends ride this bitch while she's strapped down to the bed for the rest of her life.' Ron then leans over more and licks her nipple before looking up at the camera and smiling sadistically as the video stops. Bob is on the couch in tears now he can only imagine what this man is putting Rosanna through. He has a big decision to make which is already made up in his mind but how will he get the rest of his men to move from Amber Hill. Everyone in the family loves Amber Hill as it is such a nice town and getting them to move with their families will be a hard task. ---------------------***------------------- Tony is walking down the main street of Amber Hill looking at the beautiful place. He had to get out of the house and go for a walk as he was fed up with sitting around doing nothing while Ron Jackson got away with the murder of Sammy. As he walks down the street in the distance he notices a group of black men sitting on the steps of a house on the main street. More and more blacks appear to be moving here everyday Tony thinks to himself. Look what they have done already imagine what it will be like if we keep letting them move down here. Tony takes another look at the black punks before pulling out his mobile phone and dialing a number. 'Yeah its Tony.' 'Hey Tony what can I do for you,' the voice on the other end answers. 'I need you to meet me at the nightclub and I want you to bring a few friends.' 'I'll be there in half an hour.' Tony hangs up the phone and begins walking toward the nightclub. 'We'll kill those fucking black assholes off in no time.' ------------------***----------------- Tony and four other men that each look intimidating as far as size goes are gathered around a table talking. There is a light that hangs low from the ceiling that provides the light for the room. There are box's of DVD's stacked in the back corner which are most likely stolen. 'In case you haven't noticed all these black fuckas have been moving here.' The other men look at Tony in silence. They all have a lot of respect for Tony and listen to everything he says. 'Yeah I had a run in with one of them the other day and all these other nigger fucks stepped in and they called themselves a gang.' The relatively young member of the Vurdo family speaks in a classic high pitched Italian accent. 'A fucking gang... What the fuck is that? They aren't a fucking gang.... They don't know what they are talking about. They give the world of organized crime a bad name.' The other young man obviously takes pride in the fact he works for Bob Vurdo as all these men do. 'As you know Sammy was killed by one of these nigger fucks and we can't let them get away with it. If they get away with this it will make us look weak and they will think they can walk right over us. No they can't we're the fucking Vurdo family and we have owned Amber Hill since the beginning.' The other men nod at Tony and are obviously listening to what he has to say as they don't take their eyes off him. 'I want you all to stay away from them when by yourselves as they hunt in packs like wolves. We need to take them out strategically so before you kill any of these men you see me. We will get rid of them and we will do it quick but we need to do it in a planned out manner otherwise it could end up lethal. They have the advantage of knowing where we live as we have been here for years and we don't have a fucking clue where their homes are.' Tony pauses and looks around at these young men who have so much respect for him. 'Go home tonight and stock up and during the next few days I'll give you your orders.' Tony doesn't say good bye he just gets up and walks out of the room.
