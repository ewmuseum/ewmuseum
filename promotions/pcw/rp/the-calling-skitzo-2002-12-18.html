The Calling (Skitzo)

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18 December The scene opened in the middle of nowhere, swamp and mash land as far as the eye could see, far from the hectic City life in a rural setting where people tend not to venture. The whole area had an eerie silence with the occasional rustle of bushes and the odd croak from neighbouring toads, suddenly a swarm of birds flood the sky as they leave their nesting area of the tree line. Rippling water and a surrounding forest of bushes and thick trees that have been there for century undisturbed suggest a place confined in the heart of the Florida everglades. The camera panned in the hope of finding activity and failed doing so, no signs of human life, not even a tread mark left by a crocodile. Swaying left to right like a metal detector and having similar success a glistening can was unveiled, a can of Gatorade to be specific, well a line of cans that littered the moist ground. The cans stretched into the dark undergrowth and with a huge dose of curiosity the camera tracked the litterbug who would be the culprit for such debris. One can after another and the amount kept rising as the cans continued to multiply, obvious the owner had a serious thirst, a serious thirst for Gatorade meaning only but one individual-.... SKITZO!!! As the bushes finally cleared, low and behold stood a dank dwelling built above the water line with wooden poles submersed into the moist ground. The hut had a thatched roof and was built from natural resources, ninety percent wood bonded together with straw creating an effective rope. A rickety building hidden from humanity couldn't be deemed inviting, more of a Texas Chainsaw Massacre feel, yet with intrigue the camera pressed on trying to unearth the truth. Since the structure was above the ground level the entrance had a flight of steps to tackle and with every step the wood creaked and warped as though the scene were something from the horror movie genre. Tackling the final step, which was frail and almost shattering under the cameraman’s weight the door stood as the next daunting obstacle, a wooden structure made of vertical slats that had been left ajar. 'TOODLE DOODLE DO!' The deafening sound came from within. 'TOODLE DOODLE DO TIDDLY PEEPS!' The next image was almost unbelievable as we witnessed Skitzo slouched in a dusty sofa covered in patches, sitting fixated on a television set that been tuned into a children’s programme. The unbelievable episode was the fact that this damp, dank dwelling had indeed electricity; from first inspection you would assume a wig-wam would have such a luxury over such a shabby home. 'Dis be some mothafuckin white boyz programme homes, holla back youngin!' murmured the thirst driven Skitzo as he opened another can of Gatorade, gulping down the content and discarding the packaging to the floor. 'Stop talking like a Scottish Negroid, huh?!?' said Mr. Psycho slapping himself across his grime and bristle ridden face. 'Like hurt'zzies, hello, you hit me and not number nine. This programme is very educational and so well presented, while you lugs sit guzzling Gatorade all day you could be paying The Hoobs some attention and be learning their secret welcoming gesture!' retorted Skitzo to himself, his face curling up into a cringe and his eyes glazed over with an evil glint. Suddenly the PCW superstar began to sway his hands into a peculiar welcoming gesture as shown on the show, entitled The Hoobs. Meanwhile though his face became enraged as his hands continued to take on a life of their own, performing countless child-like gestures, when suddenly his body sprung from his seating position and onto the bare wooden floor. 'AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH~!' Rolling about obscured from view by the positioning of the sofa, it looked as though Skitzo were beating himself into a bloodied pulp as he howled in pain, followed by the occasion evil chuckle then back to his dog like yelps. 'Oh, blood I'm going to pass out-....' 'AND I'M GOING TO FUCKING KNOCK YOU OUT BITCH!!!' 'Whoa guys break it up, we've all got to live under the same roof!?!' said Skitzo defensively in a tone of authority. Almost immediately Skitzo sprung up from the ground, standing erect as though he were a young student being told off by an authority figure. His cheekbone bruised, a small cut on the ridge of his forehead and a trickle of blood running from his nose, collecting over the top of his upper lip, which he cleared up courtesy of a tissue he unveiled from his shirt pocket. 'Now say your sorry!' roared the official spokesman of PCW endorsed product, Gatorade. With his eyes roaming from side to side in a pendulum fashion, devious and squint and looking rather wary his right arm suddenly reached across to his left and Skitzo exchanged a handshake with himself. Smiling, looking all jovial, and as though by an act of God his self inflicted injuries had vanished, the multi persona individual returned to his seat in a fog of dust. With his fingers wandering over the nearby remote, the video creaked into action and began to play footage of his bout against the then Tag Team Champions Pranks With Attitude. 'Look at the way I beat that pussy green Jester-....' 'WAIT!!!' 'That's moi fucking up dat Crackaz With Attitude biatch Gazzy G!' 'I think not - You didn't even show up-....' 'THAT WAS SOOOOO ME GIVING MR. GREEN THE SKITZO SLAP 'O' DOOM~!' 'FUCK OFF!!! YOU COULDN'T EVEN BEAT BLADED?!?' 'YOU COULDN'T BEAT BLADED? That's right, you ran from Avalanche at Alliances2K2, did you run because he has a better work rate - Ah love you!' concluded one of the many personalities of Skitzo, which spurned a hoard of varied laughter, from chuckles, to child like giggles, to heavy wheezing that represented laughter. As the hysterics grew, sounding like a pack of whaling hyenas, the party was intruded upon by the crashing sound that came from the wooden door. Sounding like someone was knocking with a clenched fist, Skitzo merely sighed and waltzed over to answer unveiling a scrawny teenage boy ridden in a serious case of bad acne. Scratching and rubbing his scalp, that flaked he stood rather grotesque and in silence, spurning Skitzo into questioning his reasons for being there. 'Arr-... Err-... Can I help you squire?!?' asked the strange being to another. 'Ye got ya a letter here, it be almost a week 'ol now!' replied the young lad slow and monotone as he continued to stand statue like at the entrance of Skitzo's homely dwelling. 'Talk mothafuckin African-American white biatch, how does ya expect a bruv'er to understand dat fuckin nonsense without a white boy at hand to translate, huh?!?' injected the Ebonics ranting Skitzo with his own dose of Playaz Inc's Statuz Quo, yet another personality from his existing eighty six. 'Why my friend means is, could you be a little more specific?' said another Skitzo. The boy stood dumbfounded, his mouth ajar and looking all confused, even before he can retort any statement Skitzo tried to usher the answer with another personality taking centre stage. 'FUCKING HURRY UP ALREADY MAN?!?' Having the letter snatched from his tight grip the teenage lad snarled and questioned Skitzo's actions once more, his voice almost robot like showing those watching the native tongue of these parts. 'Are ye taking da piss of me boy - If so I'd be giving you a fick lip, ok!' As the boy raised his clenched fist, Skitzo began to have a spasm, twitching violently and frantically jerking about on the spot, watched on by his assailant who scratches his head star struck and dumb. Suddenly after several minutes of what appeared as an epileptic fit, Skitzo miraculously created eleven other clones that stood with their own personal fighting stances and the odds look to have favoured Mr. Psycho in this particular contest. With a huge gulp and a pivot in his foot the lad turned darting off the edge of the raised platform and face first into the bog below, scurrying into the sunset that now looms. 'AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH~!' With one Skitzo ripping open the letter, the others gathered around and Skitzo number one read aloud as the group felt contempt just to listen. 'Dear Skitzo/s, We Pranks With Attitude would like to offer you the opportunity to become number one contenders for the Tag Team throne by merely following the directions enclosed on the back of this letter and attending a special meeting with all those we have deemed worthy. We have no ulterior motives, nor sinister master plan, this is a chance for you to get back into the title scene within Primetime Championship Wrestling and hopefully strengthen the Tag division. Enclosed are the address and relevant information for this event. Thank You Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A)' A few minutes of an eerie silence became broken up as each Skitzo in this twelve-man party voiced their views with this special meeting conducted by recent nemesis's Pranks With Attitude. One after another each spoke, reveling in the opportunity to adorn gold and be the second only individual to solely claim the Tag Team Championship alongside Akiyama. 'This is to good an opportunity to throw away - FELLOW SKITZO'S I HERE BY DECLARE THAT WE SHALL ATTEND THIS SHENANIGAN AND CONQUER, TOO THE LONG SHIPS!!!' cried one jovial Skitzo holding his arms aloft in a triumphant pose as the rest poured from the building and down to the murky shore below. Cluttering into the undergrowth each four-man team of Skitzo's emerged with a canoe that had been hidden from sight by the placing of thick bushes and plant life. Entering the vessels the three small four-man crafts ventured into the water as they headed into the distance in unison. One stroke after another all in unison the boats finally disappeared from view, being but dots in the distance the only tell tale signs being the disturbed and rippled waters left behind by their rowing. - To be continued -
