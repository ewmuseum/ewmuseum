Becoming…

28 April 2003

Date: 28/04/03 Pretty Cool ChroniclesChapter #4: Becoming… You know, last time on ‘Pretty Cool Chronicles’, I really did have that little solemn hope that Will Smith would have come along to Turmoil with us this coming week, even if it were to just… you know, scream like a little Girl and hope that PCG were going to come through in both of their exciting matches this week. I mean, I would have been very scared and probably questioned his whole performance, but perhaps it would have driven us along the route to victory and then, the Tag Team Championships a few weeks afterwards? In any case, Ryan and myself were going to have to look around the United States of America for somebody else to join our most awesome movement. You know, the one that tries to convince people that just because we like to sing PoP music almost all the time, we’re not exactly homosexual and we don’t produce anything more than cheap poop reborn from the 1970’s! Anyway, forgetting about all of those little problems that we’re trying to get through before the end of time, today was set to be something special for both Ryan and myself. You see, ever since MTV and such came into our little sad lives, we’ve always dreamed of being able to have something where, we would be given the chance to act like our heroes. Well, perhaps not our musical heroes… or even our favourite Power Rangers, but we would like to act like two very special people that also recently made it across to PCW. You see, if two people… exactly like them, can make it all the way over to a company as grand as this, wouldn’t that mean that… we’d also be able to make ourselves seem somewhat better? -- Production Start Production Date: 28/04/03Current Location: Some Street that’d lost its nametag X.x;; The scene slowly begins to open in what could only be described as a busy street, crowded on both sides of the sidewalk, cars seemingly… bumper to bumper in the road itself. Today was going to be another one of those special days that we always tend to be having, although, there just won’t be any Will Smith coming along and walking away after sitting down for the smallest matter of five minutes. Today, we were going to imitate a couple of people that, well… need to be imitated for the greater need of mankind and the World’s community as a whole. ‘Who are those two very special people?’ I hear any and all cry from their confinement of their resting place. Well, they just happen to be a couple of people that I like to call… E2 and what that ‘E’ just happens to stand for, I guess I’ll try to find out before the end of next week or something? Anyway, not getting too lost on a question that’ll no doubt stimulate the brain of men and women alike for many generations to come, this whole charade isn’t to mock our new heroes… its not to, make them look stupid or actually lower their true value in the eyes of society… which isn’t going to be too hard, I mean… they’re called ‘E2’ for crying out loud, doesn’t that show you enough about their value? As everything finally comes through to being an open view, I find myself standing on the sidewalk dressed like John Gargantuanko, a man that… just seems to be a bit bigger than myself in both the department of height and weight. To compensate for this lack of weight, I’d buffed myself up with a few pads and stuff. Aside from making me a little sweatier than I’d hoped, I’m sure that I’ll be able to pull off the impression without any hassles. As for Ryan Moore, he’d be dressed like up our other new icon, Tyre Preston… a man that doesn’t seem to be as heavy as me although I’ve still got to find out the result with regards to the issue of who’s the taller athlete. Regardless, that’s the character that Ryan Moore is currently portraying… and whilst, in reality we appear to look nothing like those two individuals, I’m sure that due to the fact their popularity isn’t really anything great… we’re going to be able to pass ourselves off as them without too many issues. “… Damn Ry… I mean, Tyre, how long do you think it’s going to be until something worthwhile happens? I’m walking around this God damn city and I still don’t have any idea what the Hell, the ‘E’ in our name means?” I shouted out aloud, raising my hands up, grasping the air and pulling quite the strain on my face. People in the packed street began to look at me with funny expressions in their eyes, although a lot of them just happened to keep moving along, taking care of their own personal business ventures. “Rich… Uh, I mean, Gargantuanko… I’m not quite sure if something GOOD is going to happen to us ever at the moment. I mean, we’re going to be stuck in that awesomely bad… Tag Team match with two people, who are… probably going to smash me into the ground, twice in the same night or something. Not to mention, I still don’t know whether the ‘E’ in our name means, Excellence… or, something like Evangelion?” Ryan said shortly after I yelled out, showing the same kind of intensity and interest that I’d also shown. A few more people turned their attentions to us now, continuing to wonder what the Hell was happening. In any normal case, we would have been more than willing to reveal all of our plans to these, typically kind and serene people, but if we were to continue making those ‘E2’ people look as bad as possible, then keeping our identity partially hush-hush was the way to go. As people slowly began to return their attention to what they were doing, Ryan and I, dressed exactly the same as their opponents for Turmoil, slowly began to rise to our feet, helping each other reach that vertical base. I looked around for a few seconds and wiped my brow, flicking some sweat off onto the floor moments later. I really shouldn’t have tried to make Gargantuanko look like some kind of obese man with all these pads… damn, they were so coming close to killing me with their insulation of heat. Regardless of this new found annoyance, both Ryan and myself continued to walk along the street dressed as our new heroes, waving to people, trying to get as much attention on ourselves as possible. Finally, after an hour and a half of walking through people that really couldn’t give two hoots about what we were doing, a teenage boy walked up to us. He was dressed in all that punk gear that people like wearing and his face wore a… somewhat, startled expression. “… Just who the Hell are you two supposed to be?” He asked, sounding somewhat hostile and volatile. I’d told Ryan about the dangers of these snot nosed teenage males that would stumble around the street, doing nothing but looking for insane levels of trouble. I took a step back and pretended to admire the decoration on a wall as Ryan stepped forward and looked at the youth. “Well, I happen to be from this new PCW Tag Team called ‘Evangelion 2’ and my partner over there, John-Boy Garganuanko, decided that it’d be cool for us to come onto the street and try and make ourselves look somewhat… well, no, just make ourselves noticed in the eyes of the public. You know, break ourselves into the flow of media.” He said, sounding professional with every word. I found myself turning away from the wall, looking over to Ryan and this individual as they started to slip into some form of conversation. Personally, I didn’t want to even be around here anymore… with the heat and everything, eh… and now the fact that the suit was started to stick to my body in more ways than one. “Ah… I heard about you two making your mega-elusive debut over in PCW against those little woofer boys, the “Pretty Cool Guys.” Yeah, I thought I’d heard about you two on the Howard Stern show… but I just wasn’t sure about what you looked like or anything.” The youth responded, bouncing a little in the way that those thug kids would, wiping his nose with the back of his hand, a trait that was generally only found in those icky gang-banger people. “Anyway…” he soon started again, looking around his immediate area before continuing, “… I know how you two seem to be loaded now with your big contracts and everything, so like… how about you just pull out your wallets, spare a brotha a little cash and be on your way, eh?” “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!” I screamed, suddenly bursting out in hysterics. I felt like rolling around the floor, screaming out as this kid was seemingly going to try and steal a load of money from us. I had no idea that he wasn’t joking at the time, but when a 13yr old kid comes up to somebody like us and tries to demand the smallest sum of money… what in Gods name are we supposed to do? Hand it over and move along like this act of pure crime never happened? As much as I’d like to say ‘yes’… I don’t… instead, I wait for everyones favourite superhero, “Rent-A-Hero” to come along and save the day. I mean, we sung at one of the mans parties… so at the very least, he could come along and save his two PoP pals from having all their cash stolen. “What’s so funny?!” the kid screamed to the two of us, pulling out what seemed to be a flick knife from the pocket of his pants. He flicked it open and held it out like some kind of overly offensive weapon… an action which actually forced the two of us to take a couple of steps backward, you know… to just, try and avoid becoming best friends with the overly sharp object! I looked into the sky for some kind of sign that would help us get on top of things… for some kind of, image or weapon that’d help us in our quest to get away from the bastardised individual. “R-r-r-r-rent-a-hero? Oh Mister Rent-a-Hero?” Ryan said out aloud in a nervous tone, his eyes darting from left to right, from top to bottom, desperately searching for the protector of all that is good, clean and nice in this World. I looked around in the same kind of frenzied panic, continuing to take steps backward whilst the assailant continued to take those little steps towards us. As the seconds continued to pass us by, we kinda realized that nobody was going to come along to save the day or our lives… if anything good was to happen, then we’d have to make it happen ourselves. However, dressed as these two individuals, could we really do anything that’d make the ‘E2’ duo seem any more pathetic that what we wanted them to seem? “… Ryan, if we happen to do something and totally apprehend this criminal, don’t you think that ‘E2’ will be treated like heroes or something?” I whispered to Ryan, my eyes now focused straight ahead, not even considering the chances of reverting away to look in another direction. “Oh… Damn, I forgot all about that little hitch in our plan. If we do anything that would seem remotely good, they could be the ones going into the match with all this press and hype about being some form of mega-hero that took down some scummy youth thief!” he said in a tone that was a little louder than just whispering. The young assailant caught wind of the ‘Scummy’ comment in the line and decided to turn the tempo on his pressuring a little more. “Look, I don’t care about who you are or what you’re wanting to do… all I need is a little money to get the few magazines that I want. Playboy… Shonen Jump and some other stuff, that’s all I’m going to be asking you for and if I get that, then nobody is going to be getting hurt.” the youth informed the two of us, raising the knife a little higher now, stopping as the tip of the blade became level with his shoulder. I contemplated about pulling out my wallet and just handing over the money that was required… but then, I remembered that I’d left it in my proper clothing back home. “Dude, look… we’re kinda poor at the moment and haven’t got our wallets on us or anything. Is there any chance that you’d be willing to take some form of IOU? Or perhaps a couple of tickets to the concert of… the Pretty Cool Guys?” Ryan offered, his voice showing a few signs of fear, a few beads of sweat beginning to formulate over his brow and run down the side of his face. “… I don’t want to have any of th…” the thief started to say before I jumped ahead and knocked into him with my padding. The kid dropped down to the floor and his knife flew over somewhere else, the screams of some elderly person coming shortly thereafter. Assuming that it was nothing more than screams from a horrific sight somewhere, I jumped up and came crashing over the fiendish villain with a gentle stomp to his chest! “… Rich, are you sure you wanted to actual do something of that kind to the kid?” Ryan asked, questioning my actions. I looked down to the kid and saw that I may have done a little more damage than would have been intended. Chuckling nervously, I stepped off the boys frail body and slowly began to walk off with Ryan Moore, assuming that nobody had seen any of what had happened. Our plan to make ‘Evangelion 2’ look pathetic had failed somewhat, at least… except for the mindless screaming at the beginning of it all, nobody really wanted to pay that much attention to what we were doing. … All I can do for now, is wonder what the Hell will happen the next time something like this happens to us? With the scene slowly beginning to fade into the confinements of darkness, Ryan and myself hightail it outta that street… running off into yet another musical sunset! -- Production End
