Mentality pt1- The Arrival

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th Apr As seen on Fusion, The Mexican Devil El Diablo was taken away kicking and screaming all because of one of Flaymin Aymin's plans. For Flaymin Aymin has managed to get El Diablo locked away in a Mental Institute somewhere in South Carolina. The question surrounding everything though is if El Diablo will still be able to wrestle for PCW or be locked up in the Institute like a rat in a cage. 'Raul Diaz!' a guard shouts out. El Diablo is of course in a waiting room at the front of the Institute where all new inmates have to go to get signed in and generally have their dignity and self-respect taken away from them. Diablo stands up now that his name has been called. He looks around curiously at the waiting room, it's rather small with only six or seven seats in there and a table in the middle. Of course the seats and table are bolted to the ground, and the walls of the room are painted a plain white with a light blue carpet to finish off the room. 'Mr Stephenson will see you now,' the guard adds. An angered looking El Diablo slowly walks towards the door where the guard is at and casually looks at him as he walks by. The guard is much taller than the 5'9' Diablo but doesn't look as muscular as Diablo is. He has shaved brown hair and just looks like a genuine hard bastard. The guard now walks behind El Diablo leading him through a little hallway up to Mr Stephenson's office. 'Go in,' the guard demands. The Mexican Devil shakes his head and opens the brown wooden door into, before entering the room. He then shuts the door behind him and takes the nearest seat available. El Diablo looks around and notices that this office is larger than the waiting room. The walls are painted lilac and lots of framed honors are across the walls. The carpet is dark blue, and on top of it are Mr Stephenson's desk, a filing cabinet and four chairs. Diablo also notices that the window view Mr Stephenson has is quite pleasant. Mr Stephenson now lifts his head up from the desk after finishing some files. 'Hello Raul, I see you already found a seat so lets just begin,' he speaks politely. Diablo smirks as he recognises whom this Mr Stephenson is, it's one of the two people that dragged him away on Fusion. Mr Stephenson is obviously the overseer of the Institute, or 'Warden' if you will. This Mr Stephenson looks like he is in his late thirties - early forties, as he has thinning brown hair with many gray bits in it, and has many wrinkles under his eyes. To top it off, he is wearing his white coat. 'So Raul, do you know why you're here?' the Warden asks him. The Mexican Devil runs his hands through his dyed red hair and then looks Mr Stephenson dead in the eyes. 'Not a clue. I'm sane damn you, sane,' Diablo says in a low toned voice that is surprisingly calm. Mr Stephenson taps his pen on the desk while looking at Diablo's clothing, which consist of a black T-Shirt that reads 'The Pinnacle of Perfection' and his black denim jeans. 'You see Raul, that is not the impression we get,' The Warden states slowly, 'Mr Aymin went to the liberty of showing us some video tapes of you and your antics. And I must say that you didn't look calm or sane in any of them,' he finishes while adding some new notes on El Diablo. Diablo sighs loudly and looks down at the blue carpet. 'Of course I wouldn't, he will have showed you all the wrong moments of me. Everybody gets crazy every once in a while, and what he did on Fusion was justification enough for me to do what I was going to do,' El Diablo responds, trying his hardest to keep his cool. Mr Stephenson takes some more notes. 'I see,' he comments while taking these notes. Diablo is not happy at all about being trapped here and is looking forward to only one thing- getting out of there. But it doesn't look likely to be an answer, no matter how calm El Diablo plays it. 'I have a wife. You're going to take me away from her?' Diablo asks Mr Stephenson. The Warden lifts his head and smiles. 'Most of the inmates here have families. So don't worry, she'll be allowed to visit once a week,' Stephenson replies, insinuating that seeing his wife Angela once a week is enough for him. This prompts Diablo to nod his head and smile. 'Yeah, I know what this is,' he exclaims. The Warden looks baffled. 'Excuse me?' Diablo's smile goes to that of a serious look. 'IT'S BECAUSE I'M MEXICAN, INNIT?' he suddenly yells. 'No, not at all,' Mr Stephenson replies in panic. Diablo stands up all red in the face. 'Yeah, yeah. We're good enough to scrub your toilets clean and steal land from, but we're not good enough to make a lot of money and be famous,' he states loudly. Mr Stephenson looks a little worried now. 'No, that's not the case,' he tries to reassure the angered El Diablo. 'Yeah. Take our land, give us menial jobs, but the second we become a success we get carted away. BIGOT! BIGOT!' El Diablo yells. Mr Stephenson now stands up. 'Sit down Mr Diaz. It's wild accusations like that, which will make your stay here even longer,' The Warden tells El Diablo sternly. Diablo reluctantly sits down and scratches the goatee on his chin. The look in El Diablo's eyes is of disillusion. Disillusion of why he is even there. 'There now isn't that better?' The Warden asks him while he goes to sit down. Diablo stares at him with evil eyes. 'No, no it's not,' he mutters out a response. Mr Stephenson now goes back to writing some notes on the new inmate, before looking back up at him. 'Now Raul, after what we've seen in the past and what we've seen today we're not giving you a set date of when you can leave. But during your time here, we are going to make you into a respectable person ready to fit in to today's society,' he says to El Diablo, almost in a mocking tone. Diablo now looks very pissed off as he scratches at his goatee. 'I'm a wrestler, how am I meant to wrestle if I'm locked away here?' Diablo asks, conceding that he'll be in this institute a while. The Warden smiles. 'That's the beauty of this arrangement. Whenever you are scheduled to wrestle you will be temporarily released, but must have at least three Institute workers go with you. However, if you are not scheduled to wrestle you will not be free to leave this institute,' Mr Stephenson reassures El Diablo that he will still be able to do the thing he loves most, wrestle. Diablo smirks momentarily. 'See, isn't that much better? We're not being too harsh regarding your status,' he states to El Diablo while taking more notes on him. The Mexican Devil stands up with an angry look on his face again. 'No, this isn't better. I'd rather be in the real world than be stuck in rubber walls land. I AM THE PINNACLE OF PERFECTION, I AM THE APEX OF EXCELLENCE AND I AM THE SUMMIT OF SUCCESS! I do not deserve this treatment, I merit more dammit,' Diablo yells. Meanwhile Mr Stephenson simply remains writing. 'LISTEN TO ME YOU SON OF A BITCH! YOU'RE LOCKING ME UP, YET ALLOWING THAT MORON FLAYMIN AYMIN TO WALK THE STREETS. HOW INSANE ARE YOU? HE'S THE ONE THAT MAKES ME CRAZY. HE MAKES IT HIS ROLE IN LIFE TO RUIN MY LIFE. HAS THE WHOLE DAMN WORLD JUST GONE MAD?' the self-proclaimed Pinnacle of Perfection fumes at The Warden. The Warden now looks up at him after finishing writing some more notes on him. 'No Mr Diaz, just you,' he aptly responds. Diablo sits back down, almost pulling out his hair in sheer frustration. 'If anything, this place will make me crazy,' the Apex of Excellence comments. Mr Stephenson now stands up and leans forward. 'Now I must ask you for all of your belongings on you, and your shoe laces,' he asks politely. Diablo shakes his head and starts to empty his pockets. 'Stupid dumb, fucking place, bastard Aymin ruining my life once more,' he mutters under his breath is distress and anger. He then unties his shoelaces, pulls them out of his shoes and hands them over to The Warden, along with his wallet and house keys. 'We'll lock them away for you, so you may have them on your permanent release from here,' Stephenson adds. Mr Stephenson now walks around the desk and gets El Diablo to get to his feet. 'Now we'll show you to your room,' The Warden comments nicely. El Diablo gets lead by the arm out of the room and all the way into the main building until he reaches his set room. Mr Stephenson opens the room door and has El Diablo go in. 'Your clothes are waiting for you on your bed, and you'll need to hand these clothes over when you've changed. You can wear them on your temporary releases. The restrooms are down the hall and to the left, and you have a shower in the room just next you,' The Warden tells him all the information before closing the door leaving El Diablo in the room with his new roommate, so to speak. El Diablo hears the main electric doors shut to the main building, which indicates his freedom disappearing. He turns around and behind him is a white man with a huge afro. 'Great. Flaymin gets me put in here, and now I'm stuck in here with his look-alike. Could my life get any worse?'
