Date: 03.12.02 (#4 TT, #2 SA) Abner feels lonely. Maria, his one true love, due to an unforeseen set of circumstances (and also to a pissed off pimp), is gone…..gone forever….. His family, thousands of miles away, are more distant from him now than at any time in his life. Locked up in prison for four years, Abner vowed that when he escaped the horrors of D-29, he’d return to them. But apparently, there was nothing to return to….. But most of all, perhaps more than anything in the world, Abner missed home…..to him, he’s been gone for nearly five years. And though technically he’s only been away for a mere six months, the time he spent at D-29 seemed to be spent not quite at “home”. For without the fresh air of his beloved native state, even life seemed “stale” to him. The luscious evergreen forest of old Vermont, the beautiful Green Mountains, and the wild, crazy-ass redneck parties…..gone….all gone….. And so Abner, someone whom the only other place he’s ever been to was New Hampshire (the state next door, merely across a shallow river)….. as you might imagine, must feel very low. And indeed, he does. You see, New Hampshire even felt quite foreign to him…..so Mexico, as one would guess, has the feeling of almost “otherworldliness”, to him. Mexico, a dry, desolate and unforgiving place, just won’t due for Abner. But luckily for him, he’s got other thinks on his mind at the moment….. Certain…..”distractions”….. ---------------------------------------- Abner, stepping out of the shower, quickly grabs his towel, wraps it around his waste, and exits the bathroom. Goddamnit! I’ve got a match in less than ten frigin hours, and I ain’t even got ten hours of studyin’ in yet….. And there’s good cause for this. Due to his heavy drinking, a bad habit inherited during his teen years, Abner wasn’t able to get much accomplished. But for the last few days, unlike other times, he drank not in pleasure…..but in sarrow. Upset because of his loss last Friday to Kai….. Sad because of the loss of his beloved Maria….. Sobered because of his match verses Token and El Romantico tonight….. Nervous because of his eight-way tag match coming this Sunday…… The list goes on and on…..and on. But not to excuse, or put up a defense for such disgraceful taste in…..in recreation……but merely to shed light and clarify the situation. But let’s not muddy the waters here. Because, to be certain, Abner’s life is still relatively simple: Eat, sleep, wrestler, and drink…. not necessarily in that order, but you get the point. Basically, to tell it like it is, Abner doesn’t have to “think” very often. Uncapable of handling the same mental challenges that you and I might face on an everyday level, Abner chooses to keep things……plain. But “plain”, oddly enough, is not a good word to use when describing this individual…..determined, feisty, strong-willed, stubborn, and in his own way, witty, Abner is quite an interesting character. Made up of intricate, fine-tuned layers of both “good” and “bad” elements, it’s scientifically nearly impossible to describe him. And his mine, seemingly simple is not…..for somewhere, deep beneath the redneck “ignorance”, “shallowness” and “simplicity”, there is, a living, breathing, conscious and lively sole of incredible intellect and strength. But will we ever find it? Probably not….. “Horseshit!” Abner yells out in dismay. Coming back to reality, we find that PCW’s favorite hick/ex-con/anarchist, is angered by something. What’s new? Instinctively, the young hick begins to mumble underneath his breath. Cussing left and right, eventually, we reach the heart of the problem. “Frigin big-hotel chain, cock-suckin’ hippies! All I wanted was to take a damn shower…..come outta the damn bathroom, and mah goddamn clothes are gone!” Something catches his eye, and making his way to bedside, “……I’ll show them…..sue their stupid asses again! By-God, and this time, I’ll get some cash out of it too….” And doubt, if the circumstances were any different, he would. But not now. Not today. For today, and today only, he musk focus on the task at hand. Reaching down, he picks up a notice left on his pillow. He now holds a small white piece of paper, and in small black print, it reads: YOUR LAUNDY HAS BEEN TAKEN CARE OF. DUE BACK: BY 1:00 PM. ENJOY YOUR STAY AT THE HOLIDAY INN. THANKS. And so Abner, grumpy as he already is, roughly rips up the pieces, and tosses the remaining pieces in the trash. Letting out a volley of curses, he knocks over his lamp and heads for the door. Storming down the hallway, Abner doesn’t even care about his “attire”, and more than once the towel slips, exposing…..stuff…..for all to see. Huh! Romantico and Token tonight, and Adrenaline, four of the sons-a-bitches….. Jon Kano, Mark Michigan, Bob Vurdo and Eddie Mandrake…..I’ve got more important things to deal with than…..than clothing! And maybe he’s right. And so making his way into the elevator (luckily uninhabited), Abner sets his mind to quickly retrieving his lost (“stolen”) clothing, and then continuing on with his scheduled plan: three hours of video study, followed by several more of physical training. The “forbidden fruit” and a Mexican queer, who’s next? What else does the Front Office want to throw at me, huh? Peter Jennings and Barney the frigin Dinosaur! He shakes his head in disgust as his ride comes to an abrupt stop. But for better or for worse, these two are the opponents PCW picked out for him, and for better or for worse…….in Abner’s mind, hopefully better…..he would face them tonight. Now whether technically prepared or not, he certainly would be so mentally…. Cock suckin’, mother mountin’, ass-eatin’cock-a-poopies, I’ll get their asses yet! And though he has no personal illness toward either competitor, certainly, in light of the current circumstances, his character has been……motivated. And so he steps out of the elevator and peers all around. Ah-hah! Luckily, to the newcomer’s delight, his clothing is merely a few feet away. “Excuse me, sir?” A dismayed attendant steps back in dismay as Abner grabs a bundle of clothing from him. “Gimme that!” Abner snaps, pushing him away. The attendant, a young, colored man of maybe twenty years of age, steps forward. Then, looking into the stranger’s face, he recognizes the PCWer, and slowly backs off. “Oh, I’m sorry sir…..I didn’t know that you…..” He cuts himself short, apologetically looking up at the bigger man. “Shaddup!” Abner takes his clothing, turns over the basket, and empties the rest of its contents out onto the floor. Quickly removing his towel, Abner throws that on the floor too. Noticing the shock of the man in front of him, Abner can’t help but grin. And then, politely nodding his at the attendant, turns and heads back toward the elevator. Abner, butt naked and seemingly not even caring about this disturbing act of nudity, boards the machine. When the doors slide shut, the attendance quickly picks up his jaw, and takes in deep breath. Bending over to pick up the mess laid out before him, he can’t believe what he just saw. “Ha! Stupid carnie…..” Abner mutters, not having any idea as to what he’s talking about. Suddenly, the elevator comes to a stop. Quickly throwing on his clothes, Abner waits for the door to open, and then makes his way out into the hallway. All of a sudden, he freezes, and turning to face the elevator, “Goddamanit! Now that I got mah clothes back…..’spose I should ‘ead on down to trainin’ now…..” So getting back into the elevator, the agitated redneck prepares to take his leave of the hotel….. Onto Turmoil…..
