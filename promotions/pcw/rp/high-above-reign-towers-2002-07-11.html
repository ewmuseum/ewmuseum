High above Reign Towers..

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 11, 2002 The wind blew softly as I stood perched above Reign Towers’ roof, staring over at the skyline of San Francisco. It was beautiful, like watching a sunset after a hard day’s work. If only I could remember what it was like to fly, I could dive off of this perch and soar like an eagle. But I wasn’t an eagle. Flying wasn’t one of my powers. That right was reserved for guys like Superman, The Human Torch, and even the Vulture, who had to be one of the ugliest guys I’ve ever seen. But I wouldn’t complain. Nah. Tom Lexian: If only I had wings… I could see a crimson figure floating in the air towards me. I arched an eye brow watching Thinh Alexander showing off. So what if he could float? Didn’t mean a big deal to me. Tom Lexian: [watching Thinh land] Nice job of showing off their, buddy. Why don’t you show everyone your fangs too, while you’re at it? Thinh just hissed at me, like the good ol’ vampire he was. That’s all I needed right now – someone hissing at me. The Crimson: Who’s hissing? You’re the one copping an attitude at me. Always upset since you got beat up on Turmoil. I pointed my finger at him, upset that he even mentioned it. Tom Lexian: Watch your tone with me. Besides, you and Robert got your ass dropped with one punch, didn’t you? Now wasn’t that embarrassing? The Crimson: Look, it wasn’t our fault. We were trying to come in and help you, but they caught us off guard. You can’t blame us. We just came off breaking up that big drug ring the night before. Drug ring? No. Some kids were stealing prescription Flinestone vitamins and selling them on playgrounds. Now, if that doesn’t sound ridiculous, I don’t know what does. Maybe they could have been in the gym working on their punching skills instead. Tom Lexian: [shakes his head] Do you have any idea how ridiculous that sounds? Do you have any idea? One punch. ONE PUNCH! I think my grandmother punches harder than Clyde. The Crimson: And she sort of looks like him too. Did you see all that shit on his face? I nearly passed out from the shock. Tom Lexian: [arches an eyebrow] You’re a vampire, Thinh. I hate to break it to you, but you don’t exactly look like N’Sync either. You look sort of pale, actually. The Crimson: I haven’t fed tonight. I need to go eat a hamburger, extra rare. That was the way Thinh tried to satiate his thirst for blood – he ate rare hamburgers. Admittedly, it wasn’t the greatest tasting thing to him, but it was better than feeding on humans and cats. And besides, cats scratch. Tom Lexian: All of the fast food places closed almost an hour ago. Where are you going to find an extra rare burger? The Crimson: [thinks for a moment] I’ll have Robert cook one up for me. Tom Lexian: God dammit. I have him on a diet. Why the hell are you going to go and get him to break it? The Crimson: I hate to break it to you, Tom, but he’s been breaking it for a long time now. In fact, I think he’s at the grocery store stacking up on twinkies and stuff. The scene changes to a shot of Robert Yarney, the Crusader himself, hauling over about fifty boxes of Ding-Dongs over to the counter. As he did, a man at the counter talking with the owner smirked. Man: [laughing] Well, well, well. What do we have here? You think you’ve got enough ding-dongs there? Robert Yarney: [throws the boxes onto the counter] Whew! That was tough! Man: This is truly sickening. Couldn’t you try and clean your act up a little bit? I mean, this isn’t healthy for you. Robert eyes the man for a second. He was used to people jeering him and trying to make fun of him, even as a child, but he had learned to deal with it. After all, if you’re overweight, you’re going to get a lot of attention. And it’s almost always negative. Robert Yarney: Let me explain something to you. Just because I’m not thin doesn’t mean I’m not a person… Man: [looks over to the store clerk] Oh please. Save me the sob story. I don’t need my conscience tested by a guy that just hit the white pastry lottery, okay? Robert Yarney: Oh, I’m not going to make you feel guilty… He concentrated and suddenly sent the man flying across the store. He fell into a stand of chips and fell over unconscious. Robert scratched his ear and then tossed a twenty dollar bill at the store clerk, who seemed star struck. Store Clerk: How…how…how did you do that? Robert Yarney: [shrugged] I don’t know. I hate those freak mini-tornadoes. Always cause so much trouble… Robert grabbed his ding-dongs and walked out of the store. The scene changes as he lugs his warez over to Thinh and Tom, who are still perched over Reign towers. Robery Yarney: [gives Tom a ding-dong] Here you go, oh fearless leader. Tom waves him off. Robert Yarney: Oh please. Don’t act like you don’t want it. Tom Lexian: [rolls his eyes] I don’t want it. And don’t act like you don’t know what’s wrong, because you know exactly what you’ve done to piss me off. Robert looks over to Thinh, who just smirks. He knew that Robert had a weakness for sweets, and while that wasn’t an excuse, it still was funny. How many people do you know that can buy twenty boxes of ding-dongs? Who could eat all of that? Robert Yarney: What’s the matter with him? The Crimson: He’s a little peeved about Turmoil I think. Robert Yarney: [throws his hands up] Aren’t we all? I mean, I got knocked down to the ground with ONE PUNCH? You don’t see me getting all upset. Tom stood up quickly, sticking his finger in Robert’s face. Tom Lexian: Don’t you dare try to play this off. I’m the Continental champion, and when you’re a champion, people look up to you. What are all of the good people over in PCW thinking when they see their hero getting the hell beat out of him? And to make matters worse, they took you out with one punch. Robert Yarney: I’m not arguing with that. But at least Chris Chase came out… Tom Lexian: Yeah, and then he got his ass beat too. Face the facts, we lost a lot of fans on Turmoil. And to make things even worse than that…[runs his hand through his hair]…Duke Williams saved the day. Tom began to pace around, his face turning red. All of the talk about Duke Williams not being a hero, about him not saving the PCW from the Dark Ages. He even turned his back on Legend Inc. But what happened after that? Duke Williams comes back and with a bat beats down the Dark Ages by himself. Well, that was just perfect. Now who’s the real hero? Tom Lexian: I’ve come here to clear my head. I need to try and figure this all out. Robert Yarney: [sitting down with his ding-dongs] Well, a bit of crime fighting always helped you relax. Go find some thugs and beat them up. Tom Lexian: [looking out into the clear night] It’s actually been a pretty slow night. I guess all of the scum and dirty bags decided to take a night off. Ain’t that some shit? They decided to take a break the night I need to bust some heads. Robert Yarney: It could have been worse. We could be swamped with cases right now. Look at it this way, we don’t have to slay any vamps tonight. Tom Lexian: I don’t like doing that anyways. Takes too much energy, and they all want to kill me, so there’s the pressure there to, you know, stay alive. They both look over at Thinh, who just shrugs. The Crimson: If you guys want to kill somebody, look elsewhere. I’m not your vampire. Tom Lexian: I’ll do my share of fighting on Turmoil next week. Apparently I have to go and fight those three losers from the Dark Ages. And guess who’s on my team? Duke Williams… The Crimson: [chiming in] You guys almost won this week… Tom Lexian: The fucker wouldn’t cooperate with me. He left my ass for dead, so I don’t call that a good thing. Robert Yarney: You got Chris Chase. He’s a former world champion. I guess he could help. Tom Lexian: [sarcastically] Like how he “helped” this week? Please. I don’t have time for guys who can’t pull their own weight. He glares at Thinh and Robert, heeding his own words. He just laughed – it was just too funny. Robert Yarney: Why are you laughing? Dammit, why couldn’t I be psychic instead of telekinetic? Suddenly, a scream could be heard from the alleyway. Tom looked over the side of the building, watching a little girl running away into a dark corner being chased by two men. Tom grabbed the amulet and rubbed it for good luck. It looks like he would bust some heads tonight after all.
