The meaning of music

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-13-02 It’s written down on paper and told through sound played by instruments. The name of the system is music. Music has been around for centuries and as times change so does the music. Varieties of music are played on radios, Discman and computers. Quality of music has changed. Producers can make music sound crystal clear without hearing a single breath from the singer. Music is the ultimate medicine for different emotions. Sports players use music to pump themselves up before a game, tragedies use music to heel the world and sometimes music is used to tell a story. Music can sum out a life story all in three minutes. But to me music is something more important. Music is a healer that no human hand, no medicine can cure. Music has been there for me in good times and in bad. Music can take your mind off a subject you wish not to think about or it can heal feelings. Music is the most powerful device ever to be created. Music has pushed limits, broken barriers and brought different diversities together. The topics expressed in songs have true meaning and are the writer’s way of letting out that packed up emotion that has been bottled inside. Growing up in New York City was not one of the easiest things in the world. Survival was key and survival of the fittest was a game played everyday. Music would tend the cuts inside and patch them up like no bandage or stitch could fix. Rock n’ Roll was my antidote for help and music was always there to tend up all that was disturbing my inner feelings. A sweet sound of a guitar talking to me through an album player in my room was something that always calmed down my worse fears or worries of the world. This guitar was played by a legend, Jimi Hendrix. I would listen very closely when a solo would take place and it sounded like the guitar itself was actually speaking. The soothing sound that was played is something I feel even to this day when I place “The Jimi Hendrix Experience” in my CD player. Everyone of my friends wonder why I liked Jimi Hendrix and why I wasn’t involved in the punk movement instead. My friends always reminded me that Jimi Hendrix was dead twelve years before I started to listen to him. I would just shake my head in agony because they did not understand how his music could touch a soul. It all started in a local album shop a couple of blocks from my home. I was looking for “The Who’s” latest album and I found “The Jimi Hendrix Experience” the psychedelic cover with Jimi on the cover absorbed me into buying the album. I heard the name Jimi Hendrix before but never was able to hear his music. I put the album in the album player in my room and Purple Haze began to play. The guitar licks blew my mind; it felt like the guitar was actually talking to me. It was something I never have forgotten when I first listened to Jimi Hendrix. It was a mind-blowing experience and I was blown away by the ability that Jimi Hendrix had. It was sad to hear that Jimi Hendrix was dead and wondering what could have been. As times changed so did the music interest for me. Led Zepplin was a band that really drew me into their unbelievable lyrics and the great Jimmy Page playing that guitar like no other. One of my favorite songs that has ever been written was made by Led Zepplin and that song is “Stairway to heaven” a song about the journey of life and the parish of death. The song had deeper meaning then any other person could express. Other musicians were able to be very powerful to me when I needed it the most or just wanted to escape. The Who was one of those bands that I most enjoyed. From Baba O’Reilly to Pinball Wizard each of their song was enjoyable to listen to and the lyrics had meaning in them unlike songs today. Baba O’Reilly expressed the teenage use of drugs in their era and how it was becoming a teenage wasteland. They basically said they didn’t care about anyone or anything except themselves. There were some political views that were in the song that was very typical for songwriters to do in that era of music. Songwriting expressed what the band or band mate felt at the time. Songs about war, love and everyday life are expressed through song. The music in the era of the sixty’s/early seventy’s was the music that came from the heart, the music that described what they felt and most importantly it was real. I spent many hours sitting my room listening to my albums over and over again. Music was very powerful in taking your mind off situations you didn’t want to think about. There was many times when I came home and I heard friends got in fights with gangs that ran around are block and it pissed me off. So I would put in some Rock to pump the adrenaline and I would head down they’re looking for some of the gang mates. I might have been twelve at the time looking for these eighteen year olds but it didn’t matter to me because I was the size of one at the ago of twelve. Music was always there to give you some sort of help in your darkest days. Music is a better medicine then alcohol sometimes but some might disagree with me there, but who can complain when you don’t remember what you were worried about because your so god dam drunk. I mean what’s wrong with that? You might be numb but your worries are gone. Anyway like I was saying…music is always there to comfort your worst fears as a kid, things that are bothering, a hard day at work, a death of a friend and family, no matter the situation it’s always there to give you a boost and help you out in the time of need. But as stated before, times change and so does the music. The music today proves with the way times have been moving. Music today is at a very low point, boy bands that sing about their girls and how they want to treat them right. Rap artists talk about their “bling, bling,” bitches, hoes, drugs, drinking and their cars that they pimp up. Rock music is at a minimum cause no new rock bands today have balls like the other rock artists. Rock has gone soft and they sing about lonely nights without their babies. I just shake my head in disbelief when I’m forced to listen to this shit with my wife. Music has lost its edge and vulnerability to not give a dam about others feelings. Songs have no meaning and are usually spoken over and over again. It gets very annoying turning on the television and putting on MTV. MTV is a sad situation. The teenyboppers listening to crappy music and don’t realize it yet till they are in there thirty’s hopefully sooner. But the fact of the matter is that music has always been one the most important things is my life. Without it I don’t know where the rest of the world and I would be. Music will always be an huge part in my life and in many others and for all the time put into music I’m very thankful for being able to listen to the wonderful that is played through, car stereo’s, Boom boxes, headphones, album players, television and arenas. Words told through a song never sounded so wonderful until Music.
