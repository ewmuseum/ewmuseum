Spiritual Stuff...or something - Part 2 - Wise Andy

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 29, 2003 [When Tommy was eight years old he accidentally stumbled upon something so sacred that it completely changed his life forever. It was true. Many eight year olds were playing with action figures and making tree forts. Tommy was not. After what he saw, after the sacred viewing, none of these things even appealed to him in the least. Tommy found 'Night Call Nurses' in his fathers drawer. He thought that it was an educational tape, you know, sort of like that bodacious Sesame Street show. Well, it wasn't. It was adult entertainment. When Tommy popped it into the Beta VCR what he saw completely distorted his views of what an eight year olds life is like. He watched two ladies lick each other in places that he never even knew existed. At first he closed his eyes as if something scary were on the screen, but slowly he pulled his hand away just to take another peek. Then that's when the questions started to pour into his head. What are those mountains on her chest? AND WHERE IS HER PENIS?! The two questions plagued his mind for weeks as he did nothing but view that movie inside and out. He was like the John Madden of porno. Analyzing every scene, circling the key points of the motion in the ocean. Little Tommy became some what of an addict, but not so far deep that he couldn't be saved. Then, after weeks of viewing, he hooked up with Allison Garber, the hottest eight year old in California. He invited her to the secret Tubular treehouse for a bodacious game of House. House is a gnarly game to play when you have Tommy's mind, because once they got going Tommy began to learn that dudes and dudettes are MOST different. With a pull of her dress and her panties Tommy learned that only dudes have a penis and that only dudettes have a virginia...or was that vagina? He doesn't seem to recall, but he CAN recall what he saw. Only she didn't have those huge mountains on her chest like on Night Call Nurses. Luckily, Tommy would later learn that the mountains come about five more years down the road. And it was a moment that changed the direction of his life forever.....] [As the 1975 Green Chevy Malibu screeches toward the wide eyed Tommy, it's most weird that while going toward death he was thinking about his first porno, and the first time he ever saw a girl naked. As the Malibu makes a slight turn, Tommy begins smiling at the fact that he thought about Night Call Nurses and Allison Garber while there are so many other things in life that he should be thinking about at that point. As Tommy chuckles to himself the car as done a perfect 45 degree turn and is only inches away from Tommy. Harry, who is still standing on the sidewalk in front of Subway, looks on in absolute amazement] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: YOUR TRULY EVIL, MAN!! [Tommy shrugs his shoulders and begins walking on toward the 2002 Blue Mustang that he paid for, right on the spot, with cold, hard cash. As Tommy makes his way to the car the driver of the Malibu makes his voice heard] Driver: Tommy? 'Totally Tubular' Tommy?! [Tommy turns to see the face of someone REALLY familiar. His bushy ZZ Top-like beard flows down to his chin, his circular glasses make his hazel eyes look like silver dollars, and the green headband that reads 'Andy' still doesn't ring a bell with Tommy] Driver: I can't freakin' believe it!! TTT: Wise Andy? [The hippy looking man holds his arms out as if he's signaling Tommy to give him a huge hug, but Tommy doesn't do hugs...he's a super villain] Wise Andy: Holy poop balls! I thought that I was gonna kill you, but it turns out I used my god damn Demolition Derby driving skills not to kill you. You know your one crazy sumbitch to just be laughing like a madman when a killin' machine like a Malibu is barrelin' at youl. Harry: He's a super villain, man. [Andy looks at Harry with a bit of distain and then turns back to Tommy] Wise Andy: Well SHEEEOT! Super villain! I'd say after you whippin' that masked fella that YOU WOULD be a super villain. Made him your beotch, from what I saw. You better make that blind fella and that blacky your beotch come Friday if you wanna hold onto that gold of yours. TTT: Trust me, Andy...it's in the bag. Wise Andy: Good!! Hey...you guys wanna come over to my place and get shitfaced? [Tommy looks over to Harry, who is shaking his head in disagreement. The Tubular One is taken back long ago, right after high school graduation, when it was the last time the three of them hung out. To make a long story short, Harry accidentally blew up Andy's Hot Rod and Andy has never forgiven him since. Andy tried to retaliate by blowing HARRY up, but Andy was far too drunk to even get a good shot off] TTT: That would be most excellent. Wise Andy: Is the asswipe coming? TTT: Of course, he's my brother. Wise Andy: Keep him away from my cars. [Andy drives the boys through a series of deep woods, and Harry suddenly feels like he's in that not so far out flick, Deliverance. Harry just knows that he's not squealing like a pig for anyone, especially Andy. The crazy mofo tried to kill him because he was drunk of left C4 next to his car. Stupid jerkface!! Tommy really can't wait to see the huge house that Andy built himself. Don't let the dudes appearance deceive the mind, because Wise Andy is a pretty rich dude. He got himself involved with some Internet ventures and made a ton of cash. Enough so he could get his dream Hot Rod back after that most heinous accident. As the car goes through a pile of mud, it comes to a stop in front of a cave. A big, stinky cave] TTT: Dude, where's your pad? [Andy points directly at the cave] Harry: That's a cave, man. Andy: Well, we gots ourselves a regular ALEX TREEEEBECK here! It is a freakin' cave! [The three men slowly approach the cave as Tommy and Harry seem to be a bit reluctant to enter a place like this] Wise Andy: I figure you'd dig this place, being a super villain and all. TTT: Well, I like to run my bodacious operation out of my gnarly beach house. Harry: There is cable there, man. Wise Andy: Well, I got Jack Daniels. That's MUCH better than dat stupid cable stuff. Harry: I don't know, man... Wise Andy: SHUT UP! No one likes you! [Harry looks like a scalded child] TTT: Andy, this would be perfect for you. Harry has been having these totally weird feelings that he's been reincarnated from someone famous. Maybe you can help him out with that, dude. Wise Andy: How am I supposed to do somethin' like dat? TTT: Because your Wise Andy. Wise Andy: Come on, Tom! You know that they call me Wise Andy because I'm DUMB AS DIRT! TTT: That's kindo of stupid, dude. Wise Andy: Well, it's one of those freakin' reverse nick names. Like when they call a REALLY big guy Tiny or a bald guy Hairy. You see what I'm gettin' at? Harry: I'm lost, man. TTT: We're in the woods, dude. Harry: No, not that kind of lost, man. Just...nevermind.... Wise Andy: That's right, beotch. You better say nevermind or I'll shove my empty JD bottle up ya ass! TTT: Guys, I've got to tell you something. It may sound bogus, but as Andy's car came barreling at me I had this thought in my head. Wise Andy: You thinkin' about killin' a black man? [Tommy and Harry look at Andy as if he's absolutely insane. Then Tommy goes back to his explanation] TTT: I was in a situation where death was knocking at my door. I could hear the most heinous reaper knocking at my door, and I could even hear his voice. That's when I saw it, when I viewed my first ever porno. It was Night Call Nurses and I remember how BOGUS it was at first, but as the film went on the bogusness level began to level off. Soon, I was viewing one of the most bodacious things I have ever seen. Then I remember taking Allison Garber into the Tubular treehouse. Harry: You took Allison Garber into our treehouse, man? TTT: Totally. Harry: Did she get NAKED? TTT: Totally. Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!!! Wise Andy: I hate you, but I gotta admit that is far out. TTT: And through the heinous eyes of death, I thought back to when I was eight years old and saw Allison Garber naked. Now tell me that isn't a bit bogus. I mean with all the most excellent things that have been happening to me lately, you would figure I would have thought about SOMETHING different. Maybe like my radical father, or my mom. Maybe when I won my first ever surfing award, or my FIRST pro wrestling match! But I didn't even have a notion of those bodacious things, just the porno and Allison. Harry: I think God is telling you to call up Allison, man. Wise Andy: THAT'S STUPID! He's tellin' ya to watch some porn, Tom! Lets go watch some PORN! [Tommy and Harry give Wise Andy that look as if he's absolutely lost his mind. Wise Andy looks at both of them with an innocent look and shrugs his shoulders] Wise Andy: What? TTT: Dudes. Isn't it weird that I get that vision in my head, and Harry begins thinking that the soul he has is someone famous reincarnated? I mean it's pretty weird after beating that heinous Rent-A-Jerkface at Shanghaied and now I have to face Skylar Green and Dante Jackson on Fusion. When will I, The Tubular One, finally catch some sort of break?! [Harry's eyes shoot open wide] Harry: I AM MARTIN LUTHER KING, MAN!! Wise Andy: He's a blacky!!!!!! Harry: I HAVE A DREAM, MAN! [Andy attacks Harry as Tommy begins walking through the woods, as if something is calling his name] Fade to black To be continued....
