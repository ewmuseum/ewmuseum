The Meaning of Perfect

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-13-02 Perfect, adj. 1. having all the desired qualities. If you was to create the perfect wrestler what qualities would you give him? Speed is a must. Strength should go without saying. Talent and skill he must have in aces. He has to be able to handle himself in certain situations. But most of all he must have the drive. The reason for wrestling. If he has nothing to push him he can not wrestle. Ask around, I can hit you with the Perfect Storm with lighting speed. It comes out of nowheres like thunder and crashes down like lighting. I am rather strong even for my frame. I am built sure but I have trained my body hard and long. I am much stronger then what I look. People think I can not lift them up for the Perfect Storm but you would be surprised. My moves have been toned and adjusted. My mat skills are top notch. I move with grace and beauty like the rain falling softly. Like the mighty tornado, I can adjust to my situation if needed. The tornado is unpredictable and can jump from place to place. I can jump from submissionist to technical to martial arts. And for drive? Just like the hurricanes I drive through and leave mass damage in my wake. I have my reasons for stepping in the ring and they continue to push me everyday of my life to be better and better. 2. having no flaws or shortcomings. Sometimes an opponents strenghts is not where he is defeated but rather his weakness that is his downfall. An opponent could outclass one, but finding that one weakness can be all that is needed. As great as Achiles was, he still had his heel. The mighty Golath was taken down because of a single flaw. Titan and man. God and mortal. One to another, they all have a single mistake in their design that allowed them to be defeated in some way. Try to find something wrong with me. I dare you. My body? It's toned and ripped. I am not so large where I hurt my movement though. My body is chipped from marble and made to the exact detail a human was meant to be. My technical skill has no flaws in it. I know countless moves and countless reversals and blocks for those moves. Each counter and block I know another counter and block for. I could read sumission holds off in alphebetical order. Then lock them in with even greater ease. 3. correct in every detail. Throughtout history man has tried to play God and create the perfect human. He would be correct in every detail. During World War Two the German's tried to create the perfect race. In the fifties, sixties and sevenites the United States tried to create the perfect soldier. The former Soviet Union even had a hand in trying to create the perfect human. Even before then societies have tried and failed in creating a being that was perfect in every way. The ideal human. The perfect being. None have successed. Or have they? I never really asked where I came from before. I just worried about right now. But that all changed when I met Tessa Windsor. She showed me the meaning of family and love. She made me question where I came from, who my parents where, and most of all, why I was born. 'Born. . .' I quickly said. To be honest I wasn't sure if I was even born. Maybe I just ended up on Genesis' door step and that is where my life begun. I use to just accept the fact that everyone was born from a women. That is a fact and that is where I am from and that was enough. I never thought about it again. Then again, I also though everyone was human. How wrong was I? Dispite all the weird things I have already seen in the short time I have been in the PCW, that still doesn't change the fact. I am the Perfect Storm. The perfect wrestler. I have all the desired qualities. I have no flaws or shortcomings. I am correct in every detail. From my golden blond hair to my ocean blue eyes. Every hair perfectly trimmed and clean. When given the chance I will show off my perfect wrestling talent. My exellent Hammerlocks and my beautiful Suplexs. All of which reasons Tessa loves me so. I am the perfect male. She stares at me with those eyes and I melt into her. Her candy lips kiss me and the rest of the world fades away. 'ARGHHH!!' the pain! It came so suddenly? What the hell is going on? My head felt like it was on fire. It came so suddenly. I fell to my knees as I tried to reach for something to help support me. It felt like something was crushing my head. I looked around in a daze trying to figure out what caused this sudden pain. I could feel consciousness slipping away from me. 'No....' I didn't want to fall into the realm of dreams again. That is where I saw the visions. The visions of a past I didn't know. The pain grew and I could tell my eyes was slowly drawing shut. I tried to fight it with all I could mutter up but the pain won the battle and I calapsed on the floor. I could see the images coming back to me. Things I had tried to forget. They all came back to me now. The chill was horrible. I felt like I could puke. The images was different this time. It wasn't of places and times I couldn't tell. But rather I knew what it was and where I was. I was at the mall. I knew what was going on. It was a day I dreaded and hated. The day my Tessa was nearly taken from me. . . . Tessa had taken Christa with her to the mall as part of a girl's night out. Unknown to her Josh Starwind was there. And as always, where Josh went trouble was sure to follow. Like on clock work as soon as the two saw each other there was an attack. Someone that looked just like Josh Starwind was killing people in the mall, trying to attack the 'Deathsyce Outlaw'. He even went so far as to grab Christa as bait. Tessa and Josh fought against this madman and he was defeated but Tessa had nearly died in doing so. And the worst part of it all? I was no wheres near to help her. I could not protect my Tessa Bear when she needed me the most. I did not find out about it until I came home and found the apartment empty. Then I saw it on the news. Coverage of the whole ordeal. My face went white, my pulse stopped, my heart didn't beat. My entire world fell into a black obisk. I will never forgive Josh Starwind for putting my Tessa in that type of situation. 'I will get that bastard back, even if it's the last thing I do' I had told myself countless times. I was starting to regain consciousness. I sat up and leaned against my bed as I sat on the floor. The vein on my forehead was sticking out. Just thinking about that event made me angry again. That son of a bitch endangered the life of the person that means the most to me. He had to play. He was a curse to society. Like a black plague. Where he went chaos and destruction ruled. I don't care about that bullshit they try to feed me about him being a hero. He's no hero! He is the reason most of that happened. Had he not went to Tessa's home state, there would not have been mass destruction there and I would not had needed to ask her to stay with me in New York. 'I don't care if he is her brother. . ' I told myself. It was true. Josh Starwind is Tessa Windsor's brother. Which with Tessa and I's relationship, that nearly made him family to me. But you would never get me to admit that out loud. Josh is a black rose. Everything he cares about dies. Everything he touches wilts away. So there was no way in hell I was going to let him get near Tessa ever again. But fate, ah cruel and funny fate, she loved to play games on the mortal man such as myself. As Tessa and I left the WIW for the greater PCW, leaving Josh Starwind behind, fate intervened and he signed shortly there after. We was once again together. In WIW and now PCW. We was simply destinied to fight. And that time is now. December 15, 2002. PCW is the place. Chris Storm and Josh Starwind finally go face to face. A match that was stated to be a part of WIW history. Highly expected and wanted. Now held in another company. Those within the PCW do not know the match they have. The diamond laying in the rough. This match will be something special. I know it. Josh Starwind knows it. All of the WIW knows it. The PCW, they don't have a clue. I smirked as I finally stood back up. I shook the pain away and let it fall off my body. The cocky grin returned to my face. I could see the match already. The lights, the camera, and my goodness the action. The question however is, can Knoxville, Tennessee hold the action? 'The Deathsyce Outlaw' is about to meet the Perfect Storm and he will learn why mother nature truely is a bitch.' I laughed to myself. Oh this was going to be good. If the front office in PCW had only known how badly I had been wanting to dance with Josh they would have booked this long ago. Hell last Sunday I would have faced him. To hell with the contract signings. We would just throw down in the backstage and go at it. Man I felt like a rookie again, going at it for the first time ever. Years in the business and I have never been this excited about a match before. My Kami, Chris Storm and Josh Starwind finally wrestling. What a night Adrenalin is turning out to be. The night a little bit of history is made, and alot of ego is crushed.
