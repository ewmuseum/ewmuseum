The scene opens up in what looks like a huge courtroom while a white, bald judge walks into the court room in his flowing black muumuu as everyone in the back and the defendants and plaintiffs, whose faces can’t be seen. A serious voice comes out, loud and clear. “Today, we will see a man who is accused of a crime against humanity, a crime very terrible, but will he be able to stand the heat? Let’s see…” Bailiff: You may all begin to play that funky music white boy. The bailiff says this with extreme sincerity, and reporters begin to take down notes while the crowd, defendant, and plaintiff all go to sit down. Judge: Today, we will be trying one man, Rush Mason, of being…EXTREME!! The camera shows Rush Mason’s face, and Rush is eating a turkey sandwich as he nonchalantly listens. However, just as the word “extreme” comes out of the judge’s mouth, “Play That Funky Music” by Wild Cherry starts to play loudly and every single person in the courtroom begins to dance, Rush begins to dance with his turkey sandwich James Brown style while the judge starts to do some break-dancing but order is quickly restored as the music stops. Judge: Ahem…yes Rush Mason is being accused of being…of that crime and now Mr. Mason, your prosecution will rise and present their case. As the judge announces this, the door opens and some serious sounding court music plays, and two tall sharp-looking slick-haired Italian men walk in side-by-side with a small space in between them where the camera pans down to reveal a well-dressed midget, hair slicked back with black Oakley sunglasses. A priest stands up in the crowd and yells out. Priest: Is that a little boy? With that, everyone in the crowd begins to spit towards the priest, and the midget attacks the priest, dropping him with a nut shot and then pins him. 1…2…3!! Jeff Marx: A MIDGET~! A MIDGET~! I SAY! With that, the midget wipes his brow and heads up towards the prosecution table and stands in front of Rush, who’s just finishing off that turkey sandwich. Midget: Your honor, my name is Michael McRedding, and I’m representing Tyler Nelson’s side in this case, as his honorable greediness couldn’t make it here because he was attending to CEO matters. Rush looks up from his newly cleaned plate down at the little person and exclaims sleepily a strange statement to no one in particular. Rush: Doesn’t this ride have a height limit? Everyone looks at Rush, who responds to the great pressure by falling asleep on top of the table. McRedding: For my first witness, I’d like to call out Xavier Kannon’s valet, Eleanor Hall. A figure walks through the door, though it is definitely not Ellie~! The figure, in a wig, actually is black and appears to be Jordan X Shabazz in a wig and a dress. McRedding: Now, Ms. Hall, what have you witnessed from Mr. Mason that would result in him being convicted of being extreme? Suddenly, the music stops and order is restored once again. “Ellie~!” replies in a very stretched voice that sounds fake. “Ellie~!”: Well, my Xavi like SO teamed with that Negroid guy. McRedding: Ahh…and what happened when they teamed up? “Ellie~!”: That was the first time I tasted the beautiful Crunk stick. McRedding: And by that you would be referring to Mr. Mason’s- Rush springs awake and blurts out the first thing that comes to mind. Rush: Sea men!! The sea men are trying to attack us!!! Jordan slips for a second, going back to his old voice, but then catches himself. “Ellie~!”: Aw hell naw, that nigga’s…I mean, like SO totally yes! Michael McRedding climbs up to the judge’s seat and whispers into the judge’s ear, then the judge addresses the entire group. Judge: The prosecution has requested that Ms. Hall step down and go back to wait at Mr. Mason’s apartment seeing as Mr. Kannon, her supposed fiancée is off giving blowjobs for pushes to Tyler Nelson. The entire crowd nods in agreement and acknowledgement. “Ellie~!” steps down and walks out the door, just as Duke Williams walks into the room woozily, almost tripping over his own feet as the crowd begins to be sucked into the depths of hell, one by one. Duke: Hey hozzeseses, did you just see that Ellie~! Out there? Rush looks at Duke’s drunken state, and hits a button on the desk, wondering what it is. All of a sudden, a hole opens up right underneath where Duke is standing, and in cartoon-like fashion, Duke tries to run through the air but trips over himself again and falls down the hole, snoozing loudly. Rush stands up and announces to no one in particular once again. Rush: I now call my witness to ROCK~! The stand. Out of nowhere comes Kano with a Kanospear onto the little person, Michael McRedding.
