The Trip to PCW (Part 1)

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-12-02 (Scene opens to a beautiful summer morning in July as Scorpion is outside of his home in New York entering his 1996 Blue Chevrolet Monte Carlo. As Scorpion enters in the car he places the New York Times on the passenger seat of his car. He places the keys in the ignition and the car engine begins to rev up as Scorpion begins to pull out of his driveway and begins to pull onto the dead end road he lives on with his wife Stephanie Steiner and his son ten-month year old son, Toby Steiner. Scorpion reaches for the radio knob turning up the volume, the radio station number appears on the screen, it reads 101.3. As 101.3 is read on the screen it begins to fade where the time then appears. 10:07 a.m. A song by Michelle Branch comes on and Scorpion begins to turn the station very quickly. Scorpion comes across a rock radio station playing Pink Floyd’s "Break in the wall.") We don’t need no education…..TEACHERS! Leave those kids alone…. all in all it’s just another break in the wall…. (Scorpion drives the car onto a local highway and directly into steady traffic. Scorpion sits in traffic listening to the music, bobbing his head, and singing with the songs played on the radio from "Break in the wall" to "Baba O’Reilly." Horns honking, people yelling are the sounds coming from the traffic. Scorpion finally pulls off the highway and onto exit 53. Scorpion travels a mile to the local Duncan Donuts. Scorpion parks the car in front of the Duncan Donuts. Scorpion takes the key out the ignition and as he does the car engine turns off and the radio, which at the time was playing "Rock you like a hurricane" turns off in mid-song. Scorpion slams the driver side door and walks into the Duncan Donuts. The line in the Duncan donuts is consisted of two people, an Asian man and teenager.) Dam it this line is always jammed, why can’t they get some help in here. (Scorpion looks at the help wanted sign in the window and then glances over to the women behind the counter and shakes his head.) Dam it what time is it? 10:30? Dam it I got to go back soon, my flight is at 12:35. (Looks at Asian man getting his food.) Could he move any slower? I’m so hungry I could jump behind that dam counter. (Teenage boy approaches the counter.) Finally the line is moving. I got to work out later, geez just a couple days ago I was working for CCW now I’m working for PCW. I still can’t get over CCW closing, I was just beginning to climb that ladder to success and the ladder was pulled from under me leaving me face first on the ground entering PCW. Now I begin to prove myself at a higher level. The ladder climb will be more difficult but I just got to keep pushing myself to the brink. (Teenage boy grabs his food.) Finally it’s my turn. (Scorpion then approaches the counter.) Scorpion: Hello I’d like one medium black coffee with no sugar. Duncan Donuts employee: Is that all sir. Scorpion: Yep, that’s it. (The lady turns around to a coffee pot and then proceeds to grab a styrofoam cup and begins to pour the coffee from the pot into the cup. She finishes pouring the cup and placing it on the counter. She then reaches under the counter and grabs a styrofoam top for the cup.) Duncan Donuts employee: That’ll be $2.50 Scorpion: All right. (Scorpion reaches into his pocket and pulls out a black leather wallet and grabs two one-dollar bills and two quarters and puts them on the counter.) Scorpion: There you go. Duncan Donuts employee: Thank you very much sir and you have a nice day. Scorpion: Thanks, you to. (Scorpion puts his wallet back in his pocket and grabs his coffee. His sips it very slowly and steady as the steam from the coffee rises off through the opening of the top of the cup where you would drink the coffee. Scorpion then walks outside and opens up the door to his car. He takes a seat in the drivers seat and puts the coffee in the cup holder right next to the passenger seat. Scorpion pulls out of the parking lot and heads about a mile down the road and back onto the parkway where there is no on coming traffic at all on his side of the road while the other side is bumper to bumper traffic. Scorpion switches the radio to a different station as the radio station he was listening prior to enter the Duncan Donuts was on a commercial break. He comes across to a classic rock station and leaves that on as Bad Company song "Bad Company" is playing.) Bad Company till the day I die, Bad, Bad, Bad Company….I can’t deny….. (Scorpion gets off at exit 53 and begins to drive down a local street for about a mile and then turns right onto a dead end street. Scorpion reaches the third house on the left and pulls into the driveway. Scorpion turns off the radio and takes the key out of the ignition turning the car off. He grabs the keys and puts them in his right hand pocket and grabs the cup of coffee and opens the driver side door and gets out of the car. He shut the door and walks inside the house. Scorpion walks into the house and down the hallway into the kitchen where Scorpion’s wife Stephanie and his ten-month year old son, Toby our. Stephanie is feeding Toby some baby food as he sits in a highchair near the dining room table.) Stephanie: Hey hunny. Scorpion: Hey baby. (Scorpion kisses Stephanie, looks at Toby.) Scorpion: Hey how’s my little buddy. Toby: Da da. Scorpion: That’s my boy. High five? (Toby gives Scorpion a high five.) Scorpion: Good boy Toby. You be good for mommy while I’m gone. Stephanie: Jeff you be careful out there. Scorpion: Don’t worry Steph, I know what I’m doing. Stephanie: All right Jeff, we will be watching you. Scorpion: Ok, I’ll call you as soon as I land in Mississippi. Stephanie: Ok you better. Scorpion: You know I will. Stephanie: All right. Scorpion: Ok let me get my bags. (Scorpion walks out of the kitchen and to the door where his bags are and picks them up.) Scorpion: All right Steph, I should be in around two, two thirty. I’ll call you as soon as I land. Stephanie: Ok baby make us proud. Scorpion: You dam right I will. (Scorpion kisses Stephanie and then kisses his son Toby who is in Stephanie’s arms.) Scorpion: If there is a problem call me on my cell ok. Stephanie: Got ya. Scorpion: I’ll call you guys whenever I get the chance. Stephanie: I’ll right, Jeff be safe. Scorpion: Don’t worry Steph. (Scorpion opens the door and walks down to his car and puts his bags in the back of the car.) Scorpion: I’ll right, I’m off the PCW. Stephanie: Good luck. (Scorpion and Stephanie kiss one more time as Scorpion gets in the car and puts the key in the ignition and starts up the car. He backs out of the driveway as the scene closes.)
