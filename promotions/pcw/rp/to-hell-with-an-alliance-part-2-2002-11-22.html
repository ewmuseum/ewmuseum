To hell with an alliance! (Part 2)

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 22, 2002 [Night. Of course, the darkest time of day. We start just outside of the Canadian Forces Base at Trenton. Most of the buildings are dilapidated, thanks to the repeated budget cuts from the government. However, in some weird and disturbing way, the base is still active, but there are no night guards, no lights, and very little equipment. Within barracks building A, we have a company of light infantry who is sleeping. All look pretty much the same, bald, wearing army regulation undergarments, which consists of white tank top with white boxers. All the bunks are fast asleep, not a creature stirring. We then approach one of the recruits who is snoring rather loudly, most likely after a long day of basic training. Then, a gloved hand is placed over the mouth of the recruit, and a needle stuck into his arm. As the plunger of the needle draws closer and closer to the end, the young recruit’s strength draws closer and closer to nothingness, until finally, he goes limp. The hand turns to a body as it picks up the recruit.] Fast Forward a few hours [It has turned to day, we see the young recruit, now captive to this unknown individual. The room itself is unfinished, and looks worn. The drywall has holes in it, and the only light is from one window facing the east. He is strapped to a wooden chair with the handyman’s secret weapon, duct tape. His arms and legs are bound with that wonderful substance, and he has a strip around his mouth as well. A voice booms from the shadows] Voice: Well, well…a fresh young army recruit…someone who wants to serve his country. I admire that. However, you have picked the wrong military to serve in. [The voice exposes itself to the light, and it is the former bounty hunter, Eddie Mandrake.] Eddie: You, my friend, managed to join up with a cash-strapped, haphazard, obsolete military, whose army seems, on the outside to be normal, but on the inside is a shell of it’s cold war self. The Canadian navy has obsolete ships, and leaky submarines, where the sailors use duct tape to patch the holes in the ship. The air force, barely has enough gas to get airborne, and has to drop fruit as bombs from 5,000 feet! [The captive’s eyes widen significantly. Eddie paces around the room.] Eddie: Did I mention that the ocean search and rescue’s choppers are so corroded that it is a matter of time before they fade to a pile of oil-based paint and rust? [The captive shakes his head.] Eddie: WELL THEY ARE! [Eddie’s breathing gets a little heavier as he grabs a steel chair, and takes a seat in front of the captive.] Eddie: You know something…I admired the military for a long time. Especially during the campaign that Princess Patricia’s Canadian Light Infantry did in Afghanistan. They showed poise and prowess…but not enough to EVADE A 2000 POUND BOMB HEADED THEIR WAY! SENT BY AN AMERICAN! Do you want to be in that unit, son? [The captive nods] Eddie: I see. I guess the war on terrorism has hit your heart as well. The U.S. government tried to hire me to find bin Laden you know? I turned it down because I do not hunt terrorists. Even for $25,000,000, I have my own morals. [Eddie stands up and takes the chair to sit beside the captive.] Eddie: Your aim is the infantry correct? [The captive nods.] Eddie: So, you’d end up being an Infantry man then…Reminds me of someone…Ah, yes…my quote-unquote team mate for Alliances, BladeD. [The captive looks puzzled.] Eddie: You see, he is a complete and total moron. He doesn’t want to listen to reason…namely my manager, Doug Ellington; call sign Spider. He has tried, and tried to get him, me, and the other complete and total fucktard Trigger, to work together. Also, Jamie Celt has helped as well, but there is simply no way of going about trying to get us to get along. [Eddie then gets up again and sits down again in front of the captive.] Eddie: Obviously, the powers that be have placed the three of us together in a match. Fucktard Trigger, Myself, and…the infantry man. Do you think that your training is going to help you in a war-time scenario? [The captive nods.] Eddie: Funny. I doubt any training he has…even if he was trained by the finest army in the world is going to save him from my wrath. He cost me MY cruiserweight title, and placed it within a tournament for people to fight over. For that, like you, there is only one punishment. [The captive begins to perspire, a bead of perspiration begins to roll down the side of his head, as Eddie’s eyes widen. His breathing rate increases even further, and his eyes begin to grow bloodshot.] Eddie: He will get his receipt tomorrow. Such as Trigger will. However, he will receive more change from the payment that he gave me a few weeks ago. [Eddie arises yet again from his chair, but this time walks up to the ear of his captive.] Eddie: Do you know what it’s like…to taste the fear of your adversary? [The captive shakes his head.] Eddie: I don’t think you ever will. The Canadian army isn’t the army the world should fear anymore. Fifty years ago, definitely, now…not really. You will never know what it’s like. I have experienced it. It is like something that tastes really good, and you want more, and more, and more. Until finally…your adversary just gives up…and dies. You, being a soldier, should be able to kill, and devour the fear of the enemy without a second thought! However, here you sit…tied in duct tape…and I can smell the fear dripping from you. It’s dripping from you like a Thanksgiving Turkey! Your fear…it has a quality to it that sickens me. Maybe I should just end it now! [Eddie reaches into a utility belt that he is wearing and pulls out a spiked dagger. He rears back and lets out a guttural growl, as the captive closes his eyes. Eddie thrusts the dagger across the throat of his captive, and kicks the chair over. The eyes of the captive are open as the pupils close to eventual nothingness. A pool of blood begins to form around the head of the now dead captive. Eddie turns his back on the body.] Eddie: BladeD…look…look upon this visage…and see your fate. Tomorrow…when we meet in that ring, I will show you, what exactly, this malevolent being is made of. When you awaken after your defeat…pray that you don’t wake with me in your face. Good Nightmare, BladeD. [Eddie pulls the spiked knife out of the head of the deceased captive. He uses the same bloody knife to cut the duct tape off of the captive, and he carries the body out of the sunny room.] A few more hours later [We return to the army base back at night, however, the silence that was the sleep patterns of the other soldiers are violently disturbed because of the standards of Reveille. The sergeants storm into the room.] Sergeant: REVEILLE! REVEILLE! DROP YOUR COCKS AND GRAB YOUR SOCKS LADIES! [He walks over to the bed of the former captive.] Sergeant: Private Johnson, do you realize how long we’ve been looking for you?! [No response.] Sergeant: Answer, Numb nuts! [The body falls out of the bunk onto the floor, still bleeding.] Sergeant: SWEET GEORGIA JESUS! GET THE MEDICS OVER HERE, TRIPLE TIME! [As the puddle of blood begins to form around the neck of the deceased cadet, we will fade to black.] [The vigilante’s code; Kill the criminal, fuck the police. Something that we all know too well, that vigilante justice is the best kind. With that, we begin on a cold and windy day in the Toronto suburb of Mississauga. The snow that was on the ground are all but large piles of grey and brown ice near the sides of the road. We then head to the one of the most popular shopping centres in the Greater Toronto Area. That mall is the Square One Shopping Centre, home to a Wal-Mart, a Sears, and a few major superstores. On the north-east corner of the mall, there is a Toronto Dominion-Canada Trust bank. It is bustling with the noon-hour crowd; the line-up is long and full of commoners wanting to get a human touch to their cash, a lone security guard watches the door for any sort of aggression. Outside, a blue Dodge Caravan pulls out and out walk three men wearing black ski masks and all black outfits. One of them pulls a Beretta out from underneath his black jacket, points it at the security guard, and fires. The shot penetrates the skull of the guard, killing him instantly. The group of three walk into the bank, and one fires a machine gun into the air. The crowd ducks instinctively. Some scream in fear.] Robber: Alright! I want everyone to give me their money and valuables and throw them into this sack. Joey, get the teller’s cash, Bobby, handle the safe would ya? [The other two go off for their duties. Joey, the larger one of the group begins to ransack the tills of the tellers, grabbing every single paper bill in there and throwing them into a burlap sack. Bobby, the smaller one, heads for the safe, and enters it. He begins to crack the safe deposit boxes. However, as he opens one…the safe door slams shut, and locks.] Joey: Bobby? Bobby! Hey Dan! Bobby’s done, he’s locked in the safe! Dan: What? Oh well, the cops’ll get him, and he better not squeal. You done there? Joey: Yeah! Let’s get outta here! [Joey runs for the exit, and heads out the door. However, Joey’s head is detached from his shoulders, thanks to a piano wire noose. Dan, the ring-leader, is shaken because of the horrifying visage. He slowly walks out of the bank, keeping his wits about him, and trying to keep his eyes open for any more piano wire. He walks into his parked van, now two members less, but at least $100,000 richer. He gets into the drivers seat, and begins to count the cash that his quote-unquote friends got for him.] Dan: Ah…I have gotten away with robbery…and now, I don’t have to split the loot! YEEHAW! Voice: That loot belongs to the people of the bank. [The voice booms from the back of the van, as another line of piano wire comes across the neck of Dan. Dan struggles as he chokes. However, the struggle is for naught as Dan eventually succumbs to the lack of air and passes on. The side door of the van opens and the sack of money is tossed back into the bank, as mall security forces respond to the cries for help that the bank provided. The van drives away with an unknown person at the wheel.] ***A few hours later*** [Now it is sunset, in a dilapidated room, somewhere in downtown Toronto. The room itself is unfinished, and looks worn. The drywall has holes in it, and the only light is from one window facing the east. The door flies open and in walks Eddie Mandrake, once again, dragging the dead body of the ringleader of the brazen daylight robbery. Eddie places the body onto a wooden table, stained with a dark brown stain.] Eddie: You son of a bitch. Torturing the innocent, just for your own personal gain. You may think, what’s the difference between you and me? I, kill other people because they are guilty of something. Whether it is divorce, domestic assault, murder, or another sort of crime that some dickhead managed to commit in order to further his own ambitions. You, on the other hand, terrorize other people to gain money, power, and fame within the seedy underbelly of the city. That is our difference. [Eddie walks to the right hand of the deceased.] Eddie: You have an itchy…trigger…finger…don’t you? [He lifts the limp arm to look at the hand, in particular, the trigger finger.] Eddie: Indeed…you did kill someone with a gun recently. [He drops the hand onto the table.] Eddie: The word Trigger…means to activate, to set off…or an event that precipitates other events. Indeed, you set off a series of events that lead to your death, and Trigger set off a series of events that will lead to his eventual death. [He walks to the head of the corpse.] Eddie: You see Trigger started the whole business by looking at me cock-eyed in the locker-room. Then I destroyed changed his life by sending him to the hospital for a few weeks after I gave him a severe concussion. Then he returns, thinking he has a case against me. This will be his final downfall. [He walks to the left arm of the corpse.] Eddie: Now, we see that thanks to that no-brained, bleached blonde, ditz, Ellie. Trigger is teamed with me for the Pay-Per-View, and she now wants to break the team up before the pay per view? FINE BY ME! [Eddie walks to a table containing a large array of bladed weapons, most of them spiked on the handles.] Eddie: Granted, my manager has tried to bridge the gap, but obviously, that little bastard doesn’t want to see the greater and much larger picture. As far as I’m concerned, I will show him this picture and cram it down his throat if necessary. No one sees how much of an idiot he is. I will show him what the greater good really is. [Eddie grabs a spiked dagger from the table. The spikes dig into this skin drawing blood. He also separates the trigger finger from the rest of the hand.] Eddie: When I enter that match. Look across the ring to see BladeD, and Trigger, my team-mates and arch enemies. I will very simply take their very careers into my fists…and end them both in a HEARTBEAT! [Eddie raises his arm and strikes the finger with the knife, chopping the trigger finger off the hand. He lifts it to his face.] Eddie: Think of this as a symbol, gentlemen. Take it for what you will. You will experience the fact that I am not your worst nightmare, but your every nightmare! [The lights grow dim, and we fade to black.]
