Johnson's lucky day...

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 (OOC: Steve Johnson gave me permission to use his character. As did I for him if he so decides to in any of his next rps before Tuesday.) The camera opens to a blurry mess. Whatever it is that the camera is trying to focus on is unknown to us. But as we sit there watching our television monitors the big blurry mess begins to be revealed as the camera pans back. To reveal nothing more than a pair of eyes, both of which are blood shot. The puffiness around them tells us that who ever they belong to has been in need of rest for some time. A slight wince can be seen as the person’s voice booms out through the speakers built into our factory made televisions. “He likes to think that he’s somebody. He likes to think that he knows what he is talking about. His words and actions are meant to tell us that he’s focused, prepared, and ready for his upcoming match. He’s in a gym working out. Cuts his promos for the entire world to see, good for him. That’s his style.” The words fade out and scenes from Steve Johnson’s last cut promo begin to play. Sweat is dropping off his face and is covering his shirt and shorts. He looks as though however he isn’t even tired yet. “Steve Johnson wants us to feel as though he’s a superior athlete. He wants us to believe that he is in better shape than his upcoming opponent for Tuesday Turmoil.” “Steve Johnson’s words tell us he’s cocky, arrogant, and conceited. All things everyone has seen before.” The set of eyes fade black as an eerie sounding music begins to play. As the music continues to play the scene fades back in but is in black and white. A set of trash cans are seen with flies buzzing around and maggots eating at some unknown rotted meat. The scene then fades out and back to the pair of eyes. “Johnson we’ve all seen and heard trash before. So then why open your mouth and remind us of the stench we fight hard to forget everyday. We don’t care if you think you’re better than your so called friends. We don’t care if you can sit in front of a camera and talk smack. It’s all scripted… If it weren’t for your specialty placed cue cards you would be lost and begin to stammer. For all we know it could all be a voice over. My point is rather simple trash talking is for guys like you. Therefore don’t feel as though I’ve forgotten about you during the past week. I’ve just had more important things to take care of. I have had to take a sh!t a few times this past week. That in itself is more important. Yet, it seems to smell just as bad as when you open your trashcan hole. Johnson your time is coming… or should I say your time is about to end…” The scene fades out… I sat there on the stood as the little round man that was the production manager yelled. 'Cut.' Looking around at everybody. 'Ok people, that's a wrap.' I didn’t wait for another word. I thought this was stupid and thought so even more now that it’s over. Thank God it was over. Looking to the clock on the far wall I find that this little waste of time cost me half the afternoon. I wasn’t about to waste another minute standing around waiting for someone to pat me on the back and tell me what a great job I had done. I’d leave those sort of things to Johnson. He seems like the sort of guy that needs a lil pat every now and then. As I walked past the crew working to finish things up some of them smiled at me while others ducked their heads. I just ignored them all as I walked into the outer hallway. Once there I had seen something or someone that is that I hadn’t planned on seeing here, Steve Johnson himself. He was at the far end of the hall standing in front of a vending machine. I began walking towards him as he pulled change from his pocket and placed it into the coin slot. He hadn’t heard or paid any attention to me as of yet, and I was closing in on him. He pressed the digit for his selection. Once he bent over and took out a snickers from the drop tray. He turned and for the first time came eye to eye with me. We both just stood there staring each other in the eye. He’s about an inch shorter than me so as when I was looking straight ahead his eyes were looking up at me in an angle. I liked the fact that he was shorter and small than me. I think it plays on the back of his mind. He stood his ground as he spoke. “Rip.” A wince in his eyes as he said my name. “John… son.” I let the last syllables of his name roll off my tongue. He then slowly peeled the wrapper away from his candy bar and took a bite. Both of us watching the other carefully as he did so. In between bites he ask. “What you doing here?” “You questioning me… lil man?” I peered deep into his eyes. As if trying to sneak a peak into his soul. He smirked as he confidently replied. “We’ll see who’s the little man come this Tuesday at Turmoil.” We then both stood our ground and eyed each other. Neither wanting to give the other a mental edge. After a few moments he slowly made his way around me. Once he made his way by he didn’t look back. As if knowing I wouldn’t attack him from behind. Perhaps I should do something. He’s about to pass a window. It would take much to grab him and force him through it. I think we’re on the third floor. A man his size could survive a fall that far, couldn’t he? I watched him as he turned a corner at the far end of the hall. After he was out of site I as well made a hasty exit. I had a lil work to do before I left the PCW building for the day. ***Scene Fades*** I stood at the edge of the building waiting for him to walk out. I had gotten to the parking lot before him. On the way I stopped and asked the person security guard at his post near the front of the parking lot which car he had driven in with. He pointed to a red ford explorer. The guard must have had a hard night. For not only thirty minutes after I had asked him about the car, and he began to dose off. Which gave me plenty of time to work with. I had barely finished when the guard had woke up. But I found my way to my current position by that time. After a few moments the man I had been waiting for walked out of the building and made his way towards his car. My opponent for next Tuesday was about to have a very disappointing day. Steve Johnson strolled toward the center of the parking lot and got into a black sports car right next to the red ford explorer. Damn security guard. As Johnson pulled away. I looked down to my hand where I was holding onto part of a brake line I had cut away from the ford explorer. Wrong car. I shrugged, dropped the line, and walked off. Somebody other than Johnson was going to have a bad day. But apparently Johnson’s was going to have to wait till Tuesday due to some stupid security guards mistake…
