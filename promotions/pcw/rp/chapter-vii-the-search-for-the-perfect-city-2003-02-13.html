Chapter VII: The Search For The Perfect City

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-2-03 Chapter VII: The Search For The Perfect City Mark’s head is banging against the cold window as the bus driver goes through all the pot holes possible on the highway. The bus driver is singing an old barber shop tune as he has flashbacks to his younger days. He can still picture himself wearing that white top hat with the blood red band around it. He remembers the white suites with the red pinstripes going down the side as well. He can see himself singing into the mic at the one he loves in the crowd with the rest of the guys who are standing right next to him. “HOLY SHIT!” the driver screams as he hears the sound of a semi. The bus driver crosses back over into his own lane of traffic as he was over on the other side. Mark jumps out of his sleep as he goes up in the chair. His back rubs against the back side of the cloth as his back heats up from the friction created between the cloth and his skin. “What in the fuck are you trying to do?” Mark screams as he asks the question so that the old bus driver can hear. “Are you trying to kill us?” The bus driver doesn’t say anything as Mark sits back down in his chair and he starts to relax once more, but he can’t. He starts to bite the nails on his right finger since the nerves in his body are all kicking in and he needs to be doing something. Mark starts to look out the window as well and he can’t tell where they are at. “Shoobie dobbie oh oh oh.” The bus driver starts to sing out as Mark starts to cover his ears. “Can you keep it down up there old man?” Mark asks as he leans his head up next to the window once again and closes his eyes. Mark starts to clear his mind with all of the negative in his life. He can hear the wind slam up against the bus and its starting to sound like a tornado is in the air. Once again the bus driver is still dreaming about his old days as his singing starts to get louder and louder and he seems to be getting more emotional with it as well. Mark hears a nearby car go past as it is faint from the sound of the wind. Mark can feel the bus starting to drift as he opens up his eyes and he peers out into the dark. “Dude get the bus back in your fucking lane and take the next exit off of this damn road.” Mark says as he stands up in his seat. “Fine! Have it your way.” The guy says as he pulls off to the right and he barely misses the guardrail along the exit ramp. The bus steams up the ramp with some power gearing behind it. Mark sits in his seat anxiously as he is waiting for the bus to come to a stop. The bus driver is snapped out of his dream world as he looks like a pissed off guy. The bus driver takes a right at the stop sign and starts going down the road towards the light of the nearby city. The bus driver than is almost to the city of lights but than stops the bus hard as all of the passengers go forward in their seats. “Get the hell off of my bus!” The guy yells as Mark gets up. “Thanks for the ride damn barber shop fucker.” Mark says as he is walking down the aisle toward the exit. Mark gives the bus driver an evil look as he exits the bus in style with waving his arms up. “FUCK THE COONS!” Mark yells as he exits the bus to get everyone’s attention. Mark starts running down the black paved road with the dimmed yellow street lights shining on the road just outside of the city. Mark is looking around and doesn’t notice any familiar surroundings. Mark doesn’t care though he looks as if he has just been freed from jail as his body is running into the cold wind. Mark doesn’t look as that fazes him. But then Mark just stops running as he gets to the city’s border. He drops down on his knees and starts to breathe heavy. He lifts his head up to the sky. “FFFRRREEEDDDOOOMMM!!!!!” Mark screams at the top of his lungs as it echoes through the houses of the city. Mark just sits on his knees for a while to get his breath back and as he does so he looks around at the houses and they seem all like the Victorian houses from the old days. Mark makes his way up to standing but notices his right leg. The skin is starting to deteriorate on his leg and as he looks down a piece of skin just falls to the ground reveling the bottom layer of his skin. “What is happening to my body?” Mark asks himself out loud though. Mark just shakes his head as he doesn’t care since he has made it to a new town. A new town that could change his life around. Mark starts walking to all of the bright lights in the town as he is just seeing houses but brighter business lights in the distance. As Mark walks near the sun is starting to come up in the horizon in the distance. People are starting to come out of their house for yet another beautiful day on their nice green lawns. Men are coming out in their bathrobes with some still unshaven. They are all lining up on their lawns as Mark walks past them with a big smile on his face like he just got laid last night. “All hail your new leader Mark Makonie.” Mark shouts as he is walking down the middle of the street on the main street. “Who in the hell are you?” A guy asks as he picks up his paper from the lawn. “I am here to change your town.” Mark says as he stops for this guy so that he can talk to him. “How are you going to do that?” The guy asks as he is licking his lips and licking the shaving cream right off. “Why would someone like you ask such a dumb question like that?” Mark asks as he is still standing in the middle of the street. “You should just be delighted that your master has stopped walking for you. You should be on your knees kissing the old great ones feet. I am here to regain the power of the whites back.” The guy starts to get on his knees as Mark is puffing up to make himself look a little bit bigger for intimidation. He then starts to crawl towards the street to get closer to kiss the ground Mark is on. But then Mark notices that the man is not wearing any underwear and that his balls are just flopping all over the place. Mark then starts to march into town some more as he leaves that guy on his knees with his balls out of place. “If that’s how easy this town is to persuade, this is not going to be a problem.” Mark says to himself as the day is just beginning with the lights being turned off in the town.
