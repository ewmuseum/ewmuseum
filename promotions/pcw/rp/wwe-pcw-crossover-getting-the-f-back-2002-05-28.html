WWE/PCW Crossover...GETTING THE 'F' BACK!

Title: WWE/PCW Crossover...GETTING THE 'F' BACK! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 5-28-2002 ..::/::..Our story begins in a mysterious and dimly illuminated alleyway, somewhere in the deep heart of the city. A lone streetlight flickers on and off on it's last breaths of light, barely lighting the trashy and unkept alleyway, littered with various needles, paper, and open beer bottles and cans. If there was any location unbefitting of a Rent-A-Hero intro, it would have to be this one. And speaking of the spandex clad superhero, we find him on the very corner of this alley, watching various passersby lead their lives and walk the streets with their own personal agendas, our hero dressed in a full trenchoat with only his mettallic black and red Spiderman 2099 mask able to be seen. He seems to be waiting in deep anticipation for someone or something, tapping his foot repeatedly and with wearing patience as he awaits the event which is unknown to us, staring up at the night sky and admiring the hundreds of jewels laced within it called stars..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Holy Mystery Men...what am I doing here in this alley? I need to be at home, training and raising my power level so I shall be able to combat my fellow Crusierweight opponents on Tuesday Turmoil and reclaim the belt that I had never lost. I've never wanted to have something more in my life since the day I realized of loved Amy...and just like she is an integral part of my life, that title...the belt I gave countless amounts of Energon to, shall be back in my grasp and safe where it belongs. I'm sure all those other Crusierweights are incredible, all worthy enough to make claim to the title 'Crusierweight Champion' but if one of them wins it...it will be because they BEAT ME, the rightful Crusierweight champion, for it...not winning it through some tournament where the true champion isn't given his chance to defend. You don't steal Superman's cape...you don't take the squishness out of Gummi Bears, and you for darned sure don't take the PCW Crusierweight title from me unjustly WITHOUT A FIGHT!! So I call upon the powers of Greyskull, help me and aid me in my battle to vanquish those pretenders to the Crusierweight throne, give me strength. On a side note, I sure hope no one notices me...I mean, this is an ingenius disguise.' *Completely oblivious to the fact his mask if fully visible and everyone can see him.* ..::/::..Before our hero can continue his usual long-winded monologue, a strange mystery man spears our hero forcefully into the alleyway, both men falling within the dark abyss of the alley unbeknownst to anyone walking the streets at that moment. Our hero shakes his head and rises to his feet startled, raising his fists as he squints his eyes and looks about in the darkness, trying to find the figure who attacked him. He spins about, looking around as his anger starts to rise before he finally outbursts..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'SHOW YOURSELF EVIL KNAVE OR KNOW MY WRATH!! THIS ALLEY MIGTH BE DARKER THAN A NATIONAL N.A.A.C.P. CONVENTION, BUT I SHALL FIND YOU!!'Mysterious Voice:'No need friend...'..::/::..The aforementioned streetlight flickers on suddenly, the flash lasting long enough to reveal another man in a trenchcoat standing right before our hero. The light flickers a few more times before finally staying lit, a dim light cast over the filthy alley as the trenchcoated Rent-A-Hero stands before a man in a green trenchcoat with a matching green scarecrow hat, his hands tucked within his pockets as our hero looks at him with a look of question. Rent-A-Hero asks..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'I recieved your message on my answering machine...who are you and you better have a good reason for calling me here, Vampire Hunter D was playing on the Sci-Fi channel and you've got me missing it you MISERABLE MYSTERY MAN O' MYSTERIOUSNESS YOU!! I want more answers than a game show, and I want them NOW!'Mystery Man:'Lower your violent tone, there is no need for those FEROCIOUSLY HARSH INSULTS my friend...me and you are on the same side and fighting for a common cause! There is a situation I'd like to make apparent to you and it has something to do with the WWF, now known as the WWE. It seems that since the 'F' has gotten out, many believing because the 'F' was harshly abused and given no reason to stay...letters have feelings you know, that we have lost integral parts of what made the WWF the number one federation and show on cable. There is a strange force at work causing this and it must be stopped and I can't do it alone...I need your help my friend..'Rent-A-Hero:'Well...the WWF has gotten about as stale as rye bread in a Nuvosian sun, anything that will at least make it WATCHABLE again would be welcome. But what do you care STRANGE STRANGER O' STRANGE DEEDS...you make it sound like your a hero or something...'Mystery Man:'Maybe because...I AM....STAND BACK RENT-A-HERO...seriously stand back, cause when I throw this trenchcoat off it's going to fly in your face and enwrap you and that'd make for a bad spill..' *Rent-A-Hero nods and backs up as told.* 'NOW STAND BACK...'..::/::..At that instant, the trenchcoat and hat are tossed off the mysterious man, revealing a not so mysterious man after all....it's none other than WWE'S superhero...THE HURRICANE!! He is crouched with forearm in front of him, looking battle ready in his green and black spandex costume complete with swirly 'H's' on the chest, complimented by his green and black mask and apple green dyed hair tips. He then exclaims again..::/::..The Hurricane:'THERE'S A HURRICANE COMING THROUGH!!!'Rent-A-Hero:'Ahh the Hurricane, your various exploits have been documented and I must say I am impressed by you as a fellow hero...but do you have the power to restore credibility to an entire Crusierweight division?!' The Hurricane:'Well DO YOU have the power to job to men fo lesser talent then you while still retaining a face pop and what little credibility you have my spandex clad partner for awhile?'Rent-A-Hero:'You've won this round...'The Hurricane:'We have no more time to jest fellow superhero, we have an F to attain and bring back to it's rightful place my friend!'Mysterious Voice:'I'm afraid we can't let you do that...' ..::/::..The WWE and PCW superheroes turn around fully, striking their respective fighting stances towards the entrance of the alleyway as they see three shadows within the very outskirts of the alley, one of the shadows a completely huge figure. The superheroic wrestling dup hold their ground, keeping their stances tight as they can hear the synchronized footsteps of the three men approaching them, the sound of the dry and crackling cement growing louder beneath their feet. Finally, the three men enter the light...revealing themselves to be none other than....THE WWE D-TEAM!!..::/::..!!!~GASP~!!!..::/::..The WWE D-Team, consisting of the untalented Billy Gunn, the annoying and leech-like X-Pac, and the overweight Big Show stand before our duo, cracking their knuckles and looking to the outnumbered heroes as the Billy Gunn says, fixing his red and white 'Billy' headband.>::/::..Billy Gunn:'Well well well, if it isn't the two superheroes...we're sorry boys, but there is NO WAY your putting the 'F' back in the 'WWF' cause when the F was in, lazy, untalented, leech wrestlers like us strove for a spot...now with the F gone, we can dominate the card with as little effort as possible while the talented guys still have to work for spots!! IT'S A JOBBER'S DREAM COME TRUE!! So surrender before I fuc...I MEAN BEAT YOUR A**!!'..::/::..X-Pac and Big Show take a slight step away from Billy Gunn, teetering on the line between straight and flaming homosexual. They then return their forces to the newfound superhero force as Rent-A-Hero exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'NEVER SHALL WE SURRENDER IN THIS FIGHT!! THANKS TO YOU, YOU ARE DESTROYING THE RATINGS AND CREDIBILITY OF WRESTLING AND THAT SHALL NOT CONTINUE, SO PREPARE TO GET SPANKERED YOU TRIO OF TOTOLITARION JOBBERS!!'The Hurricane:'YOUR GONNA GET HURRICIZED!!'Rent-A-Hero:'Hey, that hero-ism wasn't half bad, can I use it some time?' The Hurricane:'Sure why not, but only if you let me rent that 'Holy Booty Shorts' one, that's as classic as when Wonder Woman was wearing the nurses outfit on episode 67.' ..::/::..Both the Hurricane and Rent-A-Hero look to the sky, drooling at the thought of Linda Carter in a tight and revealing nurses outfit, still eccentuating her curves and stunning beauty...not to mention booty ;) Shortly, the two stop their sligth day dream and rush towards the trio ready for battle. The Big Show steps forward and puts the palms of his hands out, The Hurricane and Rent-A-Hero conveniently running their faces into his palms, making the two run in place as they throw wild punches which hit no mark, the WWE D-Team laughing in delight. Finally, the Big Show is tired of toying with the two defenders of quality wrestling everywhere, putting his hands around their throats and preparing to lift them up before he yells in his contorted giant voice..::/::.. The Big Show:'IT'S TIME FOR A CHOOOOOOOOKESLAM!! AHHHHHHHH!! CHOOOOOOKESLAAAAAAM!!!'X-Pac: *speaking in usual wigger talk* 'Ummmm Show...why do you keep saying that thangs like that boy-eeeeee, kno what I mean HOOOMES?!'Billy Gunn:'Don't stop him...it's kinda sexy...I MEAN, KINDA SCARY!!'..::/::..The whole alley grows quiet after Billy's second homosexual comment for the night, Rent-A-Hero and the Hurricane looking towards each other while in the grasp of the Big Show and saying..::/::..The Hurricane:'HOLY HUMAN TORCHES RENT-A-HERO...THERE'S A FLAMING HOMOSEXUAL IN OUR MIDSTS!!'Rent-A-Hero:'MY RENT-A-SENSES DETECTED HIS EXTREME GAY POWER LEVEL HURRICANE...BUT WE MUST ESCAPE THIS PREDICAMENT!! I say we use the Super Saien Buzzkill Trifecta Blaster...'The Hurricane:'But where are we gonna find a princess, two electric batteries, some oregano, a lighthouse, and about one million bolts of energy?!' Rent-A-Hero: *pouts while fighting the Show's grip.'It's nice to imagine...'The Hurricane:'How about the unfancy yet effective, DOUBLE NUTCRACKER O' DOOM!!'..::/::..Both heroes nod as the Show prepares to lift them both high in the air and send them crashing to the ground for the chokeslam, but with split second timing, our heroes both put out a foot, the force of The Big Show's lift causing them both to crash into his crotch as he screams and topples over, crashing atop X-Pac and Billy Gunn as the WWE D-Team has been defeated in one fell swoop..::/::..The Big Show:'AHHHHHH!! MY NUUUUUUUTS!!!'X-Pac:'QUIT TRIZZILIPPIN' BOY-EEEE, IT[S GONNA BE AIGHT....besides, you have to HAVE ni-zuts for it to hurt boy-eeee.' Billy Gunn:'Hey guys...all these hot sweaty men....on me...is making me kinda...'Show & Pac:'SHUT UP BILLY!'..::/::..With the WWE D-Team defeated, The Hurricane and Rent-A-Hero stand unscathed and ready for more battle as the Hurricane exclaims..::/::..The Hurricane:'COME PARTNER, WE HAVE TO REGAIN THAT F!! COME...TO THE HURRICYCLE!!'Rent-A-Hero:'Can't we use the Rent-A-Car...I mean, it just needs a good jump or two from the magic CABLES O' JUMPING, but it's reliable...for the first mile.'The Hurricane: *ahem* 'I SAID...TO THE HURRICYCLE!!'..::/::..Our hero mopes and couples it with a grumble, both heroes dashing out of the alley with intewnt to save the world of wrestling from being diluted by boring matches and inane interviews. As the scene begins to fade, Billy can be heard saying..::/::..Billy Gunn:'Hey Show...show me where your hurt out, I'll kiss it and make it better...'Big Show:'X-PAAAAC....I'm scared...'X-Pac:'Just shut up and take it Show...I mean it ain't the first time we'll get pleasured by a man...why else do you think we still have jobs boy-eee.'Big Show:'But Vince just said he was cleaning my colon....GYAAAAAAH!!'..::/::...With that said, the scene fades, the adventure not over for our two allied superheroes.
