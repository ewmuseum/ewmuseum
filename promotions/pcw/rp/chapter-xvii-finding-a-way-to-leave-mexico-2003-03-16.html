Chapter XVII: Finding A Way To Leave Mexico...

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16-3-03 ::Introduction:: Tonight is the night all the Madness is going on. Its where thirty men will enter one ring, one by one, but only one will prevail to call themselves the PCW Champion. The competition is stiffening up as some wrestlers are showing what they are made of throughout the week. Even now some people are coming out of their shells to give some wrestlers a run for their money. Some people are going to be tired already come to the battle royal, but they are going to have to fight through that and try to prevail as other people. Akiyama will come back to PCW as he has not appeared in so long and other people who are unknown for their work in the ring might be able to show what they have really in them. But there are five people who have not been released for the battle royal, and I wonder who they are going to be. Are they going to have an impact on the royal and how it ends up? Or are they just newbies who haven’t experienced this before? All the questions will be answered tonight along with how good the performance of Mark Makonie is. He has been waiting for this night all week long so that he could show some people what he is made of and how he can fight with the top carders. ::End Introduction:: Chapter XVII: Finding A Way To Leave Mexico… The yellow taxi cab speeds down the interstate with only one person inside. The person is the new driver of Mark Makonie as he has just killed Pedro. Mark looks down at the radio and he starts to go through all of the radio stations. There is Latin music on and that is pretty much all that makes up the radio stations. “Why in the fuck do they just have Latin music on these waves?” Mark says to himself as he pushes the radio off. “I have to find a way to get out of this country and a quick one.” Mark starts to reach down into his front pocket where the rocks are at. He feels the rocks and remembers that he had some cash left with him still. He feels around and finds a paper that feels like a bill. He pulls that out of his front pocket and he holds it in his hand. He goes up to just above the rear view mirror and he pushes the button to get the light to come on in the car. He looks at the bill. “How in the fuck do I only have twenty dollars left out of my last PCW pay check?” Mark asks himself as he doesn’t remember where it all went. “I can’t get out of Mexico with this money. Because I have to get pesos, and there isn’t a currency place open this early in the morning.” Mark looks at the fuel gage and the fuel light comes on as he looks down. Mark takes his palm and he slams it against the steering wheel as the car almost goes off of the interstate. Mark gets a hold of the wheel again as he straightens the car so that it starts to go straight back on the road. “How in the hell am I going to get out of here?” Mark asks himself again as he could be stranded in this country forever. “I am not going to stay here for as long as I live. I couldn’t take it here any longer.” The cab starts to putter out as Mark pushes on the gas hard as he works it down to the floor. The mile per hour gage is starting to fall as the car is coasting down the interstate. Mark turns the wheel as it starts to head towards the side of the road. The car slows down all the way as Mark just makes it to the gravel as he puts the car in park. Mark opens up the door as he walks out from the lighted car. The moon shines as it reflects some light off of Mark’s bald head up to the black night. The stars are out as well as they light up the sky and the soft blowing wind catches the shirt of Mark. The sound of a train is hear faintly in the dark as Mark starts to walk towards the sound. “I can catch the train and sneak back into the States.” Mark says as he starts to run, but his leg is not helping him get there any quicker. The leather boots that Mark has on are getting wet again at the tip as the dew on the grass is coming up on the tip. Mark’s socks are not wet at all; they are as dry as can be. The bone in Mark’s leg is starting to be a problem to the physical activities that are in Mark’s life like right now. The train sound gets louder and louder with every step that Mark takes. Mark takes a right into the woods as he thinks that is a quicker way to get there. The leaps over the logs and the lands are making his leg less effective with one of those taken. Leaves are getting thrown up behind Mark as he runs past the trees. Animals are scattering everywhere as they can hear the vibrations in the earth with Mark’s footsteps. Thoughts are running around in Mark’s mind again. What is going to happen now into the future after he gets into the States? How is he going to live with only his twenty dollars? There are many questions that need answered, but only time will answer them. The train is coming closer as there is a train station up ahead so that they can check the weight and what is on the train. Mark appears out of the woods as right in front of his face is the train station. It looks like it has been built a long time ago, the same with the tracks. The station was built with the tracks and they haven’t touched it since. Mark walks up to behind the train station as the guards come out so that they can check out the train. Mark walks over to the train as he is going to try to find a way on. Mark gets down on his hands and knees as he starts to crawl around to the side of the train station. “What is on the cargo?” A guard shouts from inside. “Just a few cattle and some hogs.” One of the inspectors say as they come out from one of the train boxes. The inspectors walk out of the boxes as Mark is watching from the side. Mark then goes to the back of the train as he is trying not to have any one see him. The guards are in a ground talking with one another as what they have found in the train. As they do this Mark goes around to the backside of the train as he tries to get one of the sliding doors open. The doors are not budging as on the other side the sound of the doors closing is heard. Mark just starts to pull on the doors, but still are not moving anywhere. The train sounds as it is getting ready to leave the station. “Come on you fucking door!” Mark says in anger as he is still trying to get the door open. The train starts to roll out of the station as Mark gives the door one final tug. The door opens with the force of the train moving as the sound rumbles through the station of the steel hitting steel. The guards look around as they don’t see anything from the noise as they let the train go on.
