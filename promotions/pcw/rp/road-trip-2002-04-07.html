Road Trip?

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th April OOC Comment: I'm gonna start the novel style real soon. “Create Sound” by 4Fit serves as our musical backdrops as the scene slowly opens up. Our camera hovers around a large, single decked coach, which is cautiously driving along a double lane tarmac road, which cuts through the sides of some extremely dangerous looking mountains. The outer shell of the coach is basically polished chrome, which gleams with the sun’s reflection. The words “Pearce’s Executive Travel” is printed in large blue letters across the side of the coach. Some of the curtains, which line the many windows on the coach, are drawn closed for some strange reason, seeing that the weather on the outside is perfect sunlight. We can also see through some of the windows that most of the seats on the coach are taken, it’s a full load, consisting mainly of old age pensioners and tourists. Our camera hovers around the outsides of the bus for a few more seconds as it twists and turns down the mountain road, when the scene suddenly changes. We are now inside the interior of coach, right at the front. The driver is a short, tubby man who has very little in the way of hair. He has heavily tanned skin and a black moustache; he is wearing a white & blue striped shirt and a pair of black pants. The driver has a steady look of concentration on his face as he navigates the bus down the long, winding roads. Our camera rotates round, so that now we are facing directly up the thin aisle of the bus between the two rows of seats at either side. We can now see that the bus is indeed full, and there is not enough room to swing a cat inside. Our camera then slowly begins to move up the middle of the aisle … We pass all sorts of weird and wonderful people including an exceptionally large women and her runt husband, a pair of midgets and an elderly couple dressed in elaborate Hawaiian shirts, who are of course, asleep. But we aren’t here to talk about old people are we? No … Because this is an Andy Murray promo, suckah! The camera stops moving along the aisle, and turns slowly around where we see Andy Murray sitting alongside his moron sidekick Jon, last seen during Andy’s GWF & PWA stints. Jon sits closest to the window, and his chin rested on the rubber coating around the window frame. He seems to be staring out into the wilderness, and is wearing a blue silk shirt and a pair of blue shorts, not forgetting his “pet” Davey Crockett hat named Bear of course. Andy is wearing a black tank top white a large black leather coat, which is open on top of it, a pair of black jeans, some black boots and a pair of shades. Andy seems to be having a conversation on his cell phone. Andy Murray: No … I haven’t decided what Im going to do with myself … The voice on the other end of the phone responds, it’s none other than Ben J. Pailthorpe, fellow PCW superstar and Forgotten Ones member. BJP: Just make sure you don’t screw anything up, ok? Andy Murray: BJP, I am not going to screw a single thing up. I am sure that you know what I have gone through in life, and if all the fragile memories have left me with something, it is how to make a good decision. BJP ponders his response for a moment. BJP: Hmmm … Well Andy, just remember you’re in the big rising star title battle royal tonight. Andy Murray: I remember … But at the moment matches are far from my thoughts. You understand that this is a major turnaround in life for me, and that tonight will be just another chance for me to unleash my frustration upon PCW. BJP: Hmmm … Well, I am glad to see that you have finally ditched the dark side. Andy Murray: Indeed, Satanism serves no purpose to me anymore, just don’t expect me to follow in your footsteps and join the side of Christianity. BJP: That was the least I was expecting. Andy Murray: Good, because right now I do not need a God or idol to overlook my every action. From now on I am going to take life one step at a time, I will be the one who is in control. There is a pause between Murray and BJP, before BJP keeps the conversation alive. BJP: Well, just as long as you are happy I suppose. Andy Murray: Happiness … What is happiness? Is it the enjoyment of activities and pastimes? Or is it something deeper than that? I think feel that I have never been truly happy, and I doubt if I ever will be. BJP: Riiiiight? Andy Murray: Besides, how do we know if we are ever happy with ourselves? The feeling we call happiness may just be materialistic. BJP sighs, then continues, desperately trying to change the subject. BJP: Where the hell are you anyway? Shouldn’t you be training or something? Andy Murray: Well BJP … Im … Urrr … Well, to tell the truth I don’t really know where I am. Jon managed to persuade me into coming on a bus trip with him … Why I ever listen to him I’ll never know. BJP: Damn … Well make sure you get to the arena before the match, we can’t have you do a “no show” now can we? Andy Murray: As I have told you, the match does not concern me at the moment. I shall bid you a farewell now, goodbye. Andy quickly lowers the phone from his ear, and presses the “end call” button, before sliding it back down into the pocket of this jeans. Andy sighs to himself, and then glances at Jon, who is still staring out of the window. Andy then turns back around, and sits in silence for about 10 seconds, before breaking the silence … Andy Murray: Jon … Jon is startled by Andy, and turns around quickly. With a serious look on his face (well, as serious as Jon gets), Jon responds, speaking in an unusually deep tone of voice. Jon: Who calls at this unearthly hour! Andy Murray: Ummm … I do, and what the hell are you talking about? It’s 2 in the afternoon, peon. A look of confusion spreads wide across Jon’s face, before turning into a look of thought and then a look of acknowledgement. Jon: Ahh … ‘Tis Murphy! Andy Murray: Murphy? Who is this Murphy you speak of? If it is me you refer to, my name is Murray. Jon: Oh … Some ugly boy called Bob told me you was called Murphy … Silly Bob … He’s almost as stupid as Criggyton! Andy stares at Jon with an emotionless expression on his face, he knows that Jon has absolutely no idea what he is talking about. Andy Murray: Idiot … His name is Crichton, and he is not stupid in the least. John Crichton is my friend, he is not one of the pathetic jobbers that roam PCW so freely these days. Jon: What’s a jobber? Is that someone who doesn’t have a job? Andy sighs. Andy Murray: Well, if you look it up in the dictionary, you’ll probably find the words “Mike Frampton” or “Reeco Suave” printed right next to it. These are the type of guys who have no hopes or aspirations that they can live up to in life, they are the type of people who shall fall from grace when The New Nightmare strikes upon them. Jon: Are you saying that Reeco Suave and Mike Frampton aren’t good wrestlers? Andy Murray: Well done Jon! You managed to work that out all by yourself! Jon: I know … Im a clever boy. Andy Murray: No, you’re a mentally challenged dumbass, but that’s beside the point … That confused look spreads across Jon’s face again; he then scratches the top of “Bear”, his hat, before an idea comes to his head. Jon: I know! I shall consult the might Bear! Dun Duh Dah! Andy sighs, and then slaps his forehead, as Jon pulls the hat off of his head. He holds “Bear” in front of his face, and starts speaking to it. Jon: Bear, am I related to a word called “a dumbass”? Jon then speaks an in extremely high-pitched tone of voice, trying to make it like Bear is speaking back to him. Jon/Bear: No Jon … I don’t know what he means! Jon: Thank you Bear! Jon then fixes the hat back onto his head … His “hat talking antics” seem to have attracted some unwanted attention, as most of the passengers on the coach seem to be now staring at Andy and Jon. Andy sighs again, Jon sits there with a smirk on his face. Jon: See, even Bear doesn’t know what you mean! You silly! Andy Murray: Don’t you have some Froot Loops or something you can keep yourself occupied with? Because quite frankly I have had enough of you’re gibberish. That fool Shawn Mosley makes more sense than you do! Jon: Shawn Mosley? Do you mean Shawn Measly? Andy Murray: No Jon … I mean Sean Mosley. You’re memory really is very poor indeed, you need to start remembering peoples names a bit better. A childish grin spreads over Jon’s peculiar face. Jon: I like this story! Tell me more! Andy Murray: Idiot, this is not a story … I am merely preaching a few facts in your direction which could really serve you well in the mental department. Jon still sits grinning, his “Rabbit In The Headlamps” eyes are as wide as always. Andy glances at him with a confused look spread across his face. Andy waves his hand in front of Jon’s face, this has no effect however, so Andy tries other methods … Andy Murray: Umm … Jon? … JON! … WAKE UP! Jon continues staring for another few seconds, before suddenly he recoils, and shakes his head, he then gives Andy the thumbs up, indicating for him to continue. Andy Murray: So kind of you to wake up Jon … So Jon … After all this, do you still think I have a chance of winning tonight? Jon pauses for a moment, to ponder his choice as Andy awaits, expecting Jon to predict Murray for the tournament. Jon: Hmmm … Well … I would have to say … CHARLIE LOC is so totally gonna kick ass! Andy Murray: CHARLIE LOC!? Jon: Yeah! He da man! Jon gives two cheesy thumbs up, Andy just shakes his head and smirks. Outside of the window, we can now see that we are no longer passing through the mountains, but the coach is now cruising slowly through some village that wouldn’t look out of place in a Clint Eastwood flick. The majority of the buildings in this village appear to be made out of wood, and the people we can see passing buy us all look like misfits, a bit like Mexico then? The bus begins to slow, until we hear the hissing of the brakes as we come to a complete stop in the middle of the village. We then hear a **Click** sound, and the bus driver starts to speak over the Coach’s PA system. Driver: Stop number 4 … Location: Parts unknown … For all departing passengers, please remember to take all items of baggage and personal belongings with you … Thank you. The driver stops speaking over the PA system. Andy Murray: Is this our stop? Hmmm … Guess so. Come on Jon! Jon: AGHA! Andy stands up from his seat, and pulls Jon up to his feet by his arm. Andy throws Jon out from the chairs, and into the aisle. Andy then makes sure that he hasn’t left anything on the bus, and tries to walk down the aisle, but finds Jon standing right in front of him staring right at him. Andy points to the front of the coach in frustration. Andy Murray: MOVE IT! Jon: Yes Mommy … Jon then starts to walk down the length of the bus, with Andy following closely behind. When Jon reaches the front of the buss, he kneels down, and starts staring at the driver. It takes a while for the driver to actually notice this, but when he does turn around he jumps at the sight of Jon’s peculiar face staring right up close at him. The driver swallows hard, before finding something to say. Driver: Ummm …. Hey Mister … Nice hat … Jon nods his head in agreement. Jon: He’s called Bear. Driver: Erm … I see. Jon: He likes peanuts, and sometimes in the winter, we build snowmen. The driver nods his head with a look across his face, which says “Save me from this retard.” Andy then grabs Jon buy his shirt, and literally throws him out of the bus door and onto the dusty road outside. Andy then exits the coach himself, as the door closes and the bus pulls away. Jon gets to his feet, and dusts some of the dust from his clothes. Andy looks around at this strange little town with a confused look on his face, as the strange people brush by him. Having no idea where he is, Andy stops a little kid who was trying to run by. Andy Murray: Hey, kid … The kids looks at Andy with a blank face, there is a sense of evil about his eyes from some strange reason, but Andy continues. Andy Murray: Hey … Where the hell exactly am I? Do you know? Kid: I see dead people … Andy Murray: What the fu … Before Andy can say anything else, the kid runs away out of the screen. Andy sighs, and Jon looks around the place like a kid in a candy store. Our scene slowly fades out, leaving Andy and Jon lost in the strange little town.
