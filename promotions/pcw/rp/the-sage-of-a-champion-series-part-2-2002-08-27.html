The Sage Of A Champion Series Part 2

Date: 8-27-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion'The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!' Chapter 1: The Return BeginsDay 2: Part 1 of the battle against BattlePowers (The scene starts with BladeD in his bedroom laying on his 'BladeD' sized custom bed… Thoughts of all sorts are running through his head… His match, “BattlePowers”, Saber’s life, ect. Trying to get this stuff off his head is hard for BladeD… He has to think it over about a million times, basically to the point where it gets old and he is sick of thinking of it.) ^BladeD’s Thoughts^ BattlePowers…. The most diabolical field ever created. It is almost suicide in itself… Yet used by many who can’t learn how to fight has the generals would say…. It is basically simulation of war in real time… Yet people get hurt in this “training” itself like they would in war with the only difference is that there are few or no deaths when these simulations go down… It is still very dangerous and should be outlawed, but the generals won’t allow government officials to whom these simulations in fear of the officials removing BattlePowers for the danger the “soldiers” go through. Yet they really do need to remove BattlePowers because it isn’t even necessary… That is what basic and advanced training is for… FOR WAR~! Yet they still have to put their soldiers through this simulated war… … … … It is the most dangerous, judicial, and in sanest thing I have ever heard in my life. After which some of these BattlePowers I am going to make sure that Saber takes no part in any of this because I don’t want my little brother going through the dangers this simulation has to offer…. It is too big of a risk. They may even try to kill him like they tried to kill me a year ago. Nothing is going to happen to my little brother and that I will make sure of… He hasn’t gotten near the training to avoid any sort of danger I had to avoid to survive and they all know that….My brother needs to clam down and stop trying to act like me…. Trying to act all big and bad… It is that kind of crap that as almost gotten me killed on more than one occasion and I don’t want my brother going through the same things I had to go through…. We share the same blood, but I just don’t think he has near what it takes to be the person that I am… I wish I could get into a ring with him and show him how much more superior I am too him, so he would understand because other people telling him just isn’t enough. Man I wish I could make him understand… It just seems impossible because he seems to take on a lot of my characteristics which isn’t good for him… He really needs to be his own person and stop trying to live in my shadow. I like the fact that he wants to be like me, but he will never come close to being me nor will he ever exceed any of the things that I have accomplished in my lifetime… It just seems hopeless for him.BladeD stops his thinking there, but he is still lying in his bed. The time is about 8:00 o’clock in the morning right now and the sun is out brightly, but Saber isn’t up yet and BladeD isn’t fully awake yet, but he is getting ready for his morning freestyle to wake him up. -- ::BladeD turns on some instrumental music:: BladeD: Yo… Yo… Yo…. Listen to how the beat goes… Yo… Just give it a second and I will hit that flow… Yo… Yo… Here I go… I wish my bro would understand all the dangers that lie ahead in his future and just stop trying to be the man that I am.Stop trying to be me formerly known as BCB and get with the program.It really doesn’t make any sense to me the way he is acting.I am to be honest isn’t really isn’t attracting to girls.If that is one of the reasons why he is acting like this.He really needs to understand that he needs to be his own man.But he just won’t do it and it makes me mad.It would make me glad if you would listen to reason.He is just being so stupid.That kid needs to get his life and stop trying to live mine.My life with rhymes crucial to life.You know they cut like a knife… Bladed… You know how it is.Leaving scars worse than getting hit by a car coming out of the bar after drinking too hard. -- BladeD stops rapping for a minute because he has just impressed himself with what he has just said and it is making him laugh while gasping for air. -- Yo…. Yo… You know how it goes.After stopping for airAnd catching my breathI stare around in the roomWhile trying to stop laughing before deathAnywayI think I am done for the dayIn fact, I am about to go out this way.The way meaning on that awesome rhymeI wouldn’t waste anymore timePaying another dimeOn anything other than thatBecause my rhymes are just phatAnd there is nothing else better than thatBecause unlike the rest I am the bestNo one can beat meIn fact, no one can defeat me.Formerly known as BCB.BladeD Out~!--BladeD turns off his music and gets up out of his bed and finally gets ready for the day… The day is very nice for once because lately there has been a good amount of rain and all kings of storms… It has made all the ground wet and yucky making it hard to get outside and do anything, but today the ground is just a little damp from all the rain… The sun is out… It is nice and shiny, yet BladeD is going to be in an office building taking care of other matters… which kind of sucks for him, but at the same time it isn’t that big of a deal for him either. Anyway, BladeD begins to ahead out for the day and realizes that Saber is missing… He doesn’t think much of it though; figuring Saber just left and forgot to leave a note and ahead out for the day. It is a busy day for BladeD because he is trying to get rid of “BattlePowers.” His location for the day will be the NMA Of NYC (The National Military Association of New York City)…. Yea he is an infantryman and all, but he started out in the military and the NMA is where he always reports… The NMA’s main headquarters is located in Little Rock, Arkansas, but there is a NMA building in every state. So the scene cuts in the middle floor the building; floor 25 and in the room all the way down the hall, turn left into the secondary hall, then the third door on the left side… At the top of the door a sign reads General J. Powers. The scene cuts as BladeD walks in-- BladeD: Hello General. General: Well, Hello BladeD… Next time try knocking first. BladeD: Do I ever knock first? General: (Grins) That is the problem. BladeD: Anyway, We need to talk about BattlePowers for real this time. I am very serious when I say that I don’t like the idea of it. General: Well BladeD. I have told you time and time again that it is a good system that helps. BladeD: It gets people hurt for real and I don’t like that… It would be a better system if fake bullets or something of that sort were used. General: But we want the simulation to run in complete real time. Which even means that all the traps and bullets must be real. BladeD: Screw the real time General. It is getting REAL people hurt and I don’t like that idea. General: Well BladeD. I don’t know what else to tell you other than it is working right now and as long as no one dies BattlePowers will stay fully functional until farther notice.-- Out of nowhere (kind of like movies on cable television) the scene cuts with warning. I guess that means we are going to have to wait until next time before we find out how this conversation ends. -- Get ready for Chapter 2: Day 2. Because it is next~!
