Assanation Series: Prologue - 'Main Event Status'

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/5/03 “How in the name of Satan’s butt-hole did that happen?” Blind Rage rhetorically asked, staring at the piece of paper in disbelief. “Main event status.” Blind Rage stood in the far corner of the living quarters, on the fourth floor of the Justice Inc building. Warrior and Linda sat on the couch, watching an old black and white television set. “Rage, buddy, the first thing we have to do when we establish a steady income, is to by a half decent TV set.” He said, absently taking a sip of Dr, Pepper. “This just plain sucks.” “I agree. You just can’t see the colour of David Borneaz’s on this TV!” Linda pouts crossing her arms. Blind Rage absently walked over to the couch. “Guy’s…we’re in a main event!” Linda and Warrior looked up from the TV and simply stared at the massive superhero. “What main event?” Linda simply said. “This Friday at Fusion.” “But we got our asses kicked last week against E2…why would they throw us into the main event?” Warrior asked, wearing a bemused expression. The question was left hanging in the air for a second before an aura of realisation found both Blind Rage and Warrior at the same time. Almost in synchronisation the two superheroes accusingly glared at their agent. Linda seemed to be able to sense the glares, burning a hole in the back of her head. She turned around and looked up at them. “What?” she simply asked. “What did you do…or more to the point; WHO did you do to get us in the main event at Fusion?” Blind Rage demanded. “How dare you suggest that a professional woman such as me would stoop so low as to sleep with somebody at Primetime Championship Wrestling just to further your careers!” She said, stopping only to catch her breath. “And more to the point, why is it that every time something good happens to the team, do you assume it’s because I’ve slept with them?” “He didn’t tell you his name…did he?” Blind Rage “No.” Linda replied sheepishly. “But on the other hand this could have been some kind of reward!” “Reward? How did you comer up with that?” Warrior asked. “Well…you did beat the current Tag Team champions in a non-title match a couple of weeks back!” Linda replied. Warrior turned to face Blind Rage. “She’s right. We are fantastic!” Blind Rage sighed. “It just doesn’t make sense…that’s all.” “Who cares whether it makes sense or not…the bare bone fact is that you ARE in the main event.” Linda reassured the sensitive mammoth. “I guess you’re right!” Blind Rage replied, folding the piece of paper up and carelessly discarding it in the trash. “Rage…do you understand what this means?” Warrior asked, a hopefully spark in his eyes. “What?” “If we make a good impression on Fusion we could remain in the main event status and dominate the industry, and from there the sky is the limit.” “You’re right! Once achieving main event status we will become icons and people will begin to hire us once again!” As a resultant, Blind Rage grabbed Warrior by the collar of his shirt and effortlessly lifted him up from the couch and then dragged him towards the door. “Hey!” Warrior yelled. “What the hell are ya doin’?” “We’re going to put in extra training this week and then we’re going out to patrol the streets tonight.” “But Badger Man and Handbag Warrior are away this week! We can’t function without them!” “Oh yes we can. We functioned well when it was just me and you back in the old days. We will do so again.” “Damn…big mouthed…son of bitch…shit faced…monkey wrested…bastard” Warrior mumbled, as he was dragged out of the door. ---------- “Damn you Little Willie!” The ‘outrageous’ Owl-Man yelled, aimed at the small midget who wasn’t even present. ‘You’ve really fucked up this time!” The Owl-Man stood in front of a massive computer terminal idly prodding buttons with his incredibly podgy fingers. He was looking through the super-villain’s guide to causing havoc, under the hired hand index. He needed a placement for Willie. He needed to bring Justice Inc down to their knees. The question many asked him was; why? He didn’t need a reason he was a super-villain! It was his job to bother the superhero and make their lives a living hell. World domination just wouldn’t be the same without it. He passed a view more introductory and f.a.q pages and finally found himself on the extensive, alphabetical name list. “The Aardvark…no, too stupid. Bitch Fister…no, too crude. Corporal Chaos…no, too common. Desdemoda Penniscule…no, too unpronounceable. Dildo Basher…no, too filthy. Ah…here we go…Bullseye.” The Owl-Man pressed another key and as a resultant the villains bio appears on the massive screen in front of him. The more he reads, the bigger his smile develops. “I think I have my man!” The Owl-Man declared.
