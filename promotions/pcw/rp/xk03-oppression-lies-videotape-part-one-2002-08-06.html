XK03 : “Oppression, Lies & Videotape. Part One.'

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/02 XK03 : “Oppression, Lies & Videotape Part One.” Location : St. Louis, Missouri.Date : The Morning after March Madness, 1am. “That is IT!” Our shot opens up on the pained face of Xavier Kannon. His face is totally devoid of the cocky arrogance it now bares, and is masked with anger and anguish... Encased in frustration at the one moment. The one lack of focus that had him eliminated from the March Madness battle royal. Xavier : “Where was MY back up? While I was looking out for Miyagawa who was looking out for ME? I’m no more than a fucking lackey.” The Scientologist is walking away from the arena where he failed in his bid to win the PCW World Title, alone, his then valet Eleanor nowhere in sight as the dejected Hubbard follower kicks at a can on the sidewalk. Nobody saw Xavier Kannon in the 3 days between March Madness and the following Turmoil where he turned up to scream oppression and ultimately destroyed the Asylum stable by turning his back on Jeremy Howard... Many thought he just went home and stewed in his own self-pity.... He didn’t speak to an Asylum member during those days... Only he, Ellie~! and a few others know what happened... None would ever tell. After he watches the can skip of the asphalt he stops and holds his head, doubling over to clear his thoughts as he tugs away at his long, Red locks. Xavier : “Urgh.. I just need sleep. It’ll all seem better in the morning, and Jeremy said he’d call.. At least one of us did well.” As he rises his head once more, tossing his mane from over his face he looks calmer... As if he has finally come to he senses, accepting his mistake and preparing to redeem himself. But we all know it didn’t play out that way. *THUD* He reaches for the back on his head... An impact there sending a wave of sharp pain around his skull that centre itself above his Green eyes... His knees buckle, muscles loosing their tension and his body folding. The bright Green of his eyes loses it’s shine as they roll up into his skull while his body makes the rapid descent to the concrete below. The Scientologist lays motionless on the Grey paving slabs.. No wound visible on his head. “This is the one... He’ll tell us what we need to know.” “It’s him all right.... They say he has a bit of a conscience to him and he was only converted six months ago.” “He’s our best bet then.. We’ll tell him what we know and hopefully he’ll see the wrongs of Scientology and spill the beans.” The voices are dull and monotone... As vague as the identities of their owners. By Kannon’s body we see 2 pairs of shiny Black shoes walks beside him and pull the Scientologist up from the sidewalk, his dazed form hanging limp as they drag him away, followed by his shadow then nothing but the streetlight is left as we fade to Black. “Okay.... Game over... Who the FUCK are you?” As we fade in once more we again see Xavier’s face. This time there is no anguish, no frustration... He looks like his animal side. Stubble has grown. His eyes are furious Green once more. A snarl is mapped onto his lips. A bright spotlight then shines into his face, both to highlight him and mask the person who shines it. It works as Xavier screws his face up against the harsh brightness, twisting and turning his neck to try and escape it’s glare but failing and growing more frustrated. Xavier : “HEY! Knock it the fuck off... Who in Hubbard’s name are you and where is this fucking place?” This camera is no longer the one we were viewing from before... It is amateur... There is a white line framing the picture and a Red light visible in the corner... The footage is raw, grainy... Real. From what we see it is clear that Xavier didn’t simply go home and plot the fall of Asylum that night... That choice was taken from him. Xavier : “Would you PLEASE get that fucking light out of my eyes before I have Xenu strike you down.” The light is not removed but it is dulled, accompanied by the camera zooming out to show that the Gold Patron Meritorious is tied to a sturdy looking wooden chair on the floor of what can best be guessed to be an abandoned warehouse. The floor is cold, Grey and dusty. Behind Xavier the light just shows some wooden beams that hold up the roof and their are struggling noises that hint to the fact he is not the only man pulled from the night. With the light no longer blinding him, the Scientologist can see what is in front of him. A row of five people that can best be described as a sinister documentary crew, all dressed smartly in Black suits. In the hands of 2 are a handheld camera and a small boom mic, and behind them makeshift stacks of TV’s show the footage of Xavier live. Every movement he makes he sees played back to him behind his captors who are not moving a muscle, not concerned at all that he is watching himself.. Just studying him.. Watching as he frustrates himself. Xavier : “Hey.. I got something for the highlight reel.... FUCK YOU!“ For the first time his voice has a cockiness to it... Used as a defence mechanism, not wanting the five before him to know that he is worried. That he is weakening. They may have him tied and isolated but he isn’t going to give them the satisfaction of enjoying his mental defeat. His look switches to the next man in line, working his way along. Xavier : “And YOU.... And YOU... And YOU....” He is now looking right down the lens at the man with the camera, a smug grin forming on his mouth as the spotlight highlights the bags under his eyes and the stubble on his chin. Xavier : “And..... Mr Camera Man.... FUCK.... YOU!“ As he levels the final insult at the camera man... An insult born of both frustration and, although he would never admit it, fear, the light is directed right back into his face and the Green orbs of the Scientologists eyes retreat behind his eyelids and his head sharply turns to the right to escape the glare, sending his Red mane flying around. The eyes try to open but are burnt by the sharp, aggressive glare of the light. From the edge of the picture a clenched fist cracks across the Scientologist’s jaw and instead of turning away from the light his head now just drops, his blood dribbling from the corner of his mouth as we fade out into the White of the light. “We know you are a high ranking Scientologist Mr Kannon.... WE know that YOU know about the break-ins... The kidnappings.... And YOU are going to tell US...” Again the brightly lit face of Xavier stares at us through the light... His mind previously clouded by self doubt, defeat and embarrassment is now alive, burning with anger, hate and fear. There is a thin line between his eye-lids where the slightest hint of his eyes can be seen.... Beads of sweat run down his forehead from the hot light that mats some of his Red locks to his forehead while the rest mixes with his already saliva diluted blood that is flowing untamed from the corner of his mouth. He grins at them, showing the blood on his teeth and tongue as a statement of defiance. Xavier : “So that’s what this is about? Because I’m a fucking SCIENTOLOGIST!?!?!” There is no reply to his question. In talking to them he is doing no more than talk to himself as lost in the bright glare of the spotlight they could have earphones in for all he knows. It had begun. He was on the right course before, ready to put his head down and work harder but “They” turned him. Strangers who seemingly want to hurt Scientology had only made one of their high profile members stronger. here it began, the first seed of oppression planted in the fertile soils of his fragile mind.... He did not hate them now for kidnapping him, he hated them for kidnapping him because he was a Scientologist. Now everything will be linked to this moment. Losses. Parking tickets. Being short changed. All because he is a Scientologist. Xavier : “Silent type huh? You think I’m some weak minded peon? Think I’m gonna start blubbering because of harsh lighting?” The Scientologist sneers as he chuckles to himself, then defies his captors further by mocking a distressed, whiny voice.. making sure to look right down the camera as he does so to both defy and anger them. But he is simply making entertainment for the Black suits. Xavier : “Oh.. Oh.. I was wrong.. Scientology is evil, I’ll leave and tell you everything you know.. Really, I will... I’ve seen the light...” The sarcasm stops and the face reverts to dead, cold seriousness. The shot freezes on the expression. Symbolic of both disdain and defiance at his captors that he would later go on to show to PCW fans... A sneer is apparent in the left corner of his mouth as blood has caked it’s path down his stubbled chin. Beads of sweat sit trapped in his Auburn eyebrows, cut off on their path from his dusty, clammy forehead to his bright Green, angry eyes. He is animated once more. Xavier : “And it’s that light you fucking peons are shining in my face.... Want me to beg to you? Want me to give in?” His eyes make all the gesturing his restrained body can before he coughs up some phlegm to mix with his blood and saliva.. Then expels it from his mouth onto the camera lens.. The sticky Red mixture slides down the lens, masking Xavier’s face as he delivers more defiant words. Looking into the bright light he has never had more faith in Scientology. When people die they say they see a bright White tunnel.. Upon this feeling they are about to meet their maker their faith is at it’s strongest. That is what Xavier is feeling.. His back is against the wall and all he has is Scientology and sarcasm.. Both will be used liberally. Xavier : “Fuck that... Fuck you.” A gunshot is then heard as the saliva migrates south to show the face of Xavier no longer defiant. Panicked. Sacred. Weak... Still only having Scientology and sarcasm to fall back on. His belief in the works of Hubbard fills the void the gunshot created as he looks to his right to see a man tied up similar to him fall, along with the chair, to the cold concrete floor and begin the process a Paramedic would call “Bleeding Out.” His face goes pale. His jaw drops. A drip of blood mixed with phlegm hangs from his bottom lip. The making of Kannon has begun. The man was killed because he was a Scientologist. Xavier saw it because he is a Scientologist. Soon.. It would all be because he was a Scientologist. As Jeremy Howard and PCW found out.
