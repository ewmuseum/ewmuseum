Contractual Obligations...

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/17/02 The End has Sandy Wong forward. Sandy is barely clothed at all, only white bra & panties covering her body. She doesn't even shoes on, her height goes from the 6'2' in heels to her natural 5'11' as she must stand in the foul water draining off the rooves through the rank, fungi-clogged gutters. The windows along the side of the buildings in this back alley are so dirty that her relfection doesn't even show. The End is on his ring attire that has become something of an alternate all-purpose clothing for him. They eye Sandy like they have been presented with a rotting cow. 'What would we need with this?' The End shrugs his shoulders. The End:'I was to present you with something dear to my heart for the Sacrifice Pact. Nothing is more important to me than Sandy.' She has her hands folded over her chest, but just for trying to keep warm, the air is cool enough this night that a person could see their breath if the focused enough. 'You didn't even take the time to clothe her.' Sandy Wong:'That was my idea, to come like this.' They shake their heads in confusion and slight disgust. Pointing to The End's Extreme Title... 'We want that.' The End takes it off his shoulder and tosses it to them. The End:'If that's what you want, then take it.' A hand reaches out with speed and precision, catching the belt and as the lights from the buildings around the corner gleam off its surface, the belt pulled back into the dark shadow cast by the building behind whomever stands there. 'This will do just nicely. We shall hold the belt in trust for you, in our name. All you must do is defend it whenever the need should arise.' The End:'Very well.' '... But now, please, there is one more matter that is to be attended to...' *Later* Cyclonica presents The End with a piece of paper, more just tossing it at him. The Celestial Realm grants The End sight, which is why he was brought here. There is not really any light, darkness all around. The realm seems to amplify each individual's own energy to create a sort of immediate bioluminescence, therefore each object is seen at normal light in the darkness. Cyclonica is dressed as before, only hours since The End was cut open. He still has no shirt on, and though his body healed up the cut around his heart, there's still a scar that will persist for the better part of the week. Cyclonica:'Read it and sign it.' The End, with his Celestially acquired eyes, puts them to good use for the time that he is in the realm and reads the paper that Cyclonica threw at him, Cyclonica floating in front of him with her arms crossed, eyeing The End vindictively. ***Celestial Contract of the Order of the Neo Void*** The individual exists in the corporeal realm, but understands and longs for eternal life in the ethereal realm, the realm of Celestials. While this realm is unattainable by all mortals, He grants that His followers shall receive their own realm, a subsection of His Void, in which all of His followers shall be properly rewarded for their service and dedication unto Him. Each individual that chooses so may form a contract with Him. The individual does this in the hopes that he or she may gain His favor and be granted everlasting life and prosperity beyond whatever vices the corporeal realm could possibly provide. However, not every individual that wishes to form a contract with Him is worthy of such a blessing. He knows of mortal desires and understands completely the capacity and capability of the human heart, the human mind, and the human soul. He knows that there are inevitably those that wish His favor and His gifts, but are ultimately destined to be unworthy of such blessings. He also knows that there are those that have since birth been eligible to receive His gifts. So, in order to sift through the fickle, fawning masses, He has developed a set of guidelines, criteria that the individual must meet in order to prove oneself worthy of the favor and the gifts that He may bestow upon the individual. Only the individuals that may pass all of these tests are truly a member of the “elect,” and may attain eternal life and happiness beyond mortal comprehension in the pure darkness that is the Void. Blood PactThe individual must first undergo a process of self-cleansing in a show that they individual wishes to better oneself from the current condition of filth created and propagated by existence in the corporeal realm. Great pain must be endured; the blood of the individual must be spilled. How the individual wishes to do this is the choice of the individual. Sacrifice PactIn addition to the Blood Pact, the individual must give up a corporeal possession that they hold dear to their heart. There can be nothing held up above Him in the eyes, thoughts, and heart of the individual. Public offering of the sacrifice must be made to Him. He knows your heart, and He knows whether the sacrifice is genuine or false. DO NOT attempt to “trick” Him. He has killed for less than that. Individuality PactBy becoming one of His disciples, the individual acknowledges that the individual is no longer one, but one of many, and may be identified and treated as such. Total Faith PactLast, but not least, in fact perhaps most important, the individual understands, acknowledges, and believes with the very core of his or her being that He is right and just in all His decisions. Orders are to be followed immediately without question. To question Him is to renounce your faith in Him, an offense punishable as He sees fit; remember, He is capable of inflicting punishments 1,000 times worse than anything conceivable by mortal minds. Even within the corporeal realm, the individual is given slight rewards for proving that the individual is a member of the elect. The individual may choose to keep one’s former corporeal name or designation, choose a new designation with His blessing, or request that He grant the individual a new designation. The individual, when given no specific orders from Him, and when left to oneself, may act as the individual wishes. In gaining His favor, He then campaigns on the individual’s behalf, and may get the individual booked to title matches and other important events. Few mortals can call themselves the elect, and this only after all of His tests have been passed, after the individual proves to Him that he or she is indeed a member of the elect. The process in which one must prove oneself to Him will be difficult: there is no easy path to enlightenment, no painless route to gaining His favor. The journey, however, is not what matters. The pain, the trials and torment that the corporeal individual must face are nothing at all compared to what blessings that He may offer his elect. He has conceived this contract; therefore it is incontestable and must be followed to the letter. There may be no deviation from these words from the Celestial Overlord, lest you become not one of the elect, but one of the targeted. There are three types of mortals by His word: The Elect – the few that have proved themselves worthy of receiving His gifts. The Masses – the most numerous group of mortals which include the ignorant, the non-believers, and those in the process of proving themselves to be among the elected. The Targeted – this group is comprised of those that have chosen to defy Him. Non-antagonistic non-believers are not among this category. The highest-priority of enemy within the Targeted are former disciples that renounce their status as elect. No one defies Him. No one. The specific duty of the elect is to recruit potential members, ignore the masses, and fight the Targeted. Other instructions are received on an “as needed” basis. With the compacts and duties of the potential elect clearly indicated, the individual must now prove him or herself. Convenience is not a factor; the faster one proves oneself a member of the elect, the better for the individual. The End, known to the Celestials now as the Blind Ninja, nods having fulfilled the terms of the Contract, and signs it with a feather pin dipped into a bottle of his own blood...
