Outgrowing the legend

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/27/2k2 The scene opens back in the lobby of the same office building we left Ricky in where he was waiting onhis uncle to get off work. He had been discussing his past and trying to outgrow his brothers legacy. Ashe had a seat in the plush leather lined, oak chair he was getting ready to read the latest wrestling worldmagazine when he noticed the front cover. Supreme Machine and Thunder to PCW?Thunder's little brother leads his family name to PCWJune 2002Ricky closes his eyes a bit as he takes a deep breath, he then flips the magazine to the article on Ricky andSupreme Machine.Thunder and SuMa to PCW?Apparently so folks. Or at least that is what we are gathering from our backstage sources. It seems thatafter backstage politics were detected in a loss for SuMa, a lot of anger and rage caused the former 5xCCW Heavyweight champion to take his leave of CCW. Ricky Thunder apparently feeling unappreciatedby the fans and management in CCW, decided it was time to take his leave as well. The two mencombined, formed what was known as The Supreme Team. This name may ring a bell to some of you'old school' CCW fans. It was Shane Thunder, Ricky's older brother, and former CCW HeavyweightChampion, that teamed with SuMa forming this connection originally. Ricky Thunder appeared on thescene trying to prove that he had completely healed from a neck injury, just 2 years prior. One fatefulnight, on CCW Lockdown, Ricky Thunder was set to face Yama Tatsu and a mystery partner, a match inwhich Ricky claimed he needed no help. The night of the event came around and Ricky as stated arrivedin the ring alone, until the former champ's music hit and the crowd blew the roof off the arena. TheSupreme Machine made his way to the ring where the two of them decimated Yama Tatsu and an unrulyKensuke Suzahara. The Supreme Machine, after the match stated to the world he had come back to CCWto watch over Ricky. That he had promised his dying brother, Shane, that he would keep him from harm. Ricky became alarmingly upset with this appearance by SuMa as he did not want CCW to know of hisfamily legacy. Ricky was hired on his own abilities, and now, CCW would relate his name to that of hisbrother. The two had disagreements over the whole situation, but eventually were able to come to aconclusion. The Supreme Team would be rebuilt on that night. After about a three month tenure inCCW, with Ricky and SuMa feeling as if they had been 'screwed over' by CCW management, Rickydecided he was joining PCW. The next day, SuMa applied. Both men, have allegedly been accepted,unfortunately we can not confirm that in this edition. We will have more on this story, in July's edition,and you can bank on that. Will Ricky finally break free from his brother's shadow in this new PCWenvironment? Or will this turn out to be another useless struggle for the impressive rookie. Only timewill tell folks. Ricky closes the magazine and tosses onto the little table beside the chair. He looks up and notices hisuncle sitting in the chair in front of him. His uncle still wearing that concerned look. Ricky just nods athim as he gets to his feet. He makes his way over to and out the sliding glass door and his uncle silentlypasses him and leads the way to a new 2002 Chevy Blazer parked near the front of the parking lot. Thecross the smooth blacktop and Ricky hops into the passenger side as his uncle starts up the Blazer. Theypull out of the parking lot and make their way out onto Haddon Avenue. Tony: So what's up Ricky? What's going on? Ricky: Just a culmination of things. I mean all the stuff I do to get into CCW. Everything I worked for,and everything I earned. All wasted. It all meant nothing. Once the word spread that I was Thunder'syounger brother all in ring credentials vanished. It was like they were never there. Nobody feared me. Nobody avoided me. My skills meant nothing. These people condemned me for living out my dream. They accused me of riding my brother's tide. I never wanted anyone to know he was my brother. I mean Iam proud of what he did, but look at all the trouble he has caused. Tony: That's not Shane's fault Ricky. He is gone, remember? You have to focus on the situation at handnow. You no longer have to deal with the rubbish in CCW. You have moved on to PCW. Nobody therewill care what Thunder has managed to do. Ricky: Tony, didn't you see what I was reading? Didn't you read that article about The Supreme Team? This isn't just a CCW issue. This is a world wide situation for me. Everywhere I go I hear about Shane'saccomplishments, and people asking me if I think I will ever be as good as he was. I mean come on,Tony. Shane never made it to PCW, and I did it in only 3 months professional experience. Not countingthe Indy feds of course. I mean I am going to prove to the world that I am better than Shane ever couldhave been. *The Blazer pulls into a parking lot for a Denny's. They pull to the backside of the lot, and exit out. There are no other cars in this particular area, and directly across the street is a few young thugseyeballing the duo carefully. Tony glances back at them and shoots a quick grin, the two young thugsleave the area and disappear back inside the old abandoned building. Ricky and his uncle head inside therestaurant where a jukebox sits right inside the door and booths line the wall with a few available seats atthe bar. They make their way to the corner booth and have a seat as their waitress comes over to taketheir order* Ricky: Just a glass of water for now thank you. Tony: I'll have the #4 platter with a diet coke. *The waitress jots down the order and makes her way to the back, as Ricky and Tony stare silently acrossthe table at one another. Tony's face plagued with pain, the pain one goes through when a close friend orrelative is suffering. Ricky's face spattered in flashes of hate, violence, and fear. Ricky buries his head inhis hands as his hair falls through his fingers.* Ricky: I just don't' know what to do Tony. I mean, I know I have a chance for a new start here, but whateveryone else needs to know, is my past. They need to respect me for what I have done, they need torespect me for who I am. Not for my brother's accomplishments. Tony: Ricky, how about this? You start over. Clean slate. No more past, no more future, just right now! You need to pick a place to begin and right now it seems you can do that. With your fresh start in PCW itgives you the opportunity to divulge in greater things. Look at what you accomplished in CCW in yourshort tenure. You now have the chance to prove yourself in a new fed. I mean come on Ricky, you arethe 'King of Extreme'. Ricky: Speaking of which. I signed up for an Extreme Title match next week. It is a school groundsmatch. First man off the grounds wins. There will be legends and former champions in this match. I amrisking a lot here. I could prove once again why I was the King of Extreme or I can be embarrassed, andagain falter where I should flourish. Tony: Don't worry about it Ricky. You have what it takes. You have always had it in you, thatcompetitive spirit. That drive of carnal anger. You can be the one to lead PCW into it's next era, youknow it as well as I do Ricky. The Phantom Booker does not sign just any ordinary, no talent, slacker. Ifhe signed you he sees your potential. Now it is time to grab that bull by the horns and take charge. Thisis your time Ricky. You are now PRIMETIME! *The waitress arrives with Tony's food and their drinks, as Tony begins to chow down on his meal, Rickyleans back against the bench watching the traffic shoot by on the Avenue. The scene fades out as Rickygets up and exits the restaurant with his uncles eye's following him.*
