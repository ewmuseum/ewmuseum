So much to comtemplate, such little time.

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/3/02 So much time has past, so much has happened, can you return to what you love, to what you desire, despite stabbing that thing in the back, staying away for a long time, and expect to return, and have everything the way it was? It is impossible for such a thing to happen, you must live up to you fears, stare them in the eyes, responsibility was never meant to be easy, but it has to be done. So much time had past since Anton’s last match in PCW, since holding the Cruiserweight Title, it seemed that he had less booking and more time on his hands, but this week, he would not be so lucky. For he would have to compete against The Multiple Man, again, for the Cruiserweight title. It was the anticipated re-match, last time Anton has faced The Multiple Man, it was TMM who was holding the title, but this time it was different, and Anton didn’t intend on losing the title anytime soon. Though, that would not be the last scheduled meeting of Anton and The Multiple Man, for the would collide for the third time at PCW Spring Fling 2002, where there would be a Ladder Match for the Cruiserweight title, but The Multiple Man would not be Anton’s only opponent in that event, he would also have to compete against the magnificent recently re-signed, Rent-A-Hero. That would surely be an epic match. So after so long with no bookings, along come two for Anton to take on. Though he would not sit down, he would not let International Incident get embarrassed, he would rise to the top, and win all his matches, and keep the Cruiserweight Title as long as necessary! He was good enough to be the Champion, and good enough to stay the Champion. La Cruces, a hot dry and busy place, though a place in which Anton ‘the Kid’ would make his first PCW Title Defence, against none-other than then Man that Anton defeated for the belt originally, The Multiple Man. Anton was outside the Pan American Centre, in La Cruces, New Mexico, the day was hot, and Anton had been training all morning. The current time was 12:15 pm. Since 8:30 am Anton had been in the Arena Gym training for his vital match against The Multiple Man. Outside with him, stood the Chef, leader of International Incident. Sweat trickled down Anton forehead, but he quickly wipes it away with the back of his right hand. His black ‘Official PCW Warm-up shirt’ was covered with sweat at the top. Despite all this sweat, his hair still seemed to stay in perfect position, sue to the mounds of gel that infests it. The Chef also seemed to sweat lightly; he was wearing some of his own, merchandised Training gear. It was obvious that International Incident were hard in training, for their bid to dominate PCW. Anton was sitting down on a bench at the back of the arena, a place no fans could get to, and The Chef and Anton were the only to out in the bright spring sky. “I know I am putting pressure on you, but I must insist that you win your match on Turmoil Anton, it is essential for International Incident’s development, we already have you as the Cruiserweight Champion, and Golden Game as the tag Champions, now we must build on this success and not lose it.” The Chef exclaims to Anton, who was listening attentively. “I realize that, and I promise, I will do everything in my power to defeat The Multiple Man, whether the crowd are on my side or not!” Anton responds, while staring Chef in the eye. “The crowd matter not! Only we can bring our success they mean nothing, they are just illiterate, lower-class hillbillies that deserve none of our time.” Chef proclaims. Anton did not like Chef talking in such a manner, for he loved the crowd, whether they love him or not. He loved having the crowd cheer him on, the adrenaline rush was incredible, and it played a lot in him winning matches, but he knew, if he wanted to be respected and successful, International Incident would be where he stayed. The Chef walks around, looking into the bright sky. His arms are behind is back and he seems very focused. “Spring Fling will be a big night for us, a very big night. You will have a chance to win or retain the Cruiserweight title; Reeco Suave has a chance to win the Rising Star title, Golden Game could retain or win the tag titles, Frisco has a chance to be named ‘The Legend’ in the ‘Battle of the Legends’ match, and I shall have a chance to punish the meddling Alan Steele! Yes, it shall be a night the International Incident shall taste glory!” Anton listens attentively to the Chef’s words, and realizes, he must win on Turmoil and Spring Fling to show his worth to the other International Incident members. He would make sure he would rise to the top and defy all odds, he hasn’t been on the top of his game lately, but for Spring Fling, he would be right back up there, and would keep his Cruiserweight title! So much to contemplate, such little time.
