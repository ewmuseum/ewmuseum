Golden Heart Chronicles: GOOAALL~!!!! (Chapter 4 Part 1)

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/02 Golden Heart Chronicles Chapter 4 Part 1: The first two chapters of the Golden Heart chronicles were the insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we were able to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The first two chapters in the Golden Heart Chronicles are where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. In chapter three and it’s following parts will focus on Juan and his relationship with being a fighter. He puts other things behind him and works on becoming a determined fighter. Right now in Chapter Four e step away from the usual course, and show Juan Borro is on of the proudest matches he may ever take part of in his entire life, The PTC International Match. Read and Enjoy, hopefully this will fulfill you’re thoughts on how Juan Borro really is. Time: 1:36 P.M Location: Lisboa, Portugal Scene: Film Set (GGGGGGGGGGGGOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLL~!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!) The way things turn out in life are sometimes a mystery. The only way you can do anything about these mysteries is by preparing for them. Wrestling is one of those mysteries. While you’re in that ring, absolutely anything could pretty much happen to you. You could do a move wrong and break something, or you could just underestimate your opponent in get crushed. Training sometimes works, but the real way to accomplish you’re (GOAL~!) in the ring, is through your mind. What better way to believe in yourself, by reminding yourself of how you’re going to accomplish your (GOAL~!). You see this (GOAL~!) of Juan’s isn’t like the others though. This (GOAL~!) is much more important because it’s for his country. Juan feels what better way to prepare for his match and representation of his country then holding a good old festival, where Juan is the centerpiece. That’s right the Golden party maker is making a comeback for this one. Juan has invited all Portuguese citizens to attend, as he will show everyone around the world, why Portugal is the best. The scene opens with Juan making final adjustments with the many people working and putting together the ornaments and decorations for his big bash. He’s had to make a bunch of plans getting the permission of the city and law. Once he got that he had to find a way to jam all of these people into a party extending thirty blocks. Of course it will be kind of crazy, but that’s the Portuguese, and that’s Juan’s (GOAL~!). “Hey Juan, is this all right, or should I brush it up a little bit.” One of the men who is making sign for Juan says. Juan takes a look at the golden sign with his name on it. He looks at it in different angles and finally speaks. “That’s great, now all you have to do is make it ten times the size, so people can see it all the way from China. Oh and you’ll have to do it very soon, because were losing time, capiche.” Juan says sticking his hand out like the Godfather. “Huh.” the man replies, not knowing what capiche means. “Yea, well it’s Italian, who gets those people anyway. You know the truth is they stole from us. It was us who really discover America, but that Italian explorer and those no good Spanish stole it from us. It is wasn’t for our people they’d be lost. And you know another thing…..” Juan keeps babbling on, until the man making the signs for him stops him. “Yea, whatever I’ll just be back with the bigger sign” Juan shrugs, and moves on his way making sure everything else is all right. As he keeps moving Juan notices the people making the bulletins and fliers for Juan’s big bash tired and not knowing what to do. “What’s all of this nonsense, why aren’t you slaves of mine doing any work. Oh, I know why you must be bedazzled by my good looking pictures?” Juan says as he strokes his hair and smiles. “No, that’s not it at all.” One of the female workers says responding very abruptly, causing Juan to be insulted. “Yea, we’re a little bit out of ideas. You see all we can really come up with “Come all to word wide megastar Juan Borro’s big bash. And then under that we just have your picture” Another one of the workers says being tired and out of ideas. “I’m sorry, but that makes me sound a little bit lame. I didn’t think it was possible for anything that I had taken part in to be lame or boring, but that my friend is lame and boring. Grab a pen and copy down what I have to say.” Juan says pacing cockily like he’s the greatest. The workers grab pens to take notes with as Juan begins. “How about you say, “ Everyone come to the…….worldwide megastar Juan Borro’s party”, oooh that’s good, write that down. Then under it we can put my picture.” Juan says feeling so wise. “Excuse me, but that’s practically the same thing we just said.” A woman not afraid of how Juan may respond says while giving Juan a moronic look. “Yea, to the simple mind it would, but it isn’t now is it?” Juan says trying to make like he’s smarter than the other and makes sense. “Umm…no it’s the same thing.” Another worker replies as Juan finally gets irritated and storms off, but before he does he leaves with one more things to say. “Oh yeah, well here’s another idea. How about you put directions in the back to help all of my adoring people.” Juan says while striding away confidently. While walking away Juan says “What do I even pay these people for?” until one of the workers whistles over and flips the add they already had, with directions already placed in the back. Juan notices and walks off upset. He then goes on to all of the other stations of his party making sure things are fine. It was actually six different places where Juan went before he decided to give in. 1. Juan first went over to the music department making sure they had his entire list of demands. He even went as far as to quiz the people there making sure they knew exactly what he wanted. He checked all of the sound utilities, which were already perfected, and couldn’t help but get up and do a little bit of a dance himself. 2. Secondly, Juan checked the food. He made sure that all the food was what he wanted and exactly the same brands. Of course he had to taste all of the food, which for Juan was more than just small bits, but more like practically the whole thing. From there he checked the expiration date of all the food making sure it is still fresh enough to eat. Juan was so stressed and being an annoyance to the others, that is seemed he was looking for things to be messed up. 3. Next, Juan made his way to the photographer. This was probably Juan’s pickiest field, seeing as he is so vain and must look completely gorgeous in front of the camera. Juan was no rookie about cameras though. He knew exactly what kind of lighting and lenses he wanted to catch his exotic and sex side. The one only the ladies get a preview of. 4. After that long and useless ordeal Juan drew his attention to the camera team who would be videoing all the events. He made sure to tell them that the camera must always be focused on his no matter what, twenty four hours during the whole broadcast. He made sure the camera added no weight to his extraordinary physique and also gave the cameraman a three-hour training center on things you can’t do in the presence of him. 5. Step five, was probably one of the most useless steps of all. It had to do with the car Juan would be entering in. It started off as eight vehicles for Juan to choose from, and took him two hours just to narrow it down to six. It was already the next morning, but Juan had finally found the car that was perfect for him. Only problem was it had no engine. Juan, picky as he is thought if the engineers and mechanics worked on it, they’d get grease on it, so he hired a group of men to go under the car, and carry it, with him on it, across the city. 6. Step six, was the publicity. This one was probably the only one where Juan really had to be present. Juan was supposed to meet with a bunch of channels to broadcast this event, but it had been a whole hour and no one had showed up. Finally, Juan saw two men, one with a microphone and one with a camera. Juan quickly went over to them and began speaking. “Hello men, I see you’ll be interviewing me today. And I think it’s just great how you came all the way out here to see me. Of course a lot of people would, but what can I say, my people love me and I mean how could they not. I mean have you checked this party out?” Juan says with a big smile. (The man is about to speak, but Juan interrupts him.) “I know exactly what you’re thinking. You’re wondering how hard working I must be to take the time out of my busy schedule to do this, but let me tell you, it’s nothing but a pleasure.” Juan says as the man tries to speak. (Interrupted again.) “Yes, for the audience at home incase they were living under a rock there whole lives I am Portugal’s dream child and soccer star Juan Borro. For this reason there will probably not be a single citizen of this country not here.” Juan says as his head is practically getting bigger and bigger by the seconds. (Interrupted again.) “Yes, in case you have seen me I also wrestle. I’m in PCW and I’m probably the best wrestler there. I’m one of the smallest, but I can still beat anyone they put me up against. They’ve tried to put me down, but I always just get back up again. It’s the way I am, what can I say.” (Interrupted again) “Did I mention the IPPV event in PTC I’ll be in. Well I’ll be representing my gracious land of Portugal going for the (GOAL~!) of winning it all. I most likely will too, since I’m so talented compared to my competition.” (Interrupted once more.) “And don’t forget to be here tomorrow night, when everything will be going down and my bash called the (GOAL~!) will be talked about worldwide.” Juan finally finishes speaking when the people he was talking to respond. “Umm we just came from an electronics store and were getting ready to film our documentary the Lisbon Witch Project. We’re not reporters.” One of the young men says as the two walk away. With that said Juan walks away searching for a place where he can look important, until then he’ll get ready for his (GOAL~!)
