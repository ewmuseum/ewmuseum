Date: Whenever *We open 20 minutes after the last End promo. A single digital camera held by a wandering camerawoman testing out the equipment records a Sandy sitting at a cherrywood desk covered in clutter, loose pages of paper scattered on the desk. More of the same papers litter the concrete floor. There are posters and pictures of the wrestlers Sandy was talking about covering the off-white brick walls. Apparently this is Sandy's office. The filing cabinets are disheveled, disorganized as she's recently been going through them. Sandy is scribbling something on the paper, mumbling it to herself as she writes it down. She notices the camera and turns to Belinda, the camerawoman. Her face is sweaty, she's a little pallor. She's still wearing the same blue dress, and her hair hangs out. Sandy takes offense to being videotaped and is agitated in tone.* Sandy:'What do you want?' Belinda:'I'm just testing the new digital cameras, we've still be using the old cameras but we got funded for better equipment now.' Sandy:'Well, I'm busy writing promos for Sky against his opponents so feck off!' *Belinda zooms in to the paper but Sandy's handwriting is so bad it can't be made out what's written down.* Belinda: 'So what are you doing?' *Sandy sighs, the sound and her body language showing that she's obviously annoyed by Belinda's presence. But her small size would prevent him from forcibly removing the camerawoman, so she must give Belinda (who's somewhat a dimwit) clues to get him to leave.* Sandy: 'If you must know... you know that I'm Sky's new promo director and writer, don't you?' Belinda: 'Yeah, so you're not a real manager?' Sandy: 'No, I don't have a manager's card.' Belinda:'So what are you doing?' *Sandy's getting more agitated by Belinda's presence and ignorance.* Sandy: 'Didn't I just say 'I'm busy writing promos for Sky against his opponents so feck off!' huh? Didn't I?' Belinda: 'Yeah.' *Sandy turns to Belinda and opens her palms fingers to the ground, moving her arms in and out as a way of saying, 'SO LEAVE, DUMBASS!'* Sandy: 'So?' Belinda: 'So, you write all of Sky's promos.' Sandy: *Burst of anger* 'DEAR GOD, YES! And I'm writing one now against Sky's opponents! Now would you please leave?' *Belinda just stands there. About ten seconds pass by of silence with Sandy scribbling more on the papers.* Sandy:'Why are you still here?' Belinda: 'You look like this is hard on you.' Sandy: 'YOU THINK?!' Belinda: 'What's wrong?' Sandy:'I've got to write a promo for Sky against his opponents because he obviously can't handle himself in these things.' Belinda:'Aren't you supposed to be the nice, friendly foil to The End?' *Sandy attempts to calm down.* Sandy: 'Oay, I'm sorry. It's just frustrating to trying to cut a promo the mook against his opponents. Speaking of the mook, where is Sky?' Belinda:'She's playing Tekken 4 with some of the crew, waiting for you to call them for another promo.' Sandy:'How is that even possible?' *Sandy shakes her head at the thought.* Sandy:'You know what, forget it. This little conversation we've had got my brain going. I've got my brain ready to write the best darn promo....' Belinda:'But isn't End going up against Totally Tubular Tommy, Rush Mason, Chet King, Snow, and Dante Jackson?' *Sandy seems to stop sweating and her breathing slows. She really does start to calm down.* Sandy:'Say that again.' Belinda:'But isn't End going up against Totally Tubular Tommy, Rush Mason, Chet King, Snow, and Dante Jackson?' *Sandy nods.* Sandy:'That's what I thought you said.' *Sandy gathers up all the papers and throws them in the trash, crushing them in with her foot. She takes out a book of matches and strikes them, tossing it in with the papers. Then Sandy opens up the window at the side of the room on the wall adjacent to the desk, which was out of view before, and tosses the papers out into the water below. Apparently End lives on a waterfront property now.* Belinda:'What are you doing now' Sandy: 'I don't need to stress myself coming up with promos. I could have Sky stand on-camera and pick his nose and it would beat whatever anyone else has to say.' Belinda:'But what are you doing now?' Sandy:'Are you kidding? I'm taking a nap! Now, would you do me a favor and get out?' Belinda:'Fine, I'm running out of battery power anyway.' *Sandy takes a few steps over to the mahogany leather couch on the opposite wall from the desk. She lies down across it and closes her eyes, her arms and legs crossed....* *End*
