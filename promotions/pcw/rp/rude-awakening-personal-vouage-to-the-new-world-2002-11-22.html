Rude Awakening – Personal – Vouage to the New World

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 22nd, 2002 The waved crashed and turned as the struck the port side of the boat, spinning us around and causing paranoia. One of the most dangerous times of the year on water and we decided now was the best time to leave. We rocked back and forth, until we found a soft spot in the storm, which only saved us from the brutality of the waves. The rain came down with such velocity that it broke holes into what ever was in its path. The sharp pain slide up my arm and throbbed through my entire body that was still being shelled by thousands of tiny bombs. It seemed to be every man for them self, and me being the only child on board was placed into a unique test of strength and courage. I didn’t have courage… I was nothing more then a coward… The storm came in so fast; no one had a chance to prepare. All the men coward down in the ships lower decks but I stayed on the top deck. Things were bad enough for that I didn’t need them poking jokes and taunting my thoughts with things that I already knew. So there I sat all night long, rocking back and forth with the boat, as the giant waves smashed in, drawing water onto the deck and soaking my clothing. From below the deck, sounds of voices could be heard. Singing, enjoyment in a time like this, while the serpent of the sea unleashed it’s furry on the plains of the water, adding more danger to an already dangerous voyage. It was one of those times I wish I would have stayed home and never token up this fight, or even got involved. But with my parents forcing me to leave what could I do. “Hey kid, you going to come in.” My eyes glanced over as a middle aged man pocked his head out the cabin door and looked at me across the wooden deck. He held the hatch in his hand and slowly made his way up the ladder to the main deck. He was fast to close the hatch, for water was still coming on deck, not so much, but enough to make a mess of things below. “What’s the matter with you,” he said to me as he walked up close, pulling a coat over his big bulk body and thrusting his hands into his pockets. He was known as Pallon to everyone on board, but to me he was known as a friend. One of the only guys to actually lend me a hand instead of shoving me aside when food came up, making me eat what was left of the scraps. He was kind of the father I never had in a sense he was always there even if I didn’t need him. “You really shouldn’t be out here at a time like this.” My life’s story was summed up like that. Everything was repeated to me, no one ever thought I could get anything on the first try but I guess if you’re a child they think you don’t know any better. So much for a childhood, since I have been in war most of my life. “I know, but it doesn’t really matter were I am.” “What do you mean,” he added taking a seat on one of the railings that was holding the sail up. “It’s not like they cared were or even who I was anyway. They would rather just let me die.” I think I struck a cord with him. He was very easily motivated and tried to push that onto others. He would tell me stories of his childhood days, and some actually made sense, but compared to my life so far, he has never seen the true nature of brutality. The chilling wind still brushed my face, sending tears down my cheeks and flushing my face. I tried to wipe them away, but some part of me wanted to just let it all out. I lost everything already so what was a few tears to me and to everyone else, who already thought I was a weak fool in the first place. But I had to hold back. I could just give into them now. Not after all the torture they put me through. “Come on now Vilas. There is so much more in the world to see.” I stopped and took a quickly look at him, smiling at the thought of his own adventure. He knew were he was going and that was all that mattered anyway. The dreams in his mind were starting to flourish into thoughts of a brilliant future. So much for trying to keep this problem silent. “Come on Vilas, there has to be something you want to do with your life,” he said, walking up and giving me a smack on the back, jolting the air from my lungs while I stumbled forward. “There is nothing I want in my life right now.” My life was full… Full of worry… Hope… And unity… I could bear to think of anything else besides my friends, maybe still trapped and dying back on the main land. If I could only call out and have the wind carry my message to them, just to know they were safe, so I could finally put my fears to rest. But with no messenger I was still stuck in the same rut I was before I came aboard. “Surly you don’t mean that,” he added, turning me around to face him. I just looked to the side and avoided his contact at all cost. “Surly I do. I have no dreams, no desires, and no ambitions. I just want to be left alone again and never be bothered.” He released his grip and looked at me with concern. He stepped back to his seat before and rubbed his chin. To me, he was wondering something or at least trying to come to some conclusion, but through his thick beard it was hard to tell what he was thinking in the first place. I turned away; facing the on coming wind and tide that was pushing the ship across the sea. “You can’t tell me you never had a dream or a life goal. A young child like you should have millions of things to do in life.” My options were slowly fading away, and the repeating interrogation was more of an annoyance then the slashing waves crashing onto the boat deck. I turned around and faced him again, still soak from the water. I took a deep breath and just spoke what my mind was telling me to say. “I want to represent Thailand.” He smiled, “That is better. How you going to go about this dream.” I liked from the right words and just fired them out, “I want to be a competitor. The best Thailand has ever seen.” “That’s a very interesting goal for such a young child,” he added, trying to get me to talk about my problems more and my shed some light into the situation. “Interesting or not, it’s something I strive for all my life.” PERFECTION… He had me all fired up and I was more motivated then ever. I totally forgot about Blassa and Safina, still sitting in their cells awaiting rescue, but that was not on my mind. My dreams kept me going and I couldn’t slow my self-down. My options lay before my eyes and the sight of them blinded me. “My problem has always remained the same. How do I want to be remembered?” He smiled and started to laugh. I failed to see what was so funny in all of this, considering his life was full and complete and he knew what he was here for. But for my goal, there was no need to be laughing and making jokes. The depressing mood had set back in as I stood there wondering how to be remembered. It had to be something not only Thailand would see but the world. “Maybe you should think more of what you can achieve rather then what makes you a star,” he added making me more confused then ever. I knew what needed to be done. I was a competitor…A fighter…and more importantly a child of honor…But can I make it last long enough to survive this war? What fears me the most is failing not only my friends and my country, but also myself in the process. One failed mistake and I would be done for, and there would be no turning back. I had to make things perfect. “Don’t worry so much about it kid. Once we hit Japan you have all the time in the world to think about your life,” he added standing up and heading back over to the hatch that lead down into the lower decks. He stopped before entering and looked back at me with a smile. “Your welcome to join me. I am going to check out some of the Japan competitions they have tomorrow.” I nodded and smiled back. He went down the hatch and closed it behind him, rejoining the already drunken party going on below decks. I stood in the shivering cold, rapping my arms around my body trying to keep the warm air moving inside me. An idea popped into my mind and it was the only thing I could think about. I had the plain… I would become someone great… I would be remembered… I NOW HAD MY LIFE… «««««To be continued»»»»»
