Fritz von Johansson - Part 3 - The Truth Revealed

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 14, 2003 [Darkness. It's what consumes him at this very moment. Even though he seems to be in total darkness, it feels like he's floating somewhere. It's sort of like sideways, more than upwards. It's a most bodacious feeling that is leaving a weird sensation in the pit of his stomach. It's the small feeling he gets when he rides a totally gnarly roller coaster. The lift stops and he's just standing there in pure darkness. If this is going to be a most heinous haunted house I'm going to have to kick somebody's ass, he thinks to himself. The slide on the way down wasn't that great, could have been better, but you can't ask for everything to be perfect. A spotlight suddenly appears over Tommy, highlighting his blue and yellow Armani suit. The bright light gleaming off of the yellow stripes, making Tommy look sort of golden] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: What is this? Voice: Excellence! TTT: WHA? Voice: Tommy, you have come here to change your life for the better. You have been brought here because you have reached that pivitol point in your life. You are a lucky man, and one blessed with a lot of gifts. [The voice is obviously masked, with a very deep voice. It could be anybody behind the microphone, sort of like in that totally bodacious film 'The Wizard Of Oz'. Tommy looks around him to try and search for some sign of windows, or a peephole. Yes, he does have a bright spotlight on him with darkness all around, but Tommy isn't that bright] TTT: Are you a babe? Voice: Yes. TTT: Are you hot? Voice: Yes. TTT: Wanna go play some Yahtzee. Voice: Sure. TTT: Bodacious! Voice: WAIT! You almost tricked me. I'm here to bless you as one of the Excellent. TTT: Then can we go play some Yahtzee, baby? Voice: Of course. [Fritz von Johansson doesn't exactly OWN the Swedish bikini team, but his large sums of money keep the lucious, sexy women around enough to make people believe that he does own them. Right now, though, they wish they hadn't taken Fritz up on his latest offer of entertaining some stoner guy who listens to Twisted Sister. They figured they would dance for a while, he'd eventually get bored, and then they could go shag Tommy in the guest bedroom area. Well, that wasn't the case. This stoner ends up sitting there for three hours, watching them dance like crazy underneath the warm Los Angeles sun. He keeps his dirty little hands underneath his legs the whole time, until...he snapped. He popped up like some sort of demon going for the kill and practically tackled the lovely Petra, leader of the bikini team. Now, he's passed out on top of her, and nobody can move his unconcious body from poor Petra] Petra: GET A CRANE!! LouAnn: We don't have one of those! Petra: Well, call up some strong man to get this pile of blubber off of me. His breath smells like dog food! LouAnn: Maybe we should let Mad Dawg out of his cage. Maybe he'll maul this stupid stoner off of you. Petra: GREAT IDEA!! [The other two bikini girls go over to a very large hatch and begin to open the cage] LouAnn: Ohhhh, Mad Dawg!! ATTACK! [The spotlight beats down on Tommy, making beads of sweat drip down from his brow. He can't believe that he has to listen to this disguised voice go on and on about something that he already knows about himself. Yes, Tommy is excellent, in fact, he's 'Totally Tubular' which is many, many steps above Excellence. How does Tommy come about this conclusion? Well, he's a surfer who is stuck in the 80's, that's how] Voice: You've lost a father at a very young age, and yet you struggled through life without a true male role model in your life. It may not have been the best thing in the world for a boy like you, but you have turned out quite... TTT: Sexy? Voice: Yes. TTT: Thank you. Voice: Your welcome. You have finally embraced who you truly are, and all of us that are Excellent, have come to respect that. The event that made Fritz approach you was the capture of the PCW Continental Title. You showed that you have the true warriors instinct that the brotherhood has been missing out on for SO LONG! TTT: I thought you were a babe. Voice: I am. TTT: But you said brotherhood, dude...er...dudette. Voice: Did I? TTT: Totally. Voice: Sorry. TTT: Apology accepted. Voice: You defeated all those men in one match, in one night. You proved that you were physically capable to become a part of this society. Fritz knows EXACTLY how to pick them and he was right about you. Wealthy, powerful, and built. TTT: Don't forget sexy, babe. Voice: And sexy. Tommy, you will now be blessed with the honor of being part of 'The Excellence'. If you would please stand completely still as you are blessed. [Tommy tightens up with his eyes shut and a large blue light surrounds him, and then quickly fades away] Voice: Welcome to the brotherhood, Tommy. [Tommy opens his eyes and seems disappointed] TTT: Don't I get to drink some spiked Kool-Aid? [A doorway opens and a man's figure emerges] Man: No. TTT: Who are you? Man: The voice. TTT: WHAT?!?! You said you were a babe. Man: I love you, Tommy. Come to me!!! TTT: NO!! Man: Yes. TTT: I'm not GAY!! Man: But your in a national gay society. [Tommy stops running and looks back at the man in horror] TTT: GAY SOCIETY?!?! NOOOO!!! [Tommy runs as fast as he can toward anywhere, but where he's at] [Petra can feel the slob of a man slowly being worked off of her. The angry dog, Mad Dawg, bites Harry's head and face, trying desperately to protect the woman] Petra: Bite harder!! LouAnn: BITE HIS CROTCH! Mad Dawg: GRRRRR!!! [Harry doesn't budge] [Suddenly, a screaming surfer comes running into the scene] TTT: Harry, lets go!!! [Behind Tommy is The Voice and Fritz von Johansson, leaders of a national gay, rich man society] Fritz: Come on, snuggle bunny, we had so many sexy things planned for you. TTT: GET AWAY!! [Harry wakes up] Harry: boobs.... TTT: Harry lets go. [Harry leaps up and trots behind Tommy, the dog following, biting Harry's leg. Tommy then looks at the beautiful Swedish bikini team] TTT: You...you...you...and YOU!! Come with me. Get naked...AND LETS SCREW!!! [The six of them run through Fritz's house and out to the car. The sound of the blue and yellow 2002 Mustang is heard peeling out in the driveway and then off through the distance. Fritz looks at The Voice with sadness] Fritz: Next time, you dress like a women. The Voice: Ok. [Fade to black]
