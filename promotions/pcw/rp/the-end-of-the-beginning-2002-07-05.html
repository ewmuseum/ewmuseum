The End of the Beginning

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th July 2002 We arrive at our destination like we usually do, but this time it’s not at the Asylum Gym. It’s not even at a stadium. Hell it’s not even in Mrs Stormbringer’s House of Whores. No, it’s actually at Los Angeles City Hospital, in the maternity unit. And like all hospitals it’s a cream coloured corridor with the occasional light blue trim and of course, the art prints in frames upon the walls. And it’s always art prints of artworks that you’ve never heard of. Miyagawa is sitting down on a chair in the corridor opposite a doorway marked “Ultrasound Room”. We meanwhile switch inside the room where Natalia Miyagawa is lying down on a bed with her shirt pulled up revealing her belly (don’t worry; she is still wearing a shirt). A young blond haired male doctor is setting up the machine and pulls it over beside the bed. Doctor: I presume you know all the ins and outs of this. Natalia grins and nods. Natalia: Of course. Only six weeks to go now. Not really all that long comparatively. The young doctor smirks at her response. Doctor: I hear something of an accent, where are you from? Natalia: Russia, just south of Moscow in a little town you’ve never heard of. I’ve lost most of my accent already though. My father always complains about it on the phone when we talk on the weekends. The doctor just grins as he plugs the machine into the socket on the wall and gets the sensors ready. Doctor: Is that your husband outside? Natalia: Da, yes he is. Been married now for about eight months. Doctor: Wow, you guys didn’t waste any time did you? Natalia smiles at the doctor as he gets out a tube of something. Doctor: Now this will be a little cold... are you sure you don’t want your husband in here. Natalia: Ok, bring him in... he probably didn’t see you come in here or else he would have been in here like a shot. The Doctor goes to the door and opens it and off camera you can hear a brief conversation between the doctor and Alexander Miyagawa... of the entire conversation only this one line is heard. Miyagawa: YOU’RE NOT OLD ENOUGH TO BE A DOCTOR!!! A few more seconds’ pass of a conversation, so much so that Natalia lifts herself up on her elbows to try and see what the pair are doing exactly. A couple more seconds pass and finally the Doctor leads Alexander into the room, who immediately takes a seat next to Natalia so that he’s facing the machine. Natalia looks at him curiously. Natalia: What was that about? Miyagawa (grinning): Just overcoming a minor age barrier. Doctor: He thought I was a first year medical student. Miyagawa: It was an easy enough mistake to make I guess. I’m sure you get it all time. Doctor: Only every other person or there about. It’s like a really dodgy episode of Scrubs in here sometimes. Anyway, this might be a bit cold. The doctor empties some of the contents of the tube onto Natalia’s belly and with his rubber gloved hands he spreads out the jelly like contents a little. He flicks the on switch on the machine and places the head of the sensor into the jelly on Natalia’s belly and moves it around a picture coming to life on the screen. Doctor: There we go, an easy find today... sometimes it picks up the liver or intestines instead, but that’s your baby right there. Natalia reaches out and takes Alexander’s hand in her own. Doctor: Would you like to know the sex of the child? Alexander and Natalia look at each other deeply for a second before Alexander turns to the Doctor: Alexander: After all this waiting I think we can wait for another six weeks, thank you. Natalia: This is the end of the beginning. Miyagawa smiles joyfully and grips her hand a little bit harder to show his happiness. And with that we leave the duo to this touching moment.
