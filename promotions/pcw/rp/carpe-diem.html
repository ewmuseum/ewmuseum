Carpe Diem

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Qwertyuiop We are surrounded by lies. Surrounded by hate. Surrounded by so much anguish it's unbearable. We feel bounded by hate. The world tells us this: That being alive is so beautiful, but also so horrible. I don't say this to say 'Go kill yourself' but meant to take it as a caution. Snakes hiss behind our dreams and goals, making every turn a painful drag so redundant and hopeless it's almost pointless in our mind. But don't fade away. There's no reason. You live for experience. You live for the joyous occasion of being alive. To go out and enjoy the world. To carpe diem and take life the way you want. To enjoy it and love it. Cry when you want. Cry when you need. Cry when your feelings tell you to. Don't hold it in a womb. Free the pain, and feel the emotion surrounding you. Let it wash over you. Being a human being is such a wonderful feeling. Nobody is useless. Nobody was put on Earth for no point. We all have a point to be on Earth. We all fail, we all feel bad about it, we all have some joyous occasion, a feeling of happiness. Be grateful for both and never, ever let life bring you down. You are stronger then life. I have a strong feeling and a reason to write this, because I do know people who don't take this into mind. They look at life with a cynical look at it. 'This sucks, that sucks.' Well, yeah, life sucks...at times. Even life may seem like it has no upwards climb. People look at life so badly it depresses myself even. Take into mind things we learned in Kindergarten. Play fair. Share with others. If you make then mess, clean it. Flush. Warm milk and cookies are good. Eat healthy and grow strong. Like the plants, we grow and we die. Say your sorry if you hurt someone. Don't be a baby. Be nice. If you do good, you'll get good in return. Work your hardest. Your teacher tries to help you and not be mean. Finish your plate if you want desert. Do things together I bet you may laugh at them, looking back at them now. But look at them in a non-literal way. 'Be fair' can be 'Have a equal view on every human being without being a judge of what they do'. 'Don't be a baby' could be 'We all make mistakes and feel things are unfair, but the sun still rises and goes down'. 'Like the plants, we grow and we die' is a statement that is a bit depressing to me because I am growing up and my time on Earth will be gone. But it's a fact that we have to put up with, and even though it's hard too, it's a truth. Life's only as hard as you make it. Do what you want (except for injuring others!) and, again, carpe diem. Seize the day and make your life better. Don't make it worse then what it could be. I talked to me father today in the hospital. It was an intense feeling, adding the fact he cursed every fews seconds. He told me to make Makonie pay. I'm going to try. He's a judgemental man, Makonie is, and my father did tell me he needed to pay for judgemental views. Even though I am facing Skitzo this week for the Extreme title, beating Skitzo is like showing Makonie different races can triumph. That's what I say about the deal. It's what my father said. He told me skin is protection: Not something to judge somebody on. We have skin to protect us. Everything a man without skin touched would seem to hurt him. I learned many things in Kindergarten I still use. 'Do things together' is a big one. We all united and I try to direct many things to unity, because the power to unite is stronger then the power to divide. Go into the world with a friend who can stand up for you. It's also nice to know somebody's by your side. My hand is starting to hurt, so that's what I have to say for tonight. Journal Entry for March 23rd, 2003
