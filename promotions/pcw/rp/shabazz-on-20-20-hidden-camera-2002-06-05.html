Shabazz On 20/20 Hidden Camera

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/02 Date: June 3rd, 2002. Location: Downtown Atlanta, Georgia.Time: 2:20 PM.Weather: HOT! 98 degrees in fact. When we last saw our Freedom Fighter, our Civil Rights Hero, Jordan X Shabazz, he was dressed in unusual attire and wearing make up. No, he has not become a flaming homosexual. Instead, Jordan was dressed as a white man for a day, by a psychologist, it was his idea, he thought that it would allow Jordan to see what white people went through everyday. And boy did Jordan see. Free food. That’s right, if you didn’t know before, then it’s probably already happened to you. For the simple fact that he looked white, Jordan was given some free food at a convenience store in the mall. Next, he was sitting by an old lady, and she started making small talk with him, for no apparent reason! Can you believe this? When Jordan was dressed normally for him, and spoke Ebonics, he was treated like a common street thug, even though he was an up and coming Civil Rights Activist and professional wrestler. Then, the idea popped into Jordan’s head. He knew it all along; white people were two-faced backstabbing bastards who couldn’t be trusted [Unless of course, they were in the Malice Crew.]. The idea was that Jordan would have a hidden camera placed on his body, and he could show a day in the life of a regular black man, and then a day in the life of a regular white man. It was genius. Now the only thing he had to do was get some major networks to sponsor him. Jordan called everywhere he could, Dateline NBC, 60 Minutes, the FOX channel, and he had to get on somewhere. But for some reason, some of the bigger companies weren’t biting. Jordan was almost ready to quit altogether, when he finally tried the popular Friday night television show, 20/20. They bit, hook, line, and sinker. They were prepared to have hidden camera crews follow Jordan around, and they were prepared to have Jordan wear a hidden camera. Which brings us to today. Jordan X Shabazz is now dressed normally, back to normal in skin tone and everything. Jordan wore regular clothing for him, baggy blue Phat Farm jeans, some brown Phat Farm boots, and a large Platinum Fubu t-shirt. He also decided to wear his doo rag and Toronto Maple Leafs baseball cap, for the simple fact that it gave the impression to old white people that he was someway affiliated with a gang, that would look good on camera, he thought. The scene opens with Jordan having the final touches being put on him. The microphone is on correct, now he is getting a pep talk from one of the shows producers. He appears to be a veteran in the business because of the simple fact that he looks really old. His hair is uncombed and his clothing is in a state of disarray. He stands before Jordan, sweating in the hot Georgia heat. Producer: “Jordan, I’m telling you, try and act like a jackass, try and provoke these people to be rude and mean to you. The worse they treat you, the more the controversy, and the more the controversy, the more the ratings for 20/20. You understand, kid?” Jordan nods slightly towards the producer, as he gets ready to start his journey. He has been told that he will be driving a brand new, never driven before, Cadillac Deville, and he’s going to go the ‘Georgia Springs’ mall. There he is supposed to enter several high priced clothing departments and act as suspicious as possible without actually giving away the fact that the whole thing is a charade. Jordan gets in the car, and his journey begins. ***************************************** 1 hour and thirty minutes later. Jordan X Shabazz, cameras and all, has finally arrived at the ‘Georgia Springs’ mall. The mall parking lot was packed as usual with nice looking vehicles, which gives you the impression on what kind of people frequent the place. Jordan is familiar with the area, at one time while he was in the NTICW they passed through the area and this is where he went shopping with a couple of his friends. He gets out of the vehicle and ruffles his clothing. He immediately walks towards the entrance, and when he enters the place, he is surprised to see the amount of wrestling paraphernalia in the area. It was never like this before, but I guess with the resurgence of the PCW and the emergence of the OSW, the mall has decided to try and bring some of the rich kids into the mall by promoting professional wrestling. Jordan looks around and notices the lack of African Americans in the place, and is it any wonder? Most of the rich black people live in California and New York. The few that live in Georgia would definitely not do their shopping at such a posh and high class place. It’s too uptight for them. Jordan begins walking in search of a store that sells ‘Gucci’ type clothing. He finds one finally, and it’s appropriately named ‘Gucci.’ Heh. The Civil Rights Extremist enters the store and immediately notices the fact that the store is full of women. The founder and owner of N.I.G.G.A begins searching through the racks of clothing looking for some clothes… or at least that’s what he wanted the security and workers of this store to think. Before long, he had two security guards watching him closely. One of the store saleswomen comes up to Jordan and taps him on the shoulder, he turns around and looks at her. “Yes?” “Are you lost or something?” asks the saleswoman to Jordan, trying her best to sound not rude. “Bitch, does it look like I’m lost?” Jordan replies angrily. The woman is completely taken aback at the language that Jordan has decided to use. But honestly, could you blame Jordan? He had been minding his own business, sure he wasn’t exactly in the store for reasons that he should be proud of, but God damn it the lady was wrong in asking him if he was lost. “Well, I apologize if I offended you sir, I really didn’t mean to. We just don’t get your type coming around these…” “NO THE FUCK YOU DIDN’T!” Jordan screams. Suddenly, the security hops in front of Jordan and they separate him from the woman. “IT’S RACISM! OPPRESSION OF THE BLACK MAN, BEING HELD DOWN BY THE WHITE MAN FOR THEIR OWN PERSONAL GAIN! THIS ISN’T THE FIRST TIME! YOU DID IT TO OJ! YOU DID IT TO OJ!” Jordan is throw violently to the ground by the security guards. He attempts feebly to break away from the guards. Jordan is placed in cuffs and risen back to his feet. Both of the security guards are large and have to be at least 300 and 6 foot each. One of them looked Jordan in the face. “Get out of this store,” the guard said. “Well it’ll be kinda hard since you’ve got me cuffed up like a common criminal. THAT’S ANOTHER REASON I DON’T LIKE Y’ALL WHITE PEOPLE! ALWAYS CUFFING A NI**A FOR NO APPARENT REASON!” Jordan screams. BAM! BAM! BAM! Jordan is hit hard in the face three times. “I don’t want to hear any of that preaching, Big Darkeylicious. Now get out!” the guard said while uncuffing Jordan and shoving him towards the door. Though Jordan didn’t physically assault the man, he did it verbally, did it deserve the beating that he got? Definitely not. But is that security guard going to get fined or fired for it? Definitely not. Welcome to the life of a black man. The scene fades with Jordan stumbling towards the exit of the mall, looking to get back to his vehicle where he will check if his camera caught the whole thing.
