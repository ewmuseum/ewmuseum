The Weekend - Part 2 - Caught On Tape

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 3, 2002 [Camera's are far out, man. A video camera was used to capture Los Angeles police officers beating Rodney King, to watch airplanes smash into the World Trade Center, and for naked women. Let's face it...it all comes down to naked boobies, man. The Internet if filled with some much useful information that it can expand our minds, but what does the young man do? He's checking out the latest porn site for some decent content. 'Heavy Metal' Harry has been addicted to becoming the next highly esteemed director of pornography. Forget Hugh Hefner, it'll be Harry that rolls out of the mouth's of the people once he is through. New York City is far out in a major way and Harry needs to get some cool footage of the city, it's a must do. His mind may be stoned and on another dimension most of the time, but Harry does think about things more than people know he does. Usually he's thinking about far out ways to make bongs, but right behind that he's always thinking about what his next moves should be in the ring. After all, his brother, The Tubular One, does need to be backed up occassionally. Harry knows the seriousness of the upcoming Pay Per View match against Redemption Song. There is a bitchin' score to be settled with those dudes, but defeating them takes them a step closer to finally wearing the tag team gold around their waists. Harry knows that he has to be on top of his game the entire night if the tag titles are what they are going to get. He has to be far out, man] [It's night time in New York, but with all the lights and life it doesn't seem like it's night time. Harry walks through Times Square holding his Sony Digital camera, taking shots of anything and everything that he can. With the far out editing software on his computer he can put some bad ass Twisted Sister tunes to the scenes of New York City. That won't be sold on E-Bay, it'll be placed in a personal collection...just like some of the porn. Harry catches glimpses of where they shoot TRL for MTV, he then thinks he'll edit in some bitchin' explosions to make it look as if it were being blown to pieces. Then Harry begins thinking that maybe Britney Spears will be in New York, and he can get a shot at her. Now that would be far out] Woman's Voice: WOOOO!! [Harry is immediately drawn to the loud Wooing of a mysterious woman. He zooms in the the digital camera to spy someone quite familiar. Harry knows this woman, he knows that he's seen the chick before. It could have been television, the plane ride, or maybe even on the streets earlier in the day. The possibilities are endless. It appears that she has two very large and muscular men with her. By the smiles on their tan squared jawed faces, they are going to get some far out lovin' this night. That's when it clicks into Harry's mind...no, not knowing her, but using the camera for far out footage. He'll follow them back to the room and get some bitchin' footage to sell on E-Bay. He'll call it 'A Harry New York Adventure'. It has far out written all over it!] Mysterious Woman: I think that we should go back to my room for this. Trust me, I know my roommate isn't going to be around. I told him what his place was in the relationship. [Both men look shocked that her roommate is a guy] Guy # 1: Your roommate is a guy?! Is it your husband or something? Guy # 2: Yeah. Mysterious Woman: No, it's not my husband! Not even close to that. He's just some guy that I'm staying with until I get what I need to get...then I'll just move on to something bigger and better. Guy # 1: I think that you already have. [He smiles slyly to the very attractive woman] Guy # 2: Yeah. [Harry keeps a safe distance behind the threesome, trying to get it all on tape as a precursor for the 'big bang'. Now the two men have their arms around her, just making themselves feel comfortable around the lady. This is far out beginning footage, man] Guy # 1: Who is this guy anyway? He must be some kind of putz to be staying in a room with you and not doing anything about it. Guy # 2: Yeah. Mysterious Woman: He's a professional wrestler, and he's stupid. Besides I won't let him doing anything to me, but that still does make him a putz. [Harry's eyes light up, wondering what other professional wrestlers besides he and Tommy are here? Maybe it's the girlfriend of Semore Smoke or something. That moron!! His girlfriend is cheating on him, man! Far OUT!! Harry is getting this momentous occassion on tape, people are going to pay huge money for this on E-Bay!] Mysterious Woman: Tommy, isn't the brightest peg on the Lite Brite, but he is a pretty damn good wrestler. For a guy like that to get to the Semi-Finals of the GTT is a large feat. I think I made a wise decision dropping those losers I was with before. [Harry has an arguement going on in his head at the moment and blocks out the two guys letting their opinions be heard. This chick apparently is talking about some guy named Tommy being in GTT...is Inphino Blitz really named Tommy too?? That would be FAR OUT!! Harry decides that he's going to let Tommy know about this amazing discovery, but he can just show it to him on tape!!] Mysterious Woman: Yeah, his brother is such a pain in the ass too. He brought this dumb camera with him for the trip. Tommy tells me he's been using it to record people having sex and then sells it on E-Bay...some hobby to have. All I say is that this Harry punk better shape up in time for the Pay Per View. [Far Out! Inphino is really named Tommy and he has a brother named Harry too! This is just too bitchin' to describe, man. It seems that they've come to their destination too. Hey! It's that far out hotel that they are staying at too! This is really weird. This chick knows that Inphino is really named Tommy, and that he also has a brother named Harry, and is staying at the same hotel as them!! In New York! Plus, it's all on video tape, man! Far Out!] Guy # 1: Your gonna love it, babe. Guy # 2: Yeah. Mysterious Woman: You know I am. [The three get on the gold plated elevators to make their way up to the room. Harry watches with video camera in hand and notices the numbers at the top count all the way up to the number '15'. Hey! That's the floor that Harry and Tommy are staying on...this just keeps getting weirder and weirder] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE! [Harry whips around, seeing his brother decked out in his workout clothes. White PCW T-shirt, blue biking shorts, and Nike Tennis Shoes. Tommy is covered in sweat as he takes a quick gulp of the bottled water in his hand. Talk about a major workout session he must have had with Misty...wait a minute.....] TTT: I hope that you got some bodacious shots of New York City, I'm going to have to check them out tonight. Then tomorrow afternoon we'll hang out and visit it all again! It'll be a most gnarly time. Let off some of that bogus stress that we've been feeling lately. Harry: Man... [Harry hits the playback, and the video plays back on the LCD screen. Tommy looks over Harry's shoulder to see Misty with those two dudes. She wears the largest smile on her face, ready to take both of these compadres on. Tommy's face begins to sag into sadness as he looks up to the number 15 on the elevator. Harry shakes his head, still wondering what this all has to do with Inphino Blitz. It doesn't make any sense, man] TTT: This is totally bogus, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: She's doing to us, what she did to Redemption Song. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: We're going to have to forget having a valet, dude. Harry: No, man. TTT: Dude...we have to get rid of this bogus bitch if we're gonna win. So, lets do it. [Harry shrugs...just wanting more footage]
