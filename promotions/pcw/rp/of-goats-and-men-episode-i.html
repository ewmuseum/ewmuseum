Of Goats and Men: Episode I

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-8-02 OOC-this may not be up to par for me but i gotta knock off the old ring rust anyhoo...i hope you kids enjoy this feeble attempt at humor... Sometimes the world can be so perfect it makes you forget about all the bad things going on. Take for example a certain old tree lined dusty road. In the center of the road, two trees bend over so that they’re touching, forming a sort of bridge. The sun is shining down, however you wouldn’t know it through the thickness of the trees. It’s a perfect picture of peacefulness. It seems like if you could make any one spot in the world never change-always stay the same-this would be that place. The kind of place you would keep in a little glass box away from the global warming, the terrorist attacks, the sorrows of breaking hearts. However, nothing is perfect and soon, a large blue 1930’s pickup truck comes barreling down the way, creating a beautifully chaotic whirlwind of dust and shattered dreams. Abruptly, the truck comes to a screeching halt and making the man riding in the back smack his face against the back window. Inside the truck sit two greasy men in white wool shirts and denim overalls, also covered in black grease spots. After hearing the thump, both men turn around to see what had happened. Bill: Whatcha reckon that was? Denny: Why Bill I don’t rightly know. The two southern men sit in silence for a good three minutes as cries of agony arise from the bed of the truck. Voice: Sweet Jefferson Davis that hurt! Denny: Maybe it was a deer! Bill: You think it coulda been? The two men step out of the truck and go around to the back, creeping quietly so as not to disturb their trophy. The whole time glorious strings of vulgarity swirl up like whirlwinds from the back of the truck. Denny grabs a stick off the ground as Bill gets ready to let down the tailgate. Bill: I’ll bet it’s at least ten points! Denny: Yeah, twenty at the most! Denny poises the stick to attack as Bill lowers the tailgate to reveal.... Bill: Aww man, it ain’t even got no antlers yet. Denny: You dumb bastard it ain’t a deer. That’s a goat! Bill: The hell it is! Suddenly the thing in the back of the truck gets up and wipes a speck of blood off it’s face. Dustin: I can assure you I’m not a goat or a deer, although I once played a goat in the television mini-series Not Without My Goat: The Amy Fisher Story. If you want you can probably still catch it on Lifetime. It’s all about revealing your inner woman and stuff. You know, when they approached me about doing this movie, I told them that I didn’t have an inner woman, but I was wrong. You guys probably have inner women too, want me to try and reveal it for you? Denny: I’ll be damned! That goat talked to me! Bill: Let’s run! Dustin: Don’t run. I’m not a goat, I swear. Go take a look in your rearview mirror at yourselves. Bill and Denny both look at each other and walk to the rearview mirror. They slowly peak into the glass at their reflection... Bill: Oh no! Denny, we done got turned into goats! Denny: Now wait a minute Bill, if we was goats, could we drive a truck? Bill thinks about it for a second. Bill: Now that’s a noggin’ scratcher if I ever heard one. I saw this one goat at a circus that flew an airplane... Dustin: Wait a minute guys. You saw a goat that flew a plane? Bill: Yes I did. His name was Sparkles and he was the most decorated pilot in World War I. Dustin: I’d like to see this goat. Do you know where we can find him? Denny: If I ain’t mistaken, he lives on old farmer Dough’s ranch. We can take you there if you’d like. Dustin: That would make my year! Dustin hops out of the back of the truck and throws his arm around Denny. The three begin to walk down the dusty road. They get about half a mile when Bill suddenly stops in his tracks. Bill: Now wait just a dad gummed minute! You never did tell us why you was in the back of my truck! Denny: How do we know you ain’t gonna rape us? Dustin: Well first off, I wouldn’t rape you mainly because your hideously unattractive. The two men nod. Denny: He’s got a point there. Dustin:...and second, I’m not Reeco Suave so raping dudes isn’t exactly my fortè. Bill: Reeco Suave? The original Latin Explosion? Bill and Denny begin singing that terrifically annoying song whilst dancing erotically. Dustin: No guys, not the song, the wrestler. The two men stare blankly Dustin: In PCW? Bill and Denny: Oh! Dustin: You see, I’m a wrestler except I kinda got set on fire and then died but my best friend in the world Chris Chase, PCW Champion, saved my life and now I get to fight Reeco Suave at Turmoil on Wednesday. I know it’s a complete waste of time but I gotta start somewhere. You guys want to know who is Public Enemy Numero Uno in the Book of Downey? Denny: Milton Berle? Dustin: Exactly...I hate that old bastard. But right next to him is Jason Snow. He thinks he can waltz back into PCW and have my best buddy to himself? Well I’m not going to go down without a fight and Snow can bet his bottom dollar on that. Without Chris I don’t know what I’d do. I mean, I’m a simple moron, born to be a sidekick, but I can’t very well be a sidekick to myself can I? Well guys, after I destroy Reeco on Turmoil I’m gonna set my big moronic cross hairs on Jason Snow. Denny: So you gonna wrassle eh? Dustin: No, I’m going to dominate silly. My days as a mere speck on the PCW map are over. I’m Dustin Downey dammit! Nobody is safe from my awesome wrath! Just then Dustin trips over a twig and falls face first in the dust. Denny helps him up and wipes the dust off Dustin’s signature bathrobe. Bill: You were saying something about “awesome wrath”? Dustin: Never mind that. I must see this goat. If he’s as great as you say he is, he’ll help me derive a plan to get Snow and beat Reeco! Besides, if he can’t help me, at least I get to see a goat that flew a fighter plane in the Great War. So can we get on with this magical journey? Denny: I suppose so. I ain’t got nothing to do today no how. Dustin: What about you Billy boy? Bill: I don’t know Mr. Downey. My wife’s givin’ birth to my seventeenth child tomorrow.... Denny: I thought you guys was on your twentieth. Bill: We’re gettin’ there. Shucks, I suppose I can go for seeing that goat again. So looks like we got ourselves an adventure! The three men begin walking again down the road, singing to themselves as the scene fades to black. The scene then comes back up on the three men sitting around a campfire in the middle of a vast field. Crickets are chirping, frogs are croaking and Downey is prattling on about some odd thing... Dustin: But these weren’t regular midgets, these were evil midgets. They stole my foosball table and ate all my Rice Crispy treats. So I was chasing these guys down an alley when Chris said “Dustin, you’re dreaming again” and I woke up. Denny: Dustin, tell me more about being a wrestler would you? Dustin: Sure thing Denny. Well like I said I must fight myself a jobber on Turmoil. Reeco Suave. Who is this guy? I’ve fought some of the greats. By “some” I mean Miyagawa, Ivan Stanislav, and Da Westsidaz. I even got the hell beat out of me by Meanstreak. What has Reeco done other than job his brains out? Suddenly, Cal Seaver pops out of nowhere.... Cal: Need I remind you that YOU jobbed your brains out to all the aforementioned people? Dustin: Go away Cal. Cal hangs his head and leaves.... Dustin: So I wasn’t the best, but I’m better than a million Reeco Suaves. I won’t even break a sweat as I break every bone in Reeco’s body. This man is one of the reasons PCW is in a slump. I’ll be doing myself and PCW a huge favor by disposing of a dead weight like Reeco. I may be dumb but I know enough to know that this man is doing PCW no good at all. Now guys let’s get some sleep before we continue our journey to see the goat. Dustin falls asleep to the sound of crickets. Once Dustin falls asleep, Denny and Bill walk a little way from the campfire. They begin talking, however they don’t sound near as hick like.... Denny: By Jove, I thought he’d never succumb to sleeps enticing lullaby. Bill: I second that my incredibly British evil spy brother! Now, let’s get down to business, when Downey wakes up we need to have him in Toledo. So here’s the deal, you put him in the trunk and I’ll watch. Deal? Denny: I suppose...now remind me again for no obvious reason why we’re doing this? Bill: Because we plan to figure out what makes him so stupid and secrete the enzyme so that we may make the whole world STUPID! Bill and Denny laugh evilly as the scene fades to bright pink....why bright pink? Why not? TO BE CONTINUED....
