A Song at a Price... a small one mind you...

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st June Its time...Its time...Hell, its time for the only BAND... the only 'Boy Band' in the history of PCW... and the history of the WT... at least, to my very limited knowledge. Its time for the boys that you love to cheer for... its time for them to do something worthwhile... not only to the community that cheer for them with every match... but for the little 13yr old Girls that scream their girly heads off at every one of the bands concerts. The duo.. had been defeated by Xavier Kannon at PCW Fusion this past week... the world of the HaRdCoRe not being a location for the squeaky clean image that the boys of PoP need to uphold. Despite the knowledge that goes around with losers... 'The Pretty Cool Guys' were nothing but losers in the eyes of the fans that cared... all three of these people still cheered for the boys, even in their greatest time of need. Why? Why you wonder... looking to the screen with all the intensity that deserves to be expressed on the face of Nick Carter when he realizes the world found out about his conviction. The answer to this question is simple... they cheer for the boys because they are the only people that make music worth listenning. In this episode of the series that makes Pokémon seem alot more interesting... we will join our anti-heros at the scene of a concert... a concert that will not only contain the egotisical know-how of Rich Sherwin... but the expressive vocals that come with Ryan Moore and his pre-puberty like voice. Tonight... there will not be the excitements that came with the Dragonball Z intro music... instead, in the words of every man in the world... let the curtain rise... and the show... begin... | - ^ - ^ - | Chapter 3 : How much is that Title... in the window? Woof-Woof?!Installment I : Songs of Praise... Songs of Wisdom.. There comes the time in everyones life where you have to upstage the man... the men... the community who believe they are alot bigger than yourself. The same time comes when you need to impress a couple of chicks that may be waiting for you in the audience. Much like the two known as the 'Price Brothers'... today, PCG will attempt to show they are not just the worlds greatest Tag Team in the ring... no, they intend to show all the doubters that they are by far... by far... all the way to Australia!, that they are the most dominant out-ring athletes in the business today. So what... the competition may have the concepts of unpredicable events to their awesome arsenal of... something... and something a little better than the first something. Our scene firstly begins to open on the confinements on the stage in one of the many malls across America. We are introduced to the picture by the visual of a big stage... and by the word 'Big'... I can promise the world that this term isn't being used sparingly. As the visual begins to move around to the left of the scene... we get the impression and the visual of fans waiting for the next hot act to make their way through the curtains. Thousands of screaming... Girls... come into view, their arms waving in the air... their collective voices sounding worse than nails being dragged down a disgustingly screachy chalkboard. The lights around the immediate area began to die down somewhat... exposing the stage for what it really was.. something cheap, cheerful... and only able to accomodate the worst of the stars in the game. As the lights hit the wooden base of the stage floor, two circles form on the floor... colored in a beautiful shade of blue. The fans in attendance continue to cheer their idols... almost begging for them to come upon the stage. We don't know whats going to happen... if anything is even going to happen at all... for now, the trauma of anything happening for these fans is too much for them to take in. 'We want... Rich!', scream half of the crowd, trying to beckon out at least one half of the possible... future, PCW World Tag Team Champions. There remains the tranquil sound of nothingness as the the stage remains even more vacant than the beginning of the Promo. The girls all look at one another... adjusting their makeup and things to look good for anyone that may stick their head out from behind the curtain.... even if it is that tackle showing fiend, Nick Carter. A few more seconds of nothingness comes upon the stage... and then, the other half of the all female crowd shout out, 'We want... Ryan!'. The girlies looked at the stage... expecting to see something of a character step out from behind the darkness of the stage... and into the beams of light that were being projected from the stage... however, there was no such luck. As the milliseconds, seconds... and eventually minutes became to pass... the crowd started to get a little restless towards the show. The truth was beginning to settle in... there was going to be no appearence from the group know as PCG... for what reason, nobody really understood... did Sherwin and Moore recieve vicious beatings in the time of and after PCW Fusion? Had Rich Sherwin been the unfortunate victim of a vicious attack from somebody that was losing in the sales charts to PCG? Time seemed to pass like the nothing ever known before... and then... it happened... 'Baby... did I ever... let you know?' was the first voice to come out from the stage. A light portion of Piano solo was playing in the background... soothing the ears of the fans that had made the journey here. The girls turned to look at the stage... Rich Sherwin walking into one of the two spotlights with a microphone in his hand... now was the time... for an emotional performance... '... that when... the Price came to me... I opened me eyes to see... the stars... and the sky..', Sherwin continued... captivating the emotions of all the girls watching the show. They stood still in their spots as Sherwin walked over to the edge of the stage, staring across at his underage female admirers. In a few seconds of action, Sherwin flashes the crowd a smile... sending them all into sexual obsession. He walks across the stagefront as the music continues to play within the background. Moments later however, a new voice is heard from the confinements of the back... '... it was then... that I saw... the memories in your heart... oh could we beat... these boys?', he called out in his wonderfully artistic singing voice. The girls among the crowd went crazy again, screaming out at the top of their lungs for the song to continue. Ryan Moore could only look on to the females and flash them a smile before walking out in the same way that Sherwin had done, stopped at the end of the stage and looking down into the crowd of fans. '... The answer.. that you ask for... is nor yes... or no... but the fact that I love you... will surely deserve you... and those boys... will face our wrath..', he sang before ending on the note and returning to the further part of the stage with Sherwin. Both men began to dance around on the spot, jiv-ing to the lyrics that were being blasted out around them... alongside the musical content and the screams of female fans. The two boys pounded out a few stomps to the left... before juking it on the right and stepping forward a few times. They both looked at one another before coming out with what was supposed to be the chorus... 'Yes... its true... the Price isn't right for you... is it for me? Can you not count to three... the girls on the sides all like my rhymes... the girls with Michelle all give me dimes.. what am I gonna do.. my baby, baby... FREEZE..', they both chanted before coming to a complete pause on stage in mid-movement. The women continue to scream like crazy in the crowd... looking up to both Sherwin and Moore as the hits continue to flow like water from a tap. Sherwin took the step forward now and continued to bounce along on the stage before looking deep into the crowd and continuing to pump out the lyrical content... 'The lights go out... the Price is worth nowt... so what... the hell, do ya gotta do? Ya gotta take on step to the left and bounce it straight on-to the right... ya gotta make the hands connect and scream the girls to call a fight.', he said before walking back onto the backdrop, allowing Ryan Moore to come forward and end the song with his final line... well, maybe not the final line of the song... but like stated, his final line... 'Fight they scream... fight is me... the PCG will take it down here...', Moore called out to the fans before stopping in position and acting as though the musical content had finally come to a halt. Upon realizing this little data piece, the music in the immediate area stopped coming from the speakers... and the girls in the crowd all screamed at shouted at the two boys... calling their names and throwing them pieces of paper with phone numbers issued on. The camera moved forth to see Ryan Moore and Rich Sherwin walking down some wooden steps into the 'Backstage' area of the set... 'Oh My God..', Ryan Moore began in his usually frantic voice. He stopped on the spot and breathed in slowly before even considering to continue the line. As soon as he came through, he carried on with, '... that show was so cool... did you check out how hot we were tonight... and The Price Brothers call themselves hot?' Rich remained in the same spot for a few moments, looking around the immediate area before shaking his head from side to side in respectable fashion. The so-called 'Intellectual' of the band looked over to his partner before saying, '... They can spend all the time in the world... jumping off cliffs... diving infront of cars... it really doesn't matter... nobody... and by God, I mean nobody... is going to ever come close to picking up the amount of chicks that we got today.' Ryan nodded his head somewhat while starting to walk again... this fact being very disturbing due to the fact that both Sherwin and Moore are pretty much out of the age range of their fans... however, maybe they're just trying to show the Price Brothers who can pull in the biggest crowd... ever if both the boys are only able to pull in the chicks that were making their trips through puberty. The two continued to walk through the back, the camera moving just infront of them to keep up with everything that was being said on the matter. 'So... you think you we can defeat Chet and his brother on Turmoil next week?', Ryan asked with a hint of discomfort. He didn't sound too confindent about going against this team... especially after they were able to defeat... Politically Incorrect...' Sherwin looked at the ceiling before looking down at the floor and letting out a large breath of shock. The PoP Icon turned and looked at Ryan, placing his left hand on Moores right shoulder. Rich lets out a long sigh before starting to say a few words about the Tag Team contest on Turmoil... 'Ryan Moore... this week, we are going to do the same thing that we did to those other people... just one short week ago... the time has come for us to show the world why Kakarot came down and taught us his Fusion Technique... and me a few other moves. You should not fear the duo that will go up to us... for they are the Price Brothers... and in the words of all that is harsh and Jordan Crazy Crunk... 'The Price just ain't right'... not by a long shot...' Ryan nods his head somewhat and the 'Boy Band' duo begin to walk off into the confinements of the backstage area deeper in the backstage area (O_o;;). As Sherwin and Moore dissappear into the scene of darkness... we are left to wonder what PCG could do next about their opponents... one lame song to the fans isn't going to get them through to the next round... so what the hell could?!
