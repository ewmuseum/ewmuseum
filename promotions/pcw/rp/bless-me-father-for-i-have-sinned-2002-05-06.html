Bless Me Father for I Have Sinned......

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/02 Tales of the Golden Toe: The Golden Heart as passed and the Golden Toe has come back again. The Golden Toe is the old Juan Borro, the fun loving foreign soccer player who did his all to help others. Through the tales of the Golden Toe Juan gives us insight into present experiences with his life and duties and encounters with people. The Golden Toe has seen his calling, and that calling is to do good be good, and be a power for good. No force will stop Juan’s GOAL~! Time: 3: 30 P.M.Location: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Church Confessional “Bless me Father for I have sinned.” The last few months Juan has been all around the place. He’s been everywhere from a loving relationship in New York to New formed international alliance to being accused of murder to seeing friends die to being close with his father once again to breaking up with his girlfriend to trying politics and finally to leaving it all to pursue the two things he loves most, wrestling and soccer. You can say these times were times where Juan was in need of guidance or someone to talk to. When Juan really needed these things he didn’t have time for them. Juan has had so many things to do and hardships that he hasn’t had time for the one thing he grew up with always by his side and that’s GOD! Not a fake God or a person that calls themselves God because they think if makes them sound more powerful or like a higher being, but the real one and only God, The father of Jesus of Nazareth. You see Juan is a devout Roman Catholic. He’s been going to church once or twice a week since the day he was born practically. Juan finally thought today would be the day he came back to God. It’s been about a five-month drought, but Juan is back and ready to have God with him every step of the way. Juan was they’re for the morning sermon and the Eucharist, but now he’s ready to let out all in the confessional. What sparked this change of heart and why Juan wants to tell all? Well Juan wants to be cleansed. He doesn’t want anything on his chest for the PPV. He wants to be able to go out their and perform incredibly, not having anything else in his head. Of course the church wouldn’t really approve of fighting, but Juan thinks of it more as being a warrior that just being someone who gets into fights. The day is here, and the time is now for Juan to take on a new lead. Not a lead based on national heroism, not a lead based on politics, not even a lead based on women and having a good time, but a lead based purely on GOD! The scene opens with Juan kneeling in front of the altar and making the sign of the cross. Juan looks up at Jesus on the cross. He thinks of all of the suffering Jesus must of went through for all of us. Juan wishes on Sunday he could push through the pain he was in to help progress. Juan gets up from kneeling and makes his way to the confessional. As he waits in line he can almost hear the whispers of the person in front of him. When the person leaves they look ashamed leaving their head low. Juan wishes he would never come out of anything with such an ashamed look. As Juan enters the confessional he kneels down and opens the window. The room is dark and all Juan could see is one little bright light. Through this light Juan can only see his feet, but it’s enough for him. Juan then begins the process. Juan: Bless me father for I have sinned it has been about five months since my last confession. Priest: Tell me of them my son? Juan: Well I have lied, I have used violence for personal satisfaction, I have left hearts broken, and ended friendships. I’m not an angry mad, but I have lashed out in anger. I have also committed their innocent sins, which escape my mind now. But father may I ask you a question? Priest: Yes! You may my son. Juan: Is it wrong to fight others if you do it as part of your job? Priest: I’m afraid I don’t really understand what you’re getting at? You have a job to hurt people? Juan: No you see it’s not like I’m in the mob or anything I’m a professional wrestler. I work for a corporation named Primetime Championship Wrestling, have you ever heard of it? Priest: No, I’m sorry I don’t get around to watching television much. Juan: Well you see my position is that I am booked against other wrestlers and we fight for support from our fans and for titles. Priest: It doesn’t sound like something God would condone. It sounds like your fighting for money and for entertainment rather than in difference or for a cause. Juan: Yea, that does make it sound kind of bad, but if that’s the case why do I like it so much, I mean I’m a good catholic shouldn’t I be trying to stay clear of these kind of things in life? Priest: The only idea that I could come across is that from this wrestling you feel like you’re a warrior of some type. Juan: Yea, I guess I do get a feeling like that. Priest: Well in that case it isn’t a sinful act. You’re probably so indulged in wrestling because it makes to feel strong and like a warrior the way Jesus was. Warriors fought for pride and something they believe in, all causes Jesus would most likely approve of. Juan: But doesn’t Jesus not condone of violence unless in the act of a Holy War or something of that sort? Priest: That’s not true. As long as you know the consequences of what you do and are not out to physically harm or mentally harm someone else then everything should be good with you and the Lord. Juan: I hope they are. I mean I don’t go out and say I want to hurt my opponent, I do it to win, but sometimes I do hurt the person in the process. Priest: That can’t be stopped and I don’t know Gods outlook on it, but I think if this is what makes you happy then you should stick with it while you still can. Juan: Thank you Father, this is the guidance I’ve been looking for. Priest: It’s all in a days work my son. Now recite the Act of Contrition. (Juan recites the prayer.) Priest: Now go out and say three Haily Mary’s and Our Father and reflect on the things that really make you happy in life, because without those things you’ll never enjoy the wonders God made for us. Juan: Thank You. Juan left the confessional to go say his prayers. As Juan stood their kneeling in front of the Lord he reflected. He reflected on things that put a smile on his face. At that moment Juan promised himself that he’d take better care of him self and set out do things that made his truly happy. It took God to bring out new Juan and Juan just hopes he could continue his path so he can avoid all bad and enjoy a path of happiness. To Juan his happiness will begin this Sunday. A two time Cruiserweight Champion, The title that is Juan’s greatest GOOOOAAALLLLL~! With this Juan makes the sign of the cross and the scene fades to black.
