Revenge Saga Part 14: Another Chinese Encounter...

26 June 2002

Date: 26/6/02 OOC Note: Words between means the speech has been translated from Russian. Revenge Saga Part 14: An encounter of the Chinese kind…Part 2 Crichton punched the last of the Triads in the gut, then followed it up with an elbow to the jaw, knocking him unconscious instantly. The rather limp body of the Chinese Triad fell, motionlessly, to the ground, in a pile along the other two unconscious Triads. Crichton patted down his knee long leather jacket, until he comes to the arm of the coat. He pauses for a second, then develops an even angrier look upon his face. Crichton: YOU BASTARDS! You’ve got blood on my damn coat! Crichton proceeds to kick all three of them a couple of times while they are down, but because they’re unconscious they’re unaware of any such action. He then turns and walks to the fire steps, the only exit from the roof. As he predicted the battle had given him some answers, not very many, but after all the fight was very small compared to his usual standards. He realised that Jaden could have been right. Yeah, he blamed himself for her death and yeah, he did still love her, but he was too obsessive with it. Jaden was his girlfriend now, and he had to make her feel comfortable with that. Leaving pictures of his dead girlfriend around, like some sort of shrine to her memory was indeed too much. He realised that now, but he still could not forgive Jaden for burning those pictures. It was a drastic move to say the least and he felt that she should have come to him and explained her feelings. But would he have listened to her? He would have liked to say yes, but he realised that he probably wouldn’t have. The death of Aeryn had changed him, made him more devoted to her than ever before, after all revenge needed to be served. He needed to tone it down a little, and perhaps separate his whole revenge thing from his life with Jaden. But how do I do that? he silently questioned, stepping off the last of the fire escape steps and jumping down into the alley way. He knew what he had to do, but didn’t know how to do it. The story of his life really. He now knew that all of this couldn’t be resolved without talking to Jaden about it, but the way he stormed out of the motel room meant that he had to gain some serious browny points before she would even talk to him again. He didn’t mind the shouting during in arguments, bur he sure as hell hated the silence that soon followed them. He also hated it when an argument spilled out into the public, because a woman does not give a shit about having an argument in public. So he had come up with a solution, a severely out dated and over used solution at that. Buy her off! That was it, get back into her affections by showering her with gifts. Genius. What should I buy her though? He silently questions, hand dipping into his pockets to retrieve his wallet. Before he could buy her anything, he had to check his funds. Last nights luxurious dinner had set him back over a hundred dollars and now he only had fifty dollars left. What the hell should I buy her? He silently questioned for the second time in as many minutes. Half of him didn’t want to think about it at all, and simply get the traditional chocolates and flowers, but he thought that it was a bit cliched and Jaden didn’t seem to be a flowers and chocolates kind of girl. He decided to head into the city and see if any of the shops inspired him to make the appropriate purchase. He bloody hated shopping! ************ It was the day after the meeting with the Russians, and Fleming had set a weapon’s demonstration in the vast gardens of the Zemoff Mansion, in order to show the Russians the capabilities of the weapons designs. He really couldn’t blame them; the thing about shady deals was that there was no contracts and upon extension no trust. Fleming had spent the best part of three hours haggling and negotiating a suitable price. The final price that they agreed upon was a little less than he expected, but his plans could still be accomplished. Initially he was going to sell them a decent range of battle suits, but since the fee was a little less than expected, Fleming had decided to simply sell them a standard series of battle suits. He had his doubts that the Russians would fall for the trick, but he had to try. In order for his plans to succeed he needed 40% of the fee to pay for the materials and the labour and the remaining 60% of the fee was to ensure The Thunderbolt support and to create a advance weapon systems. The initial deal with the Russians for the advanced technology, would mean that 80% of the fee would be needed to pay for materials and the manpower and 20% to pay for his plans. By producing the standard battle suits for them, 20% of the fee would cover materials and manpower, leaving 80% for his plans. The last thing he wanted to do was to piss the Russians off, but he had no choice now. The Russians were already assembled on a variety of seats, on the back porch of the mansion, watching Fleming’s trusted scientists get the standard battle suit in position. From what he gathered, the Russians were impressed with the sight before them, it was just up to him to make sure that they were equally impressed by the results. He took his suit jacket off and made his way toward the battle suit. Within minutes, Fleming was successfully hooked up to the machine and was proceeding with initial system tests. One of the scientists makes his way over to the Russians and places a specialised laptop in front of them. Scientist: If you will look at the screen before you… [gesturing towards the screen with his left hand] …these bars represent the battle suit systems, they will show the level of performance that the battle suit will achieve. Colonel Kolof: Thank you. With that the scientist is joined by the rest of the scientists, who now take precautions and get a safe distance way. Then four, fully armed soldiers make their way from the house and stand before the Colonel. Colonel Kolof: All Soldiers: Colonel Kolof: [Turning to his second in command] ************ It had taken Crichton about thirty minutes to reach the centre of the city, and he had spent another fifteen minutes window shopping, and still never had an idea of what to buy Jaden as a make up gift. The only thing that really stood out as a decent gift, was a nice black, specialised bowling ball, but it cost more than fifty dollars. He saw it as the ideal gift for Jaden, but his bad luck destroyed his hopes and dreams. The ball cost $55, a mere $5 more than he had. He tried to haggle with the man at the pro-shop, but he got nowhere fast. So her took to the streets once more. Crichton was looking at some particularly nice jewellery in a shop window, when he saw three familiar faces staring back at him via the reflection in the window. Crichton sighs, then slowly turns to face his tormentors for today, the Chinese Triads. Crichton: Didn’t I kick your ass a while back? I’m new to the whole street brawling, but aren’t you supposed to stay down and stay away? Triad 1: [A vicious smile developing upon his face] We can’t! We’re on a vowed mission to take you to the Chinese Military! Crichton: [Nodding in understanding] I see. So we’re essentially gonna have to through it all again then? Triad 2: [Nodding nervously] Ye…yes. Crichton: [Sighing] Right! With that Crichton extends his arm, lightening fast, and connects with the Triad on his right. The Chinese Triad instantly heads south, the Triad on his left attempts to catch him off guard with a kick to the face, but Crichton grabs him by the leg and throws him violently to the ground. Without hesitation the last Triad (the only one still conscious) charges, Crichton steps to the left, leaving his foot in place, forcing the Triad to stumble a little towards the wall, Crichton follows up by ramming his head into the wall. Crichton: [Sighing] I don’t need this shit! Crichton then simply walks away from the three unconscious bodies, oblivious to the passers by, who are still staring at him in shock. Crichton: [Muttering] I REALLY don’t need this shit!
