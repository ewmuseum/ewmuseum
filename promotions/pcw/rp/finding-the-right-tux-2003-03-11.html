Finding the Right Tux

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-03 What to wear? For some that can be a very hard question to answer. For others it’s remotely simple. However, whatever you wear there is one thing almost everyone seems to have trouble deciding to wear. Well for guys anyways; their tuxedo for their wedding day. Something Chris Storm now had to go through. His wedding was coming up and things still needed to be arranged. The flowers weren’t picked out, the caterer wasn’t decided, and the tux and dresses wasn’t brought. With the wedding coming up, things needed to happen and they needed to happen now. “Armstrong Tuxedo’s: Promising you the best in men’s tuxes in all the designer names.” Chris laughed to himself. He still hadn’t picked out his tuxedo yet. Chris looked down at Evan and Alex, both of whom were holding his hands. Chris walks them into the store and looks around. Different manikins posed in the store with designer tuxedos on, fine black suits and elegant whites. A voice rings out from the back. “Hold on, I’ll be with you in just a minute, teehee!” A shiver went down Chris’ spine. He knew that teehee! Chris turned around to leave but it was too late. The person walked out from the back and spotted him. “Chris Storm! Well my goodness, slap me with a pig and color me pink! I haven’t seen you in ages. Give ol’ Tony Blair a hug you be lug!” called out former WIW: NG wrestler, and open homosexual, Tony Blair. Chris slowly turned back to Tony but soon found him locked into a big bear hug from him. Chris nearly falls over from the crushing squeeze of Tony. Chris pulls Tony off him and stands back. He pulls the kids out between the two. Chris does not have a happy expression on his face. Chris tries to put on a smile for Tony, as hard as it maybe. Finally his curiosity got the best of him. “Uh Tony, what are you doing here?” Chris asked. Tony just winked at him as he smiled. “Silly Billy, I work here. As great as wrestling with those big sweaty men was, it just wasn’t my calling. But here I can size up guys and see which one is just the right size for me.” He said with a laugh. Chris nearly doubled over and threw up. Tony laughed knowing he was messing with Chris pretty badly. “So Chris, are you still in the movies?” he asked. Chris’ eyes lit up and he quickly pointed down to the two smaller children. Tony finally noticed them. He bends down and looks at the two. He smiles at Alex. “Well I remember this little fellow. You are Alex correct, Tessa’s boy?” Alex nodded in agreement. Tony looked over at Evan, not knowing him. “And who are you little man?” Tony asked him. Evan had a frightened look on his face. He turns his stare up to Chris and grabs his hand tight. Chris laughs and kneels over beside Evan. “This little man is Evan. Tessa and I adopted him together.” Chris answered for him. Chris gives Evan a little push toward Tony. “Come on Evan, I know he may seem strange but Mr. Blair isn’t that bad of a guy. Go say hello.” Slowly moving his feet, Evan walks up to Tony and says hello. Tony just laughs and greets him back. Chris and Tony stand back upright. Tony looks at the two kids. “Alright you two, there is some toys over in the corner of the store. Go have fun.” He said as he pointed to a corner filled with different building blocks and toys. The two children quickly rush over to the toys to get their pick. Tony looks back at Chris with a serious gaze. “So you are a father now?” he asks. Chris scratched the back of his head. “Yeah I guess. Tessa and I are getting married soon so I guess before long I will be father to three. Which is why I am here. I need to get a tux before the wedding.” Tapping his chin with his index finger, Tony thought about the situation. His eyes brightened and a broad smile came to his face. Tony spun on his toes, hugged himself, and gave Chris the peace symbol with one hand. “By George cutie, I have just the tux for you!” Tony squealed out in delight. He hopped over to the far wall and looked at the different tuxes hanging there for display. He starts jumping for joy as he spots the tux he had in mind. He grabs a tux from the wall and skips back over to Chris. Sweat forms on Chris’ forehead as he watches this whole act before him. His only hope is the kids are not watching this openly odd act by Tony. Tony comes skipping back to him with a tux in hand. Chris raises an eyebrow and looks at the tux in hand. “Come on, take it! See if you like it.” Tony urges him. Chris takes the tux and holds it out. It’s a fine black tux with a silver trim to it. A smile creeps to his face. Tony notices the smile and gets behind Chris. He starts pushing him towards the changing booth. “Now go on silly! Go see if it fits or not. Then we can take your measurements.” Tony says as he continues to shove Chris into the changing booth. He finally gets Chris in there but Chris doesn’t notice Tony went in there also until he starts unbuckling his pants. “GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE RIGHT NOW TONY!” Alex and Evan’s attention turn to the changing booth where a very happy Tony is ran out of the booth. Chris sticks his head out, his face red in anger. He pops his head back into the changing booth. Tony just looks at the kids and wave. Moments later Chris steps out of the changing booth with the tux on. Chris does a quick walk and turns on his heels. He looks both ways and walks back to where he was. Tony applause as the kids goes back to their building blocks. Chris walks back to the center of the store. “I like it. I think I’ll take it.” Chris says. Tony pulls out the measuring tape and walks up to Chris. Taking the tape, he goes to place it from Chris’ buttocks to his heel. Chris notices Tony and stops him from getting to close to his backside. Tony looks up at him. “Come on now stubborn head! I need to take your measurements.” He tells him. Tony tries to take the measurement again but once more Chris steps away from him. Chris fixes his collar. “That won’t be needed Tony. I have all my measurements wrote down alright.” To prove his point, Chris walks to his other set of clothes and pulls out a notepad out of the back pocket. Inside all Chris’ measurements are wrote. Tony drops the measuring tape and kicks the ground. “Oh pooh, and you had such a cute little behind too.” He said with a sad look on his face. Chris walks over to the mirror and admired himself. Tony walks up from behind and looks at the mirror reflection. Chris starts to talk. “Very fine suit you have here Tony. Say…I know this may sound a little strange but do you believe in vampires?” Chris asked him. Looking in the mirror Chris’ mind started to wander about different things. The PCW March Madness and his chance to become the World champ. Something Chris sadly knew wasn’t his time for yet. Then there was the day after, his match with Nerezza. Nerezza was a strange one indeed. In all his years he had never encounter anything like him. This is saying a lot seeing as Chris trained with Genesis. But still, there was just something different about him. He was…. a vampire. “Do I believe in vampires? Oh course silly head. I believe in anything that enjoys sucking.” Came the reply of Tony. His voice was so normal that Chris couldn’t tell if he was joking or not. So instead Chris merely backed away from Tony slowly. Tony looked at himself in the mirror. “Oh my gosh! Look at that sexy bastard. Mmm, I bet some vampire would love to suck on him.” Tony said to his reflection. Simply shaking his head, Chris turned away from Tony. “Look I have a match against some guy named Nerezza. I think he’s a vampire. If so then I need some really cool vampire-hunting clothes for the match! You know some real kick ass threads or something.” Chris said. Tony turned his head and looked at Chris, a serious glaze on his face. “Come this way,” he said. Tony walked over to one of the manikins and pulled down on its arm. Suddenly part of the wall opens up revealing a door. Tony holds the door open for Chris to see. Chris stands in the doorway amazed. “Wow! With this stuff I would be unstoppable! I can see it all now! Nerezza would fall to the great Vampire Hunter Chris Storm!” Chris starts to laugh but soon falls over as a wooden block comes out of nowhere and beans him on the side of the head. Tony looks down at Chris on the floor. “Or maybe not…” he says. Alex and Evan walk over to Chris to see if he’s all right. They look down at him, Chris’ eyes still closed. Alex looks at Evan. “I don’t think Nerezza has that much to be worried about, do you?” the little one asks. Evan just shakes his head. Well the tux was picked out and Chris has found a nice hole in the wall vampire store. The only question now is, with a Chris Storm vampire hunter walking on the streets is anyone safe? Let’s only hope Chris can avoid any more shots to the head before his match on Tuesday. Nerezza….please don’t hurt him too much.
