Stubborn

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-3-02 Nathan’s hands are crossed against his chest. He leans up against Brandi’s desk and has a stubborn look on his face. Brandi stands in front of him. She places one hand on each of his shoulders and stares into his face. Hoping to make him forget about whatever is troubling him, Brandi kisses his lips gently. When she pulls away Nathan’s face is still twisted into a look of determination. Brandi- Nathan, please. Nathan- No! I explained what happened, and you still can’t accept that. It was an accident. Brandi- An accident that cost me a match. Nathan- But an accident is an accident! He didn’t cost you the match on purpose. You know that he wanted you to win just as much as you did. Brandi lets go of Nathan and turns her back to him, facing the door. She takes a few steps away from him before turning back around in a final attempt to change his mind. Brandi- Nathan, you just don’t understand. He should have been paying attention to what he’s doing. I can’t just simply forget all about it. Besides, you have no reason to be angry with me. I haven’t done anything to you. Why don’t we just forget about this whole thing? Nathan- You say you can’t forget about the match, so why should I forget about this? He asked me to do a favor for him, and I told him that I would. Please, just speak to him about it. Brandi- Naaaaathan. I can’t. Nathan- So you just expect to walk into Turmoil with a chip on your shoulder and to beat Tomas and HIT when you won’t even talk to Tim? Tell me that’s not what you think is going to happen. Brandi- I never said that’s what I think. Nathan- Well then what is it that you’re thinking? I’m trying to figure it out, but you’re confusing me. You’re upset because you lost to Teresa on Friday night and you have a chance to beat her in a few days, along with getting a shot at HIT, and you’re going to blow it because you can’t work side by side with Tim? Brandi- I’m not going to blow anything. Nathan- I would hope not. I know that you want a shot at the tag team titles. Brandi- Yeah, I do. Nathan- Well, if you don’t win you won’t get that shot. Besides, if you lose then Walden has to be Tomas’ valet. Brandi thinks about this for a few minutes and giggles quietly. The laughter stops when Nathan shoots her an angry look. Instead she smiles and walks towards the young man once again. She unhooks his arms from each other and places them instead on her hips. Brandi places her own hands on the man’s muscular chest and smiles. Brandi- Come on Nathan. Let’s go watch a movie or something and forget all about business for awhile. Just go out and have a good time. She brushes her cheek against Nathan’s and gently kisses his lips once again. This time the kiss is returned, and she feels relieved. Nathan- I’d love to. Really, I would. Brandi- Then let’s go. Soon, I’m not going to be able to get away from work. The mall will be opening soon. I have mounds of paperwork. There’s the match on Tuesday, hopefully followed by a tag title match. Nathan- We can’t. Brandi- Why not? Nathan- I already told you! I’m not moving until you agree to work things out with Walden. At least talk to him. Brandi- I don’t want to work things out with him right now. Now please, you’re giving me a headache. Let’s just forget about it right now. Maybe we can work something out later. Nathan- You mean it? Brandi- Sure I mean it. I mean, I’ll have to talk to him eventually, won’t I? Nathan- Yeah, I suppose you will. Brandi- Ok then. You’ll go with me? And we’ll have a great time and forget all about this nonsense? Nathan leans forward and kisses the Brandi’s forehead. Nathan- Of course. Brandi- Good. Now we can leave in just a second. I just want to write Tim a note real quick and have you run it over to him. Brandi sits down in her leather chair and dugs through her desk drawer. Eventually she manages to find a pen and a blank sheet of paper. Quickly, she scribbles something on the paper and shoves it in an envelope. Brandi licks the envelope and presses it closed, before setting it on the edge of the desk for Nathan to grab. He picks it up and tucks it inside his jacket. Nathan- Why don’t you get ready while I run this over to him. Brandi- Alright. I’ll see you in a few minutes then. Nathan leaves the room without seeing the devilish grin that has spread across Brandi’s face. She leans back in the chair and rests her feet up on the desk. As she sits there thinking, Brandi find something funny and once again starts giggling. Brandi- Let’s see how much Walden likes that.
