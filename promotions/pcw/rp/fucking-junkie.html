Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th April &#039;03 OOC: Sorry to Skylar, Xavier and Tommy about not getting an RP in for Turmoil. I've just had exams on for the last week and didn't have time. -------------------------------------- The scene opens up in the now infamous grey room, where Chet King’s been addressing members of the PCW roster. He’s sitting in the chair in the corner of the room and staring directly down the barrel of the camera. Dude, you watching? I like totally hope so, because bro you I’m about to lay some gnarly words down for you. Tommy, you’re not smoking at the moment are you? I want you to understand what I’m about to say. So throw that roach away, don’t even think about making another joint, oh and get rid of the bong too. And while we’re at it, flush your secret stash of pills and crack down the toilet as well. Don’t look surprised, we all know you’re more then just an average pot head. Fucking junkie. You got rid of yet? No? Fucking junkie. Well I know you’re feeling a bit jumpy after that hit of crack, but listen up anyway boy. Here’s what I’ve got to tell you. You listening yet? Come on man, get rid of that speed. Look, here’s what I’ve got to say. You think you’re going to win on Fusion? Not-a-fucking-chance. You want to know how I know that. I’ve fought you in one on one competition twice, and I’ve beaten you twice. I guess it’s not really much of an accomplishment to beat a junkie, but I’m just letting you know you’ve got no chance at Fusion, dude. As far as Turmoil went, the only reason you’re team came out on top was because Skylar lost, not me, Skylar. So don’t think that means you hold a victory over me. I wasn’t really ever expecting much with Skylar as my partner, so I’m not too worried about it. Oh yeah, quickly as a side note, regarding your partners chair shot to my head. I’d go get revenge physically on him, but I’m a bit worn out after riding Ellie earlier today. I do have to say that I thought Ellie would be a better fuck then she was, considering how much practice she’s had with men, women, animals, inanimate objects, etcetera. Sorry for that brief interruption Tommy…come on dude! I was just talking about Ellie for twenty seconds and you’ve started snorting speed? Please man, I’m trying to tell you some stuff. Put it away. I’m sure if your brain wasn’t as fried as it is from all the acid you’ve been taking, you’d remember that I was also in that world title match where you won the gold, and you’d claim you beat me there. But I’d hardly count a cluster like that as a match where you beat me. Besides I was cost that match by a certain someone, a certain bitch, a certain Tit…tit…ah just plain old Tit. But I don’t want to get stuck talking about Tit this promo considering I’ve got a million and one things to say about him and his little Tit sucking friends. I mean I would launch into an all out assault on Tit and the Tit suckers, but then I’d lose your attention Tommy…oh shit, it’s too late, you’ve already gone back to shooting up. Tommy, please can you try to pay attention to me? Look that can of gasoline will still be there when I’ve finished talking, you can sniff it then. What are you doing now? Oh look, just get rid of that LSD. It’s obvious I’m not going to be able to get through to this junkie any more. Tommy, if you don’t want to listen to me, at least call the Betty Ford clinic. The pills have fucked you up, you’re a waste of skills. You’re smoking that crack too much you fucking junkie. You need help. Look Tommy, I know you’re body’s passed out on the floor while your minds tripping in the sky, but if you can still hear me just know that I’ve beaten you two out of two times, and that perfect records going to continue on Fusion. What now? Get rid of that metho Tommy!
