Date: 09.02.02 ~*~ Fade in... My road to PCW was a longer one than I had ever wanted it to be. I’m still considered young...after all, I’m only a 20 year old...yet my patience grew wary as I began to pave myself a road to a wrestling career from the mere age of 5. Patience, I have little of. You’re only young, agile, and at your pinnacle once...and to me, my height was at my late teenage years. Of course, I am made to improve with time, experience, and training. Such a height I could not obtain if I had attempted to join an Indy wrestling promotion at the age of 17...when my impatience held me the most. Yet I refused, in my mind, to sit there and train, idle, in some job I hated more than my childhood...until I was 30... At such point where I would not be so young, fresh, agile, and ready to be taught. Yet after 4years of being on my high school’s wrestling team, while backyard wrestling mindlessly at the side... And one and a half years of training at a wrestling school, while traveling every now and then down to Mexico [hey, I didn’t have the money to travel to Japan]—to learn more of the “lucha” wrestling style, as well as high flying like no retired, washed out, has-been wrestler could ever teach me... I knew in my mind’s eye I was prepared to take a dive into the real wrestling world. While I was still at the wrestling school—before the owner saw me fit to graduate—I had been sending out my tapes to local Indy feds. I finally got an answer in a call one night from some federation who liked what they saw. Mind them, I hadn’t even heard of their fed—I couldn’t even remember ever sending a tape there...but it was like a small prayer answered...A modest beginning. The only problem was, what they liked from the tape. No, it wasn’t the cute little pathetic list of about 20 basic wrestling moves I could master back then...it was my looks. What would they want more than some cute little 18 year old chick with a nubile little body and cute hair to have a run as a manager? Oh, I admit...it was pathetic, it was degrading...it was everything I stood against to be an eye candy manager. But, with nowhere else to turn...hey, it was a beginning. Maybe I could train with the pros, and sharpen my skills...and eventually get recognized as a wrestler. Hey, it payed money...and at least I could go around and say, ‘look guys, I made it to XHWO!’ I know, Who?!?! But at least it was above some washed up little wrestling school where most people still believed women belonged at home—“makin’ babies and mah dinner!”. Sickening, it was. While I was putting myself through wrestling school...and, not to mention, one little course at a local community college... I was working two part time jobs [they refused to give me any more hours]. I remember working such odd jobs...anything that brought in some money, minimum wage or whatnot. I’d work at restaurants [never fast food ones, mind you]; deliver mail at law firms and real estates; clean out cages in animal shelters; work with little brats at Day Care Centers... whatever paid the rent. I also found that, even upon joining that little Indy fed; I still had to work a full-time job on the side. Little promotions never pay much...never mind enough for most people to afford to live normally. I guess that’s another reason—besides recognition—that people want to get to the bigger promotions, like PCW, with the desire for the big money. Out of all those countless odd jobs I’ve been working since the age of 16...one little place of temporary work that went down on my résumé made an imprint in my memory... More specifically, one little incident did. All the places I’ve worked in have their own little crazy stories...and this wasn’t a major one. In fact, I haven’t thought of it in a couple of years. But I dreamt of it recently...and a comment my friend made yesterday made me relive that pitiable yet somewhat amusing [considering the boredom that job brought] night... _____________________________________________________________ Date: 01. 03. 00Setting: Somewhere in Queens, New YorkLocation: Rite Aid Pharmacy_____________________________________________________________ I stood behind the counter, resting my head against the cold tiles of the pale, sea green walls. Boredom had got the best of me at this point. It had been two and a half hours since I had gotten a customer. I was working at a local Rite Aid pharmacy. Normally, I’d just work the front counter... But the pharmacist was out today, so I was working the back counter. It would’ve been double duty—‘cause there’s also a Film Developing Center counter at the back...but, Hell, it was raining. Hardly a customer had come in during my 10-hour shift today that would end at 3 in the morning...which, thank God, was only in a couple of hours. I wasn’t sure at this point if they should be paying me nothing for doing nothing, or double for suffering boredom and losing sleep for nothing. Hell, I could be out clubbing or chilling...or even, sleeping. But here I was...Working this boring ass shift. I chewed on my lip with a sigh, running a hand through my midnight azure locks. My hair was getting static, since it was January...early winter. My thin, v-necked powder blue sweater was doing shit for the harsh draft of cold air that came in through the windows and doors. I moved closer to the heater, but it did little besides make my baggy, dark blue skater pants get static and cling to my legs. It was annoying...and every time I went to that little bathroom to wash my hands with hot water, I got a static shock from the metal doorknob. My nose was turning pink, and my lips had gone a bit dry from the cold. I thought for a moment that I should give Daemon or Bianca a call...as, for some reason; no one was tying up the lines wanting their prescriptions refilled or their film developed. But then my hunger overtook me for a moment, and I decided to help myself to a Kit Kat. After all, there was no one else working in this tiny store. They had shut down the front desk and the other parts of the stores...’cause most stores aren’t open 24 hours a day, and only the Pharmacy and Film Development was open past 12... So there was no one to wave a finger at me and tell me that eating a $0.70 chocolate bar and not paying for it, I would be depleting the entire company—and cause them to go bankrupt... Or, something to that affect. I remembered vaguely then that I had forgotten to eat breakfast yesterday morning...so fuck whatever their security cameras caught me doing. I popped another stick of Kit Kat in my mouth, and headed to the phone. I was just about to make a call to Daemon and ask him where he was now, and if I could crash there... when I felt a draft of wind. It was a telltale sign that someone had just entered the door to the side of me. I looked to that spherical mirror hanging over the counter, and saw some dude. Aww, man...just when I thought I was being paid to do nothing, and could head home a couple of hours early [with the hopes that my boss wouldn’t call in to check on me tonight]. But I wasn’t shocked at all...the rush of people usually came closer to the end of my shift...just when I was packing it up and getting ready to leave. It was a bitch, but that’s life... I smirked as soon as this dude came into my vision. He was swaggering towards the counter, whistling some annoying tune. I wanted to laugh, looking at his thick, bulky leather jacket; his black, fingerless gloves [what the hell were the point in those? Especially in the winter...I never got it]; and his tight, faded blue jeans. He was Caucasian, and by the way he dressed, and with his cheap, dark tan—he must’ve been either a cowboy or a construction worked during the day. I’d had pretty annoying encounters with construction workers, so I had every right to hate ‘em... Oh, I didn’t like to make assumptions and label him to be as such... But it was kind of funny to, so I did it out of boredom...as I had seen a million guys like this—even if it was New York, and not Texas. He looked about 30-ish on my first impression. And, not to judge, but he just looked like your average asshole. This could be fun... The first thing he did was look me up and down, slowly and deliberately...as if to say, ‘I got myself a young nubile little thing tonight’. I rolled my eyes in disgust, finally putting down the phone from my cold-stiffened fingers. I ate the last stick of Kit Kat, noting that his eyes stopped on my blue hair. He stared at it for a long, amused while, as I stifled a yawn. Please, I wouldn’t even look at a guy that age if we were both on the streets...and I wasn’t obligated to, as I was now...he was my costumer after all. I told myself it could be worse...I could be some prostitute, and I’d have to definitely deal with him... so I’d just have to make this encounter as short, sweet, and painless for me as possible. If it came down to it, I’d make it painful for him... He finally looked to my violet eyes with an attempted cocky smile. “Hello there, little girl, I’m Joe...what name did your parents give a pretty young thing like you?” Oh my God...how corny was that? I tried not to laugh out loud. No, I’d never give him my real name...of course, my last name was on the tag of my uniform, but I wasn’t wearing that now. So, he’d never have to know. “It doesn’t matter. What did you come here to get? Just buy it and be gone.” No expression of any type of reaction to my words crossed his face. He continued to look at me with that same ugly, annoying, smarmy little grin. Idiot. “Hehe, I think I know what I want right now...but I needed to buy some rubbers.” Oh Jesus...This guy as a condom customer. Just what I needed right now... I suppose that was meant to make me blush. I didn’t at all. I just gave him a once-over, and then reached behind me and handed him a box of small-sized Trojans. He looked down at them, and then back up to the blank expression on my face. He only laughed, a bit insulted this time. He handed them back. “Hahaha, nah, I don’t take this si—” “Oh, I know...you want a one size fits all, eh? It’s less embarrassing, I understand.” With a smirk I went to get a box of those. He made a motion to stop me, with an even less amused laugh. I snickered a bit. “Eh, it doesn’t matter what’s on the box. Your boy...-cough- girlfriend will find out your size anyways—.” he leaned over the counter, interrupting me. He spoke in a secretive little voice, as if I’d think that was sexy. “Oh, no, no, no...I’m not that, eh, small at all. In fact, I’m kind of...big. Painfully big. That’s why I always have to get ribbed condoms, so my girlfriends aren’t too much in pain when we, ah, make love. You know what I’m saying?” He winked at me, and checked me out again. By now, my patience was growing thin. “You know, a girl like you would probably know what condom I should get for that. What do you recommend, Miss Pharmacist? Lifestyles? Trojan? Rough Rider?” He emphasized, licking his lips. “Excuse me? A girl like me?” At that moment, before I could further question his intentions, the store’s door opened and some more customers walked in. I gave them a little motion, as if to say, “Be right with you”. Then I turned back to Mr. Cocky Cowboy here. I arched a blue eyebrow and eyed him blankly. “Actually, Mister, we have just what you want—” I turned and reached behind the counter with a sweet, cool expression on my face. I noted his eyes widen, and he grinned, looking to impress the two women and the man that were waiting behind him for their prescriptions. The people behind him peered over his shoulder to see what I had gotten, as he went into his wallet to pay. “That’ll be $4.95” I punched the code up on the register, and rang it up, handing his box to him. He handed over a five-dollar bill, as I bagged his condoms and handed them to him. “Here you go, sir. Your Trojan Special Flavored Condoms.” That expression on his face—as it turned white, then a fire-engine red—was absolutely priceless. Fade out. Fin
