Reliving a nightmare. Part 2

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 I was sitting in my living room listening to some hard rock music when the door bell rang. I had figured it had to be somebody I didn’t know for the simply reason that I didn’t ever have any visitors. I stood up from my soft leather chair I had been sitting in and walked towards the front door. As I passed by the window I noticed a black and white squad car out front. Great what did these guys want? Neighbors complaining about the loud music most likely. Maybe they were here to just check up on me. Or perhaps my growing fame had found somebody’s eye wanting to make a few easy bucks and decided to sue me for some little thing famous people get sued for. I twisted the knob and opened the door to the two pigs standing on my porch. The both looked as though they were rookies and had nothing better to do then bother some citizen sitting at home and enjoying a rather quiet afternoon. “Mr. Ryckman?” The shorter one said starring up at me. “Yeah, what the f(censored)k do you want?” I was about to stand here and listen to these guys very long. I hate cops. “Could we talk to you?” His partner intervened. “You are, so what do the two of you want?” I was ready to get to the point. “It’s sort of a touchy matter. May we come in?” “No you may not. Now if you would get to the point of the two of you being here. So I may get back to doing something I’d rather be doing.” These two were going to be a pain. This was already obvious. “It concerns your ex-girlfriend.” The shorter man spoke up. My ex-girlfriend? What in the world? I was ordered to stay away from her. And I haven’t been near her since that night. What could the two of them want to see me about, about her? “Go on.” I wanted to hear this. “It boils down to this. She came to us to make sure you stay away from her. She wants nothing to do with you. And she wants us to remind you that you are ordered by the court to stay away. Do you understand this?” Understand? Of course I understand it, but do I really care to obey it. Do these two think they could actually keep me away from her? That anyone could keep me away from her? That if I wanted that I couldn’t find her?“Is there anything else officers?” I definitely had hoped not. “Yes, one other thing. If you don’t mind turn down your stereo.” The tried to peer in as if looking for someone I might have been holding against their will. That I was sick and twisted enough to keep a captive. “Have you had any complaints about the volume of my music officer?” He looked at me sort of odd. “Not that I can think of.” I smirked. “Then I do mind. And I won’t turn the volume down. You can’t do sh!t until you had a complaint now can you. Once my door is closed you can’t do sh!t. Now if you want to write me up something then just do it. Otherwise get the h*ll off my property.” The lil guy began to get angered. He looked up at me knowingly that he was six inches shorter than myself. “Listen here. We’re the cops and you’re nothing. So you do as we say. You understand that.” I could care less what these two pricks had to say. I turned and slammed the door in their faces. What are they going to do knock down my door and take me in for disrespecting them. If they did that they’d be the laughing stock of their department. Walking back to my soft leather chair I plopped down. Through the window I could see the two men get back into their car. They sat there for a few minutes before driving off. Why did every cop seemed to think they had any right to tell someone what to do? Just because they wore a d@mn shield? I cranked the volume up on my stereo and let the music fill me. The pounding of the rhythm the words caressing my soul. Hard Rock music was for me what vitamins did for so many others. It was vital to my sanctity. I craved loud music just as I crave the urge to pound someone into the ground. As I sat there listening to the words of a song. I began to wonder why Emily had sent the pigs over here to remind me of something I already knew. Apparently something had happened to cause her to think of me. I had heard she was having nightmares from that night. That she was in therapy. I had also heard that she had gotten married to her high school sweetheart. But what could have gotten her to think about me again? Did I plague her dreams? Did I leave apart of myself imprinted on her very mind and soul? Did she want me back? After all this time apart did she want to see me? Was her going to the cops her way of saying come to me? Take me away? I love you? I didn’t know why she sent the pigs over here. I just know that she did. The reasoning was the if’y part. Perhaps I should pay her a visit. This way we could talk this out just between the two of us. Her and me together again. I’ve spent the last few months sitting here listening to music and drinking. All the time thinking of her. Thinking that someday perhaps she would change her mind. Realize that what had happened was an accident. That I didn’t mean to do what I had done. That I really did love her and want to be with her. At the court hearing she said I was obsessed with her. That I was stalking her weeks prior to what had happened. She told the court she was afraid of me. She slandered my name and in front of the good people of the community. Maybe she was ready to say she was sorry. Maybe she was ready to say that she was wrong. Maybe she was ready to beg for forgiveness from me. All these thoughts came into my head. I thought of the lonely times I spent by myself since. The hours and minutes that I wish we could have been together. Times they may have changed and we may not be together anymore, because of her. But deep inside a voice was telling me something. Go to her… Yes, it was him. The Ripper was speaking to me. When he talked I couldn’t ignore him. Especially this time. I should go to her. I had to see her. I had to see her face. See those eyes. Hear that voice. Only then could I be sure that she was over me. Only then could I be sure she didn’t want me. Only then could I hear her say I love you. Only then could I forgive her for what she did to me. Only then…
