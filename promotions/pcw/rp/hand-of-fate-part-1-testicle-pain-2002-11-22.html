Hand Of Fate - Part 1 - Testicle Pain

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 22, 2002 [There comes a time in a man's life when he's face with things that are out of his control. Things just happen, and for no reason. Fate is what all those dudes call it. Those little gnarly strings being plucked by the ever bodacious fate. She plucks on the lifeline to cause a ripple effect that changes the entire path of the person. The man must react to this sudden change and make an important choice. This is where that whole fork in the road thing comes into play. You go left, one fate. You go right, it's a completely different fate. Which one should he pick? Which one will he pick? No one really knows, not even the man. Fate is laughing because it seems either way the man is screw. That's life, says fate. Shit happens soon follows the statement. Yeah, because the ever so bogus fate makes it happen, so technically shit is fate. Well, something like that, man. As the subject was lost, a return to reality. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry make up a faction that is feared by every other tag team in PCW, and they are The Cowabunga Connection. Both men have walked their paths and many times it's been the same path. Fate set them up as best friends. Fate killed their fathers. Fate brought them together as brothers. Fate has kept them away from the PCW Tag Team Titles. Fate has also made Harry lose in the PCW Cruiserweight Title Tournament. Damn fate. Everywhere the Cowabunga's turn it's been peeking it's totally heinous head up. The bogus chick plucking those strings like she's imitating Eddie Van Halen hitting a guitar solo. Damn her. Damn fate and all of her bogusness. Where she's done gnarly things, she's doubled the totally heinous] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: My nut hurts, dude. [Tommy sits at the kitchen table in his blue and yellow bath robe, his right hand down his blue and yellow boxer shorts. Harry sits across from Tommy, staring at him wtih fright over the Los Angeles Times. Harry wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, leather boxer shorts, and...well, nothing else really. Just showing off his pale and hairy legs. Tommy has gotten used to them by the way] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Come on, man. I'm trying to eat my Lucky Charms. [The dim sunlight peeks through the skylight above the two men, providing Harry just enough light to make out the big words in the newspaper. The glass indentations on the kitchen table are making it hard for Harry to make notes as to which words to look up in the dictionary. As Tommy keeps digging in his boxer shorts with a grimace on his tanned face, Harry circles a few more words in the newspaper with a mask of confusion across his face] TTT: I'm serious, dude. My right nut has been hurting me since last night. Harry: Maybe that fat cow sat on your crotch too hard. [Tommy stops his testicle inspection to look up at his brother with a very serious look. Yes, Tommy was locked in Harry's hotel room with Jabba The Slut, but nothing happened. Everyone at the party didn't believe Tommy when he burst out of the room with his face filled with lipstick marks. He assured all the dudes and dudettes there that she was only able to get in a few kisses, nothing else. They all laughed surrounding Tommy in a semi-cirlce. They pointed in their drunken state at the jester, the fool. The joke was supposed to be on Harry. It was supposed to lighten his mood before his most gnarly match against Gaz Green. Meh. Like it matters, nothing happened...and that's that. Well, that's what Tommy keeps telling himself...] TTT: Compadre, nothing of that sort happened. I got out of there before she put her Jabba The Slut mode into overdrive, dude. It was bogus to do to you, and it was heinous to have turned on me. Now...what is REALLY heinous is this annoying pain that I have in my right testicle. [Tommy sticks his hand down his blue and yellow boxers once again, feeling around. His fingers prodding his testicle gently for a little tubular check up. He was only at the doctor's a few weeks ago and he got a totally righteous clean bill of health. Nothing was wrong. Now this pain last night. Nothing to make him scream in agony, but when a man's testicles hurt, damnit, they hurt] Harry: Let me take a look, man. TTT: DUDE!!! [Tommy jumps up from his chair, making it tip over backward onto the hardwood floor. Harry's eyes are wide and frozen with the newspaper. Tommy leans over to pick the chair up, but the pain seers through his testicle. He winces as he places the chair on all fours and leans against the back to catch his breathe. Such pain, dudes. Too much] TTT: This is totally bogus, dude. [Harry looks through an article about how smog is taking Los Angeles hostage, soon destroying the clean air once and for all. Harry circles one of the words... 'environment'...what the hell is up with that word, man? Harry can't even pronounce it] Harry: Maybe it's cancer, man. [Tommy looks up at Harry with shocked eyes. Of all the bogusness that has crossed his bodacious mind, cancer is one of the things that hasn't crossed his mind. Tommy is optimistic, always looking on the bright side of things, but for some reason cancer makes sense. At any moment, any place this horrible illness called cancer can ravage someone's body, their soul. Suddenly everything cell, every bone, and every muscle in Tommy's body begins to shake. His breathing becomes erratic....'Totally Tubular' Tommy becomes frightened] TTT: Dude... Harry: What, man. [Harry just circles words in his newspaper, not really paying attention to what could be going on. Tommy's brother doesn't understand the seriousness of this matter. It's insane. Cancer could be eating away at his body as they speak, or as Tommy shakes like a frightened child and Harry circles big words] TTT: I could have cancer in my testicle, dude. [Harry looks up from the newspaper] Harry: Who said that, man? TTT: YOU DID!!! [Harry stares at Tommy with that blank stare in his eye. He then leans his brown, bloodshot eyes up toward the right as if he's trying to recall something. He has. He remembers saying something about cancer, but never said Tommy had it] Harry: Hmmm, maybe I did, man. Well, maybe we should go to the emergency room, man. TTT: You think so, compadre? Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy lets out a sigh, all his personal demons beginning to creep up to the front of his mind. All the debts that he owes, which aren't many, begin coming to him. The people whom he loves plague his mind. What will they do if he were to leave this world so quickly? So viciously? Without even some kind of signal. All because of his testicle gaining cancer he'll miss out on so much in life. Good and Bad. Fate has made yet another bogus pluck on the string of The Tubular One. Just when things were about to get good] TTT: Lets go, dude. [The emergency room of Los Angeles Memorial Hospital is most bogus to be. The off-white walls, well used to be off-white, are lined with all kinds of insprirational and happy posters. One is of a kitten trotting in a field that says 'Enjoy life'. Now what a bodacious little kitten has to do with enjoying life is beyond Tommy, but maybe somebody else gets it. The chairs are very uncomfortable with the room being the size of what seems to be a sardine can. Everyone with illnesses, wounds, and cancer...all of them stuck together to share their misery. Tommy and Harry sit in the far right corner, away from the main receptionist. Tommy is still in his blue and yellow robe forgetting to actually get in any clothes, his mind was too cluttered with thoughts of death that changing didn't really mean that much to him anymore. Harry sits there staring at some of the other people in the room. A black man with a blue bandana on his head sits, hunched over, across from them. A pool of blood forms underneath him as it seems he was in some kind of stabbing. The black man looks up at Harry] Black Man: What'choo lookin' at, sucka! [Harry points at the man's wound] Black Man: Some nigga stabbed me! Thought he was cool tryin' to step on our turf, well, I taught dat dawg a lesson when I slit his throat. Right before dat he caught me in my gut, dawg! You happy now... *cough cough* [Harry shrugs and gives the black man a slight nod. Tommy then looks at Harry with anger mixed with pain. Harry shrugs at Tommy looking like some sort of innocent little child. Harry then notices across the room a man lies out on a ffew chairs with his ass in the air. It appears that a lead pipe is sticking out of the man's ass] Harry: Look at that guy's ass, man. TTT: Dude, that's totally rude. Harry: But there is a lead pipe sticking out of it, man. I should have brought the camera. TTT: Dude, that is kind of weird. I'll have to admit....I just hope that I don't have cancer. That could be totally bogus. Harry: Yeah, man. We have a big match to compete in on Sunday. TTT: Totally. Even though we have beef with Redemption Song, we have to team with those totally heinous compadres. I don't know what that's going to be like, but I know all four of us have that will to win. We'll stick together, and if they don't want to cooperate, then at least we know the Cowabunga Connection can handle that. Harry: Yeah, man...but not with a cancerous nut. TTT: Dude.... [Just then a beautiful brown haired women opens the wooden door and peaks out with her hypnotizing blue eyes. She wears a very colorful nurses uniform which looks like a party favor exploded all over it, pretty gnarly] Nurse: Tommy...they have some room for you. Just come on back here. [Tommy slightly smiles and hobbles his way toward the back of the waiting room, to enter his room. Harry looks on in amazement, now making his way to the receptionist at the front] Receptionist: Can I help you? Harry: Yeah...I think my testicle is hurting me now... [Fade to black]
