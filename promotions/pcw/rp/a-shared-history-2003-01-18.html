A Shared History

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/18/03 It was a very vibrant day as the clouds were spaced out along the enchanting light blue sky. The streets were crammed with people wanting to take advantage of such nice weather. The camera pans around and stops on a woman in a black trench coat running towards a building, with her reddish brown hair bouncing behind her. She breaks right in front of the door and stops reading a sign that says 'Closed'. The camera moves up right next to her and we see it's Brianna who is looking quite upset. She takes a moment to think, and runs around the outside of the tall building and stops at another door. She tries to pull it open, but it's locked. Brianna kicks the door and takes a seat on the step leading up to it with her head in her hands. 'You were supposed to be here over an hour ago Brianna.' Brianna looks up at the owner of the voice and her face turns to disgust as she looks away biting her lip. 'Well it's nice to see you as well.' he replies sarcastically. 'Thomas why don't you just go bother someone else.' 'This is the thanks I get for reminding you about the Creations Convention? I told you the time, the rest was up to you.' Thomas flashes a smile as he places his hands in his black pants pocket. Brianna stands up looking him right in the eye. 'I was not intentionally late. Someone sent a Jehovah's witness over my house, who couldn't take the hint that I didn't care at all about what he had to say and offer.' Brianna starts to walk away and Thomas runs up to her, walking along her right side. 'So you're saying I tried to sabotage you? Look Bri, I want you to get your business out there as much as you do.' 'Just leave me alone before I throw you into the middle of the street.' Brianna deliberately rushes across the street and as Thomas follows he is almost hit by several cars as they swerve last minute out of his way. As they make it across the street Thomas lets out a huge sigh as he realizes how lucky he is. He then directs his attention back to Brianna ready to bother her some more. 'Wow, now you're threatening me. So I guess all that wrestling crap is toughening you up? Should I be shaking in my boots?' Thomas lets out a little chuckle as he stays on his mission to pester Brianna. Bri stops next to a dark blue Saturn and pulls out a set of keys turning off the alarm. 'Wrestling is not crap, it's my profession, so I would appreciate it if you wouldn't bash it.' She opens up her door a crack, and Thomas walks over towards her and stands in front of the door. Brianna, who is getting upset with him being around her at all, pulls the door out in a quick motion slamming it right into Thomas's package. He let's out a huge groan as he bends down holding where the pain is surging. 'That was mighty childish of you.' He squeaks as he yells in her direction. Brianna's window rolls down. 'It's been fun but I have more important people to see.' She takes off speeding down the road as Thomas looks on. As Brianna gets stopped at a red light her mind starts to wonder. The scene changes. We see Brianna sitting at a table, in a nice red dress, at a fancy restaurant. Sitting across from her is Thomas, and oddly enough both are smiling at each other. Thomas reaches his hand out on the table and Brianna brings hers out and places it in his. The scene then cuts to those two again, except they are yelling this time. 'I'm sorry Thomas, but I'm not ready to get married. I'm too young and I have my whole life ahead of me.' Brianna says as she places a hand on Thomas's shoulder who is gripping tightly on a ring box in his left hand. 'Ok so I guess I'm just a waste of time huh Brianna? I guess these last two years have also meant nothing right?' He throws the ring box up against the wall, and it opens up and slowly the ring falls to the hard wood floor as if time was stopped around it. The scene cuts back into the inside of Brianna's car as she is once again traveling down the road. Brianna starts thinking more about how life would have been different if she would have accepted Thomas's proposal a few years back. Then she pushes it out of her mind remembering how he acts towards her now. 'Shoot! I almost forgot to pick up Milo.' She makes a u-turn nearly clipping a rusted Chevrolet which causes an old lady to curse her flashing the middle finger. Ten minutes pass and Brianna pulls into the parking lot for a huge mall. She jumps out of her car and steps inside. As she steps inside she is engulfed into a swarm of people going in and out of different stores. Brianna looks around and starts walking towards a Hot Topics store. Sure enough, standing there looking at shirts is Milo. 'It's about time. I thought I was going to have to take a bus home.' 'You would not.' 'True, but you're still late.' Milo says as he walks with Brianna out of the store. 'So how did that convention go? Get any interested buyers?' 'I really don't want to talk about that right now.' she replies sharply as they make there way to the exit. Before they can leave, a group of teenage boys rush over towards Brianna. “Whoa! Are you that one chick Brianna Washington from PCW?” one of them asks as the others stand back a few inches away. “Uh yeah.” Brianna replies leisurely. “Well could we have your autograph?” “I don’t see why not.” Brianna takes the black marker from the boy and signs a PCW magazine he has in his hand. His friends crowd around, and Brianna spends a few more minutes giving her signature. “Thanks!” The boys just stand there as Brianna and Milo walk away. Milo can’t help but laugh as they start walking through the parking lot. “What’s so funny?” Brianna asks confused. “One day your just plain old Brianna, and the next you’re a superstar.” “Superstar? Hardly Milo. They just remembered my face from last week is all. I still have a long way to go before I become really big.” “Hun you’re already there! Now you’re a big time wrestler.” Milo laughs again as he catches Brianna blushing as they open up the doors to her car. Brianna revs up the engine and takes off as the scene slowly fades…. Brianna has a flawless record thus far in PCW. Though she has only had two matches, she has managed to come out on top both times. At this rate good things could come very quickly for her. On Sunday she has her first ever tag team match which could prove to be an odd experience. Who knows what kind of gadgets she is willing to break out to help her and Tessa Windsor succeed. It’s girl power time baby!
