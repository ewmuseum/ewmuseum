Jackass!

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/5/02 Prior to coming back from their home city of Detroit, The Price Brothers arrive in Teaneck, New Jersey back with Primetime Championship Wrestling. The scene is set on the inside of a huge hotel room. The walls are covered with a red paper surrounded by golden flower patterns. A huge white marbled fireplace is up against the far right wall of the room. In the center of the room is a huge pinewood brown dining table. A bedroom doorway is to the left side of the room and a bathroom doorway is over to the right. Surrounding the place are chairs, couches and a huge black TV set placed in the corner by the fireplace. We can here a television set on playing the sounds of a strumming guitar. In the main room, we can see the television set is not on, so it must be coming from the bedroom. The camera pans forward towards the bedroom. As it turns into the doorway we can see full view of the whole room. A bed covered in sparkling white sheets is placed up against the far wall. The walls are covered with the same wallpaper as the main room. Pinewood cabinets and desks are pressed up against the walls all over the place. To the left of the camera is a large black television set, very similar to the one in the main room. The television set is switched on. The camera pans around facing the screen dead on. We make a sudden switch to what the screen is showing. *A man diving onto a car then into a lake* *Matt Hoffman riding a BMX around a large wooden hoop, then falling of about half way through* *An elevator packed full of people, the a man in a wolf mask pouncing up from below everyone growling* With the recognizable faces on screen, this TV show is spotted as non-other than MTV’s Jackass. The camera quickly turns to its regular view. This time, blocking view of the television set is Michael Price. Michael is wearing a white polo T-shirt and a pair of cream colored pants. Michael begins to speak in a slow and posh manner. Michael: “Hello and welcome to another production from The Price Brothers. Today we bring you an update of just how my brother and I are. To tell you the truth, we are not all peaches and cream, oh no. I’m afraid to say that even though I’m quite all right, my brother seems to have taken a turn for the worse. You see, doctor’s say he has come down with a bad case of; how did he pronounce it? Ah yes. Jackass-enitus.” Michael is trying to keep a straight face as he talks. He looks to be on the verge of laughing. Michael: “Ooh, not so good. You see, this disease as some may put it causes the carrier to act like a…” Another male voice loudly interrupts him. The voice is recognized as his brother, Chet Price. Chet: “Jackass!” Michael: “Exactly.” Chet: “What? Who are you talking to? I wanna know where the remote is, jackass!” Chet appears from the right of Michael coming from the doorway. He is wearing a black T-shirt and a pair of green army pants. Chet looks at the camera then begins to draw nearer and nearer with his face. As he practically squashes his face up against it, he quickly darts backward and looks at Michael. He points at the camera and produces a shocked look on his face. Chet: “There’s people in there! There’s people in that camera!” Michael looks at Chet in disbelieve. Michael: “That’s the viewers moron!” Chet thinks to himself as he stares down at the floor. Chet: “Oh yeah.” Michael: “I was just telling these viewers how you have entered the stage of Jackass-enitus. Do you wanna explain what it is does to you?” Chet: “Apparently, it makes me do crazy stunts just like Johnny Knoxville; so they say. They say it travels around through touching someone else.” Chet turns to Michael, who is still looking deep into the eye of the camera. Suddenly, Chet slaps him! Michael falls backwards onto the TV set, then rolling onto the floor. Chet turns back to the camera. Chet: “Now you see, Michael has the disease too. Now, if you’ll excuse me I’m going to run around in my underwear through Teaneck’s huge park and scream that I’ve lost my pants. To the Jackass Mobile!” Chet literally jumps away from the screen. The camera pans in on the floored Michael who is holding his face. Michael: “Damn Jackass!” The scene comes to a short end. We re-open in a large park. Forests of tall green trees cover the whole left side of the park. To the right are a couple of wooden park benches. In the center is a long and thin gravel pathway stretching of toward a large jungle gym covered with many children. The camera stays focused on the jungle gym. Shockingly, after a couple of seconds the sounds of children screaming render through the park. Children at the jungle gym seem to be running for their lives, each one darting in a different direction. The cameraman begins to run with the camera looking to see what is going on and maybe helping out. From behind a long red slide appears a man. The man has spiked blonde hair, a black T-shirt and…no pants? The camera slams to a sudden stop as this man is recognized as PCW superstar, Chet Price. Chet: “Where are my pants? I can’t find my pants!” Chet spots the camera and slowly jogs over. He stands in front of the screen laughing. A pair of black jeans are thrown from behind the camera. Chet grabs the pants and quickly throws them on. Chet: “When did you get my jeans?” Chet cocks his head in a questionably manner. A man from behind the camera begins to speak in response. Man: “Your brother bought them a few minutes ago telling us to watch out for you. He suggested we buy a piece of string to cover but then thought you may be insulted.” Chet: “Yeah…wait! What?” The scene comes to an end.
