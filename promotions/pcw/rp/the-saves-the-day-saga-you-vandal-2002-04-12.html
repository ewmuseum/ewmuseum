The Saves the Day Saga: You Vandal

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-12-02 A rock, when procured from its spot on the dense and unforgiving asphalt, or from its spot in the dirt, laying between blades of grass can become many things. A paper weight, a door jam, a projectile. It all depends who holds it, most of the world is like this, anything can go from artsy post modern decoration to a primative killing machine. This rock, which was picked up from the corner of Broad and Fifth, was sent crashing through the window of a local home, just down the street. The small houses front picture window shattered and tinkled to the ground, and the thrower and his friends laughed and ran, their Fubu jeans (despite their overwhelmingly white skin color) cut down wind resistence, but they still got away with only a glimpse of them seen by one of the inhabitance of the house. The boy, not a day older then 17, looked out the window with hatred searing through his brown eyes. He knew who and why, it was the same group of wiggers that messed with him every day at school, and it was because he was different. Green hair, piercings, Marilyn Manson T-shirt, he was a freak. And proud of it. But people like this wouldn't let him be. His revenge would come soon. -------------------------- Brayden walked from his house the following day, his messanger bag a tad heavier then usual, but otherwise, everything else was normal. You see, last night was the last straw. Through his entire life in this god forsaken school he was tortured by the same group of people day in and day out, he couldn't walk the streets without them fucking with him. He was sick and tired of it. His bag was heavy with his father's 9mm handgun. It was either them or him. What he didn't realize is that, no matter how distressed he was, he was being watched from somewhere else, because he reminded someone of themself. And, although this is not a good thing, it is his protection. -------------------------- He walked into school and when unnoticed for two periods, until it was time for him to go to Gym class. 'Hey, faggot!' And it began, he turned around to see the same four thugs that shattered his window last night walking towards him. Last year, a broken window wouldn't have bothered him, but now... he and his father were having financial problems, and a broken window is a kick to their well being, not that these assholes cared. The first one to approach him was named Kevin, he was the ring leader and shoved him up against a locker. Breyden growled but the four just laughed at him. Kevin: Ooooo. The pussy has fangs, what, are you a fucking... werewolf or something. More laughs, at the horrible pun. None of these kids have IQ's higher then their dick size. Breyden: Get the fuck away from me, wigger trash. He was shoved against the lockers again, a crowd now forming. Kevin: What did you call me! Breyden: You fucking heard me, your a wigger, you arent yourself, you think because you listen to rap and dress like a idiot that people are gonna like you, your nothing... and get your HANDS OFF ME. The other three thugs pounced on him, one taking the care to throw him down the stairway leading to the first floor, Breyden hopped up, and went to grab the gun and put a end to all this once and for all, but his hand was yanked and he himself pulled into the teachers lounge. Breyden: WHO THE F-.. 'SHUT UP!' And he was silenced. Standing in front of him was the DarkStar. DarkStar: Every damn Pseudo depressed goth kid has to be overly dramatic about things doesnt he. Are you trying to give us all a bad name... again! God I hate being the only damn diety left... freakin God... why can't that asshole be doing th-... Breyden interuppted him. Breyden: They have screwed with me far too much, and I was going to end it. DarkStar sighed and looked down. DarkStar: Be creative then, and give me that damn gun. He handed it to him, and DarkStar began to laugh. DarkStar: This? You were going to strike fear into the hearts of the thug trash with THIS! This couldnt kill a pigeon! Hahaha! The kid put his hand down, he was right, this was a bad idea, maybe he should just kill hi-... DarkStar: Oh dont start that suicide bullshit now. You remind me way too much of myself when I was a child. Friend, since you remind me so much of myself.. i'll do you a favor... come along. Breydon: How did you kno-... DarkStar: Do you want a job? -------------------------- Night had fallen now. The white stars twinkled in the perfectly clear sky. Kevin and his posse of trash looked down the street and saw Breyden sitting on the corner. Now was a perfect time to give that pussy what he deserved. When they got within ten feet, Kevin ripped the hood off his head and was about to warn him about his imminent doom... This wasnt Breyden? Breyden had green hair... Kevin: Oh look... another fucking freak, blue hair is so queer. His friends laughed, well two of them, the other one seemed to be bleeding from the mouth, quite badly. Everyone froze and stared at him as nothing but guttural sounds came out from his throat. The crimson stained the pavement as he fell forward. The large combat knife stuck in his stomach now protruding through his back. Kevin: Oh shit! DarkStar stood, swatting the ring leader away with one shot, he fell unconcious. DarkStar: A freak? I am not just another freak my friend... I am the Lord of Dreams... I am darkness... I am the DarkStar... and... for now... I am your GOD. The other two tried to sprint away, but somehow DarkStar was in front of them, he grabbed one of them by the head and threw him through a store front window, the glass shattered much like it did in the beginning of this story. He cried out in pain when he noticed the large shard sticking through his neck. The second one began to get away, but 'Fate' clicked in on that one. As he ran into the middle of the street, he was in too much of a hurry to even see the truck that killed him. DarkStar just smiled. Maybe he could get used to this. -------------------------- Kevin saw two blurry images as his eyes began to focus, his jaw was broken and his head hurt incredibly badly. He tried to move but couldn't, his arms were tied together behind some stick and his feet were tied also. He struggled but caught a punch to the stomach... he tried to scream out because of the pain... But his mouth had been sewn shut. He cried. DarkStar: Crieing? And you call my friend here a pussy... He pointed down to Breyden, who was pouring gasoline around Kevin's tied up body, Kevin finally noticed where he was, standing on a pile of wood, tied up... This was a witch burning. He cried some more. DarkStar: Man, I've died before and it wasn't this big of a production, did you see me crying? No, now stop... DarkStar patted himself down for some matches but couldn't find them. Breyden just smiled, and clicked his Zippo on, the flame stood high and reflected in Kevin's tear filled eyes. Breyden: I would tell you to go to hell, But I don't know where you end up in this situation... but don't worry, eventually you will pay eternally for all you have done. Goodbye, pussy. And he flung the lighter, it hit the wood and engulfed, almost quick enough to explode, the threads on his lips ripped through as Kevin screamed in pain. This was music to both their ears. DarkStar grinned and looked down at the 17 year old kid and almost saw himself at that age. Although his murders were more advanced and poetic, not quite as brutal, but he could learn. DarkStar: So. Did you need a job? -------------------------- DarkStar stepped back into his stately house and layed down on the velvet couch in the living room. Switching on the TV and seeing nothing but stories about how religion was falling apart and funerals were becomming more and more pointless as less people died every day. The world was collapsing because of this blasted Other World problem. He put a hand to his head and closed his eyes. He felt a weight on his chest and opened his eyes, seeing his lover Talena laying on him, he smiled and put his arm around her. Talena: Who were you today, lover? DarkStar thought about it for a second. DarkStar: A role model. --------------------------To Be Continued
