The Rooftops...

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 August 2002 The rooftops… They were the cliffs of the city…the treetops of the urban terrain. From their posts, one could stand tall above all and survey all that happens before them. This place belongs to the Neo Void God… He was already at the top of the game; standing tall over all the talent of this pathetic place called PCW…being champion was just a fluke title to place upon a person whose ego needed pampering. Kannon makes that title proud under those circumstances. But if the god were to take that belt from you…which he will after the PPV is over…then it would be meant for much more than that… Not just a trophy… His time would come…but for now there were other flat-scans that wanted to step into the way. Nights were the best time to stand amongst the rooftops…glaring down at the movements of the ants that scurried back and forth, left and right, to and fro in their repeated paths. It was like this every day…going to the same places over and over, saying the same things to each other, and coming back with the same actions that only made things all the more dull and droll… The city…the name isn’t important. But atop one of that city’s tall structures sat the duo that already claimed ruling over PCW… they already have their dominance set out before the people, and the scummy mortals have accepted it, whether they say so or not. If they didn’t like it…they were most certainly scared to step up and do something about it. Oh, you say what about Pharaoh? Duke? Chase? El Diablo? Those three on Turmoil have a better chance training to survive rather than training to win against the god. Not like any of them is training…Pharaoh’s trying to copy Duke now and poison his body with whiskey, like copying Frisco and his army wasn’t enough. Duke…his idea of training includes a cheap midget prostitute and half a bag of pot. Chase…it just gets harder and harder to remember that he still works for this company. Point being…these three are nothing… But that doesn’t mean that El Diablo is taking away the attention of the nVg. No… El Diablo is only proving to keep the mind of the god from totally dropping into utter boredom and brain deadness. If you could only imagine how stale it gets for Akiyama to constantly take the fight to these three jobbers once a month? Its like a headache that keeps coming back…an annoying one at that… But this HAND OF FATE…it shall be the aspirin that clears up all the nonsense. It wont be long…not long at all people… Cyclonica sat on the edge of the rustic stone cathedral high above, her black vinyl clad legs crossed and draped over the edge. Her silver spiked stiletto boots clicked and clanked against the wall slowly, in a rhythm as she swung them forwards and backwards. Leaning forward, she stared out unemotionally with her chin propped up in her hands, her elbows resting on her well-toned thighs. The breeze fluttered slightly, scattering a bit of her hair across her face. She sighed…a melancholy sigh that almost echoed through the air, but most certainly not loud enough to do so. “You’re fighting Duke, Chase, and Pharaoh…how wonderful.” The enthusiasm was dryer than a Pharaoh promo. There was nothing wrong with the match…it was just that Akiyama didn’t have time to go around and put a beating on everyone that jumped up and raised his or her hand for one. Gods had things to do as well…like bringing about the end of the universe and such… “Don’t these fools have anything better to do than to waste your time? You’re a very busy man, and shouldn’t waste your time with trash.” It was almost like she was talking to herself, that’s how low her voice was. Each word off her ice blue lips were like a gust of the wind…almost silent, but whispering past your ear close enough for you to hear. “I hate it…it makes me drowsy.” A yawn came up just on cue there. “These three are nothing…and they’re trying to take us down for what? For the honor of defeating a god? They’re feeble-minded.” It didn’t look like her pale skin was getting chilled by the night air…though she only wore a ice blue crop top underneath a black mesh shirt. Her entire torso was exposed, and not a bit of her body was shivering. As if she were adjusted to being cold. “Duke wants communication…that’s his problem. He’s not smart enough to know when he’s being insulted, then he needs to retire his old @ss. I thought he was retired at this point…sitting over there at that announce table and all. Gods do not speak on the same level as mortals…so therefore if you do not chose to hear the word of the god, then you will miss out on his warning.” Chimes came through the air…tinkling softly in a breath. That was the chuckle of the Divine Prophetess. “His repeated refusal to acknowledge Akiyama as PCW’s god as well as his own god is quite humorous… but it’s an old tale from the Malice man…a tale that the god is quite tired of. What a fool he is, seeing how that last time he refused to admit to Akiyama’s authority, he found his Absolute Destiny Apocalypse on the outside of the ring…it’s a long way from over the top rope to the outside ya know. If that’s what Duke wants from Akiyama again…we’ll be more than happy to give him the ADA off the stage this time…” A slow breath exhaled from her…a white vapor trailing from her lips. Her dark eyes were closed now…like a conscious sleep. “Chase…I wonder why he’s even still here. After the mess he pulled with Big Alan Steele and the New Breed…I thought he would have had enough with being humiliated. Even after he was left amidst the pile of wrecked bodies when Akiyama won the World title shot, I would think that he would have had enough of being a laughing stock.” Her eyebrow flickered, the brow twitching. “Shows that some humans will do anything to get themselves in the spotlight.” Silence for a bit…nothing except the sounds of the city below and the clanking of the heels above. “Chase is a waste…nothing more I can say about that.” That would only leave one man left on the agenda for Turmoil…the one man who’s proved in the past that he’s a nuisance to the god…in some way or another… “Pharaoh…Pharaoh…Pharaoh…” she hissed lowly under her breath. “It’s been a while…long, long while to say the least. Last time we saw him, we were leaving him a wreck in the middle of the ring and preparing to banish his wife yet again…and would have succeeded if not for the meddling ways of Jay Sylo. Too bad this isn’t the time for cordiality exchanges…this is the time, however, for action.” Behind her the brass bells of the clock tower chimed off the hour…their tone both melodic and a nuisance to her ears. Her right eyebrow arched…that’s all she showed of her annoyance. “We’ve both come across our own bothersome pests since we parted ways…you with Kannon, becoming the Egyptian Gimp each night…and now with Frisco…where you had no course of action except to rip off his Argentine army in order to match offence with him. Akiyama…he had the entire tag team division…which he toyed with show after show to display his superiority over mankind…and then there was El Diablo…the Devil that would get his face kicked in to once again put Akiyama up to the standards of God himself. He’s gone on to take status that you mortals would acclaim among any other human, yet chose to disavow because the god holds them. What has our old friend Titu done?” Scoff, scoff, went the Celestial. “Going from Kannon’s whipping boy to Frisco’s punching bag. And now its time for him to return to the true position that he belongs in…Neo Void B!tch.” The bells kept going on…ringing on and on in the background. Then suddenly…they stopped. “The bells toll for thee, boys…but once that bell ceases, it’ll be the nVg standing tall amongst you flat-scans…you will be left as sponges, left to absorb each and every drop of torment into your pain soaked bodies. All of you…will never deny the god of your fear and of your respect ever again…” Heavy footsteps came from behind her…like a giant stomping towards one to squish them. Cyclonica made no movement as the image of the god hulked over her. In his arms was a huge brass church bell. The veins bulged from his arms as he lifted the bell onto his shoulders and chucked the bell from the top of the church. The clapper of the bell rattled around as it plummeted through the air. Cyclonica stood to her feet on the edge of the roof, her eyes never leaving the bell. Akiyama stood next to her with his teeth gritting, his face wrapped in anger. “Brass falls down from Heaven…but it shall be gold that’s plucked out from Hell…from around the waist of the biggest devil of them all. Kannon…El Diablo…all of you flat-scans…prepare for your pain…it’s the least you can do so that your body doesn’t go into complete shock after the beating you all will receive. Comas are so boring after putting mortals in them for a number of times.” She stepped off the edge and walked away from the roof edge. Akiyama followed, and they made no gesture as a huge explosion rocked from below, a faint ring of a bell sounding before the roar of flames surged up. “So says the Divine Prophetess…”
