Date: 7/07/02 Across a crowded room a boy hugs the wall, the noises of his fellow partygoers becoming a blur. A few drinks in him only, nothing to impair his thoughts......he must drive home, and that is always a dangerous task. On the far side of this dance hall, this place of merriment and mirth, his eye falls by complete and utter chance. A girl there......his age, young. Her skin flawless.......her auburn hair resting in a small ponytail, drifting to the small of her bare back. RIP He smiles at her.....and again through the utmost of luck, the crowd parts. As Moses once parted a sea, the mass of humanity drifts to the left and to the right, clearing a path between the two. She looks at him. And she smiles. RIP RIP The boy takes her hand, out on this balcony......asks her name. She replies, her voice as sweet as the greatest nectar. In that moment a hole in his spirit mends......the youth gone through so much. He is complete again.... RIP RIP RIP The sound of music plays again, though this time much louder, echoing through the confines of an automobile. A merry grin splays across the faces of both boy and girl......they sway their heads, in the eternal dance of their generation, their race. RIP Their bodies lock together in the twilight, swaying back and forth with the eternal dance of life. RIP Eyes cry out, tears welling and falling in a cascade. They mourn, they mourn......as the body lowers, the young and old mourn and cry. Small whispers whisper forth from the onlookers, comments, rumors......another of the true bases of life. “I heard they don’t have enough left of them for a viewing.” “It was that bad?” “Yeah....in fact I heard.....” RIP RIP RIP In the dance floor they sway, and shift.....others dance around them as well, but they are the only ones in the room. The only ones that matter......he leans in close, she feels his hot breath on her shoulder. RIP The box lowers into the ground, as the heat of mid July pounds down upon the darkly dressed mourners. “So young.” “But did you hear what really happened?” RIP Hands glide across supple skin, kisses raining as they move. Madly, madly, for an endless eternity..... RIP The boy takes her into his arms, dipping her to the applause of the crowd. Somewhat surprised they turn.....sheepishly grin and wave. RIP The car speeds through the darkness, gaining momentum. He jerks the wheel to the left and to the right, as his companion laughs hysterically.....too much alcohol in her system to realize what a situation she is in. RIP He mouths her breast, sucking lightly as she writhes below him. RIP Dancing closely, the music slowly entering both of their souls. RIP Their arms entwined, the boy and the girl place their drinks to each other’s mouths. Sipping, they grin and look at each other over the tops of the containers holding the pretty poison. RIP She arches her back, pushing against him. RIP Lower, lowering and fading into eternal damnation. RIP Dancing, dancing, faster and faster. RIP Thrusting, faster in the beautiful darkness. RIP Reaching a high rate of speed, the boy laughs and steps on the brakes......then looks down stupidly as they sink to the floor. RIP Her screams fill his mind. RIP The impact of the car hitting the tree launches him forward, rocketing him through the windshield. He hits the tree with his right shoulder, spiraling through the night air. RIP “So young.” RIP “I wonder what.....” RIP Dancing. RIP Dancing in the darkness. RIP The boy looks up, blood seeping into his eyes. His legs pinned beneath the twisted mass that used to be his car, he struggles weakly but to no avail. RIP Suddenly a form walks out of the gloom.........in his dazed state of mind, he calls her name in spite of the fact the footsteps are much too heavy to be her. RIP A foot presses down onto his gut. It pushes slightly, and in the blinding pain he knows something internal is shattered. The young man sputters.........then looks up, his eyes seeing but his mind not registering the horror. RIP His love, his only love looks to him.........nerves hanging from her severed neck. Her life’s blood drips onto him, hot and boiling.........the face above her regards him with sheer apathy. Pure disgust. RIP His screams pierce the night, as the burned man closes in on him. RIP Words fade to silence, light fades to dark. Hope fades to despair, and life fades to death. RIP RIP Again. Again. Again we spiral into the mindless void......your own self loathing. The hatred accumulated, the blinding madness that is your own doing, that YOU created. Rip, and rip. There is logic no longer. We have crossed the point of no return....irrevocably, eternally. He rips through the veil of goodness, this demon of the night.....once more, a pair of lives extinguished. Ripping through sanity.....ripping through hope. RIP RIP RIP Your doing. Yours and yours alone. Welcome back to Hell.
